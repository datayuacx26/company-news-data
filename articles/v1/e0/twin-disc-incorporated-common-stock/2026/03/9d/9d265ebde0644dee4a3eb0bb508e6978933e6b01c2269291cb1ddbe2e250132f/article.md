# SMM Hamburg 2026

The global maritime industry gathers once again at SMM Hamburg, one of the world’s leading trade fairs for the maritime sector. Held in Hamburg, this international exhibition brings together shipowners, shipyards, designers, suppliers and maritime professionals to explore the latest technologies, exchange knowledge and shape the future of shipping.


During this event, several leading brands from the maritime industry will be present at the same stand, including Katsa, Twin Disc, Rolla, Kobelt and Veth Propulsion. Together, these companies represent a broad portfolio of solutions for modern vessels, ranging from advanced propulsion systems and high-performance propellers to power transmission technology and precise control systems.


Visitors will have the opportunity to discover how these complementary technologies contribute to improved maneuverability, efficiency and reliability across a wide range of maritime applications. From commercial vessels and offshore operations to specialized maritime projects, the combined expertise of these companies supports shipbuilders and operators worldwide.


The teams will be available throughout the exhibition to discuss the latest developments in propulsion, driveline integration and vessel performance. Whether you are looking for innovative solutions for newbuilds or upgrades for existing vessels, a visit to their stand at SMM Hamburg offers valuable insights into the technologies driving the maritime industry forward.
