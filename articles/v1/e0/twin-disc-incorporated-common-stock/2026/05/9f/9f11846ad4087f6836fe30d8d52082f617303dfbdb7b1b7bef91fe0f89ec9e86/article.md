# Twin Disc to Present at Noble Emerging Growth Virtual Conference

## Twin Disc to Present at Noble Emerging Growth Virtual Conference


May 28, 2026

(NASDAQ: TWIN), today announced that Chief Executive Officer John Batten and Chief Financial Officer Jeff Knutson are presenting at the Noble Capital Markets Emerging Growth Virtual Equity Conference on Thursday, June 4 at 8:30 am Central. **About Twin Disc**


Twin Disc, Inc. designs, manufactures and sells marine and heavy-duty off-highway power transmission equipment. Products offered include marine transmissions, azimuth drives, surface drives, propellers and boat management systems, as well as power-shift transmissions, hydraulic torque converters, power take-offs, industrial clutches and control systems. The Company sells its products to customers primarily in the pleasure craft, commercial and military marine markets, as well as in the energy and natural resources, government and industrial markets. The Company’s worldwide sales to both domestic and foreign customers are transacted through a direct sales force and a distributor network.
