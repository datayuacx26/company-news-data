# First Commonwealth Bank Names Manager at White Oak Branch


**INDIANA, PA (January 13, 2026)** ― First Commonwealth Bank has announced a new, but familiar manager for its White Oak branch, located at 1527 Lincoln Way White Oak, PA.


Vincent Bereznicki (burz-nick-E), who previously worked at First Commonwealth, returns to manage its White Oak branch. He has spent the past six years of progressive experience in branch management, sales, and operations.


“Vince’s customer-first approach and managerial experiences in banking will be great assets to FCB,” said Cliff Davidson, First Commonwealth Bank’s Area Manager. “Vince’s familiarity with this market and our bank, combined experienced in a financial-goal focused environment and administrative and managerial skills in the financial industry will be tremendous benefits in this key role helping customers and businesses with their financial needs.”


Prior to returning to First Commonwealth, Bereznicki held bank manager positions at several local community and national financial institutions. He held teller positions at First Commonwealth in 2019 and 2021-22.


Bereznicki lives in Pittsburgh, PA.


**About First Commonwealth Financial Corporation**


First Commonwealth Financial Corporation (NYSE: FCF), headquartered in Indiana, Pennsylvania, is a community bank committed to its mission of improving the financial lives of its neighbors and their businesses. First Commonwealth operates 126 branches throughout Pennsylvania and Ohio and also has business banking centers in Pittsburgh and Harrisburg, Pennsylvania; and Cleveland, Canton, Cincinnati and Columbus, Ohio. First Commonwealth, through subsidiaries First Commonwealth Bank and First Commonwealth Insurance Agency, provides a full range of commercial and retail banking services, including mortgage, wealth management and insurance solutions, along with equipment financing and SBA and indirect lending.
