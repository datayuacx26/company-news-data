# First Commonwealth Names New Development Officer to Equipment Finance Group | First Commonwealth Bank

First Commonwealth Names New Development Officer to Equipment Finance Group


**INDIANA, PA (April 30, 2026)** ― First Commonwealth Bank announced the appointment of a new Development Officer to its Equipment Finance Group (EFG).


Mike Basile brings nearly 15 years of equipment financing and banking experience to First Commonwealth. In this role, he is responsible for cultivating new equipment finance relationships in FCB’s Pennsylvania markets and expanding existing business with current clients by working directly with lenders as a dedicated product partner.


“Mike has extensive experience in this niche area of the banking industry,” said Mike Kaplan, Middle Market Development Leader for First Commonwealth’s EFG. “His strong relationships in this market along with his extensive experience will be great assets to our team to open opportunities with new companies.”


The past two years, Basile served as a business development officer for Wintrust Commercial Finance in Garnet Valley, PA. Previously, he also held similar business development positions in the equipment finance channel for several financial institutions through central and eastern Pennsylvania.


A graduate of the University of Delaware with a bachelor’s degree in marketing, Basile lives in Garnet Valley, PA.


**About First Commonwealth Financial Corporation**


First Commonwealth Financial Corporation (NYSE: FCF), headquartered in Indiana, Pennsylvania, is a community bank committed to its mission of improving the financial lives of its neighbors and their businesses. First Commonwealth operates 126 branches throughout Pennsylvania and Ohio and also has business banking centers in Pittsburgh and Harrisburg, Pennsylvania; and Cleveland, Canton, Cincinnati and Columbus, Ohio. First Commonwealth, through subsidiaries First Commonwealth Bank and First Commonwealth Insurance Agency, provides a full range of commercial and retail banking services, including mortgage, wealth management and insurance solutions, along with equipment financing and SBA and indirect lending.
