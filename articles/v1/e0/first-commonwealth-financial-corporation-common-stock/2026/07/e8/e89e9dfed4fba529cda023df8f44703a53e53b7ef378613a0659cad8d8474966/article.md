# First Commonwealth Names New Financial Advisor to Central Ohio Advisory Team


**COLUMBUS, OH (April 23, 2026)** ― First Commonwealth Bank announced the appointment of a new financial advisor to its Central Ohio Advisory Team.


Mark S. Yandura brings 25 years of experience in the financial/investment service industry to First Commonwealth. In his role, Yandura will be responsible for all aspects of sales, new business development, compliance, risk management, operations and revenue generation.


“Mark has extensive experience in the wealth management industry and great knowledge of our Central Ohio markets,” said David Buckiso, First Commonwealth’s Wealth Services Manager. “His proven success in creating and accelerating revenue/sales and developing new business solutions will be great assets for our Central Ohio team.”


Most recently, Yandura spent the past five years as a senior financial advisor for a regional credit union, based out of Beavercreek, Ohio. Prior to that he spent 20 years at several financial institutions, serving various financial advisory roles.


Yandura lives in Delaware, Ohio, and will be based out of First Commonwealth’s Lewis Center office.


**About First Commonwealth Financial Corporation**


First Commonwealth Financial Corporation (NYSE: FCF), headquartered in Indiana, Pennsylvania, is a community bank committed to its mission of improving the financial lives of its neighbors and their businesses. First Commonwealth operates 126 branches throughout Pennsylvania and Ohio and also has business banking centers in Pittsburgh and Harrisburg, Pennsylvania; and Cleveland, Canton, Cincinnati and Columbus, Ohio. First Commonwealth, through subsidiaries First Commonwealth Bank and First Commonwealth Insurance Agency, provides a full range of commercial and retail banking services, including mortgage, wealth management and insurance solutions, along with equipment financing and SBA and indirect lending.
