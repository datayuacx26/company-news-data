# Seven First Commonwealth Bank Mortgage Lenders Earn Top Industry Award | First Commonwealth Bank

Seven First Commonwealth Bank Mortgage Lenders Earn Top Industry Award


**INDIANA, PA (April 7, 2026)** ― Seven mortgage bankers from First Commonwealth Bank―five from its Pennsylvania markets― received Top Originator Awards from the industry-leading publication, the Scotsman Guide.


Receiving the awards were: **Nathan Boyle** , Cranberry, PA; **Bob Quinn** , Mars, PA; **Lynne Tomkowski** , Johnstown, PA; **Zaine Tomkowski,** Johnstown, PA; **Kaitlyn Tomlinson** , Hollidaysburg, PA; **Antonio Benton** , Columbus, Ohio; and **Karen Sharp** , Cleveland, Ohio.

“Winning a Scotsman Guide Top Originator Award is a testament to our loan originators’ unwavering commitment to their customers and their craft,” said Tom Sullivan, First Commonwealth’s Mortgage Banking Executive. “It is one of the top performance recognitions in the retail mortgage industry and is quite an achievement. This ranking is widely regarded as the Gold Standard in the mortgage industry, showcasing the top performing professionals in the country.


“It is also important to note that these accomplishments cannot happen without exceptional operational support. Our operations team is relentless in their efforts to help us win, each day,” added Sullivan. “We want to congratulate all seven mortgage originators selected for this prestigious award.”


Among the most prestigious honors in the mortgage industry, the Scotsman Guide Top Originator Awards annually acknowledge the best and brightest in the field, showcasing their exceptional talent, dedication and innovation.


The Scotsman Guide, a leading resource for mortgage originators, evaluates thousands of professionals across the country to identify the top performers in various categories. From loan volume to customer satisfaction, these awards recognize excellence in every aspect of the mortgage origination process.
Individuals receiving the awards not only excel in closing deals but also prioritize integrity, transparency, and personalized service. They understand that behind every loan application is a dream, a goal, or a life-changing opportunity, and they approach their work with the utmost care and professionalism, according to the award criteria.


**About First Commonwealth Financial Corporation**


First Commonwealth Financial Corporation (NYSE: FCF), headquartered in Indiana, Pennsylvania, is a community bank committed to its mission of improving the financial lives of its neighbors and their businesses. First Commonwealth operates 126 branches throughout Pennsylvania and Ohio and also has business banking centers in Pittsburgh and Harrisburg, Pennsylvania; and Cleveland, Canton, Cincinnati and Columbus, Ohio. First Commonwealth, through subsidiaries First Commonwealth Bank and First Commonwealth Insurance Agency, provides a full range of commercial and retail banking services, including mortgage, wealth management and insurance solutions, along with equipment financing and SBA and indirect lending.
