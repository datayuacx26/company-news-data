# First Commonwealth Announces New Manager at Downtown Greensburg Branch


**INDIANA, PA (March 17, 2026)** ― First Commonwealth Bank announced the appointment of a new manager to its Downtown Greensburg Financial Solutions Center, located at 111 S. Main Street in Greensburg.


Toni Antoniono has served First Commonwealth for the past 14 years, most recently as the manager at the Natrona Heights branch.


“Toni has made a great impact as a terrific leader during her nearly 15 years at First Commonwealth,” said Britney Zacherl, Financial Solutions Network Area Manager for the Westmoreland-Somerset markets. “Her leadership and operational skills along with her managerial experiences will be tremendous benefits in this key role helping customers and businesses with their financial needs. We are so happy to have her join our Westmoreland-Somerset team.”


Antoniono began her career with First Commonwealth in 2012 as a part-time teller. From 2015-2020, she rose in the ranks from full-time teller to Commercial Banker and eventually to Senior Relationship Banker. In 2020, Antoniono was promoted to Senior Sales Manager at the Forest Hills branch before taking over as Manager at the McDonald office. After leaving First Commonwealth for several months, Antoniono returned to the bank as the manager at the Natrona Heights branch, where she has served since 2022.


Antoniono, who resides in Greensburg, assumes her new role April 1.


**About First Commonwealth Financial Corporation**


First Commonwealth Financial Corporation (NYSE: FCF), headquartered in Indiana, Pennsylvania, is a community bank committed to its mission of improving the financial lives of its neighbors and their businesses. First Commonwealth operates 126 branches throughout Pennsylvania and Ohio and also has business banking centers in Pittsburgh and Harrisburg, Pennsylvania; and Cleveland, Canton, Cincinnati and Columbus, Ohio. First Commonwealth, through subsidiaries First Commonwealth Bank and First Commonwealth Insurance Agency, provides a full range of commercial and retail banking services, including mortgage, wealth management and insurance solutions, along with equipment financing and SBA and indirect lending.
