# Tizeti CEO Kendall Ananyi shares 2021 Plans

Tizeti CEO Kendall Ananyi shares 2021 Plans


##### By[TIzeti Press](https://www.tizeti.com/author/lastnightout2000/)


September 11, 2021 In[TIZETI Unlimited Internet](https://www.tizeti.com/category/tizeti-unlimited-internet/)


Tizeti CEO Kendall Ananyi shares Tizeti’s plan for the next year at the Tizeti NextGen 2021 event.


Highlights


- Tizeti Overview
- Tizeti Journey
- WifiCall launch of Smart IVR and Voice OTP
- Tizeti Enterprise Service launch of work from home plan
- Tizeti Next Gen Internet Plans
- Tizeti’s payment partnership with Aella and Kudi


Other talks at the event can be watched here:


1.[Demo of our Next generation internet service](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JwxqbWwXoG8&t=312s) that delivers 1Gbps to our customers (200x speed of our current plans)


2. Eniola Campbell, Country Manager of **Nokia**[overview on 5G](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L9hdduf-6XY)


3. Akin Jones CEO of **Aella App** talk on 1st in Market[Buy-Internet-Now-Pay-Later partnership](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hLRgP_5nPoA)


4. Olubunmi Elizabeth Ogun, DGM Sales of **MainOne’s** talk on[Delivering Broadband internet in West Africa](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xs6SHGixQM4) through partnerships.


5. Yinka Adewale, CEO of **Kudi** talk on our[internet and bills payment partnership](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=64VnDGGXIHg) on their expansive agent network


6. Femi Kuti, CEO of **RelianceHMO** talk on how they[utilized WifiCall API’s in their telemedicine service](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RAvB4mQCtQs) over the pandemic
