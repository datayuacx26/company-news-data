# Lattice Named to G2’s 2026 Best Software Awards, Ranking Among Top 50 HR Software

We are excited to announce that Lattice has been named to[G2’s 2026 Best Software Awards](https://www.g2.com/best-software-companies/top-hr) , ranking among the Top 50 HR Software products. What makes this especially meaningful is that it is rooted in feedback from the thousands of customers who use Lattice every day.
‍
As the world’s largest and most trusted software marketplace, G2 reaches over 100 million buyers annually. With its recent acquisition announcement of Capterra, Software Advice, and GetApp, that reach will expand to more than 200 million buyers annually.


Its annual Best Software Awards rank the world’s best software companies and products based on authentic, timely reviews from real users. ​​Being part of G2’s 2026 Best Software Awards underscores Lattice’s ongoing mission to make work meaningful.


As G2 Co-founder and CEO Godard Abel shared:


> *As buyers increasingly shift to AI-driven research to discover software solutions, being recommended in the ‘answer moment’ must be earned with credible proof. Our Best Software Awards are grounded in trusted data from authentic customer reviews. They not only give buyers an objective, reliable guide to the products that help teams do their best work, but they’re also the proof AI search platforms rely on when sourcing answers. Congratulations to this year’s winners, including Lattice. Earning a spot on these lists signals real customer impact.*


For us, this recognition also reflects the trust our customers place in Lattice every day.


> *This award means so much because it’s driven directly by our customers’ voices. Every review reflects the real impact our platform has on HR teams and their employees. We’re committed to listening, learning, and continuously innovating to help our customers build high-performing organizations. — Stephanie Waldner, Chief Customer Officer, Lattice*


## **A Year of Innovation for Strategic HR Teams**


This award comes on the heels of a milestone year for Lattice. In 2025, we delivered more than 275 product releases across our platform, including the launch of Succession Planning, Performance Improvement Plans, and the next-generation AI Agent.


Today, more than 4,500 businesses and over 875,000 users rely on Lattice to help employees grow, managers lead effectively, and companies succeed.


*Improve manager productivity, simplify people operations, and act on employee insights with the Lattice People + AI Platform.*
