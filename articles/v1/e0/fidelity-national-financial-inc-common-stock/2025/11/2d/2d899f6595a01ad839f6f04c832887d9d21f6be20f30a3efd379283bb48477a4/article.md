# Fidelity National Financial CEO Mike Nolan and CFO Tony Park to Speak at the KBW 2025 Title Insurance Day

JACKSONVILLE, Fla.


, Nov. 25, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- Fidelity National Financial, Inc. (NYSE: FNF) today announced that Mike Nolan, Chief Executive Officer, and Tony Park, Chief Financial Officer, will participate in a virtual fireside chat at the KBW Title Insurance Day on Wednesday, December 3, 2025 at approximately 10:00 am Eastern Time.


The fireside chat will be available live and for replay via audio webcast on[investor.fnf.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4566764-1&h=2746428718&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.investor.fnf.com%2F&a=investor.fnf.com) .


**About Fidelity National Financial, Inc.**


Fidelity National Financial, Inc. (NYSE: FNF) is a leading provider of title insurance and transaction services to the real estate and mortgage industries. FNF is the nation's largest title insurance company through its title insurance underwriters - Fidelity National Title, Chicago Title, Commonwealth Land Title, Alamo Title and National Title of New York - that collectively issue more title insurance policies than any other title company in the United States. More information about FNF can be found at[fnf.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4566764-1&h=242839158&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.fnf.com%2F&a=fnf.com) .
