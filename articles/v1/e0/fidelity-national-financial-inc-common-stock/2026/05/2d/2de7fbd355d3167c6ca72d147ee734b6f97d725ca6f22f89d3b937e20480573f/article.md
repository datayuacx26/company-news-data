# Fidelity National Financial Announces Quarterly Cash Dividend of $0.52

JACKSONVILLE, Fla.


, May 7, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Fidelity National Financial, Inc. (NYSE: FNF) ("FNF") today announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.52 per share of common stock. The dividend will be payable June 30, 2026, to stockholders of record as of June 16, 2026.


**About Fidelity National Financial, Inc.**


Fidelity National Financial, Inc. (NYSE: FNF) is a leading provider of title insurance and transaction services to the real estate and mortgage industries. FNF is the nation's largest title insurance company through its title insurance underwriters - Fidelity National Title, Chicago Title, Commonwealth Land Title, Alamo Title and National Title of New York - that collectively issue more title insurance policies than any other title company in the United States.
