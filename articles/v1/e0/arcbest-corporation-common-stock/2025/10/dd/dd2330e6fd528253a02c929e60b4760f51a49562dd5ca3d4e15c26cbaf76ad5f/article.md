# ArcBest Philanthropy and Employee Highlights: Q3 2025

### Advancing education


-


**Fill the Bus:** Collected essential school supplies for the annual[United Way of Fort Smith Area](https://www.unitedwayfortsmith.org/)[Fill the Bus](https://www.unitedwayfortsmith.org/agencies-programs/fill-the-bus/) campaign for over 30 school districts in six counties.


-


**Union Christian Academy ImpactU** : Contributed to the ImpactU sponsorship program for the areas of greatest need within the school.


-


[Community School of the Arts](https://www.csafortsmith.org/) : Sponsored the school’s high school performance of Sister Act, marking our ninth year donating to CSA.


### Supporting People


-


[Breakthrough T1D](https://www.breakthrought1d.org/) : ArcBest’s 2024 Leadership Academy graduates held several fundraisers for Breakthrough T1D, including a “Soak the Suits” event, food and treat sales and a Razorback football ticket raffle.


-


**Tunnel to Towers 5K:** Members of our[VERG ERG](https://arcb.com/about-arcbest/sustainability/people/belonging) joined the Tunnel to Towers 5K in Medina, OH, honoring the life of 9/11 firefighter Stephen Siller and benefiting the[Tunnel to Towers Foundation](https://t2t.org/) .
