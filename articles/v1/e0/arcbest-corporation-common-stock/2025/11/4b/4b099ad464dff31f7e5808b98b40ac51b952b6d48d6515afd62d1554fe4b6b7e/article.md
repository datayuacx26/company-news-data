# ArcBest Named Top Company for Women to Work in Transportation

## ArcBest earns elite status for empowering women in logistics


ArcBest has been named a “[Top Company for Women to Work in Transportation](https://www.womenintrucking.org/press-releases/women-in-trucking-association-names-2025-top-companies-for-women-to-work-in-transportation) ” by the Women In Trucking Association (WIT) for the third year in a row (2023-2025), earning the distinguished “Elite 30” status once again.


This recognition is based on how we champion women across the organization, with a workplace culture that supports gender diversity, work-life balance, competitive pay and benefits, and growth and development opportunities.


“In an industry that never stops moving, the women at ArcBest are instrumental in keeping supply chains strong. Their dedication, innovation and leadership are essential to how we serve our customers, move freight and deliver excellence every day,” said Erin Gattis, ArcBest chief human resources officer. “Being recognized for a third consecutive year tells us that women across trucking and logistics see ArcBest as a place where they’re supported and encouraged to grow and thrive. We’re honored to receive this recognition from Women in Trucking.”


More than 21,000 professionals across executive management, operations, safety, HR/talent management and driving roles voted on the companies that best support women in the workplace. Companies that received the highest number of votes earned a place in the “Elite 30.”


## Hear from the women driving ArcBest forward


ArcBest offers a wide range of career paths, award-winning development programs and a wellness package that supports employees through every stage of life. Additionally, our Women in Supply Chain (WISC) employee resource group focuses on empowering, supporting and advocating for all women in the supply chain industry and has four chapters employees can connect with.


Here’s what some of our team members had to say:


“ArcBest fosters a culture that values and encourages work-life balance, making it possible for women to be present and successful personally and professionally. We don’t have to choose between career and family. I’m grateful for the authentic leadership of women like Judy McReynolds, Erin Gattis and Lori Brooks, who exemplify this and have helped break down barriers and pave the way for women to thrive in the workplace.” – Robin Childers, ArcBest senior manager of enterprise account development


“ArcBest is committed to creating an inclusive environment where women are empowered to lead and thrive. From mentorship opportunities to leadership development programs, the company actively invests in supporting women at every stage of their careers. It’s inspiring to be part of an organization that values diverse perspectives and is helping shape the future of the transportation industry.” – Caitlin Roth, MoLo manager of talent acquisition


“I feel like my input matters, and it gives me purpose in my day-to-day job. I’ve been with the company for 27 years, and this is a big part of why I’m here. It can be a fast-paced work environment, and we try to make it as fun as possible. Everyone that I work with around the system is helpful, knowledgeable and kind.” – Lisa Stoa, ABF Freight service center clerk, Omaha, NE

## About Women in Trucking


From the driver seat to the C-suite, the Women In Trucking Association serves the commercial transportation industry with an emphasis on trucking. Founded in 2007, WIT is a non-profit organization with the mission to encourage the employment of women in the trucking industry, promote their accomplishments, and minimize obstacles faced by women working in the industry.
