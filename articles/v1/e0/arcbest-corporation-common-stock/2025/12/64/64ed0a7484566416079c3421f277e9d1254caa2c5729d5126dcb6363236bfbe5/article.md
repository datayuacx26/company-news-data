# ArcBest Recognized as one of America’s Most Reliable Companies

## Dependability that moves businesses forward


ArcBest has been named one of[America’s Most Reliable Companies of 2026](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-most-reliable-companies-2026) by *Newsweek* and Statista, earning a spot among 300 organizations that have proven themselves as the most dependable in their industry. This award highlights our ability to provide reliable supply chain solutions, communicate with customers clearly and consistently deliver on the promises we make.


“Reliability is foundational to the way we operate at ArcBest. Customers count on us to deliver their freight when, where and how they need it, and that trust drives us every day,” said Seth Runser, ArcBest president and CEO-elect. “We listen to understand their businesses and challenges, then build solutions that make it easier for them to succeed. With more than a century of logistics experience and a commitment to bold innovation, we’re proud to be recognized as one of America’s Most Reliable Companies, and even more proud to be a partner our customers can depend on.”


## Why customers trust ArcBest


As supply chains grow more complex, businesses need confidence that their freight will arrive as expected. That’s what ArcBest delivers every day. We make logistics easier with solutions designed around each customer’s needs. Our integrated approach combines transportation, technology and 100+ years of expertise, giving businesses one partner who can handle it all. From a single shipment to a fully managed supply chain, every solution is backed by a team committed to problem-solving and guided by values that put customers first.


## Methodology


The 2026 list is based on an independent survey of over 2,400 U.S. business decision-makers who regularly engage with B2B companies. These respondents — who have purchased from, used the services of or researched B2B companies in the past three years — provided more than 80,000 individual evaluations, measured across five key metrics: likelihood of recommendation, ease of doing business, value for money, consistency of deliverables and reputation for dependability. The survey was conducted from April to May 2025, and the top 300 companies with the highest scores were recognized.[Read the full methodology](https://assets.newsweek.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Newsweek_Americas_Most_Reliable_Companies_2026_Methodology.pdf) .
