# Primis Bank celebrates the retirement of longtime leader Mark Zuskin - Primis Bank

### **Primis Bank celebrates the retirement of longtime leader Mark Zuskin**


**COLONIAL HEIGHTS, Va.** — After nearly 23 years of dedicated service at Primis, Mark Zuskin, senior vice president and commercial relationship team lead, is retiring. Known for his selflessness and unwavering commitment, Zuskin has been a driving force behind the success of the commercial banking team, leaving a lasting impact on the bank, his team, clients and the community.


Throughout his career, Zuskin was dedicated to helping clients achieve their goals, building trusted relationships and serving as a true partner in their success. His genuine care for his clients and steadfast leadership will be felt at Primis for years to come.


“Primis has been more than just a place of work — it’s been a family,” Zuskin said. “It’s grown quite a lot since my first day, and I’m very proud of what I’ve been able to accomplish in my time here. I’m incredibly grateful for the talented team I’ve had the privilege to work alongside and for the trust our clients have placed in me over the years. It’s been an honor to help businesses in our community grow and thrive.”


“Mark’s impact on Primis is immeasurable,” said[Rick Fulk,](https://primisbank.com/people/rick-fulk/) Primis Bank president. “Having been colleagues for over 20 years, I’ve seen firsthand the dedication and passion he brings to his work. His leadership, mentorship and genuine care for both his team and his clients have made him a cornerstone of our bank. I wish him all the best in this next chapter of life and thank him for his extraordinary contributions.”


To celebrate Zuskin’s remarkable career, Primis invites the community to a retirement reception at its[Colonial Heights location](https://primisbank.com/location-hours/colonial-heights/) (3012 Boulevard, Colonial Heights, Virginia) on Tuesday, April 1, from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. The event will feature light refreshments and food, offering an opportunity for colleagues, clients and friends to express their appreciation and well wishes.
