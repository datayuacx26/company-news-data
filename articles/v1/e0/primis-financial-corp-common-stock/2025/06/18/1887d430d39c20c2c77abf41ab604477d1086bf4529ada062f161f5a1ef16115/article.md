# Primis Bank Promotes Longtime Leader to Associate Director - Primis Bank

### **Primis Bank Promotes Longtime Leader to Associate Director**


**RICHMOND, Va. —** Primis Bank is proud to share the promotion of Casie Taylor to Associate Director of In-Market Banking.


Casie has been a vital member of the Primis team since 2009, where she started as a banker in the Northen Neck and worked her way up the ladder and evolved into a standout leader—someone who truly embodies what it means to know and serve people well. Her ability to build authentic, lasting relationships has left a mark on both customers and teammates across the bank.


“Casie leads with heart, and she leads by example,” said Amanda Gravely, Senior Vice President and Director of In-Market Banking. “She’s known for her ability to handle big challenges with a calm confidence and for always putting people first. This is a well-deserved step forward for someone who has been instrumental in shaping our culture and future.”


In her new role, Casie will support Market Center leadership and also work closely with the V1BE team to further expand its influence across the bank’s footprint. While her scope is growing, her focus remains the same: helping people succeed both inside and outside the organization.


This move is part of Primis’ broader effort to strengthen leadership and continue evolving the way the bank serves teammates, customers and communities. Casie’s promotion reflects not just her incredible track record, but the trust and respect she’s earned over the years.
