## Primis Bank Adds Talented Commercial Banker to Eastern Market


**Kate Craig joins Primis to strengthen relationships with area businesses.**


**RICHMOND, V.a. –** Primis Bank is thrilled to announce the hire of Kate Craig as vice president, commercial relationship manager serving the bank’s Eastern Virginia market.


Craig brings over 15 years of banking experience to the Primis team, with stops at BB&T (now Truist), Union Bank and Village Bank along the way. Most recently, she was a vice president on the commercial banking side at M&T Bank based out of Richmond, Virginia.


“Our team is very excited to have Kate on board,” Primis senior vice president Mark Zuskin said. “As the Primis brand grows nationally, it’s a main priority of ours to keep our roots and work together to form impactful relationships with local businesses. The experience she brings is vital, but it’s her passion to help others that is second to none.”


Craig’s roots grow deep in Eastern Virginia. As a longtime resident of James City County and an active member of the Kiwanis Club of Toano, she is passionate about helping local businesses thrive in her community.


“Being able to work with businesses in my community means a lot to me,” Craig explains. “It’s not hard to notice that the bigger banks tend to overlook community businesses. Primis feels like the best of both worlds, where we can offer products that rival the industry giants but still have that personal touch that community banks are loved for.”
