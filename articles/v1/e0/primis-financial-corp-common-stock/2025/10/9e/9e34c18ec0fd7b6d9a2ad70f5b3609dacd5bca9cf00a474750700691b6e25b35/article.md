# Primis Bank Promotes McLean Bean to Director of Customer Care and Talent Development - Primis Bank

### **Primis Bank Promotes McLean Bean to Director of Customer Care and Talent Development**


*Bean to bring proven strategies from Customer Care Center to company-wide employee development programs*


**RICHMOND, V.a. —** Primis Bank is happy to announce that McLean Bean has been promoted to director of customer care and talent development.


Bean will continue to lead the customer care team while expanding talent development programs across the organization. Since joining Primis just over two years ago, he has transformed the bank’s 24/7 Customer Care Center into a high-performing, talent-ready division and will now bring those best practices to the broader company.


“McLean has been an incredible leader for our Customer Care Center, and his promotion reflects the impact he’s made in a short time,” said Ann-Stanton Gore, chief marketing officer of Primis Bank. “We’re excited to see how his vision for talent development will strengthen our culture and position us for continued success.”


The talent development strategy will focus on two areas: job function development, which provides structured, function-specific training to elevate customer experiences, and soft skills development, which builds leadership, communication, and supervisory capabilities.


This promotion reflects Primis’ commitment to developing talent that drives excellence across the organization. Bean’s leadership in customer care and talent development will help ensure employees have the skills and support to deliver exceptional experiences for customers. We congratulate him on this well-deserved advancement and look forward to the impact he will make in his expanded role.
