## Primis Bank Brings in New Vice President on Commercial Side


*Anthony Ganguzza joins the Primis team as Vice President, Commercial Relationship Manager*


**McLEAN, V.a. —** Primis Bank is excited to share that Anthony Ganguzza was hired as Vice President, Commerical Relationship Manager. In this position he will help the bank draw in new business clientele and serve their current business customers in the DMV region


Prior to Primis, Ganguzza worked his way up the ladder on the commercial side at Burke & Herbert Bank, most recently serving as Vice President, Commercial Banking Executive. At Burke & Herbert, he managed relationships with clients in the Northern Virginia, Fredericksburg and Richmond markets.


“Having someone like Anthony on our team is crucial to managing our current commercial banking relationships and forming new ones,” Primis Bank President[Rick Fulk](https://primisbank.com/people/rick-fulk/) said. “He’s got the experience and brings the energy necessary to expand our reach in the DMV market.”


A graduate of Pennsylvania State University, Ganguzza has worked hard to get to where he currently stands. In his short time at Primis, he has been praised for his work ethic and drive to serve the bank’s business customers.


“It’s very exciting to join a team like the one I’m a part of here at Primis,” Ganguzza said. “Working closely with local business owners is something that I take a lot of pride in, and I cannot wait to expand our reach in the DMV.”


Outside of the office, Ganguzza serves on the board of the Southeast Fairfax Development Corporation (SFDC), a nonprofit that implements and supports projects designed to drive economic growth in the Richmond Highway corridor in Fairfax County, Virginia. He and the SFDC work closely with local business owners and residents to revitalize this prominent Northern Virginia community.
