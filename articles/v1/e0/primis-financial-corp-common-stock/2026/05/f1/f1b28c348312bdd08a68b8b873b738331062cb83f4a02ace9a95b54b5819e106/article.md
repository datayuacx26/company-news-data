# Primis Bank Welcomes Michelle Douglas as Director of SBA Lending and Executive Credit Officer - Primis Bank

## Primis Bank Welcomes Michelle Douglas as Director of SBA Lending and Executive Credit Officer


**McLEAN, Va. —** Primis Bank today announced the return of Michelle Douglas as Senior Vice President, Director of SBA Lending and Executive Credit Officer, reinforcing the bank’s commitment to building a best-in-class SBA platform as a core component of its long-term growth strategy.


Douglas brings 30 years of experience in SBA lending, credit administration, and banking operations. She previously spent 14 years with Primis from 2007 to 2021, where she built deep relationships across the organization and played an instrumental role in strengthening the bank’s SBA capabilities. Most recently, she served as Senior Vice President and SBA Program Manager at Potomac Bank.


Her return marks a significant milestone for Primis as the bank continues investing in leadership, infrastructure, and specialized expertise to accelerate growth within its[SBA division.](https://primisbank.com/business-loan/sba-loans/)


“Michelle’s decision to return to Primis speaks volumes about who we are becoming as an organization and the culture we’ve built,” said[Rick Fulk,](https://primisbank.com/people/rick-fulk/) President of Primis Bank. “I want to be direct about what this means for our bank. SBA lending at Primis is not just another product line — it is a flagship strategy central to the Core Bank’s strategic growth goals. This is something we intend to win in — not simply participate in. We are building a dedicated SBA Department Center with the leadership, resources, and organizational commitment to compete at the highest level. We’re thrilled to welcome Michelle back home to the Primis team.”


In her new role, Douglas will lead the strategic direction and continued expansion of Primis’ SBA lending platform, helping drive scalable growth while maintaining the strong credit discipline and customer-first approach that define the bank’s culture.


“Michelle is exactly the caliber of leader this initiative demands,” Fulk added. “She understands our business, our people, and our vision for the future. Her experience and leadership will be invaluable as we continue building a premier SBA banking platform.”


Primis continues to invest in talent and specialized banking capabilities designed to support entrepreneurs, business owners, and communities across its physical footprint in Virginia, Maryland, and Washington, D.C., while continuing to expand its reach and serve clients nationwide.
