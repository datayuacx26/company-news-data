# Primis Mortgage Welcomes Lee Williamson As Business Development Manager - Primis Bank

## Primis Mortgage Welcomes Lee Williamson As Business Development Manager


**Charlotte, N.C.** – Primis Mortgage is proud to announce the addition of Lee Williamson as its new business development manager. With nearly three decades of experience in banking, mortgage, and business development, he has built a career focused on scaling with intention and fostering long-term relationships


Before transitioning into the mortgage space, Williamson developed a foundation rooted in discipline and teamwork as a former NFL quarterback for the Houston Oilers. His team-first mindset continues to shape his business approach today, strongly valuing collaboration and accountability.


“Lee is the type of strategic thinker and relationship builder that makes an immediate impact,” said Chris Blevins, national sales director at Primis Mortgage. “His extensive experience across business development and talent-focused growth aligns perfectly with our vision.”


In his new role, Williamson will drive thoughtful expansion by supporting recruiting initiatives and strengthening industry relationships, while maintaining a focus on Primis Mortgage’s culture and values.


“Primis Mortgage continues to attract leaders who understand that growth is about putting people first,” said John Owens, CEO of Primis Mortgage. “Lee’s background, leadership style, and relationship-driven philosophy fit seamlessly with our passion for customer obsession. We’re thrilled to welcome him — he’ll play an important role in our team’s next chapter.”


For Williamson, joining Primis Mortgage meant aligning his experience with purpose.


“This company truly understands the value of teamwork and shared success,” said Williamson. “I’m excited to hit the ground running, accelerating growth and building meaningful relationships.”


Primis Mortgage looks forward to the positive impact Williamson will have as the company continues to expand its national footprint.
