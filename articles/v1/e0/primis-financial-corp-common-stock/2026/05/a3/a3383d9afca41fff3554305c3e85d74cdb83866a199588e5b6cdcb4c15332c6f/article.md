# Primis Bank Earns Eight 2026 Top Workplaces Awards - Primis Bank

## Primis Bank Earns Eight 2026 Top Workplaces Awards


**National Recognition Reflects the Bank’s Commitment to Building a High-Performing Culture Where Teammates Thrive**


**RICHMOND, Va. —** Primis Bank is proud to share that it has earned eight 2026 Top Workplaces awards, through a nationally recognized workplace excellence program based entirely on teammate feedback.


Primis received Top Workplaces recognition in the following categories:


*Culture Excellence Awards*


- Top Workplaces for Appreciation
- Top Workplaces for Compensation & Benefits
- Top Workplaces for Employee Well-Being
- Top Workplaces for Innovation
- Top Workplaces for Leadership
- Top Workplaces for Professional Development
- Top Workplaces for Work-Life Flexibility


*Industry Awards*


- Financial Services Top Workplaces


The results were driven by exceptionally strong teammate engagement, with 56% participation in the survey and extremely positive feedback across key areas.


“These recognitions mean a great deal because they came directly from our teammates,” said[Ann-Stanton Gore,](https://primisbank.com/people/annstantongore/) chief strategy officer at Primis. “As we continue to grow, we’ve been intentional about protecting the culture that makes Primis special. We believe people do their best work when they feel appreciated, supported, challenged and connected to a larger mission.”


At Primis, culture is deeply tied to strategy. The company has made deliberate investments in leadership development, teammate recognition, professional growth, flexibility and well-being to create an environment where people can thrive personally and professionally.


The recognition also reinforces Primis’ broader commitment to building a modern banking company that feels different from traditional financial institutions. One where leadership is accessible, teammates feel empowered and strong culture fuels strong performance.


“Reputation and culture are critical pillars of our strategy,” Gore added. “These awards validate the kind of company we’re working hard to build. One where great people want to stay, grow and make an impact.”


As Primis continues to expand nationally, the company remains focused on strengthening the foundation behind its momentum: its people.
