## Primis Warehouse Lending Strengthens Relationship Team with Addition of Industry Veteran Ron Wood


**FRISCO, Texas** — Primis Warehouse Lending today announced the addition of Ron Wood as vice president, senior relationship manager, further strengthening its rapidly growing warehouse lending platform.


Wood brings more than 45 years of diversified experience across mortgage banking, consumer lending, correspondent lending, title insurance and the broader financial services industry. A respected industry leader, he is known for his ability to lead through growth, manage complex operational and marketing challenges, and build strong, customer-focused relationships across organizations.


“With the momentum our Warehouse Lending team continues to experience, adding talent like Ron is critical,” said Drey Roberts, president of Warehouse Lending at Primis. “His depth of experience, strategic thinking and relationship-first approach fit exactly with how we operate. We’re extremely excited to welcome him to the team and believe his leadership will be invaluable as we continue to scale.”


Most recently, Wood served as vice president, senior relationship manager at Flagstar Bank, where he spent 15 years as a leader within the relationship management department. During his tenure, he played a pivotal role in redesigning and re-launching Flagstar’s warehouse lending platform, converting the business from a captive model to a traditional warehouse offering. That transformation helped drive growth from approximately $300 million to more than $13 billion in commitments.


Throughout his career, Wood has demonstrated a strong ability to balance operational excellence with responsive, relationship-driven service. His analytical, conceptual and interpersonal skills, paired with a collaborative leadership style, have consistently helped teams deliver results while meeting the evolving needs of clients and the organization.


Wood joins Primis at a time of significant growth for the Warehouse Lending team, which continues to expand its national footprint and invest in experienced talent to support independent mortgage bankers across the country.
