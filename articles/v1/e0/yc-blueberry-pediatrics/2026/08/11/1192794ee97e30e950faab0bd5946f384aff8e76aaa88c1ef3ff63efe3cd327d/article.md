## Who Is Eligible?


GLP-1 medications are FDA-approved for use in adolescents ages 12 and older. However, eligibility is highly individual — your teen's pediatrician will evaluate their specific health profile, medical history, and needs to determine whether GLP-1 treatment is appropriate for them.


Certain conditions may prevent your teen from using GLP-1 medications, including a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN2). GLP-1 medications also carry FDA warnings for acute pancreatitis, acute gallbladder disease, hypoglycemia (particularly when combined with insulin secretagogues), and suicidal ideation or behavior. Your teen's pediatrician will review all eligibility criteria and safety considerations during the initial consultation, and will monitor for these risks throughout treatment.


## What to Expect: Common Side Effects


GLP-1 medications are generally well-tolerated, but gastrointestinal side effects are common, particularly during dose escalation. In the STEP TEENS clinical trial, approximately 60% of adolescents taking semaglutide reported GI side effects, compared with about 36% on placebo. The most common side effects include:


- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Abdominal pain
- Constipation


These side effects are typically mild to moderate and often improve as your teen's body adjusts to the medication. Your Blueberry Balance care team actively monitors for side effects at every check-in and adjusts the treatment plan as needed to keep your teen comfortable.
