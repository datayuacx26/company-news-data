# Northrop Grumman Announces Date for First Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Webcast

## Northrop Grumman Announces Date for First Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Webcast


March 19, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.northropgrumman.com/node/43781/pdf)


FALLS CHURCH, Va., March 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Northrop Grumman Corporation (NYSE: NOC) announced today that its first quarter 2026 financial results will be released on Tuesday, April 21, 2026, prior to the market opening. The earnings announcement, and accompanying earnings presentation, will be available on the company’s website at[http://investor.northropgrumman.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=mI5zVs1XGdjw_mkDtCu8aL8TXI9CU5Hdr9iKQ97OoDVlCr348yjXEkgFmet7b2JGTyWgmQni-tI31hTOoPZYTe0WCRirYfM8tSNOxcUFSPuQ22wv9bmEAI3Xtc9AsKtmjaNW2WrK_E1wCFj7tTtXyA==) .


**Earnings Call Webcast**


The company will host a live, audio only, earnings call webcast at 9:30 a.m. ET the same day. This webcast can be accessed on the company’s website at[http://investor.northropgrumman.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=mI5zVs1XGdjw_mkDtCu8aL8TXI9CU5Hdr9iKQ97OoDUzHWXdcquXT1ks6LjgY87WXjNg1ba0YoHqdTZE2081kBwsBGuFy8xoWlLUrX4ibYZ3jVp0E_zBM0eaOq4VWr24abI_bWt3-BV1DBZkpG-Vhg==) . A replay of the webcast will be available shortly after the call and will remain available for a limited time.
