## What is GovEagle?


GovEagle is an AI-powered tool built for government contractors, covering RFP shredding, compliance matrix generation, and proposal narrative drafting. It helps teams build compliance matrices, generate annotated outlines, and surface relevant past performance and incumbent data to support bid decisions. The tool works natively inside Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and SharePoint, which keeps it accessible for teams already running proposals through that stack.


Where GovEagle draws a clear boundary is scope. It is purpose-built for proposal work and does not extend into capture management, contract administration, pricing, or BD pipeline tracking. For contractors who want a focused writing aid that plugs into their existing Microsoft environment without requiring a broader workflow change, that narrow focus can be a reasonable fit.


The practical trade-off is that teams using GovEagle still need separate systems to manage opportunities upstream and contracts downstream, which means data does not carry across the full pursuit lifecycle in one place.


## What is GovDash?


GovDash is the AI platform for winning government contracts, connecting opportunity discovery, capture, pricing, proposal development, and post-award contract management in one system. Dash, the AI agent, and the Data Library run across every module, so the intelligence your team builds during capture carries forward into proposals and beyond.


Here is what that looks like in practice across the core workflow areas:


- Opportunity discovery pulls relevant solicitations automatically, so BD teams spend less time scanning SAM.gov and more time assessing fit against their win criteria.


- Capture management ties pursuit data directly to proposal inputs, so nothing gets lost between the pipeline and the writing phase.
