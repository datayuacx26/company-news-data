#### Where the Money's Moving


1. [Get the numbers in Capturing FY27:](https://www.govdash.com/industry-reports/capturing-fy27) The $350B defense reconciliation ask just got cut to $60B in committee, MDA SHIELD has zero incumbents on a $185B ceiling, and three eligibility gates now decide who gets scored on an FY27 bid.


2. [180 Days to Map Your Supply Chain or Start Losing Contracts:](https://tots.nerdrums.com/p/the-white-house-wants-your-supply-chain-mapped) Map your sub-tier suppliers now. DOD's new executive order gives every prime and sub 180 days to prove domestic sourcing, and contractors who can't show progress lose contracts once the guidance drops.


3. [NDAA FY27 Stuck at $1.15 Trillion, No Floor Vote in Either Chamber:](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/07/house-passes-2027-ndaa-while-measure-remains-stalled-in-the-senate/) Don't bank FY27 program starts on a fall conference date. Build the slip into your pipeline forecast now, because the Senate failed 50-46 to even open debate.


4. **[GSA Is Widening OneGov Past Software and Cloud](https://www.gsa.gov/about-gsa/newsroom/news-releases/gsa-announces-coras-partnership-through-onegov-07282026) :** Check which of your product lines could qualify for a OneGov agreement before GSA locks the next wave of categories. Deputy Administrator Michael Lynch is using Year One's results to justify the expansion right now.


5. [Federal CIO Barbaccia Walks Out August 31, Leaves the Hard Part Behind:](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gjbnyc/) Watch who fills this seat. Barbaccia is naming the Paperwork Reduction Act and Privacy Act as the statutes blocking AI-era modernization, and his replacement inherits both that fight and the OMB-agency model he built.


6. [TMF's Money Disappears September 30:](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=(title:40%20section:11301%20edition:prelim).) Get your short-sprint, outcome-tied proposal now if your customer has a fundable use case. TMF's acting director already said projects with no measurable outcome are not worth her time.
