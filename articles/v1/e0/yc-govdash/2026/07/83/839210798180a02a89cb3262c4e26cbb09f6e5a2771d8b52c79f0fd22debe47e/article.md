# Introducing @Dash: Now in Slack and Teams

*Connect GovDash to your Slack or Teams workspace, and anyone in a channel can tag @Dash and get a direct answer from your actual pipeline, your proposals, your contracts, your past performance. The answer comes back in the thread, visible to everyone in the channel, without anyone leaving the conversation.*


### ***How it works***


*Tag @Dash in any channel with a plain-language request:*


*@Dash What proposals are due in the next two weeks?*


*@Dash Update the AFWERX opportunity: met with the KO at WEST, she confirmed a Q4 award date.*


*@Dash Log that the DHS contracting officer confirmed the award is delayed 60 days as a note.*


*@Dash Which key personnel should I recommend for a machine intelligence solicitation? @Dash What is the status on Volume 2 of the DHS bid?*


Tag @Dash in a channel, and the answer is visible to everyone in it. When a BD lead asks about an opportunity, the capture manager and the proposal lead are already in the loop. No separate sync or thread needed.


DM @Dash directly and it responds privately, using your individual GovDash access. Use it to check something before you bring it to the team, or look up data that isn't relevant to the broader channel.


GovCon teams used to track pipelines across spreadsheets, shared drives, and email chains. Past performance lived in a folder. Capture notes were in a notebook or document. The data existed - getting to it meant knowing where to look and who to ask.


GovDash put all of it in one place. @Dash makes it reachable without leaving the conversation.


***Built for how GovCon organizations run***


Every channel's @Dash is scoped to the GovDash permissions set for it.


Workspace administrators connect GovDash to Slack or Teams, specify which channels Dash is active in, and set monthly token budgets at the organization and channel level. Every request and every response is logged and reviewable.


Once a channel is configured, anyone in it can tag @Dash immediately.


### ***Getting started***


*@Dash in Slack and @Dash in Teams are available today for all customers.* Go to[GovDash Settings > Integrations](https://support.govdash.com/docs/slack-integration-setup) to connect your Slack or Teams workspace as an Admin. Test @Dash in a private channel first, then open it to the team.


*Dash is also available in Slack, GovSlack, Teams, a Browser Extension, Excel, and PowerPoint. Every module sits on a single data layer, so context compounds across all the tools your team uses.*
