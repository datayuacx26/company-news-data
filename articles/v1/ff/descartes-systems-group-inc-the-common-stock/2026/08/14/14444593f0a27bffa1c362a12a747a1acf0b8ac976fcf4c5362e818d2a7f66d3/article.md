# Back-to-School Price Competition and Social Trends Put Pressure on Ecommerce Sellers

Back-to-school shopping in 2026 is being shaped by aggressive pricing and product trends emerging on social media, creating a more competitive environment for ecommerce sellers.


Major retailers including Walmart, Target, and Staples are emphasizing affordability as consumers become increasingly value-conscious. Walmart[says](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/07/15/walmart-helps-americans-navigate-their-annual-back-to-school-reset-with-unmatched-value-convenience-and-style) it is offering its lowest prices since 2019 on the 14 most popular school supplies found on classroom lists nationwide, while Target[says](https://corporate.target.com/press/release/2026/07/target-helps-students-head-back-to-school-and-college-with-style-and-savings) nearly all its school supplies are priced at or below last year’s levels.


Social media is also influencing which products shoppers consider. Retailers are partnering with creators and promoting trending merchandise as consumers increasingly research purchases on platforms such as TikTok. *Modern Retail*[reports](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/big-box-briefing-this-back-to-school-season-is-all-about-low-prices-and-social-trends/) that shoppers are also using AI tools during the research process.


For ecommerce sellers, these trends could increase pressure on pricing, inventory planning, and product visibility. Back-to-school shopping has also become more widespread, with retailers introducing promotions and new products throughout the season rather than relying on one major sales period.


Ecommerce sellers can respond by monitoring demand closely, adjusting inventory forecasts as trends develop, and keeping product information accurate across sales channels. Maintaining visibility into inventory and orders can also help sellers respond to changing demand without creating unnecessary stock.
