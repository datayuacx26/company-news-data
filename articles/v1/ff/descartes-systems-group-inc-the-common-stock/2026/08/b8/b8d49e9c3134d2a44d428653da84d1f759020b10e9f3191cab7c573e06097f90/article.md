# Reuters: July US container imports hit fourth-highest on record, Descartes says

Reuters highlights the latest Descartes July Global Shipping Report, which found that U.S. container imports reached their fourth-highest monthly total on record despite ongoing geopolitical and tariff uncertainty. The data points to continued resilience in global trade while underscoring the importance of visibility and analytics to help businesses navigate shifting trade policies and changing import patterns.
