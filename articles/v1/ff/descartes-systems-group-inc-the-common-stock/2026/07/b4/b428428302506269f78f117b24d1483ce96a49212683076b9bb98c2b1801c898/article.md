# IT Toolkit: Delivering the Route to a More Efficient Fleet

Inbound Logistics shares how Richards Building Supply replaced paper-based delivery processes with Descartes’ route planning and mobile proof-of-delivery solutions. Across more than 60 locations and a fleet of several hundred vehicles, the company is gaining greater visibility, creating safer and more efficient routes, and providing customers with digital signatures and photos that confirm when and where materials were delivered.
