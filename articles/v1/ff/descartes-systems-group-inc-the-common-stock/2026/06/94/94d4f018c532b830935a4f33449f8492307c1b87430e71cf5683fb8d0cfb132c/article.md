# NPTC National Safety Conference

NPTC National Safety Conference


Join us at the **NPTC National Safety Conference** , a leading educational event bringing together private fleet professionals committed to safety, compliance, and performance.


Designed specifically for private fleet operators, safety leaders, and transportation professionals, this conference delivers expert-led presentations, interactive workshops, and peer networking opportunities centered on today's most pressing fleet safety challenges. Attendees will gain practical, solution-focused strategies they can implement within their own operations to enhance driver safety, reduce risk, and strengthen compliance.


Discover how Descartes’ fleet solutions help private fleets improve driver performance, increase operational visibility, and build a stronger culture of safety across their organizations.


Visit us at **Booth 206** to connect with our team, explore innovative approaches to fleet safety, and learn how Descartes can help your organization reduce risk, improve compliance, and drive safer, more efficient operations.
