What are the best strategies for gaining a competitive edge in international trade while managing duties and tariffs efficiently?


For globally active businesses, this is no longer a theoretical question, it’s a critical operational challenge. With increased cost pressures, complex trade regulations, and shifting global markets, companies need to reduce duties and tariffs without compromising compliance.


The answer lies in data-driven automation, accurate and up-to-date tariff data, and leveraging Free Trade Agreements (FTAs), all areas where Descartes delivers proven results. Rather than relying on manual processes or reactive decision-making, companies can implement smart digital strategies that optimise trade flows, minimise costs, and stay ahead of regulatory requirements.


In this blog, we explore how businesses can turn duty management into a competitive advantage by using FTAs, automation, and smart trade practices to reduce costs and optimise international operations.


## The Problem: Complexity and missed opportunities in global trade


Even when businesses qualify for preferential treatment through trade agreements, they often still overpay on duties. Why? Common barriers include:


- Lack of visibility into applicable FTAs
- Inefficient documentation and compliance tracking
- Uncertainty around rules of origin and recordkeeping
- Manual and product classification errors that disqualify goods from preferential treatment


Without a centralised, automated solution to manage duties, compliance, and agreement eligibility, companies not only lose out on cost savings, but they also expose themselves to compliance risks and operational inefficiencies.

**Benefit** : Stay compliant while reducing the administrative burden on internal teams.
