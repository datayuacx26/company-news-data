# AUECO 2020

AUECO 2020


The University of Pennsylvania invites you to participate in the “AUECO” 2020 Conference on the Impact of Export Controls on Higher Education and Scientific Institutions. The Conference will be held on April 27-29, 2020 at the Sheraton Philadelphia University City Hotel on the campus of the University of Pennsylvania (3549 Chestnut St., Philadelphia, PA 19104). Speakers will include university export compliance officers, federal policy and enforcement personnel, legal practitioners, and other experts. Please plan to join us in Philadelphia to learn, engage, and collaborate.
