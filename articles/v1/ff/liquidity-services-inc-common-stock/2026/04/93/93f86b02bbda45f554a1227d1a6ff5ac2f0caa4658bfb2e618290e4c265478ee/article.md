# Life on Wheels: Motorhomes & Travel Trailers Find New Owners Online

If it feels like everyone is itching to hit the open road lately, you’re not imagining it. From full‑time RV living to weekend campground escapes, motorhomes and travel trailers continue to draw strong attention on the GovDeals marketplace, and sellers across the country are seeing real success moving these high‑demand assets.


Over the past year, buyers have shown up in force for surplus RVs of all shapes and sizes, motorhomes, compact travel trailers, and everything in between. Whether these assets come from military bases, state surplus programs, or special projects, one thing is clear: there’s an engaged audience ready to purchase.


##### **Proof Is in the Listings**


Recent sold motorhome listings on GovDeals show that buyers are actively bidding on a wide range of units from newer, road‑ready motorhomes to older models with plenty of life left in them.


One standout sale shows what’s possible with the right approach. A large lot of **[105 travel trailers](https://www.govdeals.com/en/asset/34605/782)** , made up of different makes, models, and sizes, sold for $693,000. The result highlights that even large, complex RV sales can perform well with national exposure. For sellers, this means the ability to move high volumes efficiently reaching qualified buyers, driving competitive bidding, and maximizing returns without managing multiple individual sales. Strong buyer demand continues to help government sellers turn surplus RVs into real, measurable returns.


Another notable example was this vintage[1965 GMC Custom Motorhome](https://www.govdeals.com/en/asset/1/28614) sold in Alabama. The restored motorhome was fully loaded and generated strong bidder interest and several bids before selling for $66,000. This sale underscores the continued demand for unique, well‑kept motorhomes and the strong value buyers place on quality assets offered through trusted sellers. Listings like this highlight how well‑maintained motorhomes continue to attract competitive bids and bring strong returns.


The sale of this[2025 Catalina Coachman Travel Trailer](https://www.govdeals.com/en/asset/49504/357) further highlights how sellers can tap into the power of online auctions to reach motivated buyers. With national exposure, the listing attracted strong interest and ultimately sold for more than $15,000, proving that even when operational and mechanical conditions are unknown, the travel trailers can still generate solid returns when marketed to a competitive audience.


> In the last three years, Motorhomes and Travel Trailers sold on GovDeals have driven nearly $4 million in revenue. This highlights the strong demand and resale value of these assets.


##### **Why Buyers Keep Bidding**


So what’s driving all this activity? A few key factors consistently show up in motorhome and travel trailer sales:


Lifestyle Appeal


Remote work, road trips, and outdoor recreation continue to influence buying behavior. RVs and trailers offer flexibility and buyers are actively hunting for deals that make that lifestyle more accessible.


Nationwide Buyer Reach


GovDeals connects sellers with bidders across the country, not just locally. That wider audience increases visibility and competitive bidding, especially for niche assets like campers and motorhomes.


Variety Brings Value


From compact tow‑behind trailers to full‑featured motorhomes, variety matters. Listings from public agencies offer buyers options they may not find through traditional retail channels.

If your agency or organization has motorhomes, campers, or travel trailers sitting idle, now is a great time to consider bringing them to sale on GovDeals. Buyer demand in this category is strong,


and surplus RV assets are consistently attracting competitive interest from a global pool of buyers.

With buyers already watching this category closely, Motorhomes and RVs have a strong opportunity to gain quick visibility and drive maximum return.
