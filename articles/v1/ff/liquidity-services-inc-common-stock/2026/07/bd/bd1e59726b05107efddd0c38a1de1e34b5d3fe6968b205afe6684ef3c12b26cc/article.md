# City of Fort Smith, Arkansas Surpasses $800,000 in Online Surplus Sales on GovDeals


### Milestone highlights key benefits of online auctions like increased accessibility, competitive bidding, and revenue generation


FORT SMITH, Ark., July 14, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- The City of Fort Smith, Arkansas has achieved a significant milestone of more than $800,000 in surplus auction proceeds through[GovDeals](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=YsidaTVfk-JvIn651J3nuhdIxgsrHy0MCQudHkQgQKyVd_GDv0FQbwkxQ2uPZb_vwzwzNCsNDALi_EB46SYLMdl5wbuJ9fY9pTR2sar2krCzmhfc4ZqZ8Y-tTnZbDAHB6bSpk4AAlJ3tLbuVXClKudgaYNwqeWJhLblCJcqkASCrSYH0DnGOnSgRlLdDDm_TI3F13-Dg1bGRQ6JiuVsUr4EjqMqxCbETRo1ZQXDluPo=) , the leading online marketplace for government agencies and educational institutions to sell surplus assets.


Since first transitioning its surplus sales program online in 2019, Fort Smith has successfully sold more than 150 surplus items through the GovDeals platform. By leveraging online auctions, the city has expanded access to a broader pool of bidders, creating a more competitive environment that helps maximize returns on surplus property.


“Moving our surplus auctions online has been a smart decision for the City of Fort Smith,” said Jeff Dingman, Acting City Administrator. “GovDeals has allowed us to reach more bidders than ever before, making the process more open and accessible while helping us generate additional revenue that supports essential city services. This milestone reflects the success of that approach and our commitment to responsible asset management.”


Fort Smith has utilized GovDeals to sell a wide variety of surplus assets, ensuring that unused equipment and materials are efficiently repurposed while delivering strong financial returns for the community. The online format provides transparency throughout the auction process and gives buyers convenient, around-the-clock access to participate in sales from anywhere.
