## Everywhere Care Happens


We are everywhere healthcare happens—and everywhere it is going, with essential solutions in the hospital, physician’s office and other sites of care. Every day, millions of patients and healthcare providers rely on our unmatched portfolio of connected solutions, medical devices and advanced injectable technologies to help deliver smarter, more personalized care. Together, we are redefining how care is delivered to make a greater impact today, tomorrow and beyond.
