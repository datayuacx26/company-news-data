### Baxter to Host First-Quarter 2026 Financial Results Conference Call for Investors


April 8, 2026


DEERFIELD, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Baxter International Inc. (NYSE:BAX), a leading global medtech company, will host a conference call to discuss its first-quarter 2026 financial results on Thursday, April 30, 2026 at 7:30 a.m. Central Time.
