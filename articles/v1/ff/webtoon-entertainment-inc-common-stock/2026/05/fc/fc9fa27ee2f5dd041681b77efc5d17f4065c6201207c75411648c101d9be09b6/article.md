[WEBTOON Entertainment](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=2RnxYX135QnYbsnb2qPtx3UE60i1VobkvPQrIcBh5sbZqnrTgcKqAlEGyTXjYG9luPw5jcgIUtjAyx96aY-Gk8si4vBxiKVs1J-I8oKLges=) is a leading global entertainment company and home to some of the world's largest storytelling platforms.
