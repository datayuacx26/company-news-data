# Wyvern and Nara Space partner to expand access to hyperspectral data across Asia

Through this collaboration,[Nara Space](https://naraspace.com/) will distribute Wyvern’s cutting-edge data products across Asia, enabling new applications in environmental monitoring, agriculture, defence, and beyond.


With four satellites already in orbit and more on the way, Wyvern’s Dragonette constellation delivers the highest-resolution commercial hyperspectral imagery available today. By combining Wyvern’s advanced imaging capabilities with Nara Space’s expertise in space systems and data solutions, the partnership will make it easier for organizations in South Korea and throughout the region to leverage hyperspectral insights for critical decision-making.


Hyperspectral view of the mountains east of Jogyesan Provincial Park in South Korea, shown in CIR (left) and RGB (right) *. Image taken with Dragonette-001 on 2025-08-24 00:51:52 UTC.*


“We’re thrilled to be working with Nara Space to broaden the reach of our hyperspectral data,” said Chris Robson, CEO of Wyvern. “This partnership will open the door to powerful new use cases for Earth observation.”


“Wyvern’s hyperspectral capabilities bring a unique and much-needed dimension to the Earth observation ecosystem,” said Minsik Kim, Co-founder & VP Business Development at Nara Space. “By combining their advanced data products with our technology and regional expertise, we can deliver powerful new insights to customers across Asia. We are excited to bring Wyvern’s data to our network and explore new opportunities together.”

##### About Wyvern


Wyvern, an Edmonton-based space data company, is revolutionizing Earth observation with cutting-edge hyperspectral imagery. Their Dragonette constellation delivers the highest-resolution commercial hyperspectral data from space, powering mission-critical applications in defence, wildfire prevention, environmental monitoring, and resource management. By revealing the unseen chemical and material signatures of our planet, Wyvern enables decision-makers to act faster, smarter, and more sustainably: creating a Better Earth, From Space.


##### About Nara Space


Founded in 2015, Nara Space Technology is a leading South Korean space company that provides end-to-end satellite solutions — from small satellite design and manufacturing to mission operations and cutting-edge AI data analytics. The company has demonstrated its technological leadership by successfully developing and operating Observer-1A, the nation’s first commercial 16U optical satellite, achieving TRL 9. With vertically integrated capabilities spanning the entire satellite value chain, Nara Space delivers high-frequency Earth observation imagery and advanced analytics to government agencies, large enterprises, and international partners. As the company expands into constellation operations and commercial downstream services, it aims to accelerate the growth of the global space ecosystem and drive the future of satellite-based services.
