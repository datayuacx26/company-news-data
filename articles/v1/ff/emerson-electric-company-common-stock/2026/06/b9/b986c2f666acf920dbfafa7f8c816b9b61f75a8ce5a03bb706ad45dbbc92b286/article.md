# Contributing to the Design of Energy‑Efficient, Robust Pyrolysis Plants at Scale Through Advanced Instrumentation

As advanced recycling moves from pilot projects to industrial scale deployment across Europe, pyrolysis OEMs face a common challenge such as


**scaling quickly while maintaining energy efficiency, robust design, and reliable long-term operation** . Achieving this requires more than individual instruments: it requires a partner who understands the realities of high temperature, variable feedstock processes and works alongside OEM teams from design through deployment.

Emerson takes a


**partner first approach** to advanced recycling. By combining the world’s broadest instrumentation portfolio with standardized, OEM ready solutions and full lifecycle support, we help OEMs develop systems that are simpler to engineer, easier to scale, and more predictable to operate.


Energy efficiency is driven by stable, accurate measurement. Emerson technologies enable closer control of critical parameters without adding mechanical complexity. High temperature pressure measurement solutions allow direct installation without impulse lines or heat tracing, reducing heat loss and failure points. Advanced temperature measurement eliminates many traditional thermowell challenges, simplifying reactor design while supporting operation at very high process temperatures.


Reliable mass and energy flow measurement supports consistent feedstock control, even with variable waste streams, helping OEMs deliver stable conversion efficiency and repeatable performance. Standardized radar level measurement for liquids and solids improves availability and simplifies plant design, while real time gas analysis and liquid chemistry monitoring support ongoing optimization and regulatory compliance.


For pyrolysis OEMs, the result is not just reliable instrumentation, but


**a measurement strategy designed for scale** . By focusing together on energy efficiency, simplicity, and robustness, Emerson helps OEM partners turn innovative recycling technologies into dependable, commercially viable solutions.
