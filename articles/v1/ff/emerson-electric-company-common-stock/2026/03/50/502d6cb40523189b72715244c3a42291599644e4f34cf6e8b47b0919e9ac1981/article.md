# Emerson to Present at the 2026 J.P. Morgan Industrials Conference

ST. LOUIS


, March 10, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Emerson (NYSE: EMR) today announced Chief Operating Officer, Ram Krishnan, and Chief Financial Officer, Mike Baughman, will present at the 2026 J.P. Morgan Industrials Conference on Tuesday, March 17th in Washington, D.C. The presentation will begin at 11:30 a.m. Eastern Time, 10:30 a.m. Central Time.


The audio will be webcast and archived on Emerson's website at[https://ir.emerson.com/](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4636650-1&h=890014010&u=https%3A%2F%2Fir.emerson.com%2F&a=https%3A%2F%2Fir.emerson.com%2F) .
