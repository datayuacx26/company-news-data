# Yasmeen Yehia: Defeating My Own Imposter

# Yasmeen Yehia: Defeating My Own Imposter

My name is Yasmeen Yehia and I am an Egyptian Computer Science student. I am writing this from Glasgow, Scotland where I’ve been for the past 4 months as an exchange student. I’m not going to tell you about how I got here, but I want to let you know that in a few months I’m moving permanently to Germany to work for Freiheit in Hamburg.


Exciting, right?!


Press enter or click to view image in full size


It started all the way back when I was in the 10th grade. That’s when my passion for coding started. It simply came natural to me, and my teachers immediately saw that. They signed me up to national competitions, and even encouraged me to register with the top STEM school in Egypt.


However, I was living in a paradox: the more I learned about this world, the more I felt that I really knew nothing. For me, my dream was to work in Google, but that dream would come way later in my life and career. Where I was at that time, I wasn’t on route to Google. I wasn’t on route anywhere really!


Press enter or click to view image in full size


And this was when I came across the YouTuber Eman Sobhy’s series “What If?”, and her video about Manara. She (along with the several stories from Manara alumni on Medium) held my hand tight just when I was giving up hope on that dream and took me back to my intended path.


Manara made me realize that my dream was attainable, so I applied. After getting accepted, I got to work! I drilled down on the coding tests and assignments, and the mock interview preparations. I discovered a whole new world of exceptional mentors and a supportive community who were always there for me.


But there was always a doubt in me. Always that person in me kicking me down. But who picked me up? Iliana Montauk, Manara’s co-founder and CEO. She made me expand my dreams beyond the confines of my country. She, along with the entire community of staff, mentors, teachers, and peers, made me realize that there is a whole world out there within my reach. A world where someone like me can leave her country and work in any tech company she wanted!


Press enter or click to view image in full size


And it was around this time that Freiheit’s name came up through an online conference Manara organized with them for their students. I admit, I didn’t know much about them when I started interviewing, but I immediately fell in love with their office culture. How did I know? During my interviews I would sneak a peek behind the interviewer to look at their office and the people working there! Looked like a lot of fun!


With Manara, I found the real Yasmeen. The one who was hiding behind this imposter kicking me down. And I love her!
