Lana Theodory from Jerusalem


My name is Lana Theodory and I am a Palestinian resident of Jerusalem. I work remotely as Frontend Engineer with Upwork, the world’s work marketplace based in California. I want to share my journey with Manara, hoping that it would inspire someone out there to dare to take a chance and pursue their dreams.


That dream was something that came out of the blue. I graduated from Birzeit University with a Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting, but as soon as I was handed that degree, I made up my mind and decided not to pursue a career in that field. I want to work in IT.


It was a step that needed courage.


Thankfully I had all the support I needed from my family. “There is no harm in trying; you can always fall back to accounting,” they said. And with that, I started taking courses and signed up to coding bootcamps and learn everything I can get my hands on.


I was learning a lot, but I wanted more. I wanted to apply everything I was learning to something practical, so I started looking for a job.

It was around this time that Manara caught my attention. They were collaborating with the popular influencer Alaa Hamdan on a campaign, and I realized that this was my opportunity. But I could not get myself to do it. I did not have the confidence! I kept telling myself, “The people who are signing up to the Manara program all have degrees in this field. How am I supposed to compete with them?!”


Added to that, as a Palestinian woman living in the Arab world, I always felt that there were restrictions placed on us. We were always told that “you cannot be this and cannot be that.” And when all is said and done, we have very little to choose from.


So I took the lead of the inspiring women who had already graduated from the Manara program and went ahead. I had already taken that first step in changing careers from accounting to IT, so what’s another step?


And this is where Manara gave me the assistance and guidance.


Press enter or click to view image in full size


Manara built a community that was both extremely professional and friendly. Initially I had a lot of difficulty getting used to the workload and the amount of new information I was expected to learn and process, but with such a positive atmosphere around me, my peers and mentors guided me to finish the program.


The opportunity to work full-time at Upwork presented itself around this time, and I approached it with a positive attitude, knowing full well that I got the proper training. Of course I had dreamed of working abroad, but working remotely for a company such as Upwork outweighed that desire to move. Having a flexible schedule and being among family and friends was the winning factor.


One day I will realize that dream, but for now, I am working from home with the satisfaction of knowing that I overcame my fears to achieve something I myself did not think possible.
