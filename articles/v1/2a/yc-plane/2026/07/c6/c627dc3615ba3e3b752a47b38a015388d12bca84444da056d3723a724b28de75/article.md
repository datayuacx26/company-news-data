# Salary research and calculations done for you

You shouldn’t need a PhD in math to crunch salary numbers.


Claude and ChatGPT can do it for you, through Plane’s MCP server. Ask a plain language question like, “What does an 8% salary increase look like for this employee, and when should it take effect?” and get an instant answer with:


-


New salary details


-


Compensation ratio against the targeted pay band


-


Suggested effective date pulled from start date anniversary and regular pay cycles


The numbers come directly from your live records, removing any guesswork and eliminating spreadsheets from the equation.


Dig deeper to compare the proposed salary with market data. Create a pros and cons list for different compensation strategies.


With Plane, “done by hand” means typing questions and letting Plane and your preferred AI agent handle the rest. If you prefer to use Plane Agent, the AI teammate from Plane that lives in your Slack, that's another option available to you. Here are step-by-step walkthroughs of each option.
