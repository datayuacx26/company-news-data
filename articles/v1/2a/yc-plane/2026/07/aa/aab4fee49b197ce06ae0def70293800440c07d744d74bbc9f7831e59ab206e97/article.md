# Features catalog: See everything Plane offers and customize your team's workspace

Your team has enough to keep track of already. As new Plane features launch, it can be easy to miss what’s new, what’s available, and what’s relevant to the way your team works.


We wanted to create a simpler way for you to stay informed about new functionality and adopt it on your own terms — when it makes sense for your workflow.


That’s why we built the Features catalog: a central place to explore everything available in Plane, get access to beta features like Plane Agent and Workforce Planning as soon as they’re ready, and hide features that aren’t relevant to your team.


## Easily turn on beta features


The Features catalog is where our newest features land first. Before something becomes generally available, you can opt in and start using it while it's still in beta; no waiting for a wider rollout. Recent additions you can enable today include:


-


**Plane Agent** : Your AI-powered teammate that does your back-office work for you, right in Slack.


-


**Offers** : Craft, send, and track offer letters directly in Plane, so you don’t have to jump to another platform.


-


**Workforce Planning:** Plan ahead by modeling future headcount and seeing the fully-loaded employer cost, including salary, payroll taxes, and statutory payments.


Enabling a beta feature does two things at once. You get hands-on with the newest part of Plane, and you and your team get to help shape where it goes next. Our product team is always eager to hear your feedback, especially when it comes to beta features. Just reach out to ourteam ; your feedback directly influences where these features go.


## More control over your Plane workspace


Not every team needs every feature. If your team doesn't need Time tracking, for example, you can switch it off so it doesn’t clutter your navigation. The Features catalog gives you a clear inventory of what's available and lets you turn off what you don’t need, so your workspace reflects exactly what you use.
