# AI can help spot attrition risk before your best people leave

42% of employees who left their job said their manager or company could have done something to make them stay,[according to Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/646538/employee-turnover-preventable-often-ignored.aspx) .


But to save your best people, you first have to know they’re at-risk.


Most leaders don’t have time to comb through rows of HR data and stacks of spreadsheets looking for risk signals. The good news: AI can do it in minutes, surfacing a list of at-risk employees you can target for career conversations and salary reviews.


Run an analysis of your existing HR data to spot attrition weak spots. Here’s how to use an AI app like Claude or ChatGPT to do it.


*Disclaimer: This use case surfaces employee record signals (e.g., tenure, pay history, and role history) that may indicate someone is about to leave. It isn’t a performance evaluation or a prediction, and it isn’t legal or HR advice.*
