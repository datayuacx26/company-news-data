# Ask for a PTO dashboard, AI builds it

A PTO dashboard should help you quickly visualize real-time data and make decisions. But most of the time, it means spending half a day in Excel, building an already outdated pivot table.


With Plane, your live data is pulled instantly into custom visual dashboards. You prompt. AI builds. You get on with your day.


Auditing your current PTO policy? See who’s taking too little or a lot of PTO.


Staff planning for peak season? Get a snapshot of who can cover additional projects.


Worried about employee burnout? Identify which individuals or teams could use more time off.


Create custom PTO dashboards for the data you want to see now. Here’s how:

Your AI app can help if you don’t have a clear picture in mind for what you want your dashboard to look like. Simply ask for options.


Start by requesting a few different layouts to pick the format that shows the data you want to see. Note that how you word your prompt will depend on how you word your leave policies and what specifically you’re looking for (e.g., “paid time off” vs. “vacation”).
