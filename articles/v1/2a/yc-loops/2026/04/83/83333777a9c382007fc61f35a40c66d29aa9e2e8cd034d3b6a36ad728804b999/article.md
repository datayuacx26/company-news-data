# Zoom in the loop builder

You can now zoom in the loop builder, with navigation improvements designed for complex canvases. This release includes viewport zoom and pan updates, touch gestures and Cmd/Ctrl + wheel zoom. These updates make it easier to move through large loop diagrams without losing your place.


In practice, you can now pan across a dense canvas, zoom into a specific area to adjust a section of your automation, then reset and continue editing the full flow. If you are building larger journeys with multiple branches, this makes navigation more predictable and faster while you work.


Learn more in the[Loop Builder docs](https://loops.so/docs/workflows) .
