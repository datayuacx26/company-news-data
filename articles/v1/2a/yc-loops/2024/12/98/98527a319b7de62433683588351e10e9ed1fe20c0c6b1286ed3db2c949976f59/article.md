# Account switcher

Chris from Loops here with a few new features to wrap up the year and one big pricing change.


But first:


I want to take a moment to personally thank you for being part of Loops. We’ve watched founders fail and rise again, witnessed some achieve remarkable success at an incredible pace, and seen many more steadily working toward their dream. Connecting with all of you this year has been inspiring, and I look forward to seeing what you’ll create in the future.


Ok let’s get into it!


### **Changes to pricing**


We no longer charge for transactional email. Previously transactional email was billed at $1 per 2,000 sends. Free plans are still capped, but any paid plan now includes unlimited sending.


Free plan limit caps have doubled to 4,000 sends per month up from 2,000.


Our goal is to help you send all of your email from one place and hopefully this is one less blocker for you.


### **Team switcher**


You can now join and manage multiple teams from a single email address! Great for agencies, contractors or if you’re managing multiple loops accounts for any other reasons :)


### **Shipping early next year**


Webhooks are coming soon! We decided to handle most of the infra ourselves so we’re able to make it affordable and scalable as possible for you. Soon you will be able to sync pretty much all activity on Loops with other services.
