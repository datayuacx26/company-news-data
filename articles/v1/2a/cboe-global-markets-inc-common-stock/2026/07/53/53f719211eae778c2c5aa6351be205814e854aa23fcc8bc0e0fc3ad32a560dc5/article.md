# Market Metrics That Matter: Cboe FX May Volume Highlights

- In May 2026, Cboe FX Spot Average Daily Volume (ADV) reached $54.9 billion, a 14.4% increase from $48.0 billion in May 2025.
- Cboe SEF ADV1 topped $4.8 billion, representing a 54.5% increase from May 2025's ADV of $3.1 billion. Cboe SEF also observed a new single-day record of $7.2 billion on May 5.
- At $20.1 billion, Cboe FX ECN Firm ADV2 accounted for 51.4% of the Spot ECN total, a 14.5% increase from $17.5 billion in May 2025.
- At $2.9 billion, Cboe FX ECN Metals ADV increased 26.5% from May 2025's ADV of $2.4 billion and accounted for 7.7% of the Spot ECN total.
