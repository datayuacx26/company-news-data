# Market Metrics That Matter: U.S. Cash Equities June Volume Briefing

## June 2026 Highlights


Cboe’s market activity increased in June with a record Average Daily Volume (ADV) of 23.3 billion, a high not seen since March 2020.


- U.S. market ADV rose 20.5% month-over-month, with exchanges holding 51.2% of the volume.
- NYSE and MEMX’s market share increased while IEX, MIAX and NASDAQ saw noticeable declines.
- Cboe's share of addressable market share increased to 21.9%, attributable to the continued adoption of premium products and Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) listings.


## Continued Growth in Premium Products


Cboe’s premium execution products continued upward momentum as member firms seek positive performance sourcing on-exchange liquidity.


- Quote Depletion Protection (QDP) ADV increased 26.4% month-over-month
- Cboe EDGX Equities Exchange (EDGX) Retail Price Improvement (RPI) grew 27% month-over-month.
- Five member firms transacting daily, exclusively with retail removing orders.
- Over 460 unique symbols traded in June across corporate listings and ETFs.
- Over 93% of liquidity providing orders captured almost full spread.
- Cboe BYX Equities Exchange (BYX) RPI grew 10% month-over-month, with 12 firms providing liquidity.
- BYX Periodic Auctions grew slightly with 18 firms transacting in Periodic Auctions.
