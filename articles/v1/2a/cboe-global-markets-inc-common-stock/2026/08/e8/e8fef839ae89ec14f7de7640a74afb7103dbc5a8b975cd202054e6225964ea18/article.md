# Market Metrics That Matter: U.S. Cash Equities July Volume Briefing

## July 2026 Highlights


Total U.S. equities market Average Daily Volume (ADV) fell 25.43% month-over-month (down 3.41% year-over-year) to 17.42 billion shares traded in July. Off-exchange volume expanded to 50.1%, up 126 basis points month-over-month. Despite the broader market ADV contraction, Cboe BZX Equities Exchange (BZX) grew its market share to 3.7%, up 38 basis points month-over-month. Cboe BYX Equities Exchange (BYX) also posted a minor gain.


On Cboe EDGX Equities Exchange (EDGX), overall retail participation decreased to 23.0% of venue activity; however, Retail Priority activity rose to 10.0%.


Despite dominant Trade Reporting Facility expansion across price groups, gaining momentum from high-priced stocks down to lower groups, Cboe stood out as the only exchange gaining in the $1–$5 category1.


## EDGX Retail Price Improvement (RPI)


- Cboe EDGX Retail Price Improvement Program launched on April 10, 2026, allowing market participants to offer price improvement in increments of $0.001 exclusively to incoming retail-attested order flow.
- Five member firms are actively adding EDGX RPI orders daily, executing in 513 unique stocks.
- Retail attested orders seeking liquidity entered across more than 9,000 symbols, across corporate and Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) securities.
- 97% of EDGX RPI executions capture most of the spread, trading between the near touch and at the midpoint of National Best Bid and Offer (NBBO).
- EDGX RPI performance remains strong compared to EDGX non-displayed trading mid-to-mid notionally weighted.
