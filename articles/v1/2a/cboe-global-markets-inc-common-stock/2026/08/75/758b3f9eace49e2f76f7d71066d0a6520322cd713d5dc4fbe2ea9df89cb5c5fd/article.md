# Market Metrics That Matter: Cboe FX July Volume Highlights

- In July 2026, Cboe FX Spot Average Daily Volume (ADV) reached $56.8 billion, a 24.6% increase versus July 2025's ADV of $45.6 billion.
- Cboe SEF ADV1 topped $4.3 billion, representing a 46.5% increase from $2.9 billion in July 2025. The LD4 venue recorded $1.1 billion **** in notional traded on July 31, a new single-day high-water mark.
- At $23.1 billion, Cboe FX ECN Firm ADV2 accounted for 55.7% of the Spot ECN total, the highest recorded percentage of Firm ADV on Spot ECN, and a 43.2% increase from July 2025's ADV of $16.1 billion. July 30 marked a new all-time high for Firm liquidity on Spot ECN, with $44.7 billion traded.
- At $2.0 billion, Cboe FX ECN Metals ADV decreased by 6.5% from $2.1 billion in July 2025 and accounted for 4.7% of the Spot ECN total.
