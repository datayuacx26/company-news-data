# Old Republic International Declares First Quarter Regular Cash Dividend

Our parent Company, Old Republic International Corporation (NYSE: ORI), announced that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of 31.5 cents per share.


This dividend is payable on March 19, 2026, to shareholders of record on March 09, 2026. Subject to Board approval of each quarter's new rate, the full year's cash dividend will amount to $1.26 per share compared to $1.16 per share paid in 2025, an 8.6% increase.


2026 marks the 45th consecutive year that Old Republic has increased its regular cash dividend and the 85th year of uninterrupted regular cash dividend payments.
