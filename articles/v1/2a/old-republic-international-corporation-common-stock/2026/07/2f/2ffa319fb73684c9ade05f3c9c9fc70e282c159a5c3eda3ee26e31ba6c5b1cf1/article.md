# Old Republic Announces Formation of a New Inland Marine Entity

---


**Old Republic International Corporation** (NYSE: ORI) has formed a new venture to provide commercial Inland Marine insurance solutions. The new company, Old Republic Inland Marine ("ORIM"), will focus its operations on specialized Inland Marine products through retail and wholesale distribution channels. The new venture will be led by **Matt Bisig** as President. Matt brings important intellectual capital and business relationships cultivated over many years of professional, client-centered service.
