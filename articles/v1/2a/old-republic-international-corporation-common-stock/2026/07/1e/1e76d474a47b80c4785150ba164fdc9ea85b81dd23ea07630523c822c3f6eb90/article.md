# Why You Need Title Insurance in the Age of Cybercrime

While technology has brought unprecedented efficiency and convenience to real estate, it has also made transactions a prime target for increasingly sophisticated financial crime. Today’s transactions face a dual threat: hidden title problems and modern digital fraud. In this environment, title insurance is a critical risk management tool, protecting the transaction as well as the long-term integrity of property ownership, and for lenders, the priority of their liens. Read on to learn why protecting your property with title insurance matters more than ever.


#### The Cost of Fraud is Rising


The cost of real estate fraud has never been higher, thanks to AI-generated schemes like[deepfake video impersonation](https://www.oldrepublictitle.com/blog/how-to-spot-real-estate-fraud/) ,[voice impersonation](https://www.oldrepublictitle.com/blog/voice-impersonation-fraud/) , and Business Email Compromise (BEC). Imagine finding out someone digitally impersonated you and sold your property right out from under you. Or realizing too late that the “agent” email you trusted was fake – and you just wired your life savings to a cybercriminal. As shocking as it sounds, these scenarios happen far more often than people think.


According to[CertifID’s 2026 State of Wire Fraud Report](https://www.certifid.com/article/2026-state-of-wire-fraud-report) , nearly one in four homebuyers reported receiving a fraudulent or suspicious communication during their closing in 2025. Even simpler and faster transactions like refinances are not immune. A recent ALTA-commissioned[study](https://www.alta.org/news-and-publications/news/20251118-Mortgage-Refinances-Have-Significant-Fraud-Risks-Study-Shows) found that forgery and fraud represented nearly 40% of residential refinance title insurance claims in 2025, with an average loss of nearly $207,000 per claim.


#### Human Expertise Still Matters


As technology-assisted fraud continues to evolve, the “curative” work performed by title professionals has never been more important. A[study](https://www.alta.org/news-and-publications/press-release/Title-Professionals-Spend-Significant-Time-Resources-to-Protect-Property-Rights) by ndp | analytics found that title experts typically spend between 22 and 45 hours per transaction fixing errors — and for good reason. Automated data pulls often miss “off-record” risks that only a human expert can catch, including:


- Fraud and forgery
- Recording errors
- Boundary disputes
- Missing heirs
- Undocumented easements
- Unreleased liens
- Off-record claims


Title professionals have the time, resources, and experience to spot red flags. They carefully examine documents, compare names and signatures, and investigate discrepancies. They also mail advisory notices to property owners listed in public records to confirm the legitimacy of a pending sale on vacant land to prevent[deed theft](https://www.oldrepublictitle.com/blog/deed-fraud-prevention/) . Title companies also have many safeguards in place to help prevent[wire fraud](https://www.oldrepublictitle.com/blog/preventing-wire-fraud/) , which cost the real estate industry over[$500 million](https://www.certifid.com/article/state-of-wire-fraud-2025-what-consumers-want-real-estate-professionals-to-know#:~:text=%E2%80%8D-,Why%20wire%20fraud%20is%20so%20rampant%20in%20real%20estate%20in,are%20high%2Ddollar%2Dvalue%20deals.) in 2024. Technology alone cannot match the scrutiny, expertise, or judgment that title professionals provide to make sure that real property can be bought, sold, and financed with confidence.


#### Protection Beyond the Title Search


Even the most meticulous title search may not reveal hidden title problems that can remain undiscovered for months or years after a property is sold. That's why purchasing title insurance is a worthwhile investment. The average cost of title insurance and associated settlement services is only about[0.67% of a property’s purchase price](https://www.alta.org/file/Understanding-the-Cost-of-Title-Insurance.pdf) , yet it protects policyholders from hidden risks that can surface long after closing – and cost tens of thousands of dollars to litigate in court.


#### The Benefits of Title Insurance


Title insurance is a financial safety net for policyholders, backed by a regulated underwriter that provides the following assurances:


- **A duty to defend:** The underwriter is obligated to provide legal defense for covered title claims, including attorney fees and court costs.
- **Protection against hidden title defects:** These may include forgery, fraud, missing heirs, unreleased liens, and boundary disputes.
- **Financial strength:** Claims are paid from statutory reserves, up to the full policy amount.
- **Long-term coverage:** Protection lasts for as long as the homeowner or their heirs owns the property (owner's policy), or for the life of the loan (loan policy).


#### What You Risk Without Coverage


For **borrowers** without an owner’s policy of title insurance, the risks can be devastating:


- Out-of-pocket legal costs to defend title claims
- Trouble selling or refinancing due to unmarketable title
- Loss of equity from undiscovered liens or claims
- Foreclosure or the loss of property


**Lenders** rely on a loan policy to protect their financial interests. If a title defect should arise, the policy insures the validity, enforceability, and priority of their mortgage lien as of the policy date. Without it, potential risks may include:


- Out-of-pocket legal costs to defend against title claims
- Exposure to repurchase requests or reduced secondary marketability
- Continued responsibility for title defects under applicable Government-Sponsored Enterprise (GSE) requirements


#### The Bottom Line


True affordability is more than the price you pay to acquire a property or secure a loan; it’s about safeguarding the property over time. In an environment where modern digital fraud is growing and hidden title problems can surface without warning, it's more important than ever to stay vigilant during digital transactions, practice good cyber hygiene and protect your property rights with title insurance.
