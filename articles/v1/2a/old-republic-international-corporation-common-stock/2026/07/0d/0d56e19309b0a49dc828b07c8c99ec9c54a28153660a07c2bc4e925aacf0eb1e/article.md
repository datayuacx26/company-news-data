# PMA Companies Announces John Santulli, III as President and Chief Executive Officer

PMA Companies announced effective January 1, 2019, John Santulli, III will become President and Chief Executive Officer of PMA Companies.


Vincent T. Donnelly,


currently Chief Executive Officer, will become Executive Chairman of PMA Companies.
