# Old Republic Title Launches New Rate Calculator for Title Agents

**Get a premium quote for your next transaction.**
Old Republic Title is excited to announce that StarsLink, our exclusive title agent portal, includes a new rate calculator. It has a sleek, modern look and several new features, including:


- An intuitive interface for easier quote calculations
- A user-friendly preview screen
- The ability to adjust allowable premiums
- An option to add a file number or reference property address
- The ability to generate and download professional co-branded title insurance quotes


These enhancements make it quicker and simpler for our title agents to generate premium quotes to share with their clients.
