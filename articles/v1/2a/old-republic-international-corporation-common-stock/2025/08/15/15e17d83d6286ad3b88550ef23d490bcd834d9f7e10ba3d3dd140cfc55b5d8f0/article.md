# Celebrating 118 Years of Excellence

On August 25, Old Republic Title (ORT) proudly celebrates our 118-year anniversary!


Since 1907, our Company has helped countless Americans achieve homeownership by protecting property rights. We offer a comprehensive suite of title insurance and related products and services to support real estate transactions.


With 270 branch and subsidiary offices, approximately 4,300 employees and roughly 8,000 independent title agents, ORT is now one of the largest title insurance groups in the nation.


“Our long-term approach continues to deliver results,” says **Carolyn Monroe** , President and CEO of Old Republic Title. “This milestone is a testament to that commitment, and we’re proud to celebrate it!”


Looking ahead, ORT remains focused on combining trusted expertise with innovative technology to better serve our agents and customers.


Here’s to 118 years—and many more to come!
