Tredegar Corporation Announces Susan Sweeney Joins Company as Vice President and Chief Human Resources Officer


05/26/2026 at 4:05 PM EDT


[PDF Version](https://tredegarcorp.gcs-web.com/node/21011/pdf)


RICHMOND, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 26, 2026-- Tredegar Corporation (NYSE:TG) today announced that Dr. Susan Sweeney will join the company as Vice President and Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO), effective June 1, 2026. Dr. Sweeney will report directly to the CEO.


Bapi DasGupta, CEO and President of Tredegar, said, “Dr. Sweeney brings a rare combination of global operational expertise, business leadership, and deep human resources experience.” He added, “Her track record of leading organizational transformation and developing people-centered strategies within complex environments makes her an exceptional fit for Tredegar.”


Dr. Sweeney joins Tredegar from Virginia Transformer Corporation, where she served as Executive Vice President and Chief Human Resources Officer. Her career began at General Motors as Director of Operations, followed by leadership roles at GGB Bearing Technology, where she served as Vice President of Global Operations and later President with full P&L responsibility. She subsequently held dual President and CHRO roles and later served as CHRO at EnPro Industries before joining Virginia Transformer. In addition to her executive career, Dr. Sweeney serves on the Board of Directors of Miller Industries and is an adjunct instructor at Wilmington University.


Dr. Sweeney holds a Doctorate of Education in Organizational Leadership and Innovation from Wilmington University, an MBA from the University of Detroit, and a BA in Economics from Kalamazoo College.
