RICHMOND, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 6, 2026-- Tredegar Corporation (NYSE:TG) plans to release financial results for the fourth quarter of 2025 on March 11, 2026.


**About Tredegar** Tredegar Corporation is an industrial manufacturer with two primary businesses: custom aluminum extrusions for the North American building & construction, automotive and specialty end-use markets and surface protection films for high-technology applications in the global electronics industry. With approximately 1,700 employees, the Company operates manufacturing facilities in North America and Asia.
