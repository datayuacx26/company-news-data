# Hyster-Yale Materials Handling Ranked Among Top 35% by EcoVadis


Recognition received for fourth consecutive year reflects the company’s continued progress in environmental stewardship, labor and human rights, ethics, and sustainable procurement.


August 11, 2026


Hyster-Yale Materials Handling, Inc. (HYMH) has earned a Bronze medal in the 2026 EcoVadis sustainability assessment, placing it among the top 35% of more than 150,000 companies assessed worldwide. The recognition marks the company's fourth consecutive year receiving an EcoVadis medal and follows another year of improvement in its overall score, reflecting ongoing progress in environmental stewardship, labor and human rights, ethics, and sustainable procurement.


“As we work to transform the way the world moves materials from port to home, we are always aiming to improve core values like corporate responsibility,” says Don Buckman, Americas Division Environmental Health and Safety Manager, Hyster-Yale Materials Handling. "This recognition underscores the meaningful progress we've made across our sustainability initiatives and the dedication of employees throughout the organization who help drive those efforts forward every day."


EcoVadis is one of the world’s most widely used business sustainability platforms, evaluating more than 150,000 companies across 250 industries and 185 countries worldwide. Its assessment evaluates the effectiveness of a company's sustainability management system through its policies, actions and results. HYMH's score increased by two points year over year, placing the company in the 73rd percentile globally and demonstrating its commitment to continuous improvement in sustainability performance.
