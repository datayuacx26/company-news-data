# Honors & Achievements

##### Committed to Operational Excellence


---


###### [Frost & Sullivan Recognition](https://hymh.hyster-yale.com/en-us/newsroom/press-releases/frost--sullivan-material-handling-company-of-the-year/)


Frost & Sullivan named Hyster-Yale Materials Handling its 2026 North American Material Handling Company of the Year, recognizing our innovation in automation, electrification and connected technologies, along with our focused customer care philosophy.


###### [Manufacturing Leadership Award](https://manufacturingleadershipcouncil.com/leadership-awards/2026-manufacturing-leadership-award-finalists/)


In the 2026 Manufacturing Leadership awards, Hyster-Yale Materials Handling was named a finalist in the Business Model Transformation category. The award honors the company’s commitment to simplifying automated lift truck accessibility and adoption through its automation portfolio.


###### [Environmental Steward](https://hymh.hyster-yale.com/en-us/newsroom/press-releases/environmental-steward-recognition/)


The Greenville, NC plant has been recognized by the State of North Carolina as an Environmental Steward. This prestigious designation honors a wide range of sustainability initiatives the plant has implemented to minimize environmental impact.


###### [EcoVadis Rating](https://hymh.hyster-yale.com/en-us/newsroom/press-releases/ecovadis-bronze-medal/)


Hyster-Yale Materials Handling earned a Bronze medal in the EcoVadis sustainability assessment in 2025. The EcoVadis rating is a sustainability assessment that evaluates a company’s performance in the areas of environment, labor and human rights, ethics and sustainable procurement


###### Investors in People


Since 1998, the company has collaborated with Investors in People across multiple EMEA locations to demonstrate its commitment to continuously developing people and practices. This framework validates our dedication to empowering employees to achieve business goals.


###### [Great Place to Work Certified](https://hymh.hyster-yale.com/en-us/newsroom/press-releases/great-place-to-work-certified/)


The Hyster-Yale India facility has been Great Place To Work® Certified™ from January 2025 to January 2026. This certification underscores the company’s commitment to fostering a workplace culture built on trust, collaboration, and employee well-being.
