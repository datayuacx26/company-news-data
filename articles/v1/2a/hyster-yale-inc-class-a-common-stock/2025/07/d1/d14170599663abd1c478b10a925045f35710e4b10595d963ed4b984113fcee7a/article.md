# New HYMH Germany Location with Exhibition Space Opens


New location with exhibition space to feature Hyster and Yale brands and host customers opened in Germany.


July 05, 2025


Hyster-Yale Deutschland GmbH relocated to Mörfelden-Walldorf near Frankfurt Airport, Germany, at the beginning of April 2025. With approximately 1,100 m² of exhibition, training, and office space, the new location supports collaboration with customers and dealers, helping strengthen the presence of the Hyster® and Yale® forklift brands in Germany and the surrounding countries.


Hyster-Yale manages activities in Central Europe, Eastern Europe, and Turkey from its site in Frankfurt. Many key Hyster and Yale customers are headquartered in Germany so there is a particular focus is on German-speaking region. The new location aims to support further growth in Germany - Europe’s largest market with approximately 100,000 forklift trucks sold annually.


At the heart of the new site at the Sergro Business Park is an exhibition space featuring a customer experience centre. Here, visitors can test the company’s Hyster and Yale branded lift trucks in various applications. The new centre reflects the company’s Impact Philosophy, and is based on a similar project in Charlotte, NC, USA, and its training centre in Nijmegen, the Netherlands.


The conveniently located new meeting point will serve as a hub for Hyster and Yale dealers to collaborate with customers on a conceptual basis. They can also train their service and sales staff at the site, particularly around product launches. Its proximity to the airport also makes it possible to hold international training courses and events. In the medium term, an authorized dealer will expand the site’s capabilities by providing an additional base to serve the Rhine-Main area.


“We're excited to have a space that connects our teams more closely with Hyster and Yale dealers and customers. We'll be bringing it to life in the coming months, and this concept will be a great way to demonstrate our application-oriented approach to developing individual customer solutions. This opens up new growth opportunities for us in the German-speaking region,” explains Vince Cooke, Vice President Sales, EMEA and Global Key Accounts.


SEGRO Park Mörfelden, Hessenring 15, 64546 Mörfelden-Walldorf, was built in 2018 and is located just ten minutes from Frankfurt Airport, on the A5 motorway with excellent connections to the entire Rhine-Main area. The commercial property meets modern energy and design standards and exemplifies the use of alternative materials, a clean energy balance, and a state-of-the-art appearance. The mixed-use concept offers flexible, long-term expansion options with warehouse and office space.
