# Introducing Character Chat: Engage with Your Favorite Characters Like Never Before!


[Stipop](https://substack.com/@stipopofficial)


Mar 18, 2024


Character Chat Beta - Soon to be available on the Stipop iOS and Android App


We're excited to introduce


**Character Chat** , soon to be released as a beta on Stipop! This feature will let you engage in authentic conversations with your beloved characters, all officially confirmed by their creators.


#### Authentic Conversations with a Twist


Character Chat stands out by providing:


-


**Official Endorsement** : Every character is backed by the original IP creators, ensuring genuine interactions.


-


**Customized UIs** : Each chat room is uniquely designed to match the character's world, enhancing your immersive experience.


-


**Interactive Content** : Get ready for a dynamic chat with stickers, videos, and comics shared by the characters themselves!


### **How to Enjoy a Fun and Respectful Chat**


To make the most out of Character Chat, remember to:


1.


**Be Kind and Respectful** : Treat characters with the same courtesy you’d show real people.


2.


**Explore and Engage** : Dive into different topics and interact with the content shared by characters.


3.


**Stay Open and Provide Feedback** : Enjoy the novel experiences and share your thoughts to help us refine this feature.


### **A Note to Our Creators**


Character Chat offers a unique opportunity to bring your characters to life in a new, interactive format. We're excited to see how your creations will enhance the Stipop universe for fans around the world.


Character Chat is here to transform your connection with your favorite characters, making each interaction more engaging than ever. Dive in, explore, and let us know if you’d like to have your own character chat activated.
