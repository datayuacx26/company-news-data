# Step-by-Step Guide to Getting Started as a Creator on Stipop

# Step-by-Step Guide to Getting Started as a Creator on Stipop


[Stipop](https://substack.com/@stipopofficial)


Jan 08, 2024


Are you a character creator who wants to grow your character into an IP business? Or are you thinking of creating a new character? Consider starting your creator account at Stipop. It’s free of cost and super easy to get started.


Here is a step-by-step guide to getting started as a creator on Stipop:


#### 1. Sign up on Stipop Studio


Visit the studio website and


[sign up](https://studio.stipop.io/auth/sign-up) by entering your email address. You’ll also be asked to enter your mailing address, which is required to receive any payouts from Stipop.


#### 2. Upload your first sticker pack


Design a sticker pack according to


[sticker guidelines](https://studio.stipop.io/guidelines) . You can have 10~30 stickers per pack. Try 10~15 stickers per pack to get started. Next, upload the sticker pack from the


[Upload page](https://studio.stipop.io/my-studio/upload) . Learn more about uploading stickers on Stipop


[here](https://blog.stipop.com/p/how-to-upload-a-sticker-pack-on-stipop) .


#### 3. Share your sticker pack with your audience


It’s crucial that once your sticker pack is live, you should let your audience know about it. Try sharing a story or a post on your Instagram or Facebook account. You can also share the link to your sticker pack with your friends and families.


#### 4. Add ‘Wallet’ information and receive payouts


Go to the


[Wallet page](https://studio.stipop.io/my-studio/wallet/payment) on Studio, enter tax info, and connect your PayPal account to start receiving payouts. If you have any questions regarding this, please contact us at artist@stipop.io.


#### 5. Consider starting your own Subscription


At Stipop, you can choose to sell your own subscription, meaning once enabled, your fans can start subscribing to your creator account and get access to all your content. Learn more about creator subscription


[here](https://blog.stipop.com/p/should-i-enable-my-creator-subscription) .


After enabling your own Subscription, you’ll be able to upload and sell premium sticker packs, which are priced higher than paid stickers.


#### 6. Continue the creative process!


Once all is set, keep working on new creative artwork and upload new sticker packs every month to keep your fans amused and grow your revenue.


---


We are here all the time to support your journey and answer any of your questions. If you have ideas or feedback, please do not hesitate to reach us. Reach us at any time through the 1:1 message on Studio or email us at artist@stipop.io.
