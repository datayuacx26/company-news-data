# Stipop B2B Update - ZAPPY


### Improving Online Conversation Experience With The Next Gen of AI Messaging and Social App, ZAPPY.


[Stipop](https://substack.com/@stipopofficial)


Jan 29, 2024


Image from ZAPPY


[Homepage](https://www.zappy.kr/)


We are thrilled to announce an exciting collaboration between Stipop and


[ZAPPY](https://www.zappy.kr/) , the next generation of AI messaging and social app. As of December 2023, Zappy has integrated Stipop's


[sticker API](https://stipop.io/) , introducing an expansive sticker feature for its users.


**Enriching User Experience**


This partnership marks a significant milestone in enhancing the way people communicate digitally. With Stipop's diverse sticker library now available on ZAPPY, users can express themselves more vividly and creatively in their conversations. Try the Zappy app here! (


[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/zappy/id6463830917) /


[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.two.zappy) )


ZAPPY App screenshot from the iOS App Store


**Growth and Opportunities for Creators**


For our talented community of character creators, this integration presents a remarkable opportunity. The inclusion of Stipop's stickers on Zappy is expected to drive user engagement and open new avenues for creator revenue. As more users interact with these stickers, creators can anticipate a noticeable boost in both visibility and earnings. Learn more about B2B sticker revenue for creators


[here](https://studio.stipop.io/start-earning) .


**A Future of Enhanced Interaction**


We believe that this collaboration with Zappy will not only elevate the user experience on their platform but also contribute significantly to the growing ecosystem of digital art. We are excited to see how this partnership unfolds and look forward to the positive impact it will have on our creators and the broader Stipop community!
