# Goodbye 2021, let’s Welcome 2022


[Stipop](https://substack.com/@stipopofficial)


Jan 14, 2022


Last year ended on a bitter-sweet note and people from all over the world welcome the new year either by celebrating in open or closed boundaries. But one thing that hasn’t changed during all this time is online classes, work from home, restrictions due to pandemics, etc., more or less the story goes like this people are now spending more time communicating and discussing things online. I am sure even your little kid is busy right now attending his classes online and browsing through his unfinished assignments.


At


[Stipop](https://studio.stipop.io/) also many creators celebrated 2022 with exclusive new sticker packs reflecting new feelings, excitement, and resolutions for this year. In today’s post, we are going through some of this exciting New Year and Winter collections pack !!


## **New Year and Winter Collection Pack**


A new year means new resolutions, new joy, new beginnings, and much more. Needless to say, winter is almost at its peak and Jon Snow might be saying: WINTER IS COMING now !! Around last year's end also we received a great response from the creators and many artists uploaded New Year & Winter collection packs that for sure had brightened people’s daily online conversations. Among many these three amazing creators have made a difference in reflecting Winter and New Year excitement with the stickers.


#### **1. Catto’s Winter Adventure by Saya**


This cute adorable cat with funny texts and expressions garnered a lot of attention and impression from the sticker fans. Saya has been quite active this year publishing different kinds of stickers with new themes every time and they all have been phenomenal. Thank you, Saya, looking forward to seeing more such amazing work.


#### **2. Happy Winter Bears by Kikilinda**


Kikilinda has been quite a well-known name by now always coming up with something more artistic and trendy. These cute, furry smiling bears will surely make your day and winter less chilling. Thank you kikilinda for these teddy bears and we are confident that you will keep us entertained with such amazing uploads !


!


#### **3. Winter Beebee Bird & Baebi 2 by nanshi**


Last but not least – nashi, a creator with almost 50 sticker packs now !! Beebeebird and Baebi are two of this creator’s most popular sticker characters. Looking at these stickers you can also feel the winter chill. Hope you are also enjoying the winter like this cute guy. Keep rocking nanshi, your work is fantastic


.
