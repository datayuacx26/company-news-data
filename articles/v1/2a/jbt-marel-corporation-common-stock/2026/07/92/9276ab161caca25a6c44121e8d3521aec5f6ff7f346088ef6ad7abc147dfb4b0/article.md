# The momentum behind Fresh’n Squeeze™ juice extractors

Taking center stage was the Fresh’n Squeeze 1800 Citrus Juicer, which earned a prestigious 2026 Kitchen Innovations (KI) Award just weeks before the show. For more than two decades, the KI Awards have recognized equipment that solves real industry challenges, with entries judged by experts for impact, functionality, and long-term value.


The Fresh’n Squeeze 1800 earned its place by delivering up to 50% more juice per fruit than traditional cut-and-press methods, a cleaner taste, and the durability operators rely on every day. The juiciest part? The 1800’s tabletop profile allows coffee shops, cafes, hotels, and other retailers to leverage the power of Whole Fruit Extraction in a compact format…where every square foot counts.
