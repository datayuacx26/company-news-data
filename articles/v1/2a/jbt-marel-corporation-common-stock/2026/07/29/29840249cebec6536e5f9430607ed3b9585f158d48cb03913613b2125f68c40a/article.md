# Aurora Packing and JBT Marel celebrate ribbon-cutting ceremony at the new beef processing facility

JBT Marel joined Aurora Packing Company in celebrating the ribbon-cutting ceremony for its new state-of-the-art processing facility in Illinois, marking the successful completion of a nearly two-year partnership between the two companies.


The new facility features an integrated suite of[JBT Marel equipment](https://jbtmarel.com/en/meat/cattle-beef/) and[software](https://jbtmarel.com/en/software/meat/across-the-factory/) , engineered seamlessly together to enhance efficiency, food safety, and traceability across Aurora Packing’s latest beef processing operation. Spanning the full process from live animal receiving through finished product output, the facility showcases JBT Marel’s primary offerings for the beef industry in North America. By combining JBT Marel’s advanced hardware and software, the installation gives Aurora Packing real-time visibility and control over its production processes – helping the company boost throughput, reduce waste, and deliver consistent, high-quality product to its customers.


For the JBT Marel team, the ribbon-cutting represents more than the completion of an installation, it reflects nearly two years of close collaboration across sales, project management, engineering,[service](https://jbtmarel.com/en/service/) and software teams, working together with colleagues around the globe to bring the project to fruition.


“We are extremely proud and appreciative to have partnered with Aurora Packing on their goal of building a state-of-the-art facility to move their business forward. The success of this project is due in large part to the engagement, feedback and dedication of the Aurora Packing's team with JBT Marel,” said Lisa Burdick, Account Manager, JBT Marel Meat NA.


“Two years in the making, this project has transformed vision into reality. Through the AXIN Software platform, we've built an end-to-end manufacturing execution solution that connects data, automation, and people to enhance traceability, operational control, food safety, and efficiency across Aurora Packing's beef processing operation,” added Kathy Campbell, Software Project Manager, JBT Marel.
