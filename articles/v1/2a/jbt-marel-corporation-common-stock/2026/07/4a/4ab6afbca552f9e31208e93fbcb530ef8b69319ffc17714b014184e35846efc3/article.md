# How Townsend skinners became the standard in the United States

#### Availability as part of operational continuity


Availability has become an increasingly important factor in equipment decisions. Long lead times can delay projects or limit capacity. In response, Townsend skinners are supported through JBT Marel with a focus on reducing delivery times and improving availability.
For processors, this is not separate from reliability. The ability to access equipment when it is needed is part of maintaining continuity in operations.


#### Townsend within JBT Marel


Townsend is now part of JBT Marel, which provides a broader support structure and access to additional processing capabilities. At the same time, the core design and role of Townsend skinners have remained consistent. The equipment continues to reflect the same principles that have defined its use in the industry for decades. For customers, this combination offers continuity in the equipment itself, with expanded support behind it.


#### A proven approach to skinning


For processors evaluating skinning equipment, the decision is often based on risk and predictability. Townsend skinners are widely understood, broadly used, and supported across the U.S. market. That familiarity reduces uncertainty and makes it easier to integrate equipment into existing operations.


Rather than introducing a new approach, Townsend represents a continuation of an established one. That is a key reason why it continues to be seen as the standard in the United States.
