# JBT Marel adds Flip & Wash bin washer demos at Lakeland Customer Innovation Center

##### See the Flip & Wash process in action


During demonstrations at Lakeland, visitors can follow the complete bin washing process from loading to unloading. One bin at a time is placed onto the chain conveyor, which moves the bin into the Flip & Wash module. Inside the enclosed washing area, the bin is sprayed with high-flow, low-pressure water, inverted 180 degrees, washed across interior and exterior surfaces, returned upright, and moved out of the system for unloading.


The system installed for demonstrations in Lakeland is designed to clean Macro 34FV produce bins and is capable of visibly cleaning up to 120 bins per hour. Wash time can also be adjusted as needed, helping customers explore how different cycle settings may support their operating requirements.


This live demonstration gives customers a practical way to evaluate automated bin washing technology. Visitors can see how the equipment handles bin movement, inversion, surface coverage, and cleaning consistency, then discuss application-specific needs with JBT Marel experts.


##### Expanding customer collaboration in Lakeland


The Lakeland Customer Innovation Center gives customers a place to test, validate, and better understand technologies before making decisions for their own operations. The renovated facility includes spaces for live equipment demonstrations, product testing, training, and collaboration with JBT Marel specialists.


With the addition of Flip & Wash, customers interested in automated bin washing, produce bin cleaning, or fresh produce processing equipment can now experience the technology firsthand in Lakeland. The demo capability helps connect equipment performance with real operational challenges, from manual cleaning demands to the need for more repeatable results.


At JBT Marel, innovation moves forward through collaboration. By continuing to expand demonstration capabilities at the Lakeland Customer Innovation Center, we’re helping customers explore practical solutions, ask better questions, and prepare for what comes next in food processing.
