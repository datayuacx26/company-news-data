# JBT Marel supports the modernization of India’s poultry industry

#### End-to-end solutions


JBT Marel responds to this shift by offering integrated solutions that enable Indian processors to manage and optimize the entire poultry value chain. It starts with humane transport systems from farm to processor, avoiding damage, reducing transport costs, and making the best use of resources, all the way through to final products for retail and food service, such as nuggets in fast food restaurants. The whole process in between is where JBT Marel can act as a full-line supplier. End-to-end systems are designed to improve efficiency, animal welfare, product quality, and yield, while reducing manual labor and optimizing logistics, helping Indian processors operate more consistently and at higher capacity.


At the core of JBT Marel’s approach is the idea that a processing plant should function like a well-tuned, smoothly running engine. Margins in poultry processing are often tight, so success depends on maximizing yield, maintaining consistent quality, and keeping operational costs under control.


#### Primary process


Primary processing remains a key focus area in India, where many plants are developing their capabilities. JBT Marel has strong expertise in equipment and technologies that improve hygiene, increase speed, and enhance process control across the primary process, including live bird handling, stunning, killing, scalding, plucking, evisceration, and chilling. These solutions are essential for Indian processors aiming to meet higher standards while keeping future growth opportunities open.
In particular, for evisceration, the core of the primary process, JBT Marel offers multiple solutions to address a wide range of conditions that may arise in India, including variations in bird size, weight, and species, from broilers and local breeds to layers.
