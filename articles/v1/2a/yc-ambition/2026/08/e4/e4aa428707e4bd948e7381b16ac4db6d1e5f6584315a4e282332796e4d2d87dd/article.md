# Can Ambition Display Quota Attainment and Forecast Coverage on TVs?

**Yes. Ambition can display your team's quota attainment, forecast coverage, and other sales metrics on TVs.** Any metric you build in Ambition can be included on a TV, giving reps and managers an easy way to see progress throughout the day.


This can be especially useful for teams that want important metrics to stay visible without asking reps to check another dashboard.


Here are a few ways you can use Ambition TVs to display quota and forecast data.


## 1. Show progress toward quota with Goal Slides


If you want reps to quickly understand where they stand against a goal, **Goal Slides** are a good place to start.


Goal Slides can display progress toward individual or team targets. For example, you might show a rep's current attainment against their monthly quota or a team's progress toward a shared revenue goal.


Instead of asking reps to look up their progress, the information stays visible to the team.


This can help answer questions like:


- How close are we to our monthly goal?
- Which reps are on track to hit quota?
- How much progress has the team made this week?
- Where do we need to focus before the end of the month?


Goal Slides are particularly helpful when you want the TV to focus on progress rather than ranking reps against one another.


## 2. Compare performance with Leaderboard Slides


You can also display quota-related metrics using **Leaderboard Slides** .


Leaderboards rank reps or teams based on the metric you choose. Depending on how your team works, you might display revenue, meetings booked, opportunities created, or another metric tied to your sales goals.


This gives the team a quick way to see how everyone is performing.


Leaderboards can also be useful during sales competitions or other times when you want to recognize strong performance across the team.


## 3. Use graphs to give your team more context


Sometimes a single number does not tell the full story.


Ambition's graph TV slides allow you to display analytics reports so your team can see how performance is changing over time.


For example, you could compare current performance with a previous time period or show how an important sales metric has changed throughout the month.


This can help managers and reps understand not just where a number stands today, but whether performance is improving, declining, or staying consistent.


## 4. Display current forecast coverage with Most Recent Value metrics


Forecast data works a little differently from many other sales metrics.


For something like calls made or meetings booked, you often want to add up activity over a period of time. Forecast coverage is different because managers typically care about the **current value** , not the sum of every value recorded throughout the week.


That is where Ambition's **Most Recent Value** metric type can help.


A Most Recent Value metric uses the latest value received from your CRM rather than adding together previous values.


For example, if a rep's forecast coverage changes several times during the week, you can use the Most Recent Value metric to show the latest figure on your Ambition TV.


This makes it a useful option for metrics that represent a current state, including forecast data.


## Why display sales metrics on a TV?


Sales teams already have plenty of dashboards and reports. A TV serves a different purpose.


It keeps a small number of important metrics visible without requiring someone to open a report or search for the information.


For managers, that can be useful for reinforcing the goals the team is currently focused on. For reps, it provides a quick way to check progress throughout the day.


The key is choosing metrics that are useful to your team rather than trying to fit every available number onto the screen.


If quota attainment and forecast coverage are priorities, both can be displayed alongside other metrics your team already tracks in Ambition.
