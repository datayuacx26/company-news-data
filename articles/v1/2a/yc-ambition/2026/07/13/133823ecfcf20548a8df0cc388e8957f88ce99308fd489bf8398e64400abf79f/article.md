## What key metrics should VPs of Sales track in a performance management platform?


The metrics you track should connect daily activities to revenue outcomes. Focus on leading indicators that predict results rather than lagging indicators that only tell you what already happened.


Start with quota attainment rate, which shows how your team is progressing against plan. Add pipeline velocity to understand how quickly deals move through stages. Win rate by segment reveals where reps are most effective and where they need development.


Activity-to-outcome ratios matter more than raw activity counts. Tracking how many discovery calls convert to demos, and how many demos convert to proposals, shows which behaviors actually drive revenue. Ramp time to productivity helps you measure whether your coaching and onboarding programs are working.
