# Agree Realty Declares Monthly Common and Preferred Dividends

[View All Press Releases](https://investors.agreerealty.com/press-releases/default.aspx)


### Agree Realty Declares Monthly Common and Preferred Dividends


May 12, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s206.q4cdn.com/455732330/files/doc_news/Agree-Realty-Declares-Monthly-Common-and-Preferred-Dividends-8c885bd24-2026.pdf)


ROYAL OAK, Mich.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Agree Realty Corporation (NYSE: ADC) (the “Company”) today announced that its Board of Directors has authorized, and the Company has declared, a monthly cash dividend of $0.267 per common share. The monthly dividend reflects an annualized dividend amount of $3.204 per common share, representing a 4.3% increase over the annualized dividend amount of $3.072 per common share from the second quarter of 2025. The dividend is payable June 12, 2026 to stockholders of record at the close of business on May 29, 2026.


Additionally, the Company’s Board of Directors has authorized, and the Company has declared, a monthly cash dividend on its 4.25% Series A Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock of $0.08854 per depositary share, which is equivalent to $1.0625 per annum. The dividend is payable June 1, 2026 to stockholders of record at the close of business on May 22, 2026.


**About Agree Realty Corporation**


Agree Realty Corporation is a publicly traded real estate investment trust that is **RETHINKING RETAIL** through the acquisition and development of properties net leased to industry-leading, omni-channel retail tenants. As of March 31, 2026, the Company owned and operated a portfolio of 2,756 properties, located in all 50 states and containing approximately 57.5 million square feet of gross leasable area. The Company’s common stock is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol “ADC”.
