# From First-Time Users to Believers: How Ambient A.I. Changed the Way Muleshoe Providers Document

Muleshoe Area Medical Center (MAMC) is a 25-bed Critical Access Hospital that has proudly served Bailey County Texas and surrounding counties for over 65 years. Like many rural hospitals, their providers carried the weight of time-consuming clinical documentation that could otherwise be spent with patients or simply going home on time.


When MAMC made the decision to go live with Microsoft Dragon Copilot integrated with TruBridge, it marked a significant milestone: all 7 providers were completely new to AI-assisted documentation and ambient listening technology. There was no prior benchmark, no comparison tool — just a team willing to embrace something new.


The results spoke for themselves. Providers who once spent 20+ minutes per patient note taking are now finishing documentation in as little as 6–10 minutes, a 50%-70% decrease. Notes are more detailed, more complete, and require minimal editing.
