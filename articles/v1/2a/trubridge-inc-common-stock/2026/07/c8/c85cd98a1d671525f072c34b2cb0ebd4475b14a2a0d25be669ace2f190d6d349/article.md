# How Cozad Community Health System Optimized Its EHR for Lasting Success

For rural healthcare providers, efficiency isn’t a buzzword — it’s a requirement. Even small workflow improvements can have a meaningful impact on day-to-day operations, both improving provider satisfaction and maximizing resource efficiency.


In this environment, partners that help streamline operations play a pivotal role in sustaining rural healthcare delivery.


For Cozad Community Health System (CCHS), located on the Great Plains of central Nebraska, resources and staffing are consistently tight. Teams serve patients across a broad rural region and must continually balance demand with limited capacity.


“We’ve had to think outside the box to enhance productivity,” says Erin Chytka, Chief Ancillary Officer at CCHS. “In this effort, TruBridge has been by our side providing


[tailored solutions](https://trubridge.com/resources/building-better-community-care-with-tailored-solutions/?utm) that work for us so we can continue to provide lifesaving care.”
