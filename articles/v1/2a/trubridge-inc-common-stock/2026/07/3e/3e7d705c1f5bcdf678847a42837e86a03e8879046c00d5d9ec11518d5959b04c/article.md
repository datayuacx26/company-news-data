# Texas Institute for Surgery Case Study

**Challenge**


There were a few challenges Texas Institute faced. First, they needed to restructure their arrangement with their umbrella company. With nTrust, they knew they would bring in enough cash to achieve this restructuring successfully.


Next, there was a significant knowledge gap with their business office employees; they couldn’t keep up with the complexities of modern healthcare billing—specifically, there was a daily struggle to get claims out the door. Given this, they need to find employees who could hit the ground running to process a high level of claims daily, while not having the time or resources for training.


And finally, Texas Institute wanted more out of their data to participate in the population health initiatives as they understood the importance within their community.
