# What Is a Clearinghouse in Medical Billing?

## Common Problems a Clearinghouse Helps Solve


In a hectic provider billing environment, where staff are often stretched to the limit, errors and omissions are inevitable. The costly consequences include claim denials and payment delays.


The right medical billing clearinghouse can help protect your revenue stream by preventing problems due to formatting errors, missing codes, incorrect patient data, duplicate submissions, and other issues. Additional benefits of partnering with the right clearinghouse include improved tracking and visibility of payer responses and adjudication timelines, as well as easier, more efficient multi-payer workflows.
