# Louisiana critical access hospital strikes RCM partnership

[Jonesboro, La.](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/finance/louisiana-critical-access-hospital-strikes-rcm-partnership/) -based Jackson Parish Hospital is partnering with TruBridge to support revenue cycle management and other financial services.


The 25-bed critical access hospital will deploy TruBridge’s Complete Business Office technology-enabled service to support revenue cycle outsourcing, coding and billing, and claims management, according to an Aug. 4 TruBridge news release.


Jackson Parish Hospital previously had a seven-year partnership with TruBridge before partnering with another vendor in 2019.
