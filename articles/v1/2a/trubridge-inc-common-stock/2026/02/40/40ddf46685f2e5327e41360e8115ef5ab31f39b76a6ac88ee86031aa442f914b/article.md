# Single Path Coding: Optimizing Your Coding Workforce for 2026

As hospitals and health systems enter 2026, healthcare leaders face mounting pressure:


tight labor markets, increasing clinical documentation complexity, and unprecedented changes to the physician fee schedule payment regulations. Coding teams are essential to revenue cycle performance, yet staffing shortages, turnover, and burnout threaten both accuracy and productivity.


While many hospitals still maintain separate facility and professional coding teams, the


[benefits of single path coding](https://trubridge.com/resources/the-benefits-of-single-path-coding-how-trubridge-can-help) go beyond operational efficiency, they help organizations stabilize their workforce, reduce stress, and plan for future challenges.
