# We supported Aberdeen Technical School with a donation of 20 iPads and scholarship!

The RGC Foundation has supported countless families, individuals, and organizations by providing grants and gifts to assist children with ADHD/ASD and elders in financial distress.


As the Chairman & CEO of Regencell Bioscience and the RGC Foundation, he understand the challenges of ADHD and ASD firsthand. Growing up, he faced learning disorders, speech difficulties, and struggled academically. Yet, through perseverance and determination, he overcame these obstacles and eventually graduated from UC Berkeley.


Our mission at RGC is to improve lives through a natural and holistic Traditional Chinese Medicine formula to treat ADHD and ASD. We’ve witnessed remarkable results, and we are committed to expanding our impact.


Today, we are proud to support Aberdeen Technical School with a donation of 20 iPads and scholarship to further the efforts in providing quality education and opportunities for students. Together, we can empower the next generation to achieve their dreams and make a difference in the world.
