# Ms. Margaret Hoor Han Lo

Ms. Margaret Hoor Han Lo is a seasoned investor with over 30 years of extensive experience in the real estate investments. She is also deeply committed to philanthropy, actively participating in various charitable initiatives and volunteer work.


She was a previously healthy woman but experienced a sudden and severe onset of transverse myelitis in January 2006, an extremely rare neurological condition caused by inflammation of the spinal cord which left her in complete paralysis of the lower body, in extreme pain, and loss of bladder and bowel control. Despite extensive testing and hospitalization for three months, doctors at the hospital offered no effective treatment or explanation, and she was declared permanently disabled. Painkillers proved ineffective and caused severe side effects, leaving her in constant agony.


In May 2006, she began treatment with Mr. Sik-Kee Au, a TCM practitioner. Remarkably, within five weeks, she regained movement, and after only three months, she could walk and control bodily functions. Over three years of continued treatment, her condition steadily improved, including the disappearance of previously diagnosed tumors and cysts, normalization of CA125 levels, and cessation of chronic coughing, suggesting organ function improvement. She continued to be his patient until 2015. Today, she remain mobile and live free of constant pain, a transformational impact she attribute to the TCM treatment.
