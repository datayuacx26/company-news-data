# Your AI Strategy Is Only as Smart as Your Voice Infrastructure

Every enterprise is talking about AI. From Microsoft Copilot to intelligent customer service automation, smart organizations are investing heavily in technologies designed to improve productivity, accelerate decision-making and enhance customer experience.


In fact, McKinsey's 2025 *State of AI* survey found that 88% of organizations now use AI in at least one business function, highlighting how rapidly AI has moved from experimentation to mainstream business adoption. 1


Yet the same research found that most organizations are still struggling to scale AI and capture enterprise-wide value. That’s because success depends on more than AI models alone. It requires the right infrastructure, data and communications foundation.


And that starts with voice.


## Why Enterprise Voice Suddenly Matters Again


For years, enterprise voice was viewed as a utility. Essential, but rarely strategic. As long as calls connected, most organizations were satisfied. Today, that mindset is changing fast.


Voice conversations contain some of the richest business intelligence available. Customer sentiment, employee expertise, operational challenges and buying signals are all hidden within everyday interactions. The rise of AI has transformed voice from a communications channel into a valuable source of actionable data.


The challenge? Most enterprises still operate fragmented voice environments built around a mix of legacy PBXs, cloud platforms and disconnected analytics tools. These silos make it difficult to extract insights, automate workflows or deliver consistent customer experiences.


## Microsoft Teams Is the Hub, But Voice Completes the Picture


Microsoft Teams has become the digital workplace for more than 320 million daily users worldwide. 2


It serves as the center of collaboration, meetings, messaging and, increasingly, AI-powered productivity. However, simply deploying Teams does not automatically create an intelligent voice environment.


Organizations still need to connect users, contact centers, devices, carriers and AI applications into a seamless ecosystem. Without that foundation, AI initiatives risk becoming isolated projects rather than enterprise-wide business enablers. The real opportunity lies in creating a unified voice strategy that integrates communications, customer engagement and AI-driven intelligence.

## From Conversations to Business Intelligence


Modern enterprises need more than dial tone. They need the ability to capture, analyze and act on the information generated through customer calls, meetings and employee interactions.


AI-powered voice technologies now make it possible to automatically transcribe conversations, identify sentiment, surface key actions, monitor compliance requirements and uncover trends that would otherwise remain hidden.


This transforms voice from a cost center into a business asset. Customer service teams gain deeper visibility into performance, IT teams simplify management and governance and leadership teams gain access to insights that support faster, more informed decisions.


## The Contact Center Becomes a Growth Powerhouse


To take just one example, nowhere is this shift more visible than in the contact center.


Traditional contact centers often struggle with rigid workflows, disconnected systems and rising customer expectations. By combining Microsoft Teams with AI-first customer engagement solutions, organizations can modernize at their own pace while introducing automation, self-service capabilities and omnichannel experiences.


The result is faster service, improved customer satisfaction and greater operational efficiency, all without the disruption of large-scale rip-and-replace projects.


## The Future Belongs to Intelligent Voice


The next generation of enterprise communications will not be defined by phone systems or carriers, but rather by how effectively organizations connect people, AI and business processes.


Enterprises that treat voice as strategic infrastructure will be better positioned to unlock the full value of Microsoft Teams, accelerate AI adoption and create more intelligent customer and employee experiences.
