# AudioCodes Meeting Insights Extends Offering with CRM Connectivity and Enhanced Mobile Operation

## Enhancements include integration of information captured in meetings into CRM records and a new mobile app that summarizes face-to-face conversations


**Or Yehuda, Israel – March 17, 2025 – AudioCodes (NASDAQ: AUDC) Press Release**


AudioCodes, a leading provider of unified communications voice, contact center, conversational AI applications and services for enterprises, today announced the addition of new major capabilities into its acclaimed[Meeting Insights](https://www.audiocodes.com/applications/meeting-insights) solution. These enhancements include integration with leading CRM systems such as Salesforce and Dynamics 365, and the release of a new mobile application client that facilitates capturing and summarization of meeting information during face-to-face interactions. These strategic advancements aim to address the challenges organizations encounter in effectively capturing and utilizing customer interaction data across various communication channels.


"The integration of Meeting Insights with leading CRMs bridges the gap between meeting content and CRM systems, preventing the loss of crucial insights and minimizing the risk of manual errors," said Shabtai Adlersberg, CEO of AudioCodes. "By connecting Meeting Insights with CRM solutions, we are enabling organizations to seamlessly transform meeting intelligence from any meeting, whether online or face-to-face, into valuable CRM data. This integration is a significant step in our mission to empower enterprises to drive smarter, data-driven decisions across teams, and enhance informed decision-making for management."


By automatically capturing and analyzing meetings content and streamlining updates to CRM records, Meeting Insights enhances CRM data with rich meeting context and insights. The AI-powered integration ensures that CRM updates are based on actual meeting content and conversations rather than relying solely on manual notes that may be incomplete or reflect individual interpretations. As a result, teams can spend more time driving value, accelerating sales cycles and improving customer satisfaction, ultimately enhancing overall business productivity and growth.


The CRM integration with Salesforce and Dynamics 365, as well as the new mobile application, are being made available to selected customers initially. Integration with the Salesforce CRM and the mobile app will be generally available at the beginning of Q2 2025. Please visit the[Meeting Insights webpage](https://www.audiocodes.com/applications/meeting-insights) or contact your AudioCodes representative for more information.
