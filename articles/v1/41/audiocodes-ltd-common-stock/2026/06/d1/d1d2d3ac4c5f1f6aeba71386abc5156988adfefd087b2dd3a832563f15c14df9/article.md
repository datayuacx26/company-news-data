# The Untapped Managed Service Opportunity in Meeting Intelligence

Meeting AI is turning into a problem that organizations must take care of. Employees are already bringing AI assistants into meetings, customer calls and internal project discussions because the value is immediate and obvious. Meetings become easier to manage, note-taking becomes less intrusive and follow-up actions are captured far more efficiently.


However, much of this adoption is happening from the bottom up. Employees are using personal AI notetakers and uploading sensitive business conversations into tools with limited visibility into data retention, compliance controls or access permissions. A recent global study confirmed this phenomenon, finding that 57% of workers conceal their use of AI from their employers and nearly half admit to uploading company data into public AI tools. 1


This creates significant risks around privacy, compliance and business confidentiality, especially when sensitive information is processed, stored or accessed by unauthorized parties. As a result, many organizations are drifting into unmanaged AI adoption also known as shadow AI.


## Meeting Intelligence Is Becoming a Critical Business Asset


But at the same time, leadership teams are beginning to recognize that these conversations contain valuable operational insight that the business cannot afford to lose. This creates a far greater opportunity for MSPs than simple software resale.


For MSPs, the importance of this shift extends well beyond productivity. AI is fundamentally changing what a meeting generates. Conversations that once disappeared as soon as a call ended are now being transformed into structured business data that can now be captured automatically, often across hundreds of meetings every week.


This information can quickly become one of the most valuable datasets within an organization, but many businesses do not yet have the tools to govern, secure or manage it effectively.


## The Real Opportunity Is Governance


This is where the MSP opportunity begins to emerge. For many, meeting AI is just another SaaS product to resell, focusing primarily on licensing and deployment. But the real value does not come from the AI tool itself. It comes from the governance, visibility and operational structure surrounding the intelligence that these platforms create.


The strategic conversation is no longer about helping customers buy AI notetakers. It is about helping them manage and operationalize the growing intelligence layer forming around their communications. That shift allows MSPs to move beyond transactional licensing conversations and position themselves as strategic partners responsible for governance, oversight and business continuity.


## From Productivity Tool to Managed Service


Businesses are also discovering that AI summaries alone are not enough. Actions are still missed, customer commitments still fall between teams and valuable knowledge remains fragmented across individuals. Even with more meeting data, leaders often lack clear visibility into wider organizational trends.


This is precisely where meeting intelligence becomes a real managed service opportunity.


A governed meeting intelligence service enables MSPs to help customers define policies for meeting capture, retention and access, while connecting meeting outputs to CRM, ticketing and operational workflows. Meetings no longer remain isolated conversations – they become part of the business process.


Over time, this creates more than productivity improvement. It builds organizational memory – a searchable, structured record of customer needs, project risks, operational blockers and recurring business issues. For MSPs, that turns meeting intelligence into a recurring, high-value managed service.
