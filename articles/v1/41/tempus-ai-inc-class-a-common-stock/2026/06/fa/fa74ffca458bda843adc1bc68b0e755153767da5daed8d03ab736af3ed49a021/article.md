# Tempus to Present at the Goldman Sachs 47th Annual Global Healthcare Conference

## Tempus to Present at the Goldman Sachs 47th Annual Global Healthcare Conference


June 5, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.tempus.com/node/10241/pdf)


CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 5, 2026-- Tempus AI, Inc. (NASDAQ: TEM), a technology company leading the adoption of AI to advance precision medicine, today announced that it will participate in the Goldman Sachs 47th Annual Global Healthcare Conference.


Tempus Founder and CEO Eric Lefkofsky will participate in a fireside chat at 2:00 p.m. ET on Monday, June 8. A live webcast of the conversation will be available[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevent.webcasts.com%2Fstarthere.jsp%3Fei%3D1766192%26tp_key%3D2ceb6b291c%26tp_special%3D8&esheet=54548421&newsitemid=20260605552468&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=1&md5=b3efddeb75f284d7f228b4752be3e5d8) .


**About Tempus**


Tempus is a technology company advancing precision medicine through the practical application of artificial intelligence in healthcare. With one of the world’s largest libraries of multimodal data, and an operating system to make that data accessible and useful, Tempus provides AI-enabled precision medicine solutions to physicians to deliver personalized patient care and in parallel facilitates discovery, development and delivery of optimal therapeutics. The goal is for each patient to benefit from the treatment of others who came before by providing physicians with tools that learn as the company gathers more data. For more information, visit tempus.com.
