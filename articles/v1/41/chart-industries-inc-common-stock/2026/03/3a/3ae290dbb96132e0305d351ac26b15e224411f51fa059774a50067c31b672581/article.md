## Vaporizer Selection


### Chart Industries | March 19, 2026


Selecting the right cryogenic vaporizer technology is crucial for achieving the best balance between efficiency, performance, and operational cost.


**Ambient Air Vaporizers(AAVs)**


- Operate energy-free using natural or forced-draft air heating
- Environmentally friendly with zero fuel consumption
- Ideal for moderate-load and moderate-temperature applications


**Water/Steam Heated Vaporizers (WHVs/SHVs)**


- Designed for high-throughput requirements with stable outlet temperatures
- Perfect for LNG terminals and ASU backup systems
- Utilize existing plant steam or hot water for cost-efficient performance


**Electrically Heated Vaporizers (EHVs)**


- Offer precise temperature and pressure control in a compact footprint
- Fully automated with integrated monitoring and control systems
- Ideal for smaller facilities, peak-shaving applications, or sites with limited utility infrastructure
