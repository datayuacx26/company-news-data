# ChartWater™ appointed as exclusive distributor for PFAS Guard, LLC

## ChartWater™ appointed as exclusive distributor for PFAS Guard, LLC


### ChartWater | July 3, 2025


ChartWater, a leader in advanced water treatment technologies and a brand of Chart Industries, is proud to announce a strategic partnership with PFAS Guard, LLC, becoming the exclusive distributor of their innovative, patent-pending PFAS treatment systems. This collaboration marks a significant advancement in the delivery of rapid, cost-effective solutions to address PFAS contamination in drinking water.


PFAS Guard’s modular treatment kits are designed for quick deployment, easy installation, and reliable performance. Key features of the system include:


- Simple, modular design with practically everything above the slab:


- NSF-approved buffered anion exchange resin for reliable PFAS removal, pre-installed in ASME Code pressure vessels with stainless steel internals, designed to remove/replace the resin with multiple methods.
- NSF-certified hoses for safe and compliant operation.
- Standard sampling locations for consistent monitoring.
- Durable utility enclosures – built without wood for enhanced resilience.


- Standardized sizing and model numbers for up to 600 gpm
- Single use design license
- Quick turnaround - kits can be shipped within weeks, and easy installation allows for quick start-up


"This partnership enhances our ability to provide cutting-edge, field-ready solutions to help water systems quickly meet federal requirements for PFAS,” said Tyler Butel, VP of Sales for ChartWater. “Together with PFAS Guard, we’re delivering not just equipment, but complete, optimized solutions tailored to each customer’s needs.”


With over 1,000 systems installed since 2002, ChartWater brings unmatched experience in contaminant removal and system integration.
