# Chart Ferox claims environmental awards

## Chart Ferox claims environmental awards


### Chart Ferox | December 30, 2025


We proudly recognize an outstanding achievement by our Chart Ferox team in Decin, Czech Republic, who have been honored in a national competition celebrating companies that embed environmental responsibility and sustainability into their core business practices.
In a highly competitive field, Chart Ferox achieved 3rd place in the category “TOP Environmentally Responsible Company” and was the 1st place winner in the category “TOP Innovation for Sustainability.” This is a remarkable accomplishment and a strong testament to the team’s long-term commitment to sustainable operations. This award-winning initiative reflects Chart Ferox’s approach to viewing waste as a valuable resource rather than a problem to be disposed of. Since introducing this concept, the Decin site has delivered measurable and impressive results, including:


- Achieving zero waste to landfill for more than three years
- Realizing annual cost savings of over $100k previously associated with waste disposal and packaging
- Implementing systematic recycling and re-use of all waste streams
- Achieving a 30% reduction in overall waste produced


This success would not have been possible without the daily commitment of our team members in Decin who have fully embraced a culture of sorting waste at source, as well as the strong cooperation of our suppliers and partners.
