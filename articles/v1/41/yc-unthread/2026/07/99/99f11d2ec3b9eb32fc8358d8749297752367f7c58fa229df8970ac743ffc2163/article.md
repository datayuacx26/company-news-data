# Moveworks vs Unthread: Choosing the Right AI Help Desk for Internal Support

Selecting the right AI help desk platform determines how efficiently your IT, HR, and operations teams handle employee requests. While Moveworks operates as an enterprise-focused conversational AI platform now owned by ServiceNow,[Unthread](https://unthread.io/products/slack-support?ref=unthread.io) delivers Slack-native internal ticketing with purpose-built AI agents that resolve requests without forcing employees to leave their workflow. The platforms differ fundamentally in architecture, deployment approach, and pricing model: Moveworks positions itself for large enterprises requiring extensive system integrations and multilingual support across global workforces, while Unthread serves Slack-first teams from seed stage to Fortune 500 seeking rapid implementation and transparent costs. Understanding these fundamental differences helps growing companies select the approach that matches their budget, deployment timeline, and internal support objectives.

## **Understanding the Foundation: AI Help Desk Solutions**


Modern AI help desks use large language models and conversational AI to automate ticket deflection, route requests to appropriate teams, and generate responses from knowledge base documentation. These platforms transform how IT, HR, and operations teams handle internal support by reducing manual triage and accelerating resolution times.


The core functionality includes natural language processing to understand request intent, automated routing based on content analysis, and integration with enterprise tools like Jira, Salesforce, and HRIS systems. However, the delivery model, pricing structure, and implementation requirements vary dramatically between platforms.


Unthread positions itself as "the AI helpdesk that does the work for you," offering deep AI integration designed from inception for conversational support within Slack. The platform serves five primary use cases: IT teams handling infrastructure requests, HR teams managing employee queries, customer support teams operating Slack-based channels, employee support teams running general internal helpdesks, and RevOps teams coordinating cross-functional operations.


Moveworks operates as a conversational AI platform serving 350+ enterprise customers with 5.5 million employees across their client base. In May 2024, ServiceNow announced its intent to acquire Moveworks, integrating the conversational AI platform into ServiceNow's broader IT service management ecosystem.


The fundamental difference lies in accessibility and deployment speed: Unthread offers published pricing and a faster setup path, with a first internal support channel able to go live quickly, while Moveworks typically involves enterprise sales cycles and longer implementation planning.
