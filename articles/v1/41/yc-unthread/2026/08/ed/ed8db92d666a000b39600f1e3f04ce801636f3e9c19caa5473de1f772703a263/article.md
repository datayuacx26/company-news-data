## **Understanding AI Help Desk Software for IT Teams**


Help desk software with purpose-built AI agents uses machine learning, natural language processing, and workflow automation to handle employee service requests. Unlike traditional ticketing systems that simply log and route issues, platforms with purpose-built AI agents can understand request intent, draft responses, route requests, and trigger approved workflows with varying levels of human oversight.


The distinction between AI copilots and agentic AI matters for IT teams. AI copilots assist human agents by suggesting responses and surfacing knowledge base articles. Agentic AI operates autonomously, resolving tickets end-to-end by executing workflows, updating systems, and communicating directly with employees.


For internal IT support, this means employees can submit requests through familiar channels like Slack, receive instant responses for common issues, and get routed to the right specialist when human expertise is needed.
