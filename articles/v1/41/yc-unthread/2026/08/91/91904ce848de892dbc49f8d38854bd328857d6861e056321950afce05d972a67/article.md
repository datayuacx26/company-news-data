## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **What is AI help desk software and how does it benefit internal support teams?**


AI help desk software uses artificial intelligence to automate ticket routing, generate responses from knowledge bases, and resolve common employee requests without human intervention. Internal support teams benefit through reduced response times, lower ticket volumes for human agents, and consistent service quality across departments. Platforms with agentic AI go beyond answering questions to take autonomous action like provisioning accounts, resetting passwords, and updating records in integrated systems.


### **How can AI help desks improve employee self-service and reduce resolution times?**


AI help desks enable self-service by providing instant answers to common questions through knowledge base search, chatbots, and conversational interfaces. Agents using AI-based suggestions saw a[22% reduction](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/when-ai-chatbots-help-people-be-more-human?ref=unthread.io) in average handle time in a study of 256,934 service chats. Self-learning knowledge bases automatically generate new documentation from resolved tickets, continuously improving self-service coverage over time.


### **Can AI help desk software integrate with existing HR and IT systems?**


Leading platforms integrate with 20+ enterprise tools including HRIS systems like Workday and Rippling, identity providers like Okta, project management tools like Jira and Asana, and CRM systems like Salesforce and HubSpot.[Unthread's integration capabilities](https://unthread.io/products/integrations?ref=unthread.io) include bidirectional sync with existing ticketing platforms and SCIM sync for automated user provisioning from HR directories.


### **What security and compliance considerations matter for AI help desk platforms?**


Internal support teams should verify SOC 2 Type II certification, evaluate HIPAA compliance availability for healthcare data, and confirm SSO support for enterprise authentication. Organizations handling sensitive employee data should consider platforms offering bring-your-own-LLM functionality to maintain control over AI processing. Isolated hosted environments provide additional security for organizations with strict data residency requirements.


### **How does a self-learning knowledge base work for internal support?**


Self-learning knowledge bases analyze resolved ticket patterns to identify frequently repeated questions and automatically draft help articles for team review. The AI detects when existing documentation becomes outdated based on ticket trends and flags articles needing updates. This approach reduces manual content creation workload while ensuring knowledge bases stay current with organizational changes and new policies.
