### **How does a self-learning knowledge base work?**


Self-learning knowledge bases analyze resolved tickets to identify patterns and frequently asked questions. The AI drafts help articles based on successful resolutions, flags outdated documentation when ticket patterns change, and shows clear before/after changes for approval. This approach keeps documentation current without requiring dedicated content creation time. Unthread's[knowledge base](https://unthread.io/products/knowledge-base?ref=unthread.io) syncs from existing sources like Google Drive and Notion while continuously learning from new tickets.
