## **How to Choose the Right Platform**


Selecting internal help desk software for remote teams requires evaluating several factors beyond feature lists.


### **Communication Platform Alignment**


If the organization lives in Slack, prioritize[Slack-native solutions](https://unthread.io/solutions/it-service-desk?ref=unthread.io) that eliminate context-switching. Platforms requiring employees to leave their primary communication channel create friction and reduce adoption.


### **Cross-Departmental Requirements**


Consider whether support needs span IT, HR, finance, procurement, and legal from one platform. Solutions like Unthread and ServiceNow handle multi-department workflows, while others focus primarily on specific domains.


### **AI Capabilities and Resolution Rates**


Evaluate purpose-built AI agents versus retrofitted AI features. Look for documented resolution rates and case studies proving real-world performance. Automatic resolution rates vary widely by request mix, workflow coverage, and knowledge quality. In one documented deployment, Lemonade reported that Unthread automatically resolved about 40% of incoming tickets across several internal teams.


### **Admin Setup and Ongoing Configuration**


Remote teams often lack dedicated IT resources for complex platform administration. Consider ease of initial setup and the effort required to adjust workflows, routing rules, and automations as requirements change.
