# Parsons Awarded Contract to Help Modernize Knoxville’s McGhee Tyson Airport Terminal

# Parsons Awarded Contract to Help Modernize Knoxville’s McGhee Tyson Airport Terminal


08/18/2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.parsons.com/node/14916/pdf)


**Key Takeaways:**


- Parsons was selected by the Metropolitan Knoxville Airport Authority to provide PM/CM services for the Knoxville Airport Terminal Development Program.
- The five-year program will help Knoxville Airport safely accommodate passenger growth, modernize aging infrastructure, and improve traveler experience.
- Parsons’ aviation program delivery experience, digital program management tools, and Federal Aviation Administration funding compliance expertise will support transparent, data-driven project delivery.


CHANTILLY, Va., Aug. 18, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Parsons Corporation (NYSE: PSN) announced today that it has been selected by the Metropolitan Knoxville Airport Authority to provide[program and construction management (PM/CM)](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=MBTl1TJxthuYEo11eIjJfrQV4-oe_IJolPqT-J94DSlomh85n9yn_25ZxAwAkky0N3GFbgODJ8ThtqvIeSQ8Z49BiVnGBroD-59KjQw6a6vg2nMF1dxT7OEiXBb4bvxDnUUb5sdJ1YW7eTI1yflefI-iB-1QkyaRLAIWzNrHAEs=) services for the McGhee Tyson Airport (TYS) Terminal Area Development Plan in Knoxville, Tennessee. The five-year contract, which is new work for the company, expands Parsons’ aviation infrastructure portfolio with a new customer.


Under the contract, Parsons will support a complex capital improvement program focused on terminal modernization, passenger growth, and long-term airport development to enhance the traveler’s experience. The company will provide centralized program oversight to help manage cost, schedule, stakeholder coordination, and compliance with Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) funding requirements.


“Parsons’ proven expertise spans the entirety of our business, from[delivering complex infrastructure at major airports throughout North America and the Middle East](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=nI23M2Qa8MTTscXC4svQp6GgiYT8L8FyyPq0Xlk-n0A6Qv7F3zz9sc3CAz_ydOScKbT3rykl4_eCO4SDUi4bIgsFTmAyOEHytjmqK5WCjDEKMUFJ2SRwiey1iXhJ9ecNtYPlZ8rudVvQu6HyWM9aqnJHNZaxsiiWZvMuvJeqkhY9_fO4NcQ71FL_Ay6gOTNkrN_kxVqclQg-cz51cmQmkadsIvDN2mVh6ilDTOFCg7X23xhMoY9KXiKMP7xrt6MlaVYGO4oh80_uGE9bGD8CHkk0xJLi9fJV8emRb81v1-cJJoAhfP_4qmVG7cvUH_Zh) , supporting the[Federal Aviation Administration’s next-generation modernization program](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=biK5DCfeYKVDk7Vi2mA9xsguijRnba09YH6az_TriNMP7rUOiHnE7OVXlaop7uGrFpw9XWrzM6wDG0KFgniIlpI1mW2rJHTnYIU3XGWV0aCptnAdIL0hPeg4PHM6F6xoRH0-z6HFmy4C9rPb75MwnSQK7wiEMqG3hyvyM5Akd4In6joyTS7KAocRAeGzewtM) , and[executing fire-fighting foam transitions](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=tx0HSbDKoSHEmWx9hHAnSjmBHd6RTeECUDTECCGVQBCBOHKRBheEZz2ZAYoU7gwgMktfK1t23V8-kRDP-mo0Nc-kQ1rZZEK2coFZ7ecySndW8r2iO9cZMrWxI1f0_0E8WC0T5jZwWzhItgnrQeY28Q==) ,” said Martin Boson, president of Engineered Systems for Parsons. “This award expands our position in the aviation market with a new strategic airport customer, and we look forward to supporting the Metropolitan Knoxville Airport Authority as it advances a terminal development program that will enhance operations, improve the passenger experience, and support the region’s continued growth.”


Parsons will support transparent delivery by aligning program controls, stakeholder coordination, reporting, and construction oversight throughout the expected period of performance. In addition, the company will leverage digital program management and infrastructure delivery technologies to improve decision-making, including tools for managing cost and schedule; executive dashboards; cloud-based document and construction management systems; and data analytics for forecasting and performance monitoring.


Parsons, a leading[global aviation solutions provider](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=KLFpEiQLFVUksxFapOKHcXN9b4pZQonyy3-VnkMgizgLQnjrxMlfFTbUdGOqnk_AA6lKDfJIxLAplRhZYcBY9tVZDht836AseaxA9tdKDiM1U_BxBnhgSdKeYErh1_f_) , has planned, designed, constructed, managed, enhanced, and sustained terminal, landside, and airside infrastructure for over 450 airports in 40 countries. This includes work on major airports including Zayed International Airport and Sharjah International Airport in the United Arab Emirates, Los Angeles International Airport in California, Newark Liberty International Airport in New Jersey, and John F. Kennedy International Airport in New York. From modernizing infrastructure to enhancing operational efficiency, the company leverages cutting-edge technology to address the evolving needs of the aviation industry.
