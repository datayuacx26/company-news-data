# Parsons to Announce Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on July 29, 2026


07/01/2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.parsons.com/node/14716/pdf)


CHANTILLY, Va., July 01, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Parsons Corporation (NYSE: PSN) will release second quarter 2026 financial results before the markets open on Wednesday, July 29, 2026. The company will host a conference call at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time that day to discuss its earnings results and guidance, followed by a question-and-answer session.


Access to a webcast of the live conference call can be obtained through the Investor Relations section of the company's website ([https://investors.parsons.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=rUixdOYIp0ZgcwTOyVRAEXPWzpnb9KFws4EMUoiv3kQ0fxuMl86OzkM-v1l-LOdvdjZdSzz5CENqyRt_YtagQpyDbXbdhp-0_f7DuNXMyhQQCiRXGD_zLzYH5RHo2zqC) ). Those parties interested in participating via telephone may register on the Investor Relations website or by clicking[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=ag8rkPXZt8qPza6_npL1XYY1-sTWDzbcon7mV1xoIb6j4I8n1Rtx7fQZmlDx2039hIw31VajpOlspaL1b418GhEBJPwfx9Mlvpp9tcCkQMOBvQX9PfQcd848S_ns46jC2Qfmo3GOw3ylFTHSgDhWh1gKIkpzbsPT37GjHP7EHVs=) .
