# Manganese Metal (Mn)

Manganese metal (Mn) is best known for its technical significance in steel. However, pure Mn is also a very important element to both aluminum alloys and copper alloys. Normally sold as electrolytic flakes (eMn flakes), Pure Mn is typically 2″ x downsize with 99.7% and 99.9% chemical purity available. Ferro Manganese (FeMn) alloys for iron base materials come in a wide range of carbon content and varying granule sizes from lumps to powders.
