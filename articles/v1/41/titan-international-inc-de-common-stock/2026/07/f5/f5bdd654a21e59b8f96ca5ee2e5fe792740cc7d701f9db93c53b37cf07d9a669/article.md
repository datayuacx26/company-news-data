# ITP Kinetic UTV Tire

ITP Kinetic 32x10.00R15NHS tires on a Polaris Ranger.


##


Jul 20, 2026


**Titan strengthens American-made ITP lineup with new Kinetic™ UTV tire line**


*Built in the USA for next-level traction, durability, and control on any terrain.*


**QUINCY, Illinois <July 21, 2026>** —


Titan International continues to raise the bar in powersports innovation with the debut of the ITP Kinetic™ tire line, designed to deliver confident, all-terrain performance for today’s largest, heaviest and most powerful UTVs without sacrificing traction or durability. The Kinetic tire line combines performance with precision providing enthusiasts and powersports extremists with unmatched confidence in nearly any application.


“UTV owners today are pushing their machines further than ever,” said Todd Hinson, Powersports Product Manager at Titan. “The new Kinetic tire line was engineered to keep up with users that push their machine’s limits and utilize their UTVs in many versatile and challenging tasks and applications. Giving our customers one tire for their ever-changing needs.”.”


**Built for riders who expect more** The ITP Kinetic tire line is engineered for drivers of all skill levels who expect the tires on their machines to handle the terrain no matter where the trail leads.


**** **Innovation that performs**


The elements of the tire design provide more confidence, control and capability than most other all-terrain options on the market. Key features include:


- **All-terrain superiority** — open tread pattern tackles everything from hard pack and loose dirt to rocky trails and ruts.


-


**Enhanced grip** — strategic siping in select tread blocks enhance flexibility and grip on slippery or uneven surfaces.
- **Precision handling** — dimpled tread elements improve stability and steering precision.
- **8-ply strength** — radial construction provides a smooth, comfortable ride with superior strength and puncture resistance.
- **Sidewall armor** — reinforces protection against cuts and punctures, offering peace of mind on rugged terrain.
- **Rim protection** — a built-in rim guard shields wheels from debris and damage, maintaining appearance and integrity.
- **Proudly American-made** — built with precision and pride at ITP’s manufacturing facility in Clinton, Tennessee.


The combination of aggressive styling and balanced performance makes the ITP Kinetic a versatile choice for riders who want a tire that can confidently handle multiple terrains without compromise. “This tire does just about everything riders could ask for,” added Rhett Turpin, Director of Sales – Powersports.. “It’s quiet on hard surfaces, aggressive on rough terrain, and built to last.”


The ITP Kinetic tire is available today in 5 of the most popular sizes through dealers nationwide.


For more information about the ITP Kinetic tire line or to find a retailer, visit[https://www.itptires.com/find-a-retailer/](https://www.itptires.com/find-a-retailer/) or contact your local powersports dealer.
