# Charles River Associates (CRA) to Host Second-Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Results Conference Call on August 6

[PDF Version](https://crainternationalinc.gcs-web.com/node/21756/pdf)


*Conference Call to be Webcast Live at 10:00 a.m. ET*


BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 23, 2026--[Charles River Associates](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com&esheet=54575269&newsitemid=20260723830418&lan=en-US&anchor=Charles+River+Associates&index=1&md5=80d78c5e7470a871746b4d495fda2266) (NASDAQ: CRAI), a worldwide leader in providing[economic, financial, and management consulting services](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com&esheet=54575269&newsitemid=20260723830418&lan=en-US&anchor=economic%2C+financial%2C+and+management+consulting+services&index=2&md5=1ce3c974ef99e8f4279f8b5f67c6591a) , today announced that the company will webcast its second-quarter 2026 financial results conference call on Thursday, August 6, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. ET. The conference call will be hosted by President and Chief Executive Officer[Paul Maleh](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2Fexpert%2Fpaul-maleh&esheet=54575269&newsitemid=20260723830418&lan=en-US&anchor=Paul+Maleh&index=3&md5=15415c0eff0107c811ddb314f4c0dfc5) , Chief Financial Officer[Eric Nierenberg](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2Four-people%2Feric-nierenberg%2F&esheet=54575269&newsitemid=20260723830418&lan=en-US&anchor=Eric+Nierenberg&index=4&md5=79105bc13e5e9cf6b9fa4b49c7e8d369) , and Chief Corporate Development Officer[Chad Holmes](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2Fexpert%2Fchad-holmes&esheet=54575269&newsitemid=20260723830418&lan=en-US&anchor=Chad+Holmes&index=5&md5=a3fc195ac5a1b3c38f503389be5fe106) .

**About Charles River Associates (CRA)**


Charles River Associates® is a leading global consulting firm specializing[in economic, financial, and management consulting services](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2F&esheet=54575269&newsitemid=20260723830418&lan=en-US&anchor=in+economic%2C+financial%2C+and+management+consulting+services&index=10&md5=9991d93b37c0aa2ddf1dc14ef2b5191e) .
