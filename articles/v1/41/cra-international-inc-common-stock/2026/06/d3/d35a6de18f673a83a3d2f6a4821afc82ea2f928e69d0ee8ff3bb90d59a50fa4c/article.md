# Charles River Associates (CRA) to Present at the Sidoti Small-Cap Virtual Conference

[PDF Version](https://crainternationalinc.gcs-web.com/node/21661/pdf)


BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 3, 2026--[Charles River Associates](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2F&esheet=54546609&newsitemid=20260602327202&lan=en-US&anchor=Charles+River+Associates&index=1&md5=5d994b0344dda2a9d8fecf0c61c41fdd) (NASDAQ: CRAI), a worldwide leader in providing[economic, financial, and management consulting services](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2F&esheet=54546609&newsitemid=20260602327202&lan=en-US&anchor=economic%2C+financial%2C+and+management+consulting+services&index=2&md5=02608c6178d9f94b3ca7adcd6489e7d4) , today announced that the Company will participate in the Sidoti Small-Cap Virtual Conference on June 17 and 18, 2026. CRA Chairman and Chief Executive Officer[Paul Maleh](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2Four-people%2Fpaul-maleh%2F&esheet=54546609&newsitemid=20260602327202&lan=en-US&anchor=Paul+Maleh&index=3&md5=a6a0fbd4b364ac4758b2ef96c4ea561c) is scheduled to present at 10:00 a.m. EDT on Wednesday, June 17. A live webcast will be available on the Company’s[investor relations website](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.crai.com%2F&esheet=54546609&newsitemid=20260602327202&lan=en-US&anchor=investor+relations+website&index=4&md5=628d7398c82f76df24a2c4ae34f3deeb) . An archived replay will be available after the event.


Mr. Maleh, Chief Financial Officer[Eric Nierenberg](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2Four-people%2Feric-nierenberg%2F&esheet=54546609&newsitemid=20260602327202&lan=en-US&anchor=Eric+Nierenberg&index=5&md5=fcc8ae0d945e1897b067479c092441ea) , and Chief Corporate Development Officer[Chad Holmes](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2Four-people%2Fchad-holmes%2F&esheet=54546609&newsitemid=20260602327202&lan=en-US&anchor=Chad+Holmes&index=6&md5=df317e0e24613409381cc9d8fd41db81) will be available for virtual meetings with investors.
