# FirstEnergy Ohio Utilities’ Competitive Bidding Process Auction: Information Session for Prospective Bidders on Thursday, April 16, 2026

[PDF Version](https://crainternationalinc.gcs-web.com/node/21516/pdf)


BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 15, 2026--[CRA International, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2F&esheet=54506156&newsitemid=20260415478509&lan=en-US&anchor=CRA+International%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=5af91515ec4a043056c96eceb9ac95ff) (NASDAQ: CRAI), a worldwide leader in providing[economic, financial, and management consulting services](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fcrai.com%2F&esheet=54506156&newsitemid=20260415478509&lan=en-US&anchor=economic%2C+financial%2C+and+management+consulting+services&index=2&md5=683879535804a29492804cedfe8a64ee) , today announced that an auction process will be conducted for FirstEnergy Corp.’s (NYSE: FE) Ohio subsidiaries – Ohio Edison Company, The Cleveland Electric Illuminating Company, and The Toledo Edison Company – to procure full requirements service for their Standard Service Offer customers. The auction process will lead up to the auction scheduled for Tuesday, June 9, 2026.


The bidding process will use a descending-price clock auction format. The auction will be managed by the Auction Manager, CRA International, Inc. The auction is being conducted pursuant to approval from the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio (PUCO).


The Information Session for prospective bidders for the June auction is scheduled for Thursday, April 16, 2026. Instructions on how to join the Webinar session are available on the Auction Information Website at[firstenergycbp.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.firstenergycbp.com%2F&esheet=54506156&newsitemid=20260415478509&lan=en-US&anchor=firstenergycbp.com&index=3&md5=5543d65620acfa3f8c07b950bc8161d9) . Part 1 Applications from prospective bidders will be accepted starting April 17, 2026, and are due no later than May 5, 2026. For successful Part 1 applicants, the submission window for the Part 2 Application process will begin on May 12, 2026.


Additional information about the auction process can be found at the Information Website at[firstenergycbp.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.firstenergycbp.com%2F&esheet=54506156&newsitemid=20260415478509&lan=en-US&anchor=firstenergycbp.com&index=4&md5=13c90dc11585f44d0f297b2ead0efc14) .
