# Charles River Associates (CRA) to Present at Noble Capital Markets Virtual Conference

[PDF Version](https://crainternationalinc.gcs-web.com/node/21636/pdf)


BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 26, 2026--[Charles River Associates](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2F&esheet=54540871&newsitemid=20260526099013&lan=en-US&anchor=Charles+River+Associates&index=1&md5=599d0cdde6a6fc3bc1db367171e38fe7) (NASDAQ: CRAI), a worldwide leader in providing[economic, financial, and management consulting services](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2F&esheet=54540871&newsitemid=20260526099013&lan=en-US&anchor=economic%2C+financial%2C+and+management+consulting+services&index=2&md5=2121202b0c94f792dfdc149b76e34fbb) , today announced that the Company will participate in the Noble Capital Markets Virtual Equity Conference on June 3 and 4, 2026. CRA Chairman and Chief Executive Officer[Paul Maleh](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2Four-people%2Fpaul-maleh%2F&esheet=54540871&newsitemid=20260526099013&lan=en-US&anchor=Paul+Maleh&index=3&md5=a3f739741cce4a564d83e1e55aaf171d) is scheduled to present at 11:00 a.m. EDT on Wednesday, June 3. A live webcast will be available on the Company’s[investor relations website](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.crai.com%2F&esheet=54540871&newsitemid=20260526099013&lan=en-US&anchor=investor+relations+website&index=4&md5=53886a95a3a5eba9adccdc4b55dffed1) . An archived replay will be available after the event.


Mr. Maleh and[Brian Langan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.crai.com%2Four-people%2Fbrian-langan%2F&esheet=54540871&newsitemid=20260526099013&lan=en-US&anchor=Brian+Langan&index=5&md5=0210587106c27c5f1315134293b7dc2b) , CRA’s Executive Vice President and Chief Strategy and Business Transformation Officer, will be available for virtual meetings with investors.
