# CRA advises AWS on CMA’s cloud services market investigation

On 5 October 2023, an investigation was initiated by the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) into the supply of public cloud infrastructure services in the UK. The investigation follows a referral from Ofcom, which identified potential competition concerns within the cloud services market.


A CRA team led by[Mikaël Hervé](https://ecp.crai.com/team/mikael-herve/) ,[Sebastian Panthöfer](https://ecp.crai.com/team/sebastian-panthofer/) ,[Christian Michel](https://ecp.crai.com/team/christian-michel/) ,[Mariam Arutyunyan](https://ecp.crai.com/team/mariam-arutyunyan/) ,[Areen Dakessian](https://ecp.crai.com/team/areen-dakessian/) ,[Federico Navarro](https://www.linkedin.com/in/federico-navarra-73868511a/?originalSubdomain=it) , and[Charles Conquest](https://www.linkedin.com/in/charles-conquest/?originalSubdomain=uk) advised AWS on the matter. The team provided analysis on market dynamics and assessment of competitive effects. The investigation is ongoing, with a statutory deadline set for August 4, 2025.
