**Who Can Request Images?**


- Members of the media, for public media publication purposes
- Elected and appointed members of Local, State and Federal Governments and their agencies
- Publishers of curriculum-based educational material


**How to Request Permission and Images**


- Note the multimedia titles which you would like to request.
- Click on '[Request Images](https://news.alcoa.com/media-center/photo-request-form/default.aspx) ' link here

- On approval of your request, you will receive high-resolution images by email.
