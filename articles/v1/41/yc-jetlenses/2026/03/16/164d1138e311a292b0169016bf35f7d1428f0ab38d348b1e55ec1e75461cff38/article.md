## Our Clinician Results


At Verse, we strive to improve clinician experience in all that we do. In 2025, we introduced new software features and optimized care delivery, so our clinician partners could focus on spending time with patients.


### 1. EMR Integrations


Clinicians at hospitals and clinics can submit and track orders within their existing EMR more easily than ever. This was a big driver of our 95% facility retention rate.


### 2. Payor-Specific Coverage


Verse flags coverage criteria and quantity limits during order submission, enabling clinicians to ensure order compliance, reduce back-and-forth, and deliver on-time patient care.


### 3. Verse AI


Verse AI extracts demographic, diagnosis, and coverage details from the medical record. This reduces average time to order to 1-2 minutes and enables a 99.9% order accuracy rate.
