A channel manager keeps your calendars across Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com synchronized in real time so that a confirmed booking on one platform immediately blocks those dates everywhere else. Without it, the gap between a booking confirmation and the next iCal refresh is exactly where double bookings happen. A double booking doesn't just inconvenience hosts; it triggers automatic negative reviews, financial penalties, and potential account suspension.


API-connected channel managers close that window entirely. iCal-based syncs do not. One pricing reality worth naming alongside calendar sync: hosts using a PMS are subject to Airbnb's fee model on their own terms.
