# Best AI Messaging Tools for Aparthotels in 2026

*Written by Punn Kam, ex-Google AI engineer and Y Combinator repeat founder. He is the co-founder of Conduit, an AI agent platform built for hospitality, serving 300+ hospitality brands in 140+ languages. Last updated August 15, 2026.*


The best AI messaging tool for an aparthotel is one that can hold two things at once: hotel-grade brand control (one persona, one set of SOPs) and apartment-length stay logic (weekly housekeeping, utilities, residential rules, building-specific access). Guests stay long enough to ask apartment questions and still expect a hotel to answer them, and corporate bookers call while leisure guests WhatsApp. Most tools were built for one side or the other.

## What should an aparthotel require?


- Hotel brand control: one persona, one SOP set, reused across buildings.
- Apartment-length inventory: weekly housekeeping schedules, utilities, residential rules, parcel handling.
- Voice *and* messaging on one agent. Corporate bookers call; leisure guests WhatsApp; both should reach the same brain.
- A property layer that can hold building-specific vendors and access rules without forking the agent.
- PMS read *and* write, so a stay extension or a mid-stay clean is booked in the call rather than noted for the morning.

## FAQ


### What is the best AI messaging tool for serviced apartments?


The one that holds hotel-grade brand control and apartment-length stay logic at the same time. Conduit if you need voice and text on one agent across more than one building. Duve if the guest app is the product. HiJiffy if website chat is the gap.


### Do aparthotels need voice AI?


Usually yes. Corporate bookers and extended-stay guests still call, especially for extensions, access problems, and invoices. A messaging-only tool leaves that traffic on voicemail.


### Can a hotel chatbot handle aparthotel guests?


For website questions, yes. For a three-week stay with weekly cleans, utilities, and building access rules, a single-site chatbot usually cannot hold the property-level facts, and it cannot change the reservation.


### How much does AI messaging for aparthotels cost?


Conduit publishes $18 per unit per month for Messaging, including inbound and outbound Voice AI, rising to $48 per unit per month for Full Suite. Duve, HiJiffy, Canary, and ALICE do not publish list pricing.


### Can one tool cover aparthotels and hotels in the same group?


Yes, if it has a brand layer and a property layer, so the hotel and the aparthotel share an agent and differ only in local facts. That is the same structure hotel groups use to roll out across brands.
