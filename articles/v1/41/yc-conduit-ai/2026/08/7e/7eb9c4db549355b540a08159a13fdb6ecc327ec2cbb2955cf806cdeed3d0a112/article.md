Score every app on two axes. **Distribution reach** measures how many booking platforms it connects to and how large the traveler audience is. **Operational depth** measures automation rule sophistication, AI-driven response speed, API openness, and dynamic pricing connectivity.


An app that scores high on reach but low on depth fills your calendar while quietly eroding margin on every booking, a dynamic consistently reported by operators who prioritize distribution reach over operational depth.
