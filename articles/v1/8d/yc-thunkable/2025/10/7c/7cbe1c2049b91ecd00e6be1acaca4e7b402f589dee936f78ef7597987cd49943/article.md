# Thunkable Named to the 2026 GSV 150 - Second Year Running

## 2026 GSV 150 Recipient


We’re proud to announce that Thunkable has been named to the[2026 GSV 150](https://hubs.li/Q03NXnfG0) for the second year in a row. Selected from more than 3,000 global companies, the GSV 150 recognizes organizations that are most transformational in digital learning and workforce skills.


This year’s list collectively surpasses $50 billion in annual revenue, reaches over 3 billion learners, and shows stronger profitability. The list spans K–12, workforce learning, higher education, and consumer learning, with representation from across the world.


## Why This Matters


Thunkable’s mission is to empower everyone, regardless of technical background, to bring ideas to life through app creation. Low-code/no-code tools are expanding access to learning and enabling creators to build real, functional products with ease. Being named to the GSV 150 again validates our mission and the global impact our community is delivering.


## What We’re Proud Of


- Two years running on the GSV 150 for sustained growth and educational impact.
- Continued product momentum reflected in recent platform updates and design-first improvements.
- A growing, global community using Thunkable to build apps that solve real problems in classrooms, small businesses, and beyond.


## What’s Next


- Enhanced Gen AI Prompt Builder: Our AI-powered project creation now understands natural-language inputs and produces editable, working prototypes in minutes. Users get real, testable apps to customize and ship quickly.
- Improved Visuals and Design System: Modernized default UI components and better image generation make Thunkable apps look and feel native and professionally designed right out of the box.


This launch is the first step in a continued push to make app creation more accessible, design-forward, and AI-assisted.


## Thank You


This recognition belongs to the thousands of creators, educators, partners, and team members who use and build with Thunkable. We’re honored to be part of a community shaping the future of learning and work, and we can’t wait to see what you create next.
