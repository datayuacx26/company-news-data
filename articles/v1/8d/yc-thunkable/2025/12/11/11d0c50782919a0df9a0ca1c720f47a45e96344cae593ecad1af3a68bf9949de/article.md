# 2025 Thunkable No-Code App of the Year

### **2025 App of the Year**


### **Rukus Flashcards**


Creator: Brooke Muse


Built using Thunkable by Brooke Muse, Rukus shows how a focused idea can go from concept to App Store without a large team or traditional software engineering. Rather than locking users into rigid lessons or forcing them to stare at chord diagrams mid-song, Rukus helps players internalize chord shapes and transitions by briefly showing diagrams and then removing them, leaving only the chord letter on screen while you play. That small design choice encourages muscle memory, smoother transitions, and more confident playing.


Built during feeding sessions and baby naps, Brooke and Rukus reflect the best of Thunkable’s community of creators.


Get Rukus on[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rukus-guitar-chord-flashcards/id6746895141) and[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=rukus.flashchords)

### **BalCoNes**


**Turning a city into a living story**


Built by Mireia Garcia Sevilla, BalCoNes is a geolocation storytelling app that invites users to discover Barcelona through the voices of people who once lived there.


BalCoNes uses geolocation and proximity alerts to trigger stories at precise locations. The experience unfolds naturally as users move through the city, revealing personal histories tied to both famous landmarks and overlooked corners of Barcelona.


Available in Spanish and English, BalCoNes demonstrates how creators use Thunkable to showcase the cities they love.


Get BalCoNes on[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/balcones/id6748852682) and[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gmail.mireiagarcia40.balcones)


‍


### **TeamTime**


**Attendance tracking made simple**


TeamTime is an attendance and time-tracking app built for small businesses and growing teams.


Using QR-code clock-ins, optional GPS verification, automated logs, and exportable reports, TeamTime shows how businesses can replace spreadsheets, paper timesheets, and manual systems. Built for both web and mobile, TeamTime is an example real software for the business world.


Get TeamTime on[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gmail.mauriziopolverini89.teamtime&pli=1)
