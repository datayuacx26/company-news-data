# Build Smarter, Look Better: A New Era of App Creation Begins on Thunkable

**By Arun Saigal, Co-Founder & CEO of Thunkable**


At Thunkable, we believe that building your own app should be fast, fun, and beautifully functional. It should feel like you're turning your ideas into something real—not wrestling with a blank screen or hunting for code snippets.


That’s why I’m thrilled to announce today’s launch:


Our enhanced **Gen AI Prompt Builder** and **visual design upgrades** are now live.


This launch is more than just a set of features. It’s a step forward in how you bring your imagination to life—and how polished your final product looks and feels.


### **What’s New**


#### **Enhanced Gen AI Prompt Builder**


We’ve buckled down and improved our AI-powered project creation capabilties to the next level. Now, when you tell Thunkable what kind of app you want to build, the new prompt builder:


- Understands natural-language inputs


- Gives you editable, working prototypes instantly


Whether you want a fitness tracker or a tool for your classroom, our Gen AI now builds you a *real app* —not just a concept or a few components. You’re minutes away from something you can test, customize, and share.


#### **Improved Visuals and Design System**


Let’s be honest: how your app *looks* matters just as much as how it works. That’s why we’re rolling out:


- Modernized default UI components


- Better image generation


Thunkable apps now look and feel like native, professionally designed products right out of the box. Whether you're a student, entrepreneur, or developer, your app will *wow* .


### **What’s Next?**


This launch is just the beginning. We’re continuing to improve how you co-create with AI and how you ship apps that look and feel exceptional—without hiring a designer or developer.


Try the new AI builder.Design something beautiful. And build something real.


We can’t wait to see what you create.
