# From Idea to App Store: Meet Thunkable AI, the Fastest Way to Build and Launch Mobile Apps

What if building a mobile app was as simple as describing your idea?


Today, we’re excited to introduce **Thunkable AI** - our most powerful step yet toward making app creation accessible to everyone. Whether you’re a creator, entrepreneur, educator, or part of a large enterprise team, Thunkable AI turns your ideas into fully functional, native mobile apps - no coding required.


##### **A New Era of App Creation**


Since the early days of the app economy, building a mobile app has required a mix of technical expertise, design knowledge, and patience to navigate complex publishing processes. For many people with great ideas, those barriers were simply too high.


The “I wish there was an app for” dream remained unfulfilled.


Thunkable was built to change that, and now deploys conversational AI to make it easier than ever.


By combining the latest advancements in generative AI with over a decade of no-code innovation, we’ve created an end-to-end platform that empowers anyone with access to a device to build, refine, and publish native iOS and Android apps.


With more than **12 million published apps already built on Thunkable** , we’re doubling down on our mission: turning passive consumers of technology into active creators.


##### **Focus on Your Idea, Not the Code**


Thunkable AI is designed so you can spend your time where it matters most - on your idea.


Instead of worrying about programming languages or infrastructure, you can:


- Turn a concept into a working app in minutes
- Iterate and improve your app in real time
- Publish directly to app stores without complex workflows


##### **More Than a Prototype: Real Native Mobile Apps**


While many AI tools stop at prototypes or web-apps, Thunkable AI goes further. Every app you build is:


- Fully native (not just a wrapper or mockup)
- Ready for real users
- Publishable to Apple and Google app stores


And we don’t just help you build - we handle the publishing, from submission to updates, streamlining one of the most challenging parts of app development.


##### **How Thunkable AI Works**


Here’s how simple it is:


*1. Generate Your Project*


Start by describing your app idea in plain language. Thunkable AI instantly translates the prompt into a functional starting point.


*2. Build and Refine*


Use our visual editor and logic tools to customize your app’s design and functionality. Need help? Discuss Mode lets you collaborate with AI in real time - just tell it what you want to change.


*3. Publish with Ease*


When you’re ready, publish your app directly to the Apple App Store and Google Play. No extra tools, no complicated steps - just a seamless path to launch.


##### **Built for Everyone**


Thunkable AI is for:


- First-time builders and hobbyists
- Creators and solopreneurs
- Startup teams
- Educators and students
- Enterprise teams - including companies in the Fortune 100


In fact, **80% of our users are non-developers** - proof that you don’t need technical experience to build something powerful.


##### **The Future of Creation Is Here**


We believe AI should do more than automate - it should unlock creativity and expand access to opportunity.


Now, anyone with a device and an idea can create an app or launch a business. No gatekeepers. No coding barriers. Just your imagination and the tools to bring it to life.
