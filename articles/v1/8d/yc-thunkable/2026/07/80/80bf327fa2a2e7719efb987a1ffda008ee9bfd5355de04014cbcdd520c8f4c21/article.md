# Thunkable Is Heading to WV ACTE

# Thunkable is excited to be attending the West Virginia Association for Career and Technical Education (WV ACTE) Conference on August 3–4.


###### We can't wait to connect with the educators, administrators, and counselors who are shaping the future of CTE across the Mountain State.


‍


## **Why We're Showing Up**


CTE educators are on the front lines of preparing students for a workforce that's changing faster than ever, and AI is one of the biggest forces driving that change.


At Thunkable, we've spent the past year working directly with classrooms across West Virginia through our AI Education Pilot Program, helping students move from consuming technology to creating it. WV ACTE brings together exactly the community we want to continue learning from and building alongside: the teachers and administrators who see firsthand what students need to succeed.


## **What We'll Be Sharing**


Here's a preview of what we'll be talking about at the conference:


### **📚 Our AI Education Pilot Results**


We'll share what we learned from bringing hands-on AI literacy, entrepreneurship, and app development into high schools across Monongalia, Kanawha, and Berkeley counties, and highlight what nearly **120 students** created through the program.


### **📱 How Thunkable Works in the Classroom**


See how our no-code, AI-powered platform helps students go from an idea to a real, functional mobile app, all without needing a coding background.


### **🚀 What's Next**


We'll also talk about how we're thinking about expanding accessible AI and entrepreneurship education to more schools, more students, and more communities across West Virginia.


## **Come Say Hello!**


If you're attending **WV ACTE** , we'd love to meet you.


Whether you're curious about bringing Thunkable into your classroom, want to hear more about our AI Education Pilot Program, or simply want to chat about Career and Technical Education, AI, and the future of work, stop by and say hello. We'll be there throughout both days and would love to connect.


We're proud to be part of a community that is so passionate about preparing West Virginia's students for what's ahead, and we're looking forward to two inspiring days of learning, sharing ideas, and meeting educators from across the state.
