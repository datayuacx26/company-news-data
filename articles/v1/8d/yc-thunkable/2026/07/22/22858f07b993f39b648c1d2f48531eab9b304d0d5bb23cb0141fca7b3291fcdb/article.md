# February 2026 Product Updates

In February we introduced exciting updates to the Thunkable platform. We just launched three features designed to help you build polished apps faster and with more confidence.


- Make **faster edits** without long build times
- **Restore** earlier versions of your app
- Use **custom images** using our searchable library or upload your own


Here’s a closer look at what’s new and how these updates can improve your workflow.


‍


### **Fast Edits**


Making quick tweaks shouldn’t slow you down. With our new **Fast Editing** feature, you can make small updates without waiting through a full rebuild.


Use Fast Edits when you’re making lightweight changes such as:


- Updating text or copy
- Adjusting layout or UI elements
- Fixing small visual issues
- Testing minor improvements


### **Image Generation**


Generate apps with built-in visuals from your very first prompt. Your initial app now includes images automatically, and you can update, replace, or add at any time using Unsplash or your own uploads.


You can:


- Generate new apps that include relevant images
- Replace or update visuals at any time
- Browse and insert images from Unsplash
- Upload and use your own custom images


### **Version History**


With Version History, you can review and restore earlier versions of your project at any time. That means you can test new ideas, redesign flows, or try bold changes without worrying about losing work.


Version History allows you to:


- View previous versions of your app
- Restore a prior version instantly
- Experiment freely without risk
- Maintain confidence as your app evolves
