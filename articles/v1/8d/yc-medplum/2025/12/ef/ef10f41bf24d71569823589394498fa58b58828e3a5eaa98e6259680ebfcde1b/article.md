# Medplum Year in Review 2025

An incredible year of growth, innovation, and community. From v5 release to expanding our developer ecosystem, 2025 marked several milestones for Medplum's open-source healthcare platform.


As we close out 2025, the Medplum team would love to thank our community and customers for joining us on this journey.


We wanted to highlight a few memorable moments and reflect on all that happened during the year. It was a lot of fun, and huge thank you to the team who pushed so hard to make all these things happen.


✅ Released **[Medplum v5](https://www.medplum.com/blog/v5-release)** , our most significant platform modernization to date, featuring full FHIR terminology validation, GraphQL Patch support, and comprehensive dependency upgrades to Node.js 22/24, PostgreSQL 14/18, and React 19


✅ Launched our **new brand identity** , reflecting our evolution as a leading open-source healthcare infrastructure platform. See the details[here](https://www.medplum.com/brand) .


✅ Hosted our first annual developer conference **[PlumCon 2025](https://www.medplum.com/blog/plumcon-2025-materials)** in San Francisco, bringing together healthcare innovators from around the world


✅ Delivered **many new features and integrations** , including enhanced AI capabilities with[MCP server support](https://www.medplum.com/blog/unlocking-healthcare-ai-medplum-support-mcp) ,[custom FHIR operations](https://www.medplum.com/blog/custom-fhir-operations) ,[IHE IRA radiology reporting certification](https://www.medplum.com/blog/ihe-ira-radiology-reporting) , and expanded integrations with health information exchange, medications, and revenue cycle.


✅ A special thank you to those who contributed this year - we now have **157 talented contributors** from around the world building the future of healthcare together


✅ Incredible ecosystem growth: **1,462 pull requests** , **55 releases** , **551 issues resolved** , **2.1K GitHub stars** , and **500K+ Docker downloads** powering healthcare applications globally


✅ Added many wonderful customers, and several have made videos of their implementations. See[case studies](https://www.medplum.com/case-studies) and[PlumCon recap](https://www.medplum.com/blog/plumcon-2025-materials#demo-showcase) for videos.


✅ Organized **[several events](https://www.medplum.com/blog/events-calendar)** throughout this year, including[FHIR + AI for Life Sciences](https://www.medplum.com/blog/fhir-ai-for-life-sciences) ,[Scheduling Agents for Healthcare Operations](https://www.medplum.com/blog/scheduling-agents-unity-ai) , and[Visualizing Patient Flow](https://youtu.be/QvC093fAGGU) , building our community of healthcare developers


Thank you, dear reader, for being part of our community. See you[on Discord](https://discord.gg/medplum) .
