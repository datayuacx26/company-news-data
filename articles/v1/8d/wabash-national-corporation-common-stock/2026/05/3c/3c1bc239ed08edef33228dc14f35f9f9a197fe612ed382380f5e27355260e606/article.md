# WABASH RECOGNIZES OUTSTANDING SUPPLIERS FOR 2025

*Lafayette, Ind. – May 26, 2026* – **Wabash (NYSE: WNC)** , a leader in end-to-end supply chain solutions for the transportation, logistics and infrastructure markets, recognizes 37 of its top suppliers with 2025 Wabash Supplier Awards for supply chain excellence.


Each year, Wabash presents awards to top suppliers for excellence in supply chain performance, considering criteria such as innovation, quality, delivery, cost and service. The company’s highest honor is its Pinnacle Award, which recognizes the company’s supplier of the year. This year’s Pinnacle Award winner is Goodyear of Akron, Ohio.


“At Wabash, our capacity to drive meaningful innovation starts with the partners we choose to work alongside," said Richard Mansilla, vice president, global supply chain for Wabash. Goodyear has consistently demonstrated what true partnership looks like — bringing forward-thinking ideas, tackling difficult problems with us, and playing an integral role in the quality and dependability our customers rely on. It is a pleasure to recognize them with our most prestigious supplier award.”


Goodyear, founded more than 125 years ago, is one of the world’s largest tire companies and a global leader in tire innovation and performance. The company delivers a comprehensive portfolio of premium tires, retreads and integrated solutions designed to help fleets reduce downtime, improve fuel efficiency, and optimize total cost of ownership. This award marks Goodyear’s fifth overall Wabash supplier award and its first Pinnacle Award.


“Goodyear’s commercial business is built on a foundation of premium products, advanced technology and a dependable supply and service network that help fleets operate with confidence,” stated Joe Stuglis, Goodyear’s vice president of commercial sales for North America. “Our extensive portfolio, combined with the performance and reliability our customers expect, positions us to support commercial equipment manufacturers across the industry. Being recognized by Wabash is an honor and a meaningful reflection of the trust we have earned over more than two decades of working together. We are grateful for this recognition and look forward to continuing our collaboration."


Thirty-two companies earned Platinum Awards for excellence in supply chain performance. This select group of suppliers has demonstrated an ongoing commitment to excellence and logistics optimization that support Wabash’s growth and accelerating innovation.
