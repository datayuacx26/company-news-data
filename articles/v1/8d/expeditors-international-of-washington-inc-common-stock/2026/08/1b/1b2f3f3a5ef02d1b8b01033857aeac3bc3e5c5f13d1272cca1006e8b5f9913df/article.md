# Enhancing Customs Operations Through Visibility, Reporting, and Analytics

For Customs and Compliance teams, staying on top of import activity requires greater visibility and control than ever before. Customs and Compliance professionals are expected to monitor declarations in progress, understand the financial impact of tariffs and duties, prepare stakeholder reports, and support compliance requirements, often while managing large volumes of data across multiple systems.


These responsibilities can create operational challenges that affect visibility, decision-making, and day-to-day efficiency. Through customer feedback and industry engagement, Expeditors has identified several common pain points faced by Customs and Compliance teams:


-


Limited visibility into declaration status and potential delays


-


Difficulty understanding tariff and duty impacts


-


Time-consuming reporting and audit preparation


-


Increasing compliance requirements and oversight responsibilities


While every organization faces unique challenges, these issues consistently impact the ability of Customs and Compliance teams to make informed decisions, respond quickly to disruptions, and maintain confidence in their customs operations.


Addressing Customer Challenges Through EXP.O NOW


To help address these challenges, Expeditors continues to enhance[EXP.O NOW](https://www.expeditors.com/technology/expo-now) with solutions designed to improve visibility, simplify reporting, support analysis of import tariffs and duties, and strengthen compliance oversight.


-


Declarations by Status Panel - The Declarations by Status Panel gives Customs and Compliance teams a real-time view of declaration progress throughout the clearance process. By bringing status updates into a single dashboard, customers can quickly identify declarations that require attention or may be at risk of delays. This visibility helps teams respond proactively, improve operational efficiency, and keep goods moving as planned.


-


EXP.O NOW Tariff Calculator - The[Tariff Calculator](https://www.expeditors.com/technology/expo-now/tariff-calculator) helps users understand U.S. duty rates and the financial impact of trade actions on imported goods. Organizations can compare U.S. duty costs across countries of origin, evaluate sourcing options, and improve import forecasting. By identifying applicable U.S. duties and tariffs in advance, the tool supports more informed decisions, greater cost visibility, and stronger compliance planning.


-


EXP.O NOW Visibility Reports - Visibility Reports provide on-demand access to customs data through self-service and scheduled reporting capabilities. Customers can easily access declaration and invoice-level details to support internal analysis, compliance reviews, and audit preparation. By centralizing key customs information, Visibility Reports help improve oversight and enable more confident decision-making.


-


EXP.O NOW Analytics - EXP.O NOW Analytics provides dashboard-driven insights across Transportation, Customs Brokerage, and Order Management data. Through Brokerage Timing Analysis and Duty Recovery Analysis, customers can identify process inefficiencies, evaluate performance, uncover duty recovery opportunities, and track refund progress. By bringing operational and financial insights together in one place, Analytics helps customers make more informed decisions and optimize customs performance.


Across visibility, reporting, tariff and duty management, and analytics,[Expeditors](https://www.expeditors.com/services/customs-compliance/customs-brokerage) continues to enhance EXP.O NOW with capabilities designed to support Customs and Compliance operations.


By providing greater access to data, insights, and self-service tools, EXP.O NOW improves visibility, strengthens compliance efforts, and supports more informed decision-making. Expeditors remains committed to delivering solutions that provide greater efficiency, transparency, and control across customs operations.
