# Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) for Reliable Automotive Production

In the[Automotive & Mobility](https://www.expeditors.com/capabilities/industries/automotive-mobility) industry, production environments operate with minimal tolerance for disruption. Tight build schedules, complex global sourcing, and just‑in‑time (JIT) manufacturing models require supply chains that are stable, predictable, and highly visible.


Expeditors’ Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) solution is designed to support these needs by ensuring critical components are positioned, managed, and delivered to match production demand. Our VMI solution helps manufacturers, suppliers, and OEMs reduce variability, protect continuous production, and maintain control across complex supply chains.


Why VMI Matters in Automotive & Mobility


Automotive and mobility supply chains operate under intense pressure; small disruptions can have major impacts. These supply chains are characterized by:


-


High part volumes and complexity


-


Global supplier networks


-


Minimal buffer inventory


-


Strict production scheduling


Even minor disruptions can result in line stoppages or costly recovery efforts. Expeditors’ VMI is built to stabilize lead times, ensure just-in-time delivery, and reduce the impact of demand swings, while maintaining compliance and visibility across borders.


Key Stakeholder Benefits


Automotive OEMs experience greater production continuity and more reliable just-in-time performance. Inventory is available exactly when needed, without tying up excess working capital. Part-level visibility and synchronized plant delivery make your operations more predictable and efficient.


Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers keep ownership of their inventory until it’s used, with improved insights into demand and usage. This helps reduce forecasting risk and limits the need for multiple regional storage facilities.


Mobility and emerging manufacturers benefit from scalable, flexible support during launch and ramp-up phases. The model adapts as volumes evolve and reliably manages complex, globally sourced parts.


VMI Enables Just-in-Time Delivery


We position inventory close to OEM plants and key manufacturing centers. This reduces transit times, supports frequent deliveries aligned with your production schedule, and allows manufacturers to keep lower buffer stocks while staying responsive to changes.


Through integrated systems, Expeditors ensures your automotive supply chain runs smoothly. JIT production depends on five things happening together:


-


Inventory Positioned Near Production:


VMI hubs close to OEM plants reduce transit time and support regular, production-aligned deliveries.


-


Real-Time Inventory Visibility:


Get shared, real-time insight into inventory levels, movement, and consumption.


-


Consumption-Based Replenishment:


Inventory is replenished using agreed min/max and safety stock parameters.


-


Integrated Logistics and Customs Management:


[Transportation](https://www.expeditors.com/services/transportation) ,[customs](https://www.expeditors.com/services/customs-compliance/customs-brokerage) ,[warehousing](https://www.expeditors.com/services/warehousing-distribution) , and delivery unified in one model.


-


Production-Ready Handling:


Services such as repacking, kitting, inspections, and returnable packaging are managed from the VMI hub.


By combining local inventory positioning, real-time visibility, and comprehensive logistics integration, we help you achieve true just-in-time production. With Expeditors’ VMI, your supply chain remains agile, efficient, and ready to meet evolving production demands without disruption.
