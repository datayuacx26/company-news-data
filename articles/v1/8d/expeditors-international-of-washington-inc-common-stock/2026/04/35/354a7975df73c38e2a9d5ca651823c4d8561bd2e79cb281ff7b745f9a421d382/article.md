# Temperature Controlled Logistics, Executed with Precision in Chicago

Temperature‑controlled logistics leaves little margin for error. When products are sensitive to time, handling, and environmental conditions, consistency and control matter at every point in the supply chain.


Expeditors’ temperature‑controlled facility in Chicago reflects that reality. Located in one of North America’s key logistics hubs, the operation plays a critical role in supporting customers that depend on disciplined processes, reliable execution, and seamless coordination across global trade lanes.


**Chicago as a Key Hub for Temperature‑Sensitive Cargo**


Chicago sits at the intersection of major air, ground, and international freight networks, making it a natural gateway for temperature‑sensitive shipments moving across regions and borders. For customers managing products that cannot tolerate delays or variability, proximity to capacity and connectivity is essential.


The Chicago temperature‑controlled facility is designed to support GDP compliance, offering 33,


000 sq ft of +15°C to +25°C and an additional 10,000 sq ft of +2°C to +8°C storage space. These environments are supported by specialized teams trained on cGDP, embedded into daily operations, helping maintain required conditions throughout key transfer points. Temperature‑sensitive freight is managed through structured workflows that reduce exposure risk and support continuity as shipments move through the supply chain.


Standardized operating procedures guide how shipments are received, staged, and prepared for onward movement, supporting consistent outcomes across a range of shipment profiles. Visibility and communication are also central to the operation, enabling proactive coordination and timely exception management to support informed customer decision‑making.


**Chicago’s Role Within Expeditors’ Global Network**


The value of the Chicago facility extends beyond its local footprint. It operates as an integrated node within Expeditors’ broader temperature‑controlled network, connecting regional execution with global trade lanes.


Across regions, Expeditors supports temperature‑sensitive cargo through temperature‑controlled facilities, air and ocean freight solutions aligned to product requirements, and ground transportation options designed to maintain continuity between nodes. This connected approach allows customers to scale temperature‑controlled strategies while maintaining consistent standards across markets.


By linking operations in Chicago with a global network of capabilities, Expeditors helps customers manage complexity without sacrificing control or reliability.


**Supporting Temperature‑Controlled Supply Chains**


Customers relying on temperature‑controlled logistics span multiple industries, each with unique regulatory requirements and risk considerations. Expeditors’ approach is built around adapting processes to product needs rather than forcing products into generic workflows.


Our network facilities support a range of shipment profiles where product integrity is critical and supply chain disruption can carry significant downstream impact. Combined with Expeditors’ expertise and global footprint, this capability helps customers navigate changing market conditions with confidence.


**A People‑Led Approach to Precision Logistics**


Facilities and infrastructure matter, but temperature‑controlled logistics ultimately depends on people. Expeditors’ people‑led model emphasizes training, accountability, and local decision‑making, all of which are essential in environments where precision is required.


Across Expeditors’ global organization, teams are empowered to manage exceptions, communicate early, and coordinate across functions to keep shipments moving as planned.


**Designed to Protect What Matters Most**


The Chicago temperature‑controlled facility demonstrates how Expeditors brings global capability to life through local execution. By combining disciplined processes, experienced teams, and a connected temperature‑controlled network, Expeditors is well positioned to support customers with complex, temperature‑sensitive supply chain needs.
