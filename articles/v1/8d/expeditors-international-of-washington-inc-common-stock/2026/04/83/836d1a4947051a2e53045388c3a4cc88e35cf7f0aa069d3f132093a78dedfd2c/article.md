# Connecting Global Trade Through Mumbai: India’s Trade Hub [VIDEO]

As India continues to strengthen its role in the global economy, Mumbai remains a critical gateway for trade. Home to major financial institutions, international connectivity, and one of the country’s busiest ports, the city plays a central role in how goods move between India and global markets. For businesses operating here, supply chains must be built for scale, speed, and adaptability.


**A Regional Presence with Global Reach**


Expeditors’ presence in Mumbai reflects the importance of this region. With teams located within and across the surrounding region, including Pune, Indore, Nagpur, and Vapi, Expeditors supports customers across a wide range of industries navigating both domestic and international trade flows. This regional footprint allows teams to stay close to customers while remaining connected to a global network.


**Connectivity at the Core of Trade**


Connectivity is a defining advantage of the region. Proximity to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport and the Jawaharlal Nehru Port (Nava Sheva) enables efficient multimodal movement, supporting time-sensitive shipments and complex routing strategies. As Mumbai and neighboring cities continue to grow as industrial and technology hubs, supply chain requirements across the region continue to evolve.


**Infrastructure and Expertise Supporting Growth**


To support that growth, infrastructure and local expertise play a key role. Strategic warehouse locations and a nationwide distribution network allow businesses to position inventory effectively and respond to shifting demand. Combined with deep regional knowledge, Expeditors helps customers manage complexity and move cargo with confidence.
