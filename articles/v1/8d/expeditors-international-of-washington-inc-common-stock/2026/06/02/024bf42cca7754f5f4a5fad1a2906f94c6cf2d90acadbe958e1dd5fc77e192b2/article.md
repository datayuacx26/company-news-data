# Expeditors Customs: Celebrating 45 Years of Trusted Solutions [VIDEO]

Expeditors Customs marks 45 years of supporting global supply chains through constant change. This milestone offers an opportunity to reflect on how the industry has evolved and where it is heading next.


Across decades of evolving regulations and growing complexity, one priority has remained constant: helping goods move across borders efficiently, compliantly, and with clarity, while maintaining a long-term focus on what matters most to our customers.


Technology continues to reshape execution, improving speed and reducing manual effort. But technology alone is not enough; experienced teams provide the judgment and context required to navigate increasingly complex global trade environments and to continue earning the role of trusted advisor to customers through change.


Resilience Through Every Challenge


Customs has evolved alongside global trade. What began as manual, locally driven processes is now a fast-paced, data-driven discipline shaped by shifting regulations and global events.


Despite this transformation, the fundamentals; compliance, operational discipline, and practical experience remain essential. Deep institutional knowledge continues to provide the context needed to manage and make informed decisions in an increasingly complex environment. That capability has been built through sustained investment in our employees over time, developing expertise, strengthening judgment, and training globally so teams can support customers consistently across regions.


We asked past and present Expeditors Customs product leaders to reflect on our journey and what’s ahead.

Reliable Partnership for What’s Next


Global supply chains will continue to face disruptions, driven by regulatory shifts, geopolitical change, and technological advancement.


While automation and data will play an increasing role, interpreting that data and applying it with sound judgment will remain critical.


Experience, collaboration, and expertise will continue to define how challenges are managed, balancing innovation with proven discipline.


After 45 years, the focus remains clear: adapting to change without compromising discipline, clarity, and a long-term perspective - for our customers, through continued investment in our people, and in the trusted advisor role we continue to earn globally.
