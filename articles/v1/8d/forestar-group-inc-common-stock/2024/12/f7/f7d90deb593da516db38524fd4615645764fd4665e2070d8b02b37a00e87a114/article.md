# Forestar Group Inc. to Release 2025 First Quarter Earnings on January 21, 2025

ARLINGTON, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Forestar Group Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.forestar.com%2F&esheet=54161431&newsitemid=20241203151402&lan=en-US&anchor=Forestar+Group+Inc.&index=1&md5=3965e430653451cf94d4e76d5796ca21) (NYSE: FOR) announced today that the Company will release financial results for its first quarter ended December 31, 2024 on Tuesday, January 21, 2025 before the market opens. The Company will host a conference call that morning at 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time (ET). The dial-in number is 888-506-0062. When calling, please reference access code 917712. Participants are encouraged to call in five minutes before the call begins (10:55 a.m. ET). The call will also be webcast from the Company’s website at[investor.forestar.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.forestar.com%2F&esheet=54161431&newsitemid=20241203151402&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.forestar.com&index=2&md5=cc8ea61db95e31a6ec1b4ca7d2082d69) .


A replay of the call will be available after 3:00 p.m. ET on Tuesday, January 21, 2025 at 877-481-4010. When calling, please reference replay passcode 51730. The teleconference replay will be available through January 28, 2025. The webcast replay will be available from the Company’s website at[investor.forestar.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.forestar.com%2F&esheet=54161431&newsitemid=20241203151402&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.forestar.com&index=3&md5=6614385236aa133ced79e21acdbdee66) through April 30, 2025.


**About Forestar Group Inc.**


Forestar Group Inc. is a residential lot development company with operations in 59 markets and 24 states. Based in Arlington, Texas, the Company delivered more than 15,000 residential lots during fiscal 2024. Forestar is a majority-owned subsidiary of[D.R. Horton, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.drhorton.com%2F&esheet=54161431&newsitemid=20241203151402&lan=en-US&anchor=D.R.+Horton%2C+Inc.&index=4&md5=a5266e9d9a303f30d460450bc8bf2f10) , the largest homebuilder by volume in the United States since 2002.
