# Forestar Group Inc. to Release 2025 Third Quarter Earnings on July 22, 2025

ARLINGTON, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- As previously announced,[Forestar Group Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.forestar.com%2F&esheet=54265368&newsitemid=20250604293384&lan=en-US&anchor=Forestar+Group+Inc.&index=1&md5=5be6b8e446db45bb8e02e28612e0b811) (NYSE: FOR) will release financial results for its third quarter ended June 30, 2025 on Tuesday, July 22, 2025 before the market opens. The Company will host a conference call that morning at 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time (ET). The dial-in number is 888-506-0062. When calling, please reference access code 716713. Participants are encouraged to call in five minutes before the call begins (10:55 a.m. ET). The call will also be webcast from the Company’s website at[investor.forestar.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.forestar.com%2F&esheet=54265368&newsitemid=20250604293384&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.forestar.com&index=2&md5=5bfde91ef4ac325dcafc19aa935153d0) .


A replay of the call will be available after 3:00 p.m. ET on Tuesday, July 22, 2025 at 877-481-4010. When calling, please reference replay passcode 52408. The teleconference replay will be available through July 29, 2025. The webcast replay will be available from the Company’s website at[investor.forestar.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.forestar.com%2F&esheet=54265368&newsitemid=20250604293384&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.forestar.com&index=3&md5=b86af77ea019118d64180b5faab302cc) through November 15, 2025.


**About Forestar Group Inc.**


Forestar Group Inc. is a residential lot development company with operations in 65 markets and 24 states. Based in Arlington, Texas, the Company delivered more than 14,300 residential lots during the twelve-month period ended March 31, 2025. Forestar is a majority-owned subsidiary of[D.R. Horton, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.drhorton.com%2F&esheet=54265368&newsitemid=20250604293384&lan=en-US&anchor=D.R.+Horton%2C+Inc.&index=4&md5=ef5eee12566986a4307aee7790740af3) , the largest homebuilder by volume in the United States since 2002.
