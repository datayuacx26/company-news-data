## Meet our Product Manager LKQ RHIAG Italy Carolina Zucco


In my job, I drive two very different roads at the same time. By doing this, I’m building connections between the branch operators and suppliers. The product categories I’m responsible for represent a mix of what LKQ is all about – one part heritage and experience, but also leading innovation and looking to the future. I’m able to see some products from the initial stages to selling – in addition to gaining insight into trade, supply chain and customer service elements in between. So it’s important that I remain agile and efficient to quickly analyze and map the market, and understand customer trends and behaviours. I’m committed to making a difference on both a strategic and operational level. And I’m glad that I can feel proud about the projects I work on, which contribute to creating a more unified company, while keeping the unique strengths of our regions. Together, we are stronger.
