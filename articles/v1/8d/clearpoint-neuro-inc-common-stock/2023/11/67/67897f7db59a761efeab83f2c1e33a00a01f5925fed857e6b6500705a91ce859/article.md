#### From Joe Burnett, President & CEO:


As we move into the last few months of the year, I'm delighted to share some highlights from this past quarter:


- **ClearPoint Prism™ Neuro Laser Therapy System:** We continue to execute on our limited market release and are in the process of collecting real-world experience for both epilepsy and brain tumors. To that end, the team from[Skåne](https://vard.skane.se/en/skane-university-hospital/) University Hospital[presented data at ESSFN from their initial experience with our non-cooled laser to treat patients with recurrent glioblastoma from the podium](https://clinicallaser.se/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ESSFN_Abstract_Laser-interstitial-thermal-therapy-using-an-uncooled-laser-catheter-in-a-diagnostic-MR-suite.pdf) .
- **Biologics and Drug Delivery:** Our strategy to build deeper and more strategic partnerships continues making progress resulting in a year-over-year growth of 55% in Q3. This growth is largely driven by our investments in tools and talent to add[clinical, development, regulatory and other pre-clinical CRO services to our portfolio](https://www.clearpointneuro.com/biologics-drug-delivery/) .

This new software release is the second since we introduced the Array platform in 2021, demonstrating our ongoing commitment to rapid software iteration, and incorporation of meaningful voice of customer feedback.
- **New Manufacturing Facility:** We completed the transition to our brand new manufacturing facility in Carlsbad, and have permanently shut down our previous facility in Irvine, ahead of schedule.

#### [Prism Laser Abstract ESSFN Podium Presentation](https://clinicallaser.se/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ESSFN_Abstract_Laser-interstitial-thermal-therapy-using-an-uncooled-laser-catheter-in-a-diagnostic-MR-suite.pdf)


On September 28, Dr. Hjálmar Bjartmarz and team from of[Skåne University Hospital](https://vard.skane.se/en/skane-university-hospital/) presented an abstract entitled: **[Laser interstitial therapy using an uncooled laser catheter in a diagnostic MR suite](https://clinicallaser.se/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ESSFN_Abstract_Laser-interstitial-thermal-therapy-using-an-uncooled-laser-catheter-in-a-diagnostic-MR-suite.pdf)** on the podium at the[XXVth Congress of the European Society for Stereotactic and Functional Neurosurgery (ESSFN)](https://essfncongress.org/) in Stockholm.


The abstract from the open-label, single center, early feasibility and safety phase 1-2 clinical trial featured results from 5 patients with recurrent glioblastoma. The team found that LITT, using a non-cooled laser catheter inside an outpatient MRI suite, was feasible and reproducible. They also concluded that the procedure may reduce risks associated with LITT procedures.


**[Read the Abstract](https://clinicallaser.se/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ESSFN_Abstract_Laser-interstitial-thermal-therapy-using-an-uncooled-laser-catheter-in-a-diagnostic-MR-suite.pdf)**


#### [Initial Clinical Experience with Intraputaminal Delivery of Upstaza using ClearPoint Platform Presented at ESGCT](https://www.clearpointneuro.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/ESGCT-2023-Abstract_Umberto-Policlinico-I.pdf)


Dr. Luca D'Angelo and team from[Sapienza Università di Roma](https://www.uniroma1.it/it) presented a poster at the European Society of Gene and Cell Therapy (ESGCT) held in October in Brussels.


The poster entitled: **[Our initial experience with intraputaminal delivery of Upstaza using MRI-guidance for cannula placement and confirmation of infusion target coverage](https://www.clearpointneuro.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/ESGCT-2023-Abstract_Umberto-Policlinico-I.pdf)** is the first peer-reviewed abstract documenting the clinical use of an approved, commercially-available gene therapy delivered through the SmartFlow cannula and guided by the ClearPoint navigation platform.
