# Avino Ranks 5th on 2025 TSX30

Avino is proud to announce that Avino has been included in the Toronto Stock Exchange’s 2025 TSX30™. Avino has distinguished itself by reaching the 5th position on the TSX30 2025 ranking, which is a flagship program recognizing the 30 top-performing TSX stocks on a dividend-adjusted share price appreciation over a three-year period.


‍


This recognition highlights our 610% share price growth and 778% increase in market capitalization, achievements made possible through the dedication of our team and commitment to transformational growth.
