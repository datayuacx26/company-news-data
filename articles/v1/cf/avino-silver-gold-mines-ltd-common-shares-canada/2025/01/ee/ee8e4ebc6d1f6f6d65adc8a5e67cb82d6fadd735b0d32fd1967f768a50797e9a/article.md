# What are Tailings?

In the world of mining, there's a term that might sound unfamiliar. Tailings.


‍


But what is it really? Let us explain it to you in a simple way. Tailings are the material left behind after valuable minerals have been extracted from the rock in a mine. It's a mixture of water, crushed and ground rock, and residual substances.


‍


While some may think that tailings are toxic waste, that's not the case. In reality, the majority of tailings are handled safely and responsibly. Before storage, tests are conducted to ensure they don't contain harmful substances.


‍


Now that you're informed about tailings, you can have confidence that mining companies are actively striving to safeguard both the environment and local communities. At Aino Silver and Gold Mines, we are committed to caring for the environment and our people.
