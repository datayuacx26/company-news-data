# March 1, 2024 About Copper Learn More Arrow Right

Did you know that copper plays an important role at the Avino Mine? In fact, copper production is a key part of the company’s overall profile, and recent updates to Avino’s Mineral Resource Estimate highlight its growing significance.


‍


##### Avino Property Highlights


Compared to the 2020 NI 43-101 Mineral Resource Estimate, Avino has reported significant increases across multiple metals, including copper:


161 million measured and indicated silver equivalent ounces (+38%), made up of:


70 million silver ounces (+35%)


136.7 thousand copper tonnes (+18%)


596 thousand gold ounces (+23%)


24.7 million measured and indicated metric tonnes (+28% overall)


70 million inferred silver equivalent ounces (+90%)


These results confirm copper’s rising role within Avino’s resource base.


‍


##### Copper’s Growing Importance


Beyond Avino, copper has gained wider recognition on the global stage. Recently, copper was added to the U.S. Critical Minerals List, acknowledging its crucial role in modern industries. This designation reflects copper’s importance for infrastructure, advanced technology, and especially the transition to sustainable energy.


Copper is essential in:


Power grids and renewable energy systems


Electric vehicles and batteries


Everyday technology like phones and computers
