Organizations are investing heavily in AI, automation, and digital transformation initiatives to improve efficiency, accelerate growth, and remain competitive. Yet many transformation efforts fall short because they attempt to automate complexity rather than eliminate it.


This whitepaper explores why process simplification is the critical first step to successful AI transformation. Drawing on industry research and real-world transformation experiences, it demonstrates how complex, fragmented workflows can reduce the effectiveness of automation, increase costs, and hinder adoption.


In this whitepaper, you'll learn:


- Why complexity is one of the biggest barriers to transformation success.
- How AI and automation can unintentionally scale inefficiencies when applied to broken processes.
- The business benefits of simplifying and standardizing workflows before transformation.
- A practical framework for sequencing simplification, standardization, AI adoption, and transformation initiatives.
- Lessons learned from large-scale ERP and digital transformation programs.


Download the whitepaper to discover how a simplification-first approach can help your organization maximize AI value, reduce risk, and build a stronger foundation for long-term transformation success.
