Global tax functions are under pressure from AI adoption, expanding e-invoicing and real-time reporting mandates, regulatory volatility, and shifting business models. The SAPinsider Global Tax Management benchmark report explores how tax and finance leaders are responding, and where expectations for tax technology are outpacing realized outcomes.


The research shows that global tax teams are prioritizing automation, standardized processes, master data remediation, and governance-first AI adoption. For organizations running SAP, the report highlights why connected tax platforms, clean tax-sensitized data, and stronger governance are essential to improving compliance accuracy, audit readiness, and operational efficiency.


Download the report to learn what SAP tax leaders are investing in, which capabilities matter most, and how organizations can close the gap between technology ambition and measurable business value.
