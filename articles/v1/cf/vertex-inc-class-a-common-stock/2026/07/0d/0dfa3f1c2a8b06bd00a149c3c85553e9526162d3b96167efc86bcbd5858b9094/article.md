# E-Invoicing in 2026: The Operational Reality for IT and Finance Teams

For IT and Finance teams, e invoicing is rarely the only priority. In 2026, it lands alongside ERP programs, automation work, data cleanup, and cost pressure—often with fixed timelines and little room to absorb disruption.
What sets e invoicing apart is where it cuts in. Mandates now sit inside core systems and shared processes, turning implementation choices into operational decisions. As organizations move from awareness to execution, many are finding complexity was underestimated, ownership was fragmented, and assumptions about ERP readiness no longer hold. What looked contained becomes harder to scale as requirements expand and change.


In this session, Vertex experts and industry practitioners will cover:


- the operational realities teams face in 2026,
- where complexity is surfacing during execution,
- how ERP, integration, and data decisions shape outcomes,
- and which choices made now affect readiness for what comes next.


Join us for practical lessons from active implementations and a clearer path from reactive delivery to sustained operational control.
