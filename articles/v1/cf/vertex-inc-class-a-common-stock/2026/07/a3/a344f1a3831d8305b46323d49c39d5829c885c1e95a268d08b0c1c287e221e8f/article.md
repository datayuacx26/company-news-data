# What Is Vertex O Series? Features, Benefits, and What Makes It Different

As your business grows, managing tax becomes more difficult. New products, new markets, and new systems often mean more tax rules to manage. What once worked in a single ERP or business application can become difficult to maintain across multiple systems.


If you're evaluating tax automation software, you may be asking: **What is Vertex O Series, and how is it different from other tax engines?**


Here's what it is, how it works, and what makes it different.


## What is Vertex O Series?


[Vertex O Series](https://www.vertexinc.com/solutions/products/vertex-indirect-tax-o-series) is an enterprise tax engine that automates and centralizes indirect tax determination. When a transaction is created—through an ERP, e-commerce platform, procurement application, billing system, or financial application - O Series evaluates key transaction details such as the product being sold, customer information, location data, and applicable tax rules. It then determines the appropriate tax and returns the result to the source system so the transaction can be completed.


With O Series, you have a single source of truth for tax across your business, helping reduce manual effort, improve consistency across systems, and support downstream compliance.


## What Makes Vertex O Series Different?


Vertex O Series helps you manage tax more consistently across systems. Instead of maintaining tax rules and content in multiple places, you can centralize tax determination in one platform.


- **Manage multiple tax obligations in one place** across sales tax (SUT), consumer use tax (CUT), value added tax (VAT), and industry-specific requirements (communications, retail, medical, leasing, etc.).
- **Stay current with changing tax rules** through monthly content updates that help keep rates, rules, and taxability logic aligned with evolving requirements.
- **Connect tax to the systems you already use** with 150+ integrations and technology[partnerships](https://www.vertexinc.com/partners/browse/filter/partner-type/technology-partners?page=0) across SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, NetSuite, Shopify, BigCommerce, Coupa, and other leading platforms. ERP-specific accelerators can help simplify implementation and integration, while robust APIs support systems without a prebuilt connector.
- **Make more accurate tax decisions** by evaluating 100+ transaction data elements and supporting company-specific taxability rules.
- **Scale as your business grows** with architecture and deployment options (cloud, on-premise, hybrid, and[edge](https://www.vertexinc.com/solutions/products/vertex-o-series-edge) ) designed to support your current needs and adapt as your tax complexity, systems, and operations evolve.
- **Maintain visibility and control** with built-in reporting, security, audit trails, and role-based access.
