# Trusted AI for Tax: Applying AI Across Tax Workflows

Artificial intelligence is creating new opportunities for tax teams, but successful adoption requires more than automation. It requires trust. Join Vertex experts as they discuss how organizations are applying AI across tax workflows to reduce manual effort, improve operational efficiency, and respond more effectively to evolving tax requirements while maintaining governance and confidence in the results.


During this webinar, attendees will learn why tax demands a higher standard for AI, including explainability, governance, and trusted data. The session will also explore how AI can help improve classification accuracy, accelerate access to information, and streamline manual processes that often consume valuable time and resources.


Vertex product, technology, and tax experts will share practical insights on evaluating AI opportunities, adapting more efficiently to tax change, and implementing AI solutions while maintaining the oversight and control needed for business-critical tax operations.
