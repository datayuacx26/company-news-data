## **Plumbing Scheduling Software FAQs**


### **What software do plumbers use?**


Plumbing businesses typically use two categories of software. Field service management tools like Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, and Workiz handle customer appointments, technician dispatch, job tracking, and invoicing. Employee scheduling and time tracking tools like Homebase manage who works which shifts, GPS clock-ins at job sites, and payroll.


### **What is the 135 rule in plumbing?**


The 135 rule limits how much a horizontal drain pipe can change direction before requiring a vent or cleanout. A drain section should not exceed 135 degrees of total horizontal direction change, the equivalent of one 90-degree turn plus one 45-degree turn. The rule appears in many local plumbing codes derived from the International Plumbing Code (IPC); verify the version adopted in your jurisdiction through the[International Code Council](https://www.iccsafe.org/) .


### **What is the best scheduling software?**


For plumbing businesses that need to manage customer appointments and technician dispatch, Jobber and Housecall Pro consistently earn the highest ratings on G2 and Capterra. For businesses that need to manage employee shifts, GPS time tracking at job sites, and payroll, Homebase is a strong option with a free plan available for one location. The right tool depends on which scheduling problem you're solving: the customer side, the team side, or both.


### **What is the best plumbing estimating software?**


Plumbing estimating software generates job quotes and proposals, while scheduling software assigns technicians and manages when the work happens. For most service-and-repair plumbing businesses, FSM tools like Jobber and Housecall Pro include built-in quoting and flat-rate pricing, covering estimating needs without a separate tool. Commercial plumbing contractors working from blueprints and needing detailed material takeoffs often use dedicated tools like McCormick Systems or FastPIPE.
