# Best Workable Alternatives for Small Businesses in 2026

Workable's Standard plan is $299 a month for teams of up to 20 employees. Texting ($89), video interviews ($109), and candidate assessments ($59) are sold separately as add-ons. For teams that hire often and have someone dedicated to running the process, the price makes sense. For everyone else, it's paying for complexity that doesn't fit how they actually hire.


If you're looking for a Workable alternative that fits your hiring process, the options are better than they used to be. This guide covers five of the best Workable alternatives for small businesses in 2026. Each entry includes current pricing, user feedback, and an honest take on who each tool is right for, and where it falls short.

## **How We Chose the Best Workable Alternatives**


Workable serves a lot of different business types, but this list is built specifically for small teams: companies under 100 people where the person doing the hiring is usually also the person running the floor, not a dedicated recruiter. That narrowed our candidate list significantly.


Tools built for enterprise hiring programs, teams with complex multi-location recruiting pipelines, or companies hiring at scale weren't considered here, regardless of their review scores. We evaluated each tool on:


- **Pricing transparency.** Public pricing is a baseline requirement. Any tool that requires a sales call before you can see what it costs didn't make the list.

We checked that each tool covers them without relying on integrations to fill the gaps.
- **Small business fit.** Fast setup, no implementation project, and an interface a business owner can figure out without HR training. We looked at how quickly someone can post a job and see applicants, not how the tool performs at scale.
- **Ease of use and mobile access.** Small business owners run their businesses from their phones. We checked whether each tool supports that, or whether managers are tied to a desk to use it.
- **User ratings and review patterns.** G2 and Capterra scores were verified against each app's own current page. Review patterns were drawn from verified user reviews, not aggregated summaries. Where a pattern appeared across multiple reviews, we named it.
- **Honest trade-offs.** Every tool has something it doesn't do well. We name it. A tool that's right for 80% of small businesses might be wrong for yours, and you deserve to know why before you sign up.

### **BreezyHR.**


[BreezyHR](https://breezy.hr/) is a web-based applicant tracking tool that automates the manual work in recruiting, from job distribution to pre-screening, interview scheduling, and follow-up emails.


**Best for:** Small businesses and startups that want hiring on autopilot without enterprise pricing


**Rating:** G2 4.4/5 (686 reviews) · Capterra 4.5/5 (1,420 reviews)


**Key features:**


- **Job posting** to 50+ job boards, including LinkedIn and Indeed, from a single submission
- **Drag-and-drop pipeline** to move candidates through stages with automated email sequences triggered at each stage
- **Interview self-scheduling** with Google and Outlook calendar sync, so candidates book their own slot
- **Native iOS and Android apps** for reviewing applicants and taking action on the go


**Pricing:**


- Bootstrap: Free. 1 active position at a time.
- Paid plans start from Startup: $157/month (annual) or $189/month (monthly). Unlimited positions, automations, mobile app, calendar sync.


**What users like:** Most reviews focus on how fast non-HR hiring managers can get started: the drag-and-drop pipeline and automated email sequences are cited most often as the features that save real time.


**What users criticize:** Slow load times and occasional glitches are the most consistent complaints, with a secondary pattern of automated candidate emails landing in spam rather than inboxes.


**Our take:** For BreezyHR, the automation is the differentiator: once the pipeline is set up, pre-screening, follow-ups, and calendar scheduling all run automatically without you having to push each step. Worth knowing before you go live: automated candidate emails sometimes end up in spam, so test the flow before you rely on it.


### **Manatal.**


[Manatal](https://www.manatal.com/) is an applicant tracking tool with a built-in recruitment CRM. It uses AI to recommend and rank candidates, parse resumes, and enrich applicant profiles with data pulled from LinkedIn and other sources.


**Best for:** Small businesses that want AI-powered screening without paying enterprise prices


**Rating:** G2 4.8/5 (147 reviews) · Capterra 4.6/5 (141 reviews)


**Key features:**


- **AI candidate recommendations** that rank applicants by job fit automatically, before you open a single resume
- **Social profile enrichment** pulling data from LinkedIn and 20+ other sources into every candidate record automatically
- **Job posting to 2,500+ free and premium boards** , including Indeed, LinkedIn, Monster, and ZipRecruiter, from a single submission
- **Branded career page builder** with custom application forms and mobile-friendly design


**Pricing:**


- Paid plans start from Professional: $15/user/month (annual) or $19/user/month (monthly). Up to 15 active jobs.
- 14-day free trial, no credit card required.


**What users like:** The LinkedIn integration and social profile enrichment are the most praised features, with many users who switched from pricier tools noting they got most of what they needed at a fraction of the cost.


**What users criticize:** Reporting depth is the most consistent limitation, with users across both G2 and Capterra wanting more customizable dashboards and analytics than the standard plan provides.


**Our take:** If most of your hiring happens at a desk, Manatal does a lot of the research work for you: AI ranking, profile enrichment, and 2,500+ job board distribution. Where it stops short is reporting; if you need to track source quality or pipeline performance, you'll hit that ceiling fast.


### **JazzHR.**


[JazzHR](https://www.jazzhr.com/) is a recruiting tool built for small-to-medium businesses. It offers a customizable hiring workflow on the base plan, with candidate texting, video interviews, e-signatures, and advanced reporting available as paid add-ons.


**Best for:** Small businesses that want a dedicated ATS with an interface hiring managers can use without training


**Rating:** G2 4.4/5 (857 reviews) · Capterra 4.3/5 (492 reviews)


**Key features:**


- **Job posting and board syndication** to major boards from one submission, with premium listing options
- **Customizable hiring workflows** per role, so each opening can have its own pipeline stages and evaluation criteria
- **Candidate evaluation forms and scoring** to keep assessments structured and consistent across the hiring team
- **Interview scheduling** with Google and Outlook calendar sync (eSignatures and advanced reporting are on the Pro plan at $499/month)


**Pricing:** Paid plans start from Hero: $99/month or $1,000/year. 3 active job postings. Annual billing saves up to 24%


**What users like:** The clean interface and low learning curve for non-HR hiring managers are cited most often, with reviewers repeatedly noting that managers unfamiliar with ATS tools can get into the system and review candidates without a walkthrough.


**What users criticize:** Limited reporting is the most cited frustration, and a notable pattern in recent reviews specifically calls out the cancellation process as difficult, requiring 30-day written notice tied to a billing date and a friction-heavy email back-and-forth that multiple reviewers describe as customer-unfriendly.


**Our take:** JazzHR is the easiest tool on this list to hand to a manager who has never done formal recruiting before; they'll figure it out without a walkthrough. One thing worth looking into before you commit: the cancellation process has a reputation in reviews for being harder than it should be.


### **AvaHR.**


[AvaHR](https://avahr.com/) (formerly VivaHR) is a hiring tool built for small businesses. It focuses on broad job board distribution, a unique Culture Profiles feature for employer branding, and straightforward candidate management for teams without a dedicated HR function.


**Best for:** Small businesses with limited HR support that want maximum candidate reach without managing multiple job board accounts


**Rating:** G2 4.7/5 (98 reviews) · Capterra 4.9/5 (89 reviews)


**Key features:**


- **Job posting to 100+ boards** , including free job boards and premium niche sites, from a single form
- **Branded career page builder** so candidates land on a page that reflects your business
- **Candidate scorecards and pipeline management** to track and evaluate applicants across stages
- **Culture Profiles** for showcasing company culture through photos, videos, and text directly on the branded career page


**Pricing:** Paid plans start from Standard: $129/month. 3 active job postings, 1 user, 1 location. 7-day free trial available.


**What users like:** Most reviews center on how quickly a job post goes live across 100+ boards at once, with small business owners frequently noting it replaced expensive individual job board subscriptions and cut their hiring cost.


**What users criticize:** Persistent upsell prompts when posting jobs and receiving unqualified international applicants are the most mentioned frustrations across both G2 and Capterra.


**Our take:** AvaHR is worth a look if you're posting jobs yourself and want to reach as many candidates as possible without managing multiple accounts. The ratings are the highest on this list, which is a good sign, though the review base is smaller than any other tool here.

## **Workable Alternatives FAQs**


### **Is Workable worth it?**


Workable is worth it for businesses with consistent hiring volume and a recruiter to run the process. At $299/month before add-ons, the cost is hard to justify for teams that hire occasionally or don't have HR support. The tools on this list cover the core hiring workflow for less.


### **What is the 70/30 rule in hiring?**


The 70/30 rule is a guideline for structuring hiring decisions: base roughly 70% on verifiable qualifications and experience, and 30% on cultural fit. It's a way to keep evaluations consistent and reduce over-reliance on gut instinct. No single organization owns it; it's a widely referenced HR heuristic.


### **What is the best hiring app for small businesses?**


It depends on how you hire. BreezyHR is Capterra's top-rated recruiting tool and works well for small businesses that want a clear pipeline and automation. For small businesses with hourly teams that also need scheduling and payroll in the same place, Homebase is built specifically for that.


### **Are Workable jobs legit?**


Yes. Jobs on Workable are posted by real companies using it as their hiring tool. It's an applicant tracking system used by 35,000+ teams globally, not a job board with anonymous listings. Verify any employer independently before sharing personal information.
