## **The 7 Best Small Business Hiring Software Options**


The right[small business recruiting software](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/recruitment-software-for-small-business) depends on how often you hire, what kind of team you're building, and how much you want your hiring to connect to the rest of your business. Here's a closer look at each option.


### **1. JazzHR**


JazzHR is a purpose-built applicant tracking system designed for small businesses that hire on a regular basis. It covers the full hiring process from job posting to offer letter, without adding HR tools you won't use. The entry price is accessible, and the job board integrations are strong enough to reach candidates across Indeed, LinkedIn, and Glassdoor in one step.


- **Best for:** Small businesses that hire consistently and want a dedicated ATS without extra HR features
- **G2 rating:** 4.4/5 (857 reviews) | **Capterra rating:** 4.3/5 (492 reviews)
- **Pricing:** Hero ($1,000/yr, ~$83/month) / Plus ($3,480/yr, ~$290/month) / Pro ($5,508/yr, ~$459/month). Free trial available.
- **Free tier:** No
- **Key features:**


- Multi-board posting to free and premium job boards in one step, with LinkedIn Recruiter integration
- Customizable hiring pipeline stages, email templates, and workflow automation
- AI-powered candidate matching via TalentFit (Plus and Pro plans)
- Offers and eSignatures (Pro plan)


- **What users like:** On G2 and Capterra, reviewers consistently highlight the clean, easy-to-learn interface and multi-board job posting as JazzHR's biggest strengths. Teams that hire regularly praise how quickly new users get up to speed and how much time the tool saves on job board management.
- **What users dislike:** The most common complaints on both platforms center on reporting tools, which reviewers describe as limited and hard to customize. Several Capterra reviewers also flag the cancellation process as a pain point, citing a 30-day notice requirement and a multi-step offboarding flow before an account can be closed.


### **2. Breezy HR**


Breezy HR gives small businesses a free starting point and one of the most polished careers pages in this category. The Bootstrap plan is free forever and includes distribution to 50+ job boards for a single active position. When you're ready to scale, paid plans unlock unlimited positions, automated candidate workflows, and interview self-scheduling.


- **Best for:** Small businesses that want a branded careers page and a free way to get started
- **G2 rating:** 4.4/5 (686 reviews) | **Capterra rating:** 4.5/5 (1,420 reviews)
- **Pricing:** Bootstrap (free, 1 active position) / Startup ($157/mo billed annually, or $189/mo monthly) / Growth ($273/mo or $329/mo monthly) / Business ($439/mo or $529/mo monthly). Annual billing includes 2 months free. 14-day free trial on paid plans.
- **Free tier:** Yes. 1 active position, unlimited users, distribution to 50+ job boards.
- **Key features:**


- Branded career site, fully customizable and hostable on your own domain
- Distribution to 50+ job boards from one step, including free and premium listings
- Drag-and-drop candidate pipeline with stage-based automation on paid plans
- Interview self-scheduling with Google Calendar and Outlook integration (Startup and up)


- **What users like:** On G2 and Capterra, Breezy earns consistently strong marks for its clean, easy-to-learn interface and how quickly teams get up and running. Reviewers on both platforms highlight the drag-and-drop pipeline and automated workflows as genuine time-savers.
- **What users dislike:** The most common complaints are around reporting flexibility, which reviewers describe as limited, and occasional performance slowdowns when the system loads under heavier use. A handful of reviewers also note that automated emails sometimes end up in candidates' spam folders.


### **3. BambooHR**


BambooHR is a full HR system that includes a solid applicant tracking system as one of its core modules. It's a good fit for businesses that want hiring, onboarding, time-off tracking, and performance management all in one place — rather than a standalone ATS. The collaborative hiring tools let multiple team members review and score candidates, and onboarding connects directly to HR records the moment someone is hired. Payroll is available as a paid add-on.


- **Best for:** Growing teams that want hiring built into a full HR system, especially those managing salaried or office-based employees
- **G2 rating:** 4.4/5 (5,730 reviews) | **Capterra rating:** 4.6/5 (3,285 reviews)
- **Pricing:** Core ($10/user/mo) / Pro ($17/user/mo) / Elite ($25/user/mo). Teams of 25 or fewer employees start at a flat rate of approximately $250/month. Payroll, Benefits Administration, and Time Tracking are paid add-ons. Contact BambooHR for an exact quote — pricing is not fully published online.
- **Free tier:** No (free trial available)
- **Key features:**


- ATS with job posting to Indeed, Glassdoor, and ZipRecruiter, plus offer letter templates
- Collaborative candidate review with team scorecards and hiring workflows
- Digital onboarding that connects new hires to HR records, e-signatures, and new hire packets
- 150+ integrations spanning payroll, benefits, identity management, and more


- **What users like:** On G2 and Capterra, BambooHR earns some of the highest marks on this list for ease of use. Reviewers praise its clean interface, strong onboarding workflows, and employee self-service features.
- **What users dislike:** The most common criticism is reporting — reviewers across both platforms describe the analytics as limited and difficult to customize. Several reviewers also note that costs climb quickly as you add modules, and that payroll features are US-only.


### **4. Workable**


Workable is a structured hiring platform that has expanded into a full HR suite. Interview kits, scorecards, and candidate self-scheduling help you run a consistent, multi-stage process without extra coordination overhead. All plans include an HR module covering onboarding, time off, and payroll reporting. The 15-day trial is long enough to run a real hiring process before committing.


- **Best for:** Small businesses that run multi-stage hiring with structured evaluation at each step
- **G2 rating:** 4.4/5 (705 reviews) | **Capterra rating:** 4.4/5 (661 reviews)
- **Pricing:** Standard ($299/mo) / Premier ($599/mo) / Enterprise ($719/mo). All plans include unlimited active jobs (subject to fair usage limits) and a 15-day free trial. Annual billing saves up to 20%.
- **Free tier:** No
- **Key features:**


- Multi-board posting to 200+ job boards with one-click distribution, including LinkedIn and Indeed
- Interview kits and customizable scorecards for structured, consistent candidate evaluation across teams
- One-way video interviews and science-backed assessments (add-ons on Standard; included on Premier and Enterprise)
- AI-powered candidate matching and resume screening across all plans; Workable Agent (autonomous sourcing, outreach, and screening) available as a paid add-on


- **What users like:** On G2 and Capterra, reviewers praise Workable's clean, intuitive interface and note that teams across experience levels get up to speed quickly. Collaboration features — shared notes, scorecards, and team-based workflows — earn consistent praise for keeping hiring teams aligned.
- **What users dislike:** The most common complaint on both platforms is reporting: reviewers describe the analytics as limited and hard to customize. Several small business reviewers also flag pricing as steep, noting the entry point roughly doubled after Workable removed its lower-tier Starter plan.


### **5. Zoho Recruit**


Zoho Recruit's free plan is the most capable free tier on this list: one active job, one recruiter, and a functional candidate pipeline at no cost. It's a good fit for businesses that hire once or twice a year and want a structured process without paying for one. The interface takes some time to learn, but the core hiring workflow is solid.


- **Best for:** Low-volume hiring or businesses that want to test ATS software before committing
- **G2 rating:** 4.4/5 (1,843 reviews) | **Capterra rating:** 4.5/5 (1,241 reviews)

- **Free tier:** Yes. 1 active job, 1 recruiter license.
- **Key features:**


- Multi-board posting to 75+ job boards on paid plans, including Indeed and LinkedIn
- Customizable hiring pipeline with workflow automation and knockout application questions
- Resume parsing and AI-powered candidate scoring via Zia (Enterprise plan)
- 50+ integrations on Standard, including Google Workspace, Outlook, and other Zoho apps


- **What users like:** On G2 and Capterra, reviewers consistently highlight Zoho Recruit's value for money and strong customizability — small teams and staffing firms alike praise the ability to tailor pipelines and workflows to fit how they hire. Automation features and resume parsing earn frequent credit for cutting manual admin work.
- **What users dislike:** The most common complaint on both platforms is the learning curve, particularly during initial setup and when configuring advanced workflows and automations. Customer support quality gets mixed reviews, with some users — especially on free plans — reporting slow response times and limited guidance.

Manatal uses AI to score and rank candidates against your job description the moment they apply. For businesses that get high application volume, that automatic prioritization saves real review time. The pricing is lower than most enterprise AI tools, making it accessible for small businesses. It works best for roles where you're sorting through a large number of applicants quickly.

- **G2 rating:** 4.8/5 (147 reviews) | **Capterra rating:** 4.6/5 (141 reviews)

- **Key features:**


- AI candidate scoring based on job description fit, with ranked candidate shortlists across all paid plans
- Enriched candidate profiles pulling from LinkedIn and other public sources automatically
- Multi-board posting to thousands of job boards with a branded career page on all plans

- **What users like:** On G2 and Capterra, reviewers consistently highlight Manatal's competitive pricing and ease of initial setup. AI-powered candidate scoring, LinkedIn profile enrichment, and one-click job board distribution earn frequent praise for speeding up the screening process.
- **What users dislike:** The most common complaints center on navigation — reviewers describe the interface as harder to use at higher volumes, with limited filtering options making it difficult to search large candidate pools. Customer support runs on email and chat, with several reviewers noting delayed responses at lower tiers.

- **Key features:**

Recurring bugs, slow load times, and frequent session timeouts are cited consistently, and some reviewers flag that pricing grows significantly as modules are added.


## **Frequently Asked Questions About Small Business Hiring Software**


### Do I need hiring software if I only hire a few people a year?


Even occasional hiring benefits from a structured process. When you only hire a few times a year, each decision carries more weight, and losing a strong candidate to a slow follow-up is a real cost. A free plan from Breezy HR or Zoho Recruit gives you a consistent process without a monthly bill. You don't need to be hiring constantly for it to be worth setting up.


### What's the difference between free and paid hiring software for small businesses?


Free plans typically limit you to one active job posting with basic candidate tracking. Paid plans unlock multi-board posting, automated screening, structured interview tools, and analytics. For most small businesses, a free or entry-level plan covers day-to-day needs. Upgrading makes sense when you're filling multiple roles at once or want automated screening to cut the time you spend reviewing applications.


### Can small business hiring software help with compliance?


Yes. Most tools help maintain EEOC compliance by standardizing job descriptions and creating an audit trail for hiring decisions. Some, like Homebase, also handle I-9 collection and digital document storage as part of the[onboarding flow](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/essential-hr-and-onboarding-tips-for-seamless-small-business-hiring) . For[small businesses without an HR team](https://www.joinhomebase.com/hr-compliance/small-business-hr) , a documented and consistent process is one of the easiest ways to reduce legal risk when hiring.


### How long does it take to set up hiring software for a small business?


Most small business hiring tools are up and running within an hour. You'll set up your business profile, create your first job posting, and connect your preferred job boards. Homebase is designed to get you posting your first job in minutes, with hiring and applicant tracking available on the free Basic plan.
