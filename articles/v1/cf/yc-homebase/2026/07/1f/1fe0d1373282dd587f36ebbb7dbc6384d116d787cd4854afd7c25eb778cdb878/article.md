### **Jobber.**


**Best for:** Home service businesses with 1 to 15 technicians who need scheduling and job management in one place


‍[Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/) is the go-to tool for small home service teams that want to get organized without a steep learning curve. It handles scheduling, quoting, invoicing, and automated reminders from one calendar, making it easy to move off spreadsheets without overbuilding your stack.


**Key features:**


- Drag-and-drop calendar with day, week, and month views, color coding, and filters
- Find a Time smart scheduling that recommends open slots based on team availability and drive time
- Client notifications and appointment reminders via SMS and email
- Mobile app for technicians to access job details, navigate to the site, and update job status


**Pricing:** Core plan starts at $29/month billed annually, or $49/month on a rolling monthly basis, for a single user. No free tier; 14-day free trial available.


**Ratings:** Capterra 4.6/5 (1,400+ reviews) · G2 4.6/5 (500+ reviews)


**What users like:** Across both platforms, users most commonly praise how easy Jobber is to learn and use day-to-day, with the scheduling calendar and integrated invoicing workflow called out as the biggest time savers.


**What users criticize:** The most repeated complaints across both platforms involve intermittent bugs and slowdowns, along with a sense that the feature set starts feeling limited as teams grow.


**Our take:** Jobber is the right first move for most growing home service teams. It sets up fast, covers scheduling and billing without overbuilding, and the ceiling on what it can handle is higher than most small teams ever reach.

## **Frequently Asked Questions About Technician Scheduling Software**


### **Is there free technician scheduling software?**


Most field service management tools don't offer a permanent free plan, though several offer free trials ranging from 7 to 14 days. Homebase has a free plan for shift scheduling and time tracking for teams of up to 10 employees, though it doesn't include job dispatch or invoicing.


### **What is the difference between technician scheduling software and field service management software?**


Technician scheduling software, sometimes called service schedule software, handles the who and when: assigning jobs, tracking availability, and pushing real-time updates to the field. Field service management software is a broader stack that adds invoicing, customer records, work order management, and inventory tracking on top of scheduling. Many tools marketed as technician scheduling software are actually FSM platforms with scheduling as their core feature.


### **What scheduling software do HVAC companies use?**


HVAC companies commonly use Housecall Pro and FieldPulse for residential operations in the 5 to 20 technician range. ServiceTitan is the dominant choice at the enterprise level for commercial HVAC contractors. FieldEdge is another option used specifically by HVAC businesses that want tight QuickBooks integration for recurring maintenance billing.


### **How do I get my technicians to actually adopt a new scheduling app?**


Pick a tool with a mobile-first design your least tech-savvy tech can figure out without a manual. Run a two-week pilot with two or three technicians before rolling it out to the whole team, and keep the first phase simple: schedule visibility and clock in only. Adoption almost always fails because of complexity at launch, not resistance to the tool itself.
