## What is a work schedule template?


A work schedule template is a pre-designed document or digital file that outlines your employees’ working hours, days on and off, and the tasks they’ll perform while they’re on the job. Work schedule templates ensure everyone’s in the right place at the right time to meet your business’s needs.


They can also take hours off your administrative duties, since you’re not creating a schedule from scratch each time. In fact, statistics show that with an efficient scheduling tool, you can spend[as little as 0.63%](https://webinarcare.com/best-employee-scheduling-software/employee-scheduling-statistics/#7) of your work week making schedules.


Since not all schedules are 9 to 5, a good work schedule template will accommodate a number of[different schedule types](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/types-of-work-schedules/#post-grid) depending on the nature of your business. For example:


### Shift work schedules


Any work schedule that falls outside the hours of 7am and 6pm is considered shift work.[Shift work schedules](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/shift-schedule) apply to employees working evening, late night, or early morning hours. These schedules may include 8-12 hour shifts, and evening, weekend, or holiday shifts.


### Rotating shift schedules


With a rotating work schedule, you’ll see employees working a variety of shifts for a specified number of days. Once those days are up, it’s time for the employees to rotate. For example, an employee might work the day shift for 5 weeks, and then work the[night shift](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/working-the-night-shift) for the next 5 weeks. This type of schedule is common in[24/7 operations](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/24-hour-shift-schedule) .


### On-call work schedules


On-call schedules mean that employees may be asked to come in outside of their regular work hours, often on short notice. They might fill in for a[sick teammate](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/employee-calling-in-sick-to-work) or help out if there’s a rush. On-call schedules are most common in retail and hospitality, and are often used to keep labor costs low. While they benefit business owners,[on-call schedules can be a challenge](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/on-call-scheduling-critics-shift-scheduling-software) for employees.


### Monthly work schedules


A monthly work schedule outlines the work shifts and assignments for employees over the course of a month. Employees get a longer-term view of their assignments and shifts, and follow the schedule until the end of the month—unless they[swap shifts](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/scheduling-how-to-make-shift-swaps-easier) , get sick, or request other changes. These schedules can be tailored for shift work, on-call employees, or other industry-specific needs.

## How to make a monthly work schedule template


There are a few ways you can create a monthly work schedule template. Some are more efficient or cost effective than others, depending on your needs.


### Pen and paper


If digital tools aren’t readily available or they’re too expensive, you can take the manual approach. Create a grid, write in employee names, and fill in the shifts. While paper templates work in a pinch, they’re prone to error, easy to lose, harder to amend, and tough to share with your team.


### Spreadsheets


Spreadsheets like[Google Sheets or Excel](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/2-free-ways-employee-shift-schedule) are accessible, versatile, and easily shareable. They offer built-in functions for calculations and special formatting so you can tally up hours worked, overtime, and other metrics. The downside? You have to design your template from scratch and the integrations are limited.


### Calendar apps


Calendar applications, like Outlook, Google Calendar, or Apple Calendar, can be used to create simple monthly work schedules with features like color coding and sharing. They’re not as robust as dedicated scheduling tools, so they’re best for creating basic schedules.


### Project management tools


Project management tools like Trello, Asana, or Monday can be repurposed to create work schedules. Use boards or projects to represent weeks or months and assign tasks or shifts to team members. Because they’re not designed specifically for scheduling, project management tools may not have all the features you want. Plus, they can be expensive for each person using them.


### Employee scheduling apps


Made with scheduling in mind, these apps provide all the features you need to create, share, and update your schedule.[Scheduling apps](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/best-employee-scheduling-apps-2024) like Homebase also offer free customizable work schedule templates that make scheduling simple.
