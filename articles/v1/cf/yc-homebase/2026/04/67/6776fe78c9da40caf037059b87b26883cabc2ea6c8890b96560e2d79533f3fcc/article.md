## What are payroll services?


Payroll services manage the complete[payroll process](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-process) to help businesses ensure that everyone gets paid accurately and on time. This includes calculating employee pay, managing tax withholdings, and processing end-of-year forms while staying compliant.


This can either be managed by an external payroll services provider or managed in-house through dedicated software. Either way, the goal is the same: less manual work and fewer mistakes without ** needing to get a finance degree.


## What do payroll services actually include?


Payroll services include a wide range of one-time and ongoing tasks, including:


- [Setting up payroll](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/setting-up-payroll) **:** Configuring pay schedules, creating employee profiles, determining tax withholdings, and setting up direct deposit from day one.
- **Tax calculations and filings:** Automatically calculating federal, state, and local taxes, then filing them on your behalf. Multi-state tax law should be taken into consideration.
- **Direct deposit:** Paying employees on time without manual transfers. If your small business permits employees to be paid by cheque, your payroll service will manage that element as well.
- [Payroll report](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-report) **generation:** Generating detailed records of wages, deductions, and tax contributions for your records and audits. This is key for staying compliant.
- **Year-end forms:**[W-2s](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/w2-payroll) and[1099s](https://www.joinhomebase.com/glossary/1099-form) are prepared and distributed without last-minute scrambling.


### The difference between full-service payroll vs. DIY payroll software


The difference between full-service payroll and DIY payroll software comes down to your level of involvement as a business owner:


- **Full-service payroll:** A provider handles every aspect of[payroll management](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-management) , and all you have to do is review and approve what you see. Outsourced payroll service costs more, but lets you keep payroll entirely off your brain.
- **DIY payroll software:** Payroll stays in your hands, but once setup is complete,[payroll automation](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-automation) takes care of the crunchy parts. Better for owners who want visibility and lower monthly costs, but still requires regular involvement.


Handing off payroll entirely or managing it in-house comes down to measuring your comfort level against your time and budget.


These days, most full-service providers are running the same software you could learn yourself, so you're mostly paying for the extra support. That's great insurance if your needs are complex, but if your situation is fairly standard, keeping it in-house can save you a lot of money.


### When to combine HR and payroll services


Integrated HR and payroll services can be a serious timesaver, especially when you’re growing your team. Having onboarding documents, benefits enrollment,[PTO tracking](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/pto-tracking-software) , and payroll compliance tools fed into the same system cuts out duplicate data entry.


Rather than juggling separate tools, an integrated platform keeps everything in sync, so when someone's hours, role, or benefits change, it's automatically reflected in their paycheck.


## How much do payroll services cost?


Underestimating what[payroll services cost](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-services-cost) can blow a tight budget mid-year, but overestimating might hold you back from a tool that could genuinely save you time and money. Let’s get into the numbers.


### Typical pricing models: per employee vs. flat rate


Most online payroll services manage[payroll costs](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-cost) in one of two ways:


- **Per-employee model** starts with a base monthly fee (~$20–$50) plus a per-person charge (~$4–$12 per employee per month). A great deal for a small team, but costs can climb quickly when you grow.
- **Flat-rate pricing** bundles everything into one predictable monthly fee regardless of headcount. Better option for businesses with larger or fluctuating teams, but the cost of entry may be higher.


Price out your options against your current headcount before committing, and consider what numbers might look like as you scale. If you hope to double your team by next year, how will that affect your budget for payroll systems?


### Hidden payroll services fees to watch out for


[Payroll processors](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-processors) often advertise a low base price—and then layer on fees that can significantly inflate your actual bill.


Before you sign, find out if providers charge extra for:


- One-time setup fees
- Year-end W-2 and 1099 preparation
- Off-cycle payroll runs
- State and local tax filing fees
- Contractor payments


### Is there a catch with free payroll options?


If you're trying to figure out[how to do payroll](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/how-to-do-payroll) on a shoestring, fully free software can be tempting, but it usually comes with limitations. Many free payroll services cap the number of employees, skip automated tax filings, or even leave out direct deposit entirely.


That said, starting with a payroll service that offers a free tier isn't a bad move. You can learn the platform at no cost, then upgrade to paid features as your business grows. There’s no reason to pay for features you don’t currently need.

## How to choose the right payroll service for your small business


With the number of options there are for payroll solutions software, it can be hard to know where to start. The right choice is the one that fits your actual workflow.


### Questions to ask before you sign up


Before you commit to any payroll program, get a clear idea of your needs by asking:


- How many employees do I have? How many do I plan to hire in the next year?
- Do I need benefits administration, or just pay and taxes?
- Do I have hourly workers, salaried employees, contractors, or a mix?
- What software am I already using for accounting or scheduling?
- What are the most common[payroll errors](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-errors) I currently see each month?
- Who are the most trusted payroll services in my industry?
- Which payroll services are easiest to switch to if I change my mind?
- Will I need multi-state payroll support?
- What's my overall budget?


If you’re caught between options, answering these questions will help you figure out which demo you want to give the first shot.


### Signs it's time to switch payroll providers


Uncertain if your payroll provider is giving you the value you were hoping for?


If any of these situations sound familiar, it might be time to[switch payroll providers](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/how-to-switch-payroll-providers) :


- **You're still losing lots of time to payroll:** If running payroll takes more than an hour or consistently requires corrections, your current payroll service simply isn’t doing its job.
- **Errors keep showing up:** Overtime miscalculations, missed deadlines, and mistakes with tax deductions can land you in serious legal trouble—not to mention damage trust with your employees.
- **Your plan can’t keep up with your growth:**[What is payroll](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/what-is-payroll) without growth opportunities? A tool that worked for five employees may not work well for twenty, or be financially sustainable.
- **You're paying for features you don't use (or missing ones you need):** If you’re feeling annoyed by super-advanced AI tools, don’t pay for them! You have the right to opt in or out of payroll systems that don’t align with your needs.
- **Customer support is hard to reach:** When something goes wrong with payroll, answers can’t wait. Slow or unhelpful support is a genuine liability.


‍


The point of payroll solutions software is to save you time, money, and brainpower. If your current option is an ongoing source of frustration, there’s definitely something better out there.


### What to look for if you manage hourly or shift-based teams


Hourly teams have more specific payroll needs than businesses with fixed schedules, and your payroll service needs the features to meet them:


- **Time tracking integration:** Eliminate manual entry, and the errors that come with it, by having hours flow directly into payroll.
- **Overtime calculations:** Auto-calculation for daily and weekly overtime thresholds keeps you compliant and your labor costs in check. If your payroll system[integrates with scheduling software](https://www.joinhomebase.com/employee-scheduling) , you'll get alerts before overtime becomes a problem.
- [Tip management](https://support.joinhomebase.com/s/article/Tip-Pooling-with-Homebase) **:** This is especially important for restaurant and hospitality teams. Look for payroll systems that automatically calculate, distribute, and add tips to pay.
- **Variable scheduling support:** Hours are going to fluctuate from week to week, and a flexible drag-and-drop menu will make your life easier. Better yet, schedule templates can help you create foundations for busier seasons of the year.
- **Mobile access:** Employees and managers should be able to interact with the system from their phones. No more emails and text chains asking payroll questions—just one shared dashboard with all the info everyone needs.


## Frequently asked questions about payroll services


### What are the best payroll services for small businesses with less than 25 employees?


Homebase, Gusto, and OnPay are some of the best payroll services for teams of under 25 people. Each is priced with smaller teams in mind, and can be used without a dedicated HR or finance person. Homebase is ideal for hourly teams, and has free scheduling and time tracking tools for teams with fewer than 10 employees.


### What are common payroll services?


Common payroll services include new hire management, tax calculation and filing, direct deposit, report generation, and year-and W-2 or 1099 preparation. Payroll solutions software often includes additional features like employee self-service portals, and time-tracking and accounting integrations.


### Can I do my own payroll for free?


Yes, some payroll systems let you do payroll for free, but free tiers usually come with limitations. You will likely be capped on the number of employees that can be enrolled, and lose access to automated tax filing or direct deposit.


### Can ChatGPT do payroll?


No, ChatGPT cannot do payroll. ChatGPT can answer questions about[how payroll works](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/how-does-payroll-work) and explain basic concepts, but it can't process payments, file taxes, connect to your bank, or interact with your HR systems. Compliant online payroll services or trained professionals are built to work with financial systems, and also have more trustworthy information.


### How do payroll services work?


Payroll services calculate pay based on employees’ hours and pay rates, then withhold appropriate taxes and process direct deposits into employees’ bank accounts. On the backend, it also handles remitting taxes to state and federal agencies, and generating reports for your records.


### What payroll services are designed for startups?


Gusto is one of the most popular payroll services for startups. It can handle a combination of W-2 employees and 1099 contractors, and manage benefits for a lower price point than other payroll companies. Multi-state features are also beneficial for remote teams.
