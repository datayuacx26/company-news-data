## What to look for in the best payroll processing software


Some payroll features are more useful than others to a small team’s[payroll process](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/how-does-payroll-work) . The best[payroll for small businesses](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-services-for-small-business) focuses on automation, accuracy, and simplicity.


Look for these features when shopping for a new payroll tool:


### Automated payroll tax processing


Payroll tax processing software should handle tax calculations, filings, compliance, and payments automatically. For small business owners, this is one of the biggest time-savers and reduces the risk of costly mistakes. It can[reduce payroll processing costs by up to 80%](https://www.pivotalsolutions.com/2024-payroll-management-50/) .


### Time clock and scheduling integration


The best payroll processing software connects directly with your time tracking and scheduling tools. This ensures hours worked flow straight into payroll without manual entry.


When a shift runs late or someone swaps shifts, those changes automatically update in payroll. That means fewer errors and less time double-checking[timesheets](https://www.joinhomebase.com/timesheets) .


### Ease of setup and ongoing use


It should be easy to[set up your software for payroll processing](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/setting-up-payroll) and even easier to use. Look for tools with guided setup, simple dashboards, and clear workflows.


If it takes weeks to configure or requires constant troubleshooting, then it’s slowing you down. You should be able to run payroll in minutes, not hours.


### Pricing transparency — what you actually pay


[Pricing for payroll solutions software](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-services-cost) can vary widely. Some tools charge per employee, while others bundle features into tiers. The best payroll processing software is upfront about costs. You should know exactly what you’re paying and what’s included before you commit.


### Customer support and reliability


Payroll isn’t something you can afford to “figure out later.” When something goes wrong, like a missed payment or tax issue, you need fast, reliable support from your payroll processing software provider.


Look for tools that offer responsive customer support, clear documentation, and consistent system uptime. The best payroll for small business owners is dependable every time you run payroll, not just when everything goes smoothly.


Now that you know what to look for, let’s compare the[best payroll processing software for small businesses](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/best-payroll-software) .


## Best payroll processing software for small businesses in 2026


Choosing the best payroll processing software depends on your current workflows and software, team size, and how you manage your business. Below are some of the top options for[small business payroll software](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-processors) .

- **Cons:** Can feel complex for new users, higher cost as team grows.


### Gusto — Best for teams that want HR built in


Gusto combines payroll processing software with HR tools, making it a strong all-in-one option.


- **Top features:** Payroll, benefits, onboarding, compliance tools.
- **Pricing:** Starts at $49/month + $6/employee per month.

- **Cons:** Higher cost for advanced features, which may be more than you need or over budget.

[Homebase](https://www.joinhomebase.com/payroll) is built specifically for hourly teams, combining scheduling, time tracking, and payroll processing software.


- **Top features:** Time tracking, scheduling, payroll, team communication.

- **Pros:** Built for small businesses with hourly teams, reduces manual entry, all-in-one tool.
- **Cons:** Not suitable for salaried-only teams or large businesses.


### OnPay — Best for budget-conscious small businesses


OnPay offers a simple, full-service payroll at an affordable price point, making it a good option for small businesses.


- **Top features:** Full-service payroll, automated tax filing, benefits integration.
- **Pricing:** Starts at $49/month + $6/employee per month.
- **Pros:** Transparent pricing, easy to use, strong feature set.
- **Cons:** Fewer advanced integrations, no built-in time clock.


### Patriot Software — Best low-cost option for DIY tax filers


Patriot is one of the most affordable payroll processing software options available, making it the best option for businesses comfortable[handling some tax tasks themselves](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/how-to-do-payroll) .


- **Top features:** Basic payroll, optional tax filing services, accounting tool.
- **Pricing:** Starts at $8.50/month + $4/month per worker paid. Timekeeping is an add-on, and their accounting software is a separate monthly cost.
- **Pros:** Very affordable, simple interface, reliable customer service.
- **Cons:** Pay more for automated tax filings, fewer features, many add-on options that can add up.


### Square Payroll — Best for Square POS users


Square Payroll works best if you already use Square for payments or POS.


- **Top features:** Payroll synced with POS, contractor payments.

### Friday App — Best for small businesses that want simplicity


[Friday](https://fridayapp.com/) is built exclusively for small businesses with 1–50 employees, making it a strong pick for owners who want straightforward payroll without enterprise-level complexity or surprise fees.


- **Top features:** Payroll processing, time tracking, PTO management, tax filings, W-2s and 1099s, and employee self-service — all included.
- **Pricing:** Startup plan starts at $59/month base fee + $12/month per user. Small Biz Pro is $99/month base + $16/month per user. Time-tracking-only plan available at $9/user/month with no base fee.
- **Pros:** Tax filings, W-2s, and 1099s included with no surprise charges. Transparent pricing with no hidden fees, same-day direct deposits, and a 14-day free trial. Consistently praised customer support.
- **Cons:** Customization options are limited, and the mobile app is less polished than the desktop version. Basic integration capabilities may hinder businesses with complex workflow automation needs. Not suited for teams larger than ~50 employees.


## Free payroll processing software — what's actually free and what isn't


With so many options available, you may be wondering if you can just start with free payroll processing software instead. Free payroll processing software can be appealing, especially when you’re just starting out. But “free” often comes with limitations.


### What free plans typically include


Free payroll processing software usually includes basic payroll calculations and limited employee management. Some tools offer free tiers with optional paid add-ons for tax filing or direct deposit.


These plans can work for very[small teams or businesses with simple payroll needs](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/how-to-do-payroll-for-small-business) . But they often require more manual work and oversight.


So, if you’re considering a free option, think about whether the functionalities you get outweigh the limitations—if they don’t, then maybe a paid option would be better.


### When it makes sense to pay for payroll software


Paying for payroll processing software makes sense when you want features that aren’t included in the free plan, like[payroll automation](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-automation) and compliance support. Automated tax filing and integrated time tracking can quickly justify the cost, especially with the time you’ll save.


As your team grows, manual[payroll management](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-management) becomes harder. Investing in the[best payroll for small businesses](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/online-payroll-services) helps you scale without adding complexity.


### Risks of relying on free payroll tools


Free payroll processing software can work in the short term, but it often comes with trade-offs. Limited automation, manual tax handling, and fewer integrations can increase your workload and risk of errors.


As your team grows, those limitations start to show. What saved you money upfront can end up costing more in time, mistakes, or compliance issues, especially without built-in payroll tax processing software.


Once you narrow down your options, it’s worth taking a closer look at how specific tools can fit your workflow, whether they’re free or paid.

## How to choose the right payroll processing software for your business


[Choosing the right payroll processing software](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/choosing-payroll-provider) comes down to how your business actually runs. The goal is to find a tool that fits your workflow, team, and budget.


### Start with your team type (hourly vs. salaried)


Your team structure has a big impact on what payroll processing software you need.


Hourly teams require time tracking, scheduling integration, and shift-based payroll, while salaried teams are usually more straightforward. This also affects your[total payroll costs](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-cost) and how you’ll budget for new software.


If you manage hourly workers, look for small business payroll software that connects scheduling and payroll. This reduces manual work and helps ensure employees are paid accurately for every hour worked.


### Map your current workflow (and where payroll breaks)


Before choosing software for payroll processing, take a step back and look at your current[payroll process](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-process) . Where are you spending the most time? Where do mistakes or delays happen?


Common pain points include manual data entry, disconnected systems, and last-minute changes from time tracking. Identifying these gaps helps you choose payroll solutions software that actually solves your problems.


### Prioritize must-have features vs. nice-to-haves that you’ll use


It’s easy to get distracted by extra features, but not all of them matter for your business. Focus first on must-haves like automated payroll tax processing, direct deposit, and accurate time tracking.


Nice-to-haves like advanced[payroll reporting](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-report) or HR tools can be helpful, but only if you’ll actually use them. The best payroll processing software keeps things simple while covering your core needs.


### Think about where your business is going (not just today)


Your payroll needs today might not match your needs six months from now. If you’re planning to hire more employees or[expand locations](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/multi-location-payroll) , your software should be able to grow with you.


Choosing scalable payroll processing software for small business helps you avoid[switching payroll tools later](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/how-to-switch-payroll-providers) . It also ensures your systems stay consistent as your team grows.


### Compare payroll processing software and choose what works for your needs + budget


Once you understand your workflow, team type, and priorities, it’s easier to compare options. Look at pricing, features, ease of use, and how well each tool fits your day-to-day operations.


The best payroll for small businesses is the one that saves you time,[reduces payroll errors](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/payroll-errors) , and fits your budget. Keep your decision practical, and choose the tool your team will actually use.


## Frequently asked questions about payroll processing software


### Which software is mostly used for payroll?


The software most commonly used for payroll includes QuickBooks Payroll, Gusto, and ADP. These platforms are widely adopted because they combine payroll processing software with compliance and automation features.


For small businesses, the most used payroll tools are usually ones like Homebase or Gusto that balance ease of use with affordability.


### What is the best payroll software?


The best payroll processing software depends on your business needs, team size, and workflow. For example, Homebase works well for hourly teams, while Gusto is better for all-in-one HR and payroll.


The best payroll processing software for small businesses is one that saves time, reduces errors, and fits how you already operate. Ease of use and automation should be top priorities.


### What is better for payroll, ADP or QuickBooks?


Whether ADP or QuickBooks is better for payroll depends on your business complexity and budget. ADP offers more advanced features and scalability, while QuickBooks Payroll integrates easily with QuickBooks Accounting.


For small businesses, QuickBooks is often easier to manage. Larger or more complex organizations may benefit from ADP’s broader capabilities.


### Can ChatGPT do payroll?


No, ChatGPT cannot do payroll or replace payroll processing software. Payroll requires secure systems to calculate wages, handle taxes, and process payments.


While tools like ChatGPT can help you understand payroll concepts or compare software, you’ll still need dedicated payroll solutions software to actually process payroll and pay your team.
