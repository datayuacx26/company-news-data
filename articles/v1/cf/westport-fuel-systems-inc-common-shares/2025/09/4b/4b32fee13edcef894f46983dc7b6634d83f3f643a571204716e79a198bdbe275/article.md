# AUTOGASDAY 2025: the future of alternative LPG mobility discussed in Rio de Janeiro

**LPG** is one of the most environmentally friendly and economical alternatives to traditional fuels, already chosen by over **27 million motorists** worldwide. It reduces emissions and operating costs, contributing to more sustainable and accessible mobility.


On **September 25, 2025** , **AUTOGASDAY** , the international event dedicated to alternative LPG mobility, will be held in **Rio de Janeiro** as part of **LiquidGasWeek** .


Speakers and experts from around the world will share updates, trends, and strategies for a more sustainable and efficient future in the automotive sector.


LPG is the third most widely used automotive fuel in the world and the most accessible alternative fuel. LPG emits negligible particulate matter, less CO2, and reduces emissions, including NOx. Driving an LPG vehicle is safe, easy, and generally cheaper than a gasoline or diesel model. The validity of LPG as a clean and modern automotive fuel becomes even stronger with the arrival of LPG from renewable sources.
