# Zavoli at Automotive Dealer Day 2025: technology and networks at the service of alternative mobility

**Zavoli** will be present at **Automotive Dealer Day 2025** , scheduled for **May 13-15 in Verona** , to present its solutions dedicated to sustainable mobility and, above all, to promote one of its strongest assets: the **network of specialized Zavoli Gas Point workshops.**


In a constantly evolving sector, where the energy transition requires new strategies and new skills, **Zavoli is the ideal partner for operators in the automotive supply chain** , thanks to a complete range of alternative fuel systems (LPG and methane) and a solid, widespread network of qualified workshops, which are a point of reference throughout Italy.


The **Zavoli Gas Point** network is **much more than just an after-sales service** : it is a true ecosystem of industry professionals, offering technical advice, assistance, certified installation, and ongoing support. For those involved in vehicle distribution, sales, or service, getting in touch with Zavoli means gaining access to a **valuable and skilled network** , ready to accompany the customer every step of the way.


**What you will find at our stand:**


- **Advanced solutions for alternative mobility**
- **The strength of a specialized technical network**
- **A concrete commitment to sustainability**
- **Opportunities for partnerships with industry professionals**


**Zavoli looks forward to seeing you at Automotive Dealer Day 2025, Hall 11 – Stand 18.**
