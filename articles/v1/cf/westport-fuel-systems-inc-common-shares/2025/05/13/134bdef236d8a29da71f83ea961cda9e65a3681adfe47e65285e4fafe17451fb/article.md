# BRC Gas Equipment will be present at Autopromotec 2025

**BRC Gas Equipment** will participate in the **30th edition of Autopromotec** , one of the leading international trade fairs dedicated to the automotive aftermarket, which will be held at **Bologna Fiere from May 21 to 24, 2025.**


**HALL 21 – STAND A37**


Opening hours:


*• May 21–24: 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.*


Come and visit us to discover all our latest products, including new LPG conversion systems for hybrid gasoline vehicles: a practical and sustainable solution to meet the mobility challenges of today and tomorrow.


Register for free and get your admission ticket at the following link:[https://www.autopromotec.com/visitatori/biglietti](https://www.autopromotec.com/visitatori/biglietti)


*We look forward to seeing you there!*
