# Westport Fuel Systems takes centre stage at major events dedicated to the energy transition

In recent weeks, **Westport Fuel Systems has taken part in two major international events,** underlining its commitment to developing innovative solutions for more sustainable mobility.


**At the Automotive Dealer Day in Verona, held from 19 to 21 May,** the company showcased its gas technologies, meeting with dealers, operators and industry players to share its expertise and explore opportunities for collaboration in the automotive market.


Westport Fuel Systems was also present at A **gremgas 2026, the leading event dedicated to LPG in Latin America, held in Colombia on 4 and 5 June.** The event provided an important opportunity to engage with institutions, businesses and industry professionals on the themes of innovation and sustainable development.


Participation in these events has enabled the company to strengthen its dialogue with international partners and stakeholders, consolidating its role as a leader in promoting efficient, accessible and future-oriented technologies.
