# Westport Fuel Systems is hosting the latest edition of: “𝗔𝗹𝗹 𝗔𝗿𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗙𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗼𝗿𝘆”. In Cherasco, where innovation meets technologies for the future.

**Cherasco, April 16, 2026** – Westport Fuel Systems Italia hosted the first stage of the new This year’s edition of “ **All Around the Factory** ,” the event promoted by the Mechanical Engineering Section of Confindustria Cuneo, in collaboration with the Politecnico University of Turin, is dedicated to innovation in all its forms.


The afternoon opened with a presentation by **Chief Financial Officer** and **CEO of Westport Fuel Systems Italia, Frank Spiniello** , who outlined the group’s key focuses, specializing in the development and production of LPG, natural gas, and hydrogen fuel systems for the transportation sector. He provided an overview of its core activities, developed technologies, and future prospects.


**Technical Director Danilo Ceratto and Politecnico University of Turin professor Luciano Ronaldo** then alternated on the topic of “Innovation in the Automotive Industry: Not Just Electric and Autonomous Driving. Combining the Competitive Advantage Needed Today with the Development of Tomorrow’s Technologies.”


**Westport Fuel Systems then opened its doors to attendees** for a guided tour of its mechanical production facility, allowing them to observe the company’s innovative processes and solutions closely related to the topics discussed during the event.
