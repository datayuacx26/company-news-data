# A New Look for the Next Phase of Fazeshift

We're excited to unveil Fazeshift's new brand identity—a visual evolution that reflects how far we've come and where we're headed next as the leading AI platform for accounts receivable automation.


Since launching out of Y Combinator and[raising our seed round led by Gradient Ventures](https://www.fazeshift.com/post/fazeshift-raises-4m-seed-round-led-by-gradient-to-revolutionize-accounts-receivable-automation-with-ai) , we've scaled rapidly: growing our team, expanding our product capabilities, and earning the trust of finance leaders at companies ranging from fast-growing startups to billion-dollar enterprises. Our early brand served us well, but as we've matured into an enterprise-grade AI company reshaping finance operations, it's time for our visual identity to catch up.


## **What's New**


Fazeshift's refreshed brand introduces a modern visual system designed to capture what we do best: motion, intelligence, and precision.


**A New Logo and Visual Identity** Our new logo embodies the concept of "shift"—transformation, forward momentum, and the seamless flow of data (and cash) through intelligent systems. The updated color palette and typography reflect the dual nature of our platform: the reliability and trust expected from enterprise-grade software, combined with the innovation and agility of cutting-edge AI technology.


**Refined Design Language** From our website to our product interface, we've elevated each customer interaction point to create a more cohesive, intuitive experience. The new design system mirrors how our AI agents work—intelligent, precise, and effortlessly powerful.


**What Hasn't Changed** While our look has evolved, our commitment to our customers remains unchanged. The same dedicated team, the same obsession with accuracy, and the same mission to transform how finance teams work—that's the foundation we're building on.


## **The Meaning Behind the Design**


Every element of our new brand has been thoughtfully crafted to represent what Fazeshift stands for.


The visual language draws inspiration from intelligent systems—the way data flows, transforms, and activates within our platform. The concept of "shift" appears throughout: in motion graphics that suggest transformation, in design elements that convey the seamless movement of information, and in color transitions that represent the journey from manual chaos to automated clarity.


Most importantly, the brand encapsulates our core differentiator: AI agents that think and act like experienced finance professionals. Just as our technology brings intelligence and automation to accounts receivable workflows, our visual identity brings clarity and confidence to how we present ourselves to the world.


## **The Evolution of Fazeshift**


We've moved beyond the promise of AI to the reality of AI. Our platform doesn't just analyze data—it takes action. It automates contract parsing, billing, cash application, and collections workflows that once required entire teams. It integrates seamlessly with existing systems, and it delivers results that finance leaders can trust.


This brand refresh signals where we are now—a company trusted by finance leaders to automate their most critical workflows. At the same time, it's a visual representation of the maturity, sophistication, and ambition that will define our next phase. We’re outwardly showing the world the innovation and value we've already delivered to our customers, and the ambition we have for the future of finance operations.


We've continued building aggressively for our customers, with a pipeline of new features and improvements that will make our platform even more powerful. We're deepening our integrations, expanding our AI capabilities, and continuing to push the boundaries of what's possible in finance automation.


To our customers, partners, and team: thank you for being part of this journey. We're just getting started, and we're excited to build the future of accounts receivable automation together.


**Join us for the next phase.** Explore our new website, book a demo, or reach out to learn how Fazeshift can bring powerful AI to your finance team. The best is yet to come.
