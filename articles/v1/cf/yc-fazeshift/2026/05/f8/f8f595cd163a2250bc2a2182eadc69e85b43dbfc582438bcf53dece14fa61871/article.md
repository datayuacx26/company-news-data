# Announcing Our Series A: $22M to Build the Future of Accounts Receivable

Today, we're thrilled to announce Fazeshift's Series A, bringing our total funding to **$22M** .


## **The problem we're solving**


Accounts receivable remains one of the most manual, painful workflows inside modern companies. It lives in spreadsheets, scattered email threads, and disconnected systems. It slows down how businesses get paid, ties up working capital, and forces finance teams to spend their time chasing invoices instead of driving strategy.


That's exactly why we built Fazeshift: an AI-native platform that automates and scales AR, helping finance teams move faster, operate with clarity, and unlock cash flow.


## **What we've built since 2024**


Since we kicked things off, the team has executed relentlessly. A few highlights:


- **12x revenue growth**
- **8 unicorns** now running their AR on Fazeshift
- **Team grew from 2 to 26**
- **Opened our San Francisco HQ and New York office**


Every one of those numbers reflects something more important: customers trusting us with how their business gets paid, and a team showing up every day to earn that trust.
