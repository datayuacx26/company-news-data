# Turning Everyday Homebuyers into Cash Buyers w/ Tim Herman

Robert Leonard (@therobertleonard) talks with Tim Herman about a newly launched mortgage product that turns everyday homebuyers into cash buyers, what underwriting is and how it works, why individual homebuyers are now competing with large institutional players when buying single family homes, and much, much more!


Tim Herman is a former US Navy Fighter Pilot and MBA student at Harvard Business School prior to becoming an entrepreneur and founding his current company, UpEquity. Tim was also part of the renowned startup accelerator YCombinator, and a graduate of the US Naval Academy.
