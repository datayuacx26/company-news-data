## TL;DR


Drift is a conversational marketing platform built for enterprise sales teams. Clarm is Clarm Atlas that captures and qualifies inbound autonomously—no playbook configuration, no SDR headcount. If you're building a developer product and want to capture inbound from Discord, GitHub, and docs—not just web chat—Clarm is the better fit.


## The Core Difference


Drift was designed for B2B SaaS marketing teams with dedicated sales development reps. Its value comes from routing conversations to the right human at the right time using complex playbooks. If you have a sales team, Drift can be powerful.


Clarm assumes you don't have that team—and don't want to build one. The AI handles the entire conversation: qualifying, answering technical questions, and triggering actions without human intervention.


## Feature-by-Feature Comparison


### Setup Time


Drift requires weeks of playbook design, routing rules, and SDR training before it delivers value. Clarm learns from your docs and starts working in hours. Point it at your documentation, connect your channels, and it's live.


### AI-First vs Human-First


Drift relies on SDR routing—the AI assists, but humans close. Clarm's AI is the primary operator. It handles conversations end-to-end, only escalating when a human genuinely needs to be involved.


### Channel Coverage


Drift focuses on web chat and email sequences. It doesn't connect to Discord or GitHub. Clarm captures inbound from every developer-native channel: Discord, Slack, GitHub Issues, web chat, and email.


### Buyer Intent Scoring


Both platforms offer intent scoring, but Drift's is designed for traditional B2B sales cycles with MQLs and SQLs. Clarm's intent detection is tuned for developer products—recognizing signals like pricing inquiries in Discord, enterprise evaluations in GitHub Issues, and compliance questions in community channels.


### Revenue Signal Webhooks


Clarm pipes revenue signals directly to Slack and CRM via webhooks. Drift has partial webhook support, but its primary flow routes through its own UI and requires SDR interaction.


### Content Engine & SEO


Drift doesn't include a content engine. Clarm generates SEO-optimized technical articles that drive developer traffic—turning support knowledge into organic growth.
