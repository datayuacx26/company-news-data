# Clarm vs Lindy: General AI Assistant vs Governed Agent Builder

Clarm and Lindy are both no-code agent builders that optimise for different things. Lindy optimises for breadth and speed: capable general-purpose assistants stood up quickly across email, scheduling, sales, and support. Clarm optimises for governance and data grounding: agents that answer from your own data with citations, pass through an approval gate, and leave an audit trail. The right pick follows from whether your work has to satisfy an auditor.


## Where Lindy wins


Speed to a working general-purpose assistant, a wide template library, and breadth across the common business functions. For a team that wants a capable assistant this week and whose outputs do not have to clear strict compliance evidence requirements, that breadth and pace are exactly the point, and the governance layer Clarm leads with would be overhead.


## Where Clarm wins


Clarm leads where trust has to be provable. Four properties are part of the substrate:


- **Grounding with source citations.** Every answer traces to the document it used, and the agent declines when the answer is not in your data.
- **A named-owner checkpoint** held as an invariant for outputs that matter.
- **An audit trail by default** , append-only and exportable.
- **Tenant isolation and bring-your-own model** , so your data stays separated and the model is your choice.


For a non-technical operator in a bank or a hospital, that combination is what makes the agent approvable by compliance.


## The honest split


If you want broad general-purpose assistants fast and have no compliance constraints, Lindy is a strong fit and Clarm’s governance would be weight you do not need. If the agent has to ground in your data, satisfy an approval step, and answer to an auditor, Clarm gives you that without rebuilding the compliance layer yourself.
