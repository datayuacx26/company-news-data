## TL;DR


Drift is still recognizable in the conversational marketing category, but many B2B teams now want faster setup, better AI handling, and fewer dependencies on SDRs. The best alternatives depend on whether you care most about support, CRM workflows, or AI-native qualification.


## Top Drift Alternatives


### 1. Clarm


Best for teams that want Clarm Atlas instead of a playbook-heavy chatbot. Clarm captures inbound across web chat, docs, Slack, Discord, GitHub, and email, then qualifies buyer intent and routes revenue signals automatically. Live Slack integration lets you hop into video calls with visitors in real time, voice input lowers the engagement barrier, and agentic browsing navigates visitors to the page they need instantly.


**Pricing:** Team plan at $2,000/month with unlimited agents in one workspace - a fraction of Drift's enterprise contracts. Includes premium features like visitor deanonymization.


### 2. Intercom


Best for support-led teams that already want live chat plus an established helpdesk workflow.


### 3. HubSpot Chat


Best for companies already committed to HubSpot CRM, forms, and nurture flows.


### 4. Zendesk AI


Best for larger support organizations that want better ticket efficiency, not necessarily revenue routing.


## How to Choose


- **Choose Clarm** if you want AI-first qualification, omnichannel capture, and support plus revenue in one system.
- **Choose Intercom** if you need a mature support platform and have humans available to work inbound.
- **Choose HubSpot Chat** if your team already lives in HubSpot and wants CRM-native chat.
- **Choose Zendesk AI** if support ops is the main problem you are solving.
