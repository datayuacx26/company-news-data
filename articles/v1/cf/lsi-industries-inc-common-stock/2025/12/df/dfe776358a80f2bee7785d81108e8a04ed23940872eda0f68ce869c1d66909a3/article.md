# LSI Houston Named #25 Largest Manufacturer

**Houston, TX** — LSI Industries (NASDAQ: LYTS), a leading U.S.-based manufacturer of lighting, brand imaging, and display solutions, announced that its Houston operations have been ranked the #25 Largest Manufacturer 2025 in the Houston metropolitan area by the *Houston Business Journal* .


The ranking reflects LSI’s expanding presence in the region and highlights the company’s continued investment in manufacturing capabilities, workforce development, and market innovation.


“We are extremely proud to be recognized by the Houston Business Journal,” said Michael Prachar, LSI’s Chief Marketing Officer. “Our Houston team plays a vital role in delivering integrated brand imaging solutions to customers across key vertical markets including petroleum, C-store, grocery and beyond. This ranking is a testament to their dedication and performance.”


LSI has a longstanding presence in Houston, where the company employs 260 local team members and operates more than 230,000 square feet of production, assembly, and distribution space.

**ABOUT LSI INDUSTRIES**


Headquartered in Cincinnati, LSI is a publicly held company traded over the NASDAQ Stock Exchange under the symbol LYTS. The company manufactures advanced lighting, graphics and display solutions across strategic vertical markets. The company’s American-made products, which include non-residential indoor and outdoor lighting, print graphics, digital graphics, refrigerated and custom displays, help create value for customer brands and enhance the consumer experience. LSI also provides comprehensive project management services in support of large-scale product rollouts. The company employs approximately 2,000 people at 19 manufacturing plants in the U.S. and Canada.
