# LSI Industries Donates Food and Supplies

CINCINNATI, OH — LSI Industries employees proudly collected and donated nearly 800 pounds of food and essential supplies to the[NEEDS Food Pantry](https://cincyneeds.org/) for the holiday season. LSI is proud to be an active member of the Cincinnati community and remains committed to supporting our those in the areas we operate.


“We were pleasantly surprised when you dropped off 800 pounds of food and personal care items at our pantry,” said Janie Sadlon, NEEDS Pantry Operations. “Your donation will directly help the less fortunate in the community where LSI operates.”


LSI extends a sincere thank you to all employees who contributed to making this food drive a success.


“The kindness and teamwork behind this effort show exactly what makes LSI special,” said Michael Prachar, LSI Industries Chief Marketing Officer. “I’m incredibly proud of our people.”

**ABOUT LSI INDUSTRIES**


Headquartered in Cincinnati, LSI is a publicly held company traded over the NASDAQ Stock Exchange under the symbol LYTS. The company manufactures advanced lighting, graphics and display solutions across strategic vertical markets. The company’s American-made products, which include non-residential indoor and outdoor lighting, print graphics, digital graphics, refrigerated and custom displays, help create value for customer brands and enhance the consumer experience. LSI also provides comprehensive project management services in support of large-scale product rollouts. The company employs approximately 2,000 people at 19 manufacturing plants in the U.S. and Canada.
