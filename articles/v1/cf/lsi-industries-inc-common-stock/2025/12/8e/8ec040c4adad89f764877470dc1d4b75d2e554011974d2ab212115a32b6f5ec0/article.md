# LSI Cincinnati Named #22 Largest Manufacturer

**Cincinnati, OH** — LSI Industries (NASDAQ: LYTS), a leading U.S.-based manufacturer of lighting, brand imaging, and display solutions, announced today that its Cincinnati operations have been ranked the #22 Largest Manufacturer 2025 in the Cincinnati metropolitan area by the *Cincinnati Business Courier* .


As the company’s headquarters and one of its most technologically advanced manufacturing hubs, the Cincinnati operation plays a pivotal role in supporting LSI’s customer base across key vertical markets including refueling/C-Store, grocery, sports venues, automotive, retail/office and parking. The facility serves as a center for engineering, product development, testing, and large-scale production of integrated lighting solutions and light pole manufacturing.


“We’re honored to be recognized in our home city and proud to be a United States manufacturer,” said Michael Prachar, LSI’s Chief Marketing Officer. “Our Cincinnati team is committed to delivering exceptional solutions for our customers, and this ranking reflects their dedication to quality, innovation, and collaboration. Everything we produce here helps our clients create stronger, more compelling brand experiences.”


LSI Industries was founded in Cincinnati in 1976 and has more than 500 employees across its headquarters and 3 manufacturing facilities in the area.

**ABOUT LSI INDUSTRIES**


Headquartered in Cincinnati, LSI is a publicly held company traded over the NASDAQ Stock Exchange under the symbol LYTS. The company manufactures advanced lighting, graphics and display solutions across strategic vertical markets. The company’s American-made products, which include non-residential indoor and outdoor lighting, print graphics, digital graphics, refrigerated and custom displays, help create value for customer brands and enhance the consumer experience. LSI also provides comprehensive project management services in support of large-scale product rollouts. The company employs approximately 2,000 people at 19 manufacturing plants in the U.S. and Canada.
