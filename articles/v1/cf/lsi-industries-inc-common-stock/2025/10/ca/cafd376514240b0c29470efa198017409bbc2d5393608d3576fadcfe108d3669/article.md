# LSI’s V-LOCITY™ Area Light Earns Distinction in 2025 IES Industry Progress Report

**CINCINNATI** — LSI Industries is proud to announce that its[V-LOCITY™ Area Light](https://lsicorp.com/lighting/product/v-locity-area-light-medium/) has been selected for recognition in the[2025 IES Industry Progress Report](https://ies.org/) , the Illuminating Engineering Society’s annual review of noteworthy advances in the lighting industry. This selection affirms LSI’s ongoing commitment to innovation, performance, and value in outdoor lighting solutions.


The IES Industry Progress Report spotlights lighting products and advances that stand out in terms of uniqueness, technical achievement, and potential significance to the lighting community. As described by the IES Progress Report guidelines, acceptance is determined via an impartial judging process evaluating innovation, impact, and differentiation. progress.ies.org


LSI’s V-LOCITY™ Area Light was acknowledged for its blend of advanced optical engineering, flexible deployment features, and strong performance metrics. A few of its many standout attributes include:


- **Exceptional photometry & efficiency** — Best-in-class distributions, optimized for maximum target efficiency and lumens-per-watt.
- **Field adaptability** — Interchangeable mounting arms, field-rotatable optics, and replaceable light engine/optics allow for on-site adjustments and retrofits.
- **Robust design & ratings** — Rated for ambient temperatures up to 50 °C, with options for integral sensors (Bluetooth, motion/photocell) and backed by a 5-year warranty.
- **Wide application range** — Engineered for parking lots, roadways, walkways, commercial sites, sports courts, loading zones, and more.


“Being recognized by IES in their annual Progress Report is a meaningful honor for us,” said Michael Prachar, Chief Marketing Officer at LSI. “It validates the hard work our engineering, product development and design teams invest in pushing boundaries and delivering real performance advantages to specifiers, installers, and end users.”


Since its launch, V-LOCITY™ Light has been well received in the market, valued for its combination of high performance, field flexibility, and long-term value. This accolade from IES further strengthens its position as a benchmark product in outdoor area lighting applications.


For more information on V-LOCITY™ Area Light, including specifications, photometrics, and installation guides, please visit LSI’s product page.


To explore the full IES Industry Progress Report and other recognized innovations, visit the IES Progress website.

### ABOUT LSI INDUSTRIES


Headquartered in Cincinnati, LSI is a publicly held company traded over the NASDAQ Stock Exchange under the symbol LYTS. The company manufactures advanced lighting, graphics and display solutions across strategic vertical markets. The company’s American-made products, which include non-residential indoor and outdoor lighting, print graphics, digital graphics, refrigerated and custom displays, help create value for customer brands and enhance the consumer experience. LSI also provides comprehensive project management services in support of large-scale product rollouts. The company employs approximately 2,000 people at 19 manufacturing plants in the U.S. and Canada.
