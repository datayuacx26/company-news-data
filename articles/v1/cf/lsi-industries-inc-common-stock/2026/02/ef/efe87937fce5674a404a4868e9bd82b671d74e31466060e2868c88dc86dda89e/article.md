# LSI Lighting Featured in Multiple ASBA Award-Winning Athletic Facilities

**CINCINNATI, Ohio** – LSI Lighting is proud to be featured in multiple ASBA award-winning athletic facilities recognized during the[American Sports Builders Association](https://sportsbuilders.org/) ’s (ASBA)[2025 annual awards program](https://sportsbuilders.org/page/facilityawards) .


ASBA, the national organization for builders and suppliers of materials for athletic facilities, presents its awards annually to projects built by ASBA members that exemplify excellence in design, construction and overall performance. Award categories include Tennis Courts, Running Tracks, Sports Fields, Pickleball Facilities, Multi-Purpose/Fieldhouse and Track & Field Facilities, with select categories recognizing both single- and multiple-field facilities.


New for 2025, ASBA introduced a Tennis and Pickleball Facilities category to reflect the continued growth of multi-sport venues as communities invest in facilities that support both sports.


Award winners were announced at ASBA’s 2025 Technical Meeting, held in San Antonio, Texas. The meeting coincided with ASBA’s 60th anniversary and drew a record attendance of nearly 1,400 industry professionals.


Projects were evaluated based on criteria including layout and design, site work, drainage, base construction, surface quality, amenities, innovation and overall impression. Winning entries met or exceeded ASBA’s established scoring standards.

**ABOUT LSI INDUSTRIES**
Headquartered in Cincinnati, LSI is a publicly held company traded over the NASDAQ Stock Exchange under the symbol LYTS. The company manufactures advanced lighting, graphics and display solutions across strategic vertical markets. The company’s American-made products, which include non-residential indoor and outdoor lighting, print graphics, digital graphics, refrigerated and custom displays, help create value for customer brands and enhance the consumer experience. LSI also provides comprehensive project management services in support of large-scale product rollouts. The company employs approximately 2,000 people at 19 manufacturing plants in the U.S. and Canada.
