# LSI Houston Named #34 Largest Manufacturer

**Houston, TX** — LSI Industries ([NASDAQ: LYTS](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/lyts) ), a leading U.S.-based manufacturer of lighting, brand imaging, and display solutions, announced that its Houston operations have been ranked the #34 Largest Manufacturer 2026 in the Houston metropolitan area by the[Houston Business Journal](https://www.bizjournals.com/houston) .
The ranking reflects LSI’s expanding presence in the region and highlights the company’s continued investment in manufacturing capabilities, workforce development, and market innovation.
“What sets LSI apart is our ability to translate brand strategy into physical environments, and our Houston operation is central to that mission,” said Michael Prachar, Chief Marketing Officer at LSI. “This ranking reflects the team’s ability to deliver integrated graphics and architectural solutions that elevate customer experience across entire brand networks.”
LSI has a longstanding presence in Houston, where the company employs **215 local team members** and operates more than 230,000 square feet of production, assembly, and distribution space. LSI has nearly **3,000 employees** across its **23 facilities** in the United States and Canada.

**ABOUT LSI INDUSTRIES**
Headquartered in Cincinnati, LSI is a publicly held company traded over the NASDAQ Stock Exchange under the symbol LYTS. The company manufactures advanced lighting, brand imaging and display solutions across strategic vertical markets. The company’s American-made products, which include non-residential indoor and outdoor lighting, print graphics, digital graphics, refrigerated, heated and custom displays, help create value for customer brands and enhance the consumer experience. LSI also provides comprehensive project management services in support of large-scale product rollouts. The company employs approximately 3,000 people at 23 manufacturing plants in the U.S. and Canada.
