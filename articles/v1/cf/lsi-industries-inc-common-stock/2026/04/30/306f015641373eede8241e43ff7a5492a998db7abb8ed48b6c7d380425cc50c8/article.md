# LSI Cincinnati Named #23 Largest Manufacturer

**Cincinnati, OH** — LSI Industries ([NASDAQ: LYTS](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/lyts) ), a leading U.S.-based manufacturer of lighting, brand imaging, and display solutions, announced today that its Cincinnati operations have been ranked the #23 Largest Manufacturer 2026 in the Cincinnati metropolitan area by the[Cincinnati Business Courier](https://www.bizjournals.com/Cincinnati) .
As the company’s headquarters and one of its most technologically advanced manufacturing hubs, the Cincinnati operation plays a pivotal role in supporting LSI’s customer base across key vertical markets including refueling/C-Store, grocery, sports venues, automotive, retail/office, restaurant and parking. The facility serves as a center for engineering, product development, testing, and large-scale production of integrated lighting solutions and light pole manufacturing.
“As Chief Marketing Officer, I see firsthand how critical our Cincinnati operations are to the strength of the LSI brand,” said Michael Prachar, Chief Marketing Officer at LSI. “As we celebrate 50 years in business, this recognition reinforces the role we’ve built over decades in shaping modern retail and fueling our customers’ growth.”
LSI Industries was founded in Cincinnati in 1976 and has more than 500 employees across its headquarters and 3 manufacturing facilities in the Cincinnati area. LSI has nearly 3,000 employees across its 23 facilities in the United States and Canada.

**ABOUT LSI INDUSTRIES**
Headquartered in Cincinnati, LSI is a publicly held company traded over the NASDAQ Stock Exchange under the symbol LYTS. The company manufactures advanced lighting, brand imaging and display solutions across strategic vertical markets. The company’s American-made products, which include non-residential indoor and outdoor lighting, print graphics, digital graphics, refrigerated, heated and custom displays, help create value for customer brands and enhance the consumer experience. LSI also provides comprehensive project management services in support of large-scale product rollouts. The company employs approximately 3,000 people at 23 manufacturing plants in the U.S. and Canada.
