# EMI Industries announced as finalist for BAMA’s 2026 Manufacturer of the Year Award

**TAMPA, FL** – EMI Industries is proud to be a finalist for the Bay Area Manufacturers Association’s 2026 Manufacturer of the Year Award.


BAMA, a member organization for manufacturers located in the Tampa Bay area, has annual awards focused on innovation, leadership and development in the Tampa Bay community and manufacturing industry.


“EMI is honored to be recognized by BAMA for our innovation and achievement in manufacturing,” said Alan Harvill, CEO of EMI Industries. “As a United States manufacturer, we remain committed to serving our community in Tampa and bringing quality and dedication to everything we produce.”


EMI, an LSI company (NASDAQ: LYTS), employs approximately 150 people at its headquarters in Tampa, where they have operated since 1979. With multiple regional locations, EMI provides standard and custom millwork, refrigeration and metal displays and equipment for retail and restaurant locations across the country.


The winner will be announced at the BAMA Annual Awards Banquet on June 18 in Tampa.


To learn more about EMI’s commitment to quality store fixtures and food service equipment, visit[Handcrafted Custom Millwork – EMI Industries](https://lsicorp.com/emi/)

**ABOUT LSI INDUSTRIES**
Headquartered in Cincinnati, LSI is a publicly held company traded over the NASDAQ Stock Exchange under the symbol LYTS. The company manufactures advanced lighting, graphics, and display solutions across strategic vertical markets. The company’s American-made products, which include non-residential indoor and outdoor lighting, print graphics, digital graphics, refrigerated and custom displays, help create value for customer brands and enhance the consumer experience. LSI also provides comprehensive project management services in support of large-scale product rollouts. The company employs approximately 3,000 people at 23 manufacturing plants in the U.S. and Canada. Additional information about LSI is available at www.lsicorp.com.
