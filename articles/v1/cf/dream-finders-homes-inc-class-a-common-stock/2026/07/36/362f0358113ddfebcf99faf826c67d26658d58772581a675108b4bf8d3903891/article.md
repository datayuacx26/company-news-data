## What Is a VA Loan?


# **A VA loan is a mortgage backed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and offered through approved private lenders. The program was created to help eligible military members, veterans, and certain surviving spouses achieve homeownership with fewer financial barriers than many traditional loan options. While the VA guarantees a portion of the loan, you'll still work directly with a participating lender throughout the mortgage process. The result is a financing option designed to make buying a home more affordable and attainable for those who have served our country.**


### Key Benefits of a VA Loan


# **One of the biggest reasons buyers choose a VA loan is the combination of flexibility and long-term savings. Depending on your eligibility and lender requirements, you may enjoy benefits such as: No down payment for qualified buyers. Many eligible borrowers can finance the full purchase price, making it easier to buy a home without years of saving for a large down payment. Competitive interest rates. Because the loan is backed by the VA, lenders often offer favorable rates compared to other mortgage programs. No private mortgage insurance (PMI). Unlike many conventional loans with smaller down payments, VA loans generally do not require monthly PMI, helping reduce your overall housing costs. Flexible credit qualifications. While lenders establish their own credit guidelines, VA loans are often more flexible than conventional financing, making homeownership possible for a broader range of qualified buyers. These advantages can make a meaningful difference for military families relocating to the Fayetteville area or purchasing their first home after military service.**


## Who Is Eligible for a VA Loan?


# **VA loans are available to eligible individuals who meet military service requirements established by the Department of Veterans Affairs. Before beginning your home search, you'll want to obtain a Certificate of Eligibility (COE), which confirms that you're eligible to use your VA home loan benefit. The easiest way to obtain your COE is through a VA-approved lender, who can often request it electronically as part of your mortgage application.**
