## What Is a Resort Lifestyle Community? Everything You Need to Know


Imagine having resort-inspired amenities, social events, and spaces to relax just steps from your front door. That’s the appeal of a resort community. A neighborhood designed around connection, recreation, and a more enjoyable way to live. Whether you’re looking for an active lifestyle, more opportunities to meet neighbors, or everyday conveniences, resort style communities bring a unique alternative to traditional neighborhoods.


## What Amenities Are Typically Found in a Resort Lifestyle Community?


One of the biggest draws of resort lifestyle communities is the variety of features designed to make every day feel like a getaway. Many communities also include a Lifestyle Director who curates activities, events, and experiences that help residents connect and make the most of the community.


- **Resort-style pools** : cabanas and lounge areas
- **Clubhouses** : resident events, clubs, and social activities
- **Fitness centers** : workout spaces and group classes
- **Sports courts** : pickleball, bocce ball, and tennis
- **Outdoor spaces** : parks, walking paths, and green spaces


## What’s the Difference Between a Resort Lifestyle Community and a Regular Neighborhood?


A traditional neighborhood typically focuses on the homes, while resort lifestyle communities are designed around a complete experience. Beyond a home, residents enjoy shared spaces, planned activities, and amenities that encourage recreation and connection.


Communities like Dream Finders Homes’ newest resort lifestyle community,[Reverie at Looking Glass](https://dreamfindershomes.com/active-adult/co/parker/reverie-at-looking-glass/) , offer a blend of thoughtfully planned spaces, social opportunities, and everyday experiences.


## Are Resort Lifestyle Communities Only for Retirees?


While some active adult communities are designed specifically for residents 55 and older, many modern resort communities welcome a variety of ages. Families, professionals, and empty nesters are increasingly choosing these neighborhoods for their amenities, social opportunities, and low-maintenance lifestyle.


## What HOA Fees or Costs Come With Resort-Style Living?


HOA fees are common in resort style living and help maintain shared amenities, landscaping, community spaces, and events. Costs can vary so it’s important to understand what’s included when comparing communities. Learn more about what to consider with Dream Finders’[HOA vs. CDD fees guide](https://dreamfindershomes.com/blog/hoa-vs-cdd-understanding-the-true-cost-of-community-living/) .


## What Types of Resort Communities Are There?


There are several types of resort lifestyle communities designed around different interests and lifestyles, including:


- **Active adult communities** with social activities and age-focused experiences
- **Golf communities** centered around courses and club experiences
- **Waterfront communities** providing access to lakes or beaches
- **Master-planned communities** with extensive amenities for residents of all ages
