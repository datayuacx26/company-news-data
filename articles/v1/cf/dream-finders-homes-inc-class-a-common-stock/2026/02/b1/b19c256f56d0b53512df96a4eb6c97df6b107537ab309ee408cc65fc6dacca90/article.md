# Wood Framing vs. Cinderblock: Understanding the Difference

If you’re shopping for a new home in Jacksonville, you may notice that builders use different construction methods. At Dream Finders Homes, we build with wood framing, and many buyers ask how it compares to cinderblock (also known as concrete masonry unit, or CMU) construction.


****


Both methods meet strict building codes, but wood framing offers a combination of performance, efficiency, and flexibility that fits modern lifestyles and Florida living exceptionally well. Here’s a clear, side‑by‑side look at the differences and why Dream Finders Homes trusts wood framing in


[our Jacksonville communities](https://dreamfindershomes.com/new-homes/fl/jacksonville/) .


## The Core Differences: Wood Framing vs. Cinderblock


### Wood Framing


****


Wood framing has been the standard for residential construction across the U.S. for decades. Today’s homes are built with engineered, treated lumber designed to resist moisture, pests, and rot.


Key advantages include:


****


Strength with flexibility: Wood naturally flexes under pressure, helping it absorb wind forces rather than crack.


****


Faster build times: Wood is quicker and easier to work with, which often means earlier move‑in dates.


Energy efficiency: As a natural insulator, wood delivers strong thermal performance and improved indoor comfort.


****


Design versatility: Open floor plans, larger windows, and future layout changes are easier to achieve.


Proven performance: Millions of wood‑framed homes meet or exceed modern safety and durability codes.


### Cinderblock (CMU)


****


Cinderblock construction is more common in certain regions and building types, but it comes with trade‑offs.


****


Considerations include:


****


Solid construction: CMU is durable and resistant to some forms of wear.


Higher costs: Materials and specialized labor typically increase the overall home price.


Longer timelines:


CMU construction takes more time, which can delay completion.


Lower natural insulation: Additional insulation is often required to meet energy standards.


Limited flexibility: Structural changes and renovations are more complex and costly.


## Why Wood Framing Makes Sense in Jacksonville


Some buyers assume heavier materials automatically mean stronger homes. In reality, modern wood framing is engineered to perform exceptionally well, especially when built to Florida codes.


Here's why wood framing stands out:


****


Built to Code and Beyond


Wood‑framed homes are constructed to rigorous standards for safety and longevity. Today’s treated lumber is stronger and more resilient than ever.


****


Superior Wind Resistance


Properly built wood framing can flex and absorb high winds without cracking. When paired with hurricane straps, bracing, and code‑approved fasteners, it performs well in Jacksonville’s climate.


Better Energy Performance


Wood’s natural insulating properties help keep homes cooler in summer and reduce HVAC strain, an important benefit in Florida’s heat.


Unmatched Value


Because wood framing builds faster and requires less specialized labor, it offers excellent value without sacrificing quality or durability.


Easier to Adapt Over Time


From future renovations to layout updates, wood framing allows your home to evolve with your needs, without major structural hurdles.
