# Emobi Partners with Keysight to Enhance Plug & Charge Technology for the EV Industry

At Emobi, we’re committed to providing seamless, secure, and innovative solutions for the ever-evolving electric vehicle (EV) ecosystem. Today, we’re excited to announce our partnership with Keysight Technologies, a leader in design, emulation, and test solutions. This collaboration will elevate our Plug & Charge technology and streamline the EV charging experience for manufacturers (OEMs) and charger operators.


‍


**What This Partnership Means for the EV Industry**


As North America’s largest EV charging ecosystem, Emobi is already at the forefront of making EV charging easier, more accessible, and more reliable. Our Plug & Charge technology automatically identifies the EV and securely processes payment without the need for driver interaction, making the charging process faster and more convenient.


‍


Through our partnership with Keysight, we’re taking our commitment to the next level. By integrating Keysight’s advanced hardware testing capabilities, we can ensure that every Plug & Charge deployment meets the highest standards of quality and reliability. This means fewer disruptions for our customers and smoother integration of Plug & Charge across a wide range of EV manufacturers and charger operators.


‍


**What’s New with Emobi’s Plug & Charge Solution?**


With this new partnership, Emobi will provide every new customer—whether an EV OEM or charger operator—with Keysight hardware for Plug & Charge testing. From initial setup to commercial deployment, Emobi will manage the entire process, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free experience for all parties involved.


‍


We understand that time and resources are precious when adopting new technology. That’s why we’ve designed our Plug & Charge system to be as seamless and efficient as possible. With Keysight’s support, we can now offer industry-leading testing and validation, eliminating the need for additional testing or setup headaches for our customers.


‍


John Wilson, Keysight Automotive Solutions Director, highlighted the importance of this collaboration:


*“Emobi’s solution enhances the user experience by simplifying charging and payment, making it easier for both OEMs and charger operators to integrate Plug & Charge into their infrastructure. Partnering with Keysight for self-certification helps ensure that Emobi’s solutions meet the highest standards of quality and reliability. It’s great to see such collaborative efforts driving the future of e-mobility!”*


‍


**Looking Ahead**


This partnership marks a significant step toward creating a more efficient, secure, and reliable EV charging infrastructure. As demand for EVs and charging stations continues to grow, Emobi’s collaboration with Keysight will provide the testing, certification, and integration support needed to keep Plug & Charge technology at the forefront of the industry.
‍


With Keysight hardware supporting our solutions, Emobi is poised to offer customers the tools and confidence they need to implement cutting-edge EV charging technology, all while minimizing operational disruptions.


‍


Stay tuned for more updates as we continue to expand our partnership with Keysight and work toward our vision of a fully connected and sustainable future for e-mobility.


‍
