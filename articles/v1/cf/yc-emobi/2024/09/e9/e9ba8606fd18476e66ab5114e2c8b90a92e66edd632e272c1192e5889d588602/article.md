# Emobi and Noodoe Partner on AI-Powered Plug & Charge Solution to Revolutionize the EV Charging Experience

In today’s rapidly evolving world of EVs, seamless charging is key to driving the adoption of sustainable transportation. At[Emobi](https://www.emobi.ai/) , we have continued to elevate the EV charging experience through our partnership with[Noodoe](https://www.noodoe.com/) , a leader in EV charging management solutions. This collaboration, which began last year, enhances our Plug & Charge model with comprehensive training data and advanced technology, leading to a more efficient and error-free EV charging experience.


‍


**A Game-Changing Plug & Charge Experience**


The Plug & Charge model is revolutionizing how EV drivers access charging stations. This advanced technology facilitates automatic identification, secure payment, and quick charging without any manual intervention. With Emobi’s AI-driven ecosystem and Noodoe’s robust data from historical sessions and errors, the collaboration promises an efficient and error-free charging process.


As the demand for charging infrastructure grows with the expansion of EV adoption, Plug & Charge offers scalability to support global sustainability efforts. Its seamless approach eliminates charging complexity, allowing drivers to simply plug in their vehicles and let the system handle the rest.


‍


**Data-Driven Innovation**


By providing extensive historical data on charging sessions and errors, Noodoe helps train Emobi’s AI models, ensuring automated error handling and efficient charging retries. This integration reduces downtime, optimizes operational efficiency, and improves data quality—all critical to delivering a superior charging experience.


"Partnering with Emobi to introduce AI-assisted Plug & Charge solutions marks a significant step forward,” said Roman Kleinerman, VP of Products at Noodoe. "This integration not only elevates efficiency but also sets a new industry benchmark for mission-critical software development."


The collaboration addresses some of the most pressing challenges in the EV charging sector: enhancing data accuracy, streamlining charging processes, and reducing operational costs for charging station hosts. The result is a consistent, reliable, and user-friendly solution that benefits drivers, station operators, and the broader push toward sustainable transportation.


‍


**A Vision for the Future of EV Charging**


For both companies, this partnership represents a win-win solution for the EV industry. “Emobi’s mission has always been to enhance the EV charging experience through innovation,” said Lin Sun Fa, CEO of Emobi. “Partnering with Noodoe allows us to further integrate their capabilities and data into our ecosystem, making EV charging more enjoyable for users and site hosts alike.”


As the world shifts toward electrification, solutions like Plug & Charge will play an integral role in building a robust and reliable charging network. Emobi and Noodoe are at the forefront of this revolution, paving the way for an electric future that’s both sustainable and convenient.
