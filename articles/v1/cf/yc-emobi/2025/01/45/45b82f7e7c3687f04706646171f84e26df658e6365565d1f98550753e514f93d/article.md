# Emobi and SBD Technologies Partner to Revolutionize Fleet Charging Experience

SBD Technologies, a leader in AI-driven EV adoption for municipal and commercial fleets. This collaboration brings together Emobi's expansive network of over 120,000 charging locations across 26+ networks and SBD’s innovative FleetCharge solution, simplifying the EV charging experience for fleet operators and drivers.


‍


**What This Partnership Means for Fleet Operators**


As North America’s leading digital infrastructure for EV charging, Emobi is already at the forefront of making charging more seamless, accessible, and reliable. With FleetCharge, fleet operators will now have streamlined access to one of the largest and most diverse charging networks, backed by Emobi's advanced data and AI-powered insights.


‍


The integration of Emobi’s technology with FleetCharge addresses critical challenges such as range anxiety, unreliable charging data, and payment complexities. Fleet managers will now benefit from a unified, user-friendly platform that consolidates charging data, tracks energy usage and costs, and handles payments across home, depot, and public locations.


‍


**How Emobi's Data Enhances FleetCharge**


Emobi’s Hub technology acts as the backbone for this integration, delivering AI-enhanced data to help fleet managers make smarter, real-time decisions when selecting charging stations. This data refinement ensures that fleet operators have access to the most accurate, reliable, and context-rich information, enhancing operational efficiency and improving the user experience.


‍


**A Step Toward a Greener Future**


*“Our partnership with SBD Technologies is a major leap forward in enabling EV adoption,”* said Lin Sun Fa, CEO of Emobi. *“Together, we’re not only making fleet operations more efficient but also empowering fleet managers to adopt sustainable practices with greater ease and confidence.”*


‍


Kamal Ayad, President of SBD Technologies, echoed this sentiment, saying, *“By integrating Emobi’s infrastructure into FleetCharge, we are equipping fleet operators with the tools they need to efficiently manage their EVs while removing barriers to widespread EV adoption.”*


‍


**Looking Ahead**


This partnership highlights our shared vision to accelerate EV adoption in North America. Together with SBD Technologies, we’re creating smarter, more sustainable fleet operations that will drive the future of EV charging.


‍


Stay tuned for more updates as we continue to work with SBD Technologies to transform fleet charging and bring our vision of a sustainable, EV-powered future to life.
