# Gilead Sciences: Research Scholars Program

## **Program overview**


The Research Scholars Program supports meaningful research from emerging investigators that help to address knowledge gaps and drive medical breakthroughs within the following therapeutic areas:


#### HIV and Viral Hepatitis


#### Primary Biliary Cholangitis


#### Oncology, Solid Tumors


The Research Scholars Program provides two years of financial support to junior faculty researchers. Award amounts vary globally, although generally each recipient is provided $180,000 USD paid in two annual installments directly to the scholar’s institution. Funding for the second year is contingent upon submission of a progress report and approval by the chair of the program’s scientific review committee.


We also offer a Public Health program for junior researchers across low- to middle-income countries as part of our commitment to advancing global health.


*Award amounts can vary globally
