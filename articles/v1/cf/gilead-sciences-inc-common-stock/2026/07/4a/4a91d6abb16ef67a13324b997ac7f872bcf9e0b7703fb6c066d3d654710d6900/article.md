# Gilead Sciences’ Approach to Research & Drug Discovery & Development

## **Accelerating Innovation**


Our track record of success inspires our pursuit of transformative medicines and cures across virology, oncology and inflammation. We’re driven to generate advances in drug discovery, out-innovate current therapeutics and help save the lives of millions of people around the world.


Over our 35-plus-year history, through our extensive early research and development program, we have consistently identified best-in-class molecules, biologics, cell therapies and combinations and brought them forward with speed and efficiency. We have helped transform global public health challenges like HIV and hepatitis C into manageable or even curable conditions and are poised to deliver revolutionary therapies in oncology and inflammation.
