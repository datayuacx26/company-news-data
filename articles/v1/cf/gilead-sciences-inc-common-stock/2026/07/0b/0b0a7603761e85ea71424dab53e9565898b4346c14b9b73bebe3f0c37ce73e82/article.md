## **Inflammation**


RFP Programs and Topics of Interest


We offer both Request for Proposal (RFP) Programs and RFP Topics of Interest. An RFP Program specifically describes the research topic and may include detailed instructions on submitting a Letter of Interest (LOI) and full proposal. RFP Topics of Interest are brief descriptions of research topics that are of interest to the company. Both RFP types can be submitted through Gilead’s online proposal submission portal, Gilead OPTICS.


For RFP Programs focused on community organizations, visit[Community-Based Participatory Research](https://www.gilead.com/science/research/research-funding-programs/community-based-participatory-research) .
