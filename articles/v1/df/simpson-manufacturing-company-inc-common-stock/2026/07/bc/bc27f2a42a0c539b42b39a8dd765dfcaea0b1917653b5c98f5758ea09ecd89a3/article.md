# Simpson Strong-Tie Donates $25,000 to the Red CrossFollowing Venezuela Earthquakes

07/09/26


Simpson Strong-Tie Donates $25,000 to the Red CrossFollowing Venezuela Earthquakes


Simpson Strong-Tie has donated $25,000 to the American Red Cross in support of relief efforts following the devastating earthquakes in Venezuela on June 24, 2026. The 7.2- and 7.5-magnitude earthquakes have so far claimed the lives of nearly 3,350 people, with close to 50,000 still unaccounted for, while causing structural losses across La Guaira, the capital city of Caracas and several states.


The International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement has deployed assistance in the affected areas, including 326 volunteers and 62 staff members from the Venezuelan Red Cross. Setting up a field hospital and first aid stations, distributing humanitarian aid, providing shelter, and family reunification are among the immediate priority support actions underway.


“The images coming out of Venezuela over the last several days have been extremely heartbreaking to see; our thoughts are with the Venezuelan people as they deal with this overwhelming tragedy,” said Simpson Strong-Tie President and CEO Mike Olosky. “As search-and-rescue efforts continue, we’re grateful to the Red Cross and Red Crescent for their swift and dedicated response on the ground and across their network.”


Along with the initial donation, Simpson Strong-Tie is matching employee donations up to a total of $5,000 per year.


This contribution reflects the company’s ongoing commitment to aid in disaster relief and recovery efforts around the world. To donate, visit www.redcross.org.
