## **What Is a Loan Origination System?**


A loan origination system (LOS) is the system teams use to manage business loan files from first submission through credit review and decision.


It brings every part of[loan origination](https://www.herondata.io/blog/what-is-loan-origination-software) into one place, allowing MCA brokers, funders, and SMB funding teams to review files without switching between emails, folders, and spreadsheets.


In simple terms, an LOS helps teams handle business applications, MCA submissions, and full commercial loan packages in a clear and consistent way. Applications come in, documents are attached to the correct file, and teams review the information needed to move the[credit process](https://www.herondata.io/blog/credit-process-automation) forward.


For credit unions and community financial institutions, a loan origination solution supports loan growth by keeping each business loan portfolio organized and review-ready.


Instead of manual document handling, teams rely on built-in document management, basic credit scoring inputs from credit bureaus, and checks tied to regulatory requirements.


This setup helps reduce credit risk, improve borrower satisfaction, and support modern lending without changing how teams already work.

## **FAQs About the Loan Origination System**


### **What is the difference between LMS and LOS?**


An LOS handles the intake, review, and approval of new business loan applications, while an LMS manages loans after funding, such as payments, balances, and servicing. Some teams also use LOS steps to support regulatory compliance, mainly for recordkeeping and approvals, often through automated workflows that prepare files before underwriting.


### **How does a loan originator get paid?**


A loan originator usually gets paid through commissions, fees, or a percentage of the funded loan amount, depending on the agreement and loan type. In business lending, pay often reflects deal volume and quality rather than consumer lending compensation models.


### **What are the four stages of the loan origination process?**


The four stages are application intake, document collection and review, underwriting and credit decision, and approval or decline. Many systems support these stages through customizable workflows, handling files that arrive digitally or in branch, including signed documents and e-signature records.


### **What are the 7 types of loans?**


Common loan types include term loans, lines of credit, merchant cash advances, equipment financing, invoice factoring, SBA loans, and commercial real estate loans. A loan origination system helps teams manage these loan types in one place by keeping applications, documents, and review status tied to each business file.


### **How do teams evaluate loan origination systems before choosing one?**


Teams often review options through demos, comparisons, and vendor resources such as a product overview or white paper.
