# One For All successful participation at IFA 2025

### UEI's retail brand One For All showcases innovation at IFA Berlin 2025


IFA Berlin—often considered the European counterpart to CES in Las Vegas—was a **fantastic success** for One For All, UEI’s Retail brand. This year, the **Retail team proudly introduced new solutions for home entertainment, work-from-home, and gaming experiences** , and the response from both the trade and the press was **outstanding** .


#### **Product Launch Highlights**


At the heart of the showcase was the **Smart Control Pro Touch** the new flagship model of the Smart Control Pro family. Designed to simplify and personalize home entertainment, the Smart Control Pro Touch brings together:


- **Touchscreen innovation** with an always-on e-paper display
- **App-powered customization** through the One For All Advanced App
- **Premium ergonomic design** featuring a dark metallic finish and full keypad backlight
- **Versatile compatibility** with up to 12 devices, including Bluetooth-controlled devices like Google TV, Apple TV, and Amazon Fire Stick
- **Smart features** such as customizable dashboards, activities & macros, “find your remote,” and continuous updates
Together, these innovations confirmed One For All’s position as a


**benchmark in universal remote control solutions** .


#### **Presence at IFA Berlin**


One For All’s booth in **Hall 25, Stand 317** was **vibrant, busy, and buzzing with visitors** throughout the exhibition. Partners, retailers, and industry professionals had the chance to explore not only the Smart Control Pro Touch but also a full portfolio of **new retail solutions** designed to elevate the customer experience.
