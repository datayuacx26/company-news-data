# UEI Showcases Next-Gen Energy Management and HVAC Control at AHR 2026

****


**Scottsdale, AZ –** At AHR 2026 in Las Vegas, Universal Electronics Inc. (UEI) will debut its latest advancements in intelligent climate control, headlined by QuickSet homeSense and TIDE Touch. These solutions are designed to redefine how homes manage energy and optimize HVAC performance through adaptive intelligence.


#### **QuickSet homeSense: Intelligence for Energy Optimization**


Designed for residential, multi-dwelling units (MDU), and utility environments, the TIDE Touch ecosystem offers a unified platform for smart home management. Key features include:


- **Seamless Climate Integration:** Unified control of indoor climate, lighting, and sensing for a secure, connected experience.
- **Energy Insight & Optimization:** Real-time monitoring and smart scheduling to lower utility costs.
- **Broad Interoperability:** A customizable rules engine that adapts to user routines, automating comfort and security settings across diverse device categories.
- **Remote Diagnostics:** Cloud connectivity supports remote updates and diagnostics, ensuring long-term hardware reliability and peak HVAC performance.


> Our focus at AHR 2026 is delivering innovations that provide real-world value through smarter energy management. We are shaping the future of the connected home by ensuring our sensing and control solutions lead the way in sustainability and HVAC efficiency.”
>
>
> Richard Carnifax, Interim CEO/COO, Universal Electronics Inc.


#### **Experience the Future of Energy Management and HVAC Control at AHR 2026**


Visit UEI at the Las Vegas Convention Center, **Booth #C353** in the Building Automation and Control Solutions area, February 2–5, to see these energy management solutions in action. Fill in the form to book a meeting with the team.


**Universal Electronics Inc.** (NASDAQ: UEIC) is the global leader in universal wireless control solutions for the home. The company brings to life millions of innovative control products each year that focus on a user-centric approach to designing and creating solutions and applications that simplify user interaction with highly complex technologies in the home and removing interoperability challenges as a roadblock for user adoption, with a privacy first and secure by design approach to today’s smart devices.
