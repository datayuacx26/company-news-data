# ProQR recognised as a Great Place To Work

Great Place To Work is a globally recognized leader in workplace culture with three decades of experience in helping companies create a culture of trust. The GPTW survey measures trust, credibility, respect, honesty, pride and camaraderie in an organization.


The GPTW initiative provided an opportunity for ProQRians to have their voices heard and to shape our unique culture. At ProQR, we recognize that our people are our greatest strength. That’s why we’re committed to fostering a collaborative, growth driven culture where innovation thrives, and individuals can grow – both professionally and personally – as we work to develop transformative RNA therapies for patients and their families.
