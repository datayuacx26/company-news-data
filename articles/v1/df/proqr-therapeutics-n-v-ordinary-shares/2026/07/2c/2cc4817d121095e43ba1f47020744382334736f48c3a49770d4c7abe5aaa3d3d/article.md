# Today marks an expansion of the ProQR and Lilly partnership

Since the beginning, ProQR has been focused on RNA technologies to develop medicines for people and families with high unmet medical need. In August of this year, we[focused our strategy exclusively on furthering the development of our Axiomer RNA editing platform technology](https://www.proqr.com/community-stories-and-news/proqr-update-august-2022) , which allows selective editing of individual bases in the RNA.[The collaboration, originally announced in September 2021](https://www.proqr.com/press-releases/proqr-announces-axiomerr-rna-editing-licensing-and-research-collaboration-with-lilly) , applied ProQR’s proprietary Axiomer™ RNA editing platform to target disorders of the liver and nervous system. Through the course of work to date, advances in the platform have significantly increased editing efficiency and refined biodistribution in both the liver and nervous system, opening up new potential applications to not only correct known mutations, but also introduce protective variants in specific transcripts.
