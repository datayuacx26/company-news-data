### Why Recruiters Need More Than a People Search Tool


Finding a candidate's name and LinkedIn profile does not get them on your calendar. The real problem in proactive hiring is low response rate, and search depth alone rarely moves the needle. Most candidates worth hiring are[passive](https://www.linkedin.com/business/talent/blog/talent-acquisition/recruiting-active-vs-passive-candidates) . They did not apply. They are not refreshing their inbox waiting for your message. Getting a response from them requires persistence, the right channel, and a message that feels personal.


Juicebox addresses the search side of this equation well. It does not fully address what comes next: multi-channel follow-ups, phone calls, WhatsApp nudges, and the iterative outreach cadence that turns a cold profile into an interested candidate.[Weekday](https://weekday.works/) was built specifically to close that gap.


#### Common Gaps Recruiters Hit with Search-Only Tools


- **Single-channel outreach:** Most AI sourcing tools send an email and stop. Candidates who do not respond to email often respond to WhatsApp or a phone call.
- **No outreach automation:** Recruiters manually copy-paste contact details, draft individual messages, and track replies in a spreadsheet. That process does not scale.
- **No done-for-you option:** Small teams with one or two recruiters cannot run a full outbound motion on top of their day-to-day work. A contingency or managed model solves this.
- **Global databases with thin India coverage:** Most Western sourcing tools have weak data depth for Indian white-collar professionals,[the fastest-growing technical talent pool in the world](https://nasscom.in/knowledge-center/publications/indias-tech-industry-talent-demand-supply-analysis) .


These gaps are precisely why Weekday exists. The platform was built by founders who lived this problem while hiring engineers for their own startup, and it is architected to handle everything from candidate discovery through interested candidates landing on your calendar.


### What to Look for in a Juicebox Alternative


The right alternative depends on where Juicebox is failing your team. Here is what separates good sourcing-and-outreach platforms from pure search tools.

- **Database quality and depth:** Size matters less than accuracy. A 300M-profile database of verified contacts beats a 1B-profile database full of stale emails.

- **Engineering and technical focus:** If you are hiring engineers, your sourcing tool needs deep signal from GitHub, Stack Overflow, and technical communities, not just LinkedIn.
- **Managed or contingency option:** Teams with limited recruiting bandwidth need a partner who runs the hiring motion end to end, not just a tool to add to the stack.
- **Transparent pricing:** Hidden pricing and mandatory sales calls add friction. The best tools let you understand cost before committing.
