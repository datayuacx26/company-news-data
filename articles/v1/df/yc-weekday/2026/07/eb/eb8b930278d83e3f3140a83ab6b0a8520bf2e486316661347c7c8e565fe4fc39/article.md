## Research Methodology: How We Evaluated HR AI Tools


We evaluated 25+ HR AI platforms used by Indian organizations across five weighted criteria:


AI Capabilities (30%): Depth of AI-driven sourcing, screening, analytics, and automation. Does the platform use machine learning, NLP, and predictive models to reduce manual work?


India Compliance & Localization (25%): Native support for Indian statutory requirements (PF, ESI, PT, TDS), multi-state handling, and India-specific workflows including WhatsApp integration.


Workflow Coverage (20%): Does the platform cover recruiting, HRMS, payroll, performance, or analytics? Is it best-of-breed specialist or all-in-one suite?


Pricing & ROI (15%): Cost structure, transparency, and value relative to capabilities. Does pricing match the target market (startup, SMB, mid-market, enterprise)?


User Experience & Support (10%): Interface quality, mobile accessibility, implementation speed, and customer support responsiveness based on verified reviews.


Platforms were scored on each dimension, cross-referenced with G2, Capterra, and TechJockey verified reviews, and compared against real deployment data from Indian organizations.

Unlike traditional ATS systems, which primarily focus on tracking applicants through the hiring funnel, talent intelligence platforms provide a holistic approach to talent acquisition and management. Indian HR teams typically need both: a recruiting specialist to fill roles proactively and an HRMS to manage employees compliantly.
