# Day Zero Gemma 4 Support on Overshoot

[← Back to all posts](https://www.overshoot.ai/blogs) All Gemma 4 models available on Overshoot Playground, with 2 hours of free inference on sign up.


Day zero Gemma 4 launch. Google has rejoined the open model map with a forceful entry in the Gemma 4 family, dropped today on April 2, 2026.


All Gemma 4 models are already live on the[Overshoot Playground](https://playground.overshoot.ai/) , so if you want to skip the launch posts and just try them, start there.


The short version, focused on the part we care about: Gemma 4 is a new open model family from Google released under Apache 2.0, and all four models natively process images and video. Google also says the E2B and E4B variants add native audio input, the edge models carry a 128K context window, and the larger models go up to 256K. In their launch writeup they specifically call out visual tasks like OCR and chart understanding, which is the right place for an open multimodal family to start. Read the full launch post[here](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemma-4/) .


## The Models


- Gemma 4 E2B
- Gemma 4 E4B
- Gemma 4 26B-A4B
- Gemma 4 31B


The two small models are the obvious edge story. The 26B-A4B is the MoE latency play. The 31B is the dense quality play.


Google's launch graphic showing Gemma 4's open-model chat arena positioning.
