# Cheap, Low-Effort, Low-Calorie Meal Prep Ideas

Meal prepping doesn’t have to be expensive or time-consuming. With the right approach, you can create nutritious, low-calorie meals on a budget and with minimal effort. This guide provides practical tips and meal ideas to help you stay on track with your health goals without breaking the bank or spending hours in the kitchen. Plus, discover how Rex.Fit’s tools can simplify your meal planning process.


#### 1. **Why Opt for Cheap, Low-Effort Meal Prep?**


Meal prepping is an excellent way to ensure you eat healthy meals throughout the week. However, the process can seem overwhelming if you’re on a tight budget or have a busy schedule. Fortunately, there are many ways to prepare cheap, low-effort, and low-calorie meals that fit into any lifestyle. For more guidance on efficient meal planning, check out[Rex.Fit’s Meal Plan Tool](https://mealplan.rex.fit/) , which can help you create balanced, calorie-conscious meal plans effortlessly.


#### 2. **Easy and Affordable Meal Prep Ideas**


1. **Vegetable Stir-Fry** : Quick and customizable, vegetable stir-fry can be made with a variety of low-calorie vegetables like bell peppers, broccoli, and carrots. Use a simple sauce made from soy sauce, garlic, and ginger for flavor without added calories.
2. **Chickpea Salad** : Chickpeas are a budget-friendly source of protein and fibre. Mix them with chopped cucumbers, tomatoes, and a light vinaigrette for a refreshing, low-calorie meal. For more recipe inspiration, see[Delish’s Quick Low-Calorie Meals](https://www.delish.com/cooking/menus/g1281/quick-low-calorie-cheap-meals/) .
3. **Egg Muffins** : These are great for breakfast or a snack. Simply whisk eggs with spinach, onions, and bell peppers, pour into muffin tins, and bake. They’re low-calorie, easy to make, and store well.
4. **Soup** : A hearty vegetable soup made with ingredients like tomatoes, zucchini, and spinach is filling and low in calories. Plus, it’s budget-friendly and easy to prepare in large batches.[BBC Good Food’s Cheap and Healthy Recipes](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/collection/cheap-and-healthy-recipes) offers several delicious soup ideas.
5. **Quinoa and Black Beans** : This combination is not only affordable but also packed with protein and fibre. Mix cooked quinoa with black beans, corn, and avocado for a satisfying and low-calorie meal.
6. **Oven-Baked Chicken Breasts** : Chicken breasts are lean and versatile. Season and bake them in bulk, then use throughout the week in salads, wraps, or as a main dish.

#### 4. **Tips for Successful Low-Calorie Meal Prep**


1. **Buy in Bulk** : Purchasing staple ingredients like grains, beans, and frozen vegetables in bulk can save money and reduce the frequency of grocery trips.
2. **Prep in Advance** : Allocate a few hours each week to prepare and portion out your meals. This reduces daily cooking time and helps you stick to your meal plan.
3. **Keep It Simple** : Opt for recipes with fewer ingredients and simpler preparation methods to minimize effort and cost.
4. **Use Leftovers Wisely** : Incorporate leftovers into new meals to avoid waste and maximize your budget.
