# Best AI Calorie Counter Apps

In today's digital age, maintaining a balanced diet has never been easier thanks to artificial intelligence. AI-powered calorie counter apps leverage advanced technology to provide more accurate and efficient ways to track your food intake. This article highlights some of the best AI calorie counter apps available, while also showing how[Rex.Fit](https://www.rex.fit/) integrates with these technologies to enhance your overall health and wellness strategy.


#### **Top AI Calorie Counter Apps**


1. [Calorie Mama](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calorie-mama-ai-diet-counter/id1121789860) Calorie Mama is an advanced AI-driven app that simplifies calorie counting by using image recognition technology. Users can snap photos of their meals, and the app will estimate the calorie content, making tracking easy and accurate.
2. [Snapcalorie](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/26/snapcalorie-computer-vision-health-app-raises-3m/) Snapcalorie offers a cutting-edge AI solution that uses computer vision to analyze food images and provide detailed nutritional information. This app is a great option for those looking to integrate AI into their dietary management.
3. [Nutricapture](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gpt.calorie.counter&hl=en) Nutricapture combines AI with food photography to track calorie intake. Its free version includes essential features for accurate calorie counting and meal tracking.
4. [Foodvisor](https://www.forbes.com/health/wellness/best-calorie-counting-apps/) Foodvisor uses AI to recognize and log food items from photos, offering users an easy way to track their nutritional intake. It provides detailed feedback and suggestions to help users stay on track with their health goals.
