# How to Count Calories in Meal Prep While Saving Money

Meal prepping is a fantastic way to manage your diet, but when combined with counting calories, it can seem like a daunting task—especially if you’re also trying to save money. Fortunately, with the right strategies and tools, you can efficiently count calories, stick to your budget, and still enjoy nutritious meals. Here’s a detailed guide to help you master the art of calorie counting in meal prep while keeping costs low.


#### 1. **Understanding Calorie Counting in Meal Prep**


Counting calories in meal prep involves tracking the number of calories in each meal or portion to ensure you meet your dietary goals. This practice not only helps with weight management but also ensures balanced nutrition. For detailed information on how to get started with calorie counting, check out[Rachel Trotta's Guide](https://racheltrotta.com/fitness/meal-prep-into-calories/) , which offers practical advice on integrating calorie counting into your meal prep routine.


Rex.Fit’s[Meal Plan Tool](https://mealplan.rex.fit/) is an excellent resource for creating calorie-conscious meal plans that fit your nutritional needs and budget. By using this tool, you can easily plan your meals while keeping track of your calorie intake and making adjustments as needed.


#### 2. **Tips for Counting Calories While Saving Money**


1. **Plan and Budget** : Start by planning your meals for the week and creating a budget. Look for sales and discounts on healthy ingredients, and use them to your advantage.[Sweat Ethic’s Tips](https://sweatethic.com/blogs/news/meal-prepping-can-save-you-money?srsltid=AfmBOooN3lLMrK8A__7bS90yJ4Q9qFsPJthDiJv3yGy2VWoLyqKThtOP) highlight how meal prepping can be a cost-effective way to maintain a healthy diet.
2. **Use a Calorie Counting App** : Utilize apps like[Rex.Fit’s App](https://app.rex.fit/) to track your calorie intake. These apps often have features that allow you to log your meals, calculate calories, and monitor your overall nutrition effortlessly.
3. **Portion Control** : Invest in a kitchen scale to accurately measure your ingredients and portion sizes. Accurate measurements help in precise calorie counting and prevent overeating. For more on portion control, refer to[Maxwell Forbes’ Post](https://maxwellforbes.com/posts/calorie-counting-meal-planning/) , which discusses how portion control can simplify meal prep and calorie counting.
4. **Incorporate Nutrient-Dense Foods** : Focus on nutrient-dense, budget-friendly foods such as beans, lentils, and seasonal vegetables. These foods are often lower in calories and cost-effective.
5. **Batch Cooking** : Prepare large batches of meals and freeze them in individual portions. This approach not only saves money but also makes calorie counting easier as you can portion out meals in advance.
6. **Calculate Calories Using Tools** : Use tools like the[Rex.Fit TDEE Calculator](https://www.rex.fit/total-daily-energy-expenditure-tdee-calculator) to determine your daily caloric needs based on your activity level. Adjust your meal prep accordingly to ensure you stay within your calorie goals.


#### 3. **Staying on Track with Your Goals**


1. **Track Your Progress** : Regularly review your calorie intake and meal prep effectiveness. Adjust your meal plans as needed based on your progress and feedback from your tracking tools.
2. **Seek Support** : Join online communities or forums for additional support and tips on meal prepping and calorie counting. For example,[Reddit’s Meal Prep Community](https://www.reddit.com/r/MealPrepSunday/comments/ajjjk3/whats_the_easiest_way_to_count_calories_and/) offers discussions and advice on calorie counting and meal prep strategies.
3. **Maintain Flexibility** : Be open to adjusting your meal prep routine based on your evolving needs and goals. Flexibility can help you stay committed to both calorie counting and budget management.


### Conclusion


Counting calories while meal prepping can seem challenging, but with effective planning and the right tools, it’s entirely feasible. By leveraging Rex.Fit’s resources and incorporating these tips, you can achieve your dietary goals while keeping costs under control.
