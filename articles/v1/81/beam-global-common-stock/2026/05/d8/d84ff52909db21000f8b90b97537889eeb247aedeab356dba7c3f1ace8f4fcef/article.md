**What Is GFX?**


Government Fleet Expo & Conference (GFX), is the largest annual conference for public fleets in the nation. Built from the ground up by the team behind *Government Fleet* magazine, the most trusted industry resource for news, training and information – GFX is known for its robust conference program, large and immersive exhibition and most of all, serving as a true annual hub for public fleet professionals.


GFX has built a legacy of greatness and connections. Since 2008, our close-knit community has returned each year for our trusted and relied upon public fleet-specific training, networking opportunities with like-minded peers, and access to top products and technology displays.
