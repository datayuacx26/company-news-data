# Upcoming – Beam Global Webinar – EVs Are Here. How Will You Fund the Infrastructure?Date Tuesday. August 25, 2026 at 11 AM PST / 2 PM EST

As electric vehicle adoption continues to accelerate across public agencies, commercial fleets, airports, parks, educational institutions, utilities, and private organizations, securing funding has become one of the biggest challenges to deploying EV charging infrastructure.


Join Beam Global and federal funding expert Amanda Wood of Becker & Poliakoff for an interactive discussion on today’s EV infrastructure funding landscape. We’ll review current federal funding opportunities, recent program updates, and practical strategies to help organizations identify funding sources that can accelerate EV charging projects while minimizing capital investment and avoiding the cost, construction, and delays associated with traditional grid-connected infrastructure.


Whether you’re planning your first EV charging deployment or expanding an existing fleet, this webinar will provide actionable insights into funding opportunities available today and what’s expected next.


**What You’ll Learn**


- Current trends driving EV adoption across public, private, and fleet markets.
- The latest federal funding opportunities supporting EV infrastructure, with an emphasis on resiliency and emergency preparedness programs.
- Lessons learned from recently closed funding programs and how to prepare for upcoming opportunities.
- Real-world Beam Global deployment examples across airports, parks, municipalities, commercial fleets, and other emerging markets.
- How Beam’s rapidly deployable, infrastructure-independent charging solutions can help simplify EV deployments.
- How Beam Global’s partnership with Becker & Poliakoff provides organizations with a complimentary funding review to identify grant and funding opportunities for qualifying projects.
- Live discussion and audience Q&A with funding expert Amanda Wood.


**Who Should Attend**


- Government agencies
- Fleet managers
- Sustainability and energy managers
- Facilities and operations leaders
- Transportation planners
- Universities and school districts
- Airports, ports, parks, and public works organizations
- Commercial organizations planning EV infrastructure


**Complimentary Funding Review**


Every attendee will have the opportunity to request a complimentary funding review with Amanda Wood and Becker & Poliakoff to explore federal funding opportunities that may support their EV infrastructure projects.
