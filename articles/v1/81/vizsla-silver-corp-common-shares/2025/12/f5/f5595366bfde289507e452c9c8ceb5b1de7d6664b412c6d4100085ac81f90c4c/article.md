# Vizsla Silver Files Feasibility Study Technical Report on the Panuco Project - Vizsla Silver Corp.

## Vizsla Silver Files Feasibility Study Technical Report on the Panuco Project


December 9, 2025


[View PDF](https://wp-vizslasilvercorp-2024.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/media/2025/12/VZLA-NR-FS-Dec-9-2025.pdf)


**Vancouver, British Columbia (December 9, 2025) – Vizsla Silver Corp. (TSX: VZLA) (NYSE: VZLA) (Frankfurt: 0G3** ) (“ **Vizsla** ” or the “ **Company** ”) is pleased to announce the filing of a technical report titled, “Panuco Project – NI 43-101 Technical Report and Feasibility Study” (the “ **FS** ”), with an effective date of November 4, 2025. The FS was completed by Ausenco Engineering Canada ULC, supported by Mining Plus Canada Consulting Ltd. and SGS Canada Inc., in accordance with National Instrument 43-101 – Standards of Disclosure for Mineral Projects. The results of the FS were previously announced in the Company’s news release dated November 12, 225.


The FS is available under Vizsla Silver’s SEDAR+ profile at[www.sedarplus.ca](http://www.sedarplus.ca/) ,[EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/#/ciks=0001796073&entityName=Vizsla%252520Silver%252520Corp.%252520(VZLA)%252520(CIK%2525200001796073)) or on the Company’s website at[www.vizslasilvercorp.com](https://vizslasilvercorp.com/) .


**About Vizsla Silver and the Panuco Project**


Vizsla Silver is a Canadian mineral exploration and development company headquartered in Vancouver, BC, focused on advancing its flagship, 100%-owned Panuco silver-gold project located in Sinaloa, Mexico. The Company recently completed a Feasibility Study for Panuco in November 2025 which highlights 17.4 Moz AgEq of annual production over an initial 9.4-year mine life, an after-tax NPV(5%) of US$1.8B, 111% IRR and a 7-month payback at US$35.50/oz Ag and US$3,100/oz Au. Vizsla Silver aims to position itself as a leading silver company by implementing a dual track development approach at Panuco, advancing mine development while continuing district scale exploration through low-cost means.
