# A View From Above: Touring the Eskay Creek Project

In October, Skeena had the honour of hosting some very special guests at the Eskay Creek site. Tahltan leadership from the Tahltan Band, Iskut Band, and Tahltan Central Government, along with board members and employees from the Tahltan Nation Development Corporation (TNDC), toured the site by helicopter and vehicle, guided by Tahltan Skeena team members and Skeena leadership. Together, we shared an in-depth look at the Eskay Creek Project, highlighting our commitment to environmental stewardship and our partnership with the Tahltan Nation. Mēduh to all who attended for sharing their valuable insights!
