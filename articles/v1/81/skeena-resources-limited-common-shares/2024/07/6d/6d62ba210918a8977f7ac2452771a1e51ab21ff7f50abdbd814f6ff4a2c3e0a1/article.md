# Hosting Tahltan Elders for a Tour of Eskay Creek

Elders are keepers of traditions, beliefs, and values, and play a vital part in maintaining the continuity between the Tahltan Nation’s cultural heritage and their future.


The tour provided Elders with an opportunity to view the site by helicopter and on foot, and learn more about Eskay Creek during this pivotal point in the project. Our team provided the Elders with an in-depth briefing while highlighting our commitment to environmental stewardship and collaboration with the Tahltan Nation. The Elders also met with Tahltan members who work at the site and provided valuable feedback on our operations.


As we move forward with the project, we will continue to prioritize engagement with the Tahltan Nation and the surrounding communities. By working together, we can lead a project that respects the Land, honours cultural heritage and contributes to the prosperity of all involved.


We extend our deepest gratitude to the Tahltan Elders for their participation and insights during the site visit and look forward to our continued collaboration.
