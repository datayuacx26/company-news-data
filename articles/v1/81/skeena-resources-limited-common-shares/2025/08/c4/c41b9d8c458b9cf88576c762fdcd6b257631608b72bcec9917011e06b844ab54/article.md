# Supporting our Communities – Fall 2025

Skeena is actively investing in the communities where we operate. We continue to seek opportunities to support community led initiatives and organizations. Please reach out to our Community Development Lead, Jodi Payne (jpayne@skeenagold.com), or our Community Relations Specialist, Samantha Raven (sraven@skeenagold.com) if you have a project that could use our support.


Learn more:[https://skeenagoldsilver.com/esg/donation/](https://skeenagoldsilver.com/esg/donation/)


**Stewart Community Days**


Members of the Skeena team were honoured to engage with amazing people at a local community celebration hosted by Stewart Community Connections Society (and keeping attendees connected with our free Wifi). The event featured a pancake breakfast sponsored by Skeena.


**Terrace Riverboat Days**


We had a blast celebrating Terrace Riverboat Days with community members. Thank you to everyone who stopped by our tent at the Concert in the Park.


**Hazelton Pioneer Days**


A little rain couldn’t stop the smiles at Village of Hazelton Pioneer Days. Thanks to everyone who visited our tent for pancakes, and kudos to It’s All Fun and Paints for painting some happy faces.


**Smithers Main Street Market**


We had an amazing time at Smithers Main St. Market while supporting terrific local businesses Out of Hand, Love of Rocks, UFO on Main, and The Sausage Factory with our door prize. Our appreciation to Town of Smithers for bringing so much vibrancy into downtown Smithers.
