# King Charles III Coronation Medal

A heartfelt congratulations to Skeena’s VP, Environment & Regulatory Affairs, Nalaine Morin, on being awarded the King Charles III Coronation Medal. The Coronation Medal is a Canadian honour created to commemorate the Coronation of His Majesty King Charles III, recognizing those who have made significant contributions to Canada and their home province. Nalaine’s active role in advocating for Indigenous involvement in B.C.’s resource sector has brought positive change in the drive for more inclusive and sustainable development.


Skeena would like to recognize Iskut Band Chief Marie Quock who was also honoured with a Coronation Medal for her many years of service to the Iskut Band and the Tahltan Nation.
