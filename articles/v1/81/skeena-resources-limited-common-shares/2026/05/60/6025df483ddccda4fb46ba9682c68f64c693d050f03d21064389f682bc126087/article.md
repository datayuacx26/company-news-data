# Skeena sponsors 3 Indigenous Student Awards and 2026 CMTN Foundation Golf Scramble

Skeena Gold + Silver is proud to be the Platinum Sponsor of the 2026 CMTN Foundation Annual Golf Scramble—Coast Mountain College’s largest annual fundraising event supporting student education. As part of this collaboration, we’re excited to offer three student awards to support Indigenous learners pursuing post-secondary education, with a focus on skilled trades:


- [Indigenous Student Education Award](https://coastmountaincollege.ca/awards-bursaries-scholarships/award-or-bursary-details/skeena-gold---silver-indigenous-student-education-award)
- [Indigenous Industrial Trades Education Award](https://coastmountaincollege.ca/awards-bursaries-scholarships/award-or-bursary-details/skeena-gold---silver-indigenous-industrial-trades-education-award)
- [Female Indigenous Industrial Trades Education Award](https://coastmountaincollege.ca/awards-bursaries-scholarships/award-or-bursary-details/skeena-gold---silver-female-indigenous-industrial-trades-education-award)


These awards will help create real pathways into meaningful, in-demand careers critical to regional growth.


For more information about these awards and how to apply, visit the[Coast Mountain College website.](https://coastmountaincollege.ca/)
