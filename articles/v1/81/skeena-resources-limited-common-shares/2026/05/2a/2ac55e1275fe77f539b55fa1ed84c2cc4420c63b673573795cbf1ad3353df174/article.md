# Project Update: Reaching the Midway Mark

Heading into spring, we’re pleased to report that the Eskay Creek project is about halfway complete.


Among the major highlights, we have made strong progress on the process plant, water treatment and management systems, camp construction and electrical infrastructure.


Our strategy of early preparation and initiating development work before receiving final permits allowed us to get a jump on initial activities. By acting early, we reduced project timeline risk and minimized inflationary impacts across key scopes by securing procurement contracts earlier. Initial production remains on schedule, targeted for the second quarter of 2027 and commercial production expected in the third quarter.
