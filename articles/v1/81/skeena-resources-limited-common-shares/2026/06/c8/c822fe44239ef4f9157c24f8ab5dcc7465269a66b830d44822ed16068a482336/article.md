# Fast Facts

What will Eskay Creek look like in Operations?


**High Grade**
Eskay Creek will be one of the highest-grade open-pit gold mines in the world, with an average grade of 5.5 gpt gold equivalent in its first five years of production – more than triple the average grade for open-pits worldwide.


**Canada’s Largest Silver Mine**
Eskay Creek will produce an average of 9.5 Moz of silver per year during years 1 – 5, making it the largest silver mine in Canada.


**Low-Cost, Clean Hydro Power**
Eskay Creek will be powered by clean and low-cost energy from a nearby hydroelectric facility, with rates positioned well below global mining industry energy cost standards.


**Direct Jobs:**
**1500 During Construction, 950 During Operations**
Eskay Creek will be a cornerstone of economic growth for the Province of British Columbia, supporting workers, businesses and long-term regional growth.
