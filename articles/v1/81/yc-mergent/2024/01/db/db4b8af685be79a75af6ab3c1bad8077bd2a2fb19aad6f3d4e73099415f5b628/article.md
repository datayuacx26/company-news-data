# Announcing Mergent Teams

You can now manage your team and team members directly in Mergent’s dashboard.


All personal accounts and organizations have been consolidated into the new Mergent teams. Project billing and team member management have also been migrated to teams.


Team members are free to add to any team, even teams on our free plan. So go forth and collaborate!✨
