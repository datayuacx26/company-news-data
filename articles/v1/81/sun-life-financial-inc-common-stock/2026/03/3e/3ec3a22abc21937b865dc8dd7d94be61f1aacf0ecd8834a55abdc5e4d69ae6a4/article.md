# Sun Life to host 2026 Annual Meetings on May 6, 2026

TORONTO


, March 24, 2026


/CNW/ - Sun Life Financial Inc. (TSX:SLF ) (NYSE: SLF) (the "Company") today announced that its Annual and Special Meeting of common shareholders and the Annual Meeting of voting policyholders of Sun Life Assurance Company of Canada will be held on Wednesday, May 6, 2026. Shareholders and voting policyholders are invited to participate online or in person.


The Company also announced that its[2025 Annual Report](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4646704-1&h=3508413092&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sunlife.com%2Fen%2Finvestors%2Ffinancial-results-and-reports%2Fannual-reports%2F%3FWT.mc_id%3Den-ca%3Apr%3Amediarelease%3Aagm_nr%3Aagm26%3Aagm_nr_ar_en&a=2025+Annual+Report) and its[Notice of Annual and Special Meeting and Management Information Circular](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4646704-1&h=2658062553&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sunlife.com%2Fcontent%2Fdam%2Fsunlife%2Fregional%2Fglobal-marketing%2Fdocuments%2Fcom%2F2026-asm-notice-and-circular-e.pdf&a=Notice+of+Annual+and+Special+Meeting+and+Management+Information+Circular) for the 2026 Annual and Special Meeting are now available.


**Annual and Special Meeting of Shareholders and Annual Meeting of Voting Policyholders** Shareholders and voting policyholders can attend the meeting online or in person at Sun Life's global headquarters in Toronto. They will have the opportunity to ask questions and vote on a number of important matters regardless of whether they attend online or in person.


Shareholders and voting policyholders are advised to periodically check the[Annual Meetings page](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4646704-1&h=2161565133&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sunlife.com%2Fen%2Finvestors%2Fpresentations-and-events%2Fannual-meetings%2F%3FWT.mc_id%3Den-ca%3Apr%3Amediarelease%3Aagm_nr%3Aagm26%3Aagm_nr_am_en&a=Annual+Meetings+page) on[sunlife.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4646704-1&h=2752860169&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sunlife.com%2F&a=sunlife.com) in advance of the meeting for updated information.
