# Sun Life President and Chief Executive Officer Kevin Strain to speak at National Bank's Financial Services Conference

TORONTO


, March 23, 2026


/CNW/ - Kevin Strain, Sun Life's President and Chief Executive Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at National Bank Capital Markets' 24th Annual Financial Services Conference in Montreal on March 24, 2026. The fireside chat will be moderated by Gabriel Dechaine, Managing Director and Senior Equity Analyst at National Bank.


**Date:**


March 24, 2026


**Time:**


2:00 p.m. ET


To access the live webcast or a replay, please visit our Sun Life[Investor Relations](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4646489-1&h=1179385491&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sunlife.com%2Fen%2Finvestors%2Fpresentations-and-events%2Fupcoming-events%2F&a=Investor+Relations) page.


**About Sun Life** Sun Life is a leading international financial services organization providing asset management, wealth, insurance and health solutions to individual and institutional Clients. Sun Life has operations in a number of markets worldwide, including Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Hong Kong, the Philippines, Japan, Indonesia, India, China, Australia, Singapore, Vietnam, Malaysia and Bermuda. As of December 31, 2025, Sun Life had total assets under management of $1.60 trillion.
