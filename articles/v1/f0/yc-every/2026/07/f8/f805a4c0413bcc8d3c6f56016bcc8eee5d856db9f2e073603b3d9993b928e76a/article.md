# Every is Sponsoring Founder Institute's FounderX To Help Founders Win in the Agentic Era

Time is founders’ most-valued currency. Tools and frameworks that help founders save time and reduce tedious tasks are indispensable as they open more headspace for strategic decision making, networking, and skill-building.


As an all-in-one back office solution for high-growth companies, increasing founder velocity is one of[Every’s](https://www.every.io/) core missions. That’s why we’re thrilled to be sponsoring[Founder Institute’s](https://fi.co/)[FounderX](https://founderx.com/) 2026, one of the best events of the year for founders aspiring to hit the next phase of growth.


## What is the Founder Institute?


[Founder Institute](https://fi.co/) is an AI-native company builder that helps early-stage entrepreneurs turn their start-up idea into a real, fundable company. With structured programs delivered across 200+ cities in over 100 countries, Founder Institute provides a step-by-step company-building curriculum, mentorship, AI tools, and access to investors so that founders can start scaling without months—or years—of trial and error. As the AI revolution continues to change the way in which companies are built, Founder Institute’s AI-native curriculum enables founders to adapt their company strategy to the AI-first world as it evolves.


## What is FounderX?


[FounderX](https://founderx.com/) is Founder Institute’s pinnacle gathering—a three-day working session that brings founders, active investors, and ecosystem leaders together from August 25 to 27 in Silicon Valley for hands-on workshops, closed-door roundtables, and private dinners designed to produce real outcomes.


The attendee mix at FounderX 2026 includes more than 200 startup founders, more than 100 angel investors and venture capitalists, and representatives from more than 40 countries, with a focus on invites and attendee curation rather than ticket sales.


Alongside unique networking opportunities and keynote presentations, the event also includes Featured Workshops on the core capabilities founders need to compete in the agentic era.


## How Does Every Support Founder Institute Members?


Building an early-stage startup can feel like navigating a maze. Unclear on what to do and where to focus, it’s easy to work tirelessly for weeks only to find you’re not any closer to hitting your goals. Founder Institute provides founders with the tools, curriculum, and mentorship they need to turn their ideas into viable businesses as quickly as possible. With a clear structure for how to grow, Founder Institute enables founders to avoid losing time to the ambiguity of the startup journey.


And when your blue sky idea begins to turn into a real business, Every makes it easy to incorporate and automate back-office admin workflows so that you can continue to spend time building—not balancing the books. Every’s unified operating system brings together everything a company needs to launch, operate, and scale—free C-corp incorporation, business banking and cards, investment and cash management, bookkeeping, payroll and HR, benefits, and tax preparation. Founders can incorporate and open a business bank account in under a week, and continue to automate financial and compliance work as they scale.


By protecting headspace and increasing clarity, both Every and Founder Institute are helping founders maximize their velocity so that they can build stronger companies, faster. In support of that common goal, Every is now[offering exclusive deals](https://www.every.io/partners/founders-institute?utm_source=founders-institute&utm_medium=partner&utm_campaign=partner-page) to both VC funded and non-funded Founder Institute members.


## Meeting Every at FounderX


In addition to Every’s event presence as a sponsor, Every CEO[Rajeev Behera](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rajeevbehera/) will also be leading a featured workshop on founder branding. Rajeev is a multi-time founder who has raised over $100M in funding from VCs like Andreessen Horowitz, Lightspeed, and Redpoint. Before Every, Rejeev founded Reflektive, which he led to $20M ARR and which was ranked the 13th fastest growing company in North America by Deloitte in terms of revenue growth.


Having amassed notable followings across LinkedIn and X, developing a strong founder brand has been instrumental to Rajeev’s success as a founder. At FounderX, he’ll be breaking down how founders can find their own narrative and use AI to post in their own voice at scale—creating a founder content engine that elevates personal and company brand.
