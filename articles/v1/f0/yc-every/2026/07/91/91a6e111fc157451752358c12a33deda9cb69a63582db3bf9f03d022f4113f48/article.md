# How Every helped MLPal.ai avoid a $100K compliance gap

Building enterprise-grade AI is hard. For a first-time founder, building the company around it can be even harder — especially when the back office comes with a maze of federal and state regulations and no clear checklist to follow.


That was the reality facing Sai Vivek Peddi, founder of[MLPal.ai](https://mlpal.ai/) , a company building custom enterprise agentic AI systems. “Because I'm a foreigner, I first had to figure out how to transition my visa situation,” Vivek explained. “Foremost in my mind was the related question — how do I set up my startup's back office and ensure my books are in good order? The last thing I needed was a surprise bill from the US government for taxes or penalties that I had overlooked.”


To avoid those unseen pitfalls, Vivek partnered with Every — the AI-native, all-in-one back office — on day one.


## Why Vivek Chose Every for MLPal.ai


Every is an all-in-one back office built to equip founders with everything they need to run their company — incorporation, banking and cards, treasury, payroll, bookkeeping, and taxes — all in one platform. For Vivek, two things stood out.


### 1. Compliance handled from day one


When it comes to complying with a myriad of federal and state government regulations, there's no easy checklist for founders.


> "Compliance is an important thing I cared about, and now it's sorted with Every." SAI VIVEK PEDDI — FOUNDER,[MLPAL.AI](http://mlpal.ai/)


“For example, I didn't know that a startup my size was supposed to offer Workers' Compensation Insurance by law,” said Vivek. “You can be fined anywhere from $20,000–$100,000 by the government if you're not compliant. For a startup budget, that's huge. But during my onboarding, Every provided a useful checklist of everything I needed to do to be compliant. It was a savior.”


In addition to helping MLPal.ai find the right Workers' Compensation coverage, Every's in-house HR expert also walked Vivek through the different healthcare benefit options to find the right fit for his team size. And Every's payroll automatically deducts the necessary taxes and pays both personal income and corporate taxes — one less thing to worry about.


### 2. A partner invested in long-term founder success


When Vivek evaluated different back-office solutions, Every stood out on his very first call. “What I liked best is that Every didn't charge me a single penny up front. Every invests in the long-term. That was a differentiator for me — they believed in my future success,” he said.


MLPal.ai started with Every's complimentary incorporation assistance and bank account. As the startup scaled, Every scaled with it, adding HR Payroll, Bookkeeping, and Taxes. Most of all, Vivek likes having everything in one place — banking, payroll, taxes, and bookkeeping. “Plus everyone at Every is very proactive. If I send an email, an Every expert responds within a day. I don't want an automated voice assistant when I'm in trouble,” he added.


## What's next for MLPal.ai?


With the back office off his plate, Vivek can focus on what inspires him. “Every took a lot of effort off my plate, and I got to focus on what inspires me. I'm a builder at heart, and nothing inspires me more than seeing how my customers are wowed by how my product works out of the box,” he said.


MLPal.ai is ahead of the curve with its horizontal approach to enterprise agentic AI. Instead of enterprises struggling to slot a multitude of vertical AI agents into legacy systems, MLPal.ai builds custom agentic systems in hours, not months — no integration headaches and no data silos. Customers are taking notice, with three enterprises already running pilots.
