# Teddy Yip and Joanna Chen are Building Narrative AI to Automate Manufacturing for Creator-Founders

Modern creators are changing the way that brands are being built. Having developed loyal communities on platforms like YouTube and Instagram, content creators from[MrBeast](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCX6OQ3DkcsbYNE6H8uQQuVA) to[Emma Chamberlain](https://www.youtube.com/c/emmachamberlain) are launching their own product lines and controlling the narrative for their brands.


But while these creators have an intuitive understanding of their audience, manufacturing is unfamiliar territory.


**That's exactly the gap Joanna and Teddy are closing, and they're doing it faster than anyone in the industry expected.**


Content creator[Joanna Chen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joanna-chen/) built a community of over 600,000 people on social media, becoming one of the most recognized creators. When she set out to launch her own clothing label, JACNANA, she had the audience, the taste, and the vision. What she didn't have was a clear path through the labyrinth of global manufacturing. Finding trusted factories, communicating technical requirements, and producing the detailed garment blueprints, known as tech packs, was opaque, slow, and expensive. So she decided to do what great founders do—she built the solution herself.


Joanna teamed up with[Teddy Yip](https://www.linkedin.com/in/teddy-yip-543ba241/) , a seasoned operator with a track record of launching and scaling startups, and together they founded[Narrative AI](https://www.joinnarrative.ai/) , a platform purpose-built for the new wave of digital creator-founders entering physical product businesses.


## **Introducing Narrative AI: Automating Manufacturing Workflows for Emerging Brands**


Narrative AI is a fashion tech platform that gives creator founders and independent labels everything they need to move from concept to finished product—at a speed the industry has never seen before.


While audience building and distribution are evolving drastically, the traditional path to manufacturing is broken. Brands spend months attending trade shows and cold-emailing factories just to find a production partner. Once they do, collaboration devolves into endless email chains, paper-based workflows, and manually produced tech packs—the detailed technical documents manufacturers require before touching a single pattern. It's a process that leads many creator-founders, who usually don't have a supply chain and technical drawing background, to give up.


"We help anybody that wants to launch their own brand, whether they're a content creator or a startup brand on its own, get started really quickly on the operational side of manufacturing," says Teddy. Narrative AI is providing emerging brands with a more streamlined solution, enabling creator founders to find the right manufacturers for their needs, translate creative designs into tech packs, and simplify communication workflows.


Through this “manufacturing matchmaking,” as Teddy describes it, Narrative is helping customers reduce time-to-market by 50% or more—from the industry standard of nine months to as few as three to four.


## **From Frustration to a Platform Backed by Top Investors**


The idea for Narrative AI wasn't born in a boardroom. It came from Joanna's lived experience of hitting every wall the fashion industry puts in front of a new founder, and deciding that those walls shouldn't exist.


What began as a personal problem has become a company. In just over a year, Narrative AI has grown to a team of six, secured backing from respected firms including[Antler](https://www.antler.co/) , launched its public beta, and was named to the[SuperAI Genesis Top 50 for 2026](https://www.superai.com/genesis-startup-competition) , a recognition of the most promising AI companies globally.


The momentum is real, and the market timing couldn't be sharper. As a generation of creators with massive built-in audiences turns toward launching physical brands, Narrative AI is positioned to be the operating system underneath all of it.


## Teddy’s Advice for Fellow Founders


Teddy’s experience launching and scaling multiple startups has taught him that the way you enter an industry can be pivotal to your success.


"Go-to-market is super important, and understanding how you want to enter an industry is key,” he explains. “You need to carve out a niche, understand your beachhead, and create great value for that segment to start. From there you can swim upstream or expand in different ways.”


He adds that this is especially important for startups with limited resources. “You have to understand how to deploy even your attention appropriately,” says Teddy. “Carve out a niche segment as part of your ICP and your go-to-market, and just focus."


Joanna and Teddy are doing exactly that, and they're just getting started.
