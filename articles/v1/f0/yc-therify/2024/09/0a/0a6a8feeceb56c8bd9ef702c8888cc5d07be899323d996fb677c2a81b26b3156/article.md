# Mental Health in Sales: A Conversation with Brooke Adair of Ironclad

In this podcast episode of *In Pursuit Of* , Therify CEO James Edward Murray engages in conversation with Brooke Adair, a seasoned sales leader and Head of Mid-Market Sales at Ironclad, about the critical intersection of mental health and performance within sales teams.


Brooke shares personal experiences of navigating grief while excelling in a high-pressure sales environment, highlighting how investing in mental health can drastically improve one's ability to lead and perform.


The discussion also explores practical strategies for sales leaders to foster a supportive and psychologically safe environment for their teams, emphasizing the importance of vulnerability, empathy, and shared language in the workplace.
