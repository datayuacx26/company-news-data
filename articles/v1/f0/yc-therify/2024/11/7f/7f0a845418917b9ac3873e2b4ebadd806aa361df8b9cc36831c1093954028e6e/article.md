# From Passion to Profession: Indeed's Sarah Sloan on Leading Wellbeing in the Workplace

In this episode of "In Pursuit Of," host James Edward Murray engages in an insightful conversation with Sarah Sloan, the Global Well-Being Team Lead at Indeed. Sarah shares her journey of turning a personal passion for connecting people with resources into a 20-year career in employee well-being.


The discussion explores her unique approach to merging personal passion with professional success, the challenges of building a business case for wellbeing initiatives, and the importance of finding balance in a demanding career. Sarah also provides a glimpse into the future of well-being, highlighting the role of AI and the growing focus on mental health and career development within the workplace.
