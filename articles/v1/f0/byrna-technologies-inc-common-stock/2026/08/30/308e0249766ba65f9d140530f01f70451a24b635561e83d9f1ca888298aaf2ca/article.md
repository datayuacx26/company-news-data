## The Backpack That's Ready When You Are: Meet the Byrna Ballistipac


At first glance, the Byrna Ballistipac looks like exactly what it appears to be — a sleek, versatile everyday backpack built for the office, the trail, the airport, and everything in between. It carries your laptop, your gear, and your daily essentials without drawing a second look. But behind that unassuming exterior is something most backpacks can't offer: rapidly deployable ballistic protection, ready the moment you need it.


### Protection in seconds, with one hand


The Ballistipac is designed around a simple idea — that safety shouldn't slow you down. With just one hand, the front ballistic panel deploys in seconds, giving you bullet-resistant coverage across your chest and torso. Meanwhile, a second ballistic panel stays fixed in the back of the pack to help cover your six. Front and back, you're protected, and you never had to stop moving to make it happen.


That's the difference between armor you have to plan around and protection that's simply there when life takes an unexpected turn.


### Built to fit your life, not the other way around


Open the Ballistipac up and it works like the everyday carry you already rely on. Inside you'll find a padded laptop pocket plus two additional zippered compartments — all completely separate from the ballistic panel storage. That means your tech, your notebook, your charger, and your daily gear get plenty of room without ever crowding out your protection. Nothing about carrying the Ballistipac asks you to compromise.


For those who want to build out their setup, there's also a version with **MOLLE webbing** on the front panel. It lets you customize the pack with compatible pouches, holsters, and accessories, so the Ballistipac can flex to fit the way you move through your day. Prefer a cleaner, lower-profile look? A no-MOLLE option keeps things sleek and understated.


### Choose the level of protection that fits you


Not everyone faces the same risks, so the Ballistipac gives you options. Its ballistic panels come in multiple NIJ-rated configurations:


- **Level IIIA (handgun rated)** offers protection from common pistol rounds — the everyday threat most people are most likely to encounter.
- **Level III+ (rifle rated)** steps up to protection against common rifle threats, including AR-15 and AK-pattern rounds.


Whichever you choose, the protection is certified to a recognized NIJ standard, so you know exactly what you're carrying and what it's built to stop.


### Everyday carry, elevated


Here's what makes the Ballistipac special: you don't have to choose between looking normal and being prepared. It's a genuinely good backpack first — comfortable, organized, and versatile enough for the commute, the weekend trip, or the daily grind. The ballistic capability doesn't announce itself. It just waits quietly in the background, ready if you ever need it.


That's protection designed for real life. Not a bulky tactical rig you'd only grab in an emergency, but a pack you'll actually reach for every single day — one that happens to have your back, literally, when it counts.


### Confidence you can carry


The Byrna Ballistipac is more than a great way to move your everyday essentials from point A to point B. It's adaptable protection built for the way you work, travel, train, and live. It's the peace of mind that comes from knowing you're prepared, folded neatly into something you were going to carry anyway.


Because being ready shouldn't mean changing your life. Sometimes it just means changing your backpack.
