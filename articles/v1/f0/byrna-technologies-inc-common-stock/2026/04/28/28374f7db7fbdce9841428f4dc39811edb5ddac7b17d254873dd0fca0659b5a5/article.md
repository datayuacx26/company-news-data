# Air Gun Expert Puts the Byrna LE to the Test

## Real-World Testing Highlights the Power and Accuracy of Less-Lethal Launchers


Less-lethal self-defense technology continues to gain attention as more individuals seek effective alternatives to traditional firearms. Independent testing and hands-on demonstrations play a key role in understanding how these tools perform in real-world conditions.


Recent testing of a[Byrna LE Launcher](https://byrna.com/products/le-kit) provided insight into both its power and accuracy. During the evaluation, lightweight nylon projectiles were used to assess impact on a dense, natural target. Despite weighing only a few grams, the rounds delivered noticeable force, leaving visible damage on a hard surface.


### Power Meets Practical Performance


One of the most notable takeaways from the test was the launcher’s ability to generate significant energy relative to its size and projectile weight. Designed to deliver measurable impact, these systems aim to **disrupt or deter a threat without causing lethal harm** .


The observed results aligned with expected performance levels, demonstrating how less-lethal launchers can produce meaningful stopping power while maintaining a focus on safety.


### Accuracy at Defensive Distances


Accuracy is a critical factor in any self-defense tool. During testing, consistent hits were achieved on a small target from approximately 15 feet—a distance that reflects many real-world defensive scenarios. Reliable accuracy at this range is essential for ensuring that users can respond effectively under pressure.


The use of integrated sights also contributed to consistent shot placement, reinforcing the importance of proper design and user familiarity.


### Why Testing Matters


Hands-on testing provides valuable perspective beyond product specifications. It allows users to better understand how a tool performs in realistic conditions, including:


- Impact effectiveness
- Shot consistency
- Ease of use under repeated firing


This type of evaluation helps bridge the gap between theory and application, offering a clearer picture of what to expect in a defensive situation.


### A Growing Interest in Less-Lethal Options


As awareness increases, more individuals are exploring less-lethal solutions for personal protection. The combination of **power, accuracy, and reduced risk** makes these tools an appealing option for those seeking a balanced approach to self-defense.


Real-world testing continues to demonstrate that modern less-lethal launchers are capable, reliable, and increasingly relevant in today’s evolving safety landscape.
