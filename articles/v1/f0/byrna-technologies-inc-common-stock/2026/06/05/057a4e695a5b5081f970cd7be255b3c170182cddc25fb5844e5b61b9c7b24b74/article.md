# What are the Different Types of Projectiles?

## Byrna Ammo Explained: Choosing the Right Rounds for Training and Self-Defense


One of the biggest advantages of the[Byrna platform](https://byrna.com/collections/byrna-non-lethal-guns) is its versatility. Whether preparing for everyday carry, home defense, or regular training sessions, Byrna offers several types of ammunition designed for specific purposes. Understanding the differences between these projectiles can help users build the setup that best fits their needs.


### Byrna Chemical Rounds: Pepper and MAX


[Byrna's chemical projectiles](https://byrna.com/collections/61-less-lethal-self-defense-ammo) are designed to incapacitate threats without relying solely on direct impact. Both[Byrna Pepper](https://byrna.com/collections/61-less-lethal-self-defense-ammo/products/byrna-0-61-cal-pepper-projectiles) and[Byrna MAX](https://byrna.com/collections/61-less-lethal-self-defense-ammo/products/byrna-0-61-cal-max-projectiles) rounds are frangible projectiles, meaning they break apart on contact and release a powdered chemical agent.


Byrna Pepper rounds contain a blend of OC (oleoresin capsicum) and PAVA, ingredients commonly found in high-strength pepper sprays. Byrna MAX rounds combine OC with CS, the active ingredient used in tear gas formulations, creating an even more powerful deterrent.


Upon impact, these rounds disperse a chemical cloud approximately three to five feet wide. This allows users to engage a threat directly or create a chemical barrier by targeting the area around an aggressor.


### Byrna Kinetic Rounds


[Byrna Kinetic rounds](https://byrna.com/collections/61-less-lethal-self-defense-ammo/products/byrna-0-61-cal-kinetic-projectiles) serve a dual purpose as both self-defense and training ammunition. Unlike chemical projectiles, kinetic rounds are made from solid polymer and do not break on impact.


These rounds rely on pain compliance to deter aggressive behavior while remaining less-lethal. Designed to deliver significant stopping power, Byrna Kinetic rounds can also be used repeatedly during training sessions, making them a cost-effective option for building confidence and accuracy.


Because they are reusable, kinetic rounds pair well with target tents, cardboard boxes, and other safe training setups.


### Byrna Pro Training Rounds


For users looking to simulate the performance of chemical rounds without the chemical effects,[Byrna Pro Training rounds](https://byrna.com/collections/61-less-lethal-training-ammo/products/byrna-61-cal-pro-training-projectiles-25-count) offer an ideal solution.


These projectiles are designed to mimic the flight characteristics and impact behavior of Byrna Pepper and MAX rounds but contain an inert powder instead of active chemical agents. This allows for realistic scenario-based training while maintaining a safe practice environment.


### A Round for Every Purpose


Available in both .61 and .68 caliber options, Byrna ammunition allows users to customize their launcher for training, preparedness, and less-lethal defense. Whether practicing accuracy, building confidence, or preparing for real-world situations, choosing the right Byrna ammo helps ensure users are ready when it matters most.
