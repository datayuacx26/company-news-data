# If They Only Had a Byrna: Delivery Drivers Robbed

## Delivery Driver Robberies Highlight the Importance of Personal Safety on the Job


Delivery drivers face unique risks every day, especially when working late-night shifts in unfamiliar neighborhoods. A recent string of violent robberies in Quincy, Illinois, serves as a reminder that personal safety should be a priority for anyone whose job requires meeting strangers and making deliveries after dark.


According to local reports, five separate robberies targeting delivery drivers occurred within the same area over a one-month period. In one incident captured on surveillance footage, a driver arrived at a delivery location only to be confronted by multiple individuals. What appeared to be a routine stop quickly escalated into a violent assault.


The video shows one individual waiting near the street before several others joined in, surrounding the driver and attacking him. The suspects allegedly stole money and personal belongings before fleeing the scene. The victim suffered multiple injuries and was left without an immediate way to call for help.


While authorities have arrested several suspects connected to one of the robberies, investigators believe additional individuals may be responsible for other incidents in the area.
