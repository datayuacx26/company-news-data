# Can You Fly with Your Byrna Launcher?

## Flying with a Byrna Launcher: What Travelers Need to Know


Whether traveling for business, vacation, or a weekend getaway, personal safety doesn't have to stay home. One of the most common questions Byrna owners ask is whether they can fly with their[Byrna launcher.](https://byrna.com/collections/byrna-non-lethal-guns) The good news is that traveling with a Byrna is straightforward when a few simple guidelines are followed.


### Can You Bring a Byrna on an Airplane?


Byrna launchers are legal in all 50 states and do not require firearm permits or reciprocity agreements. However, like many self-defense tools, Byrna launchers must be transported in checked baggage when flying on commercial airlines.


Unlike firearms, Byrna launchers generally do not need to be declared at the airline counter. Travelers can place their launcher and projectiles in checked luggage and proceed through the standard baggage check process.


As always, travelers should verify current airline, TSA, and local regulations before departure, as policies can change over time.


### What About CO2 Cartridges?


[CO2 cartridges](https://byrna.com/collections/byrna-co2) require additional planning. While federal aviation regulations may allow certain compressed gas cartridges, TSA policies and airline-specific rules can vary.


For travelers using[Byrna SD](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-sd-non-lethal-self-defense-pistol) ,[Byrna CL](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-compact-launcher-universal-kit) , or other launchers that utilize 8-gram CO2 cartridges, a simple solution is to purchase cartridges at the destination or ship them ahead of time when appropriate.


Owners of Byrna launchers that use 12-gram CO2 cartridges may find replacements readily available at sporting goods stores, hardware stores, and many major retailers after arriving at their destination.


### Travel Prepared and Travel Smart


Preparing ahead of time can make traveling with a Byrna launcher simple and stress-free. Packing the launcher in checked baggage, understanding CO2 restrictions, and planning for replacement cartridges upon arrival can help ensure a smooth travel experience.


Whether traveling by car or by air, having a personal safety plan is an important part of any trip. With the right preparation, Byrna owners can maintain confidence and readiness wherever their travels take them.
