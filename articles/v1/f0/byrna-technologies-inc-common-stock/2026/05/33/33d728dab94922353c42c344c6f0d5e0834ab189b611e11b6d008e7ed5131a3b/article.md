# How Long Can You Leave the CO2 in the Launcher?

## Can a Byrna Launcher Stay Loaded? What Owners Need to Know About CO2 and Readiness


One of the most common questions about[Byrna launchers](https://byrna.com/collections/byrna-non-lethal-guns) is whether they can safely remain loaded for everyday readiness. For many people who rely on less-lethal self-defense tools, having equipment ready at a moment’s notice is an important part of personal protection and preparedness.


The good news is that Byrna launchers are specifically designed for convenient long-term readiness thanks to their innovative CO2 deployment system and detachable magazine design.


### Can a Byrna Launcher Stay Loaded?


Yes, Byrna launchers are intended to remain loaded with projectiles so they are ready when needed. The detachable magazine system allows users to preload their preferred ammunition configuration, including kinetic rounds, Max rounds, or pepper rounds, depending on personal preference and likely use scenarios.


This flexibility allows owners to customize their self-defense setup while keeping the launcher prepared for quick deployment.


### How Long Can CO2 Stay in the Launcher?


A major advantage of the Byrna Launcher platform is Byrna’s patented pull-pierce technology. Unlike many CO2-powered systems, the cartridge does not become activated when inserted into the launcher.


Instead, the first trigger pull simultaneously pierces the CO2 cartridge and fires the first round. Because the cartridge remains unpierced until activation, it can generally stay in the launcher indefinitely without losing pressure or effectiveness.


This design eliminates additional preparation steps during a stressful emergency and helps ensure the launcher is ready when seconds matter.


### When Should CO2 Be Replaced?


Once a CO2 cartridge has been pierced during training or defensive use, it is recommended to replace it with a fresh cartridge before returning the launcher to a ready state.


Fresh CO2 helps maintain consistent performance and reliability, especially in situations where dependable operation is critical.


### Everyday Readiness Made Simple


Byrna launchers are built around accessibility, preparedness, and ease of use. The ability to safely store a loaded launcher with unpierced CO2 gives users a practical less-lethal option that remains ready without complicated setup or maintenance requirements.
