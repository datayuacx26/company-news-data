# Former Chief of Police Touts Byrna as a Great Less-Lethal Solution

Law enforcement agencies around the world continue to search for safer and more effective alternatives between verbal commands and deadly force. Less-lethal technology has become an increasingly important part of modern policing, offering officers additional tools to de-escalate dangerous situations while reducing the risk of fatal outcomes.


Former Tijuana Police Chief Alejandro Lares recently highlighted the growing importance of less-lethal options like Byrna Launcher for police departments across Mexico and Latin America.


## Why Less-Lethal Tools Matter in Modern Policing


According to Lares, many officers historically had limited options when responding to violent encounters. In many cases, departments relied primarily on batons, pepper spray, or lethal firearms during high-risk incidents.


As threats evolve, law enforcement agencies are increasingly recognizing the need for tools that create distance between officers and suspects while helping avoid deadly outcomes whenever possible.


Byrna launchers provide officers with a less-lethal option capable of deploying kinetic and chemical projectiles designed to stop or deter aggressive individuals without relying immediately on firearms.


## Creating Distance During Dangerous Encounters


One of the major advantages of less-lethal launchers is the ability to engage threats from a safer distance. This added separation can give officers more time to assess situations, issue commands, and attempt de-escalation before resorting to deadly force.


For police departments facing volatile encounters, this capability can improve officer safety while also helping protect suspects and bystanders.


Lares described how less-lethal technology could have changed the outcome of past hostage situations and violent incidents where officers previously had few alternatives beyond firearms.


## Expanding Less-Lethal Training in Latin America


As awareness of less-lethal technology continues to grow, more agencies throughout Mexico and Latin America are exploring how these tools can fit into modern law enforcement operations.


Training remains a critical part of successful implementation. Officers must understand how and when to deploy less-lethal tools effectively while maintaining proper escalation protocols.


Byrna launchers continue gaining attention among law enforcement professionals because they offer a practical intermediate option designed to help save lives while improving public and officer safety.


## The Future of Less-Lethal Defense


The demand for less-lethal solutions continues to rise as agencies seek safer ways to manage dangerous encounters. For many departments, tools like Byrna launchers represent an important step toward reducing unnecessary injuries while expanding defensive options for officers in the field.
