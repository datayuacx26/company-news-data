# Top 5 College Safety Tips

## Top 5 Byrna Products Every College Student Should Consider


Heading to college is an exciting milestone, but it also comes with new responsibilities—including thinking about personal safety. Whether moving into a dorm, walking across campus after dark, or commuting to class, having the right safety tools can help students feel more prepared for everyday situations.


Here are five Byrna products designed to help college students stay confident and ready throughout the school year.


### 1. Byrna Shield


The[Byrna Shield](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-shield-backpack-body-armor?_pos=1&_psq=shield&_psid=d7a1c86d5&_ss=e) is a lightweight, bullet-resistant backpack insert that fits inside most backpacks. Designed to provide an added layer of protection against many common handgun threats, it allows students to carry discreet protection without changing their daily routine.


### 2. Byrna Ballistipac


For students seeking enhanced preparedness, the[Byrna Ballistipac](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-ballistipac-basic?_pos=3&_psq=ballistipac&_psid=fe24993fa&_ss=e) features integrated ballistic protection on both sides of the backpack. Its rapid-deployment design offers additional protection while remaining functional as an everyday backpack.


### 3. Byrna DUO


The[Byrna DUO](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-duo?_pos=1&_sid=c3d597123&_ss=r) combines two personal safety tools into one compact device: a 130-decibel personal safety alarm and Byrna's[Bad Guy Repellent (BGR)](https://byrna.com/collections/bgr-max-pepper-spray) chemical agent spray. The alarm can attract attention during an emergency while the spray helps create an opportunity to escape and seek safety.


### 4. Byrna Concealed Carry Waist Pack


A quality carry solution is just as important as the safety tools themselves. The[Byrna Concealed Carry Waist Pack](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-concealed-carry-waist-shoulder-pack-gen-2?_pos=1&_sid=de50f8f2d&_ss=r) provides quick access to compatible Byrna products while offering convenient storage for everyday essentials, making it an ideal option for walking across campus or traveling between classes.


### 5. Byrna CL Compact Launcher


The[Byrna CL Launcher](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-compact-launcher-universal-kit) is Byrna's compact less-lethal launcher, designed for discreet everyday carry while delivering effective stand-off capability. Many colleges prohibit firearms on campus, but policies regarding less-lethal launchers can vary. Students should always review and follow their school's rules and all applicable local laws before carrying any personal safety device.


## Start the School Year Prepared


College is about gaining independence, and personal safety is an important part of that journey. From backpack protection and personal alarms to less-lethal self-defense options, Byrna offers products that can help students feel more prepared wherever campus life takes them.


Before the semester begins, take time to understand campus safety policies, plan safe routes, and choose the personal safety tools that best fit your lifestyle. A little preparation today can help build greater confidence throughout the school year.
