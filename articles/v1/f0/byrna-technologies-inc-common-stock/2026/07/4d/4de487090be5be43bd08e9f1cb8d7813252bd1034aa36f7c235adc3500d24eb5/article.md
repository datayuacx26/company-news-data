# Byrna's Retail Launcher Maintenance Program

## How to Maintain Your Byrna Launcher for Reliable Performance


Proper maintenance is an important part of keeping any self-defense tool ready when it's needed most. The good news is that[maintaining your Byrna Launcher](https://byrna.com/collections/byrna-non-lethal-guns) is quick, straightforward, and requires only a few simple steps. With regular care, users can help ensure their launcher continues to perform reliably for both training and personal protection.


### Why Regular Maintenance Matters


The frequency of maintenance depends on several factors, including how often the launcher is used, the number of projectiles fired, environmental conditions, and the amount of time since its last use. Dust, debris, and residue can accumulate over time, making routine maintenance an important part of long-term performance.


### Cleaning the Byrna Launcher


Before beginning any maintenance, always verify that the launcher is unloaded and that the CO2 cartridge has been removed.


Using the[Byrna Oiler Kit](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-oiler-kit?_pos=1&_psq=oiler&_psid=93eb8d5c8&_ss=e) , start by cleaning the barrel with the included brush to remove any dirt, powder residue, or debris. A clean barrel helps maintain consistent performance during training and self-defense use.


### Lubricating Internal Components


After cleaning, insert the supplied oiler guide into the CO2 chamber. Using the oil applicator, place two to three drops of the recommended lubricant into the launcher.


Next, install a fresh CO2 cartridge and cycle the launcher according to the manufacturer's instructions. This allows the lubricant to circulate through the internal components, helping reduce wear and keep the launcher operating smoothly.


### Make Maintenance Part of Your Routine


Routine maintenance only takes a few minutes but can make a significant difference in the long-term reliability of a Byrna Launcher. Incorporating regular cleaning and lubrication into a training schedule helps ensure the launcher remains ready for everyday carry and emergency situations.


For complete maintenance instructions, always consult the official Byrna user manual for the launcher model being used.


A well-maintained launcher provides greater confidence during training and helps ensure dependable performance when it matters most. Taking a proactive approach to maintenance is one more step toward staying prepared with a reliable less-lethal self-defense solution.
