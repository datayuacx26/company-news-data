# Back-to-School Essentials: Byrna Has You Covered

## Back-to-School Safety Starts with More Than School Supplies


As families prepare for a new school year, backpacks are filled with notebooks, pencils, calculators, and everything students need to succeed in the classroom. But some of the most important essentials can't be found on a traditional school supply list—confidence, situational awareness, and personal safety.


Whether heading to[elementary school or high school](https://byrna.com/pages/back-to-school-gift-guide) -- or[going away to college](https://byrna.com/pages/back-to-school-gift-guide-college) , being prepared means more than having the right classroom supplies. It means having tools that can help provide greater confidence and peace of mind.


That's why Byrna offers a range of personal safety products designed to help students feel more prepared wherever the school day takes them. From the[Byrna Shield](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-shield-backpack-body-armor) , a lightweight ballistic backpack insert, to[Byrna Bad Guy Repellent](https://byrna.com/collections/bgr-max-pepper-spray) , a powerful personal safety spray, and the[Byrna Banshee](https://byrna.com/products/byrna-banshee-personal-safety-alarm) , a compact personal safety alarm that can quickly draw attention during an emergency, there are options for students of all ages.


While no one can predict every situation a student may encounter, taking proactive steps toward personal safety can help families feel more prepared.


This back-to-school season, don't just pack the supplies needed for learning—help equip students with the confidence and readiness to face each day. Because peace of mind belongs in every backpack.
