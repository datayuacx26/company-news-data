# People Trying Byrna for the First Time: See Their Reactions

## First-Time Shooters Put Byrna to the Test


Nothing builds[confidence in a self-defense tool](https://byrna.com/collections/byrna-non-lethal-guns) like experiencing it firsthand. That's exactly why the Byrna trailer recently stopped at several **Big 5 Sporting Goods** locations, giving shoppers the opportunity to test-fire a Byrna launcher, ask questions, and discover what less-lethal self-defense feels like before making a purchase.


For many attendees, it was their very first time ever shooting any type of launcher—and their reactions were overwhelmingly positive.


### First Impressions: Easier Than Expected


One of the most common comments from participants was just how easy the Byrna launcher was to use.


Many expected the launcher to feel intimidating or difficult to operate. Instead, first-time shooters consistently described it as lightweight, comfortable to hold, and surprisingly intuitive. Several were especially impressed by the virtually recoil-free shooting experience, making it easier to stay on target and build confidence with every shot.


For people who had never fired a self-defense tool before, the experience quickly replaced hesitation with excitement.


### Power That Surprised Everyone


While the Byrna launcher proved easy to shoot, attendees were equally surprised by its performance.


Many participants commented that the launcher delivered significantly more power than they expected while maintaining excellent accuracy. After experiencing the launcher firsthand, several visitors said it completely changed their perception of what a less-lethal self-defense tool could do.


Others noted that Byrna offers an appealing option for protecting their home and family while avoiding some of the concerns they associate with traditional firearms.


### Why Hands-On Demonstrations Matter


Reading reviews and watching videos can only go so far. Actually holding a Byrna launcher, feeling the trigger, and seeing its performance in person gave attendees a new level of confidence.


Byrna's mobile trailer events allow prospective owners to ask questions, compare models, and experience the platform in a safe, controlled environment before making a decision.


### Experience Byrna for Yourself


The reactions from Big 5 shoppers highlighted one consistent takeaway: the best way to understand Byrna is to try it.


Whether someone is completely new to self-defense or looking for a less-lethal option for home and personal protection, a hands-on demonstration provides the confidence that only firsthand experience can deliver.


Keep an eye out for the Byrna trailer at a Big 5 location near you—you may walk away just as impressed as these first-time shooters.
