## Top 5 Back-to-School Safety Tips Every Family Should Know


Back-to-school season is an exciting time for students and parents alike, but it's also the perfect opportunity to review important safety habits. From walking to school to using social media responsibly, small preparations can make a big difference in helping students stay safe throughout the school year.


Here are five back-to-school safety tips every family should consider.


### 1. Think Before Posting on Social Media


First-day-of-school photos are a favorite tradition, but they can also reveal more information than intended. School names, grade levels, uniforms, and daily routines can all provide personal details that should remain private. Parents should also encourage children to be mindful about what they share online and understand the importance of protecting their personal information.


### 2. Know the School's Emergency Procedures


Every student should understand their school's evacuation plans and emergency response procedures. After school, ask children what they learned during safety drills and make sure they know where to go and how to respond during an emergency. Staying informed helps build confidence and preparedness.


### 3. Practice Situational Awareness


One of the most effective personal safety habits is simply paying attention. Encourage students to stay aware of their surroundings by noticing exits, emergency equipment, suspicious behavior, and potential hazards. Awareness can help individuals recognize problems early and react more effectively if needed.


### 4. Choose Safe Routes to School


Whether walking or biking, students should use well-lit streets, marked crosswalks, and routes with crossing guards whenever possible. Traveling through busy, populated areas can help reduce unnecessary risks and provide additional visibility.

### Be Prepared for the School Year


The new school year is about more than academic success—it's also about helping students feel confident, aware, and prepared.
