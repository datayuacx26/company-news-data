# The Best of Byrna's Stories

## Real-World Self-Defense Scenarios Highlight the Value of Less-Lethal Protection


Unpredictable situations can arise anywhere—from private property and public roads to hiking trails and everyday routines. Real-world encounters continue to show how quickly threats can escalate and why preparedness plays a critical role in personal safety.


In one incident, a homeowner encountered an intruder who had entered a gated property carrying chainsaws. Faced with an immediate threat, the individual deployed a less-lethal launcher, striking the attacker in the leg. The intruder retreated shortly after, ending the confrontation without further escalation.


In another situation, a driver encountered an aggressive individual exhibiting signs of intoxication during a roadside confrontation. Recognizing the unpredictability of the situation, the individual prepared a self-defense tool before the encounter escalated. When the aggressor approached within close range, the visible presence of the device helped de-escalate the situation and create distance.


Outdoor environments can also present unexpected risks. In one case, a person hiking with family members was followed by a mountain lion for several minutes. When the animal approached within close proximity, a **Byrna SD Launcher** was used to deploy rounds in the surrounding area. The noise and impact caused the animal to retreat, allowing the group to exit safely.


Additional encounters highlight similar patterns—individuals approached by aggressive or intoxicated strangers, or confronted unexpectedly in close quarters. In these cases, maintaining awareness, creating distance, and having access to a self-defense tool proved critical in preventing escalation.


Less-lethal tools are designed to provide a **practical option for disrupting threats without resorting to deadly force** . Whether used to deter an aggressor, create space, or signal readiness to defend, these tools can offer valuable seconds in high-pressure situations.


Across a wide range of scenarios—home defense, travel, outdoor activity, and daily errands—the common theme is preparedness. Situational awareness combined with accessible, easy-to-use tools can make a meaningful difference when facing unpredictable threats.


As these real-world examples demonstrate, having a plan and the right equipment can help individuals respond effectively while reducing the likelihood of serious or irreversible outcomes.
