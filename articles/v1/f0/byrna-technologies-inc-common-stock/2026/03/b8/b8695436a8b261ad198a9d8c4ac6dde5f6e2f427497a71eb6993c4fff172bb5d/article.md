# The Byrna CL Highlight Ft. Amber Elle

# Byrna CL: Compact, Powerful, and Built for Everyday Protection


The Byrna CL—short for *Compact Launcher* —is one of the most compact less-lethal self-defense tools available today. Designed for accessibility and ease of use, it offers a practical solution for individuals seeking effective personal protection without the use of a traditional firearm.


---


## What Makes It a “Launcher,” Not a Firearm?


At first glance, the Byrna CL may resemble a firearm, but it operates very differently.


There are:


- No bullets
- No gunpowder
- No explosive discharge


Instead, the Byrna CL is powered by CO2 and launches projectiles. This distinction is what classifies it as a less-lethal launcher rather than a firearm.


---


## CO2-Powered Performance


The Byrna CL uses **8g CO2 cartridges** , which are inserted into the front of the launcher. A key advantage is that these cartridges remain **unpunctured until the first trigger pull** .


This design offers an important benefit:
Unpunctured CO2 cartridges do not leak over time, meaning the launcher can remain staged—whether in a purse, vehicle, or bedside—with full power available when needed.


Once the trigger is pulled, the cartridge is punctured and the launcher deploys a projectile at maximum capacity.


---


## Understanding the Projectiles


Instead of bullets, the Byrna CL uses **projectiles** , with multiple options depending on the situation:


### Kinetic Rounds


These rounds are designed to deliver **blunt force impact** , helping to:


- Deter a threat
- Create distance
- Provide an opportunity to escape


### Chemical (Max) Projectiles


For enhanced stopping power, users can choose projectiles that contain a blend of:


- **OC (oleoresin capsicum)** — commonly known as pepper spray
- **PAVA** — a synthetic compound significantly more potent than traditional pepper spray


These rounds combine physical impact with an incapacitating chemical effect, increasing their effectiveness in defensive situations.


---


## Simple, Integrated Design


One of the standout features of the Byrna CL is its **all-in-one design** , making it easy to stage, carry, and maintain.


- A **hex key is built into the bottom of the magazine** , eliminating the need for extra tools
- CO2 cartridges can be installed quickly and efficiently
- The magazine holds **five rounds**


This streamlined setup ensures the launcher is always ready without added complexity.


---


## Easy Loading Process


Loading the Byrna CL is straightforward:


1. Insert the CO2 cartridge into the launcher
2. Secure it using the integrated hex key
3. Load up to five projectiles into the magazine by pressing down the follower
4. Insert the magazine into the launcher


Once loaded, a **visual indicator (“tattletale”)** shows that a projectile is chambered and the launcher is ready.


Importantly, the CO2 cartridge remains unpunctured until the trigger is pulled—allowing the device to stay staged without losing pressure.


---


## Designed for Real-World Readiness


The Byrna CL is built for simplicity, reliability, and accessibility. Its compact size and intuitive design make it easy to carry and deploy, while its less-lethal approach provides a practical option for personal protection.


Everyone deserves to feel safe—and to have tools that support that goal in a responsible, effective way.


---


## Final Thoughts


The Byrna CL combines compact design, ease of use, and powerful less-lethal performance into a single platform. Whether carried daily or staged for quick access, it offers a straightforward solution for those looking to take a more active role in personal safety.
