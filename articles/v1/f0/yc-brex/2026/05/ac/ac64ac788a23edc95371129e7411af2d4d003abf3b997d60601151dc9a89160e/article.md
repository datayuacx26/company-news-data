# How to build a wellness stipend on Brex

In Brex, you can use preconfigured policies with common rules around receipts, memos, and approvals, or[build custom policies](https://www.brex.com/support/policy-rule-builder#create-or-edit-a-policy) to get as granular as you want.


For example, for a wellness stipend, you can forgo requiring receipts and memos, or require them for expenses over a certain amount. Transactions that fall within the approved categories will be auto-approved by[Brex AI](https://www.brex.com/platform/intelligent-finance) . No manual review or micromanagement required.


For internal auditing, you can run a[quarterly report](https://www.brex.com/platform/reporting) on the spend limit to review usage, check for necessary[employee repayments](https://www.brex.com/support/employee-repayments) , or export your spend limits to leverage the data in other systems.
