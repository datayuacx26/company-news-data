It learns and enforces[expense policies](https://www.brex.com/spend-trends/expense-management/expense-policy) , completes expense reports and reviews, and simplifies accounting so employees and finance teams can focus on work that’s more worthy of human brain power.


Best of all, intelligent finance gets smarter with every interaction and can anticipate what needs to be done and suggest a course of action or even autonomously take action on your behalf. The power of your team will be multiplied. Welcome to the next frontier of finance.


## What exactly is intelligent finance?


Intelligent finance is a revolutionary, AI-native approach to[financial operations](https://www.brex.com/journal/financial-operations) from Brex. It adds layers of learning, context, and autonomous decision-making to accelerate finance on a global scale.


While traditional automation executes “if this, then that” logic, intelligent finance goes five steps further. It doesn’t just run rules. It looks for and understands patterns, asks questions when things don’t match, and improves over time.


Brex AI monitors your spending, receives your responses to alerts and suggestions, and syncs with your ERP, HRIS, and other tools. And it begins to understand the story behind every transaction. It learns your norms, flags deviations, and suggests ways to fill gaps even without prompting.


The result is that companies gain both operational speed *and* financial control simultaneously, finally breaking the age-old tradeoff. Companies on Brex routinely process expenses 9 times faster, maintain ~98% employee policy compliance, and close accounting cycles 3 times faster.

#### 1. Autonomously completes expenses


## With Brex’s intelligent finance platform, every employee gets an[AI Assistant](https://www.brex.com/platform/intelligent-finance) that functions like a personal assistant embedded in the expense workflow.
