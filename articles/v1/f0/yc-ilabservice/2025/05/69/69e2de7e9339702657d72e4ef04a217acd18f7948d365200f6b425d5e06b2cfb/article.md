# iLabService and Synertec Intelligence Sign Long-term Strategic Partnership Agreement to Expand into Southeast Asia

**About**


has officially signed a long-term strategic partnership agreement with


**Synertec Intelligence**


from Singapore. This collaboration represents a strategic expansion and continuation of the partnership between the two parties, following their successful business implementation in the Singapore market, and will fully leverage iLabService’s years of technical accumulation and customer experience in the SmartLab field, as well as Synertec Intelligence’s extensive customer base and service capabilities in Southeast Asia, to jointly promote the market expansion of SmartLab in the region.


Over the years, iLabService has been committed to providing integrated digital service platforms for laboratories using Internet of Things, artificial intelligence, and cloud technologies. The company has served hundreds of customers across industries such as life sciences, testing and inspection, government agencies, food and chemicals, healthcare, academic research, etc. Synertec Intelligence, a laboratory management and consulting team with significant influence in Southeast Asia, is dedicated to driving the digital transformation of laboratories and offers services ranging from Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS) to smart lab solutions.


This cooperation will bring more efficient and smarter management experiences to laboratories in Southeast Asia. By integrating the strengths of both parties, the SmartLab solution will better meet the needs of customers in the region and help laboratories achieve digital transformation that ensures safety, compliance, and cost reduction. In the future, the two parties will work together to continuously promote the widespread application of smart labs in the Southeast Asian market and provide stronger support for scientific research and innovation.


##


**About Synertec Intelligence**


Synertec Intelligence Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based company specializing in the


digitization and modernization of laboratory environments. The company focuses on creating "Smart Laboratories" by integrating advanced software solutions, cloud services, mobile technologies, new instruments, monitoring devices, and IoT. Our mission is to optimize laboratory operations through advanced technology, software, solutions, and data analytics.


##


**About iLabService**


Established in 2016 in New Jersey, USA and with APAC headquarter in Hong Kong, iLabService operates an extensive distribution and service network across major cities in Greater China & Southeast Asia. Our 100+ R&D engineers and scientists possess cross-industry expertise spanning laboratory operations, information technology, and professional consulting.


Dedicated to revolutionizing lab management, iLabService integrates IoT-enabled hardware and cloud-based software solutions to centralize lab operation data through a unified dashboard. By applying cutting-edge AI, IoT, and cloud technologies, we empower laboratories to achieve digital transformation in three core areas: safety compliance enhancement, operational efficiency optimization, and cost reduction strategies.


iLabService makes labs more intelligent, management easier, scientists more focused, and research more efficient!
