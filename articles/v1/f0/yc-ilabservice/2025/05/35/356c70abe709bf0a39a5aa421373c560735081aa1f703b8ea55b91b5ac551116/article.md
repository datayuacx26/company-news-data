# iLabService Announces Official Entry into Hong Kong Science Park (HKSTP)

iLabService announced its official entry into the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks (HKSTP), signifying a major milestone in its international business expansion. This move not only underscores iLabService’s strategic advancement into global markets but also highlights HKSTP’s appeal as an international innovation hub.


##


**Hong Kong Science and Technology Park: A Global Benchmark for Biomedicine Innovation Ecosystem**


Leveraging its mature research ecosystem and policy support, HKSTP has become a core hub for biomedicine in Asia and globally. The park hosts over 2,000 technology companies and has established a comprehensive support system covering the entire lifecycle from “R&D to translation and commercialization,” including the Biomedicine Special Fund, shared laboratory platforms, and international collaboration networks. Notably, HKSTP has developed unique strengths in biomedicine: its clinical data is recognized by regulatory agencies worldwide, and as the largest biotech financing hub in the Asia-Pacific region, it attracts numerous startups and multinational pharmaceutical companies. These advantages align closely with iLabService’s strategy of serving high-tech sectors such as biomedicine and new materials, making Hong Kong a pivotal first stop in its global expansion.


##


**Deepening Shanghai - Hong Kong Collaborative Innovation Through 01LAB@HK**


iLabService previously supported the SHinno by deploying its intelligent laboratory management system in the Shanghai Campus, and in “01LAB@HK,” a joint incubator established by HKSTP and Shanghai Pharmaceuticals. The platform focuses on cutting-edge fields such as gene and cell therapy and anti-aging, offering 1,300 square meters of modular laboratory space and cross-border translation support. It has already incubated multiple high-potential enterprises. Building on its technical and product experience with 01LAB@HK, iLabService has gained localized service expertise. Moving forward, the company will use this foundation to provide digital laboratory solutions, data analytics, and compliance management services to biomedicine enterprises within the park, enhancing R&D efficiency.


##


**Collaborating with Partners to Shape the Future of Smart Research**


iLabService is committed to revolutionizing laboratory operations through AIoT, big data, and other advanced technologies. Its solutions already serve hundreds of R&D institutions worldwide. Following its entry into HKSTP, the company will further integrate resources to deliver more efficient and compliant innovation support to clients across the Asia-Pacific region and beyond.


> HKSTP’s global vision and innovation ecosystem provide an ideal platform for us to connect with the Greater Bay Area and global markets. We look forward to collaborating with more enterprises, research institutions, and investors to drive the intelligent transformation of laboratories.


Li Kang, Founder and CEO of iLabService, stated.
