# iLabService Earns ISO/IEC 27017:2015 & ISO/IEC 27018:2019 Certifications

Thrilled to announce a significant milestone for iLabService: We’ve successfully achieved both ISO/IEC 27017:2015 and ISO/IEC 27018:2019 certifications this November! This dual achievement isn’t just a badge of honor—it’s a testament to our unwavering commitment to setting the highest standards in cloud service security and data privacy.


In an era where 96% of organizations express moderate to high concerns about cloud security (Fortinet, 2024) and 78% adopt hybrid or multi-cloud strategies, these certifications carry profound industry significance. ISO/IEC 27017:2015—specifically designed for cloud service providers—establishes rigorous controls for cloud-specific risks, from virtualization security to multi-tenant isolation. Complementing this, ISO/IEC 27018:2019, the first international code of practice for personally identifiable information (PII) protection in public clouds, addresses the critical need for transparent and responsible PII handling in today’s data-driven landscape.


For our clients, this dual certification translates to tangible, actionable value:


- **Uncompromised Data Protection**


: Our compliance with ISO 27017 ensures your cloud-hosted data and applications are safeguarded by internationally recognized controls, mitigating risks of breaches and service disruptions. For PII-specific needs, ISO 27018 guarantees clear transparency—you retain full visibility into how your data is processed, and we never use or share your data without explicit consent.


- **Global Compliance Confidence**


: As businesses operate across borders, these certifications simplify adherence to stringent regulations like GDPR, CCPA, and regional data protection laws. You can trust that our cloud services align with global privacy frameworks, reducing regulatory risks and audit complexities.


- **Clear Responsibility Alignment**


: We define and uphold clear security responsibilities between iLabService and your organization (per ISO 27017’s shared responsibility model), eliminating ambiguities that often lead to security gaps.


Earning these certifications required more than just meeting checkboxes—it demanded a rigorous audit of our entire cloud security governance, from risk assessment protocols to incident response mechanisms. Built on our existing ISO 27001 foundation, this achievement reflects our proactive investment in security innovation and our team’s deep expertise in navigating the evolving cloud threat landscape.


At iLabService, we believe cloud security isn’t a feature—it’s the foundation of trusted partnerships. Whether you’re scaling a multi-cloud environment, managing sensitive customer PII, or expanding into global markets, these certifications underscore our promise to keep your data secure, compliant, and in your control.
