# Redwire to Host Briefing and Discussion of Microgravity and Pharmaceutical Development Following the Announcement of SpaceMD’s First Commercial Mission on SpaceX’s Starfall Spacecraft

**Event Date:**
Friday August 7, 2026, at 10:30 AM EST.


**Location:**
National Press Club
529 14th Street, N.W., Washington, DC
Frank Holeman Lounge, 13th Floor.
Note: Lounge entrance located on 14th St


**RSVP:** Your RSVP is required to attend. Please RSVP by emailingTere.Riley@rdw.com


Following the public announcement of[SpaceMD’s first commercial Starfall mission](https://rdw.com/newsroom/redwires-spacemd-announces-its-first-commercial-mission-on-spacexs-starfall-spacecraft-to-scale-in-space-pharmaceutical-development/) with SpaceX, Redwire will host an in‑depth industry event to discuss how this milestone marks a transformational moment for biotech, in-space manufacturing, and the emerging space-enabled economy. Public and private sector leaders as well as trailblazing researchers will describe how microgravity can transform both the space and pharmaceutical / biotech fields.


**Speakers Include:**


- Mike Gold, President, Redwire Space
- John Vellinger, CEO, SpaceMD
- Taylor Jordan, Director, Office of Space Commerce
- Gabriel Swiney, Deputy Director, Office of Space Commerce
- Paul Reichert, Former Principal Investigator, Merck Research Laboratories
- John Thompson, Deputy Assistant Secretary for Space and Environment, Department of State (invited)


The event will also be livestreamed via[Redwire’s YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCkJH9NJoaVjUrVp-lN397iw) .
