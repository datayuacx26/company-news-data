# Engage with Redwire at the 40th Annual Small Satellite Conference

Join Redwire at booth #1529 to explore our next-generation solutions engineered for today’s most complex missions.


**Tuesday, August 25**
3:25–4:10 p.m. MT
**Location:** Salt Palace Room 251 B/C (second level)


**Panel:** A Look at What’s Limiting Constellations Now: Rethinking Power, Volume, and Scale


As satellite constellations expand, new constraints are emerging in power, stowed volume, and production throughput. This session will explore where current architectures are falling short and how the industry is evolving to meet modern mission demands.


**Speakers**


- Garrett Reim, Space & Emerging Technologies Editor, AviationWeek (moderator)
- Carrie O’Quinn, Principal Director, Space Development Agency Support, The Aerospace Corporation
- Harry Yates, Capture & BD Director, Redwire
- Jaroslaw Jaworski, CEO of Edge Aerospace
- Charles Anderson, Deputy Mission Area Executive, National Security Space, JHU/APL
