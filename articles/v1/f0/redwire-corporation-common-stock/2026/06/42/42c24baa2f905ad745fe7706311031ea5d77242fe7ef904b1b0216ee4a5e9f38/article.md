# Ukraine Armed Forces Embrace Penguin for Day-to-Day Operations

The Redwire Penguin uncrewed aerial system (UAS) has been a fixture in Ukraine’s defense against Russia since 2022. To date, Redwire has delivered more than 200 combat-proven Penguin UAS to the Ukraine Armed Forces for deployment in one of the world’s most challenging contested environments, where advanced drone technology is changing the very nature of modern warfare.


Ukrainian media recently followed engagement operations in which Ukraine Armed Forces used Penguin UAS.


Redwire is dedicated to supporting our allies’ defense and national security needs, and with a well-established – and growing – presence in Europe, Redwire is uniquely positioned to offer solutions that evolve at the speed of the mission. This rapid and agile response to real-world challenges gives our customers a distinct in-field advantage.
