# Redwire to Showcase UAS and ISR Capabilities at Eurosatory 2026

The Redwire team will be in Paris, France from June 15-19 for Eurosatory 2026 to connect with international government, military, and industry and explore the growing interconnections within the naval, space, and cyber domains.


Redwire will be showcasing its Penguin uncrewed aerial system (UAS) and Octopus surveillance systems, both built in the E.U., along with our U.S.-built Stalker UAS in booth C254. Our products are combat proven, with hundreds of thousands of flight hours across six continents and nearly three decades of experience supporting international allied defense initiatives.


Stop by the Redwire Lounge at booth D281 for refreshments and an opportunity to meet with the Redwire team.
