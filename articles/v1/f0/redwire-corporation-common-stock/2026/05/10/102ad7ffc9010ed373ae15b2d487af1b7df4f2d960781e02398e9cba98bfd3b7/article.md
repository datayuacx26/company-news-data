# Redwire Showcases UAS and ISR Technology at SOF Week 2026

From May 18-21 the Redwire team will be in booth #1533 at the Tampa Convention Center. Stop by to learn about our Stalker and Penguin UAS, two combat proven small fixed-wing uncrewed aerial systems that are delivering the mission capability of larger UAS and the agility needed in the field. We’ll also be showcasing our Octopus line of optical gimbal camera payloads – high-performance, compact, and ITAR-free to empower the operator to see farther, interpret faster, and act on intelligence with greater confidence. Meet our team of experts to see how our range of capabilities is supporting the warfighter.


Stop by the Redwire booth for custom coffee bars on May 18th and 20th and a special happy hour on May 19th.
