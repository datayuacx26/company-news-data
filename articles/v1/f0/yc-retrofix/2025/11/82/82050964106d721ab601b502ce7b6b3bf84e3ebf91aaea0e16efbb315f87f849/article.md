## What Makes Reducto Different


Traditional OCR sees a document as a flat image and tries to extract text. It fails spectacularly with:


**Complex layouts** : Multi-column formats, nested tables, mixed orientations


**Handwritten content** : Annotations, signatures, corrections


**Mixed media** : Documents combining typed text, handwriting, and images


**Non-standard formats** : Government forms, utility bills, technical drawings


Reducto uses advanced vision models and LLMs to actually understand document structure. It processes each element—tables, images, graphs, handwritten notes—through specialized pipelines optimized for that content type.
