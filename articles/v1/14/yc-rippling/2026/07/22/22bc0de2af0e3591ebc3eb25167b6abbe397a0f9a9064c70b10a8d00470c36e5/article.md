# The 8 Best IT Inventory Management Software

Managing IT assets is a critical but complex task for any business. From procuring and deploying new devices to employees, to tracking those assets throughout their lifecycle, to eventually retrieving the devices when an employee leaves—it's a lot to stay on top of.


The pains are amplified for distributed workforces. IT admins and managers find themselves spending Friday afternoons at FedEx shipping laptops to remote new hires or picking up company computers sent by departed employees. These headaches point to the clear need of a centralized system that streamlines the entire IT asset management process end to end.


While spreadsheets may work for very small operations, most companies find that dedicated IT inventory software is the best way to efficiently manage their technology assets at scale. But with numerous options on the market offering different features and capabilities, choosing the ideal system for your organization's needs can be challenging. This piece will help you make an informed decision.


## What is IT inventory management software?


IT inventory management is the process of tracking, managing, and optimizing all the hardware, software, and other technology assets within an organization. This includes devices like laptops, desktops, monitors, mobile devices, and tablets, as well as software licenses, subscriptions, network equipment, and any other IT assets a company uses. For businesses that sell products, this can also include point of sale systems and other specialized equipment used in retail or e-commerce operations.

Inventory management software enables companies to maintain a real-time database of their complete IT asset inventory. Beyond just tracking what assets you have and who they are assigned to, these systems also help manage the entire lifecycle of assets from procurement to retirement.


**Real life example:** Your marketing team is expanding and needs new laptops with licensed design software. With an IT asset management system, you could:


1.


Easily see current laptop inventory and availability


2.


Procure the right number of devices with a few clicks


3.


Automatically assign the laptops and software licenses to the new hires


4.


Ship the devices pre-configured directly to the employees


5.


Track the location and status of those assets over time


6.


Reassign or dispose of the assets when no longer needed


Having a unified system enables much smoother logistics, tighter security, and better visibility compared to trying to manage everything manually.


Some IT inventory management solutions focus purely on the software side—providing a centralized platform to track assets. ## Must-have features in an IT asset inventory management system


When evaluating different IT inventory management solutions, here are some of the key features and capabilities to look for:


### Asset discovery and tracking


A core component of any IT asset management system is maintaining an up-to-date inventory of all hardware and software assets. Automated discovery tools can help find and import new assets into the system. Unique identifiers like barcodes or RFID (radio frequency identification) tags enable each asset to be scanned and tracked individually. Logging key asset details like device specs, serial numbers, costs, and warranties provides a comprehensive record.


### User assignment and location tracking


To effectively manage assets, you need to know not just what you have but where those assets are located and who they are assigned to. Look for features that allow you to assign assets to specific users and departments, track assets across multiple office locations, and get stock alerts if any assets go missing. Integration with your HR system will help keep asset records in sync as employees join and leave the company.


### Software license management


With software increasingly moving to subscription models, managing licenses is a key part of IT asset management. Software license management capabilities allow you to track how many licenses you own for each application, which users or devices those licenses are assigned to, and when licenses are set to expire. This helps avoid surprise bills, software audits, and compliance issues.


### Reporting and analytics


As the saying goes, you can't improve what you don't measure. Robust reporting is essential for IT asset management. Look for a system with customizable reports and dashboards that give you real-time visibility into your asset inventory, status, and activities. Analyzing metrics over time can help uncover opportunities to optimize asset usage and costs.


### Lifecycle management


Effective asset management requires overseeing the entire lifecycle of assets from acquisition to disposal. Procurement features help streamline purchasing of new assets. Maintenance tracking ensures assets are properly serviced and updated to extend their useful life. End-of-life management capabilities facilitate the secure disposal of retired assets, ensuring data is wiped and hardware is responsibly recycled.


### Integration capabilities


To be most effective, an IT asset management system should integrate with your other key business platforms. Integrations with procurement and finance systems aid purchasing and billing. Syncing with your HR platform ensures asset records are updated as employees join and leave. Connections to your IT service desk enable assets to be seamlessly tied to support tickets and requests. For retail businesses, integration with POS systems can provide valuable insights into hardware performance and usage patterns at checkout locations.


### Security and compliance


With IT assets containing sensitive company data and access, security is paramount. The asset management system should offer features like role-based permissions, audit trails, and secure disposal workflows. Compliance with relevant industry regulations like[SOC 2](https://www.rippling.com/blog/it-beginners-guide-system-and-organization-controls-2-soc) , ISO 27001, or HIPAA may be necessary.[Mobile device management (MDM)](https://www.rippling.com/blog/it-beginners-guide-mobile-device-management-mdm) capabilities to enforce encryption and other[endpoint protections](https://www.rippling.com/blog/endpoint-protection-the-impact-of-ai-and-ml-on-threat-detection) are also valuable.

## Frequently asked questions


How do I choose the right IT inventory management system?


Consider factors like the size of your organization, types of assets you need to track, level of automation and integration desired, and your budget. Assess different solutions based on the feature checklist above. Look for a provider that can scale with your needs and has a track record with similar companies. Take advantage of free trials to test systems before committing.


What IT assets can I track with IT inventory software?


Any physical hardware like laptops, desktops, mobile devices, servers, printers, or network equipment can be tracked. Software licenses and cloud subscriptions can also be monitored, along with peripherals like monitors and keyboards. Some solutions may support non-IT assets as well, or have modules specifically for those.


Are IT inventory management systems suitable for small businesses?


Yes, even small businesses can benefit from a centralized way to track IT assets as they grow. Many solutions offer low-cost or even free tiers geared towards small businesses, with the ability to upgrade as your needs expand. Investing early in an IT management platform can save time and money in the long run by establishing good practices from the start.
