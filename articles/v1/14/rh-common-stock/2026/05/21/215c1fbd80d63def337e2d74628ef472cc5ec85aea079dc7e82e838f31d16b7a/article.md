# RH Chairman & CEO Gary Friedman Pens a Letter to Milan, Announcing the Opening of RH Milan, the Gallery on Corso Venezia, Seven Levels of Furniture, Design, Food & Wine


May 15, 2026 6:00 AM PDT


CORTE MADERA, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- RH (NYSE: RH) announced today that **Chairman & CEO Gary Friedman** penned a letter to the city and people of Milan announcing the recent opening of RH Milan, The Gallery on Corso Venezia, Seven Levels of Furniture, Design, Food & Wine.


The letter, brought to life in a dramatic video, is a gesture of respect and recognition that, “In Milan, the measure is mastery. This we know and have designed accordingly.”
