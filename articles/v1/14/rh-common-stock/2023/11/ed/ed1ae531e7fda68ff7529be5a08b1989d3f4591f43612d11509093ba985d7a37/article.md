# RH Announces the Opening of RH Indianapolis, the Gallery at the DeHaan Estate


November 20, 2023 6:17 AM PST


The *151-Acre Estate and Palladian Villa has been Reimagined as a First-Of-Its-Kind Design and Hospitality Destination Featuring a Dramatic Restaurant, Wine Bar, Lakeside Terraces, Art Installations, and an Interior Design Studio and Services*


CORTE MADERA, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- RH (NYSE: RH) announced today the opening of[RH Indianapolis, The Gallery at the DeHaan Estate](https://rh.com/us/en/indianapolis) **** – the former home of celebrated philanthropist Christel DeHaan. One of the most accurate Palladian-style villas ever built in the United States, the property is a study in balance, symmetry and perfect proportions – the core pillars of the RH design ethos. The estate spans 151 acres and over 60 rooms, and represents one of the largest, most inspiring and immersive physical expressions of the brand to date.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231120093020/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231120093020/en/)


RH INDIANAPOLIS, THE GALLERY AT THE DEHAAN ESTATE (Photo: Business Wire)


The unrivaled experience of RH Indianapolis seamlessly integrates luxury home furnishings collections from[RH Interiors](https://rhinteriors.rh.com/us/en/) **,**[Contemporary](http://rhcontemporary.com/) ,[Modern](http://rhmodern.com/) and[Outdoor](http://rhoutdoor.rh.com/) with rare art, antiques and artifacts from across the globe. An elegant juxtaposition of past and present, relaxed and refined, the landmark property has been thoughtfully reimagined with great respect for its original vision as a prestigious private residence. RH Indianapolis also reflects the brand’s continued foray into hospitality with The Dining Room, a dramatic restaurant that opens – along with a Wine Bar and Terrace – onto a 35-acre private lake, lush formal gardens and vast forest landscape.


“The DeHaan Estate is one of the most magical properties in North America, and we are honored to open its gates to the public for the first time,” said **RH Chairman & CEO Gary Friedman** . “From the immaculately groomed grounds and tree lined driveway, to the massive stone terrace and boat dock overlooking the lake and forest, and the perfectly proportioned rooms filled with natural light from the abundance of windows, RH Indianapolis is a place to come and dream, be inspired by the architecture; get lost in the views, enjoy a meal in the grand ballroom or sip a glass of wine while strolling through the gardens.”


Guests enter the property via a dramatic drive flanked by a double linden-tree allée, which leads to the main house, whose crescent-shaped wings create an elegant forecourt. Passing through hand-forged iron doors, visitors will encounter a graceful, curved foyer – the first in a striking sequence of rooms showcasing inspiring interior vistas, Italian-travertine floors and solid walnut doors handcrafted in Paris.


**The Dining Room**


Commanding the grand ballroom is[The Dining Room](https://rh.com/us/en/indianapolis/restaurant) **** restaurant. Aglow in natural light from a wall of soaring windows and fluted pilasters, this palatial space features a 27-foot groin-vaulted ceiling, banquettes of Italian wool velvet and a stunning lighting installation by acclaimed Los Angeles-based master glass designer, Alison Berger. An evocation of rain, the piece is composed of 210 pendants, each a drop of blown glass that cascades through the space. With French doors framing mesmerizing views of the lake, guests will enjoy a thoughtfully curated menu of enduring classics featuring the freshest ingredients from select sources, simply and elegantly prepared.


**The Wine Bar**


In the west wing, a luminous Calacatta gold-marble **Wine Bar** serves exceptional Champagnes and wines from around the world for visitors to enjoy in adjacent lounge seating or while exploring the rest of the estate, including limited-production offerings from Napa Valley’s renowned small vintners.


**The East & West Wings**


Artistic lifestyle installations featuring **RH Collections** by internationally acclaimed designers are displayed throughout the east and west wings. And in the east wing, the former billiard room now houses the[RH Interior Design Studio](https://rh.com/us/en/interior-design) **.** This interactive creative space provides clients an unprecedented level of professional design services with a private presentation room, state-of-the-art technology and design libraries showcasing a vast assortment of textiles, furniture and lighting finishes.


**The Lakeside Terrace**


Offering serene views, **The Lakeside Terrace** adjoins the north side of the main house. Influenced by the great classical gardens of Europe, this immersive destination encompasses a series of intimate **RH Outdoor** lounge spaces defined by a layered composition of parasol trees, sculptural evergreens and French limestone fire tables. Here, the central axis that defines the estate opens to radiating steps that gently descend into the lake. **The West Gardens,** with their rhythmic progression of geometric topiaries and chanticleer pear trees, feature additional outdoor collections.


To view the beautifully appointed bedroom suites on the upper level, guests will ascend an elliptical floating stair of stacked St. Germain French limestone that rises nearly 30 feet to culminate in a domed ceiling.


RH Indianapolis is located at 4501 North Michigan Road. Hours of operation are 10am to 9pm Sunday through Saturday.
