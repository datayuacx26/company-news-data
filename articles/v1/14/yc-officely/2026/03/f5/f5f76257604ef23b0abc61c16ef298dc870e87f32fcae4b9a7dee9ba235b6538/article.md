# Skedda vs Officely

## Choosing Your Office's "Operating System": Which Tool is Right for You?


Picking the right **office management software** in 2026 can feel like a huge decision. You're not just buying a tool; you're setting the tone for how your team interacts with the office. You've likely narrowed it down to two big names: Skedda and Officely. Both are powerful platforms, but they're built on fundamentally different ideas about where work actually happens. This isn't just a feature-for-feature comparison—it's about choosing the workflow that fits your company's culture.

## The Core Difference: A Separate App vs. Your Team's Favorite App


This is the most important distinction. The biggest difference between Skedda and Officely isn't a single feature, but their entire philosophy on user experience.


‍


### Skedda's Approach: The Standalone Platform


Skedda is a powerful, traditional web application. It lives in its own browser tab, completely separate from where your team communicates all day. To book a desk, an employee has to:


1. **Stop** what they’re doing in Slack or Teams.


2. **Switch** to another app or browser tab.


3. **Log in** to a different system (and maybe hunt for the password).


4. Navigate a new interface to find and book their spot.


This process creates friction and adds to "tool fatigue." The biggest risk? Low adoption. When a tool is out of sight and out of mind, people simply won't use it, leaving you with inaccurate office data and a wasted investment.


‍


### Officely's Approach: Meet Your Team Where They Already Are


We designed Officely around a simple, powerful idea: the best tools are the ones you don't have to think about. That’s why **Officely works 100% inside Slack and Microsoft Teams.**


Office management shouldn't be a chore. With Officely, booking a desk is as natural as sending a message. There are no new apps to download, no passwords to remember, and zero training required. If your team knows how to use Slack or Teams, they already know how to use Officely. It’s this seamless experience that makes us the perfect solution for[modern hybrid companies](https://getofficely.com/blog/officely-for-hybrid-companies) .

## Feature Face-Off: How Do They Stack Up?


Now that you understand the core difference in philosophy, let's see how their features compare in practice.


‍


### Desk & Meeting Room Booking


- **Officely:** The whole process is designed to be fast, simple, and social, directly from Slack or Teams! The automated daily broadcast will display which of your colleagues are coming in so you can join them with just one click. It transforms booking from a task into an easy way to plan a great day at the office.


- **Skedda:** Skedda offers robust booking capabilities, but to use them, your team has to leave their main communication hub every single time. When you’re choosing a[desk booking software](https://getofficely.com/blog/choose-best-desk-booking-software) , the path of least resistance always wins—and that means staying where the conversation is happening.

### Integrations


- **Officely:** This is our superpower. Officely doesn't just *integrate* with Slack and Teams—it was **built for them** . It’s not a clunky bot that sends you notifications; it’s a full-featured app that lives natively inside the tools your team uses all day, every day.


- **Skedda:** Skedda connects to external tools like Google Calendar, but it remains a separate platform that sends data back and forth. The work of booking and managing space still happens outside of your team's natural workflow. ### Analytics and Office Insights


- **Officely:** You get straightforward analytics that give you a true picture of how your office is being used. Because adoption is so high, the data is actually reliable. You can easily track occupancy trends, identify popular days, and make smarter decisions about your real estate needs.


- **Skedda:** Skedda also provides detailed reports. The catch is that their accuracy depends entirely on whether your team remembers to log in and use the separate app. Incomplete data can lead to poor decisions about your space.

## So, Who Should Use Skedda?


Skedda can be a solid choice for organizations with very complex, granular booking rules, especially if high employee adoption isn't the primary goal. It might be a fit for you if:


- Your company culture **doesn’t revolve around Slack or Microsoft Teams** .


- You need to manage complex rules for specialized, non-desk resources like lab equipment.


- You prefer a standalone system with intricate controls over a seamless user experience.
