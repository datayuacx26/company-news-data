## Key Features to Look for in Your Software


Not all platforms are created equal. When searching for the best office space management software, make sure these non-negotiable features are on your list.


‍


### Seamless Desk and Room Booking


This is the bare minimum. Your team should be able to find and book a desk or meeting room in just a few clicks. The process should feel effortless, not like another chore.


‍


### Slack & Microsoft Teams Integration


**This is the game-changer.** Your team already lives in Slack or Teams. The best software meets them where they are, eliminating the need to learn another app or remember another password. This single feature drives adoption and makes office management a natural part of everyone's workflow, which is essential for any[hybrid company](https://getofficely.com/blog/officely-for-hybrid-companies) .


‍


### Office Usage Analytics & Reporting


Data is your best friend for making informed decisions. Look for a tool that provides clear, actionable insights into peak attendance days, department-level usage, and which desks are most popular. This data is critical for an effective[office space management system](https://getofficely.com/blog/office-space-management-system) .


‍


### Visitor & Parking Management


While not essential for everyone, integrated visitor and parking features can streamline the entire office experience, making it smoother for employees and guests alike.
