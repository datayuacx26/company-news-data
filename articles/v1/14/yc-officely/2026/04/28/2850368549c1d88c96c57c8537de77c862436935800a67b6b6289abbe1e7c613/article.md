# Officely Pricing Compared to Skedda

## Choosing Your Desk Booking Software: Is It All About Price?


When you're on the hunt for the right **desk booking software** , it’s tempting to let the price tag make the final call. But let's be real: the *true cost* of any new tool goes way beyond the invoice. It includes the time spent on training, the headache of managing it, and the risk of paying for something your team simply doesn't use.


This is where the comparison between Officely and Skedda gets interesting. You have Officely—the friendly, powerful platform that plugs directly into Slack and Microsoft Teams. Then you have Skedda—a capable, standalone tool for managing office spaces.


This article will give you a clear, no-nonsense look at not just the price, but the real-world value you get from each platform. Our goal is to help you find the[best desk booking software of 2026](https://getofficely.com/blog/top-desk-booking-software-2025) .


‍


## The Big Difference: Integrated Hub vs. Standalone Tool


Before we get into dollars and cents, let’s talk about the one huge difference that will shape your team's entire experience with your office.


‍


### Officely: Your Office Command Center in Slack & Teams


Officely is built to live where your team already works every single day. There are no new apps to download, no extra logins to remember, and no clunky new workflows to learn. Booking a desk or seeing who's in the office is done in seconds, right from the chat tools they already have open.


This deep integration makes our[hot desk booking system](https://getofficely.com/solutions/desk-booking-software) feel completely effortless. And when a tool is that easy to use, people actually use it. The result is better adoption, which means you get accurate data to make smarter decisions about your workspace.


‍


### Skedda: A Powerful, Separate Booking Platform


Skedda is a dedicated space management tool that operates as a separate application. While it offers ways to connect with calendars, the core booking experience happens on its own platform, pulling your team out of their main communication hub.


The biggest downside of this approach? It forces everyone to stop what they're doing, switch contexts, and navigate a different system. That one extra step, no matter how small, is a known barrier to adoption and can create friction in your hybrid work model. This is a fundamental difference in philosophy compared to an embedded tool like Officely[\[1\]](https://getofficely.com/blog/officely-vs-skedda-comparison) .

## Officely Pricing vs. Skedda Pricing: A Head-to-Head Breakdown


Okay, let's talk numbers. Here’s how the pricing models stack up and what you actually get for your budget.


‍

We believe pricing should be transparent and easy. Officely's model is refreshingly straightforward: you pay based on the number of users you have. That’s it. This keeps your costs[Simple, Predictable](https://getofficely.com/blog/officely-annual-subscription-pricing) and makes budgeting a breeze as your company grows.


**What you get with Officely's pricing:**


- **A generous free plan:** We offer a robust[free plan for small teams](https://getofficely.com/blog/officely-pricing-for-small-business) so you can get started and see the benefits immediately, with no credit card required.


- **Truly all-inclusive plans:** Our paid plans give you the whole package. You get desk booking, hybrid work scheduling, office usage analytics, and policy management—all included. We don’t gatekeep essential features.


- **No hidden costs:** Your price is based on people, not assets. Add more desks, meeting rooms, or parking spots without worrying about surprise fees on your next bill.


‍


### Skedda's Pricing: A More Complex, à la Carte Approach


Skedda’s pricing is typically based on the number of "spaces" you manage and the specific features you need. This is a common approach for many standalone booking systems, and it's always wise to explore a few alternatives to understand the market[\[2\] \[2\]](https://archieapp.co/blog/skedda-alternatives-pricing-reviews) .


**What to watch for with this pricing model:**


- **Base cost vs. total cost:** A starting plan might seem affordable, but the price can climb quickly as you add more spaces or realise you need "core" features like single sign-on (SSO), which are often reserved for higher-priced plans[\[3\]](https://www.uctoday.com/unified-communications/officely-review-the-streamlined-desk-booking-app) .


- **Budgeting challenges:** With this à la carte model, it can be difficult to predict your final costs. Your initial budget can easily expand as your office needs change or your company scales.

### The Skedda Consideration: For Complex, Top-Down Management


Skedda is a powerful tool, particularly for organizations that need highly specific, complex scheduling rules driven by a central administrator.
