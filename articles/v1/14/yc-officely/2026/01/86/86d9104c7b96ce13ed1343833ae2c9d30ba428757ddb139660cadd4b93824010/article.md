# Why Officely Beats Envoy for Slack-Based Desk Booking

Envoy is a well-known name in office management, and for many things, it's a great product. But if your team lives and breathes in Slack, you've likely run into its primary weakness: it lives in a separate web app, creating friction and pulling employees out of the tools they use all day. For a truly seamless, Slack-based desk booking experience, you need a tool that was built to live *natively* within Slack from day one.


This is where Officely shines. Built from the ground up for Slack, Officely eliminates the friction that causes employees to abandon "yet another app." It’s no wonder so many companies that run on Slack are now searching for **Envoy alternatives** that can deliver an experience their teams will actually embrace.


## The Problem: Why Envoy's Slack Integration Isn't a True Solution


Simply having an **Envoy Slack integration** doesn't automatically create a smooth workflow. Envoy’s integration feels like a feature that was bolted on later, rather than a core part of its design. It connects to Slack, but it doesn't truly live there.


‍


### A Clunky, Command-Based Experience


Interacting with Envoy inside Slack requires your employees to learn and remember specific slash commands. For example, to book a desk or check in, they may need to type /envoy-desk or /envoy-in into the chat box[\[1\]](https://envoy.help/en/articles/7947825-using-slack-with-envoy) . In a world of intuitive, visual, and click-based apps, this command-line interface feels dated and creates a learning curve—a significant barrier that often discourages adoption from the start.


‍


### The "Yet Another App" Syndrome


Even with the integration, your team will find that full functionality often requires them to switch over to Envoy's separate web or mobile app. This constant context-switching is a major point of friction. As we point out when comparing Officely vs Envoy, Envoy makes your employees download yet another app, which they won't use. This friction is a primary driver of low engagement with workplace tools.


‍


### Missing the "Why": A Lack of Social Context


Envoy's roots are in visitor management, and its employee tools reflect that. The Slack integration focuses on individual actions like checking in or viewing your own booking. What's critically missing is the social element: the ability to easily see which of your colleagues are planning to be in the office. This missing social context is what actually drives office attendance, a detail that the Envoy Slack integration overlooks.


‍


## The Officely Advantage: A Truly Native Slack Experience


Officely was built on a simple but powerful idea: your office management tool should live where your team already works. We designed Officely specifically for hybrid companies that use Slack as their digital headquarters.


‍


### Live and Work Directly in Slack


With Officely, everything happens 100% inside Slack. There are no new apps to download, no separate logins to remember, and no new interfaces to learn. Booking a desk, checking who's in, and managing office days feels like a natural part of the Slack workflow. This truly native design is the secret to achieving high employee adoption and making office coordination effortless.


‍


### Smart Features That Actually Boost Attendance


Officely goes beyond simple booking. Our "Who's In" feature gives everyone visibility into office plans, encouraging teammates to come in on the same days. Smart recommendations even suggest the best days for you to go in based on when your colleagues will be there.


We also make it easy to foster community by automatically starting a daily chat for everyone who's in the office. These features transform the office from just a place to work into a destination for collaboration and connection.


‍


### Simplicity and Fair Pricing


Unlike Envoy, which expanded into employee tools from guest management, Officely was purpose-built for hybrid work. Our focus is clear, and so is our pricing. We offer flexible, month-to-month contracts, a stark contrast to Envoy's typical annual lock-ins. You get straightforward, cost-effective pricing without paying for complex features your team doesn't need.
