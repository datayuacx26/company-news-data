## Questions? We're Here to Help.


### What is desk booking software?


Desk booking software, also known as hot desking or desk hoteling, is a tool that allows employees to reserve a workspace—like a desk, office, or cubicle—for a specific time. It's essential for managing flexible and hybrid offices, as it helps companies optimize their space while giving employees the freedom to choose where and when they work.


‍


### How does a desk booking system support hybrid work?


In a hybrid model, not everyone is in the office every day. A desk booking system provides clarity and organization. It allows employees to see who is in the office and book a spot nearby, which fosters collaboration. For managers, it provides data on office usage, which is critical for making smart decisions about real estate. While a tool like Officely is powerful, a successful hybrid strategy also requires clear communication and policies; the software supports the culture you build[\[2\]](https://hbr.org/2025/07/hybrid-still-isnt-working?ab=HP-latest-text-6?ref=nodesk) .
