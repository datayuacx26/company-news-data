# How to Manage a Hybrid Workplace Using Officely & Slack

Managing a hybrid workplace can feel like herding cats. You’re juggling who's coming in, trying to avoid the dreaded "empty office" days, and drowning in endless back-and-forth messages. The chaos is real, but the solution is simpler than you think.


The secret to a thriving hybrid model isn't more spreadsheets or endless polls; it's using smart, integrated tools that meet your team where they already work—in Slack. With over 90% of organizations now operating on a hybrid model, getting this right isn't just a nice-to-have, it's essential for success[\[1\]](https://www.workhuman.com/blog/managing-a-hybrid-team) . This guide will show you, step-by-step, how to use Officely and Slack to effortlessly **manage your hybrid workplace** , implement hot desking, and bring your team back together.

## The Hybrid Work Challenge: Why Spreadsheets and Manual Check-Ins Fail


Remember the old way of doing things? That clunky spreadsheet for desk booking, the shared calendar nobody updated, or the daily "Who's in?" poll on Slack. These manual methods don't just create administrative headaches; they actively undermine the hybrid experience.


They lead to:


- **Wasted Commutes:** Employees travel to the office expecting to collaborate, only to find it empty.
- **Expensive, Empty Space:** You're paying for real estate that sits unused, draining your budget.
- **Disconnected Teams:** Without a way to coordinate, spontaneous chats, brainstorming sessions, and team culture wither away.


In 2026, flexibility isn't a perk—it's an expectation. A staggering 87% of the U.S. workforce opts for flexible work arrangements when available[\[4\]](https://www.dialpad.com/blog/hybrid-work-stats) . To meet this demand without the chaos, you need a dedicated, integrated tool designed for the modern workplace.
