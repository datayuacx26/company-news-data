# Seacoast Bank Named Among Inc. Magazine’s Best Workplaces of 2025

**STUART, Fla.** -- Seacoast Bank, the banking subsidiary of Seacoast Banking Corporation of Florida (NASDAQ: SBCF) and one of the largest community banks in Florida, has been named one of Inc. Magazine’s Best Workplaces for 2025, a prestigious recognition honoring companies that excel in creating exceptional workplace cultures. This is Seacoast’s first time earning a spot on the highly competitive list, which evaluates thousands of companies each year.


To determine the list, Inc. conducts a rigorous selection process that includes a detailed benefits review, anonymous third-party employee surveys, and a comprehensive background check by the publication’s research team. Companies must demonstrate a high level of employee satisfaction and integrity across all areas of operation to be selected.


This latest recognition builds on a growing list of accolades earned by Seacoast in recent years for its associate-first culture and positive workplace environment.


## 2024 – 2025 Associate Engagement Awards:


- 2025 Inc. Best Workplaces – first year
- 2025 South Florida Business Journal Best Places to Work – third consecutive year
- 2025 America’s Best Workplaces by Best Companies Group – first year
- 2024 American Banker Best Banks to Work For – fifth consecutive year
- 2024 Fortune Best Workplaces for Women™ – second consecutive year
- 2024 Fortune Best Workplaces in Financial Services & Insurance™ – first year
- 2024 Orlando Business Journal Best Places to Work – fourth year
- 2024 Great Place to Work® Certified – second consecutive year
- 2024 Fortune Best Workplaces for Millennials™ – first year


This honor reflects Seacoast’s commitment to fostering a workplace culture where associates feel valued, supported, and empowered to grow.
