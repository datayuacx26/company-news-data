# Seacoast Ranks Among Best Workplaces by South Florida Business Journal

**STUART, Fla.** -- Seacoast Bank has earned the distinction of being named to the prestigious “Best Places to Work” list by the South Florida Business Journal for 2025, demonstrating its dedication to creating a workplace environment where employees feel valued, supported and empowered to succeed. This marks the third consecutive year the bank has earned this recognition since entering the Miami-Dade market and deepening its investment in South Florida.


Seacoast Bank was honored on the publication’s annual list, which celebrates organizations that exemplify excellence in fostering workplace culture, employee engagement and job satisfaction. Seacoast’s placement highlights its ongoing commitment to providing employees with meaningful opportunities for professional growth, collaboration and community impact.


“This recognition is a reflection of the incredible culture and team we’ve built at Seacoast,” said Chuck Shaffer, Chairman and CEO of Seacoast Bank. “Our people are the heart of our organization, and we’re deeply committed to nurturing an environment where everyone can thrive and contribute to our shared success.”


Seacoast Bank prioritizes its employees’ well-being, offering robust professional development programs and a collaborative culture. These values remain integral to Seacoast’s vision of fostering a thriving team that delivers exceptional service to its customers and communities.
