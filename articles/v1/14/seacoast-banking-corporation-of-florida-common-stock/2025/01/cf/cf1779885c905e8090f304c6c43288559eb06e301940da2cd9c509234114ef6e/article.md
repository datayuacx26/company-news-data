# Seacoast Bank Names Jeff Semonovich as EVP, Chief Information Officer

**STUART, Fla.** -- Seacoast Bank, the banking subsidiary of Seacoast Banking Corporation of Florida (NASDAQ: SBCF) and one of the largest community banks in Florida, is pleased to announce that Jeff Semonovich has joined as Executive Vice President and Chief Information Officer. Semonovich will oversee the bank’s technology vision and strategy, driving innovation to enhance the client experience and operational efficiency.


With over 30 years of experience in technology leadership, Semonovich has spearheaded transformative initiatives in cybersecurity, data management, and digital innovation. At Western Alliance Bank, he modernized technology infrastructure to support business growth. Earlier in his career, at KeyBank, he implemented cutting-edge payment platforms and data solutions that positioned the bank as a leader in commercial banking technology.


"Jeff’s expertise in aligning technology with business objectives will be pivotal as we continue to enhance Seacoast Bank’s capabilities," said Julie Kleffel, EVP and Chief Operating Officer. "His passion for technology and exceptional track record leading high performing teams uniquely positions him to lead strategic change."


Semonovich’s strategic leadership will play a key role in Seacoast Bank’s digital transformation, ensuring that the bank delivers innovative solutions while maintaining the highest standards of security and reliability.
