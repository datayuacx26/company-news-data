# Seacoast Ranks Among America’s Best Workplaces by Best Companies Group

**STUART, Fla.** -- Seacoast Bank, one of the largest community banks in Florida, has been recognized among the recipients of the 2025 America’s Best Workplaces Award by Best Companies Group, in partnership with BridgeTower Media. This prestigious honor reflects Seacoast Bank’s unwavering commitment to fostering an inclusive, dynamic workplace where employees feel valued, engaged, and empowered to grow.


Seacoast Bank joins an elite group of organizations recognized for their exceptional workplace culture. The award celebrates companies that lead the nation in employee satisfaction and excellence in workplace practices across small, medium, and large business categories.


“Being named one of America’s Best Workplaces is a testament to the incredible culture our team has built,” said Chuck Shaffer, Chairman and CEO of Seacoast Bank. “At Seacoast, our people are the foundation of our success, and we’re deeply committed to creating a rewarding environment where every employee can thrive and contribute meaningfully to our mission.”


The award stems from a rigorous evaluation by Best Companies Group, which combined insights from workplace policy reviews and an employee survey. The proprietary analysis assessed key areas such as workplace culture, employee satisfaction, and organizational practices to determine the top-ranking companies.[Best Companies Group](https://bestcompaniesgroup.com/) is an independent research firm that since 2004 has specialized in identifying and recognizing great employers to work for.


“Our recognition as one of America’s Best Workplaces is only possible thanks to the passion and dedication of our team,” said Dan Chappell, Seacoast’s Chief Human Resources Officer. “We work hard to provide meaningful career opportunities and a collaborative environment that supports both personal and professional growth, and we’re proud that these efforts are being recognized in such a meaningful way.”


Seacoast Bank remains committed to fostering a workplace where employees are empowered to succeed and make a positive impact in the communities they serve.
