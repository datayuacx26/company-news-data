# Seacoast Bank Named a Best Community Bank

**STUART, Fla.** -- Seacoast Bank, one of the largest community banks in Florida, has proudly secured a top-three spot in the "Best Community Bank" category of the Daily Business Review's 2024 "Best Of" readers survey. This recognition by Florida attorneys underscores Seacoast’s steadfast dedication to delivering integrity-driven, personalized financial services across Florida communities.


The Daily Business Review’s “Best Of” survey highlights top companies across industries based on input from legal professionals and decision-makers in Florida’s legal and business sectors. Readers provide feedback that determines rankings in categories such as law, finance, real estate, and community services.


“We’re incredibly honored to be recognized by the talented legal professionals who read the Daily Business Review,” said Chuck Shaffer, Chairman and CEO of Seacoast Bank. “We understand the unique demands of the legal community and take great pride in providing personalized banking solutions that meet their needs. This award reflects our commitment to reliability and trust, values critical to our customers.”


The recognition for Seacoast Bank, the banking subsidiary of Seacoast Banking Corporation of Florida (NASDAQ: SBCF), by the Daily Business Review adds to the list of its recent accolades, including the Great Place To Work® Certification™ for 2024, with 91% of employees affirming it as an outstanding workplace. The bank was also recognized by American Banker as one of the Best Banks to Work For in 2024, highlighting Seacoast’s dedication to a positive workplace culture.


“Our goal is to meet the unique needs of our community partners, and being acknowledged by our customers, especially those in the legal field, is incredibly meaningful,” said Chris Rolle, Executive Vice President of Commercial Banking at Seacoast Bank. “This recognition reinforces our commitment to providing tailored financial solutions that make a difference.”
