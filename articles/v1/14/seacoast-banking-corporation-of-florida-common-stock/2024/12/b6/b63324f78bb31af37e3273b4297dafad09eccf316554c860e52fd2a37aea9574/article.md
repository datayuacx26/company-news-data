# Seacoast Bank Recognized by United Way Martin County

**STUART, Fla.** -- Seacoast Bank is proud to announce its recognition as one of the United Way of Martin County’s Top 10 Most Generous Workplaces for the seventh consecutive year. This year, Seacoast has secured the number two spot, just behind Publix Supermarkets, Inc., highlighting the bank’s unwavering commitment to community support and charitable giving.


Seacoast Bank’s associates are dedicated to making a difference in the lives of customers, neighbors, colleagues, friends, and communities. Through financial contributions and volunteer hours, they exemplify Seacoast’s Fourth Promise, demonstrating the bank’s core values in action.


“Our associates are the heart of Seacoast Bank, and their commitment to giving back is truly inspiring,” said Chuck Shaffer, Chairman and CEO of Seacoast Bank. “Being recognized by the United Way of Martin County is a testament to the collective impact our team has on the communities we serve.”


In addition to this recognition, Chuck Shaffer has been appointed as the Campaign Chair for the United Way of Martin County’s 2024-2025 season. With nearly a decade of service as a United Way board member, Shaffer brings extensive experience and dedication to this leadership role. The United Way has expressed gratitude for his commitment and expertise, which will further enhance collaboration with businesses and community outcomes.


Seacoast Bank’s recognition and leadership in community initiatives underscore its dedication to fostering a culture of generosity and support. The bank remains committed to making a positive impact through continuous engagement and support for non-profit agencies throughout the year.
