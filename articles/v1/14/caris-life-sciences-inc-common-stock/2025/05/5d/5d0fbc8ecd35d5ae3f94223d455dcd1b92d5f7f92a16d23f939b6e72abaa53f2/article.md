# Caris Molecular Tumor Board Report


## From data to decision — redefining tumor board intelligence.


The Caris Molecular Tumor Board Report is an innovative research-use-only (RUO) tumor profiling report that builds on the comprehensive foundation of the Caris Molecular Profiling Report to offer even deeper insights into tumor biology. The Molecular Tumor Board Report enhances the industry-leading capabilities of the standard Caris report, presenting cutting-edge data from[Caris molecular profiling](https://carislifesciences.com/physicians/physician-tests/mi-profile/) in more insightful ways to build the complete molecular blueprint of a patient’s cancer.


## Tailored Insights for Every Tumor


The Caris Molecular Tumor Board Report embodies the future of precision oncology. Data from Caris’ AI-enhanced comprehensive molecular profiling is displayed in innovative, easy-to-interpret modular graphs and tables. In addition to pan-tumor reporting modules, the report also features tumor-specific options from its extensive menu.

## Visualization Tools for Molecular Data


#### Pan-Tumor Report Modules Include:


- Next Generation Sequencing (NGS) Metrics
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) Variants
- Enhanced eKaryotype
- Exon-Level Copy Number Alterations
- Relative RNA Expression Heatmap
- Visualization of RNA Fusions
- Immune Cell Deconvolution


*Visualization of RNA fusions detected using whole transcriptome sequencing*


#### Tumor-Specific Report Modules Include:


- CMS Subtype (colorectal cancer)
- PAM50 (breast cancer)
- purIST (pancreatic cancer)
- MGMTai (glioblastoma and
MGMTmet-positive cancers)
- COSMIC Signatures (various cancers)


**MGMTai prediction of temozolomide treatment benefit in glioblastoma.**


## When Every Insight Counts


Available upon request, the Caris Molecular Tumor Board Report is a powerful tool for molecular tumor boards, case consults, and clinical trial decision-making. The AI-driven insights provided by the Caris Molecular Tumor Board Report can also be leveraged to confirm diagnoses and examine specific genomic features, such as fusions and molecular signatures.
