# Caris Nanoverse


## Exploring the Biological Nanoverse to Better Detect, Categorize and Inform Healthcare


Through the power of AI and vast amounts of molecular data, Caris has developed a revolutionary mechanism to unravel the biological complexity of diseases. The Caris Nanoverse™ provides physicians and researchers a new way to identify patient populations, predict therapy responses and identify targets for drug development in ways never before possible.


## Informing a Molecular Revolution in Medicine


Nestled deep in our molecular coding is a universe of biological networks that provide the source of life. From how our cells grow, divide and even die, this biological web holds the keys to better detect, categorize and inform healthcare in an entirely new way. Discover how Caris is pushing the boundaries of what is possible and leading a molecular revolution in medicine.
