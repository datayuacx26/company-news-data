## **A Closer Look at the Male Annual Physical Exam Process**


A complete male physical blends evidence-based testing with honest discussions about lifestyle and habits. It’s not just a checklist. It’s a personalized approach to maintaining good health and detecting early issues.


Here’s what your provider typically reviews:


- **Vitals:** Blood pressure, heart rate, height, and weight to establish your baseline data.
- **Blood Work:** A cholesterol screening, fasting glucose, and blood test panels help assess metabolic and cardiovascular health.
- **Screenings:** Age and risk-appropriate cancer screenings, sexually transmitted infections (STIs) screenings, and review of recommendations for annual vision and dental checks identify issues in the early stages.
- **Immunizations:** Stay up to date on your vaccines to protect against preventable diseases.
- **Lifestyle Review:** Discuss exercise, nutrition, alcohol use, sleep, and stress management.

## **Men’s Health Checklist by Age: Annual Exams and Screenings You Need**


Men’s health changes with every decade, and each age group brings new priorities for prevention. This annual physical exam checklist shows what to prioritize and when to discuss screening options with your provider.


### **In Your 20s — Build Strong Foundations**


Your 20s are about building healthy routines that last a lifetime.


- Schedule your first comprehensive physical exam to establish a relationship with a primary care provider.
- Get baseline blood pressure, cholesterol, and STI screenings.
- Learn about testicular exams and stay aware of potential testicular cancer symptoms.
- Prioritize consistent exercise, proper nutrition, and sleep.


**Circle Insight:** Early checkups create a foundation your provider can use to track your progress across decades.


### **In Your 30s — Manage Stress and Maintain Balance**


Your 30s often bring more stress and increased risk for chronic health conditions.


- Reassess cholesterol and glucose levels through routine blood tests.
- Monitor BMI trends and health risk factors such as high blood pressure.
- Discuss fertility, family planning, and mental health management.
- Maintain routine dental exams and health screenings.


**Circle Insight:** Circle Medical’s healthcare professionals make sensitive topics like testosterone and burnout easier to address privately.


### **In Your 40s — Watch the Numbers Closely**


Your 40s are a turning point for men’s health metrics.


- Track blood pressure, cholesterol, and glucose annually.
- Begin colon cancer screening or colorectal screening at 45—or screening earlier if you have a family history.
- Prostate cancer screening and prostate exams start in your 40s if you are considered at higher risk
- Reassess mental health and physical activity.


### **In Your 50s — Prioritize Preventive Screenings**


This decade is about staying proactive with routine screenings.


- Continue colon cancer or colorectal cancer screenings.
- Start prostate cancer screening if you are at regular risk.
- Discuss heart disease risk and lung cancer screening if you have a smoking history.
- Keep vaccines up to date for flu, shingles, and pneumonia.

### **In Your 60s and Beyond — Focus on Longevity and Quality of Life**


Your 60s and beyond focus on independence and overall health.


- Continue prostate cancer and cardiovascular assessments.
- Evaluate mobility, balance, and chronic diseases such as diabetes or arthritis.
- Review medications with your primary care physician for safety.
- Discuss long-term wellness and advance care plans.
