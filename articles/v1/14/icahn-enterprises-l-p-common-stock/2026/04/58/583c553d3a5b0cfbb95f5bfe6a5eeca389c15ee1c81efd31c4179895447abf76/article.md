# Icahn Enterprises L.P. Announces Q1 2026 Earnings Conference Call

SUNNY ISLES BEACH, Fla.


, April 28, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Icahn Enterprises L.P. (Nasdaq:IEP) announced today that it will discuss its first quarter 2026 results on a webcast on Wednesday, May 6, 2026 - 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time. To access the webcast, viewers should go to this link ([webcast](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4675103-1&h=2111832432&u=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.media-server.com%2Fmmc%2Fp%2Frpj4afq9&a=webcast) ). We encourage viewers to access the webcast 15 minutes ahead of the scheduled start time. A replay of the webcast will also be available for at least twelve months at[Icahn events and presentations](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4675103-1&h=1497992420&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ielp.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents-presentations&a=Icahn+events+and+presentations) .


Icahn Enterprises L.P., a master limited partnership, is a diversified holding company engaged in seven primary business segments: Investment, Energy, Automotive, Food Packaging, Real Estate, Home Fashion and Pharma.
