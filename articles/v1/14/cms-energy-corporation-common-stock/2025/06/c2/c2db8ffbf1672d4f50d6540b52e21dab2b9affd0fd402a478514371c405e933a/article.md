# Visit these Michigan Farmers Markets this Summer

# Visit these Michigan Farmers Markets this Summer


Posted by[Jodie Solari](https://force4michigan.com/author/jlsolari/) | Jun 11, 2025


|[Empowering Communities](https://force4michigan.com/category/empowering-communities/) |[0](https://force4michigan.com/visit-these-11-michigan-farmers-markets-this-summer/#comments) |


Summertime in Michigan means the start of Farmers Markets! Shopping at Farmers Markets is a win win-win for farmers, shoppers and communities; farmers receive more of the food dollars, shoppers receive a huge selection of the freshest food from their area and local economies flourish. Fresh fruits and vegetables are a given at farmers market but depending on the location and time of year there can be a variety of additional products for sale like baked goods, flowers and local crafts.


We know that supporting Farmers Markets means supporting local communities across the state; in the last 13 years we have supported Farmers Markets across Michigan with grants totaling more than $600,000. If you’re looking for a new farmers market to check out this summer, try one of our previous grant recipients.


**Lapeer Farmers Creek Market**


Location: 272 N. Court St. Lapeer, MI 48446


Hours: Wednesdays & Saturdays May – Oct. 9 a.m. – 3 p.m.


Website:[http://www.historicfarmersmarketoflapeer.com/](http://www.historicfarmersmarketoflapeer.com/)


**Kalamazoo Farmers Market**


Location: 1204 Bank Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49001


Hours: Saturdays starting May 4, 7 a.m. – 1 p.m.; Tuesdays & Thursdays (starting in June) 8 a.m. – 1 p.m.


Website:[Kalamazoo Farmers Market — PFC Markets](http://pfcmarkets.com/all-markets/kalamazoo-farmers-market/)


**Bronson Farmers Market**


Location: 106 E. Chicago St., Bronson, MI 49028


Hours: Saturdays starting first Saturday in June, 8 a.m. – 12 p.m.


Website:[CITY OF BRONSON (bronson-mi.com)](https://www.bronson-mi.com/residents/farmers_market.php)


**Hudsonville Terra Square Farmers Market**


Location: 3380 Chicago Dr., Hudsonville, MI 49426


Hours: Wednesdays, June – September, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.


Website:[Hudsonville Michigan Farmers Market \[official\] | Terra Square](https://terrasquare.com/farmers-market/)


**SVRC Marketplace (Saginaw)***


Location: 203 S Washington Ave., Saginaw, MI 48607


Hours: Year-round Wednesday – Saturday 11 a.m. – 7 p.m.


Website:[SVRC Marketplace](https://www.svrcmarketplace.com/)


**Frankenmuth Farmers Market**


Location: 534 N. Main Street, Frankenmuth, MI 48734


Hours: Saturdays Mid-May – Mid-October 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.


Website:[Frankenmuth Farmers Market | Keeping it Fresh Since 2005](https://www.facebook.com/frankenmuthmarket/)


**Muskegon Downtown Farmers Market**


Location: 242 W Western Ave, Muskegon, MI 49440


Hours: Tuesdays, Thursdays, Saturdays 8 a.m. – 2 p.m. May through November


Website:[Homepage (muskegonfarmersmarket.com)](https://www.muskegonfarmersmarket.com/)


**Grand Rapids Downtown Market***


Location: 435 Ionia Ave SW, Grand Rapids, MI 49503


Hours: Year-round Monday – Thursday 11 a.m. – 7 p.m.; Friday 11 a.m. – 8 p.m.; Saturday 10 a.m. – 8 p.m.; Sunday 10 a.m. – 7 p.m.


Website:[Grand Rapids Downtown Market (downtownmarketgr.com)](http://downtownmarketgr.com/)


**Fulton Street Farmers Market (Grand Rapids)**


Location: 1145 Fulton St. E Grand Rapids, MI 49503


Hours: Main season: May 7 – October 29 Wednesday, Friday, Saturday 8 a.m. – 2 p.m.; Second Season – Saturdays: 10 a.m. – 1 p.m.


Website:[Home (fultonstreetmarket.org)](https://www.fultonstreetmarket.org/)


**Allen Farmer’s Market (Lansing)***


Location: 1629 E Kalamazoo St. Lansing, MI 48912


Hours: Wednesdays, 3 – 6:30 p.m.


Website:[https://allenneighborhoodcenter.org/market/](https://allenneighborhoodcenter.org/market/)


**Spring Lake Farmers Market**


Location: Tanglefoot Park – 312 W Exchange St, Spring Lake, MI 49456


Hours: June 6 – October Thursday 9am – 2pm


Website:[https://springlakevillage.org/community/farmers-market/](https://springlakevillage.org/community/farmers-market/)


*These markets are year-round and have a variety of vendors selling food and other goods.


If the above locations are too far from where you live, check out this[interactive map](https://mifma.org/find-a-farmers-market/) to locate all the Farmers Markets across Michigan.
