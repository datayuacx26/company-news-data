# Topgolf Callaway Brands Officially Changes its Name Back to Callaway Golf Company, Reinforcing Pure-Play Golf Focus

CARLSBAD, Calif.


, Jan. 16, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Callaway Golf Company (the "Company", "we," "our," "us") (NYSE: CALY) formerly Topgolf Callaway Brands Corp., today announced the completion of its corporate rebrand and name change, reflecting the Company's reaffirming its position as a leading pure-play golf business.


Effective today, the Company will trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the new ticker symbol "CALY." The Company has also launched a new investor relations website at[ir.callawaygolf.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4597276-1&h=3647691156&u=https%3A%2F%2Fir.callawaygolf.com%2F&a=ir.callawaygolf.com) , serving as the primary source for financial information and investor updates.


Under the Callaway Golf Company name, the Company will continue to operate its portfolio of golf brands, including Callaway, Odyssey, TravisMathew, and OGIO.


For more information, please visit[ir.callawaygolf.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4597276-1&h=3647691156&u=https%3A%2F%2Fir.callawaygolf.com%2F&a=ir.callawaygolf.com) .


**About Callaway Golf Company (NYSE: CALY)** Callaway Golf Company (NYSE: CALY), is a premium golf equipment, gear and apparel company with a portfolio of global brands, including Callaway Golf, Odyssey, TravisMathew, and OGIO. Through an unwavering commitment to innovation and premium craftsmanship, Callaway designs, manufactures, and sells high-performance golf clubs, golf balls, apparel, bags, and other accessories—setting the standard for performance in the game of golf.
