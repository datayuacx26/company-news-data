### 2. Understand the Key Players Involved:


There are **three key players** in the card issuing ecosystem in Zambia, or anywhere in Africa. These are:


**Issuing Banks:**


This refers to the licensed financial institution that issues cards to the cardholder. In Zambia, these are the only institutions licensed and authorized to issue payment cards to individuals or businesses.


In some cases, they act as BIN sponsors to fintech institutions, allowing them to issue cards under their license.


Here, they handled settlement, BIN, compliance, etc.


**Acquiring Banks:**


These are the merchant’s banks. They accept and process card payment transactions on behalf of businesses.


For example, when a merchant uses a POS terminal to collect a card payment from a customer, **the acquiring bank** processes that transaction and ensures the funds are settled into the merchant’s account.


**Card Networks:**


This set of players set the rules, standards, and infrastructure that connect banks globally. They facilitate communication between the issuing and acquiring banks to ensure proper authorization and execution of transactions.


Examples are Visa, Mastercard, American Express, etc.


Other key players are **cardholders** (the end-customer or business using the card), **payment processors** (the company that handles the technical side of the transaction), and more.

### 3. Know Zambia’s Financial Regulations:


To issue cards, here is a non-exhaustive list of regulations that you must be aware of and comply with.


- Banking and Financial Services Act, 2017 (Act No. 7 of 2017)
- Bank of Zambia Act, 2022
- National Payment Systems Act, 2007
- Directives on Electronic Money Issuance 2018
- Financial Intelligence Centre Act


**Note** : It’s always advisable to consult a legal counsel to guide you through these regulations. However, you can significantly reduce the workload by partnering with a licensed[card-issuing company in Zambia](https://www.miden.co/) that already meets regulatory requirements.
