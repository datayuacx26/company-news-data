## What is a Virtual Card API?


A virtual card API is an Application Programming Interface (API) that lets developers integrate the functionality of creating and managing virtual cards into their applications.


## What Are Virtual USD Cards?


Virtual USD cards are digital-only payment cards issued in US dollars. They work just like regular cards — but you create them online, set your own rules, and use them immediately for global payments.


They’re especially useful for businesses in Africa that:


- Run remote teams and pay for global SaaS tools
- Spend on international ad platforms (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn)
- Need better control over team or departmental expenses
- Want to avoid the hassle of blocked local cards


## Why Virtual Cards Work Better for Business Payments


Here’s what makes virtual cards more practical than using one company debit card for everything:


- ✅ **Global Acceptance:** They’re more likely to work on platforms that reject Naira or local currency cards.
- ✅ **More Control:** You can set custom spending limits, expiry dates, or even restrict usage by merchant.
- ✅ **Real-Time Tracking:** Know who’s spending what — and where — as it happens.
- ✅ **Easy Reconciliation:** Attach receipts, export reports, and simplify audits.
