## What Makes a Good Card API


Here is a list of things to look out for when choosing the right card API for your fintech program:


### 1. Virtual vs. Physical Card Support:


What card format are you looking forward to using? While physical cards are used for offline and POS experiences, virtual cards are known for their durability and sustainability for in-app purchases, BNPL, gig payout systems, and more.


### 2. Multi-Currency Support:


Whether you’re targeting cross-border payment authorizations, fixed on regional scalability, multi-currency issuance is an essential factor when choosing a good card API for a fintech program.


Your chosen provider should be able to offer local currency support for TZS and other major trade currencies, such as USD.


### 3. Reconciliation & Settlement Support:


Behind every card swipe is a complex trail of funds movement. Top providers offer clear, real-time reconciliation dashboards, downloadable reports, and APIs that let your finance team stay in sync without endless spreadsheets.


### 4. Regulatory Compliance:


A good card API should support Tanzania regulatory requirements, such as the Bank of Tanzania (BoT) regulations, NDPC data protection rules, and PCI-DSS for[cardholder data security](http://www.miden.co/) .
