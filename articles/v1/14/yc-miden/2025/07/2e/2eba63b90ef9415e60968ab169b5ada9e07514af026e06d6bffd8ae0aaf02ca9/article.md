Launching a card program in Kenya can feel like a maze. Between navigating regulations, finding the right issuing partner, and incorporating preferred services, it’s easy to get stuck before you even launch.

## Common Challenges Businesses Face When Launching a Card Program in Kenya


Many financial and non-financial institutions in Kenya often face the following challenges when launching a card program:


### 1. Complying with Local Regulations:


Kenya has an evolving but complex regulatory landscape. Businesses must align with Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) guidelines, ensure PCI-DSS compliance, and sometimes deal with data protection rules (like Kenya’s Data Protection Act). Without the right guidance, this can delay launch or cause costly missteps.


### 2. Finding the Right Partner:


Many businesses struggle to find a reliable[card-issuing partne](https://www.miden.co/) r that can support local needs, such as KES settlement or domestic BIN sponsorship. Some providers offer global solutions that don’t fit local infrastructure, leaving businesses stuck with limited control or poor customer experience.


### 3. Lack of Developer-Friendly APIs:


Not all card APIs are built with developers in mind. Inconsistent documentation, poor sandbox environments, or a lack of test data can significantly slow down integration. Product teams often spend more time decoding the API than actually building their solution.


### 4. Limited Visibility and Reporting:


Without real-time insights into card transactions, authorizations, and failed payments, businesses can’t manage user experience or financial performance effectively. Many legacy systems don’t offer transparent dashboards or programmatic access to key data.


### 5. Integration Failure:


When launching a card program, businesses are typically required to connect the fragments of multiple services, such as KYC, fraud tools, reporting dashboards, etc. However, without a streamlined partner or platform, teams can quickly feel overwhelmed trying to connect all the moving parts.
