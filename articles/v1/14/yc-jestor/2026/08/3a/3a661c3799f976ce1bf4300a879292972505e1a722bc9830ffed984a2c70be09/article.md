# How to identify priority processes to automate first

**Priority processes to automate** are usually the ones consuming the most team time, generating the most errors, and most affecting the end customer when they go wrong — in that order. Jestor's **dashboard** and **BPMS** help you see these three criteria with data, not guesswork.


### How Do You Prioritize Which Process to Automate First?


- Ask which process consumes the most manual hours from the team each week.
- Check which process generates the most rework or error complaints.
- Assess which process, if it stalls, directly affects the customer or revenue.
- Check which process already has organized data, making for a quicker automation.


### Why Is Automating the Wrong Process First a Common Mistake?


Teams tend to automate whatever is easiest to configure, not what generates the most impact. That creates a sense of progress without actually solving the bottleneck that hurts the operation the most.


### How Does Jestor Help Identify These Priorities?


- The **BPMS** organizes each process's data into phases, making it easier to see where time is being spent.
- **Chat with Data** lets you ask, in natural language, which process has the most cards stuck or the longest phase time.
- The **dashboard** consolidates metrics from multiple processes, making it visible which one weighs most on the operation.


Criteria Question to ask


**Time consumed** How many manual hours does this process take per week?


**Error frequency** Does this process generate rework often?


**Customer impact** Does a delay here directly affect the customer?


### Frequently Asked Questions


### Should I Automate the Easiest Process or the Most Impactful One First?


The most impactful one usually brings more results, even if it takes more effort to configure initially.


### Does Chat With Data Help Prioritize Processes?


Yes, it lets you ask in natural language where the biggest bottlenecks are, with no manual report needed.


### Do I Need a Consultant to Do This Prioritization?


Not necessarily — with data in the dashboard, the team itself can identify the priorities.
