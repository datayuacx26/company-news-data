# Common mistakes when automating internal processes and how to avoid them

Among the most frequent **mistakes automating processes** , you'll find automating a poorly mapped process, granting overly broad access to sensitive data, and editing a live workflow without testing it first. Jestor's **BPMS** , **Security** , and **App Versioning** help you avoid exactly these three mistakes.


### What Are the Most Common Mistakes When Automating Processes?


- Automating a confusing process, just transferring the mess into the new system.
- Granting broad access to data that should be restricted by department or role.
- Editing a live automation without testing it, breaking the process for current users.
- Automating everything at once, without validating the first process before expanding.
- Ignoring real-world exceptions, creating rules too rigid for cases that vary.


### How Do You Avoid Each of These Mistakes?


- Map the process before configuring any automation, clearly defining steps and owners.
- Set up granular permissions from the start, not after a data leak has already happened.
- Test changes in a separate version before publishing to the whole team.


### How Does Jestor Help Avoid These Mistakes?


- The **BPMS** requires structuring the process into phases before automating, which naturally exposes poorly designed processes.
- **Security** lets you restrict access by field, role, and action, avoiding unnecessary exposure of sensitive data.
- **App Versioning** lets you edit and test a new version of the process without impacting people already using the live version.
