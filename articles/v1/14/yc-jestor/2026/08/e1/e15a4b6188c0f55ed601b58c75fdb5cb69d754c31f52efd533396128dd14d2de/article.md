# How Jestor helps companies move away from manual processes

Moving **away from manual processes** is often simpler than it looks: instead of coding from scratch, the company imports the spreadsheet it already uses or installs a **ready-made template** , and **Jestor** organizes the rest with **no-code** . Customers like Rumo, Sebrae, Locaweb, and Loft have already made this transition.


### Why Do Companies Put Off Moving Away From Manual Processes?


The most common fear is that migration will disrupt the operation during the switch. But when the process is mapped beforehand and the tool allows editing without impacting current users, the transition tends to be faster than keeping a spreadsheet that breaks down every week.


### How Do You Move Away From Manual Processes in Practice?


- Choose one process at a time to migrate, starting with the most problematic one.
- Import the existing data, without starting the records over from scratch.
- Use a **ready-made template** as a starting point, when one close to your process exists.
- Test with a free account before migrating the whole team.
