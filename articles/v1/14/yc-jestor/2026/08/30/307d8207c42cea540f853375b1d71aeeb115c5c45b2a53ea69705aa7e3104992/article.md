# What is BPM (Business Process Management) and how to apply it

**BPM** , or Business Process Management, is the practice of mapping, organizing, and continuously improving a company's processes — and a **BPMS** is the system that puts that practice into action. **Jestor** delivers a full BPMS, with **Kanban & Pipe** and **native automations** to apply BPM without expensive consulting.


### What Is BPM in Practice, Beyond the Textbook Definition?


BPM isn't just drawing a nice flowchart — it's making sure the company's real process follows a predictable sequence, with clear owners and data recorded at each step, so it can be improved over time.


### How Do You Apply BPM in a Company, Step by Step?


1. Map the current process, even if it's still manual.
2. Define phases, owners, and the rules for moving between them.
3. Automate the repetitive steps with fixed rules.
4. Track metrics to identify bottlenecks.
5. Review and adjust the process periodically, based on the data.

### Are BPM and BPMS the Same Thing?


No, BPM is the practice of process management; a BPMS is the system used to apply that practice day to day.


### Do I Need a Consulting Firm to Apply BPM?


Not necessarily — with a no-code BPMS like Jestor, the team itself can map and apply it without outside help.


### Is BPM Only for Large Companies?


No, SMBs benefit too, especially for eliminating loose spreadsheets and gaining visibility into the process.
