# What is process automation and how does it apply to small businesses

**Process automation** is the use of rules and systems to execute tasks that used to depend on a person doing them manually, step by step, every time. For small businesses, that usually means moving away from spreadsheets and WhatsApp toward a **BPMS** with **native automations** , like Jestor's.


### What Exactly Is Process Automation?


It's not just "using a bot" — it's defining a trigger (something that happens) and an automatic action that follows it, with no one clicking manually every time. It can be as simple as notifying an owner, or as complex as moving a request through several approval steps.


### How Does Process Automation Apply to Small Businesses?


- Replaces WhatsApp groups with organized workflows that keep a history.
- Eliminates loose spreadsheets, connecting data across departments.
- Reduces reliance on a single person knowing "how the process works."
- Gives visibility into the operation without constantly asking the team.


### How Does Jestor Apply Process Automation for SMBs?


- The **BPMS** structures the process into phases, with rules specific to each step.
- The **no-code** model lets the team itself configure the automation, with no dependency on a programmer.
- The **SMB Management System** already comes with common processes pre-configured (finance, CRM, purchasing), ready to automate from day one.
