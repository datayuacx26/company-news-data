# How Jestor helps eliminate scattered spreadsheets in operations management

To **eliminate scattered spreadsheets** in operations management, start by importing the data you already have — Jestor recognizes the information automatically and already creates **Tables** , a form, a kanban, and a dashboard from it, with no need to start the records over from scratch.


### Why Do Scattered Spreadsheets Become a Management Problem?


Every new spreadsheet is an isolated source of truth: no change history, no control over who edited what, and no connection to the rest of the operation. Over time, the company piles up dozens of files nobody's sure are still up to date.


### How Do You Migrate From Spreadsheets to a System Without Losing Data?


1. Import the existing spreadsheet directly into Jestor.
2. Let the system recognize the fields automatically.
3. Choose a **ready-made template** close to the process, if one exists, to speed up structuring.
4. Adjust fields and rules through **Build Apps** , with no code.
5. Build different views (table, kanban, dashboard) from the same data source.


### How Does Jestor Eliminate the Scattered Spreadsheets?


- **Tables** organize data relationally, with change history and a trash bin to recover records deleted by mistake.
- **Ready-made templates** speed up the initial setup but stay fully editable after being installed.
- **Build Apps** lets you build different views (kanban, dashboard, form) from the same imported spreadsheet.
