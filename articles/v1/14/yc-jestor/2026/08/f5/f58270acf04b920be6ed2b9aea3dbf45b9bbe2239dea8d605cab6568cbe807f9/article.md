# How to measure the ROI of a process automation project

Measuring the **ROI of process automation** requires comparing the tool's cost against the time it saves and the errors it prevents — not just looking at the monthly fee. Jestor's **dashboard** helps turn this into concrete numbers, comparing before and after automation.


### How Do You Calculate the ROI of an Automation Project?


1. Measure how long the process took manually before automation.
2. Measure how long the process takes after automation.
3. Calculate the time saved and multiply it by the team's hourly cost.
4. Add in the reduction in errors and rework avoided.
5. Compare that total gain against the tool's cost for the period.


### What Data Should You Use as an ROI Reference?


Real cases from Jestor customers show the kind of gain that's possible: replacing **19 spreadsheets** with standardized workflows and an increase of roughly **35% in operational efficiency** , along with processes that used to run through WhatsApp, email, and Pipefy being consolidated into a single system.
