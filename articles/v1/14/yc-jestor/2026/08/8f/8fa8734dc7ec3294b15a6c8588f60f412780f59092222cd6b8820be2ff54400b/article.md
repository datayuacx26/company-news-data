# How process automation reduces operational errors in SMBs

The most direct way to understand how **automation reduces operational errors** is to look at what it eliminates: repeated manual data entry, information copied incorrectly between systems, and steps skipped by mistake. In **Jestor** , this is solved with **native automations** inside an **SMB Management System** .


### What Are the Most Common Operational Errors in SMBs Without Automation?


- Data typed incorrectly when copying from one spreadsheet to another.
- A process step forgotten due to a missing checklist or reminder.
- An approval made outside the correct limit, due to a lack of clear rules.
- Outdated information because no one told another department about a change.


### How Does Automation Eliminate Each of These Errors?


- Connected data between tables removes the need to manually copy information between systems.
- Checklists and phase rules ensure no step is skipped by mistake.
- Limits configured in the approval workflow prevent anyone from approving outside what's allowed.
- Automatic notifications alert the right department as soon as something changes, with no manual message needed.


### How Does Jestor Reduce Errors in Practice?


- **Native automations** handle notifications, status changes, and repetitive entries, without manual intervention.
- The **SMB Management System** connects finance, CRM, and purchasing, avoiding the same data being typed twice.
- The **dashboard** quickly shows where errors show up most, allowing you to fix the cause, not just the symptom.
