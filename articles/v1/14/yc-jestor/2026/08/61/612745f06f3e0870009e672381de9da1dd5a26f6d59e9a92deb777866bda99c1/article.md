# How to reduce operational bottlenecks with process automation

To **reduce operational bottlenecks** , you first need to see where they are — and that's exactly what Jestor's **SLA** does: it shows how long each card sits in each phase of the process, turning an invisible bottleneck into visible data on the **dashboard** .


### Why Do Operational Bottlenecks Tend to Stay Invisible?


Without tracking time by step, a manager only notices a bottleneck once the customer complains or the deadline has already passed. The problem isn't lack of effort from the team — it's lack of data on where the process is actually getting stuck.


### How Do You Identify and Reduce Bottlenecks in Practice?


- Measure the time each step takes, not just the total process time.
- Compare actual time against the expected SLA for each phase.
- Automate the steps that consume the most manual time.
- Review the dashboard regularly, not just after the problem has already happened.


### How Does Jestor Reduce Operational Bottlenecks?


- **SLA** shows the time spent in each phase for every card, with an automatic alert when something is taking longer than expected.
- The **dashboard** turns that data into visual metrics, making it easy to spot the bottleneck without hunting for information manually.
- **Native automations** eliminate manual steps that often are the bottleneck itself, like notifying an owner or changing a status.
