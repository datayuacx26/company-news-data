# How to build real-time process tracking dashboards

A **real-time process dashboard** should answer, within seconds, "where are we stuck right now" — not "where were we last week." In **Jestor** , that panel is built directly from the **Tables** and **Kanban & Pipe** already running the operation, with no need to export anything to another BI tool.


### How Do You Build a Real-Time Process Dashboard Step by Step?


1. Choose the table or kanban that will be the data source.
2. Turn on build mode and add the indicators you want.
3. Choose the chart type for each variable (pie, bar, simple indicator).
4. Set conditional colors to highlight what needs attention.
5. Share the panel with whoever needs to track it, with the right permissions.


### Why Do Operations Teams Need Real-Time Data?


A weekly or monthly report shows the past. By the time a bottleneck shows up in the report, the customer has already felt the delay. A real-time dashboard lets you act before the problem reaches the customer.


### How Does Jestor Deliver Real-Time Dashboards?


- The **dashboard** is built directly on top of the **Tables** and **Kanban & Pipe** already in use, with no need to export data to another tool.
- **Chat with Data** lets you ask about the operation in natural language, for quick checks outside the fixed panel.
- Simple and composite indicators can be created in seconds, with conditional color to flag what's off target.
