## Why financial decisions end up waiting on a report


At many companies, a simple question like "how much have we spent with this vendor this year?" requires asking someone in finance to generate a report — turning what could be an instant decision into a wait of hours or days.


## How to speed up this kind of decision


By connecting ERP data to a natural-language question interface, any authorized person can get the answer on the spot, with no need to depend on someone else generating a specific report.


**Common pains from waiting on financial data:**


- Simple questions waiting on a specific report
- Dependence on someone in finance for any one-off query
- Decisions delayed by data not being available when needed
- Rework for the finance team answering the same questions repeatedly


**How this gets solved:**

- Any authorized person can query data without depending on finance
