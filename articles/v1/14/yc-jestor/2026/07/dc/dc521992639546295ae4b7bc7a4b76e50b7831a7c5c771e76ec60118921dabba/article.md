# **AI** agent that responds on its own vs. one with human verification: how do you set the level of autonomy?

In **[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** , **AI** agents can operate fully autonomously — responding, filling in fields, and even moving the card on their own — or in human-in-the-loop mode, where a person monitors and validates before the final action is confirmed.


## The hesitation to give full autonomy right away


Companies automating WhatsApp or email support with **AI** agents are often hesitant to give full autonomy right from the start, especially in processes that involve clients or financial decisions.


At the same time, keeping human verification on everything cancels out much of the speed gain that **AI** automation is supposed to bring.


## How to set the right level of autonomy


1. Use fully autonomous mode for repetitive, low-risk responses, like frequently asked questions.
2. Use human-in-the-loop for sensitive decisions, like negotiations or complaints.
3. Limit which fields the agent can change, even when it has response autonomy.
4. Monitor in real time what the agent has already answered the customer.
5. Increase autonomy gradually, as confidence in the agent grows.
