# How to group apps by department inside a custom app navigation?

In **[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** 's custom navigation tool, you can create sections — like finance, HR, or specific teams — and group the apps related to that department inside each one, building a kind of personalized home right inside the app itself.


## Why does grouping by department help larger teams find their way around?


As a company grows and accumulates more apps, a generic navigation with everything mixed together makes it harder to find the right app quickly. Grouping by department — finance on one side, HR on another — points each person straight to what's relevant to their team, without having to search through dozens of options.


## How to build this grouped navigation in Jestor?


1. Add the custom navigation component inside the app.
2. Create a section with the department's name, like "Finance."
3. Group the apps related to that area inside that section.
4. Repeat the process for other sections — HR, team A, team B, company A, company B — based on the company's structure.


Element Function


**Section** Groups apps from the same department or team


**Grouped apps** Show up inside the corresponding section


## Why choose Jestor?


Beyond grouping by department, this same custom navigation lets you choose between button style or app icon style, with configurable colors and outlines, keeping the experience aligned with each company's visual identity inside **[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** .


### Frequently Asked Questions


**Can the same app show up in more than one section?** Yes, you can repeat an app across different sections if it's relevant to more than one department.


**Does this replace Jestor's standard side menu?** This custom navigation is an additional component within the app, and can coexist with the platform's standard navigation.


**Can I reorder sections after creating them?** Yes, you can rearrange the position of sections and the apps within them.
