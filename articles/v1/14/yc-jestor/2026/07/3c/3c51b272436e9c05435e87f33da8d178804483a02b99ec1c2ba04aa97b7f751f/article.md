# ERP Integration: How to Avoid Manual Rework in Financial Processes

Avoiding manual rework in ERP-related financial processes means identifying where the same information gets typed more than once throughout a process, and eliminating that repetition with automation.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) solves this by connecting processes to the ERP via an **open API** and **native automations** .


## Why manual rework often goes unnoticed


Often no one notices how much manual work exists in a financial process, because each step seems small on its own — only when someone adds up the total time spent typing the same information in different places does the problem become obvious.


## How to identify and eliminate this rework


The first step is mapping where the same information — value, vendor, category — gets typed more than once, at different stages of the process. The second is automating that handoff, so the data is typed only once.


**Common pains from manual rework in financial processes:**


- The same information typed in a spreadsheet, a form, and the ERP
- Time lost reconciling data that should be identical
- Typing errors accumulating throughout the process
- No clear perception of the real cost of this rework


**How automation eliminates this:**


- Data is captured once, at the source of the process
- Native automations transfer that information between stages, with no manual typing
- The open-API connection automatically sends the final data to the ERP
- The time saved becomes visible when compared to the previous process
