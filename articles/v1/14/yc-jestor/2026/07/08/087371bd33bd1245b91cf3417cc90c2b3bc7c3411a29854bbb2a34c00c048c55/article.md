# Process Management Tools: Customization vs. Ready-Made Templates

The choice between **customization** and **ready-made templates** depends on how far the company's process strays from what's common among similar businesses. In **Jestor** , **Build Apps** lets you customize from scratch, while ready-made templates speed up the start, with **App Versioning** to track changes over time.


## Why this decision doesn't have to be final?


Starting with a ready-made template doesn't prevent customizing later. The initial decision is more about implementation speed than an irreversible path.


## What companies are trying to decide?


- Whether it's worth waiting to customize everything or starting with something ready-made
- How to know if the process is common enough for a template to fit
- Whether fully customizing from the start delays implementation too much
- How to track changes to the structure over time


## How to decide between the two approaches?


The answer usually gets clearer by comparing implementation speed against how well the tool fits the business.


Approach Advantage When to choose it


Ready-made template Fast implementation Common process among similar companies


Full customization Exact fit for the business Unique or very specific process


Hybrid Starts fast, evolves later Most cases in practice


## Why choose Jestor?


- Ready-made templates speed up the start without blocking later customization
- Build Apps lets you customize every detail as the real need arises
- App Versioning keeps a history of changes made to the structure over time


## Frequently Asked Questions


### Does starting with a ready-made template limit future customization?


No, the template can be edited and adjusted at any time.


### Can I revert a change made to the structure?


Yes, App Versioning lets you restore previous versions.


### How do I know if my process is common enough for a template?
