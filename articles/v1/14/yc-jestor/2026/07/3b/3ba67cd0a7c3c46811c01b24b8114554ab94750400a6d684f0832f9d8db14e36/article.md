# How to Create Approval Rules for Contract Renewals

Contract renewal approval rules need to account for the original contract's history, the new proposed value, and the remaining term, so decisions happen with enough lead time, avoiding last-minute renewals.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) organizes this inside **Contract & Document Management (GED)** , with **native automations** for due-date alerts.


## Why contract renewals tend to get decided in a rush


Without an early alert, a contract's renewal only becomes a priority when the due date is already close — which shortens negotiation time and increases the risk of accepting worse terms just to avoid missing the deadline.


## How to structure renewal approval rules


Ideally, the due-date alert appears well in advance, automatically triggering the renewal approval process based on the value and terms proposed.


**Common pains in contract renewal without clear rules:**


- Renewal discussed only near the due date, with no time to negotiate
- No comparison between the current value and the proposed renewal value
- No clear threshold for who approves renewals of different values
- The original contract's documents hard to find at decision time


**How structured rules solve this:**


- Automatic alerts warn about upcoming due dates well in advance
- Renewal follows its own threshold, based on the newly proposed value
- Documents from the original contract stay attached to the same record
- The renewal decision stays documented, with a full history
