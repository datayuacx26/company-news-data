# Where Can You Separately Track AI Credit Consumption and Pages Processed by OCR in Your Account?

In **Jestor** , **AI** credit consumption and the number of pages processed by **OCR** show up separately on the account's usage page, each with its own metric.


### Why these metrics were split


Before, document reading by AI (OCR) was counted together with general AI credits, which made it unclear how much each read consumed. Splitting the metrics makes it clearer how much is being used for each type of resource.


### What shows up on Jestor's usage page


- AI credits consumed, used for text generation, PDFs, and other AI tasks
- Pages processed by OCR, counting each page or image read as one unit
- Other general metrics, like number of messages, tables, apps, and automations
- Usage specific to the plan you're on


### How to track AI and OCR usage in Jestor


1. Go to the usage page inside account settings
2. Check the total AI credits used, separately
3. Check the total pages processed by OCR
4. Use this data to understand where to adjust flows that consume more resources


### AI tools for SMB operations with transparent usage


Having clear visibility into **AI** and OCR consumption helps **AI tools for operations teams at SMBs** get used more predictably, avoiding surprises when scaling up automation usage.

Metric What it measures


**AI credits** Text generation, PDFs, and other AI tasks


**Pages processed (OCR)** Each page or image read

## Frequently Asked Questions


**Are OCR and AI credits billed the same way?** No, they're separate metrics, each tracked independently on the usage page.


**Does each page of a PDF count as one OCR unit?** Yes, a three-page PDF counts as three units.


**Where do I find these metrics inside the platform?** On the usage page, inside your account settings on[jestor.com](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) .
