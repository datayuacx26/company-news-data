# How to Structure a Scalable Approval Layer for Growing Companies

A scalable approval layer is one that keeps working even as the company doubles in size, opens new units, or changes its org structure — without needing to be rebuilt with every change.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) keeps this layer adjustable with **App Versioning** and **White Label** support for each new unit.


## Why rigid approval layers don't survive expansion


An approval structure designed for the company's current stage rarely serves it well after significant expansion — new roles, new cost centers, new business units all require adjustments that, without a flexible foundation, end up turning into a full rebuild project.


## How to prepare your approval layer to grow


The trick is designing the process modularly from the start, with version history, so future adjustments are incremental, not rebuilds.


**Common pains when expansion catches the process unprepared:**


- An approval structure that only works for the company's current size
- Every new unit requiring the process to be rebuilt from scratch
- No identity of its own for new brands or units within the same group
- Difficulty tracking how the process evolved through expansion


**How a scalable layer solves this:**


- The process is designed modularly, making incremental adjustments easier
- New units reuse the same base, with small tweaks
- Each unit can have its own visual identity, without losing process standardization
- Version history documents how the structure evolved over time
