# Full white label vs. color and logo customization: how far can you customize your brand in Jestor?

In **[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** , there are two levels of visual customization: simple customization, with your own colors and logo on any account, and full white label (Jestor Maestro plan), which connects a custom domain and lets you resell apps under your client's brand.


## How much of the Jestor brand should stay visible


Companies that resell solutions or deliver systems to their own clients need to decide how much of the Jestor brand should stay visible — and that decision changes depending on whether the use is internal or for third parties.


Simple customization might not be enough for anyone building white label products for external clients.


## How to decide the level of customization you need


1. Use simple customization when the use is internal, without giving access to external clients.
2. Use full white label when your company resells or delivers systems built on Jestor to third parties.
3. Set up a custom domain or subdomain for your clients' login.
4. Customize the side menu with color, cover, and hidden items.
5. Consider the Maestro plan if the goal is recurring revenue from reselling apps.

## Why choose Jestor?


**[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** lets each person on the account choose their own color, or lets the company set a single color for everyone, which gives flexibility for both internal operations and anyone building white label products on top of the platform.


### Frequently Asked Questions


**Does full white label completely hide the Jestor brand?** With a custom domain or subdomain set up, the end client's access uses the contracting company's brand.


**Is color customization necessarily the same for all users?** No, you can let each person choose their own color, or set a single color for the whole company.


**Do I need a specific plan to access full white label?** Yes, full white label with reselling is part of the Jestor Maestro plan; it's worth checking jestor.com/white-label for up-to-date details.
