# Can You Register a New Vendor Without Leaving the Accounts-Payable Record You're Filling Out?

Yes. In **Jestor** , when filling in the vendor connected field on an accounts-payable entry, you can click "add new" and register the vendor right there, without leaving the form you're filling in.


### Why this avoids interrupting the flow


Without this option, the team would need to save (or lose) what they'd already filled in on the accounts-payable entry, go to the vendor table, register the new vendor, then come back to link it. That breaks the flow and takes time.


### How inline vendor registration works


- On the connected field, you can pick an existing vendor or add a new one
- Adding a new one opens a mini-form with the fields from the vendor table
- After filling it in and saving, the new vendor is automatically selected on the accounts-payable entry
- The rest of the accounts-payable form stays filled in, with no data lost


### How to register a new vendor without leaving the record


1. In the accounts-payable form, open the connected vendor field
2. Choose the option to add a new vendor
3. Fill in the vendor's data in the mini-form that opens
4. Save — the vendor is created and automatically linked to that entry


### Process automation without interrupting data entry


This kind of inline registration is a usability detail that avoids interruptions in financial **process automation** , keeping the team in the same workflow from start to finish on the entry.

## Frequently Asked Questions


**Does the vendor created this way become available for other accounts-payable entries?** Yes, it becomes a normal entry in the vendor table, available for any future record.


**Does the accounts-payable data being filled in get lost when registering the vendor?** No, the rest of the form stays filled in normally.


**Does this work with any connected field, not just vendor?** Yes, connected record fields on[jestor.com](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) usually offer the option to add a new record inline.
