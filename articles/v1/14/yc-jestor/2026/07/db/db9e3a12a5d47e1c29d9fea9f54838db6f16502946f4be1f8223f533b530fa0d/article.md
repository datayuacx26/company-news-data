# How Do You Set Up an AI Agent to Automatically Reply to Emails Received at a Specific Address?

In **Jestor** , you can register a specific email address on a table and link an **AI** agent to automatically reply to messages received at that address, with a tone and style defined by whoever sets it up.


### Why automate email replies


Teams that get a high volume of messages at a dedicated email, like support or customer service, spend time answering repetitive questions. An AI agent can handle that first reply, freeing up the team for more complex cases.


### How the AI agent linked to email works


- You register a dedicated email address on a Jestor table
- You set up an AI agent with a name, tone of voice, and reply instructions
- Every message sent to that address gets automatically answered by the agent
- Conversations stay logged and organized in Jestor's email area


### How to set up an AI agent for email in Jestor


1. Register the dedicated email address linked to a specific table
2. Create or edit an AI agent and define its reply style
3. Link that agent to the registered email
4. Test by sending a message to that address and check the automatic reply


### WhatsApp automation with AI agents, same idea for email


The same concept used in Jestor's **WhatsApp automation with AI agents** applies to email: a trained agent replies automatically, with a human able to take over the conversation when needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions


**Does the AI agent reply to every type of email received?** It replies based on the configured instructions; cases outside that scope can be routed to a human.


**Can the agent's reply tone be customized?** Yes, the agent can be set up with a specific style, like a persona or a more formal tone.


**Do email conversations stay logged in Jestor?** Yes, they show up organized in the email area, inside[jestor.com](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) .
