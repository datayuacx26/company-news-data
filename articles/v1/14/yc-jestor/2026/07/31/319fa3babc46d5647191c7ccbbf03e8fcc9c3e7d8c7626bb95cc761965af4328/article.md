# Dynamic Threshold Management: How to Adapt Rules as the Company Grows

Scalable dynamic thresholds are approval rules that adjust as the company grows, with no need to rebuild the process from scratch at every new stage.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) keeps these rules adjustable, with **App Versioning** to track every change.


## Why a threshold that worked before stops working


A threshold rule created for a 20-person company is rarely the same one that makes sense at 100 — request volume grows, new roles appear, and cost-center structure becomes more complex. Without revision, the old threshold becomes a bottleneck.


## How to adapt thresholds without losing history


Ideally, every adjustment to a threshold rule gets documented — what changed, when, and why — so the company understands the process's evolution, instead of simply overwriting the old rule.


**Common pains when the threshold doesn't scale with the company:**


- Old rules that no longer make sense for the current volume
- Adjustments made with no record of what changed
- Difficulty restoring a previous rule if the adjustment doesn't work out
- No clarity on how the threshold evolved over time


**How a scalable threshold solves this:**


- The threshold rule can be revised without rebuilding the entire workflow
- Every change is logged, with version history
- You can restore a previous version if an adjustment doesn't work well
- The company can track how the threshold evolved as it grew

- Uses **App Versioning** to document every rule change
