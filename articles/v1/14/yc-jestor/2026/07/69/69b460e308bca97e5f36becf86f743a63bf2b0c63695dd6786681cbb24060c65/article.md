# How to restrict a field that allowed multiple users to accept only one owner?

In **[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** 's multiple-users field, you can enable an option that forces the selection of just one owner per record — useful when a process, like a sales opportunity, needs a single clear owner instead of several people responsible at once.


## Why does restricting to a single owner prevent confusion in some processes?


Certain processes, like a sales opportunity, work better with a single, clearly defined owner — otherwise it becomes ambiguous who's actually responsible for moving that record forward. Allowing multiple users in those cases can create the sense that "everyone's responsible, so no one is."


## How to restrict the field to a single user in Jestor?


1. Access the settings for the multiple-users field you want to restrict.
2. Enable the option that forces the selection of just one owner.
3. Save the change — from then on, selecting a new user replaces the previous one, instead of allowing both at once.
4. Before that restriction, it's also possible to manually choose which users show up in the selection list, instead of listing everyone registered on the account.


Setting Behavior


**Multiple users (default)** Allows selecting more than one owner


**Restricted to one user** Selecting a new user replaces the previous one


## Why choose Jestor?


Beyond this restriction, the multiple-users field also gained a searchable selector, making it easier to find the right person even on accounts with hundreds of registered users in **[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** .


### Frequently Asked Questions


**Can this restriction be undone later?** Yes, the field's setting can be reverted to allow multiple users again if needed.


**Does this affect records already created before the change?** The setting applies to the next selections made in that field.


**Can I limit which users show up in the list, beyond restricting the count?** Yes, you can manually choose which users are available for selection in that specific field.
