# Tools to Replace Spreadsheets in Managing a Company's Operations

Replacing spreadsheets in managing a company's operations requires a tool with real relational **Tables** , not just a grid of cells. In **Jestor** , **Build Apps** turns existing spreadsheets into structured applications, with built-in **no-code** automation and access control.


## Why spreadsheets stop keeping up as the operation grows?


Spreadsheets don't have real version control, granular permissions, or native automation. They work fine at first, but become a source of error and rework as soon as more than one person starts editing the same data.


## What companies are trying to solve when leaving spreadsheets behind?


- Typos and conflicting versions edited by different people
- No automation for repetitive tasks inside the spreadsheet
- No control over who can view or edit each piece of information
- Difficulty connecting data across different spreadsheets


## How migrating from a spreadsheet to an app works?
