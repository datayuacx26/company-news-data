# How to use the replace formula to strip spaces from a phone number before a WhatsApp automation?

**[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** 's new replace formula substitutes one piece of text for another — for example, a space for nothing — letting you automatically clean phone numbers that have spaces, dashes, or symbols before using that data in a **WhatsApp** automation.


## Why do phone numbers typed by different people cause problems in automations?


Everyone types a phone number differently: one person with spaces between the digits, another with dashes, another with nothing at all. When that data feeds a message-sending automation on **WhatsApp** , those formatting variations can break the send. The replace formula solves this by standardizing the text before it's used.


## How to configure the replace formula in Jestor?


1. Create a formula field on the table where the phone number is registered.
2. Use the replace formula to substitute spaces, dashes, or other unwanted characters with nothing, "gluing" the number into a single format.
3. Use that already-cleaned field as the reference in the message-sending automation via **WhatsApp** .
4. The same formula can be reused for other text, like making it easier to generate an ID.

**Standardize text** Makes it easier to generate IDs or other derived fields
