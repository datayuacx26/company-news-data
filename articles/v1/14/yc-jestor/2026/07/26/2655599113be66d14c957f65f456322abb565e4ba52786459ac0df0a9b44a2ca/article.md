# How to Automate Service Order Approval Integrated with Your ERP

Service order approval connects the decision to execute (or not) a service to the same data used by sales and the ERP, avoiding rework between the two teams.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) solves this with its **CRM with Service Orders Integrated to Omie** model.


## Why service orders tend to create rework between teams


When the sales team closes a service sale in one system and execution is tracked in another, information commonly gets lost in the handoff — what was sold doesn't quite match what was executed or approved.


## How to integrate service order approval with the rest of the process


The solution is having the service order created already connected to the sales opportunity that originated it, with formal approval before execution, and data automatically following to Omie.


**Common pains in service order approval without integration:**


- Duplicate data between the sales system and execution tracking
- No formal approval before starting execution
- Discrepancy between what was sold and what was actually executed
- Rework between sales and operations to reconcile information


**How integration solves this:**


- The service order is created already connected to the originating sales opportunity
- Formal approval happens before execution starts
- Data automatically follows to Omie, with no duplicate entry
- Sales and operations share the same information base


**Where Jestor fits in:**


- Uses the **CRM with Service Orders Integrated to Omie** model
- Applies formal approval inside **Approvals** before execution
- Connects approved data directly to **Omie**
- Reduces rework between sales and operations


Stage Where it happens


**Order origin** Sales opportunity in the CRM


**Execution** After approval, data posted in Omie
