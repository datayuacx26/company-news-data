# Financial Automation: How to Reduce Rework Between Your ERP and Spreadsheets

Reducing ERP and spreadsheet rework means eliminating the need for parallel Excel controls just because the ERP doesn't offer the view the team needs day to day.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) replaces those parallel spreadsheets with **Financial Management** built on **Tables** connected to the ERP.


## Why parallel spreadsheets alongside the ERP keep existing


Even companies with a well-implemented ERP often keep a "just to track better" spreadsheet — because the ERP doesn't offer the exact view the team needs, or because it's faster to edit a cell than to navigate the tax system.


## How to eliminate this duplication


The solution is having your own relational data layer, fed by the same process that generates the ERP posting, with no duplicate data entry between spreadsheet and tax system.


**Common pains with parallel spreadsheets alongside the ERP:**


- The same information typed twice, in the spreadsheet and in the ERP
- Discrepancy between what's on the spreadsheet and what's in the tax system
- Rework keeping the spreadsheet always up to date manually
- Dependence on one specific person who knows how to maintain that spreadsheet


**How automation solves this:**


- Financial data lives in relational tables, not loose spreadsheets
- The same information feeds both the management panel and the ERP posting
- No duplicate typing between the tracking view and the tax system
- Any authorized person accesses the same information, with no need for a specific file

- Uses relational **Tables** as the data foundation, instead of loose spreadsheets
- Expands the ERP via **ERP Expansion** , with no duplicate typing
- Removes dependence on specific files maintained by a single person
