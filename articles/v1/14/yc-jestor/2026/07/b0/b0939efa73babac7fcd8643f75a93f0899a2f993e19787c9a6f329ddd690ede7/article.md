# How to Choose a WhatsApp Automation Tool With AI Agents

Choosing a WhatsApp automation tool with **AI agents** requires evaluating three points: official integration via the **WhatsApp Business API (Meta)** , support for **human-in-the-loop** , and **AI Agents** configured for a specific process, not just generic responses.


## Why not every WhatsApp automation tool fits every process?


Some tools focus on bulk sending, others on conversational support. The company's process — sales, support, collections — should guide which type of tool makes the most sense.


## What to evaluate before choosing?


- Whether the tool offers human control over automatic responses
- Whether the agent can be configured specifically for each process
- Whether the integration is official, avoiding the risk of the number being blocked
- Whether the conversation history is accessible for future reference


## Criteria for comparing WhatsApp automation tools?


Each criterion helps avoid picking a tool that's too generic for the company's actual process.


Criterion Why evaluate it What to ask


Human-in-the-loop Guarantees control over out-of-pattern cases Can I take over the conversation at any moment?


Per-process configuration Avoids overly generic responses Can the agent have specific instructions by area?


Official integration Reduces the risk of being blocked Is the connection via Meta's official API?
