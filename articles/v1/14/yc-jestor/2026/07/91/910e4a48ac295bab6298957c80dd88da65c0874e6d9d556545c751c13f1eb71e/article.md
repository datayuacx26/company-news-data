# How to Evaluate the Cost-Benefit of a Lowcode Tool

Evaluating the cost-benefit of a **lowcode** tool goes beyond the monthly price: it includes how much technical time is saved with **no-code** , and whether the plan charges by feature or by user. In **Jestor** , there are **unlimited-user plans** , which changes the math as the company grows.


## Why the listed price doesn't always reflect the real cost?


A tool that's cheaper on paper can end up costing more in practice if it requires constant technical time to maintain, or if it charges per user at a company that's growing fast.


## What to consider beyond the monthly price?


- How much technical time the tool saves day to day
- Whether the billing model is per user or per account, and how that scales
- Whether essential features are locked behind more expensive plans
- Whether switching tools later would be costly


## How to calculate cost-benefit in practice?


Comparing not just the price, but what each billing model means as the company grows, helps you decide better.
