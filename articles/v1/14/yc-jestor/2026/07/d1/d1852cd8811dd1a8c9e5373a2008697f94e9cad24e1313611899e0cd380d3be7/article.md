# Can orders and incidents be viewed on a map in real time based on the registered address?

Yes. In **Jestor** , the smart address field connects the platform directly to Google Maps, and as soon as an address is registered, orders and incidents automatically show up geolocated on the map, in real time.


## How automatic geolocation works


Typing the address into a smart field, Jestor completes and validates the information automatically through the Google Maps integration, reducing typing errors. From that record, the address data is already used to place that entry on the map, letting you see where orders and incidents are distributed across the operation with no extra manual effort.


Step What happens


**Registering the address** The smart field connected to Google Maps completes and validates the information


**Map view** Orders and incidents appear geolocated automatically


## How to use the smart address field


1. Add a smart address field to the orders or incidents table.
2. Register the address as usual, letting Google Maps validate and complete the information.
3. Access the map view to see records distributed geographically.
4. Track orders and incidents in real time as new records are registered.


## Why choose Jestor


- **Zero human error on addresses** : automatic validation prevents incomplete or incorrect records.
- **Native geographic view** : no external tool is needed to visualize data on a map.
- **No code required** : geolocation just works with the address field configured on the table.


### Do you need to set up any external integration to use the map?


No, the connection to Google Maps is already native through the smart address field.


### Is the map updated automatically as new orders are registered?


Yes, data goes to the map automatically as soon as the address is recorded on the form.


### Does this feature work only for orders, or also for other record types?


The principle works for any table that has a smart address field configured, including orders and incidents.
