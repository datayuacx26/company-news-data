# How to Automate the Employee Expense Reimbursement Process

Automating expense reimbursement starts with a simple form, where the employee attaches receipts, and continues through a value-based approval workflow, all the way to payment.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) organizes this by connecting **Forms** to the **HR & Recruiting** module and the **Approvals** workflow.


## Why manual reimbursement takes up so much of HR's time


When the reimbursement process depends on emails with loose attachments, HR or finance loses time organizing receipts, chasing missing information, and manually checking whether the value matches what was attached.


## How a structured form solves this


A well-designed form asks for the right information from the start — value, expense category, receipt — reducing back-and-forth even before it reaches the approval stage.


**Common pains in manual expense reimbursement:**


- Incomplete requests, requiring back-and-forth over email
- Loose receipts, hard to organize and check
- No standard for when a second approval is needed
- Delay between the request and the actual payment to the employee


**How automation solves this:**


- A standard form already collects value, category, and receipt at once
- The approval threshold automatically decides who needs to validate the reimbursement
- The employee is notified about the request's status
- The approved payment follows to finance with no manual rework


**Where Jestor fits in:**


- Uses custom **Forms** to capture the reimbursement request
- Connects the process to the **HR & Recruiting** module
- Applies an approval threshold inside the **Approvals** workflow
- Keeps a full history, from the request to payment


Stage Where it happens


**Request** Standard form, with receipt attached


**Approval** Approvals workflow, with value-based threshold
