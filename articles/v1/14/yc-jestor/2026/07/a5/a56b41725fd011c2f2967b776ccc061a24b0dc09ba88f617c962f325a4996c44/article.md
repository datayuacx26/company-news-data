# Can the forced title bar be removed from an external tool embedded in the app?

Yes. In **[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** 's embed block, the show-title option now comes turned off by default, letting you fully remove that bar which used to show up mandatorily on top of any external tool brought into the app.


## Why does having control over an embed's title bar matter?


External tools like a YouTube video, a Power BI dashboard, or a PDF used to come with a fixed title bar, creating an odd margin that visually clashed with the rest of the app. For anyone who wants an embed to feel like a natural part of the app, that forced bar got in the way of a seamless visual integration.


## How to remove an embed's title bar in Jestor?


1. Add the embed block and paste the link or iframe of the external tool you want.
2. In the block's style options, turn off the title display.
3. For an even more integrated look, combine this with a transparent background and no border, putting the full focus on the embedded tool.
4. Also choose between rounded or square corners, depending on how you want the embed to fit into the rest of the app.


Style option Effect


**Show title** Off by default; can be turned on if needed


**Transparent background** Removes any extra border around the embed


**Square corners** Makes the embed sit flush with the rest of the app, no margins


## Why choose Jestor?


**[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com)** 's embed block accepts any tool that provides a link or iframe — from Power BI to videos and PDFs — giving you flexibility to bring external solutions into the app without losing visual control over the rest of it.


### Frequently Asked Questions


**Does this work for any type of embedded tool?** Yes, the style option applies to any link or iframe brought into the embed block.


**Does removing the title affect how the embedded tool works?** No, it's purely a visual option; the tool keeps working normally.


**Do these style changes already apply to older embeds?** Yes, the update is automatic for anyone already using builder mode.
