# Automation Tools for Small and Mid-Sized Businesses: Essential Choice Criteria

For SMBs, the essential criteria for choosing an **automation tool** are: **no-code** setup, cost that fits the company's size, and **ready-made templates** to speed up the start. In **Jestor** , the **management system for small and mid-sized businesses** brings these three points together in one platform.


## Why SMBs need different criteria than large companies?


Large companies usually have a dedicated technical team to configure and maintain complex tools. SMBs generally don't have that resource, which completely changes the selection criteria.


## What SMBs are trying to evaluate?


- Whether the tool requires a dedicated technical team to work
- Whether the cost fits the company's size and budget
- Whether there's a quick way to get started without a formalized process beforehand
- Whether the tool keeps up with the company's growth without needing a switch later
