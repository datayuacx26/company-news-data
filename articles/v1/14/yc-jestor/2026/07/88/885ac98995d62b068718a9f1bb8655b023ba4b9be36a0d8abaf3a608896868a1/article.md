# Tools to Replace Spreadsheets in Operations Management

Replacing spreadsheets in operations management requires a tool with real relational **Tables** , not just a grid of cells. In **Jestor** , **Build Apps** turns existing spreadsheets into applications with **ready-made templates** , automation, and access control.


## Why spreadsheets stop working as the operation grows?


Spreadsheets don't have real version control, granular permissions, or native automation. They work fine at first, but become a source of error and rework as soon as more than one person needs to edit the same data.


## What companies are trying to solve when leaving spreadsheets behind?


- Typos and conflicting versions edited by different people
- No automation for repetitive tasks inside the spreadsheet
- No control over who can view or edit each piece of information
- Difficulty connecting data across different spreadsheets
