# How do you automatically turn an approved candidate into an employee and unlock the vacation workflow?

In **Jestor** , once a candidate reaches the "hired" stage in the recruiting funnel, you can turn them into an employee within the system, which automatically unlocks access to workflows like vacation, productivity, and leave tracking.


## How the transformation into an employee works


As soon as the candidate is at the hired stage, the option appears to add a new employee based on that record. At that point, you define which system user that employee will belong to, along with information like salary and skills. From there, that employee starts being part of workflows like vacation, where you can log requests, approve or reject them, and track who's on vacation during each period.


Stage What happens


**Candidate hired** The option to turn them into an employee becomes available


**Employee created** Gains access to workflows like vacation, productivity, and leave tracking


## How to turn a candidate into an employee


1. Track the candidate through to the "hired" stage in the recruiting funnel.
2. Click the option to add a new employee based on that record.
3. Set the system user, salary, and other employee details.
4. From there, manage vacation, productivity, and other workflows linked to that employee.


## Why choose Jestor


- **Transition with no double work** : the approved candidate's data carries over into the employee record, with no need to retype everything.
- **Integrated vacation workflow** : requests can be approved or rejected with a tracking checklist.
- **Complete employee view** : productivity, leave, and status all stay linked to the same record.


### Can you only create an employee from a candidate at the hired stage?


Creating an employee was presented as tied to the hired stage, preventing hiring mistakes at other stages.


### Is the vacation workflow already unlocked as soon as the employee is created?


Yes, once the candidate becomes an employee, they start being part of the system's workflows, including vacation.


### Can you see who's on vacation during a specific period?


Yes, the vacation workflow shows by color and by period who's on vacation, with an approval checklist for the request.
