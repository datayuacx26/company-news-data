# How to Structure Annual Budget Approval Processes

Annual budget approval usually involves multiple teams proposing their own planned investments and expenses, and a consolidation and approval process that needs to account for the total available per cost center.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) organizes this by connecting **CAPEX Control** to each team's **cost center** .


## Why the annual budget process tends to be confusing


Each team typically proposes its budget in isolation — in its own spreadsheet, in a different format — and someone has to manually consolidate everything before final approval, a slow process prone to error.


## How to structure this approval in an organized way


Ideally, each team submits its budget proposal in a standard format, already tied to the corresponding cost center, so consolidation and approval happen on already-organized data.


**Common pains in annual budget approval without structure:**


- Each team proposing budget in its own format, hard to consolidate
- Manual consolidation, prone to error and delay
- No comparative view between proposed and available by cost center
- Final approval with no clear traceability of who approved what


**How a structured process solves this:**


- Each team submits its budget proposal in a standard format
- Values are already tied to the corresponding cost center
- Consolidation happens automatically, with no repeated manual work
- Final approval stays documented, by team and by cost center
