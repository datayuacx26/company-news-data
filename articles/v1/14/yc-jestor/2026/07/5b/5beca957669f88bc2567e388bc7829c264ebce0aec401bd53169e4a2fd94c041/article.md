# How do you prioritize candidates by score to highlight the best ones on the recruiting dashboard?

In **Jestor** , each candidate can receive a hiring priority level — ranging from low to high, or on a custom numeric scale — and candidates with a higher priority automatically show up highlighted on the recruiting dashboard.


## How prioritization works


The recruiting system lets you classify each candidate with a priority field, which can be configured as high and low or even on a 0-to-10 scale, based on the company's preference. The higher the priority assigned, the more qualified the candidate is considered for that role. Candidates with high hiring priority show up highlighted on the dashboard, giving immediate visibility to the strongest profiles.


Priority level What it indicates


**High** Most qualified candidate, highlighted on the dashboard


**Low** Candidate with lower hiring priority


## How to classify candidates by priority


1. Open the candidate's card within the recruiting funnel.
2. Set the hiring priority level, whether on a simple or numeric scale.
3. Check the recruiting dashboard to view candidates with the highest priority.
4. Combine with other filters, like role or stage, to narrow down the best profiles.


## Why choose Jestor


- **Automatic highlight for the best candidates** : high priority already shows up highlighted on the dashboard, with no manual effort.
- **Flexible scale** : priority can be simple (high/low) or numeric, based on the company's preference.
- **Complete recruiting funnel** : from screening to interview, everything stays organized in the same workflow.


### Is the priority scale fixed as high and low?


No, it can be configured as high and low or on a numeric scale, like 0 to 10, based on the company's preference.


### Does the recruiting dashboard automatically show high-priority candidates?


Yes, candidates with high hiring priority show up highlighted on the recruiting dashboard.


### Can a candidate's priority be changed after it's set?


Yes, the priority field can be adjusted at any time during the hiring process.
