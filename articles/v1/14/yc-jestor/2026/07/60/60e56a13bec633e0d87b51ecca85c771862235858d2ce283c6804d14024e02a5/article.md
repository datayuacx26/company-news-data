# How Do You Track Message Volume and Response Time in a WhatsApp Dashboard?

In **Jestor** , the **WhatsApp** panel shows, right outside the card, the exact number of messages exchanged in each conversation and the time spent in each phase, plus a full dashboard with data, charts, and indicators for the operation.


### Why tracking these numbers matters


Without visibility into volume and response time, it's hard to know if the team is responding within expectations or if a given WhatsApp process is overloaded. These indicators give a quick management view of the operation.


### What the WhatsApp dashboard shows


- Total message counter per conversation, visible right on the panel, outside the card
- Time spent in each phase of the conversation, useful for tracking **SLA**
- A visual indicator of who replied last: the internal team or the customer
- Customizable charts and indicators for the WhatsApp operation as a whole


### How to track volume and time on Jestor's dashboard


1. Open the app linked to the configured WhatsApp number
2. Check the dashboard with general operation indicators
3. Open specific conversations to see individual message counters
4. Use time-per-phase data to identify service bottlenecks


### WhatsApp automation with AI agents and full visibility


Combining this dashboard with Jestor's native **AI** agent on WhatsApp gives the team a complete view: how much was automated, how much needed human intervention, and where support is taking longer than expected.
