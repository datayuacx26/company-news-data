# How Do You Use Emojis or Custom Icons in an Internal Approval Checklist in Jestor?

In **Jestor** , the checklist field lets you choose from ready-made styles — like green/red, yes/no, happy/sad face — or customize it with your own emojis for each item on the internal approval checklist.


### Why customizing the checklist's style helps


An audit or approval checklist is easier to scan quickly when the visual style reflects what's being checked. Different teams also have different preferences, and the default green/red isn't always the most intuitive for every process.


### Style options available on the checklist


- Green check and red X, for positive and negative
- Yes or no, in text format
- Happy or sad face, for a more informal tone
- Custom emojis, freely chosen for each situation


### How to set up emojis on a checklist in Jestor


1. Add a checklist-type field on the desired table
2. Choose the style for positive and negative options
3. If you prefer, customize it with specific emojis for your process
4. Set whether the field is required before saving the form


### Process automation with more visual auditing


A checklist with a clear visual style reinforces auditing and internal approval **process automation** , since the responsible person quickly spots what's already been validated, without depending on long text to understand the status.
