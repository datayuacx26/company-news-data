# Can you attach a YouTube tutorial video directly inside a form to explain how to fill it out?

Yes. In **Jestor** , custom forms (Forms) let you attach YouTube tutorial videos directly inside the form itself, helping whoever's filling it out understand what's being asked in each field.


## How form personalization works


Besides attaching explainer videos, Forms lets you customize the look with a cover, logo, and the company's brand colors, organize long forms into steps with pagination, and create conditional logic so questions appear or disappear based on a previous answer. The form can be shared via a public link, making it accessible to anyone who needs to fill it out.


Feature What it allows


**Attached YouTube video** Visually explains how to fill out the form


**Visual customization** Cover, logo, and brand colors


**Conditional fields** Questions appear or disappear based on the previous answer


## How to attach a tutorial to the form


1. Open the form you want to customize inside Forms.
2. Find the option to attach a YouTube tutorial video.
3. Paste the link to the desired explainer video.
4. Publish the form and share the public link with whoever needs to fill it out.


## Why choose Jestor


- **Forms with their own identity** : cover, logo, and colors make the form feel more aligned with the brand.
- **Fewer filling-out questions** : the tutorial video cuts down on mistakes and repeated questions about how to fill it in.
- **Native conditional logic** : questions adjust automatically based on previous answers.


### Does the video need to be hosted on Jestor itself?


No, the video is attached from a YouTube link, with no need to upload the file directly to the platform.


### Can you split a long form into several steps?


Yes, Forms lets you use pagination to break long forms into smaller steps.


### Do conditional fields also work on public forms?


Yes, conditional logic works both for internal forms and ones shared publicly through a link.
