# Lowcode Tools for Companies: What to Consider Before Choosing

Before choosing a **lowcode tool for companies** , it's worth evaluating three points: how much of the setup is truly **no-code** , whether there's an **App Builder com IA** to speed up creation, and how flexible the **Build Apps** feature is for the company's specific process.


## What's the difference between lowcode and a fully no-code tool?


Lowcode usually still requires some technical setup at specific points, while a **no-code** platform aims to cover the whole process without that knowledge, even in advanced cases.


## What companies are trying to evaluate?


- How much of the tool truly requires no technical knowledge
- Whether there's AI support to speed up app and automation creation
- Whether the tool lets you adapt the process, not the other way around
- Whether there's support for advanced cases that need code when necessary
