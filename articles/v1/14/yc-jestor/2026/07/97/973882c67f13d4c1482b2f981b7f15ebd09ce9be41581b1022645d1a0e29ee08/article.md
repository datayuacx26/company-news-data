# ERP and HR Integration: How to Automate Benefits Approvals

Benefits approval HR ERP connects an employee's request — a benefit, an adjustment, an exception — to the required approval threshold and, afterward, to the corresponding financial posting.[Jestor](https://jestor.com/?ref=blog.jestor.com) ties the **HR & Recruiting** module to the **Approvals** workflow, connected to the ERP.


## Why benefits tend to stay outside the formal approval process


Benefit requests — a one-off adjustment, a policy exception — tend to be handled more informally than operational expenses, often decided in a direct conversation, with no formal record and no connection to finance.


## How to structure this approval


A benefit request can follow the same principle as any other approval workflow: a standard form, a clear threshold, and the approved amount automatically following to finance.


**Common pains in informal benefits approval:**


- Decisions made in conversation, with no formal record
- No standard on who can approve each type of benefit
- Rework manually posting the approved amount in finance
- Difficulty auditing benefit decisions over time


**How automation solves this:**


- The benefit request is created through a standard HR form
- The approval threshold automatically decides who needs to validate it
- The approved amount follows to finance with no repeated manual entry
- The history of benefit decisions stays documented
