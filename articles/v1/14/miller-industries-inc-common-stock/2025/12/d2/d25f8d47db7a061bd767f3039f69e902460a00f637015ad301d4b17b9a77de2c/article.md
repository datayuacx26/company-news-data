# Kurtis Barks Wins 2025 Towing Museum Auction

We at Miller Industries would like to take a moment to congratulate Kurtis Barks for placing the winning bid of the 2025[International Towing and Recovery Museum](https://towingmuseum.com/) auction! Held on October 10th, Barks competed against various other towing groups from all across the US for the highest bid. The carrier, an[LCG 12 Series](https://www.millerind.com/products/car-carriers/12-series-lcg) , was sold to Barks for a bid of $142,500. He was able to pick up his prize recently from our Ooltewah plant (pictured below). Located in Kansas City, Missouri, Barks represents the[Cardinal Legacy Towing Group](https://cardinallegacytowing.com/) , and we're sure he will put his truck to good use.


We would also like to take a moment to shoutout[Hino Trucks USA](https://www.hino.com/) for their contribution of the cab and chassis portion of the donation. For the 2025 International Towing and Recover Museum auction, we partnered with Hino Trucks USA to deliver the full LCG 12 Series.
