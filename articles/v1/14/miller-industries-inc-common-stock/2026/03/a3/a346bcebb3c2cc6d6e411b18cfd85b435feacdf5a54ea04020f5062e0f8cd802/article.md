# Miller Industries Partners with Hino for 2026 Florida Tow Show Giveaway

Every year at the[Florida Tow Show](https://floridatowshow.com/) , Miller Industries partners with[Hino Trucks](https://www.hino.com/) for an exciting raffle giveaway, and this year is no exception. Combining our Chevron car carrier bed with a Hino Trucks Chassis, The PWOF will raffle off this year’s prize at the 2026 Florida Show; a 2026 Hino L-Series with a Chevron[12-Series LCG](https://www.millerind.com/products/car-carriers/12-series-lcg) Carrier.


Tickets are sold for $10 each, with bundle options of 12 tickets for $100, and 25 for $200. Once payment is received, your ticket(s) will be mailed, with the option of returning the stubs to the PWOF office or placed in the raffle barrel at the show. Orders of tickets must be received by March 27 *th* , 2026 to be eligible.


The drawing will occur at the Miller Industries booth at the Florida Tow Show on Saturday, April 11 *th* . The winner does not need to be present to be eligible for the drawing. For more information, or to enter the raffle, head towards the[PWOF website](https://www.pwof.org/) and fill out the entry form there, or[click this link here!](https://floridatowshow.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2026_RaffleTruck_EntryForm.pdf)
