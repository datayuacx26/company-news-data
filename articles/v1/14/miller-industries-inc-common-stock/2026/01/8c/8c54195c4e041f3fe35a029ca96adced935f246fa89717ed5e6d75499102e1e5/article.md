# The VLA Carrier Lives up to the Name

The VLA (Very Low Angle) Carrier is another fantastic tool in your toolbox for exxotic vehicle and equipment loading and transportation. With a low 6° load angle, this carrier allows these movements to be effortless, making it the ideal choice for handling lower clearance vehicles. With a 23’ aluminum bed, the VLA sports a 10,000 lbs. deck rating, along with a wheel lift rating of 3,000 lbs. This carrier also contains a 12,000 lbs. worm gear winch, along with multiple winch options to better support your specific needs.


The VLA Carrier uses an advanced twin-motor rack-and-pinion system as well. This allows the aluminum bed to travel the full length of the 270” subframe, sliding the bed almost completely flat to the ground. If you want safe, simple, and smooth, the VLA Carrier is the way to go.
