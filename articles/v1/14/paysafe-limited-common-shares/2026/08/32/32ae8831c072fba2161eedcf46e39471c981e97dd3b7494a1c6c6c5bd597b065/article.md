# PaysafeWallet is now live in Poland: Your money, your way

#### Aug 10, 2026


*Consumers can pay, send and spend in złoty, with a debit card in their pockets or on their phones*


**August 10, 2026. London, U.K. –**[Paysafe](https://www.paysafe.com/us-en/?utm_campaign=psg_PressRelease_Traffic&utm_content=article_web_inArticle&utm_medium=pr) (NYSE: PSFE),[PaysafeWallet](https://www.paysafecard.com/en-pl/paysafewallet/) has arrived in Poland and it’s built for a new generation. Polish consumers can now access a digital wallet giving them their own personal debit card, on their phone, built for the way they live, spend and have fun today.


Poland has strong adoption of[PaysafeCard](https://www.paysafecard.com/pl-pl/) , and this builds on that foundation. PaysafeWallet extends what customers already trust into a broader, more complete wallet experience.


With PaysafeWallet, everything is in one place. Customers can top up with cash, send money to friends in seconds, pay at their favorite store or restaurant, spend online, send and receive bank transfers, or withdraw cash whenever it is needed. Customers receive a personal payment account with an IBAN and a virtual debit card that works wherever they need it.


“Young consumers in Poland are digital natives and expect their money to move as fast as they do,” said Bob Legters, Paysafe’s Chief Product Officer. “PaysafeWallet gives them something that feels made for them: their own debit card on their phone, the freedom to pay, send and spend however they want, and the reassurance that cash is still an option when they need it. We’re really excited about what we’re building for this generation.”


With approximately 600,000 users joining PaysafeWallet across Europe in just 18 months, the success of the service highlights strong consumer demand for payment solutions that fit everyday life. Paysafe remains committed to giving customers greater choice and flexibility, whether they prefer cash, card, or a seamless combination of both.


Existing PaysafeCard account holders can simply click on the Wallet tab to get started, while new customers can download the PaysafeCard app for Android or iOS and sign up in minutes.
