# Associated Bank Foundation Donating $125,000 to Fox Valley Area Storm Relief Efforts

Associated Banc-Corp (NYSE: ASB) today announced its foundation is donating $125,000 to support local recovery and relief efforts in response to the devastating storms that impacted Menasha and communities across the Fox Valley on July 27, 2026.
