# Associated Bank announces $17.7M loan for townhome community acquisition in suburban Des Moines, Iowa

Associated Bank announced completion of a $17,700,000 term loan to Tricap Investments for the phased acquisition of the Townhomes at Magnolia Heights, located at 5641 155th Street in Urbandale, Iowa, a suburb of Des Moines, Iowa.
