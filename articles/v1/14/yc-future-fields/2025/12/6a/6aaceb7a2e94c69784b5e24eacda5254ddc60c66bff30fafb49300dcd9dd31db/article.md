## Case study at a glance


N-glycosylated purified EntoEngine-EPO appeared at a MW of ~25 kDa. PNGase treated EPO to remove N-linked glycans shifted to a lower MW of ~20 kDa while PNGase enzyme appeared at ~36 kDa.
