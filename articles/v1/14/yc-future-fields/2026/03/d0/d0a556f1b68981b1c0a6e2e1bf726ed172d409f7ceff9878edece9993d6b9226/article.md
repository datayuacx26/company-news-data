# Scaling Cell and Gene Therapy Ancillary Materials with the EntoEngine **®**


Download case study


Quality and scale-up bottlenecks still exist for interleukins used as ancillary materials in cell and gene therapy development. This whitepaper highlights how our whole insect-based EntoEngine protein expression platform addresses the challenges with traditional systems with IL-2, IL-10, and IL-12 as model proteins.


### Common quality risks with traditional systems:


- Mammalian: High cost and risk of adventitious agents
- Bacterial: Risk of lipopolysaccharide (LPS) endotoxins
- Yeast: Hypermannosylation and immunogenic epitopes


**Our fruit fly-based bioreactor ensures that the interleukins produced today are identical to those produced years later.**


Offering product consistency and refining clonal validation documentation to build a stable foundation for long-term commercial supply of recombinant cytokines.
