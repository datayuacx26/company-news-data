## **TL;DR — Principles of a Great Returns Policy**


✅ Write for **customers, not lawyers**
✅ Make key info obvious: time windows, eligibility, and costs
✅ Default to **exchanges or store credit** where possible
✅ Keep the **returns portal visible and frictionless**
✅ Align the policy with **brand values** (sustainability, care, speed)
✅ Ensure **regional compliance** and keep content updated
✅ Structure for **AI visibility** : clear headings, FAQs, and concise steps


## **1. Write Your Policy for Customers**


Your returns policy should read like **instructions** , not a **contract** . Use short sentences, clear headings, and numbered steps.


**Include upfront:**


- Who qualifies and what’s eligible
- Return window and item condition
- Costs (shipping or restocking)
- Compensation options (exchange, credit, refund)
- How to start (link directly to your returns portal)


💡 *Pro tip:* Add a small box titled **“How Returns Work in 3 Steps”** near the top of your policy.


## **2. Don’t Omit the Essentials**


If you sell multiple product types, clarify exceptions (e.g., final sale or hygiene items). Define:


- **Time limits:** e.g. 30 days from delivery
- **Condition:** unworn, tags attached, original packaging
- **Shipping ownership:** who pays for return shipping
- **Processing times:** for refunds and exchanges
- **Regional rules:** e.g., EU 14-day withdrawal rights


🧭 *Clarity reduces pre-purchase friction and post-purchase support.*


## **3. Convert Returns into Exchanges & Store Credit**


Most fashion returns come from **fit or color** , not dissatisfaction. Help shoppers find their right size or variant instead of refunding.


**Design tips:**


- Promote **exchanges first** in the flow
- Offer **AI-powered size/fit suggestions** during return steps
- Provide **store credit** as a quick, flexible option
- Incentivize store credit with **bonus credit or free shipping**

## **5. Copy-Ready Policy Template (Fashion eCommerce)**


Use this structure as your foundation and tailor it to your brand tone and regions.


### **Overview**


We want you to love your purchase. If something isn’t right, returns are fast and simple.


**Return window:** 30 days from delivery
**Eligibility:** Unworn, tags attached, original packaging
**Options:** Exchange, store credit, or refund


### **What’s Eligible**


Items must:


- Be in original condition (unworn, unwashed)
- Include all packaging and accessories
- Be returned within the stated window


### **What’s Not Eligible**


- Final sale or customized items
- Hygiene-sensitive products
- Damaged or altered goods
- Hazardous materials


### **Fees and Shipping**


- **Exchanges:** free shipping
- **Store credit:** free or discounted shipping
- **Refunds:** return shipping deducted (where applicable)
- **International:** duties and taxes managed via the portal


### **How to Start a Return**


1. Go to the **returns portal** (link in email or footer)
2. Enter your order number and email
3. Choose: exchange, credit, or refund
4. Select pickup or drop-off
5. Print or scan the label

### **Exchanges**


Exchanges ship as soon as the original item is scanned by the carrier. If unavailable, we’ll issue store credit or a refund.
