# REVER is one of the 5 most innovative startups in Spain

The newspaper[El Referente](https://elreferente.es/ecosistema/bankflip-gana-los-premios-elreferente-2022/) has celebrated in the Impact Hub Madrid the III Edition of **El Referente 2022 Awards** ; a private event that brought together the finalist startups, the main Venture Capital of Spain and corporations, on the occasion of the Ranking of the best 100 startups in Spain 2022.According to **the main database of startups and investors in Spain: Ecosistema Startup** , which monitors more than 4,100 startups, 500 investors and more than 3,000 public investment operations, **Catalonia and Madrid** continue to be the main innovation hubs, with Saas, Health, Fintech and Foodtech sectors and technologies standing out.The sponsors of the El Referente Awards, including **Deloitte StartmeUP** and **Banco Sabadell's BStartup** , have rewarded **REVER for being part of the Top 5 most innovative startups in Spain between 2020 and 2022** .REVER was born a few months ago with the aim of simplifying and streamlining the returns process. Thanks to the simplicity of the interface, it only takes two clicks for customers to return their product. Besides, companies can create rules to automate returns, exchanges or refunds and build an efficient management with all the necessary information in one place.
