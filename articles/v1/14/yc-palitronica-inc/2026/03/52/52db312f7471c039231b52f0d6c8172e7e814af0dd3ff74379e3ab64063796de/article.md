# On-demand Webinar: The Hidden Risk That Can Crash Your Drone Manufacturing

In this webinar,


join Sebastian Fischmeister, pioneer in electronics integrity defense, and Seth Lloyd, Defense contractor advisor and U.S. Naval aviator, as they discuss the risks associated with COTS drones, and explore ways to secure rapid-delivery manufacturing.


Modern drone manufacturers are being asked to deliver commercial-scale volume at commodity prices, while still meeting mission-critical reliability and security requirements. The manufacturing systems behind most drone programs were never designed for this reality, and at scale they fail quietly and expensively.


This webinar breaks down why conventional approaches fail at scale, why COTS is still the only viable path forward, and how a new class of incoming inspection makes it possible to scale drone manufacturing without surprises, re-qualification churn, or unacceptable risk.
