# AI-Powered Firmware Assessment Without Source Code

By combining AI-powered fuzzing with power side-channel analysis,


**PowerFuzzer** detects undocumented functionality, hidden behaviour, firmware changes, and potential backdoors without requiring source code access. Rather than relying solely on what a device reports through its interfaces, PowerFuzzer observes how the firmware actually behaves, using power consumption as independent evidence of internal activity.


This enables organizations to validate supplier firmware, confirm software changes, assess compliance, and identify risks that conventional testing methods often miss.

##


**Trust Firmware You Didn’t Build**


Modern products increasingly depend on firmware developed by suppliers, partners, and third-party vendors. While these systems may function correctly, hidden commands, undocumented features, and uncommunicated software revisions can introduce operational, cybersecurity, and compliance risks.


PowerFuzzer provides a fully black-box approach to firmware assessment. It requires no source code, firmware image, or proprietary design information, making it ideal for validating supplier firmware, commercial-off-the-shelf products (COTS), and embedded systems where visibility is limited.


PowerFuzzer helps organizations confirm that firmware behaves as expected and identify changes before they become field failures, security incidents, or costly integration problems.


##


**Built for Deep Inspection Across Embedded Systems**


PowerFuzzer is designed for organizations that need greater confidence in the firmware powering critical products and infrastructure. It supports the assessment of embedded systems across aerospace, automotive, industrial, security, networking, and critical infrastructure applications.


Whether validating supplier firmware, searching for undocumented functionality, confirming compliance, or investigating unexpected system behaviour, PowerFuzzer provides a fast, repeatable way to uncover risks hidden beneath the software interface.
