# Atour Lifestyle Holdings Limited to Report Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Financial Results on March 17, 2026

[PDF Version](https://ir.yaduo.com/node/8176/pdf)

The Company will host a conference call at 7:00 AM U.S. Eastern time on Tuesday, March 17, 2026 (or 7:00 PM Beijing/Hong Kong time on the same day).
