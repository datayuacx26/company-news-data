# Product Update: September

## Wrapping up a summer of updates


Summer is officially over. While some of us will miss the warm temperatures in NYC, the rest of the team reminds us that Authzed is fully remote and we're free to move someplace warmer 🤣. Throughout the summer, we've accomplished a ton. Publicly, we've had quite a few large open source launches this summer. Privately, we've been laser-focused on maturing[SpiceDB Dedicated](https://docs.authzed.com/products/dedicated) : our product for private, fully-managed cloud deployments perfect for taking your permissions into production. But, all of that was not without some fun in the sun at our off-site in upstate New York.


Major highlights this summer include:


- SpiceDB v1.10 through v1.13 were released bringing[performance gains](https://twitter.com/jimmyzelinskie/status/1570527276634361856) and the[LookupSubjects API](https://authzed.com/blog/lookup-subjects/)
- The[SpiceDB Kubernetes Operator](https://github.com/authzed/spicedb-operator) is now open source
- Authzed[joined the CNCF](https://authzed.com/blog/cncf-membership/)
- The[Discord](https://authzed.com/discord) community has grown to over 700 people


ICYMI: in addition to posting[weekly blog posts](https://authzed.com/blog) , we're now officially posting monthly wrap-ups of events and new functionality we've added to Authzed. We're also giving everyone a little taste of our Slack's random channel and all of its off-topic goodness.


## Updates


### Open Source


- The[SpiceDB Kubernetes Operator](https://github.com/authzed/spicedb-operator) is now open source!
- Our library for building Kubernetes Operators[controller-idioms](https://github.com/authzed/controller-idioms) is now open source!
- Zed v0.5 through v0.7 were released adding the` --explain` flag and[LookupSubjects API](https://authzed.com/blog/lookup-subjects/)
- We consolidated various examples into an[examples GitHub repository](https://github.com/authzed/examples)
- We joined our friends at Polar Signals in[sponsoring open source developers](https://twitter.com/authzed/status/1562092590480216065) we depend on


### Authzed


- Authzed had a company-wide off-site in upstate New York!
- Authzed[joined the CNCF](https://authzed.com/blog/cncf-membership/)
- The[Playground is now powered by WASM](https://authzed.com/blog/some-assembly-required/) , decreasing latency by 90%
- We completely redesigned our[pricing page](https://authzed.com/pricing)


### Community


- We created an[awesome list](https://github.com/authzed/awesome-spicedb) thats projects in the community
- Jake presented SpiceDB's CRDB datastore at[RoachFest 2022](https://roachfest.cockroachlabs.com/)
- Jimmy gave a webinar on[Cloud Native Authorization](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BfMUB902t0) for the CNCF
