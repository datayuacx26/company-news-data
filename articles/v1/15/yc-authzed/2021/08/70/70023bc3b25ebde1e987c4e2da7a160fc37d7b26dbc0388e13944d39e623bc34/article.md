# Product Update: July

## To the First of Many 🥂


While you continue to wage the endless war for Inbox Zero, you'll now have a brief moment of rest every month while you check out the Authzed Product Update!


In addition to posting[weekly blog posts](https://authzed.com/blog) , we're now officially posting monthly wrap-ups of events and new functionality we've added to Authzed. We're also giving everyone a little taste of our Slack's random channel and all of it's off-topic goodness.


If you haven't signed up, you can find a sign-up form on the bottom of[authzed.com](https://authzed.com/) or you can visit the[subscribe page](http://eepurl.com/hEeb6z) .


## July Updates


- Joey announced a[new Schema Language](https://authzed.com/blog/defining-systems-lucidly/) that replaces Zanzibar's verbose and complex "Namespace Configs"
- [The Playground](https://play.authzed.com/) also got deep[semantic understanding](https://authzed.com/blog/syntax-and-semantics/) of the Schema Language and we covered how it catches any issues in your designs
- The website got a[new paint-job](https://authzed.com/) ! Huge thanks to[Kaori Ogawa](https://kaori.is/) for her design work
- Sam[teased](https://authzed.com/blog/zanzibar-implementations/) our Zanzibar round-up from Jake's[Papers We Love presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nbSbe3kw2U)
- We started[exploring](https://authzed.com/blog/what-to-build/)[Authzed Connect](https://authzed.com/connect/) -- the ability to check permissions against external sources like Stripe, Salesforce, GitHub, or your pre-existing Postgres database.
- Jake led our first[scheduled maintenance](https://status.authzed.com/incidents/h5d3zxd2b275) which went off without a hitch. It's not our first rodeo
- Jimmy cut the first release of a[Java client](https://github.com/authzed/authzed-java) for everyone stuck with JVM languages
- Joey cut the first release of a[NodeJS client](https://github.com/authzed/authzed-node) along with TypeScript headers
- [Lookup](https://docs.authzed.com/v0/api#aclservicelookup) found its way into all of the v0 client libraries
- We co-hosted a[developer happy hour](https://twitter.com/stephenaugustus/status/1420432444222808067) in NYC. Keep an eye on[Twitter](https://twitter.com/authzed) for future invites!
- We post our first[Product Update](https://authzed.com/blog/july-2021) . You're reading it now!
