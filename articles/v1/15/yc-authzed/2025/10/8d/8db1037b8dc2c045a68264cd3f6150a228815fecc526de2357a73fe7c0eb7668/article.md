# AuthZed Dedicated Now Available on Microsoft Azure

Today, AuthZed is announcing support for Microsoft Azure in AuthZed Dedicated to provide more authorization infrastructure deployment options for customers. AuthZed now provides customers the opportunity to choose from all major cloud providers - AWS, Google Cloud and/or Microsoft Azure.


AuthZed customers can now deploy authorization infrastructure to 23+ Azure regions to support their globally distributed applications. This ensures fast, consistent permission decisions regardless of where your users are located.


> "I have been following the development of SpiceDB and AuthZed on how they are providing authorization infrastructure to companies of all sizes," said Lachlan Evenson, Principal PDM Manager, Azure Cloud Native Ecosystem. "It's great to see their support for Microsoft Azure and we look forward to collaborating with AuthZed as they work with more Azure customers moving forward."


This launch is the direct result of customer demand. Many teams asked for Azure support, and now they have the ability to deploy authorization infrastructure in the cloud of their choice.


## What is AuthZed Dedicated?


[AuthZed Dedicated](https://authzed.com/products/authzed-dedicated) is our managed service that provides fully private deployments of our cloud platform in our customer’s provider and regions of choice. This gives users the benefits of a proven, production-ready authorization system—without the burden of building and maintaining it themselves.


Industry leaders such as[OpenAI](https://authzed.com/customers/openai) ,[Workday](https://authzed.com/customers/workday) , and[Turo](https://authzed.com/customers/turo) rely on AuthZed Dedicated for their authorization infrastructure:


> “We decided to buy instead of build early on. This is an authorization system with established patterns. We didn’t want to reinvent the wheel when we could move fast with a proven solution.” — Member of Technical Staff, OpenAI
