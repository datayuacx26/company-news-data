# SpiceDB Playground is Open Source

Play in your own SpiceDB Playground:[SpiceDB Playground](https://github.com/authzed/playground) is now open source and self-hostable. Dive into modeling permissions instantly - it’s the quickest way to start using[SpiceDB](https://authzed.com/spicedb) !


Every month thousands of users leverage the[AuthZed hosted Playground](https://play.authzed.com/) , which will continue to be available for those who want to build and share their schemas online.


## Quickest and easiest way to use SpiceDB


Whether you’re new to[SpiceDB](https://authzed.com/spicedb) or onboarding your dev team, the Playground is an excellent starting point. It allows you to build, test, and iterate modeling permissions non-destructively, with preloaded examples and relationship data to get you started.


### Rich schema and relationship editors


The Playground features a rich text editor with SpiceDB schema syntax highlighting and checking, alongside a relationship data editor with schema-aware autocomplete.

### Full SpiceDB and zed instances running via Wasm


The Playground runs[full instances of SpiceDB and zed](https://authzed.com/blog/some-assembly-required) , the SpiceDB CLI, via WebAssembly (Wasm). This ensures that authorization queries and validations run identically in the Playground as they do in a deployed SpiceDB instance, so you can have confidence in schemas designed in your Playground. Additionally, users have access to a fully-featured[zed](https://github.com/authzed/zed) CLI instance within the Playground, facilitating the testing of commands for scripting SpiceDB.


### Collaborative workspaces


Easily collaborate on permissions system designs by sharing exported Playground YAML files, which contain portable schema definitions and relationship data.


## Join and contribute
