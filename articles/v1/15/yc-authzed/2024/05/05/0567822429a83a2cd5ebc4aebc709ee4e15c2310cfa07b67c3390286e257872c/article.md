# Winning with AI podcast episode: Entrepreneurship, AI and Authzed with Jacob Moshenko

Here are the key takeaways for experienced software developers:


- SpiceDB is a permissions system used to manage access control in complex applications. It is inspired by Google Zanzibar, the system Google uses to manage permissions internally.
- AI is not a silver bullet for solving permission problems. While AI can be helpful for some tasks, such as summarizing data or writing code, it is not reliable enough to make access control decisions.
