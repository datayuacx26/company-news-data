# User Permissions at Scale

AuthZed's CEO and co-founder Jake Moshenko joined the Partially Redacted podcast. The video dives into:


- The challenges of traditional permission models at scale.
- AuthZed's approach, leveraging a relationship-based model and schema language for flexible permission management.
- Benefits, including cross-product integration, scalability, and efficient permission lookups.
- Deployment options for AuthZed, including open-source and hosted solutions.
- Security considerations and OED's commitment to data privacy and compliance.


If you are looking to streamline user permissions and enhance access control, this video offers valuable insights and potential solutions.
