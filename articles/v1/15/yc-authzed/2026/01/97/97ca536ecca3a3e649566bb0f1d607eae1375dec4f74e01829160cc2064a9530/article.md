# Try Out AuthZed Cloud, Get AuthZed Cloud Credits

When we launched AuthZed Cloud, we opened the doors for organizations of all sizes to have access to scalable authorization. Now with the AuthZed Cloud Starter Program, we are providing individuals at organizations with a $700 credit to try out AuthZed Cloud and see the value of a scalable authorization infrastructure solution. This credit is enough to fund weeks of authorization!


[Apply for the Starter Program](https://authzed.com/cloud/starter-program) to get your credits.


Building a permissions system that doesn't slow down as you grow is incredibly hard. Many companies start with simple role-based authorization only to discover that they hit a wall as their products evolve to require more fine-grained authorization logic.


AuthZed Cloud is the fast path to implementing the same authorization technology used by giants like OpenAI. With the trusted, open source SpiceDB as the technology at the core, AuthZed Cloud is a managed service that handles the work of authorization infrastructure and monitoring so developers can focus on their own products.


With the flip of the calendar year we want to make it even easier for developers to try AuthZed Cloud. The Starter Program gives you 30 days and $700 in credits—enough runway to build a real proof-of-concept, not just kick the tires.


## What Current Customers Say


Current AuthZed Cloud customers say:


- They like that they can deploy a permissions system or a SpiceDB cluster in minutes and delete it as soon as they don't need it anymore. Many current customers have multiple development or test deployments running at various times.
- They love that we let them consume observability data in the context that matters to them, from built-in dashboards on the Metrics page to an official integration with Datadog.
- They like that scale is not a problem they have to solve. AuthZed Cloud is ready to scale as their products scale.


## Flexible Usage


AuthZed Cloud is a pay-as-you-go service with no up-front commitment and no termination fees. The Starter Program credits can go toward anything you like. You could deploy a small development permissions system in order to build a proof-of-concept with your team, or you could go as far as launching a production environment. Or you could do both! AuthZed Cloud lets you control what you provision and where, how you scale, and who you invite to join your project.
