# Voice agents need browser agents

Voice agents are the future of front office automation. From healthcare clinics to supply chain logistics, companies are embracing voice AI agents to gather data and process customer requests more efficiently than ever before.


But there is a big problem. After the voice agent hangs up, they can’t integrate into the back-office systems. Companies are discovering their clients’ CRMs, EHRs, and other legacy platforms were never designed for this. Either no API is available, or getting a new endpoint built is an expensive, months-long process with external vendors.


This creates a difficult choice: delay deals and burden your clients’ engineering team, or hire humans for manual data entry, hamstringing growth and defeating the point of automation.


> 💡 Asteroid offers a third, better option. Browser AI agents that connect voice agent outputs to legacy systems in days, not months.
