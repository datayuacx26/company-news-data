# How Meshed cut insurance quoting time by 93% with Asteroid's agents

## About Meshed


As a leading business insurance broker, Meshed is revolutionising the industry by using AI to find the best policies, reduce fees, and keep their customers covered in real-time. Their core challenge was scaling their quoting process.


To provide the best value, Meshed gets quotes from hundreds of insurance provider portals, but many of them lack APIs. This was forcing their team into tedious, manual data entry.


Meshed needed a solution that could reliably and automatically fill out complex quoting forms across dozens of unique platforms. **Asteroid’s browser agents were the perfect solution.**


## The Automated Workflow in Action


Meshed has now deployed Asteroid’s browser agents across 50 different insurance platforms. The process is seamless:


- Meshed automatically triggers an Asteroid agent via API whenever a new quote is required.
- The agent securely logs into the provider portals, navigates the complex multi-page forms, and intelligently fills in all the required data from Meshed’s existing systems.
- After completing the form, the agent extracts the results, downloads all necessary documents (like quote PDFs), and feeds the structured data directly back into the Meshed platform.
- Throughout the process, a Slack bot keeps the Meshed team informed of the progress and escalates any unexpected issues for human review, giving complete oversight.


## What Made Asteroid the Perfect Solution


Asteroid has solved Meshed’s most significant challenges with minimal overhead:


- **Automation without APIs:** Asteroid’s agents operate directly in the browser, meaning Meshed can automate quoting on legacy carrier portals that offered no API access.
- **No Data Overhaul Needed:** Asteroid’s agents worked with Meshed’s existing unstructured data, intelligently mapping it to the required form fields. This dramatically reduced the engineering time needed to get to production.
- **Trust and Transparency:** Every agent execution is recorded and evaluated. This, combined with Asteroid’s guardrails, gives the confidence and transparency needed to rely on the automations for mission-critical workflows.
- **Built for Rapid Scale:** The agentic, graph-based nature of Asteroid’s platform means Meshed can rapidly update workflows or expand to new insurance products. They are now able to deploy production-ready agents in days, not months.


## Results


By partnering with Asteroid, Meshed were able to rapidly automate their most critical workflows, unlocking immense value for their customers at a fraction of the cost and time of other solutions.
