# How to Share Your Claude Artifact Game

## Why can't I just send my Claude artifact link?


Claude's artifact sharing has limitations. When you share a Claude conversation, the other person needs a Claude account. The artifact might not render correctly. And you can't track plays or add features like leaderboards.


For a game you want friends to actually play, you need to host it somewhere permanent.

The fastest way to get a shareable link for your Claude artifact game:


1. **Copy your artifact code:** In Claude, click the code icon or "Copy code" on your artifact

## Method 2: Share Claude Conversation Link


You can share your Claude conversation directly, but there are limitations:


1. Click the share button in your Claude chat
2. Copy the conversation link
3. Send to friends


**Downsides:** Recipients may need a Claude account. Artifacts don't always render. No play tracking. Can't add features.


## Method 3: Download and Self-Host


For technical users who want full control:


1. Copy the HTML code from your artifact
2. Save as an` index.html` file
3. Upload to Netlify, Vercel, or GitHub Pages


**Downsides:** Requires technical knowledge. No built-in features. More setup time.
