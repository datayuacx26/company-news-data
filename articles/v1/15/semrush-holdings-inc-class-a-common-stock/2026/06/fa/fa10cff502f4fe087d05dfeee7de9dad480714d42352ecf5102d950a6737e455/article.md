# Semrush’s New Traffic Insights Explains Changes In Competitor Traffic Performance

06/11/26


Semrush’s New Traffic Insights Explains Changes In Competitor Traffic Performance


New AI-powered Traffic Insights in Traffic Analytics help teams identify competitor traffic spikes and drops, understand the drivers, and act faster.


Semrush, an Adobe company, today announced the launch of Traffic Insights in the Traffic Analytics dashboard, within the Traffic & Market Toolkit. Traffic Insights gives marketers, agencies, and competitive intelligence teams a faster way to understand why traffic has increased or decreased across the competitors they track.


By bringing AI-powered explanations directly into Traffic Analytics and automated email alerts, Traffic Insights helps teams spot meaningful traffic changes, understand the likely reasons, and move from detection to action faster.


Spot The Shift. Understand The Cause.


The Traffic Insights feature streamlines competitive analysis by helping users:


- Identify significant spikes and drops directly in Traffic Analytics.
- View AI-powered explanations in traffic trend graphs and set automated email alerts on traffic shifts.
- Understand likely drivers, including seasonality, traffic channel shifts, regional changes, and demand signals.
- Focus on anomalies that require action, not every minor movement in the data.


This gives teams the context they need at the moment they discover a change in their competitor’s performance, to quickly understand why it may have happened and decide what to do next.


Built For Teams That Need Sharper Signals And Faster Reactions


Traffic Insights gives different teams a faster way to read competitor movement and act on traffic changes.


- **Marketing teams** can spot competitor surges or slowdowns, then investigate the channels, regions, seasonality, or keyword signals behind the shift.
- **Agencies** can track clients and competitor sets at scale, with alerts that surface the changes worth investigating.
- **Leadership teams** can catch early signs of competitor growth or decline before market shifts become blind spots.
- **Sales enablement teams** can turn competitor movement into timely, data-backed talking points for prospects and accounts.


By pairing traffic alerts with AI-powered context, Semrush helps teams move from, “What changed?” to “What should we do next?”


Traffic Insights is now included in the Semrush Traffic & Market subscription and ready to activate today.
