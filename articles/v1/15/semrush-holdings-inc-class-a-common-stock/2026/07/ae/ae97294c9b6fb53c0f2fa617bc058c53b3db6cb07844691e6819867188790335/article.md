# Digital PR for AI visibility: 5 tactics + how to measure

Digital public relations (PR) helps improve AI visibility by building the internet’s entire record of your brand, which AI search platforms rely on to learn about you.


These platforms don’t just rely on what your site says about your brand. That’s because these claims, on their own, can seem unverified or self-serving. But they become more worthy of mentions when trusted third-party sources corroborate them.


Investing in digital PR is increasingly important as AI search platforms replace Google as people’s first stop for answers. Here’s the proof that makes digital PR a viable AI visibility strategy, and five tactics to apply to start growing your brand’s AI mentions and citations today.


## AI search platforms read the internet to learn about you, not just your site


AI search platforms read the entire internet to learn about you, not just your site. The greater the number of independent sources that back up your site’s claims, the more likely these platforms are to mention them in responses. Your website may also become an AI citation source if third-party sites link to it, increasing its discoverability and authority.


For example, if your latest product launch generates coverage and backlinks from multiple outlets, AI search platforms are more likely to mention it. This is because the third-party coverage indicates the launch is a verifiable, notable event. Some platforms may even cite your website as the information’s source alongside third-party ones.


Two factors make a source especially credible to AI systems:


- **Independence from the brand they describe** : A claim about a brand made by a third-party source with little stake in the brand’s success holds more weight than an identical claim the brand makes on its own site
- **Authoritativeness** : Claims from authoritative sources are seen as deserving of higher weight because their information is often fact-checked and published by organizations with reputations to protect


A[digital PR](https://www.semrush.com/blog/digital-pr/) strategy that gets authoritative third-party sources to mention your brand can thus do more than increase intangible brand awareness. It also builds the external evidence layer that AI search platforms use to understand and validate brands, translating into[AI mentions](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-mentions/) and citations, plus other measurable visibility gains.


## The data that makes digital PR a viable AI visibility strategy

Take Patagonia. The outdoor clothing brand has over 87,000 AI mentions across platforms in the United States, according to our[AI Visibility Index 2026](https://ai-visibility-index.semrush.com/downloads/ai-visibility-index-2026-study-h1) .

Similarly, Shopify has earned over 45,000 AI mentions across platforms. This strong visibility is the result of the ecommerce platform earning a significant volume of mentions on G2 (1,100+ mentions), Capterra (800+ mentions), Reddit (44,500+ mentions), LinkedIn (15,400+ mentions), and other third-party sites, per the same report.


Earning mentions on the sources AI search platforms already favor is thus a clear path to stronger AI visibility for your brand.


## Where AI goes to decide who gets cited


Since not all third-party sources carry the same weight, your PR targets should match the ones AI search platforms prefer to rely on. Here are the sources these platforms typically use to decide which brands to mention and cite:


- **Encyclopedic/reference sources** like Wikipedia, Wikidata, and Crunchbase
- **Tier-one editorial and trade press** like Forbes, Reuters, TechCrunch, and industry-specific publications, e.g., Modern Healthcare for the healthcare industry

## Five PR moves that build AI visibility


Use these five PR moves to grow your brand’s third-party record and build AI visibility at scale:


### 1. Original research: Turn proprietary data into publisher citations


Publish proprietary data that publishers build stories around and cite. When publishers reference and link to the research’s source page on your site, the page gains authority, which makes AI search platforms more likely to mention and cite it.


For example, Houzz published a[2026 U.S. renovation trends study](https://www.houzz.com/magazine/2026-u-s-houzz-and-home-study-renovation-trends-stsetivw-vs~185090855) that’s gotten over 180 backlinks from 81 referring domains, according to Semrush’s[Backlink Analytics](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/backlinks/) tool:


AI search platforms have also cited the study’s source page on Houzz in more than 180 prompts, according to Semrush’s[AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/) .


To turn proprietary data into publisher citations, create a research benchmark, survey, index, or trend report relevant to publishers’ beats. For instance, if your brand sells mattresses and you’re targeting citations from healthcare publications, you could publish a survey on your customers’ sleep patterns.


Next, promote your research so publishers actually see and cite it. Share it on social media, and email it to publisher contacts you already have relationships with.


Aim to publish new proprietary data every quarter. The more data you publish, the more publisher coverage and citations you might earn, driving higher AI visibility.


### 2. Third-party roundup placement: Get mentioned in the lists AI already uses


Get mentioned in the product comparisons and roundup lists that AI search platforms cite. AI already trusts these pages, which means your brand is more likely to earn AI mentions if it’s featured on them.


Product comparisons are pages that evaluate the pros and cons of two or more products (e.g., “QuickBooks Online vs Xero”). Roundup lists are pages that cover the best products in a category (e.g., “the best accounting software”) or alternatives to a product (e.g., “alternatives to QuickBooks Online”).


AI search platforms tend to cite both types of pages when users ask for brand recommendations. For example, ChatGPT cited Fit Small Business’s roundup list in response to the prompt “Which is the best accounting software for small businesses in the US?”:


In fact, ChatGPT cited this third-party list not once, but **six** times. And it didn’t cite the websites of its recommended brands at all.


To get cited in the third-party product comparisons and roundup lists AI search platforms cite for prompts relevant to your brand, you first need to learn what these pages are. Use a tool like Semrush’s[Prompt Research](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/prompt-research/) to help.


Enter a topic relevant to your brand into the tool, and then click “ **Analyze** .”


Scroll down to the “Related topics” section and click “ **Prompts** .” Next, enter one of these words into the “ **Filter by prompt** ” search bar:


- **“vs”** : to filter for product comparisons. To filter more specifically for product comparisons between your brand and a competitor, enter your brand’s name as well (e.g., “\[your brand’s name\] vs”).
- **“best”** : to filter for roundup lists for the best products in a certain category
- **“alternatives”** : to filter for roundup lists for alternatives to a product. To find pages that could feature your brand as an alternative to a competitor, add your competitor’s name to the filter (e.g., “\[competitor’s name\] alternatives”).


Click the magnifying glass icon next to the search bar to apply the filter.


Click “ **View full response** ” next to any prompt to see the response’s[AI citations](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-citations/) .


Shortlist third-party pages where your brand could be a relevant addition. Then, reach out to their publishers about mentioning your brand on them.


Don’t replace third-party validation of your brand with self-serving content on your site. For example, by publishing a roundup list that ranks your product as the best, followed by your competitors.


This is because it’s the independence of third-party sources that makes their recommendations more credible and thus more worthy of AI citations.


Publishing self-serving content might also inadvertently give your competitors more AI visibility than you. Google’s AI Overviews have been shown to cite a brand’s own comparison page or roundup list as a source, but recommend a competitor named within it instead,[according to Lily Ray, Vice President of SEO & AI Search at Amsive](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/why-calling-yourself-the-best-could) .


### 3. Expert quotes: Get into cited industry coverage


Get quoted as an expert in industry coverage that AI search platforms cite. They treat this coverage as a signal of authority and may be more likely to mention you as a result.


Contributing expert quotes also helps these platforms learn about and mention your brand’s expertise to users, just like how Claude quotes Chef Mike Saperstein’s insights on searing meat:


To maximize AI visibility, prioritize contributing expert quotes to journalists and publications that already appear as AI citations. Semrush’s[Prompt Research](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/prompt-research/) helps you view these AI citation sources for prompts relevant to your brand.


Alternatively, use expert-sourcing platforms like HARO to connect with journalists actively seeking quotes.


When responding to quote requests, have relevant subject matter experts from your business provide clear perspectives and supporting data. Generic commentary is less useful — and less quotable — than specific, evidence-backed insights.


### 4. Review platform presence: Build corroboration beyond editorial coverage


Build a presence on review platforms to help AI search platforms corroborate your brand’s positioning with what real users say about it. This independent corroboration, alongside other factors like brand authority and content quality, may help these platforms mention your brand more often.


For example, software categories with 10% more reviews on review platform G2 tend to have about 2% more AI citations,[according to an analysis by Kevin Indig, Growth Advisor and author of Growth Memo, published by G2](https://learn.g2.com/do-more-g2-reviews-mean-more-ai-visibility) . Claiming a Trustpilot review profile can also increase AI citation rates from 1% to 54%,[as a Trustpilot and Seer Interactive study](https://business.trustpilot.com/marketing/what-ai-says-about-your-brand) found.


Build a presence on review platforms that customers and AI search platforms use to research brands in your industry. For example, G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot are relevant review platforms for software products, while Yelp, Tripadvisor, and OpenTable are relevant for restaurants.


To find review platforms relevant to your specific brand, use this prompt in ChatGPT, Claude, or other AI platform: “What do customers say about \[brand\]? Cite your sources.”


If your brand’s online presence is low, use a close competitor’s name in the prompt instead.


In the example below, ChatGPT heavily cited G2 while answering a prompt about customer reviews of Descript. So, if we were handling Descript’s digital PR, we’d focus on improving the brand’s presence on G2, among other review platforms.


After shortlisting review platforms, create listings on each of them if you haven’t already. Check that your brand’s positioning on these listings matches that on your site to communicate a consistent picture of your brand to users and AI search platforms.


If your brand operates physical stores, register[Google Business Profiles](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-my-business/) (GBPs) for each store as well, because these profiles also display reviews. From there, consider connecting your GBPs to Semrush’s[Listing Management](https://www.semrush.com/listings-management/) to automatically create and update listings on multiple other review platforms.


Once your listings are up, work on improving their review volume and recency, and the quality of your responses to reviews. Review data signals user sentiment toward your brand, which influences whether and how AI search platforms describe it to users.


For example, get more reviews by sending automated post-purchase emails that ask for reviews, or creating QR codes for customers to scan to leave reviews.


### 5. Entity clarity: Reinforce core facts about your brand


Work to make your brand’s core facts consistent across your site and authoritative third-party sources. Doing this helps AI search platforms recognize your brand as a standalone entity with certain distinguishable characteristics. They may then mention it more often.


Audit your brand’s information on authoritative third-party sources AI systems routinely rely on for information. They include:


- Wikipedia
- Wikidata
- Crunchbase
- Google Business Profile
- LinkedIn
- YouTube
- Review platforms like G2, Yelp, and Tripadvisor


These sources should all communicate the same essential facts about your brand, including its name, category, founding date, and product descriptions. They should also corroborate key claims, like whether your brand offers money-back guarantees.


Edit your brand’s information on these sources if they’re inaccurate or missing certain details. And update them whenever your brand’s information changes.


## How to measure whether your PR is moving AI visibility


Focus on metrics like citation lift, AI share of voice, and competitive citation displacement to[measure whether your PR efforts move AI visibility](https://www.semrush.com/blog/measure-ai-visibility/) instead of just placements.


Here’s what each metric means:


- **Citation lift** : The percentage increase in AI citations of your brand across AI search platforms over time
- **AI share of voice** : The percentage of AI mentions of your brand in answers to non-branded prompts compared to competitors. Avoid tracking individual prompts. The phrasing variations are endless, so tracking each one is futile. It’s more useful to track your brand’s aggregate visibility across a broader[prompt portfolio](https://www.semrush.com/blog/prompt-tracking/) instead.
- **Competitive citation displacement** : Changes to the positions of AI citations of your brand and competitors

From there, scroll down and click “ **Cited Pages** ” to view which of your site’s pages have earned AI citations. Click any page to see the prompt and AI response that led to the citation.

## FAQs


### Does digital PR get AI to cite my website, or just mention my brand?


Digital PR primarily helps with getting AI search platforms to mention your brand because these platforms favor surfacing independently corroborated claims. But digital PR can also increase the likelihood of these platforms citing your brand if the third-party coverage links to your pages, improving their discoverability and authority.


### How long does digital PR take to show up in AI answers?


Digital PR might take anywhere from days to months to influence AI mentions and citations. AI search platforms that conduct live web searches may mention and cite new content within days of search engines indexing it. Platforms that rely solely on training data may update their mentions only when the companies behind them release new AI models.
