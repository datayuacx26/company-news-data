# The 7 best AI visibility tools to win AI search in 2026

They show where you're mentioned, where competitors outrank you, and which content gaps to close.


Choosing the right one helps you avoid common pitfalls, like data overload, misleading signals, or investing in tools that don’t match your team’s goals, scale, or ability to act.


This guide covers seven AI search monitoring tools tested against real AI search workflows. Rather than being another generic AI visibility tools comparison, we evaluated each platform based on where it excels, where it falls short, and which teams it's best suited for.

## How to choose the best AI visibility tool


Most teams don't need the most powerful AI search visibility tools. They need the one that fits their existing stack, matches their team size, and supports how they actually work.


Before you make a decision, answer three questions:


1. **Does it fit your current stack?** If you already use a platform like Semrush for SEO, adding a standalone AI visibility tool means a separate budget, onboarding, and reporting format. Tools that integrate with your existing workflow and offer[SEO + AI search reporting](https://www.semrush.com/blog/create-ai-seo-search-marketing-report/) reduce friction.
2. **How much data does your team actually need?** Smaller teams can get useful signals from lightweight tools like the[Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/overview/) or Otterly AI. Larger brands operating across multiple markets need granular tracking by region, product, and persona — tools like[Semrush’s Enterprise AIO](https://enterprise.semrush.com/solutions/ai-optimization/) and Botify are built for that complexity.
3. **Do you need monitoring, execution, or both?** Some tools only track visibility. Others let you act on[AI visibility gaps](https://www.semrush.com/blog/find-ai-visibility-gaps-with-semrush/) directly — finding topic ideas, getting recommendations, creating content, identifying outreach targets, or deploying technical fixes. If you have writers and strategists ready to execute, monitoring is enough. If you need help going from insight to action, choose a tool with built-in content or optimization workflows.


Some platforms offer free trials or free tiers, while others require a demo call or paid subscription before you can test the product. If hands-on evaluation is important to your team, trial availability should also be part of your decision-making process.

## FAQs


### What is the best AI visibility tool for small businesses?


The best AI visibility tools for most small businesses are Semrush and Otterly AI.


The Semrush AI Visibility toolkit combines AI visibility tracking with SEO, competitor research, and content optimization, while Otterly AI offers a lower-cost entry point for monitoring mentions, citations, and visibility trends.


The best choice depends on whether you need a complete AI visibility platform or a lightweight monitoring tool.


### What platforms do AI visibility tools track?


AI visibility tools track some combination of these platforms, depending on the tool:


- ChatGPT
- Google AI Overviews
- Google AI Mode
- Gemini
- Perplexity
- Claude
- Microsoft Copilot
- Grok
- DeepSeek


Enterprise platforms typically offer the broadest coverage, while lower-tier plans may only include a subset of these platforms.


### Do AI visibility tools require a free trial before buying?


No, AI visibility tools don't require a free trial before buying, but availability varies by platform.


Some platforms offer free trials, free audits, or free tiers, while others require a paid subscription or demo before you can access the product.


For example, Peec AI offers a free trial on select plans, while Athena and Profound currently do not offer free trials.


### How is AI visibility different from SEO?


AI visibility is different from SEO in what it measures. SEO focuses on improving visibility in traditional search, while AI visibility focuses on how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers from platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode.


The two are closely related because many of the signals that influence search rankings also affect AI visibility, but they measure different outcomes.


### What is the cheapest AI visibility tool?


Based on publicly available pricing, Otterly AI currently offers one of the cheapest AI visibility tools, with plans starting at $29 per month.


However, pricing changes frequently, so it's always worth checking the vendor's website before making a decision.


### What is the best AI search monitoring tool?


The best AI search monitoring tool depends on your goals. If you want AI visibility tracking combined with SEO and content insights, Semrush is a strong choice.


If you need analyst-focused reporting and raw data exports, Profound may be a better fit. If your priority is content creation and execution, Writesonic combines monitoring and content workflows in one platform.
