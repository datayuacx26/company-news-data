### 2. Otterly: Best for prompt-specific GEO tracking


[Otterly.AI](http://otterly.ai/) monitors your AI search performance across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, and Gemini.


It works especially well for teams focused on prompt-level performance and query tracking.


You can also track how your visibility compares against competitors, including overall AI coverage, brand mentions, and average position.


This gives you a quick view of which brands appear most often and where your own visibility is falling behind.


Otterly also detects when AI platforms cite your content with links.


For example, if ChatGPT recommends one of your guides when answering an industry-related question, you can see the cited page and analyze which content is earning visibility.


Just keep in mind that all[Otterly.AI](http://otterly.ai/) plans include coverage across four AI platforms.


Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Mode require paid add-ons, so costs can increase quickly if you need broad AI platform coverage.

### 4. Profound: Best for real-user AI prompt intelligence


[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/) stands out by helping teams choose prompts based on what people actually ask AI platforms.


Its Prompt Volumes data is built from anonymized conversations licensed from double-opt-in consumer panels, then modeled to reveal demand and intent.


This gives marketers a stronger starting point than simply guessing which prompts their audience might use.


Profound runs selected prompts daily through consumer-facing AI platforms rather than APIs, showing how your brand appears and which sources drive weak or inaccurate coverage.


The platform also helps teams act on those findings.


Its Agents turn citation gaps into content drafts and optimization recommendations, while Agent Analytics tracks crawler access and AI-referred traffic.


For retailers, the Enterprise plan adds ChatGPT Shopping analysis covering product visibility, descriptions, and merchant checkout links.


That said, the entry-level plan is considerably narrower than the broader platform. It tracks only 50 prompts in ChatGPT, while full Prompt Volumes searches and ChatGPT Shopping require an Enterprise plan.


### Top features


- Prompt research grounded in anonymized real-user AI conversations
- Daily browser-captured tracking across AI platforms
- Citation analysis and inaccurate-claim detection
- Content creation and optimization based on visibility gaps
- AI crawler monitoring and traffic attribution
- ChatGPT Shopping analysis for Enterprise customers


### Pricing


Profound plans start at $99/month for brands, with separate options for agencies and custom-priced Enterprise plans.


Higher plans expand prompt and answer engine coverage and add capabilities like ChatGPT Shopping, data exports, and enterprise support.

## FAQs


### How to choose a generative engine optimization tool


To choose the best generative engine optimization tool, you should pick the tool that aligns most with your goals, whether that’s driving more traffic from AI platforms, monitoring brand sentiment, or understanding how competitors appear in AI-generated answers.


The best solution should go beyond basic visibility tracking and help you take action on the insights it provides.


Here’s what to look for:


- Visibility insights that clearly show how your brand appears across AI platforms
- Competitive analysis to understand where you lead or lag behind competitors
- Recommendations that help improve content, positioning, or brand visibility
- Reporting and collaboration features that match your team's needs


### What is the best free GEO tool?


Most GEO tools are paid products, but several offer free trials or free entry points.


For example, Scrunch offers a 7-day free trial and XFunnel provides a free AI search audit.


Semrush also offers a[7-day free trial](https://www.semrush.com/semrush-free-trial/) which includes both Semrush’s[AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/ai-seo/) and[SEO Toolkit.](https://www.semrush.com/seo/)


If you're evaluating GEO software, free trials are often the best way to compare visibility tracking, prompt monitoring, and reporting features before committing to a paid plan.

The platform helps teams understand how AI platforms portray their brand and unique product lines, compare performance against competitors, and identify opportunities to improve visibility across AI-generated answers. Plus, it comes with more dedicated support and expert guidance to help enterprise teams get started with generative engine optimization.


### What is the best GEO tool for tracking AI brand visibility?


Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit is one of the strongest options for tracking how AI platforms describe your brand.


It helps you understand how your brand is positioned across platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode, while also surfacing recommendations to improve visibility and perception.


### What’s the difference between GEO tools and LLM monitoring tools?


LLM monitoring tools primarily track how your brand appears in AI-generated answers. They measure metrics such as mentions, citations, sentiment, share of voice, and competitor visibility across platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.


Whereas, GEO tools typically offer these monitoring capabilities alongside features that help you improve your visibility. These may include prompt research, content recommendations, technical audits, citation analysis, and content optimization workflows.


The terms often overlap because many GEO tools include LLM monitoring.
