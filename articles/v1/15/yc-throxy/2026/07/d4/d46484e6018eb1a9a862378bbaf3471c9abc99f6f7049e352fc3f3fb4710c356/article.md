# Throxy launches "Inside The Pipeline" podcast! - Throxy

Throxy has officially launched a podcast! "Inside The Pipeline" Episode 1 went live on Thursday, November 27th.


While the business podcast space is undeniably crowded, with over 14,400 active podcasts in English-speaking countries alone, throxy is taking its own approach to cut through the noise. While eating our guests’ favourite takeaway, Inside The Pipeline goes deep on GTM strategy, entrepreneurship and investing in 2025.


The inaugural episode features Will Bennett from Seedcamp, whose background offers a unique dual perspective that's rare in the industry. Will’s experience spans both sides of the startup ecosystem. He was an operator at Fuse Energy (which recently achieved a £5 billion valuation) before transitioning to investing at Seedcamp. Even though early in his career, the combination of being both a builder and a backer provides insights that many industry voices don't possess.


The conversation with Will explores current growth opportunities, particularly in deep tech, patterns he's observed across founders, and some spontaneous moments that takes the conversation in a slightly different direction…


**Where to find the podcast?**


The full episodes are available on Spotify and Apple Podcasts, while short form clips are being distributed across LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook. We encourage audience participation - feel free to comment and make your voice heard!
