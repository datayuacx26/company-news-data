# The press on H2 building with Brickanta

#### Article


The partnership between Brickanta and H2 is an example of AI entering Swedish construction companies' decision-making, in an industry where much work is still done manually.


Construction firm H2, now with billion-kronor revenue, is adopting AI company Brickanta's platform early in its construction process. The platform is meant to support early project decisions, analyzing materials and flagging things that could otherwise take days to find manually.
