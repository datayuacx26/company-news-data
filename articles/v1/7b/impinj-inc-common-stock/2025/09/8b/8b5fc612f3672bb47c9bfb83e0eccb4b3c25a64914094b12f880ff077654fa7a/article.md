# Global Partners Select Impinj R700 Series RAIN RFID Readers to Solve Complex Enterprise Operational Challenges Across Industries


## Enterprise-grade solutions combine R700 series readers with tags based on Impinj M800 series endpoint ICs to unlock the full benefits of autonomous RAIN RFID reading


From retailers and food suppliers to healthcare facilities and logistics hubs, today’s global enterprises face daily supply chain challenges – including mounting pressure to improve operational efficiency and speed. Increasingly, innovative enterprises are addressing these challenges with next-generation supply chain integrity solutions, enabled through autonomous RAIN RFID—systems that automatically capture item data without human scanning or manual intervention—unlocking the power of scalable real-time item intelligence. For example,[UPS has publicly touted](https://www.impinj.com/library/Blog/with-rain-rfid-packaging-and-shipping-are-faster-a) that autonomous RFID reading enables the shipping and logistics giant to eliminate 20 million manual package scans daily. And Fast Retailing added autonomous RAIN RFID readers to its stores to power the[automated self-checkout kiosks used by Uniqlo](https://www.impinj.com/library/Blog/How-Uniqlo-Tracks-Style-and-Savings-with-RAIN-RFID) .


Designed to support RAIN RFID solutions that need industry-leading performance and enterprise-grade reliability,[Impinj R700 series readers](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Readers/Impinj-R700) are at the center of this transformation. When combined with tags based on[Impinj M800 endpoint ICs](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Tag-Chips/Impinj-M800-Series) with built-in support for[Gen2X](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Technology/Gen2x) , solution partners are creating powerful[autonomous reading solutions](https://www.impinj.com/library/Blog/Harness-the-Power-of-Data-with-Autonomous-RAIN-RFI) .


Impinj partners are developing and deploying R700 autonomous reading solutions to drive efficiency and enhance customer experience: wirelessly tracking shipments, ensuring food arrives fresh and on time, securing expensive medical implants, automating shipment verification at warehouse dock doors and on conveyors, and powering next-generation biotech asset tracking for labs. Across all of these use cases and many more, global enterprises demand trusted solutions – Impinj R700 readers rise to every challenge.


“Our R700-based supply chain visibility solutions, combined with SLS tags based on Impinj endpoint ICs, are allowing enterprises to reduce mis-shipments, chargebacks, and inventory write-offs, while realizing significant productivity gains,” said SLS General Manager Rich Nemesi. “We’ve partnered closely with Impinj from Day 1 to continuously innovate new solutions to the toughest supply chain challenges by delivering the enterprise-grade performance and reliability our customers demand.”


### Transforming Industries with Proven Use Cases


Autonomous RAIN RFID reading solutions built with R700 series readers are helping unlock the power of real-time data to optimize efficiency, speed, and customer experiences across many of the world’s most critical industries:


- **Food & Grocery:** PLM TrustLink and Avery Dennison leverage R700 readers to provide traceability from farm to customer, reducing waste and ensuring perishables reach destinations on time. “Impinj is a world-class provider of RAIN RFID tag chips and RAIN RFID readers,” explained PLM Technical Solution Consultant Levi Harris, noting that Impinj’s solutions “are certified and tuned to work in concert with PLM TrustLink.”
- **Logistics & Retail:** Clustag’s RFID MOT Station uses R700 readers to verify shipments at scale with near-100% accuracy, ensuring the speed and precision needed for high-efficiency omnichannel fulfillment. Thanks to RAIN RFID, the tunnel system can read and validate the item contents of boxes — up to 400 tags per box at speeds of up to 1,400 boxes per hour, or up to 560,000 tags per hour, improving shipping accuracy by up to 100%, reducing operating time by up to 40%, and increasing carton throughput.
- **Supply Chain & Warehousing:** Smart Label Solutions (SLS) integrates R700 readers with its D-Series™ dock door solutions, automating shipment verification. “The best-in-class R700 performance enables our D-Series dock door solution to deliver industry-leading accuracy and fast event alarming by allowing us to better control the read zone,” noted Matt Smith, SLS Senior Director of Engineering.
- **Healthcare:** RFiD Discovery uses R700-powered smart cabinets to safeguard surgical implants, ensuring critical devices are available wherever and whenever they are needed, improving patient health, safety, and operational efficiency. “The Impinj R700 was the natural choice for our solution’s smart cabinet,” said RFiD Discovery’s Lead Consultant for Healthcare Simon Dawkins, citing R700’s “flexibility to allow our software to be installed directly onto the reader.”
- **Biotech & Pharmaceuticals:** AssetPulse’s AssetGather solution relies on R700 readers to track vital assets in high-tech labs, enabling real time tracking of every critical asset. By placing Impinj readers strategically at exits, throughout the desired areas of coverage, and at choke points within a lab facility, labs can provide comprehensive tracking of all assets wherever they may be. Additional readers are placed on shelves, cages, and cabinets, while the AssetPulse asset tracking system facilitates the entire equipment calibration process, making audits easier.


The Impinj R700 series readers are the enterprise standard for speed, precision, and scalability. With unmatched receive sensitivity, the R700 series reads tags faster and more accurately, even in challenging high-density or high-speed environments.


R700 is a versatile reading engine for IoT solution developers, who can easily build and deploy custom enterprise applications with a secure Linux OS, intuitive REST API, and native support for industry-standard data formats and protocols, including MQTT. Powerful processing and expansive on-reader memory enable embedded applications that transform RAIN data into actionable business intelligence.


### Get More with Gen2X


When paired with Impinj M800 endpoint ICs, R700 readers unlock Impinj’s Gen2X solutions toolbox, enabling advanced, industry-leading capabilities for autonomous reading solutions that:


- **Improve the readability of newer, smaller tags** that have disproportionately weaker reply signals. Gen2X’s advanced tag reply formatting enables reading solutions to hear these newer, smaller tags at 2x the range.
- **Improve read-zone confinement** through the use of on-reader algorithms that utilize read count to accurately identify tag location within a reader’s transmitted radio beam. Gen2X improves performance of these algorithms by increasing read count (or reinventory speed), reducing cross-reads in multi-reader environments, and silencing already-inventoried tags to reduce clutter.


For enterprises seeking high performance RAIN RFID autonomous reading solutions, Impinj Gen2X helps unlock new use cases and is easily available by utilizing solutions that combine Impinj R700 series readers with M800 series endpoint ICs.
