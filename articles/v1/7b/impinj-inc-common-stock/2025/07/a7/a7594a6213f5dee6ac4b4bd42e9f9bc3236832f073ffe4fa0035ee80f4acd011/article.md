# RAIN RFID: A Strategic CPG Opportunity Disguised as a Mandate


## Impinj helps CPG brands turn Walmart’s mandate into a supply chain advantage, boosting accuracy, efficiency, and resilience across operations.


Consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands today face an increasingly complex and volatile operating environment. Unpredictable tariffs, ongoing supply chain disruptions, and persistent labor shortages continue to strain margins and challenge business resilience. On top of these pressures, new technology mandates — from Walmart’s item-level RFID tagging requirements to the European Union’s[Digital Product Passport (DPP)](https://www.impinj.com/Digital-Product-Passports) — are accelerating digital transformation. For CPG brands, these aren't just IT projects; they’re supply chain inflection points that will shape competitiveness for years to come.


While[RFID mandates like Walmart’s](https://www.impinj.com/library/Blog/all-in-on-rain-rfid-walmart-lights-the-way-for-ret) can pose challenges to CPG suppliers, they also present a powerful opportunity: a chance to strengthen operations, improve efficiency, and transform compliance into a strategic asset.


### Mandates as a catalyst for transformation


Retail RFID mandates are putting pressure on CPG suppliers. Many are struggling to keep up, at potential risk of facing fines, chargebacks, or even lost shelf space for non-compliance. But mandates are also pushing the industry toward modernized, connected supply chains — something CPG brands need now more than ever.


Rather than viewing mandates as a checklist item, forward-thinking companies are using them as a springboard to address broader industry challenges:


- **Margin pressures from tariffs** demand more efficient operations and reduced waste.
- **Labor shortages** require greater automation and streamlined workflows.
- **Supply chain volatility** calls for end-to-end visibility and adaptability.


[RAIN RFID](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Technology/about-rfid) helps address all three painpoints by improving inventory accuracy, enabling real-time visibility, and automating manual tasks — delivering faster fulfillment, fewer chargebacks, and stronger retail partnerships.


According to[a recent industry survey](https://www.impinj.com/library/Resources/Whitepapers/new-report-inventory-labor-and-sustainability-how) , 75% of companies using RFID reported high confidence in their supply chain agility — compared to just 51% of non-RFID adopters.


### Turning a mandate into business value


A standout example of this transformation in action is Paris Corp., a Walmart supplier in the stationery category. To meet Walmart’s RFID mandate,[Paris Corp built a custom in-house tagging program](https://www.packworld.com/coding-printing-labeling/coding-marking/article/22918056/paris-corp-meets-walmart-rfid-tagging-mandate) that involved designing encoding software, integrating print-and-apply systems into their packaging lines, and streamlining tag inventory. Their goal was to create a flexible program that would allow them to tag at scale across a diverse product portfolio.


But they didn’t stop at compliance.


Partnering with[Impinj partner FineLine Technologies](https://www.impinj.com/partners/FineLine-Technologies) , Paris Corp. was able to go beyond the mandate and unlock operational benefits, including simplified labeling for both in-house and third-party production, improved inventory visibility, and enabled precise quality checks and more accurate shipping. These changes not only ensured compliance but also enhanced supply chain agility, helping Paris Corp. better manage costs, protect margins against tariff-related pressures, and overcome labor constraints.


What began as a required compliance project turned into a core part of their operational infrastructure. Today, Paris Corp. uses RFID across its business to verify inventory in real time, trace products more quickly, and improve sustainability by customizing label placement to minimize material waste. The result is more streamlined processes, reduced errors, and a more resilient supply chain.


Deployments like Paris Corp.'s highlight how critical tag performance is in complex, large-scale environments. That’s why Impinj partners use[leading endpoint ICs](https://www.impinj.com/walmart-rfid-label-selection-guide) like the[M800 series](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Tag-Chips/Impinj-M800-Series) to build small, reliable inlays that work on a broad range of items, from tiny bottles of makeup and pieces of jewelry to larger items like boxes and pallets. M800 endpoint ICs also offer powerful performance on typically hard-to-tag items such as those that hold liquid or are made of metal. With superior read sensitivity and accuracy, M800-based tags ensure fast, accurate tracking — making scalable, value-driving RFID deployments possible.
