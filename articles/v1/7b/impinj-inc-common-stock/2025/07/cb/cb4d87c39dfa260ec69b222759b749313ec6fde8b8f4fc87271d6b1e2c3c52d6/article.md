# Gen2X-enabled RAIN RFID Products Now Widely Available


## New Gen2X-enabled RAIN RFID tags, readers, and reader modules unlock innovative enterprise use cases by boosting RAIN RFID performance and lowering solution cost.


Enterprises looking to boost the performance and reduce the cost of their RAIN RFID solutions can now take advantage of a wide range of products that leverage[Gen2X](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Technology/Gen2x) — an enhancement to the RAIN radio standard (Gen2) that improves the performance of existing RAIN RFID solutions, reduces solution cost, and opens the door to use cases previously thought unsolvable. Gen2X is widely available to the industry, and now a[broad range of Impinj partner tags, reader modules, and readers support it](https://www.impinj.com/partners?query=&pagesize=24&partnerprofile=hasgen2xsupport.true) .


Gen2X is enabled in all tags that are based on[Impinj M800 series endpoint ICs](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Tag-Chips/Impinj-M800-Series) , the fastest-growing Impinj endpoint IC ever. Impinj partners have launched more than 85 production Gen2X-enabled inlays. These inlays leverage the enhanced performance of the Gen2X-enabled endpoint ICs to deliver small, high-performance tags that enable enterprises to connect more items and unlock new use cases.


### Wide-scale Gen2X reader support


RAIN RFID devices based on the market-leading Impinj E-family reader IC can be upgraded to support Gen2X with a seamless firmware update. Impinj device partners have upgraded[more than 50 RAIN RFID readers, modules, and printers to support Gen2X](https://www.impinj.com/partners?query=&pagesize=24&partnerprofile=hasgen2xsupport.true) . These include a wide range of devices that address a variety of form factors and solution needs such as inventory management, shipment verification, automated checkout, location systems, and more.


Because Gen2X and Gen2 interoperate seamlessly, these devices and modules support the Gen2X performance enhancements when reading tags based on the M800 series endpoint ICs, while still delivering market-leading performance when reading tags based on other Impinj and non-Impinj endpoint ICs. The more M800-series endpoint ICs in the deployment, or tag population, the more the solution can take advantage of Gen2X performance enhancements — but there is no requirement to exclusively use M800 series endpoint ICs in an enterprise deployment to take advantage of Gen2X performance enhancements.

### Realizing real-world gains with M800 and Gen2X


We are already seeing the real-world benefits of Gen2X with the M800 series endpoint ICs, as demonstrated by innovative products and solutions from Impinj partners. In the video demonstration below, a densely populated shelf of general merchandise items is inventoried utilizing a handheld reader. Impinj worked with[tagging partner r-pac International](https://www.impinj.com/partners/r-pac-International) to design the small, 24mm Gen2X-enabled tags for traditionally hard-to-read products such as liquid hand soap and liquid makeup. The[Gen2X-enabled Chainway handheld reader](https://www.impinj.com/partners/Chainway/C5-UHF-RFID-Reader) is able to inventory 98% of the items in 13 seconds, versus just 79% of the items using the standard Gen2 mode in the same amount of time.

### Gen2X unlocking enterprise solutions today and tomorrow


Fast, accurate inventory capture of tightly packed, hard-to-read items is just one of many examples that demonstrate how Gen2X-enabled IoT products and solutions from Impinj partners are helping enterprises solve tough operational challenges and reduce solution cost. Evolving enterprise use cases and item categories are driving the need for RAIN performance advancements — and Gen2X delivers. In addition to improving readability of small tags on traditionally hard-to-read items, Gen2X is also:


- Reducing handheld inventory counting time, saving labor cost
- Reducing stray reads in retail loss prevention, allowing consumer self-checkout
- Improving area coverage and accuracy in real-time retail inventory and location systems
- Decluttering conveyor-to-truck shipment loading, reducing labor costs and mis-shipments
- Boosting dock-door reading and directional accuracy, reducing mis-shipments and costly mistakes
- Addressing consumer privacy at point-of-sale
- Inhibiting tag and item counterfeiting, fraudulent returns, or both
