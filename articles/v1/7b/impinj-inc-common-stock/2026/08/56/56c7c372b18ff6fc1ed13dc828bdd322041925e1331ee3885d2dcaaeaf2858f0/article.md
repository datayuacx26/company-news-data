# Keonn Speeds Retail Inventory 7X With Impinj Gen2X Overhead RFID | Impinj

# Keonn and Impinj Automate Retail Store Operations with Real-Time Item Visibility Using Gen2X


- Keonn’s AdvanTrack-600 overhead RFID solution uses Impinj E Family reader ICs with support for Gen2X to continuously deliver accurate item-level inventory and location, helping retailers address the $1.7 Trillion inventory distortion problem
- Impinj Gen2X and E Family reader IC optimizations enabled Keonn to speed inventory time by over 7X and reduce missing inventory items by more than 50%
- Impinj Gen2X improves overhead system area coverage with next-generation smaller tags that are harder to hear


Every year, retailers lose an estimated[$1.7 trillion annually](https://www.ihlservices.com/product/fixing-inventory-distortion-are-we-there-yet/) to inventory distortion – the combined impact of out-of-stocks and overstocks – as a direct result of inaccurate, periodic inventory processes that are slow, labor-intensive, and error-prone. To reduce these losses while supporting increasing demands for just-in-time replenishment and[omnichannel fulfillment](https://www.impinj.com/solutions/inventory-management) , retailers are increasingly exploring automating their store operations with continuous overhead RFID reading solutions. However, these systems historically fell short of delivering the accuracy, coverage, and speed retailers demand, especially in challenging back-of-store conditions.


Keonn Technologies – a leader in advanced RFID systems for retail stores – recently upgraded its[AdvanTrack-600](https://keonn.com/systems-product/advantrack-600/) overhead solution, powered by the Impinj E710 reader IC with support for[Gen2X](https://www.impinj.com/products/technology/gen2x) . Leveraging Impinj Gen2X and[Impinj E Family reader IC](https://www.impinj.com/products/reader-ics) optimizations to enhance tag reading performance, the updated AdvanTrack-600 significantly improves item-level inventory accuracy and speed across both the sales floor and back-of-store.


“Using Impinj’s innovations, our overhead readers are dramatically reducing inventory times and numbers of missing tags, differentiating our AdvanTrack series from competing solutions,” said Ramir De Porrata-Doria, Co-Founder and Managing Director, Keonn Technologies. “Gen2X capabilities are hugely impactful for inventory management efficiency and accuracy, improving scanning automation in retail stores, warehouses, and factories.”


To support an ongoing enterprise deployment scenario, Keonn and Impinj recently tested a full-scale retail store layout, shown below, with more than 9,000 tagged items and multiple AdvanTrack-600 systems. Historically, achieving the desired inventory accuracy at reasonable cycle times is challenging, especially in back-of-store where items are more densely packed, resulting in inventory blind spots and slower inventory refresh rates. So Keonn and Impinj upgraded the systems with support for Gen2X improvements in tag read range and inventory speeds, as well as optimizations to E Family reader ICs for overhead reading.


In the past, achieving over 90% inventory accuracy in back-of-store operations took nearly an hour. Using the upgraded AdvanTrack-600 with support for Gen2X reduced the average time for over 90% inventory accuracy to under eight minutes – a greater than 7X improvement. In addition, the upgraded overhead solution reduced the number of missing items by over 50%, reducing back-of-store inventory blind spots. These improvements allow retailers to identify inventory issues sooner, replenish shelves faster, and maintain more accurate stock positions across stores. They also help reduce out-of-stocks and inventory shrink, lower excess inventory, and support better customer experiences through improved product availability, easier item location, and more effective omnichannel operations.


Area coverage is also another common concern as retailers explore overhead reading solutions. This becomes even more challenging as small next-generation tags are being introduced to reduce manufacturing costs and track smaller form factor items: Small tag replies are harder for readers to hear. When a reader cannot hear tag replies, Gen2X increases reader listening range. As shown below in Impinj lab testing utilizing the Keonn AdvanTrack-600 overhead system, Gen2X improves area coverage significantly, while also improving inventory speeds.


Reducing inventory distortion is a historic challenge for retailers, but offers massive rewards for those who successfully implement accurate real-time physical intelligence solutions. Achieving this requires continuous inventory solutions that perform reliably, even in the most challenging retail environments. Keonn expects to have 1,600 stores online by the end of 2026, with 80,000 devices deployed across more than 20 different retailers in five continents. AdvanTrack-600 demonstrates how utilizing the Impinj Platform with support for Gen2X can help retailers significantly improve inventory speed, accuracy, and area coverage across both the sales floor and back-of-store.
