# At EuroShop, One Thing Was Clear: RAIN RFID is Mainstream

# At EuroShop, One Thing Was Clear: RAIN RFID is Mainstream


## Enterprises large and small find RAIN RFID accessible, affordable — and essential.


Attending EuroShop just weeks after NRF offered a rare vantage point across two of retail’s largest global stages. One message came through clearly: RAIN RFID has crossed the mainstream threshold.


Not long ago, the industry focused on proving RFID's value. Today, that conversation has shifted. Retailers aren’t asking if they should deploy RAIN — they’re asking how to get more performance, more precision, and more scalability from it.


RAIN has become ubiquitous with retail operational efficiency. From global leaders like[Decathlon](https://sustainability.decathlon.com/product-traceability-and-rfid-technology-at-decathlon) and Inditex (ZARA) to growing regional retail chains, RAIN now underpins inventory accuracy, loss prevention, automated checkout, and broader store optimization. And it wasn’t isolated to a single booth at EuroShop. RAIN RFID was a part of a range of solutions on display at EuroShop — integrated as a mainstream technology essential for conducting business.


Adoption is no longer limited to global enterprises. Small and mid-size retailers are realizing they, too, can deploy RFID at scale — and benefit from it. What was once innovation is now part of the everyday operational infrastructure.


### The Ecosystem Is Driving Innovation


What stood out most at EuroShop wasn’t just adoption – it was momentum.


Across the show floor, I saw[Impinj partners](https://www.impinj.com/Our-Partners) proudly showcasing products enabled by Impinj Gen2X, the Gen2 standard-plus innovation from Impinj, that improves performance and opens the door to use cases previously thought unsolvable. From handheld devices to overhead readers, products from Impinj partners such as[Seuic](https://www.impinj.com/partners/SEUIC-Technologies-Co-Ltd) ,[Unitech](https://www.impinj.com/partners/unitech-electronics-co-ltd) ,[Urovo](https://www.impinj.com/partners/UROVO-Technology-Co-Ltd) , and[Keonn](https://www.impinj.com/partners/KEONN-TECHNOLOGIES-SL) , powered by Impinj technology, emphasized measurable performance improvements as a clear differentiator.


The industry has moved beyond pilots. The focus now is on optimization. Stronger read reliability, better performance for small and harder-to-tag items, and scalable deployments across entire store networks.


One moment crystallized this shift. After showing a Gen2X demo to a solution provider evaluating options for a small retailer, their response was immediate. They wanted to move forward on the spot. And today, they can. RAIN solutions are more accessible than ever — no longer requiring massive investments. The breadth of the ecosystem now supports retailers of all sizes with complete, scalable, and affordable solutions.


Our role at Impinj is to advance the underlying RAIN innovation to support our partners as they build faster, more reliable, and more scalable solutions for enterprises worldwide.


### From Store Operations to Consumer Transparency


One of the most forward-looking moments I saw at the show came from our collaboration with[r-pac](https://www.impinj.com/partners/r-pac-International) . Together, we demonstrated RAIN RFID functionality enabled through a[Qualcomm-powered mobile device](https://www.qualcomm.com/news/onq/2025/08/how-integrated-rfid-enhances-handheld-devices-qualcomm-dragonwing-q-6690) — a vision for integrating Gen2X reader capability into personal smartphones.


This wasn’t just a technology demo; it pointed to where the industry is headed.


As[Digital Product Passport (DPP)](https://www.impinj.com/Digital-Product-Passports) initiatives gain momentum in the EU, consumers will increasingly expect direct access to product-level information — authenticity, sourcing, sustainability — from their own devices. Bringing RAIN into mobile platforms opens the door to that transparency.


RAIN RFID is evolving from a back-of-house operational tool into a consumer-facing technology.


### AI, Vison, and the Power of Better Data


AI and computer vison were also front and center at EuroShop. Retailers are investing heavily in analytics, store floor optimization, and automation. Partners like[Sensormatic](https://www.sensormatic.com/shopper-insights/retail-analytics) showcased new vision trends focused on measuring and benchmarking shopper data to help optimize enterprise operations and enhance the customer experience.


[In our recent market research](https://www.impinj.com/library/Resources/Whitepapers/Supply-Chain-Integrity-Outlook-2026-Consumer-Expec) , more than 90% of retail leaders indicated they are testing new technologies — AI leading the charge — to enhance customer experience and drive in-store engagement. That level of interest and engagement, underscores the need for transparent and data-driven systems.


But AI is only as strong as the quality of data feeding it.


RAIN RFID delivers item-level visibility that strengthens those systems, providing cleaner , more accurate inventory and real-time data. It doesn’t compete with AI or vision technologies; it enhances them. Reliable data at scale is what enables true automation.


### Grocery: The Next Major Frontier


Innovation was also evident in grocery.


At the Avery Dennison booth, RAIN RFID-enabled fresh food solutions were on display,[launching the AD IndentiFresh inlay series](https://www.impinj.com/library/Blog/avery-dennison-launches-ad-identifresh-to-unlock) powered by[Impinj M800 series ICs](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Tag-Chips/Impinj-M800-Series) with Gen2X enhancements. This was not just a product moment; it's a breakthrough for fresh food.


Fresh food has historically been one of the most challenging environments for RFID. This announcement signals real progress, proving that key operational barriers in food retail environment can be overcome, with improve read performance on densely stacked items, particularly in high-moisture cold environments like meat cases.


Item-level visibility in bakery, meat, deli, and produce has the potential to dramatically improve freshness management and reduce waste. The urgency is real; retail food waste losses are projected to reach $540 billion in 2026, a challenge recently highlighted by EE Times in its coverage of[advancements in RFID labeling for perishables](https://www.eetimes.com/how-rfid-labels-could-help-tackle-540b-food-waste-losses-in-2026/) .


When RAIN RFID performance improves in the toughest environments, entirely new categories become viable.


### What this Means for RAIN RFID


EuroShop underscored a pivotal shift. The question is no longer whether RAIN RFID will shape the future of retail. It already is. It’s which industry it will shape next. RAIN RFID is moving from product to solution, from cost concern to ROI driver, and from operational nice-to-have to enterprise must-have.


As the RAIN RFID innovator and industry leader, our role is to continue pushing performance boundaries — enabling our ecosystem to solve increasingly complex challenges, from store optimization to food waste, with Gen2X and other innovations.


The next phase of RAIN RFID is about expanding the impact across automation, consumer transparency, and new segments like grocery — all powered by ecosystem collaboration and continued performance innovations to deliver robust solutions, capturing high-quality data that will drive more automation from accurate data-driven AI-assisted decisions.
