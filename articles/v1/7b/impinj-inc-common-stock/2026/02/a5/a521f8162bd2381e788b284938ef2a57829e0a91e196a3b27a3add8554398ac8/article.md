# EuroShop 2026: RAIN RFID Innovation Across Our Partner Ecosystem


## Where to find Impinj-powered RAIN RFID solutions, devices, and tagging innovations that our partners are showcasing at EuroShop 2026.


Held only once every three years, EuroShop 2026 is set to be a defining moment for retail innovation. From February 22-26, EuroShop will bring together the global retail community to explore the technologies and ideas shaping the future of stores and supply chains. At this year’s show, you can expect to see RAIN RFID and the[Impinj partner network](https://www.impinj.com/Our-Partners) throughout the show floor – including a live Impinj demo in the R-Pac booth –, showcasing breakthrough advancements powered by[Gen2X-enabled](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Technology/Gen2x)[M800 endpoint ICs](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Tag-Chips/Impinj-M800-Series) ,[E Family reader ICs](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Reader-Chips) , and[R700-series readers](https://www.impinj.com/Products/Readers/Impinj-R700) .


As retailers look for technologies that improve item traceability, automate workflows, and unlock real-time insights, Impinj partners exhibiting at EuroShop will showcase how RAIN RFID is helping retailers redefine what’s possible in modern store and supply chain environments.


Demos will highlight practical applications, innovative system designs, new tagging approaches, and an expanding list of use cases, including automating inventory counts, individual item location and movement tracking, and loss prevention — made possible or enhanced by Gen2X performance and protection capabilities.


If you’re attending EuroShop, don’t miss the opportunity to see how leading innovators with enterprise-grade solutions solve the toughest operational challenges, even in emerging categories such as food, health & beauty, and other historically hard-to-read product types.


Looking to make the most of your time at EuroShop? Find us at the R-Pac booth or[request time with an Impinj expert](https://www.impinj.com/About-Us/Events/NRF-Event-2024-(3)) to navigate the show floor, meet the right partners, and see firsthand how Impinj-enabled solutions are improving efficiency across the retail supply chain from source to store.


Here’s a snapshot of where you can find Impinj-powered RAIN RFID solutions, readers, and tags at our partner ecosystem booths on the show floor:


**Avery Dennison (Hall 6, Booth F80)**
Avery Dennison is expected to showcase intelligent labeling and RFID-enabled solutions that strengthen inventory accuracy, expand tagging into grocery and fresh items, and support sustainability and DPP readiness. Look for demos of how next-gen inlays can improve traceability and visibility across the supply chain.


**Bixolon (Hall 6, Booth A32)**
Bixolon is known for mobile and industrial printers built for fast, reliable RFID tag encoding – supporting high-volume labeling and extending RAIN RFID into more flexible, on-the-go workflows.


**Hangzhou Century (Hall 5, F44)**
Expect to see RFID innovations through live demos of advanced labels, item-tracking solutions, and smart retail applications designed to improve accuracy and operational efficiency. Find next-gen apparel labels – including M830-based designs – and technologies that support connected store environments.


**Checkpoint Systems (Hall 6, Booth E23)**
Checkpoint will showcase interactive RFID solutions highlighting scalable hardware, software, and label solutions. Demos will feature Impinj M800 series-based inlays for small and specialty items like jewelry and beauty.


**Clustag (Hall 6, Booth A79)**
Clustag will highlight RFID solutions that streamline store operations – from accurate receiving to stock movement and omnichannel workflows. With a focus on read precision and guided processes, it will show how integrated systems improve speed, accuracy, and day-to-day efficiency.


**Datalogic (Hall 6, Booth C46)**
Datalogic will feature its latest scanning and mobility devices, including new handhelds and AI-enhanced scanners designed to speed up checkout and improve product identification.


**GIGA TMS (Hall 6, Booth B20)**
GIGA TMS will show RFID solutions and middleware that integrate item-level data into broader enterprise systems, helping unify visibility and analytics across channels.


**Honeywell (Hall 6, Booth F58)**
Honeywell will highlight its handheld and mobile devices built for fast item lookup, verification, and movement through the store, showcasing tools that bring real-time data and efficiency to everyday retail workflows.


**Keonn (Hall 5, Booth E21)**
An innovator of RFID middleware and platforms such as shopOS, Keonn is expected to showcase RFID software and device solutions that bring real-time store visibility and item tracking through integrated sensor data.


**Koamtac (Hall 6, Booth E53)**
Koamtac is expected to highlight compact RFID-enabled handhelds and mobile accessories – including its new E710 – designed for fast item lookup and efficient, on-the-go inventory tasks.


**Maxim (Hall 5, Booth B25)**
Highlighting its expertise in RAIN labeling, Maxim draws on its global experience to support efficient, scalable item identification across retail.


**Nedap (Hall 5, Booth F30)**
Nedap will focus on RFID-enabled inventory as a foundation for retail operations, with demos showcasing how accurate, always-on item visibility supports decisions in areas like availability, replenishment, and loss prevention.


**Newland AIDC (Hall 6, Booth A41)**
This provider of RFID readers, mobile computers, and scanning solutions enables real-time data capture across store and supply-chain operations.


**R-Pac (Hall 6, Booth D49)**
R-Pac will feature a live Impinj-powered demo highlighting how Gen2X is raising the bar for accelerated inventory cycles and stronger item-level visibility – enabling real-time traceability, authentication, and Digital Product Passport-ready product identity across retail workflows – alongside its RFID tagging solutions, including the “Connected Shirt” experience.


**SATO (Hall 6, Booth G61)**
SATO will showcase a connected product journey built around the Digital Product Passport, using QR, RFID, and BLE to create accurate item identities from manufacturing through store operations.


**Sensormatic Solutions (Hall 6, Booth C01)**
Sensormatic will showcase its Inventory Intelligence capabilities, including real-time RFID visibility, expiry tracking, and autonomous cycle counting. Their demos highlight how item-level insights support unified commerce, loss prevention, and stronger store operations.


**Seuic (Hall 5, Booth E33)**
Seuic will feature its latest RFID handhelds built with Impinj reader technology and highlighting Gen2X capabilities. Demos will focus on fashion, jewelry, and cosmetics workflows, showing reliable item reads even on small-form and liquid products.


**Unitech (Hall 6, Booth E51)**
Unitech will demo its Gen2X-enabled mobile solutions, showing how their handhelds read tags at short range and then switch to long-range Gen2X mode when docked. They’ll also highlight iPhone pairing for simple, high-performance RFID data capture.


**Urovo (Hall 6, Booth C68)**
Urovo will showcase its latest RFID and Gen2X-ready mobile devices, featuring demos that highlight fast item reads, reliable performance, and practical workflows using M800-tagged items.


**Zebra Technologies (Hall 6, Booth A79)**
Zebra will showcase its latest handhelds, readers, and software that support real-time inventory workflows and smarter store operations—helping retailers improve item visibility and streamline daily tasks in RFID-enabled environments.
