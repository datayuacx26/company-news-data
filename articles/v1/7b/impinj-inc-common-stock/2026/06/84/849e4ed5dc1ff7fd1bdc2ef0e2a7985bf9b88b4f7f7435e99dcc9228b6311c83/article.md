# Gen2X RAIN RFID: 5 Enterprise Facts About Open Standards | Impinj

# Five Facts About Gen2X and RAIN RFID Open Standards That Enterprises Need to Understand


Open radio standards have been foundational to the success and scale of countless wireless technologies, including RAIN RFID. Since the first release of the[“Gen2” RAIN RFID radio standard](https://www.impinj.com/products/technology/rfid-standards) (GS1 UHF Gen2 and ISO/IEC 18000-63) in 2004, the RAIN industry has become the fastest-growing RFID market, connecting billions of everyday items to the internet. Adopting RAIN technologies has become a corporate imperative for any business that wants to gain efficiencies, reduce waste, and deliver amazing consumer experiences.


Continuous innovation is essential to the success and scale of any wireless technology. As our industry continues expanding to automate supply chains and revolutionize omnichannel shopping experiences, enterprises increasingly require connecting more items, unlocking new automated use cases, and driving down solution costs. The RAIN radio standard cannot feasibly anticipate the needs of every enterprise and every use case. This is why – like other wireless technologies – RAIN system innovations are often introduced first as extensions to the radio standard by innovative solution providers with end-to-end system capabilities. If these innovations prove broadly useful over time, they are often added to future versions of the standard. This type of innovation cycle is the sign of a growing, healthy industry.


**A Toolbox for Solutions, Enabled by Innovation**


For nearly two decades, Impinj has helped visionary Fortune 500 enterprises solve tough operational challenges with RAIN technology. Solving these challenges led us to develop key RAIN innovations – a solutions toolbox – called[Impinj Gen2X](https://www.impinj.com/products/technology/gen2x) . In December 2024, we released Gen2X widely to the industry to drive RAIN adoption.


Gen2X is a standards-compatible enhancement to the radio and logical layers of the Gen2 RAIN RFID protocol that, at the most fundamental level, either solves previously unsolvable use cases, reduces overall solution cost, or both. For example, Gen2X reduces retail inventory counting time, saving labor cost. It declutters conveyor-to-truck shipment loading, reducing costly misshipments. It boosts dock-door directional accuracy, reducing costly shipping mistakes. It improves the readability of small, embedded tags and tags on hard-to-read items, reducing tag form factor and associated cost and enabling new use cases like food tagging. It inhibits fraud by verifying the endpoint IC as genuine. And Gen2X is not static – Impinj continues to add new innovations to the toolbox.


Gen2X is now widely available across the industry, but there are still some misconceptions out there that I wanted to set the record straight on today.


**Fact # 1 – Readers and Tags Can Be Gen2-Compliant and Also Support Gen2X.**


For example,[Impinj M800 endpoint ICs](https://www.impinj.com/products/endpoint-ics/impinj-m800-series) – which power billions of tags and labels built by the RAIN industry – are certified as Gen2 standard-compliant by GS1 and also support Gen2X. RAIN RFID tags based on M800 ICs seamlessly interoperate using both Gen2 and Gen2X, meaning they can be read by any reader that conforms to the RAIN radio standard regardless of whether the reader supports Gen2X or not.


RAIN RFID readers conform to the Gen2 RAIN radio standard and can also be upgraded to support Gen2X. Because of its compatibility with Gen2, Gen2X reader operations seamlessly interoperate with Gen2 operations, allowing enterprises to take advantage of the Gen2X performance boost and advanced capabilities when Gen2X-enabled tags are deployed, and have full backward-compatibility with Gen2 when tags that do not support Gen2X are deployed.


**Fact # 2 – Gen2X Consistently Delivers Performance Benefits in Mixed Tag Populations.**


RAIN RFID readers can dynamically alternate between Gen2 and Gen2X operations for mixed tag populations, where the use of Gen2X consistently improves performance in mixed tag populations, even in tag populations with a small percentage of tags that support Gen2X. This allows enterprises and solution providers to enable Gen2X operation in their deployments today regardless of tag population statistics. As more tags supporting Gen2X are added to a deployment, system performance improves.


**Fact # 3 – Gen2X Does Not Require Investment in New Infrastructure or Specialized Tags.**


Gen2X support is now available in over 50 production partner readers and modules, from a wide range of device makers across the industry. For most if not all of these readers and modules, including[Impinj R700 readers](https://www.impinj.com/products/readers/impinj-r700) , Gen2X support is available via firmware upgrades to already deployed solutions.


Gen2X is included in all tags that support the market-leading M800 endpoint ICs – the fastest-growing Impinj endpoint IC ever – and the industry has launched over 100 production Gen2X-enabled inlays. These Gen2 compliant, ARC-certified inlays can take advantage of Gen2X performance boost and advanced capabilities when deployed with infrastructure that has also been upgraded to support Gen2X.


**Fact # 4 – Gen2X Expands Enterprise Opportunities and Does Not Limit Freedom of Choice.**


Gen2X is widely available to the industry across a broad range of supporting tags and readers. Impinj offers a Gen2X license to every company in the industry, with notable announced licensees today including Zebra and EM Microelectronics. Enterprise solutions can also opt to use or not use Gen2X, as well as only use parts of the Gen2X solution toolbox they need for any given use case. And, as covered earlier, readers and tags that support Gen2X also support the Gen2 RAIN RFID radio standard.


So Gen2X does not limit enterprise choice – in fact, it expands what is possible with standard-compliant RAIN RFID systems, giving enterprises the ability to connect more items, unlock new use cases, and lower solution costs.


**Fact # 5 – Gen2X Is Already Proven in Real-World Deployments.**


As stated earlier, Impinj created Gen2X through work with Fortune 500 enterprises, and as such, Gen2X is proven in global large-scale enterprise deployments today. Just a few examples include self-checkout and loss prevention at a visionary European retailer, loading and shipping accuracy at a major global logistics provider, and food inventory at a leading grocery chain.


Since releasing Gen2X widely to the industry in December 2024, our global partner ecosystem has also embraced Gen2X to solve enterprise pain points in a myriad of ways. At the Impinj Gen2X Developers Conference in November 2025, our partner ecosystem shared numerous customer success stories with Gen2X, including:


- Automating supply chains with near-perfect item-level shipment verification in seconds using RFID tunnels, even in the most challenging conditions
- Protecting medical assets with smart cabinets that deliver 100% inventory of densely packed metal-sealed bags in less than 5 seconds
- Reducing labor for inventory counts by speeding inventory by up to 75% for small inlays on emerging item categories such as cosmetics, jewelry, and liquid products
- Automating inventory visibility at retail stores with up to 40% increased area coverage for overhead reading systems and 50% reduction in inventory cycle time
- Improved inventory management accuracy of densely packed items, including increased hotel linen accuracy by up to 75%, and health and beauty accuracy up to 70%


**Innovation and Open Standards Are Compatible – and Ideal for Everyone**


As Impinj continues our journey to connect trillions of items per year, the growth of our industry depends on both interoperability and innovation. Open standards provide the common foundation that enables ecosystem growth, while standards-compatible innovation helps the industry solve new operational challenges and unlock entirely new enterprise use cases. Gen2X is a perfect example of these principles: Developed by Impinj, available to the entire RAIN RFID industry, and widely adopted by global leaders in retailing, logistics, and supply chains.
