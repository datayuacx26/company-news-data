# Upgraded Impinj R700 Reader Delivers Enterprise-Grade Edge Intelligence for Autonomous Reading Solutions

# Upgraded Impinj R700 Reader Delivers Enterprise-Grade Edge Intelligence for Autonomous Reading Solutions


## New Impinj R700 RAIN RFID reader combines industry-leading performance with enhanced 64-bit processor, OS, and application memory


Impinj high-performance fixed readers – led by our flagship Impinj R700 series – are the trusted engine for the most demanding RAIN RFID systems. Originally introduced in 2020 and now[deployed across thousands of enterprise sites globally](https://www.impinj.com/library/blog/global-developers-leverage-impinj-r700-to-solve-operational-challenges) , R700 readers deliver best-in-class tag readability, enterprise-grade security, and modern IoT developer tools that enable our global ecosystem to build high-performance autonomous reading solutions.


In 2024, we expanded R700’s capabilities with Gen2X, allowing for a new wave of autonomous applications that push the limits of RAIN RFID performance. Now, we are upgrading R700 to meet the increasing computational demands of these challenging use cases with the introduction of a[more powerful and versatile R700](https://www.impinj.com/products/readers/impinj-r700) . With twice the processor performance and three times the embedded application memory size of its predecessor, the upgraded R700 maintains its best-in-class RFID performance while adding significant edge computing power, allowing enterprises to collect and manage data at the edge of networks, enabling real-time edge intelligence that helps optimize speed and automation.


#### Designed with Autonomous Reading Solutions in Mind


Innovative enterprises are increasingly shifting to next-generation supply chain solutions, enabled through autonomous RAIN RFID systems that automatically capture item data without human scanning or manual intervention. These solutions are unlocking the power of reliable, real-time data about every item that is manufactured, transported, and sold. Supply chains rely upon these solutions to automate high-speed package sortation and item-level shipment verification at dock doors, conveyors, and other transition points. Retail stores use these solutions to deliver always-on inventory visibility, automated checkout experiences, and high-precision loss prevention. Across industries, autonomous reading solutions empower enterprises to solve operational challenges, reduce labor costs, and increase supply chain integrity.


“As early adopters of the new Impinj R700 RAIN RFID reader, we’re excited to see Impinj build on the R700’s best-in-class reading performance with increased computational power to meet the demands of today’s challenging autonomous applications,” said Matt Smith, Senior Director of Engineering,[SLS](http://www.impinj.com/partners/smart-label-solutions-llc) . “These enhancements improve read-zone control and enable real-time item-level insights. Our SLS D-Series dock-door solution uses Impinj R700-series readers to deliver highly accurate reads and fast event alarming, helping our customers ensure accurate deliveries and shipments, lower shipping expenses, and improve ROI.”


To deliver the reliability enterprises require from real-time autonomous solutions, RAIN RFID fixed reading systems must provide high accuracy and high-throughput reading performance in challenging use cases, where items are often in motion, densely packed, and/or inside tough and unpredictable RF environments. Traditional fixed reading systems rely on a single RFID tag read to make business-critical decisions, a model that often falls short for autonomous reading use cases, especially in challenging conditions. Today’s solutions often require multiple tag reads across multiple antennas and/or readers, as well as the fusion of sensor inputs with RFID tag reads. Enhanced edge processing capabilities enable on-reader applications that analyze these richer data streams at the network’s edge, transforming raw RAIN tag responses into precise, actionable, real-time insights that improve solution accuracy and latency while reducing network bandwidth use.


#### Combining Best-in-Class Tag Readability and Edge Processing Performance


The new Impinj R700 combines best-in-class R700 series RAIN RFID performance – further extended with[Gen2X Performance](https://www.impinj.com/products/technology/gen2x) capabilities – with industry-leading edge processing capabilities that are essential for deploying edge intelligence directly on the reader. It also extends the proven capabilities of the R700 series reader, enabling enterprises to:


-


**Ensure accurate and reliable RAIN system performance.** The new R700 features industry-leading receive sensitivity and support for advanced features that enable reading connected items farther and faster, even in the most challenging and unpredictable RF environments. When R700 is paired with Impinj M700 and M800 series endpoint ICs, the Gen2X solutions toolbox further improves tag readability, especially for smaller tags, while also reducing stray or undesired tag reads that negatively impact system performance for tags-of-interest.


-


**Leverage critical edge intelligence.** With 2x more powerful edge processing performance thanks to a dual-core 1 GHz 64-bit ARM A53 processor with a 64-bit Linux 6.6 operating system, as well as up to 3x more on-reader memory, the new R700 enables embedded applications and algorithms that transform RAIN data into actionable business intelligence at the edge while delivering enterprise-grade security and reliability.


-


**Simplify enterprise IoT solution development and deployments.** Developer-friendly tools, multi-antenna support, rich connectivity, and seamless migration from existing R700 designs enable quick and efficient development of IoT solutions and faster time-to-market. Built on extensive learnings and a proven track record of enterprise-grade reliability from large-scale R700 deployments at Fortune 500 enterprises, the new R700 continues to meet strict information security and reliability requirements that keep enterprise data safe and operations up and running. A single R700 firmware version simplifies deployment and management across all R700 readers, reducing operational complexity and making it easier to scale. The updated R700 Embedded Toolkit enables developers to build a single application that runs across all R700 readers.


As global demand for RAIN RFID continues to grow, we will continue to[expand our Gen2X solution innovation toolbox](https://www.impinj.com/about-us/news-room/2025/impinj-expands-gen2x-to-unlock-new-enterprise-rain-rfid-use-cases) . The new R700 is designed to support Gen2X Tag Sensitivity Improvements, a new Gen2X capability that delivers more power to tags to increase read range and readability. It also introduces Gen2X Tag Selection, which enables readers to better specify which tags should respond during inventory and which should remain quiet, helping enterprise solutions read only desired tags, along with additional Gen2X-only reader modes and future Gen2X innovations. An FPGA-based modem inside the new R700 gives readers the flexibility to add future Gen2X innovations via software upgrades.
