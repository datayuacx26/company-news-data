# Research Solutions Upgrades Article Galaxy With AI-Enhanced Scientific Discovery & Context-Aware Search

*Advanced Citation Analysis & Filtering Tools Help Researchers Cut Through The Noise & Surface The Most Relevant, Trustworthy Studies*


HENDERSON, Nev., May 14, 2025 /PRNewswire/ --[Research Solutions, Inc.](https://www.researchsolutions.com/) (NASDAQ: RSSS), a leading provider of AI-powered scientific research tools, announces the integration of powerful new search capabilities into its Article Galaxy platform. This enhanced search functionality, powered by Scite (the AI platform acquired by Research Solutions in December 2023), allows researchers to search across citation statements from full-text articles—a significant advancement beyond traditional title and abstract searches.


The upgraded search system represents Research Solutions' ongoing commitment to platform innovation and feature enhancement based on valuable customer feedback. Researchers can now find more relevant papers through both basic keyword searches and sophisticated Boolean queries, with AI-powered Full-Text Excerpts leveraging Scite's technology to provide deeper context and relevance beyond traditional abstract-only search results. Importantly, all existing functionality—including the platform's streamlined article ordering process, lookup tools, and rights management—remains fully intact and optimized.


"As the volume and complexity of scientific literature continues to grow, it's becoming increasingly difficult for researchers to identify the most relevant, trustworthy papers," said Roy Olivier, President and CEO of Research Solutions. "While ensuring our document delivery service remains just as efficient and reliable as ever, this powerful new search functionality significantly strengthens Article Galaxy's position as a comprehensive platform for discovering, acquiring, and managing scientific literature. We've deployed our advanced capabilities across our product family to create a more robust ecosystem that encourages greater platform utilization while improving research efficiency."


The new search capabilities include:


- **Integrated AI:** AI-enhanced search results that identify precise keyword matches within specific sections of full-text articles, plus intelligent Related Articles suggestions based on contextual analysis to help researchers discover additional relevant literature
- **Advanced Query Builder:** Sophisticated Boolean search functionality (AND, OR, NOT) with fields spanning title, abstract, author, affiliation, topic, MeSH descriptors, journal, publication type, and more
- **Flexible Results Sorting:** Multiple options including relevance, publication date (newest/oldest), citation metrics (most/least cited), and support/contrast indicators from Scite
- **Comprehensive Filtering:** Refine search results by paper type, journal, editorial notices, author, and affiliation
Integrated Workflow Features: In-line article ordering, fully integrated re-use rights management, and direct saving to Article Galaxy References
- **Search Management:** Saved search functionality for quick access to frequently used queries
- **Scite Badges Integration:** Every search result includes Scite Badges that provide researchers with contextual insights about how scientific papers have been cited
- **Enhanced Insights:** Scite badging provides deeper information about article credibility and impact, all powered by AI
- **Expanded Export Options:** Export search results to RIS, XML, or CSV formats


"The ability to search across citation statements represents a significant advancement in how researchers discover relevant literature," said Josh Nicholson, Chief Strategy Officer at Research Solutions. "When combined with our existing document delivery and rights management capabilities, this creates a seamless experience from discovery through access and management, addressing a critical workflow gap for our customers."


Improved literature discovery tools represent a significant growth area within scientific research technologies, as organizations seek more efficient ways to navigate the exponentially increasing volume of published research.
