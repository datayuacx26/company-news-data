# Research Solutions Unveils New AI Capabilities To Extract Data From Scientific Literature

HENDERSON, Nev., Feb. 19, 2025 /PRNewswire/ --[Research Solutions, Inc.](https://www.researchsolutions.com/) (NASDAQ: RSSS), a leader in AI solutions built for researchers, announced the launch of Tables in Scite Assistant, a significant advancement in research capabilities that helps researchers automatically extract and analyze data from scientific literature. This enhancement reinforces Scite's position in the growing market for AI research platforms while introducing powerful new capabilities that drive user engagement and satisfaction.


Scite Assistant's Tables functionality allows researchers to extract and structure data from peer-reviewed literature using AI, significantly reducing the manual effort, time, and resources typically taken when conducting literature reviews. The innovative approach allows researchers to leverage AI to extract data from the world's most comprehensive collection of research articles, so they can quickly find key data points in the literature. Going beyond other tools, Scite allows researchers to do this at a larger scale and with more content, which represents a significant advancement in how research organizations manage and analyze scientific information.


"With Scite Tables, we're going beyond AI chat – we're creating entirely new ways for enterprises to interact with scientific literature," said Roy W. Olivier, CEO of Research Solutions. "Early user feedback has been exceptionally positive, with customers reporting unprecedented levels of engagement with research content."


Key capabilities of Scite Tables include:


- Automated extraction of structured data from scientific publications
- Customizable columns to organize research details from cited studies, such as sample size, study limitations, geographic location, cell type, and other key parameters
- Enhanced ability to identify and analyze biomarkers, clinical outcomes, and competitive intelligence
- Streamlined systematic review processes for regulatory and research purposes
- Flexible data organization tools that adapt to specific user workflows


Early adoption across teams like Medical Affairs, Research & Development, and Clinical Operations has already delivered significant value in key areas such as competitive intelligence, biomarker identification, and post-market surveillance. Users report substantial improvements in research efficiency through improved data management and automated extraction of actionable insights, eliminating the need for manual review of full publications and accelerating strategic decision-making processes.


"User feedback has been central to the development of Scite Tables," said Josh Nicholson, PhD, Chief Strategy Officer at Research Solutions. "Our enterprise customers have reported that the ability to customize and organize research data in multiple formats has transformed their approach to literature review and analysis. They reiterated that the key is to provide more than just AI summaries of abstracts, underscoring the critical importance of our partnerships with publishers."
