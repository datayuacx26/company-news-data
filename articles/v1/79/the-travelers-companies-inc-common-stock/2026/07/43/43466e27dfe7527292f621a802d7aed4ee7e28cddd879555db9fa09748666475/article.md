# Travelers Expands Product Liability Insurance to California Life Sciences Companies

### Travelers Expands Product Liability Insurance to California Life Sciences Companies


July 28, 2026


HARTFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


The Travelers Companies, Inc. (NYSE:[TRV](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.travelers.com%2F&esheet=54578243&newsitemid=20260728748625&lan=en-US&anchor=TRV&index=1&md5=00dd3032dc86035b48985bd3fbb6e45a) ) today announced that Travelers Synergy® – its product liability insurance offering for life sciences companies – is now available in California, completing its rollout across all 50 states.


**What *Travelers Synergy* Covers** *Travelers Synergy* is designed to protect life sciences companies against product-related claims at any point in the product development life cycle – from research and development through manufacturing and distribution. Core coverage addresses bodily injury and property damage, and can be supplemented with optional add-ons, including:


- Errors and omissions liability.
- Product recall expenses.
- Clinical trial medical expenses.


**What’s New for *Travelers Synergy* Policyholders** Travelers has introduced broader Sales Professional Liability coverage for life sciences companies whose sales representatives work in operating rooms, procedure rooms or other clinical settings. Available under either a shared policy limit or separate dedicated limit, the optional endorsement covers bodily injury or property damage arising from a sales representative’s actions.


“A company developing its first device has very different needs than one managing a large sales force in clinical settings, and California has no shortage of either,” said Jennifer Ampulski, Assistant Vice President and Life Sciences Lead at Travelers. “ *Travelers Synergy* is built to serve companies at any stage and is the result of more than 40 years working with the life sciences industry.”


**Why California Matters** California is home to more than 17,000 life sciences companies, ranking among the top states in the nation for biotech activity. The state’s intricate regulations and legal requirements can be challenging for companies to navigate, making specialized product liability coverage particularly critical.
