### Travelers Schedules Conference Call to Review Second Quarter 2026 Results


June 12, 2026


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


The Travelers Companies, Inc. (NYSE:[TRV](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.travelers.com%2F&esheet=54552525&newsitemid=20260612689200&lan=en-US&anchor=TRV&index=1&md5=12ac02bded0e67ad917731ed0dee432e) ) will review its second quarter 2026 results at 9 a.m. ET on Friday, July 17, following the release of results earlier that morning.


Investors can access the call via webcast at[investor.travelers.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.travelers.com%2Fhome%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54552525&newsitemid=20260612689200&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.travelers.com&index=2&md5=ab11f34b8a54010b81122a9012e43fbf) and by dialing 888-440-6281 within the United States or 646-960-0218 outside the United States. A slide presentation, statistical supplement and live audio broadcast will be available on the same website.


Following the event, replays will be available via webcast for one year at[investor.travelers.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.travelers.com%2Fhome%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54552525&newsitemid=20260612689200&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.travelers.com&index=3&md5=942d187361bfb87eb3252d7c7b4407a5) and by telephone for seven days by dialing 800-770-2030 within the United States or 647-362-9199 outside the United States. All callers should use conference ID 5449478.
