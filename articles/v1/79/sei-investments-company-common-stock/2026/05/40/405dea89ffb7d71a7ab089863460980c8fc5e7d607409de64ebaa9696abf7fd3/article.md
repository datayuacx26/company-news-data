# SEI to Present at William Blair and Morgan Stanley Conferences

OAKS, Pa.


, May 26, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[SEI](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4695304-1&h=1820972278&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.seic.com%2F&a=SEI) ® (NASDAQ:SEIC) today announced that the company will participate in the William Blair 46th Annual Growth Stock Conference in Chicago, IL and the Morgan Stanley U.S. Financials Conference in New York City.


Sean Denham, SEI's Chief Financial and Operating Officer, will present at the William Blair conference at 4:20 p.m. Eastern time on June 2, 2026. A live webcast of the presentation will be available[here](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4695304-1&h=4149078194&u=https%3A%2F%2Fevent.summitcast.com%2Fview%2FDTqswnj6gGSWZ7ywFcvpAo%2Fguest_book%3Fsession_id%3DboiMY9huz4LLbbn4Dwdrxf&a=here) .


Denham will present at the Morgan Stanley conference at 4 p.m. Eastern time on June 9, 2026. A live webcast will be available[here](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4695304-1&h=4290923607&u=https%3A%2F%2Fevent.webcasts.com%2Fstarthere.jsp%3Fei%3D1764874%26tp_key%3Deb74a6eed3%26tp_special%3D8&a=here) .
