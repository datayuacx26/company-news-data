# Your Analytics Just Joined Discord

Every launch thread has that one message.


##### can someone pull the numbers?


And it just… sits there. Everyone sees it. Everyone scrolls past.
Nobody wants to be the dashboard person today.


So we put the **Amplitude Agent in Discord** .


## What it looks like


Type` /amplitude` and ask a question.


No new tab. No filter fiddling. No screenshot dropped into a conversation that moved on 20 minutes ago.


Follow-ups, one tap away.


## You already ship in Discord


Now the data shows up in the same place the decisions happen.


Not just a faster answer for you, the whole team sees it.


Same channel, no “let me DM you the chart.”
Same time, no one person holding all the context.


And because it’s the Amplitude Agent, it’s answering with your actual product data, not vibes.


## Go say hi


Amplitude.com → Settings → Personal Settings → Profile → Connected Apps → Discord


1. Hit **Connect** to link your Discord account
2. [Add the Amplitude bot](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1520218286057324726&scope=bot%20applications.commands&permissions=8) ( **Discord** **server admin** has to do this part)


1. On the EU data region?[Use this invite link instead](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1524560915717034125&scope=bot%20applications.commands&permissions=8) !


3. Run` /amplitude` in any channel and ask something!


Still stuck?[Discord setup docs](https://amplitude.com/docs/analytics/integrate-discord)
