## How do you build a launch dashboard with AI?


Say you just launched a new homepage for your company, AmpliMoney. Now you want to understand traffic, conversion, and channel performance. Instead of spending hours building this out chart by chart, describe what you need to Global Agent.


##### “Create a marketing performance dashboard for our new homepage launch. Track daily traffic, conversion funnels, from page view to sign-up, traffic sources by channel, and engagement metrics. Include the last 30 days for baseline.”


Global Agent builds the entire dashboard and automatically organizes it. What used to take you half a day now takes minutes.


You have a complete view of traffic trends, conversion funnels, and engagement metrics. That’s everything you need to monitor the launch in a single view.


## How do you monitor launch results without checking dashboards?


Babysitting dashboards is a time suck. You don’t have time to check your dashboard every day, trying to decipher trends on your own.


Instead, set up an Amplitude[dashboard agent](https://amplitude.com/docs/amplitude-ai/dashboard-agent) to analyze changes, compare trends, and flag what matters.


Just connect it to your team Slack channel. Tell it what to look for, and get insights delivered automatically to your whole team without anyone pulling a report.


In this scenario, your dashboard agent flags healthy traffic but weak CTA conversion. You make the call: reposition the CTA, tighten the button copy. The agent keeps watching. A few days later, it's back in Slack with an update that conversions are climbing.
