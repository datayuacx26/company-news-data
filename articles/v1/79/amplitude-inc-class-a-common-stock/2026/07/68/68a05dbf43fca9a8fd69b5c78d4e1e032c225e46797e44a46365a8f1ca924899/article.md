# Amplitude to Host First Quarter 2026 Earnings Webcast on May 6, 2026

**SAN FRANCISCO— April 14, 2026—**[Amplitude, Inc.](https://amplitude.com/) (Nasdaq: AMPL), the leading AI analytics platform, today announced that it will release its financial results for the first quarter of 2026 after market close on Wednesday, May 6, 2026. Amplitude will host a video webcast that day at 2:00 PM PT to discuss its financial results and provide its financial outlook for the second quarter and full year 2026.


The webcast will be available on the Investor Relations section of Amplitude’s website at[investors.amplitude.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.amplitude.com%2F&esheet=52781223&newsitemid=20220718005043&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.amplitude.com&index=2&md5=3423e3366ea036f0a42a00e50b9d3b4e) . A replay of the webcast will be available on the same website a few hours after the conclusion of the event.
