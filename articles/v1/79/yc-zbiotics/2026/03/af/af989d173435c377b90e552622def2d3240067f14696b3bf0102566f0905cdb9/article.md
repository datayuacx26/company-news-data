# Meet the Women Behind Quality at ZBiotics

March is Women's History Month, and we're taking a moment to celebrate two of the remarkable women on our team who help make sure every[ZBiotics product](https://zbiotics.com/products/zbiotics) is something we're genuinely proud to put in customers' hands.


Savannah, our Senior Director of Quality, and Katie, our Quality Manager, bring different backgrounds and career paths to the same mission: building quality systems that are rigorous, science-driven, and worthy of the trust our customers place in us. We sat down with both of them to talk about what brought them to this work, what keeps them motivated, and what they'd tell the next generation of women in science and quality.


## Building quality from the ground up: Savannah's story


Savannah didn't take a straight road to[ZBiotics](https://zbiotics.com/) . She spent over 15 years in food and beverage, working her way through the kinds of large, established companies where quality systems are decades old and change moves slowly. That experience was valuable—but what brought her here was the chance to do something different.


"At those larger companies, you're often working within systems that have been in place for years, decades sometimes, and there is very little opportunity to make impactful change," she says. "What excited me about ZBiotics was the chance to take what I'd learned in my career and help build something from the ground up at a company that genuinely values its science, transparency, and the customer experience."


From her very first interview, it was clear that quality wasn't an afterthought at ZBiotics. "Leadership didn't view quality as just a box to check," she says. "And that's a quality professional's dream."


That shift in environment changes everything about how quality gets done. At many companies, quality teams spend significant energy convincing leadership why investments matter. At ZBiotics, that fight doesn't exist. "Instead of pushing for buy-in, we get to focus on building thoughtful systems and making sure quality shows in the product and the experience our customers have," Savannah explains. "It creates a much more proactive environment."


But building great systems doesn't mean every call is easy. Savannah is candid about the reality of working in a field where protecting people is the job. "If the customer isn't protected, the business doesn't move forward," she says. "Those tough calls are never comfortable. Every quality team has its non-negotiables. There are times when the answer truly is 'no,' with no room for compromise. But more often, the work is about being a strong partner to the business and evaluating risk in a thoughtful, data-backed way."


That steady, collaborative approach has also shaped how Savannah thinks about leadership—especially as a woman who has spent a lot of time in rooms where she wasn't always the default voice in the room.


"I've spent a lot of time in facilities and on plant floors working with engineers and operators, and there have definitely been moments where I've been dismissed or underestimated," she says. "I've been in supplier meetings, client introductions, and even audits where I was the lead communicator, but the default was still to direct questions to the male colleague on my team."


Her response to those moments? Resilience, and a hard-won confidence in her own expertise. "I hit a point in my career where I realized I earned my seat at the table and don't need to prove it every time I walk into a room."


For women aiming for senior roles in quality, regulatory, or operations, Savannah's advice is clear: "Be comfortable with and confident in your expertise. Stand firm when you need to. But also build strong relationships. The most effective leaders I've seen in quality combine technical expertise with a genuine willingness to work together toward whatever the shared goal may be."


For Savannah, the fulfillment of this work is deeply personal. "Knowing that the work we do helps keep people safe and allows them to trust what they're buying is incredibly rewarding," she says. "Quality mostly happens behind the scenes, but it has a real impact on the experience people have with the product."


She's also quick to acknowledge what makes ZBiotics uniquely special. "I genuinely love where I work. I get to work alongside smart, compassionate, and fun people who are all working towards the same goal. There are multiple colleagues I consider a mentor, and I get the honor of being a mentor to others. I feel so incredibly privileged."


## When science meets systems: Katie's story


Katie came to ZBiotics in June 2025 with a background that's equal parts rigorous and curious: a BS in Food Science from Ohio State, a PhD from UC Davis, and hands-on experience in product development and bringing novel products to market. When she joined the quality team, she was drawn in by something specific: the chance to apply a scientific mindset to systems that had never been built before.


"I enjoy that quality is very hands-on with the product," she says. "I was drawn to being a critical part of bringing the best possible products to customers." Her food science training shapes everything about how she approaches the work. "It helps me approach our work with a data-focused and multidisciplinary viewpoint."


That mindset is especially important at ZBiotics, where we’re pioneering new safety and quality standards. "Having extra data points gives us more clarity into the quality and safety of our products, which is extra important when we are making unique products that have never been done before," Katie explains. "Quality is more than checking boxes. We take extra steps to ensure we know what is going on with our products."


For Katie, one of the most rewarding parts of the role has been watching small process improvements ripple outward in meaningful ways. "It's been exciting to see how even small improvements can have meaningful impacts across our team and how we are able to respond to rapidly changing conditions," she says. "Whether that's better data tracking or updating documentation, seeing my work make a difference when we are working with a supplier, partner, or customer has been rewarding."


Katie is also thinking about what's ahead. As ZBiotics continues to advance what’s possible with[genetic engineering](https://zbiotics.com/pages/how-it-works-genetic-engineering) , quality and safety aren't just support functions—they're central to what makes the innovation possible in the first place. "I'm excited about how our company is leading the charge to reimagine what genetic engineering can do," she says. "I know our work is critical to moving innovation forward. I want to see a world where biotechnology is used to solve important problems."


On her journey as a woman in science, Katie counts herself lucky to have had strong role models along the way. "I've been fortunate to have incredible women as managers and mentors at various times in my life," she says. "They modeled how to show up at work with confidence and compassion, which I try to bring to my own roles now."


Her message to young women considering technical or quality-focused paths is direct: find your people. "Finding mentors or role models that can help support and guide you is invaluable. Having those channels to be curious, ask questions, and learn more about what careers can look like is a great tool to build a successful future."


## Quality as a shared value


What Savannah and Katie share—beyond their expertise and their commitment to the work—is a belief that quality isn't something imposed on a company from the outside. It's something that has to be built into the culture from the beginning.


At ZBiotics, that belief runs deep. It's why quality has been a brand pillar since day one, why the team is empowered to make hard calls, and why the people doing this work feel genuinely proud of what they're contributing.


This Women's History Month, we're grateful for the scientists, leaders, mentors, and advocates on[our team](https://zbiotics.com/pages/team-zb) who make that possible—and who show up every day to make sure the products we send to customers are worthy of their trust.
