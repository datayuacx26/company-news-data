# How Customer Feedback Shaped Our New Sugar-to-Fiber Flavors

We launched Sugar-to-Fiber in September 2024—it’s a genetically engineered probiotic designed to[convert some of the sugar you eat into levan](https://zbiotics.com/pages/how-sugar-to-fiber-works) , a rare and beneficial prebiotic fiber. It was built to fit seamlessly into everyday life and help address a problem many of us face: modern diets that are low in fiber.


From the start, customers were excited about the science and its benefits. As more people began using our[Sugar-to-Fiber flavors](https://zbiotics.com/products/sugar-to-fiber) daily, we started hearing very consistent feedback.


A lot of you told us versions of the same thing:


- *“It doesn’t dissolve very well.”*
- *“The flavor is kind of funky.”*
- *“It’s a little grainy.”*
- *“I love what it does—I just wish it were easier to use every day.”*
- *“Didn’t love the taste.”*


None of these comments questioned the benefit of Sugar-to-Fiber. Instead, they pointed to something just as important: the experience. And when a product is designed to be used daily, experience matters.


## Improving the experience: starting with the fundamentals


Before thinking about new flavors, our team focused on the basics.


As feedback continued to roll in, it became clear that improving the Sugar-to-Fiber experience would require changes beyond taste alone. Customers were telling us about graininess, mixability, and how the product felt when used every day—especially in drinks like coffee or smoothies. So our R&D team went back to work to address those friction points directly, while keeping the underlying science exactly the same.


That work led to a reformulation designed to improve texture and help Sugar-to-Fiber dissolve more smoothly across a wide range of foods and drinks. At the same time, we revisited the format itself. Increasing the stick pack size and moving to a standard 30-serving format made it easier to build Sugar-to-Fiber into a[consistent daily habit](https://zbiotics.com/blogs/journal/habit-stacking-science) —one that fits naturally into a full month of use.


These updates happened hand in hand with our flavor work, because experience isn’t defined by any single detail. Taste, texture, and mixability all matter. Improving one without the others wouldn’t have solved the problem customers were actually describing.


## Exploring flavor options through customer sampling


Then, we turned our attention to flavor. In October 2025, we sent out samples of two different flavors of Sugar-to-Fiber:


- An Unflavored option, designed to completely disappear into food and drinks
- A Maple option, designed to feel warm and easy to enjoy—especially in morning routines


The goal of sampling wasn’t to choose a winning flavor. It was to validate whether these improvements actually solved the problems customers had told us about.


The feedback confirmed something important: there wasn’t one perfect experience. Some customers wanted Sugar-to-Fiber to be completely undetectable, while others enjoyed a subtle flavor that complemented their daily rituals. Improving the experience necessitated giving people choices that fit how they already live.


## A seasonal test: limited-edition Maple


Based on the strong positive response to the Maple samples in our first round of feedback, we released a limited-edition Maple flavor during the fall and holiday season. This was a fun seasonal surprise for customers, but more importantly, it allowed us to observe how the updated formulation and flavor performed at a slightly larger scale, in real routines.


The limited release reinforced what we’d been learning all along: when people really enjoy the experience of taking Sugar-to-Fiber, it fits more seamlessly into their everyday routine.


## Introducing two new Sugar-to-Fiber flavors


With feedback from the original launch, the reformulation, customer sampling, and the limited Maple release, the path forward became clear.


Instead of continuing with the original flavor and formulation, we made a deliberate choice to move ahead with two different options—each designed to meet different preferences while delivering the same core benefits.


**Unflavored**


Our Sugar-to-Fiber unflavored option is neutral and designed to fit seamlessly into whatever you’re already drinking or eating. Smoothies, water, juice—you shouldn’t even know it’s there.


**Maple**


Without any added sweetener, the Sugar-to-Fiber maple flavor adds a subtle warmth that complements morning routines. It’s subtle enough to complement your routine without overpowering it, especially in morning rituals like a cup of coffee or yogurt bowl.


## What you told us


Since sampling, the limited Maple launch, and now, the full rollout of both flavors, the feedback has been clear: these changes made Sugar-to-Fiber easier to use and easier to enjoy.


Here are just a few things customers have shared:


- *“I can’t taste it at all. This is exactly what I was hoping for.”*
- *“This fixed my one issue with Sugar-to-Fiber.”*
- *“It mixes so much better now.”*
- *“I loved the science before, but now the experience matches it.”*


Replacing the original flavor with Maple and Unflavored reflects something we at ZBiotics believe deeply and work to live up to: listening doesn’t stop after launch. It continues through iteration, testing, and paying attention to how people actually use our products.


Because results depend on consistency, and consistency depends on experience.
