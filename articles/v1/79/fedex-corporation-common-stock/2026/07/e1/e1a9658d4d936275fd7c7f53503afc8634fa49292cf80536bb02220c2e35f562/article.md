# Wines of Argentina and FedEx Strengthen Strategic Collaboration to Support Argentina's Wine Industry

As part of its ongoing efforts to support the growth of Argentine wine in international markets Wines of Argentina (WofA), the organization responsible for promoting the country’s wine brand abroad, has renewed its long-standing collaboration agreement with FedEx, a global leader in transportation and logistics.


The agreement enhances the efficiency, security, and sustainability of shipments supporting Wines of Argentina’s international activities, particularly the transportation of samples for trade fairs, tastings, and other strategic events. The agreement provides Wines of Argentina and its member wineries with a range of benefits, including preferential rates, specialized logistics support, and packaging solutions specifically designed to protect high-value products such as wine.


Collaboration is especially significant for an industry that depends on precise, reliable, and competitive logistics to expand its presence in international markets. Leveraging its global network and expertise in express transportation, FedEx helps Argentine wineries reach new destinations, meet the evolving demands of global markets, and mitigate risks associated with the shipment of samples.


**First Joint Initiative: Recognizing Sustainability-Focused Journalism**


As the first milestone of this renewed agreement, Wines of Argentina and FedEx launched a special Journalist’s Day initiative in collaboration with 10 Argentine wineries. As part of the program, exclusive gift kits were delivered to nearly 100 journalists and media influencers from Tier 1 and Tier 2 outlets in Buenos Aires and Mendoza.


The kits featured a curated selection of wines representing the diverse regions, styles, and expressions that define Argentine winemaking. The initiative highlighted the quality and richness of the country’s wine industry, while FedEx logistics expertise ensured each package transportation of the kits from Mendoza to Wines of Argentina in Buenos Aires with the care required for such a premium product.


Reflecting the shared values of both organizations, the initiative incorporated sustainability considerations throughout its execution. The delivery was made using an electric vehicle and 100% recyclable packaging, further advancing the company’s ongoing collaboration with the wine industry to optimize wine shipments through safer, more efficient, and sustainable packaging solutions. Designed to protect a delicate product while minimizing transportation risks, these innovations are part of the company’s continued investment in specialized logistics solutions tailored to the sector.


Participating wineries included:

- Bodega Luigi Bosca
- Bodega Vistalba
- Familia Schroeder
- Huentala Wines

**A Collaboration Designed to Enhance the Competitiveness of Argentine Wineries:**


The agreement between Wines of Argentina and FedEx aims to deliver tangible benefits to member wineries, particularly in support of their international promotional efforts, while providing access to the FedEx global network, which connects more than 220 countries and territories worldwide.


Key benefits available in 2026 include:


- Complimentary high-security packaging solutions: **** FedEx will provide specially designed wine shipping boxes, along with a sample reconditioning service for trade shows, tastings, and promotional events. The company will absorb the cost of these materials and services, ensuring no additional expense for participating wineries.
- More sustainable packaging solutions: Developed and manufactured in Argentina, the wine shipping boxes are made from recyclable materials and were specifically designed to meet the needs of the wine industry. The packaging helps protect a delicate product, optimize space utilization, and accommodate shipments of either three or six bottles, combining logistical efficiency with sustainability considerations.
- Tools for more informed and sustainable logistics decisions: FedEx also offers solutions such as[FedEx® Sustainability Insights](https://www.fedex.com/es-ar/about/sustainability/carbon-footprint-insights.html)


, which provides customers with estimated CO₂e emissions data for eligible shipments moving through the company’s network. By increasing visibility into shipment-related emissions, the tool helps businesses make more informed decisions and advance their sustainability strategies.


Wineries interested in learning more about these and other benefits available through the agreement may contact Wines of Argentina for additional information.


Through this collaboration, Wines of Argentina and FedEx are reinforcing their shared commitment to the growth of Argentina’s wine industry, advancing logistics solutions that are safer, more efficient, and aligned with the environmental challenges of today and the future.
