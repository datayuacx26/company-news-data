# FedEx Opens New Station in Caguas, Strengthening its Network in Puerto Rico

FedEx announces the opening of a new station in Caguas, expanding its operations network in Puerto Rico and bringing faster and more convenient service to customers across the eastern region of the island with 20 dedicated routes and extended pick up times.


Located in the Angora Industrial Park, the new station supports both freight and parcel services and is positioned to serve numerous municipalities in eastern Puerto Rico, including Cayey, Cidra, Caguas, Gurabo, Juncos, Las Piedras, Humacao, Yabucoa, Maunabo, Naguabo, Ceiba, and Vieques.


“The opening of our station in Caguas represents an important milestone in optimizing our operational capabilities in Puerto Rico,” said Basil Khalil, vice president of operations for FedEx in the Caribbean and Central America. “By bringing our operations closer to our customers, we are strengthening our ability to support key industries, improve efficiency, and deliver a better overall experience for businesses and residents across the region.”


“On behalf of the Autonomous Municipality of Caguas, and in my capacity as Mayor, I extend my sincere appreciation to FedEx for its investment in our city,” said William Miranda Torres, Mayor of the Autonomous Municipality of Caguas. “The opening of this station marks a meaningful step forward in strengthening logistics and operational efficiency, expanding opportunities for local businesses. We remain committed to working together to foster sustained development and economic growth for our community.”


Previously, service to the eastern region of the island was supported by the FedEx station in Carolina. The addition of the Caguas facility allows FedEx to rebalance capacity and strengthen its network flexibility. The new location features modern infrastructure, including a FedEx Ship Center, updated office space, and an efficient working environment for its 30 team members.


The opening underscores the long-term commitment of FedEx to Puerto Rico, reinforcing support for local businesses and vital industries such as manufacturing and healthcare, while enhancing the island’s connectivity to global markets.
