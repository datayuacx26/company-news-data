# FedEx Recognized by General Motors for Supply Chain Excellence

**Memphis, Tenn., May 20, 2026** — FedEx has been awarded 2025 Supplier of the Year in the Creativity Team of Customer Care and Aftersales (CCA) Logistics by GM honoring its consistent performance, reliability, and long‑standing relationship of 35 years supporting its supply chain operations.


“Our customers are at the center of everything we do,” said Brie Carere, executive vice president and chief customer officer, FedEx. “This award highlights the trust built through our strong relationship with GM, as well as our team’s dedication to delivering creative, reliable solutions. As the heartbeat of the industrial economy we are committed to the entire automotive industry, from global manufacturers to aftermarket shippers.”


Over the years, FedEx has supported GM across a range of operational needs, demonstrating agility and resilience even during periods of heightened disruption.


“Supplier of the Year is one of those key moments our whole team looks forward to every year because it highlights the partnerships behind every vehicle we build,” said Shilpan Amin, senior vice president, global chief procurement and supply chain officer, General Motors. The results our suppliers deliver throughout the entire product development cycle are central to our ability to deliver world-class vehicles to our customers. When our suppliers, such as FedEx, lean in with us on new technology and flawless execution, we can move faster, compete harder, and unlock more value across the entire supply chain.”


Support for GM is delivered by a dedicated team that works closely with the automaker to keep parts and shipments moving efficiently. This recognition reflects a collective effort across operations, account management, and frontline teams working together to serve a leading automotive manufacturer.


FedEx was formally recognized at GM’s supplier event in Austin, TX, where representatives from both companies came together to celebrate the achievement. FedEx has received the Supplier of the Year Award 7 times over the past 8 years.

**About GM Supplier of the Year Award:**
In 2025, GM’s 34th annual Supplier of the Year and Overdrive awards recognize 103 suppliers spanning 14 countries. These suppliers deliver outstanding performance, partnership and innovation in support of GM’s global operations.Awardees are selected by a global GM team based on performance across key categories such as safety, innovation, execution, resilience and customer support, along with their alignment to GM’s core values and strategic priorities.
