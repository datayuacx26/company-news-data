# Luis Carlos Honored as 14th Purple Eagle at FedEx St. Jude Championship

**MEMPHIS, Tenn., August 12, 2026 –** Today, 15-year-old Luis Carlos was honored as the 14th Purple Eagle during a special plane dedication ceremony at the FedEx St. Jude Championship at TPC Southwind. Luis Carlos is the grandson of Kevin Brown, a 10-year FedEx team member on the Properties and Facilities Maintenance team, and a patient at[St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital®](https://nam12.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.stjude.org%2F&data=05%7C02%7CBrian.Didlake%40alsac.stjude.org%7C7688b89a78414f2bd01608deef008ae9%7C62a96f9aa5614bfba97870a73a08dc02%7C0%7C0%7C639210982772432886%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=vzkE17IV7wum%2BwSuYeMee1qfHCrJqgendEEk2APTLAc%3D&reserved=0)


.


The Purple Eagle program, a cherished FedEx tradition since 2012, honors a St. Jude patient who is the child or grandchild of a FedEx team member. As the 2026 honoree, Luis Carlos will have his name permanently displayed alongside the iconic St. Jude logo on a FedEx Cessna Caravan aircraft.


In 2022, at the age of 11, Luis Carlos was diagnosed with medulloblastoma, a cancerous brain tumor. His family turned to St. Jude, where he underwent surgery followed by proton beam radiotherapy and chemotherapy. Luis Carlos successfully completed his treatments in 2023. Today, he is a thriving teenager who enjoys golfing, playing baseball, and spending time with his family, including being a proud older brother to his two younger sisters, Luna and Nova Grace.


His mother, Maria, described the experience at St. Jude, saying, “Everything felt so surreal when we first got the diagnosis. But everybody at St. Jude was wonderful, completely wonderful, and they took great care of him through and through.”


“Luis Carlos’ incredible resilience, positive attitude, and strength of spirit are a true inspiration to our entire team,” said Josh Canfield, senior vice president of Airline Technical Operations at FedEx. “We are privileged to continue this beautiful tradition, celebrating his journey while shining a light on the life-saving mission of St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital.”


As Luis Carlos looks forward to reaching major teenage milestones like getting his driver’s license and landing his first job, his named Cessna Caravan will fly as a symbol of hope and recovery across the skies, joining 13 other planes decorated with the names of Purple Eagle honorees, a testament to the longstanding FedEx and St. Jude partnership.


“The FedEx Purple Eagle ceremony is a powerful reminder of the special bond between FedEx and St. Jude families,” said Ike Anand, President and CEO of[ALSAC](https://nam12.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.stjude.org%2Fabout-st-jude%2Fhistory%2Falsac.html&data=05%7C02%7CBrian.Didlake%40alsac.stjude.org%7C7688b89a78414f2bd01608deef008ae9%7C62a96f9aa5614bfba97870a73a08dc02%7C0%7C0%7C639210982772458527%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=uNJ4gj3BR0JEgAHx1LHbZwjiTuV8yNlebL2BHXxNfnE%3D&reserved=0)


, the fundraising and awareness organization for St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital. “For decades, FedEx has helped advance the St. Jude mission through its extraordinary partnership and generosity. Today, we celebrate Luis Carlos’ inspiring story and express our deep gratitude for the compassion and commitment FedEx continues to demonstrate for St. Jude patients and families. Their support helps make it possible for St. Jude to advance research and treatment so that more children can survive and thrive.”


The Purple Eagle ceremony is part of the opening schedule of the 2026 FedEx St. Jude Championship, the first event of the FedEx Cup Playoffs.
