# FedEx Vietnam Wins Two Prestigious HR Asia Awards for Third Consecutive Year

Federal Express Corporation, one of the world’s largest express transportation companies, has once again been recognized as one of HR Asia’s Best Companies to Work for in Asia and received the Sustainable Workplace Award in Vietnam. This marks the third consecutive year FedEx Vietnam has earned both prestigious accolades.


The recognition reflects FedEx Vietnam’s continued commitment to creating an inclusive and supportive workplace where team members can thrive, while embedding sustainability into its business practices and contributing positively to the communities it serves. The HR Asia Awards recognize organizations across the region that demonstrate strong employee engagement, workplace excellence and a commitment to responsible and sustainable business practices. Receiving both awards for three consecutive years reinforces the strength and consistency of the culture FedEx Vietnam has built together with its team members.


“We are deeply honored to receive these recognitions from HR Asia for the third consecutive year,” said Ee-Hui Tan, managing director of FedEx Vietnam and Cambodia. “The past year was pivotal for FedEx in Vietnam as we expanded our operations to better serve our customers. Throughout it all, our people supported one another, learned together, and overcame challenges as one team. These awards reflect their resilience, care, and commitment, and the stronger organization we are building for our customers and communities.”


As part of its continued investment in Vietnam, FedEx has expanded its local workforce. The expansion has strengthened the company’s operational and support capabilities while creating new employment and career development opportunities in the market.


Workforce growth also required team members across the organization to welcome and support new colleagues, share knowledge, and help them adapt to new roles, systems, and ways of working. This collective effort reflects a culture in which employees are encouraged to collaborate, learn from one another, and contribute to the success of the wider team.


Together, the two awards demonstrate how FedEx Vietnam’s people and sustainability commitments reinforce one another. By investing in its workforce, supporting employees through periods of change, and encouraging them to contribute to a more sustainable future, FedEx is building a stronger organization for its people, customers, and communities. FedEx Vietnam remains committed to building on this recognition by continuing to invest in its people, strengthen its workplace culture, and advance responsible business practices.
