For frozen bakery producers, being first isn’t enough. You also have to be fast; consistently, reliably and at scale.


One of our customers,[Bakerman](https://bakerman.de/en/about-us) – a producer in frozen baked goods – has built its business around this reality. By identifying trends early and rapidly bringing new products to market, they compete in narrow windows where timing determines everything. In this environment, success doesn’t go to the biggest player – it goes to the fastest.


But speed doesn’t stop at product development. To truly win, it has to extend across the entire cold chain: from production and storage to distribution and final delivery.


Because in a market that moves this quickly, the supply chain doesn’t just support growth – it defines it.


## The challenge: Turning volatility into opportunity


Trend-driven frozen categories operate on a different rhythm. Demand can spike overnight and disappear just as fast. Unlike traditional products, forecasting is limited – and hesitation comes at a cost.


This creates a high-pressure operating environment:


- Retail listing windows measured in weeks, not months
- Sudden and unpredictable capacity constraints
- Increasing service expectations, including complex delivery requirements
- Zero tolerance for delays: miss the window, lose the opportunity


In one critical moment, a new product opportunity emerged with just weeks to execute – but without the infrastructure in place to support it. No available capacity. No logistics plan. No margin for error.


This is the reality for fast-moving food manufacturers today: the ability to act quickly is only as strong as the supply chain behind it.


## The solution: Building a cold chain designed for speed


To compete in this environment, Bakerman needed more than storage and transport. They needed a cold chain that could move at the same pace as their ambition.


Together with Lineage, the focus shifted from simply managing logistics to enabling growth through agility, visibility and execution – supporting a model where 48-hour supply is mandatory and trend-led launches can move from concept to shelf in weeks, not months.


This meant:


- Flexible[frozen storage capacity](https://www.onelineage.com/services/cold-storage-warehousing) to absorb demand spikes and support rapid product launches, including sudden surges up to 3x volume
- Operational readiness to meet compressed retail timelines with confidence, including launches as fast as three weeks from decision to shelf
- Reliable[distribution](https://www.onelineage.com/services/foodservice-solutions) execution aligned to customer expectations, including 48-hour requirements across European markets such as Spain and Poland
- Seamless coordination across the supply chain, reducing friction and enabling faster decision-making


The result is a cold chain that doesn’t slow innovation down but actively accelerates it.


## The impact: Speed that builds trust and drives growth


In fast-moving categories, execution is everything. When products consistently reach shelves on time, even under pressure, something powerful happens: trust is built.


With a responsive cold chain in place, Bakerman can:


- Secure more retail listings within tight timeframes (as fast as 4 weeks to listing)
- Protect high-stakes launches where time-to-shelf can be six weeks from idea, or three weeks from decision
- Meet rising service expectations, including 48-hour supply requirements across Europe
- Scale innovation with more confidence through volatility, even when demand shifts drive needs up to 3x volume


Because every successful delivery does more than fulfill an order – it reinforces reliability. And in competitive retail environments, reliability wins repeat business.
