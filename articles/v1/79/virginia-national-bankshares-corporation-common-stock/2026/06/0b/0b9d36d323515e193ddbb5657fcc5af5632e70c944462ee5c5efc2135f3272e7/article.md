## Botanical Garden of the Piedmont Garden Groundbreaking Ceremony


Virginia National Bank was thrilled to attend the **[Groundbreaking Ceremony](https://piedmontgarden.org/Groundbreaking2026/)** on June 4, 2026 to mark the important milestone for the **[Botanical Garden of the Piedmont](https://piedmontgarden.org/)** .


This dedication of the 15-acre site in McIntire Park in Charlottesville is a momentous occasion that signals the next chapter of the Garden. Construction is beginning and the Garden is moving from vision to visible progress. This site will grow into a large landscape of gardens and spaces for community members. Learn about the future garden **[here](https://piedmontgarden.org/future-garden/)** .


Groundbreaking ceremony


Wendy Stone, Trust & Estate Services; Abbie Ford, Chief Corporate Services Officer; and Tim Sims, Commercial Lender of Virginia National Bank attended the ceremony.
