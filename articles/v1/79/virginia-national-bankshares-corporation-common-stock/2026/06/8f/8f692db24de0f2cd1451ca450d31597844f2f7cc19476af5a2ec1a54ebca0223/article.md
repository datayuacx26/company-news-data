## Celebrating 250 Years of America


Virginia National Bank is looking forward to celebrating the 250th Anniversary of the United States of America and the signing of the Declaration of Independence!


As your community bank, we are proud to commemorate America’s 250th Anniversary and are honored to play a small part in helping our community thrive. Together, we continue to invest in what matters most – our neighbors, local businesses, and in the future generations that will shape America’s next 250 years.


***Happy 250th Anniversary, America!***
