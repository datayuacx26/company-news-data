# VNB Celebrates 28th Anniversary!

[« BACK](https://www.vnb.com/blog)


July 23, 2026


## VNB Celebrates 28th Anniversary!


We invite you to celebrate Virginia National Bank's 28th Anniversary on Wednesday, July 29th, at all VNB locations. Our branches will have sweet treats available for clients who drop in!


In addition, our headquarters at Pantops in Charlottesville will have an extra special celebration! From 11:00 a.m. and 1:00 p.m. on July 29th,[Z95.1 radio station](https://www.z951.com/) will join our anniversary bash with a live radio broadcast with Lisa Allen! Stop by for great music, food provided by[The Catering Outfit](https://www.cateringoutfit.com/) ,[MarieBette Café & Bakery](http://www.mariebette.com/) pastries and cake, and giveaways!


Virginia National Bank opened its doors to the public on July 29, 1998 at the historic downtown mall in Charlottesville. From the beginning, our mission has been to provide great personal service, local access to decision makers, fast response, and continuity in your relationship.


As a true community bank, we not only meet the needs of our community, but we are also a part of its growth.


As we continue to grow, we will never lose sight of the critical ingredients to our continued success. Exceptional service begins and ends with people. That's why we say, "It's all about people ... and always will be."


**VNB thanks our clients, shareholders, and community members for the 28 years of serving you!**
