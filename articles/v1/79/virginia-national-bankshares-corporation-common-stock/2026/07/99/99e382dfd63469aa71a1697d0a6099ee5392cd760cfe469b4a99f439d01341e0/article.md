## Debit Card Sweepstakes


### [Official Contest Rules](https://www.vnb.com/images/uploads/Virginia_National_Bank_Debit_Card_Sweepstakes_Official_Rules_-_07-08-2026_Final__.pdf)


**NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. A PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING.**


### 1. SPONSOR


The Virginia National Bank Debit Card Sweepstakes (“Promotion”) is sponsored by Virginia National Bank (“VNB” or “Sponsor”), headquartered in Charlottesville, Virginia.


### 2. PROMOTION PERIOD


The Promotion begins at 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on July 15, 2026, and ends at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on August 28, 2026 (“Promotion Period”).


### 3. ELIGIBILITY


The Promotion is open to natural persons who:


- Are eighteen (18) years of age or older;
- Maintain or open a Virginia National Bank consumer checking account during the Promotion Period;
- Possess a debit card linked to an eligible VNB consumer checking account; and
- Are customers in good standing at the time of the applicable drawing.


For purposes of these Official Rules, “good standing” means the customer’s consumer checking account is open and not overdrawn at the time of the drawing.


Joint account holders are eligible to participate. Entries are attributed to the individual debit cardholder whose qualifying transaction generated the entry.


Employees, officers, directors, and immediate family members of Virginia National Bank are not eligible to participate. Immediate family members include spouses, parents, children, siblings, grandparents, grandchildren, and their respective spouses.


Void where prohibited by law.


### 4. HOW TO ENTER


### *A. Debit Card Entries*


Eligible participants will automatically receive one (1) entry for each settled debit card purchase transaction of twenty dollars ($20.00) or more posted to an eligible Virginia National Bank consumer checking account during the applicable weekly entry period.


Eligible transactions include PIN-based and signature-based debit card purchases.


The following transactions are not eligible:


- ATM withdrawals;
- Returned transactions;
- Reversed transactions;
- Fraudulent transactions;
- Transactions under dispute or subject to chargeback;
- Any transaction that does not fully settle during the applicable entry period.


There is no limit to the number of entries that may be earned through eligible debit card transactions.


### *B. Alternative Method of Entry (“AMOE”)*


To enter without making a purchase, hand-print your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address on a 3” x 5” card and mail it to:


Virginia National Bank
Debit Card Sweepstakes
P.O. Box 2853
Charlottesville, VA 22902


Each properly completed 3” x 5” card submitted in a separate envelope and received during the applicable weekly entry period will constitute one (1) entry.


There is no limit to the number of mail-in entries an eligible participant may submit; however, each entry must be mailed separately and received by Sponsor during the applicable weekly entry period to be eligible for that week’s drawing.


Mechanically reproduced, photocopied, bulk-mailed, automated, or otherwise artificially generated entries are void and may be disqualified at Sponsor’s sole discretion.


### 5. WEEKLY ENTRY PERIODS AND DRAWINGS


Entries are eligible only for the weekly drawing corresponding to the entry period in which they are received or posted. Entries do not carry over into future drawings.


Winners will be drawn at random from eligible entries received during the prior period (seven-day period beginning on Wednesday and ending on Tuesday). For example, the drawing held on July 24, 2026, will be from all eligible entries received on and between July 15, 2026, and July 21, 2026.


Random drawings will be conducted by Virginia National Bank’s Marketing Department on or about the following dates:


- July 24, 2026
- July 31, 2026
- August 7, 2026
- August 14, 2026
- August 21, 2026
- August 28, 2026


Sponsor’s decisions regarding all drawings and winners are final and binding.

Three (3) winners will be randomly selected in each weekly drawing.


Each winner will receive:


**One (1) prize consisting of a $250.00 credit deposited into the winner’s eligible Virginia National Bank consumer checking account.**


Prize credits will be deposited on the next business day following the applicable drawing.


A participant may win only one (1) prize during the Promotion Period.


The total maximum prize pool available during the Promotion is $4,500.00.


Prizes are non-transferable and non-assignable except at Sponsor’s sole discretion.
