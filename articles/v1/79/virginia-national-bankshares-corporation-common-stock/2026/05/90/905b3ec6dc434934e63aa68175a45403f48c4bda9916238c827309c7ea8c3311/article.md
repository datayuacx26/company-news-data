## VNB Supports the 25th Annual Hospice of the Piedmont 5K


Team VNB had a wonderful time at the 25th annual Hospice of the Piedmont 5K!


Virginia National Bank is a proud sponsor of this fundraising event for walkers and runners of all ages and abilities. The final stretch of the race, the Memorial Mile, is lined with names of those we have lost – honoring their memory.


All proceeds from the 5K support the Hospice of the Piedmont’s services to provide no-cost care for exceptional hospice, palliative care, and grief support to patients, families, and bereaved community members in Central Virginia.


Learn more at **[www.hopva.org](http://www.hopva.org/)** .
