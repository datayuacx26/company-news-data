### Validation Is Not the Same as Automation


When an invoice hits a government portal, the network checks its structure against local XML schemas. Once the tax authority accepts it, your billing team still has to track payment status, work disputes, and reconcile the transaction in the ledger. Different job entirely.


Without native workflow tools, you are building custom middleware to bridge the compliance network and your internal AR systems. That overhead bites hardest when a tax authority updates its technical specs and IT has to adjust data mappings to avoid rejections.

The difference lies in the distinction between document validation and end-to-end workflow automation. ### 3. What happens if I ignore e-invoicing mandates?


Non-compliance carries financial penalties, audit failures, and operational disruptions. Depending on the jurisdiction, tax authorities may impose flat fines per incorrect invoice or annual penalties for systematic non-compliance. In some regions, government portals will immediately reject transactions that lack the required electronic formatting or QR codes, blocking payments entirely.


### 4. Should I trust Pagero’s online review scores?


It is best to weight documented enterprise deployments over aggregate star ratings.
