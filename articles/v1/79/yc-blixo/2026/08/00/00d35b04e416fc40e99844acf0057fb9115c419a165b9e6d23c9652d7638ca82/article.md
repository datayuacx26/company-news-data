## Why AR Automation Matters


Pagero, on its AR side, is a compliance-first invoicing tool. That’s powerful if your bottleneck is getting invoices out the door across multiple tax jurisdictions. It’s less powerful if your real problem is converting those delivered invoices into collected cash, which is exactly where recurring-revenue businesses get stuck.


The infrastructure is built to handle complex international tax mandates, and it does. But compliance and collections aren’t the same capability. The stretch between “invoice sent” and “payment received” is where subscription businesses quietly lose cash flow.


### The real cost of manual AR


77% of AR teams say manual tasks overwhelm them. That’s not just an efficiency problem. It’s a cash-flow problem. Teams spend about 30% of their time gathering information for collections and only 20% actually talking to customers. When you’re hunting down invoice statuses instead of discussing payment plans or clearing disputes, you’re not collecting faster. You’re just busy.


Manual AR creates three problems. Late or wrong-format invoices delay cash and give customers an excuse to pay late. Errors trigger disputes: 52% of CFOs at highly automated firms reported fewer invoicing errors after automation, so the inverse holds, manual processes breed the dispute friction that slows collections. And without visibility into payment behavior, you’re reacting to late payments instead of predicting them.


### Who benefits most from AR automation


High invoice volume sees the fastest ROI. Send hundreds or thousands of invoices a month and automation pays for itself on labor alone. Some customers report cost reductions of up to 80% after implementing AR automation, a figure that reflects both direct labor savings and the indirect gains from faster collections and fewer disputes.


**Subscription businesses** need a different kind of automation. Your invoices aren’t one-off transactions. They’re recurring touchpoints in an ongoing relationship. When a payment fails, you want dunning logic that retries intelligently without damaging goodwill. When a customer disputes a charge, you want their payment history and subscription status in front of you before you decide how to respond. Pagero handles the delivery. It doesn’t handle relationship-aware collections.


**Cross-border businesses** benefit most from Pagero’s core strength. If you invoice into markets with mandatory e-invoicing, government clearance, or complex VAT rules, the compliance engine is a real advantage. Companies operating across the EU, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific hit different formats, tax calculations, and submission protocols in each market. One business that integrated AR with its CRM saw a 40% reduction in invoice disputes, which counts double when the disputes involve international tax compliance.


### What automation actually improves


62% of businesses saw Days Sales Outstanding improve after automating AR. That’s the metric that matters: how long it takes to go from invoice to cash. Faster invoicing shortens that timeline. Automated reminders keep invoices top of mind without manual chasing. Payment-gateway integration strips friction out of the payment step itself.


But DSO isn’t only about speed. It’s about cutting the time your team spends on non-collection work. When they’re gathering data instead of talking to customers, they’re optimizing for busywork, not cash flow. Automation shifts the hours from information-gathering to decisions. Real-time dashboards for aging, payment trends, and customer risk scores let teams prioritize high-value accounts and step in before small issues turn into cash-flow ones.


That gap is sharpest in subscriptions. You can automate delivery, but if your dunning logic ignores customer lifetime value, tenure, or past behavior, you treat every late payment the same. A customer three years into a $50K annual contract who missed one payment needs a different touch than a two-month-old $500 MRR account with three failed charges. Pagero delivers the invoice. It doesn’t deliver that collections intelligence.


## Automated Invoicing with Pagero


The invoicing engine is where Pagero earns its reputation. It handles **touchless distribution** , format validation, and audit-ready e-archiving with real polish. If your job is getting a clean invoice out the door across a dozen tax regimes, this is strong software.


The payoff is workflow speed. Swap manual data entry for automated validation and finance teams move from multi-day processing cycles to same-day turnarounds. Fewer keying errors, less rework, more time for work that actually needs a human.


### How does touchless distribution actually work?


**Touchless distribution** means invoices get created, validated, and delivered across formats with no manual keying. Pagero adapts each document to the recipient’s requirements, converting between standards like PEPPOL BIS, UBL, cXML, and EDI, then handling government clearance and currency conversion where local rules demand it.


That flexibility matters because your customers sit at different maturity levels. Some want structured e-invoices. Others still expect a PDF. Pagero sends paper, PDF, and electronic formats so nobody gets left behind.


There’s a catch worth naming, though. Pagero’s own e-invoicing guide argues that only **true system-to-system exchange** delivers the measurable gains, and that multi-format support is really a stepping stone. PDF automation buys adoption. The cost and speed wins come from genuine electronic exchange. Treat mixed formats as a migration phase, not the destination.


### Do paper savings or time savings drive the ROI?


Administrative time savings help organizations of every size, but physical distribution costs scale hard with volume. For high-volume enterprises, cutting physical mailings is a direct, measurable drop in overhead.


For smaller operations, the return comes mostly from reclaiming staff hours once lost to manual data entry and error correction.


### Where does the analytics gap leave subscription teams?


Analytics is where automated invoicing quietly stops short of collections. Industry surveys put late payment on roughly **half of all B2B invoices** , with the average buyer settling about **two weeks past terms** . Most teams speed up delivery and never use the resulting data to time follow-ups or spot which accounts are about to slip.


For recurring revenue, that’s the whole game. E-invoicing tells you an invoice went out and landed. It doesn’t run the subscription-aware dunning or the human payment conversations that recover a lapsed renewal. That’s the layer Blixo’s automated collections and subscription management focus on.

## Pagero Network Integration and Security


On security, the story is genuinely strong. Invoices move through the network encrypted end to end, sealed with digital signatures, and stored in **audit-ready e-archives** that satisfy country-specific retention rules. If a tax authority asks for proof three years later, it’s there.


That posture is what Pagero customers value most. On the network and compliance layer you get **format validation** , government clearance handling, and tamper-evident storage baked in. As Katarina Rhodin, Global Process Architect Finance at Munters, put it:


> “It is important to stress that e-invoices are much more secure. You always know where you have the invoice. It won’t get lost somewhere.”


### How does Pagero network integration protect your data?


Integration works by connecting your ERP or financial system into a shared exchange, so documents flow system-to-system instead of by email or PDF. Each invoice is encrypted in transit, signed, and validated against the recipient’s local requirements before delivery. That closes the door on lost or altered documents.


The security benefits are real. A signed, cleared invoice proves the document arrived intact. It says nothing about whether the money followed. Most teams secure and deliver flawlessly, then treat everything after delivery as a separate problem no one owns.


### Where does secure delivery stop being enough?


Secure delivery gets a clean invoice into the buyer’s system. It does nothing for the conversation when payment stalls. Encryption confirms who sent a document and that no one tampered with it, but it can’t tell you which accounts are about to slip past terms or which customer needs a call today. Tamper-evident storage doesn’t move that needle.


This is the part that matters for subscription revenue. When a renewal invoice goes unpaid, the risk isn’t a lost document. It’s a churned customer. A locked-down archive won’t reopen that relationship. A human-aware dunning sequence can.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### 1. Does Pagero handle collections and dunning for recurring invoices?


Pagero focuses on compliant invoice delivery across its global network but offers limited collections capabilities. It tracks invoice status but doesn’t provide subscription-aware dunning sequences, automated payment reminders across multiple channels, or intelligent prioritization of delinquent accounts that recurring revenue businesses need.


### 2. At what invoice volume do paper and postage savings justify automation costs?


The financial return on eliminating paper processes scales with invoice volume. For high-volume enterprises, the direct savings on printing, paper, and postage quickly justify the implementation costs.


### 3. Can I use Pagero for domestic-only invoicing without international compliance needs?


You can, but Pagero’s core strength is cross-border tax compliance and multi-jurisdiction e-invoicing. If you invoice domestically in one country without complex regulatory mandates, you’re paying for compliance infrastructure you won’t use while missing subscription-focused collections features that matter more for recurring revenue.


### 4. What happens after Pagero delivers an invoice and the customer doesn’t pay?


Pagero confirms delivery and tracks invoice status but doesn’t automate follow-up. Teams still handle collections manually, deciding which accounts to chase, timing reminders, and conducting payment conversations. That gap between delivered invoice and collected cash is where subscription businesses lose cash flow despite automated invoicing.


### 5. How do format conversion and touchless distribution reduce invoice errors?


Pagero automatically converts invoices between various electronic data interchange and XML standards while validating them against recipient requirements before transmission. This process eliminates manual data entry and format mismatches. By ensuring compliance before delivery, organizations significantly reduce billing discrepancies, which in turn minimizes the payment disputes that typically delay cash flow.


### 6. Why does subscription billing need different collections logic than transactional invoicing?


Subscription invoices represent ongoing customer relationships rather than isolated transactions. When a payment fails, the recovery process must account for customer history, lifetime value, and account health. An established client experiencing a temporary payment failure requires a collaborative, soft-touch resolution, whereas a new account with immediate payment issues may require automated, structured reminders to protect cash flow without causing unnecessary churn.
