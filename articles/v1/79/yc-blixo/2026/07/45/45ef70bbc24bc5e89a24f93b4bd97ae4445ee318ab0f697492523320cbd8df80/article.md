- Failed payments drive 20%–40% of subscription churn, per Stripe. That makes dunning one of the cheapest revenue levers you have.
- Recovery speed comes down to one number: average days to cash after a missed payment. Mid-range speed with high customer-impact tends to beat pure speed.
- Rule-based dunning engines go live in a day, but expect to tune them weekly to keep collection rates from drifting.
- AI-driven platforms need a two-week pilot to train the model. After that, monthly upkeep drops below two hours.
- ERP-integrated suites take multi-week rollouts and usually a dedicated analyst, but they match cash better and need far fewer manual tweaks.

### [How Do We Connect Dunning Logic to Payment Gateways?](https://blixo.com/blog/en/post/dunning-and-collections-automation-for-scalable-subscription-businesses-38cf/#how-do-we-connect-dunning-logic-to-payment-gateways)


Through webhook listeners and idempotent retry endpoints. The gateway posts a` payment_failed` event to a secure URL; the dunning engine records it, runs the smart-retry schedule, and calls the gateway’s retry endpoint with the original transaction ID. Because the call is idempotent, repeat attempts never double-charge the card. Double-charging is the fastest way to lose the trust you’re trying to protect.


- **Webhook security** : verify signatures and enforce TLS so no one can spoof a failure.
- **Retry payload** : include` transaction_id` ,` amount` , and a` retry_reason` flag so the gateway can apply issuer-specific rules.
- **Response handling** : read success, temporary decline, or permanent failure codes to decide between a nudge and an escalation.


Keep the integration responsive and you fix cash-flow timing without burying the customer in redundant emails.


### [What Data Mapping Practices Keep the Customer Experience Intact?](https://blixo.com/blog/en/post/dunning-and-collections-automation-for-scalable-subscription-businesses-38cf/#what-data-mapping-practices-keep-the-customer-experience-intact)


We map three core objects between the billing system and the dunning workflow: customer profile, payment method, and invoice status. A stable` customer_id` is the primary key, and every gateway event carries it so the billing record updates instantly. When an Account Updater service flags an expired card, Blixo can refresh the stored payment token before the next retry, so the customer never feels the friction.


- **Field alignment** : standardize` last4` ,` expiry_month` , and` expiry_year` across systems so nothing mismatches.
- **State machine** : define clear states (` pending` ,` retrying` ,` awaiting_customer` ,` canceled` ) with transition rules that trigger the right channel.
- **Audit trail** : log every sync event with timestamps. That log becomes the source of truth for support and cuts manual investigation time.


When the data stays consistent, your messages can name the exact problem (“your card ending in 1234 expired”) and the tone reads helpful instead of accusatory.
