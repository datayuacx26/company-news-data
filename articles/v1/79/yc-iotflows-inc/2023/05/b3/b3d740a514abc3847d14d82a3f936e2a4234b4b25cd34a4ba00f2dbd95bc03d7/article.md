# Safeguarding the Manufacturing Industry

In an increasingly digitalized world, the manufacturing industry plays a critical role in the global economy. However, this sector's reliance on complex interconnected systems also makes it an attractive target for cybercriminals. The year 2021 witnessed a surge in cyberattacks against manufacturers, resulting in substantial financial losses. ### The Vulnerability of the Manufacturing Industry


The manufacturing industry's vulnerability to cyberattacks stems from its reliance on interconnected systems and the immense value of its data and intellectual property. Disrupting manufacturing processes, stealing sensitive information, or compromising critical infrastructure can have devastating consequences, both financially and operationally. With the increasing integration of Internet of Things (IoT) devices, the attack surface has expanded, leaving manufacturers exposed to a wide range of cyber threats.
