# Top OEE monitoring tools for manufacturing

Manufacturing companies are constantly looking for ways to increase efficiency and productivity, reduce downtime, and improve the overall effectiveness of their operations. OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) is a key performance indicator that measures the effectiveness of a manufacturing operation. OEE monitoring tools provide real-time visibility into the performance of manufacturing equipment, enabling manufacturers to identify bottlenecks, optimize production processes, and improve efficiency.
