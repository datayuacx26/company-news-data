# Travel + Leisure Co. Named a 2026-2027 Best Company to Work For by U.S. News & World Report


Jun 30, 2026 9:00am EDT


ORLANDO, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Travel + Leisure Co.](https://www.travelandleisureco.com/) (NYSE: TNL), a leading leisure travel company, today announced that U.S. News & World Report has named the company among its 2026-2027 list of Best Companies to Work For.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260630361957/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260630361957/en/)


Travel + Leisure Co. (NYSE: TNL), a leading leisure travel company, today announced that U.S. News & World Report has named the company among its 2026-2027 list of Best Companies to Work For.


Travel + Leisure Co. was recognized in four categories: Best Companies to Work For (Overall), Best Companies to Work For – Hospitality and Entertainment, Best Companies to Work For – South, and Best Companies to Work For – Supporting Family Caregiving. Companies ranking in the top 25% within their industry and region receive the Best Companies designation.


To help employees and job seekers identify organizations that align with their priorities, U.S. News evaluated the 5,000 largest publicly traded U.S. companies across a range of workplace factors.


The rankings evaluate organizations across key workplace attributes, including pay and benefits, work-life balance and flexibility, job and organizational stability, physical and psychological well-being, sense of belonging and recognition, and opportunities for career growth and development.


The recognition underscores Travel + Leisure's commitment to fostering a workplace where associates can grow, lead, and thrive. Through investments in career development, leadership opportunities, well-being programs, workplace flexibility, and support for working families, the company empowers associates to succeed both professionally and personally.


"We are honored to be recognized as a Best Company to Work For," said Kimberly Marshall, Chief Human Resources Officer of Travel + Leisure Co. "This recognition reflects the dedication of our associates around the world and our continued focus on fostering a workplace where they can thrive and deliver exceptional experiences for our owners, members, and guests."

**About Travel + Leisure Co.**


Travel + Leisure Co. (NYSE: TNL) is a leading leisure travel company, providing more than six million vacations to travelers around the world every year. The company operates a diverse portfolio of vacation ownership, travel club, and lifestyle travel brands designed to meet the needs of the modern leisure traveler, whether they’re traversing the globe or enjoying destinations closer to home. This includes experiential brands such as Sports Illustrated Resorts, Eddie Bauer Adventure Club, Margaritaville Vacation Club, and Accor Vacation Club, as well as cornerstone brands, Club Wyndham, WorldMark, and RCI. With hospitality and responsible tourism at its heart, the company’s more than 19,000 dedicated associates worldwide help fulfill its mission to put the world on vacation.
