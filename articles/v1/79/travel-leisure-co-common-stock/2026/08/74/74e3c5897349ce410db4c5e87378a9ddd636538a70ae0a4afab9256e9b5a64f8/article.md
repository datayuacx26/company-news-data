# Travel + Leisure Co. Named to Business Insider’s America’s High-Growth Companies 2026 List


Aug 4, 2026 12:00pm EDT


ORLANDO, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Travel + Leisure Co.](https://www.travelandleisureco.com/) (NYSE: TNL), a leading leisure travel company, today announced it has been recognized on the *Business Insider* America’s High-Growth Companies 2026 list, which recognizes top-performing U.S. public companies demonstrating sustained growth, financial strength, and long-term stability.

Travel + Leisure Co. (NYSE: TNL), a leading leisure travel company, today announced it has been recognized on the Business Insider America’s High-Growth Companies 2026 list, which recognizes top-performing U.S. public companies demonstrating sustained growth, financial strength, and long-term stability.


Developed by Business Insider in partnership with Plant-A Insights, the[inaugural America's High-Growth Companies list](https://www.businessinsider.com/americas-high-growth-companies-2026-6) recognizes U.S. public companies that have achieved strong, sustainable growth while demonstrating long-term financial stability. More than 3,000 eligible companies were evaluated for the 2026 ranking using a proprietary scoring methodology based on 11 financial metrics across four key pillars: Financial Performance, Profitability Growth, Investor Value, and Stability. Rankings were determined primarily using publicly available SEC filings, supplemented by additional compliance screening.


"We are proud to be recognized on Business Insider's inaugural America's High-Growth Companies list for 2026," said Michael D. Brown, president and chief executive officer of Travel + Leisure Co. "This recognition reflects the strength of our multi-brand strategy, the successful execution of our long-term growth priorities, and the commitment of our associates around the world. Through our diverse portfolio of trusted brands, industry expertise, and focus on innovation, we continue to expand our global reach, deliver exceptional vacation experiences, and create sustainable value for our owners, members, guests, and shareholders."


Travel + Leisure Co. is a leading leisure travel company offering vacation ownership, travel membership, and hospitality experiences that help millions of travelers discover the world with greater flexibility and confidence. The company’s portfolio includes Club Wyndham, WorldMark, Margaritaville Vacation Club, Sports Illustrated Resorts, Accor Vacation Club, Eddie Bauer Adventure Club, RCI, and other travel brands serving diverse traveler needs across markets worldwide.

Travel + Leisure Co. (NYSE: TNL) is a leading leisure travel company, providing more than six million vacations to travelers around the world every year.
