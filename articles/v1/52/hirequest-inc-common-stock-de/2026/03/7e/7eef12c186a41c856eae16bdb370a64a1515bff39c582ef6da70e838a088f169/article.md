# TradeCorp Expands Midwest Presence with New Office in St. Louis

*03/10/25*


**ST. LOUIS, MO** – TradeCorp, a leading provider of skilled trades workforce solutions and a trusted partner to companies building and maintaining essential infrastructure, today announced the opening of its newest office in St. Louis, Missouri. The expansion strengthens TradeCorp’s growing national footprint and reinforces its commitment to supporting the people and companies that build and maintain essential infrastructure.


TradeCorp delivers reliable skilled trade talent and workforce solutions that prioritize safety, compliance, and performance. Specializing in connecting top-tier construction professionals with companies that need skilled trades to get the job done right, TradeCorp partners with organizations that are building, renovating, and scaling their workforce across the construction and industrial sectors.


The St. Louis office will be led by Jason Taylor, who brings more than 20 years of experience in workforce solutions, with a strong focus on skilled trades within the Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) space. Jason partners with contractors, facilities teams, and industrial organizations to deliver high-impact talent solutions across HVAC, electrical, plumbing, controls, and field service disciplines.


Jason began his career with Aerotek/TEKsystems, where he spent nearly a decade building a diverse client portfolio across commercial, engineering, environmental, scientific, automotive, and IT sectors. He built his reputation on a disciplined screening process, a consultative approach, and a commitment to developing long-term partnerships with both clients and candidates.


Over the past decade, Jason expanded into internal talent acquisition leadership, designing and executing sustainable staffing strategies that supported operational growth and improved workforce retention. This experience gives him a unique advantage understanding not only how to source top talent externally, but how hiring decisions impact budgets, project timelines, safety, and long-term organizational performance.


“At TradeCorp, we understand the critical role skilled tradespeople play in bringing construction projects to life,” said Taylor. “From electricians to plumbers and sheet metal HVAC mechanics, our network is built on dependable professionals who take pride in their craft. I’m excited to expand TradeCorp’s presence in St. Louis and partner with companies that need reliable, experienced talent to execute safely and perform at a high level.”


TradeCorp’s solutions include contract, contract-to-hire, and direct hire placements tailored specifically to the skilled trades market. Through its National Accounts team, TradeCorp is able to support skilled trade workforce needs across the country delivering consistent service, compliance standards, and performance-driven results for multi-location and enterprise clients.


“TradeCorp’s growth reflects the ongoing demand for skilled trades nationwide,” said Dave Gerstner, Vice President of Operations at HireQuest. “Jason’s leadership and deep understanding of the MEP and construction markets position the St. Louis office for success. We’re excited about the continued expansion of TradeCorp and the impact it will have in supporting contractors and infrastructure projects across the country.” As labor shortages continue to impact construction and industrial markets nationwide, TradeCorp remains focused on being a trusted partner delivering vetted skilled tradespeople who show up, perform, and help projects stay on time and on budget.


### **About TradeCorp**


TradeCorp is a leading provider of skilled trades workforce solutions, connecting dependable, vetted tradespeople with companies that build and maintain essential infrastructure. From electricians and plumbers to sheet metal HVAC mechanics and controls technicians, TradeCorp delivers contract, contract-to-hire, and direct hire solutions that prioritize safety, compliance, and performance. Whether supporting local contractors or multi-state enterprises through its National Accounts team, TradeCorp is the trusted partner for sourcing reliable and experienced skilled trade talent across the United States.
