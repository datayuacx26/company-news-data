# Best AP Automation Solutions for Skilled Nursing Facilities [2026]

The best AP automation solutions for skilled nursing facilities in 2026 handle multi-entity invoice processing, facility-specific GL coding, configurable approval workflows, and ERP integration — without requiring per-facility staff additions. Leading options include AI-native platforms like Morphik that automate outcomes end-to-end, alongside established tools like Stampli, Ottimate, Paymerang, and CoreIntegrator that streamline specific AP steps.


For SNF operators, AP automation isn't optional. With[administrative overhead consuming roughly 14% of total costs](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12626023/) and[87% of nursing homes operating at a loss](https://goringo.com/blog/cutting-staffing-costs-while-maintaining-care) , the back office is one of the few cost categories where technology can deliver material savings without affecting care quality. Industry benchmarks show AP automation cuts processing time by 60% or more.


## What to Look For in AP Automation for SNFs


AP automation for skilled nursing has specific requirements that general-purpose solutions often miss. Before evaluating vendors, understand the features that matter for multi-site healthcare operations.


Criteria Why It Matters for SNFs


**Multi-entity support** Each facility is a separate legal entity with its own vendors, GL codes, and bank accounts


**Facility-specific GL coding** The same vendor may code to different GL accounts at different facilities


**Configurable approval workflows** Approval thresholds and chains vary by facility, expense type, and amount


**ERP integration** Must connect with your system of record — QuickBooks, Sage Intacct, AppFolio, etc.


**Payment execution** ACH and check cutting from the platform, not just invoice processing


**AI/learning capabilities** Ability to learn your coding patterns and improve accuracy over time


**Scalability** Adding a new facility shouldn't require proportional setup effort

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What is the best AP automation for skilled nursing facilities?


The best AP automation for skilled nursing facilities depends on your ERP, portfolio size, and scope of automation needed. For unified back-office automation (AP + payroll + billing), Morphik is purpose-built for multi-site operators. For dedicated AP with strong Sage Intacct integration, Stampli is proven at scale. For payment optimization, Paymerang offers rebate revenue.


### How much does AP automation cost for a multi-site operator?


AP automation pricing varies by vendor and model. Per-seat pricing ranges from $50-$200/user/month. Per-invoice pricing ranges from $1-5 per invoice. Usage-based AI platforms like Morphik scale sub-linearly with volume. For a 50-facility operator processing 500+ invoices per facility per month, total costs range from $3,000-$15,000/month depending on the solution and scope.


### Can AP automation handle facility-specific GL coding?


Yes — this is a core requirement for multi-site healthcare operators. AI-powered solutions like Morphik and Stampli learn facility-specific coding patterns over time, applying the correct GL codes based on vendor, expense type, and facility. Non-AI solutions require manual GL mapping per facility and don't adapt to pattern changes.


### How long does it take to implement AP automation?


Implementation timelines range from days to weeks depending on the solution. Stampli advertises one-day setup per entity. AI-native platforms like Morphik typically deploy initial workflows within 2-4 weeks. Enterprise platforms like AvidXchange may take 4-8 weeks for full deployment.


### Does AP automation integrate with QuickBooks?


Most AP automation platforms integrate with QuickBooks, including Morphik, AvidXchange, and others. Stampli's deepest integration is with Sage Intacct but also supports QuickBooks. CoreIntegrator and Ottimate support QuickBooks as well. Always verify the specific QuickBooks version (Online vs Desktop) compatibility with your chosen vendor.
