# Being a Fundamental Investor in Crypto w/ Toe from Theia

In this episode of Galaxy Grid, the hosts are joined by Toe from Theia to discuss the widening gap between institutional crypto adoption and market apathy. Toe breaks down why traditional token models are broken, how compressed DeFi multiples are creating historic entry points for fundamental buyers, and how MetaDAO’s launchpad model is fixing high-FDV predatory unlocks with smart-contract investor protections. Next, the crew dives into the escalating social trading war between Pump.fun and FOMO, analyzing the mechanics behind 0-minute average hold times and mobile-first chain abstraction. Finally, they debate the controversial L1 issuance proposals, including Ethereum's EIP-8363 and Solana's SGP-002 disinflation vote and unpack why attempting to over-engineer network inflation creates friction for core DeFi builders.
