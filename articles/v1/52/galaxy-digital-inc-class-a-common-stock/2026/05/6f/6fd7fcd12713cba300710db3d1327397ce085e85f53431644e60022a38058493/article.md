# Galaxy at Consensus Miami 2026 | Event Highlights

From May 5–7, Galaxy brought it all to the Consensus Miami Conference floor: institutional expertise, the[GalaxyOne](https://www.galaxy.app/) consumer app, live[podcast](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/podcasts/galaxy-brains) recordings, and a real-time market sentiment survey capturing the pulse of attendees spanning retail investors, builders, and institutional allocators.


On stage: **Michael Harvey,** Head of Franchise Trading, broke down how derivatives ecosystems are being built and where the opportunities lie. **Steve Kurz** , Global Co-Head of Digital Assets, delivered a fireside and took the Solana Accelerate main stage to unpack the SWEEP Fund. **Alex Thorn** , Head of Firmwide Research, closed out the week on the evolving rules around tokenization — the very topic that dominated our survey results.


Two-thirds of survey respondents said they're bullish heading into H2 2026. RWA tokenization led as the most exciting area in crypto, and nearly half said their primary objective is long-term investment — the HODL mentality is alive and well.
