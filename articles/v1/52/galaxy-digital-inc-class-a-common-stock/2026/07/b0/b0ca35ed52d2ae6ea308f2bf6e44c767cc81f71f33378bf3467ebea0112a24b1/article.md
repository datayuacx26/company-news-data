# Galaxy Is a Proud Sponsor of the National Polo Center for the 2026 U.S. Polo Season

Galaxy today announced its sponsorship of the National Polo Center as an official partner of the 2026 U.S. Polo Season in Wellington, Florida, which is home to some of the most competitive polo in the world and the premier venue for the sport in the United States.


The USPA Gold Cup Final and the U.S. Open Polo Championship Final are the marquee events of the season, drawing the world's top players and a passionate global community to Wellington each spring. Galaxy will be on the ground for both, hosting clients and guests at the Gold Cup Final on Sunday, March 29, and the U.S. Open Polo Championship Final on Sunday, April 26, with activations before and after each match from 2:00pm to 6:30pm. The Galaxy and GalaxyOne teams will be near the Asado area — stop by to connect and take part in a polo challenge on the field.
