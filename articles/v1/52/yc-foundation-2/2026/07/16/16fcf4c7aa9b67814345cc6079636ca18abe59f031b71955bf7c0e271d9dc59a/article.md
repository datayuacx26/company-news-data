# What's New - May 2026

### Smarter automation. Cleaner handoffs. More control for every team.


May’s updates are focused on helping your team move faster with smarter automation, less manual work, and AI-assisted workflows that bring more speed and clarity to critical moments between builder teams and customers.


### WarrantyOS


[WarrantyOS](https://youtu.be/APdto-_loX0?si=QNxZ4USm9WRUzfsB) continues to help customer care teams manage warranty operations at scale. This month’s updates give teams better vendor communication, clearer service request context, and fewer manual steps in day-to-day warranty work.


##### What's new in WarrantyOS:


-


**Customer Signatures** — Builder warranty operations and customer care teams can assign and capture customer signatures directly in the field for completed approvals and sign-offs.


-


**AI Proposal Feedback** — Operators can now provide a reason when rejecting AI-generated Work Order proposals, helping improve future recommendations.


-


**Vendor Communication Improvements** — Manage multiple contacts per vendor, handle vendor-property associations in bulk, and ensure the right vendors are notified when assignments or cancellations change.


-


**Service Request Visibility** — Filter Service Requests by age, see how long it has been since a home closed, and flag likely out-of-warranty requests so teams can prioritize follow-up faster.


### Operator


We also made improvements that[help teams](https://buildwithfoundation.com/products/operator) keep customers and workflows moving efficiently across each stage of the journey. These updates reduce duplicate work and help your team stay focused on the next sale and closing.


##### What's new in Operator:


-


**Community Team Member Backfill** — When adding a team member to a community, operators can now optionally backfill that person to existing transactions by status.


-


**Community Document Backfill** — Community-level documents can now be added to existing transactions by status, helping teams keep buyer documents current without manual updates.


-


**Pre-Close Action Item Due Dates** — Pre-close action items can now calculate due dates from the transaction contract date, giving teams more flexibility in how workflows are configured.
