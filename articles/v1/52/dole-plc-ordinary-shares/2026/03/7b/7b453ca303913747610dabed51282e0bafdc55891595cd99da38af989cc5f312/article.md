## Dole plc 2025 Annual Report Available to Shareholders


Mar 02 2026


Download (opens in new window)


DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Dole plc (NYSE: DOLE) (the “Company”) announced today that its annual report on Form 10-K, including its audited financial statements for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 (the “Annual Report”), filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on March 2, 2026, can be accessed in the “Financials” section of the Company’s website at[www.doleplc.com/investor-relations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.doleplc.com%2Finvestors&esheet=54436426&newsitemid=20260302304065&lan=en-US&anchor=www.doleplc.com%2Finvestor-relations&index=1&md5=d53f90015e6e48ac840083bd6f8298bb) , as well as on the SEC’s website at[www.sec.gov](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.sec.gov&esheet=54436426&newsitemid=20260302304065&lan=en-US&anchor=www.sec.gov&index=2&md5=33e79e7345c99ab27d4b8b8ccc6109af) .


Shareholders may request a hard copy of the Company’s Annual Report, free of charge, by contacting the Company at Dole plc, 29 North Anne Street, Dublin 7, D07 PH36, Ireland, Attention: Investor Relations. If shareholders prefer, they can also request a hard copy by emailinginvestors@doleplc.com .


This is the Company’s first annual report filed on Form 10-K, following its voluntary election to file on U.S. domestic issuer forms. The Company believes this action will provide enhanced consistency and comparability with other U.S. public companies, while improving the eligibility for inclusion in certain U.S. equity indices, among other potential benefits.


**About Dole plc:**


A global leader in fresh produce, Dole plc grows, markets, and distributes an extensive variety of fresh fruits and vegetables sourced locally and from around the world. Dedicated and passionate in exceeding our customers’ requirements in over 85 countries, our goal is to make the world a healthier and a more sustainable place.
