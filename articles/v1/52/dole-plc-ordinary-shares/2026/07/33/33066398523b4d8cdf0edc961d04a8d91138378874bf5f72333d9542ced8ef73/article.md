## Dole plc Schedules Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results Release


Jul 20 2026


Download (opens in new window)


DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Dole plc (NYSE: DOLE) (the “Company”) will announce its financial results for the second quarter of 2026 on Monday, August 10, 2026, prior to the market opening. The Company’s management will host a webcast on the same day at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time.


A presentation to accompany the discussion will be uploaded to the Company website along with a press release and other supplemental financial information.


The live webcast and a replay after the event can be accessed at the[Investor Relations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.doleplc.com%2Finvestor-relations&esheet=54571844&newsitemid=20260720976564&lan=en-US&anchor=Investor+Relations&index=1&md5=d38696ec4ddd52218ad61da716b1dd0d) section of our website or directly at[https://events.q4inc.com/attendee/911565068](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F911565068&esheet=54571844&newsitemid=20260720976564&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F911565068&index=2&md5=b03aa88b3854962d01dfffed0be052f1) .


**About Dole plc:**


A global leader in fresh produce,[Dole plc](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.doleplc.com&esheet=54571844&newsitemid=20260720976564&lan=en-US&anchor=Dole+plc&index=3&md5=2c758d15dfb512d927e09205ac68a254) grows, markets, and distributes an extensive variety of fresh fruits and vegetables sourced locally and from around the world. Dedicated and passionate in exceeding our customers’ requirements in over 85 countries, our goal is to make the world a healthier and more sustainable place.
