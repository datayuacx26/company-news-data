# Best Family Office Accounting Software for 2026

If you're running a family office, you already know: QuickBooks wasn't built for you. Neither was NetSuite, Xero, or most "enterprise" accounting platforms. The moment you're managing 10+ entities across trusts, LLCs, LPs, and foundations all with intercompany transactions, multi-currency positions, and alternative assets, generic accounting software starts breaking.


2026 has brought a wave of purpose-built platforms designed specifically for this problem. We evaluated the leading family office accounting solutions based on what actually matters to controllers and CFOs: multi-entity consolidation, investment integration, automation, implementation speed, and total cost of ownership.


## Here are the 9 best family office accounting platforms for 2026.


### What to Look for in Family Office Accounting and Investments Software


Before diving into individual platforms, here's what separates family-office-grade software from generic accounting tools:


- **Multi-entity consolidation** — One-click roll-ups across dozens of entities with automatic intercompany eliminations, not month-long spreadsheet exercises
- **Investment integration** — Public equities, private equity, real estate, hedge funds, and direct investments tracked alongside the GL, not in a separate system
- **Multi-currency support** — Automatic FX gain/loss calculations, not manual journal entries every month
- **Flexible chart of accounts** — Family offices don't follow rigid GAAP structures; your software shouldn't force you into one
- **Approval workflows** — Configurable JE approval chains, not a free-for-all
- **Audit trail** — Immutable records for posted entries, not editable history
- **Implementation timeline** — Weeks, not quarters

Sage Intacct is the most established name in family office accounting. - **Multi-entity consolidation** — Automates consolidation including currency conversion, inter-entity transactions, and local tax reporting. New entities can be set up in minutes.

- **Direct data feeds** — Automated feeds from custodians, prime brokers, banks, and pricing sources. Minimizes manual data entry.
- **Cash management** — Handles complex capital movements between equity and bank accounts with purpose-built tools.
- **Bill pay** — Integrated bill tracking, budgeting, check writing, invoice storage, and approval workflows.
- **Budgeting and cash flow** — Entity-level, account-level, and sub-account-level budgeting with cash flow forecasting.


### Considerations


- **SEI ownership** — Archway is now part of SEI, a large financial services company. This provides stability and resources, but some offices prefer independent, focused vendors.
- **Legacy architecture** — While functional and reliable, the platform's UX feels more traditional than newer entrants. If modern, intuitive interfaces matter to your team, evaluate the user experience carefully.
- **Pricing transparency** — Pricing is not publicly available and requires direct engagement with the sales team. Family offices have reported that pricing can be opaque, particularly around data feeds and add-on modules.
- **Implementation** — More involved than newer, cloud-native platforms. Expect a structured onboarding process.


### Pricing


Contact Archway directly. Not publicly disclosed.


### Best For


Offices where investment activity drives accounting complexity — particularly those with heavy alternative investment allocations, frequent capital calls/distributions, and multiple custodian relationships. Archway's automatic JE generation from investment activity is a genuine differentiator.

### Overview


Addepar is the dominant platform for investment reporting and portfolio analytics in the family office space. If your primary pain point is getting a unified, accurate view of a complex multi-asset portfolio — including illiquids, alternatives, and direct investments — Addepar is the category leader. ### Key Strengths

- **Alternative investments** — AI-driven workflows automate collection, processing, and storage of alternative investment documents (capital calls, distribution notices, K-1s). Converts unstructured docs into verified data.
- **Navigator** — Projection and scenario modeling tool for long-term investment strategy. Model asset allocations including illiquid assets against client goals with various capital market assumptions.
- **Addison AI** — AI assistant embedded in workflows for faster question-to-decision cycles.
- **Open API** — Integrates with CRMs, financial planning tools, and other fintech platforms. Frequently used alongside a GL like Asseta or Sage Intacct.

### Considerations


- **Not an accounting platform** — Addepar is investment reporting and portfolio management, not a general ledger. You'll need a separate GL for your books, AP, bill pay, and financial statements. Most Addepar clients pair it with another platform on this list.
- **Cost** — Premium pricing reflects its position as the market leader in portfolio analytics. Not the right tool if your primary need is accounting, not investment reporting.
- **Complexity** — The platform's power comes with a learning curve. Smaller offices with straightforward portfolios may find it more than they need.


### Pricing


Contact Addepar. Enterprise pricing, typically based on AUM and number of accounts.


### Best For


Family offices with complex, multi-asset portfolios (especially heavy in alternatives and private investments) that need institutional-grade analytics and reporting. Best paired with a separate GL platform like Asseta for accounting.
