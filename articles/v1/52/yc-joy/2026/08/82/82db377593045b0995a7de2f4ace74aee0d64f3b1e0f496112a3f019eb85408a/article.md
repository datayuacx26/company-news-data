## Essential Pages Every Destination Wedding Website Needs


The template sets the mood. The content keeps guests informed. Destination celebrations involve more moving parts than a hometown wedding, so let your website handle the logistics so you do not have to answer the same questions fifty times over text. Here is what to include. For a full walkthrough, check out our[destination wedding planning checklist](https://withjoy.com/blog/destination-wedding-checklist/) .


- **Travel logistics page:** List the nearest airport (with the airport code), ground transportation options, and driving directions from nearby cities. If guests are flying internationally, note passport or visa requirements.
- **Accommodations page:** Share your room block details with booking links and deadlines. Include a range of hotel recommendations at different price points, and note any Airbnb options near the venue.
- **Schedule of events:** Shoreline weddings often span a full weekend. Lay out the welcome dinner, ceremony, reception, and farewell brunch with times, locations, and what to wear for each.
- **Dress code guidance:** Beach formal and beach casual mean very different things. Be specific. Remind guests that heels sink in sand and suggest wedges or flats. Mention lightweight fabrics and sun protection.
- **Weather and practical tips:** Include average temperatures for your wedding month, note any rainy season risks, and suggest sunscreen and bug spray. Guests will thank you.
- **RSVP with meal choices:** Especially useful for venues with limited capacity. Collect dietary restrictions and plus one confirmations in one place.
- **FAQ page:** Address the questions you know are coming: Do I need a passport? Are kids welcome? What about gifts? A thorough FAQ cuts down on the group chat noise in the months before the wedding.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What should a beach wedding website include?


A beach wedding website should include your ceremony and reception details with time zone information, travel logistics (airport, transportation, accommodations), a dress code guide appropriate for a beach setting, an RSVP page, a registry link, and a schedule of wedding weekend events. Many couples also add weather tips and a FAQ page addressing common destination wedding questions.


### Can I get matching beach wedding invitations and a wedding website?


Yes. Some platforms offer coordinated stationery suites where your beach wedding invitations, save the date cards, and wedding website all share a consistent coastal or tropical design. This creates a cohesive look across every guest touchpoint from the first announcement through the event itself.


### Are beach wedding website templates free?


Many platforms offer free beach wedding website templates with core features like RSVP management and event details. Some charge for premium features such as custom domains or advanced design customization. Free options from all in one wedding platforms typically include more functionality than free templates from general design tools.


### How far in advance should I set up my destination wedding website?


Set up your destination wedding website 8 to 12 months before the wedding. Guests need extra lead time to arrange travel, book flights, and request time off work. Send save the dates 6 to 8 months out with a link to your website so guests can start planning.


### What is the best color palette for a beach wedding website?


Popular beach wedding color palettes include ocean blues and sandy neutrals for a coastal look, lush greens with cream for a tropical botanical feel, soft watercolor blues and greens for an artistic style, and coral or pink with teal for a vibrant tropical celebration. Choose colors that complement your venue and personal style.
