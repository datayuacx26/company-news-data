# Wedding Vows Examples: 50+ Heartfelt, Funny, and Traditional Vows to Inspire You

Last Updated on August 3, 2026 by[Joy Editors](https://withjoy.com/)


Writing your own wedding vows is one of the most meaningful things you can do on your wedding day — and one of the most intimidating. Whether you want something traditional, deeply personal, or a little funny, the right words exist. This guide gives you 50+ real wedding vow examples across every style, plus a step-by-step framework for writing your own.


**Quick Answer:** The best wedding vows are specific to your relationship, promise something real, and take 1-2 minutes to read aloud. Start with a personal memory, make 3-5 concrete promises, and close with a declaration of commitment. Aim for 150-250 words.


## Traditional Wedding Vows Examples


Traditional vows have lasted centuries because they say everything that needs to be said. These work for religious and non-religious ceremonies alike.


### Classic Protestant Vows


> “I, \[Name\], take you, \[Name\], to be my lawfully wedded \[husband/wife/partner\], to have and to hold from this day forward, for better, for worse, for richer, for poorer, in sickness and in health, to love and to cherish, until death do us part.”


### Catholic Wedding Vows


> “I, \[Name\], take you, \[Name\], for my lawful \[husband/wife\], to have and to hold, from this day forward, for better, for worse, for richer, for poorer, in sickness and in health, until death do us part.”


### Simple Non-Religious Traditional Vows


> “I take you as my partner for life. I promise to love you unconditionally, to support your dreams, to stand by you in every season, and to choose you — every single day.”


### Short and Sweet Traditional Vows


> “I promise to love you, to respect you, to be honest with you, and to grow with you. You are my person, and I am yours.”


## Personal Wedding Vows Examples (Write-Your-Own Style)


Personal vows are the most memorable — they tell your specific story. These examples show how to blend a personal memory with real promises.


### Romantic Personal Vows (Him to Her)


> “I knew I wanted to marry you the night you laughed so hard at your own joke that you cried. In that moment, I thought: I want to make this person laugh for the rest of my life.
>
>
> I promise to be your safe place when the world is loud. I promise to tell you the truth even when it’s hard. I promise to show up — not just on the good days, but especially on the hard ones. And I promise that no matter what life brings, you will never face it alone.”


### Romantic Personal Vows (Her to Him)


> “You are the person I call first when something good happens, and the person I need most when something goes wrong. That is not a small thing.
>
>
> I promise to love you not just when it is easy, but when it is work. I promise to listen more than I speak. I promise to grow alongside you, not ahead of you. And I promise that choosing you today is the easiest decision I have ever made.”


### Personal Vows for Same-Sex Couples


> “I did not know what home felt like until I met you. Now I carry it with me everywhere.
>
>
> I promise to celebrate your victories as loudly as my own. I promise to be patient when patience is hard. I promise to keep choosing this partnership — not because it is easy, but because it is ours. You are my favorite person, and today I get to tell the whole world.”


### Personal Vows That Reference Hard Times


> “We have already been through things that would have broken other couples. We did not break. We bent, and we held on, and we came out on the other side still choosing each other.
>
>
> I promise to keep choosing you. Through the seasons that are easy and the ones that are not. I promise to be the person you can fall apart with, knowing we will put ourselves back together. I love you — all of you, including the parts that are still figuring it out.”


## Funny Wedding Vows Examples


Humor in vows works when it is specific and warm — not a stand-up routine. These examples get a laugh without losing the sincerity.


### Funny Vows That Still Land Emotionally


> “I promise to love you even when you load the dishwasher wrong. I promise to pretend I do not notice when you eat the last of the leftovers I was saving. I promise to let you have the good pillow — most of the time.
>
>
> But more than any of that: I promise to be your person. The one who shows up. The one who stays. The one who thinks you are the funniest, smartest, most infuriating person I have ever met — and would not change a single thing.”


### Funny Vows for the Groom


> “I promise to always ask for directions before we are hopelessly lost. I promise to actually watch the shows you recommend instead of just nodding. I promise to kill every spider without being asked twice.
>
>
> And I promise, with everything I have, to love you every single day of my life. You make me better. You make me braver. And you make me laugh harder than anyone I have ever known. I am the luckiest.”


### Short Funny Vows


> “I promise to love you, honor you, and never tell you ‘I told you so’ — even when I definitely told you so.”


## Short Wedding Vows Examples


Sometimes less is more. Short vows can be just as powerful — and easier to memorize.


- “I choose you. Today and every day after.”
- “You are my home. I promise to keep coming back to you.”
- “I love you. I like you. I choose you. Always.”
- “I promise to love you fiercely, to hold you gently, and to never stop showing up.”
- “You are the best thing that has ever happened to me. I promise to spend the rest of my life proving I know that.”
- “I take you as you are, and I promise to love who you become.”


## Religious Wedding Vows Examples


### Christian Wedding Vows


> “Before God and these witnesses, I take you as my \[husband/wife/partner\]. I promise to love you as Christ loved the church — sacrificially and completely. I promise to pray for you, to grow in faith alongside you, and to build a home rooted in grace and gratitude.”


### Jewish Wedding Vows (Non-Traditional Supplement)


> “With this ring, I am consecrated to you according to the laws of Moses and Israel. I promise to cherish you, to honor you, and to walk with you through every chapter of our lives.”


### Hindu-Inspired Vows (English)


> “I take these seven steps with you and promise: to nourish you, to grow with you, to be your strength, to share in your joy and your sorrow, to bring children into our home with love, to be with you through every season, and to be your truest friend.”


## How to Write Your Own Wedding Vows


The best vows are the ones that sound like you. Here is a simple framework that works for almost every couple.


### The 4-Part Structure


1. **Open with a specific memory or observation.** Not “I love you because you’re kind” — but “I knew I loved you when you stayed up until 2am helping me finish that presentation I’d been avoiding.”
2. **Make 3-5 concrete promises.** Avoid vague promises like “I’ll always be there.” Instead: “I promise to be the person you call first — even when the news is bad.”
3. **Acknowledge the hard stuff.** Vows that only mention the good days feel incomplete. A line about facing difficulty together makes the whole thing more believable.
4. **Close with a declaration.** End with a clear statement of commitment. “I choose you today, and I will keep choosing you.”


### Practical Tips


- **Target length:** 150-250 words reads in about 1-2 minutes. Any longer and guests lose focus.
- **Write separately, share the length.** Agree on a word count before you write — nothing is more awkward than a 3-minute vow followed by a 30-second one.
- **Read them aloud.** What looks good on paper can be hard to say. Practice until the words feel natural.
- **Bring a printed copy.** Memorizing is great; having a backup is better.
- **Avoid inside jokes only 2 people understand.** Your guests are witnessing this — make it something they can feel too.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Wedding Vows


### What is a good wedding vow example?


A good wedding vow is specific to your relationship, makes real promises (not just “I’ll love you forever”), and takes 1-2 minutes to read aloud. The best vows include one personal memory, 3-4 concrete commitments, and a clear closing declaration.


### What are the basic wedding vows?


The most common traditional vows are: “I take you to be my lawfully wedded \[partner\], to have and to hold, for better or worse, for richer or poorer, in sickness and in health, to love and to cherish, until death do us part.” These work for both religious and civil ceremonies.


### What is a short but sweet wedding vow?


Short vows work best when they are specific and direct. Examples: “I choose you today and every day after.” or “You are my home. I promise to keep coming back to you.” Aim for 3-5 sentences that feel true to how you actually talk.


### What are the 7 promises in marriage?


The seven promises (Saptapadi) come from Hindu wedding ceremonies, where the couple takes seven steps together, each representing a vow: nourishment, strength, prosperity, happiness, family, togetherness, and lifelong friendship. Many couples adapt these into modern personal vows regardless of religious background.
