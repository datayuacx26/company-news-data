## Sallie Mae Expands Access to Responsible Financing For Students Pursuing Graduate and Law Degrees


05/07/2026


Download this Press Release PDF Format (opens in new window)


*Company Offers Competitive Interest Rates, No Origination Fees, and Multiple Repayment Options Ahead of Changes to Federal Student Loan Program*


*Expanded Access to Graduate and Law Loans Follow Recent Enhancements to* *Medical School and Dental School Financing Options*


NEWARK, Del.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Sallie Mae (Nasdaq: SLM) today announced expanded access to responsible financing for students pursuing graduate and law degrees. The[Sallie Mae ® Graduate Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fgraduate-student-loans%2Fgraduate-school-loan%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Sallie+Mae+%26%23174%3B+Graduate+Loan&index=1&md5=2f64ad9e29d95f3b1a803700f37adf55) and[Sallie Mae ® Law School Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fgraduate-student-loans%2Flaw-school-loan%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Sallie+Mae+%26%23174%3B+Law+School+Loan&index=2&md5=ce52166b8d1e9d6201b721d67438a7aa) offer competitive interest rates, no origination fees, and multiple repayment options during school, and can cover up to 100% of the school-certified cost of attendance.


Graduate and professional programs vary in cost, length, and structure, and many students rely on borrowing to fill gaps after[scholarships](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sallie.com%2Fscholarships%3Futm_campaign%3Dcc_eng_b2c_u_u_u_u_u%26utm_source%3Dpressrel%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_content%3DGrad%2520%26%2520Law&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=scholarships&index=3&md5=4aa780b4744377edad51d331ab5d9c42) ,[grants](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sallie.com%2Ffinancial-aid%2Fcollege-grants%3Futm_campaign%3Dcc_eng_faf_u_u_u_u_u%26utm_source%3Dpressrel%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_content%3DGrad%2520%26%2520Law&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=grants&index=4&md5=aee9a2364a804cec834680fa4141207d) , and[federal financial aid](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sallie.com%2Ffinancial-aid%3Futm_campaign%3Dcc_eng_faf_u_u_u_u_u%26utm_source%3Dpressrel%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_content%3DGrad%2520%26%2520Law&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=federal+financial+aid&index=5&md5=077e97e342ade1d40c1c03519a0f7c02) . Sallie Mae has made enhancements so that more qualified students may access funding and designed its graduate and law school loans to help students cover school costs, including tuition, fees, and living expenses. These enhancements build on Sallie Mae’s market-leading graduate-degree loan options, including recent updates to its[Medical School Loan and Dental School Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fnews.salliemae.com%2Fnews-releases%2Fnews-releases-details%2F2026%2FSallie-Mae-Expands-Graduate-Loan-Options-For-Medical-and-Dental-Students%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Medical+School+Loan+and+Dental+School+Loan&index=6&md5=4c091dfe437008a93a038b817b7b5e46) .


**Graduate and Law School Loans Designed for Advanced Degree Programs and Timelines** Sallie Mae’s[Graduate Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fgraduate-student-loans%2Fgraduate-school-loan%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Graduate+Loan&index=7&md5=bde0b98cb49eaa6c6308b31f8d887c16) and[Law School Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fgraduate-student-loans%2Flaw-school-loan%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Law+School+Loan&index=8&md5=dfc61e9359fb4028aa218fb6ba154aa7) share core features, including:


- **Ability to[prequalify](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fprequalify.salliemae.com%2Fs%2Fgetting-started&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=prequalify&index=9&md5=ed1232bb536896c5dfce8f9dc60fa21d) with no impact on credit scores,** allowing students to check eligibility and receive an estimated rate before applying.
- **Repayment flexibility, including up to 12 months** of interest-only payments, allowing students to ease into repayment as they transition into their careers.
- [Cosigner release](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fmanage-your-private-student-loan%2Fapply-to-release-cosigner%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Cosigner+release&index=10&md5=eaf629b93a180c628c1e357c12595f39) **option,** allowing qualified borrowers to apply for cosigner release after meeting graduation, on-time payment, and other eligibility requirements.
- **Dedicated graduate and professional student support teams** , with experience supporting advanced degree timelines and requirements.
- **100% U.S.-based loan servicing** , providing support from application through repayment.


**Graduate Loan Options for a Range of Programs** The[Sallie Mae Graduate Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fgraduate-student-loans%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Sallie+Mae+Graduate+Loan&index=11&md5=f06950002699e566ccac529d1596f1ac) supports students pursuing master’s, doctoral, and professional degrees, as well as select graduate certificate programs at participating schools. The loan offers a six‑month grace period after graduation and up to 48 months of deferment for qualifying internships, residencies, or fellowships, giving students time and flexibility before full payments begin.


**Law School Loan Features Designed for Legal Education and Bar Preparation** The[Sallie Mae Law School Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fgraduate-student-loans%2Flaw-school-loan%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Sallie+Mae+Law+School+Loan&index=12&md5=f70a13d5aab96bd004a1df9bba493264) is built specifically for the law school journey, recognizing the unique costs and career timelines law students face, from coursework and clerkships to bar exam preparation. Repayment terms include a nine‑month grace period after graduation and up to 48 months of deferment for qualifying clerkships or internships. Qualifying law students may also apply for the[Sallie Mae® Bar Study Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fgraduate-student-loans%2Fbar-study-loan%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54531427&newsitemid=20260507216481&lan=en-US&anchor=Sallie+Mae%26%23174%3B+Bar+Study+Loan&index=13&md5=93cc3f29ca2c224195ee79c8624f2316) to help cover bar exam preparation costs.


“Students pursuing advanced degrees need a financing partner they can rely on, especially as they navigate changes to federal student loan programs,” said Patrick Freeman, Senior Vice President, Sallie Mae. “We’ve long been a trusted name in responsible student financing, and we’re well prepared to support more students with options to help them confidently access and complete their graduate education programs.”
