## Sallie Mae Launches New Parent Loan to Help Families Cover College Costs


06/30/2026


Download this Press Release PDF Format (opens in new window)


*New Loan Option Designed for Parents, Family Members, and Other Creditworthy Individuals Provides Flexibility as Federal Loan Programs Evolve*


NEWARK, Del.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Sallie Mae today announced the launch of a new Parent Loan designed to give families an additional, flexible option to pay for higher education as[federal student loan options evolve](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Ffederal-plus-loan-guide%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54562367&newsitemid=20260630673167&lan=en-US&anchor=federal+student+loan+options+evolve&index=1&md5=45c5b31b7a833f26634d4e3f1dc025e3) . The[Sallie Mae® Parent Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fcosigners-and-parents%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54562367&newsitemid=20260630673167&lan=en-US&anchor=Sallie+Mae%26%23174%3B+Parent+Loan&index=2&md5=369895c3cdd343d0ed40cbdd20025bd0) allows credit-qualified individuals, including parents, guardians, or significant others, to support the financing of undergraduate and graduate education for students and offers competitive interest rates that can be lower than federal Parent PLUS loans, no origination fees, a choice of repayment options, and can cover up to 100% of the school-certified cost of attendance.


The new[Sallie Mae Parent Loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fcosigners-and-parents%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54562367&newsitemid=20260630673167&lan=en-US&anchor=Sallie+Mae+Parent+Loan&index=3&md5=728760423ae50d918f34cc85cfc44ddf) adds to the company’s broader suite of responsible financing products for students and families pursuing higher education. “Families have different priorities when deciding how to pay for college; while many parents choose to cosign a[student loan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Fstudent-loans%2Fundergraduate-student-loans%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54562367&newsitemid=20260630673167&lan=en-US&anchor=student+loan&index=11&md5=1691322cdff3c4f4966c50f8854eda37) , others prefer to borrow in their own name to support their student’s higher education goals,” said Patrick Freeman, Senior Vice President, Sallie Mae. “Our new Parent Loan gives them that flexibility and another responsible option backed by the experience and expertise of a trusted partner.”


**Sallie Mae Parent Loan features include:**


- **Ability to[prequalify with no impact on credit scores](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fprequalify.salliemae.com%2Fs%2Fparent-loan-landing%2F%3Fdtd_cell%3Dsmccmpsccmocototothothrn030000%26utm_campaign%3Dcc_u_u_u_u_b_pros_b%26utm_source%3Dbw%26utm_medium%3Dpr%26utm_content%3Dpressrelease.html&esheet=54562367&newsitemid=20260630673167&lan=en-US&anchor=prequalify+with+no+impact+on+credit+scores&index=12&md5=c6e211fb56b09d017ebced2c9718d994) ,** allowing families to check eligibility and get an estimated rate before applying.
- **No origination fees** compared to the federal Parent PLUS program, which includes an origination fee and charges interest on that fee.
- **Competitive fixed and variable rate options** to help families choose what best fits their budget.
- **Coverage for up to 100% of the school-certified cost of attendance,** including tuition, fees, housing, and other essentials.
- **Multiple repayment options,** including interest-only or full principal-and-interest payments.
- **A 0.25 percentage point interest rate reduction** when making automatic payments.
- **100% U.S.-based support teams,** helping families navigate decisions with confidence from application through repayment.
