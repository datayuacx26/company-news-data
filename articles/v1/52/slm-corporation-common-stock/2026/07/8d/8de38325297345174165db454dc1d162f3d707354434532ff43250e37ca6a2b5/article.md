## Sallie Mae Co-President and CFO to Speak at 2026 Morgan Stanley US Financials Conference


05/29/2026


Download this Press Release PDF Format (opens in new window)


NEWARK, Del.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Sallie Mae® (Nasdaq: SLM), formally SLM Corporation, today announced Co-President and Chief Financial Officer Pete Graham will speak at the 2026 Morgan Stanley US Financials Conference on Wednesday, June 10, at 7:30 a.m. ET.


A live audio webcast and replay will be available at[SallieMae.com/investors](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com%2Finvestors%2Fwebcasts-and-presentations%2F&esheet=54544271&newsitemid=20260529047762&lan=en-US&anchor=SallieMae.com%2Finvestors&index=1&md5=35dbf98d0e0d61108fec170bc822809c) .


**Sallie Mae** (Nasdaq: SLM) believes education and life-long learning, in all forms, help people achieve great things. As the leader in private student lending, we provide financing and know-how to support access to college and offer products and resources to help customers make new goals and experiences, beyond college, happen. Learn more at[SallieMae.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com&esheet=54544271&newsitemid=20260529047762&lan=en-US&anchor=SallieMae.com&index=2&md5=ac06e42699faf20121bff48e0aab40f1) . Commonly known as Sallie Mae, SLM Corporation and its subsidiaries are not sponsored by or agencies of the United States of America.
