NEWARK, Del.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Sallie Mae (Nasdaq: SLM), formally SLM Corporation, today released second quarter 2026 financial results. Complete financial results and related materials are available at[www.SallieMae.com/investors](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.SallieMae.com%2Finvestors&esheet=54576046&newsitemid=20260723616900&lan=en-US&anchor=www.SallieMae.com%2Finvestors&index=1&md5=ad72bed682ac0c13f8733fde22c54620) . The materials will also be available on the Securities and Exchange Commission’s website at[www.sec.gov](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.sec.gov&esheet=54576046&newsitemid=20260723616900&lan=en-US&anchor=www.sec.gov&index=2&md5=07b5243273397737143463e553e71af9) .


Sallie Mae will host an earnings conference call today, July 23, 2026, at 5:30 p.m. ET. Executives will be on hand to discuss various highlights of the quarter and to answer questions related to Sallie Mae’s performance. A live audio webcast of the conference call and presentation slides may be accessed at[www.SallieMae.com/investors](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.SallieMae.com%2Finvestors&esheet=54576046&newsitemid=20260723616900&lan=en-US&anchor=www.SallieMae.com%2Finvestors&index=3&md5=a8523bb543925fe7e5c2118d0915c818) and the[hosting website](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevent.on24.com%2Fwcc%2Fr%2F5418257%2F943090BC5AB19CA02BE775B7D49D221F&esheet=54576046&newsitemid=20260723616900&lan=en-US&anchor=hosting+website&index=4&md5=c7681ace0449a00ee7e8335d3c2f448b) .


A replay of the webcast will be available via the company’s investor website approximately two hours after the call’s conclusion.


**Sallie Mae** (Nasdaq: SLM) believes education and life-long learning, in all forms, help people achieve great things. As the leader in private student lending, we provide financing and know-how to support access to college and offer products and resources to help customers make new goals and experiences, beyond college, happen. Learn more at[SallieMae.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.salliemae.com&esheet=54576046&newsitemid=20260723616900&lan=en-US&anchor=SallieMae.com&index=5&md5=d1dcc0e713e565588623aad9a5b74d88) .
