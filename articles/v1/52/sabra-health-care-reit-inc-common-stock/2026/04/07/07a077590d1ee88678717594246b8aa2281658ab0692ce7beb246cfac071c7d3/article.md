# Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc. to Attend Wells Fargo’s 29th Annual Real Estate Securities Conference

### Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc. to Attend Wells Fargo’s 29th Annual Real Estate Securities Conference


April 20, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s206.q4cdn.com/352443609/files/doc_news/Sabra-Health-Care-REIT-Inc--to-Attend-Wells-Fargos-29th-Annual-Real-Estate-Securities-Conference-2026.pdf)


TUSTIN, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc. (Nasdaq: SBRA) announced today that Rick Matros, the company’s Chair and Chief Executive Officer, Michael Costa, the company’s Chief Financial Officer, Darrin Smith, the company’s Chief Investment Officer, and Lukas Hartwich, the company’s Executive Vice President of Finance, will attend Wells Fargo’s 29th Annual Real Estate Securities Conference on May 4 – May 6, 2026, at The Charleston Place in Charleston, South Carolina.


**About Sabra**


[Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fsabrahealth.com%2F&esheet=54517679&newsitemid=20260420449657&lan=en-US&anchor=Sabra+Health+Care+REIT%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=1572616bcb17de74dc53762890f66d4f) , a Maryland corporation, operates as a self-administered, self-managed real estate investment trust (a “REIT”) that, through its subsidiaries, owns and invests in real estate serving the healthcare industry throughout the United States and Canada.
