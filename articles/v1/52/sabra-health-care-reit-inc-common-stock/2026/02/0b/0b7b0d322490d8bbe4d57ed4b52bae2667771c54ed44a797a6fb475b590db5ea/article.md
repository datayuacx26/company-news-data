### Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc. to Attend Citi’s 2026 Global Property CEO Conference


February 13, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s206.q4cdn.com/352443609/files/doc_news/Sabra-Health-Care-REIT-Inc--to-Attend-Citis-2026-Global-Property-CEO-Conference-2026.pdf)


TUSTIN, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fsabrahealth.com%2F&esheet=54424235&newsitemid=20260213248136&lan=en-US&anchor=Sabra+Health+Care+REIT%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=3ac96cc40be8587b934c81a64c331cbc) (Nasdaq: SBRA) announced today that Rick Matros, the company’s Chair and Chief Executive Officer; Michael Costa, the company’s Chief Financial Officer; Darrin Smith, the company’s Chief Investment Officer; and Lukas Hartwich, the company’s Executive Vice President of Finance, will attend Citi’s 2026 Global Property CEO Conference on March 1-4, 2026, at The Diplomat Beach Resort in Hollywood, Florida.


**About Sabra**


[Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fsabrahealth.com%2F&esheet=54424235&newsitemid=20260213248136&lan=en-US&anchor=Sabra+Health+Care+REIT%2C+Inc.&index=2&md5=43fe388ef6bd65d5dc096a30727def8c) , a Maryland corporation, operates as a self-administered, self-managed real estate investment trust (a “REIT”) that, through its subsidiaries, owns and invests in real estate serving the healthcare industry throughout the United States and Canada.
