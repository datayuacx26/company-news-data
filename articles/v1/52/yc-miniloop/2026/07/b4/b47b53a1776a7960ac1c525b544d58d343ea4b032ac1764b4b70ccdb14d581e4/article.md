Lastly, the Team plan at $439/month is geared towards larger teams with substantial automation needs, offering an expansive unit allocation suitable for extensive or simultaneous campaigns.


Cost management within each tier is influenced by factors such as the frequency of automation runs, the amount of data processed, and the complexity of the tasks automated. Understanding the unit consumption of each automation type is key to estimating potential expenses accurately. To effectively manage expenses, PhantomBuster users can utilize the platform's internal dashboard to track unit consumption and adjust their automation strategies accordingly.
