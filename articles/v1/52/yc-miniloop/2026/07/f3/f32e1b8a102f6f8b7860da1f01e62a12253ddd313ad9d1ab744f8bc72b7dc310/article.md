## What are the billing units in Zendesk's pricing model?


Zendesk's pricing model is primarily agent-based, which means businesses are charged for each individual who accesses the platform. This structure can lead to significant costs as a company scales and requires a larger support team. Additional fees also apply for usage-based features, such as advanced analytics or AI-driven support capabilities, making it crucial for businesses to understand the full cost implications of their chosen tier and feature set.


Customers have the option to select between monthly and annual billing plans. Opting for an annual commitment can result in substantial savings, as Zendesk typically offers discounts for businesses willing to pay upfront for the year. This approach is beneficial for organizations with stable, predictable support needs that align well with the features and limitations of a chosen Zendesk tier.


The distinction between monthly and annual billing can affect cash flow management for businesses. A monthly plan might suit startups or smaller companies focused on maintaining flexibility without long-term commitments. However, larger enterprises with consistent usage may find the annual savings more appealing.
