# ZoomInfo Alternative: 10 Tools Worth Considering in 2026


*Last updated: July 2026*


**The top ZoomInfo alternatives for GTM teams are[Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io/?utm_source=miniloop) (best all-around replacement for the price, free plan, paid from $49/mo per user),[Cognism](https://www.cognism.com/?utm_source=miniloop) (best for EU/GDPR-compliant data, custom pricing),[Lusha](https://www.lusha.com/?utm_source=miniloop) (best for fast LinkedIn-based prospecting, free plan, paid from $22.45/mo per seat),[LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/?utm_source=miniloop) (best for AI-drafted outreach, free plan, paid from $45/mo).**


ZoomInfo still has the biggest contact database in B2B sales, but that's rarely why teams start looking elsewhere. It's the $15,000-$30,000+ annual contracts, the multi-seat minimums, and intent data that leans hard on IP tracking, an approach that gets noisier every year as more of the workday happens over VPN and remote networks. The 10 tools below cover the range of teams actually switching: startups that need a cheaper Apollo-style all-rounder, EU-based teams that need Cognism-level compliance built in, lean teams that just need one specific job (email finding, phone-verified data, AI-written openers) done well, and, at the other end, teams whose needs actually look more like ZoomInfo's and are choosing between enterprise-grade ABM platforms instead of downgrading to something smaller.


## Why Teams Look for a ZoomInfo Alternative


Four complaints come up repeatedly across ZoomInfo reviews and user reports, and each one points to a different kind of replacement.


**Pricing is opaque and expensive.** ZoomInfo doesn't publish pricing. Users report contracts starting around $15,000/year for basic access, with multi-year, multi-seat commitments the norm rather than the exception. We cover the full breakdown in[ZoomInfo Pricing 2026: What It Actually Costs (Hidden Fees Included)](https://www.miniloop.ai/blog/zoominfo-pricing) , and it's the single biggest reason smaller teams start comparing alternatives, since tools like Apollo.io and Hunter.io publish per-seat pricing from $34-49/month.


**Contact data goes stale.** Reviewers consistently flag outdated job titles, wrong company sizes, and dead contact records. Teams that need current data lean toward providers that manually verify contacts on a fixed schedule (SalesIntel re-verifies every 90 days) rather than relying purely on automated scraping.


**Intent data leans too hard on IP tracking.** ZoomInfo's buyer-intent signals are heavily IP-based, which struggles in a remote, VPN-heavy work environment where the visiting IP no longer reliably maps to a company. Alternatives built around other signal types, job changes, hiring activity, verified firmographic triggers, hold up better.


**Billing and setup are complicated.** Multiple reviewers cite a steep learning curve and unclear feature-to-cost mapping. Simpler, single-purpose tools (a contact finder, an email verifier) trade platform breadth for a setup you can understand in an afternoon.

## 10 ZoomInfo Alternatives Worth Evaluating


### Apollo.io


[Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io/?utm_source=miniloop) is a combined prospecting and outreach platform: it finds contacts, enriches company data, and runs the email sequences that follow. Multiple ZoomInfo comparisons point to it as the default starting point for teams that want data and execution in one subscription instead of stitching a database tool to a separate sequencer. Where ZoomInfo sells contact access as the product, Apollo.io treats the contact record as step one of a workflow that ends in a sent email.


**Best for:** Teams that want prospecting and outreach in a single subscription.


**Key features:**


- Contact and company search across public and private sources, including social profiles and job boards
- Live email verification, with a cap on cell-phone lookups at lower tiers
- Built-in sequence and campaign tools for outbound email
- AI-assisted message drafting and testing
- CRM integrations including HubSpot, Salesforce, and Outreach
- Sales triggers for tracking accounts showing buying signals


**Pricing:**


- Free plan available
- Paid plans start at $49/month per user, credit-based for enrichment and exports


**Strengths:** Covers data and outreach in one place, so smaller teams skip buying and maintaining a second tool just for sequencing. The database is broad enough to cover most SMB and mid-market prospecting without a dedicated enrichment layer.


**Weaknesses:** Pricing is credit-based, and reviewers note that heavy exporting or enrichment can get expensive faster than a flat-rate plan would. Data depth on large enterprise accounts still trails ZoomInfo's.


**Choose Apollo.io when:** you want prospecting and outreach in one subscription and don't need ZoomInfo's deeper enterprise data set.


### Cognism


[Cognism](https://www.cognism.com/?utm_source=miniloop) built its positioning around European coverage: GDPR-compliant sourcing and Do-Not-Call list checks across the US, Canada, Australia, the UK, and multiple EU countries. For teams selling into Europe, that compliance layer is part of the core product rather than an add-on bolted on to satisfy a legal team. ZoomInfo also supports GDPR and CCPA, but Cognism markets compliance as the reason to switch, not a checkbox.


**Best for:** Teams selling into EU/UK markets that need compliant data as a default, not an add-on.


**Key features:**


- Contact, firmographic, technographic, and intent filters sharpened with AI-based scoring
- Bombora-powered buyer intent data
- DNC list checking across the US, Canada, Australia, the UK, and other European countries
- Browser extension that works across LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Recruiter, Salesforce, and company sites
- Sales-trigger and signal data alongside contact records


**Pricing:**


- Custom pricing. Cognism doesn't publish rates; quotes are based on seats and region.


**Strengths:** Compliance and EU/UK data coverage are stronger defaults than most competitors on this list, ZoomInfo included. The DNC-checking layer alone removes a step legal and RevOps teams otherwise have to build themselves.


**Weaknesses:** No published pricing makes it harder to budget for before a sales conversation, and quotes vary enough by region that comparing Cognism to a flat-rate tool takes an actual sales call.


**Choose Cognism when:** GDPR and DNC compliance are non-negotiable and your buyers are concentrated in Europe.


### Lusha


[Lusha](https://www.lusha.com/?utm_source=miniloop) pulls contact data from public records and business profiles, verified through its own algorithms, and is built around fast, credit-based lookups rather than a full outreach platform. It's designed to be opened, used for ten minutes to pull a list, and closed again, not lived in all day the way Apollo.io or ZoomInfo are.


**Best for:** Lean teams that need quick, accurate contact lookups without a heavier platform.


**Key features:**


- Chrome extension for pulling contact data directly from LinkedIn and company sites
- Bombora intent-data integration to flag accounts actively researching
- AI-suggested leads based on existing sales activity
- Dynamic, auto-updating prospecting lists ("Playlists")
- Bulk enrichment for existing contact lists
- Web platform, Chrome extension, and API access


**Pricing:**


- Free plan available
- Pro: $22.45/month per seat (billed annually)
- Premium: $52.45/month per seat (billed annually)
- Scale: custom pricing


**Strengths:** Fast to start, and the credit system means you pay for what you use rather than a flat platform fee. The Chrome extension is simple enough that a new rep needs almost no onboarding.


**Weaknesses:** Phone-number credits cost far more per lookup than email credits do, which adds up fast for phone-heavy outbound. There's no built-in sequencing, so Lusha still needs to feed a separate outreach tool.


**Choose Lusha when:** you want quick, verified lookups and don't need a built-in outreach or sequencing layer.


### LeadIQ


[LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/?utm_source=miniloop) pulls contact data in real time from LinkedIn, AngelList, and company sites, then syncs it straight into Salesforce or HubSpot. Its built-in AI tool, Scribe, drafts personalized cold emails from the prospect data it just captured, collapsing the gap between finding a contact and writing to them.


**Best for:** Teams that want AI-assisted first-touch emails built into the contact-capture step.


**Key features:**


- Chrome extension that captures emails and direct dials while browsing
- Scribe AI writes personalized cold-email openers from live prospect data
- Job-change alerts to track when contacts move companies
- One-click export to Salesforce, HubSpot, and other CRMs with custom field mapping
- Account tracking so reps see movement inside target companies, not just individual contacts


**Pricing:**


- Free plan: 50 emails, 5 mobile numbers, 40 email generations per month
- Essential: $45/month
- Pro: $89/month
- Enterprise: custom pricing


**Strengths:** The AI drafting step is built into the same tool that captures the contact, so there's no handoff between data and outreach copy. Job-change alerts are a genuinely different signal type than ZoomInfo's IP-based intent data.


**Weaknesses:** Reviewers report emails sometimes default to genericname@company.com formats that bounce, and LinkedIn filtering is less flexible than dedicated prospecting tools. Phone-number coverage is thinner than email coverage.


**Choose LeadIQ when:** you want AI-drafted outreach copy generated at the point of capture, not as a separate step.


### SalesIntel


SalesIntel differentiates on verification: a research team manually checks every contact and refreshes the database on a 90-day cycle, instead of relying purely on automated scraping the way faster, cheaper tools do. That tradeoff, slower refresh cadence for higher confidence per record, is the opposite bet ZoomInfo makes with its larger, faster-updating but noisier database.


**Best for:** Teams that prioritize verified accuracy over raw database size.


**Key features:**


- Manual research team verifies every contact directly
- Database refreshed on a 90-day cycle
- Advanced filtering across 40+ criteria, including technographics and firmographics
- Bombora-powered real-time intent signals
- RevDriver Chrome extension for pulling verified data while browsing LinkedIn
- On-demand research for hard-to-find contacts not already in the database


**Pricing:**


- Individual plans from roughly $99-129/month for 100 credits
- Pro plans from roughly $225-299/month for 300 credits
- Team and Enterprise plans priced separately, starting in the five figures annually


**Strengths:** Manual verification means fewer dead contacts and wrong titles than fully automated databases, and the on-demand research option covers gaps a static database can't.


**Weaknesses:** Coverage is strongest in the US and thinner internationally, there's no free trial to test data quality first, and enterprise pricing isn't published upfront.


**Choose SalesIntel when:** contact accuracy matters more than raw database size and you're mostly selling into US accounts.


### Hunter.io


[Hunter.io](https://hunter.io/?utm_source=miniloop) does one job well: given a company domain, it finds the email addresses tied to that domain and verifies whether they're safe to send to. It doesn't try to be a ZoomInfo replacement in scope, only in the one function most teams actually reach for ZoomInfo to solve day to day.


**Best for:** Teams that only need email finding and verification, not a full contact database.


**Key features:**


- Domain search surfaces publicly available emails tied to a company, with job titles and sources
- Real-time email verification to reduce bounce rates
- Simple multi-step cold-email campaigns with open tracking
- Bulk search and verification for list-based prospecting
- Google Sheets add-on for finding and verifying emails without switching tools


**Pricing:**


- Free plan: 25 searches and 50 verifications per month
- Starter: $49/month
- Growth: $149/month
- Scale: $299/month
- Enterprise: custom pricing


**Strengths:** The cheapest way on this list to solve just the email-finding problem, with a usable free tier and a simple, intuitive interface.


**Weaknesses:** No phone-number lookup, no CRM-native calling or sales-engagement features, and support is email-only with no 24/7 option.


**Choose Hunter.io when:** email finding and verification is the only gap in your stack, not a full contact database.


### RocketReach


[RocketReach](https://rocketreach.co/?utm_source=miniloop) is a contact-lookup platform covering more than 35 million companies, built for teams that want fast, verified emails and phone numbers without committing to a full sales-intelligence platform the way ZoomInfo asks buyers to.


**Best for:** Teams that want simple contact lookups without buying into a larger platform.


**Key features:**


- Search by title, company, seniority, location, or industry
- Real-time email verification with bounce-prevention scoring
- Chrome extension for finding contact details on LinkedIn and company sites
- AI-suggested lead lists based on your ICP
- Bulk list upload plus org-chart and company-trend data on paid tiers


**Pricing:**


- Essentials: $99/month for 100 lookups per user
- Pro: $165/month for 250 lookups per user
- Ultimate: $300/month for 500 lookups per user
- Enterprise: custom pricing


**Strengths:** Straightforward pricing tiers and a Chrome extension simple enough to hand a new rep on day one, with meaningful time savings reported on bulk prospecting tasks.


**Weaknesses:** No built-in outreach, lead scoring, or intent data, and users report aggressive auto-renewal billing plus CRM sync gaps affecting a portion of HubSpot and Salesforce users.


**Choose RocketReach when:** you already have outreach and CRM tools in place and just need a reliable contact lookup layer.


### Lead411


Lead411 focuses on qualified leads backed by verified phone numbers and real-time sales-trigger alerts, positioning itself as a leaner alternative for teams that don't need ZoomInfo's full database or price tag but still want more than a bare contact finder.


**Best for:** Teams that weight verified phone numbers and trigger-based alerts highly.


**Key features:**


- Triple-verified mobile numbers and direct dials
- Sales-trigger alerts for funding rounds, leadership changes, expansions, and hiring
- Advanced filters by industry, revenue, location, and tech stack
- Chrome and Gmail extensions for in-browser and in-inbox prospecting
- Built-in email templates with open and click tracking


**Pricing:**


- Free trial: 7 days, 50 free leads
- Basic Plus Unlimited: $99/month per user ($75/month billed annually)
- Enterprise: custom pricing


**Strengths:** Verified phone data and real-time trigger alerts stand out relative to price, and the trigger alerts give reps a reason to reach out beyond a cold list.


**Weaknesses:** Monthly download and export limits can be restrictive for high-volume prospecting, additional exports cost extra, and reviewers flag outdated contact info after company mergers.


**Choose Lead411 when:** trigger-based alerts and verified phone numbers matter more than a broad, all-purpose database.


### UpLead


[UpLead](https://www.uplead.com/?utm_source=miniloop) verifies every email address live at the point of export rather than at the point the record was first added to the database, which is a direct answer to the stale-data complaint that pushes some teams away from ZoomInfo in the first place.


**Best for:** Teams that already run outreach elsewhere and just need clean, verified data to feed it.


**Key features:**


- Live verification at export, not just when the record entered the database
- 50+ search filters, including job title, company size, industry, revenue, location, and tech stack
- CRM integrations and API access for custom workflows
- Chrome extension for capturing contacts while browsing LinkedIn or company sites
- Technographic filtering to target by tech stack


**Pricing:**


- Free trial: 7 days, 5 credits
- Essentials: $99/month for 170 credits
- Plus: $199/month for 400 credits, adds data enrichment, technographics, and advanced filters
- Professional: custom pricing, annual billing only


**Strengths:** Verifying at export rather than at collection time cuts down on the bounce-driving staleness problem other databases have.


**Weaknesses:** Every export consumes credits whether or not the contact turns out to be useful, and unused credits don't roll over month to month.


**Choose UpLead when:** you already have a prospecting or outreach workflow and just need a cleaner, verified data source to feed it.


### 6sense

It's an account-based intelligence platform built for teams running full ABM programs, not a lighter contact finder, and it's worth including because some teams comparing ZoomInfo alternatives actually need more platform, not less.


**Best for:** Teams running account-based marketing programs that need buyer-journey tracking across channels, not just contact records.


**Key features:**

**Strengths:** Gives sales and marketing a shared view of account engagement and can surface buying signals earlier in the cycle than a static contact database.


**Weaknesses:** Pricing isn't published and starts well above every other tool on this list, reports can be dense to parse, and teams outside Salesforce, HubSpot, or Salesloft do more manual work to get full value.


**Choose 6sense when:** you're running ABM at a scale where account-level intelligence matters more than a per-contact database, and the budget looks more like ZoomInfo's than a bootstrap tool's.

## How to Choose the Right ZoomInfo Alternative


The right ZoomInfo alternative depends on which specific complaint sent you looking, not on company size alone.


**If budget is the constraint,** start with Hunter.io's free tier, Lusha's $22.45/month entry plan, or Apollo.io's $49/month plan. All three publish pricing upfront, unlike ZoomInfo, so procurement isn't a surprise after a sales call, and each covers enough of the core job (finding a contact, verifying it's live) to get a first outbound motion running without a five-figure commitment.


**If EU/GDPR compliance is the constraint,** Cognism is built around it by default: DNC list checks across the US, Canada, Australia, the UK, and multiple EU countries, plus GDPR-compliant sourcing baked into the core product rather than bolted on. ZoomInfo also supports GDPR and CCPA, but Cognism markets compliance as the reason to buy, which shows up in how the product is built, not just in a legal disclaimer.


**If data accuracy is the constraint,** SalesIntel's manual verification and 90-day refresh cycle directly answers the stale-data complaint that drives a lot of ZoomInfo churn. Lusha and Lead411 also lean on verification rather than pure automation, and UpLead verifies at the point of export specifically to catch records that went stale after they were first collected.


**If you only need one function solved,** don't buy a platform. Hunter.io solves email finding, RocketReach solves lookup-and-verify, and neither charges you for modules you won't use. This is usually the fastest way to cut cost without losing coverage on the one thing your team actually does every day.


**If AI-assisted outreach matters more than database size,** LeadIQ's Scribe drafts first-touch emails from the same data it just captured, collapsing two steps most teams run through separate tools.


**If your needs actually look like ZoomInfo's,** a full ABM program with multi-channel account tracking, not just a cheaper contact list, 6sense is a more honest comparison than any of the lighter tools above. It costs more than ZoomInfo in most cases, but it's solving a different problem than a contact finder.


None of these decisions are permanent. Teams commonly run two tools at once, Apollo.io for general prospecting and Cognism for EU accounts, rather than forcing every use case through a single platform the way a ZoomInfo contract tends to. The tradeoff is more vendors to manage, which is exactly the kind of GTM busywork the next section covers.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What is the best alternative to ZoomInfo?


Apollo.io is the most commonly recommended ZoomInfo alternative for teams that want broad contact coverage plus built-in outreach tools, with plans starting at $49/month per user versus ZoomInfo's $15,000+/year contracts. Cognism is the better pick if EU/GDPR compliance is a hard requirement, and Lusha or LeadIQ fit lean teams that want faster, cheaper prospecting without a full platform.


### Is there a free alternative to ZoomInfo?


Several ZoomInfo alternatives offer usable free tiers, including Apollo.io, Lusha, LeadIQ, and Hunter.io. They're limited in monthly search or export volume and won't match ZoomInfo's database depth, but they're enough to test data quality and workflow fit before committing to a paid plan.


### Why are sales teams switching away from ZoomInfo?


The most cited reasons are opaque, expensive pricing (contracts starting around $15,000/year with multi-seat minimums), contact data that goes stale between refreshes, buyer-intent signals that rely heavily on IP tracking (which struggles with remote and VPN traffic), and a complicated billing and onboarding process.


### What is the cheapest ZoomInfo alternative?


Lusha has the lowest paid entry point on this list at $22.45/month per seat (billed annually), with a free tier covering five credits a month. Hunter.io's paid plans start at $49/month with a free plan covering 25 searches and 50 verifications a month. Both are far below ZoomInfo's five-figure annual contracts.


### Which ZoomInfo alternative is best for GDPR and EU data compliance?


Cognism is built specifically around EU and UK compliance: GDPR-compliant data sourcing and Do-Not-Call list checks across the US, Canada, Australia, the UK, and multiple European countries are part of the core product rather than an add-on. It's the clearest choice for teams selling primarily into European markets.
