# Jasper + Claude: Bringing Marketing Intelligence Into Your Workflow

Marketing teams have gone all in on AI; it writes the emails, drafts the decks, researches competitors, and builds the campaigns.


And yet something keeps breaking.


According to[Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-04-07-gartner-says-artificial-intelligence-projects-in-infrastructure-and-operations-stall-ahead-of-meaningful-roi-returns) , just 28% of AI projects meet ROI expectations.[Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/report/RES193461) found that 70% of brand owners have experienced an AI-related brand incident, like off-brand copy going live or inaccurate claims. The AI tools are powerful, but there is a gap between what they generate and the quality and consistency enterprise marketing actually requires.


That gap is marketing intelligence: the context and governance that turn raw AI output into assets that drive revenue and authentically represent your brand.


Jasper has partnered with Claude, a leading AI model for marketing and knowledge work, to give marketers the best of both worlds: best-in-class reasoning and long-form capabilities from Claude, paired with Jasper IQ's marketing intelligence to deliver context, governance, and on-brand content at scale.


## **Where general-purpose AI falls short for marketers**


For a single task, general-purpose AI tools can get you somewhere. But even the best LLMs require pasting in guidelines, re-explaining your audiences, and re-establishing your voice every single session in order to stay on brand.


It’s a time burden that slows production and can compromise the brand:


- **Context gets rebuilt every session.** Rebuilding brand context for every project is time intensive and manual, and often becomes inconsistent across sessions. Extensive setup with skills and fine-tuning memory helps, but on-going management takes both time and access while output still varies.
- **Governance and visibility are limited.** Marketing leaders have no centralized window into what their teams are generating, whether it meets brand standards, or where risk is creeping into the workflow.
- **Brand consistency breaks down.** Fifty people prompting their own way means fifty versions of the brand voice. Over time, messaging drifts and campaigns can become incohesive.
- **ROI is hard to prove.** Content gets created, but the measurement layer isn't there. Without analytics and governance, activity doesn’t translate to impact.
- **Point solutions don't fix the underlying problem.** Plugins help in the moment but can't deliver the persistent brand context, campaign memory, and governance that enterprise marketing actually runs on.


Solving these challenges requires an intelligence layer that works *with* the power of LLMs like Claude, embedding your voice into every output and delivering[brand consistency](https://www.jasper.ai/blog/brand-consistency) at enterprise scale.

## **When to use Jasper vs. Claude (and when to use both)**


The partnership between Claude and Jasper works because they’re built for fundamentally different kinds of marketing work. Knowing which to reach for—and when to use both—is what maximizes their value in the marketing tech stack.


### Taxonomy-mapped work goes to Jasper


This is your structured campaign execution: email copy, social content, content variants, segmentation, and localization. They’re repeatable, multi-dimensional, governed outputs where brand consistency matters most.


You define the campaign once, and[Jasper Grid](https://www.jasper.ai/grid) executes your content strategy at scale across all channels, segments, and regions, keeping every variant on-brand.


### Judgment-dependent work goes to Claude, grounded in Jasper IQ via MCP


This includes your large, one-off projects: strategic narratives, executive communications, long-form research synthesis, and anchor assets. The full scope can't be defined in advance, human judgment drives the creative direction, and the content drafting process is exploratory.


Claude excels at this open-ended, reasoning-heavy work. Claude's ability to hold nuance across long contexts, reason through ambiguity, and produce structurally sophisticated output makes it the right partner for work that can't be templated.


When connected to the Jasper MCP, Claude can draw on the same brand intelligence, governance standards, and institutional knowledge that power content created in Jasper.


Claude handles the open-ended. Jasper handles the governed. MCP bridges the two.
