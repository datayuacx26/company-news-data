# What’s New in July 2026

Generating content is easy now. Every team has enterprise AI, and that's exactly the problem. Volume without governance produces commodity content that answer engines increasingly overlook. The real work is operationalizing content: governing it so it's accurate and on-brand, keeping quality consistent across teams, and earning measurable visibility in the AI answers where your buyers now make decisions.


This month is built for that work. The Jasper[GEO Agent](https://www.jasper.ai/geo) turns AI-search optimization into a continuous, autonomous motion. The[GEO Hub](https://www.jasper.ai/geo) extends your view across every engine that matters.[Jasper IQ](https://www.jasper.ai/jasper-iq) governs every output, so what you ship is the kind of content AI actually cites.


Here's everything new this month, and how to put it to work.


## Automatically Optimize for How Buyers Discover You


Getting cited in AI answers isn't one task, it's hundreds, repeated continuously as models and competitors move. Most GEO tools stop at monitoring: they show you where you stand and leave the work to you. The Jasper[GEO Agent](https://www.jasper.ai/geo) closes the loop. It uses the same visibility picture the[GEO Hub](https://www.jasper.ai/geo) gives you and works on a cadence, so your visibility is maintained, not just measured once. Because it builds on persistent memory run over run, your optimization compounds instead of resetting.


- **Understand what's happening and what to fix first** . Drill into your GEO Hub scores, see why they moved over time, and get a clear read on the highest-impact fixes.
- **Turn recommendations into publish-ready content** . The GEO Agent produces, edits, and optimizes the actual content that earns citations. Not a backlog of suggestions, but work that's ready to ship.
- **Keep insight and action in one place** . Push completed drafts straight into[Asana](https://www.jasper.ai/blog/jasper-asana-integration) ,[Monday.com](https://www.jasper.ai/blog/jasper-monday) , or[Webflow](https://help.jasper.ai/hc/en-us/articles/18618715638171-Jasper-Webflow-Integration) , and pull keyword and competitive data from[Semrush](https://help.jasper.ai/hc/en-us/articles/53731968390555-Integrations-Semrush) . No tool-switching, no manual handoffs.
- **Put optimization on autopilot** . Schedule recurring analyses and fixes so the work keeps running on its own. For example, a weekly competitor check happens without anyone remembering to start it.


Everything the GEO Agent ships stays accurate and on-brand because of[Jasper IQ](https://www.jasper.ai/jasper-iq) . The GEO Hub finds the opportunity, the GEO Agent acts on it, and Jasper IQ is the governance and truth layer that keeps every action grounded in your approved Brand Voice, Style Guide, Audiences, and Knowledge. That's the difference between adding to the AI slop and publishing content AI wants to cite.


## Own Your Brand Across Every Engine


You can't defend a footprint you can't see. Winning in AI search means controlling how your brand is represented everywhere buyers ask. This month the[GEO Hub](https://www.jasper.ai/geo) extends your view to one of the most important surfaces in AI search:[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) is now a selectable engine. Because[Perplexity tends to cite 2x more sources per answer than most engines](https://quickseo.ai/blog/chatgpt-vs-perplexity-for-ai-visibility-in-2026-citations-traffic-and-conversion-compared) , it's a high-leverage place to be measured and optimized. Teams who add it to their analyses often see their overall citation rate rise.


- **Track across every major engine** . Measure presence, citation rate, sentiment, and share of voice across[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) ,[Claude](https://claude.ai/) ,[Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) , and now[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) in one shared view.
- **Cover more in a single pass** . The per-analysis page limit jumped from 300 to 1,000, so large sites get real coverage every run.
- **Move faster on setup and reporting** . Duplicate an existing analysis instead of rebuilding it from scratch, and export any analysis to PDF for stakeholder reporting.


Tracking your footprint is only half the job. The content you act on has to be citable. Answer engines reward accuracy, differentiation, and consistency, not raw volume.[Jasper IQ](https://www.jasper.ai/jasper-iq) grounds every output in your approved brand, audience, product, and knowledge context, so the work you action is high-quality and on-brand.


## Everything Else: Cleaner Handoffs, Sharper Visuals, and Control at Scale


This month also includes a set of supporting improvements that make everyday work faster, cleaner, and easier to govern across teams.


- [Reach every market and every team](https://www.jasper.ai/agents/translation) . Chinese now splits into three explicit variants: Simplified, Traditional, and Traditional (Hong Kong). Available everywhere from the editor to the browser extension.
- **Find and launch the right agent, fast** . The new Agent Selector lets you easily switch agents right inside Chat, Canvas, and Grid with your context and Jasper IQ carried along.
- **Hand content off without issues** .[Export to Wrike](https://www.jasper.ai/blog/jasper-wrike) , open the exact document with true Grid deep links, export tables to[SharePoint](https://help.jasper.ai/hc/en-us/articles/51554263123483-Integrations-SharePoint) and[Box](https://help.jasper.ai/hc/en-us/articles/18618604425883-Export-Content) with formatting intact, and export in bulk by Canvas section. Finished content lands where your reviewers already work.
- **Ship sharper visuals with less rework** .[Image Upscaler v5.0](https://developers.jasper.ai/docs/using-images#upscale) delivers natural, photo-realistic results instead of over-sharpened output.[Uncrop v2.2](https://developers.jasper.ai/docs/using-images#uncrop) adds color-shift correction to remove the seams and color mismatches that used to require a manual redo.
- **Govern and measure at scale** . New User Tags let admins segment usage by team, region, or business unit for a clear picture of adoption. Public API updates (attachments, project settings, and custom metadata) let technical teams wire Jasper and its governed IQ context into their own systems.
