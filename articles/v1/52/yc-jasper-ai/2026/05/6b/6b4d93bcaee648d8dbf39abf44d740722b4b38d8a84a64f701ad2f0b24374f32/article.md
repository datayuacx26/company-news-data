# What’s New in April 2026

AI search is quickly becoming one of the most important ways buyers discover, evaluate, and trust brands. According to[Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/webinars/buyers-journey-answer-engine-optimization/) , 94% of B2B organizations are already using AI in their buying process, and 69% of buyers are considering different vendors because of it. If your content isn’t showing up in AI-generated answers, you’re effectively invisible to a growing share of your market.


This month’s Jasper release is built to close that gap. We focused on helping teams turn AEO/GEO strategy into repeatable execution, set up the brand intelligence that powers better content, and strengthen the controls that keep every output aligned.


The result is simple. Your team can create AI-search-ready content faster, scale the workflows that work, and stay on-brand everywhere Jasper is used.


## Turn AI search strategy into repeatable execution


Most teams know AI search matters. The harder part is knowing what to do next. This month, Jasper introduces AI Visibility Workflows in Grid, giving marketers a structured path for creating, improving, and competing with content designed for AI-native discovery.


- **Follow a structured system designed for AI citation** : Each workflow walks you through a multi-step process that mirrors how AI models evaluate and surface content. This ensures your content is not just discoverable, but usable in AI-generated answers.
- **Choose the right workflow for your goal** : Upgrade existing content to meet GEO best practices, build new content designed for AI visibility from day one, or analyze competitors and identify where your content can win
- **Generate outputs that AI systems are more likely to use and cite** : Every workflow produces structured, AI-friendly formats like FAQs, key takeaways, and schema markup—alongside long-form content. These formats increase clarity, extractability, and citation potential across AI platforms.
- **Measure readiness with built-in GEO scoring** : Content is evaluated against a rubric aligned to current AI search best practices, giving teams a clear signal of how ready their content is before it goes live—and where to improve. **‍**
- **Turn strategy into execution at scale** : Once inputs are defined, Jasper’s agents handle the heavy lifting across every step. This makes it possible to apply AI visibility best practices consistently across campaigns, topics, and teams—not just as one-off efforts.


## Scale proven workflows instead of rebuilding them


Most teams already have workflows that work. The problem is that they often live in one-off Grids, scattered prompts, or individual team knowledge. This month, Grid Templates become easier to reuse, share, and improve over time.


- **Edit templates with the full Grid experience** : Update columns, rows, cells, sample data, and workflow structure without recreating a template from scratch.
- **Save and share your best workflows** : Turn any Grid into a reusable template for yourself or your workspace, complete with a name, description, category, permissions, and sample data.
- **Find the right starting point faster** : The Template Library now includes Jasper Templates, Workspace Templates, and My Templates, with search to help teams quickly get moving. **‍**
- **Keep workflows improving over time** : Autosave and live editing make templates feel like living systems your team can refine as campaigns, markets, and processes change.


## Everything else: More control, collaboration, and scale


Scaling AI across a marketing organization requires more than generation. Teams also need governance, collaboration, and clear controls that make Jasper more precise, more consistent, and easier to manage day to day.


- **Set up Jasper IQ in minutes with the IQ Agent** : Automatically generate Brand Voice, Audiences, and Knowledge Base assets using your company profile—so your team starts with high-quality context from day one.
- **Apply Style Guides inside Canvas workflows** : Ask Jasper and Slash Commands now inherit the document’s Style Guide, so inline edits and additions stay aligned.
- **Control casing for branded terms and acronyms** : Case sensitivity gives teams more precision for terms where capitalization changes meaning.
- **Handle first mentions more naturally** : New rules make it easier to spell out a term once, then use a shortened version after.
- **Reduce manual cleanup** : Improvements to abbreviation handling, hyphens, and nested JSON responses help Style Guide rules apply more reliably.
- **Control Company Memory** : Admins can turn Company Memory injection on or off from Workspace Settings.
- **Share the exact part of a Canvas** : Deep links now let teammates open a specific Canvas asset or section without searching through the whole workspace.
- **Move finished content into Box** : Enterprise customers can export Jasper content directly to Box.
