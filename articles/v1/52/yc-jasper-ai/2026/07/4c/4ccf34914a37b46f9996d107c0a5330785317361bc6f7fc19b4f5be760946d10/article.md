# How To Use the Jasper × Wrike Integration

Marketing content rarely moves directly from creation to publication. A single campaign can pass through strategists, writers, designers, reviewers, regional teams, and outside agencies before it reaches the customer.


Wrike gives teams a place to coordinate that work, from intake and assignments through approvals and delivery. But what it can’t do is produce content from a brand-governed source for each campaign, asset and variant.


So the content gets made somewhere else, and then it has to travel. Someone downloads the draft, opens the right project, creates the task, attaches the file, and tags the reviewer. Do that once, and it is a minor chore. Do it across an intake queue, feeding four teams and a set of external partners, and the moving becomes the job.


The bigger cost is not the clicks. It is what happens to the work in transit. Wrike's own[Dark Matter of Work research](https://www.wrike.com/newsroom/dark-matter-of-work-financial-cost-of-work-complexities/) found that up to 55% of the work inside an organization is invisible to the people who need to see it, in part because tools do not talk, which drives duplicated effort and wasted time. A draft attached to a task today is a snapshot. Edits keep happening in the source, the file on the task ages, and a reviewer three steps down the chain marks up a version already out of date.


AI raises the stakes on that gap. When anyone can generate a campaign's worth of content in an afternoon, producing it is no longer the hard part. Scaling it responsibly is: keeping every piece on-brand, reviewed, and traceable as volume climbs. More output pushed through broken handoffs does not get you more done. It gets you more versions to reconcile, and more room for an off-brand or outdated piece to reach a customer. Responsible scale is achievable, but only when brand context and a single connected source travel with the work, instead of getting stripped away at each handoff. That is the difference between the appearance of scale and the real thing.


## What is the Jasper × Wrike integration?


The integration connects on-brand content creation in[Jasper Canvas](https://www.jasper.ai/canvas) and[Grid](https://www.jasper.ai/grid) to Wrike, where your team coordinates the work. Open a piece in Jasper, choose how it should travel, pick where it belongs, and export. Jasper creates a new task or adds to one already in the project, names it, and links back to the source.


From there, the work is Wrike's to run: the assignee, the status, the approval path, its place in the wider work plan, and the reporting your team already relies on. Jasper produces the content. Wrike coordinates the people who touch it.


A single blog post exports the same way as a full campaign. Generate a run of localized landing page sections and social variants in Grid in one sitting, and each piece lands in the right Wrike project as its own task, ready to route, instead of being carried over one file at a time.


## Why does it matter?


Campaign work rarely stalls in the writing. It stalls in the handoffs, and in the doubt that creeps in when no one knows which file is current. A draft sits in a downloads folder for two days. A batch of variants lands in the wrong project. A reviewer pings the writer, asking where the latest version lives. By the time a piece clears the chain, someone has to stop and confirm it still matches what went out everywhere else. These delays are real, cumulative and costly.


Because every piece is generated through[Jasper IQ](https://www.jasper.ai/jasper-iq) , the layer that holds your[brand voice](https://www.jasper.ai/brand-voice) , approved messaging, and[company knowledge](https://www.jasper.ai/knowledge-base) , the content that lands in Wrike already reads like your brand. The version your team tracks and approves is one people can stand behind, and reviewers spend their time confirming accuracy, rather than rewriting voice from a raw draft. When the export carries a link back to the live Jasper doc, the task stays tied to its source, so the trail from creation to approval holds together instead of scattering across tools.


That traceability is the point. In teams where work must survive an audit or compliance check, a task that links back to where the content came from is worth more than a file with no history.


## How do you set it up?


Setup takes a couple of minutes and happens in two layers. A workspace admin enables Wrike once under Workspace Settings > Team Integrations, then each person connects their own Wrike account. No IT project and no waiting on a technical team.


For step-by-step instructions, see the[Jasper Help Center](https://help.jasper.ai/hc/en-us/articles/53702694254619-Integrations-Wrike) , or head straight to Workspace Settings in Jasper to connect.


## How do you send content from Jasper to Wrike?


In Canvas or Grid, open the piece you want to send, select Export, and choose Wrike. Add the content to a task you are already working in, or create a new one, and it lands in the right project. Confirm Wrike is toggled on in the integrations panel the first time you use it.


### Choose how content travels with the task


At export, you decide how the content reaches the team. Three options:


1. **Add a link to the Jasper doc.** A link to the live document in Jasper is added to the Wrike task, so reviewers work in the source and every edit stays current. Best when you want one place, the latest version always lives.
2. **Attach the Jasper doc to the task.** The content rides on the task as an attached Word file (.docx). Best when a stakeholder needs to open, mark up, or pass along the copy without a Jasper seat.
3. **Save to cloud storage, then link from the task.** The document is saved to your connected cloud storage, such as SharePoint or Google Drive, and a link to it is added to the task. Best for teams that keep final assets in one library and want the task to point there.


Routing every export to the same destination pulls scattered reviews back into one place and keeps the Wrike task tied to a single source, so the version of record does not fragment as it moves.


## How marketing teams put this to work


If your team runs on Wrike and produces content at real volume, the integration fits a job you already do every week: turn an intake request into on-brand content and move it through the right hands-on time. Three ways that plays out.


### Marketing operations running an intake model


An ops team fields requests from across the business through Wrike forms, then routes each one to the right owner. With the integration, the content generated against the brief in Jasper exports straight into the matching Wrike task, so the request and its deliverable stay together from intake to delivery instead of living in two systems.


### Creative and agency teams coordinating with outside partners


A team working with external designers, freelancers, or an agency needs everyone touching the same current version. Export each finished piece into the shared Wrike project, attach the file or link the source, and partners pick it up where the rest of the work is tracked, without a Jasper seat and without a separate email thread carrying the latest draft.


### Regulated teams that need a traceable version of record


A marketing team in healthcare or financial services has to show its work: which version went to review, when, and where it came from. Exporting from Jasper into Wrike with a link back to the source gives each task a clear line from content to approval, so the trail holds up when compliance or an audit asks for it, instead of being reconstructed from folders and inboxes after the fact.


## Looking ahead


When content has to be downloaded, renamed, re-uploaded, and hand-routed into a work management tool, campaigns inherit delays that compound as teams scale, and the version of record gets harder to trust with every handoff.


The Jasper Wrike integration keeps content moving through the process your team already runs. Marketers stay in Jasper to create on-brand content. Coordinators stay in Wrike to assign owners, manage reviews, and track delivery. As AI makes it possible to produce more content than ever, the advantage goes to teams that can keep that content moving, and keep it traceable, from creation to customer.


## Frequently asked questions


***Does the Jasper Wrike integration work with both Canvas and Grid?*** *‍*


Yes. You can send content to Wrike from both Jasper Canvas and Jasper Grid, including high-volume Grid runs. Each exported piece lands as a task in the project you choose, either a new task or one already there.


***How does the content reach the Wrike task?*** *‍*


Three ways to export: add a link to the Jasper doc on the task, attach the content as a Word file (.docx), or save it to connected cloud storage such as SharePoint or Google Drive, and add a link to the task. Cloud storage must be connected first.


***Who needs to set it up?*** *‍*


Setup happens in two layers. A workspace admin enables Wrike under Workspace Settings > Team Integrations, then each person connects their own Wrike account.


***Does the export keep a link back to the original content in Jasper?***


Yes. Each export can link to the live Jasper document, so the Wrike task stays tied to its source, and the current version is always one click away.


***How does this integration keep content on-brand as it moves across tools and channels?***


Brand control cannot live in one tool when the work does not. Every piece you create in Jasper is generated through Jasper IQ, the layer that applies your brand voice, approved messaging, and company knowledge to each output. Content is on-brand before it leaves Jasper, whether it lands in Wrike, a cloud storage library, or anywhere else your team works. As you scale volume, that standard holds across every surface instead of drifting tool by tool, so reviewers confirm accuracy rather than reset voice.
