## What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?


Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing content so that LLMs like ChatGPT and Gemini cite it as a trusted source in their responses. To do this, it's first important to understand how LLMs source their answers.


Unlike traditional search engines that provide a list of ranked links for users to parse, LLMs synthesize answers from multiple sources. It's looking for content that's easy to parse and reference, and that has authority indicators showing it can be trusted.


GEO works by structuring content intentionally to make it AI-friendly in these ways, with a focus on authority, clarity, and factual accuracy.


Key GEO tactics include:


- **Evidence-backed authority:** Original research, verifiable statistics, and data-driven insights get cited more frequently than unsupported opinions. AI models prioritize factual, well-sourced information.
- **Clear structure:** Schema markup, descriptive metadata—including meta titles and descriptions ([Jasper's Meta Description Generator workflow](https://www.jasper.ai/workflows/meta-description-generator) produces click-optimized titles and descriptions at page scale)—organized headings, charts, and tables help models parse and accurately reuse your information.
- **Transparency signals:** Clear authorship, proper citations, and data provenance build credibility with AI systems. Models favor content where they can verify the source and expertise behind the information.


## What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?


Answer engine optimization (AEO) formats content for AI search features like Google's AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, and Perplexity's instant answers. It structures content to appear in answer-first responses that users increasingly rely on in their searches.


Instead of ranking pages, answer engines extract and present information directly within the interface. This means content must not only be optimized to rank through keywords or topical relevance, but also hyper-aligned with the questions users ask and the intent behind their searches.


AEO ensures that content can be easily extracted and delivered in these environments, with a focus on clarity, relevance, and immediate usefulness.


**Key AEO tactics include:**


- **Directness over cleverness:** Answer user questions clearly and immediately. Content that gets to the point quickly is more likely to be surfaced in answer-first results.
- **Structure for extraction:** Use formats like FAQs, bullet points, tables, and lists that align with how answer engines present information.
- **Machine-readable language:** Write in plain, precise language that's easy for AI systems to interpret. Avoid ambiguity, complex phrasing, or unnecessary jargon that could reduce extractability.


## How do GEO and AEO differ from traditional SEO?


In practice, SEO, AEO, and GEO should operate as a complementary, three-layer strategy:


- **SEO** establishes baseline visibility across traditional search engines
- **AEO** ensures accessibility in answer-driven search features
- **GEO** positions content as trusted reference material for generative outputs


GEO and AEO don't replace SEO. In fact, SEO lays the foundation for better success in AI search visibility.[Nearly 40%](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10/#:~:text=New%20data%20from%20863K%20SERPs:%20just%2038%25,76%25%20a%20year%20ago.%20Here's%20what%20changed.) of Google's AI Overviews, for example, rank in the top 10 organic search results, and nearly 70% rank in the top 100. So while AI widens the playing field outside of page one, top-ranking content is still a factor for AEO in particular.

## Frequently asked questions


#### **What is the difference between GEO and AEO?**


Generative engine optimization (GEO) focuses on getting cited by large language models like ChatGPT and Claude in their generated responses, while answer engine optimization (AEO) optimizes for AI-powered search features like Google's AI Overviews and answer snippets.


#### **Do I still need traditional SEO if I implement GEO and AEO?**


Yes, traditional SEO remains essential. GEO and AEO enhance rather than replace SEO, as generative engines rely on many of the same authority and relevance signals that traditional search algorithms use.


#### **How do I measure success with generative engine optimization?**


Track citations in AI responses, brand mentions in generated content, referral traffic from AI platforms, and conversion rates from AI-referred visitors rather than traditional ranking metrics.


#### **Which content formats work best for AI search optimization?**


Research reports, data-driven articles, comprehensive FAQs, how-to guides, and structured comparisons perform well across both GEO and AEO optimization strategies.[Content that helps buyers make decisions](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/aeo-changes-what-content-must-do-help-buyers-decide/) is more critical than ever. But most importantly, content should be original, accurate, and aligned with your latest messaging and positioning to ensure your brand surfaces consistently everywhere your audience is searching.


#### **How often should I update content for AI search optimization?**


Refresh and update content regularly to maintain accuracy, add new research findings, and ensure information remains current for AI citation worthiness. A study by[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/) found that ChatGPT is the most likely to cite newer pages.
