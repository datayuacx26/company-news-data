# Jasper Named HyperCUBEd Innovation Award Winner in 2026 Tech Innovation CUBEd Awards

Jasper has been named the **HyperCUBEd Innovation Award winner (Startups category)** in SiliconANGLE Media’s 2026 Tech Innovation CUBEd Awards.


This annual awards program recognizes leading companies, technologies, and leaders shaping the future of B2B and B2B2C technology. Winners are selected by a panel of industry analysts, practitioners, and technologists and evaluated on innovation, differentiation, real-world impact, and execution. The HyperCUBEd award represents the program’s highest level of recognition for companies that consistently push what’s possible in technology and lead the market with bold ideas and measurable results.


We’re honored to be recognized by SiliconANGLE Media and theCUBE at a pivotal moment for enterprise AI. Marketing teams are moving beyond AI experimentation toward operational transformation, embedding AI directly into the systems and workflows that power day-to-day execution. That shift requires more than standalone tools. It demands structure, governance, and coordination across teams and markets.


By combining intelligent agents, structured content pipelines, and built-in governance, Jasper enables organizations to operationalize AI at scale. The result is faster execution, stronger brand consistency, and enterprise-grade control, turning strategy into measurable business impact.


We’re proud to share this milestone with our customers, partners, and team, and we remain focused on building the infrastructure that helps enterprises move from AI potential to AI performance.


Learn more about the 2026 Tech Innovation CUBEd Awards and view the full list of winners[here](https://www.thecube.net/awards) .
