# Academy Sports + Outdoors Kicks Off National Bike Month with the Launch of New E-Bikes

## Academy Sports + Outdoors Kicks Off National Bike Month with the Launch of New E-Bikes


May 21, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.academy.com/node/11846/pdf)


*Company is also offering exclusive deals on adult bikes, kid’s bikes, and e-bikes throughout May*


**KATY, Texas, (May 21, 2026)** –[Academy Sports + Outdoors](https://www.academy.com/) (“Academy”) ([Nasdaq: ASO](https://investors.academy.com/) ), a leading full-line sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer, today announced the company is celebrating National Bike Month from Monday, May 11, through Monday, May 25. Customers can enjoy exclusive deals and new product launches designed to get riders of all ages and skill levels outdoors to join in on the fun.

**About Academy Sports + Outdoors**
Academy is a leading full-line sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer in the United States. Originally founded in 1938 as a family business in Texas, Academy has grown to more than 300 stores across 21 states and counting. Academy's mission is to provide "Fun for All" and Academy fulfills this mission with a localized merchandising strategy and value proposition that strongly connects with a broad range of consumers. Academy's product assortment focuses on key categories of outdoor, apparel, sports & recreation and footwear through both leading national brands and a portfolio of private label brands.
