# Rebellyous Foods Expands K-12 Reach in the Northeast Through Affinity Group Partnership

We are excited to announce a new strategic partnership between Rebellyous Foods and Affinity Group to support our continued growth and commitment to serving the K-12 school segment in the Northeast region.
Effective July 1st, Affinity Group will represent Rebellyous Foods as our official broker partner for K-12 school foodservice across the Northeast market. This collaboration marks a significant step forward in our mission to provide delicious, nutritious, and sustainable plant-based options that meet the evolving needs of students and foodservice operators alike.
Affinity Group brings deep expertise in the K-12 segment, a strong regional presence, and a shared passion for delivering innovative, student-friendly food solutions. With their support, we aim to expand access to our products and better serve the needs of school districts throughout the region.
Rebellyous Foods is committed to making plant-based eating accessible for every student—with nutritious, kid-approved options that meet USDA NSLP guidelines and support the goals of modern child nutrition programs. We’re confident that this partnership will enhance our ability to support your programs with efficiency, responsiveness, and service.
