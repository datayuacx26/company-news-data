### The High Cost of Manual Errors


A single typo in an Excel measurement chart or a wrong material code in your Bill of Materials (BOM) can quietly sabotage an entire production run. This one simple mistake can lead to rejected samples, wasted materials, and thousands of dollars lost.


This manual process also creates the version control nightmare every production manager dreads. When your factory receives a file named` T-Shirt_TechPack_V5_FINAL_use_this_one.pdf` , how can you be certain they are working from the correct information? This confusion is a leading cause of production errors, all stemming from the lack of a single source of truth for your[tech pack](https://www.aitechpacks.com/blog/tech-pack-clothing-complete-guide) .


### Disconnected Workflows Kill Speed and Collaboration


The gap between a visual design in Illustrator and a data-driven spec in Excel is where speed and collaboration go to die. Your designer thinks in vectors and layers, while your production manager lives in component codes and measurement tables. The time and effort spent translating information between these two worlds creates a "translation tax" on every single style you develop.


This constant back-and-forth makes feedback loops painfully slow and effective collaboration nearly impossible. For production to run smoothly, your tech pack must serve as a clear, unambiguous blueprint for your factory[\[1\]](https://supplyscope.io/how-to-design-a-good-tech-pack) , a goal that’s difficult to reach when critical data is scattered across different file types and email chains.
