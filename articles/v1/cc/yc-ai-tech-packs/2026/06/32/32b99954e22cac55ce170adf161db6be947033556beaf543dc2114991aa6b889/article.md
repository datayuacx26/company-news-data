# What is AI Tech Packs? Factory-Ready Tech Packs in 3 Minutes

## What is AI Tech Packs?


AI Tech Packs is a software tool that uses AI to create and edit tech packs for apparel products. You bring your design, we generate a complete, factory-ready techpack in about 3 minutes. Flat sketches, POMs, BOM, size charts, construction details, all of it.


That's the short answer. But there's a longer one, and it starts with a rule we don't break.


## Our golden rule


We are not a design tool. We only make tech packs.


AI Tech Packs is a production tool. We're not here to help you design, brainstorm, or conceptually change your vision. Don't come to us expecting the tool to explore ideas with you. Come to us when your design is final and you're ready to communicate it to the factory.


That sounds like a limitation. It's actually the whole point. The tools that try to do everything end up doing the production part badly. We picked one job, communicating a finished design to a factory, and we do that job better than anyone.


## First, what's a tech pack?


If you're new to apparel production, a tech pack (sometimes written as techpack) is the blueprint your factory uses to make your garment. Flat sketches, measurements, materials, colorways, stitching, labels. No serious factory will quote or sample your product without one.


A good tech pack is the difference between getting back the sample you imagined and burning three more revision rounds fixing it. And it's a document that changes constantly through sampling, from 1st proto to SMS to PP. Which brings me to the real point.


## "Can't AI already make a tech pack?"


Yes. It's 2026. You can ask basically any AI to generate a techpack and it'll spit out something that looks like one.


So why use AI Tech Packs?


Because generating a tech pack was never the hard part. Editing it is.


A tech pack is a living document. Measurements change after the first fitting. Your factory asks for a different seam spec. Your buyer wants two more colorways. A generic AI tool gives you a static output, and the second you need to change one POM or swap a fabric in the BOM, you're re-prompting from scratch or rebuilding the whole thing in Excel.


AI Tech Packs is built for that loop. You edit any part of an AI-generated techpack directly. Change a measurement, the size chart updates. Re-export to Excel or PDF in the format your factory already accepts. The AI gets you 90% of the way in minutes. The editing gets you the last 10% without fighting the tool.


Making a techpack isn't special anymore. The UI and UX of how you edit techpacks, and how they connect to your operational workflow, is.


## Built for apparel, nothing else


We're hyper optimized for apparel products. Not a general design tool that happens to output spec sheets. That focus is why generation takes 3 minutes instead of an afternoon, and why the output matches what factories actually expect.


Also, our team knows how real clothing brands work. The product is built around the actual sampling workflow. Proto, SMS, PP, TOP. Revisions, version history, the unglamorous stuff that decides whether your production calendar holds.


## It plugs into your workflow


Tech packs don't live in isolation. They live next to your PLM.


AI Tech Packs connects to PLM systems like BlueCherry by CGS. We're not replacing your PLM. Your PLM tracks tech packs. We help your team make them, at the sampling and revision stage where designer hours actually get burned. The two sit side by side.

- Factories building techpacks for their customers
- Independent brands making their first tech pack and wanting it done right


If your team is spending hours per techpack in Excel, that's the problem we built this for.
