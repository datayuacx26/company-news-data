# Rezolve Ai Named a Contender in The Forrester Wave™: Commerce Search and Product Discovery Solutions, Q3 2025

Rezolve Ai has been named a Contender in *The Forrester Wave™: Commerce Search and Product Discovery Solutions, Q3 2025* . This recognition affirms our place among the most significant providers in the category and highlights our commitment to reimagining how retailers and shoppers connect in the age of AI.


## **Forrester’s Perspective on Rezolve Ai**


In its evaluation, Forrester called out several areas where Rezolve Ai excels. Our solution was recognized for **superior internationalization** , making global deployments seamless with streamlined translation and multilingual support. Forrester also highlighted our **product data enrichment tooling** , which balances the power of AI with intuitive controls (catalog-as-a-service) that give merchandising teams clarity and confidence.


Our **continuous roadmap innovation** stood out, as we are actively rolling out new features that make advanced discovery capabilities more accessible to business users. Reference customers noted positive relationships with our team, citing ease of engagement and accessibility with account contacts. Together, these strengths position Rezolve Ai as a credible partner for enterprises ready to elevate their commerce search and discovery.


## **Multimodal Commerce: A New Era of Discovery**


Today’s shoppers expect more than a search box. Rezolve Ai is ushering in the era of multimodal commerce, empowering discovery through text, image, and conversational interactions. Whether a customer types a query, speaks to their device, or uploads a photo, Rezolve delivers results with context and relevance that feel less like a search engine and more like speaking with the ultimate sales associate.


This is discovery without limits – human-like, intuitive, and designed to guide shoppers seamlessly from intent to purchase. For retailers, this translates into higher engagement, faster conversions, and a more loyal customer base.


## **Looking Ahead: Redefining the Commerce Journey**


The recognition from Forrester is only one step in our larger vision. Rezolve Ai is not simply digitizing yesterday’s commerce models, we are building the AI-native models of growth that will define tomorrow.


We see a future where cart abandonment disappears entirely, not because shoppers change their behavior, but because the cart itself becomes obsolete. Mobile-first, single-action checkout enables purchases to happen the moment intent is formed, creating what we call the instant conversion rate, a new KPI for retail leaders.


We envision commerce moving from “omnichannel” to “every-channel”, where any customer touchpoint, an image, a live stream, a print ad, even a conversation, becomes instantly shoppable. Marketing no longer serves only awareness; it becomes a direct revenue engine.


And we know personalization must evolve. History-based recommendations are no longer enough. Rezolve Ai is pioneering real-time engagement, where AI interprets signals in the moment and responds with proactive, predictive intelligence that feels human, not automated.


All of this rests on a foundation of trust. Consumers adopt frictionless AI quickly, but only if transparency and control are built into every interaction. At Rezolve Ai, we design for consented, user-first engagement, giving shoppers confidence while giving retailers growth.


## **A Contender Today, a Catalyst for Tomorrow**


Being recognized in *The Forrester Wave™* is validation that Rezolve Ai is already delivering measurable value in commerce search and product discovery. But our ambitions extend further: to become the AI sales agent of choice for retailers worldwide. One that can listen, understand, predict, and act across every medium, every moment, and every market.


For enterprises, the future is not just better search. It is the ability to engage every shopper as if they were speaking with your most skilled salesperson – powered by Rezolve Ai.
