# From Search to Sale: Unlocking Retail Growth with Agentic Commerce in Germany

We’re excited to join[Digital Fashion & Retail Days](https://sph-ag.com/en/events-2/dfrd2025/) **** to share how *Agentic Commerce* is transforming the retail experience — helping businesses close the gap between what customers search for and how they buy.


Germany is Europe’s largest retail market, generating more than €650 billion in revenue in 2024 (Statista). Yet profit margins are among the lowest in Europe, averaging just 2–3% (Roland Berger). At the same time, German consumers are some of the most digitally savvy and value‑conscious shoppers in the world. E‑commerce alone accounted for €85 billion in sales in 2023, making Germany the continent’s second‑largest online market (EHI Retail Institute).


**The challenge is clear:** German retailers must deliver efficiency and personalization while modernizing legacy systems under tight economic conditions. That’s where Rezolve Ai’s Agentic Architecture comes in.


## Our intelligent agents can:


- Anticipate intent from the very first search.
- Synchronize ERP, CRM, and inventory seamlessly — even with legacy systems.
- Create personalized recommendations shoppers want (57% of Germans expect this according to Salesforce).
- Enable frictionless checkout and fulfillment at scale.


What’s more, with over 70% of German shoppers researching online before buying in‑store, and 60% expecting consistency across channels (PwC Germany Consumer Insights 2024), the ability to unify experiences across touchpoints has never been more critical.


Agentic Commerce enables retailers to do exactly that — bridging the online‑offline divide, reducing margin pressure with smarter automation, and strengthening customer loyalty in one of the most competitive markets in Europe.


At Digital Fashion & Retail Days, we’ll showcase how Rezolve Ai is helping retailers in Germany unlock growth from search to sale, while building the foundations for the next era of intelligent commerce.
