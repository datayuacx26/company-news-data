# The Agentic Commerce Playbook: Building and Scaling in the Age of AI Agents


AI agents are fundamentally changing discovery, comparison, and purchasing. In this exclusive webinar, Deborah Weinswig (CEO, Coresight Research) and David Ingram (Group CPO, Rezolve Ai) unpack the five-tier adoption roadmap and the STORE framework to help retailers lead this transition.


## Retail Didn't Break.
The Buying Experience Did


The traditional e-commerce model is breaking under the weight of endless scrolling and filtering. As we approach Holiday 2026, the brands that win will be those that embrace conversational, agentic experiences. This session breaks down exactly how to navigate that shift.

Actionable insights on how agentic commerce will impact the upcoming critical holiday shopping season.
