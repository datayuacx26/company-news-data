# The Agentic Commerce Playbook: Your Step-by-Step Guide to Building, Scaling, and Winning in the Age of AI Agents


AI agents are transforming the processes of discovery, comparison, and purchasing, condensing the entire retail journey into a single interaction. Retailers who do not take proactive steps risk becoming invisible to the upcoming generation of shoppers. This playbook from Rezolve Ai and Coresight Research provides you with a clear framework, a technology roadmap, and an ROI model to help you lead this transition rather than simply react to it.


## Agentic Commerce Is Not a Feature. It Is the New Operating Model


AI agents are increasingly taking on the role of intermediaries. They are curating options, making personalized recommendations, and even executing purchases on their own. Retailers that do not engage in these AI-driven ecosystems risk fading into obscurity and losing their relevance, no matter how strong their brand or product range may be.
