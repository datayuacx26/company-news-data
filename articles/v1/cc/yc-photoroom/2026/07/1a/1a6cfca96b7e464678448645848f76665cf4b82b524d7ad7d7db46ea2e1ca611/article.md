## See the difference: Studio HD in action


Studio HD makes your product images look as real and polished as they deserve to be. Whether you sell clothing, home goods, cosmetics, or food, our most advanced AI model helps your photos feel natural, professional, and tailored to your brand.


### Clothing and apparel


Natural draping. True-to-life shadows. Realistic textures. Studio HD handles clothing like it was shot in a professional studio.


*Perfect for: online stores, look books, or campaign visuals.*


### Furniture and home goods


Bring your products to life in warm, realistic settings, without staging a photo shoot. Studio HD places you furniture in believable spaces that complement your style.


*Perfect for handmade goods, home brands, or furniture sellers.*


### Cosmetics and beauty


Studio HD captures reflections, finishes, and fine details, making beauty products look polished and premium without sacrificing realism.


*Perfect for skincare, makeup, or beauty accessories.*


### Food and beverages


Set the scene with Studio HD. Natural lighting and inviting backgrounds make your food products look ready to enjoy.


*Perfect for: cafés, packaged goods, or food delivery brands.*


## How it works


Creating realistic, high-quality product images with Studio HD takes just a few clicks:


1.


**Open the Photoroom app** on mobile or web


2.


Head to the **AI Tools** tab and select **AI Backgrounds**


3. Upload your product photo
4. In the top-right corner, choose **Studio HD** from the dropdown


5. Try different backgrounds - you can create any background using a prompt or search in our backgrounds library


6. Generate and download or share your new image 🎉
