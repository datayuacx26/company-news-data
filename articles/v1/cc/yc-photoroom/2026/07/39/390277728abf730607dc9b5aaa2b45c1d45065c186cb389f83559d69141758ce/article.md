What started as a pandemic pastime has blossomed into a full-fledged business dedicated to spreading joy. Meet Maria Hetrick, the creative mastermind behind[Clay Dazing](https://www.claydazing.com/) , a vibrant brand of polymer clay earrings that are as playful as they are unique.


Maria’s journey began in 2019, when she discovered a polymer clay earring kit from[DewDropInc](https://www.etsy.com/shop/DewDropInc) on[Etsy](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/how-to-boost-sales-on-etsy) while searching for a hands-on hobby to relieve stress. Fueled by her love of experimenting with styles and a desire to create something meaningful, she turned her early creations into a business in 2020. Today, Clay Dazing is all about sharing “little bursts of joy”—rainbows, playful designs, and bright colors that bring smiles not just to Maria’s customers but to Maria herself.


> “When I think about my business, it’s all about spreading happiness. I like to say, ‘from ear to ear’—because literally, I just think they’re so cute and fun. I’ve even had customers tell me they feel happier when they wear my earrings, and that’s really special.”
> ***-Maria Hetrick, Founder, Clay Dazing***


Since launching her business, Maria has combined her marketing prowess with the impact of community building to drive considerable growth online, almost entirely organically. In fact, Maria hasn’t put out a sponsored post for Clay Dazing on social media since 2021. She sells (primarily) online and in-person at a variety of popups. She also has her earrings in stock at local Nashville stores like[GloveBox Kids](https://www.instagram.com/gloveboxkids/?hl=en) , and has both in-store and online collaborations with[Black Box Ice Cream](https://www.instagram.com/theblackboxicecreamtruck/?hl=en) ,[Callie Danielle](http://www.instagram.com/shop.callie.danielle) , and other artists and shops.


In 2023, shortly after becoming a mom for the first time, Maria found herself juggling the chaos of motherhood with the demands of running a business (she actually runs two, Clay Dazing and a marketing agency called[Blue Note Creative](https://bluenotecreative.net/) ). She knew she was going to need to adopt new tools to help her manage it all.


That’s when she discovered what felt like a gift for her business—Photoroom! Before she knew it, Maria had effectively eliminated a large majority of her product photography workflow with far better results than she had been able to produce on her own with tons of time and effort.


This is her journey.
