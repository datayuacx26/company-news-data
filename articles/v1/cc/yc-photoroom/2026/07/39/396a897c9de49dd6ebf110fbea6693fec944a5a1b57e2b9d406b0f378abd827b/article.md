[Smartly](https://www.smartly.io/) builds advertising automation for brands running paid social at scale, handling media buying, creative production, and campaign optimization across channels.


When Smartly's team began building paid social campaigns for Emma, an e‑commerce company specializing in sleep products, they ran into a familiar problem: Emma's product catalog images had backgrounds that weren’t visually appealing, making them unsuitable for the ad creative Smartly’s team wanted to produce.


For campaigns running on Facebook, TikTok, and Pinterest, visuals needed to look professional and consistent. But the raw catalog images made that difficult before any creative work could even begin.


## **The challenge: 30 hours to process images for a catalog**


Smartly's team first tried an internal solution to fix the issue with Emma’s image backgrounds. The approach involved creating target images for each product, then manually removing and replacing the backgrounds. It was functional but slow; each catalog took roughly 30 hours to edit.


> "We were spending around 30 hours per catalog on manual background removal. It was time-consuming, especially when we needed to launch campaigns quickly across multiple channels."
>
>
> — Hamza Abdou, Customer Success Manager at Smartly


This manual image production process also wasn't scalable due to the varying product types, image compositions, and the complexity of automating keywords for different products. The team could manage one catalog at a time, but scaling to multiple campaigns across multiple channels was not realistic without a more automated approach.


## **From a manual process to an automated image processing platform**


After evaluating options, including the internal manual process that had already proven too slow, Smartly selected Photoroom's[Remove Background API](https://www.photoroom.com/api/remove-background) for its processing speed and compatibility with existing workflows. The API connected directly to Emma's existing catalog systems, preserving dynamic elements such as real-time price updates while automatically removing backgrounds.


**The difference in speed changed how quickly the team could move.** Photoroom's API processes images in under 300 milliseconds, which meant editing was no longer the step that delayed campaign launches for Smartly. Instead of spending days preparing images for a single catalog, the team could process an entire product set and move straight into creative production.


That shift in speed further unlocked scale. **Smartly went from advertising one catalog to running seven catalogs at once** , across TikTok, Facebook, and Pinterest. Six distinct campaigns ran simultaneously on the three channels, totaling 18 campaign variations, all with consistent image quality. **The team redirected the hours they had previously spent** on image editing toward creative strategy and campaign optimization.

-
