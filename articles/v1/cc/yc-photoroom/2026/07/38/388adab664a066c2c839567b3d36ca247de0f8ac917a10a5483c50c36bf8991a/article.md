# Photoroom featured on This Week in Startups: Our journey to 300M users

Photoroom co-founder and CEO Matt Rouif recently sat down with Jason Calacanis on[This Week in Startups](https://open.spotify.com/episode/27hWMlFVdKbpsziNBdnzOM) to talk about our journey to 300M users — and what it really takes to build an AI-powered product that creators love.


In the 40-minute conversation, Matt covers:


-


How Photoroom became the #1 photo editing app for e‑commerce sellers


-


How we grew from a scrappy startup to $50M+ in revenue


-


Lessons in product-led growth (from eBay sellers to DoorDash & Amazon)


-


The future of personalized ads, content, and design


Whether you’re scaling an AI product or just curious how we think about growth at Photoroom, this is a conversation worth tuning into.
