# 1. Stop losing sales to bad photos


We[surveyed 1,356 sellers earlier this year](https://www.photoroom.com/industry-trends/product-photography-cost-for-smbs) and found that **59% had lost sales because of poor product photos.** Not because their products were bad. Because their images did not do the products justice.


In our interviews, sellers described the same frustration in their own words: they were doing their photos themselves, and they knew it showed. Lighting was off. The background did not look professional. The garment had a crease that a reshoot would cost half a day to fix.


This year, we closed that gap.


#### Photoroom Product Fixer: correct AI-generated images without spending credits


The[Product Fixer](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/product-fixer) corrects AI-generated images using your original photo as reference, at zero AI credits. It is the safety net that every seller needs to ensure high product fidelity. When an AI-generated image is not quite right, one brush stroke corrects it by comparing the area selected directly against your original product photo.


In short, you are never stuck with a result you cannot fix, nor do you have to burn through credits to correct inaccuracies. In Batch, you can apply it across your whole session, hundreds of images at a time, without switching tools.


#### The new 4k standard: superior image quality across all core tools


**4K quality is now the standard across every major tool.** Photoroom is the only AI product photo editor to deliver 4K quality exports across all seven major tools in a single platform.


4K and 2K quality tiers have been rolled out across[Edit with AI](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/edit-with-ai) ,[Recolor](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/recolor) ,[Flat Lay](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/flat-lay) ,[Product Beautifier](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/product-beautifier) ,[Product Staging](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/product-staging) ,[Ghost Mannequin](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/ghost-mannequin) , and[AI Model](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/virtual-model) .


-


Standard (1K) for everyday listings.


-


Advanced (2K) for sellers who want to stand out.


-


Premium (4K) for Ultra users who need their products to look flawless on any screen. Sharper details, truer colors, better texture preservation. The images that used to require a professional studio are now one click away.


#### **Listing Score for Shopify** : instant insight into the strength of your listings


The Listing Score[for Shopify](https://www.photoroom.com/integrations/shopify) makes the gap between a strong listing and a weak one visible. Every product in your Shopify catalog gets a rating and a progress indicator. Instead of guessing which products are holding back your store, you can see exactly what needs fixing and act on it immediately.


#### AI Ironing: Unwrinkle garments automatically with one click


Photoroom’s new[AI Ironing](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/remove-wrinkles-clothes) feature means a crease never costs you a sale again. Simply upload a wrinkled garment and Photoroom makes it look like it just came off a professional steamer. No reshoot. No delay.


With our batch processing feature, you can unwrinkle hundreds of images at once, in addition to applying other edits like background removal.


#### The Photoroom Enterprise Guarantee: If the output isn’t right, you don’t pay


The[Enterprise Guarantee](https://www.photoroom.com/api/photoroom-api-guarantee) is the first contractual AI visual fidelity guarantee in the industry, built for brands that cannot afford inaccurate images at scale. Enterprise customers set their quality criteria upfront and pay only for outputs that pass.


If an image has altered colors, missing details, or distorted shapes, Photoroom fixes or regenerates it until it meets the agreed standard. No review process after the fact, no production budget wasted on images that miss the mark. And for app users, they also get automatic refunds for failed generations for many AI tools in Photoroom.
