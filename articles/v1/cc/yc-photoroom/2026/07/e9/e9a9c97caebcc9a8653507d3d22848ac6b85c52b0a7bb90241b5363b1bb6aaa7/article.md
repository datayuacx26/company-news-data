Born from the skate culture of Los Angeles and shaped by Italian fashion sensibilities,[Palm Angels](https://www.palmangels.com/) sits at the intersection of streetwear and luxury. Known for its bold graphics and distinctive silhouettes, the brand’s rebellious West Coast aesthetic is instantly recognizable across campaigns, lookbooks, and e‑commerce.


As a brand deeply invested in sports and culture, the 2026 Winter Olympics in Palm Angels’ hometown, Milan, presented a perfect moment to create a limited-edition[Milano Cortina capsule](https://www.palmangels.com/en-it/broken-bear-capsule-cortina) available exclusively in Italy. Inspired by the duality of the city of Milan and mountains of Cortina, the limited-edition capsule features Palm Angels’ iconic Broken Bear graphic reimagined through alpine codes and urban attitude on a t-shirt and a hoodie. The Broken Bear wears a Palm Angels puffer from the current seasonal collection and a scarf emblazoned with “Cortina” to memorialize the 2026 Winter Olympics.


With a short timeline to launch, the team faced a familiar challenge: how to produce high-end PDP imagery with models, styling, and brand consistency without organizing a full-scale production.


Instead of assembling a traditional crew, Palm Angels turned to Photoroom to generate AI-powered visuals using their existing products and brand guidelines. This enabled the team to go from assets to live images in just four days without requiring any photoshoot.


## A limited-edition drop on a tight timeline


For a luxury brand, even simple PDP imagery typically requires:


-


Casting models


-


Hiring stylists


-


Renting a studio


-


Assembling a photography crew


-


Lighting setup


-


Post-production retouching


With Milano-Cortina approaching, Palm Angels required a time and capacity-saving approach. As the team explains:


> "We needed a quicker, more efficient approach to produce high-quality imagery while still capturing the essence of the collection.” - Marketing Manager, Palm Angels


By using Photoroom, the team bypassed the time, cost, and logistics of a full production. Leveraging only their product files and creative guidelines, they were able to generate high-quality, on-brand imagery in just a few days, with no full production crew needed.


> "We were able to move from product assets to final live images very quickly. The entire process took just three to four days.” - Marketing Manager, Palm Angels


## Maintaining Palm Angels’ signature visual identity


Introducing AI naturally raised questions: would the models look natural? Could it match the quality of a traditional shoot? With Photoroom, the answer exceeded expectations.


> "We were confident Photoroom could meet Palm Angels’ visual standards because of their expertise in high-quality, product-focused imagery and their ability to deliver consistent, clean, and detailed visuals.” - Marketing Manager, Palm Angels


By applying detailed visual guidelines, clear creative standards, and reviewing outputs closely, Photoroom’s[Virtual model](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/virtual-model) tool was able to capture accurate proportions, natural posture, and styling that kept the garments front and center. The result was luxury-level imagery produced faster and more efficiently than traditional methods.


> “Despite being AI-generated, the models captured accurate proportions, posture, and styling, ensuring the garments remained the focal point of every image.” - Marketing Manager, Palm Angels


This experience reinforced a key principle for Palm Angels: clear creative standards and close collaboration are essential when integrating AI into luxury production workflows.


## The impact: agility without compromising luxury standards


The Milano-Cortina capsule demonstrated how AI can support, not replace, traditional creative production. By streamlining the creation of e‑commerce imagery, Palm Angels was able to launch on time while maintaining the visual standards expected of a luxury brand.


> “While AI doesn’t replace the creativity and expertise of traditional methods, AI can complement them, helping brands meet tight timelines and adapt quickly without compromising their aesthetic standards.” - Marketing Manager, Palm Angels
