# Comparing flat lay vs. white backgrounds for e‑commerce product photos

Apparel sellers need to balance improving click-through rates with avoiding marketplace suppression. Since most platforms allow multiple photos per listing, you can use a variety of shot types to satisfy main-image requirements and display clothing in lifelike settings that appeal to potential buyers.


For instance, a[flat lay image](https://www.photoroom.com/glossary/flat-lay-photography) displays a garment on a level surface, shooting straight down to show styling, drape, and coordination. White background photos isolate an item on a pure white background. Both are common photo staging choices that can be used separately or in combination, depending on[marketplace compliance](https://www.photoroom.com/industry/marketplaces) needs and product context.


In this article, we’ll explain how to use flat lay and white background product photos in your e‑commerce listings. We’ll also show how you can use Photoroom to create and scale both formats, without an expensive studio or manual editing bottlenecks.

### Marketplace listings for Amazon, Walmart, and Google Shopping


Marketplaces usually enforce rules to keep search results uniform, but those requirements vary in scope and flexibility. On the stricter side,[Amazon](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1881) and[Walmart](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Item%20setup/Item%20content,%20imagery,%20and%20media/Product-detail-page:-Image-guidelines-&-requirements) both require pure white backgrounds (RGB 255, 255, 255) for main images, and they will pull all e‑commerce listings that fall short.


[Google Shopping](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324350?hl=en#Image_guidelines) is a bit less restrictive. It recommends white or transparent neutral backgrounds as a best practice for primary images, but it doesn’t auto-suppress listings based on background color.


### Shopify, Etsy, and other DTC storefronts


On DTC storefronts, it’s important for each product to tell a compelling story. Styled flat lay product photography shines here because it shows lifestyle context, garment drape, and full-outfit styling.


Consumers on Etsy and Shopify are hunting for inspiration, so a folded sweater styled next to coordinating accessories helps them picture the complete look. To drive engagement and conversions while keeping production streamlined, you can create stylized, on-brand images[using Photoroom’s Flat Lay tool](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/flat-lay-photography) .
