## **What is image enhancement?**


[Image enhancement](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/B978012821049900006X) is about improving the overall quality and appearance of an image without altering the essence of what it captures. Unlike photo manipulation, which involves changing a picture’s appearance in dramatic ways, enhancement focuses on improving the image as it is. This means tweaking brightness, contrast, sharpness, or color balance to make the photo more appealing. If you’re creating visuals for a business, enhancing an image helps you tell better stories, attract customers, sell more products, and build brand image over time.
