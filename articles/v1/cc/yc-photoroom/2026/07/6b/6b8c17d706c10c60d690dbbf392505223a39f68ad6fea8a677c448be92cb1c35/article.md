# AI Images: a visual toolkit for businesses

Small businesses often face the challenge of high design expenses, time constraints and creative roadblocks. Photoroom’s AI Images is here to help. Designed to empower small businesses with easy-to-use, powerful design tools to elevate their branding, AI Images is a visual toolkit that leverages AI to create professional assets from scratch in just seconds.


Unlike other tools that rely on pre-made templates, AI Images allows businesses to realize their vision by transforming simple prompts into unique, professional designs. Whether it’s crafting logos, eye-catching Instagram stories, or generating engaging text, AI Images makes professional branding accessible, efficient, and endlessly creative.


## **Who is it for?**


AI Images is designed for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) seeking to boost their creativity, save time and resources, and build a strong, cohesive brand.


Currently available on iOS and on the web app, AI Images is coming soon to Android.


It is perfect for teams who need a centralized platform to create, access, and share on-brand visual assets with ease. Whether it’s crafting a new logo, brainstorming product packaging ideas, or designing engaging social media visuals, AI Images ensures everyone in the team stays aligned.


For seamless collaboration, you can create a[Photoroom Team](https://www.photoroom.com/teams) to make your business asset creation more efficient than ever.


## **What can I create with AI Images for my business?**


### **1. Logo**


Creating a logo for your business has never been easier. With the logo maker, you can instantly generate a professional logo that elevates your brand—no design experience required. Simply enter a one-sentence prompt about your business, and AI will deliver a range of logos in seconds. Customize your favorite design by selecting different sizes and unique styles to perfectly align with your vision.


Once your logo is ready,[add it to your brand kit](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/how-to-create-consistent-branding-with-photoroom-brand-kit) on Photoroom, along with your brand colors, fonts, and other assets, ensuring your team has everything in one place for consistent, on-brand visuals. It’s the ultimate tool for building your brand effortlessly and professionally.


### **2. Backgrounds**


Whether you’re creating for social media, product showcases, or promotional content, the background maker is your go-to tool for professional, polished visuals. Transform your visuals with stunning backgrounds, generated instantly with just a few words. Simply enter a keyword like "beach" or "Christmas," and in seconds, you'll have a high-quality background tailored to your needs.[Save to your brand kit](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/how-to-create-consistent-branding-with-photoroom-brand-kit) to reuse later or further refine them using Photoroom's editor.


You may also be interested in **[AI Backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/instant-backgrounds)** , which blends your product into the perfect background setting.


### **3. Product photography**


Create professional product shots in seconds with the product photography maker. Simply type in a word or description of the product image you want to create, and watch as AI brings your vision to life. Whether you're seeking inspiration, designing a product prototype, or crafting promotional materials for unique product types, this tool is a game-changer.


### **4. Product packaging**


Spark creativity for your product packaging with ease. Generate diverse packaging concepts tailored to your product by simply describing your brand and product.


This tool is perfect for getting inspiration and exploring design ideas that align with your brand identity. Whether you're brainstorming or finalizing concepts, it’s a great way to jumpstart your packaging journey before creating personalized designs that customers will recognize and connect with.


### **5. Lifestyle photography**


Elevate your brand with captivating lifestyle images that showcase your products in real-life settings. Perfect for promoting your business on your website, marketplaces, and social media, these images help create a unique vibe and resonate with your target audience. Whether you're adding flair to online listings on platforms like Etsy or enhancing your website with authentic visuals, lifestyle photography brings your products to life, making your brand more relatable and memorable.


### **6. Instagram story**


Boost your Instagram game with stunning visuals designed to captivate your audience. The Instagram Story maker lets you promote your products or services with eye-catching designs that drive engagement and keep your followers hooked. Effortlessly create, engage, and grow your presence with professional, scroll-stopping stories.


Want to extend your reach beyond Instagram? Use the **[Resize](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/image-resizer)** tool to seamlessly repurpose these visuals for other platforms, ensuring your brand shines everywhere.


### **7. Text**


The text maker transforms any text into an eye-catching sticker, making it an invaluable tool for elevating your brand’s visual appeal.


By turning words into bold, engaging visuals, it helps enhance your brand personality, making your content more memorable and impactful. Perfect for emphasizing critical details such as discounts, product launches, or upcoming events, it can be used on social media, in marketing campaigns, or promotional materials as a powerful way to boost engagement and leave a lasting impression on your audience.


### **8. Icons**


The icons maker is a versatile tool that transforms any concept into a clear and engaging icon, making it perfect for enhancing communication across various platforms. Icons created with this tool are especially effective for transcending language barriers, allowing businesses to communicate effortlessly with international customers through universally recognizable visuals.


These attention-grabbing icons are easy to understand and can be used consistently across product listings, websites, emails, social media, and more. By incorporating icons into your branding strategy, you ensure a cohesive, professional look while effectively conveying key messages to diverse audiences.


### **9. Poster**


The poster maker allows you to create stunning posters instantly, making it an essential tool for businesses looking to elevate their visual marketing. Whether you’re promoting an upcoming event, advertising specials at a restaurant, or showcasing seasonal promotions, this tool helps you design eye-catching posters with ease. With professional-quality results in seconds, your business and offerings stand out instantly.


### **10. Gift cards**


Create customized gift cards tailored to your customers. Add recipient names, specific card values, and even optional redeem codes for a seamless gifting experience. Perfect for promoting your business, these personalized gift cards help you connect with your customers on a deeper level, strengthening relationships and enhancing brand loyalty.


### **11. Inspirational quotes**


Elevate your brand with stunning images featuring inspirational quotes that resonate deeply with your audience. Perfect for creating impactful social media content, these visuals help you connect with your customers on a more personal level, fostering engagement and loyalty. Inspire, motivate, and leave a lasting impression with every post.
