## What to look for when choosing an AI image generator


While many AI image generators can whip up creative imagery, most of them don’t have the capabilities that small businesses or enterprise teams require to[create efficient workflows](https://www.photoroom.com/customer-stories/wolt) .


Instead of getting overwhelmed, look for an AI image generation tool that meets the following criteria.


### Custom outputs


If you have to upload every image you edit to a new platform to resize and export it, you’ll spend more time than you save. Look for an AI image generation tool that allows you to tailor your images by size, resolution, and file type.


### Batch editing


[Batch editing](https://www.photoroom.com/batch) lets you generate and edit images at scale, elevating your AI image generation tool from a creative assistant to a business partner.


### Consistency


Some AI models are prone to hallucinating—they generate information that isn’t accurate, just because the tool thinks it fits your use case. But inconsistent imagery erodes customer trust. Look for a model that preserves your product's integrity across outputs.


### Commercial usage rights


AI image generation tools draw from an existing image database to generate new pixels. A tool with clear commercial usage rights helps you avoid putting yourself or your business at legal risk.


The best AI generators can all make pretty pictures. But e‑commerce businesses looking to scale need features that drive real results.

An AI image generator is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to create and modify images from text-based descriptions (prompts). An AI image generation tool can generate new scenes for creative reference, place a model in new surroundings, edit the color of an existing product, and much more. Using an AI image generation tool is often less expensive and more efficient than manual image generation and editing.
