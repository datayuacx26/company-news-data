When Hennessy released a limited edition VS bottle to mark Hip Hop's 50th anniversary, the brand wanted more than a product launch. They wanted fans to feel like part of the celebration. Laundry Service, Hennessy's partner agency, brought in[Solarflare Studio](https://solarflarestudio.co.uk/) , a creative technology agency based in London, to make that happen.


Solarflare's concept was called "Rep Yours": a selfie-based portrait generator that let fans pick their favorite Hip Hop era and coast, then see themselves transported into a custom visual from that moment in the culture's history. The portraits were designed to be shared instantly on social media, extending the campaign's reach beyond the room.


The idea was ambitious. But making it work at a live event, where guests are standing in front of a screen expecting an instant result, introduced a problem that most digital campaigns never have to solve.


## **Creating instant portraits at a live event with no room for error**


At a live brand activation, the margin for failure is zero. A guest takes a selfie, and the system needs to remove the background, composite the portrait into a themed template, and return a finished image, all within seconds. If the cutout is rough, the portrait looks wrong. If the system is slow, the line backs up. Either way, the experience falls apart in front of the person waiting for it.


**Solarflare needed a background removal solution that could[process real-time selfies](https://www.photoroom.com/use-cases/marketing-campaigns)** with enough accuracy to produce shareable, high-quality portraits on the spot. The tool also had to run reliably across multiple live events in different cities, with no downtime during the campaign.


## **The API solution that worked on the first try**


Solarflare assessed multiple background removal solutions before choosing Photoroom's[Background Remover API](https://www.photoroom.com/api/remove-background) . **The deciding factor was trust:** the team had seen the technology used across similar live experiences and validated it as a tried-and-tested solution before committing.


**Here's how the selfie generator worked after Solarflare integrated the Photoroom API:**


1.


A fan stepped up to the activation, took or uploaded a selfie, and the system sent the image to Photoroom's API.


2.


The API removed the background in real time and placed the cutout into one of several era-themed Hip Hop portrait templates.


3.


The fan then received the finished image within seconds, ready to download and share on social media.


This process ran in multiple cities where Hennessy hosted events for their anniversary.
