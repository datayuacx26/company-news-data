# What's new in product: October 2023

Get ready as Photoroom unveils fang-tastic product updates this October! In this month’s product recap, you'll discover new releases for iOS, Android, and Web, as well as a sneak peek of upcoming product improvements to help you create hauntingly good product photography.


## New Updates


### **Switch backgrounds easily in Photoroom**


It's now easier than ever to switch to different types of backgrounds in Photoroom for iOS. You can easily change your background to a new color or image or create one from scratch in[Instant Backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/instant-backgrounds) .


**For** : Free and paid users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/8155196-replace-a-background)


###


Sales-ready Halloween and Black Friday templates


Try our fresh set of seasonal templates to prepare your brand for promotions this holiday season, from Halloween to Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Check it out in the app!


**For** : Free and paid users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS, Android and Web


[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/3515874-how-to-get-started-with-photoroom-templates)


## Coming soon


### Using image as background inspiration on mobile


We recently launched a new feature on Photoroom Web that allows you to upload an image of your choice or search for an image from our Unsplash library as inspiration for Instant Backgrounds. Soon, mobile users will also have this option under custom backgrounds (the pencil icon) within Instant Backgrounds. With this technology, our AI will generate similar backgrounds tailored even more to your preferences.


**For** : Pro and Business users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS and Android


[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/7835526-create-your-own-instant-backgrounds-with-custom-prompts)


### Personalized profile picture


You should soon be able to add a profile picture and display your initials on the Photoroom app. This is especially handy for users who collaborate with other teammates on Photoroom.


**For** : All users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS, Android and Web
