# What's new in product: March 2024

With spring around the corner, Photoroom is excited to announce new offerings and product updates to nurture your business's growth. From new ways to collaborate with your team seamlessly to enhancing our current user experiences, read on to find out what’s blooming in our latest release! 🌸


## New Updates


### Share your Pro subscription with 2 other people for free


You can now invite 2 additional people to your Pro Team for free. Whether they are team members, freelancers or assistants who want to be included in the design process, or family members eager to co-edit cute photos of your children or pets, you can easily facilitate seamless collaboration at no extra cost. After all, sharing is caring!


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS, Android and web


**For** : All Pro users


➡[Learn more and create your new team.](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/8989869-creating-a-pro-team-on-mobile)


### Add comment and reactions to shared designs


On the Web and iOS versions of Photoroom, you can now add comments or emojis to show your reactions to your teammates’ design. It’s even easier to collaborate, approve designs and show some appreciation to each other! Coming soon to Android.


**Platform** : Photoroom for the Web and iOS


**For** : All users in a Team


➡[See how it works.](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/9041972-comment-on-team-designs-and-templates)


### Stay in the loop with Activity Feed


For users collaborating within a team, you can now easily see all the activity of your team under the new Activity Feed. Never miss any updates when a new member joins your team and your team creates or edits a design. This is first available on the Web and will is coming soon to mobile.


**Platform** : Photoroom for Web


**For** : All users in a team


➡[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/9042394-activity-feed)


### Regenerate AI background automatically when your object moves


In the past, if you moved your object in front of an AI Background, you might have seen an outline of your object in the old background and would have to manually click on the background again to regenerate the background. Now we have made this adjustment automatic so you can easily move objects around without worrying about the changes in the background. This is first available on iOS and is coming soon to Android.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


**For** : All Pro users


### Easier ways to create a collage for multiple products


We have added new “Collage” templates where you can easily place multiple products within the same image to use as flyers and other marketing material for your brand.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS, Android and Web


**For** : All users (some templates are Pro only)


## Coming soon


### More creative control with AI Fill


Soon, you can customize an area of your image with an AI prompt to add or correct the details of your image. It’s never easier to control and fine-tune your image to match your vision.


**For** : Pro and Business users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


### Enhance your images with AI Upscale


If you want higher-resolution photos for your website or just want to restore the details of old photographs, our upcoming AI Upscale tool can help. Stay tuned!


**For** : Pro and Business users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS
