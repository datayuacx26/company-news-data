# 5 ways to use AI product photography for the holidays

Holiday season is the busiest and most competitive time of year for e‑commerce. Shoppers compare similar products, often at similar price points and what wins the click isn’t always copy or discount.


**It’s the product photo.**


The real challenge isn’t taking *a* good picture:
it’s producing **enough** high-quality visuals for every marketplace, ad format, social channel and promo **fast enough to keep up.**


That’s where[AI product photography](https://www.photoroom.com/ai-product-photography) comes in. Instead of starting from scratch for every holiday campaign, you can turn a single product shot into multiple seasonal assets in minutes without reshooting, restaging, or blocking design resources.


## **5 practical ways to use AI product photography for holiday sales**


AI product photography lets brands scale holiday visuals without reshoots: one image becomes many, formatted, festive, and ready to sell.


### **1. Reuse existing photos by removing and replacing backgrounds**


If you already have a product catalog, your fastest holiday win is to **recycle those images** instead of organizing new photoshoots.


With an AI background remover, you can:


-


Cut out your product from its original setting


-


Place it on a clean, consistent background (e.g., white for marketplaces)


-


Drop it into different holiday scenes, colors, or layouts


In Photoroom, the **[Background Remover](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/background-remover)** automatically detects the subject and removes the background in seconds. Paired with **[Batch Editing](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/batch-mode)**[,](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/batch-mode) you can apply the same change to hundreds of product photos at once: deal if you have a large catalog or multiple sellers to support.


**Why this helps during the holidays**


-


You don’t have to re-shoot products to make them “holiday-ready”


-


You keep the focus on the product while updating the visual look


-


You achieve a consistent style across your store, even if original photos came from different sources


### **2. Add seasonal scenes and backgrounds without redoing your shoots**


Holiday visuals don’t have to mean full lifestyle photoshoots with sets, props, and models.


Using **AI-generated backgrounds and scenes** , you can:


-


Turn a plain product shot into a cozy holiday setting (e.g., next to a fireplace, under a tree, on a festive table)


-


Create multiple visual themes (minimalist red + white, luxury gold, rustic, playful, etc.) for different audiences


-


Test which style performs best in ads or on your PDPs


In Photoroom, you can use **[AI Backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/instant-backgrounds)** to generate seasonal scenes or choose from templates designed for holiday campaigns. You control the tone while the AI handles the composition.
