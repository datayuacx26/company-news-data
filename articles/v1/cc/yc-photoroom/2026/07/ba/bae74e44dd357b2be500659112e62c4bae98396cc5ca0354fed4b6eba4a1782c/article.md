Alex Mahl didn't set out to become a reseller when she started selling her own clothes 10 years ago. At 18, she was simply helping her parents move and needed to clear out her closet. But what began as a closet clean-out quickly evolved into a side business.


"I started helping other people local to me sell their own clothing as well," Alex recalls. During the COVID-19 pandemic period, she’d go to clients' homes and help them list their items on Poshmark for an hourly fee.


But Alex, who works full-time as an office manager and at a law firm’s finance department, knew she needed a more sustainable business model. "It was getting very overwhelming, and I wasn't really able to focus on my own closet anymore,” shares Alex. “I was like, okay, I'm helping all these people make all this money. Yes, I'm making an hourly fee, but what can I do to make it better for myself as well?"


## **From personal decluttering to a consignment business**


At first, Alex stopped her one-to-one listing offer. She then hired people across New York to thrift clothing items for her business, managed the listing process on her shop, and hosted livestream sales shows on Poshmark to build her brand presence.


But the real turning point came after her[New York Times feature](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/05/10/business/live-shopping-hosts.html) story caught the attention of a celebrity with 1.2 million followers, who contacted Alex to sell her items on consignment. Two weeks later, Alex picked up 14 garbage bags of designer clothing, and her business model changed fully to consignment.


As a consignee, she now receives items from individuals and only pays them after the item sells. This advantage wasn’t possible in the previous model, which required her to pay thrifters upfront even if shoppers didn’t end up buying the item.


> **“Working with thrifters wasn't terrible, but I wasn't seeing as much profit as I wanted to because I was constantly paying off my credit card.”**
>
>
> ***— Alexandra Mahl, Founder, APM FINDS, LLC.***


Today, Alex runs a successful[Poshmark shop](https://poshmark.com/closet/alexpmahl/about-me) , where she has nearly 8,000 active listings. She’s been featured in[Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/poshmark-seller-side-hustle-create-listings-for-others-2023-5) , appeared in a Poshmark TV commercial, and even spoke on a[Photoroom panel at PoshFest](https://www.instagram.com/p/DOe7QrWDwbg/?img_index=4) .


With this growth and a steady stream of consignment clients, she began exploring ways to improve her products’ presentation online. But she faced a product image problem that limited her strategy.


## **The photography problem that was limiting growth**


As a[Poshmark ambassador](https://blog.poshmark.com/2017/10/11/posh-tip-how-to-become-a-posh-ambassador/) and savvy e‑commerce entrepreneur, **Alex knew that product visuals with clean backgrounds help boost online sales. And yet she couldn't find the perfect backdrops.**


> **“Think of Poshmark store profiles like dating apps. If your first photo isn’t aesthetically pleasing, no one's going to click on it. So, I tried different scenes in my house to capture products in the best backdrops. But no matter what I did, whether it was a rug, the back of my door, all my sheets, the image background just didn't look clean.”**
>
>
> ***— Alexandra Mahl, Founder, APM FINDS, LLC.***


Nothing gave her the professional, polished look she needed. And Alex needed every advantage she could get in the competitive resale market, especially when reselling items from popular brands like Lululemon. "I thought, well, Lululemon is a saturated market. How am I going to make my item sell over someone else's?" shares Alex.


Between her full-time job and listing 20-30 items per day, plus packing 10-20 packages every night, **Alex needed a solution that could transform her basic iPhone photos into professional-looking product images without adding hours to her already packed workload** .


And so she went searching.


## **Alex found her visual partner in Photoroom**


She explored Google for a solution, looking for a platform that could remove backgrounds and replace them with cleaner ones, like white backgrounds or marble flooring. That's how she discovered Photoroom.


> **“I’m serious about my business. So, once I found Photoroom, I knew it was the right solution for me and paid for it instantly. I didn't even do a free trial because I was already sold.”**
>
>
> ***— Alexandra Mahl, Founder, APM FINDS, LLC.***


Today, Alex uses Photoroom to edit images for 20-30 items daily.[Her workflow](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DQJlqfJAOPz/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=MzRlODBiNWFlZA%3D%3D) takes just three minutes for 10 listings:


1.


She starts by snapping 10 products and listing them on Poshmark.


2.


Next, she opens the[Photoroom app](https://app.adjust.com/13mku062?campaign=alexmahl) , selects the[Batch feature](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/batch-mode) , uploads all first images of the items she listed, waits a few seconds for the tool to automatically[remove the image backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/background-remover) and[add white backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/white-background) , and saves the edited photos in her camera roll.


3.


Finally, she returns to her Poshmark app and changes the first picture of each listing, replacing the old images with the newly edited ones.
