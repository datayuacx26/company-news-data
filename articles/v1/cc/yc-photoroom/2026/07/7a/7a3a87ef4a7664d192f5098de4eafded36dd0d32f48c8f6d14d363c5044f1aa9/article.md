## **How do AI fashion model generators work?**


The best AI fashion model generators prioritize garment accuracy, realistic fabric rendering, and consistent output quality across your entire catalog. Here's the typical process:


**1. Upload your garment photo**


Most tools accept various input formats like flat lays, ghost mannequin images, or photos of garments being worn.


**2. The AI analyzes the garment**


It reads fabric texture, color, pattern, fit, and construction.


**3. The model is generated wearing your garment**


The system recreates accurate draping, folds, and proportions.


**4. Customize the model and scene**


Adjust body type, skin tone, age, background, lighting, and pose.


AI fashion model generators work by analyzing garment details and recreating them on virtual models. Their success depends on how accurately the tool preserves fabric drape, color fidelity, and fit without distortion.
