### **Empowering workwear for women**


Founded by Adrienne Kronovet straight out of college,[Ameliora](https://ameliora.com/) is a US-based women’s clothing brand built around timeless button-up shirts made from premium performance fabrics.


Adrienne always knew she wanted to go into fashion and got the idea for the brand in her senior year of college, where she spent a year working on a business plan and launched right after graduation.


Eight years on, Ameliora has grown from a limited collection into a full wardrobe, now stocked in over 100 stores across the US, with sights on expanding internationally.


With five core shirt silhouettes offered in up to 15 colors each, the brand has built a loyal customer base that keeps asking for more. Adrienne is most proud of the impact Ameliora has on its customers:


> “I hear from women re-entering the workforce after years of absence that our clothes give them the confidence to do so. Hearing that you’re able to make an impact in somebody’s life is one of my greatest accomplishments.”
> - Adrienne Kronovet, Founder, Ameliora


### **No mannequin shots, no press coverage**


Before discovering Photoroom, creating professional product imagery was both costly and complicated. Ghost mannequin photography alone cost $40–$60 per image and required specialized equipment available at only a handful of studios in the US. With front and back shots billed separately, a single product could cost up to $120 to photograph, not including retouching and color change fees or close-up shots.


> “For a small business like myself, this kind of photography was extremely cost-prohibitive, so we really wouldn’t do it that often.”
> - Adrienne Kronovet, Founder, Ameliora


Beyond cost, studio shoots brought their own challenges. Coordinating photographers, studios, models, hair and makeup, outfits, and usage rights was overwhelming, all while trying to maximize a shoot that could cost several thousand dollars.


“Normally, we’d work with two models at a time,” Adrienne says, “so if one model is shooting, the other’s changing, just to get in as many pictures and looks as we can.”


And the work didn’t stop once the shoot wrapped: reviewing thousands of raw images, making selects, and providing granular back-and-forth feedback to the photographer, such as, “Can you crop this?” added even more time and complexity.


Ghost mannequin and product photography are essential for an emerging fashion brand like Ameliora. Beyond elevating product pages and line sheets, it’s critical in the US market, where press outlets often require ghost mannequin imagery upfront. Without it, press coverage is difficult, if not impossible, to secure.

> “Something that used to take me three months, I can now do in two minutes. Every time I use it, I’m amazed by what Photoroom is able to create.”

**Virtual model**


Adrienne also uses Photoroom’s[Virtual Model](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/virtual-model) tool to visualize how designs will look on a body during the production process, helping her streamline design decisions before a single garment reaches the studio.


### Photoroom produces AI-powered color variants in minutes


More recently, Adrienne has been working closely with the Photoroom team on our[Conversion Lab](https://www.photoroom.com/conversion-lab) , a co-building partnership with e‑commerce sellers to develop and improve our product photography solutions, which she describes as “an incredible experience.”


> “As a retailer on Shopify, I have a strong understanding of the type of photography Shopify needs. And as a brand owner, I’m able to give guidance on the tools that would genuinely make my life easier, and then work with the team to build them.”
> - Adrienne Kronovet, Founder, Ameliora


One of the tools we developed together is the new[recolor](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/recolor) feature, an instrumental tool for Adrienne that lets her transform a single product image into multiple color SKUs using conversational AI. Like many brands, she primarily works with one silhouette offered in multiple colors (for instance, 8 for her top-selling Dawn shirt and 14 for the Boyfriend shirt).


Previously, reshoots for new colors were prohibitively expensive, and manual Photoshop edits were time-consuming and prone to errors, such as accidentally changing buttons or flattening textures, which made fabrics look artificial.


Now, Photoroom’s Recolor tool preserves fabric details, folds, stitching, and natural shading, keeping clothing images realistic and consistent across her catalog.


> “Instead of uploading an entirely new image, I can simply say, ‘change it to black,’ ‘change it to white,’ or ‘change it to grey.’ It’s been an incredible feature and extremely useful, and it’s been really fun to see it come together.”
> - Adrienne Kronovet, Founder, Ameliora


By capturing the full color spectrum of each garment from the accurate hex color codes onto the ghost mannequin photos, Adrienne has “an increase in customers buying more colors rather than just the basic neutrals.”


The results go beyond speed: the accurate color representation encourages more varied purchases, with customers now confidently choosing bolder shades beyond neutrals, boosting overall sales variety by 30%.
