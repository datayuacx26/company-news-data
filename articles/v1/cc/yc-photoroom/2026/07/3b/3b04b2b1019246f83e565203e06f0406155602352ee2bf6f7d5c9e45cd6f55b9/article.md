# What's new in product: August 2024

As we soak up the last rays of summer, we're thrilled to bring you some new product improvements for free users, Pro users, and iOS users. Dive in and make your summer creations shine brighter than ever!


## New Updates


### No more watermark for free users


We're excited to bring even more value to our free users—starting now, free users will enjoy a cleaner look in their images as the Photoroom watermark in the bottom right corner is no longer visible.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS, Android and the web


**For** : free users


### Auto-regenerate your AI backgrounds on the web app


If you move your object in front of an AI Background on the web app, a new background with very minimum changes will automatically regenerate to match the object's new position, saving you the hassle of manually refreshing it.


**Platform** : Photoroom for the web


**For** : Pro users


### Enhance your images with AI Upscale


If you need[higher resolution photos](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/ai-upscale) for your website or just want to restore details of old photographs, our AI Upscale tool can help. Unblur your image with “enhance”, add details with “transform” or be creative with “reimagine” all within the same tool. It should now be available to all iOS users with app version 6.0.7 and above.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


**For** : Pro users


### **Better AI Images**


We have made more improvements to AI Images to deliver results that more precisely align with your prompt, especially for images containing text. Give it a try and stay tuned for more upcoming improvements for this feature!


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


**For** : Pro users


### More intuitive AI Backgrounds (beta)


AI Backgrounds now feature an enhanced user experience on iOS, offering easier ways to customize prompts and greater control over how closely your image resembles your inspiration image.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


**For** : Pro users


## Coming soon


### Batch with color background on the web app


Web app users will soon be able to batch edit photos with color backgrounds, effortlessly applying a specific color palette to multiple images in just seconds.


**Platform** : Photoroom for the web


**For** : Pro users


### Make your background more realistic (beta)


iOS users will soon have the option to click a "Make Realistic" button, instantly transforming flat color or image backgrounds into more lifelike scenes with AI Backgrounds.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


**For** : Pro users
