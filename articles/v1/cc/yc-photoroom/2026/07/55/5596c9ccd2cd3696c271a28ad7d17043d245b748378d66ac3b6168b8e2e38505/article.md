# AI for e‑commerce: Automate workflows and scale product visuals

Starting and growing an e‑commerce business is no small feat. You're constantly juggling the demands of marketing products, supporting customers, keeping inventory up to date, and much more.


The challenge of consistently producing fresh, captivating content that converts browsers into buyers can feel like a never-ending battle, and targeting the right audience at the right time with your marketing campaigns is complex and overwhelming.


Fortunately, AI tools for e‑commerce can help improve some of these processes and free up your time and brain power so you can focus on the most important task of all, growing your business.


With the right artificial intelligence technology, you can automate tedious tasks, personalize customer experiences, and generate content that resonates with your target audience, all while gaining valuable insights into your business's performance.


But who has the time to evaluate and compare different e‑commerce AI tools? We know you’re busy. That’s why we spent hours searching for and comparing AI tools that you can use to boost productivity, creativity, and sales in your e‑commerce business. We used factors like customer ratings, pricing, ease of use, and core features to create this roundup for you.


## What to look for in AI tools for e‑commerce


Before you choose the right e‑commerce AI tools for[your online store](https://www.photoroom.com/use-cases/sell-online) , it's crucial to understand which characteristics or features are most important. Use this checklist as a guide while evaluating AI tools:


✓ Is it user-friendly and does it simplify tasks instead of complicating them?


✓ Is it accessible on both mobile and desktop devices?


✓ Will it enhance creative workflows within your e‑commerce business?


✓ Does it have the capability to boost customer engagement and sales?


✓ Does it provide customer support when needed?


✓ Are you able to provide product feedback to aid in the improvement of its AI tools and features?


✓ Can you easily add team members and train your staff on how to use the AI tool?


✓ Does it offer an API for integration with sales channels like your e‑commerce website, social media, or marketplaces?


✓ Can it scale with your business to prevent the need for switching or migrating to another e‑commerce AI tool in the future?
