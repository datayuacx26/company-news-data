# What's new in product: November 2024

As the holiday season approaches, we’re unwrapping some exciting updates at Photoroom to help you create stunning visual content with ease and style! From crafting consistent brand for your business with our new brand kit to designing personalized cards for loved ones with the new Christmas card maker, we’ve been busy adding features to make your creative projects shine. Stay tuned for the magic ahead with more AI features and enhancements launching next month and let’s make this season your most inspired yet!


## New Updates


### **Create consistent branding with brand kit**


Personalize and apply your brand kit with your brand’s colors, fonts, and logo. By maintaining consistency through a brand kit, businesses can create a cohesive look and feel that strengthens their identity, builds trust, and ensures customers know exactly what to expect, wherever they encounter your brand. You can find brand kit under “your content”.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS and the web; coming soon to Android


**For** : all users


[Learn more](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/how-to-create-consistent-branding-with-photoroom-brand-kit)


### Christmas card maker on AI Images


Have you tried our new AI Images? In addition to creating a logo, product packaging, Instagram story, and other stunning visuals for your business, you can now create a personalized Christmas card in seconds for your loved ones, just in time for the holiday season!


Go to AI Images, then select Christmas cards maker.


**Platform:** Photoroom for iOS and web; coming soon for Android


**For:** Pro users


[Learn more](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/visual-toolkit-for-businesses)


### **Make your image more realistic**


iOS users now have the option to click a "Make Realistic" button, instantly transforming flat color or plain image backgrounds into more lifelike, realistic scenes with AI Backgrounds.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


**For** : all users


### In-app communities on Photoroom


Discover our new Photoroom in-app communities! Whether you're a PhotoGroomer, Depop Seller, Sneakerhead, or simply love connecting with others around your craft, there's a community waiting for you. Connect, collaborate, and grow with other like-minded creators like you.


[Join a community now](https://photoroom.notion.site/Find-your-community-and-create-together-121a0f7f3eee8008aa77faa026b1566f)


**Platform** : Photoroom for the web, Android, and iOS


**For** : all users
