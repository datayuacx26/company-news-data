# What's new in product: October 2024

Get ready, SMBs and ecommerce sellers: this month we've launched new and improved tools designed to streamline your workflow and boost your brand in record time, whether that's[generating a business logo with AI Images](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/ai-images) , or[expanding the edges of your image](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/ai-expand) realistically to repurpose assets for your social media.


Just in time for[Halloween promotions](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/spookify-your-photos-this-halloween) and the[Black Friday](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/black-friday-sales) rush, these tools are here to save you time while creating amazing visual content in seconds. And stay tuned, because even more game-changing tools are on the horizon to promote your brand for the holidays and beyond.


## New Updates


### AI Images mini tools


We are rolling out an improved version of[AI Images](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/ai-images) for English and French speaking iOS and web users. You can use AI to create even more intuitive, powerful and realistic images. Whether you want to create a banner, a logo, or a lifestyle photo, you can get a stunning result within seconds based on your need. We will keep iterating and expand access to more users internationally and across devices in the future.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS and the web


**For** : English and French Pro users


### Reactions for designs


See all the emoji reactions to your design at a glance.


**Platform:** Photoroom for iOS and web, soon for Android


**For:** Free and Pro users in a Team


### Resize with AI Expand on the web app


You can now use generative AI to realistically expand the edges around your image on the web! It’s never easier to repurpose your image for another platform. You'll find this option under[Resize](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/image-resizer) .


**Platform** : Photoroom for the web and iOS. Coming very soon to Android


**For** : Pro users
