When Warner Bros. set out to promote *Barbie the Movie* , the marketing team had a clear ambition: create a social media moment that fans would spread themselves. A participatory campaign rather than a traditional one.


The concept was[BarbieSelfie.ai](https://www.barbieselfie.ai/intl/) , a web tool where anyone could upload a photo, have their background instantly removed, and see themselves placed into an official *Barbie the Movie* poster. A fan-centered campaign like this prompts users to share their creation with friends and followers, expanding its organic reach.


But making that experience feel effortless for millions of people at once meant solving a very specific technical problem, fast.


**The challenge**


## **A global campaign with an unpredictable volume of selfies**


For the Barbie Selfie Generator to work, Warner Bros. needed a[background removal](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/background-remover) solution that could **process images instantly, handle unpredictable scale, and deliver flawless quality** on every photo.


These photos weren't product shots taken against a white backdrop in a studio. They were selfies taken in bedrooms, at restaurants, on the street, in cars. Every image was different, and every output would be shared publicly with the Barbie movie branding attached. A rough cutout would look sloppy and reflect poorly on the film itself.


The pressure was compounded by the timeline. This was a global movie launch, and millions of people would use the tool within a short window. Each fan expected a poster-quality result in seconds, and **Warner Bros. needed the infrastructure to deliver that without a single visible failure.**


**The solution**


## **One API for instant quality at global scale**


Warner Bros. tested 10 different background removal services in a single day, measuring each one on output quality, processing speed, and ease of integration. Photoroom stood out across all three criteria, and the[Warner Bros. team embedded Photoroom’s API](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/photoroom-and-warner-bros-join-forces-to-create-viral-barbie-selfie-generator-using-cutting-edge-ai-technology-301881685.html) as image infrastructure.


> **"We took one day to test 10 different services and less than an hour to implement Photoroom API."**
>
>
> **— Matt Winkler, VP & Head of Engineering, Warner Bros.**


**Here’s how the Photoroom API powered the Barbie AI selfie generator:**


-


**Integration:** Matt and his team went from selecting Photoroom to having a working implementation in **under one hour** , with no complex onboarding or configuration required.


-


**Processing speed:** When fans visited BarbieSelfie.ai and uploaded a selfie, Photoroom’s[Remove Background API](https://www.photoroom.com/api/remove-background) returned processed images in **under 300 milliseconds** , fast enough that users never felt like they were waiting.


-


**Output quality:** Each uploaded photo, across the full range of real-world selfies, returned as a clean cutout that the campaign's creative layer composited into the branded poster template.


As the campaign gained traction, volume scaled rapidly. The API handled millions of calls without downtime, quality degradation, or throttling during peak traffic. For a campaign built entirely on user participation, that kind of reliability was what separated a viral moment from a modest campaign.
