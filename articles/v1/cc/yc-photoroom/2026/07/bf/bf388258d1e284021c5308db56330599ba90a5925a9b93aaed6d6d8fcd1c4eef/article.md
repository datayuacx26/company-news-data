# What's new in product: June 2024

Welcome to the latest Photoroom product update! Besides fresh new seasonal templates from Pride to Euro Cup and Summer themes, we are excited to introduce an array of new features and improvements to help you improve your creativity, streamline collaborative efforts and make your photos stand out. Dive into these updates and make your summer snapshots shine!


## New Updates


### More creative control with AI Fill (beta)


Want to replace an ashtray with a coffee mug, fix a closed eye with open eyes, or add some details to your image to make it perfect? We are slowing rolling out the ability for you to customize an area of your image with an AI prompt to instantly add or correct the details of your image. It’s never easier to control and fine-tune your image to match your vision and make your image perfect. Just go to Retouch, choose “Fill” and type in what you want for your selected area.


Note this is in beta for iOS users from version 6.0.1 and it will first be available to the US and slowly become available to other English speaking countries.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS (6.0.1 version onwards). Coming soon to the Web app and Android


**For** : Pro and Business users in the US, and soon all other English speaking countries


➡[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/9400796-replace-part-of-an-image-with-ai-fill-beta)


### Share a design with anyone


You can collaborate with anyone by sharing a design link, regardless of whether they are in your Team. Just simply copy the link and share with your team, assistant or close ones on WhatsApp, Messages and more.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS, Android and Web


**For** : everyone


➡[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/9531165-share-a-design)


### Switch AI Backgrounds versions on the web app


We recently introduced the option to switch back to the older version of AI Backgrounds on iOS and Android. Now, you can also do so on the web app by going to “preferences”.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS, Android and the web


**For** : All Pro users


➡[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/9367114-switch-to-different-version-of-ai-backgrounds)


### Quick access to last opened designs


Easily access and re-edit your last opened design directly from the home screen.


**Platform** : Photoroom for Android, iOS and the web


**For** : Everyone


## Coming soon


### More intuitive AI Backgrounds (beta)


AI Backgrounds will soon have improved UX on iOS. Be prepared to experience easier ways to customize prompts and have more control of how much you want your image to resemble the inspiration image.


**For** : Pro users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


### Enhance your images with AI Upscale (beta)


If you need higher resolution photos for your website or just want to restore details of old photographs, our upcoming AI Upscale tool can help. It will be first available to iOS users.


**For** : Pro and Business users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS
