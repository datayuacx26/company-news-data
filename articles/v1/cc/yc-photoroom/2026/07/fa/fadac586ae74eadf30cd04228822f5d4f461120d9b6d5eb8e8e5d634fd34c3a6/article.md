### DoorDash


DoorDash requires that all photo images include at least 80% of the dish centered in a horizontal format with no people, added text, or graphic overlays. Unlike other food delivery apps, you can’t use “overly colorful” and creative, white, or transparent backgrounds for your images, so stick to a non-distracting, neutral setting rather than elaborately staged lifestyle photography. Photoroom can easily remove and[replace backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/instant-backgrounds) , so you can avoid unnecessary reshoots just to meet this requirement.


### Uber Eats


Although Uber Eats gives you a little more flexibility with the aspect ratio (between 5:4 and 6:4), double-check that your dishes aren’t cut off by the frame. Neither Uber Eats nor Wolt have strict background requirements, but for the best chance of approval, use a simple background and avoid harsh shadows and added text. Uber Eats also checks images for sanitary conditions, so make sure that your setting is clean and hygienic (e.g., doesn’t include dirty silverware, packaging, or surfaces nearby).


### Wolt


Wolt is owned by DoorDash, but has different image requirements from its parent company. Its image size requirements are smaller—at least 1,000 x 563 pixels—and the app won’t accept entirely AI generated images, though it encourages AI-powered edits through its[partnership with Photoroom](https://www.photoroom.com/customer-stories/wolt) . All images must accurately match what your customers will receive, so start with a picture of your real menu offering to properly show off portion sizes and presentation.


Like most food delivery apps, Wolt asks for a centered image that’s clearly visible (bright lighting without excess contrast or harsh shadows), a clean background, with no people, text overlays, or added graphics.


### Deliveroo


In addition to standard food delivery app requirements, Deliveroo doesn’t allow the inclusion of styled raw ingredients in the shot. As a general rule for this app, avoid making edits that could misrepresent your food, and opt instead for simple, uncluttered visuals. Deliveroo also needs files labeled with the correct dish names before uploading to be accepted.
