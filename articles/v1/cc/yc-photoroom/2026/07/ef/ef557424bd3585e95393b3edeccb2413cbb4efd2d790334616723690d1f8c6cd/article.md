# What's new in product: August 2023

Welcome to the first edition of our monthly product recap! Here, you'll discover recent product releases and a sneak peek of what's next on Photoroom for iOS, Android, and Web. You can also scroll down to the bottom of the page to learn how you can influence the future of Photoroom - we can't wait to hear from you!


## New Updates


### **List and sell faster**


Our Batch Mode feature has been enhanced to provide even faster processing times! With just a few clicks, you can now conveniently apply Instant Backgrounds and Instant Shadows to as many as 50 images at once.


**For:** Pro and Business users


**Platform:** Photoroom for Web


[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/8037837-how-to-batch-edit-with-instant-backgrounds-on-the-web)


### **Customize your photo background**


Generate your own backgrounds with Create a Scene (within Instant Backgrounds), otherwise known as the "Pencil". You can now describe any background you want on Photoroom for Web and get results in just a few seconds. It's time to unleash your creativity and design backgrounds that align perfectly with your creative vision.


**For:** Pro and Business users


**Platform:** Photoroom for Web


[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/7835526-how-to-use-the-pencil-to-create-a-scene-with-instant-backgrounds)


### **More consistent backgrounds (beta)**


Our latest update now offers better Instant Background consistency in Batch mode. Simply turn on the new "high consistency" button to ensure that your Instant Backgrounds will look more uniform across multiple pictures.


**For:** Pro and Business users


**Platform:** Photoroom for Web


##


### **Fun new emojis, humans and fruits to insert in your picture**


You can now improve your images with our fresh collection of emojis, customer reactions and ratings, human faces, and so much more! Simply click "insert" to add these options.


**For:** Free, Pro and Business users


**Platform:** Photoroom for iOS and Android

Soon, you will have the option to upload an image of your choice to serve as inspiration for Instant Backgrounds. Our AI will then attempt to generate similar backgrounds tailored to your preferences.


**Platform:** Photoroom for Web
