Josh Goree started[Completeful](https://completeful.com/) in 2016 with a straightforward idea: handle manufacturing and fulfillment for online sellers so they could focus on growing their businesses. The platform now offers over 100 customizable products, from engraved whiskey glasses to personalized doormats, and serves over 1,000 sellers across[Etsy](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/how-to-boost-sales-on-etsy) ,[Amazon](https://www.photoroom.com/use-cases/sell-on-amazon) , and[Shopify](https://www.photoroom.com/product-catalog/shopify) , letting individuals build online businesses without warehouses or upfront stock.


But every one of those products needs photos that sell. And for years, getting those photos was the slowest, most manual part of a seller's job.


## **When every new product meant weeks of photography**


When a seller on Completeful wanted product photos that stood out, they had to order a physical sample of the item, wait for it to ship, then stage and photograph it themselves for every setting they wanted to test.


Despite their best efforts, the results were often poor-quality photos, and introducing a new product took two to three weeks. Relying on static backgrounds and traditional photography also capped how many listings a seller could realistically create.


> "Before Photoroom, when we introduced a new product, the process would take anywhere from 2-3 weeks and require so much work. But now, sellers don't have to wait for the perfect lighting or the perfect setting. Photoroom takes care of all of that. And the results are perfect every time."
>
>
> — Josh Goree, CEO, Completeful


On marketplaces where many sellers offer similar products, the photo is often what decides the sale. A distinctive image gets the click; a generic one gets scrolled past.


Completeful needed a way to give sellers professional product images at the speed they create listings, inside the platform they already used.


## **From one product photo to a thousand marketplace-ready images**


The team explored several options first, but the integration processes were overly complex, and in the meantime Completeful was advising clients to use external tools for their mockups.


> "With other solutions, you're very limited on what you can do. They're not changing the background; they're just editing the photo they have versus what we have now with Photoroom, where we can provide one standard photo and generate 1,000 different photos for every single client. That's unmatched."
>
>
> — Josh Goree, CEO at Completeful


Then Derek Murphy, Completeful’s CTO, found Photoroom's[Image Editing API](https://www.photoroom.com/api/edit-image) , which meant that mockup creation could finally live inside the Completeful application itself.


> "I remember the first time Derek showed Photoroom to me and I entered a prompt like, 'I want a photo of this whiskey glass on a counter with a leaf,' and Photoroom generated a GREAT photo of a whiskey glass on a counter with that leaf. I was like, holy cow, this is going to change the game for us. And it 100% has. It's been amazing."
>
>
> — Josh Goree, CEO, Completeful


### **How Completeful uses the Photoroom API**


Today, a seller provides one standard product photo, and the API does the rest inside Completeful's platform.


-


**Sellers[generate image backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/api/generate-background)** matched to the audience they're selling to, such as a beach, a golf course, or a wedding, so a single product can appear in whatever setting fits the niche.


-


**The API edits, centers, pads, and resizes** each product image automatically, so every image meets marketplace requirements without manual adjustment.


-


**Completeful generates up to 1,000 different images** for every client using a single photo.

For sellers whose entire business depends on volume, the speed matters as much as the quality.


> "Our customers are individuals selling to consumers. They're trying to generate as many photos, as many listings as they possibly can, and get them out there. They need to attack every single niche, every single market that they can. Automating their product imagery workflow and greatly enhancing the quality of that imagery makes all of this so much simpler for them."
>
>
> — Derek Murphy, CTO at Completeful
