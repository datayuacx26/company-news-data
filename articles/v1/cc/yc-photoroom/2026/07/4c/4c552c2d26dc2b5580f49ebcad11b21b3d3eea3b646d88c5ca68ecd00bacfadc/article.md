# Photoroom helps Beadhouse attract new customers in a highly niche market

**The challenge:** Running[Beadhouse](https://beadhouse.nl/) as a solopreneur, Ivonne found it difficult to keep up with product photography for over 17,000 items listed on her online store. And despite having thrived in a highly niche market for 20 years, she found herself needing a technological and creative edge to grow her online presence and brand identity, and attract new shoppers.


**Our solution:** Photoroom's[AI Background Remover](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/background-remover) and[AI Backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/instant-backgrounds) tools allows her to easily and quickly customize the backdrop of her product photos to create professional-quality listings, streamline her workflow, and maintain full creative control—all without the expense of hiring a photographer.


**Results:** Photoroom has profoundly impacted Beadhouse, leading to more first-time buyers, a growing social media following, and a stronger overall brand identity. Photoroom's ongoing updates and responsive support have kept Ivonne engaged and excited about her business's future, empowering her to showcase her products in the best light.


Ivonne Kronenberg, the creative and driving force behind[Beadhouse](https://beadhouse.nl/) and a jewelry-maker herself, embarked on her entrepreneurial journey over 20 years ago when she moved from her home in the Dutch Antilles to start a business in the Netherlands. Initially focused on ceramics, Ivonne's passion gradually shifted entirely to beads, leading her to establish Beadhouse—a perfectly named haven for bead enthusiasts.


“With beads, I just have this teeny-tiny part of the craft business, and it still surprises me just how many people are into it. But there’s a lot you can do with these tiny beads, from simple stringing to elaborate works of art.”


Starting with a small brick-and-mortar store, Ivonne's business has grown significantly over the course of two decades, moving locations four times to accommodate an ever-expanding inventory. Beadhouse now operates both in-store and online, offering a huge selection of beads— from glass and porcelain to metal and gemstone, and everything in between—sourced from around the world including the U.S., Japan, and the Czech Republic.


Running Beadhouse as a solopreneur with only the occasional hired help, Ivonne has worn just about every hat there is throughout the years, managing everything from procurement to the website to customer service and operations. Despite the challenges of keeping up with the relentless pace of market trends and ever-evolving marketing demands—in addition to new bead shapes, materials, colors, and finishes—Ivonne and Beadhouse have consistently thrived by staying open to learning, adapting, and innovating.


### Discovering Photoroom: A game changer for Beadhouse


Running a web-based business means Ivonne is always in need of high-quality photos and images. While some suppliers provide product images, many do not, leaving her to manage a significant portion of the photography herself. With over 17,000 products available on her web store, this can quickly become an overwhelming and almost impossible task for one person.


So when her customer recommended Photoroom, Ivonne was immediately intrigued and decided to give it a try. After all, she had nothing to lose and everything to gain.


> “I put Photoroom on my phone and started playing with it. It was the free version. And then—after only a week—I thought, that’s it. I need this! Now, if I don’t have a picture, I just put the product on a white sheet and let Photoroom do its magic.”
> - **Ivonne Kronenberg, Founder & CEO, Beadhouse**


Despite not being a professional photographer by any means, Ivonne found Photoroom incredibly user-friendly and effective. Hiring a professional photographer for such a vast inventory of products was too expensive and just out of the question for the small business owner, but Photoroom's[AI-powered tools](https://www.photoroom.com/tools) allows Ivonne to maintain control over the entire process and save time and resources.


And the impact of Photoroom on Beadhouse’s online presence has been significant.


> “I’m on[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/IvonneKronenberg/) , but I had taken Beadhouse off of[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/beadhouse.nl/) because I was so frustrated with figuring out what images to use for my posts. But with Photoroom, I can make hundreds of stunning product photos of my beads in no time. It’s wonderful. I’m a huge fan.”
> - **Ivonne Kronenberg, Founder & CEO, Beadhouse**


The simplicity Photoroom provides is key for Ivonne as she single-handedly manages what feels like a hundred different tasks for the business each day. “I take my picture, choose my background, up it goes.”


Ivonne also loves the flexibility and creative inspiration that Photoroom offers. “You get to play around with the different backgrounds and features, and it's so much fun. In the beginning, I always chose one of the[pre-designed templates](https://www.photoroom.com/templates) , but right now, I’m really into[AI prompts](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/instant-backgrounds) . I put in exactly what I want and either tweak it to my liking if needed or use it as is.”


Photoroom has not only enhanced Ivonne’s product photography but also boosted her overall confidence in how she showcases her products and engages with her audience. In embracing Photoroom, she has unlocked new potential for Beadhouse, showcasing her products in the best light and connecting with customers more effectively than ever before.
