**The challenge:** When it came time to take his beloved business to the next level, Saad Basir faced a major challenge in visually showcasing the unique qualities of his diverse inventory at[Watches, Inc.](https://thewatchesinc.com/) Using plain, standard backgrounds for product photography limited his ability to communicate the personality and story behind each watch. This constraint impacted customer engagement and ultimately sales, as potential buyers weren’t fully captivated by the product images and their potential uses.


**Our solution:** Photoroom’s powerful[Background Remover](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/background-remover) and[AI Backgrounds](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/instant-backgrounds) allowed Saad to create visually engaging scenes that matched the mood and story of each watch without the logistical challenges of setting up countless physical backgrounds. This capability gave Saad the flexibility to enhance the visual appeal of his product photos, making them more relatable and appealing to his audience.


**Results:** After adopting Photoroom, Saad saw a 40% increase in qualified leads, with product inquiries jumping from 20% to 60% per post on social media. The streamlined process facilitated by Photoroom allowed him to edit and share more high-quality images, boosting productivity and engagement. The enhanced visuals not only attracted more potential customers but also reinforced the credibility and trustworthiness of Watches, Inc., further cementing its reputation in a very competitive market.


In May 2022, Saad Basir turned his lifelong passion for watches into a dream business. At[Watches, Inc.](https://thewatchesinc.com/) , fellow watch enthusiasts can shop a large, diverse range of luxury and everyday new and pre-owned timepieces. Each piece tells a different story, allowing the customer to express themselves in a unique and personal way. It’s what Saad has always loved so much about watches.


His passion-led venture first came to life in his home country, Pakistan, and today is run out of both Pakistan and the UK—where he also splits his time. Previously a marketing professional, Saad is now enjoying an entrepreneurial journey a lifetime in the making.


> “I’ve been fascinated by watches ever since I was a kid, even though I couldn’t afford them back then. As I entered my professional life, watches became my go-to reward, and that passion inspired me to turn this into a business opportunity.” - **Saad Basir, Founder, Watches, Inc.**


With a vast inventory including high-end Swiss pieces and popular Japanese watches, in addition to other renowned brands and watch care accessories like cases and straps, Watches, Inc. quickly gained traction. The growing brand has enjoyed substantial growth over the course of just two years, and is well-positioned to maintain this upward momentum and continue to expand in the future.


Starting everything from his home—managing photography, customer interactions, and now supported by a dedicated small team—Saad has grown a loyal customer base exceeding 1,000 watch lovers around the world. He’s also turned Watches, Inc. into one of the biggest watch dealerships in Pakistan that only deals in authentic new and pre-owned watches.


> “Even today, I handle most customer interactions personally, responding to nearly every query. This hands-on approach keeps me connected and ensures exceptional service for my customers.”
> - **Saad Basir, Founder, Watches, Inc.**


Despite his initial success, Saad faced unique challenges like competing with large-scale retailers and managing high-quality photography for his inventory—a key aspect of appealing to serious watch enthusiasts online who want to be sure they’re buying from a quality provider.


This challenge led him to discover Photoroom through an Instagram ad. The powerful and easy-to-use-right-out-of-the-box app streamlined the process and elevated the quality of his product images almost immediately. Photoroom proved to be a game-changer, helping Saad maintain and even expand the high customer experience standard he set for Watches, Inc. while ensuring every timepiece is showcased at its best to attract customers.


This commitment to quality and attention to detail has also played a crucial role in building trust with his customers, reinforcing the credibility and reliability that Watches, Inc. is known for.

One of the biggest challenges when photographing watches is matching the visual mood to each unique piece. Watches are more than just timekeeping instruments; they embody different themes and personalities. So capturing that essence means pairing each photo with the right background and setting a tone that resonates with the product's story. For example, a sports watch might call for an adventurous, rugged scene, with bold and dark hues, while a classic dress watch needs a softer, more refined backdrop, and a diving watch needs a dynamic, aquatic background that evokes the sense of being underwater.


Photoroom gave Saad the flexibility to create these critical visual stories without the logistical complexity of setting up countless physical scenes. With his large inventory of watches, it would have been nearly impossible to stage the appropriate background for every product. Photoroom simplified that process, allowing Saad to pair high-quality images with perfectly tailored digital backdrops to enhance the customer experience.


> “Photoroom has been a game-changer in maintaining consistency and elevating the overall visual impact of my product photos.”
> - **Saad Basir, Founder, Watches, Inc.**

That’s why Photoroom has become an integral part of his workflow, helping him turn already high-quality images into compelling scenes that invite trust and spark interest.
