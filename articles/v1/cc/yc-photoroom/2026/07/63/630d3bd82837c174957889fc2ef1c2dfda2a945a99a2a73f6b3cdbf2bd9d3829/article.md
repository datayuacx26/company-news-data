# Explore new Gen AI features coming soon to the Photoroom API

With the launch of our brand new AI model, we have additional solutions for our API on the way that we're excited for you to try:


**Higher quality AI Backgrounds:** A more enhanced AI background generator that provides crisper, higher resolution, and more realistic backgrounds. Great for revitalizing your product catalog and for ad creative testing.


**AI Expand:** Our generative AI will realistically expand the scene around an existing image.


**AI Fill:** Customize an item or area of your image with AI for more creative control. Instantly modify your visuals without photo shoots.


**AI Images:** Create brand new images using text prompts.


**AI Upscale:** Get higher resolution images or restore the details of old photos. Automatically elevate your product imagery for your e‑commerce and point of sale materials.
