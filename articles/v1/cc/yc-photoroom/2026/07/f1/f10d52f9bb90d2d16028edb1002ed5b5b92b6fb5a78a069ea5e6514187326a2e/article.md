## Why Black Friday sales are harder than ever


Every brand wants a record-breaking Black Friday. But the reality is that winning this sales season has never been harder.


Consumers are[spending less overall](https://content-naf.emarketer.com/marketers-boost-cost-consciousness--ai-tools--aligned-values-reach-gen-z-holiday-shoppers) due to economic uncertainty and[tariff threats](https://www.pymnts.com/consumer-insights/2025/consumers-draw-new-spending-lines-as-tariff-threats-loom/) , putting overall strain on sellers and shrinking profit margins. It’s also harder—[and more expensive](https://www.upcounting.com/blog/average-ecommerce-customer-acquisition-cost) — than ever to acquire new customers. The cost of customer acquisition (CAC) is[up 40%](https://www.upcounting.com/blog/average-ecommerce-customer-acquisition-cost) since last year,[ad costs are soaring](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/facebook-ads-bfcm) and engagement rates are shrinking.


At the same time, shopping behavior has shifted.


Roughly[69% of Black Friday purchases](https://business.adobe.com/resources/holiday-shopping-report.html) are expected to happen on mobile, where visual decisions happen in seconds. LLM-driven shopping has also[entered the fray](https://content-naf.emarketer.com/openai-shopify-chatgpt-ecommerce-integration) , which means brands need to entirely rethink how they optimize their product listings—with photographs expected to play a key role.


The instinctive response to these challenges is to discount more heavily to stay competitive. But this only shrinks margins further. With brands[already losing $29 per new customer](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220719005425/en/Brands-Losing-a-Record-29-for-Each-New-Customer-Acquired) , competing on price alone is unsustainable.


Consumers are scrolling through hundreds of deals. They want to easily find the items that are different and most suitable to them. And in 2025, product photos are how you’re going to help them do that.


In this environment, the brands that win are those that think and act differently. This year, high-quality product visuals, produced at scale using Photoroom, are the secret weapon to help you do that.
