# What's new in product: May 2024

Have you heard the exciting news? Photoroom has won the Webby Award, made Forbes' AI 50 list, and our["Erase your Ex" campaign with OK Cupid](https://www.photoroom.com/erase-your-ex) has been a hit. While we are celebrating these wins, we’ve also been paying close attention to our user feedback and are pleased to roll out significant improvements to our AI backgrounds feature, along with other updates to help you express your creativity, collaborate with teammates and effortlessly create professional photos. Read on to learn more about the latest updates!


## New Updates


### Improved AI Backgrounds and the ability to switch to the old version


We have made further improvements to AI Backgrounds with the updated v3 model so you can expect to see finer details, prompts with better results and higher quality images for iOS users with version 5.9.8 onwards and Android users with 4.9.7 version onwards. While we think the new v3 model has better results, you may have the option to switch back to the older v2 version temporarily in settings under “Preferences”.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS and Android. Coming soon to the web app


**For** : All Pro users


➡[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/9367114-switch-to-different-version-of-ai-backgrounds)


### **Your vote counts: help us improve AI**


To help Photoroom improve our image quality, users on iOS can now vote for a pair of images at an end of an export. Simply choose the image that looks better, or click “unsure” or “both look bad” to help improve our algorithm and have a direct impact on our product.


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS. Coming soon to Android.


**For** : All Photoroom iOS users


### Bigger editing space on the web app


Introducing a sleek new interface for the Photoroom web app – featuring a top navigation bar, expanded editing space to expand your creativity, quick background switcher and detailed info about the settings you used for AI Backgrounds and AI Shadows.


**Platform** : Photoroom for Web


**For** : Photoroom users on the web


➡[Learn more](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/9368268-discover-the-new-single-panel-editor)


### **Comments on your Team design (new for Android)**


Android users, you can now add comments or emojis to show your reactions and send feedback to your teammates’ design. It’s even easier to collaborate, approve designs and show some appreciation to each other! This feature already exists on iOS and the web app for anyone in a Team.


**Platform** : Photoroom for Android, iOS and the web


**For** : All Photoroom Team users


### See who’s viewed your design


If you work in a Team on Photoroom, you can now see who’s viewed or is viewing your design on the web app.


**Platform** : Photoroom for Web


**For** : All Photoroom Team users


## Coming soon


### More creative control with AI Fill (Beta)


Want to replace an ashtray with a coffee mug, fix a closed eye with open eyes, or add some details to your image to make it perfect? We are slowing rolling out the ability for you to customize an area of your image with an AI prompt to instantly add or correct the details of your image. It’s never easier to control and fine-tune your image to match your vision and make your image perfect.


**For** : Pro and Business users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS


### Enhance your images with AI Upscale


If you want higher-resolution photos for your website or just want to restore the details of old photographs, our upcoming AI Upscale tool can help. Stay tuned!


**For** : Pro and Business users


**Platform** : Photoroom for iOS
