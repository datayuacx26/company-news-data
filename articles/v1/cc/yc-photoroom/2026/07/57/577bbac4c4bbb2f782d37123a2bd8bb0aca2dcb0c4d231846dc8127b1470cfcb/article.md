## What is photo retouching?


**Photo retouching is the act of editing or manipulating a digital image to either improve it visually, or adjust its technical qualities (or both).**


Basic photo retouching can include actions like:


-


Re-sizing


-


Color correction


-


Cropping


-


Shape adjustments


-


Defect or object removal


Historically, photo retouching has been done through manual desktop applications that require steep learning curves and expertise to operate. Today, there are a host of AI photo retouching apps—like Photoroom—that both broaden the possibilities for photo retouching, and simplify the process.

## 5 things to know before retouching product photos


Before we jump into some tools to retouch product photos, let’s look at a few best practices.


Here are five tips to make your photo retouching successful:


-


**Start with high-quality images.** Retouching works best when the original photos are clear and well-lit. Low-resolution, blurry, or pixelated images can be difficult to fix, and in many cases, may require a reshoot.


-


**Lighting and exposure matter.** If your images are too dark or overexposed, retouching may only offer limited improvements. Aim to capture photos in natural or consistent lighting to reduce post-production challenges.


-


**Simple backgrounds are key.** The more complex or “busy” a background is, the harder it will be to clean up or remove unwanted objects. Shooting against a plain background makes retouching faster and more effective.


-


**Be realistic about object removal.** Removing unwanted items is easier when they’re surrounded by solid, uniform areas. Heavy clutter or uneven shadows can complicate the process and affect the final result.


-


**Not every image needs to be perfect.**[Tools like Photoroom](https://www.photoroom.com/comparisons/photoroom-vs-aftershoot) can handle common issues, such as removing messy backgrounds or enhancing focus, giving you flexibility to improve even less-than-ideal shots.

Tips for achieving professional results from photo retouching


Picking the right AI photo retouching tool, while important, is only half the battle. You still need to follow some general guidelines to get the best results from your efforts.


Here are five:


-


**Prioritize consistency** to create a cohesive, professional look across product listings. Consistent lighting, angles, and background styles help establish brand recognition and make your store visually appealing.


-


**Use retouching to highlight product features** like textures, colors, and unique details. Subtle enhancements, like sharpening edges or boosting contrast, can draw attention to what makes your product stand out.


-


**Avoid over-retouching to maintain authenticity.** While perfecting every detail may be tempting, excessive edits can make images look unrealistic and risk misrepresenting the product.


-


**Test images across different devices** to ensure they appear clear and vibrant on both desktop and mobile screens, accommodating various online shopping behaviors.


-


**Align images with platform guidelines,** as different e‑commerce marketplaces have specific requirements for size, resolution, and backgrounds. Meeting these specifications ensures a seamless listing process.


With the right techniques, photo retouching can turn ordinary images into professional assets that attract customers and improve conversion rates across your online store.
