# Sell from anywhere: Photoroom for Shopify comes to Android

*Mobile-first sellers can now build, edit and publish complete Shopify listings entirely from their phone, with results including Ameliora +30% conversions, Layer +50% sales and Palm Angels' four-day capsule launch.*


Photoroom for Shopify is now available on Android, making the app available across Android, iOS and web for the first time. Mobile-first sellers can now build, edit and publish complete Shopify listings entirely from their phone, capturing products on mobile, refining on desktop, and managing everything from a single catalog.


The launch marks a significant step in Photoroom's evolution from AI-powered image editing toward connected e‑commerce workflows, letting merchants go from product photo to published Shopify listing without leaving the app.


**One of e‑commerce's most repetitive tasks, solved**


The workflow is designed to remove the fragmented process that currently forces sellers across four or more separate tools before a listing is ready to publish. Merchants can batch-edit up to 250 products at once, use AI Shot List and Listing Score, generate titles and SEO metadata, and publish directly to Shopify in one click.


Dan Spooner, Product Lead at Photoroom, said: "For most independent retailers, creating product listings isn't difficult because they can't take a good photograph. It's difficult because it's one of the most repetitive parts of running an online business. Every product needs images, descriptions, organization and publishing across multiple channels. We've spent years helping merchants create better images. The next step is helping them build better listings, and with today's Android launch, that's something any seller can now do entirely from their phone."


**Sell from anywhere, on any device**


The Android launch completes Photoroom's cross-platform availability. Products can be captured on mobile, refined on desktop and managed across devices, with web, iOS and Android operating from a shared catalog engine and a single source of truth. Shopify is Photoroom's first commerce platform connection, with additional platforms on the roadmap.


The launch builds on growing demand from retailers seeking enterprise-quality content production without enterprise-scale creative teams or budgets. Customers already using Photoroom in their commerce workflows have reported measurable results: US workwear brand Ameliora improved its conversion rate by 30%, UK retailer Layer increased online sales by 50%, and Palm Angels launched a full capsule collection in four days.


Photoroom for Shopify is available now on Android, iOS and web in the Shopify App Store as the Photoroom AI Listing Assistant.
