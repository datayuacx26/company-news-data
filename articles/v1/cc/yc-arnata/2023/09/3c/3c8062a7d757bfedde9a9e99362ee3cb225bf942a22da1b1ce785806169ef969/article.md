# Zerobroker Raises $6.5M in Seed Funding

Zerobroker has raised $6.5 million in Seed funding from Flexport, FundersClub, Streamlined Ventures, and many other notable investors and operators.


I want to take a moment to express our deepest gratitude to our investors, partners, early customers, and our amazing team. Without your belief in our vision, none of this would be possible. Thank you!


At Zerobroker, we're solving one of the most pressing issues in logistics – the freight brokerage fees that add up to a staggering $300 billion in the United States alone. Our innovative platform connects shippers directly to truckers, cutting out the middleman and eliminating 100% of freight brokerage fees. This isn't just about saving money; it's about streamlining the entire logistics process from start to finish.


Imagine being able to create a shipment, find and vet the truck, make bookings, handle payments and paperwork, and update all stakeholders in under 60 seconds.


[That's the power of Zerobroker](https://shipper.zerobroker.com/sign-up/contact-info) . We're centralizing and automating up to 90% of the tedious and time-consuming tasks that logistics and operations teams face daily.


Having spent more than 15 years solving logistics challenges globally, I understand the pain points that businesses like yours face. I've seen firsthand the frustration of reaching out to brokers for quotes, managing loads with limited visibility, chasing paperwork, and manually juggling suppliers and customers. It's a problem that has persisted for too long, and it's time we change the game.


Our growth since launching in February 2022 has been nothing short of remarkable. From venture-backed startups to SMBs and mid-sized manufacturers to public companies, we're serving businesses of all sizes and helping them redefine their logistics operations.


Businesses that don't modernize their logistics now will find themselves at a disadvantage compared to their competitors and will eventually go out of business.
