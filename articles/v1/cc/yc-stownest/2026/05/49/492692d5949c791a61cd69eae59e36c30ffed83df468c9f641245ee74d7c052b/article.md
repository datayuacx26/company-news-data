## 2. Verify Company Registration


A good moving company will always be registered and legitimate.


1. Request GST Registration, Business Address, Company Website, and Customer Support Details


This is an effective strategy to minimize potential loss from fraud.


1. Give Due Importance to Packing Materials


Good packing is vital.


Professional packers typically use:


-


Bubble Wrap


-


Corrugated Sheets


-


Foam Protection


-


Stretch Film


-


Carton Boxes


-


Waterproof Packaging


All of these packing materials can help save your valuables.


1. Get Multiple Quotes


You should never book with the first company you find.


Compare the following:


-


Service Inclusions


-


Packing Charges


-


Transportation Charges


-


Insurance Coverage


-


Additional Charges


If you see a quote that is a lot cheaper than the rest, they likely have poor quality service.


1. Verify Insurance is Offered


Things can always go wrong during a move.


You should always ask:


-


Is Insurance for Transit Offered?


-


What Damages Will be Covered?


-


What is the Claim Process?


Insurance can make the moving process less of a hassle.

Will there be supportive staff while the items are in transit?
