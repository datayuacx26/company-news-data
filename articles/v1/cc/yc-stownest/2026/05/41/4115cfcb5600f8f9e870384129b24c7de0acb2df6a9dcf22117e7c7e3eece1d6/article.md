## **[What Are Fulfillment Center Services?](https://stownest.com/business-storage)**


[Fulfillment center services](https://stownest.com/business-storage) refers to the management of logistics and shipping by a third party servicer. Fulfillment services routinely include:


### Inventory Storage


Unit inventory is kept in secure storage.


### Order Processing


Once the online marketplace places an order, the fulfillment center automatically imports the order.


### Picking and Packing


The fulfillment center warehouse staff personally packs the orders.


### Shipping and Delivery


Orders are shipped by the center's partnered shipping courier services.


### Returns Management


The fulfillment center staff reviews, processes and restocks inventory.


Ecommerces engaged in these services regain their focus on business by increasing sales, marketing, and servicing customers.
