## Considerations for Choosing Packers and Movers


### Investigate Reputation


You should always check for:


-


Google reviews


-


Customer reviews


-


Ratings from customer safety sites


-


Business history


Reliability usually corresponds with how long a company has been in business.


### Confirm Legalities


Avoid moving companies that do not provide:


-


GST certification


-


Proper business invoices


-


Business documentation


-


Insurance


Insurance protects you from losing money to a fraudulent business.


### Price


Low mover pricing should not be the only factor in moving company selection.


Consider:


-


Services included


-


Quality of packing


-


Insurance offered


-


Transportation method


-


Hidden costs


To aid in selecting a moving company, request at least three quotes.


### Insurance


There should always be an option for Transit Insurance, which protects against the financial loss from damaged or lost items.


### Pre-Move Survey


Most reliable movers will conduct a survey for:


-


An estimate of the volume of goods to be relocated


-


An estimate of the packing required


-


An estimate of the size of the vehicle required


-


An estimate of the number of laborers required


More precise estimates result from conducting a pre-move survey.

## Avoidable Mistakes


### Choosing Unbelievably Low Prices


Lowest price offerings are usually at the cost of service quality and/or come with concealed charges.


### Not Getting Feedback


Service quality and reputation of a company can be gauged from feedback and comments.


### Forgoing Insurance


Transit damage and the costs involved can be considerable.


### Booking at the Last Minute


Lack of early booking can result in lost opportunity for better rates and availability.


### Moving Without Decluttering


Transporting unneeded items increases transportation costs.
