## **The Importance of Proper Household Storage**


The value of properly storing household items is often not realized until items are damaged, such as furniture and clothing, or electronics become inoperable due to the moisture and mold that can result. The change in seasons in Pune, from dry to heavy rains during the monsoon, can severely affect the same household items if not stored correctly.


Proper household storage protects your valuables and maximizes your living space. Knowing proper storage techniques saves you damage and money whether you are moving or organizing your home.


### **1. Don't Store Things You Don't Want**


Sort and categorize belongings before you store. It should be easy to determine which belongings are used regularly, and which ones are not. Now is the time to recycle, sell, or donate the belongings you no longer want or use.


Using a Home Declutter Storage in Pune helps you save space in your home, and provides a place to store belongings that are important, but not used often, without getting rid of them. The most important consequence of decluttering before you store is the money you save. You pay for the space you use.


### **2. Pack Storage Strongly**


The quality of packing materials directly affects the quality of packing. The lower the quality of packing materials, the greater the likelihood of storage and moving mishaps.


Good quality packing materials are expensive, but the better the packing materials, the better the likelihood of protecting your belongings and the lower the overall cost of storage and moving. Pack Fragile belongings especially well.


### **3. Control Storage Climates**


Of all the things that threaten belongings in storage, humidity ranks at the top. It causes swelling in wooden furniture, and it can affect clothing and important documents.


To protect belongings from moisture:


- Put silica gel packets in storage boxes.


- Use vacuum-sealed bags for clothing.


- Use breathable protective covers for furniture.


- Use dry, dust-resistant containers for electronics.


Self storage is superior to using home garages and balconies for moisture prevention.


### **4. Use Storage Boxes with Clear Labels**


Boxes must have a label. Most boxes just say contents are Miscellaneous. Be specific with your labeling. Examples include “Books,” “Kitchen Utensils,” or “Winter Clothes.” You can also number the boxes and keep the inventory on your phone.


Most boxes can be opened in a short amount of time if contents are labelled and contents will be of practical use, so your box doesn't have to be emptied.


### **5.Store Items of Different Weights and Fragility Correctly**


Storage boxes must be stacked in a certain way. The most fragile and lightest items should be at the top and heaviest at the bottom.


Electronics, mirrors, and decorative items need extra care during storage such as bubble wrap or foam sheets.Storage boxes should be stacked in a safe and intentional way.
