## Moving for work to the IT corridor in Hyderabad? This is what self-storage has to provide for movers to HITEC City.


If you are moving for work to the IT district in Hyderabad to HITECH City, Gachibowli, Madhapur, and Kondapur it is common to require **[self-storage in Hyderabad](https://stownest.com/self-storage-hyderabad)** to accommodate a short, unpredictable hiatus between departing a location and arriving in a new one. Of primary concern in this context are flexible pricing for the short term, no facility visit required, and easy retrieval of items held in storage.


---


## **The Unexpected Two Week Timeframe**


This is a typical story for those relocating to Hyderabad for IT jobs. An offer is made and accepted. A short notice period means a new apartment in Gachibowli or Madhapur won't be ready for weeks. There's the trouble of deciding where your belongings will go. Family and friends who are nearby often have very little room for additional items. Most **[storage units](https://stownest.com/self-storage)** are rented for at least a month, which makes a month long rental to cover the two week time span a waste of time and money.


This problem is pretty unique and is common in the tech corridor of Hyderabad because so many people come here from other locations for work.


---


## **Why a Job Relocation is a Unique Type of Move**


In this circumstance, you are often given a job relocation start date with little notice. Considerable time is not available for planning the packing and transporting of household belongings.In most job relocations, the need for storage is temporary versus the typical job relocation, which is long term. The new home is often not far away, for example, on the outskirts of Hyderabad.


Relocation for a new job on the contrary may not involve transport of belongings. The job relocatee may not have transport arranged yet. In this case, the idea of transporting belongings and storing them in a storage unit is not practical.More important than cost per unit is the flexibility of the unit. A monthly commitment to the storage unit is not practical considering the storage need may only be for two to three weeks.


---


## **What Self Storage Actually Needs to Offer For This Situation**


For the storage needs of the job relocatee and transport need to be integrated. The storage need, transport of belongings and storage need, and the return of belongings need to be flexible so the job relocatee is not committed to a full month with the storage facility.


Although storing your belongings in a traditional storage unit is an option, it doesn't solve the problem of transporting your belongings to and from the unit. Traditional storage options also don't accommodate shorter and more flexible storage durations that are often needed when relocating.


---


## **How Pickup-and-Delivery Storage Accommodates This Particular Requirement**


A pickup-and-delivery model means someone comes and picks up your belongings from your home. There is no need for you to rent and drive a vehicle or even make a trip yourself to that city and more. Before you even settle into the city, the company takes care of everything for you. Everything is scanned and accounted for so nothing is lost on the way. The way they charge is for the amount and for the duration it is stored. This works a lot better for variable storage durations as opposed to a unit where you have to pay a fixed rate per month. When your permanent place is ready near HITEC City, Gachibowli, or Kondapur, you request delivery.


---


## **Who This Actually Helps in Hyderabad**


This solution is beneficial for a condensed list of people: IT professionals with very short notice that are moving to Hyderabad for the first time, people moving within the corridor between neighborhoods when their apartments lease up and new leases have yet to begin, and people whose new apartments are not ready yet, necessitating an old apartment vacant by a set deadline. This move is about closing short-term gaps without the **[long-term storage](https://stownest.com/self-storage)** component.


---


## **The Real Need Isn't Storage It's Flexibility**


Most people moving to Hyderabad’s tech corridor never had trouble finding a place to store their things. The real struggle was finding a place that would not stretch the storage commitment to a month for a service that was only needed for a couple of weeks. That is the gap that is worth solving .
