## **An Unspoken Monsoon Issue That Everyone Faces**


If you ask a Pune resident who has lived through a full monsoon, they will tell you what happens to cardboard boxes in balcony storage rooms. They don't make it. A neighbor of mine in Kothrud lost her family photo albums and an old wooden cabinet to damp last July. It wasn’t that she didn’t care, it’s because her flat had no dry locations to store them for half a year.


It’s a very Pune problem. This issue only represents a fraction of the complications Pune has with storage space.


---


## **What's Behind Pune's Storage Crunch**


1.


**Damage caused by the monsoon to improvised storage.** There are many places in Pune where storage made from balconies, lofts, and ground floor store rooms are not suitable to withstand moisture for months at a time. There is a search for storage options that are comfortable and appropriate to the climate rather than just available.


2.


**Relocating for work in the IT hub** . Numerous companies locating in different areas lead to employees being relocated in different directions. A job switch means a move while still overlapping the lease on the old place, so storage becomes critical for the duration of the move.


3.


**Older housing societies redevelop.** Like many other Indian metros, Pune has many older housing societies that redevelop, leading to relocating residents, often coupled with a considerable lack of storage in the new accommodations.


4.


**Constant churn among students and young professionals.** Pune’s student and early-professional population leads to continual relocations (hostel to PG, PG to shared flats) where the belongings exceed the storage.

## **Why Conventional Storage Units Don't Work Here**


Traditional storage units on the outskirts of Pune[resolve your space issues](https://stownest.com/blog/top-reasons-why-you-need-a-self-storage-unit) , yet create a greater problem of accessibility. You need to have a means to transport your things to and from the unit. Not all units have temperature controls, and thus your issues with the monsoon may persist. Finally, you pay for a fixed size unit and a fixed duration, regardless of how often or how infrequent, you need to access your stored belongings. Having to check on your belongings means you have to make yet again a trip.


For someone already dealing with a move and a beyond busy job transition, that's not a small inconvenience.
