## An overview of self-storage in India that analyzes the growth in demand, examines the process, and describes the benefits of using self-storage during moving, renovating, or downsizing. Self-storage services follow a simple process that involves secure units of multiple sizes to which customers have access 24/7.


**[Self storage services](https://stownest.com/self-storage)** are a convenient and affordable way for the individual or business looking to store items outside of their office or home. Clients pay a fee for the time and space used. The urban housing crisis, corporate relocation, and the expansion of small businesses demand more space have driven the growth of self storage in India, where the service is now offered in every major metropolitan area through the construction of traditional facilities and the rise of companies offering transport-based storage.

## **A Service That Didn't Really Exist a Decade Ago**


Less than 10 years ago, your options for temporary storage of large home furnishings between moves were limited to the good graces of a relative with a garage, a rented, seldom used godown, or simply not moving until the new home was ready. Outside of the major cities, the service was completely nonexistent.


This has changed. As Indian cities have grown denser and populations have begun to shift for jobs, smaller apartments, and home renovations, self storage has moved from a specialty service to something that all urban residents will likely use at some point in their lives.


---


## **The Rising Trend of Self-Storage in India**


Housing units get smaller and smaller as time goes by. Apartments in bigger cities contain even less space than what was the standard just 10 years ago. People have to make trade offs and get creative on where to store seasonal items and possessions they don't want to get rid of.


More frequent relocation is on the rise. With more IT and services jobs, people are moving cities/neighborhoods far more frequently than the generations before.


Long-term tenants are being forced out. Aging housing societies/independent houses are being demolished, and there is very little notice given. That means residents have to find a place to store their possessions.


More businesses need flexible space.Many ecommerce sellers and small retailers need a storage space to keep selling inventory. But quite a few of them don't want to make that commitment and rent a warehouse.

## **How Self Storage Works**


Self storage describes a method of temporarily securing possessions for a span of weeks to years. Here's how it works in India.


With traditional storage, you rent a unit to store your belongings for a time. Units are typically located on the outskirts of the city. You are responsible for all packing and transport, both for going to the unit and for bringing items back.


The other option is **[pickup-and-delivery storage](https://stownest.com/packers-movers)** . In this case, the storage company sends a team to your location to collect your items. The team is responsible for packing, and you do not have to deal with transport and storage of your items. The storage company delivers the requested items back to you. This method typically charges you for the storage you actually use instead of a unit of a fixed size.

## **What People and Businesses Are Storing**


In self storage facilities all across India, you can find some recurring reasons as to why storage is needed. People are looking to store items, typically furniture and other goods, during a relocation. People wish to store seasonal items or goods, like storage for winter clothing or decorations. There are self storage units that hold items during home renovations. People are also searching for self storage to hold goods during the downsizing process. Lastly, businesses are also looking into self storage for business merchandise or documents that are not fitting into the work area.


As shown above, there can be multiple reasons for each Indian city. An example is the humidity along with monsoon damage in the cases of Mumbai and Pune. Also, relocation of businesses in the IT industries in the cases of Bangalore and Hyderabad is more pronounced. However, the need for safe and convenient self storage is the same everywhere.

## **Cost of Self Storage in India**


The cost of self storage units varies greatly depending on the model. Traditional units charge a flat monthly rate for a specific unit size regardless of how much is stored in each unit. This model is most cost effective for those storing the complete unit size. Pay-per-use models are better suited for small volume storage as cost is based on the volume stored. You should be provided a price quote depending on your specific storage needs.

## **What to Expect from Self Storage in India**


Regardless of which city you are in, same basic questions can determine the extent a provider goes to ensure customer satisfaction: is there 24/7 security monitoring, what are the policy limits of the insurance offered, does the provider maintain inventory tracking at the item level or at the unit level which provides no helpful information, and if a visit to the storage facility is required or can everything be done by pick up and drop off.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


**1. Is self storage provided in all major Indian cities?**


Yes, self storage is now available in all of the major cities such as Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Gurgaon, Noida and many more.


**2. What's the difference between traditional storage and storage with a pickup and delivery service?**


In traditional storage, the customer is responsible for transporting the items to and from the storage facility. A storage service with a pickup and delivery option manages both legs of the transport for the customer and the customer will not need to visit a storage facility.


**3. How much does self storage cost in India?**


The cost estimate is based on volume, duration and the provider's pricing model. Storage options that charge for use on a per basis will be cheaper than storage units that charge a monthly fixed rate for smaller and/or shorter storage needs.
