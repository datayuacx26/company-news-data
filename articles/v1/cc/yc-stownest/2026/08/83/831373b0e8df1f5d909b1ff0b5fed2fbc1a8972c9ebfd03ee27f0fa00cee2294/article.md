## **What is Causing Bangalore's Space Shortage?**


### **Independent houses are being redeveloped fast**


Across Bangalore, older independent houses are being bought out and redeveloped into multi-unit apartments. This redevelopment means residents in those houses are often subject to sudden and non-negotiable move-out dates. These residents often do not have any leads for where they are supposed to put their possessions after the move.


### **Bangalore’s tech corridor continues to displace residents**


Bangalore’s tech employees are notoriously mobile. Job changes between companies in Electronic City and those in the Outer Ring Road, or even between companies in Whitefield and those in Sarjapur, means employees often have to relocate to a new area, and sometimes do this without a new apartment lined up.


### **Apartments are being built without storage**


Bangalore developers can now construct multi-unit apartments that maximize the existing footprint. One observation that has been made, among many, is that 2BHK apartments have less storage than 10-year-old 1BHK apartments.


### **The impact on businesses**


Small businesses and ecommerce sellers based in Bangalore face the same challenges as Bangalore residents. It is not possible to hold excess business inventory in a competitive rental market for Central Bangalore.
