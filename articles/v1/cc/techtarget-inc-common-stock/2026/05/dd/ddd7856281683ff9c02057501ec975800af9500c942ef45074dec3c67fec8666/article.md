# Informa TechTarget Celebrates its Best Journalism Awards Performance to Date, With 57 Award Wins Across Three Prestigious Editorial Awards Programs

Informa TechTarget (Nasdaq: TTGT), an indispensable partner for go-to-market success in the B2B sector, today announced that it has won 57 awards across three prestigious programs for journalistic excellence: the ASBPE’s (American Society of Business Publication Editors) Azbee Awards; the SIIA (Software & Industry Information Association) Jesse H. Neal Awards; and the British Society of Magazine Editors (BSME) Talent Awards. Two of the most established peer‑reviewed honors in B2B media, the Azbees and the Neal Awards recognize industry journalism that meets the highest standards for reporting, editing, and overall editorial quality. The BSME Talent Awards celebrate editorial achievements in UK publishing across both the consumer and B2B sectors.


“It’s an honor to be recognized as the best of the best in B2B journalism. This is a testament to the talent and dedication of the journalists at Informa TechTarget who serve our audiences every day with unique insights that help them stay ahead of the curve in their markets,” said Davide Savenije, SVP of Editorial, Informa TechTarget.


“Informa TechTarget’s business model is rooted in high-value journalism for B2B professionals, so it’s incredibly meaningful to be recognized by the Azbee, Neal, and BSME Talent Awards for our editorial teams’ work,” said Jane Qin Medeiros, General Manager and EVP, Brand and Content at Informa TechTarget. “I’m incredibly proud of the hundreds of journalists dedicated to serving our audiences daily. It is particularly important at this juncture when we are seeing disruption to every industry we cover.”
