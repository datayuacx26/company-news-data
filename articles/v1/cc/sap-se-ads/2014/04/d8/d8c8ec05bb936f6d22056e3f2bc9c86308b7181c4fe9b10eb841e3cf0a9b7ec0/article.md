# SAP Analyst Relations

The SAP Analyst Relations team offers dedicated support to industry analysts working in market research firms involved in:


- Evaluating technologies
- Analyzing applications, trends and end-user requirements
- Advising clients on IT strategies


Through the SAP Analyst Relations team, analysts have special access to executive insights, events, and other activities designed to help them assess SAP’s business and product strategies for topics including:


- Products and Solutions
- Industries
- Services and Support
- Technology and Innovation
- Ecosystem partnerships and industry alliances
