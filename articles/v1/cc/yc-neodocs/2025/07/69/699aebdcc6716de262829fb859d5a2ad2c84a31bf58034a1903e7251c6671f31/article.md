## Understanding Sperm Count Standards


Q: What is considered a healthy sperm count?


A: As per the World Health Organization (WHO), healthy sperm count should be more than 15 million/ml and sperm count per ejaculation should be more than 39 million. The Neodocs Alpha test is specifically calibrated to detect whether your sperm concentration meets or exceeds this WHO-established threshold for normal fertility.


Q: What is azoospermia in males?


A: Azoospermia is a medical condition in which no sperm is found in the semen. It's a common cause of male infertility, affecting about 10-15% of infertile males. This condition would be detected by the Neodocs Alpha test as it specifically looks for the presence of sperm through SP-10 protein detection.
