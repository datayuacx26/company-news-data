## What Does Full-Stack Web Development Mean?


Full-stack development is the complete process of developing an app or software from an initial idea. It involves front-end and back-end development, project management, development, and operations. In joining the full-stack web development program at Microverse, you will learn JavaScript, React, data structures and algorithms, databases, object-oriented programming, API development, and much more.


Read on to see how Microverse’s Full-stack Web Development program is helping students expand their knowledge and skills.


## What Will I Learn in Microverse’s Full-Stack Web Development Program?


Microverse’s full-stack web development program is a great place to enhance your technical and non-technical skills. By teaching students non-technical skills like building a professional network and job searching tips, Microverse's curriculum aims to help students get jobs.


The curriculum allows students to learn web development, collaborate through group projects, and improve the communication skills necessary to work as international full-stack developers. Microverse also prepares students for remote work through practicing and improving their cross-cultural and remote collaboration skills.


During this full-time program, you will learn both technical and professional skills. The course consists of the following modules:


### 1. Introduction to Microverse & Remote Collaboration


You will learn how global students collaborate at Microverse. Also, you will gain fundamental knowledge about HTML forms and responsiveness, accessibility and CSS frameworks, and more.


### 2. JavaScript & Networking


This module focuses on developing dynamic websites and APIs and building a global professional network.


### 3. React & Job Application Materials


In this module, you will learn about React as a front-end framework, Redux as a state management tool, and effective ways to create your job application materials.


### 4. Ruby, Databases & Interviewing


You will learn about databases, Ruby, object-oriented programming, and helpful interview tips through mock interview practice.


### 5. Rails & Preparing for Job Search


This module will help you understand model-view-controller architecture, database connection, and authentication and authorization through the Rails framework. Also, you will learn best practices and tools for job searching.


### 6. Final Capstone Group Project


You will collaborate with other developers to build a full-stack web application. Further, Microverse will offer you an industry professional mock interview to prepare you for future job opportunities.


### 7. Full-time Job Searching


The final module of the program is job searching. Here, you work with a career coach and peer group to support you in job searching and applying for your first role after Microverse.

## How to Join Microverse’s Web Development Program?


Here are the steps to apply and join this program:


### Step 1: Create a Profile


To start your application process, visit[apply to Microverse now](https://apply.microverse.org/) and create your profile. You must enter your first and last name, email address, and password. Once you have created a profile, you can move on to the next step in the process.


### Step 2: Solve Coding Challenges


After completing your profile, you will need to solve three coding challenges within four hours. This test checks if you are serious about pursuing a career as a[software developer](https://www.microverse.org/blog/starting-salary-in-software-development-around-the-world-2022) . You will have to solve these algorithmic problems using JavaScript. Microverse offers the opportunity to receive support from coordinators and understand what it is like to be a student at one of our schools.


### Step 3: Sign up for Microverse Trials


After successfully passing Microverse coding challenges, you will submit a[check-in form](https://support.microverse.org/en/articles/4031245-what-is-the-application-check-in-form) for Microverse Trials. In the Trials, students learn about remote collaboration and pair programming. You can show your coding skills to Microverse and know if it is the best choice.


### Step 4: Pass the Microverse Trials


The next step is to help students determine if pair programming and collaborative learning suit them. In the[Microverse Trials](https://support.microverse.org/en/articles/3047017-what-are-the-microverse-trials) , you will collaborate with other applicants on a video call and work on coding projects, and all applicants will rate each other at the end of the project.


### Step 5: Appear in an Interview


It will take you 20 minutes to complete a remote video interview. This interview is asynchronous, so you can do it whenever is best for you. More information about the admission interviews can be found[here](https://support.microverse.org/en/articles/3456604-what-is-the-admission-interview) .


### Step 6: Join an Acceptance Call


Once accepted, you will be invited to attend a Microverse acceptance call led by the Microverse team. Once accepted, you can complete your student registration forms, pick your start date, and get set to join the program!
