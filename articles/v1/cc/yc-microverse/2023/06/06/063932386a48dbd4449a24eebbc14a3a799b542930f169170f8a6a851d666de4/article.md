# Temporarily Pausing Enrollments for Microverse’s Full-Stack Web Development Program

#### **As of June 2023, we have temporarily paused new enrollments to Microverse’s Full-Stack Web Development Program.**


We are temporarily pausing new enrollments to focus our efforts on improving our program and collaborative learning model, as well as investing in new program offerings, while we continue to support our current student body. We have made the decision to pause enrollments so that we can focus our efforts time and attention into the most important work, and ensure the best chances of success, for our students. We will be working hard on many updates and aim to relaunch enrollments in 2024.


Our students are at the heart of everything we do at[Microverse](http://www.microverse.org/) , and we have made this decision because of our commitment and dedication to them and our community. Students currently in our Full-Stack Web Development Program will continue through it, and our team remains dedicated to supporting them on their journey to life-changing roles.


You are likely wondering why we have made this decision. I shared more about that below, but I want to assure you that a lot of thought has gone into it and how we will use this time. We will use it to invest deeply in our program and collaborative learning model to continue delivering a world-class education to people worldwide, and to innovate and prepare for the path ahead.


## **Why We Are Temporarily Pausing New Enrollments to Microverse’s Full-Stack Web Development Program**


### **1. To improve the learning experience**


We want to ensure that your learning experience with Microverse is the best it can be. By taking a break from accepting new enrollments, we can focus more on significantly improving our Program and learning methodology, which will benefit students.


### **2. To invest in innovation**


**‍** We believe in offering the most innovative and effective collaborative learning experience to help people worldwide improve their skills and access life-changing opportunities. During this temporary pause, our team will use all the learnings from the last four years to create the next iteration of our world-class collaborative learning experience. We will implement new techniques, technologies, and industry best practices and expand our program offerings. This means that when you join our program(s) in the future, you'll gain access to a world-class collaborative learning model and the most up-to-date skills to set yourself up for success in the global market.


### **3. To prepare for our next stage of growth**


Our community is expanding rapidly, with more than 3,000 students who have joined Microverse and nearly one million people worldwide who have expressed interest in Microverse. With a growing global community interested in quality peer-to-peer education available worldwide, we want to ensure we can accommodate everyone effectively. By temporarily pausing enrollments, we can also scale up our infrastructure, resources, and support systems. This will allow us to serve our growing community better and provide the best training, support, and guidance.


### **4. To align with market adjustments**


**‍** Today, the demands of the market and our students are different, and to ensure we can continue living up to our mission — we need to adjust to these changing conditions. Thus, by temporarily pausing enrollments, we can make the necessary improvements to keep up with this.


### **5. To ensure quality**


**‍** We are committed to maintaining the highest quality standards in our program. By pausing enrollments, we can focus on refining and optimizing our curriculum, platform, mentorship, and learning experience. This will ensure that as we continue to grow, each student receives the training and support they need to set them up for success.


### **6. To invest more in *your* future**


**‍** Our student's success is our top priority. By temporarily pausing new enrollments, we can invest more resources into the future for every Micronaut. When we resume enrollments, our community will have access to an improved program and more offerings — all designed to increase your chances of achieving your career goals and realizing your potential.


We will not accept new applications to Microverse’s Full-Stack Web Development Program during this pause so we can focus all our efforts on this work. We will share when enrollments are relaunched and look forward to welcoming new students into our improved offerings.
