HERCULES, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Bio-Rad Laboratories, Inc. (NYSE: BIO and BIO.B), a global leader in life science research and clinical diagnostics products, today announced that the company will participate in the following upcoming investor conferences:


**2026 RBC Capital Markets Global Healthcare Conference** Format: Fireside chat and one-on-one investor meetings
Participants: Roop K. Lakkaraju, Chief Financial Officer
Date and Time: Tuesday, May 19, 2026, at 8:30 AM Eastern Time; a live[webcast](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fkvgo.com%2Frbc%2Fbio-rad-laboratories-inc-may-2026&esheet=54531829&newsitemid=20260508350759&lan=en-US&anchor=webcast&index=1&md5=9dd8f8a2a3557fc1fa53a9d177e792ac) and subsequent replay of the fireside chat event will be available in the Investor Relations section of Bio-Rad’s website at[bio-rad.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.bio-rad.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54531829&newsitemid=20260508350759&lan=en-US&anchor=bio-rad.com&index=2&md5=7619b8d32c5e2018d23e919c8c5db8be)


**Jefferies 2026 Global Healthcare Conference** Format: Fireside chat and one-on-one investor meetings

**About Bio-Rad**


Bio-Rad Laboratories, Inc. (NYSE: BIO and BIO.B) is a leader in developing, manufacturing, and marketing a broad range of products for the life science research and clinical diagnostics markets. Based in Hercules, California, Bio-Rad operates a global network of research, development, manufacturing, and sales operations with over 7,400 employees, and $2.6 billion in revenues in 2025. Our customers include universities, research institutions, hospitals, and biopharmaceutical companies, as well as clinical, food safety and environmental quality laboratories. Together, we develop innovative, high-quality products that advance science and save lives.
