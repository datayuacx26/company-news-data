Aclaris Therapeutics to Participate in the Leerink Partners 2026 Global Healthcare Conference


March 3, 2026


WAYNE, Pa., March 03, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: ACRS), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing novel product candidates for immuno-inflammatory diseases, today announced that on Tuesday March 10, 2026, at 1:40 PM EDT, Aclaris’ Chief Executive Officer Dr. Neal Walker and other members of Aclaris’ senior leadership team will participate in a fireside chat during the Leerink Partners 2026 Global Healthcare Conference in Miami, FL.


A live and archived webcast of this event will be accessible on the[Events page](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=qR189fJTo2B75G78V5EXQMkRhhLM-KH_mzieHT395gP3oibOW4JIMwEO6iUpX-4UipC22-5offrWaLaydWZ_F4cTlDQjwxJPFMaIovOt73A=) of the Aclaris website,[www.aclaristx.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=LCY4kuU6Hcg2tuTCG4tx4znBzk9k0SVUBWJW92fJS2-ud8sjM2WgDD1wXo_WAoBuixDSaDe4P-SVRuU_cpbR1-hC4tF0QFU70XlI7EqcdpQ=) . The webcasts will be available on the Aclaris website for at least 30 days.


**About Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc.**


Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing a pipeline of novel product candidates to address the needs of patients with immuno-inflammatory diseases who lack satisfactory treatment options. The company has a multi-stage portfolio of product candidates powered by a robust R&D engine.
