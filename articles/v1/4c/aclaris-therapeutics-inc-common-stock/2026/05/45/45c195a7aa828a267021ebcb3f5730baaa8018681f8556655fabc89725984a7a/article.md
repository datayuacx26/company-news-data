Aclaris Therapeutics to Participate in the 2026 Bank of America Healthcare Conference


May 8, 2026


WAYNE, Pa., May 08, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: ACRS), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing novel product candidates for immuno-inflammatory diseases, today announced that on Thursday May 14, 2026, at 9:35 AM PT, Aclaris’ President and Chief Operating Officer Dr. Hugh Davis will provide a corporate presentation during the 2026 Bank of America Healthcare Conference in Las Vegas, NV.


A live and archived webcast of this event will be accessible on the[Events page](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=LSXdPN_wgX-yPN6RNUWr3Ciq4mTISyiLLrw8KX6ixeOuQGn59XDS-GpIX7lO06Rv8kb81zaYP0FsHs9uFyy9ArcwSUl2m9LSu-4Sm0DtjAg=) of the Aclaris website,[www.aclaristx.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=aBRWkMhpTZjxFEcc12L9nn8b4_ZcSdN66C1YP8qc98FAd2nxBElv222vL4Owhz295WGe1edjHQKn4zKUy6CjSl7JuegxUIgaXusnhoU0Rg4=) . The webcast will be available on the Aclaris website for at least 30 days.


**About Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc.**


Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing a pipeline of novel product candidates to address the needs of patients with immuno-inflammatory diseases who lack satisfactory treatment options. The company has a multi-stage portfolio of product candidates powered by a robust R&D engine.
