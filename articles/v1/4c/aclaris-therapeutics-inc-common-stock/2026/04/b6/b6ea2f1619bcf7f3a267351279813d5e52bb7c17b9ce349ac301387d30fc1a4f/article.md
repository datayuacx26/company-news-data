Aclaris Therapeutics to Participate in the H.C. Wainwright 4th Annual Inflammatory Skin Disease Virtual Conference


April 8, 2026


WAYNE, Pa., April 08, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: ACRS), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing novel product candidates for immuno-inflammatory diseases, today announced that on Tuesday, April 14, 2026, at 12:00 PM EDT, Aclaris’ Chief Executive Officer Dr. Neal Walker and other members of Aclaris’ senior leadership team will participate in a fireside chat during the H.C. Wainwright 4th Annual Inflammatory Skin Disease Virtual Conference.


A live and archived webcast of this event will be accessible on the[Events page](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=XXrYA2FzZkOaQn8tSErHtpKbF2j2hZYd2Imqe8a82Tjn0CPmn4GWrf7qorvsiJoM8HL5G2iqwHx4HWV6dsnhAYao1lxe36JnpRC6ku3CeWs=) of the Aclaris website,[www.aclaristx.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=z3w-DscAc1CR3ckkEI5r_vpkqRRU1ZfblZkZRYSwGnIYXPEe25maViZKZY3IWaqUQMj_nzBHvVuP4HSTxI3PJDnx0fjiZmOXH4U-lM2B90M=) . The webcast will be available on the Aclaris website for at least 30 days.


**About Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc.**


Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing a pipeline of novel product candidates to address the needs of patients with immuno-inflammatory diseases who lack satisfactory treatment options. The company has a multi-stage portfolio of product candidates powered by a robust R&D engine.
