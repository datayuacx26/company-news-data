Aclaris Therapeutics to Provide Clinical Update on Key Biologic and Oral Inhibitor Programs on April 28, 2026


April 27, 2026


WAYNE, Pa., April 27, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Aclaris Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: ACRS), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing novel product candidates for immuno-inflammatory diseases, today announced that it will provide the full results from its Phase 1a single and multiple ascending dose trial of its anti-TSLP/IL-4R bispecific antibody ATI-052 and the results of its lead indication selection process for its ITK/JAK3 Inhibitor ATI-2138 before the opening of the financial markets tomorrow, April 28, 2026.


Aclaris will host a webcast and conference call with slides tomorrow morning at 8:30 AM EST to discuss these updates. The live and archived webcast will be available on the Events page of the Company’s website:[https://investor.aclaristx.com/events](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Rv5CmxNuTC8r7SpSl1yrpZ20h-mecvyf78WZLsbQoeVKAs9Eiki7jLLFaP1-Xf6QJ63ljqzbxSdRP7JNc39-6weRhylRB3Kp1FPOku1AdU1XFgNNmACKo-Cw6P2dp0GplBwvCB4hAy0n7FNmPlFLNA==) . The webcast will be archived on the same page for 30 days following the event. If you would rather access the call via telephone: To register and receive a dial in number and unique PIN to access the live conference call, please[follow this link](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=kZI1qxnR9RRiCEkuoS6gmy0zxmKWSBREsperidftKK6YSIfJRH18GXY40aWC_xy1GK99SunFgItydDau84wCas4OogS52P8k-BRbNQoR6qpvVaSHv0yFCb3ILRG-Tp_H1Db4ye7q0WBS1-d1lS1JwuSwv-H1BoCQxlLim9QgOTY=) to register online. Upon registering you will receive the dial-in info and a unique PIN to join the call as well as an email confirmation with the details.
