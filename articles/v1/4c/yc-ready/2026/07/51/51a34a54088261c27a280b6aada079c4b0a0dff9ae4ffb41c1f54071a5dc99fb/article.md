# The BEAD Restructure Reality

The new BEAD guidance gives you 90 days to restructure. But if you factor in procurement, onboarding, testing, public comments, and NTIA review, you have less than 50 working days to deliver.


And here’s the hard truth: open-ended timelines and manual reviews guarantee you won’t make it.


The new rules require:


- Tech-neutral, side-by-side comparisons of fiber, fixed wireless, LEO, cable, etc.
- Standardized scoring aligned with statute
- Clear audit trails and airtight compliance


Trying to manage it all with spreadsheets, emails, and time-and-materials contracts is exactly how you miss the deadline.


That’s how your funding falls through.


That’s how your well-designed program loses public trust.
