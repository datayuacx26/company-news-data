# COMSTOCK RESOURCES, INC. ANNOUNCES FIRST QUARTER 2026 EARNINGS DATE AND CONFERENCE CALL INFORMATION

FRISCO, TX, April 08, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Comstock Resources, Inc. (NYSE:CRK) plans to release its first quarter 2026 results on May 5, 2026 after the market closes and host its quarterly conference call at **10:00 a.m. CT** on May 6, 2026 to discuss the first quarter results.


Parties interested in participating in the conference call telephonically will need to register at[https://register-conf.media-server.com/register/BIfdab657d67b245688283195b41fda6fb](https://url.us.m.mimecastprotect.com/s/REZxCJ6ARWI8XxDvtLsxSymA-q?domain=register-conf.media-server.com) . Upon registering to participate in the conference call, participants will receive the dial-in number and a personal PIN number to access the conference call. On the day of the call, please dial in at least 15 minutes in advance to ensure a timely connection to the call.
