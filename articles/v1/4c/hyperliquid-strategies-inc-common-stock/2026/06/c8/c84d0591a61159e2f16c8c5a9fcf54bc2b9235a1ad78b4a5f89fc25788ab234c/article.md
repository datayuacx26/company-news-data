# Hyperliquid Strategies Inc Added to the Russell 3000, Russell 2000 and S&P Global BMI Indices

NEW YORK


, June 29, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Hyperliquid Strategies Inc (NASDAQ:PURR ) ("HSI"), the premier digital asset


treasury platform focused on the Hyperliquid ecosystem, today announced that its common stock has been added to the Russell 3000 Index and the Russell 2000 Index in connection with FTSE Russell's 2026 annual reconstitution, effective at the open of U.S. trading on June 29, 2026. The Company also announced that its common stock has been added to the S&P Global Broad Market Index (BMI), effective June 22, 2026.


Membership in each index is determined solely by the respective index provider in accordance with its published, rules-based methodology, which considers factors such as market capitalization, float and liquidity. The additions reflect the growth in the Company's market capitalization and trading profile since its transition to a HYPE treasury platform in December 2025.


*"Inclusion in these widely followed benchmarks reflects the growth of the Company and the continued broadening of our shareholder base. We remain focused on the disciplined execution of our treasury strategy on behalf of our shareholders."*


David Schamis, Chief Executive Officer, Hyperliquid Strategies Inc


**About the Indexes**


The Russell 3000 Index measures the performance of the largest U.S.-listed companies and is intended to represent substantially all of the investable U.S. equity market. The Russell 2000 Index is the small-capitalization segment of the Russell 3000. The Russell U.S. indexes are reconstituted annually. The S&P Global BMI is a comprehensive, float-adjusted index designed to measure global equity market performance across developed and emerging markets. Additional information is available from FTSE Russell and S&P Dow Jones, respectively.
