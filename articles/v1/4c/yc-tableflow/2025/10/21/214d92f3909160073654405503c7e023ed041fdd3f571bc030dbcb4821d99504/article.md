# Meet Flows: Automate What Comes After Extraction

At TableFlow, we started with one goal: to extract clean, structured data from messy documents. Our platform processes PDFs, Excel sheets, CSVs, or images—and intelligently organizes the information you need. This core functionality remains the foundation of our platform.


As we worked with more teams, we realized that data extraction is just the first step. The real challenge lies in what happens next—manually processing that data to fit into unique workflows. To address this, we're excited to introduce **Flows** , our solve for automating custom, file-based workflows end-to-end.


## From Data Extraction to Full Workflow Automation


Flows transforms TableFlow into a comprehensive automation platform. It enables you to design and execute tasks your team handles after receiving a document. Flows manages repetitive, detail-heavy jobs so your team can focus on value-driven work.


With Flows, data extraction becomes the starting point for automated actions, connecting document processing to your business operations. It's about building intelligent systems that don't just process data but know what to do with it.


## Solving Real Business Problems with Flows


Flows handles complex, multi-step processes specific to your business, improving and automating what you already do.


### Example 1: Reconciling Shipping and Order Data


Your logistics team might receive packing lists in various formats—PDFs, scans, or spreadsheets. Matching these to purchase orders can be quite a bit of manual work. With Flows, this process becomes:


1. 1. A packing list arrives via email or upload.
2. 2. TableFlow extracts line items like product descriptions, quantities, and lot numbers.
3. 3. The Flow pulls corresponding purchase data from your ERP or database.
4. 4. AI-powered matching compares items, flagging discrepancies like quantity mismatches.
5. 5. A detailed report is sent to you via Slack.


What used to take hours now happens in minutes, letting your team focus on resolving, not searching for, issues.


### Example 2: Processing Customer Orders from Emails


Many businesses still receive orders as email attachments, or even plain text in the email. Manual data entry into an ERP is slow and error-prone. Flows automates the process:


1. 1. An email with an order triggers the Flow.
2. 2. TableFlow extracts customer and order details.
3. 3. The Flow verifies this information against your ERP for accuracy and inventory checks.
4. 4. It creates a draft order in your system, ready for review.


This turns your inbox into an automated order processing system, speeding up fulfillment and improving accuracy.
