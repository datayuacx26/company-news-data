# 9 Ahrefs Alternatives for AI Visibility Tracking (2026)

### TL;DR


- Best overall alternative: Siftly
- Best for budget-conscious teams: RankScale
- Best for European and multilingual brands: Peec AI
- Best pure-play AI visibility monitor: Otterly.AI
- Best for full-stack SEO bundled with AI tracking: Semrush


Ahrefs built its name on Site Explorer and one of the largest backlink databases in SEO, and for years that was reason enough to pay for it. In 2026, it folded a new module called Brand Radar into its core plans, promising to track how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews mention your brand alongside your usual keyword and backlink data. On paper, that sounds like a convenient bundle.


In practice, a growing number of teams are finding the AI side of Ahrefs doesn't hold up the way the rest of the platform does. Independent testing has caught Brand Radar undercounting AI mentions by a wide margin, the tool skips Claude and Grok entirely, and the deeper prompt tracking you actually need sits behind pricier tiers. If AI visibility is now a real part of your job, and not just a checkbox next to your keyword rankings, it's worth looking at what else is out there.


## Why You Might Be Looking For An Ahrefs Alternative


Ahrefs remains a strong SEO suite, but its AI-visibility layer has drawn some consistent criticism.


1. Brand Radar has an accuracy problem: Independent testing cited by multiple reviewers found it undercounting ChatGPT mentions by roughly 97%, in one case reporting 3 mentions where manual checks found 123.
2. Historical data isn't stable: G2 reviewers report that past metrics change retroactively every time Ahrefs updates its index, which makes trend reporting hard to trust.
3. No Claude or Grok coverage: Despite marketing Brand Radar as an AI tracking feature, it only checks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.
4. Prompt tracking is thin unless you pay up: The entry Lite plan only tracks 5 prompts, and getting to a usable volume means jumping to a $449 or $1,499 monthly plan.
5. The core product has its own complaints: Ahrefs sits around a 2.0 out of 5 on Trustpilot across close to 300 reviews, with rising costs and credit-based limits coming up often.

## How to Make the Right Choice


Choosing the right Ahrefs alternative comes down to what you actually need from an AI visibility tool, not just which one has the longest feature list. Here's what's worth weighing before you commit.


- Monitoring vs. execution: Some tools stop at telling you where you stand. Others, like Siftly, close the loop by generating content and running outreach based on what they find. Decide whether you want a dashboard or a tool that acts on the data.
- Engine coverage that matches your audience: Check which AI models a tool actually tracks, and whether they are included by default or locked behind a pricier plan, since several tools in this space quietly gate them.
- Citation accuracy: A mention count means little if it's undercounting by a wide margin. Look for tools that separate brand mentions from actual source citations, and be skeptical of any vendor that can't explain its methodology.
- Attribution to real traffic: AI visibility on its own doesn't pay the bills. Prioritize platforms that tie mentions and citations back to GA4 or Search Console data, so you can see whether the work is moving actual numbers.
- Pricing that scales the way your usage will: Credit-based and prompt-based pricing can climb fast. Map out what your prompt or credit volume will look like in six months, not just today, before picking a tier.
