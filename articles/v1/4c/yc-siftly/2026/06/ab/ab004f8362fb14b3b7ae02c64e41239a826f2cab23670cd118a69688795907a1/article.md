# How to Rank in ChatGPT Search (and Appear in Its Answers)


Last updated June 2026 · By[Chalam Vatti](https://siftly.ai/team/chalam)


**To rank in ChatGPT search, earn citations in the trusted sources ChatGPT retrieves, structure your content so answers are easy to extract, and make sure ChatGPT's crawlers can access your site.** ChatGPT doesn't "rank" pages the way Google does — it composes an answer from sources it trusts, so the goal is to become one of those sources.
