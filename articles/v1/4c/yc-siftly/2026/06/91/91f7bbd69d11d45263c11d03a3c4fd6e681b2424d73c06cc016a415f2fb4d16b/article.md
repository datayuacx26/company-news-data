# The Best Tools to Track Brand Mentions in ChatGPT

## How do you check if ChatGPT mentions your brand?


You can ask ChatGPT directly ("what are the best \[category\] tools?") and see if you appear — but you'd need to repeat it many times, across many phrasings, to get a reliable read. A tracking tool automates that: it samples the same prompts repeatedly, logs your mention rate, and flags when it changes.
