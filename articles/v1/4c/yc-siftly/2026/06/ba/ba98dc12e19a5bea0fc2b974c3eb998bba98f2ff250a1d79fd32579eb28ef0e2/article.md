# 10 Best AI Brand Monitoring Tools (2026, Compared)

Do you want monitoring plus the layer that helps visibility? Siftly monitors and reports AI mention/citation numbers through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews, and adds GEO along with experimental content to help visibility. Enterprise depth? Profound. Mid-market, gap analysis? Peec AI. Budget or free? Otterly, Mangools, Genrank. Real-conversation data vs. scripted prompts? Aiso. Already a Semrush user? Check out the AI Visibility.


Thanks mainly to these platforms, it is now entirely possible to monitor brand mentions in AI answers. The category barely existed pre-2024, and there are already more than a dozen platforms you can trust. Here's how to select one, plus a comparison of the top ten.


## What to look for in AI brand monitoring


Give one point for each of the following:


- **Engine coverage** — does it monitor the popular AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, etc.)?
- **Sampling rigour** — does it check AI answers once, or ask many prompts that produce different answers?
- **Cited references** — does it display the cited URLs where the AI mentioned you or your company?
- **Action layer** — does it just report, or does it show you how to improve?
- **Benchmarking against competitors** — share of voice vs. specific competitors.
- **Price + workspaces** — how much relative to the brands and campaigns you require?
- **Data source** — a scripted prompt executed on a timer, or real, untethered dialogue from a consent-based panel?

## The tools


1. **Siftly** — Tracks brand citations/mentions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Siftly benchmarks your share of voice vs. competitors and goes a step beyond by including GEO content and A/B tests. The difference is monitor → optimize → measure vs. monitor only.
2. **Profound** — Large-enterprise-focused tool with depth and breadth of analytics across 10+ engines, approximately $499/month. A good tool for large teams, but less focused on doing the remediation for you.
3. **Peec AI** — Mid-market analytics (starting around €89/month) and a quality analyst tool for a competitive gap analysis among ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini — useful for identifying which things you should fix first.
4. **Otterly** — Introductory-level monitoring (starting around $29/month) that tracks the core engines and performs a GEO audit. A reasonable entry point.
5. **Scrunch** — Citation trend analysis for brand monitoring that tracks increasing, decreasing, and newly emerging citations before they appear in metrics.
6. **Writesonic** — AI-visibility tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude on a content-centric platform. A good tool if your focus is on content.
7. **Semrush AI Visibility** — Mention and visibility tracking within the Semrush tools for about $99/month per domain. Tracks gaps, but won't fill them.
8. **Mangools** — An affordable SEO tool set with a complimentary AI Search Grader that rates your brand across 8 LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Mistral, Llama, and more); its AI Search Watcher provides monitoring. Inexpensive.
9. **Genrank** — A ChatGPT-focused rank-and-mention tracker with a Web Search mode and prompt trends. A narrow tool set, but if your priority is tracking ChatGPT, it does the job.
10. **Aiso** — Offers another way to pull data: using a consented panel of actual ChatGPT conversations, it provides user intent and sentiment for your category on ChatGPT and Gemini.


## Can you track brand mentions in AI search?


Yes. Purpose-built platforms automate sending buyer questions to AI engines at intervals and catalog brand mentions (if any) and the ways in which brands are mentioned. Because answers differ on each run, reliable tracking requires automating this regular sampling — something manual testing cannot replicate the way these tools do.

- **Just starting / low budget:** Otterly, or a free grader (Mangools, Genrank).
- **Mid-market:** Peec AI, for a competitive gap analysis.

- **Want both monitoring and the content/experimentation layer** that helps with brand mention tracking: consider Siftly as a direct comparison to Otterly, Profound, and Peec.


## Brand monitoring vs citation tracking


Brand monitoring is a wider category that shows whether your brand was mentioned, while citation tracking is a more detailed function that lists which engine source URLs were used. Monitoring is the broad "are we showing up?" view, while citations are the focused layer you can act upon. If you want to measure and improve your citation tracking specifically, consider the AI citation tracking tools.
