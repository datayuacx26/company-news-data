# How to Monitor Brand Mentions in Perplexity

## Why monitor Perplexity specifically?


Perplexity displays the sources behind every answer, so it's a transparent window into what AI engines trust in your category. **If you can see which URLs Perplexity cites, you know exactly which sources to influence** — a clarity ChatGPT doesn't give as readily.


## How to track brand mentions in Perplexity


1. List your buyer prompts.
2. Run them through Perplexity repeatedly (a platform automates this).
3. Record mention, rank vs competitors, and the cited sources.
4. Compare against your other engines.


[Siftly](https://siftly.ai/features/ai-brand-monitoring) tracks Perplexity alongside ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, and pairs it with[AI citation tracking](https://siftly.ai/guide/ai-citation-tracking) so you can act on the sources Perplexity reveals.


## How to get mentioned in Perplexity


Because Perplexity leans on freshness and visible citations: keep content updated, structure it for extraction, and earn mentions in the trusted sources it already cites for your topics. The same playbook that wins[ChatGPT mentions](https://siftly.ai/blog/best-tools-to-track-mentions-in-chatgpt) applies, with extra weight on freshness.
