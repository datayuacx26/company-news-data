# AI Overviews Trackers: How to Monitor AI Search Rankings in 2026

Almost nobody ranks well for this term yet, which makes it a clear opportunity to own. Here's how these tools work and which to use.


## What is an AI Overviews tracker?


It runs the queries you care about, captures the AI Overview (and AI Mode) result, and logs whether your domain is cited, which competitors are cited, and what sources the answer pulled from. Because AI Overviews now appear for a large and growing share of informational queries, tracking them is now part of rank tracking, not a separate hobby.

## Techniques for boosting visibility in AI search algorithms


1. **Answer the question in the first sentence** — extractable answers get pulled into Overviews.
2. **Add FAQ and How-To schema** — structured data helps models parse and cite you.
3. **Earn third-party citations** — corroboration across trusted sites raises selection odds.
4. **Keep content fresh** — dated, updated pages are favored, especially by Perplexity.
5. **Cover the sub-questions** — query fan-out means AI rewards pages that answer related questions too.
