## What does citation rate mean?


Citation AIs pull from a small set of webpages to answer a query. Your citation rate is how many times your website was one of those sources. It is the most actionable AI-visibility metric. Unlike a vague "mention," a citation points to a specific URL.


**Citation rate (for each engine)** = (number of tracked queries where your URL was cited) / (total number of tracked queries) × 100


Example: you keep track of 50 specific prompts that your buyers actually used. In Perplexity, one of your URLs sourced 9 prompts → an 18% citation rate. In Google AI Overviews it sourced 4 → 8%. In ChatGPT, sources appear only while it is browsing, so a citation there can come down to chance. You have to measure each search engine separately, and after a content update you measure again to see if there was an actual increase.

## Citation Tracking


Tracking citations is the first step. Improving citations is the next. Here's an effective pattern:


- Determine which pages are cited by AI for your prompts. Those URLs are your competition. Citations show which sources are considered the best for answering the prompts by the AI.
- To increase your citations, either match or outperform these sources. Answer the prompt directly in the first sentence, use headings, and include specific details, facts, or entities that may be pulled directly.
- Every AI engine functions differently. To show work, compare citation rates. Keep the other variables constant in order to show that the changes made are the sole reason for improving citation rates.
- Citation rates vary by industry. Instead of trying to measure against an absolute, measure against your competition. After changes are made to improve your page citation rates, the most important number is how the citation rates compare to your competition.


## Brand Monitoring vs Citation Tracking


Brand monitoring and citation tracking are commonly confused for the same thing. Brand monitoring shows if a page citation includes your business. Citation tracking shows which URLs were cited. For a wider view of where your business is mentioned, use AI brand monitoring tools. For more in-depth views on AI citation tracking, read the tracking guides.
