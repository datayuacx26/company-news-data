# How to Monitor Competitor Mentions in AI Search Results

## Why track competitors in AI search?


When AI answers a buying question, it names a short list. **If a competitor appears in 60% of answers and you appear in 15%, that 45-point gap is quantified lost pipeline.** Tracking it tells you exactly which prompts and topics to win.


## How to monitor competitor mentions (step by step)


1. **Define your competitor set** — direct rivals AI is likely to name.
2. **Track shared prompts** — the buying questions you both want to win.
3. **Record share of voice** — your mention rate vs each competitor's, per engine.
4. **Analyze their cited sources** — when a competitor is cited and you're not, open the source; that's your content gap.
5. **Track sentiment** — how AI characterizes them vs you.
