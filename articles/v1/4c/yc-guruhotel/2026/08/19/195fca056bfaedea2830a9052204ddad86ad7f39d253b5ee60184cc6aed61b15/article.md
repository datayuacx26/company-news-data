# Best hotel website builders in 2026: an honest comparison

## What actually matters in a hotel website builder


Five things decide whether a hotel website earns money: an integrated booking engine with live availability (not a widget bolted on), page speed on mobile where most guests book, structured data so Google and AI assistants understand your property, payment processing that supports your currencies and wallets, and ownership of guest data for remarketing. Design templates are table stakes — the booking path is the product.


## General-purpose builders: Wix, Squarespace, WordPress


Great for design freedom and low entry cost, and fine for a restaurant or portfolio. For hotels, the booking engine is always an external plugin or iframe: availability sync gets fragile, the checkout looks different from the site, and you assemble SEO, payments and maintenance yourself. WordPress adds full control at the price of hosting, security and updates being your job. Budget real time — or an agency — to make any of them hotel-ready.


## Hotel-specific platforms compared


SiteMinder, Cloudbeds and Little Hotelier offer website builders as add-ons to their distribution or PMS products — solid if you already live in their ecosystem, with templates and a connected booking engine. GuruHotel approaches it website-first: AI generates a complete, conversion-optimized site from your property data in minutes, with booking engine, Stripe payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link) and AI-search readiness (structured data, llms.txt) included, while your existing PMS stays the source of truth via direct or Channex connectivity.


## How to choose (and the red flags)


Ask three questions: does the booking engine live inside the site or in an iframe? Who owns the guest data and the domain? What does it cost when you add payments, SSL, hosting and support? Red flags: checkout redirecting to a third-party domain, no structured data in the templates, per-booking fees stacked on top of subscription, and 'SEO included' that means nothing more than editable meta titles. Test the mobile booking flow of any builder's demo before signing.
