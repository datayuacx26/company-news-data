# Agoda extranet (YCS): how to manage your property on Agoda

## Logging in and finding your way around YCS


Sign in at ycs.agoda.com with the credentials issued when your property joined Agoda. The main areas: Calendar (rates, availability, restrictions), Bookings, Promotions (Agoda pushes these hard — flash deals, member rates), Reviews, Property settings and Finance. Agoda also offers a YCS mobile app for on-the-go updates.


## Where Agoda fits in your channel mix


Agoda (part of Booking Holdings) is strongest in Southeast Asia, Japan, Korea and China outbound travel. If your market receives Asian travelers, it's a channel worth optimizing; if not, it usually rides along via your channel manager with minimal extra work. Commission models include net-rate and margin setups, so read your contract carefully.


## Sync YCS automatically


Like the Booking.com extranet and Expedia Partner Central, YCS is best treated as a read-mostly dashboard: let a channel manager connected to your PMS push rates and availability, and let reservations flow back automatically. Manual YCS edits should be the exception (promotions, content), not the daily routine.


## Promotions without cannibalizing your direct channel


Agoda's promotion engine can move volume, but stacked discounts erode rate integrity. Keep your best publicly available rate on your official website, use targeted OTA promotions (member-only, geo-targeted) for incremental demand, and make sure your direct site converts the travelers who Google your name after finding you on Agoda.
