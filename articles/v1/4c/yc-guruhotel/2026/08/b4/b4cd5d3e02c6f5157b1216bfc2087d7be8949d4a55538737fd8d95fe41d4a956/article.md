# llms.txt for hotels: what it is and why your website needs one

llms.txt is a simple text file at the root of your website that gives AI models a clean, curated map of your most important content. For hotels, it helps assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity understand your property and direct-booking offer accurately instead of guessing from cluttered HTML.


## What goes in an llms.txt


A short intro describing the property, then curated links to your key pages (rooms, amenities, location, policies, direct-booking and contact) with one-line descriptions. Keep it factual and concise. Many setups also publish an llms-full.txt with the complete knowledge base and markdown mirrors of each page.


## Why it matters for direct bookings


When an AI assistant can read your facts cleanly, it's more likely to cite your official site and surface your direct-booking link rather than an OTA listing. That keeps you in the conversation at the exact moment a traveler is deciding where to book.


## How to publish one


Create a plain-text file named llms.txt and host it at yourdomain.com/llms.txt. Keep it updated as your offer changes. With GuruHotel, the website ships with llms.txt, llms-full.txt, markdown mirrors and the right schema out of the box, so your site is AI-ready from day one.
