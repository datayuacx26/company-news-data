# Schema.org for hotel websites: a practical guide

Schema.org structured data tells search engines and AI exactly what's on your hotel pages — the property, rooms, offers and FAQs — in a machine-readable format. Done right, it powers rich results, improves eligibility for AI answers, and reduces ambiguity about your rates, location and policies.


## The schema types that matter for hotels


Start with Hotel or LodgingBusiness for the property (name, address, geo, amenities), Offer for rates and packages, and FAQPage for your question-and-answer blocks. BreadcrumbList and Organization help engines understand site structure and brand.


## Match the markup to visible content


Google requires structured data to reflect what users actually see on the page. Don't mark up rates or amenities that aren't shown, and keep your NAP (name, address, phone) identical across the site, Google Business Profile and major listings to build trust.


## Validate and maintain it


Test pages with Google's Rich Results Test and Search Console. Keep schema in sync when rooms, rates or policies change. GuruHotel-built sites generate the right schema automatically, so structured data stays correct without manual upkeep.
