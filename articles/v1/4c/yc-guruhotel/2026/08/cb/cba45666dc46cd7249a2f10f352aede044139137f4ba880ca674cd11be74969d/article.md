# GEO & AEO for hotels: how to get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews

Travelers increasingly start trip planning inside AI assistants. GEO (generative engine optimization) and AEO (answer engine optimization) make your hotel quotable by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews through clear, factual, answer-first content and structured data — so AI recommends your direct site, not just OTAs.


## What GEO and AEO actually mean


Traditional SEO optimizes for ranked links. GEO and AEO optimize for being the cited source inside an AI-generated answer. Instead of competing for a blue link, you give answer engines a clean, factual passage they can quote with confidence — definitions, numbers, and direct answers to the questions guests ask.


## Write answer-first, structured content


Lead every page with a 40–60 word direct answer, then expand. Use question-style headings that mirror how guests search, short factual paragraphs, and FAQ blocks. Keep facts (location, amenities, policies, rates) explicit and consistent across your site, Google Business Profile and listings so AI can trust them.


## Add structured data and machine-readable files


Mark up your pages with Hotel/LodgingBusiness, Offer and FAQPage schema, and make sure it matches the visible content. Expose machine-readable summaries (an llms.txt file and markdown mirrors) so AI agents can ingest your facts cleanly. Finally, allow the AI crawlers you want in robots.txt.
