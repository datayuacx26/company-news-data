# Expedia Partner Central: a practical guide for hotels

Expedia Partner Central (partnercentral.expedia.com) is the portal where properties manage their presence across Expedia Group sites — Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo. You control rates, availability, content, promotions and payouts there. Here's how it's organized, how it compares to other extranets and how to keep it in sync automatically.


## What Expedia Partner Central covers


One login manages distribution across the Expedia Group marketplace. The main areas: Rooms & Rates (inventory, rate plans), Reservations, Property content (photos, amenities), Promotions (member deals, packages), Guest reviews and Finance. Rate and availability changes propagate to all Expedia Group points of sale.


## Partner Central vs the Booking.com extranet


They play the same role for different marketplaces. Expedia leans more on packages and its loyalty program (One Key), while Booking.com emphasizes standalone hotel stays. Most independent hotels list on both — which doubles the manual workload unless a channel manager keeps everything synchronized from the PMS.


## Commissions and the real cost of the channel


Expedia commissions typically range from 15% to 25% depending on market and program participation. Add promotions and member discounts and the effective take can be higher. That's the price of reach — worth paying for new-guest acquisition, expensive for guests who would have booked with you anyway.


## Use OTAs for reach, your website for margin


The billboard effect is real: travelers discover hotels on Expedia, then search the hotel's name. If your official website offers a fast booking experience with the best available rate, that search converts commission-free. Make sure your direct channel is at least as bookable as your OTA listing.
