# The independent hotel's direct-booking playbook for 2026

Growing direct bookings comes down to a fast, conversion-focused website, a frictionless booking engine, a clear reason to book direct, and visibility in search and AI. This playbook walks independent hotels through the steps that shift revenue away from OTA commissions and back to your own channel.


## 1. Make your website convert


Fast load times, mobile-first design, clear room and rate options, and a booking button visible on every key page. Strong photography and storytelling build trust; a confusing site sends guests straight back to an OTA.


## 2. Give guests a reason to book direct


Within rate-parity rules, offer a clear direct advantage: a small discount, a free perk (late check-out, breakfast, upgrade), or flexible terms. Show a price-comparison cue so guests see the value of booking on your site.


## 3. Get found in search and AI


Publish answer-first content, list on Google for Hotels, and add structured data so both search engines and AI assistants can recommend your direct site. Capture guest emails to remarket and convert repeat stays directly.
