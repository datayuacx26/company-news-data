# Tariffs Are Squeezing Distributors

As of August, new tariffs, some as high as 50%, are landing and hitting wholesalers and distributors where it hurts most: inventory costs, margins, and the speed of quoting. Economists warn that after an initial buffer, the real price hikes are starting to bite. Both businesses and consumers are beginning to feel the squeeze.


### Distributors Are Feeling the Pressure


**Inventory & Sourcing Costs:** If you stockpiled early, that shield is fading. Landed costs are rising, whether you import directly or buy through OEMs. Tariff-driven inflation across the supply chain is unavoidable.


**Margin Compression:** Tight gross margins leave little room. Even modest tariff increases can erode profitability quickly. Absorb the costs and profits fall. Pass them along and customers may walk.


**Customer Relationships:** Unstable pricing frustrates customers and puts relationships at risk. Contractors and resellers expect clarity. Being transparent with customers goes a long way.


**Operational Complexity:** Sales teams are juggling renegotiated supplier agreements, shifting quote templates, and fluctuating pricing. That creates friction exactly where Distro exists to smooth things.


**Industry Exposure Varies:** Sectors like HVAC, plumbing, electrical, MRO, and industrial equipment are especially vulnerable because of heavy import exposure. Others with more domestic sourcing may fare a little better, but still feel the upstream cost pressure.


### How Tariffs Ripple Through Quotes


**Behind-the-scenes challenges:** Counter and inside sales reps are re-keying prices, running new margin math, and recalibrating bundles manually while the clock ticks. The result is slower quotes, friction at the counter, and missed upsell opportunities.


**Margin leaks accelerate:** Errors aren’t just costly; they can wipe out already thin margins when costs change so rapidly.


**Customer trust weakens:** Confusion over sudden price changes or inconsistent quotes undermines relationships that took years to build.


It’s a perfect storm unless you have the right tools in place.
