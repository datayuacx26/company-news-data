# AI Quote Automation & Order Entry: The Engine That Transforms Quoting from Hours to Minutes

### Executive Summary


**Distro’s AI Quote Automation & Order Entry is more than quoting automation. It’s a generative quoting engine purpose-built for distributors for real-time supply chain and procurement visibility. It transforms chaotic RFQs into structured, supply-chain-validated quotes faster with more accurately and with instantly actionable data.** By connecting contractor requests directly into ERP and supplier networks, our AI Quote Automation & Order Entry tool compresses hours of manual quoting into minutes, while protecting margins and strengthening trust across the value chain.


## Why Are RFQs So Difficult in Distribution?


RFQs are mission-critical. Contractors need the **exact parts, systems, or accessories** at the right price and the right time. Yet requests often arrive fragmented and incomplete:


-


PDFs, spreadsheets, or emails missing key details.


-


Handwritten notes or images requiring manual interpretation.


-


Long, complex bills of material that require line-by-line validation.


Distributors spend hours manually retyping, cross-checking, and calling suppliers. The result: **delays, higher error rates, and eroded margins.**


## Why Procurement Automation and Supply Chain Visibility Change the Game


Quoting isn’t just about price. It’s about confidence in **availability, accuracy, and speed.** Our AI Quote Automation & Order Entry tool applies generative and procurement automation principles to quoting, delivering a structured, procurement-aware engine:


-


**RFQ Structuring (Procurement Data Capture)**


Our tool digitizes and normalizes contractor RFQs in any formation (PDF, email, spreadsheet, handwritten, etc), just as procurement tools ingest fragmented supplier data—eliminating hours of re-entry.


-


**AI-Driven Enrichment (Supplier Data Intelligence)**


Every line item is enriched with specs, codes, and compliance data, mirroring supplier master-data enrichment in procurement platforms.


-


**Live ERP + Supplier Integration (Procurement Visibility)**


Real-time supplier and ERP data ensures that every quote reflects **actual inventory, lead times, and landed costs** —not stale spreadsheets.


-


**Documentation Automation (Procurement Compliance)**


Like procurement systems that package compliance documentation, our tool automatically attaches warranties, certifications, and installation guides.


-


**Continuous Learning (Supplier & Buyer Behavioral Insights)**


Our AI Quote Automation & Order Entry tool learns from reps, suppliers, and contractor preferences, adapting quoting behavior just as procurement platforms adapt sourcing strategies
