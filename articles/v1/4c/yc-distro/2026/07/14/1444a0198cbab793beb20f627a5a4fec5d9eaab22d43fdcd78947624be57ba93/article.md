# Cracking the Quote Code: How Quoting Automation Transforms HVAC/R RFQs

In HVAC/R distribution, winning jobs comes down to one thing: speed and accuracy in quoting. Whether it’s rooftop units for a commercial build or compressors for a service repair, your ability to provide clear, competitive RFQs (Requests for Quotation) is what keeps contractors coming back and what protects your margins.


But let’s be honest: RFQs in HVAC/R are messy. Multiple SKUs, spec sheets buried in PDFs, inconsistent formats, and tight timelines make quoting feel like a guessing game. Many distributors still rely on manual processing of takeoffs and retyping requests, which slows teams down and eats away at margins.


That’s where a sharper approach powered by Distro’s AI quoting automation platform makes all the difference. This guide breaks down how to streamline your RFQ process, eliminate costly mistakes, and deliver quotes that convert.


## Decoding RFQs and Takeoffs in HVAC/R Distribution


Unlike a broad RFP, an RFQ is about specifics. Contractors need *the exact part, system, or accessory* that fits their project at the right price, in the right timeframe. A vague or incomplete RFQ wastes time, creates friction, and ultimately leads to lost business.


The challenge? RFQs rarely arrive neatly packaged. Contractors send requests as PDFs, spreadsheets, emails, photos of handwritten notes, or even scribbles on cardboard. Distributors spend hours doing manual Takeoffs, cross-checking SKUs, and chasing manufacturers for details.


This is where Distro steps in. Our platform automatically reads contractor requests in any format, extracts line items, and matches them to your ERP inventory. No more manual Takeoffs. No more missed items. Just clean, structured data that’s ready to quote — powered by Distro’s AI for distribution.


## How Quoting Automation Builds a Better RFQ


A well-structured RFQ does more than request pricing, it reduces ambiguity, speeds responses, and builds trust. Here’s what every HVAC/R RFQ should include, and how Distro’s quoting automation makes it happen:


1.


**Project & Contractor Information**


-


Jobsite, scope, and contact details.


-


Distro automatically captures this info from emails or file attachments, so nothing gets lost.


2.


**Scope of Work & Specifications**


-


Equipment requirements (e.g., tonnage, SEER rating, refrigerant type).


-


Accessories (filters, linesets, controls).


-


Distro enriches each line with spec sheets, warranty details, and AI-generated product descriptions for clarity.


3.


**Project Schedule & Deadlines**


-


Bid submission and delivery windows.


-


Distro connects directly to your ERP to instantly confirm availability and lead times.


4.


**Additional Requirements**


-


Certifications, insurance, or warranties.


-


Distro attaches relevant product docs (AHRI ratings, installation guides) automatically. No manual digging required.
