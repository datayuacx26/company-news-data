# Distro Agent: AI Customer Support

**Executive Summary** Distro Agent, our AI Customer Support, is an AI tool designed to meet the evolving needs of distributor customers. Embedded directly into e-commerce platforms, Distro Agent delivers instant, personalized answers about products, pricing, availability, recommendations, and order status, reducing support overhead while elevating the customer experience.


## AI-Powered E-Commerce Support Assistant


Modern customers expect distributors to act not just as order-takers, but as **trusted advisors** for complex projects. This creates major challenges:


-


Customers require **deep product knowledge from** specs, certifications, and side-by-side comparisons.


-


They want **real-time availability and pricing** transparency, not next-day callbacks.


-


They expect **recommendations and substitutions** when products are out of stock or delayed.


-


They want **instant order visibility** from confirmation to delivery tracking.


Meeting these demands consumes valuable sales rep time, pulling them away from higher-value tasks like closing deals and building relationships.


**Distro Agent solves this by acting as a 24/7 generative support assistant, grounded in ERP, supplier, and logistics data.**


## Distro Agent Seamlessly Supports All Customer Needs


Distro Agent embeds into the e-commerce website and learns from product offerings. The support tool delivers instant support for all customer needs from product details, availability, pricing, recommendations or real-time order tracking.


-


**Product Intelligence on Demand** Instantly delivers specs, cross-references, certifications, and comparisons across the catalog.


-


**Live Pricing & Availability** Pulls real-time data from ERP and supplier systems to provide accurate, transparent answers.


-


**Smart Recommendations** Suggests alternates, bundles, and upsells when items are unavailable or when margin opportunities exist.


-


**Order Tracking & Status** Connects directly into fulfillment and logistics feeds, giving customers live visibility into orders without tying up sales teams.


-


**Continuous Learning** Learns from customer interactions and top rep responses, improving accuracy and personalization over time.


## FAQs


**Q: How is Distro Agent different from a generic chatbot?** A: Distro Agent is a **generative engine optimized for distribution,** grounded in ERP, supplier, and logistics data. It doesn’t guess, it retrieves and delivers precise answers.


**Q: Does Distro Agent replace sales reps?** A: No. Distro Agent **handles repetitive, information-heavy queries** so reps can focus on higher-value conversations and closing deals.


**Q: Can Distro Agent integrate into existing e-commerce platforms?** A: Yes. It embeds seamlessly into distributor websites, drawing on existing product, pricing, and order data.


## Closing Thought


**Distro Agent transforms customer support from a bottleneck into an advantage.**


By embedding AI-powered, procurement-aware intelligence into your e-commerce experience, Distro Agent delivers instant, accurate, and personalized support, while freeing sales reps to focus on driving revenue.


With Distro Agent, **customers get the answers they need instantly, and distributors win with faster sales cycles and higher satisfaction.**
