# Distro Agent: AI Sales Assistant for Faster, Smarter Distributor Sales

**Executive Summary** Distro Agent, our AI sales assistant, is purpose-built for distributor sales teams, integrating with the ERP. It combines live product data, supplier availability, and AI-powered knowledge retention to help reps sell faster, smarter, and with greater confidence.


With Distro Agent, every sales rep has expert-level knowledge at their fingertips: detailed specs, cross-references, availability, and pricing, backed by real-time supply chain data. The AI platform continuously learns from top performers, scaling expertise across the entire sales force and accelerating deal velocity.


## Why Generative AI is Needed for Sales Reps


The modern distributor sales landscape is increasingly complex:


-


Industry consolidation means reps must cover broader product portfolios of hundreds or thousands of SKUs.


-


Constantly changing specs, pricing, and availability are impossible to track manually.


-


Time spent on research reduces selling time and erodes customer experience.


Without automation, reps spend more time searching for information than closing deals.


## How Distro Agent Accelerates the Sales Lifecycle


Distro Agent acts as a procurement-aware sales assistant:


-


**Instant Product Intelligence**
Retrieves specs, certifications, and cross-references in seconds, always validated against ERP and supplier systems.


-


**Smart Selling Guidance** Suggests upsells, bundles, and accessories that maximize margins, based on historical buyer behavior and supply chain context.


-


**Next-Best-Action Coaching** Guides reps toward next best actions, such as quote creation, order follow-ups, or proactive outreach


-


**Knowledge Preservation & Onboarding** Captures the expertise of top reps and feeds it into the engine, ensuring new hires ramp faster and veterans always sell like experts.


-


**Continuous Learning** Adapts to customer preferences, market shifts, and rep behavior allowing Distro Agent to get smarter with every interaction.
