# Plan & Spec: Transforming Project Specs into Quoting-Ready Material Lists - Distro

**Executive Summary Plan & Spec is a generative, procurement-aware AI tool that transforms messy contractor drawings into structured, quoting-ready material lists.** By grounding outputs directly in ERP and supplier catalogs, Plan & Spec eliminates manual takeoff work, reduces errors, and accelerates bid cycles, empowering estimators and sales reps to quote complex projects with speed, accuracy, and confidence.


# Why Project Takeoffs Are Still A Bottleneck in Distribution


After project are finalized, distributors must work across contractor drawings, specs and project documents to procure material lists. The more complex the project, the higher the risk of:


-


Errors from manual entry, counting and data re-entry


-


Delays in bid turnarounds


-


Margin erosion due to misquotes


## Automatic and Instant Transformation from Drawings to Procurement


-


**Direct ERP Integration** Project specs are instantly normalized into ERP catalog SKUs, ensuring precise and quoting-ready line items.


-


**Elimination of Manual Work** No more measuring, counting, or data re-entry. Plan & Spec reduces rework and costly errors by automating the entire takeoff process.


-


**Accelerated Bid Cycles** Move from drawings to quoting in minutes, delivering consistent, accurate takeoffs across multiple projects.


-


**Empowered Estimators & Sales Reps** Handle large, complex jobs faster, supported by intelligence that preserves best practices and continuously learns from prior bids.


## FAQs


**Q: How does Plan & Spec ensure accuracy?** A: Every extracted line item is grounded in ERP and supplier catalogs, validated with specs, and matched to availability in real time.


**Q: Will Plan & Spec replace estimators?** A: No. Plan & Spec augments estimators and reps, removing repetitive tasks so they can focus on strategy, pricing, and customer relationships.


**Q: Can Plan & Spec adapt to different drawing formats?** A: Yes. The generative engine ingests PDFs, CAD outputs, images, and other formats—standardizing inputs into structured takeoff lists.
