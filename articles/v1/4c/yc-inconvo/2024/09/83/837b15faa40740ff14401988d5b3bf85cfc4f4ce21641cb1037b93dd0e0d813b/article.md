# Inconvo + Y Combinator

We’re rethinking the future of conversational analytics, shaping how it evolves from 2023 onwards. With YC as our partner, we’re positioned perfectly to turn this vision into reality.


### What’s Inconvo again?


Inconvo is a conversational analysis API for developers. You can think of it as the AI version of Metabase.


### Why YC?


YC is the best startup accelerator out there. Exceptional API first-companies like Stripe, Resend, and Recall all came out of it.


Liam and I participated in the Summer ‘23 batch and it was a privilege to be a part of this program with an amazing group of founders and partners.


YC gave us the tools we needed to keep building fast and over the long term. This is just the beginning and we’re looking forward to sharing every step of the way with you all.
