# Inconvo is joining Attio

Two years ago, we started Inconvo to make one thing easy: getting answers from your data by just asking, in plain language. We built it in the open and learned a huge amount from the developers who helped shape it.


Somewhere along the way, one thing became really clear to us. Natural language access to data isn’t a nice add-on. It’s infrastructure, and it belongs right at the centre of the tools teams use every day.


That’s why we’re excited to share that we’re joining[Attio](https://attio.com/) , the agentic CRM.


We’ve used and loved Attio long before we joined. It has the developer-first platform and the data infrastructure to make CRM useful, not just a cool demo. And when we spent time with the team in London, what struck us was how much they care, both about hard engineering problems and the people who rely on the product. That’s the kind of team we want to build with.


We think AI agents are going to become a core part of how teams work with customer data. Now we get to help shape what that looks like, on a platform engineered for it.


To our customers, and everyone who helped shape Inconvo, thank you. Inconvo has been sunset, and if you have any questions or need support with migration, pleasereach out .
