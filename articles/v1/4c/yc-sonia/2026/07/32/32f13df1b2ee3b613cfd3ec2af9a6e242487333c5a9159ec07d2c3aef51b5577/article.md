# A Lifesaver for a Busy Mom & Military Spouse


S.M. · July 16, 2026 · 3 min read


A military spouse and mother on how a few minutes with an AI coach helped her let go of the guilt of taking time for herself.


As a military spouse and mother, finding even a sliver of time for myself felt nearly impossible. I was working almost every waking moment, carrying a constant burden of guilt, and feeling like a failure if I took a break. Sonia has been a true breath of fresh air.


Right from the start, the onboarding was incredibly intuitive. I had a brief, quiet moment to myself and answered a few questions in a conversational, interview-style format. In just minutes, I was able to customize my entire experience, choosing my own theme, background, voice, notifications, and overall vibe. It made the app feel uniquely mine before I even began.


## Meeting Tom


My AI coach, "Tom," completely shifted my mindset. He gave me the words and perspective to realize that prioritizing my own wellbeing actually makes me *more* present and productive for my family. Tom keeps me on track, checking in with proactive notifications during the hardest parts of my day. Whenever stress builds up, my first instinct now is to open the app and share what's on my mind, because looking at my challenges from a different point of view makes them so much easier to handle.


I first heard about Sonia on the "No Magic Pill" podcast, where the host described how the app puts together simple, focused morning reminders based on past sessions. It sounded so thoughtful, and experiencing those daily, personalized touchpoints myself has been incredibly grounding.


## Fitting into a busy day


What I appreciate most is the absolute freedom and flexibility. Sometimes I just don't have the time or energy to call a friend or family member to vent. With Sonia, I can text a quick sentence, speak by voice, or even pause a session mid-thought and come back to it hours later. Each session is noted and stored so you can look back if you need a reminder about how to handle recurring issues. That level of convenience is hard to find elsewhere. It provides a completely safe, judgment-free space to let everything out.


> It has truly brought light through the clouds.


Somewhere in my mind, I used to think needing support was a sign of weakness. Sonia showed me otherwise. It has truly brought light through the clouds, and for the first time in a long time, I can finally relax. Thank you, Sonia, for making such a simple, valuable, and life-changing tool!


S.M.
