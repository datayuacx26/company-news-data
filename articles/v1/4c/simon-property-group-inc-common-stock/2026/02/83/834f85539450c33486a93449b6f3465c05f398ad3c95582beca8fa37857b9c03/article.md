# Simon Property Group Announces David Simon's Resignation from Klépierre Supervisory Board

INDIANAPOLIS


, Feb. 20, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Simon®, a real estate investment trust engaged in the ownership of premier shopping, dining, entertainment and mixed-use destinations, announced today that David Simon has resigned from the Klépierre S.A. Supervisory Board effective February 19, 2026.

Our properties across North America, Europe and Asia provide community gathering places for millions of people every day and generate billions in annual sales.
