# Simon® to Present at Citi's 2026 Global Property CEO Conference

INDIANAPOLIS


, Feb. 26, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Simon](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4628285-1&h=1309665058&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.simon.com%2F&a=Simon) ®, a real estate investment trust engaged in the ownership of premier shopping, dining, entertainment and mixed-use destinations, announced today that the Company will present at Citi's 2026 Global Property CEO Conference on Tuesday, March 3, 2026 at 3:35 p.m. Eastern Time.


A live audio webcast of the presentation will be accessible from the Investors section of the Company's website at[investors.simon.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4628285-1&h=2865200131&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.simon.com%2F&a=investors.simon.com) . An online replay will be available following the presentation at the same location.
