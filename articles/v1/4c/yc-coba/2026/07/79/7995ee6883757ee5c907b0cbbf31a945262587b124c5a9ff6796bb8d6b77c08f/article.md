# Wyoming LLC paying contractors in Mexico: what to check

A Wyoming LLC can be a simple US entity structure for a founder. Paying contractors in Mexico from that company is a different question.


The payment workflow has to handle US funding, MXN delivery, contractor expectations, and clean records for bookkeeping.


This is especially important when the team is paid on a recurring schedule.


## Checklist before paying contractors in Mexico


Before running the first payroll batch, confirm:


- the legal name of the US company;
- who controls and approves payments;
- contractor names and bank-account details;
- whether each contractor should receive MXN or USD;
- how the FX conversion is approved;
- what payment proof the contractor receives;
- how the payment will appear in bookkeeping;
- who handles follow-up if a payment is delayed.


The more recurring the contractor list is, the more valuable it is to turn this into a template.

## Keep the records simple


When a US company pays Mexico contractors, finance usually needs to know:


- the original USD funding amount;
- the MXN amount received;
- the FX rate or spread context;
- the contractor or invoice reference;
- the proof of payment;
- the date funds were released and received.


If those details are scattered across messages and bank screens, payroll becomes harder to audit later.
