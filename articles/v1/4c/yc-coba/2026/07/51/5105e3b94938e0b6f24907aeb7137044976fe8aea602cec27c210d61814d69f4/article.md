# Batch payments for Mexico contractors: a founder checklist

If you pay the same Mexico contractors every week or every month, the payment flow should not depend on rebuilding a spreadsheet every time.


Batch payments are useful because they turn recurring contractor payroll into an operating process: who gets paid, how much, in what currency, with what approval, and with what proof.


## What belongs in a contractor-payment batch


A useful batch template should include:


- contractor name;
- legal or invoice reference;
- receiving bank details;
- MXN or USD payment instruction;
- recurring amount or variable amount field;
- approver;
- proof destination;
- accounting reference.


The goal is not to make payroll complicated. The goal is to avoid improvising when everyone is waiting for money.


## Before you click approve


Run a quick payroll check:


1. Are all contractor bank details current?
2. Are new contractors separated from recurring contractors?
3. Is the FX rule clear for this pay cycle?
4. Does someone know who receives payment proof?
5. Can finance reconcile this payment next month without asking operations?
6. Is there a backup approver if payroll happens outside normal hours?


This checklist matters more as the team grows.


## Why payment status matters


For contractors, silence creates anxiety. If the payment is moving but not yet visible, a status update or proof trail can reduce the number of follow-up messages.


For the company, that visibility also helps separate a real payment issue from a normal processing window.
