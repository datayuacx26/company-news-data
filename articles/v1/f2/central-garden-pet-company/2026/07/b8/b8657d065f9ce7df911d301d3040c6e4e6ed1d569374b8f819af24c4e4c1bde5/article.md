# Central Garden & Pet to Participate in Investor Conferences


July 30, 2026 8:55am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_4d4faa60e1800aad1bd0a433694c9000/central/news/2026-07-30_Central_Garden_Pet_to_Participate_in_Investor_487.pdf)


WALNUT CREEK, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Central Garden & Pet Company (NASDAQ: CENT) (NASDAQ: CENTA), a leading consumer goods company in the pet and garden industries, today announced that it will participate in the following upcoming investor conferences:


- **CG 46th Annual Growth Conference (August 11)** *Boston, Mass.* – Niko Lahanas, Chief Executive Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at 10:30 a.m. ET. Senior management **** will also host one-on-one meetings with institutional investors.


- **Barclays 19th Annual Global Consumer Conference (September 10)**
*Boston, Mass.* – Brad Smith, Chief Financial Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at 8:15 a.m. ET **.** Senior management will also host one-on-one meetings with institutional investors.


To schedule meetings with Central, please contact your respective institutional sales representative.


Webcast links and related materials will be available on Central’s Investor Relations website at[https://ir.central.com/](https://ir.central.com/) .


**About Central Garden & Pet**


Central Garden & Pet Company (NASDAQ: CENT) (NASDAQ: CENTA) is a leading consumer goods company in the pet and garden industries. Guided by the belief that home is central to life, the company's purpose is to proudly nurture happy and healthy homes. For over 45 years, its innovative and trusted solutions have helped lawns grow greener, gardens bloom bigger, pets live healthier, and communities grow stronger. Central is home to a diversified portfolio of market-leading brands including Amdro®, Aqueon®, Best Bully Sticks®, Cadet®, C&S®, Farnam®, Ferry-Morse®, Kaytee®, Nylabone®, Pennington®, Sevin® and Zoëcon®. With fiscal 2025 net sales of $3.1 billion, the company has strong manufacturing and logistics capabilities supported by a passionate, entrepreneurial growth culture that incorporates sustainability. Central is headquartered in Walnut Creek, California, and employs over 6,000 people, primarily across North America. Visit[www.central.com](http://www.central.com/) to learn more.
