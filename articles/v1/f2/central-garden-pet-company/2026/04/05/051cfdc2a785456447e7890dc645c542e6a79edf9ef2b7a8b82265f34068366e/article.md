# ‘Home Improvement’ Star Richard Karn Returns to DoMyOwn as ‘Chief Solutions Officer,’ Continuing to Help Homeowners Take Charge of Their Homes — This Time Through Pest Control

ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[DoMyOwn.com](https://www.domyown.com/) , the leading online retailer for professional-grade do-it-yourself pest control and lawn care products, and part of the[Central Garden & Pet Company](https://www.central.com/) portfolio, announces the continuation of its partnership with television personality and DIY home improvement expert Richard Karn, who returns for a second year as the brand’s Chief Solutions Officer.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260331425779/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260331425779/en/)


DIY home improvement expert Richard Karn returns for a second year as DoMyOwn’s Chief Solutions Officer to help DIYers tackle pest problems.


After a successful first year helping homeowners simplify lawn care, Karn’s focus in year two shifts to one of the most common homeowner challenges: pest control. As homeowners prepare for peak spring and summer pest activity, Karn’s continues his work with DoMyOwn to help DIYers of all experience levels feel ready to tackle pest problems.


Through a new set of engaging videos, Karn demonstrates that pest control can be simple and more achievable than many expect. The content introduces DoMyOwn’s pest control solutions and reinforces that, with the right products and support, many pest issues can be handled with ease.


For many homeowners, pest control begins with uncertainty – identifying the pest, choosing the right treatment, and knowing where to begin. DoMyOwn’s research found that 53% of consumers researched pest solutions before making a purchase, highlighting the need for clearer guidance and trusted expertise throughout the process.


Karn’s trusted, approachable style pairs naturally with DoMyOwn’s pest control know-how. Known to generations of viewers for his role on the hit television show *Home Improvement* , Karn remains a relatable guide for homeowners seeking practical ways to care for their homes.


“Nobody wants ants in the kitchen or mosquitoes taking over their backyard,” Karn explains. “Pest control can sound intimidating at first, but the good news is that DoMyOwn really helps simplify it. They make it easy to find the right products and how to use them, so you quickly realize, ‘Hey, I can do this.’ I’m excited to keep working with DoMyOwn to help people protect their homes and enjoy their spaces again.”


In year two of the partnership, Karn continues helping homeowners confidently solve pest problems with DoMyOwn. From dealing with ants, mosquitoes and roaches to preventing termites and controlling seasonal pests, DoMyOwn customers have access to the same professional-grade products used by the pros, along with a robust library of how-to videos, articles and support from the company’s knowledgeable customer care team. DoMyOwn also offers fast, free shipping, making it easier for customers to get what they need delivered to their door.


“Richard’s ability to make home improvement approachable has resonated strongly with our customers,” said Jill Pierce, Senior Director of Consumer Care for DoMyOwn. “His continued partnership helps us deliver on our mission to empower homeowners with the knowledge and professional-grade products they need to confidently manage pest control challenges around their homes.”


DoMyOwn offers a broad range of products, services, and expert support to help homeowners identify, manage, and prevent pests such as ants, mosquitoes, roaches, rodents, and termites—supporting comfortable, pest-free living spaces. With DoMyOwn, homeowners and professionals alike can access the tools and resources needed to implement effective pest control strategies year-round.
