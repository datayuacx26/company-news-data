# SiriusPoint announces new partnership with ISC

# SiriusPoint announces new partnership with ISC


-


01/15/26


SiriusPoint has announced a new strategic partnership with Integrated Specialty Coverages (ISC). ISC is a multi-line program administrator, committed to delivering tailored programs across the broker and carrier markets.


SiriusPoint has partnered with ISC to support the continued expansion of ISC’s exclusive and wholesale insurance programs, providing underwriting capacity and strategic collaboration across specialty lines. The partnership focuses on ISC’s flagship general liability program, which is designed to provide cost-effective insurance solutions for small contractors such as landscapers and roofers.


Patrick Charles, Head of North America Insurance at SiriusPoint said: “This partnership supports our strategy of working with established MGAs which are building sustainable, specialist businesses with a focus on excellent underwriting, strong back office support, clear distribution strategies and aligned cultures…”
