# SiriusPoint Strengthens Crisis Solutions Team with Appointment of Ella Stevenson

# SiriusPoint Strengthens Crisis Solutions Team with Appointment of Ella Stevenson


-


06/24/26


SiriusPoint has announced the appointment of Ella Stevenson as Crisis Solutions Underwriter, effective September 28, 2026.


Ella will be based in London and will join SiriusPoint’s newly formed Crisis Solutions team, led by Joint Heads Ed Winter and Paul Beattie. She will support the underwriting and development of SiriusPoint’s Crisis Solutions portfolio.


Ella joins SiriusPoint from Chaucer Group, where she most recently held the role of Underwriter within the Political Violence and Terrorism team.
