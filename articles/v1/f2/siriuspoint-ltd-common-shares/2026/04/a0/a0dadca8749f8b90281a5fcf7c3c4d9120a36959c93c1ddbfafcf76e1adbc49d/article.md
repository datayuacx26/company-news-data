# Scott Egan on The Voice of Insurance podcast: progress, discipline and what comes next

# Scott Egan on The Voice of Insurance podcast: progress, discipline and what comes next


-


04/14/26


SiriusPoint CEO Scott Egan returned to The Voice of Insurance podcast for a conversation with Mark Geoghegan, reflecting on the company’s results, the mindset behind performance, and focus for the year ahead.


Scott spoke candidly about the importance of continual improvement, disciplined underwriting, and building momentum across the business. He also discussed SiriusPoint’s evolution beyond turnaround, progress on earnings quality and growth, and the mindset guiding capital deployment, partnerships, and culture.


> “What we’re trying to create here is a brilliant business… We’re a business that’s growing, we’re a business that’s improving our profitability… The part I’m most proud of is the people that are here every single day who have risen to that challenge and I couldn’t be prouder of them.”


The full episode also covers SiriusPoint’s evolving global structure including the London Market Specialty division, approach to MGAs and programs, views on market conditions, and Scott’s perspective on AI as an evolutionary rather than revolutionary tool for the industry.
