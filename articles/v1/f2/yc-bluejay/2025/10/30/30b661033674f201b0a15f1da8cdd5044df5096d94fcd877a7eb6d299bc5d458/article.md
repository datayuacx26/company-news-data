# Bluejay x Twilio x Rime x Groq: Halloween Voice AI Meetup Made with Restream Studio

[Bluejay (YC X25)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/get-bluejay/) hosted another incredible gathering of builders and thinkers in Voice AI alongside[Rime](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rime-ai/) ,[Twilio](https://www.linkedin.com/company/twilio-inc-/) , and[Groq](https://www.linkedin.com/company/groq/) , and graciously hosted by[SVB](https://www.linkedin.com/company/svb-financial-group/) .


Leaders from across the industry shared candid insights on building and scaling Voice AI systems, featuring:


[Punn Kam](https://www.linkedin.com/in/punnkam/) Co-founder,[Conduit AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/useconduitai/)
[Lily Clifford](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lilyjclifford/) CEO,[Rime](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rime-ai/)
[Aarush Sah](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aarushsah/) Head of Evals,[Groq](https://www.linkedin.com/company/groq/)
[Ankit Jain](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jainankit/) CEO,[Infinitus Systems, Inc.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/infinitusai/)
[Lauren Jo Sypniewski](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lauren-jo-sypniewski-bb998375/) VP of Data Operations,[Deepgram](https://www.linkedin.com/company/deepgram/)
[Faraz Siddiqi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/farazmsiddiqi/) , CTO,[Bluejay (YC X25)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/get-bluejay/)


🔑 Highlights from the discussion:
1. The toughest early technical challenges in bringing Voice AI products from zero to one — and what didn’t scale (but still had to be done)
2. Choosing and optimizing the right model stack for low-latency voice experiences
3. Preventing performance regressions when updating system prompts


The evening wrapped up with live Q&A, some open-mic stories, and in the spirit of Halloween, a few scary Voice AI horror stories from the panelists and how they conquered them. 🎃


Huge thanks to everyone who joined, and to our friends at[Rime](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rime-ai/) ,[Twilio](https://www.linkedin.com/company/twilio-inc-/) ,[Groq](https://www.linkedin.com/company/groq/) , and[SVB](https://www.linkedin.com/company/svb-financial-group/) for helping make it such a special night.
