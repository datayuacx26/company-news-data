# Diving Deep into Rime and TTS with CEO Lily Clifford

🚀 New Skywatch Episode Just Dropped!


Today I sat down with[Lily Clifford](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lilyjclifford/) , CEO & Co-Founder of[Rime](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rime-ai/) , one of the teams pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in text-to-speech and conversational AI.


Rime is building linguistically-grounded TTS models. Their work is already reshaping industries like fast food, support, and agentic voice automation.


In this episode, Lily and I dive into:
🔹 Rime’s path from research to real-world deployment
🔹 How they’re powering next-gen conversational agents in high-volume industries
🔹 The hardest challenges in TTS (latency, emotion, prosody, multilingual alignment)
