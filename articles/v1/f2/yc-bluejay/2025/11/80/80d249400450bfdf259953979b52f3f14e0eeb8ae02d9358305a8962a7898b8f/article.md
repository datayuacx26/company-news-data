# From Text to Lifelike Speech: Cartesia's Co-founder Arjun Desai On Voice and AI Agents

Today I’m proud to announce that my car has been promoted. It is no longer just a car. It is now… a podcast studio.


Introducing Skywatch —[Bluejay (YC X25)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/get-bluejay/) 's new podcast that takes a bird's eye view on conversational AI filmed inside my very real, very un-soundproof vehicle while driving around SF.


For episode one, we welcomed[Arjun Desai](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arjun-desai-4a731ba4/) , co-founder of[Cartesia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cartesia-ai/) to hop in the passenger seat and talk about building insanely fast, insanely high-quality voice systems… while I try not to hit a cyclist.


We covered:
🔹 Why Sonic 3 actually changes the game in voice AI
🔹 What “first-principles thinking” looks like when you’re shipping real models
🔹 The trade-offs behind speed, quality, and realism
🔹 The future of voice agents and what still needs to be solved
