# Building Tomorrow's Infra With LiveKit: A New Era In Computing

You know what’s better than a car podcast interviewing one[LiveKit](https://www.linkedin.com/company/livekitco/) founder? Interviewing both of them. 😄🚗🎙️


Today I’m excited to drop the newest episode of Skywatch —[Bluejay (YC X25)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/get-bluejay/) 's podcast taking a bird’s-eye view on conversational AI, filmed inside my car while driving around SF.


This time, I had both LiveKit co-founders,[Russ d'Sa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/russelldsa/) and[David Zhao](https://www.linkedin.com/in/davidzhao/) , and also my own co-founder[Faraz Siddiqi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/farazmsiddiqi/) hop in and unpack what it actually takes to build and scale real-time infrastructure powering modern voice and agentic systems.


We went deep on things you've never heard publicly:


🔹 Never-told stories from early LiveKit deployments and the real scaling challenges behind production voice + real-time AI
🔹 The founders’ journeys — from[Y Combinator](https://www.linkedin.com/company/y-combinator/) startups to building LiveKit
🔹 Where LiveKit is headed — and what a new era of real-time computing means for AI


If you’re building in voice, real-time infra, or agentic systems — you’ll love this one.
