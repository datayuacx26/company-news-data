# Inside Infinitus: CEO Ankit Jain on Guardrailed LLMs, Healthcare Compliance, and AI Agents

Join Rohan Vasishth on the Skywatch Podcast as he sits down with Ankit Jain, Co-founder and CEO of Infinitus, to unpack what it really takes to deploy AI agents in healthcare — where “pretty good” isn’t good enough. In this episode, Ankit walks through his journey from early Silicon Valley startups to Google (and launching Gradient Ventures), and the moment that sparked Infinitus: realizing the most powerful voice technology shouldn’t be reserved for spa reservations — it should fix the broken workflows in healthcare. They dive into why healthcare is adopting AI fastest in the back office first, what “clinical” actually means in practice, and how Infinitus built a compliance-first architecture that evolved from fine-tuned small models and intent classification to more flexible LLM-driven conversations. Rohan and Ankit also explore the core tension in regulated voice AI: how to preserve the “magic” of natural conversation while enforcing guardrails that must be 100% compliant. They discuss why many teams get stuck at ~96% without middleware, why outcomes-based pricing may beat per-minute billing, and what it will take for AI agents to move from cost containment to real revenue-driving utility. A must-listen for founders, operators, and engineers building agentic systems in regulated industries — and anyone who wants the honest version of “AI agents everywhere” once the hype wears off.
