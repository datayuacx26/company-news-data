# Coastal States Bank Announces Jamie DeLoach as Effingham Market President

SAVANNAH, GA / RINCON, GA (April 9, 2026) – Coastal States Bank (“CSB”), a community bank located in Savannah, has announced its expansion into Effingham County with the appointment of Jamie DeLoach as Effingham Market President.


DeLoach brings more than 15 years of banking experience to CSB. With an extensive background in commercial lending, business development and client services, he has helped businesses navigate their finances with tailored banking solutions to support business growth. As Effingham Market President, DeLoach will focus on growing client relationships and expanding the bank’s services throughout the local market.


“We’re pleased to bring our local banking model to Effingham under Jamie’s leadership as we continue serving businesses and individuals across our region,” said Brian Smith, CSB’s Coastal Regional President. “Jamie values the importance of community and relationship banking, and at CSB, we believe strong communities are built on trusted partnerships.”


DeLoach is actively involved in the community, serving as a county commissioner for Effingham County and a member of the Effingham Exchange Club and the Effingham Health System Foundation Board, where he currently serves as Vice Chair.

**About the Company**


Coastal States Bank builds relationships by providing a full range of banking services designed for businesses, their owners and individuals looking for a local banking partner. CSB has a community banking presence in Savannah, the Lowcountry of South Carolina (Hilton Head Island, Bluffton and Beaufort) and the Atlanta Metropolitan market and serves communities across the country through its Government Guaranteed Lending, Senior Housing, Correspondent Marine and Mortgage Banker Finance lines of business. At present, CSB has approximately $2.3 billion in total assets.


CSB is a wholly-owned subsidiary of CoastalSouth Bancshares, Inc. (NYSE: COSO).
