# Georgia CEO Feature: What Coastal States Bank’s IPO Means for Georgia Businesses

**Why the Right Banking Partner Matters**


For business owners and leaders, the right banking partner can make a meaningful difference in how a company grows. Businesses need a local partner who understands their goals, industry and the importance of relationship-based banking.


For more than 20 years, Coastal States Bank has supported businesses across Georgia and South Carolina through strong, local relationships. Following our recent IPO and listing on the New York Stock Exchange, we have strengthened our capital position—expanding our ability to provide financing that supports businesses' operating needs, real estate investments, and long-term growth.


Even with this growth, local decision-making remains at the center of every client relationship at CSB. Businesses continue to work with experienced bankers who understand their markets and the realities of running a company in Georgia.
