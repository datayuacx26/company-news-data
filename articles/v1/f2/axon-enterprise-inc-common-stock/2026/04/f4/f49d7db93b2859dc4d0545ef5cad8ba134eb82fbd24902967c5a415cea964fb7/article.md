# A deep dive into Axon's unified AI platform

## *Announcing the latest advancements from Axon Week 2026*


Axon’s unified AI platform connects data, devices, and workflows across the public safety ecosystem, transforming how information is understood and acted upon in real time. These new and expanded AI-powered capabilities are designed to reduce complexity, surface what matters and help agencies respond with greater clarity and speed.


## Announcing Axon Vision: From Visibility to Understanding


As policing becomes increasingly digitized, agencies are now responsible for managing more video than teams can realistically digest, leaving critical moments buried in hours of footage. Too often, video is only used after the fact, missing key moments to act sooner. Axon Vision shifts video to real-time awareness, helping teams recognize what matters in time to act. Axon Vision addresses this gap by analyzing live camera feeds and surfacing specific, relevant activity as it happens.


**What is Axon Vision and how does it work?**


Axon Vision uses computer vision models embedded within Fusus to recognize observable activity such as physical altercations, unauthorized access, medical emergencies, or hazardous conditions. These events are detected through predefined detection types and appear as alerts within operator workflows for human review.


**How does Axon Vision improve public safety workflows?** ****


In environments with hundreds or thousands of cameras, it is impossible to monitor every feed in real time. Axon Vision turns large volumes of video into real-time awareness by surfacing relevant activity, helping agencies respond with better context. This reduces noise and enables faster, more focused response.


**Does Axon Vision use facial recognition or identify individuals?** ****


No. Axon Vision is designed to identify activity, not individuals. It does not perform facial recognition or biometric identification. All alerts require human review before action is taken. Detection criteria are defined and controlled to focus on observable, verifiable activity, and data remains within each agency’s environment. Axon continues to work with its Ethics and Equity Advisory Council to develop model documentation, validation processes and performance benchmarks that support transparency and accountability.


Learn more about[Axon Vision](https://www.axon.com/blog/rethinking-vision-building-systems-that-understand-not-just-see)


## Expanding Axon Assistant: Secure, Compliant AI Across the Entire Workflow


Axon Assistant is providing a secure and protected environment that public safety professionals can trust to leverage AI-driven workflows while keeping agency data secure within the boundary of a CJIS-compliant environment. Public safety professionals operate across multiple systems under time pressure, often needing to simultaneously gather information, interpret context and complete reporting. These workflows are often manual, fragmented and time-consuming.


**Is Axon Assistant secure?**


Axon Assistant operates in a CJIS-compliant environment. All interactions are authenticated and auditable to preserve evidentiary integrity. The system is designed to support human decision-making, ensuring that final actions remain with the officer.


**What is Axon Assistant and how does it work?**


Axon Assistant is a CJIS-compliant, AI-powered interface available across the Axon product line, including body-worn devices, mobile applications, Axon Evidence, and real-time operations platforms. It allows users to interact with data using natural language to retrieve information, generate reports, and analyze inputs in real time. Axon Assistant expands beyond its original voice-enabled capabilities on Axon Body 4 into a system that supports real-time analysis and workflow execution across platforms.


**How does Axon Assistant improve public safety workflows?**


Axon Assistant enables officers and analysts to move from question to answer instantly without switching systems. Queries persist across workflows, allowing users to begin a task in one environment and continue it in another without interruption.


Learn more about[Axon Assistant](https://www.axon.com/products/axon-assistant)


## Elevating Axon 911: Connecting the First Moment to the Full Response


The first and often most critical information about an incident comes from a 911 call, yet that data has historically been disconnected from downstream systems. Built on the acquisitions of Prepared and Carbyne, Axon 911 changes that.


**What is Axon 911 and how does it work?**


Axon 911 connects emergency call data directly into Axon’s ecosystem, including real-time operations, body-worn cameras, and evidence systems. Carbyne offers modern, cloud-native infrastructure for an AI-enabled future and Prepared's assistive intelligence helps amplify the abilities of our first responders.


**How does Axon 911 improve emergency response?**


Information from the initial call becomes immediately available across the response workflow, providing dispatchers, officers and analysts with shared, real-time context as an incident unfolds.


**Why is integrating 911 data important for public safety agencies?**


This reduces the gap between call intake and response, helping responders arrive on scene with better information and improving coordination and outcomes in critical situations.


**How is Axon 911 data kept secure and controlled?**


Access to 911 data is permission-based, controlled by the agency and fully auditable. Sensitive information is only available to authorized users and handled within secure, CJIS-compliant systems.
