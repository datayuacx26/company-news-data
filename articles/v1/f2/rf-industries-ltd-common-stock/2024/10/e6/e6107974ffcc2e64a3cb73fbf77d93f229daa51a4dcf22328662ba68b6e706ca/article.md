#### A New Era of Energy Efficiency and Virtuous Operational Synergies


– RF Industries (RFI) is carving out a strong position in the telecom industry by offering cutting-edge solutions to optimize energy efficiency and operational performance. Our Direct Air Cooling (DAC) ecosystems, developed by Schroff Technologies International, Inc. (a division of RFI) aim to significantly reduce energy consumption at telecom sites, enhance existing HVAC equipment longevity, and significantly reduce operational costs.


The DAC ecosystems have made significant progress in providing sustainable and complementary benefits alongside traditional HVAC systems. Over the past few years, RFI has successfully refined several cost-saving frameworks for its customers. By calculating the expected energy consumption savings and the extended HVAC lifespan, RFI can clearly demonstrate to customers the path to return on investment.
