# Humans of CXAI

##### *Episode 10: Behind the tech. Behind the change. Meet the humans of CXAI. Our behind-the-scenes series spotlighting the passionate people driving innovation at CXAI.*


# Meet Harpreet Singh


Meet Harpreet Singh, Senior Success Engineer at CXAI. Harpreet shares how he supports enterprise customers through deep technical integrations and day-to-day problem solving, with a constant focus on removing friction from the employee experience.


#### In this conversation, Harpreet shares:


-


Why his favorite CXAI “feature” is seamless access to workplace tools through secure authentication and integrations


-


A customer story where a virtual IT kiosk in the app helped eliminate hundreds of hours of IT tickets globally


-


What great workplace + employee experience looks like when content and information are easy to find and easy to use


-


Where he sees Agentic AI having the biggest impact by automating everyday workplace tasks and decisions
