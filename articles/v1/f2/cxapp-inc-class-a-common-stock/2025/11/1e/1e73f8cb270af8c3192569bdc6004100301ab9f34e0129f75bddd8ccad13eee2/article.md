# Humans of CXAI

##### *Episode 8: Behind the tech. Behind the change. Meet the humans of CXAI. Our behind-the-scenes series spotlighting the passionate people driving innovation at CXAI.*


# Meet Tyler


Meet Tyler, VP of Customer Success and Sales at CXAI. In this Humans of CXAI episode, Tyler shares how connecting with workplace leaders fuels his day, why flexibility matters in technology, and how AI is shaping the future of work.


####


#### What you’ll see:


-


A day in the life:


Tyler’s work revolves around conversations, connecting with workplace professionals across IT, real estate, and experience teams.


-


Beyond the role:


He reads up to 20 books a month, finding inspiration that shapes how he leads and connects with people.


-


Favorite feature:


From booking parking to finding meeting rooms, Tyler loves how CXAI creates a seamless, human-centered workplace experience.


-


Customer challenges:


CXAI cuts through digital friction and underused spaces by bringing all tools and data into one unified platform.


-


Transformation in action:


A customer leveraged CXAI to roll out a critical alert system, achieving over 95% adoption and stronger employee safety.


-


On misconceptions:


Workplace tech shouldn’t be one-size-fits-all, and CXAI adapts to every organization’s unique needs.


-


Looking ahead:


Tyler sees AI as the next big leap helping people focus on purpose and productivity while technology handles the rest.


-


CXAI’s promise:


To make the digital transformation of your workplace easy and efficient.
