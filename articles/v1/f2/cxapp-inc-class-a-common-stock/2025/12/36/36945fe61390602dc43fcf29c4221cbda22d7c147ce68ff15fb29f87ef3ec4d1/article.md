Hybrid work has become the norm across enterprises, but many organizations continue to see gaps between what they’ve invested in and the experience employees actually have day-to-day.


At CXAI, we recognize this as one of the biggest challenges facing IT, HR, CRE, and Workplace Experience today. The insights from Gartner ®

###
[The Challenge: Fragmented Workplace Technology](https://cxapp.com/gartner-report-enhance-employee-experience-with-adaptive-workplace-solutions)


Across the enterprise, workplace technology stacks have evolved quickly but unevenly. Employees often jump between:


Booking tools


Collaboration platforms


Visitor systems


Space management solutions


Communications tools


Workplace experience apps


According to Gartner ®


, this fragmentation is a major contributor to workplace friction and unmet employee expectations especially in hybrid environments.


At CXAI, we see this daily in conversations with customers:


complexity reduces engagement, lowers productivity, and obscures the data needed for real-time decision making.


###
[Why Adaptive Workplace Experiences Are Becoming Essential](https://cxapp.com/gartner-report-enhance-employee-experience-with-adaptive-workplace-solutions)


Gartner ®


outlines a framework for adaptive workplace experiences that evolve with an employee’s context - helping them navigate their day with less friction and more support. This includes:


### Anticipating space needs


### Connecting employees to colleagues and resources


### Automating time-consuming tasks


### Recommending optimal days to come onsite


### Adjusting building experiences based on intent and behavior


This progression from “Sync Me” to “Be Me”, aligns closely with how CXAI approaches workplace experience design.


We build systems that not only connect people, places, and spaces but also adapt dynamically based on preference, usage, and organizational patterns.
