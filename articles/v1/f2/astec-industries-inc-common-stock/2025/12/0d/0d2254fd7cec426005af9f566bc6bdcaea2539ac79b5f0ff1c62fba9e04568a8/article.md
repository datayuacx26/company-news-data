**CHATTANOOGA, Tenn., November 17, 2025** – Astec Industries Inc. (NASDAQ: ASTE) is proud to return to World of Concrete 2026, showcasing its commitment to innovation, reliability, and customer success in the concrete industry. At Booth N937 in the Las Vegas Convention Center, attendees will experience Astec’s latest advancements in concrete equipment, parts solutions, and immersive technology.


“Astec is proud to serve the concrete industry with a comprehensive lineup of solutions from RexCon, CON-E-CO, and BMH,” said Harpreet Singh, VP, Sales – Concrete at Astec Industries. “Our presence at World of Concrete 2026 reflects our ongoing commitment to supporting producers and contractors with reliable equipment, essential parts, and innovative technologies that drive performance and efficiency.”


**Supporting the Industry**


Astec’s dealer network for CON-E-CO, RexCon, and BMH Systems provides localized expertise and access to top-tier equipment and parts. With decades of experience and a focus on reliability, Astec continues to help contractors and producers meet the demands of modern construction.


**Step Into the Future with XR**


Visitors to Booth N937 will be invited to explore Astec’s equipment through extended reality headsets, offering a virtual walkthrough of 3D models like the CON-E-CO 327SS compact batching plant. This immersive experience brings Astec’s technology to life, allowing attendees to interact with equipment in a whole new way.


**Join Us**


Astec invites all World of Concrete attendees to stop by Booth N937 to connect with the team, explore innovative solutions, and discover how Astec is shaping the future of concrete.

## About Astec


Since 1972, Astec Industries, Inc. has been connecting communities by providing innovative rock to road solutions for our customers. United by our purpose – Built to Connect – Astec is a leading global manufacturer of specialized equipment for asphalt road building, aggregate processing and concrete production. Operations are divided into two primary business segments. Infrastructure Solutions includes road building, asphalt and concrete plants, thermal and storage solutions. Materials Solutions includes aggregate and other material processing solutions. For more information, follow us on social media.
