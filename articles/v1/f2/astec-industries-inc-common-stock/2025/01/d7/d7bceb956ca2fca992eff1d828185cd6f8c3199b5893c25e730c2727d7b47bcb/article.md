### Displaying Innovative Products at PAVE/X Booth and Live Demo


**CHATTANOOGA, TENNESSEE January 21, 2025 –** Astec Industries will participate in industry discussions and showcase equipment at the[PAVE/X Show](https://www.pavexshow.com/) on Jan. 28-30 from 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. in the Chattanooga Convention Center.


At the PAVE/X Show, attendees will have the opportunity to attend educational sessions, explore products from over 40 paving and paving maintenance companies on the show floor and watch live equipment demonstrations. Astec will display their products
and share how they drive progress in the industry at booth 617.


“As a company headquartered in Chattanooga and employing over 2,000 people locally, Astec is honored to participate in the PAVE/X Show right here in our hometown,” said Astec CEO Jaco van der Merwe. “We’re proud of the industry
changing equipment we design and manufacture and look forward to any opportunity to showcase products to our customers and community.”


At the event, Astec will showcase two commercial class pavers, a[Carlson CP130](https://astecindustries.com/product/carlson-cp130-commercial-class-asphalt-paver) and a[Carlson CP75 II](https://astecindustries.com/product/carlson-cp75-ii-commercial-class-asphalt-paver) . In addition, a[Roadtec RX-405](https://astecindustries.com/product/roadtec-rx-405-cold-planer) cold planer will be on site.


Live equipment demonstrations with a[Carlson CP100 II](https://astecindustries.com/product/carlson-cp100-ii-commercial-class-asphalt-paver) commercial class paver will take place at 1791 Reggie White Blvd, Chattanooga, TN 37402.


To purchase tickets for PAVE/X and view a detailed event schedule, visit[PAVE/X’s website](https://www.pavexshow.com/) .

## About Astec


Since 1972, Astec Industries, Inc. has been connecting communities by providing innovative rock to road solutions for our customers. United by our purpose – Built to Connect – Astec is a leading global manufacturer of specialized equipment for asphalt road building, aggregate processing and concrete production. Operations are divided into two primary business segments. Infrastructure Solutions includes road building, asphalt and concrete plants, thermal and storage solutions. Materials Solutions includes aggregate and other material processing solutions. For more information, follow us on social media.
