### The Astec SiteLine system sets a new standard for operator environmental awareness in the road construction industry.


**CHATTANOOGA, Tenn., March 4, 2025 –** Astec Industries, a global leader in road construction equipment, is proud to announce the launch of the revolutionary SiteLine™ operator environmental awareness camera system for its cold planers. This industry-first technology empowers operators with an unparalleled panoramic view of their surroundings, significantly enhancing safety and efficiency on the job site by being able to view previously unseen obstructions.


Key Features:


- Panoramic View: The SiteLine system provides a custom panoramic view around mobile equipment, starting with the cold planer products, eliminating blind spots and potential hazards.
- Seamless Stitching: Advanced image processing seamlessly stitches multiple camera feeds together, creating a natural and uninterrupted view for the operator.
- Enhanced Safety: This comprehensive view empowers operators to identify and avoid potential hazards, promoting a safer work environment for themselves and their colleagues.
- Increased Efficiency: Improved situational awareness allows operators to work more efficiently and confidently, leading to faster project completion times.
- Zoom In Feature: Ability to tap any one of the camera screens to zoom in and get a close up look at a specific area of the machine, then be able to return to the bird’s eye view.


“The SiteLine camera system represents a significant leap forward in the realm of road construction safety,” said Tom Lenger, Road Recycling Product Manager at Astec Industries. “This cutting-edge technology is more than just a camera system; it’s a testament to our dedication to creating safe, more efficient job sites. By empowering operators with enhanced visibility and situational awareness, we’re not only protecting the workforce but also driving the success of our customers’ projects.”


After a successful debut at World of Asphalt 2024, Astec is thrilled to offer the SiteLine operator environmental awareness camera system as an option on[Roadtec cold planers](https://astecindustries.com/product-type/road-construction/cold-planers/) . We’re also excited to announce plans for expanding SiteLine across the full Roadtec road construction lineup, with upcoming AI enhancements and advanced safety features set for release in Q4 2025. Stay tuned for more details.
