# TerraSource set to make its debut in the Astec Booth at CONEXPO‑CON/AGG 2026

**CHATTANOOGA, Tenn. (January 30, 2026)** — CONEXPO‑CON/AGG 2026 will mark the first appearance of TerraSource Global equipment in the Astec booth following the company’s acquisition of TerraSource Global in 2025. With TerraSource brands such as Gundlach Crushers, Jeffrey Rader, Pennsylvania Crusher and Elgin added to Astec’s portfolio, producers will see a broader range of options for crushing, sizing and material conditioning.


“Being part of the ASTEC family has brought an incredible wave of momentum and collaboration,” said Kevin Hambrice, CEO, TerraSource. “Our teams have come together with a shared passion for disciplined execution, operational excellence and making a measurable impact for customers. Seeing TerraSource equipment featured in the ASTEC booth at CONEXPO 2026 is a proud moment and a powerful sign of what we can achieve together.


Attendees will find a lineup of new and upcoming equipment at the Astec Booth that demonstrates a full value chain of innovation.


### Featured TerraSource Equipment at Astec Booth #C30236


[Gundlach 2050D Multi-Roll Crusher Pro Series](https://astecindustries.com/product/gundlach-2050-multi-roll-crusher-pro/)
Gundlach Crushers offer a range of Multi-Roll Crushers known for their precise product sizing, minimal fines, and excellent dimensional control. These are the only crushers able to produce cubical product with uniform sizing and less fines and come in four model series.


[Gundlach Crushers Cage-Paktor®](https://astecindustries.com/product/gundlach-crushers-cage-paktor/)
The Cage-Paktor® is a versatile crushing solution that excels in handling challenging materials, from wet and sticky substances to friable materials like coal and fertilizers.


[Jeffrey Rader 45WBEH EnduraHog Flextooth®](https://astecindustries.com/product/jeffrey-rader-45wbeh-endurahog-flextooth/)
Designed to crush harder mining and aggregate materials with specialized hammers built to offer maximum power at lower, more efficient speeds.


[Elgin Separation Apex 14FHD™ Decanter Centrifuge (Direct Drive Hydraulic/Electric)](https://astecindustries.com/product/elgin-separation-apex-14fhd-decanter-centrifuge/)
The Apex 14FHD™ is Elgin’s most advanced decanter centrifuge to date—blending the raw power of hydraulic drives with the precision of digital control.


[Elgin Separation CSI-H4™ Vertical Cuttings Dryer (Direct Drive Hydraulic/Electric)](https://astecindustries.com/product/elgin-separation-csi-h4-vertical-cuttings-dryer/)
CSI Vertical Cuttings Dryers Cyclone Pro™ H-Series are the world’s first electronically controlled hydraulic-driven dual-drive dryer on the market today. This novel technology allows operators the ability to adjust the performance of the dryer based on the nature of the solids being fed to it.


Equipment specialists, engineers and application experts will be onsite to walk producers through the latest design updates, automation enhancements and processing advantages behind each system. Adding to the excitement, Astec will premiere a never‑before‑seen piece of equipment live on the show floor exclusively at CONEXPO 2026.


### Visit Astec at CONEXPO‑CON/AGG 2026


Attendees are encouraged to visit the Astec booth in the Central Hall (#C30236) to see the full Astec lineup and explore the integrated technologies that make Astec Built to Connect.

## About Astec


Since 1972, Astec Industries, Inc. has been connecting communities by providing innovative rock to road solutions for our customers. United by our purpose – Built to Connect – Astec is a leading global manufacturer of specialized equipment for asphalt road building, aggregate processing and concrete production. Operations are divided into two primary business segments. Infrastructure Solutions includes road building, asphalt and concrete plants, thermal and storage solutions.
