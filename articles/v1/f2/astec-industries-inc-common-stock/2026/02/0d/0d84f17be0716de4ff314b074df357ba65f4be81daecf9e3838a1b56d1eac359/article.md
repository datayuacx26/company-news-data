# Astec Materials Solutions Group to Unveil Innovative Lineup at CONEXPO‑CON/AGG 2026


Astec is preparing to unveil its most expansive lineup of aggregate and material…


February 25, 2026


Share:


**CHATTANOOGA, Tenn. (February 25, 2026)** — Built on decades of proven performance and engineered for what’s next, Astec Industries, Inc. (NASDAQ: ASTE) is preparing to unveil its most expansive lineup of aggregate and material processing equipment to date at **CONEXPO‑CON/AGG 2026** , March 3–7 in Las Vegas. The Astec booth will showcase trusted legacy machines alongside several new, **** next‑generation solutions integrating cutting‑edge engineering and smarter technologies designed to deliver higher performance, greater efficiency and more intelligent operation.


This continued innovation and product development reinforces Astec’s leadership in delivering solutions that help producers improve throughput, maximize uptime, and stay competitive in a rapidly evolving industry.


### A Full Value Chain on Display


Inside the Central Hall at booth #C30236, attendees will experience a comprehensive lineup of new and upcoming equipment demonstrating Astec’s full value chain of innovation. This includes high‑quality crushing, screening, washing & classifying, rock breaking, tracked mobile plants, and material‑handling equipment, all rooted in a legacy of proven performance.


Visitors can also explore product features through extended‑reality demonstrations, guided by Astec product experts to provide deeper insight into machine capability and design intent.


### Showcasing What’s Next for Aggregate Producers


“CONEXPO is a great opportunity for Astec to spotlight what’s next in aggregate, recycling and mining production,” said **Michael Norris, Group President** . “Innovation is at the core of everything we do here, and attendees will see how that focus is driving a dynamic new lineup of equipment designed to move the market forward. It’s the next chapter of a portfolio rooted in proven performance and designed for the future.”


### Introducing a New Global Product Line


As part of its expanded offering, Astec will introduce a new global product line designed around the evolving needs of producers worldwide. CONEXPO‑CON/AGG will serve as the stage for the world premiere and first public reveal of several products from this new line.


“This is just the beginning of an exciting new chapter for Astec,” said **Ron Earl, Group VP Sales & Marketing** . “What you’re seeing today is part of a much larger pipeline of innovative, next‑generation crushing and screening equipment, tracked mobile plants, washing systems and material‑handling solutions rolling out in the months ahead. We’re incredibly energized by the scale of this product evolution, and with the strength of our global dealer network behind us, we’re positioned to deliver more value, more efficiency and more smart technology to producers than ever before.”


### Expanding Through Strategic Growth


Astec’s product evolution also includes the recent acquisition of **TerraSource Global** . CONEXPO‑CON/AGG 2026 will mark the first time TerraSource products are showcased within the Astec booth, offering attendees a unified view of the combined capabilities across both portfolios.


### Global Strength, Local Support


Astec delivers the strength of a global brand backed by the support of a local partner. Through an extensive worldwide dealer network, producers receive immediate access to service, parts, and aftercare right where their operations are. This local‑on‑the‑ground support is a vital part of the Astec value proposition, keeping customers running, minimizing downtime, and ensuring industry‑leading support whenever and wherever it’s needed.


### Trusted Solutions, Tailored Support


At this year’s show, Astec is placing a spotlight on parts fulfillment and aftermarket support as key areas of investment and growth. Within its main exhibit, Astec will feature dedicated parts mini‑booths designed to create more opportunities for direct conversation around parts fulfillment, service support, and maintenance needs. At the aggregates parts mini‑booth, attendees are invited to engage with Astec experts and share feedback on how the company can better support their operations throughout the equipment lifecycle.


“Our focus is on helping customers get more value from their equipment over its entire lifecycle,” said **John Swift, VP of Parts and Service** . “By strengthening our parts fulfillment and service support, we’re focused on lowering total lifecycle cost, improving uptime, and delivering practical solutions that help customers operate safely and productively. It’s about being a long‑term partner and supporting customers at every stage of equipment ownership.”


### Featured Aggregate Equipment at CONEXPO‑CON/AGG 2026


**[A60 Jaw Crusher](https://astecindustries.com/product/a-series-jaw-crushers/)**


The Astec A-Series Jaw Crusher is engineered to incorporate the greatest strengths of legacy Astec crushers and provides a heavy-duty frame for improved crushing action and increased efficiency.


Building on decades of Astec engineering, the Astec A‑Series delivers a modern, customer‑led evolution in jaw crushing bringing new products to market faster while setting fresh benchmarks in durability, performance and safety. With the A50 debuting at WOA/AGG1 last year and the A60 now launching at CONEXPO, the A‑Series demonstrates Astec’s commitment to rapid, purpose‑built innovation that directly reflects customer feedback.


**[Telsmith](https://astecindustries.com/product/titan-cone-crushers/)**[®](https://astecindustries.com/product/titan-cone-crushers/) **[T400 Cone Crusher](https://astecindustries.com/product/titan-cone-crushers/)**


The T400 Cone Crusher returns with the same heavy duty bushing design that makes it a dependable choice in tough applications. It’s engineered for long bearing life, easy serviceability, and reliable performance across a wide range of crushing conditions. The T400 incorporates TRAC10® automated control system, designed specifically for Astec’s line of cone crushers. It is a stand‑alone, fully automated crusher control platform that enhances safety, consistency, and performance by monitoring, adjusting, and protecting the cone crusher during operation.


**[8203LPV Horizontal Screen](https://astecindustries.com/product/vari-frequency-screen/)**


Incorporating Astec’s patented Vari‑Frequency Screen Technology, the 8203LPV horizontal screen is engineered to minimize blinding, maximize material flow, and maintain optimal screening efficiency across a wide range of applications.


“Across our crushing and screening portfolio, we’re accelerating innovation in a way that directly reflects the needs of producers. The rapid progression of the A‑Series from the A50 debut at WOA/AGG1 2025 to the appearance of the A60 here at CONEXPO shows how quickly we can move when we combine customer insight with the engineering strength of our legacy brands. The updated Trac10 Technology across all of Astec’s line of cone crushers and the expansion of our Vari‑Frequency Screen Technology into our horizontal screens highlights our commitment to delivering dependable, high‑performing solutions across every stage of the material processing circuit. These products represent the next chapter in how Astec is building a smarter, more efficient lineup for producers who expect more from their equipment,” said **Stephen Whyte, VP of Product Management.**


[Kolberg 9225 Dual Sand Plant](https://astecindustries.com/product/modular-sand-classifying-plants/)


The Kolberg 9225 Series Dual Sand Plant delivers dependable, specification‑controlled sand by combining efficient dewatering with precise classification now enhanced with a unique dual‑split design that enables producers to generate two high‑quality sand products from a single plant. This not only increases product flexibility but also maximizes operating efficiency and overall profitability. “This solution is designed to the latest BS EN 1009 standard, which exceeds current safety requirements in areas such as walkways and access, placing it ahead of much of the existing market,” Whyte commented.


**Frontier SF20 Mobile Screen**


The SF20 Mobile Screen brings a modern design, simplified operation, and advanced telematics to finishing applications. The Frontier SF20 incorporates industry‑leading safety features and is supported by our Signal® telematics platform, giving producers easier access to vital performance insights and simplified maintenance.


“The Frontier SF20 marks another successful product delivered through our New Product Development culture, where close collaboration with our dealers and customers ensures we design solutions that address real operational challenges. Its aggressive screen box, built with the largest in‑class bearings, delivers exceptional productivity, reliability, and uptime.” said **Dave McCracken, Technical Sales Director.**


**Ranger** ® **I35 Impactor**


The Ranger® I35 Mobile horizontal shaft impactor (HSI) is a compact, track mounted impact crusher designed for versatile performance on smaller job sites. With easy transport, simple operation, and a small footprint, it delivers reliable crushing capability for construction, landscaping, aggregate applications, and plant and tool hire operations.


“Astec has a long-standing reputation for providing some of the most productive Mobile HSI plants in the industry, and the Ranger® I35 continues to build on this legacy. When you combine a large rotor and feed opening with a simple direct drive, you get a high performing mobile crusher that is equally comfortable in recycling, demolition, and aggregate applications. Add in a compact footprint and you have an easy to transport machine that fits well in tight urban areas. We are excited to show off our new Ranger® branding with the launch of the I35,” added McCracken.


**36125T Mobile SuperStacker** ®


The 36125T Mobile SuperStacker is a tracked, radial, telescoping conveyor engineered for fast setup, high mobility, and efficient stockpiling.


Alan Gilmore, Regional Sales Manager, commented ‘We are proud to introduce the tracked SuperStacker – 36125T, the most versatile stacker on the market. This unit is self-contained with an on-board genset, tracked mobile and trailer transportable. Complete with a 125ft telescopic conveyor for huge stockpile capacity whilst maintaining high throughput rates at up to 880tph. Fitted with our patented Wizard Touch auto-stockpiling program for desegregated piles, this is a machine that ticks all the boxes.”


[BTI Adaptative Stroke Technology (AST) Series Hydraulic Breakers](https://astecindustries.com/product/hydraulic-breakers/)


BTI’s new AST Series Hydraulic Breakers deliver smarter, stronger performance with adaptive stroke technology, advanced durability features, and high‑output power engineered for the toughest breaking conditions.


“The BTI brand from Astec returns with the launch of a new line of hydraulics breakers, the AST Series. This new line comes with automatic 2-stroke control for changing material hardness. Supplied with our Signal® telemetry and breaker mounted auto-lube systems as standard. The AST Series packs a powerful punch with intelligent and worry-free operation. Our customers demand performance, durability, and ease of maintenance from BTI. The AST Series provides this and more,” said **Terry McKague, Technical Sales Manager.**


**[BTI Breaker INTEL](https://astecindustries.com/product/breakerintel/)**


Breaker INTEL delivers long-distance teleoperation and automated boom control for rock breakers in demanding mining environments. Operators can run systems from a control room, far from hazardous areas, while benefiting from enhanced precision, reduced maintenance, and 24/7 global accessibility.


### Built on Legacy. Built for Innovation. Built to Connect.


Equipment specialists, engineers and application experts will be onsite to walk producers through the latest design updates, automation enhancements and processing advantages behind each piece of equipment.


Attendees are encouraged to visit the Astec booth in the Central Hall (#C30236) to see the Materials Solutions lineup and explore the integrated technologies that make Astec Built to Connect. To learn more, follow Astec on social media for live updates from the show.

## About Astec


Since 1972, Astec Industries, Inc. has been connecting communities by providing innovative rock to road solutions for our customers. United by our purpose – Built to Connect – Astec is a leading global manufacturer of specialized equipment for asphalt road building, aggregate processing and concrete production. Operations are divided into two primary business segments. Infrastructure Solutions includes road building, asphalt and concrete plants, thermal and storage solutions. Materials Solutions includes aggregate and other material processing solutions. For more information, follow us on social media.
