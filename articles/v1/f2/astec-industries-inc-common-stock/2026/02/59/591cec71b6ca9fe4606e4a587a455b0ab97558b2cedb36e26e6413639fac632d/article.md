# Astec Sets a New Standard in Asphalt Production with 100% Hydrogen-Capable Phoenix® Talon 2 Burner

**CHATTANOOGA, Tenn., February 17, 2026** – Astec Industries Inc. (NASDAQ: ASTE) has proven that its **best-inclass Phoenix Talon 2 burners** can successfully fire on **100% hydrogen** , marking a major milestone for the asphalt industry. This breakthrough establishes Astec as the **first to deploy a premix hydrogen burner for asphalt applications** , delivering **lower NOx emissions than conventional burner technologies used by competitors** .


As the first premix hydrogen burner designed specifically for the asphalt industry, the Phoenix Talon 2 represents a decisive step forward in clean combustion. Premix technology enables more complete and controlled combustion, resulting in ultra-low emissions performance while maintaining the reliability and output asphalt producers demand.


Engineered for producers seeking the lowest possible emissions without sacrificing performance or flexibility, the Phoenix Talon 2 delivers a future-ready combustion solution that supports evolving fuel strategies and sustainability goals.


### Phoenix® Talon 2 Hydrogen Burner – Key Advantages


- **Elimination of CO₂ Emissions**
Designed to eliminate CO₂-related outputs, including **CO, CO₂, and soot** , when operating on hydrogen fuel.
- **Industry-Leading Low NOx Performance**
As the **first premix burner in the asphalt industry** , the Phoenix Talon achieves **lower NOx emissions than traditional burners** , setting a new benchmark for environmental performance.
- **Unmatched Fuel Flexibility**
Capable of operating on **pure hydrogen, hydrogen blended with natural gas** , or **100% natural gas** , allowing operators to adapt seamlessly as fuel availability and infrastructure evolve.
- **Low Energy Consumption**
Equipped with a **VFD-driven combustion motor** , the Phoenix Talon optimizes energy usage while maintaining precise combustion control. See the Proven Technology at ConExpo 2026 Visitors to **ConExpo 2026** will have the opportunity to **see the actual Phoenix Talon 2 burner used to prove 100% hydrogen capability** , on display in the **Astec booth** . By combining **premix combustion technology** , ultra-low emissions, advanced fuel flexibility, and energy-efficient operation, the Phoenix Talon 2 positions Astec at the forefront of sustainable innovation—delivering performance today while enabling the transition to tomorrow’s fuels.
