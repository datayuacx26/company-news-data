# Astec Introduces Roadtec Renew as Part of Expanded Rebuild and Repair Support for Road Construction Customers


Roadtec Renew delivers trusted OEM rebuild and repair solutions to extend the life…


March 1, 2026


Share:


**CHATTANOOGA, Tenn., March 1, 2026** – Astec Industries Inc. (NASDAQ: ASTE) announces **Roadtec Renew** , a dedicated rebuild and repair program offered as part of Astec’s comprehensive support for customers in the road construction industry. Through Astec Parts, Roadtec Renew provides **trusted solutions and tailored OEM support** designed to restore, extend, and protect Roadtec and Carlson road construction equipment while lowering total cost of ownership.


Roadtec Renew makes it simple for customers to access OEM rebuilds and repairs through their local dealer or Astec regional sales manager. From inspection and repair through full equipment rebuilds, the program supports customers across the entire lifecycle of their equipment—helping maximize uptime, performance, and return on investment.


“You’ve made a significant investment in the equipment that helps you accomplish your goals,” said Larry Johnston, Astec VP Parts Sales. “Roadtec Renew helps you get the most out of that investment by restoring and extending equipment life with OEM expertise and genuine OE parts.”


As part of Astec’s rebuild and repair service support, **visitors to ConExpo 2026 can see the quality of a Roadtec Renew rebuild firsthand at the Astec booth C30236, March 3–7** , where a rebuilt paver will be on display to showcase OEM craftsmanship, quality, and attention to detail.


Roadtec Renew includes rebuild and repair services for Roadtec and Carlson equipment such as:


- Pavers
- Cold planers
- Shuttle Buggy® material transfer vehicles
- Screeds
- Components such as the clutch, pump drive, drum drive and more coming soon!


All Roadtec Renew repairs and rebuilds are performed by experienced technicians using **genuine OE parts** , restoring equipment to original specifications and ensuring efficiency, reliability, and productivity in the field.


### Why Choose Roadtec Renew?


Roadtec Renew delivers measurable value as part of Astec’s commitment to long-term customer support, including:


- **Lower total cost of ownership** through cost-efficient rebuild and repair solutions
- **Renewed and extended equipment life** at a fraction of the cost of new equipment
- **OE parts solutions** integrated with repair and rebuild offerings
- **Knowledgeable OEM equipment expertise** with quality built into every process
- **Certified technicians** performing repairs and rebuilds using genuine OE parts
- **Restoration to original specifications** , ensuring optimal performance
- **Regimented inspection protocols** that identify issues before they become costly failures


In addition to rebuild and repair services, Roadtec Renew inspection services help customers protect their investment by proactively identifying wear and performance concerns early—supporting uptime and long-term reliability.


Roadtec Renew reinforces Astec’s commitment to supporting customers throughout the entire lifecycle of their equipment, delivering dependable rebuild and repair solutions that keep road construction operations moving forward.
