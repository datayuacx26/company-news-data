# Theneo Secures Strategic Investments to Transform API Documentation with AI-Driven Technology

We are excited to share that[Theneo](https://www.theneo.io/) , an AI-driven platform focused on creating, managing, and publishing high-quality API documentation, has secured investments from Atlassian Ventures, Y Combinator, Diaspora Ventures, Merus Capital, and Negma Ventures. These investments highlight our pioneering vision to redefine API documentation, fueling accelerated growth and extended influence.


‍


Theneo offers a compelling array of value propositions to revolutionize API documentation, including:


- **Proprietary AI Language Model:** Theneo harnesses a proprietary AI language model tailored specifically for APIs, streamlining the creation of highly effective documentation.
- **ChatGPT Integration:** Seamless ChatGPT integration allows users to rapidly access vital information and effortlessly generate code.
- **Comprehensive Developer Tools:** Theneo empowers developers with a comprehensive suite of real-time developer tools and an intuitive, user-friendly dashboard.
- **Collaborative Editor:** Collaboration becomes seamless through our editor, promoting dynamic content teamwork.
- **Advanced Capabilities:** Theneo's platform includes advanced capabilities such as robust access controls, SEO optimization, automatic changelogs, and an immersive API explorer.›
- **User-Centric Design:** These features cater to developers, technical writers, and project managers, transforming how API documentation is crafted and maintained.
- **Trustworthy Solution:** Theneo's holistic approach has earned the trust of over 5,000 companies, solidifying its reputation as a dependable solution in the field.


‍


"We are immensely honored to have such an esteemed group of investors," stated the CEO of Theneo, Ana Robakidze. "This funding not only validates our vision but also equips us with the capital and expertise necessary to scale our operations and deliver significant value to our mutual customers. With the support from our investors, we're poised to make tremendous strides in the field of API documentation."

"Theneo has built a focused and powerful AI-enabled solution, based on their proprietary technology and models. Their customers receive immediate value and return on investment”, said David Rangel, General Partner at Merus Capital.


"Theneo is a trailblazer in the API management industry. Their proprietary language model and robust parsers, which support a wide variety of API formats, are key components in their cutting-edge technology. At Atlassian Ventures, we’re eager to support Theneo’s continued innovation, including their integration with Compass, our developer experience platform, and the value we can bring together to Atlassian’s 250,000+ cloud customers,” said Peter Lenke, Head of Atlassian Ventures.

Impressively, our clients have witnessed an astounding 300% surge in API adoption, coupled with a lightning-fast 67% improvement in customer and developer onboarding—all directly attributed to Theneo's prowess. This latest round of investments serves as yet another triumph in our journey, propelling us forward as we continue to experience explosive growth and resoundingly positive market feedback.
