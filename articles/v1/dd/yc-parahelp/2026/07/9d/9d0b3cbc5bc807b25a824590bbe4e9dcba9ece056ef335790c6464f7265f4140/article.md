# Introducing our Plain integration

You can now integrate your Parahelp agent into Plain to resolve tickets across email, live chat, and Slack.


We've partnered with Plain - the team has built an incredible ticketing system for B2B teams.


Cursor, Framer, Fyxer, and many other fast-moving startups already run their Parahelp agents in Plain. Their agents resolve tickets end-to-end - from product questions to technical troubleshooting - with a resolution rate of >65%.


> "We wanted an AI agent that could take action, not just provide information. Parahelp delivers on that!"
> - Yannis Karagkiouloglou, Fyxer


**How it works**


You connect your Parahelp agent as a machine user in your Plain workspace, and it works the queue like a team member.


-


When a ticket is assigned to it, it starts working on it - responding to customers across email, live chat, and Slack


-


When it needs to escalate a ticket, it reassigns the ticket to a team member, following your trial rules.


-


As it handles a ticket, it keeps Plain up to date - ticket state, priority, tags, and customer data


You define how your agent handles tickets by[describing it to Parahelp](https://parahelp.com/blog/parahelp-3) - how it behaves in Plain, uses your other tools, what policies it follows and knowledge it uses, when and how it transfers a ticket, and more.
