# Building a More Sustainable Future: NPK Publishes 2025 Sustainability Report

We are pleased to announce the release of our **[2025 Sustainability Report](https://npki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/NPK_2025-Sustainability-Report.pdf)** , which provides customers, investors, and other stakeholders with an overview of NPK’s approach to responsible operations, long-term value creation, and sustainable growth across energy infrastructure markets.


The 2025 Sustainability Report highlights how NPK delivers reliable and environmentally responsible solutions that help our customers improve the efficiency and sustainability of their operations. This focus continues to drive innovation and the development of next-generation products and services aligned with evolving industry needs.


This report also outlines our progress and priorities across key environmental, safety, and governance areas, as well as our role in supporting the energy transition through sustainable technologies and services. These disclosures reflect our commitment to transparency and continuous improvement as we advance sustainability across our business.


The 2025 Sustainability Report is now available and can be accessed here: **[2025 Sustainability Report](https://npki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/NPK_2025-Sustainability-Report.pdf) .** To learn more about NPK's commitment to sustainability, visit our website at[npki.com/sustainability](https://npki.com/sustainability/) .
