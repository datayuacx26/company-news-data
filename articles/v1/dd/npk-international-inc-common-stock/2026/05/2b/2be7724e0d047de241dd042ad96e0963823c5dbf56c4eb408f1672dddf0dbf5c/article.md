**ABOUT NPK INTERNATIONAL**


NPK International Inc. is a temporary worksite access solutions company that manufactures, sells, and rents recyclable composite matting products, along with a full suite of services, including planning, logistics, and site restoration. The Company delivers superior quality and reliability across critical infrastructure markets, including electrical power transmission, oil and gas exploration, pipeline, renewable energy, petrochemical, construction, and other industries.
