# Arm Holdings plc reports results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended 2026

**Cambridge, England, May 6, 2026** : Arm Holdings plc (NASDAQ: ARM), the company that is building the future of computing, has today published a letter to its shareholders containing the company’s results for its fourth quarter and fiscal year ended March 31, 2026. The letter is available on its investor relations website ([https://investors.arm.com/financials/quarterly-annual-results](https://investors.arm.com/financials/quarterly-annual-results) ). The shareholder letter will also be furnished to the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on a Form 6-K and will be available on the SEC website at[http://www.sec.gov](http://www.sec.gov/) .


Arm will host an audio webcast to discuss its results at 14:00 PT / 17:00 ET / 22:00 BST today, May 6. The live webcast will be available at[https://edge.media-server.com/mmc/p/parfpki9](https://edge.media-server.com/mmc/p/parfpki9) and a replay will be at[https://investors.arm.com/financials/quarterly-annual-results](https://investors.arm.com/financials/quarterly-annual-results) .
