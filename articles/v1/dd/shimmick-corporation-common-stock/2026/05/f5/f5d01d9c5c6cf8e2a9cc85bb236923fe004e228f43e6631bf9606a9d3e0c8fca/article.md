# Shimmick Corporation to Announce First Quarter 2026 Financial Results on May 14, 2026

## Shimmick Corporation to Announce First Quarter 2026 Financial Results on May 14, 2026


May 4, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.shimmick.com/node/8606/pdf)


IRVINE, Calif., May 04, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Shimmick Corporation (“Shimmick”) (Nasdaq: SHIM), a national leader in complex infrastructure solutions, today announced that the company will release its first quarter 2026 financial results after market close on Thursday, May 14, 2026.


Shimmick will also host a video webcast conference call to discuss those results at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time on the same day. The conference call will be live-streamed via the Company’s Investor Relations website ([https://investors.shimmick.com/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=W1UNKbX8YjeJiIFhfqbhyxUtumXTequkgxFMi3yypCvXOdlWNBqDSo3R4Nibc5ieVG1w5qo6CVRw93ZSKMaRR2Nxq9d8HkHBKkNph7qtpmzSCD9woOC81Jiy9v6sTe83) ). A copy of the earnings call presentation will also be posted to our website.


A replay of the video webcast will be available through the same link following the conference call for a limited time beginning immediately following the call.
