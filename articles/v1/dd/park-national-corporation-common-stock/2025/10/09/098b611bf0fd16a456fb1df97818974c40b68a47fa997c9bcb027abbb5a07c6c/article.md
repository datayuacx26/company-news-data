# Park National Bank names Tim Camp market president, celebrates 40-year career of John Kimberly


**SPARTANBURG, SC** – Park National Bank (Park) announced that Tim Camp, senior vice president and commercial team leader in its Carolina Region, has been promoted to market president for the Carolinas. Camp joined the bank in 2006, and in his current role has led commercial lending teams, built strong client relationships and supported growth across the region.


*Tim Camp*


Camp earned a bachelor’s degree in financial management from Clemson University. He is an active leader in the community, serving with organizations such as the USC Upstate Capital Foundation, Spartanburg Housing Board of Commissioners, and the Boys & Girls Club where he has held roles as board member, treasurer and president. Camp is also a member of the Personnel, Finance and Officer Placement committees and serves as an Elder for Spartanburg First Presbyterian Church.


**Celebrating John Kimberly’s leadership**


*John Kimberly*


Camp succeeds John Kimberly, who is retiring after a distinguished 40-year banking career, leading Park’s growth in the Carolinas. Kimberly’s decades of service to banking and his involvement with organizations, including the North Carolina Bankers Association, South Carolina Bankers Association, the Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond Asheville-Upstate Industry Roundtable, Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Carolina, Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Carolina Foundation and Christ School, embody the spirit of giving back and building stronger communities.


He also served on the board and chaired the annual campaign at the United Way of Asheville and Buncombe County, chaired the board at the Mission Healthcare Foundation, and held key leadership roles on the boards of Mission Health System and North Carolina Baptist Hospital among others.


“John and Tim’s commitment and leadership have been instrumental in helping our colleagues, customers and communities thrive,” said David L. Trautman, chair and CEO of Park National Bank. “We are deeply grateful for John’s extraordinary contributions and wish him and his family all the best in the future.” Trautman continued, “As we move forward under Tim’s leadership, I’m confident our Carolina Region will continue to uphold our promises to do what’s right, serve more and remain a trusted partner to our neighbors.”
