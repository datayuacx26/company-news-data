# Mannatech Announces Promotion of Peter Griscom to Chief Operating Officer

### Mannatech Announces Promotion of Peter Griscom to Chief Operating Officer


June 26, 2025


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s206.q4cdn.com/434838798/files/doc_news/Mannatech-Announces-Promotion-of-Peter-Griscom-to-Chief-Operating-Officer-2025.pdf)


FLOWER MOUND, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


***[Mannatech, Incorporated](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fus.mannatech.com%2F&esheet=54282975&newsitemid=20250626916328&lan=en-US&anchor=Mannatech%2C+Incorporated&index=1&md5=0bd39dbbf9ce0e6dbbda3c656d24ee05)*** *(NASDAQ:[MTEX](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ffinance.yahoo.com%2Fquote%2Fmtex%3Fltr%3D1&esheet=54282975&newsitemid=20250626916328&lan=en-US&anchor=MTEX&index=2&md5=c0a94206a43a4bf3bf163fe0e8337025)* ) announces the promotion of Peter Griscom to Chief Operating Officer of Mannatech Incorporated (the "Company") effective July 1, 2025.


Peter Griscom brings more than 10 years of experience in the direct selling channel and has extensive experience in working in the consumer goods space, including launching, leading, and restructuring companies while creating efficiencies in manufacturing, sales, and marketing. Prior to his promotion, Mr. Griscom served as the Company’s senior vice president of global operations since August 21, 2024 and prior to that served as the senior vice president of product, innovation, & supply chain from July 3, 2023 through August 20, 2024. He holds a B.S. from the University of Auburn and an M.S. from the University of Utah.


Commenting on Mr. Griscom’s promotion, Landen Fredrick, the Company’s President and CEO stated, “Peter’s drive and determination to create operational efficiencies and streamline processes are instrumental in reducing costs and moving the Company forward. Elevating Peter to this new role will have a positive impact on the Company’s future.”


**About Mannatech:** Mannatech is a leading global health and wellness company committed to empowering everyone to live their best life through cutting-edge, science-backed nutritional products, patented technology, and a rewarding business opportunity. With operations in more than 25 markets†, Mannatech helps people to live healthier, more fulfilling lives while making a positive social impact in the world. Individuals interested in Mannatech's products or in exploring its business opportunity can learn more at[Mannatech.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fus.mannatech.com%2F&esheet=54282975&newsitemid=20250626916328&lan=en-US&anchor=Mannatech.com&index=3&md5=a750c50e58aa4743b0c293fa0f00bc96) .


† *Mannatech operates in China under a cross-border e-commerce platform that is separate from its network marketing model.*
