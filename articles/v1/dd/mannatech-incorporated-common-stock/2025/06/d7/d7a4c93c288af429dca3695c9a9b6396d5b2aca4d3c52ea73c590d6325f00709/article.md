### Mannatech Announces Results of Annual Shareholders’ Meeting


June 5, 2025


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s206.q4cdn.com/434838798/files/doc_news/Mannatech-Announces-Results-of-Annual-Shareholders-Meeting-2025.pdf)


FLOWER MOUND, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Mannatech, Incorporated](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fus.mannatech.com%2F&esheet=54265719&newsitemid=20250604400505&lan=en-US&anchor=Mannatech%2C+Incorporated&index=1&md5=fe2fbcd0c2db1ef266aa7fd1585c9efc) (Nasdaq:[MTEX](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ffinance.yahoo.com%2Fquote%2Fmtex%3Fltr%3D1&esheet=54265719&newsitemid=20250604400505&lan=en-US&anchor=MTEX&index=2&md5=de68b0baa56faf4aee0a36de7032dca4) ) ("Mannatech"), a global health and wellness company committed to transforming lives to make a better world, announced that its shareholders passed all proposals put to a vote at Mannatech’s annual shareholder meeting (the "Meeting") held Tuesday, June 3, 2025.


Mannatech’s Chairman of the Board, J. Stanley Fredrick, chaired the Meeting, and James Clavijo, Mannatech’s Chief Financial Officer, spoke to the shareholders sharing the company’s financial results for 2024.


There were 1,900,930 outstanding shares of Mannatech’s common stock as of April 8, 2025 entitled to vote and 1,478,342 shares, or approximately 77.8% represented at the Meeting, either in person or by proxy.

**About Mannatech**


Mannatech, Incorporated, is committed to transforming lives through the development, marketing, and sales of high-quality, proprietary nutritional supplements, topical and skin care and anti-aging products, and weight-management products distributed through its global network of independent associates and members. The company has been operating for more than 25 years of experience with operations in 25 markets^. For more information, visit[Mannatech.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fus.mannatech.com%2F&esheet=54265719&newsitemid=20250604400505&lan=en-US&anchor=Mannatech.com&index=4&md5=544fc914db8285829654f8fa4b9e8879) .


^ *Mannatech operates in China under a cross-border e-commerce platform that is separate from its network marketing model.*
