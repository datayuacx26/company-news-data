*“The Long Run” showcases a world-record journey powered by Glycan Replenishment Therapy and unmatched determination.*


FLOWER MOUND, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Global health and wellness innovator Mannatech is proud to premiere *Steve Edwards: The Long Run* , an 11-minute short documentary chronicling the unbelievable true story of Steve Edwards—an everyday man turned endurance icon. With over 1,000 official marathons completed in record-setting average times, Edwards’ legacy is one of resilience, quiet determination and the pursuit of human potential. The film debuts globally today, May 21, and is followed by an in-depth interview with Steve Edwards himself following the documentary. It is available to watch free at[events.mannatech.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.mannatech.com%2Fevent%2Fworld-premier-steve-edwards-long-run%2F&esheet=54258462&newsitemid=20250521388269&lan=en-US&anchor=events.mannatech.com&index=1&md5=7a01af56b43f7819a0e8ad4e7be98667) (registration required) and on the[company’s Facebook page](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.facebook.com%2FMannatechInc%2F&esheet=54258462&newsitemid=20250521388269&lan=en-US&anchor=company%26%238217%3Bs+Facebook+page&index=2&md5=c11a757a77c255194ae28e92d7fbad43) , beginning at 7:30 p.m. CT.


From a modest upbringing in Coventry, England, to three Guinness World Records and a final race that brought a crowd to tears, Steve’s journey defies logic. Despite genetic challenges that made endurance athletics significantly harder, Steve pushed beyond the odds to set a new world record for running 1,000 official marathons in a faster average finish time than anyone else on the planet—averaging 3 hours, 21 minutes, and 47 seconds per race.


Steve credits Mannatech’s Glycan Replenishment Therapy with helping him recover faster, train harder and extend his running longevity well into his 60s.* *“These products were a game-changer,”* he says in the documentary’s companion interview. *“Better recovery meant better training, and that meant better performance. The real impact showed up in my longevity—that’s why I was able to go from 500 marathons to 1,000.” ** His daily regimen includes[Ambrotose Life® powder and other foundational glycan-based supplements,](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwelcome.mannatech.com%2Flongrun%2F&esheet=54258462&newsitemid=20250521388269&lan=en-US&anchor=Ambrotose+Life%26%23174%3B+powder+and+other+foundational+glycan-based+supplements%2C&index=3&md5=e17a238c9e9a1300a2843a86f07043ea) which he says helped support his immune system and remain resilient across decades of high-level competition.*


Landen Fredrick, CEO of Mannatech, shared the company’s pride in presenting the story: *“Steve’s commitment to excellence—despite circumstances that would stop most people in their tracks—reflects the values we hold close at Mannatech. We’re honored to have played even a small role in his journey and proud to bring this inspiring story to the world.”*
