# Kornit Digital at FESPA 2026: One Solution, Infinite Possibilities

FESPA has always been more than a trade show. For print businesses and production leaders, the annual FESPA exhibition is where the industry comes to see what is possible next.


This year at FESPA 2026 in Barcelona, Kornit Digital is bringing that future into focus with the message: One Solution, Infinite Possibilities.


From May 19–22, visitors can meet Kornit at


**Fira Barcelona Gran Via, Hall 3, Booth 3-A95** , where the company will demonstrate the


**Kornit Atlas MATRIX** live.


The system is built for a new era of digital textile production, where businesses demand flexibility, quality, and durability.


## **Key Takeaways**


- Kornit Digital will exhibit at FESPA 2026, May 19–22, at Fira Barcelona Gran Via, Hall 3, Booth 3-A95.


- Visitors will see Kornit Atlas MATRIX live, with single-step printing on polyester, cotton, and blends.


- The system is designed to help print businesses expand into sportswear, performance apparel, licensed merchandise, and on-demand production.


- Kornit’s booth will also showcase the wider ecosystem supporting production quality and business growth.


- Kornit will host a dedicated theatre area featuring demos of Apollo and the PrintFactory workflow.


## **See Atlas MATRIX Live at FESPA 2026**


At the center of Kornit’s FESPA experience is


[Kornit Atlas MATRIX](https://www.kornit.com/printer/kornit-atlas-matrix-printer/) , a single digital production system designed to expand what print businesses can offer.


The system supports printing on


**polyester, cotton, and blends** through a single-step workflow. That matters because modern apparel production is no longer limited to one material, one product type, or one production model.


[Sportswear](https://www.kornit.com/direct-to-garment-printing-for-sportswear-with-kornit/) , fashion, promotional apparel, and branded merchandise all require different fabric behavior and durability standards.


Atlas MATRIX was built to help businesses serve these needs without adding unnecessary complexity to production.


One of its key capabilities is printing on dyed polyester while addressing dye migration, which is a common challenge in performance fabrics. Powered by Kornit’s Karbon Technology, the system is designed to deliver migration-free digital printing on dyed polyester and performance materials, while also supporting cotton and blends.


## **More Than a Machine: A Complete Production Ecosystem**


Kornit’s presence at FESPA 2026 is not only about showcasing hardware. It’s also about showing how digital textile production connects across the full business workflow.


Atlas MATRIX is part of a broader Kornit ecosystem that includes:


- Inks


- Consumables


- Workflow tools


- Data


- Support


- Training


- Applications


- Global fulfillment capabilities


The system supports


[Kornit Konnect](https://kornitkonnect.com/) for production data insights and is designed to integrate with


[KornitX](https://www.kornit.com/for-brands/global-fulfillment-network/) workflow.


This matters because modern print businesses are not just asking, “Can we print this?” They are asking:


- Can we produce it profitably?


- Can we repeat the same quality every time?


- Can we support short runs, customization, and faster delivery?


- Can we reduce operational complexity while expanding into new markets?


Kornit’s FESPA booth is designed to answer those questions through live demonstrations, partner activity, and direct conversations with textile printing experts.
