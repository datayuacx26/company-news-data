# WATCH: Joanne Crevoiserat on the Opening Bid Unfiltered Podcast

By **Kristy Williams**


April 15, 2026 02:04 PM Eastern Daylight Time


Tapestry CEO Joanne Crevoiserat joined Brian Sozzi on **Yahoo Finance** ‘s Opening Bid Unfiltered podcast for a conversation on how the company is driving momentum through disciplined brand building, consumer-obsession, and innovation.


Learn more and watch the full conversation on Yahoo Finance’s Opening Bid:[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQdzoXEUPqU&list=PLx28zU8ctIRrPPoWZxI2uK-uEGDIx0DDD&index=3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQdzoXEUPqU&list=PLx28zU8ctIRrPPoWZxI2uK-uEGDIx0DDD&index=3)


Short on time? Catch up with these key takeaways:


1. Self-expression over status. Gen Z doesn’t want to wear a brand as armor. They want brands that reflect their values and help them express who they are – insight that comes from spending time with our consumers, in their world, on their terms.


2. The first bag is everything. Joanne talks about how that first luxury purchase creates brand love for a lifetime. With 25 million women turning 18 every year in our markets, there’s an incredible opportunity for our brands to connect with consumers during the moments that matter to them.


3. Physical retail isn’t dead, it’s evolving. Gen Z craves in-person, community-driven experiences. We’re reimagining our stores as spaces for co-creation and genuine connection, bringing our brands to life in a whole new way.
