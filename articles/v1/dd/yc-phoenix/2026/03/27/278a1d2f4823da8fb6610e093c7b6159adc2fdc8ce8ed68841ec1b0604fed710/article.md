## Barriers to Getting Ozempic in Canada


From August 2023 to March 2024, Canada experienced significant[Ozempic shortages](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/drug-products/drug-shortages.html) due to increased worldwide demand for the medication. The surge in demand was largely driven by social media, particularly content about the health journeys of Hollywood icons, and off-label use. Healthcare professionals recommended increasing the supply to the manufacturer, and production was ramped up to meet the demand by the spring of 2024.


Now that the demand for Ozempic has been met, the only remaining roadblock to men seeking Ozempic in Canada comes from the healthcare system itself.


According to a[2024 study from the Fraser Institute](https://www.fraserinstitute.org/studies/waiting-your-turn-wait-times-for-health-care-in-canada-2024) , Canadian patients faced an average wait time of 30 weeks from a general practitioner referral to seeing a specialist, a significant increase from previous years. Finding a family doctor can also be challenging, with many people waiting months or even years to find a practitioner. On top of that, only 26% of Canadians got same-day or next-day appointments in 2023 according to the[Canadian Medical Association](https://www.cma.ca/sites/default/files/pdf/physician-data/10-Canada-E.pdf) .
