## What is EOR Software?


Employer of Record (EOR) software is a category of HR tech that lets companies hire workers in foreign countries without setting up a local legal entity. The EOR becomes the legal employer on paper, handling payroll, taxes, benefits, and local labor law compliance on behalf of the hiring company. You retain day-to-day control over the work your team members do.


In practice, EOR software sits at the intersection of legal infrastructure and HR operations. It gives you a dashboard to onboard new hires, run payroll across currencies, and manage contracts, all without piecing together data from separate tools at month-end.


### Who typically uses EOR software?


EOR software tends to fit a few specific situations well:


- Startups and scale-ups hiring their first international team members, where the cost and time of entity setup in a new country would far outweigh the headcount being brought on. ## FAQ


### How do I choose the right EOR software from options like Bolto, Remote, Multiplier, Oyster HR, and G-P?


Start by matching your stage and hiring volume to the product. Early-stage teams making their first one or two international hires tend to do well with guided, self-serve options like Oyster HR, while companies scaling across many countries simultaneously need deeper compliance infrastructure and faster onboarding timelines, which is where Bolto and G-P tend to fit better. Budget, support model, and whether you need recruiting built in are the three factors that narrow the list fastest.


### Is Bolto better than Remote or Oyster HR for startups hiring their first international employee?


For startups that need speed and want recruiting, payroll, and compliance in one place, Bolto's 48-hour onboarding and AI-powered candidate shortlisting within 72 hours give it an edge over Remote and Oyster HR, both of which focus on EOR and payroll without integrated talent sourcing. Remote and Oyster HR work well if you already have a candidate and need a clean, self-serve EOR experience, but you will need separate tooling for sourcing.


### When should I consider G-P instead of Bolto or Multiplier for international hiring?


G-P fits best when you are running high-volume, multi-country hiring programs at an enterprise scale and your legal or finance team needs deep in-country compliance infrastructure across 180+ countries. For early-stage or growth-stage companies that want faster setup, more pricing flexibility, and recruiting built into the same workflow, Bolto or Multiplier are likely a better fit for your current stage.


### What is the difference between EOR software and a PEO, and which do I need?


An EOR becomes the legal employer in a country where you have no local entity, making it the right choice for international hires. A Professional Employer Organization (PEO) operates as a co-employer, which requires you to already have a legal entity in the country where you are hiring. If you are hiring internationally without a local entity, you need EOR software; if you are managing US payroll and HR under your own entity, a PEO may be the better fit.


### How do I weigh whether Remofirst's lower pricing is worth the trade-offs compared to Bolto or Multiplier?


Remofirst's lower per-head cost works well if your priority is keeping early international hiring costs down and you can accept a leaner feature set with less integration depth. As your team scales, gaps in onboarding speed, compliance automation, and integrations with your HR and finance stack tend to become more expensive to work around than the per-seat savings support, which is where platforms like Bolto or Multiplier recoup their higher starting cost.
