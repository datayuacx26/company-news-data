## What is an Employer of Record (EOR) and How It Works


An[Employer of Record (EOR)](https://www.bolto.com/employer-of-record) is a third party organization that becomes the legal employer for your international team members. While you manage your employee’s day to day responsibilities, projects, and performance, the EOR handles everything else on the backend.


Here is how it typically works:


1. **You Find the Talent:** You recruit and select the candidate you want to hire, anywhere in the world.
2. **The EOR Hires Them:** The EOR, which has a legal entity in your candidate’s country, hires them on your behalf through a locally compliant employment contract.
3. **The EOR Manages HR & Payroll:** The EOR manages all the administrative tasks, including running payroll in the local currency, withholding taxes, managing statutory benefits (like health insurance and pensions), and ensuring full compliance with local labor laws.
4. **You Manage the Work:** The employee integrates seamlessly into your team, reporting to you and contributing to your company goals just like any other team member.


Essentially, an EOR provides the[global HR infrastructure](https://www.bolto.com/global-hr) you don’t have, letting you tap into a global talent pool without the multi month delays and six figure costs of establishing your own entities abroad.

## How to Choose the Best EOR for Startups: Evaluation Criteria


Not all EORs are created equal. When evaluating your options, consider these key factors to determine what is the best EOR for startups like yours.


### Key Evaluation Criteria:


- **Country Coverage:** Does the EOR have its own legal entities in the countries where you want to hire, or do they rely on third parties? Direct ownership often means better service and more consistent pricing.
- **Transparent Pricing:** Look for simple, flat fee pricing. Avoid EORs that charge a percentage of salary, as this model penalizes you for giving your team raises and can create unpredictable costs. For reference, transparent EOR pricing often falls in the $500 to $600 per month range.
- **Integrated Platform:** Does the EOR offer more than just payroll? A truly valuable partner provides a unified platform that might include contractor payments, HRIS features (onboarding, time off, documents), and even global recruiting services. An all in one platform can save immense administrative effort.
- **Quality of Support:** When an issue arises with international payroll or a complex legal question comes up, you need fast, expert support. Look for providers that offer dedicated account managers or 24/7 human support, not just slow ticket queues.
- **Startup Focus:** Is the platform built for companies like yours? Many legacy providers are designed for large enterprises. A startup focused EOR understands the need for speed, simplicity, and founder friendly support.

## Compliance and Risk Management with EORs


One of the greatest values an EOR provides is peace of mind. International employment compliance is a full time job, and mistakes can lead to significant fines, back tax liabilities, and even legal action.


An EOR mitigates several key risks:


- **Permanent Establishment Risk:** This is the risk of your company accidentally creating a taxable presence in a foreign country by having employees there. An EOR, as the legal employer, helps shield you from this risk.
- **Employee Misclassification:** The rules for classifying workers as employees versus contractors vary dramatically by country. An EOR ensures your team members are classified correctly and receive all legally mandated protections and benefits, avoiding misclassification penalties.
- **Payroll and Tax Compliance:** EORs handle all tax withholdings, social security contributions, and filings with local authorities, ensuring your payroll is always accurate and on time.
- **Labor Law Adherence:** From minimum wage and overtime rules to termination procedures and severance pay, the EOR’s local legal experts ensure every aspect of the employment relationship is compliant.

## Pricing and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for EORs


EOR pricing models generally fall into two categories: a flat monthly fee per employee or a percentage of the employee’s total compensation. For startups, the flat fee model is almost always superior. It is predictable, transparent, and does not create a disincentive to increase your team’s pay.


- **Flat Fee Model:** A fixed price per employee per month. For example, Bolto offers[Global EOR for a flat $599 per month](https://www.bolto.com/pricing) . This is a clear, budget friendly price.
- **Percentage Model:** The EOR charges a percentage (e.g., 10 to 18 percent) of the employee’s salary plus benefits. This can be costly, especially for highly paid roles like senior software engineers.


When calculating the total cost of ownership, compare the EOR fee to the alternative: the tens of thousands of dollars in legal and administrative costs to set up your own entity, plus the ongoing costs of hiring local HR and payroll staff. For nearly any startup hiring fewer than 10 people in a single country, an EOR provides a significantly better return on investment.

### Can I use an EOR to hire just one employee in a country?


Yes, absolutely. Using an EOR is one of the most cost effective ways to hire a single employee in a new country, as it allows you to test a new market or secure a key team member without the massive upfront investment of setting up a legal entity.


### What is the difference between an EOR and a PEO for a startup?


An EOR (Employer of Record) becomes the legal employer for your staff in countries where you have no entity. A PEO (Professional Employer Organization) co employs your staff in a country where you *already* have a legal entity, bundling HR services for your existing employees. Startups use an EOR for international expansion and a PEO for domestic HR outsourcing.

With a modern EOR platform, you can often onboard a new employee in just a few days. Once you have selected your candidate, the EOR can generate a compliant contract and complete the necessary HR and payroll setup very quickly.
