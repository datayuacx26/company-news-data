## FAQ


### How quickly can I hire a remote developer in India using an EOR?


You can complete onboarding in 3-10 business days once you've identified your candidate, with the fastest providers onboarding in as little as 48 hours. This assumes background verification is already underway and your EOR has existing registrations in the candidate's state.


### What costs should I budget beyond the developer's salary when hiring in India?


Budget for statutory employer contributions of 12-15% covering Provident Fund and ESI, plus EOR fees ranging from $199-$700 per employee monthly. For a $30,000 annual developer salary, expect total employment costs around 30-35% above base salary.


### Can an EOR help me avoid the 30-90 day notice periods Indian developers typically have?


No, notice periods are contractual obligations between the candidate and their current employer that an EOR cannot eliminate. You can negotiate notice buyouts directly with candidates, but expect them to factor this cost into their salary expectations.


### What happens if my Indian developer works from a state where my EOR isn't registered?


Multi-state registration adds processing time to your onboarding timeline, typically 5-14 additional days. Choose an EOR with owned entities across multiple Indian states instead of partner networks to minimize this risk.


### Do I need to set up my own legal entity in India if I only want to hire 2-3 developers?


No, that's exactly what an EOR prevents. Entity setup costs $3,000-$10,000 and takes 80-120 days before you can even post a job, making it impractical for small teams where an EOR can have you hiring in days.
