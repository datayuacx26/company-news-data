## What is a Global Payroll Solution?


A global payroll solution is software or a service that lets companies pay employees and contractors across multiple countries while staying compliant with local tax laws, labor regulations, and employment standards. Each country has its own rules around tax withholding, statutory benefits, mandatory reporting deadlines, and termination requirements. For teams new to managing payroll across borders, this can feel overwhelming.[Multi-currency payments](https://www.bolto.com/blog/global-payroll-payments-founders-guide) add further complexity, since exchange rates, local bank rails, and payment timing all vary by market.[Global payroll compliance requirements](https://payroll.org/news-resources/news/news-detail/2026/02/24/global-payroll-more-complex-than-ever) continue to evolve, making expert guidance increasingly valuable.


These solutions consolidate that complexity into one workflow. Understanding[what global payroll is](https://www.bolto.com/blog/what-is-global-payroll-guide) helps teams evaluate which approach fits their needs. Companies can process payments, calculate deductions, and meet local filing requirements without building separate payroll infrastructure in every country they hire. Following[global payroll best practices](https://nativeteams.com/blog/global-payroll-best-practices) keeps your system compliant as regulations change. For teams with international headcount, that consolidation cuts both administrative load and compliance risk considerably.

Remote is a solid choice for startups and mid-size companies hiring in a handful of countries, but companies with more complex multi-country payroll needs may find it hits a ceiling fairly quickly.

## FAQ


### How do I choose the best global payroll platform for my team?


Start by identifying your specific needs: number of countries where you'll hire, whether you need EOR services or just payroll processing, contractor vs. employee mix, and your budget. Match these against each platform's country coverage, pricing transparency, and whether they handle your full workflow or require multiple integrations.


### Which global payroll solution works best for startups vs. larger companies?


Startups hiring in a few countries typically benefit from platforms with transparent pricing, fast onboarding, and self-serve interfaces like Remote or Oyster. Larger companies with complex multi-country needs often require consolidated reporting, deeper integrations, and dedicated support, making solutions like Papaya Global or Bolto better fits for scale.


### Can I use one platform to pay both employees and contractors internationally?


Yes, most modern global payroll solutions handle both, but implementation quality varies. Look for platforms that process contractor payments and employee payroll in the same workflow instead of forcing you to toggle between separate systems, which creates reconciliation headaches and increases error risk.


### What's the difference between global payroll software and an EOR service?


Global payroll software processes payments and calculates taxes for employees you already legally hire through your own entities. An EOR becomes the legal employer in countries where you have no entity, handling contracts, statutory benefits, and compliance on your behalf while you manage day-to-day work.


### How long does it typically take to start paying employees in a new country?


Timeline depends on your setup. If you use an EOR, onboarding can take as little as 48 hours to two weeks. If you're setting up your own legal entity first, expect several months for registration, tax accounts, and local payroll infrastructure before running your first payment.
