## About the webinar


AI is becoming an everyday part of renewable energy operations. But with so many new tools and bold claims, it can be difficult to separate real value from the hype.


In the first edition of **AI Unfiltered** , we’ll explore where AI helps asset managers improve daily operations, where human expertise remains essential


, and how the two work best together.


Drawing on **SmartHelio’s** experience with **GAIA** , our AI agent for solar and battery storage, we’ll share practical insights from real operational challenges, focusing on what truly works in the field rather than the hype.


Join us for an open **coffee session** filled with honest conversations, practical takeaways, and fresh perspectives on the future of AI in renewable energy.


## Who should attend


This session is designed for professionals who want practical insights, not marketing promises.


Whether you’re:


-


- An Asset Manager overseeing solar or storage portfolios
- An O&M Manager looking to streamline daily operations
- A Performance Engineer exploring AI-powered workflows
- An Asset Owner evaluating where AI can create real value
- Or simply curious about the future of AI in renewable energy


## What you'll walk away with


During this coffee session, we’ll explore:


-


- Where AI is already delivering measurable value for asset managers
- The operational tasks that still require human judgment and expertise
- Common misconceptions surrounding AI in renewable energy
- Practical ways to combine AI with existing workflows instead of replacing them
- A live discussion with industry experts, including the opportunity to ask your own questions
