# How Uncovered Hidden Solar Losses Across a Japanese Portfolio?


Monthly site visits showed that the plants were underperforming, but many of the causes remained invisible across the **three-plant Japanese portfolio** .


SmartHelio’s Autopilot connected to the plants’ existing data and built a physics-based digital twin to compare **actual vs. expected performance** .


It then detected anomalies, classified the loss patterns, and quantified their financial impact, helping separate **recoverable losses from structural losses** .


Across the **5.92 MWp portfolio in Japan** , **¥66.48M in losses were quantified** , including **¥16.05M in confirmed recoverable losses** .
