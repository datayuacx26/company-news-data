The Wall Street Journal used Structured AI as its primary case study for why European AI founders are moving to the US. Brandon Abreu Smith's account: months in London unable to close funding, then one week in San Francisco and $500k raised. His word for the difference: "night and day." The piece covers the YC Delaware flip as a structural driver of European founder migration and why, for AI startups, time spent waiting for a check in London is time lost to competitors already moving in SF.
