# Structured AI Listed in AEC Magazine AI Directory for Automated Drawing Review

AEC Magazine, the leading publication covering technology in architecture, engineering, and construction, has listed Structured AI in its **AI Directory** . The directory profiles AI-driven solutions reshaping how AEC professionals design, coordinate, and deliver projects. Structured AI is recognized for its AI agents that automate quality assurance and quality control on technical drawings and documents across multiple engineering and architectural disciplines.


### What AEC Magazine Highlights


The AEC Magazine AI Directory entry describes how Structured AI uses visual language models and computer vision trained specifically on engineering symbols and layouts. Our AI agents cross-reference drawings against specifications, schedules, and building codes while learning a firm's specific standards, covering mechanical, electrical, civil, structural, plumbing, fire protection, and architectural disciplines.


As noted in the directory, our agents return "every flagged issue with an exact location and a recommended fix, in 20 minutes instead of two days with manual checking." This speed and precision is what sets AI-powered drawing review apart from the manual QA/QC processes that still dominate the industry.


### Why This Matters for Engineering Firms


Design and construction errors cost the U.S. construction industry an estimated $177 billion in annual rework. Senior engineers spend up to half their time manually reviewing drawing sets and cross-checking specifications. That time could be spent on high-value design work, mentoring junior staff, and winning new projects.


AEC Magazine's recognition of Structured AI in its AI Directory reflects growing industry awareness that automated drawing review is no longer experimental. Engineering firms across MEP, civil, structural, and architectural disciplines are deploying AI agents in production to catch coordination errors, missing annotations, and code compliance issues before drawings leave the office.
