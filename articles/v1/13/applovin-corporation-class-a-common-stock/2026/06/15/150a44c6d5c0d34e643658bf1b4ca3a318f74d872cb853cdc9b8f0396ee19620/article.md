# AppLovin Ads is now open to all advertisers AppLovin | Blog

Last October, we launched our self-serve platform on a referral basis. Advertiser feedback during the referral phase shaped how we shortened the path to get live, simplified integration, and optimized the funnel from signup to campaign launch. We're approaching one-click campaign and ad creation, so any brand can get started in minutes with no investment in creative resources required.


The platform is ready to be opened up. For 14 years, advertising with AppLovin required a relationship with our team. For the past eight months, it required a referral code. Starting today, it requires neither. Any business can sign up and reach our billion-plus daily active users.


## Going forward: AppLovin Ads


When we launched our self-serve platform in October, we called it Axon. Everything we do is iterative, and we're willing to revisit decisions. When we first named the company AppLovin, it was an $8 domain we'd bought for an app, and we had no idea how far it would go. AppLovin became one of the largest advertising platforms in the world.


Over time, we realized that the AppLovin brand was stronger than we gave it credit for. Even after rebranding the platform to Axon, our customers kept calling us AppLovin. New customers discovered us as AppLovin. The name has become synonymous with the technology and results we deliver. And when something works, there's no reason to change it.


Going forward, our ad platform is simply called AppLovin Ads, and it lives on the AppLovin domain. One company, one brand. Axon remains the name of our AI recommendation system.


## Why mobile gaming inventory matters


More than a billion people play mobile games on our platform every day, one of the largest consumer audiences on the planet. They aren't just gamers - they're parents, professionals, homeowners, and shoppers. The person playing a crossword or puzzle game is a different consumer than the social media power user.[Studies](https://axon.ai/en/blog/mobile-gaming-consumer?utm_campaign=40001393-Axon&utm_source=PR&utm_medium=release) consistently show that mobile gaming audiences skew adult and represent households with meaningful purchasing power.


AppLovin Ads, powered by Axon, connects advertisers with the customers most likely to buy what they’re selling, turning one of the world's largest audiences into one of its most effective growth channels.


## Who AppLovin Ads is for


Our[model](http://axon.ai/en/blog/axon-business-model) is performance marketing. An advertiser spends a dollar, acquires a user, and makes more than a dollar back over the customer’s lifetime, which is a better return than most things a business can do with its money. It’s scalable for the advertiser and for us.


There are three ways to buy on the platform today:


- **Return on ad spend.** Advertisers who focus on revenue goals, like games and online shops, set a ROAS target, and the platform maximizes their return as they scale spend.
- **Cost per purchaser.** Advertisers who want to maximize the number of purchasers, like rideshare, subscription brands, and prediction markets, buy at a target cost per purchaser.
- **Leads.** Lead buying is the newest way to buy on our platform. Advertisers set a value per lead and AppLovin delivers leads at a cost below that value. We built this for categories like auto insurance, health insurance, and home services.


We don't do brand marketing. Every advertiser on the platform buys based on measurable performance.
