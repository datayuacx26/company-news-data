# Rail Fans Invited to Witness Big Boy No. 4014's Historic Return

Pushed and pulled by modern diesel electric UP freight locomotives, Big Boy No. 4014 will depart Fairplex, home of the RailGiants Museum in Pomona, Calif., for the first time in 52 years.
