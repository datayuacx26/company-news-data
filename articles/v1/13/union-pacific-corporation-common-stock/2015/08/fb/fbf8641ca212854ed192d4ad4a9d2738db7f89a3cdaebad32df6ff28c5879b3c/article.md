# Track Inspector's Good Deed Goes Viral

Josh Cyganik has waved to 75-year-old Leonard Bullock every morning for the past four years. Bullock’s house sits across the street from a portion of the Union Pacific main line in Pendleton, Oregon, where Cyganik meets his co-workers every day to begin his work maintaining tracks across UP’s western region. “He sits on that front porch all day long,” Cyganik said. “I never talked to him. Not until I heard what those two kids said.”
