### Pioneer Power to Host 2025 First Quarter Financial Results Conference Call on Monday, May 19, 2025 at 4:30 p.m. ET


**Fort Lee, NJ, May 12, 2025 / BUSINESS WIRE /** –Pioneer Power Solutions, Inc. (Nasdaq: PPSI) (“Pioneer”), a leader in the design, manufacture, service and integration of distributed energy resources, power generation equipment and mobile electric vehicle (“EV”) charging solutions, today announced that management will host a conference call on Monday, May 19, 2025 at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time to discuss Pioneer’s 2025 first quarter financial results with the investment community.


The company will release results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2025 on Thursday, May 15, 2025 after the markets close.
