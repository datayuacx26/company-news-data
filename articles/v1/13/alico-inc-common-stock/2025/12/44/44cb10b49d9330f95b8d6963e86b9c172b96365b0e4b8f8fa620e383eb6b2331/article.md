# Alico to Participate at 28th Annual ICR Conference


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_d48dcc1f3275389050f3a74fc3712b3d/alicoinc/news/2025-12-29_Alico_to_Participate_at_28th_Annual_ICR_1450.pdf) December 29, 2025


FORT MYERS, Fla., Dec. 29, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Alico, Inc. (“Alico” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: ALCO) today announced that John Kiernan, the Company’s President and Chief Executive Officer, will host a fireside chat and one-on-one meetings with investors at the 28th Annual ICR Conference, taking place on January 12-14, 2026 in Orlando, FL.


The Company’s fireside chat will begin at 11:00AM ET on Tuesday, January 13, 2026. The fireside chat will be webcast live and can be accessed through the “News / Events” tab on the investor relations website at[https://ir.alicoinc.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=r28Dx-oAYFOC_PT5Y_nImkO0vdXVlfsduDg-8dvqEekIQ_hRm70vquzcBXcs7jeNdb7NxHxBRdhl7gQOh7ZUcrkN3Qmddcpzh3ITYIEjcyQF7-M4cBEiie9ChUwin6le5ZuVRCvAFhZwFqYWhK1uB2OrXjGf1iw1RuSxmpIJxxsXLuMKmqzQmiNQaj3HNbU2SYWUg1CZVhntxXjsYyEHR3MXDjfpI97NiYfsb-iej9Eq-eRkjKRkXuORtbQaj_h3yCJkxGsB23L7OJ8meU3LPg==) .


**About Alico**


Alico, Inc. (Nasdaq: ALCO) is a Florida-based agribusiness and land management company with over 125 years of experience. Following its strategic transformation in 2025, Alico operates as a diversified land company with approximately 49,537 acres across 8 Florida counties as of September 30, 2025. The Company focuses on strategic land development opportunities and diversified agricultural operations, leveraging its extensive land portfolio to create long-term shareholder value while maintaining its commitment to responsible land stewardship and conservation.
