# Combinely achieves SOC 2 Type II certification

AG


Arthur Granacher


CTO


Security has been a priority at Combinely from the very beginning. When you are handling sensitive financial data and confidential client information, there is no margin for shortcuts or tradeoffs.


## SOC 2 Type II


Today, we are announcing that Combinely has achieved SOC 2 Type II certification. This reflects our commitment to meeting high standards for security, availability, and confidentiality, not just in theory, but in practice.


## Independent Validation


The certification followed a rigorous audit of our systems, processes, and controls over a six-month period. Everything was reviewed, from infrastructure and data handling to access controls and internal policies, with independent verification at every step.


## What This Means


For our customers, this provides assurance that Combinely meets the security expectations of large accounting firms operating at scale. For us, it reinforces the security-first culture we have built from day one and will continue to hold ourselves to as we grow.
