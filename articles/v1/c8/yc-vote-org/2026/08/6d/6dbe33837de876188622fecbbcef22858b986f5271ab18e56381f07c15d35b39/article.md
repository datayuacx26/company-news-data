# Ten Million Times, America Has Turned to Vote.org to Register

The power is in our hands. Ten million times over, Americans have used Vote.org to power their voter registration.


Today, Vote.org passed a historic mark: ten million times, an American has turned to our platform to register to vote. We are the largest nonpartisan, nonprofit voter registration and get-out-the-vote (GOTV) technology platform in America, and this milestone belongs to every person who decided their voice was worth being heard.


Behind that number are 10 million moments when someone decided to take the first step. Whether it was a first-time voter registering from her dorm room the week she turned 18, a family updating their registrations after a move across the country, or a working mom registering on her phone during a 15-minute break between shifts, the power is in our hands, and when the barriers come down, people register to vote.


"Ten million times, an American has turned to us to register, and every one of those moments is someone claiming their place in this democracy," said Andrea Hailey, CEO of Vote.org. "We build the technology to meet voters where they are, at the scale this country demands. The power to shape this nation was always meant to be in the people's hands. Our job is to make sure nothing stands between people and their power."


## Technology is the foundation. People are the driver.


Voters who tagged @Votedotorg in their content


Behind a single number is a platform that never sleeps. Our tools are free, open to anyone, and built to meet voters where they are: on their phones, in their communities, and through the brands, creators, and organizations they already trust.


Keeping that platform online for voters across all 50 states is round-the-clock work. Our engineers build and scale the platform to hold steady when registrations surge by tens of thousands in a single hour, and they capture the data behind every action. Our legal team tracks 50 states' worth of shifting rules, deadlines, and ID requirements so a voter never hits a dead end. Our communications team answers real questions from real people and shares trusted information when democracy comes under attack.


The work goes well beyond registration. Americans have turned to our tools more than 24 million times to check and confirm their registration, nearly 7 million times to request a mail-in or absentee ballot, and hundreds of thousands of times to see what is on their ballot before they vote. In 2026 alone, more than 4 million people have used our platform to get election-ready.


## The most cost-effective investment in democracy


The way we reach ten million is the case for why this work deserves support.


We move voters to action for a fraction of what traditional politics spends: often under $2 per verified action, and sometimes as little as 60 cents. Since our founding, we have helped more than 40 million people get election-ready, which makes us one of the most cost-effective and scalable tools in the civic space. Every dollar we raise goes toward access for voters.


That work carries new urgency. Across the country, there are coordinated efforts to close the doors we exist to keep open, aimed squarely at the younger and more diverse voters who represent the future of this country. Democracy demands action, and right now it demands scale. A gift to Vote.org, a partnership that brings our tools to a new community, or a share that reaches one more voter keeps this platform running and keeps the door to democracy open. "A milestone like this is a mandate," Hailey added. "Millions of eligible Americans have yet to register, and there are forces working to keep them out. Ten million is proof of what we can do when we build this together. The power is in our hands, and we intend to use it to reach even more."

## About Vote.org


Vote.org is the largest 501(c)(3) nonprofit, nonpartisan voting registration and get-out-the-vote (GOTV) technology platform in America. We exist to remove the barriers that keep voters from making their voices heard at the ballot box. Since our founding, we have helped more than 40 million people get election-ready by using technology, data, and strategic outreach to ensure every eligible American has the information and access they need to cast a ballot. Vote.org does not support or oppose any political candidate or party.
