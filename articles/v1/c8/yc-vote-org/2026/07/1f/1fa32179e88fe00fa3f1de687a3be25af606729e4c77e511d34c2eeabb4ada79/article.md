# Vote.org Condemns Trump's Dangerous, Baseless Voter Fraud Claims

Vote.org CEO Andrea Hailey issued the following statement in response to President Trump's address last night making unsubstantiated claims of widespread voter fraud and election vulnerabilities.


"President Trump's speech last night was dishonest and dangerous. He claimed our elections are 'rigged' and 'stolen' without oﬀering a shred of new evidence, because there isn't any. The 2020 election was secure and accurate – that has been affirmed and reaffirmed in five years of audits, recounts, court cases, and intelligence reviews.


"Voter fraud is not a crisis in this country. It is exceedingly rare, and our elections are already secure because they are run with real safeguards, transparency, and accountability built in at every step.


"A healthy democracy hinges on voters having trust that their vote will be counted and that their elections are secure. Our leaders should be working to strengthen that trust and expand access to the ballot – not erode it and restrict access. Trump's baseless and irresponsible attacks threaten the cornerstone of our democracy. That makes it all the more important that organizations like Vote.org and the countless other civic groups committed to this cause keep doing the hard work of debunking these lies, reinforcing the public's faith in our election system, and encouraging voters to make their voices heard."

## About Vote.org


Vote.org is a 501(c)(3) that uses technology to simplify voter participation and increase turnout nationwide. It is the largest nonpartisan voter registration and get-out-the-vote (GOTV) technology platform in the United States. Through corporate and nonproﬁt partnerships, a large-scale community of grassroots donors, diverse coalition building, and digital outreach, Vote.org leverages innovative solutions that help eligible voters register, understand their options, and successfully cast a ballot. These efforts have established Vote.org as the most trusted and accessible online resource for voter registration and election information, providing clear, reliable guidance at scale so voters can access accurate information when, where, and how they need it.
