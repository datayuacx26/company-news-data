# Under Coordinated Attack: Vote.org Fights Back to Protect Voter Registration

At Vote.org, our mission is clear and urgent: to use technology, data, and strategic outreach to ensure every eligible American has the information and access they need to cast a ballot, and we have remained laser-focused on this mission.


Vote.org has been attacked, and we’re fighting back against the coordinated efforts to undermine America's voter registration infrastructure. These systematic attacks, carried out by a handful of bad actors, are directly harming our ability to serve millions of Americans and threatening the democratic process itself.


Today, we filed a federal lawsuit to protect the infrastructure that millions of voters depend on. This isn't about organizational disputes. This is about defending every citizen's fundamental right to accessible, reliable voting tools.


## The Democracy Infrastructure Under Attack


When Americans need to register, verify their status, find their polling place, or get their ballot, they come to us. We've built the largest nonpartisan voter engagement platform in America, essentially creating the infrastructure that makes democracy accessible in the smartphone age.


**Coordinated attacks on this infrastructure have real costs for democracy. We are seeing a systematic pattern designed to undermine voter access nationwide, including the following direct attacks against Vote.org:**


- **False Regulatory Complaints:** Debra Cleaver filed knowingly false allegations with the IRS and multiple state Attorneys General in an effort to trigger costly investigations and create the false impression that enforcement actions are pending, even though officials declined to proceed
- **Trademark Infringement:** Evidence supports that Debra Cleaver, without authorization, used Vote.org's name and logo in anonymous letters to donors and stakeholders in an attempt to discredit the organization and its leadership, and to divert funds away from Vote.org
- **CEO Impersonation:** Creating fake email accounts to impersonate leadership and harass past, incoming, and present employees
- **Partner Disruption:** Filing false complaints with essential data providers to damage critical partnerships
- **Donor Interference:** Debra Cleaver has encouraged current supporters and potential donors not to support Vote.org, all as part of her continued efforts to “destroy” the organization
- **Media Manipulation:** Coordinating with journalists to publish knowingly false stories designed to damage the reputations of Vote.org and its officers
- **Staff Intimidation:** Debra Cleaver, as supported by evidence, targeted new employees to discourage them from working for the organization
- **Frivolous Litigation:** Filing a lawsuit that was ultimately dismissed, forcing Vote.org to spend resources on legal defense
- **Public Defamation:** Making false statements on podcasts and at conferences to damage Vote.org's credibility


**[Full legal complaint with detailed evidence and documentation available here](https://www.vote.org/forms/complaint_09-08-2025.pdf)**


## Despite All Attacks, We Remain America's #1 Voter Registration and Get-Out-the-Vote Platform


Even while defending against these coordinated efforts, Vote.org delivered unprecedented results in 2024:


**Massive Impact:**


- **40M+** visits to Vote.org website, with **87M+** voter outreach touchpoints
- **2.2M+** voters registered
- **71.7%** voting rate among people who used our tools, **17 points higher** than the national average
- **2.6M** voters reached in battleground states through targeted campaigns
- **345K** students reached on nearly **200 campuses** , increasing likely voter participation by almost **10%** among these students


**Infrastructure at Scale:**


- **100s of national partners** use Vote.org tools, making us the backbone of civic engagement nationwide
- **Crisis response capability** that enables Vote.org to rapidly reach voters with critical information. From natural disasters to countering disinformation, Vote.org has remained the nation’s most trusted resource for election information and rapid response
- **Measurable turnout impact** – Vote.org pioneered a nationwide radio program reaching millions of listeners across 40 states increasing voter participation by **3 to 11 points** , making it the most cost efficient and effective GOTV effort over several election cycles
