# Curebase Expands AI-Powered eClinical Platform and Launches Sitebase, Free Modern Technology for Research Sites

Research sites are the backbone of clinical trials, yet they're often stuck with outdated technology or priced out of modern solutions entirely. Meanwhile, sponsors struggle with data quality issues stemming from disconnected systems and siloed workflows. The result? Medical innovations take longer to reach patients.


Curebase saw an opportunity to fix this fundamental imbalance. By building an eClinical platform from the ground up with both sponsors and sites in mind, they could create a stronger ecosystem where everyone wins: sites get top-tier technology, sponsors get pristine data, and patients get a better clinical trial experience that ultimately results in faster access to breakthrough treatments.


## The Expansion: New Capabilities Across the Platform


Curebase today announced the expansion of its AI-powered eClinical platform with new innovations designed to accelerate trials and improve data quality. The enhanced platform now includes:


- **Curebase Recruit:** Advanced patient recruitment capabilities that accelerate enrollment timelines while maintaining data integrity
- **eCOA Vigilance:** A proprietary AI-powered tool that proactively monitors patient compliance and data quality, shifting from reactive data cleaning to real-time risk management
- **Voice AI Agent, Hope:** Conversational AI woven throughout the entire platform, from a Voice Receptionist integrated directly into CTMS to natural language scribe for data capture during patient visits
- **Enhanced Core Features:** Innovations across ePRO, eConsent, EDC, and Participant Engagement


Built entirely in-house with a structured architecture, the platform enables study data to flow seamlessly and error-free between sponsors and sites, resulting in pristine data quality and accelerated timelines.


## Hope: AI That Works Across the Entire Platform


Central to this expansion is[Hope, Curebase's Voice AI Agent](https://www.curebase.ai/ai-for-clinical-trials) , now embedded throughout the platform. Hope handles inbound patient calls through a Voice Receptionist built directly into CTMS, pre-screens potential participants, captures data through natural conversation during visits, and keeps patients engaged between appointments. Rather than bolting AI onto legacy systems, Hope was designed alongside the platform from day one, creating seamless workflows that feel intuitive for both sites and patients.


> "We didn't want to build AI as a separate add-on that study teams have to learn and manage. Hope is simply part of how the platform works. When a patient calls, Hope answers. When data needs capturing, Hope listens. It's AI that disappears into the workflow."— Storm Stillman, CEO of Curebase


## Introducing Sitebase: Modern eClinical Technology, Free


for Sites


As part of this expansion, Curebase is launching Sitebase, a fundamental shift in how clinical research technology is delivered to sites. The platform includes core technology across Clinical Trial Management (CTMS), Investigator Site Files (eISF), and eSource at no cost to research sites, along with access to Hope's Voice Receptionist.


> "Sites deserve to have the most modern software, without breaking the bank. We believe financial barriers and outdated interfaces should never stand in the way of medical progress happening on the ground at clinical research sites."— Storm Stillman, CEO of Curebase


Unlike legacy systems built on decade-old code or patched together through acquisitions, Sitebase offers contemporary technology that's actually easy to use. When sites love their software, studies run more smoothly. For sponsors and CROs, this translates to complete, error-free data and accelerated timelines. For sites, from private independent research centers to large networks, hospitals, and universities, it means modern technology that CRCs and principal investigators actually enjoy using.


By bringing sponsors and sites onto a single platform, Curebase eliminates data silos entirely. Sponsors get higher quality, trusted data. Sites get access to top-tier technology, free, allowing them to focus on what matters most: caring for patients.
