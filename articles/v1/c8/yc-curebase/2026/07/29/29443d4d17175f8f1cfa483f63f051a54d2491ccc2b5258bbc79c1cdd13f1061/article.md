We are so excited to welcome several new team members who are already making an impact across Curebase:

Patrick brings deep leadership experience in building and scaling modern software platforms. He'll be leading our product and engineering teams as we execute on a bold, product-driven vision.


### 👋 Grant Rheingold, Head of AI


Grant is pioneering new ways to bring AI into clinical research, from intelligent automation to enhanced data workflows, helping study teams work smarter and faster.


### 👋 Joshua Munk, Senior Product Manager


Josh is focused on delivering intuitive, high-quality tools that drive study success. In just a short time, he's already added momentum and clarity to our product roadmap.

Welcome to the team! 🚀


## About Curebase


Curebase is dedicated to advancing health outcomes through modern, user-friendly eClinical software. Our AI-native platform transforms clinical research with exceptional usability and innovation. Featuring ultraconfigurable tools—including ePRO/eCOA, eConsent, and EDC—our platform integrates AI at its core to facilitate rapid study launches, capture high-quality data, and maximize patient engagement.
