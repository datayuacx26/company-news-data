# Welcome Brad Stefanovic: New VP, Operations & Strategy Driving Curebase's Next Phase of Growth

We are proud to announce the appointment of Brad Stefanovic as the new Vice President, Operations & Strategy. With over 18 years of experience in the Life Sciences sector, Brad joins the executive team at a pivotal moment as the company enters a new era of expansion.


Stefanovic brings a wealth of expertise to Curebase, characterized by a deep background in clinical operations and a proven track record of leadership. His appointment underscores the company's commitment to supporting faster, more accessible clinical research with modern, AI-powered technology.


## A Strategic Leader for a Growing Platform


As Curebase continues to expand, Stefanovic will be responsible for guiding operations and strategies to build a next-gen, structured platform for sponsors and sites, making clinical trials faster and more efficient.


> "We are entering a critical phase of growth at Curebase. Brad brings not only nearly two decades of industry experience but also a genuine vision for how technology can modernize the clinical landscape. We are thrilled to welcome him to the team and look forward to what we will achieve together."— Storm Stillman, CEO of Curebase


## Passion for Innovation


Stefanovic's career has been defined by a focus on operational excellence and a drive to improve clinical trials. He joins Curebase with a specific passion for leveraging technology to solve complex challenges in trial execution.


> "I have spent my career in Life Sciences observing the challenges that researchers and patients face, and I believe we are at a turning point. Curebase is at the forefront of improving clinical trials through technology, making them more accessible and efficient with next-gen technology. I am incredibly excited to join this talented team and help lead the company through this next chapter of expansion."— Brad Stefanovic, VP, Operations & Strategy
