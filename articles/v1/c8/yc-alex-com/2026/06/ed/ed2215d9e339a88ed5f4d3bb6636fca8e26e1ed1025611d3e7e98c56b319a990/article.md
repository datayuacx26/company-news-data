Five core products address these capabilities: AI Interviews conducts qualification conversations, Coordinator maintains bi-directional Applicant Tracking System (ATS) synchronization, Verify confirms candidate identity and detects fraud, Talent Match surfaces candidates through natural language ATS search and silver-medalist rediscovery, and Resume Screens evaluates applications with transparent scoring. ## **The Implementation Reality: Why Enterprise Deployments Take 18-24 Months**


Vendor sales decks promise 6-9 months. Reality differs. For organizations with 15-30+ recruiters, expect 18-24 months from kickoff to stable operation, accounting for change management, legacy system integration, and recruiter skill development.


Two factors accelerate success. First, executive communication: successful transformations are[8 times more likely](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organizational-performance/our-insights/successful-transformations) when senior managers communicate openly about progress. Second, positioning: frame automation as role enrichment that shifts recruiters from administrative tasks to client relationships, not as headcount reduction.


Plan for three phases: foundation and business case (months 1-6), technical implementation and training (months 6-15), and enterprise rollout with optimization (months 15-24). Budget for the full timeline to avoid compressed schedules that increase failure risk.


## **How to Evaluate Platforms for Your Organization**


Start with workflow mapping, not vendor demos. Before evaluating recruitment automation software, identify where your recruiting team currently spends time: administrative tasks, manual screening, interview scheduling, ATS updates, or candidate communication.


### **1. Does the platform execute tasks autonomously or just organize your manual work?**


AI Interviews conducts entire screening conversations, not just schedules them. Coordinator synchronizes candidate data bi-directionally, not just exports reports. Verify confirms identity in real-time during interviews, not just checks resumes afterward.


### **2. Can it scale during volume spikes without adding headcount?**


Evaluate whether the platform enables 24/7 processing of[high-volume candidate pools](https://www.alex.com/customer-stories/university-of-alberta-case-study) while maintaining consistent evaluation standards.


### **3. Does it integrate bi-directionally with your specific ATS?**


One-way data pushes create reconciliation headaches. Enterprise deployments require demonstrated capabilities in live production environments for your specific ATS.


### **4. How does it address modern fraud patterns?**


With candidates increasingly using AI during applications and interviews, verification capabilities are essential. These four evaluation criteria help distinguish enterprise-ready platforms from tools that add complexity without delivering autonomous execution.
