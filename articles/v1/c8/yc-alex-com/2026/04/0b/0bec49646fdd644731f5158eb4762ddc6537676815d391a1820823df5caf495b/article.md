## **How to Choose the Best Greenhouse Integrations for Tech Recruiting Teams**


When requisition volume outpaces headcount, the wrong tool stack forces recruiters back into spreadsheet hell. Your engineers wait a week for coding assessment results while coordinators burn half their day on scheduling emails. The fix is choosing integrations that eliminate busywork and accelerate decision-making.


Start by mapping your current workflow end-to-end. Where do candidates stall? Are technical screens taking five days when they should take one? Is interview scheduling eating 20 hours per week of coordinator time? Quantify those delays to establish ROI calculations for any integration purchase.


Evaluate every integration against six operational criteria:


- **Data-sync reliability determines everything:** Look for bidirectional APIs that push updates in real-time. Manual data entry between systems kills recruiter productivity. Greenhouse provides best practices for maintaining data integrity and preventing duplicate records during integrations.
- **Security and compliance aren't optional:** Candidates trust you with sensitive data.[Greenhouse holds ISO 27701 certification](https://www.greenhouse.com/blog/greenhouse-is-one-of-the-first-ats-companies-iso-277012019-certified) and undergoes SOC audits, but not all integration partners meet these standards. Check security documentation before you demo. If vendors can't show equivalent certifications, move on.
- **Configurable fit prevents future migration headaches:** Your stack includes GitHub, Slack, and custom data infrastructure. Choose integrations with open APIs so system changes don't break your workflow. Greenhouse Customer-Preferred Integrations provide proven compatibility.
- **Operational impact means measurable time savings:** Ask one question: How many recruiter hours does this eliminate each week? Research shows double-digit reductions in manual tasks when tools deliver end-to-end automation rather than prettier dashboards.
- **AI and analytics depth drives high-volume decisions:** Tech hiring succeeds through pattern recognition. Integrations that streamline automation and organize candidate data help you steer the process rather than react to it.
- **Adoption curves determine ROI.** Brilliant platforms that recruiters ignore deliver zero value. Intuitive UX and strong candidate experience are key factors in successful rollouts alongside automation and employer branding.


Set success metrics before signing contracts. "Cut scheduling time by 70%" or "increase coding test to onsite conversion by 15%" give you measurable targets. Run small pilots, measure against KPIs, then scale what works.


Integrations compound when they work together. Teams pairing AI sourcing platforms with coding assessments and scheduling automation see multiplicative gains: more qualified candidates, objective skills data flowing to Greenhouse, and interviews booked in minutes instead of days.


Review performance quarterly. Tech recruiting moves fast, and your integration stack should too.
