## **2. Scale Technical Assessment with Structured Interviews**


Generalist recruiters struggle to evaluate technical candidates accurately, leading to unqualified candidates advancing while strong performers get filtered out. This assessment gap costs teams time, credibility with hiring managers, and ultimately placements.


[Structured interviews](https://home.ubalt.edu/tmitch/645/articles/McDanieletal1994CriterionValidityInterviewsMeta.pdf) achieve 0.84 reliability coefficients compared to 0.68 for unstructured formats, about 20–25% improvement in measurement consistency. AI Interviews applies this structured approach at scale, giving every candidate identical assessment standards through adaptive follow-up questions and transparent 100-point scoring.


Implementation requires proper validation per EEOC guidance: employers must verify AI tools are job-related and conduct regular fairness testing, as[bias in AI employment](https://www.alex.com/blog/bias-in-ai-recruiting-tools) falls under EEOC enforcement priorities.

## **7. Deploy Multi-Signal Fraud Detection**


Candidate fraud has evolved beyond resume embellishment. Today's candidates use AI tools to generate polished interview responses, hire proxy interviewers, and manipulate video backgrounds. When fraudulent candidates reach client presentations or final rounds, they damage relationships and waste everyone's time.


[91% of U.S. hiring managers](https://www.greenhouse.com/newsroom/an-ai-trust-crisis-70-of-hiring-managers-trust-ai-to-make-faster-and-better-hiring-decisions-only-8-of-job-seekers-call-it-fair) have caught or suspected AI-driven candidate misrepresentation. The most common fraud tactics include fake voices and backgrounds (32%), AI scripts during interviews (32%), and deepfakes (18%). Only 60% of companies conduct identity checks as part of pre-employment screening, creating significant liability exposure.


Verify addresses modern fraud patterns through multi-signal analysis: AI-generated response detection, identity verification, behavioral anomaly detection, and IP/location mismatch flagging. Unlike one-way video tools that candidates easily game, Verify works in real-time during two-way conversations, catching[identity fraud](https://www.alex.com/blog/8-tactics-candidates-are-using-to-cheat-in-interviews) before candidates advance.
