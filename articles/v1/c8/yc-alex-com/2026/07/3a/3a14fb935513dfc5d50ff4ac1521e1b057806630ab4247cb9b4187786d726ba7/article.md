## Why Hiring Teams Are Looking Beyond Mya Chatbot


Mya Chatbot earned its place in the market through effective conversational screening — engaging candidates via SMS, web chat, and messaging apps to handle early qualification, interview scheduling, and basic ATS integration. For frontline, high-volume roles, that was enough. It's no longer enough.


Enterprise hiring teams now face two converging pressures. First, AI screening tools reduce time-to-hire by 40–70%, raising the bar for what automation should deliver. Second, AI-generated applications and proxy test-takers have become sophisticated enough to sail past basic natural language processing checks undetected. Mya's surface-level Q&A was never designed to catch them.


The gaps show up across three dimensions. On screening depth, platforms like[Mokka conduct comprehensive pre-screening interviews](https://www.gomokka.com/resources/choosing-ai-recruiting-partner.html) that collect accomplishment examples and measurable outcomes — producing candidate profiles that go well beyond what resume parsing or simple qualification questions can surface. On fraud protection,[Alex Verify's real-time behavioral anomaly detection and deepfake identification](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) address threats that Mya's basic checks cannot mitigate. And on workflow integration, tools like Alex Coordinator's[bi-directional ATS sync](https://www.alex.com/) automate candidate status updates across more than 33 platforms, eliminating the manual handoffs that cost recruiting teams hours every week.


Assessment depth is another fault line. Mya handles scheduling competently, but it offers nothing comparable to HireVue's AI-driven asynchronous video interviews or TestGorilla's library of over 350 customizable skills tests — capabilities that give hiring managers substantively richer signals before a single human interview takes place.

## ATS Integration & Workflow Automation


A powerful screening engine that lives outside your ATS is, in practice, just another source of manual work. Data silos, status update delays, and workflow friction cost recruiting teams hours every week and quietly erode data quality over time. Deep, bi-directional connectivity isn't a nice-to-have — it's the baseline worth demanding from any modern platform.


Leading tools achieve this through REST APIs for reads and writes, webhooks for real-time event triggers, and SSO/SCIM for permission management. When your ATS fires an event — a candidate advancing a stage, a status change, a new application — a properly integrated platform responds automatically, keeping records synchronized without human intervention.


Alex Coordinator syncs bi-directionally with more than 33 ATS platforms, including Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever, automatically updating candidate statuses as interviews progress. Mokka's deep ATS integration is similarly designed to eliminate tool bloat, pushing enriched profile data directly into existing workflows rather than requiring recruiters to toggle between systems. SeekOut lets users export selected candidate profiles from SeekOut into Greenhouse and Lever via manual export actions, and HireVue maintains an established integration with Greenhouse that can send candidates to HireVue assessments and return status updates into Greenhouse; HireVue's Workday integration depth is not specified in the cited documentation.


For tools where integration specifications aren't publicly documented, sandbox testing during the evaluation process is the most reliable way to verify real-world connectivity before signing a contract.
