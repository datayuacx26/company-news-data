# Alex vs. SmartRecruiters

SmartRecruiters is an applicant tracking system with an agent, Winston, layered into it, and if you already run it, the appeal of automation that needs no integration is obvious. Alex is an AI recruiter: it conducts interviews across channels, scores them, and writes the results back into the ATS you already own. The real question is whether chat-and-schedule automation goes deep enough for the roles that are actually stuck. Here's the side-by-side.


## What each tool actually is


**SmartRecruiters** is an ATS with the Winston agent. The platform holds requisitions, pipelines, careers-site applications, offers and reporting. Its candidate-facing work is chat plus scheduling, with fraud-risk flags applied during application review.


**Alex** is an[AI recruiter](https://www.alex.com/platform) . It runs interviews itself over video, phone or WhatsApp, scores it,[verifies](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) integrity and identity,[coordinates scheduling](https://www.alex.com/product/coordinator) against live calendars, and syncs results back to your ATS.


One owns the record, the other owns the conversation, and for most teams they're complementary.

## Where SmartRecruiters fits


**No integration to maintain.** Winston acts on data already in the system — nothing to map, no sync to monitor.


**It's the ATS.** Records and pipeline reporting in one audit trail, which Alex writes into rather than duplicates.


If a single-platform footprint is your only constraint, SmartRecruiters will do the job.


## Where Alex pulls ahead


### An agent in the workflow, or a specialist doing the interview


Chat plus scheduling fits structured intake. It's a poor fit for evaluation. A chat thread doesn't hear hesitation, doesn't follow an unconvincing answer with a harder one, and doesn't produce a scored, reviewable transcript.


Alex conducts the interview. Phone and video are the interview modalities; WhatsApp does both calls and messages and carries global hiring. SMS handles invitations, reminders and scheduling.


Judge this on completion. A chat thread a candidate never finishes produces no signal at all, and a warehouse associate, a home-health aide or a driver frequently won't finish a desktop web session. Completion drops hardest on the roles you most need filled.


### Fraud flags on the application, integrity on the interview


Alex's cheat detection scores video, audio and transcript for external assistance, AI-generated answers, multiple speakers, eye and head movement and voice-modulation tools, against a threshold you set per role. Crossing it flags the interview for human review, and the report breaks signals out by analysis type. Fraud verification runs alongside it on email, phone, IP and digital-footprint signals, and ID verification adds an optional document-and-selfie check.


Configurability is the part buyers underrate: an integrity system you can't tune produces either noise or nothing.


## What to test in a pilot


Run both against the same requisition and compare:


- Completion rate on your hardest-to-reach role, not your easiest
- Days from application to a scored interview a hiring manager will read
- Percentage of interviews flagged for integrity review, and how many survive review
- Whether the screening output is a structured, scored record or a chat log


## The verdict


Choose SmartRecruiters alone if a single-platform footprint is non-negotiable and chat plus scheduling clears your screening bar. Choose Alex alongside it if the roles that are stuck need a real interview, on channels those candidates answer, with integrity scoring behind it.
