- [Verify](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) layers fraud detection into every conversation, catching[AI-generated answers](https://www.alex.com/blog/how-to-detect-cheating-with-chatgpt-during-interview) , coached responses, and identity misrepresentation before they reach your clients.
- [Talent Match](https://www.alex.com/product/talent-match) resurfaces qualified candidates already sitting in your ATS.
- [Resume Screens](https://www.alex.com/product/resume-screens) evaluates incoming applications with transparent scoring and structured rationale.
- [Coordinator](https://www.alex.com/product/coordinator) manages the handoff from AI to human, coordinating availability in real time so qualified candidates book their next round immediately, with complete interview briefings attached to every calendar invite.


Together, these products deliver end-to-end autonomous recruiting that scales without adding headcount.


Alex executes over 5,000 interviews daily, with 48% occurring outside business hours so your pipeline advances overnight. The platform maintains a 92% candidate satisfaction rating, significantly higher than industry benchmarks, by delivering natural two-way conversations rather than one-way video recordings. AI Interviews also ignores demographic signals, such as name, gender, and previous employers.
