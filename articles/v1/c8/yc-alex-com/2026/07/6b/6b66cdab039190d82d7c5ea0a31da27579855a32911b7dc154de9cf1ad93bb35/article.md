# Alex vs. Metaview

Metaview is an AI notetaker for recruiting. It listens to the interviews your team runs, writes the notes your recruiters would otherwise type at 7pm, and does it well enough that people notice when it's switched off. Alex is an AI recruiter: it conducts interviews, moves candidates forward, and joins later stage interviews to transcribe. Here's the side-by-side.


## What each tool actually is


**Metaview** is an AI notetaker. It captures live interviews, transcribes them, and turns them into structured notes and summaries mapped to your scorecards, with native integrations into a focused set of ATSs. It removes admin from a conversation a human is already having. It does not screen candidates for you.


**Alex** is an[AI recruiter](https://www.alex.com/platform) . It runs the initial interview itself over video, phone or WhatsApp, scores it against the role,[verifies](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) integrity and identity,[coordinates scheduling](https://www.alex.com/product/coordinator) against live calendars, and syncs the result back to your ATS. After the initial interview, it can join human-round interviews to take notes.

## Where Metaview fits


**Notetaking is the whole product.** Metaview captures the interviews your team already runs and turns them into scorecard-shaped summaries — the right buy if humans are conducting every interview and only the admin work is a bottleneck.


**Easy rollout.** Recruiters adopt it; candidates never see it.


If documenting interviews your humans conduct is your only constraint, Metaview will do the job.


## Where Alex pulls ahead


### Reaching candidates, not just recording them


Alex runs on video, phone and WhatsApp, with SMS handling invitations, reminders and scheduling. Alex does full calendar and scheduling coordination, including time zones inferred from area codes and blackout hours so a call never lands at 11pm.


### Integrity at the interview stage


Alex checks the interview for integrity. Cheat detection reads video, audio and transcript for external assistance, AI-generated answers, multiple speakers and voice modulation, against a threshold you set from 0 to 100 per role; crossing it flags the interview for human review, with signals broken out by analysis type in the candidate report. Fraud verification scores identity separately from email, phone, IP and digital-footprint signals, and optional ID verification adds a document-and-selfie check enabled per job.


This matters at the top of the funnel specifically, because that's where volume is and where candidate-side AI assistance is improving fastest. A transcript, however clean, doesn't tell you whether a model wrote the answers.


## What to test in a pilot


- Completion rate on your hardest-to-reach role, not your easiest
- Days from application to a scored, reviewable interview
- Share of interviews flagged for integrity review, and the false-positive rate on review
- Whether output lands in your[ATS](https://www.alex.com/integrations) in the fields your team already reads


## The verdict


Choose Metaview if your interviews are happening and the notes are the problem. Choose Alex if the interviews aren't happening fast enough, or at all, and you need screening conducted, scored and verified at the top of the funnel.
