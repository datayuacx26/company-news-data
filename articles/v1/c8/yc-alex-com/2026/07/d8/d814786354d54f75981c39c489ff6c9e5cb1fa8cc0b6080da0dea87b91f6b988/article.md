# Alex vs. ConverzAI

ConverzAI is a voice virtual recruiter, purpose-built to call candidates at volume over the phone. Alex is an AI recruiter built across channels — video, phone and WhatsApp interviews, plus SMS outreach — with integrity scoring, 100+ ATS integrations and certified compliance. If your screening never leaves the phone line, ConverzAI covers it. If reach, verification and where the results land are what's slowing hiring down, Alex is built for that. Here's the side-by-side.


## What each tool actually is


**ConverzAI** is a voice virtual recruiter. It conducts screening conversations over the phone at volume and handles calendar coordination around them. The phone is the product and the only published channel.


**Alex** is an[AI recruiter](https://www.alex.com/platform) . It runs interviews over video, phone or WhatsApp, scores it,[verifies](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) candidate integrity and identity,[coordinates scheduling](https://www.alex.com/product/coordinator) against live calendars, and syncs results directly into your ATS.

## Where ConverzAI fits


**Voice as the whole product.** Engineering effort concentrates on call quality and recovery from a bad line, an advantage that ends when a candidate doesn't answer in the first place.


**Nothing to install.** A call arrives and the screen happens.


If phone-only screening and an ATS on their list are your only constraints, ConverzAI can do the job.


## Where Alex pulls ahead


### Reach beyond the phone


A phone line is one channel, and it fails in predictable ways: unknown numbers go unanswered, voicemail eats attempts, and international dialling turns into cost and time-zone management.


Alex adds video for the roles you need to see, and WhatsApp for everywhere the phone underperforms. WhatsApp does both calls and messages, runs on wifi rather than billable minutes, and is the default way much of the world is reached. SMS carries invitations, reminders and scheduling. Alex also detects time zone from area code and respects blackout windows, so calls land at a reasonable hour.


### Where the results have to land


Alex[integrates with 100+ ATS platforms](https://www.alex.com/integrations) ; ConverzAI publishes roughly ten. You can create interviews from ATS jobs and sync scores and transcripts back to the candidate record.


If your system isn't among their ten — an older staffing platform, a regional ATS, anything acquired rather than bought new — the fallback is a CSV and a person maintaining it. That cost never shows up in the pilot, and it never goes away afterwards.


### Integrity on paper


Cheat detection scores audio, video and transcript for external assistance, AI-generated answers, multiple speakers, eye and head tracking and voice-changing tools, against a threshold you set per role anywhere from 0 to 100; flagged interviews go to human review with the signals broken out by analysis type in the candidate report. Fraud verification produces a separate identity risk score from email, phone, IP and digital-footprint signals, and a Stripe-backed document-and-selfie ID check can be switched on per job.


## What to test in a pilot


Run the same requisition through both and measure:


- Answer and completion rate on your hardest-to-reach candidate segment
- Time from application to a scored, reviewable screen
- What integrity signals each vendor can show you on a completed conversation
- Whether results reach your ATS record without a manual export


## The verdict


Choose ConverzAI if your screening is phone-only, your ATS is on their list, and a single well-built modality is what you want to buy. Choose Alex if you need candidates reached on more than one channel, integrity signals you can inspect, a hundred-plus ATS integrations, and compliance certification you can put in front of your legal team.
