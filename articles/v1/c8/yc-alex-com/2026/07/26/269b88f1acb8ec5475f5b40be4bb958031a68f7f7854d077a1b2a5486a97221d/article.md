# Alex vs. HireVue

HireVue is an assessment platform that added interviewing. Alex is an AI recruiter that owns the whole process — reaching candidates on the channel they'll actually answer, verifying they are who they claim to be, scoring the conversation, and landing the result in your ATS without anyone touching a spreadsheet. If your hiring turns on validated psychometric scoring for desk-based roles, HireVue's assessment library is the reason to buy it. For volume, frontline, hourly, and global hiring, Alex is built for the job. Here's the side-by-side.


## What each tool actually is


**HireVue** is an assessment and interview platform. The core product is a library of structured, validated assessments built to produce comparable evaluations across large applicant pools. The interview itself happens over video.


**Alex** is an[AI recruiter](https://www.alex.com/platform) . It conducts the screening conversation over video, phone or WhatsApp, with SMS carrying invitations, reminders and reschedules. It scores the conversation,[verifies](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) integrity and identity,[coordinates scheduling](https://www.alex.com/product/coordinator) against live calendars, and syncs results back to your ATS.

## Where HireVue fits


**Assessment validation.** HireVue's assessments carry I-O psychology validation and published adverse-impact documentation, which is worth having if your legal team mandates that paper trail, though it governs the scored exercise, not whether you reached the candidate in the first place.


**Procurement maturity.** Long enterprise deployments mean well-worn security review paths.


If validated psychometric scoring for desk-based roles is your only constraint, HireVue will do the job.


## Where Alex pulls ahead


### Candidates you can actually reach


HireVue's interview is video. Video works for a candidate at a desk with reliable bandwidth and twenty uninterrupted minutes. It does not work for a warehouse associate mid-shift, a home-health aide between visits, a driver, or a candidate abroad who won't install anything to take an interview.


Alex runs on video, phone and WhatsApp. WhatsApp does calls as well as messages, it's already on the candidate's phone, and it runs on wifi rather than billable international minutes, which is why it carries most of the weight for global hiring. Alex also detects time zone from area code and respects blackout windows, so a call never lands at 6am local time.


### Integrity scoring, not a recording to review later


HireVue's cheat detection rates as limited. Alex's is a core product surface. Cheat detection scores video, audio and transcript for external assistance, AI-generated answers, multiple speakers and voice-changing tools, against a threshold you set per role; interviews that cross it are flagged for human review with the signals broken out by analysis type.[Fraud verification](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) scores identity separately — disposable emails, VOIP numbers, VPNs, CNAM mismatches, résumé-to-LinkedIn inconsistencies — and optional ID verification adds a document-and-selfie check.


Candidate-side AI assistance is improving faster than after-the-fact review can absorb. A tool that records an interview without scoring its integrity will pass along answers AI wrote confidently.


## What to test in a pilot


Run both tools against the same requisition and measure:


- Completion rate on your hardest-to-reach role
- Days from application to a scored, reviewable interview
- Share of interviews flagged for integrity review, and the false-positive rate when your team reviews them
- Whether scores and transcripts write back to your[ATS](https://www.alex.com/integrations) without manual export


## The verdict


Choose HireVue if validated psychometric scoring is a hard requirement and your candidates are desk-based. Choose Alex if what's slowing your team down is reaching candidates, verifying who they are, and getting scored results into your ATS without manual work.
