# Alex vs. HeyMilo

HeyMilo is an AI interviewer: it runs the screening conversation over voice, video or phone, scores it, and puts candidates into an async self-schedule flow. Alex owns the whole process. It coordinates real calendars instead of handing out a link, adds WhatsApp and SMS to video and phone, writes back to 100+ ATS platforms, and carries SOC 2 Type II with certified CPRA and TCPA. Choose HeyMilo for a light first-round screen. Choose Alex when coordination, reach and audit evidence decide it. Here's the side-by-side.


## What each tool actually is


**HeyMilo** is an AI interviewer. It conducts the screening conversation over voice, video or phone, scores it, applies behavioral integrity signals with an ID check, integrates with 15+ ATS platforms, and puts candidates into an async self-schedule flow.


**Alex** is an[AI recruiter](https://www.alex.com/platform) . The distinction is scope: as well as running the interview over video, phone or WhatsApp — with SMS carrying invites, reminders and reschedules — Alex[coordinates scheduling](https://www.alex.com/product/coordinator) against live calendars and[verifies](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) integrity against a threshold you set per role from 0 to 100, with signals broken out by analysis type in the candidate report, separate fraud scoring, and an optional document-and-selfie ID check. Results sync into 100+[ATS platforms](https://www.alex.com/integrations) . Interviewing is one step; the handoffs around it are where volume hiring loses days.

## Where HeyMilo fits


**A lighter deployment.** Async self-scheduling needs no calendar integration project and no availability rules. There's less to configure, and nothing that picks up the coordination work after the screen.


**Voice, video and phone.** Three ways into the interview, none of them the messaging channel most candidates actually answer on.


If an async first-round screen is your only constraint, HeyMilo will do the job.


## Where Alex pulls ahead


### Coordination, not a self-schedule link


Alex manages the whole process step by step, coordinating against real calendars: it detects time zone from area code, respects blackout periods so calls and texts don't land at 6am, offers real mutual availability when a human interviewer is involved, and handles reschedules without a recruiter mediating.


Volume funnels rarely stall with the interview. It's usually the days between a passed screen and a booked follow-up with a recruiter copying times into email. A self-schedule link solves the first booking; coordination solves every one after it.


### Channels a candidate will answer


HeyMilo's interview runs over voice, video and phone. All three still ask the candidate to take a call or open a session on a device, at a moment they've set aside.


Alex adds WhatsApp and SMS, and that changes who finishes. A WhatsApp message gets read when a call from an unknown number does not. Across much of the world it *is* the default phone. SMS then covers the outreach layer — invite, reminder, reschedule — which is where funnels leak before a conversation ever happens. Compare the two tools on completion rate for your hardest-to-fill role, because abandonment concentrates precisely there.


### Type II, and compliance that's certified


HeyMilo's SOC 2 is Type I, a point-in-time review of control design. Alex's is Type II, which tests whether those controls operated over a period of time. This is the version enterprise security teams insist on, and months of audit work, not a naming convention.


## What to test in a pilot


Run both tools on the same requisition and compare:


- Completion rate on your hardest-to-reach role, not your easiest
- Days from application to a scored interview, and days from passed screen to a booked next round
- Share of interviews flagged for integrity review, and the false-positive rate
- Whether your[ATS](https://www.alex.com/integrations) is supported natively, and whether results arrive without a manual export
- Which SOC 2 report type each vendor can send today


## The verdict


Choose HeyMilo if async self-scheduling fits how your team books interviews and a light first-round screen is all you need. Choose Alex if the delay in your funnel is coordination rather than interviewing, if candidates need WhatsApp to reach you, or if SOC 2 Type II is a hard requirement.
