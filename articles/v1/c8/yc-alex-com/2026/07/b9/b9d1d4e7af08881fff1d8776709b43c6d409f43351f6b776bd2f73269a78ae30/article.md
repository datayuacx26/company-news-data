**Understanding Braintrust's Three Product Lines**


Before evaluating competitors, understand what you're actually replacing. Braintrust operates three distinct products that serve different functions in the hiring workflow.


### **Braintrust AIR: AI-Powered Interviews**


Braintrust AIR conducts automated candidate screening through one-way recorded video responses. Candidates answer preset questions without real-time interaction, and recruiters review recordings asynchronously.


### **Braintrust Nexus: Workflow Automation**


Nexus handles operational automation: job distribution, candidate routing, and basic Applicant Tracking System (ATS) synchronization. It connects Braintrust's marketplace to your existing hiring infrastructure.


### **Braintrust Talent Marketplace**


The marketplace provides access to pre-vetted contractors and full-time candidates, primarily in technical and professional roles. Braintrust takes 15% of placement fees compared to traditional staffing agencies charging 20-30%.

## **FAQs**


### **What products does Braintrust actually offer?**


Braintrust operates three distinct products: AIR for AI-powered video interviews using one-way recordings, Nexus for workflow automation and ATS connectivity, and their talent marketplace providing access to pre-vetted contractors and full-time candidates primarily in technical roles.


### **How does Alex compare to Braintrust?**


Alex and Braintrust solve different problems. Braintrust provides an external talent marketplace with built-in screening. Alex automates your entire internal hiring workflow: AI Interviews conducts live two-way conversations, Verify detects fraud in real time, Resume Screens evaluates applications with transparent rationale, Talent Match surfaces candidates already in your ATS, and Coordinator handles scheduling and handoffs to hiring managers. Teams that source candidates externally through Braintrust or elsewhere use Alex to screen, verify, and advance those candidates autonomously.


### **Why does interview format matter for fraud detection?**


One-way recorded interviews allow candidates to prepare extensively, read from AI-generated scripts, or have someone else present entirely. Alex's AI Interviews conducts live two-way conversations with adaptive follow-ups that probe deeper when responses seem scripted, while Alex's Verify detects AI-assisted answers and validates identity in real-time.


### **Should I use Alex's Talent Match or external talent marketplaces?**


It depends on your existing candidate database. Most companies have thousands of past applicants who never received proper consideration. Alex's Talent Match surfaces these candidates through natural language search before you pay marketplace fees for external talent. If your ATS is genuinely empty or you need highly specialized skills, external marketplaces like Toptal or Braintrust may be necessary.


### **How does Braintrust's pricing compare to alternatives?**


AI interview platforms typically use subscription pricing based on interview volume and feature tiers. Braintrust's marketplace charges 15% placement fees, but their AI interviewer (AIR) uses separate pricing. Alex, HireVue, and Paradox all use subscription models. For high-volume hiring teams, compare total cost per interview and factor in what's included: Alex bundles fraud detection through Verify and automated scheduling through Coordinator, while competitors may require separate tools for those capabilities.


### **Can I use multiple competitors simultaneously?**


Yes, many teams combine platforms: Alex for[AI interviewing](https://www.alex.com/product/ai-interviewer) and[fraud detection](https://www.alex.com/product/verify) , an external marketplace for specialized searches, and their existing ATS for workflow management. The key is ensuring your tools integrate cleanly to avoid duplicate data entry and candidate experience fragmentation.
