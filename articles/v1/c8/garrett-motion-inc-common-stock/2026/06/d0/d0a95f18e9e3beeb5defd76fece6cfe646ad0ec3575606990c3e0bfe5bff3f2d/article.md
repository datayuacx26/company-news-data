# Garrett-Powered Drivers Set Records and Capture Wins at GRIDLIFE Road Atlanta


Garrett-powered teams earned wins, records, podium finishes, and personal bests at GRIDLIFE Road Atlanta.


GRIDLIFE Special Stage ATL at Michelin Raceway Road Atlanta brought together some of North America’s fastest time attack competitors, and Garrett-powered teams rose to the challenge with record-setting performances, class victories, podium finishes, and personal best lap times.


From Street Mod to Unlimited, drivers relying on Garrett G-Series turbochargers demonstrated the speed, consistency, and reliability needed to perform at one of the most demanding circuits in the country.


Road Atlanta’s combination of elevation changes, high-speed sections, heavy braking zones, and long full-throttle straights creates a true proving ground for both drivers and equipment. Throughout the weekend, Garrett-powered competitors delivered impressive results across multiple classes.


## Dewey DeWitt Resets the Street Mod Record


One of the standout performances of the weekend came from Dewey DeWitt, who captured 1st Place in Street Mod while setting a new class record with an impressive 1:26.515 lap time.


Competing in his IAG Performance Subaru equipped with a Garrett G35-1050 turbocharger, DeWitt established a new benchmark for the class while showcasing the capability of a well-developed Garrett-powered time attack package.


Dewey DeWitt’s IAG Subaru set a new Street Mod record with Garrett G35-1050 power.


The Garrett G35-1050 is designed for high-horsepower applications that demand strong airflow capability, rapid response, and durability. At a track like Road Atlanta, maintaining consistent performance lap after lap is critical, and DeWitt’s record-setting run highlighted exactly that.


Garrett G35-1050-powered Subaru engine bay prepared for competition at Road Atlanta.


### Result


- Driver: Dewey DeWitt
- Class: Street Mod
- Finish: 1st Place
- Lap Time: 1:26.515
- Achievement: New Street Mod Record
- Turbocharger: Garrett G35-1050


Dewey DeWitt celebrates a class-winning weekend and a new Street Mod record.


## Chris Boersma Sets a New Track Mod FWD Record


Chris Boersma added another milestone to his season by setting a new Track Mod FWD record with a lap time of 1:28.172, while also securing a 2nd-place finish in class.


Powered by a Garrett G30-900, Boersma’s front-wheel-drive Honda Civic showcased the balance of power delivery and responsiveness required for competitive FWD time attack racing.


Chris Boersma set a new Track Mod FWD record using a Garrett G30-900 turbocharger.


For front-wheel-drive competition, power application is just as important as horsepower output. The Garrett G30-900 delivers the airflow and response characteristics needed to maximize corner-exit performance while maintaining drivability.


### Result


- Driver: Chris Boersma
- Class: Track Mod
- Finish: 2nd Place
- Lap Time: 1:28.172
- Achievement: New Track Mod FWD Record
- Turbocharger: Garrett G30-900


## Jimmy Rauck Records a New Personal Best


Jimmy Rauck also delivered a strong performance at Road Atlanta, recording a new personal best lap time of 1:22.0 while securing a 2nd-place finish.


Using a Garrett G30-900, Rauck demonstrated the versatility of Garrett’s G-Series lineup across multiple competitive applications during the event.


Jimmy Rauck achieved a personal best 1:22.0 lap time with Garrett G30-900 power.


A 1:22 lap at Road Atlanta reflects an exceptionally competitive pace and requires a combination of driver confidence, chassis setup, and repeatable turbocharger performance under demanding track conditions.


### Result


- Driver: Jimmy Rauck
- Finish: 2nd Place
- Lap Time: 1:22.0
- Achievement: Personal Best
- Turbocharger: Garrett G30-900


## Mike Dusold Takes the Unlimited Class Victory


In the Unlimited category, Mike Dusold delivered one of the fastest laps of the weekend aboard his aggressive 1967 Camaro powered by a Garrett G47-1850 turbocharger.


Dusold secured 1st Place in Unlimited with a blistering 1:20.464 lap time, finishing just 0.2 seconds shy of the existing Unlimited RWD record.


Mike Dusold’s Garrett G47-1850-powered Camaro took the Unlimited class win.


The Garrett G47-1850 sits at the extreme end of the G-Series lineup and is engineered for applications requiring exceptional airflow and power capability. Dusold’s performance demonstrated the turbocharger’s ability to support competitive results at the highest levels of time attack competition.


### Result


- Driver: Mike Dusold
- Class: Unlimited
- Finish: 1st Place
- Lap Time: 1:20.464
- Achievement: 0.2 Seconds Off Existing Record
- Turbocharger: Garrett G47-1850
