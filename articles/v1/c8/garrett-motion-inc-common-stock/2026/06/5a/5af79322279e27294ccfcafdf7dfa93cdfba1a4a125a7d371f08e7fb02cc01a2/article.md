# Power Unleashed: Skoda Superb Build with Garrett PowerMax


##


## Powering Up the Skoda Superb with Garrett PowerMax


A Garrett customer set out to unlock the full potential of their Skoda Superb, equipped with the VW/Audi 2.0L TSI engine. The goal was to achieve significant, reliable performance gains while maintaining OEM-level integration and drivability.


By selecting the Garrett PowerMax turbocharger, the build focused on a solution that delivers both power and simplicity, transforming the vehicle into a stronger and more responsive performer on the street and dyno.


## The Build and Direct-Fit Upgrade Advantage


The Skoda Superb platform offers a strong foundation for performance tuning, and the Garrett PowerMax turbo was chosen for its direct-fit design and proven performance capability.


The installation process required no custom fabrication, thanks to the bolt-on configuration and calibrated actuator, allowing seamless integration with the factory system. This makes PowerMax an ideal upgrade for enthusiasts looking to increase performance without unnecessary complexity. The installation and tuning were completed by Ignite Performance, ensuring the setup was properly calibrated to maximize the performance potential of the Garrett PowerMax turbocharger.


Skoda Superb build featuring Garrett PowerMax turbo upgrade


## Dyno Results and Performance Gains


Following installation and calibration, the vehicle delivered clear improvements across all key performance areas:


- Increased horsepower and torque throughout the rev range
- Faster throttle response and improved boost delivery
- Stronger mid-range acceleration and top-end power


The Garrett PowerMax platform is capable of supporting builds up to 600 BHP (447 kW), providing ample headroom for further optimization and future upgrades.


### Efficiency That Translates to Real Performance


With proper tuning, the upgraded turbocharger enhances airflow and efficiency, allowing the engine to generate more usable power without sacrificing reliability. The result is a well-balanced setup that performs consistently under both daily driving and high-load conditions.


Dyno results highlighting performance gains achieved with Garrett PowerMax turbo


## Advanced Capability for Tuning and Monitoring


Garrett PowerMax turbos feature a fully machined speed sensor port, enabling advanced monitoring and tuning optimization.


- Turbo speed data for precision calibration
- Improved reliability through better control of operating limits
- Compatibility with Garrett monitoring solutions


Supported options include the G-Smart Bluetooth module (PN 923586-0002) and speed sensor kits for both street and professional applications.


Garrett PowerMax turbocharger engineered for direct-fit performance and OEM-level reliability


## OEM Quality Meets Performance Engineering


Garrett engineered the PowerMax lineup to meet OEM standards for durability and long-term reliability while delivering significant performance improvements.


- Direct-fit, bolt-on design for easy installation
- Supports up to 600 BHP (447 kW)
- Integrated speed sensor capability for advanced tuning
- Built for durability and consistent performance


### Key Takeaways


- Seamless OEM integration with bolt-on installation
- Noticeable gains in horsepower, torque, and responsiveness
- Improved efficiency and tuning capability
- Scalable platform supporting higher power goals
- Proven Garrett engineering and reliability


## Conclusion


This Skoda Superb build highlights the effectiveness of a properly matched turbocharger upgrade. The Garrett PowerMax turbo not only increases peak performance but also enhances overall drivability and consistency.


For enthusiasts seeking a reliable, high-performance upgrade, PowerMax provides a direct path to measurable results without compromising the integrity of the factory platform.

Performance results will vary depending on engine configuration, supporting modifications, fuel type, and calibration strategy. Maximum power figures are achievable with appropriate tuning and supporting components.
