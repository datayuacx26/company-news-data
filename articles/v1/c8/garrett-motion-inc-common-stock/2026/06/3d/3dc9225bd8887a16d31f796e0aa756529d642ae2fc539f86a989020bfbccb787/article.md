# Garrett PowerMax™ Turbo Upgrade Adds 233 WHP to Bronco Raptor


The Ford Bronco Raptor is one of the most capable off-road performance vehicles on the market, but enthusiasts continue to push the platform even further. Equipped with Ford’s twin-turbocharged 3.0L EcoBoost® engine, the Bronco Raptor provides an excellent foundation for performance upgrades.


To demonstrate the capability of Garrett’s PowerMax™ direct-fit turbocharger upgrade, Full-Race Motorsports tested a Bronco Raptor equipped with Garrett PowerMax™ turbochargers, a Nostrum high-pressure fuel pump kit, and custom tuning. The results were impressive: 568 wheel horsepower and 591 wheel torque on E50 fuel, representing gains of 233 WHP and 174 WTQ over stock.


## The Build and Direct-Fit Upgrade Advantage


As the original equipment turbocharger supplier for the Bronco Raptor’s 3.0L EcoBoost engine, Garrett engineered the PowerMax™ upgrade specifically for this application. The complete turbocharger package includes left and right turbocharger assemblies with calibrated electronic actuators for seamless integration while maintaining OEM-style fitment and drivability. Compared to the factory turbochargers, the Garrett PowerMax™ upgrade delivers increased airflow capability through larger compressor technology and improved overall efficiency.


Ford Bronco Raptor featuring Garrett PowerMax™ direct-fit turbocharger upgrades


## Dyno Results and Performance Gains


To validate the capabilities of the Garrett PowerMax™ turbocharger upgrade, Full-Race Motorsports conducted dyno testing on a Ford Bronco Raptor equipped with Garrett PowerMax™ turbochargers, a Nostrum high-pressure fuel pump kit, and custom tuning.


The results demonstrated substantial gains over the stock turbocharger system:


- 568 WHP
- 591 WTQ
- +233 WHP over stock
- +174 WTQ over stock


These results highlight the significant performance potential available from the Bronco Raptor’s 3.0L EcoBoost engine when paired with Garrett PowerMax™ turbochargers and the appropriate supporting modifications. Dyno testing and performance results courtesy of Full-Race Motorsports.


### Proven Performance on the Dyno


The combination of increased airflow capacity, OEM-level integration, and proper calibration enabled this Bronco Raptor build to achieve 568 wheel horsepower and 591 wheel torque on E50 fuel. Compared to the stock configuration, the gains of 233 wheel horsepower and 174 wheel torque represent a dramatic increase in performance across the operating range.


Dyno results courtesy of Full-Race Motorsports and Goose Tuned showing 568 WHP and 591 WTQ with Garrett PowerMax™ turbochargers.
