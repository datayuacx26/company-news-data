# Garrett Motion Rings Nasdaq Bell to Mark 70 Years of Innovation

October 29, 2025


# Garrett Motion Rings Nasdaq Bell to Mark 70 Years of Innovation


Garrett Motion celebrated its 70th anniversary today with a powerful moment in Times Square — President and CEO Olivier Rabiller rang the Nasdaq Opening Bell as Garrett’s name lit up the Nasdaq Tower.
More than a celebration, it marks a defining milestone in Garrett’s journey — a tribute to its legacy of innovation and a symbol of its future: pushing the boundaries of technology to serve a world in motion.


## **From Turbocharging Pioneers to Electrification Trailblazers**


Since delivering its first turbocharger in[1955](https://www.garrettmotion.com/corporate/our-story/) , Garrett has evolved from a turbocharging pioneer into a global technology leader driving efficiency, emission reduction and zero-emission mobility.
Its breakthroughs in turbo systems and[electric boosting](https://www.garrettmotion.com/emission-reduction/garrett-e-turbo/) have powered millions of vehicles worldwide with enhanced performance and reduced emissions.


Today, Garrett’s portfolio reflects a bold transformation toward zero-emission technologies, including:


- [Fuel Cell Compressors](https://www.garrettmotion.com/zero-emission/fuel-cell-technology/) – enabling hydrogen-powered mobility through high-efficiency air management.
- [E-Powertrain Systems](https://www.garrettmotion.com/?page_id=1066979) – delivering compact, lightweight, high-performance electric propulsion.
- [E-Cooling Compressors](https://www.garrettmotion.com/zero-emission/e-cooling-compressor-for-ev/) – providing advanced thermal management for next-generation Electric Vehicle platforms and non-automotive applications, such as building rooftop cooling and data center cooling.


These innovations help meet global emission targets without compromising on performance or efficiency in the automotive industry and beyond.


## **Differentiated Technology That Moves the World Forward**


Garrett’s strength lies in its deep systems expertise — combining hardware, software, and advanced controls to deliver differentiated solutions across combustion, hybrid, and electric powertrains.
Its technology roadmap focuses on:


- High-speed rotating machinery
- High-speed electric motors
- Power electronics
- Model-based controls and algorithms


This unique blend of mechanical and digital capabilities positions Garrett as the partner of choice for OEMs navigating the transition to electrified mobility.


## **A Future Fueled by Passion and Possibility**


As Garrett celebrates 70 years, the message is clear: this is just the beginning.
With a global team of innovators, trusted partners, and visionary customers, Garrett is poised to lead the next era of mobility transformation.


“We will bring Garrett even further, turbocharging its future by bringing even more innovations that will help our customers in the automotive and industrial sectors,” said Olivier Rabiller, President and CEO.


## **Expanding into New Markets**


Garrett is accelerating its diversification into new industries, including:


- Marine propulsion
- Power generation
- [HVAC and industrial refrigeration](https://www.garrettmotion.com/news/newsroom/article/garretts-centrifugal-compressor-for-low-gwp-refrigerants/)


These markets demonstrate Garrett’s agility and its commitment to applying automotive-grade technologies to new frontiers.


On this milestone occasion, Garrett thanks its employees, partners, and customers for 70 years of progress — and invites them to continue shaping the future together.
