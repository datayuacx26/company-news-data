# VirnetX Announces New Infrastructure Intelligence Platform, iSCOUT


ZEPHYR COVE, Nev.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 9, 2026-- VirnetX Holding Corporation (Nasdaq: VHC), a global leader in secure communications and zero-trust cybersecurity solutions, today announced iSCOUT (Intelligent Situational Command, Orchestration & Unified Telemetry), a new platform designed to give organizations a single, real-time situational awareness view of their most important operational data. Leveraging AI and advanced analytics, iSCOUT pulls together multiple data sources into one clear picture, helping leaders spot issues earlier, reduce risk, and make faster, better-informed decisions in complex environments. Secured by the VirnetX One™ platform, iSCOUT is designed to protect sensitive communications and critical decision workflows from edge to cloud. Early use cases include humanitarian assistance and disaster relief, mission mobility, and protection of critical infrastructure, where resilient and secure situational awareness is required. iSCOUT is derived from work being performed under VirnetX government programs and is now being adapted for broader commercial and public-sector missions.


**About VirnetX**


VirnetX Holding Corporation is an Internet security software and technology company specializing in patented Zero Trust Network Access (“ZTNA”) for secure network communications. The company’s solutions, including its Secure Domain Name Registry and its flagship platform, VirnetX One™, and products like War Room™ and VirnetX Matrix™, are designed to be device and location independent and enable secure, real-time communication environments for U.S. defense, intelligence, and government agencies, as well as enterprise applications and critical infrastructure. The company also offers Digital Engineering services that align with defense strategies, including its comprehensive Cyber Threat Intelligence and assessment services and Model-Based System Engineering processes.
