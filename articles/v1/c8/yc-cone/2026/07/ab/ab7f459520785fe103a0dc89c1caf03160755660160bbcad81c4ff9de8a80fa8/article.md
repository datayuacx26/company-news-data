# Everhour: Detailed Review

Ever wondered how many hours your team actually works, but can’t prove it? That tiny time gap between what’s tracked and what actually worked might be costing you thousands every month.


Businesses that want to plug such leaks and optimize their revenue with precise, data-driven time tracking are turning to tools like Everhour. But is that the only choice? And more importantly, is that the right choice for your business? In this blog, we explore whether Everhour lives up to this demand, breaking down its features, pricing, ideal users, and how it compares with top alternatives


## Introduction


Everhour is a time tracking and project management solution that works in the cloud and helps teams track hours, organise tasks, and simplify billing processes. It integrates with major project management solutions and provides real-time visibility into team performance and project status.

## Key Features of Everhour


Everhour offers core functionalities that simplify time tracking, project management, and team collaboration, all from a single, user-friendly platform.


- **Time Tracking:** Use timers or manual entry to log hours with accuracy
- **Integrations:** Connects with tools like Asana, Trello, ClickUp, and QuickBooks
- **Project & Task Management:** Organise work, assign tasks, and track progress
- **Custom Reports:** Generate reports to analyse productivity and team performance
- **Invoicing:** Create professional invoices directly from tracked time and expenses


***Pro Tip:*** *If you’re a freelance consultant, tools like Cone or Everhour offer specialised features for*[consultant time tracking](https://www.getcone.io/blog/consultant-time-tracking) *that help you stay billable without the admin overload.*


## Who is Everhour For?


Everhour is best suited for teams and professionals who value simple yet powerful time tracking and seamless integration with popular project tools, making it a great fit for:


- **Freelancers & Small Teams** : Easy to use with minimal setup
- **Project-Based Teams:** Ideal for those using tools like Asana or Trello
- **Businesses That Bill by the Hour:** Helps ensure accurate, billable time tracking


## Pricing and Plans


Everhour offers a straightforward pricing structure:


- **Free Plan:** Suitable for up to 5 users, offering basic time tracking features without integrations.
- **Team Plan:** The Team Plan is priced at $10 per user per month (or $8.50 per user per month if billed annually), and offers all features, integrations, reporting tools, and invoicing as well.


Everhour also offers a 14-day free trial.
