GoProposal is a popular software for its professional and consistent proposal generation, quoting tools, and automation features that helps put your best foot forward with clients. ## What is GoProposal?


GoProposal is a cloud-based proposal and pricing software tailored for accounting and bookkeeping firms. It streamlines the whole process of writing complex and professional proposals, engagement letters, and pricing mechanisms. GoProposal, with an intuitive user interface and a host of automation features, offers a clear and transparent pricing structure that complies with regulations, generates proposals, and professional quotes that can increase client confidence.


## Top Features of GoProposal


GoProposal is built to streamline client engagement with structured pricing and professional proposals.


Below are some of its standout features:


- **Automated Proposals and Engagement Letters** : Create professional proposals and engagement letters instantly, ensuring consistency and compliance.
- **Standardized Pricing Structure** : Create custom pricing models that align with your firm’s services and value propositions.
- **Dynamic Fee Calculations** : Adjust pricing in real time based on the scope and complexity of the client’s needs.
- **E-signature Integration** : Securely send proposals for[e-signatures](https://www.getcone.io/proposal-software/features/esign) , reducing turnaround time.
- **CRM and Accounting Integrations** : Seamlessly integrate with tools like[QuickBooks and Xero](https://www.getcone.io/blog/simplifying-finances-xero-vs-quickbooks-for-small-businesses) .
- **Custom Branding** : Tailor proposals with your firm’s logo, color schemes, and branding for a professional look.
- **Renewal and Fee Reviews** : Easily review and update client fees to ensure profitability and alignment with service value.
