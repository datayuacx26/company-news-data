## Financial Cents Pricing Plans


Financial Cents offers flexible pricing plans to accommodate solo practitioners, small teams, and large accounting firms. Below is a breakdown of its annual and monthly plans:


#### 1. Solo Plan


- $19/month (billed annually), there is no monthly plan
- Features include workflow automation, secure file sharing, client portal, invoicing, and QuickBooks integration


**Best for:** Solo practitioners


#### 2. Team Plan


- $49/month per user (billed annually) or $69/month per user (billed monthly) - Minimum of 5 users
- Includes everything in the Solo Plan, plus integrated email, e-signature, and unlimited tags and custom fields


**Best for:** Small to mid-sized firms


#### 3. Scale Plan


- $69/month per user (billed annually) OR $89/month per user (billed monthly) - Minimum of 5 users
- Includes auto-follow-ups, task dependencies, SmartVault, OneDrive, and Google Drive integrations


**Best for:** Firms requiring advanced automation


#### 4. Enterprise Plan


- Custom Pricing (both annual and monthly options available)
- Features include IP whitelisting, 2FA, webhooks, and a dedicated success manager


**Best for:** Large firms requiring advanced security and customization
