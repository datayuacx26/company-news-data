A proposal is the first chance you get at making a good impression on your client. While a weak proposal can cost you the deal, a slow one might cost you the client. The modern-day competitive business landscape means there’s no room for clunky, time-consuming proposal processes.


Better Proposals is built to eliminate that friction. It combines smart templates, live tracking, and instant signing into one streamlined platform designed to help you close deals faster. ## Better Proposals: Best Features


Better Proposals offers a host of features that simplifies how businesses create, send, and manage proposals. The key features are:


- **Professional Proposal Creation** : Start with high-quality, pre-built templates tailored to different industries
- **Web-Based Proposals:** Clients can access proposals through an interactive link
- **Smart Tracking and Notifications:** Get notified the moment a client opens a proposal
- **Built-in Digital Signatures:** Clients can sign instantly without leaving the proposal
- **Integrated Onboarding:** Replaces manual onboarding emails, payment tools, and booking links
- **Team and Document Management:** Manage who can create, edit, and send proposals
- **Extensive Integrations:** Works with Salesforce, HubSpot, Trello, Asana, and more


## Who Is It For?


Better Proposals is ideal for individuals and small teams who send proposals often and want a faster, more consistent process. It is best suited for:
