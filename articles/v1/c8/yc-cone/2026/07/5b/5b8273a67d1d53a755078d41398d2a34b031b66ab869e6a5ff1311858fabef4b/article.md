Essentially known for its time-tracking feature, Harvest is a cloud-based software that helps you manage basic everyday tasks. It provides a platform for time management for projects, expense management, invoice generation, and analytics such as checking project profitability, team performance, and time spent on specific tasks.


**Key Features:**


- Time-tracking
- Invoice generation
- Easy user-interface
- Expense tracking
- Integration with project management tools.


## Why Look For Harvest Alternatives?


While Harvest offers several features that can help you optimize your team’s output in terms of time management, it also has certain drawbacks. Its limitations offer certain setbacks to not only bigger businesses but also regular users such as freelancers and agencies.


**Limited Options For Customisation:** Harvest does offer customization, but some users find the options limited. The available templates may not cater to the highly specific workflows or complex needs of your businesses.


**Pricing:** Harvest offers a limited free plan, with its most basic features. While the free plan might suffice for your business, you may need to go for additional features in the future. At those times, the pricing might be expensive, especially for smaller teams and agencies.
