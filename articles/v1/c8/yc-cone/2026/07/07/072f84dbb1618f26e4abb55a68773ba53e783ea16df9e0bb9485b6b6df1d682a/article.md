## Pricing and Plans


Anchor Payment adopts a straightforward pricing model:​


- **Per Payment Received:** $5 per payment received, with no subscription fees, hidden costs, or limits on users, clients, proposals, agreements, or invoices. ​
- **Payment Processing Fees:** Credit card processing fees are 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction, which can be passed on to clients or absorbed by the firm. ​


‍
