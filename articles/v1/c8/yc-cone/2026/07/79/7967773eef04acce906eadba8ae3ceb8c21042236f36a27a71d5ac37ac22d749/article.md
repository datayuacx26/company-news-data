# Proposify: Detailed Review

## What is Proposify?


Proposify is a proposal creation software that assists businesses with creating professional sales documents through an automated system. Teams use Proposify to make quick, efficient, and professional on-brand proposals alongside quotes, contracts, and agreements.


## Best Features of Proposify


Proposify offers a range of features designed to simplify the proposal process. Below are some of Proposify’s key features:


- **Proposal Automation:** Quickly create consistent, on-brand proposals using customizable templates.
- **Interactive Quotes:** Build interactive quotes that let prospects choose options and adjust pricing.
- **Contract Management:** Improve team collaboration through proposal document comment and edit placements.
- **E-Signatures:** Clients can securely sign documents through any device.
- **Document Analytics:** Know when your proposal is opened, and how long they spent on each section, and use that information to follow up strategically.
- **CRM and Tool Integrations** : Connect to major CRMs, such as HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho, to bring in client data and keep your company records in sync.
- **Controlled Content and Branding:** Lock certain parts of the proposal to prevent unauthorized edits to that content.
- **Team Collaboration:** Set up internal approvals so proposals meet company standards before being sent out.
