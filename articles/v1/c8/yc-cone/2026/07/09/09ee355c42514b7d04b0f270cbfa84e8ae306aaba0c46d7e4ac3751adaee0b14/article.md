## What is Firm360?


Firm360 is a cloud-practice management platform for accounting firms. It features a mix of functionality such as client management, document management, time management, billing, and project management in the same place. Firm360 is designed to simplify day-to-day operations, boost team collaboration, and improve efficiency within firms.

Firm360 offers a comprehensive suite of features to address the multifaceted needs of accounting practices:


- **Client Management:** Client Management gathers all related information about your client in one place.
- **Document Management:** Document Management helps with secure storage and organization of documents to allow easy access and sharing among teams.
- **Secure Client Portal:** Clients can upload and download documents that enhance communication through a secure client portal.
- **Time Tracking and Billing:** You can track billable hours accurately and invoice clients quickly to get paid on time.
- **Advanced Reporting:** Get customized reports about how the firm is doing, find out what works and does not work and make better decisions.
