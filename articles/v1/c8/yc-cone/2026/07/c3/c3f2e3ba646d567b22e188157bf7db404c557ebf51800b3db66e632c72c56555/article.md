## What is GoCardless?


GoCardless is a payment solution that combines features of Open Banking and Direct Debit processing to help businesses automate payment collection. It is typically used to collect recurring payments ranging from subscriptions to invoicing and payments in installments. GoCardless is particularly popular for its direct bank-to-bank payments that reduces high transaction fees, tackles slow network problems, and reduces failure rates.


‍ **Key Features of GoCardless:**


- Automated recurring payments via direct debit
- Integration with accounting software like Xero and QuickBooks
- International payment collection in multiple currencies
- Low transaction fees compared to card payments

## Why Consider a GoCardless Alternative?


There are several reasons businesses seek alternatives to GoCardless. GoCardless only supports direct debit, giving no options for credit cards or instant payments. It specifically serves Europe, Australia, and North America, confining worldwide reach. Transactions can take several days for account processing, impairing cash flow. Customers also complain about low customization options. ** For these reasons, many look for GoCardless alternatives.
