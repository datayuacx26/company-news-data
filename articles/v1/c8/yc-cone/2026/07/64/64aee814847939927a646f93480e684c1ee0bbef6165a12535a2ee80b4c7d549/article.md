## What is Stripe?


Stripe is a payment processing platform that helps businesses accept online payment through credit cards, debit cards, and various local and international transfers. Stripe is widely used for e-commerce, SaaS, and subscription-based services due to its easy integration and developer-friendly tools. Some of its top features include:


- **Multiple Payment Methods:** Supports credit/debit cards, wallets, bank transfers, and buy now, pay later options
- **Global Reach:** Accept payments in over 135 currencies
- **Subscription Billing:** Recurring billing with[invoice automation](https://www.getcone.io/blog/invoice-automation)
- **Customisable Checkout:** Hosted checkout pages and APIs
- **Fraud Prevention:** Built-in tools like Stripe Radar to detect and prevent fraudulent transactions
- **Developer-Friendly API:** Well-documented APIs for integration with websites and apps
- **Reporting and Analytics:** Real-time reporting tools to track payments, revenue, and customer data
- **Third-Party Integrations:** Works with platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, and Xero
