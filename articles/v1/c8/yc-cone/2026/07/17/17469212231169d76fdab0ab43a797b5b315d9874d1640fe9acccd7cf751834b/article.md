## What is Accelo?


Accelo is an online work management platform developed for professional services companies such as accounting, consulting, and IT services. Accelo allows you to automate work, keep track of work-in-progress through features like[consultant time-tracking](https://www.getcone.io/blog/consultant-time-tracking?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=proposals) , manage client communication, and optimize your processes—all from a single dashboard.


## Features of Accelo


Accelo offers a comprehensive suite of features that streamline operations for service businesses:


- **Project Management:** Track projects, deadlines, and team productivity in real time
- **Client & Sales Management:** Automate client onboarding and track communications
- **Billing & Invoicing:** Generate accurate invoices and track payments seamlessly
- **Time Tracking:** Log billable hours and improve project profitability
- **Automated Workflows:** Reduce manual tasks with AI-powered automation
- **Collaboration Tools:** Centralized communication for improved team productivity
