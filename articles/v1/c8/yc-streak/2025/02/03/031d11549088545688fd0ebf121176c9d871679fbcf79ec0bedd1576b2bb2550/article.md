# Build custom reports and dashboards in Google Sheets with Streak’s new export features

We’ve rebuilt Streak’s exporter to give you greater access to raw data, making it possible to create customized reports and dashboards in Google Sheets.


As businesses grow, teams need deeper insights to track performance, refine workflows, and scale what works. With these updates, you can now export more types of data, apply precise filters, and automate exports to keep reports up to date—without the extra manual work.


**Streak CRM data exports now allow you to:**


✅ Schedule exports of your team’s task and interaction data to Google Sheets: task completion, call logs, and meeting notes
✅ Filter and customize exports for cleaner data and specific date ranges
✅ Schedule Google Sheets exports with automated data refreshes
✅ Join data among multiple tables with box keys
✅ Get notified as soon as your exports are ready


Keep reading to learn how these updates help you export your data into Google Sheets and create reliable reports and dashboards for your team.


## Export your team’s task and interaction data into Google Sheets


In addition to exporting pipeline data like boxes, contacts, and organizations, you can now export data from your[call logs and meeting notes](https://www.streak.com/post/call-logs-and-meeting-notes-feature-release) to better understand how team interactions impact deal progress and success.


Exporting interaction data for top-performing reps helps you identify patterns, like whether a higher volume of calls and meetings leads to more closed deals or faster sales cycles. These insights can help refine sales strategies, optimize team workflows, and improve coaching efforts.


Access to task and call log data from your CRM allows you to create any number of custom reports, including:


- Tasks marked completed by team member each week
- Leaderboard of calls completed each week, by team member
- Overdue tasks that each team member currently has outstanding


## Set precise filters to export exactly the data you need


Streak captures a wealth of data as your team works on deals. With new filtering options, you can now set precise date ranges and choose only the data that matters—ensuring cleaner, more targeted data for your reports.


### Choose your data source


Instead of exporting your entire pipeline, new data source options let you be more specific about which deals you want to see.


You can now choose to export:


- All deals in pipeline
- Selected boxes (using the check box in pipeline view)
- Deals included in a saved view segment


This gives you greater flexibility to analyze specific opportunities and track progress in key areas to generate more actionable insights.


### Select date ranges


Select date ranges for your export to report on your data monthly, quarterly, or at other frequencies.


This allows you to track trends over time, compare performance across different periods, and measure progress toward key goals. By aligning exports with your reporting cycles, you can generate more relevant insights and make data-driven decisions with up-to-date information.


## Schedule exports and automate data refreshes


Eliminate manual exports and keep your data fresh with scheduled exports. Schedule exports daily, weekly, or monthly so your[KPI dashboards](https://www.streak.com/post/kpi-dashboards) always reflect the latest CRM insights—no extra effort needed.


Schedule exports to Google Sheets to create reports and dashboards or use Google Sheets as a live data source for tools like Tableau and Looker Studio. This automates your data connection with fresh, accurate information for reporting and decision-making.


Once you’ve scheduled an export, you can easily manage the schedule—view, pause, delete, or track export history—for complete control over your data.


Scheduling exports is available on[Pro+ and enterprise plans](https://www.streak.com/pricing) .


## Export team activity newsfeed to Google Sheets


Previously, newsfeed data—the history of your team’s CRM activity—could only be exported as a CSV, making it difficult to keep reports up to date. Now, you can schedule newsfeed exports directly to Google Sheets and update an existing sheet with new data. This ensures your data automatically refreshes on a regular schedule.


Schedule newsfeed exports to track real-time activity, like:


- Updates to deal stages or other important CRM fields
- Team interactions
- Assignments and changes to deal ownership


With scheduled newsfeed exports, teams can analyze patterns in deal progress, measure sales rep activity, and spot bottlenecks in their pipeline. Managers get a clearer view of how deals move through the CRM, helping them identify trends, optimize workflows, and improve team performance.


## Join your data tables with box keys


Now, every exported deal includes a box key, making it easy to join different tables of data for more comprehensive analysis.


Instead of working with isolated spreadsheets, you can link deal records with interactions and pipeline activity like call logs, meeting notes, tasks, and other team activity to create a complete view of your pipeline.


Joinable data allows teams to create more powerful reports in Google Sheets, Looker Studio, or Tableau without manual data wrangling.


## Get notified when your export is ready


No more waiting or checking manually—now, you'll receive a notification email as soon as your export is complete. The email includes a direct link to your Google Sheet export so you can access your updated data immediately. If you prefer, you can also download the export as a CSV file with a single click.


## Fresh, actionable CRM reports – automatically


New Streak export features give you greater control over your CRM data, making it easier to track team activity, automate reports, and analyze trends over time.


Whether you’re building real-time dashboards in Google Sheets or importing your Streak data into tools like Tableau and Looker Studio, these updates help you work more efficiently with cleaner, more actionable data.


To start using these features, head to your Streak pipeline and explore the new export options.


[Pro+ plan](https://www.streak.com/pricing) users can set up scheduled exports to keep your reports up to date automatically.
