## How to configure common Streak and DocuSign automations


Contract workflows often follow the same pattern: a deal reaches a certain stage, a specific document needs to go out, and the next step depends on that document being signed. When you integrate Streak and DocuSign, those steps can happen automatically.


The examples below show how real teams use simple triggers in Streak and DocuSign to send documents, collect signatures, and keep their pipeline up to date without manual work.


### Automatically send contracts or documents when a deal reaches a certain stage in your pipeline


Here's how real teams automate sending signature requests to your Streak contacts in Zapier.


1. **Streak → Box change stage**
Example trigger: A box is moved into the stage "In Contract".
2. **Streak → Find the contact in Streak**
Tell Streak to find the contact's email address in the box.
3. **DocuSign → Create signature request**
Select the correct DocuSign template.


### Update the box in Streak when a document is signed


Once a document is signed, your pipeline should reflect that immediately. Automation removes the need to check DocuSign or update your CRM manually.


1. **DocuSign → Envelope completed**
This means the contact or client has finished the document.
2. **Streak → Find a Box**
Locate the corresponding box in your Streak pipeline
3. **Streak → Edit box**
Move your box into a "Contract signed" stage to track finalized contracts.


## Advanced settings for DocuSign automations in Streak


As your workflow becomes more specialized, you may want to tailor which documents go out and what information they include. Zapier gives you flexibility to adapt each automation to the details in your Streak pipeline so the right version of a document is sent every time.


### Sending specific documents based on deal information


If your contracts differ depending on what a client purchases, you can set up Zapier to choose the correct document automatically.


For example, teams with multiple service packages often create a separate DocuSign template for each option.


When a deal reaches the contract stage, Zapier checks the value in your Streak pipeline—such as the selected package or service tier—and sends the matching template. This keeps your workflow accurate even when different clients require different documents.


### Customizing the document based on deal information


Some teams use a single contract template but need the details inside it to change from one deal to the next. In these cases, Streak fields become the source of truth for information like pricing, dates, quantities, or terms.


Zapier can pull those values directly from the box and insert them into your DocuSign document before sending it. This ensures each contract reflects the correct details without editing the template manually.

## How to decide what to automate first


When you’re starting with automation, the best place to begin is with the steps you find yourself repeating the most. These patterns usually reveal strong candidates for automation, especially when they rely on the same information or actions every time.


Look at your workflow for:


- **Repetitive actions** — copying details into DocuSign, sending the same documents, or updating stages after signatures.
- **Back‑and‑forth moments** — switching between Streak and DocuSign to track progress or move deals forward.
**Predictable sequences** — steps where one action always follows another, like sending a contract when a deal enters a specific stage.


Once you understand these patterns, it becomes easier to see where automation can remove friction.


For example, if moving a deal into a contract stage always leads to sending a specific document, Zapier can handle that step automatically. If a signed agreement always means updating the deal in Streak, automation ensures your pipeline stays up to date without manual work.


It’s also helpful to think beyond contracts. Streak offers direct integrations with several apps and Zapier integrates with more than 9,000 apps; once you automate the core Streak and DocuSign steps, you may find additional places where information needs to move from one tool to another.

Streak × DocuSign integration FAQs


### Do I need Zapier to connect DocuSign and Streak?


Yes. Zapier allows you to integrate Streak with DocuSign and automate your contract and document workflow.


### Can I send different contracts automatically?


Yes. You can create separate templates or use dynamic fields from Streak to update DocuSign documents with details from the deal or contact.


### Can I attach the signed document to my Streak box automatically?


Yes. When DocuSign triggers the “Envelope completed” event, Zapier can pass the signed PDF back into Streak and attach it to the correct box. You can store it in the box’s files, update fields, or trigger your next workflow step.


### Can Zapier update multiple fields in Streak after a document is signed?


Yes. In the Edit Box action, Zapier can update any field in your pipeline—stages, dates, amounts, or custom fields—based on the DocuSign data returned in the envelope.


### Why is the “Find Contact” step required?


Streak boxes can include multiple contacts, so Zapier needs to know which contact to send the document to. The “Find Contact” step ensures the correct email is used.
