## How to migrate from Zendesk Sell to Streak (step-by-step)


Zendesk lets admins export data from their Sell accounts, including:


- Leads
- Contacts
- Deals
- Notes
- Tasks
- Smart lists


However, Zendesk cannot export activity history, appointments, emails, call logs, or documents. Plan ahead for how you’ll handle this information — for example, keeping archives or moving important notes into fields that can be exported.


### Step 1 — Export your data from Zendesk Sell


Teams can[export Zendesk Sell data](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408828443674-Exporting-data-from-Zendesk-Sell) in a few simple steps.


1. In Sell, go to Settings → Tools → Data Export.
2. Click Request data export.
3. When the export completes, you’ll receive an email with a download link to a ZIP file.


⚠️ **Important:** each link is valid for 30 days, so be sure to download and save your export promptly.


If you have advanced needs or very large datasets, Sell’s REST API and Firehose are available, but most teams will not need this.


### Step 2 — Install Streak and create your first pipeline


1. **Install Streak.** Add Streak to your Gmail inbox from the[Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/streak-crm-for-gmail/pnnfemgpilpdaojpnkjdgfgbnnjojfik) or Mac App Store for Safari users.
2. **Create pipelines.** Streak's customizable pipelines can be created in minutes and allow you to mirror your Sell pipelines.
3. **Add stages and fields.** Create columns for all required data (text, date, dropdowns, checkboxes, etc.).
4. **Invite your team.** Add teammates and set permissions to access the pipelines and boxes they need.
5. **Enable automatic email sharing.** Turn on sharing so Gmail threads for linked Contacts/Organizations appear in box timelines automatically.


### Step 3 — Import your CRM data into Streak


You can import data into Streak’s Pipelines, Contacts, and Organizations.


#### A) Import Deals (Boxes) into your pipeline


Import deals directly into your pipelines. You'll be able to include related contacts and organizations for each deal. Contacts and organizations will automatically be added to your contact list and organization list when you import them as part of a deal in your pipelines.


1. **Open the Streak importer in your target pipeline.** Choose the CSV option to import your data from Zendesk Sell.
2. **Map your columns.** Map each column to a Streak field (e.g., Stage → Stage, Owner → Assigned To).
3. **Import the full file.** Complete the import to add deals to your pipelines.


#### B) Import Contacts


Import directly to your contact list to add people who aren’t yet linked to a deal (e.g., unqualified prospects or leads).


1. **Open your contact list.** Click “Import” and choose your file.
2. **Map your columns.** (Email → Email, etc.).
3. **Import.** Complete the import to populate your contact list in Streak.


#### C) Import Organizations (Companies)


Import directly to your organization list to add businesses that aren’t associated with deals in your pipeline.


1. **Open your organization list.** Click “Import” and choose your file.
2. **Map your columns.** Ensure organization names match the names you referenced in your Contacts/Deals files.
3. **Import.** Complete the import to populate your organization list in Streak.


### What doesn’t transfer automatically (and workarounds)


- **Email templates / sequences:** rebuild key templates in Streak or your sending tool.
- **Tasks:** Use shortcuts to create tasks quickly in Streak, automate new task creation.
- **Call/SMS logs and recordings:** keep in your archive; add critical notes to boxes or import into a Notes column or historical reference.
- **Attachments:** store in Drive and link important files in boxes.


Need help setting up your account? Each team receives onboarding included in their Streak plan, which gives you access to a Customer Success Representative who can help you import data and transfer over any data not included in the import.

### Why is Zendesk retiring Sell?


Zendesk says it’s focusing resources on its core customer service and employee experience products.


### Will Zendesk migrate my data to Pipedrive automatically?


No. Zendesk provides resources and recommends Pipedrive, but you may choose your next CRM option and are responsible for exporting and migrating your data.


### What happens if I don’t migrate?


You’ll lose access to Sell after August 31, 2027, and your data will be deleted.

No, you must export your data before the shutdown date. Export links remain valid for 30 days once generated.
