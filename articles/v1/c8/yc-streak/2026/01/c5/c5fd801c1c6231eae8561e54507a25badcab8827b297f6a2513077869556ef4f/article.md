# LinkedIn CRM Integration: Automatically Turn Profiles into Enriched Contacts

LinkedIn is where many sales conversations start. Streak makes it easy to turn those LinkedIn profiles into CRM contacts—without copying information into another tool.


With Streak’s LinkedIn integration, you can create contacts directly from LinkedIn and automatically capture key contact details, including:


- First and last name
- Email address (when available)
- Role and company
- LinkedIn profile URL


You’ll also see Streak context directly on LinkedIn, so you can quickly tell if your team is already working with a prospect and jump into your CRM when you’re ready to follow up.

## How Streak’s LinkedIn integration helps you prospect smarter


The LinkedIn integration is designed to make prospecting faster, clearer, and more collaborative. Instead of juggling tabs or guessing whether someone is already in your CRM, you can create enriched records in Streak and immediately see relevant context while you’re on LinkedIn.


### Save LinkedIn profiles to Streak


The Send to Streak button lets you add prospects from LinkedIn directly to your CRM.


You can:


- Add someone to your contact list
- Create a lead (box) in any of your pipelines


When you save a profile, Streak creates a contact and fills in commonly missing details so your record is usable right away.


### See when your team is already in touch


Prospecting gets messy when you don’t know if someone is already in your CRM.


With this integration, you’ll see whether a LinkedIn profile already exists in your team’s contact lists or pipelines. That means you’ll know right away if:


- A teammate has already reached out
- You’ve worked with them in the past
- They’re part of an active deal


No more duplicate outreach or stepping on each other’s toes.


### Jump into the full picture when you need it


The LinkedIn integration gives you control over when and how you move from LinkedIn to Streak.


Add prospects early to keep your pipeline accurate, then keep messaging them on LinkedIn without breaking your flow. When the time is right — for example, when someone becomes more qualified or shows real interest — jump to Streak to start working the deal and tracking full email history, notes, and tasks.


It’s a flexible way to capture leads early and manage relationships deeply, all without losing momentum.


## How to set up and use the LinkedIn CRM integration


Enabling the LinkedIn integration takes less than a minute—no new extensions to install.


1. **Turn it on in Streak** In Gmail, go to **Settings → See all settings → Streak settings** .
Scroll down to the External Integrations → LinkedIn “Send to Streak” section and click **Enabled** .
2. **Grant Permissions**
Allow the necessary permissions for Streak to integrate with LinkedIn.
3. **Open a LinkedIn profile** Head to LinkedIn and view any person’s profile. You’ll see a **Send to Streak** button on the page.
4. **Add them to your CRM** Click **Send to Streak** and choose whether to **create a Contact** or **create a Lead (box + contact)** . If you’re on multiple teams or have several pipelines, select the right one. If you don’t have a pipeline yet, Streak will automatically create a **Sales** pipeline for you.
5. **See Streak context on LinkedIn** After saving, you’ll see the contact or lead details right on their LinkedIn profile—including when they already exist in your team’s contact lists or pipelines. When you’re ready to go deeper, click the contact or lead to open it in Streak.
