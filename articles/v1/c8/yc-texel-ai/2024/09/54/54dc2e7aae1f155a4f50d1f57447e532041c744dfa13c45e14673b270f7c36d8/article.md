# Texel Announces Partnership with Kapwing to Power Auto Lip Sync Service

Texel is proud to announce our partnership with[Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/) , integrating **our advanced auto lip sync service into their video editing platform as the default provider for their new**[AI Personas](https://www.kapwing.com/ai/personas) **feature** . Kapwing users can now automatically synchronize lip movements in their videos to any script, choosing from a set of pre-defined personas or creating their own.


Texel’s auto lip sync service is rooted in cutting-edge developments from the open-source community, and our API is designed to make video processing more accessible and scalable. **It uses a technique where no training is necessary.** Our partnership leverages Kapwing’s popular video editing platform to help bring easy AI-based video creation to a large user base.


Over the past year, we have worked with Kapwing to experiment with a number of AI-first solutions including image generation and eye contact. Since then, we have worked hand-in-hand with the Kapwing team on this feature to get it to where it is today. **The optimized video pipeline’s performance, our fantastic support, and competitive costs are why Texel’s lip sync service is the default on the Kapwing platform.**


Things you can now do with Kapwing’s AI Personas and Texel today:


- **Personalized video creation** : Want to send a customized message to your entire team? Record a single video, generate a personalized script for each team member, and let the platform take care of the rest.
- **Create content in multiple languages** : Have a training video you need in multiple languages? Use the same video, translate the script to your preferred language, and our lip sync service will cover as many different languages as you’d like, with minimal effort.
- **Fix old recordings** : Got a video where you forgot what to say and there’s an awkward silence? Generate an audio file of your voice and plug it in to AI Personas to fill the gap and make it look seamless, without re-recording the whole thing.


The integration of Texel’s fast, reliable auto lip sync service ensures that creators can stay on schedule and produce professional videos without compromising quality.


We’re very excited to see how content creators leverage this new feature, and we look forward to continuing our collaboration with Kapwing to push the boundaries of AI-based video creation technologies.


Of course, this is just the beginning with much more to come! 🚀
