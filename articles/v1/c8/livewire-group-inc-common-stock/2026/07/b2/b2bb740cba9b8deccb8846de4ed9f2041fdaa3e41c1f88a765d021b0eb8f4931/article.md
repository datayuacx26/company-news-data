# RECAP: RELOAD LAND 2026


Berlin set the backdrop. Formula E brought the energy. Reload Land 2026 turned it into something you could feel.


Set just steps from the circuit, this year’s Reload Land existed in perfect tension. On one side, the precision and speed of world-class racing. On the other, a growing movement redefining what two wheels look and feel. And right in the middle of it all, a community showing up to be part of it.


Started in a garage, Reload Land now moves at a global scale.


Forget the traditional motorcycle show format. Reload Land continues to push into something more immersive. Part exhibition, part cultural experience. And this year, the energy hit differently.


## A PRESENCE BUILT FOR WHAT’S NEXT


This was our first event with a fully reimagined footprint. New van, new kit, and a new way of showing up. At the center: the S4 Honcho.


Positioned alongside Alpinista Corsa, our S2 lineup and LiveWire ONE, the space became less about display and more about conversation. People didn’t just pass through. They hung out. Asked questions. Looked closer. Came back again after test rides to ask more questions.


A huge thank you to everyone who spent time with us, took in the details, and leaned into what we’re building.


## TEST RIDES IN THE HEART OF THE CITY


With Berlin buzzing and Formula E drawing crowds from around the world, we put bikes directly into people’s hands.


From first-time riders to seasoned media and creators, the test ride experience carried that same moment we see every time. The shift from curiosity to understanding the second the throttle is twisted.


It wasn’t about numbers. It was about connection and that look when someone comes back from a ride and smiling from ear-to-ear.


## THE SILENT RIDE: BERLIN, REIMAGINED


Saturday evening. The city sets the tone.


Hundreds of riders moving through Berlin without the usual engine noise. Just wind, motion, and presence. With Formula E still echoing in the distance, the contrast couldn’t have been clearer: two visions of electric performance, happening in the same moment.


To everyone who rode with us, you made that moment what it was.


## FROM DAY TO NIGHT


As the sun set and the main event cleared, we shifted gears to the Reload Land x S4 Honcho afterparty.


Smaller, more intimate. A reset after the intensity of the day. Riders, builders, and friends reconnecting off-bike, sharing stories, letting the night stretch out a little longer.


Those are the moments that stick.


## MORE THAN AN EVENT


Reload Land isn’t just about motorcycles.


It’s designers, artists, technologists, commuters, and tastemakers all coming together around a shared curiosity for what mobility can become. Especially in a city like Berlin, where history, subculture, and forward motion all collide. And being set against Formula E only amplified it. Two sides of the same future, playing out in real time.


## LOOKING FORWARD


We left Berlin with more than memories.


We left with momentum.


Grateful to Reload Land for continuing to build something that’s real, intentional, and growing fast. Grateful to everyone who came through, spent time with the S4 Honcho, rode with us, and brought their energy into the space.


If you were there, you felt it.
If you weren’t, you’ll want to be next year.


“IT’S NOT ABOUT WATCHING WHAT’S NEXT. IT’S ABOUT BEING PART OF IT.”
