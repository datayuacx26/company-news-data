# The new Mednet Search: a better way to find trusted, physician expert answers – faster.

At theMednet, physicians come to get clear, trusted guidance on real clinical questions. With over 25,000 answers from 4,000 expert physicians across 21 specialties and subspecialties, finding the right information quickly is essential.


Today, we’re introducing a better way to[Search](https://www.themednet.org/v2/search/questions) , and get answers faster.


**Type your full clinical question – instantly find expert answers**


Until now, search on theMednet worked like most platforms: physicians typed in a few keywords around a specific topic, then sifted through matches. With theMednet’s new[Search](https://www.themednet.org/v2/search/questions) , you can now **type your full clinical question** – just as you’d ask a colleague – and get answers from experts who’ve addressed similar cases before.


**Built for how doctors actually search**


theMednet’s new[Search](https://www.themednet.org/v2/search/questions) doesn’t just match keywords, it understands clinical context and returns relevant expert Q&As, with highlighted excerpts that show exactly where your question is addressed.


It’s fast, intuitive, and designed around how you think.


You can now:


**Ask your question the way you’d ask a colleague.**


For example, if you’re a hematologist needing guidance on discontinuing anticoagulation in a DVT patient, you could search, “Do you use D-dimer to guide the duration of anticoagulation in patients with VTE?”


Or perhaps you’re a primary care physician or neurologist seeking peer input on managing nighttime leg cramps, you might search, “What are your preferred treatments for nocturnal leg cramps?”


**Explore a general topic by using keywords.**


Looking for a broader overview? Enter a keyword or phrase to quickly explore expert discussions and insights.


For example, you can search “Brain fog” to browse recent questions and expert discussions on the latest strategies for managing cognitive symptoms in patients.


Similarly, searching for “CHALLENGE trial” will surface current expert insights on theMednet related to this recent clinical trial for colon cancer patients.


We’ve tested our[new Search experience](https://www.themednet.org/v2/search/questions) with physicians over the past few months, and made significant upgrades based on user feedback. You’ll see more results per page, with a smarter, snappier search that helps you get to the right information, faster.


**What Comes Next**


We’ll continue improving[theMednet Search](https://www.themednet.org/v2/search/questions) , with a focus on making it even better at handling nuanced clinical questions and helping you find answers across specialties. We’re also working on expanding this enhanced search experience to include clinical practice guideline-based questions, making it easier to search for answers aligned with evidence-based standards of care. All theMednet users can start using the new[Search](https://www.themednet.org/v2/search/questions) today at themednet.org. Just log in and ask your full question in Search to get started.
