# NearWave Co-founder Wins First Place in 1st Source Bank Commercialization Awards

We are happy to announce that NearWave co-founder Thomas O’Sullivan was awarded first place in 1st Source Bank’s 2023 Commercialization Awards. The Commercialization Award is presented annually to faculty from the University of Notre Dame or the Indiana University School of Medicine-South Bend for exceptional research discoveries and the role they have played in supporting the translation of these discoveries into marketable products and/or services. The first-place award carries a $25,000 cash prize. We are very grateful to have received this award!
