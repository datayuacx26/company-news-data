# Code of Balance Wellness Toolkit: Nourishing Your Roots

Welcome to the WWCode Code of Balance — your wellness toolkit for diverse technologists. This month, we’re exploring “nourishing your roots” as a diverse technologist. Engage with this toolkit through thought-provoking self-reflections, valuable resources, cool tools, affirmations to guide you, and a curated Spotify playlist to empower you throughout the workday.


As we celebrate Earth Day, it’s a reminder of the interconnectedness between nature and our lives. Much like the intricate web of roots that anchor a towering tree, our journey as diverse women in technology is deeply intertwined with our roots – our values, experiences, and connections. In this spirit, Earth Day serves as a powerful metaphor for the importance of nourishing our roots as we navigate the ever-evolving landscape of the tech industry. Just as a healthy ecosystem relies on the vitality of its roots to flourish, nurturing our roots empowers us to thrive as technologists, advocates, and leaders. Join us as we explore the metaphorical parallels between “nourishing our roots” and Earth Day, uncovering insights and inspiration for cultivating resilience, growth, and sustainability in our tech journeys.


This toolkit will help you nourish your well-being and give you a healthier foundation for your tech career journey.


❣️ *Just a reminder, we are not mental health professionals—we are your techie besties sharing what works for us! If you are experiencing any health issues or mental health crises in the workplace, please contact your People Team/HR or a mental health professional.*

**Affirmations for Self-Nourishment**


An affirmation is a positive statement you say or think that reinforces self-belief or personal goals. Research tells us that affirmations work because of neuroplasticity — the brain’s ability to reorganize itself by forming new neural connections in response to learning. You can change your brain by changing your thinking, which can support a more constructive mindset.


Take a few minutes, find a quiet place, and read this month’s WWCode affirmations on nourishing your roots as a diverse technologist:


- I am proud of my diverse background and the valuable perspectives I bring to the tech industry.
- My unique experiences empower me to navigate challenges and seize opportunities in my tech journey.
- I am committed to continuous learning and growth, expanding my skills and expertise in technology.
- I embrace my cultural heritage as a source of strength and inspiration in my career as a technologist.
- I am a champion for diversity, equity, and inclusion, advocating for positive change in the tech community.
- I am a mentor and role model, empowering others from underrepresented backgrounds to pursue careers in tech.
- I prioritize my well-being and balance, nurturing my resilience and sustainability in the fast-paced tech industry.
- I lead with integrity, fostering inclusive environments where everyone can thrive and contribute their best.
- I am a lifelong learner seeking new challenges and opportunities to innovate and make a difference.
- I trust in my abilities and value my contributions, knowing that I have the power to create positive change in the world of technology.
- These affirmations can serve as reminders for diverse technologists to embrace their identity, strengths, and potential, empowering them to nourish their roots and thrive in their tech careers.
