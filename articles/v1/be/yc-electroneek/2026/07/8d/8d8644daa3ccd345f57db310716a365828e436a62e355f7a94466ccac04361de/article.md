In parallel, the team built strong in-house automation expertise, enabling them to configure and implement complex use cases at scale. In 2026, Factum plans to close three new strategic deals and collaborate with ElectroNeek on conferences and joint marketing initiatives to further strengthen brand positioning in their market.
