# ElectroNeek 12.0: Smarter Automation, Powered by AI Agents

Automation is evolving, and so is ElectroNeek. Since our last major release, we’ve been redefining what’s possible with AI-driven automation. With **ElectroNeek 12.0** , we’re bringing AI Agents front and center—seamlessly integrated into our **Process Management** module to give businesses more flexibility, intelligence, and control over their workflows than ever before.


## **Meet AI Agents: The Brainpower Behind Your Processes**


Imagine an automation assistant that doesn’t just follow the rules but actively helps you get things done. That’s exactly what AI Agents bring to the table. Embedded within **Process Management** , they can **analyze data, retrieve information, and make decisions within workflows** , making automation smarter and more adaptive.


- **Dynamic Decision-Making** : AI Agents can now evaluate conditions, suggest next steps, and even trigger the right automations based on real-time data.
- **Seamless Integrations** : Connect AI Agents with existing business systems, CRMs, and knowledge bases for faster, more accurate responses.
- **Enhanced AI Interactions** : Users can now create multi-step AI-driven workflows, combining large language model capabilities with structured automation tasks.


## **What Else is New in ElectroNeek 12.0?**


### **Smarter IntelliDocs for Flawless Document Processing**


IntelliDocs has undergone a **major upgrade** , making document processing more powerful and flexible than ever. With an improved interface and advanced customization options, extracting and organizing data from documents is now faster and more efficient.


- **Redesigned Interface for Better Navigation** : The updated IntelliDocs UI makes it easier to create and manage document processing workflows, ensuring a smoother experience.
- **Customizable Recognition Parameters** : Fine-tune how documents are processed by defining specific recognition rules, improving accuracy and control.
- **New Document Templates** : Pre-configured templates simplify the setup process, allowing users to quickly extract data from commonly used document formats.
- **Enhanced Import and Export Capabilities** : Users can now seamlessly import and export document processing configurations, making it easier to replicate workflows across different projects.
- **Bulk Web Recognition** : Enables users to process multiple web-based documents simultaneously, streamlining data extraction and reducing manual effort for businesses handling large document volumes.


### **Process Management with More Control & Visibility**


With AI Agents now embedded in **Process Management** , automation workflows are smarter than ever. But we didn’t stop there—we’ve also introduced powerful upgrades to give you more control over execution and visibility into what’s happening behind the scenes.


- **Guest Users for Task Execution** : You can now invite guest users to participate in workflows, allowing them to execute assigned tasks without granting full process control. This makes it easier to collaborate with external teams while maintaining security and oversight.
- **Flexible In-Process Logic** : The Logic Stage in Processes now allows unlimited custom cases instead of just three pre-determined paths. Users can define conditions that trigger specific actions, such as advancing to a stage, jumping to any stage, or finalizing a job with success or failure. A **Default Case** ensures an action is always executed if no conditions match.
- **Easier Workflow Management** : The improved **job execution system** allows users to adjust the order of process stages as needed for greater flexibility.
- **Full Control Over Running Jobs** : Users can now **pause, resume, or cancel jobs** while they are in progress. If a job is paused mid-task, it will resume correctly when restarted, ensuring smooth execution without data loss.
- **UX and UI Enhancements** : Improved navigation and usability updates make managing processes more intuitive, reducing friction in workflow creation and execution.


### **A Faster, More Responsive Platform**


Nobody likes waiting around. That’s why we’ve worked under the hood to make ElectroNeek **run faster, load quicker, and execute workflows with greater efficiency** .


- **Optimized Workflow Execution** : Enjoy noticeably faster automation execution times, reducing delays in critical processes.
- **UI & Navigation Enhancements** : A refreshed experience that makes it easier to find what you need, when you need it.
- **Reduced Processing Overhead** : Lower resource consumption means smoother automation, even for large-scale workflows.


## **Lead Center: Empowering Automation Businesses**


ElectroNeek 12.0 introduces **Lead Center** , a powerful tool designed to help automation businesses grow by providing a steady stream of new leads. Now available for qualified partner accounts, Lead Center connects businesses with potential clients and streamlines the sales process.


- **A Constant Flow of New Leads** : Access fresh leads directly within the platform.
- **Partner Quota for Contact Reveal** : Control how many contact details can be unlocked.
- **Limited Partner Access to Leads** : Ensure fair distribution by capping the number of partners who can engage with each lead.
- **Built-in CRM for Sales Tracking** : Manage and track sales progress with an embedded CRM.
