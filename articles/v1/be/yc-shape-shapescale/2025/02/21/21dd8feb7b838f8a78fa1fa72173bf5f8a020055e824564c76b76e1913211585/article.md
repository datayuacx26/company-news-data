# Unlocking Growth in Aesthetic Medicine: Insights from Dr. Johnny Franco & Austin Plastic Surgeon

In a recent webinar hosted by ShapeScale, Dr. Johnny Franco, founder of Austin Plastic Surgeon, shared how 3D body scanning technology is transforming his approach to patient care and practice management. This webinar highlighted the potential of data-driven solutions for aesthetic clinics seeking to improve outcomes, strengthen client relationships, and boost operational efficiency.


## Meet Dr. Johnny Franco and Austin Plastic Surgeon


Dr. Johnny Franco is a board-certified plastic surgeon renowned for his expertise in body contouring and aesthetic transformations. As the founder of Austin Plastic Surgeon, one of Texas’s most innovative practices, Dr. Franco combines cutting-edge technology with a commitment to holistic patient care. His journey into integrating 3D scanning was inspired by the need to better understand patient transformations and to offer personalized, data-backed treatment plans.


Reflecting on his approach, Dr. Franco remarks, “ *Patients want to see progress in ways that go beyond the scale. Technology allows us to show them the bigger picture.* ” This philosophy underpins his use of advanced tools to elevate both clinical results and the patient experience.


## Challenges Before Adopting 3D Scanning


Prior to implementing 3D scanning, Dr. Franco’s clinic faced common challenges in aesthetic practices. Many patients focused solely on their weight, often missing key metrics like muscle gain or fat distribution changes. This limited their ability to fully appreciate the impact of treatments. Additionally, inconsistent manual measurements and the lack of visual data made it harder to communicate progress effectively, potentially impacting patient retention.


“ *It’s difficult for patients to visualize the results of their treatments without clear data,* ” Dr. Franco explains. These gaps highlighted the need for an innovative solution to improve tracking and communication.


## How Austin Plastic Surgeon Uses 3D Scanning


At Austin Plastic Surgeon, 3D scanning technology has become an integral part of the patient journey. From initial consultations to follow-up visits, it plays a pivotal role in engaging patients and showcasing their progress. By offering precise, comprehensive body composition analyses, the technology helps patients focus on meaningful changes beyond the number on the scale.


Weekly scans allow Dr. Franco’s team to monitor shifts in muscle mass, fat reduction, and overall body composition. During consultations, these results are displayed visually to highlight progress, even when weight loss plateaus. This data-driven feedback motivates patients to stay committed to their treatments and fosters trust in the process.


“ *By showing patients real, measurable results* ,” Dr. Franco notes, “ *we’re able to build stronger relationships and keep them engaged in their journey.* ”

## Key Benefits Of ShapeScale for Austin Plastic Surgeon


Since incorporating 3D scanning technology, Austin Plastic Surgeon has experienced remarkable improvements in both clinical outcomes and business performance:


- **Improved Patient Engagement** : The visual nature of the scans provides patients with tangible proof of their progress, fostering a deeper understanding of their results.
- **Enhanced Retention** : Patients are more likely to continue their treatments when they can see clear, data-backed progress. Many now stay engaged for longer periods.
- **Streamlined Operations** : Automated measurements save time, allowing the team to focus more on delivering personalized care. This has also enabled the clinic to serve more patients efficiently.


Additionally, the clinic has leveraged the technology for marketing. Sharing before-and-after scans with patient consent on social media has generated buzz, attracting new clients. By creating unique, visually engaging content, the clinic stands out in a competitive market.


## Pro Tips for Clinic Owners On How To Integrate ShapeScale Into Practice


Dr. Franco’s experience offers several key strategies for clinics looking to integrate 3D scanning technology:


- **Educate Patients** : Use visual data to explain treatment progress and set realistic expectations.
- **Incorporate into Marketing** : Highlight the technology in your promotional efforts to differentiate your practice. Free scans during consultations can be an effective draw.
- **Focus on Long-Term Relationships** : Offer ongoing scans as part of follow-up visits to keep patients engaged and motivated.
- **Optimize Workflow** : Use automated metrics to save time and ensure consistency in tracking progress.
