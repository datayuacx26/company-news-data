# Century Oaks Energy Center helps strengthen local power grid reliability in Huron County

**BAD AXE, Mich., July 8, 2026 -** NextEra Energy Resources today celebrated the start of operations at the Century Oaks Energy Center in Huron County with a ribbon-cutting event alongside Consumers Energy and community members. The project brings low-cost, reliable energy, supports local jobs and delivers long-term value for Huron County and for the state of Michigan.


The 200-megawatt (MW) battery energy storage facility is owned and operated by Century Oaks Energy Storage, LLC, a subsidiary of NextEra Energy Resources, LLC, America’s largest energy infrastructure developer and builder.


**Why it matters:**


- Century Oaks Energy Center supports grid reliability by safely storing excess energy and delivering it back to the grid when demand is highest. The facility supports greater energy integration, improves grid performance and provides a cost-effective solution to meet Michigan’s growing energy needs.


- Battery energy storage systems act like a reserve for the electric grid, helping make power available when homes and businesses need it most, playing a key role in meeting the region’s growing energy needs.


- By storing electricity and delivering it when it is needed most, facilities like Century Oaks Energy Center support reliable service for homes, businesses and essential services across the region.


**By the numbers:**


- 200 MW of battery energy storage capacity
- An investment of more than $279 million
- Approximately $34.9 million in projected tax revenue over 30 years
- More than 300 construction jobs created


**Community impact:** Century Oaks Energy Center supported local businesses during construction and continues to contribute to economic activity across Huron County. The project is expected to generate millions in additional tax revenue benefiting schools, infrastructure and essential public services in the community.


**A word from** **Consumers Energy vice president David Hicks:** “Consumers Energy is excited to collaborate with NextEra Energy Resources to use the power from Century Oaks to meet the needs of our growing state. Century Oaks and other battery storage projects help us to provide affordable power to nearly 2 million homes and businesses across Michigan.”


**A word from NextEra Energy Resources vice president Dexter Liu:** “You can already see the impact a project like this has in Huron County, from the jobs and local activity during construction to the economic benefits created now that Century Oaks is up and running. We’re grateful for the strong support from Colfax Township and Huron County, whose collaboration helped make this project possible. It’s also rewarding to see Colfax Township recognized with the EGLE Michigan Renewables Ready Communities Award for its leadership in bringing this 200-megawatt battery energy storage project forward. We look forward to building on these relationships as we continue operating Century Oaks and working together for years to come.”


NextEra Energy Resources has a longstanding presence in Michigan, supporting local communities through energy investments and contributions to schools and nonprofits across the state. As part of this ongoing commitment, during the ribbon-cutting ceremony, the company presented a donation to the Huron Area Technical Center to support workforce training and local students.
