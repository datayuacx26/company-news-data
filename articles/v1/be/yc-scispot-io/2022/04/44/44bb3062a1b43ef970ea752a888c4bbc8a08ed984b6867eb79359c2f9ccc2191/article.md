# Scispot Impact : Micro Meat

Our Co-founder & CPO, Satya Singh sat down with Gricelda Mendiola, Researcher/Scientist at Micro Meat, to talk about how Scispot is helping Micro Meat digitize and automate their workflows.


‍


**What was the problem you needed to solve?**


We had trouble documenting our protocols, data, and images in a comprehensive way that would make it easy for researchers to use and reproduce. We also needed a way to automate the protocols that we execute multiple times a day.


‍


**What do you particularly like about Scispot?**


I’d say the templates. When a protocol needs to be repeated, you can templatize it with the click of a button and use it as many times as you want without losing any data or missing any protocol steps.


‍


**How would you summarize your experience as a whole?**


The platform is very accommodating to new users because it’s easy to navigate and familiarize yourself with.
