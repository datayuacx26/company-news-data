## **How to Connect Your ATS to ChatGPT (Step-by-Step Guide)**


### Step 1: Enable Developer Mode in ChatGPT


Go to:


**Settings → App & Connectors → Advanced Settings**


Turn on Developer Mode.


### Step 2: Create a New Connector


Go back to **Enabled Apps & Connectors** , then click **Create** .


###


### Step 3: Add Stardex ATS


In the connector setup:


-


Name: **Stardex ATS**


-


URL:[https://stardex.com/mcp](https://stardex.com/mcp)


###


### Step 4: Authorize Your Account


You'll be redirected to Stardex to log in and approve access.


Click **Allow** .


Once done, Stardex will appear in your connected apps.


###


### Step 5: Update Candidates Using Plain English


This is where the integration becomes really powerful.


Instead of manually updating a candidate profile, you can tell ChatGPT what to do.


For example:


"Move this candidate to the final interview and add a note that they're actively in process."


ChatGPT handles everything in one go. It updates the stage, adds the note, and logs the activity inside Stardex ATS.


In a real workflow, it looks like this:


The call ends. The candidate is moving to the final interview, and you told the client you'd log it right away.


You type:


"Move Seth to the final interview, note that the client flagged a strong cultural fit."


Done. Stage updated, note added, activity logged even while you're still on the call. No "I'll update that later" that turns into tomorrow.


### Step 6: Confirm the Update in Stardex


After ChatGPT completes the action, you can quickly check inside your Stardex ATS.


You'll see the candidate moved to the correct stage, with the note added.


This shows that ChatGPT isn't just suggesting actions, it's actually executing them inside your ATS in real time.


### Step 7: Generate Reports Instantly


You can also use ChatGPT to generate reports from your ATS data.


For example, asking about the geographic distribution of candidates will analyze the data and return a structured summary, often with tables or charts.


Instead of exporting data and building reports manually, you ask.


Here's what that looks like in practice:


A client emails asking how many candidates you've submitted across three open roles and which are still active.


You type:


"Submission summary for XYZ Search Firm, all open roles."


You reply before they've even moved on to their next email.


### Step 8: Get Quick Candidate Summaries


Need a quick refresher before a meeting? Just ask ChatGPT to summarize a candidate.


It will pull together key details like experience, notes, and highlights into a clean overview you can review or share.


This is especially useful when you need to get up to speed fast without digging through profiles.
