## **Why do recruitment agencies need specialized software?**


Recruitment agencies operate differently from corporate hiring teams. The challenges are unique. The workflows are complex. The technology requirements are specific.


-


**Agencies manage two-sided marketplaces simultaneously:** They source candidates while selling services to clients. Corporate ATS platforms optimize for one-sided hiring. They assume a single employer with internal requisitions. This fundamental mismatch creates friction everywhere.


-


**Client relationships span years and multiple placements:** Generic systems treat clients as basic contact records. They lack the depth agencies need for account management, pipeline tracking, and relationship nurturing across long sales cycles.


-


**Speed determines competitive advantage:** When a client calls with an urgent need, agencies must surface qualified candidates instantly. Slow search functionality costs placements to faster competitors. Sub-second response time becomes non-negotiable.


-


**AI maturity varies dramatically across platforms** : Some vendors label basic keyword matching as artificial intelligence. Others build genuine machine learning that recognizes patterns and improves with usage. The difference shows up in placement velocity and quality.
