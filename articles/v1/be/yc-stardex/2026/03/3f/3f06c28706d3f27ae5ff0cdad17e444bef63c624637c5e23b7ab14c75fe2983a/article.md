## How to Connect Your ATS With Claude (Stardex Step-by-Step Guide)


### Step 1: Connect Stardex ATS to Claude


Start by connecting Stardex inside Claude.


Go to **Connectors** , then:


-


Click **Customize**


-


Click the **+ button**


-


In the modal, enter ‘Stardex ATS’ as name and then add Stardex MCP URL` https://stardex.com/mcp`


-


Click **Add**


-


Verify that, in Connectors, you see Configure as an option (instead of Connect, which you saw earlier). This means that the connector is successful.


Once added, Stardex will appear as a tool Claude can use. This is what links your ATS to Claude so it can access your recruiting data.


*Note: You need Claude premium account to enable the connector. Once connected, it can work across Claude, Claude Cowork as well as Claude Code.*


### Step 2: Authorize Access


After adding the connector, you’ll need to approve access.


Click **Connect** , then click **Allow** when prompted.


This step gives Claude permission to read and update your ATS data. Without it, Claude won’t be able to take any actions inside Stardex.


### Step 3: Enable Always-Allow Permissions


Once connected, you can set Stardex tools to **Always Allow** .


This removes the extra step where Claude asks for permission every time it does something. With this turned on, actions like searching for roles or updating candidates happen instantly, making the whole experience feel much smoother.


### Step 4: Start Using Your ATS Through Chat


Now open a new chat in Claude.


At this point, your ATS is fully connected, so you can start asking questions like:


“What are all the VP of Engineering roles that we’ve worked on?”


Claude will look up your Stardex data, find matching roles, and return them immediately. You don’t need to filter or click through anything; it just works like a conversation.


Now imagine this in a real scenario:


It’s 8:57. Your client call starts in three minutes. You remember there’s a VP of Marketing search, but you can’t recall who’s furthest along or what happened after the last interview.


You type: “Where are we on the VP of Marketing search?”


By 8:59, you have everything. Candidates ranked by stage, the latest notes on each, and even a signal that one candidate has gone quiet for 12 days. You walk into the call fully prepared.


### Step 5: Explore Roles and Candidates


Once Claude gives you results, you can naturally ask follow-up questions.


For example, who was placed in a role, which search is the most active, or which candidates are furthest along. Claude keeps track of context, so you don’t have to repeat details.


This made it easy to quickly understand which role had the most activity and who the key candidates were, without ever having to open the ATS manually.


### Step 6: Update Candidates Using Plain English


This is where the integration becomes really powerful.


Instead of manually editing a candidate profile, you can tell Claude what to do. For example, you can say:


“Move this candidate to the final interview and add a note that they’re actively in process.”


Claude will handle everything in one go; it updates the stage, adds the note, and logs the activity in Stardex.


No clicking, no switching tabs, no manual updates.


In a real workflow, it looks like this:


The call ends. The candidate is moving to final interview, and you told the client you’d log it immediately.


You type: “Move Marcus to final interview, note that the client flagged strong cultural fit.”


Done. Stage updated, note added, activity logged even while you’re still on the call. No “I’ll update that later” that turns into tomorrow.


### Step 7: Confirm the Update in Stardex


After Claude completes the action, you can check inside your Stardex ATS.


You’ll see that the candidate has been moved to the correct stage and the note has been added. This shows that Claude isn’t just suggesting actions, it’s actually executing them inside your ATS in real time.


### Step 8: Generate Reports Instantly


You can also use Claude to turn your ATS data into reports.


For example, asking about the geographic distribution of candidates will prompt Claude to analyze the data and generate a structured summary, often with tables or charts.


Instead of exporting data and building reports manually, you ask, and Claude does the work.


Here’s what that looks like in practice:


A client emails asking how many candidates you've submitted across their three open roles and which are still active.


You type: "Submission summary for Acme Corp, all open roles."


You reply to their email before they've moved on to their next message.


### Step 9: Get Quick Candidate Summaries


The next time you have a meeting and need a quick refresher, just ask Claude to summarize a candidate.


It will pull together key details like experience, notes, and highlights into a clean overview you can quickly review or share. This is especially useful when you need to get up to speed fast without digging through profiles.


We’ve seen this in[how our customers actually use this feature](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sanketchauhan_always-great-when-customers-share-feedback-activity-7407136895199191040-uMaQ?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAEOOg14BvNE4fJp7d13xtUxmXGqp3-4gXQ8) .


One of our customers was in a car on the way to a candidate call and needed to prep quickly. Using just his phone, he asked Claude for the candidate’s contact info.


In seconds, he got a record of their recent interactions and a quick summary of the candidate’s background—even though he only asked for the candidate’s contact number.


No switching apps nor devices. Just the info he needed, right when he needed it.
