# The Best ATS Tools for Executive Search Firms in 2026

Choosing the best ATS (Applicant Tracking System) for executive search firms can be challenging. Most systems claim to be "senior leadership hiring" platforms, but in reality, each one has its own quirks and may not be designed with executive search in mind. Here's a breakdown of the most popular ATS tools in 2026, along with what users actually say about their pros and cons.


Before making a choice, here are some key considerations to keep in mind:


## **Thrive**


Thrive was created by the global executive search firm True. So, like Encore, it's also purpose-built to help firms secure the top talent that organizations need. It positions itself not just as ATS/CRM, but as a TRM (Talent Relationship Management) system built for executive search. That means it's conscious of factors such as candidate lifetime value, network reuse, and, therefore, fee potential.


One of its biggest strengths is its deep fee-tracking capability, which enables firms to closely monitor revenue from placements, particularly in retained search or senior leadership hiring, where the fee structure is complex and nuanced.


However, it shares a few common downsides with other tools: it takes time to load, and searching across internal notes can be frustrating. Some users also note that while it excels at managing relationships, it lacks strong CRM functionality, leading many teams to pair it with a separate CRM tool. If you want to minimize app switching, Thrive may not be the best choice for you.


## **Clockwork**


Clockwork is known for being easy to use, but it's also one of the more static systems out there. It lacks automated or customizable reporting, which means teams often spend hours each week manually exporting data to build reports. They have recently started adding some more bolted on AI features.


There are also several issues with data formatting and consistency. Additionally, the client portal requires users to log in, which can hinder collaboration and productivity. Similar to Thrive, searching through notes is not very seamless. Clockwork's CRM features are limited, and its[resume parsing tool has been reported to be inaccurate](https://www.g2.com/products/clockwork-recruiting/reviews/clockwork-recruiting-review-671580Opens%20a%20new%20window) . And some of the pre-formulated status options are confusing and don't make sense.


Another user in the management consulting business shared that[Clockwork lacks automation features](https://www.g2.com/products/clockwork-recruiting/reviews/clockwork-recruiting-review-714942) essential for tracking candidates and keeping records of call frequency.


*"There aren't more features to expedite certain processes. For example, a "log a call button" that logs a call automatically."*


If smart automation features are non-negotiable for you, Clockwork may not be the best ATS for you.


## **Bullhorn Invenias**


Invenias is the executive search solution offered by Bullhorn, one of the oldest and most established names in recruitment software. This is both its biggest strength and its weakness. Because it has been around for so long, the user experience feels "outdated" and isn't clunky compared to modern tools.


You often have to pay extra for add-ons to unlock features that competitors now include by default. And yes, the same issue shows up again: it's hard to search across internal notes. On the CRM side, Invenias doesn't offer much power or integration either, so many teams rely on external CRMs to fill the gap.


Recent user feedback has revealed that[there is friction at every stage of the hiring process](https://www.capterra.co.za/software/132632/invenias?review=Capterra___6680286#highlighted-reviewOpens%20a%20new%20window) .


*"Adding people takes so many steps that our people gave up. They would skip verification and create massive duplicates. We had to find a tit's party and pay them to do what Invenias's competitors don't in their product."*


Indeed, expensive add-ons for features like custom reports and advanced analytics make it less practical. In fact, the same user also pointed out that Invenias' reporting feature is underperforming, which is also the same concern shared by Bullhorn users who have noted that Bullhorn isn't ideal for reporting unless Bullhorn Analytics is packaged in. So if you want seamless reporting with no additional cost, then Bullhorn Invenias is not for you

## **Ezekia**


Ezekia stands out for having good auto-tagging features, which help you organize candidate data. However, users often point out that the UX and reporting features feel weak.


Users also point to disconnected workflows, requiring them to toggle between tools for calls, meetings, and reporting, which results in additional manual effort to keep everyhing in sync.


Ezekia reportedly requires recruiters to log notes manually, update statuses, and piece together information from different channels, which slows down candidate delivery and client engagement.


Just like Invenias, Ezekia's static reports or basic dashboards are no longer meeting the level of detail and customization that many clients demand.


Ezekia is a simple tool that gets your recruitment tasks done, but it lacks the polish and features that growing executive search firms might want as they scale.


## **Encore**


Encore by Cleun has a solid concept behind it: a well-designed "workbench" that enables executive search firms to build and manage candidate lists easily. While it's especially popular among firms in the finance sector, Encore is also used by a diverse range of clients worldwide, including executive search consultants, government agencies, and universities.


The technology behind Encore was developed by an internal team with extensive shared experience in the executive search field. However, while it's reliable and niche-focused, it's not the most flexible or fastest option available out there.


Many users mention that it takes a while to load, and you can't search across internal notes, which can make finding past information a bit tedious. There's als no Chrome extension, and doesn't integrate well with note-taking tools like Otter unless you use third-party connectors like Zapier.


As one user from the staffing and recruiting industry shared:


*"This product is quite behind its competitors, and it feels like an old 'DOS' computer as compared to other similar products. It is also extremely slow and lacking many desirable features." -*[Jill M](https://www.capterra.com/p/44509/Encore/#SoftwareAdvice___382137) *.*


If you want to prioritize speed and flexibility, Encore may not be the best choice for you.
