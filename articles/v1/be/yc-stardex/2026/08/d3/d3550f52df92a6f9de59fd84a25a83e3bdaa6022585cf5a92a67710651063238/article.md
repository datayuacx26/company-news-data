# Product Update #20

The latest Stardex product updates make recruiting and executive search workflows faster, more flexible, and easier to manage. June’s releases include overlapping candidate insights, AI-generated scorecard drafts, customizable client share links, business development forecasting, Gmail integration, and several workflow improvements.


##


## Overlapping candidates


When you look at someone's experience, you'll now see an overlap badge showing where their time lined up with other people you've worked with as well as funding trajectory when they were there. Good for spotting potential backchannel references.


## Candidate visibility on share links


You can now control exactly which candidates show up on an individual client share link, as well as which fields, notes, and sections a given client sees, instead of it being all or nothing.


## AI scorecard drafts


We added detailed scorecards last month, and now there's a "Generate with AI" button in the scoring modal that drafts the scorecard for you based off the interview. This way, you are editing a first pass that pulls in the relevant context instead of starting from a completely blank slate.


## Customize your home page


Your home page can now be customized! You can now add a KPI band with the metrics you care about (fill rate, offers out, tasks due, and more), pick a layout, and arrange the widgets, searches, tasks, interviews, etc. Let us know what other widgets will be helpful for us to support here.


## Send follow-ups on nurture campaigns


For nurture email campaigns, you can now keep sending follow-ups even after someone replies. Useful when you're trying to stay top of mind over a longer stretch rather than running one-and-done outreach.


## New reporting, search-level and BD forecasting


You can now pull reports at the individual search level as well as BD forecasting by upcoming months to project your business development pipeline


## Gmail in the extension


The Stardex extension now works right inside Gmail. You can pull contacts and log activity without leaving your inbox


## Quality of Life:

Drag and drop candidates in list view: Sort your pipeline by “Latest stage first” in the “Stage” column and you can now drag and drop candidates for custom ordering within a stage.


-


New External User permission tier, so you can give people outside your firm limited access without handing over a full seat. External users can be set to only have access to notes in searches they are assigned to.

Edit deal win and loss reasons right from settings.
