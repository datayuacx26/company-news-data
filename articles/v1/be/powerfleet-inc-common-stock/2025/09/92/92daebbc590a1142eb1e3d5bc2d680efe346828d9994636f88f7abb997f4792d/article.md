# The ‘Fatal 5’: Powerfleet’s answer to road safety’s deadliest threats

In 2024, an estimated[39,345](https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/nhtsa-2023-traffic-fatalities-2024-estimates) people died in traffic crashes in the U.S. Behind each number is a family forever changed. Despite safety improvements, hundreds of lives are still lost each day to preventable accidents. The “Fatal 5” refers to the five most dangerous factors that contribute to road accidents. Let’s examine these threats and how AI, especially Powerfleet’s AI-driven solutions, is working to combat them.


## **1. Distraction: The silent killer**


Whether it's texting, talking on the phone, eating, drinking, chatting with passengers, or fidgeting with your GPS, distraction remains a leading cause of road accidents worldwide. Though these distractions are common and may seem harmless, they can be deadly. Driving demands full attention, and any distraction raises your risk of crashing.


[VisionAI,](https://www.powerfleet.com/us/products/on-road/) Powerfleet’s AI-driven video IoT solution, uses in-cab cameras to detect distractions, like phone use, and alerts drivers in real-time, potentially preventing accidents before they happen.


## **2. Fatigue: Drowsiness turns deadly**


Fatigue poses a silent but deadly threat on our roads. Aside from negatively affecting your health, drowsiness can lead to motor vehicle crashes, impaired cognition, and workplace accidents.


You can avoid fatigue by getting a good night’s sleep, especially before long car journeys or early commutes. It is recommended that you get[7-8 hours of sleep per night](https://www.nhtsa.gov/risky-driving/drowsy-driving) . Avoid heavy drinking if you know you will be behind the wheel the next day. Driving while hungover can increase impairment and drowsiness. Additionally, if you are on prescription medication, be sure to check the side effects to see if it causes drowsiness.


VisionAI’s advanced eye-tracking and facial recognition technology can detect signs of fatigue, such as prolonged eye closure or head nodding, alerting drivers or fleet managers before drowsiness becomes a safety concern. This intervention is crucial, as drowsy driving is responsible for an estimated[100,000 crashes each year worldwide](https://applications.emro.who.int/EMHJ/V28/09/1020-3397-2022-2809-695%E2%80%93700-eng.pdf?ua=1) .


## **3. Speeding: The fast track to disaster**


Speeding significantly reduces a driver’s ability to respond to hazards and increases the likelihood of more severe accidents. Speeding accounts for a staggering 54% of global traffic deaths, with a life lost roughly every 49 seconds due to excess speed, according to[ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666691X24000344) . These sobering facts show the devastating impact of speeding, not just on drivers but also on innocent road users. The deadly results of this reckless behavior highlight the urgent need for effective speed management solutions to save lives on our roads.


VisionAI’s intelligent speed adaptation systems utilize GPS and real-time data to help drivers maintain safe speeds, issuing warnings when speed limits are exceeded.


## **4. Impairment: A lethal mix**


Impairment caused by alcohol and drugs severely impacts a driver’s judgment, coordination, and reaction time.[NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/risky-driving/drunk-driving) reports that alcohol-impaired driving imposes a devastating toll on American roads. On average, the United States loses nearly 34 lives each day to drunk-driving incidents. This means a tragic fatality occurs roughly every 42 minutes.


While AI can’t prevent impaired driving directly, VisionAI can monitor erratic driving patterns and alert fleet managers, enabling immediate intervention. Proactive prevention could be the difference between safety and losing another life.


## **5. Seatbelt non-use: A simple fix, often ignored**


Wearing a seat belt greatly lowers the risk of death and serious injury in a car crash. Despite this, not wearing a seat belt remains a significant factor in road fatalities, even though it is one of the simplest safety measures to enforce.


VisionAI includes smart seatbelt reminders and occupant detection systems to ensure drivers buckle up. These systems[are crucial](https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/seat-belts) , as seatbelts decrease the risk of death by 45% and reduce the chance of serious injury by 50% in the event of a crash.

### **The road ahead**


AI isn’t a cure-all for road safety, but it’s a powerful tool. VisionAI aims to create a safer driving environment by not only reacting to dangerous situations but also predicting and preventing them. This marks a significant shift in how we approach road safety.


However, it’s important to remember that technology can help, but safe driving ultimately depends on human choice. As we adopt these AI solutions, let’s also commit to making responsible decisions while behind the wheel. Looking ahead, AI has the huge potential to save lives on our roads. It’s not just about cutting down accidents; it’s about building a safety culture that impacts every part of how we drive. With ongoing innovation and dedication, we can turn the goal of zero road deaths into reality.
