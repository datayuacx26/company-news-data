# Why Use ArchForm for In-Office Aligners

If you're an orthodontist looking to elevate your practice and streamline your aligner treatments, it's time to discover ArchForm—the software solution transforming in-office aligner production.


**Why Choose ArchForm for Your Practice?**


### Complete Control Over Patient Care


With ArchForm, you're empowered to design fully customized treatment plans tailored precisely to each patient's unique smile. The intuitive tools make tooth movement, attachment placement, and treatment staging simple, precise, and efficient—giving you full clinical control and ensuring superior outcomes for your patients.


### Streamlined, Powerful Features


ArchForm simplifies even the most complex cases:


- **Automatic Alignment and Attachment Tools:** Effortlessly automate tooth alignment and attachment placement, reducing tedious manual tasks.
- **Advanced Staging Capabilities:** Easily handle complicated movements like sequential distalization, overcorrection, bite adjustments, and extractions with confidence.
- **Bite Management and Pontics:** Seamlessly adjust deep bites and manage extraction cases with smartly adaptable pontics, delivering consistent and predictable results.


### Significant Cost Savings


Producing aligners in-house with ArchForm drastically reduces costs compared to third-party aligner labs, often cutting expenses by over 50%. Passing these savings onto your patients can make aligner treatment more accessible, boosting your case acceptance and profitability.


### Same-Day Aligners with 3D Printing


Combine ArchForm with in-office 3D printing, and you can offer same-day treatment starts. Imagine patients beginning their treatment the very same day—transforming their experience from weeks of waiting into hours of excitement.


### Easy to Learn and Integrate


ArchForm works seamlessly with a wide range of 3D printers and scanners, making integration into your existing workflow effortless. Its intuitive, user-friendly interface ensures your team can get started quickly without extensive training.


### Trusted by Orthodontists Worldwide


Orthodontists everywhere are praising ArchForm's impact on their practices. Dr. Gabriel Baumeier calls it "hands down the most user-friendly software for anyone getting started in digital orthodontics," highlighting its accessibility and efficiency.


### Stable and Predictable Pricing


In an industry often plagued by unpredictable cost increases, ArchForm remains committed to stable and transparent pricing, ensuring your practice can confidently budget without surprise expenses.


Embrace ArchForm and unlock the potential of your orthodontic practice. Deliver personalized care, enhance your patients' experiences, and position yourself at the forefront of orthodontic innovation.
