### Where Video Fits in the FinServ Revenue Lifecycle


The right video, sent at the right moment, does more than reduce no-shows. It builds the kind of relationship that


[converts a prospect into a long-term client](https://www.lairedigital.com/blog/creating-content-that-moves-financial-buyers) . Four moments in the financial services revenue lifecycle stand out as the highest-impact opportunities for personalized video.


#### Early Engagement & Pre-Meeting Confirmation


The moment a meeting is booked is when trust is most fragile.


A personalized video from your advisor, delivered automatically through HubSpot, immediately differentiates your firm. It puts a real face behind the calendar invite, reinforces why this meeting matters, and dramatically increases the likelihood that your prospect shows up ready to engage.


#### Onboarding


The first 90 days of a new client relationship determine whether you have a client for a year or a client for life.


Automated, personalized video touchpoints during onboarding keep clients engaged and reduce confusion. Better still, they create the feeling of white-glove service without requiring your advisors to spend hours on repetitive calls.


#### Ongoing Education & Market Announcements


When market volatility hits, or a regulatory change affects your clients’ portfolios, a personalized video from their advisor arrives like a trusted voice in a noisy room. It’s proactive, human, and the kind of communication that generates referrals.


#### Meeting Follow-Ups


The deal rarely closes in the meeting. It


[closes in the follow-up](https://www.lairedigital.com/blog/why-financial-firms-lose-leads-after-the-click) .


Send a personalized video recap within an hour of your conversation, and you keep the momentum alive. Your key points stay fresh, and more importantly, your face stays top of mind while your prospect weighs their decision.
