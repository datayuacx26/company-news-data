# Pre-Treatment Tips for Best Back Waxing Results

## **Prep for the Best Smooth Yet**


When it comes to achieving seriously smooth results, a little preparation goes a long way.


Back waxing works best when your skin and hair are ready for it. Taking a few simple steps before your wax helps with easier hair removal, less irritation, and a better experience from start to finish.


Ready to get smooth? Here’s exactly how to show up for your next wax at European Wax Center.


## **Why Back Wax Prep Matters**


Proper prep is about comfort and getting the results you want. Prepping your skin before your reservation helps the wax adhere to the hair, not the skin, making removal cleaner and more successful.


When your skin is calm, clean, and hydrated, your Wax Specialist can remove hair directly from the root. That means smoother skin for longer, fewer bumps, and less post-wax redness.


You’ll also help to minimize sensitivity and make it easier for your Wax Specialist to work efficiently, so your session is as quick and comfortable as possible.

## **Don’t Shave Before You Wax**


If you want a smooth finish, resist the urge to shave before your wax. Hair that’s too short can’t be gripped by the wax, which makes removal less effective.


Ideally, your hair should be about ¼ inch long—roughly the length of a grain of rice. This gives your Wax Specialist enough to work with for a clean, thorough wax.


If you recently shaved, let your back hair grow out for two to three weeks before your wax. It may feel a little awkward in between, but it’s worth it for optimal results.


## **Exfoliate, But at the Right Time**


Exfoliation is key to keeping your skin soft, clear, and ready for waxing, but timing matters.


You should exfoliate 24 to 48 hours before your reservation—not the day of. This helps lift dead skin cells, unclog pores, and free any trapped hairs so the wax can properly adhere.

## **Hydrated Skin = Happier Skin**


Well-hydrated skin is flexible and resilient, which means it can recover faster after waxing. It also helps prevent hair breakage during waxing for smoother, more even results.


In the week leading up to your wax, apply moisturizer daily to your back or have someone help with hard-to-reach areas. However, skip lotion or oil on the day of your reservation, as it can create a barrier that prevents the wax from adhering properly.


In addition to hydrating your skin, drink plenty of water the day before and the day of your reservation.


## **Dress for Waxing Success**


What you wear to your reservation matters more than you might think.


Choose loose, breathable clothing, like a cotton T-shirt or soft pullover, to avoid friction on freshly-waxed skin. Tight fabrics or rough materials can cause irritation.


Make sure the shirt you’re wearing is clean (or bring a fresh one to change into afterward). This helps reduce the risk of bacteria or sweat irritating open follicles.


After your wax, avoid wearing heavy gym bags or backpacks that rub against your back, especially for the first 24 hours.


## **What to Avoid Before Your Wax**


To help keep your skin healthy and irritation-free, there are a few things to skip before your back wax:


-


No tanning or sun exposure


for at least 48 hours prior.


-


Avoid retinoids or acne medications


that thin the skin. Call your center for further instruction if you have any specific questions.


-


Skip oils and lotions


on the day of your reservation; they can interfere with wax adhesion.


Following these simple guidelines will help your skin stay calm and ready for the smoothest possible results.
