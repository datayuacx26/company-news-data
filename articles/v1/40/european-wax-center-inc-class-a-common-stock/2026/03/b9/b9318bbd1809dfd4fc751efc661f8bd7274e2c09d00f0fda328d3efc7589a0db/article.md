# Why Full-Face Waxing Might Just Be Your Next Obsession

If you’re on the hunt for a beauty service that offers multiple benefits, full-face waxing might be the upgrade your routine has been missing. Guests love this hair-removal treatment not only for its smooth results, but also for the skincare benefits that keep their complexion looking fresh, radiant, and makeup-ready. From improved application of foundation to enhanced product absorption, full-face waxing delivers results you can see and feel.


## **Better makeup application starts with a smooth canvas**


Full-face waxing removes all types of facial hair, including peach fuzz, while also lifting away dry, dead skin cells. The result is a smoother surface that allows foundation, concealer, and other makeup products to glide on evenly. Many guests notice their makeup looks more seamless and polished immediately after a wax.


If makeup tends to cling unevenly or settle into texture, this simple step can make a noticeable difference.


## **Brighter, bouncier-looking skin**


Waxing doubles as a form of physical exfoliation. By removing the top layer of dead skin cells, full-face waxing helps renew the skin’s surface and support a brighter, more refreshed appearance. With consistent waxing, guests often notice skin that looks smoother, more luminous, and healthier overall.


****


## **Better skincare absorption**


Exfoliation does more than improve how skin looks. It also helps skincare products work more effectively. When a build-up of dry skin is removed, serums, moisturizers, and treatments absorb more easily and may even deliver better results.


If you invest time and care into your skincare routine, pairing it with regular full-face waxing can help you get more out of every product you use.


## **How to Prep for Facial Waxing**


Because waxing is already a type of exfoliation, you’ll want to avoid using acids like AHAs or BHAs on your skin in the days leading up to your wax. If you use retinol, stop using it for a week before your service to reduce skin sensitivity.


On the day of your wax, arrive with clean skin free of heavy oils or excessive makeup. We’ll take care of the rest!


Be sure to care for your skin after your service with regular, gentle cleansing and a moisturizer appropriate for your skin type. Always wear SPF when heading outside to protect your newly-waxed skin.
