## **Preparing for the Perfect Wax**


### **The Week Before**


For the best possible results, follow these pre-wax guidelines:


-


Ensure your hair is at least ¼" long (about the length of a grain of rice) so the wax has something to grip.


-


Exfoliate 1-2 days before your appointment using a gentle scrub or cloth to buff away dead skin cells.


-


Avoid excessive sun exposure; we cannot wax over sunburned or damaged skin.


### **The Day of Your Appointment**


-


Skip lotions and oils in the target area; they can interfere with the wax's effectiveness.


-


Avoid excessive alcohol or caffeine, which can increase sensitivity.


-


Wear loose, comfortable clothing to prevent friction on freshly waxed skin.


-


Arrive a few minutes early to complete any necessary paperwork and relax before your service.
