# Know All Your Options for Eyebrow Treatments

Your eyebrows are one of the most important features of your face. They frame your eyes, enhance your expressions, and are often one of the first things people notice about you. Choosing the right brow style for your face can truly be a game-changer for your look.


Because eyebrows are so important, people have come up with numerous methods for grooming, shaping, and enhancing them to keep them looking their best. Our experts at European Wax Center (EWC) break down each method so you can choose the one that’s right for your brows, budget, and lifestyle.


## **Brow Enhancements (Defining, Coloring)**


### **Microblading**


Microblading is a semi-permanent form of cosmetic tattooing. Unlike traditional tattoos, microblading uses a blade-shaped tool with a row of tiny needles to create hair-like strokes along your brows while depositing pigment into your skin. While the results can be long-lasting, microblading requires healing time and may sometimes result in an unnatural appearance.


Potential Cons of Microblading:


-


High cost, typically ranging from $400-$600


-


Potential for unnatural results


-


Risk of scarring, post-inflammation, or allergic reactions


-


Tints the skin, but not the brow hair


### **Lamination**


Eyebrow lamination involves using a chemical treatment to straighten and manipulate brow hairs, making them appear fuller. However, it doesn’t change the color of brow hair, and it may not be ideal for sensitive skin.


Potential Cons of Lamination:


-


Can cause dryness or damage to brow hairs if done too often


-


Not suitable for sensitive skin


-


Costs range from $80-$160

## **Brow Grooming (Hair Removal, Shaping)**


### **Threading**


Threading involves twisting and rolling a cotton thread over unwanted hair to remove it at the root. While it’s a popular method, threading can be painful and time-consuming, with the thread sometimes cutting the skin and potentially resulting in scarring.
