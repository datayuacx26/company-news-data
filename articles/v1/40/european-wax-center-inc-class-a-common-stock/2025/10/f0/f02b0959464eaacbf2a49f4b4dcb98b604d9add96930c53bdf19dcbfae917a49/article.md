# Our Guide to Quick and Easy Peach Fuzz Removal

Peach fuzz — those soft, fine hairs on your face — is completely natural, but if you prefer a smoother look and feel, you are not alone. Many people choose to remove peach fuzz to create a flawless base for makeup, help skincare products absorb better, or simply because they like the way their skin looks without it. The good news? Getting rid of it can be quick, easy, and totally doable as part of your regular routine.


## **What Exactly Is Peach Fuzz?**


Peach fuzz, also called vellus hair, is the thin, light-colored hair that covers most of your body. On the face, it is most noticeable along the cheeks, jawline, and upper lip.


## **Why Remove Peach Fuzz?**


The choice to remove peach fuzz is personal, but some of the most common reasons include:


-


Smoother makeup application


: Foundation, powder, and blush glide on more evenly without fine hairs in the way.


-


Brighter-looking skin


: Removing peach fuzz also takes away a layer of dead skin cells, giving skin a fresh glow.


-


Better product absorption


: Without hair acting as a barrier, serums and moisturizers can sink in more effectively.


-


Confidence boost


: For some, smooth, fuzz-free skin just feels better day to day.

## **How to Prepare for Peach Fuzz Removal**


If you are scheduling a wax, let the hair grow to at least ¼ inch long so the wax can grip it effectively.


Gently exfoliate a day or two before your wax to help lift any trapped hairs and remove dead skin cells. Avoid heavy lotions or oils on the day of your service. Try to wear as little makeup as possible to your facial wax. We’ll do a pre-wax cleanse, so no worries if you have a little!


## **Aftercare Tips for Smooth Results**


-


Avoid touching your face for the first few hours to reduce the risk of irritation.


-


Skip makeup for at least 24 hours to allow your skin to recover.


-


Protect freshly waxed skin with sunscreen


-


Keep up a gentle exfoliation routine a couple of times a week to maintain smoothness.
