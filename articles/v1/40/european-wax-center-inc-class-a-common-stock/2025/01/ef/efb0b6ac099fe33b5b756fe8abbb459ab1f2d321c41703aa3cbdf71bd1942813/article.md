# What to Know about How Long Does Waxing Last

Waxing is one of the most popular ways to achieve a long-lasting smooth, and it’s no wonder why! Waxing removes hair from the root, leaving your skin hair-free for weeks and helping you cut back on daily shaving. At European Wax Center, we make the experience as quick, comfortable, and effective as possible with our signature Comfort Wax® and expertly trained Wax Specialists.


Want to make the most of your waxing results? Understanding how to care for your skin between sessions is key! Keep reading to learn how to maximize and extend the results of your wax.


## **Typical Timelines for Waxing Results**


### **How Long Does a Wax Take?**


Most waxing services at European Wax Center take 15-30 minutes, depending on the area. Whether you’re waxing your legs, arms, or eyebrows, our Wax Specialists are trained to deliver an efficient yet thorough service.


### **How Long Does Waxing Last?**


On average, you’ll enjoy smooth skin for 2-3 weeks. Most guests re-book by week three or four, at which point the hair has grown out enough for another wax. The exact timing depends on factors like your hair growth rate and the area being waxed. For more visible areas like eyebrows, face, or underarms, some guests even prefer to schedule touch-ups every two weeks.

### **Hair Type and Growth Rate**


Everyone’s hair grows differently, but we recommend waxing when your hair reaches about ¼” in length (roughly the size of a grain of rice). Areas influenced by hormones, like the face and bikini line, tend to grow faster, so staying closer to the three-week mark may be best. Not sure how often to book? Your Wax Specialist can recommend a schedule tailored to your hair and skin type.


## **Factors Affecting How Long Waxing Lasts**


### **Hair Growth Cycles**


When you first start waxing, it may take up to three sessions to get all your hair on the same growth cycle. After this, you’ll experience fewer stray hairs between waxes and longer-lasting smoothness. Consistency is key—sticking to a 3-4 week schedule ensures optimal results as your hair grows back thinner and softer over time.


### **Skin Type and Hair Thickness**


No matter your skin type or hair texture, our Comfort Wax® is designed to work for everyone—even those with sensitive skin or coarse hair. Plus, every wax gets easier as your hair becomes finer with time.


### **Consistency**


Unlike shaving, which cuts hair at the surface and leaves a blunt, stubbly end, waxing removes hair from the root. The more you wax, the softer and thinner your hair becomes, making the process easier and your results longer-lasting. However, shaving or plucking between waxes disrupts this process, so we recommend staying consistent with waxing to see the best benefits.
