## **Factors That Affect Brow Tint Longevity**


Here’s what to avoid to ensure your tint lasts as long as possible:


### **24 Hours After Tinting**


-


Avoid water and sweat:


Skip swimming, hot showers, and intense workouts for the first 24 hours to prevent fading.


-


Steer clear of harsh products:


Avoid salt water, chlorine, and strong skincare ingredients like exfoliants during this time.


-


Protect from the sun:


Prolonged sun exposure can cause the tint to fade faster.


### **Day-to-Day Between Tints**


-


Avoid Harsh Cleansers:


Harsh or exfoliating facial cleansers can strip your tint. Opt for mild, non-abrasive products to wash your face.


-


Avoid oil-based products:


Oils break down the semi-permanent dye, so stick to oil-free moisturizers and treatments around your brows.


## **Tips to Extend the Life of Your Eyebrow Tint**


-


Stick to a 3-4 week touch-up schedule:


Regular appointments will ensure your brows stay vibrant and defined.


-


Use the right products:


Try


[EWC GROOM® Clear Brow Gel](https://waxcenter.com/collections/brow/products/clear-brow-gel) to hold your brows in place without drying them out. For added definition between tints, use our


[EWC GROOM® Tinted Brow Gel](https://waxcenter.com/collections/brow/products/tinted-brow-gel) to enhance color and provide long-lasting hold.


-


Go gentle on your skin:


Mild cleansers and oil-free products will help maintain your tint longer.


-


Be mindful with skincare:


Keep exfoliating acids like alpha hydroxy acids (AHAs) and salicylic acid (BHA) away from your brow area.


-


Avoid heavy sweating immediately after your tint:


Perspiration can cause the color to fade prematurely.
