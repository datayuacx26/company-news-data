# Eyebrow Waxing vs. Threading: Why Waxing Wins Every Time

When it comes to professionally shaping your brows, two methods tend to stand out above the rest: waxing and threading. Both have their fans, but if you're looking for clean, defined brows with results that last, waxing (especially at European Wax Center) might just become your new go-to beauty ritual. Let's chat about the differences and why so many of our guests swear by our signature brow wax.


## **What is Eyebrow Threading?**


Eyebrow threading originates from an ancient technique that started in South Asia and the Middle East. It uses twisted cotton threads to pull hair from the follicle with precision. Threading doesn't involve heat or chemicals, which is great news if you have allergies. It can be precise when removing and shaping individual hairs, and it's usually relatively affordable.


While threading can create nicely shaped brows, it does come with some downsides. Threading can be uncomfortable and may irritate your skin, especially if your skin is sensitive or it’s your first time trying threading. It also takes longer than waxing since the technician is removing hairs one by one or in small groups. Your results can vary greatly depending on how skilled your threading specialist is, and the technique can leave redness or small tears that could potentially lead to scarring.


## **What is Eyebrow Waxing?**


Eyebrow waxing is a method that removes multiple hairs at once, which makes it a better option than threading or tweezing for a few reasons. First, it’s a more comfortable experience since you aren’t targeting individual hairs over and over. Second, it means waxing is a lot more time-efficient. Waxing is also great for picking up and removing peach fuzz around the brow area (difficult to do with other methods). This ultimately results in a cleaner, more defined look.

- Comfort

At EWC, our exclusive Comfort Wax® takes comfort to another level, making the experience even more pleasant. Our Wax Specialists are trained to create gorgeous, face-framing brows that complement your unique features. Waxing also picks up any peach fuzz around the brow, resulting in a cleaner look.

## **Tips for a Successful Eyebrow Waxing Experience**


Preparation:


If you use prescription retinol products like Retin-A, take a break from them about 7 days before your appointment. Clean skin is best for waxing, so try to avoid heavy lotions or oily products before you come in since these can interfere with the wax. For the best results, remove any makeup from the area before your appointment. Finally, enhance your results by exfoliating your skin 24-48 hours before your service using


[EWC TREAT® Face & Body Exfoliating Gel](https://waxcenter.com/products/face-body-exfoliating-gel) .


Aftercare:


After getting your brows done, give your skin a little TLC by avoiding harsh products like scrubs, retinol, or exfoliating acids around the area for 48 hours. It's also smart to skip heavy sweating and heat sources like saunas and hot tubs right after your service. Don’t forget to keep your skin healthy and happy by regularly moisturizing.


Frequency:


For brows that always look on point, schedule appointments every 3-4 weeks. Since facial hair can grow faster than body hair, and your eyebrows are such a visible feature, our Wax Specialists often recommend coming in closer to the 3-week mark to keep your brows looking their best.
