# Why Great Stories Depend on HDR Delivery


The way audiences experience stories is changing, but the importance of delivering them with fidelity has not.


April 2026


Valérie Allié


[HDR](https://www.interdigital.com/blog?tags=HDR)[Video](https://www.interdigital.com/blog?tags=Video)


A recent AP-NORC poll[found](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/spotlights/2025/streaming-is-overtaking-theaters-for-movie-watchers-an-ap-norc-poll-finds/) that nearly three-quarters of U.S. adults have chosen streaming over theaters to watch new movies in the past year.


The shift is unmistakable: our personal devices and living room environments have become the primary stage for cinematic storytelling. But as distribution evolves, viewer expectations rise alongside it.


## Great Storytelling Has Always Depended on Great Delivery


From the earliest days of film, creators have understood that storytelling is as much about how a story is seen as it is about how it is told. Light and shadow. Contrast and color. Subtle gradations and bold highlights. These are not technical details – they are creative decisions.


For decades, the cinema was the only environment capable of faithfully reproducing that vision. But today, content must travel far beyond the controlled conditions of a theater and across devices, media formats, networks, and viewing environments that vary widely.


The challenge is clear: how do you preserve creative intent when the cinematic screen is no longer singular, but scaled across various devices?


## HDR: The Bridge Between Creation and Experience


High Dynamic Range (HDR) has emerged as a cornerstone technology for answering that question.


Compared to Standard Dynamic Range (SDR), HDR dramatically expands the range between the darkest and brightest elements in an image. The result is more than just visual enhancement; instead it represents a closer approximation of how the human eye perceives the world. Deeper blacks, brighter highlights, richer colors, and more nuanced details all work together to create a more immersive and faithful viewing experience.


In short, HDR does more than just improve images – it protects storytelling. This technology ensures the emotional tone crafted in the studio is preserved when it reaches the audience, whether they’re watching on a premium television, a tablet, or a mobile device.


## Building on a Legacy of Innovation


At InterDigital, our work in HDR builds on a legacy that stretches back nearly a century – rooted in the Technicolor innovations that brought vivid color to the big screen in *The Wizard of Oz* . Among the first to be filmed in Technicolor, that iconic film redefined cinematic storytelling, proving that technology can elevate artistry.


Today, we continue that mission. The research and innovation of our Video Lab focuses on ensuring that every creative decision – every shadow, highlight, and color choice – is delivered exactly as intended. From studio master to final display, we develop technologies that maintain consistency, fidelity, and quality across the entire video chain.


This includes advancements in:


- Dynamic HDR conversion
- Metadata-driven optimization
- Color management systems
- Reference implementations for production and distribution


These innovations help ensure that content remains visually stunning and consistent, regardless of where or how it is viewed.


## Enabling a Complex, Multi-Format Streaming World


Modern streaming environments are far more complex than traditional broadcast or cinema workflows. Content must seamlessly transition between SDR and HDR formats, support multiple device capabilities, and accommodate dynamic elements such as live replays and targeted advertising.


InterDigital’s HDR technologies are designed with this complexity in mind. Our contributions to global standards like MPEG and SMPTE help ensure that advanced formats are not only high-performing, but also interoperable and scalable. These standards are critical to building a reliable ecosystem where content can move efficiently from creation to consumption without compromising quality.


In this way, HDR becomes more than a feature, but infrastructure for high quality video.


## From Innovation to Real-World Impact


As HDR adoption accelerates, so does the need for solutions that can deliver consistent, high-quality experiences across the entire workflow.


At NAB 2026, InterDigital showcased innovation enabling *HDR Master Production for Advanced Streaming.* Powered by the latest SMPTE ST 2094-60 dynamic metadata standards, this solution highlights how HDR content can be optimized for streaming environments that include ad insertion and mixed-format delivery, and is an example of how innovation translates into better outcomes for platforms, creators, and audiences alike.


## Protecting the Future of Storytelling


The future of entertainment will continue to evolve. Devices will diversify and viewing habits will change, but one principle will remain constant – great stories deserve to be seen as they were created.


At InterDigital, we are committed to ensuring that this remains possible through research, standards leadership, and technologies that safeguard creative intent at every stage of the journey.


In the end, the success of a story doesn’t just depend on how it’s told. It depends on how it’s delivered.
