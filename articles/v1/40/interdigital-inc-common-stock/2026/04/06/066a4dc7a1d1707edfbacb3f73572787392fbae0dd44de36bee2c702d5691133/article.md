# The Makings of an Inventor: Spotlighting Samir Ferdi


Discover how Samir Ferdi’s innovations in V2X, AI networks, and wireless security are advancing safer transportation and shaping the future of connected technology.


April 2026


InterDigital Comms


[AI](https://www.interdigital.com/blog?tags=AI)[Wireless](https://www.interdigital.com/blog?tags=Wireless)


InterDigital’s impact is spearheaded by innovation and the engineers that drive it. Our innovation portfolio and contributions to global standards shapes the foundational fabric that connects more than 8 billion devices worldwide and empowers them to enrich, enhance, and improve our lives.


This year, InterDigital named Samir Ferdi as the 2026 Inventor of the Year, recognizing his exceptional technical leadership, sustained innovation in wireless communications, and significant contributions to global standards.


Since joining InterDigital in 2007, Samir has contributed to innovations that underpin today’s connected world; from early wireless data systems to secure, intelligent networks supporting autonomous systems and AI-driven applications. His inventions span vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communications, device-to-device connectivity, unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) integration, and cellular network security-- innovations that are helping define the future of connected transportation, automation, and AI-enabled networks.


“Samir exemplifies what it means to be an InterDigital inventor,” said InterDigital CTO Rajesh Pankaj. “He combines technical expertise with a pragmatic understanding of standards development and real-world implementation. His ability to anticipate industry needs, influence global standards, and deliver solutions that are both innovative and deployable has had a lasting impact on our portfolio and on the broader wireless ecosystem.”


Upon graduating engineering school in Lyon, Samir began his career in software implementation and telecommunications infrastructure, where he gained insight into the trajectory of technology development, before transitioning to wireless standards innovation at InterDigital. These dual perspectives have been instrumental in Samir’s approach to innovation – one that is practical and solutions-oriented.


“I always say we’re in the business of problem solving. At the most fundamental level, that’s what we do,” Samir shared. “I’m interested in solving problems that can impact society… Being able to have an influence was something that was also attractive to me – to have an impact on what people do in their lives.”


In fact, Samir’s contributions to V2X technologies are helping to enable safer and more efficient transportation systems. By innovating advancements to cellular connectivity and intelligent device-to-device communications, his work has ripples of impact through systems that can detect hazards, avoid collisions, and operate beyond line-of-sight using secure network intelligence. His work offers a foundation for the development of autonomous vehicles, robotaxis, delivery robots, and drones, and their unique applications and impact.


“Vehicles, drones use this type of device-to-device \[technology\], and use the network intelligence to detect potential collisions, to make it safe for the public,” Samir explained.


Samir’s innovation impact extends to industrial automation and robotics, enabling intelligent logistics systems that can improve safety for workers or enhance production efficiency in warehouse environments.


In addition to contributing innovation that enables communication between diverse devices, Samir is also helping to shape the framework that governs these communications and establishes guardrails to help preserve privacy and protect from threats. As an active delegate and contributor to the 3GPP SA3 working group for the past eight years, Samir’s work addresses evolving cybersecurity threats and ensures next-gen wireless systems are trustworthy.


“Everybody is very sensitive now to privacy concerns,” Samir noted. “We try to bring solutions that \[…\] preserve the privacy of individuals and the safety and the integrity of both the communication and the devices… The solution has to be secure, robust, and reliable, because it goes back to trust,” he added. “Those foundational aspects, we build on that, and we make sure we have solutions that are always secure for now, but also for the next five to ten years.”


Samir emphasized that excelling as an inventor within 3GPP requires more than just innovation; it also requires strong advocacy for your ideas. The key, he said, is to be persistent in convincing the merit and need for your solution at every step of the way.


“You have to be creative, but also you have to be pragmatic. You have to know who is your consumer,” he said.


Samir’s ability to identify emerging threat vectors early, design practical and standards-ready solutions, and advocate for their adoption has strengthened InterDigital’s influence across multiple technology generations, from 5G to the emerging 6G landscape.


In addition to his technical contributions, Samir serves as a mentor, manages a team of engineers, and is helping to cultivate the next generation of inventors at InterDigital. He advises young innovators to be curious, continue to learn, and to leverage their network to learn from other inventors with breadth of experience and knowledge.


“InterDigital’s strong focus on being a company of inventors, of engineers, is something that young inventors should take advantage of,” he said.


We celebrate Samir’s technical leadership and innovation impact, alongside the myriad inventors at InterDigital whose innovation is empowering our world today, and into tomorrow.
