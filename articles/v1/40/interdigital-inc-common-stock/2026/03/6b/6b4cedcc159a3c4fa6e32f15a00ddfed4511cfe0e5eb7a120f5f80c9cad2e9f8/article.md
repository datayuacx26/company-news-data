# InterDigital at MWC 2026


InterDigital showcased groundbreaking 6G, AI, AR, and haptic innovations at MWC Barcelona. Explore demos shaping the future of wireless, immersive tech, and connected services.


March 2026


InterDigital Comms


[AI](https://www.interdigital.com/blog?tags=AI)[MWC26](https://www.interdigital.com/blog?tags=MWC26)[Video](https://www.interdigital.com/blog?tags=Video)[Wireless](https://www.interdigital.com/blog?tags=Wireless)


Every year, InterDigital attends MWC in Barcelona to showcase the cutting-edge innovation and technological leadership fostered within our[Research and Innovation Labs](https://www.interdigital.com/research-and-innovation) . InterDigital’s MWC showcase is the culmination of months of work and collaboration to provide tangible, interactive examples of the impact of InterDigital’s wireless, video, and AI expertise.


This year’s show was anchored in InterDigital research and innovation that is shaping 6G and empowering more devices, more AI-enabled services, and richer, immersive experiences at scale. Dive in to explore what we showed:


## Demos


**Leading 6G Research** : Our experts previewed some of InterDigital’s future-looking research, including the ways Extreme MIMO and Near Field will expand network capacity, Service Aware Radio Access can significantly improve quality of experience for an array of services, and GenAI Convergence can natively integrate communication and computing to support more Gen AI in the 6G era.

## ICYMI


Throughout MWC, we welcomed partners, industry peers, and government officials from across the U.S. and EU, including esteemed guests from the European Commission, Turkish delegation, U.S. Federal Communications Commission, ETSI, and the European Internet Forum.


Elsewhere at the show, our wireless leaders participated in GSMA-hosted 6G executive dialogues and roundtables on the research shaping the path to 6G. InterDigital was also a proud supporter of the AI Telco Troubleshooting Challenge, which awarded three teams for developing AI models that support more resilient, efficient and automated networks.
