# InterDigital 2024 Year-End Newsletter

# InterDigital 2024 Year-End Newsletter


December 2024


InterDigital Comms


<p>As we close out 2024, we reflect on a year of exceptional milestones, underpinned by our innovative spirit, strategic partnerships, industry leadership, and continued research in wireless, video, and AI. This newsletter spotlights InterDigital&rsquo;s achievements of the past year.</p>
<h2><span style="color: #00aeef;">2024 Spotlight on InterDigital</span></h2>
<p>InterDigital delivered strong financial results throughout 2024, driven by high-value licensing agreements and a robust patent portfolio shaped by our foundational research and innovation. In addition to <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-2024/default.aspx">exceeding revenue expectations</a> in Q3, and announcing a <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-Announces-Annual-Recurring-Revenue-Target-Of-1-Billion-By-2030/default.aspx">2030 revenue target</a> of $1B annually, InterDigital hosted its <a href="https://s25.q4cdn.com/626766191/files/doc_presentations/2024/Sep/10/Investor-Day-Presentation-FINAL-Oct-30-2024.pdf">first Investor Day</a> under current management, featuring presentations by President and CEO Liren Chen and members of the executive team outlining InterDigital&rsquo;s mission and vision.</p>
<p>The year was also punctuated by strategic licensing successes, including new patent license agreements with <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-signs-new-license-agreement-with-Samsung-Electronics/default.aspx">Samsung</a>, <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-signs-license-agreement-with-Google/default.aspx">Google</a>, <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/UPDATE----InterDigital-and-Lenovo-agree-to-enter-into-new-license-agreement--final-terms-to-be-determined-in-binding-arbitration/default.aspx">Lenovo</a>, and <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-signs-new-license-agreements-with-TPV/default.aspx">TPV</a>. InterDigital also <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-renews-license-agreements-with-Panasonic-Entertainment--Communication-Co.-Ltd/default.aspx">renewed</a> and signed new license agreements with <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-signs-license-agreement-with-Panasonic/default.aspx">Panasonic</a>, covering our 4G, 5G, cellular, HEVC video, and Wi-Fi patents. In 2024, we signed a landmark license agreement with <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-signs-license-agreement-with-OPPO-Group/default.aspx">OPPO Group</a>, which includes OPPO, realme, and OnePlus, marking a trend that reinforces the foundational nature of our research and innovation to the mobile ecosystem.</p>
<p>In 2024, we expanded our reach in different ways, hiring <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-appoints-Ken-Kaskoun-as-Chief-Growth-Officer/default.aspx">Ken Kaskoun</a> as InterDigital&rsquo;s Chief Growth Officer, leveraging his experience at Qualcomm and SomaLogic and background as an engineer, lawyer, and experienced inventor to drive InterDigital&rsquo;s growth and technological prowess in wireless, video, and AI. InterDigital also opened a new office location in <a href="https://www.interdigital.com/post/interdigitals-melville-office-opening-spotlights--research-innovation-and-academic-collaboration">Melville, Long Island</a> in addition to celebrating more than ten years of research and innovation in our <a href="https://www.interdigital.com/post/interdigital-recognizes-heritage-of-innovation--and-collaboration-at-london-office">London office</a>. InterDigital also announced several academic partnerships to amplify our research, including joining <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-Joins-Princeton-Universitys-NextG-Corporate-Program/default.aspx">Princeton University&rsquo;s Next G Initiative</a> and collaborating with <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/InterDigital-and-Concordia-University-Announce-Research-Collaboration-on-AI-enabled-Immersive-Media-Delivery-over-5G-Networks/default.aspx">Concordia University&rsquo;s Intelligent Next Generation Networking and Multimedia (IN2GM) Lab</a> on AI-enabled immersive media delivery.</p>
<p>A full scope of our research ingenuity, social impact, and environmental &ldquo;handprint&rdquo; was outlined in our <a href="https://s25.q4cdn.com/626766191/files/doc_downloads/sustainability/idcc_esg_052324_v3.pdf">2024 Corporate Sustainability report</a>, including an update on our commitment to DEI. Among the events hosted throughout the year, InterDigital&rsquo;s inaugural <a href="https://www.interdigital.com/post/recognizing-international-womens-day--with-interdigitals-inaugural-womens-network">Women&rsquo;s Network</a> hosted a <a href="https://ir.interdigital.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/Interdigital-to-Speak-at-the-36th-Annual-Roth-Conference/default.aspx">Fireside Chat</a> with Board members Jean Rankin, Joan Gillman, and our Chief Licensing Officer Eeva Hakoranta to discuss inclusive ways of working and advocacy for women in technology.</p>

<h2><span style="color: #00aeef;">InterDigital's&nbsp;Expertise</span></h2>
<p>Throughout the year, InterDigital publishes reports, eBooks, blogs, and other content outlining our research priorities while our experts contribute their expertise to the understanding of advancements and innovation in wireless, video, and AI.</p>
<h3><span style="color: #010644;">Wireless</span></h3>
<p>Building upon our heritage of wireless research, innovation, and standards contributions, we explored the current state of wireless evolution and introduced the critical technologies and trends shaping 5G Advanced and the road to 6G.</p>
<ul>
<li>InterDigital&rsquo;s white paper with ABI Research on <a href="https://www.interdigital.com/white_papers/driving-5g-advanced-to-6g">Driving 5G-Advanced to 6G</a> explores how 5G-Advanced is revealing new enterprise capabilities and paving the way for 6G. Experts shared their expertise in a <a href="https://www.interdigital.com/webinars/unpacking-the-potential-for-isac">webinar</a> that <a href="https://www.interdigital.com/post/unpacking-the-potential-for-isac">discussed</a> the benefits and impact of 6G-enabling technology ISAC for new services, verticals, and enterprises.</li>
<li>In addition to our report and webinar, InterDigital&rsquo;s expertise was quoted in <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2024/03/01/british-telco-giant-bt-expects-to-launch-5g-standalone-this-year.html">CNBC</a> discussing network slicing as a crucial step in the technological upgrades leading to 5G-Advanced, and in a <a href="https://www.telecoms.com/5g-6g/the-telecoms-industry-s-biggest-problem-failure-to-monetise-5g">Telecoms.com</a>&nbsp;article about the telecoms operators efforts to recoup investments in 5G infrastructure. InterDigital shared some insights with <a href="https://www.ibc.org/connective-tech/features/mwc-2024-5g-still-awaits-killer-app-as-mobile-industry-looks-to-ai/20653">IBC</a> on the mobile industry&rsquo;s search for breakthrough applications and enterprise use cases for 5G, and was interviewed in <a href="https://www.lightreading.com/6g/6g-unlike-recent-gs-might-actually-excite-consumers">Light Reading</a> for our expertise on reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS) and higher frequency bands as potential breakthroughs for 6G.</li>
<li>To foster a deeper understanding of ISAC, an important 6G enabling technology where InterDigital leads innovation, we published an <a href="https://www.interdigital.com/ebooks/integrated-sensing-and-communication-use-cases-a-glimpse-at-whats-ahead">eBook</a> explaining the convergence of telecom and sensing data through ISAC and the emerging use cases for network operators and users alike. Our engineers further detailed <a href="https://www.interdigital.com/post/understanding-integrated-sensing-and-communications-benefits-and-challenges">the benefits and challenges of ISAC</a>, and were positioned as leaders advancing 6G sensing capabilities in <a href="https://www.fierce-network.com/wireless/will-integrated-sensing-be-6g-winner-or-loser">Fierce Network</a>.</li>
<li>InterDigital was recognized in <a href="https://www.6gworld.com/blog/6g-and-integrated-sensing-communications-the-essentials/">6GWorld</a> for our role in 6G innovation and fostering ISAC&rsquo;s potential to supplement fixed broadband, and Alain Mourad was featured in <a href="https://www.thefastmode.com/expert-opinion/36780-exploring-how-isac-paves-the-way-for-smart-factory-implementation">The Fast Mode</a> explaining how ISAC paves the way for smart factory implementation.</li>
<li>Throughout the year, Doug Castor contributed to a deeper understanding of the 6G roadmap and the technology trends, limitations, and milestones that will define 6G, including in this <a href="https://www.6gworld.com/projects/6g-in-2024-what-to-look-for-why/">6GWorld webinar</a>. Milind Kulkarni elaborated upon the roadmap and outlined the direction 6G standardization is taking and goals for 6G research with <a href="https://www.6gworld.com/exclusives/future-3gpp-releases-might-be-bridge-to-6g-interdigital-vp-says/">6GWorld</a>.</li>
</ul>

</ul>
