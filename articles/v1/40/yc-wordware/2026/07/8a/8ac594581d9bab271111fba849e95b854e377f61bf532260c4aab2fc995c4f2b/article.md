## 3-Step Quick-Start


1.


**Duplicate the template** Wordware → *Explore* → **“Slack to Podcast Agent”** → *Duplicate* .


2.


[Connect your Slack](https://blog.wordware.ai/announcing-our-integration-with-slack) **workspace** Paste the OAuth token (the template walks you through Slack’s *Add to Slack* dialog). Pick the channels you want.


3.


**Pick a voice & schedule** Choose a voice preset, set daily time (e.g., 5 p.m.), hit **Run** .


Typical run-time: **< 45 s** for 250 messages.

## Tips for a Smooth Roll-Out


-


**Channel curation beats brute force.** Start with 1-2 high-signal channels to avoid 30-min episodes.


-


**Keep episode titles clear** : “Product-Stand-up · May 21 · 3 min”.


-


**Respect private data** : don’t include #hr-confidential or 1-1 DMs without permission.


-


**Invite feedback** after week 1—tweak summary length & voice speed.
