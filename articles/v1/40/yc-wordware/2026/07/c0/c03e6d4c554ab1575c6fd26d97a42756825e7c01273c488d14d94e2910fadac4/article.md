1.


Scraping a prospect company's website for relevant information


2.

3.


Generating a compelling subject line and email body


4.


Creating a draft directly in your inbox ready for review and sending


### **Who It's For**


Sales professionals, business development representatives, founders, and anyone who needs to connect with prospects through personalized outreach.


### **Why It Matters**


This agent saves hours of research and writing time while significantly improving response rates through genuine personalization. Instead of sending generic templates, you can scale personalized communication that resonates with each recipient.


The agent places drafts directly in your inbox, giving you final control to review, edit, and approve before sending. This human-in-the-loop approach ensures quality while automating the most time-consuming parts of the process.


## **Agent #2: Subscription Tracker That Saves Money**


We've all experienced subscription creep—signing up for services that slowly drain our accounts month after month, often forgotten until we review our credit card statements.


This second agent:


1.


Analyzes incoming emails to identify subscription confirmations and receipts


2.


Extracts key information including service name, cost, and billing frequency


3.


Organizes this data in a Supabase database (though it works with Google Sheets, Notion, or Airtable too)


4.


Creates a central dashboard of all your active subscriptions


### **Who It's For**


Anyone managing multiple subscriptions, finance teams tracking company expenses, or individuals wanting to optimize their monthly spending.


### **Why It Matters**


This agent creates visibility into recurring expenses that often fly under the radar. By maintaining an up-to-date database of all subscriptions, you can regularly review and cancel unused services, potentially saving hundreds or thousands of dollars annually.


The beauty of this approach is that it works passively in the background, requiring no manual data entry while continuously updating as new subscription emails arrive.


## **Agent #3: Email Urgency Detector That Reclaims Your Time**


Email overload is a universal problem. This third agent acts as your personal email triage nurse by:


1.


Evaluating each incoming email against a customizable scoring rubric


2.


Assessing factors like time sensitivity, sender authority, and consequences of delay


3.


Assigning an urgency score from 0-10


4.


Sending immediate notifications for truly urgent messages (scoring 7+)


### **Who It's For**


Busy professionals, executives, managers, and anyone struggling with email overload who needs to ensure critical messages don't get lost.


### **Why It Matters**


This agent transforms how you manage your inbox by ensuring you never miss truly important emails while reducing the anxiety of constant checking. The customizable scoring criteria mean you define what "urgent" means based on your specific role and responsibilities.


In practice, this means you'll receive immediate notifications about deadline-sensitive projects, messages from key stakeholders, or issues that could block team progress—while everything else waits for your regular email processing time.
