# Janitor AI: The Ultimate Platform for Creating and Interacting with AI Characters

Janitor AI is a platform where you can chat with thousands of AI characters or build your own. It blends entertainment, storytelling, and light automation into one interface. Some people use it for fun. Others use it for writing help, roleplay, or even basic customer support bots.


You don’t need to know how to code. Just pick a character or define a new one with personality traits, a backstory, and chat behavior. Then start talking.


## What You Can Do on Janitor AI


This platform is popular for one reason: flexibility. Here’s what users actually use it for:


-


Chatting with pre-made AI characters across genres, fandoms, and styles


-


Building fully custom bots with unique voices, memories, and logic


-


Toggling between safe-for-work and NSFW modes (depending on your goals)


-


Joining the community to share creations, trade feedback, or just vibe


Whether you're experimenting with character development or just want a chatbot with personality, Janitor AI gives you the tools to build your own world.

## **Pricing Structure**


Janitor AI operates on a freemium model:


-


**Free Tier** : Access basic features including chatting with select characters and limited customization options


-


**Paid Plans** : Unlock premium features such as advanced character creation tools, NSFW content access, priority support, and higher message limits


For specific pricing details, it's best to check the official Janitor AI website as offerings may change over time.


## **Safety Considerations**


While Janitor AI provides an engaging platform, users should be aware of certain safety aspects:


-


**Content Filters** : Toggle between SFW and NSFW content, though effectiveness varies based on user settings


-


**Privacy Protection** : Conversations can be set to private, but as with any online service, sharing sensitive personal information is discouraged


-


**Moderation** : The platform includes active moderation, but users should remain cautious, especially with unfiltered content


-


**Potential Risks** : Like similar platforms, users may encounter inappropriate content, inconsistent moderation, or privacy concerns
