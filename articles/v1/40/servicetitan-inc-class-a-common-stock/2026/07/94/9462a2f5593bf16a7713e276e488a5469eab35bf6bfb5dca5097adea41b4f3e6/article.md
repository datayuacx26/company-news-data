### **What is Titan Score?**


TitanAdvisor is a personalized digital coach, baked right into ServiceTitan, that provides insight into tips and features that will help you boost business and realize goals. This powerful tool then quantifies how effectively you’re utilizing ServiceTitan and provides you with a Titan Score. A higher score means better utilization, which translates into greater value for your business. In fact, contractors who maximize their use of ServiceTitan have been shown to grow more than twice as fast.
