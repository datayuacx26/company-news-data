## **Why is There No Mention of Masturbation?**


The Bible was written in a different historical and cultural setting. Concepts such as “masturbation” as we know them today may not have been a major issue for the authors. The emphasis was on reproduction, social order, and determining God’s plan for human relationships.


## **Focus on Marriage and Children**


The Bible paints a clear picture of marriage as the optimum setting for sexual intimacy. Passages like Genesis 1:28, with its famous passage, “Be fruitful and multiply,” emphasize reproduction as a primary goal of sex within marriage. 1 Corinthians 7:2-5 goes on to cover fulfilling marital responsibilities and avoiding sexual immorality. These verses highlight God’s intention for sex as a powerful expression of love and commitment between partners.


## **Purity and Self Control: Avoiding Immorality**


The Bible focuses on purity and self-control. According to 1 Thessalonians 4:3, “It is God’s will that you should be holy: you must avoid sexual impurity.” While masturbation is not expressly mentioned, this verse encourages people to avoid behaviors that are contrary to God’s purpose for sexuality.


Some take this as a call to channel sexual energy within a marriage, while others see it as a universal lesson about avoiding lustful thoughts and acts.


## **Love and Respect: A Thread of Marital Intimacy**


The Bible instills love and respect in the foundation of marital connection. In Ephesians 5:25-28, men are instructed to love their wives “just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her.” This notion of selflessness and love continues into the bedroom. Sexual intimacy becomes a means of expressing love and devotion within a holy relationship.


## **Interpreting Silence: Different Points of View**


Despite the Bible’s silence on masturbation, numerous interpretations have emerged:


**Sinful Outlet:** Some claim that masturbation is a sort of self-gratification that is contrary to God’s intention for marital relationship. They regard it as a waste of sexual energy that can lead to lusty thoughts and possibly infidelity.


**Focus on Intent:** Others say the intent behind masturbating is more important. If it causes excessive lust, replaces intimacy with a spouse, or is accompanied by pornography, it may be troublesome. However, if used in moderation and without negative repercussions, it may not be fundamentally immoral.


**No Explicit Judgement:** Others interpret the lack of Judgement as evidence that masturbation is not fundamentally evil. They think the emphasis should be on the ideals of purity, self-control, and according to one’s marriage vows.
