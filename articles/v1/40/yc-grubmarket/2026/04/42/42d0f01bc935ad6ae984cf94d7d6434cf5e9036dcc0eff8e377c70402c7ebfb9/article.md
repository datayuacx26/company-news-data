# GrubMarket Named to TIME100 Companies Industry Leaders List


We are proud to announce that GrubMarket has been named to the inaugural TIME100 Companies Industry Leaders list, recognizing companies making an extraordinary impact across their respective industries.


This recognition reflects our continued progress in transforming the food supply chain through AI, eCommerce, and vertical SaaS solutions, as well as the strong momentum we’ve built over the past year.


---


**Recognition by TIME**


According to TIME’s 2026 Most Influential Companies coverage, GrubMarket has established itself as both a leading technology provider and a major operator within the food supply chain.


The company has scaled rapidly in recent years by combining strategic acquisitions with its AI-powered WholesaleWare platform, enabling food wholesalers and distributors to modernize their operations through automation, data-driven decision making, and digital infrastructure.


TIME evaluated companies based on impact, innovation, ambition, and success, highlighting GrubMarket’s unique position at the intersection of technology and the global food ecosystem.


---


**A Year of Growth and Innovation**


This recognition follows a year of significant expansion and technological advancement for GrubMarket.


Key milestones include:


Accelerated development of agentic AI solutions, designed to automate complex workflows, improve operational efficiency, and help businesses across the food supply chain enhance their margins and performance:


- Inventory Management AI Agent
- Reporting AI Agent
- Monitoring AI Agent


GrubMarket also continued its strategic growth through acquisitions:


- Delta Fresh Produce (April 2025), expanding our presence in Arizona
- Coast Citrus (June 2025), our largest acquisition to date and a major U.S. supplier of tropical produce
- Procurant (November 2025), adding over 850 software customers with a leading SaaS trading platform


---


**Commitment to Sustainability**


Sustainability remains a core pillar of GrubMarket’s long-term strategy.


Through our *Sustainable California* initiative, we have sponsored the planting of over 230,000 trees in at-risk regions across the state. In parallel, we continue to support underserved farmers in achieving organic certification through grants, mentorship, and technical assistance.


---


**Leadership Perspective**


“We are honored to be recognized by TIME as one of the TIME100 Companies Industry Leaders. This recognition reflects our team’s relentless pursuit of operational excellence and sustainable growth, as well as our progress against our mission to transform the food supply chain through AI, eCommerce, vertical Software-as-a-Service, and other technological innovations.”


— Mike Xu, Founder & CEO, GrubMarket


---


**Looking Ahead**


GrubMarket will continue to expand its presence across the United States and internationally, while advancing the AI-driven transformation of the food supply chain.


We remain focused on building and deploying innovative solutions that automate manual processes, improve operational performance, and drive profitability for businesses across the industry—while continuing to invest in sustainability initiatives that support a healthier, more resilient food system.
