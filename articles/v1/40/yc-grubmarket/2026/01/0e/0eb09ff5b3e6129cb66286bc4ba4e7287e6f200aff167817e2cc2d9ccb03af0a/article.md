# GrubMarket Announces Monitoring AI Agent that Detects and Alerts of Critical Business Issues

# GrubMarket Announces Monitoring AI Agent that Detects and Alerts of Critical Business Issues


Food supply chain operators are expected to move fast, stay lean, and manage constant change, all while dealing with perishability, complex logistics, and rapidly shifting customer demand. Today, GrubMarket is excited to announce the release of the newest specialized AI agent within GrubAssist AI: the **Monitoring AI Agent** .


This new capability introduces always-on, passive monitoring across key business and operational data, automatically detecting anomalies, identifying changes to market conditions, and surfacing shifts in critical performance metrics. It also sends proactive alerts based on custom trigger conditions, so teams can catch issues earlier and respond faster.


## From reactive firefighting to proactive control


In many food businesses, the day-to-day reality is reactive: problems are discovered after the fact, reporting is manual, and time is limited. Even when data exists in ERP, eCommerce, payments, and finance systems, most teams do not have the bandwidth to continuously analyze it and monitor for early warning signs.


The Monitoring AI Agent changes that dynamic by helping businesses shift from reactive workflows to proactive operations. For example, teams can configure alerts for scenarios like:


- A key customer has gone more than 7 days without placing an order
- A vendor shortage is projected to impact outgoing sales orders
- A new customer has exceeded its credit limit


## Five core capabilities that make monitoring simple


The Monitoring AI Agent is built around five high-impact capabilities that help teams stay ahead of issues:


- **Proactive Monitoring:** Passively monitors critical variables in the background, identifying shifting conditions, assessing impact, and detecting anomalies before they affect the bottom line.
- **Custom Monitoring Rules:** Configure the metrics and conditions that matter most across inventory, margins, customer activity, vendor performance, accounts receivable aging, and more.
- **Always-On Alerts:** Automatically surfaces exceptions and noteworthy changes with proactive email notifications sent to designated team members.
- **Faster Decision-Making:** Alerts include supporting context and relevant information to help teams take corrective action quickly.
- **Seamless Integration:** Works across ERP and accounting systems, with native integrations including WholesaleWare ERP, Famous Software, Produce Pro, Thyme Software, QuickBooks, and more.


## Common monitoring workflows teams can automate


Because Monitoring AI Agent workflows run continuously in the background, teams can create monitoring prompts that cover critical operational areas across purchasing, sales, warehouse management, and accounting.


Common workflows include:


- Low stock threshold alerts
- Expiring lot and aging inventory warnings
- Margin erosion alerts
- Customer order monitoring
- Accounts receivable tracking
- Supply shortage notifications


## Getting started in GrubAssist AI


Getting started is designed to be simple. Within GrubAssist AI, users create a monitoring prompt in natural language, define trigger conditions, add supplemental instructions, and set alerting preferences. From there, the agent monitors connected systems and automatically sends professionally formatted email alerts to the right recipients when important exceptions, thresholds, or anomalies are detected.
