# GrubMarket Announces the Spring 2026 Release of its AI-Powered Software Ecosystem


GrubMarket has announced the Spring 2026 product release for its software suite, introducing significant enhancements across WholesaleWare, Orders IO, and AI Orders.


As the industry’s only fully integrated technology platform spanning ERP, AI, and eCommerce, the Spring 2026 Release delivers new capabilities designed to help restaurant and foodservice distributors and produce wholesalers improve efficiency, elevate customer service, and drive profitability.


---


**Key Highlights of the Spring 2026 Release**


**WholesaleWare Enhancements**


The Spring 2026 Release introduces several new automation and operational capabilities within WholesaleWare.


- Automated Work Orders: Streamlines repacking and processing workflows through automated creation and management of work orders, including real time inventory and cost updates.
- New Price Groups: Enables flexible, rule-based pricing segmentation with support for item and category-level overrides, while expanding existing pricing capabilities such as customer-specific pricing and premium pricing for smaller units of measure.
- Automated FIFO Lot Allocation: Gives users the option to automatically allocate lots at shipment in addition to pre-allocating lots and scanning lots to pick.
- Expanded EDI integrations: New integrations with Produce Alliance and Restaurant365 provide seamless, automated order processing.
- New Reports and Platform Enhancements: Includes new operational and accounting reports such as purchasing guides, route summaries, and charge insights, along with enhancements across sales orders, drop ship orders, inventory management, lot tracking, cost management, loads, and accounting.


---


**AI Orders Enhancements**


AI Orders received major upgrades as part of the Spring 2026 Release.


- Multi-Language Order Processing: AI Orders now supports multi-language inbound orders to better support the diverse customer bases that distributors serve.
- Match Confidence Indicators: Improves order review efficiency through visual cues representing the AI’s confidence in product and unit-of-measure matches.
- Customer Product Code Recognition: Enables AI Orders to understand customer-specific product codes in addition to product names and descriptions, streamlining workflows for large retailers and foodservice contracts while enabling EDI workflows without requiring integration.
- Customer-Specific AI Prompts: Allows tailored AI training for individual customers and their unique order formats.
- Enterprise Dictionary: Introduces configuration feature to help AI Orders interpret specialized terminology used by companies and their customer bases.
- Additional Enhancements: Includes configurable product matching logic, enhanced UI displays, and improvements to historical order analysis.


---


**Orders IO Enhancements**


The Spring 2026 Release also introduces new functionality across Orders IO, GrubMarket’s eCommerce platform.


- Automatic Product Suggestions: Delivers dynamic, customer-specific upsell recommendations designed to increase order value.
- Advanced Delivery Management: Provides granular control over delivery scheduling with product and customer-specific rules, along with built-in management of holidays and non-delivery days.
- Order Guide Tagging: Simplifies order guide management through customizable tags, including tags for seasonal menus.
- Streamlined Customer Onboarding: Introduces an improved workflow with automated welcome communications that provide customers access to the online ordering platform and mobile ordering applications.
- User Experience Improvements: Includes enhancements across customer chat, product search, supplier portal, pricing management, and catalog browsing.
