# GrubMarket Expands into the UK with the Acquisition of JR Holland


We are thrilled to announce that GrubMarket has completed the acquisition of JR Holland, one of the leading fresh produce and foodservice distributors serving North East England and Scotland.


This strategic acquisition marks GrubMarket’s first entry into the UK and represents an important milestone as we continue expanding our global market presence and driving technology adoption across the food supply chain industry.


## **JR Holland: More Than Four Decades of Fresh Food Distribution**


Founded in 1983 by John Holland, JR Holland has built a strong reputation serving customers across the hospitality, education, healthcare, and public sectors for more than four decades.


Based in Gateshead, the company operates two locations in the Newcastle area totaling 65,000 square feet and employs more than 120 people. Today, JR Holland completes more than 3,000 deliveries each week with a fleet of 32 modern, temperature controlled vehicles, serving customers six days a week.


Its capabilities include:


- More than 3,000 fresh products, including more than 1,000 varieties of fruits and vegetables
- Two locations in the Newcastle area totaling 65,000 square feet
- A team of more than 120 employees
- More than 3,000 deliveries completed each week
- A fleet of 32 modern, temperature controlled vehicles
- Service across North East England and Scotland
- Customers across the hospitality, education, healthcare, and public sectors
- Complete product traceability supported by a dedicated quality control team
- Returnable crate programs, backhaul practices, packaging-recycling initiatives, and technology-enabled route planning that support efficient and environmentally responsible operations


JR Holland has built its reputation through its wide product breadth, dependable high-volume delivery, responsive customer service, and enduring customer and supplier relationships.

## **Future Growth Powered by Innovation**


As part of GrubMarket, JR Holland will have access to our suite of proprietary AI-powered technology solutions:


- WholesaleWare – SaaS ERP platform providing food industry wholesalers, distributors, and shippers with seamless financial management, powerful sales support, precise inventory management, lot traceability, grower accounting, and automated routing and logistics
- GrubAssist AI – Suite of agentic enterprise AI assistants that provide deep insights, intelligent analyses, and workflow automation
- Orders IO – Custom-branded mobile and online ordering eCommerce solution
- GrubPay – Digital payments platform specifically designed for the needs of the food supply chain industry


JR Holland will retain its brand and continue to be managed by its experienced leadership team, ensuring continuity for customers, suppliers, and employees while benefiting from GrubMarket’s technology, resources, and extensive industry network.


This exciting acquisition marks an important step in GrubMarket’s global expansion and reinforces our commitment to modernizing the food supply chain through technology, innovation, and strategic growth. Please join us in welcoming the JR Holland team to the GrubMarket family!
