# Legacy Housing Workforce Housing: Affordable, Durable “Hotels on Wheels” for Businesses

In today’s competitive economy, providing safe, comfortable, and **affordable workforce housing** is a major challenge for businesses. Whether supporting oilfield crews, H-2A agricultural workers, data center construction teams, or disaster relief efforts, companies need housing solutions that are fast to deploy, cost-effective, and built to perform in demanding conditions.


Legacy Housing Corporation’s dedicated **workforce housing division** delivers exactly that. Marketed as **“hotels on wheels,”** these rugged, factory-built units combine the comfort of modern living with the durability required for remote and high-intensity industries. Built to strict HUD-Code standards, Legacy’s workforce housing helps businesses dramatically reduce housing costs while improving worker satisfaction, safety, and productivity.

### What Is Legacy Workforce Housing?


Legacy’s workforce housing units are specialized **manufactured workforce housing** solutions designed for temporary or semi-permanent use. Often referred to as **oilfield homes** , these units feature private bedrooms with individual bathrooms, modern amenities, and reinforced construction that stands up to harsh environments.


Key advantages include:


- **Rapid deployment** — Units are built indoors in a controlled factory setting and delivered ready for setup.
- **Built tougher** — Taller, wider, and more robust than standard housing, with 40% more standard features than many competitors.
- **Cost efficiency** — Dramatic savings compared to daily hotel rates or building permanent structures.
- **Improved worker experience** — Private rooms reduce fatigue, boost morale, and enhance safety by keeping crews closer to job sites.


Legacy has supplied thousands of these units to clients across major energy regions, including the Permian Basin, Bakken, Barnett Shale, and more.
