###


### What Are Manufactured, Modular, and Site-Built Homes?


Let’s start with clear definitions — because the terms are often confused.


- **Manufactured Homes** (also known as **mobile homes** or **affordable trailer homes** in everyday searches): Built entirely in a factory to federal HUD Code standards. They arrive on a permanent steel chassis, are transported to your site, and can be set on a variety of foundations. Legacy’s homes are high-quality, energy-efficient, and fully customizable.
- **Modular Homes** : Also factory-built in sections (modules), but constructed to local and state building codes (usually the International Residential Code). Modules are delivered and assembled on a permanent foundation. They look and feel very similar to site-built homes.
- **Site-Built Homes** (traditional or “stick-built”): Constructed entirely on your land, piece by piece, by local contractors. They must meet local building codes and typically take the longest to complete.


All three can be beautiful, durable homes — but they differ dramatically in **cost** , **speed** , **financing** , and **flexibility** .

### In-Depth Breakdown: Pros & Cons


#### Manufactured Homes (Legacy’s Specialty)


**Pros:**


- Most affordable option — often 40-50% less than a comparable site-built home.
- Lightning-fast delivery: Order today, move in within weeks.
- Built in a controlled factory environment = fewer weather delays and better quality control.
- Modern designs, energy-efficient features, and open floor plans that rival much more expensive homes.
- Perfect for first-time buyers, growing families, or anyone seeking **affordable trailer homes** without sacrificing quality.


**Cons:**


- Some older financing stigma (though 2026 programs have improved this dramatically).
- May have slightly lower resale value if placed in a land-lease community (private land ownership fixes this).


Legacy eliminates the “cons” by focusing on premium HUD-code construction, strong dealer networks, and financing partnerships that make **manufactured home vs traditional home** comparisons easy to win.


####


#### Modular Homes


**Pros:**


- Faster and often 10-20% cheaper than site-built while meeting the same local codes.
- Highly customizable and permanent once installed.
- Great curb appeal — looks identical to traditional homes.


**Cons:**


- Still significantly more expensive than manufactured homes.
- Longer total timeline than manufactured (modules + on-site assembly).
- Higher transportation and crane-setup costs.


Great middle option if you want a “site-built look” but need to save time and money.


#### Site-Built Homes


**Pros:**


- Maximum customization and prestige.
- Strongest resale appreciation in most markets.
- Fully tailored to your exact lot and preferences.


**Cons:**


- Highest cost and longest wait.
- Exposed to weather delays, labor shortages, and supply-chain issues.
- In today’s 2026 market, many buyers simply can’t afford the $424,000+ price tag.
