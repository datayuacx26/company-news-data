# How Restaurants Are Embracing AI to Drive Efficiency in 2026

#### Automation


AI automation can help take day-to-day tasks off operators’ plates (figuratively, not literally… yet). Here are four areas where restaurants see opportunities for automation:


-


**Inventory management** - AI can track and project inventory levels and send alerts when it’s time to restock essentials.


-


**Employee scheduling** - AI can pick up on scheduling patterns and request offs to automate your weekly schedule.


-


**Guest/customer management** - AI can improve how restaurants interact with and understand their customers. For example, improving waiting lists so that they accurately predict wait times.


-


**Upselling** - AI can suggest common upsells on takeout/delivery apps to make larger order sizes. For example, “add on bacon to burger for an extra $2”.


These tasks add up quickly, and automation can save operators valuable time. This way, they can focus on running smooth, successful services.


#### Benchmarking


Having a firm grip on what is going on in the industry is essential to restaurant success. It’s vital to stay up to date with the current state of restaurant sales.[Benchmarking](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/7-critical-restaurant-benchmarks) is the process of comparing your business to industry averages. **With 38% of restaurants indicating they are "extremely likely" to implement AI benchmarking tools in the future,** this technology is becoming a crucial component of strategic planning.


Common goals for benchmarking include:


-


**Improving costs** - Compare food and labor costs to similar establishments.


-


**Service quality** - Analyze customer satisfaction and table turnover rates.


-


**Operational efficiency** - Assess kitchen workflows and peak performance times.


*Data is only powerful if it's personal.*[Toast Benchmarking](https://pos.toasttab.com/products/benchmarking) *shows your performance compared to similar concepts in your area- so you can spot trends, keep an eye on the local competition, and get ahead.*


By leveraging AI for both automation and benchmarking, restaurants can make more informed decisions and optimize their operations.


### Areas small FSRs are looking to use AI in the future


Smaller full-service restaurants (FSRs) often face resource constraints that make operational efficiency critical. Here’s where small FSRs are focusing their AI efforts:


-


**Shift optimization** - AI-powered scheduling tools can analyze historical data, such as sales patterns and staffing needs, to create optimized schedules that reduce labor costs while preventing employee burnout.


-


**Voice AI ordering** - When staff can’t answer the phone, voice AI steps in. This technology can handle calls, take orders, book reservations, and answer FAQs seamlessly.


-


**Marketing assistance** - Limited budgets don’t have to mean limited impact. AI can analyze customer data to create targeted promotions, recommend personalized menu items, and craft engaging email campaigns.


-


**Quoting wait times** -AI takes the guesswork out of quoting wait times, ensuring customers aren’t left frustrated and hangry.


### AI: Because running a restaurant is hard enough already


The future of restaurants lies in embracing tools that make operations smarter. AI empowers restaurant operators to focus on what they do best: providing exceptional dining experiences. Automating routine tasks and delivering actionable insights, it allows restaurateurs to reclaim their time, make informed decisions, and adapt quickly in a fast-paced industry.
