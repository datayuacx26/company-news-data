### What retail businesses need from a POS system


For brick-and-mortar retail shops, your POS needs to work as hard as your floor staff. Here are the features to look for throughout your search:


**1. Inventory management** - Retail lives and dies by stock accuracy. Look for barcode scanning, automated reorder alerts, inventory tracking, and support for large item libraries. If you sell online *and* in-store, real-time inventory sync across channels is essential to prevent overselling.


**2. Omnichannel selling** - Your customers are likely shopping both online and in stores. A strong retail POS connects your physical and digital storefronts, syncing inventory, orders, and customer data across all channels. Extra points to POS providers like Toast, which enable you to view this data anywhere from your cell phone through[Toast Now](https://pos.toasttab.com/products/toast-now) .


**3. Customer loyalty & CRM** - Look for a POS provider that can help you drive foot traffic without the use of third-party apps. With Toast, you can connect your POS with our email marketing and loyalty products to create tailored offers that drive repeat business.


**4. Fast, flexible checkout** - Whether you're using a countertop terminal, a handheld device, or a self-checkout kiosk, your POS hardware should support the way you sell and accept every payment type your customers expect.


**5. Reporting that drives decisions** - Know your top-selling SKUs, peak hours, and margin performance at a glance. Customizable dashboards make it easy to spot trends and act on them.

### What restaurants need from a POS system


For restaurants, cafés, bars, and food service operations, a[POS system](https://pos.toasttab.com/restaurant-pos) has to handle the unique pressure of a live service environment. Speed, accuracy, and kitchen communication are everything.


**1. Order & table management** - Your POS should map your floor, track table status, and route orders to the right stations instantly — whether you're running a quick-service counter or a full-service dining room.


**2. Kitchen Display System (KDS)-** Eliminate paper tickets. A KDS integration sends orders directly to the kitchen in real time, reducing errors and improving ticket times.


**3. Online Ordering & Delivery** - Guests expect to order from their phone. Look for a POS that has native online ordering built in, not just a third-party bolt-on, so orders flow directly into your kitchen workflow.


**4. Menu Management -** Update items, prices, modifiers, and 86'd items from anywhere in seconds. During a busy service, that flexibility is critical.


**5. Tip Management & Payroll** - Restaurant payroll is complex. A POS that handles tip pooling, tip outs, and integrates with payroll software saves your managers hours every week.


**6. Reporting** - Know your labor cost percentage, sales by daypart, and top menu performers all from your phone, in real time.
