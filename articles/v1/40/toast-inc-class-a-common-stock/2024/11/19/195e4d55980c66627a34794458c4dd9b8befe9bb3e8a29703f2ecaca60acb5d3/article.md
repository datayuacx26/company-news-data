When deciding where to dine, guests consider many factors —ambiance, price, and cuisine, to name a few. Across the country, countless women-led restaurants deliver unforgettable dining experiences, from innovative dishes crafted by talented female chefs to thriving eateries managed by inspiring women entrepreneurs.


If you're looking to celebrate and support women in the restaurant industry, these 37 incredible women-led restaurants should be at the top of your list.


## 37 women-led restaurants


### Northeast


### 1. ****[Blake Orchard](https://www.blakeorchardjuicery.com/about) - Portland, ME


Founded by Alexandra Blake Basinet in 2014, Blake Orchard is a juice and smoothie bar with three locations across Maine. What sets it apart is the commitment to keeping things fresh and local. Organic and locally sourced ingredients are used whenever possible, and juices, plant mylks, nut butters, and sauces are made in-house, most of them daily.


[The menu](https://www.blakeorchardjuicery.com/menu) is built around nutrient-dense, feel-good food and drinks, with standout items like the toasted coconut smoothie, lemon bar smoothie, and superfood coffee — the kind of lineup that keeps regulars coming back.


### 2.[Fox & the Knife](https://www.foxandtheknife.com/) - Boston, MA


South Boston is filled with many restaurants, but Fox & the Knife continues to stand out. Fox & the Knife is a vibrant Enoteca & Restaurant by Chef Karen Akunowicz. Here, they offer traditional Italian food and drink with a modern sensibility, focusing on handmade pastas, bright antipasti dishes, and their Apertivi and Amaro programs.


Chef Karen Akunowicz won the 2018 James Beard Foundation Award for Best Chef: Northeast and is the 2020 James Beard Award finalist for Best New Restaurant in America. Akunowicz was also named one of the “21 Badass Women Changing the Food World” by Marie Claire Magazine and is the author of “Crave: Bold Recipes That Make You Want Seconds,” which was published in September of 2023 by W.W. Norton.


Fox & the Knife’s menu highlights Akunowicz’s flair for soul-satisfying and graceful Italian cuisine, perfected during her time in Italy as a pasta maker and Chef at L’Avian Blu Enoteca. Today, Chef Akunowicz also owns Bar Volpe Ristorante, Fox Pasta Company, and Fox & Flight at Logan Airport.


### 3.[Petula’s](https://www.petulasboston.com/) - South Boston, MA


Petula's, another South Boston establishment, is an elegant spot ideal for enjoying brunch, lunch, or dinner while catching up with your best friends. The restaurant's name has a charming backstory—in an interview with[South Boston Online](https://southbostononline.com/petulas/) , owner Rachel Clark explained, "My mom really loved Petula Clark. She would blast her music every morning to wake my siblings and me up. It put us in such a good mood." This childhood memory inspired the name of her restaurant at 81 L Street.


For visitors to Petula's, we highly recommend trying the whipped ricotta, vodka tomato pasta, and seasonal veggie pizza.


### 4.[Sarm](https://www.sarmarestaurant.com/)[a](https://www.sarmarestaurant.com/) - Somerville, MA


Sarma, a vibrant restaurant and bar in Somerville, is the creative vision of Chef Cassie Piuma, who has crafted a unique dining experience centered around shared small plates. Drawing on Turkish meyhane traditions while infusing her own culinary perspective, Chef Cassie balances Mediterranean flavors with inspiration from her travels and encounters. Her distinctive approach creates dishes that feel both innovative and authentic—familiar yet exciting.


The warm atmosphere invites guests to gather around meze complemented by thoughtfully selected cocktails, craft beers, and wines. Under Chef Cassie's leadership, Sarma has become a celebration of communal dining where food, drink, and music harmoniously come together.


### 5.[Celeste](https://celesteunionsquare.com/) - Somerville, MA


Architect and general director Maria Rondeau co-founded[Celeste](https://celesteunionsquare.com/) in Union Square in Somerville. This restaurant features Peruvian staples, including ceviche and slow-cooked stews, causas, salads, and other daily specials that complement the path of this culinary route to the root of Andean food.


Check out Celeste in Union Square today!


### 6.[Casa Verde](https://www.casaverdema.com/) - Scituate, MA


Just 26 miles south of Boston, Casa Verde is a Latin American restaurant bringing together the bold, distinct flavors of Peru, Argentina, and Mexico under one roof. Restaurant entrepreneur Kara Tondorf opened the doors in 2025 and has already built a destination worth the drive with a menu that spans the full breadth of the Latin American pantry.


Save room for dessert! Pastry Chef Tiara Willis crafts composed Latin-style sweets that are very much worth lingering over.


### 7.[The Laurel](https://www.thelaurelrestaurantandbar.com/) - West Hartford, CT


Co-owned by Chef Ashley Flagg and General Manager Rebekah Flagg, The Laurel is a globally inspired restaurant built on a deeply local foundation. Every dish draws from New England's rich agricultural landscape, with seasonal ingredients shaping a food and beverage menu that is always evolving, always shareable, and never quite the same twice.


The two owners bring complementary energy to every corner of the restaurant. Chef Ashley channels her passion for the region's farmers and producers into a menu that travels the world without losing its New England roots. Rebekah is hands-on in every sense. She even designed the interior herself and is particularly excited about the future private event space at the Laurel.


### 8.[Reyna](https://www.reynanewyork.com/) - New York, NY


Reyna is a stunning New York City-based Mediterranean restaurant owned by Nicki Laborie. Nicki first opened two Reyna locations in Toronto, followed by Reyna Union Square in November 2022. This location is run by an all-female management team, from the creative director to the front-of-house manager.


The name “Reyna” honors women, reflecting Nicki’s vision to create a restaurant with a feminine ambiance where women can relax and dine comfortably. Her concepts are feminine simply because the owner, Nicki, is feminine. This tapas-style establishment is known for its standout dishes, particularly the manchego cauliflower croquettes, which come highly recommended.

### 13.[Opto](https://optonyc.com/) — New York, NY


Located in Flatiron, Opto is a Mediterranean restaurant co-owned by Nicola Kotsoni, the restaurateur behind Il Cantinori, Amali, Bar Room, Bar Six, and Periyali. For Kotsoni, Mediterranean food isn't a concept; it's an upbringing. As she told[FSR Magazine](https://www.fsrmagazine.com/feature/nicola-kotsoni-reflects-on-building-a-hospitality-legacy-rooted-in-greek-tradition-in-nyc/) , "Mediterranean food has immensely influenced my restaurants in many ways. It is what I grew up on, and the fact is that we grew our own produce between uncles and grandparents — fresh fish, vegetables, olive oil, fruits..."


That emphasis on freshness carries through to every plate at Opto, where the menu spans fresh bread, pasta, seafood, and meats. Highlights include whipped ricotta, grilled half chicken, and Rocky Mountain lamb chops.

### Challenges women face in the restaurant industry


Women aren't new to the restaurant industry, yet leadership roles tell a different story. According to[Chef's Pencil's analysis](https://www.chefspencil.com/female-chefs-6-percent-reach-the-top/) of more than 2,000 Michelin-starred restaurants across 16 countries, only 6% are led by women. The barriers can also commonly go beyond recognition. Access to capital, limited mentorship, outsized caregiving responsibilities, and workplace cultures that have historically favored men all play a role.


In fact, women-led businesses receive less than 3% of total venture capital funding in the US, per the[National Women's Business Council's 2024 Annual Report](https://www.nwbc.gov/annual-reports/2024/) , and while women-owned businesses represent 39.2% of all US businesses, they account for only 6.2% of total revenue, according to a[2025 Wells Fargo Businesses Report](https://www.wippeducationinstitute.org/2025-wells-fargo-impact-of-women-owned-businesses) . The 37 restaurants listed above are doing something about that, one incredible meal at a time.


### Eat with purpose, support women in food


By dining at one of these establishments run by women restaurateurs, you’re contributing to the growth and recognition of women in the culinary industry. And you get to enjoy a good meal while doing so!


From local bistros to nationally recognized businesses, these restaurants represent the diversity and strength of women who lead with passion. Next time you're planning a meal, take the opportunity to support these incredible women and help amplify their voices in the culinary world.


### FAQs


**What is a women-led restaurant?**


A woman-led restaurant is one that is owned, operated, or headed by a woman. This could mean the sole owner, co-owner, executive chef, or general manager is a woman.


**Are there women-led Michelin star restaurants?**


Yes, and some of the most celebrated restaurants in the world are led by women. The most notable example in the US is[Atelier Crenn in San Francisco](https://www.ateliercrenn.com/) , where[Chef Dominique Crenn](https://www.dominiquecrenn.com/chefcrenn) became the only female chef in the country to earn three Michelin stars. Women lead approximately[6%](https://www.chefspencil.com/female-chefs-6-percent-reach-the-top/) of Michelin-starred restaurants.


**How can I find women-led restaurants near me?**


You can start with this article, made up of 37 women-led restaurants. You can also search Google Maps using terms like "women-owned restaurant near me.” Many businesses now carry a verified women-owned attribute on their profile.
