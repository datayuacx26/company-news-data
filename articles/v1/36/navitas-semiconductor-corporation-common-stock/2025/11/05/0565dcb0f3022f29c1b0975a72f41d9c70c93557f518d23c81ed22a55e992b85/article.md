# Navitas Semiconductor to Participate in Upcoming Investor Event

## Navitas Semiconductor to Participate in Upcoming Investor Event


November 17, 2025


[PDF Version](https://ir.navitassemi.com/node/11196/pdf)


TORRANCE, Calif. , Nov. 17, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Navitas Semiconductor](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=iUn_FntIr_u9Oyc-qjkVSEa0OasJ6t6qyo8tBFZhZDuvGfDCFs4Axn_xKMdWpOplWJBwdfQysEYqxma1i1sNOQMdPy16Qlrqe7vTbkdG1ik=) , an industry leader in next-generation GaNFast™ gallium nitride (GaN) and GeneSiC™ silicon carbide (SiC) power semiconductors, has announced participation in the following upcoming investor event:


**UBS Global Technology and AI Conference**


December 3, 2025, 1-on-1 meetings with Chris Allexandre, President and CEO, and Todd Glickman, CFO


Location: The Phoenician, Scottsdale, AZ


**About Navitas**


[Navitas Semiconductor](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=iUn_FntIr_u9Oyc-qjkVSEa0OasJ6t6qyo8tBFZhZDtoBM9UXy0rDtwP_UsjrdwvCtbnOyCjKpppewnVu-mrB7c-suxYwapSWV2ke6FEjWU=) (Nasdaq: NVTS) is a next-generation power semiconductor leader in gallium nitride (GaN) and IC integrated devices, and high-voltage silicon carbide (SiC) technology, driving innovation across AI data centers, performance computing, energy and grid infrastructure, and industrial electrification.
