# July 2026 Expensify product update: Granular admin roles, new HR integrations, and an AI copilot that takes action

This month, we shipped one of your most-requested features: more granular admin roles. Plus, a wave of new HR and accounting integrations, and some serious AI muscle. Here's what's new.


### TL;DR – What’s new in Expensify (July 2026)


- Give more granular permissions to admins with new people, payments, and card role types
- Let auditors view workspace settings with expanded read-only access
- Connect to Workday, HiBob, BambooHR, and Certinia
- Add AI agents with custom rules to take actions in Expensify on your behalf
- Ask our virtual CFO questions and get on-the-fly spend analysis
- Set mileage rates that apply to specific date ranges
- Download expense report PDFs in bulk


## Tighter controls, smarter AI


### Scoped admin roles & read-only auditor access


Give admins exactly the access they need. New scoped roles include people admin, payments admin, and card admin. We've also expanded the read-only auditor role to view workspace settings for a better picture of company setup.[Learn more about role updates](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Managing-Workspace-Members) .


### New HR & accounting integrations


Connect your people data in a couple clicks.[Workday](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/connections/Connect-Workday-to-Expensify) ,[HiBob](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/connections/Connect-HiBob-to-Expensify) ,[BambooHR](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/connections/Connect-BambooHR-to-Expensify) , and[Certinia](https://help.expensify.com/new-expensify/hubs/connections/certinia) integrations are now live. Employees, groups, and org data now flow straight into Expensify. More connections are rolling out through the rest of the year, so stay tuned.[Learn more about our new integrations](https://help.expensify.com/new-expensify/hubs/connections/) .


### Now in beta: Upgrade your workflow with agent rules


No more rigid rule-builders. Tell our AI agents what you want in plain English (edit, code, route, hold, comment, reject, or approve expenses), and they'll figure out how to do it.


## More updates this month


Here’s a handful of additional upgrades to round out July:


-


**Consolidated travel billing for Expensify Travel:**[Centralize company travel spend](https://use.expensify.com/blog/business-travel-expense-reconciliation) without corporate cards or out-of-pocket reimbursements. Your team books through[Expensify Travel](https://use.expensify.com/travel) as usual, and Expensify bills your business bank account once a month with every trip, receipt, and statement in one place. Available now for US businesses with a verified bank account.[Learn more about consolidated travel billing](https://help.expensify.com/travel/hubs/consolidated-travel-billing/) .


-


**Date-bound mileage rates:** Set[mileage rates](https://use.expensify.com/mileage-tracking-app) that apply to specific date ranges, so[reimbursements](https://use.expensify.com/travel-expense-reimbursement) always use the proper rate based on when the trip took place.[Learn more about date-bound mileage rates](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Set-distance-rates) .


-


**Export your filtered views:** Filter your list of members down to exactly who you want, then export that view directly.[Learn more about filtered views](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/How-to-Export-Reports#how-to-export-the-columns-currently-displayed-using-export-current-view) .


-


**Bulk download expense reports as PDFs:** Select all of the[expense reports](https://use.expensify.com/expense-reports) you want to download as PDFs in one go.[Learn more about bulk downloads.](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/Search-and-Download-Expenses#download-multiple-reports)


-


**Virtual CFO:** Ask, and Expensify analyzes. Our virtual CFO functionality now reacts to your questions with on-the-fly spend analysis in workspace chats, reports, and expense threads with trends, outliers, and answers about where your money is going, all in plain language. Learn more about[virtual CFO](https://use.expensify.com/virtual-cfo) .


That's it for this month. And as always, keep the feature requests coming!
