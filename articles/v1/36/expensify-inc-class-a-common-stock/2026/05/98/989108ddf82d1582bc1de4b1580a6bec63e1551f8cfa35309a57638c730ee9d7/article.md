## What types of expenses can I track with a 1099 expense tracker?


Here's where things get interesting. The IRS allows independent contractors to[deduct "ordinary and necessary" business expenses](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/guide-to-business-expense-resources#en_US_2020_publink100078332) , but what does that actually mean for your bottom line? Think of your expense tracker as a financial detective, automatically categorizing purchases and flagging potential deductions you might otherwise miss. Here are some key 1099 expenses examples that can significantly impact your tax bill:


### Mileage and travel expenses


Every[business trip](https://use.expensify.com/travel) counts – from client meetings across town to conferences across the country. At[72.5 cents per mile for 2026](https://use.expensify.com/resource-center/guides/2026-mileage-reimbursement-rate) , that daily drive to your co-working space adds up fast. Don't forget flights, hotels, rental cars, and even that overpriced airport coffee if you're traveling for business.


**A common question** : Are[travel expenses](https://use.expensify.com/blog/business-trip-expense-management-with-expensify-travel) included in 1099 forms? Travel costs are deductible business expenses for contractors, not income that gets reported on your 1099.


### Home office deductions


Working from your kitchen table? You could be missing out on hundreds or thousands in deductions. Track the business portion of rent, utilities, internet, and office supplies. Even that ergonomic chair and standing desk converter count if they're primarily for work.


### Meals and entertainment (with a twist)


Client lunches are typically 50% deductible, but **here's what many contractors miss** : meals during business travel are often 100% deductible, and networking event tickets can be fully deductible too.


### Professional services and development


That web designer you hired, the[accountant](https://use.expensify.com/expense-tracking-for-accountants) who saves your sanity,[legal consultations](https://use.expensify.com/legal-expense-tracking) , and even industry certifications all qualify. Smart contractors track every penny spent on growing their skills and protecting their business.


### Software subscriptions and digital tools


From Zoom to Adobe Creative Suite, Canva to Slack – if it helps you work, it's likely deductible. These monthly subscription fees can add up to substantial deductions throughout the year, making proper tracking essential for maximizing your tax savings.


### Marketing and brand building


Website hosting, social media advertising, business cards, professional headshots, and even that Facebook ad promoting your services? Those are all potential deductions that build your business while reducing your tax bill.


The magic happens when your expense tracker automatically tags these purchases as you make them. No more end-of-year receipt hunts or wondering if that purchase was business-related. Smart categorization means every legitimate deduction gets captured, organized, and ready for tax time.

## Best practices for tracking 1099 expenses


Want to know the difference between contractors who stress about taxes and those who actually look forward to filing? It all comes down to systems.


Here are the game-changing habits that separate the pros from the panicked:


### Create an unbreachable wall between business and personal


**Here's a hard truth** :[mixing business and personal expenses](https://use.expensify.com/blog/write-off-business-expenses-on-personal-taxes) is like trying to unscramble eggs. Use separate[credit cards](https://use.expensify.com/resource-center/guides/credit-cards-for-self-employed) or bank accounts for business purchases. When everything runs through dedicated business accounts, categorization becomes automatic and audits become bearable. Your future self will thank you when tax season arrives.


### Embrace the "capture now, categorize later" mindset


The best time to scan a receipt is the moment you get it. Waiting until later is how receipts disappear into jacket pockets and car consoles, never to be seen again. Modern OCR technology can extract data from even crumpled receipts, so snap that photo immediately, even if you're standing in the checkout line.


### Master the art of smart categorization


Random expense categories are the enemy of good bookkeeping. Create a system that makes sense for your business and stick to it. "Office Supplies" is clearer than "Stuff I Bought." "Client Meals" beats "Food Things." Consistent categorization makes tax preparation faster and reduces errors that can trigger audits.


### Schedule monthly financial check-ins


Treat your finances like any other important client meeting. Set aside time each month to review spending patterns, check for missed receipts, and plan for upcoming tax obligations. This isn't just about staying organized; it's about spotting trends that can help you make smarter business decisions.


### Know when to level up


Still manually entering receipt data or hitting limits on your free plan? That's your business telling you it's time to invest in better tools. When you're spending more time on bookkeeping than billable work, upgrading to a robust expense tracking solution isn't an expense; it's a profit center.
