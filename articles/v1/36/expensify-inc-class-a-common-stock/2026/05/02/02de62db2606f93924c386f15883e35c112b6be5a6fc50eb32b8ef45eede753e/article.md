# How Expensify became the "Expense Management Platform of the Year"

## Why the best expense management platforms are consolidating everything


For decades,[travel and expense management](https://use.expensify.com/blog/realtime-travel-expense-management) lived in separate systems. One tool for booking flights, another for filing[expense reports](https://use.expensify.com/expense-reports) , a third for[corporate cards](https://use.expensify.com/company-credit-card) , and yet another for reimbursements. The result was predictable: manual receipt uploads, post-trip cleanup, compliance gaps, and finance teams spending more time reconciling data than analyzing it.


That model is breaking down. Businesses are increasingly looking for a single expense management platform that handles travel booking, expense capture, policy enforcement, corporate cards, and[reconciliation](https://use.expensify.com/corporate-card-reconciliation) in one place. The question is no longer whether consolidation makes sense, but which platforms are actually delivering on that promise.


[The 2026 TravelTech Breakthrough Awards](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260506556347/en/Expensify-Named-Expense-Management-Platform-of-the-Year) offered one answer.


## Expensify named Expense Management Platform of the Year


We were honored to be named Expense Management Platform of the Year at the 2026 TravelTech Breakthrough Awards. The program, run by TravelTech Breakthrough as part of Tech Breakthrough, evaluates thousands of nominations across the global travel and hospitality technology ecosystem. An independent panel of industry experts selects the winners.


Daniel Vidal, our Chief Strategy Officer, put the recognition in context:


"Travel and expense have historically been managed across disconnected systems. We built Expensify Travel to bring everything into one place, so companies can control costs while making travel easier for employees. This recognition reflects the impact of that approach, and we're honored to have it acknowledged by TravelTech Breakthrough."


Bryan Vaughn, Managing Director of TravelTech Breakthrough Awards, highlighted what set our approach apart:


"Expensify Travel reinvents corporate booking and management by fully handling business travel. Traveling can be stressful enough, whether you're flying across the country or renting a car to see a client, without having to deal with the hassle of manual receipt uploads, expense reports, and post-trip cleanup. By combining travel booking, expense automation, centralized billing, realtime collaboration, and AI-powered Concierge support into one platform, Expensify delivers a modern, scalable solution that eliminates manual work, improves compliance, and enhances the employee experience."


This recognition is meaningful because it comes from outside our walls. Independent validation confirms that the unified platform approach we have been building is resonating with the broader industry.


## How does AI improve expense management?


The award recognized several capabilities that reflect where travel and expense management is heading. At the core is a shift from reactive tools that process expenses after the fact to proactive systems that guide employees in realtime.


Our[AI-powered Concierge](https://use.expensify.com/ai-expense-management) acts as a 24/7 expense expert, flagging policy issues as they happen, automating receipt matching, and resolving questions throughout the trip lifecycle. Trip Rooms give travelers, managers, and finance teams a shared space to coordinate approvals and logistics for each journey. And centralized billing brings corporate cards, company billing, and individual cards into a single source of truth for reconciliation.


Together, these features address the pain points finance leaders and operations managers face every day: poor spend visibility, disconnected data, manual reporting, and employee friction. When AI expense management works well, it doesn’t just save time; it changes how people experience business travel.


## What this means for the travel and expense management


The consolidation trend in travel and expense management is accelerating. Businesses of all sizes, from scaling[startups](https://use.expensify.com/startup-expense-management) to global enterprises, are moving away from patchwork solutions and toward platforms that handle the full workflow.


Why? Because unified platforms reduce manual work, improve policy compliance, and give finance teams realtime visibility into[company spend](https://use.expensify.com/spend-management) .


For us, serving over 15 million users worldwide, this award reflects the direction we have been heading since we started. We believe expense management should be simple enough so employees actually use it and powerful enough so finance teams trust it. That combination of accessibility and depth is what drives everything we build.


The industry is moving toward fewer tools, smarter automation, and better employee experiences, and we’re glad to be part of that shift.
