# Contactless Payment Explained: How It Works, Types and Benefits

Table of Contents


Toggle


*A contactless payment is a digital payment method that allows customers to pay without physically inserting a card, exchanging cash, or manually entering payment details. Payments are completed using technologies such as NFC (Near Field Communication), UPI QR codes, mobile wallets, wearable devices, and UPI Tap and Pay.*


---


The concept of contactless payment has transitioned from a convenience feature to an essential customer expectation. Whether visiting a coffee shop, retail store, clinic, or paying a delivery executive, customers increasingly prefer quick digital payment options over cash.


Businesses that support multiple contactless payment methods often benefit from faster checkout experiences, better transaction records, and higher customer satisfaction. Understanding how contactless payments work can help businesses choose the right setup rather than simply adopting every available payment option.


##


**What Is Contactless Payment?**


The contactless payment system is an electronic payment method where a customer completes a transaction by tapping, scanning, or placing a payment-enabled device near a payment terminal or QR code.


Examples of contactless payments in India include:


- Tap-and-pay debit and credit cards at NFC-enabled terminals
- NFC-enabled smartphones and smartwatches
- [UPI QR code payments](https://www.cashfree.com/upi-qr-code/) through banking and payment apps
- Mobile wallet payments
- UPI Tap and Pay transactions
- [Payment links](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-links/) approved through app-based checkout flows

##


**How Contactless Payment Works**


Contactless payments use different technologies depending on the[payment method](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/payment-mode-types/) .


### 1. **NFC (Near Field Communication) Payments**


The customer taps or places a card, smartphone, smartwatch, or wearable near an NFC-enabled payment terminal.


NFC allows secure communication between two compatible devices located only a few centimeters apart. The terminal processes the payment information and completes the transaction. Depending on issuer rules and transaction value, PIN verification may be required.


### **2. QR Code Payments**


The customer scans the merchant’s QR code using a UPI or wallet application.


The process typically involves:


1. Scanning the QR code
2. Verifying the payment amount
3. Authorizing the transaction using a UPI PIN or biometric authentication


QR code payments can be:


- Static QR codes
- Dynamic QR codes


Static QR codes are commonly used by small businesses, while dynamic QR codes are often preferred by larger merchants for improved reconciliation and automation.


Regardless of the method used, contactless payments generate digital transaction records that simplify accounting, reconciliation, reporting, and refund management.


##


**Types of Contactless Payment in India**


Contactless payment includes multiple transaction formats designed for different customer and merchant environments.


### 1. **NFC Card Payments (Tap-to-Pay Cards)**


Customers tap contactless-enabled debit or credit cards on NFC-compatible POS terminals. No swiping or card insertion is required.


### 2. **NFC Mobile Payments**


Customers use NFC-enabled smartphones or smartwatches to complete payments. Payment credentials are securely stored within digital wallets or tokenized card systems.


### **3. UPI QR Code Payments**


Customers scan a merchant QR code through their[preferred UPI app](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/top-upi-apps-in-india/) , verify the payment amount, and authorize the transaction.


This method remains one of India’s most popular contactless payment options because merchants do not require specialized hardware.


### **4. UPI Tap and Pay**


UPI Tap and Pay combines UPI infrastructure with NFC technology, allowing customers to tap NFC-enabled devices to complete[UPI transactions](https://www.cashfree.com/upi-payment-gateway/) .


### **5. Mobile Wallet Payments**


Mobile wallets securely store card information, linked bank accounts, or wallet balances, enabling quick[digital payments](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/digital-payment-system/) without repeatedly entering payment details.


### **6. Wearable Payments**


Smartwatches, payment bands, and payment rings use tokenized credentials to support NFC transactions at compatible terminals.


As wearable adoption increases, this category is expected to see continued growth in India.


##


**NFC vs QR Code Contactless Payment: Key Differences**


Both NFC and QR code payments support contactless transactions, but they serve different merchant needs.


**Factor** **NFC Payment** **QR Code Payment**


Customer Action Tap card, phone, or wearable Scan QR and confirm


Merchant Setup NFC-enabled terminal required QR code required


Transaction Speed Extremely fast Fast


Setup Cost Higher Lower


Typical Usage Retail stores, transit, restaurants Small businesses, delivery, service providers


For many businesses, the ideal solution is not choosing one over the other. Supporting both NFC and QR-based payments offers customers maximum flexibility during checkout.


##


**Benefits of Contactless Payments for Businesses**


Contactless payment creates practical benefits for businesses beyond customer convenience. Here are the key advantages:


- **Faster checkout:** Payment through tapping or scanning saves time spent waiting in lines at billing counters. This will be helpful for retail shops, coffee shops, clinics, and fast-food outlets.
- **Fewer payment failures:** Offering more payment modes minimizes the risk of a customer leaving without paying due to a lack of payment methods.[UPI](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/upi-full-form-meaning-how-it-works/) , cards, digital wallets, and QR codes ensure a smooth payment process.
- **Better payment record keeping:** Payments made through digital means automatically generate transaction records. These make reconciliation and accounting easier without the need for manual counting of cash.
- **Lower cash handling effort:** Fewer cash transactions mean less time spent on counting, storing, and depositing physical money, and fewer risks around change management.


##


**Challenges and Limitations of Contactless Payments**


Despite their benefits, businesses should also understand potential limitations.


- **Internet Dependency** : QR-based payments typically require internet connectivity for authorization.
- **Device Compatibility** : NFC payments require compatible terminals and supported customer devices.
- **Payment Verification:** Merchants must verify payment confirmations before handing over goods or services.
- **QR Code Fraud Risks:** Improperly displayed or tampered QR codes can create security risks.
- **Failed Transaction Handling:** Businesses should have processes for managing payment disputes and failed transactions efficiently.


##


**Are Contactless Payments Safe?**


Contactless payments are designed with multiple security layers.


**NFC Payments Use Tokenization**


Tokenization replaces sensitive card information with a secure transaction-specific token. Merchants never receive actual card details.


**UPI Payments Require Customer Approval**


Customers must actively approve UPI transactions using a PIN or biometric authentication.


**Mobile Wallet Security**


Mobile wallets typically require:


- Device lock authentication
- Biometric verification
- PIN protection


In addition, payment providers continuously monitor transactions for unusual activity.

**FAQs**


### **1. What is a contactless payment?**


Contactless payment is a digital way to pay without swiping cards or using cash. It works through methods like NFC cards, UPI QR codes, mobile wallets, wearables, and UPI Tap & Pay.


### **2. What is the contactless payment meaning?**


Contactless payment meaning refers to making payments through a tap, scan, or digital device without physically handing over cash or inserting a card into a terminal.


### **3. Is UPI considered a contactless payment?**


Yes. UPI QR code payments and UPI Tap and Pay transactions are both contactless payment methods because they allow customers to complete transactions digitally without handling cash.


### **4. What is the difference between NFC and QR code payments?**


NFC payments require customers to tap a compatible card or device near a payment terminal. QR code payments require customers to scan a merchant QR code and authorize the payment within an app.


### **5. Are contactless payments secure?**


Yes. Contactless payments use security measures such as tokenization, PIN verification, biometric authentication, device security, and transaction monitoring.


### **6. Do contactless payments require internet access?**


Most QR-based payments require internet connectivity. NFC card payments may depend on terminal and network configurations.


### **7. Can small businesses accept contactless payments?**


Yes. Small businesses can start accepting contactless payments with a UPI QR code, often without investing in expensive hardware.


### **8. What does a business need to start accepting contactless payments?**


Businesses can start with a UPI QR code for scan-based payments. To accept NFC card payments, they need a compatible POS terminal. Online businesses typically require a payment gateway supporting multiple payment methods.
