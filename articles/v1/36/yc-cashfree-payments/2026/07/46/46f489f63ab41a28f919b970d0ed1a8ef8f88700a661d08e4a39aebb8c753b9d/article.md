**What Are International Transactions?**


International transactions refer to the exchange of goods, services, or funds between individuals or businesses in different countries. For example, an Indian exporter shipping products to Europe, a SaaS company billing customers in the US, or a freelancer receiving payments from clients overseas are all examples of international transactions.


Unlike domestic payments, cross-border transactions involve multiple currencies, banking networks, exchange rates, and country-specific regulations. ##


**What Is an International Payment Gateway?**


An international payment gateway is a secure online payment system that enables businesses to accept and process transactions from customers across the globe. Unlike a domestic gateway that only supports local cards and currency, an international gateway is designed for cross-border commerce.


- Accept payments in multiple currencies
- Process international debit and credit cards
- Support global wallets and payment methods
- Handle currency conversion
- Meet RBI and international compliance requirements
- Settle payments directly into an Indian bank account


***In short, it enables Indian businesses to accept payments from customers worldwide while delivering a secure and frictionless payment experience.***


##


How Does a Payment Gateway for International Payments Work?


When an overseas customer makes a purchase from your website, an international payment gateway securely processes the transaction behind the scenes. Here’s how the process works:


1. **Customer Checkout:** The customer selects a product or service and pays using an international credit card, debit card, or supported digital wallet.
2. **Payment Authorization:** The[payment gateway](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-gateway-india/) encrypts the transaction details and securely sends them to the customer’s issuing bank for approval.
3. **Currency Conversion:** If the customer pays in a foreign currency, the gateway processes the exchange rate and converts the amount before settlement.
4. **Transaction Approval:** Once the issuing bank verifies the payment, the transaction is approved or declined based on available funds and security checks.
5. **Settlement:** The approved payment is processed through international banking networks and settled into the merchant’s Indian bank account, usually within a few business days.
6. **Reporting & Compliance:** The gateway provides transaction reports, settlement details, and required documentation to help businesses manage accounting and regulatory requirements.


The entire process takes only a few seconds for customers while ensuring secure and compliant[cross-border payment processing](https://www.cashfree.com/cross-border-payment-solutions/) .

**How to Choose the Best Payment Gateway for International Transactions**


Entering new markets around the world generates new income sources; however, there are also new complexities, particularly regarding securing funds more quickly. Choosing the international payment gateway has to integrate properly with how people pay in different countries around the world and how you want to manage your operations in the background.


Here are a few key factors to help guide the decision:


- **Coverage of Currencies and Local Payment Options:** Customers are more likely to complete purchases when they can pay in their own currency and use familiar methods. A good gateway should support multi-currency transactions and give access to local payment options. This can include cards, e-wallets, and money transfer solutions that are common practice within a certain region.


For example, support for UPI in India or[SEPA in Europe](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/sepa-payments/) can make a noticeable difference in conversion rates.


- **Global Compliance and Regulatory Alignment:** International payments are processed under various taxation laws and privacy regulations, and protection against fraud in each country. Look for a provider that handles regional compliance requirements like PSD2, SCA, and local tax calculations, so you don’t run into trouble managing cross-border transactions.


- **Fraud Protection and Risk Tools:** Global payments bring higher fraud exposure, so built-in protection is key. Look for gateways with real-time detection, adaptive risk scoring, and customizable rules. Cashfree offers[RiskShield](https://www.cashfree.com/risk-shield-for-payment-disbursal/) , which helps flag suspicious disbursals using pattern analysis and rule-based filters. It’s especially useful for businesses managing vendor payouts or high-volume transactions.


- **Transparent Pricing and Fee Structure:** International transactions will have extra charges for things like currency exchange fees and international card network fees. Be sure to ask for a full quotation of the charges involved. Compare the charges of the payment services when it comes to refund charges,[chargeback fees](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/chargebacks-meaning-how-they-works-types-causes-prevention/) , and the time taken for payouts.
