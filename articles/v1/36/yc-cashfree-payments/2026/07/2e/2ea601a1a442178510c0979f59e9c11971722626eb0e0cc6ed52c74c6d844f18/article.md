# How Indian D2C Brands Can Build and Convert an NRI Customer Base

Table of Contents


Toggle


Selling internationally doesn’t always mean finding new customers, it can mean serving the millions of NRIs already looking for authentic Indian products. While these buyers often have high purchase intent, they’re also more cautious about payments, shipping, and trust than domestic shoppers.


This guide explains what NRI customers expect from an Indian D2C store and how the right checkout experience, international payment support, and retention strategy can help convert more overseas visitors into repeat customers.


📌


### TL;DR


The global Indian diaspora presents a significant growth opportunity for Indian D2C brands. NRI customers often have high purchase intent for authentic India-origin products but expect a trusted shopping experience. By supporting international card payments, local currency pricing, transparent shipping, and a seamless checkout, brands can improve conversions and build long-term customer loyalty.


##


The Most Overlooked Growth Segment for Indian D2C Brands


India has one of the world’s largest overseas diaspora communities, with millions of people living across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Australia. For Indian D2C brands, this represents a customer segment with a unique advantage, they already understand the products you’re selling.


An NRI customer searching for a specific regional pickle in London, Ayurvedic hair oil in New York, or handcrafted silk sarees in Dubai doesn’t usually need convincing about the product itself. They’ve often grown up using these products or have strong cultural connections that make them actively seek authentic Indian brands instead of locally adapted alternatives.


This dramatically reduces the amount of product education required compared to selling to completely new international audiences.


However, familiarity with the product doesn’t automatically translate into trust in your website.


***Before completing a purchase, NRI customers typically evaluate questions such as:***


- Is this an authentic Indian brand?
- Will my international card work?
- Can I pay in my local currency?
- How long will delivery take?
- What happens if my order is damaged or delayed?
- Can I trust this website with my payment details?


The brands that answer these questions clearly and provide a[smooth international checkout experience](https://www.cashfree.com/one-click-checkout/) are far more likely to convert visitors into customers.


For many Indian D2C businesses, improving the buying experience can unlock more international revenue than simply increasing advertising spend.


##


What NRI Buyers Look for Before They Trust an Indian D2C Store


NRI shoppers evaluate Indian ecommerce stores differently from domestic customers. While they already trust the product, they need to trust your store before making a purchase.


**What Buyers Look For** **Why It Matters** **How to Build Trust**


Product authenticity They want genuine Indian products, not diluted alternatives. Highlight sourcing, ingredients, certifications, and brand story.


Reliable international shipping Delivery delays and damaged packages are common concerns. Share shipping partners, delivery timelines, and packaging details.


Secure payments Paying on an unfamiliar website feels risky. Display trusted payment logos, SSL security, and recognizable payment gateways.


International card acceptance Failed payments are a major reason for checkout abandonment. Clearly state that international cards are accepted and supported.


Transparent returns Overseas returns can be expensive and complicated. Publish a clear policy covering returns, refunds, and damaged orders.


Among these, payment experience plays a critical role. Even if customers trust your products, they’ll hesitate if they aren’t confident their payment will go through securely.


A checkout that supports international cards, displays familiar[payment methods](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/payment-mode-types/) , and minimizes payment failures helps reduce friction and improves conversion rates. ##


Building Repeat Purchases from an NRI Customer Base


Acquiring an NRI customer is only the beginning. The real opportunity lies in turning that first purchase into repeat business.


Many NRI buyers regularly purchase products such as wellness supplements, regional foods, ethnic wear, and gifts for family and friends. A positive first experience makes them far more likely to return.


Here are a few ways to encourage repeat purchases:


- **Make Reordering Easy** : For products customers buy regularly, send timely WhatsApp or email reminders based on typical consumption cycles. A simple reminder can bring buyers back when they’re ready to reorder.
- **Offer Gifting Options** : Many NRI customers shop for family and friends in India. Make gifting easy by supporting separate billing and delivery addresses, gift messages, and a seamless checkout experience.
- **Offer Familiar Payment Options** : While many customers prefer paying with international cards, some feel more comfortable using PayPal – especially when shopping from a new website. Cashfree Payments supports PayPal for eligible merchants once it’s connected through the merchant dashboard.
- **Keep the Experience Consistent** : f a customer browses and pays in their local currency, continue that experience in post-purchase emails and order confirmations whenever possible. A consistent experience builds trust and makes repeat purchases feel effortless.

Frequently Asked Questions


#### Why are NRI customers a valuable audience for Indian D2C brands?


NRI buyers are often already familiar with Indian products and actively search for authentic brands. As a result, they usually require less product education than first-time international shoppers. Their purchase decision is more likely to depend on trust, shipping, and checkout experience.


#### Do NRI customers spend more than domestic buyers?


In many categories, such as wellness, premium foods, gifting, and ethnic wear, NRI customers often place higher-value orders. However, average order value varies depending on your product category and target market.


#### How can I accept payments from NRI customers?


A payment gateway that supports international payments enables eligible businesses to accept payments from overseas customers using international cards and other supported payment methods. Before enabling these features, confirm the available options for your merchant account and integration.


#### Should I display prices in local currencies?


Displaying prices in a buyer’s local currency can make shopping easier by reducing the need for manual currency conversion. The available multi-currency display options depend on your payment gateway integration.


#### How can I encourage repeat purchases from NRI customers?


Focus on delivering a great first experience. Timely reorder reminders, easy gifting options, transparent communication, and a smooth checkout can encourage customers to return and purchase again.
