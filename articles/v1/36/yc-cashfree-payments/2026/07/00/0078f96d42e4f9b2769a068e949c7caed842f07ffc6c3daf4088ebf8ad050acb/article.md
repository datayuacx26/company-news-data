##


COD is Still a Significant Part of Indian Ecommerce. The Refund Side of it isn’t Solved


Cash on Delivery (COD) continues to account for a significant share of ecommerce orders in India, particularly for categories such as fashion, beauty, electronics accessories, homeware, and lifestyle products. While digital payments continue to grow, many customers still prefer paying only after receiving their order, making COD an important checkout option for WooCommerce stores.


For many merchants, COD orders contribute anywhere between 20% and 50% of total sales, depending on the product category and customer demographics.


The operational side of COD is well understood. The courier collects the payment, remits the funds to the merchant, and the order is completed.


**The challenge begins when a customer requests a return.**


Unlike prepaid orders, WooCommerce cannot automatically refund COD payments because there is no original digital payment method to reverse. The store has no saved card, UPI ID, or bank account linked to the transaction. As a result, businesses often need to collect customers’ payment details separately before they can issue the refund.


As return volumes increase, this manual process quickly becomes one of the biggest operational bottlenecks for WooCommerce stores in India.


##


Why WooCommerce Can’t Automatically Refund COD Orders


WooCommerce supports automatic refunds for many[online payment methods](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/payment-mode-types/) because the original payment instrument already exists. When a customer pays using a card, UPI, or net banking through a[supported payment gateway](https://www.cashfree.com/woocommerce-payment-gateway-india/) , the refund can usually be initiated back to the same source.


COD orders work differently. Since the payment is collected offline by the delivery partner, WooCommerce has no payment destination available when a refund needs to be processed. Before sending money back, the merchant must first obtain the customer’s preferred payout details—whether that’s a[UPI ID](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/what-is-upi-id-how-to-create-find-change-it/) , bank account, or another supported payment method.


This is why COD refund management often becomes a manual workflow rather than an automated one.


##


What Actually Happens in Most WooCommerce Stores Today


When a COD return is approved, most[WooCommerce stores](https://woocommerce.com/) follow a process that looks something like this:


1. The customer requests a return.
2. The support team contacts the customer to collect their UPI ID or bank account details.
3. The customer shares these details over WhatsApp, email, or phone.
4. Someone from the operations or finance team manually initiates a bank transfer or[UPI payment](https://www.cashfree.com/upi-payment-gateway/) .
5. The refund is recorded in a spreadsheet or a separate finance tool before being reconciled with the original WooCommerce order.


On paper, the process seems straightforward. In practice, every step introduces delays. Customers may not respond immediately with their payment details.[Bank account numbers](https://www.cashfree.com/bank-account-verification/) or UPI IDs can be entered incorrectly. Finance teams spend time manually processing refunds, while support teams handle repeated follow-ups from customers asking about refund status.


Because the refund happens outside WooCommerce, teams often have to reconcile multiple systems to verify whether a customer has actually been paid. For a business handling only a handful of COD returns each month, these inefficiencies may seem manageable. But for stores processing **50–100 COD refunds or more** , manual refund management can consume valuable operational time, slow refund turnaround, and negatively affect the post-purchase customer experience.


##


Common Challenges with Manual COD Refunds


Beyond the extra operational effort, manual COD refund management creates several business challenges:


- **Longer refund turnaround times** due to manual collection of customer payment details.
- **Higher operational workload** for customer support and finance teams.
- **Greater risk of payment errors** caused by incorrect UPI IDs or bank account details.
- **Reconciliation challenges** because refunds are tracked separately from WooCommerce orders.
- **Privacy concerns** , as customers often share sensitive banking information over email or messaging apps.


As order volumes grow, these small inefficiencies compound into a process that is difficult to scale efficiently.

##


Best Practices for Managing COD Refunds in WooCommerce


Whether you’re using Cashgram or another refund workflow, following a few best practices can help reduce delays and improve the customer experience.


- **Define a clear refund policy** and display it on your website.
- **Approve returns promptly** to avoid unnecessary refund delays.
- **Keep customers informed** through automated notifications.
- **Avoid collecting sensitive banking details over chat apps** whenever possible.
- **Track refund status alongside order history** for easier reconciliation.
- **Review refund turnaround time regularly** to identify operational bottlenecks.


As your business grows, standardising the refund process becomes just as important as optimising the checkout experience.
