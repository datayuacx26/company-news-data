##


Why International Buyers Can’t Pay on Many Wix Stores


Launching a Wix store in India is relatively straightforward. Most businesses can quickly start accepting UPI, domestic debit and credit cards, net banking, and other popular local payment methods.


The challenge often appears only after international customers begin placing orders.


Imagine an NRI customer in the UK discovers your Ayurvedic skincare brand through Google or social media. They browse your products, add items to their cart, and proceed to checkout using a UK-issued Visa card. Instead of completing the payment, the transaction fails or the customer abandons the purchase because their preferred payment method isn’t supported.


For many merchants, this looks like an abandoned cart rather than a payment failure, making it difficult to identify the actual issue. This isn’t a bug in Wix. It’s simply the result of many Indian stores being configured primarily for domestic payments.


If your business serves customers outside India, even occasionally, your checkout should support the payment methods those buyers expect.

##


Common Reasons International Cards Still Fail


Even after integrating a payment gateway, international transactions may not work if certain settings haven’t been completed. Some common causes include:


- International card acceptance hasn’t been activated in the Cashfree dashboard.
- PayPal hasn’t been connected (if you intend to offer PayPal).
- Another payment provider is still configured as the active Wix payment method.
- The merchant account hasn’t yet been approved for international payment acceptance.
- Sandbox or test mode is still enabled.


Reviewing these settings before going live can help reduce checkout failures and improve conversion rates.


##


How to Enable International Payments on Your Wix Store


Setting up international payments doesn’t require a second payment gateway. If you’re already using[Cashfree Payments with Wix](https://www.cashfree.com/wix-payment-gateway/) , most of the work involves enabling the right payment methods within your merchant account.


#### Step 1: Verify Your Cashfree Payments Account


Log in to your Cashfree Payments dashboard and confirm your account is fully activated. If you plan to accept overseas payments, ensure your account is eligible for international payment acceptance.


#### Step 2: Enable International Cards


Navigate to **Settings → Payment Gateway → Payment Methods** and verify that international card acceptance is enabled for supported credit and debit cards.


If you don’t see this option, contact the Cashfree Payments support team to request activation.


#### Step 3: Connect Cashfree Payments to Wix


In your Wix dashboard, confirm that **Cashfree Payments** is configured as your active payment gateway. International card acceptance works through the Cashfree integration, so another gateway may not support the same capabilities.


#### Step 4: Connect PayPal (Optional)


If your customers prefer PayPal, connect your PayPal account from the Cashfree Payments dashboard. Once linked, PayPal becomes available as an additional payment option for eligible international buyers.


#### Step 5: Review Settlement Preferences


International payments are generally settled in INR on a T+2 settlement cycle. If your business handles significant export volumes, you can discuss EEFC account settlement options with the Cashfree Payments team, subject to eligibility.


#### Step 6: Test Before Going Live


Before launching, complete a few test transactions to verify that:


- International cards are accepted successfully.
- Payment confirmations are received.
- Orders are created correctly in Wix.
- Settlement and reporting appear correctly in your Cashfree dashboard.


A quick end-to-end test can help prevent unnecessary checkout failures after launch.

Frequently Asked Questions


#### Why can’t international customers pay on my Wix store?


Many Indian Wix stores are initially configured to accept domestic payment methods such as UPI, Indian cards, and net banking. International card acceptance typically requires activation within your Cashfree Payments account before overseas cards can be processed.


#### Do I need a separate payment gateway for international payments?


No. If you’re using Cashfree Payments with Wix, eligible merchants can enable international card acceptance within the same merchant account instead of maintaining a second payment gateway. PayPal requires connecting your existing PayPal account separately.


#### Which international payment methods are supported?


Depending on merchant eligibility, Cashfree Payments supports international Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and Maestro cards. Merchants can also offer PayPal after connecting their PayPal account, along with Apple Pay for customers in supported international markets.


#### Does Cashfree Payments support cross-border payment collection?


Yes. Cashfree Payments is an RBI-authorized **PA-CB (Export and Import)** payment aggregator, allowing eligible merchants to accept international payments through its cross-border payment infrastructure without obtaining their own PA-CB authorization.


#### Can customers pay in their local currency?


Cashfree Payments supports **multi-currency price display** across more than 140 currencies, helping international customers view prices in a familiar currency before completing checkout.
