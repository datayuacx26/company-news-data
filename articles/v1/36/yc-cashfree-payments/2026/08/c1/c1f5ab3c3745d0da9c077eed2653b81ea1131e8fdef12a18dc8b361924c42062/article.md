Wix is one of the most popular website-building platforms for businesses that want to create an online store without extensive coding. For an ecommerce business, however, building a website is only one part of the process. Customers also need a convenient and reliable way to pay for their purchases.


The efficiency and sales of eCommerce stores are related to customer shopping experience This is where choosing the right[Wix payment gateway in India](https://www.cashfree.com/wix-payment-gateway/) becomes important.


A payment gateway allows your Wix store to accept online payments through methods such as UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and wallets.


So, how do you integrate a payment gateway with your Wix store in India?


Let’s find out.


##


How to Integrate a Payment Gateway With Wix in India


To start accepting online payments through your Wix store, you’ll first need to sign up with a payment gateway that supports Wix. You may need to submit business information and complete the required KYC process before you can start accepting payments.


Once your payment gateway account is activated, you can connect it with your Wix store.


### Steps to Integrate Cashfree Payments With Wix


If you’re using Cashfree Payments, the integration can be completed through the payment options available in your Wix account.


1. Sign in to your Wix account. Then, head to Settings
2. There, select the option titled ‘Accept Payments’


1. After that, click on ‘See More Payment Options
2. Here, search for ‘Cashfree Payments’ and click on connect


1. Next step is to enter your App ID and Secret Key. This information is provided to you once you onboard with Cashfree Payments
2. Thereafter, click on Connect
3. Enter your App ID and Secret Key of your Cashfree Payments Account.
4. Click on Connect to integrate Cashfree Payments with your Wix account.


Yup, that is pretty much it.


Now, you can accept payments through your Wix payment gateway in India!

##


Which Payment Gateway Is Best for Wix in India?


The right Wix payment gateway depends on your business model, customer base, payment methods, transaction volume, settlement requirements, and integration needs.


For Indian merchants, it is particularly important to choose a provider that supports the payment methods customers commonly use in India.


For example,[UPI is a major part of India’s digital payment ecosystem](https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics) , so your payment gateway should support UPI alongside other relevant payment methods.


Here are some of the key factors to consider when choosing a payment gateway for Wix.


### 1. Multiple Payment Methods


Your customers may prefer different[payment methods](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/payment-mode-types/) depending on their device, purchase, and personal preferences.


A good Wix payment gateway should support a broad range of payment options, including:


- [UPI](https://www.cashfree.com/upi-payment-gateway/)
- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- Net banking
- Wallets
- Other supported digital payment methods


Offering multiple payment options can make checkout more convenient and reduce the chances of customers abandoning their purchase because their preferred payment method isn’t available.


Cashfree Payments supports multiple payment methods for online businesses, allowing merchants to manage different payment options through a single payment gateway.


### 2. Easy Integration and Quick Onboarding


A payment gateway should be easy to connect with your ecommerce platform. This is particularly important for small and medium-sized businesses that may not have a dedicated development team.


A[Wix-compatible payment gateway](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/plugins/wix) can simplify the setup process by allowing merchants to connect their payment account through the platform rather than building a payment integration from scratch.


When evaluating a provider, also look for:


- Clear integration documentation
- Wix compatibility
- Simple onboarding
- Developer documentation
- Sandbox or testing capabilities
- Technical support
- Account or onboarding assistance


A straightforward integration means you can spend less time dealing with payment setup and more time running your online store.


### 3. Transparent Payment Gateway Fees


[Payment gateway pricing](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-gateway-charges/) is another important factor when choosing a payment provider. The cost of processing a transaction can depend on factors such as:


- Payment method
- Transaction type
- Business category
- Domestic or international payment
- Additional payment services
- Settlement or other optional features


Before choosing a provider, review its current pricing and understand exactly what charges apply to your business. Instead of looking only at the headline transaction fee, consider the total cost of accepting payments.


This can include transaction charges, settlement-related costs, refund charges, international payment fees, and any optional services you choose.


### 4. Recurring Payments


If your Wix business operates on a subscription or recurring-payment model, recurring payments can be an important requirement.


Examples include:


- [Subscription services](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/subscription/introduction)
- Memberships
- Online services
- SaaS products
- Recurring bills
- Regular product deliveries


Depending on your business and payment setup,[recurring payment methods](https://www.cashfree.com/recurring-payment/) can include options such as:


- [UPI AutoPay](https://www.cashfree.com/upi-autopay/)
- e-Mandates
- [NACH](https://www.cashfree.com/enach/)
- Other supported recurring-payment mechanisms


Before choosing a provider, check whether the recurring payment methods you need are supported for your business and integration.


### 5. Faster Settlements


Payment settlement is another important consideration, particularly for ecommerce businesses that need regular access to[working capital](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/what-is-working-capital/) . A payment gateway collects customer payments and subsequently settles the eligible funds to your business bank account according to its settlement cycle.


When comparing providers, look at:


- Standard settlement cycle
- Settlement timing
- [Instant settlement availability](https://www.cashfree.com/instant-settlements/)
- Settlement reporting
- Settlement reconciliation


For businesses with inventory, supplier, payroll, or other regular expenses, predictable access to funds can make payment operations easier to manage.


### 6. Refunds and Payment Management


Payments don’t end when a customer completes a transaction.[Ecommerce businesses](https://www.cashfree.com/solutions/ecommerce-payment-gateway/) also need to handle refunds, cancellations, failed transactions, and payment-status queries.


Your payment gateway should therefore make it easy to:


- Initiate refunds
- Track refund status
- View transactions
- Monitor payment failures
- Reconcile payments
- Download reports
- Manage settlements


A user-friendly dashboard can make these day-to-day payment operations considerably easier.


##


Why Use a Payment Gateway Instead of a Direct Payment Integration?


For many Wix merchants, using a payment gateway is simpler than building and maintaining individual payment integrations. A payment gateway can provide a single connection to multiple payment methods and can also handle capabilities such as:


- Payment processing
- Transaction status
- Refunds
- Settlements
- Reporting
- Reconciliation
- Payment security
- Multiple payment methods


This can be particularly useful for small and medium-sized businesses that want to launch quickly without maintaining complex payment infrastructure. The right combination will depend on your business model and customer requirements.

##


How Much Does a Wix Payment Gateway Cost in India?


There isn’t one universal cost for integrating a payment gateway with Wix. Your overall payment-processing cost can depend on the provider, payment method, transaction type, business category, and other factors.


Before signing up, check the provider’s latest pricing and look beyond the transaction fee.


Consider:


- Setup fees
- Annual maintenance fees
- Transaction charges
- International payment charges
- Refund-related charges
- Settlement-related charges
- Optional payment services


This gives you a more accurate picture of the cost of accepting payments through your Wix store.


##


Benefits of Integrating a Payment Gateway With Wix


Connecting your Wix store to a suitable payment gateway can help you:


- **Offer more payment options** : Customers can choose the payment method that works best for them.
- **Improve checkout convenience:** A familiar payment experience can make it easier for customers to complete purchases.
- **Manage payments from one place** : A payment gateway dashboard can bring transactions, refunds, settlements, and reports together.
- **Support business growth** : As your order volume increases, payment APIs, reporting, reconciliation, and automated processes can help simplify payment operations.
- **Accept international payments** : If you plan to sell to customers outside India, you can choose a provider that supports the international payment methods and currencies relevant to your business.


##


Wix Payment Gateway Integration: Best Practices


Before going live, keep the following points in mind:


1. **Choose payment methods based on your customers.**
If most of your customers prefer UPI, make sure UPI is readily available at checkout.
2. **Check the complete pricing structure.**
Don’t compare providers only on the advertised transaction rate.
3. **Test the checkout experience.**
Complete test transactions and verify that successful and failed payments are handled correctly.
4. **Check refund and settlement processes.**
Make sure your team knows how to initiate refunds and track settlements.
5. **Review reporting and reconciliation features.**
These become increasingly important as transaction volume grows.
6. **Choose a provider with reliable support.**
Payment issues can directly affect sales, so access to technical and operational support matters.

Frequently Asked Questions


#### How do I add a payment gateway to my Wix website in India?


You can add a payment gateway through Wix’s payment settings. Go to Settings → Accept Payments, review the available payment providers, select your preferred provider, and follow the provider’s connection and onboarding process.


#### Which payment gateway is best for Wix in India?


The best payment gateway depends on your business requirements. Look for support for UPI, cards, net banking, recurring payments, refunds, settlements, reporting, and easy Wix integration.


#### How do I integrate Cashfree with Wix?


First, create and activate your Cashfree Payments account. Then, in your Wix account, go to Settings → Accept Payments, select See More Payment Options, find Cashfree Payments, and connect your account using the required credentials.


#### What payment methods can I accept on Wix in India?


The available payment methods depend on the payment gateway you connect with Wix. Depending on the provider and your account, you may be able to accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, wallets, and other supported payment methods.


#### How much does a Wix payment gateway cost in India?


The cost depends on the payment provider, payment method, transaction type, and other factors. Check the provider’s current pricing for applicable transaction charges, setup fees, annual fees, international payment charges, and optional services.


#### Can I accept UPI payments through Wix?


Yes. If your connected payment gateway supports UPI and is available for your Wix setup, you can offer UPI as a payment option to customers.


#### Can I accept international payments through a Wix payment gateway?


This depends on the payment provider, your business eligibility, and the countries, currencies, and payment methods supported by your account. If you plan to sell internationally, check the provider’s current international payment capabilities before integrating.
