# GST ARN Status Check Online: Easy Guide to Track GST Application

Table of Contents


Toggle


Once you submit your GST application on the GST portal, you get an application reference number (ARN). The ARN is the primary method available to check the status of your GST application. By checking your ARN status you can get details regarding your application, starting from the document verification process to clarification requests related to your application. Hence, it is important that you understand how to check your GST status by ARN number.


##


**What is ARN in GST Registration?**


The Application Reference Number (ARN number) is a unique 15-digit code generated after you successfully submit your GST registration form on the official GST portal.


**The ARN serves as:**


- Acknowledgement of GST application submission
- Tracking number for GST registration status
- Reference ID for clarification and verification


**You receive your GST ARN number in three ways:**


1. On the confirmation page after submission
2. Via acknowledgement email or SMS
3. Under “In ‘View Submitted Applications” on the GST portal


Without the ARN number, you cannot perform a GST application status check.


> In simple terms, ARN status tells you what action, if any, is required from your side before registration is completed. Monitoring this status helps businesses respond to clarification requests quickly and avoid unnecessary delays in GST approval.


##


**How to Check GST ARN Status Online in Minutes**


Here’s the process to check GST application status with the ARN number.


### **Step 1: Go to the GST Portal**


You don’t need to[log in to your GST portal](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/gst-login-password-portal-guide/) to check the ARN status. It’s accessible from the homepage of the GST portal.


### **Step 2: **Click on “Track Application Status”****


Under the Services tab, navigate to Track Application Status. It will direct you to a window where you will submit the ARN number.


Image source: gst.gov.in


### **Step 3: Select “Registration” Option**


Before you share the GST ARN track number, the portal will give you two options to choose from, and here select Registration.


Image source: gst.gov.in


### **Step 4: Enter ARN Number**


In the last step, share your ARN number and hit Search to find out the updated status of your GST application.


Image source: gst.gov.in


### **Step 5: View GST Application Status**


As you hit Search, the GST portal will provide a detailed report of your GST application status. This includes the current state, action date, and related information to the specific events.


Image source: gst.gov.in


##


**Some Common GST Registration Status Updates**


The government of India has a set protocol to issue GST numbers for businesses. As per the proceedings of this process, the GST authority processes every application in multiple stages before issuing the number. Here are the most common updates;


1. **Application Submitted:** The first GST registration status that ensures the application is successfully filed and the ARN is generated.
2. **Pending Verification:** This means the GST application is under review by the GST office and that the concerned authority is checking documents, business details, and declarations.
3. **Pending Clarification:** In case the GST office finds missing information, wrong information, or needs further clarity on any pointer, they will raise a question that the application must answer.
4. **Clarification Filed:** After submitting the required answer or documentation, the application will submit the clarification, the status update is Clarification Filed.
5. **Site Verification:** The concerned GST officer will visit the premises where the business will be conducted.
6. **Approved:** After all verifications are done, the GST officer will approve the application, and the[GSTIN](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/what-is-goods-and-service-tax-identification-number/) will be issued.
7. **Rejected:** If, for any reason, the clarifications provided aren’t up to their satisfaction, the application is rejected.

##


**What Should I Do if My Application Status Says Pending Clarification?**


If your application status reads “pending clarification”, something has been identified as needing your input. Here is how to best respond to that identification:


### **Step 1: Read your clarification notice very carefully**


When you log back into the GST portal, read the exact clarification that has been requested. A significant portion of rejections occur when the applicant does not understand what is being requested. Write down which document reference or field requires clarification.


### **Step 2: Gather any additional documentation needed**


If you need to provide additional documents (i.e. Electricity bill, partnership deed, etc.) make sure you get certified copies. Make certain all your documents are readable, legible, and clearly dated. Scanned PDFs work fine for almost all clarification requests.


### **Step 3: Submit your response prior to deadline**


Your deadline will be specified in the GST portal (typically between 7-15 Days after the notice date). Please do not miss your deadline. Once you have provided your clarification response, select ‘submit clarification’ from the options available to you in the GST portal. Missing deadlines may result in automatic rejection of your application.


### **Step 4: Check the status of your ARN after submission**


After submitting your clarification response, continue to monitor the status of your ARN. Typically, processing occurs within three to five working days. Upon successful submission of your clarification, your application status should change from ‘pending clarification’ to ‘clarification filed,’ and then proceed toward either ‘approved’ or ‘site verification.’


##


**How Long Does the GST Registration Status Take After the ARN Number?**


The amount of time it takes to register under GST largely depends upon whether you require clarification. Below is a breakdown by status stage:


**Stage** **Typical Duration**


Submission → Pending Verification 1–2 days


Pending Verification 2–4 days


Clarification Response Deadline 7–15 days


Clarification Filed → Decision 3–5 days


Site Visit (if required) 5–10 days


**Total (no clarifications)** **4–7 working days**


**Total (with one clarification round)** **17–22 working days**


##


**What Can Lead to Delays in the GST Registration Process?**


#### **Common Mistakes That Trigger Clarification Requests**


Recognizing potential pitfalls allows you to sidestep potential obstacles:


- **Business name mismatch:** your business name on your PAN record differs from your Aadhaar or actual trading name
- **Address discrepancy:** registered office address differs from operational address without proper explanation
- **Missing supporting docs:** electricity bill, rent agreement or partnership deed not uploaded or illegible
- **Incorrect NIC Code:** NIC code does not match stated business activity
- **Signature issues:** Signatures on documents don’t match Aadhaar


##


What Should I do if My Application Status Says ‘Pending for Clarification’?


If your application status says, ‘pending for clarification’ you should do the following:


- **Step 1** : Review the specific clarification request displayed on the GST portal.
- **Step 2** : Collect any required documentation (electricity bill, rent agreement etc).
- **Step 3** : Provide your response prior to the designated deadline (generally within 7-15 days).
- **Step 4:** Continuously monitor your ARN status for subsequent updates. Prompt response is key to preventing automatic rejection of your application.


##


What to Do if your GST Application is Rejected


You have 90 days (three months) to submit an appeal if your application has been rejected. Follow these steps to prepare your appeal:


### **Step 1: Access your rejection notice**


Access your rejection notice online through the GST portal. Carefully read it so you can clearly see why your application was rejected.


### **Step 2: Collect all relevant documents**


Collect all relevant documents needed to resolve the reason(s) for rejection. For example, if your application was rejected because of an incorrect address, obtain a notarized document confirming your current residence. If your application was rejected due to a discrepancy in your submitted information, collect corrected versions of your PAN card or Aadhar card.


### **Step 3: Submit your appeal**


Using the GST Portal, complete your appeal with any supporting documents and submit them under “Services > User Services > My Applications > Appeal to Appellate Authority”.


### **Step 4: Wait for authority review**


Once received by the applicable authority, they will review the appeal. If accepted, you will be issued a GSTIN. However, if rejected again, you may then appeal to the GST Appellate Authority.


Note: Most times rejections are overturned once you provide the missing or correct information.


##


How to Download GST Registration Certificate After Approval?


Once status shows “Approved”:


1. Log in to GST portal ([www.gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in/) )
2. Go to Services
3. Click User Services
4. Select View/Download Certificates


Download your GST registration certificate in PDF format.

Frequently Asked Questions


### What is an ARN number in GST registration?


ARN (Application Reference Number) is a 15-digit number generated after GST registration form submission. It is used for GST status check by ARN and tracking application progress.


### How can I check GST status using ARN?


Visit the GST portal → Track Application Status → Select Registration → Enter ARN number → Click Search.


You do not need login credentials for GST ARN status check.


### **Is GST ARN status the same as GST registration status?**


ARN is just an identification number and an acknowledgement about your GST registration process initiation. The ARN status is not the same as the GST status.


### **How many days after ARN generation will I get GSTIN?**


If no clarifications are required by the officials, you will get your GSTIN within 7 working days from the date of successful form submissions. Delays can occur if further checks and clarifications are required.


### **What does “Pending for Clarification” mean in GST status?**


Pending for clarification means when there’s more documents, answers, or clarifications required to be shared by the application. Subject to the approval of the given evidence or documentation, the authorities will process the application further.


### Can I track GST application status without ARN?


No. ARN number is mandatory for GST application status tracking.
