There is no one-size-fits-all answer. The right option depends on how your business collects payments.


- Choose a Payment Gateway if you have a website or mobile app where customers complete purchases through an online checkout.
- Choose a[Payment Link](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-links/) if you collect payments through WhatsApp, SMS, email, social media, or invoices without needing a website.
- Many growing businesses use both – a[payment gateway for website checkouts](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-gateway-india/) and payment links for invoices, customer support, offline sales, or quick payment requests.

What is a Payment Gateway?


A payment gateway is a software program that helps businesses accept online payments safely and quickly. It acts as a secure bridge between the customer, the business, and the banks to make sure money moves from the customer’s account to the business’s account without exposing sensitive information.


For large businesses, a payment gateway acts as a backbone, because it not only just accepts or processes payments but also ensures every transaction is secure and authenticated. It also helps businesses with[refunds](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/payment-gateway-refund-process/) , settlement tracking, payment reports, dispute management, and improving payment success rates.


#### **Who is Payment Gateway Beneficial For?**


A Payment gateway is suitable for businesses that sell products or services through a website or mobile app and want customers to pay during the checkout process.


**It is beneficial for:**


- E-commerce stores who require a seamless online checkout.
- SaaS and subscription businesses that[collect recurring payments](https://www.cashfree.com/recurring-payment/) .
- Online marketplaces with frequent digital transactions.
- Travel, food delivery, ticketing, and booking platforms that process high volumes of online payments.
- Businesses looking for an integrated payment experience with features like refunds, settlements, payment analytics, and fraud protection.

##


**What is a Payment Link?**


A payment link is a unique, secure URL that businesses can create and share with customers to collect payments online. The[link can be sent through WhatsApp](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/indias-most-conversion-friendly-whatsapp-payment-links/) , SMS, email, social media, or added to an invoice.


When a customer clicks the link, they are taken to a[secure payment page](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-pages/) where they can complete the payment using their preferred payment method, such as UPI, debit or credit cards, net banking, wallets, or bank transfers ([NEFT, IMPS, or RTGS](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/imps-vs-neft-vs-rtgs/) ), depending on the provider.


Unlike a payment gateway, a payment link doesn’t require a website, app, or technical integration. Businesses can generate a link in seconds, making it an easy option for collecting one-time payments,[advance payments](https://www.cashfree.com/blog/what-is-advance-payment/) , service fees, invoices, or partial payments.


#### **Who is Payment Link Beneficial For?**


A payment link is ideal for businesses that want to collect payments without a website or app or need a quick and simple way to request payments.


**It is beneficial for:**


- Freelancers and consultants who collect fees from clients.
- Small businesses and local shops who take orders through WhatsApp, Instagram, or phone calls.
- Service providers such as doctors, tutors, salons, fitness trainers, and repair professionals.
- Businesses sending invoices for one-time or partial payments.
- Anyone who wants to collect payments quickly through WhatsApp, SMS, email, or social media without any technical setup.

Frequently Asked Questions


#### Is a payment gateway the same as a payment link?


No. A payment gateway is integrated into a website or mobile app and allows customers to complete payments during checkout. A payment link is a secure URL that can be shared through WhatsApp, SMS, email, social media, or invoices to collect payments without requiring a website.


#### Can I use both a payment gateway and payment links?


Yes. Many businesses use both solutions together. A payment gateway handles website or app checkouts, while payment links are useful for invoices, customer support, advance payments, and offline sales.


#### Do I need a website to use payment links?


No. One of the biggest advantages of payment links is that they don’t require a website or mobile app. You can generate a payment link and share it directly with customers.


#### Is UPI a payment gateway?


No. UPI is a payment method or payment system, whereas a payment gateway is the technology that enables businesses to accept UPI and other payment methods securely through a website, app, or payment page.


#### Are payment gateways secure?


Yes. Reputed payment gateways use encryption, PCI DSS compliance, fraud detection tools, and multiple security measures to protect customer payment information and reduce the risk of fraudulent transactions.
