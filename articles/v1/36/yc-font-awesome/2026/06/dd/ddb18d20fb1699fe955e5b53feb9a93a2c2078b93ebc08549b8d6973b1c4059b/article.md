# WA 3.10: Random Content and New Pro+ Icons

## 🍫 Random Content


How about a little spontaneity?[<wa-random-content>](https://webawesome.com/docs/components/random-content/) helps spice things up by randomly displaying one (or more — your choice!) of its slotted child elements.


Choose whether the displayed items are *truly* random or shown in sequence, if content autoplays, and how it animates. It’s a great fit for testimonials, featured tips and tricks, and wherever you need a little variety.


*<wa-random-content>* is like a box of chocolates.[You never know what you’re gonna get.](https://youtu.be/vdtqSaJO-iM?si=Qi0M2WBakbZhmZP5&t=32)


## 👾 FA 7.3 in <wa-icon>


Five (!) new icon families are now available to use with[<wa-icon>](https://webawesome.com/docs/components/icon/) , thanks to[Font Awesome 7.3](https://fontawesome.com/changelog) .


Kick it old school with[Pixel](https://fontawesome.com/icons/packs/pixel) , piece it together with[Mosaic](https://fontawesome.com/icons/packs/mosaic) , or lay it on thick thin with[Vellum](https://fontawesome.com/icons/packs/vellum) . If the blocky Slab vibe is more your style, add even more depth with[Slab Duo](https://fontawesome.com/search?f=slab-duo) and[Slab Press Duo](https://fontawesome.com/search?f=slab-press-duo) .


For even more panache, dazzle folks with[new animations](https://webawesome.com/docs/components/icon/#animating) : *buzz* , *flip-360* , *float* , *jello* , *spin-snap* (plus *spin-snap-4* and *spin-snap-8* ), *swing* , and *wag* .


Plus, *<wa-icon>* has a[new canvas attribute](https://webawesome.com/docs/components/icon/#canvas) , replacing the *auto-width* boolean attribute, for greater control over an icon’s proportions and positioning.
