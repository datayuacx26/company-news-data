# Improve Water Conservation | Where, When and How Water is Being Used

You can’t save water if you don’t know how you are using it. That’s become a common starting point for[water conservation at universities and colleges](https://www.zurn.com/us/en/markets/higher-education.html) across the country. At one southeastern state[university, administrators](https://www.zurn.com/us/en/markets/higher-education.html) weren’t satisfied with after-the-fact water bill data or the option to install water meters in every building on campus. They wanted a deeper understanding of water usage to steer their water conservation initiative, and Zurn Elkay[smart restroom products](https://www.zurn.com/innovation-efficiency/smart-products) provided the solution.


Most of the water consumed originates from[toilets](https://www.zurn.com/products/finish-plumbing/fixtures/water-closets) ,[showers](https://www.zurn.com/products/finish-plumbing/showers) , baths,[faucets](https://www.zurn.com/products/finish-plumbing/sensor-faucets) , and leaks.


## Improve Water Conservation and Water Usage


Water conservation entails the efficient use of water to minimize unnecessary consumption. Its significance lies in the fact that clean, fresh water is both a finite and expensive resource. Furthermore, water conservation contributes to environmental preservation.
