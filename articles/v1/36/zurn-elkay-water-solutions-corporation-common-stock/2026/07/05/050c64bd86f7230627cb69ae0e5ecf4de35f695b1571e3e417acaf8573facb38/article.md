# Zurn Elkay Unveils Quartz Classic Workstation Sinks Kitchen Solutions

**MILWAUKEE, WI, September 16, 2024** – Zurn Elkay Water Solutions, the industry’s largest supplier of cleaner water solutions for drinking water, hygiene and sustainable water management, launched new Quartz Classic® Workstation sinks to offer even more functionality in your kitchen.


Elkay has the largest offering of quartz sinks on the market, with a configuration and color for every space and personality. Quartz Classic sinks offer timeless style and beauty; crafted from natural quartz and acrylic resins, they have the organic look and feel of authentic stone. These sinks come in a range of stunning hues, allowing you to match your sink to your décor. Offering timeless style and beauty, Quartz Classic sinks have been adding color to kitchens for more than a decade.


The new line of workstation sinks allows you to expand your counter space with stylish accessories. They include an acacia wood cutting board, colander, drying rack, roll-up drying rack, bottom grid and colormatching drain. They’re designed to fit perfectly in the sink and slide along the ledge for easy access, which frees up counter space and improves your kitchen routine.


Strength and durability make quartz a popular choice in sinks. Thanks to a proprietary manufacturing process that combines natural quartz with high-performance acrylic resin, Quartz Classic maintains its aesthetics while its nonporous surface stands up to daily wear and tear. Rounded, microfine quartz particles allow for an ultrasmooth, nonporous surface that’s easy to wash clean.


Zurn Elkay is committed to providing greater access to cleaner, healthier drinking water through quality products. To browse Elkay’s selection of Quartz sinks, visit[elkay.com/quartz-classic](https://www.elkay.com/us/en/sinks/collections/quartz/classic.html) .
