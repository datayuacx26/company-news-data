# Elkay Expands Pro Filtration™ Line with New Bottle Filler Only Configuration

*New model delivers same advanced filtration, smart connected capabilities and simplified maintenance of Elkay Pro Filtration™ in a streamlined bottle filler-only model*


**MILWAUKEE, June 30, 2026** —Expectations for commercial drinking water have evolved, with today’s facilities tasked with delivering cleaner, safer drinking water while also managing maintenance demands, supporting sustainability initiatives and maximizing operational efficiency.


To help meet these evolving needs, Elkay® is expanding its Elkay Pro Filtration™ line with a new bottle filler only configuration, bringing advanced filtration and smart connected capabilities to spaces where a drinking fountain isn’t required, providing greater flexibility without compromising performance.


“The launch of the bottle filler-only model extends the reach of the Pro Filtration line,” said Tanner Mascareñas, Zurn Elkay Product Marketing Manager – Drinking Water. “Facility owners and managers can now bring the same advanced water quality, simplified maintenance and smart connected capabilities of Pro Filtration to more locations throughout a building, delivering a consistent hydration experience where people need it.”


High-capacity filtration extends performance and reduces maintenance, with dual-filter configurations that allow filters to be stacked for increased capacity. Elkay Pro Filtration Ultra Capacity filters offer up to 20,000 gallons of capacity, while Total PFAS* filters provide up to 8,000 gallons.


Designed to simplify ownership, the unit features top-access filter changes in under 60 seconds. Automatic filter recognition and built-in smart monitoring provide visibility into filter status, usage trends and system performance. Automatic line flushing further supports proactive maintenance and water management programs.


Select models are also available with UV-C LED technology, which has been third-party verified to reduce up to 99.9% of Legionella pneumophila and up to 99.6% of E. coli at the point of dispense.**


Available in Midnight Black and Stainless Steel finishes, the Elkay Pro Filtration bottle filling station expands the Pro Filtration line with a flexible, new option for schools, healthcare facilities, airports, offices and other high-traffic spaces.


**Total PFAS as defined by NSF/ANSI 53*


***Tests performed under normal operating flow rate and temperature conditions, in ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratories, by personnel qualified in performing microbiological efficacy testing to established test standards.*
