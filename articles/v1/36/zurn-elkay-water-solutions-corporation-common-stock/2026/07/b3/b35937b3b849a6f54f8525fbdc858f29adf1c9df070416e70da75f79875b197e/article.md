# Elkay Releases New Stainless Steel Crosstown® Workstation Sinks

**MILWAUKEE, WI, March 3, 2025 –** Launching today, Elkay’s Crosstown Workstation sink is engineered for convenience, with accessories that help maximize valuable workspace in the kitchen and keep your countertops free of clutter. With this expansion of the workstation sink collection, Elkay continues to provide sinks that fit all kitchen styles.


The all-in-one kit includes a bottom grid, drain, roll-up drying rack and cutting board that slides back and forth along a built-in sink ledge. You can effortlessly go from meal prep to clean up; seamlessly transition from washing and chopping food to straining and even cleanup in seconds.


“While you’re doing most of your kitchen work at the sink, we want the sink to work for you as well,” says Kyle Gilbert, Product Marketing Manager – Sinks. “Our workstation sinks enhance the usability of your sink. The new Crosstown Workstation gives you the durability of high-gauge stainless steel with custom accessories so that you can maximize your workspace.”


Highest quality 18-gauge thickness and Type 304 stainless steel give lasting durability, performance and lustrous beauty. The sound-deadening pads minimize sound and vibration for a quieter time at the sink.


For a clean look, the ultrathin, flat rim virtually eliminates the barrier between the sink and counter. This sink can be installed as a drop-in or undermount. The extra-deep, single bowl offers uninterrupted space inside for stacking dishes, filling stockpots, and handling large baking sheets and roasters.


To learn more about Elkay’s Crosstown sinks, visit[www.elkay.com/crosstown](https://www.elkay.com/us/en/sinks/collections/crosstown.html)
