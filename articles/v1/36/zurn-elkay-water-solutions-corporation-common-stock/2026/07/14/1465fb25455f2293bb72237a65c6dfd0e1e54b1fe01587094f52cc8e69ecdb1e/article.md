# Zurn Elkay Releases Extensions to their Sink Catalogue

**MILWAUKEE, WI, January 16, 2025 –** Zurn Elkay Water Solutions, the industry’s largest supplier of cleaner water solutions for drinking water, hygiene, and sustainable water management, extended their sink catalogue to include new Elkay Quartz Luxe® and Circuit Chef® workstations.


From stainless steel workstation sinks to quartz and fireclay, Elkay has sinks that fit all kitchen styles. The new Quartz Luxe and Circuit Chef sinks provide even more options to match your design needs while continuing to maximize your workspace and keeping countertops free from clutter.


“Workstation sinks have been rising in popularity because they’re incredibly versatile,” says Kyle Gilbert, Zurn Elkay’s Product Marketing Manager – Sinks. “We’re excited to expand our options so that customers can find the perfect fit for their kitchen and style, without sacrificing the features that make these sinks so practical.”


The Circuit Chef workstation sink makes more room in the kitchen for the whole family. It’s engineered for superior form and function, with upgraded custom-fit accessories. Elevated aesthetics improve both form and function, with sleek handles and thicker, sturdier cutting boards. A roll-up drying rack is also included. Use accessories to expand counterspace and make more room for prepping, cooking, serving, entertaining and cleaning up. The contemporary styling stands out in any kitchen for a fresh look in a classic material; the 16-gauge stainless steel is durable for performance and beauty.


The Quartz Luxe workstation offers new features that make prep and cleanup even easier. It’s everything you want in a workstation, also including custom-fit accessories. You can expand your counter space and improve your kitchen routine. The exclusive SurePrep™ accessory ledge ensures the cutting board won’t budge while you’re using it, and keep clutter out of view with our exclusive ledges and shelves that hold cleaning tools, brushes and more. Quartz looks stunning in any kitchen, and it holds up to hot pans, dirty dishes and stains with no problem – heat safe to 535°F. It’s naturally sound-deadening and the ultrasmooth, nonporous surface resists food particles and washes clean early. Elkay has the largest offering of quartz sinks on the market, with select models crafted from approximately 99% natural, renewable, or recycled materials.


“Elkay is proud to continue to innovate and expand our sink offerings,” continues Gilbert. “With the addition of these new workstations and their upgraded accessories you can get the maximum usability with a minimal footprint.”
