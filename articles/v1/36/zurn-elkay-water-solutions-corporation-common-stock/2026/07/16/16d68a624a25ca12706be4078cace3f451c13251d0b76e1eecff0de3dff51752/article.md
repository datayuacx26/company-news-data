# Zurn Elkay Releases New Filtered Bottle Filling Stations

**MILWAUKEE, WI, January 6, 2025 –** Zurn Elkay Water Solutions, the industry’s largest supplier of cleaner water solutions for drinking water, hygiene and sustainable water management, has released an updated line of Elkay Filtered Bottle Filling Stations. The updated units combine a sleeker design with upgrades that lower long-term labor costs and simplify filter maintenance.


Since their release in 2010, Elkay’s legacy ezH20 Bottle Fillers have reduced lead, PFOA/PFOS, and other harmful contaminants to put safer, cleaner water within everyone’s reach. The newest iteration of the bottle filling station continues to do just that while giving clients a new, more modern design that delivers filtered drinking water in seconds.


With an innovative, design forward architecture, these new units provide an elevated look to enhance office spaces, gyms, and more. Newly available colors Midnight Black, Arctic White, and Standard Gray, compliment modern interiors for a seamless design.


The new line of Elkay filtered bottle filling stations delivers cleaner, safer drinking water using the most advanced filtration on the market. Elkay’s commercial-grade WaterSentry® filters boast the highest level of certification in the water delivery space, they reduce the most contaminants on the market — including lead, PFOA/PFOS, microplastics and more. With the 51600C HighCapacity Replacement Filter, the filter life is doubled to 6,000 gallons and is certified to NSF/ANSI 42, 53 and 401 for the reduction of lead, microplastics, cysts, Class 1 particulate and chlorine taste and odor. Elkay’s WaterSentry 71300C is also rated for the reduction of PFOA/PFOS, two prevalent PFAS chemicals, commonly known as “forever chemicals” for the highest-performing filtration option. The unrivaled line of filters lasts longer for fewer replacements to save time and money.


All units are also equipped with a drop-down wrapper, automatic filter light reset and intuitive LED filter status display for time-saving filter changes and minimized labor. The innovative dropdown wrapper results in quicker filter changes and less downtime, as maintenance and facilities staff can access the filter from the front and side of the cooler. Filters can be easily changed with a quick ¼ turn lock for instant alignment. The LED status lights help anticipate filter changes and automatically change back to blue, saving a step.


The units are connected-ready to offer fresh, filtered water and track usage across every unit, while reducing total cost of ownership. Clients can remotely track bottles saved, filter status and water usage, and schedule flushing events to keep lines cleaner. Ideal for large offices, airports, colleges and public facilities. Uses plumbSMART™ software powered by Zurn Elkay, which integrates with a variety of smart plumbing products.
