By installing it in a room other than the kitchen like a dining room, mudroom, guest bedroom, or bonus room, you give guests a stylish, convenient way to help themselves to fresh, filtered water without crowding your prep space or interrupting your hosting flow.


It’s a small change that makes a big difference, your guests stay refreshed, and you stay stress-free.

From the brand behind the bottle-filling stations found in schools and airports nationwide, Liv EZ brings commercial-grade water filtration home in a beautiful, minimalist design. And because it’s built into your wall, there’s no countertop clutter, no fridge filters to fuss with, and no single-use bottles piling up after your party.
