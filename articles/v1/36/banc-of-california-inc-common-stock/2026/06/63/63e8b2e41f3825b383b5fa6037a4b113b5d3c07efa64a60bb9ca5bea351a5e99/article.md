# BANC OF CALIFORNIA NAMED ONE OF AMERICA’S GREATEST WORKPLACES


**June 30, 2026**


Banc of California has been recognized by Newsweek as one of America’s Greatest Workplaces, a distinction that highlights the bank’s commitment to fostering positive workplace cultures, supporting colleagues and creating exceptional colleague experiences.


“We are honored to be recognized by Newsweek as one of America’s Greatest Workplaces,” said Jared Wolff, Chairman and CEO of Banc of California. “This recognition reflects the dedication, talent, and commitment of our team members, who help create a culture rooted in collaboration, excellence and service. We remain focused on providing an environment where our colleagues can grow, succeed and do their best work.”


Banc of California’s strong workplace culture is essential to delivering exceptional service to clients and supporting long-term success. The bank invests in programs that support its team members through professional development opportunities; engagement programs; and a comprehensive benefits package that includes health coverage, family-building and caregiving support, mental health resources, and wellness initiatives. The bank also fosters a culture of continuous improvement through Better Banc, its colleague feedback program, which empowers team members to share ideas that drive excellence and positively shape the company’s future.


“Our team members are the foundation of our success,” said Kim Tu, Chief People Officer of Banc of California. “Being recognized as one of America’s Greatest Workplaces underscores our commitment to creating an environment where people feel supported, empowered and inspired to make a difference. We continue to attract top talent nationwide.”


Other companies recognized include Apple, Adobe, Google, American Express and Starbucks. Among banks, only 30 organizations nationwide earned this distinction.

## The Next Chapter


In seven years as the Brentwood-based regional bank’s chair, president and chief executive, Jared Wolff, 57, has seen it all. He guided the bank – which provides banking and treasury management services to small, middle-market and venture-backed businesses – through the Covid-19 pandemic, the 2023 banking crisis and a merger with PacWest Bancorp the same year. Today, regional economic pressures, a high-interest rate environment and steep competition for deposits throw curveballs at regional lenders’ growth strategies and profitability. But years of experience leading L.A.’s top banks prepared Wolff for these headwinds, he said.
