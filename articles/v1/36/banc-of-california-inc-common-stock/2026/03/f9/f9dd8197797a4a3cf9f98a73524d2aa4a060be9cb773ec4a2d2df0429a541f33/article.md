# Banc of California: Business Angel of the Year Honoree


**March 30, 2026**


## The bank was recognized for its support of small businesses following the January 2025 wildfires in Los Angeles.


Banc of California, the largest independent bank based in Los Angeles, donated $1 million from its Wildfire Relief & Recovery fund to launch the Small Business Initiative with Steadfast LA, a nonprofit working to rebuild the region’s economy after the devastating Eaton and Palisades fires of January 2025.


The gift was used to provide direct grants of up to $50,000 to independent businesses— including retailers, restaurants, pharmacies, dentists and veterinary centers operating in Altadena, Malibu and the Pacific Palisades — areas that were hit hardest by the wildfires.


The Los Angeles Business Journal’s Business Angel of the Year award recognizes a for-profit organization for its ongoing support of one specific nonprofit or general cause. This includes volunteer time, fundraising and overall support.
