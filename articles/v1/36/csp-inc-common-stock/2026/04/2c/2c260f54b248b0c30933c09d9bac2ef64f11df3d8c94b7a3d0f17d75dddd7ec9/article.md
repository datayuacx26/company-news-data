# The Iranian Conflict Leads to the Latest Attack on OT Production April 13, 2026

CISA and the FBI warned that Iranian-backed cyber attackers are targeting Rockwell LOGIX® PLC deployments in Government, Energy and Water/Wastewater as well as other industries first back on March 20th 2026.


CISA further investigated the attacks and provided a more detailed assessment on April 7, 2026.


Subsequently ARIA has provided its own Bulletin on April 13, 2026, providing recommendations on how to prevent future similar attempted attacks from doing harm based on Rockwell’s initial assessment combined with its own.


See the attached bulletin on how to minimize the harm done by this type of attack.
