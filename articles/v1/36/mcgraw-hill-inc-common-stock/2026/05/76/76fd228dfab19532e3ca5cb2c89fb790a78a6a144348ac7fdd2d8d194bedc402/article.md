# McGraw Hill Announces Participation in Upcoming Investor Conferences

### McGraw Hill Announces Participation in Upcoming Investor Conferences


May 6, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/964897494/files/doc_news/McGraw-Hill-Announces-Participation-in-Upcoming-Investor-Conferences-2026.pdf)


COLUMBUS, Ohio--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


McGraw Hill, Inc. (NYSE: MH; “McGraw Hill” and the “Company”), a leading global provider of education solutions for preK-12, higher education and professional learning, today announced that it will participate in the following conferences:


**Morgan Stanley Business Services One-on-One Day** : May 11, 2026 – The Company’s Investor Relations team will participate in investor meetings.


**21st Annual Needham Technology, Media, & Consumer Conference** : May 14, 2026 – Philip Moyer, McGraw Hill’s President and CEO, will participate in a fireside chat. The presentation is scheduled for 12:45 pm ET.


**J.P. Morgan 2026 Global Technology, Media, and Communications Conference** : May 18, 2026, Boston, MA – Philip Moyer, McGraw Hill’s President and CEO, will participate in a fireside chat and in investor meetings. The presentation is scheduled for 9:05 am ET.


The presentations will be webcast, with access instructions available on[https://investors.mheducation.com/overview/default.aspx](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.mheducation.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54529515&newsitemid=20260506196147&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.mheducation.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&index=1&md5=463559873109de787c61715ce79800c7) . The webcasts will be archived on the site for 90 days.
