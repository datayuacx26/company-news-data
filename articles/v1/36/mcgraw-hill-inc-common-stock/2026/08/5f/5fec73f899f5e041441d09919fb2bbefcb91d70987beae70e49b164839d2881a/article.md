### McGraw Hill, Inc. to Participate in an Investor Call Hosted by Goldman Sachs


August 17, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/964897494/files/doc_news/McGraw-Hill-Inc--to-Participate-in-an-Investor-Call-Hosted-by-Goldman-Sachs-5841f12c0-2026.pdf)


COLUMBUS, Ohio--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[McGraw Hill](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mheducation.com%2F&esheet=54589542&newsitemid=20260817778614&lan=en-US&anchor=McGraw+Hill&index=1&md5=85bd48309f83b53db48dbf34bed1c122) , Inc. (NYSE: MH) today announced that Philip Moyer, President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors, will participate in a call hosted by George Tong, an equity research analyst with Goldman Sachs. The call will take place on Thursday, August 20, 2026, at 1:30 PM ET.


The discussion will focus on AI as a growth vector for McGraw Hill and the company’s leadership role in agentic technology. For registration, please contact your Goldman Sachs representative orgeorge.tong@gs.com .


**Call Details**


**Date:** Thursday, August 20, 2026
**Time:** 1:30 PM ET
**Location:** Virtual
**Participants:**


- McGraw Hill, Inc. – Philip Moyer, President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors
- Goldman Sachs – George Tong, Equity Research Analyst


**Sign up Details:**


- For registration, please contact your Goldman Sachs representative orgeorge.tong@gs.com .


**McGraw Hill**


McGraw Hill (NYSE: MH) is a leading global provider of education solutions for preK-12, higher education and professional learning, supporting the evolving needs of millions of educators and students around the world. We provide trusted, high-quality content and personalized learning experiences that use data, technology and learning science to help students progress towards their goals. Through our commitment to fostering a culture of innovation and belonging, we are dedicated to improving outcomes and access to education for all. We have over 30 offices across North America, Asia, Australia, Europe, the Middle East and South America, and make our learning solutions available in more than 80 languages.
