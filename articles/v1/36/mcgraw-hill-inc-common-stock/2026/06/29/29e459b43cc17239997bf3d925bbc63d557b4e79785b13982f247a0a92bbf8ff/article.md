### McGraw Hill, Inc. to Participate in an Investor Call Hosted by Goldman Sachs


June 9, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/964897494/files/doc_news/McGraw-Hill-Inc--to-Participate-in-an-Investor-Call-Hosted-by-Goldman-Sachs-2026.pdf)


COLUMBUS, Ohio--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[McGraw Hill](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mheducation.com%2F&esheet=54550159&newsitemid=20260609384528&lan=en-US&anchor=McGraw+Hill&index=1&md5=e0b97c68dca4aaf090eb600b4354db80) , Inc. (NYSE: MH) today announced that Philip Moyer, President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors, will participate in a call hosted by George Tong, an equity research analyst with Goldman Sachs. The call will take place on Friday, June 12, 2026, at 2:00 PM ET.


The discussion will focus on McGraw Hill in the context of Goldman Sachs’ AI Resilience framework in the Information Services sector. To register for the call, please contact your Goldman Sachs representative orchristina.cikowski@gs.com *.*


**Call Details**


**Date:** Friday, June 12th, 2026
**Time:** 2:00 PM ET
**Location:** Virtual
**Participants:**


- McGraw Hill, Inc. – Philip Moyer, President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors
- Goldman Sachs – George Tong, Equity Research Analyst


**Sign up Details:**


- For registration, please contact your Goldman Sachs representative orchristina.cikowski@gs.com


**McGraw Hill**

We provide trusted, high-quality content and personalized learning experiences that use data, technology and learning science to help students progress towards their goals. Through our commitment to fostering a culture of innovation and belonging, we are dedicated to improving outcomes and access to education for all.
