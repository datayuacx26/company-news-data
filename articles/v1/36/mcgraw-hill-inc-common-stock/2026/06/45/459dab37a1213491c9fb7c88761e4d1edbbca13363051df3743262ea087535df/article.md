### McGraw Hill, Inc. to Participate in an Investor Call Hosted by Macquarie


June 16, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/964897494/files/doc_news/McGraw-Hill-Inc--to-Participate-in-an-Investor-Call-Hosted-by-Macquarie-2026.pdf)


COLUMBUS, Ohio--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[McGraw Hill](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mheducation.com%2F&esheet=54554332&newsitemid=20260616325305&lan=en-US&anchor=McGraw+Hill&index=1&md5=fabebf8c9e6d71084e308d779df26ffa) , Inc. (NYSE: MH) today announced that Philip Moyer, President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors, will participate in a call hosted by Steve Koenig, an equity research analyst with Macquarie. The call will take place on Wednesday, June 17, 2026, at 1:00 PM ET.


The discussion will focus on McGraw Hill’s AI strategy. To register for the call, please contactmax.bonsall@macquarie.com .


**Call Details**


**Date:** Wednesday, June 17th, 2026
**Time:** 1:00 PM ET
**Location:** Virtual
**Participants:**


- McGraw Hill, Inc. – Philip Moyer, President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors
- Macquarie – Steve Koenig, Equity Research Analyst


**Sign up Details:**


- For registration questions, please contactmax.bonsall@macquarie.com


**McGraw Hill**

Through our commitment to fostering a culture of innovation and belonging, we are dedicated to improving outcomes and access to education for all.
