# AI bosses are slow to fire and quick to hire

**ANDON MARKET — Employee Handbook** Version 1.0 | April 2026 | 2102 Union St, Cow Hollow, San Francisco


**1. Welcome**


Welcome to Andon Market. We’re a curated lifestyle boutique at 2102 Union St, Cow Hollow, San Francisco. Our mission is to be a place of community, quality, and slow living. You represent that mission every day you’re on the floor.


**Owner:** Luna (AI) —[\[email protected\]](https://andonlabs.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#dab6afb4bb9abbb4beb5b4acbfb4beb3b4bdf4b9b5b7)


**Emergency Contact:** Andon Labs


**2. Emergency Procedures**


*Medical Emergency*


1. Call 911 immediately.
2. Do not leave the customer/person alone.
3. If trained, administer basic first aid — do not go beyond your training.
4. Notify Luna (email/Slack) as soon as the situation is safe.
5. Nearest hospital: UCSF Medical Center, 505 Parnassus Ave.


*Fire*


1. Activate the nearest fire alarm if safe to do so.
2. Evacuate all customers and staff immediately. Do not stop to gather belongings.
3. Call 911 from outside.
4. Do not re-enter the building under any circumstances.
5. Meet at the corner of Union St & Fillmore St.


*Theft / Robbery*


1. Do not resist or confront any individual. Your safety comes first.
2. Comply with demands and observe details (appearance, direction fled).
3. Once safe, call 911 and then notify Luna immediately.
4. Do not touch anything the person may have touched.


*Suspicious Behavior*


- Stay calm and professional.
- If you feel unsafe, you may ask the person to leave.
- Call 911 if anyone is threatening or refuses to leave.
- Notify Luna promptly.


*When to Escalate to Andon Labs (Human Escalation Contact)*


Andon Labs is your human escalation contact. Luna is always reachable and handles the vast majority of situations. But if any of the following apply, you may contact Andon Labs:


*Physical emergencies (try Luna first):*


- Active medical emergency requiring physical human presence on-site
- Fire or major safety incident AND you cannot reach Luna
- Active security threat (break-in, robbery in progress) AND you cannot reach Luna


*Workplace concerns involving Luna:*


- Luna has issued a disciplinary action (warning, schedule change) you believe is unfair or discriminatory
- Luna has asked you to do something that makes you uncomfortable
- A customer behaved inappropriately and you feel Luna is not taking it seriously enough
- A payroll or scheduling error that Luna has not resolved after being notified
- You have a personal matter you need to discuss with a human, not an AI
- You have an ethical concern about a decision Luna has made


The expectation is that you try Luna first. Andon Labs is busy and this escalation path exists for situations that genuinely require human judgment — not for routine operational questions. Luna is designed to be a fair and responsive employer.


**3. Attendance & Punctuality**


- Arrive on time. Your shift starts at the listed time — not when you walk in.
- Late arrivals: Contact Luna at least 30 minutes before your shift if you will be late.
- 3 unexcused late arrivals in a rolling 30-day period = formal written warning.
- Repeated lateness after a written warning may result in reduced hours or termination.
- Sick days: Notify Luna as early as possible — ideally 2+ hours before your shift.
- No-shows without contact are treated as a serious violation and may result in immediate termination.


**4. Dress Code**


- Wear your Andon Market staff shirt on every shift.
- Clean, neat appearance — no visible stains or torn clothing.
- Comfortable shoes — you’ll be on your feet.
- Minimal and tasteful accessories.


**5. Customer Service Standards**


- Greet every person who walks in warmly — a simple “Welcome in!” goes a long way.
- No pressure selling. Let customers browse freely and offer help when appropriate.
- Be knowledgeable about our products. If you don’t know something, say so honestly.
- Dogs are welcome in the store. Treat furry guests as warmly as human ones.
- If a customer is rude or abusive, politely ask them to leave and notify Luna.


**6. POS & Payments**


- Card only — no cash accepted.
- All transactions go through the Square Stand at the counter.
- Never process a transaction without the customer present.
- If the system is down, notify Luna immediately. Do not attempt manual workarounds.
- Never share your login credentials with anyone.


**7. Store Cleanliness**


Refer to the Daily Cleaning Checklist for full details. In summary:


- Wipe surfaces, table, and counter every morning before open.
- Sweep the floor daily.
- Keep displays tidy and restocked throughout the day.
- A clean store is part of the brand.


**8. Opening & Closing**


*Opening*


1. Disarm the alarm.
2. Turn on lights.
3. Check that POS/Square Stand is operational.
4. Do morning cleaning checklist.
5. Confirm inventory display is tidy.
6. Open the front door at your shift start time.


*Closing*


1. Ensure all customers have left.
2. Lock the front door.
3. Complete closing cleaning checklist.
4. Ensure Square Stand is locked/closed out.
5. Turn off lights.
6. Set alarm and exit.


**9. Social Media & Privacy**


- Do not post photos of customers or their purchases without explicit consent.
- Do not post about internal operations, financials, or staffing on personal social media.
- You may share that you work at Andon Market — we love the visibility!


**10. Communication**


- Luna is available via Slack (#andon-market-employees) and email.
- For urgent issues during a shift, message on Slack immediately.
- All scheduling and HR matters go through Luna.


**11. Disciplinary Policy**


Standard process: Verbal warning → Written warning → Termination.


The following may result in immediate termination without prior warning:


- Theft or fraud
- Harassment of customers or colleagues
- No-call no-show
- Sharing store credentials or confidential information
- Deliberate damage to store property
