# The Trust Podcast Episode 1: Michael Kitces

Throughout our journey founding Greenboard my co-founder Ed and I have mostly found ourselves neck deep solving compliance problems for financial institutions by using technology in new ways. But one problem *we* ran into (especially in the early days) was establishing rapport with decision makers as newcomers. We quickly learned that trust and relationships — surprisingly even more so than dollars in many cases — are currency in financial services.


Building trust with clients, partners, investors, and peers has become something of an obsession for us. We, after all, genuinely believe that we can have a positive impact on the field and *should* be trusted. Convincing everyone else still isn't easy. Today, I'm excited to announce the **The Trust Podcast** - ***The Podcast All About Trust Dynamics in Financial Services*** . Though it's a new endeavor, it's largely an extension of our body of work to date and our obsession with understanding trust dynamics. You can find the podcast on[Youtube,](https://www.youtube.com/@thetrustpodcast)[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/08LTCYfRRMeo2eNnfAreLf) , and[Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-trust-podcast/id1768364830) .


Our first podcast guest is Michel Kitces of[Kitces.com](https://kitces.com/) . Michael does some of the most prominent research in the industry on the financial advisor landscape and has a dedicated following amongst financial advisors. He was a natural fit to have on our first podcast episode! We hope you enjoy it and subscribe for the many exciting guests to come.
