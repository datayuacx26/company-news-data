# Gloo to Present at 38th Annual ROTH Conference

BOULDER, Colo.--([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/) )--Gloo (Nasdaq: GLOO), a leading technology platform serving the faith and flourishing ecosystem, announced the company will participate in the upcoming 38th Annual ROTH Conference in Dana Point, California.


Gloo CEO Scott Beck and executive chair and head of technology Pat Gelsinger will participate in a fireside chat at 9:00 a.m. Pacific Time on Tuesday, March 24, 2026, and host individual and small group meetings throughout the day.


A live webcast and replay of the fireside chat will be available at[gloo.com/investors/events](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fgloo.com%2Finvestors%2Fevents&esheet=54451741&newsitemid=20260317663941&lan=en-US&anchor=gloo.com%2Finvestors%2Fevents&index=1&md5=1aebc4b709ceab834ece49b0f36f75ac) .
