# Fortune Brands Appoints David Barry Interim CEO

Fortune Brands Innovations announced that[David Barry](https://www.fbin.com/leadership/david-barry/) has been appointed Interim CEO.


“I am honored and excited to step into the role of Interim CEO. Fortune Brands has the right foundation, including exceptional brands and outstanding talent. Now, we will sharpen our focus on disciplined execution, further advance our strategic priorities, and support our teams as we move into the next phase of performance and growth. I’m confident we will continue to deliver for our customers, create value for shareholders, and provide our associates with meaningful opportunities. I am excited to lead this company and to help unlock the full potential of Fortune Brands ,” David Barry, Interim CEO.


For more information, view[Fortune Brands’ March 16th press release announcing governance and leadership updates.](https://ir.fbin.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fortune-brands-announces-governance-and-leadership-updates)
