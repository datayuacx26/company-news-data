# Paperwork Slowing You Down?


Learn How to Streamline and Automate in Bullhorn


Equipping your recruiters with the right technology is one of the best ways to position yourself ahead of your competitors. 70% of candidates want human interactions during the job search. By digitising your processes, you can empower your consultants to provide the best experience, from which you’ll reap the benefits.One of the most time-consuming processes that recruitment companies face? Paperwork! Reducing the time your consultants spend on paperwork gives them more time to focus on building relationships and ultimately winning more business. Join Bullhorn specialists[Raymond Pennie](https://www.candidately.com/speakers/raymond-pennie) and[Martin Wishart](https://www.candidately.com/speakers/martin-wishart) along with some special guests as they dig deep into how the most forward-thinking recruitment companies are automating their candidate and client documentation within their CRM.
