# How We Developed the Ultimate AI-Powered Resume Builder with Our Clients.

## TL;DR


Most staffing agencies spend 15–20 minutes reformatting each resume. Candidately co-developed an AI-powered resume builder with 10 pilot clients that reduces formatting time by 80%, ensures brand consistency, and accelerates candidate submissions.


## 68% of staffing agencies reformat resumes before creating a client submission


After conducting the research for our[Client Experience Report 2024](https://www.candidately.com/client-experience-report) , we discovered that 68% of staffing agencies reformat resumes before submission, with each resume taking an average of 15 to 20 minutes to manually adjust. This time-consuming task not only slows down the recruitment process but also impacts consistency across candidate profiles. With these findings in mind, we designed theAI Resume Builder to automate the entire formatting process, ensuring uniformity while dramatically reducing the time spent on each resume. By eliminating manual reformatting, staffing teams can now focus on what truly matters — placing top talent faster and more efficiently.


## The Process: Development in collaboration with clients


Over the past few months, we set out to solve one of the biggest pain points staffing agencies face: time-consuming and inconsistent resume formatting. Partnering with 10 of our most engaged clients, we worked together to co-create a tool that truly addresses these challenges. The result? The AI-Powered Resume Builder — seamlessly[integrated with Bullhorn](https://www.candidately.com/integrations/bullhorn)

Our mission was clear: to create a tool that simplifies the resume formatting process, boosts efficiency, and empowers staffing teams to focus on what matters most — placing top talent. Here's how we tackled the challenge and what we achieved together.


## The Problem: Time-Consuming, Inconsistent Resume Formatting


Staffing agencies often struggle with manual resume formatting, which is slow, error-prone, and inconsistent. These inefficiencies lead to wasted resources and decreased productivity, making it difficult to maintain professionalism across all candidate submissions. Many agencies turn to external teams, increasing costs and raising data security concerns.


Recognizing the need for a more efficient solution, we saw an opportunity to transform the resume formatting process, helping agencies save time, improve quality, and reduce costs.


## The Solution: Co-Creating the AI Resume Builder


Rather than building the solution in isolation, we involved our pilot clients in every step of the development process. Through remote workshops and feedback sessions, we worked closely with 10 pilot customers to identify their biggest pain points. We began by hosting in-depth discussions to fully understand their challenges. From there, we moved into ideation and prototyping, where we developed early versions of the[AI Resume Builder](https://www.candidately.com/product/ai-resume-builder)


. Using an agile approach, we iterated on the tool based on real-time feedback, ensuring it solved the practical problems agencies faced. This collaborative process helped us design an AI-powered tool that automates resume formatting, integrates seamlessly with Bullhorn, and provides customizable templates to meet specific client needs — all while maintaining agency branding.
