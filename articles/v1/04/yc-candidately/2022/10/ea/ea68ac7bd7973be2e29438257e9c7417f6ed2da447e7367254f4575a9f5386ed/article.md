# eTeki.com & Candidately join forces to empower staffing companies to seamlessly submit top tech candidates

World class staffing technology companies eTeki and Candidately are partnering to enable staffing teams to evaluate and submit top tech candidates faster and easier than ever before.


The staffing industry continues to be highly competitive, particularly in the red hot tech sector. According to a recent SIA Bullhorn Staffing Indicator report, professional staffing hours have remained near or above 10% since December 2020. Demand for qualified tech workers remains strong.[IT staffing](https://www.candidately.com/glossary/it-staffing)


firms looking for an edge to shortlist and submit candidates. With the partnership staffing firms can use eTeki & Candidately in their submission process and present talent in a more attractive and complete way.


“We are thrilled to partner with Candidately to jointly enable staffing companies to identify and raise top candidates. Top talent who may have been lost in the shuffle of the hectic and competitive process. With Candidately submissions supported by eTeki scorecards and videos, agencies will now be able to provide an even better ROI and client experience” says Hans Bukow, eTeki Cofounder & CEO.


In today’s war on talent, the partnership will allow talent managers to have everything presented smooth in one place to get a complete picture of the candidate, with all data used for decision making from one screen. These two solutions together uniquely provide a more attractive and complete understanding of a professional’s abilities and persona.


“Candidately continues to transform how candidate submissions are done, and our partnership with eTeki’s[Technical Interview](https://www.candidately.com/glossary/technical-interview)


Marketplace further strengthens the capabilities we provide to IT staffing companies. eTeki shares our focus on providing a candidate-first, client-friendly experience” said Jan. Jedlinski CEO & Cofounder of Candidately.


Staffing companies need reliable, scalable, and streamlined ways to evaluate and submit top tech talent. eTeki has been on the forefront of how staffing firms present top candidates to their clients through innovative interviewing-as-a-service for several years. Candidately has created a top quality customer experience for staffing companies to manage their candidate submittals.
