# Terefic & Candidately partner to enable staffing firms to submit reference checks at Stage 1 of the process

We are very announcing a partnership between Terefic & Candidately to help staffing firms become the #1 Trusted Partner to their clients.


The current "bait & switch", "job farmer", and "fake resume" scams are the worst and most toxic we have ever seen in history. Being able to detect fraud effectively is a HUGE differentiator in this industry.


Terefic vets thousands of candidates for staffing firms every month. Its unique fraud detection toolbox allows you to start detecting fraud as soon as you have someone’s name, email, and/or phone number.


Numbers say it all. About 8-10% of IT candidates are fake. Sending over documented proofs that your candidates are skilled, honest, and real to the[hiring manager](https://www.candidately.com/glossary/hiring-manager)


early in the process increases the chances of your candidates landing an interview anywhere between 40%-100% depending on the job opening.


Candidately’s mission to simplify and improve the submission process aligns very well with Terefic’s goal to streamline the screening process and help you place more candidates.


The partnership allows our mutual clients to include Terefic’s digital ID verifications and reference checks easily on your Candidately candidate submission profile.
