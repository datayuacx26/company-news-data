# Uranium Royalty: Royalties at Cameco Restarting Production - Demand Pressure on Uranium Price Ahead

## Uranium Royalty: Royalties at Cameco Restarting Production - Demand Pressure on Uranium Price Ahead


November 8, 2022


Interview with President & CEO Scott Melbye at the 2022 Precious Metals Show in Munich. Uranium Royalty now owns 15 royalties, two of which are currently ramping up - Cameco's MacArthur River and Cigar Lake mines. Other royalties are in the development stage and are expected to come on stream in the second half of the decade. The CEO also offers insight into the uranium market, saying, "In the last 38 years, I have never been more bullish than today."
