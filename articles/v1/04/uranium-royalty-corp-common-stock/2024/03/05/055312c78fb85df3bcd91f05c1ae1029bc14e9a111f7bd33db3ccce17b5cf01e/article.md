# Uranium Royalty: Diversified Portfolio of Uranium Royalties and More to Come in 2024

## Uranium Royalty: Diversified Portfolio of Uranium Royalties and More to Come in 2024


Mar 6, 2024


An interview with URC CEO Scott Melbye with Swiss Resource Capital AG at PDAC 2024 in Toronto. Uranium Royalty is the only royalty company in the uranium sector and has a very broad portfolio and a lot of experience and contacts in the commodity segment. The portfolio is to be significantly expanded in 2024, the company can rely on the sale of physical uranium for financing, of which it holds 2.5 million pounds. Uranium Royalty is therefore ideally positioned for the current and ongoing bull market.
