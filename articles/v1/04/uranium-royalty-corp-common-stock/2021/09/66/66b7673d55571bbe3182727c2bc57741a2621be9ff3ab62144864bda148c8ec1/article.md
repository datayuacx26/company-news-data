# Uranium Royalty: The Right Model for an Outbreaking Uranium Market

## Uranium Royalty: The Right Model for an Outbreaking Uranium Market


September 20, 2021


An interview with President & CEO Scott Melbye with Swiss Resource Capital. The breakout of the uranium price above $50 drove up all uranium stocks, particularly benefiting Uranium Royalty, which can also acquire physical uranium through its strategic partnership with Yellow Cake. Uranium Royalty already owns several royalty deals, particularly in the world's best uranium district, the Athabasca Basin. This puts the company in an excellent position for a further uranium price increase, which is only a matter of time.
