# Healthy Popsicle Recipe: Mango BCAA

These BCAA pops are great after a workout, outdoor hike, or for a summer refresher!


### Ingredients:


- 1 Scoop


[PMD Peach Mango BCAA Charged](https://fitlifebrands.com/products/bcaa-charged/?sku=40733)
- 1/2 Cup Frozen Mango
- 1/2 Cup Frozen Strawberries
- 1/2 Cup Water


### Directions:


1. Combine all ingredients in a blender.
2. Pour into popsicle container and place in your freezer.
3. Wait for the popsicles to freeze
4. Enjoy!
