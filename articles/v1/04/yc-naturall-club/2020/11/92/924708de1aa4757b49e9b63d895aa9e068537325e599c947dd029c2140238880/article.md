# NaturAll's Subscription Program

Hey NaturAllistas! We’re back to answer another customer question. This week, we’re going to dive into NaturAll’s Subscription Program. At NaturAll, we stand by the belief that consistency is key. In order to see real results with our products - from adding more moisture to your hair with our Deep Conditioners to increasing growth with our Jamaican Black Castor Oil Growth Serums - it is important to develop a routine for your hair and stick with it!


NaturAll wants to make it easy for you to develop this routine for yourself. We never want you to worry about running low on products or forgetting to purchase more. That’s why we created our Subscription Program!


Not only does our Subscription Program enable you to get all your favorite NaturAll products over and over on a recurring basis, but there are also several perks that come with signing up. But let’s start with the basics - how do you sign up for a subscription?


## Signing up for a subscription


On each product page, you have the option to add the product to your cart as a one-time purchase option or as a subscription option. In the picture below, you can see the two options above the “Add to Cart” button. Simply click on the “Subscribe and Save” option and select the recurring frequency you prefer in the dropdown box directly below. It’s that simple! From that point onwards, NaturAll will make sure that the product you’ve subscribed to is shipped to your address every 1, 2, or 3 months based on the subscription period you selected.


But what’s the difference between setting up a subscription and simply re-ordering the product? The perks of course! When you subscribe to an individual product at NaturAll you save 10%, and you save 25% on bundles. Not to mention that every time the order gets shipped to you, we won’t charge you for shipping. We want to make sure that you are getting the products you love on time and often so that you can continue to care for your hair and give it all the natural, organic ingredients it needs and deserves.


## Subscriber Benefits


In addition to discounts on our available products and free shipping, we want to reward you for being a loyal subscriber. Since we know getting into the habit of maintaining a routine can be hard, we want to do everything to incentivize you to keep that subscription active and keep those products coming so that your hair always has more than enough of the freshest formulations! The longer you stay subscribed, the more perks you are able to earn! Here is a breakdown of the rewards you can get for specific renewals:


- **2nd Renewal** - 15% off your next renewal


- **3rd Renewal** - Eligibility to receive free products in your subscription box!


- **4th Renewal** - Receive a free product with your next renewal


- **6th Renewal** - Earn a NaturAll T-Shirt in your size in your next renewal order


- **9th Renewal** - Receive another free product with your next renewal


- **12th Renewal** - Get 50% off your next delivery and be able to have a call with our CEO, Muhga Eltigani!
