**Flaking**


Flaking is very normal and happens to almost everyone at least once in their lifetime.


Flaking comes from your scalp when it lacks moisture. Dry scalp can mean you’re not moisturizing your scalp enough or the cold and dry weather is irritating your skin cells to dry and shed faster than usual.


Dandruff is a bit different; it’s a condition caused by seborrheic dermatitis, a condition that leaves skin oily, red and scaly. It is caused by the fungus malassezia and it can be found anywhere on your body that has oil glands.
