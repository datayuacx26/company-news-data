# 🍂 Fall in Love with Your Curls: Autumn Curl Care Tips

Fall is finally here, the crisp air, colorful leaves, cozy sweaters, and of course… pumpkin spice everything! But let’s be real: while you’re enjoying the season, your curls might not be. The shift in weather can leave curls dry, frizzy, and begging for extra love. Don’t worry, we’ve got you covered. Here’s how to keep your curls happy, hydrated, and gorgeous all season long.


**1. Sip Your Latte, But Feed Your Curls Too**


Cooler air = drier curls. Give your hair the moisture it craves with a


**deep conditioner** . Try our


**Hydrating Conditioner p** acked with avocado and natural oils, it gives your curls that silky, bouncy feel even on the chilliest fall days.


**2. Cozy Up with a Leave-In**


A good leave-in is like your favorite fall sweater comforting, protective, and always reliable. Our


**Hydrating Leave-In Conditioner** locks in moisture, keeps frizz under control, and makes sure your curls stay soft when the weather gets crisp.


**3. Seal the Deal with Oils & Creams**


Fall weather can be sneaky, it steals hydration before you know it! Add a few drops of our


**Hydrating Oil Blend** after styling to seal in moisture. Pair it with the


**Twisting Paste** for definition that lasts all day (even on windy pumpkin-patch adventures 🍁.


**4. Protect Your Curls from Sweater Weather**


Yes, scarves and sweaters are cute but the friction? Not so much. Try protective styles (like twists or braids) and refresh with our


**Fresh Frosé Treatment** to keep curls hydrated and frizz at bay.


**5. Reset with a Trim**


After a summer of sun, swimming, and fun, your curls deserve a reset. Snip away split ends and let your curls start fresh this fall. Add in our **Hydrating**


**Shampoo + Conditioner duo** on wash days to keep your scalp happy and curls clean without stripping.
