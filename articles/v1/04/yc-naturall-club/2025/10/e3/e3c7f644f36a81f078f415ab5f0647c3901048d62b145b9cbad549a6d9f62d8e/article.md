# Curls & Costumes: What Your Halloween Look Says About Your Curl Personality

This Halloween, your curls might just reveal more about you than your costume! Whether you’re rocking a sleek updo, bold twist-out, or carefree fro, your style tells a spooky-good story. Let’s find out what your Halloween look says about your curl personality.


**The Glam Goddess (Defined Twist-Out Queen)**
Your curls are as put-together as your costume — and that says *boss energy* . You planned this look two weeks ago, and you’ve got your **Fresh Frosé Treatment** and **Moisturizer** ready for photo-ready definition.


*Curl personality:* You’re the trendsetter. When you walk into a room, everyone asks what products you’re using.


**The 70s Disco Diva (Big, Bold, and Unapologetic)**
If your Halloween costume comes with a pick and a whole lot of volume, you’re serving *main character energy* . You don’t wait for the spotlight — you *are* the spotlight.


*Curl personality:* Fearless, fun, and fabulous. You know the bigger the hair, the bigger the confidence.
Pro tip: Use **Hydrating Oil Blend** to keep your curls shiny under the disco lights!


**The Nature Fairy (Soft Curls & Floral Vibes)**
You’re gentle, artistic, and probably the one who organized the Halloween movie night. Your curls are soft, hydrated, and full of life — just like you.


*Curl personality:* Peaceful but powerful. You keep your curls glowing with **Avocado Kiwi Leave-In** and a sprinkle of self-love.


**The Edgy Rebel (Slick Bun or Braided Look)**
You’re the one who makes “scary” look chic. Sleek styles, bold makeup, and just enough edge to turn heads.


*Curl personality:* Confident and creative. You play by your own rules and keep your hair flawless with **Edge Control** — no flyaway allowed.


**The Curly Witch (Defined Curls & Dark Glam)**
You’ve got curl magic that can’t be matched. Your twist-out is *bewitching* , and you never skip your deep condition day.


*Curl personality:* Mysterious, wise, and stunning. You’ve mastered the art of curl spells — and it shows.


**Outro:**
No matter your Halloween vibe, your curls deserve to shine brighter than the full moon.
Keep them hydrated, styled, and protected — because even spooky season can’t dull your curl magic.
