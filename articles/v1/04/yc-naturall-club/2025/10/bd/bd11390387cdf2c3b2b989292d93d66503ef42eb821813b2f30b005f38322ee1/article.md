# Deep Hydration: NaturAll Products That Quench Your Hair’s Thirst

##


When your curls cry out for moisture, NaturAll has a lineup ready to answer. Below are top products from NaturAll Club that boost hydration, how they help, key ingredients, and tips to get the most from them.


### 1. **Hydrating Fresh Froze Treatment (Avocado + Kiwi)**


**What it is:**
A rich, *fresh-frozen deep conditioner* made to deliver maximum hydration and nourishment to dry or damaged hair. This mask is crafted with real, natural ingredients and kept frozen to preserve freshness and nutrient potency. Simply thaw before use for a salon-quality deep treatment at home.


**Hydration Benefits:**


- **Avocado:** Packed with healthy fats, amino acids, and proteins that deeply moisturize and strengthen each strand.
- **Kiwi:** A powerful source of vitamin C and antioxidants that help seal in moisture and add shine.
- **Aloe & Coconut Oil:** Provide slip, reduce frizz, and leave hair feeling soft, hydrated, and manageable.


**How to Use:**
After shampooing, divide your hair into sections and apply the treatment generously from root to tip. Cover with a shower cap, and for best results, apply gentle heat using a steamer or warm towel. Let it sit for **20–40 minutes** , then rinse thoroughly and style as usual.


**Why It Works:**
This treatment deeply penetrates the hair shaft, restoring hydration from within. The combination of natural oils and fatty acids helps *lock in water* inside the cuticle, improving elasticity, reducing breakage, and preventing dryness. Consistent use helps maintain soft, healthy, and moisturized curls with improved shine and bounce.

### ✨ Pro Tips for Better Hydration


- **Warm up your products:** On cooler days, warm the moisturizer in your hands for easier absorption.
- **Use a heat cap or steaming:** With the Fresh Froze or conditioner, gentle heat helps penetrate deeper.
- **Less is more:** With potent formulas, a little goes a long way, using too much can give a greasy feel.
- **Avoid over-washing:** Daily shampooing can strip hydration; aim for 1–2x per week depending on your hair.
- **Protect from heat & sun:** Use a scarf, hat, or protective style when outdoors, sun and heat dry curls fast.
