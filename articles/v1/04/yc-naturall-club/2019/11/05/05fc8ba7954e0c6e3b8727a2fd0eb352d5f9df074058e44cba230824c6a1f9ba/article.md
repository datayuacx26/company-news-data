## **Aloe + Avocado Deep Conditioner**


Our Aloe + Avocado Sensitive Scalp Solution Deep Conditioner is a treatment infused with 5 different Amazonian butters and oils that give delicate conditioning to seal in moisture and completely restore and repair dry, damaged hair.


The aloe vera allows for best-in-class detangling performance while also rejuvenating your strands, providing shine, and preventing breakage.


Did we mention that it features our new fruity scent? This aloe + avo deep conditioner is definitely the way to go!


It is luxuriously thick, yet lightweight and the result is long-lasting softness, elasticity, manageability, and shine - for hair that radiates health.


At NaturAll Club, we use ingredients from around the globe to give you the best quality products possible! Here are a few key ingredients in the Aloe + Avocado Deep Conditioner.


### **Amazonian Butters**


1.


Cupuacu Butter- great for sealing moisture in your hair and nourishes brittle and dry hair.


2.


Murumuru Butter- hydrates and restores dry and damaged hair.


3.


Tucuma Butter- strengthens hair fiber by protecting from breakage.


Also from the Amazon, can’t forget about the Babassu Oil which treats and moisturizes dry scalp. Topping it off, we used Pracaxi Oil which makes combing your wet or dry hair a lot easier.


### **African Butters**


From the Amazon we head over to Africa where we infused Shea and Mango Butter. Shea Butter repairs damaged hair and treats split ends and breakage. The Mango Butter nourishes your hair and scalp.


Lastly, we head over to Australia for Tea Tree Oil, which is great for treating dry scalp.
