**Why Flax?**


- **ELASTICITY.** Flaxseed is one of the best natural sources of omega-3 fatty acids. These compounds **moisturize** **and protect hair** . They improve elasticity and prevent breakage.
- **HEALTHY SCALP.** Omega-3s also help soothe and nourish the scalp, and can help prevent scalp conditions like dandruff and itchy scalp.
- **REPAIR AND GROWTH.** Flax is rich in antioxidants, which repair damaged hair follicles, prevent tissue corrosion, and keep your follicles healthy- creating a hospitable environment for new hair growth.
- **STRENGTH** Did you know your hair is made out of protein? Without protein, hair becomes weak and brittle. Flax is an excellent source of protein, so it will help maintain strong natural hair.
- **HOLD AND DEFINITION.** Our flaxseed gel defines and moisturizes curly and kinky hair without creating stiffness or crunchiness. It provides great hold and is flake free!
