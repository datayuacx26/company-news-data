# Environmental Impact of Hair Products

We all love the smooth feel of a brand new bottle of our favorite hair product. However, have you taken into consideration how these products can affect the planet?


Plastic is a substance that can withstand even the harshest conditions, and if it is not recycled correctly, it can have very harmful effects on the environment. It pollutes the earth, puts animals in danger, and causes soil to be less fertile which affects our entire ecosystem.


This is why it is so important for companies to ensure that the plastic that they are using is biodegradable or recyclable. Many hair companies overlook this opportunity to be more eco-friendly.
