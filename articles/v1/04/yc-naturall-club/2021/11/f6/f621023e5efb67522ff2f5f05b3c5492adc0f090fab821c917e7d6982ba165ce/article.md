# Those in Need of A Protein Boost


Since hair is made out of keratin, making sure you’re providing your hair with enough protein is essential in making sure it’s strong enough to resist breakage, split ends, and other physical damage. Getting an adequate amount of protein also allows the hair to retain moisture better, as weak hair has gaps in the structure, making it extremely easy for moisture to escape and evaporate from the strands.

[Fresh Frozé Treatment Deep Conditioners](https://naturallclub.com/collections/ice-cream-treatment-line) are made with fresh avocados and are the perfect mix of protein and moisture. In addition to being packed with protein and moisture, the deep conditioners also help to smoothen the hair strands, which helps to reduce frizz and allow the hair to remain moisturized for longer. With consistent use, the deep conditioners can help to grow, strengthen, and repair your curls.
