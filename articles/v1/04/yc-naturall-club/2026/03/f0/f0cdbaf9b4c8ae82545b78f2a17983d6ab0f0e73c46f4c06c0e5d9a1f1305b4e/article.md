# Curls & Chill: The Best Movies to Watch on Wash Day

#


*Turn your wash day into the ultimate self-care ritual.*


Wash day doesn’t have to feel like a chore. In fact, it can become one of the most relaxing parts of your week. Imagine this: freshly washed curls, cozy pajamas, a blanket, your favorite snack, and a feel-good movie playing in the background while your hair products do their magic.


Welcome to **Curls & Chill,** the perfect combination of curl care and cozy entertainment.


In this guide, we’ll share a **movie night watch list** plus a few NaturAll hair products that work perfectly during your wash day routine while you sit back and enjoy the show.


# Step 1: Set the Wash Day Mood


Before you hit play on your movie, create the perfect self-care atmosphere.


Here’s your **Curls & Chill checklist:**


✔ Cozy blanket or robe
✔ A warm drink (tea, coffee, or hot chocolate)
✔ Your favorite snacks
✔ Hair freshly washed and conditioned
✔ Curl-loving products ready to style


Now that you’re comfortable, it’s time to let your hair routine work while you relax.


# Step 2: Wash & Prep Your Curls


Start your routine with gentle cleansing and hydration.


A great wash day combo includes:


- NaturAll Hydrating Shampoo


- NaturAll Hydrating Conditioner


These help cleanse your hair while maintaining moisture so your curls stay soft and manageable. Hydrating products are especially important because natural curls thrive when moisture is locked in.


Once your hair is clean and conditioned, you’re ready for the styling step.


# Step 3: Style Your Curls While You Watch


Here’s where the magic happens.


Apply your styling products, twist or scrunch your curls, press play on your movie, and let your hair set while you relax.


Try pairing your movie time with these curl-friendly products:


- NaturAll Hydrating Moisturizer


– perfect for adding softness and hydration to curls.
- NaturAll Hydrating Custard


– great for curl definition and long-lasting hold.
- NaturAll Cold Pressed Avocado Oil with Meadowfoam+


– adds shine and helps seal moisture into your hair.


Apply your products, twist or braid your hair if desired, and let them **work while you watch** .


# Step 4: Cozy Movie Picks for Wash Day


A wash day movie should be relaxing, fun, and easy to watch while you style or detangle your curls.


Here are some **perfect Curls & Chill movies** :


### Romantic & Feel-Good Movies


- **Holidate** – A fun romantic comedy about two people who agree to be each other's holiday dates but unexpectedly develop feelings.


- **Meet Cute** – A romantic comedy about reliving the perfect first date over and over again.


### Comfort Classics


- **Notting Hill** – A cozy romantic story between a bookstore owner and a famous actress.
- **Legally Blonde** – A fun, empowering movie perfect for a self-care day.


Many people online recommend feel-good classics like **The Princess Bride, Legally Blonde, and The Parent Trap** when they want comforting background movies for a cozy day.


These types of movies are perfect for wash day because they’re **lighthearted and relaxing** .


# Step 5: Let Your Hair Set While You Relax


While your curls absorb all that hydration and styling goodness, enjoy the rest of your movie.


This is the best part of wash day — **your hair routine is working while you unwind.**


When the movie ends:


- Take down your twists or braids
- Fluff your curls
- Add a few drops of avocado oil for shine


And just like that, your curls are **hydrated, defined, and ready for the week.**


# Final Thoughts: Turn Wash Day into Self-Care


Wash day shouldn’t feel like a chore. It can be your weekly **“Curls & Chill” ritual** — a time to slow down, relax, and show your hair some love.


With the right products, a cozy atmosphere, and a feel-good movie, your wash day becomes the ultimate self-care moment.


So next time it’s time to refresh your curls:


**Grab your NaturAll products, pick a movie, and let your curls (and your mood) glow.**
