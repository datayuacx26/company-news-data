# BAT wins ‘GEP Procurement Team Award’ at World Procurement Awards

BAT has won the ‘GEP Procurement Team Award’ at the[World Procurement Awards](https://events.procurementleaders.com/world-procurement-awards/) .


Judges at the World Procurement Awards, widely recognised as one of the most prestigious accolades in procurement, praised BAT’s focus on “one team winning together in a safe and empowered environment”.


Carel Aucamp, Group Head of Procurement, said *“This achievement recognises how Procurement has been redefined as a strategic partner to the business, driven by a global, diverse and ambitious team willing to challenge convention and push the profession forward.*


*“Over the past three years, this shift has moved Procurement from a cost-focused function to a driver of growth through stronger partnerships, supplier-led innovation, and created a culture that empowers people to deliver with impact.”*


The team was commended for establishing itself as a true transformation catalyst, with 20% of roles now focused on value generation through supplier-led innovation, and a culture that encourages people to go beyond cost savings to experiment, iterate and deliver meaningful impact.
