# The Smokeless Word: The Rewind - A Look Back at The Smokeless Word So Far

Kingsley Wheaton, BAT’s Chief Corporate Officer and host of The Smokeless Word, is usually the one asking the questions. Not this time.


In the inaugural episode of The Smokeless Word: The Rewind, the tables are turned as broadcaster and journalist Iain Dale steps into the interviewer’s chair. The pair discuss The Smokeless Word behind-the-scenes, Kingsley's favourite moments and the most important lessons he's learnt from the podcast so far.


From Boris Johnson on the inner workings of Westminster to Tony Abbott on taxation and regulation, Rory Sutherland on why great innovation is often disarmingly simple, Nike Örbrink on Sweden’s approach to THR, and Will Greenwood on decision-making, join Kingsley and Iain as they look back on the ideas, challenges and moments that have defined the series.
