# BAT at Work Episode 2: Fail. Learn. Grow.

What if the moments that challenge us most are also the ones that help us grow?


In the second episode of BAT at Work, Himani Kothari is joined by Rita Kolesnikova and Khai Smith for an honest conversation about what happens when things do not always go to plan at work, and what we can learn from those moments.


Together, they explore mistakes, feedback and unexpected challenges, as well as the importance of asking for help, communicating clearly and knowing when to say “I don’t know”.

The episode offers a candid look at career development in action, and a reminder that mistakes do not have to define the journey. What matters most is how we respond, reflect, and take those lessons forward.
