Yet, persistent myths prevent many businesses from unlocking their full potential and benefits.


Let’s separate fact from fiction and reveal how robotic floor cleaners can transform your overall cleaning strategy.


## Myth 1: Automatic Floor Scrubbers Replace Human Workers


**The Truth** : AMRs are designed to work alongside cleaning teams, not replace them. By handling repetitive tasks like floor scrubbing, AMRs free staff to focus on higher-value work, like assisting customers, managing inventory, or responding to urgent needs. This helps teams improve operations and achieve a higher standard of cleanliness..


**The Takeaway** : Free up your team to focus on tasks that elevate safety, productivity, and customer experiences.


## Myth 2: Autonomous Cleaning Robots are Only for Large Facilities


**The Truth** : While autonomous cleaning robots excel in large industrial spaces, they’re equally effective in smaller facilities. The[X4 ROVR robotic scrubber](https://www.tennantco.com/en_us/1/machines/scrubbers/product.x4-rovr.autonomous-floor-scrubber.m-x4rovr.html?icid=blog-amr-myths) , for example, combines advanced mapping and 3D LiDAR technology to deliver precise cleaning even in tight or congested spaces. Its compact design and enhanced mobility make it perfect for offices, retail stores, and educational settings, where agility and reliability are key. The[T16AMR](https://www.tennantco.com/en_us/1/machines/scrubbers/product.t16amr.industrial-robotic-floor-scrubber.m-t16amr.html?icid=blog-amr-myths) , on the other hand, offers exceptional performance in larger industrial settings, making it a versatile option for a wide range of facilities.


**The Takeaway** : Choose the right floor cleaning robot for your facility’s size and cleaning needs to maximize efficiency and achieve optimal cleaning performance.


## Myth 3: Floor Cleaning Robots Are Expensive and Hard to Justify


**The Truth** : While the upfront cost of robotic cleaning machines may seem high, cleaning automation is driving positive change in industries from retail to logistics.[Veritiv, a leading global distributor](https://www.tennantco.com/en_us/resources/resource-center/case-studies/warehousing-logistics/veritiv-t16amr.html?icid=blog-amr-myths) , adopted Tennant’s T16AMR to clean over 43.7 million square meters of warehouse space autonomously. T16AMR helped Vertiv improve safety and productivity by streamlining operations and reducing manual labor. Their success shows AMRs are not a trend—but a valuable tool for tackling rising labor costs and outdated processes.


**The Takeaway** : Businesses like Veritiv show how AMRs can solve operational challenges and elevate cleaning standards.
