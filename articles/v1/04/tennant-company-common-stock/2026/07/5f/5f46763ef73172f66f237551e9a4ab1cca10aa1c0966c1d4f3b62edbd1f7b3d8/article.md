### Tennant Company to Report Second Quarter 2026 Results on August 5, 2026

MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Tennant Company (NYSE: TNC) today announced that the company will report its second quarter 2026 financial results after the market closes on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, with a conference call to follow at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time/ 9:00 a.m. Central Time on Thursday, August 6, 2026. The conference call will be webcast and can be accessed on the company’s website at[investors.tennantco.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.tennantco.com%2F&esheet=54576001&newsitemid=20260723389792&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.tennantco.com&index=1&md5=0c05788d969bb73d0f8c49449d6984de) . A replay of the webcast and accompanying slides will be available on the company’s website.


**Company Profile**


Founded in 1870, Tennant Company (TNC), headquartered in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, is a world leader in the design, manufacture and marketing of solutions that help create a cleaner, safer and healthier world. Its products include equipment for maintaining surfaces in industrial, commercial and outdoor environments; detergent-free and other sustainable cleaning technologies; and cleaning tools and supplies. Tennant's global field service network is the most extensive in the industry. Tennant Company had sales of $1.20 billion in 2025 and has approximately 4,500 employees. Tennant has manufacturing operations throughout the world and sells products directly in more than 21 countries and through distributors in more than 100 countries.
