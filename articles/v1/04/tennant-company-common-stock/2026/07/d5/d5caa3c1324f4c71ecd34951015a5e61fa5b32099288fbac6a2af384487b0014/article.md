# Tennant’s 2025 (Fiscal Year 2024) Sustainability Report

We recently released our[2025 (FY 2024) Sustainability Report](https://www.tennantco.com/content/dam/resources/web-content/sustainability/corporate-sustainability-report/corporate-sustainability-report-tennant-en.pdf) , which marks the continuation of the work on our sustainability framework *Thriving People. Healthy Planet.*


Our Thriving People. Healthy Planet. framework provides a lens through which we can set goals, align plans, and integrate sustainability across our business to create shared value for our business, our customers, and society.


The framework is centered on two pillars: Thriving People and Healthy Planet. This includes six impact areas where we know we can create change: employee success, social impact, shared spaced, climate & energy, water & chemical use, and circular products & waste – because our business cannot be successful without a healthy planet and thriving people.


We’re proud of the work we’re doing to embed sustainability across our plants, operations, and products and the significant progress against our goals and ambitions.


This year’s report reflects our continued commitment to embedding sustainability across our business through our Thriving People. Healthy Planet. framework.


We continually strive to enhance our reporting in a dynamic ESG regulatory landscape and rising stakeholder expectations, while remaining transparent, accountable, and committed to building a cleaner, safer, and healthier world together.
