# Fall Outdoor Cleaning Tips: Master Your Facility's Exterior

July 29, 2025 1:26 AM by Tennant Company


Posted in[Cleaning Solutions](https://www.tennantco.com/en_us/blog/cleaning-solutions.html) ,


[Cleaning Challenges](https://www.tennantco.com/en_us/blog/cleaning-challenges.html) ,


[Outdoor Cleaning](https://www.tennantco.com/en_us/blog/outdoor-cleaning.html) ,


As the seasons shift, so do your cleaning challenges — especially outdoors. Autumn brings falling leaves, dry debris, and clogged storm drains that can make even the most well-kept facility appear neglected. But with the right outdoor sweeper and a smart approach, you can maintain a clean, safe, and professional environment throughout the season.


Here are 4 tips to help facility managers tackle the most common outdoor cleaning concerns this fall—featuring equipment like the[Z50 Citadel™ Outdoor Sweeper](https://www.tennantco.com/content/tennant/en_us/shared/products/1274027.html) .


## **1. Make a Strong First Impression with Clean Outdoor Spaces**


Your facility’s exterior is the first thing visitors and employees notice. Litter, leaves, sand, and dust accumulate quickly during the fall, especially in parking lots, loading docks, and walkways.


**Pro Tip: Sweep Often and Strategically**


Schedule more frequent sweeps during peak leaf fall.


The **Z50 Citadel’s large hopper and powerful vacuum system** can handle heavy seasonal debris without frequent dumping, keeping outdoor areas consistently clear.


### **The Right Equipment for Exterior Cleaning**


-


**Safe and Efficient** : Look for outdoor cleaning equipment with a **tight turning radius** and **intuitive controls** to maneuver safely in crowded or narrow spaces.


-


**One-Pass Performance** : High-efficiency sweepers can pick up leaves, fine dust, and heavier debris in a single pass—saving time and labor.


## **2. Manage Dust in Outdoor Spaces to Protect Air Quality**


Dry autumn weather and dead leaves create ideal conditions for dust buildup—especially on hard surfaces like concrete and asphalt. Dust not only looks bad, it can also affect air quality and health for staff and visitors.


### **Stay Ahead of Dust with These Tips:**


-


**Use Advanced Dust Control** : Choose outdoor sweepers equipped with **multi-stage dust control systems** and **PM-10 certified filtration** to trap fine particles before they become airborne.


-


**Keep Sweeper Filters Clean** : Models with **built-in filter shakers** automatically dislodge trapped particles to maintain peak suction and dust control, even in dry, dusty conditions.


## **3. Prepare a Proactive Outdoor Cleaning Routine**


Autumn showers can carry surface debris into storm drains—clogging drainage systems and increasing environmental impact. A proactive outdoor cleaning routine helps prevent pollutants from washing into waterways.


### **Here’s How to Help:**


-


**Sweep Before It Rains** : Clean paved areas regularly to remove dirt, trash, and leaf buildup before it becomes storm runoff.


-


**Use Vacuum Sweepers** : High-performance models feature **powerful suction and fine particle pickup** , helping reduce harmful runoff into storm drains.


## **4. Don’t Forget Hard-to-Reach Areas in your Cleaning Routine**


Leaves and trash love to collect in corners, under benches, around planters, and along curbs.


### **Optimize Your Cleaning Coverage:**


-


**Side Brushes & Pressure Washing:** Select sweepers with **extendable side brooms** and **optional on-board pressure washers** to reach every nook and cranny.


-


**Plan Routes Efficiently:** Create sweeping routes that include problem areas, especially those near entrances, exits, and drainage paths.


## **Keep Your Facility Clean, Safe, and Ready for Fall**


Fall cleanup doesn’t have to be a hassle. With the right equipment and a smart cleaning strategy, your facility can stay clean, safe, and welcoming—even during the messiest season.
