Cleaning outdoor spaces comes with unique sets of challenges. Whether it’s accumulating different kinds of debris like dirt, leaves, or litter; dealing with unpredictable weather elements; or managing large areas with variable terrain — outdoor spaces come with an unpredictability that makes cleaning them harder than an average indoor environment.


Often organizations don’t think about their outdoor spaces as being connected to their business. Parking lots, sidewalks, and loading docks can feel peripheral to a business’ core focus. But all these spaces implicate the same values that drive companies to uphold a high standard of cleanliness. Customer impressions can be shaped by a filthy sidewalk just as much as by a dirty floor, and employee safety at the loading dock is just as important as on the sales floor.


Outdoor spaces are often your customer’s first impression of your business, and therefore, in many cases, it may be the most important part to consider. Research shows that first impressions are usually formed in less than a tenth of a second — making it all the more critical to ensure your outdoor spaces are putting your company’s best foot forward.


Having the right machines on your side can be a huge lift in meeting your outdoor cleaning needs. In this short blog, we’ll look at the questions to consider around what equipment to use outdoors and why.


## What matters when choosing outdoor commercial cleaning machines?


Choosing commercial cleaning equipment for outdoor spaces has its own priorities. Usually, outdoor equipment needs to withstand the more rugged terrain and elements of outdoor environments, making durability a core criterion.


Efficiency is also important — particularly for large spaces where cleaning can sap labor hours and energy.


Finally, outdoor spaces tend to exact greater wear and tear on equipment, which means whichever machines or solutions you choose should have[a strong service plan](https://www.tennantco.com/en_us/service/service-plans.html?icid=blog-outdoor-commercial-cleaning-machines) or warranty supporting them.


## What kinds of commercial cleaning machines should be used outdoors?


Outdoor sweepers are best for removing loose debris from outdoor areas. Look for ones with high-capacity waste bins, adjustable brushes, and strong suction power.

## The right cleaning equipment: an important choice


The proper equipment for cleaning your outdoor space is not an arbitrary choice. It has implications on cleanliness, safety, and aesthetic appeal. All of these redound to either your company’s benefit—or not. Choosing the right machines is therefore important, but it shouldn’t be daunting.
