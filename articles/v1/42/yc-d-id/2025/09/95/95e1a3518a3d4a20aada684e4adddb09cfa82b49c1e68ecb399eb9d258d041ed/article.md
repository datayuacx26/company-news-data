# Forbes: D-ID Acquires Simpleshow To Lead $50 Billion Interactive AI Market

-
-
-
-


D-ID is acquiring Berlin-based simpleshow to fuse **real-time interactive digital humans** with a proven **explainer-video studio** —one platform that turns one-way training and marketing into **live, two-way experiences** and sets the tone for the next UI of AI video. It’s a bold, forward-looking move aimed at leading a fast-growing interactive-AI market.


The deal is a category play, positioning D-ID to **recharge corporate communications** and help define the **next user interface for enterprise AI** by marrying lifelike avatars with familiar video-creation workflows and a large enterprise customer base. In short, a bid to move to the front of the pack as interactive AI goes mainstream.
