# Before You Take the 2026 Cold Plunge into Commerce Media: What to Know About Post-Purchase Advertising

***Insights from Fluent’s Director of Business Development* *on what to know before you jump into post-purchase advertising.***


Think of post-purchase media like a cold plunge: the first step may look intimidating, but it’s structured, quick to start, and energizing when done right. You’re entering a moment of peak intent—right after checkout—when shoppers are most open to what’s next.


In other words, the plunge isn’t about discomfort—it’s about efficiency: a cleaner signal, less waste, and more predictable outcomes.

**A:**


Traditional acquisition platforms continue to rise in cost while the available audience becomes more saturated. Many marketers are now searching for performance channels that provide:


- Authentic intent signals

- Incremental reach
- Transparent performance reporting

The channel provides incremental customers without adding pressure to your internal workflows.


## **Q: What exactly is post-purchase advertising?**


**A:**


Post-purchase advertising delivers personalized offers to consumers immediately after they complete a purchase on a retailer’s site. This moment carries a rare advantage. The consumer has already demonstrated intent, completed checkout, and is mentally primed for action.


## **Q: Is post-purchase advertising only effective for specific industries?**


**A:**


It performs well across a wide range of verticals. We see strong outcomes for subscription services, delivery, CPG, fintech, travel, home goods, and many others. Any brand that values high-LTV customers can benefit from engagement with active buyers.
