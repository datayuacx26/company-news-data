# From Fragmentation to Profit: Post-Purchase Monetization Wins

***TL;DR:** Retail media fragmentation is draining time, data accuracy, and revenue potential. With the holidays approaching, retailers need faster, simpler, more profitable monetization strategies like post-purchase ads that can launch without adding complexity.*


## Fragmentation Is Holding Retailers Back


Retailers don’t just have a tech stack problem. They have a margin problem. The opportunity to grow is there, but it’s buried under too many systems and too much complexity.


Instead of one streamlined path to revenue, most teams are juggling:


- One vendor for ad serving
- Another for billing
- A different one for attribution
- Manual exports, spreadsheets, and Slack threads trying to hold it all together


What’s missing is clear:


- One source of truth
- One clean view of performance
- One seamless path to revenue


And with peak season right around the corner, simplifying isn’t just about efficiency. It’s about protecting profit.


## Fragmentation Costs Growth


When your systems don’t talk to each other, you can’t:


- Optimize campaigns in real time
- Capture the full impact of your spend
- Measure ROI with confidence
- Launch new opportunities quickly


Competitors who simplify are already monetizing faster and more efficiently. As a result, they are capturing margin that others leave behind.


And the market is only getting bigger.[McKinsey projects](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/the-evolution-of-commerce-media-navigating-a-new-era-in-advertising) US commerce media spend will reach $100B by 2027, fueled by non-endemic and adjacent advertising. The retailers who simplify their monetization stack now are best positioned to capture that opportunity.


**Industry-proof:** Fresh Clean Threads streamlined its post-purchase monetization and went live in under an hour. Revenue per session increased by more than 40 percent, and five figures of incremental profit were generated


in the first month. At scale, that translates to $350K in incremental profit per 1M transactions while enhancing the customer experience at checkout.
