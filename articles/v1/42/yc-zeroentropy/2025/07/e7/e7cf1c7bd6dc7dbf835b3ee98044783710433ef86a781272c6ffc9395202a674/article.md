# ZeroEntropy Raises $4.2M Seed Round to Make AI Retrieval Truly Intelligent

Every day, AI developers face the challenge of building and evaluating reliable, accurate retrieval systems for large, unstructured data. Despite massive advancements in generative AI, retrieving[precise](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/concepts/precision/) , relevant information from messy knowledge bases has remained a persistent bottleneck. Until now.


Today, we’re thrilled to announce that ZeroEntropy has raised a $4.2M Seed round led by Initialized Capital, with support from Y Combinator, Transpose Platform, 22 Ventures, a16z Scout, Thomas Wolff (Hugging Face), Mathilde Collin (Front), Jean Lafleur (Airbyte), Laura Modiano (OpenAI, Sequoia Scout), Tyler Bosmeny (Clever), Founders Future, Kima Ventures, Batch Ventures, Richard Aberman (WePay), Kulveer Taggar (Zeus), and other strategic angel investors. This funding brings our total raised capital to approximately $4.4M, empowering us to accelerate our mission to revolutionize how AI retrieves and understands information. We are grateful to our early believers and partners, like Entrepreneurs First, who pre-seeded the company.


## Why Retrieval Matters: The Invisible Layer of AI Intelligence


Before founding ZeroEntropy, we were building advanced AI systems at startups and in academia. Repeatedly, we encountered the same frustration: even sophisticated[models](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/concepts/large-language-model/) failed to deliver reliable answers due to poor retrieval from unstructured data sources. Everyone was reinventing complex retrieval pipelines, wasting significant resources, and still struggling with suboptimal results.


ZeroEntropy was founded to solve this foundational, often overlooked challenge. We built our API to instantly provide highly accurate, low-latency retrieval across even the messiest internal documents. Our platform seamlessly manages the entire retrieval process, from ingestion and preprocessing to[embedding](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/concepts/embedding/) and[reranking](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/concepts/reranker/) , making it possible for developers to deploy state-of-the-art search capabilities in just an afternoon.


## New State-Of-The-Art Reranker Now Live


Today, we’re also launching ze-rank-1, our cutting-edge[reranker](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/concepts/reranker/) that outperforms every alternative on both public and private benchmarks. You can check out the[full release including benchmark results here](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/articles/announcing-zeroentropy-s-first-rerankers-zerank-1-and-zerank-1-small) .


## Already Trusted by Leading AI Teams


In a few short months, we’ve already begun serving innovative companies and AI developers who rely on precise retrieval to power their[RAG](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/concepts/rag/) and[AI agent](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/concepts/agent/) applications. Our customers have replaced fragile, complicated systems with our streamlined, fully integrated solution, dramatically improving accuracy and reducing latency.


## What’s Next: Building the Future of Reliable AI Retrieval


With this round of funding, ZeroEntropy will grow our talented team, further enhance our cutting-edge retrieval models, and expand our infrastructure to help even more developers focus on building transformative products, instead of struggling with retrieval challenges. Our vision is clear: Giving humans and AI Agents alike, instant, accurate access to relevant data. In a way,[ZeroEntropy is building the infrastructure AGI actually needs](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/articles/agi-requires-better-retrieval-not-just-better-llms) : not just powerful models, but truly intelligent and stateful systems grounded in[context](https://www.zeroentropy.dev/concepts/agent-memory/) , capable of reasoning with the full breadth of knowledge they’ve been given over time.


Stay tuned for more updates as we build the future of intelligent retrieval.
