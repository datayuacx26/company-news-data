# Stackup's New Direction

At Stackup, we've always been driven by a clear mission: to make blockchain technology accessible and efficient for businesses. Today, we're taking a significant step forward in that mission by announcing our new product aimed at revolutionizing on-chain financial operations.


## Our Journey


Since our inception, we've been at the forefront of account abstraction adoption. We've collaborated with industry leaders like Trust Wallet and Eco to launch groundbreaking smart contract wallets. In fact, we deployed the first mainnet ERC-4337 wallet back in January 2022.


As we've watched the account abstraction community grow stronger and gradually reshape the wallet experience, we've also gained deep insights into the evolving needs of organizations beyond infrastructure.


## The Challenge We Uncovered


Through our work with enterprises, we noticed a recurring theme: organizations struggling with their on-chain operations. We saw this challenge firsthand, too.


Initially, we viewed this issue through the lens of a paymaster - how to set limits around gas fees. But as we dug deeper, we realized this was just a small piece of a much larger puzzle.


## Our Solution


That's why we're building a comprehensive solution to address these challenges head-on. Our new product is designed to streamline on-chain financial operations, giving organizations the tools they need to take control of their blockchain finances.


Imagine being able to:


- Manage role-based transactions effortlessly
- Automate recurring payments
- Gain real-time insights into your on-chain cash flow


All of this from one intuitive, user-friendly interface. That's the power we're putting in your hands.


## What This Means for Our Current Services


To focus our efforts on this new direction, we'll be transitioning our bundler and paymaster infrastructure services to our trusted partner, Pimlico, over the next 30 days.


For our existing customers, we want to assure you that we're committed to ensuring a smooth transition. We've prepared a straightforward migration guide to help you through this process:[Stackup to Pimlico Migration Guide](https://docs.pimlico.io/stackup-migration)


## Looking Ahead


This new product isn't just a tool - it's a way to liberate your team from operational chaos and take full control of your on-chain finances. We're excited about the potential this has to empower organizations to master their blockchain operations.


As we build the future of on-chain financial management, we'll be keeping you updated every step of the way. We're committed to delivering a solution that's grounded in real-world needs and empowers you to achieve more with blockchain technology.


Stay tuned for more updates as we continue this journey. Together, we're not just adapting to the blockchain future - we're shaping it.
