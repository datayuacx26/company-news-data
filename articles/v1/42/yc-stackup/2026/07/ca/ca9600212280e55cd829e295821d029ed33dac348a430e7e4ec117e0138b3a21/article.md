# Introducing Stackup Founders Circle

Today marks a major milestone for Stackup. After completely rebuilding our product, we're excited to launch a special program for early supporters who share our vision of better crypto operations.


But first, watch this video to understand why we threw away our successful infrastructure business to solve crypto's biggest problem:


‍


## Why We're Creating the Founders Circle


The video tells the story of our pivot, but here's what we didn't mention: throughout our journey, certain companies have been instrumental in shaping Stackup. Their feedback, challenges, and trust helped us build something truly transformative.


That's why we're creating the Founders Circle - a special program for early adopters who want to help shape the future of crypto operations.


## What Founders Circle Members Get


If you join before December 31, you'll receive:


- 30% lifetime discount on all Stackup plans
- Priority access to new features
- Custom integration of your accounting tools (Annual plans only)
- Dedicated support channel (Annual plans only)


## More Than Just a Discount


Founders Circle isn't just about saving money. It's about joining a community of forward-thinking companies who understand that crypto operations need to evolve. As a member, you'll help shape the features and integrations that will define the future of onchain business operations.


## Limited Time Offer


This offer is available only until December 31, 2024. After that, the Founders Circle will be closed permanently.
