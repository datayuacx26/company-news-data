# Stackup is now live on Avalanche

Today, we're excited to announce that Stackup is now live on Avalanche, bringing enterprise-grade financial controls and seamless crypto operations to one of the fastest-growing blockchain ecosystems.


## Why Avalanche, why now


Avalanche isn't just another blockchain—it's built for the kind of high-throughput, low-latency operations that modern businesses demand. With sub-second finality and predictable fees, it's become the natural choice for companies serious about on-chain operations.


But here's what we've learned from talking to hundreds of crypto companies: having access to a great blockchain isn't enough. You need the right tools to actually operate on it.


Most businesses on Avalanche still manage their operations the hard way—hardware wallets for every transaction, manual processes for approvals, and fragmented systems that don't talk to each other. It's like having a Ferrari but driving it on dirt roads.


Stackup brings the infrastructure that businesses need to actually take advantage of what Avalanche offers.


## What this means for Avalanche businesses


With Stackup now on Avalanche, businesses can finally operate with the sophistication they're used to in traditional finance:


**Smart financial controls** : Set up role-based permissions that work natively on Avalanche. Your accounting team can process vendor payments without accessing your entire treasury. Your operations team can handle routine transactions without executive approval for each one.


**Automated operations** : Schedule recurring payments, set up approval workflows, and automate routine transactions. No more manual intervention for every single payment.


**Cross-chain management** : For businesses operating across multiple networks, Stackup provides unified control. Manage your Avalanche operations alongside Ethereum, Polygon, and other networks from a single interface.


**Enterprise security made simple** : Our passkey-based authentication eliminates the need for hardware wallets or complex key management. Secure enough for enterprise treasuries, simple enough for your entire team to use.


## Built for business, optimized for Avalanche


Stackup's smart account technology works particularly well on Avalanche. The network's fast finality means approval workflows execute instantly. Low fees make it practical to implement granular controls that would be cost-prohibitive on other networks.


For CFOs managing multi-million dollar treasuries, this combination matters. You get the security of a non-custodial solution with the operational efficiency of traditional banking systems.


For founders scaling their operations, it means your team can handle routine financial operations without constant bottlenecks. Your accounting team can process payroll, your operations team can pay vendors, and your treasury team can manage liquidity—all with appropriate controls and complete visibility.


## The bigger picture


Adding Avalanche support isn't just about supporting another blockchain. It's about making sure businesses can operate wherever their users and partners are, without sacrificing the controls and security they need.


We're building toward a future where managing business operations on-chain is as seamless as managing them in traditional finance. Every new network we support gets us closer to that reality.


Avalanche businesses deserve the same level of operational sophistication that traditional businesses take for granted. Today, they have it.
