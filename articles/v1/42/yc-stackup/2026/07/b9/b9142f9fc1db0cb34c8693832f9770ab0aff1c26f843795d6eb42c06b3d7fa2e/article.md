# Visa Taps Stackup's Expertise for Account Abstraction in Global Stablecoin Payments

Visa has engaged Stackup's expertise in their exploration of account abstraction technology to revolutionize global stablecoin payments. Through consultation with John from the Stackup team, Visa is investigating how account abstraction can transform digital transactions and payment infrastructure.


Account abstraction represents a significant advancement in blockchain technology, enabling more flexible and user-friendly transaction experiences. By removing the traditional constraints of externally owned accounts (EOAs), this technology allows for programmable wallets that can implement custom logic, gasless transactions, and enhanced security features.


Visa's experimentation with account abstraction, guided by Stackup's technical insights, demonstrates the payment giant's commitment to innovation in the digital currency space. The collaboration focuses on leveraging stablecoin payments through simplified user experiences that account abstraction enables.


This partnership underscores Stackup's position as a leading authority in account abstraction infrastructure and validates the technology's potential for mainstream financial applications. As traditional payment processors like Visa embrace these blockchain innovations, the path toward more accessible and efficient global payment systems becomes clearer.


For more details on Visa's account abstraction initiatives, visit their[official announcement](https://usa.visa.com/solutions/crypto/rethink-digital-transactions-with-account-abstraction.html) .
