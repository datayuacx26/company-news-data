# A guide to 6th grade math topics

In 6th grade math, your learner will take their first steps into topics like algebra, geometry, and statistics. They’ll be mastering familiar topics while exploring new ones, so getting a solid foothold now will help them stay sharp through the rest of middle school.


Duolingo Math gives your 6th grader a supportive space to practice the essential skills they’ve already learned while building a strong understanding of new topics. Here’s a quick peek at everything your learner will cover in Duolingo’s 6th grade course!

## Becoming an expert in fractions and decimals


In 6th grade, students finally master fractions and decimals before they move on to other rational numbers in grades to come. They’ll practice using standard rules to add, subtract, multiply, and divide all kinds of fractions, decimals, and whole numbers with ease.


In the Duolingo units **Fraction division** , **Operations with decimals** , **Multiplication patterns** , and **Multi-digit division** , learners will practice:


- Dividing fractions by other fractions using visual models
- Adding and subtracting multi-digit decimals and unlike fractions
- Multiplying and dividing multi-digit decimals, fractions, and whole numbers
- Identifying patterns in multiplication and using them to quickly complete operations


[LEARN FRACTIONS AND DECIMALS](https://www.duolingo.com/math/unit/G6_fraction_division_09052025?ref=blog.duolingo.com)


## Powering up with exponents


6th graders also build on their understanding of exponents by working with larger powers to see how numbers can grow very quickly. They’ll put these skills into practice by applying the order of operations to evaluate exponent expressions.


In the Duolingo units **Exponents** and **Exponent operations** , learners will work on:


- Using order of operations to solve problems with exponents and/or parentheses
- Adding and subtracting multiple powers of the same base
- Adding and subtracting different bases raised to the power of 2, 3, or 4


[TRY EXPONENTS](https://www.duolingo.com/math/unit/exponents_grades_4_6?ref=blog.duolingo.com)


## Getting to know ratios


6th grade students explore how two different numbers can relate to each other when compared in a ratio. They’ll start to understand how ratios are related to division and multiplication, paving the way for real-life problem solving.


In the Duolingo units **Intro to ratios** , **Equivalent ratios** , **Parts of wholes** , and **Ratio relationships** , learners will study:


- Using specific ratio language to describe the relationship between two values
- Completing tables of equivalent ratios to find missing numbers
- Solving multiplication or division problems using ratios
- Solving ratio problems by using tape diagrams, number lines, and other visual tools


[LEARN ABOUT RATIOS](https://www.duolingo.com/math/unit/intro_to_ratios_grades_4_0?ref=blog.duolingo.com)


## Digging deeper into rates and percentages


In 6th grade, learners start to expand their understanding of rates and percentages. They’ll convert between different units, and they’ll apply new concepts to solving practical problems about everyday topics like travel or shopping.


In the Duolingo units **Percents** , **Rates and equivalent rates** , and **Unit** ** **rates** , learners will work on:


- Finding a percentage as a rate per 100
- Multiplying rates and percentages by whole numbers
- Converting measurement units by using ratio reasoning
- Solving unit rate problems involving unit pricing and constant speed
- Solving problems that involve finding the whole when given a part and a percent


[EXPLORE RATES AND PERCENTAGES](https://www.duolingo.com/math/unit/percents_grades_4_2?ref=blog.duolingo.com)


## Discovering the world of algebra


Your 6th grader will begin their algebraic journey by learning to use variables as stand-ins for unknown numbers. They’ll start working with simple equations and inequalities, and learn that “solving an equation” just means finding the number that makes an equation true.


In the Duolingo units **Algebraic expressions** , **Distributive property and equivalency** , **One-variable equations** , and **Inequalities** , learners will practice:


- Reading and writing expressions where letters stand for numbers
- Applying the distributive property to find equivalent expressions
- Solving one-step equations and inequalities
- Using variable expressions to represent real-life scenarios


[DIVE INTO ALGEBRA](https://www.duolingo.com/math/unit/G6_algebraic_expressions_01082026?ref=blog.duolingo.com)


## Exploring negative numbers, factors, and multiples


In the 6th grade, learners explore our number system by discovering negative numbers, factors, and multiples. They’ll start doing operations with negatives, discover absolute value, and calculate the factors and multiples of different numbers.


In the Duolingo units **Negative numbers** , **Absolute value** , **Greatest common factor,** and **Least common multiple** , learners will dive into:


- Using negative numbers to describe real-life scenarios
- Understanding absolute value as a number’s distance from zero
- Comparing, adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing negative numbers
- Calculating the greatest common factor and least common multiple of whole numbers


[LEARN NEGATIVES, FACTORS, AND MULTIPLES](https://www.duolingo.com/math/unit/G6_negative_numbers_09052025?ref=blog.duolingo.com)


## Connecting shapes and the coordinate plane


6th grade math brings familiar geometric shapes to the coordinate plane. Students will learn new ideas about shapes’ properties by exploring how to draw points, lines, and polygons on a graph.


In the Duolingo units **Coordinate plane, Find distance** , **Find side lengths** , **Polygon area** , and **Translations** , learners will practice:


- Graphing points in all four quadrants of the coordinate plane using ordered pairs
- Translating points, line segments, and figures by adjusting ordered pairs
- Calculating the distance between points with the same x or y coordinate
- Identifying the length of line segments and the side lengths of polygons
- Finding the area of triangles and quadrilaterals plotted on the coordinate plane


[EXPLORE GEOMETRY AND COORDINATE PLANES](https://www.duolingo.com/math/unit/G6_the_coordinate_plane_09052025?ref=blog.duolingo.com)
