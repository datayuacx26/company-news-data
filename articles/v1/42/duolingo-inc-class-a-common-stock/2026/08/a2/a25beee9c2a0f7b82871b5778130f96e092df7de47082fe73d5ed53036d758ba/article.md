## How to conjugate *ser* and when to use it


*Ser* is an irregular verb, which means that it doesn’t follow the regular pattern for verb endings. But you’ll see the forms of *ser* so often that you’ll get lots of practice with them! Like all Spanish verbs, *ser* will change depending on who the subject is—we do this for some verbs in English, too, by saying *I **am*** but *she **is*** . Here’s how you conjugate *ser* :

There’s an easy way to remember some of the most common uses of *ser* , and that’s by remembering the acronym **DOCTOR** : Each letter in **DOCTOR** stands for one of the situations in which you’ll use *ser* !


💡


In general, **ser** is used to describe things that are fixed or permanent.


Situation Example


D **Date**
Use *ser* to tell someone the date. Hoy es el veintitrés de septiembre.


Today is September twenty-third.


O **Occupation**
Use *ser* to talk about your occupation. Yo soy una doctora.


I am a doctor.


Nosotras somos abogadas.


We are lawyers.


C **Characteristics**
Use *ser* when describing physical characteristics, qualities, or personality traits. LeBron James es famoso.


LeBron is famous.


Ustedes son introvertidos.


You all are introverted.


T **Time**
Use *ser* to talk about the time.
**Note:** Remember to use *es* for 1:00 because it is one hour (singular) and *son* for all the other times because they’re more than one hour (plural)! Es la una de la tarde.


It is 1:00pm.


Son las seis y media de la mañana.


It is 6:30am.


O **Origin**
Use *ser* to talk about where someone or something is from. Ustedes son chilenos.


You all are Chilean.


El auto es alemán.


The car is German.


R **Relationships**
Use *ser* when talking about your relationship to someone. Olga es mi abuela.


Olga is my grandmother.


Ellos son mis tíos.


They are my uncles.


## How to conjugate *estar* and when to use it


Just like *ser* , *estar* is also an irregular verb that you’ll get lots of practice with! Here’s how you conjugate it:

There is also an easy way to remember some of the most common uses of *estar* , and that’s by remembering the acronym **PLACE** : Each letter stands for one of the situations in which you’ll use *estar* !


💡


**Estar** is generally used for temporary conditions and locations.


Situation Example


P **Position**
Use *estar* to talk about where something is placed. ¿Dónde están mis lentes?


Where are my glasses?


Tus lentes están en la mesa.


Your glasses are on the table.


L **Location**
Use *estar* to talk about where something or someone is. Yo estoy en el restaurante.


I am in the restaurant.


Mis amigos están en la fiesta.


My friends are at the party.


A **Action** (what is happen **ing** )
Use *estar* to describe what someone is do **ing** .
**Note:** For Action, you’ll also need the ***‑ing*** form of the second verb! Usted está estudiando.


You are studying.


Nosotros estamos comiendo pizza.


We are eating pizza.


C **Condition**
Use *estar* to talk about the state of something or someone. ¿Están enfermas?


Are you all sick?


Yo estoy cansado.


I am tired.


E **Emotions**
Use *estar* to talk about your feelings. Tú estás contenta.


You are happy.


Ella está triste hoy.


She is sad today.
