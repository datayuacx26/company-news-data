Blockstack’s community vote platform by Democracy Earth


App Mining is a bold initiative supporting the development of new decentralized apps with 100k paid every month. As one of the App Reviewers we provide a key part of it: the governance component, allowing Stack’s token holders to express which apps should be supported. This month, our software has evolved to include always-on voting and enabling direct public conversations between token holders and App Founders throughout the voting process.


This kind of flexibility and rich communication speaks directly to the improvements on governance that we support. Our development is also highly informed by the input of all the different stakeholders involved, and we are bullish on the kinds of results that can be achieved when a network is collaborating as intensively and diligently as Blockstack’s !
