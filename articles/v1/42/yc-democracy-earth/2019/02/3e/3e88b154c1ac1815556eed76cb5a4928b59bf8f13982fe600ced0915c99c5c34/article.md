### Metrics that matter


This is our third month since the official start of App Mining’s vote — with a total of 300k in payouts. As we work to transform our governance platform into a powerful tool for the Blockstack ecosystem, there are a few metrics that we care deeply about:


Press enter or click to view image in full size


**The first is participation rate.** Engaging voters is an enormous challenge for any governance process. Through a close collaboration with Blockstack’s fierce team, we have a current outstanding 32% voter turnout. This is the result of a hands-on approach, carefully on boarding voters and walking them through every step of the process with constant, rich communications. Our aim is not only to maintain this metric as the project scales and complexities increase, but to continue growing it on a monthly basis with design choices that can foster more engagement.


**The second metric concerns the percentage of apps that received votes.** Our primary concern is to enable a fair process for all the excellent teams and technologies being built on Blockstack. Currently, ~80% of registered apps have received votes. We grew that number through our ongoing exchange with App Miners, paying close attention to their feedback. Some of the new features that came as a product of that were enabling Miners to access the platform, interact with voters and receive feedback from them, and to randomly sort the apps.


Again, we are excited to continue improving so as to surface the most optimal results with our voting platform! This is only the beginning.
