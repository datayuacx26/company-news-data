# Democracy Earth | News from the front…

Dear Earth Citizen 🌍,


The past week saw blockchain technology shaping the agenda in the ongoing tensions between the United States 🇺🇸 and China 🇨🇳.


Zuckerberg before the Financial Services Committee of the US House of Representatives.


Arguing that the prospective[Chinese digital Renminbi](https://earth.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=301becd69e00afa63c21122f3&id=620bcc8eb6&e=de07766081) is a political threat, Zuckerberg openly pitched[Libra](https://earth.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=301becd69e00afa63c21122f3&id=5dda95714f&e=de07766081) to US regulators as an instrument for American influence. Xi Jinping replied by calling his country to seize the opportunity afforded by blockchain technology — which was followed by an[unusual increase in the Bitcoin price](https://earth.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=301becd69e00afa63c21122f3&id=1942315f72&e=de07766081) , deemed by many as a Chinese statement.


While that could have been interpreted as a support to the decentralization enabled by public chains such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, it did not take very long for Xi’s views on how the technology[can be used to foster his centralized and authoritarian government](https://earth.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=301becd69e00afa63c21122f3&id=0659a0d80a&e=de07766081) to become clear.


Amidst the Libra x China conundrum, here at Democracy.Earth **we are coding the alternative:**


The upcoming release of Sovereign will provide a social networking interface to the[MolochDAO](https://earth.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=301becd69e00afa63c21122f3&id=8ed641a2f6&e=de07766081) governance framework. In a DAO, voting and subsequent resource allocation are done according to the rules encoded in[Smart Contracts](https://earth.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=301becd69e00afa63c21122f3&id=406f10ec41&e=de07766081) , that work as a trusted, automated constitution. After The DAO’s infamous hack in 2016 — which put a hold on experimentation — this year we are finally seeing the emergence of a new wave of organizations that are succeeding to organize & gather funds upon DAOs. More specifically upon the[MolochDAO](https://earth.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=301becd69e00afa63c21122f3&id=cecc5eaae9&e=de07766081)[framework](https://earth.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=301becd69e00afa63c21122f3&id=ffc4184a9a&e=de07766081) .


Being aimed at Humans, these new DAOs are creating **** metrics for on-chain human activity, **trailing the path to a decentralized & sybil resistant identity framework.** Additionally, although being at a very early stage, they demonstrate how it is possible to coordinate resources in a distributed way. Avoiding centralized organizations — such as Libra — or centralized states — such as China.
