Adding any ERC20 token to Metamask is a pretty simple process that should only take you a couple minutes. For the $HOUR token here's all you need to do:


**1 —** Open the Metamask extension on your browser (on Chrome you can find the icon next to the address bar).


**2 —** Click on the **"Menu"** option:


Press enter or click to view image in full size


**3 —** Scroll down the menu until you see the **"ADD TOKEN"** option in the bottom.


Press enter or click to view image in full size


4–Select **"CUSTOM TOKEN"** , and on the **"Token Contract Address"** field paste the following address:
**0x00319f722bd546182cb2c701ca254146d3f084fc** .


Press enter or click to view image in full size


5- The two other fields will be filled automatically. Hit **"NEXT"** , and on the following screen **"ADD TOKENS"** . You will see that your new $HOUR token balance will show!
