# Letter to our UK Community: 8x more Shareback Rewards

We have some exciting news to share.


After two years of work, WeShop Holdings Limited successfully listed on Nasdaq at the end of last year. This was a huge milestone for us and for you, allowing an important step towards our vision of giving shoppers real ownership in retail.


Because of US regulatory rules, we couldn’t talk publicly about the process while it was happening. Thank you for your patience and for sticking with us during that time.


The listing has allowed us to begin testing and expanding the platform in the US, laying the foundations for our upcoming major launch there.


A Thank You to Our Community


As a thank you for your support, we’ve increased ALL Shareback rates across UK retailers by up to 8x in the WeShop app.


There are some incredible offers available right now.


We’re incredibly excited about what’s ahead. Now that we’re listed on the stock market, we have a real opportunity to grow our vision of transforming retail through consumer ownership.


Let’s get shopping — because shopping really does start here.


Thank you again for being part of the journey.


Many thanks,


From your WeShop Team
