# EPSIUM Enterprise Limited Signs Memorandum of Understanding with Era Future (Macau) Performance Art Limited to Explore Strategic Collaboration

MACAU


, Aug. 25, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- EPSIUM Enterprise Limited (NASDAQ:EPSM ) ("EPSIUM" or the "Company"), a Nasdaq-listed holding company, today announced that it has entered into a non-binding Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") with Era Future ( Macau


) Performance Art Limited ("Era Future"), a Macau SAR-based company specializing in variety show planning, event operations, film and television production support, and integrated marketing.


The MOU outlines the parties' intention to engage in good faith discussions towards a potential strategic collaboration. Under the preliminary framework, the two companies will explore opportunities, including a potential stock swap merger and cooperation on film and television projects. The collaboration will leverage Era Future's strong regional network and reputation as a leading event organizer in Macau


, providing EPSIUM with comprehensive opportunities to enhance visibility and reach new potential customers. The agreement marks the beginning of an exclusivity period, during which both parties have agreed not to pursue similar opportunities with competitors.


EPSIUM is a leading premium alcoholic beverage distributor in Macau


, primarily supplying to the city's major integrated resort operators. Era Future, by contrast, focuses on producing and managing large-scale entertainment events. The two companies share an overlapping client base, and their collaboration is expected to create strong synergies: entertainment events such as concerts and variety shows drive significant foot traffic, which can in turn boost beverage sales at the venues, including premium alcoholic products supplied by EPSIUM. For integrated resorts and casinos


, this combined offering of event hosting and beverage services can help attract and retain more visitors, creating value for both the venue operators and the partnership. At the same time, the initiative contributes to broader goals by fostering cross-industry synergies and enhancing Macau's


cultural profile—further supporting its ambition to be a "City of Performing Arts" and a world-class international metropolis.


"Signing the MOU represents an important step as we continue to evaluate growth opportunities that could expand our presence in the entertainment and cultural sectors," said Son I Tam, Chief Executive Officer of EPSIUM. "Era Future plays a key role in bringing a wide range of performances and concerts to the Macau


region. We look forward to collaborating closely with the top-tier event organizer to strengthen our presence in the local market and drive sales through this partnership."


The MOU is not legally binding, and any definitive terms of the collaboration will be set out in formal agreements to be negotiated and executed by the parties. Both companies have agreed to maintain confidentiality regarding specific terms and details until further announcements are made in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.


**About EPSIUM ENTERPRISE LIMITED**


Through its Macau operating entity, Companhia de Comercio Luz Limitada ("Luz"), a limited liability company organized under Macau laws in 2010, EPSIUM is engaged in importing and wholesaling primarily alcoholic beverages in Macau. Through Luz, the Company imports and sells a broad range of premium beverages, primarily alcoholic beverages and, in 2022, a small quantity of tea and fruit juice. The alcoholic beverages the Company sells include Chinese liquor, French cognac, Scottish whiskey, fine wine, Champagne, and other miscellaneous beverage alcohol. Sales of Chinese liquor is by far the Company's most significant operations, and the Company is a top wholesaler of high-end Chinese liquor in Macau. For more information, please visit the Company's website:[www.epsium-group.com](http://www.epsium-group.com/) .
