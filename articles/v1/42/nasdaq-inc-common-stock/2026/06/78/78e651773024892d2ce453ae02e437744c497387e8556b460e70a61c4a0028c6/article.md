# Space Exploration Technologies Corporation to Join the Nasdaq-100 Index® Beginning July 7, 2026

[PDF Version](https://ir.nasdaq.com/node/110646/pdf)


NEW YORK, June 26, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Nasdaq (Nasdaq: NDAQ) today announced that Space Exploration Technologies Corporation (Nasdaq: SPCX) will become a component of the Nasdaq-100 Index® prior to market open on Tuesday, July 7, 2026.


For additional information, including notifications on changes to any Nasdaq Indexes, please go to[https://indexes.nasdaq.com/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=ExsT0PCnILKXyc_0VTL_29m4P8OYFtRGBeGW0IxaTlsTFYWdV4XcZKsMQlLDeVr-Bx_dCjxi3AFn8l6qkCIrTrOe_asECPUo0E815A8oCumbMZnXNRLV-4txPGvDKW2s)
