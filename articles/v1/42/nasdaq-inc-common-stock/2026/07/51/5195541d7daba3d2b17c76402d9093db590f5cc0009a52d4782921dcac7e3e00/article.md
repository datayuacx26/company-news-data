# Nasdaq Resumes Trading in Inno Holdings Inc.

[PDF Version](https://ir.nasdaq.com/node/110816/pdf)


NEW YORK, July 31, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- The Nasdaq Stock Market® (Nasdaq: NDAQ) announced that trading will resume in Inno Holdings Inc. (Nasdaq: INHD) at 12:00 p.m. Eastern Time on July 31, 2026. Trading in the company’s ordinary shares was halted on June 8, 2026 at 17:18:58 Eastern Time.
