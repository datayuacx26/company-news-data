# Press Release: GoLinks Launched Two Multimodal GenAI Solutions

Have you heard the news? On December 14th, we announced the launch of two new multimodal GenAI products to transform enterprise search and knowledge discovery: GoSearch + GoProfiles.


[Click here to read the full press release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/golinks-launches-two-multimodal-genai-solutions-to-transform-enterprise-search-and-knowledge-discovery-302014414.html?tc=eml_cleartime) , where we introduce these innovative solutions designed to tackle workplace knowledge inefficiencies.


For a quick summary, read below ↓


## [🔎GoSearch](https://www.gosearch.ai/) : Multimodal GenAI-Powered Enterprise Search


Ever feel like you’re lost in a sea of apps and documents, spending way too much time searching for crucial information? We created GoSearch to help address this common struggle. It’s like having your own internal Google Search, but tailor-made for your company’s resources.


Jorge Zamora, Founder of GoLinks, highlights the issue: “The average enterprise uses 137 SaaS apps, meaning most workers spend 19% of their time looking for the information they need.” GoSearch solves this problem by offering a single window into all your workplace apps. Just ask a question, and within seconds, get a precise and personalized answer, thanks to its multimodal GenAI technology.


Whether it’s a quick password reset or hunting down your company’s Q4 product roadmap, GoSearch summarizes insights from multiple sources, providing you with a straightforward answer. Plus, it supports over 100 major workplace collaboration tools, making it an all-encompassing solution.


## 👤[GoProfiles](https://goprofiles.io/) : GenAI-Powered Employee Directory & Peer Recognition


In a world where remote work is the norm, genuine connections between colleagues can be a challenge. We created GoProfiles to help employees know and celebrate each other— even in remote and hybrid environments.


Jorge explains, “GoProfiles is employee data for employees.” It not only helps you find basic information but goes beyond, showcasing personalities, achievements, and even facilitating peer recognition with customizable badges called Bravos.


As employees become more dispersed, purposeful connections are vital. GoProfiles bridges the gap by providing rich employee profile pages, interactive maps for local connections, team directories, and org charts for internal relationships. And yes, there’s a gamified element with Bravos displayed on profiles and leaderboards.


## The GoLinks Suite: A New Era of Productivity


In essence, we’re bringing the tools used by top companies to the broader market. With GoSearch and GoProfiles, you can leverage multimodal GenAI to connect your organization to knowledge and people for a more empowered workforce.
