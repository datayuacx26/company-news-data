# What’s New at GoLinks: November 2024

In November, we brought a brand new productivity feature to your dashboard. Now, brainstorming ideas and sharing notes with teammates is easier than ever.


Let’s dive into what’s new this month and how this feature can keep you productive within your GoLinks dashboard.


## Jots: A seamless note-taking experience


Jots are a new way to capture your thoughts, organize ideas, and stay in sync with your team, all within your GoLinks dashboard. Whether you’re creating meeting notes from a 1-1 with a teammate, making a to-do list, or brainstorming ideas, Jots let you record your thoughts quickly, without interrupting your workflow.


From your GoLinks dashboard, create a new, shareable Jot. Use text formatting, alignments, bullets, numbering, and even code snippets to organize the contents of your Jot and make information easily consumable by teammates. Emphasize a key point, list out action items, or structure your notes, effortlessly.


## Effortless resource sharing & collaboration


After you create a Jot, easily create a go link for it with just a few clicks. That way, you can quickly access your notes or share them with a teammate. No more sending links via email or chat—just create a go link and you’re good to go!


Collaboration doesn’t stop with sharing. With Jots, you can not only create your own notes but also view and edit Jots from your teammates. This makes it easy to stay in sync, whether you’re working on a project together or collecting feedback. Real-time editing ensures everyone is always on the same page.
