# GoLinks 2025 Platform Updates: Powering Connected Work

2025 was a defining year for the GoLinks platform and our growing suite of products. As organizations looked for faster access to knowledge, fewer context switches, and more intelligent workflows, GoLinks delivered meaningful improvements across linking, AI, search, and employee engagement.


From record-breaking usage to major product launches in GoLinks, GoSearch, and GoProfiles, this year reinforced our mission: make work easier by connecting people, knowledge, and tools.


## **GoLinks by the Numbers in 2025**


GoLinks continued to power daily work across thousands of teams. This year’s usage highlights show how deeply go links are embedded in modern workflows:


- 18,666,162 go links used


- 105,139 go links created


- 322,547 unlisted go links used


- 37,429 private go links used


- 9,302 multi-links used


- 1,092 geo links used


All this amounts to over 777,757 hours saved for our users in 2025!


These numbers reflect how teams rely on the GoLinks platform for secure access, flexible sharing, and location-aware routing across apps and environments.


## **What We Shipped for GoLinks in 2025**


We introduced several high-impact features designed to make go links more powerful, more intelligent, and easier to use across the tools teams already rely on.


### **Clickable Go Links in Slack DMs**


Go links now automatically render as clickable previews in[Slack direct messages](https://www.golinks.com/blog/go-links-in-slack-dms/) , just like they do in channels. This removes friction from private conversations and ensures shared knowledge is immediately accessible.


### **Advanced LLM Options for GoAI**


[GoAI expanded](https://www.golinks.com/blog/whats-new-at-golinks-october-2025/) to support advanced large language model options, giving teams more control over how AI summarizes, analyzes, and responds to information. This flexibility allows organizations to tailor AI behavior to their needs while maintaining enterprise-grade security.


### **GoLinks for Microsoft Word**


When inserting a[go link into a Word document](https://www.golinks.com/blog/whats-new-at-golinks-october-2025/) , the link stays clean, readable, and clickable. This eliminates long URLs and helps teams maintain clarity and consistency in shared documentation.


### **GoLinks for Microsoft Outlook**


[Go links shared via Outlook](https://www.golinks.com/blog/whats-new-at-golinks-october-2025/) now render as clickable previews, improving email readability and helping recipients understand destinations instantly without extra context.


### **GoLinks Basic Plan**


We launched the[GoLinks Basic Plan](https://www.golinks.com/blog/introducing-the-golinks-basic-plan/) to make the GoLinks platform accessible to more teams. The plan includes essential features, unlimited users, and role-based access, offering a simple and affordable way to standardize internal linking.


## **GoSearch in 2025: Enterprise Search Becomes Actionable**


In 2025, GoSearch redefined what enterprise search can be. Instead of simply retrieving information,[GoSearch evolved](https://www.gosearch.ai/blog/enterprise-search-in-2025-how-gosearch-redefined-ai-powered-work/) into an AI-powered system that connects knowledge with action.


Key advancements included AI agents that automate tasks across applications, federated connectors that access live data without heavy indexing, persistent AI memory for more contextual conversations, and expanded LLM support. GoSearch also embedded deeply into Slack and browser workflows, allowing users to get answers where work happens.


Together, these updates positioned GoSearch as an intelligence layer that unifies search, insights, and execution across the enterprise.


## **GoProfiles in 2025: Stronger Connections and Recognition**


[GoProfiles](https://www.goprofiles.io/blog/employee-engagement-recognition-2025-goprofiles-recap/) continued to support employee discovery, recognition, and engagement throughout 2025. Teams used profiles to highlight skills, celebrate achievements, and find expertise across the organization.


This year, employees viewed tens of thousands of profiles, exchanged thousands of recognitions, and added new skills and interests that made internal collaboration easier and more human. GoProfiles reinforced the value of visibility and appreciation in distributed workplaces.


## **Connected Work With the GoLinks Platform**


Across GoLinks, GoSearch, and GoProfiles, one theme defined 2025: connected work. Go links became more than shortcuts. They became entry points into AI summaries, geo-aware routing, enterprise search, and shared context across Slack, email, documents, and browsers.


By unifying links, AI, search, and people data into one ecosystem, the GoLinks platform helps teams move faster, reduce friction, and turn information into action.


## **Looking Ahead to 2026**


As we move into 2026, we’re focused on deeper AI capabilities, smarter automation, and continued expansion of the GoLinks platform across tools and workflows.


The goal remains the same: make knowledge instantly accessible, intelligently connected, and easy to act on, wherever work happens.
