## Element Solutions Inc Declares Q2 Dividend of $0.08 Per Share


05/20/2026


Download (opens in new window)


MIAMI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Element Solutions Inc (NYSE: ESI) announced today that its board has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.08 per share of the company's common stock. The declared dividend will be paid on June 15, 2026, to stockholders of record as of the close of business on June 1, 2026.


**About Element Solutions Inc**


Element Solutions Inc is a leading specialty chemicals technology company whose businesses supply a broad range of solutions that enhance the performance of products people use every day. Developed in multi-step technological processes, these innovative solutions enable customers' manufacturing processes in several key industries, including consumer electronics, power electronics, semiconductor fabrication, communication and data storage infrastructure, automotive systems, industrial surface finishing, and offshore energy.
