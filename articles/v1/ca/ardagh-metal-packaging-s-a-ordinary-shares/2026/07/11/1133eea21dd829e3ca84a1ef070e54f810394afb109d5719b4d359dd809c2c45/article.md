# IPPAI Lemon Sour: Japan in a can

Ardagh Metal Packaging is proud to partner with[IPPAI Lemon Sour レモンサワー](https://www.lemonsour.com/) , a beloved Japanese drink now available to European consumers in our aluminium beverage cans. IPPAI Lemon Sour レモンサワー captures the crisp, refreshing blend of citrus and shochu that has turned Lemon Sour into one of Japan’s favourite drinks for social occasions.


In Japan, cans are central to everyday beverage culture, valued for their convenience, portability and fast chilling – ensuring the drink is served perfectly cold. Lightweight and fully recyclable, cans also suit modern lifestyles while supporting more sustainable packaging solutions. At the same time, cans help to preserve the quality and freshness of the drink and its carefully selected ingredients. IPPAI Lemon Sour レモンサワー is crafted using 100% authentic Japanese shochu, Sicilian lemons and Austrian spring water – all-natural, with no preservatives or artificial additives – and the can format ensures its flavour and refreshing character are maintained from filling to the moment it is enjoyed.


As Lemon Sour ranks among the top canned alcoholic beverages in Japanese convenience stores and supermarkets, it naturally aligns with AMP’s expertise in producing high-quality aluminium cans. The IPPAI Lemon Sour レモンサワー is presented in our **Standard 33 cl can with a glossy finish** . Inspired by the simplicity of Japanese design, its clean visual identity reflects the heritage of the Lemon Sour category while conveying a sense of freshness and craftsmanship.


“Cans are a bridge between tradition and modern convenience,” said Gerlof Toenhake, Director of Marketing and Business Development at AMP-Europe. "With IPPAI Lemon Sour レモンサワー, our cans preserve the quality of the drink while telling the story of a beloved Japanese classic to a new audience.”


“Great drinks tell stories – since the 1970s, Lemon Sour has been enjoyed across Japan and has become a familiar part of everyday moments enjoyed in good company. With IPPAI Lemon Sour レモンサワー, we want to bring this iconic Japanese drink to Europe and invite people to discover a taste of Japan for themselves.” — Marcel Buchberger (Founder, IPPAI Beverage GmbH)


In recognition of its distinctive visual identity and brand concept, IPPAI Lemon Sour レモンサワー recently received a Bronze in Branding and a Shortlist recognition in Packaging at the CCA Venus Award – Austria’s most prestigious creative prize.
