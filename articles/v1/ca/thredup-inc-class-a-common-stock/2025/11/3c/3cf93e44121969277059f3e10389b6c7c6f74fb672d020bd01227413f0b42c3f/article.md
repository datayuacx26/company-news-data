# New York & Company Announces Clean Out Resale Program Enabled by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® — ThredUp Newsroom

**November 10, 2025 (Oakland, CA. and New York, NY.)** –[New York & Company](https://www.nyandcompany.com/) , the destination for modern, versatile fashion inspired by the style and spirit of New York City, today announced a Clean Out resale program that allows customers to resell gently-worn items for New York & Company shopping credit. The program is powered by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® (RaaS®), which enables the world's leading fashion brands and retailers to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers. New York & Company joins more than 50 other brands offering resale programs through RaaS, including J.Crew, Tommy Hilfiger, and Madewell.


“We’re always looking for innovative ways to deliver value to our customers,” said Laura Cantor, VP of Marketing & Ecommerce at New York & Company. “Partnering with ThredUp gives our community an easy way to earn credit toward new favorites while taking part in more sustainable shopping practices.”


**How the Program Works** Customers can earn New York & Company shopping credit for sending in gently-worn items. To participate, customers can generate a prepaid shipping label from[nyandcompany.thredup.com](https://nyandcompany.thredup.com/) , fill any shippable box or bag with apparel, shoes, and accessories from any brand in their closet, and ship it to ThredUp for free. For items that sell on ThredUp, customers receive New York & Company credit that can be used both online and in-store.


For more information, please visit[nyandcompany.thredup.com](https://nyandcompany.thredup.com/) . To learn more about ThredUp's RaaS, visit[raas.thredup.com](http://raas.thredup.com/) .


**About New York & Company**


New York & Company, Inc. is a specialty retailer of women's fashion apparel and accessories, and the modern wear-to-work destination for women, providing fashion that is feminine, polished, on-trend and versatile. New York & Company, Inc. helps its customers feel confident, put-together, attractive and stylish by providing affordable fashion. The Company's proprietary branded New York & Company® merchandise is sold through its national network of retail stores and online at www.nyandcompany.com. The Company operates over 400 stores nationwide.

Sellers love ThredUp because we make it easy to clean out their closets and unlock value for themselves or for the charity of their choice while doing good for the planet. Buyers love shopping value, premium and luxury brands all in one place, at up to 90% off estimated retail price. Our proprietary operating platform is the foundation for our managed marketplace and consists of distributed processing infrastructure, proprietary software and systems and data science expertise. With ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service, some of the world's leading brands and retailers are leveraging our platform to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers.
