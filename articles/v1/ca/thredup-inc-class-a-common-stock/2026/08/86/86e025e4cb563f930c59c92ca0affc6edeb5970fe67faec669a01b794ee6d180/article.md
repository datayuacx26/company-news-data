# OLEADA Announces Clean Out Program Enabled by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® — ThredUp Newsroom

**August 11th, 2026 (Oakland, CA. and New York, NY)** – OLEADA, the premium accessories brand known for thoughtfully designed bags and work essentials that blend style with function, today announced a Clean Out resale program that allows customers to resell gently-worn items for OLEADA shopping credit. The program is powered by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® (RaaS®), which enables the world's leading fashion brands and retailers to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers. OLEADA joins more than 50 other brands offering resale programs through RaaS, including J.Crew, Tommy Hilfiger, and Madewell.


"OLEADA was designed for women who need their bags to work as hard as they do, and that means designing things built to last well beyond one season. Partnering with ThredUp brings that thinking to a community that already shops with intention. If you are meeting us for the first time, welcome to the world of OLEADA WorkStyle. Made so you can handle your day and everything after it," said Tiffany Zhou, Co-Founder and Creative Director of OLEADA.


Through the new program, customers can earn OLEADA shopping credit for sending in gently-worn items. To participate, customers can generate a prepaid shipping label from[oleada.thredup.com](http://oleada.thredup.com/) , fill any shippable box or bag with apparel, shoes, and accessories from any brand in their closet, and ship it to ThredUp for free. For items that sell on ThredUp, customers receive credit that can be used to shop new OLEADA pieces.


For more information, please visit[oleada.thredup.com](http://oleada.thredup.com/) . To learn more about ThredUp's RaaS, visit[raas.thredup.com](http://raas.thredup.com/) .
