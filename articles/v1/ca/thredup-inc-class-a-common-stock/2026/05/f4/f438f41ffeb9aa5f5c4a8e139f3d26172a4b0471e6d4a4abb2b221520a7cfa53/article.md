# Yoga Democracy Announces Clean Out Program Enabled by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® — ThredUp Newsroom

**May 27th, 2026 (Oakland, CA. and Indianapolis, Indiana)** –Yoga Democracy, offers eco-conscious, consciously crafted activewear designed for movement, utilizing recycled materials and waterless dyeing processes to create vibrant, sustainable apparel, today announced a Clean Out resale program that allows customers to resell gently-worn items for Yoga Democracy shopping credit. The program is powered by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® (RaaS®), which enables the world's leading fashion brands and retailers to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers. Yoga Democracy joins more than 50 other brands offering resale programs through RaaS, including J.Crew, Tommy Hilfiger, and Madewell.


Through the new program, customers can earn Yoga Democracy shopping credit for sending in gently-worn items. To participate, customers can generate a prepaid shipping label from[yogademocracy.thredup.com](http://yogademocracy.thredup.com/) , fill any shippable box or bag with apparel, shoes, and accessories from any brand in their closet, and ship it to ThredUp for free. For items that sell on ThredUp, customers receive credit that can be used to shop new Yoga Democracy pieces.


For more information, please visit[yogademocracy.thredup.com](http://yogademocracy.thredup.com/) . To learn more about ThredUp's RaaS, visit[raas.thredup.com](http://raas.thredup.com/) .
