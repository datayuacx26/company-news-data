# Lands' End Announces Clean Out Resale Program Enabled by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® — ThredUp Newsroom

**January 29, 2026 (Oakland, CA. and Dodgeville, Wisconsin** ) –[Lands’ End](https://www.landsend.com/) , the classic American lifestyle brand, today announced a Clean Out resale program that allows customers to resell gently-worn items for Lands’ End shopping credit. The program is powered by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® (RaaS®), which enables the world's leading fashion brands and retailers to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers. Lands’ End joins more than 50 other brands offering resale programs through RaaS, including J.Crew, Tommy Hilfiger, and Madewell.


“Resale is a natural extension of how we think about design and durability at Lands’ End,” said Devan Thompson, Vice President, Direct to Consumer, Lands’ End. “By partnering with ThredUp, we’re giving our customers an easy way to keep great clothing in circulation and take a more intentional approach to how their wardrobes evolve over time.”


Through the new program, customers can earn Lands’ End shopping credit for sending in gently-worn items. To participate, customers can generate a prepaid shipping label from[landsend.thredup.com](https://landsend.thredup.com/) , fill any shippable box or bag with apparel, shoes, and accessories from any brand in their closet, and ship it to ThredUp for free. For items that sell on ThredUp, customers receive Lands’ End credit that can be used both online and in-store.


For more information, please visit[landsend.thredup.com](https://landsend.thredup.com/) . To learn more about ThredUp's RaaS, visit[raas.thredup.com](http://raas.thredup.com/) .
