# Bed Threads Announces Clean Out Resale Program Enabled by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® — ThredUp Newsroom

**February 6, 2026 (Oakland, CA. and New York, New York** ) –[Bed Threads](https://bedthreads.com/) , an Australian brand who creates bedding and homewares for your dream space, today announced a Clean Out resale program that allows customers to resell gently-worn items for Bed Threads shopping credit. The program is powered by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® (RaaS®), which enables the world's leading fashion brands and retailers to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers. Bed Threads joins more than 50 other brands offering resale programs through RaaS, including J.Crew, Tommy Hilfiger, and Madewell.


“We’re proud to partner with ThredUp to continue building on the sustainability initiatives we’ve been passionate about since the start,” says Genevieve Rosen-Biller, co-founder of Bed Threads. “We’ve always wanted to create products and do business in a way that is better for us, and better for the environment, and supporting a more circular fashion economy is a meaningful step in the right direction. We’re excited for our community to take part in keeping wearable garments out of landfill through this partnership with ThredUp.”


Through the new program, customers can earn Bed Threads shopping credit for sending in gently-worn items. To participate, customers can generate a prepaid shipping label from[bedthreads.com/thredup](http://bedthreads.com/thredup) , fill any shippable box or bag with apparel, shoes, and accessories from any brand in their closet, and ship it to ThredUp for free. For items that sell on ThredUp, customers receive Bed Threads credit that can be used both online and in-store.


For more information, please visit[bedthreads.com/thredup](http://bedthreads.com/thredup) . To learn more about ThredUp's RaaS, visit[raas.thredup.com](http://raas.thredup.com/) .

Sellers love ThredUp because we make it easy to clean out their closets and unlock value for themselves or for the charity of their choice while doing good for the planet. Buyers love shopping value, premium and luxury brands all in one place, at up to 90% off estimated retail price.
