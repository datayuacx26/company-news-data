# Closet Factory Announces Clean Out Resale Program Enabled by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® — ThredUp Newsroom

**February 16, 2026 (Oakland, CA. and Los Angeles, CA)** –[Closet Factory](https://www.closetfactory.com/) , leading the industry in custom closet design, today announced a Clean Out resale program that allows customers to give their gently-worn items a second life while supporting charitable causes. For items that sell on ThredUp, Closet Factory donates the proceeds to[Dress for Success](https://dressforsuccess.org/) , a global nonprofit organization that empowers women to achieve economic independence by providing professional attire, career development tools, and ongoing support.


The program is powered by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® (RaaS®), which enables the world's leading fashion brands and retailers to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers. Closet Factory joins more than 50 other brands offering resale programs through RaaS, including J.Crew, Tommy Hilfiger, and Madewell.


“At Closet Factory, we help people rethink how their homes work for them. This partnership with ThredUp is a natural extension of that. When we design a new closet, we now have a simple, built-in way to help customers clear out what they no longer need and give those items a second life, rather than sending them to landfills. Just as important, this program supports organizations that help women entering the workforce take confident steps forward. It is a practical extension of what we do every day at Closet Factory, helping people create better, more intentional spaces, and we are proud to launch it with ThredUp,” said Jeff Henderson, President of Closet Factory President.


Through the new program, customers can easily declutter their closets while making a positive impact. To participate, customers can fill a Closet Factory x ThredUp Clean Out bag with apparel, shoes, and accessories from any brand in their closet, and ship it to ThredUp for free.


To learn more about ThredUp's RaaS, visit[raas.thredup.com](http://raas.thredup.com/) .


**About Closet Factory**


Closet Factory is a family-owned company with over 60 franchise locations throughout the United States and Canada. Closet Factory is a leader in custom-designed home organization solutions, including custom closets, garages, offices, pantries, and more. Closet Factory’s team of award-winning designers work with clients from start to finish on their new, custom-built closet or home organization system. Closet Factory’s philanthropic arm, Closet Factory Cares, works every day to generate positive impact for the communities Closet Factory serves. Closet Factory Care’s flagship program, Bags For Kids, works with local nonprofit partners to ensure foster children in their communities receive a high-quality bag to store their most prized possessions. Closet Factory Cares also participates in National Foster Care Awareness Month, Time for Kids, Season of Gratitude, and other community programs.

Sellers love ThredUp because we make it easy to clean out their closets and unlock value for themselves or for the charity of their choice while doing good for the planet. Buyers love shopping value, premium and luxury brands all in one place, at up to 90% off estimated retail price. Our proprietary operating platform is the foundation for our managed marketplace and consists of distributed processing infrastructure, proprietary software and systems and data science expertise. With ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service, some of the world's leading brands and retailers are leveraging our platform to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers. By extending the life cycle of clothing, ThredUp is changing the way consumers shop and ushering in a more sustainable future for the fashion industry.
