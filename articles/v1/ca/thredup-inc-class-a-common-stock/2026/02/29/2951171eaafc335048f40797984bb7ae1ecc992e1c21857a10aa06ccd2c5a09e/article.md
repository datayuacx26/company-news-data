# Misfits Market Announces Clean Out Resale Program Enabled by ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® — ThredUp Newsroom

**February 26, 2026 (Oakland, CA. and Delanco, NJ** ) –[Misfits Market](https://www.misfitsmarket.com/) , the mission-driven online grocery retailer dedicated to reducing food waste and building a more sustainable food system, today announced the launch of its Clean Out resale program. The program allows customers to resell gently worn clothing items in exchange for Misfits Market credit, extending the company’s commitment to waste reduction beyond the kitchen and into customers’ closets. Misfits Market joins more than 50 other brands offering resale programs through RaaS ThredUp’s Resale-as-a-Service® (RaaS®), which enables the world's leading fashion brands and retailers to deliver customizable, scalable resale experiences to their customers.


“We’re thrilled to be ThredUp’s first grocery partner and to welcome a new community of shoppers who want a more conscious way to enjoy great products without the waste,” said Holly Eagleson, SVP of Brand Marketing at Misfits Market. “By connecting our work in food rescue with their leadership in circular fashion, we’re making it simpler for everyone to support farmers and small makers and fund more efficient, less wasteful production. It’s a tangible way for customers to see their everyday spending create a measurable impact.”


To participate, customers can generate a prepaid shipping label from[misfitsmarket.thredup.com](http://misfitsmarket.thredup.com/) , fill any shippable box or bag with apparel, shoes, and accessories from any brand in their closet, and ship it to ThredUp for free. For items that sell on ThredUp, customers receive Misfits Market credit that can be applied toward their next order.


For more information, please visit[misfitsmarket.thredup.com](http://misfitsmarket.thredup.com/) . To learn more about ThredUp's RaaS, visit[raas.thredup.com](http://raas.thredup.com/) .


**About Misfits Market**


Misfits Market is the online grocer that delivers fresh, flavorful food and unexpected finds that help build a better food system. Serving hundreds of thousands of customers in nearly every ZIP code in the contiguous U.S., Misfits Market curates groceries for quality, nutrition, sustainability, and flavor, prioritizing impact over appearances. By partnering directly with farmers and small producers building a better food system, the company has built a first-of-its-kind food value supply chain to reduce waste and make shopping for high-quality groceries easier and more affordable. To date, Misfits Market has rescued more than 265 million pounds of food and raised $525 million in funding.
