# The Rails Foundation

**Physicians First** has been our mantra from day one. Over the last twelve years, we've leveraged[Ruby on Rails](https://rubyonrails.org/) to build the largest network of U.S. medical professionals. Our mission is to help physicians be more productive so they can provide better care for their patients. Ruby on Rails has enabled us to build products that provide doctors with modern telehealth, communication, workflow, and continuing medical education.


We love building innovative technology that has a real, meaningful impact on physicians’ and patients’ lives. We will continue investing and building products to help make medical professionals more effective. To us, the Ruby on Rails framework and its ecosystem is still the best tool for the job.


**We're happy to announce that we're joining the[Rails Foundation](https://rubyonrails.org/2022/11/14/the-rails-foundation) as one of eight founding core members.** We look forward to contributing to this non-profit organization and providing important resources to help ensure the Ruby on Rails ecosystem remains healthy and continues to thrive for decades to come.

vp, eng
