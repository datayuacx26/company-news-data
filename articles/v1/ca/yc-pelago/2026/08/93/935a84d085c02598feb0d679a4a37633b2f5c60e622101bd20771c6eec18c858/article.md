# AI is already treating mental health. Now what?

#### **Our solution: Sona by Pelago**


Behavioral healthcare works best when support matches each person’s needs. That’s the principle behind Sona, a voice-first AI mental health specialist that helps people access the right care at the right time.


****


Sona helps people before challenges become crises. Rather than offering generic conversations or static programs, Sona builds an understanding of each member over time, remembers previous interactions, and adapts support based on changing needs.


From the very first conversation, Sona works with members to create a personalized plan instead of relying on pre-built templates or decision trees. It continuously assesses changes in symptoms, goals, and risk, helping people access the right level of support at the right time. When human care is needed or requested, Pelago clinicians remain in the loop and can step in seamlessly.


#### **A clinical architecture for AI-guided care**


Sona was designed specifically for mental healthcare, unlike general-purpose AI.


It serves as an AI front door that continuously assesses acuity, context, and comorbidities to help guide each member’s care journey. Behind the scenes, Sona runs on Pelago’s Agentic Clinical Intelligence, a system of specialized AI agents trained for specific clinical tasks such as behavioral analysis, evidence retrieval, care navigation, and member support.


For members who need higher-acuity care, Sona helps match them with the most appropriate in-network clinician based on their clinical needs, preferences, availability, and scheduling constraints. Human clinicians oversee escalations, supervise care decisions, and remain responsible for clinical judgment, ensuring members receive the right care while maintaining safety throughout the experience.


#### **Safe by design**


With AI use rising and the mental health provider shortage growing, ChatGPT may now be one of the largest sources of mental health support in the country. The problem is that generic chatbots are unsafe and unsuitable mental healthcare providers. They were initially trained to inform users, not influence them, challenge them, or guide them through care.
