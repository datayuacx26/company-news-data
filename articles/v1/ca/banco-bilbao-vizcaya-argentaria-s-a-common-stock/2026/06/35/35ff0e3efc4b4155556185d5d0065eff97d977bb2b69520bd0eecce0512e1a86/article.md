# Garanti BBVA Celebrates Its 80th Anniversary with New Exhibition

## Eight decades of banking innovation


Over its 80-year history, Garanti BBVA has introduced a number of innovations that have helped shape Türkiye's banking sector. The bank has played a pioneering role in areas ranging from **real-time online banking** infrastructure and internet banking to virtual POS systems, the Bonus payment platform, contactless payment technologies **and AI-supported applications** . Many services that are now standard across the industry were first developed through initiatives introduced by Garanti BBVA.


## Preserving a shared heritage


The bank's approach to creating value extends beyond financial services. Through initiatives spanning culture and the arts, education,[gender equality](https://www.bbva.com/es/tr/innovacion/techstars-startup-weekend-women-istanbul-concluye-con-garanti-bbva-como-patrocinador-principal/) and[environmental sustainability](https://www.bbva.com/en/sustainability/garanti-bbva-wins-two-international-awards-for-turkiyes-first-biodiversity-bond/) , Garanti BBVA has maintained a long-standing **commitment to supporting broader social development** .


An important part of that commitment is the preservation of institutional and cultural memory through its collaboration with Salt. Since 2023, a comprehensive archival project has catalogued and digitised more than 36,000 physical materials, including photographs, posters, reports, periodicals, films, brochures, advertisements and historical objects. The inventory of approximately 255,000 digital materials has also been completed.


Beginning in 2026, the first section of the Garanti BBVA Archive, comprising 10,000 documents as well as interviews documenting institutional memory, will be made **publicly accessible through Salt Research** . Developed with Salt's expertise in archiving and research, the project aims not only to preserve the bank's corporate history but also to make visible important aspects of Türkiye's economic and social transformation over the past eight decades.


## Looking ahead


With *'* We’ve Been in the Future for 80 Years', Garanti BBVA is marking a significant milestone in its history while **creating new ways to preserve and share its legacy** . Through the exhibition, anniversary initiatives and archival partnership with Salt, the bank is ensuring that its accumulated knowledge, experiences and stories remain accessible to future generations.
