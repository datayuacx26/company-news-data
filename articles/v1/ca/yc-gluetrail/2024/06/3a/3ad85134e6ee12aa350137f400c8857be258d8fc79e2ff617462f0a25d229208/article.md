###


Pro Tips for Using Gluetrail's Video Editor


**Accessing the Online Video Editor** To start, click on 'Create Video' after recording your content with Glutrail. This will take you to the online video editor. Here, you can see the video track, which serves as the timeline for your video. Hovering over the video track allows you to preview specific moments of your recording. **Adding Effects and Visuals** - **Video Track Effects** : add various effects to your video track. Select the specific moments you want to enhance, then click on your selection to add effects like zoom, skip section, decelerate, or accelerate. - **Zoom Effect** : to add a zoom effect, click on the magnifier icon. This allows you to zoom into a specific area during the selected seconds. Preview the zoom effect to ensure it meets your expectations. **Editing the Audio Track** - **Original Voice or AI Voice:** keep your original voice recording or use an AI voice. The AI voice feature is particularly powerful as it allows you to edit the transcript of your video. You can remove filler words, correct errors, and add more context. - **Generating AI Voice:** After editing the transcript, save the updates and generate the AI voice. While the voice auto syncs with the video, you may need to adjust the video speed to ensure the voice aligns perfectly with the visuals. **Additional Customizations** - **Background Color** : add a background with a specific color to make your video more visually appealing. - **Speaker Bubble Position** : If you recorded your camera during the recording, change the position of the speaker bubble to a specific corner of the screen. **Finalizing and Exporting Your Video** Once you are satisfied with your edits, click on 'Save Updates' and then 'Generate Video' to create the final version of your video. The generation process may take a few minutes, depending on the number of effects added. After generation, proceed to the Export section to export your video and article.
