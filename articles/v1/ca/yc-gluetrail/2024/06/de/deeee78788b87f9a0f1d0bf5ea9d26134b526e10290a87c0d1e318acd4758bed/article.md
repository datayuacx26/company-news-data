##


Access Ashby's Migration Guides


⭐


Who is this for? This tutorial is for users looking to migrate from their current Applicant Tracking System (ATS) to Ashby's all-in-one recruiting platform.


In this tutorial, you will learn how to find and access Ashby's migration guides to help you migrate from your current ATS to Ashby's all-in-one recruiting platform. Starting from Ashby's homepage, you will navigate to the resource section, access the migration guides, and find detailed instructions for migrating from specific ATS platforms like Lever to Ashby. If you have any questions, you can reach out to Ashby's support team.

###


Exploring Ashby's Platform


####


Start from the Ashby's website home page


###


Accessing Migration Guides


####


Go to Migrating to Ashby


Click on the 'Migrating to Ashby' button in the Resources section to access the migration guides.


####


Scroll down to the FAQ Section


####


Access Migration Guides


Click on the 'See what data is imported' button in the FAQ section. This will direct you to the migration guides from Ashby Academy, where you can select the relevant article for your current ATS.


###


Selecting Relevant Migration Guide


####


Select Migration Guide


Click on the relevant migration guide for your previous ATS to access detailed instructions on migrating to Ashby.


---


##


What's next?


After finishing this tutorial, users should start the migration process by following the detailed instructions in the relevant migration guide. If any issues arise, they should contact Ashby's support team for assistance. Additionally, users can explore other resources and training materials available on Ashby's website to fully utilize the platform's features.
