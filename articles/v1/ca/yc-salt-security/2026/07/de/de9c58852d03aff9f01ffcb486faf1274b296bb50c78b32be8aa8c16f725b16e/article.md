# Before and After: Meet the New Salt

Every mature product reaches a moment where adding another feature isn't enough anymore. At some point, you have to rethink the experience itself.


That's what Salt V2 is. A year of work, with one goal behind every change: helping security teams trust what they see, faster.


Here it is, in four before-and-afters.


## 1. See what your AI agents can actually do.


An interactive map of every agent, every MCP, every capability, and every API they can touch. Hover on any node and the full risk picture opens up. For many teams, this changes how they investigate AI risk.


## 2. Understand risk instantly.


Your inventory, redesigned to read at a glance. Risk scores, risk factors, and sources up front, and any API findable in seconds.


## 3. Stop searching. Start asking.


AI shouldn't be another place you visit. It should already know where you are. That's why Pepper now lives inside the experience instead of beside it, one click from any API, attacker, or gap. And it doesn't stop at answers: open a posture gap and Pepper writes the fix, down to ready-to-paste policy code for your gateway.


## 4. Find anything.


40% fewer main screens. One clear hierarchy. New filters everywhere. Everything you need is now two clicks away.


## Trusted by the people who tested it first


During the beta, every user kept a one-click way back to the old UI. 75% switched and never looked back.


We believe great enterprise software should disappear behind the work people are trying to do. Salt V2 is our biggest step toward that goal. It's live today for every customer, and we can't wait for you to try it.[Request a demo today](https://salt.security/demo-request) .
