# Vooma Cover - From calls to emails, we've got you covered

Vooma Cover isn’t just about phone calls anymore. It’s becoming **your all-around coverage assistant** . What started as an AI assistant for handling carrier calls now extends to **email bids, after-hours support, and deeper integrations with the tools you already use** . With **new insights and shipment views** , Vooma Cover helps your team save time, work smarter, and cover more loads without adding extra stress.


### **Email-based cover**


Vooma Cover isn’t just for phone calls anymore! It now helps you **handle the flood of email bids** coming in every day. With the same flexibility for setting negotiation rules and strategies, Vooma Cover **takes care of these requests automatically** so your team can focus on higher-value work.


Negotiate via email


### **After-hours posting and transfers**


Freight doesn’t stop after 5 p.m. – and neither does **Vooma Cover** . You can **set it up to run live, or simply collect bids overnight** so you come in the next morning with **offers already waiting** . And when a call needs to be transferred, our agent can “whisper” details to your team, making it easy to capture and log the handoff.


### **Voice and personality personalization**


Your assistant doesn’t have to sound like everyone else’s. With Vooma Cover, you can **pick how your assistant sounds** . Or work with us to build a **custom voice and personality** . Need it to sound buttoned-up and professional? Easy. Prefer something **friendlier and more relaxed** ? Done.


### **Integrations**


Vooma Cover isn’t another system to manage. It **ties into the tools you already rely on** :


- **Integrate directly with Highway and more**
- Connect with **load boards like DAT** for posting
- Work with **Slack, Google Chat, and Teams** for team notifications – for individuals or for a broader group


### **End-to-end cover**


With **End-to-end Cover** , Vooma’s **voice agent fully negotiates rates and issues rate confirmations** —all with your **preferred, fully vetted carriers.**


- **Negotiate autonomously** to secure the best rates
- **Issue rate cons automatically** once terms are set
- Start with 3PL and PDF-based support (with broader expansion coming soon)


This makes it possible to move from quote to covered load without lifting a finger.


### **… and much more!**


- Insights: get real-time visibility into call activity, including volumes and trends
- Shipments: see all your shipments in one place, with quick posting to load boards.
- Daily reports: stay on top of activity with email digests sent straight to your inbox (customers are already loving the added visibility!)
