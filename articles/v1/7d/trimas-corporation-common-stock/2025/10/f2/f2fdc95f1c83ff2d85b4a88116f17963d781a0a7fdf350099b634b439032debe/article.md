# Beautyworld Middle East 2025: TriMas Packaging to Exhibit Latest Innovations

## **Showcasing innovative, sustainable and premium packaging solutions for the beauty industry**


TriMas Packaging is excited to announce its participation at[Beautyworld Middle East 2025](https://beautyworld-middle-east.ae.messefrankfurt.com/dubai/en.html) , the region’s largest international trade fair for beauty, fragrance and wellness. The event takes place October 27–29 at the Dubai World Trade Centre, where TriMas Packaging will exhibit at Booth #8-B21.


Bringing together top brands, innovators and industry professionals, **Beautyworld Middle East** is the ideal stage to explore the future of beauty. This year, TriMas Packaging will showcase its latest packaging solutions for fragrance, beauty and personal care, with a strong focus on **premium design, performance and sustainability** .


Highlights include our[European-made fragrance pumps](https://trimaspackaging.com/news/luxury-fragrance-pumps-eu-partnering-zz/) and closures, featuring **new crimp-on, screw-on and snap-on pumps with advanced no-metal-contact technology** . These systems deliver the perfect blend of luxury and functionality, meeting the growing demand for high-end, customizable fragrance packaging. Visitors can also explore our **premium closures** , recognized for their **signature finishes and sophisticated decoration techniques** that enhance shelf presence and brand appeal.


Beyond fragrance, the exhibit will feature our **complete portfolio of dispensing and closure solutions** —from lotion, foaming and treatment pumps to airless systems, fine mist sprayers and color cosmetic packaging—all designed to **deliver exceptional product performance and an elevated consumer experience** . Our[fully recyclable, all-plastic Singolo™ dispensing range](https://trimaspackaging.com/news/recyclable-foaming-dispenser-pump/) will also be showcased, offering sustainable design and versatile functionality for today’s beauty brands.


### **Meet our TriMas Packaging team at Booth #8-B21 to see firsthand how our innovative, sustainable and future-focused packaging solutions can bring your brand vision to life.**
