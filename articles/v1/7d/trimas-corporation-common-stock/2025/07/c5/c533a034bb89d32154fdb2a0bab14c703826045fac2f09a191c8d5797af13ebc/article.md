# TriMas releases 2024 Sustainability Report

Our parent company, TriMas Corporation, has published its[2024 Sustainability Report](https://trimas.com/images/Sustainability-Report2024.pdf) , demonstrating the Company’s commitment to transparency by providing a comprehensive overview of key progress and achievements throughout the year. This report reflects TriMas’ belief that **sustainability is integral to long-term value creation, innovation and operational excellence** .


## Advancing ESG initiatives for a better future


The 2024 Sustainability Report highlights TriMas’ ongoing commitment to advancing responsible environmental, social and governance (ESG) practices across its global operations. Centered around four core pillars — **Governance & Ethics, People, Environment and Products** — the report details initiatives that integrate sustainability into every aspect of the Company’s strategy. Through enhanced disclosures, measurable performance targets and continuous improvement efforts, TriMas is dedicated to **building a more sustainable and resilient future for its customers, employees, stakeholders and the communities where it operates** .


### Sustainability progress


As global expectations around sustainability evolve, so too must our approach to leadership and long-term value creation. At TriMas, we recognize that environmental and social responsibility are not separate from business performance — they are essential to it. With that mindset, we are pleased to introduce our 2024 Sustainability Report, highlighting our continued commitment and progress, as well as the steps we are taking to build a more sustainable and responsible future.


TriMas has implemented systems to measure environmental performance, including **Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions, energy and water usage, air emissions and waste management** . In 2024, we received our first scores from the CDP, providing detailed insights into our climate-related strategies and laying the roadmap for future improvements. We also continue to advance our alignment with global frameworks such as the Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) and the[United Nations Global Compact](https://unglobalcompact.org/) (UNGC), while enhancing our environmental and social metrics to increase transparency. We are encouraged by the momentum we are building and are even more energized by the path ahead as we work toward a more sustainable future.
