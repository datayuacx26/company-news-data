# TriMas Packaging at Drinktec 2025: Beverage Caps & Closures

## A wide range of smart packaging solutions for the food and beverage market


TriMas Packaging is excited to announce our debut at **[Drinktec](https://www.drinktec.com/en-US/home/) , the world’s leading trade fair for the beverage and liquid food industry** , held only once every four years. From September 15–19, we’ll be showcasing our latest packaging solutions at Booth #424 in Hall C6 at the Trade Fair Center Messe München in Munich, Germany.


As the global stage for breakthrough technologies, **Drinktec** provides the ideal platform to present our comprehensive portfolio of caps, closures, dispensing systems and bag-in-box solutions, **each designed to deliver enhanced performance, safety and sustainability for the global food and beverage market** .


## Explore our solutions for beverages and liquid foods


### Caps


From water and juice to tea, energy drinks and dairy, **our versatile cap designs ensure freshness, safety and convenience** with every sip. Discover our advanced **tethered beverage caps, available in both sports and flat styles** , as well as customizable finishes. For a more sustainable option, we also offer lightweight designs that reduce material usage without compromising performance.


### Closures


Our premium closures combine product protection with shelf appeal. Featuring customizable finishes, including metallization, these closures are **designed to protect product integrity while elevating your brand’s shelf appeal** .


### Dispensing pumps


Perfect for syrups, sauces and liquids of any viscosity, our dispensing solutions ensure hygienic handling and precision control. Each pump is engineered to deliver consistent, accurate dosing, **improving both the consumer experience and operational efficiency** .


### Bag-in-box systems


By combining flexible bags with high-performance filling machines, our bag-in-box technology offers a **sustainable packaging solution for beverages and liquid foods** . Ideal for post-mix syrup, wine, water, dairy and sauces, these systems extend shelf life, minimize waste and simplify dispensing to keep products fresh while streamlining operations.


## **Spotlight on innovation – New sustainable beverage caps**


At Drinktec 2025, TriMas Packaging will introduce our latest innovations, including the **GME 30.40-compatible** lightweight, aseptic two-piece flip-top sports cap. Engineered to advance performance, support sustainability goals and enhance the end-user experience, this cap exemplifies our commitment to smarter, greener packaging solutions.
