# TriMas Packaging exhibits at LUXE PACK Monaco 2025

## **Highlighting high-performance dispensing and closure solutions for the beauty and personal care markets**


TriMas Packaging is proud to once again exhibit at[LUXE PACK Monaco](https://www.luxepackmonaco.com/en/) **, the premier global event for luxury packaging** , taking place from September 29–October 1 at the Grimaldi Forum, Monaco. Visit us at Booth #RD10 in the Ravel Hall to explore our latest innovations for fragrance, beauty and personal care.


As the leading platform for creativity and cutting-edge luxury packaging solutions, LUXE PACK Monaco provides the ideal stage to showcase our extensive portfolio of dispensing and closure solutions, **designed to deliver exceptional quality, design and user satisfaction** .


## **Explore our latest innovations for luxury beauty brands**


### **Fragrance Pumps**


Designed for fine perfumes, colognes and delicate mists, our[fragrance pumps](https://trimaspackaging.com/news/luxury-fragrance-pumps-eu-partnering-zz/) combine sophisticated style with reliable performance. Available in **crimp-on, screw-on and snap-on variations** , each pump features a **no-metal-contact design to protect sensitive formulations** . Fully customizable, they offer an array of colors, beads, and logo embossing options to **elevate brand identity and create a premium luxury experience** .


### Closures


Our premium closures for perfumery and spirits provide protection with elegant presentation. With **signature finishes, advanced decoration techniques and fully customizable designs** , they safeguard product integrity while enhancing the visual impact of your brand on the shelf.


### **Singolo™ All-Plastic Range**


The **fully recyclable, all-plastic Singolo™ dispensing range** is engineered for precision and effortless product delivery. Featuring **2cc and 4cc lotion pumps,[foaming pumps](https://trimaspackaging.com/news/recyclable-foaming-dispenser-pump/) , treatment pumps and fine mist sprayers** , Singolo combines high performance with sustainable materials and fully customizable options. Designed for beauty and personal care brands, it elevates both aesthetics and user experience, offering packaging solutions that perform reliably while protecting the planet.


The showcase will also feature standard lotion pumps, airless systems, sprayers and triggers, and cosmetic packaging designed to combine premium performance with refined design, reflecting the high standards of the beauty and personal care market.


### Visit Booth #RD10 in the Ravel Hall to meet the TriMas Packaging team, explore our latest innovations, and discover how we are shaping the future of luxury packaging for beauty and personal care brands worldwide.
