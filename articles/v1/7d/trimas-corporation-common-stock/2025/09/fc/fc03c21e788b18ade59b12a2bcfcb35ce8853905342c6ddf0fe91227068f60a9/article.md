# TriMas Packaging and Z&Z launch luxury fragrance pumps in EU

## Expanding luxury fragrance pump innovation for Europe’s beauty market


TriMas Packaging is partnering with **Z&Z Company** to introduce a new line of **high-performance fragrance pumps** across the European market. This partnership leverages TriMas Packaging’s advanced manufacturing capabilities at its[Waalwijk facility in the Netherlands](http://www.aartspackaging.com/) , renowned for producing luxury perfume caps.


> “Partnering with Z&Z Company represents a significant step forward in our growth strategy for the European beauty sector,” *said Fabio Salik, President, TriMas Packaging.* “Together, we are delivering fragrance pumps that meet the **highest standards of quality, performance and design, while supporting the sustainability and speed to market objectives of our customers** .”


### Localized production in Europe: sustainable supply for high‑precision luxury fragrance pumps


Localized production enables **shorter lead times, greater responsiveness to customer needs and a reduced environmental footprint through optimized logistics** . European beauty brands can now access a comprehensive range of crimp-on, snap-on and screw-on fragrance pumps designed for consistent performance and optimal functionality, while preserving the integrity of luxury formulations.


TriMas Packaging will showcase the new fragrance pump line at[LUXE PACK Monaco](https://www.luxepackmonaco.com/en/) , taking place September 29 through October 1, alongside its full portfolio of innovative and sustainable dispensing and closure solutions for beauty, personal care and cosmetic applications.


### About Z&Z Company


The Z&Z Group, comprised of Zhejiang Z&Z Industrial Co., Ltd. and Ningbo Z&Z New Materials Co., Ltd., provides one-stop packaging solutions for the cleansing, skincare, color cosmetics and fragrance sectors. Guided by the mission to drive innovation and sustainable development, Z&Z combines technological expertise, integrated manufacturing and refined aesthetics to **deliver high-quality, environmentally responsible packaging** . With a commitment to excellence, safety and smart manufacturing, Z&Z strives to be the trusted partner for globally renowned beauty brands, while advancing the packaging industry through continuous innovation.
