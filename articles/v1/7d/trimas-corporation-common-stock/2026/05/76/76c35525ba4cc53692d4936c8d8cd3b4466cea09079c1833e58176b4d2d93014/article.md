# TriMas Packaging Refill Pod Jar Wins A' Design Award

## **Recognizing innovation in sustainable packaging design**


TriMas Packaging has been named a winner of the **Iron A’ Design Award** in the *Sustainable Products, Projects and Green Design* category for its innovative **Refill Pod Jar concept** .


The[A’ Design Award & Competition](https://competition.adesignaward.com/) is a globally recognized, juried program that celebrates excellence and innovation across industries. This recognition highlights TriMas Packaging’s **strong design capabilities and ongoing commitment to developing sustainable, forward-thinking packaging solutions** .


### **Designed for the future of sustainable packaging**


The Refill Pod Jar was developed in response to the **growing demand for refillable packaging** , as both consumers and regulatory bodies increasingly prioritize sustainability. While refill systems continue to gain traction, many existing designs present challenges, including limited branding opportunities and reduced shelf appeal, as well as multi-step, less convenient user experiences.


The TriMas Packaging Refill Pod Jar addresses these challenges through a **thoughtful, user-centric design that delivers functionality, convenience, premium aesthetics and sustainability** .


### **Key features and benefits**


This versatile sustainable and refillable packaging solution is suitable for a range of applications, including creams, balms, gummies and vitamins. Key features include:


- Flip-top pod lid for easy, one-handed access
- Top-access format that eliminates the need to remove the main cap
- Tethered lid that remains attached, reducing waste and improving usability
- Tamper-evident ring for added safety without additional components
- Enhanced branding space to support strong shelf presence and product differentiation


The refill system allows users to easily replace empty pods by opening the main jar, removing the used pod and inserting a new one—providing **a simple, convenient refill experience that encourages reuse** and supports **long-term sustainability goals** .


### **Thoughtful design and development**


The Refill Pod Jar concept reflects insights from evolving global regulations, including the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), as well as increasing consumer demand for environmentally responsible packaging solutions.


The design addresses key technical considerations, including achieving **leak-proof and tamper-evident sealing** , ensuring the refill pod is securely housed without movement and selecting materials that balance durability with recyclability.


The system features a **three-part design consisting of the cap, refill pod and outer jar** . The cap and refill pod, including the integrated flip-top lid, are made from polypropylene (PP), while the outer jar is designed in either PP or PET. A mono-material PP option is also being explored to further enhance recyclability and support circular design goals.


### Advancing sustainable innovation in packaging


This award reinforces TriMas Packaging’s commitment to delivering innovative, sustainable packaging solutions that align with evolving regulatory landscapes and consumer preferences. The Refill Pod Jar represents a **next-generation approach to sustainable, refillable packaging design** , combining convenience, functionality and premium aesthetics.
