# TriMas Packaging to exhibit at LUXE PACK New York 2026

## Experience packaging innovation that performs across fragrance, beauty and personal care


TriMas Packaging is excited to once again exhibit at[LUXE PACK New York](https://www.luxepacknewyork.com/) , where we will showcase our latest dispensing and closure innovations for the fragrance, beauty and personal care markets.


### Event Details


- **Date** : May 6–7, 2026
- **Location** : Javits Center, New York City
- **Booth** : #C23


At LUXE PACK New York, attendees will have the opportunity to experience the breadth of our global portfolio—engineered to balance performance, customization and sustainability for prestige brands. Through live demonstrations, visitors can see our technologies in action and connect directly with our experts to discuss applications, finishes and next‑step development.


Highlights will include our **European‑made fragrance solutions** designed for precision, consistency and elegant actuation; **design‑driven beauty packaging** that pairs premium aesthetics with responsible material choices; and our expanding **Singolo™ all‑plastic, fully recyclable portfolio** , built for reliable everyday performance in beauty and personal care applications.


From custom projects to scalable dispensing solutions, TriMas Packaging continues to partner with brands to bring innovative concepts to life, supporting elevated consumer experiences while advancing sustainability goals.


### Visit us at **Booth C23** to explore what’s next in fragrance, beauty and personal care packaging—and experience how TriMas Packaging delivers innovation that performs.
