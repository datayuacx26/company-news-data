# Introducing the New Era of TriMas Packaging | One Global Brand

## One global brand bringing together our capabilities, expertise and solutions to deliver greater value and support for our customers worldwide


We are excited to share an important milestone in the evolution of TriMas Packaging. As part of our **One Brand initiative** , we are **bringing our global businesses together under a single, unified TriMas Packaging brand** .


While our legacy brands—including Rieke®, Affaba & Ferrari™, Taplast™, Plastic Srl and Aarts Packaging—have built strong reputations over many years, this evolution reflects the combined strength of our global organization. By uniting under one brand, we are creating a more connected business that delivers an even better experience for our customers and is designed to better serve you today and into the future.


### What This Means for You


Our unified brand allows us to provide greater value through:


- **A Broader, Integrated Product Portfolio –** Access to our full range of dispensing, closure, flexible packaging and custom solutions across industries and regions through one global organization.
- **One Point of Contact –** Our Sales organization has been aligned so you now have a single point of contact across our full portfolio, simplifying the way you do business with TriMas Packaging.
- **Expanded Capabilities –** With the combined expertise of our engineering, manufacturing and commercial teams, we can bring innovative solutions to market faster and more efficiently.
- **A Simplified Customer Experience –** A unified brand creates greater consistency across our communications, products and digital platforms, making it easier to do business with us.
- **An Even Stronger Partnership** – Increased collaboration across our global teams enables us to better anticipate your needs and deliver solutions that help your business succeed.


### **What’s Changing**


As we transition to One TriMas Packaging, you will begin to see several updates, including:


- **Legal entity names** are transitioning to TriMas Packaging. We will communicate as changes occur to ensure that your customer records, databases and systems are updated accordingly with the new name.
- **Websites and digital platforms** are being consolidated into a unified TriMas Packaging experience.
- **Social media channels** will be transitioning to a single TriMas Packaging presence.
- **Branding and visuals** will reflect our new, cohesive identity across communications, packaging and marketing materials.


These changes are intended to provide a more consistent and seamless experience across every interaction with us.


### What’s Not Changing


While our brand is evolving, our commitment to you remains exactly the same. You can continue to expect:


- Exceptional customer service
- High-quality, reliable products
- The trusted relationships you’ve built with our teams
- The expertise, innovation and technical support you rely on


### Looking Ahead


This is more than a brand update—it’s an investment in the future of our business and our partnership with you. By bringing our global capabilities together under one brand, we are better positioned to deliver **broader solutions, greater innovation and an even stronger customer experience** .


We’re excited about what this means for you and look forward to continuing to support your success under the unified TriMas Packaging brand.


If you have any questions or would like to learn more, please reach out to your sales contact.


**Thank you for your continued trust and partnership. We look forward to serving you as One TriMas Packaging.**
