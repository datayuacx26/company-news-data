# TriMas Packaging exhibits at Paris Packaging Week 2026

## **Experience innovation in fragrance, beauty and personal care packaging**


TriMas Packaging is excited to be exhibiting once again at[Paris Packaging Week 2026](https://www.parispackagingweek.com/en/) , taking place from February 5–6 at the Paris Expo Porte de Versailles. Join us at **Stand Q62** in the **PCD showcase** to explore our latest packaging innovations in **fragrance** , **beauty** and **personal care** , and meet our experts to discuss how we can support your next project.


As a leading platform for innovation in perfume, cosmetics and design, PCD Paris provides the ideal stage to showcase our broad portfolio of dispensing and closure solutions, developed to balance performance, customization and sustainability.


## **See the future of packaging in action**


### **Fragrance: Precision, Made in Europe**


Our integrated European-made fragrance portfolio is engineered for performance, aesthetics and consistency. Discover new[high-quality fragrance pumps](https://trimaspackaging.com/news/luxury-fragrance-pumps-eu-partnering-zz/) with **precise actuation** , premium **custom closures** , and **travel-friendly formats** that maintain elegance and reliable dispensing on the go.


### **Beauty: Elevate Your Brand**


From skincare to color cosmetics, our solutions combine **style, functionality and sustainability** . Explore **European-made compacts** , **lip gloss** and **nail polish packaging** featuring metallization and decorative finishes, along with our fully recyclable **0.25cc treatment pump** designed for precise, sustainable beauty dispensing.


### **Personal Care: Built for Everyday Performance**


Our **fully recyclable Singolo™ range** includes **lotion pumps (2cc and 4cc)** , **foamers (0.8–1.5ml)** and **fine-mist sprayers (130 µl)** , all engineered for reliable, controlled dispensing. Designed for everyday use, these solutions deliver consistent dosing, dependable performance and enhanced sustainability for personal care products.


### Test the Innovation, Live


Join us for live demonstrations of our newest fragrance pumps, dispensing systems and sustainable packaging solutions. Test performance in real time and speak directly with our team about applications, finishes, customization options and next steps. Discover how TriMas Packaging can bring your next beauty or personal care concept to life.


## Visit **Stand Q62** to experience how TriMas Packaging delivers **performance, sustainability and design excellence** across fragrance, beauty and personal care.
