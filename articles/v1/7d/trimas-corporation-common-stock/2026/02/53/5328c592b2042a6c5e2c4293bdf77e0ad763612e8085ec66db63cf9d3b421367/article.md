# We are upgrading to a new ERP system!

## We are upgrading our Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system across all global TriMas Packaging sites


As part of our ongoing commitment to enhancing service and operational excellence, we are pleased to announce the upgrade of our Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system across all TriMas Packaging sites worldwide.


### Key Details


#### Atkins, Arkansas (USA) Go-Live:


- **Go-Live Date:** July 1, 2025 (Completed)
- **Location:** Atkins facility in Arkansas, USA – specializing in the manufacture of food caps
- **Contact:**AuburnCustomerCare@trimas.com


#### Leicester, United Kingdom Go-Live:


- **Go-Live Date:** September 1, 2025 (Completed)
- **Location:** Leicester facility in the United Kingdom – specializing in the manufacture of dispensing pumps and airless systems
- **Contact:**LeicesterCustomerCare@trimas.com


#### New Albany, Ohio Go-Live:


- **Go-Live Date:** March 1, 2026
- **Location:** New Albany facility in Ohio, USA – specializing in the manufacture of dispensing pumps and sprayers
- **Contact:**NewAlbanyCustomerCare@trimas.com


### **Future Implementations**


All other locations are scheduled for implementation in 2026-2028.


### Why This Matters


The new ERP system will deliver a range of benefits, including improved data accuracy, stronger integration across our global network, and enhanced efficiency in our processes. This upgrade represents a significant step forward in our efforts to deliver superior service to our valued customers.
