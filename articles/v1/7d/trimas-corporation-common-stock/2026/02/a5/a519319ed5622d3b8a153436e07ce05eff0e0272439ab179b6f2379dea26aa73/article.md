# TriMas Packaging at LUXE PACK Los Angeles 2026: Innovation & Sustainability

## Advancing high-performance, sustainable packaging solutions for beauty and personal care brands


TriMas Packaging is pleased to once again participate in[LUXE PACK Los Angeles](https://www.luxepacklosangeles.com/) , where we will present our latest innovations for the global fragrance, beauty and personal care markets. Over the two-day event, attendees will have the opportunity to explore our newest dispensing and closure technologies, with a special focus on our **Singolo™ all-plastic, fully recyclable portfolio** , designed to deliver exceptional performance, enhanced recyclability and premium aesthetics.


### Event Details


- **Date** : March 4–5, 2026
- **Location** : L.A. Convention Center, Los Angeles, California
- **Booth** : #B27


This year’s exhibit will showcase the full scope of our dispensing and closures capabilities, including lotion pumps, foamer pumps, airless systems, treatment pumps, sprayers, fragrance pumps, closures and jars. Visitors will also be able to experience our latest Singolo all-plastic innovations firsthand, including 2cc and 4cc lotion pumps, Pro-Line pump, foamer pump, fine mist sprayer, new treatment pump and next-generation 1cc airless systems.


Our portfolio reflects our commitment to delivering packaging that meets the **highest standards of performance, quality and sustainability** . We will also highlight prestige custom projects that demonstrate the depth of our design, engineering and manufacturing capabilities for both luxury and mass-market brands.


TriMas Packaging remains a **trusted innovation partner** for beauty and personal care companies worldwide. We look forward to engaging with brands, designers and industry professionals at LUXE PACK Los Angeles and showcasing how our technologies enhance consumer experiences while supporting a more sustainable future.


### Visit **us at Booth #B27 to connect with our team and discover how our forward-thinking packaging solutions can bring your next project to life.**
