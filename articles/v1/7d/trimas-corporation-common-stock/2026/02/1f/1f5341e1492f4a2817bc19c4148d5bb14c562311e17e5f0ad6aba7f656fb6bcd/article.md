# TriMas Packaging Exhibit at Automechanika New Delhi 2026

## **Showcasing secure, tamper-evident and high-performance packaging solutions for automotive fluids**


TriMas Packaging is pleased to announce its participation at[ACMA Automechanika New Delhi](https://acma-automechanika-newdelhi.in.messefrankfurt.com/newdelhi/en.html) , India’s leading trade fair for the auto component and automotive aftermarket industry. From February 5–7, our team will showcase the latest **automotive fluid packaging solutions** at **Booth #1.G91** at Yashobhoomi, the India International Convention and Expo Center (IICC), Dwarka, Delhi.


TriMas Packaging will highlight its comprehensive range of **durable, precision-engineered caps, closures and dispensing systems** , designed to meet the demanding requirements of automotive fluids, including lubricants, additives, chemicals and cleaners.


### **Product Highlights**


Visitors can explore a broad selection of packaging solutions, including:


- Pull-out spout closures
- Screw caps
- Press-fit spout closures
- FLEXSPOUT closures for larger drums
- Dispensing systems
- Bag-in-box flexible packaging
- Fully customizable designs


Each solution is engineered for precision, durability, chemical resistance, accurate dosing and leak prevention, helping ensure fluid integrity while enhancing safety, efficiency and confidence across automotive applications.


### **Tamper-Evident & Anti-Counterfeiting Innovation**


A key focus of the exhibit will be our advanced tamper-evident and anti-counterfeiting technologies for the lubricant oil segment. Featured products include the SK40MM tamper-evident closure and our latest development, the SK38MM. Both offer lightweight designs with reduced plastic usage, delivering cost efficiency and sustainability benefits while maintaining secure, tamper-evident functionality that helps safeguard product integrity and brand reputation.


With a **global manufacturing footprint** and **expert local support** , TriMas Packaging can respond quickly to customer needs while delivering consistent quality and reliable performance across applications.


### Meet the TriMas Packaging team at **Booth #1.G91** to explore secure, market-ready packaging solutions and learn how we support the evolving needs of the automotive industry through innovation, performance and product protection.
