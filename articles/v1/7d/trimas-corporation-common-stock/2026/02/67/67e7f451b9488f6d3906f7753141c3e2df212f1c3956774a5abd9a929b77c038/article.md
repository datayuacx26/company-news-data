# TriMas Packaging achieves EcoVadis Gold for third year

## Recognized for consistent sustainability commitments, practices and performance under EcoVadis’ global framework


TriMas Packaging is proud to announce that our U.S.-based operations have once again achieved an **EcoVadis Gold sustainability rating** , marking the third consecutive year of this prestigious recognition.


Assessed under the legal entity name Rieke LLC, our U.S. facilities maintained Gold for their ongoing efforts and continuous improvement in sustainable practices. This achievement places our operations in **the top 5% of more than 130,000 companies evaluated by EcoVadis worldwide** , highlighting our commitment to responsible, future-focused business practices.


**[EcoVadis](https://ecovadis.com/)** , a globally trusted leader in **business sustainability assessments** , evaluates companies based on international standards, including the Ten Principles of the UN Global Compact, the International Labour Organization (ILO) conventions, the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) standards and the ISO 26000 standard. The assessment evaluates 21 criteria across four core themes: Environment, Labor & Human Rights, Ethics and Sustainable Procurement.


### TriMas Packaging’s ongoing commitment to responsible business practices


> “Achieving EcoVadis Gold for the third year demonstrates the strength of our commitment to responsible growth,” *said Fabio Salik, President of TriMas Packaging* . “We are advancing meaningful environmental initiatives, while delivering reliable, high‑performance packaging solutions. This honor reinforces that our actions are making a measurable difference.”


This recognition reflects our strong sustainability management system and structured approach to embedding sustainable practices across our operations, governance and supplier engagement. Across our U.S. sites and global operations, we continue to advance initiatives that support our broader goal of delivering innovative, responsible and sustainable solutions for our customers and partners.


As global sustainability expectations continue to evolve, TriMas Packaging remains committed to further enhancing our practices in alignment with leading international standards.
