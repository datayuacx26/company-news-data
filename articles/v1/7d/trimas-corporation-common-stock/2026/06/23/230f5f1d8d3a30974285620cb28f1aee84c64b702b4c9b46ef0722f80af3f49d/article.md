# TriMas Packaging Netherlands Earns EcoVadis Gold Rating

## **Recognized for sustained progress in sustainability performance and responsible business practices**


TriMas Packaging is pleased to announce that its facility in Waalwijk, Netherlands, assessed under the name Aarts Packaging B.V., has once again earned an **Ecovadis Gold Medal rating** .


In the May 2026 evaluation, the facility **ranked in the 98th percentile, placing it among the top 5% of more than 130,000 companies evaluated worldwide** . This recognition highlights continued progress in strengthening sustainability practices and driving measurable improvements across operations.


[EcoVadis](https://ecovadis.com/) , a leading provider of **business sustainability assessments** , provides a recognized framework for evaluating environmental, social and governance (ESG) performance, assessing organizations across key areas including **Environment, Labor & Human Rights, Ethics and Sustainable Procurement** .


This achievement reflects the company’s efforts to embed sustainability into day-to-day operations and decision-making. Through cross-functional collaboration, enhanced processes and improved transparency, TriMas Packaging continues to advance a disciplined and structured approach to sustainability management.


As demand for responsible sourcing and supply chain transparency grows, EcoVadis recognition reinforces confidence in TriMas Packaging as a **trusted partner committed to consistent, responsible and sustainable operations** .


TriMas Packaging remains focused on building on this progress by advancing ESG performance and supporting long-term, sustainable growth across its global footprint.
