# TriMas Packaging Baddi achieves ISO 50001 certification

## Enhancing efficiency and sustainability through global energy management standards


TriMas Packaging is proud to announce that our **Baddi, India facility** has achieved **ISO 50001:2018 certification, the internationally recognized standard for energy management systems (EnMS)** . This milestone highlights the company’s commitment to advancing energy efficiency, strengthening operational performance and supporting sustainable manufacturing practices across its global footprint.


ISO 50001 provides a structured framework for organizations to manage energy use more effectively, driving continuous improvement in efficiency while reducing environmental impact. Achieving this certification reflects TriMas Packaging’s ongoing focus on **implementing best-in-class processes that enable reliable, consistent and responsible operations** .


> “Achieving ISO 50001 certification at our Baddi facility marks an important milestone as we continue **expanding energy management practices across our global operations** ,” *said Fabio Salik, President, TriMas Packaging* . “Each step forward reinforces our commitment to responsible energy use, strengthens operational efficiency and supports our ability to deliver high-quality packaging solutions for our customers while advancing our broader sustainability priorities.”


This accomplishment highlights TriMas Packaging’s continued focus on **integrating energy management into its overall business strategy** . Through disciplined processes and continuous improvement, the company is **optimizing resource use, enhancing performance and creating long-term value for customers and stakeholders** .


In addition to ISO 50001 certification, the facility also maintains certifications for:


- **ISO 9001:** Quality Management Systems (QMS)
- **ISO 14001:** Environmental Management Systems (EMS)
- **ISO 45001:** Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems (OH&S)


TriMas Packaging remains focused on advancing initiatives that improve efficiency, support sustainability and strengthen its position as a trusted partner to customers worldwide.
