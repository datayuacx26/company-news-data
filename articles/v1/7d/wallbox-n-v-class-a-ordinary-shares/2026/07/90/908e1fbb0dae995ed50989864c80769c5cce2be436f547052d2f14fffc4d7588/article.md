# Beyond the Hardware: How Wallbox Pulsar Pro Makes Company Car Reimbursement Effortless


4 min read


In our[previous article](https://wallbox.com/en/blog/company-car-charging-ev-fleets-europe) , we explored a defining shift in European fleet electrification: the home has become an extension of the corporate charging network. But as fleet managers and accounting departments know all too well, installing a charger at an employee’s home is only half the battle.


The real challenge? Managing, measuring, *and* reimbursing those charging sessions accurately and efficiently.


Historically, reimbursing EV company car drivers for home charging has been an administrative headache, largely because energy measurements weren’t accurate or certified enough to meet reimbursement requirements. With the introduction of the new[Pulsar Pro](https://wallbox.com/en/pulsar-pro-home-charger-reimbursement) , Wallbox is fundamentally changing this dynamic.


By pairing **integrated, MID-certified hardware** with our **industry-leading Wallbox App and Portal** , we’ve created a plug-and-play ecosystem designed specifically to make company car reimbursement effortless for everyone involved.


## **For the Driver: A “Set It and Forget It” Experience**


The success of any corporate EV policy hinges on driver adoption and satisfaction. Employees expect charging their company car at home to be as simple as plugging in, with the peace of mind that their energy costs will be fairly and automatically accounted for.


That’s where the[Wallbox App](https://wallbox.com/en/my-wallbox-app) steps in. Highly rated by everyday users, the app puts total control and transparency in the palm of the driver’s hand. Setting up company car billing is remarkably straightforward:


- **Easy Reimbursement Configuration:** Simply enter your company’s reimbursement rate (€/kWh) in the Wallbox app once, and the system takes care of the rest.
- **Personalized RFID Access:** Drivers can register their personal RFID card directly within the portal, ensuring that company vehicle charging is distinctly separated from any private charging sessions.
- **Automated Reporting:** This is where the magic happens. Drivers can schedule automatic charging session reports (generated weekly or monthly) to be sent directly to their employer in PDF or CSV formats.


Once configured, drivers don’t have to lift a finger. The[Wallbox Portal](https://wallbox.com/en/my-wallbox-business-portal) **** automatically generates and delivers recurring reports, eliminating manual administration and giving drivers one less task to think about.


## **For Companies: Centralized Data and Accounting Efficiency**


While drivers need simplicity, finance teams and fleet managers require visibility, accuracy, and automation. Managing a handful of expense reports is manageable; processing hundreds of individual charging logs across different countries is a logistical nightmare.


The **Wallbox Portal** transforms fragmented data into actionable, centralized intelligence. It’s engineered to make life easier for accounting teams by delivering automated, company-level summaries.


- **Corporate Identification:** If multiple employees share a company email domain and reporting schedule, the system intelligently recognizes the address as a corporate entity.
- **Consolidated Summaries:** Along with individual reports, companies receive a centralized Charging Session Summary. This high-level overview aggregates all employee reports, detailing total energy consumed and total costs incurred across the fleet.
- **Simplified Accounting:** By delivering clean, accurate, and MID-certified data in one unified report, finance teams can process reimbursements faster and with complete confidence.


## **An Integrated Ecosystem at No Additional Cost**


What makes the Pulsar Pro unique in the European market is not just its robust feature set, but how those features are delivered.


Often, fleet operators are forced to purchase hardware from one vendor and subscribe to a separate software platform for reimbursement management. The new Pulsar Pro breaks this mold. It combines **built-in MID metering** and **future-proofed AFIR compliance** with the comprehensive Wallbox App and Portal ecosystem – all centralized at no additional software cost.


## **Rethinking Corporate Fleet Charging Operations**


Transitioning a corporate fleet to electric vehicles is a significant operational undertaking. The technology you choose today will dictate your fleet’s efficiency for years to come.


By focusing equally on the driver experience and the administrative backend, the Wallbox Pulsar Pro proves that managing EV reimbursement doesn’t have to be complicated. It’s just one more way we are making the transition to electric mobility easier to manage for everyone involved.
