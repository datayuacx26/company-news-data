# Powering Local: How Wallbox & DeCharge Are Expanding Access to Community EV Charging


3 min read


## ***Charging that Belongs to the Community***


The future of EV charging is about more than scale, it’s about connection.


That’s why Wallbox has partnered with DeCharge, a peer-to-peer charging network that helps homeowners, small businesses, and workplaces share their chargers with other drivers. Together, we’re making EV charging access more local, affordable, and community-driven.


This collaboration marks a new chapter for community EV charging in the U.S., bringing charging access closer to home – literally.


## **The Benefits of Community EV Charging Networks**


Electric vehicle adoption is growing faster than ever. But for many drivers, finding a *reliable* EV charging station nearby can still be a challenge, especially in suburban and residential areas.


Community charging changes that.


Instead of relying solely on large public networks, DeCharge’s model connects drivers with people who already have chargers – transforming private stations into shared public assets.


Through this partnership, Wallbox and DeCharge are making EV charging:


- **Accessible:** More places to plug in, right where people live and work.


- **Rewarding:** Charger hosts earn income and drivers earn rewards.


- **Reliable:** High-quality Wallbox hardware ensures maximum uptime and performance.


## **How the Wallbox + DeCharge Peer-to-Peer Network Works**


The concept is simple:
Anyone who owns a[Wallbox EV charger](https://wallbox.com/en_us/home-smart-solar-ev-charging-station) can list it on the DeCharge app. Once activated, their charger becomes part of a wider community network that local drivers can easily find, book, and use.


- **Hosts** : Set pricing, manage sessions, and track earnings in real time.


- **Drivers** : Find available chargers nearby and earn rewards for every session.


- **Installers & Partners** : Gain access to uptime alerts and data insights to support seamless operation.


Each charge helps strengthen a decentralized network where value stays close to home.


## **Why Wallbox’s Pulsar Plus Is Built for Community EV Charging**


To support DeCharge’s rollout, Wallbox is providing[Pulsar Plus](https://wallbox.com/en_us/pulsar-plus-ev-charger) – our best-selling AC home chargers known for their next-gen charging intelligence and sleek, compact design.


It’s the same Wallbox technology trusted by thousands of businesses and property owners worldwide, now powering a community-first charging model in the U.S.


## **Expanding EV Charging Access Across the U.S.**


The partnership will first launch in the **DMV area** , **Florida** , and **California** – three regions leading in EV adoption. As more chargers join the network, DeCharge plans to expand nationwide, targeting 200+ active ports by early 2026.


Future updates will include:


- Reward programs for referrals


- Local redemption options for earned rewards


- Advanced dashboards for tracking earnings and site performance


Together, these features help ensure that community charging grows naturally, and *sustainably* . Each new charger helps build local access and keeps revenue where it belongs: in the community.
