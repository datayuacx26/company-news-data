# Wallbox and Freenow Partner to Support Taxi Electrification Across Europe

# Wallbox and Freenow Partner to Support Taxi Electrification Across Europe


4 min read


#### ***Helping professional drivers access smarter EV charging solutions, from homes to fleet depots***


Electric mobility is reshaping urban transport across Europe, and the taxi sector is playing an increasingly important role in that transition.


To support this shift, **Wallbox has partnered with Freenow to provide tailored EV charging solutions for taxi drivers and fleet operators across key European markets** . Launching in Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Ireland, and Spain, the collaboration gives Freenow’s battery electric (BEV) and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) taxi drivers and fleet owners access to exclusive conditions across selected Wallbox charging solutions for home and business charging.


As taxi and ride-hailing networks continue to electrify, access to reliable and easy-to-manage charging infrastructure is becoming a central operational priority.


## **Supporting a Growing Electric Taxi Market**


Electrification is steadily gaining momentum across European taxi networks, driven by urban emissions targets, evolving local regulations, and growing driver interest in lower operating costs.


As electrified taxi adoption grows, **charging infrastructure becomes an important part of day-to-day operations** ; shaping how drivers charge, how fleets are managed, and how easily electrification can scale.


At the same time, taxi and ride-hailing operations present a **distinct set of operational demands** compared to private EV ownership. Drivers often work long shifts with high daily mileage and limited downtime, while fleet operators need charging infrastructure that can support multiple vehicles, shared access, and efficient site management.


For independent drivers, home charging often provides the most practical foundation for everyday use. Fleet operators, meanwhile, typically need solutions that can support multiple vehicles, shared access, and efficient management across depots or business sites.


But scaling beyond early adoption requires more than vehicles. It requires **charging ecosystems designed for real-world usage** .


## **EV Charging Solutions Designed for Different Needs**


Through this partnership, Freenow drivers and fleet operators gain access to a curated portfolio of Wallbox solutions, aligned with their specific operational needs.


### **Home EV Charging for Independent Drivers**


- [Pulsar Max](https://wallbox.com/en/pulsar-family) A compact AC charger designed for residential charging, offering smart connectivity and everyday ease of use for overnight charging routines.


### **Fleet & Shared EV Charging Environments**


- [Pulsar Pro](https://wallbox.com/en/pulsar-pro-ev-charger-socket) Built for commercial applications, with features such as user authentication, connectivity, and charging management capabilities.
- [eM4](https://wallbox.com/en/em4-commercial-ev-business-charger) Wallbox’s modular charging solution developed for semi-public, shared, and fleet charging applications where scalability and flexible deployment are important.


This combination allows both individual drivers and fleet managers to implement charging setups suited to their operational model.

## **Simplifying Access to EV Charging Infrastructure**


One of the biggest challenges in fleet electrification is its implementation, which is why this partnership goes beyond hardware.


Alongside exclusive pricing conditions, participating Freenow drivers and fleet operators will also benefit from **Wallbox’s local network of certified installation partners.**


This helps streamline the journey from product selection to installation, reducing friction for drivers and businesses introducing charging infrastructure into their operations. For professional mobility, that simplicity matters. Every step that shortens deployment and improves reliability helps accelerate adoption.


## **Built Around the Reality of Daily Operations**


As **Ignasi Alastuey, Chief Business Officer at Wallbox** , explains:


> “The shift to electric mobility in the taxi sector will depend on making charging simple, accessible, and suited to the everyday needs of drivers and fleet operators. Through this partnership with Freenow, we are helping make that transition more practical and scalable across Europe.”


That focus on usability is central to the partnership. For drivers, every minute off the road matters. For fleet operators, uptime and efficiency have a direct impact on business performance.


By integrating **smart charging, intuitive design, and scalable infrastructure** , Wallbox is enabling a system that works in real-world conditions, not just on paper.


Freenow shares that ambition. As **Felix Brand, Freenow’s Chief Strategy Officer** notes:


> “Working with Wallbox allows us to offer charging solutions that are practical, reliable, and adapted to the needs of our driver community, helping make electrification a more viable option across our network.’”


## **Supporting the Next Phase of Urban Mobility**


As countries push toward zero-emission mobility and drivers look for cost-effective, future-proof solutions; the role of charging infrastructure becomes even more critical.


Platforms like Freenow are uniquely positioned to accelerate this shift, connecting thousands of drivers across major European cities. By embedding access to reliable EV charging within that ecosystem – whether at home, in shared residential settings, or across fleet environments – this partnership aims to turn intent into action.
