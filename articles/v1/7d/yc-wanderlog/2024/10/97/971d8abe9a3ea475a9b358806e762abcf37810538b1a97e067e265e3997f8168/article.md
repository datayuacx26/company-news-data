# 7 Best Travel Apps


October 23, 2024


11 min read


A few months ago, I visited Banff, Alberta, with my husband and two college-age daughters. We all had places we wanted to see and activities we wanted to do based on posts we’ve seen on Instagram, TikTok, and other social media platforms. Since we were unfamiliar with the region, we added all our points of interest into a travel app, which allowed us to see everything all together on a map. We could plan each day’s itinerary to minimize the drive time between each spot.


In addition, the app provided us with some basic information about each attraction, including the average time visitors spend there and the admission cost. Armed with this information, we could plan each day, leaving an adequate amount of time to enjoy each place we wanted to see. And we could keep track of expenses for the day. Best of all, it was shareable. My daughters could each add to the list of attractions as they discovered more.


Travel apps are rising in popularity because they allow you to simplify the travel planning process. Not only that, but they help relieve some of your trip-planning stress, ensure that you see everything you want to see, and potentially save you money. Since you are[packing your cell phone anyway](https://wanderlog.com/blog/2024/10/24/what-is-the-best-app-that-helps-you-pack-for-a-trip/) , it just makes sense to put your device to work as your own personal digital travel planner in your back pocket.


*Which travel apps are the best?*


We looked at features like itinerary planning, budget tracking, and shareability and narrowed the ever-expanding list of travel apps down to a few that we think are most helpful for travel planners.


## The top 7 travel apps


**Best overall:** Wanderlog


- **Known for:** Itinerary builder, color-coded maps, budget tracker, group travel capabilities, and user-reviewed recommendations.
- **Downside:** Can’t book flights or excursions on the app.
- **Rating:** 4.9/5 stars App Store; 4.7/5 stars Google Play Store


**Best for road trips:** Roadtrippers


- **Known for:** Itineraries built on maps, recommendations along road trip routes, and group travel capabilities
- **Downside:** Can’t book anything on the app.
- **Rating:** 4.6/5 stars App Store; 2.9/5 stars Google Play Store


**Best for recommendations:** Tripadvisor


- **Known for:** Booking flights, hotels, excursions, rental cars, and more, as well as user-reviewed recommendations.
- **Downside:** No real planning capabilities like itinerary builder or budget tracker
- **Rating:** 4.8/5 stars App Store; 4.4/5 stars Google Play Store


**Best for Reservation Tracking:** TripIt


- **Known for:** Keeping track of all your reservations, syncing with email to gather reservation information.
- **Downside:** No map of your itinerary; can’t book anything on the app.
- **Rating:** 4.8/5 stars App Store; 4.6/5 stars Google Play Store


**Best for Booking:** Booking.com


- **Known for:** Booking everything in one place, including flights, hotels, car rentals, excursions, cruises, vacation homes, and trip packages.
- **Downside:** No trip planning or tracking capabilities.
- **Rating:** 4.8/5 stars App Store; 4.5/5 stars Google Play Store


**Best of Home Rentals:** Airbnb


- **Known for:** Booking homes and non-traditional lodging.
- **Downside:** No booking besides lodging. No trip planning or tracking capabilities.
- **Rating:** 4.7/5 stars App Store; 4.4/5 stars Google Play Store


**Best for Travel Deals:** Hopper


- **Known for:** Booking deals for flights, hotels, and rental cars.
- **Downside:** No trip planning or tracking capabilities.
- **Rating:** 4.7/5 stars App Store; 4.5/5 stars Google Play Store


## Best Overall: Wanderlog


Wanderlog checks all our boxes, which is why we are listing ourselves in the top spot. It’s a one-stop shop for travel planning and continues to be a convenient tool while you are on vacation.


Prior to your trip, Wanderlog can help you plan your itinerary, reserve accommodations, and book tours. During your trip, you can retrieve all your reservations and confirmation codes, track expenses, and navigate from one attraction to the next with ease. Here’s what Wanderlog can do for you:


**Itinerary planner:** Wanderlog’s itinerary planning tools make it easy to create a travel agenda that ensures you visit all the places you dream of while making the best use of your time. You can drag and drop each attraction and point of interest to develop a daily schedule and rearrange them as needed.


**Map:** Your destinations can be viewed on a color-coded map so you can visualize where everything is located. The map feature even shows you other points of interest near your locations, so you won’t miss a hidden gem nearby. In addition, the map feature also tells you the distance and travel time between points of interest and allows you to toggle between modes of transportation – walking, biking, driving, or taking public transportation.


**Recommendations:** Wanderlog will offer recommendations for attractions near your points of interest and ones that align with your interests. If you love history, for example, the Wanderlog app will point out museums, landmarks, and historic sites. If you are a foodie, the app will show you restaurants, coffee shops, and food trucks you may want to try.


**Shareability:** You have the ability to share your Wanderlog itinerary with your traveling companions. They, in turn, can add to the itinerary and make notes of their interests. Since Wanderlog stores all your travel reservations and booking confirmations, your fellow travelers can access these when needed. They won’t have to interrupt your scenic boat tour to ask you for the door code to your vacation rental.


**Bookings and reservations:** You can make hotel reservations from the app. You can also forward your booking emails to Wanderlog to have all your reservation info in one place.


**Budget tracker:** You can keep track of all your travel expenses, from flights and accommodations to meals and tour costs. In addition, the app displays the admission or ticket prices for various attractions and the average costs for restaurants and more. Knowing how much you have already spent and how much you can expect to pay for upcoming activities will help travelers control their travel budgets.


**Free version:** Wanderlog is free to download and use. The free version will allow you access to most of the app’s features so you can plan your travels and book your adventures. There is a Wanderlog Pro version that is available on a subscription basis and offers additional premium features like optimized routes and exporting to Google Maps.


**App Store/Google Play rating:** Wanderlog has a rating of 4.9 out of 5 stars on Apple’s App Store and 4.7 out of 5 stars on the Google Play Store.


## Best for Road Trips: Roadtrippers


While super useful, Roadtrippers is limited to road trip travel. It allows you to enter your starting point and final destination, and, like Google Maps, it suggests the optimal driving route for you, including the approximate mileage and travel time. But it goes a few steps further than Google Maps. Roadtrippers offers:


**Itinerary planner:** The app keeps track of your route and the stops you plan to make along the way, showing you a daily itinerary. You can even add ‘avoidance’ preferences to make sure that your travels don’t include unexpected surprises, like toll roads, dirt roads, or construction detours.


**Map:** Roadtrippers is primarily map-based. Therefore, the map of your route is prominently displayed, along with directions to your destination. You can zoom in to see what is nearby or zoom out to get an overview of the day’s journey. Alternative routes, points of interest, restaurants, gas stations, and more pop up along your route.


**Recommendations:** Based on your pre-selected interests, Roadtrippers will recommend attractions, points of interest, and notable side trips along your route of travel.


**Shareability:** Once you create a road trip route in the Roadtrippers app, you can share it with your traveling companions. Not only will they be able to see what you have planned, but they can add to your adventures as well. You can also download a PDF of your travel plans and map, if you are the type of traveler who wants a tangible plan.


**Bookings and reservations:** You can book a hotel room and reserve a tour time for an event or attraction without leaving the Roadtrippers app.


**Budget tracker:** Although Roadtrippers doesn’t have a budget tracking feature that encompasses your entire trip, it does have some tools that are useful for budgeting purposes. For example, the app calculates your estimated fuel costs for your road trip. It also displays admission costs and average expenses for each of the stops you plan to make throughout your journey.


**Free version:** There is a free version of Roadtrippers that allows you to do basic planning, as well as a free trial to give you a chance to test out the app. If you decide to subscribe, you can select between three different levels of features.


**App Store/Google Play rating:** On the Apple App Store, the Roadtrippers app currently has 4.6 out of 5 stars. When we go to Google Play, however, we see that this app only has 2.9 out of 5 stars.


## Best for Recommendations: TripAdvisor


Whenever I am heading to a new city, TripAdvisor is one of the first places I look to learn about the sights and attractions of the place. The app lists things to do, top attractions, places to stay, and popular restaurants, making it easy to plan a visit. Here’s what else the TripAdvisor app does:


**Itinerary planner:** TripAdvisor has built its reputation on providing comprehensive recommendations for tourists. It allows you to organize events you book into days, but doesn’t have a true itinerary planner.


**Map:** Once you save places of interest, the Trip Advisor app displays them all on a map so you can get a better idea of where everything is in relation to, for example, your hotel or the airport.


**Recommendations:** Providing recommendations is the main focus of TripAdvisor. The app lists places to stay, dining establishments, things to do, and area attractions. You can even read comments and reviews by recent visitors before deciding if that place deserves to be added to your itinerary.


**Shareability:** You can share your travel plans you made on the TripAdvisor app with your friends and family. If they are traveling with you, they can view the plans for the day. If you leave them at home, they can follow your adventures from afar and see all the exciting places you will be visiting.


**Bookings and reservations:** You can book your hotel, make your dinner reservation, and purchase tickets for tours and attractions without leaving the TripAdvisor app. The app even shows you deals and discounts on travel activities, allowing you to stretch your travel dollars.


**Budget tracker:** There is not a dedicated budget tracking feature on the TripAdvisor app, however you can see the average costs for each of the recommended attractions and restaurants. You can then make your travel plans knowing the approximate expenses you could incur.


**Free version:** TripAdvisor is free to use. The app recently discontinued its premium subscription service.


**App Store/Google Play rating:** The TripAdvisor app has a rating of 4.4 out of 5 stars on Google Play and 4.8 out of 5 stars on Apple’s App Store.


## Best for Reservation Tracking: TripIt


TripIt is a repository of all your travel plans, confirmation numbers, flight information, and booking numbers. As a single storehouse for all your trip information, TripIt is just as valuable during your travel as it is in the planning stage.


**Itinerary planner:** It doesn’t matter where or how you book your flights, hotels, or car rentals, Tripit will pull the relevant information from your confirmation emails and keep everything organized and easily accessible to you. Based on this information, it will create an itinerary for you.


**Map:** TripIt has several map features that are useful to travelers. The first one displays all your pinned locations on one map, so you have an overview of your points of interest. There is also a navigator tool that directs you from one location to the other, whether you are walking, driving, biking, or taking public transportation. In addition to the directions, it shows you estimated travel times. Lastly, TripIt has an interactive airport map feature to help you navigate through unfamiliar airports.


**Recommendations:** TripIt will show you recommended attractions, restaurants, museums, and more that fit into your itinerary. You can select options that appeal to you.


**Shareability:** Users have the ability to share their itinerary and reservation information with others via the TripIt app.


**Bookings and reservations:** TripIt does not allow users to[book flights](https://wanderlog.com/blog/2024/11/01/10-secrets-for-booking-cheap-last-minute-flights/) or accommodation or reserve tours and attractions through its app; however, once you have made your travel plans, TripIt will store all the information in one place.


**Budget tracker:** TripIt’s ‘Trip Cost’ feature lets users keep track of expenses associated with their trip.


**Free version:** TripIt has a free version that allows users to access most of its features. Additional features are available through the TripIt Pro subscription service.


**App Store/Google Play rating:** TripIt’s Google Play Store rating is 4.6 out of 5 stars. On Apple’s App Store, its rating is even higher … 4.8 out of 5 stars.


## Best for Booking: Booking.com


I use Booking.com often, especially when I am doing initial research into a destination. One of the most[popular travel apps](https://wanderlog.com/blog/2024/10/23/7-best-travel-apps/) , Booking.com does just what its name implies … helps users book flights, car rentals, and accommodations. Personally, I like that this app includes all price points so I can travel as cheaply or luxuriously as I want. I find the customer reviews to be helpful, too.


**Itinerary planner:** Booking.com has integrated AI technology to help users plan a travel itinerary. Once you select your destination, you can ask the AI Trip Planner to plan your trip based on your budget, interests, and other preferences.


**Map:** Booking.com’s map feature shows an overview of an area, displaying all the hotels, hostels, and rental properties in that area.


**Recommendations:** In addition to providing recommendations and allowing you to book flights, stays, and car rentals, Booking.com offers suggestions for attractions and activities, which can also be booked through the app. Users can even book taxis and airport shuttles via Booking.com


**Shareability:** Booking.com has an option that allows you to share your itinerary with your traveling companions so they can access your reservation details and view confirmation information.


**Bookings and reservations:** If you have made your bookings and reservations through Booking.com, you will be able to retrieve this information from the app.


**Budget tracker:** Booking.com displays the costs of hotel accommodations, travel expenses, and car rentals so you can keep track of them, but it doesn’t have the ability to track all your dining, fuel, and entertainment expenses


**Free version:** Booking.com is free to use. The app does not charge a booking fee or additional costs when booking flights, car rentals, or hotel stays through their app.


App Store/Google Play Rating: Booking.com has 4.5 stars out of 5 on the Google Play Store and 4.8 stars out of 5 on Apple’s App Store.


## Best of Home Rentals: Airbnb


Airbnb has revolutionized the travel industry by providing a way for travelers to connect with private homeowners who have a house, apartment, guest house, or spare bedroom available for short-term vacation rental. For the first time, travelers had an alternative to costly hotels and the option to stay in non-traditional accommodations, like a houseboat, treehouse, or yurt.


**Itinerary planner:** The Airbnb app allows you to plan your travel itinerary. Once you book a stay, go to the ‘Trips’ section to view your reservation information. There, you can also add the area attractions and activities that interest you from a list of popular spots in your destination.


**Map:** Airbnb’s map feature shows the locations of all the rental properties in the area you will be visiting. You can filter these by the type of accommodation, number of bedrooms, number of guests, price range, and other amenities. For instance, we often search for rental homes with outdoor spaces and grills.


**Recommendations:** When you scroll below the Airbnb reservation details, you will see the words “Find things to do near your stay.” Here, the Airbnb app offers recommendations for sightseeing, outdoor activities, entertainment, food and drinks, and shopping. Further down, Airbnb lists “Top-Rated Experiences” based on user reviews and feedback.


**Shareability:** The Airbnb app allows you to share your bookings with others in your group.


**Bookings and reservations:** The bookings and reservations you make through Airbnb’s app will be stored under your ‘Trips’ so you can access the information when you need it. In addition, all the information you need will be emailed to you ahead of your trip.


**Budget tracker:** Although you can filter by cost when searching for a rental property on Airbnb, the app does not have a travel budget tracking feature.


**Free Version:** The Airbnb app is free to download and use.


**App Store/Google Play rating:** The Airbnb app has a 4.4-out-of-5-star rating on the Google Play store and a 4.7-out-of-5-star rating on Apple’s App Store.


## Best for Travel Deals: Hopper


The Hopper travel app offers its users great deals on hotels, flights, and car rentals for their travel destinations by analyzing data and trends to determine the optimal time to book travel arrangements. So travelers can confidently know that they are getting the best deal. Hopper’s price-freeze feature even lets you lock in the low price, and its price-tracking feature sends you an alert when the cost of the flight you are interested in goes down.


**Itinerary planner:** The Hopper app does not have an itinerary planning feature.


**Map:** You can access a map of your destination city on the Hopper app. On it, you can see all the hotels in your area and their prices in much the same way as the Airbnb app.


**Recommendations:** In addition to flights and hotels, the Hopper app allows users to search popular attractions and tourist hot spots at their destination, which is useful when planning a trip.


**Shareability:** Hopper has a share feature so you can share your travel reservations with the people you are traveling with.


**Bookings and reservations:** The flights, hotels rooms, and car rentals you find on the Hopper app can all be booked directly through the app, which stores your reservation and confirmation information for you.


**Budget tracker:** Although the Hopper app allows users to save money on travel expenses, this app does not include a budget tracker feature that records and tracks all travel-related expenses.


**Free version:** The Hopper app is free to download and use.


**App Store/Google Play rating:** According to Google Play, the Hopper app has a 4.5-star rating out of 5 stars. On Apple’s App Store, Hopper’s rating is 4.7 out of 5 stars.
