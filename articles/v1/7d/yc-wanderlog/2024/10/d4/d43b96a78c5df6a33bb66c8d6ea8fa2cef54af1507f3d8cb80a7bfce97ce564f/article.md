# Best Packing Apps to Help You Pack for a Trip


October 24, 2024


23 min read


There are so many things to remember when preparing for a trip, it can be easy to forget something if you’re not organized, and few things ruin the excitement of a trip like realizing you’ve left something important at home. No matter what kind of adventure you’re heading off on, having a well-organized packing list is essential to avoid spending the start of your trip replacing what you forget instead of starting making memories.


Making a digital packing list is a game-changer because it ensures you have everything you need without the stress of second-guessing yourself. Personally, I learned this the hard way. On a trip to the beach, I was so sure I had packed everything, but after landing, I realized I’d forgotten my swimsuit — a pretty essential item for a beach vacation! I spent my first afternoon shopping for a replacement instead of relaxing by the ocean.


Since then, I’ve switched to using a digital packing list, and it’s made all the difference. Digital lists are easy to update, customizable for different types of trips, and accessible from almost anywhere, so there’s no chance of losing them. Plus, most packing apps let you check off items as you pack them, which has saved me from those “Did I pack that?” panic moments. Having a digital list ensures I never forget key items again — like that swimsuit!

## **** Overall Best Packing List App: PackPoint

### How To Create a Packing List With PackPoint


1. Open the PackPoint packing list app and input:


- Travel destination
- Start date of the trip
- Length of the trip
- Whether your trip is for business or pleasure
- Your gender
- The types of activities you will be doing and other packing list categories chosen from the following:


- Swimming
- Snow Sports
- Working
- Camping
- Gym
- Photography
- International
- Beach
- Baby
- Hiking
- Bicycling
- Running
- Fancy Dinner
- Motorcycling


1. Check if you want to repeat “basics” and if you are going to do laundry on the trip. Checking these will reduce the number of common basics (underwear, socks, casual shirts, and casual pants) on your packing list — a great feature if you want to pack as light as possible.


2. Select “Begin Packing” to get a personalized packing list based on all the information added from the above selections. Average weather will also be shown for your dates and destination, helping you know what weather you’ll need to dress for without leaving the app.


1. Begin selecting things you have packed from the “Essentials” list with 34 items and the “Toiletries” list with 15 items. Other packing lists will also show up based on what activities/types of trips you selected, such as things you need for a beach vacation or a business trip. You can also add custom packing list items if you have PackPoint Premium.


1. If you want to pack more than one of any item, click the plus button on the right-hand side and choose the number of items you want on your list.


1. If there is a pre-added item that you don’t want to pack, swipe the item left and click “Hide”


### Pros of PackPoint


- **Activity-Based Packing Lists:** PackPoint features lots of curated packing lists that match the activities you will be doing on the trip, ensuring you have all the gear you need for your upcoming adventure.


- **Pack Light and Laundry Features:** This packing list app can create a packing list for packing light (using the repeat basics and/or laundry option), which is ideal for those wanting to pack only a carry-on bag or generally keep the weight of their luggage down.


- **Weather Integration:** Once you’ve entered your destination and travel dates, PackPoint provides a weather overview for each day of the trip. This is very helpful when packing, especially if traveling somewhere with changeable weather, as you can decide what is necessary to pack without needing to look up the forecast elsewhere or risk arriving without suitable clothing.


- **User-Friendly Interface:** PackPoint is simple to navigate, making it a valuable tool for organizing your trip. With so much to keep track of, a user-friendly packing app can help ease the stress of getting ready and ensure you don’t forget anything important.


- **Packing lists are sharable:** Your packing list can easily be shared via SMS, WhatsApp, and email which is useful if someone you’re traveling with will need to pack similar items or if you want to access the packing list on another device. These are view-only, so you won’t be able to collaborate with anyone on a list.


- **Premium Options Available:** This packing list app offers some additional features for an affordable price of under $4. Premium features include adding custom items to pack and syncing with TripIt.


### Cons of PackPoint


- **Limited Customization Features:** The free version of PackPoint does not allow for much customization. You can add the number of items to pack, hide items on custom lists that you don’t want, and choose some pre-created packing lists (like those for snow sports holidays or beach vacations) but you can’t add your own specific items to pack or create custom packing lists with personalized categories. These features are only available if you pay for them which isn’t great if you’re on a tight budget and are looking for a free packing list app with as many features as possible.


- **Collaboration:** PackPoint only creates packing lists for one person. You can’t add travelers or access curated lists for multiple groups like packing lists for couples or families. This can be a drawback if you’re packing for multiple people as you can’t see everything to pack in one place and will instead have to switch between trip packing lists.


- **No Other Travel Planning Features:** PackPoint is solely a packing list app, with no options to store other things needed for travel, such as[flight bookings](https://wanderlog.com/blog/2024/11/01/10-secrets-for-booking-cheap-last-minute-flights/) and other booking confirmations, expense tracking, itinerary planning, or activity suggestions for your trip. This can be frustrating if you’re looking for additional travel planning features as you’ll have to switch between apps for different functions.

### What Is the 5-4-3-2-1 Rule for Packing?


The 5-4-3-2-1 Rule is a minimalist packing strategy designed to help you pack efficiently and avoid overpacking for trips. The numbers revolve around the number of items to pack in five common clothing categories: tops, pants/bottoms, shoes, accessories, and an outer layer. Some variations also include one or two dresses in place of accessories or an outer layer.


The idea is to limit the number of items you bring by focusing on essentials and mixing and matching pieces. Here’s how it works:


1. **5 Tops** : Pack five tops, like t-shirts, blouses, or casual shirts. Choose versatile options that can be dressed up or down.
2. **4 Bottoms** : Pack four bottoms, such as jeans, shorts, skirts, or pants. Select neutral colors that match well with your tops.
3. **3 Pairs of Shoes** : Limit yourself to three pairs of shoes: one comfortable pair for walking, one casual pair, and one for formal occasions or special activities.
4. **2 Accessories** : Choose two accessories, like a hat, scarf, or jewelry, that can enhance your outfits without taking up much space.
5. **1 Outer Layer** : Bring one outer layer, like a jacket or sweater, depending on the weather and destination.


As mentioned, you could switch one outer layer or one to two accessories with one or two dresses if this works better for your style and travel plans.
