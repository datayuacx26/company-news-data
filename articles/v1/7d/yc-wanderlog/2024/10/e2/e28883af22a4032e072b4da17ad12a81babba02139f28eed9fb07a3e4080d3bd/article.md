9 min read


While many dream of visiting iconic cities like Rome or Paris, Europe is rich in history and beauty in lesser-known, budget-friendly destinations. For example, Tallinn, Estonia, features a stunning UNESCO-listed Old Town, while Buda Castle in Budapest, Hungary, offers panoramic views and rich historical insights.


I recently traveled to Estonia and was amazed at how much it felt like I was directly walking back into Medieval times. The ancient architecture combined with authentic Medieval experiences trumped anything I had seen in Berlin or Paris.


In Plovdiv, Bulgaria, ancient Roman ruins sit alongside charming 19th-century architecture, and Albania’s Albanian Riviera entices visitors with its pristine beaches and scenic landscapes. Don’t miss Bran Castle in Romania, which is famous for its Dracula connection and stunning mountain views. These hidden gems capture Europe’s diverse culture and history, proving that adventure doesn’t have to break the bank!

## Albania


Albania is the perfect choice if you dream of a[breathtaking European getaway](https://wanderlog.com/blog/2024/09/26/10-tips-for-traveling-internationally/) without draining your wallet. With its stunning landscapes and ancient ruins, you can explore this hidden gem for as little as $50 to $80 daily. Even a luxury vacation in Albania comes at a fraction of the cost of Paris or Rome—where a high-end stay may cost only $200 per day, compared to at least $500 in those iconic cities.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Tirana**


- **Must-Do:** Stroll through Skanderbeg Square and snap photos of the colorful street art in Blloku. Don’t miss the National History Museum for a dose of local history!


2. **Shkodër**


- **Must-Do:** Explore Rozafa Castle for panoramic views, and check out the Marubi National Museum of Photography.


### What Makes Albania So Affordable?


- **Deliciously Cheap Food ($5-$10):** Albanian cuisine features hearty, flavorful dishes like byrek (savory pastry), tavë kosi (baked lamb with yogurt), and fërgesë (pepper, tomato, and cheese stew). Meals at local spots are affordable, ranging from $5 to $10, while street food, like sufllaqe (Albanian gyro), is delicious and budget-friendly.
- **Inexpensive hotels (** **$30-$40** **):** You can easily get a hostel or cheap hotel room in great areas in Tirana for between $30-$40 a night. Shkodër can be a little more expensive with prices from $35-$45.
- **Affordable Transport:** Public transportation in Albania is incredibly affordable, with most rides costing around $1. Buses and furgons (shared minibuses) are the main forms of transport connecting major cities and towns. For more flexibility, renting a scooter is a popular option, typically costing around $10 per day. This makes exploring Albania’s beautiful landscapes economical at your own pace.


### Albania vs. Paris and Rome


While meals in Paris or Rome cost $20-$30 and budget hotels start at $100, Albania offers rich culture and stunning sights for far less—meals from $5 and hotels under $50, making it a budget-friendly gem.


## Hungary


With an average daily budget of just $40-$70, Hungary offers stunning architecture, rich culture, and delicious cuisine. Here’s why Hungary should be on your travel list.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Budapest**


- **Must-Do:** Visit the iconic Buda Castle, soak in the famous Széchenyi Thermal Baths, and stroll along the Danube River.


2. **Szeged**


- **Must-Do:** Check out the stunning Votive Church and enjoy a leisurely day at the Szeged Open-Air Festival (if you’re there in summer).


### What Makes Hungary Budget-Friendly


- **Incredible Food Deals ($8-$15):** Hungarian dishes are delicious and budget-friendly. Try goulash, a hearty stew of meat, vegetables, and paprika!
- **Affordable Accommodations ($50-$100):** In the capital, you can expect to pay around $50 to $100 per night for a mid-range hotel, while luxury hotels can start from $150 and up.
- **Low Transportation Costs:** Trams and buses are efficient and inexpensive, with tickets around $1. You can even rent a bike for the day for about $10.


### Hungary vs. Paris and Rome


Hungary is an excellent, affordable alternative to Paris and Rome, where meals typically cost $20-$40, and budget hotels start at just $50. In Hungary, you can savor delicious local cuisine and enjoy rich history and stunning sights without breaking the bank. Experience all that Hungary offers at a fraction of the price, making it a smart choice for budget-conscious travelers.


## Estonia


Estonia stands out as a country with rich European history. With an average daily budget of just $50-$80, you can explore this beautiful Baltic country without breaking the bank. Here’s why Estonia deserves a spot on your travel itinerary.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Tallinn**


- **Must-Do:** Wander through the medieval streets of Tallinn’s Old Town, visit the impressive Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, and take panoramic views from Toompea Hill.


2. **Tartu**


- **Must-Do:** Explore the historic University of Tartu, stroll through Tartu’s Old Town, and enjoy the vibrant atmosphere at the Tartu Art Museum.


### What Makes Estonia Budget-Friendly


- **Delicious and Inexpensive Food ($10-$15):** Enjoy traditional Estonian dishes like verivorst (blood sausage) or karask (barley bread) at local restaurants for just $10-$15 per meal.
- **Affordable Accommodations ($15-$35):** Whether you opt for hostels or guesthouses, expect to pay $15-$35 per night, allowing you to maximize your travel budget.
- **Budget-Friendly Transportation:** Estonia has an efficient public transport system. Bus and tram tickets cost around $1, making it easy to get around.


### Estonia vs. Paris and Rome


Estonia is an outstanding summer destination (June to August) with mild temperatures and long daylight hours, perfect for enjoying stunning landscapes and vibrant festivals like Tallinn Old Town Days. In contrast, while spring and fall in Paris and Rome can be beautiful, they often draw large crowds. In late June, Estonia’s unique midsummer celebrations, or Jaanipäev, offer an authentic cultural experience.


## Malta


If you’re seeking an affordable Mediterranean getaway, look no further than Malta. This enchanting archipelago is a treasure trove of breathtaking landscapes, fascinating history, and a lively cultural scene. With an average daily budget of just $60-$100, you can fully immerse yourself in all that Malta offers without straining your wallet.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Valletta**


- **Must-Do:** Stroll through the cobbled streets of Valletta’s Old Town, visit the magnificent St. John’s Co-Cathedral, and take in the views from the Upper Barracca Gardens.


2. **Mdina**


- **Must-Do:** Wander through the medieval streets of the Silent City, explore the Mdina Cathedral, and enjoy panoramic views from the city walls.


### Why Malta is Budget-Friendly


- **Inexpensive Food ($10-$15):** Local restaurants offer fresh seafood and traditional Maltese dishes like rabbit stew (Fenek) for about $10-$15 per meal.
- **Efficient Public Transport:** Malta’s bus system is reliable and budget-friendly, with fares costing around $1.50.


### Malta vs. Paris and Rome


Malta offers a more affordable alternative to Paris and Rome without sacrificing rich culture and history. While hotels in Malta range from $50-$100 per night, you’ll likely pay $150+ in Paris or Rome. Dining and activities, like exploring Valletta or the Ħaġar Qim temples, are also cheaper. You’ll experience stunning Baroque architecture, ancient sites, and vibrant festivals in Malta—all at a fraction of the cost of its more famous counterparts.


## Croatia


With its breathtaking coastlines, historic towns, and delicious cuisine, you can uncover the beauty of Croatia for just $70-$120 daily.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Dubrovnik**


- **Must-Do:** Walk through the ancient city walls for breathtaking views, explore the charming Old Town, and visit Fort Lovrijenac.


2. **Split**


- **Must-Do:** Discover the historic Diocletian’s Palace, relax at Bačvice Beach, and enjoy the vibrant local markets.


### Why Croatia is Budget-Friendly


- **Inexpensive Food ($10-$20):** From fresh seafood to local pastries, you can enjoy delicious meals at local eateries for about $10-$20 per person.
- **Affordable Airfare ($350-$500):** Round-trip flights to Croatia typically cost between $350 and $500, depending on your departure city and travel dates.
- **Accessible Public Transport:** Buses are an economical way to travel between cities, with tickets costing as little as $10 for longer distances.


### Croatia vs. Paris and Rome


Croatia is an enticing alternative to pricier destinations like Paris or Rome. With its stunning Adriatic coastline, visitors can enjoy beautiful beaches and crystal-clear waters, ideal for sunbathing and water sports. Croatia’s unique blend of natural beauty and rich history makes it a perfect choice for an unforgettable European adventure.


## Lithuania


For an affordable European adventure, Lithuania should be high on your list. With its charming cities, rich history, and diverse landscapes, you can explore this beautiful country while keeping your budget in check.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Vilnius**


- **Must-Do:** Wander through the picturesque streets of the Old Town, visit the stunning Vilnius Cathedral, and check out the quirky Uzupis District, known for its bohemian vibe.


2. **Kaunas**


- **Must-Do:** Explore the impressive Kaunas Castle, stroll along the scenic Nemunas River, and visit the Maironis Lithuanian Literature Museum.


### Why Lithuania is Budget-Friendly


- **Inexpensive Food ($5-$15):** Local restaurants offer hearty meals like cepelinai (potato dumplings) and šaltibarščiai (cold beet soup) for just $5-$15.
- **Cheap Airfare ($300-$600):** Round-trip flights to Lithuania typically cost between $300 and $600, depending on your departure city and season.
- **Budget Accommodations ($25-$40):** From hostels to affordable hotels, comfortable stays start around $25-$40 per night.


### Lithuania vs. Paris and Rome


Lithuania’s vibrant local culture includes traditional festivals and delicious cuisine that reflects its diverse heritage. Plus, you can enjoy a[more authentic travel experience](https://wanderlog.com/blog/2024/09/12/the-19-best-travel-planning-apps-for-every-type-of-traveler-2/) with fewer crowds than in Paris or Rome. With its charm and affordability, Lithuania is the ideal European getaway for savvy explorers looking for something different.


## Poland


With its vibrant cities, fascinating history, and stunning landscapes, you can explore the captivating country of Poland on a budget of approximately $50-$100 per day.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Kraków**


- **Must-Do:** Visit the awe-inspiring Wawel Castle, stroll through the historic Main Market Square, and explore the poignant Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial and Museum.


2. **Warsaw**


- **Must-Do:** Visit the beautifully reconstructed Old Town, the Warsaw Uprising Museum, and relax in Lazienki Park.


### Why Poland is Budget-Friendly


- **Affordable Food ($5 and $15):** Dive into hearty Polish cuisine, with meals typically costing between $5 and $15. Savor traditional dishes like pierogi (dumplings filled with potatoes, cheese, or meat), bigosh (hunter’s stew), and żurek (sour rye soup). Don’t forget to try placki ziemniaczane (potato pancakes) and indulge in delicious sernik (cheesecake) for dessert.
- **Inexpensive Accommodations ($15-$40):** From cozy hostels to budget hotels, expect to pay around $15-$40 per night.
- **Affordable Public Transport:** Buses and trams are convenient and affordable, with tickets costing around $1-$2.


### Poland vs. Paris and Rome


Poland offers a captivating alternative to the well-trodden paths of Paris and Rome. Experience rich culture through its vibrant festivals and traditions, such as the lively Kraków Christmas Market and the historic Wianki Festival. Marvel at the stunning architecture, from the gothic splendor of Wawel Castle to the vibrant murals in Łódź. Explore unique attractions like the Wieliczka Salt Mine, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and enjoy the warm hospitality of locals eager to share their heritage.


## North Macedonia


With its breathtaking landscapes, rich history, and vibrant culture, you can immerse yourself in this beautiful country for about $40-$80 per day.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Skopje**


- **Must-Do:** Visit the impressive Stone Bridge, explore the historic Old Bazaar, and check out Macedonia Square with its iconic statues.


2. **Ohrid**


- **Must-Do:** Discover the stunning Lake Ohrid, visit the ancient Church of St. John at Kaneo, and explore the Ohrid Fortress.


### Why North Macedonia is Budget-Friendly


- **Affordable Food ($5 to $15):** Enjoy North Macedonia’s local cuisine, with meals averaging $5 to $15. Try dishes like tavče gravče (baked beans), ajvar (pepper spread), and skara (grilled meats), along with savory pita and sweet baklava.
- **Cheap Airfare ($400-$800):** Round-trip flights to North Macedonia usually cost between $400 and $800, depending on your starting point and the time of year.
- **Cost-Effective Transport:** Public transportation is affordable, with bus tickets costing just $1-$2 for local routes.


### North Macedonia vs. Paris and Rome


North Macedonia is a captivating alternative to Paris and Rome. Discover the stunning beauty of Lake Ohrid, one of Europe’s oldest lakes, and explore the vibrant culture of Skopje with its unique architecture and lively bazaars. Uncover rich history and enjoy the warm hospitality.


## Bulgaria


Bulgaria is an affordable gem filled with stunning landscapes, rich history, and vibrant culture. You can experience this beautiful country[on a budget of around $40-$70 per day](https://wanderlog.com/blog/2024/08/19/the-8-best-travel-budgeting-tools-of-2024/) .


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Sofia**


- **Must-Do:** Visit the iconic Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, wander through Vitosha Boulevard, and explore the historic Boyana Church.


2. **Plovdiv**


- **Must-Do:** Explore the Old Town with its Roman theater, stroll along the Kapana Arts District, and visit the ancient ruins of Plovdiv Roman Theatre.


### Why Bulgaria is Budget-Friendly


- **Inexpensive Food ($5 to $15):** Indulge in delicious Bulgarian cuisine without breaking the bank, with meals typically ranging from $5 to $15. Savor traditional dishes like banitsa (flaky pastry filled with cheese and eggs), shopska salad (fresh salad with tomatoes, cucumbers, and feta), and kavarma (slow-cooked meat stew). Don’t miss trying tarator (cold cucumber soup) and the sweet treat of baklava.
- **Affordable Accommodation ($15-$30):** Expect to pay around $15-$30 per night for hostels or guesthouses.
- **Low Transport Costs:** Public transport is very affordable, with bus tickets costing around $1-$2.


### Bulgaria vs. Paris and Rome


Compared to cities like Paris and Rome, where dining out can easily cost $20-$50 per meal and hotel prices start at $100, Bulgaria is a breath of fresh air for budget travelers. With its rich cultural heritage and stunning landscapes, Bulgaria offers incredible experiences without breaking the bank.


## Slovakia


Its neighbors often overshadow Slovakia, but it boasts breathtaking nature, charming cities, and a vibrant culture. You can explore this stunning country on a budget of about $40-$80 daily.


### Top 2 Cities to Explore


1. **Bratislava**


- **Must-Do:** Visit the historic Bratislava Castle, stroll along the Danube River, and explore the charming Old Town.


2. **Košice**


- **Must-Do:** Discover the stunning St. Elisabeth Cathedral, wander through the historic Hlavná ulica, and explore the beautiful Košice State Theatre.


### Why Slovakia is Wallet-Friendly


- **Affordable Food ($5 to $15):** Savor hearty Slovak dishes at prices typically ranging from $5 to $15. Try bryndzové halušky (potato dumplings with sheep cheese), kapustnica (sauerkraut soup), and gulaš (goulash). For a delightful treat, don’t miss trdelník, a sweet pastry.
- **Cheap Airfare ($400-$800):** Round-trip flights to Slovakia can cost between $400 and $800, depending on your departure location.
- **Inexpensive Accommodations ($15-$30):** Expect to pay around $15-$30 for hostels or budget hotels.


### Slovakia vs. Paris and Rome


Compared to popular cities like Paris and Rome, where dining out can cost $20-$50 per meal and hotel prices start around $100, Slovakia is a hidden treasure for budget travelers. Its stunning landscapes and rich history offer an unforgettable experience without the price tag.


### Final Thoughts


Exploring the lesser-known, budget-friendly destinations in Europe allows you to experience rich history, stunning landscapes, and vibrant cultures without breaking the bank. These countries offer unforgettable adventures that rival famous European hotspots from Bulgaria to Slovakia!
