# Introducing SelectQuote Local

SelectQuote is launching SelectQuote Local, a franchise model intended to extend the company’s distribution platform and healthcare services capabilities into local markets through owner-operated franchises. SelectQuote Local is structured to allow franchisees to operate businesses using SelectQuote’s systems, technology, lead generation, training, and established carrier relationships, together with local market knowledge.


SelectQuote Local is designed for leaders who want to own and operate their own business under an established insurance brand with over 40 years of experience. Franchisees will be responsible for day-to-day business operations, including sales activities, team leadership, and operating in accordance with the SelectQuote system.


## **Who We’re Looking For**


SelectQuote Local may be a fit for individuals with experience such as:


-


Professionals with sales, business development, or leadership backgrounds


-


Managers or executives interested in business ownership


-


Leaders who want to recruit, train, and manage teams


Prior insurance experience may be beneficial but is not required. SelectQuote Local may also be suitable for experienced operators seeking a franchise business opportunity.


Additional details regarding SelectQuote Local are expected to be provided in the coming months.
