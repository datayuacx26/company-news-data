# Vision in Motion: How Gauzy’s Technology Ecosystem Advances Road and Driver Safety

**Key takeaways:**


- Smart-Vision system is the industry’s first ADAS-ready camera monitor system with R46 homologation and ISO 26262 certification, providing ultra-wide HD vision and AI-powered dynamic features.
- The system supports modular ADAS upgrades including Smart Panning, Extrapanning, and blind spot detection compliant with UNECE R151.
- Complementary technologies such as SafetySide (lateral VRU detection), Driver Monitoring System (DMS), and Savety-Door (in-cabin protection) ensure comprehensive driver and road safety.
- Gauzy’s solutions are certified globally (IATF, FMCSA, CCMTA, ISO 21434 cybersecurity) and built for real-world fleet challenges
- Supported by expert training and installation, Gauzy is shaping the future of safe and scalable commercial mobility with next-generation ADAS innovations.


**Part 1: Reinventing Visibility with the Smart-Vision System Delivering Intelligent, Real-Time Awareness Beyond Traditional Mirrors**


At the core of Gauzy’s safety ecosystem lies the Smart-Vision system, a modular, AI-enhanced, ADAS-ready camera monitor system designed for commercial vehicles such as buses, trucks, and articulated fleets. By replacing or augmenting traditional mirrors, Smart-Vision offers a superior, regulation-compliant field of view that adapts dynamically to driver behavior and vehicle motion.


**Key features include:**


- Ultra-wide HD camera feeds processed with AI for sharper object recognition and reduced distortion.
- Low-latency video transmission powered by edge computing for instant situational awareness.
- Auto-adjusting brightness and contrast to maintain clarity in all lighting and weather conditions.
- Ergonomically placed, anti-glare in-cabin monitors that reduce driver fatigue and cognitive load.
- Advanced ADAS-enabled modules include Smart Panning, Extrapanning, 360° surround view, and Smart Maneuver Lines to assist precise maneuvering.
