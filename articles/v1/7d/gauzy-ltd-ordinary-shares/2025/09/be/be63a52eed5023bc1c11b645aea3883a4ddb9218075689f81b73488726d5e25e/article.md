# Luxury Meets Efficiency: Advanced Shading Solutions for Aircraft Completion Centers

**Smarter Cabin Design for Greater Efficiency**


Electromechanical shades use slimline mechanisms and refined fabrics to reduce bulk compared to traditional blinds, while smart glass panels replace multi-layer assemblies with a single integrated system. Both solutions free up valuable cabin space and contribute to more efficient design and performance.


**Faster Turnarounds with Modular Installation**


Electromechanical and dimmable shades are engineered for straightforward installation with standardized components. Both shorten refurbishment cycles, enabling completion centers to deliver more projects annually.


**Personalized Luxury Through Customization**


Electromechanical shades provide luxury with customizable fabric colors and finishes. Smart glass enables adjustable transparency, multiple tint levels, and embedded branding. Both supports highly personalized, cohesive cabin environments.


Gauzy is the only provider in the market specializing in highly customizable high-end solutions that seamlessly combine electromechanical shades with dimmable lenses for one, two, or three windows. All shades can be synchronized and controlled remotely or via an integrated touch panel.


**Certified Reliability for Aviation Standards**


All Gauzy shading systems undergo rigorous FAA and EASA certification testing for safety, durability, and flammability. Both electromechanical shades and smart glass offer proven reliability backed by aviation certification.
