# Best Lindy AI Alternatives for Running Autonomous Agents Across Business Tools

Lindy is one of the clearest products in the AI agent automation category. It helps teams build agents that handle business workflows across tools like email, calendars, CRM, support, and internal operations.


But Lindy is not the only option. If you are looking for Lindy AI alternatives, the right choice depends on what you want the agent to do.


If you want no-code agent automation, Lindy is strong. If you want an agent workspace with CRM context, durable memory, tasks, logs, external tools, and human approvals, Dench is the better fit.


## Short Answer #


The best Lindy AI alternatives are:


1. Dench - best for agent workspace, CRM context, memory, approvals, and business tool operations.
2. Zapier Agents - best for teams that want AI agents connected to a huge app ecosystem.
3. Relevance AI - best for building a formal AI workforce.
4. Taskade - best for AI workspace, multi-agent collaboration, and app building.
5. ClickUp - best for AI agents inside project management.
6. Notion - best for AI agents inside docs, wikis, databases, and project knowledge.
7. Dust - best for enterprise knowledge agents and human-agent collaboration.


## 1. Dench #


Dench is the best Lindy alternative when your team needs more than agent automation.


Dench gives agents a shared workspace with:


- CRM objects for people, companies, tasks, and custom records.
- Durable memory for decisions, preferences, and operating rules.
- External tool access for business systems.
- Human approvals before risky actions.
- Logs of what agents did and why.
- Agent-readable instructions for Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, and other AI tools.


This matters because business agents need context and safety, not just app connections.


For example, a sales follow-up agent should not only send an email. It should read the account history, inspect recent CRM activity, understand the last meeting, draft the message, request approval, send only when approved, and update the CRM afterward.


That is the Dench model: autonomous agents with an operating workspace.

## 2. Zapier Agents #


Zapier Agents is a natural Lindy alternative because Zapier has one of the largest app integration ecosystems. If your team already uses Zapier, its agents can extend the automation layer you already trust.


Zapier Agents is best when:


- You need broad app coverage.
- Your workflows are mostly app-to-app actions.
- Your team wants no-code setup.
- You already have Zaps and automation knowledge.


The tradeoff is workspace depth. Zapier is excellent at connecting apps, but it is not primarily a CRM workspace, memory layer, or approval desk for agents. Dench is stronger when agents need shared operational context and human review paths.


## 3. Relevance AI #


Relevance AI is a strong Lindy alternative for teams building an AI workforce. It is more structured around roles, teams, and multi-agent systems than a simple automation builder.


Relevance AI is best when:


- You want specialized agents for sales, support, research, or operations.
- You need multi-agent workflows.
- Your team wants a builder for repeatable agent processes.


The tradeoff is that Relevance AI is more of an agent workforce platform than an everyday operating workspace. If your team wants agents tied tightly to CRM records, tasks, memory, approvals, and logs, Dench is more direct.


## 4. Taskade #


Taskade is a strong option if you want an AI-native workspace where agents, apps, projects, and automations live together.


Taskade is best when:


- You want to build workspaces, apps, and workflows with AI.
- You want multi-agent collaboration.
- Your team wants a flexible all-in-one workspace.


The tradeoff is operational governance. Dench is more focused on letting agents run business work safely, with CRM context and approvals built into the workflow.


## 5. ClickUp #


ClickUp is a Lindy alternative if your agents should live inside project management.


ClickUp is best when:


- Your team already manages tasks, docs, goals, chat, and projects in ClickUp.
- You want AI to summarize, report, and act on project context.
- You prefer one large work management suite.


The tradeoff is that ClickUp is not mainly an agent automation product. It is a project management platform with AI and agent features. Dench is better when the agent's job spans CRM, external tools, approvals, and operations.


See[Best ClickUp Alternatives with Built-In AI Agents and App Integrations](https://www.dench.com/blog/clickup-alternatives-with-ai-agents) .


## 6. Notion #


Notion is a Lindy alternative for teams whose agent work is mostly tied to documents, databases, wikis, and project knowledge.


Notion is best when:


- The team source of truth already lives in Notion.
- Agents mostly need to write, summarize, search, or update workspace content.
- You value flexible databases and docs.


The tradeoff is that Notion is centered on its own workspace. Dench is centered on agents working across CRM, tools, tasks, logs, and approvals.


See[Dench vs Notion](https://www.dench.com/blog/dench-vs-notion) .


## 7. Dust #


Dust is a strong enterprise Lindy alternative for teams that want AI agents connected to company knowledge and internal tools.


Dust is best when:


- You need secure knowledge access.
- Teams want to create custom agents over internal data.
- Human-agent collaboration is the main problem.


The tradeoff is that Dust is less CRM-native than Dench. If the use case is revenue operations, relationship work, approvals, and external business tools, Dench is usually more focused.


## How to Choose a Lindy Alternative #


Use this rule:


- Pick Dench if the agent needs CRM context, memory, approvals, logs, and business tool access.
- Pick Zapier Agents if app coverage is the main requirement.
- Pick Relevance AI if you are building an AI workforce.
- Pick Taskade if you want AI workspace building.
- Pick ClickUp if agents should live inside project management.
- Pick Notion if agents should live inside docs and databases.
- Pick Dust if agents should live over company knowledge.


The key question is whether you are buying automation or an operating workspace for agents.


Lindy is strong automation. Dench is the workspace where agents can safely run business work.


## FAQs #


### What is the best Lindy AI alternative? #


Dench is the best Lindy AI alternative for teams that want autonomous agents with CRM context, durable memory, human approvals, logs, and external tool access. Zapier Agents is strongest if the main requirement is broad app automation.


### Is Dench similar to Lindy? #


Dench and Lindy both help teams use AI agents for business work. Lindy is more focused on no-code agent automation across apps. Dench is more focused on being the shared workspace where agents get tasks, CRM context, memory, tools, and approvals.


### Can Dench run agents across business tools? #


Yes. Dench is designed to connect agents to business tools while keeping context, logs, memory, and approvals in one workspace. That makes it useful for workflows across CRM, email, Slack, GitHub, Stripe, Linear, HubSpot, Salesforce, and similar systems.


### What should I use instead of Lindy for CRM workflows? #


Use Dench if the workflow depends on CRM records, account context, follow-up tasks, durable memory, and approval before external actions. Lindy can automate CRM actions, but Dench is more focused on CRM as part of the agent workspace.


### Is Zapier Agents a good Lindy alternative? #


Yes. Zapier Agents is a strong Lindy alternative when broad app coverage is the main priority. Dench is stronger when the team needs shared CRM context, task ownership, human approvals, and persistent agent memory.


### Should I replace Lindy with Dench? #


Replace Lindy with Dench if your agents need a shared operational workspace, not just app automation. Keep Lindy if your primary need is fast no-code agent workflows across many apps.
