# Best ClickUp Alternatives with Built-In AI Agents and App Integrations

ClickUp is a strong all-in-one work management platform. It combines tasks, docs, chat, goals, calendar, dashboards, automation, and AI. For many teams, that is exactly the point: one place to plan and track work.


But if you are searching for ClickUp alternatives with built-in AI agents and app integrations, you may be looking for a different center of gravity.


ClickUp is project management with AI agents. Dench is an agent workspace and AI CRM where agents get tasks, context, tools, memory, logs, and approvals.


## Short Answer #


The best ClickUp alternatives with AI agents are:


1. Dench - best for autonomous agents, CRM context, approvals, memory, and external tool operations.
2. Notion - best for docs, wikis, databases, projects, and Notion-native agents.
3. Taskade - best for AI workspace building, agents, apps, and automations.
4. Lindy - best for no-code agents across business tools.
5. Zapier Agents - best for app automation across a very large integration network.
6. Relevance AI - best for building an AI workforce.
7. Dust - best for enterprise knowledge agents and human-agent collaboration.


## 1. Dench #


Dench is the best ClickUp alternative if your team wants agents to run business work, not just manage project tasks.


Dench gives agents:


- CRM context for people, companies, tasks, and custom records.
- Durable memory for decisions and operating rules.
- Human approvals before risky actions.
- External tool access for business systems.
- Logs that show what agents did and why.
- Agent-readable setup for tools like Codex, Claude Code, and Cursor.


That makes Dench a better fit for workflows like:


- Pipeline hygiene.
- Meeting prep.
- CRM updates.
- Follow-up drafting.
- Lead research.
- Customer account monitoring.
- Internal ops tasks.
- Agent work review and approval.


ClickUp is excellent when the task is human project management. Dench is stronger when the task is autonomous agent operations across business tools.

## 2. Notion #


Notion is a strong ClickUp alternative for teams that want a more flexible docs-and-database workspace.


Notion is best when:


- Your team needs a wiki, docs, databases, and project pages.
- You want AI to search, summarize, create, and update workspace content.
- You prefer flexible structure over traditional project management.


Compared with ClickUp, Notion usually feels lighter and more document-native. Compared with Dench, Notion is less focused on CRM-native agent operations, approvals, and external business tool work.


Read[Dench vs Notion](https://www.dench.com/blog/dench-vs-notion) .


## 3. Taskade #


Taskade is a good ClickUp alternative for teams that want AI agents, automations, apps, memory, and team collaboration in one workspace.


Taskade is best when:


- You want to build workflows and apps with AI.
- You want multi-agent collaboration.
- You want a flexible AI-native workspace rather than a conventional project management suite.


The tradeoff is that Taskade is not primarily an agent approval desk or AI CRM. Dench is more focused on the operational layer around agents.


## 4. Lindy #


Lindy is a strong ClickUp alternative if the reason you are leaving ClickUp is that you need real business process automation across apps.


Lindy is best when:


- You want no-code AI agents.
- Your workflows span tools like email, calendar, CRM, and support.
- Your team wants agents to handle repeatable operational tasks.


The tradeoff is that Lindy is more automation-centric than workspace-centric. Dench is better if the agent needs CRM context, durable memory, approvals, and a shared place to log work.


Read[Best Lindy AI Alternatives](https://www.dench.com/blog/lindy-ai-alternatives) .


## 5. Zapier Agents #


Zapier Agents is a strong option when integrations are the deciding factor. Zapier connects to thousands of apps, so it is useful when your workflow involves many systems and you need fast no-code automation.


Zapier Agents is best when:


- App coverage matters more than project management.
- Your team already uses Zapier.
- You want AI actions across a large integration network.


The tradeoff is that Zapier is not mainly a shared workspace for CRM context, approvals, and agent logs. Dench is stronger when those are central.


## 6. Relevance AI #


Relevance AI is a ClickUp alternative for teams that want an AI workforce rather than a project management workspace.


Relevance AI is best when:


- You want to design and manage specialized agents.
- You need multi-agent workflows.
- You are automating GTM, support, research, or operations processes.


The tradeoff is that Relevance AI is more builder-oriented. Dench is more direct for teams that want agents operating inside a shared CRM and approval workspace.


## 7. Dust #


Dust is a strong option for enterprise teams that want custom agents connected to company knowledge and internal tools.


Dust is best when:


- Internal knowledge access is the primary problem.
- Teams want collaborative custom agents.
- Security and enterprise rollout matter.


The tradeoff is that Dust is less of a ClickUp replacement for task management and less of a CRM-native operations workspace than Dench.

## When to Pick Dench Instead of ClickUp #


Pick Dench if:


- Your team wants autonomous agents, not only AI summaries.
- CRM context matters.
- Agents need to work across business tools.
- Risky actions need human approval.
- You want durable memory and logs for agent work.
- Your team uses external AI agents like Codex, Claude Code, or Cursor.


Pick ClickUp if:

- Your team already lives in ClickUp tasks and docs.

## FAQs #


### What is the best ClickUp alternative with built-in AI agents? #


Dench is the best ClickUp alternative for teams that want autonomous agents with CRM context, app integrations, memory, logs, and human approvals. Notion and Taskade are strong alternatives when the main need is an AI workspace for docs, projects, and databases.


### Is Dench a ClickUp alternative? #


Yes, for teams whose main need is agent operations. Dench is not a traditional project management clone. It is a workspace where AI agents can claim tasks, use CRM context, access approved tools, request approvals, and report completed work.


### Does ClickUp have AI agents? #


Yes. ClickUp has Brain, Autopilot Agents, and Super Agents inside its work management platform. The difference is that ClickUp centers on project management, while Dench centers on autonomous agents operating across CRM, tools, memory, approvals, and logs.


### Which ClickUp alternative is best for app integrations? #


Zapier Agents is strongest for broad app coverage. Dench is strongest when app integrations need to connect back to CRM context, task ownership, approvals, and persistent memory.


### Which ClickUp alternative is best for CRM workflows? #


Dench is the strongest fit for CRM workflows because CRM objects, tasks, memory, approvals, and agent logs are part of the workspace model. ClickUp can approximate CRM workflows with custom fields and templates, but it is not primarily an AI CRM.


### Should I switch from ClickUp to Dench? #


Switch if your bottleneck is autonomous business work across tools, not project tracking. Keep ClickUp if your team mainly needs project management, dashboards, and task coordination.
