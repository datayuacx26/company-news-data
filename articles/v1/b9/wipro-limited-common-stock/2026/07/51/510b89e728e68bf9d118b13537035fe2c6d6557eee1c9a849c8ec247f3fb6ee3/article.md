# Agentic AI Fabric for Autonomous Network Operations

Telecom networks are entering a decisive phase of transformation. As networks grow more complex and service expectations become real time and intent driven, traditional automation approaches are no longer sufficient. The industry is moving toward AI native, autonomous network operations, where intelligent systems can reason, decide, and act with minimal human intervention.


Agentic AI Fabric for Autonomous Network Operations presents a new architectural paradigm—one that enables distributed AI agents to collaborate across network, IT, OSS/BSS, and customer operations, delivering scalable, governed autonomy across hybrid and multi cloud environments.
