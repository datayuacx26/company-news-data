# Consumer technology trends report 2025 shaping electronics growth - Wipro

# Industry Futures:
Consumer Electronics by 2030


Wipro’s Industry Futures for Consumer Electronics explores the future of consumer electronics, shaped by sustainable innovation, intelligent devices, energy-efficient devices, consumer electronics solutions and the rapid expansion of iOS. By 2030, the sector must prioritize energy-efficient designs, circular manufacturing, and personalized user experiences to remain competitive. The report highlights key technological advancements, emerging applications, and strategic actions for long-term growth.


[Download Report](https://www.wipro.com/content/dam/nexus/en/innovation/2025-pdfs/consumer-electronics-pov.pdf)


## Hyperpersonalization


Hyper-personalization will redefine the future of consumer electronics by transforming user experiences through adaptive devices that learn and adjust to individual preferences. Emotional intelligence in devices enables empathetic interactions, enhancing personalized user experience. Holographic communication revolutionizes remote interactions with immersive, lifelike experiences, while biometric technology ensures secure, personalized access tailored to unique physiological signatures—further reinforcing a personalized user experience. Together, these innovations create a seamless, intuitive ecosystem that epitomizes the future of consumer electronics.


## Intelligent Devices


Intelligent devices will reshape daily life through cutting-edge advancements. Personal robots assist with daily tasks—from household chores to companionship—bringing utility and comfort. Cognitive wearables monitor and analyze health data, offering personalized insights to improve well-being and productivity. Smart security devices combine advanced sensors and AI-driven features to proactively safeguard homes and offices. Moreover, intelligent devices incorporate embedded agentic intelligence to autonomously learn from user habits and adapt, delivering optimized solutions while integrating energy efficient devices for sustainable innovation. These advancements are driving the evolution of consumer electronics technology.


## Internet of Senses (IoS) Expansion


Multisensory virtual reality creates immersive environments that engage sight, sound, touch, and beyond, making digital worlds feel tangible and setting the stage for the future of consumer electronics. With IoS expansion initiatives, brain-computer interfaces establish a direct communication channel between the human brain and technology, enabling effortless control and accessibility. Sensory commerce utilizes these advancements to transform shopping, offering buyers interactive experiences that stimulate the senses. Immersive device experiences combine cutting-edge technologies to create an engaging environment that transports users into a new realm of interaction, further bolstered by strategic IoS expansion.


## Smart Device Ecosystems


A harmonious blend of interconnected technology will be created by smart device ecosystems to simplify everyday living. Unified device ecosystems ensure seamless compatibility, enabling devices to work together without friction. Low-power, high-capacity wireless connectivity allows fast, efficient communication across systems while conserving energy. Integrated smart living leverages these ecosystems to create an environment where convenience meets automation for enhanced comfort. Ambient intelligence brings sophistication by allowing devices to intuitively anticipate and respond to user needs, creating personalized and effortless interactions in smart homes and beyond.


## Sustainable and Energy-Efficient Devices


Technology is taking significant strides toward sustainability with eco-friendly innovations. Biodegradable electronics minimize waste by decomposing safely at the end of their lifecycle. Energy-harvesting devices capture ambient energy, such as solar or kinetic power, to sustain functionality without external sources, making energy efficient devices essential. Self-healing materials ensure devices can repair themselves from wear and tear, extending longevity and reducing electronic waste. Digital product passports offer transparency about a product’s lifecycle, showcasing sustainable features and encouraging informed, eco-conscious decisions. The integration of energy efficient devices with sustainable innovation is driving this transformative approach.


By 2030, the consumer electronics industry will undergo accelerated advancements, necessitating breakthroughs in AI-driven innovations, next-generation materials, and efficient manufacturing processes. To stay competitive, enterprises must prioritize sustainable practices, integrate energy efficient designs, and establish robust, adaptive supply chains. Collaborating with industry pioneers such as Wipro will empower organizations to implement cutting-edge technologies and confidently navigate the complexities of this evolving sector, securing growth and resilience in an increasingly dynamic global market.
