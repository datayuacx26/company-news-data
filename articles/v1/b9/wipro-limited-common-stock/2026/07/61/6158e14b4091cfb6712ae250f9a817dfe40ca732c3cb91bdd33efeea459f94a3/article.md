# An Agentic AI Security Framework for Manufacturing

Secure the future of autonomous manufacturing with Agentic AI Security, empowered by intelligent AI Agents and Zero Trust security principles.


**Transforming Manufacturing Security with Agentic AI**


As manufacturing industries embrace AI-driven automation and Agentic AI initiatives, ensuring robust manufacturing security becomes essential for resilience and growth. Traditional security models struggle to keep pace with the complexity of IT/OT convergence and the emergence of agentic AI security frameworks.


Discover how Wipro’s Agentic AI Security Framework combines Zero Trust principles with adaptive AI to safeguard operations, protect intellectual property, and ensure compliance.


**Key Highlights:**


- **Zero Trust for Manufacturing:** Continuous verification and least privilege access.


- **Agentic AI in Action:** Autonomous isolation of compromised assets during cyberattacks, enabled by intelligent AI agents.


- **Identity Management for AI Agents:** Secure onboarding and lifecycle governance.


- **Architecture Blueprint:** A multi-layered security ecosystem.
