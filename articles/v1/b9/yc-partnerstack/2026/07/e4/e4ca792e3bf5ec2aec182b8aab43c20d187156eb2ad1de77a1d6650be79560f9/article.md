# PartnerStack has a new Zapier integration!

We’re excited to announce that we now[integrate with Zapier](http://bit.ly/PartnerSZap) , the web automation app that allows you to integrate with the web applications you use regularly.


PartnerStack already integrates natively with Stripe, SalesForce, ChargeBee and[others](https://www.partnerstack.com/integrations) - and our new Zap will make connecting to us even easier.


Our PartnerStack Zapier App gives customers seamless integrations with Hubspot, Braintree, Paypal, MailChimp, Slack, and many other applications with our[simple Zapier templates](http://bit.ly/PartnerSZap) .


This new integration lets our customers connect with different actions in PartnerStack automatically. They can trigger zaps when a new partner signs up, a new customer is created, a new lead or deal is created, a new reward or offer is generated, a new transaction is created, etc. For example, when new customers are created and pay, you can connect the ‘customer created’ trigger to a ‘create transaction’ action that creates a new transaction every time a new customer is added.


Companies can use our Zapier templates to connect with their partners and integrate with other applications on and off Zapier - all the great things you used to do with our API but quicker and easier! Or you can always create a custom workflow with PartnerStack and Zapier.


The new Zapier App even enables finding customers or partners and tracking events by sending PartnerStack data and events to your data warehouse.


Give the new PartnerStack Zap a try and let us know what you think!
