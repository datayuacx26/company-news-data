# 6 Affiliate Program Tactics You Need to Win in 2026

If revamping your affiliate program was on your OKR list, here’s the piece for you. Affiliate marketing is changing fast — AI search is reshaping how buyers discover and evaluate products, which means how you recruit, support and measure affiliates needs to evolve too.


Below, we cover some of the strategies affiliate partners can implement to drive sales, increase revenue and win more affiliates in 2026: offering beginner-friendly tiers, building in competitive incentives, giving partners dedicated support, tracking the right data (including AI-driven attribution), bundling offers and turning advocates into affiliates.


## Try providing beginner-focused tiers


When executed well, affiliate marketing can be a highly effective growth channel, but it can also be hard for incoming B2B affiliates to stand out in a crowded[influencer](https://partnerstack.com/articles/b2b-influencer-marketing-strategies-for-vendors-and-influencers) marketplace. It’s equally difficult for[affiliate programs](https://partnerstack.com/glossary/affiliate-program) to appeal to newcomers to the affiliate space over the biggest name-brand partners.


To capture the attention of prospective partners, affiliate program managers can offer a special[tier](https://partnerstack.com/glossary/tiers) for beginners. This tier might have no minimum traffic requirements or payout requirements, or base sliding scale commissions on clicks instead of sales.


Providing a dedicated, low-barrier entry point can make your affiliate program attractive for incoming partners.

## Add competitive commission incentives


Many top affiliate programs stand out by offering perks on top of commissions. The additional perks can help keep affiliates[engaged long-term](https://partnerstack.com/articles/how-to-keep-top-performing-affiliates-engaged-and-how-to-push-them-away) and motivated to work with one particular program over another.


The travel sector is a great example of this. TripAdvisor, for instance, pays out 50 per cent of the commission it receives when a user visits their site via affiliate link, then clicks out to a hotel booking site. Sandals affiliates, meanwhile, can win free trips on top of their regular commissions. The potential incentive of a Caribbean vacation becomes a motivating factor for travel influencers to partner with one brand over another.


Partner programs can also try providing freebies for affiliates to give away to their networks. This way, users get free perks while the affiliate and your company earn commissions and additional sales. It’s a win-win-win.


## Support partners with dedicated affiliate management


One of the best things affiliate partners can get is answers to their questions — whether that’s through a dedicated support line or a marketing manager who works on their account.


Some top programs offer direct access to affiliate support teams. Others provide one-on-one access to account managers to help partners every step of the way. Determine what resources your brand needs to provide based on its range of affiliate partners.


When you're working with content creators, you can also offer help creating key narratives that will help affiliate content show up on SERPs and in answer engines — a growing part of affiliate management as more buyer research happens inside AI tools instead of traditional search.

## Refine your affiliate program based on data


Unfortunately, the “set it and forget it” approach won’t stand out when it comes to affiliate programs. Tracking the[relevant KPIs of your affiliate program](https://partnerstack.com/articles/partner-program-kpis-metrics-you-should-measure-and-optimize) and adjusting as necessary is your best bet in optimizing your affiliate strategy.


Affiliate managers can use insights gathered from data like revenue earned, conversion rates, leads generated and unique sales to rank affiliates and determine which affiliates are bringing in the most leads and sales. Then you can shift your affiliate lineups in response.


It's also worth revisiting how you're tracking impact in the first place. As more research happens inside AI tools rather than through a traditional click-through, some affiliate influence won't show up in a[click-based attribution model](https://partnerstack.com/articles/ai-search-attribution-revops) at all. An affiliate whose content gets cited in an AI-generated answer may be shaping a buyer's decision well before — or instead of — any trackable click. Programs that only measure last-click or first-click conversions risk undervaluing exactly the partners doing this kind of influence-building work.


Attribution models are starting to shift in response — moving toward multi-touch approaches that can credit an affiliate for a citation or mention, not just a click, and toward[tools built to close attribution gaps like these](https://partnerstack.com/articles/partner-attribution-audit) alongside traditional metrics.

## Bundle your affiliate offerings


Who doesn’t love a deal? Pairing an affiliate offer with a fellow vendor’s offer adds value, especially when the end user is receiving two goods or services for one checkout.


The incentive is manifold: new customers are incentivized with a discount, while both vendors can leverage their respective audiences as well as the affiliate’s.


Bundling can also work alongside other promotions like coupon codes. When deployed correctly, they’re cost-effective and trackable, so you can see how and where they’re being used, then make adjustments to the offering where you need to.


## Turn existing advocates into affiliates


Do you have customers singing your praises? With affiliate focus shifting from big publishers to niche creators, this is an opportunity to recruit the customers who already love you as[revenue-generating partners](https://partnerstack.com/resources/guides/recruit-your-first-100-revenue-generating-partners) in your program. Customers who already advocate for you (or are willing to) can help you stand out in increasingly crowded spaces where it’s less about having the biggest reach and more about having the most trusted voice.


To convince them to join your program, you can offer your usual competitive commissions along with other incentives like product access — which should appeal because they already use your product.
