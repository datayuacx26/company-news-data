# The 10 Best Places to Buy Aluminum Online in 2026: 6061, 7075, and 7050 Plate Ranked

### What is the best place to buy aluminum online?


It depends on order size and certification requirements. For certified 6061, 7075, or 7050 plate with mill certs, instant online pricing, and night or weekend delivery, NOX METALS. For a single very small piece with no minimum order or cut fees, Online Metals. For metal you can pick up within the hour, Metal Supermarkets. For a mixed bill of materials across several metals, Ryerson. For metals and engineering plastics on one order, Alro Steel with its 80-plus locations. For aluminum-specific expertise and recycled content, Schupan. For mid-size cut-to-size aluminum orders, Midwest Steel & Aluminum. For alloy breadth on one catalog, Speedy Metals. For precision sawn blanks, TW Metals or Howard Precision Metals.


### How many aluminum suppliers are there in the US?


The metals distribution industry is large and highly fragmented. The Metals Service Center Institute alone represents roughly 400 member companies operating from about 1,500 locations, and that captures only part of the field. Counting independent service centers, regional distributors, aluminum specialists, and online-only sellers, there are north of 4,000 metal suppliers and service center locations across North America. The number that can actually supply certified 7075 or 7050 plate, cut to size, with mill certs and heat traceability, is a tiny fraction of that.


### Where can I buy 6061 aluminum plate online?


All ten suppliers on this list carry 6061. NOX METALS stocks 6061-T651 from 0.25 to 10 inches thick in 48 and 60 inch widths with instant online quoting and mill certs included. Online Metals is the better choice for a single small piece because it has no minimum order and no cut fees, and Metal Supermarkets is better still if you can drive to a store. Ryerson, Alro, Midwest Steel & Aluminum, Schupan, Speedy Metals, TW Metals, and Howard Precision all stock 6061 as well, with different strengths in breadth, proximity, inventory depth, aluminum specialization, and sawing capacity.


### Where can I buy 7075 and 7050 aluminum plate online?


7075 and 7050 are narrower markets than 6061 because they are aerospace alloys, so fewer suppliers carry them in depth and fewer will quote them online. NOX METALS stocks both from 0.25 through 6 inches, in T651 and T7351 for 7075 and T7451 and T7651 for 7050, with instant quoting and DFARS-compliant material available. TW Metals stocks 7075 plate in both T651 and T7351 in the standard 48.5 and 60.5 inch widths, and Ryerson, Schupan, and Speedy Metals carry 7075 as well. Thick 7050 above 3 inches is tight everywhere and mill lead times commonly run 12 to 20 weeks, so buy from stock when you can.


### Is it cheaper to buy aluminum online or from a local supplier?


Online is usually cheaper for anything beyond a single small piece, because online suppliers publish pricing and compete on it, while walk-in counter pricing is often set for convenience. The exception is urgency. If you need material today, a local branch pickup can be worth paying more for. The larger savings lever is not online versus local at all, it is buying the right shape of material: a certified drop or remnant that fits your part typically runs 20 to 40% below cut-to-size pricing anywhere.


### Do online aluminum suppliers provide mill certifications?


Some do as standard, some charge extra, and some cannot provide them at all, so you have to ask explicitly. Certifications with heat number and lot traceability are mandatory for aerospace, defense, and most structural work. NOX METALS includes mill certs, heat numbers, and full lot traceability on every order and is ISO 9001:2015 certified. If a supplier cannot tell you the heat number of the plate you are buying, that material is not suitable for a certified program.


### What is the cheapest way to buy aluminum plate?


Buy the right shape rather than negotiating the rate. A drop or remnant, meaning certified material left over from a previous cut, is usually 20 to 40% below cut-to-size pricing for the identical alloy and paperwork, and it ships immediately. Beyond that: check the minimum billed weight before comparing per-pound rates, do not specify a more expensive alloy than your part needs, and round thicknesses to standard mill increments so you are not paying for material you machine away.


### How long does it take to get aluminum plate delivered?


From stock, one to three days is typical, and same-day cutting is achievable for common alloys and sizes. The variable most buyers overlook is the delivery window rather than the cut time: almost every supplier runs business hours, so a Friday afternoon order becomes a Monday or Tuesday arrival. NOX METALS delivers at night and on weekends, which collapses that gap. Walk-in pickup from a retail supplier like Metal Supermarkets can be immediate during store hours. A mill order for a non-standard gauge or a thick 7xxx section is a different category entirely, commonly 12 to 20 weeks. Always confirm whether your order is a stock pull or a mill order before you build the schedule around it.


### What should I look for when choosing an aluminum supplier?


Six things: whether mill certifications with heat numbers are included or extra, the minimum billed weight, whether the temper is correct for the thickness (T651 rather than T6 at 0.250 inch and above), the melt origin (DFARS, domestic, or any origin), whether cutting and processing charges are included in the quote, and the lead time in writing. Those six questions surface almost every hidden cost and schedule risk in a plate order.
