# Finance Automation with HappyRobot

## When the Money Is Already There


Most finance teams are leaving money on the table because of the overhead required to chase payments. It's high-volume and tough work for teams.


Invoices go unpaid - not because customers refuse to pay, but because no one got to them in time. Import duties sit uncollected. Documents go missing and nobody follows up. Collections teams are overwhelmed by volume. The revenue is already there. The work to recover it just doesn't get done.


What we've seen across our enterprise customers is that financial operations are one of the highest-ROI applications for AI workers - precisely because the work is high-volume, time-sensitive, and follows predictable patterns. AI workers handle the outreach, the follow-up, the escalation, and the documentation at scale.


Below are three high-impact finance applications HappyRobot customers are running today.


## 1. Payment Collections


HappyRobot AI workers run daily outbound collections workflows automatically. For every customer with a past-due invoice, the AI worker reaches out, handles the full interaction - verifying identity, presenting the invoice, answering questions, and capturing a payment commitment or promise-to-pay. If a dispute or exception comes up, it can leverage documentation or route to a human for support. Everything else gets handled end to end.


The workflow is built for compliance from the ground up - with built-in AI disclosure, recording notice, and active consent confirmation on every call. Timezone detection and retry logic ensure customers are contacted at the right time, across the right channels. And every interaction is logged and structured automatically, giving finance teams live visibility into collections performance without any manual reporting.


When contact data is stale - a common problem - the AI worker flags invalid records and is even able to collect new information, keeping the contact list clean and operations running efficiently.


### Impact


Customers running payment collections with HappyRobot have seen an 18% increase in cash collected and up to 119x ROI on recovered payments vs. cost to recover.


## 2. Document Collection


Missing documents are one of the most persistent sources of operational and financial friction in complex enterprises. When a proof of delivery, invoice, or compliance document isn't on file, it creates delays, disputes, and in many cases, revenue that can't be recognized until the gap is closed. The follow-up work to chase those documents is repetitive, time-consuming, and expensive.


HappyRobot AI workers handle document collection automatically. When a document is missing, the AI worker reaches out to the relevant party - by phone, email, or message - requests the document, and follows up until it's received. The entire process runs without human involvement unless an escalation is needed.


Once documents are shared, the AI can continue by verifying the document for any missing information and coordinating with the relevant party until all of the information is provided.


### Impact


Customers running document collection with HappyRobot have seen a 10x decrease in per-document recovery cost, a 45% increase in recovery rates, and 100% of document recovery handled by AI.


## 3. Duty Collection


Import duties are a significant and recurring revenue line, but collecting them is operationally intensive. Customers need to be informed of what they owe, guided through payment, and followed up with when they don't respond. At high shipment volumes, that work overwhelms manual teams fast.


HappyRobot AI workers run daily outbound duty collection workflows. The AI worker contacts customers with unpaid duties, informs them of the amount owed, and guides them through completing payment either online or directly over the phone. Multiple shipments for the same customer are consolidated into a single interaction, so customers aren't called several times with multiple packages.


Volume spikes are handled through batching infrastructure that spreads outreach evenly throughout the day - preventing the front-loaded call surges that overwhelm human teams when large volumes are released at once.


### Impact


Customers running duty collection with HappyRobot have seen a 13% uplift in collections and millions in annual cost savings by avoiding return shipping on unpaid duties.


## Built for the Complexity of Real Financial Operations


Across all three use cases, the common thread is the same: work that was previously limited by team capacity now runs at whatever scale the business requires. AI workers don't have a backlog. They don't deprioritize follow-ups when things get busy. Every invoice gets worked. Every duty gets chased. Every missing document gets flagged and followed up on.


On the HappyRobot platform, these workflows share context - so a collections interaction informs the customer record, and every interaction makes the next one smarter.
