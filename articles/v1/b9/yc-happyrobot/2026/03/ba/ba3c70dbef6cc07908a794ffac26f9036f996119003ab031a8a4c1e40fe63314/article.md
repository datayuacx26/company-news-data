# HR & Recruiting Automation with HappyRobot

## Taking the Bottleneck Out of HR


Recruiting and HR teams are asked to do more with less and the volume doesn't stop. Hundreds of applicants to screen. Shifts to confirm. Contractors who don't show. Onboarding milestones that slip. Exit interviews that never get scheduled. All of this is time-sensitive work and suffers when teams are stretched.


What we've seen across our enterprise clients is that the biggest drag on HR performance is execution at scale - the candidate questions, calls that need to go out, follow-ups that need to happen, checkpoints that need to be hit. AI workers handle all of it - at volume, across channels, without anything falling through the cracks.


## 1. Candidate Screening & Scheduling


For most recruiting teams, the top of the funnel is the biggest bottleneck. Candidates apply, then wait. By the time someone follows up, the best ones have moved on.


HappyRobot AI workers change that first interaction entirely. When a candidate calls in or starts a chat on your website, they're met instantly - no queue, no hold, no waiting for a recruiter to have a free moment. The AI worker engages them in a warm, on-brand conversation: answering questions about the role, collecting key qualifications like location, certifications, and years of experience, and getting a real sense of fit.


That conversation happens across whatever channel the candidate is already on - phone, web chat, WhatsApp, or SMS. If they qualify, the AI worker schedules them directly for a recruiter conversation, or passes a structured qualification summary to the recruiting team to review, depending on how your process works. Either way, candidate data flows directly into your talent software - no manual entry, no dropped records.


### Impact


One national staffing firm has conducted over 1 million interviews with HappyRobot AI workers, filling over 60% of their vacancies through the AI recruiter. More than 20,000 hires have come through the platform. Their bottleneck was candidate capacity and AI workers removed it entirely.


## 2. Shift Confirmation & Absenteeism


In contract and shift-based work, knowing who's actually showing up is as important as the work itself. Plan for fifty people and forty arrive and everything downstream suffers.


HappyRobot AI workers run proactive confirmation workflows before every shift. Outreach starts with a message - SMS, WhatsApp, or chat - giving workers a fast, low-friction way to confirm. If they don't respond, the AI worker escalates to a phone call. If a worker confirms they'll be late or won't be coming at all, the AI worker immediately notifies the site supervisor or manager with the details, and can either support finding a replacement or hand off to a human to handle from there.


### Impact


Customers running shift confirmation with HappyRobot have seen a 60% increase in confirmation rates and a direct reduction in absenteeism as a result. When workers are reminded of their shift and supervisors are kept updated about capacity, critical work gets done as planned.


## 3. Employee Onboarding & Wellness Checks


The first 30 days of a new hire's experience often determine whether they stay, but onboarding check-ins are exactly the kind of thing that gets deprioritized when teams are busy. Regular check-ins and support at key milestones can meaningfully impact employee retention.


HappyRobot AI workers make sure none of those moments get missed. At key points in the onboarding process - day one, day fifteen, day thirty, and beyond - the AI worker reaches out by phone or text to check in. How's the training going? Is anything unclear? Do they have what they need? The conversation is warm and on-brand, reflecting the company's values and voice.


The AI worker captures feedback, flags anything that needs attention, and ensures that every employee gets the same quality of experience - regardless of how busy the team is that week.


### Impact


Customers running this workflow see 100% coverage across onboarding milestones - no check-ins missed, no employees left without support during their first weeks. The result is higher employee satisfaction and better early retention.


## 4. Exit Interviews


Exit interviews are easy to schedule and easy to skip. When a team is stretched, an outgoing employee's offboarding often gets less attention than it deserves and with it, the feedback that could have changed something.


HappyRobot AI workers handle exit interviews automatically. When an employee leaves, the AI worker reaches out to conduct the conversation - by phone or by message - capturing structured feedback on their experience, their reasons for leaving, and what could have been different.


### Impact


This process ensures an open forum for feedback and that every detail is captured, driving useful insight into what's driving turnover and how to boost retention.


## Constantly improving your HR operations


The above use cases don't operate in isolation. On the HappyRobot platform, the AI workers handling recruiting, shift management, onboarding, and offboarding share context - so a candidate who was screened last quarter can be re-engaged for a new role, or an employee flagged during a wellness check can be followed up with the right information automatically.


Everything runs on one platform. The longer it runs, the smarter it gets, and the better your candidate and employee experience becomes.
