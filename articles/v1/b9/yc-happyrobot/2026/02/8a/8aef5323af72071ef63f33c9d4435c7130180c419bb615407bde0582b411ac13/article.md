# Job&Talent: AI-Powered Workforce Management at Scale

## Job&Talent: AI-Powered Workforce Management at Scale


Job&Talent is a world-leading, AI-powered workforce platform for essential industries. The company helps businesses in logistics, warehousing, manufacturing, and retail hire and manage frontline workers at scale across 10 countries. Their end-to-end platform covers the entire workforce lifecycle — from recruitment and planning to time and attendance, performance, and cost management.


In 2024, Job&Talent announced a strategic transformation into a fully AI-powered platform, embedding intelligent agents directly into its delivery model. As CEO Juan Urdiales put it: the company is now positioned to evolve into a fully integrated employment platform that helps companies manage their workforces more efficiently, with next-generation AI agents bringing major improvements in productivity and unlocking crucial cost savings.


At the center of this transformation is Clara — Job&Talent's AI recruiting agent, purpose-built for high-volume frontline hiring. Clara conducts candidate interviews autonomously via voice, screens for qualifications, and moves candidates through the hiring pipeline — calling unlimited candidates within minutes of application. She has dramatically cut time-to-hire and delivers industry-leading fill rates, even during peak demand surges, producing output equivalent to thousands of human recruiters working simultaneously.


Clara is just one of a growing suite of AI agents Job&Talent is deploying across the workforce management lifecycle — from attendance coaching to shift planning to account management — each embedded directly into the platform and trained on operational data from millions of worker placements and logged shifts.


For more on Job&Talent's AI strategy and recent funding, see their[Series F announcement](https://www.jobandtalent.com/news/series-f) .
