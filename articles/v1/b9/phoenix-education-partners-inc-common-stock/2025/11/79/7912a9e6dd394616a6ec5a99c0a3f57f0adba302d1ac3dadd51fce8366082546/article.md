## Phoenix Education Partners Schedules Fourth Quarter and Full Year Earnings Conference Call


11/07/2025


[Download (opens in new window) PDF 156 KB](https://s21.q4cdn.com/591079471/files/doc_news/Phoenix-Education-Partners-Schedules-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Earnings-Conference-Call-2025.pdf)


PHOENIX--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Phoenix Education Partners, Inc. (NYSE: PXED), the parent company of University of Phoenix, today announced it will host a conference call on Thursday, November 20, 2025 at 3:00 p.m. MST (5:00 p.m. ET) to discuss its results for fourth quarter and fiscal year ended August 31, 2025. Financial results will be released the same day after the markets close.


The call can be accessed by webcast on the Phoenix Education Partners website at[www.phoenixeducationpartners.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.phoenixeducationpartners.com&esheet=54354525&newsitemid=20251107550765&lan=en-US&anchor=www.phoenixeducationpartners.com&index=1&md5=4def5d2c7038cfd1df1be7fdd38a0050) . Please register in the Investor Relations section of the site 15 minutes prior to the call. The call can also be accessed by dialing (800) 715-9871 (domestic) or +1 (646) 307-1963 (toll), using conference ID: 8113013. The webcast will be archived for 30 days and the call replay for seven days. To access the call replay, dial (800) 770-2030 (domestic) or +1 (609) 800-9909 (toll), using conference ID: 8113013, or visit the Investor Relations section of the[Phoenix Education Partners website](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fphoenixeducationpartners.com%2Finvestor-relations%2Finvestor-overview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54354525&newsitemid=20251107550765&lan=en-US&anchor=Phoenix+Education+Partners+website&index=2&md5=de4e0c4563a09e752d46be0137b1da7b) .


**About Phoenix Education Partners, Inc.**


Phoenix Education Partners, Inc. is the parent company of University of Phoenix, a pioneer in online education for working adults. Founded in 1976, University of Phoenix provides access to higher education opportunities that enable students to develop the knowledge and skills necessary to achieve their professional goals, improve the performance of their organizations and provide leadership and service to their communities.
