# 2025 Annual Compliance Reports Available

Each year, we submit comprehensive Annual Compliance Reports (ACR) to Canada’s nuclear regulator, the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC) for our Peterborough and Toronto operations.


The purpose of these reports is to demonstrate that BWXT has successfully met the requirements of the Nuclear Safety and Control Act and its Class IB Nuclear Fuel Facility Operating Licenses. The ACRs are used by the CNSC to assist in the development of their Regulatory Oversight Report on the performance of Canada’s uranium and nuclear processing facilities, including BWXT ‘s Peterborough and Toronto facilities. Additionally, a public meeting is held to review the Regulatory Oversight Report’s findings.


The 2025 Annual Compliance Reports are available on our website **[here](https://www.bwxt.com/bwxt-nec/environment-safety/regulatory-compliance/)** .
