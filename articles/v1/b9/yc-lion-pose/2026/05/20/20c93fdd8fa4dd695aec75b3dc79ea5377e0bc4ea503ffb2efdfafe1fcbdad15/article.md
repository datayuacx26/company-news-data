## Best Practices & Expert Tips for Long-Term Skin Hydration


Achieving lasting hydration requires a strategic approach that goes beyond product selection. Dermatologists and skincare scientists recommend the following practices:


**Apply to Damp Skin** : Applying barrier repair creams to slightly damp skin enhances absorption and locks in moisture more effectively than application to dry skin.


**Layer Strategically** : Use lightweight hydrating serums with humectants like hyaluronic acid before applying lipid-rich creams to maximize moisture retention without heaviness.


**Prioritize Consistency Over Intensity** : Daily use of a well-formulated barrier cream delivers better long-term results than sporadic use of multiple products.


**Avoid Over-Cleansing** : Harsh cleansers strip natural lipids and disrupt pH balance. Use gentle, pH-balanced cleansers that preserve the barrier while removing impurities.


**Support from Within** : Adequate water intake, omega-3 fatty acids, and a diet rich in antioxidants support skin hydration and barrier function at the cellular level.


**Monitor Transepidermal Water Loss** : Pay attention to how long your skin stays hydrated after application. Effective barrier repair should extend moisture retention throughout the day.
