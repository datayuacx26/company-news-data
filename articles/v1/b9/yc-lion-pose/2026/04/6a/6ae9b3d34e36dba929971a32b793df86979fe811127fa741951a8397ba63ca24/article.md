## What is Rosacea?


Rosacea is a chronic inflammatory skin condition characterized by facial redness, visible blood vessels, bumps, and heightened sensitivity. Rosacea-prone skin exhibits a compromised barrier function, making it reactive to environmental triggers, harsh ingredients, and temperature changes. The condition affects over[16 million Americans](https://www.rosacea.org/rosacea-review/2010/winter/rosacea-now-estimated-to-affect-at-least-16-million-americans) and manifests in four primary subtypes: erythematotelangiectatic (persistent redness), papulopustular (acne-like breakouts), phymatous (skin thickening), and ocular (eye irritation). Lion Pose recognizes that rosacea disproportionately affects individuals with diverse skin tones, where inflammation may present differently, requiring specialized formulations that address barrier dysfunction while respecting skin's unique needs. Understanding your rosacea subtype is essential for selecting appropriate face creams and building an effective skincare routine.

## What Do Dermatologist Recommend for Rosacea Skincare Maintenance?


Dermatological experts recommend specific strategies that maximize face cream efficacy while minimizing flare-up risk. Lion Pose incorporates these evidence-based practices into product development and usage guidance, ensuring optimal outcomes for rosacea-prone skin.


**Patch Test New Products** : Apply new face creams to a small area for 48 hours before full-face application to identify potential sensitivities without triggering widespread reactions.


**Apply to Damp Skin** : Smooth face creams onto slightly damp skin immediately after cleansing to lock in moisture and enhance ingredient penetration for superior barrier repair.


**Use Upward Motions** : Apply products with gentle upward strokes rather than rubbing or massaging, which can increase blood flow and trigger flushing in reactive skin.


**Refrigerate for Extra Soothing** : Store face creams in the refrigerator to provide additional cooling relief during application, helping constrict blood vessels and reduce immediate redness.


**Maintain Consistency** : Use barrier-repairing face creams twice daily without interruption, as consistent application is essential for rebuilding compromised barriers and achieving lasting results.


**Avoid Layering Conflicts** : Ensure that serums and creams contain compatible ingredients, avoiding combinations like retinoids with acids that can overwhelm rosacea-prone skin.
