## What Is Rosacea and Why Does Skin Redness Happen?


[Rosacea](https://www.niams.nih.gov/health-topics/rosacea) is a chronic inflammatory skin condition that causes persistent facial redness, visible blood vessels, and sometimes small, acne-like bumps. It typically appears on the cheeks, nose, chin, and forehead, and is linked to a compromised skin barrier, inflammation, and an overactive immune response. Common triggers include heat, alcohol, spicy foods, sun exposure, and harsh skincare ingredients. Because rosacea-prone skin is more reactive than typical skin, it needs products that focus on calming inflammation and restoring the lipid barrier.
