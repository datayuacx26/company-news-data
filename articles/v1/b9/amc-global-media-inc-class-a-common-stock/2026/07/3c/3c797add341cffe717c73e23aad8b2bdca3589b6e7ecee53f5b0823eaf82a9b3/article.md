# AMC Global Media Returns to Comic-Con International with Panels, A Fan Screening & More!

AMC Global Media returns to Comic-Con International in San Diego, CA with a star-studded lineup of must-see panels, a fan screening event, exclusive news and more. The cast and executive producers of *The Walking Dead: Dead City* celebrate the upcoming season, **premiering Sunday, July 26 on AMC and AMC+,** with a Hall H panel followed later that evening by an advance screening of the season premiere for fans at The Loft at Petco Park. *The Vampire Lestat* descends on Hall H for the first time with highlights from the electrifying *The Vampire Lestat: One Night Only Live* music performance followed by a panel with the cast and executive producers discussing the epic season, which **finales Sunday, July 19 on AMC and AMC+** . Comic-Con will also give convention goers a first look at *YAGA* , the alluring, new mystery series on AMC+, with exclusive sneak peeks and a panel featuring the series’ showrunner and cast.


AMC Global Media and Shudder will also be represented on other Comic-Con International panels, including the Bold Voice of Contemporary Horror 10th Anniversary Panel featuring Deborah Ann Woll (Shudder’s *The Cycle* ) and Dan Stevens (AMC/AMC+’s *The Terror: Devil in Silver* ) and First in Fright with FANGORIA panel, featuring Devon Sawa (Shudder’s *Hell Motel: Murder at Red Mountain* ).


Additional details on AMC Global Media’s Comic-Con Panels and Events are below:


***The Walking Dead: Dead City*** **Season 3 Panel on Friday, July 24, 11:45am-12:30pm, Hall H**


Cast and executive producers, including Jeffrey Dean Morgan (executive producer, “Negan”), Lauren Cohan (executive producer, “Maggie”), Aimee Garcia (“Renata”), Raúl Castillo (“Omar”), Seth Hoffman (showrunner and executive producer), and Scott Gimple (executive producer) discuss the upcoming third season, premiering Sunday, July 26, and reveal an exclusive sneak peek from the premiere episode. The panel will be moderated by Entertainment Weekly’s Dalton Ross.


In Season 3, Maggie (Cohan) and Negan (Morgan) finally put aside their differences to build the first thriving community in Manhattan since the apocalypse, but when chaos in the city begins to arise, they are forced to question: have they learned from their old wounds or will their dark past spell doom for the entire city?


Comic-Con attendees will have the opportunity to attend a special advance screening of the series’ highly anticipated season premiere on Friday evening, July 24 at The Loft at Petco Park from 6:30-8:30pm PT, to be introduced by the cast and producers.

***The Vampire Lestat*** **Panel on Friday, July 24, 12:30pm-1:15pm, Hall H**


*The Vampire Lestat* cast and producers, including Jacob Anderson (“Louis du Pointe du Lac”), Assad Zaman (“Armand”) and Eric Bogosian (“Daniel Molloy”), Rolin Jones (creator and showrunner), Mark Johnson (executive producer) and Hannah Moscovitch (executive producer) take the stage for a panel moderated by Rotten Tomatoes’ Jacqueline Coley discussing the jaw-dropping season finale, with highlights from the electrifying music performance *The Vampire Lestat: One Night Only Live* , featuring original songs from series composer Daniel Hart and performed by Sam Reid as Lestat.


This season, Lestat (Reid) goes on an electric multi-city tour while being haunted by “muses” from his wild and rebellious past. As his band’s popularity and star power rises, so does Lestat’s influence over vampires and humans alike, leaving others to contend with Lestat’s power in the face of the Great Conversion, an unnatural surge in the vampire population.


***YAGA*** **Panel on Saturday, July 25, 12:30pm-1:30pm, Indigo Ballroom, Hilton Bayfront**


Series cast members Noah Reid ( *Schitt’s Creek, The Vampire Lestat* ), Clark Backo ( *Venom: The Last Dance* , *Letterkenny* ), and Carrie-Anne Moss ( *Matrix* Films, *Memento* ) are joined by showrunner and Executive Producer Kat Sandler for a panel discussion moderated by TV Guide Magazine and TV Insider’s Damian Holbrook that will feature a sneak preview of the new AMC+ series.


Based on the play by Sandler, *YAGA* reimagines the Slavic folk tale of the Baba Yaga witch, as a contemporary mystery thriller. It follows Rapp (Reid), a private investigator who arrives in a small coastal town to investigate the disappearance of a young heir to a powerful fishery, Henry Park (Hudson Williams, *Heated Rivalry* ). Rapp finds himself at odds with an apprehensive local detective, Carson (Backo); a charismatic university professor, Katherine (Moss) and a labyrinth of enigmatic suspects, secret lives and ancient magic. *YAGA* is an original series from Canadian streamer Crave, produced by Blink49 Studios and Front Street Pictures in association with Bell Media. *YAGA* is distributed internationally by Sphere Abacus, a Bell Media company.


**Bold Voice of Contemporary Horror 10th Anniversary Panel on Friday, July 24, 4:45pm-5:45pm, Room 6A**


Deborah Ann Woll, star of Shudder’s upcoming film *THE CYCLE* and Dan Stevens, star of AMC’s *The Terror: Devil in Silver* , join some of the most knowledgeable and influential voices in horror today to discuss the state of indie and studio horror media, and give a sneak peek at the best to come this year going bigger and bolder for their tenth anniversary. Featuring exclusive footage from future releases and stand out festival titles and a lively discussion, moderated by Overlook Film Festival Co-Founder and Director of Film Programming, Michael Lerman, with Zachary Wigon (director, *Victorian Psycho* ), Dave Boyle (SXSW Audience Award Winner *Never After Dark* ) and more.


**First in Fright with FANGORIA Panel on Friday, July 24, 8:00pm-9:00pm, Room 23ABC**


Since 1979, FANGORIA has been the definitive blood-spattered bible for horror fans, chronicling the genre across print, film, and digital media. Join Fango and a few very special guests, including Devon Sawa (Shudder’s *Hell Motel: Murder at Red Mountain* ), for a trip through the brand's house of horrors, complete with exclusive sneak peeks, scoops, and surprises surrounding this year's FANGORIA Chainsaw Awards, which will stream exclusively on Shudder on Sunday, October 25.
