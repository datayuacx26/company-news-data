# Get Your Hands on A Custom Fender LeStrat!

At San Diego Comic-Con 2025, we unveiled that AMC Global Media was collaborating with Fender to include four customized Player II Modified Stratocaster guitars in the upcoming season of[Anne Rice’s The Vampire Lestat](https://www.amcplus.com/shows/the-vampire-lestat--1073907) — and we delivered! Lestat took his “LeStrat” with him this season as the band traveled from city to city on tour, and now it’s your opportunity to enter for the chance to get in on a piece of the bloody rock ‘n roll action.


The custom guitars were designed and modified by Fender’s expert team along with the show’s Production Designer Andres Cubillan. Each guitar in the collection had a distinct design aesthetic, visually building on the previous model and becoming bloodier as the season progressed.


“I’ve been playing my American Tele through a ’65 Deluxe Reverb since I was 21 years old,” composer Daniel Hart said at the time of the collection’s announcement. “Fender has been a part of my music and my life ever since, and I’m excited to see what they’ve cooked up for our rock ‘n’ roll vampire.” So how do you get your hands on a piece of history?
