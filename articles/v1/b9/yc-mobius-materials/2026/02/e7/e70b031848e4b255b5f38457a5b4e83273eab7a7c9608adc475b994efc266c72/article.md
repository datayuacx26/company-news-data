# Introducing the Mobius E-Auction Marketplace: A Better Way to Sell Excess Electronic Components

Over the past year, Mobius has grown into the trusted spot marketplace for the semiconductor and electronic components market, serving hundreds of manufacturers around the world. Thousands of listings have been posted, and millions of dollars in bid value have already flowed through the Mobius platform.


That momentum has revealed something important. The industry is ready for a new phase in how manufacturers create liquidity from excess inventory. Electronic components should be able to move efficiently when production plans change, and sellers should not have to sit on working capital that could otherwise be unlocked.


Today, we are launching the **Mobius E-Auction Marketplace** , a major evolution in how sellers convert unused electronic components into real financial outcomes, without the slow and uncertain experience of traditional secondary market channels.


## **Why the industry needs a better way to sell excess electronic components**


Supply chains are more volatile than ever. Procurement teams face constant shifts in demand, unpredictable lead times, and tariff cycles that can reshape cost structures in a single quarter. In an environment where everything can change rapidly, liquidity and optionality matter more than ever.


There are typically over 400 active listings live on Mobius each day. Over the past year, more than 4,000 listings have been posted on the platform, generating more than $4.3 million in total bid value. In that same period, more than $30 million in inventory has been listed across more than 200 enterprise manufacturing users.


The market signal is clear. Industry demand exists for this inventory. Sellers simply need a better way to evaluate offers quickly and convert them into outcomes that contribute back to the business.
