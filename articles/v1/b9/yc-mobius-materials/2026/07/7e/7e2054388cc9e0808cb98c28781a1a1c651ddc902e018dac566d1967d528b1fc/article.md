# Selling on Mobius, Rebuilt: Three New Pages and Three Ways to Sell

For most of our platform's life, selling on Mobius happened on one page. It showed every listing, every auction result, and every sale at once. That worked for a seller with a handful of parts. It did not hold up at hundreds.


Sellers found what they needed, and the answers were all in there. Getting to them just took more steps than it should have. Checking which parts were live, seeing what an auction earned, or confirming the price set on a listing each meant moving across a few screens or pulling an export.


We have launched a redesign built on one principle: at every moment, tell the seller what they have and what to do right now.


## **Three pages, three jobs**


**Uploads** shows what you submitted and what went live. Every line from your file, the part number you provided, the one we matched, quantity, date code, and status. When something does not make it onto the platform, the page gives the reason.


**Auctions** gives each auction its own page. Results, timeline, and bid status sit together instead of pooled with every auction you have run. Listings can be added by tag, picked individually, or pulled in as a whole upload batch.


**Inventory** is where you decide how each part sells. Add parts to an auction or pull them back out, open them to bids, or set a price for direct purchase, in bulk or one listing at a time. Action items surface what needs attention, whether that is new bids, an auction entering review, or a recent sale.
