# 2x Points for Degens | Rainbow

**This week's points theme is welcoming Degen Chain to Rainbow!** Last week Rainbow added native support for Degen Chain in both the mobile app and Rainbow Extension. This week, earn **2x Points** on all transactions on Degen Chain by trading and managing your Degen Chain tokens and NFTs using Rainbow.


#### What is Degen Chain?


Degen Chain is a Layer 3 network built using Arbitrum Orbit on top of Base network. Degen Chain was born out of the DEGEN movement on[@farcaster_xyz](https://twitter.com/farcaster_xyz) and has rapidly grown to become a hub for new memecoins.[$DEGEN](https://twitter.com/search?q=%24DEGEN&src=cashtag_click) is the gas token on Degen Chain, meaning you need a positive balance in order to transact on the network.
