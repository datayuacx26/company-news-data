# EquipmentShare Named to Construction Executive’s 2026 Top Tech List

**COLUMBIA, Mo.** \[ **June 10, 2026\]** — EquipmentShare (NASDAQ: EQPT), a leader in connected jobsite technology and one of the largest construction equipment rental providers in the United States, has been named to *Construction Executive’s* prestigious 2026 Top Tech List. Published in *Construction Executive’s* annual tech publication, this ranking recognizes the premier software and hardware innovators driving digital transformation across the commercial construction sector. This milestone underscores EquipmentShare’s consistent leadership in the sector, having previously been recognized by *Construction Executive* in its “Top Construction Technology Firms™” list for four consecutive years from 2022-25.


This recognition highlights EquipmentShare’s ongoing enhancement of its proprietary and fully integrated digital platform called[T3®](https://t3.tech/) — EquipmentShare’s equipment management solution. By capturing and analyzing billions of daily data points from mixed fleets, T3 empowers contractors to overcome traditional jobsite bottlenecks like equipment downtime, low utilization, asset misplacement and unmanaged consumable spend.


"Winning this award from *Construction Executive* shows that the industry is ready to change how work gets done," said Jabbok Schlacks, founder and CEO of EquipmentShare. "For a long time, jobsites have been disconnected, and contractors haven't had access to critical insights. We built the T3 platform to bridge that gap. By connecting assets, people, and materials in real time, we're helping builders leave old, fragmented habits behind and finally get the visibility and control they need to run a world-class operation."


"We are honored to be recognized by *Construction Executive* as a top-tier technology partner for the commercial construction industry," said Paul Wright, chief technology officer at EquipmentShare. "Our focus has always been on building practical, rugged solutions that solve real problems in the built world. This award is a testament to our team’s dedication to helping contractors build safer, faster and more efficiently."


The inclusion on *Construction Executive’s* 2026 list comes during a landmark period of growth and recognition for EquipmentShare. Over the past year, the company’s tech-integrated model and operational health have received significant national recognition, including:


- **2025 Stevie® Award:** For the first time in company history, EquipmentShare was proud to receive the Gold Award for Company of the Year — Business Technology, a reflection of the company's organizational health and the depth of its leadership bench.
- **Multiyear AutoTech Breakthrough Winner:** Building on a history of consistent innovation, EquipmentShare has been named the Fleet Management Technology Company of the Year by AutoTech Breakthrough, alongside other major category wins in the past, including 2020 (“Fleet Management Technology Company of the Year”), 2021 (“Fleet Management Innovation of the Year”), 2022 (“Driver Safety Solution of the Year”) and 2023 (“Vehicle Telematics Company of the Year”), further validating its position as a leading hardware-tech pioneer.
- **The Software Report:** For the first time, EquipmentShare debuted on the Top 50 Software Companies of 2025, securing an elite top-five position at No. 4 for T3’s real-world impact on connected jobsites.
- **Inc. 5000 Fastest-Growing Companies:** As an acknowledgement of its rapid nationwide expansion and commercial momentum, EquipmentShare made the prestigious Inc. 5000 list for the first time, with an impressive 251% three-year growth rate — highlighted by a surge in annual revenue from $3.8 billion in 2024 to $4.4 billion at the close of 2025.
- **National Expansion & Milestone Public Listing:** These honors follow EquipmentShare’s highly anticipated January 2026 initial public offering (IPO) on the Nasdaq Global Select Market, marking a significant milestone in EquipmentShare’s continued growth.


Unlike traditional rental models, EquipmentShare functions as a digital-first technology company. Whether a contractor rents machines or assets, or purchases new inventory from one of the company's expanding dealership networks, every machine can be managed under the unified T3 ecosystem. T3 can also be outfitted on customer-owned assets through its nationwide installation network.


Through credentialed access keypads, individual user PIN codes, digital audit trails and automated maintenance tracking, contractors gain improved visibility, which can result in a secure, transparent jobsite environment that turns raw field data into measurable, bottom-line profitability.


*Construction Executive* is a leading source for business news and market trends impacting the commercial construction industry. Its annual Top Tech profile serves as a definitive guide for contractors looking to invest in leading-edge project management, fleet telematics and jobsite safety tools.


**About EquipmentShare**
Founded in 2015 and headquartered in Columbia, Missouri, EquipmentShare (Nasdaq: EQPT) is a nationwide construction technology and equipment solutions provider dedicated to transforming the construction industry through innovative tools, platforms and data-driven insights. By empowering contractors, builders and equipment owners with its proprietary technology, *T3®* , EquipmentShare aims to drive productivity, efficiency, and collaboration across the construction sector. With a comprehensive suite of solutions that includes a fleet management platform, telematics devices and a best-in-class equipment rental marketplace, EquipmentShare continues to lead the industry in building the future of construction.
