# Cantaloupe and Weweimi Sign Agreement to Integrate AI Smart Cooler Technology With Seed Pro and Seed Markets

*Automated Services International to distribute AI Smart Cooler solutions in North America under the BRITE by ASI™ brand*


**GASTONIA, N.C., \[Month Day, Year\]** — Cantaloupe, a 365 Retail Markets company, and Weweimi (Shenzhen) Technology Co., Ltd. today announced the signing of a services agreement to integrate and certify Weweimi’s AI-enabled Smart Cooler technology for use with Cantaloupe’s Seed Pro and Seed Markets software platforms.


Weweimi’s North American technology and commercialization entity, SenseBox, LLC, supports the deployment and expansion of its Smart Cooler solutions in North America. SenseBox solutions are distributed exclusively in North America by Automated Services International, Inc. (ASI) under the BRITE by ASI™ brand.


ASI President Joe Loparco and SenseBox Director Larry Akramongkolrojn led the agreement negotiations, with ongoing support from Adam Cook of 365 Retail Markets.


“This integration is the single most important development in our nearly two-year relationship with SenseBox,” said Joe Loparco, president of Automated Services International, Inc. “It opens the door for us to work with our extensive, long-standing independent operator base—many of whom use Cantaloupe’s industry-leading vending management system and have no intention of running multiple systems. We are grateful to our partners at Weweimi and SenseBox for the substantial investment they are making to achieve this objective, and we look forward to rolling it out in the near future.”


“At 365 Retail Markets, we are committed to giving operators the flexibility to adopt innovative technologies without disrupting the workflows and platforms they rely on every day,” said Jeff Dumbrell, chief revenue officer at 365 Retail Markets. “By integrating Weweimi’s AI Smart Cooler technology with Seed Pro and Seed Markets, we are expanding the range of solutions available to our customers, helping them unlock new revenue opportunities while continuing to manage their businesses through a single, trusted platform.”


“We are excited to collaborate with Cantaloupe and ASI to expand the use of AI-enabled Smart Cooler solutions in the North American market,” said Larry Akramongkolrojn, director of SenseBox, LLC. “By combining Weweimi’s technology development capabilities, SenseBox’s North American commercialization and integration expertise, Cantaloupe’s software ecosystem, and ASI’s strong distribution network, this collaboration will maximize the value of our Smart Cooler solutions and help more operators benefit from improved operational efficiency, greater product visibility, and a better customer experience.”


**About Cantaloupe, a 365 Retail Markets Company**


Cantaloupe, a 365 Retail Markets company, is a global technology leader powering self-service commerce. Cantaloupe offers a comprehensive portfolio of solutions, including micropayment processing, mobile ordering, connected point-of-sale systems, and enterprise cloud software. Processing more than 1 billion transactions annually, Cantaloupe’s solutions enhance operational efficiency and consumer engagement across foodservice, smart retail, hospitality, entertainment, and other self-service environments.


Following its acquisition by 365 Retail Markets in May 2026, Cantaloupe continues to deliver the trusted payment and software solutions its customers rely on while expanding the combined company’s technology offerings for unattended retail. Together, 365 Retail Markets and Cantaloupe serve more than 40,000 customers across the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Europe, Australia, and Mexico.

**About Weweimi (Shenzhen) Technology Co., Ltd. and SenseBox, LLC**


Weweimi (Shenzhen) Technology Co., Ltd. develops AI-enabled smart retail solutions, including Smart Cooler technologies, embedded hardware systems, and connected retail platforms. Through hardware design, software integration, system development, and product optimization, the company delivers scalable solutions for intelligent retail applications.


SenseBox, LLC serves as Weweimi’s North American technology and commercialization entity, supporting the deployment and expansion of Weweimi’s Smart Cooler solutions across the region. By combining technology expertise, product integration capabilities, and local market knowledge, SenseBox helps bring intelligent retail solutions to operators and industry partners.


SenseBox’s AI Smart Cooler solutions are distributed exclusively in North America by Automated Services International, Inc. under the BRITE by ASI™ brand.


**About Automated Services International, Inc.**


Automated Services International, Inc. is one of the convenience services industry’s oldest and most respected distributors of refurbished vending machines, micro markets, coolers, and office coffee service equipment.


ASI operates its corporate office and East Coast distribution center in Gastonia, North Carolina, and its West Coast distribution center in Corona, California.


ASI is the exclusive North American distributor of SenseBox AI Smart Cooler solutions, which it markets under the BRITE by ASI™ brand.
