# AHRA 2026

The eFax team is bringing its data-driven insights to the Orlando World Center Marriott for the 2026 AHRA Annual Meeting, hosted in partnership with ARIN. This year’s theme, “Where Imaging Meets Imagination,” sets a bold stage for forward-thinking radiology and medical imaging management leaders. As the imaging care continuum evolves, eFax is proud to connect with industry professionals to discuss how digital fax innovation can streamline communication between imaging specialists, clinicians, and administrative teams.


Connect with the eFax team during the event to explore how our cloud-native data extraction platforms help radiology departments eliminate operational friction and enhance data interoperability. We are excited to share how organizations can easily integrate inbound imaging documentation directly into their digital systems, improving workflow coordination and regulatory compliance while maintaining top-tier patient safety and information security across your entire health network.
