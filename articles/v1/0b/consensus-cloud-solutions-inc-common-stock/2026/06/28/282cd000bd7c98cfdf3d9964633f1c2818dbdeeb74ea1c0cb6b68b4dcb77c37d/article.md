# Health Connect South 2026 Annual Gathering

eFax is incredibly honored to attend the 13th Annual Health Connect South Gathering at the magnificent Georgia Aquarium. As the largest healthcare gathering in the Southeast, this event serves as a vital gateway to break down industry silos and spark high-level regional collaborations between innovators, health systems, and top decision-makers.


Connect with the eFax team during the gathering to explore how our secure data exchange solutions are accelerating health innovation throughout the Southeast. We are excited to discuss how our AI-powered, cloud-based data transformation platforms seamlessly convert unstructured content into actionable clinical assets, helping organizations drive regional partnerships, reduce administrative costs, and secure vital patient records without disrupting daily workflows.
