# Accessibility improvements


We updated Artillery Cloud to comply with[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) . Improvements include keyboard navigation, support for browser zoom, accessible chart descriptions, clearer form errors, improved color contrast, and labels for interactive controls.
