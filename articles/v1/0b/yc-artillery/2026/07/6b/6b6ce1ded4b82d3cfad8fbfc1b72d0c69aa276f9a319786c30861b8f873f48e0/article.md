# Self-service SSO and SCIM onboarding


Enterprise organization administrators can now start[SSO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single_sign-on) and[SCIM](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7644) onboarding from Artillery Cloud. Open **Settings → SSO** to submit your identity-provider configuration and provisioning requirements.
