## Enrich leads before an AI step


It's tempting to use AI whenever you can, but to make the most of it, you need to use it well. If you're constantly running into AI outputs that feel generic, it's probably because the input is too. A form submission with three fields doesn't give your AI tool much to work with.


ZoomInfo's data is AI-ready. It surfaces real-time buying signals across 5,000+ topics—and whether you're connecting it to Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini through[Zapier MCP](https://zapier.com/mcp) , or feeding it into an AI step inside a Zap, that context is what turns generic outputs into useful ones.


Zapier can help by enriching the lead with ZoomInfo first—like the company size, industry, role, intent, or other context—before an AI step scores it or drafts a follow-up.


For example, you can create a Zap—what we call Zapier's automated workflows—that:


1.


Triggers when a lead submits a form


2.


Looks up or enriches the company with ZoomInfo


3.


Uses an AI step to score the lead or draft a personalized follow-up


4.


Routes the lead to the right rep or sequence


[Build your Zap](https://zapier.com/editor)


**Pro tip:**[Add a path step](https://zapier.com/blog/zapier-paths-conditional-workflows/) to your Zap so only ICP-matching leads get the full AI treatment.


## Build pre-meeting briefs automatically


Pre-call research is incredibly useful, but it's usually the first thing to get skipped when calendars fill up.


With Zapier, you can pull ZoomInfo account context the moment a meeting gets booked and send a short brief to the rep before the call.


For example, you can create a Zap that:


1.


Triggers when a new calendar event is created


2.


Finds the company tied to the meeting


3.


Pulls company and contact context from ZoomInfo


4.


Uses an AI step to turn that into a readable brief


5.


Sends the brief to Slack or adds it to a doc


[Build your Zap](https://zapier.com/editor)


According to ZoomInfo, reps using enriched, real-time account context close deals faster. In fact, ZoomInfo customers like[Speakap](https://www.zoominfo.com/about/case-studies/speakap) have closed deals in half the typical sales cycle using timely account intelligence. Automating that context delivery via Zapier means every rep gets that edge, not just the ones with time to research.


Not a Slack user? This same idea works for your email or CRM notes.

## Enrich new CRM records automatically


For Salesforce and HubSpot, ZoomInfo already enriches records natively and in real time. For the rest of your stack—custom apps, ticketing systems, spreadsheets, less common CRMs—Zapier extends that same enrichment logic anywhere ZoomInfo doesn't natively integrate. Instead of asking reps to fill gaps by hand across disconnected tools, Zapier keeps every system in sync automatically.


For example, you can create a Zap that:


-


Triggers when a new contact, lead, or company is added to your CRM


-


Pulls firmographic or contact data from ZoomInfo


-


Updates the CRM record with the enriched data


-


Routes the record to the right owner or follow-up workflow
