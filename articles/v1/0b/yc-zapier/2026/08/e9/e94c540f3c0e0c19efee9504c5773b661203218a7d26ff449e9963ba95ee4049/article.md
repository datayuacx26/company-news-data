# Google Chat vs. Slack: Which is right for your business? [2026]

**Google Chat**


**Slack**


**Chat**


⭐⭐⭐ While Google Chat has adopted many of Slack's best features—like threads—its chat experience isn't as intuitive.


⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Slack's chat experience is incredibly polished; it remains the standard-bearer for workplace chat apps.


**Video and meetings**


⭐⭐⭐ Google Meet is a fantastic meetings app that integrates with Chat natively and supports 100-1,000 participants, depending on your plan. Google Chat also offers huddles and async video and audio clips.


⭐⭐⭐⭐ Slack's huddles feature is excellent for informal collaboration, although it isn't a full substitute for a meetings app (and it supports a maximum of 50 participants). You can also send audio and video clips for async communication.


**Project management**


⭐⭐⭐ You can assign tasks to colleagues, manage files, and collaborate on projects, though it's not a substitute for a dedicated project tool.


⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Slack's in-app project management features have become a real alternative to using a standalone project app. And on the Business+ plan, you get a built-in, Salesforce-powered CRM.


**Automation**


⭐⭐⭐⭐ Workspace Studio lets you build automations that connect Google Chat with other Google apps; you can add AI steps at any point in the workflow.


⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Slack's Workflow Builder lets you automate routine tasks, and there are 60+ workflow templates to speed things along; you can also use AI to generate automations.


**AI features**


⭐⭐⭐ Google Chat offers a solid (but basic) set of AI features on Business Standard and above. You can get AI-powered answers, translate your entire space's messages automatically, and summarize conversations.


⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Slack's entry-level paid plan offers automatic huddle transcripts and AI thread summaries. Business+ adds AI search, daily recaps, and Slackbot, a personal AI agent that works across your connected apps.


**Pricing**


⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Free if you use Google Workspace. Otherwise, plans start at $7/month/user billed annually. For AI features in Chat, you'll need the $14/month/user Business Standard plan.


⭐⭐⭐⭐ Slack has a free plan, though you're limited to 90 days of message history. Paid plans start at $7.25/month/user billed annually, with a full set of AI features included on the $15/month/user Business+ plan.


**Integrations**


⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ~100 integrations via the Google Workspace Marketplace; integrates with thousands more apps via Zapier.


⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ~2,600 native integrations; integrates with thousands more apps via Zapier.
