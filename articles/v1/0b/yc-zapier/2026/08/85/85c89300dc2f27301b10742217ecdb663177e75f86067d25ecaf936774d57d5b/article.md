# How to audit your LinkedIn conversion tracking setup

**The one-page audit for founders**


If you're doing your own marketing or working with a small team, the audit above is more than you need. Here's the same diagnostic designed to be done solo:


1.


Am I actually recording all the funnel events that matter to my business—not just form fills, but demos booked, proposals sent, and deals closed—in my CRM?


2.


Are those events shared back to LinkedIn?


3.


Have I changed anything in my CRM, like new fields, since I last checked on the integration?


4.


Are the events showing up in LinkedIn labeled in a way that matches how I think about my funnel? Using consistent, descriptive terms makes it easier to analyze performance and map fields across your CRM and LinkedIn.


5.


If I had to explain my current tracking setup to someone I hired, could I?


For any question where the honest answer is "I'm not sure," that's your starting point.
