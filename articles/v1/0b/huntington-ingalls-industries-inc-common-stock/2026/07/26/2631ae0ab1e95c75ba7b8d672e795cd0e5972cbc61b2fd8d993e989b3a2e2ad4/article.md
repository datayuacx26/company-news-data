# Can America Actually Build Trump’s Golden Fleet?

*Photo caption: HII President and CEO Chris Kastner joins Bloomberg Correspondent Scarlet Fu aboard the amphibious assault ship, Bougainville (LHA 8), which is under construction at HII’s Ingalls Shipbuilding division in Pascagoula, Miss.*


#### ***“Shipbuilding gets in your blood. There’s just something so rewarding about building a ship you know is going to go into service.”*** **– Chris Kastner, HII president and CEO**


As the U.S. strengthens its defense industrial base, this Bloomberg TV “Wall Street Week” segment spotlights HII’s role in building the U.S. Navy’s future fleet and advancing the workforce and technologies our nation depends on.


HII President and CEO Chris Kastner sat down with Bloomberg correspondent Scarlet Fu during her visit to HII’s Ingalls Shipbuilding division to discuss the state of U.S. shipbuilding, domestic manufacturing, and HII’s continued transformation.


The segment also highlights the workforce training underway at the Ingalls Maritime Training Academy, where the company provides apprenticeship programs across multiple shipbuilding and maritime trade disciplines.


A portion of the feature is below, or visit[Bloomberg TV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tbkN76xbRB0) for the full video story.
