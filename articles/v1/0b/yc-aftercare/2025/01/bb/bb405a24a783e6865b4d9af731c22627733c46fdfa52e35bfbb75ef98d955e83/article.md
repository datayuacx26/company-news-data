# How To Create a Survey on Consumer Behaviour

## Create Incredible Consumer Behavior Surveys That Dig Deeper Into Insights


Ever wonder what makes your customers tick? You're not alone. As business owners and marketers, we're all trying to crack the code of consumer behavior. While creating effective surveys has traditionally been challenging, new AI technologies are making it easier to craft surveys that adapt to your respondents' answers and uncover deeper insights.


‍


### Understanding Consumer Behavior: The 4 P's Framework


Before diving into survey creation, let's talk about the fundamental framework that shapes consumer behavior - the 4 P's:


#### Product


What features, benefits, and experiences drive purchase decisions? Your survey needs to uncover not just what customers buy, but why they choose specific products over others.


#### Price


How do customers perceive value? This goes beyond just the dollar amount – it's about understanding the whole value proposition and price sensitivity.


#### Place


Where and how do customers prefer to buy? This includes both physical and digital channels, and the entire purchasing environment.


#### Promotion


Which marketing messages resonate? How do customers discover and learn about products?


‍


Using this framework as our foundation, we can create surveys that dig deeper into each aspect of consumer behavior. Modern survey tools can help by automatically generating relevant follow-up questions based on initial responses, ensuring you don't miss important insights.


‍


### Mapping Questions to the Customer Journey


Let's break down essential questions for each stage of the customer journey. While you can create these questions manually, many companies now use AI-powered tools to help generate relevant questions and automatically follow up on interesting responses.


‍


#### Discovery Stage Questions


- "How did you first hear about our product category?"
- "What problem were you trying to solve when you started looking for solutions?"
- "Which information sources do you trust most when researching new products?"
- "What caught your attention about our product/service?"


Pro Tip: Consider using AI-powered surveys that can automatically follow up when customers mention specific problems or information sources. Tools like Aftercare can help streamline this process.


‍


#### Consideration Stage Questions


- "What alternatives did you consider?"
- "How did you compare different options?"
- "What features were most important in your decision?"
- "What concerns did you have during the research process?"
- "How long did you spend researching before making a decision?"


‍


#### Purchase Decision Questions


- "What finally convinced you to make the purchase?"
- "Were there any last-minute concerns before buying?"
- "How would you describe the checkout process?"
- "Did you use any discounts or promotions?"


‍


#### Onboarding Questions


- "How would you rate the setup process?"
- "Did you need help getting started?"
- "What was your first impression after starting to use the product?"
- "Were the instructions clear and helpful?"


‍


#### Usage Questions


- "How often do you use the product?"
- "What features do you use most?"
- "Have you discovered any unexpected uses?"
- "What's your favorite aspect of the product?"


Pro Tip: Look for survey tools that can automatically categorize open-ended responses and generate summaries. This can save significant analysis time and help you spot patterns more easily.


‍


### Deep Diving into the 4 P's with Smart Questions


‍


#### Product-focused Questions


- "What features do you value most?"
- "How well does the product meet your needs?"
- "What would you improve about the product?"
- "How does our product compare to alternatives?"


#### Price-focused Questions


- "How would you rate the value for money?"
- "At what price point would this product feel too expensive?"
- "At what price would you start to question the quality?"
- "How does our pricing compare to alternatives?"


#### Place-focused Questions


- "Where do you prefer to shop for this type of product?"
- "How convenient was the purchase process?"
- "What would make buying easier?"
- "Do you prefer online or in-store shopping for this category?"


#### Promotion-focused Questions


- "Which of our marketing messages resonated with you?"
- "Where do you learn about new products in this category?"
- "What type of promotional offers interest you most?"
- "How did you research before buying?"


‍


### Making Your Survey Flow Naturally


Creating a natural survey flow is crucial for getting honest, detailed responses. Modern AI-powered survey tools can help by:


- Automatically generating follow-up questions based on previous answers
- Adapting the question flow based on respondent engagement
- Digging deeper when interesting patterns emerge
- Skipping irrelevant questions to keep surveys focused


For example, if a respondent mentions a negative experience with customer service, an adaptive survey might automatically ask follow-up questions to understand:


- What specifically went wrong
- How it affected their perception of the brand
- What would have made the experience better
- Whether this influenced future purchase decisions


‍


### Analyzing and Acting on Results


Traditional surveys often leave you drowning in data. Modern survey platforms with AI capabilities can help by:


- Automatically categorizing open-ended responses
- Generating summaries of key findings
- Identifying patterns and trends
- Highlighting opportunities for improvement


Some advanced platforms even let you chat directly with your survey data, asking questions like:


- "What are the most common pain points mentioned?"
- "How do satisfaction scores vary by age group?"
- "What features do power users value most?"


‍


### Best Practices for Implementation


‍


#### Timing Your Surveys Right


- Send discovery surveys to recent website visitors
- Trigger purchase decision surveys right after checkout
- Schedule usage surveys 2-4 weeks after purchase
- Run regular pulse checks with active customers
- ‍


#### Keeping Respondents Engaged


- Use Aftercare's progress indicators to show survey length
- Let AI handle the follow-up questions naturally
- Keep surveys focused and relevant
- Offer incentives for detailed responses

Creating effective consumer behavior surveys doesn't have to be complicated. Modern survey platforms can help you:


- Generate relevant questions quickly

- Automatically analyze and categorize responses
