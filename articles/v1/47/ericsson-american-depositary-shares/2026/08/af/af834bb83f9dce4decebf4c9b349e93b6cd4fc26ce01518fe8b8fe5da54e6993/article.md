# Zain Jordan selects Ericsson to elevate next-generation digital services platform

- Ericsson and Zain Jordan deepen their long-term partnership, entering a new phase of a major Business Support Systems (BSS) transformation project.
- Scope includes deploying Ericsson's cloud-native Billing and Ericsson’s Telco DataOps Platform and establishing a catalog-driven architecture for postpaid services.
- The transformation will elevate customer experience, improve operational efficiency, accelerate the monetization of new 5G-era digital services, and lay the groundwork for AI-driven autonomous networks.
