# Ericsson and LG Uplus to advance network-based voice AI and AI-native network evolution

Ericsson and LG Uplus will collaborate to explore network-based voice AI global business opportunities and accelerate AI-native network transformation to create new customer experiences, improve operational efficiency, and drive future growth in the AI era.


- Strategic partnership combines LG Uplus’s voice AI innovation capabilities and Ericsson’s global network technology leadership.


- Collaboration aims to bring voice AI beyond app-based boundaries and into communications service provider networks.


- The partnership will help build intelligent and programmable networks that accelerate innovation and enable the next generation of AI-driven services.
