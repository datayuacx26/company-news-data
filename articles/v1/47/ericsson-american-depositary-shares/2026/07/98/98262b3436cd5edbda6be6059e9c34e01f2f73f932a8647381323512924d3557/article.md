# Ericsson South Africa launches learnership to equip young talent with telecommunications field operations skills

- Ericsson South Africa is launching the first cohort of a planned five-year learnership initiative to support skills development in South Africa
- The 12-month program will help prepare young people for careers as Telecommunications Technicians and Riggers
- The first cohort of 15 learners were welcomed at a launch event in KuGompo wi city, previously known as East London, in the Eastern Cape in July 2026
