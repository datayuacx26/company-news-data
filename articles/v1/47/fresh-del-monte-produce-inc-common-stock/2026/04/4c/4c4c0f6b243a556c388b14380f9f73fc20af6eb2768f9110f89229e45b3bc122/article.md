# Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend

### Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend


April 28, 2026


CORAL GABLES, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ffreshdelmonte.com%2F&esheet=54524586&newsitemid=20260428088348&lan=en-US&anchor=Fresh+Del+Monte+Produce+Inc&index=1&md5=8d680ef51fb2ddaa9c6da43956827964) . (NYSE: FDP) today announced that its Board of Directors has approved a quarterly cash dividend of $0.30 per share of outstanding Common Stock. The dividend will be payable in cash on June 11, 2026, to shareholders of record on May 19, 2026.


**About Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc.**


[Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ffreshdelmonte.com%2F&esheet=54524586&newsitemid=20260428088348&lan=en-US&anchor=Fresh+Del+Monte+Produce+Inc&index=2&md5=740d5c4a4e1077e304dab66dac396dca) . is a leading global producer, marketer, and distributor of high-quality fresh, fresh-cut, and prepared fruit and vegetables, with products sold in more than 90 countries worldwide. The company also operates a growing global platform across fresh, refrigerated, and shelf-stable food categories. Fresh Del Monte markets its products worldwide under the DEL MONTE® brand and other recognized brands, a symbol of quality, innovation, freshness, and reliability for more than 140 years. The company owns global rights to the Del Monte® brand, subject to certain existing licensing arrangements. Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc. is not affiliated with certain other Del Monte companies around the world, including Del Monte Asia Pte. Ltd. Fresh Del Monte is the first global marketer of fruits and vegetables to commit to the Science Based Targets initiative. The company has been recognized as one of America’s Most Trusted Companies by Newsweek and named a Humankind 100 Company by Humankind Investments. Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc. is traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol FDP.
