# What is Intelligent Document Processing?

**Key Use Case**


**Application and Benefits**


**Claims processing**


IDP automatically classifies and extracts data from unstructured documents like FNOLs and medical records, instantly feeding it into policy administration systems. This accelerates claim resolution, enables early fraud detection, and reduces the time adjusters spend on manual data entry.


**Supply chain management**


IDP reads variable logistics documents—like customs declarations and packing slips—and matches the data against existing inventory systems. This keeps supply chains moving by preventing shipping delays, reducing transcription errors, and providing real-time operational visibility.


**Patient onboarding**


IDP securely extracts patient history from paper-heavy clinical records and handwritten forms, slashing intake wait times and freeing medical staff to focus on direct patient care instead of tedious paperwork.


**Order intake & fulfillment**


IDP acts as an automated entry engine, extracting line-item details from purchase orders and routing the structured data directly into fulfillment systems. This accelerates delivery, eliminates manual errors, and lowers operational overhead.
