# What is Business Process Orchestration? 4 Essentials

[Processes](https://appian.com/bpm/business-process-definition) are the foundation of your organization. They lay the groundwork for how your business operates, from product development and customer service to procurement and HR processes—and everything in between. Even work that isn’t formally defined in a process is likely a part of one.


If you’ve ever tried to map or document a business process, you’ve probably learned that there’s no straightforward way to get it done.


That’s because processes aren’t static. And the work that goes into them is fluid. The steps in your process can vary dramatically depending on the type of work being done, the information passing through them, and the worker, technology, or department that is executing the task. Luckily, business process orchestration can help.


Business process orchestration takes business process automation a step further. It can help streamline even your most complex processes. From small, internal workflow automation to more complicated, mission-critical work, process orchestration software increases operational efficiency. It does this by connecting work across disparate systems, automating repetitive tasks, and providing insights for continuous improvement.
