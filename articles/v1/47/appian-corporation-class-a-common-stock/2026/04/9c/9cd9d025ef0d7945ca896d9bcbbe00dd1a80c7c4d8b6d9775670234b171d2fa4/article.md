# Appian To Announce First Quarter 2026 Financial Results on May 7, 2026

[PDF Version](https://investors.appian.com/node/14146/pdf)


MCLEAN, Va., April 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Appian](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=4PzTkCBGuGljqiiJfWWErH5m1dAS8oWtxAX7OyAUBejORKCOS7purYp4voZgDNcrKr4hVlwpMtwcY9ZK1JJOrA==) (NASDAQ: APPN) today announced that it will release financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026, before the U.S. financial markets open on Thursday, May 7, 2026. The company will host a conference call and live webcast to review its financial results and business outlook.

The conference call will begin at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time.
