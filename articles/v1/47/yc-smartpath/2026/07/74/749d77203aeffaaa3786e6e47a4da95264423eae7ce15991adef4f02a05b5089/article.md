# SECURE Act 2.0 in 2025: Expanded Eligibility for LTPT Workers

Long-Term, Part-Time (LTPT) workers often have a difficult time adequately preparing for retirement, especially because they have reduced access to employer-sponsored retirement plans. This is changing in the new year, because thanks to the Secure 2.0 Act **LTPT workers will have expanded access to workplace retirement plans.**


Beginning in 2025, LTPT employees who have worked at least 500 hours for *two* consecutive years will be eligible to participate in a workplace retirement plan. Until this year, the IRS required 500 hours for *three* consecutive years. Also new this year is that the provision extends to 403(b) plans in addition to 401(k) plans.


If you’re an advisor,[click here](https://www.joinsmartpath.com/content/navigating-secure-2-0-essential-changes-for-advisors-in-2025) to learn more about working with your plan sponsor to identify LTPT-eligible employees and add them to the retirement plan, so you can provide the best possible participant experience.
