# Financial Wellness in 2025: Increasing Demand for Retirement Education

One PlanAdviser study showed that[48% of employees](https://bestmoneymoves.com/5-emerging-benefits-trends-to-look-for-in-2025/) name retirement as their top cause of financial stress. Additionally, a recent survey by Goldman Sachs asked employees “What financial planning and advice services are you most interested in receiving from your employer?” A clear majority selected “[retirement saving and investing](https://am.gs.com/en-us/advisors/insights/report-survey/retirement-survey?sc_cid=us%7Eprss%7Ena%7Ertrmntsrvy%7Etl%7Elnk%7Ena%7Ena&ls=press-release&utm_source=na&utm_campaign=retirement-survey) .”


#### ‍ **The Opportunity** ‍


Unquestionably, employees want and need retirement planning support. Whether they are looking to get started, or check in on their timeline and trajectory, employees are eager for a helping hand to discern if they’re doing it “right."


#### **The Trend**


No financial wellness solution will be complete in 2025 without comprehensive retirement planning education. This may include regular classes and courses, articles, and/or access to one-on-one meetings with a retirement plan advisor.


#### **The Bottom Line** ‍


Financial wellness solutions in the workplace will only continue to grow in popularity as employees look for support to make their finances “work” now and build a sustainable future for themselves and their families. Reliable retirement education will be essential to make sure employees feel cared for and confident in their future at your organization.
