# Westlake Announces First Quarter 2026 Conference Call

## Westlake Announces First Quarter 2026 Conference Call


April 21, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.westlake.com/node/18051/pdf)


HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 21, 2026-- Westlake Corporation (NYSE: WLK) will release its first quarter 2026 earnings prior to the market opening on Tuesday, May 5, 2026. The company will host a conference call at 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time (10:00 a.m. Central Time) on the same day to discuss the earnings release.

When you dial in, you will input the PIN number to be placed into the call.
