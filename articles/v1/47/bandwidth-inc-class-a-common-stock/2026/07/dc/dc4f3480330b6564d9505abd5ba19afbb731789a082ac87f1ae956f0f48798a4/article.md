# Bandwidth to Report Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on July 29, 2026; Present at an Upcoming Investor Conference

RALEIGH, N.C.


, July 1, 2026


/PRNewswire/ — Bandwidth Inc. (NASDAQ: BAND), a leading global enterprise cloud communications company, today announced it will report its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026 before market open on Wednesday, July 29, 2026.


Bandwidth will offer a live webcast of the conference call on the Investor Relations section of the company’s website at[https://investors.bandwidth.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4723429-1&h=2166461627&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.bandwidth.com%2F&a=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.bandwidth.com) , where a replay will also be available shortly following the completion of the event.


**Conference call details:** Date: Wednesday, July 29, 2026
Time: 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time
Dial-in number (domestic): 800-715-9871
Dial-in number (international): 646-307-1963


**Replay information:** Following the completion of the call through Wednesday, August 5, 2026, a replay will be available by dialing 855-669-9658 for the U.S. or 412-317-0088 for callers outside the U.S., and entering passcode 7551038.


**Upcoming Investor Conference** Members of Bandwidth management will present at the Piper Sandler Growth Frontiers Conference in Nashville, Tennessee on Tuesday, September 15th. A live webcast and replay of the presentation will be available through the Investor Relations section of Bandwidth’s website. Management will also be available for one-on-one and small group meetings with investors.
