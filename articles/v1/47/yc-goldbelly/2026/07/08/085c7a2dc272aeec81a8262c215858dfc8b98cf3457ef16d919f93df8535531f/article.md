## **German Chocolate Cake**


German chocolate cake is layered chocolate cake frosted with coconut-pecan frosting. The world first learned of this delicious cake in 1957 when a recipe was printed in *The Dallas Morning News* .


Despite its name, this cake did not originate in Germany. The cake was the creation of homemaker Mrs. George Clay, who named the cake after its key ingredient, Baker’s German’s Sweet Chocolate.

## **Chocolate Blackout Cake**


Chocolate Blackout Cake or Brooklyn Blackout Cake is a chocolate-chocolate cake and frosting combo created in Brooklyn during World War II. The cake is an iconic NYC treat and an homage to mandatory blackouts that protected Brooklyn Navy Yard during the war. The now-shuttered Ebinger’s bakery first baked this chocolate layer cake with chocolate frosting and cake crumbs.

## **Chocolate Doberge Cake**


[Doberge cake](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/doberge-cake) is a quintessential New Orleans cake. Pronounced “doh-bash,” these cakes with 6 or more layers feature pudding in between each tiny cake layer. Chocolate Doberge Cake is topped with buttercream and fondant or a glaze.


New Orleans baker Beulah Ledner invented Doberge Cake in the early 20th century. The cake, which is based on a Hungarian Dobos torte, a sponge cake with chocolate buttercream frosting, quickly became a regional specialty.

## **Chocolate Cake FAQs**


**What is the most popular type of chocolate cake?**


Classic chocolate layer cake is the most popular, but German chocolate cake, chocolate cheesecake, and chocolate peanut butter cake are close favorites. Popularity often comes down to the filling and frosting, from coconut-pecan to creamy peanut butter.


**What is the richest chocolate cake?**


Brooklyn blackout cake is one of the richest, with chocolate cake, chocolate pudding, and chocolate frosting coated in cake crumbs. Flourless chocolate cake and chocolate mousse cake are also intensely rich thanks to their high chocolate content.


**What is the difference between German chocolate cake and regular chocolate cake?**


German chocolate cake is defined by its sweet coconut-pecan frosting and its use of Baker’s German’s Sweet Chocolate, which gives it a milder, sweeter flavor. Regular chocolate cake uses cocoa or dark chocolate and is typically frosted with chocolate buttercream or ganache.
