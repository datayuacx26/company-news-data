## **What Month is National Ice Cream Month in the United States?**


July is National Ice Cream Month in the United States. Ice cream is so popular — ice cream makers churned out 1.30 billion gallons of ice cream last year — that there is a day devoted to it as well. National Ice Cream Day is observed on the third Sunday of July. In 2026, National Ice Cream Day is Sunday, July 19.


## **How to Observe Nation Ice Cream Day**


There are many ways to celebrate National Ice Cream Day, from enjoying a simple scoop to hosting an ice cream party at home. When President Ronald Reagan created the holiday in 1984, he encouraged Americans to observe it with “appropriate ceremonies and activities,” which is really just an official excuse to eat more ice cream. Here are some of the best ways to celebrate:


- **Enjoy a classic scoop:** Grab a cup or cone of your favorite flavor, chase down the ice cream truck, or build an over-the-top sundae or milkshake.
- **Try a DIY kit:** Make your own frozen treats at home with an[ice cream kit](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/ice-cream-kits) for some hands-on fun.
- **Explore new styles:** Branch out with[frozen custard](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/custard) ,[gelato](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/gelato) , or[popsicles](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/popsicles) from across the country.
- **Keep it lighter:** If you are on a special diet, celebrate with[sorbet](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/sorbet) or frozen fruit instead.
- **Host an ice cream social:** Set up a sundae bar, run a blindfolded flavor taste test, or hold a friendly ice cream eating contest with family and friends.
- **Include your pets:** Treat your dog to pup-friendly ice cream, now sold by many parlors and supermarkets.

## **National Ice Cream Day FAQs**


**When is National Ice Cream Day 2026?**


National Ice Cream Day 2026 is Sunday, July 19. It is always observed on the third Sunday of July, which also falls within National Ice Cream Month.


**Is National Ice Cream Day the same as National Ice Cream Month?**


No. National Ice Cream Month spans all of July, while National Ice Cream Day is a single day within it, observed on the third Sunday of the month.


**Who started National Ice Cream Day?**


President Ronald Reagan established National Ice Cream Day and National Ice Cream Month in 1984, designating July as a month to celebrate the frozen treat and the third Sunday as its signature day.
