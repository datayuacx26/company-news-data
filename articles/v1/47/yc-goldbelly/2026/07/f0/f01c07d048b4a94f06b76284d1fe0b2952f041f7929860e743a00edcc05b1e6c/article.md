## **The Nuns of New Skete Story**


he Nuns of New Skete began making cheesecake in the 1970s as a way to support their monastery, and it has since become the community’s signature craft. Here is how a cloister of Orthodox nuns became one of America’s most beloved cheesecake makers.


### **A monastic community rooted in New York**


In 1969, the Nuns of New Skete moved from their Roman Catholic Poor Clare cloister in Evansville, Indiana, to Cambridge, New York, to build an Eastern Christian monastic community. They settled alongside the Monks of New Skete, who had founded New Skete Monastery in 1966 and helped the nuns get established. The word “skete” refers to a small, family-style monastic community, and the nuns chose the name “New Skete” to reflect their goal of a modern Orthodox community rooted in contemporary American life.


### **From odd jobs to cheesecake**


To support themselves, the nuns rely on donations and the proceeds of their own work. In the early years they cleaned houses, painted iconography, and tailored clothing. Everything changed when Sister Magdalene Oliver, an avid baker, arrived and began making cheesecakes. By 1975, the community had perfected its recipe and cheesecake baking became a shared calling. The rest is delicious history.


### **A daily rhythm of prayer and baking**


Today, the nuns bake their famous gourmet cheesecakes between morning and afternoon prayers. They are best known for their 6-inch and 9-inch cheesecakes, but they also craft seasonal treats, including:

## **The Nuns of New Skete Cheesecake FAQs**


**Who are the Nuns of New Skete?**


The Nuns of New Skete are a community of Orthodox Christian nuns based in Cambridge, New York. They support their monastery through their work, and are best known for the gourmet cheesecakes they have been baking since the 1970s.


**How many cheesecake flavors do the Nuns of New Skete make?**


The nuns make 16 cheesecake flavors, including New York, Chocolate, Key Lime, Amaretto & Cream, Pumpkin, and Irish Cream. They also offer samplers so you can try several best-selling flavors in one order.


**Where are the Nuns of New Skete located?**


The nuns live and bake at New Skete Monastery in Cambridge, New York, a small village in the eastern part of the state. They share the grounds with the Monks of New Skete, who founded the monastery in 1966.
