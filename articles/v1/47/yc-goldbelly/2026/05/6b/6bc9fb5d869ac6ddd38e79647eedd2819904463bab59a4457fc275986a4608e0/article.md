There’s nothing like the smell of barbecue in the air, **[smoky brisket](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/brisket)** , saucy ribs, and **[pulled pork](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/pulled-pork)** piled high. ## **What Is National BBQ Day?**


National BBQ Day is all about celebrating one of America’s most beloved cooking traditions: barbecue. From low-and-slow smoking to flame-kissed grilling, BBQ is as much about the process as it is the payoff.


**[BBQ](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/bbq)** isn’t just food, it’s culture. It’s backyard gatherings, summer holidays, and community cookouts. It’s the slow build of anticipation while something incredible cooks for hours. It’s a day to gather with friends and family, fire up the grill (or smoker), and enjoy the rich, smoky flavors that define this iconic cuisine.


## **A Brief History of BBQ**


Barbecue has deep roots that go back centuries. The word itself comes from “barbacoa,” a Caribbean cooking method where meat was slow-cooked over an open flame.


As the tradition made its way to the American South, it evolved into distinct regional styles we know and love today:


- [Texas](https://blog.goldbelly.com/guide-to-texas-bbq/) **:** Beef brisket, smoky and simple
- [Kansas City](https://blog.goldbelly.com/guide-to-kansas-city-bbq/) **:** Sweet, sticky sauces and a mix of meats
- **Memphis:** Dry-rubbed ribs, packed with flavor
- **Carolina:** Tangy, vinegar-based pulled pork


Each style tells its own story, but they all share one thing: serious flavor.

## **How to Celebrate National BBQ Day**


Not sure how to celebrate? Here are a few delicious ideas


### **Try a New Regional Style**


BBQ in America isn’t one-size-fits-all—it’s a delicious patchwork of regional traditions, each with its own signature flavors and techniques.


This National BBQ Day, switch things up and explore something new. Go tangy with Carolina **[pulled pork](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/bbq?categories.name=Pulled+Pork) ,** piled high and dressed in vinegar-based sauce, or keep it classic with **[Texas-style brisket](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/bbq?ref=gs&search_session_id=457b3816-7fad-47c9-80d9-bb03df746c4b&state.name=Texas&categories.name=Brisket)** , slow-smoked until perfectly tender with a simple salt-and-pepper crust. Feeling adventurous?


Dive into **[Kansas City ribs](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/bbq?state.name=Kansas&city=Kansas+City&categories.name=Ribs)** slathered in sweet, sticky sauce or **[Memphis dry-rubbed ribs](https://www.goldbelly.com/foods/bbq?state.name=Tennessee&categories.name=Ribs&city=Memphis)** packed with bold, smoky spice. However you do it, there’s a whole country of BBQ styles waiting to be tasted.


### **Order from a Legendary Pitmaster**


Not in the mood to tend a smoker for 12 hours? Leave it to the pros.


Some of the best BBQ in the country comes from **[legendary pitmasters](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/categories/bbq-joints)** who’ve spent decades perfecting their craft—think melt-in-your-mouth brisket, fall-off-the-bone ribs, and perfectly smoked pulled pork. The best part? You don’t have to travel to Texas or Memphis to get it!
