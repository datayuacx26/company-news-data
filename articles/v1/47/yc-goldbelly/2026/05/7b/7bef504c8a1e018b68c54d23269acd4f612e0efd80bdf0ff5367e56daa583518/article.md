This Fried Chicken Dinner partners it up with mashed potatoes, collard greens, and gravy.

This Homemade Pasta Dinner includes your choice of a wide assortment of her handmade pastas & sauces, including potato gnocchi in a traditional bolognese sauce and traditional meat sauce lasagna.


**[Homemade Pasta Dinner Kit >](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/i-trulli/homemade-pasta-dinner-kit-choose-your-own-6)**
