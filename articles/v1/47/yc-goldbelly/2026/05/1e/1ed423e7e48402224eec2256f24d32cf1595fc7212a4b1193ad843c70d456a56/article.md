Since its founding in 2002, Brooklyn Bagel & Coffee Company has emerged as one of New York’s top bagel shops. All of these classic New York bagels are hand-rolled and kettle-boiled the old-fashioned way and shipped alongside Nova and cream cheese for the perfect Bagel Brunch. By par-baking their bagels before quickly freezing and shipping them to your door, Dad can start off his big day with a *literally* fresh-baked New York bagel!

### **Brique French Toastery’s Famous French Toast**


Invented by Racquel “Chef Rocky” Rockquemore-Breiz while serving as personal chef for Dwayne Johnson in 2020, these colossal slabs of French toast went viral after he shared his love for them on Instagram. This world-famous French toast starts with brioche, which is dunked into creamy cinnamon-spiced custard before being cooked to a perfect golden brown. Just reheat and top with Peanut Butter Coconut Maple Syrup, Vanilla Bean Whipped Cream, and oven Toasted Coconut Chips!


**[World Famous Brique French Toast Kit >](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/brique-french-toastery/world-famous-brique-french-toast-kit-4-pack)**


### **** **Commander’s Palace’s Shrimp & Grits**


Commander’s Palace is one of New Orleans’ most legendary fine dining restaurants and the winner of seven James Beard Awards, and this Shrimp & Grits is one of its most iconic dishes (yes, shrimp & grits is a breakfast dish!). It’s made with wild-caught Louisiana shrimp and creamy Creole cream cheese grits, along with a rich & buttery sauce.


**[Wild Shrimp & Cognac with Creole Cream Cheese Grits >](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/commanders-palace/wild-shrimp-and-cognac-with-creole-cream-cheese-grits)**


### **** **** **Pine State Biscuits’ “The Reggie” Biscuit Sandwiches**


This biscuit sandwich might just be America’s most indulgent breakfast dish! From Portland’s renowned Pine State Biscuits (where locals line up daily for a taste of their flaky, buttery biscuits), their legendary “The Reggie” Biscuit Sandwich is stacked with fried chicken, cheddar, bacon, and gravy – be sure to add on a fried egg!


**[The Reggie Sandwich Kit >](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/pine-state-biscuits/the-reggie-biscuit-sandwich-kit-for-4)**


## **Father’s Day Grilling Gifts: Steaks, Burgers & More**


### **** **** **** **Shake Shack’s Shackburger** ®


You know and love restaurateur Danny Meyer’s fast food icon Shake Shack, but did you ever think that you could cook up their legendary ShackBurgers at home? These start with a patty of fresh-ground, custom-blend beef from Pat LaFrieda, which is grilled and topped with melted American cheese, creamy ShackSauce, and optional lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, and bacon.


**[Shake Shack Shackburger® >](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/shake-shack/shake-shack-shackburger-8-pack)**


### **** **** **** **** **Seaside Market’s “Cardiff Crack” Tri-Tips**


Seaside Market in Cardiff by the Sea, California, is home to California’s famous “Cardiff Crack,” a Tri Tip roast cut from the bottom sirloin that’s so addictive it has attracted an intense cult following. Choose from Burgundy Pepper or Spicy Chipotle marinade and grill until perfectly medium rare!


**[Cardiff Crack Tri Tip >](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/seaside-market/cardiff-crack-tri-tips-2-pack)**


### **** **** **** **** **** **Chef Francis Mallmann’s Traditional Argentinian Asado Kit**


Chef Francis Mallmann is synonymous with Argentinian cuisine. In fact, he’s South America’s most famous celebrity chef! The chef, TV host, and cookbook author is perhaps the world’s most renowned ambassador of traditional Patagonian-style open-fire barbecue, and his Traditional Argentinian Asado Feast is perfect for the grill. It’s overflowing with skirt steak, dry-aged boneless ribeye steaks, and Argentinian chorizo, served alongside Domino Sliced Potatoes, ratatouille, chimichurri, and salsa criolla.


**[Francis Mallmann’s Asado Kit >](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/chef-francis-mallmann/traditional-argentinian-asado-dinner-kit-for-6-8)**


### **** **** **** **** **** **** **Broken Arrow Ranch’s Wild Game Sausage**


Founded by Chris Hughes back in 1983 as a restaurant wholesaler, Broken Arrow Ranch field-harvests its game meats from nearly 40 Texas ranches with a mobile processing unit and ages the meat for 30 days before butchering. It’s the go-to for fine dining restaurants including Thomas Keller’s Per Se, and this Wild Game Sausage Pack is a perfect way to sample wild game including venison and wild boar.


**[Wild Game Sausage Best Seller >](https://www.goldbelly.com/restaurants/broken-arrow-ranch/wild-game-sausage-best-seller-5-pack)**
