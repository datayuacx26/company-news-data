## **History of National Picnic Day**


National Picnic Day has its roots in the 19th century, when picnicking emerged as a popular pastime among the upper class and eventually became embraced by the masses. At first, picnics were fancy events with beautiful food enjoyed in nice gardens and parks. As time went on, picnics became more casual and accessible for everyone.


This day encourages people to pause their busy lives, come together with family and friends, and enjoy a meal outside. Over the years, National Picnic Day has become a beloved tradition that continues to build connections and appreciation for the great outdoors.
