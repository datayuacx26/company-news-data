# Forrester TEI study finds Zip delivered 386% ROI for enterprise procurement

The findings at a glance


[Forrester Consulting's 2026 Total Economic Impact™ study of Zip's AI procurement orchestration platform](https://ziphq.com/resources/forrester-total-economic-impact-zip) found that a composite enterprise customer realized 386% ROI and $5.8 million in net present value, based on interviews with procurement leaders at four global enterprises ranging from $10B to $45B in revenue.


We're proud to share the results of a new[Total Economic Impact™ study](https://ziphq.com/resources/forrester-total-economic-impact-zip) from Forrester Consulting. Zip commissioned Forrester to interview procurement leaders at four global enterprises running Zip, then built a composite model to quantify the financial return.


The key figures: **386% ROI** and **$5.8 million in net present value** over three years.


The Forrester study's findings track with what we hear from procurement teams every week. Legacy procurement software has been promising adoption and value for two decades, with most of it still running on the same assumption: that buyers will navigate complex workflows because that’s how it’s meant to be.


Zip was built on the opposite premise. When[intake](https://ziphq.com/capabilities/intake-management) is a single front door, employee requesters actually adhere to purchasing policies. If procurement can see demand early, the team is able to shape outcomes rather than simply processing them. And if workflows are configurable without engineering tickets, the function moves at the speed of the business.


That's the model the Forrester TEI study examines.


## **What did the Forrester TEI study of Zip find?**


The Forrester study found that the composite Zip customer realized **$7.4 million in benefits** against **$1.5 million in costs** over three years, driven by improved spend governance, faster procurement cycles, and reduced IT administrative overhead.


Value is across three areas: cost savings from improved spend governance and renewal discipline, efficiency gains from intake and procurement orchestration, and reduced IT and administrative effort. The composite organization is a $30 billion enterprise with $2 billion of spend in scope.


**The interviewees behind the composite tell fascinating and instructive stories.**


A VP of strategic sourcing and procurement at a $20 billion retailer told Forrester their team **grew spend under management from 13% to 75%** after standing up Zip as the single intake front door. "We have far and away exceeded our savings expectations," they said. "From an ROI perspective, the business has enjoyed a **10 times return** on our investment."


A senior global director of digital procurement at a $45 billion consumer goods company described what happened in the first five weeks of moving to the Zip platform: roughly 300 requests came through Zip, more than the team had processed the entire prior year. That represented $3.9 billion in spend, about 15% of the $25 billion spend in scope. "I'll be stunned if we don't hit $10 billion next year."
