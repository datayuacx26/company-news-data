# Private: Team NexusFlow Wins Leena AI Enterprise Innovation Hackathon 2026

**GURUGRAM, India – May 30, 2026 –** Leena AI, the world’s largest independent employee-facing[agentic AI](https://blog.leena.ai/glossary/agentic-ai/) platform, today announced the results of the **Leena AI Enterprise Innovation Hackathon 2026: Advancing AI in HR & IT Transformation** . Hosted at Leena AI’s Gurugram headquarters on May 30, 2026, the event saw eight finalist teams compete before a distinguished panel of six industry leaders from across the globe.


**Team NexusFlow** emerged as the winner with their **AI Employee Service Copilot** , scoring 91.0 out of 100 — recognised for its best-in-class balance of enterprise relevance, employee experience value, HR and IT service delivery impact, technical feasibility, and[responsible AI](https://blog.leena.ai/glossary/responsible-ai/) design.


🥇


**Winner: Team NexusFlow — AI Employee Service Copilot (91.0)**


Team NexusFlow’s solution demonstrated the most compelling combination of practical AI application, measurable employee productivity impact, and enterprise-grade scalability. The judging panel praised the project for moving well beyond a conceptual assistant — delivering a solution with clear deployment potential, strong workflow integration, and thoughtful human oversight design.


🥈


**Runner-Up: Team TrustLayer — Responsible AI Governance Monitor (89.0)**


Team TrustLayer earned special recognition for directly tackling one of the most pressing challenges in[enterprise AI](https://blog.leena.ai/glossary/enterprise-ai/) adoption — governance, auditability, privacy, and[hallucination](https://blog.leena.ai/glossary/hallucination/) risk. The panel noted that as AI expands across HR and IT environments, solutions like TrustLayer will be critical infrastructure for responsible deployment.


🥉


**Third Place: Team FlowForge — Cross-Functional Workflow Automation Studio (86.0)**


Team FlowForge impressed judges with a strong workflow orchestration concept connecting HR, IT, approvals, and employee requests — delivering high operational impact potential across enterprise functions.


*“The strongest projects were those that moved beyond a simple AI assistant concept and demonstrated practical enterprise deployment potential, clear business value, measurable impact, workflow integration, user-centered design, and appropriate human oversight.”*


**– Gaurav Sharda, CTO, Beacon Mobility | Guest of Honour & Judge**
