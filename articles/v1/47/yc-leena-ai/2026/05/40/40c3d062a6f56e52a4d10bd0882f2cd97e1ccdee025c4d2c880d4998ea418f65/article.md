# Leena AI to Host Enterprise Innovation Hackathon 2026: Advancing AI in HR & IT Transformation

**GURUGRAM, India – May 27, 2026 –** Leena AI, the world’s largest independent employee-facing[agentic AI](https://blog.leena.ai/glossary/agentic-ai/) platform, today announced the upcoming final round of the


**Leena AI Enterprise Innovation Hackathon 2026: Advancing AI in HR & IT Transformation** . The highly anticipated event will take place on **Satur**


**day, May 30, 2026** , at Leena AI’s Gurugram headquarters, bringing together the company’s brightest minds to compete on the cutting edge of[enterprise AI](https://blog.leena.ai/glossary/enterprise-ai/) innovation.


Eight finalist teams have been shortlisted from an exceptional field of entrants, each presenting bold, real-world AI solutions designed to transform how enterprises manage their HR and IT functions. Projects will be evaluated across seven dimensions: innovation, business impact, technical execution, enterprise scalability, user experience,[responsible AI](https://blog.leena.ai/glossary/responsible-ai/) , and presentation quality.


## **Meet the Eight Finalist Teams**


- **Team NexusFlow** – AI Employee Service Copilot


- **Team ResolveIQ** – Autonomous IT Ticket Resolution Engine


- **Team PolicyPilot** – AI Policy Navigator for Employees


- **Team WorkWise** – Predictive Employee Experience Intelligence


- **Team OnboardAI** – AI-Powered Onboarding Journey Manager


- **Team KnowledgeGrid** – Enterprise Knowledge Graph Assistant


- **Team FlowForge** – Cross-Functional Workflow Automation Studio


- **Team TrustLayer** – Responsible AI Governance Monitor

## **Spotlight: Guest of Honour Gaurav Sharda**


A nationally recognised technology and people leader, Gaurav serves as CTO of Beacon Mobility – one of the largest special-needs student transportation providers in the United States, operating approximately 14,000 vehicles across 25 states and serving more than 350,000 students daily. Since joining in 2021, he has spearheaded large-scale AI-driven modernisation across HR, IT, and operations, earning the prestigious


**2025 U.S. National Innovation Award** , jointly presented by School Transportation News and the National School Transportation Association. Before Beacon Mobility, Gaurav held senior technology leadership roles at Life Time Inc. and General Mills, and has consulted for Microsoft and Comcast. A passionate advocate for people-first technology, he brings deep expertise in enterprise AI adoption, HR transformation, and responsible innovation.

**– Gaurav Sharda, CTO, Beacon Mobility**
