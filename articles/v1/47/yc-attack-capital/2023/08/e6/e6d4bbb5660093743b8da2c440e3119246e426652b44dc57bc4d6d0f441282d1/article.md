### [Retake](https://www.getretake.com/)


**Description:** All-in-one keyword + semantic search engine for Postgres.


-


Key Products/Features-


-


Can be integrated directly with Postgres using logical-replication-based Change-Data-Capture (CDC).


-


Vectors are automatically cached for lightning-fast query results.


-


Intuitive search SDKs that drop into any Python or Typescript application.


-


Open/ElasticSearch DSL Compatible.


-


Retake is deployable anywhere, from a laptop to a distributed cloud system.


-


Market Geography: Global


-


Competitor Companies: None


-


Technologies/Differentiator: By connecting directly to Postgres, Retake accelerates the development of AI applications that require searching, matching, or analyzing database documents.

### [PeerDB](https://www.peerdb.io/)


**Description:** Fast, native ETL for Postgres.


-


Key Products/Features-


-


Data-store nativity at its core, enabling scalable ETL


-


Postgres-compatible SQL interface to do ETL


-


Market Geography: Global


-


Competitor Companies:


-


Technologies/Differentiator: Postgres-compatible SQL interface for ETL is unique to PeerDB and enables users to operate in a language they are familiar with. Users can do ETL the same way you work with your databases.

-


Market Geography: Global


-


Direct Competitor Companies: None

### [Subsets](https://www.subsets.com/)


**Description:** The AI retention co-pilot for subscription businesses.


-


Key Products/Features-


-


Using AI Subset enables CRM teams to understand, engage, and retain subscribers by enabling personalized and proven retention initiatives.


-


Subsets can recommend initiatives that give ROI fast.


-


Market Geography: Global


-


Direct Competitor Companies: None


-

### [Converge](https://www.runconverge.com/?)


**Description:** Converge helps online stores improve ad performance and attribution accuracy through better tracking.


-


Key Products/Features-


-


Tracks all important events automatically – across the browser, store backend, and subscription platforms without any technical expertise.


-


Converge can track 100% of conversions and forward these to relevant ad platforms and analytics tools.


-


Market Geography: Global


-


Direct Competitor Companies: Segment


-


Technologies/Differentiator: Segment or other CDPs, Converge allows non-technical ecommerce operators to set this tracking up in minutes.

### [HockeyStack](https://hockeystack.com/)


**Description:** The operating system for inbound B2B revenue.


-


Key Products/Features-


-


Customers connect all data sources (CRM, ads, web, and product analytics…) to HockeyStack to create a source of truth for customer engagements. No engineering work is required.


-


HockeyStack shows which channels, campaigns, content, segments, etc., drive the most revenue and active customers.


-


HockeyStack creates lists of high-intent accounts that customers can use to launch the most efficient sales & marketing campaigns.


-


Market Geography: Global


-


Direct Competitor Companies: Factors.ai


-


Technologies/Differentiator: Instead of using tens of different data tools and spreadsheets, HockeyStack is a one-stop shop.

-
