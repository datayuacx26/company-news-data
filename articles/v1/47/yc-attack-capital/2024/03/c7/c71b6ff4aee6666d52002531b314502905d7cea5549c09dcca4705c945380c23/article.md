### [TAIKI](https://www.taiki.ai/) (Document Processing)


-


**Taiki** is building the Ultimate API for understanding and more importantly explaining how we pay taxes , currently TAIKI supports variety of Text heavy , Tax Documents which are perfect for combining trending technologies , like Retrieval Augmented Generation , Finetuned Language Models , Document Transformers , and of course OCR


-


**Taiki** currently supports US Tax Forms like W-2,1099-NEC, Bank Statements Brokerage Statements and More


-


Integrates with major payroll vendors like


**BambooHR** ,


**Amex** ,


**Cash App** ,


**Deel**

### [ARCANE](https://arcanelabs.ai/) ( Immersive Experiences)


-


Arcane is building fully AI generated immersive story Experiences , these worlds are not empty and boring instead every NPC , every Environment , every in game Event , can Talk , adapt , improvise based on what is your move


-


They have launched their first product


[Summon Worlds](https://www.summonworlds.com/) which has the said features mixed in with some community engagement , multiplayer events and stats and backstory generation


-


Built by a team a Ex Meta Engineers Rohan and Lucas who have worked on the Meta AR Glasses team


### **[Andy AI](https://with-andy.com/) (Healthtech)**


-


Andy is building an Clinical Data Scribe for Home Nurses using AI


-


Documentation especially verbose clinical forms is the major cause for burnout among Home Nurses these documentations are long , like really long a single doc could take 90 minutes to fill causing losses worth of 8 Billion USD in the industry


-


Sign up with Andy , take a 15 minute call with it , and it automatically generates a EHR report


-


Andy’s massive training data , helps them find 45 percent more details than a trained clinicians


-


Works with major EHR providers like


**WellSky, Axxes & DeVero**
