# Spok-Powered Hospitals Recognized on 2026-2027 U.S. News & World Report's Best Hospitals Honor Roll

*Leading healthcare systems continue to rely on Spok to drive care coordination and deliver exceptional patient outcomes.*


**Plano, Texas. (Aug. 6, 2026) –**[Spok, Inc.,](http://www.spok.com/) a wholly owned subsidiary of Spok Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: SPOK) and a global leader in healthcare communications, today reported that 18 of the 20 adult hospitals featured on the[2026-2027 U.S. News & World Report Best Hospitals Honor Roll](https://health.usnews.com/health-care/best-hospitals/slideshows/the-honor-roll-of-best-hospitals) use Spok technology to enhance care coordination and support exceptional patient care. For over a decade, the majority of Honor Roll hospitals have consistently leveraged Spok solutions to streamline workflows and improve care team coordination.


“We congratulate these Honor Roll hospitals on their outstanding achievement. Their recognition reflects years of dedication to patient care and clinical excellence,” said Vincent D. Kelly, chief executive officer of Spok Holdings, Inc. “We are grateful for the trust they place in Spok to strengthen communication across their care teams and remain committed to providing the communication infrastructure that helps their teams deliver outstanding care.”


The 2026-2027 Honor Roll recipients are among the more than 2,000 hospitals worldwide that depend on Spok solutions to eliminate communication barriers, accelerate clinical response times, and enhance patient experiences. As the global authority on hospital rankings, the U.S. News & World Report has compiled the U.S. News & World Report Best Hospitals Honor Roll for over three decades. This recognition program evaluates approximately 4,500 healthcare organizations annually, naming 20 of the country’s highest-performing hospitals among 14 adult specialties or specialty areas and 23 procedures and conditions. The rankings are extensively referenced because they prioritize measurable, data-driven indicators of hospital performance, including patient outcomes, nursing quality, and patient experience.
