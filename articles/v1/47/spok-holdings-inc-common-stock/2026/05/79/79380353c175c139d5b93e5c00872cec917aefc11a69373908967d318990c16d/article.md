# 4 common challenges for hospital contact centers

- High call volumes remain one of the most pressing hospital contact center challenges, driving extended wait times and higher abandonment rates that directly impact the patient experience.
- Recruiting and retaining skilled staff is an ongoing struggle for healthcare call centers, and inadequate training compounds the problem by limiting agent productivity and caller satisfaction.
- Rising patient expectations in healthcare communication require hospitals to move beyond traditional approaches and adopt digital channels that meet patients where they are.
- Disorganized software systems create communication barriers that slow hospital call center operations and frustrate both staff and callers.


Contact centers serve as a critical communication bridge between healthcare facilities and their communities, playing an indispensable role when creating ideal patient experiences. Despite the technological advancements that have revolutionized these workplaces in recent years, many hospital contact center challenges directly impact employees’ abilities to be productive and[fulfill the needs](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8177735) of the caller on the other line.


By acknowledging the contact center operational challenges that you may face, your healthcare organization is one step closer to finding the best solutions and maintaining your essential communication channel.


#### **High call volumes**


[High call volumes](https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/how-clinical-communications-tech-helped-one-hospital-lower-call-volume-70) are one of the most pressing challenges for hospital contact centers across the country, often resulting in[extended waiting times and higher abandonment rates.](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2023/08/02/first-batch-of-cms-unwinding-data-includes-key-call-center-statistics/)


By adopting new technology that evenly distributes calls and includes self-service options for simpler requests, employees can get through these high volumes at a much quicker pace.


With the help of predictive analytics, hospitals can alleviate these contact center management challenges by forecasting any spikes in call volume and schedule accordingly to this increased demand.


To learn more about how contact center technology plays a pivotal role in handling high call volumes during national disasters, learn more about[our success story](https://www.spok.com/resources/womans-hospital/) with Woman’s Hospital in Baton Rouge.


#### **Staff recruiting and retention**


While dealing with budget constraints, hospitals often struggle to attract and retain the best employees for their contact centers. Poorly trained employees often are unable to fulfil complex questions for the caller on the other line,[resulting in lowered staff productivity and patient dissatisfaction](https://www.callcentrehelper.com/images/resources/2021/procedureflow-state-of-the-contact-centre-training-report-whitepaper-210623.pdf) .


Investing in better employee benefits, such as remote work flexibility or commuter perks, along with comprehensive programs that include[culturally-sensitive training](https://www.spok.com/resources/the-call-center-of-the-future/?_gl=1*ag288m*_gcl_au*MTc4NDc5NzIxNC4xNzczNzcxNzEw) , helps healthcare organizations deliver high-quality communication experiences and address contact center operational challenges.


#### **Evolving patient expectations**


[Patient expectations](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbesbusinesscouncil/2021/06/01/healthcare-shifting-customer-expectations-require-a-focus-on-customer-experience/?sh=487216931913) towards their healthcare experiences and accessibility of their personal information continue to evolve, making it increasingly important for healthcare organizations to ensure their clinical data systems and communication channels are keeping pace with these rising standards.


To meet these demands, hospitals need to move away from traditional approaches and embrace digital transformation. Adopting new communication channels will allow employees to navigate these contact center challenges with more ease.


#### **Disorganized software systems**


In the face of mergers and acquisitions, hospital contact centers may have incompatible software systems that create more barriers in communication, leading to lost productivity and potential dissatisfaction from callers on the other line.


These challenges for hospital contact centers are further exacerbated when one system experiences technical difficulties, affecting the rest of the healthcare organization’s communication infrastructure.


By consolidating disorganized software systems into[a centralized solution](https://www.spok.com/resources/university-of-pittsburgh-medical-center/?utm_campaign=2023%20Case%20Studies&utm_content=259488298&utm_medium=social&utm_source=linkedin&hss_channel=lcp-34434) , contact center employees can dedicate more of their time towards delivering exceptional patient experiences.


A hospital’s ability to navigate contact center management challenges largely depends on having multi-faceted strategies. With emerging technologies, comprehensive training, and patient-centric approaches, your healthcare organization can pave the way for continued success.
