# Newell Brands to Webcast Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Results

[PDF Version](https://ir.newellbrands.com/node/30476/pdf)


ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 2, 2026-- Newell Brands Inc. (NASDAQ: NWL) today announced its second quarter 2026 earnings results will be released Friday, July 31, 2026 prior to market open and will be followed by a live webcast at 7:30 a.m. ET. To listen to the webcast, please select Events & Presentations from the Investors tab of the Newell Brands website at[www.newellbrands.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.newellbrands.com&esheet=54563760&newsitemid=20260702073715&lan=en-US&anchor=www.newellbrands.com&index=1&md5=81910726735dd677a8554e4a461842e9) . The live webcast will be recorded and made available for replay.


**About Newell Brands**


Newell Brands (NASDAQ: NWL) is a leading global consumer goods company with a strong portfolio of well-known brands, including Rubbermaid, Sharpie, Graco, Coleman, Rubbermaid Commercial Products, Yankee Candle, Paper Mate, FoodSaver, Dymo, EXPO, Elmer’s, Oster, NUK, Spontex and Campingaz. Newell Brands is focused on delighting consumers by lighting up everyday moments.
