# Newell Brands Declares Dividend on Common Stock

[PDF Version](https://ir.newellbrands.com/node/30306/pdf)


ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 6, 2026-- Newell Brands Inc. (NASDAQ: NWL) announced today the declaration of a quarterly cash dividend of $0.07 per share. The dividend is payable June 15, 2026 to common stockholders of record at the close of trading on May 29, 2026.


**About Newell Brands**


Newell Brands (NASDAQ: NWL) is a leading global consumer goods company with a strong portfolio of well-known brands, including Rubbermaid, Sharpie, Graco, Coleman, Rubbermaid Commercial Products, Yankee Candle, Paper Mate, FoodSaver, Dymo, EXPO, Elmer’s, Oster, NUK, Spontex and Campingaz. Newell Brands is focused on delighting consumers by lighting up everyday moments.
