# 2026 Sustainability Report

2026 Sustainability Report


melody_admin


Thu, 06/11/2026 - 11:07


Image


The 2026 report highlights Vontier's success in delivering on its mission to mobilize the future to create a better world. Among the key findings; Vontier has reduced its absolute Scope 1 & 2 emissions by 49%, surpassing its 2030 target five years ahead of schedule. In addition to climate achievements, Vontier reached its 2030 waste diversion goal early by diverting 91% of manufacturing site waste from landfills. The company also generated $8.4 million in operational cost savings via internal efficiency initiatives.
