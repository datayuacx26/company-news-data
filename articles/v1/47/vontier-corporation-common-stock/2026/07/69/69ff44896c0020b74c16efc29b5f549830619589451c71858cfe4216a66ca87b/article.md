# Vontier Debuts on Newsweek’s World’s Greenest Companies List

### Vontier Debuts on Newsweek’s World’s Greenest Companies List


July 7, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/187135268/files/doc_news/Vontier-Debuts-on-Newsweeks-Worlds-Greenest-Companies-List-2026.pdf)


RALEIGH, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Vontier Corporation (NYSE: VNT), a leading global provider of critical technologies and solutions to connect, manage and scale the mobility ecosystem, today announced that it was named one of[Newsweek’s World’s Greenest Companies for 2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Frankings.newsweek.com%2Fworlds-greenest-companies-2026&esheet=54565959&newsitemid=20260707862786&lan=en-US&anchor=Newsweek%26%238217%3Bs+World%26%238217%3Bs+Greenest+Companies+for+2026&index=1&md5=906899a7db2a8d190bb25c6613a276a9) .


This is the first year the company was included on the selective list, following earlier recognition on Newsweek’s lists of America’s Greenest and Most Responsible Companies. Inclusion on the global list represents a higher level of international distinction, requiring Vontier’s operations to meet 25 rigorous EU sustainability criteria.


“At Vontier, we see sustainability as smart business – driving efficiency, accountability and long-term value,” said Katie Rowen, EVP and Chief Transformation and Operations Officer at Vontier. “Throughout the year, we’ve mobilized hundreds of colleagues across the globe to identify and implement practical efficiency gains, creating a ripple effect of transformative change. We’re proud of the progress we’ve made thus far and are committed to continuing to raise the bar.”


The annual ranking, produced in partnership with Plant-A Insights Group and GIST Impact, honors the top 850 companies across 28 countries for demonstrated performance in environmental sustainability. Newsweek evaluated over 8,000 companies across four categories: greenhouse gas emissions, water usage, waste generation and sustainability data disclosure and commitments.


The Newsweek recognition builds on Vontier’s growing portfolio of sustainability achievements, including those from CDP, EcoVadis, USA TODAY, American Business Awards, International Sustainability Awards and TIME. For more information on the company’s nationally recognized sustainability efforts, please visit[www.vontier.com/responsibility](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.vontier.com%2Fresponsibility&esheet=54565959&newsitemid=20260707862786&lan=en-US&anchor=www.vontier.com%2Fresponsibility&index=2&md5=af4923950f7f2095a97ecdc0803b05a6) .
