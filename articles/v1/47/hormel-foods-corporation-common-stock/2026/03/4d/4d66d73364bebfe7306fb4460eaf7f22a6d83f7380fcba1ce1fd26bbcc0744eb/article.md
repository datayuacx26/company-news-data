Newsweek names Hormel Foods as one of the top 100 Most Charitable Companies


This first-of-its-kind recognition by Newsweek, in collaboration with Statista, underscores a deep commitment to strengthening the communities where Hormel Foods team members live and operate.


Ranked No. 90 among 300 companies and No. 9 within the retail and consumer goods category, this recognition reinforces the company’s commitment to charitable giving and community impact. It reflects how we come together to support our team members and their families while nourishing communities around the world.


Learn more and see the full list of companies awarded[here](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-most-charitable-companies-2026) .
