Hormel Foods Joins Ranks of America’s 2026 Climate Leaders.


The Planet pillar of our Global Impact framework,[Good Feeds Us All](https://www.hormelfoods.com/global-impact/good-feeds-us-all/) , reflects our commitment to climate leadership and building a more sustainable food company. This award recognizes the measurable progress we’ve made through innovation, accountability and long-term sustainability initiatives.


As a global food company, we follow a clear and deliberate path to drive positive impact for our people, our industry and the communities we serve. This recognition highlights how our continued investments in environmental sustainability demonstrate what it means to lead responsibly; fueling our Good Feeds Us All purpose every day.


Learn more about this climate leadership recognition and explore the full list of awarded companies[here](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/nation/2026/04/20/americas-climate-leaders-2026-list/89604420007/) .
