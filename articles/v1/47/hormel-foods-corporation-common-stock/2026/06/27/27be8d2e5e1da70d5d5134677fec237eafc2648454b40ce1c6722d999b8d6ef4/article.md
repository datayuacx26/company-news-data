June 5, 2026 Last month,[Osceola (Iowa) Food](https://www.hormelfoods.com/careers/career-center/our-locations/osceola-food/attachment/osceola-food-2/) marked an exciting milestone, 30 years, with a celebration to remember.


Operations paused for the day as team members and their families gathered onsite for a picnic filled with great food, including hot dogs, hamburgers and a special visit from the SPAM® brand food trailer serving up SPAM® Gochujang street tacos, truly bringing our purpose of Fueling Real Life to the forefront.


From family friendly games to plant tours, it was a memorable way to celebrate three decades of teamwork, dedication and community.
