February 23, 2026 Discover bold, globally inspired flavor with PLANTERS® Garlic Chili Crisp Flavored Cashews — the newest flavor to join the PLANTERS® flavored cashew lineup. A craveable mix of heat, crunch and savory satisfaction, this flavor taps into one of today’s hottest food trends.


Inspired by chili crisp’s rapid rise in popularity, this standout flavor draws from Asian culinary roots and the growing fusion of global flavors across foods people love. The result is a snack that delivers layered heat, rich umami and irresistible crunch in every bite.


Expertly roasted in peanut oil for a perfectly crisp texture, these premium whole cashews are seasoned with spicy chili peppers and a punch of garlic for a mouthwatering flavor experience.


PLANTERS® Garlic Chili Crisp cashews join a flavorful family that includes Cinnamon Brown Sugar, Dill Pickle, Rosemary & Sea Salt and Salt and Vinegar, offering a variety of bold and craveable options for every snacking occasion.


Perfect for cocktail parties, lunch boxes, road trips or outdoor adventures, these flavored cashews come packed in a 5-ounce resealable stand-up bag designed for easy sharing and snacking on the go.


With an MSRP of $4.29–$4.99, PLANTERS® Garlic Chili Crisp flavored cashews deliver bold flavor anytime, anywhere.
