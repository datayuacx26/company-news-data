# Instant Access Rewards: Instant Perks for Every Pledge

We’re excited to introduce


**Instant Access Rewards** , a new way for creators to deliver digital content, exclusive links, and codes to backers immediately after they pledge, follow, or reserve.


By giving backers something right away, you create a stronger incentive to pledge now instead of later, while also increasing the appeal of higher pledge levels.


## Drive Momentum by Delivering Immediate Value


Crowdfunding is all about momentum. Instant Access Rewards help you build it faster.


Exclusive to


[BackerKit](https://www.backerkit.com/) , Instant Access Rewards give creators a way to reward supporters in the moment, turning audience excitement into action from day one and helping drive more pledges throughout the entire campaign.


- **Reward backers instantly** with digital perks the moment they pledge, follow, or reserve


- **Increase conversion rates** by adding immediate value to pledge levels


- **Encourage higher pledges** with early-access bonuses


- **Keep backers engaged** with digital exclusives (eg. project prototypes/previews)


## What You Can Offer with Instant Access


Instant Access Rewards in action: Blades ‘68 by Evil Hat


Instant Access Rewards are flexible and easy to tailor to your project. You can deliver:


- **Exclusive content** like quickstart guides, PDFs, wallpapers, or music


- **Private community access** such as Discord servers or member hubs


- **Promo or unlock codes** for your store or future products


- **External content links** via platforms like Dropbox or DriveThruRPG


Whether you’re launching a[tabletop RPG](https://www.backerkit.com/tabletop-role-playing-games) , a book or publishing project, or any creative project or product that has the potential for a digital component, Instant Access Rewards can help you build momentum early and deliver value to your fans instantly.


**What if a backer cancels their pledge?** We recommend offering rewards that you are willing to give away for free in the event that a backer cancels their pledge or if their payment doesn’t go through. This could be a first chapter of a book, or a set of sample pages from a comic, etc. If a backer cancels their pledge, they will be unable to re-download the file.

## How It Works


For


**backers** , choosing and receiving Instant Access Rewards is seamless:


- The reward is clearly labeled as


**“Instant Access”**


- After completing their pledge, follow, or reservation, backers immediately see their reward


- They can access their content, link, or code right away. No waiting required.


For


**creators** , the set up is easy.


- Create an item in your project admin


- Enable Instant Access


- Choose your reward type:


**Link, Code, or File**
- Add optional instructions and publish
