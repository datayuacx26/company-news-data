# BackerKit’s New BookFunnel Integration: Boost Book Pledges with Instant Access Rewards

Crowdfunding a book just got a whole lot easier.


We’re thrilled to announce our new integration with


[BookFunnel](https://bookfunnel.com/) , the go-to platform for indie authors and publishers to distribute ebooks and audiobooks directly to their readers.


Now, when you[launch a book campaign on BackerKit](https://www.backerkit.com/publishing) , you can automatically deliver digital rewards to backers right at the moment they pledge, reserve, or follow your campaign. Readers who unlock digital rewards are immediately shown a unique link to their BookFunnel library where they can access their digital rewards instantly.


**For readers and backers** : This means no waiting until the campaign ends to receive digital rewards and even more reason to back your campaign on day one.


**For creators** : This offers more pledge incentives to build early campaign momentum, along with a hassle-free, automated process that solves digital rewards fulfillment – for good.


No CSVs. No manual emails. No delays . Just an easy, seamless experience for you and your readers.

### How It Works


For


**backers** , discovering and receiving instant digital rewards is intuitive and easy.


- Digital rewards are clearly marked “Instant Access” in the reward tier


- Links to access digital rewards are provided as soon as backers complete their pledge, follow, or reservation


- With a single click, they’re taken to their BookFunnel library where they can start reading or listening right away


*Note: Backers will need to setup a BookFunnel account to access their library


For


**authors and publishers** , setup is simple.


How to set up:


- Contact BookFunnel support to request your BookFunnel API key


- Connect your BookFunnel account using your BookFunnel API key


- Select the digital reward to include in your pledge levels


- Enter the corresponding Book UID for each reward in your pledge level


- Add an optional title and instructions for your backers


[See the full setup guide.](https://help.backerkit.com/article/956-how-do-i-integrate-with-bookfunnel)
