# 2025 in Crowdfunding: BackerKit’s Biggest Moments That Shaped the Year

2025 tested creators everywhere. Economic uncertainty as well as rising manufacturing and shipping costs made launching and fulfilling a crowdfunding campaign harder than ever.


Still, creators pushed forward, collaborated, and found opportunity in the fray. And on[BackerKit](https://www.backerkit.com/) , many launched their biggest, boldest projects yet.


That momentum carried across the entire platform and helped produce some of the biggest moments and milestones at BackerKit that shaped the year.


## BackerKit’s Biggest Moments & Milestones of 2025


1. **We hosted record-breaking creators, campaigns, and category growth** . Topping lists and setting trends across Films, Plushies, Graphic Novels, TTRPGs, and more.


2. **We saw the exponential growth of BackerKit Collab-Funding.** Collab-Funding campaigns grew 3x and Collab-Funding pledge-volume grew 7x year-over-year. Plus, we achieved 100% funding for all 2025 Topia projects.


3. **We shipped impactful tools built for the crowdfunding pro.** BackerKit Tariff Manager, Pre-Launch Reservation, Audience Intelligence, Patreon Integration, and so much more.


4. **We raised $3M+ through our Wefunder campaign** thanks to *you* , our community of creators and backers. Thousands of investors backed us and our vision for the future of crowdfunding built for the creative pro.
5. **We even hit new milestones behind the scenes at BackerKit** . Our people and platform are setting new industry standards for crowdfunding success.

## New Pro Tools Built for the Professional Creator


BackerKit has always focused on delivering powerful, practical tools built in close collaboration with creators. Since 2012, when it was just Maxwell and I as founders, our team has worked side-by-side with creators to build[a crowdfunding platform that evolves with their needs](https://www.backerkit.com/crowdfunding) .


Here’s what we shipped together in 2025:


### Tariff Manager


When tariffs and global costs spiked in April, creators needed clarity—fast. We responded by launching


[BackerKit Tariff Manager](https://www.backerkit.com/blog/backerkit-tariff-manager-for-crowdfunding) .


Available for both BackerKit Crowdfunding and Pledge Manager, this feature lets creators clearly communicate tariff fees and apply them as a separate checkout line item. It was built with direct input from


**500+ creators** who responded to our Tariff Survey, ensuring immediate, real-world impact.


### Pre-Launch Reservation


Bigger launches lead to bigger campaigns. In summer 2025, we introduced


[BackerKit Pre-Launch Reservation](https://www.backerkit.com/blog/backerkit-pre-launch-reservation) —a new way to activate high-intent fans before launch day.


Early results:


- **$210K+** collected in pre-launch reservation revenue


- **80% conversion rate** from reservations to pledges


- **Nearly $2M** in pledge revenue from reservers


Creators saw:


- Higher-quality, high-intent backers


- Stronger Day 1 momentum and social proof


- More predictable launches


- Less wasted spend on low-intent leads


Creators who paired reservations with clear incentives and enough pre-launch runway saw the strongest results. To further enhance your campaign, we have also released several new features, which you can explore


[here](https://www.backerkit.com/blog/6-new-backerkit-features-to-boost-your-campaign/) .


### Audience Intelligence


Before you can rally your fans, you need to understand them.


[BackerKit Audience Intelligence](https://www.backerkit.com/blog/from-followers-to-funded-4-new-crowdfunding-features-to-grow-and-activate-your-audience/) gives creators x-ray vision into their campaign’s engagement journey—tracking how visitors become followers, and followers become backers.


Creators can:


- Identify conversion drop-offs and friction points


- Optimize actions at each stage of the funnel


- Track audience growth across campaigns


- See their fan growth compound over time


For further updates on new BackerKit features designed to help you grow and activate your audience, including the crowdfunding calculator and simplified shipping, you can learn more


[here](https://www.backerkit.com/blog/from-followers-to-funded-4-new-crowdfunding-features-to-grow-and-activate-your-audience/) .


### Patreon Integration


In 2025, we launched our


[BackerKit Patreon Integration](https://www.backerkit.com/patreon) , the


**first Patreon-to-crowdfunding integration of its kind** .


Patreon creators can now seamlessly turn their existing audience into crowdfunding backers with exclusive rewards and targeted pledge levels—making crowdfunding faster, easier, and more effective.


This integration helped


**Freddie Wong** (Co-founder of RocketJump and Dungeons & Daddies)—the


**#7 Patreon Podcast Creator** —power his campaign past


**$1M** , making it the most-funded crowdfunded film of 2025 and #1 most-funded crowdfunded action film of all time.
