# From Pledge to Play: BackerKit’s DriveThruRPG Integration Instantly Delivers Digital Rewards

We’re excited to announce our new integration with


**[DriveThruRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/)** , the largest marketplace and distribution platform for RPG publishers and fans.


Now,


[Tabletop RPG (TTRPG) creators who launch a BackerKit crowdfunding campaign](https://www.backerkit.com/tabletop-role-playing-games) can automatically deliver digital rewards to backers the moment they pledge, reserve, or follow your campaign.


**For backers,** this means no more waiting until the campaign ends to enjoy digital rewards. Unlock content instantly and start exploring new adventures in minutes.


**For creators,** this means more backers following and supporting your project in pre-launch, on launch day, and beyond – building valuable early momentum that lasts throughout your campaign.


No longer will you need to copy links or wrestle with spreadsheets. Through this integration, you can avoid delivery delays and provide the fastest, most seamless way for backers to go from pledge to play.

## How It Works


Instant Access Rewards in action: Blades ‘68 by Evil Hat


For


**backers** , the experience is easy and intuitive:


1. Digital rewards show up clearly marked


**“Instant Access”** in the reward tier


2. Right after they pledge, follow, or reserve, they’ll see a link to access their rewards


3. One click, and they’re in their DriveThruRPG library onto their next adventure


Backers without a free DriveThruRPG account will be prompted to create one to access their library.


## Getting Started


For


**creators and publishers** , you’ll need the following to use the integration:


- A DriveThruRPG Partner Account.


[Create your free account today >>](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/)
- A BackerKit crowdfunding project.


[Create your project for free in minutes >>](https://www.backerkit.com/c/admin/projects/new?ref=drivethrurpg_integration_062326)
- API access from DriveThruRPG.


**Where to find your Application Key:** Creators can receive their application key from their DriveThruRPG customer account settings under[The Library App Key section of this page](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/account/settings) . Distribution should be toggled **on before getting the application key** . If you run into any issues, please[contact DriveThruRPG Partner Relations](https://help.drivethrupartners.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) .


Once you have your API key, connecting to BackerKit and adding books or media files to your reward tiers takes just a few minutes.


[See the full setup guide](https://help.backerkit.com/article/970-how-do-i-integrate-with-drivethrurpg) .
