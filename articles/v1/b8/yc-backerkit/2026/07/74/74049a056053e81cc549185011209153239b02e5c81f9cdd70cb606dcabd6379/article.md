# BackerKit at Gen Con 2026

BackerKit is back for another Gen Con! We can’t wait to dive into four days of tabletop gaming with the crowdfunding community.


Find us in Indianapolis **July 30 – August 2** , at **Booth #363** in the exhibit hall. We’ll have demos, playtests, panels, and the return of our fan-favorite **Crowdfunding Side Quest** activity.


See you there. We can’t wait to connect in person again.


We’ve got a great spot in the Exhibit Hall this year at Booth #363, and we’d love to see you there in person.


There’s no better way to strategize and collaborate on your crowdfunding plans than face to face, so stop by, say hi, and tell us what you’re working on. Be sure to[mark us on your map](https://www.gencon.com/map?c=27&f=1&lg=4.460449218750001&lt=29.611670115197406&s=17453&z=4) so you don’t miss us!


**At our booth you can expect to:**


**Participate in Playtests & Demos:** throughout the con we’ll be hosting live playtests and demos right at our booth featuring: ***As_Intended Games, Casper Haunts, Chaosium, Gifts Galore Games, Late Fee Studios, Louie’s Loops, My Mini Factory, Roll for Combat, Sunrise Tornado, Tavern Crawl Games, Twogether Studios,*** and ***Unphazed Games!***


Follow us on **[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/backerkit/?hl=en)** , **[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/backerkit.com)** , and **[Twitter](https://twitter.com/BackerKit)** for up-to-date demo schedules and alerts!


Participate in our annual BackerKit Crowdfunding Side Quest. Complete the quest this year and you’ll be rewarded with the newest enamel pin to add to your BackerKit train collection. Huge thank you to[Karen Alleluia](https://www.instagram.com/karenalleluia/?hl=en) for the amazing new art! **Talk shop with the BackerKit team.** Whether you’re planning to launch a crowdfunding project soon or it’s still a ways off, stop by our booth and let’s talk through it together. We’ll have experts on hand throughout the entire convention to help set you up for crowdfunding success.


Catch us as a guest panelist on Panda Game Manufacturing’s panel, **[Launching your Board Game: Insights into Crowdfunding, Manufacturing, and Fulfillment](https://www.gencon.com/events/304569)** . RSVP now as space is limited!


*Ever wondered what it takes to get your idea funded, manufactured, and into the hands of board gamers? Join experts in crowdfunding (BackerKit), manufacturing (Panda Game Manufacturing), and fulfillment (Quartermaster Logistics) for tips and tricks on navigating the process successfully. We’ll wrap with a Q&A so attendees can ask specific questions about their own board game projects.*


**And of course, swing by the BackerKit booth #363 while you’re at Gen Con. We can’t wait to see you all in Indianapolis soon!**
