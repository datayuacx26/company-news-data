## **How it works**


- **In pre-launch, you offer the reservation reward on your Launch Party page** (e.g. FREE dice with a $1 fee)


- **Fans pay a small fee** (e.g. $1–$5) to reserve their spot and their perk


- **At launch, fans return and pledge** with their reward automatically added to their cart for easy checkout


BackerKit Pre-Launch Reservation is a powerful expansion of our existing pre-launch features, like[BackerKit Launch Party](https://www.backerkit.com/blog/mini-museum-5-best-launch-ever/) , which help turn your fans into campaign followers, and those followers into backers.


But by adding a Pre-Launch Reservation to your Launch Party page, you give your fans a chance to extend their support one step further and signal a stronger commitment to pledge once your campaign goes live.


And since Pre-Launch Reservations are powered end-to-end on[BackerKit](https://www.backerkit.com/?utm_source=backerkit_blog&utm_medium=wordpress&utm_campaign=pre-launch_reservation_release&ref=pre-launch_reservation_release_090428) , the experience is seamless. Your backers can follow, reserve, and pledge to your campaign all in place – and you can track pre-launch activity to post-launch conversions all on the BackerKit Crowdfunding platform.

## **Pre-Launch Reservation in Action**


**World’s Largest Dungeon**


The creators from World’s Largest RPG were trailblazers and initial beta testers of our Pre-Launch Reservation, which they offered on their currently running project,


[World’s Largest Dungeon](https://www.backerkit.com/c/projects/world-s-largest-rpgs/world-s-largest-dungeon?ref=backerkit_blog_pre-launch_reservation) .


During pre-launch, they offered followers the chance to reserve a free dice, bag, and coin by paying a $1 fee. The response was impressive: nearly **10%** of their pre-launch audience made a reservation, and since the campaign launched, about **57% of those who reserved have gone on to pledge** .


This highly qualified segment helped them better forecast demand, build early momentum, and lock in support from their most enthusiastic fans.


The results speak for themselves—followers who made a pre-launch reservation were **over 12x more likely to pledge** than those who didn’t.
