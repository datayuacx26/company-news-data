# Best trust center software for repetitive questionnaires

**TL;DR**


- The portal is close to a commodity. What separates trust center software is what happens to the questionnaires the portal does not deflect, and whether the portal and the answers run from one knowledge base.
- Wolfia ranks first for teams with repetitive questionnaires because the trust center is included with the product and answers the fallback questionnaires from the same self-maintaining knowledge base, with a citation on every answer and no volume caps.

- Match the tool to your real bottleneck: pure document deflection, or deflection plus completing the repetitive questionnaires that get through.


## What is the best trust center software for repetitive questionnaires?


The best trust center software for repetitive questionnaires is the one where the portal and the answers come from a single knowledge base. Wolfia leads on that test: the trust center is included with the product, and every questionnaire the portal does not deflect is answered from that same self-maintaining knowledge base, cited on every answer, with no volume cap. Trust-center-first tools deflect the easy requests but route the harder questionnaires back to your team and a separately maintained library, which is exactly where repetitive work returns.


## Why the portal is the easy part


A branded page that hosts your SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certificate, penetration test summary, and security policies is not hard to stand up. Most vendors on this list do it well. The differentiators sit around the portal:


- **Access control depth.** Can you gate documents per category and per buyer segment, or is it one global approve-and-deny gate?
- **NDA gating.** Can you require a countersigned NDA on sensitive artifacts and log which version each buyer signed?
- **CRM integration.** Do access events update account records so sales sees who viewed what?
- **Analytics.** Can you measure deflection by account and document type to know where to invest content?
- **Questionnaire fallback.** When a buyer sends a custom spreadsheet anyway, does the same tool answer it from the same knowledge base?


Underneath all four sits one question: does the knowledge base maintain itself, or does it go stale until someone grooms it? ## 2. SafeBase


SafeBase is the strongest standalone deflection portal on this list. Drata acquired it for[$250 million in February 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/12/security-compliance-firm-drata-acquires-safebase-for-250m/) , and the product is mature: branded portals, granular access rules, automated NDA workflows, and prospect engagement analytics. Its questionnaire help is a Chrome extension that suggests answers you copy and paste across OneTrust, Panorays, ProcessUnity, ServiceNow, and other portals.


The tradeoffs for a repetitive-questionnaire workload are that questionnaire assistance is a secondary feature rather than the core product, the knowledge base is manually maintained, and its tiered pricing gates features by plan. The tradeoff is the upkeep: questionnaire help stays a copy-paste extension and someone has to keep the library current, which is worth it when deflecting inbound document requests is the main job. Our[SafeBase reviews, pricing, and alternatives](https://wolfia.com/blog/safebase-reviews-pricing-alternatives) breakdown goes deeper, and[SafeBase vs Conveyor vs Wolfia](https://wolfia.com/blog/safebase-vs-conveyor-vs-wolfia) runs the head-to-head.


## 3. Conveyor


Conveyor is a questionnaire automation tool that ships a trust center alongside it. It was founded in 2021 and raised a[$12.5 million Series A in 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/11/conveyor-raises-12-5m-to-automate-security-reviews-using-llms/) , followed by a[$20 million Series B](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/12/conveyor-uses-ai-to-automate-the-painful-process-of-vendor-security-reviews-and-rfps-with-ai/) . Its trust center runs on a credit-based model where access counts against your plan's usage limits, and its knowledge base is a manually maintained set of Q&A pairs.


The credit model is the thing to plan around for repetitive volume: the Free plan, for example, includes 10 trust center credits per month, a maximum of 15 documents, and a maximum of 15 Q&As, so meaningful use requires moving up tiers. Best for teams that want questionnaire drafting with a portal on the side and can manage credit allocations. See our[Conveyor reviews, pricing, and alternatives](https://wolfia.com/blog/conveyor-reviews-pricing-alternatives) for the full picture.


## 4. Vanta Trust Center


Vanta is a compliance automation engine that added a trust center to share the SOC 2, ISO 27001, and HIPAA evidence it generates. For teams already running Vanta compliance, setup is fast because the portal inherits the certifications and evidence Vanta already collected, and it has Salesforce integration.


For a repetitive-questionnaire workload the tradeoffs are shallower access control than dedicated trust center products, questionnaire automation capped by plan volume, and a knowledge base that requires manual upkeep. The question to weigh: will a portal with shallower access control and plan-capped questionnaire automation cover the deals you actually run? For teams whose primary purchase is compliance automation, it often does.

## What happens when the same questions arrive again


The hardest test for any trust center is not what the portal shows. It is what happens when the same buyer questions arrive for the tenth time this quarter, often as a custom spreadsheet a procurement team requires regardless of your portal. A trust center satisfies initial due diligence in many deals, but it does not replace a full questionnaire in HIPAA-covered arrangements, FedRAMP evaluations, or procurement processes that require their own template. Government-adjacent deals add[FedRAMP-compatible trust center requirements](https://wolfia.com/blog/fedramp-compatible-trust-center-requirements) a generic portal will not satisfy.


How a tool handles that fallback is what the ranking above turns on. For that handoff in practice, see[what to do when a buyer rejects your trust center and sends a custom questionnaire](https://wolfia.com/blog/buyer-rejects-trust-center-custom-questionnaire) .


## Final thoughts


The fastest way to pick is to count what actually reaches your team after the portal has done its work. If almost nothing gets through and you can staff the upkeep, SafeBase is the strongest standalone portal. If compliance automation is the primary purchase, Vanta bundles a convenient portal. But if custom questionnaires keep landing on someone's desk every quarter, weigh the tools on that fallback first and the portal second, because the fallback is the half of the job that quietly consumes your team, and it is the half where Wolfia ranks first.
