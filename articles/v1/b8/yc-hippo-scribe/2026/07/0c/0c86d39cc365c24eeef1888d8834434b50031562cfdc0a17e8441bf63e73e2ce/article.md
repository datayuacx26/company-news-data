# Hippo Scribe

**Are you a pediatric occupational therapist (OT) or speech-language pathologist (SLP) struggling with documentation while trying to provide the best care for your kiddos? Hippo Scribe is here to revolutionize your practice.**


**The Real Challenges of Pediatric OT/SLP Documentation**


As a pediatric OT or SLP, you face unique challenges in your daily practice. One of our customers puts it perfectly:


"Doing point of care documentation is almost impossible with our line of work."


This sentiment resonates with many in the field, highlighting two key issues:


1.


**Intense focus required during sessions**


-


Working with children with special needs, such as autism, demands your undivided attention


-


Engaging with patients leaves little time for note-taking


2.


**Constant battle with documentation backlog**


-


Falling behind on notes becomes a common occurrence


-


Work-life balance suffers as you struggle to catch up after hours


Another therapist shared their experience:


"We have a lot of autistic clients and young ones... I love it, but it definitely, like, you have to give a lot of your, like, self. I guess you need a little extra time to just chill and recharge again."


This highlights the emotional and mental toll of the job, emphasizing the need for a solution that allows therapists to focus on patient care without sacrificing their well-being.


**The Hippo Scribe Solution**


Hippo Scribe is an AI Scribe that records your session on your phone and afterwards generates your documentation in your own format.


**Key Benefits:**


1.


**Real-time documentation**


-


Hippo Scribe listens and transcribes your sessions as they happen


-


Accurate notes are generated without diverting your attention from patients


2.


**Customized for pediatric OT/SLP**


-


Understands specialized terminology and common interventions for both occupational therapy and speech-language pathology


-


Adapts to your unique style and preferences over time


3.


**Seamless EMR integration via Chrome Extension**


-


View AI-generated notes directly alongside your existing EMR, such as Fusion Web Clinic


-


Effortlessly transfer generated notes to your EMR with a simple copy-paste function


4.


**HIPAA-compliant and secure**


-


Ensures patient privacy and data protection


-


All data are encrypted in transit and at rest


-


Audios are automatically deleted


**Reclaim Your Work-Life Balance**


With Hippo Scribe, you can:


-


Focus entirely on the kiddo during sessions, especially those with special needs who require your full attention


-


Complete documentation in real-time, eliminating the need for after-hours catch-up


-


Reduce stress and burnout associated with administrative tasks


-


Improve the quality and consistency of your notes


-


Find time to "chill and recharge" after giving so much of yourself during sessions


**Don't let documentation hold you back from providing exceptional care or enjoying your personal time. Embrace the future of pediatric OT and SLP practice with Hippo Scribe and rediscover the joy in your profession.**
