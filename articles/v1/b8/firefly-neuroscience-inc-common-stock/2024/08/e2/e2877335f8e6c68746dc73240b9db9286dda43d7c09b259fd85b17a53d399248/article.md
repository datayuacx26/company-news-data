# Firefly Neuroscience Forms Strategic Partnership with Neurology Consultants of Dallas


August 21, 2024 / News


***Firefly Neuroscience Forms Strategic Partnership with Neurology Consultants of Dallas to Enhance Early Detection Efforts and Disease Management for Patients with Cognitive Disorders***


Firefly Neuroscience, Inc., an AI technology company developing innovative neuroscientific solutions that improve brain health outcomes for patients with mental illnesses and neurological disorders, announced that it has formed a strategic partnership with the Neurology Consultants of Dallas to help enhance early detection efforts and disease management for patients suffering from cognitive decline.
