# What Yoga Does to Your Body and Bank Account

Need more flexibility in your career? Lindsay Junk, President of YogaSix, emphasizes the inclusivity and accessibility of yoga, highlighting its low-impact nature that allows individuals of any age and fitness level to participate. She shares a personal anecdote about attending a yoga class with their elderly mother and young twin daughters, showcasing the diversity of participants in a single class. Despite the varying fitness levels—ranging from the narrator in their forties to their active twins and their less physically fit mother—they all enjoyed a rewarding experience. Lindsay emphasizes the tagline "yoga for everyone" and illustrates how the class provided benefits for all, from a good workout to fun and relaxation.
