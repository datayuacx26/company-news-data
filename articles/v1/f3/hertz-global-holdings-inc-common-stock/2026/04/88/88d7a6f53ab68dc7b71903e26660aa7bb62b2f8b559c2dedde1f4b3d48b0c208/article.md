# Hertz Reveals 2026 U.S. Spring Travel Trends -

*Hertz data reveals longer spring break travel window and top destinations*


**** New Hertz travel insights reveal how spring break travel is shifting, with travelers embracing more flexible timing and longer windows for their getaways. Instead of a single peak spring break week, Hertz data shows several high-demand periods across both March and April, opening up more opportunities for families and groups to travel and save. Additionally, one in three Hertz customers is currently renting specifically for road trips and driving vacations, reflecting renewed interest in travel by car.


**Spring Break Spans Multiple Weeks***


Spring break is no longer limited to one defined week. Hertz data reveals that travelers are heading out throughout March and April, with top pick up days forecasted to be April 9, April 17, April 23 and April 24. In March, the most popular pick up days were March 6, March 13 and March 27. This staggered demand points to smoother travel experiences for families and groups hitting the road.

Sunshine, family‑friendly activities, and ease of planning keep warm‑weather destinations at the top of travelers’ lists. The top spring destinations based on rental car demand are:


- Orlando


- Los Angeles


- Phoenix

- Miami

- Dallas


**** **Book Early for the Best Choice**


Travelers typically reserve rental cars 16 days in advance for spring trips but booking the car at the same time as flights and hotels can improve availability and support cost savings. Early bookings also provide access to a wider selection of vehicles – particularly SUVs and vans, which are among the most booked options this spring as travelers prioritize space for kids, luggage, and gear.
