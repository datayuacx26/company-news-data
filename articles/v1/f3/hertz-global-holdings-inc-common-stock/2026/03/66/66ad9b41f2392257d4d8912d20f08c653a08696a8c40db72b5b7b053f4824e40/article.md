For travelers missing or delaying vacations because of


long TSA lines, Hertz has a solution to stay in control of travel plans: **get in the car and go** .


Whether you’re stuck at the airport or at home, Hertz is


offering up to[25% off](https://www.hertz.com/us/en/deals-and-offers/us-offers/save-up-to-25-now) last-minute reservations and one-way car rentals at thousands of airports and neighborhood locations across the country.


Here’s what Hertz is seeing right now:


- Over the last week, search traffic to Hertz.com jumped 15% — signaling that travelers may be considering other ways or back up plans to reach their destination.


- One in three customers are currently renting cars specifically for road trips and driving vacations.

3. **Booking at a neighborhood location.** To avoid airport disruptions, Hertz has thousands of convenient neighborhood locations across the country.


4. **Modifying your reservation on the Hertz app or Hertz.com** if your travel plans get disrupted and you need to make changes.


****


5. **Joining**[Hertz Gold+](https://www.hertz.com/us/en/gold-plus-rewards) **(it’s free!) to skip the counter and go straight to your car when you land** . This benefit can be a huge timesaver, especially after flight delays.
