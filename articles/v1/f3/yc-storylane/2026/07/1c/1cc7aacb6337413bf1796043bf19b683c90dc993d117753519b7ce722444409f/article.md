## What is Spara?


Spara is an AI GTM platform rated 4.8 on G2 with 12 reviews. It has three distinct products: AI Chat (engages website visitors, qualifies leads, books meetings in real time), AI Voice (handles phone calls, nurtures prospects, and backs up reps from first touch to close), and AI Email (handles inbound email replies, qualifies prospects, and books meetings without manual work). The platform connects to HubSpot and Salesforce, enriches lead records with conversation data, and sends real-time rep alerts when a qualified account is active.


Each of the three products operates as a separate channel rather than a unified agent. That architecture creates real flexibility for teams that want to cover email, chat, and voice independently. It also creates friction when buyers move across channels or when qualification context from an email thread needs to carry into a website chat session. G2 reviewers specifically flag that navigating multi-product conversations can be challenging.


Where Spara's three-channel approach has a consistent gap: none of the channels can show buyers an interactive product demo. Spara qualifies and routes, but it cannot walk a buyer through how the product actually works mid-conversation. For demand gen teams trying to close the gap between visitor intent and booked meeting without relying entirely on a human rep, that limitation matters.
