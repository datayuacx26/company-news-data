Most teams spend hours on research, writing first drafts, and routine writing tasks that pull them away from strategic work.

As the current industry leader in AI chatbots, this platform is surprisingly easy to use. ### Intercom


Intercom is an AI-powered customer messaging platform that combines your live chat, help desk, email, and marketing conversations in one place.


The Fin AI chatbot resolves common customer questions — such as password resets, billing inquiries, and basic how-tos — autonomously using your help center content as a reference. This frees up bandwidth for your support team to handle more complex issues with AI assistance for faster responses.


With this setup, one support person becomes as effective as two or three, which means you can delay hiring another team member until you genuinely need the capacity.


Note


In addition to a monthly fee per seat, Intercom charges a fee for every customer conversation resolved by its AI. If your support volume fluctuates significantly month to month, your bill may too.


**Best for:** SaaS companies, ecommerce brands, any business handling 50-400 support conversations a week


**When to use something else:** When you have fewer than 30 conversations a week (in which case a simple shared inbox works fine), or you need a traditional ticketing system for tracking complex support cases over long periods


**Pricing:** Starts at $29/seat/month, plus $0.99 per AI-resolved conversation


### Buffer


Social media takes time to do right, but it's also something you don't want to do mindlessly.


Buffer lets you schedule social posts across all platforms from one dashboard, while its analytics help you track what content works on each channel so you can refine your strategy over time. AI assists with caption writing and suggests optimal posting times based on when your audience is most active.


Spend 90 minutes once a week scheduling all your posts, and Buffer publishes them automatically on schedule. You save about 10 hours a month on social media, and your posts are more consistent — which builds trust with your audience and keeps you top of mind.


**Best for:** Managing three to six social media accounts, or aiming for consistency without spending all your time on social media


**When to use something else:** When social isn't a growth channel for your business, or you only post occasionally and don't need scheduling


**Pricing:** Free tier available for up to three channels, paid plans starting at $5/month per channel


### Zapier


Zapier connects over 8,000 apps to automate data flow and repetitive tasks across your business systems. The process is simple: Describe your workflow in plain language to Zapier, and the software will build your workflow and connect any necessary accounts automatically.


If you're not sure where to start, use one of the platform's many pre-built automation templates as a guide. Zapier doesn't require any coding knowledge, so you can easily eliminate repetitive tasks without technical expertise.


**Best for:** Teams using five or more tools that need to share data with each other


**When to use something else:** When you're only using one or two tools total so there's nothing to connect


**Pricing:** Free tier (with 100 tasks/month); paid plans start at $20/month


## How to choose the best AI tools for your small business


Some teams need a full stack of tools, while others may only need a few to solve their operational problems. Here's how to find out what you need for your business:


### Find the biggest bottleneck in your business


Ask yourself which tasks take up the most time or resources but don't move your business forward. These are typically activities like:


-


Manually updating website content for every product launch
-


Recreating ad variations for different platforms
-


Answering the same customer questions over and over


Identifying these tasks will help you determine your priority when choosing your first tool. With so many AI tools available, it's easy to get sidetracked by all the promises each one makes. Focus on your goal and resist the temptation to implement everything at one time.


Tip


Test one tool at a time, giving each one two to four weeks to see results. Track how many hours a week the task used to take, and how many it takes now with the new software. Assess each platform's impact afterward and decide whether to keep it or try something else.


### Check whether they play well with your current stack


When doing your research on different AI tools, check to see whether they integrate with the software you're already using.


A slightly less feature-rich tool that integrates well often delivers better results than a powerful tool that requires manual workarounds to connect with your existing systems. When data flows automatically between tools, it prevents the context switching that kills productivity and eliminates the data silos that lead to avoidable errors.
