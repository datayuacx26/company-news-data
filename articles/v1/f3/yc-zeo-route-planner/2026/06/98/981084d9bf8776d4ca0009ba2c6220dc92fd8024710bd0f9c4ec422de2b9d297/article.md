### 2. Routific


[Source](https://www.routific.com/blog/how-to-optimize-delivery-routes)


*Routific*


Some delivery teams don’t want complexity. They want a tool that can spin up a set of routes, keep drivers from zig-zagging, and send customers a heads-up without drowning in dashboards.


That’s where Routific often fits. It’s lightweight, easy to roll out, and small businesses use it because the learning curve is low.


The catch is consistency. Users have flagged notification gaps — particularly with SMS reminders not always hitting customers. For a team that relies on tight client communication, that’s a real problem.


**What it gives you**


- A driver app with simple navigation and stop lists.
- Customer ETAs are pushed automatically, so clients aren’t left guessing.
- Analytics on delivery performance and costs, though basic compared to heavier platforms.

### 4. WorkWave Route Manager


[Source](https://insights.workwave.com/industry/home-delivery/workwave-route-managers-new-look-comes-with-powerful-precision-and-control/)


*RouteManager*


WorkWave (often called Route Manager) is aimed at service-heavy fleets — think pest control, HVAC, repair crews, and delivery runs that mix scheduled jobs with ad-hoc tasks. It’s more than just routing: managers get scheduling, driver apps, reporting, and even scenario modeling.


The gap? GPS tracking isn’t included in the base plan. You pay extra for live vehicle visibility, which feels limiting when even smaller competitors bake it in. For a lean business, that’s a sticking point.


**What it gives you**


- Route planning built around time windows and capacity.
- A mobile app so drivers can update tasks, capture proof, or flag skips.
- Digital proof of delivery, including signatures and notes.
- Order import via bulk upload or APIs.


### 5. Onfleet


[Source](https://onfleet.com/fleet-management-software)


*Onfleet*


Onfleet is built for scale. Enterprises and scale-ups adopt it because it combines dispatch, routing, proof of delivery, and customer messaging into one polished platform. It’s the kind of system you use when missing an SLA isn’t an option.


But the price bar is high. The entry plan starts at hundreds per month, and more advanced analytics and capacity sit behind steeper tiers. Smaller operators find it hard to justify unless they’re already pushing thousands of drops.


**What it gives you**


- Real-time driver tracking with live route adherence.
- Automatic ETAs and SMS/email updates to customers.
- Proof of delivery with signatures, barcodes, photos, or notes.
- A driver app that handles navigation, stop orders, and task details.
