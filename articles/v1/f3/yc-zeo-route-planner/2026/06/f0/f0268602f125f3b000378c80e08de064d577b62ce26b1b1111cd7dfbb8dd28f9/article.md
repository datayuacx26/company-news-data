Below, you’ll find five top apps in 2026 that deliver on mileage tracking tools.

### TripLog


TripLog has tons of features as a mileage tracking platform, and that’s why both solo drivers and delivery fleets use it for flexibility (through mobile apps) and optional hardware.


*TripLog*


Operators running on the high-mileage routes can use TripLog to leverage multiple ways for capturing accurate trip data.


There is—


- **Automatic mileage tracking** through GPS, Bluetooth, or plug-in devices
- **Work-hour classification** to avoid mixing personal trips with business logs

- **TripLog Drive device** available to reduce GPS battery drain and missed trips

*MileIQ*


MileIQ is a well-known automatic mileage tracker built for individuals, which works great for small teams as well. It quietly runs in the background to detect drives and offer a swipe-based classification (business vs. personal).


It’s the simplicity of MileIQ that makes it a go-to for users who want mileage logged without intervention.


**These features stand out:**


- Automated logging of drives using GPS, motion detection, and location data without needing to start or stop a timer
- The use of “Frequent Drives” intelligence, wherein the app learns how you classify repeated trips and can auto-classify them in the future
- Exports IRS-compliant reports you can use for tax deductions or reimbursement


MileIQ showed near-perfect accuracy on a 207-mile trip with stable connectivity, but later tests revealed issues. It[failed to log a 209-mile trip started offline](https://timeero.com/reviews/mileiq-review?) and overestimated short trips by up to 60% when detecting incorrect start points. Reliable only with internet enabled at trip start.


### Hurdlr


*Hurdlr*


Hurdlr positions itself as an all-in-one financial app for mobile workers. Apart from mileage, it tracks expenses, income, and even provides real-time tax estimates. This makes Hurdlr a popular platform, which makes it amongst gig drivers, independent couriers, and small delivery businesses.


The app runs in the background and logs every mile automatically, without the need for manual entries or spreadsheets.


**You can expect Hurdlr to offer —**


- **Automatic GPS** that tracks trips and records without driver input
- **Mileage rules** let users pre-define business vs. personal drives for cleaner logs
- Scale in adoption, since Hurdlr reports 1M+ miles logged daily and over 1B miles tracked across its user base


**Test Results**


In[controlled testing by Timeero](https://timeero.com/reviews/hurdlr-mileage-tracke) , Hurdlr logged 5.17 miles versus a vehicle odometer’s 6.32 miles, underreporting short trips because it only tracks movement above 10mph.
