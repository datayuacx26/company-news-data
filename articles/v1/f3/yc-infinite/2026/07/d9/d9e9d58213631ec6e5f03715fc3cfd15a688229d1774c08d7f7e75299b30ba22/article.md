# Welcoming Amelia Arnold to Infinite

We're thrilled to welcome Amelia Arnold as our Founding Senior AML Analyst, helping to build and lead our AML operations and transaction monitoring efforts.


Formerly at OKX, Stripe, and Wells Fargo, Amelia brings extensive experience across financial crimes investigations, intelligence, sanctions, and AML operations. She combines investigative rigor with deep operational expertise across both traditional financial institutions and digital assets.


At Infinite, she'll help build the systems and processes that power onboarding, transaction monitoring, and scalable compliance infrastructure from day one.


Amelia is CAMS-certified and holds an M.A. in Financial Crimes Risk Management from the Middlebury Institute of International Studies (MIIS).


At Infinite, our platform is an embedded layer across our customers' products. Amelia's expertise reinforces that layer and accelerates our mission to build the best stablecoin experience for global commerce.


Excited to have you on board, Amelia!
