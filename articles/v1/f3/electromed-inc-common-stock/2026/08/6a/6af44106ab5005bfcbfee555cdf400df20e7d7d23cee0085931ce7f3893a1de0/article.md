### Electromed, Inc. to Report Q4 Fiscal 2026 Financial Results on August 25, 2026

**About Electromed, Inc.**


Electromed, Inc. manufactures, markets, and sells products that provide airway clearance therapy, including the SmartVest® Airway Clearance System, to patients with compromised pulmonary function. It is headquartered in New Prague, Minnesota, and was founded in 1992.
