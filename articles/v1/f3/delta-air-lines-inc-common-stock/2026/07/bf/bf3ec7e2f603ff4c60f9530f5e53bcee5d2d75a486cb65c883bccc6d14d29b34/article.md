# A purpose-driven partnership with R. Riveter

Delta is marking America’s 250th birthday with a meaningful collaboration with mission-driven bags and accessories brand R. Riveter. Now live in[Delta Shop](https://shop.delta.com/collections/delta-x-r-riveter/?utm_source=delta_news_hub&utm_campaign=delta_x_r_riveter&utm_content=shop) , the exclusive collection features six bags — all handmade by military spouses and rooted in R. Riveter’s mission to support military families.


Blending premium design with purpose-led impact, the collaboration reflects Delta’s longstanding commitment to the armed services and connects travelers not only to destinations, but to stories and values that resonate nationwide.


Customers can also find the collection featured in the Shop section of the Delta Sync Wi-Fi portal **,** where SkyMiles Members can log in to enjoy fast, free Wi-Fi — plus access to entertainment, exclusive offers and travel inspiration.
