# Delta Cargo launches new five-day flexible date search


With the new five-day flexible-date search capability in its direct cargo booking channel, Delta is giving customers greater flexibility to compare flight options, identify alternative booking opportunities, and plan shipments more efficiently—all without restarting their search.


Staff Writer


Jul 22, 2026 11:30am


Delta Cargo is making it even easier for customers to shop and book shipments online with the launch of a new five-day flexible-date search experience on deltacargo.com.


“At Delta Cargo, we’re continually investing in digital capabilities that make it easier for customers to do business with us,” said Sebastian Kunze - Director, Revenue Management. “Expanding search visibility from one day to five gives customers greater flexibility to compare flights, evaluate alternatives, and find real-time solutions that best meet their shipping needs, all within a single search.”


As one of the first U.S. passenger airlines to offer customers a new five-day flexible-date search capability in its direct cargo booking channel, Delta is giving customers greater flexibility to compare flight options, identify alternative booking opportunities, and plan shipments more efficiently—all without restarting their search.


“Customer feedback played an important role in shaping this enhancement,” said Troy Easterday - General Manager, Digital Transformation and Technology. “By bringing multi-day visibility to available shipping options on[deltacargo.com](http://deltacargo.com/) , we’re making it easier for customers to compare flights, plan shipments more efficiently, and book with greater confidence.”


The new capability allows customers to view up to five days of available flight options in a single search while continuing to access real-time schedules, capacity and pricing, along with applicable customer rate and allotment indicators.


The enhancement builds on Delta Cargo’s growing suite of digital tools available on deltacargo.com, powered by Revenue Technology Services (RTS) Velocity and Accelerate solutions, where customers can shop, book and manage shipments, schedule future bookings, and track shipments in real time. Customers can also continue booking through a range of preferred partner platforms, including cargo.one, CargoWise, and Freightos.


The introduction of flexible date search reflects Delta Cargo’s ongoing commitment to delivering innovative digital solutions that make it easier for customers to shop, book, and track shipments with confidence.
