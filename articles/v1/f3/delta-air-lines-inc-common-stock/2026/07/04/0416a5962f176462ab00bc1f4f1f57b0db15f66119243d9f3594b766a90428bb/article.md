# Go beyond the summer: Delta’s sun-drenched offers are back with bonus miles, travel deals and more


This summer, Delta is helping customers keep the adventure going with the return of Beyond the Summer, an exclusive event packed with flight deals, bonus miles and limited-time offers from Delta and its suite of premium travel partners.


Staff Writer


Jul 21, 2026 9:30am


Summer may be winding down, but the rewards are just heating up. Delta's annual[Beyond The Summer Event](http://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/promotions/beyond-the-summer) returns July 21, providing customers with exclusive flight deals and limited-time offers across Delta's growing portfolio of travel and lifestyle partners while eligible SkyMiles Members receive private access to double-mile earning opportunities.


SkyMiles Membership makes loyalty mean more – giving Members more ways to earn, redeem and enjoy unforgettable experiences, all with the signature service and care that put Delta in a league of its own. The value of SkyMiles extends well beyond the flight itself, with opportunities to earn and use miles across Award Travel, vacation packages, car rentals, hotels and partner brands throughout the year. Delta’s Beyond The Summer Event builds on that everyday value by bringing together a collection of enhanced offers across Delta and its partners, helping Members earn more, save more and get even more out of their Membership.


### **Earn double miles on qualifying Delta flights**


Eligible SkyMiles Members who enroll and book qualifying Delta Main Classic or Delta Main Extra itineraries by July 23, 2026, can earn double miles when they travel on eligible flights featured in the[Beyond The Summer Event](http://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/promotions/beyond-the-summer) .


Featured destinations include Riyadh, Seoul, Honolulu, Sydney, Auckland, Rome, Paris, Frankfurt, Hong Kong, Shanghai, Cancun, Punta Cana and many more.


This 72-hour promotion gives travelers another reason to book trips to destinations across Delta's global network.


### **Earn more miles and benefits on where you stay**


Whether you’re booking a complete vacation package, reserving a hotel room or planning a home rental getaway, SkyMiles Members can earn more rewards.


[Delta Vacations](https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-vacations/offers-deals/summer) : Save up to $275 on eligible flight + hotel packages* while earning miles and MQDs toward Medallion Status.


- With Delta Vacations, choose from a curated selection of hotels, rides and activities, paired with your Delta® in-flight experience, booked all in one place. Plus, Medallion Members can still enjoy Medallion benefits, including upgrade eligibility, priority airport services and more.


[Delta Stays](https://carsandstays.delta.com/lp/b/earn-extra-miles?MDPCID=Delta-US.DPS.Delta.MLP-DeltaNewsHub-BeyondTheSummer2026.AllLOB) : Earn 4X miles per dollar on eligible hotel bookings and enjoy VIP Access Benefits, including elevated touches like room upgrades and food and beverage credits at select properties.**


**Airbnb** : Need a larger space? When SkyMiles and Airbnb accounts are linked, Members continue to earn 1 mile per dollar on eligible Airbnb Homes bookings.***


In addition, Members can earn 3 miles per dollar and receive a one-time bonus of 2,000 miles after completing a qualifying Airbnb Experience or Service of at least $50 during the promotional period (bookings through Aug. 6, 2026, completed by Mar. 31, 2027).*** Delta and Airbnb[recently expanded their partnership](https://news.delta.com/where-you-go-what-you-do-more-ways-earn-miles-airbnb) , rewarding more of the moments that make trips unforgettable.


**Minimum spend requirements apply. Offer ends Aug. 6, 2026.*


***Offers end Aug. 6, 2026.*


****Excluding taxes, discounts, incentives and other fees, when booking within 10 days from activating the booking window.*


### **Delta Cars: Earn more miles on car rentals**


Whether planning a cross-country road trip or a weekend getaway, SkyMiles Members can earn miles on every booking with[Delta Cars](https://carsandstays.delta.com/lp/b/earn-extra-miles?MDPCID=Delta-US.DPS.Delta.MLP-DeltaNewsHub-BeyondTheSummer2026.AllLOB) . For a limited time, Members can earn elevated mileage rewards* on eligible rentals:


- Up to 16X miles per dollar with[Hertz](https://carsandstays.delta.com/lp/b/hertz-car-rentals?MDPCID=Delta-US.DPS.Delta.MLP-DeltaNewsHub-BeyondTheSummer2026-Hertz.CAR) , depending on Medallion Status


- 8X miles per dollar with Dollar and Thrifty


- 4X miles per dollar with participating partners including Alamo, Enterprise, National, Avis, Budget, Payless and SIXT


**Offers end Aug. 6, 2026.*


### **Delta Cruises: Set sail and earn up to 4X miles**


SkyMiles Members can make the most of their next voyage when they book* with[Delta Cruises](https://cruises.delta.com/) , including the ability to earn:


- 3X miles per eligible dollar on interior and oceanview staterooms


- 4X miles per eligible dollar on balcony and suite staterooms


**Offer is valid on new bookings made through Aug. 6, 2026. Eligible dollars include the base cruise rate charged at the time of booking, but excludes port charges, port expenses, travel protection, taxes, fees, gratuities, room service, and other incidental charges.*


### **Delta Gift Cards: Earn up to 5X miles**


Looking ahead to your next getaway or searching for the perfect gift?[Delta Gift Cards](https://www.delta.com/us/en/gift-cards/overview) offer a simple way to share future travel experiences while earning bonus miles. Give the gift of travel and earn bonus miles* on qualifying Delta Gift Card **purchases of $300 or more** :


- SkyMiles Members: 2X miles per dollar


- Silver Medallion Members: 3X miles per dollar


- Gold Medallion Members: 4X miles per dollar


- Platinum and Diamond Medallion Members: 5X miles per dollar


**Offer ends Aug. 6, 2026.*


### **SkyMiles Shopping: Shop and earn more – up to 2,000 bonus miles**


SkyMiles Members can earn up to 2,000 bonus miles when they shop through[SkyMiles Shopping](https://www.skymilesshopping.com/p/summer.htm?source=cl|Delta||cl||link||PR|Bns|20260720&utm_source=cl&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=PR&utm_content=Bns&chan=cl&seg=&med=link&strm=&cam=PR&cont=Bns&end=1) at more than 1,000 participating retailers. Whether checking off school supply lists, refreshing a wardrobe for an upcoming trip or stocking up on everyday essentials, Members can earn bonus miles on top of the standard miles offered by participating merchants (terms apply, offer ends Aug. 10, 2026).


### **Keep those summer-time vibes going**


Whether planning a fall getaway, booking a dream international trip, shopping for back-to-school essentials or earning rewards through Delta's travel partners, this year's[Beyond The Summer Event](http://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/promotions/beyond-the-summer) offers more opportunities to save, earn and explore.


Eligible SkyMiles Members can take advantage of these limited-time offers before they end, making it easier than ever to keep summer adventures going long after the season ends.


*Terms and conditions apply. Offers are available for a limited time and are subject to change. Customers should visit delta.com for complete offer details and eligibility requirements.*
