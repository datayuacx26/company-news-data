# Stadium cleaning services in Arizona: Turning the Arizona Diamondbacks into sustainability leaders

Since becoming the Arizona Diamondbacks' cleaning partner in January 2024,[ABM has transformed Chase Field operations](https://www.abm.com/industries/sports-entertainment) by elevating cleaning performance, strengthening event execution, and implementing[forward-thinking sustainability initiatives](https://www.abm.com/solutions/sets/sustainability) now considered next-level within Major League Baseball. What began as operational stabilization has evolved into a multi-year sustainability partnership focused on water conservation, waste diversion, reusable programs, and smart restroom technology.


- 200 events annually
- 198,000 gallons of water saved in one month
- Paper waste reduced to 8%
- 42,000+ single-use cups diverted from landfills

The management team is attentive, caring, and dedicated to providing a clean and safe environment for fans who visit Chase Field for Arizona Diamondbacks games, major concerts, and special events. We are especially grateful for ABM's efforts to continually improve the efficiency of the day-to-day operation." - Mike Rock, Vice President, Ballpark Operations


## Challenge


The Diamondbacks needed a partner with the expertise and staffing capacity to[deliver consistent cleaning processes](https://www.abm.com/solutions/service-family/cleaning-maintenance) , including detail work, post-event cleaning, and pressure washing. The leadership team sought out a partner they could trust to meet their high standards, so they could focus on strategic work instead of operational oversight.


The Diamondbacks also wanted to pursue forward-looking environmental programs aligned with MLB's sustainability focus and Arizona's desert conservation needs. Achieving these goals required a strategic, innovative partner.


## Solution


ABM implemented a comprehensive event-based janitorial strategy that addressed immediate operational needs while creating capacity for sustainability innovation.


ABM assumed full oversight responsibility, relieving client leadership of post-event management duties. The team established structured pre-event, event-time, and post-event cleaning processes with proper bowl-washing protocols. ABM also developed a scalable staffing model leveraging regional labor partnerships across three Phoenix venues.


### Multi-year sustainability initiatives


With operational stability established, ABM partnered with the Diamondbacks to expand sustainability programs:


- **Year 1:** The Diamondbacks were the first MLB team to launch a reusable cups program. In partnership with the Diamondbacks and its food and beverage partner, ABM began with a pilot in the All You Can Eat Suites and Cantina Bar with the goal of expanding across the entire suite level.
- **Year 2:** ABM implemented a water conservation initiative using a data-driven bowl washing strategy. The program relies on heat mapping and third-inning visual verification for targeted cleaning in high-use zones.
- **Year 2:** The team also launched a smart restroom technology program using the KOLO coreless paper system that ensures full roll depletion before replacement. ABM staff rely on automated alerts to keep restrooms properly stocked.
- **Year 3:** ABM worked with the Diamondbacks and its food and beverage partner to expand the recycling and waste diversion program by transitioning to grab-and-go aluminum beverages to increase recyclable volume. There is now a dedicated recycling pick in the seating bowl. Data is tracked through MLB's sustainability software platform. Looking ahead, ABM will begin sorting waste in the stadium.


## Benefits


ABM's stable staffing model and improved post-event process execution have eliminated the operational oversight burden on Diamondbacks leadership. The ability to successfully manage 200+ events annually with 150-170 team members per event demonstrates scalable capacity.


The Diamondbacks are assigning ABM project work that their in-house team would typically handle, with potential future opportunity for expanding scope.


Sustainability programs have delivered meaningful results:


- The water conservation program delivers significant impact in Arizona's desert climate. In the first month alone, the program resulted in 198,000 gallons of water saved and an approximately $2,500 reduction in costs.
- Smart restroom technology reduced paper waste to 8% (below the typical average of 15-20%).
- In two seasons, the reusable cup program has diverted over 42,000 single-use cups equaling 3,430 lbs. of waste from landfills. For the 2025 season, the return rate reached 82%.
