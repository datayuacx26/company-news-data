# Capitol Federal Financial, Inc. Announces Quarterly Dividend

TOPEKA, Kan.


, July 20, 2012


/PRNewswire/ -- Capitol Federal Financial, Inc. (Nasdaq:CFFN ) (the "Company") announced today that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.075


per share on outstanding CFFN common stock.


The dividend is payable on August 17, 2012


to stockholders of record as of the close of business on August 3, 2012


.


CFFN will announce financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2012


on July 30


, 2012. The Company's Form 10-Q will be filed on or about August 3, 2012


.


Capitol Federal Financial, Inc. is the holding company for Capitol Federal Savings Bank, which operates 45 branch offices in Kansas


and Missouri. News and other information about the Company can be found on the Bank's website,[www.capfed.com](http://www.capfed.com/) .
