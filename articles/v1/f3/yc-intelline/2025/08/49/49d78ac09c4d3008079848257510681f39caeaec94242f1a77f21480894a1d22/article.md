# Intelline Works at Moparfest 2025

We visited Moparfest 2025 with our very own Emi the Hemi Challenger! A beautiful weekend in New Hamburg, we made the last minute decision to join the event as a vendor for the first time. It was quite a rush to get everything ready but we managed to get Emi polished up, finished the temporary N/A cold air intake, and also finished a 3D printed display model of our Juggernaut intake.


It was a fantastic two days seeing so many rare classic Mopars, as well as new ones with incredible customizations. We had a chance to chat with some serious racers about our intake and get some feedback - we're really happy to see customers getting just as excited as we are about the advantages of our design, and the very positive reception to how it looks so far. The coming weeks will be very exciting as we do some final N/A physical testing and simulation work, the move on to machining the first few units.


Emi is Kyle's 2012 Dodge Challenger R/T, bought in California in 2019, road tripped all the way back to Ontario (where she was originally assembled!). We'll do a separate post on Emi's mods so far, as she is often both the inspiration and test platform for the custom parts we develop.


Thank you to everyone who joined us for the weekend and stopped to chat with us. We'll be back again next year!
