# Intelline Cars & Coffee #2

Thank you to everyone who came out to our second Cars, Bikes, & coffee event! After gloomy rain all day, things brightened up and it turned out to be a great evening. We enjoyed chatting with everyone and seeing a nice mix of cars and bikes.


A beautiful Chrysler crossfire, Cummins swapped Jeep TJ, and a nice range of sport, touring, and adventure bikes made for some wonderful conversation.


Arun won our shift knob raffle this time around, and we're looking forward to giving away another at our next meet! Don't miss out!
