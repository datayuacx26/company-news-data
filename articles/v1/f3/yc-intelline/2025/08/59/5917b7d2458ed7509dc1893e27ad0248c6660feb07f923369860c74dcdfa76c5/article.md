# Intelline Cars & Coffee #3

NEXT ONE IS SEPT 30TH


The last Tuesday evening of every month, join us for some beautiful cars, bikes, and free coffee. We'll run a raffle every event, as well as open our doors for everyone to hang out in our shop and see the latest projects we're working on.


Make sure to register to be entered in the raffle. This time we'll be giving away another of our custom 5-axis machined shift knobs! https://forms.gle/72QV1S5jazC2tdQUA


Where: 614 Colby Drive, Units 7&8, Waterloo, ON, N2V1A2


When: 7pm-11pm


All cars and bikes welcome. Limited lot space so please register if you plan to show your vehicle. Plenty of street parking in a quiet industrial park for overflow. Respect the lot and our neighbors. We're here to enjoy and appreciate beautiful cars and bikes of all kinds, not to be a nuisance to others. Let's build a great regular event together.
