We’re proud to share some big news:
**Boostly has officially been named one of the fastest-growing private companies in the U.S. by Inc. Magazine.**


We ranked:


-


**#553 overall in the U.S.**


-


**#43 in Marketing & Advertising**


-


**#14 in Utah**


This is our first time on the **Inc. 5000 list** , and to say we’re honored would be an understatement. It’s a huge milestone—and a reflection of the incredible restaurants, partners, and team members who’ve helped us grow.


## **From Pizza Shops to Powerhouses**


Boostly started with a simple idea: restaurants deserve better marketing tools.


No more clunky platforms, no more one-size-fits-all software, and no more guessing if your message actually got delivered.


We’ve spent the last few years building a **SMS marketing platform made specifically for restaurants** , one that helps turn guests into regulars and data into dollars. Along the way, we’ve added new tools like AI-Powered SEO, Reviews Collection and Response, and Social Media Management to help restaurants own their guest relationships from first visit to repeat order.
