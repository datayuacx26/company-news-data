# 3 Tips to Drive More Orders and Revenue at Your Restaurant This Month

Standing out and maximizing revenue in today's competitive restaurant landscape requires more than just good food—it takes smart strategies and effective marketing. If you’re looking for ways to drive more sales and increase customer loyalty, you won’t want to miss our latest webinar, *3 Tips to Drive More Orders and Revenue at Your Restaurant This Month.* This insightful session dives deep into actionable tactics that can transform your restaurant’s performance, including how to leverage customer data, harness the power of SMS marketing, and optimize your menu for higher profits.


One of the key highlights of the webinar is how to use customer data to build stronger relationships and generate repeat business. You’ll learn how collecting phone numbers and email addresses can enable you to send targeted promotions that drive immediate orders, with a special focus on the power of SMS marketing. As Shane Murphy from Boostly explains, SMS campaigns have an almost immediate impact, turning casual customers into loyal regulars. You’ll also discover how to automate email marketing for longer-term engagement, ensuring you stay connected with your customers.


The webinar also covers the importance of menu engineering—structuring your menu to highlight high-margin items and encourage upsells. Shandy Lindley from ChowNow breaks down simple yet effective ways to increase your average order value, from bundling items to making add-ons easy for customers to select online. If you’re ready to take your restaurant to the next level, this webinar provides the full breakdown on these strategies and more. Watch the session for yourself to get the insights you need to boost your revenue and stay ahead of the competition!


###
Key Takeaways


-


**Own Your Customer Data** : Restaurants should focus on owning customer data to increase repeat business and maintain direct relationships with customers.


-


**Leverage SMS and Email Marketing** : Using targeted, behavioral-based SMS and email campaigns helps drive customer engagement and increase order volume.


-


**Optimize Your Online Presence** : Ensure consistency and accuracy across all online platforms, and actively manage your Google, Yelp, and TripAdvisor listings.


-


**Menu Engineering for Profit** : Structure menus to promote high-margin items, and use upselling and bundling techniques to increase average order value.


-


**Respond Thoughtfully to Reviews** : Handling negative feedback with a professional, solution-oriented approach can improve customer relations and protect your restaurant’s reputation.
