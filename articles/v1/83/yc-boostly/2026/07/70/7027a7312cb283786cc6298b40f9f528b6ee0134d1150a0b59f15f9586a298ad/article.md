# Why SMS Loyalty Programs Work for Restaurants (And Outperform Custom Apps)

# **Why SMS Loyalty Programs Work for Restaurants (And Outperform Custom Apps)**


**If you run a restaurant, you already know this: repeat customers are your lifeblood.** They spend more, come back more often, and are more likely to bring their friends. That’s why loyalty programs aren’t just a nice-to-have—they’re a growth tool. But not all loyalty programs are created equal.


Custom apps? Punch cards? Most of them fail to gain traction.


**Enter SMS loyalty programs.** They’re simple, effective, and way more powerful than most restaurant owners realize.


### **Why Loyalty Programs Work for Restaurants**


Loyalty programs are proven to:


-


**Increase visit frequency** by rewarding return customers.


-


**Boost average order value** by offering tiered incentives.


-


**Give you customer data** to personalize promotions.


-


**Build emotional loyalty** with exclusive deals and VIP status.


In short, they help you turn casual diners into regulars.


### **Why Most Loyalty Programs Fall Flat**


The concept of rewarding loyalty is solid. The execution, however, is often where things go wrong.


-


**Custom apps** are expensive to build, require constant updates, and rely on customers downloading, logging in, and engaging regularly. That’s a big ask.


-


**Punch cards** are easy to fake, easy to lose, and impossible to track.


Unless you’re Starbucks or Chick-fil-A, most customers won’t download your app—and even fewer will keep using it.


### **Why SMS Loyalty Programs Are a Game-Changer**


With SMS, customers simply **text a keyword** to join your program. From there, they receive automated messages with offers, reminders, and rewards.


#### **Here’s why SMS outperforms apps:**


1.


**No app to download** Everyone knows how to text. No logins. No tech learning curve.


2.


**98% open rates** Most SMS messages are read within 3 minutes. You’re not competing with email clutter or muted push notifications.


3.


**Instant enrollment and redemption** Join by texting a keyword. Redeem by clicking a link or showing a code. No lost cards or forgotten logins.


4.


**Automated engagement** Send “You’re one visit away from a free item!” or “Come back this week for double points!” without lifting a finger.


5.


**Lower cost, better results** No app dev budget needed. Just a cost-effective channel that performs.


### **How to Launch a Loyalty Program with SMS (Today)**


It’s easier than you think:


-


Send a one-time opt-in text, provide QR codes that create pre-populated text keywords for opt-ins and make the QR code visible


-


Offer a reward for joining (like 20% off or a free drink).


-


Use automated follow-ups to bring people back.
