# Ophelia is now in-network with MetroPlus Health!

Substance use disorders affect millions of Americans, with opioid use disorder (OUD) being one of the most dangerous. Fortunately, medications for addiction treatment (MAT) have shown significant success in helping individuals recover.[Suboxone®](https://ophelia.com/suboxone) is a leading medication used in this approach and is often considered the gold standard of care for OUD treatment ([important safety information](https://ophelia.com/suboxone-safety) ). If you're a MetroPlusHealth member in New York, here's how you can access Suboxone and start treatment with Ophelia.


## Why Choose MetroPlusHealth for MAT?


MetroPlusHealth stands out for its comprehensive coverage and support for individuals struggling with opioid use disorder. With a focus on evidence-based treatment options like MAT and Suboxone, MetroPlusHealth ensures that members receive the care they need to recover and lead healthier lives.


Moreover, MetroPlusHealth partners with providers and organizations that specialize in substance use disorder treatment, offering members access to quality care and personalized support throughout their treatment. MetroPlusHealth's partnership with Ophelia helps expand coverage further with a virtual treatment option through telehealth, allowing members to receive care from the comfort and privacy of their homes.


## Understanding your MetroPlusHealth insurance coverage for Suboxone + MAT


Use the following steps to navigate your coverage effectively:


- **Review insurance policy and covered medications:** Take the time to review your MetroPlusHealth insurance policy documents and formulary. Look for sections that outline coverage for addiction treatment, prescription medications, and the specific medications used in MAT, such as Suboxone.
- **Consult with Ophelia:** We can provide additional guidance and support in understanding your insurance coverage with MetroPlusHealth. Our care coordinators can help you navigate the insurance process, answer questions specific to your treatment plan, and assist with any financial concerns. Text or call us today at (215) 585-2144.
- **Contact With MetroPlusHealth:** Reach out to MetroPlusHealth directly to inquire about your coverage for Suboxone and medications for addiction treatment (MAT).


## Using MetroPlusHealth insurance with Ophelia for effective treatment


Recovering from opioid use disorder is a challenging experience. With MetroPlusHealth's coverage for MAT, Suboxone, and telehealth, you have access to effective treatments that can help you reclaim your life.
