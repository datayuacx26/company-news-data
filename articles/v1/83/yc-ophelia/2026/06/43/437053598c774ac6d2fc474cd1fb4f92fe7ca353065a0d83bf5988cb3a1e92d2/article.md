# If your SNAP benefits have been interrupted, you’re not alone

**Here’s are a few helpful resources.**


We know how stressful it can feel when food or benefit programs are disrupted, especially when you’re focused on your health and treatment. We’ve gathered a list of trusted resources to help you access food, financial, and community support.


*As new centers open or expand their services, we’ll make sure they’re included here.*
