👋 Hey There! Today, we close out our summer intern experience mini-series with part III, ft. Ethan, our Product Management intern, and Ayomikun (Daniel) Adegoke, our Mobile Engineering intern 🌟


*📖 ICYMI* 👉 *Check out*[part I](https://medium.com/ww-tech-blog/impact-community-summer-fun-the-ww-weightwatchers-internship-experience-e015ac1a75c5) ****** *&*[part II](https://medium.com/ww-tech-blog/the-ww-weightwatchers-internship-experience-part-ii-5c38e4c4b413)


### First up, let’s hear from Ethan:


> “This past summer, I worked as a Product Management intern at WeightWatchers. As part of the member education team, my project focused on determining the efficacy of various in-app messaging patterns, more specifically with educating users on how to successfully interact with WW’s application. I was pleasantly surprised that, at such a large company, I was able to do work that would be seen by real WeightWatchers members and would directly impact WW’s application.
>
>
> The organized structure of this internship led directly to its impact and success. I was paired with an amazing manager who included me in important meetings, taught me about the product management role during weekly educational sessions, and answered any questions that I had. In addition, I learned a lot from different speaker sessions from high-level employees who were focused on educating the interns on various aspects of the company. Finally, all of WW’s employees were very approachable and welcoming. I connected with, networked, and learned from team members and fellow intern peers.
>
>
> I have never learned as much as I have in such a short period of time as I had during this internship. In just 11 weeks, I enhanced my product management expertise and learned to effectively roadmap a product, lead a team of designers, engineers, and analysts, and use software such as Jira and Figma. I also improved my decision-making skills and learned how to leverage research and data to make informed decisions and successfully prioritize the decisions I make. Finally, I further developed my communication skills, learned how to listen and adapt to feedback, and experienced what it takes to cultivate a collaborative team environment. I truly valued my internship experience at WeightWatchers, and I know that the skills I learned throughout the program will help me throughout my life, and my career.


**-Ethan Sims, Product Management Intern**


### Thanks for sharing, Ethan! Up next, we’ll hear from Ayomikun, our Mobile Engineering intern:


> “This summer, I worked as a Mobile (Android) Engineering Intern on the growth team. My tasks throughout the program focused on projects that laddered up to the organization’s broader effort to modernize its codebase.
>
>
> From the first day of my internship, my manager made me feel so comfortable and an important part of the team. I was provided with all of the resources I needed to succeed and most importantly, the team was always ready and willing to help troubleshoot any challenges I faced.
>
>
> In my third week of WW’s internship program, I had already begun contributing to the codebase which was a **very exciting moment** for me. The work culture at WeightWatchers is something I have never experienced before. Everyone wants you to succeed.”
