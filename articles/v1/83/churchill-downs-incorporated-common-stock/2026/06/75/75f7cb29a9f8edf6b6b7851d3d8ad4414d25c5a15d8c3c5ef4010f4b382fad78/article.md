## What Time Is the Stephen Foster Stakes?


The Grade I[Stephen Foster Stakes](https://www.churchilldowns.com/visit/spring-meet/stephen-foster-day/) takes place Saturday, June 27, at Churchill Downs and serves as the centerpiece of a blockbuster day of racing beneath the Twin Spires.


Race day begins at 12:45 p.m. ET with a 12-race card featuring seven stakes races worth more than $4 million.


The Stephen Foster Stakes is scheduled as Race 11 with an approximate post time of **6:14 p.m. ET** .


This year's race carries a **$2 million purse** and offers the winner an automatic berth in the Breeders' Cup Classic through the Breeders' Cup Challenge Series 'Win and You're In' program.
