# The Art of Balancing Tech and Personal Touch

Where technology and human connection intersect in the dynamic customer support (CS) world, creating a seamless customer experience is both a challenge and an opportunity. Recently, I had the opportunity to speak with Tanner Nelson, a visionary in CS who’s helmed CX teams at leading companies like Netflix and Logitech. Our conversation touched upon various aspects of CX — especially integrating technology — the irreplaceable value of human empathy, and the industry’s future in an increasingly automated world. Here are the top three insights from Tanner's journey that offer helpful insights into the evolving customer support landscape.


### **Expertise and Empathy: The Agent's Role**


Tanner advocates for a CS philosophy that harmonizes technological efficiency with human connection’s warmth. In today's tech-savvy era, while automation and AI are transforming how customer inquiries are handled, Tanner emphasizes the indispensable role of human empathy. He believes in leveraging technology to eliminate mundane tasks, thereby empowering CS agents to engage in more meaningful, problem-solving interactions. Tanner also highlighted the importance of agents being users and experts of the products they support. This firsthand experience fosters authenticity and a deeper understanding, enabling agents to relate to customers and provide superior support.


As automation handles routine inquiries, the value agents bring through their unique human experiences and problem-solving abilities becomes even more critical. This approach not only enhances the customer experience but also enriches agents’ job satisfaction of agents, making CS a fulfilling career.


### **Preserving the Human Touch**


Tanner shared insights into finding the perfect balance between automation and human interaction — especially by acknowledging the efficiencies brought by AI and automation in handling routine inquiries. However, Tanner also stressed the importance of preserving the human element in customer support. He envisions a customer support ecosystem where technology streamlines processes and provides instant solutions to common problems, while human agents focus on delivering personalized, empathetic support for more complex issues. This balanced approach ensures that customers receive timely, efficient service without losing the personal touch that fosters loyalty and satisfaction.


Tanner also stressed the significance of ongoing development, coaching, and mentorship within customer support teams. Leaders can cultivate a fulfilling work environment by focusing on intrinsic motivation and connecting agents to their work's purpose. This approach not only enhances job satisfaction but also drives innovation and excellence in customer service


### **Blending Technology and Personal Insights**


Looking forward, Tanner views the customer support landscape with technology and human insight that harmoniously coexist. He advocates for a "one-stop shop" approach, where customers can address all their needs in a single interaction — enhancing efficiency and satisfaction. This ideal scenario requires sophisticated data integration and a deep understanding of customer journeys, underscoring the importance of continuous learning and adaptation in the CX domain.


As we navigate the intersection of technology and human connection, the concepts shared by Tanner offer valuable guidance for creating customer experiences that are not only efficient but also genuinely caring and personalized. The path forward is clear: embrace technology, cherish human connection, as well as innovate in ways that enrich both the customer experience and the work lives of those who serve them.
