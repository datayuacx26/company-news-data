# What Is PO Number in an Invoice?

A **PO number (Purchase Order number)** is a unique reference number linked to a **Purchase Order** issued by a buyer before placing an order with a supplier.


When the supplier creates an invoice, they usually mention the **PO number** so the buyer can match the invoice with the original order.


### Example


Here's a simple purchase flow:


1. **Buyer issues a Purchase Order**


- PO Number: **PO-2026-001**
- Product: 100 office chairs


2. **Supplier delivers the order**
3. **Supplier sends an invoice**


- Invoice Number: **INV-2026-015**

## Why is a PO Number Important?


Purchase Order Process


Adding the PO number to an invoice helps businesses:


- Match invoices with purchase orders
- Verify that the correct goods or services were delivered
- Speed up invoice approval and payment
- Reduce billing disputes
- Maintain accurate accounting records


Many medium and large businesses require suppliers to include the PO number on every invoice before processing payment.


## Do Small business need PO Number on an Invoice?


You don't need to mention a PO number on every invoice.


Include it when your customer has issued a **Purchase Order (PO)** before you supply goods or services. Adding the PO number helps the customer match your invoice with their approved purchase order, making verification and payment much faster.


A **PO number** is commonly required when you're billing:


- Large companies
- Government departments
- Manufacturing businesses
- Corporate clients
- Businesses with procurement teams


If your customer doesn't issue purchase orders, you can usually leave the PO Number field blank.


## Is a PO Number Mandatory on an Invoice?


Not always.


In India, a **PO number is generally not mandatory under GST** for a standard tax invoice. However, many businesses include it because it simplifies purchase tracking and internal approvals.

## What Happens If PO Number is Missing from Invoice?


If your customer needs a PO number but it's missing from the invoice, then your customer can:


- Reject the invoice
- Ask you to issue a revised invoice
- Delay payment until the **correct PO number** is added
- Take longer to process the invoice internally


So, always ask your customer if they have a purchase order before raising the invoice.

# FAQ for PO number in Invoice


## **1. What is a PO number in an invoice?**


A **PO (Purchase Order) number** is a unique reference number assigned to a purchase order created by the buyer. Suppliers mention this number on the invoice so buyers can match the invoice with the original order before making payment.


## **2. Is a PO number the same as an invoice number?**


No. A **PO number** is generated by the buyer before purchasing goods or services, while an **invoice number** is generated by the seller when requesting payment. They serve different purposes.


## **3. Is a PO number mandatory on an invoice in India?**


For most GST invoices, **a PO number is not mandatory** . However, many businesses, government organizations, and large enterprises require suppliers to mention the PO number for internal approval and payment processing.


## **4. Where can I find the PO number on an invoice?**


The PO number is usually displayed near the top of the invoice alongside the invoice number, invoice date, customer details, and payment terms. Its exact placement may vary depending on the invoice format.


## **5. Why do businesses include a PO number on invoices?**


Businesses include a PO number to:


- Match invoices with purchase orders
- Verify ordered items
- Reduce billing disputes
- Speed up payment approvals
- Improve accounting records


## **6. Can one purchase order have multiple invoices?**


Yes. A single purchase order can be linked to multiple invoices if goods or services are delivered in parts or billed over multiple stages.


## **7. How do I add a PO number to an invoice?**


Most billing and accounting software lets you enter the PO number while creating an invoice. Simply enter the customer's purchase order number in the designated **PO Number** field before saving or sharing the invoice.


## **8. What is an example of a PO number?**


A PO number can follow any format defined by the buyer, such as:


- PO-2026-001
- PUR-45892
- ABC/PO/1025


The format varies from one organization to another.


## **9. What happens if I don't mention the PO number on an invoice?**


If your customer requires a PO number, the invoice may be rejected or payment could be delayed until the correct purchase order reference is added.


## **10. Who generates the PO number?**


The **buyer** creates the purchase order and assigns the PO number. The **seller** references that same PO number while issuing the invoice.
