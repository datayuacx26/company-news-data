## **What Is a Digital Signature on an Invoice?**


A digital signature is an electronic signature that is added to an invoice to verify the identity of the issuer and confirm that the document has not been altered after signing.


Unlike a scanned image of a handwritten signature, a digital signature uses cryptographic technology to improve authenticity and document security.


Businesses commonly use digital signatures on:


- GST invoices
- Sales invoices
- Tax invoices
- MCA forms
- e-tender
- Government contracts
- Proforma invoices
- Credit notes
- Debit notes
- e-Invoices


## **Why Should Businesses Use a Digital Signature?**


Adding a digital signature offers several benefits:


- Makes invoices look professional

- Improves document security
- Speeds up invoice sharing
- Supports paperless business operations
- Helps maintain a consistent invoicing process


## **Is a Digital Signature Mandatory on GST Invoices?**


For many businesses issuing regular GST invoices, a digital signature is **not always mandatory** under GST law, provided the invoice meets the prescribed requirements. However, businesses often use digital signatures to increase authenticity, streamline workflows, and support electronic document management for legal purposes.


> **Tip:** Always check the latest GST rules or consult your tax advisor, or the digital signature tool provider if you're unsure about the signing requirements for your business.
