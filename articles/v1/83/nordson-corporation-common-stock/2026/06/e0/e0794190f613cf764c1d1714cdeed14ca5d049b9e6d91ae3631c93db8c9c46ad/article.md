### Nordson Corporation Declares Third Quarter Dividend for Fiscal Year 2026


June 4, 2026


WESTLAKE, Ohio--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Nordson Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.nordson.com%2F&esheet=54547096&newsitemid=20260603810627&lan=en-US&anchor=Nordson+Corporation&index=1&md5=54573e8a2fb6b1ef8870cb3561402386) (Nasdaq: NDSN) today announced that its Board of Directors declared a fiscal year 2026 third quarter cash dividend in the amount of $0.82 per common share, payable on July 6, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on June 18, 2026.


This amount equals the $0.82 per common share dividend paid in the second quarter of fiscal year 2026.


Nordson Corporation is an innovative precision technology company that leverages a scalable growth framework through an entrepreneurial, division-led organization to deliver top tier growth with leading margins and returns. The Company’s direct sales model and applications expertise serves global customers through a wide variety of critical applications. Its diverse end market exposure includes consumer non-durable, medical, electronics and industrial end markets. Founded in 1954 and headquartered in Westlake, Ohio, the Company has operations and support offices in over 35 countries.
