# The 20 Most Expensive Prismatic Evolutions Cards that Everyone Wants!


The chase cards everyone is hunting right now.


By Leah C. | Published Feb 12, 2025 | 6 min read


The Pokémon Trading Card Game has once again captured the attention of collectors and players with its latest expansion, Prismatic Evolutions, released on January 17, 2025. Building upon the success of the 2021 Evolving Skies set, which introduced the coveted["Moonbreon" card](https://www.misprint.com/card/5710675) , Prismatic Evolutions revisits the beloved Eeveelutions theme.


Among the 20 most expensive cards from this set, 14 of them are absolutely gorgeous Special Illustration Rares. This expansion also introduces Poké Ball and Master Ball parallel cards which[sparkle with personality.](https://dz3we2x72f7ol.cloudfront.net/expansions/prismatic-evolutions/en-us/SV8pt5_EN_MPH_59.mp4)


In this article, we'll explore the top 20 most valuable cards from the Prismatic Evolutions set and discuss factors that contribute to their high market value, and I have listed the best places to buy and sell these new additions to the Pokémon TCG!
