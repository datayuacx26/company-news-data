# International Women’s Day chats on sustainability, solar power and supporting women to succeed

The buzzworthy news is out –[Lindsey Roon Wiedmann](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lindseyroon/) , is lighting up the airwaves on "Seize the Yay" podcast with the delightful Melbourne (AU) based podcaster[Sarah Davidson](https://linkedin.com/in/sarah-davidson-8276899a/) .


Lindsey shares her fascinating and unpredictable way to celebrate her “yay” in life, from a Bachelor of Science to a postgrad in Law to project finance at a law firm before her current role at Maxeon Solar Technologies. She also has fascinating insights on life as a woman in the traditionally male dominated energy sector.
