# From Brothers to Founders: The Journey Behind Raven

About the authors


Building on their experience developing scalable systems for leading startups like Apna and OkCredit, **Priyansh & Sriyansh** became YC-backed founders (S22). Growing up in Surat, near the industrial hub of Hazira where giants like Reliance, ONGC, KRIBHCO, Shell, L&T, GAIL, and GSPC operate, they absorbed the language of manufacturing from an early age. Raven’s mission is to bridge the gap between advanced technology and practical plant operations needs.
