# The engineering paradox: increase speed and quality

It is the daily reality for every engineer: pressure on the design phase is increasing, while projects are becoming more complex and margins are razor-thin. However, in the race to meet tight deadlines, a dangerous paradox often arises, where speed becomes the enemy of quality.
