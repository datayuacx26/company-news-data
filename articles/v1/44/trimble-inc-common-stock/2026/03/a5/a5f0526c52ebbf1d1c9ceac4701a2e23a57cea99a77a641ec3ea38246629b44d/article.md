# Las Vegas: Winning with certainty

Precision in Las Vegas is usually associated with the roll of the dice or the timing of a fountain show. But for Brad Keselowski and the No. 6 RFK Racing Ford Mustang, it’s found in the split-second data that drives success on the track. At the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, performance is a product of digital certainty — as is the case across the entire Mojave region. From the asphalt of the Strip to the critical water systems beneath the desert floor, Trimble is the common thread ensuring Nevada never has to gamble on its future.


### **Engineering the perfect lap**


When the eyes of the world turned to Las Vegas for the Grand Prix, the track surface had to be perfect. Local contractors used Trimble technology to turn the public streets of the Strip into a world-class racing circuit. By using high-accuracy paving and 3D scanning, engineers met the extreme tolerances required for cars exceeding 200 mph. That same level of precision now serves every driver in the city, resulting in a smooth surface that withstands the desert heat and the millions of vehicles that cruise these iconic streets each year.


### **Securing the desert’s lifeblood**


In the heart of the Mojave, water is a lifeline for 2.3 million people. Managing a limited resource in one of the driest climates on Earth leaves no room for error. By using Trimble connected technology to sync real-time data,[Southern Nevada’s water authorities](https://assetlifecycle.trimble.com/resources/en-US/video/southern-nevada-water-authority-las-vegas-valley-water-district-improved-capital-planning-through-connected-data) can manage the massive infrastructure hidden beneath the desert floor with total certainty. This digital transformation ensures that as the city scales up, water continues to flow to every home, resort and community pool.


### **Powering a reliable supply chain**


Nevada’s communities depend on a supply chain that crosses some of the most unforgiving terrain in the country. To keep the region moving, the journey of every essential resource — from the fuel at the pump to the goods on the shelf — is optimized through digital coordination. By replacing manual logs with[Trimble real-time dispatch software](https://transportation.trimble.com/en/transportation-management/case-studies/flyers-energy) , providers ensure that energy and supplies reach retail centers on time. This keeps the desert economy firing on all cylinders, ensuring the lights stay on and the stores stay stocked for every family across the Mojave.


## **Data-driven results for the win**


From the high-stakes precision of the Las Vegas Strip to the essential systems that keep the desert thriving, the common thread is clear — when the margin for error is zero, the world turns to data. By connecting every phase of a project, from the initial 3D scan to the final delivery, Trimble enables teams on and off the track to race forward with Confidence at every turn™.
