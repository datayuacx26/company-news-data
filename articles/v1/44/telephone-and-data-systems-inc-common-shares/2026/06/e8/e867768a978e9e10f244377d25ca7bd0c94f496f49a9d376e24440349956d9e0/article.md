# Reset, recharge, repeat: wellness tips for every day

**Your Guide to Year-Round Wellness at TDS**


Summer is in full swing, and we’re already over halfway through the year, so we have some great ideas for how to reset your routine. Here are a few simple tips to help you stay healthy, balanced, and feeling your best year-round.


1. **Break through the barriers**
Many adults find reasons to skip a workout—but common barriers can be overcome with small adjustments. If time is tight, schedule activity into your day, wake up earlier, or take a walk at lunch. Feeling tired? Start with a 10-minute brisk walk—it can boost energy and often leads to more movement.


2. **Strengthen your mental focus**
Constant noise can make it hard to tune in. Practice mindfulness by focusing on the present moment, limit distractions, and check in with yourself regularly. Simple habits like journaling or carving out uninterrupted time can help you stay grounded and clear-headed.


3. **Snack smart**
Simple, energizing foods keep you fueled on the go. Wraps with lean protein or plant-based fillings, fresh veggies, and a handful of nuts make easy, healthy options. Add fresh fruit for a light, sweet treat.


4. **Fresh air fun**
Spend time outdoors to recharge and boost your well-being. Whether it’s a local trail, a day at the lake, or a weekend trip, fresh air offers an easy way to relax and reset.


5. **On the bright side**
A positive mindset can truly make a difference when life feels overwhelming. Focus on small ways to spread kindness and support your community and make choices that align with your values. Just as important, step away when needed and prioritize rest, movement, and activities that help you recharge.


Wellness doesn’t have to be complicated. Small steps add up over time (literally and figuratively). Pick a tip or two to start and keep building habits that help you feel your best!
