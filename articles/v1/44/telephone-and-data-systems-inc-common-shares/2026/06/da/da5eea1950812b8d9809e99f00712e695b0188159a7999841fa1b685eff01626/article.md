# Two Hallmark gifts are coming in July

July brings two different, but very special Hallmark experiences to your TDS TV.


**Hallmark Family Free Preview**


TDS TV subscribers are getting a Hallmark Family free preview for the entire month of July! It’s a great way to explore Hallmark Family’s regular timeless storytelling centered around faith, love, and community. From classic series to feel-good movies, there’s plenty to choose from for viewers of all ages.


There’s comfort watches like *Little House on the Prairie* and *Highway to Heaven* , but also newer shows you love including *Heartland* , *When Calls the Heart* , and *Good Witch* . Every afternoon you’ll find Hallmark movies such as[Love and Sunshine](https://www.hallmarkfamily.com/love-and-sunshine) ,[For Love & Honor](https://www.hallmarkfamily.com/for-love-and-honor) ,[High Flying Romance](https://www.hallmarkfamily.com/high-flying-romance) ,[My Dreams of You](https://www.hallmarkfamily.com/my-dreams-of-you) —and so many more.


You get to enjoy all this Hallmark goodness in July—for free—so, look for it on channel 335 on TDS TV+ (check your guide if you have a different platform). If you love it, give us a call or[go online](https://tdstelecom.com/) to upgrade your channel package so you can keep all the Hallmark goodness going all year long.


Speaking of Hallmark goodness …


**Christmas in July!**


The Hallmark Channel is bringing their fan-favorite holiday cheer back so you can get your Christmas movie fix during the summer.


They’re showing four brand-new movies in July—a different premiere every Saturday—plus a new season of *Christmas at Sea* starts sailing the very first day of the month.[TVLine](https://www.tvline.com/2184109/2026-hallmark-channel-christmas-in-july-movie-schedule/) has the full details, but here are the key points:


- Season 2 of *Christmas at Sea* starts **July 1**
- *Christmas Under Construction* starring Jessica Lowndes and Daniel Lissing premiers **July 4**
- *O Little Christmas Market* starring Katherine Barrell and Stephen Huszar premiers **July 11**
- *Snowbound for the Holidays* starring Vanessa Lengies and Marcus Rosner premiers **July 18**
- *Love under the Mistletoe* starring Jen Lilley and Nick Bateman premiers **July 25**


Of course, you’ll have the chance to revisit your festive favorites with plenty of cozy romances airing every day. Visit[their website](https://www.hallmarkchannel.com/schedule) for the latest scheduling information.


Here are a few bonus tips from this TDS Hallmarkie (that’s a Hallmark fan, for those of you who aren’t familiar):


- Don’t forget we have lots of[Christmas Movie Bingo cards](https://blog.tdstelecom.com/?s=hallmark+bingo) out here for you—perfect for a fun, Christmas in July, gathering!
- The channel usually kicks off Countdown to Christmas at the end of October so you won’t have to go long to wait for more holiday movies once July comes to a close.


Now’s a great time to upgrade your channel package because you’ll get to see the new premieres and everything to come. Visit[our website](https://tdstelecom.com/) , chat with us, or call us—just don’t miss this programming if you’re a fan!
