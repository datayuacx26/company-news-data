# Movie night made easy: 10 picks for every kind of watch party

National Movie Night is just around the corner—Friday, June 12—making it the perfect excuse to grab some popcorn, get comfortable, and press play. Whether you’re planning a cozy night with your significant other, a movie marathon with the family, or a fun watch party with friends, the right pick(s) can make all the difference.


With TDS TV+, it’s easy to browse and stream your favorite movies across your TV subscriptions in one place—so you can spend less time searching and more time watching. To help you get started, here are some can’t miss picks for every kind of movie night.


### ***Movies to watch with your partner***


**“Crazy Rich Asians” (2018)**


Rachel Chu expects a simple trip when travels with her boyfriend, Nick, to his hometown in Singapore, but she quickly learns he comes from one of the region’s most wealthy and influential families, making him a highly sought-after bachelor.


Thrust into a dazzling world of lavish parties and high expectations, she faces jealousy and scrutiny from those who think she doesn’t belong. “Crazy Rich Asians” is a feel-good escape to watch with your partner. Will Rachel rise above it all or be pushed out of a world that was never meant for her? Stream now on HBO Max.


**“Before Sunrise” (1995)**


American traveler Jesse and French student Celine meet by chance on a train from Budapest to Vienna and—on a whim—decide to spend a single day exploring the city together. What follows next is a quietly captivating journey filled with fleeting moments and an undeniable connection. Their connection deepens with every conversation, but with Jesse’s flight looming, time becomes their biggest obstacle. A beautifully simple story about chance and connection—can one day be enough?


**“Couples Retreat” (2009)**


A tropical getaway turns unexpectedly revealing when four couples visit a resort focused on relationship therapy. With a great star-studded cast and a mix of humor and heart, this lighthearted comedy offers plenty of laughs while also touching on the ups and downs of modern relationships—making it an easy, entertaining watch you can both enjoy.


**“Companion” (2025)**


For couples who enjoy psychological thrillers, “Companion” offers a tense and action-packed experience. What starts as a relaxing weekend getaway quickly takes a dark turn when tensions rise among a group of friends at a remote lake house. As secrets surface, the uneasy atmosphere builds into something far more unsettling. With eerie twists and mounting suspense, this thriller will keep you guessing. Watch now on HBO Max.


### ***Movies to watch with your family***


**“Zootopia” (& “Zootopia 2”)**


A fantastic pick for the whole family, the “Zootopia” franchise combines clever storytelling, sharp humor, and plenty of fun both kids and adults will enjoy. The original follows Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde as they tackle a mystery in a bustling city full of surprises, while the sequel builds on the story with even more adventure outside the fictional metropolis.


With memorable characters, witty dialogue, and messages that resonate across generations, this is one of the rare series where the second movie lives up to the hype—making it an easy, entertaining pick for everyone. Stream now on Disney+.


**“The Goonies” (1985)**


A timeless pick for the whole family, “The Goonies” is the kind of movie that never loses its luster. A group of kids discover a hidden treasure map and sets off on a thrilling adventure to save their homes. Packed with humor, danger, and nonstop excitement, this classic captures the spirit of childhood adventure and remains just as fun today.


**“Coraline” (2009)**


“Coraline” transforms your living room into a magical world that’s as enchanting as it is mysterious. Feeling overlooked in her new home, young Coraline discovers a hidden door that leads to a seemingly perfect parallel world—complete with attentive, loving parents and everything she’s ever dreamed of.


But beneath the charm lies a darker truth, and Coraline soon realizes this dreamlike world is designed to trap her.


### ***Movies to watch with your besties***


**“Legally Blonde” (2001)**


A perfect pick for a night with your besties, “Legally Blonde” is equal parts hilarious, empowering, and endlessly quotable. When Elle Woods follows her ex to Harvard Law School, she sets out to prove she’s more than what anyone expects.


What unfolds is a feel-good story about staying true to yourself, breaking stereotypes, and cheering each other on. Packed with iconic moments and plenty of laughs, it’s the kind of movie that makes you want to grab your friends and watch again and again.


**“Top Gun: Maverick” (2022)**


Thirty years after first blazing across the danger zone, Pete “Maverick” Mitchell returns to the United States Navy Strike Fighter Tactics Instructor program (also known as “TOPGUN”) with a new task—training a new generation of elite pilots for a high-stakes mission. As he faces ghosts from his past, Maverick must balance instinct with responsibility in ways he never has before. With heart-pounding action, emotional depth, and jaw-dropping visuals, this is a ride you’ll want to experience from takeoff to landing. Stream now on Paramount+.


**“Talk to Me” (2023)**


Another film for our horror and thriller-seekers, *Talk to Me* is a chilling ride that starts as a reckless game and spirals into something far more sinister. When a group of friends discover they can communicate with spirits using an embalmed hand, the thrill quickly becomes addictive—but curiosity turns to terror when one of them pushes the limits too far.
