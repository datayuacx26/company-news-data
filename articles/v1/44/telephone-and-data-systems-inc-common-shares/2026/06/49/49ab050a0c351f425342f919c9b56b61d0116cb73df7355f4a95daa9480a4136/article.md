# Father’s Day gift guide

It’s almost Father’s Day, and if you’re brainstorming gift ideas for a dad or father figure, no worries—we’ve got you covered. Whether he’s chasing after little kids, cheering on grown ones, or enjoying grandparent life, we’ve rounded up a quick list of ways to celebrate him.


**Daily gifts**


That favorite polo he wears on repeat? Refresh his outfit with a high-quality version in a new color—or replace one that’s seen better days. You could also upgrade other everyday staples, like a go-to baseball cap, comfortable lounge shorts, or fresh socks. These simple swaps are easy upgrades he’ll use—and appreciate—every day.


**Home** A solid tumbler that keeps drinks hot or cold for hours is a practical win. Add his initials or keep it simple—either way, it’ll likely become part of his daily routine. If he’s a high-tech dad and he’s a hot beverage fan, maybe even consider some of the self-warming options you can program with your phone.


**Backyard and outdoor**
Whether he’s doing yard work or hosting dinner on the patio, he can have his go-to music or favorite sports team on in the background with a solid wireless speaker (check online for Father’s Day deals). If he enjoys lawn and yard care, a tool upgrade could also be a great option—or maybe have his favorites sharpened, so they’re as good as new.


**Foodie**
For dads who like to grill or try new recipes, think grilling tools, spice sets, or even something more niche like pickling supplies to fuel his hobby. You could also plan a meal together (with you buying the ingredients, of course) and put it to use right away.


**Travel and on-the-go**
An electronics accessories organizer keeps chargers, cords, and devices all in one place—perfect for dads who are always on the move (and a win for anyone who’s helped him track down a missing charger). There’s also lots of gizmos and gadgets to consider like phone mounts you can use in the car or plane, neck pillows, packable blankets, and more.


**Events and quality time**


Is his favorite band on tour? Grab tickets to the concert, movie, or to a game of his MLB team (or catch the game together on TDS TV+!). Whether you go out or stay in, he’ll love the chance to spend time with you.
