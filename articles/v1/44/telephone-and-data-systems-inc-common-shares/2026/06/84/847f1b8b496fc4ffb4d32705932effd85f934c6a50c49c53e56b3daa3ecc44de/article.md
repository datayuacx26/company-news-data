# Important changes coming to your TDS email account

If you have an email account with us, please check your inbox for information about an important service update.


We’re reaching out about an upcoming change that will impact your email account. We are partnering with Everymail, powered by Open-Xchange, Inc. as part of this transition.


All communications about this change are being sent from the following email address:noreply@e.TDStelecom.com . Please note that Everymail’s URL is:[https://www.everymail.com/](https://www.everymail.com/)


You will receive additional communications about what options you have regarding the upcoming changes. Please keep an eye on your inbox for more details and links to additional information.


As always, we advise customers to be on alert for phishing attempts that mention your TDS services. Carefully check for:


1. A sender name and email address that match. If they don’t match, it’s a red flag the sender isn’t who they say they are.
2. Spelling and grammar mistakes. Large errors in business communications are rare but in phishing attempts are typically obvious and/or frequent.
3. Accurate links. Hover over any links to reveal the web address before clicking. If it doesn’t reflect the business who purportedly sent the communication, be on alert. Look up the sender’s contact information yourself and reach out to verify everything is on the up and up.
4. Suspicious attachments. Scammers will attach .exe files that could actually be malicious software. If you’re not expecting a file attachment, hesitate before clicking.
