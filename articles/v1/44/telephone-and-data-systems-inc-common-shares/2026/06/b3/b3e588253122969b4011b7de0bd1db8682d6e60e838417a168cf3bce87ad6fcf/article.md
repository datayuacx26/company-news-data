# TDS recognized across Utah for internet and community excellence

Across Southern Utah, fast and reliable internet keeps daily life running—from powering classrooms and supporting local businesses to streaming entertainment at home. In 2026, TDS’ continued commitment to strong service and community support earned the company statewide and regional recognition, reinforcing its reputation as a trusted local provider for internet, phone, mobile, and TV services.


This year marked a major milestone as TDS was named a Best of State medal winner for the first time, earning top honors in the telecommunications category. The Best of State Awards recognize organizations that demonstrate innovation, achievement, and a meaningful impact on quality of life across Utah.


Closer to home, TDS also stood out in the 2026 Best of Southern Utah contest. The company earned Gold for “Best Internet Provider” and Silver for “Best Telecom Provider” and “Best Community Partner.” The Gold win marks the fifth consecutive year TDS has been recognized as a top provider in either the internet or telecom categories—a testament to both reliable service and strong community connections.


“Being recognized at both the state and regional level reflects the dedication of our teams to deliver exceptional service while supporting the communities we serve,” said Drew Petersen, senior vice president of Corporate Affairs at TDS Telecom. “These honors reinforce our commitment to helping Utah communities thrive.”


#### **Investing in reliable connections**


TDS’ impact in Southern Utah is backed by long-term investment. Since 2013, the company has invested more than $165 million in its fiber-backed network, supporting communities including St. George, Ivins, Hurricane, Santa Clara, La Verkin, Leeds, Toquerville, Washington, and Cedar City.


Today, every Southern Utah address served by TDS has access to internet speeds up to 1 gigabit per second, with 8-gig speeds available in select areas.


#### **Built for how people live and work**


Beyond high-speed internet, TDS offers services designed for modern lifestyles. TDS TV+ delivers hundreds of channels, on-demand content, cloud DVR, and integrated streaming apps with flexible, contract-free options.


TDS also provides home and business phone services, including unlimited calling, voicemail, and caller ID, along with VoIP solutions like managedIP for business customers. More recently, the company launched TDS Mobile in Southern Utah—available exclusively to TDS internet customers and powered by a national 5G network with flexible plan options.


#### **Making a difference in the community**


TDS’ recognition also reflects its ongoing commitment to giving back. Through its partnership with the Washington County School District Foundation, TDS helps support local education initiatives. As a sponsor of the Fore the Kids Golf Tournament, the company funded 10 classroom grants totaling nearly $9,000, along with a $4,354 grant supporting STEM learning opportunities.


That impact extends beyond financial support. TDS associates regularly volunteer with local organizations focused on education, food security, veteran support, and community well-being, helping strengthen the communities they serve. TDS proudly employs over 100 people in Utah with family-sustaining careers and provides four hours of paid volunteer time each quarter through its Be Good Citizens program.


From major statewide honors to local recognition, TDS’ 2026 awards highlight a commitment to delivering reliable connections while making a meaningful difference in Southern Utah.
