# The best back-to-school tech in 2026

Back-to-school shopping today is way more than notebooks, backpacks, and pencils. As students get set for a new school year this fall, the right technology can help them stay organized, focused, and ready for anything that comes their way.


Below are a few key pieces of tech that are considered essential in 2026, along with some highly-rated options in each category.


First, a **reliable laptop or tablet** remains the centerpiece of most students’ tech setup. It’s still the best tool for writing papers, building presentations, joining video calls, and running school-required software. For 2026, the top-tested picks include the[Apple MacBook Neo](https://www.apple.com/macbook-neo/) ,[Apple MacBook Air](https://www.apple.com/macbook-air/) ,[HP OmniBook 5](https://blog.tdstelecom.com/news/the-best-back-to-school-tech-in-2026/amazon.com/HP-Touchscreen-Snapdragon-X1P-42-100-16-fb0000nr/dp/B0FKJF9Q6M) , and the[Lenovo Yoga 9i 2-in-1 Gen 10 Aura Edition](https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/p/laptops/yoga/yoga-2-in-1-series/yoga-9i-2-in-1-gen-10-aura-edition-14-inch-intel/len101y0055) . As you shop, it’s wise to look for a device with good battery life, a comfortable keyboard, and a lightweight design that makes it easy to carry from class to class.


### **Block out the noise**


Noise-cancelling headphones help students focus anywhere they are — whether that’s a busy library, their dorm room, or a coffee shop. A quality pair can make a big difference by reducing distractions and improving audio quality. The top picks for 2026 include the[Sony WH-1000XM6](https://electronics.sony.com/audio/headphones/headband/p/wh1000xm6-b) ,[Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro](https://www.soundcore.com/products/d1203-liberty-5-pro-anc-earbuds-clear-calls?variant=46723491233982) ,[Sennheiser HDB 630](https://us.sennheiser-hearing.com/products/hdb-630?variant=62083310485875) , and the[Apple AirPods Max](https://www.apple.com/airpods-max/) .


A dead battery can derail a school day. That’s why **portable power** is one of the most practical purchases students can make. The best options are small enough to fit in a backpack, powerful enough to charge a phone more than once, and versatile enough to support laptops, tablets, and headphones. To that end, the[Anker Nano Power Bank 10K](https://www.anker.com/products/a1638-10k-45w-power-bank) ,[Iniu Pawsible P41L-E2 Power Bank](https://www.amazon.com/INIU-Portable-10000mAh-Charging-External/dp/B0DCHWWBNS?th=1) , and the[Nitecore NB10000 Gen 4](https://nitecorestore.com/products/nb10000gen4-usbc-lightweight-power-bank) have all received high ratings this year.


### **Take notes in style**


Smart note-taking tools are one way to help students juggle classes, assignments, extracurriculars, jobs, and social plans. This could include a reusable smart notebook (like the[reMarkable Paper Pro](https://remarkable.com/products/remarkable-paper/pro) ) or a tablet featuring stylus technology (such as a[Samsung Galaxy Tablet](https://www.samsung.com/us/tablets/) ). These types of devices help students organize class materials without carrying piles of paper and are especially useful for visual learners or anyone who likes to annotate readings.


### **Stay on track**


Finally, **trackers and wearables** can assist with busy schedules. Bluetooth trackers (like the[Apple AirTag](https://www.apple.com/airtag/) ) can help locate keys, backpacks, and wallets so students can get to class on time. Smartwatches (for example, the[Google Pixel Watch 4](https://store.google.com/product/pixel_watch_4?hl=en-US) ) can provide quiet reminders, calendar alerts, and health tracking without students constantly checking their phones. For many students, these devices help reduce daily friction and keep routines on track.


What do all these devices have in common? For the most part, they are practical, portable, and built around academic needs. The best tech doesn’t just look good — it helps students stay organized, focused on learning, and makes the school year just a little easier.
