# Sink your teeth into Shark Week 2026 on Discovery

Get ready to dive in—Shark Week 2026 returns to Discovery Channel and TDS TV/TDS TV®+ on Sunday, July 26 at 8 p.m., with a full week of jaw-dropping premieres, high-stakes expeditions, and fin-tastic stories about one of the ocean’s most powerful predators.


Now in its 38th year, Shark Week kicks off at 8 p.m. and runs through Saturday, Aug. 1, bringing 20 action-packed premieres to Discovery Channel. This year’s lineup blends science, adventure, and pop culture. It includes glowing sharks and prehistoric predators, record-setting stunts, and even a K-Pop-inspired mission that’s helping change perceptions of sharks around the world.


**What to watch: the reel deal**


Opening night starts with *K-Pop Shark Heroes* , featuring Ken Jeong and GRAMMY®-nominated artist REI AMI as they use music and culture to reshape how people think about sharks. The night keeps swimming with the return of the fan-favorite *Air Jaws* franchise in *Air Jaws: Red, White and Breach* , followed by *Invasion of the Mega Sharks* , which follows researchers tracking a massive great white in the North Atlantic.


Throughout the week, viewers can look forward to specials including *Jurassic Sharks* , which uses CGI to bring ancient sharks to life; *Expedition Unknown: Shark Secrets* , where Josh Gates investigates whether an infamous sea monster may have been a shark; and *Ultimate Shark Dive* , featuring world-class cliff diver Molly Carlson attempting a record-setting dive into shark-filled waters. It’s a lineup with plenty of bite.


**How TDS TV subscribers can join the feeding frenzy**


TDS TV subscribers can catch all the Shark Week action on Discovery. On TDS TV+ it’s on channel 149 but if you don’t find it there, check your local channel lineup to confirm availability and times in your area. Hot tip: set your DVR now so you’ll get all of the shark-sized entertainment and can’t-miss moments that are sure to make a splash.


Whether you’re tuning in for the science, suspense, or spectacular underwater footage, Shark Week 2026 is set to deliver another unforgettable summer TV event. Grab the remote, cue up Discovery, and get ready to take a bite out of the greatest week in shark programming.
