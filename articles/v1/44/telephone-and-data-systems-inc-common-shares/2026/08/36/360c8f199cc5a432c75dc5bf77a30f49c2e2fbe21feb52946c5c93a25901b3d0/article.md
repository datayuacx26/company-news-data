# TDS helps schools recover after tornado

Following a recent tornado that caused widespread damage across Menasha, Wisconsin and its surrounding communities, TDS associates stepped up to help local schools restore connectivity and prepare for the return of students and staff. Working quickly in the aftermath of the storm, the team helped ensure critical communications services were back in place so schools could resume normal operations as safely and efficiently as possible. Their response reflects the commitment our associates bring to the communities where they live and work, especially during times of need.
