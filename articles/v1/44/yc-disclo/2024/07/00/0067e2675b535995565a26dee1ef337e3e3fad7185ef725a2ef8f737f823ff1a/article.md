## Unique Capabilities of Disclo's Software


‍


### Automated Accommodation Workflows:


Our software features an automated system for managing accommodation requests, making it easier for employees to submit requests while ensuring HR teams can process and manage them effectively. Disclo streamlines end-to-end workflows for HR professionals, enabling organizations to maintain compliance, boost overall efficiency, and focus on their most important asset — their people.


‍


### Data-Driven Insights and Reporting:


A key element of our software includes advanced data analytics and reporting capabilities for accommodation requests, trends, and outcomes, unlocking visibility throughout your organization. As a result, teams gain actionable insights to assess the effectiveness of DEI initiatives and programs. Disclo’s data-driven approach helps teams make informed decisions and demonstrates the impact of your modern accommodations strategy on employee performance and satisfaction.


‍


### Medical Validation:


With Disclo, your HR team doesn’t have to worry about any back-and-forth with medical providers. Our ability to integrate with medical providers ensures that every accommodation request automatically goes through secure, fast, and digital medical validation. This establishes the request’s legitimacy and helps provide the most appropriate accommodation for the employee’s specific needs at work.


‍


### Ensuring Legal Compliance:


Disclo software is designed to help you meet legal standards by ensuring your accommodations process and management comply with the[ADA](http://ada.gov/) (and other relevant laws). By validating the legitimacy and appropriateness of each accommodation request, Disclo helps organizations minimize the risk of non-compliance and costly legal issues arising from non-standard, manual practices.

# About Disclo


Built by 2x disability-focused founders, the software is a workplace ADA and PWFA accommodations platform for companies focused on making workplaces inclusive for everyone. Disclo is a HIPAA-compliant platform that requests, tracks, and manages workplace accommodations—all in one place.


Disclo makes handling accommodations seamless by combining automation, in-app medical e-verification, out-of-the-box analytics (so you can auto-track against EEOC and ADA standards), and the ability to connect to any HRIS and ATS with pre-built integrations.
