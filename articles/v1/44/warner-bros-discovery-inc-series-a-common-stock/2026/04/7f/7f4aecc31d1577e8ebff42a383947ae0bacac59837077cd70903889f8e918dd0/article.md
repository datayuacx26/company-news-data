# Warner Bros. Discovery Updates the Date and Time of its First Quarter 2026 Earnings Call

Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc. (the “Company”) (Nasdaq: WBD) today announced that it has updated the date and time of its conference call and will report its first quarter 2026 results on Wednesday, May 6, 2026 after the market close. Links to the live webcast of the conference call as well as the earnings materials will be available in the “Investor Relations” section of the Company’s website at[https://ir.wbd.com/](https://nam11.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.wbd.com%2F&data=05%7C02%7Ckelsey.garner%40wbd.com%7C7c3e9484a97a408a0f3108dea22bce8a%7C0eb48825e8714459bc72d0ecd68f1f39%7C0%7C0%7C639126506814233203%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=Wh5M3EssrELXgCae7W5NVu8gchVPH%2FkLSnR3jXK8bBU%3D&reserved=0) at approximately 4:00 p.m. ET. The Company will host a conference call at 4:30 p.m. ET that same day to discuss the results.


A replay of the webcast will also be available in the “Investor Relations” section of the Company’s website for twelve months.
