# Q3 2024 | Quarterly Newsletter

‍


Cheers,


Team FlexDesk

## Product Updates


‍


### Portfolio Management


*Track lease information, anticipate rent escalations, and never miss a renewal date.*


‍


**📝 Invoice upload and assignment:** Users now have the ability to upload any office-related invoice to FlexDesk and associate that invoice with an office lease for the purpose of tracking TCRE (Total Cost of Real Estate).


‍


**🤝 NetSuite integration:** Users can now utilize an integration with NetSuite (powered by[Merge](http://merge.dev/) ) to programmatically push all office-related invoices into FlexDesk.


‍


**🔥 UI enhancements:** Users now have functionality for adding pro rata rents, discount factors, default clause names, amendments, and visualizing the rent schedule over time.


‍


### Utilization Intelligence


*Leverage existing badge access systems to automatically track attendance, RTO compliance, and make smarter renewal decisions.*


‍


**📰 Pre-configured CSV templates:** FlexDesk continues to grow its library of pre-configured CSV upload templates - including Paxton, WeWork, Genetec, and Sentrion.


‍


**⚒️ Daily HR sync:** All HRIS integrations now sync daily by default, keeping customer data up to date. Users can select whether to override or preserve values in FlexDesk.


‍


**💥 UI enhancements:** Users now have additional reporting functionality, including more comprehensive aggregate & average metrics across locations.


‍


### Coworking Management


*Provide employees with drop-in access to thousands of coworking locations within one booking interface. Maintain spend controls and consolidated billing.*


‍


**🚀 Video launch:** We launched a video on our Coworking Booking Management solution - check it out[here](https://www.flexdesk.com/blog/product-recap-coworking-booking-management) .


‍


**🌐 Heat Map Overlay:** Admins can now visualize the density/quantity of employee locations (based on zip code) through a heat map overlay.


‍


**✨ UI enhancements:** Users now have additional reporting functionality, including filtering spend/bookings data by office and coworking brand, as well as horizontal scrolling to visualize bookings and spend over time.


‍


### Enterprise Security + Functionality


*FlexDesk is committed to providing best-in-class enterprise controls for its growing customer base.*


‍


**🪪 Authentication:** FlexDesk now supports a wide selection of authentication mechanisms - including Okta, Google, Microsoft, magic auth, and more.


‍


**⚙️ User management:** FlexDesk now supports SCIM endpoints for organizations to fetch, add, update, and delete users programmatically.


‍


**🔏 Access control:** Admins now have the ability to designate different roles within their organization (viewers, editors, etc)
