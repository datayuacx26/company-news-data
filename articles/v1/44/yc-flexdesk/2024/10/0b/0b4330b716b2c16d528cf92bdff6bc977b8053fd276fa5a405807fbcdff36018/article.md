# How ezCater used FlexDesk to facilitate team meetups in over 40 coworking spaces

### Situation


ezCater is the #1 food tech platform for workplaces and has over 1,000 employees in the United States. The company is headquartered in Boston, but it began accelerating its remote hiring during the pandemic.


‍


### Problem


As ezCater continued hiring remotely, it found clusters of employees in several cities. The company was keen to enable – but not require – team gatherings among these collocated employees.


‍


Given the high-cost and long-term length of signing a traditional lease, the company decided to leverage coworking spaces as an affordable and flexible solution.


‍


After initial research, it was clear to ezCater that provisioning access to coworking spaces would be no small feat. Employee expense reimbursements felt like a poor user experience, coupled with unnecessary administrative burdens on the accounting team. And it was important to the company that employees had a sense of agency to choose their own workspace.


‍


ezCater found FlexDesk, and less than 6 weeks later they made their first reservation on the platform.


‍


### Solution


ezCater leveraged FlexDesk’s[Coworking Management Platform](https://www.flexdesk.com/coworking-booking-management) to provide remote employees with drop-in access to coworking space.


‍


Through FlexDesk, ezCater employees can reserve desks and meeting rooms across the world’s most comprehensive network of coworking spaces.


‍


For collocated employees in remote hubs, FlexDesk helps promote a sense of engagement and connectivity.


‍


Alan Bautista, Mid-Market Acquisition BDR Manager at ezCater, describes this in more detail.


‍


“I wanted to organize sales meetups in Austin, and FlexDesk has made the booking experience seamless. I can make reservations on behalf of my colleagues, which helps avoid excessive coordination logistics and places our focus on collaboration and bonding while also having the ability to remain a remote team.”


‍


And for the company administrator, FlexDesk facilitated all aspects of the program across employee hubs, processing invoices, and ensuring adherence to policy.


‍


Nikki Ellis, Office and People Operations Manager at ezCater, explained this in more detail.


‍


“Since working with FlexDesk, I have spent very little time managing our company’s coworking program. Each time a new employee wants to get access to a coworking space, I simply point them to FlexDesk.”


‍


### Results


ezCater has seen employee adoption grow organically outside of its Boston and Denver offices, with bookings in over 40 different coworking spaces. Over 50% of employees return after their first use, and nearly 80% of bookings are for team gatherings.


‍


Employees are also making use of the diverse coverage, with 80% of repeat users booking space at more than 1 coworking brand.


‍


Alan expanded on this: “Different spaces serve different purposes, and I love having access to a wide variety of options – depending on whether I am meeting with a client, hosting a team meetup, or simply getting out of the house for solo work.”


‍
