# Product Updates


‍


### Portfolio Management


*Track lease information, anticipate rent escalations, and never miss a renewal date.*


‍


**⚒️ Rent packet workflow:** Lease administrators and finance teams can now prepare rent packets in-app, including reconciliations, approval workflows, payment remittance, and more.


‍


**📝 Lease accounting:** Lease administrators and finance teams can now generate accounting reports in-app for ASC 842 and IFRS 16.


‍


**✨ UI enhancements:** Users now have a dedicated section to store floorplan information, a document folder structure, ability to input payment schedules, tooltips for key metrics, and invoice grouping attribution by lease, expense type, and expense category.


‍


### Utilization Intelligence


*Leverage existing badge access systems to automatically track attendance, RTO compliance, and make smarter renewal decisions.*


‍


**🔥 Activity View Dashboard:** Users can now unlock employee-specific attendance patterns with comparisons across time period and prepopulated country-specific holidays to normalize utilization calculations.


‍


**🤝 Utilization vs. Show-Up:** Users can now toggle between capacity vs. assigned headcount as the denominator in calculations to track office utilization vs. employee show up rate.


‍


**💥 UI enhancements** : Users now have the ability to add annotations to contextualize attendance patterns, input a target utilization percentage, and leverage a prebuilt integration with Kisi when collecting badge data.


‍


### Coworking Management


*Provide employees with drop-in access to thousands of coworking locations within one booking interface. Maintain spend controls and consolidated billing.*


‍


**📰 Governance framework:** Users now have access to a more robust budget parameters and approval workflow framework to control spend patterns.


‍


**🌐 Inventory refresh:** Users continuously leverage the most up-to-date supply of coworking locations worldwide.


‍


**🚀 UI enhancements:** Users have access to an updated mobile app and have the ability to add guests to day offices in any location.
