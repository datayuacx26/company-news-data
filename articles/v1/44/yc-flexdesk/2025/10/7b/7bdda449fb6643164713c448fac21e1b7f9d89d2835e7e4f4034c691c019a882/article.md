# Product Updates


‍


### Portfolio Management


*Track lease information, anticipate rent escalations, and never miss a renewal date.*


‍


**✨ Surface Area over Time:** Admins now have the ability to store dynamic surface area figures over time for more precise reporting and calculations of derived metrics.


‍


**📝 Notes & Comments:** Users now have the ability to collaborate within a lease record by adding notes, comments, and tagging others.


‍


**⚒️ UI enhancements:** Admins have additional reporting capabilities, including breakouts by space type, rollups for an entire lease term, document library, and country-level rollups.


‍


### Utilization Intelligence


*Leverage existing badge access systems to automatically track attendance, RTO compliance, and make smarter renewal decisions.*


‍


**🔥 Department-level Analytics:** Users now have filtering and grouping analytics by department.


‍


**⏳ Capacity/Headcount over Time:** Users now have the ability to store dynamic capacity and headcount figures over time for more precise reporting and calculations of derived metrics.


‍


**💥 Dashboard enhancements:** Users now have access to expanded reporting functionality, including quarterly breakouts, bulk-add annotations, and export functionality.


‍


### Coworking Management


*Provide employees with drop-in access to thousands of coworking locations within one booking interface. Maintain spend controls and consolidated billing.*


‍


**🚀 Cost Center allocation:** Admins now have the ability to assign bookings to specific cost centers for invoice itemization.


‍


**🌐 Continuous Inventory Monitoring:** High-traffic coworking brands now have daily monitoring jobs for continuous site and inventory refinements.


‍


**📱 Cancellation Policy:** Users now have visibility into the coworking space’s cancellation policy at the time of booking.
