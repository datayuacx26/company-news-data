# Product Updates


‍


### Portfolio Management


*Track lease information, anticipate rent escalations, and never miss a renewal date.*


‍


**📝 Aggregate view:** Users now have the ability to see detailed metrics about their portfolio - including rent and “cost-per” metrics - at a granular level by location and at an aggregate level by region and by country.


‍


**📰 Observation period:** Users now have the ability to select a custom date range when viewing portfolio-level metrics.


‍


**🤝 Vendor auto-mapping and categorization:** Office-related invoices are now programmatically mapped to a location and to a custom vendor category for budget and spend tracking.


‍


### Utilization Intelligence


*Leverage existing badge access systems to automatically track attendance, RTO compliance, and make smarter renewal decisions.*


‍


**⚒️ Email-based data pipelines:** Users can now configure recurring emails with CSV attachments from their utilization data provider to automatically upload attendance data into FlexDesk.


‍


**🔥 User grouping:** Users can now assign groups to individuals and leverage filtering/grouping/sorting functionality for insights on attendance patterns.


‍


**💥 UI enhancements:** Users now have additional analytics functionality, including the ability to input tailgating assumptions and to (un)select days-of-week for aggregate reporting metrics.


‍


### Coworking Management


*Provide employees with drop-in access to thousands of coworking locations within one booking interface. Maintain spend controls and consolidated billing.*


‍


**🚀 Approval workflows:** Coworking administrators can now configure reservations that require admin approval - whether based on amount, meeting room capacity, length of time, and more.


‍


**🌐 Booking policies:** Users now have access to a more robust policy framework - including department-level budgets, geofencing, custom policies, and more.


‍


**✨ UI enhancements:** Users now have additional reporting functionality, including filtering spend/bookings data by reservation status, cost, individual, and more.
