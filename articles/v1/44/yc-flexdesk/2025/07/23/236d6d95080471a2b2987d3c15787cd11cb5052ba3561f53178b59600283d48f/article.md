# Product Updates


‍


### Portfolio Management


*Track lease information, anticipate rent escalations, and never miss a renewal date.*


‍


⚒️ **Liteweight abstraction:** Users now have the ability to reduce scope and store financial information by location without the need to abstract terms, clauses, and critical dates.


‍


📝 **Invoice entry:** Users now have expanded functionality for uploading, inputting, and editing invoice information from vendors when tracking total cost of real estate.


‍


✨ **UI enhancements:** Users can now exclude locations without surface area information from key metrics and leverage enhanced view/edit functionality for third-party administrators.


‍


### Utilization Intelligence


*Leverage existing badge access systems to automatically track attendance, RTO compliance, and make smarter renewal decisions.*


‍


**🔥 Custom Denominators:** Users now have the ability to define and store custom denominators (e.g., desk count, capacity, assigned headcount) when calculating office utilization metrics.


‍


**📰 Overview dashboard enhancements:** Users now have toggles between peak utilization vs. average utilization and the ability to set target utilization thresholds by location.


‍


**💥** **Activity View dashboard enhancements:** Users now have access to expanded reporting functionality, including weekly/monthly rollups, aggregate metrics, and export functionality.


‍


### Coworking Management


*Provide employees with drop-in access to thousands of coworking locations within one booking interface. Maintain spend controls and consolidated billing.*


‍


**🚀 Real-time inventory availability:** Our biggest update since launching the coworking product! Users now have visibility into real-time inventory availability for desks, meeting rooms, and day offices at WeWork locations.


‍


**🌐 Inventory refresh:** Users continuously leverage the most up-to-date supply of coworking locations worldwide.


‍


📱 **Mobile app:** Users have access to an updated mobile app with real-time availability, policy governance, and more.
