# Product Updates


‍


### Portfolio Management


*Track lease information, anticipate rent escalations, and never miss a renewal date.*


‍


**✨ Denominators over Time:** Admins now have the ability to capture an extended set of figures over time for portfolio analysis, including capacity, headcount, and more.


‍


**📝 Document Pop-Up:** Admins now have one-click access to view lease documents in-app without opening a new tab.


‍


**⚒️ UI enhancements:** Admins now have upgraded visualization aesthetics, including headcount breakouts by region/country and standard formats for prices and dates.


‍


### Utilization Intelligence


*Leverage existing badge access systems to automatically track attendance, RTO compliance, and make smarter renewal decisions.*


‍


**🔥 Individual-Level Annotations:** Admins now have the ability to contextualize attendance patterns and modify show-up rates for individual employees.


‍


**🖇️ Primary and Secondary Departments:** Admins now have the ability to enrich and analyze attendance behaviors with multiple department fields.


‍


**💥 Dashboard enhancements:** Admins now have access to expanded reporting functionality, including a user search bar, export capabilities, additional field dropdowns, and more.


‍


### Coworking Management


*Provide employees with drop-in access to thousands of coworking locations within one booking interface. Maintain spend controls and consolidated billing.*


‍


**🌐 Third-party coworking data:** Admins now have the ability to ingest on-demand coworking data from third-party sources and leverage off-the-shelf reporting capabilities.


‍


**💳 Billing Chargebacks:** Admins now have the ability to assign different cost centers to bookings for billing breakouts.


‍


**🚀 Power Users:** Admins now have pre-built charts for highest-use individuals based on booking count, cost, and more.
