# Casey’s 2025 Year in Review

# A Year of Flavor, Fun and Community Impact


As we wrap up 2025, we’re looking back at a year filled with bold flavors, exciting partnerships and heartfelt community initiatives. From pizza parties to classroom support, Casey’s brought joy to millions of guests across the Midwest and beyond. Here’s a month-by-month look at the moments that made this year unforgettable.


### January


**National Pizza Week – A Cheesy Start to the Year**


We kicked off 2025 with[National Pizza Week](https://blog.caseys.com/articles/national-pizza-week?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=yearinreviewblog) , celebrating our legendary handmade pizza with exclusive deals and limited-time offers. It was the perfect way to warm up winter and remind guests why Casey’s pizza is a Midwest favorite.


[Indulge in something cheesy](https://www.caseys.com/menu/c/pizza?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=yearinreviewblog)


###


### February


**Sauced Wings – Bold Flavors Take Flight**


February turned up the heat with our[Sauced Wings](https://www.caseys.com/sauced-wings?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=YearInReviewBlog) . Guests discovered bold new flavors and classic favorites, from Buffalo to BBQ. Whether paired with pizza or enjoyed solo, our wings soared in popularity.


[Explore variety of flavors](https://www.caseys.com/sauced-wings?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=YearInReviewBlog)


###


### March


**Rewards Matchup – Play, Earn, Enjoy**


March kicked off with the Casey’s Rewards Matchup, a fun and interactive way for guests to earn points and unlock exclusive deals. This gamified experience boosted engagement and gave fans more reasons to visit their local Casey’s.


[Join rewards to play next year](https://www.caseys.com/rewards?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=YearInReviewBlog)


###


### April


**40 Years of Casey’s Pizza Perfection**


In April, we celebrated[40 years of Casey’s Pizza](https://blog.caseys.com/articles/celebrate-40-years-of-caseys-pizza-perfection?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=yearinreviewblog) - a Midwest favorite known for its handmade dough and signature sauce. Guests enjoyed 40% off, exclusive merch and a tribute to the pizza that started it all.


[Try the pizza that fans love](https://www.caseys.com/c/menu/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=yearinreviewblog)


###


### May


**Scratch Match & Win – Instant Prizes, Big Smiles**


May brought the thrill of instant wins with Scratch Match & Win. Guests scratched digital cards to reveal prizes like free pizza, bonus rewards points, and surprise offers - making every visit a chance to win.


###


### June


*Coffee Refresh – Darn Good Gets Better*


June was all about coffee. Casey’s launched a Coffee Refresh featuring new blends and flavors that elevated our already[Darn Good coffee](https://www.caseys.com/darn-good-coffee?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=YearInReviewBlog) . Whether on the go or starting your day, our brews kept guests energized.


###


### July


**Casey’s Center – Connecting with Our Community**


July introduced the[Casey’s Center](https://blog.caseys.com/articles/a-new-era-in-des-moines-welcome-to-caseys-center?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=yearinreviewblog) , for two decades the Wells Fargo Arena has been the heart of downtown Des Moines, and now it transitions to the Casey’s Center. The renaming to Casey’s Center is more than a branding update - it’s a celebration of Iowa pride and a commitment to the community


###


### August


**Casey’s x Coca-Cola – Cash for Classrooms**


In partnership with Coca-Cola, August spotlighted[Cash for Classrooms](https://blog.caseys.com/articles/empowering-education-how-caseys-is-fueling-classrooms-with-cash-for-classrooms?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=yearinreviewblog) , a fundraising initiative supporting local schools. Every purchase helped fuel education, and together, we made a lasting impact on students and teachers.


###


### September


**40% Off Pizzas – Back-to-School Kickoff**


September’s start of 40% Off Pizzas promotion was a hit with families and football fans. Offering unbeatable deals on our famous pizzas every Saturday and welcoming new Rewards members to satisfy cravings.


###


### October


**Casey’s + 4-H – Empowering Youth**


October marked a meaningful partnership with[4-H](https://blog.caseys.com/articles/building-stronger-communities-caseys-partners-with-4-h?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=yearinreviewblog) , supporting youth development in rural communities. Through round-up donations and matched contributions, Casey’s helped empower future leaders across the country.


###


### November


**Supporting Veterans – Honoring Our Heroes**


In November, Casey’s honored veterans with donation drives, recognition campaigns, and community events. Supporting those who served is a core part of our mission, and we’re proud to give back to our heroes.


###


### December


**24 Days of Rewards – A Holiday Countdown**


December wrapped up the year with 24 Days of Rewards, a festive countdown filled with daily surprises. From free treats to bonus points, Casey’s made the holidays brighter for our loyal guests.


###


### Looking Ahead to 2026


Casey’s 2025 was a year of celebration, innovation, and community impact. As we look forward to 2026, we remain committed to delivering great food, rewarding experiences, and meaningful connections with our guests.
