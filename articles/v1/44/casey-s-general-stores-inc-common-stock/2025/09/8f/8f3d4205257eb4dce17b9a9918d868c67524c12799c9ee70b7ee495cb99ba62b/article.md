# Building Stronger Communities: Casey’s Partners with 4-H

This October, we’re deepening our commitment to community by teaming up with 4-H, America’s largest youth development organization.


### **Partnership Rooted in Purpose**


We believe education lays the foundation for strong communities by preparing children for a bright future, building a skilled workforce, and strengthening connections among families. That’s why we’re partnering with 4-H, an organization that offers opportunities and experiences to help youth grow into their full, authentic selves.


Across Casey's Country, there are more than 2.5 million young people actively involved in 4-H programs. These programs equip youth with essential life skills and learning experiences that help them grow into confident, capable leaders.


###


### **How You Can Support**


Casey’s guests have two meaningful ways to contribute to this initiative:


-


**Round Up In-Store** : From Oct 1–10, guests can round up their purchases to the nearest dollar at any Casey’s location. Donations will benefit local 4-H programs in Casey's Country.


-


**Donate Online** : From Oct 1–Nov 4, guests can add a donation to their online orders. Casey’s will match guest donations up to $150,000, maximizing the impact for local 4-H programs.


[Make an impact](https://www.caseys.com/menu/essentials/4-h-round-up-campaign/p/9843710914&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=4HBlog)


###


### **Giving Back, the Casey’s Way**


At Casey’s, making life better for communities and guests is a daily practice. That’s why we’re proud to stand alongside 4-H in their mission to empower and transform youth.


Together, we look forward to continuing our support for rural communities and making a lasting, positive impact on the next generation.
