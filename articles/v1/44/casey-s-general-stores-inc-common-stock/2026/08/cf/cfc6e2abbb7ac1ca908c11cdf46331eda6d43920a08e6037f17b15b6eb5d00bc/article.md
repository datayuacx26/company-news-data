## Casey's Announces Timing of First Quarter Earnings Release and Conference Call


August 19, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.caseys.com/node/17571/pdf)


ANKENY, Iowa--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 19, 2026-- Casey’s General Stores, Inc. (“Casey’s” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: CASY), one of the leading convenience store chains in the United States, will issue first quarter 2027 results after the market closes on September 8th, 2026. Casey’s will hold a conference call and webcast on Wednesday, September 9th at 7:30am central to review the results.


A live webcast of the event will be available on Casey’s website on the Investor Relations page at[https://investor.caseys.com/events-presentations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.caseys.com%2Fevents-presentations&esheet=54591911&newsitemid=20260819564778&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.caseys.com%2Fevents-presentations&index=1&md5=40e44e3a38538737d5e1480655f3c050) . For those unable to listen to the live broadcast, an audio replay will be available on Casey’s for twelve months.
