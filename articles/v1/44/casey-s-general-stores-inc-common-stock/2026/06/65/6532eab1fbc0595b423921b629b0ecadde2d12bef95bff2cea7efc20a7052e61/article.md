# Your Ultimate RAGBRAI 2026 Guide: Fuel Up at Casey’s, Ride Strong

RAGBRAI is back, and there’s nothing quite like riding across Iowa with thousands of fellow cyclists, small-town charm at every stop and plenty of unforgettable moments along the way. Whether you’re a first-time rider or a seasoned pro, one thing stays the same: you need great fuel and smart planning to make the most of every mile.


Here's your go-to guide for RAGBRAI 2026, including where to stop, what to eat, and why Casey’s should be on your must-visit list this year.


## What is RAGBRAI?


RAGBRAI (Register's Annual Great Bicycle Ride Across Iowa) is a multi-day cycling event that brings riders across the state, stopping in unique communities along the way. Each year features a new route, and planning your stops ahead of time helps ensure you stay energized and ready to ride.


## Supporting Riders Through the Dream Team


RAGBRAI is about more than the ride, its about community. That's why Casey's is proud to support the[Dream Team](https://www.dreamteamdesmoines.org/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=caseysblog&utm_campaign=ragbraiblog) , a five-month mentorship program that pairs Iowa youth with adult mentors to help them build confidence, life skills and lasting relationships.

## RAGBRAI 2026 Route Planning Tips


As you map your ride, keep these quick tips in mind:


-


Plan stops ahead of time so you don't run low on energy


-


Use landmarks and towns as checkpoints to break up each day's ride


-


Build in time for food, rest and exploring local areas


-


Take plenty of photos and tag @caseys when you make a stop


## Stop in Boone: Visit the First-Ever Casey's


During RAGBRAI 2026, Boone serves as an overnight town on Day 3; making it the perfect place to take a break, recharge and experience a piece of Casey's history.


Located in Boone is the site of the very first Casey's store, a landmark that helped start it all.


While you're in town:


-


Snap a photo at the original Casey's location


-


Grab your favorite pizza, snacks, or drinks


-


Rest up before the next leg of your ride


It's a quick stop that ties your RAGBRAI ride to a true piece of Iowa tradition.
