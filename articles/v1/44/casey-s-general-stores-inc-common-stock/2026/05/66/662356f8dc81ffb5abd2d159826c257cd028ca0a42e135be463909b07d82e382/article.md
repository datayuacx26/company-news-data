## Casey's Announces Timing of Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year Earnings Release and Conference Call


May 19, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.caseys.com/node/17261/pdf)


ANKENY, Iowa--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 19, 2026-- Casey’s General Stores, Inc. (“Casey’s” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: CASY), one of the leading convenience store chains in the United States, will issue fourth quarter and fiscal year 2026 results after the market closes on June 9th, 2026. Casey’s will hold a conference call and webcast on Wednesday, June 10th at 7:30am central to review the results.


A live webcast of the event will be available on Casey’s website on the Investor Relations page at[https://investor.caseys.com/events-presentations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.caseys.com%2Fevents-presentations&esheet=54538087&newsitemid=20260519268158&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.caseys.com%2Fevents-presentations&index=1&md5=acb87d744b98b28e12f73002197f9fa7) . For those unable to listen to the live broadcast, an audio replay will be available on Casey’s for twelve months.
