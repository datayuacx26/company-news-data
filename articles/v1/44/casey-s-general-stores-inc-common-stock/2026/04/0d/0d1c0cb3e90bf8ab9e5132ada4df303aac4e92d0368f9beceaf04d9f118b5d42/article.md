Alarms are set. Coffee is poured. The match is on the second it starts.


This watcher wants something dependable and ready before kickoff. Familiar flavors make it easy to focus on the game instead of the food. Ordering ahead helps everything land on time, and leftovers never go to waste later in the day.


-


**Pizza** : Classic Pepperoni Pizza, or the fan-favorite Bacon Breakfast Pizza


-


**Drink** : Hot or iced coffee or a fountain drink


-


**Snack** : Breakfast pastry or donut


Reliability and routine matter most here.

Watching alone is not an option. You’re surrounded by friends, family or both, and the group chat has been buzzing since the lineup dropped. Opinions are flying and everyone is hungry.


You need pizza that keeps the peace, but also starts conversations. Something everyone agrees on, plus enough extras to make sure no one’s counting slices. Flexibility matters because watch parties tend to grow as the match goes on.


-


**Pizza** : Large Sausage pizza, or the new Twisted Pepperoni pizza


-


**Drink** : 2L soda or multiple fountain drinks


-


**Snack** : Pepperoni Loaded Breadsticks or a shareable side (like mac & cheese)


All about sharing the moment and the food.


## The Casual Watcher


You’re here for the big moments and the overall vibe. You might jump between matches, check highlights or keep the game on while doing something else.


Pizza should be easy and distraction free. Simple slices, grab and go snacks and drinks that don’t require a pause button. This is about enjoying the experience without planning your whole day around it.


-


**Pizza** : Easy to eat slices or the Four Cheese pizza


-


**Drink** : Fountain drink or bottled beverage


-


**Snack** : New Orleans Kettle Chips, or Potato Cheese Bites


This watcher keeps things light and fun.
