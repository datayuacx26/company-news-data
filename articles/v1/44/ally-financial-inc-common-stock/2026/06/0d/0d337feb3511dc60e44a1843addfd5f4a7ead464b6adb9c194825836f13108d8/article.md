# Ally Charitable Foundation And Novant Health Support Do Greater Charlotte’s Sparkhouse Uptown

*Coalition of Charlotte partners backs new creative and entrepreneurial hub opening June 19*


**CHARLOTTE, N.C., June 4, 2026** — The Ally Charitable Foundation and Novant Health announced today they are joining Charlotte organizations investing in Sparkhouse Uptown, a new 20,000-square-foot creative opportunity hub in the heart of Uptown Charlotte. The two organizations join an established coalition of anchor partners — including Wells Fargo, Truist Charitable Fund, and the City of Charlotte — whose early investment made Sparkhouse Uptown possible, and whose collective commitment reflects a shared conviction: that Charlotte’s next chapter of growth must be more inclusive, more connected and more opportunity-driven.


Sparkhouse Uptown is designed from the ground up as opportunity infrastructure for Charlotte. Powered by Do Greater Charlotte, the space gives under-resourced youth, young adults, entrepreneurs and creatives access to what many receive through college campuses, corporate networks or family connections — workspace, tools, mentors, technology and real-world pathways to move ideas into income and potential into progress. Do Greater Charlotte’s Sparkhouse Uptown is slated to open this month, with a ribbon cutting celebration set for June 19.


**New Funders, Shared Purpose**
Ally Charitable Foundation’s $500,000 investment supports the **Ally Charitable Foundation Creative Studio** — a fully equipped space within Sparkhouse Uptown. The studio will house learning and development programming, dedicated production spaces, computers, 3D printers, screen printing equipment, and flexible collaborative areas designed for makers, entrepreneurs and creatives to work and build together. It is the kind of infrastructure that has historically existed only inside corporate campuses or costly private programs — and its presence at Sparkhouse Uptown broadens who gets access.


The Ally Charitable Foundation, the philanthropic arm of Ally Financial, is dedicated to advancing economic mobility and breaking down systemic barriers to opportunity. Established in 2020, the Foundation focuses on affordable housing, financial education and workforce development. Since 2020, Ally’s total charitable giving has exceeded $100 million in support of economic mobility and community development, and the Foundation’s support for more than 100 nonprofits across Charlotte reflects a long-standing commitment to backing organizations that help communities build their own futures.


**Novant Health’s** investment will support Sparkhouse Coffee, a community coffee shop operated by Do Greater Charlotte that will provide hands-on business and workforce experience for young entrepreneurs while serving the Uptown community. The health system has previously partnered with Do Greater Charlotte on community programming initiatives, including the Symphony at Sparkhouse concert series, which combined free family programming with health screenings and wellness resources.


**Building on a Foundation Already Laid**
As established philanthropic leaders in Charlotte, **Ally Charitable Foundation and Novant Health** are building upon the strong foundation laid by earlier partners, deepening the community’s collective investment in Sparkhouse Uptown, previously known as Creative Lab Uptown.


**Wells Fargo Bank** , **Truist Charitable Fund** , and the **City of Charlotte** were among the investors whose early support helped transform Do Greater Charlotte’s vision from concept to construction. Additional support from **Lowe’s** , **Bissell Ballantyne Legacy Foundation** , **Gambrell Foundation** , **Gresham Smith** , **DPR Construction** and **Center City Partners** has turned Sparkhouse Uptown into a reality.


Together, this coalition represents a broad cross-section of banks, health systems, corporations, foundations, civic organizations, and government choosing to invest in the same block, the same building, and the same community at the same time.


**Sparkhouse Uptown: What’s Inside**
The space in Uptown Charlotte is designed as a hub where a young person can walk in with an idea and walk out with a plan — and with the skills, network, and tools to execute it. Sparkhouse Uptown provides access to:


- Creative and digital production tools
- Entrepreneurship and workforce development programming
- Technology and digital skills training
- Mentorship and professional networking opportunities
- Collaborative workspaces and community gathering areas
- A youth-operated coffee shop and social enterprise experience


Sparkhouse Uptown is more than just a building—it’s a place designed to expand access to economic opportunity. By offering resources and support typically found in educational institutions or professional networks, Sparkhouse Uptown welcomes anyone eager to pursue their goals, providing tools and guidance to help them succeed.


Sparkhouse is located at 500 N. Tryon St. a landmark building owned by Harvey B. Gantt, Charlotte’s first Black mayor and a pioneer in design and civic leadership. Originally built in 1921 as an automobile showroom, it was later home to the Gantt Huberman Architects, one of the first racially integrated architecture firms in North Carolina.
