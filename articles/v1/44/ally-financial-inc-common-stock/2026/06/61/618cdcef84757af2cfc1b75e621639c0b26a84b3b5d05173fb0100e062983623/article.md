## Step-by-step guide to adding cash to your Ally Bank Spending Account


With approximately 90% of the U.S. population living within 10 miles of a Walmart, this method offers a secure, convenient and free way to add cash to your Ally Bank Spending Account. Follow these steps:


### Step 1: Visit a Walmart Money Center or Customer Service Desk


Head to a[nearby Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/store-finder) Money Center or Customer Service Desk, after checking the hours at your local store.


### Step 2: Generate your unique barcode in the Ally app and present it at the counter


When you open the app, you can request a barcode for your transaction. The barcode is valid for 15 minutes, but you can generate a new one if it expires.


Once you have your barcode, show it to the associate at the counter. You may be asked to show your I.D. and your phone number.


Note:


For security purposes, be sure you have location services enabled on your phone.


### Step 3: Pass your cash to the associate


Once your barcode is scanned, give your cash to the associate, and it’ll be added to your account.


You can add between $20 and $1,000 of cash per day per Ally Bank Spending Account up to five times per month.


### Step 4: Access your money within minutes


Typically, your cash should appear in your account within minutes.
