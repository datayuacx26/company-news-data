Despite utilizing **Salesforce’s Einstein bot,** their processes were slow and inflexible. The **Einstein bot from Salesforce was complicated t** o set up and required technical support for adjustments, slowing down operations.

Additionally, they needed to improve lead management and client interaction via WhatsApp.
