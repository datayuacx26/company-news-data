## **The Challenge: Scaling Merchant Acquisition Quickly**


During its expansion in Colombia, Addi needed to accelerate merchant acquisition. Relying on email for communication wasn’t delivering strong engagement or conversion rates.


“WhatsApp is widely used in Latin America, and its open rate is significantly higher than email. At Addi, we have an open rate of over 65% on WhatsApp, compared to only 6% on email,” shared Matías Vélez, Head of SMBs at Addi.
