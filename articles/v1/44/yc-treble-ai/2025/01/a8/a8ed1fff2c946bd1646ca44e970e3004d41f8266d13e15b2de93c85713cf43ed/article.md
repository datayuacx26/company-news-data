## **The Challenge: Disconnected Communication and Operational Inefficiencies**


Dentalia’s expansion brought a series of challenges.


With over **60 clinics operating** , managing communication became increasingly complicated. Each clinic operated its own WhatsApp Business account, using different and non-standardized processes.


This resulted in **inconsistent experiences for patients** , who often waited hours for a response. Additionally, the lack of centralized metrics made it impossible to evaluate the performance of interactions or identify areas for improvement.


**“Each clinic had its own way of responding to patients,”** explained Santiago. “We had no visibility over response times or consistency. This made it impossible to guarantee a unified experience.”


The problem didn’t stop there. Receptionists spent hours manually confirming appointments—a tedious process prone to errors that diverted valuable human resources.


“Phone confirmations were not only inefficient but also error-prone,” Santiago added. “We knew we needed a more agile solution.”


The COVID-19 pandemic made things even more complex. The **need for remote communication became critical** , and Dentalia realized that a centralized system integrating their WhatsApp communications with HubSpot was essential.


This shift was not only necessary to optimize operations but also to maintain the trust and personalized care patients valued.
