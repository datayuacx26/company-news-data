# How Boston Medical Group Transformed Patient Care with Treble

In healthcare operations, response time can be the difference between a patient staying in the conversation and abandoning the process. As contact volume grows, responding quickly stops being a nice-to-have and becomes essential to delivering a good experience.


That was the scenario at[Boston Medical Group.](https://bostonmedical.com.co/)


Specializing in the diagnosis, treatment, and research of leading male sexual dysfunctions, the company needed to serve more people faster, without sacrificing close, high-quality care.


**The solution came with Treble.**


## ********The challenge: serving a high volume of patients without losing agility********


Boston Medical Group is an international medical center that has been operating since 1997, with more than nine clinics in Colombia and a scientific research center.


As the operation grew, the volume of people contacting the call center increased significantly. And with that growth came a challenge common to many organizations: responding fast enough to avoid losing opportunities.


In practice, every minute of waiting increased the chances that a patient would abandon contact before care even began.


To change this, the company needed a solution capable of completely transforming its care dynamics.


### Serving patients faster


In an operation with a high volume of contacts, reducing response time was essential to prevent prospective patients from dropping off before the first point of contact.


### Simplifying team communication


Beyond gaining speed, it was important to have an intuitive platform that would let the team work with more autonomy and deliver faster care to patients.


### Integrating care with the CRM


Centralizing all information into a single flow would help track each patient’s history with more context, organization, and continuity throughout the process.


### Implementing a solution quickly


Another key aspect was finding a platform that could be implemented quickly and integrated into the team’s routine without complex rollout processes or lengthy training periods.


More than just speeding up responses, Boston Medical Group was looking for a solution that could keep pace with the operation’s growth without compromising quality of care.


### ****How Treble helped Boston Medical Group transform its care****


Treble brought together exactly the qualities Boston Medical Group was looking for: fast implementation, ease of use, and integration with the tools the company already used.


In a short time, the team incorporated the platform into its care routine without facing complex implementation processes or long adaptation periods.


Beyond its ease of use, CRM integration made it possible to centralize information and track each patient’s history within a single flow.


As a result, care gained organization, context, and speed, allowing the team to respond to patients far more efficiently.


### Automation where it adds value. Human care where it’s needed.


Among all the platform’s features, one stood out especially for the Boston Medical Group team: transfer to an agent.


When the situation calls for it, the patient can be quickly routed to an advisor, resulting in a smoother conversation tailored to their needs.


This balance between automation and human care generated important benefits for the operation:


- More agile processes.
- Shorter wait times.
- More organized care.
- A better experience for patients.
- Greater efficiency for the contact center.


In practice, technology began supporting the team without replacing human contact—an especially important aspect in a healthcare operation.


### A simple implementation from day one


Ease of implementation was also one of the aspects most valued by Boston Medical Group.


Because the platform is intuitive, the team was able to incorporate it quickly into their daily work, without needing advanced technical knowledge.


In addition, integration with the CRM they already used made it easier to track interactions and keep all information organized in one place.


This combination of simplicity and integration accelerated platform adoption and delivered results in a very short time.


## **The results achieved**


Shortly after implementing Treble, Boston Medical Group was already seeing significant changes in its contact center’s routine. Care became faster, the team gained efficiency, and patients began receiving responses in far less time.


Key results include:


- A reduction in average response time from approximately two hours to under one minute.
- WhatsApp campaigns with response rates above 90%.
- More agile, organized care.
- Integration between communication and the CRM.
- Greater efficiency in contact center management.
