# Fulcrum Insider – Issue 62

Location data quietly shapes crew productivity, planning accuracy, and public safety, whether teams realize it or not. Issue 62 of the Fulcrum Insider covers reliable geospatial data, seamless GIS workflows, and where AI actually belongs in the field. Three real-world stories round it out, showing what happens when location records fail the people who depend on them.


## Inside Issue 62


- **Empowering field teams while strengthening your Esri core.** Learn how field teams deploy simple data capture tools without adding a lengthy dev cycle or extra burden on GIS staff.
- **How reliable geospatial field data boosts field ops performance.** Discover how accurate location data eliminates the hidden workarounds draining crew productivity and shift capacity.
- **Vibe-coded vs. purpose-built.** See why custom field apps built on a purpose-built platform deliver stronger data quality and governance.
- **AI in the field: co-pilot, not autopilot.** Understand how AI sharpens worker judgment in high-stakes work without taking humans out of the decision seat.
- **Building a seamless GIS workflow from field to office.** Standardization, customization, and integration together eliminate the manual handoffs that corrupt geospatial data.
- **What we’re reading.** A North Dakota derailment, thousands of Texas pipeline strikes, and a wave of autonomous drone data all trace back to one root cause: faulty location records.
