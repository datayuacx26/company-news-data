# Fulcrum Insider – Issue 63

GIS reliability and EPA-driven inspection demand define this month’s field operations conversation. Issue 63 of the Fulcrum Insider tackles AI and data quality gaps while stretching your Esri investment further. We also build the case for a dedicated Field Operations Management platform. Three headlines close out the issue, each one tracing back to unreliable field data.


### Inside Issue 63


- **Tackle Field Operations Management to drive better AI, data quality, and safety.** Watch live as Field Operations Management confronts GIS and EAM to close crews’ toughest data and safety gaps.
- **Why the field needs a Field Operations Management platform.** Discover the case for software purpose-built for crews, standardized and integrated like every other department’s tools.
- **Empowering field teams while strengthening your Esri core.** Learn how mobile data collection extends Esri infrastructure without piling more work onto GIS staff.
- **How reliable geospatial field data improves field operations performance.** Uncover how trustworthy location data eliminates the hidden workarounds costing crews time and missed deadlines.
- **What the EPA water infrastructure funding push means for your field operations.** See how standardized workflows help environmental firms absorb a surge of permitting and inspection work.
- **What we’re reading.** A shuttered FEMA data system, a stalled state wetlands program, and a utility wildfire lawsuit all circle back to unreliable field records.
