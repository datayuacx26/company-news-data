To scale AI successfully, firms need to stop using fragmented generic point tools and start using platforms with Firm AI.


[Firm AI](https://www.intapp.com/downloadable/firm-ai/) — a category of AI built for the business of the firm — understands the context of your deals, relationships, and investment methodology. It runs operational work and grows your firm without adding headcount.


Platforms with Firm AI unify data, execute workflows, and play by your compliance rules so your firm can safely unlock value across the entire deal lifecycle — not just in isolated use cases.


Keep reading to learn about the top ways platforms with Firm AI help your firm pull ahead of its competitors by driving value at each lifecycle stage.


### Origination


[Identifying the most profitable opportunities](https://www.intapp.com/video/discover-opportunities-ai-driven-origination-private-capital/) before competitors is significantly easier and faster when you have a tech platform built with Firm AI since it can source and score pipeline opportunities for you using your firm’s investment history.


These platforms also speed up deal intake by automatically extracting data from teasers and CIMs, then mapping it into deal records.

Investor relationships are among a[private capital](https://www.intapp.com/private-capital/relationship-management/) firm’s most valuable assets — and among the hardest to manage due to siloed data and slow manual processes.[Platforms with Firm AI](https://www.intapp.com/private-capital/relationship-management/) change that. - Assemble reports using current portfolio data and flag variances that need human review
- Surface the warmest path to any contact across your firm’s network

### Due diligence


The due diligence stage generates enormous amounts of information that investment teams need to synthesize quickly.[Platforms built with Firm AI](https://www.intapp.com/webinar/dealcloud-celeste-ai-private-capital/) can do the heavy lifting. Here’s what they do to help:


- Synthesize email threads, interaction history, and deal notes into a single brief on demand
- Auto-generate first-pass LBO models that help teams reach initial decision faster
- Surface deal-risk signals so professionals can focus where it matters most
- Expedite past-deal analysis by connecting new evaluations to past IC decisions and pass notes


This saves teams vast amounts of time, and helps them arrive at every decision point with a more complete picture than your competition.


### Exit planning


A strong exit is as much about identifying the right buyers as it is about timing the market. Here’s what platforms with Firm AI do to help you achieve both:


- Help teams find buyers by matching assets to buyer profiles using your firm’s relationship and deal data
- Prioritize exit-buyer candidates based on deal history and market activity
- Automate deal marketing material creation so teams can focus on positioning
- Update deal records in real time without interrupting the flow of work


These capabilities create a more efficient, data-driven exit process that helps your firm move faster and maximize deal value.


### Conversational AI: The critical capability needed at every lifecycle stage


Platforms with Firm AI also provide teams with another highly valuable feature: conversational AI. Available across every lifecycle stage, it gives your teams instant access to your firm’s intelligence through natural language.


Professionals can ask questions or make requests in plain language, then get immediate answers grounded in firm data and governed by access controls. Here are examples of what your teams can ask conversational AI to do:


- “Summarize everything we do about the Avenrock deal ahead of Thursday’s IC.”
- “Identify and prioritize exit-buyer candidates for Drydale Corp. based on our deal history and market activity.”
- “Find companies that match the profile of our last three closed deals.”
- “Draft deal marketing outreach for Genrite Corp. using the latest financials and our standard template.”


Conversational AI substantially reduces time spent searching across systems, reports, and notes, and gives your firm an edge by providing professionals with critical, real-time intelligence right when they need it.
