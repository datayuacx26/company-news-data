# Lyra Schramm joins Intapp as Chief People and Places Officer

**Palo Alto, Calif. — July 13, 2026** —[Intapp](https://www.intapp.com/) (NASDAQ: INTA), the leading governed AI platform for professional firms in highly regulated industries, today announces[Lyra Schramm](https://www.intapp.com/profiles/lyra-schramm/) has joined the company as Chief People and Places Officer.


“We are thrilled to welcome Lyra to the Intapp leadership team,” said John Hall, CEO of Intapp. “As we continue to scale and advance our AI platform for professional firms, Lyra’s leadership will be instrumental in attracting, developing, and retaining the talent required to deliver on that mission.”


Schramm is a global Chief People Officer and human capital strategist who partners with founders, CEOs, and boards to scale technology companies through moments of hypergrowth, disruption, and transformation. She specializes in building the talent engines, operating models, and leadership teams required in AI-driven and innovation-led environments.


“Intapp has built something rare: a platform that genuinely matters to the professionals who use it, backed by a leadership team with a clear vision for where AI is taking the industry,” said Schramm. “I’m energized by the opportunity to build the organization and talent capability that will carry Intapp through its next stage of growth.”


Prior to joining Intapp, Schramm was the Chief People Officer at F5, and previously held leadership positions at Google, Gates Foundation, and Amazon.


**About Intapp**


Intapp (NASDAQ: INTA) is the governed AI platform for professional firms in highly regulated industries. Intapp’s vertically tailored agentic solutions are built for the specialized workflows, complex relationship networks, and professional compliance requirements of accounting, consulting, investment banking, law, private capital, and real assets firms. By applying Firm AI to core processes and data, Intapp helps partners, dealmakers, and advisors drive firm growth, manage compliance, and improve profitability.
