### What actually goes wrong


Consider a lawyer screened off a litigation matter because the firm represents the opposing party. In the document management system, the screen holds. They cannot open those files. Then they open an AI assistant, pull matter documents into a shared workspace, and invite a colleague who happens to be walled off from that matter. The information has just moved through a channel that the firm’s walls never see.


The screen did its job in the DMS. The exposure reappeared one layer up. An AI-enabled lateral conflict, a privileged-information leak through a shared workspace, or an outside counsel guideline (OCG) violation that surfaces in audit becomes a partner-room event, and every new AI tool the firm adopts widens the surface where it can happen.


Native AI controls handle part of the picture. Every AI platform ships with controls for authentication and workspace permissions, and they handle those jobs well for a single tool. Ethical walls ask for something more specific: lateral-hire screens, matter-level confidentiality, waiver-driven restrictions, and controls on material nonpublic information (MNPI), enforced consistently and logged for audit across every system the firm runs. Secure a second AI tool with native controls alone and the policies begin again in a separate console, rebuilding tool by tool the fragmentation the firm spent years removing.


### How Intapp Walls and Harvey work together


With the integration, firms sync their existing Intapp Walls policies directly into Harvey, and Harvey enforces them automatically across Harvey Assistant, Vault, and Spaces — the surfaces lawyers use to research, draft, and analyze every day.


Harvey inherits walls directly from the source. It never creates, changes, or deletes them. It enforces the policies Intapp Walls already manages. When access to a resource cannot be confirmed, Harvey blocks it. For compliance teams, that means one authoritative set of policies, zero parallel administration, and full consistency between what the firm’s system of record says and what Harvey enforces.


- **Enforce your walls automatically:** When a wall is created or updated in Intapp Walls, Harvey syncs it automatically. Lawyers see only the matters Walls has authorized. Firms can extend Harvey to every practice group while the walls they already validated apply from the first day, with no new round of IT configuration or compliance review.
- **Stay audit-ready from day one:** Every enforcement event traces back to both systems. If a matter’s confidentiality is ever challenged, the conflicts team can produce an unbroken record showing exactly which wall policy governed Harvey’s behavior, down to the sync timestamp. This gives the firm the evidence it needs for internal stakeholders, regulators, and courts.
- **Keep collaboration governed:** Your walls follow the work automatically. Self-maintaining walls add and remove people based on time or document activity, and matter teams can request and approve access within policy, without a compliance ticket for every change. Sensitive matters stay protected while the firm keeps moving.


### One policy layer for every AI tool


Intapp Walls enforces one firm-wide policy set across Harvey, Microsoft Copilot, and Intapp Celeste today, with additional platforms on the roadmap. Firms define a barrier once and it applies everywhere, added to each new tool through simple API configuration.


Adding the fourth AI tool looks like adding the first. As the firm’s AI footprint grows, the governed layer grows with it, and the audit trail stays in one place. Firms do not have to bet on which AI tool wins. Walls extends to whichever ones they choose.
