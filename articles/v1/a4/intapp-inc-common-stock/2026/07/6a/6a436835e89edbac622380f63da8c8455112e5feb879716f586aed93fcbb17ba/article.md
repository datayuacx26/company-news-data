# Intapp announces general availability of Celeste, the expert AI coworker for professional firms

*Celeste runs the business of the firm — from deal screening and conflicts clearance to business development and lateral hiring — on each firm’s own data, under its own rules*


**PALO ALTO, Calif. — July 15, 2026 —** Intapp (NASDAQ: INTA), the governed AI platform for professional firms in highly regulated industries, today announced at its global launch broadcast that Celeste, previewed in February, is now generally available. Celeste is an expert AI coworker that runs the business of the firm, from deal screening and conflicts clearance to business development and lateral hiring. It’s grounded in each firm’s proprietary knowledge and governed by the firm’s own methods and compliance rules.


Most firms today have AI agents on every desk — drafting, modeling, and reviewing faster than ever. But few firms are growing faster. Firms are finding that AI built for the individual practitioner speeds up the practice, but not the business behind it. That’s the gap Celeste closes.


The work that runs a firm depends on what only the firm knows: its deals, clients, matters, and relationships. It runs on the firm’s own methods — the way it originates a deal, runs a conflict check, or prepares for a pipeline meeting. And it’s bound by obligations that generic AI was never built for, from ethical walls and conflicts rules to client confidentiality. Intapp has spent 20 years building that knowledge, those methods, and that governance into the systems these firms already run on. Celeste starts there.


That’s what makes Celeste different.


- **Celeste gets the firm’s work done.** Celeste is an expert AI coworker built for the business workflows of the firm — the work that drives its growth and determines how efficiently it runs. It runs on its own rather than waiting to be asked.
- **Celeste works your way.** Celeste encodes the firm’s own methods as playbooks — multi-step workflows that automate the firm’s best practices. It ships with a library of playbooks spanning origination, business development, intake, conflicts, pricing, fundraising, lateral hiring, and beyond, and a no-code builder so teams can easily create their own.
- **Celeste knows your firm.** Every firm has access to the same models. What sets a firm apart is what those models don’t know: its deals, clients, relationships, and prior judgment. Celeste is natively integrated into the firm’s systems of record, ingests the firm’s documents, and connects to its preferred data partners through open connectors.
- **Celeste enforces your guardrails.** Ethical walls, need-to-know rules, and audits apply to Celeste exactly as they apply to people. Restricted information stays restricted no matter what anyone asks, and the firm can show clients and regulators a full record of every action.


Celeste is already at work in early-adopter firms, including Hg and BakerHostetler. In private capital, Celeste turns a confidential information memorandum into a structured investment screen, drawing on the firm’s own investment criteria, mandate, and deal history. In investment banking, it maps board and management relationships to give bankers the strongest path into a mandate. In legal, it surfaces relevant matters, partner experience, and prior pitch materials for an urgent client request in minutes rather than hours. It also runs an end-to-end lateral hire lifecycle, from candidate research and conflicts pre-screening to clearance preparation and onboarding. For accounting and consulting firms, the same governed coworker works within independence and conflicts obligations from day one.


Intapp calls this Firm AI, the category of AI aimed not at the individual or the work product, but at the business of the firm itself. Horizontal assistants speed up everyday tasks. Practice AI helps produce the work product. Firm AI runs the business behind both.
