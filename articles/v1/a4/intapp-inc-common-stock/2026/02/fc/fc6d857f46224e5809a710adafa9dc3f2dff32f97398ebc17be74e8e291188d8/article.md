# Intapp announces Partner Forum 2026 Award winners

*Awards celebrate our partners’ dedication to innovation, collaboration, and client success*


**Palo Alto, Calif. February 26, 2026** ––[Intapp](https://www.intapp.com/) (NASDAQ: INTA), the leading governed AI platform for professional firms in highly regulated industries, today announced the winners of its Partner Forum 2026 Awards. The awards recognize demonstrable client success in five categories: Data Intelligence, Integration Excellence, Client Impact, Deal Catalyst, and Industry Showcase.


The 2026 winners are:


- **Moody’s: Intapp Data Intelligence Award** —[Moody’s](https://www.moodys.com/) was chosen for its excellence in integrating its data to deliver deeper insights, enhanced decision-making, and measurable value to clients.


- **FINTRX:** **Intapp** **Integration Excellence Award** —[FINTRX](https://www.fintrx.com/data/registered-investment-advisor-database?utm_source=adwords&utm_term=registered%20investment%20advisor%20list&utm_medium=ppc&utm_campaign=LM+%7C+RIA+(saved+draft)&hsa_src=g&hsa_ver=3&hsa_ad=793934665676&hsa_mt=b&hsa_cam=23490672955&hsa_tgt=kwd-354278429&hsa_acc=4534892163&hsa_kw=registered%20investment%20advisor%20list&hsa_net=adwords&hsa_grp=192055550076&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=23490672955&gbraid=0AAAAADqM7mb8q8UWfsKvNwXGIAT7trdgH&gclid=CjwKCAiAv5bMBhAIEiwAqP9GuBmG7mUajPnvGCHKXlRAetZDNraVfiF7tka32onU4jlnrE2P8INocxoCsSYQAvD_BwE) was chosen for its innovative integration, solving complex business challenges, and uncovering entirely new use cases that expand the value of Intapp’s platform.


- **ROOX:** **Intapp** **Client Impact Award** —[ROOX](https://www.roox.pt/en/) was chosen for its ability to deliver exceptional client outcomes using Intapp solutions, enabling long-term success.


- **Microsoft:** **Intapp** **Deal Catalyst Award** —[Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us) was chosen for its ability to align teams, accelerate momentum, create a compelling joint value proposition, and help bring deals across the finish line.


- **Harbor:** **Industry Showcase Award** ––[Harbor](https://harborglobal.com/) was chosen for its ability to make a meaningful impact through industry expertise to help clients navigate complexity and achieve better outcomes.


“We’re thrilled to recognize and celebrate our award winners who go above and beyond to deliver innovation, impact, and real results for our mutual clients,” said Sebastian Hartmann, Vice President of Alliances and Partners at Intapp. “Our partner ecosystem is a true force multiplier for Intapp — helping us win client deals, new logos, expand platform adoption, and accelerate time to value.”


Partner Forum 2026 Award winners in the Data Intelligence, Integration Excellence, and Client Impact categories were selected by a panel of judges based on self-submitted award applications, partnership performance metrics, and results delivered against client objectives. The Deal Catalyst and Industry Showcase award winners were nominated and selected by Intapp leadership.
