# Intapp DealCloud named Deal Origination Solution of the Year: Credit at the 2025 Private Equity Wire U.S. Awards

*DealCloud’s AI-driven deal and pipeline management capabilities recognized forexcellence among service providers in the private equity market*


**Palo Alto, Calif., November 7, 2025** —[Intapp](https://www.intapp.com/) (NASDAQ: INTA), a leading global provider of AI-powered solutions for professionals at advisory, capital markets, and legal firms, announced today that[Intapp DealCloud](https://www.intapp.com/private-capital/private-equity/) was named Deal Origination Solution of the Year: Credit at the[2025 Private Equity Wire U.S. Awards](https://awards.privateequitywire.co.uk/us-awards-2025?registration_source=pew-site) .


Chosen by private equity industry voters, the awards recognize excellence in fund managers and service providers in the United States. More than 8,000 votes were cast to determine this year’s winners.


“Private credit has become a core private capital strategy for global investors, and a core specialization within our private capital solutions at Intapp,” said Rudy Saad, Intapp’s Global Head of Private Equity and Private Capital Markets. “We’re proud that DealCloud has once again been recognized by Private Equity Wire, following prior wins of the same awards in both the U.S. and Europe. DealCloud’s AI-powered solutions unify the specialized workflows and data that underpin private credit origination, enabling firms to grow and compete more effectively in this sector.”


[DealCloud](https://www.intapp.com/private-capital/private-equity/) is an AI-powered platform that unifies relationship, deal, and third-party market data to deliver actionable intelligence across the deal lifecycle. By automating data capture and applying AI to a firm’s collective knowledge, DealCloud helps teams spot the right opportunities sooner, capitalize on their relationship ecosystems, collaborate more effectively, and take smarter, faster action on investments.


DealCloud was also shortlisted in four other categories in the 2025 Private Equity Wire U.S. Awards. These include Deal Origination of the Year: Technology, Solution Provider of the Year: AI and Data Analytics for Deal Sourcing, Technology Innovation of the Year: AI, and Technology Innovation of the Year: Overall.


To learn how DealCloud helps firms successfully source and close more deals, visit[intapp.com/dealcloud](https://www.intapp.com/dealcloud/) .
