# Avent Is Heading to ISA26 — Come See Us at Booth #1009

We're excited to announce that Avent will be at ISA26, and we'd love to see you there. Come find us at Booth #1009 — we'll have live demos of the platform running, and our team will be on hand to talk shop about AI, automation, and the future of distribution.


## Our Workshops


We're not just setting up a booth — we're running two sessions you won't want to miss.


### Your AI is Only as Smart as Your Dumbest Supplier


Tuesday, April 21 — 3:48 to 4:03 PM, Networking Lounge


AI can only deliver results if the data feeding it is reliable. In this talk, we'll break down how distributors can close the gap between messy supplier data and accurate, automated quoting — and what it takes to make AI actually work in the real world of industrial distribution.


### \[HANDS-ON\] Kill Your Busywork: Build a Custom AI Agent for Your Distribution Team in 15 Minutes


Wednesday, April 22 — 10:33 to 10:48 PM, Networking Lounge


This one is interactive. We'll walk you through building a custom AI agent tailored to your distribution workflows — live, in 15 minutes. Whether it's quoting, order entry, or inbox triage, you'll leave with a working prototype and a clear picture of what automation can look like for your team.
