# Introducing Miniblinks

Today we’re thrilled to introduce miniblinks: the fastest, most precise way to distribute onchain experiences for your users.


Miniblinks give dapps greater reach by teleporting actions and buttons across the internet. Effortlessly ⚡.


What are miniblinks, and how can you get started?


#### What are miniblinks?


Blinks shared on Twitter are link previews with superpowers: image, title, description, & a suite of actions you can take.


With miniblinks, you can now bring individual actions and buttons from other apps right into yours. They let your users do more onchain without leaving your app.


#### DRiP x Mallow


As a part of the launch, we’ve teamed up with DRiP and Mallow to showcase what miniblinks can do. Every Mallow auction linked on DRiP is now a miniblink, letting DRiP users bid on auctions without leaving DRiP.


#### Building with miniblinks


Miniblinks are just blinks that target specific actions, and are as easy to integrate. Target an existing blink URL, add the new selector to choose the specific action you want, & you’re done. ✅


Miniblinks also come with all blinks capabilities, including customizable styles & more.


The same blink URL powering bidding in DRiP still works on Twitter, & everywhere else. That means any existing blink, of the 450+ in production today, can be used to build miniblinks.


Mint, swap, buy, trade and vote from anywhere.


#### Composability, accelerated


Composability is a crypto superpower, and blinks are its accelerant. With miniblinks, developers now have greater control & flexibility to integrate precise experiences from across the ecosystem.


Devs: you can get started with miniblinks today. Check out our docs and the examples in our github.
