# New Orleans businesses can reduce energy costs during peak summer demand

Summer is often the most expensive operating season for commercial buildings. Higher temperatures, increased cooling demand and longer operating hours can drive energy costs while placing additional strain on building systems.


For many businesses, opportunities to improve efficiency—and reduce operating costs—aren’t always easy to identify. That’s where Energy Smart can help.


Whether you manage a small business or a large commercial facility, Energy Smart provides expert guidance to help uncover energy-saving opportunities, prioritize upgrades and connect you with available rebates and incentives.


**Focus on the systems that have the greatest impact**


Cooling systems account for a significant portion of summer energy use. As equipment ages, performance can decline, causing systems to work harder and consume more energy.


For larger commercial facilities, improvements such as chiller tune-ups, chiller rebuilds and cooling system optimization can help:


- Improve system efficiency.
- Reduce energy waste.
- Extend equipment life.
- Improve reliability during periods of peak demand.
- Lower operating costs.


These upgrades can also help reduce the risk of unexpected downtime during the hottest months of the year.


**Small upgrades can deliver meaningful savings**


Not every efficiency improvement requires a major investment.


Many businesses still operate outdated lighting systems that use more energy and require ongoing maintenance. Upgrading to energy-efficient LED lighting and installing occupancy or scheduling controls can help:


- Reduce energy consumption.


- Lower maintenance costs.
- Improve operational control.
- Deliver immediate savings.
- Improve visibility and comfort


For many small businesses, lighting upgrades are one of the simplest and most cost-effective ways to improve efficiency.


**Get more value from every energy investment**


Energy efficiency upgrades can improve comfort, control, equipment life and overall building performance while reducing energy costs.


Available rebates can help lower project costs and improve the return on investment for qualifying upgrades. Whether you’re considering cooling system improvements, lighting upgrades or smart controls, Energy Smart can help identify solutions that align with your business goals.


**Work with an energy advisor**


Every facility is different, and the most effective efficiency improvements depend on your building, equipment and operational needs.


Energy advisors work with businesses of all sizes to:


- Identify high-impact efficiency opportunities.
- Improve building performance and reliability.
- Prioritize upgrades based on your goals and budget.
- Connect with available rebates and incentives.

Reducing energy costs starts with understanding where your building can operate more efficiently.
