The Gulf South region offers many attributes companies look for when making major location decisions, including energy infrastructure, pipeline capacity, access to maritime ports, rail lines, highways and the Mississippi River. These advantages become even more important as companies look for places where they can scale their operations quickly and reliably. The Gulf South region offers low energy costs, available land, a capable workforce, proximity to feedstocks and access to infrastructure built for energy-intensive operations.


With advancements like artificial intelligence and the rapid development of data centers, policymakers and energy leaders are focusing on how the electric grid can keep pace while continuing to deliver reliable service at affordable rates for customers.


Entergy is preparing for what’s next by investing in a stronger, more modern electric grid while balancing reliability, sustainability and affordability for all customers. In 2025 alone, Entergy contributed more than $145 million in economic impact across the region through philanthropic efforts alone.
