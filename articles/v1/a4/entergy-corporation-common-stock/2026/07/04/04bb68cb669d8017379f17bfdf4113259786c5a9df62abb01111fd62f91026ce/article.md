# Entergy Mississippi prepared for storms as the height of 2026 hurricane season approaches

JACKSON, Miss. – As the height of the hurricane season approaches, Entergy Mississippi is prepared for summer storms, including hurricanes, and encourages customers to prepare as well. Scientists anticipate the[2026 hurricane season](https://tropical.colostate.edu/forecasting.html) will have somewhat below-normal activity, with nine named storms expected, including four hurricanes – one of which could become a major hurricane. Regardless of how the season unfolds, we are ready — and we want customers to be ready, too.


“As we enter the peak of hurricane season, our focus remains on delivering safe, reliable and affordable energy to the communities we serve,” said Kamisha Quates, vice president, reliability. “Our teams work year-round to strengthen our systems, train our crews and enhance our emergency response plans so we can restore power quickly and safely after storms impact our communities.”


To enhance the reliability of our energy grid, Entergy continues to invest in infrastructure improvements, including upgrading existing systems, implementing vegetation management, and deploying smart grid technologies. These efforts aim to minimize storm-related outages and ensure reliable service, while keeping customer costs as low as possible.


“Reliability remains at the core of how we serve customers, especially as severe weather becomes more frequent,” said Quates. “We’re building on the progress we’ve made, improving our grid and training our teams so we can respond quickly and safely when severe weather impacts our communities.”


**Prepared for hurricane season**


We take a proactive, year-round approach to storm readiness. Ahead of the 2026 summer storm season, Entergy Mississippi completed a series of actions to strengthen our preparedness, including:


- Inspecting utility poles regularly and replace damaged or broken poles when necessary
- Inspecting transmission lines, facilities and power plants in preparation for extreme weather conditions
- Working proactive maintenance plans throughout the year
- Completing additional vegetation trimming to reduce tree-related outages
- Reviewing emergency response plans and positioning crews and equipment


Employees also took part in staging center training, logistics and resource management exercises and a companywide hurricane drill that simulated a major storm making landfall. These sessions help reinforce consistent procedures, improve coordination across teams and ensure our workforce is fully prepared before severe weather arrives.


**Customers should prepare now**
Customers should take steps to prepare their homes and families for potential hurricane impacts. Entergy’s[Storm Center website](https://www.entergy.com/stormcenter) provides guidance on building an emergency plan, assembling a disaster supply kit and safely operating backup generators. Customers are also encouraged to download the Entergy mobile app and sign up for outage text alerts to stay informed during severe weather.


Before storms strike, customers can also verify contact information on[myEntergy.com](https://www.myentergy.com/s/) , so they receive our notifications. Customers can report an outage quickly and easily through our digital options – using either our[free mobile app](https://www.entergy.com/app) , online at[myEntergy.com](https://www.myentergy.com/s/) or by texting OUT to 36778.


“Hurricanes can be unpredictable. We are committed to strengthening the grid and preparing our teams – but preparation is a shared responsibility,” said Quates. “When our customers take steps to prepare early, we are all better positioned to weather any storm.”


**About Entergy Mississippi**


Entergy Mississippi provides electricity to approximately 459,000 customers in 45 counties. Entergy Mississippi is a subsidiary of Entergy Corporation. Entergy generates, transmits and distributes electricity to power life for more than 3 million customers through our operating companies in Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi and Texas. We’re focused on keeping costs for our customers as low as possible while providing reliable energy that our communities count on. We’re also investing in growth for the future with a more resilient, cleaner energy system that includes modern natural gas, nuclear and renewable energy generation. As a nationally recognized leader in sustainability and corporate citizenship, we deliver more than $100 million in economic benefits each year to the communities we serve through philanthropy, volunteerism and advocacy. Entergy is a Fortune 500 company headquartered in New Orleans, Louisiana, and has approximately 12,000 employees.
