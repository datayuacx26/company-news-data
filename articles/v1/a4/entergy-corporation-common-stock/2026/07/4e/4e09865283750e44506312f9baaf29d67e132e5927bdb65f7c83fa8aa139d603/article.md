# Entergy Texas provides $500,000 in summer bill relief to help vulnerable customers

For many of our neighbors, summer doesn’t just bring higher temperatures, it brings higher electricity use. Extreme heat often means running air‑conditioning longer and harder, which can lead to higher energy bills. For those on fixed incomes, higher summer bills can mean making difficult choices between paying for basic needs and keeping their homes cool.


That’s why Entergy Texas is expanding its bill support


through its


[The Power to Care program](https://www.entergy.com/care) . As part of our ongoing commitment to keeping bills affordable, we’re providing an additional


**$500,000 in summer bill relief** to help more qualifying elderly and disabled customers stay safe and supported during the hottest months of the year.


The additional funding comes from proceeds raised during Entergy Texas’ 2025 The Power to Care Golf Charity Classic and supplements the program’s year-round assistance.


Funded by donations from Entergy employees, customers and shareholders, The Power to Care is administered by local partner agencies that provide direct financial assistance to customers age 60 and older, as well as individuals with disabilities that are facing hardship or in jeopardy of having their electricity cut off due to non-payment.


“Summer can be especially tough for customers already trying to stretch every dollar,” said Shirlene Cook, president of The Power to Care Board of Directors. “This extra support lets us show up for our most vulnerable neighbors when they need it most. No one should have to choose between essential needs and staying cool and safe.”


**Local nonprofit partners within Entergy Texas’ service territory will distribute the additional funding, including organizations such as Interfaith of The Woodlands.**


“We couldn’t be more thrilled. This funding means we can help even more seniors who really depend on this support.” said Jennifer Huffine, vice president of community impact at


Interfaith of The Woodlands


. “We’re always looking for additional resources. A contribution of this size will go a long way in helping us show up for our neighbors in need.”


Existing The Power to Care recipients and eligible customers can begin applying for the extra help on


**August 3** . To find a participating nonprofit partner that can help you apply,

**About Entergy Texas**


Entergy Texas provides electricity to approximately 538,000 customers in 27 counties. Entergy Texas is a subsidiary of Entergy Corporation. Entergy produces, transmits and distributes electricity to power life for 3 million customers through our operating companies in Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi and Texas. Its customers are connected to the Midcontinent Independent System Operator Inc. power grid, which is a regional transmission organization responsible for administering the transmission systems of member utilities in 15 states stretching across the central region of the United States and Manitoba, Canada. We’re investing for growth and improved reliability and resilience of our energy system while working to keep energy rates affordable for our customers. We’re also investing in cleaner energy generation like modern natural gas, nuclear and renewable energy. A nationally recognized leader in sustainability and corporate citizenship, Entergy delivers more than $100 million in economic benefits each year to the communities we serve through philanthropy, volunteerism and advocacy. Entergy is a Fortune 500 company headquartered in New Orleans, Louisiana, and has approximately 12,000 employees. Learn more at


[entergytexas.com](https://www.entergytexas.com/) and connect with


[@EntergyTX](https://www.entergy.com/social) on social media.
