# Entergy Texas helping customers stay cool and save money with bill management tools and resources

THE WOODLANDS, Texas – The heat of summer can increase electricity use and energy costs, Entergy Texas is committed to helping customers stay cool and save money throughout the hotter months with bill management tools and resources. Our online[Bill Toolkit](https://billtoolkit.entergy.com/) connects customers to energy efficiency tips and resources, as well as information about bill management and financial assistance options.


**Managing bills through payment options**


We offer several flexible payment options so customers can choose when, where and how they receive their bills.


- [Pick-A-Date](https://www.myentergy.com/s/pickdate) allows customers to pay their bills when it works best for them.
- [Level Billing](https://www.myentergy.com/s/levelbill) allows customers to “level out” seasonal energy use fluctuations, making their bills more consistent every month.
- [PaperFREE](https://www.myentergy.com/s/paperfree) billing allows customers to get their bills emailed as soon as they post and instant access to two years of billing history.
- [AutoPay](https://www.myentergy.com/s/autopay) allows customers to avoid late fees, writing checks and paying for postage by having bills automatically deduct from their bank accounts.


Entergy Texas encourages customers who may need additional payment options to reach out. To see review qualifications for deferred payment arrangements or payment extensions, visit[our mobile app](https://www.entergy.com/app/) or[myEntergy](https://www.myentergy.com/s/) .


**Finding financial assistance**


For customers needing help paying their bills, we’re here to help. Throughout this summer, Entergy Texas is implementing a series of measures through our “Beat the Heat” program focused on helping our low-income customers and communities stay cool and pay their bills. The program provides bill payment assistance, fans and energy efficiency kits and support from local community partners to help hundreds of residential customers across the state.


In addition, Entergy Texas partners with local and state organizations to connect qualifying customers with financial assistance, including:


- [The Power to Care program](https://www.entergy.com/care/) provides emergency bill payment assistance to older adults and customers with disabilities.
- [Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program](https://billtoolkit.entergy.com/) or LIHEAP provides financial assistance for energy bills and other energy-related expenses.
- [Single Stop](https://app.singlestop.org/entergy/) makes it easy for customers to quickly and confidentially check their eligibility for federal, state and local financial assistance.


**Tracking energy use through myAdvisor**


Customers can set electric usage alerts and monitor how much energy they use each day through bill management tools like myAdvisor which is available through their[myEntergy](https://www.myentergy.com/s/login/) account online. Tracking usage over time can help customers identify trends that contribute to higher usage and budget their monthly expenses. With the myAdvisor dashboard, customers have access to not only usage and cost details, but also bill history and projections, analyzer tools and more. On the Entergy mobile app, this information can be found by clicking the “usage” tab.


For more ways to save energy and money, customers can visit[BillToolkit.entergy.com](https://billtoolkit.entergy.com/) .


**About Entergy Texas**
Entergy Texas provides electricity to approximately 538,000 customers in 27 counties. Entergy Texas is a subsidiary of Entergy Corporation. Entergy produces, transmits and distributes electricity to power life for 3 million customers through our operating companies in Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi and Texas. Its customers are connected to the Midcontinent Independent System Operator Inc. power grid, which is a regional transmission organization responsible for administering the transmission systems of member utilities in 15 states stretching across the central region of the United States and Manitoba, Canada. We’re investing for growth and improved reliability and resilience of our energy system while working to keep energy rates affordable for our customers. We’re also investing in cleaner energy generation like modern natural gas, nuclear and renewable energy. A nationally recognized leader in sustainability and corporate citizenship, Entergy delivers more than $100 million in economic benefits each year to the communities we serve through philanthropy, volunteerism and advocacy. Entergy is a Fortune 500 company headquartered in New Orleans, Louisiana, and has approximately 12,000 employees.
