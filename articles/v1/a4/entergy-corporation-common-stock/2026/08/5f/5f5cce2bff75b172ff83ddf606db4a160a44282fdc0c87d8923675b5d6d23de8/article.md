# Entergy’s John Hudson honored with the 2026 Whitney M. Young, Jr. Service Award

Scouting America’s Southeast Louisiana Council has selected John O. Hudson III, chief external affairs officer and president of the Entergy Charitable Foundation, as one of the 2026 Whitney M. Young Jr. Service Award honorees. T he other honorees include Constable Lambert C. Boissiere Jr., attorney Kim M. Boyle and Dr. Margaret Montgomery.


This prestigious recognition, presented by Scouting America’s Southeast Louisiana Council, celebrates individuals whose lifelong commitment to equity, opportunity and community advancement reflects the enduring legacy of Whitney M. Young Jr.


Hudson’s dedication to strengthening communities is evident across every facet of his leadership. Through his roles at Entergy, he has championed initiatives that expand access to critical resources, empower underserved populations, and uplift families throughout the Greater New Orleans area and the Gulf South region. His work has helped drive meaningful change for youth and families, ensuring more people have opportunities to thrive.


> “Whitney M. Young Jr. was a leader who believed in the power of opportunity, equity and service. He understood that real progress happens when we uplift others, create pathways for people to succeed, and ensure that every child — no matter their background — has a chance to thrive. Tonight’s honor is meaningful because it reflects those values, and it underscores the work so many people here continue to champion every day,” said Hudson.


Additional Entergy employees who volunteer with Scouting America also attended the ceremony to celebrate Hudson’s recognition. Among them were Nicholas Hamilton and Phillip Durocher, whose ongoing involvement with Scouting America reflects Entergy’s shared commitment to youth development and community service.


A tireless advocate and thoughtful mentor, Hudson is known for leading with compassion, integrity and an unwavering commitment to service. Under his guidance, Entergy’s charitable programs have grown in impact and reach, supporting education, workforce development, economic mobility, and community resilience. His leadership continues to inspire colleagues across the company and partners throughout the region.


The Whitney M. Young Jr. Service Award highlights Hudson’s deep and lasting influence on the young people served by Scouting America. His long-standing support for youth development, mentorship, and community-building efforts aligns closely with the values at the heart of the award.
