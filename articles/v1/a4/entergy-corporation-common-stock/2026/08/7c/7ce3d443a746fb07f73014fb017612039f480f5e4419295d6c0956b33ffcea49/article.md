# Fight the humidity, love the savings

The dog days of summer are here — and so is the humidity. While you can’t control the weather, you can take steps to keep your home cool, comfy and dry. One of the easiest and most affordable solutions is to use a room dehumidifier.


Dehumidifiers work by removing excess moisture from the air in your home. The result? Dryer, more comfortable air that reduces the risk of mold and mildew build-up. Dryer air also means your home’s cooling system doesn’t have to work as hard to remove moisture, which saves energy while improving cooling performance.
