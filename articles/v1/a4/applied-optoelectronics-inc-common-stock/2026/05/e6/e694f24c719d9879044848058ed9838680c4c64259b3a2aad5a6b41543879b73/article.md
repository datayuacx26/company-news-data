# An Ecosystem Built for DOCSIS 4.0 Execution: Mediacom + AOI + Digicomm

AOI is working with Mediacom to accelerate DOCSIS 4.0 deployment through distributed access architecture (DAA), 1.8GHz amplifier technology, and scalable HFC network upgrades.


Delivering faster internet at scale takes an ecosystem that can execute market by market, with the right architecture, the right hardware, and the right supply chain behind it. That’s the model taking shape as ****


[AOI works with Mediacom](https://newsroom.ao-inc.com/news-releases/mediacom-advances-its-docsis-4-0-network-upgrades-plans-with-aoi-quantum-bandwidth/) to accelerate upgrades and continued expansion of Mediacom’s fiber and coax infrastructure.


**Mediacom’s DOCSIS 4.0 Upgrade Roadmap**
Mediacom, serving more than three million homes and businesses across 22 states, has laid out a clear roadmap: upgrade its network to deliver faster upload and download speeds to 1 million homes by the end of 2026. Using a distributed access architecture (DAA) with DOCSIS 4.0, fiber expansion, and a whole-home Wi-Fi 7 solution, deployments are already underway in key markets with continued rollout planned across Mediacom’s Midwest and Southeast footprint.


**AOI’s Role in Mediacom’s HFC Network Modernization**
AOI’s role in that roadmap is focused and operational: as the primary vendor supporting Mediacom’s upgrade across its legacy Gainmaker and Motorola system amplifier holdings, AOI is providing solutions from its ****


**[Quantum Bandwidth portfolio](https://ao-inc.com/solutions/cable-broadband/)** including the Quantum18™ MB 1.8GHz Amplifier, Quantum18™ BLE 1.8GHz Line Extender, and QuantumLink™ remote management and monitoring software. Together, these technologies are engineered to support next-generation HFC performance with cleaner signals, improved reliability, and a scalable path to future capacity.


**Digicomm Supports DOCSIS 4.0 Supply Chain Readiness**
Execution at this pace also depends on availability. That’s where Digicomm International, AOI’s CATV distribution partner, comes in—supporting Mediacom with immediate product availability and logistics services from its stocking distribution center in Englewood, Colorado. Over the past 12 months, Digicomm has shipped several hundred thousand AOI 1.8GHz amplifiers to multiple North American operators, reinforcing the supply chain readiness needed for large-scale DOCSIS 4.0 deployments.


**Accelerating Broadband Expansion Without Full Fiber Replacement**
Together, Mediacom, AOI, and Digicomm are aligning operator strategy, network innovation, and distribution readiness—creating a practical upgrade engine for DOCSIS 4.0 that can bring gigabit-class performance to customers sooner, without waiting for full fiber deployments.
