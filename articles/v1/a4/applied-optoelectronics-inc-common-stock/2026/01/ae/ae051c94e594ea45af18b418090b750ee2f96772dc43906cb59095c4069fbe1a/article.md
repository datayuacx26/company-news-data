**1. Vendor consolidation** ****


Margins are tightening, R&D costs are rising, and operators are demanding more integrated, end-to-end solutions rather than


point

**2. AI-driven outside plant management (OSP)** ****


OSP has traditionally been reactive —


identify


a failure, dispatch a crew, restore service. That model is rapidly becoming unsustainable as networks grow


denser,


and customer expectations rise. Expect to see transformation of outside plant operations through remote management, preventative maintenance, and AI.

a central role
