**Atlantis Paradise Island**


Atlantis Paradise Island in the Bahamas is a vibrant oceanside resort blending Bahamian culture, bold renovations, and reimagined guest experiences. The resort’s heart lies in its people, sustainability, and immersive programs connecting guests to the islands’ history, art, cuisine, and celebrations. Home to Aquaventure, a 141-acre waterpark with thrilling slides, river rides, 14 pools, and five miles of white-sand beaches, Atlantis also features the world’s largest open-air marine habitat, housing over 65,000 aquatic animals. Dolphin Cay, its renowned marine conservation and education center, offers up-close encounters with marine life. Accommodations span five distinct options: the iconic Royal, family-friendly Coral, water-side Harborside Resort, luxurious Cove, and residential-style Reef. Guests can enjoy over 40 restaurants, bars, and lounges, including celebrity chef venues like *Fish by José Andrés* , *Nobu by Nobu Matsuhisa* , and *Paranza by Michael White* . Additional amenities include the Mandara Spa, new tennis and pickleball center, basketball courts, 500-seat movie theater, Atlantis Kids Adventures, CRUSH teen club, golf by Tom Weiskopf, the Atlantis Casino, luxury shopping, and new attractions like Good Lies Mini-Golf and Lost City Escapes.
