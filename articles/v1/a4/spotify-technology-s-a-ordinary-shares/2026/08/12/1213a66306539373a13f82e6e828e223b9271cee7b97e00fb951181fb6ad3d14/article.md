# The Production Unit Behind Stray Kids’ Biggest Hits Takes the Mic

Every song has a story, but fans rarely get to hear it from the creators themselves. K-Pop giant JYP Entertainment is pulling back the creative curtain with its new video podcast show,


[JYP Podcast](https://open.spotify.com/show/033GExaONGcSNNvGj5ujUa?si=8553170da5704d41) , bringing audiences closer to the music and stories behind some of their favorite artists.


The show will feature a rotating lineup of the label’s artists, kicking off with


*3RACHA Session* , a six-part series hosted by 3RACHA, the production unit from


[Stray Kids](https://open.spotify.com/artist/2dIgFjalVxs4ThymZ67YCE?si=UeucohR_RbS80UfMBx06LQ) . Every Monday fans can join 3RACHA members


[Bang Chan](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5jRUIqBSxmsBPNiEwKUjgZ?si=Tj-tY1lLTE6g_2E4iKu1Jg) ,


[Changbin](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3XSid6KaiKoMAVZs2ug3yw?si=lWLeS6eNQ3up-JIHx379gA) , and


[HAN](https://open.spotify.com/artist/46YvTuKiPBUu5KP9818J2F?si=bvadX-zYQCGOrxzSJf9IqA) for a deep dive into their songwriting world.


Think of it as getting inside the group chat. “The three of us talking about music or random stuff is already what we do on a daily basis,” said Bang Chan. “On


*3RACHA Session* , fans can experience what goes on in our minds and how we experience the world around us.”

## **Producers take the mic**


As Stray Kids’ creative subunit, 3RACHA has played a pivotal role in the group’s global rise. But how does a song go from an idea to a smash hit?


*3RACHA Session* is built to answer that question, exploring the conversations, inspirations, and everyday moments that shape their music.


Each episode brings Stray Kids fans—aka STAY—into the studio conversations to get a firsthand look at 3RACHA’s creative process. Conversations flow from one topic to the next, giving STAY an inside look at how their favorite songs get made.


“The podcast reminded me how much the three of us genuinely enjoy talking with each other,” said Changbin. “Although we create the same music together, it’s our different thoughts and perspectives that lead to great synergy.”


The


*JYP Podcast* is the latest addition to Spotify’s growing library of K-Pop video content, giving fans around the world even more ways to connect to the artists and creative worlds they love, all in one place.


“Even after spending 10 years together, we got to hear stories we had never shared before, which made this experience feel fresh,” said HAN. “I’m grateful for the opportunity to spend this time together, and I hope STAY will enjoy it as much as we did.”
