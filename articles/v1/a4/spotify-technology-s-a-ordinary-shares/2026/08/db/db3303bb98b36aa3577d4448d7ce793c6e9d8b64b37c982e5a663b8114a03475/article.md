# A Night at KLUB KATSEYE

On Tuesday, more than 400 New York City-based


[KATSEYE](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3c0gDdb9lhnHGFtP4prQpn) fans—aka EYEKONS—packed Brooklyn’s Elsewhere for a special listening event celebrating their upcoming EP,


[WILD](https://open.spotify.com/prerelease/0NwDekr0r8Rqy2Kq1u5q3x) . For one night only, the venue was transformed into KLUB KATSEYE, an immersive takeover complete with hands-on experiences and, of course, a special performance from the group themselves.

[PINKY UP](https://open.spotify.com/track/4KmkJjHTNlr1jFY56Lyz4E?autoplay_ok=1) ,” “

Before the show, we caught up with KATSEYE to talk about the music that inspired


*WILD* . Tracks that topped the list included “


[Feel It Still](https://open.spotify.com/track/6QgjcU0zLnzq5OrUoSZ3OK?autoplay_ok=1) ” by


[Portugal. The Man](https://open.spotify.com/artist/4kI8Ie27vjvonwaB2ePh8T) **,** “


[Never Be the Same](https://open.spotify.com/track/4eWQlBRaTjPPUlzacqEeoQ) ” by


[Camila Cabello](https://open.spotify.com/artist/4nDoRrQiYLoBzwC5BhVJzF) **,** and “


[Don’t Cha](https://open.spotify.com/track/1gZ7i4qxXkHZb1r6eioaAP) ” by


[The Pussycat Dolls](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6wPhSqRtPu1UhRCDX5yaDJ) **.** They also revealed that “


[I’m a Slave 4 U](https://open.spotify.com/track/6ldwfK0yWgTAlmIfuQkTYN) ” by


[Britney Spears](https://open.spotify.com/artist/26dSoYclwsYLMAKD3tpOr4) heavily influenced their soon-to-be-released music video for “


[Hootie Frutti](https://open.spotify.com/track/6oUUmBcUbZa5O48V5pjgAD) .


”
