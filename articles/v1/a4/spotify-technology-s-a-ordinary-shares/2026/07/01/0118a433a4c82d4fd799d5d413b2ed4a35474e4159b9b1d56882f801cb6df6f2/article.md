# How the World’s Biggest Soccer Tournament Soundtracked the Summer

As the world’s biggest soccer tournament unfolded across North America this summer, fans turned to Spotify to soundtrack every match. A record-breaking official anthem, fan-adopted favorites, and a surge in playlist creation made the 2026 tournament the most musical—and most streamed—yet. Here’s what you need to know about the music behind the game.


## **Spain won, but Shakira stayed undefeated**


Is it really a soccer summer


[without Shakira](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-07-07/shakira-global-football/) ? Among the tournament’s official songs, “


[Dai Dai](https://open.spotify.com/track/0kosUz0jePvjiz4ctmR6wL?si=9203068de1264965) ” by


[Shakira](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0EmeFodog0BfCgMzAIvKQp) and


[Burna Boy](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3wcj11K77LjEY1PkEazffa) led the way, generating more than 138 million streams on Spotify—the most ever for an official song. That’s more than six times the streams of the most-played official track in 2022, which of course is also Shakira’s: “


[Waka Waka (This Time for Africa)](https://open.spotify.com/track/4B0ScPwmG3oAOg9Ek7ODdm) ,” at 22 million. The event also introduced millions of listeners to tournament music, with nearly 50 million fans streaming an official song or anthem on Spotify for the first time.


## **Everyone sang their team’s song**


Fans also rallied around their own team’s anthems—both official and unofficial—with underdogs and perennial contenders alike driving major streaming surges:


-


- **Paraguay:** “


[Soy Albirrojo](https://open.spotify.com/track/5XtQMHRRNoJpEPGSamUQU3?si=2c8c8b87072c4fe7) ” by


[Los Albirrojos](https://open.spotify.com/artist/13WuVZguTRvJub257oaNyy) saw the tournament’s biggest lift among team anthems, up 1,170% globally.


- **Norway:** “


[Alt For Norge](https://open.spotify.com/track/3E5IeN5MqUs16rjvMRlaT1) ” by


[Drillos](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0gSgXDEsDJj4ZsMclAGjeh) and


[Jørn Lande](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3aOVLHmQbwbOq4nl4XcGSC) rose 1,005% globally as the team’s run captured fans’ attention.


- **Mexico:** Fans boosted “


[Cielito Lindo](https://open.spotify.com/track/3XMHjiioE0J85S3B92flxj) ” by 540% globally.


- **England:**[Oasis](https://open.spotify.com/artist/2DaxqgrOhkeH0fpeiQq2f4) ’ “


[Wonderwall](https://open.spotify.com/track/1qPbGZqppFwLwcBC1JQ6Vr?si=517da3bddd5e468c) ,” which fans sang to the team after wins, saw U.K. streams rise 306% above average on July 6 before reaching No. 2 on Spotify’s Global Chart on July 12. That same day, U.K. streams of


[The Beatles](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3WrFJ7ztbogyGnTHbHJFl2) ’ “


[Hey Jude](https://open.spotify.com/track/1eT2CjXwFXNx6oY5ydvzKU) ”—a tribute to England superstar


**Jude Bellingham** —were up 282% versus average.


- **United States:** On July 1, when a confident U.S. team dispatched Bosnia and Herzegovina to advance to the quarterfinals, their newly-adoped jam—


[John Denver](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7EK1bQADBoqbYXnT4Cqv9w) ’s “


[Take Me Home, Country Roads](https://open.spotify.com/track/1YYhDizHx7PnDhAhko6cDS?si=69ac20695c3f4a6f) ”—saw stateside streams jump 100% above average on July 1.


## **Playlists scored**


Beyond individual songs, fans created a record-breaking 219,000 tournament-themed playlists during the event, nearly triple the total from 2022. More than 181,000 listeners created a soccer-themed playlist for the first time. Together those playlists generated more than 172 million streams worldwide, compared with 46 million in 2022.


## **The final matched up legends—and musical superstars**


As Spain and Argentina advanced to the final, fans rallied behind both teams with music. Listeners embraced Spanish hits like


[ROSALÍA](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7ltDVBr6mKbRvohxheJ9h1) ’s “


[DESPECHÁ](https://open.spotify.com/track/53tfEupEzQRtVFOeZvk7xq) ” and


[Aitana](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7eLcDZDYHXZCebtQmVFL25) ’s “


[SUPERESTRELLA](https://open.spotify.com/track/6hpuesKPNa3WhV48O7Fa47) ,” while streams of Argentina’s signature team track,


[La T y La M](https://open.spotify.com/artist/1FxPMQ9A0882eNDx3ZkD6B) ’s “


[Pa’ la Selección](https://open.spotify.com/track/6RG3opSuPJQy4igij9iJEj?si=12c1db12807e4de9) ,” jumped more than 270% globally and 386% among listeners in Argentina.
