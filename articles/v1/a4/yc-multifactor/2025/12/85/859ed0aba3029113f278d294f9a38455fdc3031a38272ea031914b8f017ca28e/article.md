# Multifactor raises $15M seed round to make online accounts safe for AI agents

# Multifactor raises $15M seed round to make online accounts safe for AI agents


2025-12-04


[Company](https://multifactor.com/blog/tags/company)[Funding](https://multifactor.com/blog/tags/funding)


[VN Vivek Nair Co-Founder & CEO](https://multifactor.com/blog/authors/vivek)


We are passionate about defending our digital world. Over the past ten years, we’ve watched identity become the single most exploited weakness across government, enterprise, and consumer systems - and the rise of agentic AI has created an urgent need for security foundations that simply don’t exist.


That’s why we started Multifactor, and today we’re proud to share that we’ve raised a $15M seed round led by Nexus Venture Partners, with participation from amazing funds like Y Combinator, Taurus Ventures, Honeystone Ventures, Flex Capital, Pioneer Fund, Ritual Capital, Liquid2 Ventures and leading operators across security, AI and enterprise software including Mohan & Padma Warrior, Gokul Rajaram and Mathilde Collin. We’re building Multifactor as a public benefit company because securing the modern web isn’t just a market opportunity; it’s a responsibility. Thank you to our early users, advisors, and investors for believing that identity security can - and must - be fundamentally better.


We’re focused on a simple truth: AI agents, and the people who use them, can’t safely collaborate online with yesterday’s tools. Passwords and legacy identity systems were never designed for the AI era and agents acting on our behalf. Our goal with Multifactor is to bring mathematically provable guarantees to everyday accounts, so you can share access without exposing credentials, whether the collaborator is a human or AI.


We’re scientists at heart - curious, mission-driven, and transparent. We publish research, contribute open source, and hold ourselves accountable when we miss. That’s the culture we’re scaling with this round: a team that pursues hard problems with rigor, communicates plainly about what’s possible, and ships technology that stands on first principles.


This funding helps us move faster on the work that matters most: advancing Multifactor's core technologies, expanding our APIs and enterprise controls, and growing a world-class research and engineering team. If you share our conviction that identity should be your strongest defense - not your biggest vulnerability - come build with us. The future of zero-trust is within reach, and we’re committed to delivering it.


# We're redefining zero-trust — so you can protect your accounts with confidence.


Identity is your first and last line of defense, and the root cause of most application security breaches. Multifactor's provably secure zero-trust solutions cryptographically guarantee that only authorized users can access sensitive data, turning identity into your greatest asset in the fight against cyber threats. Learn more about our research, or reach out to explore working together.
