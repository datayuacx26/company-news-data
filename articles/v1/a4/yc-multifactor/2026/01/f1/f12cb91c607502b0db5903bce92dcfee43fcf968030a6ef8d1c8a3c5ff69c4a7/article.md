When people first hear about Multifactor, they ask, "Is it like LastPass or 1Password?"


The answer is: **Multifactor is what a password manager would be if it were invented today, built for the future of digital identity.**


Traditional password managers were a necessary first step, but they haven't innovated to meet the demands of the modern web. They solved a simple problem—storing passwords—but they've been utterly outpaced by a world that uses passkeys, TOTP codes, AI agents, and complex sharing.


## Your Classic Password Manager: A Single Feature


Password managers are stuck in the past. They focus almost exclusively on helping you track a **single factor** (the password) and provide a clunky, unreliable login experience:


- **Clunky Login:** They often fail to fill forms correctly, forcing you to copy/paste or manually hunt for an account. Even worse, you *still* have to reach for your phone or open another app to get your secondary factor (like a TOTP code). **You waste time every single day.**
- **Limited Scope:** They don't reliably manage **all** your identity data—passkeys, credit cards, addresses, and secondary factors are often handled by separate, clunky tools.
- **No Autopilot:** They offer no tools to actively protect your accounts. You still have to manually rotate passwords or search for two-factor upgrades.


Multifactor delivers a **superior user experience** even just as a login tool:


### The Multifactor Difference: Login on Autopilot


Multifactor is an Account Manager designed to unify and secure your entire digital identity. Forget fumbling with apps and codes.


1. **True One-Click Login:** Whether a site uses a password, a passkey, an OAuth button, or a magic link, Multifactor handles the **entire authentication flow** in one click. We are the only platform that seamlessly manages and deploys **all** your authentication factors—passwords, passkeys, TOTP codes, and more—automatically, eliminating the need to ever reach for your phone again.
2. **Universal Identity Vault:** We don't just store secrets; we secure your whole identity. Addresses, payment information, notes, and credentials are all managed securely and reliably auto-filled with security that is **end-to-end encrypted** using advanced protocols like MFKDF.
3. **Cross-Platform, Securely Synced:** With native apps and extensions on all platforms, your fully E2EE vault is available instantly, everywhere you need it.

## The Fundamental Problem: Sharing Secrets


The most glaring flaw of traditional password managers lies in **sharing** .


**The fundamental problem: Password managers share credentials, not capabilities.**


When you use a password manager to share access, you are sharing the **full, unrevocable secret** .


### Sharing with a Traditional Password Manager


Let's say you want to share your Netflix account with your family:


1. You share your Netflix password with your spouse.
2. They save it in their vault.
3. **Now two people have your full Netflix credentials, and you have lost control.**
4. If you break up or their account gets hacked, they still have complete access to watch content, **change your billing info, or cancel your subscription.**
5. Your only option? Change the password everywhere—a huge pain.


### Sharing with Multifactor: Control and Capability


Instead of sharing secrets, we share **functionality** . Multifactor's revolutionary sharing platform treats account access like a Google Doc.


1. You create a share link for your Netflix account.
2. You set **granular permissions** : "Can watch content, **cannot change payment method** ."
3. Your family member clicks the link and gets **exactly those capabilities** —no password revealed.
4. If circumstances change, you **revoke access with one click** .
5. **No password changes needed anywhere.** Access is gone—instantly, even if they're mid-stream.

## Built for the Agentic Era


This difference matters more than ever as we enter the "agentic era"—a time when AI agents can take actions on your behalf. **You cannot safely give full credentials directly to an AI system.** They can be tricked into exfiltrating secrets, and you're trusting the security of the model provider.


Scenario Traditional Password Manager Multifactor


**Hiring a Freelancer (Adobe)** Share full Adobe Creative Suite password. They can now access your billing, change your plan, and see all your work. Give freelancer access to "edit specific projects only." They **cannot** see billing, other projects, or settings. Revoke instantly when the job ends.


**Delegating to an AI Agent (Google)** Give AI agent your Google password to manage your calendar. Agent can now read your Gmail, access your Drive, and delete your photos. Give AI agent " **calendar view and edit** " permissions only. Agent helps schedule meetings, **cannot** access email, files, or any other Google services.


Multifactor's secure, granular access control is the **only safe foundation** for AI-powered productivity.

This level of active account maintenance is impossible with passive password managers.

Password managers taught us to secure our credentials. **Multifactor teaches us to secure our capabilities.**


In a world defined by AI agents, remote teams, and digital sharing, that difference is everything.
