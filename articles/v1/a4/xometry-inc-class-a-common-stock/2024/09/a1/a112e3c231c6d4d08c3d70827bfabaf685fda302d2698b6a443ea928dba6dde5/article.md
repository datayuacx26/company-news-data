# Xometry at IMTS: Commercial Supply Chain Solutions, Laser Tube Cutting Quotes, and More

Every two years, the International Manufacturing Trade Show (IMTS) heads to Chicago, filling the Windy City with industrial professionals, entrepreneurs, and hobbyists interested in learning about the companies and technology changing the manufacturing sector.


For a few more days this week, you can find Xometry and Thomas in the West Hall at booth #433224. Xometry’s digital manufacturing marketplace and Thomas’s supplier services are the perfect combination for IMTS. Whether you’re a buyer or supplier, we have all the services you might need — from commercial supply chain solutions to helping reach a diverse set of buyers.


## Get an Instant Laser Tube Cutting, Bending Quote


Xometry is at the forefront of artificial intelligence (AI) for building accurate pricing models and lead times. Recently, we launched quoting for[laser tube cutting and bending](https://www.xometry.com/capabilities/laser-tube-cutting-services/) on the Xometry Instant Quoting Engine®. We’re the first digital marketplace to achieve this feat.


At Xometry’s IMTS booth, you can chat with our expert team about getting a laser tube cutting and bending quote, but you can also see Xometry’s capabilities in action with a special part made by The Fabrication Series (TFS) using Xometry.

## Helping Companies, Customers Help Themselves


At Xometry, we want to help customers and businesses optimize their processes. That’s why we built[Teamspace](https://www.xometry.com/xometry-enterprise/teamspace/) , an on-platform way for team members to view and collaborate on quotes and orders. Whether you're a purchaser, engineer, quality, or management, the Teamspace tool brings transparency to Xometry projects.


On day four of IMTS, Xometry’s Director of Applications Engineering & Marketing, Greg Paulsen, also hosted a session on various manufacturing processes, as there are more options than ever when it comes to manufacturing. During the session, “Should I Print, Mold, or Machine This Part?” Paulsen explored the strengths and trade-offs of additive manufacturing, molding, and CNC machining.
