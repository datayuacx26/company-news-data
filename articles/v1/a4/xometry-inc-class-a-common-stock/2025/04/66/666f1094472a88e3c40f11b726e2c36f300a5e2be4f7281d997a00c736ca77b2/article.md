# How to Handle Supply Chain Disruption

Having a resilient supply chain is essential to staying competitive. A trusted network of suppliers empowers your business to be flexible and adapt to unforeseen circumstances in a way that no single supplier can. With more than 5,000 global manufacturing partners, Xometry helps customers adapt to supply chain changes like reshoring, scaling, or tariffs, so they can stay agile, minimize risk, and maintain an advantage over competitors.


Here’s how Xometry gives you an edge when it comes to safeguarding your supply chain against disruption.

## Sourcing Flexibility: Adapt to Changing Trade Policies


Tapping into Xometry’s network of manufacturing partners means you’re not limited by the capacity or capabilities of local suppliers. Having an array of sourcing options means you can adjust to changing trade policies without compromising quality.


## Uncompromising Compliance: Vetted Suppliers, Guaranteed Quality


Some of the biggest names in the automotive, aerospace, and defense industries trust Xometry to deliver the parts they need. Xometry puts all its suppliers through a vetting process to guarantee quality results.
