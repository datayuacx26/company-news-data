# Designing Accessible 3D Printing Products for People with Disabilities

Prosthetic arms and legs are incredibly helpful solutions for people with limb differences, leaving them better equipped to tackle a variety of challenges. Unfortunately, their high cost and limited designs can keep them from being the best tool for every situation that people with limb disabilities face on a day-to-day basis.


In this recorded webinar, Xometry's Greg Paulsen and listen in to a panel discussion focused on how to design human-friendly assistive devices. CNN journalist and filmmaker Miles O'Brien and the founders of Blue Heart Hero, Amin Hasani and Jed Tango join the panel for a thought-provoking discussion about engineering products that are inclusive and accessible for people with disabilities. Designers who want to create products that will empower people with limb differences to navigate their environments are sure to love this discussion.
