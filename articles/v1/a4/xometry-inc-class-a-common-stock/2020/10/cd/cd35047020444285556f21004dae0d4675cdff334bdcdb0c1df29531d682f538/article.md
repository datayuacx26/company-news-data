# Design Guide: HP Multi Jet Fusion (MJF) 3D Printing

HP's Multi Jet Fusion is an extremely exciting, robust, and flexible addition to Xometry's manufacturing capabilities. It's excellent for prototyping, small batch production runs, or as a bridge process to injection molding. It lets you get a feel for how your parts will perform with minimal upfront costs and significant economies of scale. This guide will be an excellent reference resource when optimizing your designs for the process.
