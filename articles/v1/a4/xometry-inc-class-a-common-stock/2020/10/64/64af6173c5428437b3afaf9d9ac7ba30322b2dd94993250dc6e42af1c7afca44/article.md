# eBook: Custom Manufacturing for Aerospace and Defense

Aerospace and defense manufacturing demands uncompromising standards. From hardware traceability and specialized workholding to strict ITAR and AS9100D compliance, managing these complex details can make it difficult to secure reliable, high-quality components on schedule.


Download your copy of this eBook for actionable insights on navigating these rigorous standards. Inside, you will discover best practices for optimizing your 3D CAD models and technical drawings, guidance on certifications and inspection options, and how to leverage our platform for your sourcing needs.
