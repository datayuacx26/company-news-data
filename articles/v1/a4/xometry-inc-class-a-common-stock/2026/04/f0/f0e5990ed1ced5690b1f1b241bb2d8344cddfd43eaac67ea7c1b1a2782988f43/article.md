# T slot Aluminum: Extrusions and Framing

## What Are the Key Components of a T Slot Framing System?


The key components of a T Slot framing system are listed below.


- **Aluminum Profiles** : Aluminum profiles are the primary structural extrusions used to form the frame in T-slot aluminum systems. The extrusions come in a wide range of shapes and sizes, allowing engineers to choose the appropriate profile based on the expected load and specific design needs. The strength of the profiles forms the foundational support for the entire assembly, ensuring stability and durability in a variety of industrial applications. Creating machinery, workstations, or enclosures, aluminum profiles provide the framework for safe and efficient operations.
- **T-Nuts** : T-nuts are specialized nuts that slide into the T-slots of the profiles to create a secure threaded mounting point for hardware. The nuts are held within the channel geometry when tightened, providing secure and stable connections. Available in steel and stainless steel, T-nuts offer corrosion resistance and strength, making them ideal for a variety of environments. The components play a crucial role in ensuring that all parts of the frame remain firmly in place.
- **Fasteners** : Fasteners, including bolts and screws, are used to secure components together through the T-nuts. Different fastener head styles, such as socket head or button head, are chosen based on clearance and access needs. High-tensile steel fasteners are commonly used in T-slot aluminum systems to ensure strong, stable joints capable of withstanding significant forces. The variety of fasteners available ensures that engineers select the optimal component for each joint, whether for heavy-duty or lighter applications.
- **Corner Brackets** : Corner brackets are L-shaped components that connect intersecting profiles at a 90-degree angle, typically mounted externally to the slot. The brackets provide additional stability to joints, reinforcing them to prevent racking and movement. In more heavy-duty applications, gusseted corner brackets offer added strength to handle greater loads and reduce stress on the frame. The use of corner brackets ensures that the frame maintains its shape and integrity even under significant weight or operational forces.
- **End Caps** : End caps are plastic covers that protect the cut ends of the aluminum profiles. The caps prevent injuries by eliminating sharp edges and provide a finished, professional look to the structure. They press securely into the center bore of the profile, ensuring that the ends are properly covered. End caps are not only functional but also contribute to the overall appearance of the frame, providing a clean and polished finish.
- **Panel Inserts** : Panel inserts, made from materials such as acrylic, mesh, or polycarbonate, fit into the T-slots to form enclosures or protective barriers. The inserts are used to create custom cabinets or guards by securing the panels into the frame with gaskets to prevent rattling and vibration. The versatility of panel inserts allows for easy modification of the frame to suit specific needs, such as protecting sensitive components or enclosing machinery for safety.
- **Hinges** : Hinges provide a movable joint that allows for the installation of doors and access hatches in T-slot aluminum frames. The hinges mount directly to the T-slots, offering easy positioning and the ability to adjust the door or hatch as necessary. Depending on the weight of the door or hatch, different load ratings for hinges are available, ensuring that the structure remains stable while providing easy access. Hinges are a key component in frames that require frequent access or movement of parts.
- **Leveling Feet** : Leveling feet are adjustable mounts designed to compensate for uneven floors, ensuring that the work surface remains level. The feet thread into the tapped ends of the profiles or mount directly to base plates. Equipped with rubber pads, leveling feet help to dampen vibration, improving the stability of the frame and the accuracy of any machinery or components mounted on it. Leveling feet are essential for applications where precision and a stable, vibration-free environment are critical.


### What Types of Fasteners Are Used in T Slotted Framing?


Types of fasteners used in T slotted framing are listed below.


- **Standard T-Nuts** : Standard T-Nuts typically slide into the open ends of T-slot profiles to provide a secure threaded connection. The design of the T-Nuts prevents rotation within the slot, making them ideal for creating permanent or semi-permanent joints. Standard T-Nuts are commonly used in situations where the connection needs to be stable and resistant to movement over time, such as in structural frames or heavy-duty assemblies.
- **Drop-In T-Nuts** : Drop-In T-Nuts are designed for easy installation at any point along the T-slot profile, offering flexibility for mid-stream additions. Not all drop-in nuts have spring-ball detents. Many rely on the geometry of the nut and friction once tightened. Drop-In T-Nuts speed up the assembly process, particularly in more complex frames where adjustments or additions need to be made quickly without disassembling the entire structure.
- **Socket Head Bolts** : Socket head bolts are a preferred fastener for high-strength connections, especially in machine bases or structural joints requiring high torque. The cylindrical head of the bolt requires a counterbore to allow for flush mounting, which gives a neat and secure fit. Socket head bolts are commonly used in applications where precision and strength are essential, as socket head bolts are designed to withstand significant stress and load.
- **Button Head Screws** : Button head screws are low-profile fasteners with a rounded, smooth head, offering an aesthetically clean look and reducing the risk of snagging on clothing or equipment. Button head screws are commonly used in applications where safety and appearance are a priority, such as in safety guards, enclosures, or decorative structures. The rounded design also makes them ideal for projects requiring a sleek and minimalistic finish.
- **Hammerhead Bolts** : Hammerhead bolts (T-bolts) are designed to slide into the slot and secure components using an external nut. They lock into place by twisting 90 degrees within the channel, allowing for rapid and efficient assembly. Hammerhead bolts are particularly useful for attaching lightweight components or accessories to the outside of a frame, providing a simple and quick solution when frequent adjustments are needed.
- **Joining Plates** : Joining plates are external components used to bridge two T-slot profiles, creating a strong and rigid connection. The plates distribute the load across a larger surface area, ensuring the connection handles significant forces without failure. Available in flat or 90-degree configurations, joining plates are used in larger frames or assemblies that require a secure and stable connection between profiles at an angle or along a straight line.
- **Set Screws** : Set screws are small, headless fasteners used to lock components in place for a flush finish. The screws are ideal for securing pins or lightweight accessories within the T-slot profiles without creating protrusions. Set screws are used with T-nuts or tapped center bores in situations where a clean, unobtrusive connection is needed, such as in precision assemblies or decorative fixtures.
- **Anchor Fasteners** : Anchor fasteners are internal connectors that provide strong, hidden joints, preserving the aesthetic of the frame by keeping the fasteners out of view. The fasteners require a counterbore in one of the profiles to house the bolt, which keeps the connection secure without disrupting the smooth surface of the frame. Anchor fasteners are ideal for applications where a clean and professional appearance is important, such as in architectural or custom-built installations where the fasteners should remain unseen.

T slot systems do not require special tools for basic assembly and modification. The connections use standard metric or imperial hex keys (Allen wrenches).
