# Steel Framing: System and Construction

### What are the Key Steps in Steel Frame Construction Methods?


The key steps in steel frame construction methods are listed below.


- **Foundation Preparation** : The foundation preparation begins with the precise installation of anchor bolts into the concrete footings. The bolts serve as the primary connection points for the main structural columns of the building. Ensuring that the bolts are correctly aligned is critical, as any inaccuracies in their positioning affect the entire frame’s structure. High-precision tools and techniques are used to place the anchor bolts in their designated positions, ensuring that the frame will be constructed accurately and support the weight of the building without shifting or failing.
- **Column Erection** : Column erection involves using cranes to lift the vertical structural members and position them onto the anchor bolts. Workers use shims and transit levels to ensure the columns are plumb and base plates are level once the columns are in place, meaning they are aligned both vertically and horizontally. Temporary cables are employed to provide initial stability during the early stages of assembly. The phase is crucial for establishing the frame's foundation and ensuring that the columns are set up correctly before the remaining structure is built.
- **Beam Placement** : Beams are then placed horizontally to connect the vertical columns, effectively creating the floor and roof levels of the structure. The beams are secured using bolts inserted into pre-drilled holes, which ensures that the beams are firmly connected to the columns. The sequence of beam placement follows a detailed engineering plan, which accounts for load distribution, the structural integrity of the building, and the sequence of construction. Each beam must be carefully positioned to ensure the stability of the entire framework.
- **Plumbing and Squaring** : The crew adjusts the frame to ensure it is vertical (plumb) and square once the initial structure is in place. It is an essential step for the proper installation of the exterior cladding, as any misalignment would affect the aesthetics and functionality of the building's outer shell. Precision measurements are taken throughout this process to verify the dimensions of the structure, ensuring that the frame meets the required specifications. The phase sets the groundwork for the next stages of construction, where exact alignment is critical for the proper fitting of other components.
- **Final Bolting or Welding** : Final Bolting or Welding: The final bolting or welding is carried out to secure all joints in place once the frame is adjusted and aligned. Workers tighten the bolts to the required tension using impact wrenches, ensuring that each connection is strong and secure. Critical joints, especially under heavy load, are welded to provide additional strength and prevent any structural weaknesses. Quality control inspectors then verify the integrity of all connections, ensuring that it meets the necessary standards for strength, safety, and durability. The final step ensures the structural frame is ready for the next phase of construction.
- **Decking Installation** : The installation of steel floor and roof decks follows the completion of the primary frame. The decks are placed over the beams, forming the base of the floor and roof systems. Shear studs are welded through the deck into the top of the beams, creating a strong bond between the decking and the frame. The process is vital for creating the base onto which concrete slabs will be poured, providing a solid foundation for the building’s interior. The decks must be securely attached to handle the weight of the concrete and ensure the structural integrity of the floors and roof.
- **Fireproofing Application** : Fireproofing is applied to the steel members to ensure the building withstands fire and maintains its structural integrity during extreme conditions. Spray-on or intumescent coatings are used to provide a protective layer that helps prevent the steel from weakening under high heat. The step is crucial for meeting building safety codes, as fireproofing is a mandatory requirement for ensuring the safety of both the building and its occupants. The fireproofing application not only protects the frame from fire damage, but it also helps to ensure the longevity and durability of the structure in emergency situations.
