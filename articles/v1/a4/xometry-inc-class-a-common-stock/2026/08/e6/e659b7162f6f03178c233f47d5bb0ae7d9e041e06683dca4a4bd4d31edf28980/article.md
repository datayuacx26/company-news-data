## What Types of Colocation Services Are Available?


Colocation services come in core formats that match different business needs. Providers offer options ranging from shared rack space to fully dedicated suites. Each format balances cost, control, and physical separation differently.


The types of colocation services that are available are listed below.


- **Rack Colocation** : Rack colocation uses shared rack space inside a multi-tenant facility. Multiple businesses house equipment within the same rack structure. Shared power and cooling resources support each housed server.
- **Cabinet Colocation** : Cabinet colocation provides dedicated cabinets for housed equipment. Locked enclosures separate equipment from neighboring tenants inside the facility. Dedicated cabinets offer added security compared with shared rack space.
- **Cage Colocation** : Cage colocation offers enclosed areas built from floor-to-ceiling wire mesh. Enclosed cages separate equipment, restricting access to authorized personnel. Companies needing strict physical separation choose cage colocation for added control.
- **Private Suites** : Private suites provide dedicated rooms built within the larger facility. Walled rooms separate equipment fully from other tenants. Private suites suit companies needing maximum control over physical access.
- **Managed Colocation** : Managed colocation includes additional support services beyond basic rack space. Provider staff handles equipment monitoring, patching, and routine maintenance tasks. Managed services suit companies lacking dedicated internal IT personnel.
