# Design Guide: Die Casting

A solid CAD model is only the beginning. Creating a truly successful die-cast part comes down to understanding how molten metal actually behaves once the tool closes. We wrote this Design for Manufacturability (DFM) guide to bridge the gap between engineering theory and the physical realities of the shop floor. Inside, we unpack the practical rules of casting geometry, from optimizing wall thicknesses and safely spacing ribs, to strategically placing gussets and eliminating subtle defects like sink marks. Download the full guide today to equip yourself with the real-world insights needed to reduce tooling costs, avoid production headaches, and engineer highly manufacturable metal die-cast components from the very first shot.
