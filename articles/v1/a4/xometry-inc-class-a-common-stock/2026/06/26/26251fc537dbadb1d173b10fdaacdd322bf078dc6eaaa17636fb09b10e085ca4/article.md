# Aerohead Manufacturing Builds Its Reputation With a Solid Foundation From Xometry

When asked what he values most about working with Xometry, Rencich points to efficiency and opportunity.


“I don't have to leave my desk to find work, and I don't have to spend countless hours quoting opportunities with only a small chance of being awarded the job,” he said. “Xometry's network of buyers is actively looking for capable manufacturing partners, and if you can consistently perform, there is a tremendous amount of opportunity available.”


Aerohead’s customers span many industries, and it has contributed to components supporting major aerospace and advanced manufacturing programs. While much of the work remains confidential, Rencich enjoys seeing projects eventually become public and knowing his team played a role in bringing them to life.


Over the years, Aerohead's commitment to quality and strong customer relationships has helped the company earn repeat business and become a trusted manufacturing partner within the Xometry network.
