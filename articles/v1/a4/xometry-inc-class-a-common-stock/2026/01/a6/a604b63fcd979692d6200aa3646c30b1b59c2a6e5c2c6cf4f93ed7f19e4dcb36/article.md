## Inconel 625


**Corrosion-Resistant Superalloy**


Inconel 625 is a nickel-chromium superalloy with outstanding resistance to corrosion, oxidation, and pitting in aggressive environments. Its high tensile strength, paired with exceptional fatigue and thermal-fatigue properties, makes it a reliable choice for components exposed to seawater, caustic chemicals, and high-temperature exhaust. It is widely used in offshore oil and gas hardware, marine propulsion systems, and chemical processing equipment, where material degradation is a constant concern.


- Excellent resistance to corrosion, oxidation, and pitting


- Strong fatigue and thermal-fatigue performance across a wide temperature range


- Well-suited for marine hardware, chemical processing equipment, and exhaust components


- Compatible with cryogenic applications down to -196°C


## Inconel 718


**High-Strength Heat-Resistant Alloy**


Inconel 718 is a precipitation-hardened nickel-based superalloy that maintains its mechanical properties at extreme temperatures up to 700°C. High tensile strength, creep resistance, and excellent fatigue life under cyclic loading make it the dominant material choice for jet engine components, which must withstand thousands of thermal cycles without failure. It is also widely used for downhole tooling in oil and gas, where high pressure and temperature are constant operating conditions.


- Retains high strength and hardness at sustained temperatures up to 700°C


- Excellent creep and stress-rupture resistance under prolonged loading


- Ideal for aerospace engine components, turbine blades, and high-temperature fasteners


- Good oxidation resistance and weldability in extreme service conditions


## Maraging Steel


**Ultra-High-Strength Tooling Steel**


This carbon-free iron-nickel alloy achieves exceptional strength and hardness through a post-print aging heat treatment. Unlike conventional high-strength steels, Maraging steel undergoes minimal dimensional change during heat treatment, making it highly predictable for precision tooling applications. It is a particularly strong fit for the tooling and mold-making industry, where insert longevity, surface hardness, and tight tolerances directly impact production output and part quality.


- Tensile strength exceeding 1,900 MPa after aging heat treatment


- Excellent dimensional stability with minimal distortion during hardening


- Superior toughness compared to other high-strength steels at similar hardness levels


- Best suited for injection mold inserts, high-performance tooling, and industrial gears


## Titanium Ti6Al4V (Grade 5)


**Lightweight Aerospace-Grade Titanium**


Ti6Al4V (Grade 5) is the most widely used titanium alloy worldwide, valued for its strength-to-weight ratio, corrosion resistance, and biocompatibility. At roughly 40% lighter than steel at comparable strength levels, it is a primary material in aerospace structural design and has become the standard for load-bearing medical implants such as orthopedic and spinal hardware. Its resistance to bodily fluids and long-term corrosion also makes it a natural fit for dental implants and surgical instruments intended for repeated sterilization.


- Outstanding strength-to-weight ratio, up to 40% lighter than comparable steel parts


- Excellent corrosion resistance, including in saline and bodily fluid environments


- Biocompatible and widely qualified for orthopedic and dental implant applications


- Ideal for aerospace structural components, motorsport brackets, and surgical implants
