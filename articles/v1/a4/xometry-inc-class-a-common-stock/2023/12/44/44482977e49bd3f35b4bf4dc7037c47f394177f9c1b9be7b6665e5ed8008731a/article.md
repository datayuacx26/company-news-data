# Listen to Xometry’s Greg Paulsen on the Being an Engineer Podcast

At Xometry, we are working hard to democratize manufacturing so everyone can easily access the tools and knowledge they need to build projects.


If you’re looking to grow as an engineer, consider listening and subscribing to the "[Being an Engineer](https://teampipeline.us/being-an-engineer-podcast/) " podcast. In this engineering podcast, host Aaron Moncur interviews the world’s highest-performing engineers, asking about their personal and professional successes and helping you apply those lessons to your life and career.


Make sure to check out their episode with Greg Paulsen, Xometry's Director of Applications Engineering. It can be found **[here](https://teampipeline.us/service-hospitality-industry/)** or in the Spotify player above.
