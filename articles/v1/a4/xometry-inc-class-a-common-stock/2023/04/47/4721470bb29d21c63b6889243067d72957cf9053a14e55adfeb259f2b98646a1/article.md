# eBook: The Ultimate Guide to the Use of Artificial Intelligence for Supply Chain Optimization

In the past five years, business adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) has more than doubled.


*The Ultimate Guide to the Use of Artificial Intelligence for Supply Chain Optimization* helps procurement professionals understand the role and benefit that various types of AI can play in industry, from more accurate forecasting to improved transparency. It provides an overview of machine learning (ML), natural language processing (NLP), big data, and advanced analytics before exploring how seven industrial sectors are applying AI today.


This guide also provides insight into how AI will be used in the future for supply chain management. Here, you’ll learn about geospatial intelligence (GEOINT), tracking the world’s oil supply, and military intelligence.


CPOs can play an integral role in reimagining and modernizing supply chains to help bolster American manufacturing. *The Ultimate Guide to the Use of Artificial Intelligence for Supply Chain Optimization* offers foundational knowledge for decision-makers seeking to safeguard our supply chains against disruptions, identify suitable suppliers, and move mundane tasks off their desks so they can focus on building projects.


Here at Xometry, we see firsthand how AI can optimize supply chains and transform businesses. After all, our marketplace is powered by artificial intelligence. It helped us create a machine-learning path toward pricing part designs and lead times quickly and accurately. Our digital solution also connects individuals and businesses with projects to manufacturing service suppliers who can help bring them from concept to finished product. This eBook will help you learn from our experts.
