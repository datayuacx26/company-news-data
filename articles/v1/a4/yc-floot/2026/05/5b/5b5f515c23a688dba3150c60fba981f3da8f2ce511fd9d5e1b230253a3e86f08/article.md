# Introducing Floot Infinity: Your AI Builder Can Now Do the Setup Work Too


Building with AI has gotten a lot faster.


You can describe an app, generate a working interface, connect a database, and ship something real in a fraction of the time it used to take.


But there’s still one part of building that slows everything down:


The setup work outside the editor.


You still have to open new tabs, find the right settings page, connect accounts, configure APIs, read docs, copy keys, set permissions, and figure out what each platform needs before your app actually works the way you want.


That’s why we built **Floot Infinity** .


**Floot Infinity** is our new Chrome browser integration that lets Floot take action across the web while helping you build.


Instead of only working inside your project, Floot can now help with the steps that usually happen around your project.


You can install it here:[https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/floot-infinity/cmcjjcdjjiggoifocialkpdgddfnaecc](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/floot-infinity/cmcjjcdjjiggoifocialkpdgddfnaecc)


## The problem with AI builders today


Most AI builders are great at generating the app.


But once your build needs something external, you’re often on your own.


Want to connect Gmail? You need to find the right Google Cloud setup pages.


Want to add Stripe? You need to configure the dashboard correctly.


Want to use a third-party API? You need to read the docs, find the auth flow, and figure out what keys or settings your app needs.


Want to recreate the feel of another website? You have to inspect the visuals yourself and translate that into design direction.


These are not always “coding” problems.


They’re setup problems.


And setup problems are often where momentum dies.


## What Floot Infinity does


Floot Infinity lets Floot use your browser so it can help with the messy in-between steps of building.


That means Floot can help:


- Find the right setup pages
- Navigate docs and dashboards
- Configure API integrations
- Connect accounts
- Inspect website visuals
- Keep your project moving when setup leaves the editor


The goal is simple: Floot should not just help you build the app. It should help you get the app working.


## Why this matters


Building real software is not just writing code.


It is connecting tools, setting up permissions, configuring services, understanding documentation, and making decisions across a dozen different tabs.


For technical teams, that work is annoying.


For non-technical builders, it can be the difference between shipping and getting stuck.


Floot Infinity makes that process feel less like “here’s the app, now good luck” and more like having an assistant that stays with you through the whole build.


## One app, infinite possibilities


Floot Infinity is a step toward a more complete AI building experience.


One where your AI builder can generate, edit, troubleshoot, configure, and help with the setup work that usually happens outside the editor.


Your AI builder can now do the setup work for you.
