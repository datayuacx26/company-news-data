## What Is an AI App Builder?


An AI app builder is a tool that helps you create software using natural language.


Instead of writing every line of code manually, you describe what you want the app to do. The AI helps generate the design, pages, logic, database structure, and sometimes the backend, hosting, authentication, and deployment setup.


A simple prompt might look like:


“Build me a CRM for real estate agents with lead tracking, client notes, follow-up reminders, and a dashboard showing active deals.”


A strong AI app builder can turn that prompt into a working product you can edit, test, and launch.


AI app builders are especially useful for non-technical founders because they reduce the gap between having an idea and getting something real in front of users.
