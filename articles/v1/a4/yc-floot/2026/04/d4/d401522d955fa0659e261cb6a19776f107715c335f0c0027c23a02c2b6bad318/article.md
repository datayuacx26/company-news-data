# Earn Free Credits When Your Friends Launch Their First Project

# Earn Free Credits by Inviting Friends


We’ve updated our referral program to reward real builds—not just signups.


Previously, you earned **100 credits per referral signup** .


Now, you earn significantly more when something meaningful happens:


- **2,000 credits when your friend publishes their first project**
- **5,000 credits when they upgrade**


### Why We Made This Change


Signups don’t always lead to anything.


Someone can create an account, click around, and never come back.


We wanted to tie rewards to the moment that actually matters: when someone builds and launches something.


Because that’s when Floot becomes useful.


### What Counts as a Referral


To earn credits, your friend needs to:


Sign up using your referral link


Publish at least one project


Once they do, you’ll automatically receive your credits.


If they upgrade later, you’ll earn additional credits as well.


### Who Should You Invite


This works best if you send Floot to people who already want to build something.


- Someone with an idea they’ve been sitting on
- A creator who wants a custom site or tool
- Anyone tired of rigid templates


The more likely they are to launch, the more you’ll both get out of it.


### Start Sharing


If you know someone who’s been meaning to build something, send them your referral link.


When they launch, you’ll earn credits.


Simple as that.


Start[building](https://floot.com/) today.
