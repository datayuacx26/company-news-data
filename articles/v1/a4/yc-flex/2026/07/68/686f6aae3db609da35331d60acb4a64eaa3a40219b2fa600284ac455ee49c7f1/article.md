### Common myths and limitations of super greens


Super greens supplements often come with aggressive marketing claims online. Understanding the limitations can help you make smarter purchasing decisions.


**Myth: Greens powders replace vegetables**


Greens powders can supplement your diet, but they should not fully replace whole fruits and vegetables. Whole foods still provide fiber, hydration, and a broader nutritional profile.


**Myth: Every greens powder is identical**


Ingredient quality varies dramatically between brands. Some formulas contain premium ingredients and third-party testing, while others contain low ingredient doses or fillers.


**Myth: Greens powders detox your body**


Your liver and kidneys naturally handle detoxification. Greens powders may support overall wellness, but they do not “cleanse toxins” in the dramatic way some advertisements claim.


**Myth: You will feel instant results**


Some people notice digestive or energy improvements quickly, while others may not feel major changes. Results depend on your diet, health status, and consistency.


**Limitation: Some products contain low doses**


Many products advertise dozens of ingredients but include very small quantities of each one. Always review the Supplement Facts label carefully.


**Limitation: Taste can vary**


Some greens powders taste earthy or grassy. Many brands now improve flavor with fruit extracts, stevia, vanilla, or cocoa blends.


**Limitation: Eligibility for HSA/FSA can be complicated**


General wellness supplements may not automatically qualify for HSA or FSA reimbursement. Certain products may require a[Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN)](https://www.withflex.com/blog/what-is-a-letter-of-medical-necessity) , especially if they are intended to support a specific medical condition.

### Do you always need a Letter of Medical Necessity for super greens powder?


In most cases, yes. Super greens powders are typically considered general wellness supplements, which means you will often need a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) to use your HSA or FSA to make the purchase.


An LMN is a document from a licensed healthcare provider stating that the supplement is medically necessary to help treat or manage a specific health condition. Since greens powders are usually marketed for overall wellness, energy, nutrition, or daily health support, they are not commonly treated as automatically eligible medical expenses under IRS guidelines.


You may be more likely to qualify for HSA/FSA reimbursement with an LMN if your healthcare provider recommends super greens powder as part of a care plan related to:


- Nutritional deficiencies
- Digestive health concerns
- Physician-directed dietary support
- Specific medical or nutritional conditions
