# Is Melatonin HSA/FSA Eligible? (& Where to Buy It)

If you struggle with occasional sleepless nights, jet lag, or changes to your sleep schedule, you may have looked into melatonin supplements. But before you use your[Health Savings Accounts (HSAs) or Flexible Spending Accounts (FSAs)](https://www.withflex.com/blog/what-are-hsas-fsas) to pay for melatonin, you should understand the eligibility rules that apply to these products.


The good news is that some melatonin products can qualify for HSA and FSA spending when they meet IRS requirements. In many cases, you may also need a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) from a licensed healthcare provider before your purchase becomes eligible.


Now let's look at how melatonin fits into HSA and FSA eligibility rules and what you should know before making a purchase.


## Is Melatonin HSA and FSA eligible?


Melatonin may qualify as an HSA and FSA eligible expense when you use it to treat a medically diagnosed sleep-related condition. In almost all situations, you'll need documentation from a healthcare provider before your purchase qualifies for reimbursement or direct payment using HSA/FSA funds.


Melatonin falls into a category known as "dual-purpose", which means the IRS does not automatically consider it an eligible medical expense in every situation. Instead, eligibility often depends on why you're using it and whether a healthcare provider recommends it for a specific condition.


For example, if you buy melatonin simply to improve your general sleep routine or support wellness goals, your HSA or FSA administrator may deny the expense. However, if a licensed provider recommends melatonin to help treat a diagnosed medical issue like insomnia or a circadian rhythm sleep disorder, the purchase may become eligible.


## Do you always need a Letter of Medical Necessity to buy melatonin with your HSA/FSA?


Yes, in most cases, melatonin requires a[Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN)](https://www.withflex.com/blog/what-is-a-letter-of-medical-necessity) for it to qualify as HSA or FSA eligible. Since melatonin is commonly considered a dual-purpose supplement with general health benefits, it needs to be prescribed by a medical practitioner as part of a patient's care plan.


Without an LMN, your FSA or HSA administrator may classify melatonin as a general wellness product instead of a qualified medical expense. That said, rules can vary between plan administrators. Some HSA/FSA providers may approve certain sleep-support products without additional paperwork, while others require an LMN for nearly all melatonin purchases.


## Types of medical conditions that qualify melatonin to be HSA and FSA eligible


Melatonin may qualify for HSA and FSA spending when a healthcare provider recommends it to treat a diagnosed medical condition. In many situations, you'll also need a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) to support eligibility.


Common qualifying conditions include:


- [Insomnia](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/12119-insomnia) — Providers may recommend melatonin to help with difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or maintaining consistent sleep patterns.
- [Circadian rhythm sleep disorders](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/circadian-rhythm-sleep-disorders) — Conditions like delayed sleep phase disorder, shift work sleep disorder, and non-24-hour sleep-wake disorder may qualify when melatonin supports treatment.
- [Jet lag disorder](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/jet-lag/symptoms-causes/syc-20374027) — Frequent or severe jet lag that affects sleep and daily function may qualify if a provider documents medical necessity.
- [ADHD-related sleep issues](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/mental-health/adhd-and-sleep) — Some healthcare providers recommend melatonin to help manage sleep disruption associated with ADHD.
- [Autism spectrum disorder-related sleep difficulties](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/physical-health/autism-and-sleep) — Melatonin is sometimes used to support sleep challenges connected to autism spectrum disorder.
- [Anxiety-related sleep disruption](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/21543-sleep-anxiety) — If anxiety contributes to chronic sleep problems, a provider may recommend melatonin as part of a treatment plan.
- **Other diagnosed sleep disorders** — Additional medically diagnosed sleep conditions may qualify when supported by provider documentation and an LMN.


Because HSA and FSA administrators can interpret eligibility differently, it's important to keep receipts and medical documentation for your records. Make sure the LMN you receive (assuming you need one) clearly explains how the treatment the item enables is connected to your specific condition.


## How to buy melatonin using your HSA or FSA directly or through reimbursement


Once you confirm that your melatonin purchase qualifies, you can usually pay in one of two ways. You can either use your HSA or FSA card directly at checkout or pay out of pocket and request reimbursement afterward.


The option that works best for you often depends on the retailer, your plan administrator, and whether your purchase requires a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN).


### 1. Pay with your HSA/FSA directly at checkout


Some online retailers and wellness stores allow you to use your HSA or FSA card during checkout for eligible purchases. This is often the fastest and most convenient option because you avoid filing paperwork later.


Here's how the process typically works:


1. Confirm that the melatonin product qualifies for HSA/FSA spending
2. Complete any required LMN or eligibility verification
3. Add the product to your cart
4. Use your HSA or FSA card at checkout
5. Save your receipt for your records


Some merchants use integrated HSA/FSA payment systems that help verify eligibility automatically during checkout. These systems can reduce declined transactions and simplify compliance requirements.

### 2. Pay out of pocket for melatonin and make a reimbursement claim


If a store does not accept HSA or FSA cards directly, you can usually pay with another payment method and request reimbursement afterward.


Most reimbursement claims follow these steps:


1. Purchase the melatonin product using a personal debit or credit card
2. Save your itemized receipt
3. Obtain a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) if required
4. Log in to your HSA or FSA provider portal
5. Submit your reimbursement request and supporting documents
6. Wait for approval and reimbursement


Reimbursement timelines vary by provider. Some plans process claims within a few days, while others may take several weeks.
