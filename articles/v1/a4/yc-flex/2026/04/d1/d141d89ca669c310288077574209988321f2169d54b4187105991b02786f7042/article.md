Let's start with whether fertility treatment is eligible, and what types of care qualify.


## Is fertility treatment HSA and FSA eligible?


Fertility treatments are often[eligible under IRS guidelines](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p969) , but eligibility depends on the specific service, product, and your medical situation.


### Commonly covered HSA and FSA fertility expenses


Many fertility-related services and products qualify without much friction.


- Fertility diagnostic tests
- Ovulation predictor kits
- Pregnancy tests
- In vitro fertilization (IVF)
- Artificial insemination (IUI)
- Fertility medications
- Lab work and monitoring
- Sperm and egg storage
- Fertility monitors


### Do you always need an LMN for fertility treatment?


You don't always need an LMN. Doctor visits, prescriptions, diagnostic tests, and IVF/IUI are typically covered. You may need an LMN for tracking devices, monitors with advanced features, at-home fertility kits, and supplements.

## Types of medical conditions that qualify fertility products and treatment as HSA/FSA eligible


To qualify for HSA or FSA spending, fertility care must relate to a diagnosed medical condition.


- [Infertility](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/infertility/symptoms-causes/syc-20354317)
- [PCOS](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/pcos/symptoms-causes/syc-20353439)
- [Endometriosis](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/endometriosis/symptoms-causes/syc-20354656)
- Hormonal imbalances
- [Low sperm count](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/low-sperm-count/symptoms-causes/syc-20374585)
- Irregular menstrual cycles
- [Reproductive disorders](https://www.cdc.gov/reproductive-health/women-health/common-concerns.html)


Eligibility comes down to medical necessity.

## 3 FAQs about using your HSA or FSA for fertility expenses


### 1. Are fertility treatments automatically covered?


Not automatically, but many qualify if they meet IRS requirements tied to diagnosing or treating a medical condition.


### 2. Does travel for fertility treatments count?


Yes, travel related to qualified fertility treatment (mileage, lodging, parking) can qualify as long as it's primarily for medical care.


### 3. Can I use my HSA/FSA for surrogacy?


Most surrogacy costs are not eligible. Some specific medical expenses related to surrogacy may qualify with an LMN.
