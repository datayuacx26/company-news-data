# E-book - Communicating architecture with the C4 model

Since IcePanel started 5 years ago, we’ve helped teams transition from unstructured, messy diagrams, to a consistent and shared source of truth of their architecture. Leveraging the C4 model helped teams reason about their systems in a way that was simple, yet detailed enough to create the right amount of guardrails.


If you care about architecture, you’ve likely seen[Luca](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucamezzalira/) on LinkedIn, YouTube, or at a conference. He has a knack for explaining complex topics in a way that’s easy to understand. A perfect fit when we were looking for someone to write an e-book about the C4 model. We’ve admired Luca’s work for a long time and are excited to partner with him to bring this book to life.


Whether you’re new to the C4 model or heard about it but haven’t used it in practice, this is the book for you. After reading this book, you should be ready to apply the C4 model in your organization to help everyone, technical and non-technical, understand what you’re building.
