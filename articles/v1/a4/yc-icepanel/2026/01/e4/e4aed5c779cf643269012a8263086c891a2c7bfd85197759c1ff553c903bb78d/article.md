# New - Embed IcePanel diagrams in EventCatalog

Level up your event-driven architecture docs with interactive IcePanel views. EventCatalog users can now embed IcePanel diagrams in domains, services, messages, and custom docs.


## What is EventCatalog? 📚


[EventCatalog](https://www.eventcatalog.dev/) is a self-hosted tool that helps teams better understand event-driven architecture. It helps teams get clarity on events, services, domains, and owners.


Teams use EventCatalog to:


- Document events, services, domains, and ownership
- Visualise and design event-driven architectures
- Understand dependencies and change impact
- Introduce governance without slowing teams down


## How does it work? 📄


Update your EventCatalog to the latest version and start using the` <IcePanel />` component. Check out the[EventCatalog docs](https://www.eventcatalog.dev/blog/eventcatalog-icepanel#how-does-it-work) for detailed setup instructions.
