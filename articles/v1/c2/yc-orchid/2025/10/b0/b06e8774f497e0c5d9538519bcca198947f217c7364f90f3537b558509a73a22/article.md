# Estacada – Food Resources Guide

# **Estacada – Food Resources Guide**


If you live in or near Estacada, Oregon, there are several food assistance programs designed to help individuals and families access nutritious meals. Whether you need a free food box, pantry items, or farm-fresh produce, this guide highlights local and nearby resources to make finding support easier. Resources are organized by proximity to Estacada and include community food programs, meal delivery for seniors, and state-level support options.


Knowing where to go and when to visit can help you make the most of available resources. Many locations have limited hours or require advance notice, so it’s a good idea to call ahead. Some programs provide additional support, such as clothing, bus fare, or financial assistance for purchasing fresh produce.


## **Local Resources**


**Neighborhood Missions Free Food Market – Hoodland Lutheran Church** 59151 US-26, Sandy, OR 97055
Phone: 503-622-9213
Last Monday of the month, 9–10am. Distribution takes place in the church parking lot and is open to everyone—no income verification required.
*Type: Free Food Box*


**Sandy Action Center** 38982 Pioneer Blvd, Sandy, OR 97055
Phone: 503-668-4746
Wednesdays & Fridays, 10am–4pm. You can visit once every 30 days. Bread is available for walk-ins daily, and a clothing card is included with pantry visits.
*Type: Free Food Box*


**St. Michael’s Church** 18090 SE Langensand Rd, Sandy, OR 97055
Thursdays, 10am–2pm.
*Type: Free Food Box*


**Gleaners – Clackamas County** 13821 Fir St, Oregon City, OR
Monday–Friday, 9–11:30am. Offers a twice-per-year food box for residents; bring ID and proof of Clackamas address.
[Learn more](https://www.gleanerscc.org/how-to-join) *Type: Free Food Box*


## **Nearby Resources**


**SNOCAP Community Food Pantry** 124 NE 181st Ave, Gresham, OR 97230
Phone: 503-674-8785
Monday–Friday, 10am–2pm. Pantry operates twice per month and provides additional support services, including clothing and bus fare assistance.
*Type: Free Food Box & Support Services*


**Meals on Wheels – Hoodland Senior Center** 65000 US-26, Welches, OR 97067
Phone: 503-622-3331
Call to apply for home-delivered meals. This program serves seniors and people with disabilities who need prepared meals delivered to their homes.
*Type: Prepared Meal Delivery*


## **State Resources**


**Pacific Northwest CSA**[pnwcsa.org/financial-assistance](https://pnwcsa.org/financial-assistance) Purchase shares from local farms for fresh fruits and vegetables. Financial assistance is available for eligible participants, making farm-fresh produce accessible to all.
*Type: Farm Share / CSA Program*


**FoodFinder.org**[https://foodfinder.oregonfoodbank.org](https://foodfinder.oregonfoodbank.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) An online tool to locate food assistance near you, including pantries, meal sites, and free food events.
*Type: Food Resource Locator*


## **Tips for Accessing Food Resources**


- **Call ahead:** Some pantries require appointments or have limited availability.


- **Bring ID & proof of address:** Many programs ask for these to register you for food boxes.


- **Plan for monthly or seasonal distributions:** Programs like Neighborhood Missions or Gleaners may only operate certain days each month.


- **Explore farm programs:** CSA shares provide fresh fruits and vegetables and may accept financial assistance.


- **Check online tools:** Resources like FoodFinder.org provide up-to-date information on additional programs and pop-up food distributions.


By leveraging these resources, Estacada residents can access healthy and reliable food options, support their families, and connect with community programs that provide additional services and assistance.
