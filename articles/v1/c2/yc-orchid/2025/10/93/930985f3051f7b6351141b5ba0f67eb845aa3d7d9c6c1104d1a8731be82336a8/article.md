# Hoodland – Food Resources Guide

# **Hoodland – Food Resources Guide**


For residents of Hoodland, Oregon, there are multiple food assistance programs designed to ensure families and individuals have access to nutritious meals. From community food pantries to pop-up distributions and farm-fresh produce programs, Hoodland offers a variety of resources to meet different needs. Many programs also include support services, such as clothing, pet food, and meal delivery for seniors.


Understanding where to go, when to visit, and what to bring can make accessing these resources easier. Several programs operate on limited schedules or require advance notice, so calling ahead is strongly recommended.


## **Local Resources**


**Hoodcourse Acres** 25297 E Welches Rd, Hoodland, OR
*Type: Pop-Up / Drop-Off Food Location* Community-based distribution point for free or shared food resources.


**Coffee House 26** 67211 Hwy 26, Hoodland, OR
*Type: Pop-Up / Drop-Off Food Location* Regularly hosts community food distribution events.


**Mogul Mountain** 68278 E Welches Rd, Hoodland, OR
*Type: Pop-Up / Drop-Off Food Location* Provides access to free or low-cost food resources during designated events.


**Neighborhood Missions Free Food Market – Hoodland Lutheran Church** 59151 US-26, Sandy, OR 97055
Phone: 503-622-9213
Last Monday of the month, 9–10am. Open to anyone in need; distribution takes place in the parking lot with no income verification required.
*Type: Free Food Box*


**Sandy Action Center** 38982 Pioneer Blvd, Sandy, OR 97055
Phone: 503-668-4746
Wednesdays & Fridays, 10am–4pm. Bread walk-ins daily; clothing cards included with pantry visits; emergency food boxes available three times per year.
*Type: Free Food Box*


**St. Michael’s Church** 18090 SE Langensand Rd, Sandy, OR 97055
Thursdays, 10am–2pm.
*Type: Free Food Box*


**Gleaners – Clackamas County** 13821 Fir St, Oregon City, OR
Monday–Friday, 9–11:30am. Twice-per-year food box; bring ID and proof of Clackamas address.
[Learn more](https://www.gleanerscc.org/how-to-join) *Type: Free Food Box*

## **Tips for Accessing Food Resources**


- **Plan ahead:** Many Hoodland programs operate on specific days or by appointment.


- **Bring identification:** Most programs require an ID and sometimes proof of address.


- **Check for supplemental programs:** Some locations provide clothing, pet food, or emergency boxes in addition to meals.


- **Consider farm programs:** CSA shares are a great way to access fresh, seasonal produce, and financial assistance is often available.


- **Use online tools:** FoodFinder.org helps identify other nearby or seasonal resources that may not have regular hours.


By utilizing these resources, Hoodland residents can ensure access to consistent, nutritious food for themselves, their families, and even their pets while connecting with supportive community programs.
