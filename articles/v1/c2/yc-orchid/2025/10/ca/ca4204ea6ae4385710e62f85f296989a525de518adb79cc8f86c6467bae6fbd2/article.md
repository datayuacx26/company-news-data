## **What You Need to Know**


- Pop-up and drop-off locations may vary; check ahead for availability.


- Bring photo ID when visiting food banks or pantries.


- Bread is available daily at Sandy Action Center by walk-in.


- CSA programs may require signup or membership; some offer financial assistance.


- Local and national apps like Too Good To Go can help access discounted or surplus meals.


- Hours and availability can change; call ahead to confirm service times.
