# McKenzie River – Food Resources

## McKenzie River – Food Resources


Residents in the McKenzie River area have access to several local food resources, including community pantries and statewide food assistance programs. Resources are listed by proximity, with local options first.


### **Local Resources**


**McKenzie River Food Pantry**
*(Located at McKenzie Bridge Christian Church)*
56334 McKenzie Hwy, McKenzie Bridge, OR 97413
Phone: 541-844-8838
2nd & 4th Tuesdays, 10am–1pm
Provides free food boxes and pantry staples for local residents.
*Free Food Box*


**Lower McKenzie Food Pantry**
*(Located at McKenzie Bible Fellowship)*
45061 McKenzie Hwy, Walterville, OR 97489
Email:Lowermckenziecr@gmail.com
3rd Thursdays, 2pm–6pm
Distributes grocery boxes and household staples to individuals and families.
*Free Food Box*


### **Nearby Resources**


**Rural Food Pantries**
Additional food pantries are available throughout nearby rural Lane County communities. These include sites in Springfield, Eugene, and Veneta.


**Rural & Springfield/Eugene Meal Sites**
Several community and faith-based organizations offer free or low-cost meals. Contact Food for Lane County or visit FoodFinder.org for the most up-to-date listings.


### **State Resources**


**Food for Lane County**
[www.foodforlanecounty.org/food](https://www.foodforlanecounty.org/food/)
“Using our food finder, you can easily find both pantry and meal sites in Lane County, including summer meal sites. Search by ZIP code, city or town, day of the week, or type of site.”
*Food Resource Locator*


**FoodFinder.org**
[foodfinder.oregonfoodbank.org](https://foodfinder.oregonfoodbank.org/)
Online map to locate food assistance resources near McKenzie River and across Oregon.
*Food Resource Locator*


**Oregon Food Bank – McKenzie River Assistance Programs**
[oregonfoodbank.org](https://www.oregonfoodbank.org/)
Provides referrals, local pantry lists, and food access support for residents in McKenzie River and surrounding areas.
*Food Resource Support*


### **What You Need to Know**


- Call ahead to confirm pantry hours or seasonal availability.
- Bring photo ID and proof of residence when picking up food boxes.
- Some programs may offer home-delivered meals for seniors and individuals with disabilities.
- Food resources may change hours or distribution schedules; check online or by phone before visiting.
