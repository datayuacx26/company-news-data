# Introducing Wade Creek’s New Primary Care Provider — Emily Ward, NP

## **Introducing Wade Creek’s New Primary Care Provider — Emily Ward, NP**


Looking for a local provider who truly understands the Estacada community? Emily Ward, NP, is now seeing patients at Orchid Health’s Wade Creek Clinic, and brings over 15 years of healthcare experience to her new role as a Family Nurse Practitioner in Estacada.


Emily grew up in Estacada and recently returned to give back to the community that raised her. With a background that includes seven years in intensive care and a passion for building long-term patient relationships, she offers a personal, grounded approach to family medicine that’s rooted in real-world experience.


### **What Does Emily Specialize In?**


As a primary care provider at Orchid Health Wade Creek Clinic, Emily offers a wide range of services for patients of all ages, including:


- **Preventative care and wellness visits**
- **Chronic disease management** (including diabetes, hypertension, and asthma)
**Women’s health services**
- **Health education and lifestyle coaching**
- **Same-day or urgent care for common concerns**


Her care philosophy is all about partnering with patients—whether you're managing a long-term condition or just want to stay on top of your health with regular checkups.


### **Care That’s Close to Home**


As a local herself, Emily understands the unique needs of families and individuals in the Estacada area. She’s excited to provide care that’s community-based, accessible, and grounded in trust.


If you're searching for a primary care clinic in Estacada, or need a provider who offers preventative care, women’s health, or chronic condition support, Emily is now accepting new patients at Orchid Health Wade Creek Clinic.
