# Sandy Clinic's 2025 Fall Newsletter

### **Welcome Madison Byer, LCSW**


We’re excited to welcome Madison Byer, LCSW, as our new Behavioral Health Clinician at Orchid Health Sandy Clinic. Madison provides short-term, evidence-based counseling for students ages 5 and up, as well as families and community members in the Sandy area.


Her approach is supportive, goal-oriented, and focused on helping patients build practical tools for managing stress, anxiety, grief, or life transitions. Madison brings experience in youth and family therapy and integrated mental health care—and she’s passionate about creating a safe space where every student feels heard and empowered.


Outside of work, Madison enjoys skiing, backpacking, rock climbing, and reading. We’re thrilled to have her on the team and look forward to the care and compassion she brings to the Sandy community.


If your student could benefit from behavioral health services, please contact the clinic to learn more about referrals or visit[orchidhealth.org](http://orchidhealth.org/) .


### **Local Counseling, Close to Home**


We offer short-term, evidence-based counseling for patients ages 5 and up. Our Behavioral Health Clinician provides brief, goal-oriented sessions to support stress, anxiety, depression, and more—all right here in the Sandy community.


### **Events & Announcements — Sandy Clinic**


There’s a lot happening around Sandy this season! From Winterfest at Meinig Park to local food and meal resources, it’s a great time to connect with neighbors and enjoy all our community has to offer.


**Winterfest: Lights at Meinig Park** November 21, 2025 – January 4, 2026, nightly 4:00–9:00 pm
The park will be lit with festive displays throughout the season. Events include tree lighting, Santa visits, “Jolly Trolley” rides, and more.


**Sandy Community Action Center Food Pantry (Ongoing)** Wednesdays & Fridays, 10:00 am – 4:00 pm
Free grocery & produce access for low-income households in the Oregon Trail School District.


**Weekly Free Community Dinner** Thursdays, 3:00 – 5:00 pm at Community Church of Sandy (39290 Scenic St.)
Hosted by Sandy Activity Night — open to all; delivery available within 5-mile radius.


### **Community Resource: Sandy Community Action Center**


The Sandy Community Action Center is a local hunger-relief and support organization serving the Oregon Trail School District. They operate a food pantry (Wednesdays & Fridays, 10:00 am – 4:00 pm) and a thrift store (Monday – Friday, 10:00 am – 4:00 pm). No paperwork is required to use the pantry.


They also run senior food box distribution (2nd Thursday monthly) and sponsor a free community dinner every Thursday at 2:00 pm at the Community Church of Sandy.


Address: 38982 Pioneer Blvd, Sandy, OR
Phone: 503-668-4746
