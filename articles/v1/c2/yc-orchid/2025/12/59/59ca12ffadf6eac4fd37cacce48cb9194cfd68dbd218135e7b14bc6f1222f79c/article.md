**Why Cervical Cancer Screening Matters** Cervical cancer often develops slowly, starting with abnormal changes in cervical cells. Screening allows Orchid Health providers to detect these changes early, before they progress to cancer. According to the American Cancer Society, regular screenings significantly reduce both the incidence and mortality of cervical cancer.


Common risk factors include:


- Human papillomavirus (HPV) infection
- Smoking
- Weakened immune system
- Family history of cervical cancer


By identifying abnormal cells early, screenings at Orchid Health can prevent the development of cervical cancer and ensure timely treatment if needed.


**Recommended Screening Guidelines** Orchid Health follows the[U.S. Preventive Services Task Force](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/cervical-cancer-screening) (USPSTF) guidelines to personalize care:


- **Women aged 21–29:** Pap test every three years
- **Women aged 30–65:** Pap test every three years, HPV test every five years, or co-testing (Pap + HPV) every five years
- **Women over 65:** Screening may no longer be necessary if prior results were normal, but it should be discussed with an Orchid Health provider


Patients with higher risk factors may need earlier or more frequent screenings. Orchid Health clinicians evaluate your unique situation to create a personalized plan that fits your needs.


**Screening Options at Orchid Health** Orchid Health offers a range of cervical cancer screening services, including:


- **WhaPap Test (Pap Smear):** Detects abnormal cervical cells before they become cancerous
- **HPV Test:** Identifies high-risk HPV strains linked to cervical cancer
- **Co-Testing:** Combines Pap and HPV testing for more comprehensive care
- **HPV Self-Collection (Vaginal Swab):** In some cases, patients may qualify for HPV screening that doesn’t require a Pap smear. This private, easy-to-use self-swab can be done at home or in the clinic. Check with your Orchid Health provider to see if this option is right for you.


Our providers ensure that all screenings are comfortable, confidential, and delivered with respect and professionalism.
