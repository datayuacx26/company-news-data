# Mt. Hood + Clackamas County Emergency & Home Repair Resources

### Local emergency communication


- **Warming shelter info (locations + updates):**[https://www.clackamas.us/relief](https://www.clackamas.us/relief)


## Home repair + energy help


### Clackamas County Home Repair Loan


- **Program page:**[https://www.clackamas.us/communitydevelopment/repair](https://www.clackamas.us/communitydevelopment/repair)
- **What it can cover:** single major investments like **primary heat** , **roof** , **septic** , and **exterior repairs**
- **Basic requirements (per program description):**


- **Income limits apply**
- Must **own the home**
- **No manufactured homes**


### Clackamas County Weatherization Assistance


- **Program page:**[https://www.clackamas.us/cfcc/weatherization](https://www.clackamas.us/cfcc/weatherization)
- **What it is:** **weatherization grants** (energy-efficiency improvements)
- **Eligibility:** up to **200% FPL** (Federal Poverty Level)


### USDA Rural Home Repair Loans & Grants (Single Family)


- **Program page:**[https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/single-family-housing-programs/single-family-housing-repair-loans-grants-12](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/single-family-housing-programs/single-family-housing-repair-loans-grants-12)
- **What it supports:** **home repairs** through USDA rural housing programs
