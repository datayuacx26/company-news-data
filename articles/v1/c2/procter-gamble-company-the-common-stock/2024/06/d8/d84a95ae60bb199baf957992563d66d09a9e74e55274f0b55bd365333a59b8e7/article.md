# P&G Brands Top Circana’s 2025 New Product Pacesetters List

In the dynamic world of consumer goods, what truly defines a breakthrough product? At P&G, we believe it’s a relentless focus on understanding evolving needs of our consumers and a commitment to innovation that genuinely elevates everyday experiences.


That philosophy is once again validated as P&G’s brands have earned an incredible seven of the top 10 spots on[Circana](https://www.circana.com/) ’s highly anticipated[2025 New Product Pacesetters](https://www.circana.com/post/2025-new-product-pacesetters-redefining-innovation-and-consumer-impact-in-cpg) . This recognition goes far beyond initial sales; a “[Pacesetter](https://www.circana.com/post/circana-unveils-2025-new-product-pacesetters-as-top-innovations-drive-6-2-billion-in-year-one-sales) ” position is Circana’s gold standard, signifying a product’s ability to meet critical market demands, deliver superior features, and provide the quality and experience that drive sustained consumer loyalty and repeat purchases.


This year’s results are a powerful testament to our dedication. We are proud to announce that P&G secured the **#1 overall non-food product launch with[Luvs](https://www.luvsdiapers.com/en-us) Platinum Protection, alongside six other top 10 placements in the non-food rankings.** Our success extends further, with 11 products making it into the top 25 and an impressive 21 products within the top 100 — a feat that ties our company’s highest total in the report’s 30-year history.
