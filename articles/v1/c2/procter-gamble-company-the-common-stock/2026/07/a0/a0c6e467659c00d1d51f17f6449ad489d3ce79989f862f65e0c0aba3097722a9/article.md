# Downy Introduces an Immersive Fragrance Experience Where Consumers Can Step Inside the World Behind Luxury Laundry Scent

*This two-stop pop-up straight from Downy's in-house perfumery, the Downy Fine Fragrance House, will engross consumers in laundry fine fragrances from the Boutique Botanicals and Unstopables Unlimited Collections.*


CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Today,[Downy](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fdowny.com%2Fen-us&esheet=54579692&newsitemid=20260730604384&lan=en-US&anchor=Downy&index=1&md5=966ec25ed1ad47c247b7d33869bf5f09) is inviting consumers to step into a NEW era of laundry, bringing luxury to every load, with the launch of the Downy Fine Fragrance House. The immersive pop-up debuts in Charleston, South Carolina, where consumers can experience its in-house perfumery firsthand, featuring the newest Boutique Botanicals and iconic Unstopables Unlimited Collections.


The fragrances in this luxury tier are designed by Downy's world-class perfumers – from the first spark of inspiration to the last note left on clothes. Each scent is built with the same layered complexity and intentionality as a bottle of perfume: balanced, deep, emotive, and made to last. The end result is a collection of sophisticated fragrances that truly luxe up laundry, and Downy is bringing this to life on a whole new level.


**The Downy Fine Fragrance House Experience**


- **Guided Scent Journey:** Guests will get to know Downy’s luxury scent beads by entering the world of Boutique Botanicals, inspired by the artistry of nature, and the Unstopables Unlimited Collection, premium scents that elevate your laundry.
- **Squeeze & Sniff:** In this interactive environment, attendees can engage with the product by squeezing and sniffing the scent bead bottles to experience the luxurious fragrance notes firsthand.
- **Interactive Elements:** The pop-up will offer photo-worthy moments and branded Downy Fine Fragrance House giveaways.


"We are thrilled to invite consumers inside the world of our in-house perfumery to experience Downy’s scents that luxe up laundry,” said Benjamin Sattler, Vice President of North America Downy. "We’ve brought high-end fragrances to laundry at affordable price points, as we believe our scent collections should be worn on any clothing, and by anyone. Through the Downy Fine Fragrance House experiences, we’re proud to spotlight the perfumers behind these long lasting, luxury scents.”

- **What are scent beads?**


- Scent beads dissolve during the wash cycle to infuse fabrics with scent.


- **How do you use these scent beads?**


- **Step 1:** Add the detergent.
- **Step 2:** Before opening the scent beads, give the bottle a squeeze with the cap on to get a quick whiff of the scent.
- **Step 3:** Next, take off the cap, shake the beads right into it, and toss them directly into the empty drum.
- **Step 4:** Add clothes and fabrics on top.
