# Braun NEVO: A Masterclass in Design, Durability, and a Perfect Shave

### Consumer-Led Innovation, Backed by Rigorous R&D


Behind the scenes, the development of the NEVO reflects P&G’s integrated approach to innovation, pairing decades of user research with deep technical mastery and quality engineering at Braun’s **Kronberg Design Studio in Germany.** High-tech processes and human experience work together to ensure superior performance. The process includes extensive lab testing and consumer in-use assessments, evaluating each device down to the smallest component. Lab tests simulate up to seven years of use, including extreme drop tests, rotation “washing machine” checks and strength demos, helping ensure a highly reliable experience.


The NEVO lab results were excellent, and Wilson remembers wondering how to show people just how durable the shaver is. The solution? They invited guests and arranged for a Porsche 911 to drive over one. The NEVO emerged unscathed, turning rigorous engineering into a simple, memorable proof point.
