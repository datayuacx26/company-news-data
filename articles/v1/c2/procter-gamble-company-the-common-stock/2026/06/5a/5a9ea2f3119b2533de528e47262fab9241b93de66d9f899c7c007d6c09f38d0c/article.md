# Cascade® Launches New Clean and Dry Booster Rinse Aid to Provide a Superior Shine and Dry Experience

*New Rinse Aid complements Cascade® Platinum Plus during the final rinse cycle, offering 5x better drying and advanced spot prevention*

Designed to work in tandem with[Cascade® Platinum Plus](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcascadeclean.com%2Fen-us%2Fproducts%2Fcascade-platinum-plus-actionpacs-dishwasher-detergent-fresh-scent%2F&esheet=54560468&newsitemid=20260630627916&lan=en-US&anchor=Cascade%26%23174%3B+Platinum+Plus&index=3&md5=c112d17376fe6ab0e85cafa740f7ec97) , this premium dishwasher enhancer establishes the ultimate dishwashing routine to tackle people’s biggest frustrations, including stubborn water spots, cloudiness, hard water sediment, and damp dishes. Together, the power duo clears away food residue and film for a brilliant dry and shine every time.


Recent Cascade® research highlights widespread consumer pain points with traditional dishwashing machine usage:


- 72% of consumers report that dishes are still wet at the end of a full dishwasher cycle.
- 64% of consumers notice dry spots and streaks on their glassware.


These persistent issues contribute to "dish dread" - the frustration of having to hand-dry or re-wash items. Cascade’s new regimen offers a faster, more effective solution to eliminate these extra steps entirely.


**Why Use a Rinse Aid?**


Modern dishwashers are engineered to rely on rinse aids for optimal drying. Without one, water clings to dishes as cycles cool, leaving behind spots and mineral residues. Cascade® Clean and Dry Booster utilizes advanced sheeting action to prevent water from pooling on surfaces, allowing dishes to rinse cleaner and dry significantly faster. The result is an incredible shine with **5x better drying and spot prevention** than using detergent alone.


“Rinse aids are used to treat the water during the rinse cycle of your dishwasher, to lower the surface tension so water cascades off dishes, rather than beading up on the surface,” explains Morgan Eberhard, P&G Principal Scientist. “By using a rinse aid like Cascade® Clean and Dry Booster, you’re minimizing or even eliminating the droplets left behind - so no more pesky water spots!”


**How to Use a Rinse Aid**


Using Cascade® Clean and Dry Booster is simple and requires no extra cycles. Just fill your dishwasher's rinse aid dispenser, which is typically located directly next to the detergent chamber, and let it work during the final rinse cycle to prevent water droplets from clinging to dishes, even in hard water environments. Dishes are then ready to be put away and used for your next meal, no pre-rinse, soak or scrub needed.


- **Easy Pour Technology:** Leverage the easy-to-pour, no-drip cap to fill the dispenser to the top, securing the cover back on before you close the dishwasher door.
- **Monthly Refills:** Refill your rinse aid on a monthly basis to ensure a shiny result with each use.
- **Dishwasher Regimen:** Pair with Cascade® Platinum Plus for best results.
