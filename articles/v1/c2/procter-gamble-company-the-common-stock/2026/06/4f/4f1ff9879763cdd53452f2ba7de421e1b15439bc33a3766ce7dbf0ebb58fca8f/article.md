# Native Launches Reformulated Deodorant and Body Washes with Enhanced Performance and Improved Formulations

*Native’s updated formulas deliver enhanced performance while staying true to what consumers already know and love about the brand.*


SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Native](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.nativecos.com&esheet=54558954&newsitemid=20260625794447&lan=en-US&anchor=Native&index=1&md5=60bebf5525dcbe8b187a30cefbb89c69) , the personal care brand known for their clean, simple and effective formulas, announces upgraded deodorant and body wash offerings, marking the brand’s latest step forward in performance-driven, thoughtfully sourced personal care.


The upgraded deodorant, available in both stick and spray formats, delivers up to 72 hours of enhanced odor protection while remaining gentle on skin, while the reformulated body wash offers an improved cleansing experience designed to leave skin feeling soft and smooth with 24 hours of gentle freshness.


Featuring the same signature scents consumers know and love, these updates reflect Native’s continued commitment to refining its core essentials—combining high-performance ingredients with a streamlined approach to everyday personal care.

- **Cashmere & Rain:** Captures a moment of soft knits and fresh air.
- **Citrus & Herbal Musk:** A blend of zesty freshness with soft, earthy calm.

With growing demand for personal care products that are both clean and effective, consumers are placing greater emphasis on performance and ingredient integrity. Native continues to meet this moment by evolving its formulas, ensuring its products stay true to its simple, clean approach.


“At Native, we believe you shouldn’t have to choose between ingredients you trust and performance you can rely on,” said Christopher Talbott, Chief Executive Officer at Native. “With our updated deodorant and body wash, we focused on delivering stronger odor protection and a more refreshing cleanse, while maintaining the thoughtfully sourced ingredients and signature scents our consumers love.”
