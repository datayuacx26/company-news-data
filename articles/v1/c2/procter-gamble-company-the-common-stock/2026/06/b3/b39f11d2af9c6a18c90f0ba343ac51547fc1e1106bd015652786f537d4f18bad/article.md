# P&G Products Recognized with Good Housekeeping’s 2026 Cleaning Award | P&G

At P&G, innovation means creating products that work harder so consumers don’t have to.


From cleaner clothes and fresher laundry to shinier floors and spotless dishes, P&G scientists develop products designed to make everyday cleaning easier and more effective. Each formula is rigorously tested to meet high standards for quality, performance and safety, and independent recognition from trusted institutions such as[Good Housekeeping](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com/) offers another meaningful measure of that work.
