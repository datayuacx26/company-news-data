# P&G and iHeartRadio Launch the Elton John Impact Awards Celebrating and Honoring LGBTQ+ Trailblazers

From advocacy and storytelling to music and sports, each honoree has helped redefine possibilities and expand visibility for LGBTQ+ people around the world. Building from Elton John’s leadership and legacy, the program celebrates these trailblazers and their achievements, increasing their impact.


A panel of inspirational leaders worked closely with Elton John to select this year’s honorees. Selection Committee Members include Chair of the Elton John Foundation and CEO of Rocket Entertainment David Furnish; three-time Tony plus Grammy, Emmy and Drama Desk-winning artist Billy Porter; Grammy and Emmy Award-winning and Oscar-nominated musician Brandi Carlile; Elton John AIDS Foundation CEO Anne Aslett; and the Elton John Impact Awards’ Founding Producers P&G’s Brent Miller and iHeartMedia's John Sykes.


## Which Organizations Benefit from the Elton John Impact Awards?


Community impact is at the heart of the Elton John Impact Awards. The program raises awareness for and empowers LGBTQ+ nonprofits with the resources they need to grow, scale and drive lasting impact. The program will help drive visibility and funding for frontline organizations working to support the LGBTQ+ community. In partnership with Brandi Carlile’s[Looking Out Foundation](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lookingoutfoundation) , we will deliver grants to support the critical work of[CenterLink](https://www.linkedin.com/company/centerlink/) ,[GLAAD](https://www.linkedin.com/company/glaad/) ,[Outright International](https://www.linkedin.com/company/outrightintl/) , National Black Justice Collective,[SAGE](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sageusa-lgbtq/) and[The Trevor Project](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-trevor-project/) . P&G will also provide funding to the[Elton John AIDS Foundation](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/elton-john-aids-foundation) to support its efforts to expand access to lifesaving care to build a healthier future.


By combining storytelling with tangible support, the Elton John Impact Awards help ensure these organizations can continue to address various critical needs across the LGBTQ+ community, such as supporting and advocating for LGBTQ+ elder care, increasing access to preventative health services and treatment, and providing mental health support for young people.


## What is the Format of the Elton John Impact Awards?


The Elton John Impact Awards are the first-ever podcast awards ceremony and feature candid, personal conversations with Elton John, David Furnish, and this year’s legendary and iconic honorees.


Hosted by Tony, Grammy, Emmy & Drama Desk Award–winning artist Billy Porter and Radio Music Award-winning radio personality and host of “Elvis Duran and the Morning Show” Elvis Duran, listeners are invited behind the scenes to hear the stories, challenges and defining moments that shaped each honoree’s journey, told in their own words like never before. To mark the inaugural broadcast, pop star Dove Cameron is delivering a special performance of "Your Song" in tribute to Elton John’s legacy.


The program’s audio format allows these stories to resonate with listeners on a more powerful level, unlocking intimacy and creating an enduring connection in a deeply human way.


## How Can Audiences Listen to the Elton John Impact Awards?


The Elton John Impact Awards will air as a special audio broadcast across 300+[iHeartRadio](https://www.iheart.com/) stations on June 1, 2026, reaching more than 188 million listeners nationwide. The program is also available on the[iHeartRadio app](https://news.iheart.com/apps/) and all major podcast platforms, making these powerful stories accessible to audiences everywhere.
