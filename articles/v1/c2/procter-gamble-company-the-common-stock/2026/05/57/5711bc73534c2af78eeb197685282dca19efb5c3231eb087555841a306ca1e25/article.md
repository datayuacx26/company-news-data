# Better Solutions, Faster Results: The P&G PRO Advantage for Commercial Cleaning

Across each of these examples, the goal is consistent. Not just stronger cleaning but reducing the friction that slows businesses down and making the work easier for the people doing it.


## From the Lab to Real-World Impact


For the teams behind P&G PRO, innovation starts with a deep understanding of people. Because P&G has spent decades studying how consumers clean their homes and what they expect from their environments, we know exactly what a guest or customer is looking for when they walk through your doors.


But we also study the professionals doing the work. By understanding the physical and operational toll of commercial cleaning, P&G PRO is uniquely positioned to develop solutions that not only perform effectively but also help make physically demanding work easier for staff. It’s about solving for efficiency, consistency, and performance at scale, while also meeting the expectations people bring into every space.


In laundry, for example, that means delivering results that hold up under constant use while still meeting the high visual standards customers expect. Clean linens and uniforms are often one of the clearest signals of quality — and one of the easiest places for customers to notice when something falls short.


In surface cleaning, it means delivering reliable results on high-touch areas under constant use. Spotless countertops, tables, and reception desks are often some of the most immediate signals of a business’s quality and care.


“In a business environment, you’re not solving for one surface or one load,” said Bryan Smith, P&G PRO Vice President of North America. “You’re solving for consistency across an entire operation while still delivering the consistent clean customers expect every time.”


That perspective shapes how solutions are developed, tested, and refined, always with the goal of making cleaning more efficient, more reliable, and easier to execute at scale.


## A Different Kind of Growth Opportunity


Clean has always been part of the job. What is changing is how directly it connects to business performance.


For P&G, this is about applying decades of research and consumer understanding to a professional audience with more complex needs and higher stakes. For businesses, it is about finding solutions that do more than clean, supporting operations in a way that helps everything else run as it should — and empowering teams with tools that make their hardest tasks feel a little simpler.


Because in the end, clean is not just a standard to meet. It is a signal that everything behind the scenes is working as it should.
