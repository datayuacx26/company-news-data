# Listen, Learn, Innovate: How P&G and Pampers Serve Every Baby

Our commitment to consumer understanding extends across the world. In China, we sought to understand the needs of highly discerning parents in the super-premium baby care segment. Through deep observational research, we discovered that these mothers, who our teams described as the "CEOs of their families," judged a diaper’s superiority based on three key "moments of truth": the immediate "first touch," flawless performance, and the social validation of their choice.


Armed with these insights, our Pampers team in China created[Pampers Prestige](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/22/procter-gamble-diapers-silk-china.html) , a super-premium offering that resonated with a powerful cultural symbol: silk. For over two millennia, silk has represented luxury, softness, and status in China. As P&G President and CEO[Shailesh Jejurikar](https://us.pg.com/leadership-team/shailesh-jejurikar/) states, this innovation is truly unique: "Pampers Prestige is the only leading diaper brand that has silk materials in the product, delivering the ultimate experience of skin comfort and protection."


To bring this vision to life, our R&D teams achieved a series of technical breakthroughs. As **Bobby Fan, P&G R&D Senior Director** , explains, the team developed a way to blend **real, high-quality silk fibers** directly into the diaper's topsheet — the layer that touches the baby’s skin. For the exterior, they engineered an outer cover with **ultra-fine fibers — just 1/12th the thickness of a human hair** — to create what mothers described as a "melting softness." This complete sensory experience extends to the packaging itself, which is silky-smooth to the touch and uses a **micro-embossing technology** to create the fine, recognizable lines of silk fabric, ensuring every detail was designed to meet the discerning parent's desire for a product that is both functionally superior and culturally resonant.
