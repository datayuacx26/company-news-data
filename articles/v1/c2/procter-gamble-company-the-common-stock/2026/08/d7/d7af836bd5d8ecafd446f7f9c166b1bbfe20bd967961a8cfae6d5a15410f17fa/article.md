# Old Spice Introduces This Year’s Limited-Edition Fallidudes Collection, Serving Up Peak Fall Freshness with Pumpking, Apple Crumbro and Frankenstud

CINCINNATI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Pumpkin spice may have met its match. This fall, Old Spice is bringing fresh energy to the season with the seasonal return of its limited-edition Fallidudes Collection. This year's lineup features three bold characters: Pumpking, Apple Crumbro and Frankenstud. Inspired by the scents that define autumn, the collection delivers long-lasting freshness with a playful twist, helping guys smell incredible from hayrides to haunted houses.


Meet the Fallidudes:


- Pumpking – the fan-favorite fall scent returns, bringing its bold take on the pumpkin spice craze back for another season
- **NEW** Apple Crumbro – the crisp, cozy scent of apple cider bottled up for your shower routine
- **NEW** Frankenstud – a monstrously vanilla pistachio scent that's perfect for spooky season


Available for a limited time in select aluminum-free deodorant sticks, antiperspirant, body wash and 2-in-1 shampoo + conditioner, the Fallidudes collection delivers peak fall energy with every swipe, wash, and sniff. Whether you’re stocking up for yourself or building the perfect boo basket, trust Old Spice to keep you smelling fantastic while you *Creep it Fresh* all season long.


"The best part of fall isn't just the changing leaves, it's the return of the scents everyone waits all year for," said Kate DiCarlo, Senior Director, Communications, Personal Care Portfolio, Procter & Gamble. “With Old Spice’s limited-edition Fallidudes collection, we’ve bottled the scents of everything guys love about fall—from smoky bonfires and football tailgates to cinnamon-spiced comfort food—into three unforgettable fragrances that keep them confidently fresh all season long.”


The limited-edition Fallidudes collection continues Old Spice's tradition of turning seasonal favorites into unforgettable scent experiences, offering fans a fun new way to celebrate autumn beyond the coffee cup.


The Old Spice Fallidudes collection will be available for a limited time at select retailers nationwide in singles, value packs, and seasonal displays while supplies last.
