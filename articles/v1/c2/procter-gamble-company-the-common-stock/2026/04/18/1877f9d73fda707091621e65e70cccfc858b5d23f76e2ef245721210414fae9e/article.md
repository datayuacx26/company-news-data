# Showing Up When It Matters Most: Leslie’s Story of Disaster Recovery and Gratitude

Leslie, a resident of Ripley, Mississippi, saw his daily life turn upside down when a severe winter storm swept through his community. Living alone in a remote area, he faced significant challenges as temperatures plummeted. "It felt like living in your freezer," Leslie recalled, describing how his home dipped below freezing. Without electricity, heat, or running water, access to even the most basic daily essentials became difficult. "I couldn't even shower for days," he shared, a stark reminder of the importance of everyday comforts of home in the aftermath of a disaster.


During this difficult time, support arrived. Friends and neighbors cleared trees that had fallen in front of his driveway, allowing him to leave home. Local community organizations provided heat and food. And P&G and Matthew 25: Ministries offered critical everyday services such as mobile shower trailers stocked with health and hygiene supplies from trusted P&G brands like Head & Shoulders and Oral-B, as well as clean clothes through our[Tide Loads of Hope](https://tide.com/en-us/our-commitment/loads-of-hope) program. “It was a wonderful feeling of fellowship,” Leslie remembers. “I can’t describe how it feels to be rescued.”


The support provided by P&G and Matthew 25: Ministries represented more than access to daily essential products and services – it was a moment of deep relief. The ability to refresh and regain a sense of routine and personal dignity provided not only practical aid, but also emotional comfort.
