# Why “Consumer First” Always Wins

### What does “Consumer First” look like inside P&G today and how is it championed from the top down?


**Kirti Singh:**
For us, it shows up in how we operate every day.


It’s about staying continuously connected to consumers and bringing that perspective into decisions across the business. That expectation applies to everyone – from our newest hires to the C-suite.[P&G CEO Shailesh Jejurikar](https://us.pg.com/blogs/analytics-insights-2025-world-tour/) underscored this commitment and raised the bar by beginning his first day as CEO with a personal visit to a consumer’s home to better understand her daily routines.


What matters most is going beyond surface-level behavior. We focus on understanding context, what’s happening in people’s lives, what they’re trying to solve, and what truly feels “worth it” to them. That’s what leads to better choices and superior experiences.
