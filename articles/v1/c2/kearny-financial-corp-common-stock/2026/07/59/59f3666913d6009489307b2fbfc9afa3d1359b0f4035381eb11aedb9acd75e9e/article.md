# Kearny Bank Donates $10,000 to Wayne D. Zitt Jr. Civic Association

**FAIRFIELD, NJ (JULY 20, 2026)** **–** **FOOD PANTRY SUPPORT –** A $10,000 donation was recently presented to the Wayne D. Zitt Jr. Civic Association by Kearny Bank, through its KearnyBank Foundation. The funds will support the association’s start-up Guttenberg Food Pantry Initiative, which will be available to all Guttenberg residents experiencing hardship.


Those wishing to provide financial support to the new food pantry may do so by calling 201.458-3481.


**** **About Kearny Bank**


Combining the finest traditions of community banking with the industry’s latest, most effective financial products and services, Kearny Bank provides business and personal clients with the tools for success.
