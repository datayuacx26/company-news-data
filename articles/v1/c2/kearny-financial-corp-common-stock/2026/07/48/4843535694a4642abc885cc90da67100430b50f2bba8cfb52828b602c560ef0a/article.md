# More Than $592,000 Gifted to Charities by Kearny Bank During Fiscal 2026

**FAIRFIELD, NJ (July 28, 2026)** – A total of **$592,295** was donated to charitable organizations by[Kearny Bank](https://www.kearnybank.com/) during the 12-month period from July 1, 2025, through June 30, 2026. Made through the KearnyBank Foundation, these contributions supported nonprofit organizations throughout New Jersey and New York.


The largest Kearny Bank grants during the past year were **$65,000** to Junior Achievement of New Jersey in support of student financial literacy and career readiness. Habitat for Humanity of Greater Newark received **$50,000** for its Critical Repair Program, while **$32,500** funded[scholarships for high school students](https://www.kearnybank.com/news/2026/june/allentown-hs-student-athlete-wins-2026-strength-award-scholarship-from-kearny-bank--presented-in-conjunction-with-njsiaa) throughout New Jersey. Additional highlight grants included **$18,000** to Tovah for its annual[Back2School Store](https://www.kearnybank.com/news/2023/july/national-council-of-jewish-womenessex-county-section-staging-15th-annual-back-2-school-store) , at which Kearny Bank employees also volunteered; **$18,000** as an event sponsor of the Robeson Classic High School All-Star Football Game; **$15,000** to the Our House Foundation to help construct a new group home in Summit; **$15,000** to the Boys & Girls Club of Clifton; and **$14,000** to the Student/Partner Alliance.


"Supporting organizations that strengthen our communities is central to Kearny Bank's mission," says Craig Montanaro, Kearny Bank President and CEO. "Whether helping students prepare for the future, expanding access to housing, supporting families and young people, or making similarly essential investments, we're proud to partner with nonprofits that make a meaningful difference in people’s lives."


The[KearnyBank Foundation](https://www.kearnybank.com/about-us/kearny-bank-foundation) divides its charitable giving into five distinct categories: Education, Community, Quality of Life, Housing and Scholarships.
