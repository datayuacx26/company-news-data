# Boonton Resident Named 2nd VP / Senior IT Manager by Kearny Bank

FAIRFIELD, NJ - Boonton, NJ resident Allen Apgar has been elevated to the position of 2nd Vice President / Senior IT Manager by Kearny Bank. In this role, Apgar leads day-to-day operations of the bank’s IT team, including oversight of systems, security, and network maintenance.


Apgar joined Kearny Bank in 2018, following the bank’s acquisition of CSBK. Prior to this latest promotion, he was serving as Vice President / IT Security Operations.


“Allen knows our systems inside and out, and the team relies on him to ensure everything consistently runs at peak effectiveness,” says Jeff Dunn, 1st VP / Director of Information Technology. “When it comes to our daily operations, we look forward to Allen being an outstanding leader.”


Apgar is a graduate of the Chubb Institute, where he studied network engineering.
