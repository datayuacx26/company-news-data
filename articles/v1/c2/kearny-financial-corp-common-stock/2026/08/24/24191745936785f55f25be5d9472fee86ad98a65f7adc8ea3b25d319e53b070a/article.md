# Daniel Benker Promoted to SVP / Director of Facilities and Administrative Services by Kearny Bank

**FAIRFIELD, NJ (August 12, 2026) –** Hewitt resident Daniel Benker has been promoted by Kearny Bank to the position of Senior Vice President / Director of Facilities and Administrative Services. He previously served as the bank’s First Vice President / Facilities and Administrative Services.


Benker, who joined Kearny Bank in 2004, heads the Facilities and Administrative Services division, overseeing facilities operations at more than 40 locations, procurement, administrative services, mailroom operations, property management, fleet management, life safety, branch maintenance, capital improvement projects, and regulatory compliance.


“Daniel consistently leads a cross-functional team that maximizes the efficiency of our operations and facilities,” says Kearny Bank Executive Vice President / Chief Administrative Officer Erika Sacher “His oversight is instrumental in ensuring that our clients and employees have access to the resources needed for success.”


An award-winning member of the International Facilities Management Association, Benker has more than 20 years of professional experience in the banking sector.
