# Link-in-Bio Stores with UPI: How Creators & Small Sellers Can Sell Without a Website

You have built an audience on Instagram, YouTube, or WhatsApp. Now you want to sell – a course, a batch of cupcakes, a coaching slot, a thrifted jacket. That means DMs full of “send it to this handle,” screenshots as proof, and no clean record of who paid for what. It is a workflow that breaks the moment orders start scaling. A bio link store UPI setup fixes this. It turns your single link into a proper checkout where buyers pay by UPI in seconds, and every payment maps to an order automatically. This guide shows you how to build one, price it, design it for mobile, and stay compliant, all without a website or a single line of code.

## Step-by-Step – Setting Up a UPI-Enabled Bio Link Store (No Website Required)


You can sell without a website using UPI in an afternoon. Here is the sequence, start to finish.


1. **Create a payment gateway account.** Sign up with an RBI-authorised payment aggregator, then complete KYC. You will typically need a PAN and a bank account; operating as a sole proprietor is fine. Authorised aggregators hold customer funds in escrow, adding a layer of trust that a personal handle lacks.
2. **Create a payment page or link.** Set the price, upload a product image and description, and enable UPI as the default payment method. For a multi-item store, use a hosted payment page; for a single offer, a payment link is enough.
3. **Paste one URL into your bio.** Add it to your Instagram bio, YouTube description, WhatsApp Business profile, and X bio. This single URL becomes your storefront entrance across every channel.
4. **Test the full buyer flow from a mobile device.** Open the link on your phone, tap through to UPI, and confirm the app switch and payment complete cleanly. Most buyers arrive on mobile, so test where they shop.
5. **Set up order confirmation and digital delivery.** Configure an automatic confirmation and, for digital products, an auto-delivery of the download or access link.


> **Pro-Tip:** Place UPI as the first payment option in your checkout layout. With UPI making up over 80% of retail digital payments and 96% of Indians online via mobile, leading with UPI cuts the taps between “I want this” and “paid.”

## Frequently Asked Questions


**Can I accept UPI payments without a website?**
Yes. Using payment links or a hosted payment page, you can collect UPI payments from your Instagram, YouTube, or WhatsApp bio without building a website. You simply place one checkout URL in your bio and buyers pay via UPI directly, with each payment mapped to an order.


**What’s the difference between a UPI payment link and a payment page for a bio link store?**
A payment link is a single-transaction URL set to one amount, ideal for a custom order or a one-off sale. A payment page is a hosted, branded catalogue with multiple items and a full checkout. For a bio link store, the payment page URL is what goes in your bio.


**Is there a fee on UPI transactions for my bio link store?**
Under the Government’s incentive scheme, eligible small merchants pay zero MDR on low-value UPI, though a gateway platform fee may still apply depending on your plan. A separate proposal to charge MDR on UPI transactions above ₹2,000 is under discussion but targets large merchants and is not yet law, so most small sellers are unaffected.


**What is the maximum I can collect via a single UPI payment?**
RBI caps most single UPI transactions at ₹1 lakh. Higher limits of up to ₹5 lakh apply to specific categories, subject to KYC and your bank’s policy. For high-ticket offers, split payments into instalments or use bank transfer to avoid hitting the ceiling.


**How do I sell a membership or course subscription from my Instagram bio using UPI?**
Set up a subscription plan with a UPI Autopay mandate. The buyer authorises the mandate once from a link shared in your bio, and renewals are debited automatically. This works well for communities, gated newsletters, and course instalments where you want predictable recurring income.
