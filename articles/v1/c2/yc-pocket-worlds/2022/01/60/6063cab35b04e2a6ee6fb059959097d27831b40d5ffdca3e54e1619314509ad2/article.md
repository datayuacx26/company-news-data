### **Blackmarket launch**


We’re currently reviewing the data — there were millions of listings with hundreds of thousands of items purchased over a 2-week period. The black market ended up being a super popular feature that Highrise plans to bring back in the near future!


Some bugs and glitches were noted following the closure of the Black Market where users did not receive gold and/or items that remained uncollected at the time of its closure. There was also a bug where some users received duplicates of items. The development team has spent the last two days processing transactions, lost items, and uncollected gold with scripts currently running to fix these issues. For those that are still waiting, the team expects users to receive all missing gold and items by the end of Friday, so please refrain from messaging support at this time. If gold and items are still missing by the end of the day tomorrow, please create a ticket through support!


### **Why did Highrise disable the use of the Black Market?**


Highrise is currently in the process of assessing the Black Market’s impact on the economy. The strategy behind issuing a 30% sales commission on the Black Market was to sink gold out of the economy and deflate prices on higher valued items; the development team reviewed the data and noted this strategy to be effective.


As stated above, there are plans to bring the Black Market into the game frequently (possibly even permanently) — however, at this point, the team will continue to assess the economy and sustainability of this feature by cycling it in and out of the app. The development team will continue to improve the Black Market for future launches with richer item profiles. There will be an update to see where items came from — you are currently able to do this through[https://joinhighrise.com/catalog](https://joinhighrise.com/catalog) with plans to integrate this web-based feature in the mobile app to make it more marketplace friendly.


### **Recent bug related to tipping and users not receiving tips**


Highrise temporarily disabled a queue system while fixing issues related to items and gold not automatically being returned to the user following the exit of the Black Market. This queue system includes furniture that may have been moved from a room and not returned to the inventory; trades that were canceled and not immediately returned to the inventory; and items that may have been removed from a grab and not automatically returned to the inventory. Once the queue system was re-enabled, all transactions listed above were processed. Tips may have been delayed 10 or so hours during this process.


## **What’s to Come (and go) in 2022**


### **NFTS:**


We are using a carbon-neutral and environmentally friendly technology. See this blog for more info:[https://medium.com/pocketworlds/why-nfts-at-highrise-are-carbon-neutral-d22d156a7956](https://medium.com/pocketworlds/why-nfts-at-highrise-are-carbon-neutral-d22d156a7956)


### **Pets:**


The Highrise team is currently working on a pet mechanic and pet system: this will include skeletons to support different pet structures (bi-pedal pets, quad-pedal pets, flying, etc.). Currently, this project remains in the design process phase, but the team is excited to announce this upcoming feature as it’s been a frequently requested idea. The team will likely launch the first introduction of pets as NFTs to allow for true ownership for players. This feature is expected to be much easier to transact with, unlike the Creature Club experience. More information to come!


### **Token/Token Design**


Highrise is currently working in partnership to introduce a crypto token to Highrise. More information coming soon.


### **Update on Skypass**


Creature holders will now automatically receive free SkyPass+ in-game once their wallet is connected to their in-game account via[https://joinhighrise.com/nft](https://joinhighrise.com/nft) . This is one of the in-game perks for owning a Creature.


### **More NFT initiatives — Land Sale:**


Around our giant Highrise Metaverse, there will be *land* permanently available for user ownership. Landowners will be able to do as they wish with their land.


This feature will come with a total revamp of the current website to make it more modern and marketplace-friendly. The land sale will support both cryptocurrency and fiat (your native currency) as some countries don’t support crypto. Immutable X, on which all Highrise blockchain efforts are built, is a blockchain that is efficient, carbon-neutral, and environmentally friendly. Transactions on Immutable X don’t cost as much to run; additionally, we can have a separate payment processor for credit card transactions or cash, which can then send items to and from different users on the blockchain.


### **Pocket Coins**


This currency will soon be leaving the app due to minimal use. Pocket Coins will be converted into gold at the rate of 300 gold for 100 pocket coins, or the equivalent of $1 USD.


### **Live Ops: Valentine’s Day Design Contest and Upcoming Events**


The #SeasonOfLove Valentine’s Day contest is currently in motion for users to submit their grab ideas. The deadline to submit is January 9th.


Android users encountered a bug with the current #SeasonOfLove contest that prevented them from submitting entries through the usual Design Contest tool. There is an app update available for Android users: please look for version 1.31.2.


As of February 15th, a new design contest will launch using a brand new system that will disallow popularity as a factor for winning these contests. This setup, created in response to the community’s feedback on the current contest system, will support anonymous submissions and voting. The new web-based solution involves two separate phases for submission and voting, thereby preventing entries submitted early from unfairly accruing an advantage. Please keep in mind that some artists may continue to earn multiple wins due to the high-quality art they are submitting and the positive response from members of the community!


The next event theme is “Your Culture” which started today, January 6th, at 9am PST. This event features designs by community members submitted through a previous art contest.


### **A Run Down on Support**


Submit a ticket to support by opening the FAQ section from within your profile Settings on the top right-hand corner within the mobile app. There is a new bot system in place that sorts tickets. Users can tap through the bot prompts: it will offer some recommendations and/or suggestions on how to resolve the present issue. 17–20% of tickets are resolved with these suggestions; in turn, this speeds up response times for users requiring help that the bot can’t resolve.


There is currently an average of a 75 minute response time for each support ticket. Support handles a variety of topics such as payment issues, missing items, and tracking trades. This is a full-service team of 10+ people with over 18 hours of coverage, and it is continuing to grow and improve with the addition of new features. The ticket system is a major contact point between the staff and the community — feedback is helpful to distribute information to the correct teams working within Highrise.


### **Bug process**


Bugs are sent to the development team for quicker and more accurate resolutions. There is a new process in place to escalate bugs to the QA team which has since assisted in faster and more organized fixes.


### **Trust and Safety**


The team has expanded with new hires and received a full system revamp on how the team reviews tickets takes actions.


**New:** a recent feature is the muting system for less egregious offenses (users can’t talk but can still event/play within the app)


### **Minor Safety and Steps Taken: TOP PRIORITY!**


Users are strongly encouraged to continue reporting users that are suspected to be underage. Users under the age of 13 are not allowed on the app and will be banned on notice. Reports are reviewed constantly — if a user is suspected to be underage, their account will be frozen until ID is verified by staff.


### **Predatory behavior**


The AI chat scan system in place automatically flags predatory chats and posts. Users that participate in this type of behavior will be permanently banned from the app.


### **Minor safety/sexual content — Removed a lot quicker**


AI scans everything that is uploaded and said — there is a process in place that if something does go through (child pornography for example) a connection is made with Cybertips to notify the FBI/police. There is a new team lead for this, Nikki, who has a large background in trust and safety. She is planning to take this to a whole new level and start a fresh new process, including the creation of a new safety website to share more information on how to stay safe as a user; and if you’re a parent, how to manage your child’s safety in app. Users can expect more changes to be made to the guidelines with more actions to be taken in the future. Nikki will be on next week’s AMA on *Wednesday, January 12th at 12pm PST.*


## **User Asked Questions**


Please note user questions will be answered from submissions placed on the Highrise EN form rather than in the AMA room to make it fair for all spectators who may be unable to enter the room. Users are encouraged to submit their question on the form prior to the AMA if they would like their questions answered.


### **Q: Can account bound items be made tradeable for a certain duration?**


**A:** Account bound items allow Highrise the ability to give more items away without the worry of people exploiting that mechanic. If these items were to be made tradeable, people would exploit them/make new accounts and find ways to get more of these account bound tokens and in turn, would have a negative impact on the economy. Highrise is an economy based game. A lot of it is based on how rare and scarce items are — there will be less value in items the more available they are to users in game.


### **Q: Why is there a cost to enable voice chat?**


**A:** Highrise charges its users to utilize the voice chat feature to try to filter out toxic behavior. The idea is that if there is a cost, it makes it less available for some people to utilize by limiting new accounts/alts from creating rooms with the intention to participate in negative behaviour. Room owners that allow voice chat are responsible for what is being said in the room and who has access to speak. If found to be utilized inappropriately, this may result in the room owner receiving a warning on their account for not complying with the guidelines. Possible subscription for voice in the future.


### **Q: Will Highrise Events stop with user-generated content?**


**A:** There are no plans to stop Highrise planned events, however, there are plans to enable players to create and host better experiences — this is being worked on throughout 2022!


### **Q: Can we dye/color our items?**


**A:** A product lead designed something over the Christmas break where you can use a template to create a custom piece of clothing. It may be possible to implement something like this in the game in the future.


### **Q:** Why aren’t accounts that are receiving a lot of reports not being banned right away even with sufficient evidence being provided?


**A:** Previous offenses/no prior offenses are reviewed as a factor in determining the extent of a user’s punishment. Depending on the severity of the case, Highrise does try to provide opportunity for growth and learning with the tree system: warn, mute suspension, permanent ban. Underage/predatory/scamming: more serious offenses escalate punishment and will receive a ban instantly.


### **Q: Bullying/Racism occurring within the app?**


**A:** Highrise is a super international app: some people have different beliefs, values, and educational backgrounds. We want to provide an opportunity for teaching and growth by encouraging users to participate in open discussions and offer others the opportunity to learn. In more grey areas, staff slowly escalates punishment to teach users to understand what our values are and how to abide by them. People learn best through exposure. If people aren’t given a chance to learn about other cultures or communities, they won’t.


### **Q: Why do most items in-game require coins to purchase?**


**A:** Our economy is a gold-based economy. Rarity is determined by the economy. It’s tough to enforce scarcity with bubbles as they are easier to obtain and users can exploit them by creating multiple accounts.


### **Q: When will Android players be able to see what others are wearing by clicking on a post or outfit of the day?**


**A:** This was not intentionally removed — there was likely a bug that resulted in its removal. The Android dev team will follow up with this.


### **Q: When will emote loop be available for android users?**


**A:** Added to the list to be worked on.


### **Q: What inspired the origin of HR?:**


**A:** Highrise was founded about 8 years ago with the thought that traditional social networking, though growing rapidly, was not very dynamic or exciting. Sometimes it’s hard to make social connections through prevailing social media, and perhaps, the future of social networks could look more like a game. Could there be a way to deliver a more interactive, socially engaging experience online where the community owns the environment and the experiences? Could there be an app where you can be expressive while connecting and meeting new people through that experience?


Highrise has evolved over time with team-wide expansions. They are now a team of over 70 members. Today, Facebook has rebranded to Meta, Roblox has grown — there is a special connection between a social network and a game with plans to keep building and growing. It’s important for companies to stay sustainable: many companies didn’t evolve their products or haven’t found ways to sustain their economy. Highrise vows to constantly experiment and evolve in large ways by introducing and testing new ideas to monitor sustainability (obtaining feedback and monitoring data). The goal is for Highrise to be a space that people can always come back to.


### **Q: What are the plans to keep Highrise a social-first platform as we navigate into blockchain technology? Where is the gamers’ middle ground?**


**A:** NFTs/Crypto are technologies that can be used in a variety of different ways. It’s an idea of private property — you can own something and no one can take it away from you! It’s a unique object or item that allows users the ability to buy, sell or trade on any marketplace. It’s not competitive to the social first nature of the platform, as our platform is already oriented around virtual/digital goods and our economy and scarcity. This may actually amplify the virtual good aspect within Highrise with plans to migrate virtual goods content off chain in the future.


Highrise will continue to work on social first aspects of the app by building better rooms and experiences alongside tools for users to create their own content. Players will then have a reason to want to build the best room or the best event, as it can be a way for them to make money from owned content. Which in turn, should provide a better social experience all around!
