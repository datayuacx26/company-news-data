## New Horizons Ahead


Item designers, 3D spaces, community-made games and events: there’s a lot to look forward to with our upcoming Highrise 4.0 engine and the introduction of LAND in the greater *Highrise World* experience. In our effort to place this creative potential in the hands of our community, we’re constantly adapting our release strategy to provide our users with greater access to our most high-value features. Here’s how Phase 3 of our LAND sale has changed to serve the real owners of the Highrise World.

## How to buy LAND


Phase 1 of our LAND sale offered parcels to Creature Club NFT holders via whitelisted access, with each parcel of LAND priced at 0.06ETH; Phase 2 opened the following day with the same accessibility and LAND priced at 0.09ETH.


For Phase 3, we’re hosting an event in the Highrise app next week to help our community get more involved with building the metaverse. For those familiar, we’ll be utilizing the mechanics of our Lucky Token Blitz event to give special boosts to LAND owners — and provide some unique rewards to the top-performing crews and users.


Along with this event, we’re adding a **new way** to buy LAND: in addition to Ethereum, LAND will also be purchasable with a new currency called *LAND Deeds.*


*LAND Deeds* will be available starting next Tuesday, May 10th, via in-app purchase at $69.99USD per Deed token: 3 Deed tokens are required to purchase a parcel of LAND. To help you prepare, we’re launching Deeds a few days ahead of the official opening of the Phase 3 sale, which is currently scheduled for late next week. If you’re not in the Highrise app (yet!), LAND can also be acquired for 0.09ETH in Phase 3.


Looking for **Premium LAND plots?** Since these parcels come with extra perks and an ideal spot on the Creature Island map, they’ll require 9 Deed tokens to purchase.


Check out our site to view available LAND:[https://highrise.game/land](https://highrise.game/land)


## Already a Holder?


### Creature Club Holders


Own a Creature? Lucky you! We’re going to be airdropping Holders *1 Deed token per Creature owned.* If you’re one of the even luckier folks who owns a Legendary or 1-of-1 Creature, you’ll receive *3 Deed tokens.*


***Need to connect your Creature to your Highrise Account?*** Find our detailed FAQ on linking your wallet here:[https://highrise.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-highrise---your-avatar-community/faq/148-how-do-i-create-and-link-a-wallet-to-my-highrise-account/](https://highrise.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-highrise---your-avatar-community/faq/148-how-do-i-create-and-link-a-wallet-to-my-highrise-account/)


### LAND Owners


We’re sending all current LAND owners an additional *Deed token for each parcel owned.* If you’re the owner of a Premium LAND parcel, you’ll receive *3 Deed tokens.*


***As a quick note,*** these Deeds are **not** tradable. Right now, they can only be used in exchange for LAND. If you already own either NFT, these Deeds are our way of discounting your future LAND purchases.


## Minting your LAND


To prevent fraud and ensure the launch of the Deed currency is as smooth as possible, any LAND purchased using Deed tokens *will not be on the blockchain to start.*


However, you **will** be able to mint your new LAND just a week or two after Phase 3 of the LAND sale opens (we’re thinking after May 19th or so). It’s important to note here that **minting will require you to cover any gas fees.** If you purchase LAND during Phase 3 using Ethereum, the LAND will already live on the blockchain.

## I’ve got LAND! What’s next?


Congratulations! Here are a few of the features we’re most excited to see come to life with the launch of our new engine and Highrise World.


## Build your tower and form Estates


Each parcel of LAND is **yours** to do with as you please! Your LAND comes with its own Highrise tower: the appearance of your tower and the number of rooms it contains depends on whether it’s located on a Standard or Premium plot of LAND. Premium parcels are more centrally located near our world’s home base, *Highrise 101 (* aka, the Highrise experience that you already know), and also offer a few extra benefits, which will be unveiled the closer we get to our official opening.


**Estates** combine an individual LAND owner’s adjacent parcels into one mega-plot. Not only does this enable you to build deeper experiences, but you can also customize your Estate’s thumbnail on the World’s map and fully customize the branding for visitors to your Estate. In other words, you can attract a bigger audience and enhance your audience’s experiences.


## Create!


Highrise is a community of creators, so we’re excited to equip users with a host of dynamic tools and environments on our new Unity engine. We’ll keep sharing more technical details as the launch of Highrise World draws near, but you can look forward to:


**Item Designer:** Create your own clothing, avatar features, furniture and more with our Item Designer tools. These features are designed to be accessible, intuitive and easy to use, so anyone with a vision can transform their ideas into real assets. Anything you make will be yours to use as you please within your Highrise tower: decorate rooms with original interior pieces, deck out your avatar with home-drawn fits, or sell your work to earn an income.


**World Builder:** Okay, so you’ve got the assets — now it’s time to use them. We’re expanding our beloved isometric platform into 3-dimensional space with the migration to our Highrise 4.0 engine built in Unity. Not only will it be easier create your environments with a more intuitive design UI, but we’re incorporating a suite of complex game-making tools so that you can host interactive events and richer experiences for your tower’s visitors.


## Share with your community


Build and run your community exactly as you see fit. Imagine a subreddit or Discord server, but with all the customization and true ownership of a virtual world built on the blockchain. With cross platform compatibility, you can advertise your business or share your art to build your following across web and mobile alike. As each LAND parcel and tower develops its own identity and aesthetic, our platform is designed to make it easy for visitors to stop by and play, watch, and add to the experiences you create.
