## **Question:**


**What is Trust and Safety? What does the Trust and Safety team do? How do we contact the Trust and Safety team?**


## **Answer:**


Our Trust & Safety team is a team dedicated to keeping our application safe. They do this by resolving user reports and proactively monitoring user behavior in the newsfeed and rooms to remove bad actors as quickly as possible.


Depending on the situation, there are two main routes a user can take to help protect the community. Most issues can be resolved without directly contacting the T&S team: these issues may cover a broad spectrum, but could include things like hate speech, for example. Users can report guideline violations in-game using our report system to notify our T&S team; after sending a report, we recommend blocking the offending user.


For serious violations that can’t be handled within the scope of our in-game report system, users can escalate the issue to us directly. For emergencies requiring immediate attention, players can go to the FAQ and choose the **Report a Player** option to escalate. Please note, though, this is for serious violations: if your Report isn’t high priority, you may be directed to instead report the content in-game.

## **Question:**


**Why can’t Highrise return items to players that are lost when they are ‘hacked’?**


## **Answer:**


We understand that it can be stressful when your account is compromised, especially when items are lost. This is why we advocate for players to make sure their accounts are as secure as possible. We provide our users with the tools they need to keep their accounts safe, such as the safety lock feature, and we are also looking into adding additional features such as 2FA in the future. That being said, at the end of the day, account security is a user’s responsibility. So long as players do not share their passwords or safety pins, their accounts and items should remain uncompromised: if a user decides to share this information, then they are accepting the possible consequences of that decision. Password and pin sharing issues make up 99.99% of all of our tickets regarding compromised accounts. So please, don’t do it!


As for the lost items specifically, it’s simply not feasible to return lost items. By the time the player who compromised your account is tracked down and removed, these items have often been sold to unsuspecting players in legitimate trades. Unfortunately, we are not able to reverse all these trades; nor can we issue another copy of that item, as this type of duping could harm our in-game economy. So, at the end of the day, the best way to ensure your items stay safe is to keep your account as secure as possible. If you’re interested in securing your account, here are some things you can do:


1. Set an email address to your account that has not been involved in any database leaks. You can check this on[https://haveibeenpwned.com/](https://haveibeenpwned.com/)


2. Once you have this email set, make sure to set up 2FA if available on your email. This ensures no one can gain access to this email address and reset your password.


3. Use a secure password! Do not set a password that is easy to guess or that you’ve used on other applications. Set something unique with at least eight characters. Make sure to include punctuation and random characters (@,!,&). Doing this makes it impossible for a user to simply guess your password. You can even use an application that can generate and store extremely secure passwords for you, and the best part is it’s free —[https://www.lastpass.com/](https://www.lastpass.com/)


4. Enable **Safety Lock** on your account. Make sure the 6-digit pin is one you can remember but also one that other users cannot guess.


5. Never share your password or Safety Lock pin with anyone. This almost always leads to a user losing their items or account.

## **Question:**


**What tools is Highrise using to help keep users safe?**


## **Answer:**


We have a host of tools — some built in-house and some from software partners — to keep our users safe.


📝 **AI for text:**


We have an AI system that scans messages sent in public and analyzes their context and intent, and moderates messages based on our settings for each specific category. For example, we may allow cursing, but if the system detects a curse word being used in a child grooming manner, it would not let this message go through, and the message would immediately be flagged to us. This system is working behind the scenes, and not only is it enabled on our English servers, but it also functions many other languages.


📸 **AI for Photos:**


Whenever a player uploads a photo, we have a system in place that scans each photo before it is made public. If it determines that the photo contains pornographic content, it will block that photo from being uploaded. We are also looking into adding[Microsoft PhotoDNA](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/photodna) on top of this system. This will allow us to report CSAM material much quicker to authorities.


⚠️ **Report System & Manual Moderation:**


Users have the ability to report to us any violating content in-app. When these reports are sent to us, they come into a back-end system with loads of information, including the specific violating content that you reported. Our Trust & Safety team reviews each of these reports, and if they verify a guideline violation has occurred, they take appropriate action. These actions can be a warning, a temporary mute/suspension, or a permanent mute/suspension. Our team also monitors rooms and removes any rooms that are not meeting the standards set in our guidelines.


Players can also escalate issues to our T&S team that are outside the scope of our in-game report system. To do this, they can visit our FAQ page and open up a conversation with our concierge team and choose the ‘ **Report a Player** ’ option.


🔞 **Ability to Age Gate Features:**


We have the ability to age-gate features and content. If we ever release a feature that we think is more on the risque side or has a potential for abuse, we can always set a minimum age to use it. For example, the voice feature in our rooms is age-gated, and a player must be a minimum of 17 years of age to use it.


↕️ **Priority system:**


We’ve listened to our player’s concerns and have set a priority system for reports and issues our Trust & Safety team handles. With this system, we can prioritize our most important issues, such as Minor Safety and Sexual Content, to ensure we are removing this content as quickly as possible and removing and reporting the bad actors posting it.


## Question:


**What does Highrise do when they find and remove CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material)?**


## Answer:


When we confirm CSAM, we remove it immediately as well as the user that posted it. We then take all of the information we have regarding that user and report them to[Cybertips](https://report.cybertip.org/) . Cybertips then makes sure to act on the information and reports it to the appropriate authorities. If the authorities then reach out to us to follow up on a CSAM/Minor Safety case, we fully comply and provide any information needed. Minor Safety is our top priority and we will do everything in our power to make sure that anyone trying to abuse children on Highrise is brought to justice.
