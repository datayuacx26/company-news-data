Pocket Worlds has a ton of exciting benefits to help our crew lead healthy, balanced, awesome lives. No matter where you’re located, here are some pocket perks you can look forward to:


- Education budget
- Fitness allowance
- Paid parental leave
- Equipment and workspace sponsorship
- Remote, flexible hours
- Unlimited PTO
- Vacations and retreats!


### **BODY AND BRAIN: EDUCATION & FITNESS**


When it comes to self-improvement, we’ve got your back. You can count on a healthy yearly stipend for classes and seminars. Grab a new certification, or learn a new skill with specialized training- or maybe pick up that book you’ve been eyeing by your favorite industry leader. If you’re not sure what to study, no worries- our leadership team can guide you to find your next superpower, and will totally take time out of their busy days to do some research for you. Yeah, they’re THAT nice.


Okay so brain health is a go, but what about that body? While sitting isn’t necessarily the new smoking, that doesn’t mean that movement isn’t essential to feeling and performing your best. Meatheads rejoice! PW will fund your monthly gym or yoga studio membership. If your sport of choice doesn’t require a gym, use the fitness allowance to register for your next race or competition. If you only just picked up a new sport, don’t worry; your allowance accrues for up to three months.

### **MATTERS OF THE HEART: FAMILY**


Lots of us already have little ones, so trust us when we say we want you to take time for your family. Primary caregivers (obviously including adoptive and surrogate parents here!) are eligible for up to two months of 100% paid leave, and secondary caregivers can take one month of paid leave. If you need more time with baby, please take it! You can extend into additional unpaid leave for as long you want. Depending on where you live, you may qualify for statutory parental leave benefits from your state, in which case we’ll coordinate with you to fill the gaps. Those first smiles and steps only come once, so enjoy them! And maybe share some pictures with the PW fam ❤

### **WORK ANYWHERE, ANYTIME**


Being a fully distributed team means that you can get to live wherever you want, anywhere in the world, and hop online any time of day that works best for you. This isn’t a typical 9–5; we’re scattered across several timezones, so chances are there’s always at least one of us online! Set your own schedule, and don’t worry about checking a timesheet for hours clocked. We make an effort to cut down busywork so that you can focus on meaningful projects.

### **YOUR NEST: WORKSPACE AND EQUIPMENT**


To help you set up a dedicated workspace, we’ll reimburse you for your new desk, chair, lamp, fidget spinner- whatever you need to get focused and make some magic. If you feel like a home office is cramping your style, why not look for a co-working space? We’ll help fund your membership if you’d prefer to rent out an office nook in your city. Ah, breathe the fresh air!


We don’t stop at physical office spaces- we’re delighted to sponsor your new computer, or cover any components that you feel need an upgrade. Best of all, that new hardware is entirely yours to keep after a two year tenure with us, so don’t be shy!


### **VACATIONS AND TIME OFF**


Did someone say unlimited paid time off? Take the time you need to run errands or hang out with your family without worrying about running out of vacation hours. We trust that you know what’s best for you!


We do a pretty good job of kicking it in Slack and Zoom, but you better bet we love seeing each others’ gorgeous faces in person too. Each team gets a special budget to coordinate in-person meet-ups, virtual happy hours, and game nights. We also book annual all-inclusive getaways in diverse locales for the whole staff to reconnect, relax, and reinvigorate. So make sure that your passport is up to date, and we’ll see you there!
