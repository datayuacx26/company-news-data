# Welcoming Edward Hsu as SingleStore’s Chief Product Officer

# Welcoming Edward Hsu as SingleStore’s Chief Product Officer


Aug 3, 2026


•


2 min read


•


[Raj Verma, CEO](https://www.singlestore.com/blog/author/raj-verma/)


At SingleStore, we are building the data platform for a world in which AI is no longer separate from the applications and decisions that run a business. As that shift accelerates, product leadership requires more than a strong technology roadmap. It requires a clear understanding of how data, AI and customer needs come together in products people can use and businesses can scale.


Today, I’m excited to welcome Edward Hsu to SingleStore as our Chief Product Officer.


Edward brings an unusual combination of technical depth, product leadership and commercial experience. He has spent his career helping companies navigate major platform shifts, from virtualization and cloud infrastructure to AI-powered applications. He understands how to turn ambitious technical capabilities into coherent products, align engineering and go-to-market teams around a shared direction and build the operating discipline needed to deliver that vision.


Most recently, Edward served as Chief Product Officer at Mixpanel, where he led the company’s expansion into AI-powered product intelligence. He defined the vision for Mixpanel AI, bringing together continuous KPI monitoring, autonomous root-cause analysis and experimentation in a new agentic product experience. Before that, as Chief Product Officer at Rescale, he helped lead the company’s evolution from a cloud high-performance computing platform into AI-powered digital engineering. Earlier in his career, he advised companies on enterprise IT strategy and optimization at McKinsey.


What stands out to me is not simply the range of Edward’s experience, but its relevance to the moment SingleStore is entering. AI is changing what enterprises expect from their data infrastructure. Applications must work with data as it changes, understand its business context and produce answers that people and agents can trust. That is the opportunity behind SingleStore and Aura Intelligence: bringing transactions, analytics, search and AI together so customers can build intelligent applications without assembling a maze of databases, pipelines and disconnected tools.


As Chief Product Officer, Edward will lead our global product organization and help translate that opportunity into a focused product strategy. He will work closely with engineering, customers and our go-to-market teams to shape the next generation of SingleStore products and make their value clear from the first experience through the largest enterprise deployments.


Edward has repeatedly helped technology companies turn major platform shifts into products customers can adopt and businesses can scale. His command of AI, infrastructure and product strategy makes him the right leader for this moment at SingleStore. I’m excited to work with him as we build the data foundation for the next generation of intelligent applications.


We are delighted to welcome Edward to SingleStore and to our leadership team. His arrival strengthens our ability to execute on an ambitious product vision at precisely the moment when the market is ready for it.
