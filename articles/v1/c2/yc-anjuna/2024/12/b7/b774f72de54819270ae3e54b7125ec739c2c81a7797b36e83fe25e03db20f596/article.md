# Anjuna Launches Anjuna Northstar: A First-of-Its-Kind AI Data Fusion Clean Room Now Generally Available

**PALO ALTO, Calif., Dec 2, 2024** –[Anjuna](https://www.anjuna.io/) , creator of Anjuna Seaglass, the Universal Confidential Computing Platform, today announced the general availability of[Anjuna Northstar](https://www.anjuna.io/product/northstar) , the world’s first AI Data Fusion Clean Room. This transformative solution defines a new category designed to enable limitless AI-driven data value discovery. The company also announced two of its product design partners:[JUMO](https://jumo.world/) , a market leader in AI-led financial technology, and[Ascendo AI](https://www.ascendo.ai/) , a leader in AI agents and coworkers for technical support.


**Overcoming Limitations of Traditional Data Clean Rooms**


As demand for collaborative AI analysis grows, organizations need solutions that balance security and privacy with flexibility. Traditional data clean rooms were built for narrow use cases and fail at handling complex workflows and integrating custom AI models. This limits data utility and restricts opportunities for collaborative growth and revenue optimization.


**Fusing AI with Enterprise Data to Discover its Hidden Value**


Anjuna Northstar helps organizations combine datasets from multiple sources to conduct complex AI-driven analytics without compromising security and privacy. This novel approach defines a new category for AI Data Fusion Clean Rooms, allowing data scientists to discover the hidden value of data assets that unlocks new business opportunities between and within organizations. Key capabilities include:


- **Fast and Scalable** : Create new confidential clean rooms with a single click, across any cloud provider, with no development effort required, enabling faster time-to-market.
- **Universal** : Built on general-purpose Confidential Computing instances, it securely supports running in any cloud and any application, AI model, tool or code—without modifications.
- **Interactive** : Data scientists can prepare datasets within the secure environment using standard off-the-shelf Jupyter Notebooks, reducing bottlenecks.
- **Intrinsically Secure** : With a Confidential Computing foundation, data and code are protected during ingestion, processing, and analysis. Remote attestation and data-in-use isolation help minimize risk and maintain full access control.


> “Anjuna Northstar is built for any enterprise that views data as a strategic asset, and today, that includes nearly every business,” said Ayal Yogev, CEO and Co-Founder of Anjuna. “We’ve built a unique solution that acts as the secure reactor for the most valuable fusion reaction: fusing diverse data sources through the power of AI. The result unlocks new business value, revenue streams and opportunities for innovation in ways that were previously impossible due to security or privacy concerns.”


**JUMO and Ascendo AI Discover New Business Opportunities**


Anjuna Northstar meets critical needs in a wide range of regulated industries where data security and compliance are paramount. The following design partners illustrate the platform’s versatile applications:


[JUMO](https://jumo.world/) **** has built AI-led financial technology that enables a new generation of digital credit and savings products to millions of entrepreneurs in Africa. JUMO uses Anjuna Northstar to enhance their credit risk workflow, by securely leveraging partner's data, which leads to better informed lending decisions.


> "Anjuna Northstar aids us in supporting our partners in unprecedented ways" said Fábio Matos, CTO of JUMO. "We believe this technology, which allows us to securely merge our proprietary data and AI models with our partners' customer transaction data, while keeping both sides of the IP protected, will become the AI data handling standard in the future and so we are partnering with Anjuna to be at the forefront of that important transformation. This also enables our partners to unlock even more lending opportunities in previously underserved market segments, by allowing them to securely use all their relevant data."


‍[Ascendo AI](https://www.ascendo.ai/) , **** a leader in AI agents and coworkers for technical support, uses Anjuna Northstar to securely aggregate data from sources such as support tickets and internal documentation to train and deploy customer service models. Sensitive customer data remains protected throughout the AI lifecycle, enabling their customers to deliver better, personalized service without compromising privacy.


> "With Anjuna Northstar, we can deliver tailored technical support and field service solutions that respect privacy requirements. The Anjuna platform, along with Ascendo AI’s Cognitive Privacy application, provides the security and flexibility needed to process sensitive information from multiple sources, driving both compliance and customer satisfaction.” said Kay Narayanan, Founder and CEO of Ascendo AI.


The general availability of[Anjuna Northstar](https://www.anjuna.io/product/northstar) marks a significant milestone for Anjuna and the industry, setting a new standard for AI-data fusion and multi-party collaboration.
