# Diversified Energy Announces Second Quarter Dividend


August 05, 2026 4:16 PM EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_5e1fd2d4b581702e765d626d94424b67/dgoc/news/2026-08-05_Diversified_Energy_Announces_Second_Quarter_228.pdf)


BIRMINGHAM, Ala., Aug. 05, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Diversified Energy Company (NYSE:DEC, LSE: DEC) (“Diversified” or “the Company”) is pleased to announce that the Board has declared a dividend of 29 cents per share in respect of the three-month period ended June 30, 2026.


Key dates related to this dividend include:


**Record Date:** December 2, 2026


**Payment Date:** December 31, 2026


**Default Currency:** US Dollar


**Currency Election Option:** Sterling


**Last Date for Currency Election:** December 8, 2026


Diversified will pay the dividend in U.S. dollars while continuing to make available to shareholders a sterling election. For those shareholders who wish to receive their dividend payment in sterling, and who have not yet completed a currency election form, the Company has made available a dividend election form on its website at[https://ir.div.energy/dividend-information](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=UI3280wBDwiLVGj-pUaR_1dVV3lGUoFA0WzxQZfMnDJO-Ot2CQrb5vj9oY2s420dyg8JfYEmmkFauglL72brTKuSa5LETBA-bHsZJZN_WOvc-Zu-vtoIHJwmiVXFaHhljNqeSxbvWt15KSbJ7Y0swg==) . Shareholders who wish to receive sterling should submit the currency election form to Computershare Investor Services no later than December 8, 2026.


Diversified will announce the sterling value of the dividend payable per share approximately two weeks prior to the payment date.
