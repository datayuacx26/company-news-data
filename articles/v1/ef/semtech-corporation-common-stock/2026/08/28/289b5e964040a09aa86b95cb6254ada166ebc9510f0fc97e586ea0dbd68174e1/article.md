# Semtech Corporation to Host Data Center Teach-in Event on Oct. 15, 2026


*Event to feature strategy overview, technology portfolio and long-term financial objectives from Semtech leadership*


**CAMARILLO, Calif. Aug 17- Semtech Corporation (Nasdaq: SMTC),** a leading provider of high-performance semiconductors powering AI data center networking and intelligent, connected Internet of Things (“IoT”) devices worldwide, will host a teach-in for its Data Center business on Thursday, Oct. 15, 2026, in San Jose, Calif. The event will begin at 9 a.m. PT.


Semtech President and Chief Executive Officer Dr. Hong Hou, Senior Vice President and General Manager of its Signal Integrity Products Group Imran Sherazi, and Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer Mark Lin, will provide an in-depth overview of the company's strategy, differentiated technology portfolio, key growth opportunities, and long-term financial objectives. The event will also feature a panel discussion with industry experts, question-and-answer sessions, product demonstrations, and opportunities for in-person attendees to engage with members of Semtech management.


Additional information regarding the agenda and registration will be provided in the coming weeks. Due to space limitations, in-person attendance will be limited, and advance registration will be required.


A live webcast of the teach-in, along with the accompanying presentation materials, will be available under "News & Events" on the Investor Relations section of Semtech’s website at[investors.semtech.com](https://investors.semtech.com/) . A replay of the webcast will be available following the conclusion of the event.
