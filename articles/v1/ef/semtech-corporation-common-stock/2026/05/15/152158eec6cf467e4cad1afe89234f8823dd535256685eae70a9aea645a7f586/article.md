# Semtech Announces First Quarter of Fiscal Year 2027 Conference Call


*Semtech announced plans to release the financial results of its first quarter of fiscal year 2027 after the close of the market on Tuesday, May 26, 2026.*


**CAMARILLO, Calif. May 12- Semtech Corporation (Nasdaq: SMTC)** , a leading provider of high-performance semiconductors powering data center networking, Internet of Things (“IoT”) connectivity and cellular infrastructure solutions, announced plans to release the financial results of its first quarter of fiscal year 2027 after the close of the market on Tuesday, May 26, 2026. The results will be released through Business Wire and posted at[www.semtech.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.semtech.com&esheet=54529894&newsitemid=20260505623374&lan=en-US&anchor=www.semtech.com&index=1&md5=73e1c14464fb99ae98744e3f2ee63ecc) .


Semtech will host a conference call at 1:30 p.m. PT (4:30 p.m. ET) on Tuesday, May 26, 2026, to discuss its first quarter of fiscal year 2027 performance and events, current business activities and conditions, and the outlook for the business. Participants can dial in to the call at 877-407-0312 (toll-free) or 201-389-0899 (toll/international) with conference ID 13760245. The event will also be[webcast live](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.webcast-eqs.com%2Flogin%2Fsemtechq1_27&esheet=54529894&newsitemid=20260505623374&lan=en-US&anchor=webcast+live&index=2&md5=ec46ad703832f123398678c4b8341c6e) . The webcast link will be accessible under the[Investor Events](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.semtech.com%2Fnews-events%2Finvestor-events%2F&esheet=54529894&newsitemid=20260505623374&lan=en-US&anchor=Investor+Events&index=3&md5=fea8c7602ad49da996996f288bae35ff) section of Semtech’s[Investor website](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.semtech.com%2F&esheet=54529894&newsitemid=20260505623374&lan=en-US&anchor=Investor+website&index=4&md5=b72a11fbb41a28f04d351413c91bcb15) . A replay and transcript of the webcast will be available after the conclusion of the live call.
