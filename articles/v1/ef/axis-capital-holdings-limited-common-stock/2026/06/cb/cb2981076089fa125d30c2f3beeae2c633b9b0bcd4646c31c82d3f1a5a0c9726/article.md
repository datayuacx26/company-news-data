**Christina Geller to Lead Wholesale Financial Lines at AXIS**


PEMBROKE, Bermuda, June 3, 2026--AXIS Capital Holdings Limited (“AXIS Capital” or “AXIS” or the “Company”) (NYSE: AXS), today announced the promotion of Christina Geller to Head of Wholesale Financial Lines in North America.


Previously, Ms. Geller served as Head of Wholesale Financial Institutions. She has spent a combined 18 years serving in senior underwriting and leadership positions within AXIS from 2005 to 2012 and then returning to the firm from 2013 to present.


Reporting to John Van Decker, Head of Global Financial Lines, Ms. Geller will develop and deliver the strategy for long-term profitable growth across AXIS’ portfolio of North American wholesale commercial and financial institutions business.


“Christina’s promotion and broadened role reflect the exceptional underwriting expertise, market insight, and leadership she brings to AXIS,” commented Mr. Van Decker. “She has built a strong reputation within the industry for technical excellence, and for her commitment to developing talent. I’m confident Christina will further elevate how we go to market and service our customers.”


Ms. Geller also previously held management liability underwriting roles at AIG and CNA Insurance.
