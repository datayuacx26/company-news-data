# Tessa AI is now Altrina

**Tessa AI is now Altrina.**


This rebrand is a reflection of how far our vision has expanded.


When Harvey and I started building what eventually became Tessa, we assumed the bottleneck inside organizations was tooling. We were software engineers who spent too much time doing… everything *except* the work we were hired for. Jira tickets. Reviews. Automated tests. Endless sheets. Meetings where half the room didn't want to admit they weren't fully aligned.


Across Nvidia, TikTok, Google — the story was the same:
**As teams grew, the amount of time spent actually creating value went down.**


The deeper problem wasn't automation gaps or missing integrations.
It was human hesitation. Misalignment. A fear of "looking out of the loop."
Decision-making slowed. Execution slowed. Creativity slowed.


So we built a browser agent to wrap around existing tools.


But after dozens of customer interviews, including one pivotal visit where a single "workflow" turned out to be step 1 in a painful 22-step human process, we realized something else:


**Organizations don't need more tools.**
**They need clarity. Alignment. Shared understanding.**
**They need a system that knows what everyone is doing, why, and where it all connects.**


And that's not a browser agent problem.
That's a human problem.


### Why Altrina


*Altrina* comes from *Alternate + Intelligence* :
a parallel system that doesn't replace humans, but replaces the unfulfilling jobs that block humans from doing their *actual* work.


We still use browser agents, but only as instruments inside a much larger platform; something capable of handling entire processes end-to-end. Not point solutions, but delegable systems. A kind of organizational autopilot.


An AI-native workspace that learns the values, goals, and objectives of your team, and then keeps everyone aligned without the bureaucracy.


**If we succeed, a Fortune 500 company will move as fast as a 50-person startup.**


Judgment and creativity remain human.
Repetition, coordination, information chasing — Altrina handles that.


### This is the next chapter


Beyond an automation platform, we're building an **organizational operating system** *.* Something that gives people back their time, removes the fear of misalignment, and replaces internal bureaucracy with clarity.


In every organization we've studied, people quietly ask themselves every day:


- *What exactly am I doing?*
- *What's the end result?*
- *How does this fit into the bigger picture?*


Altrina answers those questions automatically.


This is what the next decade of work will look like, and today, we're proud to take the first step under our new name.


**We're not trying to replace humans.**
**We're trying to replace unfulfilling work.**


Welcome to Altrina.


-[Mo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohammednasir/) , CEO & Co-Founder
