# Roost Design and Build Selects Fiber Fast Homes to Install Fiber to the Home Internet in Its Belle Vie Development in Honey Grove, TX

*Customer Service, Fiber Bandwidth Big Reasons Roost Chose Fiber Fast Homes*


[Fiber Fast Homes](https://www.fiberfasthomes.com/) , rapidly growing fiber to the home Internet service provider, announced it will partner with Roost Design & Build to provide fiber to the home Internet to 1800 homes in their[Belle Vie](https://bellevietx.com/) community outside Dallas over the next 10 years. Fiber Fast Homes’ fiber 2 Gigabit solution will allow excellent Wi-Fi coverage to the expansive community.


Belle Vie is one of the area’s premier developments designed by Josh and Ashley Eckel, who have recently been featured on HGTV for their architectural prowess. In pre-sale now, Belle Vie will feature a beautiful state-of-the-art marina on Bois D’ Arc Lake, a chapel, a boutique hotel and quaint restaurants throughout the community. The development’s design took inspiration from the charm of Lake Como, Italy and incorporated the high design standards found at Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach in Florida.


“We are thrilled to partner with Roost on the Belle Vie development,” said Randy Cosby, CEO of Fiber Fast Homes. “We eagerly anticipate contributing to the cutting-edge vision that the Roost team has for this community. Our expertise in delivering top-notch fiber Internet infrastructure and customer service was a key factor in Roost’s decision to work with us. Roost’s commitment to future-proofing their homes with high-bandwidth fiber connectivity ensures that residents will have the robust and reliable Internet service they need now and for years to come.”


“We are very happy to be working with Fiber Fast Homes to deliver the world’s fastest broadband technology,” said Josh Eckel, founder and CEO of Roost Design and Build. “By offering fiber to the home, we are ensuring residents will have the bandwidth to support their everyday lives.”
