# Fiber Fast Homes’ Brendan Hobbs Recognized with Jacksonville Business Journal Rising Stars Award

***Spotlighted in the Transportation and Logistics Category***


[Fiber Fast Homes](https://www.fiberfasthomes.com/) , rapidly growing fiber to the home Internet service provider, announced that Brendan Hobbs, manager, procurement and warehousing, has been recognized by the *Jacksonville Business Journal* as a Rising Stars Award winner in the category of Logistics. The Rising Stars Awards were first


According to the *Journal* , the Rising Stars is an exciting new awards program designed to recognize those in the Jacksonville, FL area who are on the ascent of their career. Throughout the course of the year, the *Journal* honors those who will be future leaders in four industries: legal, finance, real estate and logistics. The awards program is not an age-bound program and is focused on those who are on their way up: This can be someone a few years out of college, someone who has launched a new venture or someone who has embarked on a new path. Honorees are selected by a panel of local leaders and are judged based both on past accomplishments, promise of future accomplishment, involvement in the community and other attributes.


“We are very happy for Brendan to have won this prestigious award,” said Scott Sampson, CEO of Fiber Fast Homes. “It is nice to see that the *Jacksonville Business Journal* and the Jacksonville business community are recognizing Brendan for something we have known for a while that he is indeed a rising star and has a great future ahead of him here at Fiber Fast Homes.”


### About Fiber Fast Homes


Fiber Fast Homes is a fiber to the home Internet service provider that partners with developers and builders to provide fiber Internet at Gig speeds. Fiber Fast Homes offers service to new home and build-to-rent communities across the country. They take pride in the high quality of customer service they provide to both their partners and their community residents. Fiber Fast Homes is a subsidiary of Boston Omaha Corporation (NYSE:BOC).
