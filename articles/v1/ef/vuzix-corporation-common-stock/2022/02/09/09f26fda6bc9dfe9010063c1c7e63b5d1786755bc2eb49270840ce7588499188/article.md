# TeleVU and Vuzix Equip Healthcare Workers with the Power of Hands-Free AR, AI

**Removing geographical barriers to specialized care**


Virtual healthcare is taking the industry by storm…and why shouldn’t it? A world where anyone can receive immediate access to a healthcare specialist regardless of location is worth pursuing.


Remote patient care leader


[TeleVU](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://televu.ca/) has partnered with Vuzix® to make this a reality.


The arrangement is simple, yet it carries the


[potential for global change](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://finance.yahoo.com/news/vuzix-televu-webinar-highlight-smart-150000174.html) .


TeleVU creates user-friendly communication software that connects patients with doctors.


Vuzix provides smart glasses that allow specialists to harness the power of


[augmented reality, assisted reality](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/Vuzix-a-Global-Leader-in-Assisted-and-Augmented-Reality-Technology) , and


[artificial intelligence](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/Vuzix-M4000-M400-Smart-Glasses-AI-Artificial-Intelligence-TechSee) . Did we mention this access is completely hands-free?


Healthcare specialists don’t have to be on the same continent to treat a patient thanks to Vuzix’s partnership with TeleVU.


**The future of patient care**


Vuzix smart glasses provide a live audio-visual link between frontline clinicians and remote experts from anywhere in the world via TeleVU.


The two companies


[hosted a joint webinar walking healthcare leaders through the technology](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://www.vuzix.com/blogs/webinars/webinar-televu) on November 30th, 2021.


**RELATED:**[Smart Glasses Could Revolutionize Healthcare & Education in the 2020s](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/smart-glasses-could-revolutionize-healthcare-and-education-in-the-2020s)


How Vuzix and TeleVU make life easy for healthcare workers while saving lives:


- **IoT excellence: Specialists can summon information [using their voice](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vuzix-enters-into-a-relationship-with-vivoka-to-provide-industry-leading-voice-recognition-language-support-for-vuzix-m-series-smart-glasses-301402231.html) . No matter the procedure, life-saving techniques and know-how can be accessed hands-free.**
- **** **Assisted reality: Like tracing a picture, specialists can follow virtual procedure walkthroughts that project digital instructions over the real-world. [This technology is set to automate routine procedures, as even the most junior healthcare pro could step in and mimic the movements provided by the visual instructions - much like a video game.](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/Knee-Shoulder-Spine-Surgeries-Powered-by-Vuzix-Smart-Glasses)**
- **AI and machine learning: The devices feature top-of-the-line automation capabilities. The more they’re used, the more efficiently they’ll be able to help healthcare workers adapt to even the most complex procedures.**
- **Contact free: This technology is invaluable in the wake of social distancing protocols. [Doctors would never have to be in the same room as their patients](https://web.archive.org/web/20250615112636/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/Vuzix-Smart-Glasses-Augmented-Reality-AR-Tech-Surgery-Remote-Work-Technology-01) when equipped with Vuzix smart glasses.**
