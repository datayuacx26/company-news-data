# Vuzix Shield Smart Glasses Earn CES 2022 Innovation Awards Recognition

**Vuzix named multiple Innovation Awards honoree for new product line**


The CESⓇ 2022 Innovation Awards took place on January 5th, in Las Vegas. Yet, VuzixⓇ was recognized by


[the elite panel of industry expert judges who hosted the event](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://ces.tech/Innovation-Awards/Meet-the-CES-2021-Judges.aspx) before 2021 drew to a close.


[Vuzix Shield™ smart glasses](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://www.vuzix.com/technology/next-gen) , the first binocular AR (augmented reality) smart glasses for enterprise that feature a powerful set of microLED projectors and HD cameras, earned Innovation Awards honoree status.


This achievement comes amidst the highest level of competition the event ever saw.


[CES ‘22](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://ces.tech/Innovation-Awards/Honorees.aspx) received over 1,800 award submissions.


**RELATED:**[Businesses can Broadcast to the World with Vuzix Smart Glasses](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/businesses-can-broadcast-to-the-world-with-vuzix-smart-glasses)


**Why Vuzix Shield smart glasses earned CES ‘22 honoree status** :


- First binocular AR smart glasses for enterprise with HD stereo cameras
- MicroLED displays
- Prescription-ready
- [Z87.1 safety glasses rating](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://blog.safetyglassesusa.com/what-does-ansi-z87-1-certified-mean/)
- Powered by an 8-core CPU that runs Android 11 OS
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth enabled


Vuzix Shield devices will allow business owners, operators, and workers access to the metaverse. Hands-free and voice-activated, it offers seamless access to AR tools in


[healthcare](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/Healthcare-Industry-to-Benefit-from-Marriage-of-5G-and-Smart-Glasses) ,


[warehousing](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/ar-smart-glasses-warehouse-productivity) , and


[field service](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://www.vuzix.com/Blog/smart-glasses-field-service-professionals) , among other industries.


Vuzix Shield earned much attention during CES 2022. The first binocular AR smart glasses with HD stereo cameras, the device is poised to become a game changer for business leaders.


**A pillar of innovation**


2022 marks the 18th straight year Vuzix has been honored at CES.


[Vuzix President & CEO Paul Travers](https://web.archive.org/web/20251007152926/https://www.linkedin.com/in/paul-travers-534b336) acknowledged this is longer than the life span of most competitors in the wearable display space.


Whether it’s a warehouse worker scanning barcodes, or a technician streaming their first-person view to off-site colleagues, Vuzix products make it easy to perform countless tasks hands-free.
