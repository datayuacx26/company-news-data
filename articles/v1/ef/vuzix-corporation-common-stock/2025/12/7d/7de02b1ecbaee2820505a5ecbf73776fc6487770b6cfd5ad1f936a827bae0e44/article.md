# Waveguide Technology: A Critical Component for AR

Waveguide technology is a crucial component for wearable AR devices, enabling the seamless display of digital information onto the real world. It acts as a transparent medium that guides light to the eye from a micro-projector, allowing for a compact and lightweight device. For wearable AR systems, this “see-through” technology is optimal for maintaining situational awareness, or for offering blended or mixed reality (XR) creating experiences through increasingly lightweight, ergonomic, and in a word, invisible form factors. As the market for AR (and the features it enables, specifically AI assistants) grows across industries like healthcare, manufacturing, and entertainment, the demand for waveguide technology that can be integrated into a design that looks and feels like an ordinary pair of prescription eyewear is the primary challenge to widespread adoption. Building this technology across a wide range of styles that cater to end user sensibilities is yet another big milestone towards mainstreaming this technology.
