# Vuzix CES 2023: See in Infrared, Save the Power Grid, and Work in 3D

CES is in just a few days and team Vuzix is ready to walk guests through our exciting lineup of experiences. Want a sneak peek at what’s in store for the floor? Let’s jump right in.


**A blast from the past: Infrared technology**


Originally unveiled at the prestigious Digital X event in Cologne Germany earlier in 2022, Vuzix enables users to see in the infrared spectrum. Simply using our accessory adaptor and a compatible infrared camera, including FLIR cameras, your M-Series smart glasses can give you a heads-up display of what’s normally hidden from view. In fact, we can broadcast infrared video over Zoom to enable team inspections. But that’s just one small way that our ultra versatile smart glasses can assist in the workplace. Learn how other accessories can expand the power of your smart glasses and see this technology in action at the booth.


**Be the Hero!**


Vuzix is putting you in the role of some of our field and industrial use cases, and now the entire local power grid rests in your hands. Luckily you have a pair of Vuzix Blade 2 smart glasses on your side. Using on-glasses video and work instructions, even complicated tasks like resetting a power station are no sweat.


Love sexy bikes? How about sexy glasses? Put on a pair of Vuzix Shield smart glasses and prepare to lay your hands on a real MV Agusta Superveloce 800 muscle racer! Vuzix will be putting you in the role of a quality check inspector as you pull one of the most beautiful bikes ever made off the assembly line to perform a scan for equipment recalls. Is it ready for the road? Using Vuzix Shield smart glasses, you’ll view individual parts in 3D and ultimately make the call. It’s an experience you won’t want to miss!


*MV Agusta Superveloce 800; Image Credit[Cycle News](https://web.archive.org/web/20251013150948/https://www.cyclenews.com/2021/02/article/2021-mv-agusta-superveloce-800-review/)*


Last but not least, explore how major distribution centers drive efficiency every day using Teamviewer and Vuzix M400 smart glasses. When it comes to power and performance, these glasses are a must see. Pick the items and pack them correctly on a pallet like a pro!


**Glasses for the masses**


Vuzix has spent years in the design and manufacture of optical components and waveguides. Coupled with a US based manufacturing facility and the top talent in the nation, Vuzix is helping bring AR technology into new markets with our sleek smart glasses reference design, called Vuzix Ultralite. Offering fully customizable form factors, optics and more, Vuzix OEM solutions produce smart glasses and components in support of the worldwide adoption of AR that is miles ahead of the pack.


Stop by booth #16991 to explore all that Vuzix has to offer in 2023. We can’t wait to see you!
