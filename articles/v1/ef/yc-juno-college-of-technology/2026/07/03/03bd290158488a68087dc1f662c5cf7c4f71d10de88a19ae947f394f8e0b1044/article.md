# Juno College announces new Recession Relief Scholarship

## Get up to $5,000 towards your career change with Juno College’s Web Development Bootcamp


At Juno, it’s always been our mission to break down barriers and support career changers on their tech journeys. A key part of this means closely monitoring and responding to our community’s needs and concerns — which is why we’re announcing the **Recession Relief Scholarship** , which awards career changers up to $5,000 towards Juno College’s[Web Development Bootcamp](https://junocollege.com/bootcamp/web-development-toronto/) .


With the rising costs of living and a looming recession at the top of our minds, we want to do our part to ensure that our community is supported during these times of economic uncertainty, with a strong focus on those who are from communities underrepresented in tech.


Read on to learn more about the scholarship and how to apply!


### Scholarship Overview


- **Up to $5,000** towards Juno’s Web Development Bootcamp (Full-Time or Part-Time)
- Needs-based and merit-based
- Short essay reviewed by the Admissions team
- **Scholarship deadline:** 1 month before the start of the Bootcamp
- **Limited amount available** — evaluated on a first come, first serve basis!


### Application Eligibility


- 18 years old or older
- Canadian Citizen or Permanent Resident of Canada
- Dedicated to making a career change into web development
- Submitted Bootcamp application by scholarship deadline
- Submitted written reflection by scholarship deadline


### Scholarship Application Process


1. [Submit your Bootcamp application online](https://junocollege.com/apply/) and begin our Admissions process
2. Check your email for a link to apply for the Recession Relief Scholarship
3. Submit your scholarship application for evaluation by our Admissions team — **you must submit your scholarship application 1 month before the start of your Bootcamp**


You’ll be notified upon acceptance into Bootcamp if you’ll be granted the scholarship. While our goal is to support as many applicants as we can, there is a limited amount of scholarships available, and scholarships are not guaranteed.


If you’re unable to finance your education without this scholarship, Admissions will work with you to discuss[other financing options](https://junocollege.com/scholarship/) such as Financeit loans and our Income Share Agreements program.


## Apply Now


Get started by applying to our Web Development Bootcamp! Once submitted, you’ll be emailed the scholarship form.


[Apply to Bootcamp](https://junocollege.com/apply/)
