# Best Places to Learn UX Design for Free

## Curious about learning UX Design?


We’ve all visited websites where we find ourselves taking a bit of extra time to admire the eye-popping layout, seamless navigation, or the simplicity of the search features. All of those website elements have been the result of a competent and capable UX designer!


### What is a UX Designer?


UX designers are often part of a larger team and are responsible for designing wireframes, conducting user testing, and conducting audience research to determine how users best interact with a product. This product can be a website, mobile app, video game, or other software. With careers in UX Design on the upswing, you may start to wonder how to start learning UX Design or how to become a UX Designer yourself.


### User Experience Design Courses


Whether you’re already in tech or looking to get into the field, taking a UX design course is a good first step in determining if the field is right for you.


Testing out whether or not you want to become a UX designer is best done through a free UX design course. There are plenty of courses available that teach you how to learn UX design principles. Here are a few user experience design courses that will allow you to dip your toe in the UX design waters.


And if you decide to dive deeper into the world of UX design after taking a free course, Juno College offers a[part-time UX design course](https://junocollege.com/course/ux-design/) taught by professionals in the field that can help jumpstart your career in UX!

#### Coursera/Google UX Design Professional Certificate


In partnership with Coursera, Google offers a 100% free of charge[UX Design Certificate course](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-ux-design) to those interested in how to learn UX design. No relevant experience is required and study time comes in at just under 10 hours per week for six months. The course also provides you with a certificate of completion which is beneficial should you decide to search for a full-time position in the field of UX design.


#### LinkedIn Learning


A free 30-day trial of[LinkedIn Learning](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/topics/ux-design) is more than enough time to take this 12-hour self-directed UX design course. This course leads you through the basic principles of UX design and helps you design great user experiences across both mobile and the desktop platforms.


#### LearnUX.io


Created by a designer with 15 years experience at Fortune 500 companies,[LeanUX.io](https://learnux.io/) is video-based learning, offering modules on UX design principles and programs such as user testing, Adobe XD, accessibility, and careers in UX design. Course levels range from beginner to experienced and are a great way to pick and choose which areas of UX design you want to learn most about.


#### General Assembly: Intro to UX Design


General Assembly is known for its UX design, coding, and data courses, but it also provides a[free two-hour livestream on UX design](https://generalassemb.ly/education/intro-to-ux-design-free-online-livestream) . The stream is led by industry professionals who assign optional classwork that give you a taste for real life projects and even conduct a live Q&A to have all your burning UX design questions answered. Sessions are held regularly.


#### Udemy


Free courses on everything UX design can be found on[Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/topic/user-experience-design/free/) . Specific specializations such as UX design for an Apple Watch to general fundamentals can be found on the website through their tutorials. Classes range in length but all are learned at your own pace and on your own time.
