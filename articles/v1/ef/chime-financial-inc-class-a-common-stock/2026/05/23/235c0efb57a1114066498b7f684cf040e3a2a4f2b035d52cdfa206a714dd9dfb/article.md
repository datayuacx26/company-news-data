# Chime to Participate in William Blair Growth Stock Conference

## Chime to Participate in William Blair Growth Stock Conference


May 14, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.chime.com/node/7706/pdf)


SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 14, 2026-- Chime® (Nasdaq: CHYM) announced today that CEO and Co-founder Chris Britt will participate in a fireside chat at the William Blair Growth Stock Conference in Chicago on Wednesday, June 3, 2026 at 12:40 p.m. Central Daylight Time (10:40 a.m. Pacific Daylight Time).


A live webcast of the fireside chat will be accessible on Chime’s Investor Relations website at[investors.chime.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.chime.com&esheet=54534250&newsitemid=20260513045541&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.chime.com&index=1&md5=1296dc5682d7d5ba44036a00b951ef4e) . A replay will be available for a limited period of time following the conference.
