# Chime to Announce First Quarter 2026 Financial Results on May 6, 2026

## Chime to Announce First Quarter 2026 Financial Results on May 6, 2026


April 15, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.chime.com/node/7631/pdf)


SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 15, 2026-- Chime® (Nasdaq: CHYM), America’s #1 Choice for Banking1, will release financial results for the first quarter 2026 on May 6, 2026 after market close. Chime will hold a conference call to discuss the results at 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time (5:00 p.m. Eastern Time) on the same day.


A live webcast of the earnings conference call will be accessible on the Events & Presentations section of Chime’s Investor Relations website at[investors.chime.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.chime.com&esheet=54508121&newsitemid=20260415962930&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.chime.com&index=1&md5=54917fa1b52543f2fc3fbee139867613) . A replay will be available on the website following the call.


**About Chime:**


Chime (Nasdaq: CHYM) is a financial technology company founded on the premise that core banking services should be helpful, easy, and free. We offer a broad range of low-cost banking and payments products that address the most critical financial needs of everyday people. Our member-aligned business model has helped millions of people to unlock financial progress™. Member deposits are FDIC-insured through The Bancorp Bank, N.A. or Stride Bank, N.A., Members FDIC, up to applicable limits2.


1Based on a blind May 2026 representative survey conducted by Chime using a third-party survey platform, Chime was chosen among listed financial services providers more often by consumers who opened a new personal checking account at a different institution within the preceding six months. Chime offers access to checking accounts used for everyday banking.


2Chime® is a financial technology company, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided by The Bancorp Bank, N.A. or Stride Bank, N.A., Members FDIC.
