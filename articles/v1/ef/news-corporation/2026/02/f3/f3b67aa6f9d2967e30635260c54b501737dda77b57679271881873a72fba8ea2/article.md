# News Corp to Host Dow Jones Investor Briefing

**News Corp to Host Dow Jones Investor Briefing**


**New York, NY (February 5, 2026)** – News Corp announced today it will host an Investor Briefing for Dow Jones on Monday, March 16, 2026, in New York City. Presentations will begin at 4:00 PM EDT (Sydney: March 17, at 7:00 a.m. AEDT).


News Corp Chair Lachlan Murdoch and Chief Executive Robert Thomson will be joined by Dow Jones Chief Executive Officer and Publisher, The Wall Street Journal Almar Latour and other members of Dow Jones’s leadership team. The presentation will showcase the business’s transformation into a news, enterprise data and information services powerhouse, and highlight its growth strategy and financial profile.


A live view-only webcast of the call will be available via[http://investors.newscorp.com](http://investors.newscorp.com/) . A replay will be available shortly after the event’s conclusion. Due to limited capacity, in-person attendance will be by invitation only.


**About News Corp**
News Corp (Nasdaq: NWS, NWSA; ASX: NWS, NWSLV) is a global, diversified media and information services company focused on creating and distributing authoritative and engaging content and other products and services. The company comprises businesses across a range of media, including: information services and news, digital real estate services and book publishing. Headquartered in New York, News Corp operates primarily in the United States, Australia and the United Kingdom, and its content and other products and services are distributed and consumed worldwide. More information is available at:[http://newscorp.com](http://newscorp.com/) .
