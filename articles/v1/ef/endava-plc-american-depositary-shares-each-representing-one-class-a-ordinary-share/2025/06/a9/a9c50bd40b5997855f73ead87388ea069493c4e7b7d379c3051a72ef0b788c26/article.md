# From Chatbots to AI Agents: The Next Frontier | Endava

Not long ago, interacting with a chatbot felt like the height of digital innovation. You typed a question. It replied. But things have moved on –


and fast. Today, intelligent agents are not just responding to queries. They’re getting things done.


Intelligent agents are no longer science fiction –


they’re transforming how businesses operate. So, what makes these agents different?


Where traditional chatbots stick to simple Q&As, agents can take meaningful action. Need a financial report created, a meeting scheduled or an email sent? A modern agent can handle that. And that’s just the start. These systems offer operational support across functions and departments, automating tasks and enabling smarter decisions.


At the heart of this shift is Google Gemini Enterprise, **** a new enterprise architecture designed to manage this next generation of automation. Let's take a closer look:


- **Agent orchestrator** – The command centre. It interprets user intent and directs the task to the right skill agent.
- **Skill agents** – Modular and specialised, these microagents perform focused tasks like document summarisation, workflow initiation and insight extraction. They’re reusable and built for scale.
- **Security and policy management** – Enterprise-grade security ensures compliance and role-based access.
- **Observability** – End-to-end tracking feeds back into performance, enabling continuous improvement.
- **Integrations** – Pre-built connectors with GCP, REST APIs, CRMs and more help embed Gemini Enterprise into your current infrastructure seamlessly.


Gemini Enterprise is modular, scalable and built with enterprise governance in mind, making it a powerful enabler for organisations handling sensitive information.


## AI agents in action


Whether it’s streamlining workflows or improving public service delivery, agentic systems are increasingly at the core of solving industry-specific challenges.


### In media: campaigns that move faster – and work smarter


Media companies are seeing real results with campaign assistants built using Gemini Enterprise. These agents automate up to 80% of content creation and pricing updates – speeding up delivery and taking the pressure off creative teams.


### In financial services: from static reports to decision-ready dashboards


Financial institutions are deploying knowledge agents with secure access to internal databases. These agents don’t just extract and synthesise data – they visualise insights and cut research time by up to 60%. It’s a shift that empowers teams to act faster, with more clarity.


### In the public sector: stronger citizen engagement, powered by AI


Government agencies are building AI agents with transparency and reliability in mind. These aren’t pilots or proofs of concept – they’re now becoming essential infrastructure, underpinning secure, responsive interactions between public services and citizens.
