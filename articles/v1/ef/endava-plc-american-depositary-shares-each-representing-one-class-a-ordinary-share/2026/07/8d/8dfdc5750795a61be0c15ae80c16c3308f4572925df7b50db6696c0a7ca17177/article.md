# Navigating the Digital Shift

*“To navigate the fast-changing markets in the era of AI, companies must modernise their core systems and address outdated legacy structures. As we advance towards AI-driven business transformation, core modernisation is a foundational step.”*


***John Cotterell, CEO, Endava***


Digital transformation has been a top priority for businesses for several decades, but the emergence of AI has catalysed a new digital shift. In fact, 70% of firms believe generative AI has either begun disrupting their business or will do so in the next 18 months, according to new IDC research, while CEOs cite deployment of AI, ML and generative AI, modernisation of core infrastructure and optimisation as their top three priorities going forward.


This overhaul has brought with it exciting new opportunities for business to harness, while unprecedented challenges surrounding data, cost-saving and efficiencies persist. Businesses must address the next steps in their digital transformation journey to reap the wins AI has to offer, while those who don’t run the risk of being left behind.


This IDC InfoBrief, sponsored by Endava, helps


business leaders understand how the AI-driven digital shift is impacting organisations and providing insights to support AI-led transformation. This guide reveals how they can modernise and unlock new opportunities – so long as they make the right core infrastructure investments.
