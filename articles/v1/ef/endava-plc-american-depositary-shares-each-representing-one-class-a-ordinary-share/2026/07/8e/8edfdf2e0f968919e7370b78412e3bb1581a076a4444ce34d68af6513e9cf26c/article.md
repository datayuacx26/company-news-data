# How Business Leaders View AI in Life, Work and Society | Endava

As we face up to the massive power of AI, hyperbole is everywhere. Vendors, analysts, and researchers are all making bold claims about its seismic impact. The AI wave is here, but in truth, no one can fully predict all the changes it is bringing.


As governments around the world pin their hopes on the technology for economic growth, it’s vital to turn our attention to society, trust, governance and ethics, as well as business. Operational productivity gains are welcome, but for AI to work for everyone everywhere, we need to view it in terms that go beyond immediate business needs.


Endava’s latest research report explores the reality of AI, not as a prediction but as people are experiencing it in the here and now. We spoke to 500 UK business leaders, asking them about AI in business, in their personal lives, and their views of AI in society and government’s role.


Let’s find out what they said.
