What is Agentic AI?


Agentic AI goes beyond traditional AI by enabling systems to make autonomous, contextual decisions. Designed to act like human partners, these agents execute complex workflows, adapt in real-time and integrate seamlessly with enterprise systems. From proactive personal assistants to intelligent data processors, this evolution marks a shift toward more human-like, purpose-driven AI across industries.
