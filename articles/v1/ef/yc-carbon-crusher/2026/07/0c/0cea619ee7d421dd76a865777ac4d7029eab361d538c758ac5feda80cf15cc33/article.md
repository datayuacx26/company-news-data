# Crushing and Recycling

**Carbon Crusher** delivers full-depth reclamation and on-site recycling that cuts costs, reduces the need for raw materials, and lowers environmental impact.


Our method not only benefits the planet—it also speeds up construction, increases road strength, and extends the lifespan of roads, significantly reducing long-term maintenance needs.


**Longer lasting roads**


A road’s lifespan is significantly increased by strengthening the stabilizing layer beneath the surface. Many of the surface problems we see—like **alligator cracking, rutting, edge break, and frost heave** —are often caused by structural weaknesses deeper in the road.


Carbon Crusher tackles these issues from the ground up. Our biobinder technology reinforces the road’s foundation, boosting load-bearing capacity by up to 350%. By stabilizing the core layers, we prevent surface failures before they happen—delivering smoother roads with fewer repairs and longer life.


That’s the Carbon Crusher way.
