# CRAAS Construction Partners

Through the Carbon Crusher Partner Program, you gain access to our cutting-edge Full Depth Reclamation process, powerful mobile Crushers, sustainable BioBinders, and the intelligent SkyRoadsAI pavement management system—all delivered in one streamlined, cost-effective package.


Our partnership isn’t just about tools. It’s about full support from our expert team of Green Jackets and the know-how to deliver high-performance, climate-positive roads with a truly unique product offering.


‍ **BioBinders**


We leverage biopolymers sourced from sustainable forestry as binders to recycle and enhance existing road structures. Our nature-based stabilizing agents contain a high amount of carbon absorbed from the atmosphere, enabling road structures as a carbon sink to sequester CO₂ while simultaneously reinforcing the roads to withstand heavy traffic loads and reduce deformations.


By combining our crushing technology and BioBinders, our solution is made applicable to most road scenarios and material types, and works in both extreme-heat and extreme-cold climates


**Crushers**


At Carbon Crusher, we don’t just rebuild roads—we reinvent how it’s done. Backed by 18 years of R&D, our advanced stone crushers are built to take on the toughest materials—from asphalt and gravel to solid rock—reaching depths of up to 35 cm.


Our crushers are also precision mixers, combining crushed material and binder in one seamless process. That means faster execution, fewer passes, and higher-performing roads—every time.


The result? Stronger, more resilient roads with lower emissions and maintenance needs. It’s not just a machine. It’s a smarter, cleaner way to rebuild the world beneath our wheels.


**Green Jackets**


Your Expert Support Team


Our Carbon Crusher Green Jackets are the backbone of partner success. They work hands-on with our CraaS partners, bringing deep expertise in project planning, execution, quality control, and mix design. From setting up efficient workflows to optimizing material blends on-site, they ensure every project meets our high performance and sustainability standards.


With deep knowledge of the SkyRoadsAI platform, Green Jackets also help partners unlock smarter road management—backed by real-time data and predictive insights.
