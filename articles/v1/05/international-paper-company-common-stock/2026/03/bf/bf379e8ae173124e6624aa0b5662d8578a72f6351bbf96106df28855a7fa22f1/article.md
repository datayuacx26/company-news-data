## Understanding OCC and Mixed Paper


Before setting up a recycling process, it helps to understand the materials involved.


OCC (Old Corrugated Containers) refers to corrugated cardboard used for shipping, storage and promotion. This includes:


-


Shipping boxes


-


Corrugated sheets


-


Packaging inserts


OCC is one of the most valuable recyclable materials because it can be reused multiple times to create new packaging.


Mixed paper includes a variety of everyday paper products, such as:


-


Office paper


-


Envelopes and mail


-


Magazines and catalogs


-


Paperboard packaging


Both OCC and mixed paper are commonly accepted in commercial recycling programs across Eugene.

## How Businesses Can Set Up OCC Recycling in Eugene


Corrugated cardboard tends to make up a large portion of recyclable material in warehouses and distribution centers. Setting up an OCC recycling process is often the first step.


### Create a Consistent Collection System


Designate areas where cardboard can be collected throughout the facility. Place these zones near receiving docks, packing stations, and high traffic areas.


Making recycling accessible encourages consistent participation.


### Prepare Cardboard Properly


Flattening boxes helps save space and improves handling, if baling or compacting equipment isn't available. Keeping cardboard clean and dry ensures it can be processed effectively.


For larger operations, baling equipment can compress cardboard into manageable bundles, making it easier to store and transport.


### Align Pickup with Volume


Businesses generating large amounts of cardboard may need frequent collection. Recycling services in Eugene can often tailor pickup schedules based on your output.


Matching pickup frequency to your volume helps prevent overflow and keeps operations running smoothly.


## How to Manage Mixed Paper Recycling


Mixed paper requires a slightly different approach, especially in office environments.


### Use Clearly Labeled Bins and Containers


Place recycling bins or containers in common areas such as workstations, meeting rooms, and break areas. Clear labeling helps employees understand what belongs in each bin.


### Keep Materials Clean


Mixed paper should be free from food waste, liquids or other contaminants. Clean materials are more likely to be successfully recycled.


### Separate When Needed


In some cases, separating certain paper types can improve recycling outcomes. Businesses should follow guidance from their recycling provider to make sure materials are handled correctly.


By incorporating these practices, companies can make mixed paper recycling an easy part of daily operations.
