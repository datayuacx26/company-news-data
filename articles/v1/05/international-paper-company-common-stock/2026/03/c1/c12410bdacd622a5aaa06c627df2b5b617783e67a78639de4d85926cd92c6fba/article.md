## Understanding Bulk Cardboard in Commercial Settings


Bulk cardboard typically comes from corrugated packaging used in shipping and storage. This includes:


-


Shipping boxes


-


Corrugated sheets


-


Packaging inserts


-


Large volume product containers


These materials, often referred to as OCC (Old Corrugated Containers), are highly recyclable and can be reused multiple times to create new packaging.


Businesses that participate in Salt Lake City recycling efforts help make sure this material stays in circulation rather than being discarded or sent to landfill.


## Setting Up an Efficient Cardboard Recycling System


Creating a reliable recycling process starts with understanding how cardboard moves through your facility.


### 1. Identify Key Collection Points


Look for areas where cardboard accumulates most often, such as:


-


Receiving docks


-


Packing stations


-


Production areas


Placing recycling collection stations in these locations makes it easier for teams to dispose of materials properly.


### 2. Establish Clear Collection Areas


Designated recycling zones help keep cardboard organized and prevent it from mixing with general waste. Clear signage and easy access encourage consistent use.


### 3. Prepare Cardboard for Recycling


Flattening boxes reduces the amount of space they take up and makes handling easier. Keeping materials clean and dry is also important to ensure they can be processed effectively.


For large-volume operations, equipment such as balers and compactors can compress cardboard into compact bundles for you, improving storage and transportation efficiency.


## Choosing the Right Recycling Solution


Not all recycling setups are the same. The right solution depends on your facility’s size, volume, and workflow.


When evaluating options for recycling in Salt Lake City, consider:


**Pickup frequency**
High-volume operations may need frequent collection to keep materials moving, so it's important to find a recycling partner who can match your requirements.


**Material volume**
The amount of cardboard generated will determine whether simple collection containers or equipment like balers and compactors are needed.


**Space availability**
Facilities with limited space may require compact solutions, while larger operations can support dedicated recycling areas.


**Operational fit**
Recycling should integrate smoothly into existing processes without slowing down productivity.


Selecting the right setup ensures that recycling supports your operation rather than complicating it.


## Integrating Recycling Into Daily Operations

Businesses can improve their approach to recycling in Salt Lake City by:

Keeping recycling stations close to work areas


-


Training employees on proper handling


-


Maintaining clean, organized collection zones


These habits help create a steady flow of materials from use to collection.


## How Recycling Supports the Packaging Cycle


Cardboard recycling is part of a larger recycling ecosystem that keeps materials in use. When businesses recycle corrugated packaging, the fiber is recovered and reused to create new products.


This cycle includes:


-


Producing packaging from new, renewable fiber as well as recovered fiber


-


Using boxes to transport goods and conduct daily operations


-


Collecting and recycling used cardboard


-


Turning recovered fiber into new packaging again


By participating in recycling in Salt Lake City, businesses help support this ongoing, circular cycle and extend the life of renewable materials.
