## How Recycling Pickup Works


## Step 1: Assess Your Material Volume


The first step is determining how much cardboard and paper your business generates. This helps establish the right pickup frequency and container setup.


Businesses generating larger volumes may require more frequent service or baling or compactor equipment, while smaller operations may need less frequent collection.


## Step 2: Set Up Collection Points


Recycling bins and containers should be placed in convenient locations throughout the facility. Common areas include:


-


Office spaces for paper collection


-


Receiving and shipping areas for cardboard


-


Centralized zones for bulk materials


Clear placement helps ensure materials are sorted correctly from the start.


## Step 3: Schedule Regular Pickups


A recycling provider will establish a pickup schedule based on your needs. Consistent collection is key to preventing buildup and keeping materials moving.


For businesses that are recycling in Kent, WA, reliable scheduling helps maintain efficient operations without interruptions.


## Step 4: Collection and Processing


During each pickup, materials are collected and transported to a recycling facility. There, they are sorted, processed, baled and transported to end user manufacturers to use the recovered fiber to create new paper and packaging products.
