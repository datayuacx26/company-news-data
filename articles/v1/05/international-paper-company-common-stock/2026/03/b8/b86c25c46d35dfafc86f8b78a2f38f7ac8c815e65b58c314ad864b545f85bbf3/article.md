## How the Pickup Process Works


While every operation is different, most commercial recycling programs follow a similar process. Understanding each step can help businesses set up a system that runs smoothly.


### Step 1: Evaluate Your Volume and Needs


The first step is understanding how much material your business generates. Offices may produce steady amounts of paper, while warehouses often handle large volumes of cardboard.


This helps determine the right level of service for recycling in St. Paul, including pickup frequency and container size.


### Step 2: Set Up Collection Containers


Recycling programs typically include designated containers placed throughout the facility. These may include:


-


Indoor bins for office paper


-


Larger containers or carts for cardboard


-


Large volume equipment like open top roll-off dumpsters, compactors and balers


-


Centralized collection areas for bulk materials


Proper placement makes it easier for teams to participate and keeps materials organized.


### Step 3: Schedule Regular Pickups


Once containers or equipment are in place, a pickup schedule is established based on your needs. Some businesses require multiple pickups per week, while others may need less frequent service.


Reliable scheduling is a key part of successful recycling in St Paul, helping prevent overflow and keeping materials moving.


### Step 4: Collection and Transportation


During each pickup, recyclable materials are collected and transported to a recycling facility. There, they are further sorted and prepared for processing and baling.


Paper and cardboard are then shipped to paper and box manufacturers and turned into new products, keeping fiber in use and supporting a circular system.
