# Why Woodward Academy chose Flint to personalize learning for students with AI

When Woodward Academy partnered with Flint back in October 2023, studies had only just come out about how[cheating concerns with AI were overblown](https://www.flintk12.com/blog/why-companies-lie-about-detecting-ai-writing#:~:text=A%20September%202023,enforcing%20academic%20integrity.) . The discussion was beginning to shift more and more towards concrete ways student use of AI could benefit learning. Over the course of the 2023-24 school year, the Woodward team has been intentional in leveraging their AI Committee, defining an[AI policy](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RkHPujl68x6euSSpPhREhCyUnUrJoXUG/view) , selecting AI tools, and communicating with faculty about proper usage and security. The Committee stress-tested, explored, and compared many different uses of AI and Flint, allowing Woodward to make a fully informed decision to continue with Flint as an “all-in-one” AI tool endorsed by the school for the 2024-25 school year.


### About Woodward


[Woodward Academy](https://www.woodward.edu/) is a Pre-K through 12th-grade private day school with around 2,700 students. Located in the southern part of the Atlanta metro area, Woodward is intentional in bringing together diverse students to form a richer learning environment with exceptional academic opportunities. Connie White has been directing learning and innovation at Woodward for over 9 years and is an active speaker and thought leader in the independent school community. In the following case study, she describes how her team crafted an AI policy for their school. She also speaks about how they love Flint because of the Flint team’s “exceptional responsiveness to our feedback” and the ability to rely on Flint as “a comprehensive solution” and “confidently use AI with both students and teachers.”


### Challenges Woodward sought to solve


After exploring the AI landscape and concluding that AI as a technology is here to stay, administrators at Woodward were looking for a “solution for the entire school”, not just for teachers or just for students. Top of mind for Connie and her team was ensuring that AI could be rolled out at the school in a way that would:


-


Keep student data secure and ensure that it would not be used for training AI models


-


Cover the needs of both students and teachers


-


Allow teachers to better “personalize the learning for students” with AI


### Solutions Flint provided


Flint was the right choice for Woodward thanks to its robust data privacy measures (no student or teacher data is ever used to train or fine-tune AI models), and due to the focus on providing teachers with easy ways to personalize student learning with AI.


Beyond the ability of Flint to meet the core requirements from a technical perspective, Connie, as well as teachers and tech specialists at Woodward, have highlighted the responsiveness from the Flint team in both assisting with professional development for faculty as well as in rapidly iterating on the platform to best meet Woodward’s needs. For example, Connie describes how the School AI Committee has been “incredibly impressed” with the platform, and how Flint “adapted their product to meet the evolving needs of our teachers and students.”


### Impact of Flint so far


From the onset, Flint provided teachers and administrators at Woodward with a safe way to use AI both with students as well as for their own assistance, without concerns of data privacy. Over time, however, the value of Flint as a tool for personalized learning became abundantly clear.


Connie, for example, designed an AI tutor in Flint to have students in her Ethical Dilemmas in Science and Technology Senior Capstone interview Jennifer Doudna, who wrote the book Code Breakers. Connie’s students better understood how Doudna won the Nobel Prize for the invention of CRISPR gene editing by getting to ask her any question in fully personalized conversations.


Teachers at Woodward have made use of Flint across a wide variety of subjects – ranging from English to Physics – to give students extra assistance and practice.
