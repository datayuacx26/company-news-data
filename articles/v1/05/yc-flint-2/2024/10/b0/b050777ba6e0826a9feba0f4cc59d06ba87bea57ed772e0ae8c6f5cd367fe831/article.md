# November 2024: Tutors renamed to activities, improved sessions view, and more!

It’s now much easier to view student sessions on Flint! Now, when you click on an activity that you’re an owner of (e.g. an activity that you’ve made), you’ll be brought to an “Overview” page.


This “Overview” page includes a wide table view of all sessions for that tutor so far. Feel free to search for specific sessions, or sort based on fields like student name, grade, or submission time.
