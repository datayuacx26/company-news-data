# October 2025: Start dates for activities, full-screen attachments, iPad camera flip, and status center!

Teachers told us that sometimes the work itself needs to take center stage. That’s why we added **full-screen mode for attachments** , such as whiteboards, documents, and even our code editor.


We made learning the star! Whether you’re debugging, writing essays, or sketching out diagrams, now you have your entire screen to focus. And when presenting, students can clearly see the math equation, passage, or block of code without distractions, no matter their seating arrangement.
