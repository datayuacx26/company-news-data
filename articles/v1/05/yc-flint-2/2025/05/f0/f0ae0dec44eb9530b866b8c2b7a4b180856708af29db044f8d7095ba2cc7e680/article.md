# May 2025: School activity library, admin AI guidelines, and last name sorting!

School administrators can now specify educational values and goals that Flint should align its responses with before replying to any message.


For example, schools can now customize Flint to respond according to specific religious worldviews, or ensure that certain pedagogical practices are enforced by Flint when helping teachers with tasks like lesson planning. More details, including sample prompts, can be found[here](https://help.flintk12.com/en/articles/11173343-providing-your-school-s-mission-and-background-to-flint) .
