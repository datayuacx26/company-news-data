# Introducing Flint V6: Meet Sparky, connect your Google Drive, get live activity alerts, and more!

We’ve also rolled out a number of additional changes to make Flint smoother, faster, and more reliable for teachers, admins, and studnets:


-


**Smarter chat names** : Sparky will now auto-name your chats once it has enough context, helping you stay organized and find past conversations more easily.


-


**All workspace settings in one place** : A new “Workspace Settings” modal brings all your member and platform controls together for easier management.


-


**One-click Edlink setup** : Admins can now enable LMS/SIS integrations like Google Classroom or Canvas instantly.
