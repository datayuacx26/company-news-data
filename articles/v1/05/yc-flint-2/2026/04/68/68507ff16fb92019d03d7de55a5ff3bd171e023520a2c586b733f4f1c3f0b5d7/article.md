# April 2026: Learning reports, grade customization, personalized home page, and more.

We've made adding members and assigning grade levels easier. You can now upload a CSV file and Flint with automatically populate grades, or bulk assign grades in the Members panel of workspace settings. So if you’re pulling data from another system, it’s much easier to get everything set up in Flint without manual work.[Learn more about managing grade levels.](https://help.flintk12.com/en/articles/13897763-how-to-manage-grade-levels-in-flint#h_e333eb3c3f)
