## ✏️ Edit your onboarding answers anytime

## 📊 Gradebook is now live in Flint


The **Flint Gradebook** is here, making it easier to track student progress and review completed activities throughout the semester.[Learn more about Gradebook here.](https://help.flintk12.com/en/articles/13133685-everything-you-need-to-know-about-flints-gradebook)

## 🤝 Moderate relationship building chats with Sparky

## 🗂️ View archived chats and sessions


Admins and activity owners can now **view archived chats and sessions** , improving transparency and oversight when reviewing student activity.[Learn more here.](https://help.flintk12.com/en/articles/13281575-how-to-view-archived-chats-and-sessions)


## 🔁 Turn chats into activities


You can now **convert chats directly into activities** , making it easy to reuse effective prompts, discussions, and learning moments.[Learn more here.](https://help.flintk12.com/en/articles/13251895-how-to-convert-chats-to-activities-in-flint?q=m)

Chats and sessions are now **more clearly organized in user profiles** , helping teachers and students find what they need faster.
