# June 2026: Keep student safe, track their growth, and provide better follow-ups

Get a clearer view of how students are growing. The new Alignment tab shows Bloom's taxonomy distribution and a soft skills radar. You can filter by any skill to see evidence cards with direct links to the messages where students demonstrated that skill.


You'll also find Pulse insights and demonstration carousels that make it easy to spot patterns and celebrate growth.[Learn more about alignment analytics in Flint.](https://help.flintk12.com/en/articles/15122879-workspace-analytics-in-flint)
