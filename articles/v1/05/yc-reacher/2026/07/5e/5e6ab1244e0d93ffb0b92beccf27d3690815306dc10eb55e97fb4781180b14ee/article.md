## Frequently Asked Questions


### How much does it cost to sell something on TikTok Shop?


Selling items on TikTok Shop primarily involves paying a referral fee or commission on each completed transaction. Exact percentages vary based on your specific product category and promotional events. You should factor these processing fees into your pricing strategy from day one to protect your profit margins.


### How do beginners start selling on TikTok Shop?


Beginners should start selling on TikTok Shop by registering through the Seller Center and submitting required business documents for verification. After your identity and legal standing are confirmed, you can upload your product catalog and design your storefront. New sellers must then focus on creating engaging video content to drive initial traffic.


### Do I need 1000 followers to have a TikTok Shop?


You do not need 1000 followers to open a standard TikTok Shop seller account. The platform requires a minimum of 1000 followers specifically to grant users the ability to add links in bio or host live shopping sessions. Anyone with an approved Seller Center account can list items regardless of their current follower count.


### Can anyone sell stuff on TikTok Shop?


Most individuals and registered businesses can sell stuff on TikTok Shop provided they pass the official verification process. Applicants must submit valid identification, tax records, and business registration details to prove their legitimacy. The platform restricts certain prohibited product categories, so sellers must review the guidelines before listing.


### Is selling on TikTok Shop worth it?


Selling on TikTok Shop is highly profitable for brands seeking direct access to an actively engaged global audience exceeding one billion users. The platform removes friction from the buying journey by allowing customers to purchase without leaving the app. Brands that execute a structured strategy see significant revenue growth and expanded market reach.


### How can I scale my TikTok Shop using creators?


Brands scale their TikTok Shop operations by identifying high-performing creators and automating massive outreach campaigns. You can describe your ideal creator and use AI to search a database, filtering by GMV, category, and audience demographics. This data-driven approach lets you recruit thousands of partners daily across direct messages and email to drive massive sales.
