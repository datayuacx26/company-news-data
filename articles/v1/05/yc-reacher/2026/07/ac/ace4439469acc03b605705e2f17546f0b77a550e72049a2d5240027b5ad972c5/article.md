# TikTok Shop Best Sellers: How to Find Products That Fit

## Frequently asked questions


### What is the best-selling product on TikTok Shop?


There is no single answer across every market and date. Choose a country, category, channel, time range, and metric, then check current Product Ranking or your Seller Center analytics.


### Where can sellers find TikTok Shop best sellers?


Eligible sellers can review Product Ranking and Product Analytics in Seller Center. Product Opportunities can add trend and demand signals. Feature availability varies by market and account.


### Should I sell a product because it ranks by GMV?


No. First evaluate contribution margin, supply, returns, compliance, listing quality, competition, and creator fit. GMV is not net profit.


### Which metrics should I compare?


Use GMV and units with clicks, conversion, price, traffic source, inventory, refunds, returns, creator costs, and contribution per delivered order.


### How often should bestseller research be updated?


Update it often enough for your inventory and campaign cycle, and always save the market, filters, period, and observation date. A static list becomes stale quickly.
