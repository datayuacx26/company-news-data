# TikTok Shop Affiliate: A Current Guide for Sellers and Creators

## How should you choose a TikTok affiliate program?


Choose an affiliate offer based on product fit and contribution margin, not the highest advertised commission. Sellers must also confirm demand, fulfillment capacity, refund exposure, and market access. Creators should assess audience demographics, content style, purchase intent, and whether the product belongs naturally in their niche.


Use this process to evaluate an offer:


1. Confirm the TikTok affiliate program requirements and the countries where the offer is available.
2. Calculate expected revenue after product cost, creator commission, shipping, discounts, refunds, and platform fees.
3. Select open or targeted collaboration based on the level of partner screening and campaign control required.
4. Review the product, claims, sample process, disclosure expectations, and creative brief before publishing.
5. Measure attributed orders and net revenue before increasing commission or expanding outreach.


For partner discovery, Reacher supports competitor creator discovery and one-click outreach targeting. Its TikTok Shop benchmarks cover brands, creators, products, and videos by GMV, giving teams commercial context before they commit samples or budget.


Track every creator against the same commercial measures.[Commission terms](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) , audience quality, conversion rate, refund rate, and net revenue tell you more than reach alone. Reacher’s[TikTok Shop performance dashboard](https://www.reacherapp.com/features/dashboard) adds market context to that review.


Creators and sellers should also review[disclosure expectations](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/affiliate-marketing.html) before publishing affiliate content.


Affiliate marketing relies on partnerships in which creators promote products and receive compensation for resulting sales. Review the[affiliate marketing model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affiliate_marketing) when comparing TikTok Shop with other performance channels.


**Selection rule:** Choose the offer and workflow that produce profitable, attributable orders, not merely high reach.
