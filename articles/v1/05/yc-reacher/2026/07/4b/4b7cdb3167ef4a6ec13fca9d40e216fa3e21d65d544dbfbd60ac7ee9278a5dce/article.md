## Frequently Asked Questions


### How many followers do I need to request free samples on TikTok Shop?


TikTok’s standard requirement is 5,000 followers for creators who want to request complimentary products. That threshold is separate from TikTok Shop Affiliate Program enrollment. Meeting the affiliate threshold does not automatically qualify you for product requests. TikTok Shop may also review account activity, commerce history, content quality, and seller-specific conditions. Check the current rules in the[official TikTok Seller Center guidance](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5764641632306946&lang=en) before you plan your outreach.


### Can I get free samples without any followers?


Creators below the standard threshold should check their current TikTok Shop account status and seller terms for available options. Access is not a universal route available to every account. Sellers still want evidence that you can produce useful product content and reach a relevant audience. Start by publishing consistent videos in one clear niche, improving your profile, and building engagement around products that match your viewers. Do not pay a seller simply because an offer promises guaranteed access.


### What is the difference between free and refundable samples?


A complimentary sample does not require an upfront product payment. You still agree to the seller’s content requirements and delivery schedule. A refundable sample requires you to pay before receiving the item. The seller refunds the qualifying amount only after you meet a stated GMV target or another performance condition. Before accepting, verify the sales window, refund deadline, shipping cost, return terms, commission structure, and proof required. If the target exceeds your normal sales performance, reject the offer. ### How do I request a sample from a seller?


Open the product or campaign listing in TikTok Shop, review the eligibility conditions, and submit the request through the available creator workflow. Write a focused message that identifies your audience, explains the video concept, and states how the product fits your content niche. Include relevant proof, such as recent product videos, engagement quality, affiliate activity, or livestream results. Sellers approve requests based on promotional fit and reliability, not follower count alone. Keep a record of the request, shipping status, required disclosure, affiliate link, and publishing date. ### What is the 120-day activity rule?


The 120-day rule means a creator must have been active on TikTok Shop for at least 120 days under the standard eligibility process. This is a commerce activity requirement, not a substitute for the follower threshold. An account can have enough followers and still lack access if its TikTok Shop history is too recent. Keep your affiliate activity consistent, publish compliant content, and monitor your account status rather than relying on old screenshots or advice from social media forums. Policy details can change, so official TikTok documentation should guide your decisions.


### What happens if I miss the sample posting deadline?


[TikTok Shop guidance](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5764641632306946&lang=en) requires creators to publish sample content within 14 days of receiving the product. Missing that deadline can weaken your seller relationships, reduce future approval prospects, or affect account standing. Contact the seller promptly if a shipment arrives damaged, a product is unavailable, or a legitimate production issue prevents timely posting. Do not accept more campaigns than your filming schedule can support. The best operating rule is straightforward: confirm the deliverable before requesting the item, film soon after delivery, and publish with the correct product link and disclosure.
