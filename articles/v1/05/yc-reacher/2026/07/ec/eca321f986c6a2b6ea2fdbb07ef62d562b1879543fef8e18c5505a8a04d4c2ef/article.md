### Proven Templates: How to Pitch Brands via Email and DMs


When reaching out to brands directly, a well-crafted pitch is essential. For email pitches, start with a clear, concise subject line that immediately states your purpose, such as "UGC Creator Collaboration Inquiry - \[Your Name/Brand Name\]." In the body, briefly introduce yourself, mention your specific interest in their brand, and highlight how your content style aligns with their marketing goals. Importantly, include a link to your portfolio or a dedicated page showcasing your best work. Keep the pitch brief, focusing on the value you can bring. Performance varies by audience, creative, offer, and placement; use current campaign data to set expectations. Always include a clear call to action, like suggesting a brief call to discuss potential projects.


For direct messages (DMs) on platforms like Instagram, the approach needs to be even more condensed. Start by complimenting a recent campaign or product. Then, quickly state that you are a UGC creator specializing in their niche and have created sample content demonstrating how you could help them. Include a link to your portfolio or a few top-tier examples. Avoid lengthy paragraphs; aim for a few short, focused sentences. Remember, brands receive many messages, so yours must stand out instantly. A strong pitch demonstrates your professionalism and understanding of their marketing needs, making it easier for them to see you as a valuable partner for creating content, whether it's for how to become a ugc creator on tiktok or Instagram.
