# How to Join the TikTok Affiliate Program

## TikTok Shop Affiliate Requirements: Age, Location, Followers, and Account Type


### Minimum Age and Geographic Availability


To participate in the TikTok Shop affiliate program, creators must meet specific age and location criteria. Generally, applicants must be at least 18 years old. This age requirement ensures that participants are legally able to enter into agreements and manage financial transactions. The program's availability is tied to specific regions where TikTok Shop is officially launched and operational. As of current guidelines, this includes countries like the United States, the United Kingdom, and others where TikTok Shop has established its e-commerce infrastructure. Eligibility for the[Reacher Affiliate Program](https://reacherapp.com/affiliate) management tools also aligns with these geographic and age restrictions, ensuring compliance across the board.


It is imperative for aspiring affiliates to verify that their TikTok account's region setting matches a country where TikTok Shop is supported. Attempting to join the program from an unsupported region will result in disqualification. Brands looking to partner with affiliates should also be aware of these geographic limitations to ensure their campaigns target eligible creators and markets. This geographic and age gate is a standard practice to maintain program integrity and[comply with local commerce regulations](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) .


### Follower Count Requirements and the No-1k-Followers Workaround


Traditionally, a primary requirement for joining the TikTok Shop affiliate program as a creator has been a minimum follower count, typically 1,000 followers. This threshold was established to ensure creators had a baseline audience size capable of generating meaningful traffic and sales. This standard requirement is often a barrier for emerging creators eager to start their affiliate marketing journey. TikTok has introduced pathways for accounts that do not meet this follower count to still participate, often referred to as the "no-1k-followers workaround."


Accounts with verified identities or those demonstrating prior e-commerce success may be eligible to bypass the 1,000-follower rule. Some sources indicate that accounts with fewer followers might gain access through specific invitations or by meeting other performance indicators. TikTok Shop Seller University pages may outline alternative eligibility criteria or specific application processes for accounts under the typical threshold. This flexibility is designed to foster a more inclusive creator economy, allowing promising creators to build their affiliate business even before reaching significant follower milestones. Understanding these alternative routes is key for both creators looking to join and brands aiming to discover new talent.


### Account Type Requirements: Personal vs. Business vs. Creator Accounts


TikTok offers different account types: Personal, Business, and Creator. For the affiliate program, clarity on which account type is preferred or required is essential. While Personal accounts are standard for general users, Business accounts are geared towards brands and marketers. Creator accounts are designed for individuals who focus on content creation and audience engagement. Historically, the TikTok Shop affiliate program has often prioritized Creator accounts, as they are optimized for audience building and monetization through content.


The platform's requirements can evolve, and some documentation suggests that a standard TikTok account, regardless of whether it's 'Creator' or 'Business,' can be eligible as long as it meets other criteria like age and follower count. The key is that the account must be in good standing and adhere to TikTok's community guidelines. For brands, understanding these account types helps in identifying and recruiting the most suitable partners. If you are an individual creator looking to maximize your earning potential, ensuring your account is set up to highlight your content and audience engagement is advisable. The focus remains on your ability to influence purchasing decisions.


To become a TikTok Shop affiliate, you generally need to be 18+, reside in a supported country, and meet follower count requirements (often 1,000, but workarounds exist). While Creator accounts are common, the primary focus is on having an account in good standing that can drive engagement and sales.
