# TikTok Shop Commission Rate: The Full Guide

## How should you choose a TikTok Shop commission rate?


Choose an offer by calculating expected net income rather than selecting the highest available percentage. Start with product price, creator payout, platform deductions, refund exposure, and the content effort required for a sale. A $40 product at 15% produces a $6 gross commission before adjustments. A lower-priced item may produce less per order but convert more consistently. Review demand, customer reviews, shipping reliability, and repeat-purchase potential before committing resources.


Creators should confirm the exact terms attached to each product. Check whether the percentage applies to organic affiliate content, Shop Ads, livestream sales, or a specific collaboration invitation. Review attribution windows, canceled orders, sample costs, and payment status.[Disclose the affiliate relationship](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) clearly, and use TikTok’s Shop Ads documentation to verify whether paid promotion carries a separate rate.


**Selection rule:** Estimate earnings with this formula: product price multiplied by commission percentage, multiplied by completed orders, minus refunds and applicable fees. Compare that figure with the hours required for filming, editing, livestreaming, audience replies, and reporting.


Beginners learning **how to earn commission on TikTok Shop** should verify eligibility, choose a small group of products that suit their audience, and test content before increasing publishing volume. Build each piece around a buyer problem, demonstrate the product clearly, disclose the affiliate relationship, and track clicks, product views, add-to-cart activity, conversion rate, delivered orders, and refunds.


Sellers should set the percentage after calculating contribution margin. Include product cost, fulfillment, shipping subsidies, discounts, creator payout, transaction charges, refund administration, and advertising expenses. A generous offer can attract creators yet become unprofitable if returns rise or paid distribution carries different terms. A modest offer can perform well when demand, creative quality, fulfillment, and customer value are strong.


Cash flow needs its own review. MediaLabs reports that payouts generally arrive approximately 14 days after delivery, not immediately after purchase. Pending balances can change when an order is canceled, returned, or otherwise becomes ineligible. Creators should record approved, pending, delivered, and reversed commissions. Sellers should review the same statuses when assessing campaign profitability.


Audience research can inform product selection and content planning. Recent[teens social media and technology research](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2023/12/11/teens-social-media-and-technology-2023/) provides context about younger users’ platform habits, although creators should validate audience fit through their own analytics.
