# TikTok Ad Copy Specs: The key guide

## Spark Ads and the Creator Workflow: Scale TikTok Shop Copy Without Re-Editing Every Asset


### Spark Ads step by step: run creator posts as ads with native captions intact


Spark Ads let you promote an eligible organic TikTok post from a creator or brand account while preserving its native post identity, engagement, and caption. Confirm creator authorization and the required video code, select Spark Ads in TikTok Ads Manager, enter the code, choose the approved post, and connect the campaign to the correct TikTok Shop product or destination. Set budget, audience, schedule, optimization event, and tracking parameters before publishing.


This workflow avoids unnecessary re-editing because the original post remains the creative source. The creator’s hook, delivery, comments, social proof, and native caption stay connected to the ad. Review the post for product accuracy, permissions, audio rights, disclosure, and policy compliance before activation. The source video still needs clear vertical framing and text that avoids interface areas.


### How to brief creators for ad-ready UGC


A useful creator brief turns technical requirements into filming instructions. Specify the product SKU, approved claims, disclosure, talking points, CTA, filming ratio, deadline, authorization window, and product-link process. Ask creators to record vertically with the subject and product near the center, leaving space around interface areas. Require clean audio, stable lighting, readable demonstrations, and an opening that presents the shopper’s problem quickly.


Give creators room to use their own voice while defining non-negotiable facts. Include prohibited claims, pricing rules, promotional dates, usage instructions, and a review checklist. Request the raw file, published post URL, caption text, usage permissions, and Spark authorization code in one submission. This makes each video easier to approve for TikTok Shop promotion.
