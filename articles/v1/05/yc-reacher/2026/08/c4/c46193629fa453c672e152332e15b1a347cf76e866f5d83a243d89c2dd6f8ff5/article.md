### General Profile and Competitor Trackers: Exolyt, Pentos, and Socialinsider


**Best for:** content benchmarking, account audits, and reporting across social channels. Exolyt is built for TikTok-focused account, hashtag, and video research. Pentos supports trend observation and competitor monitoring over time. Socialinsider is a reporting choice for agencies that need presentation-ready engagement and publishing data across multiple networks. These platforms help answer who is gaining attention, which formats attract interaction, and how your publishing cadence compares.


#### Pros


- Useful for public profile research and content benchmarking
- Clear reporting for engagement, posting, and trend analysis


#### Cons


- Commerce attribution may require a separate shop data source
- Creator discovery and outreach workflows can remain manual
