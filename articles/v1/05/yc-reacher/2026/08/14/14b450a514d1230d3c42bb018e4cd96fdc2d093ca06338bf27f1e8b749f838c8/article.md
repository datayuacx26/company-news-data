# What Does GMV Mean on TikTok? A Reporting Guide

## Shop GMV vs. Commission GMV vs. Net Revenue


GMV shows the value of merchandise sold, while net revenue reflects what remains after relevant deductions. Commission GMV is a separate attribution measure for affiliate-driven sales. Keeping these figures distinct gives your team a cleaner view of campaign performance.


For creator-led growth,[AI creator search](https://www.reacherapp.com/features/creator-discovery) helps teams find relevant partners. Reacher lets you describe an ideal creator and rank the database using signals such as GMV, engagement, and post rate. Evaluate each partnership with conversion rate, units sold, attributed sales, refunds, commission expense, and contribution margin. Not GMV alone.


### What Is Commission GMV?


Commission GMV is the gross sales value attributed to an affiliate[creator](https://www.reacherapp.com/features/creator-community) or another commission-based channel. It isn’t the creator’s payment. Creator earnings come from the commission rate applied to eligible attributed sales.


### What Does GMV MVP Mean on TikTok?


The meaning of GMV MVP can vary by TikTok program or dashboard. Check the applicable TikTok documentation and the underlying orders, refunds, and margin before using the label in performance analysis.


### What Does GMV Mean on YouTube?


On YouTube, GMV has the same broad meaning: Gross Merchandise Value, or the value of merchandise sold through an associated shopping feature,[creator](https://www.reacherapp.com/features/creator-crm) promotion, or commerce campaign. Reporting definitions vary by integration, so compare the platform’s GMV with settled revenue and costs.
