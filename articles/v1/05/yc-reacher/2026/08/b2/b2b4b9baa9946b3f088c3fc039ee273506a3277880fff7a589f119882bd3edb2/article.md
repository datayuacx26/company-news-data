# TikTok Brand Partnerships: The Revenue Playbook

## Structuring TikTok Partnerships That Convert: Compensation and Creative Briefs


Compensation should reflect the creator’s workload, distribution value, audience fit, and expected business outcome. Set the model before production begins, and document deliverables, commission attribution, payment timing, usage rights, exclusivity, revisions, and cancellation terms. A clear structure protects the creator’s work while giving the brand a reliable basis for evaluating campaign performance.


### Compensation Models Compared: Flat Fee, Affiliate, Gifting, and Hybrid Deals


Model Best use Brand risk Creator upside


Flat fee Defined posts, UGC, or planned campaign deliverables Payment occurs without guaranteed sales Predictable income for completed work


Affiliate or commission Trackable product sales through TikTok Shop or a unique link Revenue depends on creator performance Income can grow with conversion volume


Gifting Product seeding and voluntary discovery content No guaranteed publication or reach Free product and potential future relationship


Hybrid Campaigns requiring committed content plus sales motivation Higher initial cost with commission exposure Base payment plus performance earnings


### Writing Creative Briefs That Keep Branded Content Authentic


A useful brief sets the destination without dictating every sentence. Provide the campaign objective, target customer, product facts, mandatory claims, prohibited claims, offer details, disclosure requirement, deliverables, deadline, approval process, and tracking link. Give creators room to choose the hook, setting, pacing, and visual treatment that suit their audience. Over-scripted content often loses the personal delivery that makes TikTok recommendations persuasive.
