# The Sound of a Second Chance: DaVita Patients Celebrate Receiving Their Kidney Transplant

The journey to receiving a kidney transplant can be a complex and deeply emotional experience. To navigate the intricate path that includes education, care coordination and personal support, patients often rely on their multidisciplinary care teams as they work to reach this life-changing milestone.


DaVita teammates — from facility administrators and social workers to patient care technicians and nurses — work side-by-side with patients every step of the way.


They help patients understand their treatment options, untangle complex insurance paperwork, and coordinate directly with transplant centers. Sometimes, the most important care they provide is simply being a source of encouragement, helping to calm nerves as surgery draws closer and reminding patients that they are not alone. This dedication to treating the whole person builds lasting bonds that extend well beyond the walls of the dialysis center.


Listen to patients express their heartfelt gratitude for the gift of transplant and to the DaVita teammates who played an essential role in their transplant journeys.
