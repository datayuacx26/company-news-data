# Welcome - 2024-2025 | KBR Technical Journal

# A Message from Stuart Bradie


#### KBR President and CEO


Welcome to the KBR Technical Journal.


At KBR, our people do things that matter. The KBR Technical Journal is proof. It’s my privilege to welcome you to a new edition highlighting just a few of those amazing things our people do every day to make the world a safer, more secure and more sustainable place.


The journal features papers authored or co-authored by KBR experts that showcase the latest in cutting-edge research and thought leadership. The papers focus on just a few areas across KBR’s portfolio of industry-leading solutions and technologies, including artificial intelligence and machine learning approaches, ground-breaking human performance research, and sustainable energy transition and energy security expertise. Importantly, the journal represents the spirit of ingenuity, commitment to excellence, and dedication to our shared mission, purpose and values that form the bedrock of our culture.


I would like to thank all who contributed to this edition of the KBR Technical Journal, and I congratulate the authors who won top honors. I’m proud to acknowledge all our experts for their work, which is helping solve the great challenges of our time. The knowledge and expertise captured in this journal, and across KBR, has the power to help overcome those challenges and to change the world.
