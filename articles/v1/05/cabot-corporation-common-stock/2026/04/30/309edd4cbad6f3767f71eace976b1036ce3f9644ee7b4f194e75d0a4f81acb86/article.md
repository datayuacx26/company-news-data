# Cabot Corporation to Announce Second Quarter Fiscal 2026 Operating Results

[PDF Version](https://investor.cabot-corp.com/node/26766/pdf)


BOSTON, April 09, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Cabot Corporation](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=c6Tzr2_mY36sFOvkcDh0Ep-nO4ECYK3QfRPLjpGlnD0mXOhobCCYOzzQZNgyv95XuDjdPEAEmIhzeyZhNEZ_lQL-u9z0eElrR2cdCQ_lAWg=) (NYSE: CBT) today announced that it will release operating results for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 on Tuesday, May 5, 2026, after the market close. The Company will host a conference call and live webcast to review the second quarter results beginning at 8:00 AM (ET) on Wednesday, May 6, 2026.


The call will be webcast by Notified and may be accessed through Cabot’s website at[https://cabotog.gcs-](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=3BAjYmnAWh_Kv-m2y5jGGjyZdMEQyLcQPxLt46l8qa6DQHaJLWfDdlo8KhQZ9ZvFQaHps4s90XnD_dzzlq8yJ94YP2a8AY-7KLHToN3LqZk=)[web](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=yJFcNGOglxvirbfA5tYX_1_USPhtMmSh2oNVWGV27OeaOjg68A9xt8ZUGfRTId6b5XdRHh67AgOV3S2CAMdbFg==)[.com/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=EWmADjJfE-9M6xPQEjwHHxjmB3s6ILd2TZcCuK1Dc9hVERisl_iCa2G75xxIbWrR6QQJz9kJae04cbZZNOU4vw==) .
