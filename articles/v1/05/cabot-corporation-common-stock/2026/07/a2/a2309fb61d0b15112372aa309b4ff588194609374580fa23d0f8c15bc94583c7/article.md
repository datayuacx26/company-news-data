# Cabot Corporation Board Declares Dividend

[PDF Version](https://investor.cabot-corp.com/node/26921/pdf)


BOSTON, July 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- On Friday, July 10, 2026, the Board of Directors of[Cabot Corporation](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=blEUQP_V5-wWa8mrCkCbA24_uc2wm8YpH-vvTTUDZewH4bfPArp14LLESGyxhedJsbp1b4SCkaD_8zYJs1d2uI2QqvxqvIM4JMNsycYJiFo=) (NYSE: CBT) declared a quarterly dividend of $0.4725 per share on all outstanding shares of the Corporation’s common stock. The dividend is payable on September 11, 2026, to stockholders of record at the close of business on August 28, 2026.
