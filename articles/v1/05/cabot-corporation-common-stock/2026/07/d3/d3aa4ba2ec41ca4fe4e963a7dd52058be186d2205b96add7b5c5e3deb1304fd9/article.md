# Cabot Corporation to Announce Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Operating Results

[PDF Version](https://investor.cabot-corp.com/node/26906/pdf)


BOSTON, July 09, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Cabot Corporation](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Cz-bogKHLDg7aT75ztgnjPvGAPjXSK0NxL93fOgTXT69klRsQ8tXdGrLvJ5vrj0FHrZ9f_f76waYbncecFKnUcC5Z0XGEwKLHTAmaiONyuk=) (NYSE: CBT) today announced that it will release operating results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 on Monday, August 3, 2026, after the market close. The Company will host a conference call and live webcast to review the third quarter results beginning at 8:00 AM (ET) on Tuesday, August 4, 2026.
