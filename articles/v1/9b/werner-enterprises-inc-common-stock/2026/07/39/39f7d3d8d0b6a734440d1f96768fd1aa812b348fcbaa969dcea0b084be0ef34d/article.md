## New Advanced Temperature-Controlled Capabilities


PowerLink has expanded into refrigerated operations, marking an exciting new frontier for our power-only network. This specialized division handles high-volume, temperature-sensitive commodities across regions, including:


- **Frozen Foods:** High-priority, consistent loads moving items directly to grocery freezer sections.


- **Confectionery:** Specialized handling for raw ingredients and finished candy products requiring strict 50-to-60-degree Fahrenheit environments.


- **Cross-Border Proteins:** Time-sensitive, live-loaded shipments of chicken, beef and pork moving into and out of Mexico.


A catalyst for this temperature-controlled growth is Werner’s strategic cross-dock facility in Laredo, Texas. Built for freight turnover rather than long-term storage, this facility eliminates border bottlenecks and secures a streamlined workflow for cross-border hauling.


Describing the facility’s layout, Laredo Regional Logistics Manager Oscar Alarcon emphasized that the space is intended for speed:


“We have a 17,500-square-foot cross-dock in Laredo with 21 doors, eight of which are refrigerated,” Alarcon said. “The cross-dock is kept at a 36-degree Fahrenheit temperature. It’s not a warehouse, it’s just in-and-out transload from the Mexican trailer to the U.S. trailer, or we can do it southbound.”


## Expanded Opportunities and Geographic Reach

Our network primarily serves the eastern half of the United States (from Texas eastward), anchoring operations around major drop-and-hook hubs in Laredo, TX; Edwardsville, IL; Allentown, PA; Chicago, IL; and Atlanta, GA.


Carriers can take advantage of multiple freight modes:


- **Dedicated Solution:** Consistent origins and destinations for drivers who want to return home regularly.


- **Regional:** Offers multiple power-only loads in regions where drivers prefer to operate.


- **Cross-Border:** Provides cross-border power-only loads to and from Mexico.


- **On-the-Road** (OTR): Maximizes miles for drivers who are typically on the road for two weeks at a time.


## How Do Some Power-Only Opportunities Reduce Waste?


The goal in every business is to eliminate waste. Werner has some power-only opportunities that pay all miles and eliminate your waste by paying empty miles between loads. While deadhead miles don’t technically disappear, on some occasions Werner pays miles to carriers at the same rate as loaded miles, creating a consistent revenue stream for both the carrier and the driver.
