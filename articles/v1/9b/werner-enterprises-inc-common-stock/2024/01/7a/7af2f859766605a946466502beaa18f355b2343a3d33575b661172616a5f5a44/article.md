# Werner Wrapped 2023

We looked back at 2023 to create our annual #WernerWrapped.


Check out our 2023 highlights.


**Werner Wrapped is here.**


## **Top of the Class**


In 2023 we received 29[awards](https://www.werner.com/about/#awards) highlighting the amazing work of our professional drivers and associates.


## **One in a Million**


Our professional drivers continued achieving[amazing safety milestones](https://investor.werner.com/news-and-events/press-releases/news-details/2023/Werner-Professional-Driver-Tim-Dean-Achieves-5-Million-Accident-Free-Miles/default.aspx) in 2023. Our active fleet of million milers have driven a cumulative of 1.068 billion safe miles with us.


## **Every day is Earth Day**


Since 2020, Werner reduced its emissions by 16%. Since 2007 we saved 382 million gallons of fuel. Read the 2023 CSR report[here](https://s1.q4cdn.com/812671447/files/doc_downloads/ESG/Werner-2023-CSR-Report.pdf) .


## **Delivering Hauliday Spirit**


In 2023, a team of four Werner professional drivers delivered the U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree. We were thrilled to play a part in this rich American tradition!


## **New Kids on the Block**


In 2023, we welcomed[Michelle Greene to our Board of Directors](https://investor.werner.com/news-and-events/press-releases/news-details/2023/Werner-Appoints-Michelle-D.-Greene-to-Board-of-Directors/default.aspx) and[Chris Wikoff as our new CFO](https://investor.werner.com/news-and-events/press-releases/news-details/2023/Werner-Enterprises-Names-Chris-Wikoff-as-Chief-Financial-Officer/default.aspx) .


## **Blue Brigade**


Last year, the[Blue Brigade](https://www.werner.com/about/#csr) pitched in more than 2,324 volunteer hours to their local communities.


## **Too Cool for School**


3,158 drivers graduated from[Roadmaster](https://www.werner.com/cdl-training/#rds) and[joined the Werner team](https://www.werner.com/truck-driver-jobs/) .


We had a great 2023! We’re excited for the opportunities that 2024 brings for Werner, our drivers and our associates.
