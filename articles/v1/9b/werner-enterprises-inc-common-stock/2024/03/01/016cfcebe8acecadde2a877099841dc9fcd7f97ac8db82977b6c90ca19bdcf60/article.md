# The View from the Bridge

We’re excited to announce two new features that will make it even easier to find and book freight with Werner Bridge℠:


- Email alerts: You can now start receiving automated email alerts with available freight matching your preferences. We’ll only send up to two emails per day, so you won’t be inundated with messages. This is a great way to stay on top of the latest opportunities and never miss a load that’s a perfect fit for you.
- Mobile app: Our Werner Bridge℠ mobile app is now available! The app will give you even more flexibility and control over your shipments. You’ll be able to easily search for freight, book loads, track shipments, and more, all from your phone or tablet.


We’ve recently made some improvements to our search options, including the ability to negotiate bids. This means that when you need to negotiate a rate, you’ll receive an instantaneous response back and can then book the load with just a few clicks.


We’re confident that these new features will make it even easier and faster to find and book freight with Werner Bridge℠. We encourage you to try out the new email alerts and sign up for the mobile app as soon as it’s available.
