# Case Study: VIP Charter Operator Elevates Guest Experience with AI-Powered IFE

## The Challenge


A premium VIP charter operator managing a fleet of Gulfstream G650 and Bombardier Global 7500 aircraft needed to enhance their in-flight entertainment offering. Their high-net-worth clients expected cutting-edge technology, but installed IFE systems would require costly STCs and extended aircraft downtime.


## The Solution


The operator deployed **Icarus ONE with Anna AI** virtual flight attendant across their fleet:


- 8 Icarus ONE units for fleet of 6 aircraft (plus spares)
- Custom content library with premium 4K HDR movies
- Anna AI configured for VIP service queries
- Multilingual support for international clientele (40+ languages)
- White-label branding with operator logo and colors

## Anna AI in VIP Service


The Anna AI virtual flight attendant became a key differentiator:
