# Case Study: Regional Airline Transforms Passenger Experience with Portable IFE

## The Challenge


A growing regional airline operating ATR 72 and Embraer E175 aircraft needed to differentiate their passenger experience without the cost and complexity of installed IFE systems. With aircraft lease terms of 3-5 years, permanent modifications were not feasible.


## The Solution


The airline deployed **Icarus ONE portable IFE units** across their fleet of 12 aircraft. Key implementation details:


- 12 Icarus ONE units deployed across the fleet
- Zero aircraft modifications required
- Deployment completed in one week
- Content customized with airline branding
- Anna AI configured with route-specific destination information
