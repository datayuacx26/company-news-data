# Latham Pool to Hire 40 Additional Production Workers in Jane Lew

Delegates Patrick Martin, R-Lewis, and Ben Queen, R-Harrison, today praised Lewis County manufacturer Latham Pool Products for its plans to hire 40 additional production jobs at its Jane Lew facility.


“This is tremendous news for our region,” said Delegate Martin, who also serves as Vice-Chairman of the House Industry and Labor Committee. “This is a very tough time for businesses everywhere due to the COVI D-19 pandemic, and I’m glad and proud this local business is not only doing great, but thriving and investing further in our community.”
