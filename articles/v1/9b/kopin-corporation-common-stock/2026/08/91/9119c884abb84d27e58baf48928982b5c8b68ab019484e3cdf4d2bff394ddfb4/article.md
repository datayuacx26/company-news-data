# Kopin to Participate in Canaccord Genuity’s 46th Annual Growth Conference

WESTBOROUGH, Mass. –


[Kopin Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.kopin.com&esheet=54585424&newsitemid=20260806972858&lan=en-US&anchor=Kopin+Corporation&index=1&md5=d601b7f16e1f24e8e7e69232a4c1740e) (NASDAQ: KOPN), a leading provider of application-specific optical systems and high-performance microdisplays for defense, training, enterprise, industrial, consumer and medical products, today announced that management will attend Canaccord Genuity’s 46th Annual Growth Conference taking place August 11-13, 2026 in Boston, Massachusetts.


Kopin’s Chief Executive Officer Michael Murray is scheduled to host a fireside chat and, together with Chief Financial Officer Erich Manz, participate in one-on-one meetings with institutional investors throughout the duration of the conference.


**Canaccord Genuity’s 46th Annual Growth Conference** Fireside Chat Date: Tuesday, August 11, 2026


Fireside Chat Time: 4:30-4:55 p.m. Eastern time


Webcast:[https://event.summitcast.com/view/WuFmFdTcA9mVsUGHZJFU62/3akKjgVHpuixrUGqz7J3VM](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevent.summitcast.com%2Fview%2FWuFmFdTcA9mVsUGHZJFU62%2F3akKjgVHpuixrUGqz7J3VM&esheet=54585424&newsitemid=20260806972858&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fevent.summitcast.com%2Fview%2FWuFmFdTcA9mVsUGHZJFU62%2F3akKjgVHpuixrUGqz7J3VM&index=2&md5=edfcd847ada86ee4c823c8dff1a03a9d)


A live audio webcast and archive of the fireside chat will be available using the webcast link above. Registration is required for conference participation. For more information or to schedule a meeting, please contact your Canaccord Genuity representative.


Michael Murray commented: “We are pleased to participate in Canaccord Genuity’s Growth Conference and to engage with institutional investors as we advance our position across the defense and enterprise markets. This event provides an excellent opportunity to share how Kopin’s application-specific optical solutions are supporting next-generation warfighter systems and commercial platforms, and to discuss our strategic priorities and the milestones ahead.”


**About Kopin**


Kopin Corporation (NASDAQ: KOPN) is a leading developer and provider of innovative display, and application-specific optical solutions sold as critical components and subassemblies for defense, enterprise, professional and consumer products. Kopin’s portfolio includes microdisplays, display modules, eyepiece assemblies, image projection modules, and vehicle mounted and head-mounted display systems that incorporate ultra-small high-resolution Active Matrix Liquid Crystal displays (AMLCD), Ferroelectric Liquid Crystal on Silicon (FLCoS) displays, MicroLED displays (µLED) and Organic Light Emitting Diode (OLED) displays, a variety of optics, and low-power ASICs. For more information, please visit Kopin’s website at[www.kopin.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.kopin.com&esheet=54585424&newsitemid=20260806972858&lan=en-US&anchor=www.kopin.com&index=3&md5=9a5a2aeed3fad434b3095aa2e2895e39) .
