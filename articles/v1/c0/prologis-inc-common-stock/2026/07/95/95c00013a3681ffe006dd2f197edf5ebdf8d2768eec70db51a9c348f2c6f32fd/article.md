# Flash Report: 2Q26 U.S. Logistics Real Estate Market Snapshot

Prologis, in collaboration with Colliers, CBRE, Cushman & Wakefield, and JLL, is releasing a comprehensive snapshot of U.S. logistics real estate activity for the second quarter of 2026. The report presents Prologis Research’s long-standing proprietary methodology applied to available brokerage data alongside a brokerage average derived from real-time brokerage data covering 34 leading U.S. logistics markets. This continues a collaborative effort to bringing greater consistency, transparency and timeliness to market participants across our industry.
