## Hovnanian Enterprises to Present at J.P. Morgan's 2026 Global Leveraged Finance Conference


February 25, 2026


[PDF Version](https://khov.gcs-web.com/node/21136/pdf)


MATAWAN, N.J., Feb. 25, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Hovnanian Enterprises, Inc. (NYSE: HOV), a leading national homebuilder, announced that senior management will be hosting a fireside chat at J.P. Morgan's 2026 Global Leveraged Finance Conference taking place on Monday, March 2, 2026, at 11:00 am ET.


The presentation will be webcast live through the “Investor Relations” section of Hovnanian Enterprises’ website at[http://www.khov.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=RtNbqu3BD2CzqGXQxClxk3HAEcMjvA4nGFckyRl8AeYGHiyMlsZH1q56lpRQx_gEHxSajEPLpF8X6qgPWEU2UHbPO-5yI0BES1t7d3FQVpY=) . It is suggested that participants access the webcast event page at least five minutes before the live event. For those who are not available to listen to the live webcast, an archive of the broadcast will be available under the “Past Events” section of the “Investor Relations” page on the Hovnanian website at[http://www.khov.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=RtNbqu3BD2CzqGXQxClxk54dR7PMcsi6wQV0vbsWE0qbiZXHzDsEgIVvofng2DTHzkz3pDG6mCubWWUGNhcNR4vLrkh2rqJ0UqpxRrx0zjg=) .


**About Hovnanian Enterprises, Inc.**


Hovnanian Enterprises, Inc., founded in 1959 by Kevork S. Hovnanian, is headquartered in Matawan, New Jersey and, through its subsidiaries, is one of the nation’s largest homebuilders with operations in Arizona, California, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Maryland, New Jersey, Ohio, Pennsylvania, South Carolina, Texas, Virginia and West Virginia. The Company’s homes are marketed and sold under the trade name K. Hovnanian Homes. Additionally, the Company’s subsidiaries, as developers of K. Hovnanian’s Four Seasons communities, make the Company one of the nation’s largest builders of active lifestyle communities.
