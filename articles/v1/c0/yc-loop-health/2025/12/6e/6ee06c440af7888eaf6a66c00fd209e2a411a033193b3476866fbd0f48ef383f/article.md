Here's what actually matters: claim settlement ratios backed by IRDAI data, hospital networks that cover where you'll actually get treated, and financial stability that means your insurer will still be around when you need them.

## **What changed in 2024-2025 (and why it matters)**


IRDAI's Master Circular on Health Insurance (May 2024) introduced consumer-friendly reforms now benefiting Mumbai residents:


**Pre-existing disease waiting periods dropped from 4 to 3 years** , and insurers can no longer refuse coverage for severe conditions including cancer, heart disease, renal failure, and AIDS.


**1-hour cashless authorization became mandatory** , transforming hospital admission experiences. Industry-wide cashless delays dropped from 1.87% to 0.39% by H1 2025-26.


**The 30-day free look period** provides uniform policy review rights, and the **moratorium period reduction from 8 to 5 years** means claims cannot be rejected for non-disclosure (except fraud) after five years of continuous coverage.


Mental health insurance claims surged 41% year-over-year in 2025, prompting expanded coverage options from multiple insurers. Women-specific products like Bajaj Allianz HERizon Care address gender-specific health needs increasingly demanded by Mumbai's working women population.

## **What to actually look for in corporate coverage**


**1. Sum insured flexibility:** Standardized coverage doesn't work. Your C-suite needs ₹25-50 lakhs. Junior employees need ₹5-10 lakhs. Middle management falls between. Look for grade-based sum insured options, not one-size-fits-all.


**2. Pre-existing disease handling:** The waiting period dropped from 4 to 3 years (IRDAI 2024), but some insurers, like ICICI Lombard's Jumpstart reduce it to just 30 days. For companies with older employees, this matters enormously.


**3. Parental coverage:** 60% of Indian employees financially support parents. Including parent coverage (with reasonable premiums) becomes a major retention tool, especially in Mumbai's expensive healthcare market.


**4. Mental health parity:** Mental health claims surged 41% year-over-year in 2025. Your policy needs EAP integration, counseling coverage, and psychiatric treatment without sub-limits or stigma.


**5. OPD benefits:** Most policies cover only hospitalization. But employees need coverage for doctor visits, diagnostics, medicines, and preventive care. OPD benefits reduce out-of-pocket expenses and encourage early intervention.


**6. Wellness infrastructure:** Annual health camps are performative. You need ongoing engagement: bi-weekly wellness programs, digital health tracking, chronic disease management, nutrition counseling, and fitness support.
