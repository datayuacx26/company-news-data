# Helpshift focuses on their employees' health via Loop Health

Helpshift picked Loop Health as their partner to offer premium health insurance to over 100+ of their employees and family members.


[Helpshift Technologies Pvt. Ltd](https://www.helpshift.com/) ., a mobile-first customer service platform, and[Loop Health](https://www.loophealth.com/) , a provider of technology-driven health benefits, have announced an exclusive collaboration. Loop provides Helpshift employees and their families with group mediclaim benefits. With robust premium coverage for hospitalization, and unlimited, free-of-cost primary care wrapped in a single package, this agreement aims to help Helpshift employees live healthier lives.


Loop Health reimagines employee health benefits by offering free benefits such as unlimited primary care, medical advice, and mental health counseling. With a wide network of cashless hospitals and a team of seasoned physicians, Loop Health aims to reduce Helpshift medical claims by care-routing at a time of need.


Speaking about the collaboration, **Mayank Kale, CEO, Loop Health** added, “It has been a great year so far. We are constantly adding to our services for providing efficient medical solutions to all our members. We aim to ensure that all Helpshift employees live a healthy life. If there is a need for assistance then our medical and claim management team is available for them.”


This year, with Loop Health's managing Helpshift’s health benefits, they expect more employees to get involved with their health. Helpshift HR leaders agree that Loop Health will play a major role in their efforts to build a healthy, happier, and more efficient workforce.
