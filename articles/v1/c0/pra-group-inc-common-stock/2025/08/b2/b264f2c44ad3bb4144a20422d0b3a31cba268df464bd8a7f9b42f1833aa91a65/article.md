# PRA Group Colleagues Support Students Through School Supply Drive


#### *Employees collaborate to collect essential back-to-school supplies for children in Hampton Roads, Va.*


**NORFOLK, Va., Aug. 21, 2025** – As students were soaking up the final weeks of their summer vacation,[PRA Group](https://www.pragroup.com/) colleagues got to work collecting school supplies to help make sure children in Hampton Roads, Va., have what they need when they head back to the classroom.


In partnership with[WHRO Public Media,](https://www.whro.org/about-whro/overview) PRA Group employees held a school supply drive to collect and donate essential back-to-school supplies to local school districts. Among the items collected were notebooks, binders, pencils, markers, scissors, glue and cleaning supplies.


---


#### Supporting Students with Essential Supplies


“These are items teachers often purchase with their own money, so supporting this school supply drive goes a long way to help make sure local classrooms are stocked and students are set up for success,” said Zulema Green, U.S. national managing attorney at PRA Group.


WHRO will distribute the supplies to schools in the Hampton Roads area, which surrounds[PRA Group](https://www.pragroup.com/about-pra-group/) ’s global headquarters. WHRO Public Media is owned by a group of 21 local public school districts, collectively known as the[Hampton Roads Educational Telecommunications Association](https://education.whro.org/hreta-member-school-divisions) .


“Each year, children in our community attend their first day of school without basic educational tools. With your help, we can bridge the gap between underserved and academic success.”

#### Community Engagement and Philanthropy at PRA Group


“Our colleagues are always looking for meaningful ways to give back, and it’s wonderful to see an employee-led initiative like this school supply drive get such a strong response,” said Giovanna Genard, vice president of global communications and external affairs at PRA Group. “We’ve[collaborated with WHRO](https://www.pragroup.com/2024/08/pra-group-celebrates-back-to-school-season-with-supply-drive-for-whro/) on[numerous education](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/pragroup_pragroup-globalreach-localtouch-activity-7297343148484186112-qnto?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAA4qhqABzqIOjspWOO8ph1M02veY6__-j4c) and[literacy initiatives](https://www.pragroup.com/2024/02/pra-group-black-excellence-ergs-book-drive-collects-over-100-books-for-whro/) over the years, and we’re proud to continue supporting Coastal Virginia’s teachers and students into the 2025-2026 school year.”


Contributing to WHRO’s School Supply Drive is just part of PRA Group’s broader commitment to support the communities in which we work and live. Throughout the year, our teams organize[volunteer activations](https://www.pragroup.com/2025/06/spring-into-action-pra-group-volunteers-make-a-lasting-impact/) and opportunities to work with impactful nonprofits. To learn more about[our commitments](https://www.pragroup.com/our-commitments/) to philanthropy and volunteerism, visit our website.
