## **What makes a local business data provider different from a B2B database?**


Most B2B databases are built for corporate prospecting. They index LinkedIn profiles, company registries, and contributory networks to find VP-level employees at mid-market and enterprise companies. That works well if you're selling SaaS to a Director of Revenue Operations.


It doesn't work if you're selling to the owner of a plumbing company, a restaurant group, or an independent auto shop. Those owners don't have LinkedIn profiles. They don't appear in corporate directories. Their contact information lives in business registrations, tax filings, Google Maps listings, and local web data — sources most B2B databases don't index at all.


Local business data providers are built around this different data sourcing model. The best ones give you verified owner emails and direct contact information, not just the business phone number listed on the front door.

**What is the difference between a local business database and a B2B database?**


B2B databases like ZoomInfo and Apollo are built to find corporate employees — VPs, directors, and managers at mid-market and enterprise companies. **How accurate is local business contact data?**


Accuracy varies by provider and use case. Openmart reports 97-99% data accuracy across its 200M+ records, sourced from public business registrations and verified through regular update cycles. Data freshness matters more for local businesses than for corporate contacts — ownership changes, business closures, and email reassignments happen frequently. Providers that verify contacts monthly or in real time reduce bounce rates significantly versus static databases.


**Is it legal to use local business contact data for cold outreach?**


Yes, in the US. CAN-SPAM permits cold email to business contacts without prior consent, provided you include a physical address, an opt-out mechanism, and honor unsubscribes within 10 business days. CCPA applies to California residents but requires opt-out compliance rather than opt-in consent. For EU contacts, GDPR requires a documented legitimate interest basis. Openmart is GDPR and CCPA compliant by design — see the full guide to[GDPR, CCPA, and CAN-SPAM compliance for SMB prospecting](https://www.openmart.com/blogs/gdpr-ccpa-compliant-smb-data-providers) .
