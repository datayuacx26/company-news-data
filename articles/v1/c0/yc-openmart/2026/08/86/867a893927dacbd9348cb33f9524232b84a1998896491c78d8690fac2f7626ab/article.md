## Why revenue filtering is hard for HVAC leads


Picture an equipment financing rep staring at a list of 500 HVAC contractors, tasked with prioritizing the ones large enough to finance a $200,000 rooftop unit order. Nearly all of them are privately held, locally owned, and legally required to report revenue nowhere. No SEC filing, no press release, no public firmographic feed carries their numbers. That absence is exactly why sorting HVAC owners by size defeats most B2B data tools.


Generic contact databases were built around employees and decision-makers at companies large enough to appear in corporate data feeds. A three-truck HVAC shop doing $1.2M a year rarely shows up cleanly, so its revenue field comes back empty or wildly wrong. Live-crawl pitches promise to fix this by searching the web per lead, but they inherit their own accuracy problem at scale.


Two approaches compete to solve this, and the rest of this article evaluates both. One relies on a pre-verified, refreshed database of local business owners. The other sends AI agents to crawl the live web for each contractor on demand.

## Live web search vs. a pre-verified database


The live-crawl pitch sounds right until you check the numbers a sales team actually cares about. Advocates argue that static databases miss local HVAC owners and that live web search fixes the gap by pulling fresh data at query time. Freshness is real, but crawling each lead on demand means every contact goes through its own extraction, and the quality of any single record depends on what the crawler happened to find that moment. One owner's email parses cleanly, and the next comes back with a stale listing or a generic office line.


A pre-verified database avoids that variance because the work of confirming each contact already happened before you ran the filter. Openmart verifies records against its[200M+ local business database](https://www.openmart.com/products/local-business-database) and refreshes HVAC data weekly, so you get both current information and consistent per-lead accuracy across the whole list. You apply a revenue and company-size filter, and every row in the export has already cleared the same verification bar.


The difference shows up in deliverability. A B2B SaaS vendor selling to HVAC, plumbing, and home-service businesses ran scraped lists and saw bounce rates between 18% and 25%, high enough to threaten sender reputation. After switching to Openmart's verified contacts, bounces dropped to 3-5%. Cold email reply rates landed at 5-8%, and tighter regional campaigns reached 8-12%.


Weekly refresh gives you the freshness the live-crawl narrative promises, and pre-verification gives you the accuracy live crawling can't guarantee lead by lead. For a revenue-filtered HVAC list you want to email this week, you need both.
