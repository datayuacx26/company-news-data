# Legora expands to South Korea with new office in Seoul

South Korea is one of Asia’s largest legal markets, with a legal services sector worth an estimated $17.7 billion and some 32,000 practicing lawyers. The country’s leading firms operate at the standard of their London and New York counterparts, advising Korea’s global conglomerates on transactions and disputes that span the markets Legora already serves.


“Korea’s top firms work at the level of the best firms anywhere, and they advise companies whose legal work stretches across nearly every market we operate in,” said Max Junestrand, CEO and Co-Founder of Legora. “Korea is one of the most sophisticated legal markets in the world, and the concentration of major firms, legal departments and regulators in Seoul makes it a natural fit for how we work.”


Legora enters the market with a product built to support Korean practice from day one. This includes a Korean-language interface, HWP document support and Korean legal sources that are expanding rapidly. The company is now adding key features to localise the product further for the Korean market, meaning lawyers can research, review and draft across Korean- and English-language matters in a single workflow.


Legora is hiring a Seoul-based team spanning legal engineering, technical support and commercial roles, with Korean speakers across the team.


"We’ve seen a sustained period of demand from Korean firms and legal teams, making Seoul the natural next step for us in the region," said Heather Paterson, VP APJ at Legora. “The hardest part of AI in legal isn't the technology alone, it's adoption. This is what law firms need to get right. That's why we're committing to Korea, with a team on the ground from day one.


Legora has operated in Asia-Pacific since opening its Sydney office in 2025 and expanded to Singapore and Tokyo earlier this year. Its customers in the region include MinterEllison, Allens, Hamilton Locke, HWL Ebsworth, HSF Kramer, White & Case, K&L Gates, Dentons and Baker McKenzie.
