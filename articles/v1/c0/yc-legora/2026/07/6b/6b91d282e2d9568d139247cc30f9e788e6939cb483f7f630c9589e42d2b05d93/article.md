# Fasken accelerates its legal AI journey with Legora partnership

Fasken is pleased to announce a strategic partnership with[Legora](https://legora.com/) , the leading-edge agentic operating system for legal professionals, to support client work across the Firm. By adopting Legora’s collaborative, agentic AI workspace, Fasken is enhancing its ability to deliver more efficient and innovative legal services to better serve its clients while shaping the future of the legal practice.


Fasken selected the platform for its breadth of functionality, depth of agentic capability, and enterprise-grade security and privacy standards and their vision of how legal work is done. Legora provides a collaborative, agentic AI workspace that helps lawyers review, analyse and draft across complex matters, executing multi-step work within the tools they already use and integrating with core firm systems.


“At Fasken, we view artificial intelligence as an integral part of modern legal practice,” said[Clarke Barnes](https://www.fasken.com/en/clarke-barnes) , Firm Managing Partner. “By partnering with Legora and deploying this technology firmwide, we are positioning Fasken at the forefront of this transformation, enabling us to harness AI’s potential to deliver high-quality legal work faster while providing excellent client service.”


Fasken was an early adopter of Walter AI, an agent-native pioneering legal AI platform. Under a Joint Innovation Program with the company, Fasken lawyers played a key role in the development of the Walter AI Assistant. Walter AI was acquired by Legora in March 2026, bringing that technology, along with Fasken’s early collaboration, into the Legora platform, deepening its commitment to delivering a fully integrated agentic experience for legal teams.


Building on the innovation program first established with Walter, Fasken and Legora will continue collaborating to develop and refine agentic capabilities for legal workflows. This partnership reflects a shared vision to transform how legal work is delivered and the belief that meaningful innovation is built together.


“We are delighted to partner with Fasken, a firm known for its leadership at the intersection of law, technology, and agentic AI," said Max Junestrand, CEO and Co-Founder of Legora. "Fasken's deep understanding of where this technology is going makes them an ideal partner as we build what's next together and expand Legora's operations in Canada."


“Through our partnership with Legora, we are strengthening how we deliver legal services by embedding AI capabilities that support efficiency, consistency and high-quality outcomes for our clients.” said[Andrea Alliston](https://www.fasken.com/en/andrea-alliston) , Fasken partner and leader of the Firm’s Knowledge and Practice Innovation programs. “As we do that, it is critical that we use trusted tools like Legora to provide outstanding services, protect client interests, and remain transparent about how AI supports our work.”


As agentic AI continues to evolve, Fasken and Legora will explore new opportunities to enhance efficiency, unlock deeper insights and support increasingly sophisticated legal work. The collaboration will remain focused on delivering practical, client-centred innovation, ensuring advancements in AI help lawyers respond more effectively to client needs, navigate growing complexity and provide greater value. Together, they are committed to shaping the next generation of legal service delivery.
