# DKSH deploys Legora globally

Legora, the agentic AI platform for legal professionals trusted by more than 100,000 users at 1,200 leading law firms and in-house legal teams across over 50 markets, today announced that DKSH has selected Legora to support its legal operations globally.


Founded in 1865 and headquartered in Zurich, DKSH is one of the world's leading Market Expansion Services providers, helping companies grow in Asia Pacific, Europe, and North America. Listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange with a market capitalization of approximately CHF 4.04 billion, DKSH operates across four Business Units: Healthcare, Consumer Goods, Performance Materials, and Technology, serving multinational clients including Bayer, Eli Lilly, and Reckitt. With around 26,840 employees in 35 countries, the company reported net sales of CHF 11.1 billion in 2025.


Given this scale and global reach, DKSH's in-house legal function faces a level of complexity that demands more than conventional tooling. Legora will support the legal team across research, contract review, and drafting, enabling them to manage greater volume and cross-border complexity without compromising the precision that DKSH's multinational clients expect.


"At DKSH, AI is helping our legal team work smarter by accelerating routine tasks while preserving the judgment and expertise that matter most. Through our partnership with Legora, we have improved the efficiency of legal review, research, and drafting, enabling our lawyers to focus on strategic matters that support the business and our stakeholders." Jasprit Sahnsi, General Counsel, DKSH


"DKSH is a remarkable organisation: 160 years old, operating across four divisions and 36 jurisdictions, with some of the world's most demanding multinational clients. When a company of that heritage and complexity chooses Legora, it is a signal that enterprise-grade AI for legal use is no longer a future ambition. It is the infrastructure that organisations like DKSH need today." Leonard Schreij, VP Revenue EMEA, Legora.
