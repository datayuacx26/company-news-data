# Simmons & Simmons builds on AI frontrunner status with partnership with Legora

The wider integration of Legora further supports the work of client-facing teams across the firm’s global offices, strengthening AI fluency and the strong culture of innovation at Simmons & Simmons, operating in tandem with its existing, generative AI tools, such as Percy and Copilot.


Simmons is a market-leading AI firm – offering multi-award-winning in-house AI expertise, together with a renowned AI law practice supporting some of the world’s biggest companies with AI regulation, governance and implementation.


The partnership with Legora will build on the firm’s existing capabilities, further enhancing Simmons & Simmons’ successful “build and buy” approach to AI adoption.


The partnership is the latest milestone in the firm’s AI strategy. The strategy focuses on reimagining the delivery of legal services, by taking a human-led, trusted AI approach, improving knowledge capture, governance, skills and client service.


**Alex Brown, Head of TMT and the firm’s AI executive sponsor, said** : *“Legora is a powerful platform that will further drive efficiency, elevate our work and enhance our level of service to clients, supporting our existing group of exceptional AI tools, including Percy, Copilot and the hundreds of agents that are now in operation within the firm.”*


**Global Managing Partner Emily Monastiriotis added** : *“We’re pleased to be working with Legora, a platform that will boost our leading AI capabilities and support our continued growth. Legora’s deployment marks another step forward towards our goal of becoming a truly AI-enabled law firm.”*
