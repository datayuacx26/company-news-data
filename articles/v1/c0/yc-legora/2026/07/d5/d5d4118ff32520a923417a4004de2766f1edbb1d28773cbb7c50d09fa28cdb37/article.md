# Veritas Legal selects Legora as its legal AI partner

Legora, the global Legal AI platform trusted by more than 1,500 law firms worldwide, today announced that Veritas Legal, one of India's leading corporate and dispute resolution firms, has adopted Legora as its legal AI platform. The decision followed a rigorous evaluation on various performance, accuracy and security parameters. In the firm's assessment, Legora’s agentic architecture was well suited to the size and complexity of the Firm’s clients including handling a greater volume of documents and more complex, multi-step tasks, with higher accuracy and quality, and the results made a compelling case to adopt the platform.


"Our lawyers tested every leading AI tool in the market before making this decision. Legora came out ahead on the things that matter most to us in our daily work streams. It was accurate, the quality of its output was well structured and intuitive, and it handled a greater volume of documents and more complex, multi-step tasks. Legora was tested by all our practice groups, and the results were in line with expectations of an AI platform for both disputes and corporate practice areas, as per the feedback given by lawyers across the Firm," said Manav Raheja, Senior Partner, Veritas Legal.


"Veritas Legal is one of India's most highly regarded law firms, recognised year-on-year in top tiers by Chambers and Partners and The Legal 500 for its M&A, private equity, disputes and real estate practices. The firm brought one of the most rigorous evaluation cultures we have encountered, testing Legora alongside other AI tools in the market, running a direct, side-by-side comparison of all legal AI products in the market against Legora, before making the choice to adopt Legora. We are proud that its lawyers chose us on the strength of that process, and we look forward to a long and productive partnership," said Heather Paterson, VP of APAC, Legora.


Veritas Legal joins a growing group of Indian law firms and legal teams that have moved from AI evaluation to production deployment with Legora, including Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas, Dua Associates, DSK Legal, and Chandhiok & Mahajan.
