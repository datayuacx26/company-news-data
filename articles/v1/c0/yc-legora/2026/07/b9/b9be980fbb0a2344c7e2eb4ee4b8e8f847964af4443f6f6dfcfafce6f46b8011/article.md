# Arthur Cox LLP partners with Legora to scale its AI-enabled legal services

The partnership builds on Arthur Cox's well-established AI and innovation capabilities, delivering tangible enhancements in how the firm provides legal services to clients. Arthur Cox is the first Irish firm to establish a dedicated Innovation Lawyer Group, reflecting its long-standing focus on developing AI-enabled legal services.


That focus has shaped a delivery model that brings together lawyers, legal engineers and technologists to design and deliver solutions around client needs, rather than deploying AI tools for their own sake. The firm's approach combines proprietary technology built in-house with selected partnerships, supported by formal governance structures to ensure responsible and controlled adoption.


Legora now becomes part of that wider ecosystem, complementing Arthur Cox's own solutions and existing technology investments, and supporting the work of the firm's Innovation Lawyer Group.


"At Arthur Cox, we have a multi-faceted AI and innovation strategy. Our partnership with Legora is an important part of our broader technology ecosystem. We have invested significantly in the people, governance and capabilities needed to deliver AI-enabled legal services, and Legora complements our strategy to develop market-leading client solutions and deliver exceptional legal advice. We look forward to deepening the collaboration between Legora and our teams,” commented Geoff Moore, Managing Partner, Arthur Cox LLP.


"Arthur Cox has built one of the most credible innovation functions in the Irish legal market," said Max Junestrand, CEO and Co-Founder of Legora. "Firms that invest in people and governance the way they have are exactly who we built Legora for, and we're proud to support their lawyers in delivering exceptional outcomes for clients around the world."


Legora's team has been onsite with Arthur Cox over the past six weeks, working closely with the firm to support onboarding throughout the firm-wide rollout. The hands-on approach reflects Legora's commitment to the partnership and to ensuring lawyers across the firm are set up to get value from the platform from day one.


The collaboration marks a further step in Arthur Cox's ongoing work to evolve and scale its innovation capabilities, and adds to Legora's growing base of leading law firms using its platform to deliver AI-enabled legal services.
