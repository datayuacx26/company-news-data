# Legora and Tinexta in exclusive partnership to bring full corpus of Italian legal content directly to Legora

Under the exclusive deal, Legora users will benefit from comprehensive coverage of Italian case law, legislation and commentary. The partnership is based around the content currently available within LextelAI (owned by Tinexta Visura) — more than 7 million documents in total — including but not limited to:


**Case law (5M+ judgments)**


-


*Corte di Cassazione (Supreme Court)* — complete civil and criminal judgments


-


Lower-court (" *merito* ") decisions from the *Tribunali* and *Corti d'Appello* via the *Banca Dati di Merito* , under LextelAI's Ministry of Justice agreement — expanding toward full coverage as the corpus matures, with a further ~1M decisions expected by the end of 2026


-


Administrative justice — full coverage of the *Consiglio di Stato* and the Regional Administrative Courts ( *TAR* )


-


Tax courts — full first- and second-instance coverage


-


*Corte dei Conti* (Court of Auditors) — full coverage


-


*Corte Costituzionale* (Constitutional Court) — full coverage


-


EU courts (Court of Justice and General Court) — full coverage


-


*Arbitro Bancario Finanziario* (Financial Banking Arbitrator) — full coverage


**Legislation (2M+ documents) — fully consolidated, with daily updates**


-


National legislation and codes from 1947, including full legislative history (point-in-time versions)


-


Regional legislation from 1971


-


EU legislation from 1988


**Regulatory and authority materials**


-


Decisions of the regulatory authorities — AGCM, AGCOM, CONSOB, the *Garante per la Privacy* (Privacy Authority) and IVASS


-


Guidance and materials from Banca d'Italia — ANAC, ARAN, INAIL, INL, the *Agenzia delle Entrate (Italian Tax Authorities)* , INPS and the Ministry of Labour


**Commentary and doctrine (760K+ documents)**


-


*Il Foro Italiano*


-


*Codici commentati* ( *commented codes* ) and case notes


-


Leading Italian legal journals


The content will be available to Legora's entire user base, with users able to directly access original documents within the Legora platform. This will enable lawyers to research, draft and verify with greater speed and confidence — grounded in authoritative Italian sources.


Italy is one of Legora's fastest-growing markets, with thousands of lawyers at leading Italian firms using the platform daily. Authoritative, well-structured Italian content is critical to clients’ work, particularly access to lower-court decisions and practitioner commentary.


"Italian legal practice runs on authoritative case law, legislation and the commentary that interprets it," said Melanie Brown, Senior Director Legal Data . "This partnership brings that content into the workflow itself, so lawyers can move from question to well-grounded answer without leaving the tools they use every day. It's a clear example of our content strategy: pairing best-in-class AI with the trusted, jurisdiction-specific content lawyers rely on."


"We are delighted to partner with Legora to make our content available where Italian lawyers increasingly work," said Andrea Vingolo, Director General, Tinexta Visura. "We chose Legora as our partner because we believe in the quality of their product and the strong cultural fit with the Italian legal market. Together we can deliver real value to the Italian legal market and grow the reach of trusted legal content in the AI era."
