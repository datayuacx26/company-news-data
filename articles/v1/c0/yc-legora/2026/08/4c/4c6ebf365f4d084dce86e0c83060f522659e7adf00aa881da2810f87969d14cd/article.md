# Legora appoints Bryan Tsao as Chief Product Officer

Bryan joins from Jasper, where he served as CPO. Before Jasper, he ran PLG and Data at Dropbox, building growth and data functions at scale. He led product and design at Namely from Series C through Series E. Earlier, at Kabam, he helped scale the company from 16 to 800 people through a $1 billion exit. Across every stage, Bryan has brought the same thing: a data-driven rigour and an execution obsession that translates directly into product quality.


Legal work is high-stakes and unforgiving. The lawyers and organisations that use Legora rely on it for consequential decisions, complex matters, and work where precision is not optional. As Legora grows into the standard platform for global legal work, product has to keep pace with both the ambition and the standards the profession demands. That requires someone who knows how to build with rigour, scale with discipline, and stay focused on what matters to the people doing the work.


Bryan will lead product at Legora, bringing the execution depth the next phase of our growth requires.
