# Legance announces adoption of Legora across all practice areas

Legora, the agentic AI platform for legal professionals trusted by more than 100,000 users at 1,200 leading law firms and in-house legal teams across over 50 markets, today announced that Legance has rolled out Legora firm-wide. The platform is now available to every professional at the firm, across all practice areas and every level of seniority.


Founded in 2007, Legance is an independent, full-service law firm with offices in Milan, Rome, and London, and a leading player in the Italian and European legal landscape. The firm has grown to more than 400 professionals in under two decades, building a reputation for anticipating client needs and acting ahead of the market. The decision to deploy Legora across the entire firm is the latest in a series of strategic choices that have established Legance as a leader in the Italian and European legal ecosystem.


The implementation was a substantial undertaking. Completed over three months, it brought together a dedicated Legora team, Legance's full IT team, and senior representatives from every practice area.


The project was developed with the strategic and operational support of Irnerius, a consultancy firm founded by Andrea Arosio and specialising in AI within the legal sector.


“The decision to adopt Legora firm wide has been taken after and in depth analysis carried out with the support of our chief information officer Roberto Cantuccio, the involvement of our Innovation and strategic committee and a large number of colleagues” - said Monica Colombera, Legance Senior Partner and Head of Innovation & Strategic Committee - “Following the first months of firm-wide implementation of Legora, the initial results have been extremely encouraging. While efficiency gains are undoubtedly part of the value proposition of any AI-powered platform, our early experience suggests that the true opportunity lies elsewhere. We do not see AI merely as a tool to perform existing tasks faster. Rather, we see it as an enabler of a new generation of legal services—allowing us to deliver increasingly sophisticated, tailored and strategic advice to our clients”.


Legance's practice spans the full range of corporate, finance, dispute resolution, regulatory, and advisory work that defines a full-service firm. Across each of these areas, Legora supports lawyers in research, review, and drafting on complex matters, allowing teams to handle greater volume and complexity without compromising the quality their clients expect.


"Legance is one of the most respected independent firms in Europe, and the way they approached this says everything about why," said Max Junestrand, CEO and Co-Founder of Legora. "They committed to a full firm-wide deployment from the start, and they invested the people and the planning to do it properly. An implementation of this scope, delivered this quickly, is rare anywhere in the market. It reflects a firm that treats AI as core infrastructure"
