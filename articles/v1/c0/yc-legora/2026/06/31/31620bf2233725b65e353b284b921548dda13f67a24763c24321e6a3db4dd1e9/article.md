# Introducing Monitors

Today we're launching Monitors within the Legora aOS™ — the only horizon scanning product built into a legal AI platform.


Regulatory change is accelerating, and the cost of missing it is climbing. Banks alone paid[$4.5 billion in compliance-related fines globally in 2024](https://finbold.com/us-accounted-for-90-of-global-bank-fines-imposed-in-2024-finbold-report/) . Most teams still track it with manual website checks, vendor newsletters, and spreadsheets, with gaps in between.


[76% of compliance managers still scan regulatory websites by hand](https://www.metricstream.com/insights/state-of-compliance-survey-report-2021.htm) . That doesn't scale, and the blind spots tend to sit where visibility is weakest — at the edges of a global footprint.


### How Monitors works


Choose the jurisdictions and topics that matter to your business. Legora scans thousands of official sources, classifies and summarizes every article, and delivers a focused feed to your team.


**Set up.** Choose your jurisdictions and regulatory topics. Control the sources behind your coverage.


**Triage.** Articles include a summary, metadata, and a link to the original source. Filter, prioritise, and assign owners.


**Act.** Compare current law, assess impact, track tasks, and produce final deliverables — without switching tools.


### Comprehensive, authoritative, and traceable


-


10,000+ official primary sources across 100+ jurisdictions.


-


Every source checked every 60 minutes.


-


Every article links to its original source. You see and control exactly which sources are followed.


-


Plain-language summaries, noise filtering, topic classification, and deduplication.


This is what separates Monitors from a general-purpose AI tool. A chatbot runs a web search and returns whatever the rankings surface that day. Monitors checks a defined set of official sources on a schedule and processes every item in scope, so nothing slips through.
