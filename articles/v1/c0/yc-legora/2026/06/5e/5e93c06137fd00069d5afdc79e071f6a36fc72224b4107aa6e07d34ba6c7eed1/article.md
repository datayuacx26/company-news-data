# Introducing Lists

Today we're launching[Lists](https://legora.com/product/lists) , the structured way to manage legal work.


Legal teams spend a surprising amount of time tracking and structuring their work. Whether it’s a closing checklist, a chronology, or a regulatory tracker, the work needs structure, owners, deadlines, and a record of where every item came from.


The existing solutions in the space are fragmented, disconnected, and highly manual. Lawyers spend hours organizing work, then even more keeping it up to date as the matter moves. When something changes, they fall behind and the work with it.


That's why we built Lists: the one place where legal work lives and gets managed, populated by the Agent, cited to source, and shared by the whole team.


#### How it works


Getting started with Lists is straightforward. You can pick from one of our templates, ranging from Closing Checklists to Chronologies to Prospectus Verification. There are templates across Corporate, Finance, Capital Markets, Disputes, Real Estate, and Regulatory practices. When the work in front of you doesn't fit a template, you can easily create your own.


Upload your source documents — a credit agreement, evidence files, a draft prospectus — and the Legora Agent populates the list. Every condition precedent on the closing checklist. Every event on the chronology. Every material statement in the verification log. Each row arrives structured, with a status, an assignee, and a link back to the exact clause or paragraph of origin.


From there, your team gets to work on the list. Assign owners. Add comments. Update statuses. Sign off. Everyone working from the same live version. Whether your team is on Legora's Agent or the Assistant, both are wired to help you modify, update and complete your list items.


Every item shows its source, so when a partner asks where an item came from, the answer is one click away. In legal work, defensibility is half the job, and Lists is built for that.


#### Lists and the aOS


Lists sits at the heart of the Legora aOS: the agentic operating system for legal work. The Agent populating your lists is the same Agent completing legal work elsewhere in Legora. The source files connected to every row are the same files your team is reading, drafting from, and acting on. The same workspace your team uses every day now holds the structured records of every matter.


Lists brings task and fact management directly into the Legora platform.


#### What's next


This is the first iteration of Lists. As the aOS extends, Lists will extend with it into Portal, Tabular Review, Outlook and beyond, to become the connective tissue at the heart of Legora.
