# TransAct Technologies Announces Successful OEM Partnership w

[PDF Version](https://transacttech.gcs-web.com/node/20426/pdf)


*MedVantage Customers Improve Food Safety and Compliance*


HAMDEN, Conn.–(BUSINESS WIRE)–May 19, 2026– TransAct Technologies Incorporated (Nasdaq: TACT) a leading provider of cloud-based software and integrated hardware solutions, today announcedthe continued success of its OEM partnership with MedVantage, a leading provider of operational solutions to healthcare and food service environments.


Now in its third year, the partnership has driven significant adoption of TransAct’s purpose-built hardware and SaaS platform solutions bringing efficiency and visibility to the back-of-house operations in food service – helping customers improve food safety and compliance in hospitals and healthcare facilities across the US.


Through the agreement, MedVantage serves as an OEM distributor of TransAct’s food service technology, supporting GoFreshDate® Kiosk a labeling and compliance solution designed to enhance food safety, streamline operations, and reduce risk.


**Proven Market Traction and Customer Impact**


Since launching the partnership, MedVantage has successfully deployed TransAct’s solutions across a growing base of healthcare customers, delivering measurable improvements in food safety, compliance, efficiency, and cost control.


Customers leveraging the platform have reported:


- Improved food safety and labeling accuracy
- Reduced labor time associated with manual processes
- Greater consistency across multi-site operations
- Enhanced regulatory compliance and audit readiness


**Strengthening Operational Excellence in Healthcare**


“MedVantage has been an outstanding partner in bringing our back-of-house food service technology to market,” said John Dillon, Chief Executive Officer at TransAct Technologies. “Their deep relationships and domain expertise in healthcare environments have accelerated adoption of our solutions. Together, we are helping organizations modernize their operations while improving food safety and compliance.”


“Over the past two years, we’ve seen strong demand from our customers for smarter, more automated back-of-house solutions,” said Michael Atanasio, General Manager at MedVantage. “TransAct’s technology has enabled us to deliver a differentiated offering that addresses critical operational challenges while driving tangible ROI.”


**Expanding Reach Through OEM Collaboration**


The OEM relationship enables MedVantage to offer customers access to TransAct’s award-winning technology through its broader portfolio of solutions, further supporting their customers in healthcare and food service innovative ways to improve food safety and compliance.


**About MedVantage**


MedVantage delivers technology-driven labeling and inventory management solutions for food service and healthcare environments. As a veteran-owned small business with over 30 years of experience, MedVantage provides customizable, user-focused systems that use automation to streamline workflows, reduce human error, and improve operational efficiency.


In addition to its food service solutions, MedVantage supports the healthcare industry with specialized medical labeling technologies, including SteriDate® for sterile processing and IntelliDate® for medical labeling workflows, helping facilities maintain compliance, improve traceability, and enhance patient safety.


**About TransAct Technologies Incorporated**


TransAct Technologies Incorporated is a leading provider of cloud-based software and integrated hardware solutions that redefine how organizations connect operations, technology and data to drive measurable business value. Through its BOHA!® solutions, serving over 19,000 food service locations worldwide, TransAct combines purpose-built hardware with a cloud-based SaaS platform to help food service operators automate food safety, improve operational efficiency and maintain trusted brand relevance. In the casino and gaming market, TransAct’s award-winning EPIC solutions enable ticket-in/ticket-out (TITO) functionality and advanced promotional capabilities that enhance player engagement and drive revenue for operators globally. TransAct also provides a comprehensive portfolio of consumables and service solutions, allowing customers to simplify operations and partner with a single, trusted provider across their technology ecosystem.


TransAct is headquartered in Hamden, CT. For more information, please visit[transact-tech.com](https://www.transact-tech.com/) or call (203) 859-6800.
