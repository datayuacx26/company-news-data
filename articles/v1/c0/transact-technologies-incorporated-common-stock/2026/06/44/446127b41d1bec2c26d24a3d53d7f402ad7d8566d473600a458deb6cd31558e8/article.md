# Next-Gen BOHA! SaaS Platform Launch | TransAct Technologies

June 30, 2026


[PDF Version](https://transacttech.gcs-web.com/node/20456/pdf)


HAMDEN, Conn.–(BUSINESS WIRE)–Jun. 30, 2026– TransAct Technologies Incorporated (Nasdaq: TACT), a leading provider of SaaS platform and integrated hardware solutions, today announced the launch of its next-generation BOHA!® SaaS platform, delivering a major advancement in enterprise foodservice technology. The launch marks a major milestone in TransAct’s strategy to accelerate innovation and modernize back-of-house operations through a scalable cloud platform for customers worldwide.


TransAct has completed a strategic migration from a legacy hosted infrastructure to Microsoft Azure, significantly enhancing the platform’s scalability, security, resiliency and performance. This cloud architecture enables TransAct to fast-track innovation, customer requested enhancements and deliver seamless integrations to other in-house applications, such as POS, with improved agility.


The foundation of BOHA! is built on one of the world’s most trusted cloud infrastructures, Microsoft Azure, now offering enterprise grade uptime, high availability, and disaster recovery across large multi-location deployments.


“This launch marks a transformational milestone for BOHA!,” said John Dillon, Chief Executive Officer of TransAct Technologies. “By migrating to Microsoft Azure, we can bring innovation and new capabilities to market faster, seamlessly integrate AI-powered workflows, and continuously deliver greater operational value to foodservice organizations worldwide.”


TransAct has invested heavily in upgrading the BOHA! platform architecture leading up to the migration to Microsoft Azure. This launch is the foundation for AI-enabled solutions, IOT integrations, real-time data access and retrieval, and seamless efficiency throughout the BOHA! ecosystem.


Customers can now benefit from:


- A modern platform built for continuous advancement.
- Faster and moreresponsive software development for customer requested enhancements.
- A more reliable, scalable, and secure enterprise-grade platform.
- Steady integration with the back-of-house systems customers rely on.
- Scale every location with greater efficiency and control.


“BOHA! has evolved into a true enterprise-grade technology platform designed to help the world’s most valued brands operate more efficiently, effectively and economically,” Dillon added. “Our vision is to transform fragmented back-of-house technologies into a unified enterprise platform that connects operations, standardizes workflows, and delivers real-time visibility across every location. Today’s announcement underscores our commitment to modernizing the foodservice industry.”


BOHA! by TransAct helps restaurants, convenience stores, grocery and contract dining operators digitize and automate back-of-house operations, improve food safety compliance, streamline labeling workflows and optimize labor efficiency across enterprise environments.
