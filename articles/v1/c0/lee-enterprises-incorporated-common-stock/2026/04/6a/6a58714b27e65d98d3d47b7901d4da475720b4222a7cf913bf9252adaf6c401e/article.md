# Lee Enterprises plans quarterly call and webcast May 7, 2026

[PDF Version](https://investors.lee.net/node/19941/pdf)


DAVENPORT, Iowa, April 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Lee Enterprises, Incorporated (NASDAQ: LEE), a major subscription and advertising platform and a leading provider of high quality, trusted, local news and information in 72 markets, has scheduled an audio webcast and conference call for Thursday, May 7, 2026, at 9 a.m. Central Time. Lee plans to issue a news release before the market opens that day with preliminary results for its quarter ended March 29, 2026.


A live webcast of the conference call may be accessed via the Investor Relations portion of Lee’s website or[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=wsdo8LTdlCsVvHcSoqTaFLf6aUuyxk0sOQ0LXZupz0nGuQPpdfWHuiUEqPYDhtvvRWwFiN_B5I7Ww5ljdPnGB9tQbQJ33BDUECQl8g-fIb0=) . To participate in the live conference call via telephone, please register[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=wsdo8LTdlCsVvHcSoqTaFMF9x0ONnCRtzCCGdZ38dm5h7auKGjDwvTcD-ozBXh5cpNqZpoV8G0NU43ge1zIReF7efCjGoVTgd6C-C2lFI8XVlyghymvFAMv3JA-dBdlnBWolr16YKO7Z5yHKU4SVJbRShA4iCZQZh2xk09PfMpY=) . The live webcast will be accessible at[lee.net](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=n0blBx5bEXwiAN1gF_zAGHAEqHvMBrSePdIywiSs6bsDJ9ftuygAy1wNPdn0Pnz6t8ygu-BxTJlfpdYW8p7OgQ==) and will be available for replay 24 hours later.
