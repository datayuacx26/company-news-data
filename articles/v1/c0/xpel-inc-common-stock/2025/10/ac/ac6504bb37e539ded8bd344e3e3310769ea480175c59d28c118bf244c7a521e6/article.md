# XPEL and BMW Team Up Once Again for the 2025 Rebelle Rallyd

For the third year in a row, XPEL is proud to partner with


**BMW of North America**


in the


**Rebelle Rally**


— the longest competitive off-road navigation rally in the United States and the first women’s endurance off-road rally in America. The Rebelle Rally is a test of precision, endurance and grit, where competitors rely on their navigational skills, not GPS, to cross over 1,500 miles of rugged desert terrain.


### A Rally Like No Other


Taking place October 8–18, 2025, the Rebelle Rally brings together teams from around the world to tackle the breathtaking yet unforgiving landscapes of the American West — from the high deserts of Nevada to the dunes of California’s Imperial Sand. Each team navigates the course using only maps, compasses, and strategy, making it an event that celebrates adventure, resilience and skill.


[This year, Team 205 — the “R2D2 Team”](https://www.rebellerally.com/2025teams/205-r2d2-team-bmw-usa/) **—** features veteran driver **Rebecca Donaghe** , who returns to the Rebelle behind the wheel of a striking **BMW X5** , and **navigator Rebecca Dalski** , BMW North America’s Product Manager for the X5. Together, they combine expert driving with a rookie navigator who will experience the ultimate test of the vehicle firsthand. Their determination and teamwork define the Rebelle spirit.


### A Stunning Transformation: The BMW X5 in Monza Red


Before hitting the start line, the BMW X5 underwent a head-turning transformation thanks to XPEL’s **Monza Red COLOR Paint Protection Film (PPF)** . Combining vibrant color with industry-leading protection, XPEL’s COLOR PPF allows vehicles to stand out while defending against scratches, rock chips and harsh off-road conditions.


> “Color PPF challenges you in the best way. Every contour, every stretch, every reflection has to be flawless. But seeing it come together for a Rebelle Rally build made it more meaningful. It’s protection with personality, and it represents women pushing boundaries — both behind the wheel and behind the squeegee,” said **Ashley Maddux, PPF Installer.**


In the true spirit of the Rebelle Rally, XPEL assembled an **all-female team of expert installers** to apply the film to the X5. The project showcased not only the skill and craftsmanship behind every XPEL installation but also the company’s ongoing commitment to supporting women in the automotive industry. See the vehicle's transformation[HERE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZL2MjnAO4eY) .


> “Installing colored PPF for the Rebelle Rally really meant something deeper for me. It’s one thing to transform the look of a vehicle. It’s another to protect it for a challenge like this. Being part of an all-women’s Install where every detail matters reminded me why I love what I do. It’s about more than just film and finish — it’s about women showing up strong, skilled, and capable in every part of the automotive world.”


### Proven Protection in the Harshest Conditions


The Rebelle Rally is an extreme proving ground — both for the participants and the products that support them. Over the course of more than 1,500 miles of jagged rock, sand and dust, every inch of the BMW X5 will be tested. XPEL’s PPF plays a crucial role in maintaining the vehicle’s bold appearance while providing durable, real-world protection against the punishing desert environment.


This sponsorship continues XPEL’s mission to push the limits of what’s possible for surface protection technology, and to highlight how its products perform in the most demanding conditions imaginable.

From the all-female installation team to the experienced duo piloting the BMW X5, XPEL’s partnership with BMW of North America exemplifies innovation, teamwork and endurance — values that drive both the Rebelle Rally and the XPEL brand.
