Big news: Acely has officially partnered with ACT®, the organization that writes the exam itself, and we're proud to now be a preferred test prep provider of the ACT.


If you're prepping for the ACT, this changes what's possible. You can now study directly with official ACT content inside Acely's personalized platform, so there's no guessing at what test day will actually feel like.

## What the ACT partnership means for you


**Study with the real thing.** Acely now includes official ACT-created material, so the practice you're doing matches the exam you'll actually take. The official questions are mixed into the question bank, practice tests, and your study plan.


**Built for the new ACT.** The ACT rolled out an enhanced format in 2026, and all Acely content for the ACT has been updated to match it, so you're never practicing for a version of the test that no longer exists.


**A study plan that keeps up with you.** We build a personalized study plan for the ACT from your very first practice session, then adjust it automatically as you improve.


**Not sure ACT is even your test? Find out for free.** Your subscription includes full access to our SAT and ACT prep. That means you can practice both, compare your performance, and go all-in on whichever test you're actually stronger on, without paying for two separate subscriptions to figure it out.
