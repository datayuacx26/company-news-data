## Honestly: what to expect


Acely works. The results and reviews are consistent. Students can improve 200 to 400 points when they start in lower bands, and top scorers can get even higher into 1500+ / 34+ range. Starting score doesn't determine outcome. Consistency, time, and structured practice are the primary drivers.


But like any prep resource, you get out what you put in. Students who commit to a real study window, take multiple full-length practice tests, and work through their mistakes see the strongest results. If you're looking for a last-minute cram the week before your test, this isn't designed for that.


That said, Donna's daughter improved over 100 points in 3 weeks of 15 to 60 minutes a day, so even a shorter window with consistent effort can move the needle.
