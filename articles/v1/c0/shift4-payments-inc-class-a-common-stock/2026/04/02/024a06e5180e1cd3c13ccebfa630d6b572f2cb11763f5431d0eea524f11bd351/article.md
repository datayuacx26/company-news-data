CENTER VALLEY, Pa.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Shift4 (NYSE: FOUR) today announced the date for the release of its first quarter 2026 financial results.


**Q1 2026 Earnings Conference Call**


Shift4 will release its first quarter 2026 financial results pre-market open on Thursday, May 7, 2026. Management will also host a conference call at 8:30am ET to review these results.

**May 19, 2026** – Management will participate in a fireside chat at the J.P. Morgan 2026 Global Technology, Media, and Communications Conference beginning at 2:15PM ET. **About Shift4**


Shift4 (NYSE: FOUR) powers the experience economy, enabling businesses to deliver the moments that matter. Transforming how people shop, dine, stay, and play, Shift4's commerce technology allows for a seamless experience at any scale. From your neighborhood restaurant to the world’s largest event venues, Shift4 handles billions of transactions annually for hundreds of thousands of businesses around the world.
