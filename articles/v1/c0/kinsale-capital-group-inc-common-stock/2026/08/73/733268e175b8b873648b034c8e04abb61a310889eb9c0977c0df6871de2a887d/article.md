### Kinsale Capital Group Announces Dividend Declaration


August 12, 2026


RICHMOND, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Kinsale Capital Group, Inc. (NYSE: KNSL) today announced that its Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.25 per share of common stock. This dividend is payable on September 14, 2026 to all stockholders of record as of the close of business on August 28, 2026.


**About Kinsale Capital Group, Inc.**


Kinsale Capital Group, Inc. is a specialty insurance group headquartered in Richmond, Virginia, focusing on the excess and surplus lines market.
