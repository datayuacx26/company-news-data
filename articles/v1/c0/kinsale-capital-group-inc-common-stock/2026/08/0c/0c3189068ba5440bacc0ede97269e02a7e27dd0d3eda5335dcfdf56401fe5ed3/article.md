# 3 Divisions & 1 Broad Appetite for Storm & Restoration Contractors


## **Why Storm and Restoration Contractors Need Coverage Year-Round**


Hurricanes take the spotlight from June 1 to November 30, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration’s official Atlantic hurricane season—and for a good reason.[Even a single storm](https://www.kinsaleins.com/find-coverages-for-cat-exposures/) can cause billions of dollars in damage overnight. However, hurricanes are only part of the picture. Tornadoes, hailstorms, extreme ice and snow, and other CAT events present year-round risk, not just a seasonal one.


Brokers handling contractor accounts know all too well the flood of submissions storms can create. A restoration company can carry claims from three separate “storm seasons” and still be untangling coverage in the fourth, long after a single named storm has come and gone. That’s why submissions from storm clean-up, restoration, and remediation contractors don’t cluster into one quarter of the calendar. They arrive year-round, in every size and shape, from a two-person crew to a multi-entity operation.
