##### **Water Softening & Filtration Contractor: Non-Renewed, Bound in 24 Hours**


**Unique Characteristics:** Non-renewed out of the standard market due to losses; submitted two days prior to expiration
**Coverage Solution:** Quoted same day; bound next day
**Premium:** $40,000


When a Tennessee water softening and filtration contractor was non-renewed out of the standard market due to losses, the broker’s window was closing fast. When our underwriter received the submission with just two days to go before the policy expired. The account was quoted the same day it was submitted and bound the very next day, giving the broker a big win and the contractor the continuous coverage they needed.


##### **Commercial Elevator Contractor: New Venture, Next-Day Quote with NY Labor Law Coverage Included**


**Unique Characteristics:** New venture; NY labor law exposure in a challenging market
**Coverage Solution:** Quoted next day; NY labor law coverage included
**Premium:** $300,000


A startup elevator contractor operating entirely out of New York City brought its broker a tight timeline and no loss runs of its own. Our underwriter reviewed the principals’ prior experience, underwrote the risk on that basis, and had a quote back the next day with the required NY labor law coverage included. For a new venture entering one of the country’s most demanding construction markets, that kind of turnaround makes all the difference.


**Note:** Kinsale’s New York appetite is for interior operations only


##### **Residential General Contractor: Large Loss History, Quoted Within 24 Hours**


**Unique Characteristics:** New account with a large jobsite injury claim in loss history; submission cleared Friday afternoon
**Coverage Solution:** Quoted within 24 hours (Monday morning); uninsured subcontractor deductible; expanded residential limitation up to 100 units
**Premium:** $26,000


Choices felt limited for a residential general contractor in Washington thanks to a large job site injury claim in their loss history. With time running out, the broker submitted the account on a Friday afternoon, timing that might slow down many carriers. By Monday morning, our underwriter had the quote delivered. In addition to the quick turnaround, our underwriter had also structured the policy with an uninsured subcontractor deductible and expanded the residential limitation up to 100 units, giving the contractor the flexibility it needed to keep building.


##### **HVAC, Plumbing & Electrical Contractor: Non-Renewed with Prior Loss, Same-Day Quote**


**Unique Characteristics:** Non-renewed by incumbent due to losses, including a $632,000 fire damage claim from a breaker issue; submission received 2 days prior to expiration
**Coverage Solution:** Quoted same day; policy terms silent on fire; coverage placed despite prior fire-related loss history
**Premium:** $75,000


A multi-trade contractor in Los Angeles County had a difficult file with non-renewal by its incumbent carrier due to losses, a $632,000 fire damage claim in its history, and only two days until the policy expired. The broker turned to Kinsale. Our underwriter reviewed the account and quoted it the same day, structuring terms that were silent on fire—meaning the insured received coverage without an exclusion tied to the claim type that had caused its non-renewal. The account was bound in time to prevent a coverage gap.


##### **HVAC & Refrigeration: New Height Limitation; A Work-at-Height Solution Delivered Next Day**


**Unique Characteristics:** Incumbent added a height limitation at renewal, restricting coverage for the contractor’s core work
**Coverage Solution:** Competitively priced; work-at-height coverage fully included; quoted next business day
**Premium:** $76,775


When a Florida HVAC and refrigeration contractor came up for renewal, the incumbent added a height limitation, a practice that’s become increasingly common in the Florida contractor market. The broker brought the account to Kinsale, where height work is a part of our appetite. Our underwriter delivered a competitively priced quote the next business day, with work-at-height coverage fully included.
