##### **Here’s how it works:**


If a primary policy carries a $25,000 AOP deductible and a $500,000 Wildfire deductible, it won’t respond to a wildfire loss until the loss exceeds $500,000. However, Kinsale can issue a Wildfire-only Deductible Buyback policy that attaches at $25,000 with a $475,000 limit and covers the $25,000–$500,000 layer.


So, on a $1,000,000 wildfire loss:


**Insured Retention**
$0 – $25,000


**Buyback Policy**
$25,000 – $500,000
($475,000 limit)


**Primary Policy**
$500,000 – $1,000,000
