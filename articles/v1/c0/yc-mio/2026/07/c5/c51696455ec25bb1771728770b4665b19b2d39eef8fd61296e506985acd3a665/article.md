# Mio and Master Concept partner to enhance intelligent collaboration

## Introduction


We are excited to announce our partnership with Master Concept, an Asia Pacific-based Google Workspace Premium Partner.


Master Concept is committed to creating intelligent, efficient, and seamless office collaboration experiences through innovative technology. Their solutions simplify workflows and improve team productivity with intelligent tools that ensure organizations stay ahead of the curve.


Our partnership will bring Mio's Google Workspace interoperability solution to the APAC market, empowering enterprise collaboration between Google Chat and Microsoft Teams or Slack.


## Partnership benefits


1. **Smooth communication** : Seamless cross-platform communication promotes collaboration among teams and accelerates the decision-making process.
2. ‍ **Strengthened data security** : Built with security in mind, Mio prioritizes the protection of your data, ensuring the integrity and privacy of information. **‍**
3. **Professional support** : Certified engineers from Master Concept and Mio’s technical teams will provide support to ensure smooth implementation and ongoing maintenance. **‍**
4. **Enhanced admin support** : Mio's native solution includes built-in automations to reduce admin upkeep and manual tenant management.

## About Mio


Mio is the leading provider of chat interoperability solutions. Users of team collaboration apps like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack can use Mio to chat with colleagues who use different messaging apps. The Austin, Texas-based company was founded by Tom Hadfield and James Cundle in 2016. Mio has raised $17 million from investors including Zoom, Cisco, Goldcrest Capital, Eniac Ventures, Two Sigma Ventures, Khosla Ventures, Y Combinator, and Capital Factory. Learn more at[www.m.io.](https://www.m.io/google)
