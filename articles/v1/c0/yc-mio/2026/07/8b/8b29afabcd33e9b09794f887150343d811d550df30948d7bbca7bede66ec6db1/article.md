# Mio and Gapps partner to drive cross-platform communication for Google Workspace customers

Mio, a leading provider of chat interoperability solutions, is excited to announce a new partnership with Gapps, a renowned digital transformation consultancy with deep expertise in Google Workspace. This partnership aims to enhance cross-platform communication capabilities for enterprises leveraging both Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, creating a seamless and collaborative environment for all users.


## Enabling interoperability for modern enterprises


With Mio’s advanced interoperability solutions, enterprises can now ensure that their employees using Google Workspace can communicate effortlessly with colleagues using Microsoft Teams. This integration is especially valuable for businesses managing hybrid environments, allowing them to unify communication channels without requiring users to switch tools.


> “We’re thrilled to partner with Gapps to offer an even greater range of solutions for enterprises using Google Workspace alongside other collaboration tools,” said Jake Bailey, Chief Commercial Officer at Mio. “Together, we can bridge communication gaps and deliver a more unified, efficient experience for teams who rely on cross-platform collaboration.”


## Gapps: A trusted partner in digital transformation


Gapps has an established reputation for guiding organizations through transformative projects, equipping them with tools that boost productivity and collaboration. With Google Workspace specialization, Gapps will now offer Mio’s interoperability solutions to address a growing need for seamless multi-platform communication.


> “At Gapps, we're dedicated to empowering our customers with the best tools for seamless and secure collaboration. This partnership with Mio allows us to break down communication barriers and offer a truly unified experience for businesses using both Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. We're excited to help our customers achieve greater productivity and connectivity no matter what techstacks are being used across teams, said Marc Josefsson CEO at Gapps. "With Mio’s solutions, our customers can unify their teams without the limitations of single-platform reliance. We’re excited to offer a more connected experience for our customers across platforms. ”


## Empowering teams to work smarter across platforms


Through Mio and Gapps’s collaboration, businesses can benefit from key interoperability features such as:


- **Chat Sync** : Enable real-time messaging across Google Chat, Microsoft Teams, and Slack, keeping teams connected on their platform of choice.
- **Directory Sync** : Simplify contact management, allowing users to search and connect across different platforms seamlessly.
- **File Sync** : Ensure smooth document sharing and collaboration across Google Drive and SharePoint, regardless of the user’s preferred platform.
- **Calendar Sync:** Enhance the Google and Microsoft calendar integration with cross-platform user directories and instant event updates.


This partnership brings together Mio’s leading-edge interoperability technology and Gapps’s extensive experience in digital transformation, empowering organizations to break down communication silos and support efficient, cross-functional collaboration.
