# Mio and Dynamo6 Join Forces to Revolutionize Workplace Collaboration

We’re excited to announce our new partnership with Dynamo6, a forward-thinking digital transformation agency based in New Zealand and Australia. Dynamo6 provides a mix of cloud, security, and software solutions and holds both Microsoft Modern Work and Google Workspace Work Transformation specializations. Through this partnership, Dynamo6 will now offer Mio’s native chat interoperability solution to connect the growing number of organizations wanting to run both Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.


This partnership brings together Dynamo6’s expertise in modern work solutions and Mio’s mission to break down communication barriers across platforms. Businesses across New Zealand and Australia can now unify their communication environments, helping their teams work more effectively without switching tools.


Korrin Balmain, Change & Partnerships Director of Dynamo6, said,


> "Our focus on effective and secure solutions made it a natural choice to partner. The Mio solution allows teams to work better together, regardless of their preferred productivity tools and more so than the native integrations can allow. This helps businesses break down silos, improve efficiency, and ultimately achieve their goals."


Mio offers a unique interoperability solution that enables native, bi-directional communication across platforms like Google Chat and Microsoft Teams. Features like Directory Sync and File Sync allow employees to collaborate in real-time and across platforms without the burden of managing multiple accounts or licenses.


Jake Bailey, Chief Commercial Officer of Mio, said,


> “Our partnership with Dynamo6 allows us to expand into the New Zealand and Australian markets with a partner who shares our passion for innovation. Together, we’ll empower businesses to work smarter by eliminating communication barriers across platforms.”


Dynamo6 is known for supporting organizations through every step of their digital transformation journeys. With a strong focus on customer success, they design tailored solutions that help businesses adapt, innovate, and succeed in today’s evolving workplace.


To learn more about how Mio and Dynamo6 can streamline communication in your organization, visit[Mio’s website](https://www.m.io/) .
