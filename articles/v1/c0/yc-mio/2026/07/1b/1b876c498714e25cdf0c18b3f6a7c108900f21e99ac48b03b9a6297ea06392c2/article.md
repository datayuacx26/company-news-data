# Announcing the Mio and Brio Partnership: A New Era of Cross-Platform Collaboration

We’re thrilled to announce our latest partnership with Brio, a cloud consulting services provider based in India. Brio specializes in cloud solutions, IT automation, and digital transformation for businesses. Brio will now offer Mio’s bi-directional native chat interoperability solution for businesses using Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.


Brio’s expertise in IT services and digital transformation perfectly complements Mio’s mission to deliver bi-directional chat and file-sharing interoperability. With this partnership, companies around the world can now seamlessly connect their Microsoft and Google environments, ensuring a smooth collaboration for all of their employees.


Abdul Muneem, Cofounder, Director of Brio, shared his excitement:


> “We’ve been looking for a solution for the South Asian market that breaks down the barriers between Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. Our partnership with Mio is the answer. We are excited to offer our customers what is the only bi-directional interoperability solution for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.”


Mio is the only middleware solution that enables real-time interoperability for teams using Microsoft Teams and Google Chat. With features like Directory Sync, businesses can connect employees across platforms without worrying about managing multiple accounts or licenses.


Tom Hadfield, CEO of Mio, added:


> “Partnering with Brio gives us the opportunity to bring Mio’s interoperability solution to a wider audience. We’re excited to work together to empower businesses with seamless communication tools.”


Brio has built a reputation as a trusted IT partner in India, offering tailored solutions to help organizations achieve their digital transformation goals. By joining forces with Mio, they can now offer even more value to businesses looking to optimize their workflows and eliminate work silos.


To learn more about how you can bridge the gap between Microsoft and Google Workspace, visit[Mio’s website](https://www.m.io/) .
