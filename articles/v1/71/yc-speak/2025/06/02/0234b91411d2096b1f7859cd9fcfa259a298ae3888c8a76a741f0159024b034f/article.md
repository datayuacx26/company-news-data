# Introducing our 21-day challenge: Win a tickets to practice the language you’re learning IRL

Summer travel is here, and so are 4 new language courses on Speak: French, Italian, Japanese and Korean.


Ready to put your language learning into action? Join our 21-day challenge this summer for your chance to win two round-trip tickets to a country where you can practice the language IRL that you’ve been working so hard to learn.


From June 17 to July 15, we’re challenging you to complete a 21-day streak on Speak, and rewarding a few of you for your consistency, creativity and commitment in a big way.


Whether you’re a beginner or getting back into the habit, Speak is here to help you build confidence through consistent, real-world speaking practice.


Let’s go places — literally.


**The Challenge**


To enter, all you need to do is:


- Use Speak for 21 consecutive days between June 17 - July 15
- Join the 21-day challenge in the app
- Follow[@speak_official](https://www.instagram.com/speak_official/) on Instagram
- Post (1) video on Instagram sharing your learning experience and where you want to go to practice your language + tag[@speak_official](https://www.instagram.com/speak_official/) .


That’s it! Show up, stay consistent, and share your story.


**The Prize**


We’re giving away two round-trip tickets to the destination of your choice (in the form of airline gift cards), so you can finally practice your speaking skills IRL.


We’re picking (1) winner per language pair we offer, so 6 winners total across Spanish, French, Korean, Italian, Japanese and English.


**Eligibility**


Full terms & conditions[here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1q2v4n1dowDNB6-ghmpekC9bOEOiF7Xkl/view?usp=sharing) . A few highlights:


- You must be 18 years or older to enter
- You must reside in the contiguous United States
- You must complete your 21-day streak within the 30-day window (June 17-July 15)


Winners will be selected and notified on July 21, 2025.


Let’s go places — literally. Join the challenge here, we can’t wait to hear how fluent you become!
