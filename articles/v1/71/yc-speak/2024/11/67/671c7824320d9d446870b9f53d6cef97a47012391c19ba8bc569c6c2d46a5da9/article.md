# Speak named Google Play’s “Best App of 2024” in Hong Kong, Korea and Taiwan

Our team is filled with gratitude today as we are named Google Play’s Best App of 2024 in Hong Kong, Korea and Taiwan – three of our largest and fastest growing markets.


When we started our company eight years ago, we had the same vision we had today – build the best path to spoken fluency. We’re proud to have stayed true to the mission and see people all around the world becoming fluent in English with Speak.


We’re excited for what 2025 has to bring as we continue building a conversation-first approach to language learning.


Thank you to our loyal community, Google Play and our incredible team – we celebrate this together. Download Speak today to begin communicating, one conversation at a time.
