# Meet the Micro-Retailers: Part I

Previously, we talked about the critical role on-site small businesses play in enhancing the quality of walkable neighborhoods. Micro-retail businesses help foster personal relationships between shop owners and customers, are more attainable retail spaces for first-time business owners, and are conveniently located for residents and visitors. Let’s introduce you to three of Culdesac’s micro-retail businesses: Dose of Youth, Sew Used, and Complete Comforts!


**Dose of Youth**


The first small business you’ll encounter upon arrival at Culdesac is Dose of Youth. Committed to delivering quality results in a safe and welcoming environment, Nurse Giannii combines medical expertise with a passion for wellness to offer rejuvenation from the inside out. As a registered nurse specializing in IV therapy, vitamin injections, and ‘tox treatments, Nurse Giannii’s goal is to help you look and feel your best. Whether you're seeking a boost of energy, a radiant glow, or simply a moment of self-care, Dose of Youth has what you need to support a better, healthier you.


Instagram:[@doseofyouth](https://www.instagram.com/doseofyouth/?hl=en)


**Sew Used**


At Sew Used, Justin and Jalissa curate a collection of secondhand fashion that includes vintage, Y2K, and trendy pieces. Their eye for style and upcycling yields a store teeming with pre-loved garments ready for a new home. Justin and Jalissa's expertise extends beyond the racks. With professional alteration services, they breathe new life into old favorites, ensuring a perfect fit for every customer. Pop into Sew Used to revamp your wardrobe, just in time for summer. Tip: Shop the $10 rack outside their store for affordable pieces that rotate daily!


Instagram:[@sewused_](https://www.instagram.com/sewused_/?hl=en)


**Complete Comforts**


Rooted in a passion for home and well-being, Complete Comforts offers a variety of homemade and thoughtfully chosen products. From organic teas and local honey to fragrant candles and cleaning products, Emmanuel and Tiffany want to help you create a space that nourishes both body and mind. But their commitment to comfort extends beyond the confines of their store – they also offer same-day home cleaning services you can sign up for in-store. Stop by Complete Comforts to enjoy a warm, delicious cup of homemade tea or browse their curated collection of eco-friendly home goods while you’re in the neighborhood!


Instagram:[@completecomforts](https://www.instagram.com/completecomforts/)
