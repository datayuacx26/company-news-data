# A Very Merry Culdesac Holiday Gift Guide

Not sure where to start with gifts for your loved ones this year? We’ve got you covered, from the ebike enthusiast to the little urbanist in the making! At Culdesac, we believe mobility, community, and open space are three core needs and pillars of living life to the fullest.[Studies](https://www.theatlantic.com/business/archive/2014/10/buy-experiences/381132/) show that spending money on experiences rather than stuff alone brings people the most joy. That’s why we suggest gifting people you love (including yourself) items that will bring you and your loved ones happiness this winter season by supporting shared experiences built around mobility, community, and open space.


(By the way, these aren’t sponsored–just cool things we genuinely can’t get over!)


### **A Community Gift Guide: Share what you love with your neighbors**


While games and goodies are fun to enjoy at home, they become even more delightful when enjoyed with your broader village. Whether you’re looking for a weatherproof outdoor game or utilitarian gear to support your endeavors in the neighborhood, we’ve got you covered:

Give the gift of color this season with an assortment of non-toxic, washable jumbo chalk. Easy to find at many local stores, there will be plenty to share with friends, family, and neighbors alike. Creativity belongs to everyone!
