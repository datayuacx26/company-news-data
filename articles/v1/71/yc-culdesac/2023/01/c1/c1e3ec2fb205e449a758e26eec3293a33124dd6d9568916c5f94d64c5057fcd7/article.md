# From our friends at Lugg

**At Culdesac Tempe, we’re excited to announce our partnership with**[Lugg](https://lugg.com/) **, an on-demand moving and delivery service. ‍ ‍** Think Lyft for moving, Lugg will help you get settled into your new apartment with ease. We know that sometimes things get too heavy to carry on foot or you need to make a long trip across town with lots of boxes or furniture pieces. When those moments arise, Lugg can help you out! Plus, all Culdesac Tempe residents receive a discount when using Lugg!


‍[Moving](https://lugg.com/services/small-moves)


Move into your new apartment at Culdesac Tempe with ease. Instead of renting a truck and straining your back, use Lugg to handle everything for you. Book on-demand for pick up within 30 minutes, or schedule out 30 days on the hour of your choice. All crews are equipped with moving blankets, stretch wraps, tie-down straps, and all the materials needed to ensure every item is protected along the way.


[Same-Day Store Delivery](https://lugg.com/services/store-delivery)


Lugg is an awesome option for getting furniture or other items delivered from the store to your home whenever you need them. They’ve partnered with retailers nationwide like IKEA, Restoration Hardware, Lowes, Home Depot, and more to enable on-demand same-day delivery within an hour.


[Marketplace Delivery](https://lugg.com/services/used-item-delivery)


Lugg is perfect for used item delivery from marketplaces like Facebook, OfferUp, Etsy, eBay, Craigslist, and more! They’ll pick up from any online marketplace and go the distance to deliver that item you love, up to 150 miles outside their service area.


[Package Delivery](https://lugg.com/)


Lugg isn't just for moving or delivery, they'll bring your packages to the local post office, UPS, FedEx, or wherever they need to go. Skip hauling large packages in a Lyft or on the bus. Lugg will pick up and deliver your items where they need to go for an affordable price, saving you time and energy.


[Donations](https://lugg.com/services/donations) or[Junk Removal](https://lugg.com/services/junk-removal)


Lugg will help you donate your items to any charity of your choice so you can feel good knowing you're helping to make a difference. If you need a receipt, just let your crew know at pick-up. If your items are too damaged to donate, Lugg can help you by disposing of them at the nearest recycling center of your choosing.


[Storage Moves](https://lugg.com/services/storage-moves)


Lugg will pick up and deliver your items to the storage facility of your choice. The best part is that if you need your items back, they’ll be delivered to the location of your choosing. You can even have Lugg deliver them directly to a location for an event, such as a birthday party or wedding.


[Moving Labor](https://lugg.com/)


Lugg is a great option for helping move heavy items around the house. For example, if you have heavy furniture, gym equipment, or appliances that need to be rearranged. Lugg will come do the heavy lifting so you can sit back and relax. Just enter the same address for pickup and drop-off.


Similarly, if you're hosting an event at your place (whether it's a family reunion or office party), Lugg can be used to set up tables and chairs or even provide extra "oomph" when lifting heavy items.


[The Lugg App](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id919765207)


The Lugg app allows for peace of mind knowing exactly where your belongings are, every step of the way. You can see when your Luggers arrive at each location and their estimated arrival time at their final destination. Call or text your crew directly through the app with any special instructions or questions. Available on both the[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id919765207) and[Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lugg&pli=1) .


[Trust and Safety](https://lugg.com/safety)


As a company, we place a high value on trust and safety. All our Luggers are background checked, backed by our insurance policy, and thoroughly vetted to ensure a 5-star experience. With millions of happy customers, Lugg's reviews speak for themselves. They also have a dedicated[Support Team](https://lugg.com/help) to help you every step of the way.
