# Announcing Culdesac’s Partnership with Lectric eBikes

Culdesac Tempe is proud to announce our partnership with[Lectric eBikes](https://lectricebikes.com/) , one of the fastest growing ebike companies in the world, based right here in the Valley of the Sun. This partnership is a new addition to Culdesac’s comprehensive suite of[mobility benefits](https://culdesac.com/tempe/faq) and will provide residents with another option for getting around Tempe and beyond.


As part of this partnership, the first 200 residents of Culdesac Tempe will receive a[Lectric XP 2.0](https://lectricebikes.com/collections/xp-2-0-series) ebike for free. They'll only need to cover a small service charge. The award-winning Lectric XP 2.0 is valued at $999. It is equipped with both pedal assist and throttle, a 48V lithium ion battery, and a front fork suspension. Residents interested in a different Lectric ebike model will receive a $300 credit toward an ebike of their choice.

To commemorate this incredible partnership with Lectric, Culdesac has named the pathway off the light rail stop between The Hub and Cocina Chiwas “Lectric Avenue”. This partnership is one of the many ways we strive to expand mobility options and make car-free living easier, more accessible, and fun for our residents.
