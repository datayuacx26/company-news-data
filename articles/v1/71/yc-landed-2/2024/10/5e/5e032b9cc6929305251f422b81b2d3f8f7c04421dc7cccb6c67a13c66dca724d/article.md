# LANDED - Inc. Names LANDED as a 2024 Power Partner Award Winner

Inc., the leading media brand and playbook for the entrepreneurs and business leaders shaping our future, today announced its third annual **Power Partner Awards.** The prestigious list honors B2B organizations across the country that have proven track records supporting entrepreneurs and helping startups grow.


This year, **LANDED** is honored to be listed among other great companies across marketing and advertising, health and wellness, financial services, legal, logistics, public relations, and productivity, as well as other critical areas of business.


Every company on the Inc. Power Partner award list received top marks from clients for being instrumental in helping leadership navigate the dynamic world of startups. These B2B partners support entrepreneurs across various facets of the business, including hiring, compliance, infrastructure development, cloud migration, fundraising, etc., allowing founders to focus on their core missions.


> “This is our definitive listing of vendors and suppliers who have demonstrated excellence in serving midsize and enterprise customers,” says **Inc. editor in chief Mike Hofman** . “As part of the vetting process, our team of editors, researchers and reporters gathered information on companies’ products and services, assessed their reputation as captured in online comments and forums, and collected customer testimonials to ensure that the sales pitch matches the actual client experience. In every case, we spoke to founders like you who were happy to attest to a vendor’s genuine commitment to a mutually beneficial business partnership. We’re happy to be the conduit for that positive word of mouth.”


> “We are thrilled to be recognized for the strides we’re taking to build the AI-enabled future of work for blue collar workers,” says **Vivian Wang, Founder and CEO of LANDED.** “Our inclusion on Inc.’s Power Partner Awards list is a huge testament to the difference that we’re making in our industry, and I couldn’t be prouder of our team. We are so grateful to our customers and partners for helping us reach this milestone. This is just the beginning!”


LANDED is the definitive end-to-end hiring integration in the restaurant and hospitality industry. By leveraging advanced generative AI, LANDED automates manual tasks such as resume review, candidate screening, and interview scheduling, resulting in game changing cost and time savings during the hiring process. LANDED has redefined recruitment for hourly workers, working with clients like Erewhon, KFC, Five Guys, and Tao Hospitality Group to transform their recruitment strategies.


Over the past two years, LANDED has 4X'd in size and has quickly become one of the most trusted hiring partners in the restaurant and hospitality space. In addition to their inclusion on Inc.’s Power Partner Award list, LANDED was also featured in[Gartner’s 2024 Hype Cycle for Talent Acquisition Report](https://employer.gotlanded.com/press-release/gartner-talent-acquisition-report) , and was the 2023 winner of[MURTEC’s Start-Up Alley.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBMIZQUkP5A)


To view the complete list of Power Partner Award winners, visit:[https://www.inc.com/power-partner-awards.](https://www.inc.com/power-partner-awards/2024)

**About Inc.**
