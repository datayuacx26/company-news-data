# 5 Top Tips to Supercharge Your Restaurant’s Applicant Tracking System

Whether you use ADP, Workday, TalentReef, UKG Pro, or any of the other Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) out there, one thing is clear:


Your ATS is only as good as *how you use it.*


While your chosen system may boost all of the latest bells and whistles, if you haven’t figured out a way to optimize it for your restaurant’s hiring process, all of those features may as well be moot.


In fact, an underutilized ATS system may be slowing down your operators and adding more frustration to their already overfilled schedules.


Today, we’re diving into 5 top tips to supercharge your restaurant’s Applicant Tracking System and secure the best hires in the shortest time.


And don’t worry! Our tips apply to *any* ATS.


# What is an Applicant Tracking System?


Before we begin, let’s take a quick refresher on what an Applicant Tracking System is.


An Applicant Tracking System is a software tool used by businesses to track candidates through the recruiting process. From posting a job description to delivering a final offer, your ATS system should be your source of truth for all things hiring.

## Are there different types of ATS?


All ATS systems serve the same purpose – to help your restaurant manage their talent acquisition process.


But, certain features, such as integrations, candidate tracking metrics, and AI-powered capabilities, set certain ATS’s apart from others.


Now that we’ve covered what an ATS system is, let’s dive into our **5 top tips to help you leverage the system that you already have.** ‍


## 1) Perform regular process audits


Begin by auditing the ways your team currently uses your ATS, taking a detailed look at the entire hiring process. This will help to identify inefficiencies and areas for improvement.


Consider these questions:


- Are all of your hiring managers using the system in the same way? Some may be more knowledgeable than others – leverage this to upskill your entire team.
- Are there any unnecessary steps or bottlenecks slowing things down?


By compiling companywide knowledge, you can create a manual of best practices tailored to your restaurant’s needs. Encourage regular reviews of key ATS processes, including candidate vetting, applicant-to-interview rates, interview-to-hire rates, and hiring manager feedback.


Implementing a regular audit schedule will ensure that your restaurant’s hiring process remains fresh and relevant.


This schedule will also come in handy as you onboard new managers. After all, it’s important to know *how* to use an ATS, but even more crucial is understanding the nuances of *how your team* uses the ATS.


A regular review cadence provides an accessible point of entry for new hires to understand the ATS culture of your business.


## 2) Open a feedback loop with your ATS provider


Your ATS provider isn’t just a vendor—they’re a partner in optimizing your hiring process. After all, there’s no bigger expert than the provider themselves.


If your ATS provider offers customer support or consulting, make sure to take advantage of it.


Here are a couple topics to kick off the conversation:


- **What are your restaurant’s unique hiring needs?** Break them down to ensure that your system setup is aligned with daily usage.
- **What are the latest feature updates to your ATS?** Your decades old ATS may have a couple new tricks that you’re not even aware of. Chat through them with your point of contact and define a strategy to integrate them into your workflow.
- **Which areas of your hiring process are underperforming?** Ask your ATS provider for best practices or recommendations.


## 3) Establish a clear hiring framework


One of the best ways to optimize your ATS is to implement a structured framework for hiring.


To avoid confusion, create and maintain standardized procedures for your hiring managers to follow when using the ATS. Regularly review and update vetting criteria—especially for management roles—to ensure that you’re selecting the right candidates for every position.


Sit down with your team to clearly define how to handle candidates, ensuring they are given priority and visibility.


If you haven’t done so recently, coordinate a database cleanup to get rid of outdated data. Duplicate submissions or misfiled information can lead to missed opportunities.


You might also consider setting up a structured feedback process for departments to review hiring data regularly. Adjust your strategy based on real insights.


Establishing a clear framework will prevent missteps, keep your team aligned, and allow your ATS to truly work as intended.


## 4) Leverage integrations to make your life easier


Chances are that your business uses multiple tech tools to keep your restaurant running smoothly.


But did you know that toggling between applications may be taking up a significant portion of your time?


According to the[Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org/2022/08/how-much-time-and-energy-do-we-waste-toggling-between-applications) , the “toggle tax” – that is, switching back and forth between different windows or apps – can take up an estimated **9% of annual work time.**


Reduce your “toggle tax” by taking advantage of the many ATS integrations that exist, such as:


### HRIS Integrations


Consider integrating your ATS with other platforms you use regularly, like your Human Resources Information System (HRIS).


This allows your teams to automatically generate reports for workforce analysis and seamlessly manage employee changes.


### E-Signature Applications: Dropbox, Namely, DocuSign


Integrating your ATS system with an e-signature application such as Dropbox, Namely, or DocuSign allows you to save time on one of the most important steps of the interview process: sending an offer letter to successful candidates.


Given the high volume of hourly workers employed by multi-unit franchises, any way to cut down on the time it takes to hire talent is a win in our book.


### Collaboration Applications: Slack, Microsoft Teams


Collaboration apps are often the backbone of communication between leadership, whether at the same location or across the larger franchise family.


Integrating your chat app with your ATS can streamline the recruitment process through real-time communication. Via instant message, hiring teams can make faster decisions, keep track of candidate updates, and easily share feedback.

In a landscape where job seekers apply for up to 14 positions in one sitting, speed to respond is key to elevating their experience while securing the talent you want. And given that companies that focus on building a positive employee experience are 4x more profitable than others and generate 2x the average revenue (Harvard Business Review), boosting your employer brand is something that you’ll want to pay close attention to.


By connecting your collaboration apps to your ATS, you’ll create a more efficient, transparent hiring workflow that benefits both your team and your candidates.

## 5) Utilize data to stay on top of hiring trends


Data is your best friend when it comes to improving your hiring outcomes.


Your ATS generates valuable metrics that can guide decision making and help you finetune your process.


Here are some questions to approach your data with:


- Which job boards are producing the best candidates?
- Which times of year are your hiring efforts most successful?
- Is there a certain time of the day or night that applicants are most responsive?
- Have your hiring managers been struggling with interview ghosting? At which locations?
- What age range do your applicants typically fall in?


By searching for data driven answers to these questions, you’ll be able to implement meaningful solutions.


For example, certain job boards might be more popular with certain age ranges. Maybe your Millennial candidates are more active on Indeed, while Gen X candidates prefer to come into the store with a paper resume.
