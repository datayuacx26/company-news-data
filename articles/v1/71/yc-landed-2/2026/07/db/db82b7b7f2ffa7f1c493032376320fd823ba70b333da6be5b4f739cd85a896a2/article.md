ChatGPT 3.5, GPT's most widely used tier, only has a **70-75% accuracy rate** across all topics. GPT-4 ranks slightly higher at **85-88%.** This number scales up and down depending on the topic, with STEM fields ranking at the highest accuracy percentages (88.9%) and clinical medical questions scoring 30-40%—sometimes with potentially harmful errors.


That's not to say AI isn't useful. Studies have shown that ChatGPT helps general users complete tasks **up to 37% faster** , significantly boosting productivity and saving almost an hour each day.

## Role Transfer: Never Lose a Quality Candidate


Candidates apply for roles they aren't an exact match for all the time. But just because a candidate isn't the right fit for one particular job doesn't mean they should be written off.


LANDED's Role Transfer is an automated, AI-powered capability that allows candidates who don't qualify for one role to be seamlessly recommended and redirected to an alternative fallback role within the same company.


Here's how it works: if a candidate fails one of your preset vetting questions, LANDED's Generative AI automatically runs a compatibility check.


We compare the candidate's answers against the minimum requirements of other roles. Someone who has applied to be a Full-Time Manager may not have the required availability, but could be a wonderful Weekend Manager.


Our GenAI invites the candidate to consider the new role. Should they accept, they're moved into that role's pipeline and will continue with any vetting questions not already asked.


This feature is an incredible opportunity to boost top-of-funnel without going back to the drawing board every time. Viable candidates are redirected, not lost, and your brand doesn't have to sink more dollars into sourcing new talent.
