## What Fyxer does, and why people look elsewhere


Fyxer is an AI email assistant that connects to your Gmail or Outlook account and does three things: it sorts incoming mail into categories like "To Respond", "FYI" and "Marketing", it writes reply drafts in your writing style, and it sends a notetaker into your video calls that leaves a follow-up email waiting in your drafts folder. The company behind it is Fyxer AI Limited, a UK business, and the product now brands itself simply as Fyxer rather than Fyxer AI.


It is genuinely good at triage and drafting, which is what earns the 4.1 rating. Where it runs into trouble is billing, inbox coverage and the single seat, the three things reviewers name most. Two of those limits come straight from Fyxer's own pages, which is worth knowing before you compare anything.


The first: it never sends. Fyxer's own pages state that it cannot send emails on your behalf, only draft them. Every reply still waits for you. The second: it runs on Gmail and Outlook, and nothing else. If your business lives in Apple Mail, Fastmail, or anything self-hosted, Fyxer is not an option at any price.


Add the per-seat billing on top and three questions decide the choice. What will it actually cost me, which inbox does it run on, and how much of the work does it finish?


## Why people leave Fyxer


The reviews cluster into five complaints, and the loudest one is money.


### 1. The bill grows seats you did not add


Four one-star reviews name it between June and July 2026, more than any other complaint in that window. One reviewer described being[charged $2,400 after four seats appeared on their account](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a5825298f433372ee009944) , which they say happened because other people with a similar email domain signed up. Another signed up at £23 a month and[found the plan had moved past £90](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a357acd6bed2a5fbc68aed6) . A third reported being[double-billed and then missing the customer call booked to sort it out](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a6b4c78d4fadfc2038015fb) .


Fyxer charges per seat and a seat is one person. What the reviewers describe is seats they did not create: one says Fyxer told him people on a similar email domain had signed up. Fyxer's own pricing page says only an admin can change the roster, so the extra seats reviewers describe are not something Fyxer's documentation explains. Check your own seat count directly.


### 2. It drafts, but it will not send


Fyxer states this plainly: it drafts your emails and never sends them. For some people that is exactly right, and a real safety feature. For others, never sending is the whole disappointment, because what you wanted handled still comes back as a first draft you have to read, edit and send.


Answer that before anything else, because it decides whether SaneBox belongs on your list at all: it files mail and writes nothing.


### 3. Gmail and Outlook, or nothing


Fyxer supports Gmail and Outlook. That covers most business email, but not all of it, and there is no workaround. This is why "Fyxer alternative for Outlook" and questions about other providers come up constantly in search.


If you are on Outlook you have options here. If you are on Apple Mail, Fastmail or your own server, SaneBox is the tool built for you, since it filters inside whatever client you use. Proton is the one setup nothing on this page fixes, because its encryption exposes no IMAP for a filtering service to sit on.


### 4. The categories are its categories


Fyxer sorts mail into its own fixed buckets and learns from your corrections. One reviewer, an executive director at a small nonprofit,[described the learning curve honestly](https://www.g2.com/products/fyxer/reviews/fyxer-review-12924351) : mail landing somewhere other than where they would have filed it, the line between "FYI" and "notification" taking a while to click, and occasional drafts that never appeared for messages it had marked as needing a reply.


One reviewer also[had a meeting summary go out to every participant because a box was checked](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a6248f0459ff443f9fb0a81) , and asked for something Fyxer does not currently do: different tones for different recipients, so a note to a friend does not read like a note to a prospect.


### 5. It is one seat, one person


Fyxer is built for an individual. The Starter plan connects one inbox and one calendar; Professional adds more inboxes for the same person. What it does not give you is a shared inbox, where two people work the same support or bookings queue and can see who has replied. Upgrading from Starter to Professional adds inboxes for the same person rather than a shared queue, so a two-person team hits the same wall on either plan.


## What actually matters in a Fyxer alternative


Matching Fyxer feature for feature is the wrong test, because the feature you are leaving over is rarely the one you would have compared on. Ask these four questions instead.


### 1. Does it send, or only draft?


Fyxer answered this for you every day: it drafts and never sends. If that felt safe, Superhuman Mail, Shortwave and Inbox Zero keep the same behaviour. If it felt like extra work, Marblism prepares the reply and sends it once you approve. A $50 drafting tool and a $24 doing tool are not solving the same problem.


### 2. Which inboxes does it actually run on?


Fyxer runs on Gmail and Outlook and nothing else, so you already know what it feels like when the provider decides for you. Check coverage first, because it rules out most tools in one move. Shortwave and Gmelius are Gmail-side, Superhuman Mail, Serif and Inbox Zero match Fyxer's two, and SaneBox works with whatever you already use.


### 3. How does a seat become a bill?


Per-seat pricing is normal and fine, right up until seats can be created without you. Ask three things: who can add a seat, what happens when someone on your domain signs up, and whether you get told before the charge or after. If I could only ask one question of any tool on this page, it would be that one, because it is the question behind the $2,400 charge and the jump from £23 to £90 above. Flat pricing sidesteps the whole issue, because a plan with no per-seat line has nothing to inflate.


### 4. Is email the whole job, or part of it?


Email is often the loudest problem rather than the only one. If your week also has social posts, follow-ups, missed calls and content going unwritten, an email-only subscription solves a fifth of it and you are looking again in a month. Marblism covers all six of those jobs for close to what Fyxer Starter costs. Lindy can reach outside the inbox too if you are willing to build it, and Missive adds SMS and social, but everything else here is built around the inbox alone.

## How to switch off Fyxer without losing anything


Moving is straightforward, but do it in this order. Most of the billing complaints come from what happens after you think you have left.


1. **Open your Fyxer dashboard and count the seats.** Before anything else, check how many seats are on your subscription and whether you recognise every one. This is the step the one-star reviews are all missing.
2. **Run the new tool alongside Fyxer for a week.** Do not cancel first. Most tools here give you 7 to 30 days free, so use the overlap to see whether the drafts actually sound like you.
3. **Do not let two tools sort the same inbox.** Switch Fyxer's categorization off before the new tool starts filing. Two systems filing the same mail leaves you with folders that disagree, and you spend the trial week sorting that out instead of deciding whether the drafts sound like you.
4. **Cancel through the dashboard, then confirm the charge stopped.** Deleting your Fyxer account reverts the inbox to how it was. Check your card statement on the next billing date rather than assuming.
5. **Export anything you need from the notetaker.** Meeting notes and recordings live in Fyxer. If you want them, pull them before the account closes.

## Frequently asked questions


### About Fyxer


#### Is Fyxer AI worth it?


For one person on Gmail or Outlook who uses both the email drafting and the meeting notetaker, yes. Fyxer holds 4.1 out of 5 on Trustpilot and does its core job well. It is worth less if you only wanted triage, since cheaper tools do that, and it is a poor fit for a team, because it is built around a single seat rather than a shared inbox.


#### Does Fyxer work with Outlook?


Yes. Fyxer connects to both Gmail and Outlook, and those are the only two providers it supports. There is no path for Apple Mail, Fastmail or a self-hosted server, and Proton is beyond every tool here because its encryption exposes no IMAP. If that is your setup, SaneBox is the tool in this comparison built for it, since it sits on the mail server rather than replacing the client. Marblism and Missive both connect any IMAP account too, Marblism to draft your replies and Missive to run a shared team inbox.


#### How much does Fyxer cost per month?


Fyxer Starter is $30 per user per month billed monthly, or $22.50 billed annually. Professional is $50 monthly or $37.50 annually and adds multiple inboxes, scheduling and a HubSpot integration. Enterprise is custom priced with a 50-seat minimum. There is a 7-day free trial and no free plan. Pricing is per seat, a seat is one person however many inboxes they connect, and several reviewers report being billed for seats they did not add, so check the seat count on your own subscription.


### Choosing an alternative


#### What is better than Fyxer AI?


Different tools, depending on what you are leaving over. Superhuman Mail is rated higher at 4.8 out of 5 and is faster, and Inbox Zero does the same triage and drafting for $20 a month instead of $30. ### Cost and switching


#### Why did my Fyxer bill go up on its own?


The most common cause reported by reviewers is extra seats. Fyxer charges per person, and several reviewers describe discovering multiple seats they did not create, with charges that followed automatically. One says Fyxer attributed it to other people on a similar email domain signing up, though Fyxer's own pricing page states that only an admin can change the roster. Check the seat count in the Fyxer dashboard, and check it before a renewal date rather than after.


#### What is the cheapest Fyxer alternative?


SaneBox at $2.04 a month on two-year billing, though it only filters and does not draft replies. Among tools that actually draft, Inbox Zero and Shortwave Pro tie at $18 per user per month, Inbox Zero on annual billing and Shortwave on its personal tier, with Gmelius just above at $19 on yearly billing. Marblism is $24 a month on annual billing and includes five more roles beyond email, which changes the comparison from price per tool to price per job.


#### What happens to my inbox when I cancel Fyxer?


Nothing is lost. Fyxer's own FAQ says deleting your account puts the inbox back the way it was, so the categories it applied come off and your mail stays where it always was. What does not undo itself is the billing, which is why the switch-off steps above start with counting your seats rather than cancelling.
