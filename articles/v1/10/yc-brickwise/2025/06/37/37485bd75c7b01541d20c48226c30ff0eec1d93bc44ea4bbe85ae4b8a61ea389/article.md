# Top 5 Maintenance Issues Handled by AI — With Zero Landlord Involvement


Maintenance issues are one of the biggest headaches for both tenants and landlords. Traditionally, every step — from logging the issue to following up — has involved slow calls, missed emails, and manual coordination.


But AI is changing that.


With platforms like **Brickwise** , the entire repair workflow can be automated — meaning less stress for tenants and more time for landlords and agents to focus on growth.


---


## Why AI Makes a Difference in Maintenance


AI doesn't just log tickets. It handles every step:


- Talking to the tenant
- Understanding the issue
- Gathering key details (photos, urgency, access)
- Scheduling the right contractor
- Following up after the fix


**The result?** Faster resolutions. Clearer communication. And fewer problems escalating.


---


## The Top 5 Repairs Brickwise Handles Daily


### 1. Leaking Taps & Pipes


Among the most common complaints. Brickwise asks:


- Where is the leak?
- When did it start?
- Can you send a photo?


Then it books a plumber automatically and shares updates with both the tenant and landlord.


---


### 2. Heating Not Working


Especially urgent in winter.


Brickwise:


- Checks tenancy records
- Identifies heating system type
- Schedules an engineer


If urgent, it escalates within minutes. If not, it finds the next available slot.


---


### 3. Broken Appliances


Tenants often struggle to describe issues. Brickwise guides them:


- "Is it turning on?"
- "Any error lights?"
- "Can you send a short video?"


This avoids unnecessary callouts and gives contractors useful context.


---


### 4. Pest Infestations


Usually vague at first:
*"I saw something in the kitchen."*


Brickwise narrows it down:


- Type of pest
- Frequency
- Location


Then it triggers pest control and alerts the landlord if needed.


---


### 5. Electrical Problems


AI excels here for **safety-first triage** .


- "Is the power out in the whole flat or just one area?"
- "Any burning smells or sparks?"


If dangerous, it skips all steps and dispatches emergency contractors.
