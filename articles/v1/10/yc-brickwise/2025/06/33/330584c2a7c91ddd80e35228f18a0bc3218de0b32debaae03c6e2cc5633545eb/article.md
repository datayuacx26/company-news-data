## What 24/7 Really Means — And Why It Matters


"24/7 support" isn't just a nice-to-have anymore. It's quickly becoming a baseline expectation for renters, especially in urban markets.


Tenants live busy, irregular lives. Some work late shifts. Others are students with odd hours. And maintenance issues? They rarely appear at convenient times.


When something goes wrong at night — whether it's a heating issue in winter or a security concern — a delay in response causes stress. That stress turns into distrust. Distrust turns into churn.


Offering **true 24/7 support** builds credibility and prevents escalation. It doesn't just make tenants feel safe — it also:


- **Reduces late-night complaints**
- **Lowers the volume of emergency callouts**
- **Prevents voids by increasing satisfaction**
- **Protects your brand reputation on review sites and social media**


Tenants today are used to instant service — whether from banks, retailers, or airlines. They expect no less from their letting agency or landlord.

## Emergency vs. Routine — AI Knows the Difference


One of the key benefits of Brickwise's AI engine is its ability to **prioritize intelligently** .


A dripping tap at 1am doesn't require an emergency callout.
Water pouring from the ceiling? That's a different story.


Brickwise assesses urgency based on:


- **Keywords** : "Flooding," "locked out," "heating off"
- **Contextual cues** : Whether it's winter, time of day, or tenant vulnerability
- **Property records** : Past repair logs and typical failure points


Routine issues are politely acknowledged and scheduled for the next available slot during working hours. Urgent ones are escalated instantly — either to your team or your preferred contractor list.


This approach ensures resources aren't wasted on false alarms, while real emergencies are handled with speed and care.
