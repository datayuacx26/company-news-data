#### **What is Spend Management Software (SMS)?**


At their core, spend management software refers to digital tools designed to optimise and automate your company’s purchasing and expense processes. They enable your business to track, control, analyse, and streamline spending from purchase requests to approvals, payments, and reimbursements.


A good[spend management](https://www.kodo.com/blog/importance-spend-management) software integrates with accounting and[ERP systems](https://www.kodo.com/blog/erp-ap-automation-integration-guidehttps://www.kodo.com/blog/erp-ap-automation-integration-guide) to provide seamless workflows that prevent unnecessary delays, reduce errors, and ensure compliance. They provide a unified view of your company’s financial data, allowing real-time insights into spending patterns and helping you identify opportunities to save.

**Key Features to Look For in Your Spend Management Software**
The perfect spend management software solution is more than just a tool to track business expenses. Instead, it should offer a range of functionalities that cater to your unique business needs and support your growth trajectory.


Here are some crucial features you must look for in your spend management software:


-


**User-Friendly Interface and Accessibility** : Always look for software that is intuitive and easy to use. A good SMS should offer a user-friendly interface that allows both finance teams and employees to easily submit and approve expenses, manage payments, and access reports. It must also be accessible via web and mobile platforms, ensuring flexibility for remote or on-the-go teams.


-


**Integration with Accounting and ERP Systems** : Ensure that the software integrates seamlessly with your existing accounting or ERP system. This eliminates manual data entry, reduces errors, and allows for real-time updates between your spend management platform and financial records.


-


**Customisable Approval Workflows** : Make sure that your spend management software allows you to customise approval workflows based on your company’s needs. This ensures that expenses are routed to the appropriate team members for review, helping you maintain control over your spending.


-


**Multi-Platform Payment Options** : A versatile spend management platform should also support multiple payment options, such as bank transfers, credit cards, and digital wallets. This flexibility becomes more crucial if your business has a diverse vendor base and varied payment requirements.


-


**Reporting and Analytics Capabilities** : Your chosen spend management software must also offer customisable reporting options, including budget tracking, category-wise spend analysis, and vendor performance reviews. This helps your business make data-driven decisions to optimise spending.

#### **How to Choose the Perfect Spend Management Software for Your Business**


Selecting the right spend management software is a pivotal decision that can significantly impact your business's financial efficiency and growth trajectory. With numerous options available, it’s important to select a solution that meets your business needs, scales with growth, and simplifies expense tracking.


To ensure you make an informed choice, consider the following key factors:


-


**Assess Your Business Size and Needs** : Before diving into software options, evaluate your company’s size and growth trajectory. If you’re a small business, you might just need basic features like expense tracking. Whereas, if you’re a large enterprise, look for comprehensive platforms with procurement, invoicing, and compliance capabilities to manage complex operations.


-


**Narrow Down the Most Important Features** : Prioritise features that address your business's unique challenges and objectives. Look for critical features like automated expense tracking, customisable approval workflows, or seamless integration with accounting systems. Mobile accessibility, policy enforcement, and real-time reporting are also crucial.


-


**Think About Ease of Use and Implementation** : Choose an SMS with an intuitive user interface to minimise the learning curve and ensure smooth employee adoption. Prioritise platforms that offer clear onboarding processes and training support. The easier the software is to use, the faster your team will adopt and utilise it effectively.


-


**Consider Pricing and ROI** : Balance the software pricing with its potential return on investment (ROI). Look for a cost-effective pricing model, either subscription-based or per-user, that aligns with your budget. Don’t forget to consider hidden costs like implementation or training fees. Before making your decision, evaluate how the software will save time, reduce errors, improve compliance, and be scalable.


-


**Evaluate Customer Support Experience** : Lastly, ensure the software vendor offers robust customer support during your business hours through different channels like phone, email, and live chat. You can also research vendor reviews on G2, Gartner, Trustpilot, and Capterra to assess their reputation and user experience before making your final decision.
