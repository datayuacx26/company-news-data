# AECOM appoints David Rottblatt to lead strategic growth across private-sector aviation markets

**DALLAS (June 12, 2026)** — AECOM, the trusted global infrastructure leader, today announced its appointment of David Rottblatt as Senior Vice President, Director of Strategic Private sector client growth in the Aviation Market Sector. Based out of California, Mr. Rottblatt will be responsible for expanding AECOM’s presence across private sector aviation clients and emerging aviation markets, while strengthening alignment with core commercial airport clients.


“We are pleased to welcome David to AECOM,” said Dwight Pullen, Senior Vice President, Global Aviation and Surface Transport Leader. “His deep industry experience, strategic perspective, and proven track record of driving growth across the aviation sector make him an outstanding addition to our team. As the aviation industry continues to evolve, David’s leadership will help us strengthen client relationships, expand into new markets, and deliver even greater value for our clients worldwide.”


AECOM continues to grow its aviation business by deepening relationships across the broader aviation ecosystem, including developer-led airport infrastructure partners, private operators, OEMs, logistics providers, and emerging aviation companies. In his new role, Mr. Rottblatt will help strengthen these strategic connections while aligning AECOM’s industry-leading airport delivery, program management, and advisory capabilities with evolving market opportunities.


“I am delighted to be joining AECOM at such a pivotal time for the aviation industry,” said Mr. Rottblatt. “As a connector between private-sector and commercial aviation clients, my goal is to demonstrate a commitment to delivering demonstrated value while highlighting our unique capabilities across the aviation system. I look forward to strengthening relationships, driving growth, and capturing opportunities for collaboration across business lines to better serve our clients and partners.”


Joining AECOM from Supernal, where he served as the Chief Operating Officer, Mr. Rottblatt brings more than two decades of leadership experience and 15 years in the global aviation industry. Throughout his career he has successfully led high-preforming international teams, developed and scaled strategic go-to-market frameworks, and overseen complex aviation frameworks valued at billions of dollars. His deep expertise in operational excellence, business growth, and industry innovation positions him to further strengthen AECOM’s leadership in the aviation sector.


Mr. Rottblatt’s role is effective immediately.
