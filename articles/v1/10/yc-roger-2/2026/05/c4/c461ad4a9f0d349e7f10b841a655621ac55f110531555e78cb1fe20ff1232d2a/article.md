# Attio Pricing in 2026: Plans, Credit Costs, and What You'll Really Pay

Attio pricing starts at **$0 for the Free plan** , then moves to **$29 per user/month billed annually or $36 per user/month billed monthly for Plus** , and **$69 per user/month billed annually or $86 per user/month billed monthly for Pro** . **Enterprise is custom and billed annually.**


That is the quick answer. The bigger budgeting question is what happens after your team needs more seats, more records, more reports, more email volume, or more workspace credits for AI and automation.


This guide breaks down the full public Attio pricing ladder, the monthly-versus-annual math, the credit packs most buyers miss, and when Attio is worth the spend.

2. The headline seat price is only part of the bill. **Workspace credits, record limits, reports, and email limits** can all push a team into a higher tier or an add-on purchase.


## What each Attio plan includes


### Free


Attio's Free plan is meant for individuals or very small teams. The official help content calls it ideal for teams with **up to three seats** .


Free includes:


- 100 seat credits per user each month
- 250 workspace credits per month
- up to 50,000 records
- up to 3 reports
- one synced email and calendar mailbox per user
- 200 emails per user per month
- bulk sending capped at 10 emails at once


Free is generous enough to learn the product, but it is not built for a larger outbound or RevOps workflow.


### Plus


Plus is Attio's first real paid tier. It costs:


- **$36 per user/month** on monthly billing
- **$29 per user/month** on annual billing


Plus raises several practical limits:


- 500 seat credits per user per month
- 1,500 workspace credits per month
- up to 250,000 records
- up to 15 reports
- 1,000 emails per user per month
- bulk sending up to 50 emails at once


This is the tier where Attio starts to feel like a serious team CRM instead of a free test environment.


### Pro


Pro is the step up for teams that are running more serious GTM workflows. It costs:


- **$86 per user/month** on monthly billing
- **$69 per user/month** on annual billing


Public Pro highlights include:


- 1,000 seat credits per user per month
- 10,000 workspace credits per month
- up to 1 million records
- up to 100 reports
- unlimited monthly email sends
- two synced email/calendar mailboxes per user
- more advanced permissions and higher-touch support


If Plus is the collaboration tier, Pro is the scale tier.


### Enterprise


Enterprise is quote-based and billed annually. Publicly, Attio shows:


- 2,500 seat credits per user per month
- custom workspace credits
- custom record limits
- 100+ reports
- unlimited teams
- 3+ accounts per user for synced email/calendar access
- advanced security and admin controls


In other words, Enterprise is not a normal self-serve plan. It is a sales-led package for teams that have already outgrown the public tiers.


## Monthly vs annual billing: how much does Attio save on annual plans?


For most buyers, the monthly-versus-annual choice is the easiest savings lever.


### Plus


- Monthly: **$36/user/month**
- Annual-effective: **$29/user/month**
- Difference: **$7 per user/month**


That means a five-user team pays:


- **$180/month** on monthly billing
- **$145/month effective** on annual billing


Over a year, that is a difference of **$420** .


### Pro


- Monthly: **$86/user/month**
- Annual-effective: **$69/user/month**
- Difference: **$17 per user/month**


A 10-user Pro team pays:


- **$860/month** on monthly billing
- **$690/month effective** on annual billing


That is a difference of **$2,040 per year** before you add credits or any custom services.


If you are confident Attio is your CRM, annual billing is the cleaner cost structure. If you are still uncertain about fit, monthly gives flexibility but at a meaningful markup.


## The hidden cost most buyers miss: credits and plan gates


The reason Attio pricing can feel straightforward at first and more expensive later is that the seat price is only one layer.


### 1. Workspace credits


Attio publicly lists extra workspace-credit packs for paid plans:


Extra workspace credits Monthly billing Annual-effective pricing


+5,000/month $85/month $70/month


+10,000/month $150/month $120/month


+25,000/month $330/month $260/month


+50,000/month $595/month $475/month


If your team leans heavily on AI, automations, enrichment, or workflow-heavy usage, these packs matter. A buyer who looks only at the $29 or $69 seat price can miss how quickly credits change the bill.


### 2. Record limits


Attio also uses record caps as a soft upgrade lever:


- Free: 50k records
- Plus: 250k records
- Pro: 1M records
- Enterprise: custom


For a small GTM team this may be fine. For a larger database, multibrand setup, or heavier enrichment motion, record limits become part of pricing whether you like it or not.


### 3. Email and mailbox limits


The email upgrade path matters too:


- Free: 200 emails/user/month
- Plus: 1,000 emails/user/month
- Pro and Enterprise: unlimited sends


Synced email/calendar account limits also change by plan:


- Free: 1 account/user
- Plus: 1 account/user
- Pro: 2 accounts/user
- Enterprise: 3+ accounts/user


That means some teams will upgrade for workflow reasons before they upgrade for raw CRM reasons.


### 4. Report limits and permissions


The plan jumps are not just about data volume. Reporting and permissions also push buyers upmarket:


- Free: up to 3 reports
- Plus: up to 15 reports
- Pro: up to 100 reports
- Enterprise: 100+ reports


If RevOps, sales leadership, and GTM ops all need cleaner access controls and reporting, Pro can become the practical floor.


## Attio pricing calculator: example budgets


A clean way to think about Attio is to price the team you actually have, not the logo price you see on the pricing page.


### Example 1: 5-person startup team on Plus


Assume 5 users on Plus.


- Annual-effective pricing: 5 x $29 = **$145/month**
- Annual commitment: **$1,740/year**
- Monthly billing alternative: 5 x $36 = **$180/month**


This is the likely starting point for a small team that needs more than the 3-seat Free cap but is not yet pushing into Pro-level scale.


### Example 2: 10-person GTM team on Pro


Assume 10 users on Pro.


- Annual-effective pricing: 10 x $69 = **$690/month**
- Annual commitment: **$8,280/year**
- Monthly billing alternative: 10 x $86 = **$860/month**


That is a meaningful budget jump, but it buys larger record limits, unlimited sends, and better reporting/control.


### Example 3: 10-person GTM team on Pro with extra credits


Take the same 10-user Pro team and add a 10,000-credit workspace pack.


- Pro annual-effective seats: **$690/month**
- Extra 10,000 workspace credits annual-effective: **$120/month**
- New effective monthly spend: **$810/month**
- New annualized spend: **$9,720/year**


That example shows why Attio can move from` reasonable per-seat CRM` to` meaningful GTM software line item` faster than the headline rate implies.


## Does Attio offer discounts?


Yes, but not all discounts apply to every buyer.


### Annual billing discount


The simplest public discount is annual billing:


- Plus drops from **$36** to **$29** per user/month effective
- Pro drops from **$86** to **$69** per user/month effective


### Startup program


Attio also has a public startup program. Qualified startups can get:


- **80% off the annual Pro plan and credits for the first year**
- **30% off the second year**


That is a major discount, but it is not universal self-serve pricing. The official page says startups must be **venture backed or affiliated with one of Attio's startup partners** to qualify.


### Enterprise pricing


Enterprise is still quote-led and billed annually. If your team needs Enterprise, the price conversation moves from self-serve list pricing to a sales process.


## Is Attio worth the price?


For the right team, yes.


Attio is worth it when you want:


- a flexible CRM with a modern data model,
- a system your GTM team can shape to fit its process,
- better workflow and reporting depth than a basic lightweight CRM,
- and enough usage scale to justify paid seats plus credit consumption.


Attio is less compelling if you are mainly hoping the CRM itself will create outbound pipeline.


A CRM can organize work. It can improve workflow. It can help a team move faster. But it still does not replace the work of actually creating qualified conversations.

## FAQ


### How much is Attio per user?


Attio is free for up to 3 seats. Paid pricing starts at **$29/user/month billed annually** or **$36/user/month billed monthly** for Plus. Pro is **$69/user/month billed annually** or **$86/user/month billed monthly** . Enterprise is custom.


### Does Attio have a free plan?


Yes. Attio's Free plan supports **up to 3 seats** and includes baseline CRM, enrichment, and email-sync functionality, but it also comes with tighter limits on records, reports, and email sends.


### What are Attio credits?


Attio uses both **seat credits** and **workspace credits** . They support AI and automation-related usage. Each plan includes a different amount, and Attio also sells public add-on packs for extra workspace credits.


### Does Attio offer discounts?


Yes. The two public discount paths are **annual billing** and Attio's **qualified startup program** . The startup program offers **80% off the annual Pro plan and credits for the first year** and **30% off the second year** for eligible startups.


### Is Attio worth it for startups?


It can be, especially if you qualify for the startup program. If you do not qualify, the better question is whether you need Attio's flexibility badly enough to justify the seat price and future credit usage, or whether a simpler stack is enough for this stage.
