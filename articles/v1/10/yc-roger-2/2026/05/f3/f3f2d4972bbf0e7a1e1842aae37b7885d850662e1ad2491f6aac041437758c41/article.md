# Instantly Pricing in 2026: Plans, Credits, Add-Ons, and Real Cost

If you are researching **Instantly pricing** , the first thing to understand is that Instantly is **not really one subscription** .


The headline price on the pricing page is only the starting point. In practice, buyers are often evaluating some combination of:


- **Outreach** ,
- **Instantly Credits** for lead database and AI actions,
- **CRM** ,
- **Website Visitors** ,
- **Inbox Placement** ,
- **Done-for-you or pre-warmed email infrastructure** ,
- and any third-party mailbox or domain costs they manage outside Instantly.


That is why the best Instantly pricing page cannot stop at` Growth starts at $47/month` and call it a day.

## Why Instantly pricing feels more complicated than the headline price


Instantly's pricing page is not wrong, but it is incomplete if you read it as` this is all I will pay` .


Three things make the pricing feel messier than the sticker price suggests.


### 1. Instantly sells multiple products, not one package


The official help center is clearer than the homepage on this point. It separates pricing into:


- Email Outreach,
- Instantly Credits,
- CRM,
- Website Visitors,
- Inbox Placement,
- DFY services,
- and pre-warmed accounts.


That matters because many buyers end up subscribing to more than one layer. A founder testing cold email may only need Outreach. A scaled outbound team often wants Outreach plus Credits, and sometimes Inbox Placement or CRM.


### 2. Monthly and annual pricing create instant confusion


The live pricing page shows both monthly and discounted annual-effective pricing on Outreach. That is why some third-party pages quote` $47` and others quote` $37.60` for the same Growth plan.


Both numbers can be right. They just refer to different billing choices.


For planning, the safest rule is:


- use the **monthly price** as your default baseline,
- and treat the annual-equivalent figure as the discounted version if you know you are comfortable committing upfront.


### 3. Instantly's own public sources are not perfectly synchronized


This is the strongest information-gain angle in the current SERP.


On the live pricing page, **Hypergrowth** is shown with **100,000 emails monthly** . In the official` Instantly Plans Overview` help article, Hypergrowth is listed with **125,000 emails monthly** .


That does not make Instantly untrustworthy. It does mean buyers should confirm the live contract or checkout terms before locking in budget assumptions, especially at volume.


## How Instantly pricing actually works


To evaluate Instantly properly, you need to understand how the pricing model behaves in the real world.


### It is volume-based, not seat-based


Instantly's public plan structure is organized around sending capacity, uploaded-contact caps, and module selection, not classic per-user SaaS pricing.


That can be a real advantage for teams that want multiple people working inside the same outbound motion without stacking user fees.


But it also means the core budgeting questions are different:


- how much email volume do we need?
- how many contacts do we need active in campaigns?
- how much lead-data research do we need?
- how much supporting infrastructure will we attach?


### Uploaded contacts are fixed caps, not monthly resets


This is an important operational detail from the official help center.


Instantly states that the uploaded-contact limit is a **fixed limit across all campaigns** , not a monthly allowance that refreshes like email volume.


That makes the contact cap more important than many buyers realize. A team can feel fine on monthly send volume and still hit workflow friction because the workspace is holding too many active contacts.


### Credits are a separate product layer


Instantly Credits power several functions, including:


- SuperSearch / lead database actions,
- lead enrichment,
- lead verification,
- Copilot,
- AI reply agent,
- and AI sales agent actions.


That means the` starting at $47` Outreach plan is **not** the same as having a usable Instantly lead-data budget.


If you want Instantly to help source leads, enrich them, verify them, and assist with AI actions, credits become part of the real spend.


### If you only buy credits, campaign usage is constrained


Another helpful detail from the official help article:


if you buy Instantly Credits **without** an Email Outreach plan, your campaign uploads are limited to **250 leads** .


So the lead-data layer is not meant to be a clean substitute for Outreach. It is designed to sit alongside it.


### Annual discounts are clearest on Outreach


The clearest official annual savings show up on Outreach pricing:


- Growth:` $47` monthly vs` $37.60` annual equivalent
- Hypergrowth:` $97` monthly vs` $77.60` annual equivalent
- Light Speed:` $358` monthly vs` $286.30` annual equivalent


That works out to roughly a **20% discount** on the Outreach tier when billed annually.


The rest of the modular stack is less elegantly documented in public, which is another reason the article needs to focus on clarity rather than a fake precision the official sources do not fully support.

- unlimited email accounts,

### Hypergrowth


**Current public price:**` $97/mo` monthly or` $77.60/mo` annual equivalent.


Officially, Hypergrowth includes:


- unlimited email accounts,
- unlimited email warmup,
- 25,000 uploaded contacts,
- and premium live support.


The important caveat is the email-volume discrepancy:


- pricing page:` 100,000 emails monthly`
- help center:` 125,000 emails monthly`


That discrepancy is worth calling out in the article because many third-party explainers either ignore it or quietly choose one number.


### Light Speed


**Current public price:**` $358/mo` monthly or` $286.30/mo` annual equivalent.


Light Speed layers on:


- 500,000 to 500,000+ monthly emails,
- 100,000 to 100,000+ uploaded contacts,
- and the **SISR** system for server and IP sharding / rotation.


This is clearly the high-volume tier.


### Enterprise


**Current public price:** custom.


Enterprise adds a more managed motion with:


- dedicated account management,
- shared Slack support,
- and a private deliverability network.


## Instantly Credits


Credits are the second big pricing layer.


Official help-center pricing currently shows:


- **Nano:**` $9/mo` for 150 credits

If your buyer workflow involves frequent list building, enrichment, or AI-assisted actions, this layer can become unavoidable.

## CRM


Instantly sells CRM separately from Outreach.


Current public pricing:


- **Growth CRM:**` $47/mo`

That makes CRM optional, not bundled. For some buyers that is a positive because they already run HubSpot, Salesforce, or another CRM. For others, it is another line item in the pricing stack.

Website Visitors is also separate.


Current public pricing:


- **Hyper Visitors:**` $97/mo`

Official notes say this focuses on **US-based traffic** and that a credit is only consumed when Instantly resolves a visitor profile with a business email and/or LinkedIn profile.


## Inbox Placement


Inbox Placement is sold separately too:


- **Growth Inbox Placement:**` $47/mo`

## DFY, pre-warmed, and VIP services


Instantly also monetizes infrastructure and managed setup:


### DFY accounts


- ` $15/year` per domain
- ` $5/mo` per email account


Instantly handles registration, email creation, and DNS setup, but official docs note that these accounts still need an Outreach plan and require a` 2+` week warmup period before campaigns launch.


### Pre-warmed accounts


- ` $15/year` per domain

These are priced higher because they are already warmed and optimized for faster deployment.


### VIP


VIP is custom-priced and clearly positioned as a setup / managed-support tier rather than a simple software plan.

## FAQ


### How much does Instantly cost per month?


The core Outreach product currently starts at` $47/mo` on monthly billing or` $37.60/mo` effective monthly on annual billing. But total spend can rise materially once you add Credits, CRM, Website Visitors, Inbox Placement, or Instantly-managed email infrastructure.


### Does Instantly charge per seat?


Its public pricing is primarily volume- and module-based rather than classic per-seat pricing. The bigger budget levers are send volume, uploaded-contact limits, and the add-on products you choose.


### What is included in the Growth plan?


Officially, Growth Outreach includes unlimited email accounts, unlimited email warmup, 1,000 uploaded contacts, 5,000 monthly emails, and chat support.


### Does Instantly include lead data?


Not by default in the Outreach sticker price. Instantly's lead database and related actions are powered through the separate **Instantly Credits** layer.


### Is Instantly cheaper than hiring SDRs or using Roger?


That depends on what you are buying. If you only need a sending platform and your team wants to operate outbound directly, Instantly may be economical. If you are stacking multiple modules and still carrying heavy manual SDR work, Roger may be the cleaner economic comparison because it is built around AI SDR execution rather than modular software ownership.
