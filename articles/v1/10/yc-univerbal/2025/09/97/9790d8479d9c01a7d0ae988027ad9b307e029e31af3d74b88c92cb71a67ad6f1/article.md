# Quazel is now Univerbal 🎙️🌐 The Universal AI Language Tutor

### From German pun to a name everyone can spell


After becoming part of Y Combinator’s W23 cohort, we set out to build the best AI language tutor app -[loved by tens of thousands of users](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=32993130) . But there was one big problem: Quazel (a play on the German “quasseln,” meaning “to chat”) was nearly impossible for non-German speakers to spell, search for, or remember.


*From left to right: Sam, David and Philipp (with the sign) at YC*


With a global vision for accessible, interactive language learning, we chose a name that reflects our universal mission: **Univerbal** .
