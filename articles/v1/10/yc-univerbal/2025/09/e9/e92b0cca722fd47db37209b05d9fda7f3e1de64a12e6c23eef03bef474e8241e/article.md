## Why We Started Quazel (Now Univerbal): The Mission Behind the App


At **Quazel** (now Univerbal), our goal has always been clear: **to make language learning as immersive and supportive as possible - as if you had your own caring tutor by your side, anytime, anywhere** .[With the power of AI](https://blog.univerbal.app/ai-language-tutor) , we’re making that vision real for language learners everywhere.


## How Khosla Ventures is helping us reach more learners


We’re proud to announce the successful completion of our Seed funding round, raising **$1.5 million** with the support of[Khosla Ventures](https://www.khoslaventures.com/) . Their partnership will help us:


-


Accelerate development of our personalized AI language tutor


-


Bring accessible, individualized language practice to the world


-


Improve and scale our conversational learning technology


Together, we’re on track to offer custom conversation and real-time support to every learner, everywhere.


## 50,000 users in just two days


Even before our official launch, the demand was overwhelming:[50,000 people](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=32993130)[tried the app](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=32993130) and shared their feedback within just two days!


We’re so thankful for our early adopters and the vibrant community that’s helping us build an even better experience.
