# Fractional Ownership vs. Full Ownership: The Smart Way to Own a Private Airplane

Owning a private airplane is the ultimate luxury, offering unparalleled freedom, flexibility, and convenience for travel. However, the costs associated with full ownership can be substantial, making it a significant financial commitment. That's where fractional ownership comes in, providing a more affordable and practical alternative for those seeking the benefits of private aviation without the hefty price tag.


‍


At Airhart, we're excited to introduce our innovative 4-seater, semi-autonomous airplane, the Airhart Sling, which is revolutionizing the way people approach private aircraft ownership. With a purchase price of just $500k, the Airhart Sling is not only more accessible than many other private planes but also offers unique advantages that make fractional ownership an even smarter choice.


‍


One of the most compelling reasons to consider fractional ownership of the Airhart Sling is the opportunity to share the costs and responsibilities with a group of friends or like-minded individuals. By splitting the purchase price, maintenance expenses, and other associated costs, you can significantly reduce your individual financial burden while still enjoying all the perks of private plane ownership.


‍


But the benefits of fractional ownership with the Airhart Sling go beyond just cost savings. Our cutting-edge technology and intuitive controls make flying easier and more accessible than ever before, even for those without extensive piloting experience. This means that you and your co-owners can learn to fly the plane together, strengthening your bonds and creating unforgettable shared experiences.


‍


In fact, the Airhart Sling's advanced features can help you and your friends achieve your private pilot licenses (PPL) in record time. Traditionally, obtaining a PPL requires around 80 hours of flight time, which can be a significant time and financial investment. However, with the Airhart Sling's semi-autonomous capabilities and simplified controls, you may be able to cut that flight time in half, potentially completing your training in as little as 40 hours.


‍


The Airhart Sling's "point and fly" control system, Airhart Assist, provides robust guidance and protection, with envelope/stall prevention and fault-tolerant redundancies seamlessly integrated. This advanced technology not only enhances safety but also streamlines the learning process, allowing you and your co-owners to master the skills of flying more efficiently.


‍


Fractional ownership of the Airhart Sling also offers greater flexibility compared to full ownership. You and your co-owners can coordinate schedules and share the airplane's usage, ensuring that everyone has access to the plane when they need it. This can be especially beneficial for those who may not fly frequently enough to justify the costs and responsibilities of full ownership.


‍


Furthermore, fractional ownership allows you to avoid the hassles and expenses of aircraft storage, maintenance, and upkeep. These tasks can be shared among the co-owners or delegated to a management company, freeing up your time and energy to focus on enjoying the flying experience.


‍


In conclusion, fractional ownership of the Airhart Sling presents a smart, cost-effective, and rewarding way to own a private airplane. By sharing the costs and responsibilities with friends, accelerating your path to a PPL, and enjoying greater flexibility, you can experience the joys of private aviation without the burdens of full ownership. If you're ready to take your travel experiences to new heights, consider fractional ownership of the Airhart Sling – it might just be the smartest decision you'll make.
