### Gaming and Leisure Properties Releases New Investor Presentation


[PDF Version](https://investors.glpropinc.com/node/13831/pdf)


WYOMISSING, Pa., March 06, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Gaming and Leisure Properties, Inc. (NASDAQ: GLPI) (“GLPI”) announced today that it has posted a new investor presentation to its corporate website. The presentation provides additional detail for investors on items of significance to Gaming & Leisure Properties’ success and prospects.


The presentation can be accessed through the “Presentations” section under “Investor Relations” on[GLPI’s website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=aOrQLrVlJ4hqbJvBqBx7gy3fltL3XJrRGMNZ58EQan3H8qDoIBNeM6PjTf0b60yXOeFq_naP2EKZqoL4NggP_e93MbMLCZvdIYUb7YUeNsiuOhN3gbQTfDKcWVOrSyEorFZeW5SJ5T5LZjzagfiYHQ==) .


**About** **Gaming** **and** **Leisure** **Properties** GLPI is engaged in the business of acquiring, financing, and owning real estate property to be leased to gaming operators in triple-net lease arrangements, pursuant to which the tenant is responsible for all facility maintenance, insurance required in connection with the leased properties and the business conducted on the leased properties, taxes levied on or with respect to the leased properties and all utilities and other services necessary or appropriate for the leased properties and the business conducted on the leased properties.
