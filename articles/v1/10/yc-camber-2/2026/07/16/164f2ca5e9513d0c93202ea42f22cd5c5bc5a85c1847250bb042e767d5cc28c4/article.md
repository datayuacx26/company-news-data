The Announcement


## Four Years in the Making


[Today](https://www.axios.com/pro/health-tech-deals/2025/02/26/camber-raises-30m-medical-claims-automation-billing-rcm) (Feb 26, 2025) marks a pivotal moment as we — after four years of working in relative stealth — publicly announce the launch of Camber, and the $50M in funding we've raised from phenomenal supporters at a16z, Craft Ventures, YCombinator, Manresa, and many others.


For most of us, this journey started a lot longer than four years ago. In many ways, we've all struggled with the deeply painful experiences of getting the care we needed paid for at some time or another. We felt there had to be a better way.


Over the past four years, we've been able to build that better way.

What We've Built


## The Hard Parts No One Sees


Building this has not been an easy journey, and we expect the difficulty to only continue. In the process, we've built:


> Thousands of rulesets to understand how payers will treat our claims, relying on millions of past sessions.


> Dozens and dozens of integrations with EHRs, clearinghouses, LLM services, third party healthcare services, and many others.


> Data models that encapsulate every possible edge case in healthcare — believe it or not, this has been the hardest part of the journey.


The capital we're announcing today will allow us to better serve our existing providers, and continue expanding into new verticals. We're just getting started.


### Why the name Camber?


Camber is a physics term that refers to a few different types of angles — like the tilt of a wheel, or the curve of an airfoil. Just like camber provides stability and efficiency to a car on the road or lift to an aircraft wing, we're here to help clinics navigate change, build strong foundations, and maximize their potential.
