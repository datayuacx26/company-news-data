The Announcement


## A Formal Partnership Built on Proven Results


**NEW YORK, NY — October 15, 2025** —[Flychain](https://www.flychain.us/) , a tech-first accounting and financial intelligence platform designed specifically for healthcare, and[Camber](https://www.camber.health/) , the automated and intelligent revenue cycle management (RCM) platform built for healthcare providers, today announced a formal strategic partnership.


Together, Flychain and Camber are helping Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) providers build lasting financial resilience. Flychain complements that by delivering healthcare-focused bookkeeping, access to working capital, tax support, and CFO-level insights. Camber ensures providers collect more of their contracted reimbursements, faster, through accurate, automated billing workflows — powering over 20% of all ABA insurance claims in the U.S. This combination brings tools once reserved for national providers to local clinics across the country.


The partnership has already proven to be impactful. Clinics supported by Flychain and Camber have navigated major industry disruptions — including TRICARE transitions and the Change Healthcare outage — without missing payroll or stalling operations. By connecting revenue and expenses in a single view, providers gain new visibility into margins, labor costs, and payer performance, enabling smarter, forward-looking financial decisions.

## About the Companies

Flychain's all-in-one financial platform combines Accounting and Bookkeeping, Working Capital, Taxes, and CFO Intelligence.
