# Submit to the Bits to Atoms Software Database


## Register to submit and keep your software information up to date


Bits to Atoms is in the process of creating an extensive guide to the current landscape of design, optimization, and manufacturing software applicable to additive, and advanced manufacturing.


The complexity and lack of transparency in understanding the functionalities and interconnectivity of these software tools have become significant challenges.


This initiative is designed to clarify the software ecosystem, providing a valuable resource for individuals and organizations to navigate the technological environment effectively. It aims to facilitate the process of making knowledgeable choices about software utilization, acquisition, and development.


In the rapidly evolving field of additive manufacturing, the visibility and accessibility of innovative software solutions are crucial for progress and integration.


Bits to Atoms invites developers and companies to **register their software on our platform** , ensuring that your cutting-edge solutions are discoverable to a wide audience of professionals seeking to advance their capabilities in design, optimization, and manufacturing.


By contributing to our comprehensive map, you not only gain visibility but also contribute to a collaborative ecosystem where transparency and accessibility foster technological advancement and operational efficiency.


Maintaining your software’s information up to date on our platform is equally important.


Regular updates ensure that potential users are aware of the latest functionalities, improvements, and the unique value your software brings to the additive manufacturing landscape.


This ongoing engagement not only enhances your software’s visibility but also supports the community in making informed decisions. We encourage you to be part of this essential resource, helping to streamline the search for software solutions and thereby accelerating the adoption and innovation of additive manufacturing technologies.
