# AM Software Pricing Survey

## Subscribe for Updates


The cost of additive manufacturing software for design, print preparation and simulation can sometimes be difficult to find out.


While some software has clear, transparent pricing that can be easily found online, other vendors often make potential customers speak to a sales person, and request information they may not be ready to give, just to get a ballpark figure as to whether the software solution may be a viable option.


We would be interested in getting pricing quotes so that we can then provide these ‘ball park figures’ back to interested parties.


Please fill out the form if you are interested in contributing, all responses are **100% anonymous** , if you are interested in seeing the results, you can subscribe by adding your email, or separately to be informed of updates once they are published.
