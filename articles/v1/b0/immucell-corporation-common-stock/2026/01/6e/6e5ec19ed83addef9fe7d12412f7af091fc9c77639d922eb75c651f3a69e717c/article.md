## Maternity barn: setting the stage for healthy beginnings


Prioritizing herd health starts in the maternity pen because immediately after a calf is born, every decision matters to its future productivity. We explored five ways for modernizing the maternity pen to gain benefits for your calves and dairy operation.


**Cleanliness and comfort**
Ask yourself how high or low your facility ranks in terms of how easy it is to keep clean. Easy is best. A clean pen can prevent injuries, reduce stress, lower disease incidence and more.


**Biosecurity**
Make sure you take stringent biosecurity measures. You will gain the upper hand disease prevention, productivity and food safety.


**Colostrum**
Create a dedicated areas for colostrum collection and storage, and design the pen with timely colostrum administration in mind.


**Overall efficiency**
Keep your team in mind, too. Provide easy access to calving tools and visibility to your standard operating procedures (SOPs). Plan for an overall efficient layout and design.


**Visibility**
Take advantage of monitoring technologies to track calving progress, for example. Use them to track each animal’s health, too.
