## Securing the right number of replacements: Every heifer matters


Your management expertise as it relates to beef-on-dairy calves matters more than ever. Trading heifer replacements for beef-cross calves while breeding back through an investment in genetics is a challenge.


Sure, you can get a high price for beef-cross calves today. But the replacement heifer supply is tight. You want to avoid being left milking your lower producers or unnecessarily buying expensive replacements.


This article explores strategies for you to consider for the best possible outcomes when making your beef-on-dairy calf decisions.
