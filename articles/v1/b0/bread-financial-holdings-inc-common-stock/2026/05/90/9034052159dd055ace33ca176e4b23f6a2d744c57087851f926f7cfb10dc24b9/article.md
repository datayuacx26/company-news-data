# Quicktake: Gen Z not giving up on summer travel plans


### *Younger travelers are more likely to spend more, fly to destination*


Gen Z travelers aren’t letting high inflation and fuel costs deflate their travel plans. This generation is going all-in on summer travel, with **54%**


of Gen Z summer travelers planning a longer trip than last year’s vacation and **60%**


saying they plan to spend more this year. A majority ( **51%**


) will be flying to their destination and **20%**


of them describe themselves as “looking for luxe,” willing to spend on all of the best experiences.


---


Gen Z intends to spend more


**Six in 10**


Gen Z summer travelers plan to spend more on this year’s trip compared to last year, higher than the average of **45%**


. Only **8%**


of Gen Z plans to spend less this year, compared to the average at **13%**


.


**Gen Z more likely to plan to spend more**


Image


Most will shop to prepare for their trip


Nearly all ( **95%**


) of Gen Z travelers plan to shop to prepare for their trips. Apparel tops their shopping list ( **63%**


), statistically flat to the average. Gen Z leads in plans to purchase jewelry ( **31%**


), sports equipment ( **24%**


), and books/activities ( **24%**


).


**Gen Z’s pre-trip shopping list includes apparel, jewelry**


Image


Financing will play an important role


Gen Z summer travelers appear to be savvy when it comes to paying for their travel expenses. While **56%**


will use debit and **45%**


will use credit cards, Gen Z leads in plans to use buy now, pay later/pay over time ( **18%**


), gift cards ( **16%**


), and cryptocurrency ( **12%**


)


**Gen Z leads in plans to use many different payment types**


Image


Key takeaway: Gen Z travelers are not shortchanging their experiences


Despite the macroeconomic headwinds, Gen Z is ready to splurge on a vacation – including plans to do some pre-trip shopping. Brands looking to engage with Gen Z summer travelers should appeal to the aspirational nature of vacationing, highlighting feel-good moments worthy of sharing with family and friends.


*Source: Bread Financial proprietary study, Summer Travel Plans, April 2026.*
