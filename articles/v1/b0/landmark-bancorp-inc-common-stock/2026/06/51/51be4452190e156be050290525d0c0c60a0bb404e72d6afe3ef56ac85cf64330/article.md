# Why Scammers Ask for Crypto or Gift Cards and What You Should Do Instead


One of the biggest red flags in modern scams is a request to pay with cryptocurrency or gift cards. If someone asks you to buy gift cards, send Bitcoin, or transfer money through a crypto wallet, it’s almost always a scam.


At Landmark National Bank, we want you to feel empowered to pause, question, and verify before sending funds. Understanding why scammers ask for gift cards and why crypto scam payment requests are so common can help you protect your money and avoid irreversible financial loss.


## Why Scammers Prefer Crypto and Gift Cards


Scammers choose these payment methods for a reason. Crypto and gift cards are fast, hard to trace, and extremely difficult to reverse.


### 1. Transactions Are Nearly Instant


Once a gift card code is shared or cryptocurrency is sent, the scammer can drain the funds within moments.


### 2. Payments Are Hard to Track


Most cryptocurrency fraud involves anonymous wallets that make it difficult for law enforcement to identify the scammer.


### 3. Funds Are Almost Impossible to Recover


Unlike bank transactions, gift card and crypto payments offer little to no consumer protection.


### 4. They Bypass Traditional Banking Safeguards


Scammers use these methods because they avoid the security checks that banks put in place to protect customers.


## Common Scenarios Where Scammers Use Gift Cards or Crypto


Understanding the situations where these scams appear can help you spot gift card scam warning signs early.


### IRS or Government Impersonation


A caller claims you owe taxes or fines and must pay immediately using gift cards or Bitcoin.


### Tech Support Scams


Someone pretending to be from a major tech company says your computer is infected and demands payment in crypto.


### Romance Scams


A new online connection asks for financial help and insists on cryptocurrency because it’s “easier” or “private.”


### Sweepstakes or Prize Scams


You’re told you’ve won money with the caveat that you must pay fees or taxes using gift cards.


### Emergency Scams


A scammer impersonates a family member and urgently requests gift cards to handle an “emergency.”


## Major Red Flags to Watch For


These are the clearest Bitcoin scam red flags and gift card scam warning signs:


- Urgent or threatening messages.
- Requests to buy gift cards and read the numbers aloud.
- Instructions to send Bitcoin or other cryptocurrency.
- Being told not to tell anyone.
- Pressure to act immediately.


Legitimate businesses and government agencies never request payment this way.


## What You Should Do Instead (How to Avoid Gift Card Scams)


If someone asks for crypto or gift cards, here’s how to protect yourself:


- Stop communication immediately.
- Do not buy gift cards or send cryptocurrency.
- Contact your bank for guidance.
- Verify the claim using official contact information.
- Report the scam attempt to the FTC or local authorities.


Taking a moment to verify can prevent irreversible loss.
