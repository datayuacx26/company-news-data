# Customer Champions Series Elevator: Building Space for Businesses to Grow


In 2021, like many entrepreneurs, the founders of Elevator weren’t just building a business, they were building it out of their home.


What started as an e-commerce pet supply company quickly outgrew its space. First, the basement, then the garage, and eventually the living room became part of the operation. It was a familiar startup story, fueled by ambition, but constrained by logistics.


For co-founders Emiliano and Shannon, that experience didn’t just highlight a challenge. It sparked an idea.


## A Problem Worth Solving


As their business grew, so did the need for something more sustainable: space to hire employees, streamline shipping and receiving, and separate work from home life. But when they looked at traditional commercial real estate options, they found a gap.


Long-term leases. Limited flexibility. Few solutions tailored to small, growing businesses.


So, they built what they couldn’t find.


Elevator was created to give entrepreneurs a place to grow, without the rigid commitments that often hold them back.


## More Than Just Space


At its core, Elevator offers flexible commercial workspace designed for businesses that need room to operate, ship, and scale. One of its most unique features? Month-to-month memberships which is an uncommon model in the commercial real estate world.


This flexibility allows business owners to adapt as they grow, whether they need space for a few months or are scaling rapidly.


But what truly sets Elevator apart isn’t just the physical space.


It’s the community.


Members gain access not only to workspace and logistics support, but also to a network of fellow entrepreneurs navigating similar challenges. It’s a place where connections are made, ideas are shared, and businesses evolve together.


As Emiliano put it, “People come for the space, but they stay for the community.”


## Growing with Purpose


Since opening their first location in 2022, Elevator has experienced rapid growth, expanding to multiple locations and building a team of more than 15 people along the way.


That growth hasn’t come without challenges. Like many entrepreneurs, Emiliano and Shannon are constantly focused on what’s next, the next milestone, the next opportunity, the next problem to solve.


But what stands out most is their passion.


In every conversation, it’s clear that Elevator isn’t just a business, it’s something they deeply believe in. Their mission goes beyond providing space; it’s about helping other entrepreneurs build something meaningful of their own.


And as their team continues to grow, so does their impact.


## The Role of the Right Partner


“As we started growing and opening new locations, having a banking partner that understood what that kind of growth actually looks like made a big difference. It’s not always linear, and it helps to have someone who can adapt with you.” -Emiliano and Shannon


As Elevator expanded from its first location to multiple sites in a short period of time, that kind of adaptability became essential.


That’s where Landmark National Bank has played a supporting role, providing the kind of steady, flexible partnership that growing businesses rely on behind the scenes.


Not as the center of the story, but as a partner helping make the next phase possible.


## Elevating What’s Next


Entrepreneurship is rarely a straight line. It’s a series of challenges, pivots, and progress, often happening all at once.


Elevator was built from that reality.


Today, it stands as a place where businesses can take their next step with confidence, whether that means moving out of a living room, hiring a first employee, or scaling to meet new demand.


Because sometimes, the right space and the right community can change everything.
