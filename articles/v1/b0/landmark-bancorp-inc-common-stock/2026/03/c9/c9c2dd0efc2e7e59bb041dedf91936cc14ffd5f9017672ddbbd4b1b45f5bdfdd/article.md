# First‑Time Homebuyer Help: Understanding Down Payment Assistance and Other Financial Support Options


Buying your first home is an exciting milestone, but it can also feel overwhelming, especially if you’re unsure how much you need to save or what support options are available. Many first


‑


time buyers assume they need a large down payment, perfect credit, or years of savings before they can even start the process.


In reality, there are


first-time homebuyer programs


, down payment assistance programs, and other resources designed to make homeownership more accessible.


Landmark National Bank works with buyers every day who are surprised to learn just how many options they have at their disposal. Whether you’re renting, building your savings, or simply exploring what’s possible, understanding these programs can help you take confident steps toward owning a home.


## Who Qualifies as a First‑Time Homebuyer?


Many people are considered “first


‑


time homebuyers” even if they’ve owned a home before. Most


[first-time homebuyer programs](https://www.banklandmark.com/home-loans/first-time-home-buyer-programs/) define a first


‑


time buyer as someone who:


- Has not owned a home in the past three years.


- Is a single parent who’s owned a home recently, but only with an ex-spouse.


- Owned a manufactured home that wasn’t permanently attached to land.


This broader definition means more people qualify for


first-time homebuyer programs


than they realize.


## Why Down Payment Assistance Matters


Saving for a down payment is one of the biggest barriers to homeownership. That’s why down payment assistance programs exist, as they can dramatically reduce upfront costs so buyers can get into a home. These programs may offer:


- Grants that do not need to be repaid.


- Forgivable loans that disappear after a set number of years.


- Low


‑


interest or zero


‑


interest loans for down payment or closing costs.


Many buyers combine these programs with first-time homebuyer loans such as


[FHA](https://www.banklandmark.com/home-loans/fha-loans/) ,


[VA](https://www.banklandmark.com/home-loans/veterans-affairs-home-loans/) , or


[USDA](https://www.banklandmark.com/home-loans/usda-loans/) mortgages to make homeownership even more affordable.


## Types of Homebuyer Assistance Programs


Homebuyers can choose from several programs designed to reduce upfront costs and make the path to homeownership more affordable.


### 1. Government‑Backed Loan Programs


These loans are designed to support buyers who may not have a large down payment or perfect credit.


- **FHA loans:** Require as little as 3.5% down and offer flexible credit requirements.


- **VA loans:** Available to eligible veterans and active


‑


duty service members; often require no down payment.


- **USDA loans:** Designed for rural and some suburban areas; may also offer zero


‑


down options.


These first-time homebuyer loans can be paired with local or state homebuyer assistance programs for even more support.


### 2. Kansas Down Payment Assistance and Homebuyer Programs


Kansas offers several strong options for buyers who need help with upfront costs:


- **KHRC** **First-Time Homebuyer Program** **:** Provides 15 to 20% of the home’s purchase price as a second loan that may be forgiven over time for income


‑


qualified buyers.


- **Kansas Housing Assistance Program:** Offers a 30


‑


year fixed


‑


rate mortgage plus down payment assistance grants that may cover up to 5% of the purchase price.


- **Kansas First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account:** Allows individuals or couples to save for a home with tax


‑


deductible contributions.


- **Federal Home Loan Bank Homeownership Set** **‑** **Aside Program:** Offers $2,500 to $15,000 for down payments, closing costs, or repairs for qualifying buyers.


### 3. Missouri Down Payment Assistance & Homebuyer Programs


Missouri also provides several homebuyer assistance programs to help first


‑


time and repeat buyers:


- **MHDC First Place Program:** Offers below


‑


market mortgage rates and optional cash assistance for down payment and closing costs.


- **MHDC Next Step Program:** Provides assistance for buyers with higher income limits, including help with down payment and closing costs.


- **MHDC Down Payment Assistance:** Offers up to about 4% of the mortgage amount as a forgivable second loan.


- **Local City Programs:** Cities like Columbia and Springfield offer additional forgivable loans for qualifying buyers who need help with making a down payment or covering closing costs.


### Grants vs. Loans vs. Forgivable Loans


Understanding the differences can help you choose the right option:


- **Grants:** Free money that never needs to be repaid.


- **Loans:** Funds that must be repaid, sometimes at low or zero interest.


- **Forgivable loans:** Loans that are erased after you live in the home for a set number of years.


Many


first-time homebuyer programs


use forgivable loans to encourage long


‑


term homeownership.


## Common Misconceptions About Buying Your First Home


Many first


‑


time buyers delay homeownership because of misunderstandings. Here are a few myths worth clearing up:


**The belief:** “I need 20% down.”


**The facts:** Many buyers put down far less, especially with down payment assistance programs. Even without those programs, many loan programs require 5% or less with private mortgage or government-backed insurance.


**The belief:** “I need perfect credit.”


**The facts:** FHA and other first-time homebuyer loans are designed for buyers with a range of credit scores.


**The belief:** “I should wait until I have everything figured out.”


**The facts:** Talking with a lender early can help you understand your options and create a realistic plan for


money saving tips for families.


**The belief:** “Renting is always cheaper.”


**The facts:** With rising rents, owning a home can sometimes be more affordable, especially with help for first-time homebuyers.


## Money-Saving Tips for Families Preparing to Buy


Families planning for homeownership can start building financial confidence with simple steps:


- Open a


[personal bank account](https://www.banklandmark.com/personal/personal-savings-and-investment-accounts/) dedicated to home savings.


- Automate monthly transfers to build your down payment steadily.


- Track spending to identify areas where you can save.


- Use tax


‑


advantaged tools like Kansas’s First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account.


- Build an emergency fund to support long


‑


term financial stability.


These


money saving tips for families


can make the homebuying process feel more manageable.
