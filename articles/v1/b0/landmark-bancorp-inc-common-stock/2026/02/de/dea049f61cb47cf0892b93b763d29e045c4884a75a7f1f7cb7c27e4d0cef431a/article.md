# Building LINK: Babette Macy on Vision, Resilience, and the Power of Partnerships

Babette Macy on Vision, Resilience, and the Power of Partnerships


When Babette Macy launched LINK, it wasn’t just another business venture—it was the culmination of 25 years of experience in the built environment and a passion for connecting people and ideas. After decades working alongside developers, constructors, and owners, Babette knew the Kansas City market inside and out. She had the vision, the expertise, and the drive to create a platform that would share insights and foster collaboration in an industry she loved.


But even with all that knowledge, starting a business came with challenges. “Launching LINK was exhilarating, but it was also overwhelming,” Babette recalls. “You’re juggling ideas, clients, and deadlines while navigating financial decisions that can make or break your future.”


## The Entrepreneurial Reality


Babette’s story is one many entrepreneurs can relate to: the excitement of building something new paired with the uncertainty of managing resources and planning for growth. For her, one of the biggest lessons was understanding the importance of relationships—not just with clients and collaborators, but with partners who could help her navigate the complexities of business ownership.


“Cash flow is king, but banking is about more than deposits and withdrawals,” she says. “It’s about planning for growth, securing funding, and having someone in your corner when the unexpected happens.”


## Finding the Right Support


As Babette mapped out her vision for LINK, she realized she needed a financial partner who understood her goals and could provide guidance through every stage of growth. That’s when she connected with Landmark National Bank. For Babette, this wasn’t about convenience or rates—it was about trust and shared commitment.


“They listened,” she says. “They helped me outline a financial plan and secure the loans I needed to launch LINK. As my business grew, they were there through every milestone and every storm, including the uncertainty of the COVID-19 pandemic.”


## Lessons for Other Entrepreneurs


Babette believes that finding the right partners—whether in banking, marketing, or operations—is one of the most important steps for any entrepreneur. “The right partner can set you up for financial success, calm the storm of constantly changing regulations, and help you make connections to move your business forward,” she explains.


Her advice to others starting out? Don’t underestimate the power of relationships. “Choose partners who believe in your vision as much as you do. That kind of support is priceless.”


## Looking Ahead


Today, LINK continues to grow, fueled by Babette’s expertise and her commitment to building a stronger, more connected industry. Her journey is a reminder that entrepreneurship isn’t a solo endeavor—it’s a collaboration. And when you have the right people in your corner, the possibilities are endless.
