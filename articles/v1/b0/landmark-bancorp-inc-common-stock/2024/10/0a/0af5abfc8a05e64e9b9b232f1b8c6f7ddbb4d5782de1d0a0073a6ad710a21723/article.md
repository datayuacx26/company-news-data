# How Lifestyle Inflation Can Derail Your Financial Goals


When you get a pay raise, it’s tempting to spend more on your lifestyle. Before you spend money on new gadgets, luxury trips, or a bigger home, however, consider how lifestyle inflation could hurt your long-term financial goals. Spending wisely can still help you reach your goals and enjoy what matters.


## What Is Lifestyle Inflation?


[Lifestyle inflation](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/l/lifestyle-inflation.asp) occurs when people start spending more as they earn more, making saving for the future harder. It’s also called lifestyle creep because your spending creeps upward as you eat out more, upgrade electronics, or move to a bigger house or apartment. Even though it feels good to enjoy the rewards of your hard work, spending more without being careful can mess up your budget.


## The Hidden Cost of Lifestyle Inflation


The desire to justify spending money is understandable, especially after a big raise or bonus. However, lifestyle inflation can hurt your future financial plans, not just your current situation. It’s even common to have


[more debt than you did before your pay raise](https://www.businessinsider.com/personal-finance/banking/lifestyle-creep#:~:text=Lifestyle%20creep%2C%20or%20lifestyle%20inflation,debt%20than%20before%20your%20raise.) if you don’t check lifestyle creep in check. Over time, the more you spend now, the less money you’ll have to handle rising costs or emergencies.


## How To Combat Lifestyle Inflation


Want to keep your financial plans on track? Here are a few tips for combatting lifestyle inflation after your salary increases:


1. [Set clear financial goals](https://www.banklandmark.com/blog/common-financial-goals-anyone-can-set/) . Set short-term and long-term financial goals, like saving for a house or investing for retirement. Knowing what you’re working toward can help you stay motivated to save.


2. **Adjust your budget strategically** . Set aside part of your income for savings and investments. For helpful tips, check out our guide on


[managing your budget](https://www.banklandmark.com/blog/simple-steps-to-reclaim-control-of-your-budget/) .


3. [Practice mindful spending](https://www.calm.com/blog/mindful-spending) . What you buy must be worth it and bring lasting value, not just a quick thrill. Also, check that your spending fits with your main financial goals.


4. **Remember the power of investing** . Instead of funneling all extra income into spending, put some toward investments that will grow over time. This approach guards your future and shields your finances from inflation’s impact.


5. **Seek professional advice** . A financial advisor can help make a plan that works for you and guide you through each step.
