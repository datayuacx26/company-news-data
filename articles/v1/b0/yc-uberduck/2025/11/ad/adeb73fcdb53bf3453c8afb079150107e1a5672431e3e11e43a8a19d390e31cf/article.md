# AI Singing Generator Guide: Compose Songs with updated AI song generator


## What is an AI singing generator?


An AI singing generator (sometimes called an AI song generator) is a creative tool that turns text prompts into complete music. Instead of juggling DAWs, sample packs, and studio time, you can describe the mood you want—“dreamy synth pop duet with sparkling arps”—and Model v3 does the heavy lifting: chords, instrumentation, even background vocals. Uberduck pairs that composition engine with voice cloning, so you can layer custom vocals on top of the instrumental the moment it renders.


## How to create a song with AI song generator


You can launch straight into AI song generator from our[music generation page](https://www.uberduck.ai/music-generation) or open[https://www.uberduck.ai/app/song](https://www.uberduck.ai/app/song) with the v3 model selected. From there:


1. Describe the instrumentation in the main prompt. Call out BPM, genre references, and arrangement (“intro build, drop, bridge”).
2. Toggle “include vocals” if you want AI song generator to generate its own topline or keep it instrumental for a clean backing track.
3. Add structured sections. Each section lets you specify duration, energy (“half-time drums, filtered pads”), and optional lyrics.
4. Press “Generate Song.” The render takes ~45 seconds and appears in the global audio player. Renders are saved in your history for easy re-download.


## Prompt tips


Strong prompts make AI song generator feel like an in-house producer. A few favorites:


- **Lyric anchors.** Drop chorus lyrics in the “Hook” section so the generated vocals sit on rhythm. For verses, you can feed full paragraphs or leave AI song generator space to improvise.
- **Reference songs.** It’s OK to mention broad vibes (“like Dua Lipa’s Future Nostalgia”) as long as you don’t ask the AI to copy melodies verbatim.
- **Song length.** Specify 30–120 seconds. Short loops are great for TikTok; longer renders are perfect for demos and background beds.


## How vocals & lyrics work


You have full control over whether your track includes AI-generated vocals, and how much of the lyrics are specified by you versus invented by the model.


- **Toggle vocals:** When creating a new song, use the “include vocals” toggle to decide if your track should have singing. Turning it off gives you a fully instrumental backing.
- **Supplying lyrics:** Enter any lyrics you want sung in a section’s prompt. You can paste an entire verse or just add a catchphrase for the chorus. If you leave the lyric field blank, the AI will invent its own lines matching your topic and vibe.
- **AI lyric style:** The model generates lyrics that are rhythmic and natural-sounding. Specify a language or mood if you want, or let the AI be creative.
- **Multi-section storytelling:** Use the “sections” editor to craft song structures—give each verse, hook, or bridge its own lyric and mood for more intricate storytelling.


You can mix and match: write your own chorus, let the AI handle the rest, or feed full lyrics for total control. The choice is yours!
