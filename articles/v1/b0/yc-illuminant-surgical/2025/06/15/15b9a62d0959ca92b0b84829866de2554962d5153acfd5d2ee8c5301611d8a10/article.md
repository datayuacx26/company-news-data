# Illuminant Surgical Exhibits at SIGGRAPH 2025

## Illuminant Surgical Exhibits at SIGGRAPH 2025


By[Illuminant Surgical](https://www.illuminant.ai/author/admin_illuminant)


|


June 20, 2025


|


1 min read


Illuminant Surgical is participating at the SIGGRAPH 2025 Posters Program in Vancouver, Canada.


The software engineering team is showcasing their work on “Skylight: Real-Time Projection Mapping for Surgical Navigation”. It introduces Skylight, a non-invasive projection mapping system that uses skin-adhered fiducials and real-time surface registration to guide surgical navigation with high precision.


We’re excited to be part of SIGGRAPH 2025 and to join the conversation with leaders advancing the future of XR, graphics, and surgical technology.
