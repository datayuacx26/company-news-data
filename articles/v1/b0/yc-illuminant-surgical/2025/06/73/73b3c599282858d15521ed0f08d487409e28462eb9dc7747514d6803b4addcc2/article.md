# Illuminant Surgical Leads Workshop at SIGGRAPH 2025

## Illuminant Surgical Leads Workshop at SIGGRAPH 2025


By[Illuminant Surgical](https://www.illuminant.ai/author/admin_illuminant)


|


June 12, 2025


|


1 min read


Illuminant Surgical is leading a workshop at SIGGRAPH 2025 in Vancouver, Canada.


The workshop “XR in Medicine: From the Dev Floor to the Operating Room” brings together voices at the forefront of extended and virtual reality in surgical innovation. Moderated by Enoch Omale (Software Engineer), Nick Shelton (Sr. Software Engineer), and Dr. James Hu (Co-Founder of Illuminant Surgical), the panel will also feature six distinguished physicians pushing the boundaries of immersive healthcare technology, offering clinical perspectives that shape the design and implementation of XR solutions.


We’re excited to demonstrate how XR and VR transform surgical care by connecting advanced graphics technologies with real clinical needs. Join us at SIGGRAPH 2025 to explore how user-driven design, inclusive development, and collaboration with physicians shape the next generation of XR tools for the operating room.
