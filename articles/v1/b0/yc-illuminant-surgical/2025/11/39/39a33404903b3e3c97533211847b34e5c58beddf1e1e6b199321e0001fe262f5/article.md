# Presentation at 2025 MDIC XR Summit

## Presentation at 2025 MDIC XR Summit


By[Illuminant Surgical](https://www.illuminant.ai/author/admin_illuminant)


|


November 1, 2025


|


1 min read


Illuminant Surgical will be presenting at the 2025 MDIC MedXR Summit, November 4–6 in Hyattsville, Maryland.


Our poster, “Simplifying Cybersecurity for Start-Ups: A Practical Framework for eSTAR-Ready Documentation” will cover our in-house framework harmonizing AAMI TIR57, SW96, ISO 14971, and FDA guidance. We're excited to share this unique approach with the MedXR community, designed to reduce ambiguity, deliver right-sized, eSTAR-aligned security documentation, and empower early-stage MedTech to achieve design velocity without compromising rigor.


Learn more at[https://mdic.org](https://mdic.org/)
