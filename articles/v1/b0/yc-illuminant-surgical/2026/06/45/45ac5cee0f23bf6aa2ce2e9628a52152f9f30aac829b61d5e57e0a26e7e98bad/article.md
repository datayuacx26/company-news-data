# Illuminant Surgical Hosts Second Frontier Workshop at SIGGRAPH 2026

## Illuminant Surgical Hosts Second Frontier Workshop at SIGGRAPH 2026


By[Illuminant Surgical](https://www.illuminant.ai/author/admin_illuminant)


|


June 29, 2026


|


1 min read


Illuminant Surgical is set to lead its second frontier workshop for SIGGRAPH 2026 in Los Angeles, California.


Following the success of Illuminant Surgical's first SIGGRAPH workshop, “Graphics in Medicine: From the Dev Floor to the Operating Room 2” brings together graphics developers, medtech researchers, and clinical professionals to bridge the gap between technical novelty and true clinical value. Moderated by Enoch Omale (Software Engineer), and Anthony Le (Clinical Engineer), this year's workshop shifts the focus from theoretical design to practical technical execution.


We are thrilled to showcase how immersive tech and real-time graphics are solving critical operating room problems by translating advanced tools into true clinical value. Join us at SIGGRAPH to connect with top doctors, tackle live medical design challenges, and explore the future of healthcare technology.
