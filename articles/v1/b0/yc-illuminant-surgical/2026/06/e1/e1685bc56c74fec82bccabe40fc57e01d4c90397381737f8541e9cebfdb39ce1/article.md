# Illuminant Surgical Cuts Ribbon On New Nexus Headquarters

## Illuminant Surgical Cuts Ribbon On New Nexus Headquarters


By[Illuminant Surgical](https://www.illuminant.ai/author/admin_illuminant)


|


June 5, 2026


|


1 min read


Illuminant Surgical welcomed over 130 distinguished guests for its first official launch party and ribbon cutting ceremony at the Nexus, the new company headquarters in Los Angeles. The ceremony gathered friends, family, advisors, and investors to commemorate Illuminant Surgical's momentous milestones.


The event celebrated three distinct breakthroughs for the company: the grand opening of the Nexus facility, the closing of an $8.4M fundraise, and the completion of the first production-equivalent units for Illuminant Surgical's upcoming FDA submission. This celebration marks a profound leap forward in Illuminant Surgical’s mission to transform the operating room.
