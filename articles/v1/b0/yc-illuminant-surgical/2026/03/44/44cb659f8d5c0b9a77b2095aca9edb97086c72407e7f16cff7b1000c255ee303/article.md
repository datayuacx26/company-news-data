# Dr. James Hu Presents at NVIDIA GTC 2026

## Dr. James Hu Presents at NVIDIA GTC 2026


By[Illuminant Surgical](https://www.illuminant.ai/author/admin_illuminant)


|


March 18, 2026


|


1 min read


Our Co-Founder and Co-CEO, Dr. James Hu, had the incredible opportunity to present our work at Illuminant Surgical on the big stage for NVIDIA GTC 2026. With over 30,000 attendees, GTC was the epicenter of showcasing how AI and compute would transform various industries.


The company is excited by the vision shared by Jensen Huang during his keynote on the future of AI as critical infrastructure in all sectors. At Illuminant Surgical, our team is dedicated to shaping the future of Physical AI for medicine, including the dynamic display of anatomy, predicting tumor movement during procedures, and enhancing the capabilities of medical imaging.
