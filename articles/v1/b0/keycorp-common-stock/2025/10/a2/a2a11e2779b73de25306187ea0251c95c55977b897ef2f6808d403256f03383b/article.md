# KEYCORP TO PRESENT AT THE BANCANALYSTS ASSOCIATION OF BOSTON CONFERENCE

CLEVELAND


, Oct. 30, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- KeyCorp (NYSE:KEY ) announced today that Victor Alexander, Head of Consumer Banking, and Clark Khayat, Chief Financial Officer, will present at the BancAnalysts Association of Boston Conference on Thursday, November 6, 2025, at 10:30 a.m. ET in Boston.


KeyCorp plans to review its performance, strategy, and outlook, and the discussion may include forward-looking statements and other material information. The live audio webcast will be available on the day of the conference at[www.key.com/ir](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4545578-1&h=1788047250&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.key.com%2Fir&a=www.key.com%2Fir) . If you are unable to join the live webcast, or wish to hear a re-broadcast, access[www.key.com/ir](http://www.key.com/ir) and select Events & Presentations.
