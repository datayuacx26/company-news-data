# KEYCORP LOWERS ITS PRIME LENDING RATE TO 6.75 PERCENT

CLEVELAND


, Dec. 10, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- KeyCorp (NYSE:KEY ) and its banking affiliates have lowered their prime lending rate to 6.75 percent from 7.00 percent, effective tomorrow, Dec. 11, 2025.
