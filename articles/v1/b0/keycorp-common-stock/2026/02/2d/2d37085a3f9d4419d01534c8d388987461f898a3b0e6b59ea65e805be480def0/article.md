# KEYCORP TO PRESENT AT THE UBS FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE & THE BANK OF AMERICA SECURITIES FINANCIAL SERVICES CONFERENCE

CLEVELAND


, Feb. 2, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- KeyCorp (NYSE:KEY ) announced today that Ken Gavrity, Head of Commercial Banking, will speak at the UBS Financial Services Conference on Monday, February 9, 2026, at 3:30 p.m. ET in Key Biscayne, Florida.


Chris Gorman, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, will speak at the Bank of America Securities 2026 Financial Services Conference on Tuesday, February 10, 2026, at 8:50 a.m. ET in Miami, Florida.


KeyCorp plans to review its performance, strategy, and outlook, and the discussion may include forward-looking statements and other material information.
