# Halliburton Wins Integrated Drilling and Completions Contracts for Granmorgu Deepwater Project Offshore Suriname, Operated by Totalenergies

HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 13, 2026-- Halliburton (NYSE: HAL) wins major integrated well construction contracts for the GranMorgu deepwater development offshore Suriname, operated by TotalEnergies. The agreement includes[drilling](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.halliburton.com%2Fen%2Fwell-construction%2Fdrilling%3Futm_campaign%3Dcorporate%26utm_id%3D7018X000001aqSLQAY%26utm_source%3Dpress_release%26utm_medium%3Dpdf%26utm_content%3Dgranmorgu_suriname&esheet=54568511&newsitemid=20260713936982&lan=en-US&anchor=drilling&index=1&md5=3d7340f6c9a4421e2552f8d3378e0df8) and[completions services](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.halliburton.com%2Fen%2Fcompletions%3Futm_campaign%3Dcorporate%26utm_id%3D7018X000001aqSLQAY%26utm_source%3Dpress_release%26utm_medium%3Dpdf%26utm_content%3Dgranmorgu_suriname&esheet=54568511&newsitemid=20260713936982&lan=en-US&anchor=completions+services&index=2&md5=de49afbbcb8ad21b4294ff5d99d1b6d4) for a long-term program. Halliburton will deploy a fully integrated, digital and automation execution model that unites planning, engineering, and operations to improve performance, accelerate learning, and reduce total cost of ownership throughout well construction.


“This award reflects the value of integrated execution, collaboration, and digital technology in complex deepwater developments,” said Franco Delano, vice president, Caribbean, Halliburton. “The GranMorgu project demonstrates how aligned teams and advanced well construction capabilities support safe, efficient delivery and maximize asset value for our customers.”


Halliburton will apply integrated digital workflows, real time data and remote operations control for drilling and completions to improve well placement accuracy, and delivery assurance. These capabilities connect surface operations with subsurface execution to enhance recovery while lowering total cost of ownership for TotalEnergies.


The project supports local capability development through major infrastructure investment and collaboration with local suppliers. As part of the project scope, Halliburton worked with local suppliers to upgrade its liquid mud and cement plant. The company also supported the construction of Suriname’s first state-of-the-art completions and drilling workshop, featuring advanced maintenance and repair capabilities.


Halliburton will prioritize local talent acquisition and suppliers to support national economic growth. The project supports the expansion of Suriname’s offshore energy industry and establishes a benchmark through the first global alliance between Halliburton, TotalEnergies, and Noble.


This integrated approach establishes a new benchmark for international unconventional fracturing. It combines electrification, automation, and advanced digital workflows to improve efficiency, consistency, and emissions-intensity reduction efforts. A common digital platform supports collaborative, phased integration of next‑generation[intelligent fracturing](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.halliburton.com%2Fen%2Fcompletions%2Fstimulation%2Fhydraulic-fracturing%2Fintelligent-fracturing%3Futm_campaign%3Dcorporate%26utm_id%3D7018X000001aqSLQAY%26utm_source%3Dpress_release%26utm_medium%3Dpdf%26utm_content%3Dgranmorgu_suriname&esheet=54568511&newsitemid=20260713936982&lan=en-US&anchor=intelligent+fracturing&index=3&md5=41c2e68aadbf5e74ee5ba26a5051ebc3) and[advanced subsurface monitoring](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.halliburton.com%2Fen%2Fsubsurface%3Futm_campaign%3Dcorporate%26utm_id%3D7018X000001aqSLQAY%26utm_source%3Dpress_release%26utm_medium%3Dpdf%26utm_content%3Dgranmorgu_suriname&esheet=54568511&newsitemid=20260713936982&lan=en-US&anchor=advanced+subsurface+monitoring&index=4&md5=1b41bf028f8304dfa7f47211f0e82a75) .
