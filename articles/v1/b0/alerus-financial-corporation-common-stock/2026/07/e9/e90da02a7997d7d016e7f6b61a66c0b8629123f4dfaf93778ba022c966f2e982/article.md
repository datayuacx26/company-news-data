# Finance and Commerce Readers Vote Alerus Top Business Bank, Wealth Provider


##### July 1, 2026


Readers of Twin Cities-based Finance & Commerce have once again voted Alerus as a top provider of multiple financial services, including business banking.


Alerus was voted the top provider in the following categories:


- Best business bank
- Best small business banking
- Best commercial mortgage lender
- Best wealth management


See the complete Reader Rankings[here](https://finance-commerce.com/2026/06/finance-commerces-2026-reader-rankings-final-winners/) .
