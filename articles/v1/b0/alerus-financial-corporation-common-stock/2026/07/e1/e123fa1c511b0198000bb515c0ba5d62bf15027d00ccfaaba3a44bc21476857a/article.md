# Alerus Adds Gavin Hain as Business Banker


##### July 29, 2026


Gavin Hain has joined Alerus as associate vice president, business banker, based in Grand Forks. In this role, Gavin works closely with business clients to deliver financial solutions that support their long-term goals. He serves as a trusted guide and primary point of contact, connecting clients with Alerus’ diversified financial services to provide seamless, efficient experiences tailored to their unique needs.


Before joining Alerus, Gavin spent three years with American Family Insurance. He holds a bachelor’s degree in business economics and finance from the University of North Dakota. An accomplished athlete, Gavin played five years of Division I men’s hockey at UND, serving as assistant captain for two seasons, before spending three years playing professionally.
