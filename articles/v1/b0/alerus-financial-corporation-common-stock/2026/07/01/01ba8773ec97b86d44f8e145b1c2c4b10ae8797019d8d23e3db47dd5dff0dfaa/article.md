# Bobbi Sondreal Joins Alerus as Senior Wealth and Fiduciary Advisor


##### July 29, 2026


Alerus is pleased to announce Bobbi Sondreal has joined the company as a senior wealth and fiduciary advisor, based in Grand Forks.


Bobbi is a Certified Trust and Fiduciary Advisor with nearly 20 years of financial services experience, specializing in trust administration and wealth management for high-net-worth individuals and families. She contributes her expertise to help clients navigate complex financial and fiduciary matters, providing personalized guidance, exceptional client service, and regulatory expertise to support their legacy-focused planning. She also works directly with clients, attorneys, CPAs, and other experienced professionals to administer a diverse portfolio of trusts, conservatorships, custodial accounts, IRAs, and estates.


Before rejoining Alerus in June 2026, Bobbi served as a succession and retirement officer at AgCountry Farm Credit Services in Grand Forks. She previously spent nine years with Alerus as a member of the wealth management and trust team. She holds a bachelor’s degree in business administration from North Dakota State University.
