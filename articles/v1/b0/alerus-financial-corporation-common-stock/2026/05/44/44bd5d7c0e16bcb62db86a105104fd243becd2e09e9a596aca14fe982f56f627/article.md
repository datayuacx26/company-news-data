# Clara Price joins Alerus as Senior Treasury Management Consultant


##### May 15, 2026


Alerus is pleased to welcome Clara Price as a senior treasury management consultant. In this role, Clara partners with commercial bankers and clients to provide tailored cash management solutions to help clients improve efficiencies, reduce expenses, increase income, and protect their assets.


Clara has 15 years of financial industry experience, with extensive expertise in treasury management. Before joining Alerus, she served as a treasury consultant for BMO U.S. in Phoenix. She holds a bachelor’s degree in finance from the Northern Alberta Institute of Technology and a master’s degree in business administration from the University of Fredericton in New Brunswick, Canada. She is based in Phoenix.
