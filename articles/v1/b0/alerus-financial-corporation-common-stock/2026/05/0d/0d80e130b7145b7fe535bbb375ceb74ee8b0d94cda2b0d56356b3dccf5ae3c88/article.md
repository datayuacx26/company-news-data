# Ben Hildestad joins Alerus as Treasury Management Consultant


##### May 26, 2026


Alerus is pleased to announce Ben Hildestad has joined the company as a treasury management consultant based in Fargo.


In this role, Ben works closely with the commercial banking team to provide comprehensive cash management solutions tailored to each client’s unique needs. With nearly a decade of financial industry experience, he specializes in helping clients increase their income, reduce expenses, improve efficiency, and protect their finances.


Before joining Alerus, he served in roles as a cash management officer at Choice Bank and client services specialist at First International Bank and Trust. He holds a bachelor’s degree in finance and business administration from the University of Mary in Bismarck, North Dakota.
