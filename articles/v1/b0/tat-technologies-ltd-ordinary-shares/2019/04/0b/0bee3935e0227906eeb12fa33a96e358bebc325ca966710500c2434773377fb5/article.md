# TAT Technologies signs a new agreement with one of Europe’s leading system manufacturers in the Aerospace industry

**TAT Technologies signs a new agreement with one of the leading European system manufacturers for the Aerospace industry valued at up to USD 20 million in total revenue for the years 2019-2023**


TAT Technologies Ltd. (NASDAQ: TATT – News) (“the Company”), a leading provider of services and products to the commercial and military aerospace and ground defense systems, today announced that it has signed a five (5) year agreement with a leading European system manufacturer for the Aerospace industry for the supply of heat exchangers. The expected revenues from this contract are US$20 million for the years 2019-2023. Under this agreement, TAT will be the exclusive heat exchanger provider for these specific products.


Mr. Igal Zamir, CEO and President of TAT Technologies stated, “ *We are pleased with the renewal of this partnership which will enable us to increase sales in the heat exchanger OEM segment and is aligned with our growth plans for 2019-20”.*
