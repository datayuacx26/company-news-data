# TAT Technologies Announces Expansion of Group Sales Department & Opening New Centralized Sales Offices in Mooresville, NC

GREENSBORO, NC, November 2020 – **TAT Technologies recently announced two major expansion moves, implemented with the aim of strengthening the company’s presence in the US and expanding its portfolio of services.**


TAT Technologies recently announced the expansion of the Group Sales Department and opening new centralized sales offices in Mooresville, NC, to consolidate its presence in the US. The TAT Technologies Group Sales Team will be responsible for managing the sales for all four of TAT Technologies’ subsidiaries (OEM, MRO, Commercial, and Defense markets).


## TAT Technologies team expansion: new Group Sales Team


The development of the group sales department is a recent move at the group level that will ensure better communication with the aviation industry, as well as a smoother sales process for this particular sector.


“Five new team members have recently joined TAT Technologies to form our new Group Sales Department,” announces Igal Zamir, TAT Technologies CEO. “Lee Levy, Bill Greer, Alyssa Conover, Marc Bajaj, and Andre Op’t Hof have joined us to help with the implementation of TAT’s global group sales strategy.”


The global defense portfolio will be mentored by retired Air Force General Officer Lee Levy who brings 34+ years of global defense and security experience to TAT’s team.


Mr Lee K Levy, Executive VP Global Defense


“Warfighter support is a cornerstone of TAT’s ethos,” Mr Levy says, “and that resonates with me now just as it did while in uniform. Couple that with the amazing global talent that TAT has assembled, and I am confident that TAT is well-positioned to take warfighter support to new heights.”


Mr. Greer is joining TAT Technologies as the new Senior Director of Military Sales and will lead the military sales team. Mrs. Conover is the newly appointed Director of Military Sales, Mr. Bajaj will occupy the position of Military OEM Director of Accounts Management, and Mr. Op’t Hof will become the Commercial MRO Director of Account Management.


“They possess extensive experience in the field,” highlights Mr. Zamir, “and are valued additions to our group sales department.”


More experts have joined TAT Technologies’ departments. TAT Technologies Group Sales and Marketing Department welcomes Jerry Fannin as VP Airline Business Development and Mark Wilson as MRO Director of Strategic Accounts.


Jerry will focus mainly on the GTCP331-200/250 APU business and brings to the table more than 30 years of experience in numerous areas such as strategic planning, management, growth strategy, new product introduction management, strategic partnerships and more.


Mark brings various experiences in sales and marketing roles in selling industrial products, fasteners and cutting tool services through direct and indirect channels by building strong partnerships with major clients. Mark and Jerry possess the skills that adopt our proactive account management policy and will be instrumental in creating this culture.


## TAT Technologies opening new centralized sales offices in Mooresville


TAT Technologies is in continuous development, with the company recently opening new centralized sales offices in Mooresville. The new location encompasses about 1,100sq ft of offices and is located at 106 Langtree Village Drive, Mooresville NC 28117.


Chap Berrier, TAT Technologies Vice President of MRO Strategic Accounts, says that the move is, “part of the company’s strategic plan to reinforce its position in the United States as a leading provider of thermal management solutions and power and actuation services. Mooresville is part of the fast-growing Charlotte Metro, the largest municipality in Iredell Country. This makes it a strategic location for TAT Technologies’ expansion plans.”


## About TAT Technologies

TAT Technologies is a strategic global partner to leading players in the aerospace industry and specializes in innovative commercial, defense, OEM and MRO solutions.
