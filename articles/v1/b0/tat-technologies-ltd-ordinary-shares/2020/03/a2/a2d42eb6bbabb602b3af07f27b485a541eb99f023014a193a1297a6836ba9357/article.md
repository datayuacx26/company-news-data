# TAT Technologies Announcement – COVID-19

TAT Technologies and companies are taking the COVID-19 pandemic very seriously and dedicated a team of company officials to monitor and manage this crisis. Our goal is to keep our employees and families protected as best possible that will enable us to provide our customers with uninterrupted services. During this time, we have put many procedures and practices in place to support these efforts.


We have conducted worse case scenarios to test our infrastructures and systems to be prepared in case of emergencies. Our goal is to have flexibility in non-direct production employees the ability to work remotely in order to limit exposure and possible spread of the virus.


We have worked with our supply chain partners to identify potential risk in disruption with corrective actions where necessary. Supply Chain/Logistics will continue to monitor any disruption to our supply chain and take the appropriate measures as needed.


As a valued customer of TAT Technologies, we want you to know we are here to support you and your operations. Recognizing this remains a fluid situation our team is meeting weekly (more if needed) to assess our COVID-19 prevention plan as well as in process status on current repair orders. No doubt this crisis will pass, and the aviation community will rebound so let’s work together to get through it. Should you have any questions please direct them to the below team members that are tasked to guide our companies through the next couple of months and assure our services are uninterrupted.


TAT Technologies will continue to monitor Local and Federal Governments and World Health Organizations for any mandatory directive that could affect our business. Should we see that a mandated directive will impact our day to day business your designated point of contact below will be in touch to discuss with you.
