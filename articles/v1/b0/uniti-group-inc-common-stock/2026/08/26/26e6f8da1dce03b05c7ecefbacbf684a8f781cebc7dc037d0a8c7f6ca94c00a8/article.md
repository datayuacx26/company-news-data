## Hominy Now a ‘Gig-Ready’ Community From Kinetic’s Fiber-Optic Network Expansion


August 12, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.uniti.com/node/16646/pdf)


- *More than 75% of area homes now have access to Kinetic’s Multi-Gig, Next-Generation Internet*
- *1,200 locations benefit from better internet experience supporting economic growth, telehealth, work, streaming*


HOMINY, Okla., Aug. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Kinetic, the leading insurgent residential and business fiber internet provider, announced today that Hominy is officially recognized as a Kinetic[‘Gig-Ready Community](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=9Jq42QvSSbEJIEffnEoRMYcnvMMqbi43jtpiuec-oIlRqYt7fTR_dmrP8Y_2qD8a3MBeUe0On4UeYFlFUxbD53WMz6ZyP2_jSfKSpywNj4hqLwtckeyhUjRa3KO0QXpn2YT38Oj3zsuIA65EMgHTV0YQfd3e0Xg3wDekmm6NWQVERAtU-dhtNBMIRmMJWoQb5BghBzeJm8WoFu-_nycy9w==) ,’ meaning more than 75% of the area’s homes now have access to Kinetic’s high-speed, next generation internet.


This major technological milestone marks a significant advancement in the community’s digital infrastructure and provides many residents with award-winning technology - at a better value - and an overall better internet experience to help enhance everyday life.


“The experience has been much better than with our previous provider. Great customer service and internet connectivity,” said Andrew Riddle, a Kinetic customer in Oklahoma.


To date, more than 1,200 homes in Hominy have access to Kinetic’s future-proof internet to better support work, education, streaming and telehealth.


Kinetic’s continued investment in the community’s fiber-optic network is providing fast and reliable connectivity that is essential for economic growth and opportunities. According to[research](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=lfHncBe-MC_CHAU03zsMlsSzey0vZPAidIuOjp7HCFl2NS6vZ0DINYHvenklBxNJy11hwfHftUt4FlUwIz04-L_Rxl8uTHNz5C2lEFLoU_BGJJxsI-4HhCpyJh_yBU40N7r7KiQ7HifZjAukz07o1Z9jubzv5NBlONUePPYKWat9vodh_EJRBAlsOKUAebXfxf0pdOpybII7xQvzMPD-lA==) , fiber-connected communities see meaningful gains, including:


- [213%](https://fiberbroadband.org/resources/beyond-connectivity-the-role-of-broadband-in-rural-economic-growth-and-resilience/) higher business growth
- [10%](https://fiberbroadband.org/resources/beyond-connectivity-the-role-of-broadband-in-rural-economic-growth-and-resilience/) higher self-employment
- [14-17%](https://fiberbroadband.org/resources/economic-benefits-of-fiber-deployment/) increase in home values


“High-speed fiber plays a vital role in how people live, work, learn, and stay connected in today’s digital world,” said Stacy Hale, Kinetic’s state operations president. “That’s why we’re continuing to invest in our network and build more Gig-Ready communities like Hominy, giving more residents access to the reliable connectivity they need to grow, unlock new opportunities, and improve everyday life.”


Kinetic has been a long-term partner to Hominy and many other communities across Oklahoma. Kinetic was recently named CNET’s “2026 Best Rural Fiber Provider,” and the company’s continued local investment in the state builds on its national recognition for fiber performance and reliability.
