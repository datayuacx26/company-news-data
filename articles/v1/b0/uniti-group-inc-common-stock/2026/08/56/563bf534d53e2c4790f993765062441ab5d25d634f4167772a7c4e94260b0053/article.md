## Oklahoma Benefits From 8,000 New Kinetic Fiber Builds


August 5, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.uniti.com/node/16586/pdf)


- Rapid Q2 2026 fiber expansion benefits communities and customers by enabling remote work, gaming, streaming, agriculture and telehealth
- 141,000 total fiber locations added across Kinetic’s 18-state footprint in Q2


BROKEN ARROW, Okla., Aug. 05, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Kinetic today announced a major broadband expansion in Oklahoma, delivering multi-gig fiber internet to 8,000 additional homes across more than 30 communities in the second quarter of 2026. The expansion highlights Kinetic’s commitment to delivering better technology, at a better value for customers.


In the second quarter of 2026, Kinetic added fiber locations in Broken Arrow, Hominy, Stilwell, Elmore City, Checotah, Fairfax, and more. And, according to Ookla’s 2026 H1 Speedtest data Kinetic delivers “the most responsive internet speeds” in Broken Arrow.*


“Our experience \[with Kinetic\] has been much better than with our previous provider. Kinetic has great customer service and internet connectivity,” said Cody Saunders, a Kinetic customer in Broken Arrow.


To date, more than 76,000 locations across the Sooner State have access to Kinetic’s high-speed, next-generation connectivity.

Fiber is also significantly more sustainable than copper cables and uses up to[95% less energy per gigabit](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=A6OgOszazsQpwOYkbVT2ja8TSOyCK29iFI_gAydihw4Lkz93arlbal7ZKlYMNFVN9_xtt6AtPS-6bn6cFcHcuNjsBnPHLUsrp1I6G1EVpPwhEEOpz6PGnnEDq_IHG8ncYFXgjM20Ejjy8MdYCYtcT3vsGbg2OknEoMT3D4JvbM5n26MBPjSThmhA2fAEXaax) . It requires less maintenance over time, which reduces environmental impacts and community disruptions.


“Fast, reliable fiber internet is more important than ever; it can change the way people live, work and learn in today’s digital world. Better connectivity can create new opportunities in communities of every size, and we’re committed to delivering it,” said Stacy Hale, Kinetic’s state operations president. “Our focus is on building strong, future-ready infrastructure that helps residents, businesses and families stay connected and succeed for years to come.”


Kinetic fiber customers can benefit from seamless 4K+ streaming across multiple devices with no data caps and no usage charges. Multi-gigabit connections support remote work, online learning and low-latency gaming and, through partnerships with YouTube TV, AT&T for wireless bundles, and eero, an Amazon company, households and businesses receive a unified high-performance connectivity experience.
