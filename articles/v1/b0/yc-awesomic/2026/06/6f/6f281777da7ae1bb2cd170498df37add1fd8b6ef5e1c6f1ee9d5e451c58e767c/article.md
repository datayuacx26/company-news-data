## FAQs


### What should I expect from motion graphic design services in turnaround time?


Turnaround varies by project type. Simple social ad packs can take a few days, while a full explainer video may need several weeks. Planning extra time helps handle revisions and ensures quality. Always ask your agency about their typical timelines before starting.


### How do motion design agencies handle rights and source file delivery?


Most top motion design agencies provide usage rights after payment, but source files may or may not be included. Check this upfront to avoid surprises. Some services include source files as part of the package, especially subscription models. Always confirm terms in writing.


### Why choose subscription motion design services over traditional agencies?


Subscription services offer predictable costs and faster iterations through vetted talent pools. They are well suited for ongoing projects and frequent updates. However, the quality can vary by individual creatives, so review portfolios and vetting methods to find a good fit for your needs.


### What makes top motion design agencies for tech companies different?


They often have strong experience with UI/UX animation, Lottie exports, and technical storytelling. These agencies understand tech audiences and data-driven optimization. Look for teams with real-time workflows and good client reporting to maximize impact.


### How do motion graphic design agencies use AI tools today?


AI helps speed up concepting and testing multiple creative versions quickly. However, human review remains essential to maintain quality and brand voice. Many agencies combine AI tools with expert animators to get the best results efficiently.
