## How top brand design agencies work: A clear look at their process and how Awesomic speeds it up


When you hire top brand design agencies or the best brand design agencies, you want to know exactly how they work and what to expect. Let’s break down the usual steps and tools they use, so you see how things unfold from start to finish—and how our platform, Awesomic, aligns with this but moves way faster.


**Here’s the typical journey of brand and identity design services:**


- **Discovery/Immersion (1–3 weeks)**
Teams dive deep. They hold workshops (usually 90 to 180 minutes long), interview key stakeholders, talk to customers, audit your website analytics, and scan competitors. A sample 90-minute workshop agenda might cover company history, audience needs, competitor gaps, and brainstorm core values. Interview scripts focus on what customers value, pain points, and decision triggers. Surveys might ask about brand perception or product satisfaction.
- **Strategy (2–6 weeks)**
Next, agencies use classic frameworks: brand DNA templates (purpose, belief, promise), brand pyramids (attributes to benefits to values), brand identity prisms, perceptual maps, and SWOT/TOWS analyses. For example, a SWOT might show “strong design” as a strength, which turns into a TOWS action to “leverage design to increase market share.” Personas and customer journey maps are built from data like Google Analytics, CRM lists, and interviews. Each persona includes demographics and pain points; touchpoints in the journey have KPIs linked, like engagement or conversion rates.
- **Concept & Design (4–12 weeks)**
The creative phase often follows design sprints—either one 5-day sprint or iterative 2-week cycles. Agencies encourage feedback loops using tools like Figma, where teams comment directly. They keep a clear change log to track versions and decisions.
- **Build & QA (4–16 weeks)**
Deliverables like design tokens, responsive breakpoints, and accessibility reports are finalized. Agencies run QA checklists covering basics: accessibility, SEO, and website speed (usually under 2.5 seconds load time).
- **Launch & Activation (2–8 weeks)**
Teams roll out the brand with launch checklists, marketing collateral, and internal training.
- **Measurement & Iteration (Ongoing)**
Agencies track KPIs, compare them to baselines, and optimize campaigns or design based on real data.


*To keep these phases on track, agencies use templates, like creative briefs covering project goals, audience, tone, timelines, budget, and deliverables. Design review templates and measurement plans help ensure success.*
