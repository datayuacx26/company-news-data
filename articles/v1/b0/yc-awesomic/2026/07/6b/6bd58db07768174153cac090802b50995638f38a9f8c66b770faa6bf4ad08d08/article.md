## What is design as a service?


When I first stumbled upon design as a service, I was honestly a bit skeptical. But after using several tools in real projects, I realized how game-changing this approach really is.


Simply put, design as a service means getting professional design help exactly when you need it, without long-term hiring or big upfront costs. It’s like having a design team on call, ready to jump in.


It can also mean the ability to quickly[hire a Figma designer](https://www.awesomic.com/hire/hire-a-figma-designer) as part of a flexible service, so you get expert UI/UX work without going through a full recruitment process.


You’ll learn how design as a service works through five ultra-practical points:


1. **On-demand expertise** → You tap into skilled designers whenever your project calls for it. No more scrambling to find freelancers at the last minute.
2. **Scalable support** → Whether you need just one design or a full suite of screens, the service scales with your product’s needs.
3. **Consistency and quality** → Tools like Figma and Adobe XD ensure smooth workflows and polished results every time.
4. **Fast turnaround** → Teams using frameworks like Agile or Lean UX deliver designs quickly, keeping your development moving.
5. **Cost-effective solution** → Pay for what you need, nothing more. This lowers expenses and speeds up your time to market.


By now, you see why design as a service isn’t just a buzzword. It’s a smart, flexible way to get top-notch design that fits your software company’s growth and pace.


When you combine the right tools with solid approaches, your team becomes unstoppable.

Startups should look at design as a service companies when they need quick, ongoing creative support but can't hire full-time designers. It helps keep costs low while getting professional help that scales up or down as the business grows.
