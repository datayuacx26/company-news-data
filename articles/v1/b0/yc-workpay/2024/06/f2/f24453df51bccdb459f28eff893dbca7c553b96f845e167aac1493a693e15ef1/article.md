# The Functionality of Payroll Software


Payroll softwares typically offer a wide range of functionalities to streamline and automate the process of paying employees. These functionalities include;


- Employee information management
- Time and attendance tracking
- Appropriation of salaries and wages
- Direct deposit and payment
- Compliance
- Reporting
- Self-service portals
- Employee benefits administration
- Payment beyond boundries


### 1. Employee Information Management


A payroll software allows your human resource team to securely store and manage employee information such as contact details, tax withholding forms, banking details for direct deposit, and employment history. A payroll system allows the human resource team to record and gather all the information pertaining their employee from recruitment to termination. It must also include an employment eligibility form containing personal and professional information about the employee.


### 2. Time and Attendance Tracking


A payroll software is integrated with a[time and attendance tracking system](https://www.myworkpay.com/features/time-and-attendance) to enable a correct payroll appropriation. The payroll system monitors all employee time and attendance behavior by tracking of staff attendance and working hours, leaves and times off.


### 3. Appropriation of Salaries and Wages


An ideal payroll software automatically calculates wages based on employee working hours, hourly rates, overtime rates, and any other relevant factors as designed by the organization. This payroll system also accommodates for deductions such as taxes, benefits, and retirement contributions.


### 4. Direct Deposit and Payment


Facilitates direct deposit into employees' bank accounts, eliminating the need for paper cheques. Some software also supports alternative payment methods like pay cards. In this case the tasking process of employee payment is foregone especially for firms with a high number of workforce or a firm with different entities established in different localities.


### 5. Compliance


A payroll system helps organizations comply with tax regulations by calculating and withholding the correct amount of federal, state, and local taxes. The payroll app is fed with compliance rules and regulations that should be considered in payroll generation. The system also alerts administrators to changes in tax laws and compliance requirements to ensure that payroll processes remain compliant with regulations.


### 6. Reporting


Some basic end-of-year reports are included as standard in most payroll software. You should choose software that has flexible reporting and supports online. A payroll report is a document that contains specific information relating to company payroll and components contained within it. If you are working with an outsourced provider, a payroll report is something that you would request from them. If you’re using payroll software then then you can downloaded yourself


### 7. Self-Service Portals


Provides access to payroll functions and services via mobile devices allowing employees to access their payroll information, update personal information, and manage direct deposit choice and preferences through a secure and mobile, online portal.


### 8. Employee Benefits Administration


Payroll system calculates employee benefits based on their contract and employee benefit regulations in the country in which the business operates.


### 9. Payment Beyond boundaries


This is mainly applicable for businesses operating in multiple countries or with diverse employee bases, supporting different currencies and languages. The payroll system is able to disburse payments based on the locality of every employee.
