# Africa's Workforce Deserves More Than a Payslip

# **This is Why the Labor force in Africa Deserves More Than just a Payslip**


For decades, the conversation around HR and payroll in Africa has centred on one thing: making sure employees get paid accurately and on time. And while that matters enormously, it has never been the whole story.


This is because a payslip, however accurate, does not tell you how an employee feels on the way to work. It does not capture the weight of a school fees deadline looming three weeks before payday, or the quiet anxiety of an unexpected expense with nowhere affordable to turn. It does not measure what financial stress costs a business in lost focus, absenteeism, and turnover.


At Workpay, we have always believed that managing Africa's workforce means more than processing payroll. It means investing in the people behind it and that is why we built **Workpay for Employees.**


## **From employer tools to employee solutions**


Workpay started with a simple mission: give African businesses the modern infrastructure they need to pay, manage, and retain their people. Today, thousands of companies across the continent use Workpay to run payroll, manage HR, and stay compliant with local regulations.


But we know that infrastructure alone is not enough.


Workpay for Employees extends that same commitment directly to their employees. Alongside the payroll and HR tools employers rely on, employees now have access to financial wellness solutions designed for the realities of working life in Africa.


**Earned Wage Access** ; so that employees can access wages they have already earned, before payday arrives, without resorting to expensive credit.


**Short-term loans** ; affordable,disciplined borrowing with straightforward terms, available directly through Workpay.


**A digital employee wallet** ; to spend, save, and manage money in one place, seamlessly connected to their employer's payroll. This is currently available for Kenyan employees only, with an Africawide roll out plan in place.


## **What this means for employers**


Financial stress is one of the leading drivers of disengagement and attrition across African workplaces. When employees have access to tools that give them genuine financial breathing room, the results show up at work, in productivity, retention, and loyalty.


Offering Workpay for Employees costs nothing to set up for companies already running payroll with us. It is simply the next step in building a workplace where people can do their best work.


## **What this means for employees**


It means your employer's investment in Workpay now works for you too. Not just on payday,but on the days in between, when life does not wait for the calendar.


Workpay has always been about closing the gap between African businesses and their full potential. Now, we are closing a different gap; the one between what employees earn and what they can actually access when it matters most.
