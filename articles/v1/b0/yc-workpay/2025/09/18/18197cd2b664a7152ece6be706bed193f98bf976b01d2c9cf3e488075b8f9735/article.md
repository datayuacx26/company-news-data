# Client Case Study: Verto

# Simplifying HR & Payroll Operations


## **Overview**


**‍** Verto, a fast-evolving business in the financial services space, partnered with Workpay to streamline its HR and payroll processes. Over a two-year collaboration, Verto has experienced the value of having a reliable partner in navigating the dynamic needs of workforce management across Africa.


## **The Challenge**


**‍** As markets continue to shift, businesses like Verto require more than just tools, they need partners who understand regional nuances and can adapt to changing operational needs. Verto needed a solution that would simplify payroll, ensure compliance, and maintain timeliness in salary disbursements.


## **Our Approach**


**‍** Workpay took a collaborative approach, taking time to understand Verto’s unique context. We supported their HR and payroll functions by offering tailored tools and processes that could scale with their growth. Through consistent communication and a problem-solving mindset, we ensured the systems we implemented continued to meet their evolving requirements.


## **Results** ‍


- **Streamlined Salary Disbursements:** Timely and accurate payroll runs have removed administrative friction and helped build trust within their team.


- **Responsive Support:** Verto appreciated prompt and meaningful support across all touchpoints — a reflection of our customer-first mindset.


- **Sustained Relationship:** The foundation of our collaboration with Verto is trust and reliability, built over time through dependable delivery and ongoing support.


## **Client Reflection**


**‍** Verto’s team described their experience with Workpay as one marked by professionalism and commitment. They emphasized the value of working with a team that prioritizes strong, long-term relationships over transactional engagements.


## **Conclusion**


**‍** This partnership reflects Workpay’s broader mission for EOR in Africa, to support businesses with tools and care that empower them to focus on growth, not admin. For Verto, having a consistent and thoughtful partner has made all the difference.
