# Smithfield Foods to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences


November 19, 2025 6:45 am EST[Download as PDF](https://investors.smithfieldfoods.com/_assets/_cf00706aef2d6b4092ace8dcbb87e9e0/smithfieldfoods/news/2025-11-19_Smithfield_Foods_to_Participate_in_Upcoming_1425.pdf)


SMITHFIELD, Va., Nov. 19, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Smithfield Foods, Inc. (Nasdaq: SFD), an American food company and an industry leader in value-added packaged meats and fresh pork, today announced its participation in two upcoming investor conferences.


**Wednesday, December 3**


- **Morgan Stanley Global Consumer & Retail Conference, New York, NY** President and CEO Shane Smith Fireside Chat 2:15 pm EST
Webcast link[here](https://investors.smithfieldfoods.com/news-events/ir-calendar/detail/20251203-morgan-stanley-global-consumer-retail-conference)


**Wednesday, December 10**


- **Nasdaq 53** **rd** **Investor Conference, London, UK** CFO Mark Hall Fireside Chat 10:00 am EST; 3:00 pm GMT
Webcast link[here](https://investors.smithfieldfoods.com/news-events/ir-calendar/detail/20251210-nasdaq-53rd-investor-conference)


**About Smithfield Foods**
Smithfield Foods, Inc. (Nasdaq: SFD) is an American food company with a leading position in packaged meats and fresh pork products. With a diverse brand portfolio and strong relationships with U.S. farmers and customers, we responsibly meet demand for quality protein around the world.
