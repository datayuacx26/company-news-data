# Palette Wins G2 Badges for Fall 2023

We are excited to announce that Palette has been awarded new G2 badges for Fall 2023. G2 is a software peer review site with over 80 million annual visitors. These badges are based on customer reviews across multiple categories, and demonstrate Palette's excellence in the sales compensation software category. In addition to recognition as an overall High Performer, Palette has received badges for Easiest to do Business With, Best Support, Easiest Admin, Best Meets Requirements, and more.
