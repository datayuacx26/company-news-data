# CRM Commission Tracking

Nothing fosters trust and transparency within your team more than fair and timely disbursement of their sales commission. Recognizing the need to reward the best sales professionals efficiently, companies have employed different ways to **calculate sales commission payouts** over the years: from **basic spreadsheet** and **customer relationship (CRM) software** to fully automated **incentive compensation management software** .


## Is CRM Commission Tracking Worth It?


CRM software is one of the most elegant innovations in the business world. It has automated many tasks that ordinarily would've been tedious and labor-intensive. As a result, it has improved many companies' relationships with their customers. But will the CRM tracking template get the job done?


The long and short of it: Yes, but it is not without problems.


CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot are fantastic pieces of software. However, they're not designed to manage your organization's incentive compensation program. At best, CRM tracking is wanting. At worst, it might be costly as CRMs don't have the features to calculate and track incentives efficiently.


For instance, **Salesforce or HubSpot commission tracking** lacks these essential features:


- Commission planning and optimization tools.
- Tools to calculate clawback.
- Sales quota management.
- Customization for complex commission structure.


There are two workarounds to this issue: either build a commission system in your CRM or choose an incentive compensation management software that integrates with your chosen CRM.


Many CRMs have features that allow your organization to make custom calculations. Thus, creating your commission software in Salesforce or HubSpot is possible. However, you'll need to hire a team of developers to develop and maintain your commission system in your CRM. Plus, you don't always have the assurance that you get all the features and tools functioning seamlessly. Building a system from scratch is tedious work and requires some heavy lifting before implementation.


And so, the better option is to choose software that integrates well with your CRM. For example, Palette integrates well with Salesforce, HubSpot, and other CRMs. Thus, you can centralize all your data and quickly roll out commission plans.


## Sales Commission Tracking Spreadsheet


Some businesses also use sales commission tracking spreadsheets to process and track sales commissions. And while Excel is a powerful tool in your arsenal, a commission-tracking Excel template is not the best solution for processing incentive compensations. Here's why:


### High Risk for Error


To use a commission tracker Excel template, you need to manually input data like the commission rates, sales amount, and other relevant information into the template. Unfortunately, manual data entry increases the likelihood of error. Typos, wrong formulas, missing information, or even a misplaced comma could throw the compensation calculation out of whack. Errors could engender dissatisfaction and distrust in your sales team. And it could adversely affect their performance.


### Time-Consuming


Maintaining a sales commission tracker in Excel is not just time-consuming because of manual data entry; pay disputes could also be a headache. In addition, your staff could spend many hours combing through the mountain of data to verify and modify in case of discrepancies. And it's hours that could be better spent on things that impact your company's bottom line more.


### Limited Analysis and Reporting


A sales commission calculator template may work when you're starting. But as your company grows, your sales team produces more sales; the process also generates more data. These volumes of data could give you more insights into key metrics. And a sales commission tracker in Excel doesn't have the functionality to handle these data. By missing out on these critical insights, you could be leaving money on the table or unable to explore opportunities to scale your business to new heights.


### Inefficient Launch of New Commission Structure


Any business must innovate. And sometimes, changes in the commission structure are necessary as organizations grow or to keep up with the demands of the time. However, rolling out new commission structures using spreadsheets could be slow. Plus, if you use a more complex commission structure, Excel might need more functionality to support that.
