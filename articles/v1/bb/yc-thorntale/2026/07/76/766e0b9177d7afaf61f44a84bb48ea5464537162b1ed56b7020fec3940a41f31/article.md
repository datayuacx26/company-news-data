# Introducing Thorntale AI!

You may have noticed a new icon in Thorntale’s deck editor.


Today we’re excited to launch the world’s most powerful AI Presentation Assistant: Thorntale AI.


How is Thorntale AI different from other AI-powered productivity tools? Well, it’s not here to replace you. It’s here to work with you. (And it’s specifically integrated with Thorntale’s world class presentation design system.)


We’ve got a hot take: We don’t think the point of AI tools is to make your argument for you. You already know how to convince a customer to renew their contract, or make sure your team is aligned on next quarter’s goals. So instead, Thorntale AI is designed to make it quicker and easier for you to do your best work.


### “I’m a doctor, not a PowerPoint stylist!”[*](https://youtu.be/Emjw-xouW2U?si=Dels_XrbWF14BwLp&t=12)


Modern presentation software is incredibly powerful and flexible. However, this comes at the cost of becoming unwieldy to use; making a great-looking presentation means spending a long time learning how to use tooling that isn’t actually related to your subject.


Does this look familiar to you? Because I’ve spent too much of my life digging through menus like this.


That’s why Thorntale AI already knows all the options, and can use them for you. Thorntale AI can be the *presentation building* expert, so you can be the expert in *what you’re actually presenting on* .


(And Thorntale’s AI doesn’t forget where to find that one setting.)


### Your Presentation Co-Creator


Thorntale AI can be whatever you need in a collaborator, giving you feedback when you want it, suggesting ways to make your points more clear, or proofreading your draft for errors.


As we add more features to Thorntale, we plan to integrate those into Thorntale AI as well! We’re excited to see how AI-powered interfaces can turn powerful tools into accessible ones, while retaining the flexibility that modern users need.


Our free[Thorntale Basic](https://www.thorntale.com/pricing) plan gives you 500 Thorntale AI messages a month, more than enough to experiment. Check it out today!
