# Create with Thorntale AI

We’ve all been there: your meeting is in 5 minutes and you’re supposed to present. But your slides aren’t ready!


Panic no longer. We’ve upgraded Thorntale AI with a full deck creation engine, allowing you to create a perfect deck from scratch in less time than it takes to make your morning coffee.


Thorntale’s deck creation can start from a set of notes, a quick brief, or even one line of text.


Thorntale first generates an outline for you to approve, allowing you to make any modifications or add more context before it creates full slides. When you’re ready, select your theme then hit ‘Generate Deck’ to get your slides.


We’ve also added AI Quick Actions to make it faster and easier to modify slides after you’ve created your presentation.


You can find these under the AI icon in the slide menu. Of course, you can still ask Thorntale AI directly to make any changes we don’t have a quick action for.


We’d love to hear about how you use Thorntale AI!
