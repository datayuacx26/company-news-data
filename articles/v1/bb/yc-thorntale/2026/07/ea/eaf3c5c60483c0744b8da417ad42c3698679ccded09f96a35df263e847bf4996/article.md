# Thorntale Presentations Shutting Down


Today we’re sorry to announce that Thorntale Presentations is shutting down.


To all our users over the past couple years, **thank you** for trying us out! We’ve loved helping you make beautiful presentations, and we hope you loved making them.


If you have any important presentations in Thorntale Presentations you need help exporting, please reach out tosupport@thorntale.com .


#### John Stimac / CTO


John builds Thorntale’s frontend and data visualizations.
