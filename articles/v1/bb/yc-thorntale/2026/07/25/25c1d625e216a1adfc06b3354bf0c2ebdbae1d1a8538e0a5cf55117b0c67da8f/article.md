# Introducing AI Presentation Review

Today we’re excited to launch AI Presentation Review! You can try it out now at[review.thorntale.com](https://review.thorntale.com/) .


“What exactly *is* AI Presentation Review?” you ask. Simple: it’s a way to practice.


Upload a PDF deck, give your presentation, and the selected **AI personas** will analyze the slides and transcript to provide feedback. At the moment, we’ve tuned personas to give you feedback from the perspective of an investor, a teacher, or a marketing lead (as well as a few entertaining extras).


The tool is totally free during beta, and works with any presentation (not only Thorntale presentations). Even if you don’t use Thorntale, you can still practice with Presentation Review.


Try it today, and go into your next presentation more prepared and more confident.
