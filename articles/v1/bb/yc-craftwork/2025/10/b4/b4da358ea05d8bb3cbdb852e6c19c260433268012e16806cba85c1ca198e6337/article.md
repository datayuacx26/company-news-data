# Color Drenching: Top 6 Monochromatic Paint Colors


Explore our favorite colors for the internet's new favorite maximalist trend


## **What is Color Drenching?**


Once again, we have another bold and transformative trend for 2024. What is it this time? Drenching your entire room in the same, fabulous color. The walls, woodwork, ceiling and even radiators or furniture. While bold single-color schemes are still in, there's a shift towards using Color Drenching to create a completely immersive experience once you step inside the room.


Since everything is painted in this one shade, color has never been more important.
