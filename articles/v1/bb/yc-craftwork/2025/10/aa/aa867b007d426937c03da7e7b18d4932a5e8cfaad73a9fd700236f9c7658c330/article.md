## Exterior Paint Color Suggestion


Although home buyers may not know the latest color trends, there are a few rules to help guide your decision-making process. When it comes to exterior paint colors, your home should have no more than 3 colors. We recommend the panels, stucco, or brick be one shade, the garage doors and trim be another, and the final shade should adorn your front door and shutters with just a pop of color.


If homes in your neighborhood all fall in a similar color palette or you have an HOA, it is best to use your existing colors or look to a neighbor a street over for inspiration. Confirm with your HOA if they have required shades for exterior paint colors.
