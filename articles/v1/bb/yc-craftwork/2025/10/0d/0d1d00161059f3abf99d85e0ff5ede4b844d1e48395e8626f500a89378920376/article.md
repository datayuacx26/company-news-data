# Cabinet Painting or Replacement? What’s Right for Your Charlotte Home


Here’s what Charlotte homeowners need to know — with expert advice from local designers and realtors.


## **TLDR**


**Question:** *Should I paint or replace my kitchen cabinets in Charlotte, NC?*


**Answer:** If your cabinets are in good shape and you’re not changing the layout, **painting is the better choice** — faster and more affordable. If your cabinets are damaged or you're planning a major remodel, **replacement makes more sense** for long-term value.


## **Should You Paint or Replace Your Cabinets?**


Homeowners across Charlotte — from Myers Park to NoDa — are upgrading their kitchens and baths, and one question keeps coming up: **Is it better to repaint or replace your cabinets?**


Let’s break it down with help from local designers, real estate agents, and painting pros.


## **Why Charlotte Homeowners Are Choosing to Paint**


Painting your cabinets is **cost-effective** , **faster** , and lets you keep your existing layout — a big plus in older Charlotte homes where craftsmanship and layout often shine.


**Benefits of Painting:**


-


60–75% less expensive than replacement


-


3–5 day timeline (vs. weeks for custom cabinetry)


-


Retains original layout and charm


-


Popular in areas like Dilworth and Plaza Midwood (pictured below)


[Greek Villa x Sherwin-Williams](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/white-paint-colors/sw7551-greek-villa)


## **When Replacement Is the Better Choice**


Cabinet replacement makes sense if:


-


The boxes are damaged or low quality


-


You’re changing the floor plan


-


You want to switch to a completely different style or material
