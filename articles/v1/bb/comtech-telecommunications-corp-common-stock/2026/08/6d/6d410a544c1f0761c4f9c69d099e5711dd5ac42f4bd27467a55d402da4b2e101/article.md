# Comtech’s High-Reliability Electronic Components for Space

As the global space sector accelerates—from rapidly expanding satellite constellations to increasingly ambitious exploration missions—the demand for dependable, high performance electronic components has never been greater. Extreme radiation, temperature fluctuations, vibration, and the impossibility of on orbit repair place extraordinary pressure on the smallest microelectronic parts. Comtech has spent decades addressing these challenges, delivering high reliability electrical, electronic, and electromechanical (EEE) components and specialized engineering services that empower mission success.


**[Comtech’s S&S Electronic Components Group](https://comtech.com/capability/eee-components-parts-for-space/)** has supported the global space community for 50 years—delivering high reliability microelectronics, testing services, and procurement for spacecraft and launch vehicle manufacturers. Comtech provides engineering, procurement, and qualification test services, all under a unified value added model. Mission teams can source hard to find components, mitigate technical risk, and streamline procurement through a single, expert partner. Comtech’s expertise is rooted in a legacy of supporting hundreds of successful missions. The team’s mission is clear: ensure that every component meets the stringent reliability requirements needed to withstand the harsh realities of space.


**Why These Components Matter?**
Microelectronics might be small, but their impact is enormous. Space systems depend on electronics that meet strict criteria across size, power efficiency, processing capability, radiation tolerance, temperature resilience, and fault tolerance. Comtech’s disciplined approach to part selection, traceability, and quality assurance ensures that every component supports long mission life and reliable performance.
Comtech’s proactive supply chain strategies, parts management systems, and long term storage solutions help customers maintain program schedules, and avoid costly redesigns caused by disappearing or counterfeit components.


**Modern Mission Requirements**
The space industry is undergoing transformation. Constellations are growing. Launch cadence is increasing. Missions are demanding longer lifetimes, more onboard processing, and greater resilience.
Comtech helps mission teams with solutions that include long term storage, custom screening, supply chain consolidation, complex BOM procurement, and parts availability planning that anticipates obsolescence risks.
Comtech’s experience in engineering high reliability components directly supports missions that require:
• radiation tolerant performance
• fault tolerant redundancy
• robust thermal and environmental resilience
• qualified, traceable component lifecycles
• support for both traditional and new space platforms
As missions become smaller, more efficient, and more advanced, reliable microelectronics remain essential to maintaining performance.


**Importance to the Market: Reliability that Scales with Innovation**
The industry’s evolution—including more commercial spacecraft, hybrid mission architectures, and emerging applications—means programs must balance innovation with dependable component supply. We help integrators, primes, and emerging space companies ensure that the smallest elements of their spacecraft are engineered, tested, and sourced with precision—so they can focus on designing the future of space.
