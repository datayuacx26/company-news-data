# REGENT Rising: Co-founder and CEO Billy Thalheimer on the latest progress

I get so incredibly hyped when I think about the awesome progress REGENT has made over the past few months.


Building and testing hardware in the real world is hard - so it makes it all the more sweet when things work.


We now have our[drone Seaglider - Squire -](https://www.regentcraft.com/news/regent-squire-seaglider-drone-flight-demonstration) doing fully autonomous operations and carrying payloads at military exercises. That represents a huge effort by our software and perception teams to get Squire reliably doing end-to-end missions itself.


We’ve gotten our large platform -[the Viceroy prototype](https://www.regentcraft.com/news/regent-2026-seaglider-test-campaign) - all the way up to takeoff speed at ~50 mph! The level of engineering precision, control redundancy, and safety architecture is just unbelievable.


And - in prep for our big next step of production - we opened our quarter-million sqft[manufacturing facility](https://www.regentcraft.com/news/regent-completes-worlds-first-seaglider-manufacturing-facility) . Right now its a big empty canvas. Soon we’ll fill it wall-to-wall with Seaglider production.


Team is rolling on all cylinders. This is why I got into this industry. Could not be more excited about what’s ahead.
