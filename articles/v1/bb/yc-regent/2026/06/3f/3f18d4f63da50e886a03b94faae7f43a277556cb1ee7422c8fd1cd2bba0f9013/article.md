# REGENT announces ‘The Twenty Five’ as first U.S. delivery and launch partner

**North Kingstown, RI, June 17, 2026** — REGENT Craft, the developer and manufacturer of Seaglider™ vessels, announced today that The Twenty Five, a luxury coastal travel operator and membership platform, will be its first delivery and launch partner for Seaglider operations in the Northeast and Southeast U.S.


REGENT previously[announced](https://www.regentcraft.com/news/regent-announces-seaglider-order-from-private-member-club-xxv-for-u-s-east-coast) that The Twenty Five had ordered 30 of the 12-passenger Viceroy Seaglider vessels for U.S. deployment on the U.S. East Coast, with an initial focus on the Northeast and Florida, serving routes including New York to the Hamptons, Boston to Nantucket, Palm Beach to Miami, and Miami to the Caribbean. The Twenty Five has since expanded its order for up to 60 vessels for these and other coastal destinations.


The Twenty Five will work with renowned maritime operator Hornblower to bring Seaglider operations to life. Hornblower is one of North America's largest and most experienced marine transportation and hospitality companies, operating ferry services, water taxis, and charter vessels across more than 100 ports and destinations throughout the U.S. and Canada.


> "We're excited to work with partners like The Twenty Five who share our commitment to efficient, convenient, and comfortable coastal travel," said Adam Triolo, VP of Commercial Business Development, REGENT. "This partnership marks a major milestone as we bring Seaglider operations to some of the most sought-after coastal routes in the country."


## **About REGENT**


REGENT is pioneering the future of maritime mobility through the development and manufacturing of Seaglider™ vessels — high-speed hydrofoiling wing-in-ground effect (WIG) craft that operate over the water within a wingspan of the surface, combining the speed of an aircraft with the convenience of a boat. A dual-use company headquartered in Rhode Island, REGENT has secured global commercial orders valued at more than $10 billion from leading airline and ferry operators around the world, as well as contracts with the U.S. Marine Corps. REGENT has raised more than $100 million from investors including 8090 Industries, Founders Fund, Japan Airlines, and Lockheed Martin. Learn more at www.regentcraft.com.
