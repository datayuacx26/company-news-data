# Meet us at FOSDEM, Brussels, Belgium

[FOSDEM](http://fosdem.org/) in Brussels is one of our favorite conferences each year as it fully reflects our love for Free and Open Source software.


We strongly believe in sharing knowledge not only internally, but also with the tech community around the world. This is one of the reasons why we support Open Source software through development and sponsorship. For example, we are the[second biggest supporter on Open Collective](https://twitter.com/opencollect/status/1218082459453136896) and we have a ton of our own Open Source projects too. (Check out our[Open Source page](https://tech.trivago.com/opensource/) for more info.)


If you’re also coming to FOSDEM (on the 1st and 2nd of February), we will have around 20 of our engineers attending this year. Our friend and colleague, Luca Pizzamiglio (present on[Twitter](https://twitter.com/pizzamig) and on[Github](https://github.com/pizzamig) ), will even be speaking about[Orchestrating Jails with Nomad and Pot](https://fosdem.org/2020/schedule/event/orchestrating_jails/) in the[BSD devroom](https://fosdem.org/2020/schedule/track/bsd/) .


We would love to talk about Frontend, Backend, UI/UX, and more – if you have the same interest, shoot us an emailtech@trivago.com or ping us on Twitter ([@trivago_tech](https://twitter.com/trivago_tech) ) to grab a coffee (or some other beverage of your liking)!


We are looking forward to meeting all of you!


See you this weekend.
