# Beyond trivago Tech Pt. 2: Four More Side Projects from Our Developers

Beyond trivago Tech Pt. 2: Four More Side Projects from Our Developers


This is part two of our series about trivago engineer’s side projects. The first part is[here](https://tech.trivago.com/2020/09/08/beyond-trivago-tech-pt.1-side-projects-from-our-developers/) .


As always, take a look and feel free to reach out to any of them if you’d like to exchange on any of their topics!


## Hello Rust


Our backend engineer Matthias Endler is running a[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/hellorust?sub_confirmation=1) about the Rust project as a side-project. In there he explains the ins and outs of the language, but his goal is not only to present information; rather, he’s striving to inspire fellow developers to try the language and stay curious. Each episode is between 15 and 30 minutes long and ranges from topics like idiomatic Rust over systems programming and foreign function interfaces.


If that sounds like something you’re into, go check out[his blog](https://endler.dev/) and the[website for the channel](https://hello-rust.show/) . Make sure to give him some feedback through[his Twitter account](https://twitter.com/matthiasendler) .


Follow[@matthiasendler](https://twitter.com/matthiasendler) and[@hellorustshow](https://twitter.com/hellorustshow) , and check out[Hello Rust](https://github.com/hello-rust/show) on Github.


## You Learn Better When You Teach


Being the son of two teachers, Tom Bartel has inherited the joy of passing on knowledge. He does so on his blog, which focuses not on code, but rather on communication and engineering management. For example, you can learn about[how to properly give critical feedback](https://www.tombartel.me/blog/a-primer-on-giving-critical-feedback/) or how to gracefully[watch your people become better than you in technical things](https://www.tombartel.me/blog/surpassed-gracefully/) .


However, Tom also enjoys teaching programming. He has a German[introductory course on Node.js at Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/course/node-js-professionell-und-komplett/) , where he explains the very fundamentals of creating a web server in Node, why it makes sense to use a framework, and how Promises really work. While creating this course, it became very evident to him that the best way to really learn something is to teach it to others.


Follow[@twbartel](https://twitter.com/twbartel)


## Smart Home Devices


One of our Site Reliability Engineers, Hendrik Maus, is literally having a life on a pony farm. The farm is powered by an on-premise Kubernetes cluster and is automated in many areas. For example, the power fences for the horse paddocks are monitored continuously using[ESP8266](http://esp8266.net/) microcontrollers and report their status to[Prometheus](https://prometheus.io/) which utilizes[Alertmanager](https://www.prometheus.io/docs/alerting/latest/alertmanager/) in order to ensure an uninterrupted service by notifying the inhabitants of anomalies.


Follow[Hendrik](http://github.com/hendrikmaus) on Github.


## Social Entrepreneurship During the Pandemic


Our data scientist and engineer,[Sayon Kumar Saha](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sayonkumarsaha/) , loves to immerse himself into the local tech-community and social-awareness events! His voluntary initiatives range from co-organizing monthly[Düsseldorf Data Science Meetups](https://www.meetup.com/Dusseldorf-Data-Science-Meetup/) to spreading technology and sustainability awareness with[TEDx Kassel](http://www.sayonkumarsaha.com/tedx-kassel/) .


Continuing the streak, he participated in the[EUvsVirus Hackathon](https://www.euvsvirus.org/) led by European Commission when the world went on lockdown beginning of 2020. He wrote a[blog post](http://www.sayonkumarsaha.com/corona-euvsvirus-hackathon-voicemed/) about his experiences. Since that successful hackathon, he continues to work part-time with[Voicemed](https://www.voicemed.io/) on the machine learning topic of[Covid-19 pre-diagnosis](https://youtu.be/gJA8tvhFRVY) with cough, breath and voice samples. He enjoys being part of the passionate group, coming remotely from across the globe to hack the crisis, and[shaping a health-startup](https://www.voicemed.io/about-us) out of a series of hackathons.


Follow[Sayon](https://twitter.com/crazy_lens) ’s blog at[www.sayonkumarsaha.com](http://www.sayonkumarsaha.com/) or his instagram at[crazy_lens](https://www.instagram.com/crazy_lens/) for more tech-community, photography and travel stories!
