# December Product Update

We've rolled out many major updates the past few months including: a brand-new dashboard page, code templating functionality, and much-improved exporting options.


# Dashboarding


A critical part of managing web data workloads is having a holistic understanding of the pipeline: how much data is coming in and where are failures arising. Without this, managing hundreds of sites can quickly become overwhelming.


We believe that keeping track of everything shouldn’t feel like a full-time job. That’s where our new dashboard comes in. It makes things simple, giving you a clear view of your web data pipeline in one place.


Here’s what you can do with it:


- See outputs over a specific time period.
- Track the total number of unique outputs and see how many pages were visited.
- Check the review status of all your websites.
- Keep tabs on the number of files downloaded - and more!


# Templates


When you're scraping websites at scale, you'll quickly notice something interesting: many sites rely on the same third-party platforms to host their data—think Shopify or WooCommerce for e-commerce.


You can now create and reuse templates from your existing code, rather than writing new Playwright scripts for each website.


# Exports


Previously, exports were buried at the individual website level, hidden deep within nested pages. This made it difficult to export data and was impossible to see full export history.


With our new dedicated export page, you can:


- Select the exact site(s) you want data from.
- Export the data in your preferred format - JSON or CSV.
- Pick a date to export data scraped after that day.
- Access export history.
- Redownload previously exported data.


All from one convenient page.
