# Reworkd Raises Seed Round, Bringing Total Funding to $4M+

We’re excited to announce our $2.75M Seed round, bringing our total funding raised to date to $4 million. Read the[full TechCrunch announcement here](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/24/reworkd-paul-graham-nat-friedman-daniel-gross-scrape-ai-agents/) .


We’ve partnered with some incredible investors in this round including (but not limited to): Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross (AI Grant), SVA, Paul Graham, General Catalyst, Y Combinator, Panache Ventures, Rebel Fund, and many others.


At Reworkd, we’ve been working at the application layer of LLM agents since their inception in early 2023. During this time, we’ve hit over 30k stars on GitHub, and over 1M users. This round of funding will help accelerate our efforts to deploy web agents at scale to retrieve high quality, structured data from the web.


# Our vision


*Our vision is to build the unified API for the internet* . We’re starting this vision through read access: enabling our customers to ingest data from hundreds of websites, regardless of whether they’re looking for text, images, documents, etc. We’re working with many companies today using our web data to:


- Train their own custom language models
- Build completely new data constrained product offerings
- Fortify existing datasets with domain specific data


There are many incumbent competitors today, but they focus *solely* on providing *infrastructure* for web data pipelines and leave the bulk of the engineering and maintenance burden on the end user. As a customer however, you shouldn't *have* to know what a headless browser or a residential proxy is. Nobody is building the exceptional product experience that gets customers from URLs to desired data as fast as possible. We want to change that.
