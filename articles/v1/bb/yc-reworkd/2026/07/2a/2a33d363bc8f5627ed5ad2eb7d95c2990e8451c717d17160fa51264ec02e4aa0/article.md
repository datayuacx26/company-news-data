# How Axis Automated Regulatory Data Scraping from 2,500+ Sites with Reworkd

# About Axis


Axis is a knowledge base of government regulations and officials, helping large companies expand into foreign markets with ease. For instance, if a business wishes to operate in Germany, Axis pinpoints the relevant laws they must follow and the officials from whom they must seek approvals. This simplifies compliance challenges and accelerates market entry.


# The Challenge


> “We’d been manually scraping data from a few regions using a third-party vendor, but it was still incredibly time-consuming,” says Mishaal, CEO of Axis. “When we decided to expand our market coverage into Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and North Africa — scraping regulations data from over 2,500 sites — our current vendor simply couldn’t keep up.”


For Axis, extracting accurate, up-to-date regulatory data was essential to building their core product. They needed a scalable solution that could handle data extraction from thousands of different websites while ensuring rigorous accuracy and reliability. The question became whether to build an in-house scraping infrastructure — an expensive undertaking outside of their core competency — or find a third-party solution capable of meeting their large-scale needs.


Scaling data scraping across multiple regions was already challenging, and ensuring complete accuracy made it even more complex. So, finding a platform that could meet both criteria proved difficult.


# Why Reworkd


After extensive research and evaluating multiple data-scraping providers, Axis chose Reworkd for its superior performance and competitive pricing.


Reworkd’s approach, which uses LLMs to dynamically generate and maintain Playwright scraping code for each site based on the given schema and the customer’s input, made it possible to easily scrape 2,500+ government websites for regulatory data.


As with any AI system, there can be some uncertainty. To address this, Reworkd also provides a fully managed solution that includes ongoing QA and maintenance of the generated scraping code and resulting data, removing any scraper maintenance burden from Axis’s internal team.


"The Reworkd team is extremely responsive, and their fully managed solution means we don’t have to worry about the quality assurance of our data. Combine that with their competitive pricing, and it was a no-brainer for us."
