# Verastem Oncology to Present at Jefferies Healthcare Conference

[PDF Version](https://investor.verastem.com/node/15936/pdf)


BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 27, 2026-- Verastem Oncology (Nasdaq: VSTM), a biopharmaceutical company committed to advancing new medicines for patients with RAS/MAPK pathway-driven cancers, today announced that its management team is scheduled to participate in a fireside chat at the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, at 8:10 am ET in New York.


A live webcast of the fireside chat can be accessed under “Events & Presentations” on the Company’s website at[www.verastem.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.verastem.com&esheet=54541637&newsitemid=20260527590882&lan=en-US&anchor=www.verastem.com&index=1&md5=e8930638c43e31f9818bfdd0e58cc1bc) . A replay of the webcasts will be archived on the website for approximately 90 days following the presentation.


**About Verastem Oncology**


Verastem Oncology (Nasdaq: VSTM) is a biopharmaceutical company committed to developing and commercializing new medicines to improve the lives of patients diagnosed with RAS/MAPK pathway-driven cancers. Verastem markets[AVMAPKI® FAKZYNJA® CO-PACK](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verastem.com%2Four-medicines%2F&esheet=54541637&newsitemid=20260527590882&lan=en-US&anchor=AVMAPKI%26%23174%3B+FAKZYNJA%26%23174%3B+CO-PACK&index=2&md5=e0396e9cf2857186c9327f6f387ea275) in the U.S. Our pipeline is focused on novel small molecule drugs that inhibit critical signaling pathways in cancer that promote cancer cell survival and tumor growth, including[RAF/MEK inhibition](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verastem.com%2Four-science%2Fraf-mek-fak-inhibition%2F&esheet=54541637&newsitemid=20260527590882&lan=en-US&anchor=RAF%2FMEK+inhibition&index=3&md5=cc04b1a93c1b2863383c7591eb90fa72) ,[FAK inhibition](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verastem.com%2Four-science%2Fraf-mek-fak-inhibition%2F&esheet=54541637&newsitemid=20260527590882&lan=en-US&anchor=FAK+inhibition&index=4&md5=d860418204e831ad066c40771f950215) , and[KRAS G12D inhibition](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.verastem.com%2Four-science%2Fkras-g12d-inhibition%2F&esheet=54541637&newsitemid=20260527590882&lan=en-US&anchor=KRAS+G12D+inhibition&index=5&md5=f82e311a1c795af90e6a9097e6f68136) .
