# How interoperability supports healthcare operations and learning

## A conversation with Kevin O'Hara


Poor interoperability and platform integration are becoming increasing liabilities as healthcare technology experts estimate these issues contribute to industry losses exceeding $8 billion annually.


With healthcare organizations having to manage everything from credentialing and scheduling to staff learning and development, creating an easy-to-navigate environment that empowers clinicians and improves patient care is essential.


Hear from experts to understand why interoperable platforms are important for health systems' success and learn:


- How interoperability affects efficiency and productivity
- Common industry pain points and how to address them
- Why learning management is crucial for staff retention and success
