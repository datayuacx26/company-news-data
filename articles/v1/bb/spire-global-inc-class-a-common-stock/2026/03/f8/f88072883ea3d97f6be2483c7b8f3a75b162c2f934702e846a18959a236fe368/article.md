## Amadeus vAPOC: Centralizing operations for enhanced efficiency


At its core,[Amadeus vAPOC](https://amadeus.com/en/airports/products/virtual-airport-operations-center-apoc) is a collaborative, cloud-based solution that provides airports a comprehensive operational plan and a unified, real-time view of the airport ecosystem. With native support for Microsoft Teams, Amadeus vAPOC enhances communication and coordination, ensuring that all stakeholders – airport operations, airlines and ground handlers – can respond swiftly and efficiently to dynamic operational challenges by accessing the same accurate, up-to-date information.


One of the most powerful aspects of Amadeus vAPOC is its ability to manage a rolling airport operations plan, allowing staff to anticipate and mitigate disruptions before they escalate. By consolidating critical operational data, the platform supports proactive decision-making, ultimately leading to improved passenger experiences, reduced delays, and enhanced efficiency across airport workflows.

## Leveraging real-time aircraft tracking data


To further enrich operational intelligence, Amadeus integrates a variety of external data sources into Amadeus vAPOC, including real‑time aircraft tracking from Spire Aviation. This enhanced data set gives airports greater visibility into flight movements, enabling more accurate monitoring, prediction, and response to operational developments, while ensuring staff have access to reliable, up‑to‑the‑minute flight information. Spire’s combination of multi-source ground and space-based data fusion, enables this capability for enhanced airport situational awareness, enabling teams to plan for potential disruptions, assess flight arrival and departure patterns more effectively, and optimize resource allocation.

## Key benefits for airports


By integrating real-time data and fostering seamless communication, Amadeus vAPOC offers a range of tangible benefits for airport operations, including:


- **Enhanced situational awareness:** A holistic, real-time view of airport activities allows staff to anticipate challenges and respond proactively.
- **Optimized resource allocation:** By leveraging predictive insights, airports can allocate staff, gates, and ground services more efficiently.
- **Improved coordination:** Centralized communications and data sharing ensure all stakeholders remain informed and aligned.
- **Reduced operational disruptions:** Proactive planning capabilities help airports mitigate delays and disruptions more effectively.
- **Future-ready functionality:** With ongoing advancements in AI and machine learning, Amadeus is exploring additional capabilities such as scenario-planning and intelligent forecasting to further enhance airport management.
