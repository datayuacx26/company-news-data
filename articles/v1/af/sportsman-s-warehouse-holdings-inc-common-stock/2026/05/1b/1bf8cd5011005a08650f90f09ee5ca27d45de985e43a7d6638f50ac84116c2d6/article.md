# Sportsman's Warehouse Holdings, Inc. Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Conference Call

## Sportsman's Warehouse Holdings, Inc. Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Conference Call


May 19, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.sportsmans.com/node/12106/pdf)


WEST JORDAN, Utah, May 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Sportsman's Warehouse Holdings, Inc. ("Sportsman's Warehouse" or the "Company") (Nasdaq: SPWH) today announced that it will hold its quarterly conference call to discuss first quarter 2026 financial results on Tuesday, June 2, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time.


A live audio webcast of the conference call will be available on the Company’s investor relations website at[http://investors.sportsmans.com/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=IQGXrLFglawiQWhG_tXd4r2RGXr4d-5j-xDMiO90Wo-YY9Z9Gm-07bNVFuOiwCEQ6TDf0wxQxQlU0Vj2fP2OKhIDhk5Dr46lGCAmpJh0yJyR4GZVLwyH6ydf1qYFKQpq) . A replay of the webcast will be available within two hours of the conclusion of the call through June 9, 2026, and can be accessed on the Company’s investor relations website.


**About Sportsman's Warehouse**
Sportsman's Warehouse is an outdoor specialty retailer focused on meeting the everyday needs of the seasoned outdoor veteran, the first-time participant, and everyone in between. We provide outstanding gear and exceptional service to inspire outdoor memories.
