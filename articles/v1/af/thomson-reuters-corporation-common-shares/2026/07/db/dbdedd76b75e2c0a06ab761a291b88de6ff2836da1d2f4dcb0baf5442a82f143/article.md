# CoCounsel case study: Civil Rights firm achieves 5x ROI

## **Outcomes: 5x ROI and a practice that scales without sacrificing quality of life**


The results have been transformative across every dimension of the practice. Justly Prudent achieved a 5x return on investment, with Howlette reporting, "For what we pay, my ROI easily was like five times what we were paying." The firm doubled its case capacity from 25 to over 50 cases without adding staff, while CoCounsel's document synthesis capabilities cut discovery and review time in half. Most remarkably, these efficiency gains translated into dramatically improved work-life balance—Howlette went from facing 16-hour workdays to maintaining reasonable hours while handling twice the caseload.


The entire team benefited from the implementation. Paralegals gained direct access to Westlaw, creating what Howlette calls, "a game changer when I look at ROI from their billable hours." The firm's ability to process discovery efficiently, prepare higher-quality work product, and handle greater volume translated directly into increased revenue and client satisfaction. With a diverse revenue model spanning hourly billing, hybrid contingency, and subscription-based transactional services, Justly Prudent has operated at full capacity for over a year—a testament to CoCounsel's ability to help the firm triage and accept more cases without compromising quality or overburdening staff.


Howlette's tech-forward approach has become a competitive advantage in the civil rights litigation space. His message to colleagues is unequivocal. "Do not wait. You have to get on board. If you don't start now, you're going to get left behind."


The firm's success demonstrates that even solo practitioners can compete with large established firms by embracing AI-powered tools early, training teams thoroughly, and letting technology handle routine tasks while attorneys focus on high-value strategic work. As Howlette affirms, "We would not be able to do the work we do now without these tools."
