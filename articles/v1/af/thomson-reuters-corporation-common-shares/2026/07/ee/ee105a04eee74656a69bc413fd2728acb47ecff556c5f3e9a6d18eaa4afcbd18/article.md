# CPA firm accelerates audits with CoCounsel Tax & Audit

## **The solution: Secure, accounting-focused AI that fits real audit and tax work**


After ongoing outreach from Thomson Reuters and rising industry "chatter," the firm conducted their due diligence, including speaking with a 100‑person peer firm whose successful test led to an all‑firm rollout. That endorsement helped resolve internal debate about timing and accelerated adoption. Security was also a deciding factor as George explains, "One of the biggest selling points is around the security of CoCounsel, the fact that it's an enclosed LLM, that it has the SoC certification so that we can load up whatever we wish into the platform."


Just as importantly, the curated research foundation aligned to tax and audit work, George states "Because CoCounsel is so enclosed with a massive amount of curated research material, I know that I have the best tool to evaluate taxes, financial statements, accounting services."


Implementation began with clear direction for the audit practice. George tasked all his auditors to use it to analyze and summarize all contract types and board minutes, and to use that summary as part of their workpapers. Day to day, partners and managers use CoCounsel to validate technical positions across tax and financial reporting, replacing time‑consuming title lookups and enabling quick, cited confirmations before responding to clients. The firm continues to learn prompting nuances and expand scenarios, early usage spans financial‑statement analysis, lease reviews that feed into the team's existing calculation tools, and nonprofit‑related research, including Form 990 questions.
