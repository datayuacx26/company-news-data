# How Paul Stopped Stressing About Credit Cards with Atlas

Most people check their credit card. Paul forgets about his.


That's not a complaint. It's the whole point. Paul signed up for Atlas because he wanted a card that would do the work in the background: pay his bills, set money aside, earn him cash back at the places he already shops. He didn't want to have to feel stressed about remembering to pay his balances.


He got exactly that. A card that runs itself.


> "I don't have to think about my Atlas card. Money just goes from where I need to go."


### A Credit Card That Pays Itself with Smart Pay


For Paul, the appeal of Atlas wasn't a flashy feature. It was the absence of one. Smart Pay handles his balance automatically from his direct deposit, so there's no logging in to make a payment, no calendar reminders, no late fees.


### Smart Save Builds Up Savings in the Background


Paul also uses Smart Save, which moves a small amount into savings every time he gets paid. He doesn't set a target or move money manually. *‍*


> *"It just throws something aside for you when you're not thinking about it."*


Over time, that adds up to what Paul calls his "fun money." He even won a sweepstakes through Atlas.


### Cash Back at the Pump, Plus Built-In Phone Coverage


Paul uses his Atlas Card most often at gas stations and convenience stores, where he earns cash back on everyday spending. He also pays his cell phone bill with his Atlas Card, which comes with built-in phone protection as part of his Atlas membership.


> *"I get cash back at convenience stores and gas stations."*


### A Stress-free Way to Manage Money


For Paul, Atlas isn't about big financial moves. It's about removing small daily decisions. The bills get paid. The savings build up. The cash back accumulates.


That's what stress-free actually looks like. A feeling of having one less thing on your plate. *‍*


> *"It thinks for you if you want a card that's stress-free."*


If you'd rather spend your attention on anything other than your credit card, see what Atlas can do for you.
