# Farmers

## Delivering a Difference: Farming


Domino’s understands that farmers, like wheat, dairy and tomato farmers, are fundamental to our business. We are proud to give directly to organizations that impact and strengthen the agriculture industry, and that promote the success and well-being of farmers.


### Future Farmers of America


Domino’s has donated $2 million to the National Future Farmers of America (FFA) Organization since the partnership launched in 2017. FFA is an intracurricular organization that prepares students for success in the agriculture industry by providing vital education and leadership experience.


In 2024, Domino’s expanded its sponsorship of FFA to strategically address the nonprofit’s needs. Domino’s increased annual donation will fund programs designed to support future and current agriculture educators, and initiatives exposing students to the Food Processing & Products career pathway.


[Read More about Domino’s support of FFA](http://biz.dominos.com/stewardship/national-ffa-organization/)


### American Farmland Trust


Domino’s helps farmers across the nation by working with American Farmland Trust (AFT) and its Brighter Future Fund. AFT takes a holistic approach to agriculture through their focus on the conservation and management of rural land.


The Brighter Future Fund creates opportunities for underserved and underrepresented farmers by investing in the viability of their farms and ranches. This fund provides grants to help farmers grow and sustain their work, strengthens local food systems, educates on regenerative farming practices, and assists a variety of farmers, ranging from community garden farmers to large scale cattle farmers. Since 2022, Domino’s support has funded grants for more than 25 farmers. To hear about the impact of the Brighter Future Fund on a local farmer,[click here.](https://media.dominos.com/stories/aft-bff/?ref=biz.dominos.com#:~:text=This%20year,%20Dazmonique%20Carr,%20a%20local%20urban%20farmer%20in%20Detroit,)


### The Nature Conservancy


In 2024, Domino’s launched a relationship with The Nature Conservancy (TNC) to support its North America Dairy Program, reaching dozens of farms across Michigan, Wisconsin and Idaho. The Nature Conservancy collaborates with farmers on implementing regenerative agricultural practices. We look forward to sharing updates as TNC’s program progresses.


### National Dairy FARM Program and FARM ES


Domino’s committed to donate a total of $100,000 in 2024 and 2025 to the National Dairy Farmers Assuring Responsible Management (FARM)™ Program, to support the updates and rollout of a tool known as FARM ES (Environmental Stewardship). The tool empowers farmers to enter data for a more accurate carbon footprint, and conduct scenario analysis, which provides farmers with more informed decision-making on adoption of practices and technologies.
