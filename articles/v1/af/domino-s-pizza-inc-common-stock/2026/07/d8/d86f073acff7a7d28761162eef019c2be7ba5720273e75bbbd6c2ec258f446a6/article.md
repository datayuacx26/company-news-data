# Customer Testimonials


> It has been a pleasure to partner with Dominos during the school year. We currently serve Dominos Smart Slice to our Middle and High Schools and the students always look forward to their biweekly menu rotation of the Smart Slice program. We currently serve Cheese Pizza and Pepperoni Pizza and look forward to implementing additional options in the future. The customer service that my team and I have received from Dominos has been phenomenal. The leadership team has always responded promptly, communicated effectively, and worked hard to correct any concerns as soon as possible. Also, Dominos has been instrumental in their operational flexibility. Whenever schools are closed due to inclement weather, Dominos has been great at rescheduling pizza deliveries for the next business day (if possible) without any complaints. I look forward to working with Dominos in the future!


> Domino's Day is by far our largest meal count in our cycle menu. Those days are also our biggest source of ala carte revenue. We can always depend on Domino's Days.


> Domino’s Day is easily the most exciting day on our menu and students genuinely look forward to it each time it comes around. We’ve even seen one of our schools serve at full enrollment on Domino’s Day, which really highlights its popularity. Our elementary students especially love the cheese pizza, and the product is consistently high quality and tastes great. When feeding around 8,000 students daily, it can be challenging to find options that appeal broadly, but Domino’s Day consistently helps solve that by bringing in strong participation and enthusiasm. Overall, Smart Slice has been a very positive addition to our program, and we truly appreciate the partnership.
