# Charles Griffin speaks with Law360 about the Blue Cross Blue Shield settlement opt-outs

[Charles Griffin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/charles-griffin/) speaks with Law360 about the Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS) settlement opt-outs. Hospitals and other healthcare providers face a deadline on March 4, 2025, when they must decide whether to opt out of the class in the BCBS antitrust litigation, which alleges insufficient reimbursements to hospitals, physician groups and other healthcare providers.


Griffin says: “‘We think this gives hospitals and healthcare companies in general a very concrete example to discuss how legal finance can help their business, in terms of accelerating their recoveries and de-risking their claims…It helps the light bulb go off. ’”
