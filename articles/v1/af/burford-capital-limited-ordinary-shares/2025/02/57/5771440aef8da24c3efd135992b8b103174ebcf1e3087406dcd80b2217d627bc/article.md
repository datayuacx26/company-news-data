# Mergers & Acquisitions features Burford Capital

*Mergers & Acquisitions* features Burford's[Evan Meyerson](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/evan-meyerson/) in an article about the growth of private equity and institutional capital in the legal finance industry.


In the piece, Meyerson discusses the growth of legal finance among corporations: "'Our core audience had historically been law firms who understand the cost of litigating and the risks of litigating,' says Burford Managing Director Evan Meyerson. 'In the last five years, the pendulum has swung to investing more with corporate entities directly.'"
