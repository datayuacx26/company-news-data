# New York Law Journal features article on Burford's 15th anniversary research

*New York Law Journal* features an article by[Evan Meyerson](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/evan-meyerson/) on Burford's recent[15th anniversary research](https://www.burfordcapital.com/insights-news-events/insights-research/2024-research-legal-finance-at-15/) .


Meyerson writes: "Since its founding in October 2009, Burford has witnessed—and contributed to—significant changes in the business of law. Once niche, legal finance is now a critical tool for businesses and law firms across the globe, helping them make better financial and strategic decisions. To mark Burford’s 15th anniversary, we commissioned independent research to delve deeper into how legal finance is not only growing but also redefining the way businesses approach and manage risk."
