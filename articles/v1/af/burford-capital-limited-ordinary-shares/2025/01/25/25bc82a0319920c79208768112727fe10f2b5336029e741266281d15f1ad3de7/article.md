# Law360 Pulse: Legal Finance is entering 2025 on "solid ground"

Law360 Pulse features both Burford Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) and Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) , noting that legal finance is entering 2025 on "solid ground."


David Perla says that he is seeing an increasing number of large corporations "coming to him for advice about conditions in the U.S. legal landscape, 'the same way clients look to bankers to educate them about the markets.'"


Perla adds: "'I wouldn't say the industry is fully mature, but it's firmly in its adolescence now...More and more, corporations are using us more like a typical capital provider.'"
