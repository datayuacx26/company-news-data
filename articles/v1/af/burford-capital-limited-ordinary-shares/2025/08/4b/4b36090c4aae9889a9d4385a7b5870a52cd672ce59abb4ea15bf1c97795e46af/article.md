# IAM Strategy 300 recognizes Burford Capital's IP team members

Burford Capital is pleased to share that IAM Strategy 300 has recognized six Burford team members as world-leading IP strategists. These individuals have been " identified by market sources for their exceptional skill sets and profound insights into patent matters."
