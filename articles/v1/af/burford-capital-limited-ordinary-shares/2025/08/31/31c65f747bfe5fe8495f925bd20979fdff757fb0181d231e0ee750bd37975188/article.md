# Law360 covers Burford's approach to law firm equity investment in the UK

*Law360* writes about Burford's interest in seeking strategic minority stakes in UK law firms. The article includes insights from Chief Executive Officer[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) , as well as Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) , on Burford's approach to these investments and how it differs from that of private equity.


The article refers to Lenkner's comments that "while private equity investors usually aim to exit within five years, Burford takes a longer-term view."
