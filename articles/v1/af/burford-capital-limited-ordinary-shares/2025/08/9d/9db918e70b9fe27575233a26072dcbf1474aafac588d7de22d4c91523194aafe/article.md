# Reuters speaks with Travis Lenkner about law firm equity

Reuters speaks with Burford's Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) about Burford's discussions with US law firms about law firm equity partnerships.


Lenkner says: "This is law firms seeing what's happening in other jurisdictions, in other professions, and responding to the competitive challenges in their own market, and looking for a solution to the otherwise inefficiency of the partnership structure."
