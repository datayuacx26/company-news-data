# Jon Molot speaks with the Financial Times about law firm equity investments

In an article from the *Financial Times* entitled "Burford Capital seeks to buy equity stakes in US law firms," Burford Chief Investment Officer[Jonathan Molot](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jonathan-molot/) discusses the trend of law firm equity investments. He notes that these investments have precedent in other industries, saying, "It’s a crazy thing that the capital markets and the market for legal services have had no interaction historically, when law has grown up to be a multitrillion-dollar industry and there are US firms that are multibillion-dollar revenue businesses.”
