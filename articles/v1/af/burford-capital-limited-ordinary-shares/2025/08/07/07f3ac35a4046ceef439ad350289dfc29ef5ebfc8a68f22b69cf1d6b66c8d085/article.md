# The Law Society Gazette writes about Burford's desire to buy stakes in law firms

*The Law Society Gazette* writes about Burford's intent to make strategic minority investments in law firms, as discussed by Chief Investment Officer[Jonathan Molot](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jonathan-molot/) in the *Financial Times* .


Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) is also quoted within the piece: "As the world’s leading legal finance provider, we understand the business of law and the importance of law firm culture and professional independence. We are pursuing strategic minority investments in UK firms where we can support firms’ growth and expansion plans as a passive investor."
