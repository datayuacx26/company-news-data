# Law360 covers Burford's approach to law firm equity investment in the US

*Law360* writes about Burford's interest in seeking strategic minority stakes in US law firms.


In the article, Chief Development Officer Travis Lenkner says: "'We see interest from across the market, from some of the largest firms in the world and in the U.S...'Law firms are asking us about this; they are the ones driving demand for this type of structure,' he added. 'That's because they're exploring every possible avenue to stay competitive.'"
