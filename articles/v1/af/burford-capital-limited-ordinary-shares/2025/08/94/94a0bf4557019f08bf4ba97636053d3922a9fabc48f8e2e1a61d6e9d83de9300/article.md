# Travis Lenkner speaks with Bloomberg Law about Burford's intent to make strategic minority stakes in US law firms

Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) talks with Bloomberg Law about Burford's intent to acquire strategic minority investments in US law firms.


Lenkner says: "Burford is probably the most natural investor in this space,” Lenkner said. “We’re not structured predominantly as a private equity fund, so we can be a very patient, permanent investor in a structure like this."
