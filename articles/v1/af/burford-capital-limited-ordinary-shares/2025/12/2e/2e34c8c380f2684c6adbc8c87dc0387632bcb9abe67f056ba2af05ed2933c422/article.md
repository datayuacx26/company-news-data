# David Perla co-authors article in Carrier Management and Insurance Journal calling for clarity—not confrontation—between the legal finance and insurance industries

In an article entitled "From Confusion to Collaboration," Burford Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) and Parabellum Capital Managing Partner and Corporate Counsel Dai Wai Chin Feman co-author a piece for *Carrier Management* and *Insurance Journal* about how persistent misconceptions about claims costs and so-called "social inflation" highlight the need for clarity between insurers and the legal finance industry.


Read the article in *Carrier Management*[here](https://www.carriermanagement.com/features/2025/12/09/282156.htm) or *Insurance Journal*[here](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2025/12/12/850906.htm) .
