# Non-Billable writes about Burford's minority stake in Kindleworth

Non-Billable writes about Pallas Partners, and how the firm is "one of a growing number of boutiques to launch with institutional-style infrastructure rather than traditional law firm back offices."


The article notes that Pallas Partners worked "with legal consultancy Kindleworth on its setup, a model that has gained attention following litigation funder Burford Capital’s recent minority stake in Kindleworth as funders look for closer exposure to law firm economics."
