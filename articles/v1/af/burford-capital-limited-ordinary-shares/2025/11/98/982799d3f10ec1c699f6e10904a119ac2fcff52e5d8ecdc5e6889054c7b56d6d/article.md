# CDR features Philipp Leibfried on the CAT's collective action regime


November 26, 2025

*CDR* highlights growing consumer openness to class actions and notes that the UK regime is maturing, with experts pointing to recent landmark cases. Burford Capital’s[Philipp Leibfried](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/philipp-leibfried/) comments that the Competition Appeal Tribunal's (CAT) first-ever damages award in *Kent v Apple* marks a true “coming of age” for the system, signaling increasing confidence in collective redress.
