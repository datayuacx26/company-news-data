# Nikkei features Burford Capital

Nikkei interviews CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) , who notes that "AI will drive legal finance demand." Bogart focuses on the role of legal finance as it relates to intellectual property—especially in the technology and health care sectors—in the Asian market. Noting that current AI litigation is “largely copyright-related,” Bogart believes that the collateral effect of AI will drive incremental litigation volume, particularly for clients with portfolios of valuable intellectual property. Clients turn to Burford for capital and risk management solutions across the IP lifecycle, including infringement claims or portfolio monetization through licensing.
