# The American Lawyer interviews David Perla on how legal finance supports law firm succession planning

*The American Lawyer* speaks with Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) about the role of legal finance in law firm succession planning.


In the article, Perla says *:* “One of the reasons people use \[management services organizations or MSOs\] is to figure out how to ...transition a firm, particularly where you’ve got a couple of equity owners at the very top...Effectively, they want to know when they retire, they still have some equity value even without necessarily being partners in the law firm.”
