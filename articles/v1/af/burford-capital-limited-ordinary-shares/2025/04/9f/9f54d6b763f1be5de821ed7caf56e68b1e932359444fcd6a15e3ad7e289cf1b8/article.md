# Kate Tellez writes about patent monetization for New York Law Journal

Senior Vice PresidentKate Tellez writes for *New York Law Journal* about patent monetization and how businesses with significant IP are monetizing assets in their portfolios, in an article titled "Best Practices for Navigating Privilege and Work Product in Funded Patent Cases."


Tellez writes: "Overall, financed divestitures present a nuanced but navigable scenario worthy of careful planning and consideration in advance. As more corporations take advantage of financed divestitures and continue to monetize their patents, these considerations will undoubtedly become increasingly important."
