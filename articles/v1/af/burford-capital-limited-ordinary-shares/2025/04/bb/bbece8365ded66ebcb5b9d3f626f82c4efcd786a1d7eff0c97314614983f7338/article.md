# Lawdragon recognizes Burford on list of 100 Global Leaders in Litigation Finance

Lawdragon has named 14 Burford employees to its list of the 100 Global Leaders in Litigation Finance for 2025, making Burford once again the most represented company in the rankings.


Burford’s[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) ,[Jonathan Molot](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jonathan-molot/) ,[Aviva Will](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/aviva-will/) ,[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) ,[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) ,[Craig Arnott](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/craig-arnott/) ,[Elizabeth O'Connell](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/elizabeth-o-connell-cfa/) ,[Jeffery Commission](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jeffery-commission/) ,[Christiane Deniger](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christiane-deniger/) ,Andy Lundberg ,[Quentin Pak](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/quentin-pak/) ,[Emily Slater](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/emily-slater/) ,Emily Tillett and[Katharine Wolanyk](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/katharine-wolanyk/) all made the list.
