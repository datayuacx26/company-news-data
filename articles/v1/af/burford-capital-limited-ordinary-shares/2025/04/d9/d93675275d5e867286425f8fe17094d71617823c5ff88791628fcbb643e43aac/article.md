Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) is a guest on the "Financial Modeler’s Corner” podcast, where he discusses litigation risk modeling for finance professionals.


As the podcast description reads: "From managing long litigation timelines to leveraging Monte Carlo simulations and AI tools, David shares how Burford builds sophisticated models to guide investment decisions and mitigate risk."
