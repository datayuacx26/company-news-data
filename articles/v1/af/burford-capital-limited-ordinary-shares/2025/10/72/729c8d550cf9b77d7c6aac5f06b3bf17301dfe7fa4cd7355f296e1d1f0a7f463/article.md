# Law360 previews the inaugural International Legal Finance Association (ILFA) awards

Law360 highlights the inaugural International Legal Finance Association awards, which will be presented at the organization’s gala in London on November 13th.


Nearly 50 organizations will be recognized for excellence in key areas including collection action, commercial litigation, funder representation, advisory services, arbitration, technology and law firm innovation.


In the article, Burford Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) says, "'the evolution of legal finance has fundamentally transformed the business of law...What began as an innovative means of helping clients manage litigation costs has matured into a sophisticated and essential financial instrument that is reshaping how legal services are delivered, financed and valued...'"
