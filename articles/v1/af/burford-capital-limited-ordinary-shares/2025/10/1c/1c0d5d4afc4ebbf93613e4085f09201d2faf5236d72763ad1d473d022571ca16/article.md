# The American Lawyer highlights Travis Lenkner on the growing role of MSOs in legal services

*The American Lawyer* features a quote by Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) in relation to management services organizations, or MSOs, in legal services.


Lenkner says: "'Burford Capital is the world’s largest purpose-built investor in the legal industry. From our conversations with leading global firms, it’s clear that larger, more sophisticated firms are actively exploring how to use MSOs to secure long-term competitiveness.'"
