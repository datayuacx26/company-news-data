# The Korea Economic Daily discusses Burford's industry leadership

*The Korea Economic Daily* writes about Burford's leadership in the legal finance industry and quotes[Jonathan Molot](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jonathan-molot/) , citing his comments in this year's shareholder letter: "'Our asset modeling is based on a vast amount of proprietary data. This allows us to precisely measure the risk and potential returns of the litigation we invest in.' He added, 'We're not simply a lender; we're a technology-driven financial firm that quantitatively analyzes and prices legal risk.'"
