# David Lat's Original Jurisdiction features a conversation with Christopher Bogart

Following a conversation with CEO Christopher Bogart, Original Jurisdiction (by David Lat) features an article entitled "4 Biglaw Trends Shaping Litigation Finance." These trends are: "1. biglaw billing rates are continuing to accelerate; 2. how corporations are increasingly open to utilizing dispute-resolution processes, including litigation; 3. managed service organizations (MSOs) are on the rise; and 4. Some jurisdictions are exploring reforms to legal-ethics rules that would allow investment into law firms or the provision of legal services by non-lawyers."
