# ALM/Law.com reports that Burford Capital has launched operations in Spain

ALM/Law.com reports that Burford Capital has launched operations in Madrid, Spain.


Burford’s presence in Spain comes amid continued rising interest in legal finance, with businesses seeking alternative capital solutions to pursue meritorious claims without impacting liquidity. With an office in London and teams on the ground in five other European countries, Burford offers clients in Spain the benefit of local insight and cross-jurisdictional expertise.
