# Andy Lundberg writes for New York Law Journal about legal finance and insurance

*New York Law Journal* has featured a piece written byAndy Lundberg titled "Why Legal Finance Isn't the Villain Insurance Says It Is."


Lundberg writes: "Although the interests of legal financiers and insurers can conceivably conflict at the margins of commercial disputes, the core businesses of the two groups are largely unrelated, leaving insurers largely unaffected by commercial legal finance."
