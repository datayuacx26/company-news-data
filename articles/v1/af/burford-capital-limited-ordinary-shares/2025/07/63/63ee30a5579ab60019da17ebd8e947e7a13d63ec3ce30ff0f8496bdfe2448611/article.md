# Christopher Bogart speaks with The American Lawyer about Burford's recent completion of a $500 million debt offering

Burford CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) speaks with The American Lawyer about Burford's recent completion of a $500 million debt offering.


Bogart shares that the debt offering was "'substantially oversubscribed, reflecting strong demand and market confidence in Burford’s performance.'"


Bogart also says that Burford "is likely going to be broadening its participation in the legal ecosystem. Bogart "notes that litigation funding has always been a core part of its business, 'and what we have signaled is a willingness to also participate in some of the other things that go into making litigation happen.'"
