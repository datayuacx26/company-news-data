# Christopher Bogart quoted in GAR on UK court rejecting post-PACCAR challenge

CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) is quoted in a Global Arbitration Review (GAR) article entitled “UK appeal court rejects post-PACCAR challenge to funding agreements.”


Bogart says: “‘The Court of Appeals’ decision was significant not only for cabining the impact of PACCAR and ensuring the availability of at least some litigation financing in the UK, but also for its thorough analysis and acceptance of the kinds of economic terms that are necessary to unlock capital for use in the English courts.’”
