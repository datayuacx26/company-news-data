# The Korea Times speaks to Burford about the patent monetization opportunity in South Korea

*The Korea Times* features an interview with[Christopher Freeman](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-freeman/) ,[Eric Carlson](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/eric-carlson/) and[Quentin Pak](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/quentin-pak/) during their recent trip to Seoul.


The article discusses patent monetization trends in South Korea and the opportunity for companies in the region to maximize their patent portfolios.


Freeman says: ""All other top companies in Korea are already doing it. So even though you're not already thinking about this, it's a revenue stream you should be thinking about unlocking."
