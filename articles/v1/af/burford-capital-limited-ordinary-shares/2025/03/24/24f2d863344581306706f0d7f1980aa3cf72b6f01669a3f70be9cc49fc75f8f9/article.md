# Leaders League recognizes Burford's legal finance expertise in the US, UK and Europe

We are proud to announce that Leaders League has recognized Burford Capital in the "leading" tier for arbitration finance in the US and UK, as well as for litigation finance in the US.


Additionally, our litigation finance presence in the UK and Europe has been ranked in the "excellent" tier.
