# CDR speaks with Travis Lenkner about Burford's Kindleworth investment

CDR speaks with Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) about Burford's recent strategic minority investment in Kindleworth. Lenkner says of the investment announcement: "Every announcement talks about synergies and strategic alignment, but \[in this case it\] has the benefit of being very true as they are in a space that is exciting and represents a big part of the future of legal services."
