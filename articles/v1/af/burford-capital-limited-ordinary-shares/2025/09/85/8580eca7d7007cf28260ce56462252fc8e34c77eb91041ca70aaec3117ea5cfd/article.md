# Travis Lenkner speaks with Legal Futures about recent Kindleworth investment

*Legal Futures'* coverage of Burford's Kindleworth investment includes insight from Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) about how Burford's goals with law firm equity investments differ from those of private equity firms.


Lenkner says: "An investor that can be passive and patient is far better for those firms than getting on a conveyor belt where the interest that one firm holds today has to be exited in a certain number of years and sold to someone else in a process that can’t be stopped."
