# Burford contributes a chapter in Concurrences Competition Law Review

*Concurrences Review* ** has included a new article authored by Charles Griffin and Alyx Pattison as part of its latest issue on Antitrust Third-Party Litigation Funding.


The chapter explores why legal finance is increasingly central to the growth of funded opt-out claims in


[antitrust litigation](https://www.burfordcapital.com/what-we-do/disputes-we-finance/antitrust-competition/) , including:


-


The cost and complexity of pursuing high-stakes antitrust litigation and how legal finance levels the playing field


-


The recent increase of


[corporate opt-outs](https://www.burfordcapital.com/insights-news-events/burford-quarterly/2025-issue-1/hospital-antitrust-opt-outs/) , with companies seeking greater control over litigation strategy and potential recoveries


-


Findings from Burford’s


[2025 survey](https://www.burfordcapital.com/insights-news-events/insights-research/2025-opt-out-claims-survey/) of in-house counsel, which analyze the financial incentives to opt outing and the barriers (particularly cost and risk) that deter many companies from pursuing claims directly


-


How legal finance, including


[fees and expenses financing](https://www.burfordcapital.com/what-we-do/fees-expenses/) and


[monetization](https://www.burfordcapital.com/what-we-do/monetization/) structures, provides solutions that enable companies to pursue stronger recoveries without straining business resources.


-


Case studies showing how Burford has partnered with companies to


[unlock capital from pending opt-out claims](https://www.burfordcapital.com/what-we-do/case-studies/case-study-de-risking-pursuit-of-opt-out-claims/) and


[support broader corporate goals](https://www.burfordcapital.com/what-we-do/case-studies/case-study-reallocating-cash-from-claim-to-investment/) .


The full chapter can be found


[here](https://www.concurrences.com/fr/review/issues/no-9-2025/dossier/antitrust-third-party-litigation-funding/x-antitrust-third-party-litigation-funding-part-x-business-considerations) .
