# WSJ PE Pro speaks with Burford CEO Christopher Bogart

WSJ PE Pro features Burford CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) in an article about Burford's interest in minority law firm investments. The article states that "Bogart is confident the U.S. will eventually permit outside investment in law firms, as the U.K. has long done."


Bogart says: "'It seems odd that law is not able to do what other businesses are able to do, which is to access the very deep capital markets that exist in the U.S.'"
