# The Lawyer reports on Burford's Kindleworth investment

*The Lawyer* covers Burford's recently announced minority investment in Kindleworth, combining Burford's global reach and legal expertise with Kindleworth's track record of empowering top legal talent.


In the article, Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) says: "'We are...exploring investment across the UK and the US and around the world, as part of our announced commitment to finding additional ways to generate revenue for Burford, within the legal services industry.'"
