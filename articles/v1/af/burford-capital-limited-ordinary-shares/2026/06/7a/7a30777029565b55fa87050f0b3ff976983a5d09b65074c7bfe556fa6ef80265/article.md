# Lawdragon profiles Evan Meyerson as he discusses the evolution of legal finance

Lawdragon profiles[Evan Meyerson](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/evan-meyerson/) , managing director and head of U.S. commercial investments at Burford Capital, examining the continued evolution of legal finance and its growing role in helping companies and law firms manage risk, pursue claims and access capital. The conversation explores how the industry has matured, the expanding range of financing solutions available to clients and the increasing recognition of legal claims as valuable business assets.
