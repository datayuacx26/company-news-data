# Patrick Savage discusses the future of law firm investments on Non-Billable

The rise of private equity and alternative investment models is reshaping conversations about how law firms fund growth, attract talent and compete in a changing market. On *The Non-Billable Podcast* , Burford Capital Vice President[Patrick Savage](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/patrick-savage/) explains why Burford's permanent capital model is distinct from traditional private equity and how that longer investment horizon aligns with industry trends such as consolidation and AI that are likely to play out over decades.
