# David Perla on The Future Is Bright Podcast

As technology accelerates change across the legal sector, law firm leaders are being forced to rethink everything from talent pipelines and workforce planning to growth strategies and firm financing. On *The Future Is Bright* podcast, Burford Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) discusses how AI, permanent capital and shifting market dynamics are challenging long-held assumptions about how law firms grow, compete and prepare for the future.


As written in the episode description: "Perla's advice is deceptively simple. Get curious. Ask hard questions of people who think differently. The firms that navigate this moment well are the ones willing to challenge assumptions before the market forces the issue."
