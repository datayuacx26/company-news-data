# Chambers Litigation Support 2026 Guide recognizes Burford and its global team

The Chambers 2026 Litigation Support rankings are out, and Burford has ranked Band 1 across seven categories: US litigation funding, UK litigation funding, Europe litigation funding, Middle East litigation funding, US intellectual property, asset tracing & recovery and international arbitration.


Congratulations to[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) ,[Aviva Will](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/aviva-will/) ,[Katharine Wolanyk](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/katharine-wolanyk/) ,[Jörn Eschment](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/joern-eschment/) ,[Eric Carlson](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/eric-carlson/) ,[Chris Freeman](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-freeman/) ,[Quentin Pak](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/quentin-pak/) ,[Jeffery Commission,](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jeffery-commission/)[Charlie Rooke](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/charlie-rooke/) and[Joe Durkin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/joe-durkin/) for being recognized.


See[here](https://chambers.com/legal-guide/litigation-support-58) for the complete rankings and individual rankings.
