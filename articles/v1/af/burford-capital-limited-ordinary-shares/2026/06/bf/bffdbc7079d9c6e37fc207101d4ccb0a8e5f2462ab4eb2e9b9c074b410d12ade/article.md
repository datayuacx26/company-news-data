# Law360 reports on growing demand for law firm equity investments

Law360 reports on growing interest in law firm equity investments as firms explore new approaches to capital, growth and long-term strategic planning. The article highlights comments from COO[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) , who notes that market activity around these investment structures has accelerated in recent years and is likely to continue as firms reassess traditional funding and ownership models in response to broader changes across the legal industry.
