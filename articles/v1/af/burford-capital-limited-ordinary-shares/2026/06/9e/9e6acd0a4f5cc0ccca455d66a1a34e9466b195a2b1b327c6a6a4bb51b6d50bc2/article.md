# The new reality of litigation: Higher fees, tighter budgets


June 11, 2026


**Philipp Leibfried & Evan Meyerson**


## Summary


Rising litigation costs are reshaping how corporate legal departments think about disputes. What was once viewed primarily as a question of managing outside counsel spend has instead become a broader strategic consideration of capital allocation, risk management and the economic value of legal claims themselves.
