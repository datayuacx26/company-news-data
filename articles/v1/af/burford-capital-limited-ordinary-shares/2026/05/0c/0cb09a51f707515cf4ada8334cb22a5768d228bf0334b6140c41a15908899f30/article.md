# Solicitors Journal reports on Travis Lenkner's appointment as Chief Operating Officer

Solicitors Journal reports that[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) has been appointed Chief Operating Officer, where "he will be responsible for overseeing execution and operating performance across the firm's various business units."


In the article,[Chris Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) says: “Travis’s expanded role as Chief Operating Officer strengthens the firm and underscores the depth of leadership we have built across the business. He has built a strong partnership with our Chief Financial Officer,[Jordan Licht](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jordan-licht/) , and together they will co-chair our Operating Committee, driving execution and continued growth across the business, while[Jon Molot](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jonathan-molot/) and I will continue to focus on portfolio performance and overall strategy.”
