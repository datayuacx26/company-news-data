# Burford promotes Travis Lenkner and Carrie Tendler, reports CDR

CDR reports that Burford Capital has promoted Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) to Chief Operating Officer and elevated Special Counsel[Carrie Tendler](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/carrie-tendler/) to Managing Director and Head of Asset Recovery. CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) says the pair “have quickly established themselves as strong business leaders,” as Burford continues to expand its leadership bench across key areas of the business.
