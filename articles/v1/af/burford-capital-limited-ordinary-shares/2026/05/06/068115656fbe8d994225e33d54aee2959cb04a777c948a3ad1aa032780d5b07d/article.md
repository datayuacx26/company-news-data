# Law360 reports Travis Lenkner's promotion to Chief Development Officer

Law360 reports that Burford Capital has promoted Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) to Chief Operating Officer, expanding his role overseeing execution and operating performance across the business. The article also notes the promotion of[Carrie Tendler](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/carrie-tendler/) to Managing Director and Head of Asset Recovery, as Burford continues to build out its senior leadership team.
