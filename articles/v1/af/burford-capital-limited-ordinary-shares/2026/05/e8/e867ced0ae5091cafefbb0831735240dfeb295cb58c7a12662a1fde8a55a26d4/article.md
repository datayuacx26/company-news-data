# Spain’s litigation finance market gains momentum, reports El Confidencial

Spain is emerging as an increasingly attractive market for litigation finance, driven by its growing role as an arbitration hub and rising demand for alternative legal funding solutions. In an interview with *El Confidencial* , Burford CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) and Madrid-based Senior Vice President[Teresa Gutiérrez Chacón](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/teresa-gutierrez-chacon/) discuss the evolution of the market and the significant opportunities they see ahead.
