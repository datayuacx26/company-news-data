# New York Law Journal features Greg McPolin and Charles Griffin on litigation strategy in a capital-constrained healthcare market

Writing in *New York Law Journal* ,[Greg McPolin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/greg-mcpolin/) and[Charles Griffin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/charles-griffin/) examine how healthcare organizations are approaching litigation strategy in a challenging financial environment. The article explores how legal finance can help providers pursue affirmative recoveries while preserving capital and maintaining focus on core operations.
