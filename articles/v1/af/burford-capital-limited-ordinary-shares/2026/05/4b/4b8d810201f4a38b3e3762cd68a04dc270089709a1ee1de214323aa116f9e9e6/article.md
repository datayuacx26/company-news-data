# Legal 500 ranks Burford Tier 1 in US Litigation Funding and Asset Recovery

Burford has earned Tier 1 rankings in both US Litigation Funding and Global Asset Recovery, underscoring its leadership in the legal finance market.


The US Litigation Funding report highlights that Burford "excels in the financing of high-value, complex disputes, including antitrust and competition claims, and bankruptcy and insolvency matters."


Meanwhile, the Global Asset Recovery report recognizes Burford as the "leading provider of legal finance with an in-house global asset tracing and enforcement capability that supports clients in the enforcement of major judgments and awards worldwide."
