# Elizabeth Shin and Quentin Pak write for GAR's Asia-Pacific Arbitration Review 2027

In the latest edition of GAR’s *Asia-Pacific Arbitration Review 2027* ,[Elizabeth Shin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/elizabeth-shin/) , Senior Vice President, and[Quentin Pak](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/quentin-pak/) , Director, authored a chapter on “ *South Korea’s legal finance evolution: unlocking capital for cross-border disputes and arbitration* .”


The chapter explores how South Korea’s increasing exposure to international arbitration and cross-border litigation is driving the evolution of legal finance, highlighting key regulatory developments, the emergence of local funders and the growing role of funding within Korea’s rapidly developing disputes market.


You can find the full chapter[here](https://globalarbitrationreview.com/review/the-asia-pacific-arbitration-review/2027/article/south-koreas-legal-finance-evolution-unlocking-capital-cross-border-disputes-and-arbitration) .
