# Podcast: Burford Briefings - Biglaw's Embrace of Contingency Litigation

In a recent Burford Briefing, “Biglaw’s Embrace of Contingency Litigation,” David Lat moderated a discussion on the changing economics of legal services and the growing role of affirmative litigation in law firm strategy. The conversation featured insights from Eva Cole (Partner, Winston Taylor), Avi Weitzman (Co-Chair of Complex Litigation and Arbitration, Paul Hastings) and Evan Meyerson (Managing Director, Burford Capital).


Recorded live at the Harvard Club of New York City on May 6, the discussion is now available as a special episode of the Original Jurisdiction podcast.
