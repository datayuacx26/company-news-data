# The Korea Times profiles Burford’s expansion in Korea

*The Korea Times* reports on the growing demand for sophisticated legal finance solutions in Korea, alongside Burford Capital’s appointment of[Elizabeth Shin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/elizabeth-shin/) as Senior Vice President and Head of Korea.


The article highlights opportunities in high-value patent disputes, complex arbitration and cross-border commercial matters as Korean companies play an increasingly prominent role on the global stage.
