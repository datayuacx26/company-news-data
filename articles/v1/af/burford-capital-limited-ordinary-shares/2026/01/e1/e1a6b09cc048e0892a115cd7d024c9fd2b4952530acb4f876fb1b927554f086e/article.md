# Law.com reports on Burford's hiring of Head of Korea amid rising demand for legal finance

*Law.com International* covers Burford's hiring of[Elizabeth Shin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/elizabeth-shin/) as Senior Vice President and Head of Korea as a response to rising demand for sophisticated legal finance in the Korean market. The article highlights Shin’s background in international arbitration and cross-border disputes and explores how Korea’s growing role in global patent and commercial matters is shaping Burford’s expansion strategy in Asia.
