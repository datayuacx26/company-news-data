# Law360 covers Burford’s Korea hire as part of broader growth strategy

**Law360** reports on Burford's appointment of[Elizabeth Shin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/elizabeth-shin/) as Senior Vice President and Head of Korea, situating the hire within the firm’s wider push to expand its geographic footprint. The article highlights Shin’s focus on capital-intensive, cross-border disputes involving Korean companies, and includes commentary from Managing Director of Burford Asia[Quentin Pak](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/quentin-pak/) on the growing use of legal finance as a strategic tool for managing complex litigation risk.
