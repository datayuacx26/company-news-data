# Non-Billable covers Kindleworth's plans for US launch

Speaking on *The* *Non-Billable Podcast,* the founders of Kindleworth set out plans to launch its first US office on the East Coast, pointing to growing frustration in Big Law and more favorable conditions for partners spinning out to form specialist boutiques. The founders highlight how improved access to capital and technology, including backing from Burford, has made law firm start-ups faster and less risky.
