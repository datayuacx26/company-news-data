# Litigation Finance Insider speaks with David Perla and Evan Meyerson about 2026 legal finance trends

The latest edition of the *Litigation Finance Insider* newsletter surveys legal finance leaders on how litigation funding is evolving as the industry enters 2026, with a sharper focus on discipline, duration risk and realized performance.


Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) highlights the role of technology as decision-support rather than a substitute for legal judgment, while Managing Director[Evan Meyerson](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/evan-meyerson/) points to growing appetite for more creative, structured funding solutions that expand access to capital without disrupting firms’ core business models.
