# The Korea Economic Daily reports on rising demand for litigation finance in cross-border disputes

*The Korea Economic Daily* , or *Hankyung* , reports that litigation finance is emerging in Korea as companies increasingly look to external capital to fund international disputes. The entry of global providers is expected to reshape how Korean businesses approach these matters.


By enabling companies to finance legal costs and mitigate risk, the model is gaining traction as part of a broader shift in how disputes are financed and managed.
