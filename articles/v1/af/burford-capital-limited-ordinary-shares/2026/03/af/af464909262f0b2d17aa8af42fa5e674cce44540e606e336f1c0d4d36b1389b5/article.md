# Los Angeles Times reports that mid-market firms explore new funding paths to compete and scale

*Business by LA Times Studio* , The


*Los Angeles Times* ' business magazine reports that mid-sized law firms are increasingly turning to outside capital to compete, recruit and retain talent, invest in technology and support growth. As traditional models come under pressure, alternative funding structures are gaining traction.


Burford Managing DirectorAndy Lundberg says that legal finance gives mid-sized firms the ability to compete both with large firms operating on hourly models and with those able to deploy contingency at scale.
