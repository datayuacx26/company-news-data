# Craig Arnott and Charlie Rooke unpack UK housebuilders claim in GCR

Burford’s Chief Investment Officer - International[Craig Arnott](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/craig-arnott/) and Director[Charlie Rooke](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/charlie-rooke/) were featured in GCR regarding a proposed UK class action against leading housebuilders, offering insights into the case and the evolving collective actions landscape.


Rooke pointed to questions around how competition investigations define the scope and duration of alleged conduct, while Arnott noted that the UK remains an attractive forum for class actions despite recent rulings, highlighting the importance of a clear framework: “The most important thing is to have certainty because then you can work out the level of risk attached to these matters.”
