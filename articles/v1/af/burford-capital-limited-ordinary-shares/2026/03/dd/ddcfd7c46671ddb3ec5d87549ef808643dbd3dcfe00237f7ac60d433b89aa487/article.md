# Travis Lenkner featured in Legal Business Magazine on law firm equity

*Legal Business* reports that Burford Capital’s Chief Development Officer,[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) , sees external investment playing an increasingly important role in the legal sector—while stressing that Burford’s model is distinct from traditional private equity. The piece highlights rising interest from top-tier firms and the growing recognition that equity investment is becoming a key part of the profession’s future.
