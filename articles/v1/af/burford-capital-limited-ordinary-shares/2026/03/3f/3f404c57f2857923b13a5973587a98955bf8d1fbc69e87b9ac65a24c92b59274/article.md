# Jeffery Commission featured in Mining.com on arbitration finance

*Mining.com* features a video of[Jeffery Commission](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jeffery-commission/) discussing how arbitration finance has moved into the mainstream, particularly as mining companies face rising disputes driven by geopolitical tensions and tighter regulation. He explains how legal finance is increasingly used not just to fund costly investor-state and commercial arbitrations, but also to manage balance sheet risk and unlock value from awards.
