# CDR covers Burford’s 39% increase in new definitive commitments in FY25

Burford Capital’s FY25 results reflect continued growth in new business and a portfolio positioned for long-term value creation, according to reporting by CDR.


“The quality of the portfolio remains high, and we believe the future is bright in terms of growing the business and the potential for asymmetric upside value for shareholders," said Burford CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) on Burford's[Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 earnings call](https://investors.burfordcapital.com/events-presentations/upcoming-archived/event-details/2026/3Q25-Financial-Results-Conference-Call/default.aspx) , which occurred on February 26, 2026.
