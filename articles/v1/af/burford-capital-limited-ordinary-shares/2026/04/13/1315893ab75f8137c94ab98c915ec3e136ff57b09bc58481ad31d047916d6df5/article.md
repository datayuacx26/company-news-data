# ALM examines alternative investment models in Big Law

ALM reports on growing interest in minority investment structures for law firms in the U.S. and U.K., as traditional private equity approaches face structural challenges in partnership-based firms. Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) describes a model centered on long-term capital, where investors participate through profit distributions, aligning more closely with law firm economics and governance.
