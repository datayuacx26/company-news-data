# Original Jurisdiction reports on traditionally defense-focused Big Law firms entering plaintiff-side litigation

David Lat, author of Original Jurisdiction, reports that traditionally defense-focused Big Law firms are increasingly representing corporate clients as plaintiffs. Driven by client demand and supported by legal finance, firms are taking on high-value affirmative matters using more flexible fee structures, allowing them to compete for plaintiff-side work while managing risk and preserving their core economic model. The article features perspectives from Burford’s[Evan Meyerson](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/evan-meyerson/) and[Vanessa Biondo](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/vanessa-biondo/) .
