## Summary


For many organizations, enforcing intellectual property rights across Europe has long been costly, fragmented and unpredictable, requiring parallel actions in multiple jurisdictions, with inconsistent outcomes and significant time to resolution. This has made it difficult to assess risk, deploy capital efficiently or fully realize the value of patent portfolios.
