# Webcast: Reflecting on ten years of the CAT

In a recent webcast with Lexology, “[The CAT at ten: Collective actions and the future of competition litigation in the UK](https://www.burfordcapital.com/insights-news-events/events-webcasts/charlie-rooke-and-craig-arnott-host-lexology-webcast/) ,”[Charlie Rooke](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/charlie-rooke/) and[Craig Arnott](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/craig-arnott/) examined the evolution of collective proceedings over the last ten years and explore what the future holds for competition litigation in the UK. The discussion featured insights from Belinda Hollway (Partner, Hausfeld) and Patrick Teague (Partner, Geradin Partners).


You can watch the webcast below.
