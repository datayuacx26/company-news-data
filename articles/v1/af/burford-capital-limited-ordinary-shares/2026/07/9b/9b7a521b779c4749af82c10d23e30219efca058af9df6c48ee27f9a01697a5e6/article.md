# MDD's Litigation Series explores Korea's evolving disputes market with Elizabeth Shin

As Korea's role in cross-border disputes continues to expand, the latest episode of MDD's *Litigation Series: Taking the Stand* podcast examines the forces reshaping the region's legal market.


Burford Senior Vice President, South Korea,[Elizabeth Shin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/elizabeth-shin/) discusses the internationalization of Korean businesses, the growing sophistication of claimants and legal advisers and the key trends driving litigation and arbitration across North Asia.
