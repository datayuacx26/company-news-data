# The European covers the London Disputes Report 2026

As commercial realities increasingly shape litigation strategy, a new article in *The European* explores why many strong legal claims are never pursued despite their legal merits. The findings draw on Burford Capital's latest research,[The London Disputes Report 2026](https://www.burfordcapital.com/insights-news-events/insights-research/2026-the-london-disputes-report/?utm_source=pr&utm_medium=press_coverage&utm_content=research&utm_term=2026-the-london-disputes-report&utm_campaign=2026_the_lawyer) , published in association with *The Lawyer* .


The article examines how rising costs, risk concerns and demands for greater cost certainty are changing the way businesses and their advisers evaluate commercial disputes, with legal finance emerging as an important tool for managing risk and unlocking value.
