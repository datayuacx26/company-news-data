# Reuters reports on Burford-backed ICC arbitration award against Cameroon

Reuters reports on Burford’s counterparty, Sundance Resources, prevailing in an ICC arbitration against Cameroon, with a tribunal awarding approximately US$616 million in damages, interest and costs related to the Mbalam-Nabeba iron ore project.
