# City AM explores how external capital is reshaping law firms with Burford's Travis Lenkner and Patrick Savage

*City AM* examines how growing investment demands, particularly around AI and technology, are prompting law firms to explore corporate-style capital models. The article features insights from Burford's Chief Development Officer,[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/patrick-savage/) , and Managing Director,[Patrick Savage](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/patrick-savage/) , on how innovative capital solutions can help firms fund strategic growth while preserving partner control and independence.
