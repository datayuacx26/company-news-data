# Solomonic's Year In Review features Burford on legal finance trends in 2025

In Solomonic’s *Year in Review 2025* ,[Charlie Rooke](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/charlie-rooke/) and[James MacKinnon](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/james-mackinnon/) reflect on a pivotal year for legal finance, as the market navigated regulatory uncertainty, the impact of the PACCAR decision and continued growth in complex, cross-border disputes.


The feature explores how rising litigation costs and increased scrutiny of funding frameworks are reshaping how companies and law firms manage risk and finance disputes. It also considers the UK Government’s proposed reversal of PACCAR and what renewed certainty could mean for the Competition Appeal Tribunal and the broader UK market in 2026.
