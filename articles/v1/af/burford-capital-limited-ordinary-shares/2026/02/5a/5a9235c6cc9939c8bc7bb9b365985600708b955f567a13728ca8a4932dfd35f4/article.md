# Innovation in the business of law: Chris Bogart on the "New Law Order" podcast

On *New Law Order* , a new podcast by TalksOnLaw, hosts Joel Cohen and John Morley explore how litigation has evolved from a legal cost into a financial asset, with insights from Burford CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) on risk, capital allocation and the growing role of institutional finance in complex disputes.


As the legal industry changes, questions remain about the pace of transformation and who is best positioned to adapt. This wide-ranging conversation between Cohen, Morley and Bogart examines the forces reshaping legal services and what the next chapter may hold for the business of law.
