# Legaltech News features David Perla on the future of AI and legal tech trends in 2026

Ahead of Legalweek 2026, Law.com's *Legaltech News* speaks with Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) , who discusses what comes next for legal technology.


David Perla discusses a shift from experimentation to real-world deployment and measurable ROI, growing scrutiny on valuations and the likelihood of tech consolidation, along with longer-term ripple effects for law firm economics, associate training and talent development.
