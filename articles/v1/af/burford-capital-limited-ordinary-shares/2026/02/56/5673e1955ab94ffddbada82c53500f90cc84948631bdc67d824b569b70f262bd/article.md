# JUVE Patent quotes Christopher Freeman on the UPC and the future of patent finance

Burford Managing Director[Christopher Freeman](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-freeman/) is quoted in JUVE Patent on how growing certainty around the UPC is reshaping risk assessment and driving the evolution of patent finance in Europe.


“As case law develops and investor familiarity increases, legal finance will become an essential part of patent enforcement in Europe. The UPC not only changes how litigation is conducted but also redefines how patents are valued and financed,” commented Freeman.
