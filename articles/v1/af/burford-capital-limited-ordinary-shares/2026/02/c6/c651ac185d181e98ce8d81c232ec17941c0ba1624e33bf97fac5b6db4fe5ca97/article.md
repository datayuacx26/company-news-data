# Law.com highlights affirmative recovery programs as a strategic lever for retailers

Retailers navigating compressed margins, supply chain disruptions and volatility are rethinking how best to protect enterprise value. In a *New York Law Journal* expert opinion,[Aviva Will](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/aviva-will/) and[Alyx Pattison](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/alyx-pattison/) reflect on insights from the 2025 Retail Industry Leaders Association (RILA) conference and examine how affirmative recovery programs can strengthen financial resilience.


Affirmative recovery programs enable legal departments to move beyond cost centers and contribute directly to liquidity and value creation. By proactively identifying and pursuing meritorious claims, and partnering with legal finance providers, retailers can unlock capital, increase flexibility and reinvest in core growth initiatives, even in uncertain markets.
