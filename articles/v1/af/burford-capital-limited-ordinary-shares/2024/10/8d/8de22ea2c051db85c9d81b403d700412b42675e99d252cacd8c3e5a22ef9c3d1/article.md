# Christopher Bogart provides commentary to Law360 on investor-state arbitrations

Burford CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) provides commentary to Law360 on investor-state arbitrations.


Bogart says: "'Professor Philippe Sands decided that the world needed a polemic from him on what he describes as a serious and real issue ― 'third-party funding' ― now enshrined in Rule 14 of the ICSID Arbitration Rules, that, he says, risks subverting the purpose of investor-state arbitration...Given that I have been funding investor-state cases for more than 15 years, it certainly seems to be taking a long time for that subversion to appear; if anything, funding is propping up the arbitral system and is used by respondents as well as claimants because it is economically sensible and financially efficient.'"
