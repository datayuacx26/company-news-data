# Law360 speaks with David Perla and Travis Lenkner about AI in legal finance

Law360 speaks with Burford's Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) and Chief Development Officer[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) in an article entitled "Insurers Have Long Used AI; Now, Litigation Funders Want In."


The article discusses insurers' past use of AI technologies and how companies like Burford are taking measured steps to incorporate AI into their underwriting practices, keeping in mind that there is "data asymmetry" between the two industries, making the application of AI into legal finance a slower process.
