# Burford contributes a chapter on funding and enforcement for Globe Law and Business

Globe Law and Business has published a guide to third party funding in international arbitration written by eminent funders, brokers and lawyers.


Burford’s[Craig Arnott](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/craig-arnott/) ,Michael Redman andMick Smith provided a chapter on funding and enforcement in the arbitration context.


The chapter covers the following topics:


·


Issues for award enforcement in commercial arbitration


·


Issues for award enforcement in investor-state arbitration


·


Asset recovery and funded award enforcement


·


Assignment of arbitral awards


[Read the special report in full here](https://www.globelawandbusiness.com/special-reports/funding-international-arbitration) .


Burford's in-house asset recovery team provides comprehensive services in asset tracing, litigation support and dispute resolution, offering critical intelligence to inform strategic decisions in commercial disputes.


Burford’s asset recovery can help clients to trace assets, prove ownership, investigate fraud, retrieve evidence and fund judgment enforcement.
