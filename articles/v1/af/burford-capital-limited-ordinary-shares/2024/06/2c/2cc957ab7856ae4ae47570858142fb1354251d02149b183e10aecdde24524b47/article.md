# Law360 speaks with Charles Griffin about trends driving commercial disputes in the US Healthcare Sector

Law360 speaks with Burford’s[Charles Griffin](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/charles-griffin/) about the business trends driving high-value commercial disputes in the US healthcare sector, to coincide with the publication of a new *Burford Quarterly*[article](https://www.burfordcapital.com/insights-news-events/burford-quarterly/2024-issue-3/2024-healthcare-litigation-landscape/) on the subject.


The article states: “In general, Griffin said the American healthcare industry landscape is so fraught right now that more litigation going forward is nearly a foregone conclusion. All the instances discussed in the article ‘are just the tip of the proverbial iceberg,’ Griffin said.”
