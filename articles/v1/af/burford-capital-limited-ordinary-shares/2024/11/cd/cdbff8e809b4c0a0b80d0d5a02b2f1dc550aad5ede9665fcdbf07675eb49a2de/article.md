# Law360 reports on Burford's Energy Transition Research

Law360 covers Burford Capital's recently commissioned research entitled[Energy transition disputes: GCs and senior lawyers on the business impacts of legal challenges to come](https://www.burfordcapital.com/insights-news-events/insights-research/2024-energy-transition-research/) , which delves into the key challenges and implications of the likely increase in commercial disputes related to the transition, and how it will reshape the legal landscape and impact businesses worldwide.


In discussing Burford's approach to research generally, Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) says, "'We see ourselves as something more than a capital provider...We see ourselves as the partner to our clients, whether they're at law firms or whether they're in-house.'"
