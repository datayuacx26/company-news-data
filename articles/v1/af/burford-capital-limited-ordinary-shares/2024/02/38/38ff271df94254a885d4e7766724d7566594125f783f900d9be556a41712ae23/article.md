# Mark Klein speaks with Corporate Counsel

Burford General Counsel and Chief Administrative Officer[Mark Klein](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/mark-klein/) speaks with *Corporate Counsel* about how "legal departments can fix their isolation problem."


"'If I’m going to be an obstruction, if I'm going to make your life more complicated, you're going to avoid me,'" said Mark Klein, general counsel of Burford Capital, adding that GCs must be "'facilitators rather than obstructors.'"
