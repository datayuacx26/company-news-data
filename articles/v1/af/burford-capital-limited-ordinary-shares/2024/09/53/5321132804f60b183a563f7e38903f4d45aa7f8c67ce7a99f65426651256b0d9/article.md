# Law.com reports on Travis Lenkner's return to Burford Capital with "an Eye on Future Growth Opportunities"

Law.com reports that Travis Lenkner has returned to Burford Capital with "an eye on future growth opportunities."


In an interview featuring CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) and[Travis Lenkner](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/travis-lenkner/) , Burford has announced Lenkner's new role of Chief Development Officer, where he will be "...focused on strategic initiatives for Burford such as potential investments in law firm equity, legal technology, including artificial intelligence, and the alternative delivery of legal services."
