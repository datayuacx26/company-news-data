# Jordan Licht on the GrowCFO Show podcast

Burford CFO[Jordan Licht](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/jordan-licht/) is a guest on the GrowCFO Show podcast.


As described on the program's website: "The core focus of the discussion centers around Burford Capital and the unique world of litigation finance. Jordan provides an overview of how Burford invests in commercial litigation cases…and explains Burford’s thorough underwriting process, the challenges of estimating case durations, and the benefits their capital and expertise can provide to clients. The conversation also touches on Burford’s global presence, their approach to building a non-law firm culture, and the complexities of funding and managing the company’s own capital requirements."
