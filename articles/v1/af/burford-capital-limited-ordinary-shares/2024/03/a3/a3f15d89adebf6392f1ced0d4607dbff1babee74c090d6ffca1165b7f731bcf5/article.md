# Evan Meyerson speaks about law firm growth in contingency practices

[Evan Meyerson](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/evan-meyerson/) speaks with *The National Law Journal* about the increase in interest to build out contingency practices at law firms.


“‘It’s fair to say we’ve seen a noticeable increase in interest in building out plaintiff side practices among the more traditional defense-oriented law firms,’ said Evan Meyerson, director at Burford Capital, in an interview. ‘There’s a growing desire to become—and comfort level with becoming—truly the one-stop shop for their institutional clients.’”
