# Christopher Bogart on John Quinn's podcast to discuss AI and data science in legal finance


March 20, 2024


## Summary


Burford CEO Christopher Bogart appears on John Quinn's Podcast, "Law, disrupted,” for an episode entitled "AI and Data Science in Litigation Finance." Bogart and Quinn discuss the use of AI and data science in legal finance decisions and Bogart explains how advances in data science have allowed funders to improve funding decisions.
