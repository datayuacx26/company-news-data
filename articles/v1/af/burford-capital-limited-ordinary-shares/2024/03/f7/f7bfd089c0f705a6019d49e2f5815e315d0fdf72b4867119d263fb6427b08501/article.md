# David Perla writes for PLI Chronicle on legal finance and AI

Burford Co-COO[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) writes for PLI Chronicle on the use of AI in legal finance.


Perla says: "In this article, I will explore the current and potential applications of AI in legal finance—the practice of valuing and monetizing legal assets, most often in the form of providing capital to finance meritorious claims to accelerate the expected value of claims, judgments and awards. Specifically, I will delve into how AI is used in sourcing investment opportunities, making informed investment decisions, and predicting outcomes once investments have been established."
