# Director Jörn Eschment wins Lexology's Client Choice Award for legal finance in Germany

Lexology (formerly Who's Who Legal/WWL) has recognized Burford Director[Jörn Eschment](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/joern-eschment/) as part of its 2025 Client Choice Awards. Mr. Eschment is the exclusive winner for Third-Party Funding Germany (Experts), ranking him among a small group of “Global Elite Thought Leaders.”


Established in 2005, the Client Choice Awards recognize partners around the world who stand apart for the excellent client care they provide and the quality of their service.
