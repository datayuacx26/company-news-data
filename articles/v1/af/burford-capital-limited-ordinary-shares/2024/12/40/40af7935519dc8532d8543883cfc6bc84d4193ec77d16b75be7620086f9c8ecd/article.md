# The Financial Analyst reports on Burford's deliberate shift away from London dispute resolution activities, highlighting problems within the UK’s legal and regulatory framework

The Financial Analyst covers[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) 's interview with[CityAM](https://www.cityam.com/burford-capital-turning-its-back-on-london-over-government-delays/) , where he highlighted that the desirability of investing in UK-based legal funding ventures could be reduced due to the uncertainty surrounding the PACCAR case and the postponement of the Litigation Funding Bill.


The article states: "Burford Capital’s strategic pivot away from London is not just a corporate decision; it reflects deeper issues within the UK’s legal and regulatory framework. The implications for the UK economy and investors are profound, as a decline in London’s appeal for dispute resolution could reshape the landscape of legal services and investment in the country for years to come."
