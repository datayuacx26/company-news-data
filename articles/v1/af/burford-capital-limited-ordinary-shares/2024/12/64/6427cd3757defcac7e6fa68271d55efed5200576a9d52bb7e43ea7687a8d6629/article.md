# David Perla and Michael Redman featured in Legal Business article about 2024 legal finance trends

Burford Vice Chair[David Perla](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/david-perla/) and Co-Head of EMEA and Asset RecoveryMichael Redman are featured in a *Legal Business* article discussing the latest trends in legal finance.


Among other topics, the article references shifting perceptions of legal finance as a result of increased use, quoting Perla, who states that legal finance is becoming "much more accepted as a common cost of doing business; as corporate finance for law."
