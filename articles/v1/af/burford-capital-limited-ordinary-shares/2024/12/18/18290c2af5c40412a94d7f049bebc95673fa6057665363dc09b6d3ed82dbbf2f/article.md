# Legal 500 ranks Burford Tier 1 in US and Tier 2 in UK


December 23, 2024

Burford has been ranked Tier 1 in the US and Tier 2 in the UK by the Legal 500's funder rankings. The report notes that Burford "excels in the financing of high-value, complex disputes, including antitrust and competition claims, and bankruptcy and insolvency matters."
