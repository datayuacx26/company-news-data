# Christopher Bogart speaks with The Times about how "[L]egal services in England are harmed by inaction on litigation funding"

Burford CEO[Christopher Bogart](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/christopher-bogart/) is quoted in *The Times* on how "\[L\]egal services in England are harmed by inaction on litigation funding."


Bogart says, "'We’ve already moved some of our dispute resolution work out of London to other markets like Paris, Singapore and New York,'" and adds that Burford is 'not alone in moving work to other jurisdictions.'" He continues: "'the government has ‘an acute issue it needs to fix — and urgently — otherwise it’s going to continue costing the legal sector valuable business.'"
