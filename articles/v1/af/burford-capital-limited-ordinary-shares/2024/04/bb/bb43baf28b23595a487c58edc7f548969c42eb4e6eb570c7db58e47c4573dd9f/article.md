# Andrew Cohen speaks about disclosure with The Daily Journal

Managing Director[Andrew Cohen](https://www.burfordcapital.com/about-us/our-team/andrew-cohen/) speaks about disclosure with *The Daily Journal* .


"'The concerns about foreign interference in litigation are completely without evidence,' Cohen says in the article, and continues: "'You can cherry-pick some examples of Chinese entities that bring a claim in the U.S. but there are tons of U.S. companies with Chinese investors that exist, so there's nothing nefarious or strange about these situations.'"
