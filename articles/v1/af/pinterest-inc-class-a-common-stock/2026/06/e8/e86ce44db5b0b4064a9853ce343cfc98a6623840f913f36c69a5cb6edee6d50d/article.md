# Pinterest Manifestival at Cannes | Pinterest Newsroom

At Pinterest, we’ve built a place where people come to dream, decide and do. While other platforms can sell the illusion of living, Pinterest helps people create a life they actually want to live. That’s the idea behind our award-winning Pinterest Manifestival at the Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity.


Pinterest Manifestival is where attendees can interact with the trends, aesthetics and ideas shaping culture, putting phones aside, taking a cue from Gen Z and getting creative through IRL experiences designed to spark discovery and turn inspiration into something tangible and real.


“


People come to Pinterest to go from inspiration to action, not perform for likes. Pinterest Manifestival brings that difference to life in a way only Pinterest can: a real-world space where trends become tangible and guests leave with something they made, tasted, wore, or created. It's a reminder that the most meaningful thing you can find online is a reason to go offline.”


– ***Claudine Cheever,***


CMO, Pinterest


#### **Where Inspiration Becomes Experience**


Through personalized, unplugged experiences, the Pinterest Manifestival brings guests inside a real-life Pinterest board that turns inspiration into something they can see, taste, wear and make their own.


-


**Phones down. Eyes up.** Upon arrival, guests receive a Pinterest-branded phone sticker with the phrase “Less URL, More IRL”. The message sets the tone for the experience, inviting attendees to be fully present.


-


**The Original Pinterest Tattoo Parlour.** The Pinterest Tattoo Parlour returns with a fresh Pinterest Predicts[Funhaus](https://business.pinterest.com/pinterest-predicts/2026/funhaus/) twist, offering trend-inspired tattoos that have become one of the most talked-about permanent souvenirs on the Croisette.


-


**Pinterest Patisserie.** Through a unique tasting experience, guests explore Pinterest’s hyper-personalization through custom desserts. After answering a prompt inspired by their personal taste, they’ll receive bespoke treats inspired by Pinterest Predicts trends.


-


**Pinterest Visual Search Studio.** In partnership with **Adobe** , this experience invites guests to visually discover their personal aesthetic by using handheld scanners, or “Pinterest search bars”, to capture what they love. On-site designers then use Adobe Express to transform their selections into a custom-printed journal.


-


**Pinterest Style Lab.** In this hands-on activation, guests collaborate with our lead artist and designer,[Clara Chu](https://www.pinterest.com/clarachuworld/) , to create a one-of-a-kind accessory, customized with upcycled details and expressive add-ons inspired by Gen Z style and self-expression.


-


**Offline Social Club feat. La Poste** . Attendees recharge in a new space created for IRL connection, unplugging and engaging in hands-on activities. Showcasing the[Pinterest Predicts “Pen Pals” trend](https://business.pinterest.com/pinterest-predicts/2026/pen-pals/) , guests send handwritten postcards inspired by Gen Z's rising love for analog letters, in partnership with the French postal service, **La Poste Française and the Cannes Lions Festival for Creativity.**


#### **Pinterest and Sephora Bring Color to the Pinterest Palette: Bleach Club**


This year Sephora is partnering with Pinterest to bring the Pinterest Palette: Bleach Club to life. Attendees explore bold, personal approaches to color and styling, from hair to clothing, turning this year’s[Pinterest Palette](https://business.pinterest.com/pinterest-palette/) colors into wearable self-expression. Think: frosted tips, bold brows, expressive hair stenciling and bleach-painted clothing, taking trends off the screen and into the real world.


“Looking for inspiration and adding your own twist is a core part of any beauty lover’s journey to defining and evolving their own self-expression. This is why it felt like such a natural fit for Sephora to partner with Pinterest to provide an experience that is as unapologetically unique as the trend-forward beauty community we both share,” said Zena Arnold, Chief Marketing Officer of Sephora US.
