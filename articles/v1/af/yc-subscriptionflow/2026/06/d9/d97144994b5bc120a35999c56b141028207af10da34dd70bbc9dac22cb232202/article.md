## **Why Integrate Yoco with Shopify?**


Shopify is well-suited to establishing your online store. But once your store is established, Shopify’s payment processing features are limited, especially in South Africa, where native Shopify Payments is unavailable. To deal with it, merchants need to rely on third-party gateways. When integrated with Shopify, Yoco creates a unified commerce experience that simplifies operations and supports growth at every stage.


#### **Simplify Payment Collection**


Yoco’s payment infrastructure is built considering the problems of African merchants. It enables card payments, mobile point-of-sale, and online payment capabilities. When integrated with Shopify, it allows merchants to collect payments from customers across all channels.


#### **Create a Seamless Checkout Experience**


Customers expect to have a smooth checkout experience. Any payment processing issue can lead customers to abandon the cart. With Yoco powering your payment collection inside Shopify’s checkout environment, buyers move quickly from cart to confirmation without unnecessary redirects or confusing payment steps.


#### **Trusted Local Experience**


Yoco offers a familiar infrastructure. Customers can checkout easily via familiar payment methods, including Visa, Mastercard, and[Apple Pay](https://www.subscriptionflow.com/apple-pay-recurring-payments/) , with bank level 3D secure authentication to prevent fraud. This helps merchants to offer a localised ecommerce checkout experience.


#### **Support Business Growth Across Channels**


When integrated with Shopify, Yoco keeps your payment and stock synced, whether you are selling at a physical market or managing an online store. A multi-channel foundation can simplify the process of scaling operations without compromising visibility and control.


### **Why Subscription Businesses Need More Than a Payment Gateway**


Many merchants have a common assumption that a payment gateway alone is capable of managing subscriptions. But in reality, a payment gateway is only designed to process transactions. Subscriptions need a dedicated subscription management platform.


This is because recurring payments work differently from one-time payments. A one-time transaction is simple: the customer pays, the order is delivered, and the customer’s billing relationship is over.[Recurring payments](https://subscriptionflow.com/recurring-payments/) , on the other hand, are a whole other ball game. They need to be scheduled for billing automatically, require logic to deal with failures, and demand workflows for dealing with each stage of the subscriber lifecycle.

Many merchants who try to charge recurring payments manually with Yoco and Shopify face a pattern of issues:


- Manual Renewals: In the absence of automation, it requires constant oversight to manually process each renewal, which is unsustainable to scale and very prone to mistakes.


- Failed Recurring Payments: Declined cards, outdated payment options, and low funds are among the factors that lead to payment failures. If not rectified, they ultimately lead to the loss of subscribers.


- Subscription Cancellations: Customers can cancel subscriptions at any time. When handled manually, it causes billing errors that erode business trust from its customers.


- Upgrades and Downgrade Requests: Each upgrade or downgrade triggers changes in billing amounts, access to different products/services, and new billing dates. Manual management of changes in subscription plans cause confusion and lead to errors.


Therefore, when businesses scale, spreadsheets and manual processes can’t keep up with the growing number of customers. So, merchants need subscription automation to ensure revenue integrity, seamless customer experience, and scalable customer service.
