# Chief Human Resource Officer (CHRO) Insights

### Employee Value Proposition


Culture is currency in every workplace, and today, many CHROs are rethinking what they’re offering.


Fewer than half of CHROs believe their current culture is fit for future success, and 72 percent say their EVP needs a refresh to keep attracting new talent.


Real culture transformation starts when people understand the change and see their role in it. That’s why Chief HR Officers are reimagining their EVP as a shared story—one that connects strategy to everyday experience.
