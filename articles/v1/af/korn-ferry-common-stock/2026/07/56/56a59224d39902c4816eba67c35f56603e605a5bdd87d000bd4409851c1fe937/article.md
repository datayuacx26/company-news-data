# Dominic Cottone Joins Korn Ferry as Senior Client Partner

**New York, July 2, 2026** – Korn Ferry (NYSE: KFY) today announced that Dominic Cottone has joined the firm as a Senior Client Partner in Life Sciences. He will be based in the firm's New York office.


Cottone brings more than 25 years of experience advising boards, CEOs, and senior leaders on leadership effectiveness, succession planning, and organizational transformation. His expertise includes helping organizations strengthen leadership capability and enhance executive team performance.


“Dom has a strong track record in leadership and organizational effectiveness,” said Jonathan Wildman, North America Life Sciences Lead, Korn Ferry. “His experience helping organizations develop leaders and build high-performing teams will be a tremendous asset to our clients.”


Cottone holds a master's degree in organizational communication, learning and design from Ithaca College.
