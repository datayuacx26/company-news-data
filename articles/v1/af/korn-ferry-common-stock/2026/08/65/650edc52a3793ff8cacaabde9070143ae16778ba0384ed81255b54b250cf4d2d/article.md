# Stephen Mork Joins Korn Ferry as Senior Client Partner

**San Francisco, August 19, 2026** – Korn Ferry (NYSE: KFY) today announced that Stephen Mork has joined the firm as a Senior Client Partner in the Total Rewards practice. He will be based in the firm's San Francisco office.


Mork brings more than 20 years of global experience developing executive compensation and workforce rewards. He advises organizations on integrated total rewards strategies that align with broader business objectives.


“Stephen brings deep expertise in total rewards and executive pay, along with a strong market reputation in the life sciences and technology sectors,” said Todd McGovern, Global Leader of Total Rewards, Korn Ferry. “As a seasoned consultant and trusted C-suite advisor, his experience will further strengthen our ability to deliver impactful rewards strategies and solutions for clients.”


Mork holds a bachelor’s degree from the University of Minnesota.
