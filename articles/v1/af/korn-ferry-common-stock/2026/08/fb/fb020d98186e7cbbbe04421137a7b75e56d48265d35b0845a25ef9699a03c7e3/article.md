# Ricardo Rocco Joins Korn Ferry as Senior Client Partner

**Miami, August 4, 2026** – Korn Ferry (NYSE: KFY) today announced that Ricardo Rocco has joined the firm as a Senior Client Partner in the Consumer Practice. He will be based in the firm's Miami office.


Rocco brings more than two decades of corporate leadership and executive search experience across the consumer, beverage, and industrial sectors, advising clients on leadership needs across the Americas and global markets.


“We are delighted to welcome Ricardo to our global Consumer Practice,” said Dominique Virchaux, President, Global Consumer Market, Korn Ferry. “His expertise and deep relationships with boards and executive teams will further strengthen our ability to deliver exceptional leadership solutions for clients across the consumer sector.”


Rocco holds a Bachelor of Science in Aeronautical Mechanical Engineering from ITA (Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica), Brazil.
