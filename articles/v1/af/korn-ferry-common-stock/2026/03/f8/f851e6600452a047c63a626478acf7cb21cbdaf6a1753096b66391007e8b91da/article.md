# Hailey Herleman Joins Korn Ferry as Senior Client Partner in Healthcare Practice

**Dallas, March 13, 2026** – Korn Ferry (NYSE: KFY) today announced that Hailey Herleman has joined the firm as a Senior Client Partner in the Healthcare practice. She will be based in the firm’s Dallas office.


Dr. Herleman brings over two decades of experience advising organizations, grounded in her deep expertise in industrial and organizational psychology. She focuses on helping companies connect their talent strategies with business objectives to drive measurable results.


“Hailey brings a powerful blend of scientific insight and practical expertise to organizational performance,” said Greg Button, President, Global Healthcare Market, Korn Ferry. “Her experience leveraging data, analytics, and technology will be a valuable addition to our Healthcare practice as we help our clients solve complex workforce challenges.”


Herleman holds a doctorate in industrial and organizational psychology from Clemson University.
