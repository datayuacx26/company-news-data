# Scott Rowe Joins Korn Ferry as Global Account Leader in Financial Services Practice

**Chicago, May 7, 2026** – Korn Ferry (NYSE: KFY) today announced that Scott Rowe has joined the firm as a Global Account Leader in its Global Financial Services practice. He will be based in the firm’s Chicago office.


Rowe has more than 20 years of experience in financial services and consulting, supporting private equity firms and their portfolio companies across the investment lifecycle. He helps organizations unlock value through integrated strategies that connect leadership, talent, and organizational effectiveness.


“We are pleased to welcome Scott,” said Mike Franzino, President, Global Financial Services, Korn Ferry. “He brings extensive experience in private equity and a strong track record of helping organizations align their teams around a clear, unifying vision to drive sustained growth.”


Prior to his business career, Rowe was a professional golfer, competing on the European, Asian, and Canadian tours. He holds a master’s degree in business administration from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business.
