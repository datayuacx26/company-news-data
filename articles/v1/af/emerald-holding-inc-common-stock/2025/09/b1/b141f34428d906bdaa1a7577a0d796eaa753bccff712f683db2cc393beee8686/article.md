# Women in Exhibitions International Welcomes Emerald as an International Member

Klimmen, Netherlands, 18 September 2025 – Women in Exhibitions (WiE) International is proud to announce that Emerald
(NYSE: EEX) has joined its growing global network as an international member. As largest U.S.-based business-to-business
event organizer, Emerald owns and operates a curated portfolio of B2B events – including trade shows, conferences, B2C
showcases, and a scaled hosted buyer platform – and delivers dynamic solutions across leading industries through robust
content and e-commerce marketplaces.


This partnership comes at a pivotal time for Emerald, as the company continues to grow its global footprint through recent
acquisitions. By joining Women in Exhibitions, Emerald reinforces its commitment to gender equity, talent development, and
inclusive leadership across the exhibition and events industry. This move strengthens the international reach of the WiE
community and reflects a shared belief in the importance of creating more visible pathways for women to thrive in the sector.


Emerald now joins a prestigious roster of international WiE members, including Informa plc, the Rai Amsterdam, RX
and Tembo, all of whom have demonstrated leadership in advancing diversity and inclusion in their organizations and the
wider industry.


“This is not just another membership for us. At Emerald, we are committed to being engaged and active participants in
Women in Exhibitions International. With half of our senior leadership comprised of women, we recognize the important role
women play in shaping our industry and value the opportunity to collaborate with peers across the globe to strengthen
leadership, innovation, and the future of exhibitions,” said Ren Akinci, EVP & Chief People Officer at Emerald.


“We are thrilled to welcome Emerald into the Women in Exhibitions community. Their commitment to innovation,
inclusivity, and excellence aligns beautifully with our shared mission to elevate and empower women across the exhibitions
industry. Together, we continue to build a stronger, more equitable future where every voice is heard, and every leader has a
place.” said Oana Cipca, Founder & President Women in Exhibitions International.


**About Women in Exhibitions**
Women in Exhibitions (WiE) was officially launched in 2018 to support the continued professional development of women
working in the exhibitions industry and to provide opportunities for them to connect, grow, and lead. With six active chapters
and new ones launching globally, WiE brings together women from across all roles and levels to exchange knowledge, gain
visibility, and build networks that foster long-term career success.


**About Emerald**
Emerald Holding, Inc. (NYSE: EEX) is the largest U.S.-based B2B event organizer, empowering businesses year-round by
expanding meaningful connections, developing influential content, and delivering powerful commerce-driven solutions. As
the owner and operator of a curated portfolio of B2B events spanning trade shows, conferences, B2C showcases and a scaled
hosted buyer platform, Emerald also delivers dynamic solutions across leading industries through its robust content and ecommerce
marketplace. Emerald is a trusted partner for its thousands of customers, predominantly small and medium-sized
businesses, playing a pivotal role in driving ongoing commerce through streamlined buying, selling, and networking
opportunities. Powered by an experienced team, Emerald is fostering impactful engagement and delivering unparalleled
market access with a commitment to driving business growth 365 days a year. For more: https://www.emeraldx.com
