# Generis Group Named One of Canada’s Top Growing Companies 2025 by The Globe and Mail

Generis Group has been named one of Canada’s Top Growing Companies for 2025 by *The Globe and Mail* ’s Report on Business magazine, ranking #136 on the list of 400 companies with verified three-year revenue growth of 264%. The company is listed within the Entertainment category of this year’s ranking.
