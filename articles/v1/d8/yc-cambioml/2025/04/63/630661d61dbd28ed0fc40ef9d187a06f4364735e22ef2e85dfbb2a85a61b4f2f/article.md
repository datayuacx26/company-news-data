# Energent.ai Launch

## Energent.ai: Your AI teammate who gets hands-on work done, visibly, controllably and securely


Hey everyone! We're Rachel and Kimi, founders of CambioML. We built **Energent.ai** to help knowledge workers automate the messy, multi-step tasks on a desktop. It's your AI teammate that actually gets things done, without integration or coding.


Energent.ai works side-by-side with you on a secure desktop to automate data-heavy multi-step workflows, without integration or coding — from gathering public info online, to cleaning messy format data, to building custom Excel macros.


## Beyond Chat: AI That Acts


We are solving: "The barrier of entry of being productive and secure with AI tools is too high. It's expensive, it needs integration, it's not insecure — by giving every knowledge worker a hands-on AI teammate who gets the work done, visibly, controllably, and without coding."
