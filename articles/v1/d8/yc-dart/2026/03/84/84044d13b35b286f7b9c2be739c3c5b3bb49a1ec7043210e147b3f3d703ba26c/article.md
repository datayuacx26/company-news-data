# Dart v7.7 – Pinned chats and dictation shortcut

Today we’re rolling out our latest update. This release focuses heavily on quality-of-life improvements, making your AI chats both easier to use and organize.
[View our full changelog here](https://itsdart.us14.list-manage.com/track/click?u=6ffefa993304e3b4b9d967039&id=f1f1a8a529&e=44ec99aaf7) .


## Organize your AI chats


We’re making it easier to keep track of your most valuable AI conversations and workflows.


- You can now pin or bookmark your favorite[AI chats](https://itsdart.us14.list-manage.com/track/click?u=6ffefa993304e3b4b9d967039&id=2dcf31d927&e=44ec99aaf7) for quick and easy access later
- You now have the ability to manually rename chats in your history, keeping your sidebar organized and searchable


## Dictation and AI skills


We’ve implemented improvements to dictation and AI skills to keep you in flow for longer.


- Added a new dictation keyboard shortcut for much faster voice-to-text entry
- The comment and description toolbars now hide the attachment and dictate buttons when not actively in use to reduce clutter
- Made UI improvements to our[AI skill interactions](https://itsdart.us14.list-manage.com/track/click?u=6ffefa993304e3b4b9d967039&id=775633e522&e=44ec99aaf7) , making the chat and skill editor much more intuitive


## Search and UI


- Refreshing a search page now properly reloads your recent query instead of showing a blank screen
- Improved the reactivity of the Home and Inbox views to better adapt to various screen sizes, and fixed tooltips to prevent overlapping layouts
- Reports folders now show a contextual emoji rather than an empty folder icon, and we've improved the topbar styling and date icons


## More improvements


- Resolved memory leaks and timeout issues to prevent app crashes
- Ensured the "new task" row properly shows up at the bottom of lists even if task groups are hidden
- Resolved an issue where users were occasionally unable to add new teammates to their workspace
- Fixed bugs where pasting links into task descriptions was occasionally unresponsive.
- Fixed bugs that caused layouts to accidentally overlap
- Deleting a space or dartboard now reliably moves associated tasks to the trash
- Fixed a bug where creating a status or type with a duplicate name would break and leave it with no name
- Added safeguards to prevent accidentally misconfiguring general or personal spaces
- Fixed an issue where the "Fill out with AI" tag option wouldn't close
- Resolved visual bugs where avatars displayed incorrectly if they hadn't fully loaded
