# SafetyKit | The Take It Down Act (2025): What Platforms Need to Know About Federal NCII Compliance

On May 19, 2025, the Take It Down Act became the first federal law to directly regulate AI-generated content and impose mandatory takedown requirements on platforms. If you operate a website or app with user-generated content, you have until May 19, 2026 to implement compliant systems.


Meeting these requirements requires sophisticated AI detection and automated workflows. ## **What the Act Requires: Prohibitions and Platform Compliance**


The Take It Down Act creates federal liability for non-consensual distribution of intimate imagery and mandates platform action within 48 hours.


**Key prohibitions:** Publishing intimate content or AI-generated deepfakes without consent (with intent to harm), any intimate imagery of minors, or threatening to share such content for coercion or extortion.


**Platform obligations:**


- Accept victim reports with electronic signature and sufficient location information
- Remove content within 48 hours of valid notice
- Prevent re-uploads through duplicate detection
- Maintain audit trails of all takedown actions


Failure to comply is treated as a violation of the FTC Act, with civil penalties reaching ~$51,000 per violation.


## **Technical Requirements for Compliance**


Meeting the 48-hour deadline requires platforms to implement:


- **AI-Powered Detection:** Identify NCII and AI-generated deepfakes that evade traditional hash-matching
- **Duplicate Prevention:** Catch re-uploads even when content is cropped, filtered, or modified
- **Automated Triage:** Prioritize urgent reports within compliance windows
- **FTC-Ready Audit Trails:** Document every decision to demonstrate good faith efforts
- **Cross-Account Monitoring:** Track distribution patterns to prevent serial re-uploaders
