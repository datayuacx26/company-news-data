# SafetyKit | Merchant Verification for Agentic Commerce Payments

Agentic commerce is estimated to drive $1 trillion in transactions by 2030 as AI agents move from discovery to purchase. Payment processors like Stripe, PayPal, and Mastercard are building infrastructure to enable agent-driven payments.


But payment authorization only confirms permission to pay. It doesn't verify merchant legitimacy, product compliance, or chargeback risk.


## **The Risk Payment Platforms Face**


Agents complete thousands of transactions in seconds. Without real-time merchant trust infrastructure, payment platforms face growing chargeback exposure, processor relationship risk, and compliance liability.


Traditional fraud tools weren't built for agent commerce. Legacy systems may block legitimate agent transactions by flagging high-velocity patterns as fraudulent. Meanwhile, sophisticated merchant fraud that doesn't fit simple rules goes undetected. This creates three critical risks:


1. **Processor relationships depend on merchant quality:** Card networks evaluate payment platforms on merchant quality metrics. High rates of fraud, restricted categories, or policy violations can trigger increased reserves, higher fees, or platform restrictions that impact the entire merchant portfolio.
2. **Chargeback exposure increases:** Agents may authorize payments to fraudulent merchants selling counterfeit goods or prohibited items. By the time users dispute transactions, merchants have been paid and platforms absorb the chargeback costs.
3. **Compliance violations create regulatory exposure:** When agents process transactions from merchants selling restricted items or operating in prohibited categories, payment platforms face regulatory scrutiny and potential penalties.
