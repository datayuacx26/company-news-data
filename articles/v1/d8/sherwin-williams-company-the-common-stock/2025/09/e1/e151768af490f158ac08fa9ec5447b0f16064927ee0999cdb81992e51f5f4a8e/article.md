# Elevate the Everyday with Our 2026 Color of the Year: Universal Khaki

In a world that’s constantly shifting, there’s comfort in the classics. Our[2026 Color of the Year](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-of-the-year/2026) , Universal Khaki, is the ultimate warm, grounding neutral that celebrates the beauty of life’s bare essentials. Chosen by our[Trendsight Team](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-collections/colormix-forecast/meet-the-team) , Universal Khaki is more than just a color, it’s a reflection of our collective desire for simplicity, functionality, and lasting style. This subtle midtone neutral evokes a sense of familiarity and ease, the timeless appeal of a well-worn jacket or the blank canvas of a fresh start.


*Featured Color:[Universal Khaki SW 6150](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/yellow-paint-colors/SW6150-universal-khaki)*


## Tailored & Timeless


Universal Khaki is a foundational color that easily blends into any space and style. Its subtle, refined elegance and everyday practicality make it a go-to choice for both designers and homeowners. Whether layered into cozy, inviting interiors or used as a soft backdrop for bold accents, this ultra-usable shade offers endless adaptability. “Understated and hardworking, warmer neutrals like khaki blend seamlessly with their counterparts,” says Sue Wadden, Director of Color Marketing *.* “They provide a foundation that sets the tone for an entire design.” Whether it’s anchoring a modern minimalist living room or softening a layered, eclectic space, Universal Khaki adapts with grace. It’s a true chameleon—effortlessly pairing with warm woods, soft whites, and rich, earthy tones.


## A Palette Crafted for Comfort


[Universal Khaki](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/yellow-paint-colors/SW6150-universal-khaki) is the heart of a curated palette designed to bring warmth, depth, and quiet sophistication to any space. Each supporting hue was chosen to complement its timeless character.


*[Garden Gate SW 6167](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/green-paint-colors/SW6167-garden-gate)* introduces a rich, earthy green that calls to mind the serenity of nature and adds a grounding presence to interiors. It’s perfect for creating cozy corners or accent walls that feel lush and enveloping.


*[Watery SW 6478](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/blue-paint-colors/SW6478-watery)* brings a breath of fresh air with its soft, tranquil blue-green hue. It pairs beautifully with neutrals to create spa-like retreat vibes or airy, coastal-inspired rooms.


*[Special Walnut MW 224](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/homeowners/color/find-and-explore-colors/paint-colors-by-family/special-walnut-mw224)* , the 2026 Minwax® Color of the Year, adds a layer of organic texture with its deep, wood-toned stain. It’s ideal for cabinetry, furniture, or trim that needs a touch of timeless craftsmanship and an echo of the natural character of the palette.


*[Tarragon SW 9660](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW9660-tarragon)* offers a moody, modern take on blue-gray, adding sophistication and depth to minimalist spaces or industrial-inspired designs.


*[Henna Shade SW 6326](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/red-paint-colors/SW6326-henna-shade)* infuses warmth and character with its spicy terracotta tone. It’s a bold yet comforting choice that brings energy and personality to any palette.


*[Lemon Chiffon SW 6686](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/yellow-paint-colors/SW6686-lemon-chiffon)* adds a ray of brightness to the mix with its cheerful, sunlit yellow hue. It’s a perfect accent for kitchens, nurseries, or any space that benefits from a dose of optimism.


*[Cream and Sugar SW 9507](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW9507-cream-and-sugar)* softens the palette with a creamy, inviting neutral that complements both warm and cool tones. It’s a versatile backdrop for layered textures and mixed materials.


*[White Snow SW 9541](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/white-paint-colors/sw9541-white-snow)* rounds out the collection with a crisp, clean white that adds clarity and contrast. It’s the go-to choice for trim, ceilings, or modern monochromatic schemes.


Together, these shades create a palette that feels more collected than curated, like a home that’s evolved over time, with care and intention.
