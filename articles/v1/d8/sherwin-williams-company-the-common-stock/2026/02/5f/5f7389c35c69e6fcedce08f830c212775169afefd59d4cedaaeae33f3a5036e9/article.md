# Our Favorite Restorative Darks from Colormix® 2026

Step into a palette of deep, nocturnal hues that invite you to slow down and sink in. These moody, enveloping shades bring a sense of quiet luxury to any room. Whether you’re creating a cozy corner or refreshing a whole space, this rich, restful palette shows how darker colors can inspire calm and transform the everyday into something beautifully serene.


*Featured color:[Rojo Marrón SW 9182](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW9182-rojo-marron)*


## Rojo Marrón


[Rojo Marrón’s](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW9182-rojo-marron) deep shade of burgundy features subtle brown undertones that make a dramatic statement. Keep this dark hue feeling balanced with other muted neutral tones and crisp shades of white.


*Featured Color:*[Relic Bronze SW 6132](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/yellow-paint-colors/SW6132-relic-bronze)


## Relic Bronze


We love the grounding influence that[Relic Bronze](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/yellow-paint-colors/SW6132-relic-bronze) adds. This earthy hue features a beautiful blend of brown and green undertones that work together to create a calming hue. It’s a result that’s bold and understated at the same time.


*Featured Color:[Tarragon SW 9660](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW9660-tarragon)*


## Tarragon


[Tarragon’s](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW9660-tarragon) dreamlike, jewel-toned shade can be the main attraction or fade quietly into the background. This versatile hue has a universal quality that works with so many different styles, making it a color that can take on anything.


*Featured Color:[Garden Gate SW 6167](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/green-paint-colors/SW6167-garden-gate)*


## Garden Gate


A rich green like[Garden Gate](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/green-paint-colors/SW6167-garden-gate) creates a lush backdrop with dark, forest-inspired tones. Whether it’s an accent or the main attraction, pairing this neutral with darker furniture and accessories can create a traditional looking space.
