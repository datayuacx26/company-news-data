# Light Direction and Hue: The Color Compass

A room is more than four walls with windows. The way the sun shines into the space can change the feeling of any color you have in your space. The same paint swatch can drastically change how it looks in a room, whether cool, warm, muted or vibrant, based on where the room faces. This guide will help you decide what colors to use in your north- , south- , east- or west-facing rooms.


***Featured Color:[Universal Khaki 6150](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/yellow-paint-colors/SW6150-universal-khaki)***


## **North-Facing Room**


*North-Facing Room Light:* Soft, diffused, consistent, and naturally cool.


*Key Feature:* Minimal direct sunlight.


North-facing rooms have fleeting natural light that leans cool and soft. These rooms are great places for any space one may look for a soft, quiet atmosphere. With this light’s frosty qualities, peachy neutrals or sunny yellows like[Lemon Chiffon](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/yellow-paint-colors/SW6686-lemon-chiffon) or[Malted Milk](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/orange-paint-colors/sw6057-malted-milk) add a cozy warmth to the walls. Want a more true neutral? Try[Alabaster](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/white-paint-colors/SW7008-alabaster) or our 2026 Color of the Year,[Universal Khaki,](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-of-the-year/2026) which can add depth to any space feeling bare and help you find strength in simplicity.

## **South-Facing Room**


*South-Facing Room Light:* Bright, warm, consistent and golden throughout the day.


*Key Feature:* Vibrant and warmest sunlight.


Looking for some balance in your south-facing room? Try some cooler colors to balance the warm rays coming through your windowsill. Dreamy blues like[Watery](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/blue-paint-colors/SW6478-watery) or[Upward](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/blue-paint-colors/SW6239-upward) act as a harmonious wave of calm in the lively southern light. You could also use this light to invigorate more muted tones such as[Drift of Mist](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW9166-drift-of-mist) and[Natural White](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/white-paint-colors/sw9542-natural-white) . These south-facing light and color duos will work in any room you want brightness and warmth all day.

## **East-Facing Room**


*East-Facing Room Light:* Bright, warm in the morning. Softer, cooler in the evening.


*Key Feature:* **** Fresh and energizing early; muted and calm later.


From living rooms to kitchens, an east-facing room paint color needs to be able to match the tone of light it is given throughout the day. Take advantage of the early morning sun with warm undertones, and enjoy that they can also be toned down in the setting rays of the evening. With gorgeous neutrals like[Frosted Fern,](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW9648-frosted-fern)[Sanderling](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/neutral-paint-colors/SW7513-sanderling) or[Cheviot](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/white-paint-colors/SW9503-cheviot) , you can design a space that is versatile. ## **West-Facing Room**


*West-Facing Room Light:* Gentle, cool in the morning. Golden, vibrant in the evening.


*Key Feature:* Calm early; fiery and dramatic later.


As the sun moves throughout the day, the windows let in beautiful golden-hour lighting during the late afternoon to evening. Take advantage of this special time frame with stunning whites like[Gypsum](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/white-paint-colors/sw9543-gypsum?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=20034111831&gbraid=0AAAAADmwGVbWvEzTKLyOYResbO25Kz7N7&gclid=EAIaIQobChMIz9jvhuuhkgMVBhmtBh10sixpEAAYASAAEgICePD_BwE) or[Clean Slate](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/white-paint-colors/sw9621-clean-slate) .
