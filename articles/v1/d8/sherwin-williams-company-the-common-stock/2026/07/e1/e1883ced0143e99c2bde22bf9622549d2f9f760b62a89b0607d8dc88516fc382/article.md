# Trusted Terracottas We’re Loving

Terracotta-inspired paint colors effortlessly make any space feel warm, grounded and inviting. Drawing inspiration from sunbaked clay, desert landscapes and natural earth pigments, these hues introduce richness without overwhelming a space. Whether used as a subtle backdrop or a statement-making feature, terracotta shades create a timeless yet modern vibe. From soft pink-beiges to bold clay reds, these earthy colors can add depth, comfort and character throughout the home.


*Featured Color:[Reddened Earth SW 6053](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/red-paint-colors/sw6053-reddened-earth)*


## **Create a Sense of Rich Sophistication**


For a bathroom that feels like a luxurious retreat,[Reddened Earth](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/red-paint-colors/sw6053-reddened-earth) brings a sophisticated dose of warmth. This deep, earthy shade creates a cocooning effect that can instantly transform a functional space into one that feels spa-like and intentional. Paired with natural stone, brass fixtures or warm wood accents, Reddened Earth brings richness and depth to create a space that feels inviting, grounded and elevated.


*Featured Color:[Pinky Beige SW 0079](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/red-paint-colors/sw0079-pinky-beige)*


## **Set the Tone for Rest & Relaxation**


Bedrooms are meant to be restful, and[Pinky Beige](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/red-paint-colors/sw0079-pinky-beige) nestles into the sweet spot between warmth and softness. This muted terracotta-adjacent neutral blends gentle pink undertones with dusty beige, creating a calming, soulful environment that fosters relaxation. It works beautifully alongside layered textiles, light woods and organic materials, giving the room a cozy yet refined vibe. Its subtle warmth makes it an ideal choice for creating a bedroom that feels serene and welcoming.


*Featured Color:[Pink Shadow SW 0070](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/red-paint-colors/sw0070-pink-shadow)*


## **When Style Meets Function**


Laundry rooms deserve just as much love as the rest of the home, and[Pink Shadow](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/red-paint-colors/sw0070-pink-shadow) proves that utilitarian spaces can be stylish, too. This delicate, blush-infused hue adds a fresh, cheerful quality that instantly brightens the room. Soft and approachable, Pink Shadow brings warmth without feeling overly sweet, helping to create a space that feels clean, uplifting and thoughtfully designed.


*Featured Color:[Persimmon SW 6339](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/orange-paint-colors/sw6339-persimmon)*


## **A Warm Welcome at the Front Door**


Make a memorable first impression with[Persimmon](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/orange-paint-colors/sw6339-persimmon) in a small foyer space. This vibrant terracotta-orange hue welcomes guests with energy and warmth from the moment they step inside. In compact entryways, Persimmon creates a sense of personality and charm while highlighting architectural details and decorative accents. Balanced with neutral décor and natural elements, this bold color brings vibrancy to the space without overwhelming it, setting an inviting tone for the rest of the home.
