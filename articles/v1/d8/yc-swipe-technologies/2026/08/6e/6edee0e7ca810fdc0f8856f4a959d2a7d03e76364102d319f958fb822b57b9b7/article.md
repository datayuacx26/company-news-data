# New GST E-way bill rules 2026: Mandatory ship-to GSTIN in bill-to/ship-to transactions - extended till 1st August 2026

# **New GST E-Way Bill Rules 2026: Mandatory Ship-To GSTIN in Bill-To/Ship-To transactions - Extended till 1st August 2026**


> Accordingly, the mandatory capture of **"Ship To GSTIN"** in Bill-To/Ship-To transactions and the **Voluntary Closure of E-Way Bill** functionality shall be implemented with effect from **1st August, 2026** , instead of **15th June, 2026** .
>
>
> Drivers and authorised persons can close E-Way Bills using their mobile numbers after goods are delivered. The mobile number must be added for closure purposes. After OTP verification, the portal shows active E-Way Bills linked with that number. The driver or authorised person can then select the relevant E-Way Bill and submit the closure request.


## **Introduction**


The E-Way Bill system is getting a useful update from 15 June 2026, **however, the implementation date has been extended till 1st August 2026** . Under this update, a voluntary E-Way Bill closure facility will allow businesses and transport teams to mark an e-Way Bill as closed once the goods are delivered.


Earlier, many E-Way Bills remained open in the system even after delivery was completed. This often created confusion during tracking, reconciliation and compliance checks. With the new closure facility, businesses can maintain cleaner records and confirm that the goods movement linked with an E-Way Bill has been completed.


The most practical part of this update is the mobile number-based closure. It allows drivers and authorised persons to close active e-Way Bills using their mobile numbers after delivery completion.


## **What is E-Way Bill Closure?**


E-Way Bill closure means marking an E-Way Bill as completed after the goods have reached the delivery location. It confirms that the goods movement connected with this E-Way Bill is complete.


Closure is not the same as cancellation. Cancellation is used when an E-Way Bill is generated by mistake or when goods are not moved. Closure is used only after goods have been transported and delivered.

For drivers and authorised persons, a mobile number must be provided for closure purposes. Once the number is linked, they can view and close the active E-Way Bills connected to that mobile number.


## **How mobile number based Closure works**


In many businesses, the E-Way Bill is generated by the accounts or dispatch team, while the actual delivery is completed by the driver, transport staff or authorised logistics person. Because of this, mobile number-based closure makes the process more practical.


When an E-Way Bill is generated, a mobile number can be entered for closure purposes. This number may belong to the driver, transporter staff or any authorised person responsible for delivery confirmation.


The mobile number can also be updated later during vehicle updation, consolidated E-Way Bill activity or extension of validity.


After the goods are delivered, the driver or authorised person can visit the E-Way Bill portal, use the closure option under the Search section, verify the mobile number through OTP and close the relevant active E-Way Bills.


## **Steps to Close E-Way Bill using mobile number**


Drivers and authorised persons can follow these steps:


- Visit the[E-Way Bill Portal.](https://ewaybillgst.gov.in/)
- Go to the Search option.
- Select the E-Way Bill closure facility.
- Enter the mobile number linked to closure.
- Verify the OTP received on that mobile number.
- View active E-Way Bills linked to your mobile number.
- Select the E-Way Bill or multiple E-Way Bills that need to be closed.
- Submit a closure request.
- Once submitted, the selected E-Way Bills will be marked as closed.

## **When should an E-Way Bill be Closed?**


An e-Way Bill should be closed only after the goods are delivered. It should not be closed while goods are still in transit.


Situation Closure Allowed?


Goods are still in transit No


Goods have reached the destination Yes


Same day of delivery Yes


Immediately, the next day after delivery Yes


Before delivery completion No


Businesses should ensure that drivers, transporters and authorised persons close the e-Way Bill on time. This helps reduce the mismatch between actual delivery status and portal records.


## **Why this facility is useful**


The mobile number-based E-Way Bill closure facility is useful because it gives businesses a better way to confirm completed deliveries. It reduces the number of active e-Way Bills that remain open even after goods are delivered.


Key benefits include:


- Cleaner E-Way Bill records.
- Better delivery tracking.
- Reduced confusion during audits.
- Better coordination between accounts, dispatch and transport teams.
- Improved proof of completed goods movement.
- Reduced dependency on taxpayer login access for delivery confirmation.
- Better matching between invoices, transport and delivery records.


For businesses that use ERP or accounting systems, this update is important. They should ensure their software and internal processes can capture mobile numbers for closure and train staff before using the facility.


## **Common mistakes to avoid**


Businesses, drivers and authorised persons should avoid the following mistakes:


- Do not close the E-Way Bill before delivery.
- Do not enter the wrong mobile number for closure.
- Do not confuse closure with cancellation.
- Do not delay closure after delivery.
- Do not assume that every driver can close every E-Way Bill.
- Do not ignore OTP verification.
- Do not close the wrong E-Way Bill by mistake.


## **Business readiness checklist**


Before using the mobile number-based closure facility, businesses should prepare internally.


- Train drivers and authorised persons.
- Please add the correct mobile number for closure.
- Update dispatch and logistics SOPs.
- Inform transporters about the new process.
- Keep the proof of delivery for your records.
- Update ERP and accounting systems if required.
- Close E-Way Bills on the same day or the next day after delivery.
- Review active e-Way Bills regularly.


## **Conclusion**


The mobile number-based E-Way Bill closure facility is a practical update for businesses, drivers, transporters and authorised persons. This allows the person handling the actual delivery to close the E-Way Bill after the goods are delivered.


Although the facility is voluntary, it can help businesses maintain cleaner records, improve delivery tracking and reduce confusion during compliance checks. Companies should train their dispatch teams, drivers and authorised persons so that E-Way Bills are closed correctly and on time.

## **FAQs**


### **Can a driver close an E-Way Bill?**


Yes, a driver can close an E-Way Bill if the driver’s mobile number is provided for closure and the goods have been delivered.


### **Can an authorised person close an E-Way Bill using a mobile number?**


Yes, an authorized person can close the active E-Way Bills linked to their mobile number after OTP verification.


### **Is OTP required for E-Way Bill closure?**


Yes, OTP verification is required when closing an E-Way Bill through the mobile number-based closure facility.


### **When should an E-Way Bill be closed?**


An E-Way Bill should be closed after delivery, either on the same day or on the next day.


### **Is E-Way Bill closure mandatory?**


No, E-Way Bill closure is voluntary, but it helps businesses keep records cleaner and more accurate.


### **Is E-Way Bill closure the same as cancellation?**


No. Closure is used after successful delivery, while cancellation is used when an E-Way Bill is wrong or when goods are not moved.
