## **1. International Payments Capability**


PayU supports international payments, but access is typically **approval-based** and depends on additional configuration and underwriting. This can introduce delays and inconsistency for businesses that rely heavily on global revenue.


xPay is designed specifically for cross-border use cases. International payments are **enabled by default** , allowing businesses to accept global transactions without additional setup layers.


For companies targeting international customers, this distinction becomes critical in reducing time to go live.


## **2. Payment Success Rates and Revenue Impact**


Payment success rate is one of the most important metrics for global businesses. Even small improvements can significantly increase revenue.


PayU provides standard processing capabilities, but it is not optimized specifically for cross-border approval scenarios.


xPay focuses on improving authorization rates through:


-


Local acquiring


-


Intelligent routing


-


Issuer-level optimization


This results in **higher approval rates and reduced payment failures** , particularly for international cards and wallets.
