# How Locket Grew Subscriptions by 77% with Helium

Matt and team were excited about Helium's vision of self-improving paywalls and decided to reach out. Within just a few days, their team had implemented the iOS SDK with RevenueCat and was off to the races.


> “Onboarding was a breeze. Once I got the API key it took just a day or two and 15 lines of code and we were live.”


The Helium team worked with Locket to create an experimentation plan, and then started generating the new test paywalls with Helium’s AI Editor, starting by testing a new set of creatives.


Helium started by improving the basics, like plan display, CTA, and exit logic. Then, we generated new heroes drawing on some of Locket Gold's main features.


Speaking to Matt, he highlighted “The Helium team really is great. They’re experts in subscription flows. It’s so nice to know that I can send a slack any time of day and get a response back immediately. It’s awesome to work with a team that moves as fast as we do”


Even after the first experiment, subscription revenue was up substantially. Through paywall improvements, we were able to identify which features spoke most to users, encourage more users to adopt the annual subscription, and improve overall conversion.


> “Our first A/B test alone made more than 15-times Helium’s costs, and we’re just getting started. It’s hard to find better ROI than that”


The Helium team did additional testing to identify how to best convert users at each entrypoint and internationally, and have seen an outsized conversion lift for new users.
