# Relationship Business Money Market


The Relationship Business Money Market **¹** is an account designed to provide higher Relationship Rates when you have additional business accounts opened with Western Alliance Bank.


## Features & Benefits


- Make unlimited deposits
- Earn tiered interest, according to the balances kept within the account


## How it Works


- To qualify for the Relationship Business Money Market2; similar ownership must be listed on related accounts and hold an aggregate balance of $50,000. Qualifying related account types include: Business Checking, Analyzed Business Checking, Business Money Market, Business Savings, or Business CDs
- To qualify for the Relationship Business Money Market with balances under $50,000, New Money of $10,000 is required
- New Money requirement and Account Relationship requirement is waived for Relationship Business Money Market accounts with a balance of $1,000,000 or more
- Interest Rate Tier is determined by aggregate balance of all related accounts held in similar ownership
- Interest Earned is determined by balance held in the Relationship Business Money Market Account
