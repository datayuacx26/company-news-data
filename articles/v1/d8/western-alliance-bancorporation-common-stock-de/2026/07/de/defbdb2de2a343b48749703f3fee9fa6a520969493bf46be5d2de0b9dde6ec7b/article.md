Ben Reneau is vice president for Western Alliance Bank’s Private Client Group. Based in Las Vegas, he delivers white-glove, concierge-style private banking to high-net-worth clients across the Nevada market, providing tailored solutions and a relationship-driven experience to serve clients’ personal and business banking needs.


Mr. Reneau joined Western Alliance in 2025, bringing more than two decades of experience partnering with high-net-worth individuals and business owners, building long-term relationships and delivering strategic solutions that drive business growth, leadership development and effective problem solving. He offers clients a strong ability to anticipate client needs and provide immediate, direct access to deliver innovative solutions and proactively manage banking priorities for an elevated banking experience.


Mr. Reneau volunteers as a youth baseball coach with Paseo Verde Little League in Henderson, where he has spent five years mentoring young athletes and instilling teamwork, leadership and life skills. An avid golfer, he is a longtime member of Anthem Country Club in Henderson, Nevada, where he serves on the Membership Committee to support the club’s long term growth and enhance the overall member experience.


He earned a Regents Bachelor of Arts degree from West Virginia University Institute of Technology.

## Western Alliance Bank


Western Alliance Bancorporation (NYSE: WAL) is one of the country’s top-performing banking companies and has ranked as a top U.S. bank by American Banker and Bank Director since 2016. Its primary subsidiary, Western Alliance Bank, is a leading national bank for business that puts customers first, delivering tailored business banking solutions and consumer products backed by outstanding, personalized service and specific expertise in more than 30 industries and sectors. With $90 billion in assets and offices nationwide, Western Alliance excels at helping businesses of all sizes capitalize on their opportunities to solve today and succeed tomorrow.
