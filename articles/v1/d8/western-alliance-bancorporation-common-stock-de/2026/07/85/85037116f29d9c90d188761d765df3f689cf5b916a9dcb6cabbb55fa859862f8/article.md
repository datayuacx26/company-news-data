# Business Money Market


With Western Alliance Bank's Business Money Market, your money works as hard for your business as you do. You earn interest, have access to online banking with check-writing capabilities and more.


## Features & Benefits


- Offers competitive interest rates
- Business Money Market is a tiered interest account
- Monthly service charge of $10 waived for non-profit organizations, or for holding a daily balance of $2,500
- Includes no-fee online banking2


## Other Key Facts


- Minimum daily balance of $0.01 required to earn interest
- Requires $1,000 opening deposit
- Up to 25 items deposited per month; $0.35 per item deposited in excess of 25
