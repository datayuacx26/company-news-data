# WA - Business Checking Accounts - Hybrid Business Checking

Commercial Checking with Interest-Bearing Benefits


# Hybrid Business Checking


Hybrid Business Checking accounts are available for our commercial customers and combine the benefits of an Analyzed Business Checking account with the features of an interest-bearing savings account in one.


## Features & Benefits


- Receive an earnings credit allowance calculated on the investable balance1 in the account and earn interest on excess balance once balances offset service charges
- Combine multiple Hybrid Business Checking accounts for billing purposes2


### Other Key Facts


- Take advantage of our full suite of Treasury Management Services


### Account Services


Additional tools and services are available for accounts:


- Overdraft protection3
- Online banking4
- ATM/Debit cards
- Ordering checks
- eStatements
