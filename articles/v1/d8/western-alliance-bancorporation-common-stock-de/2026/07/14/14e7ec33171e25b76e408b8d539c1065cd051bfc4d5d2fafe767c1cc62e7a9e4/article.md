Robby Pittaway is vice president and relationship manager for Western Alliance Bank's Private Client Group. Based in Phoenix, he advises high-net-worth individuals, business owners, and their families across the country on private banking and lending strategies.


With 15 years of banking experience, including extensive experience in commercial banking and relationship management, Mr. Pittaway brings a unique perspective on the intersection of business ownership, liquidity, credit strategy and personal wealth management. He works closely with clients and their professional advisors to align banking and lending solutions with broader financial objectives, delivering a high touch experience tailored to each client's unique circumstances.


A graduate of Arizona State University, Mr. Pittaway resides in Phoenix with his wife and children. Away from the office, he enjoys spending time outdoors, traveling, and playing golf.

## Western Alliance Bank
