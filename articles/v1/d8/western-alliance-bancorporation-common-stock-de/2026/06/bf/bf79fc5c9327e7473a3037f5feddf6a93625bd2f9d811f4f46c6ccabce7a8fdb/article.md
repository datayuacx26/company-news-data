# WA - Named # 1 Best Bank in Arizona on Forbes 2026 Best-In-State Banks List

**PHOENIX —** Western Alliance Bank (NYSE: WAL) today announced that it has been named to Forbes 2026 America’s Best-in-State Banks List, ranking #1 in Arizona and standing as the only Arizona-based bank included.


In its ninth year, the Forbes Best-In-State Banks list is among the industry’s most prestigious recognitions. In partnership with market research firm Statista, Forbes surveyed 26,000 U.S. consumers and analyzed more than 1.2 million public online reviews collected between February 2023 and March 2026. Banks are evaluated on key factors including trust, customer service, digital tools and quality of financial advice.


Headquartered in Phoenix, Western Alliance serves businesses and consumers nationwide with industry-specific expertise across more than 30 different sectors. The company employs more than 3,500 people across 34 states.


“We’re honored to be recognized by our clients on this year’s Forbes list,” said[Kenneth A. Vecchione](https://www.westernalliancebancorporation.com/find-a-banker/kenneth-vecchione) , Chairman, President and CEO of Western Alliance Bank. “Trust, transparency and responsiveness matter most to our clients. We stay focused on delivering strong banking capabilities and practical solutions that help them move forward with confidence. Being ranked #1 in Arizona makes this recognition especially meaningful, as it reflects the strength of the relationships we’ve built in our home market.”
