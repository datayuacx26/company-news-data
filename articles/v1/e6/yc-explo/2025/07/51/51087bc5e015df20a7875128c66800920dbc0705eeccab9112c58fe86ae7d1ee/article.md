# Explo named G2’s "Momentum Leader" in Embedded Business Intelligence

**Explo received 23 badges in G2’s 2024 Winter report. Here are some of the highlights:**


- Explo was ranked #1 out of 42 vendors for “Customer Relationship” in the Embedded BI category.
- Explo led all “High Performers” in the Embedded Business Intelligence category (#1 out of 8).
- Explo achieved a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 96, third highest in the Embedded BI category.
- 98% of users found Explo to meet or exceed all of their requirements.
- Explo received 100% satisfaction scores across its Predictive Analytics, Data Visualization, and Big Data services.
- Explo received a 9.08/10 score for usability (ease of admin, ease of use, and met requirements)
- Explo improved its average user rating to 4.8 out of 5 (previously 4.7).


*Note: These data points are not pulled from a small user review sample size, Explo had the highest number of user reviews (104) in the High Performer category for*[Embedded Business Intelligence](https://www.explo.co/blog/embedded-business-intelligence) *.*


Explo moves into the #1 spot across multiple performance metrics as the **leading Higher Performer in G2's Winter Report for Embedded Business Intelligence** . Customers’ belief in Explo was evident with 100% of users responding they believe Explo is headed in the right direction, and 99% of users would recommend Explo to a colleague. Furthermore, Explo has been honored with its first “Best Relationship” award, a testament to Explo’s customer-first mindset.
