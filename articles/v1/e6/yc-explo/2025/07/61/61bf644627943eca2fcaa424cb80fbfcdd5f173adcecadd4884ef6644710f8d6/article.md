# Explo Earns #7 Spot on G2’s 2024 Best Software Awards for Analytics

As the world’s largest and most trusted software marketplace,[G2](https://www.g2.com/?utm_source=vendor-news&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=best-software-2024) is visited by 90 million software buyers each year. Its annual Best Software Awards rank the world’s best software companies and products based on authentic, timely reviews from real users. G2 recently announced its annual[2024 Best Software Awards](https://www.g2.com/best?utm_source=vendor-news&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=best-software-2024) , **placing Explo #7 on the**[Best Software for Analytics list](https://www.g2.com/best-software-companies/top-analytics) .


Here’s what Gary Lin, co-founder of Explo had to say about the recognition;


> "Being named among the best analytics products for 2024 by G2 is not just an honor; it's a testament to our commitment to excellence and innovation in the analytics space. It validates our efforts to foster strong relationships with our customers and our product direction moving forward. We are incredibly proud to stand out in such a competitive field, and we see this as a motivation to continue pushing the boundaries of what's possible *in an analytics world* ."


[Explo’s mission](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K0zhZ5BqCXc) is to change how companies share data and build reports for every user. Leveraging low-code and Generative AI capabilities infused in its products, customers can create embeddable dashboards and[report builders](https://www.explo.co/products/report-builder) directly into their SaaS platform or web portal.


G2’s 2024 Best Software Awards feature more than 30 different lists, ranking software vendors and products using G2’s proprietary algorithm, which is based on G2’s verified user reviews and publicly available market presence data. To be eligible for the Best Software Awards, a software company or product must have received at least 50 approved reviews during the 2023 calendar year. Scores reflect only data from reviews submitted during this evaluation period.


> “B2B software buyers, just like consumers, start their purchasing journey with research,” said Sara Rossio, Chief Product Officer at G2. “Congratulations to the less than 1% of vendors listed on G2 who made one of our 30+ lists this year, achieving recognition driven by verified data rooted in the source that truly matters — authentic customer voice.”


In G2’s[recent Winter Report](https://www.explo.co/blog/explo-named-g2s-momentum-leader-in-embedded-business-intelligence) , Explo was named the "Momentum Leader" and moved into the #1 spot across multiple performance metrics as the leading Higher Performer for Embedded Business Intelligence. Customers’ belief in Explo was evident with 100% of users responding they believe Explo is headed in the right direction, and 99% of users would recommend Explo to a colleague. Furthermore, Explo was honored with its first “Best Relationship” award, a testament to Explo’s customer-first mindset.
