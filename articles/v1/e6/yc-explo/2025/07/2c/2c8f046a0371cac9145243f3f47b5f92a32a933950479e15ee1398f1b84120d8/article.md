# Explo named G2’s 2024 Summer "Momentum Leader", "Best Support", and “Best Customer Relationship” in Embedded Business Intelligence

**Explo received 22 badges in G2’s 2024 Summer report. Here are some of the highlights:**


- Explo was ranked **#1 out of 42 vendors for “Customer Relationship** ” in the Embedded BI category.
- Explo was named a **“Momentum Leader”** for Embedded Business Intelligence ahead of; Microsoft Power BI, ThoughtSpot, Tableau, Looker, Luzmo, and Sisense.
- Explo received **100% user satisfaction scores** across its Dashboards, Data Visualization, and Big Data services, beating the category average by 10%.
- Explo achieved **100% user satisfaction scores for Self-Service** .
- Explo received a **93/100 score for usability** (ease of admin, ease of use, and met requirements)
- Explo improved its **average user rating to 4.9 out of 5** (previously 4.8).


*Note: The Grid® scores represents the democratic voice of real software users, rather than the subjective opinion of one analyst. G2 rates products from the Embedded Business Intelligence category algorithmically based on data sourced from product reviews shared by G2 users and data aggregated from online sources and social networks.*


Explo was named a Momentum Leader for receiving the highest customer satisfaction performance metrics **in G2's Summer Report for Embedded Business Intelligence** . Explo’s customers rated the embedded analytics platform with a 99% "likelihood to recommend," a perfect 100% for "ease of doing business with," and 99% for "quality of support." Furthermore, Explo has been honored with its third consecutive, “Best Relationship” award, a testament to Explo’s customer-first mindset.
