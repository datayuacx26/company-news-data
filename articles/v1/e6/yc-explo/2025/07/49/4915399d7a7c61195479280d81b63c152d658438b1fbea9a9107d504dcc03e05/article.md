# Introduction


Data is the new gold. As organizations across the globe are navigating the nuances of a rapidly digitizing world, data has emerged as a central pillar of this new economy. Be it through identifying patterns, inferring insights, or projecting future trends, data plays an indispensable role in all strategic and operational decisions. However, these myriad data points are of little use if they cannot be processed, understood, and analyzed in an effective and efficient manner. This is where data visualization comes into play, transmuting raw, complex data into a form that is easily understandable, analyzable, and actionable.


Among the various data management systems available today, Rockset stands out for its ability to allow developers and data scientists to build data applications swiftly. Rockset is a real-time indexing database built for cloud, providing full-featured, fast SQL, and easy scaling without operational overhead. It leverages the agility of the cloud to deliver speed, scale, and simplicity, serving as an incredible tool for businesses that need to make quick and precise decisions based on vast amounts of data.


But how can we take advantage of Rockset’s capabilities and present this data in a manner that can be intuitively understood and leveraged by end-users? This is where Explo comes into the picture. It empowers users to create stunning visualizations without requiring extensive technical know-how, making it an excellent partner to the robust data-handling capabilities of[Rockset](https://www.explo.co/blog/introduction-to-visualization-in-rockset-and-explo) .


The combination of Rockset and Explo is a powerful one. By integrating these two platforms, users can transform their raw data stored in Rockset into meaningful insights using Explo's dynamic, interactive visualization tools. This integration has the potential to drastically enhance your data operations, turning the often-complex process of data exploration and visualization into a more streamlined, accessible, and informative experience.


Rockset's powerful real-time indexing capabilities allow for the rapid ingestion, transformation, and serving of data, ensuring up-to-date insights are readily available. Its cloud-native architecture means that it scales automatically with the data volume, reducing the operational overhead that is often a burden for many organizations. Moreover, with its Converged Indexing™ and distributed SQL engine, Rockset provides fast, efficient analytics over semi-structured data, which makes it a perfect fit for organizations dealing with diverse data sources and structures.


Explo, on the other hand, offers a user-friendly interface for building interactive dashboards and data apps. It is designed to democratize data, bridging the gap between complex data systems and end-users who need to understand and make decisions based on this data. With Explo, creating rich, interactive visualizations becomes a smooth process, without the need for extensive coding skills or deep knowledge of data analysis techniques. It's an agile tool, designed to allow users to quickly and efficiently build and iterate on data visualizations, based on their changing needs and evolving data landscape.


The seamless integration of Rockset and Explo paves the way for an enriched data visualization experience. With Rockset handling the data's scalability, indexing, and real-time processing, Explo can focus on presenting this data in an easy-to-understand, interactive format. Users can leverage the combined power of these platforms to dig deeper into their data, identify trends and patterns, make informed decisions, and convey complex information in a digestible manner.

## **Step 2: Establish a Connection from Rocket to Explo**
