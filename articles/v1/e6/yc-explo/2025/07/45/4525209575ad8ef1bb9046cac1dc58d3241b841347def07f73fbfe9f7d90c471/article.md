# Product Spotlight: Editable Dashboard Sections

We’re thrilled to spotlight a highly versatile and user-friendly feature: **Editable Dashboard Sections** .


This feature allows your users to create their own dashboard with a library of charts that you’ve defined. Create a standard template to start, and your users can add/remove charts and reformat the dashboard to show the most relevant metrics for their own organization.


With Editable Dashboards, we’ve bridged the gap between our standard Dashboards and[Report Builder](https://www.explo.co/products/report-builder) , providing your users with the ability to create their own dashboard without needing to create their own charts from scratch.


## Editable Dashboard Sections 101


Here are the key features and functionality you need to know about:


**Versatile Placement**


Editable Sections can be seamlessly integrated into an existing dashboard or used as a standalone dashboard. This versatility ensures that you can adapt it to fit perfectly within your current workflow or create a new, dedicated space for your custom data views.


**Flexible Functionality**


Enjoy the freedom to edit details and layouts according to your needs. Whether you’re fine-tuning specific elements or reimagining the entire layout, Editable Sections offer the flexibility to make it happen.


**Customizable Charts**


Choose from a variety of charts and visualizations to best represent your data. This feature allows you to tailor the presentation to suit different audiences and purposes, ensuring that your data is always communicated effectively.


**User-Level Customization**


Editable Sections support customization at both the end-user level and the tenant level. This means you can provide a personalized experience for individual users or implement consistent customization across your entire application.


**Create Unique Experiences**


The highly flexible nature of Editable Sections allows you to create a variety of unique experiences. Whether you need a detailed analytical dashboard or a simple overview, this feature can accommodate your requirements.


**User-Friendly Design**


Designed with non-technical users in mind, Editable Sections make it easy for everyone to create and customize dashboards. No specialized skills are required, empowering all users to take full advantage of this powerful feature.


**Export data**


Share data on the custom dashboard or export the entire dashboard. Explo support CSV, Excel, PDF, and image exports.

With Editable Sections, you have the power to offer a personalized end-user experience without overwhelming users with raw data.


**Seamless Integration** : Editable Sections fit effortlessly into your existing workflow, providing a streamlined experience without disruptions.

## How to Use Editable Sections


Using Editable Sections is straightforward and designed to be user-friendly, even for non-technical users. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you get started:


**Step 1: Access the Feature**


Navigate to the page or dashboard where you want to add an Editable Section. Look for the option to add or edit sections in your dashboard settings.


**Step 2: Choose Placement**


Decide whether you want to integrate the Editable Section into an existing page or create a new standalone dashboard. Select the appropriate option based on your needs.


**Step 3: Customize Layout and Details**


Use the intuitive drag-and-drop interface to arrange the layout of your section. You can add, remove, and resize various elements to fit your desired structure. Edit the details of each component, such as text, images, and data inputs, to ensure everything aligns with your requirements.


**Step 4: Select Visualizations**


Choose from a range of charts and visualizations to represent your data. Simply drag the desired chart type into your section and configure the data sources and display settings.


**Step 5: User-Level Customization**


Decide if you want the section to be customizable at the end-user level or the tenant level. Enable user-level customization to allow individual users to personalize their own dashboards, or apply settings universally for consistent branding and layout.


**Step 6: Save and Share**


Once you’re satisfied with your Editable Section, save your changes. You can then share the section as an image file or CSV, making it easy to distribute and collaborate with others.


**Step 7: Continuous Improvement**


Editable Sections are designed to be flexible, so you can continuously improve and update them as needed. Regularly review and refine your sections to keep them relevant and effective.
