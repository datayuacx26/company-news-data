# Explo Named #1 for Usability in Embedded BI, Earns 26 Badges in G2’s Summer 2025 Report

The results are in — and Explo has once again emerged as a leader in Embedded Business Intelligence. In G2’s **Summer 2025 Report** , Explo was recognized across **36 reports** , earning **26 total badges** including:


- 🏆 **Best Usability** (Ranked #1 out of 40 vendors in Embedded BI)
- 💪 **Momentum Leader** in Embedded BI
- ⭐ **Best Meets Requirements**
- 👨‍💻 **Easiest to Use**
- ⚙️ **Easiest Setup**
- 🤝 **Best Support**
- 🌎 **High Performer** for Embedded BI in the Americas
- 🧭 **Grid Leader** for Data Visualization
- 📈 **High Performer** for both SMB and Mid-Market Analytics Platforms


These wins reflect our continued focus on helping SaaS companies bring fast, flexible, and user-friendly analytics experiences to their end users — without the engineering lift.


### A Consistent Theme: Ease of Use That Drives Adoption


One of the most meaningful stats from this quarter’s report? Explo users reported a **70% average adoption rate** , compared to the **industry average of 52%** .


Explo also outperformed the industry on some of the most critical Embedded BI features:


- **Data Filtering:** 99% satisfaction (vs. 88% industry avg)
- **Data Visualization:** 99% (vs. 88%)
- **Data Governance:** 98% (vs. 85%)


With a **G2 review rating of 4.7/5** , customers consistently praise Explo for making analytics simple, customizable, and scalable — without slowing down dev teams.


### Powering Usability with AI


Much of this improvement in usability stems from our recent AI innovations, including the launch of **Explo AI Report Builder** — which allows users to generate dashboards and reports using natural language queries.


> “Our recent investments in AI — especially the Explo AI Report Builder — are making analytics dramatically more accessible for business users. These usability awards are a direct reflection of that progress.”


> — *Andrew Chen, Co-Founder at Explo*


Whether it’s empowering product teams to ship dashboards faster, or helping non-technical users generate insights on demand, Explo is raising the bar for what embedded analytics can (and should) feel like.
