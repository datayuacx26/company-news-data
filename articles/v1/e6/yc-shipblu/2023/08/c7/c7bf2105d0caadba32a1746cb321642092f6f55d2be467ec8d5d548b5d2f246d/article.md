# From Browsing to Buying: How BNPL Can Boost Your Online Sales

As an eCommerce business owner, you’re always on the lookout for ways to boost your online sales, attract new customers, and keep existing ones coming back. One of the latest trends in online shopping is **[Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)](https://www.investopedia.com/buy-now-pay-later-5182291)** , which allows customers to purchase items and pay for them later in installments. But what are the benefits of BNPL for online merchants, and how can you leverage this payment option to drive growth? Let’s dive in.


### Breaking Down the Benefits of Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) for Online Merchants


##### 1. Increased Sales and Conversion Rates


One of the biggest advantages of BNPL for online merchants is the potential for increased sales and conversion rates. By offering customers the option to pay for their purchases in installments, you can remove one of the biggest barriers to online shopping: **upfront costs** . This can be especially helpful for high-ticket items or products that customers might not otherwise be able to afford. By making it easier for customers to purchase from your store, you can boost sales and improve conversion rates.


##### 2. Attract New Customers


Another benefit of BNPL is the ability to attract new customers to your eCommerce store. Many shoppers are looking for ways to stretch their budgets, and BNPL can be a compelling option. By promoting BNPL as a payment option on your website, you can reach new customers who might not have considered shopping with you otherwise. And once they’ve made their first purchase using BNPL, they’re more likely to become loyal customers.


##### 3. Increased Average Order Value


Offering BNPL can also help you increase your[average order value (AOV)](https://www.shopify.com/blog/average-order-value#:~:text=Average%20order%20value%20(AOV)%20is%20the%20average%20dollar%20amount%20customers,your%20total%20number%20of%20customers.) . When customers have the option to pay in installments, they may be more likely to add additional items to their cart. This can lead to larger orders and higher AOVs for your eCommerce business. By promoting BNPL as a way to make larger purchases more manageable, you can encourage customers to spend more and boost your bottom line.


##### 4. Improved Customer Loyalty


Customer loyalty is critical for eCommerce businesses, and BNPL can be a powerful tool for building it. When customers use BNPL to make a purchase, they’re essentially entering into a relationship with the provider of the service. By providing excellent customer service and support throughout the repayment process, you can build trust and loyalty with your customers. And when they’re ready to make another purchase, they’ll be more likely to come back to your store.


##### 5. Reduced Risk of Fraud


BNPL can also help reduce the risk of fraud for online merchants. Because the provider of the service assumes the risk of non-payment, you don’t have to worry about chargebacks or fraudulent transactions. This can save you time and money, as you don’t have to spend resources investigating and disputing fraudulent activity.


##### 6. Competitive Advantage


Finally, offering BNPL can give you a competitive advantage in the crowded eCommerce marketplace. As more and more retailers adopt BNPL, it’s becoming an increasingly popular payment option for shoppers. By offering BNPL alongside traditional payment options, you can stand out from your competitors and provide a more convenient and flexible shopping experience for your customers.


##### Conclusion


In conclusion, Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) can offer a range of benefits for online merchants. By using this payment option to increase sales and conversion rates, attract new customers, increase AOV, improve customer loyalty, reduce the risk of fraud, and gain a competitive advantage, you can scale and enhance your eCommerce business. Whether you’re just starting out or looking to grow your existing eCommerce store, BNPL is worth considering as part of your payment strategy.
