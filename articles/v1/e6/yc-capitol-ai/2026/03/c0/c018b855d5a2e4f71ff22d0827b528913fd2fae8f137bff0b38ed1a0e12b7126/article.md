# CapitoI Has Established a New Advisory Board

Capitol AI has established a new advisory board as part of its continued international expansion, bringing together senior figures from media, government and industry to help guide its growth across the UK and Europe.


The board will provide strategic counsel on client priorities, market dynamics, and sector-specific challenges, as the company scales its presence in regulated and high-stakes industries.


The advisory board launches with two high-profile appointments: Lionel Barber, former Editor of the Financial Times, and Lord Vaizey of Didcot, member of the House of Lords and former Minister for Culture, Communications and Creative Industries.


Both bring deep experience at the intersection of policy, media, and technology, with the advisory board’s formation coming as Capitol AI accelerates its expansion into the UK and Europe.


On his appointment, Lionel Barber commented: *“I am delighted to be part of this exciting new venture at the forefront of the AI revolution. Shaun, Tom, and Capitol AI have already established an impressive track record. I look forward to helping the team move to the next level”.*


Lord Vaizey of Didcot said: *"It is fantastic to see a US company recognising the strength of the UK’s AI and tech landscape, and the opportunities it offers. Capitol helps high-stakes sectors move away from reliance on singular models, optimising performance and cost-efficiency. I am excited to be involved in their journey, and look forward to supporting them as they continue to expand into the UK and European market".*
