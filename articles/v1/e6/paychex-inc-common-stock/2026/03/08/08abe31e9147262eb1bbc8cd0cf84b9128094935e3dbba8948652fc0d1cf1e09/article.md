# Paychex Schedules Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Earnings Conference Call on March 25, 2026


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_e28818058960ba3ff3291d4ce02af69d/paychex/news/2026-03-11_Paychex_Schedules_Third_Quarter_Fiscal_2026_828.pdf) March 11, 2026 8:30am EDT


ROCHESTER, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Paychex, Inc.](https://www.paychex.com/) (Nasdaq: PAYX), an industry-leading human capital management ("HCM") company, will release financial results for its fiscal 2026 third quarter ended February 28, 2026 on Wednesday, March 25, 2026, before the financial markets open.


The company will host a conference call at 9:30 a.m. ET on Wednesday, March 25, 2026 to discuss these results. Participating in this call will be[John Gibson](https://www.paychex.com/newsroom/executive-bios/john-gibson) , President and Chief Executive Officer, and[Bob Schrader](https://www.paychex.com/newsroom/executive-bios/bob-schrader) , Chief Financial Officer.


The conference call will be webcast live and available for replay on the[Paychex Investor Relations portal.](https://investor.paychex.com/)


**About Paychex**


Paychex, Inc. (Nasdaq: PAYX) is the digitally driven HR leader that is reimagining how companies address the needs of today’s workforce with the most comprehensive, flexible, and innovative HCM solutions for organizations of all sizes. Offering a full spectrum of HR advisory and employee solutions, Paychex pays 1 out of every 11 American private sector workers and is raising the bar in HCM for approximately 800,000 customers in the U.S. and Europe.
