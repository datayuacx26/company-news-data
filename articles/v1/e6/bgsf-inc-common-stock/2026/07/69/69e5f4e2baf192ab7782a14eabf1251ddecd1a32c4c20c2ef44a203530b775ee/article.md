# Your Amenities Might Be Costing You Renewals

When was the last time you looked at your amenity package through the eyes of your residents?


For years, multifamily communities have competed by adding more. Larger fitness centers, coworking lounges, dog parks, package lockers, rooftop spaces, coffee bars, and smart home technology. Amenities have become a major selling point, and in many markets, they're expected.


But here's the question more operators should be asking:


***Are your amenities actually driving renewals, or quietly driving residents away?***


The answer may have less to do with


*what* you offer and more to do with


*how* those amenities are maintained, managed, and supported.


##


**What's Costing You Renewals?**


###


**Residents Notice the Experience, Not Just the Feature**


A state-of-the-art fitness center sounds impressive during a tour. A fitness center with broken equipment, poor cleaning, and inconsistent hours? That's what residents remember when renewal season arrives.


The same is true for nearly every amenity:


-


Pools that frequently close for maintenance


-


Package rooms that overflow


-


Coworking spaces with unreliable Wi-Fi


-


Dog parks that aren't maintained


-


Clubhouses that are difficult to reserve


-


Smart technology that regularly malfunctions


Residents aren't comparing your amenities to the brochure. They're comparing them to the experience they have every day.


###


**The Hidden Cost of Understaffed Communities**


Many communities have invested heavily in amenities over the past decade. But maintaining those spaces requires something that can't be purchased during construction:


**People.**


Maintenance teams, porters, leasing professionals, and community managers all play a role in keeping amenities clean, functional, and enjoyable.


When communities are understaffed, priorities naturally shift toward emergency work orders, resident requests, and move-ins. Preventive maintenance and amenity upkeep often fall to the bottom of the list.


The result? Amenities slowly become liabilities instead of value-adds.


###


**Expectations Continue to Rise**


Today's renters have more choices than ever. They're also accustomed to seamless experiences in nearly every aspect of daily life.


If a resident pays amenity fees or premium rent, they expect those amenities to consistently deliver. Small frustrations can add up over the course of a lease:


-


Equipment stays broken for weeks.


-


Shared spaces aren't cleaned regularly.


-


Outdoor areas become unusable.


-


Technology doesn't work as advertised.


-


Residents stop using amenities altogether.


When renewal time comes, these experiences become part of the overall perception of the community.


##


**Staffing Is Part of Your Amenity Strategy**


Amenity investments don't end after installation. Communities need adequate staffing to:


-


Perform preventive maintenance


-


Respond quickly to repairs


-


Maintain cleanliness and curb appeal


-


Support resident events and shared spaces


-


Ensure technology remains operational


-


Create positive resident interactions


Even the newest amenities lose value when they aren't properly supported and maintained.


##


**A Better Question for Operators**


Instead of asking,


*"What new amenity should we add?"*


Ask:


-


Are residents actually using our existing amenities?


-


Which amenities generate the most service requests?


-


What resident complaints appear most frequently?


-


Are maintenance teams able to keep up with preventive maintenance?


-


Do we have enough staff to support the resident experience we promise?


Sometimes improving an existing amenity delivers a greater return than building a new one.


##


**The Amenity Experience: Where Renewals Are Really Won**


Residents rarely renew because of one impressive feature. They renew because they feel comfortable, valued, and confident that their community is well managed.


*A clean pool. A working package room. A reliable fitness center. A welcoming clubhouse. A responsive maintenance team.*


These everyday experiences may seem small, but they create the trust and satisfaction that influence long-term resident loyalty. Communities that pair thoughtfully designed amenities with the staffing and support needed to maintain them are better positioned to improve satisfaction, protect their investments, and strengthen retention.


Before investing in your next amenity, take a closer look at the ones you already have. Strengthen your community with the right staffing support.
