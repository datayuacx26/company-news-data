# Work stress is putting pressure on romantic relationships

---


### Methodology


- **Online survey** conducted by Dynata
- **Field dates:** November 7–15, 2025
- **Respondents:** 2,001 U.S. adults


### Qualifications & Quotas


- U.S. resident
- Age 18–64, with quotas:


18–24 (12%), 25–34 (22%), 35–44 (22%), 45–54 (22%), 55–64 (22%)


- Gender quotas: 50/50 split between male and female (non-binary included in total)
- Annual pre tax household income of $50,000+, with quotas:


$50k–$74k (12.5%), $75k–$99k (18.75%), $100k–$149k (18.75%), $150k–$199k (18.75%), $200k–$249k (18.75%), $250k+ (12.5%)


- Annual pre tax individual income of $25,000+
- Currently employed part time (8–31 hours/week) or full time (32+ hours/week)


- Self-employed respondents excluded to maintain focus on employer impacts
