## OGE Energy Corp. Board of Directors declares quarterly dividend

OKLAHOMA CITY


, Dec. 3, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- OGE Energy Corp. (NYSE: OGE), the parent company of Oklahoma Gas and Electric Company, announced today that its Board of Directors approved a first quarter dividend of $0.425 per common share of stock, to be paid on January 30, 2026, to shareholders of record on January 5, 2026.
