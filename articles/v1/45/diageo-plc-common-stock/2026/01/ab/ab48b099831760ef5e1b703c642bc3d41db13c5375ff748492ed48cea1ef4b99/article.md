# Diageo completes sale of Sheridan’s to Casa Redondo

## Diageo completes sale of Sheridan’s to Casa Redondo


**30 January 2026:** Today, Diageo has completed the sale of Sheridan’s, a two-part coffee-cream liqueur, to Casa Redondo. A Transitional Services Agreement has been agreed to ensure a smooth transition and continuity of the business.


This transaction is an example of Diageo’s sharp focus on effective portfolio management and maximising shareholder value. It follows previous European transactions including the sales of Pampero, Cacique and Safari.

**About Diageo**


Diageo is a global leader in beverage alcohol with an outstanding collection of brands across spirits and beer categories. These brands include Johnnie Walker, Crown Royal, J&B and Buchanan's whiskies, Smirnoﬀ and Ketel One vodkas, Captain Morgan, Baileys, Don Julio, Tanqueray and Guinness.


Diageo is a global company, and our products are sold in nearly 180 countries around the world. The company is listed on both the London Stock Exchange (DGE) and the New York Stock Exchange (DEO). **About Casa Redondo**


Casa Redondo is a third-generation beverage-alcohol company started in 1940 by José Carranca Redondo. The Company’s flagship brand is Licor Beirão, the best-selling spirit brand in Portugal. The Company also manages Aperitivo Per Se, Amarguinha, FoxTale Gin and has recently expanded its portfolio with the purchase of Safari from Diageo.
