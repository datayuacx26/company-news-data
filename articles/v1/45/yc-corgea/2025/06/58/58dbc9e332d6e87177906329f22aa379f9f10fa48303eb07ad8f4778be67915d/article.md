# Introducing the new Scan Details Page

The scan details page represents a significant advancement in security vulnerability management, offering teams an unprecedented level of insight and control over their codebase’s security posture. At its core, this feature provides a comprehensive, interactive dashboard that transforms how security teams analyze and address vulnerabilities found by[Corgea AI SAST](https://corgea.com/products/ai-sast) and related scanners.


What sets this feature apart is its innovative dual-view system, allowing teams to analyze vulnerabilities through two distinct lenses: by CWE (Common Weakness Enumeration) classification or by file path. This flexibility enables teams to approach security issues from both a vulnerability-type perspective and a code-location perspective, making it easier to identify patterns and prioritize fixes based on their specific needs.


The page’s sophisticated filtering system allows teams to drill down into specific aspects of their security landscape. Users can filter by severity levels (Critical, High, Medium, Low), fix status, and even focus on newly discovered issues. This granular control, combined with real-time updates, ensures that teams can quickly identify and address the most pressing security concerns.


A standout feature is the intelligent fix tracking system, which automatically categorizes vulnerabilities into statuses such as “Fix Available,” “On Hold,” “False Positive,” and “Processing.” This automated categorization, combined with SLA status tracking, helps teams maintain clear visibility into their remediation progress and ensures nothing falls through the cracks. The broader reporting layer is covered in[Corgea Reporting](https://corgea.com/blog/corgea-reporting-security-and-developer-insights-in-one-view) .


The page also includes a powerful overview panel that provides at-a-glance metrics about the security status of the codebase. This includes severity distribution, fix status percentages, and processing progress, giving teams immediate insight into their security posture and remediation progress.


What makes this feature particularly valuable is its ability to adapt to different team workflows. Whether teams prefer to work by vulnerability type or by file location, the interface provides the necessary tools and information in an intuitive, accessible format. This flexibility, combined with the comprehensive filtering and tracking capabilities, makes the scan details page an essential tool for modern security teams looking to maintain robust security practices while maximizing development efficiency.


The feature’s integration with existing project management systems and its ability to handle both repository-based and zip-upload scenarios further enhances its utility, making it a versatile solution for teams working in various development environments. This combination of powerful analysis tools, flexible viewing options, and comprehensive tracking capabilities positions the scan details page as a game-changing tool in the security vulnerability management landscape.
