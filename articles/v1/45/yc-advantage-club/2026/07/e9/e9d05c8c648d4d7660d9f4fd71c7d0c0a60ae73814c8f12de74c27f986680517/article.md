# Stay Interviews: 15 Questions to Ask Before Employees Think About Leaving

Most employees don’t decide to quit on a random day. The decision usually builds over time through small frustrations, unmet expectations, or a growing sense of disconnect. By the time those issues come up in an exit interview, there’s little chance to change the outcome. Stay interview questions help managers and HR teams understand what employees value, what’s getting in their way, and why they choose to stay. For organizations in manufacturing, retail, and healthcare, these conversations can make a measurable difference in retention.
