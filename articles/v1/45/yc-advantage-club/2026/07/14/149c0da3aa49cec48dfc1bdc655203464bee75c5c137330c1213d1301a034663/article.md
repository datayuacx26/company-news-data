# 10 Proven Ways to Improve Your Glassdoor Rating & Employer Reputation

1. **Step 1:** Respond promptly, while the conversation is still relevant, so candidates and employees see that feedback is taken seriously.
2. **Step 2:** Thank employees for their feedback, regardless of tone; starting with appreciation builds trust.
3. **Step 3:** Acknowledge concerns without becoming defensive; focus on understanding the issue, not debating it.
4. **Step 4:** Share improvements where appropriate, briefly note relevant changes to show you’re following through.
5. **Step 5:** Learn from recurring feedback; the same complaint showing up again and again usually points to a real operational or cultural issue worth fixing.
