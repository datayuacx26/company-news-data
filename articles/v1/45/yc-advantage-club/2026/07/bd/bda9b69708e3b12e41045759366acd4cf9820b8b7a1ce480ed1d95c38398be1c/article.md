# Leadership Insights: A conversation with Dana McCormick

**Dana:**


So involve your employees, and ask them how they want to be recognized. Some employees don’t want public recognition, and some employees want money. Some employees want to be recognized in front of all their peers. So what can run the gamut on what that looks like? But I’m a huge proponent of involving employees before we roll out any types of programs because you get their buy-in, and then it helps them to kind of take ownership of the program.


So, from a motivation standpoint, I would ask, what does that look like? At one company, one of our values was innovation, and so we rolled out an innovative program that asked for not the old school employee suggestion program, but we asked them for innovative ideas. And these innovative ideas could be something that is for the company, it could be for employees, it could be anything pertaining to servicing our clients better. There was nothing off the table. So involve those employees who are actually doing the job. They’re going to know better how to explain it than anybody else. So seek their involvement.


And we rolled out an innovative idea suggestion program, and employees would come forward with their ideas. And it was actually documented. So it became visible, and it was assigned an owner. And the owner had to be a manager in the company. They took ownership of that so that it wasn’t just something that was the flavor of the day. And then once a week, they had to give an update on the progress. Can it be implemented? Can it not? And what is this desired outcome? So if you want to, for example, one of the innovative ideas is to cut down on the number of days that it takes to fulfill a client’s request, whatever it may be, what’s the outcome? You want to cut it down from seven days to five days, two days, whatever that looks like. So then you had a well-formulated approach as to how you were going to roll this out and then what the outcome was.


So we always welcome the ideas of the employees. And that’s just one. And there are certainly several other types of recognition programs here at Simeio. We have an HRIS that acts as a social media type of platform, and employees give kudos to others, their peers, and other employees all the time. So we see it, and it’s a common occurrence here at the company. People regularly get kudos.
