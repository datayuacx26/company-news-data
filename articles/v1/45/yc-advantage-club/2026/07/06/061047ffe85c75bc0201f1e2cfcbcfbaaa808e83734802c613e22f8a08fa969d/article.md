**Girija**


So I think personally, if I had to say my cheerleaders for my career have been three key people from my family, my mother, because I think I remember growing up, or when I’m talking to my niece about it, and keep telling her she’s in her teens. So I keep talking to her about various things and life skills and stuff. And I remember my mother always told me, and I come from a small town where my parents or their generation have probably been from the rural side and have moved to a city.


They have been the first generation to come to a city. And I’m from a family where I don’t think anyone in my parents’ generation, the women, has been career-oriented.


But my mother’s always been imbibing these values equally between my brother, my sister, and me, that you have to have an education that will help you be independent in your life.
