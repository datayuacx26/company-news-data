# Employee Engagement vs. Employee Happiness: 5 Key Differences HR Leaders Should Know

**Dimension**


**Happiness**


**Engagement**


**Why It Matters**


Core question


“Do I enjoy working here?”


“Am I committed to this organization’s success?”


Perks can buy happiness; they can’t buy commitment


Volatility


Shifts daily with workload, mood, shift demands


Stays steady, rooted in trust and purpose


Engagement is a better predictor of long-term retention


Effort level


Employees complete assigned tasks


Employees volunteer, problem-solve, go beyond scope


Discretionary effort is where engagement pays off operationally


How it’s measured


Pulse surveys, satisfaction scores, sentiment


Participation, recognition activity, goal completion, retention


Different metrics — tracking only one gives an incomplete picture


Business impact


Shapes culture and day-to-day morale


Drives productivity, collaboration, and customer/patient outcomes


Engagement has a stronger downstream effect on performance
