# Referral Programs vs. Loyalty Programs: 6 Differences to Help You Decide Which to Build First

1. **Define your primary goal:** Do you need more customers, more repeat visits, or lower acquisition costs? Your answer points toward referrals or loyalty.
2. **Evaluate your existing customer base:** Look at repeat purchase frequency, satisfaction, and how often customers already recommend you. If they’re already spreading the word naturally, a referral program can build on that. If repeat purchases are inconsistent, loyalty may deliver better returns.
3. **Assess purchase frequency:** Businesses with frequent purchases, such as retailers and healthcare providers offering recurring services, often see quicker returns from loyalty programs. Manufacturing businesses with longer sales cycles may benefit more from referral programs instead.
4. **Consider budget and technology:** Before launching either program, confirm you have a clear reward structure, incentive budget, customer data for personalization, and a platform to automate tracking.
5. **Plan for future integration:** Most organizations start with one program based on immediate priorities and add the other as customer relationships mature.
