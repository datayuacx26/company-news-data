# Employer Branding vs Employee Branding: 7 Key Differences Explained

1. **Company Story vs Employee Voice:** Employer branding delivers official messaging; employee branding reflects authentic, unscripted experiences.
2. **Talent Attraction vs Daily Representation:** One drives recruitment; the other shapes ongoing workplace reputation.
3. **HR Ownership vs Shared Responsibility:** Employer branding sits with HR and leadership; employee branding depends on managers and employees as active contributors.
4. **Brand Promise vs Brand Experience:** Employer branding sets expectations; employee branding is the reality employees actually live.
5. **Marketing Campaigns vs Everyday Behaviors:** One is planned communication; the other is built in daily interactions.
6. **External Reputation vs Internal Engagement:** Employer branding shapes candidate perception; employee branding drives motivation and belonging.
7. **Short-Term Hiring Goals vs Long-Term Culture Building:** Employer branding tracks recruitment metrics; employee branding sustains engagement and retention over time.
