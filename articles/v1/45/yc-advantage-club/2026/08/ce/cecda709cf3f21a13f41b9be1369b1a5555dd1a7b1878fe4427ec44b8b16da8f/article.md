# Digital Wellness: 8 Ways to Manage Screen Fatigue and “Always-On”

Digital wellness at work means helping employees use technology in ways that support productivity without compromising their wellbeing. As workplaces become increasingly connected, many employees struggle with screen fatigue at work, constant notifications, meeting overload, and difficulty disconnecting after work. These challenges are no longer just technology issues; they affect employee experience, engagement, morale, and retention. By redesigning digital work practices and personalizing support, HR leaders can create healthier workplaces without slowing business operations, an increasingly core part of any
