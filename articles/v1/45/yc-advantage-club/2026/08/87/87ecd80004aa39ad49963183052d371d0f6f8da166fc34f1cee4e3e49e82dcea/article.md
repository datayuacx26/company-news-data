# Employer Brand Audit: A 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist for HR Teams

**Audit Area**


**Key Question to Ask**


**Red Flag to Watch For**


Brand Promise vs. Reality


Does the EVP match employee feedback?


Recurring complaints that contradict recruitment messaging


Recognition Consistency


Is recognition equitable across departments?


Some teams recognized far more than others


Frontline Experience


Can shift and floor employees access recognition easily?


Frontline scores consistently lag corporate scores


Leadership Visibility


Do employees trust and see leadership?


Leadership seen as distant or inconsistent


Employee Voice


Are listening channels actually used?


Falling survey participation over time


Internal Communication


Is messaging consistent and two-way?


Employees hear news externally before internally


Well-being and Belonging


Do employees feel psychologically safe?


Rising burnout or disengagement signals


Recognition Programs


Is recognition frequent and fair?


Recognition clustered around a few individuals


Data Visibility


Can you track sentiment and analytics?


No reliable way to measure culture health


Continuous Improvement


Is there a review cadence and ownership?


Audit findings never translate into action
