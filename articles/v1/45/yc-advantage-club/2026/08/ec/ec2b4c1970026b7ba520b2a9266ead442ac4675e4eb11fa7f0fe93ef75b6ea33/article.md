# Cross-Team Peer Recognition: 7 Ways to Break Down Department Silos

**#**


**Strategy**


**What It Looks Like**


1


**Cross-Department Recognition Challenges**


Run monthly collaboration themes that encourage teams to recognize partners outside their own department.


2


**Peer Recognition Across Shared Projects**


Build recognition into product launches, store openings, and quality initiatives from the start.


3


**Recognition Circles Between Departments**


Create structured moments where marketing thanks operations, HR thanks IT, or nursing thanks facilities.


4


**Celebrate Everyday Collaboration Moments**


Recognize small wins and daily problem-solving, not only major milestones. Don’t wait for the big wins to say thanks.


5


**Build Recognition into ERGs and Communities**


Extend appreciation through employee resource groups, hobby clubs, and support networks.


6


**Spotlight Frontline Collaboration**


Highlight recognition of cross-functional collaboration in manufacturing, retail, and healthcare settings specifically.


7


**Use Digital Recognition Walls That Everyone Can See**


Ensure digital-first visibility so recognition reaches every department, not just the one giving it.
