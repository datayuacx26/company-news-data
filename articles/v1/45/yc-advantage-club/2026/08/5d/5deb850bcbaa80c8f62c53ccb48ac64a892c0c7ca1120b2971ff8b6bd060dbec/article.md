# 9 Wellness Program Ideas for Shift Workers and 24/7 BPO/Call Center Teams

**Shift Type**


**Primary Well-being Risk**


**Where Standard Wellness Programs Fall Short**


Day shift


Burnout from back-to-back meetings


Day-shift employees are often assumed to need less support, so well-being efforts may receive less attention.


Evening shift


Social isolation from family and peers


Wellness content and events are timed for daytime, so this group is scheduled out by default


Night shift


Circadian disruption, poor sleep quality


Generic sleep tips ignore inverted schedules and daytime sleep environments


Rotating shift


Inconsistent recovery, unstable routines


Programs built around fixed habits (daily steps, morning check-ins) don’t fit a schedule that changes weekly


Weekend/holiday shift


Feeling overlooked despite covering critical gaps


Recognition programs cluster around weekday, business-hours activity
