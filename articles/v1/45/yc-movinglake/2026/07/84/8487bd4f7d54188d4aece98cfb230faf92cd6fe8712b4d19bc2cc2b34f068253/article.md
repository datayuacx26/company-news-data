# Introducing Natural Language Querying: The Revolutionary New Feature from MovingLake

At MovingLake, we're always looking for ways to make data analysis more accessible to our users. That's why we're excited to announce the release of a brand new feature within our webapp at[app.movinglake.com](https://app.movinglake.com/) : **natural language querying.**


With natural language querying, users can now ask questions about their data in plain English. For example, you can ask " **What's the order count for March 2023?** " or " **Give me the average daily revenue for this year by day ordered by date** ". Our LLM (Language Language Model) technology understands the intent behind these questions and derives the necessary information from the tables in your dataset. It then generates the final SQL query for you, so you can get the answers you need without knowing how to write SQL code.


This launch is a game-changer for users who don't have the time or resources to learn SQL. It makes data analysis more accessible and democratizes access to insights. **Now anyone can access data visualizations and reports without needing to know how to write complex SQL queries.**


We're confident that natural language querying will be a valuable addition to our platform, and we can't wait to see how our users will take advantage of it. Whether you're a business analyst, marketer, or just curious about your data, this feature will help you get the insights you need faster and more efficiently.


To get started with natural language querying, simply log in to app.movinglake.com and try asking a question in plain English. Our LLM model will take care of the rest, generating the SQL query for you in seconds. We hope you enjoy this new feature and look forward to hearing your feedback!
