# Announcing MovingLake's Partnership with OwnerRez

We are pleased to announce our official partnership with[OwnerRez](https://www.ownerreservations.com/?utm_source=movinglake&utm_campaign=movinglake) , a renowned and leading Property Management System. Through this partnership, OwnerRez and MovingLake customers can now replicate their PMS data to a plethora of destinations ranging from Google Sheets to data warehouses, ERPs, CRMs, data lakes, message queues and more!


### About OwnerRez


OwnerRez is a leading Property Management System bringing an "Elite" experience for hosts and property managers of vacation rentals and short-term rentals. OwnerRez stands out as being named Elite Connectivity Partner by VRBO, Preferred Software Partner by Airbnb and Advanced Partner by Booking.com.
