## **The November 2024 Salary Restructuring**


The compensation adjustment implemented by Union Bank in November 2024 is the most material data point for any candidate evaluating an entry-level offer from the institution. Verified by an internal memo obtained by TechCabal and reported by multiple outlets including Legit.ng, the 40 percent increase affected all employees from executive trainees to general managers and outsourced associates. Entry-level executive trainees saw their monthly take-home rise from N260,000 to N364,000 per month, representing a N104,000 improvement in monthly compensation. This was the third salary adjustment the bank had implemented since 2022, reflecting a consistent pattern of upward revision in response to Nigeria’s inflationary environment. The bank’s personnel expenditure is projected to rise to N47.6 billion in 2024 as a result of this adjustment, up from N34 billion in 2023.

**What triggered the November 2024 salary increase at Union Bank?**


The increase was driven by the combined pressures of Nigeria’s currency devaluation and sustained inflation, which had significantly eroded the real value of employee salaries. Union Bank’s internal memo, as reported by TechCabal, cited the bank’s commitment to aligning compensation with industry standards and managing the financial strain on its workforce. The adjustment was also in line with similar moves by GTBank and Sterling Bank in the same period.


**Is Union Bank still a good employer after the Titan Trust acquisition?**


The post-acquisition period has been characterised by efforts to modernise the institution’s talent and compensation structures. The three salary adjustments since 2022, the November 2024 restructuring, and the reported investment in personnel costs all suggest that Titan Trust’s ownership has not been adverse for Union Bank employees.
