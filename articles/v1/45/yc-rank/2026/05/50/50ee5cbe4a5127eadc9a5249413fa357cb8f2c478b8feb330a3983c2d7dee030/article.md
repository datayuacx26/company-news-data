## **The Recent Salary Reset**


The most important context for understanding Deloitte Nigeria graduate trainee salary today is that the firm lifted its entry-level pay meaningfully during the 2024 Big Four pay reset. Graduate trainees previously earned approximately N300,000 monthly. The current published average sits at around N408,000 monthly, with the full confirmed Year 1 take-home range running from N470,000 to N535,000 net per month once allowances and step-ups are factored in. That is roughly a 36 percent increase from the pre-reset baseline.


The driver was talent competition. Through 2023 and into 2024, fintech firms, banks and consulting firms outside the Big Four were offering strong graduates entry packages at parity with or above Deloitte’s pre-reset figures. The reset brought Deloitte back into competitive position with PwC, EY and KPMG. Deloitte continues to operate a band-based grade structure rather than the explicit chartered and non-chartered split that some peers run, so all Year 1 graduate trainees enter on a unified pay structure regardless of whether they hold ACA from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria, ACCA, or no professional qualification at offer stage.


Chartered status still matters at Deloitte, but as a promotion accelerator and senior-grade pay differentiator rather than a Year 1 base pay differentiator. Deloitte continues to fully sponsor ACA, ACCA and CFA professional qualifications for trainees, including exam fees and paid study leave. The advice for new joiners remains the same; chart up as early as you can, even though the Year 1 pay no longer requires it.

## **Year-over-Year Salary Progression**


Deloitte operates a structured promotion ladder that starts with the Graduate Trainee or Associate grade and progresses through Senior Associate, Manager, Senior Manager and Director on the way to Partner. Promotions in early career typically come every 18 to 24 months for trainees who meet performance expectations, with each promotion bringing a 15 to 25 percent salary increase. Indeed reviews from Deloitte Nigeria staff describe career growth as “almost 100% guaranteed” for trainees who perform.


A typical progression starts at the Graduate Trainee grade in Year 1 with monthly net take-home of N470,000 to N535,000. Trainees move into the Senior Associate grade by Year 3 to 4 with a meaningful pay step-up, and Manager promotions typically come between Year 5 and Year 7. Senior Manager and Director grades follow from Year 8 onwards, with Partner positions structured on a profit-share basis representing a different compensation tier altogether.


The unified pay at the graduate level does not mean chartered status loses its value across a Deloitte career. From Senior Associate onwards, ACA and ACCA qualifications drive faster promotion timing and access to specialised audit and tax tracks that pay above the firm’s median. Across a typical seven-year tenure at Deloitte, a trainee who charts up early can comfortably reach Manager grade ahead of an unchartered peer, which compounds into a meaningful cumulative compensation difference at later grades. Deloitte’s particular strength is the global mobility programme; high performers who chart up early often access international rotation opportunities to Deloitte offices in the UK, US, UAE and South Africa from Year 3 onwards.
