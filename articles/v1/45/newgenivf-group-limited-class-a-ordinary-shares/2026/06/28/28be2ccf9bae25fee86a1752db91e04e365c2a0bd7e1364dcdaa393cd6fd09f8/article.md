# NewGen Issues 3,666,667 New Shares in Connection with Strategic K25.ai Acquisition, Reaches Milestone of US$7.6 Million Market Capitalization

**NewGen (Nasdaq: NIVF)** increased total outstanding shares to **10,073,535** after issuing **3,666,667** new shares tied to acquiring a **10%** equity interest in **K25.ai** . Market capitalization was **US$7,631,710** on June 18, 2026.


According to NewGen, this valuation is below its **US$25.98 million** net asset value as of December 31, 2025, and follows a **US$10 million** investment in K25.ai, supporting a strategic pivot toward AI, digital platforms, and a digital assets treasury strategy while aiming to satisfy Nasdaq’s proposed **US$5 million** MVLS threshold.
