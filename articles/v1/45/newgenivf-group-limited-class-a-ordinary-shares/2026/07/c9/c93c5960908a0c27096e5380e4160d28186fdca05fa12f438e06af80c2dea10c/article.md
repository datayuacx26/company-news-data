# NewGen’s Strategic Pre-A Investment in K25.ai Doubles in Value as K25.ai’s Valuation Reaches US$200 Million

**NewGenIVF Group (Nasdaq: NIVF)** reported that portfolio company **K25.ai** has closed a Series A round, reaching a post-money valuation of **US$200 million** , up from **US$100 million** in its Pre-A round completed less than 60 days earlier.


NewGen invested **US$10 million** in K25.ai’s Pre-A round for an approximate **10% ownership stake** , which is now valued at about **US$20 million** . According to NewGen, this implies an unrealized gain of roughly **US$10 million** , expected to be booked in **2026** . The company describes this outcome as aligned with its diversified growth strategy across AI, digital assets, real estate, and healthcare.
