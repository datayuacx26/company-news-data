## Neutral Coat Over Casual Outfit


One of the must-have pieces for your wardrobe and likely something you already own, is a coat! Trench, shearling, longline blazer, leather, or a peacoat: you name it, these are essentials! As a foundational piece, a coat in a neutral color is your best bet, as it is easy to mix and match with just about anything in your closet. **If you are shorter in stature, super-long outerwear can feel overwhelming so opt for a midi-length or one that hits just around the knee!**


A fashionable outfit ensemble for those casual days is to style a pair of jeans with a warm long-sleeve top! Then add the coat, your favorite accessories, some booties, and you are set. *Neutral does not mean boring!* In fact, if you want an elevated look, try one with unique details and/or patterns.

## Skirt & Sweater Combo


*Sweaters will forever and always be one of the hallmarks of the winter season!* The trend as of late has been to pair them with skirts, especially longer ones like midis and maxis. If you are extra fashion-forward, you may like to try this out in the form of a coordinated set. Especially as part of a capsule, this can allow even more outfit options.


**Midis can be great for us petites, but if the weather is more conducive to a warmer maxi, try pairing it with a more fitted sweater to balance out proportions.** Go classic or get creative with the neckline. Add some booties or fashion sneakers depending on the occasion for a cute outfit to sip hot cocoa in!

## Top With Faux Leather Pants


As it exudes such chicness, faux leather is probably my all-time favorite trend. Looser silhouettes are in style at the moment, but for those who enjoy looks with a bit more edge, you can always go for slimmer styles and an oversized sweater! **Whatever faux leather silhouette you choose, an inseam that fits your petite proportions is key.** Check out our[Short Story Inseam Guide](https://blog.shortstorybox.com/how-to-find-your-perfect-petite-inseam/) to find your best fit!


A fun and elevated outfit combo would also be to pair some faux leather pants with a tee and a blazer to polish off the ensemble. For even greater minimalist simplicity, pair them with a fitted bodysuit and add that overcoat we previously discussed!

## Sweater & Trousers


*Trousers, trousers, trousers!* For good reason, they are all the rage! They are easy to dress up or down and a true classic that will withstand the test of time. A combination you will want to have on repeat through the winter is definitely the trouser + sweater look. Add some heels if you are headed into the office or some fashion sneakers if you are headed out to lunch with friends.


For capsule purposes, try a neutral petite option in colors like black, beige, and grey which are immensely adaptable and lend themselves well to a variety of wardrobes and aesthetics. **When choosing a sweater, try a cropped style or one that can be tucked in to balance out your petite frame.**

## Sweater Dress With Boots


For those who adore the ease of dresses, a sweater dress is undoubtedly one you will want to add to your capsule collection! **They come in a variety of shapes and silhouettes according to your petite frame, so if you gravitate toward fit and flares, or tend to reach for more form-fitted styles, definitely consider including one in your rotation** . Add your favorite heeled boot or bootie for that extra “wow” factor!


Solid colors tend to be the easiest to match, but if you’re a pattern gal, fear not! You can certainly try cable-knitted styles which give a similar element of visual appeal and uniqueness. No sacrifice necessary!
