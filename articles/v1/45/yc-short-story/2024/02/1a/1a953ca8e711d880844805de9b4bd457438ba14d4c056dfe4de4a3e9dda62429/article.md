## The Silky Maxi Dress


Nothing says Valentine's Day like a dress. Dresses instantly elevate your everyday style and scream special occasion. Reds, pinks, and whites are the obvious go-to's that never go out of style.[Finding a dress](https://blog.shortstorybox.com/ask-a-stylist-what-dress-silhouette-is-best-for-my-petite-body-type/) that compliments your petite body type is key to feeling and looking your best. **A dress is a classic and feminine choice that will flatter your petite figure and show off your curves.**


Go for a simple and elegant with a midi or maxi dress like the **Ramona Front Cinch Maxi Dress in Moss** for a streamlined look. Add some accessories like a necklace, earrings, a clutch, or a belt to complete the outfit! 5

## The One-Piece-Wonder Jumpsuit


[A jumpsuit is a one-piece wonder](https://blog.shortstorybox.com/finding-your-petite-fit-series-jumpsuits-how-to-style-them-like-a-pro/) that can make you look chic and sophisticated in no time. It's the perfect option for a Galentine's Day outfit with your besties too! **A wide-leg jumpsuit will create the illusion of height, while a tapered-leg jumpsuit will show off your ankles.** The **Abigail Wrap Jumpsuit in Marsala** is the perfect choice and will show off your favorite strappy heels!


For a more elegant look, choose a jumpsuit in satin or silk. Style with a blazer or a cape for a glam touch. For a more flirty and playful look, search for details like ruffles, lace, or cut-outs to add some flair!


Abigail Wrap Jumpsuit in Marsala


## The Outfit with Romantic Details


If you want to keep it casual but still romantic, lace details and floral prints are an effortless go-to for a date night out. A lace top can add some texture and elegance to your outfit and keep things cute. Ask for the **Selena Button Down Tank in Blush** in your next box!


**For petites, it's important to choose a top that is not too loose or too long, as it can make you look shorter.** You can also tuck in your top or wear a belt to create some shape. Style with a flowy floral maxi skirt like the **Ryley Printed Side Ruffle Skirt in Berry** to finish the crazy, sexy, cool look outfit!

## The Daring Neckline


*Valentine's Day is the perfect excuse to take risks and unleash your inner fashionista!* Be playful and experiment with different necklines like square, scoops, and the aptly named "sweetheart" style for looks you will fall head over heels over.


**V-neck styles can help elongate the neck on petites by creating the illusion of length and help highlight the décolletage for a flirty look.** To try a deep v-neck, the **Rowyn Floral Jumpsuit in Rosewater** is a fun choice and gives off the illusion that you are wearing a dress. Plus, it has pockets, perfect for keeping your lipgloss close!
