## How to Determine the Best Fit


Finding the perfect fit for bodysuits can indeed be a challenge, especially since regular sizes often cater to longer torsos. For petites eager to embrace this trend, it might be a bit tricky to find that just-right fit. Given that everyone has a wonderfully distinct body shape, various silhouettes will naturally suit different people in unique ways.


**Wrap styles like the Gabriella bodysuit can especially be great for those seeking to flatter a fuller bust size, while mock necks can be amazing for those of us with smaller bust sizes.** Now, let's talk about how to style a bodysuit.

## Laid-Back Vibes **|** Styled with Jeans


One of the easiest ways to master an easy and fashionable look is to pair a bodysuit with some jeans. **On a petite frame, this especially adds greater balance and proportionality.** For a laidback ensemble, try styling your[favorite denim in your preferred inseam](https://blog.shortstorybox.com/how-to-find-your-perfect-petite-inseam/) with a basic bodysuit in a neutral shade for a minimal look.


Because bodysuits tend to be more form-fitting, they often pair best with jeans that feature looser silhouettes, like straight, boyfriend, and wide-leg. Try the fitted **Leon Long Sleeve Bodysuit** for our exclusive Sculpt line!

## Flirty Fun **|** Styled with Skirts


From easy breezy pleated maxis to office pencil silhouettes, skirts are one of the best bodysuit pairings. For petites, midi skirts are a particularly great choice. Aim for a midi that gracefully ends at mid-calf for the most flattering fit. Combine it with a breezy bodysuit, like the **Sophia Short Sleeve Wrap Bodysuit** , to strike a perfect balance between relaxed and polished.


For a business formal or business casual look, try styling a trouser skirt with a high-neck bodysuit and a blazer. *Whatever styles and proportions suit your unique petite frame best, and make you feel most confident, that’s the way to go.*


Sophia Short Sleeve Wrap Bodysuit


## Modern Chic **| Styled with** Trousers


*Effortless style.* Two words that immediately come to mind when we think about the dynamic duo that is a bodysuit and a trouser combo. Whether you are going to work or out to a weekend brunch with friends, this pairing is the dream if your aim is a perfectly polished ensemble.


An effortlessly chic option is the **Jane Square Neck Bodysuit** with a flattering neckline. **When looking for trousers, focus on finding your preferred inseam by choosing a true petite pair to ensure the best length to fit your petite proportions!**


Jane Square Neck Bodysuit


## Fresh Feels **| Styled with** Statement Outerwear


*Outerwear can truly tie an outfit together!* Regardless of your personal style whether it's edgy, boho, or preppy– you can add a piece to your look to create extra dimension and flair. Bodysuits like the **Sira Mockneck Bodysuit** work well because they are thin enough to wear under any layering piece.


Add a blazer to your trouser and bodysuit combination for work;[a moto jacket to your jean](https://blog.shortstorybox.com/the-classic-moto-jacket-not-just-for-the-edgy-fashionista/) and bodysuit combination for a girls’ night out; or a denim jacket to your jean and bodysuit combo for a weekend adventure. **Look for petite options for the best fit!**
