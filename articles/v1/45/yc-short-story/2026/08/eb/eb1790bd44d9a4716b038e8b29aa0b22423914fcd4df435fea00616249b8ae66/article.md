# Meet The Short List: A Facebook Community Made for Petite Women

Finding clothes when you’re petite can feel like its own full time job. The inseam is too long. The waist hits in the wrong place. The sleeves swallow your hands. And somehow, “petite” still means something different at every brand. That is exactly why we created **The Short List** , our Facebook community for petite women.


It is a place to talk about the stuff that only other petites really understand.


Need help styling something from your latest box? Post a photo.


Found the perfect pair of jeans for a shorter inseam? Share the win.


### A petite fashion community built around real conversation


The Short List is not another feed of perfectly styled outfits. It's a community.


Members swap fit tips, ask for styling advice, share petite friendly finds, talk about brands they love, and help each other solve the everyday challenges that come with dressing a petite frame.


### Join The Short List


If you are petite and want a smarter, more fun way to talk about style, we would love to have you.


[Join The Short List on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/984640807188404/) **** and meet your people.
