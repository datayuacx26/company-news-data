# AMC Theatres® Expands Feature Fare Menu Nationwide With New Craveable Favorites Including Street Corn Poppers, Hot Honey Sausage Pizza, and Popcorn Chicken

# AMC Theatres® Expands Feature Fare Menu Nationwide With New Craveable Favorites Including Street Corn Poppers, Hot Honey Sausage Pizza, and Popcorn Chicken


May 18, 2026 8:15am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_c2c3599664a8e783d22abca53fa9b130/amctheatres/news/2026-05-18_AMC_Theatres_Expands_Feature_Fare_Menu_Nationwide__425.pdf)


*AMC continues evolving its nationwide Feature Fare menu with bold new flavors, snack innovations and expanded hot food offerings designed to elevate the moviegoing experience*


*Popcorn Chicken bites, Hot Honey Sausage Pizza, Dill Pickle Pretzel Bites and Street Corn Poppers are some of the items guests can now enjoy at more than 400 AMC Feature Fare locations*


LEAWOOD, Kan.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- AMC Theatres® (NYSE: AMC), the largest theatrical exhibitor in the United States and the world, is introducing an expanded lineup of new menu items at AMC Feature Fare locations nationwide, giving moviegoers even more ways to enjoy bold flavors and elevated snacks alongside this summer’s biggest movies.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260518034349/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260518034349/en/)


Popcorn Chicken bites, Hot Honey Sausage Pizza, Dill Pickle Pretzel Bites and Street Corn Poppers are some of the items guests can now enjoy at more than 400 U.S. AMC Theatres Feature Fare locations


Available at more than 400 AMC locations in the United States, AMC Feature Fare continues to evolve beyond traditional movie theatre concessions with a growing lineup of hot foods, savory snacks, sweet treats and limited-time offerings inspired by popular food trends and guest demand.


New Feature Fare additions arriving at AMC-branded theatres nationwide include:


- Street Corn Poppers – Hot, poppable street corn bites packed with flavor.
- Popcorn Chicken – Crispy, bite-sized chicken perfect for sharing or snacking during the movie.
- Hot Honey Sausage Pizza – A sweet and spicy combination featuring sausage and hot honey flavors.
- Dill Pickle Pretzel Bites – Soft pretzel bites with a bold dill pickle seasoning twist.
- Dill Pickle Bavarian Legend – A 1.5 pound, shareable pretzel with a bold dill pickle seasoning twist.


AMC is also continuing to expand its Premium Snacks lineup with new additions available on the candy rack, including:


- Cheez-It® Snack Mix
- Pringles® Mingles Jalapeño Queso
- SNICKERS® Peanut Butter Ice Cream Bar


Since launching in 2017, AMC Feature Fare has continued introducing new food and beverage options designed to complement the modern moviegoing experience. From handheld favorites and premium snacks to expanded hot food offerings, AMC continues investing in ways to give guests more variety and more reasons to make moviegoing part of a night out.


“The AMC Feature Fare menu is always evolving as we explore fun new opportunities and listen to our guests about what they most enjoy while watching a movie on the big screen,” said Hank Green, Senior Vice President, Food & Beverage, AMC Theatres. “With new additions like Street Corn Poppers, Hot Honey Sausage Pizza, and Dill Pickle Pretzel Bites, we’re continuing to build a menu that gives our guests more variety, more flavor and more reasons to treat themselves when they come to the movies.”


Guests can learn more about AMC Feature Fare menu offerings by visiting[amctheatres.com/food-and-drink/feature-fare](https://www.amctheatres.com/food-and-drink/feature-fare) .
