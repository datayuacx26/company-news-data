## Why genetic testing awareness matters


As genetic medicine continues to transform healthcare, timely genetic testing has never been more important. For individuals with global developmental delays and intellectual disabilities, epilepsy, autism, cerebral palsy and many other unexplained medical concerns, genetic testing can help identify an underlying cause, inform medical management and treatment, connect families to support communities, and open doors to research and clinical trial opportunities.


Too many individuals and families who could benefit from genetic testing never receive it—or even know how to ask for it. On average, families wait five years for a rare disease diagnosis, seeing multiple specialists, undergoing repeated tests, and living with uncertainty before the underlying cause of symptoms is identified.


Increasing awareness is a critical step toward changing that reality. When patients, families, and healthcare providers recognize when genetic testing may be appropriate, more people can access testing earlier and benefit from the answers it can provide.


That's why awareness matters.


**A genetic diagnosis may help individuals and families:**


- Understand the underlying cause of a condition
- Make more informed treatment and care decisions
- Access specialized resources and support communities
- Participate in research and clinical trials
- Better understand recurrence risk and support future family planning


At GeneDx, we have seen firsthand how obtaining a genetic diagnosis can transform years of uncertainty into understanding and provide a clearer path forward for patients and caregivers.
