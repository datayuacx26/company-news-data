# Best AI SDR Platforms for Mid-Market B2B (2026)

## The AI SDR landscape in 2026


The category has exploded in the last 18 months. As of mid-2026, the notable platforms for B2B outbound are:


RocketSDR — AI SDR with deep signal-based research, 1B+ real-time prospect data, managed deliverability infrastructure, multichannel orchestration (email + LinkedIn + phone + WhatsApp), and an agentic ABM engine for account-based selling. Both autonomous and approval modes. YC-backed. Strongest on: research depth, deliverability, and the only agentic ABM solution in the category.


11x (Alice) — AI SDR with strong marketing presence and press coverage. Well-funded (a16z-backed). Higher-priced than most alternatives. Strongest on: brand visibility and marketing. Worth evaluating but the premium pricing doesn't necessarily translate to premium results.


AiSDR — mid-market AI SDR with strong Salesforce/HubSpot integration. Strongest on: CRM integration depth and pricing transparency.


Apollo — not strictly an AI SDR, but its AI features plus its massive contact database (260M+) make it the de facto baseline most teams compare against. Strongest on: data volume and price ($49–119/mo).


Reply.io — multichannel outreach with AI capabilities. Strongest on: LinkedIn automation and agency-friendly multi-account management.


Smartlead / Instantly — email-sending-focused platforms with AI bolt-ons. Strongest on: raw email volume at low cost. Weakest on: everything beyond email.
