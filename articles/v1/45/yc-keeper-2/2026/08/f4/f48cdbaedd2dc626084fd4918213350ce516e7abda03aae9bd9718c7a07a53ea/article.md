# TikTok Shop Taxes: The Complete 2026 Guide for Sellers & Affiliates

### The 2026 1099-K threshold


For the 2025 tax year onward, the federal 1099-K threshold is **more than $20,000 in gross sales AND more than 200 transactions.** However, there are 2 big caveats to this threshold:


1.


**Some states require a 1099-K at much lower amounts.** States like Maryland, Massachusetts, Vermont, Virginia, and Illinois have thresholds as low as $600–$1,000. If you live in one, you should expect to get a 1099-K!


2.


**You still owe taxes even if you didn't meet the threshold or didn't get a 1099-K.** Even though the threshold is $20,000 and 200 transactions, if you made $8,000 and never get a 1099-K, that $8,000 is still taxable!


### Your 1099-K won't match your payouts, and here's why


Most people realize the number on their 1099-K is almost always higher than the cash that actually hits their bank account.


That's because the 1099-K reports **Unadjusted Gross Sales (UGS)** - product price plus shipping fees, minus only your discounts. It excludes refunds, TikTok's platform fees, commissions, shipping subsidies, and timing differences.


Practically, that means if you simply report the 1099-K number as your income, you'll overstate your revenue and overpay in taxes.


> **Keeper pro tip:** Make sure to pull your **1099-K detailed report** (or Orders/Settlement report) from Seller Center and reconcile it before filing!


### Why did TikTok withhold 24% of my payments?


When you set up TikTok Shop, you're asked to provide tax info (a **W-9** with your SSN or EIN). If you don't, TikTok is required to withhold 24% of your payments (what's called a "backup withholding"), and you may lose the ability to sell. 😱 To add your tax info, go to **Account Settings → Tax** .


## Does TikTok Shop collect and remit sales tax?


Yes, in almost all states, TikTok Shop automatically collects and remits sales tax on your behalf.


Under state "marketplace facilitator" laws, the platform (not the individual seller) is responsible for collecting and paying sales tax on sales made through it. That covers all 45 states (plus DC) that levy sales tax.


As a seller, that means:


-


You generally **don't need to collect or file sales tax** on your TikTok Shop sales.


-


But you may still have obligations if you sell on other channels (your own store, in person), or if you have physical nexus (e.g., inventory stored in a state).


### Why is TikTok Shop charging higher sales tax?


Sales tax is destination-based. It's calculated on your buyer's location, and many areas stack state, county, and city rates. So the rate varies order to order!


> **Keeper pro tip:** Sales tax and income tax are totally separate. TikTok remitting sales tax does nothing for your income tax. You still owe income plus[self-employment tax](https://www.keepertax.com/posts/what-is-self-employment-tax) on your profit.


## How much tax will I owe from TikTok Shop sales?


You owe two things on your profit (income minus expenses):


1.


[Self-employment (SE) tax](https://www.keepertax.com/1099-tax-calculator) **: 15.3%** (12.4% Social Security plus 2.9% Medicare). This is on top of income tax and catches most first-timers off guard.


2.


[Income tax](https://www.keepertax.com/income-tax-calculator) **:** at your marginal rate, which depends on your total household income.
