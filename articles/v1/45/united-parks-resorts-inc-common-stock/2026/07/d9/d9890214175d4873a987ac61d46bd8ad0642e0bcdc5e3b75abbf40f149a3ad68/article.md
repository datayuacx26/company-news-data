# Rescue Jr., the New Kid-Friendly Interactive Adventure Zone Themed Around SeaWorld’s Animal Rescue Efforts, is Now Open

# Rescue Jr., the New Kid-Friendly Interactive Adventure Zone Themed Around SeaWorld’s Animal Rescue Efforts, is Now Open


With the attraction now open, the FREE Preschool Card is the perfect way for three- to five-year-olds to experience this ALL-NEW interactive, hands-on adventure zone that includes exciting opportunities to tackle immersive rides, splash through vibrant water features and bring to life the SeaWorld team’s rescue and rehabilitation work


**SAN ANTONIO, Tx., (March 8, 2025)** – Today, SeaWorld San Antonio opened its newest attraction, Rescue Jr., an immersive, kid-friendly adventure zone designed to inspire and entertain guests of all ages. The park held a grand opening ceremony that included local children assisting with a ribbon cutting. The ceremony also featured members of the SeaWorld rescue team and a variety of rescued small terrestrial animals. Rescue Jr. offers thrilling rides and interactive play areas that highlight SeaWorld’s rescue, rehabilitation and conservation efforts. The exciting new rides are perfect for three- to five-year-olds who can now get a free Preschool Card offering unlimited admission to SeaWorld all year long. Parents of little ones can also get unlimited visits when they take advantage of the current Spring Break Sale – Buy a SeaWorld Pass and get Aquatica for free! Guests should not wait as both offers will end soon.


"Rescue Jr. is an exciting new chapter in SeaWorld’s commitment to inspiring the next generation of animal advocates, and there’s no better time than Spring Break for families to come and experience it as it makes its official debut,” said Byron Surrett, SeaWorld Chief Parks Operations Officer. “This immersive play area brings our rescue efforts to life in a fun and educational way and celebrates the important work our zoological team does to protect and care for marine life."


**Get A Free Preschool Card and Enjoy Rescue Jr. All Year Long**


Don’t miss out! Guests can get a **free Preschool Card** today and experience all the fun Rescue Jr. has to offer for all of 2025. For a limited time, parents can get a free Preschool Card for children ages three to five, the perfect ages to enjoy Rescue Jr. The card allows young guests unlimited free admission all year long to experience all of SeaWorld’s exciting animals, thrills and attractions. With thrilling rides, interactive play zones and unforgettable family moments, Rescue Jr. is set to be the most exciting addition to SeaWorld yet! Preschool Card registration ends soon! Guests can register now at[www.SeaWorldSanAntonio.com](http://www.seaworldsanantonio.com/) .


**New Play Area Perfect for Kids**


Children will become the heroes of the park at Rescue Jr. where they can learn about animal rescue and rehabilitation in the coolest way possible – through hands-on, active and engaging experiences, including:


- **Beach Rescue Racer** , the new 1,300-foot roller coaster with cars in the shape of a beach jeep will give guests the feeling of rushing to the rescue of animals in need. Young heroes **38 inches and up** get to jump in the driver’s seat and zoom to the rescue!
- Guests can embark on an exhilarating train ride adventure themed around a sea rescue on the **Ocean Quest Express** . Explorers as little as **36 inches** can ride while playing a game of “I Spy” looking for hidden sea life along the way.
- **Tide Pool Tumble** lets little adventurers **42 inches and taller** ebb and flow as they glide in and out with the tide on a seaside joy ride.
- Rescuers will take to the skies and soar on **Seabird Swing** , virtually parasailing high above the ground to observe seabirds. Guests **36 inches and taller** can take flight in this thrilling giant swing.
- **Ocean Rescue Carousel** is perfect for young and old and will keep guests thrilled as they go round and round taking in the sights of the new play area. **Everyone can enjoy** the ride, but those at least 42 inches can ride it on their own!


SeaWorld is open daily for Spring Break until March 23, 2025.


**About SeaWorld**


SeaWorld is a leading marine life theme park and accredited zoo and aquarium that provides experiences that matter while educating and inspiring guests of all ages to care about marine life. Welcoming millions of guests every year, the parks offer fun and enriching experiences from up-close animal encounters and year-round educational programs to award-winning marine-life-themed rides and attractions, special events, and exciting entertainment. For more than 60 years SeaWorld has advanced the conservation of marine life in and outside its parks through science, education, and exceptional animal care that is Humane Certified by American Humane and accredited by the Alliance of Marine Mammal Parks and Aquariums and the Association of Zoos and Aquariums. SeaWorld is one of the largest marine animal rescue organizations in the world, helping more than 41,000 animals to date. The[SeaWorld Conservation Fund](https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fswbg-conservationfund.org%2F&data=05%7C01%7CLisa.Cradit%40seaworld.com%7C76012dd0ae334b142a0508db47461b5d%7C9efc8ad4f77b495089650b28880ed003%7C0%7C0%7C638182138656777942%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=yDAl%2BGNs38sVexfUEtt10QU8a7s6Od008uJH0sEDNlE%3D&reserved=0) , a non-profit foundation established in 2003, has provided more than $20 million to nearly 1,400 organizations to advance critical research on every continent. A portion of park proceeds goes toward supporting these longstanding conservation commitments. SeaWorld parks are in Orlando, San Antonio, San Diego, and Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates (UAE). SeaWorld is part of the United Parks & Resorts Inc. (NYSE:PRKS) portfolio of theme park brands. For more information, visit us at SeaWorld.com.
