# Phoenix Roadshow 2026 – Tokyo​

ShareShareShare


**Date:** September 3, 2026


**Time:** 1:00 pm – 4:00 pm (TBC)


**Location:** Shinagawa, Tokyo


## Summary


Join us in Shinagawa, Tokyo for an exclusive, half-day, on-site workshop dedicated to the future of PK/PD modeling and simulation with Phoenix. This event offers an in-depth look at the latest innovations in the Phoenix Cloud platform, including TFL Studio, AI-powered PK Reporting, and the enhanced PK Submit workflow, along with hands-on opportunities to work with the tools throughout the workshop.


## Benefits of the event


- **Experience Phoenix Cloud** – See how cloud-based workflows are transforming productivity​
- **Advance the Science** – Learn about the latest advances in machine learning for pre-clinical PK/PD analysis​
- **See New Phoenix Features** – Get a live demo of enhanced and new functionality​
- **Share Your Voice** – Join an open feedback session and help shape future product development


## Register now


## Who should register


- Phoenix Cloud/Desktop users in Pharma, Biotech, or CROs​
- Pharmacometricians who want to learn the latest features of the PK/PD analysis tool, Phoenix Cloud​


* Please note that this event is intended for industry professionals in Pharma, Biotech, or CRO organizations. Due to limited seating, we kindly ask that academic participants refrain from registering so we can prioritize space for industry attendees. ​
