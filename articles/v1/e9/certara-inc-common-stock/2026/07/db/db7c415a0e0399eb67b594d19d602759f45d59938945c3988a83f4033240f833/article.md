# 2026 SEND Explorer Virtual User Group Meeting

ShareShareShare


**Date:** Tuesday, October 20, 2026


**Time:** 10:00am – 12:00pm ET


## Driving decisions for the next generation of nonclinical research


Nonclinical safety teams need to do more with their data than ever before—comparing findings across studies, species, and compounds; surfacing signals faster; and turning compliant SEND submissions into real scientific insight. Growing data volumes and rising expectations for AI-assisted analysis are reshaping what toxicologists need from their tools.


Join the worldwide SEND Explorer community for our fourth annual User Group Meeting to see how SEND Explorer NextGen (v13) answers that challenge—shaping cloud and AI technology to the way toxicologists work.


## Highlights include:


- A modernized interface with streamlined single- and multi-study navigation
- Automated Endpoints of Interest flagging with AI-generated summaries
- New multi-endpoint Scatterplot Timecourse and many other customer enhancements!
- Data Lake–enabled advanced analytics


You’ll also hear the SEND Explorer roadmap and get a chance to share your perspective on our field’s direction and potential.


Register now to save your seat. Agenda details coming soon.
