# Phoenix Pharmacometrics Day – Korea​

ShareShareShare


**Date:** September 1, 2026


**Time:** 1:30 pm – 5:00 pm


**Location:** Seoul, Korea


## Summary


Join us in Seoul, Korea for a half-day, on-site event dedicated to new PK/PD with Phoenix Cloud and pharmacometrics modeling & simulation.​


​Discover how the latest Phoenix Cloud innovations, advanced modeling capabilities, and real-world case studies help improve workflow efficiency and support decision making.


## Benefits of the event


- **Learn from case studies** – Discover the latest pharmacometrics and machine learning approaches shaping drug development.​
- **Explore Phoenix Cloud** – See \[specific new feature/capability\] and how it’s transforming PK/PD workflows. ​
- **Connect with local experts** – Build relationships with pharmacometricians across Korea’s pharma and biotech organizations.

## Who should register


- Pharmacometricians or Phoenix Cloud/Desktop users in Pharma or Biotech​
