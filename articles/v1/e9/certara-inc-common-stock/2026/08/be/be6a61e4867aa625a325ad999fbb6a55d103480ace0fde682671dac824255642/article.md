# A Quantitative Systems Pharmacology (QSP) Model for Preclinical to Clinical Translation of ADC Efficacy and Thrombocytopenia

Certara scientists developed a mechanistic QSP model that accurately translates preclinical antibody-drug conjugates (ADC) data into clinical outcomes. Using trastuzumab emtansine (Kadcyla™) as a model compound, this study demonstrates how integrated pharmacokinetic, efficacy, and thrombocytopenia modeling can help optimize dose selection and inform clinical trial design for next-generation antibody-drug conjugates.


Key Highlights:


- Mechanistic modeling of ADC efficacy and toxicity (thrombocytopenia)
- Translation from in vitro and in vivo data to virtual clinical populations
- Application to metastatic breast cancer (mBC) and HER2-targeting ADCs
