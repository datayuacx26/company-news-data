## Why Google Still Drives 90% of Your Conversions


You have limited budget. AI search gets all the headlines. Does Google still matter?


Let's look at the actual numbers.


Every startup founder faces this question right now. The media narrative screams that traditional SEO is dead. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude dominate tech Twitter. Your competitors are pivoting their entire content strategy to AI optimization. The pressure to abandon Google and bet everything on AI search feels overwhelming.


Here's what the data actually shows: Google still dominates by massive margins.


Google processes 16.4 billion searches daily. ChatGPT handles 2.5 billion prompts, but only about a third of those are searches for information (not creative tasks or conversations). That brings the real comparison to roughly 800 million AI searches versus Google's 16.4 billion.


#### Research Finding:


[ChatGPT reached 2.5 billion daily prompts by July 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/21/chatgpt-users-send-2-5-billion-prompts-a-day/) , with approximately one-third representing searches for information rather than creative tasks or conversations.


The volume difference is staggering, but it's only part of the story. Google maintains approximately 90% market share (ranging between 89-93% throughout 2025), while AI platforms currently drive less than 1% of global web traffic.


#### Research Finding:


[AI platforms account for less than 1% of global internet traffic as of early 2025, compared to 48.5% from organic search](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-traffic-research-study/) , (with the balance from direct, social, referral, and other sources), according to SE Ranking's analysis of 63,987 websites with Google Analytics data showing figures as low as 0.15% across most industries. For SaaS companies specifically, these numbers may be higher, potentially accelerating the shift toward AI conversions.


For most startups, Google drives 90% or more of search-driven conversions today. The volume gap isn't close.


But before you dismiss AI search entirely, here's what those raw numbers don't tell you about conversion quality.


##
Why AI Traffic Converts Better Despite Lower Numbers


Raw volume isn't the full story. AI search traffic shows something remarkable: superior engagement and conversion rates that change the entire competitive equation.


### The AI Engagement Advantage: Why Longer Sessions Drive More Conversions


AI traffic doesn't just convert better. It converts at rates that fundamentally change the economics of search visibility.


#### Research Finding:


Analysis of 12 million website visits shows[AI traffic converts at rates 4-5x higher](https://www.superprompt.com/blog/ai-search-traffic-conversion-rates-5x-higher-than-google-2025-data) than Google on average, with results ranging from small gains to 9x depending on industry and implementation quality. The average AI visitor converts at 14.2% compared to Google's 2.8%, with platform leaders showing conversion rates from Claude at 16.8%, ChatGPT at 14.2%, and Perplexity at 12.4%.


Why the conversion advantage? AI users arrive with different intent. They've already researched, compared alternatives and refined their requirements through conversation with an AI assistant. When they click through to your site, they're further along the buyer journey. They convert faster.


#### Research Finding:


Pew Research's October 2025 study reveals[users click 47% less with AI Overviews](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) in Google results (8% click rate with AI Overviews vs 15% without). More telling: 26% end their browsing session entirely after getting AI-generated answers, compared to just 16% without AI Overviews.


This isn't about AI stealing Google's traffic. It's about a fundamental shift in how people search and make decisions. They got what they needed without clicking.


##
How AI Drives Equal Conversions with Only a Quarter of the Traffic


The conversion advantage creates a counterintuitive reality: AI doesn't need to match Google's volume to match its results.


With a 4-5x conversion advantage, AI only needs roughly 25% of total traffic to generate the same number of conversions as Google's 75% of total traffic.


Let's make this concrete. Today, if your startup gets 3,000 monthly visitors split 99% Google and 1% AI:


- Google generates roughly 83 conversions (at a 2.8% conversion rate)
- AI generates 4 conversions (at a 14.2% conversion rate)
- Google dominates, AI barely registers


But as AI's traffic share grows, that quality advantage compounds. When AI reaches 25% of your traffic with Google at 75% of it, the math shifts dramatically:


- Google generates 63 conversions
- AI generates 107 conversions
- Suddenly AI is your primary conversion driver despite being the minority traffic source


The tipping point isn't about volume parity. It's about when quality overtakes quantity for actual business results.


Note: Comprehensive analysis of prompt types is only available for ChatGPT (32.7% of prompts are searches for information). We apply this figure conservatively across general-purpose AI assistants, recognizing search-focused platforms like Perplexity likely have higher percentages.


Here's when our analysis suggests that crossover could happen.
