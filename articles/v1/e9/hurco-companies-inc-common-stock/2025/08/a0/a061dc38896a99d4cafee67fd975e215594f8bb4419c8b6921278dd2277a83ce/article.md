# Hurco Names Integra Machine Tool as Distribution and Service Partner for Illinois and Wisconsin

**Indianapolis, Indiana, August 21, 2025** – Hurco Companies announced that it has signed an agreement with Integra Machine Tool to represent Hurco machines in Illinois and Wisconsin, as well as Takumi machines in Illinois. Under the new partnership, Integra will provide sales, service, support and applications development to customers within its assigned territory.


## Strategic Partnership


Integra Machine Tool brings decades of experience in the machine tool industry to this partnership, with a proven track record of providing exceptional service and support to manufacturing customers throughout the Midwest region.


- **Territory Coverage:** Illinois and Wisconsin
- **Product Lines:** Hurco CNC machines (mills and lathes), Takumi machines (Illinois only)
- **Services:** Sales, service, support, and applications development


## About Integra Machine Tool


Integra Machine Tool is a trusted distributor known for its commitment to customer success and technical expertise. Their team of experienced engineers and service technicians will provide comprehensive support to Hurco and Takumi customers in the region.
