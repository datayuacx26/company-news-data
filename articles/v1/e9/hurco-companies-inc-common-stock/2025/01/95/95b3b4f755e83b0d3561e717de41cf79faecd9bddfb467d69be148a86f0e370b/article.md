## Hurco Poised to Pioneer the Future of Manufacturing with AI and Autonomous Machining


**Indianapolis, Indiana** – Hurco Companies, Inc. announced its vision to drive the future of Autonomous Machining Centers by harnessing the power of AI, mechatronics, motion, and simulation technologies.


## Vision for the Future


Hurco is committed to pioneering the next generation of CNC machine tools that leverage artificial intelligence and advanced automation to create truly autonomous machining centers. This vision builds on Hurco's legacy of innovation in control technology and conversational programming.


## Key Technology Areas


- **Artificial Intelligence:** Machine learning algorithms for process optimization and predictive maintenance
- **Mechatronics:** Advanced integration of mechanical and electronic systems
- **Motion Control:** Enhanced precision and speed through advanced motion algorithms
- **Simulation:** Digital twin technology for virtual machining and optimization


> This announcement represents Hurco's commitment to leading the industry into a new era of smart manufacturing, where machines can learn, adapt, and optimize themselves to deliver unprecedented levels of efficiency and quality.
