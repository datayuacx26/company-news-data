# New TM8MYi Lathe Offers Affordable Access to Multi-Axis Turning with Hurco Control

**Indianapolis, Indiana** – The newest addition to Hurco Companies, Inc.'s lathe lineup, the TM8MYi was built to fill the gap between Hurco's general purpose TM Series and high-performance TMX Series of turning centers.


## Affordable Multi-Axis Turning


The TM8MYi offers shops an affordable entry point into multi-axis turning with Y-axis capability, live tooling, and the powerful Hurco WinMax control that machinists already know and love.


## Key Features


- **Y-Axis Capability:** Off-center milling operations without secondary setups
- **Live Tooling:** Powered tool positions for milling, drilling, and tapping
- **Hurco WinMax Control:** Intuitive conversational programming and NC/G-code flexibility
- **Compact Footprint:** Designed for shops with limited floor space
- **Competitive Pricing:** Affordable access to advanced turning capabilities


## Bridging the Gap


The TM8MYi is positioned between the general-purpose TM Series lathes and the high-performance TMX Series, offering an ideal solution for shops ready to upgrade from basic turning to multi-axis machining without the premium price tag.
