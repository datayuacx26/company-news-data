# Teladoc Health’s Ethan Berke Named to Modern Healthcare’s 50 Most Influential Clinical Executives List for 2026

June 8, 2026 — Teladoc Health (NYSE: TDOC), the global leader in virtual care, today announced Ethan Berke, MD, MPH, chief medical officer and senior vice president of integrated care, has been named to Modern Healthcare’s 50 Most Influential Clinical Executives[list](https://www.modernhealthcare.com/awards/50-most-influential-clinicians/2026/) for 2026.


The annual recognition honors clinical leaders making significant contributions to innovation, care delivery and health outcomes across the industry.


Dr. Berke leads Teladoc Health’s virtually enabled integrated care practice, which includes the largest nationwide network of virtual care providers. Since joining the company in May 2025, Dr. Berke has led the company’s evolution of virtual care, including transforming[its 24/7 Care offering](https://www.teladochealth.com/newsroom/press/teladoc-health-launches-enhanced-24-7-care-service) into a more integrated model with coordinated clinical support and richer insights to help close care gaps.


A trusted expert on the future of care delivery, Dr. Berke is regularly featured in the media and at leading industry and academic forums.


A board-certified family physician and epidemiologist, Dr. Berke brings experience across primary care, urgent care, emergency medicine and disaster response, including service with the National Disaster Medical System.


Each year, Modern Healthcare recognizes licensed clinicians serving in executive roles for exceptional leadership, innovation and impact in improving care and serving their communities.


Read more about Dr. Berke’s perspective on what better health looks like in the digital era in his Executive Conversation with Modern Healthcare[here.](https://www.modernhealthcare.com/content-studio/executive-conversation/mh-virtual-care-quality-access-value-teladoc-health/)
