#### SunOpta Welcomes Jen Caro as Senior Vice President of Sales


Minneapolis, Minn. – June 5, 2025 – SunOpta (Nasdaq:STKL) (TSX:SOY) – a company that delivers customized supply chain solutions and innovation for top brands, retailers and foodservice providers across a broad portfolio of beverages, broths and better-for-you snacks – announced the appointment of Jen Caro as Senior Vice President of Sales and a member of the company’s Senior Leadership Team.


Following an extensive national search, Jen emerged as the ideal leader to guide SunOpta’s sales function and help propel the company’s growth strategy forward. With more than 18 years of experience as a seasoned sales executive, Jen brings a proven track record of success across blue-chip companies, family-owned businesses, and private equity-backed organizations.


Most recently, Jen held senior leadership roles at Dessert Holdings and PepsiCo, where she consistently drove strong results by building high-performing teams and fostering a customer-solutions mindset. She has helped lead complex businesses ranging in size from $250 million to $1 billion in annual revenue and is widely recognized for her results-driven focus and “people-first” leadership style. Her approach blends strategic thinking, vulnerability, and decisive execution to deliver sustainable performance.


“Jen is passionate about aligning culture and performance to accelerate customer engagement and strategic outcomes,” said Brian Kocher, SunOpta Chief Executive Officer. “Her leadership will play a vital role as we continue to grow, innovate and deliver great solutions for our customers and our ambitious goals for the company.”


“I’m incredibly excited to join SunOpta at such a pivotal time in the company’s growth journey,” said Jen Caro, SunOpta Senior Vice President of Sales. “The company’s strong product portfolio, commitment to innovation, and purpose-driven culture immediately stood out to me. I’m especially energized by the opportunity to work alongside such a passionate, talented team to strengthen customer solutions and help drive bold, strategic results on behalf of and for the benefit of our customers. I’m honored to be part of what’s next for SunOpta.”
