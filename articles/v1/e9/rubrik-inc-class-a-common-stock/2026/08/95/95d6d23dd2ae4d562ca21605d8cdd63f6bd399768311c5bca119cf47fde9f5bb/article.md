# Rubrik to Report Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results on August 27, 2026

### Rubrik to Report Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results on August 27, 2026


August 6, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/667520861/files/doc_news/Rubrik-to-Report-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027-Financial-Results-on-August-27-2026-2026.pdf)


PALO ALTO, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Rubrik, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.rubrik.com%2F&esheet=54584038&newsitemid=20260806091431&lan=en-US&anchor=Rubrik%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=f47b6f129c1186ddbe31463ab4c3eb05) (NYSE: RBRK), the Security and AI Operations Company, today announces that it will release financial results for its second quarter fiscal 2027 ended July 31, 2026, after the market closes on Thursday, August 27, 2026.


Management will also host a live conference call that day at 2:00 pm PT / 5:00 pm ET to discuss the Company’s financial results.


A live webcast of the conference call and related materials can be accessed from the Company’s investor relations website at[https://ir.rubrik.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.rubrik.com&esheet=54584038&newsitemid=20260806091431&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fir.rubrik.com&index=2&md5=b1d3030fcc1c83edd91e9e952b274ee7) . Following the call, a replay of the webcast will also be available on the investor relations website.
