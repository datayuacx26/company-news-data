# How to decorate your virtual office for the holidays

Decorating for the season or holidays is an easy way to delight your team and bring fresh energy to your virtual workspace. Imagine everyone’s surprise when a pumpkin appears on their desk, or everything turns green for St. Patrick’s Day!


Plus, part of the[benefit of a virtual office](https://www.gather.town/blog/virtual-office-benefits) is that you can decorate it however you want, whenever you want, with minimal setup and takedown time. (It's a lot easier to redecorate with the click of a mouse than it is to haul boxes out of storage!)


This guide will teach you how to decorate your virtual office for the holidays and share all the items we have available to make each occasion special for your remote team.


## How to decorate your Gather office


There are two ways you can decorate for the holidays. The quick way is to use the[Desk Decorator](https://support.help.gather.town/articles/4223087616-decorate-office?lang=en) . Open the **Main Menu** and click **Decorate Desk** . Find the object you want, and click to place it. It will instantly appear to everyone in the office.


For a bigger makeover, Admins can use[Gather Studio](https://support.help.gather.town/articles/1963307749-studio-overview?lang=en) . Open the **Main Menu** and click **Edit the office** . Find the object you want, and click to place it. These changes won’t appear until you click **Publish** , making it better if you want to surprise everyone with a full seasonal refresh.
