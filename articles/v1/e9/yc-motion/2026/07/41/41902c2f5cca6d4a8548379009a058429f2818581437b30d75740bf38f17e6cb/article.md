## How AI is Revamping Sales Prospecting


Sales prospecting has often been one of the most grind-heavy tasks in sales. There's a deep hustle required to master tasks like finding leads, following up, and building first connections.


And with the help of AI, those mundane lead finding and follow-up tasks can be automated instantly, leaving sales teams time to master imperative higher-value activities like perfecting their pitch, overcoming objections, and measuring and iterating on success.


AI can even help sales teams become even more effective with those high-value activities by handling tasks like analyzing customer data patterns to identify high-intent prospects, providing a real-time analysis of buyer behavior to help reps know exactly when to follow up and how, and even handling pipeline reporting.


But that's a lot, right? And knowing where and how to start can feel overwhelming.


Fortunately, utilizing AI BDRs is one of the most approachable ways to start implementing AI into your prospecting process, and it requires no technical expertise.


## How Human BDRs & AI BDRs Can Work Best Together


AI employees are designed to work alongside human employees, not replace them. The collaboration between human and AI BDRs can lead to increased productivity and efficiency, and result in more closed deals, but there's a balance that needs to be perfected to do so.


Below, we dive into the specific ways human BDRs and AI BDRs can work well together.


### Tackling Large Volumes of Tasks


The nature of BDR work is inherently task-intensive. From identifying potential leads to sending follow-up emails and scheduling calls, the volume of tasks can quickly become overwhelming. AI can automate many of these repetitive tasks, allowing human BDRs to focus on more strategic and high-impact activities.


### Processing Large Quantities of Data


BDRs are frequently swamped with a deluge of information, from their target customers' pain points to prospect activity, and learning when and how to best act on it. Sifting through this manually is not only time-consuming but also prone to errors. AI has the capability to swiftly process large volumes of data, offering actionable insights for more informed decision-making.


### Finding Ways to See Success in Cold Outreach


Once a dependable approach, cold outreach is now met with increasing indifference. Prospects are more prone to disregard unsolicited messages, resulting in diminished response rates. AI can be leveraged to pinpoint optimal outreach times and to compose tailored messages that better engage potential clients.


### Personalizing Outreach at Scale


AI tools are adept at analyzing historical interactions and customer behaviors to craft highly personalized outreach strategies. This level of personalization can lead to a substantial increase in engagement rates, making it easier for BDRs to connect with prospects and cultivate meaningful relationships. Furthermore, AI can write in your exact tone of voice, so you sound consistent and personable.

## Best Practices for Using AI for Sales Prospecting


Because AI is already taking sales prospecting by storm, we're unfortunately already hearing quite a few stories of it being used improperly and negatively impacting a company's reputation as a result. We highly encourage you to think through each of the areas below to ensure you're using AI as tactfully and professionally as possible.


### Ensure Your Personalization Efforts Are Accurate


Personalization is a critical component of effective sales prospecting. AI tools can help by analyzing customer data to create tailored messages. However, it's important to ensure the accuracy of this data. Inaccurate personalization can result in a poor customer experience and diminish the effectiveness of your outreach.


### Work with a Team of AI Engineers


Teaming up with AI engineers can greatly amplify the effectiveness of your sales prospecting. They are adept at fine-tuning AI algorithms to meet your specific needs and ensure peak performance. Regular consultations with these experts can keep you ahead of the curve and maximize your ROI.


Motion offers support to its clients from its world-class engineering team and can help them create custom AI employees for their business needs.


### Remember That Every Customer Touchpoint Matters


Each interaction with a potential customer is a chance to foster trust and solidify a relationship. AI is a powerful tool in ensuring that every touchpoint is both timely and pertinent. From the first contact to subsequent emails, each message should be meticulously composed to captivate the prospect and propel them through the sales pipeline. Double-check all of your AI-drafted content, enhance and edit when necessary, and even remove AI content when you're at a pivotal selling moment.


### Think Past ChatGPT for AI Sales Prospecting


While ChatGPT is a powerful AI tool for text generation, it is only one piece of the AI puzzle in sales prospecting. To truly excel, BDRs need a comprehensive solution that goes beyond text generation. This means integrating tools that can handle the entire sales process, from lead identification to follow-up.


Additionally, it's important to remember that sales is a workflow. You don't want your reps to just randomly use ChatGPT for their one-off requests. The result of this would be lack knowledge sharing and best pracrices across the team. Instead, they should consistently automate their most important tasks in a workflow that is built and run on an AI tool that uniquely understands your business, customers, tone, and preferences.
