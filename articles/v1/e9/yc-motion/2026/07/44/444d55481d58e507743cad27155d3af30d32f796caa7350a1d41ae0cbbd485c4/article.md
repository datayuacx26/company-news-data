# Introducing Inbox: Your Central Hub for Motion Notifications

## Why Inbox?


At Motion, we know staying on top of your tasks, projects, and team updates is critical to your productivity. Until now you had to rely on email alerts, which sometimes led to missed notifications and disrupted workflows. Inbox gives you a dedicated space inside Motion where you can quickly see, manage, and act on updates from your teammates.


## What You Can Do with Inbox


- **All your updates in one place:** Stay on top of activity from team members so you never miss an important update.
- **Quickly triage with shortcuts:** Toggle read status, search, filter, and mark all as read to cruise through your inbox.
- **Maintain flow:** Click any notification to jump straight to the relevant task, project, doc, or comment thread without leaving Inbox (or your flow state).


## How Inbox Fits Into Your Workflow


Inbox is designed with busy professionals and teams in mind. Whether you're a founder juggling multiple roles, a project manager tracking deliverables, or simply focused on getting things done, Inbox helps you streamline your daily workflow by centralizing your updates and making them actionable.


## What’s Next?


We're rolling out Inbox to all users over the next few weeks! We've also got some exciting improvements planned including Inbox coming to mobile.
