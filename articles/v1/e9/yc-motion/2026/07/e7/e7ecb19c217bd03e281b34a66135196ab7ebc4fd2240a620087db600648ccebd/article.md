# Document Version History is Here

Just a few months ago, we launched **Motion Documents** to streamline note-taking, knowledge management, and collaboration. We've been hard at work making it even better, and we're thrilled to announce the release of one of your most requested features: **Version History** !


Now, you can draft, revise, and collaborate confidently knowing every version of your work is always protected.


## Track Every Change


**Document Version History** keeps a clear timeline of every edit made to your document—whether it’s you, your teammates, or even suggestions from Motion AI.


Access it directly from the document header to see who changed what and when. Preview any version before restoring it, then roll back with one click.


## Get To Final Draft Faster


- **Risk-free exploration:** Experiment with content ideas knowing you're one click away from any previous state.
- **Audit trail:** See exactly who made which edits and when they happened.
- **Peace of mind:** Edit fearlessly knowing you can always recover deleted content.


## Rolling Out Now


Version History will be available to all users over the next week. Happy editing!
