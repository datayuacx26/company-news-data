# Cambio's AI-powered Retrofit Analytics Drive GRESB Points

### Unlocking AI-Powered Energy Opportunities


GRESB season has traditionally been viewed as a reporting obligation - a necessary but time-intensive process focused on data aggregation, documentation, and submission.


At Cambio, we see it differently.


We’re proud to be supporting clients globally with their GRESB preparation and submissions - not just to improve scores, but to transform reporting into a catalyst for portfolio-wide decarbonization and value creation.


### Moving Beyond Static Reporting


For many real estate investors and operators, GRESB reporting workflows remain fragmented. Data lives in multiple systems. Energy audits sit in PDFs. Retrofit recommendations are static and quickly outdated. And reporting often becomes a backward-looking exercise.


Cambio changes that dynamic.


Our AI-native retrofit project management suite and decarbonization identification tools transform raw building data - utility bills, system inventories, historical performance - into validated, property-level insights. Rather than simply reporting on performance, clients can identify and prioritize high-impact energy improvement opportunities across entire portfolios.


To date, Cambio has helped identify **over $80 million in energy improvement opportunities** across global portfolios - turning compliance into measurable financial and environmental outcomes.


### Supporting GRESB’s Technical Building Assessments (RA2)


GRESB’s evolving framework increasingly emphasizes Technical Building Assessments and the identification of energy performance improvement opportunities (RA2).


This shift reflects a broader market reality: investors and stakeholders expect not just accurate data, but credible, forward-looking decarbonization pathways.


Cambio’s platform directly supports this reality by:


- Systematically identifying asset-level retrofit opportunities
- Quantifying emissions reductions and financial impact
- Maintaining transparent documentation of methodology and assumptions
- Tracking project implementation through completion


The result is stronger alignment between sustainability reporting and real-world capital planning - ensuring that improvement opportunities are not only identified, but acted upon.


### From Compliance to Competitive Advantage


The most forward-thinking organizations are using GRESB not just as a benchmark, but as a framework to strengthen asset-level strategy.


By integrating decarbonization planning, financial modeling, and data validation into a single system, Cambio enables teams to:


- Improve data confidence and auditability
- Reduce manual reporting burden
- Accelerate retrofit execution
- Align sustainability targets with ROI-driven investment decisions


GRESB becomes less about responding to a questionnaire - and more about demonstrating disciplined, data-backed portfolio management.
