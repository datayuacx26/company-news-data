## Altria to Host Webcast of 2026 First-Quarter Results


4/16/2026


Download this Press Release PDF Format (opens in new window)


RICHMOND, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Altria Group, Inc. (Altria) (NYSE: MO) will host a live audio webcast on Thursday, April 30, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time to discuss its 2026 first-quarter business results. Altria will issue a press release containing its business results at approximately 7:00 a.m. Eastern Time the same day. The webcast can be accessed at[altria.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.altria.com%2F&esheet=54512569&newsitemid=20260415386882&lan=en-US&anchor=altria.com&index=1&md5=4df8582499cf1ee25184129fa6ef74e4) .


During the webcast, Billy Gifford, Altria’s Chief Executive Officer, and Sal Mancuso, Altria’s Chief Financial Officer, will discuss the Company’s 2026 first-quarter business results and answer questions from the investment community and news media.


The webcast will be in a listen-only mode. Pre-event registration is necessary; directions are posted at[www.altria.com/webcasts](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.altria.com%2Fwebcasts&esheet=54512569&newsitemid=20260415386882&lan=en-US&anchor=www.altria.com%2Fwebcasts&index=2&md5=58f8c1e4a6d8d03fe8fd9ee4b1ad72e2) . An archived copy of the webcast will be available on[altria.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.altria.com%2F&esheet=54512569&newsitemid=20260415386882&lan=en-US&anchor=altria.com&index=3&md5=83c7c393a2356412f942f587041e76db) .
