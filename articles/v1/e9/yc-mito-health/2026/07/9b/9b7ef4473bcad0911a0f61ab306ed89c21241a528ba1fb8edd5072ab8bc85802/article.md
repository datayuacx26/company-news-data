# Skip the Lab: Mito At-Home Test Kits

Getting your bloodwork done has always meant the same thing: book an appointment, drive to a collection site, roll up your sleeve for a phlebotomist, and hope the parking wasn’t terrible. For a lot of people, that friction is the reason a “I should really get my levels checked” thought never turns into an actual result.


We wanted to remove that friction. Today we’re launching **seven at-home test kits** you can order online, collect from your own kitchen table, and mail back — with the same lab analysis, clinician review, and dashboard you’d get from a full Mito panel.


## No needle, no finger stick


The kits use the **Tasso+ collector** , a small single-use device that sits on your upper arm. You press it into place, a tiny puncture and a gentle vacuum draw a capillary blood sample, and you’re done in a few minutes. There’s no venipuncture, no vial, and no repeated finger-pricking — just one painless collector and a sample container.


Every kit ships with everything you need:


- The single-use Tasso+ collector
- Collection materials and a sample tube
- Step-by-step instructions
- Prepaid return packaging (both directions)


Once you’ve collected your sample, you drop it in the prepaid mailer and send it back to the lab. Your results post straight to your **Mito dashboard** as soon as the lab finishes, reviewed by a clinician — the same experience as our in-lab panels, minus the trip.

## How the process works, start to finish


1. **Order online.** Pick your kit and check out. Members see at-cost pricing automatically.
2. **Kit ships to you.** Expect it in about **2–5 business days** via USPS. You can track it from your dashboard.
3. **Collect your sample.** Follow the instructions, use the Tasso+ collector on your upper arm, and fill in the collection form. Take a minute to double-check your name and date of birth match your order — the lab needs those to line up.
4. **Mail it back.** Drop the prepaid return package in the mail. Samples travel back to the lab overnight and stay stable for several days in transit, so a normal mail turnaround is well within range.
5. **Get clinician-reviewed results.** Your numbers post to your Mito dashboard once the lab completes testing, with plain-language context and next steps.


## Frequently Asked Questions


**How soon should I collect after the kit arrives?** Collect and mail it back promptly. The sample is designed to stay stable in transit, and the prepaid return shipping is expedited so it reaches the lab quickly.


**Where do I find tracking?** Both the outbound and return shipments are visible in your dashboard.


**What if my kit arrives damaged?** Don’t use it — reach out to support and we’ll get a replacement sent.


**Can I cancel after ordering?** There’s a short window to change or cancel before a kit is fulfilled. Once it ships, it’s on its way to you.


**Why does my info need to match exactly?** The lab matches your sample to your order using the details on your collection form. Small things like a nickname or a typo are usually fine, but if your information can’t be confidently matched, the lab may not be able to run the sample — so accurate details protect your result.


## One important note


At-home collection is available **only for these seven kits.** Every other Mito lab and panel — including our Essential, Core, and Ultra panels and any à la carte markers — still uses a standard blood draw at a collection site near you. Scans like DEXA and MRI are booked at imaging centers as usual.


The at-home kits aren’t a replacement for comprehensive testing; they’re a lower-friction way to check the things that benefit from regular tracking, or to get started before you commit to a full panel.
