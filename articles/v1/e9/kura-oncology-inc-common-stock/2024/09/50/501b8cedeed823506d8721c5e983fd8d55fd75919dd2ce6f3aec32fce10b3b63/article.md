# Fostering a Workplace Place for Innovators to Thrive

Throughout my career, I’ve been lucky enough to work with innovative and creative minds who push the biopharma field forward. Great innovators think outside-the-box, aren’t afraid to speak up, and set ambitious goals for themselves. But the best minds can easily be stifled if they aren’t in a company that supports them and their development. Thankfully, Kura Oncology is place where innovators grow, with patients always at the forefront of our work.

. This external recognition highlights what I already knew: Kura is a place to innovate and push boundaries.


> Team Kura is walking the walk as science-driven innovators focused on working as one for patients. We are empowered to find the best avenues for treatments, leaving no stone unturned. We understand the importance of our work for patients and are deeply committed to pushing the field of oncology forward through development of precision treatments for acute leukemias and more.


One recent example of Kura’s pioneering work was our place at the forefront of implementing the U.S. Food and Drug Administration’s (FDA) Project Optimus, which seeks to ensure cancer patients receive the lowest possible dose of treatment with the greatest possible benefit, rather than the highest tolerable dose. When the program was brand new, we took on the new guidance without missing a step. Through close, cross-functional internal collaboration and consistent dialogue with the FDA, the Kura team moved quickly on our feet to make real-time changes to the KOMET-001 clinical trial to ensure we were compliant with Project Optimus and design a trial with patient benefit in mind. Kura’s work with the FDA has laid the foundation for other companies to follow suit.


Another example of an innovative mindset across the company is our work in pay equity. It’s rare for companies our size to undergo a pay equity analysis, but our human resources and leadership team are consistently ahead of the curve. We brought in external experts to conduct the analysis to ensure all employees are paid fairly and there are no differences on the basis of gender, race or ethnicity. The analysis showed that Kura team members are in fact compensated fairly, showing that Kura values innovation and hard work regardless of demographic. Additionally, it highlights our industry-leading, visionary operations team that worked to conduct this analysis in the first place.
