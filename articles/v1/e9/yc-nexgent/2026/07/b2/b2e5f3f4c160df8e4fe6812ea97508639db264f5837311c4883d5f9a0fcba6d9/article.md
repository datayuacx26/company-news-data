## What Are the Best Certifications and Courses for Beginners?


To maximize your job opportunities and salary, pursue certifications that are widely recognized by employers. Here are some top options:


- **CompTIA Security+:** A foundational cybersecurity certification, highly valued for entry-level security roles.
- **Certified Network Defender (CND):** Focuses on network defense, risk management, and forensics—ideal for aspiring network defenders.
- **Cisco CCNA:** Core networking skills for anyone moving beyond help desk roles.
- **Network Defense Essentials (NDE):** Entry-level, self-paced, with labs and capstone projects.
- **Incident Response and Forensics:** Learn how to handle breaches and investigate cyberattacks.
- **Penetration Testing Fundamentals:** For those interested in offensive roles like ethical hacking.
