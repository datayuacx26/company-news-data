## Real Success Story: From Help Desk to Cybersecurity Engineer


Consider Alex, a 32-year-old with five years of help desk experience but no computer science degree. Like many, Alex wanted higher pay, job security, and a more meaningful role. Mentorship at NGT Academy provided the roadmap, accountability, and industry insight that made his transition possible.


### Alex’s Step-by-Step Journey with Mentorship


1. **Building a Solid Foundation:** Alex reviewed networking, operating systems, and security basics with mentor-led labs and feedback.
2. **Specialization and Skill Development:** Guided by mentors, Alex specialized in SOC analysis, mastering threat monitoring and incident response via hands-on simulations and CTF competitions.
3. **Certifications and Portfolio Building:** With a mentor’s encouragement, Alex earned his CompTIA Security+ and built a public portfolio, including vulnerability assessments and malware analysis write-ups.
4. **Career Coaching and Job Search:** Mentors helped polish Alex’s resume, practice interviews, and expand his professional network.


The result? Alex landed a cybersecurity engineer role in under a year with a 35% salary increase. Mentorship was the constant thread guiding each step.

## Key Steps to Land Your First IT or Cybersecurity Job with Mentorship


1. **Set Clear Goals:** Define your desired role (e.g., SOC analyst, network engineer) and identify the skills required.
2. **Engage in Structured Training:** Choose programs with hands-on labs and real-world projects.
3. **Work with a Mentor:** Meet regularly with mentors for feedback, accountability, and industry insights.
4. **Build a Portfolio:** Document labs, simulations, and independent projects on LinkedIn or GitHub.
5. **Earn Certifications:** Obtain credentials like CompTIA Security+ and CySA+ to validate your skills.
6. **Prepare for the Job Search:** Use career coaching for resume reviews, interview preparation, and networking strategies.
