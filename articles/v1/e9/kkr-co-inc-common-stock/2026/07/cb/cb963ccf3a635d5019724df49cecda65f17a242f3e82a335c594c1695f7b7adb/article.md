In our **Q3 2026 Global Wealth Investment Playbook** , we argue that investors are navigating a fundamentally different market environment, one shaped by geopolitical uncertainty, structurally higher inflation, and rapid technological change. The defining characteristic of this cycle is divergence **.** Growth, inflation, earnings, labor markets, housing, and the benefits of productivity are increasingly diverging across sectors, regions, and income cohorts. This backdrop is complicating central bank policy and reinforcing our view that portfolio construction, manager selection, and relative value are becoming increasingly important drivers of long-term investment outcomes.


Against this backdrop, we continue to advocate for “high grading” portfolios, emphasizing quality assets, durable cash flows, diversification, and thoughtful portfolio construction. Public markets remain supported by productivity gains, but persistently elevated valuations and tighter credit spreads suggest investors may need to be more selective about where they take risk going forward.


Specifically, we believe investors should consider:


- **Prioritizing quality across asset classes, sectors, and capital structures by ‘high-grading’ portfolios.**
- **Building more resilient portfolios** as stock-bond correlations remain elevated and traditional diversifiers become less reliable.
- **Increasing exposure to Private Markets** to access contractual cash flows, operational value creation, inflation-linked assets, and differentiated sources of return.
- **Remaining invested through periods of volatility** while maintaining disciplined diversification.
- **Focusing on durable business models and real-world assets** that can compound value despite ongoing AI-driven disruption and market concentration.


Within Private Markets, we continue to see compelling opportunities across Private Equity, Infrastructure, Real Estate, and Private Credit. In an environment characterized by a wider range of investment outcomes, we believe Alternatives can play an increasingly important role in generating income, preserving capital, and enhancing long-term returns. Ultimately, this quarter’s Global Wealth Investment Playbook is designed to help financial advisors build portfolios that are positioned to benefit from a market where divergence, rather than synchronization, is increasingly defining investment outcomes.
