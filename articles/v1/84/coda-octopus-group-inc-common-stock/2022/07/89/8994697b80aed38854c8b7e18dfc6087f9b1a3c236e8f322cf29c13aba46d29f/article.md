# Meet Coda Octopus Engineering at 2022 Navy Gold Coast

Coda Octopus Engineering, a subsidiary of the Coda Octopus Group (NASDAQ: CODA), will be exhibiting at the upcoming Navy Gold Coast event from September 6-8 at the San Diego Convention Center. The NDIA Department of the Navy Gold Coast Event is the premier Navy Small Business procurement conference, and provides a forum to educate, guide, and assist businesses, large and small, in support of the warfighter mission within Department of the Navy and throughout the DOD.


This event is a great opportunity to learn about Coda Octopus Engineering's work with the Navy, which includes participation on the[AQS-24 Minehunting System](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/case-studies/an-aqs-24-mine-hunting-system/) , and the[Phalanx Close-In Weapons System (CIWS)](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/case-studies/phalanx-close-in-weapon-system/) . Coda Octopus Engineering has also recently won new Navy/NAVAIR program work for the US Navy Carrier Advanced Recovery (ARC) program, and a VME circuit card update for an airborne pod. We plan to display our powerful[Thermite® line of rugged embedded computers](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/products/) powering naval and other defense missions around the world.
