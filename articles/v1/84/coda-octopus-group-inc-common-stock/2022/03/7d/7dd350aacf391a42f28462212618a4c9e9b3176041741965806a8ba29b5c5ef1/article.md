# Visit Coda Octopus Engineering and Coda Octopus at Sea-Air-Space 2022

Coda Octopus Engineering and our sister organization[Coda Octopus](https://www.codaoctopus.com/) are proud to be attending the Navy League's Sea-Air-Space Expo. This event is a great opportunity to learn about our work developing SONAR hardware and software, and telemetry systems engineering. Learn more about the powerful Thermite® line of[rugged embedded computers](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/products/) powering naval missions around the world and ask us about our role in the[AN/AQS-24 Mine Hunting program](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/case-studies/an-aqs-24-mine-hunting-system/) and other ongoing US Navy programs.


Our CEO Kevin Kane and other team members will be at Booth #107 April 4th through 6th. We look forward to seeing you there!
