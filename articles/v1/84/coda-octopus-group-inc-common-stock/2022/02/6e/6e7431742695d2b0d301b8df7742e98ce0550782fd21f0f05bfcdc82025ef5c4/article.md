# Coda Octopus Engineering at AFCEA WEST

Coda Octopus Coda Octopus Engineering is very pleased to be exhibiting at the upcoming AFCEA West show at the San Diego Convention Center (Booth 2914), February 16-18. John De Leo, Director, Business Development, and Kevin Kane, Chief Executive Officer, will be attending. Be sure to stop by and find out more about Coda Octopus Engineering’s latest[Thermite® products](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/products/) and[engineering capabilities](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/) , as well as our experience with key US Navy programs such as[Phalanx Close in Weapons System (CIWS)](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/case-studies/military-weapon-system-support/) , and the[AQS-24 Minehunting System](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/case-studies/an-aqs-24-mine-hunting-system/) .


AFCEA WEST connects the industry professionals who design and build the platforms, equipment and weapons with the designers of communications and technical systems capabilities that support the Sea Services' operations.


Visit[WEST 2022](https://www.westconference.org/West22/Public/Enter.aspx) for more information on location, accommodations, show hours and an exhibit[floor map](https://www.westconference.org/West22/Public/eventmap.aspx?shavailable=1&ID=91474&sortMenu=105010) . We hope to see you at the show next week!


*P.S. we will also be exhibiting at[Sea, Air & Space](https://seaairspace.org/) in National Harbor, Maryland April 4-6 at[booth 107](https://sas22.mapyourshow.com/8_0/floorplan/) .*
