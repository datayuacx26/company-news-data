# Coda Octopus Engineering Receives AS9100:D Certification

Murray, Utah - Coda Octopus Engineering, a subsidiary of the Coda Octopus Group (NASDAQ: CODA), is pleased to announce that it has received its AS9100:D certification (including ISO9001:2015) for its quality management system.


AS9100 is the International Quality Management System standard specifically for the Aerospace and Defense industry and provides suppliers with rigid requirements for creating and maintaining a comprehensive quality management system that helps ensure the provision of safe and reliable products.


“We are extremely proud to receive the AS9100 Certification. This is a well-earned achievement and demonstrates Coda Octopus Engineering's commitment to quality management,” said Kevin Kane, Chief Executive Officer, Coda Octopus Coda Octopus Engineering. “We look forward to continuing to partner with our defense customers to design and manufacture the highest quality solutions for the most challenging environments.”


## About Coda Octopus Engineering


Coda Octopus Engineering, a subsidiary of the Coda Octopus Group (NASDAQ: CODA), is a trusted DoD sub-contractor and leader in the design and production of custom-engineered electronic solutions for demanding defense applications, with a distinguished past performance record going as far back as 1977. A qualified small business certified to AS9100:D, Coda Octopus Engineering also provides the Thermite® family of products, a competitive range of rugged embedded computing solutions for military applications which can be customized for specific needs.
