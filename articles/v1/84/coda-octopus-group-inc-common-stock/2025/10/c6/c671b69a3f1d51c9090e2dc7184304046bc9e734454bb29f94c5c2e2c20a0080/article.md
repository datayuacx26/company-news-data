# Coda Octopus Engineering Announces Comprehensive RFSoC Design and Integration Services for Defense Applications

Murray, Utah – Coda Octopus Engineering, a subsidiary of Coda Octopus Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: CODA), today announced its comprehensive capabilities for designing, packaging, and integrating RF System-on-Chip (RFSoC) solutions for defense and aerospace applications. Drawing on four decades of expertise in thermal management, FPGA development, and circuit card engineering, the company enables customers to transition their RFSoC development systems from desktop prototypes to production-ready, ruggedized military hardware.


Modern RFSoC platforms have revolutionized RF system design by enabling rapid development through digital signal processing without the complex RF hardware design traditionally required. However, transitioning these systems into compact, thermally-managed packages suitable for demanding military environments requires specialized cross-disciplinary expertise.


"The miniaturization and packaging of modern RF Systems on Chip represents an ideal showcase for our full-spectrum engineering capabilities," said Jim Balent, Chief Executive Officer of Coda Octopus Engineering. "Our team understands how to take customer’s RFSoC development platforms and transform them into ruggedized solutions that meet the demanding requirements of military applications for use in unmanned systems and in pods."


Coda Octopus Engineering's RFSoC integration services include custom packaging and miniaturization for size, weight, and power (SWaP) constrained applications; thermal management solutions; designs for harsh environments; FPGA development and optimization; circuit card design with RF signal conditioning when required; integration with compute engines and custom I/O boards; and firmware development for complete system functionality.


The company's expertise extends from initial concept through full-rate production in its AS9100:D certified manufacturing facility in Salt Lake City, Utah. All products are manufactured to IPC-A-610 Class 3 standards and undergo rigorous environmental stress screening to ensure mission-critical reliability.


Coda Octopus Engineering has successfully supported major defense programs for over three decades, including the MK-15 CIWS (Phalanx/SeaRAM), the AN/AQS-24 Mine Hunting System, and the AN/TPS-71 ROTHR system. The company's Thermite® line of ruggedized embedded computers currently operates in demanding applications across land, air, and sea platforms worldwide.


"Whether you're working with Xilinx Zynq RFSoC devices or other platforms, our team can help realize your RF system vision," Balent added. "We offer the unique combination of small business agility with deep technical expertise and proven performance on critical military programs."


### About Coda Octopus Engineering


Coda Octopus Engineering is a qualified small business subsidiary of Coda Octopus Group (NASDAQ: CODA), specializing in defense systems engineering, rugged embedded computing, and custom electronics development. The company maintains comprehensive qualifications including AS9100:D certification, NIST 800-171 compliance, and DCAA-compliant accounting systems from its Salt Lake City facility.
