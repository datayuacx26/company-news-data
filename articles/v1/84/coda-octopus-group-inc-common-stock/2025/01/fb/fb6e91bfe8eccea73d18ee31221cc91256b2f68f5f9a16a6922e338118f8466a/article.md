# Coda Octopus Engineering Secures Prime Position on Navy's $5 Billion SeaPort NXG Contract

**Murray, Utah** - Coda Octopus Engineering, Inc., a subsidiary of the Coda Octopus Group (NASDAQ: CODA), is proud to announce that it has been awarded a prime position on the SeaPort NXG services and support contract. This achievement positions the company as a trusted provider of Naval services and support under the SeaPort NXG contract which is valued at approximately $5 billion. Coda Octopus Engineering’s contract is for four-years.


## Expanding Our Naval Domain Expertise


The SeaPort NXG contracting vehicle is designed to procure a wide range of professional services and technical support across multiple domains. With over 40 years of experience in naval systems and defense engineering, Coda Octopus Engineering brings proven capabilities that align perfectly with the Navy's evolving needs.


"This award validates our decades-long commitment to naval defense systems and recognizes our unique expertise in solving complex engineering challenges," said CEO, Jim Balent. "From our historic role in discovering the Titanic to our ongoing support of critical systems like the AN/AQS-24 Mine Hunting System and Phalanx CIWS, we understand the naval domain like few others."


## Leveraging Four Decades of Naval Experience


Coda Octopus Engineering's selection for[SeaPort NXG](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/articles/coda-octopus-engineering-secures-prime-position-on-navys-5-billion-seaport-nxg-contract/) builds upon an impressive track record of naval program support:


- **Mine Hunting Systems** : Over 30 years supporting the[AN/AQS-24 Mine Hunting System](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/case-studies/an-aqs-24-mine-hunting-system/) , including the design of high-speed telemetry systems and underwater data recording solutions
- **Weapons Systems** : More than 20 years providing full life-cycle support for the[MK-15 CIWS](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/case-studies/phalanx-close-in-weapon-system/) (Close-In Weapons System), including both Phalanx and SeaRAM configurations
- **Radar Systems** : Ongoing support for the[AN/TPS-71 ROTHR](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/case-studies/an-tps-71-rothr/) (Relocatable Over-the-Horizon Radar) program with advanced RF signal conditioning systems
- **Deep-Sea Exploration** : Foundational work in underwater telemetry and data systems, including participation in the discovery of the RMS Titanic and German battleship Bismarck


## Comprehensive Engineering Solutions


Under the SeaPort NXG contract, Coda Octopus Engineering will leverage its full spectrum of capabilities:


- [Systems Engineering](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/systems-engineering/) : Technical data packages, factory acceptance tests, compliance matrices, and comprehensive documentation
- **Hardware Design** :[Custom circuit card design](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/pcb-design/) ,[RF systems](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/rf-design/) , power management, and[thermal solutions](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/thermal-management/)
- **Software Development** :[Embedded systems](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/embedded-real-time-systems/) ,[user interfaces](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/user-interface-design/) , mission software, and post-mission analysis tools
- **Obsolescence Management** : Technology insertion and component[lifecycle management](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/obsolescence-management/) to extend system life
- **Manufacturing** : AS9100:D certified production facility with[HASS/ESS testing capabilities](https://www.codaoctopusengineering.com/services/manufacturing-testing/)


## Four-Year Performance Period Creates Strategic Opportunities


The four-year performance period provides Coda Octopus Engineering with a stable platform to expand its naval program support while continuing to innovate in critical technology areas. The company's NIST 800-171 adherence and AS9100:D certification ensure it meets the stringent security and quality requirements expected by naval customers.


"SeaPort NXG opens new doors for us to apply our engineering expertise across a broader range of naval programs," added CTO, Jason Martin. "Whether it's developing next-generation rugged computers, solving complex obsolescence challenges, or creating innovative solutions for emerging threats, we're ready to support the Navy's mission."


## About Coda Octopus Engineering


Coda Octopus Engineering, a qualified small business and subsidiary of Coda Octopus Group (NASDAQ: CODA), specializes in defense systems, custom electronics, and software development. With AS9100:D certification and NIST 800-171 compliance, the company provides full lifecycle support for critical military systems from its Salt Lake City facility.
