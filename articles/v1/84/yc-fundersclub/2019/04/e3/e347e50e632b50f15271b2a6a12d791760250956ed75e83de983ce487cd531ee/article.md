# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q1 2019

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds:


- [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/) announced its $30M Series B led by Accel. The company provides transparency for the blockchain economy to banks, business, and governments. We invested in the Seed and Series A.
- [SendBird](https://sendbird.com/) announced its $52M Series B led by ICONIQ. SendBird provides APIs for chat, voice and video calls, and support. We invested in the Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Zum](https://www.ridezum.com/) announced its $40M Series C led by BMW i Ventures, with participation by Sequoia Capital, Spark Capital, and Volvo. Zum provides schools with a one-stop partner for safe and reliable student transportation. We invested in the Seed, Series B, and Series C.
