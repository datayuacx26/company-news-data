# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q2 2019

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds:


- [Webflow](https://webflow.com/) announced its $72M Series A led by Accel for its no-code platform to design and build responsive, interactive websites and web apps. We invested in the Seed and Series A.
- [Notable Labs](https://notablelabs.com/) announced its $40M Series B led by B Capital for its drug discovery and development platform. We invested in the Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Substack](https://substack.com/) announced its $15M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz for its subscription newsletter infrastructure platform for writers in the creator economy. We invested in the Seed.
- [Outschool](https://outschool.com/) announced its $8.5M Series A led by USV for its marketplace of live online education classes and camps for kids ages 3-18. We invested in the Seed and a Seed follow-on.
