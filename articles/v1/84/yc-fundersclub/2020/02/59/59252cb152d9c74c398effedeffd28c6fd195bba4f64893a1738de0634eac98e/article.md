# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q4 2019

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds:


- [Gecko Robotics](https://www.geckorobotics.com/) announced its $40M Series B led by Drive Capital for its industrial inspections robotics and software company. We invested in the Seed and Series B.
- [Embrace](https://embrace.io/) announced its $7.5M Series A led by AV8 for its performance monitoring and analytics platform built for mobile apps. We invested in the Seed.
