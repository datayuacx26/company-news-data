# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q3 2020

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds:


- [Rippling](https://www.rippling.com/) was minted a unicorn with the close of a $145M Series B led by Founders Fund. They help companies with employee onboarding, identity/device management, and more. We invested at Seed.
- [ShipBob](https://www.shipbob.com/) , a tech-enabled 3PL, closed a $68M Series D led by Softbank. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [TruePill](https://www.truepill.com/) , an API for pharmacy fulfillment and telehealth, closed a $75M Series C led by Oak HC/FT. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Outschool](https://outschool.com/) , a marketplace for live online classes and camps for kids 3-18, closed a $45M Series B led by Lightspeed. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Forage](https://www.theforage.com/) (fka InsideSherpa), a virtual work experience platform, closed a $9.3M Series A led by Lightspeed. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/) , which offers tools to grow mobile subscription businesses, closed a $15M Series B led by Index Ventures. We invested at Seed.
- [DemoDesk](https://demodesk.com/) , which powers remote sales meetings and customer conversations, closed a $8M Series A led by Balderton Capital. We invested at Seed and Series A.
