# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q3 2021

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds and events:


- [GitLab](https://about.gitlab.com/) (NASDAQ: GTLB), a SaaS platform for DevOps, completed a successful public listing on October 14, 2021. We invested at Seed, Series B, and Series D.
- [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/) , provider of cryptocurrency investigation and transaction monitoring solutions, closed a $100M Series E led by Coatue. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Eight Sleep](https://www.eightsleep.com/) , a smart mattress and sleep company, announced an $86M Series C led by Valor Equity Partners. We invested at Seed and Series B.
- [Jerry](https://getjerry.com/) , a consumer app for car insurance, financing, repairs, and more, announced a $75M Series C led by Goodwater Capital. We invested at Seed and Series C.
- [Solugen](https://solugen.bio/) , a carbon negative chemical manufacturer, announced a $357M round led by GIC. We invested at Seed.
- [Homelight](https://www.homelight.com/) , a real estate technology platform, announced a $100M Series D led by Zeev Ventures. We invested at Seed.
- [EquipmentShare](https://www.equipmentshare.com/) , a construction equipment rental technology platform, announced a $230M Series D led by Tiger Global. We invested at Seed.
- [Outschool](https://outschool.com/) , a marketplace providing live classes and after-school activities for children, announced a $75M Series C co-led by Coatue and Tiger Global, and a $110M Series D led by Tiger Global. We invested at Seed, Series B, and Series D.
- [Human Interest](https://humaninterest.com/) , a SaaS solution for businesses to offer and administer 401(k) plans, announced a $200M Series D led by The Rise Fund/TPG. We invested at Seed, Series C, and Series D.
- [Rappi](https://www.rappi.com/) , a leader in on-demand retail in LatAm, announced a $500M round led by T.Rowe Price. We invested at Seed.
- [Go1](https://www.go1.com/) , which provides curated online learning materials and tools to businesses, announced a $200M Series D co-led by Softbank Vision Fund, AirTree Ventures, and Salesforce Ventures. We invested at Seed.
- [Ripio](https://www.ripio.com/) , a LatAm-based crypto services firm, announced a $50M Series B led by DCG. We invested at Seed and Series B.
- [Goldbelly](https://www.goldbelly.com/) , a curated marketplace for gourmet good and good gifts, announced a $100M Series C led by Spectrum Equity. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Rose Rocket](https://www.roserocket.com/) , a trucking TMS SaaS company, announced a $25M Series A led by Addition and Shine Capital. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Ontop](https://www.ontop.ai/) , a global platform for onboarding, managing, and paying team members, announced a $20M Series A co-led by Tiger Global and Point72. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Forage](https://www.theforage.com/) , a provider of virtual experience programs, announced a $25M Series B led by Blackbird Ventures. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Kyte](https://drivekyte.com/) , a rental car provider that delivers vehicles to customers, announced a $30M Series A co-led by Park West Asset Management and Sterling Road. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Leena AI](https://leena.ai/) , an employee helpdesk and employee experience company, announced a $30M Series B led by Bessemer Venture Partners. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
