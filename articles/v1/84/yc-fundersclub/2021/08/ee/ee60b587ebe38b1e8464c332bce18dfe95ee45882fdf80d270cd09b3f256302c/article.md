# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q2 2021

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds and events:


- [Embark Trucks](https://embarktrucks.com/) , an autonomous trucking technology company, announced its intention to go public at a pro forma market cap of ~$5.16B via merger with Northern Genesis Acquisition Corp. II, a NYSE-listed SPAC with $414M cash in trust, and a $200 million fully committed PIPE from investors including CPP Investments, Sequoia Capital, and Tiger Global. We invested at Seed, Series B, and Series C.
- [Shipbob](https://www.shipbob.com/) , an ecommerce fulfillment provider, announced a $200M Series E led by Bain Capital. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Aircall](https://aircall.io/) , a cloud-based call center SaaS company, raised a $120M Series D led by Goldman Sachs Asset Management. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Shippo](https://goshippo.com/) , a shipping software company for ecommerce, closed a $50M Series E led by Bessemer. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Substack](https://substack.com/) , a paid newsletter platform, raised $65M Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz. We invested at Seed and Series B.
- [Human Interest](https://humaninterest.com/) , an SMB 401(k) provider, announced a $55M Series C led by Glynn Capital. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series C.
- [Bitso](https://bitso.com/) , a cryptocurrency exchange based in Latin America, announced a $250M Series C led by Tiger Global and Coatue. We invested at Seed.
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/) , a subscription revenue management SaaS platform, raised a $37.5M Series B led by Y Combinator Continuity. We invested at Seed and Series B.
- [Grin](https://grin.co/) , a SaaS platform for ecommerce companies to manage influencer marketing, raised a $17M Series A-2 led by Imaginary Ventures. We invested at Seed, Series A-1, and Series A-2.
- [Zeroheight](https://zeroheight.com/) , a SaaS platform for managing Design Ops, raised a $10M Series A led by Tribe Capital. We invested at Seed and Series A.
