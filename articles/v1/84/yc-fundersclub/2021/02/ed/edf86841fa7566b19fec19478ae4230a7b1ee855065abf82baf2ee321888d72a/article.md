# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q4 2020

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds and events:


-


[DoorDash](https://www.doordash.com/) completed an IPO. We became early shareholders via their acquisition of portfolio company, Rickshaw.


-


[Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/) announced it has confidentially filed Form S-1, expressing its intent to go public. We invested at Seed, Series A, Series D, and Series E.


-


[Instacart](https://www.instacart.com/) announced $200M in new financing led by D1 and Valiant. We invested at Seed and Series D.


-


[Webflow](https://webflow.com/) announced its $140M Series B led by Accel. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.


-


[SecondMeasure](https://secondmeasure.com/) announced its acquisition by Bloomberg L.P.. We invested at Seed.


-


[Yotta](https://www.withyotta.com/) closed a Series A led by Base10. We invested at Seed and Series A.


-


[Leena AI](https://leena.ai/) closed a Series A led by Greycroft. We invested at Seed and Series A.


-


[Kyte](https://drivekyte.com/) closed a Series A led by DN Capital. We invested at Seed and Series A.
