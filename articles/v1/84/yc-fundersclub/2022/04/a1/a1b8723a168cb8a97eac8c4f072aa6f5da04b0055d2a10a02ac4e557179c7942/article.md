# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q1 2022

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds and events:


- [Gecko Robotics](https://www.geckorobotics.com/) , an industrial inspections robotics and software company, announced its $73M Series C led by XN with participation from Founders Fund, Drive Capital, and others. We invested at Seed, Series B, and Series C.
- [Filmhub](https://filmhub.com/) , a film distribution technology company, announced a $6.8M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from 8VC and others. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Convictional](https://www.convictional.com/) , which helps retailers connect with their dropship, marketplace, and wholesale suppliers, announced its $40M Series B led by Y Combinator Continuity. We participated at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Rain Neuromorphics](https://rain.ai/) , creators of brain-inspired neuromorphic hardware for artificial intelligence, announced a $25M Series A led by Prosperity 7 Ventures, a venture capital fund of Aramco Ventures. We participated at Seed and Series A.
