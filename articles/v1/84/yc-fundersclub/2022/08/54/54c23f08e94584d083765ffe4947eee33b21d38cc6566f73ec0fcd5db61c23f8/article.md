# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q2 2022

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds and events:


- [Rippling](https://www.rippling.com/) , the HR & IT software company, announced its $250M Series D led by Kleiner Perkins and Bedrock Capital. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/) , the blockchain data platform, announced its $170M Series F led by GIC with participation from Accel, Blackstone, and others. We invested at Seed, Series A, Series B, and Series F.
- [Webflow](https://webflow.com/) , the no-code visual development platform, announced its $120M Series C led by Y Combinator with participation from CapitalG, Accel, and others. We invested at Seed, Series A, Series B, and Series C.
- [Clicoh](https://clicoh.com/) , the e-commerce logistics platform for LatAm, announced its $25M Series A led by Tiger Global. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Treble](https://www.treble.ai/) , which helps companies better integrate their cloud tools with messaging apps used by consumers, announced its $15M Series A led by Tiger Global. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Fort Robotics](https://www.fortrobotics.com/) , a pioneer in communications and control for smart machines, announced its $25M Series B led by Tiger Global. We invested at Seed, Seed 2, and Series B.
- [Paragon](https://www.useparagon.com/) , which helps cloud apps offer better integrations with other cloud apps, raised a $13M Series A led by Inspired Capital. We invested at Seed and Series A.
