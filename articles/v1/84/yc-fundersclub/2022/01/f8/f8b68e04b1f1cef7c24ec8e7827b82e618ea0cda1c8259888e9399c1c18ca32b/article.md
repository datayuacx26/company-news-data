# FundersClub Portfolio Follow-On Rounds Update - Q4 2021

We'd like to congratulate FundersClub portfolio companies on their ongoing progress, as well as to welcome aboard their new VC investor partners.


Selected recent FundersClub portfolio company follow-on rounds and events:


- [GitLab](https://about.gitlab.com/) , the SaaS DevOps platform, completed an IPO (NASDAQ: GTLB). We invested at Seed, Series B, and Series D.
- [Embark Trucks](https://embarktrucks.com/) , an autonomous trucking company, completed a public listing (NASDAQ: EMBK). We invested at Seed, Series B, and Series C.
- [Rippling](https://www.rippling.com/) , providers of software and services to manage HR and IT, announced a $250M Series C led by Sequoia Capital. We invested at Seed.
- [Spendesk](https://www.spendesk.com/) , which provides company cards and spend management software, announced a $114M Series C extension led by Tiger Global. We invested at Seed, Series B, and Series C.
- [Truepill](https://truepill.com/) , providers of infrastructure for telehealth, pharmacy, and diagnostics, announced a $142M Series D. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Grin](https://grin.co/) , a creator economy SaaS company, raised a $112M Series B led by Lone Pine capital. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Weave](https://www.getweave.com/) Communications, a SaaS company offering scheduling and customer interaction software for SMBs, completed an IPO (NYSE:WEAV). We invested at Series A.
- [Cleartax](https://cleartax.in/) , a tax and financial software company targeting India and the global market, announced a $50M Series C led by Composite Capital. We invested at Seed and follow-on.
- [Fountain](https://get.fountain.com/) , an applicant tracking system for high-volume hiring, announced an $85M Series C led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2. We invested at Seed.
- [Embrace](https://embrace.io/) , a mobile observability and data platform, raised a $38M Series B led by NEA. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Wonderschool](https://corp.wonderschool.com/) , which provides software for childcare centers and microschools, announced a $25M Series B led by Goldman Sachs. We invested at Seed, Series A, and Series B.
- [Headout](https://www.headout.com/) , a marketplace for booking same-day tours, events and activities, announced a $12M Series B led by Glade Brook Capital. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Platzi](https://platzi.com/) , a professional education and re-skilling platform targeting LatAm, announced a $62M Series B led by Prosus. We invested at Seed and Series B.
- [Tamatem](https://tamatem.co/) , a mobile game publisher focused on the MENA region, announced a $11M Series B led by Krafton. We invested at Seed.
- [CoLab Software](https://www.colabsoftware.com/) , a provider of collaboration and issue-tracking SaaS for engineering teams, announced a $17M Series A led by Insight Partners. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [PopSQL](https://popsql.com/) , a SQL collaboration and editing platform with dbt support, raised a $14M Series A led by Tiger Global. We invested at Seed and Series A.
- [Together](https://www.togetherplatform.com/) , which provides corporate mentoring software, announced a $5M Series Seed led by AltaIR Capital. We invested at Seed.
