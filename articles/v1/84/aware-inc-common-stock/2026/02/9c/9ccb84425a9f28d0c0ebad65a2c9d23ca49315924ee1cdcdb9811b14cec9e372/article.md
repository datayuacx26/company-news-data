# Aware Sets Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Webcast for Wednesday, March 4, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time


**BURLINGTON, MASS. –** February 18, 2026 –[Aware, Inc.](https://www.aware.com/) (NASDAQ: AWRE), global leader in biometric identity and authentication solutions, will hold a webcast on Wednesday, March 4, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern time to discuss its financial results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. Financial results will be issued in a press release before the call.


Aware management will host the webcast presentation, followed by a question-and-answer session.


**Date:** Wednesday, March 4, 2026


**Time:** 5:00 p.m. Eastern time (2:00 p.m. Pacific time)


**Webcast:**[Register Here](https://event.on24.com/wcc/r/5226308/FF9D582B73A517D7A82511BE51089696)


Interested parties may submit questions in advance of the webcast by emailingAWRE@gateway-grp.com .


The presentation will be made available for replay in the[investor relations](https://www.aware.com/investor-relations/) section of the Company’s[website](https://www.aware.com/) . The audio recording will be available for approximately 90 days following the live event.
