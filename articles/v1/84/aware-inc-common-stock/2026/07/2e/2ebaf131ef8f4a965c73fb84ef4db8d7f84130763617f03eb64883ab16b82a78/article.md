# Aware Sets Second Quarter 2026 Webcast for Wednesday, July 29, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time


**BURLINGTON, MASS. –** July 15, 2026 –[Aware, Inc.](https://www.aware.com/) (NASDAQ: AWRE), global leader in biometric orchestration and identity solutions, will hold a webcast on Wednesday, July 29, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern time to discuss its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026. Financial results will be issued in a press release before the call.


Aware management will host the webcast presentation, followed by a question-and-answer session.


**Date:** Wednesday, July 29, 2026


**Time:** 5:00 p.m. Eastern time (2:00 p.m. Pacific time)


**Webcast:**[Register Here](https://event.on24.com/wcc/r/5420819/094F00A8B2674A378AC89EE9CF0FF44D)


Interested parties may submit questions in advance of the webcast by emailingIR@Aware.com


The presentation will be made available for replay in the[investor relations](https://www.aware.com/investor-relations/) section of the Company’s[website](https://www.aware.com/) . The audio recording will be available for approximately 90 days following the live event.
