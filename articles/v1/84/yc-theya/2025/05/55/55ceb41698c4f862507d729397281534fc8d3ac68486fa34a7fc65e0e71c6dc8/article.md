# Announcement: Theya to Integrate Spark for Fast, Low-Fee Payments

At Theya, we’re building tools that make Bitcoin not only secure to hold but easy to use. Today, we’re excited to announce that support for **Spark** is coming soon to Theya.


With this integration, Theya users can send and receive Bitcoin using **instant, low-fee payments** over the Spark network. It brings the ease of a modern payment experience to Bitcoin, without giving up control of your funds.


### **Why Spark?**


Spark is a Bitcoin-native Layer 2 protocol designed for instant, low-cost payments, including native stablecoins. It enables developers to build self-custodial wallets and apps that scale, interoperate with Lightning, and settle in any currency or sats.[Learn more about Spark](https://www.spark.info/news/introducing-spark?utm_source=chatgpt.com) .


By integrating Spark into Theya, we’re extending Bitcoin’s usefulness. You’ll still get the same self-custodial protections you expect from Theya, now with a smoother experience for making everyday payments.


### **A Better Way to Pay with Bitcoin**


Once Spark support is live, you’ll be able to:


- **Send and receive Bitcoin instantly**
- **Use your Bitcoin like you would a payment app** , while staying self-custodial.
- **Create shared Bitcoin wallets** with multiple participants for added flexibility and security.


These features will make Theya ideal not just for saving Bitcoin securely, but for spending it with confidence.


### **What’s Next**


Spark support will be rolling out in the coming months. We'd love to hear from you if you’re a developer, early adopter, or business interested in building with it.
