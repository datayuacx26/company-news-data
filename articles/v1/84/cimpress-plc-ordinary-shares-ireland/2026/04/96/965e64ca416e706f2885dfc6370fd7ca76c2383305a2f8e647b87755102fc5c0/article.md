# Cimpress to Host Public Q&A Call Following Q3 FY2026 Earnings Announcement

[PDF Version](https://ir.cimpress.com/node/25751/pdf)


DUNDALK, Ireland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 16, 2026-- Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) announced that it will host a public Q&A call on Thursday, April 30, 2026 at 8:00 am ET following the release of its Q3 FY2026 **** financial results at 4:05 pm ET on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. The live call will be accessible on[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents&esheet=54506562&newsitemid=20260416208113&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=1&md5=b1ffcbd6ae255b8ca02a677805d325c1) , and a replay will be available at the same link following the call. We will take live questions on the call via chat, and investors may also presubmit questions any time before 10:00 pm ET on Wednesday, April 29, 2026 by emailingir@cimpress.com .


**About Cimpress**


Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) helps millions of businesses build brands, stand out, and grow via custom print and promotional products. Founded in 1995, Cimpress is the global leader in web-to-print mass customization, delivering high-quality, affordable custom products quickly and conveniently—even in low quantities.
