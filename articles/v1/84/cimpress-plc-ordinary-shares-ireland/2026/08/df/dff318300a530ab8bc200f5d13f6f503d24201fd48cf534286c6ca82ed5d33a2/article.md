# Cimpress to Present at the 17th Annual Midwest Ideas Conference on August 26, 2026, in Chicago

[PDF Version](https://ir.cimpress.com/node/26051/pdf)


DUNDALK, Ireland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 12, 2026-- Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) today announced that Sean Quinn, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, will present at the 17th Annual Midwest Ideas Conference on August 26th, 2026, at 9:35 am ET.


The 35-minute presentation will be webcast and can be accessed through the Cimpress Investor Relations website at[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents&esheet=54587605&newsitemid=20260812562511&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=1&md5=6663cfae0d77f62c26436ae833f4f661) , and a replay of the presentation will be available at the same link until August 26, 2027.


**About Cimpress**


Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) helps millions of businesses build brands, stand out, and grow via customized physical marketing products and branded merchandise. Founded in 1995, Cimpress is the global leader in web-to-print mass customization, delivering high-quality, affordable custom products quickly and conveniently—even in low quantities.
