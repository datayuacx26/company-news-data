# Cimpress Announces Closing of $1.1 Billion Term Loan B Maturing in 2033

[PDF Version](https://ir.cimpress.com/node/25906/pdf)


DUNDALK, Ireland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 4, 2026-- Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) has closed on its previously announced new $1.1 billion senior secured Term Loan B due 2033 (the “Term Loan B”) bearing interest at SOFR (with a SOFR floor of 0.00%) plus 2.50%. Cimpress’ prior Term Loan B due 2028 has been repaid and terminated in conjunction with this transaction, which was approximately net leverage neutral on a pro-forma basis, as previously disclosed.


After giving effect to the transaction, Cimpress’ debt structure primarily consists of a $250 million secured revolving credit facility now maturing in 2031 (currently undrawn), the new Term Loan B, and $525 million aggregate principal amount of 7.375% senior notes due 2032.


**About Cimpress** Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) helps millions of businesses build brands, stand out and grow via customized physical marketing products and branded merchandise. Founded in 1995, Cimpress has become the global leader in web-to-print mass customization, delivering high-quality, affordable customized physical products quickly and conveniently, even in very low quantities. Cimpress brands include VistaPrint, WIRmachenDRUCK, Pixartprinting, Pens.com, BuildASign, druck.at, Drukwerkdeal, easyflyer, Exaprint, Packstyle, Printi, Tradeprint and BoxUp.
