# Cimpress to Present at the 16th Annual East Coast Ideas Conference on June 11, 2026 in New York

[PDF Version](https://ir.cimpress.com/node/25896/pdf)


DUNDALK, Ireland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 4, 2026-- Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) today announced that Meredith Burns, Vice President of Investor Relations and Sustainability, will present at the 16th Annual East Coast Ideas Conference on June 11, 2026 at 10:35 am ET.


The 35-minute presentation will be webcast and can be accessed through the Cimpress Investor Relations website at[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents&esheet=54546325&newsitemid=20260604447933&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=1&md5=bf8931d5b0b89612f045045ca2e1bee6) , and a replay of the presentation will be available at the same link until June 11, 2027.
