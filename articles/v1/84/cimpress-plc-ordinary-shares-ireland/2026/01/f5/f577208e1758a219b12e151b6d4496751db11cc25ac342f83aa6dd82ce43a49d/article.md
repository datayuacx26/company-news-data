# Cimpress Reports Second Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Results

[PDF Version](https://ir.cimpress.com/node/25681/pdf)


DUNDALK, Ireland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jan. 28, 2026-- Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) has posted on its investor relations website at[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2F&esheet=54405891&newsitemid=20260128684334&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=1&md5=143f9ab1401173488bcf9db0d7cc05d4) its financial results for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026, in a PDF file called "Q2 Fiscal Year 2026 Quarterly Earnings Document," along with an accompanying spreadsheet with historical financial results and operating metrics.


As previously announced, Cimpress will host a public Q&A session tomorrow, Thursday, January 29, 2026 at 8:00 am ET. The live audio event will be accessible on[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents&esheet=54405891&newsitemid=20260128684334&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=2&md5=6fd53f23b1401294f9acc8fb3f1174de) , and a replay will be available at the same link following the call. We will take live questions via chat, and investors may also presubmit questions any time before 10:00 pm ET today by emailingir@cimpress.com .
