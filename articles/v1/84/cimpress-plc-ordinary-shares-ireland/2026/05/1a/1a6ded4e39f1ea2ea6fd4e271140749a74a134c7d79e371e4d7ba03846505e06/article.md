# Cimpress to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences

[PDF Version](https://ir.cimpress.com/node/25816/pdf)


DUNDALK, Ireland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 6, 2026-- Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) announced today that the company is scheduled to participate in the investor conferences detailed below. A live webcast will be available for the event at which the company has a scheduled presentation, and will be available on Cimpress’ investor events page at[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2F&esheet=54529355&newsitemid=20260506300322&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=1&md5=1b85d54ae17d76840ce3eae531924e74) . A replay will be available after the presentation.


**Event: 21st Annual Needham Technology, Media, & Consumer Conference, New York, NY** **Presentation date and time:** Tuesday, May 12, 2026 at 3:45 pm ET (webcast available)
**Speaker:** Meredith Burns, Vice President of Investor Relations & Sustainability


**Event: J.P. Morgan 2026 Global Technology, Media and Communications Conference, Boston, MA** **Date and time:** Monday, May 18, 2026 | 1x1 Meetings
**Attendees:** Robert Keane, Founder, Chairman & Chief Executive Officer and Meredith Burns, Vice President of Investor Relations & Sustainability


**Event: 20th Annual Barrington Research Virtual Spring Investment Conference** **Date and time:** Wednesday, May 27, 2026 | 1x1 Meetings
**Attendees:** Sean Quinn, Executive Vice President & CFO and Investor Relations representatives


**About Cimpress** Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) helps millions of businesses build brands, stand out and grow via customized physical marketing products and branded merchandise. Founded in 1995, Cimpress has become the global leader in web-to-print mass customization, delivering high-quality, affordable customized physical products quickly and conveniently, even in very low quantities. Cimpress brands include VistaPrint, WIRmachenDRUCK, Pixartprinting,[Pens.com](http://pens.com/) , BuildASign, druck.at, Drukwerkdeal, easyflyer, Exaprint, Packstyle, Printi, Tradeprint and BoxUp.
