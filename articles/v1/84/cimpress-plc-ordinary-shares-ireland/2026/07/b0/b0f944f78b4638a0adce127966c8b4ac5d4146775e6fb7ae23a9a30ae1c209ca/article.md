# Cimpress Reports Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Results

[PDF Version](https://ir.cimpress.com/node/25971/pdf)


DUNDALK, Ireland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 29, 2026-- Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) has posted on its investor relations website at[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2F&esheet=54578618&newsitemid=20260729773381&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=1&md5=99cf0ce412060a228f92ef18ae2e8916) its financial results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year 2026, in a PDF file called "Q4 & Fiscal Year 2026 Quarterly Earnings Document", along with an accompanying spreadsheet with historical financial results and operating metrics. The company has also posted its fiscal year 2026 annual letter to investors from Robert Keane, founder, chairman and chief executive officer.


As previously announced, Cimpress will host a public Q&A session tomorrow, Thursday, July 30, 2026 at 8:00 am ET. The live audio event will be accessible on[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents&esheet=54578618&newsitemid=20260729773381&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=2&md5=9a4d717697e9e6855d7078f673aa1210) , and a replay will be available at the same link following the call. Investors may submit live questions on the call via chat, and may also presubmit questions any time before 10:00 pm ET today by emailingir@cimpress.com .


**About Cimpress** Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) helps millions of businesses build brands, stand out, and grow via customized physical marketing products and branded merchandise. Founded in 1995, Cimpress is the global leader in web-to-print mass customization, delivering high-quality, affordable custom products quickly and conveniently—even in low quantities. Cimpress brands include VistaPrint, WIRmachenDRUCK, Pixartprinting, Pens.com, BuildASign, druck.at, Saxoprint, Drukwerkdeal, easyflyer, Exaprint, Packstyle, Printi, Tradeprint and BoxUp.
