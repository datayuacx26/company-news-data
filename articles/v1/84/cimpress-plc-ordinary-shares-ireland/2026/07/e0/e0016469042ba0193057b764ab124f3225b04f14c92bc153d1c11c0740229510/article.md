# Cimpress to Host Public Q&A Call Following Q4 FY2026 Earnings Announcement

[PDF Version](https://ir.cimpress.com/node/25941/pdf)


DUNDALK, Ireland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 16, 2026-- Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) announced that it will host a public Q&A call on Thursday, July 30, 2026 at 8:00 am ET following the release of its Q4 FY2026 **** financial results at 4:05 pm ET on Wednesday, July 29, 2026. The live call will be accessible on[ir.cimpress.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cimpress.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents&esheet=54570576&newsitemid=20260716510364&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.cimpress.com&index=1&md5=0e7ac31fefb79b8ef35844135cbba004) , and a replay will be available at the same link following the call. Investors may submit live questions on the call via chat, and may also presubmit questions any time before 10:00 pm ET on Wednesday, July 29, 2026 by emailingir@cimpress.com .


**About Cimpress**


Cimpress plc (Nasdaq: CMPR) helps millions of businesses build brands, stand out, and grow via customized physical marketing products and branded merchandise. Founded in 1995, Cimpress is the global leader in web-to-print mass customization, delivering high-quality, affordable custom products quickly and conveniently—even in low quantities.
