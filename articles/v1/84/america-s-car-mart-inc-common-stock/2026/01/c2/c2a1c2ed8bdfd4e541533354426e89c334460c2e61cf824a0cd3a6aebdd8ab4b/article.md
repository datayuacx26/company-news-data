# From Bus Ride to Driver’s Seat: Jana and Anthony’s Journey with America’s Car-Mart

**In This Article, You’ll Learn:**


- Why Jana and Anthony keep coming back to America’s Car-Mart after five purchases over 25 years.
- How Car-Mart goes beyond the sale to support customers when it matters most.
- Why Car-Mart’s selection and service are a reliable choice for busy families.
- Why Car-Mart enjoys helping customers and making a difference in their lives.


When Jana Degadoi talks about America’s Car-Mart, she doesn’t sound like a customer. She sounds like family. “I will always go back to Car-Mart,” says Jana. And when you learn her story, it’s easy to understand why.


Over 25 years ago, Jana and her husband, Anthony, were riding the city bus to[shop for used cars](https://www.car-mart.com/cars/) . One day, they passed America’s Car-Mart and spotted a blue 1999 Oldsmobile Royale. They had to stop.


“We fell in love with that car,” Jana smiles. “We took it on so many trips.” They drove that car for years until it reached the end of its road. But their Car-Mart experience stayed with them, and they came back for more.


## A relationship, not a transaction


The Degadoi family has purchased five vehicles from Car-Mart: the Oldsmobile, a Chevy Lumina, a Town & Country van that took them on countless trips to St. Louis, a Ford Edge they still own today, and, most recently, a Chevy Equinox.


Customers Jana and Anthony Degadoi shake hands with General Manager Colten Harris.


What keeps them coming back isn’t just the cars, it’s the people. “The Car-Mart people will talk to you,” Jana explains. “I’ve been to other dealerships where there was no communication. Here, they listen.”


Jana appreciates the teamwork at Car-Mart, especially Sales Associate Sabrina Conrow, who guided her through her recent car purchase. The process was smooth and unhurried.


## A personal touch from America’s Car-Mart


For Jana, the true meaning of Car-Mart became clear during one of her most challenging moments. Hospitalized with COVID, the Car-Mart team helped. “They restructured our loan,” she says. “They went above and beyond to help us the best they could. They worked with us.”


Now, walking into Car-Mart feels like coming home. “When you walk through the door, it’s like visiting family again,” Jana says. “You’re greeted right away. Smiles.”


Customers Jana and Anthony Degadoi enjoy some popcorn while visiting with Colten Harris, General Manager at America’s Car-Mart of Springfield, Mo.


There’s even a routine. Anthony heads for the popcorn (for his wife, he says), grabs a coffee, and settles in. It’s comfortable. Personal. Human.


“What we enjoy most about helping customers like Jana and Anthony is knowing that our work truly makes a difference, not just for individuals, but for the communities we serve,” says Colten Harris, General Manager at Car-Mart of Springfield South. “Helping customers isn’t just part of our jobs; it’s a responsibility we take seriously. When challenges arise, and we’re able to deliver real value, it strengthens our relationships and reinforces Car-Mart’s mission, vision, and values. And it reminds me why we do what we do!”


## Reliable cars for real life


With three kids, outdoor hobbies, and ten acres to manage, reliable transportation matters. They trust the vehicles and the care Car-Mart provides. “Car-Mart goes over the vehicles with a fine-tooth comb,” says Jana. “If I see something and ask, I know it’ll get fixed.”


Even when finances were tight, Car-Mart worked with them. That’s why Jana recommends Car-Mart to friends and family. “Go to Car-Mart and tell them what you need,” she says. “They’ll work with you.”


After five cars and decades of memories, Jana sums it up simply: “It’s a great experience buying from America’s Car-Mart. They don’t rush you out. They get to know you. They ask how you’re doing, how the family is. That’s why we’ll always come back.”
