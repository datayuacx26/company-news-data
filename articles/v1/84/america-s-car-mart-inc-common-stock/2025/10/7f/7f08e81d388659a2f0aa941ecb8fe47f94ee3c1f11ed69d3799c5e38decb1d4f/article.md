# 17 Years of Giving: Our Holiday Toy Drive is Back!

The holidays are here, and that means it’s time for one of our favorite traditions at America’s Car-Mart.


Now in its 17th year, our[Holiday Toy Drive](https://www.car-mart.com/holiday-toy-drive/) continues to bring joy to kids and help families get behind the wheel with a little extra cheer. When customers bring in two new, unwrapped toys (valued at $10 each), they can get into a quality used vehicle for just $499 down* on select vehicles.


##### **A legacy that keeps growing**


It all started in 2009, at a single dealership in Claremore, Oklahoma. Today, it’s a company-wide tradition that brings our associates, customers, and communities together, making a real difference and creating lasting memories for countless children across the communities we call home.


Over the years, we’ve delivered thousands of toys to local hospitals and children’s organizations in the states we serve.


##### **Every America’s Car-Mart is a toy drop-off location**


During November, all[Car-Mart dealerships](https://www.car-mart.com/locations/) will accept toy donations, not just from customers, but from anyone who wants to help.


Drop off your unwrapped toys Monday through Saturday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., and help make the holidays brighter for a child in your community.


##### **One toy can make a difference. One act of kindness can light up a holiday.**


At America’s Car-Mart, giving back isn’t just a holiday tradition—it’s a core value. Our Holiday Toy Drive exemplifies this commitment by supporting both our customers and the communities we serve. A small toy can brighten a child’s day, especially in a hospital, and a low down payment on a vehicle can make a big difference for our customers.


Here’s to 17 years of giving—and many more to come.
