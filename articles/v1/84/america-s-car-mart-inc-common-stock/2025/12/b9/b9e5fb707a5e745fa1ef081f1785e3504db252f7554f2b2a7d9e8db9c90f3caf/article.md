# Five Years Strong: Larry Roach’s Journey with America’s Car-Mart

When you talk to Larry Roach, one thing is clear: he doesn’t stay with a company unless it feels right. For the past five years, America’s Car-Mart of Corinth, Miss., has done exactly that – felt like home.


Larry’s Car-Mart journey began in July 2020 with a trade-in that met his used-car-buying needs at the time. He returned in 2021 and, most recently, in November 2025, for another used-car upgrade, finding a Chevy Silverado 1500 that perfectly matches his lifestyle. Every step of the way, Car-Mart was there to make the financing and purchasing process smooth and straightforward, helping him find the right vehicle without any hassle.


Every vehicle reflects the same reason he stays loyal: he trusts the people and the process. “I like the people, and I like the vehicles,” says Larry.


## Feeling at home every time he walks into America’s Car-Mart


What truly sets Car-Mart apart for Larry is the team. He says, “When you’ve been doing business with a place for so many years, you get to know them, and they get to know you. That makes everything easy.”


The used car-buying process is always smooth and stress-free. “Some companies try to talk you into things,” Larry adds. “But not Car-Mart. I never feel pressured.” That straightforward approach is a significant reason he feels confident referring other car buyers, and he has sent several people to America’s Car-Mart over the years.


## For Larry, the service means as much as the vehicle


Even something as simple as paying in person is a highlight for Larry. “I like dealing with people one-on-one,” says Larry. “Car-Mart makes you feel like you’re at home.”


The Car-Mart team agrees. “Larry is a great customer,” says Traci Underwood, Office Manager. “It’s like your friend coming to visit. We’re proud to have him as a customer.”


A lifelong Chevy fan, Larry values vehicles that are familiar and reliable. His latest Chevy Silverado features updated technology, including a touchscreen that enhances the driving experience.


Even though America’s Car-Mart has grown and evolved, the welcoming environment has remained the same. “Car-Mart still has nice people,” he says. “That’s important. They’re good, honest people.”


Five years with one company is meaningful, and for Larry, it’s his longest business relationship, and he wouldn’t have it any other way. Honesty, dependability, a good environment, quality vehicles, and people who treat him like a friend, not a transaction—these are the factors that keep him coming back.


That’s why Larry Roach chooses America’s Car-Mart. And it’s why Car-Mart is proud to choose him, too.
