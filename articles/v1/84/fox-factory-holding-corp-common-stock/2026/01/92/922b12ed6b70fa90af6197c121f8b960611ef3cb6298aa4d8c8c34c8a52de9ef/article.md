# Black Widow F-150 Lifted vs Lowered: Which is Right for You?

Whether you’re shopping for a custom truck or building one, truck owners end up in one of two camps: lowered or lifted. That’s not to say that some might own a version of both, but typically, you’re more likely to settle for one or the other. This is highly dependent on your use case and preferences. If you do a lot of off-road driving, you’re going to want a vehicle with some clearance to get through rough trails. If you do a lot of city driving, however, you could really go either way.


**At a glance:**


- **Lowered Black Widow F-150**


- Designed for street performance, sharp handling, and an aggressive stance.


- Best for enthusiasts who want a daily driver and a street truck with muscle influences.


- Lower center of gravity boosts responsive cornering.


- **Lifted Black Widow F-150**


- Built for ground clearance, off-road capability, and tough jobs


- Best for drivers who tackle trails, work sites, or challenging conditions.


- Suspension tuned for comfort and capability off-road.


Lucky for you, **Black Widow** trucks offer custom-upfitted truck models with both lowered and lifted suspension kits installed. The


Black Widow F-150


specifically is offered in either a lowered or lifted package, giving you another option to enjoy the best-selling truck in America. Today, we’ll go over the pros and cons of both models and help you make an informed decision on your next vehicle.

## The Lifted Black Widow F-150


### Who It's For


To put it plainly, the lifted Black Widow F-150 is for those who need to do more than just commute. This truck is for truck owners who need to get work done, need to have off-road capability at the trailhead, or frequently navigate unpredictable weather. The **lifted F-150** caters to buyers who are looking for ground clearance but still want to keep a comfortable ride. Whether you pick up the lowered or lifted version, the Black Widow F-150 keeps its OEM towing capability at 10,300 lbs. (about 4672 kg). max towing and 2,025 maximum payload.


Shown: FOX Performance Elite series


### Performance


The **lifted Black Widow F-150** comes standard with either a 6-inch BDS Suspension lift kit or FOX Performance Elite coilovers with FOX rear shocks, 20-inch custom wheels, and 35-inch premium all-terrain tires.
