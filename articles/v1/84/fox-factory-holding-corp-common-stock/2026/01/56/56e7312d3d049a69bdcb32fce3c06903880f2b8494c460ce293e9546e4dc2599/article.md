## 1. Real, Reliable Power Delivery


The Ram Black Widow isn't just a Ram with bigger tires and a loud exhaust. Under the hood, it’s powered by one of the most versatile powertrain lineups Ram has offered in years. For 2026, RAM brought back the


**5.7-liter HEMI® V8**


by popular demand, while continuing to offer the


**twin-turbocharged 3.0-liter Hurricane inline-six,**


giving buyers a choice between traditional muscle and efficiency.


Standard on the Black Widow Ram, the HEMI V8 features that signature V8 sound, paired with eTorque hybrid assist, which


[replaces a conventional alternator with a belt-driven motor generator](https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/ram/press/the-legend-returns-2026-ram-1500-offers-5-7-liter-hemi-v-8-etorque-engine-with-proven-performance-and-capability) to improve responsiveness and efficiency. It also features fuel-saving technology, such as variable camshaft timing and cylinder deactivation.


For drivers who prioritize a more modern approach, the Hurricane inline-six offers strong output with improved efficiency, and uses two low-inertia, high-flow turbochargers to build torque quickly and efficiently across the powerband. RAM says that this design


[allows the engine to deliver strong output](https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/ram/press/new-2025-ram-1500-with-more-powerful-more-fuel-efficient-hurricane-engine-family-unveiled) and towing capability without the constant fuel demands of larger, naturally aspirated engines.


No matter what powertrain you choose, the Black Widow Ram is designed to be fully capable for everyday use. Whether you’re merging on the interstate, towing a trailer on the weekend, or driving between job sites, this upfitted custom truck backs up its promise of capability without compromise.

Pulling together your own lift kit can present its own set of challenges, from wandering steering to a harsh ride and constant adjustments. Like all Fox Factory Vehicles, the Black Widow Ram avoids all of that with a suspension setup that works as a system with


[FOX 2.5 Performance Elite Reservoir](https://bds-suspension.com/collections/fox-2-5-performance-elite-series-shocks) shocks and a


[BDS Suspension 6-inch lift kit.](https://bds-suspension.com/collections/6-to-7-5-inch-lift-kits)


The


**FOX 2.5 Performance Elite Reservoir shocks**
