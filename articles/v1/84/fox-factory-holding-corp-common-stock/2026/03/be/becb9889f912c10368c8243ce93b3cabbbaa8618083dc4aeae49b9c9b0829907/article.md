# Fox Factory Vehicles Wins Booth Award at Barrett-Jackson

The first event of the 2026 Barrett-Jackson car auctions in Scottsdale, Arizona, drew over 310 thousand show patrons and created some new, lucky owners of some classic collector vehicles. Scottsdale has been the home of the


[world-famous car auction since the 1960s](https://www.barrett-jackson.com/company) , founded by Tom Barrett and Russ Jackson. Its first event in 1971 featured 75 vehicles in the auction, compared to today’s show numbers in the hundreds, from classic cars and trucks to modern collector cars.

## Fox Factory Vehicles Win Award for Display at Barrett-Jackson


During the show, Fox Factory Vehicles showcased its latest upfitted vehicles, ranging from the off-road-dominating


[Fox Factory Silverado](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/fox-factory-silverado) to the new


[Black Widow F-150 SR810](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-f150-sr810) street truck. This year, Fox Factory Vehicles received an award from


[Barrett-Jackson](https://www.barrett-jackson.com/)


for excellence in exhibitor design.


This honorary award is a testament to the craftsmanship and dedication that go into our vehicles, our brand, and the customers who support us. We strive for excellence in everything we build, including how we showcase our hard work.
