# Fox Factory Brings the Heat at Petit Le Mans

Last week, the


**Motul Petit Le Mans**


, the final race of the


[IMSA WeatherTech Championship](https://www.imsa.com/news/2025/10/13/check-out-michelin-raceway-road-atlanta-race-results-and-point-standings-here-3/) , descended upon the Road Atlanta Michelin Raceway in Braselton, GA, for an intense week of action and a passionate fanbase. For the past 25+ years, this 10-hour racing event has been a staple for racing enthusiasts in the southeast U.S. with non-stop action. Podium winners for the Road Atlanta 10-Hour event were **Team Cadillac Whelen** piloting a Cadillac V-Series R, **Aston Martin Thor Team** piloting an Aston Martin Valkyrie, and the **Porsche Penske Motorsport team** third piloting a Porsche 963.


During Friday of race week, the


**Fox Factory 120**


took place as a 120-minute race that showcased athletes piloting BMW, Porsche, McLaren, Chevrolet, and more as they fought for the podium. Taking first place in a McLaren Artura GT4 were Moisey Uretsky and Michael Cooper on the


[Ibiza Farm Motorsports](https://www.instagram.com/ibizafarmmotorsports/?hl=en) team. (


[View the IMSA recap here](https://www.imsa.com/news/2025/10/10/ibiza-farm-mclaren-vgrt-cupra-double-up-michelin-pilot-challenge-wins-at-michelin-raceway/) ) Fox Factory, of course, is no stranger to battles for the checkered flag, with athletes and partners actively participating in racing events around the country and the world.


From endurance racing to tearing up the desert of Baja, Fox Factory showed up as it always does, with the most advanced suspension technology on the market and the upfitted vehicles that use it. Built on racing heritage, new and old fans alike turned out to see the Fox Factory vehicles in person.

The equipped


[Ridetech](https://ridetech.com/)


suspension is built to hug mountain roads in a way you wouldn’t expect from an SUV with track-ready performance.

Upgraded components include adjustable shocks and struts, lowering springs, and adjustable front and rear sway bars for a fully dialed driving experience. Whether you plan to track it, drift it, or just drive it to work, the RTR Spec 3 Mustang is ready to show up and show out.

### Outside Van Piggyback


For those who love adventure and the outdoors, Fox Factory showcased the

### The Fox Factory Truck & New Street Car Shocks


Last but certainly not least in the show’s lineup: the Fox Factory Silverado 1500. This Super Truck is the most advanced off-road truck readily available on the market and features a complete off-road-ready lighting package. Pushing 700-HP with a Supercharged 6.2L V8 and 14.5 inches of travel with a dialed-in coilover suspension kit, the Fox Factory Silverado is a force to be reckoned with.


The FOX 3.2 Live Valve coilover shocks on the FFT are designed to soak up anything you throw at it with semi-active, electronically controlled compression with position-sensitive internal bypass technology. To put it in layman’s terms, it makes for a smooth, comfortable ride on off-road terrain with aggressive control and stability.


Outfitted with Baja Kits Long Travel Front Suspension, this truck has a 6” wider overall track width over stock, which improves its capability and stability. Combine all of this with 17“ All-terrain tires with Bead Grip technology, and this Super Truck is ready for all driving conditions. ## Connecting with Fans


This year’s Petit Le Mans was the best event yet at Road Atlanta with a completely sold-out show. The event was filled to the brim to the point of turning potential event goers away, which is a testament to just how fast this event has grown, and how much of an impact it has had on enthusiasts here in the Southeast U.S.


When asked how attendees received the Fox Factory booth, Tyler Porter had this to say, “Our booth was in a prime location inside the fan zone, and we had a steady stream of traffic the entire weekend.” Besides the Outside Van Piggy Back, he also noted that the RTR Mustang and Fox Factory Truck were major attention-getters for attendees.
