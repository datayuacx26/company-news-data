## Black Widow silverado sr


Taking to the streets in 2025 with the other lowered lineup from the Fox Factory family, the **Black Widow Silverado SR** lives for the thrill of the unexpected. To back up its aggressive styling, the lowered Silverado has a lot going for it under the hood with a 420-hp 6.2L V8 with optional Supercharger. Despite its lowered stance, it doesn’t lose its utility by maintaining the Silverado’s maximum towing capacity of 13,300 lbs.


The Silverado SR is equipped with


[Ridetech](https://ridetech.com/)


suspension technology that turns just a basic truck into a high-performance muscle truck. Fully adjustable, the kit on the SR optimizes geometry and allows for fine-tuning, sharper handling, and better ride quality to complement the aggressive look.


Image:[Black Widow Silverado SR](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-silverado-1500-lowered) and[Black Widow Tahoe SR](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-tahoe-lowered) shown


### Black Widow Silverado Lifted


Equipped with the same 6.2L EcoTec3 V8 engine setup as the lowered version, the Black Widow Silverado Lifted truck is ready to tackle any job, big or small. Both the lowered and lifted Silverado are also available with a 3.0L Diesel, 5.3L Gas or 6.2L Gas engine, with gas being the only options available supercharged. In true Black Widow fashion, the lifted Silverado commands attention with its aggressive styling in a way that demands respect.


In terms of suspension, the lifted Silverado is equipped with industry-leading technology from BDS Suspension with a 6-inch lift and FOX 2.0 Series shocks. With a 3-inch cat-back exhaust system, the lifted Silverado has a low, tasteful rumble that won’t annoy your passengers (or yourself), but still puts a smile on your face.
