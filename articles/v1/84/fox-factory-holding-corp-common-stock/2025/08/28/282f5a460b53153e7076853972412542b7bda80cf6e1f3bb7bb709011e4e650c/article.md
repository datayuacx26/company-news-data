# What are Adventure Vans? Meet Outside Van, the Leader in Van Conversions

If you want to enjoy the outdoors without sacrificing comfort,


**adventure vans**


are a step up from sleeping on the ground. Imagine watching the sunrise with a cup of coffee while your kids sleep soundly in their bunks, all from a beautiful spot only a nimble van can reach.


These versatile vans are built to last with enough off-road capability to get you and your gear to places others can’t. With smart layouts that emphasize utility and functionality, you can have peace of mind going off-grid and have a seamless experience in the great outdoors.


Whether you’re a family driving cross-country, a solo adventurer chasing trailheads, a professional working remotely, or planning a long-term escape into the wild, an adventure van gives you the flexibility to explore on your terms.


## What separates Adventure Vans from a Traditional RV or DIY Camper Van Build?


Traditionally, an RV is built by adding a large box onto a modified truck or van chassis. These boxes are typically built with lightweight materials and contain all the living facilities that make up the RV. An adventure van, on the other hand, contains living spaces built into the existing structure of the chassis, like an **Outside Van.**


This translates to a much more compact and nimble vehicle that offers all the same luxuries as a full-sized RV, but allows for a performance-driven and stealthy experience, ideal for full off-grid camping or urban boondocking. The base chassis, whether it’s a Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, RAM Promaster, or Ford Transit, is a common commercial vehicle that is perfectly suited to navigate tight city streets, just like any delivery van. It also adds off-road performance by keeping the entire vehicle compact and in spec with the factory wheelbase.


Compared to RVs, **Sprinter** conversions often offer better fuel efficiency, with most vans achieving between 21 and 24 mpg on the highway. Ownership costs can be lower with off-grid solar, which means fewer campground fees, no hookups, and less time at dump stations.


(Image: Rooftop solar panel featured on the Outside Van Approach)


The DIY conversion van route might sound tempting, but there’s far more involved than the **van-lifers** on Instagram make it out to be. We’ve all watched a few reels and felt convinced we could ’totally’ do a bus conversion and live in it, but what if you didn’t have to? Full-scale van and bus conversions require specialized tools, skilled hands in plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and finish carpentry that may be more than the average person is willing to tackle.


That’s why many adventurers, remote workers, families, and thrill-seekers choose pre-built camper vans like those from


Outside Van


. These vans are expertly crafted, feature flexible layouts, and are ready for off-grid travel, allowing you to focus solely on your next destination.


## Meet Outside Van


Before #VanLife became a trend, the founder of Outside Van had already been building vans for his own adventures. What started as a hobby evolved into a business in 2007, focusing on crafting unique camper vans that are both comfortable and versatile.


In 2021, Outside Van was acquired by


**Fox Factory**


, aligning craftsmanship with the high-quality engineering and suspension systems FOX is known for.


Today, Outside Van is recognized as a leader in van conversions, producing over 100 fully built vans each year.


**Justin Anderson**


**,** Brand Manager at Outside Van, says, “We have been building Sprinter adventure vans for over 18 years (before Instagram and #vanlife were even a thing!) and were basically just doing our thing because it worked, not what the industry wanted us to do.”


He adds, “By being independent from the RV world in our beginnings, we were able to develop, refine, and build processes that are unmatched by any other RV manufacturer. We design our builds to last, because our customers are high-action people and we expect the vans to be used in high-action environments.”


From their headquarters in Oregon, a dedicated team of designers, fabricators, and craftspeople work together to hand-build each van from the inside out. Most of the components are designed and produced in-house, giving the team total control over quality, fit, and finish.


### Who should buy an Outside Van?


Outside Van makes vans for everyone, not just action sports people and adrenaline junkies. “Our goal as a brand is to enable a lifestyle of joy and connection with the outdoors,” Anderson explained. “There is so much to explore off the beaten path in this country, and our vans are one of the best ways to experience everything this land has to offer.”


Whether you’re traveling with your kids for summer, downsizing, or need a content-creation base, there’s a van to help you “live beyond.”

## What makes Outside Van different?


There are plenty of van conversion companies out there, but few offer the same level of quality, customization, and thoughtful engineering as Outside Van.


“The build process for an Outside Van is something very unique in the industry,” Anderson explained. “We have spent years developing what we call an infrastructure kit. This is basically the foundation for our builds. We take an empty van and then install strategically placed galvanized steel plating which everything is our build is then built off. Most other RV manufacturers and DIY builders do NOT do this.”


Each build starts with a Mercedes-Benz Sprinter chassis, chosen for its superior performance and ability to support advanced off-grid upgrades. These vans are tailored to your gear, group, and lifestyle, maximizing utility and durability.


The interior is crafted from top-quality materials, designed to withstand heavy use, wet gear, and muddy boots. The custom electrical and plumbing systems are engineered for true off-grid living, and you can add options like a TV or WiFi boosters if you need to check in with civilization. Every detail is carefully selected for long-lasting comfort, even in the most extreme conditions.


Anderson adds, “Most RV manufacturers build for speed and volume, and what happens is their builds end up falling apart after being on the road and tossed into the conditions we expect our customers to be in. Our builds do not fall apart, they don’t rattle or squeak, and they will last a very long time in the harshest conditions.”
