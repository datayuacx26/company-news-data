# LA Auto Show 2025: Ram DC650, Commando, F-150 SR810 and More

The Annual Los Angeles Auto Show is a real “who’s who” of the automotive industry. From new model unveilings to creative influencer awards, the show was a representation of the heaviest hitters and names.


**Fox Factory Vehicles**


, along with


[Outside Van](https://outsidevan.com/)


, both had booth setups at the LA Auto Show showcasing the latest offerings from the family of upfitter brands. This show is an important visit each year for overall brand awareness, but most of all, product education and consumer engagement. We’ll cover the featured models from the show, and the reactions of the show attendees.

## Fox Factory Booth at LA Auto Show 2025


At the entrance of South Hall, the Fox Factory family of vehicles stood out on red carpet that drew consumer focus both entering and exiting the show. The model lineup showed the full breadth of capability and relationships between FOX and manufacturers, highlighting the diverse skillset and knowledge of the company’s engineering team. Three of the latest upfitted vehicles were real attention-grabbers:


**the lowered**


[Ram DC650](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-ram-1500-lowered)


**, lowered**


[F-150 SR810](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-f150-sr810)


**and**


[Jeep Commando .](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/jeep-commando)


Shown: Black Widow F-150 SR810 Single Cab


During the show, the


F-150 SR810


was a one-of-one concept vehicle as a single cab, lowered F-150 with 810-horsepower. Going to full production estimated Q1 2026, show-goers ogled over the details of the build in anticipation.


Keeping the Stellantis family together, the buzz on the left side of the booth stayed centered on the new Jeep Commando, and lowered


Ram DC650


builds. Released on Veteran’s Day 2025, the Jeep Commando has applications pouring in for the build available only to current and veteran service members.


A few of those applicants stopped by the booth to see the Commando in person, anticipating delivery.


## Vehicles Featured


### Outside Van Adventure Vans


Down the west hall, the Outside Van booth was a hive of activity. Working together with OVR Magazine, Dometic, Front Runner, and Jeep, the “OVR Outpost” was an overlanding-focused section of the **2025** **LA Auto Show.**


Complete with a central camp-out area equipped with camp chairs, faux fire pits, and a special selection of curated vehicles, the Outside Van area created a transformative experience that not only encouraged visitors to experience the vans firsthand but also educated and inspired them to become more involved in camping and the outdoors


During the LA Auto show’s influencer event, the OSV booth served as a final destination for a scavenger hunt with participants from social media influencers to media contributors with snacks and takeaways.


## Moving Forward


The energy at this year’s LA Auto Show confirmed what we already know: the appetite for purpose-built, premium, performance-driven vehicles is only growing. From lowered trucks to adventure-ready vans, Fox Factory’s presence highlighted just how diverse and capable the family of upfitted brands has become.


The conversations, reactions, and connections made throughout the week will shape where we go next, and we’re already looking ahead to what’s coming in 2026.
