# First Look: Meet the 2025 Black Widow Tahoe SR

Some premium SUVs try to blend in, but the new


[2025 Black Widow Tahoe SR](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-tahoe-lowered) was built to stand out. Introducing a lowered, factory-built custom SUV with the attitude of a muscle car, but the refinement of a luxury flagship SUV. Whether you’re fighting the concrete jungle or showing out in the parking lot, the Tahoe SR delivers a striking presence that’s backed by Fox Factory engineering.


With a choice of 5.3L or 6.2L V8 engines, an optional


Whipple supercharger


upgrade, and exclusive performance coilover suspension, this is more than your average grocery-getter.


This performance machine was built with purpose and meticulously engineered using proprietary components that are fully validated for performance, reliability, and warranty support. This platform was made for those who demand more from their premium SUV.


## The Black Widow Tahoe SR Demands Attention


The heart of the


[Tahoe SR](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-tahoe-lowered) ’s street performance is its


[Ridetech](https://ridetech.com/)


coilover suspension with custom-tuned FOX shocks. The lowered stance enhances ride quality while delivering sharper handling that leaves you confident around any corner. Developed with proprietary suspension components and track-ready tuning, the


Black Widow Tahoe SR


delivers a planted feel that’s just as good behind the wheel as it looks at a stoplight.


Complimenting the suspension are massive 24-inch alloy wheels, available in Gloss Black, Satin Black, or Matte Gunmetal. Drivers can personalize the look further with matte, gloss, or premium black hi-hat paint options. To finish the look, the Black Widow signature badging and windshield banner tells anyone in-the-know that this is more than a dealership trim package.


## On-Demand Power in an SUV


Whether you choose the 5.3L or 6.2L V8, the Tahoe SR comes ready to perform. Both engine options are compatible with an available


**Whipple supercharger**


upgrade, delivering 600 horsepower with the 5.3L and up to 675 horsepower with the 6.2L.


Paired with a


**Borla stainless steel cat-back dual exhaust system**


with carbon fiber tips, the Tahoe SR delivers a deep, aggressive tone worthy of its performance pedigree.


*(* *Note:*


*The Whipple Supercharger option is not available in all states. Check with your local dealer for available options for your Tahoe SR build.)*


## Custom Interior Feel


Inside, the Tahoe SR adds refinement to an already premium cabin. Standard upgrades include 17.7” screen infotainment system, red LED interior lighting, all-weather floor mats, 50-state legal window tint, and custom


[Black Widow-stitched headrests](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-tahoe-lowered) . For drivers who want even more, opt for the available diamond-quilted leather seats with suede inserts for an elevated look.


This interior screams custom but maintains the OEM comfort for everyday use.

The 2025 Black Widow Tahoe SR redefines what a[custom Tahoe](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/overview-tahoe-lowered) should be. Built to handle winding roads and conquer the concrete jungle, this is an SUV for drivers who want more.
