# F-150 Black Widow Review: The Ultimate Lifted Ford Truck

Looking for a lifted Ford F-150 that delivers bold style and off-road capability? Meet the[Black Widow F-150](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/vehicles-f150-features-black-widow) **,** a custom lifted truck that blends brawn with luxury. Aggressive styling, vibrant accent colors, and that signature “Widow” spider logo create a look that’s unapologetic yet refined. Combine that with the dependability of the Ford F-150, and you have a truck built to turn heads downtown and tackle challenging terrain.


In this review, we’ll dive into the specs, design details, and standout features that make the Black Widow F-150 a class-defining lifted truck.


## What makes the Black Widow F-150 special?


This truck is the epitome of the phrase “go big or go home.” Under the hood, the 5.0L Coyote V8 puts out a solid 400-hp and 410 lb-ft of torque that’s more than enough to back up its aggressive looks. Paired with a tuned cat-back exhaust system, you get all of the rumble you want without the annoying drone in the cabin.


Inside, the **Black Widow Lifted F-150** takes OEM up a notch. Stitched headrests and floor mats paired with the SpiderGlow lighting package brings a luxurious red glow to the cabin. Optional upgrades like custom leather, suede seats, and powered side steps add even more comfort and convenience. To build on the “standard” Black Widow package, the Black Widow Limited package is available as well. With this sinister upgrade, you get our exclusive Matte Metallic Gray custom paint, red painted accents, upgraded FOX suspension and individually serialized badging.


## Built for Adventure (and the daily drive)


The elevated design, luxurious interior feel and advanced performance options make this lifted truck not only a clean daily driver but excellent for off-road exploration. Tow your boat or[UTV](https://upfitutv.com/) with confidence with up to 13,500 lbs. towing capacity and 2,445 lbs. payload.


Both the Spider and Limited packages include a[BDS suspension lift kit](https://bds-suspension.com/collections/lift-kits) , FOX shocks, Black Widow wheels and all-terrain tires. On the Limited package, you’ll step up to FOX Performance Elite Reservoir shocks, and 22-inch wheels wrapped in 35-inch all-terrain tires (versus 20-inch wheels on the Spider).


Looking for more muscle? You have the option to add-on a Whipple Supercharger that’ll take your lifted truck over 700-hp.


## A Truck that turns heads


The bottom-line is this truck screams exclusive, from a custom Black Widow grille to color-matched fender flares. Choose between aggressive wheel-to-wheel side steps or sleek power retractable running boards to match your style.


(Image: Black Widow Limited Package with retractable running boards.)


### Premium Suspension Technology


The Black Widow F-150 Limited edition steps up its game with[FOX Performance Elite Reservoir shocks](https://bds-suspension.com/products/fox-2-5-coil-over-shocks-w-dsc-reservoir-adjuster-6-inch-lift-performance-elite-series-ford-f150-15-20-4wd) in the rear. These race-proven coilovers are designed to work in conjunction with the included BDS lift kit to deliver superior ride quality.


(Image: FOX Performance Elite coilovers shown on the F-150 Black Widow Limited Package.)


The **DSC (Dual-Speed Compression) adjusters** on the Performance Elite shocks offers precision tuning with 10 low-speed and 12 high-speed compression settings that are managed via a sleek black anodized aluminum reservoir for efficient suspension cooling.


Interested in knowing more about the Dual-Speed Compression (DSC) technology and how it works?[Check out this article.](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/blogs/gear-and-grit/dual-speed-compression-what-is-hsc-and-lsc)


## Why choose the lifted black widow f-150?


Starting at $84,595 for the **Spider** edition and $104,595 for the **Limited** , you can expect luxury in an off-road lifted truck without the fluff. To make it even sweeter, Black Widow trucks retain the full factory warranty, covered for 3 years/36,000 miles with easy financing at the dealership of your choice. This truck is for real enthusiasts who demand more than the status quo, but quality parts to back it up the looks.


With a balance of premium practicality with a slice of indulgence, the lifted Black Widow F-150 stands in a league of its own.
