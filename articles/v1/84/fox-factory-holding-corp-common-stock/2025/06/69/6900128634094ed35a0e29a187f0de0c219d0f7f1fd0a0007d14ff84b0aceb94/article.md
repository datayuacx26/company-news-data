The **AGwagon Super Duty** was built for hard-working farmers, ranchers, and rural business owners who need a vehicle that delivers reliability and versatility on and off-road.


Developed with feedback from people who work the land every day, this truck is ready for transporting livestock, hauling equipment and other unique challenges that come with modern agriculture.

## Built for Farm-Use


While most heavy-duty trucks cater to the average person, the AGwagon pushes beyond average to make life easier. Practical features like an onboard air system with load-leveling air bags and 360-degree LED lighting, make light work of long days and unexpected challenges. The Molle bed panels offer versatile storage options for tools, feed, and supplies, while the onboard air compressor can power tools and top off tire pressure right there in the field or job site.


Under the hood, the AGwagon Super Duty offers a choice between a 6.7L High Output V8 Turbo Diesel and a 7.3L Gas Engine, paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission. While you’re plowing fields, pulling trailers, or navigating a bumpy pasture, the equipped


[FOX Performance Elite Shocks](https://bds-suspension.com/collections/fox-2-5-performance-elite-series-shocks) provides additional support when hauling heavy loads. With a towing capacity of up to 27,000 lbs. and a payload capacity of 3,643 lbs., this truck doesn’t just meet expectations; it exceeds them.
