## Fox Factory Truck Gets a Major update


The Fox Factory Silverado got a major update in 2025 with custom color options, lighting configurations and single or dual tire carriers. Building off FOX’s racing heritage, the FFT is equipped with **FOX 3.2 Live Valve equipped shocks** , Baja Kits long travel suspension with 14.5”/15.5” of travel on 37-inch BF Goodrich off-road tires.


Not only is this truck built to perform, but its bold design, functional accessories and luxury interior are made for those who want to have fun but ride comfortably.
