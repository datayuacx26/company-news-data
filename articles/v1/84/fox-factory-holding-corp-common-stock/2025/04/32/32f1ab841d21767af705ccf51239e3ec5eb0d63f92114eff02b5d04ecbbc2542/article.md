# 2025 Fox Factory Truck Redefines Custom

When it comes to dominance in the truck world, there’s no playing it safe. With the[2025 Fox Factory Truck](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/fox-factory-silverado) , Fox Factory Inc. delivers exactly what off-roaders, truck enthusiasts, and adrenaline junkies crave. They don’t just want a vehicle, but a bold statement of power, performance, and their own individuality.


In 2024, Fox Factory Inc. introduced a groundbreaking super truck based on the Chevrolet Silverado 1500 4WD platform. Created in collaboration with industry leaders and Fox Factory’s Power Vehicle Division, the 2025 FFT builds on customer feedback to deliver a more refined and customizable design than ever before.


Wondering what’s new? We’re about to take you through why this updated beast earns the title of the most customizable, premium super truck on the market.


## More Than a Truck, It’s a Statement


The 2025 Fox Factory Truck comes loaded with features built for adventure. Premium comes standard starting with a Chevrolet’s Silverado LTZ trim, this model combines comfort with durability designed for tough conditions. It features heated and ventilated power seats, upgraded tech, and a refined driver interface.


The Fox Factory Silverado goes further to offer new levels of personalization and performance upgrades to make it truly your own.


## Power Meets Precision Engineering


Under the hood, the 2025 Fox Factory Silverado features a **[supercharged 6.2L V8 engine](https://vehicles.ridefox.com/pages/fox-factory-silverado-details?section=performance) **** pushing 700 HP and 655 lb-ft of torque. Pair that power with the longest travel suspension system and largest semi-active shocks of any product truck, and you’ve got a truck that effortlessly eats up dunes, mud, and gravel.


If you’re here to dominate the dirt, this could be your weapon of choice.


## Customization That Lets Your Truck Stand Out


Why blend in when you could own the spotlight? The 2025 Fox Factory Truck raises the bar on customization, letting you tweak every detail of your ride to match your style and performance needs.


**Choose from three tiers of bold exterior color options:**


- **Standard Colors (Included)** : In addition to standard Black, the FFT is now available in these OEM GM colors: Summit White, Iridescent Pearl Tricoat, Sterling Gray Metallic, Slate Gray Metallic, Cypress Gray, Riptide Blue Metallic and Black. Additionally, an optional Matte Clear is offered over the OEM colors at no additional charge.
- **Premium Colors (Upgrade):** Want to stand out even further? Choose between these colors options: Iconic Silver (Matte), F8 Green (Matte), Mojave Sand (Matte), M9A Crayon Gray (Gloss), Anvil Gray (Gloss), and Orange Crush (Gloss).
- **Custom Options:** Go beyond conventional with the paint-to-sample program to bring your dream color to life. If you can imagine it, Fox Factory can make it happen.


### Accents and Next-Level Lighting


Choose between orange or black accents, paired with carbon fiber reveals over the hood and bedsides. Whether you prefer bold and high-contrast, or subtle, refined style, the 2025 FFT offers a perfect balance of both.


Late-night off-roading also just got an upgrade. With the signature 8-pod Nacho LED lights, plus optional 12- or 14-pod configurations and bed rack lighting, this beast lights the path to wherever your ambition takes you.


### Spare Tires on Lock


Go all in with single or dual vertical tire carriers for added convenience in remote locations. Whether you’re hauling extra tools or fuel, these versatile options ensure you stay prepared and unstoppable.


## Confidence Comes Standard


The 2025 FOX Factory Truck is supported by a 3-year / 36,000-mile warranty and built with the same obsessive attention to detail that defines FOX Factory engineering.
