# Ambient.ai Partners with Convergint to Expand Global Adoption of AI-Powered Security

## San Jose, CA (Apr. 30, 2024)


Ambient.ai, the AI-powered computer vision intelligence (CVI) company transforming the physical security industry, has announced a partnership with Convergint, a leader in service-based systems integration. This partnership unites Ambient.ai’s proactive, automated solutions and Convergint’s expertise in integrated security systems to deliver AI-powered security to a broader global customer base.


Through this collaboration, Ambient.ai and Convergint advance their shared mission to strengthen physical security postures by empowering organizations across the globe with innovative AI technology. Ambient.ai and Convergint’s commitment to safety and security ensures that customers receive the most effective solutions for reducing risk and enhancing security measures.


“As someone who has witnessed the visionary approach of Convergint throughout my career, I’ve seen firsthand their commitment to pioneering solutions that anticipate tomorrow’s challenges,” said Colby Mulry, Director of Channel Sales at Ambient.ai. “Aligned with our shared mission to safeguard against every security threat imaginable, this new partnership is poised to redefine the landscape of safety and innovation. We here at Ambient could not be more excited to bring the future of AI to our customers together.”


Trusted by leading organizations within tech, energy, manufacturing, and more — including Adobe, The Harker School, and SentinelOne — Ambient.ai transforms security operations by preventing security incidents before they happen. The platform applies AI and computer vision intelligence to existing sensors and cameras to monitor 100% of cameras 24/7, reduce false access control alarms by 95%+, speed up forensic investigations by 20x, and provide real-time site intelligence and security performance KPIs.


“Partnering with Ambient.ai signifies our commitment to embedding state-of-the-art AI technology into the security solutions we develop for our clients,” said Joe Young, Vice President of Global Partner Development & Strategic Alliances at Convergint. “This collaboration boosts our capacity to meet the dynamic security demands of our worldwide customers and underscores our dedication to pioneering innovation within the industry.”


Convergint has solidified itself as a leader in the physical security industry with over 10,000 colleagues and more than 220 locations globally. With a mission of being its customers’ best service provider, and a culture of service and innovation, Convergint provides companies with cutting-edge security solutions.


## About Ambient.ai


Founded in 2017 by CEO Shikhar Shrestha and CTO Vikesh Khanna, Ambient.ai is a unified, AI-powered physical security platform that helps enterprise organizations reduce risk, improve security operational efficiency, and gain critical insights. Seven of the top 10 U.S. technology companies, along with multiple Fortune 500 organizations across a variety of industries, leverage Ambient.ai to unify their security infrastructure and significantly enhance their security posture. For more information, please visit[ambient.ai](https://ambient.ai/) .


## About Convergint


Convergint is a $2.5 billion USD global, industry-leading systems integrator that designs, installs, and services electronic security, cybersecurity, fire and life safety, building automation, and audio-visual systems. Listed as the #1 systems integrator in SDM Magazine’s Top Systems Integrators Report for the past 6 years, Convergint leads with over 10,000 colleagues and more than 220 locations worldwide.
