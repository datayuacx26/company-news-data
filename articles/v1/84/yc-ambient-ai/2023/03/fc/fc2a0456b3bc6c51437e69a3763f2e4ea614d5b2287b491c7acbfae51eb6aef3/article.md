# Ambient.ai Receives LenelS2 Factory Certification Under the LenelS2 OpenAccess Alliance Program

## San Jose, CA, March 23, 2023


‍[Ambient.ai](https://www.ambient.ai/) , the computer vision intelligence company transforming physical security, today announced that it has received LenelS2 factory certification and joined the[LenelS2](https://www.lenels2.com/) OpenAccess Alliance Program (OAAP). Ambient.ai’s Signals Intelligence solution now interfaces with the OnGuard® access control system to help operators prioritize incoming alarms and events with the help of Ambient.ai’s AI Context Graph. This allows operators to quickly focus on the most crucial security events while significantly reducing the time spent on non-actionable and nuisance alerts.


“Ambient.ai has completed required factory testing at LenelS2 to validate the functionality of its interface to the OnGuard system. The Ambient.ai interface complements the OnGuard system’s built-in, real-time alarm monitoring interface to provide end users with AI-contextualized alerts and enhanced unauthorized access detection,” said John Marchioli, OAAP product management at LenelS2. “We look forward to Ambient.ai’s continued involvement in the LenelS2 OpenAccess Alliance Program.”


“More than ever, security teams are demanding solutions that allow them to focus on important alerts and threats, while minimizing noise and false alarms,” said Shikhar Shrestha, co-founder and CEO of Ambient.ai. “We are excited that the combined Ambient.ai and LenelS2 solutions now allow users to prioritize critical alerts and focus on taking preventative action against legitimate threats.”


Ambient.ai’s Computer Vision Intelligence Solution has recently been recognized by[SecurityInfoWatch as Top Video Surveillance Software in 2022](https://www.ambient.ai/2022/11/08/ambient-ai-named-top-video-surveillance-software-in-inaugural-securityinfowatch-readers-choice-awards/) and by[Fast Company on its 2022 List of the Next Big Things in Tech](https://www.ambient.ai/2022/11/17/ambient-ai-named-to-fast-companys-next-big-things-in-tech/) . To learn more about how Ambient.ai’s solutions are empowering security teams, schedule a demo[here](https://www.ambient.ai/get-demo/) – or visit Booth #21125 at the ISC West Security Conference in Las Vegas next week from March 29-31.
