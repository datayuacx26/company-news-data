Launched in late 2024, Google’s[Health AI Developer Foundations (HAI-DEF)](https://research.google/blog/helping-everyone-build-ai-for-healthcare-applications-with-open-foundation-models/) program was created to provide global developers with open weight models, subject to the[HAI-DEF Terms of Use](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/terms) , to solve complex healthcare challenges. Following the addition of[MedGemma](https://research.google/blog/google-research-at-google-io-2025/) , Google's most capable open model for Health AI development, we introduced[MedGemma 1.5](https://research.google/blog/next-generation-medical-image-interpretation-with-medgemma-15-and-medical-speech-to-text-with-medasr/) this past January and launched the[MedGemma Impact Challenge](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/overview) in collaboration with Kaggle.


The entries from over 850 teams demonstrate HAI-DEF’s potential to transform global health. We saw developers tackle diverse challenges that previously would have required much more resource intensive, ground-up development. Today, we are thrilled to announce the winners.


## 1st place: EpiCast


[EpiCast](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/new-writeup-1770830823676) is a mobile-first demo solution built to bridge a critical gap within the Economic Community of West African States. By using a fine-tuned[MedGemma](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medgemma) model alongside[MedSigLIP](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medsiglip) and[HeAR](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/hear) , the system enables community health workers to transform unstructured clinical observations in local languages into structured[WHO](https://www.who.int/) Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR) signals, facilitating the early identification of disease outbreaks.


## 2nd place: Sunny


[Sunny](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/sunny-private-skin-health-tracker) is a mobile-first demo designed to help users self-examine and track skin changes for potential signs of cancer. Using a fine-tuned[MedGemma](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medgemma) model, it generates structured reports from skin photographs while preserving user privacy.


## 3rd place: FieldScreen AI


Designed for resource-limited settings,[FieldScreen AI](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/fieldscreen-ai) demonstrates a novel AI-based tuberculosis screening workflow for community health workers. It uses a fine-tuned[MedGemma](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medgemma) to analyze chest X-rays and a classifier built based on the[HeAR](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/hear) model to detect signs of TB in cough audio. The system runs entirely on-device, using[MedASR](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medasr) for voice input and[TranslateGemma](https://huggingface.co/collections/google/translategemma) for local language output.


## 4th place: Tracer


[Tracer](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/new-writeup-1771923230850) demonstrates an AI-driven workflow using[MedGemma](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medgemma) to help prevent medical errors. By extracting hypotheses from physician notes and reconciling them against incoming test results, the model assigns confidence scores to flag potential discrepancies or incomplete tests for immediate human review.


## Special technology winners


These awards honor three specialized themes: agentic workflow improvement, fine-tuning for novel tasks and edge-AI solutions for on-device deployment.


Together, they celebrate teams demonstrating the versatility of HAI-DEF models in driving efficiency, expanding capabilities, and delivering real-world impact.


## The Novel Task Prize 1: ClinicDx


[ClinicDX](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/new-writeup-1771522062957) , an integrated clinical AI demo, uses a custom fine-tuned[MedGemma](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medgemma) model to bring advanced diagnostics directly into[OpenMRS](https://openmrs.org/) for health centers in sub-Saharan Africa. It operates entirely offline, querying over 160+[WHO](https://www.who.int/) and[MSF](https://www.msf.org/) guidelines to inform its outputs.


## The Novel Task Prize 2: UniRad3s


[UniRad3s](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/UniRad3s-reimagining-radiology-with-spot-segment) is a demo that uses a fine-tuned[MedGemma](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/medgemma) model and incorporates[MedSAM2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.03600) to streamline radiology workflows into three pillars: "Spot" (anomalies), "Segment" (3D lesions) and "Simplify" (patient-friendly automated reporting).


## The Edge AI Prize: BridgeDx


[BridgeDX](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/new-writeup-1771945306600) , an offline decision-support demo, is inspired by the healthcare gaps witnessed during the 2015 Nepal earthquake. It anchors its reasoning in guidelines from the[WHO](https://www.who.int/) and[MSF](https://medicalguidelines.msf.org/) and the[Orphanet rare disease database](https://www.orpha.net/) to support community health workers and first responders.


## Agentic Workflow Prize 1: CaseTwin


[CaseTwin](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/new-writeup-1771703368723) is a clinical decision-support demo that matches acute chest X-rays with historical "twins" and uses an agentic workflow to accelerate referrals, turning an hours-long manual retrieval process into a few minutes in rural hospitals.


## Agentic Workflow Prize 2: BigTB6


[BigTB6](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/bigtb6) is a voice-driven screening demo designed for tuberculosis and anemia screening. It identifies critical biomarkers through cough analysis, chest X-ray and physical pallor assessment to support triage in resource-constrained environments.


## Honorable mentions

- [Sentinel](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/med-gemma-impact-challenge/writeups/new-writeup-1770655870541) is an on-device mental health monitoring demo for veterans between clinical visits.
