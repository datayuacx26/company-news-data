# Experience the legacy of Estadio Azteca on Google Earth.

# Experience the legacy of Estadio Azteca on Google Earth.


Sebastián Hiernaux


Strategic Partnerships Development Manager


Jul 16, 2026


---


Mexico City's Estadio Azteca made soccer history last month during the tournament opener between Mexico and South Africa. In hosting this match, it became the first stadium in the world to host games in three global soccer showdowns: 1970, 1986 and 2026.


And now you can[experience the storied arena without being in the stands](https://earth.google.com/web/data=MkEKPwo9CiExZzlWWnV3VktUUnNGUmx5d19QS0pPd2VmQ0gtOVJXRk4SFgoUMEQ1NkEzNTlCMjQwQTEzNEJFMTMgAUICCABKBwj29rAPEAE?hl=en_US&authuser=0&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=keyword&utm_campaign=estadio_azteca&utm_content=) .


Through a Google Earth and Fundación ICA collaboration, you can get an up-close look at Estadio Azteca through an interactive digital canvas and imagery dating to the 1960s.


From its origins in 1961 to the clashes that crowned legends like Pelé and Maradona, the stadium has been home to six decades of sporting history that are now yours to explore. Experience the full story and[take the field on Google Earth.](https://earth.google.com/web/data=MkEKPwo9CiExZzlWWnV3VktUUnNGUmx5d19QS0pPd2VmQ0gtOVJXRk4SFgoUMEQ1NkEzNTlCMjQwQTEzNEJFMTMgAUICCABKBwj29rAPEAE?hl=en_US&authuser=0&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=keyword&utm_campaign=estadio_azteca&utm_content=)
