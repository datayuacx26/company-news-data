# Switch to Android easily — and bring your data with you.

# Switch to Android easily — and bring your data with you.


Jul 22, 2026


---


Switching to Android just got even easier. With the launch of Samsung’s new Z Series at today’s[Galaxy Unpacked](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/galaxy-unpacked-2026) , we introduced a new native migration experience built directly into Android 17 that lets you transfer even more data types from an iPhone wirelessly — without downloading a separate app.


With the upgraded method, you can easily bring over what matters most. This includes your photos, videos, contacts, messages, and calendar — plus, newly supported data, such as your Google Account, passwords, Wi-Fi credentials, and even your eSIM.


This upgrade has already started rolling out to select Pixel devices. It will also be available on the new Samsung Galaxy Z Flip8 and Z Fold8 series devices and come to more Android devices soon.


Visit[android.com/switch](http://android.com/switch) for more switching to Android tips.
