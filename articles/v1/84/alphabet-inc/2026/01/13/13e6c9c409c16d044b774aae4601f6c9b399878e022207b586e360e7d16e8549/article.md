# Android XR lights up Sphere in Las Vegas for CES.

# Android XR lights up Sphere in Las Vegas for CES.


Menaka Shroff


VP of Marketing


Jan 05, 2026


---


Since introducing Android XR, our operating system for next generation headsets and glasses, we’ve begun moving from vision to reality. Samsung recently launched the[Galaxy XR](https://blog.google/products/android/samsung-galaxy-xr/) and we just previewed upcoming devices on[The Android Show | XR Edition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oy_ayfHcVWA) .


Combining Gemini with an awareness of your surroundings, Android XR brings you new ways to experience apps. Your AI assistant provides real-time help, whether you're building a virtual workspace or diving into a game.


Today, we’re bringing Android XR to the Las Vegas skyline by turning the outside of Sphere into an immersive portal of imagination.[Watch](https://www.instagram.com/p/DTIu7TCCV8u/) as our Android bot discovers what’s possible with an Android XR headset: soaring through space, painting mid-air, getting help while gaming and more. It’s a larger-than-life look at how Gemini transforms the way you watch, explore and create in XR.


Check out[The Android Show | XR Edition](https://youtu.be/oy_ayfHcVWA?si=z1FNBIEW28gUywfl) to learn more and see what’s coming.
