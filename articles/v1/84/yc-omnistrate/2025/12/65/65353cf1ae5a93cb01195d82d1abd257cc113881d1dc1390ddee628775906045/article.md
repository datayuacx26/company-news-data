# The Challenge


## Optimizing Distribution for Speed and Scale


DataRobot powers AI for the world's most demanding enterprises. To serve these customers, they must deploy software anywhere:[On-premises](https://omnistrate.com/blog/why-customers-choose-omnistrate-a-deep-dive-into-the-byoc-landscape) , in customer VPCs (BYOC), or via Multi-Cloud Single Tenant SaaS (STS).


To execute this distribution strategy, DataRobot needed to solve two distinct infrastructure hurdles without slowing down their core product innovation:


1. High-Friction Delivery for On-Prem


For their established private cloud business, the strategic priority was velocity and efficiency.


Deployment Velocity


Deploying into complex enterprise environments was a high-touch process taking up to 3 months. To accelerate revenue and customer success, DataRobot needed to drastically compress this timeline.


Support Efficiency


As they scaled, high-touch support for manual upgrades and troubleshooting took time away from high-value work.


2. Inefficient SaaS Scaling


Separately, DataRobot sought to launch a managed Single-Tenant SaaS (STS). However, because STS requires a dedicated environment for every customer, manual provisioning created a massive bottleneck.


The Provisioning Trap


Without automation, spinning up a new single-tenant instance was as labor-intensive as an on-prem deployment. This limited how fast they could onboard new SaaS customers.


The 3-Year Barrier


Building the internal platform to automate this provisioning and manage these fleets globally was projected to take[up to 1 year per cloud](http://clickhouse.com/blog/building-clickhouse-cloud-from-scratch-in-a-year) , stalling their ability to scale.


Multi-Cloud Consistency


Maintaining specific configurations for AWS, Azure, and GCP created unnecessary engineering overhead.


Unified Governance


Enforcing security standards and tracking licensing across disparate environments required a centralized approach to ensure consistency.

DataRobot partnered with Omnistrate to implement a control plane capable of resolving specific infrastructure challenges. By abstracting the complexity of the underlying environments, Omnistrate allowed DataRobot to support every deployment case—from strict on-premise regulations to global SaaS—without reliance on "one-off" configurations.


1. Automating On-Prem & BYOC Delivery


To solve the deployment friction in their private cloud business, DataRobot leveraged Omnistrate to shift from manual intervention to automated delivery for their On-Premise and BYOC offerings.


Automated Lifecycle Orchestration


DataRobot deployed Omnistrate's application packaging tools to automate delivery, enabling **seamless, zero-downtime upgrades** and ensuring customers always run the latest version.


Compliant Deployments


The team implemented automated infrastructure provisioning that enforces security standards—including **SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA and FTR.**


2. Accelerating the STS Launch


Separately, DataRobot utilized Omnistrate to launch their new Single-Tenant SaaS without the multi-year infrastructure build.


Multi-Cloud SaaS Infrastructure


DataRobot utilized the platform's cloud adapters to architect and provision their Single Tenant SaaS (STS) staging clusters across AWS, Azure, and GCP from a single interface.


Instant Global Governance


The platform provided immediate centralization for security, compliance, and user management, allowing them to launch a standardized global service in months rather than years.


Global Standardization


The solution established a standardized infrastructure abstraction layer, allowing DataRobot to automatically **bootstrap infrastructure in new regions** as needed without repetitive engineering.
