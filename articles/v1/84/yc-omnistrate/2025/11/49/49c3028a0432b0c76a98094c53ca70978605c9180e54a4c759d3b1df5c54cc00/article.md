# The Challenge


## The $200B Opportunity and the Multi-Cloud Challenge


FalkorDB provides a blazing-fast graph database built for modern AI applications. With the explosion of data-hungry Large Language Models (LLMs), FalkorDB saw a critical opportunity within the **$200B GenAI market** : to power the next wave of AI applications, such as GraphRAG and advanced security analytics, with its ultra-low-latency database.


To seize this opportunity, they needed to move beyond an open-source model and offer their product as a fully managed, multi-cloud SaaS. This would allow them to target the 90% of enterprises operating in multi-cloud environments and provide the ease of use, scalability, and security that modern AI teams demand.


This strategic goal, however, was blocked by a massive engineering hurdle. Launching a true multi-cloud SaaS product required building a sophisticated control plane from the ground up—a project that would divert critical resources from their core database technology.


Intense Time-to-Market Pressure


The GenAI market moves at an unprecedented pace. A traditional "DIY" build-out of a control plane could take years to build and an army of engineers to operate, forcing FalkorDB to miss the critical market window.


Prohibitive Complexity & Cost


Building a multi-cloud control plane meant navigating disparate APIs and services across AWS, GCP, and other clouds. This would require specialized expertise and significant upfront capital investment in infrastructure before landing a single customer.


Resource Drain on Core Product


Every engineer assigned to building billing, user management, or deployment orchestration was an engineer not improving FalkorDB's core database technology—the company's true differentiator.
