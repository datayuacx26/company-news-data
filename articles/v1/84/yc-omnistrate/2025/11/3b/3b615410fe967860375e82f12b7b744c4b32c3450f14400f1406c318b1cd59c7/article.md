# The Challenge


## Aligning an Innovative Platform with Enterprise Deployment Needs


TileDB developed the revolutionary **Carrara omnimodal data platform** , which is uniquely designed for the complex data needs of regulated industries like Healthcare & Life Sciences (HLS). While TileDB's product was a perfect fit for this market, their deployment model needed to evolve to meet the stringent requirements of their largest-scale customers.


These high-value enterprises, including major pharmaceutical and hospital networks, have strict data sovereignty and compliance needs (e.g., HIPAA). Their policies **require** that platforms like TileDB run inside their own private cloud accounts—a model known as["Bring Your Own Cloud" (BYOC)](https://docs.omnistrate.com/usecases/byoc/) .


This presented a strategic opportunity and a set of new requirements for TileDB:


Expanding Cloud Support


TileDB needed to support BYOC deployments on Microsoft Azure, a mandatory requirement for a large and critical segment of their target market.


Scaling Customer Onboarding


The existing process for provisioning new dedicated instances was manual and engineer-intensive. To support enterprise growth, TileDB needed an automated, self-service experience.


Meeting Compliance at Scale


TileDB needed a programmatic and scalable way to guarantee the strict data residency and performance isolation their HLS customers required for compliance.


The "DIY" Consideration


TileDB's engineering team faced a classic "build vs. buy" decision. According to **leading cloud providers and industry reviews** , a "DIY" control plane requires a **$2M-$4M investment and[12-18 months](https://clickhouse.com/blog/building-clickhouse-cloud-from-scratch-in-a-year) to build the initial (limited) version** . This path also creates significant, ongoing **Day 2 operational overhead** —forcing teams to manage monitoring, upgrades, and compliance—which would have diverted resources from the core Carrara platform and **significantly delayed time-to-market** for BYOC and Azure support.

## Omnistrate's Control Plane Delivers Enterprise Scale


TileDB made the strategic "buy-over-build" decision to accelerate its enterprise readiness. They selected[Omnistrate's Control Plane-as-a-Service](https://omnistrate.com/control-plane-demystified) to solve the complex challenges of enterprise software distribution.


Omnistrate's platform provided the complete deployment and management framework that TileDB needed to operate at enterprise scale:


Accelerated Readiness on Microsoft Azure


Using Omnistrate's control plane, TileDB was able to quickly prepare its platform on Microsoft Azure as a secure BYOC deployment, immediately addressing a key market requirement.


Full Lifecycle Automation


Omnistrate automated the entire customer lifecycle for TileDB. The platform now supports **self-service provisioning** for new dedicated TileDB stacks. Omnistrate provides the foundation for managing the deployment, metering, logging, and future upgrades for TileDB, without manual intervention.


Guaranteed Customer Isolation


Omnistrate's architecture ensures that every TileDB customer receives a **dedicated, isolated deployment** of the TileDB platform running securely within their own cloud account. This directly satisfies the core data sovereignty and compliance requirements of HLS and other regulated industries.


Multi-Cloud Foundation


Omnistrate provided TileDB with a single, unified framework to manage deployments across all major cloud providers, starting with AWS and Azure, without TileDB's team needing to become experts in each cloud's specific tooling.
