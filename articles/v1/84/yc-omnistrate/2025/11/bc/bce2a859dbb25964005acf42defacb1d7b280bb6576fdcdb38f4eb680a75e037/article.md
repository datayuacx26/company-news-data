# The Challenge


## Winning the Market While Staying AI-Focused


Experio's early success created urgent demand from large, global consulting firms for a fully managed, enterprise-ready platform. To convert pilots into large-scale deals, Experio had to deliver a robust set of operational features:


Enterprise Security & Compliance


Non-negotiable requirements for robust **tenant isolation** , auditability, and readiness for standards like **SOC and GDPR** .


Multi-Cloud Distribution


The ability to offer customers the choice of[BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud)](https://omnistrate.com/blog/deep-dive-into-the-on-prem-distribution-landscape-why-omnistrate-is-the-only-comprehensive-platform) and deployment across major vendors (AWS, Azure/GCP) for data residency and compliance.


Engineering Focus


Experio, an **"AI-first team"** , realized that building the multi-cloud, multi-tenant control plane is a massive undertaking requiring[12-18 months of upfront engineering effort](https://clickhouse.com/blog/building-clickhouse-cloud-from-scratch-in-a-year) **and 20-30% of the constant engineering bandwidth to evolve as you scale** . This DIY path would have consumed their elite talent and created significant **Day 2 operational overhead** (monitoring, upgrades, observability). Choosing to build this undifferentiated infrastructure would have killed their competitive advantage in the fast-moving Agentic AI space.
