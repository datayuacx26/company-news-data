# How Plotline Streamlines Internal Deployments to Support Massive Scale and Free Engineers to Innovate

## At-a-Glance


Company


Plotline, a Seed-stage no-code platform that empowers product and marketing teams at B2C mobile apps to launch in-app experiences in minutes.


Category


Product Experience Platform (PXP)


Challenge


Plotline's core business, supporting over 1.5 billion daily API calls, was being hampered by fragile, ad-hoc internal deployment processes, rising costs, and valuable engineering time being diverted to infrastructure maintenance.


Result


Plotline adopted Omnistrate as a comprehensive Internal Delivery Platform, reclaiming significant engineering time, avoiding a costly DIY build, and enabling developers to self-serve environments to focus on product innovation.


# The Challenge


## Supporting Massive Scale with Fragile Tools


Plotline's business is built to handle massive scale. Serving high-stakes industries like fintech and gaming, the platform processes over **1.5 billion API calls** daily and serves over 700 million monthly impressions. To provide a simple "no-code" experience to its customers, Plotline must manage an incredibly complex, high-stakes technical backend.


As the company scaled, their homegrown infrastructure began to show cracks, creating operational friction that threatened to slow innovation.


Fragile & Inconsistent Infrastructure


The team was relying on makeshift deployment methods and brittle scripts. This created inconsistent service patterns and a growing maintenance burden that was difficult to manage.


Developer Inefficiency


Valuable engineering teams were spending their time managing infrastructure instead of focusing on product innovation. The lack of automation meant developers were bogged down by provisioning and maintenance tasks.


Rising & Unpredictable Costs


Plotline was experiencing increasing costs and struggled with an inability to avoid over-provisioning. They needed a way to model, predict, and optimize their infrastructure spend.


Specialized Expertise Required


Managing their multiple open-source services in production demanded specialized DevOps and platform engineering expertise, which is expensive to hire and difficult to maintain.

# The Solution


## A Pre-Built Internal Delivery Platform


Plotline's leadership faced a strategic "build vs. buy" decision. They recognized that their competitive advantage was their customer-facing application, not building internal DevOps tooling. Instead of diverting significant engineering time to build a platform from scratch, they chose Omnistrate as their[Internal Delivery Platform (IDP)](https://docs.omnistrate.com/usecases/internal-saas/) .


Omnistrate provided a complete, pre-built, and unified platform that solved Plotline's operational challenges immediately.


Automated Internal SaaS


Omnistrate managed the underlying infrastructure and deployment complexity, allowing Plotline to host their internal projects as managed services for internal teams.


Developer Self-Service


Plotline's developers can now provision their own environments in minutes. This unlocked faster iteration and freed engineering time for product innovation.


Cost & Spend Management


The platform provided the tools for a TCO-focused story, enabling Plotline to utilize AWS reserve instances and gain the ability to forecast spend.


Unified Operational Control


Omnistrate's platform provided deployment consistency, removing the unreliable service patterns and fragile infrastructure that had slowed the team down.


# The Impact


## From Operational Overhead to Accelerated Innovation


By adopting Omnistrate, Plotline transformed its internal operations from a growing bottleneck into an accelerator for business growth. The engineering team was freed from undifferentiated heavy lifting and could refocus on what matters most: building their core product.


Saved Significant Engineering Capacity


Plotline avoided the need to build DevOps tooling from scratch—a project that would have diverted engineering capacity for months. This allowed them to immediately reinvest that capacity into product innovation.


Eliminated Developer Friction


The self-serve platform empowers engineers to move quickly and independently, significantly reducing the maintenance burden and letting them focus on product, not operations.


Enabled Cost Control & Forecasting


Plotline moved from reactive, rising costs to a proactive, predictable TCO. Omnistrate's solution allowed them to **consolidate different vendors** , providing **consistency in capabilities and tooling** . This move not only enabled accurate spend forecasting but also **saved them 50-60%** over other alternatives.


Accelerated Market Agility
