# The Challenge


## The Sovereign AI Compliance Barrier


Rox is an innovative technology company at the forefront of the AI revolution. Its **AI Agent Swarm** acts as a Revenue Operating System, automating complex sales workflows and eliminating the manual work that slows down GTM teams.


Rox's AI Agent Swarm was ready for the enterprise, but their distribution model wasn't. Large, regulated customers in international markets demanded more than a standard SaaS login; they required:


Dedicated Deployments


Customers required fully isolated, single-tenant environments hosted by Rox to ensure strict data separation.


Deployment in Customer VPC


Security-conscious buyers demanded the software run entirely within their own cloud accounts (Bring Your Own Cloud) to maintain total data sovereignty.


Air-Gapped Installs


The most sensitive customers required deployments in offline, air-gapped environments where no external connectivity is permitted.
