# The Challenge


## Enterprise Demand for Safe, Compliant Deployments


Autoblocks helps companies develop and validate AI applications before they reach production. Its platform enables teams to simulate real-world scenarios, evaluate model behavior, and continuously improve AI performance with real-world scenarios and subject matter expert (SME) feedback.


Autoblocks had validated market traction, but scaling to enterprise buyers revealed four major friction points:


Data Residency & Compliance


Distributing Autoblocks software to Healthcare and financial customers with regional data residency controls to meet HIPAA and GDPR mandates.


AI Infrastructure Complexity


Managing GPU orchestration, CUDA environments, and third-party AI integrations (e.g., Amazon Bedrock) across clouds demanded scale that a small team couldn't support.


Operational Burden


Each enterprise install required manual, bespoke DevOps support, slowing sales and consuming engineering time.


Monetization & Visibility


Lacking built-in usage tracking and billing, Autoblocks couldn't measure cost efficiency or expansion metrics investors wanted to see.

# The Solution


## Omnistrate's SaaS Control Plane


To meet enterprise demand for secure, customer-controlled deployments, Autoblocks needed a scalable way for customers to run its AI testing platform within their own cloud environments. Rather than building this complex infrastructure from scratch, Autoblocks partnered with Omnistrate.


Autoblocks used Omnistrate's powerful Agentic Control Plane as a Service to power the[Autoblocks Deployment Portal](https://www.autoblocks.ai/blog/deployment-portal) — a self-service environment where enterprise customers can deploy, manage, and monitor Autoblocks directly in their own cloud infrastructure, all with centralized visibility and automated lifecycle management.


Secure, Customer-Managed Deployments


Enabled Bring Your Own Cloud (BYOC) and on-prem deployments so enterprises can run Autoblocks entirely within their own environments — ensuring data sovereignty, security, and compliance.


AI-Native Infrastructure Support


Delivered GPU orchestration, CUDA support, and Amazon Bedrock integrations to power high-performance AI workloads with fast model startup and scaling.


Unified Operations Layer


Centralized monitoring, upgrades, and lifecycle management — giving Autoblocks' DevOps and security teams a unified operations layer and single point of control across all customer environments.


Integrated Billing & Marketplace Readiness


Delivered built-in usage metering and per-tenant cost insights, automated invoicing, and AWS Marketplace integration — giving Autoblocks full visibility into resource economics while enabling usage-based pricing and faster enterprise monetization through cloud marketplaces.
