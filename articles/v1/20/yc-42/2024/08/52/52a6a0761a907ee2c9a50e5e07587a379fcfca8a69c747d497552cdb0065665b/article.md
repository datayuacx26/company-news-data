# What’s Your RTOI? (We’re Coining a Phrase)

In retail, time is like that bag of M&Ms in your bottom desk drawer: we could have *sworn* there was a lot more left (seriously, where did it all go??). As the saying goes, you can always make more money, but you can never make more time. And that’s too bad, since in retail, every minute saved on backend tasks is a minute that can be redirected to enhancing customer experiences, boosting sales, and refining operations. You know, the stuff that really moves the needle.


Well, what if you could get a lot of that time back? You know all about ROI – return on investment. But what about your *RTOI* ? We’re coining the phrase right here: Returned Time On Investment. (We’re just not sure how to pronounce the acronym yet. ar-TOY? ar-TWAH? Let us know.)


# How to Invest in Time


No, we’re not selling you timeshares. Rather, we’re going to pitch you on saving time the new-fashioned way: using the right modern tools to do your[retail data analytics and reporting](https://blog.42technologies.com/the-impact-of-data-driven-reporting-on-retail-sales-growth/?utm_source=Blog&utm_medium=Internal&utm_campaign=Link&utm_id=Articles) .


Of course, most retail brands already know that mining their data can lead to solid ROI. But often, that’s easier said than done. Without the right data tools, even[48% of data scientists say they spend more time just organizing data than actually using it](https://www.hp.com/us-en/workstations/learning-hub/data-science-saving-time.html?ref=blog.42technologies.com) . If data scientists are losing time to data management, it’s no wonder that retail teams often want to avoid analyzing their data altogether. Again, *time is precious* .


But if retailers can transform the way they handle data, turning what used to be a laborious process into a streamlined, insightful, and time-saving operation; then they can have their cake and eat it too. Namely, pulling insights from their data and also reinvesting their saved time back into their business for exponential growth.


# The Right Data Tool for the Data Job


At its core, retail data reporting and analytics involve the collection, processing, and interpretation of vast amounts of data generated from various touchpoints in the retail ecosystem. Historically, this process was cumbersome, requiring significant manual effort to collate data, generate reports, and analyze results. However, with modern analytics platforms available for all retailers today, this process has been revolutionized, allowing them to save time and make more informed decisions. The right data reporting tool gives you:


## Automated Reporting


One of the standout features of a good data reporting tool is its ability to automate the reporting process. Instead of manually pulling data from various sources, retailers can set up automated reports that compile all relevant data into easy-to-read formats. This not only saves time but also ensures accuracy and consistency in reporting.


## Real-Time Insights


Gone are the days of waiting for end-of-day or weekly reports. Retailers now have access to real-time insights. This immediate access allows for quicker decision-making, whether it's adjusting inventory levels, tweaking marketing strategies, or responding to sales trends as they happen.


## Customizable Dashboards


The best analytics platforms offer customizable dashboards that provide a snapshot of[key performance indicators (KPIs)](https://blog.42technologies.com/the-top-kpis-all-retail-brands-need-to-track-in-2024/?utm_source=Blog&utm_medium=Internal&utm_campaign=Link&utm_id=Articles) . Retailers can tailor these dashboards to highlight the most critical metrics for their business, making it easy to monitor performance at a glance. This eliminates the need to sift through mountains of data, allowing managers to focus on what truly matters.


## Advanced Analytics


From predictive analytics to trend identification, the more advanced analytics tools on the market today enable retailers to delve deeper into their data, uncovering insights that drive strategic decisions. The right data reporting tools go way beyond your pivot tables, and provide insights down to the individual SKUs.


# Reinvesting Time Saved into Business Improvement


So, with all this time saved, what should retailers do with their newfound hours? Here are several areas where investing this extra time can yield significant dividends:


## Enhancing Customer Service


Great customer service is the cornerstone of retail success. With more time on their hands, retailers can focus on training staff to provide exceptional service, ensuring that every customer interaction is positive and memorable. Additionally, staff can spend more time engaging with customers, understanding their needs, and building relationships that foster loyalty.


## Boosting Sales


With the insights gained from better data analytics, retailers can develop more effective sales strategies. This might involve personalizing marketing efforts, optimizing product placements, or launching targeted promotions. The time saved on data management can be redirected towards creative and strategic initiatives that drive sales growth.


## Streamlining Operations


Efficient operations are critical to maintaining profitability. Retailers can use their extra time to refine operational processes, from inventory management to supply chain logistics. By identifying inefficiencies and implementing improvements, retailers can reduce costs and enhance productivity.


## Innovating the Customer Experience


The modern retail environment demands constant innovation. With more time available, retailers can explore new ways to enhance the customer experience, whether through in-store technology, personalized shopping experiences, or unique service offerings. This focus on innovation helps retailers stay competitive and meet evolving customer expectations.
