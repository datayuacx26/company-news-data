# Model ML Partners with LSEG to Bring Licensed Financial Markets Data Into Every Workflow

Model ML today announced a partnership with LSEG (London Stock Exchange Group), one of the world’s leading providers of financial markets data and analytics.


Financial workflows are only as good as the data behind them. Through LSEG's MCP connector, users can now work from LSEG's trusted, licensed data directly inside Model ML. This includes fundamentals, estimates, ownership, macro indicators, ESG data, and news across all their workflows, skills, and surfaces.


#### LSEG in Model ML: Use Cases by Sector


-


**Private Equity:** Ground portfolio monitoring and diligence in LSEG fundamentals and estimates, and screen targets against ownership and macro indicators alongside internal models and IC memos.


-


**Investment Banking:** Build comps, pitchbooks, and IC materials on LSEG fundamentals and estimates, and track relevant developments through licensed news as deals progress.


-


**Consultancies:** Support market analysis and client deliverables with LSEG macro indicators, ESG data, and news, combined with filings and internal research.


If you have an LSEG license, you can start using it inside Model ML today.
