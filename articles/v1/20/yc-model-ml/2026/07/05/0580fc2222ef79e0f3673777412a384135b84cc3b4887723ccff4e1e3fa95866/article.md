# Model ML Q2 Results: ARR Doubled and 47,000+ Seats Added

This quarter, Model ML doubled ARR and added over 47,000 seats across some of the world's largest banks, consulting firms, and asset managers.


Beyond numbers, usage patterns have also shifted. Daily activity per user has tripled in the past 12 weeks, and users are increasingly working with their agents the way they would with colleagues; a meaningful change in how teams engage with AI day to day.
