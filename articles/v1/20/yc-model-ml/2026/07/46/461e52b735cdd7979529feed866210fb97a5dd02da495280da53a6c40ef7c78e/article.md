# PwC Scales Model ML AI Agents Globally

Model ML today announced that PwC and Model ML have been co-developing AI capabilities, now being scaled globally across PwC.


Over the past several months, the two teams have worked in close partnership to co-create a suite of AI solutions side by side. Together, Model ML and PwC have developed a combination of off-the-shelf and custom agents designed to support transaction-related work and streamline the review of PwC documents.


PwC selected Model ML following its demonstrated velocity, commitment, and collaborative approach, with feedback frequently reflected in the platform within hours. Because Model ML is model-agnostic, each task is routed to the model best suited to the dimension that matters most, whether a frontier model or a faster, lower-cost open-source model running on Model ML's infrastructure or within PwC's own environment, across every surface the team works on.


"A huge thank you to Dan, Colin, Toby, Joseph and the wider PwC team. It's rare to see a firm this size move so decisively on AI, and rarer still to get to build it with them," said Chaz Englander, CEO of Model ML.
