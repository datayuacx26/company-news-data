# West Lane Capital Partners Deploys Model ML to Power Long-Term Value Creation Through AI

[West Lane Capital Partners](https://www.westlanecap.com/) , a leading middle-market private investment firm, today announced a firmwide rollout of Model ML, the leading AI workflow builder for investment firms. The collaboration places West Lane at the forefront of the middle market, leveraging Model ML’s platform to fuel its AI initiatives.


“West Lane understands that speed and decisiveness are critical as technology continues to reshape financial services,” said Chaz Englander, CEO and Co-Founder of Model ML. “We’re excited to provide the technology that helps them to further strengthen their bespoke investment and operational approach across their entire portfolio.”


“Our philosophy is grounded in the belief that every transaction is unique and requires a tailored approach to create long-term value,” said Nick Sternberg, Managing Partner at West Lane Capital. “Model ML’s AI-powered workflows give us the equivalent resources of much larger institutions with a small team, enabling us to evaluate more opportunities, go deeper on each deal, and spend more time on strategic, high-impact work.”


As part of the rollout, West Lane is deploying AI-powered workflows across every stage of the investment lifecycle, from sourcing to LOIs, IC memos, diligence, and execution, enabling deeper focus on qualitative insights, strategic growth levers, and long-term value creation.


**About West Lane Capital Partners**


West Lane Capital Partners is a lower middle market private equity firm focused on partnering with companies and management teams that can benefit from their operational expertise in professionalizing and scaling businesses. The firm brings a hands-on approach to building scalable infrastructure, strengthening teams, implementing systems, and driving profitable growth.
