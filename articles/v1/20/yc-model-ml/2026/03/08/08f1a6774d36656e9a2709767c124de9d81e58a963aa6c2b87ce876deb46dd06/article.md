# Model ML expands partnership with PitchBook, the leading provider of private capital market data

Model ML has expanded its partnership with PitchBook to bring the full universe of authoritative private capital market intelligence deeper into the Model ML platform, enabling investment teams to combine high-fidelity data with AI workflows across the entire deal lifecycle.


With this expanded integration, PitchBook data can now power a broader set of AI workflows inside Model ML. This will allow analysts to move from simple inputs to structured outputs in minutes, including research summaries, investment memos, comparable analyses, and presentations—all without leaving their workflow environment.


PitchBook’s best-in-class datasets are widely recognized as the most comprehensive, accurate, and timely source of private capital market intelligence. Model ML users can now access more than 9.8 million private companies, 3 million deals, 617,000 investors, and 160,000 funds across global markets—with new profiles added daily. By combining PitchBook’s data with Model ML’s advanced AI workspace and workflow builder, teams can now ground every workflow in trusted market intelligence, and automate the manual processes that traditionally slow down research and diligence.


Strengthen your workflows and invest confidently with Model ML, now powered by PitchBook. If you already have a PitchBook subscription, follow this[setup guide](https://pitchbook.com/help/pitchbook-premium-connector-model-ml) to get started.
