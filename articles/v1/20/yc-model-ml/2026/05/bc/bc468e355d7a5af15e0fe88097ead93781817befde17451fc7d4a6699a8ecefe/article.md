# Model ML Integrates With Preqin To Bring Alternative Assets Data

We're pleased to announce that Preqin, the leading data provider for alternative assets, is now integrated into Model ML's platform. Preqin is the benchmark data source for alternatives, covering private equity, venture capital, hedge funds, real estate, infrastructure, and private debt across hundreds of thousands of funds and managers globally.


For fund managers and allocators, they can now benchmark portfolio performance against Preqin's industry data directly inside Model ML. And when analysts need to move quickly on due diligence or market research, they can interrogate Preqin's dataset alongside their internal data through natural-language questions, collapsing hours of work into minutes.


"Preqin is the data layer that serious alternatives professionals already trust,” said Chaz Englander, CEO of Model ML. “Bringing it natively into Model ML means our users can benchmark, research, and target LPs without ever leaving their workflow.”


The partnership access covers Preqin's core datasets: fund performance metrics including IRR, TVPI, and DPI; LP and investor profiles; fund manager track records; benchmark comparisons by vintage, strategy, and geography; and deal and fundraising activity. All this data is now queryable on demand.


Preqin's alternatives coverage is unmatched, and now it's available exactly where investment teams are already working. The result is faster research, sharper fundraising prep, and better decisions at every stage of the investment lifecycle.
