# Equirus Group Selects Model ML to Automate Banking Workflows

Equirus Group has selected Model ML to automate workflows across its Banking, Research, and Institutional Equities businesses, marking Model ML's firmwide go-live at the firm and an expansion of its presence in the APAC region.


The decision follows an extensive evaluation of the market, with Equirus choosing Model ML to embed AI directly into the analysis, drafting, and reporting processes that underpin the firm's core operations.
