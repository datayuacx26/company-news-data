# What is the ROI of full-funnel Advertising

Most advertising ROI models overvalue last-click conversions and undervalue the long-term revenue impact of upper-and mid-funnel media. This method moves beyond isolated bottom-funnel tactics and nurtures the customer from the first moment of brand discovery through the final transaction.


**Brands using a full-funnel strategy align their advertising efforts with every stage of the buyer’s journey** , creating a cohesive experience that builds trust and familiarity. Continue reading to learn how integrating these stages optimizes budget efficiency and maximizes long-term ROI.


**Key Highlights**


- **Traditional attribution models overvalue last-click metrics; full-funnel ROI accounts for the combined impact of upper, mid, and lower-funnel tactics.**


- **Full-funnel advertising generates three distinct types of ROI: Brand Equity (TOFU), Pipeline Growth (MOFU), and Immediate Conversion (BOFU).**


- **Nielsen data reveals that full-funnel strategies boost overall marketing ROI by up to 45% compared to isolated bottom-funnel campaigns.**

## What Full Funnel Advertising ROI Means


[Full-funnel advertising](https://perion.com/perion-one/) **ROI is the total financial return generated by marketing campaigns that target consumers at every stage of the buying journey, from the initial brand awareness to the final purchase.**


Unlike isolated direct-response campaigns that focus only on immediate conversions, a


**full-funnel approach acknowledges that a consumer rarely buys a product on their first** **exposure** . It calculates the cumulative impact of funnel touchpoints working in tandem.


Measuring this requires moving away from flawed last-click attribution models. If a user sees a high-impact Digital Out-of-Home


[(DOOH) ad](https://perion.com/dooh/) , reads a sponsored article a week later, and finally clicks a paid search ad to buy, last-click attribution gives 100% of the credit to search. Full-funnel ROI accurately distributes value, ensuring the budget is allocated to the upper-funnel tactics that feed the conversion pipeline.


### 3 Types of ROI Generated Across the Funnel


To accurately measure a full-funnel strategy, you must segment your returns by stage of the funnel.


There are three main types of ROI generated across the funnel.


- Immediate conversion ROI (BOFU)


- Pipeline ROI (MOFU)


- Brand equity ROI (TOFU)

### How to Calculate and Measure Immediate Conversion ROI


Immediate conversion ROI focuses entirely on the bottom of the funnel (BOFU). This is the traditional,


**short-term return on ad spend that finance teams lean on because it is highly tangible** .


The formula to calculate immediate conversion ROI is:


Immediate conversion ROI =


Direct revenue from campaigns – Ad Spend /


Ad spend


Some of the measurement strategies for immediate conversion ROI include using


**conversion tracking pixels** to capture immediate checkout, sign-up, and download events. You can also measure conversions with


**promo codes and custom landing pages** by using unique identifiers for specific low-funnel ad variations.


### How to Calculate and Measure Pipeline ROI


**Pipeline ROI ties initial awareness and final conversion. It measures how effectively mid-funnel advertising nurtures prospects, increasing consideration and lowering the cost of future conversions.**


The formula to calculate pipeline ROI is:


Pipeline ROI =


Total value of marketing-qualified leads /


MOFU ad spend


Measurement strategies at this stage of the funnel include


**multi-touch attribution,** **using linear or time-decay models** to credit mid-funnel content downloads, webinar registrations, and retargeting clicks. You should also


**track how quickly users move from the awareness stage to an active opportunity.**

### How to Calculate and Measure Compounding Brand Equity ROI


Top-of-the-funnel advertising does not yield immediate transactions, but it creates the mental availability that makes all future advertising more effective. This compounding return is called


**brand equity ROI.**


The formula to measure brand equity is:


Brand equity ROI =


Lift in Organic Revenue + Lifetime Value /


TOFU Ad Spend


Measurement strategies for the top of the funnel include


**brand lift studies, which are controlled surveys to measure increases in brand awareness, consideration, and purchase intent.** Another strategy is to use


**organic search, direct traffic, and media mix modeling.**


## Why Does Full-Funnel Advertising Deliver Higher ROI?


Full-funnel advertising avoids the drawbacks of siloed campaigns. With a full-funnel strategy, each stage subsidizes the efficiency of the next. When brands run isolated, siloed campaigns, they pay a premium for performance


When a consumer recognizes a brand from a premium


[CTV](https://perion.com/connected-tv/) or high-impact display campaign, they are significantly more likely to click on a paid search or social ad later.


**This drives up**[click-through rates](https://perion.com/glossary/click-through-rate-ctr/) **and lowers**[cost-per-click.](https://perion.com/glossary/cost-per-click-cpc/) ****


Customers who buy from a brand they trust typically exhibit greater brand loyalty, making repeat purchases without requiring heavy retargeting. A full-funnel strategy is also


**resistant to ad fatigue** by refreshing the pool of prospects.


### Full-funnel Advertising ROI Benchmarks


Full-funnel thinking is no longer a fringe theory; it’s where the industry is actively moving. According to


[Nielsen’s Marketing ROI Blueprint](https://www.nielsen.com/news-center/2025/nielsen-unveils-makerting-roi-blueprint/) , 38% of marketers now prioritize sales or ROI as their top success metric, a clear pivot away from reach-only thinking. Confidence and practice, though, do not yet match: 85% of marketers say they are confident in their ROI measurement, but only 32% actually measure it holistically across channels.


That gap shows up at the organizational level, too.


[The Multiplier Playbook](https://www.warc.com/en/the-multiplier-playbook-2026) report from WARC, developed with the Association of National Advertisers, Analytic Partners, BERA.ai, Prophet, and System1, found that 49% of companies still run separate brand and performance teams, and 65% still split their budgets between the two. Sixty percent of marketers say their own C-suite does not fully understand the role advertising plays in growth.


The upside case for closing that gap remains strong.


[Nielsen’s Market Lift meta-analysis](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2026/proving-advertising-works-from-exposure-to-sales-impact/) of CPG campaigns found that full-funnel activation, integrating upper- and lower-funnel tactics rather than running awareness alone, drove 1.5 times greater sales lift. The same analysis found that scale, creative variety, and sustained campaign duration each compounded the lift, reinforcing that full-funnel gains come from coordinated execution, not any single tactic.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Full-Funnel Advertising ROI


### What ROI Can You Expect from Full-Funnel Advertising?


While returns vary by industry, brands benefit from moving from a pure-performance model to a balanced full-funnel model. According to


[The Multiplier Effect](https://www.warc.com/en/analysis/in-focus/the-multiplier-effect) report from WARC developed with Analytic Partners, BERA.ai, Prophet, and System1, shifting from a performance-only strategy to a balanced brand-and-performance approach lifts total revenue ROI by 25% to 100%, with a median uplift of 90%


**.** This manifests as lower customer acquisition costs (CAC) and higher conversion rates at the bottom of the funnel.


### How Long Does Full-Funnel Advertising Take to Show ROI?


Immediate conversion ROI from BOFU tactics can be seen within days. However,


**the compounding pipeline and brand equity ROI from TOFU and MOFU tactics typically require time to fully manifest** . Data models like Media Mix Modeling, used primarily in industries with long buying cycles, can take weeks to process data and analyze campaign ROI.


### What Budget Split Should You Use for Full-Funnel Advertising?


A classic, time-tested benchmark by Les Binet and Peter Field in their book, The Long and Short of It, is the 60/40 rule: 60% of the budget allocated to long-term brand building (TOFU/MOFU) and 40% to direct-response conversions (BOFU). This split can be adjusted based on brand maturity and market share. However, it is far from an ironclad rule. Companies can vary from 70/30 to 60/40 according to their performance.
