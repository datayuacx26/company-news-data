# The political brief can’t wait. Neither should your DOOH buy

Political advertising has always operated under a different kind of pressure. Broadcast windows get reserved months in advance. Digital inventory prices surge as election day closes in. And traditional Out-of-Home (OOH), for all its proven ability to reach voters at scale, has historically been the channel most resistant to the pace at which campaigns actually make decisions.


Programmatic Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) changes that equation. For political buyers, it offers the targeting precision of digital alongside the physical presence and voter impact of outdoor, with activation that happens in a DSP in minutes rather than weeks.


## **Why traditional political OOH falls short on timing**


Any political media buyer who has run a traditional OOH campaign in a competitive district knows the friction. Individual insertion orders with each media owner, custom creative specifications for every format, and a setup timeline that rarely aligns with a campaign’s actual decision window. By the time the buy goes live, the news cycle has often moved on.


A single programmatic deal ID solves this. It activates across an entire network of pre-approved, opted-in screens without requiring IO negotiation, custom setup, or weeks of lead time. For campaigns working to tight timelines in fast-moving races, that operational difference is significant.


## **Targeting built around how campaigns actually plan**


Political campaigns don’t plan by DMA. They plan by district, by precinct, and by voter concentration, and the inventory they need has to map to that geography precisely. Perion’s political DOOH deal IDs are built to that same logic, geo-fenced to six key battleground states and 12 targeted congressional districts, with civic proximity targeting built into each package.


That means inventory near polling locations, transit hubs, and government buildings: the environments where voters are making decisions and where large-format DOOH has the highest contextual relevance. Venue context and audience indexing are already done. The buyer pulls the deal ID and pushes it to their DSP.


For campaigns that need coverage beyond packaged geographies, custom deals are available with a 48-hour turnaround.


## **Compliance built into the deal, not bolted on afterward**


Political creative carries requirements that most standard inventory sources weren’t designed to accommodate. Disclaimer language, content category flags, and DSP-level political clearance each introduce friction that slows activation precisely when speed matters most.


Perion’s political deal IDs are pre-approved for political content, with compliance guardrails built into the deal parameters from the outset. Buyers spend their time running the campaign rather than navigating clearance paperwork, and there is no risk of discovering mid-flight that inventory is blocked.


## **The 2026 cycle is moving**


In political media, the inventory that can activate today is worth more than a traditional buy that goes live in three weeks. Perion’s Curated Marketplace political deals are live now across battleground states and competitive districts, available at PMP priority above open exchange, with large format and video formats supported.


The 2026 cycle isn’t waiting. The inventory is ready.
