# 17 Types of Advertising Every Business Should Know

From traditional media to advanced digital platforms, various types of advertising are available to suit different marketing needs.


Understanding these types is essential for selecting the right approach and maximizing campaign impact. In this blog, we explore the different types of advertising, including traditional and digital options, that help brands craft successful marketing strategies.


**Key Highlights**


- Traditional advertising still holds value for a mass audience, engagement, local campaigns, and brand credibility.


- Digital advertising enables AI-powered, data-driven campaigns with real-time optimization across channels, helping advertisers improve measurable business outcomes.


- Innovative methods such as influencer marketing, programmatic advertising, and connected TV (CTV) provide brands with unique and personalized ways to connect with audiences in real time.


- The integration of traditional and digital advertising approaches helps brands optimize reach and engagement, creating seamless omnichannel marketing experiences.

## Traditional Types of Advertising


Although these days digital advertising is in the lead, there are sectors where traditional and offline advertising types are still relevant. Traditional advertising remains a vital part of many marketing strategies. These methods have stood the test of time and continue to deliver impactful results, especially when integrated with digital efforts.

### Television Advertising


Television advertising is the most commonly known by viewers, as every TV spectator has likely seen a TV ad. The reason it is so popular is that this type of advertising reaches massive audiences with engaging video content. By broadcasting commercials during prime-time slots, advertisers can capture the attention of diverse demographic groups. Television ads are particularly effective for storytelling and demonstrating product features visually.


For instance, major global brands often


[invest heavily in Super Bowl commercials](https://www.statista.com/topics/2113/sports-on-tv/#topicOverview) to gain mass exposure. Despite the rise of streaming services, TV advertising remains influential for large-scale campaigns, especially in live sports and news events.


### Radio Advertising


Radio advertising connects with listeners through audio messages, which makes it a cost-effective way to reach local and regional markets. According to research by Statista,


[ad spending in the traditional radio advertising market worldwide](https://www.statista.com/outlook/amo/advertising/audio-advertising/traditional-radio-advertising/worldwide) is expected to reach $28.47. Bn in 2025.


Radio ads are often used during commuting hours when people are tuning into their favorite stations. For instance, local businesses frequently advertise on the radio to promote store openings or sales events.


With the advent of digital radio and podcasts, audio advertising has expanded its reach, maintaining relevance in today’s media landscape.


### Print Advertising


Print advertising utilizes newspapers and magazines to communicate targeted messages to specific audiences. Newspapers are ideal for timely announcements, such as sales, while magazines offer a more polished platform for visually appealing content. Despite print ad spending being in decline, with a


[negative growth of -4.3%](https://www.statista.com/outlook/amo/advertising/print-advertising/worldwide) for 2025, print ads have a


[9%](https://www.rcbrayshaw.com/how-effective-is-print-and-direct-mail-marketing/) rate of response rate compared with other channels.


Source:


[Statista](https://www.statista.com/outlook/amo/advertising/print-advertising/worldwide#ad-spending)


For instance, luxury brands often choose high-end lifestyle magazines for their advertisements to maintain brand image and exclusivity. In spite of the growth of digital media, print media still holds value for niche markets and readers seeking in-depth content.


### Outdoor Advertising – Out of Home Media (OOH)


Outdoor advertising leverages billboards, transit ads, and signage to achieve maximum visibility in high-traffic areas. Billboards along highways or in urban centers can capture the attention of thousands of commuters daily. Transit ads, such as those on buses and subway stations, ensure


**frequent exposure to city dwellers** .


Outdoor ads have evolved into digital billboards, and advertisers are taking advantage of the high reach and recall. Leading companies leverage prime advertising placements like the digital billboards in Times Square in NY, or Shibuya Crossing in Tokyo, to launch products or highlight their brands.


### In-store Advertising


In-store advertising focuses on


**reaching consumers at the point of purchase, influencing their buying decisions directly** . This type of advertising includes product displays, shelf signs, digital screens, and promotional materials within a retail environment. For example, a grocery store might feature end-cap displays highlighting seasonal products or limited-time promotions. Digital signage near checkout areas can be used to promote impulse buys such as snacks or beverages. In-store advertising is effective because it engages shoppers when they are already in a purchasing mindset, increasing the likelihood of conversion.

### Native Advertising


[Native advertising](https://perion.com/glossary/native-advertising/) seamlessly


**integrates into the user experience by appearing as recommended content on websites and apps, creating a non-intrusive way to engage audiences.** Unlike traditional ads, native ads match the look and feel of the platform, making them more likely to capture attention without disrupting the browsing experience.


This subtle approach often results in higher engagement and click-through rates. For example, a tech company might publish a sponsored article on a leading tech news site to showcase innovative solutions or industry insights, naturally connecting with readers interested in technology trends.


## Emerging Types of Advertising


As technology evolves, new advertising methods continue to emerge. These innovative approaches help brands stay ahead of the competition by engaging audiences in unique and personalized ways.


### Influencer Marketing


Partnering with social media influencers allows brands to


**tap into highly engaged and loyal audiences.** The influencers share authentic product experiences, often in creative and relatable ways, making their recommendations more trustworthy and impactful.


This approach helps drive both brand awareness and conversions. For instance, a beauty brand might team up with a popular makeup artist on Instagram to showcase a new product line through tutorials or reviews. Influencer campaigns frequently generate higher engagement and meaningful customer interactions compared to traditional advertisements.


### Native Advertising


[Native advertising](https://perion.com/glossary/native-advertising/) **integrates seamlessly into digital content, providing a non-disruptive user experience that feels natural to the audience.** These ads match the form and function of the platform they appear on, such as recommended articles on news sites or sponsored posts on social media feeds. For example, a travel company might publish a sponsored story highlighting top vacation destinations on a popular travel blog.


This subtle approach often results in better click-through rates and higher engagement compared to traditional display ads, as users perceive the content as informative rather than intrusive.


### Programmatic Advertising


**Programmatic advertising automates the ad-buying process by leveraging data-driven algorithms to automate and optimize campaigns.** This approach enables advertisers to efficiently target specific demographics based on real-time online behavior, ensuring that the right message reaches the right audience at the right time.


By analyzing user data,


[programmatic advertising](https://perion.com/glossary/programmatic-advertising/) helps brands deliver personalized and impactful ads. For instance, a retail brand can target shoppers who abandoned their carts with timely reminders and special offers to encourage conversions.

## FAQs


### What’s the most popular type of advertising?


Digital advertising has become the most popular type due to its ability to target specific audiences and track performance in real time. Social media, search, and programmatic ads are among the most widely used formats.


### What is the main difference between traditional and digital advertising?


The primary difference lies in delivery and tracking. Traditional advertising uses channels like TV, radio, and print, while digital advertising leverages online platforms, allowing for precise targeting and performance measurement.


### Which type of advertising is most effective?


The effectiveness depends on the audience and campaign goals. Digital advertising often provides better targeting and tracking, while traditional methods can be impactful for mass awareness.


### What is the best advertising method for small businesses?


Small businesses often benefit from social media and local search advertising, which are cost-effective and allow for precise targeting.
