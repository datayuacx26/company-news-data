# What’s the Difference Between CTV and OTT Advertising?

Understanding the difference between Connected TV (CTV) and Over-the-Top (OTT) platforms is essential for advertisers seeking to leverage these powerful digital mediums. These channels have revolutionized how audiences consume content, offering opportunities for targeted advertising. This article breaks down their definitions, features, and benefits, helping marketers craft effective strategies for audience engagement.


**Key Highlights**


- CTV refers to internet-connected devices like Smart TVs that enable streaming, while OTT delivers video content through the internet across various devices, including smartphones and tablets.


- CTV enables immersive and interactive formats like shoppable ads, while OTT uses versatile formats such as pre-roll and mid-roll ads across devices.


- When choosing between CTV and OTT, keep in mind that CTV is ideal for niche, high-impact campaigns targeting specific demographics, while OTT is better for broader audience reach across multiple devices.


- Still, combining CTV and OTT enhances reach and engagement, with CTV focusing on personalized ads and OTT providing consistent multi-device visibility.

### The Technology Behind CTV and OTT


While they work together, CTV and OTT rely on different but complementary technologies to deliver video content.


[CTV refers to internet-enabled hardware](https://perion.com/glossary/connected-tv-ctv/) **,** such as smart TVs, streaming devices, and gaming consoles, that allow users to stream content directly. These devices operate within an application that enables access to streaming platforms like Netflix, Hulu, and YouTube. This hardware’s ability to connect to the internet results in a smooth video experience and interactive ad formats, such as clickable banners or shoppable TV ads.


**OTT focuses on delivering video content through streaming platforms that don’t use cable or satellite providers** . It includes platforms like Disney+, Peacock, and HBO Max, which operate via OTT technology. OTT makes it possible to access content from other devices like smartphones and tablets. OTT is more device-agnostic, unlike CTV.


Both offer seamless access to on-demand video content, allowing a user to start watching a video on their smartphone via an OTT platform and continue watching on a smart TV.

## How to Choose Between OTT and CTV?


> *While CTV often requires a higher initial investment due to its premium formats, it delivers higher engagement. OTT offers cost-effective options for sustained, high-frequency campaigns.*


Choosing between OTT and CTV may depend on the advertiser’s goals, target audience, and campaign objectives. Some things to consider:


**CTV is ideal for targeting specific demographics with immersive and interactive ad formats.** For instance, a luxury car brand might use CTV to showcase a virtual showroom experience, targeting affluent households during prime-time viewing hours.


**The precision targeting capabilities of CTV make it suitable for campaigns with niche audiences or high-value products.**


**OTT, on the other hand, is better suited for campaigns aiming for a broad audience reach across multiple devices.** For example, a fast food chain launching a new menu item might leverage OTT to deliver ads across smartphones, tablets, and laptops, ensuring maximum visibility. The device-agnostic nature of OTT allows brands to keep the message consistent across different touchpoints.


Something advertisers should consider is the budget allocation and campaign duration. While CTV often requires a higher initial investment due to its premium formats, it delivers higher engagement. OTT offers cost-effective options for sustained, high-frequency campaigns.

## FAQs


### What is the main difference between CTV and OTT advertising?


Both approaches differ primarily in their focus.


**CTV (Connected TV)** refers to the internet-connected devices or TVs used to access streaming content, such as smart TVs, streaming devices (like Roku or Fire Stick), and gaming consoles.


**OTT (Over-the-Top)** refers to the streaming content delivered via the internet and accessible across multiple devices, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and CTVs.


### What types of ad formats are available for CTV and OTT?


CTV offers interactive ads, such as clickable banners, shoppable videos, and QR codes on large screens. OTT, on the other hand, commonly uses pre-roll, mid-roll, and post-roll video ads embedded within streaming content.


### How does audience targeting differ between CTV and OTT advertising?


Both approaches offer advanced targeting options, but they differ slightly. CTV focuses on household-level targeting and behavioral insights specific to smart TV users. OTT targets audiences across devices, enabling advertisers to reach users on mobile, desktop, and TVs using data like location demographics and streaming behavior.


### Which is more effective for advertisers CTV or OTT?


The effectiveness of CTV or OTT will depend on the campaign goals. CTV is more suitable for creating high-impact, immersive campaigns that leverage the large screen format, while OTT is better for reaching a wider audience across multiple devices.


### Could you use CTV and online video OTT together?


Combining both platforms can be highly effective. For example, a movie studio might use OTT to generate initial awareness for a film release and follow up with CTV ads featuring exclusive behind-the-scenes content, driving more engagement.
