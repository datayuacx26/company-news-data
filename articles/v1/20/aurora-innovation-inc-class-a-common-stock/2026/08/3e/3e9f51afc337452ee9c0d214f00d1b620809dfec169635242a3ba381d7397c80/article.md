# Aurora to Host a Retail Investor Town Hall on August 20, 2026


August 06, 2026 9:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://ir.aurora.tech/_assets/_517426f8bc51422df8d7e1783cfaddd4/aurora/news/2026-08-06_Aurora_to_Host_a_Retail_Investor_Town_Hall_on_149.pdf)


PITTSBURGH--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Aurora Innovation, Inc. (NASDAQ: AUR) today announced it will host a virtual Retail Investor Town Hall with Co-founder and CEO Chris Urmson and CFO David Maday on August 20, 2026 from 12:00 p.m. - 12:30 p.m. Eastern time. The event will be livestreamed at[Aurora Retail Investor Town Hall](https://info.aurora.tech/virtual-retail-investor-town-hall-2026) .


Investors are invited to submit questions for management prior to the livestream via the[submission page](https://app.sli.do/event/brS6V94y1wv2Zrg5RgAF9z/live/questions) . Question submissions and upvoting will be available until August 20, 2026 at 11:00 a.m. Eastern time.


A replay will be available for 30 days following the event.


Investors can emailir@aurora.tech for any support inquiries.


**About Aurora**


[Aurora](https://aurora.tech/) (Nasdaq: AUR) is delivering the benefits of self-driving technology safely, quickly, and broadly to make transportation safer, increasingly accessible, and more reliable and efficient than ever before.[The Aurora Driver](https://aurora.tech/) is a self-driving system designed to operate multiple vehicle types, from freight-hauling trucks to ride-hailing passenger vehicles, and underpins Aurora’s driver as a service product for trucking. Aurora is working with industry leaders across the transportation ecosystem, including AUMOVIO, FedEx, Hirschbach, McLane, NVIDIA, PACCAR, Ryder, Schneider, Toyota, Uber, Uber Freight, Volvo Trucks, Volvo Autonomous Solutions, and Werner. To learn more, visit[aurora.tech](https://aurora.tech/) .
