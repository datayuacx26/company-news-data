[Arjun Mahadevan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mahadevanarjun/) , the brain behind **Doola** , is no stranger to the challenges faced by entrepreneurs. ## The Birth of Doola: A Solution to Modern Entrepreneurial Challenges


Starting a business can be a difficult job, especially with a lot of legal and[administrative](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/administrative) tasks involved. **Doola** was born out of the desire to make this process seamless and accessible for entrepreneurs around the globe. By offering a comprehensive suite of services, **Doola** ensures that its clients can focus on what they do best – **building their business.**


From securing an EIN and U.S. address to providing bookkeeping and tax support, **Doola** covers all bases. The company’s unique selling proposition lies in its ability to cater to both web2 and web3 entrepreneurs, helping them from the U.S. LLCs, C Corps, and[DAO LLCs](https://www.doola.com/blog/what-is-a-dao-llc-your-complete-guide-to-daos/) .
