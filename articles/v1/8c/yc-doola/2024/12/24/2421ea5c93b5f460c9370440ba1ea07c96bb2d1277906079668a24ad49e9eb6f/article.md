## Bookkeeping Basics for Amazon: Recording Transactions from Amazon Sales


Accurate transaction recording is the cornerstone of bookkeeping basics for Amazon sellers, providing financial clarity and setting the stage for growth.


Here’s how to handle key aspects of Amazon sales:


#### 1. Sales and Revenue:


Amazon payouts aren’t your total sales—they come after deductions for commissions, shipping, advertising, and more.


Always break down each payout into gross sales and specific deductions. This transparency ensures a clear understanding of your true revenue streams.


#### 2. Expenses:


Track every operational cost tied to your Amazon business, including:


- **Fulfillment fees** : Charges for Amazon’s FBA services.


- **Subscription costs** : Monthly Seller Central fees.


- **Marketing expenses** : Costs for running Amazon ads or external campaigns.


- **Shipping and storage charges** : Fees for warehousing and logistics.


Organize expense receipts with[digital tools like Expensify](https://www.expensify.com/) or Google Drive for efficient record-keeping and easy access during tax season.


#### 3. Inventory Management:


Inventory plays a crucial role in your books. Keep a meticulous record of purchase costs, stock levels, and unsold items.


Use inventory management tools that sync with your bookkeeping software to streamline SKU tracking, cost calculations, and profit analysis.


By systematically managing these aspects, you’ll master bookkeeping basics for Amazon sellers and set your business up for scalable success.

#### 1. Neglecting Reconciliation


Failing to reconcile Amazon payouts with your bank records can lead to costly discrepancies and financial confusion.


###### ✅ Solution:


To prevent this, implement a regular reconciliation process to ensure your records match, making it easier to identify errors and maintain accurate financials.

#### 2. Mixing Personal and Business Expenses


One of the biggest bookkeeping mistakes Amazon sellers make is mixing personal and business expenses.


While it may seem convenient to use your credit card for business purchases, doing so can lead to a tax-season nightmare.


###### ✅ Solution:


To simplify your finances and ensure accurate deductions, set up[separate business bank accounts](https://www.doola.com/go-mercury/) and credit cards.
