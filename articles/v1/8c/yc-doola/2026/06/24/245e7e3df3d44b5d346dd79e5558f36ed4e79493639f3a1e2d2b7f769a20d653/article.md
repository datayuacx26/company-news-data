## How to Connect doola MCP in Perplexity | 1 Minute


You can connect doola MCP to Perplexity using the Connectors option in your Perplexity Pro workspace.


Once connected, you’ll be able to access doola MCP inside Perplexity.


1. Open your **Perplexity Pro** workspace.
2. Go to **Settings → Connectors** .
3. In the Connectors search bar, search for **doola** .
4. Click the **+ Connect** button next to doola.
5. Sign in to your existing doola account, or create a new account if you don’t already have one.
6. Review the requested permissions and complete the OAuth authorization flow.
7. Once authorization is complete, you’ll be redirected back to Perplexity.
8. Verify that **doola** appears in your connected connectors list.


You’re now ready to use doola directly within Perplexity.
