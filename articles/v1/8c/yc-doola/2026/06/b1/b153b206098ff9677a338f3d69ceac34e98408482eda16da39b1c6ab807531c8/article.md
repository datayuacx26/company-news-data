## FAQs


#### What is an ITIN?


An Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) is a tax processing number issued by the IRS to individuals who are required to have a US taxpayer identification number but are not eligible to obtain a Social Security Number (SSN). It is used exclusively for federal tax reporting purposes.


#### Who needs an ITIN?


Foreign nationals who have US tax reporting obligations, including non-US members of US LLCs or corporations who receive income from the business, typically need an ITIN. You do not need an SSN or US residency to apply.


#### Can every company member apply for an ITIN?


Not necessarily. ITIN eligibility depends on whether a member has a US tax filing requirement.


doola’s dashboard identifies which of your company’s members are eligible so you don’t have to determine this on your own.


#### How do I know which members are eligible?


Once you[log into your doola dashboard](https://app.doola.com/signup/registration) , eligible members are automatically surfaced within the ITIN section. You’ll see clearly who can apply and who already has an active application or issued ITIN.


#### Can I apply for an ITIN directly inside doola?


Yes, you can. The entire application flow, from eligibility check through passport upload to payment, is now available inside the doola dashboard.


#### What documents are required for ITIN registration?


The primary document required is a valid passport. doola’s guided flow walks each applicant through exactly what’s needed and where to upload it securely.


#### Can I track each ITIN application separately?


Yes, absolutely. Each eligible member now has their own individual application with its own status tracking.


You can monitor every application independently from submission to issuance.


#### How long does ITIN processing take?


ITIN processing times vary and are ultimately determined by the IRS.


Typical timelines range from 7 to 11 weeks, though this can vary depending on the time of year and application volume.

Yes, once an ITIN is issued, you’ll receive a notification directly inside the doola dashboard, there’s no need to manually check or follow up.
