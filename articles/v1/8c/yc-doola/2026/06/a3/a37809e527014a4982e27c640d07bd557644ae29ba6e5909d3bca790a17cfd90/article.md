## Step-by-Step: How to Connect doola MCP to Vercel v0


The video above walks through the full setup.


**Step-by-Step**


1. Open v0.
2. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** .
3. Click **Add MCP** .
4. Select **Custom MCP Server** .
5. Enter:


- **Name:** doola
- **MCP Server URL:**` https://mcp.doola.com`


6. Select **OAuth** as the authentication method.
7. Click **Add** .
8. Sign in to your existing doola account or create a new doola account.
9. Complete the OAuth authorization flow.
10. Start a new conversation.
