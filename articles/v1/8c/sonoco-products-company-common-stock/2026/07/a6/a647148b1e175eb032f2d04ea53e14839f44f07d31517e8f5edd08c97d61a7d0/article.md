# Making Decisions Differently Workshops: Sonoco’s Manufacturing Safety Training Program

[Download Now](https://www.sonoco.com/sites/default/files/images/PDFs/The_Shrinking_Basket-Final.pdf)


Two forces are simultaneously impacting consumer grocery shopping: elevated food prices and the popularity of weight-loss medications. While brands and consumers navigate these trends, Sonoco explored the current behavioral shifts and how they impact brands.


**Every Grocery Purchase Becomes Deliberate.**


Impulse grocery purchases have all but disappeared. No longer seasonally stocking or overloading pantries, consumers are purchasing less and finishing those items before buying more. Creating a new baseline, this move to making smaller but more frequent purchases, reducing waste and scrutinizing items on the shelf has been reinforced by:


- An increase in eating food at home
- A decrease in the consumer sentiment index (consumer sentiment toward personal finances, business conditions and buying conditions)
- A larger increase in price growth than in volume growth
- A 50% increase in steel import tariffs


Today’s consumers, according to Circana’s 2025 tracking, are focused on **value per bite** . Following consumers’ lead as volume declines, canned food brands have a new opportunity: offering right-size packaging in smaller sizes. Taking advantage of this not only helps brands deliver more value but also positions can-packaged products as the obvious choice on grocery store shelves.


**GLP-1s Create an Opportunity for Canned Goods**


GLP-1 drugs are known to reduce appetites, decreasing calorie intake by 15-30%. As a result, consumers are eating less, eating less often and buying less food. This change alters their relationship with food and the center of the store. **This is actually good news** for brands willing to see the commercial opportunity.


GLP-1 medications reduce appetites across all nutrition, protein, fiber, vitamins, minerals, and empty calories, which provides an opportunity for food brands that offer exceptional nutritional value and empty-calorie snack foods.


**Steel Can Packaged Goods Offer Essential Nutrients**


When positioned correctly, steel packaged goods, like canned tomatoes and beans, can help consumers on GLP-1s manage their nutrient intake with some of the most nutrient-efficient foods offered in the grocery store due to:


- Being packed within hours of harvest
- Offering nutrient-dense, calorie-efficient foods
- Providing exceptional affordability


**Paper Can Packaged Goods Offer High-Value Snacking.**


While consumers on GLP-1 drugs may be consuming less empty-calorie snack food, they still desire snacks that deliver on value. Supporting the value-per-bite trend, paper cans offer customizable, smaller formats for snacks.
