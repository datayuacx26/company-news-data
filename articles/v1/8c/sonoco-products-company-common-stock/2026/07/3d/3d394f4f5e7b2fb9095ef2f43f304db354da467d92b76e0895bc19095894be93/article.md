# Food waste is changing consumer expectations — packaging plays a key role

## Reducing food waste at the point of use


Food waste is increasingly influencing how consumers choose where to shop and which brands to trust.


A recent report from[Too Good To Go](https://www.toogoodtogo.com/en-us) and[La Distribución Anged](https://anged.es/) found that 94% of consumers would be willing to switch supermarkets in favour of one that takes action to reduce food waste. It also found that 64% of consumers see supermarkets as key players in reducing waste.


These findings also suggest that consumer expectations do not always reflect where food waste occurs across the value chain. While supermarkets play an important role, a significant share of food waste occurs later, including in the home.


## Consumers expect practical, visible action


The food distribution sector has already made strong progress, with waste levels in retail below 1%, supported by improvements in stock management, logistics, packaging innovation and collaboration across the value chain.


This progress is not always visible to consumers. The same study found that 41% believe retailers do not communicate the action they take, and 52% think it is communicated only occasionally. That makes practical, easy-to-understand innovation especially important — and easy to see.


## Packaging supports waste reduction


Packaging plays a role beyond protecting products and supporting shelf life. It also influences how efficiently the product is used after opening.


At Sonoco, we focus on packaging innovation that delivers practical performance benefits across the food value chain.
