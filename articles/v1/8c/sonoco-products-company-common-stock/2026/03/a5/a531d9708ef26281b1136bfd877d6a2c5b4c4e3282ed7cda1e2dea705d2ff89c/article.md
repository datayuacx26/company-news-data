# Foodservice Trends Driving QSR Efficiency and Growth

As foodservice industry trends continue to evolve, quick-service restaurants (QSRs) are under growing pressure to deliver speed, consistency, and quality without adding complexity to operations. Trends in foodservice today demand smarter, more efficient solutions behind the counter, as operators navigate labor challenges and rising customer expectations.


In the fast-paced QSR world, food quality and speed must go hand in hand.[Sonoco Industrial & Specialty Plastic](https://www.sonoco.com/na/products/industrial-packaging/industrial-specialty-plastics) supports these needs with[custom smallwares](https://www.sonoco.com/na/products/industrial-packaging/industrial-specialty-plastics/foodservice-smallware) , from holding trays to seasoning dispensers, that improve consistency, prevent sogginess, and extend hold times.


We spoke with Sonoco foodservice expert Scott Peterson about the latest QSR trends and solutions shaping the industry.


### What are the biggest trends shaping foodservice today?


One of the biggest foodservice industry trends is the demand for speed and consistency. QSRs must deliver the same high-quality experience every time; repeatability is essential. Customers expect consistent quality with every visit, regardless of location or time of day.


Restaurants are also increasingly adopting technology and automation. From smart kitchen tools to custom trays and other QSR products, these innovations help reduce labor strain, improve efficiency, and meet rising customer expectations.


### What are the most common pain points you hear from QSR brands?


Maintaining product integrity during hold times is a major challenge. Holding cabinets, though essential for throughput, can cause moisture buildup, uneven temperatures, and quality loss, especially during peak hours.


To address this, many QSRs are turning to heat-resistant trays that preserve texture and taste, along with[high-heat seasoning dispensers](https://www.sonoco.com/sites/default/files/PDFs/AccuSalt_Short%20Product%20Flyer_FINAL.pdf) that ensure consistent flavor.[Custom compartmentalized trays](https://www.sonoco.com/sites/default/files/images/PDFs/Sonoco-Sonoware%20Updated%20Flyer-Proof.pdf) allow operators to keep multiple SKUs fresh within a single footprint, supporting speed, flow, and food quality.


### How do Sonoco’s high-heat plastic trays outperform traditional metal trays?


Traditional metal trays tend to heat unevenly, which can cause food to stick, overcook, or dry out. Sonoco’s high-heat plastic trays offer more consistent temperature control, helping preserve texture while reducing sticking and waste.


They’re also more durable, easier to clean, and safer for staff to handle. Designed for long-term performance, these trays are well suited for a wide range of QSR environments and operational demands.
