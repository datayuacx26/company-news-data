# Sonoco Celebrates 125 Years of Better Packaging. Better Life.®


May 3, 2024


Sonoco’s roots go back to 1899—125 years ago. As we celebrate this meaningful milestone, we’re reflecting on our history of people building businesses by doing the right thing.


Explore our story that spans from our global team of approximately 23,000 across more than 300 operations back to our founding team of 12 in a rented warehouse in Hartsville, South Carolina.
