# Denny Sanford PREMIER Center Selects Daktronics to Refresh Digital Display Experience for Visitors

# Denny Sanford PREMIER Center Selects Daktronics to Refresh Digital Display Experience for Visitors


### Centerhung with continuous curve being installed in 2025


BROOKINGS, S.D. – Aug. 18, 2025 –[Daktronics](http://www.daktronics.com/) (NASDAQ-DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, is once again providing LED technology at the Denny Sanford PREMIER Center in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, as a digital refresh of the original installation for the building’s opening in 2014. The project includes a continuously-curved centerhung video display being installed this summer and 11 courtside scorers tables that will be delivered ahead of the Summit League Tournament in March.


“Fan experience is a top priority for the Denny Sanford PREMIER Center,” said a City of Sioux Falls representative. “The new centerhung video display and courtside scorers tables are a significant improvement in clarity and size from those they replace. These new assets will provide an improved fan experience that visitors will appreciate.”


**Centerhung Details**


Featuring one large display with a continuous curve, the centerhung will deliver 1,775 square feet of digital content. The display will measure 18 feet high by 98.5 feet in circumference and will feature a 3.9-millimeter pixel spacing to bring excellent image clarity and contrast to visitors in every seat of the venue.


The display totals more than 10.8 million pixels to show live video, instant replays, game information, statistics, graphics, animations and sponsorship messages, depending on the needs of the event. It is capable of variable content zoning to show one large image on each side of the display or to show multiple zones of digital content simultaneously.


“Daktronics is thrilled to once again partner with the Denny Sanford PREMIER Center and the City of Sioux Falls on these exciting upgrades,” said Matt Warnke, Daktronics sales representative. “The new displays, including the new centerhung, will provide for more flexibility in promotion of their events, sponsors and in-game enhancement. Having the opportunity to showcase our latest product offerings and highlight what our employees help create makes projects like these even more memorable.”


**Scorers Table Details**


Eleven LED scorers tables will be delivered ahead of the basketball tournament in March. Each table measures approximately 2.5 feet high by nearly 10 feet wide and each features a 3.9-millimeter pixel spacing. These can be organized in any configuration, including multiple tables on each side of the court, to appeal to fans with supplemental statistics, graphics and game information.


Included with the equipment installation, Denny Sanford PREMIER Center will receive a content package that will be produced and delivered by Daktronics Creative Services to help bring the displays to life.
