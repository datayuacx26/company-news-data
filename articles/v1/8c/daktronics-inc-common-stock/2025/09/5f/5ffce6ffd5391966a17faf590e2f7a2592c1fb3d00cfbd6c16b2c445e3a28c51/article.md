# Daktronics Women in Sports Online Event Returns in October

# Daktronics Women in Sports Online Event Returns in October


### Lisa Pinkston joins the event to share experiences as Athletic Director for Assumption High School


BROOKINGS, S.D. – Sept. 17, 2025 –[Daktronics](http://www.daktronics.com/) (NASDAQ-DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, has scheduled the next Women in Sports Online Event for October 7 at 10:30 a.m. CT with Assumption High School (AHS) Athletic Director Lisa Pinkston. She is slated to share her experiences in leading her high school in Kentucky to multiple state championships this past year.


Pinkston leads the largest all-girls Catholic high school in Louisville, Kentucky, and prides herself on a leadership style that fosters a championship culture. This is evidenced by the six state championships the high school was awarded this past school year for field hockey, cross country, dance, volleyball, track and field, and softball. As an all-female school, AHS is dedicated to providing exceptional opportunities for young women to pursue careers in sports and related fields.


“I’m excited to chat with Lisa Pinkston and learn more about her all-female production team and the impact of Rockets Taking Initiative - real-world experience like that is so valuable for students,” said Val Ellens, services marketing for Daktronics.


To register for the October event and hear about Pinkston making game day an exciting experience for both athletes and fans,[click here](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/events/women-in-sports) .
