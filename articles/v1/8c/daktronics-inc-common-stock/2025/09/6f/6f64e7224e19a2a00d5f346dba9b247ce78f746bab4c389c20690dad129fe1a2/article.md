# Daktronics Delivers 80-Foot Viewing Experience at Circa Kentucky Downs

# Daktronics Delivers 80-Foot Viewing Experience at Circa Kentucky Downs


### Four displays installed to engage viewers at Circa Sports Kentucky’s sportsbook


BROOKINGS, S.D. – Sept. 11, 2025 –[Daktronics](http://www.daktronics.com/) (NASDAQ-DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, partnered with[Circa Sports Kentucky](https://www.circasports.com/circa-sportsbook-kentucky) to deliver a state-of-the-art[video display](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/photos?k=kentucky%20downs&project=205049-006&media=WP-026275) in their sportsbook at The Mint Gaming Hall Kentucky Downs in Franklin. The sportsbook features an 80-foot-wide display, a 31-foot-wide odds board and two ticker displays for a 5,100-square-foot visual experience. Displays were installed over the winter months as the space is now open to visitors in Kentucky.


“We are thrilled to continue our partnership with Daktronics, the world leader in dynamic sports & entertainment audiovisual solutions," said Circa and Circa Sports CEO Derek Stevens. "I believe that some of Circa's most electrifying features at our properties throughout Nevada and now in Kentucky, are our Daktronics video walls. They continue to deliver reliable video products that offer our guests such exclusive experiences, and we are excited to bring that to guests at Circa Sports Kentucky.”


Circa Sports Kentucky now features the largest sportsbook in the state of Kentucky and one of the largest sportsbooks east of the Mississippi River. The sportsbook had been in a soft launch mode leading up to its official ribbon-cutting event in late August 2025.


**Sportsbook Display Details**


The four displays at the Kentucky Downs location combine for 2,000+ total square feet:


- Main sportsbook: 16 feet high by 80 feet wide featuring 1.9-millimeter pixel spacing
- Main oddsbook: 16 feet high by 32 feet wide featuring 2.5-millimeter pixel spacing
- Sportsbook ticker: 2 feet high by 117 feet wide featuring 2.5-millimeter pixel spacing
- Vestibule ticker: 1 foot high by 66 feet wide featuring 2.5-millimeter pixel spacing


The main sportsbook is the visual centerpiece that shares live video of races and sporting events for viewers to keep updated on all the action right before their eyes. The odds board lets audiences know their options and helps make informed betting decisions. The ticker displays ensure all the up-to-date information is available at all times.


“The emphasis Circa Sports places on quality of technology and the customer experience is second to none,” said Josh Francois, Daktronics regional sales manager. “We are thrilled to extend our partnership with Circa Sports throughout the country and to help them provide cutting-edge sports entertainment.”


Daktronics and Circa have a great partnership that includes top-tier projects at[Circa Resort & Casino](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/photos?k=Circa&project=205049-003&media=WP-024091) ,[Circa Silverton](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/photos?k=silverton&project=205049%20001&media=WP-026279) ,[The D Casino & Hotel](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/photos?k=The%20D&project=205123-001&media=WP-024587) ,[Golden Gate Hotel & Casino](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/photos?k=Golden%20Gate&project=205123&media=WP-024589) , all in Las Vegas,[Legends Bay Casino](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/photos?k=legends%20bay&project=214745-001&media=WP-024851) in Sparks, Nevada, and[The Pass Casino](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/photos?k=henderson&project=156907-001&media=WP-024590) in Henderson, Nevada.
