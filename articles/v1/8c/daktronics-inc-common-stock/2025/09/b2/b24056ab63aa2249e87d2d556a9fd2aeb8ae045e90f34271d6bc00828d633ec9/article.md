# Daktronics Releases Data Studio Volleyball for 2025 Season

# Daktronics Releases Data Studio Volleyball for 2025 Season


### Volleyball is the latest sport to receive this cloud-based, real-time data interface


BROOKINGS, S.D. – Sept. 29, 2025 – Expanding its Data Studio solution,[Daktronics](http://www.daktronics.com/) (NASDAQ-DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, has announced the release of this cloud-based, real-time data interface to support the sport of volleyball beginning in 2025. This solution integrates directly with Daktronics Show Control, an industry-leading production solution. It is now available to support collegiate indoor and beach volleyball.


“This new innovation for volleyball is the next step in bringing a comprehensive stats platform to our customers,” said Bill Hadsell, Daktronics Data Studio product manager. “We’re excited to expand into the sport of volleyball to bring dynamic storytelling capabilities to customers using this additional data and information to bring their fans closer to the action.”


Data Studio Volleyball is part of Daktronics control solution ecosystem in Venus Control Suite. It ensures game-day producers now have seamless access to volleyball data and information necessary for delivering game-day experiences that are memorable and engaging.


The solution provides a comprehensive stats platform designed for volleyball by delivering game information, scoreboard data, player information, team standings and out-of-town scores. This includes all the statistics and details fans expect to see at a volleyball game, including game score, match score, blocks, digs and more.


“Visuals you can now create with the data available are sure to create an even more engaging and energizing experience inside the arena,” added Hadsell. “Bringing the stats and visuals fans commonly see on a television broadcast to the video board in-arena helps deliver more memorable moments that will keep fans coming back again and again to experience it in person.”


Volleyball joins the family of Data Studio sport interfaces. Contact Daktronics for more information about[Data Studio Volleyball](https://daktronics.widen.net/s/cgfccwlwfc/cutsheet_data-studio-vb) , Basketball or Hockey today.
