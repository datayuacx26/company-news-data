# Daktronics to Ring Nasdaq Stock Market Closing Bell on April 8, 2026

## Daktronics to Ring Nasdaq Stock Market Closing Bell on April 8, 2026


April 2, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.daktronics.com/node/21676/pdf)


**Bell ceremony precedes Daktronics’ 2026 Investor Day on April 9**


BROOKINGS, S.D., April 02, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Daktronics](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=DtXVsmBzg6P-5-io_iiFQaXDX3AYBj6GT8_Xv5GzwL-npY2VR5RKIHmUDUWmmwGeeUyigZcjFbNOEpsT-Q-bcQ==) (NASDAQ: DAKT), the leading U.S.-based designer and manufacturer of best-in-class dynamic video communication displays and control systems for customers worldwide, today announced the company will ring the Closing Bell at the Nasdaq MarketSite in New York’s Times Square on Apr. 8, 2026.


The ceremony will be led by Ramesh Jayaraman, president and CEO of Daktronics, accompanied by members of the company’s leadership team, Board of Directors, employees and other key stakeholders who have been instrumental in supporting Daktronics’ journey.


“Ringing the Nasdaq Closing Bell will be an exciting milestone for our team and an important moment for our shareholders,” said Jayaraman, who[began in this role on Feb. 2, 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=yeU-6Zs_8vHJTi_MxhOLKyKJBjDuiF5W4ea6wNDRFAtA2Hq5u-ZvGdYq6lGdHj4fxGJW-6MgLeacWmhWNNYbZgnxwXDhOndqff28KkHQX8gi6HhKAb9m68uSwQpNKtLV8gtXT9yZ2z6XCpCM9W3bcu-9zuIBsOcuW9yNEctJERc2Atz3loplG5te8amreUPoggbaFo5Q83u7PSCz2yDhRyrrTyu18w8zK6VHsLKaSb5zk3kI_92IrdyPxYJkMPqU) . “We are on a journey to accelerate profitable growth, and it’s events like this that bring us all together to celebrate both how far we’ve come and to focus on our bright future.”


The ceremony comes on the heels of Daktronics announcing its[2026 Fiscal Third Quarter Results](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=RR2cxiScP3yEsa2wX_E8JIsO4Pu3dj6bsENw9SbZ-ZUjLP17EVz4O6KV1wXlJVtMbj90U4kj05h4oSE1_xKgUBsXmbcxu-2YqdPqTWfN2ifGC-2L46etB8pYNCmpYgb53vOtrI5hR_uxDu64WxoYX6Nj5Kc5lGic1v4AXJPRqPkZEKiMDXQzBM8j77CG6lYPpPPweDuyE9jusS5LYLV_j-BqimtAWpeC4cluWKHPFZSjUid9-PvUdYcyXpiJpZy-) and in advance of the company’s 2026 Investor Day – also at the Nasdaq MarketSite – on April 9.


Daktronics went public on the Nasdaq Stock Exchange in 1994.


The live broadcast of the Nasdaq Closing Bell ceremony will begin at 3:45 p.m. Eastern Time, with the bell scheduled to ring at 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time. To view the broadcast, visit:[https://www.nasdaq.com/marketsite/bell-ringing-ceremony](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=28PDojS1WmkGpQwe_zV-3UaSA-bYD24t2BuuIlosz2Da0MHBLR0o-Xy18pg-cQhvsh-sysOn1W3Wm2XRG3ewEuYvOAktIkM0X6iIa_CogDfPeKUKveZvewQV5v0p3jP9z8N5VW0YJPkIKO4NxmxWk4I0c0h55X2E5mOQiewIKdoUlESzT_af0JVmlhZG6uLm) .


Institutional investors and analysts who wish to attend Investor Day in person may register in advance by contacting Investor Relations at[investor@daktronics.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=nO_Ca99Ebp7THHreiIIqU7O9L24NlrlTaFh3SGW1i1mMj63QfyoZm3B8-i8Pok208rjNQ3oUoNGHIvBOe8FCiMB37NEXlAlERuuy_U232ok=) .
