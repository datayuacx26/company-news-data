# Daktronics to Join Grass Valley at NAB in Las Vegas

# Daktronics to Join Grass Valley at NAB in Las Vegas


### Latest integrations and COB dvLED technology on display in booth C2408


BROOKINGS, S.D. – Apr. 13, 2026 –[Daktronics](http://www.daktronics.com/) (NASDAQ: DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, will be at the[NAB Show](https://www.nabshow.com/) in partnership with Grass Valley at their booth C2408 inside the Las Vegas Convention Center from April 19 to 22. Daktronics and Grass Valley team members will be available to discuss their latest integration and products as well as network with individuals within the industry.


“We’re excited to continue our partnership with Grass Valley, further enhancing our integration together and sharing this integration with the industry at NAB in Las Vegas,” said Daktronics President and CEO Ramesh Jayaraman. “Combining our LED display and content management system with Grass Valley’s live production technology transforms the fan experience at premier live event venues, and we’re looking forward to our teams discussing this integration with visitors to our booth at this show.”


Daktronics will be showcasing a 1.2-millimeter[COB (Chip On Board) display](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/products/video-displays/video-wall/chip-on-board) as a testament to the company’s commitment to innovation of reliable products and dedication to customer service beyond the initial installation. This dvLED display will measure 9 feet high by 26 feet wide and demonstrate smoother, more uniform image quality with reduced glare and minimal color shift, ensuring content looks intentional and not distracting under studio lights or in high-end environments.


“Partnerships like this are about making live production and display work as one cohesive system, not disparate parts,” said Jon Wilson, CEO of Grass Valley. “By combining Daktronics’ dvLED technology with our live production capabilities, we enable the world’s leading venues to deliver consistent, high-quality visuals that support the content and scale required for every audience. NAB Show an important opportunity to show how this integration works in practice as we showcase the combined strength of our growing partnership.”


“This delivers the desired immersive experience in a seamless manner that people expect from today’s video screen technology,” added Jayaraman.


To learn more, visit Daktronics and Grass Valley in Las Vegas at NAB. Visitors can register for NAB Show 2026 using guest pass code NS9393.


### About Grass Valley


Grass Valley empowers over 90% of the world’s top media brands to create and deliver compelling stories that captivate audiences. At the heart of its solutions is the GV Media Universe (GVMU), combining advanced hardware solutions, with the agility, elasticity, and adaptability of the AMPP Media Operating System. Together, they form a seamless, scalable ecosystem for efficient and innovative workflows.


With a portfolio spanning from camera to consumer, Grass Valley provides the flexibility to build solutions for every need – from a single creative harnessing immense production power to globally distributed teams collaborating seamlessly. This breadth of capability enables customers to craft efficient workflows that inspire and engage their audiences.


With over 65 years of expertise, Grass Valley has consistently driven the evolution of live content creation and storytelling. Headquartered in Montreal and supported by Black Dragon Capital, the company remains dedicated to shaping and leading the media revolution.

### About Daktronics


Daktronics helps its customers to impact their audiences throughout the world with large-format LED video displays, message displays, scoreboards, digital billboards, audio systems and control systems in sport, business and transportation applications. Founded in 1968 as a USA-based manufacturing company, Daktronics has grown into the world leader in audiovisual systems and implementation with offices around the globe.
