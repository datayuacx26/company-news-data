# Daktronics Hires Jeff Taggart to Serve High Schools and Community Facilities

# Daktronics Hires Jeff Taggart to Serve High Schools and Community Facilities


### Eastern Missouri and southern Illinois are Taggart’s region of focus


BROOKINGS, S.D. – Apr. 16, 2026 –[Daktronics](http://www.daktronics.com/) (NASDAQ: DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, announces Jeff Taggart as the newest sales representative supporting high schools and community facilities in eastern Missouri and southern Illinois.


In this role, Taggart will partner with athletic directors, school administrators, activity coordinators and local sign companies as they explore upgrades to their scoreboards, timing systems, and LED video displays. He will serve as the primary contact for project questions, product recommendations and guidance on Daktronics’ industry-leading solutions designed specifically for high school sports and community recreation venues.


As a new member of the High School, Parks & Recreation team, Taggart brings a customer-focused approach centered on communication, collaboration and ensuring schools receive the support they need from project planning through installation and long-term service.


Daktronics looks forward to supporting schools and community partners throughout the region as they enhance game-day experiences, strengthen fan engagement and bring modern technology to their facilities.


If you have questions or would like additional information, please contact Taggart directly atjeff.taggart@daktronics.com .


### ​About Daktronics


Daktronics helps its customers to impact their audiences throughout the world with large-format LED video displays, message displays, scoreboards, digital billboards, audio systems and control systems in sport, business and transportation applications. Founded in 1968 as a USA-based manufacturing company, Daktronics has grown into the world leader in audiovisual systems and implementation with offices around the globe.
