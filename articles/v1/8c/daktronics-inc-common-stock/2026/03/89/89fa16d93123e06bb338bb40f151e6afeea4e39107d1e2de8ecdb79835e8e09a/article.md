# Daktronics and SEGD Celebrate Two Decades of Partnership


### SEGD welcomes Daktronics as a 2026 Platinum Industry Partner & as a Presenting Sponsor of Branded Environments, March 18-19


BROOKINGS, S.D. – Mar. 9, 2026 – As sports, entertainment and design intersect in new ways,


[Daktronics](http://www.daktronics.com/)


(NASDAQ: DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, has signed on as a Platinum Industry Partner with SEGD, which includes support of


[SEGD’s Branded Environments](https://segd.org/events/2026-segd-branded-environments/)


event. Taking place at the[Gensler office](https://www.gensler.com/projects/gensler-los-angeles)


in downtown Los Angeles on March 18-19, the event will feature talks and tours centered around one of the world’s most influential centers for sports culture, entertainment and immersive brand experiences. SEGD will be bringing together designers, brand leaders, architects, technologists and venue professionals to explore how experience design is reshaping audience connection across sports and entertainment.


SEGD Director of Sales & Marketing Kristin Bennani says, “ Branded Environments explores how the places built around sports, entertainment, and culture are evolving into civic gathering spaces—where fandom becomes a shared public experience and design shapes how people connect to one another and to place. This event brings together designers, architects, technologists, and brand leaders to explore how storytelling, media, and the built environment work together to create those moments. Daktronics has been a valued partner to SEGD for more than 20 years, supporting us in producing events that are experiences in themselves and helping bring the level of production value our members expect from a global community of designers of experiences.”


The event’s mission is to forge a dynamic and creative hub that examines brand and experiential activations in the realms of sports and entertainment. The gathering is intended to be a haven for industry experts, design enthusiasts and thought leaders to provide a platform for revolutionary design perspectives, innovative strategies and collaborative exchange.


“What you’re going to see at this event is industry leaders and like-minded people collaborating on ways to bring these areas of focus together in a more intentional way,” said Steve Bayer, Daktronics director of special projects. “As our company supports both industries, it’s a natural fit for us to support and participate in Branded Environments.”


The[2026 Branded Environments](https://daktronics.widen.net/s/nc8m5bljgv/video_daktronics-2026-segd-branded-environments_brand-reel)


registration is currently open and is a great fit for experience designers, brand strategists, architects, fabricators, technologists, sports and entertainment professionals, and anyone shaping how brands are experienced IRL. Attendees also have optional behind-the-scenes tours available of Crypto.com Arena, BMO Stadium, Rose Bowl Stadium or an HKS_MADE (materials, applications & digital exploration) lab. Register today by


[clicking here](https://segd.glueup.com/event/2026-segd-branded-environments-163353/)


.


In addition to being a presenting sponsor at Branded Environments, Daktronics is also supporting


[SEGD’s Xlab Conference](https://segd.org/events/2026-segd-xlab/)


in NYC as the Closing Mixer Sponsor and as the Digital Stage Screen Provider for the Annual


[2026 SEGD Conference Experience Kansas City](https://segd.org/events/2026-segd-conference-experience-kansas-city/)


, being held October 22-24. Daktronics has provided digital display technology for SEGD’s annual conference for more than a decade, helping to bring energy and visibility to the stage and programming.


### **About SEGD**


SEGD (the Society for Experiential Graphic Design) is the global community of designers of experiences connecting people to place. We are a multidisciplinary group of graphic and exhibition designers, fabricators and architects, media developers and creative technologists, students and educators. Each of our members brings a diverse set of expertise, but we all share a common motivation: to make the built environment more inclusive and intuitive, emotive and engaging, sustainable and shared. For over fifty years, SEGD has been the go-to resource for wayfinding, placemaking, and experience design. Policymakers and developers look to us as thought leaders and expert collaborators. Find out more at


[www.segd.org](http://www.segd.org/)


.


### About Daktronics


Daktronics helps its customers to impact their audiences throughout the world with large-format LED video displays, message displays, scoreboards, digital billboards, audio systems and control systems in sport, business and transportation applications. Founded in 1968 as a USA-based manufacturing company, Daktronics has grown into the world leader in audiovisual systems and implementation with offices around the globe.
