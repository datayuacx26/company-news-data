# Bowling Green State University Set to Unveil New Daktronics Display at Doyt Perry Stadium


### Elevated fan experience coming to football and other events held at the venue starting this fall


BROOKINGS, S.D. – July 13, 2026 –[Daktronics](http://www.daktronics.com/) (NASDAQ: DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, was selected by Bowling Green State University (BGSU) to manufacture and install a new LED video display at Doyt Perry Stadium in Bowling Green, Ohio. The installation will be completed ahead of the fall 2026 sports season to deliver an elevated fan experience for football and other special events.


"This project is a testament to our mission to enhance our fan experience within our venues," said BGSU Vice President of Athletics Strategy Derek van der Merwe. "We are looking forward to witnessing what the new technology will bring to our football game days and how the new video board will engage our fans in unique and exciting ways."


**Video Display Details**


The south end zone video display will now measure roughly 31 feet high by 39 feet wide and will feature a 10-millimeter pixel spacing to deliver excellent image clarity and contrast to fans throughout the stadium’s seating bowl. It is capable of variable content zoning allowing it to show one large image or a combination of graphics, statistics, data and sponsorship messages.


"We are excited for our fans to be able to experience game day in a new and enhanced way at the Doyt," said Mike and Jan Wilcox Head Football Coach Eddie George. "The increased size of the video board will only help our already incredible atmosphere and grow our home field advantage. We appreciate and are grateful for everyone that is helping make this possible."


Powering the display, Daktronics installed a custom control solution with[Data Studio Football](https://www.daktronics.com/news/daktronics-introduces-data-studio-football-for-college-marketplace) . This cloud-based, real-time data interface integrates with the control solution and third-party data sources to deliver relevant stats and information to create engaging and memorable game-day presentations.


“Bowling Green State University has been an outstanding partner with Daktronics through the years, and this upgrade to its football stadium continues an outstanding relationship,” said Joe Hernandez, Daktronics sales representative. “Daktronics was able to provide the Falcons with the perfect product at Doyt Perry Stadium that will enhance the game-day experience for all its fans. As a member of the Mid-American Conference, it is necessary for Daktronics to provide the best product at the best price, and that combination was delivered with this upgrade.”


Daktronics has grown with the sports industry from the company’s beginning in 1968. Today, the company has LED video display installations at hundreds of colleges and universities across the United States. For more information on what Daktronics can provide for the collegiate market, visit[www.daktronics.com/college](http://www.daktronics.com/college) .


### About Bowling Green State University Football


Entering the 108th season in program history, the Bowling Green State University Football program been a staple in the Mid-American Conference for decades. With over 570 wins all-time, the Falcons have accumulated more than 60 winning seasons in the program's history as well as 12 MAC Championships, 16 bowl appearances and the 1959 National College Division National Championship title. Led by head coach Eddie George, now entering his second season at the helm of the Falcons, Bowling Green is set to open their season at home against Tarleton State on Sept. 5.
