# Daktronics and Grass Valley Win the Best Solution for Large Venues or Live Events at InfoComm from rAVe Pubs


BROOKINGS, S.D. – June 25, 2026 – At[InfoComm 2026](https://www.infocommshow.org/) in Las Vegas,[Daktronics](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us) (NASDAQ: DAKT) of Brookings, South Dakota, and[Grass Valley](https://www.grassvalley.com/) were presented the[rAVe Pubs](https://ravepubs.com/) Best Solution for Large Venue or Live Events Award. The recognition was presented at the show and highlights the companies’ integration, uniting Daktronics Show Control solution with Grass Valley’s live broadcast technology into a single in-venue workflow.


“We’re honored to be recognized for our efforts with Grass Valley as we partner together to deliver best-in-class experience to the live-event industry,” said Daktronics President and CEO Ramesh Jayaraman. “We’re very excited about how this integration will deliver a positive impact on live event operations.”


The integration focuses on production and display environments for live event venues and brings them into a seamless workflow with tighter synchronizations and precision timing for content playback. This API-level integration connects Grass Valley’s production switchers and control systems into Daktronics Show Control and Display Studio environments, allowing operators to manage both solutions in one place.


Daktronics and Grass Valley[announced this integration](https://www.daktronics.com/news/daktronics-to-join-grass-valley-at-nab-in-las-vegas) earlier in 2026.
