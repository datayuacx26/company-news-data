# Voker Fest 2026 Recap

Last week, we celebrated the launch of our new agent analytics platform with Voker Fest! We took over a stunning Oakland mansion overlooking the bay with San Francisco glittering in the background and welcomed over 100 people to celebrate with us before our Product Hunt launch.


‍


‍


We went all out: a fire dancer, a magician, a DJ so good people were actually dancing (a genuine rarity at a tech event), and a tarot reader who never had fewer than 10 people in line. We kept the vibes on-brand with signature cotton candy, and our creative director — who also designs merch for Meow Wolf — put together free swag that was too good to leave behind. Oh, and of course unlimited tacos and an open bar didn't hurt either. For anyone coming from SF, we ran a party bus across the bridge so no one had to figure out logistics on their own.


‍


‍


Voker Fest was exactly what we hoped it would be: a real night out with the kind of people who get what we're building. We're so grateful to everyone who came, danced, grabbed a taco, and helped make this launch so much fun! Maybe we should make this a yearly thing?
