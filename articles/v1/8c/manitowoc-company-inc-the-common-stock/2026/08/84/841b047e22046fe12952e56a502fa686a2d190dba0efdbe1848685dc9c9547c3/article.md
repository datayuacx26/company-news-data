# J&D Pierce receives more Grove all-terrain cranes to create UK’s largest fleet

Outside of the cranes’ features and benefits, another factor in J&D Pierce’s ongoing investment in Grove is the strong aftermarket support provided by Manitowoc.


“The level of service from Manitowoc is second-to-none, and the aftersales support has always been exceptional. Whether it’s technical advice, parts support, or service assistance, the Manitowoc team is always available. Strong manufacturer support helps maximize uptime and maintain project schedules so we can continue to deliver efficiently and productively for our clients,” said Jamie Pierce.
Customer support for J&D Pierce and other crane owners in the UK is further strengthened, with the launch of[MGX Equipment Services in the UK](https://www.mgxequipment.com/blog/mgx-equipment-services-launches-in-the-uk/) in July 2026. MGX is a wholly-owned subsidiary of The Manitowoc Company and will now offer comprehensive equipment support to crane owners, contractors and rental companies across the UK, for all brands of cranes.


J&D Pierce was founded in 1975 and is one of the UK’s leading structural steelwork contractors, working on some of the country's most iconic construction projects. Since purchasing its first GMK3050 in 1999, J&D Pierce has acquired almost 30 Grove all-terrain cranes ranging from 50 t to 250 t capacity.


The long-standing relationship reflects J&D Pierce's confidence in both the equipment and the support provided by the Manitowoc team, said Derek Kidd, the Company’s UK (North) sales manager.


“Congratulations to Jamie and the team at J&D Pierce on their latest Grove deliveries. We are grateful for their ongoing trust and look forward to supporting the company’s crane fleet as the business continues to grow.”
