# How accessories from Potain keep tower cranes running smoothly

## Putting people first


Several ancillary products are designed to support those who assemble, service, and operate the crane. This includes a partnership with GRIPPS, a leader in engineered drop-prevention solutions and tool-tethering equipment. A full selection of grapples, wristbands, tethers, grippers, tool catchers, and more is available for purchase through the global Potain dealer network to help technicians work more effectively and securely.


The same principle applies to the unique ProTECHtor™ system, which allows the technician to take full control of operations during maintenance, preventing untimely crane movements that could endanger workers. With ProTECHtor™, the technician can stop crane movements or remotely authorize them at pre-set speeds – and there’s an emergency stop. The system works with top-slewing and self-erecting tower cranes, either with or without CCS (Crane Control System) technology, and it can be purchased as a portable system or included as an in-built feature on new cranes.


For the operator, Potain also now offers greater comfort with autonomous urinals located next to the cab. These chemical-free units need no water or drainage, and are odorless and self-cleaning. Installation is quick and easy: the 700 mm x 300 mm unit weighs just 15 kg and requires only a 230 V socket.


“The fundamentals of construction success are always tied to the performance and reliability of the tower crane. But having the right tools, training, and support to back up the tower crane can make a substantial difference. That’s why Potain’s customer support is focused on all aspects of crane ownership, maintenance, and operation. When a crane performs to the best of its ability, the whole project benefits,” said Jérôme Chanel, VP Sales & Aftermarket, Potain Europe.
