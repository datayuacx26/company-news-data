# Wagenborg Nedlift makes strong addition to the fleet with a new Grove GMK5250XL-1 for projects in Germany and the Netherlands

Powering the GMK5250XL-1 is a Mercedes Benz OM471LA six cylinder diesel engine. It offers 360 kW of power at 1,600 rpm, while a maximum torque of 2,600 Nm is possible at 1,300 rpm. It’s paired with a Mercedes G 280 transmission offering 16 forward and two reverse gears.


In addition to its impressive powertrain, the GMK5250XL-1 provides the mobility and flexibility that Wagenborg Nedlift needs. Its compact dimensions make traveling through urban areas effortless. Operators also benefit from a premium working environment, which includes a 20° tilting superstructure cab, a suspended seat, and the intuitive CCS crane control system. These features ensure smoother handling and greater precision, resulting in consistently positive feedback from the crane operating team.


Wagenborg Nedlift is a leading provider of crane and heavy-lift logistics as well as transport solutions in the Netherlands, Germany, and other European markets. With a modern fleet, strong safety culture, and a clear focus on efficiency and sustainability, Wagenborg Nedlift delivers lifting and transport projects for industry, construction, and infrastructure – from planning to execution, all from one source.
