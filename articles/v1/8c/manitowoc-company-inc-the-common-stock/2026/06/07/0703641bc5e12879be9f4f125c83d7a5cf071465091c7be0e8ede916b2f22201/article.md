# Two Grove GMK5150XL cranes power offshore platform construction in Azerbaijan

The GMK5150XL is a class leader, with a 68.7 m main boom – the longest for a 150 t mobile crane on a five-axle chassis. Its extended reach enables HAREKET to perform specialized lifts at height using only the main boom, reducing the need for a jib. This significantly reduces setup time, improving efficiency on the space-constrained site in the Caspian Sea.


With a 69-year legacy, Turkish company HAREKET operates an extensive network across Eurasia, the Middle East, and Africa. Following the gas platform project in Azerbaijan, the company will use the GMK5150XL all-terrain cranes for other applications, including wind turbine assembly, refinery maintenance, and shipyard operations. HAREKET’s latest acquisitions underscore Grove’s growing influence in the Caspian energy corridors, where high-capacity, versatile lifting solutions are essential for the next generation of infrastructure.


“The GMK5150XL is an incredibly versatile asset. Its combination of long boom and compact footprint makes it the ideal solution for energy and infrastructure sectors,” said Cihan Bektaş, after-sales service manager at Karun.
