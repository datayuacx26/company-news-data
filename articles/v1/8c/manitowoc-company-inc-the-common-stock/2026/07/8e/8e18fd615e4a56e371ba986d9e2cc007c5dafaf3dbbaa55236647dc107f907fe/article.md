# Manitowoc employees unite through “Empowering Warriors & Raising Hope” initiative to support K9s For Warriors

Across all 17 MGX Equipment Services locations, 358 employees participated in a “22 Lunch” fundraiser, contributing $2.22 for a hot dog, chips, and a soda.


Employees at Manitowoc’s corporate office in Milwaukee also supported the effort. On June 9, 40 employees packed 22 backpacks with supplies and collectively completed 22 laps along a designated walking path.


Employees and supporters also purchased 45 limited-edition “22” shirts, with proceeds benefiting K9s For Warriors. Employee contributions and branch fundraising generated just over $4,000, while the Manitowoc Foundation committed an additional $10,000, bringing the current contribution to more than $14,000.


“Empowering Warriors & Raising Hope gives our employees a meaningful way to come together in support of veterans and their families,” said Heather Monn, HR business partner and Manitowoc VERG chair. “The participation across Shady Grove, Milwaukee, and every MGX location shows how strongly our people believe in this cause. Whether they walked laps, packed backpacks, purchased a shirt, or made a donation, every contribution helped extend the impact of the campaign.”
