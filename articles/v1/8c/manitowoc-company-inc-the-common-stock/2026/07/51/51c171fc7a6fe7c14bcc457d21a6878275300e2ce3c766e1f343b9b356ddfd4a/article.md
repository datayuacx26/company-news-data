# UK’s Davies Crane Hire strengthens fleet with two high-capacity Grove cranes

The six-axle Grove GMK6300L-1 delivers outstanding load charts with its 80 m main boom, extendable to 120 m. This allows for high-performance lifting across multiple applications. The crane also offers fast, efficient setup, including counterweight rigging, along with a compact design that allows access to constrained jobsite areas.


Similarly, the five-axle Grove GMK5250XL-1 combines strong lifting capacity with extended reach. It features a 78.5 m main boom and can achieve up to 109 m of tip height when fitted with the jib and extensions. Based on Grove’s single-engine concept, the GMK5250XL-1 reduces fuel consumption by up to 30% compared to previous models, meaning lower emissions and operating costs for owners.


[Davies Crane Hire](https://www.daviescranehire.co.uk/) was founded in 1979 and is one of the largest independent crane rental companies in Wales. It has four depots in Carmarthen, Port Talbot, Pembroke, and Cardiff, from where it covers south and west Wales, as well as the wider UK.
