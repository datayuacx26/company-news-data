# UK’s Wire Cranes continues growth trajectory with Grove additions

- Grove five-axle GMK5150XL and GMK5250XL-1 models join the growing fleet of Wire Cranes to serve projects across the UK.
- Industry-leading customer support from Manitowoc helps Wire Cranes deliver first-class service to customers.


[Wire Cranes](https://wirecranes.com/) is continuing to make its mark in the UK crane rental industry with the addition of two new Grove all-terrain cranes. The company has added a GMK5150XL and a GMK5250XL-1, helping it handle more work and expand into heavy-lift applications as demand grows.


“We’re a young company, but our standards, investment, and ambition have been clear from day one. Our modern and versatile fleet provides customers with a one-stop solution, and the addition of the GMK5150XL and GMK5250XL-1 is another big step forward. As a business, we believe in investing in the right equipment, people, and support to ensure we deliver for our customers,” said Andy Unsworth, managing director at Wire Cranes.


The first job for the GMK5150XL was supporting construction on a precast-concrete project, where it demonstrated its speed, strength, and versatility. For the GMK5250XL-1, the first project was at a local industrial plant, where it supported ongoing site expansion, underscoring its capability for large-contract lifting and major project work.


Wire Cranes specified the new Grove cranes with a host of additional features to ensure maximum flexibility and performance. Added options include the MAXbase™ system for asymmetrical outrigger positioning for greater set-up flexibility and bi-fold swingaway jibs for higher reach.


The GMK5150XL is a class leader, with a 68.7 m main boom – the longest for a 150 t mobile crane on a five-axle chassis. The GMK5250XL-1 redefines the limits of five-axle mobile cranes. Its 78.5 m long boom is the longest in its class and can reach a tip height of up to 109 m with optional jib and boom extensions.
