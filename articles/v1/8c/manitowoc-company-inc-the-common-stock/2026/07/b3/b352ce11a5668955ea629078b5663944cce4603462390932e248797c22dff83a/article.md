# CST Transporte expands fleet with Grove GMK5200-1 to support high-performance lifting operations

CST Transporte will harness the crane’s built-in Grove CONNECT™ telematics system, providing real-time access to machine data to support preventive maintenance, improve fleet visibility, and enable simplified, more efficient communication with service teams. Further strengthening support for CST Transporte is Manitowoc’s local customer support network, which includes the German aftermarket headquarters in Lagenfeld, and a team of well-equipped mobile service technicians to ensure rapid response and maximum uptime.


“The Grove GMK5200-1 is a perfect fit for our operations. Its strong lifting capacities are crucial for our work in concrete and precast construction, and the ability to move the crane when rigged with a full counterweight provides us with a real efficiency gain. With Grove CONNECT™, we also have access to important machine data at all times and benefit from significantly simplified communication with the service team — a clear value add for our daily operations,” says Christian Schug, owner of CST Transporte.


CST Transporte supports customers across construction and infrastructure projects with modern equipment. In addition to crane rental, CST offers a range of construction services, supporting projects from planning through execution. The latest investment in a Grove GMK5200-1 underscores its commitment to delivering efficient, reliable lifting solutions for demanding applications.
