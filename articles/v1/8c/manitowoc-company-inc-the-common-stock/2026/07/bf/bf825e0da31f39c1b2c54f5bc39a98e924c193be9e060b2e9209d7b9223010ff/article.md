# Potain appoints PT Potaindo Machinery as dealer for Indonesia

- The partnership strengthens nationwide service and coverage for Potain customers across Indonesia.
- PT Potaindo Machinery will offer full sales and service support for customers, including expanded parts availability to help maximize crane uptime.


Potain has appointed[PT Potaindo Machinery](https://potaindo.com/) as its official dealer in Indonesia, providing sales, service, and parts support to customers nationwide. The appointment expands Potain’s presence in Indonesia, giving customers access to local expertise and on-the-ground assistance throughout the lifecycle of their cranes.


PT Potaindo Machinery has extensive experience with Potain tower cranes and a well-established team of sales, technical, and service personnel. The company works throughout the country and supports a range of construction projects and lifting applications.


Central to this partnership is PT Potaindo Machinery's commitment to its "3S" values: Safety, Service, and Satisfaction. By prioritizing these pillars, the company delivers premium, reliable services. Reflecting this, PT Potaindo Machinery maintains a comprehensive stock of genuine Potain spare parts. The company also offers crane rentals with experienced operators, providing customers with complete lifting solutions backed by local technical expertise.


“PT Potaindo Machinery brings strong technical expertise and a deep understanding of the local market, enabling us to deliver the reliable service our customers expect. This partnership ensures customers across Indonesia have access to responsive service, parts availability, and experienced teams,” said Johnny Kuah, regional sales manager, Potain.
