# How to Choose the Right Order Picker for High-Volume Warehouses

# How to Choose the Right Order Picker for High-Volume Warehouses


May 14, 2026


In a high-volume warehouse, every second counts. The faster and more accurately products move from storage to shipment, the more efficient the operation becomes. That’s why selecting the right order picker is an important decision for distribution centers, e-commerce fulfillment facilities, retail warehouses, and manufacturing operations.


## Start by Evaluating Your Picking Strategy


Before comparing equipment models, take a close look at how products move through your facility.


Questions to consider include:


- How high are inventory locations?


- Are operators picking full pallets or individual items?


- How narrow are warehouse aisles?


- What percentage of orders are picked daily?


- Are most picks performed at ground level or elevated positions?


The answers can help determine whether a low-level order picker, high-level order picker, or narrow-aisle order picker is the best fit. For facilities experiencing rapid growth, reviewing current workflows may also reveal opportunities to improve efficiency through updated


[warehouse design](https://materialhandling.altg.com/warehouse-solutions/warehouse-design/) and equipment selection.


## Understanding Low-Level Order Pickers


A low-level order picker is commonly used for fast-moving inventory stored at lower rack levels. Operators can easily enter and exit the platform while picking individual cases or items throughout a shift.


These machines are often found in:


- Retail distribution centers


- Grocery warehouses


- E-commerce fulfillment operations


- Consumer goods facilities


Benefits of low-level order pickers include:


- Faster ground-level picking


- Reduced walking distances


- Improved order fulfillment efficiency


- Increased operator productivity


When the majority of inventory is stored within easy reach, a low-level unit may provide the best return on investment.


## When a High-Level Order Picker Makes Sense


Warehouses with significant vertical storage often require a high-level order picker to maximize available space. Unlike traditional forklifts, these machines elevate both the operator and load handling controls, allowing workers to access products stored at greater heights safely and efficiently.


A high-level order picker may be ideal when:


- Storage density is a priority


- Inventory is located multiple rack levels above the floor


- Warehouse expansion space is limited


- Facilities want to increase cubic storage capacity


Many high-volume warehouses


[throughout Michigan, Illinois, New York, Ontario](https://materialhandling.altg.com/locations/) , and other major distribution markets rely on vertical storage strategies to accommodate growing inventory demands.


## The Advantages of a Narrow-Aisle Order Picker


Warehouse space is expensive. For many operations, the goal is not expanding the building but maximizing the space already available. A narrow-aisle order picker is specifically designed to operate in tighter aisle configurations while maintaining access to elevated inventory locations.


Potential advantages include:


- Increased storage density


- Improved warehouse utilization


- More pallet positions within the same footprint


- Efficient product retrieval in compact spaces


When paired with the right racking system and warehouse layout, narrow aisle equipment can significantly improve operational efficiency.


## Key Features to Compare Before Buying


Not all order picker forklift models offer the same capabilities. Comparing specifications can help ensure the equipment aligns with your operation. Be sure to consider details such as:


- **Lift Height:** Determine the highest storage locations in your facility and select equipment capable of safely reaching those levels.


- **Load Capacity:** Different products require different capacities. Consider average order weight as well as peak demand requirements.


- **Travel Speed:** High-volume operations often benefit from equipment designed for faster travel and picking cycles.


- **Ergonomics:** Operator comfort can influence productivity and reduce fatigue during long shifts.


- **Technology Integration:** Many modern warehouse order picker models offer advanced features such as


[fleet integration](https://materialhandling.altg.com/parts-and-services/fleet-management/) , operator access controls, impact monitoring, productivity, and safety systems. These technologies can provide valuable insights into equipment utilization and operational performance.

## FAQs


**What is the difference between a low-level and high-level order picker?**


A low-level order picker is designed for inventory stored near ground level, while a high-level order picker allows operators to access products stored at elevated rack positions.


**Is an order picker forklift the same as a standard forklift?**


No. An order picker forklift is specifically designed for order fulfillment tasks where operators retrieve individual items or cases from storage locations. Standard forklifts are typically used for moving full pallets.


**How do I determine which order picker is right for my facility?**


The best choice depends on factors such as storage height, aisle width, inventory turnover, picking volume, and future growth plans. A material handling specialist can help evaluate these requirements and recommend the most appropriate solution.
