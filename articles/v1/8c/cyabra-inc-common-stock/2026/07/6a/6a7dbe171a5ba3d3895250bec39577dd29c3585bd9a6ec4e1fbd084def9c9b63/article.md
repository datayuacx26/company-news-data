# Introducing: Cyabra’s Coordinated Activity Detection AI Agent

Cyabra is introducing Coordinated Activity Detection, powered by a new AI agent that automates the work of a human analyst.


TL;DR


- Cyabra's AI agent automatically turns investigative signals into a clear verdict on suspicious activity
- The verdict draws on eight distinct signals, each backed by specific evidence
- The feature runs automatically on every scan, no manual work required.
- The capability is built on the same methodology Cyabra's analysts have long used, now packaged into a downloadable, shareable report
- Coordinated Activity Detection is live now as part of Cyabra's solution


Online influence operations are rarely obvious. Teams often have to piece together timing, narratives, profile behavior, and engagement patterns to figure out whether suspicious activity is truly coordinated. That process can take time, and it usually depends on skilled analysts connecting signals manually. In today’s environment, organizations need a faster way to get to the answer. That is why Cyabra is introducing **Coordinated Activity Detection** : a new capability powered by Cyabra’s AI agent that turns investigative signals into a verdict.


By automating Cyabra’s proven analyst methodologies and processing signals into clear results, this new feature removes the need for customers to manually interpret clusters, timing, narratives, and more. Every scan provides a definitive, evidence-backed result: is this coordinated, or not.


## What Coordinated Activity Detection Does


Coordinated Activity Detection automatically analyzes Cyabra’s scans across eight signals, profile concentration, synchronized posting, shared content, aligned narratives, hashtag deployment, amplification behavior, geographic clustering, and cross-platform coordination, and generates an automatic downloadable report. The goal is to help teams understand not only whether coordinated activity is present, but **why** that conclusion was reached and what narratives may be driving it.


## Key features


- **A final verdict:** Every scan is evaluated using the same investigative methodology Cyabra’s own analysts use, producing one clear determination. No manual interpretation required.
- **Automation:** For each scan at scale, the report is generated automatically. No setup, no separate request.
- **Evidence you can stand behind:** The report shows exactly what pointed to coordination, down to the activity peaks, topics, narratives, and key profiles shaping the conversation.
- **Downloadable, shareable output:** A downloadable PDF report that teams can easily review and circulate internally or with stakeholders.


## How Cyabra’s AI Agent Works


Every scan runs through Cyabra’s investigative AI, which does the work a human analyst used to do by hand:


- Correlates signals across all eight categories
- Connects related activity across accounts and platforms
- Identifies coordination patterns
- Evaluates supporting evidence
- Generates explainable findings


Get a clear verdict on whether the conversation is authentic or coordinated.


## The Eight Signals


Each coordinated activity verdict is built from eight signals:


- **Profile concentration:** the share of accounts in the conversation that are inauthentic
- **Synchronized posting:** posting activity that clusters tightly in time across many accounts
- **Shared content:** identical or near-identical text, phrasing, or templates reused across accounts
- **Aligned narratives:** multiple accounts pushing the same claim or storyline
- **Hashtag deployment:** the same hashtags deployed at the same time across accounts
- **Amplification behavior:** accounts systematically replying to or boosting the same high-reach posts
- **Geographic clustering:** inauthentic accounts concentrated in specific countries or regions
- **Cross-platform coordination:** the same activity appearing simultaneously across multiple platforms
