# The Invisible Threat: How to Stop Silent Killers and Reduce MTTR in Microwave Networks

Modern microwave networks are the backbone of critical communications. Yet, hidden issues—what we call ‘ **silent killers** ’—can quietly degrade performance, increase costs, and impact customer experience. These problems often lurk beneath the surface, undetected by traditional monitoring tools.


## The Growing Challenge


As spectrum becomes more crowded and technologies like **Wi-Fi 6E** proliferate, interference and misconfigurations are no longer rare. Even well-behaved devices can create **additive interference** , and seemingly healthy links can mask packet loss or congestion. For decision-makers, this means higher operational risk and potential revenue loss.


## Why Traditional Monitoring Falls Short


Traditional Network Management Systems (NMS) often present a **false sense of security** . They may show all links in a “green” state as long as they are minimally communicating, hiding underlying degradation.


Conversely, NMS platforms can flood teams with alarms and raw data without providing actionable insights. In large networks, this leads to longer **Mean Time to Repair (MTTR)** , higher costs, and frustrated customers.


### The risks fall into three primary categories that chip away at your network’s health:


### **1.**


Sporadic Interference


The growing prevalence of unlicensed **6 GHz devices** (like Wi-Fi 6E) can cause periodic, unpredictable interference in the licensed spectrum. This subtle noise can compound across multiple devices, leading to degraded customer performance that is nearly impossible to spot manually.


### **2.**


Mis-provisioning and Hidden Congestion


Configuration errors, such as incorrectly set queues or thresholds, can cause intermittent packet loss and congestion that traditional alarms simply miss.


### **3.**


Data Overload


Modern equipment generates massive amounts of performance data—potentially millions of data points per link per year. This makes it impossible for human staff to manually triage and identify the most critical issues.

## Health Assurance Software (HAS)


### Key Function:


Analyzes and correlates link performance data (Utilization, Congestion, Propagation Anomalies). Provides a probable root cause and likely next steps.


### Business Impact


**Real-time Network Health:** Delivers two critical business values.


1. Early fault detection and proactive maintenance, helping you address potential issues before they impact your customers.
2. Provides clear visualization of link utilization trends, empowering your team to make informed, just-in-time capacity upgrade decisions.


Together, these capabilities reduce troubleshooting time, minimize service disruptions, and support efficient network growth


## Frequency Assurance Software (FAS)


### Key Function:


Analyzes high-resolution Paperless Chart Recorder (PCR) data to detect and identify the source of RF interference (e.g., bursty Wi-Fi 6E vs. steady external radio).


### Business Impact


**Spectrum Protection:** Protects your investment in expensive spectrum by maintaining optimal link performance. Enables proactive intervention before customers experience outages.


## Key Benefits for Your Business


Implementing ProVision Plus with HAS and FAS delivers tangible, measurable business benefits :


- **Reduced Operating Costs (OpEx):** Automate complex troubleshooting, lowering the reliance on a large team of expensive, highly experienced microwave radio engineers.
