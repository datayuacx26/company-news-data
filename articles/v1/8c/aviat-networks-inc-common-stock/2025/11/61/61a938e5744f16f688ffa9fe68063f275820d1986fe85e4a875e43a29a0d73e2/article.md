# Aviat Networks to Host Fireside Chat and Participate in 14th Annual Roth Technology Conference

# Release Details


## Aviat Networks to Host Fireside Chat and Participate in 14th Annual Roth Technology Conference


November 17, 2025


[PDF Version](https://investors.aviatnetworks.com/node/18576/pdf)


AUSTIN, Texas


, Nov. 17, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- Aviat Networks, Inc. (NASDAQ: AVNW), the leading expert in wireless transport and access solutions, today announced that it will host a fireside chat with Scott Searle, Senior Research Analyst at Roth at 4:30 pm ET on November 18, 2025.


To listen to the fireside chat, investors can access the webcast through[this link](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4559522-1&h=1516842209&u=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.media-server.com%2Fmmc%2Fp%2Fgjgfs5rw&a=this+link) or through Aviat Network's Investor Relations website at[https://investors.aviatnetworks.com/events-and-presentations/events](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4559522-1&h=3449332677&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.aviatnetworks.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fevents&a=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.aviatnetworks.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fevents) . A replay of the event will be available on the Company's Investor Relations website.


The following day, Aviat Networks will participate in 1x1 meetings at the 14th Annual Roth Technology Conference to be held in New York, NY on November 19, 2025. Investors interested in scheduling a meeting with the company should contact their representative at Roth.


All presentations and supplemental materials will be available under the Investor Relations section of Aviat's website following the event.


**About Aviat Networks, Inc.** Aviat is the leading expert in wireless transport and access solutions and works to provide dependable products, services and support to its customers. With more than one million systems sold into 170 countries worldwide, communications service providers and private network operators including state/local government, utility, federal government and defense organizations trust Aviat with their critical applications. Coupled with a long history of microwave innovations, Aviat provides a comprehensive suite of localized professional and support services enabling customers to drastically simplify both their networks and their lives. For more than 70 years, the experts at Aviat have delivered high performance products, simplified operations, and the best overall customer experience. Aviat is headquartered in Austin, Texas.
