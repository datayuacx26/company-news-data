# Secure Your Fleet, Slash Your Costs: Introducing the Aprisa LTE 5G for Mission Critical Mobile Connectivity

For public safety agencies, utility services, and major delivery fleets, mobile connectivity is mission-critical. However, many organizations are facing an aging fleet of LTE routers, the unwelcome industry shift to mandatory, costly subscriptions, and ever-increasing security threats.


Aviat is answering these challenges with a revolutionary solution: the **Aprisa LTE 5G** rugged router combined with the flexible **Provision Plus** management system. This combination delivers high performance, unmatched security, and a unique, cost-saving ownership model.


## **Beyond Connectivity: A Full-Stack Mobile Solution**


The Aprisa LTE 5G is engineered for the demanding mobile environment, supporting everything from **Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD)** for first responders to **Automatic Vehicle Location (AVL)** for utilities and in-vehicle systems like **Mobile Data Terminals (MDTs)** and **OBD-2** diagnostics.


Key hardware and performance features include:


- **Rugged Reliability:** The device is **FirstNet Trusted** and ruggedized, meeting **Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE)** standards for vibration and operating in extreme temperatures.
- **Vibration-Proof Design:** It utilizes **QMA RF connectors** (instead of standard SMA), which are secured with a coupling sleeve to prevent loosening due to vehicle vibration, enhancing RF reliability.
- **Rapid Deployment:** The router can perform a cold boot and connect to the cellular network in **under a minute** , critical for fast-response scenarios.
- **High Availability:** It supports **dual SIM** (single radio) configuration, allowing for rapid failover between carriers like FirstNet, T-Mobile, or Verizon in about 30 seconds if service is lost.


## **Aviat VRF Protect: The New Standard for Mobile Security**


In modern vehicles, mission-critical applications often share the same router as less-secure systems, creating a major security risk. For example, a police MDT accessing the highly sensitive **Criminal Justice Information System (CJIS)** database might sit next to a third-party-managed video camera system.


The Aprisa LTE 5G solves this with **Aviat VRF Protect** (Virtual Routing and Forwarding).


- **Network Segmentation:** VRF allows the physical router to function as **multiple independent virtual routers** , completely isolating traffic between connected devices.
- **Reduce Attack Surface:** This prevents a compromise on a vulnerable system (like a video recorder) from transgressing the network and gaining access to a secure network segment, such as the one used for CJIS access.


## **Your Management, Your Way**


Aviat’s Provision Plus platform allows centralized monitoring of Aprisa routers and third-party devices. Features include:


- Real-time signal diagnostics
- Bulk configuration and firmware updates
- Location tracking and data usage alerts
- Custom dashboards for fleet-wide visibility


Unlike most competitors who force an OpEx/subscription-based model for router management and support, Aviat offers **complete flexibility** .


- **CapEx Ownership:** With Aviat, you can purchase a **perpetual management license** for Provision Plus, allowing you to own the management system outright. This **CapEx model** eliminates mandatory annual renewal fees, resulting in substantial **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)** savings—potentially saving hundreds of thousands, or even millions, of dollars against subscription models over a nine-year asset life.
- **Flexible Deployment:** Provision Plus can be deployed on your terms, allowing for either **cloud-based or on-premise** management, depending on your organization’s security and policy needs.
- **24/7 Local Support:** Your investment is backed by 24/7/365 US-based technical support, readily available to help with any network issues.


The Aprisa LT 5G is more than a router—it’s a mission-critical networking solution built for the demands of modern fleets. With flexible deployment options, robust security, and a cost-effective ownership model, it’s a compelling alternative to subscription-heavy competitors.
