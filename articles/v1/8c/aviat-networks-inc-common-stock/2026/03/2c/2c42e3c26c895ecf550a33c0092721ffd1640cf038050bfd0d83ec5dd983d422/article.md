## Industrial Ruggedness for Zero Maintenance


### Certified Protection: ISO 7637-2 and SAE J1455


- **Power Protection:** Police vehicles experience massive voltage spikes when starting engines or running sirens. The Aprisa is certified for ISO 7637-2 (vehicle electrical transients), meaning fewer blown fuses and less downtime for the fleet.
- **Shock & Vibration: SAE J1455** – the industry standard for the design and testing of ruggedized electronic equipment for vehicle applications. It defines tests for thermal, humidity, salt spray, vibration, and electrical endurance to ensure durability under harsh, real-world operating conditions.
- **Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC): EN 301 489** – harmonized European standard for radio equipment that defines essential immunity and emission requirements to protect equipment from electromagnetic disturbances in vehicle deployments.
- **Thermal Management:** Unlike competitors that may throttle 5G speeds when sitting in a hot trunk in 100°F weather, Aviat uses passive heat-sinking to maintain full 3.4 Gbps throughput without fans.
