# Nextlink Expands Partnership with Aviat to Drive BEAD-Funded Broadband Across 12 States

## Funding the 12-State Rural Broadband Footprint


### Rural broadband is having its infrastructure moment, and Nextlink Internet is at the center of it.


Nextlink, one of the largest rural-focused fixed wireless providers in the United States, has expanded its existing agreement with Aviat Networks to support its Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment (BEAD) program buildout. Deploying **[Aviat’s microwave, E-Band and multi-band wireless transport systems](https://aviatnetworks.com/products/)** , Nextlink will use the expanded agreement to connect thousands of unserved and underserved locations across its 12-state footprint in the Central United States.


The scale of what Nextlink is executing is significant. The company has secured over $180 million in BEAD funding, on top of the $429.2 million Rural Digital Opportunity Fund (RDOF) award it previously received from the FCC, making it one of the most active fixed wireless participants in federal broadband programs. Nextlink recently activated what is reported to be the first wireless tower in the nation to be funded by the BEAD program, in Louisiana, with thousands more sites to follow across Texas, Oklahoma, Kansas, Nebraska, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Wyoming, Minnesota, Wisconsin, and South Dakota.


## Why Wireless Transport and Backhaul Matter for BEAD Compliance


The choice of wireless transport matters as much as the access technology. Backhaul,the link connecting a fixed wireless tower to the internet backbone, determines the capacity and reliability that end users actually experience. Aviat’s microwave, E-Band and multi-band platforms are engineered for exactly this environment: long spans across flat terrain, harsh weather, and the throughput demands of serving hundreds of subscribers per tower at BEAD-mandated minimum speeds.


For Nextlink CTO Cameron Kilton, the decision came down to execution certainty: “ *Aviat’s solutions have consistently performed, which means we can move fast and stay on schedule.* ”


## Overcoming Supply Chain Hurdles with Accelerated Procurement


That pace matters. BEAD subgrant recipients face deployment milestones and reporting obligations, and delays in backhaul procurement can cascade across an entire buildout program.
