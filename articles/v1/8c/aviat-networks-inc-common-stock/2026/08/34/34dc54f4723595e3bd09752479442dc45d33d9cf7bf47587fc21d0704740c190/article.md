## Does Aprisa LTE/5G support Starlink failover?


Yes. Aprisa LTE/5G automatically fails over to Starlink or Amazon Leo satellite connectivity when terrestrial 4G/5G coverage drops, including in a live deployment with Mexico City police during the FIFA World Cup.


## What is Companion Mode on Aprisa LTE/5G?


Companion Mode is an active-active dual-router configuration supporting up to four SIMs, so a single router failure does not take the vehicle offline and GPS location reporting continues uninterrupted.


## What is Aviat VRFprotect?


Aviat VRFprotect is a Layer-3 traffic isolation feature that keeps sensitive systems, such as NCIC/CJIS access, body-cam uploads, and third-party vendor traffic, walled off from each other on the same router.


## How does Aprisa LTE/5G's cost compare to Cradlepoint and Semtech?


Aviat’s pricing model for Aprisa LTE/5G compounds CAPEX and OPEX savings over time, in contrast to subscription-locked models from providers like Cradlepoint and Semtech.
