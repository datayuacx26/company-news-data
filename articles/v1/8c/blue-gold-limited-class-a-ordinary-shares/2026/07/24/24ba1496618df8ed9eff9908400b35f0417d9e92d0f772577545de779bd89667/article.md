# Blue Gold Advances Producing Mine Acquisition Strategy to Drive Cash Flow

NEW YORK, NY, July 23,2026 /PRNewswire/ — Blue Gold Limited (Nasdaq: BGL) (Nasdaq: BGLWW) (“BlueGold” or the “Company”), a gold mining company with the infrastructure todeliver gold from mine-to-wallet, today announced that it has entered intomultiple strategic acquisition term sheets as it executes its strategy to builda diversified portfolio of producing gold mining assets.


“This marks an important milestonein the execution of Blue Gold's growth strategy,” commented Andrew Cavaghan, CEOof Blue Gold. “These acquisitions have the potential to substantially accelerateour evolution into a diversified, cash-flow-generating gold producer while establishinga strong foundation for the Company's long-term growth.”


Over the past 30days, Blue Gold has executed non-binding term sheets with a number of independentmining companies to acquire all, or substantially all, of their operatingbusinesses.


Each of the proposedacquisitions represents an established operating mining business with existing orhistoric production, experienced operating teams and the prospect of near-term positivecash flow. The Company believes completion of any one of these transactions hasthe potential to materially increase Blue Gold's operating scale, generatenear-term operating profit sufficient to substantially cover corporate overheadand provide a platform for continued organic and acquisition-driven growth.


The Company believesthis strategy will reduce reliance on any single asset or jurisdiction whilecreating multiple avenues for growth. Blue Gold continues to actively pursueits claim for compensation from the Government of Ghana, which management viewsas an important and complementary component of its broader strategy to maximizelong-term shareholder value.
