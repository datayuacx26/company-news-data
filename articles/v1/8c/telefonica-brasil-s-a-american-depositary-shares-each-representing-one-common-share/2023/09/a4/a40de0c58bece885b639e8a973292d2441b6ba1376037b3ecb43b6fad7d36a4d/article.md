# Telefónica and SOMOS win 2 awards at the Mobile World Congress

They received the gold in the category of “Best participation in a trade fair”.


The 18th edition of the Eventoplus Awards Gala, the only awards in the events and marketing industry dedicated to the work of agencies with their clients, was held on 26 September 2023.


The nominations were in the categories of “Best trade show participation” and “Best technical set-up” at the Mobile World Congress 2023. Telefónica won gold in the first category and bronze in the second.


Thus, after more than 4 months of preparation and a team of more than 150 members, the magic happened and they won the awards in both nominations
