# Shard List Update

Hey Zenitheans,


Every now and then we do a deep review of our infrastructure, including the number of shards currently online. We found a handful of shards go unused in comparison to many others, therefore we have decided to retire some shards starting on Friday, July 14, 2023.


*Reminder: Accounts and characters aren’t locked to any shard or region, so no progress or data will be lost.*


We hope this will help distribute players more evenly across the shards and make the experience better for everyone!
