# Support Updates for Legacy Hardware

Greetings Zenitheans,


We here at Ramen VR have been very excited about our work on patch 1.3 Skyward Summit. However, there has been one issue that’s been hindering our development of the patch and something we need to address. After much consideration, we have made the hard decision to stop supporting certain legacy headsets. We know this news may come as a disappointment to some of our players who have been using these devices to experience the world of Zenith, and for that we apologize. Thankfully, with Zenith being a cross-platform game, you are able to continue playing with your characters on modern hardware now, or in the future.


This means that starting with our upcoming patch 1.3 Skyward Summit, the new features and improvements will not be available for certain legacy devices. At 1.3 launch, these users will remain on Minor 1.2.2 with their own dedicated shards starting on May 11. The decision to retire support for these headsets will impact each in a different way; you can find links to posts below that discuss why support is being stopped for these headsets, and what the plan is for them going forward.


- [PS VR1](https://zenithmmo.com/blogs/news/support-updates-for-ps-vr1)
- [Quest 1](https://zenithmmo.com/blogs/news/support-updates-for-quest-1)


We are committed to delivering the best possible experience to all of our players and we are confident that this decision will ultimately lead to a more stable, seamless, and enjoyable experience for all users moving forward.


We’d like to thank all of our players and hope you will continue to enjoy Zenith on newer platforms. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.


Sincerely,


The Ramen VR Team


### Update, October 23rd 2024


Players will no longer be able to run Zenith on Quest 1 or PS VR1 after November 6th, 2024. Zenith will continue to be playable across other platforms.
