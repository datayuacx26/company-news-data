# Support Updates for PS VR1

Greetings Zenitheans,


We here at Ramen VR have been very excited about our work on patch 1.3 Skyward Summit. However, there has been one issue that’s been hindering our development of the patch and something we need to address. After much consideration, we have made the hard decision to stop supporting certain legacy headsets, one of which is the PS VR1. We know this news may come as a disappointment to some of our players who have been using this device to experience the world of Zenith, and for that we apologize. Thankfully, with Zenith being a cross-platform game, you are able to continue playing with your characters on modern hardware now, or in the future.


This means that starting with our upcoming major patch 1.3 Skyward Summit, the new features and improvements will not be available for the PS VR1. The Zenith app will continue to run, but will stay on our most recent patch, Minor 1.2.2. We have tried hard to make 1.3 work with the PS Move controllers but unfortunately, it's just not possible. With 1.3 relying on modern VR controllers, 1.2.2 is as far as we could take the PS Move and therefore PS VR1.


We are committed to delivering the best possible experience to all of our players and we are confident that this decision will ultimately lead to a more stable, seamless, and enjoyable experience for all users moving forward. If you have any questions or concerns about this, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.


Sincerely,


The Ramen VR Team


### Update, October 23rd 2024


Players will no longer be able to run Zenith on Quest 1 or PS VR1 after November 6th, 2024. Zenith will continue to be playable across other platforms.
