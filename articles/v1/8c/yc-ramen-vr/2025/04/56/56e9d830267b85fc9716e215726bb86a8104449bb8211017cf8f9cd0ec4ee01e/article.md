# Updates to Shard List

Hey Zenitheans,


The number of shards for *Zenith: The Last City* will be reduced starting **April 17, 2025** .


The community has made this request to make it easier for players new and old to find each other. If we require more shards in the future, we can easily spin them up as needed. We also took a look at regions that could be consolidated; in this case, we are going to consolidate Asia and Oceania.


*Reminder: Accounts and characters aren’t locked to any shard or region, so no progress or data will be lost. *The shard list is subject to further changes after this round of consolidation. We'll let players know in advance of any further changes.**


We hope this will help consolidate players across the shards and make the experience better for everyone!
