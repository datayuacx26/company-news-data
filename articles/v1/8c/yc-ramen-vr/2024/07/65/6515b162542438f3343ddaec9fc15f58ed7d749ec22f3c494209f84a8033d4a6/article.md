# Updates to Shard List

Hey Zenitheans,


As we look towards a more sustainable vision for Zenith, we’ve reviewed the number of shards currently online. We found a handful of shards go unused in comparison to others, and have decided to retire these shards during the release of Season 4 in August 2024 (release window TBA).


*Reminder: Accounts and characters aren’t locked to any shard or region, so no progress or data will be lost.*


We hope this will help consolidate players across the shards and make the experience better for everyone!


### **TLC Shards that are retiring 🛑**


US-EAST


- Armageddon


US-WEST


- Protector


EUROPE


- Dragonfruit


### **TLC Shards that will remain up ✅**


US-EAST


- Aether


- Arboruht


- Radiant


US-WEST


- Acolyte


- Crimson


- Soulless (PlayStation only)


EUROPE


- Defender


- Titan


ASIA


- Nexus


OCEANIA


- Tidekeeper


*Edit 7/26: Note the shard list is subject to further changes after this round of consolidation. We'll let players know in advance of any further changes.*
