# The Ultimate Guide to Equipment Rental Software and Features to Look For

Last Updated on June 17, 2026


It can be tough to keep a construction project on track and on budget. Renting equipment adds another layer of complexity to any project management job: When will equipment be delivered? When is it due back? Where is it now? Is your team optimizing the full value of the rental?


Construction equipment rental software simplifies this process. The right software can help you handle everything from scheduling and tracking to maintenance and billing. It essentially acts as a personal assistant for your fleet management. It should also help to streamline operations, boost efficiency and reduce costs.


While some software options are subscription-based or can be purchased outright, free construction equipment rental software may also be included as part of your rental agreement. Read on to learn how to find the best heavy equipment rental software for small businesses and large.

Equipment rental software is a digital solution designed to help businesses manage their rental equipment more efficiently. While every solution can be different, the best equipment rental software helps centralize operations, track assets in real time and simplify tasks such as scheduling, maintenance, invoicing and reporting.

## **Different Types of Equipment Rental Software**


Not all software solutions are the same. Understand the differences so you can choose the best equipment rental software for your needs.


### **On-Premise Software**


On-premise software is installed and run on computers at your company’s own site, such as in your own office or data center, rather than in the cloud. Your company will typically purchase licenses and install the software on your own hardware. Your IT team is usually responsible for software maintenance, updates and security.


Businesses may choose on-premise software when they need full control over data, have strict security or compliance requirements, or want deep customization. This model usually involves higher upfront costs but can be more economical over a longer period versus subscription-based cloud services.


### **Cloud-Based Software**


Cloud-based software, like[ProControl by Herc Rentals](https://www.hercrentals.com/procontrol.html?signUp=true) TM, runs online rather than on a local computer or on-site server.


These software options are hosted, maintained and updated by an external, third-party provider in data centers. Cloud-based software may be included with your rental agreement or separately as a subscription or pay-as-you-go service.


Because it is internet-based, users can access cloud-based software from multiple devices and locations, making remote work and collaboration easy. It also scales on demand, allowing computing resources and storage to expand or contract as needs change.


### **All-In-One Software**


All-in-one software bundles offer many different tools and functions — like accounting, project management, communications and inventory — into one system. They can be deployed as either cloud-based or on-premise solutions. All-in-one software centralizes data and workflows. Doing so ensures users do not have to constantly log in, log out and switch between disconnected programs.


A drawback of all-in-one software is that it can often lack the depth needed for critical features. It likely offers limited customization for specialized needs, too.


### **Free Software**


Finding construction equipment rental software for free is possible. However, most options that are offered outside of rental agreements, or outside of a personalized service arrangement are limited in their features and technical support. You usually need to upgrade to a paid subscription for robust features.


The best choice is to partner with an equipment rental provider who includes software solutions as part of your overall rental agreement.


### **Mobile Apps**


Both on-premise and cloud-based software may offer mobile apps. Mobile apps are designed for smartphones, tablets and other portable devices. Their interfaces are optimized for touch screens, smaller displays, and features like GPS and cameras. Many support offline use, push notifications and device integration.


When choosing a rental software solution, make sure the mobile app will work on everyone’s devices. Some apps are “native apps,” which are built specifically for a single platform, such as iOS or Android, and installed directly on the device.


Web-based apps run through mobile browsers as responsive websites and can be used across devices. Hybrid apps blend native and web technologies while progressive web apps offer advanced capabilities, including installation, offline access and push notifications.

## **6 Essential Features to Look for in Equipment Rental Software**


There is equipment rental software for small businesses and large alike. Regardless of size, rental company software should include these must-have features.


### **1. Real-Time Inventory Management and Tracking**


Real-time inventory and[fleet tracking](https://www.hercrentals.com/procontrol.html) give instant visibility into equipment location, availability and condition. With these insights, you always know what is on hand, where it is and what shape it’s in.


An intuitive interface makes it easy to check delivery and pickup status or verify dates and times with just a few clicks.


Geofencing — a virtual boundary around a physical location — adds another layer of control by setting virtual boundaries around project sites and sending notifications when equipment enters or leaves those areas.


### **2. Online Booking and Customer Portal**


Around-the-clock access to booking, rental changes and account details are all essential for project teams, which often work beyond normal business hours.


An effective online customer portal lets users quickly extend a rental agreement, call off a rental, and update delivery or pickup locations as needs change. It also makes it easy to find the right equipment at the right price to stay on schedule and on budget.


Role-based access controls further enhance safety and reduce theft by managing who can access equipment and for how long, while mobile access to safety training provides additional value in the field.


### **3. Maintenance and Repair Scheduling**


Equipment rental management software should include tools to track maintenance data and simplify repair scheduling.


Many platforms integrate telematics into fleet management. Telematics capture information on location, engine status, fuel use, diagnostics and driver behavior. This data is turned into actionable insights.


Over time, you can reduce fuel usage and maintenance costs, minimize downtime, improve safety, prevent misuse and better calculate the true return on investment of rented equipment.


A strong user interface is equally important. Rental software for equipment should make it easy to place[service calls](https://www.hercrentals.com/content/dam/hercrentals/us/commerce/static/training/ProControl-by-Herc-Rentals-Quick-Start-Guide-for-HE-Customers.pdf) , upload photos and videos for documentation, and monitor service tickets so issues are resolved quickly and efficiently.


### **4. Invoicing and Payment Processing**


Centralizing purchase orders, invoices and payments in one system saves time, improves organization, and reduces the risk of items being missed or overlooked.


Rental equipment software should allow you to create rental agreements and purchase orders, manage invoices, and make payments at any time, day or night. Features such as split payments and linking invoices directly to rental agreements add flexibility and make financial tracking easier.


### **5. Reporting and Analytics**


Among the most valuable features of heavy equipment rental software: its ability to analyze data and turn it into clear, actionable reports. The software should offer customizable dashboards and reports in formats that are easy to use and easy to understand.


It should also allow reports to be scheduled and automatically delivered to your inbox on a set day or frequency, with download options such as PDF or Excel. For example, an equipment-on-rent summary could be generated and sent immediately or on a daily, weekly or annual schedule to selected recipients in the format you choose.


Report customization is also important. If you can customize, you can include only the data you need to streamline reports. The platform should also support flexible alerts that can be created or removed easily, with delivery options such as email, text or push notifications, and full control over who receives them and when.

Some software offers enhanced features that allow you to have better, more secure access of equipment on the jobsite.
