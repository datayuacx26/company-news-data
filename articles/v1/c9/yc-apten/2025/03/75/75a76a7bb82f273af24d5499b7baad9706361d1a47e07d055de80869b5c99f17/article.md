## Five Tips for Better SMS Lead Qualification


### 1. Send Messages at the Right Time


Timing matters. Use AI tools to figure out when your leads are most likely to respond. Stick to business hours, consider time zones, and analyze individual response patterns to fine-tune your message scheduling.


### 2. Use Personalization in Your Messages


Generic texts don't cut it. Make your messages stand out by tailoring them to your leads' behaviors and preferences. Reference specific actions, like products they’ve browsed, or include details relevant to their industry or location.

## FAQs


### How to automate lead scoring?


Automating lead scoring for SMS campaigns requires a clear plan that fits seamlessly into your overall strategy. Here's how you can do it:


1. **Set Scoring Criteria and Thresholds**


Assign point values to specific actions and attributes to measure lead quality. For example:


-


**SMS responses** : 10 points


-


**Link clicks** : 5 points


-


**Form submissions** : 15 points


-


**Demographic match** : 8 points


Establish point thresholds to determine when a lead is ready for the next step in your sales process. This ensures you're prioritizing the most relevant prospects.


2. **Use AI-Powered Tools**


AI-driven platforms can make lead scoring faster and more efficient by analyzing engagement data in real time. These tools help you identify high-priority leads quickly. As Structurely puts it:


> "Anything that needs repeating can be automated. Automated lead qualification software takes the repetitive tasks necessary to qualify a lead and does it instantly."


3. **Choose the Right Automation Software**


Pick tools that align with your needs. Platforms like Apten, Textdrip, and 360 SMS can automate scoring while providing real-time tracking, making the process seamless.


**Pro Tip** : Add SMS OTP verification to confirm phone numbers and filter out invalid leads for better results.


4. **Review and Adjust Regularly**


Keep an eye on your scoring system to ensure it aligns with changing customer behavior and campaign goals. Businesses that automate lead scoring often cut lead qualification time by 30%, all while maintaining accuracy.
