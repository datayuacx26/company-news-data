# Preserving the Promise™—Together

## How BioLife’s Brand Refresh Strengthens the CGT Community


Cell and gene therapies (CGTs) are changing what’s possible in medicine. Each vial, batch, and patient represents years of innovation and the hope of a lasting cure. But for these therapies to reach the people who need them, every step must be predictable, reproducible, and protected.


That’s where partnership matters. BioLife Solutions exists to help developers, manufacturers, and clinicians build processes they can trust, so the treatments they create will deliver the outcomes they intend. Our refreshed brand reflects that shared commitment. It’s not about what changes for us. It’s about how much stronger we can make the ecosystem we serve.


## A Clearer Brand for a Complex Field


Advancing CGTs is complicated. The science is precise, timelines are tight, and small deviations can have big consequences. A brand that communicates with the same clarity we bring to our science helps make every interaction simpler. Whether you’re[selecting media](https://biolifesolutions.com/resource-center/insights-blog/how-to-choose-the-right-freeze-media/) ,[evaluating storage containers](https://biolifesolutions.com/resource-center/insights-blog/cgt-container-standards/) , training a new team, or looking for cryobiology expertise to optimize your process, BioLife is here to help.


Our refreshed visual and verbal identity was designed for you:


- **Easier to navigate.** Clear product groupings and consistent naming help you find the right solutions faster.
- **Easier to trust.** Unified data visuals, validated claims, and direct links to supporting evidence to strengthen confidence in your decisions.
- **Easier to partner with.** Shared language across cryobiology services, R&D, QA, and commercial teams help align objectives and outcomes.


In short, this refresh is about removing friction so you can focus on the science that matters.


## Standardization That Serves You


Standardization isn’t bureaucracy; it’s the foundation of reliability. Every validated buffer, closed-system container, or automated thawing tool reduces the number of unknowns that can derail a therapy. The same thinking drives our brand: consistency across touchpoints means that when you see BioLife, you know exactly what level of rigor and responsiveness to expect. When we communicate clearly, your teams can move faster, align more easily, and translate data into decisions that protect cell quality, patient timelines, and ultimately, therapeutic success.


## Building a More Connected CGT Community


This brand refresh also strengthens connection—between scientists, engineers, and process experts across the field. Through our new[Aby J. Mathew Center for Biopreservation Excellence](https://biolifesolutions.com/resource-center/the-center/) , we’re creating spaces where customers can test hypotheses, refine workflows, and share insights that move the entire industry forward.


Your challenges shape our research. Your outcomes guide our next innovations. Our role is to provide the science, the standards, and the support that make it easier for you to deliver cures to patients who are waiting.


## Preserving the Promise—Now and Next


This new brand is not a new direction, it is a clearer expression of our shared purpose: to *preserve the promise* of every therapy, every partnership, and every patient outcome.


When processes are controlled, materials are standardized, and communication is clear, everyone benefits.


**** **Together, we’re *Preserving the Promise* of cell and gene therapy.**
