# The Cell Summit ’25 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## Everything You Need to Know Before The Cell Summit ’25


As we gear up for The Cell Summit ’25, we want to make sure you have all the details you need to plan your trip, navigate the event, and make the most of your time in Indianapolis. From hotel and shuttle logistics to what to wear and where to eat, this FAQ covers it all.


### GENERAL INFORMATION


**


**


Where is the event taking place?


The Cell Summit will be held at **[Newfields Art Museum](https://discovernewfields.org/)** , a beautiful campus located just 15 minutes from downtown Indianapolis. Transportation will be provided to and from the event each day. The presentations will be in the[Randolph H. Deer Pavilion](https://discovernewfields.org/newfields/performance-spaces/randolph-h-deer-special-events-pavilion) .


**


**


Will meals be provided?


Yes. The following meals are included in your registration:


- **Breakfast, lunch, and snacks** during both days at Newfields
- **Dinner** during our social event at the baseball game on Tuesday night


### SCHEDULE OVERVIEW


**


**


Monday, August 4 – Arrival & Check-In


- **4:00 PM – 7:00 PM:** Event check-in will be on the **2nd Floor (near Starbucks)** of the JW Marriott Hotel
- Stop by The Cell Summit check-in table to pick up your name badge, meeting packet, and other important materials.
- If you are unable to check in Monday evening, you’ll receive your badge Tuesday morning at Newfields Art Museum.


**


**


Tuesday, August 5 – Main Programming + Evening Event


- **8:00 AM:** Shuttle departs from JW Marriott to Newfields
- **8:15 AM – 5:15 PM:** Full day of presentations, panels, and networking at **Newfields Art Museum**
- **5:30 PM:** Gates open at **Victory Field** for the Indianapolis Indians game
- **6:35 PM:** First pitch!
The stadium is a short walk from the hotel—come as you are or stop by your room to change before joining us on the **First Base Terrace** . Dinner and drinks provided.
- **~10:00 PM:** Game ends


**


**


Wednesday, August 6 – Morning Sessions + Optional Afternoon Tracks


- **8:00 AM:** Shuttle departs from JW Marriott to Newfields
- **8:15 AM – 1:30 PM:** Morning sessions and wrap-up at Newfields
- **1:30 PM – 3:00 PM:** Choose your own afternoon experience:


- **Option 1:** Board the bus to **Azenta Life Sciences** for a facility tour
- **Option 2:** Stay at **Newfields** for an R&D Discussion with BioLife Solutions
- **Option 3:** Return early to the **JW Marriott** to depart or relax


- **~3:00 PM:** All buses will return to JW Marriott for afternoon departures


### HOTEL INFORMATION


**


**


What hotel should I stay at?


We have a room block at the **[JW Marriott Downtown Indianapolis](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/indjw-jw-marriott-indianapolis/overview/)** , a premier hotel located in the heart of the city.


**


**


How do I reserve a room in the event block?


If you haven’t already booked your stay,[please do so with this link](https://book.passkey.com/event/51005254/owner/1487628/home) . Our room block will close on July 15th.


**


**


If I do not plan on attending the evening event, are there other things to do nearby?


Yes, but please know this will be your responsibility to research and afford.[Here are some of the various experiences](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/indjw-jw-marriott-indianapolis/experiences/) the hotel advertises on their website.


### TRANSPORTATION & SHUTTLE SCHEDULE


**


**


Will transportation be provided to or from the Airport?


No, you will be responsible for your own transportation to the JW Marriott from the airport.


**


**


Will transportation be provided to the event venue?


Yes![Royal Indy Bus Services](https://www.royalindy.com/) will be shuttling attendees between the **JW Marriott** and **Newfields Art Museum** on both days.


**Tuesday, August 5**


- Bus departs JW Marriott at **8:00 AM**
- Bus returns from Newfields at **5:30 PM**


**Wednesday, August 6**


- Bus departs JW Marriott at **8:00 AM**
- Bus returns from Newfields at **1:30 PM**


**


**


What is the closest airport for this meeting?


[The Indianapolis International Airport](https://www.visitindy.com/directory/indianapolis-international-airport/) is about twenty minutes away from the JW Marriott Downtown Indianapolis hotel.


**


**


What are the options for the afternoon of Day 2, and how will transportation be handled?


On Wednesday afternoon, attendees will split into three tracks based on their selection during registration:


1.


**Tour Azenta Life Sciences Repository** – A bus will take this group to Azenta and return them to the JW Marriott after the tour around **3:00 PM** .


2.


**R&D Discussion with BioLife Solutions at Newfields** – This session will remain onsite, with a bus returning participants to the JW Marriott at **3:00 PM** .


3.


**Depart the meeting early** – If you’re heading out after the main session ends at **1:30 PM** , a bus will be available to return you to the hotel.


You’re also welcome to use your own transportation at any point if preferred.


### TUESDAY EVENING EVENT – BASEBALL GAME


**


**


What’s happening on Tuesday night?


Join us for an evening at the ballpark! We’ve reserved the **First Base Terrace** at **Victory Field** to enjoy an **Indianapolis Indians** game. It’s a fun, casual setting with food, drinks, and great company.


⚾ Game Time: **6:35 PM** | Gates Open: **5:30 PM**
📍[Stadium Map](https://www.milb.com/indianapolis/ballpark/map)


**


**


How do we get to the game?


Victory Field is located **right next to the JW Marriott** ; you are welcome to walk over at your leisure.


**


**


What food and drinks are included?


Our **Backyard BBQ** package includes:


- Hamburgers, hot dogs, and BBQ pulled chicken
- Mac & Cheese, Pasta Salad, Fresh Fruit Salad
- Cookies
- Assorted Pepsi products, Bud Light, Michelob ULTRA, Yuengling, a Seltzer beer and bottled water
- Two beers per guest


**


**


If I have food sensitivities, will those be considered?


Yes, when you registered for this event, you selected your food sensitivities. We will be respecting them all. If you did not do this when you registered, please email:marketingcommunications@biolifesolutions.com


**


**


How will I receive my baseball game ticket?


Tickets will be handed out prior to the game on Tuesday.


### WEATHER, DRESS CODE & PACKING TIPS


**


**


What should I wear?


Attire for the event is business casual. For the baseball game, we suggest casual, comfortable clothing suitable for an outdoor event. The terrace is open-air but covered.


**


**


What happens if it rains during the game?


The dinner will still be served rain or shine. If the game is canceled before it begins, you’ll receive a rain check ticket for another 2025 home game.


**


**


What do I need to plan to bring to the meeting?


BioLife Solutions will be providing pens, a meeting packet with notes section, and goodies (like an Indianapolis Indians baseball hat) for all attendees. If you would like to bring your computer or something else to take notes in, please do.

Who will be attending The Cell Summit ‘25?


Attendees include leaders from biotech, pharma, CDMOs, academic institutions, and tech/tool providers. You’ll meet professionals in R&D, process development, manufacturing, and strategy. It’s a curated group driving innovation across the CGT ecosystem.
