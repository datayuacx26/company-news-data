# Walker

## Walker McHugh, MSc


###### Strategic Advisor


Walker McHugh has 7+ years of experience in diagnostics and healthcare commercialization. Prior to SafeBeat, he was Founder/CEO at Refractal (YCS17) a healthcare startup who received FDA Breakthrough Device designation and raised from top VC, and angel investors. Most recently, Walker served as Director of Clinical Operations at Curebase, overseeing multiple industry-sponsored phase I/II clinical trials. Walker is a graduate of the University of Michigan, where he was named a Top-20 Entrepreneur.
