# Explore Current Research and Get the Latest in Clinical Education

CEO, Dr. Rachita Navara, a prominent member of the ACC EP council, was featured in a special podcast episode dedicated to Atrial Fibrillation (AFib) Awareness Month. The podcast offered an insightful journey into the world of digital health, wearable technology, and the future of patient-centered AFib management. She shared her expertise and vision for leveraging digital health tools to enhance AFib management. Listeners had the opportunity to explore the optimal use of wearables for monitoring heart health and gaining a deeper understanding of the condition.
