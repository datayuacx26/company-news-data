# SafeBeat Rx UCSF Clinical Trial begins Enrollment

In March, we began our UCSF clinical trial, which focuses on obtaining real user feedback on our application and reviewing algorithm results against the UCSF providers. The SafeBeat application aims to streamline provider and patient communication by providing an analysis on data acquired from FDA cleared hardware. To date, we have successfully enrolled several patients and are continuing to enroll 1-2 new patients every week.
