At the recent RIMS RISKWORLD 2026 conference, AIG leaders affirmed our commitment to serving as a trusted partner to our valued clients and brokers. As risk management challenges evolve, AIG demonstrated how we are prepared to deliver tailored solutions to help clients maximize the opportunities ahead.


“AIG is coming at this from a position of strength,” said[Eric Andersen](https://www.aig.com/home/about/leadership-and-governance/eric-andersen) , AIG President & Chief Executive Officer. “We feel really good about where we are as a firm and all that we can do in this marketplace.”


More than 130 AIG colleagues took part in over 640 meetings with our clients and broker partners in Philadelphia. The discussions centered on the many ways AIG can leverage our global reach in over 200 countries and jurisdictions, along with our technical expertise and problem-solving capabilities, to help our clients increase their resilience and grow with confidence.


“Our clients today have more fast-changing risk and more things that they are thinking about than they have for a long time,”[Jon Hancock](https://www.aig.com/home/about/leadership-and-governance/jon-hancock) , AIG’s Executive Vice President and Chief Executive Officer, General Insurance, said during a welcome breakfast with AIG colleagues. “And we have a fairly unique situation at AIG that we are so global in our outlook.”


RIMS RISKWORLD 2026, the world’s biggest annual risk management event, was held May 3-6. The event offered opportunities for personalized meetings with clients and featured sessions on building a captive strategy, geopolitical risk, the impact of AI and more.


## Driving growth from a client-centric approach


AIG continues to ensure our clients remain at the heart of our decisions. This event was a step toward strengthening that commitment and allowing us to further deepen our relationship with our clients while building new connections.


Rachel Perry, AIG’s Head of Client Engagement, North America, discussed AIG’s opportunity to grow across every line of business through a client-focused approach.


“RIMS is an opportunity for us to really advance our commitment to being a client-centric organization,” Rachel said. “AIG is a leader in this industry and we need to bring the full breadth of AIG -- all the services and solutions -- to our clients.”
