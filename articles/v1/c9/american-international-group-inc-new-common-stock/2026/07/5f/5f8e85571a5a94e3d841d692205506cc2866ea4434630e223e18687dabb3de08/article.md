# AIG Leaders Tee Up Lessons from the Golf Course

Overcoming obstacles, focusing on what you can control and dedicating time to perfecting your skills are just some of the many lessons AIG colleagues have learned on the golf course.


As the[2026 AIG Women’s Open](https://www.aig.com/home/about/aig-womens-open) approaches, Emma Woolley, CEO, Talbot, in the UK, Lisa Sun, CEO, AIG China, Ayako Aida, General Manager, Hokkaido/Tohoku Area of Japan, Carrie Kurzon, Head of Employment Practices Liability Product, Financial Lines, in the U.S., and Silke Rusch, Head of Accident & Health, DACH, in Germany, reflect on how their experiences playing golf shape their leadership style.


Their top leadership lessons include the ability to assess a situation, focus on the most important task at hand, plan ahead and maintain a long-term perspective. It also means taking lessons from the last game and adjusting your approach moving forward. They added that time spent on the golf course has helped build lasting relationships with our clients, brokers and stakeholders – including at AIG-hosted golf clinics.
