As more businesses expand outside their existing footprint, AIG is stepping in as a trusted insurer partner, helping our clients navigate local regulations and markets. For Karen Jury, Head of Multinational, AIG UK, supporting clients in managing these complexities to capture new opportunities is at the heart of her team’s work.


[AIG Multinational](https://www.aig.com/multinational) is a trusted partner for delivering tailored insurance solutions across borders. With the ability to provide insurance solutions to clients in over 200 countries and jurisdictions, and with hundreds of multinational professionals around the world -- including expertise in underwriting, operations and claims -- AIG supports clients through its global network.


“We’re making sure that for all of our Multinational clients, we’re meeting their needs in policy issuance, program structuring and risk management strategies,” Karen says. “We provide them with the best Multinational solution.”


## Local Expertise and Global Coordination


Karen’s team leverages insights from across AIG’s Multinational platform to help clients evaluate their exposures, structure their insurance programs, and address their compliance as rules and requirements evolve.


“Our expertise is very much around making sure that we can come up with the right insurance solution based on what their requirements are and making sure that it’s aligned with local regulations and insurance markets,” Karen says.


## Real-Time Insights and Informed Decisions


Karen emphasizes the value of providing clients with access to real-time data and information. With better visibility into how their global insurance program is being implemented, clients can make informed decisions and respond quickly when needs shift.


“It's a combination of our local expertise, centralizing that and bringing that into a tool that we can share with clients to make sure that we're providing them with the right advice and the right service,” she says.
