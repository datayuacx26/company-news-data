## The Best of New York, Now Boarding: JetBlue Debuts New Mint® Menus from Crown Shy and Birdee


07/29/2026


Download (opens in new window)


*JetBlue reinforces commitment to exceptional hospitality and destination-worthy dining, with new menus available now; onboard July 31*


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


JetBlue (NASDAQ: JBLU) today unveiled the first menus from its newest Mint® culinary partners – Crown Shy and Birdee – available online now and across select domestic and transatlantic Mint routes starting July 31, 2026.


Crown Shy's Executive Chef Jassimran Singh with JetBlue crewmember. Photo courtesy of JetBlue.


Developed in collaboration with Kent Hospitality Group, the new menus from New York City eateries Crown Shy and Birdee build on JetBlue's distinctive approach to hospitality, bringing a fresh, unmistakably New York perspective to Mint that captures the creativity, energy and culinary excellence of the city's dining scene.


“The best part of any new Mint menu is seeing it come to life onboard,” said Stephanie Evans Greene, senior vice president of marketing and brand, JetBlue. “Our teams worked closely with the talented chefs at Crown Shy and Birdee to thoughtfully adapt their dishes for the onboard experience. We're excited for customers to enjoy menus that bring two of New York's most exciting restaurants to their next Mint flight.”


“There are a lot of people who will get their first taste of Crown Shy and Birdee on a JetBlue flight. Whether someone is dining with us in New York City or flying around the world, the goal is always to make them feel genuinely cared for. We’re grateful for the collaboration with the JetBlue team to create dishes that stay authentic to our restaurants while translating naturally to the experience of dining in the air,” said Jassimran Singh, Executive Chef-Partner, Crown Shy and Renata Ameni, Executive Pastry Chef-Partner, Birdee.


**Restaurant-Worthy, Ready for Takeoff**


Inspired by Crown Shy, the Michelin-starred Manhattan restaurant celebrated for its modern American cuisine, and Birdee, the beloved Brooklyn bakery and all-day café known for its elevated comfort food and pastries, the new Mint menus bring bold flavor and creativity that represent the airline's hometown dining scene.


Whether customers are starting their day with breakfast or settling in for lunch or dinner, they'll enjoy thoughtfully crafted dishes that showcase bold flavors, elevated culinary technique and the airline’s authentic hospitality that defines its Mint experience.


JetBlue also continues to make personalization a priority, with options that make it easier for customers to tailor their meals, including vegetarian (V), nut-free (NF), dairy-free (DF) and gluten-free (GF) selections.


Featured menu highlights include:


**Breakfast by Birdee**


- Strawberries & cream croissant
- Mochi waffle with butter and maple syrup
- Bacon Egg and Cheese (BEC) on Parker House roll with harissa aioli
- Shakshuka with cherry pepper relish and feta


**Lunch & Dinner by Crown Shy**


- Warm Parker House Roll with olive labne spread
- Crown Shy chicken with citrus marinated thigh and hot sauce vinaigrette
- Pork Katsu with hot cherry glaze and scallions
- Razor Clam chowder with roasted carrots and thyme
- Sweet potato lasagnette with pecorino cream and chervil


**Desserts & Additional Offerings**


- Chocolate tart with sabayon and port wine syrup
- Strawberry olive oil shortcake with meringue and crème fraiche
- Warm chocolate chip cookie by Birdee

**Setting the Mint Standard—and Raising It**


JetBlue Mint offers a fresh take on premium travel, combining fully lie-flat seats, elevated dining, intuitive and residential design features and thoughtful service on select domestic and transatlantic routes. Intentionally designed to bring the comfort and privacy of a boutique hotel to the skies, Mint has redefined premium flying and quickly become a top choice for customers since its debut in 2014.
