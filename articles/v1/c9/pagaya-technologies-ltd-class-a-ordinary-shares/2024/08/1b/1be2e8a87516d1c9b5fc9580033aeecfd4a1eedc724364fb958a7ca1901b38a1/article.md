# BCG Presents Fintech Report at Pagayat | Gen AI Fintech | Pagaya

## **Boston Consulting Group Presents Their Annual Global FinTech Report at Pagaya’s Offices**


Boston Consulting Group joined Pagaya at their offices to present their annual Global FinTech Report. The report aims to draw insights from interviews with dozens of global fintech CEOs and investors. Our CEO, Gal Krubiner, was featured in this year’s report where he shared insights about the emergence of new regulatory frameworks: “It’s caused a huge, dynamic change in the market.”


This year’s report specifically explored topics such as the growth of embedded finance, the rise of connected commerce, the impact of open banking, and GenAI. Alexander Paddington, Deepak Goyal, and Soohan Choi discussed 5 key findings: fintech fundamentals remain strong, embedded finance will be all-pervasive, connected commerce is poised to take off, fintechs need to and can improve EBITDA by 25%, and governments should intervene to establish integrated DPI.
