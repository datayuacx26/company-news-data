# Pagaya Joins CNBC’s Top Fintech Companies 2024 List | Pagaya

# **Pagaya Recognized on CNBC World's Top Fintech Companies 2024 List**


*Honored for innovative digital banking solutions*


We're thrilled that Pagaya has been named to CNBC's World's Top Fintech Companies for 2024 (list[here](https://www.cnbc.com/the-worlds-top-250-fintech-companies-2024/) ). Pagaya received special recognition as a Top 25 Banking Solutions company, acknowledging our commitment to transforming the financial ecosystem through AI-driven innovation and our digital solutions for banks.


The addition of Pagaya to this prestigious list is a testament to our growth and the impact we’re making today.


As we look to expand access to credit for consumers and lending abilities for our partners, we are proud to be recognized along with Plaid, FIS, Nova Credit and others.


As we celebrate this milestone with a standout recognition for a list that received more than 1,500 submissions, we remain focused on our mission to drive innovation and build a more inclusive financial ecosystem.


We’re #PagayaProud of this achievement and invite you to share this exciting news on your social channels with the hashtag #PagayaProud.
