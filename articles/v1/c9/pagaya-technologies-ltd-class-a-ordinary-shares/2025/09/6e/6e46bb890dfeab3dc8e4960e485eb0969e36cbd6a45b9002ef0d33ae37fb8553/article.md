# Learn more about Pagaya President Joins Bloomberg TV to Discuss Buy Now, Pay Later Business Model

**Pagaya’s President and Co-Founder Sanjiv Das joined Bloomberg Markets with Scarlet Fu to discuss how the buy now, pay later lending model is enduring and profitable.**


**“We see many lenders in the ecosystem, including banks and financial institutions, poised to enter this space in a big way. It’s largely driven by consumer demand and greater acceptance of this category. We’re delighted to be right in the middle of that space across the entire lending ecosystem in this asset class.” - Sanjiv Das**
