October 07, 2023


# Pagaya Statement on the Recent Attacks in Israel


We stand together in solidarity with our Israel colleagues and the people of Israel in the wake of the tragic events that unfolded earlier this morning. Our hearts are with all those affected by the senseless acts of violence. At Pagaya, we are committed to the safety and well-being of our colleagues and their families and would like to express our deepest gratitude to the emergency services, law enforcement, and all those who are working tirelessly to find a resolution to the conflict. We stand together as a community in our commitment to peace, safety and stability.
