# Leslie Gillin at TransUnion's Financial Inclusion Forum | Pagaya

## Leslie Gillin Participated in a Fireside Chat at TransUnion's Inaugural Financial Inclusion Forum


TransUnion hosted their first ever[Financial Inclusion Forum](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/transunion_today-we-held-our-inaugural-financial-inclusion-activity-7049124137516044289-qfuI?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop) , featuring intimate discussions with financial services industry leaders around how to better serve historically marginalized consumer segments.


Leslie was interviewed by TransUnion's **Liz Pagel** , **SVP of Consumer Lending** , to talk about Pagaya's business model, how we use predictive datasets and AI to help make better lending decisions (thus extending credit to more consumers) and our data analysis work with TU.


*“We were honored to join TransUnion’s Financial Inclusion Forum to share how the use of data can make a meaningful impact, ensuring more consumers get access to the financial products and services they need to meet their future goals,” said Leslie Gillin, Chief Growth Officer, Pagaya. “Pagaya works with partners like TransUnion to help some of the largest banks, fintechs and auto finance lenders enhance their underwriting decision platforms with our credit intelligence network to bring more consumers into the mainstream economy.”*
