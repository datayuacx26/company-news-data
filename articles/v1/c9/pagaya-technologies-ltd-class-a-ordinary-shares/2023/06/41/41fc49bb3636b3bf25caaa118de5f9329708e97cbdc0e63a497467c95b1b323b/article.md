# Best Structured Credit Award | Pagaya Wins 2023 Hedgeweek Honor

## **Pagaya Wins "Best Structured Credit" Award in 2023 Hedgeweek Awards**


Pagaya is honored to win the 2023 Hedgeweek Award in the category of Best Structured Credit for the *Pagaya Opportunity Fund* .


The Hedgeweek Emerging Manager Awards recognize excellence among hedge fund emerging fund managers and service providers. The program is one of the industry's top programs celebrating a wide spectrum of investment strategies and fund types– encompassing the hedge fund, multi-manager and liquid alternatives universes.


Read more about the awards and winners[here](https://www.hedgeweek.com/2023/06/08/320782/hedgeweek-announces-winners-us-emerging-managers-awards-2023) .
