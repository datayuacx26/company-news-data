# Ads Are Coming to ChatGPT. Here's What It Means for the Future of AI.

Inference is expensive. Fewer than 5 percent of global AI users pay for subscriptions. No software product in history has reached a billion users without a free distribution model. Advertising is the only model that has achieved that level of scale again and again.


OpenAI knows this. They are preparing to bring ChatGPT and their broader model ecosystem to a global audience while turning a profit. Zero dollar per month AI is now the default.


## The Economics Behind the Shift


Most AI products today rely on subscriptions or usage-based billing to generate revenue. The problem is simple. The majority of users will not pay. The conversion rate is single digits worldwide. The cost to serve these users keeps rising as models improve. The math does not work without a way to fund inference at scale.


Every era of the internet has solved this problem the same way. Social networks did it. Search engines did it. Content platforms did it. Free products win distribution. Ads fund free. The cycle repeats.


AI is now entering that same phase, and every consumer AI app must confront a difficult question:


**How do you compete with free from the biggest players in the world?**


There are only two answers.


Either you outscale their compute advantage, which is impossible, or you adopt the one business model that has historically enabled global growth: advertising.

## Why Advertising Works for AI


Advertising is not a compromise. It is the natural fit for conversational systems.


- Users express intent directly in plain language.
- Recommendations can be relevant, helpful, and native to the experience.
- The model itself can identify opportunities for value exchange.
- The economics of inference match the economics of ad impressions.


Most importantly, ads allow AI to remain free.


Free is what creates scale. Scale is what creates defensibility. And defensibility is what creates durable businesses.


AI will not grow without a monetization substrate that supports distribution. This is the layer Imprezia provides.


## The New Reality


AI is shifting into its next phase. The cost curves, the global adoption curves, and the competitive dynamics all point to the same destination. Free AI will be the dominant form of AI.


This does not kill the market. It unlocks it.


- Developers who adapt to this model will reach the world.
