# TravelDealz.ai: Monetizing Conversations

- 7.08% CTR across thousands of intent-matched placements in 30 days.
- The first placement in a session drove 11.01% CTR.
- Intent from conversation drives stronger engagement than traditional techniques.


---


## The Problem


[TravelDealz.ai](https://www.traveldealz.ai/) is a free AI travel chat where users ask about destinations, hotels, flights, and itineraries. It has a growing user base, but every conversation costs money to run. Users expect the product to be free, and traditional display ads don't work in a chat interface. There are no banner slots, no pageviews, no scroll depth. TravelDealz needed a way to turn conversations into revenue without breaking the experience.


## How Imprezia Helped


TravelDealz integrated Imprezia's SDK to earn ad revenue directly from conversations. When a user expresses travel intent, the SDK matches that moment with a relevant travel offer and places it naturally inside the chat. One placement at the moment of intent, delivered in real-time.


## What We Found


Over a recent 30-day window across thousands of conversations:


**Overall CTR: 7.08%**


We also segmented performance by how many placements a user saw within a single session:


Impressions in Session CTR


1 11.01%


2 5.42%


3+ ~2.5%


The first placement in a session, served when intent was strongest, consistently drove the highest engagement.


## Why This Matters for AI Apps


Most AI apps aren't going to build their own ad stack. They shouldn't need to. TravelDealz shows that dropping in a lightweight SDK can turn conversations into ad revenue without hurting the user experience.


The data also shows something worth noting: more placements didn't produce better results. One relevant placement at the right moment outperformed multiple placements across a session. That means earning revenue and keeping users happy aren't competing goals. In conversational surfaces, they actually reinforce each other.


If you're building an AI app, your users are already telling you what they want. Imprezia's SDK turns those signals into revenue.
