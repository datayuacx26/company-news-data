# The Intent Exchange

The future of AI monetization is not the ad exchange. It is the intent exchange.


Ad exchanges still clear media. They will for a long time. But AI shifts value creation earlier in the interaction, to the moment intent is formed, inferred, and acted on. That requires a new exchange layer. The infrastructure does not exist yet.


## The Internet Was Monetized Around Inventory


Web monetization was built around pages, slots, queries, feeds, and impressions. The programmatic stack (SSPs, DSPs, exchanges) was designed to clear attention against media supply. A page had space. An advertiser had a message. The exchange matched them.


That model worked because the unit being bought and sold was inventory. Standardized, addressable, countable. A banner is a banner. A search result is a search result. The infrastructure could treat them uniformly, price them efficiently, and scale.


## AI Creates Intent Before It Creates Inventory


In conversational AI (chat, copilots, agents, discovery flows) the valuable signal is no longer where the user is. It is what they are trying to do right now.


A user in a conversation is not sitting on a page with ad slots. They are mid-decision. They are describing what they need in their own words, refining it in real time, moving toward action. The commercial moment is not a placement. It is the moment their intent becomes actionable.


That signal is richer than page context or keyword proxies. A keyword tells you what someone typed. Conversational intent tells you what they are trying to accomplish, how far along they are, and what would actually help them.


**This is the shift. The unit of value in AI is not the impression. It is the intent signal.**


## Ad Exchanges Clear Media. They Do Not Clear Intent.


This is not an argument that exchanges disappear. They are excellent at what they do. But they are incomplete for AI-native monetization.


Media exchanges expect standardized inventory. AI interactions are fluid, stateful, and semantically rich. There is no page. There is no slot. There is a conversation that evolves with every message.


Someone has to interpret that interaction, structure the decision state, score the commercial opportunity, and route it to the right demand in real time without breaking the experience that made the interaction valuable in the first place. That is not what exchanges do today. It is a new market layer.


## What the Intent Exchange Does


The intent exchange sits between the AI application and the demand ecosystem.


It extracts or infers commercial intent from interaction context. It normalizes that intent into a structured signal. It matches that signal to relevant demand. It clears monetization without breaking UX. And it translates conversational moments into something existing demand infrastructure can already act on.


That last point matters. Today, the intent exchange outputs signals that programmatic pipes can process: structured bid requests, category signals, decision-stage indicators. The demand is there. The pipes are there. What has been missing is the interpretation layer.


Across hundreds of thousands of conversational queries in our own AI product, matched discovery moments have driven click-through rates of 6-7%. Multiples above standard display. The difference is not novelty. It is precision. When you resolve intent instead of interrupting attention, users engage.


Over time, this exchange becomes the primary clearing mechanism for AI monetization. Not just a translator for legacy infrastructure, but the native protocol.


## Every AI App Will Need This Layer


Most AI applications cannot build ad sales, brand safety, relevance scoring, and real-time auctioning themselves. The engineering cost alone would consume the team. Even if they could build it, the demand relationships take years.


Major incumbents will keep their own walled gardens. OpenAI will run its own ads. Google will run its own ads. That is their right and their incentive.


But the broader ecosystem — the thousands of AI applications being built across travel, finance, health, productivity, shopping, and every other vertical — needs an open monetization layer. One designed for conversational context. One that understands intent as the native unit. One that connects to demand without requiring every app to become an adtech company.


That layer is infrastructure. And like all infrastructure, its value compounds with every application that plugs in.


## The Future of AI Monetization


The web monetized attention. Search monetized queries. Social monetized engagement.


**AI monetizes intent.**


The market layer that wins will be the one that can interpret and clear intent in real time, turning conversational moments into discovery, and discovery into revenue, without degrading the experience that created the intent in the first place.


That is the intent exchange. That is the layer we are building at Imprezia.


And we started it by making it work today.
