# Marchex to Report Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Financial Results on Wednesday, March 25, 2026

SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 17, 2026--[Marchex](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.marchex.com&esheet=54451086&newsitemid=20260317372418&lan=en-US&anchor=Marchex&index=1&md5=30380a586f427d67aee2688218f0e514) (NASDAQ: MCHX), which harnesses the power of AI and conversational intelligence to drive operational excellence and revenue acceleration, today announced that it will release financial results for the fourth quarter and full year ended December 31, 2025, on Wednesday, March 25, 2026, at approximately 4:20 p.m. ET. At that time, Marchex will post the press release in the Press Center section of its corporate website ([https://www.marchex.com/about-us/press/](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marchex.com%2Fabout-us%2Fpress%2F&esheet=54451086&newsitemid=20260317372418&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marchex.com%2Fabout-us%2Fpress%2F&index=2&md5=2eef0f54eb74d0d81aef7381e14f2eea) ).


Following the release, management will hold a conference call at 5:00 p.m. ET on Wednesday, March 25, 2026, to discuss the results and outlook for the company. A live webcast will be available on the Investors section of the Marchex website ([http://investors.marchex.com/financial-information/quarterly-results](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.marchex.com%2Ffinancial-information%2Fquarterly-results&esheet=54451086&newsitemid=20260317372418&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.marchex.com%2Ffinancial-information%2Fquarterly-results&index=3&md5=faaf16ced1e7962a2a32b4f6aaef9ce9) ), where an archived version of the webcast will also be available two hours after completion of the call.
