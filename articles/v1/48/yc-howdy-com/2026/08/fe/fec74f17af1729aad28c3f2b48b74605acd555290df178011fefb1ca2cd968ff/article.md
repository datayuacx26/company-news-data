# Martes de Glamour: Howdy's Community Went Ringside in Guadalajara

On August 4th, we hosted Lucha Libre Night in Guadalajara, an exclusive Howdy experience built for developers and tech professionals in the city. No conference hall, no mic and slides. We boarded a party bus, toasted with a welcome cocktail, and closed the night in premium seats at Arena Coliseo, watching live lucha libre, one of Mexico's most iconic traditions.


## **A Party Bus, a Cocktail, and the Arena Coliseo Ring**


The night opened with something you rarely see at a tech event: a party bus winding through Guadalajara while the group got to know each other before even arriving. No forced icebreakers, no name tags. By the time we stepped off the bus, conversations were already underway.


Inside the arena, the energy shifted completely. Luchador masks on, food and drinks moving around, and the ring giving everyone a reason to look up from their phone. Anyone who has watched live lucha libre for the first time knows the feeling: the noise, the theatrics, people jumping out of their seats. That energy spread fast among developers who, an hour earlier, had never met.

## **What the Community Valued Most**


We asked attendees what stuck with them, and the answers repeated: the lucha libre concept itself, described as original and memorable, the food and atmosphere, and above all, the chance to genuinely connect with developers from different backgrounds. One guest mentioned that the Howdy team went out of its way to help when a friend arrived late, a small moment that says more about the night than any headline act could.


One piece of feedback stood out for its simplicity: "The lucha libre idea was very original and made the experience much more fun and memorable." Another guest put it this way: "It felt like a great opportunity to connect with Howdy's team and developers in general." And someone closed with an "excellent organization, thank you so much for the invitation" that, coming from someone who had never heard of Howdy before that night, means more than any metric on a slide.


## **The Numbers Behind the Night**


Speaking of metrics: the night delivered results that confirm what we suspected. Of the 108 leads registered, 56 qualified as MQLs, and among the 36 attendees, 22 were MQLs, a nearly 52% lead-to-MQL conversion rate. Almost half of the people who showed up at Arena Coliseo had never heard of Howdy before: 46.3% were new audience, while the remaining 53.7% already knew the brand. The satisfaction score closed at 98.6% out of 5, and 100% of respondents said this kind of event helps outsiders get to know Howdy better.


## **Why We Keep Building Nights Like This**


At Howdy, we believe the best connections happen outside office hours and far from a LinkedIn form. A lucha libre night doesn't replace a technical talk or a meetup, but it does something those formats don't always manage: it lowers the guard, gives people a genuinely unusual shared experience, and sends everyone home with real contacts instead of just a fresh set of digital cards.


Guadalajara now has its own Martes de Glamour. The next stop isn't confirmed yet, but if this night taught us anything, it's that LATAM's tech community responds better to a luchador mask than to a corporate flyer.
