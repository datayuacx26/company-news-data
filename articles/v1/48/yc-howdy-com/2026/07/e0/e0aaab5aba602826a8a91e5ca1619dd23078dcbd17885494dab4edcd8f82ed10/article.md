# Howdy Premiere Night: An Exclusive Screening of The Odyssey in Buenos Aires

On July 16, we hosted Howdy Premiere Night in Buenos Aires, an exclusive evening that brought together developers from across our community for a private screening of Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey on Argentina's largest IMAX screen. Between the cocktail reception, the movie, and the conversations in between, the night turned into one of the clearest examples yet of what community actually looks like at Howdy.


## **A Cocktail Reception Before the Main Event**


Guests arrived at the theater to a private reception, with tickets designed just for the occasion. Over drinks, developers who might otherwise have crossed paths only online got the chance to talk shop, swap stories, and meet a few members of the Howdy team and recruiting partners face to face.


Before heading into the theater, we shared a short intro about Howdy's mentoring program, as an invitation to think about their next career move.


The reception took place right in the lobby of the same IMAX theater, a short walk from the screen where the movie would play a few hours later. No stage, no formal agenda, just people talking shop over a drink before the lights went down.

## **The Odyssey, the Way It Was Meant to Be Seen**


The main event was a premiere of The Odyssey on the largest IMAX screen in Argentina. It's worth pausing on why that detail mattered beyond the novelty: the film is the first commercial movie shot entirely with IMAX cameras, which means watching it on that screen came about as close as possible to seeing it the way its director intended.


Everyone walked in with popcorn, a movie combo, and a copy of the book that inspired the film. Small details, but the kind that guests kept mentioning afterward, alongside a set of collectible trading cards that made the whole thing feel more like a keepsake than a giveaway.


## **What Stuck With People After the Credits Rolled**


The feedback that came in afterward said more about the night than any recap could. People called out the attention to detail, the warmth of the team members who came up to talk, and the chance to meet peers and recruiters in a setting that had nothing to do with a formal interview. More than one guest mentioned it was their first Howdy event and that they hoped it wouldn't be the last.


One attendee applied for an open role the same day. Several posted about the night on their own accounts, which, as more than one person pointed out, wasn't something they usually did after an event.


It wasn't the first time we've brought the community together over a shared screen, and going by how the night went, it won't be the last.


41 developers spent an evening with us that had very little to do with resumes and a lot to do with actually enjoying being part of this community. That's the kind of night worth doing again.
