# Apollo Changelog: May 19, 2023

Having trouble keeping up with all of these Changelogs? You can get notified about every new Apollo blog post in[our Discord server](https://discord.gg/NEPmWUdKn7) !


## ApolloGraphOS Enterprise self service trial


ApolloGraphOS Enterprise is now available for a[self service trial](https://www.apollographql.com/docs/graphos/enterprise/#enterprise-trial) to the developer community.


## Apollo Kotlin office hours’ stream recording


A couple of weeks ago we had our second office hour livestream, all about Apollo Kotlin! Head over to the[blog post to rewatch it or read the transcript](https://www.apollographql.com/blog/mobile/kotlin/office-hours-apollo-kotlin/) !


And, speaking of Apollo Kotlin, the[first alpha for version 4](https://github.com/apollographql/apollo-kotlin/releases/tag/v4.0.0-alpha.1) was released a few days ago!


Can’t wait for the next office hours? I have good news, then! The next office hours is coming very soon, on the 30th of May at 14:00 UTC to be exact. Lenz will be joining us to talk about Apollo Client![Register here.](https://discord.gg/graphos?event=1109171270769201252)


## Apollo Router v1.19.0


We released Apollo Router v1.19.0 a few hours ago! This release includes improvements on both CPU usage and memory utilization, a new` require_authentication` feature for GraphOS, an update to Federation 2.4.5 (which brings improvements to the query planner), and many more improvements, you can read the[full changelog here](https://github.com/apollographql/router/releases/tag/v1.19.0) !
