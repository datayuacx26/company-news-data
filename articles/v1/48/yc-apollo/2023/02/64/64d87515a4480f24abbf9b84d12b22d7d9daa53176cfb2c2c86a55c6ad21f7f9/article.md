# Apollo Changelog: Feb 6, 2023

## Speak at GraphQL Summit 2023


GraphQL Summit is returning on October 10 in San Diego! We are[accepting proposals](https://summit.graphql.com/event/c51538f6-4b76-44e3-871e-54180c77cad8/websitePage:ee8a1bc8-1286-4106-b34b-f28798e56f93) for talks until Monday March 27. Check out the[event page](https://summit.graphql.com/event/c51538f6-4b76-44e3-871e-54180c77cad8/summary) for more details—we’ll see you there!


## A full supergraph on Kubernetes


Our solutions team maintains a[tutorial](https://github.com/apollosolutions/build-a-supergraph) on building a supergraph on Kubernetes. The tutorial includes examples for deploying to both Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Amazon Web Services (AWS). If that sounds like your mix of technologies, go check it out!


## Serverless supergraphs


Last week we hosted a livestream on[our Discord server](https://discord.gg/graphos) about deploying Python subgraphs on AWS Lambda. This week we’re following up with the sequel, where we’ll do the same thing with Apollo Server and TypeScript! Join the server before Wednesday, February 8th, to participate.


## The future of GraphQL routing


If you missed “The future of GraphQL routing” at GraphQL Summit 2022, you can now watch[a condensed version on YouTube](https://youtu.be/xih3e9BXYP4) ! Subscribe to the[Apollo GraphQL channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ApolloGraphQL) to be notified of future Summit supercuts.


## Load testing and server-driven UIs


Our solutions team regularly encounters real-world challenges and shares their learnings in the[Tech Notes](https://www.apollographql.com/docs/technotes/) section of our docs. Here’s what’s new this week:


1. Load testing is an important practice for large-scale applications, and it comes with some unique challenges for distributed systems (like federated graphs).[“Load testing a federated GraphQL API”](https://www.apollographql.com/docs/technotes/TN0035-load-testing-graphql/) provides a high level look at some challenges you should expect, along with some common solutions to them.
2. Interested in both server-driven UIs and GraphQL?[“Server-Driven UI Schema Patterns”](https://www.apollographql.com/docs/technotes/TN0033-sdui-patterns/) gives you a glimpse at how a server-driven UI might look when implemented in GraphQL.


## Rapid roundup


- [Apollo Client 3.7.6](https://github.com/apollographql/apollo-client/releases/tag/v3.7.6) &[3.7.7](https://github.com/apollographql/apollo-client/releases/tag/v3.7.7) bring improvements to TypeScript,` useLazyQuery` , and` MockedProvider` along with performance gains and bug fixes.
