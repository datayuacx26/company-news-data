# Apollo Changelog: Apr 10, 2023

## Stay in the know


We recently enabled the new “Community Onboarding” feature within[Discord](https://discord.gg/graphos) . You can now customize which types of notifications you receive, discover channels based on your interests, and show off more info about you!


**Want to be notified of future blog posts, live events, or GraphOS incidents from Discord?** Click “Channels & Roles” and select the notifications you’re interested in!


## Apollo iOS 1.1


The[latest version of Apollo iOS](https://github.com/apollographql/apollo-ios/releases/tag/1.1.0) enables manual initialization of generated models. This makes it easier to write manual data to the cache, spin up models for unit tests, create UI fixtures (loading states and Swift UI previews), and more! Additionally, this release comes with support for HTTP multipart subscriptions. In most cases, upgrading to 1.1 should require no changes to your code, so you can update today!

## Community spotlight

We’re incredibly excited about the variety of talks and are hard at work reviewing. If you’ve submitted a talk, expect to hear from us by April 28. There are several new releases improving Federation support across the ecosystem:


- **Python:**[Graphene](https://github.com/graphql-python/graphene-federation) now supports Federation v2 🎉
- **Python:**[Ariadne](https://github.com/mirumee/ariadne) added support for` @interfaceObject`
- **Scala:**[Caliban](https://github.com/ghostdogpr/caliban) added support for` @interfaceObject` and` @composeDirective`


Escape recently put out[a blog post](https://escape.tech/blog/should-i-disable-introspection-in-graphql/) challenging the conventional wisdom that GraphQL introspection should be disabled in production. What do you think? How are you using introspection? We’re starting discussion threads for all blog posts[in our Discord server](https://discord.gg/graphos) and we’d love to hear your thoughts!
