# The Apollo Team Goes to Europe!

It’s going to be a huge week in the GraphQL community with GraphQL Europe, the first ever European GraphQL conference! So we’re hitting the road and stopping by several cities and events to give some talks and connect with the community. We hope to see you there!


You’ll be able to find us by looking for the flashy “Ask me about GraphQL” shirts. Make sure to say hi, tell us what you’re up to, ask us any questions you have about GraphQL, and score some Apollo stickers while you’re at it!


To keep track of what we’re up to and where you can hang out with us,[follow @apollographql on Twitter](https://twitter.com/apollographql) , or our personal accounts below!


- Sashko:[@stubailo](https://twitter.com/stubailo)
- Danielle:[@danimman](https://twitter.com/danimman)

Here’s a rundown of where we’ll be:


## [React Europe](https://www.react-europe.org/) , Paris — May 18 & 19


Last year, React Europe was a huge conference for GraphQL, with several awesome talks about the future of GraphQL, best practices, and GraphQL clients. We’re looking forward to talking with React developers about how they are using GraphQL, and helping people get started! If you’re already excited, make sure to check out our[Full-Stack GraphQL + React Tutorial](https://dev-blog.apollodata.com/full-stack-react-graphql-tutorial-582ac8d24e3b) .


The night before the conference, come hang out with us and other conference attendees at the[bar night at The Frog](https://medium.com/@ReactEurope/reacteurope-bar-night-party-on-the-17th-of-may-for-reacteurope-attendees-e0744e80702) !


There’s some great GraphQL content lined up:


- If you’re signed up, definitely make sure not to miss[Max Stoiber](https://medium.com/u/908fb8fea30c?source=post_page-----b7f4a9cffa7c----------------------) and[Nik Graf](https://medium.com/u/ff3f225e7f5c?source=post_page-----b7f4a9cffa7c----------------------) ’s[workshop on React and GraphQL](https://www.react-europe.org/#schedule-item-782) .
- On the first day,[Danielle Man](https://medium.com/u/47b3b199a712?source=post_page-----b7f4a9cffa7c----------------------) from our team is giving a[lightning talk](https://www.react-europe.org/#schedule-item-846) about GraphQL developer tools.
- On day two,[Lee Byron](https://medium.com/u/d9b1a61823fa?source=post_page-----b7f4a9cffa7c----------------------) is giving us a[rundown](https://www.react-europe.org/#schedule-item-748) of Relay Modern (see our[launch recap post](https://dev-blog.apollodata.com/exploring-relay-modern-276f5965f827) ).


But most importantly, come find us during the breaks and hallways, and let’s all get to know each other! Meeting people that you know from the internet in person is the best part of every conference.


## [GraphQL Europe](https://graphql-europe.com/) , Berlin — May 21


GraphQL-Europe, organized by our friends at[Graphcool](https://www.graph.cool/) (congrats on the product launch today!) is the first GraphQL conference in europe, and the second ever! Having conferences dedicated to the technology really shows that GraphQL is getting big. All of the talks are about GraphQL, so we won’t list them all here, but you should definitely read up on the[full schedule](https://graphql-europe.com/schedule) . Here is what you’ll hear about from us:


- [Jonas Helfer](https://medium.com/u/39cbd38c57c8?source=post_page-----b7f4a9cffa7c----------------------) is speaking about: “[GraphQL: Evolution or Revolution](https://graphql-europe.com/schedule/graphql-evolution-or-revolution) ”, covering how GraphQL compares to other contemporary or historical API technologies.
- [Sashko Stubailo](https://medium.com/u/803918030a60?source=post_page-----b7f4a9cffa7c----------------------) is speaking about “[Adding GraphQL to your existing architecture](https://graphql-europe.com/schedule/adding-graphql-to-your-existing-architecture) ”, dispelling the concern that GraphQL is something you can’t get on top of your existing backends and technologies.


Everyone else listed above from our team will be there as well! If you’re on the[Apollo Slack team](http://dev.apollodata.com/#slack) already, join us in the #graphql-europe channel, and you can also join the[Graphcool Slack team](https://t.co/JrA9D6ZESb) .


## [GraphQL London meetup](https://www.meetup.com/GraphQL-London/events/239701157/) — May 24


As our last stop, we’re going to the next GraphQL London meetup, hosted at[Pusher](https://pusher.com/)[office](https://maps.google.com/maps?f=q&hl=en&q=28+Scrutton+St%2C+London+EC2A+4RP%2C+UK%2C+London%2C+gb) ! It starts at 6:15PM, and food and drinks are on us! If you aren’t in London, don’t worry, the talks will be recorded!


You’ll hear from Jonas about the design and development of the recently launched Apollo Client 1.0, and some other talks from people in the community!
