# Meet our speakers for GraphQL Summit 2019!

[GraphQL Summit](https://summit.graphql.com/) , held in SF on October 30 and 31, is the world’s largest conference dedicated to GraphQL. This year will feature over 60 technical talks across 3 tracks, selected to further your GraphQL knowledge with educational sessions that provide practical next steps for your next project. And with 1,200 attendees, the networking and learning opportunities will be many. Here is a small selection of great talks we’re thrilled to have in the 2019 lineup:


- **Scaling GraphQL Beyond a Backend for Frontend** by[Michelle Garrett](https://twitter.com/msmichellegar) &[Faduma Mohammed](https://twitter.com/FadumaFaralacag) from Condé Nast International
- **How do you get changes made to GraphQL?** by[Orta](https://twitter.com/orta) from Microsoft
- **Caching & GraphQL: Setting the Story Straight** by[Marc-Andrè Giroux](https://twitter.com/__xuorig__) from GitHub
- **State Management in GraphQL using React Hooks & Apollo** by[Shruti Kapoor](https://twitter.com/shrutikapoor08) from PayPal
- **GraphQL in Every Language** by[Jon Wong](https://twitter.com/jnwng) from Coursera
- **Client-side GraphQL at scale** by[Chris Sauvè](https://twitter.com/_lemonmade) from Shopify
- **Migrating to Apollo at Airbnb** by[Brie Bunge](https://twitter.com/briebunge) from Airbnb


Discover over 40 more speakers and their[sessions here.](https://summit.graphql.com/speakers)
