# VINFAST REPORTS DELIVERIES OF 21,781 ELECTRIC VEHICLES IN JULY 2026 IN VIETNAM

***Hanoi, August 11, 2026 – VinFast Auto Ltd. (“VinFast” or the “Company”) announced preliminary domestic Vietnam deliveries of 21,781 electric vehicles (“EVs”) for July 2026, representing an increase of 21% compared to the previous month. Year to date, the Company has delivered a preliminary total of 137,697 EVs to customers in Vietnam, reaffirming its leadership in the domestic automotive market.***


******


Notably, the VF 3 was VinFast’s best-selling model in July, with 5,564 vehicles delivered during the month. The VF 5 and the Herio Green, which is derived from the VF 5, collectively ranked second, with 4,407 vehicles delivered. The Limo Green recorded deliveries of 4,404 vehicles, followed by the Minio Green, with 2,429 vehicles delivered. The VF 6 recorded 1,993 vehicles delivered, while the VF MPV 7 recorded 1,674 vehicles delivered.


For the first seven months of 2026, the Limo Green and the VF 3 were VinFast’s two best-selling models in Vietnam with cumulative deliveries of 32,331 and 29,345 vehicles, respectively. The Company also delivered 24,574 VF 5 and Herio Green vehicles, 16,038 VF 6 vehicles, and 10,851 VF MPV 7 vehicles during the period.


In addition, in July 2026, VinFast opened pre-orders for the VF 2 and received approximately 28,700 reservations1 within the first three days, reflecting strong consumer demand. VinFast plans to commence the deliveries of the VF 8 new platform and the VF 2 in August 2026 and September 2026, respectively.
