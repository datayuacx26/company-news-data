# VINFAST REPORTS DELIVERIES OF 19,503 ELECTRIC VEHICLES IN MAY 2026 IN VIETNAM, BRINGING YEAR-TO-DATE DELIVERIES TO NEARLY 100,000 VEHICLES

***Hanoi, June 10, 2026 – VinFast Auto Ltd. (“VinFast” or the “Company”) announced preliminary domestic Vietnam deliveries of 19,503 electric vehicles (“EVs”) for May 2026. Year to date, the Company has delivered a preliminary total of 97,961 EVs to customers in Vietnam, reaffirming its leadership in the domestic automotive market and bringing cumulative deliveries in Vietnam to nearly 100,000 vehicles within the first five months of 2026.***


Notably, Limo Green continued to be VinFast’s best-selling model in May, with 5,108 vehicles being delivered during the month. The VF 3 ranked second, with 4,770 vehicles delivered. The VF 5 and the Herio Green, which is derived the VF 5, recorded a total of 3,399 vehicles delivered, followed by the VF MPV 7, with 1,711 vehicles delivered. The VF 6 recorded 1,674 vehicles delivered, while the EC Van recorded 1,092 vehicles delivered and the VF 7 recorded 750 vehicles delivered.


For the first five months of 2026, the Limo Green and the VF 3 were VinFast’s two best-selling models in Vietnam with cumulative deliveries of 24,059 and 20,231 vehicles, respectively. The Company also delivered 16,803 VF 5 and Herio Green vehicles, 11,057 VF 6 vehicles, 7,923 VF MPV 7 vehicles, 5,412 VF 7 vehicles and 4,071 EC Van vehicles during the period.
