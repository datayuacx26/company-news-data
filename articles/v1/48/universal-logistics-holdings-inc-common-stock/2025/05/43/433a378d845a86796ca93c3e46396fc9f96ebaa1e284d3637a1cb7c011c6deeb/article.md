# Storm Season & Supply Chains: How to Keep Freight Moving

Preparation is the first line of defense. A storm-season contingency plan should cover alternative shipping routes, secondary distribution centers, and emergency communication protocols. Think of it as your playbook when the weather doesn’t cooperate.


**Example:** If a major hurricane is forecasted to hit the southeastern U.S., having a rerouting plan through the Midwest or Northeast can prevent freight from sitting idle.
