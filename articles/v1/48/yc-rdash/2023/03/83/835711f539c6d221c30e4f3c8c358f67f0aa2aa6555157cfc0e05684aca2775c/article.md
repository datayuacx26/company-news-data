Our values help us defining the culture and guiding our actions. They serve as a set of guiding principles that shape the way that the we operates and how we interacts with stakeholders.


## How our values drive us


1. We encourage employees to align their actions with company values. We also encourage new employees to think about how their actions and behaviors reflect the values of the company and to strive to embody the values in their work
2. We offer new employees opportunities to get involved in projects or initiatives that align with the company values. This will help them to understand the values more deeply and to see how they can contribute to the company’s success.
3. We recognize and reward employees who demonstrate the company values in their work. This helps to reinforce the importance of the values and encourages others to follow suit.


We have defined our values as a mnemonic **GOALS,** where each letter stand for one of our values.

` Getting things done` as a company value is about focusing on results and taking action to achieve them. It emphasizes the importance of being productive and making progress towards goals.


` Ownership` as a company value refers to the idea that each employee is responsible for their own work and the success of the company. It means that employees are encouraged to take ownership of their tasks and to be proactive in their approach to work.


` Approachable` as a company value refers to the idea that employees are expected to be friendly, open, and easy to approach. It emphasizes the importance of creating a welcoming and inclusive work environment where employees feel comfortable interacting with one another and seeking help or guidance when needed.


` Learning mindset` as a company value refers to the idea that employees are expected to be open to learning and growth, and to embrace opportunities for personal and professional development. It emphasizes the importance of a continuous learning culture, where employees are encouraged to seek out new knowledge and skills and to take an active role in their own development.


` Self-managed` as a company value refers to the idea that employees are expected to be responsible for their own work and to take ownership of their tasks and responsibilities. It emphasizes the importance of individual accountability and the ability to work independently and manage one's own time and workload effectively.


Overall, These values help us create a positive culture that fosters collaboration, innovation, and engagement. They set expectations for behavior and communication, and promote a sense of community and belonging.
