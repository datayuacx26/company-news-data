# Why we have an engineering handbook?

3 min read

Whenever someone joins Rdash engineering department, he/she would be overwhelmed by the amount of information and context that they need to process. There are multiple teams, processes, tools, and guidelines that one has to get accustomed to. The engineering handbook serves as a comprehensive guide to streamline this onboarding process and provide a clear understanding of what is expected from them. It also acts as a reference guide for engineers to refer to whenever they need to clarify a guideline or process.


The objective of this handbook is simple, it act as an operating system for engineering department. We have extensively written down everything from the values that we cultivate in employee to coding guidelines that we want to enforce.


Our values section in the engineering handbook provides a clear understanding of the core principles and beliefs that guide our work. We believe in transparency, collaboration, and continuous improvement, and we strive to embody these values in everything we do. By aligning our actions with these values, we can better serve our customers, improve our products, and create a positive work environment for everyone on the team.

Then we have a section dedicated to our working style. It has everything from building things efficiently to guidelines for work from home. As an organization, we value a lot the practice of writing things down whenever we discuss things. Then we have guidelines to conduct effective 1–1 meetings.


We have also codified our rituals that we follow as part of our working, such as sprint planning, retrospectives, and daily stand-ups. These rituals ensure that everyone on the team is aligned and working towards the same goals, and that we are continuously improving our processes and products.

The engineering handbook also includes a section on career growth and development opportunities. This section outlines the different career paths available to engineers at Rdash and provides guidance on how to progress and grow within the organization. By providing a clear understanding of career growth opportunities, we can help our engineers to set goals and work towards achieving them. This not only benefits the individual engineer but also the organization as a whole, as we can ensure that we have a talented and motivated workforce that is equipped to tackle any challenge.


Finally, the engineering handbook includes technical guidelines and best practices for coding, code reviews, testing, and deployment. These guidelines help to ensure that our code is maintainable, scalable, and secure, and that we are delivering high-quality products to our customers. By following these guidelines, we can minimize errors and bugs, reduce technical debt, and improve the overall performance of our products. In short, the engineering handbook serves as a comprehensive guide to help engineers at Rdash understand our values, working style, and technical guidelines, and to help them operate at the highest level of productivity and success.


The engineering handbook is a living document that is constantly updated and improved based on feedback from engineers and changes in our organization. It is a reflection of our culture and values, and it helps to ensure that everyone on the team is working towards the same goals and delivering high-quality products to our customers.
