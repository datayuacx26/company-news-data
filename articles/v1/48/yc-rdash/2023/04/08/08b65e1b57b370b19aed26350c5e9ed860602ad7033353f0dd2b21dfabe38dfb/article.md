Many people believe that startups thrive on being disorganized. They think that there is no time for planning when you can ship the whole thing in that time. It’s all about moving fast and breaking things. The winning idea needs to be executed on the same day itself and prioritized over everything else.

However, what I have learnt is quite different:


- All of the efficiency in getting things done starts with being organized, no matter what stage the startup is in. Planning for your team’s time, is essential.
- The duration of planning will vary according to the stage of the product. In the initial stage, plan your days, and then plan weeks and months for late-stage companies.
- Your product is not a single winning idea. It is a series of winning ideas put together, which are iterated over with customer feedback. Therefore, it is crucial to build a system that executes winning ideas on a regular basis, rather than an ad-hoc basis. Priorities will change quite frequently, but that should not discourage you from planning.
- It is often said to move fast and break things, but moving fast randomly will not help. However, moving fast with a direction will help. You might have to change direction frequently, but don’t move in too many directions at once.
- It is essential to strike a balance between being agile and being organized. Being too rigid can stifle the creativity and innovation, but being too loose can lead to chaos and inefficiency.
- Prioritization is key. Knowing which tasks are most important and which can wait, helps to keep the team focused and productive.
- Communication is crucial. Keeping everyone on the same page with clear goals and expectations can help to avoid misunderstandings and keep the team working together towards a common goal.


Startup culture is not about being disorganized or moving fast randomly, but it is about building a system that executes winning ideas on a regular basis with direction. Prioritization, communication, and being agile and organized are also key factors in startup success.
