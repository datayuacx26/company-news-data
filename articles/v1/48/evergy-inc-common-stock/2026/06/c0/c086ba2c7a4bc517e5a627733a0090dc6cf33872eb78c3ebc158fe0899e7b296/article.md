# Energy Insights and Predictions for Evergy Energy Partners

Navigating a Rapidly Evolving Energy Landscape


The energy landscape in the United States continues to evolve at a rapid pace, driven by decarbonization goals, rising electricity demand, and the need for a more resilient grid. One of the most significant developments shaping this transformation is the expansion of the


[Southwest Power Pool (SPP)](https://www.spp.org/western-services/rto-expansion/) into the Western Interconnection, creating the first Regional Transmission Organization (RTO) to span both major U.S. grids.


This shift toward broader coordination—combined with continued investment in infrastructure and clean energy—signals a new era for how electricity is generated, delivered, and managed. Evergy Energy Partners (EEP) remains focused on helping clients navigate this changing landscape by combining strategic insight into practical solutions.


Policy and Investment Momentum


Recent years have seen unprecedented levels of investment in energy infrastructure and clean generation. Utilities across the Midwest are


[advancing large-scale capital plans](https://newsroom.evergy.com/Economic-growth-drives-new-generation-plans) that include new renewable resources, transmission upgrades, and dispatchable generations to meet growing demand.


This momentum reflects a broader industry shift toward balancing reliability, affordability, and sustainability, especially as load growth accelerates due to economic development and electrification.


Decarbonization and Demand Growth


As electricity demand continues to rise, the push toward decarbonization remains central. Power providers are planning for a future that integrates renewable energy, improves energy efficiency, and incorporates emerging technologies such as energy storage.


At the same time, this transition requires balancing intermittent renewable generation with reliable, dispatchable resources to maintain grid stability while advancing long-term carbon reduction goals.


Recent Project Milestones and Developments


Evergy Energy Partners continues to support communities and partners through the development of new energy projects and infrastructure investments.


Osawatomie Solar Array in Kansas


A key milestone includes the completion and ribbon cutting of a community


[solar array in Osawatomie](https://blog.evergyenergypartners.com/case-study-osawatomie-solar-array) . Developed in partnership with Evergy Energy Partners, this approximately 5 MW project now provides clean, locally generated power while helping diversify the community’s energy portfolio.


Ongoing Solar Development Activity


Solar projects continue to advance across the region, reflecting strong demand from municipalities and cooperatives seeking long-term price stability and sustainability benefits. These efforts build on recent groundwork and demonstrate growing interest in distributed energy solutions.


Grid Expansion and Market Evolution


The expansion of SPP into the Western Interconnection represents a major turning point for the industry. By integrating utilities across multiple western states into a unified wholesale market, SPP has improved coordination, reliability, and access to diverse energy resources.


This evolution supports:


- More efficient generation dispatch across regions


- Improved transmission planning and congestion management


- Greater market access and pricing transparency


At the same time, infrastructure investment remains critical.


[New transmission projects](https://okenergytoday.com/2026/01/evergy-transmission-line-in-kansas-and-oklahoma-to-send-power-to-missouri/) across Kansas and neighboring states are being developed to reduce congestion, improve reliability, and connect renewable generation to demand centers more effectively.


Looking Ahead


The energy sector is entering a period defined by both opportunity and complexity. Continued load growth, evolving market structures, and the integration of new technologies will shape the grid of the future.


As the industry continues to transform, staying informed and adaptable will be critical. Expanding markets, new infrastructure, and evolving policy frameworks are creating both challenges and opportunities for utilities, municipalities, and cooperatives.
