# Fractional CFO Services for SaaS Startups: A 2026 Guide

## What Is a Fractional CFO?


Fractional CFO services give a growth-stage company a part-time chief financial officer who owns strategic finance on a fixed monthly retainer, not a full-time salary. They translate the numbers into decisions a founder and board can act on, without the cost or commitment of a full-time executive hire.


A part-time CFO, sometimes called an outsourced CFO, sits above bookkeeping and controllership, not beside it. Here is the finance ladder for a growing SaaS business:


- **Bookkeeper.** Records transactions and reconciles accounts. Answers “what happened last month.”
- **Controller.** Owns the monthly close, GAAP accuracy, and ASC 606 revenue recognition. Answers “are the books right.”
- **Fractional CFO.** Owns forward-looking strategy. Answers “what should we do next, and can we afford it.”
- **CFO advisor.** Advice-only. Talks through decisions, but doesn’t build the model or own the deliverables.


Most founders reach for the top rung only after a bookkeeper and controller are already in place. The trigger is a question the historical books can’t answer. The modern CFO role has shifted toward exactly that strategic work, which[AICPA and CIMA research](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/industry/changing-role-of-the-cfo) frames as the move from financial steward to chief value creator.


Hiring a CFO for SaaS company reporting means owning the metrics investors actually underwrite:


- ARR and MRR, plus the bridge between them
- Net revenue retention (NRR) and gross revenue retention (GRR)
- Rule of 40 and CAC payback period
- Burn multiple and cash runway
- Deferred revenue and ASC 606 treatment


Unlike a CFO advisor who only talks through the decision, or a bookkeeper who only records the past, a fractional CFO builds the model and sits across the table from investors during a raise. If you want to map that need against your stage, our companion piece on[why growing businesses are turning to fractional CFOs](https://www.indinero.com/blog/fractional-cfo-growing-business/) covers the same ground.

## Scope of Work / What’s Included


A SaaS fractional CFO owns seven deliverables, from board reporting and fundraising support to KPI dashboards and forecast-versus-actual FP&A. Everything else is bookkeeping, controllership, tax, or equity administration, and gets handled separately.


1. **Investor relations and board reporting.** The board deck, the metrics package, and the narrative behind them. Per[NACD governance resources](https://blog.nacdonline.org/) , boards should review the core statements and a cash-runway projection at every meeting. A fractional CFO produces that package.
2. **Due-diligence and fundraising support.** Data-room prep, the model investors will stress-test, and live Q&A during a raise. This is the deliverable that spikes hours when a round is active.
3. **Financial modeling and scenario planning.** The three-statement model plus hiring, pricing, and fundraising scenarios. Our primer on[what financial modeling is](https://www.indinero.com/blog/financial-modeling/) breaks down the mechanics.
4. **Burn and runway analysis.** Cash-out date under multiple scenarios, burn multiple, and the trigger points where you must raise or cut.
5. **KPI dashboards.** The ARR/MRR bridge, NRR and GRR, CAC payback, and Rule of 40, tracked monthly and mirrored into the board package.
6. **M&A and strategic advisory.** Sell-side or buy-side support, valuation framing, and deal modeling when it’s relevant.
7. **FP&A, forecast versus actual.** The monthly variance analysis that ties the plan to reality and explains the drivers to the board.


What a fractional CFO does NOT do is worth stating plainly. Audit work belongs to an independent audit firm, not the CFO, though the CFO prepares you for it. Tax filing is a separate service line. Day-to-day bookkeeping and the monthly close belong to the bookkeeper and controller. Cap-table and equity administration live in Carta or Pulley, though the CFO reads from them.


When those functions sit with four different vendors, coordination lands on you. The founder becomes the integration layer.


## When You’re Ready for a Fractional CFO


You’re ready for a fractional CFO when a board-level question outgrows what your bookkeeper can answer. The clearest triggers are a closed funding round, a first audit notice, a multi-state nexus letter, headcount crossing 25, or burn becoming a standing board topic.


**Seed ($1M to $3M ARR).** Usually too early for a full engagement, but the first trigger shows up here. The CEO gets asked about runway in a board meeting and can’t answer it fast. A light tier handles runway modeling and a basic metrics dashboard, and little else.


**Series A ($3M to $10M ARR).** This is the center of gravity for fractional CFO for SaaS adoption. The board now expects a real metrics package every quarter. The round closes and investors want investor-grade reporting plus a 409A. A sales-tax nexus letter arrives after you cross economic thresholds in new states. Burn becomes a recurring board topic and you need genuine FP&A, not a spreadsheet rebuilt at 11pm.


**Series B and beyond ($10M to $20M+ ARR).** Scope deepens toward M&A readiness, tighter scenario planning, audit management, and eventually the question of whether to convert the fractional seat into a full-time hire. The Robert Half comp data is the number that decision turns on.


You don’t have to buy the top tier on day one. Start with indinero for bookkeeping at $750/mo and expand into fractional CFO advisory when you cross your next revenue threshold. No need to switch firms. ## Fractional vs Alternative Models


The right finance model depends on your stage and complexity, not on which vendor markets hardest. Here is how a fractional CFO stacks up against the alternatives, and who each one actually fits.


**Fractional CFO vs full-time CFO.** A full-time CFO starts at $195,500 to $321,750 in base salary alone, per the Robert Half 2026 Salary Guide, before bonus, equity, benefits, and recruiting cost. A fractional engagement in the $750 to $5,250 per month range is a fraction of that loaded cost. Full-time fits a $20M+ ARR company near IPO or running complex multi-entity operations. Fractional fits the $1M to $20M SaaS company that needs the strategy but would leave a full-time seat idle most of the week.


**Fractional CFO vs CFO advisor.** An advisor talks through the decision on an hourly or light retainer. A fractional CFO builds the model and owns the deliverable. An advisor fits a founder who can build the board model themselves and just wants a sounding board. A fractional CFO fits the founder who needs the work done, not just discussed.


**Fractional CFO vs doing it yourself.** DIY is “free” in cash and expensive in founder hours. It fits the pre-institutional stage. It stops fitting the day the model is only as good as the hours you can spare for it after everything else.


**Fractional CFO vs bookkeeper only.** A bookkeeper records the past for roughly $5K to $12K a year. That fits a pre-revenue company with no board and no raise on the horizon. It’s the wrong tool for any company facing a raise, an audit, or a runway question. For the full-time crossover math, our guide on[deciding between a fractional CFO and a full-time CFO](https://www.indinero.com/blog/fractional-cfo/) works the numbers.


The most expensive mistake is staying on the bottom rungs one stage too long. The second is hiring a full-time CFO at Series A to look ready, then paying $300K for a 40%-utilized seat.
