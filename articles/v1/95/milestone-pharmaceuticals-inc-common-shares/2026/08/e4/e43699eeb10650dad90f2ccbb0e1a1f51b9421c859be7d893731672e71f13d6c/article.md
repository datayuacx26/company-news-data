**About Milestone Pharmaceuticals**


Milestone Pharmaceuticals Inc. (Nasdaq: MIST) is an emerging commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company advancing innovative cardiovascular medicines to benefit people living with certain heart conditions. Milestone’s lead product is CARDAMYST™ (etripamil) nasal spray, a novel calcium channel blocker, which is FDA-approved for the conversion of acute symptomatic episodes of paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia (PSVT) to sinus rhythm in adults. Etripamil is also in development for the control of symptomatic episodic attacks associated with AFib-RVR.[https://milestonepharma.com/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=vnhA9LO7YrTfW70L_Fw2dFeAKaRWpFwCdUlKPYH68h4ls1Q1Q0LxDvFpKXtYFVpiF_21I27_OisnXPpvqSIyBJTcYhy_L7EITvJQIuIFI9cZHqQ2YB7leBPo-JVQ6U2t)
