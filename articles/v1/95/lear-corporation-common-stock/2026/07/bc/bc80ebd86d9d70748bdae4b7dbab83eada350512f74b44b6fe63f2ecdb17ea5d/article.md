# Lear Announces Date for Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Conference Call

**Southfield, Mich., June 26, 2026** -- Lear Corporation (NYSE: LEA), will hold a conference call to review the company’s second quarter 2026 financial results and related matters on July 31, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. EDT.


**To participate in the conference call:**


• Webcast link:[click here](https://ir.lear.com/events-and-presentations)


• Toll-free calls: 877-883-0383


• International calls: 412-902-6506


The conference code is 1239896.


You also may listen to the live audio webcast of the call, in listen-only mode, on Lear’s Investor Relations website at[ir.lear.com](https://ir.lear.com/) . The webcast replay will be available two hours following the call.


Note: The second quarter 2026 slide presentation will be available on Lear’s website before the earnings call begins on July 31, 2026.
