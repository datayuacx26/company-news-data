# Lear to Participate in the Bank of America 2026 Global Automotive Summit

SOUTHFIELD, Mich., March 4, 2026 -- Lear Corporation (NYSE: LEA), a global automotive technology leader in Seating and E-Systems, will participate in a fireside chat on March 17, 2026, at the Bank of America


2026 Global Automotive Summit in New York.


Representing Lear will be:


• Ray Scott, President and CEO


• Jason Cardew, SVP and Chief Financial Officer


The live webcast will begin at 8:50 AM EDT and will run approximately 40 minutes. The webcast link will be available on the Company’s website at[ir.lear.com](https://ir.lear.com/events-and-presentations) .
