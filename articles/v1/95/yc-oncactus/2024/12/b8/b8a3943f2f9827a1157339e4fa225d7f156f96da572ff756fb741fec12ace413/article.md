# Cravd raises $750K pre-seed


[Ajith Govind](https://substack.com/@ajith747)


and[Avinash Joshi](https://substack.com/@itsavinash)


Dec 06, 2024


🚨🚨 BIG NEWS! Cravd has raised $750K pre-seed to bring healthy and affordable home cooked meals to more families. Since launching in May, we've grown 40% month-over-month through referrals, hitting $20K+ in monthly subscriptions with 90% renewals 🚀


Every day, thousands of busy families struggle to put healthy meals on the table. Our solution at Cravd is to build an AI-powered marketplace connecting families with home chefs who cook in your kitchen.


[Avinash](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joshi-avinash/) and I are thrilled to be working on our second company together. As first-time dads building our previous startup, we couldn't find time for healthy meals. It turns out 90% of North Americans spend $4,000+ dining out yearly because they don't have time to cook either.


With


[Cravd](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cravd/) , we’re creating the new default to unhealthy and expensive restaurants, delivery, and meal kits. Here’s how two recent Cravd subscribers describe the experience,


- "Thanks to Cravd, we've transformed our health and lifestyle. My husband lost 6 kgs, and we both feel more energized and healthier than ever."


- "I've been using Cravd for 4 months, and it's great having a personal chef come to our home and prepare meals tailored to our specific diet and macros."
