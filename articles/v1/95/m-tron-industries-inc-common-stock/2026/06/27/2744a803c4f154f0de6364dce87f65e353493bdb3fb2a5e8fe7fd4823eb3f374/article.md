# Join Mtron at IMS 2026 in Boston June 9-11, 2026

IMS2026 is just 6 days away, and Mtron is looking forward to connecting with the RF and microwave community in Boston.


For 61 years, Mtron has designed and manufactured high-reliability filters, resonators, oscillators, and RF solutions for systems where performance and reliability matter. From material science and design to manufacturing and testing, our vertically integrated capabilities support critical applications across SATCOM, radar, electronic warfare, communications, and test & measurement.


Please visit Mtron at Booth #18025 to:
• Explore high-precision RF and timing solutions
• See a live demonstration of our ultra-low phase noise OCXO
• Discuss technical challenges and emerging RF trends with our engineering team
• Learn how Mtron supports demanding performance requirements across aerospace, defense, and communications systems


Whether you’re developing next-generation RF systems or looking for a long-term technology partner, we’d welcome the opportunity to connect.


IMS2026 takes place June 7–12 at the Thomas M. Menino Convention and Exhibition Center in Boston, Massachusetts.


We look forward to seeing you IEEE International MTT Symposia (IMS) 2026!


To schedule a meeting, contactsales@mtron.com .
