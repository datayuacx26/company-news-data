### M-tron Industries, Inc. Reminds Investors of Upcoming Investor Day on May 12, 2026


May 05, 2026


ORLANDO, Fla.


, May 4, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- M-tron Industries, Inc. ("Mtron" or the "Company") today reminded investors that it will host its previously announced Investor Day on Tuesday, May 12, 2026 and encourages all interested attendees to register.


The event will feature presentations from members of the Company's executive leadership team, who will provide an overview of Mtron's strategy, operational priorities, and long-term growth initiatives. Management will also discuss recent business developments and financial performance, followed by a question-and-answer session.


**Event Details:**


**Date:**


May 12, 2026


**Time:**


12:00 p.m. to 1:30 p.m. ET


**Location:**


New York Stock Exchange


11 Wall Street


New York, NY 10005


A replay of the webcast and presentation materials will be made available on the Investor Relations section of Mtron's website at[ir.mtron.com](http://ir.mtron.com/) following the conclusion of the event.


Investors and other parties interested in attending in person will need to register in advance by contacting the Company's Investor Relations team atir@mtron.com due to limited capacity and NYSE building security requirements. Please RSVP by Friday, May 8, 2026. Mtron looks forward to having as many investors join as possible.


**About M-tron Industries, Inc.**


M-tron Industries, Inc. (NYSE American: MPTI) designs, manufactures, and markets highly engineered, high reliability frequency and spectrum control products and solutions. As an engineering-centric company, Mtron provides close support to its customers throughout our products' entire life cycle, including product design, prototyping, production, and subsequent product upgrades. Mtron has design and manufacturing facilities in Orlando, Florida, and Yankton, South Dakota, a sales office in Hong Kong, and a manufacturing facility in Noida, India.
