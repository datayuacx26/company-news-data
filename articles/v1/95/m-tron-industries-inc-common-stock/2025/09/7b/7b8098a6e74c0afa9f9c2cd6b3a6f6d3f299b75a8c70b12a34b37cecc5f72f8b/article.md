# Mtron Awarded Production Contract Multi Domain Data Link

**September 18, 2025** **– Orlando, FL** –[Mtron](https://mtron.com/) (NYSEAM: MPTI), a leading provider of high-performance[RF components and solutions](https://mtron.com/) , today announced it has been awarded a significant production contract from a top U.S. Department of Defense prime contractor in support of a brand new, novel multi-domain data link. The system effectively joins several communication data links together to dramatically shorten the data-to-decision timeline, enabling clearer, faster intelligence sharing and enhancing the operational picture for the joint warfighter.


The contract includes several high-performance radio frequency components, including rugged lumped element filters, engineered for durability and precision in mission-critical environments. This latest award builds on Mtron’s long-standing support of defense programs, and this new program for next-level connectivity is forecasted to be in production for at least a decade.


“At Mtron, we’re proud to support this groundbreaking program with high-performance RF components, including rugged lumped element filters engineered for precision and durability in mission-critical environments,” said Cameron Pforr, CEO of Mtron.


“This award builds on our long-standing role supporting mission-critical defense programs, while also marking a step forward in the adoption of edge computing. This innovative technology turns Big Data into actionable intelligence at the source, enhancing situational awareness and accelerating decision-making in the field where it matters most.”


Work under this contract will be performed at Mtron’s manufacturing facility in Orlando, Florida, through 2026. The program is expected to continue production at least through 2036.
