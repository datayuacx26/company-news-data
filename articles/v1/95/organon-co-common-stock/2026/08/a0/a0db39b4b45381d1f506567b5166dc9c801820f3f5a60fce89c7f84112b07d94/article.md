# HCP Action Required: Complete NEXPLANON REMS Certification by August 23, 2026 to Maintain Access to NEXPLANON® (etonogestrel implant) 68 mg | Organon

## HCP Action Required: Complete NEXPLANON REMS Certification by August 23, 2026 to Maintain Access to NEXPLANON® (etonogestrel implant) 68 mg


SavePrint


August 4, 2026 12:00 am EDT


All healthcare providers who dispense **NEXPLANON® (etonogestrel implant) 68 mg** are required to complete NEXPLANON REMS certification by **August 23, 2026** to maintain access to NEXPLANON.


All providers who administer NEXPLANON must become REMS certified, including those who previously completed the Clinical Training Program (CTP). The goal of the NEXPLANON REMS is to mitigate complications due to improper insertion and removal.


**Certification requirements vary by provider status:**


- **Active inserters:** Complete certification online at **[www.NEXPLANONREMS.com](http://www.nexplanonrems.com/)** by reviewing the Prescribing Information and Healthcare Provider Guide and submitting the Knowledge Assessment and Enrollment Form.
- **Inactive or new providers:** Complete all required training, including online didactic and in-person practical training. Providers are encouraged to begin the certification process as soon as possible to allow sufficient time before the deadline.


For questions about REMS certification, contact the **NEXPLANON REMS Program** at **1-833-NXP-REMS (1-833-697-7367)** or **Organon** at **1-844-674-3200** .


Before prescribing NEXPLANON, please read the[Prescribing Information](https://www.organon.com/product/usa/pi_circulars/n/nexplanon/nexplanon_pi.pdf) , including Boxed Warning. The[Patient Information](https://www.organon.com/product/usa/pi_circulars/n/nexplanon/nexplanon_ppi.pdf) also is available.
