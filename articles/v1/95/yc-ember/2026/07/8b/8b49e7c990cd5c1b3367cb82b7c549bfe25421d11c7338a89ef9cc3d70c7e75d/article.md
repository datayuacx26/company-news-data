# Ember Achieves HITRUST e1 Certification, Strengthening Data Security for AI-Powered Revenue Integrity

*HITRUST e1 Certification validates that Ember’s platform meets foundational cybersecurity requirements through independent assessment, reinforcing how the company protects sensitive healthcare data.*


**San Francisco, CA, July 1, 2026** – Ember, the AI revenue integrity platform for leading independent groups, ambulatory surgery centers, and management services organizations, today announced it has earned HITRUST e1 Certification, confirming that its platform meets the essential cybersecurity requirements of the HITRUST CSF.


The HITRUST e1 Certification is awarded following an independent assessment against a foundational set of security controls focused on essential cybersecurity practices. As physician groups and health systems adopt AI-enabled workflows across the revenue cycle, independent validation of how vendors secure protected health information has become a baseline requirement for adoption.


> Our customers trust Ember with some of the most sensitive data in healthcare, and earning that trust is the foundation of the relationship. HITRUST e1 Certification gives them independent validation that we hold our security program to a recognized industry standard,” said Charlene Wang, Chief Executive Officer of Ember.


The certification complements Ember’s existing SOC 2 Type II attestation and HIPAA compliance.


To learn more about HITRUST e1 Certification, visit[hitrustalliance.net/assessments-and-certifications/e1](https://hitrustalliance.net/assessments-and-certifications/e1) or view Ember’s[certification report in the HITRUST Report Center](https://reportcenter.mycsf.net/assessment/3594d419-99f8-3d36-82a9-6aaab1eb3e4e) .
