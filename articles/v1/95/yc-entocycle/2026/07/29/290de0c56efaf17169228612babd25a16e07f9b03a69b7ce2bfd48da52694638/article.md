# Entocycle Secures £100,000 Water Discovery Challenge Grant to Pioneer Insect-Powered Wastewater Treatment

London, UK - The Water Discovery Challenge has awarded £100,000 to Entocycle to develop Entoremediation, a nature-based circular economy solution utilising Black Soldier Fly Larvae (BSFL) to treat, clean and valorize wastewater output.


Named a finalist in the £7.5 million competition from Ofwat’s Water Innovation Fund, Entocycle aims to standardise a biological treatment process that dramatically reduces waste mass, eliminates hazardous contaminants and establishes a commercial model that transforms liabilities into revenue-generating resources.


Entocycle will leverage the voracious feeding capabilities of BSFL to achieve two major environmental and financial goals:


1.


Target a potential **50% mass reduction** on the 3.5 million tons of biosolids produced annually in the UK, enabling traditional agricultural land applications to continue safely without requiring energy-intensive heat treatments.


2.


Target a **90% volume reduction** of municipal wastewater screenings. By cleaning screenings to absolute sanitation standards, Entocycle aims to transition this material from a costly landfill/incineration liability to a compliant recycling stream, yielding up to £10 million in industry-wide annual landfill tax savings.


This innovative, nature-based solution directly addresses the threatening collapse of traditional agricultural sludge disposal routes, threatened by rising microplastics, PFAS and heavy metal contamination, while simultaneously tackling the high operational cost of landfilling municipal wastewater screenings.


By utilising rapid biological filtration, Entoremediation not only tackles high operational disposal costs, but also transforms hazardous waste into highly valuable, circular products. These include frass (a nutrient-rich fertiliser) and sustainable feedstocks for industrial bio-energy.


The Water Discovery Challenge is part of Ofwat’s Water Innovation Fund which is supporting a multitude of solutions that tackle the water sector’s greatest challenges across multiple competitions. The Water Discovery Challenge is dedicated to supporting the development of ideas from innovators across industries, particularly those new to the water sector.


As well as seed funding, as a finalist, Entocycle will be supported with seven months of mentorship to support commercialisation and expert guidance from water companies, to push solutions further and test the feasibility of the solutions in real-world settings at scale.
