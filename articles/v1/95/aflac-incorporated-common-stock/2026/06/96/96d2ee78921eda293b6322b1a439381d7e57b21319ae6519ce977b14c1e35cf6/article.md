# Aflac Incorporated to Present at the Morgan Stanley U.S. Financials Conference

COLUMBUS, Ga.


, June 1, 2026


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / -- Aflac Incorporated (NYSE: AFL) announced today that President of Aflac Incorporated and Aflac U.S. Virgil R. Miller, along with Senior Executive Vice President and Aflac Incorporated Chief Financial Officer Max Brodén, will participate in a fireside chat at the Morgan Stanley U.S. Financials Conference on June 9, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. ET.


The presentation will be webcast live. Please click on the following link at least 15 minutes prior to the presentation to allow time to register or sign in.


[https://event.webcasts.com/starthere.jsp?ei=1765811&tp_key=900d193922&tp_special=8](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4701337-1&h=2823900420&u=https%3A%2F%2Fevent.webcasts.com%2Fstarthere.jsp%3Fei%3D1765811%26tp_key%3D900d193922%26tp_special%3D8&a=https%3A%2F%2Fevent.webcasts.com%2Fstarthere.jsp%3Fei%3D1765811%26tp_key%3D900d193922%26tp_special%3D8)


A replay of the presentation will be available within 24 hours after the conclusion of the live event using the same web address.
