# Mafia: The Old Country Now Available Worldwide | Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.

*Dive into the roots of organized crime in 1900s Sicily with a compelling new cast of characters today*


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 8, 2025-- Today, 2K and Hangar 13 announced that ***Mafia: The Old Country*** , the highly anticipated latest installment in the acclaimed *Mafia* franchise, is now available worldwide for PlayStation®5 (PS5®), Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam. The story will take players back in time to 1900s Sicily, which has been painstakingly created with the utmost authenticity and detail.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250808972468/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250808972468/en/)


Today, 2K and Hangar 13 announced that Mafia: The Old Country, the highly anticipated latest installment in the acclaimed Mafia franchise, is now available worldwide for PlayStation®5 (PS5®), Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam. The story will take players back in time to 1900s Sicily, which has been painstakingly created with the utmost authenticity and detail.


[Official Launch Trailer](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DEAEYZDgHNv8&esheet=54303564&newsitemid=20250808972468&lan=en-US&anchor=Official+Launch+Trailer&index=1&md5=8c3e52bfe4d7b41184f3cba452a1dbf6)


“The team has passionately crafted this new chapter in the *Mafia* franchise, designed to captivate both long-time fans and new players," said Nick Baynes, President of Hangar 13. “ ***Mafia: The Old Country*** ** is a premium experience in the vein of earlier *Mafia* entries, rooted in rich narrative, memorable characters and a beautiful environment, delivering what we feel is an unforgettable story at incredible value.”


Hangar 13 has spent 22 years immersing players in brutal *Mafia* worlds. For this newest chapter, the franchise veterans are exploring the roots of organized crime, challenging players to fight for survival as Enzo Favara and prove their worth to the family in this immersive, third-person action-adventure set during a dangerous, unforgiving era.


Through grit and determination, Enzo has survived a childhood of indentured labor in Sicily’s hellish sulfur mines. Now, through a twist of fate, he seizes a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to join Don Torrisi's formidable crime family, and will do whatever it takes to carve out a better life for himself – no matter the cost. With his sworn oath, Enzo binds himself to the Torrisi family's code of honor, with all the power and hardship it entails. He must never forget this simple truth: Family Takes Sacrifice.


***Mafia: The Old Country*** perfects the classic *Mafia* gameplay with grounded and dangerous combat systems, featuring high-stakes stealth, brutal knife fights, and intense gunplay mechanics. The story is brought to life by stunning visuals, unforgettable characters, and the authentic realism for which the acclaimed *Mafia* series is known.


***Mafia: The Old Country*** offers two editions of the game: ***Standard Edition*** and a digital ***Deluxe Edition*** .


- The ***Standard Edition*** includes the full base game and is available for $49.99* on PlayStation®5 (PS5®), Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam.
- The digital ***Deluxe Edition*** lets players descend into Sicily's criminal underworld in style with a variety of bonus items and is available for $59.99* on PlayStation®5 (PS5®), Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam.


- This edition includes the Padrino Pack**, featuring "Lupara Speciale" Shotgun, "Vendetti Speciale" Pistol, "Immortale" Charm, "Padrino" Outfit, "Stiletto Speciale" Knife, "Eckhart Speciale" Limousine and "Cosimo" Horse and Accessories. Additionally, it offers the Gatto Nero Pack** with the "Bodeo Nero" Pistol, "Velocità" Charm, "Gatto Nero" Racing Outfit and "Carozella Nero" Race Car, plus digital bonus materials including the Original Score and Digital Artbook full of concept art and developer notes.**


First established in 2002 with the launch of the landmark game *Mafia* , the franchise has sold-in more than 35 million copies across consoles and PC.


***Mafia: The Old Country*** is rated Mature 17+ by the ESRB.


Hangar 13 is a 2K studio. 2K is a publishing label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO).
