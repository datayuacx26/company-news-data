# CoLoop AI: $3.5M Funding for AI-Powered Customer Insights

We’re excited to share that CoLoop AI has secured a new $3.5M round of venture funding, bringing our total raise to $5.0M. With this investment, we’re doubling down on our vision of making every company as customer-obsessed as Amazon.


By harnessing AI, we’re freeing researchers from manual, repetitive tasks and enabling them to focus on strategy, storytelling, and impactful deliverables. I’m proud to say that CoLoop has already helped more than 250+ of the most innovative research agencies and Fortune 500 insight teams unlock meaningful, human-driven insights.


This funding will allow us to strengthen our existing offerings and advance into enterprise knowledge management. Our goal is to help large organisations escape messy SharePoint drives and fragmented tool stacks, and collaborate directly with research providers to organise their critical research material.


The challenges facing businesses today are clear: 1 in 4 companies has no market research strategy, 88% of executives believe customers are evolving faster than they can keep up, and 95% of products fail in their first year. At CoLoop, we are addressing these challenges by empowering teams to streamline, organise, and unleash insights data.
