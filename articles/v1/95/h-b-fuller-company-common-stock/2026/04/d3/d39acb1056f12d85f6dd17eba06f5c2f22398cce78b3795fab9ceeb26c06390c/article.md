# How Minnesota is Advancing H.B. Fuller’s Next Chapter of Global Growth

Minnesota has always been a critical part of H.B. Fuller’s story. Founded in St. Paul in 1887, our company was created to deliver innovative adhesives rooted in real world needs.


While the company’s size, scale, and global ambitions have grown over time, innovation remains at its core, and Minnesota continues to serve as its global headquarters. From manufacturing and technical development to corporate leadership, our Minnesota sites support customers across more than 30 market segments, while connecting global teams through disciplined execution and operational excellence.


Recently, we hosted our global CEO Huddle in Minnesota, where employees came together to celebrate the company’s latest wins, milestones, and employee achievements. Convening teams where manufacturing, technical development, and enterprise functions come together reinforced the values that define H.B. Fuller’s culture.


“Our values aren’t words on a wall—they’re how we show up every day,” said Celeste Mastin, president and CEO of H.B. Fuller. “Collaboration, courage, and the spirit of winning shape our culture and guide our decisions, especially when the environment is complex. That’s what allows our teams to move faster, stay connected, and deliver for customers around the world.”


### Supporting customers through advanced manufacturing


H.B. Fuller’s Minnesota manufacturing sites are where difference-making innovation moves at scale, transforming ideas into reliable, high-performance adhesive solutions.


In Fridley, our company’s oldest Minnesota location plays a vital role in serving the insulating glass, automotive, and durable assembly markets. Recent investments, including the installation of the first 4SG mixer of its kind in North America, have strengthened the plant’s ability to support the insulating glass market.


Maple Grove adds another layer of capability by bringing together adhesives manufacturing and an analytical laboratory under one roof. Home to the Twin Screw Extruder Center of Excellence, the site supports both commercial production and product development for insulating glass, solar, and other specialty applications.


“Our Minnesota sites demonstrate how operational excellence and innovation come together every day,” said Anna Kolliopoulos, regional director, Production. “The collaboration across locations and functions allows us to deliver consistent quality while staying responsive to customer needs.”


### Advancing innovation from pilot to production


The Minnesota Pilot Facility in Vadnais Heights plays a unique role in translating ideas into scalable solutions for customers around the world. Producing high-value adhesives for all three global business units, the site supports small-batch production, specialty opportunities, and customer-specific programs, as well as commercial-size polyurethane dispersions for the flexible packaging market.


Pilot batches are designed to run on full-scale industrial equipment, giving teams visibility into real-world performance before products move to commercial manufacturing. This approach improves scaleup reliability and confirms product robustness, while maintaining the same quality standards as other H.B. Fuller plants. The facility produces more than 600,000 pounds of adhesives annually across a wide range of applications.


“Employees value the chance to work closely with R&D and see how their work brings new products to market,” said Greg Herold, production plant manager. “The variety of materials and customers we support makes the work both challenging and rewarding.”


### Connecting global teams from corporate headquarters


Minnesota is also home to H.B. Fuller’s corporate headquarters, where enterprise teams support global operations and connect regional sites around the world. Functions including Finance, Human Resources, Legal, IT, Supply Chain, Customer Service, Communications, and R&D leadership work together to align priorities, support collaboration, and ensure teams everywhere have the resources they need to serve customers effectively.


Also based at our corporate headquarters is the Global Sourcing team, which brings this enterprise-wide strategy to life, working closely with manufacturing, supply chain, and commercial partners around the world to ensure we’re meeting customer demand.


“What makes H.B. Fuller exceptional is our ability to connect people, ideas, and execution across the globe,” Mastin said. “From Minnesota to our sites around the world, our teams are turning collaboration into impact and that’s how we’ll continue to shape the future of our company and reset markets we serve.”
