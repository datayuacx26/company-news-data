# Why RRO Expertise Matters in Delivering Better Roofing Solutions

In commercial roofing, details matter. Every specification, transition, fastener, primer, and adhesive contributes to long term performance. That’s why H.B. Fuller is proud to have employees who invest in advancing their own technical expertise—including earning Registered Roof Observer (RRO) certification—to better serve our customers and partners. This commitment to continuous learning isn’t just another credential. It’s a meaningful reflection of the pride our teams take in their craft, and an advantage for contractors, consultants, and building owners who rely on roofing systems to perform for decades.


### What an RRO Brings to Roofing Projects


A[Registered Roof Observer](https://iibec.org/rro/) , certified through the International Institute of Building Enclosure Consultants (IIBEC), plays a critical role in verifying that commercial roofing systems are installed correctly and meet the design intent. One of our RRO certified experts, Paul Krug, Strategic Account Manager at H.B. Fuller, describes the role this way:


“RROs observe and report roof installations. They see the roof as a system—not just individual components.”


H.B. Fuller employees are committed to developing deeper system level understanding so they can better support the contractors and consultants they work with every day.[This whole system perspective](https://www.hbfuller.com/en/markets/commercial-roofing) helps ensure installations follow design criteria, support long term stability, and maintain warranty consistency—all essential components of high performing commercial roofing.


### How RRO Expertise Strengthens H.B. Fuller Solutions


At H.B. Fuller, our RRO certified professionals do more than study roofing installations—they help shape how our adhesives, sealants, and system recommendations evolve. Their insight bridges the gap between real world roof performance and the science behind our products.


Our growing team of employees who pursue advanced training and certifications—help us:


- Anticipate the challenges contractors and installers face
- Improve system compatibility and product recommendations
- Provide informed guidance during installation and troubleshooting
- Ensure our solutions align with industry standards and real world expectations


This combined expertise gives our customers confidence that every product is backed by both laboratory innovation and practical, in field knowledge.


### Expertise in Action: RRO Insight That Makes a Difference


Real time technical expertise often plays a key role in keeping roofing projects on schedule and performing long term.


Across the industry, RRO certified professionals bring a deeper understanding of how factors like substrate conditions, environment, and specification details can affect roof performance—knowledge that helps our team provide better field guidance and support. Their insight helps contractors make timely adjustments and ensures installations meet both performance and warranty expectations.


Training is another core part of the RRO role. As Paul notes:


“RROs help train crews to be comfortable performing the work while following the specification. They are a third party that provides reports back to the design consultant.”


While H.B. Fuller employees do not perform independent RRO services in their roles here, the knowledge gained through certification enhances the technical guidance and support they provide every day.


This support doesn’t just improve installation quality—it helps prevent future issues and reduces the likelihood of costly re work.
