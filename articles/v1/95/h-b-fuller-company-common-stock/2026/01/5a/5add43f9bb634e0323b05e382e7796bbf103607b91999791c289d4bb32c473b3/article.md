# Strategic Partnerships are Reshaping Roofing Distribution—Here’s How H.B. Fuller Helps you Stay Ahead

In the competitive and fast-evolving North American roofing industry, distributors play a key role in connecting manufacturers with contractors, builders, and roofing professionals. As a manufacturer of high-performance[roofing adhesives, sealants, primers, and tapes](https://www.hbfuller.com/en/markets/commercial-roofing) , H.B. Fuller understands the challenges distributors face, and we’re committed to being more than just a supplier. We’re a strategic partner.


This article explores the role of distributors, their evolving needs, and how our tailored support helps them grow their business and better serve their customers.


### The Role of Distributors


Distributors help ensure that roofing products are available where and when they’re needed, provide technical guidance, and help contractors choose the right solutions for their projects. However, distributors are facing increasing pressure; from managing complex inventories to staying ahead of product innovations and competing in a crowded marketplace. That’s where strong supplier relationships become essential.


To thrive, distributors need more than just products on shelves. They need:


- A wide and reliable product range to meet diverse customer demands
- Technical expertise to support their teams and customers
- Marketing and sales tools to drive awareness and demand
- Training programs to stay informed and confident
- Equipment support to ensure proper product application
- Collaborative sales efforts to win new business and strengthen relationships


Meeting these needs requires a supplier who understands the distributor’s business and is invested in their success.


### How Strategic Partnerships Can Grow Business Further for Distributors


A strategic partnership between distributor and supplier is built on shared goals, open communication, and mutual trust. When suppliers actively support distributors, it can lead to increased sales through better product positioning and customer engagement, and stronger customer loyalty thanks to enhanced service and support.


### How H.B. Fuller Supports Roofing Distributors


We offer a comprehensive support program designed to empower distributors in North America and help them succeed:


**Comprehensive Product Portfolio**


Access to top-tier products and systems is essential for commercial roofing distributors. **Product Demonstrations and Training**


We provide live and virtual product demos to help distributors and their customers understand application techniques, product benefits, and best practices. These demos build confidence and drive sales.


We can also offer training programs to ensure distributor teams are equipped with the knowledge they need and are ready to advise customers.


**Equipment Support**


We provide guidance and walkthroughs on the[tools and equipment](https://www.hbfuller.com/en/applications/commercial-roofing-adhesive-application-equipment) needed for proper product application, along with troubleshooting and maintenance support.


**Sales and Marketing Support**


We offer branded marketing materials, digital content, and promotional campaigns to help distributors generate leads and boost visibility. Our marketing team works closely with distributors to tailor support to their local markets.


**Joint Sales Calls**


Our sales and technical teams join distributors on customer calls and visits to provide expert insights, answer questions, and help close deals. This collaborative approach strengthens relationships and builds trust.


Our distributor partners consistently report increased sales, improved customer satisfaction, and stronger market presence thanks to our support. By working together, we’ve helped distributors expand their reach, win new business, and build lasting customer relationships.


**Conclusion**


Distributors have a key role to play in the North American roofing industry, and we’re proud to support them with the tools, training, and partnership they need to succeed.
