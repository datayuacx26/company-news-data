### Fire Resistance: A Critical Factor in Elevator Safety


In vertical transportation systems, fire resistance isn't optional—it's essential. Elevators run through every floor of a building, often acting as a vertical conduit for heat and smoke in the event of a fire. That's why using fire-rated materials, especially for door panels, car cladding, and insulation, is vital to meet building codes and protect passenger safety.


H.B. Fuller offers adhesives that comply with[UNI EN 45545-2 fire safety standards (R1 HL 1-3)](https://dge-europe.com/en-45545-european-railway-standard-fire-safety/) . These products provide high mechanical strength and elasticity while withstanding extreme temperatures—ensuring that structural bonds hold when they’re needed most.

### Electrification of Elevators


The electrification of elevators represents a transformative shift for industrial elevator manufacturers, offering new opportunities to enhance performance, sustainability, and integration with smart building systems. By replacing traditional hydraulic or mechanical systems with advanced electric drives and controls, manufacturers can deliver elevators that are more energy-efficient, quieter, and easier to maintain. Electrified systems also enable precise motion control, regenerative braking, and real-time diagnostics, which contribute to reduced operational costs and improved user experience. As demand grows for eco-friendly and connected infrastructure, embracing electrification positions manufacturers to meet evolving regulatory standards and customer expectations while driving innovation in vertical transportation solutions.
