# Why Adhesive Selection Matters for Rigid Packaging Efficiency

In today’s high-speed manufacturing environments, adhesive selection plays a critical role in rigid packaging efficiency. Beyond simply bonding packs, the right adhesive directly impacts seal strength, machine uptime, operational reliability, and total cost of ownership.


At H.B. Fuller, industry-leading brands such as Advantra®, Swift®tak and Swift®melt are engineered to support rigid packaging manufacturers in achieving consistent better operational efficiency across demanding end-of-line operations.


### How the Right Adhesive Improves Sealing Strength in Rigid Packaging


Reliable sealing is essential to protect products throughout transportation, warehousing, and retail handling. Poor adhesive performance can result in seal failures, leaks, or damaged packs—leading too costly rework and brand risk.


The right adhesive helps:


- Maintain pack integrity during distribution and storage
- Prevent leakage, contamination, or tampering
- Support heavy or high-value rigid packs across substrates like plastic, metal, glass, and coated paperboard


Selecting an adhesive engineered for the specific packaging format and operating conditions is critical to achieving strong, reliable seals throughout the product lifecycle.


### Adhesive Selection and Machine Uptime in End-of-Line Operations


Adhesive-related issues can be a cause of unplanned downtime in rigid packaging and case sealing lines. Problems such as stringing, charring, poor open-time control, or inconsistent viscosity can interrupt packaging line operations and reduce throughput.


Choosing the right adhesive can:


- Enable clean, consistent application at high seed lines
- Reduce nozzle clogging and equipment fouling
- Improve Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) through stable performance


Adhesives engineered for thermal stability, controlled viscosity, and long pot life help minimize maintenance interventions and support sustained, efficient end-of-line operations.


### Adhesive Value Beyond Price


While adhesive cost is often evaluated on a per-kilogram basis, the true cost lies in total system efficiency. Performance-driven adhesive selection can significantly reduce operational expenses.


According to packaging industry studies, improving adhesive efficiency can lead to measurable savings in materials, maintenance, and downtime costs.


### Avoiding Common Adhesive-Related Issues in End-of-Line Packaging


Drawing on real-world packaging line experience, H.B. Fuller recommends the following best practices for rigid packaging operations:


**1. Match Adhesive Chemistry to the Application**


Different substrates and pack weights require specific adhesive formulations, such as metallocene-based hot melts or specialty solutions.


**2. Optimize Application Temperatures**


Operating adhesives outside recommended temperature ranges can cause stringing, charring, or weak bonds.


**3. Select the Right Open and Set Time**


High-speed rigid packaging lines require adhesives that balance adequate open time with fast setting for downstream handling.


**4. Maintain Clean Application Equipment**


Routine cleaning of tanks, hoses, and nozzles prevents buildup that can compromise adhesive flow and bond consistency.


**5. Validate Performance Through Line Trials**


Conducting real production trials ensures adhesives perform reliably under actual operating conditions.
