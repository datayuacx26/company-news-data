# Global Innovation In Action

At H.B. Fuller, we believe that innovation is the engine that powers our growth, strengthens customer partnerships, and shapes our long-term strategy as the largest pureplay adhesives company in the world.


That innovation-driven approach was on full display when leaders from across the company recently gathered in Chongqing, China, for our Global CEO Huddle. The quarterly event brings together leaders from around the world to share insights and showcase how local innovation fuels global performance.


Our Huddle in Chongqing also illustrated how innovation thrives when teams stay connected to customers, to each other, and to the global markets they serve.


“Innovation is central to how H.B. Fuller grows faster and strengthens our market leadership,” said Xinyu Du, senior vice president of global research and development. “Every breakthrough, every customer partnership, and every improvement in our labs moves us closer to the future we’re building as a company.”


### A Global Engine for Innovation


Located in Southwest China, Chongqing gives H.B. Fuller a strong presence in the country’s rapidly growing inland Asian market. With automotive and woodworking industries expanding across central and western regions, proximity to customers plays a critical role in speed, responsiveness, and partnership.


Like other H.B. Fuller sites, Chongqing is supported by the company’s global network of 35 technology centers, enabling close collaboration and rapid development cycles with customers across critical growth markets such as mobility, electronics, medical, and insulated glass.


Our global teams ensure that innovation is a company-wide effort. Operational excellence, supply chain expertise, digital tools, and commercial insight all contribute to translating ideas into scalable solutions that deliver measurable value for customers.


“Innovation isn’t just an R&D activity; it takes all of us,” Du said.“Innovation becomes impact when we stay connected to our customers and to each other.”


### A Hub for Innovation and Growth


By delivering faster and better solutions, Chongqing drives customers’ growth, which ultimately helps strengthen global economies.


Likewise, Chongqing supports innovation by serving as a major research and development hub for water-based and solvent adhesives, with a team of chemists supporting technical development across the Asia Pacific region.


Their work helps translate customer needs into scalable, high-performing solutions that are used in everyday applications such as vehicles, wood flooring, mattresses, and formaldehyde scavenger systems.


“Our global teams are deeply connected to customer applications,” Du said. “In many cases, our chemists understand how adhesives are used in manufacturing even better than the customer’s own engineers. That’s what makes our innovation powerful. It solves real problems.”


### Bringing Innovation and Leadership Together at the Global CEO Huddle


Hosting the Global CEO Huddle in Chongqing underscores how H.B. Fuller connects innovation, geography, and leadership.


As H.B. Fuller continues to invest in differentiated technologies, regions, and a globally connected workforce, innovation remains the common thread. This drives growth, strengthens partnerships, and positions the company for long-term success in a rapidly evolving world.
