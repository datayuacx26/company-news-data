# EternaBond® Dry Technology System: Taking an Automotive Approach to RV Sealing

### It’s Time We Started Constructing RVs Like the Vehicles They Are


Imagine you’re in the market for a new vehicle. As you inspect a car on a dealership lot, you notice the panels are screwed into the body and covered in sealant. The salesman remarks that you’ll only have to replace the sealant a few times a year to keep the vehicle from leaking.


Would you buy a vehicle with holes in the roof? The answer, for most, is a resounding “no.”


The same question can be asked for recreational vehicles (RVs). New technology allows RV manufacturers to stop relying on the old method of inserting screws through holes and then applying sealant to attach roof components, seams, and joints.


Instead, this technology enables an automotive approach. Components are permanently sealed to the RV during manufacturing, eliminating the need for seam maintenance and repair.


The automotive approach enables a new generation of RVs resistant to the elements, durable, and virtually maintenance-free. It’s the fresh perspective that gives RV manufacturers a competitive advantage when appealing to the newest generation of buyers.


### How the EternaBond® Dry Technology System Works


The EternaBond Dry Technology System (DTS) by H.B. Fuller is a patented primary sealing design approach specifically for RV manufacturing. DTS includes a layer of EternaBond prepped with a self-priming material and protected by a removable release liner (Figure 1). EternaBond uses a[proprietary micro-sealant technology](https://eternabond.hbfuller.com/en) to form an essentially maintenance-free seal that grows stronger over time, backed by a 15-year warranty.


** *Figure 1. EternaBond Dry Technology System (DTS) is a single, multi-layered product that manufacturers can apply directly to RV components.*


DTS keeps RV interiors dry without the need for liquid sealant.


“The whole concept is centered around taking an automotive approach to sealing an RV,” explains Jon Cordell, EternaBond subject matter expert. “Instead of sealing from the exterior in, DTS seals from the inside out. The idea is creating a primary seal you never have to look at or touch again—it’s one and done.”


To use the product, peel off the release liner, secure the tape to the vehicle body while applying steady pressure, and then attach the RV component. DTS can be used on every application that typically requires sealant, including but not limited to:


- Body components
- Roof
- Slide outs and perimeters
- Front caps
- Belt rails
- Rails and awnings
- Flooring
- Baggage and entry doors
- Windows


### Enabling the Next Generation of Dry, Sleek RVs


DTS replaces previous RV sealing technology—a combination of putty tape, low-grade butyl, and liquid sealant—with a single gasketing seal. Where the combination of sealing products is applied externally (on the outside of the vehicle), DTS is used internally during production. The product is virtually hidden between the RV body and components.


When mechanical fasteners are utilized, the self-healing properties of Eternabond ensure a positive seal is achieved. This approach eliminates leak paths, where water seeps in through the weakest point in the structure.


In addition to reducing the risk of water seeping into the vehicle, DTS improves RV aesthetics. Liquid sealant will degrade, attract dirt and dust, and become discolored over time. Because it does not utilize liquid sealants, DTS is virtually invisible. This provides RVs with a sharp look and clean finish that lasts the life of the unit.


### Improving Customer Loyalty and Streamlining Production


[Leaks are a major concern](https://rvbrands.org/what-is-the-problem-with-the-rv-industry/) for prospective RV customers and[can lead to bigger issues](https://nrvia.org/2025/01/how-to-avoid-buying-an-rv-with-water-damage/) , such as structural damage. Original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) can prevent leaks by sealing RVs with DTS, which boosts RV manufacturers’ reputations, attracts additional customers, and builds loyalty among consumers.


RV manufacturers are now able to[extend the warranty](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YFMPY0omuk0&t=107s) they offer customers because the system creates dry, more durable RVs. Cutting down on claims is a major win for manufacturers and consumers.


“There are millions of dollars spent on warranty claims, and most of that monetary amount is due to water intrusion,” explains Cordell. “It has a lot to do with an improper sealing system, which Eternabond DTS can eliminate.”


Not all RV components come in standard shapes and sizes. DTS offers die-cut options, enabling manufacturers to order customized products to meet their requirements, simplifying the manufacturing process. Customization also ensures sufficient coverage with reduced material waste.


### Innovative Technology Reduces Risk and Work for Consumers


DTS improves RV durability and reduces the risk of leaks that can damage the RV, giving consumers peace of mind that their purchase will provide a comfortable, lasting home base for adventures.


Depending on the manufacturer, liquid sealant must be replaced on a routine basis (sometimes twice a year) to ensure it continues to work properly. RV maintenance requirements can deter buyers, especially younger generations. According to Consumer Affairs,[more than half of RV owners are younger than 55](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/automotive/rv-industry-statistics.html) , and the 18-34 age group now accounts for 22% of owners. RVs made with DTS do not require regular maintenance on the exterior sealant—a major benefit for younger consumers.


“There’s a whole new younger generation who are making OEMs adapt,” says Cordell, “Plus, new ideas and fresh designs have increased the competition for OEMs. The construction of RVs has evolved over time, and it’s now time for the OEMs to follow suit.”
