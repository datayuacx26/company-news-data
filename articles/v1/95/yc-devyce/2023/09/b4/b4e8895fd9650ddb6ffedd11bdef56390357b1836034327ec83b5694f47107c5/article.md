# How to Write Useful Notes for a Phone Call

## Key Takeaways


1. Effective note-taking during phone calls is crucial for maintaining accurate records and enhancing communication.
2. Devyce's AI functionality can generate summaries and learn from your edits, making it easier to capture important details.
3. Utilizing call questions allows you to focus on specific data points that matter most.


## The Importance of Note-Taking


Taking notes during phone calls is essential for several reasons. It helps you remember key points discussed, follow up on action items, and maintain a record of conversations for future reference. Good notes can also improve your communication with clients and colleagues, ensuring that everyone is on the same page.


## Devyce's AI Functionality

The AI can generate summaries of your calls, allowing you to focus on the conversation rather than scrambling to jot down every detail. Additionally, the AI learns from the edits you make to previous call summaries, improving its accuracy over time.


## Using Call Questions


Devyce's call questions feature enables you to select specific data points to focus on during your calls. This targeted approach ensures that you capture the most relevant information, making your notes more useful and actionable. By asking the right questions, you can guide the conversation and ensure that all important topics are covered.


## Best Practices for Writing Notes


While Devyce's AI functionality streamlines the note-taking process, there are still best practices to consider:


- **Be concise:** Use bullet points or short sentences to capture key information without overwhelming detail.
- **Highlight action items:** Clearly mark any tasks or follow-ups that need to be addressed after the call.
- **Use clear headings:** Organize your notes with headings to make it easier to find information later.
- **Review and edit:** After the call, take a moment to review your notes and make any necessary edits. This is where Devyce's AI can assist by suggesting updates to your CRM, such as Bullhorn and Vincere, ensuring that your records are always up to date.


## Conclusion


Writing useful notes for phone calls is a skill that can greatly enhance your communication and productivity. With Devyce's AI functionality, you can streamline the process, ensuring that you capture all essential information while also keeping your CRM updated. By following best practices for note-taking, you can make the most of your phone conversations and improve your overall effectiveness in business communications.
