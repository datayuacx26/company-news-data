# Bringing Devyce to the Real Estate World

We’re incredibly proud to officially launch our strategic partnership with[Reapit](https://www.reapit.com/partner-resource-modules/partner-spotlight-devyce) today. This marks a major step into the real estate world, a market with hundreds of thousands of potential customers, including nearly 50,000 Reapit users alone, and positions Devyce as the first mover bringing modern AI-powered communications to estate agencies everywhere.


From recruiters to real estate, our mission remains the same: to revolutionise how teams communicate.


‍


With Devyce + Reapit, users can now enjoy:


- Click-to-dial calling and messaging directly from Reapit, streamlining the sales process
- Sensei, our AI call-coaching and performance suite, updated specifically for real estate
- AI call summaries, transcripts and recordings posted straight back to Reapit, tuned to capture the details estate agents need
- Real-time insights to improve every customer conversation.


‍


This is a massive opportunity, not just in the UK but internationally, with Reapit’s footprint spanning multiple continents and thousands of top-performing agencies. We’re beyond excited to be bringing our best-in-class phone system and AI functionality to a whole new market.
