# What's New at Devyce? Dec 2025

You’re not just using a phone system, you’re part of a growing community of businesses choosing to work smarter and more flexibly with Devyce. And we are so glad you’re on this journey with us!


This month we have made a series of updates focused on making your data sync faster and more reliably to your CRM as well as giving the team smarter call routing and dial out options.
