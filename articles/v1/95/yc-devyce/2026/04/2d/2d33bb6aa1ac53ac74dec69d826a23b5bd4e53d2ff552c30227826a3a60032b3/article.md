# What's New at Devyce? Apr 2026

This month’s updates are all about making your calls clearer, your selling sharper, and your support faster than ever. You can now report issues directly from your calls, explore fresh how-to articles in our ever-growing knowledge base, and enjoy clearer conversations with built-in noise suppression.We’ve also rolled out a refreshed Devyce interface — now sleeker, more intuitive, and designed to help you get things done faster.


### **Here’s what’s new**


#
**Noise suppression**


‍


‍


Say goodbye to barking dogs, clacking keyboards, and coffee machines. With noise cancelling, unwanted sounds should be reduced, but BVC (Background Voice Cancellation) goes further, isolating your voice for crystal-clear calls. Perfect for contact centres, or busy home offices!


[Find out more](https://devyce-knowledge-base.help.usepylon.com/articles/4008405847-noise-suppression-how-to-use-this-feature) **‍**


‍


# **Call reporting**


‍


‍


With our new Call Report feature in the Softphone, you can quickly capture technical issues right after a call ends. Simply flag the problem, and all the relevant call details are automatically recorded for you.


This feature doesn’t directly notify our support team. Instead, it acts as a smart triage tool, giving you everything you need in one place so you can easily report the issue when you’re ready - without the usual back and forth to gather call details.


The result = Faster, more efficient support interactions. Complete information from the start, helping issues get understood and resolved sooner, so you can get back to smooth, uninterrupted Devyce calls.


[Find out more](https://devyce-knowledge-base.help.usepylon.com/articles/8271259132-flag-a-call-issue-or-report-a-technical-issue-in-the-softphone)
