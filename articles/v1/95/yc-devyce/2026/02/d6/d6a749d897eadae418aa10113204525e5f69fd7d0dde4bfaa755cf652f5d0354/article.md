# What's New at Devyce? Feb 2026

This month’s updates are all about reducing friction and helping your teams move faster with less effort. From easier access to deeper collaboration and a more proactive assistant by your side, February brings tools designed to fit seamlessly into the way you already work.


### **Here’s what’s new**


#
**Microsoft Teams integration**


‍


‍


Devyce now works with Microsoft Teams! Our Teams bot jumps into your calls, syncs with your Microsoft Calendar, and automatically sends your notes straight to your CRM. Voice calls, video calls - whatever you’re on, Devyce has your back.


No more frantic typing, no more lost insights. Just smooth, effortless note-taking, exactly where you need it.


[Find out more](https://devyce-knowledge-base.help.usepylon.com/articles/3783624542-teams-integration) **‍**


‍


# **SSO**


‍


‍


Logging in just got a whole lot smoother. Our new Single Sign-On (SSO) feature means you can access Devyce using your existing company credentials—no more juggling passwords or hunting for login details.


It’s quick, secure, and gets you into the platform faster so you can focus on the work that matters. One login, zero hassle.SSO is now live and ready for your team to switch on. If you are interested in enabling SSO please register your interest below!


Contact us


‍


# **Sidekick**


‍


‍


Say hello to[Sidekick](https://devyce.com/sidekick) - Devyce’s agentic AI. Just make a request, and Sidekick responds. Want a job spec or candidate profile crafted straight from a call? Easy.


Sidekick uses the AI-generated summary and full call transcript to pull the info you need—fast, accurate, and hassle-free. It’s like having a super-powered assistant sitting right beside you, making every call more productive.Start prompting Sidekick and see what your calls can really do!


Contact us


‍


# **Number blocking**


‍


‍


Admins can now block unwanted numbers once, and they’ll be blocked across the entire organisation—no more repeat spam calls sneaking through different teams.


Less noise, more control, and way more peace of mind.


[Find out more](https://devyce-knowledge-base.help.usepylon.com/articles/1107904807-blocking-numbers-admin)
