# Say hello to Sidekick - Devyce’s agentic AI

Say hello to[Sidekick](https://devyce.com/sidekick) - Devyce’s agentic AI.


Sidekick is an AI-powered digital assistant for recruiters that transforms call and message history into instant action. Integrated directly into the recruiter's workflow, it eliminates administrative burden by using **context-aware intelligence** to generate job specs, perform deep memory searches for candidate details, and create personalised follow-up messages. By automating post-call tasks, Sidekick ensures recruitment momentum is never lost to manual data entry.


‍


#### **Why Sidekick is a Game-Changer:**


- **Digital Memory** : Understands the full history of past calls and messages, not just the latest interaction.
- **Instant Content** : Drafts tailored job specs and follow-up emails based on real conversation data.
- **Deep Search** : Instantly retrieves specific details like salary expectations or notice periods from weeks ago.
- **Workflow Integration** : Operates within the conversation page to eliminate tab-switching and copy-pasting.


‍


It sits alongside your calls and responds to simple requests, whether that’s pulling together a job spec, creating a candidate profile, or surfacing key details from a conversation you’ve just had.


Sidekick uses the AI-generated summary and full call transcript to find exactly what you need, without you having to rewind recordings or sift through notes.


‍


It’s like having a super-powered assistant sitting right beside you, making every call more productive.
