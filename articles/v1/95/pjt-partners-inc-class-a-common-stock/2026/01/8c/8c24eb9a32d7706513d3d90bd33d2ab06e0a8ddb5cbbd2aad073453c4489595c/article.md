# PJT Partners Inc. to Report Full Year and Fourth Quarter 2025 Financial Results and Host a Conference Call on February 3, 2026


January 27, 2026 4:43pm EST


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- PJT Partners Inc. (“PJT Partners”) (NYSE:PJT) announced that it expects to release its full year and fourth quarter 2025 financial results on Tuesday morning, February 3, 2026. The earnings release will be available through the Investor Relations section of the PJT Partners website at[https://www.pjtpartners.com](https://www.pjtpartners.com/) .


PJT Partners will host a conference call on Tuesday, February 3, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET with access available via webcast and telephone. Paul J. Taubman, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, and Helen T. Meates, Chief Financial Officer, will review the results and be available for questions.


Investors and analysts may participate in the live conference call by dialing +1 (833) 316-1983 (U.S. domestic) or +1 (785) 838‑9310 (international), passcode PJTP4Q25. Please dial in 15 minutes before the conference call begins. The conference call will also be accessible as a listen-only audio webcast through the Investor Relations section of the[PJT Partners website](https://event.on24.com/wcc/r/5216881/70E323493B23995D62B9828F061AE65B) .


For those unable to listen to the live broadcast, a replay of the webcast will be available for four months beginning at approximately 11:30 a.m. ET on February 3, 2026 through the Investor Relations section of the[PJT Partners website](https://ir.pjtpartners.com/) .


**About PJT Partners**
