*CSO Neil Bhowmick to deliver company presentation and will be available for investor meetings*


LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Kairos Pharma Ltd.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fkairospharma.com%2F&esheet=54400105&newsitemid=20260120492734&lan=en-US&anchor=Kairos+Pharma+Ltd.&index=1&md5=ea56eae55876b411d7375269e6a013ea) (NYSE American: KAPA), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on innovative cancer therapeutics, today announced that it will participate in the DealFlow Discovery Conference, taking place Jan. 28-29, 2026, at the Borgata Hotel in Atlantic City, NJ. Kairos Pharma Chief Scientific Officer Neil Bhowmick will deliver a company presentation and be available for one-on-one investor meetings throughout the event.


Event Details:


- 3 rd Annual DealFlow Discovery Conference
- The Borgata Hotel, Casino & Spa
- Atlantic City, NJ
- Jan. 28-29, 2026


Investors interested in scheduling a meeting with the Kairos Pharma management team should request an investor pass to attend the conference at[dealflowdiscoveryconference.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fdealflowdiscoveryconference.com%2F&esheet=54400105&newsitemid=20260120492734&lan=en-US&anchor=dealflowdiscoveryconference.com&index=2&md5=b005b98471b2300f6e1e43df5f08cf20) . There is no cost to attend.


**About Kairos Pharma Ltd.**


Based in Los Angeles, California, Kairos Pharma Ltd. ([NYSE American: KAPA](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.kairospharma.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54400105&newsitemid=20260120492734&lan=en-US&anchor=NYSE+American%3A+KAPA&index=3&md5=d72ddd65803ccee4eee5a47bcfe67262) ) is at the forefront of oncology therapeutics, utilizing structural biology to overcome drug resistance and immune suppression in cancer. Our lead candidate, ENV-105, is an antibody that targets CD105—a protein identified as a key driver of resistance and disease relapse in response to standard therapy. ENV-105 aims to reverse drug resistance by targeting CD105 and restore the effectiveness of standard therapies across multiple cancer types. Currently, ENV-105 is in a Phase 2 clinical trial for castrate-resistant prostate cancer and a Phase 1 trial for non-small cell lung cancer aimed at addressing significant unmet medical needs. As of the date of this press release, ENV-105 has not been approved as safe or effective by the United States Food and Drug Administration or any other comparable foreign regulator. For more information, visit[kairospharma.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fkairospharma.com%2F&esheet=54400105&newsitemid=20260120492734&lan=en-US&anchor=kairospharma.com&index=4&md5=1c184356d925400be86228a8bc9e6a6d) **.**
