# Chunghwa Telecom June Revenue NT$ 21.34 Billion, Revenue and EBITDA Both Hit Record Highs for June

# Chunghwa Telecom June Revenue NT$ 21.34 Billion, Revenue and EBITDA Both Hit Record Highs for June


2026/07/13


Chunghwa Telecom announced its unaudited consolidated operating results for June 2026. In June, the unaudited consolidated revenue was NT$ 21.34 billion. Operating income was NT$ 4.08 billion, and income before tax was NT$ 4.67 billion. Net income attributable to stockholders of the parent company was NT$3.55 billion and EBITDA reached NT$ 7.50 billion, while EPS was NT$0.46.


Chunghwa Telecom highlighted that its consolidated revenue increased by 11.4% year-over-year, primarily driven by robust growth in the ICT business, continued momentum in iPhone handset sales, and solid expansion of core telecom business. As a result, its consolidated revenue and EBITDA achieved record highs for the same period in June.


ICT business revenue grew nearly 50% year-over-year in June,


primarily driven by the multi-fold growth of integrated project revenues, mainly due to the completion of AI supply chain projects delivered in the US market. Meanwhile, cybersecurity revenue nearly doubled in June, thanks to the continued expansion of projects in power utilities, digital certificate services, and display systems sectors.


Telecom core services continued to demonstrate stable growth. Mobile service revenue increased 3.6% year over year, mainly driven by steady growth in monthly mobile subscription revenue resulting from the continued expansion of the 5G subscriber base, as well as increases in roaming revenue and enterprise SMS revenue. In addition, fixed broadband revenue increased 3.7% year over year as subscribers using 300Mbps-or-higher packages grew 14 % year over year, reflecting sustained demand for high-speed broadband services.


In addition, the 2026 FIFA World Cup™, which kicked off in June, fueled strong viewership growth for MOD and Hami Video, further boosting our channel subscription revenue. Hami Video's exclusive 24-hour football channel and replay features were well received by football fans. Even before the tournament began, subscriptions to our Sports Package Day-pass plan had reached 3.7 times the level recorded during the corresponding period of the previous tournament. This once again demonstrated Chunghwa Telecom's success of delivering high-quality, reliable, and innovative broadcasting services while bringing the excitement of the world's premier football event to audiences across Taiwan.


As of June 2026, the accumulated unaudited consolidated revenue was NT$121.35 billion. Operating income was NT$ 26.36 billion and income before tax was NT$ 27.13 billion. Net income attributable to stockholders of the parent was NT$ 20.75 billion and EPS was NT$ 2.68. Revenue, operating income, income before tax, net income attributable to stockholders of the parent and EPS all exceeded guidance.

**About Chunghwa Telecom**


Chunghwa Telecom (TAIEX 2412, NYSE: CHT) (“Chunghwa” or “the Company”) is Taiwan’s largest integrated telecommunications services company that provides fixed-line, mobile, broadband, and internet services. The Company also provides information and communication technology services to corporate customers with its big data, information security, cloud computing and IDC capabilities, and is expanding its business into innovative technology services such as IoT, AI, etc. Chunghwa has been actively and continuously implemented environmental, social and governance (ESG) initiatives with the goal to achieve sustainability and has won numerous international and domestic awards and recognitions for its ESG commitments and best practices.
