# Chunghwa Telecom July Revenue NT$19.88 Billion, Revenue and EBITDA Both Hit Record Highs for July

# Chunghwa Telecom July Revenue NT$19.88 Billion, Revenue and EBITDA Both Hit Record Highs for July


2026/08/10


Chunghwa Telecom announced its unaudited consolidated operating results for July 2026. In July, the unaudited consolidated revenue was NT$19.88 billion. Operating income was NT$4.44 billion, and income before tax was NT$4.48 billion. Net income attributable to stockholders of the parent company was NT$3.34 billion and EBITDA reached NT$7.87 billion, while EPS was NT$0.43.


Chunghwa Telecom highlighted that its consolidated revenue increased by 3.7% year-over-year, primarily driven by strong sales revenue, contributions from subsidiaries, and gains from asset revitalization initiatives. As a result, its consolidated revenue and EBITDA both achieved record highs for the same period in July.


Sales revenue benefited from strong iPhone17 sales and solid demand from consumer electronics products, alongside increased subsidiary revenue fueled by AI demand, resulting in double-digit year-over-year growth. Moreover, our ongoing asset revitalization initiatives also continued to deliver positive results, contributing to both revenue and profit growth during the month.


Telecom core services continued to demonstrate steady growth. Mobile service revenue increased by nearly 3% year over year, supported by continued 5G speed upgrades, a growing number of upgrade subscribers, as well as stronger roaming demand. Fixed broadband revenue increased 2.6% year over year as subscribers using 300Mbps-or-higher packages grew 14.4% year over year, driven by ongoing digital transformation and AI adoption and serving as a strong pillar in the core telecom service growth.


Network resilience business continued to expand, whose related revenues delivered robust double-digit growth year-over-year, driven by continued contributions from SJC2 and Apricot submarine cables, as well as satellite service growth. This performance highlights the effectiveness of Chunghwa Telecom’s "Sea-Land-Sky" strategy and strengthens its leading position in resilient connectivity services.


ICT revenue declined year-over-year in July due to a high comparison base resulting from the recognition of large one-time projects in the same period last year. Nevertheless, revenue from mobile application projects grew by nearly 30% year-over-year, and IDC recurring revenue from co-location services continued to make steady contribution. These results reflect sustained demand for enterprise digital transformation, AI applications, and cloud adoption.


As of July 2026, the accumulated unaudited consolidated revenue was NT$141.24 billion. Operating income was NT$30.81 billion and income before tax was NT$31.61 billion. Net income attributable to stockholders of the parent was NT$24.09 billion and EPS was NT$3.11. Revenue, operating income, income before tax, net income attributable to stockholders of the parent and EPS all exceeded guidance.

**About Chunghwa Telecom**


Chunghwa Telecom (TAIEX 2412, NYSE: CHT) (“Chunghwa” or “the Company”) is Taiwan’s largest integrated telecommunications services company that provides fixed-line, mobile, broadband, and internet services. The Company also provides information and communication technology services to corporate customers with its big data, information security, cloud computing and IDC capabilities, and is expanding its business into innovative technology services such as IoT, AI, etc. Chunghwa has been actively and continuously implemented environmental, social and governance (ESG) initiatives with the goal to achieve sustainability and has won numerous international and domestic awards and recognitions for its ESG commitments and best practices.
