## 2. Doodly — Closest Like-for-Like


**What it is:** Manual drag-and-drop doodle-style whiteboard animation. The closest workflow analog to VideoScribe in this list.


**How it compares to VideoScribe:** Similar manual scene-building loop with a slightly different asset library and visual style. Doodly leans casual/doodle; VideoScribe leans classic hand-drawn.


**Time per video:** Hours per video. Same order of magnitude as VideoScribe.


**When to pick Doodly:** You like manual whiteboard composition and want a doodle aesthetic.


**When to skip Doodly:** Volume. Doodly does not solve the underlying problem that brought you here. See the[Doodly alternatives comparison](https://video.golpoai.com/guide/best-doodly-alternatives-2026) .


## 3. Powtoon — If You Want Slide-Style Animation


**What it is:** Template-driven animation editor. Edit slide-by-slide templates with characters and scenes.


**How it compares to VideoScribe:** Different visual style entirely. Animated slide presentations rather than whiteboard sketches.


**Time per video:** 2–4 hours per video.


**When to pick Powtoon:** Your team produces slide-based explainers and wants light animation on top.


**When to skip Powtoon:** Slide-style outputs are often a less natural fit for procedural, technical, and document-heavy training than a whiteboard explainer. See the[Powtoon alternatives comparison](https://video.golpoai.com/guide/best-powtoon-alternatives-2026) .


## 4. Vyond — Enterprise Character Animation


**What it is:** Full 2D animation studio. Characters, lip-sync, scene transitions, full timeline editor.


**How it compares to VideoScribe:** Far more capable for character-driven scenarios; far more time-intensive per video.


**Time per video:** Hours to days.


**When to pick Vyond:** Enterprise L&D with budget and timeline for full character animation (sales training role-plays, compliance dramatizations).


**When to skip Vyond:** Whiteboard-style explainers. Vyond is the wrong tool for the format.


## 5. Animaker — Multiple Style Families


**What it is:** Drag-and-drop animation editor with broad style range — 2D cartoon, 2.5D, whiteboard, infographic, handcraft.


**When to pick Animaker:** You want to switch between styles across different videos and accept the manual editing cost.


## 6. Renderforest — Quick Template Output


**What it is:** Online template-based video maker. Whiteboard is one of many template categories.


**When to pick Renderforest:** Quick, light-customization template videos. Not a true whiteboard specialist.


## 7. Canva — Design-First Teams


**What it is:** Canva's animated video features inside their design editor.


**When to pick Canva:** Your team lives in Canva for everything else and you want to keep video inside the same workflow.
