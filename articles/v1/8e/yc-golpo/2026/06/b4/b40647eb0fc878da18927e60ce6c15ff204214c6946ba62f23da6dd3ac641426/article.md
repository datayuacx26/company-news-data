## Traditional vs AI Whiteboard Animation


The single most important decision you make is which category to buy from. The two workflows feel entirely different.


### Traditional Whiteboard Animation


- You write the script.
- You design or pick every illustration.
- You build the timeline frame by frame.
- You record or upload narration.
- You manually sync animation to narration.
- You spend hours to a full day per video.


### AI Whiteboard Animation


- You paste a prompt, script, or document.
- The AI writes (or uses) the script.
- The AI illustrates every frame.
- The AI narrates (or uses your voiceover).
- The AI syncs animation to narration.
- You spend 10–15 minutes per video.
- You can still edit any frame or line if you want to.


If you want manual control over every single drawing, the traditional category wins. If you want to convert a backlog of written content into videos — or you make more than a handful of videos per month — AI wins by a wide margin.

- **Workflow:** Drag-and-drop scene builder with a large doodle-style asset library and a hand-drawing effect.
- **Visual styles:** Whiteboard, blackboard, greenboard, glassboard, doodle.
- **Editing:** Manual — you place every asset and set every timing.
- **Languages:** TTS in a moderate number of languages; many users record narration manually.

- **Best for:** Solo creators who want to manually build classic doodle-style videos.
- **Pricing:** One-time or annual license.
- **Where it loses:** Speed, scale, document-to-video, and AI-driven script/visual generation.


### VideoScribe


- **Workflow:** Long-established whiteboard scene composer with a draw-on hand effect.
- **Visual styles:** Whiteboard, blackboard, custom backgrounds.
- **Editing:** Manual scene-by-scene timeline.
- **Languages:** Multilingual TTS; many users add narration externally.
- **Time per video:** Hours per video.
- **Best for:** Buyers who specifically want the classic VideoScribe hand-drawing aesthetic and full manual control.
- **Pricing:** Subscription.
- **Where it loses:** Volume, document-to-video, prompt-to-video, and modern AI scripting.
