# A Case for Systematic Protection Amid Market & Political Uncertainty


September 19, 2025


One year ago, we launched[Calamos Laddered S&P 500® Structured Alt Protection ETF™ (CPSL)](https://www.calamos.com/funds/etf/calamos-laddered-sp-500-structured-alt-protection-cpsl/) to address a critical gap in the market. Financial advisors needed a turnkey solution to provide structured outcome ETFs through a laddered approach, but they lacked the time and resources to implement this strategy on their own. Meanwhile, institutional clients sought upside potential while preserving assets, creating additional demand for innovative protection solutions.


## Calamos Met the Challenge with Innovation


CPSL's systematic approach to investing in 100% downside protection ETFs offers three key advantages:


- **Single-Ticker Solution:** Accesses the full suite of a dozen S&P 500 Structured Protection ETFs
- **Outcome Period Diversification:** Removes timing considerations from selecting monthly entry points
- **Protected Participation:** Delivers upside return potential from the underlying structured protection strategies


## Simplicity by Design


The real beauty of CPSL lies in its simplicity. As a fund of funds, it equally allocates capital across 12 Calamos S&P 500® Structured Alt Protection ETFs®, with each fund initiating its outcome period in a different month. This design creates continuous laddered exposure to downside protection and long-term equity upside potential.


## Recent Performance in Real Market Conditions


This design enabled CPSL to demonstrate its value during challenging market conditions. The fund provided notable downside protection during the late February to early April tariff-related selloff, which sent the S&P 500 tumbling -18.75%, while successfully capturing gains in the subsequent market recovery.


Since its inception on September 9, 2024, CPSL has generated a 7.18% return through August 31, 2025, having demonstrated effective risk management and reduced volatility through its laddered approach.


## Why the Laddered Approach Works


**Continuous Protection & Smoother Returns:** By investing in multiple structured protection ETFs with different monthly reset dates, investors access uninterrupted downside safeguards while minimizing timing risks. The goals of this approach are to reduce the risk of entering at unfavorable points and deliver smoother returns with significantly less volatility than stocks, bonds, and other buffer strategies offering partial protection.


**Diversified Market Exposure:** Markets are inherently unpredictable. Laddering spreads exposure across different market conditions, moderating volatility and capturing growth periods more effectively. This “time” diversification can lead to better long-term outcomes by capturing various phases of market cycles.


**Flexibility & Tax Efficiency:** These ETFs offer daily liquidity and possible tax advantages. Unlike most capital-protected solutions that treat returns as ordinary income upon expiration, structured protection ETFs grow and compound tax-deferred, with gains treated as long-term capital gains when held over one year.


### CPSL + S&P 500: A Potentially Better Way to Buffer


CPSL paired with an S&P 500 ETF delivered superior five-year returns (11.8% vs 10.9%) with enhanced downside protection at just one-third the cost of traditional 10% buffer ETFs. This flexible combination allows investors to customize their risk exposure while capturing more upside potential and paying significantly lower fees than standalone buffer strategies.[See our at-a-glance analysis](https://www.calamos.com/globalassets/media/documents/product-literature/product-perspectives/cpsl-and-sp-500-a-potentially-better-way-to-buffer.pdf) .


## Portfolio Applications


**As an Equity Hedge:** Advisors use laddered strategies to de-risk portfolios without sacrificing upside potential. Moving 50% of equity exposure to a 100% protection strategy may reduce downside risk by approximately 50% while maintaining meaningful upside participation.


**As a Fixed Income Replacement:** For the trillions of dollars in short-term debt instruments, structured protection strategies may offer similar or better downside management than bonds as well as meaningful, superior upside potential—especially on an after-tax basis where the tax “alpha” can far outweigh bond yields over time.


**For Institutional Balance Sheets:** Financial professionals managing institutional assets can access equity-linked growth potential without significant balance sheet risk, while maintaining the flexibility and liquidity needed for capital access.


## The Growing Opportunity


With volatility factors multiplying—from AI disruption and Fed politicization to trade tensions and stretched valuations—the case for systematic protection has never been stronger. As more investors discover the merits of equity market exposure with a high degree of built-in protection, we believe the laddered approach will continue gaining traction as an innovative solution that delivers a significantly hedged experience while retaining upside potential in a flexible, liquid, and tax-efficient framework.
