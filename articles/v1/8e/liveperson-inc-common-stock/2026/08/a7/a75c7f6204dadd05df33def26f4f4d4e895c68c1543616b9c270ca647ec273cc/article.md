# LivePerson Reminds Stockholders of Investor Town Hall on August 5

*Encourages Stockholders to Vote FOR the Proposed Acquisition with SoundHound AI*


*Town Hall Access Available at*[ir.liveperson.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4745029-1&h=4051713938&u=https%3A%2F%2Fir.liveperson.com%2F&a=ir.liveperson.com)


NEW YORK


, Aug. 4, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- LivePerson (NASDAQ: LPSN) ("LivePerson" or "the Company"), a leading provider of predictable conversational AI, today reminded stockholders of its upcoming investor town hall and Q&A webcast on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. ET to discuss the benefits of the Company's previously announced transaction with SoundHound AI, Inc. (NASDAQ: SOUN).


The town hall can be accessed by logging onto the investor relations section of the Company's website at[ir.liveperson.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4745029-1&h=4051713938&u=https%3A%2F%2Fir.liveperson.com%2F&a=ir.liveperson.com) . Stockholders may submit questions in advance of the town hall atir-lp@liveperson.com .


On the town hall, LivePerson CEO John Sabino will discuss the following topics related to the transaction with SoundHound:


- **The value and potential future upside LivePerson stockholders will receive through this transaction.**


- **The Board's comprehensive process in reaching this transaction.**


- **The negotiated resolution with the Company's debtholders.**


- **What happens if stockholders do not approve the transaction.**


"We want to ensure every investor understands what is at stake and why casting a **'FOR'** vote right now is imperative to securing the value of your shares," said Sabino. "I encourage all stockholders to submit questions and join the town hall tomorrow, and to vote to support the transaction today. Every single vote matters, and failing to vote is functionally the same as voting against the deal."
