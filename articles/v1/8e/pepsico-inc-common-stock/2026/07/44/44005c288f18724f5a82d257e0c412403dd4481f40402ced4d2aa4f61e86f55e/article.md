# How PepsiCo is helping shape the dirty soda trend

Dirty soda has grown from a regional favorite into a national beverage trend. The idea is simple: take a soft drink and add creamy flavors, syrups or other mix-ins to create something that feels fun, customized, and a little special.


The concept first gained traction in the 2010s when consumers customized sodas with cream and sweet flavors as an alternative to more traditional coffee drinks or cocktails and began posting their creations online. It has since exploded outward through social sharing, creator culture and a growing consumer appetite for playful, personalized drinks. But the creamy soda offering is not entirely new. Its origins can be found in classic soda fountain culture beginning in the 1800s, as well as cream sodas and root beer floats.


That recent mix of personalization and treat-like appeal has helped dirty soda take off, especially as more people discover and share new drink combinations online.


“Dirty soda, nostalgic flavors, and float-inspired decadence were all showing up organically in culture in repeatable ways,” said Michael Smith, VP of marketing, flavored sparkling beverages. “Our role isn’t to jump on the bandwagon of every trend — it’s to listen, identify what has staying power, and translate it into something we can scale in a way that’s true to our brands.”
