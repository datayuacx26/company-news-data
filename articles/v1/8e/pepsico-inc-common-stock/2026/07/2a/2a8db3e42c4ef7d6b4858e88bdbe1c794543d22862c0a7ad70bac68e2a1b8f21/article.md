# New limited-time flavor brings a bold kick to the Tostitos® roster in time for football season

## Frequently asked questions


**When will Tostitos Spicy Queso Blanco Flavored Tortilla Chips be available?**


Tostitos Spicy Queso Blanco Flavored Tortilla Chips will be available nationwide beginning August 9 in an 11-ounce bag.


**Does Tostitos make guacamole dip?**


Yes. Tostitos Chunky Guacamole Mild and Medium Hint of Lime Flavored Dip brings the brand's signature chip-and-dip experience to the refrigerated aisle. The guacamole starts with fresh Hass avocados, and has onions, tomatoes, jalapeño peppers, a hint of lime flavor, and other spices.


**Is Tostitos Chunky Guacamole made without artificial colors, flavors, and preservatives?**


Yes. Tostitos Chunky Guacamole is made with no artificial colors, no artificial flavors, and no artificial preservatives. It is also Non-GMO Project Verified, vegan, kosher, and gluten free.


**When and where can consumers buy Tostitos Chunky Guacamole?**


Tostitos Chunky Guacamole Mild and Medium Hint of Lime Flavored Dip will be available at Kroger and Walmart stores nationwide beginning August 15 in 8-ounce, 15-ounce, and 6-count packages of 2-ounce single-serve cups.


**What are some of the most popular snacks for watching football?**


With nearly three-quarters of Americans (74%) choosing chips and dip while watching football2, Tostitos is expanding its game day lineup with Tostitos Spicy Queso Blanco Flavored Tortilla Chips, and Tostitos Chunky Guacamole ahead of NFL kickoff.


**Are Tostitos NFL Team Bags returning this football season?**


Yes. Tostitos is bringing back the limited-edition NFL Team Bags with refreshed designs. The updated packaging features more fan-focused graphics and celebrates NFL team pride throughout football season. Participating teams include New York Giants, New England Patriots, Philadelphia Eagles, Detroit Lions, Kansas City Chiefs, Cleveland Browns, Chicago Bears, Buffalo Bills, Pittsburgh Steelers, San Francisco 49ers, Los Angeles Rams, Dallas Cowboys, Cincinnati Bengals, Minnesota Vikings, Arizona Cardinals, Seattle Seahawks, Indianapolis Colts, and Green Bay Packers.


**When can consumers find limited-edition Tostitos NFL Team Bags?**


Limited-edition Tostitos NFL Team Bags will be available regionally beginning in August.
