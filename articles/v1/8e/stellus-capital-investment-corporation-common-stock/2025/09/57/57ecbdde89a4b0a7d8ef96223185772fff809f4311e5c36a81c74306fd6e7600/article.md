# Stellus Capital Management, LLC Provides Unitranche Financing in Support of Tailwind Capital’s Investment in DMD Systems Recovery


Sep 23, 2025


######


###### SHARE


**Houston, TX** – **September 23, 2025** – Stellus Capital Management, LLC (“Stellus”) announced today that its affiliates provided senior debt financing and an equity co-investment in support of Tailwind Capital’s (“Tailwind”) investment in DMD Systems Recovery (“DMD” or the “Company”), an end-to-end IT asset disposition (“ITAD”) solutions provider.


DMD services aging data center and networking infrastructure and end-user compute assets, providing a complete ITAD solution tailored to unique customer needs with services including asset auditing, secure wiping, deinstallation, lease return management, data center decommissioning, on-site services and secure disposal. DMD’s flexible approach and range of services aims to maximize secondary resale value for its clients, and to allow customers to outsource the highly manual work of disposing of data center, networking and end user devices in an efficient, secure and responsible manner.


“We are thrilled to partner with the DMD team to support the company’s continued organic growth and M&A strategy,” said Tailwind Partner, Will Fleder, adding, “DMD services a global client base, and is well-positioned to capitalize on the broader ITAD market tailwinds. We value our partnership with Stellus and are excited to work together in support of DMD’s future growth.”


Doug Bollermann, Managing Director at Stellus, added, “We are excited for the opportunity to continue our relationship with Tailwind, who has a proven track record of scaling IT services businesses,” adding, “DMD’s solution-oriented offerings and impressive momentum since inception presents an attractive opportunity to build a broader ITAD platform.”
