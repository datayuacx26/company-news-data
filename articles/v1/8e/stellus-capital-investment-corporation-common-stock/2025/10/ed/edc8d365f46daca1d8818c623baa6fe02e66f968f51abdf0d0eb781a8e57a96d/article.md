# Stellus Capital Management, LLC Provides Unitranche Financing in Support of Palm Peak Capital’s Acquisition of Domaille Engineering


Oct 07, 2025


######


###### SHARE


**Houston, TX** – **October 7, 2025** – Stellus Capital Management, LLC (“Stellus”) announced today that its affiliates provided senior debt financing and an equity co-investment in support of Palm Peak Capital’s (“Palm Peak”) acquisition of Domaille Engineering (“Domaille” or the “Company”), a leading provider of highly advanced equipment and related accessories used to manufacture pre-terminated fiber optic cables.


Founded in 1990 and headquartered in Rochester, Minnesota, Domaille was previously a division of ADDMAN Engineering. The Company produces innovative equipment and tools used to manufacture advanced high bandwidth fiber optic cables. The Company’s diverse customer base includes leading cable assembly houses and commercial clients serving data centers, telecommunications providers, and other enterprise end markets.


“With significant intellectual property and technical manufacturing expertise, Domaille Engineering is a clear leader in fiber optic manufacturing solutions, serving customers in some of the fastest-growing areas of the digital economy,” said Dante Pavone, Vice President at Palm Peak. Jeremy Stone, Managing Partner, added, “We appreciate the support of the Stellus team in helping us accelerate Domaille’s product innovation and growth.”


“We are grateful for the opportunity to partner with Palm Peak on another transaction,” said Adam Pollock, Managing Director at Stellus. “Further, we are excited to partner with a Domaille team that has led the way in fiber optic equipment innovation and is well-positioned for their next phase of growth amidst some favorable demand drivers.”
