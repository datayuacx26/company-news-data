# Embed studies in your product, plus better translations

[Back to updates](https://www.getversive.com/blog)


February 23, 2026 •


Product Updates


### Embed studies in your product, plus better translations


Our new embed SDK lets you add Versive studies to any website, plus view translations in grid view.


---


We've shipped a couple of new features focused on making it easier to integrate Versive into your existing workflows and products.


### Embed studies on any website


You can now embed Versive studies — surveys, AI interviews, or any study type — directly on your own website or product. Our new embed SDK (` @getversive/embed` ) gives you two options:


- **Script tag** : Drop a single` <script>` tag onto your page and add a button with a data attribute. No code required.
- **JavaScript SDK** : Import the SDK programmatically for full control over when and how the study appears.


Studies can be displayed as a **modal** overlay or **inline** within your page. The SDK is lightweight (~4KB gzipped), has zero dependencies, and works with any framework.


You can also forward context — like a user ID or session identifier — so responses are tied back to the right user in your system. Allowed embed domains are managed in your organization settings, and embedding is blocked by default for security.


### Translated responses in grid view


If your study collects responses in multiple languages, you can now view translations directly in the grid view. A language dropdown lets you switch between the original responses and any available translations, with a visual indicator for content that hasn't been translated yet.
