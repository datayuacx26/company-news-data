# What's new: API and security upgrades, a new site, and more

[Back to updates](https://www.getversive.com/blog)


June 4, 2026 •


Product Updates


### What's new: API and security upgrades, a new site, and more


A roundup of recent improvements: active session management and login protection, new API capabilities, deep links to insights, invite tracking, and a refreshed website.


---


A roundup of improvements we've shipped across the platform recently, from account security to new ways to work with the Versive API.


### More you can do with the API


We introduced the Versive API earlier this year so you can build research workflows programmatically. A few additions since then:


- **Attach assets to questions** : upload images and other assets to your questions directly through the API, so studies created programmatically can be as rich as ones built in the app.
- **Trigger study translations** : kick off translation of a study from the API, making it easier to run multilingual research at scale.
- **Choose speed or depth** : when building studies through the API, you can now opt for faster responses or more thorough ones, depending on what your workflow needs.


### Stronger account security


Two updates that matter especially for teams handling sensitive research:


- **Active session management** : you can now see where your account is signed in and sign out of any session you don't recognize. If you've ever logged in on a shared or borrowed device, you can revoke that access in a click.
- **Login protection** : we added safeguards against repeated failed login attempts, so your account is better protected against password-guessing.


### Jump straight to the insight that matters


Insights are now **deep-linkable** . You can link directly to a specific question's insights and share that exact view with a teammate or stakeholder, instead of pointing them at a study and explaining where to look.


### Track your outreach


For teams using Email Outreach to invite participants, you can now **track invites you've sent** , so you know what went out and can follow up with confidence. We also added each participant's unique link to **CSV exports** , making it easier to manage and reference links outside of Versive.


### Richer study text


You can now add **hyperlinks** to the text in your studies, useful for linking out to a prototype, a reference, or context you want participants to have before they answer.


### A refreshed website


Finally, we gave[getversive.com](https://www.getversive.com/) a refresh: a redesigned homepage along with new product and solution pages that better explain what Versive can do. Take a look.
