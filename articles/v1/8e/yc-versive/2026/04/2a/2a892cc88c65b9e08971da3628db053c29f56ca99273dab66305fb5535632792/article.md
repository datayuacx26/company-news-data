# Email Outreach: invite participants from inside Versive

[Back to updates](https://www.getversive.com/blog)


April 16, 2026 •


Product Updates


### Email Outreach: invite participants from inside Versive


A built-in email outreach tool for inviting and managing study participants — with a rich composer, reusable templates, an audience contact list, and full delivery tracking.


---


We've added Email Outreach to Versive. You can now invite participants to a study, manage your contact list, and track delivery — all without leaving the platform.


Until now, recruiting from your own audience meant exporting a study link, opening a separate email tool, and pasting in contacts. Email Outreach folds that loop into the Launch tab so a study can go from "ready" to "in the field" in a few clicks.


### A real composer for participant invites


Outreach emails aren't transactional notifications — they're the first impression of your research. The composer is built for that.


- **Rich text editor** with formatting, links, and lists
- **Personalization** via merge fields like name, email, and interview link, so every recipient gets a tailored message
- **Preview mode** that shows exactly what a real recipient will see, before you send
- **Templates** to save the emails you reuse most and load them when you start a new send


Iterate on copy, save what works, reuse it across every study.


### Audience: a single contact list


Outreach is backed by a new **Audience** page — your organization's unified list of people you can email.


- **Add, edit, import, and remove contacts** with search and filtering
- **Automatic status tracking** for subscribed, unsubscribed, and bounced contacts. Anyone who unsubscribes or hard-bounces is suppressed from future sends without you having to clean the list
- **One-click unsubscribe** in every outreach email, so recipients can opt out cleanly and the change reflects in your audience immediately


Your audience grows as a clean, opt-in list over time instead of starting from a fresh CSV every study.


### Add participants from your audience to any study


When launching a study, pull in eligible contacts from your audience instead of re-uploading them. Only contacts who are subscribed, not bounced, and not already in the study show up as candidates — and the picker stays fast even with thousands of contacts.


The same audience can feed many studies, with the platform tracking who's already been invited where.


### Sending and delivery tracking


When you're ready to send, the recipient picker pulls from the participants in your study who are eligible to receive email.


- **Personalized batch sending** — every email is rendered against that recipient's data and delivered as an individual message
- **Send history** with stats per batch: sent, delivered, opened, bounced
- **Monthly send limits** are enforced per plan, with usage and limits visible in your settings


### Available now


Email Outreach is live for all customers on plans that include outreach sending. Open any study, head to the **Launch** tab, and click **Send** to try it.


If you're managing your own panel for research — internal users, customers, or a recruited list — this should save you a tool and a lot of copy-paste.
