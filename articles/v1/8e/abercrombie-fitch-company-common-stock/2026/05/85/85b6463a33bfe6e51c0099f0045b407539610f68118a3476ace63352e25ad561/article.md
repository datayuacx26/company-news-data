# Fortune’s Most Powerful Women List – 2026

For the third consecutive year, Fortune has named Abercrombie & Fitch Co. CEO Fran Horowitz to its annual Most Powerful Women list, recognizing the most influential female leaders shaping business today. Now in its 29th year, the list highlights executives leading high-performing organizations and driving meaningful impact across industries.


Under Fran’s leadership, Abercrombie & Fitch Co. has continued to evolve its Abercrombie and Hollister brands while achieving several significant milestones, including surpassing $5 billion in annual sales for the first time in company history. Fortune recognized Fran for her leadership in the continued evolution of the company’s brands, with A&F Co. maintaining strong momentum and growth under her leadership.


A&F Co. is proud to celebrate Fran’s continued recognition and grateful for her leadership, vision and commitment to moving the company forward. Congratulations to Fran on this well-deserved honor.
