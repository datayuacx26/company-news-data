# Hollister Launches Soccer-Inspired Capsule Collection

Hollister is kicking off the summer soccer season with the launch of a new capsule collection in partnership with Italian sportswear brand, Kappa. The 22-piece collaboration blends Kappa’s European sport heritage with Hollister’s fashion-first aesthetic, resulting in a collection designed for match days, watch parties and everyday wear.


The collection captures the growing intersection of sport, fashion and culture, ahead of what is shaping up to be an exciting summer of soccer tournaments and events. The collection features men’s and women’s styles inclusive of soccer jerseys, polos, graphic tees, track jackets, baggy denim, jorts, fleece shorts, reversible swimwear and accessories starting at $19.95.


Our customers know that soccer isn’t just a sport, it’s a lifestyle. And we look forward to outfitting them in versatile looks that they can wear, style and outfit all summer long. The Hollister x Kappa capsule will be available in select Hollister stores and at Hollisterco.com. The launch marks the start of Hollister’s summer soccer storytelling, with additional products, content and experiences rolling out throughout 2026.
