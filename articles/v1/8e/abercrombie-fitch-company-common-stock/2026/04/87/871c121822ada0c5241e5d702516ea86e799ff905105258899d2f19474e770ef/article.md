# Abercrombie & Fitch Revives Partnership with Sperry

Abercrombie & Fitch is continuing to expand its lifestyle offerings with the revival of a partnership alongside heritage American footwear brand, Sperry. Rooted in a partnership that dates back to the 1930s, the collection draws on the classic coastal American style both brands are known for, with inspiration from Abercrombie & Fitch’s 134-year-old history and Sperry’s relevance of the boat shoe being a modern staple for consumers across decades.


As customers increasingly turn to A&F for complete outfitting solutions, the brand has continued expanding its footwear assortment as part of its broader lifestyle offering, helping customers build a wardrobe that dresses them from head to toe. The A&F x Sperry collaboration represents that evolution, offering of apparel, footwear and other accessories designed to help customers create effortless, elevated looks. The collection will include seven apparel styles ranging from $55 to $150, featuring a Long Sleeve Rugby Polo, Essential Popover Hoodie, Cardigan, Long Sleeve Graphic Tee, Short Sleeve Graphic Tee and 5-in Relaxed Pull On Swim Shorts. It also introduces five exclusive footwear styles that reimagine Sperry’s Authentic Original Two Eye Boat Shoe and the Authentic Original Mule, featuring nautical-inspired, removable beading and charm details.


Abercrombie is excited to offer expanded assortments to its customers while simultaneously reintroducing a partnership from nearly 100-years ago. The Sperry x Abercrombie & Fitch collection will be available online April 9 at[Abercrombie.com](http://abercrombie.com/) , with select apparel styles available in Abercrombie & Fitch stores.
