# Introducing Baby & Toddler

abercrombie kids is thrilled to announce the launch of its Baby & Toddler Collection, introducing sizes ranging from newborn to 5T. This expansion allows Abercrombie customers to shop styles for their little ones from their earliest days through childhood and beyond. Previously, abercrombie kids offered children’s sizing beginning at 5 through 18. The new offering responds directly to customer feedback, delivering the same trusted quality, comfort and elevated style across more sizes than ever before.


The collection features nearly 100 new styles, including bodysuits, rompers, dresses, tops, bottoms and outerwear. Each piece is crafted with soft, durable fabrics and classic detailing that reflect the brand’s commitment to comfort, versatility and timeless design.


With the addition of newborn, baby and toddler sizing, abercrombie kids becomes a single, trusted destination for families. The collection is priced from $13 to $65 and is available now.
