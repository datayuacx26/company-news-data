# KLA Announces Webcast Details for Upcoming Investor Day

# KLA Announces Webcast Details for Upcoming Investor Day


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_b9ee755a5f60dd0fb3f9e27967aed6af/klatencor/news/2026-03-04_KLA_Announces_Webcast_Details_for_Upcoming_511.pdf) March 04, 2026 1:53pm EST


MILPITAS, Calif., March 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- KLA Corporation (NASDAQ: KLAC) today announced its Investor Day on Thursday, March 12, 2026 will be video webcast live on its investor relations website. The agenda will include presentations discussing the Company’s financial and operational performance, longer-term systems and services businesses, financial targets, and opportunities and strategies within market trends by Rick Wallace, President and CEO; Bren Higgins, Executive Vice President and CFO; and other members of executive management, starting at 9:00 a.m. ET. The presentations and a webcast question-and-answer session at the conclusion are expected to end at roughly 12:30 p.m. ET.


A live video webcast will be available on the Investor Relations page of KLA’s website at[https://ir.kla.com](https://ir.kla.com/) , beginning at 9:00 a.m. ET and will be archived for playback on KLA’s Investor Relations web page following the event.


**About KLA:**


KLA Corporation ("KLA") develops industry-leading equipment and services that enable innovation throughout the electronics industry. We provide advanced process control and process-enabling solutions for manufacturing wafers and reticles, integrated circuits, packaging and printed circuit boards. In close collaboration with leading customers across the globe, our expert teams of physicists, engineers, data scientists and problem-solvers design solutions that move the world forward. Investors and others should note that KLA announces material financial information including SEC filings, press releases, public earnings calls and conference webcasts using an investor relations website (ir.kla.com). Additional information may be found at: www.kla.com.
