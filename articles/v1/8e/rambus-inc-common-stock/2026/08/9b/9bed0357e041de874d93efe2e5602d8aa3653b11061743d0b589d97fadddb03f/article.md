# Securing Software-Defined Vehicles with an Arm-Based Root of Trust

In this webinar, Rambus will explore the changing automotive security landscape and the growing role of hardware Root of Trust technology as the foundation for trusted software execution, secure communications, lifecycle management, and future-ready protection. We will also introduce the automotive-grade Rambus CryptoManager RT-648 Root of Trust, our first Root of Trust solution built on an Arm® Cortex®-M33 processor. By leveraging an Arm-based architecture, RT-648 enables tighter alignment with Arm-centric design flows, software ecosystems, and next-generation automotive platforms, helping SoC developers accelerate integration while reducing risk.
