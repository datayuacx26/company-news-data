# Your current account and card: how it works


Your current account and your card don’t work the same way anymore. Here’s the simple version.


## 1. Your money sits on your current account


Your main balance lives on your current account. It’s **free to use and has no limits** .


That’s where you can:


- receive money
- send money
- get your salary paid in
- receive IBAN transfers
- top up or move money via mobile money
- check your balance


👉 The current account is now your main account.


## 2. Your card is your wallet


Your **card** is now separate from your current account.


It’s used only to:


- pay (online or in store)
- withdraw cash at an ATM


To use it, you first need to **load it from your current account** .


👉 It becomes your everyday wallet - a dedicated space for what you spend and withdraw.


## 3. Loading your card: simple and fast


To use your card:


1. Move money from your current account to your card.
2. You can pay or withdraw right away.


💡 **The easiest way: turn on auto-reload.** Your card refills itself from your account whenever it needs to.


## 4. Why the change?


This separation has a few clear upsides:


- more control over your spending
- more security, since your main balance stays on the account
- a clearer view of what you keep vs. what you spend
- a card that’s simpler to manage day-to-day
