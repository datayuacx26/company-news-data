# Listen to audiobooks on Audible with your Djamo card


Audiobooks, just like podcasts, have boomed over the past few years. You love a book but don’t have time to read it? Just listen to it.


This format has grown a lot thanks to smartphones and always-on internet connections.


Just like Spotify for your podcasts, Audible is the perfect platform to escape for a listening session.


Also read:[Listen to unlimited music on Spotify with Djamo](https://www.djamo.com/en-ci/learn/listen-to-unlimited-music-on-spotify-with-djamo/)


A pure Amazon product, you can use the same account to buy the latest releases if you have an Amazon Prime account.


Audible.com has over 500,000 novels, thrillers, documentaries and more. You’ll find everything you’re in the mood for. Headphones on, in the car or working out - listen to your audiobook on the platform with total peace of mind.


On Audible, you get a 30-day free trial to listen to an audiobook of your choice. After the trial, you’ll need to subscribe (9.95 €/month), which entitles you to one audiobook (rollover) every month.


## How to subscribe to Audible with your Djamo card


1. Start by creating an account


1. Then enter your personal information and your Djamo card details


1. Click the Try for free button


Done! You’ve just signed up to audible.com. By clicking the button, you authorize the site to charge 9.95 €/month to your Djamo card after the trial period. If you’re on a FREE plan, make sure to top up your card before the end of the trial to avoid rejection fees.
