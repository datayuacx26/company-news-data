# August recap: Djamo app updates 📱


August was a thrilling month. We launched several features you all loved. Here’s the recap.


## Wave, July 30, 2022


We wanted to extend Mobile Money deposits to more operators, so we added Wave to the existing options. The news was warmly welcomed. Whatever your operator - Orange, Moov, MTN, or Wave - you can deposit money on your card and use it for your payments.


## 1% Mobile Money, August 9, 2022


Not long after launch, Djamo removed the fees it charged on Mobile Money transactions. The decision fits with our mission to fight high fees. For Mobile Money deposits, you only pay the fees set by the operators.


After several rounds of negotiation, all operator fees dropped to 1% of your transaction amount.


Check out all Djamo fees on this[page](https://www.djamo.com/en-ci/pricing/) .


## Venditou and Gbêssê challenges, August 25, 2022


After the success of our first 100 MilleOKLM challenge, we launched two new ones: Venditou and Gbêssê.


The Venditou challenge is mainly for online sellers. Select the “Venditou” vault and save 50,000 F in a month.


Select the “Gbêssê” vault to save from 500 F a day and reach a 15,000 F goal in a month.


Pick the challenge you want and win cool prizes 🥳
