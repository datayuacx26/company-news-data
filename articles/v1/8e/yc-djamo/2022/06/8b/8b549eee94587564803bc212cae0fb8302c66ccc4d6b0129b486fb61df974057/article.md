# Make the best presentations on SlidesGo with your Djamo card


One of the secrets of a successful PowerPoint or Google Slides presentation is the design. With SlidesGo, level up by using original ready-made templates fully adapted to your project. Plus, you don’t need to be a graphic design or design expert for your document to grab attention.


Just like[Canva](https://www.canva.com/fr_fr/) , SlidesGo is a platform offering a wide range of free digital presentation templates for PowerPoint and Google Slides. Whether you have design skills or not, SlidesGo opens the doors of content creation for your presentation projects. On SlidesGo, you get structured, customized templates that help you capture your audience’s attention based on the project you’re presenting. If you’re a professional or a student, you’ll find ready-made templates for your presentation.

Beyond creative slides, every page of your presentation features storytelling steps that fit the project you’re presenting. The platform offers free designs as well as a premium option.


## SlidesGo’s premium offer


With SlidesGo’s premium offer, you get:


1. Exclusive access to all presentation templates plus extra resources in every template.
2. Priority support to contact customer service for any question.
3. Ad-free browsing.


If you’re a business owner, SlidesGo also offers the SlidesGo Teams option. SlidesGo Teams is the ideal subscription for businesses and schools that need to collaborate on presentation templates. The group subscription lets you enjoy all of SlidesGo’s premium content, edit it collaboratively, and easily share it with teammates or students. You also get a full license with unlimited template downloads for everyone on your group plan.


## How to subscribe to a premium plan on SlidesGo with your Djamo card


The platform offers two types of premium plans for individual subscriptions. One at 1.67 €, billed annually in a single payment of 19.99 €, and another billed monthly at 4.99 €. For the SlidesGo Teams plan, the annual business subscription is 89.95 € and 57.00 € for education centers and schools.


Subscribing to a premium SlidesGo plan is even simpler with your Djamo card.


1. On the homepage, in the top right, click the **pricing** option


1. Then choose the plan you want (annual or monthly).


1. Enter your personal info in the form, then fill in your Djamo card details for billing. If you have a virtual card, go to the **Cards** menu in the Djamo app and tap **see details** to get your virtual Djamo card info.


1. Then click **Start now** .


You’ll get an email confirming your subscription.


If you picked the monthly plan, we recommend setting a reminder on your phone to alert you near the end date of your subscription. That way, you can top up your card in time to avoid rejection fees.
