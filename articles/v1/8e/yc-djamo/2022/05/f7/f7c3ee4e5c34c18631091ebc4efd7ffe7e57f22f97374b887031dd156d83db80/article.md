# Host virtual meetings on Zoom with your Djamo card


With the pandemic, virtual meetings became more and more essential. They’re a great tool for businesses and event organizers.


Today, with the right software and gear, you can run online events or meet with colleagues from home, no matter the distance.


Zoom - alongside[Google Meet](https://apps.google.com/meet/) and[Microsoft Teams](https://www.microsoft.com/fr-fr/microsoft-teams) - is one of the leading video-conferencing platforms, used by thousands worldwide. It lets you interact with your colleagues virtually when in-person isn’t an option, and it’s also become hugely popular for social events.


Since May 2, 2022, meetings on the free Basic plan are capped at 40 minutes. For longer virtual meetings, you’ll need a paid plan.


## What do you get with a Zoom plan?


Zoom is a full-featured platform for webinars and online meetings. It runs on a subscription - pay monthly or annually with your Djamo card. Before adding your card (even for testing), make sure you have enough funds to avoid rejection fees.


Monthly billing comes in at 46 € a month. If you go annual, the license is 469 €, or 39 € a month.


With a Zoom plan and your Djamo card, you get unlimited virtual meetings with up to 300 participants and free scheduling displays for each of your meeting rooms. Inviting people to a meeting is simplified by Office 365, Google, and Exchange calendar integration. Plus, the meeting calendar shows up on the room screen and tablet (when there is one). You also get up to 1 GB of storage for meeting recordings. Just click on a meeting to launch it.


## How do you subscribe to Zoom with your Djamo card?


1. Start by creating a free account
2. Select the plan you want, then click **Continue** to finish


1.


Fill out the form with your info.


2.


Choose credit card as the payment method.


3.


Enter your Djamo card details. If you have a virtual card, go to the **Cards** section of the app, then tap **See details** to view your virtual card info. Always check you have enough funds before paying. Accept the **terms of service** to finish.


4.


You’ll see a confirmation message. Click **Place order** and you’re done.


Zoom will send you a purchase confirmation by email. With this plan you can run unlimited meetings with your team or host online conferences without hassle. Zoom has a strong online reputation with very few complaints. In a few years, it has become a market leader.
