# Manage your social media better with Hootsuite


Having a hard time centralizing the interactions and posts of your social media pages?


Go for a social media management tool to build a lasting relationship with your customers. Hootsuite, just like[Agorapulse](https://www.agorapulse.com/fr/) , is a platform that lets you manage all your social media in one place. This tool also helps you develop a presence strategy optimized for your industry and improve your company’s reputation.


## Discover Hootsuite’s features


Used by over 18 million people in more than 175 countries, Hootsuite lets you centralize your communication and saves you a huge amount of time. You can integrate over 35 social networks, including: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Google+, LinkedIn and a Twitter account. This integration ensures your presence on social media while building strong customer loyalty.


It also lets you analyze the impact of digital campaigns, monitor social media and run effective digital marketing actions. To access these features, Hootsuite offers several plans. A Free plan for a first dive into social media, and a premium plan for an ocean of options to help you manage your social media much more easily. The Free plan offers 2 social media accounts, 5 scheduled messages and 1 user/admin. **The premium plan offers much more.**


## How to subscribe to Hootsuite premium with your Djamo card


Hootsuite offers different prices based on the plan you pick. The price of a Hootsuite plan ranges from $49/month for the Professional plan to $739/month for the Business plan. You can also get a custom solution with the Enterprise plan. Every plan comes with a 30-day free trial.


After the trial, the use becomes paid and the matching amount will be charged on your Djamo card. So make sure you have enough funds before that date to avoid rejection fees. You can downgrade or cancel at any time before the end of your trial period so you’re not charged.


1. **First, choose your plan**


1. **Create your account by entering a username, an email address and choosing a password.**


1. **Choose card payment, enter your Djamo card details and click “Start my free trial.”**


1. **Then click Start my free plan**


You can now enjoy Hootsuite’s perks thanks to your Djamo card. You’ll be charged after the end of your trial period, 2 days before the exact end date of your subscription.
