# Create eye-catching resumes on CV.fr with the Djamo card


“What did you study? What did you achieve in your previous job? Why do you think this role is right for you?” Whether it’s your very first job or the promotion you’re after, having a complete resume that helps you stand out from other candidates is essential. CV.fr is a platform offering practical career tools built with experienced career-development specialists. It’s hugely popular and sits alongside sites like Cvdesignr.com, Canva, and makemycv.fr. In this article you’ll learn how to pay for a subscription with your Djamo card and land your dream job with a sharp, polished CV.


## CV.fr, for a more professional CV


CV.fr has already helped more than 1,000,000 users land jobs at top companies. The site offers more than 38 CV templates, so you can put together your resume easily without any graphic-design skills. You’ll also find cover letter templates to go with your CV. Just fill in your personal info and background in the form and start using the site. In just three steps, you get an original, personalized CV that stands out.


## How to create your CV on CV.fr and pay for a subscription with Djamo


CV.fr offers a 14-day trial for $0.99 and you need a subscription to use it. The subscription is $9.99 per month. After 14 days, that amount is billed monthly in advance for the upcoming month.


1. Start by tapping “Create a CV”. That takes you to the dashboard.


1. Fill in each section until you have the perfect CV. Take your time structuring it and add content to every section. That way recruiters can quickly spot each part of your resume - useful if they’re more interested in some sections than others.


1. On CV.fr, you can only download the CV you just created if you’ve paid with your card. To do that, first enter the email address where you want to receive your CV.


Then enter your Djamo card details and complete the payment. After your payment is received, the document is delivered straight to your inbox.


This $0.99 payment immediately starts your trial. After the two-week trial, your subscription renews automatically and you’re billed $9.99. Avoid rejection fees by making sure you have enough on your card before the renewal date. You can cancel the trial anytime.


With this subscription, CV.fr lets you edit your CV whenever you want - because you should always tailor your resume to the job you’re applying for. The site also offers cover letter writing and application tracking services. It even sends you alerts for available job offers matching your profile in your area.
