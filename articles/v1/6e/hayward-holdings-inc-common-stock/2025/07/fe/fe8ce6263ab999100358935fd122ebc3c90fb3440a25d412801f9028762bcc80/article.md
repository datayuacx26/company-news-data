#### All you want. Everything you need. One system checks every box.


##### Maintaining control of your pool shouldn’t be rocket science


Keeping up with your pool equipment manually can drain more than just energy — it wastes your time and drives up your utility bill.[OmniX](https://hayward.com/omnix) takes over through smart, automatic scheduling that keeps your pool running efficiently, so you save money and enjoy peace of mind.

##### Effortless Pool Automation


Whether you’re operating a pool, hot tub, or both, the[OmniX](https://hayward.com/omnix) system gives homeowners the ability to seamlessly automate your pool’s schedule, temperature, and[variable speed pump](https://hayward.com/products/pool-pumps.html?number_of_speeds=7244) operations.


Now, three Hayward variable-speed pumps—[TriStar VS](https://hayward.com/products/pool-pumps.html?series_pumps=9483) ,[Super Pump VS](https://hayward.com/products/pool-pumps.html?series_pumps=9475) , and[MaxFlo VS](https://hayward.com/products/pool-pumps.html?series_pumps=9458) —are available with the[OmniX module](https://hayward.com/omnix-module-400-600-800-vsp-vspomnix.html) pre-installed, making setup easy right out of the box. If you already own an XE Hayward pump, you can upgrade to smart control with a compatible[OmniX module kit](https://hayward.com/omnix-module-400-600-800-vsp-vspomnix.html) , giving you access to speed scheduling, water temperature monitoring, and automation without needing to replace your pump.


##### Smarter Control, Simplified


Say goodbye to confusing controls and lost remotes. The[OmniX app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hayward.omniflex&hl=en_US) offers fast Bluetooth® Wireless Technology


pairing and a wireless connection to your home network, giving you intuitive control directly from your smartphone. Both homeowners and pool professionals benefit from real-time access, scheduling, and system adjustments—right at their fingertips.


##### Take Control, Anywhere


The OmniX system connects via Bluetooth® Wireless Technology


for quick local setup, giving you immediate control from your smartphone while you're near your equipment. But for true smart pool automation, Bluetooth® Wireless Technology


alone isn’t enough—its short-range limits access to when you’re physically close.


That’s where the[OmniX Gateway](https://hayward.com/omnix-gateway-w3gatewayx1.html) comes in.


By connecting to your home Wi-Fi network, the[Gateway](https://hayward.com/omnix-gateway-w3gatewayx1.html) allows your system to communicate securely and reliably with the OmniX app, unlocking full remote control of your pool’s pump system. Whether you're traveling, at work, or just inside on the couch, the app gives you instant access to scheduling, performance monitoring, and system adjustments—anytime, from anywhere.


Built on a sub-GHz signal, the[Gateway](https://hayward.com/omnix-gateway-w3gatewayx1.html) ensures strong connectivity between your equipment and your network, providing dependable operation even over long distances. It’s not just for homeowners either—pool professionals can also benefit from remote visibility into the system for maintenance and support.
