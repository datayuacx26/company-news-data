##### How Does the Hayward Rewards Program Work?


Here’s how pool contractors and small pool companies can start earning:


**1. Make Eligible Purchases**


Buy qualifying Hayward equipment through approved distributors. Keep copies of all invoices — you’ll need them to verify purchases.


**2. Earn Points Automatically**


Every eligible purchase earns points. The more you spend annually, the more points you receive.


**3. Climb the Reward Tiers**


Hayward Rewards has 3 tiers that increase your point rewards:


Tier Annual Spend Reward Points


Bronze Up to $50,000 Base rate (1–1.5%)


Silver $50,000–$100,000 +20% points


Gold $100,000+ +20% over Silver


Even as a small pool business, you can plan purchases or coordinate orders to reach higher tiers faster.


**4. Redeem Points for Rewards**


Use points to get things that grow your business — or reward yourself personally.
