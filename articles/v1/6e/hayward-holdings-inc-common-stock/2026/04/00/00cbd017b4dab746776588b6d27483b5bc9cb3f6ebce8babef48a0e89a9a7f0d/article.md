# Why Pool Pros are Choosing the 4.0 HP TriStar Series Pumps from Hayward

April 16, 2026


**Why Pool Pros are Choosing the 4.0 HP TriStar Series Pumps from Hayward**


Choosing the best pump to switch in for an existing pool can be just as important as designing the right pump for a newly constructed pool. New technology emerges every year that raises the bar on pool equipment.


The[4.0 HP TriStar](https://www.hayward.com/tristar-vs-940-pump-4-0hp-vsp32940.html) and[TriStar XL VS](https://www.hayward.com/tristar-xl-vs-pump-4-0hp-hcp3040vsp.html) pumps have been designed as direct drop-in replacements, and as the pump of choice for multiple applications such as large, demanding pool builds, automated pools, feature-rich pools, and commercial pools.


Performance Curves for Tristar and Tristar XL


**The Drop-In Replacement with 4.0 HP Performance**


For existing pools, these pumps are designed with features the allow seamless replacement. The universal display provides these pumps with the ability to communicate with both Pentair and Jandy pool controllers for retrofit on automated pads.


The[4.0 HP TriStar VS Pumps](https://www.hayward.com/products/pool-pumps.html?total_hp=11143#maincontent) also include standard union connections to enable easy installation, maintenance and repair. A removable strainer housing on the TriStar XL creates further installation flexibility by allowing independent plumbing of the wet end and motor.


Whether the pool pad is automated or not, these pumps provide reliability and powerful performance to pools.


**Ultimate Control with Automation**


With new construction pools, these pumps deliver the perfect way to automate a pool with embedded technology. The 4.0 HP TriStar Series Pumps can connect to existing Omni automation or utilize the[OmniX](https://www.hayward.com/omnix) automation built into the pump.[OmniX](https://www.hayward.com/omnix) is a great option for pool pads that do not have automation or even commercial applications where you manage the equipment and want to connect via Bluetooth to check on the equipment. If a pad already has a Hayward, Pentair, or Jandy controller, these 4 HP pumps can be dropped in and communicate seamlessly.


Pair this with a new display and this pump is in a class of its own.


What’s unique about[OmniX](https://www.hayward.com/omnix) automation is that there is no need for a control panel on the wall. The automation is embedded in the pump so that it will connect right to the app. By adding on the Hayward[OmniX](https://www.hayward.com/omnix) Gateway, the pump can be controlled not just locally, but from anywhere. This is ideal for configuring equipment quickly and using integrated user management tools from the palm of your hand.


**Reliability You Can Trust**


Backed by Hayward engineering and performance testing, the[4.0HP TriStar Pumps](https://www.hayward.com/products/pool-pumps.html?total_hp=11143#maincontent) are designed to last and perform when you need them most. Each pump comes with a Hayward-backed warranty to give you peace of mind. The Tristar XL comes with a 4 year residential / 3 year commercial warranty and the Tristar has a 3 year warranty.


**American Innovation at its Finest**


These pumps are built better in Clemmons, North Carolina. The embedded technology components were designed and created in North Kingstown, Rhode Island. From Hayward’s earliest valves to today’s smart, reliable pool equipment, the mission remains the same: deliver top-quality products, delight customers, and create lasting memories around the water.


**The 4.0 HP Solution**


Whether you’re looking for a powerful drop-in replacement, or building a pool that requires elevated flow rates, the[4.0 HP TriStar Series](https://www.hayward.com/products/pool-pumps.html?total_hp=11143#maincontent) Pumps are up for the challenge. Automation or not, these pumps were designed with benefits that support the hard work of pool builders and servicers.
