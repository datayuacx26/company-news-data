**Why Gas Heaters with OmniX Stand Out**


**1. Fast, Reliable Heating Performance**


Hayward gas heaters are engineered for **rapid heat-up** , delivering warm, comfortable water in less time. This “speed-to-heat” capability makes it ideal for spontaneous pool use or cooler climates.


**2. Built for Durability**


Each unit features a **cupro-nickel heat exchanger** , offering superior protection against corrosion and chemical imbalance. This ensures longer lifespan and fewer maintenance issues.


**3. Smart Heating Control from Anywhere**


With OmniX, your heater becomes fully connected.


Using the mobile app, you can:


- Set and adjust pool temperature remotely


- Schedule heating times


- Monitor system performance


Add the optional


[gateway](https://www.hayward.com/omnixtm-gateway-open-line-w3gatewayx1.html) and you can control your pool from virtually anywhere—not just within Bluetooth range.


**4. Advanced Diagnostics & Automation**


OmniX doesn’t just control—it also coordinates.


- **Enhanced diagnostics** help identify issues quickly


- **Programmable settings** optimize performance


- **Integrated automation** coordinates with pumps and other equipment


This level of intelligence simplifies maintenance and improves long-term efficiency.


**5. Easy Installation & Service-Friendly Design**


Hayward designed these heaters with both homeowners and installers in mind:


- **Drop-in replacement compatibility** with existing systems


- **Flexible electrical connections** for simplified setup


These features reduce installation time and service costs.


**6. Energy Efficiency & Low Emissions**


Gas heaters with OmniX are built for performance *and* sustainability:


- **~82% efficiency rating** for reduced fuel usage


- **Low NOx emissions** that meet environmental standards


- **Optimized water flow** to reduce pump runtime and energy use


The result? Lower operating costs and a smaller environmental footprint.
