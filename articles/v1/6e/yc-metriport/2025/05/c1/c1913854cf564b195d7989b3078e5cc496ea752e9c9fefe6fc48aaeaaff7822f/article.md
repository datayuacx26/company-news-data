# Introducing: Condition Chronicity Flagging

As part of Day 2 of[Launch Week V2](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/metriport_launchweek-activity-7332788684229881856-og5s?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAABpB8LkBV05GNq6S2QpYC-4CtRcuiTaJuR4) at Metriport, we’re introducing **Condition Chronicity Flagging** 🗂


### **TL;DR:**


Available directly within Metriport’s UI or via API, providers and data teams can now instantly differentiate between chronic and non-chronic medical conditions.


See docs:[https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/fhir/extensions/chronicity](https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/fhir/extensions/chronicity)


### **How Does It Work?**


Under the hood, Metriport leverages the Chronic Condition Indicator Refined ([CCIR](https://hcup-us.ahrq.gov/toolssoftware/chronic_icd10/chronic_icd10.jsp) ) data set, developed as part of the Healthcare Cost & Utilization Project ([HCUP](https://www.ahrq.gov/data/hcup/index.html) ) by the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality ([AHRQ](https://hcup-us.ahrq.gov/) ).


This tool classifies conditions as chronic, non-chronic, or unspecified based on the ICD-10-CM codes.


### **Why It Matters**


- Providers can quickly identify their patients' chronic conditions for improved treatment at the point of care.
- Simplified patient medical record summaries ensures only the most relevant information is present.
- Improved data quality means improved capabilities for risk adjustment, quality measures, and more.
