# Introducing: Realtime Patient Notifications

Today, we're announcing **Realtime Patient Notifications** 🛜


### TL;DR:


Actively monitor your patients in real-time across health systems. Metriport’s Realtime Patient Notifications listen for admissions, discharges, and transfers (ADTs) across the US.


See docs:[https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/handling-data/realtime-patient-notifications](https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/handling-data/realtime-patient-notifications)


### How Does It Work?


Metriport integrates with state and regional-level HIEs to enable comprehensive monitoring across diverse patient populations in the United States.


Available via API, Dashboard, or EHR apps, notifications are delivered via webhook to maintain the real-time state of the patient journey throughout health systems.


### Use Cases


**Transitional Care Management (TCM):**


- Metriport automatically refreshes clinical data upon receiving patient alerts, retrieving discharge summaries and other relevant documents instantaneously.
- Trigger billable TCM services (CPT 99495/99496) and ensure follow-up within required 7-14 day windows by knowing the moment your patients are discharged.


**30-day readmission prevention** :


- Get alerted when patients with high readmission risk are discharged to trigger immediate outreach.
- Improve HEDIS measures and quality scores through real-time member identification.
