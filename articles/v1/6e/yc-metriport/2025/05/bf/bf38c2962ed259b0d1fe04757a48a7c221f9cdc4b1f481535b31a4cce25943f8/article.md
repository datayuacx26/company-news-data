# Introducing: Global Search

Metriport now supports a single, unified **Global Search** across the entirety of a patient’s medical history 🔎


Through a simple interactive interface, providers can now surface the most pertinent patient information they need, when they need it.


### **TL;DR:**


This new feature is available via Metriport’s[Dashboard](https://www.metriport.com/dashboard) ,[API](https://www.metriport.com/medical) , and[EHR apps](https://docs.metriport.com/ehr-apps/overview) , allowing providers to search through all resources within the Medical Record Summary, in seconds.


See docs:[https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/api-reference/fhir/consolidated-data-search](https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/api-reference/fhir/consolidated-data-search)


### **How Does It Work?**


Metriport indexes various parts of the patient's medical history in real-time, creating a comprehensive, searchable view that understands medical context and terminology.


Our intelligent search engine processes structured data (lab results, medications, vital signs, conditions, and more) alongside unstructured clinical notes, discharge summaries, and imaging reports.


### **Examples**


**Accelerated chart review:**


- Instantly find all mentions of a specific condition, medication, or procedure across years of medical history. For example, searching “Diabetes” can surface more than just relevant conditions—family member history, labs, and other relevant details get surfaced as well.


**Urgent care:**


- Quickly surface critical information like allergies or medications at the point-of-care in urgent care settings.


**Compliance & documentation:**


- Efficiently gather data for quality reporting, care gap analysis, and various regulatory requirements.
