# Introducing: the Metriport Clinician Interface

For the final day of our recent Launch Week, we're excited to introduce the new Metriport Clinician Interface 🎯
‍


Based entirely on direct feedback from 100s of providers, we've spent the last few months redesigning what an interactive patient medical record summary should look like—giving teams instant access to the exact patient information they need, when they need it.


Available now via:


- The[Metriport Dashboard](https://www.metriport.com/dashboard)


- Metriport's growing number of[EHR Apps](https://docs.metriport.com/ehr-apps/overview) ([athena](https://www.metriport.com/athenahealth-integration) ,[Elation](https://www.metriport.com/elation-app) ,[Canvas](https://www.metriport.com/canvasmedical-app) )


Want to try our Clinician Interface today? Get started with our Sandbox environment for free here:[dash.metriport.com](http://dash.metriport.com/)


[‍](http://dash.metriport.com/) Read this guide, and create a patient named Jane Smith to see it in action:[https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/getting-started/quickstart](https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/getting-started/quickstart)


[‍](https://docs.metriport.com/medical-api/getting-started/quickstart) The best part about this interface is that it's customizable to your use case and specialty. When you work with Metriport, you get more than a standalone product, but rather one that you can configure to your organization's specific needs 💪


While that concludes the final day of our Launch Week, we have plenty more in store for you soon, so follow us at[Metriport](https://www.linkedin.com/company/metriport/) for continued product innovation (we ship fast!) 😉
