# Introducing: the Metriport Network Directory Browser

For Day 4 of our Launch Week, we're proud to introduce our latest feature: the Metriport **Network Directory Browser** ! 📜


This new tool enables our customers to easily search through Metriport's national network of 100,000+ providers, to see who is connected for comprehensive clinical data exchange.


Teams no longer need to wait to see what hospitals, clinics, specialists, and provider orgs are in-network and interoperable—they can now answer these questions themselves on the Metriport[Dashboard](https://www.metriport.com/dashboard) , within seconds.
