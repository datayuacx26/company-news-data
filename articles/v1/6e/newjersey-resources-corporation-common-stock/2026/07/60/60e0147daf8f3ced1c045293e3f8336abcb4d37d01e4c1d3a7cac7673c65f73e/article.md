# NEW JERSEY NATURAL GAS INVITES MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY TO EXPLORE THE WONDERS OF THE JERSEY SHORE AT ITS 22nd ANNUAL OCEAN FUN DAYS


05/08/2025 - For Immediate Release


**WALL, N.J.** – New Jersey Natural Gas (NJNG) invites customers, residents, families and members of the community to explore the wonders of the Jersey Shore during its 22nd annual Ocean Fun Days. The two-day event will be held on May 17, 2025, at Island Beach State Park in Seaside Park and May 18, 2025, at the New Jersey Sea Grant Consortium in Sandy Hook from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., rain or shine. Admission and parking are free.


Ocean Fun Days helps raise awareness about New Jersey’s coastal ecosystem as well as the importance of protecting it.


The event is free and filled with interactive activities for the entire family, including:


·


Fiddler Crab Races


·


Face Painting


·


Seining


·


Paddle board demonstrations


·


Historical site tours


·


And more!


Plus, attendees can learn from scientists, researchers and environmentalists about marine life and their habitats.


“Ocean Fun Days is the perfect way to kick off summer and celebrate the unique coastal environment many of us call home,” said Carolyn Cannon, director of Community Relations at New Jersey Natural Gas. "Through the immersive and fun-filled activities planned, people of all ages can connect with nature, learn about vital ecosystems and discover ways to be more sustainable.”


NJNG is pleased to hold this annual event alongside state and local agencies and organizations, including New Jersey Sea Grant Consortium, Gateway National Park/National Park Service, New Jersey Division of Parks and Forestry/Island Beach State Park, the New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.


Ocean Fun Days aligns with NJNG’s commitment to sustainability and the work of its Coastal Climate Initiative (CCI). Launched in 2021, CCI is an umbrella program for the company’s commitment to environmental and natural resource stewardship efforts. In 2023, the company pledged up to $2 million over five years from its charitable foundations to support environmental stewardship initiatives throughout its service territory.


For more details about the event, visit[oceanfundays.org.](https://oceanfundays.org/)


To learn more about NJNG’s sustainability efforts and Coastal Climate Initiative, visit[njng.com/climate.](https://www.njresources.com/community/coastal-climate-initiative/)


**About New Jersey Resources**


- **New Jersey Resources (NYSE: NJR** ) is a Fortune 1000 company that, through its subsidiaries, provides safe and reliable natural gas and clean energy services, including transportation, distribution, asset management and home services. NJR is composed of five primary businesses:
- **New Jersey Natural Gas,** NJR’s principal subsidiary, operates and maintains natural gas transportation and distribution infrastructure to serve customers in New Jersey’s Monmouth, Ocean, Morris, Middlesex, Sussex and Burlington counties.


- **Clean Energy Ventures** invests in, owns and operates solar projects, providing customers with low-carbon solutions.


- **Energy Services** manages a diversified portfolio of natural gas transportation and storage assets and provides physical natural gas services and customized energy solutions to its customers across North America.


- **Storage and Transportation** serves customers from local distributors and producers to electric generators and wholesale marketers through its ownership of Leaf River and the Adelphia Gateway Pipeline, as well as our 50% equity ownership in the Steckman Ridge natural gas storage facility.


- **Home Services** provides service contracts as well as heating, central air conditioning, water heaters, standby generators, solar and other indoor and outdoor comfort products to residential homes throughout New Jersey.


NJR and its over 1,300 employees are committed to helping customers save energy and money by promoting conservation and encouraging efficiency through Conserve to Preserve® and initiatives such as SAVEGREEN®.
