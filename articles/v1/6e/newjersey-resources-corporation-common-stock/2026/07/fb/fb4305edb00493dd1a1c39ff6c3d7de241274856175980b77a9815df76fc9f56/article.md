# NEW JERSEY RESOURCES HELPS MAKE HOLIDAY WISHES COME TRUE FOR LOCAL FAMILIES IN NEED


12/22/2022 - For Immediate Release


**WEST LONG BRANCH, NJ** – In the spirit of the season, New Jersey Resources (NYSE: NJR) joined with the Vin Gopal Civic Association to help make the holidays special for deserving families in Monmouth County. NJR volunteers, including President and CEO Steve Westhoven and members of the company’s leadership team, were on hand at the Ocean First Center at Monmouth University to spread holiday cheer and distribute toys, gifts and presents to those in need. In total, the event benefitted more than 570 families.


“The joy of giving is what makes the holidays special,” said Steve Westhoven, President and CEO of New Jersey Resources. “Each year, the Vin Gopal Civic Association does a tremendous job with its Christmas Toy Drive, and New Jersey Resources is pleased to support this worthy cause. Helping holiday wishes come true for some deserving families is what the spirt of the season is all about.”


“I want to thank New Jersey Resources for its sponsorship, and its leadership team who came out in force with more than a dozen volunteers to make this Christmas Toy Drive a huge success,” said Vin Gopal, President of the Vin Gopal Civic Association. “This year we were able to spread holiday cheer to an incredible number of families in need and help make Christmas a little brighter for Monmouth communities.”


Each year, NJR and its employees dedicate thousands of hours volunteering to help support local non-profit and community organizations throughout its service territory, including the Vin Gopal Civic Association, a 501(c)3 nonprofit founded by New Jersey State Senator Vin Gopal dedicated to uplifting communities in Central Monmouth County primarily through senior outreach, youth programs, and support for other community, civic and religious organizations.


In addition to the toy drive, this holiday season NJR’s employee volunteers have helped support the Warmest Wishes Coat Drive, collecting more than 1,000 coats for distribution at local schools. They also packed over 350 holiday meals, 1,000 basic needs bags and assembled emergency food-boxes for local families in need.


Over the past year alone, NJR volunteers provided more than 2,000 hours of service and helped make a difference for over 1,800 groups.


**About New Jersey Resources**
New Jersey Resources (NYSE: NJR) is a Fortune 1000 company that, through its subsidiaries, provides safe and reliable natural gas and clean energy services, including transportation, distribution, asset management and home services. NJR is composed of five primary businesses:


- New Jersey Natural Gas, NJR’s principal subsidiary, operates and maintains over 7,700 miles of natural gas transportation and distribution infrastructure to serve over 569,000 customers in New Jersey’s Monmouth, Ocean and parts of Morris, Middlesex and Burlington counties.
- NJR Clean Energy Ventures invests in, owns and operates solar projects with a total capacity of more than 386 megawatts, providing residential and commercial customers with low-carbon solutions.
- NJR Energy Services manages a diversified portfolio of natural gas transportation and storage assets and provides physical natural gas services and customized energy solutions to its customers across North America.
- Storage & Transportation serves customers from local distributors and producers to electric generators and wholesale marketers through its ownership of Leaf River and the Adelphia Gateway Pipeline Project, as well as our 50% equity ownership in the Steckman Ridge natural gas storage facility.
- NJR Home Services provides service contracts as well as heating, central air conditioning, water heaters, standby generators, solar and other indoor and outdoor comfort products to residential homes throughout New Jersey.
NJR and its nearly 1,300 employees are committed to helping customers save energy and money by promoting conservation and encouraging efficiency through Conserve to Preserve® and initiatives such as The SAVEGREEN Project® and The Sunlight Advantage®.
