# NEW JERSEY RESOURCES NAMED ONE OF AMERICA’S BEST COMPANIES FOR 2026 BY TIME MAGAZINE


07/09/2026 - For Immediate Release


**WALL, NJ** – New Jersey Resources (NYSE: NJR), a leading diversified energy infrastructure and energy services company, was named one of America’s Best Companies 2026 by TIME Magazine.


“It is an honor to be named one of America’s Best Companies by TIME,” said Steve Westhoven, president and chief executive officer of New Jersey Resources. “This recognition is a testament to the talent of our team, the culture we’ve built and the performance we consistently deliver. More importantly, it reflects the work we do to safely, reliably and sustainably meet the energy needs of our customers and strengthen the communities we serve.”


The TIME Magazine America’s Best Companies 2026 list identifies top-performing U.S. businesses driving innovation, accountability and long-term success. Companies are ranked based on a detailed analysis of key performance indicators in three areas: employee satisfaction, financial performance and sustainability transparency. The final list recognizes the 1,000 best companies and underscores NJR’s commitment to advancing sustainable business practices and strong performance supported by an engaged and high-performing workforce.


NJR and its portfolio of complementary businesses continue to provide forward-looking and sustainable energy solutions for New Jersey and beyond that drive greenhouse gas reduction and operational innovations, including advanced mobile leak detection and distributed carbon-capture technologies. NJR and its employees are also strong stewards of environmental and community sustainability, dedicating thousands of hours of volunteerism each year. To learn more about NJR’s performance and commitment to sustainability, please visit njrsustainability.com.


America’s Best Companies 2026 is a project of Time Magazine in partnership with Statista. For more information on the rankings and methodology, please visit TIME.com.


**About New Jersey Resources**


New Jersey Resources (NYSE: NJR) is a diversified energy infrastructure and energy services company headquartered in Wall, New Jersey. NJR is composed of five primary businesses:


- **New Jersey Natural Gas** , NJR’s principal subsidiary, operates and maintains natural gas transportation and distribution infrastructure to serve customers in New Jersey’s Monmouth, Ocean, Morris, Middlesex, Sussex and Burlington counties.


- **Clean Energy Ventures** invests in, owns and operates solar projects, providing customers with low-carbon solutions.


- **Energy Services** manages a diversified portfolio of natural gas transportation and storage assets and provides physical natural gas services and customized energy solutions to its customers across North America.


- **Storage and Transportation** serves customers from local distributors and producers to electric generators and wholesale marketers through its ownership of Leaf River and the Adelphia Gateway pipeline, as well as our 50% equity ownership in the Steckman Ridge natural gas storage facility.


- **Home Services** provides service contracts as well as heating, central air conditioning, water heaters, standby generators and other indoor and outdoor comfort products to residential homes throughout New Jersey.


NJR and its more than 1,300 employees are committed to helping customers save energy and money by promoting conservation and encouraging efficiency through Conserve to Preserve® and initiatives such as SAVEGREEN®.
