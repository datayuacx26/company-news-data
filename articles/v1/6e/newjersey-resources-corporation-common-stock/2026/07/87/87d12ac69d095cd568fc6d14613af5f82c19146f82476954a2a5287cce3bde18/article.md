# NEW JERSEY NATURAL GAS HOLDS ITS ANNUAL ENERGY ASSISTANCE DAYS


08/26/2025 - For Immediate Release


**WALL, N.J.** – This September and October, New Jersey Natural Gas (NJNG) will hold its annual Energy Assistance Days throughout Monmouth, Ocean and Morris Counties. These in-person events provide hands-on assistance to help customers determine their eligibility and complete applications for free grants to help pay their energy bills. Customers behind on their NJNG bills may also qualify for payment arrangements to help bring their accounts current. These programs are also a good opportunity for customers to get a head start on managing their natural gas bills before the start of the heating season.


.


“Energy Assistance Days are designed to meet customers where they are. By attending an event, customers can meet in person with our team to explore programs that best meet their individual household needs,” said Maria Delaplain, manager of Customer Outreach at New Jersey Natural Gas. “There are a wide range of assistance programs available for households of various income levels. For example, a family of four with a household income of $153,514 may qualify. Also, customers may be eligible for more than one program.”


.


Bilingual representatives from NJNG’s energy assistance team as well as local community action agencies will be on hand to help guide customers through available grants and the application process for different programs including:


- Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)*†
- Universal Service Fund (USF)*†
- USF - The Fresh Start Program*†
- New Jersey Board of Public Utilities Payment Assistance for Gas and Electric (NJBPU PAGE)*†
- NJ SHARES Energy Assistance Grant*†
- New Jersey SHARES Smart Utility*†
- New Jersey Lifeline Credit Program*†
- Comfort Partners
- NJNG’s Gift of Warmth*


**To apply for assistance, customers must bring:**


- Social security cards for all household members
- Households where no member has a social security card may apply for USF*† ONLY
- Government-issued ID
- Proof of residence — mortgage/tax bill or lease from landlord
- Recent natural gas and electricity bills
- Proof of income for all household members over the age of 18 including, but not limited to, employment/pay stubs, unemployment, SSI, pension, self-employment federal tax income, rental income and TANF


Income-eligible customers may also qualify for no-cost** weatherization programs from New Jersey Comfort Partners*† or NJNG* to help them save energy and lower their energy bills.


.


For more information, visit[njng.com/energyassistance](https://www.njng.com/my-account/payment-options/payment-assistance.aspx) or contact 800-221-0051 and say “energy assistance” at the prompt.


.


*Terms and conditions apply.


**Terms and conditions apply. You must be a NJNG residential customer whose primary residence is a single- or in a multifamily dwelling with up to three units. You must own your home for a minimum of one year. Homes 0-5 years old or under a builder’s warranty are excluded. Both renters and homeowners must meet the income guidelines. If you’re a renter, you must obtain consent from your landlord. This offer is subject to change, including termination, without prior notice. Visit[savegreen.com/residential/income-qualified programs/home-weatherization](https://www.savegreen.com/residential/income-qualified-programs/home-weatherization.aspx) for more information.

**About New Jersey Resources**


New Jersey Resources (NYSE: NJR) is a Fortune 1000 company that, through its subsidiaries, provides safe and reliable natural gas and clean energy services, including transportation, distribution, asset management and home services. NJR is composed of five primary businesses:


- **New Jersey Natural Gas** , NJR’s principal subsidiary, operates and maintains natural gas transportation and distribution infrastructure to serve customers in New Jersey’s Monmouth, Ocean, Morris, Middlesex, Sussex and Burlington counties.


- **Clean Energy Ventures** invests in, owns and operates solar projects, providing customers with low-carbon solutions.


- **Energy Services** manages a diversified portfolio of natural gas transportation and storage assets and provides physical natural gas services and customized energy solutions to its customers across North America.


- **Storage and Transportation** serves customers from local distributors and producers to electric generators and wholesale marketers through its ownership of Leaf River and the Adelphia Gateway Pipeline, as well as our 50% equity ownership in the Steckman Ridge natural gas storage facility.


- **Home Services** provides service contracts as well as heating, central air conditioning, water heaters, standby generators, solar and other indoor and outdoor comfort products to residential homes throughout New Jersey.


NJR and its over 1,300 employees are committed to helping customers save energy and money by promoting conservation and encouraging efficiency through Conserve to Preserve® and initiatives such as SAVEGREEN®.
