# NEW JERSEY RESOURCES CELEBRATES THE HOLIDAY SEASON BY GIVING BACK TO LOCAL FAMILIES IN NEED ALONGSIDE VIN GOPAL CIVIC ASSOCIATION


12/20/2024 - For Immediate Release


**December 20, 2024** – New Jersey Resources (NYSE: NJR) and the Vin Gopal Civic Association came together to celebrate the spirit of the season by helping make holiday wishes come true for deserving families in Monmouth County. NJR presented the Vin Gopal Civic Association with a $15,000 charitable donation, supporting the event and the Association’s year-round service efforts.


.


NJR volunteers, including President and CEO Steve Westhoven, joined with New Jersey State Senator Vin Gopal and community members to distribute toys to local families in need. This year’s event, hosted at the Ocean First Center at Monmouth University, marked the third consecutive year of NJR’s participation in the Association’s toy drive.


.


“The Vin Gopal Civic Association does an outstanding job supporting Monmouth County families with its holiday toy drive, and it’s great to be a part of such an amazing cause,” said Steve Westhoven, president and CEO of New Jersey Resources. “Making holiday wishes a reality for so many deserving families in our service territory is rewarding and embodies the true spirit of the season.”


.


Each year, NJR and its employees dedicate thousands of hours volunteering to help support local nonprofit and community organizations throughout its service territory, including the Vin Gopal Civic Association. “Thank you to New Jersey Resources for the continued support and sponsorship that is critical to make this holiday toy drive a success,” said State Senator Vin Gopal, president of the Vin Gopal Civic Association. “Their efforts helped make the season brighter for nearly 700 deserving children.”


.


NJR employees have been volunteering and spreading holiday cheer all season long, from collecting more than 180 coats through the United Way of Monmouth County’s Warmest Wishes Coat to supporting Move for Hunger by packing 1,600 holiday meals for local food banks.


.


This year alone, NJR volunteers provided more than 4,000 hours of service and helped make a difference for over 2,000 organizations.


.


**About the Vin Gopal Civic Association**


The Vin Gopal Civic Association is a 501(c)3 non-profit organization. We recognize that in our Central Jersey Communities there are many groups who are affected by our unique high cost of living and struggle to maintain their economic independence.


It is the goal of our Association to help those who are the backbone of our communities to maintain a fruitful, independent life by lending a helping hand wherever we can and also by fostering a relationship with other Civic, Community and Religious groups with similar goals in helping our communities.


.


**About New Jersey Resources**


**New Jersey Resources** (NYSE: NJR) is a Fortune 1000 company that, through its subsidiaries, provides safe and reliable natural gas and clean energy services, including transportation, distribution, asset management and home services. NJR is composed of five primary businesses:


- **New Jersey Natural Gas** , NJR’s principal subsidiary, operates and maintains natural gas transportation and distribution infrastructure to serve customers in New Jersey’s Monmouth, Ocean, Morris, Middlesex, Sussex and Burlington counties.


- **Clean Energy Ventures** invests in, owns and operates solar projects, providing customers with low-carbon solutions.


- **Energy Services** manages a diversified portfolio of natural gas transportation and storage assets and provides physical natural gas services and customized energy solutions to its customers across North America.


- **Storage and Transportation** serves customers from local distributors and producers to electric generators and wholesale marketers through its ownership of Leaf River and the Adelphia Gateway Pipeline, as well as our 50% equity ownership in the Steckman Ridge natural gas storage facility.


- **Home Services** provides service contracts as well as heating, central air conditioning, water heaters, standby generators, solar and other indoor and outdoor comfort products to residential homes throughout New Jersey.


NJR and its over 1,300 employees are committed to helping customers save energy and money by promoting conservation and encouraging efficiency through Conserve to Preserve® and initiatives such as SAVEGREEN®.
