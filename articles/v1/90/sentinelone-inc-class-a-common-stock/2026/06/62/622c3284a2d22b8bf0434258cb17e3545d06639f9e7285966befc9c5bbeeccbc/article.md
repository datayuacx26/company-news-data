# SentinelOne Honours Top Partners Driving AI Security Innovation in Asia Pacific and Japan

**BANGKOK, THAILAND – June 8, 2026 –**[SentinelOne](https://www.sentinelone.com/) ® (NYSE: S), the AI Security leader, today announced the winners of the 2026 Asia Pacific and Japan (APJ) PartnerOne Awards, recognizing outstanding partner contributions to AI and security innovation in the region. The award winners, which include the top MSSPs, MDR, DFIR, VARS, Distributors in the region were honoured during the company’s APJ PartnerOne Summit taking place in Bangkok, Thailand.


“ Our APJ partners are driving the future of AI-native security across one of the world’s most dynamic and diverse technology landscapes. From Tokyo to Sydney, Singapore to Mumbai, these partners bring local expertise and global vision to every customer engagement


,” said Kris Day, Senior Vice President, Asia Pacific and Japan at SentinelOne. “ The APJ PartnerOne Summit is more than a recognition event – it’s a celebration of the partnerships that are reshaping cybersecurity in the region. Together, we’re not just responding to the threat landscape — we’re shaping the future of it.


”


SentinelOne’s APJ partner network spans some of the world’s fastest-growing economies and most sophisticated cybersecurity markets. Backed by the company’s pioneering AI-driven technology, the network empowers organizations across the region to navigate an increasingly volatile digital environment with confidence. This year’s honors reflect a shared dedication to technical excellence, customer success, and a proven track record of delivering superior protection and ROI for organizations of every size and sector.


**The 2026 APJ PartnerOne Award Winners Include:**


- 2026 APJ Partner of the Year – Shield One Inc.
- 2026 APJ IR Partner of the Year – CoreCloud Technologies
- 2026 APJ MSSP Partner of the Year – PAGO Networks
- 2026 APJ Emerging Partner of the Year (Rising Star) – AUCYBER
- 2026 APJ Distribution Partner of the Year – iValue InfoSolutions Limited
- 2026 APJ Platform Partner of the Year – Technociate Solutions Pvt Ltd
- 2026 APJ Top Sales Person of the Year – DongWon Lee of ShieldOne
- 2026 APJ Paladin of the Year – Jesse Pearce, McGrathNicol
- 2026 APJ Strategic Deal of the Year – Flipkart, Valuepoint Techsol Pvt Ltd
- 2026 APJ Cloud Partner of the Year – AWS
- 2026 ASEAN Partner of the Year – PT Permata Anugerah Abadi
- 2026 Korea Partner of the Year – ESCARE Co., Ltd.
- 2026 Japan Partner of the Year – Marubeni IT Solutions Inc.
- 2026 SAARC Partner of the Year – NetNXT Network Pvt. Ltd.
- 2026 ANZ Partner of the Year – Saltt Technologies Pty Ltd
