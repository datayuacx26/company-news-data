# GATX Corporation to Present at 2025 Goldman Sachs Industrials & Materials Conference

### GATX Corporation to Present at 2025 Goldman Sachs Industrials & Materials Conference


November 25, 2025


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s28.q4cdn.com/292281939/files/doc_news/GATX-Corporation-to-Present-at-2025-Goldman-Sachs-Industrials--Materials-Conference-2025.pdf)


CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Robert C. Lyons, president and chief executive officer, GATX Corporation (NYSE: GATX), will present at the 2025 Goldman Sachs Industrials & Materials Conference on Thursday, Dec. 4, 2025.


GATX’s presentation will begin at 10:10 a.m. ET. To listen to a live webcast of the event, please access the appropriate link at[www.gatx.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.gatx.com&esheet=54363666&newsitemid=20251125359896&lan=en-US&anchor=www.gatx.com&index=1&md5=c5c8d3c756bc58db2429dd37f8e417b2) at least 15 minutes prior to the start time. The webcast will be archived for 90 days.


**COMPANY DESCRIPTION**


At GATX Corporation (NYSE: GATX), we empower our customers to propel the world forward. GATX leases transportation assets including railcars, aircraft spare engines and tank containers to customers worldwide. Our mission is to provide innovative, unparalleled service that enables our customers to transport what matters safely and sustainably while championing the well-being of our employees and communities. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois since its founding in 1898, GATX has paid a quarterly dividend, uninterrupted, since 1919.
