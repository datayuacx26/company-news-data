# Careers

## Let's build the future of clean energy.


## Join the team solving the wasted renewable energy problem.


We are a diverse, driven team of entrepreneurs, energy experts, private equity leaders, and engineers. We are passionate about making the world greener and we have conviction about how we will bring change on a global scale.


We are equally zealous about championing our teams, relentless about developing our people, and believe strongly that Soluna’s goals and commitment to our mission are strengthened by our employee’s voices.


## Our Values


### Team Oriented


We bring out the best in each other through encouragement, trust, and collaboration. We hold ourselves and our peers accountable to build an enduring, resilient organization. We approach every relationship based on alignment of interests.


### Pioneering


We are thought leaders in our space, making bold moves to advance not just our business but the entire industry.


### Data-Driven


We apply analytical rigor to all of our decisions and quantify our success with metrics. As a data-driven team, we discuss ideas with open minds and integrity, then commit to trusting in our informed decisions together. We pursue investments that make financial sense for the company’s long-term value.


### Transparent


We operate with uncompromising internal and external communications. We pride ourselves on acknowledging vulnerability, providing complete visibility into our wins, missteps, and adaptations for future learnings.
