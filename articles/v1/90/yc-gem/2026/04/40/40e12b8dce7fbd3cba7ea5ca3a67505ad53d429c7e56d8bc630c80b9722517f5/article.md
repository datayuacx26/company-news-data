## **What is Gem's AI Fraud Detection Agent?**


Gem's AI Fraud Detection Agent is the third AI agent joining Gem's AI suite — and it's tackling a problem that's been quietly undermining recruiting teams everywhere. It automatically evaluates every inbound application for fraud risk — before a recruiter has spent a single minute on it.


**Here's how it works:** As applications come into your pipeline, the agent automatically evaluates them across multiple signals — resume metadata, email, phone, LinkedIn, person verification, and IP/device data. It evaluates all of them together (not in isolation) and assigns a clear risk level to every application: high, medium, or low. Every flag comes with a natural-language explanation of what triggered it, so your recruiters aren't left guessing.


**The whole thing happens automatically, inside Gem’s Application Review, right where your team already works.** No new tools to learn. No extra steps for candidates. No copy-pasting between tabs.


Recruiters control where and when it runs. Turn it on automatically for high-risk roles so every application gets screened from the start — or run checks on demand for specific applications when something catches your eye mid-review. Either way, it fits how your team actually works.
