# Zava delivers patient-centric online healthcare with managed ETL

Zava, a UK-based online medical services provider, needed to find a new ETL solution after Alooma discontinued AWS support. Within three months, Zava migrated to Etleap, which offered advanced data transformations and pipeline automation. Using Etleap’s browser-based Data Wrangler, Zava seamlessly handles semi-structured data, enabling faster and more accurate patient surveys and diagnoses. The secure, AWS-native platform ensures patient data remains within Zava’s network. With responsive support and smooth pipeline migration, Zava now uses Etleap to deliver actionable insights, helping patients receive healthcare faster and more affordably.
