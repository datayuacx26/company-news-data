# How Etleap Streamlined Data Integration for Moderna with Seamless Automation

Moderna, a leading biotech firm, faced rapid data growth and the need to support its scientists with secure, scalable analytics. With nearly 100 internal data sources and strict regulatory requirements, the company turned to AWS Redshift and Etleap for its modern data architecture. Etleap's AWS-native design and auto-scaling EMR clusters simplified pipeline creation and maintenance, enabling Moderna’s lean data engineering team to add multiple data sources weekly. This secure, efficient ETL environment empowers both technical and non-technical users to focus on advancing mRNA science and medicine.
