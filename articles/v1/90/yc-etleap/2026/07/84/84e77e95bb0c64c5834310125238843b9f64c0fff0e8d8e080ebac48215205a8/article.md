# Sustainable Clothing Rack meets Modern Data Stack: ThredUp’s ETL Revamp

ThredUp, a leading online resale platform, modernized its data infrastructure by partnering with Etleap during its migration to the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform. This collaboration enabled ThredUp to save $100K annually in engineering costs, optimize ad spend on platforms like Google and Meta, and power AI-driven customer experiences such as predictive search. Etleap’s robust pipelines provide accurate, timely data for ThredUp’s Customer Data Platform (CDP) and Resale-as-a-Service (RaaS), helping drive customer engagement and profitability while allowing the data team to focus on value-added projects.
