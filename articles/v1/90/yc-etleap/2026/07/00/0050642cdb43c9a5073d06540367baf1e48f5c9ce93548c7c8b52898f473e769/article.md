# Transforming Financial Data Integration at eMoney

eMoney Advisor, a fintech company serving over 70,000 financial professionals, sought to unify its data and improve customer experiences. By leveraging Etleap for ETL and Looker for business intelligence, all within an AWS Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), eMoney achieved rapid results: six weeks from kickoff to production and full enterprise-wide data adoption within a year. This cloud-based solution improved visibility into the customer journey, boosted internal efficiency, and empowered teams to make data-driven decisions while ensuring strict security and data governance.
