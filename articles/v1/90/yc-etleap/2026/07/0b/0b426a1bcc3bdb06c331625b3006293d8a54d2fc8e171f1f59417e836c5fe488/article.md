# Empark Fuels Sustainable Parking with Etleap Pipelines

Empark, Iberia’s leading mobility provider, modernized its data infrastructure with Etleap to support its growing eco-conscious consumer base. By processing over 1 million rows of data daily, Empark achieved a 400% increase in ETL efficiency, allowing one engineer to handle the work of five. Etleap’s streamlined Change Data Capture (CDC) and SQL modeling tools help unify data from car parks, EV charging stations, and control systems, enabling real-time insights and frictionless customer experiences through their mobile app. With low-latency pipelines and self-service diagnostic tools, Empark is now poised to lead sustainable urban mobility with smarter, data-driven solutions.
