# Etleap Launches 15-Second Ingestion & Modeling Pipeline on Snowflake AI Data Cloud

**SAN FRANCISCO, June 3, 2025 —** Etleap today announced, at Snowflake’s annual user conference,[Snowflake Summit 2025](https://www.snowflake.com/en/summit/) , the launch of its **15‑second ingestion & modeling pipeline** on the[Snowflake AI Data Cloud](https://www.snowflake.com/en/) . This new integration, Powered by Snowflake, will enable organizations to drive near‑real‑time analytics by shrinking the time from data creation to actionable insight to as little as 15 seconds, all while eliminating the complexity of stitching together separate ingestion, transformation, and orchestration tools.


“Every second counts when you’re driving real‑time decisions,” said **Christian Romming, CEO of Etleap** . “With our 15‑second ingestion & modeling pipeline on Snowflake, teams can surface analytics‑ready data almost instantly, replace a patchwork of ingestion, transformation, and orchestration tools with a single managed workflow, and redirect engineering time from pipeline babysitting to higher‑impact innovation.”


By leveraging the Snowflake AI Data Cloud, Etleap is joining Snowflake in empowering every enterprise to achieve its full potential through data and AI to help organizations surface insights in seconds, simplify their data stacks, and accelerate AI‑driven decision‑making. Etleap’s managed pipeline unifies streaming ingestion, Apache Iceberg-backed storage, and dbt CoreÔ–powered modeling so data teams can deliver governed, analytics‑ready data to Snowflake dashboards, AI models, and operational applications in less than 15 seconds.


“Etleap’s commitment to helping Snowflake empower every enterprise to achieve its full potential through data and AI can be seen through the launch of the 15‑second ingestion & modeling pipeline,” said **Kieran Kennedy, VP, Data Cloud Product Partner, Snowflake** . “We look forward to driving deeper value for Snowflake’s AI Data Cloud ecosystem through collaboration with Etleap to allow access to near real‑time, analytics‑ready data through Snowflake’s single, integrated platform.”


By building tools, applications and solutions on Snowflake, product and engineering teams are able to develop, scale, and operate without operational burden, delivering differentiated products to their customers. Snowflake Data Cloud Product Partners help customers maximize Snowflake’s flexibility, performance, and ease of use to deliver more meaningful insights.
