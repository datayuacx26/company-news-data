# Invoice2go helps small businesses get paid with data revamp

Invoice2go, a SaaS company helping small businesses manage invoices and payments, sought to modernize its data infrastructure to support growing customer volume and improve decision-making across teams. By replacing outdated Apache Spark clusters and migrating from Alooma to Etleap, Invoice2go streamlined its data pipelines, reducing coding complexity and improving data accessibility. The transformation enabled faster insights for product managers, marketers, and executives, while the lean data team could focus on business-specific tasks rather than infrastructure maintenance. With Etleap’s quick setup and seamless integration with AWS services, Invoice2go now delivers reliable, real-time data to every team, enhancing both internal efficiency and customer experience.
