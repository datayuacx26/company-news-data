# From Breaking Stigma to Building the Future of Health

Hims & Hers began by dismantling the barriers to essential care, turning stigmatized conversations into accessible, evidence-based solutions for hair loss and sexual health. We’ve since evolved into a comprehensive health partner that meets people exactly where they are, transforming a fragmented healthcare system into a seamless, everyday experience.


Today, we are proud to debut[Healthcare Reimagined by Hims & Hers](https://www.usatoday.com/story/sponsor-story/acumen/2026/03/27/healthcare-reimagined-by-hims-hers/89317090007/) , a featured film in the[Innovative Leaders documentary series](https://www.usatoday.com/sponsor-story/acumen/) premiering on USA TODAY. In partnership with Acumen Media, the series spotlights the pioneers selected for their impact reshaping today’s industries and societies.


Selected as the voice for the future of health, *Healthcare Reimagined by Hims & Hers* captures our journey from a bold startup challenging the status quo to a global health partner supporting more than 2.5 million customers. This film traces our evolution from providing access to treatment for isolated concerns to pioneering a proactive, lifelong model of wellness, empowering people to maintain balance at every stage of life.


We’re proud of our journey so far but even more excited for the road ahead.
