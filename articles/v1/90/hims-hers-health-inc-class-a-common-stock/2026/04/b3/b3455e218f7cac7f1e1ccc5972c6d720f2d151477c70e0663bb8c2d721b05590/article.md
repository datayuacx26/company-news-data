# Navigating the Estrogen Patch Shortage with Hers

At Hers, we refuse to let women be left behind.


The[national shortage of estrogen patches](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/patients-scramble-find-estrogen-patches-shortage-worsens-after-us-fda-champions-2026-04-09/) is a stark reminder that the healthcare system still treats women’s health as an afterthought. After years of misinformation that hormone therapy increased risk of breast cancer, the[FDA’s removal of the Black Box warning](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/hhs-advances-womens-health-removes-misleading-fda-warnings-hormone-replacement-therapy) was a clear victory for science over stigma. But it also exposed a system in crisis. With 1.3 million women entering menopause annually, the outdated supply chain can’t keep pace with a new generation of women ready to reclaim their health and vitality.


Hers is stepping into this gap to ensure that in this moment of uncertainty, women experiencing perimenopause and menopause have a reliable health partner. For any woman seeking estrogen patches during this crisis, Hers is here to help. We have built[a dependable supply](https://www.forhers.com/menopause) , allowing us to consistently deliver access to the uninterrupted, high-quality care women deserve, with intention, compassion, and reliability at every step. We are here to ensure that women’s health, and their identity, never have to wait on a backorder.


Trust in healthcare is fragile. It’s earned through action, not promises. While industry experts warn that shortages could persist for years, we refuse to accept that as the status quo for our customers. Managing menopause symptoms isn't a nice-to-have. Hot flashes, night sweats, and disrupted sleep can sideline women from their own lives. At Hers, we’ve replaced traditional hurdles with individualized care, offering eligible customers personalized treatment plans featuring estradiol and progesterone tailored to their specific health history and personal preferences.


Women can refine their treatment plans anytime in partnership with licensed providers and a Care Team trained in perimenopause and menopause care. Beyond the medication, Hers provides a holistic ecosystem of expert-led content in our app, spanning wellness, nutrition, and sleep. The entire experience is designed to ensure women don’t just manage this transition, but thrive through it.


Women shouldn't have to put their lives on hold while the rest of the industry plays catch-up. At Hers, we are all-in on supporting women at every stage of life, ensuring that the care they need is available exactly when they need it.
