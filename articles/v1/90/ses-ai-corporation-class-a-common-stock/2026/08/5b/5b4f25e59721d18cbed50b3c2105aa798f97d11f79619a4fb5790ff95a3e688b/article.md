### SES AI Appoints Paul Diemer to Board of Directors


August 13, 2026


*Diemer brings decades of experience scaling innovation in energy storage systems*


WOBURN, Mass.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[SES AI Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.ses.ai%2F&esheet=54588535&newsitemid=20260813474000&lan=en-US&anchor=SES+AI+Corporation&index=1&md5=d0285a635ae08a3ba337706cb921a29a) (“SES AI”) (NYSE: SES), a global leader in the development and manufacturing of AI-enhanced high-performance Li-Metal and Li-ion batteries, today announced it has appointed Paul Diemer to the Company’s Board of Directors.


Mr. Diemer previously served as Chief Technology Officer for Flex's Power business, where he led the global research and development and technology organizations responsible for embedded and critical power systems for AI datacenters, overseeing more than 1,000 engineers across 14 countries. In addition to Flex, Mr. Diemer previously oversaw new EV product architectures at BorgWarner and currently serves as SVP of Corporate Engineering at Generac Power Systems. During his career, Mr. Diemer has directed technology strategy for critical power and data center infrastructure, helped shape product roadmaps across multi-billion-dollar business segments while driving innovation in power electronics, energy infrastructure, and AI-enabled solutions.


“We are pleased to add Paul to the Board. His extensive experience directing technology strategy in the data center infrastructure and energy storage space should provide meaningful insight and guidance as we continue to execute on our growth plans,” said Dr. Qichao Hu, SES AI founder and CEO.


**About SES AI:** SES AI Corp. (NYSE: SES) is powering the future of global electric transportation on land and in the air with the world’s most advanced Li-Metal batteries. SES AI is the first battery company in the world to accelerate its pace of innovation by utilizing superintelligent AI across the spectrum of its business, from research and development; materials sourcing; cell design; engineering and manufacturing; to battery health and safety monitoring. Founded in 2012, SES AI is an Li-Metal battery developer and manufacturer headquartered in Boston and with operations in Singapore, Shanghai, and Seoul.
