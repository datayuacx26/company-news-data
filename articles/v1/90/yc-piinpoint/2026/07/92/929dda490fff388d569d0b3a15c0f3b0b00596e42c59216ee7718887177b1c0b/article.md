# Welcome Megan Williamson to the PiinPoint Team!

We are excited to announce that[Megan Williamson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/megswilliamson/) has joined PiinPoint as our newest Business Development Representative, based in Reno, NV. Her arrival marks a significant step in our ongoing US expansion.


Megan brings over eight years of extensive experience in the financial aspects of residential real estate and mortgages. Throughout her career, she has successfully delivered a range of services to local clientele, gaining a deep understanding of the complex supply and demand dynamics in the market. This wealth of experience equips her with the insight to swiftly identify and address the pain points of our prospects, ensuring quicker, more effective solutions.


Outside of her professional life, Megan is a passionate builder and innovator. She has constructed her own electric bike and solar generator. Megan's ultimate goal is to design a fully solar-powered bicycle.


"With Megan joining us from the West Coast, we are enhancing our US presence with representatives across the country, enabling us to provide clients with local and industry expertise," says Jim Robeson, Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder at PiinPoint.
