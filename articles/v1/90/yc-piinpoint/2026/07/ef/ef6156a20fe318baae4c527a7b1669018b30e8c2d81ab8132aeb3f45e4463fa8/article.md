# Sally Woo Joins PiinPoint as our new Director of Growth Marketing!

Today, we are delighted to share that Sally Woo has joined PiinPoint as our Director of Growth Marketing. This is a new role designed to help us further refine and execute our US go-to-market strategy for 2024 and beyond!


Sally has over 10 years of experience in B2B SaaS/Tech marketing, supporting industry-leading 3D design to mission-critical crime-fighting software. Sally is passionate about driving the growth engine of a company with integrated marketing programs that support Account-based revenue expansion, product launches, and building and executing demand creation programs. Sally received her BComm from Rotman School of Management and she is Pragmatic Management Certified - Level IV. And if that is not enough, Sally has two little ones at home, so a busy life.


“As we prioritize our growth in the US, I am confident that Sally will help us with our go-to-market program as we roll out more of our GeoAI applications and services to our clients and the broader retail community,” says Jim Robeson, Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder at PiinPoint. “We are very excited to have you Sally - Welcome!”
