# Strengthening PiinPoint’s U.S. Expansion – Meet Ryan, Our New ADR!

Welcome Ryan Poulin to the PiinPoint Team!


We're excited to introduce the newest addition to our team—Ryan Poulin!


Ryan joins us as an Account Development Representative, bringing with him a strong foundation in data and analytics, having spent the past year and a half working closely in the space.


At PiinPoint, we believe that building strong relationships starts with a consistent and knowledgeable team. While workforce shifts are a natural part of business, we focus on sustainable growth so that our customers continue to work with familiar faces and trusted expertise. Ryan's addition strengthens our commitment to that stability.


"Bringing Ryan onto the team was a proactive decision on our part—we knew we wanted the right fit, someone who could grow with us and truly support our customers. With Ryan based in the U.S., we're also reinforcing our focus on helping retailers expand and succeed across North America."— Jim Robeson, CEO of PiinPoint.


Outside of work, Ryan is a passionate golfer and a devoted Detroit sports fan—cheering on both the Detroit Lions and Detroit Red Wings. We're thrilled to have him on board and can't wait for you to meet him!


Please join us in welcoming Ryan to the team!
