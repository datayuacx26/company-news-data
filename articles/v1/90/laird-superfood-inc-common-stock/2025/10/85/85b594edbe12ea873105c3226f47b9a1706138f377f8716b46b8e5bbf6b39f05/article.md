# Pumpkin Spiced Horchata Latte

## Ingredients


8 oz Hot Water


1/3 cup Pumpkin Spice Instant Latte


1/3 cup Pumpkin Puree


4 oz Home Made Horchata


Homemade Horchata:


1 1/2 cup White Rice (uncooked long-grain)


2 Cinnamon Sticks


4 cups Hot Water


2 cups Coconut Milk


2 teaspoons Vanilla Extract


2 teaspoons Ground Cinnamon


1/3 cup Maple Syrup


## Directions


1


Add water to a sauce pan over medium heat. Once simmering add Pumpkin Spice Latte and whisk.


2


Add pumpkin puree to Pumpkins Spice Latte.


3


Warm Horchata and aerate with wand. Pour Latte into mug then pour in the Horchata. Garnish with cinnamon and Pumpkin Spice Creamer.


4


Homemade Horchata:


5


To a blender, add rice, 2 cups of hot water, and 2 cinnamon sticks.


6


Blend until the rice and cinnamon sticks are roughly ground. Add the remaining water and blend again.


7


Pour contents into a pitcher or container with a fitted lid and refrigerate overnight or for at least 8 hours. Pour the rice mixture through a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth, into a pitcher. Discard the rice. Stir in the coconut milk, vanilla, cinnamon, and maple syrup. Chill until ready to serve.
