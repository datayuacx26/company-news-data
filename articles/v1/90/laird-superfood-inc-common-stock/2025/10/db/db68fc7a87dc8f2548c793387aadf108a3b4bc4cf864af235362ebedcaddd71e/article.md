# Top 5 TikTok Health Trends Worth Trying for Better Wellness in 2025

Many people are learning about wellness as part of their daily scrolling. That's it: an accessible morning routine that gets your blood flowing, helps wake you up, and promotes positive movement. The 50 Jump Ritual can:


- Reduce overall stress.


- Improve focus.


- Increase blood flow, raising energy levels first thing.

Ending your evening with a "sleepy girl mocktail" is, according to TikTok influencers, a great way to relax at the end of the day. What's in a sleepy girl mocktail? It features tart cherry juice and magnesium, which leads to significant sleep benefits due to the tryptophan content and enhanced melatonin production. Of course, if you want to reap the benefits of the sleepy girl mocktail, it's critical to combine it with good sleep hygiene: a dark room, going to bed around the same time every evening, and avoiding screen time right before bed. ## 4. Chia Seed Water


The viral chia seed water trend on TikTok starts by adding 1-2 tablespoons of chia seeds to a glass of water. Once it's been allowed to sit for a few minutes, soaking the seeds, users can add flavoring, including lemon juice, fruit, or honey.


This little hack can offer a number of potential health benefits. Chia seeds contain fiber, omega-3 fatty acids, and antioxidants: a hard-hitting powerhouse that can increase gut health and provide sustained energy throughout the day.

## 5. Embracing Mindful Rest (i.e. Is Bed Rotting Good for You?)


What is bed rotting? The bed rotting trend is a form of intentional self-care prioritized on TikTok. This trend encourages viewers to fight against "hustle culture" by spending extended time in bed scrolling, watching TV, or otherwise engaging in deliberately restful activities.


Bed rotting can offer some benefits—especially if you're struggling with mental health challenges like burnout. When you stay in bed for several extra hours or even for an entire day, it may help you get the mental reset you need to improve your overall mental health and fight those common conditions. In fact, prioritizing rest can offer several vital mental health benefits.


Can bed rotting be good for you? Yes, in moderation. However, it's important to balance those periods of rest with physical activity. Even a light walk around the block can be energizing and help protect your body against the risks associated with excessively low activity levels.


Experts warn that excessive inactivity can lead to considerable health challenges, including the risk of developing cardiovascular problems. Set healthy boundaries around your bed rotting periods, and make sure that you're using those periods of rest as part of a lifestyle that includes plenty of healthy movement.


Alongside your mindful self-care efforts, including time deliberately spent in rest, make sure you're supporting your body with nourishing foods that can help protect both physical and mental health. Eating a healthy diet rich in vitamins and nutrients can help provide you with the energy you need to tackle the items on your To Do list once you roll out of bed again.
