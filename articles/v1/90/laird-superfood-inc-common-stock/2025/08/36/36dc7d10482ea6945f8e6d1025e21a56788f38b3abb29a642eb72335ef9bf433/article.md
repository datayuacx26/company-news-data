# Performance Coffee Date Pecan Bread

## Ingredients


1 cup Dates, pitted (soaked, chopped)


1 teaspoon Baking Soda


1 cup Performance Coffee (Brewed)


2 tablespoons Unsalted Butter (softened)


1 cup Vanilla Coconut Palm Sugar


1 teaspoon Vanilla Extract


1 Egg


1 1/2 cup All Purpose Flour


1 teaspoon Salt


1 cup Pecans Chopped


## Directions


1


Brew a pot of Lairds Organic Performance Coffee with Functional Mushrooms.


2


Pit dates. Place in a large bowl and cover with boiling water for up to an hour.


3


Preheat the oven to 400 degrees. Grease 8x4-inch pan and line with parchment paper.


4


Drain dates and place them in a small bowl with baking soda and 1 cup of the hot, fresh brewed Performance Coffee and let sit for a few minutes.


5


In a food processor add the date, coffee, baking soda mixture and blend until smooth. Set aside.


6


In a mixing bowl, mix together butter, sugar, and egg until well mixed. Scrape the sides of the bowl and add vanilla. Add Rlour, salt and date coffee mixture. *Note, you can add 1/2 of the pecans here. Transfer batter to the prepared pan.


7


Add the Pecans onto the top of the batter. Be sure to press them into the mixture slightly.


8


Place in the center of the 400 degree oven and bake for 1 hour. Remove from oven and let the bread stand for 5 minutes before removing from the pan. Remove the parchment paper and Rinish cooling on a wire rack.


9


After an hour or so and the bread has cooled through entirely. Slice and enjoy!!!
