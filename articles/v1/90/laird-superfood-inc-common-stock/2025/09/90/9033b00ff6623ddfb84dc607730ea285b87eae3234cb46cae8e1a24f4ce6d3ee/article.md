## What Is Pumpkin Cold Foam?


Cold foam is simply frothed milk or creamer that sits on top of iced coffee, cold brew, or even iced matcha. It creates a velvety layer that slowly mingles with your drink as you sip, offering a balance of creaminess and flavor without the heaviness of whipped cream.

By making pumpkin cold foam at home, you have control over the quality of ingredients. Instead of relying on sugary syrups or artificial flavorings, you can choose wholesome additions that may support your well-being while still indulging in seasonal flair.

## Tools & Prep for Making Your Homemade Cold Foam


To make frothy pumpkin cold foam at home, you'll need just a couple of[tools](https://lairdsuperfood.com/collections/accessories-more) and a little prep:


### Tools


- **Milk frother or handheld frother:** This is the simplest way to whip your cold foam into airy perfection.
- **Small mason jar with a tight lid:** If you don't have a frother, shaking vigorously in a jar is an excellent alternative.


### Prep Tips


- **Chill your base:** Cold milk or creamer froths better and holds its texture longer. Pop it in the fridge before starting.
- **If using Pumpkin Spice Instant Latte:** Dissolve a small amount in hot water, then let it cool before adding to your iced coffee base. This ensures a smooth, lump-free mix.


## The Best Pumpkin Cold Foam Recipe


Ready to whip it up? Here's a simple recipe you can tweak to taste:


### Ingredients


- 2 tbsp[Laird Pumpkin Spice Superfood Creamer](https://lairdsuperfood.com/collections/laird-superfood-creamers/products/pumpkin-spice-superfood-creamer)
- 1–2 tbsp plant-based milk (chilled)
- Optional: ½ tsp coconut sugar or monk fruit sweetener
- Iced coffee or cold brew (8–12 oz)
- Optional garnish: pinch of cinnamon, nutmeg, or pumpkin pie spice


### Method


1. **Measure:** Add 2 tbsp of Pumpkin Spice Superfood Creamer to your frothing vessel. If you prefer a fuller latte base, mix ⅓ cup Pumpkin Spice Instant Latte into your iced coffee instead.
2. **Combine:** Add a splash of chilled plant-based milk and sweetener if desired.
3. **Froth:** Use a frother until the mixture doubles in volume and forms soft peaks. If using a jar, shake vigorously for 30–45 seconds.
4. **Pour & Enjoy:** Fill a glass with iced coffee or cold brew, then gently spoon the pumpkin foam on top. Sprinkle with cinnamon or nutmeg for a cozy aroma.
