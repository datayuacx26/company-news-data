# Daily Green Superfood Schug (Sauce)

## Ingredients


20 Serrano Peppers (chilis)


6 cloves Garlic (each)


1 tablespoon Salt


1 tablespoon Ground Cardamom


1 tablespoon Coriander Seed, ground


2 tablespoon Daily Greens


2 Lemons (juiced)


1 cup Parsley


1 cup Cilantro


1 cup Avocado Oil


## Directions


1


Cut stems off of Serrano chilis.


2


De-seed Serrano Chilies and add to blender.


3


Clean 6 garlic cloves and add to blender.


4


De-stem 1 cup of packed Italian flat leaf parsley leaves.


5


De-stem 1 cup of packed cilantro leaves.


6


Measure 1 tablespoon salt and add to blender.


7


Measure 1 tablespoon cardamom and add to blender.


8


Measure 1 tablespoon coriander and add to blender.


9


Measure 2 tablespoons Daily Greens and add to blender.


10


Juice 2 lemons and add to blender.


11


Add 1 cup parsley and cilantro to blender.


12


Turn on blender and stream Avocado oil until it's incorporated.


13


Turn blender off and scrape down anything that may not be incorporated. Finish blending.


14


Adjust seasoning.


15


Store in an airtight container and refrigerate.
