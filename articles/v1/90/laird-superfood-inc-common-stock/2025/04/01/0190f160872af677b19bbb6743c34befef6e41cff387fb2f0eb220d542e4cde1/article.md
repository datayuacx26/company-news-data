# Best Ways to Enjoy All Natural Coffee: Pairing Tips and Tricks

Optimizing your creamer flavor profile with your natural coffee choice is a fantastic way to take your pleasurable morning routine into the realm of adventure every day. Many of us have fallen into the routine of examining our natural coffee choices for flavor hints that appeal to us but then don't maximize our opportunity for enjoyment by using the same creamer flavor with every cup of natural coffee.


No longer! This article will walk you through making the best of your coffee experience through thoughtful and inspired natural coffee and creamer pairings that could lead to your new favorite brew.


## Choosing the Right Coffee Bean


Start your inspired coffee pairing off right with careful consideration of your preferred roast level. It is truly amazing how the roast level of your bean can create a rich tapestry of desirable qualities. Notice some of the basic coffee descriptions and think about how these flavors could be enhanced or hidden through coffee creamer choice.


## Light roast


Light roast coffees offer a brighter flavor along with a higher caffeine content ounce per ounce of coffee. Some people prefer a light roast for the uplifting taste and feel that it is less likely to cause heartburn.


## Medium roast


Medium roast coffees can create stellar, balanced flavor profiles that allow for a wide range of subtle or powerful notes without being overpowering. With choices such as our[FOCUS Medium Roast](https://lairdsuperfood.com/collections/coffees/products/focus-medium-roast-ground-coffee) , you can start with a base that enhances many delicious creamer options.


## Dark roast


Dark roast coffees offer the boldest and potentially most intense flavor profiles. These strong brews allow the coffee to take front and center with a variety of creamer options. Choose a blend that can hold its own, such as our[Organic Peruvain Dark Roast](https://lairdsuperfood.com/collections/coffees/products/peruvian-dark-roast-ground-coffee) .


In addition to the roast, think about the flavor profile. Most quality natural coffees offer a description that allows you to make an informed choice. Some common notes include:


- Fruity
- Floral
- Nutty
- Cocoa


Knowing the flavor notes already present in your coffee can help you decide which creamers will enhance the best of your coffee choice.


A final consideration when choosing a natural coffee is to look for certifications like Fair Trade and USDA Organic to ensure high-quality, sustainable coffee.


## Exploring Creamer Pairings


Once you have the perfect bean for your current mood, build out from there with these coffee creamer pairing ideas that can help you make the most of your natural coffee experience. Consider your snack choices and see the way this pulls the best of your coffee and creamer choice to the fore.


- Pair a sweet creamer with salty snacks like plantain chips or pretzels and hummus for a balanced flavor combination.
- Combine hazelnut creamer with a spicy cinnamon breakfast bar.
- Plain doesn't mean boring! Use plain creamer to refresh your palate and balance out rich flavors, such as smoothies with dark chocolate protein powder or rich nut butter snacks.
- Try sweet creamer with snacks like fruit, yogurt plates, or sourdough scones for a decadent treat.


## Seasonal Coffee Inspiration


Embrace the beauty of the changing seasons by choosing all-natural coffee creamers that allow you to indulge in the best of what the seasons have to offer.


## Fall:


Highlight the flavors of fall with a warm pumpkin spice coffee and a cinnamon creamer.


## Winter:


Brighten up winter days with a peppermint mocha coffee and a rich chocolate creamer.


## Spring:


Enjoy a floral coffee flavor with a sweet vanilla creamer in the spring.


## Summer:


Refresh your taste buds with a fruity coffee blend and a coconut creamer in the summer.


## Coffee and Creamer Pairing Tips


No matter what flavor profiles spark your imagination and interest, use these tips to make your choice a standout feature that enhances your all-natural coffee creamer pairing ideas:


### Choose a creamer with a stronger flavor profile if you prefer a bold brew.


When working with a bold brew coffee, feel free to push the limits of creamer flavor options. As your coffee flavor will still stand out even amidst a strong flavor, you can enjoy creamers that might overpower a lighter roast.


### Consider the temperature of your coffee.


Hot coffee pairs well with steamed creamers or ones with a spicier profile, such as[Peppermint Mocha Superfood Creamer](https://lairdsuperfood.com/collections/laird-superfood-creamers/products/peppermint-mocha-superfood-creamer) , while iced coffee pairs well with refreshing creamers like a classic[Vanilla Superfood Creamer](https://lairdsuperfood.com/collections/laird-superfood-creamers/products/vanilla-superfood-creamer) .


### Experiment with different flavor combinations to find your perfect cup.


If you have found one excellent natural coffee and creamer combination, enjoy! But don't feel stuck, keep trying new pairings and revel in the possibilities!


### Don't be afraid to try new and unexpected pairings to expand your palate.


With a good selection of all-natural coffees in a variety of roasts and high-quality superfood creamers, all with inspired and tried-and-true flavor options, you really can't go wrong trying something new.


## Common Mistakes To Avoid


As you discover new ways to enjoy your natural coffee selection through inspired creamer pairing options, be aware of some common pitfalls that could undermine your experience.


- Avoid using low-quality creamers that can overpower the flavor of your coffee.
- Don't overdo it with the creamer –


too much can make your coffee taste uncharacteristically poor.
- Steer clear of creamers with artificial flavors or additives that can detract from the natural flavor of your coffee.


## The Benefits of Natural Coffee


As you explore exciting coffee options, being particular about drinking natural coffee allows for the following benefits to be a part of every coffee creamer pairing adventure:


- Natural coffee is free from artificial additives and preservatives that can harm your health.
- Choosing natural coffee supports sustainable and fair trade practices.
- Natural coffee can be more flavorful and aromatic than commercial coffee.
- Drinking natural coffee can be a healthier choice for your body and the environment.


Natural coffee is the healthiest form of coffee, and when it is from a 100% organic source, you can feel confident you are making a great choice.
