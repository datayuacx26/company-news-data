# Protein Coffee Tiramisu

## Ingredients


16 oz Mascarpone cheese (cold)


4 egg yolks


2/3 cup coconut sugar


1 tsp vanilla


Assembly ingredients


30-36 Ladyfingers


6 tbsp Protein Coffee (Sweet & Creamy or Vanilla)


12 oz water


2 tbsp cocoa powder


## Directions


1


Whisk the mascarpone cheese on medium speed for 30-60 seconds until creamy. Scrape the bowl and set aside


2


n a separate bowl, add the egg yolks and coconut sugar. Whisk on medium-high speed for around 2 minutes. The mixture will be light and fluffy. Do not overmix.


3


Pour the egg yolk and sugar mixture onto the mascarpone cheese. Add salt and vanilla, then whisk on medium speed just until combined, scraping the bowl halfway.


4


n a separate bowl, whip the cold heavy cream until it reaches medium stiff peaks. Do not overmix.


5


Add the whipped cream to the mascarpone mixture, folding gently. You will have a creamy, light mixture.


6


Assembly & Serving


7


Pour the water and protein coffee into a wide bowl, and make sure it is not hot. Dip each ladyfinger quickly into the protein coffee and spread onto your serving dish.


8


Spread half the mascarpone mix on ladyfingers. Repeat with another layer of soaked ladyfingers
