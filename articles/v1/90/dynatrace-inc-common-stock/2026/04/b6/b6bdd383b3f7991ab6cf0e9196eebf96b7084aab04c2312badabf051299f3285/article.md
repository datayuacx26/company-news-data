# Dynatrace to Report Fourth Quarter and Full Year Fiscal 2026 Financial Results


April 29, 2026 8:00 am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_9ffac748bd6d76e21b7421bc8d63d867/dynatrace/news/2026-04-29_Dynatrace_to_Report_Fourth_Quarter_and_Full_Year_422.pdf)


BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Dynatrace](https://www.dynatrace.com/) (NYSE: DT), the leading AI-powered observability platform, today announced that it will report financial results for its fourth quarter and full fiscal year 2026 ended March 31, 2026 before the U.S. financial markets open on May 13, 2026. In conjunction with this report, Dynatrace will host a conference call and live webcast to discuss the company’s financial results and its business outlook.


**Conference Call Details**


The conference call will begin at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time on May 13, 2026. To access the conference call from the U.S. and Canada, dial (866) 405-1247, or internationally, dial (201) 689-8045 with conference ID # 13760309. The call will also be available live via webcast on the company’s website,[ir.dynatrace.com](https://ir.dynatrace.com/) .


An audio replay of the call will be available until 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on August 13, 2026, by dialing (877) 660-6853 from the U.S. or Canada, or for international callers by dialing (201) 612-7415 and entering conference ID # 13760309. In addition, an archived webcast will be available at[ir.dynatrace.com](https://ir.dynatrace.com/) .
