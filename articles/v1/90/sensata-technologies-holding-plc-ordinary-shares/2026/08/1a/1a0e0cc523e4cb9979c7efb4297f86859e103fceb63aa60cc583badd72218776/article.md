# Sensata Technologies Wins Dutch Innovation Prize 2025 for Engineering Excellence

- ***Award recognizes Sensata’s leadership in sensor-rich solutions and commitment to sustainable mobility.***


Sensata Technologies is proud to announce that it has won the Dutch Innovation Prize 2025 in the Large Company category. This prestigious award celebrates organizations that demonstrate exceptional innovation and impact across industry and society.


Based on the Dutch Innovation Monitor of the Amsterdam Centre for Business Innovation at the University of Amsterdam, this accolade highlights Sensata’s innovation culture and its focus on delivering solutions for a sustainable future. Its commitment to co-creation with customers, continuous learning, and knowledge sharing across teams has been central to earning this recognition.


In the Netherlands, product development is critical, with Sensata’s Research & Development resources primarily focused on finding new solutions for Electric Vehicle (EV) technologies. When judging this award, the panel considered three of Sensata’s most recent innovations which showcase the company’s focus on technology development for a cleaner world.
Two of these innovations provide safe, efficient and practical solutions for heavy electric truck and passenger cars:


- The[MegaWatt Charge Power Distribution Unit](https://www.sensata.com/contact/newsroom/sensata-technologies-high-voltage-distribution-units-enabling-megawatt-charging) enables megawatt charging on heavy electric trucks.
- The[High Efficiency Contactor](https://www.sensata.com/contact/newsroom/sensata-technologies-launches-high-efficiency-contactor-enable-seamless-400v800v) allows safe and efficient 400V/800V battery reconfiguration, allowing 800V EVs to charge at 400V stations without costly boost converter systems.


Judges also considered Sensata’s work on advanced braking systems, including the[Brake-by-Wire Force Sensor](https://www.sensata.com/contact/newsroom/sensata-technologies-new-brake-pedal-force-sensor-enables-safer-brakes) , which improves braking response time by capturing driver intent, even before the brake pedal moves.


Harsh Kulkarni, Senior Engineering Director and Site Leader, Sensata Netherlands, said, "Winning the Dutch Innovation Prize is a proud moment for our entire team. This achievement reflects the creativity, dedication, and collaboration of our engineers, who work closely with customers years ahead of market launch to deliver breakthrough technologies."


In awarding this prize, judges also evaluated Sensata’s culture of innovation. The company invests in talent development through programmes like the Technical Ladder, which encourages, recognizes, and rewards its best technical talent by providing career progression and reporting levels paralleling those for equivalent rungs on the management ladder. This unique approach supports engineers in building long-term careers and ensures Sensata remains at the forefront of innovation in mobility and sustainability.
