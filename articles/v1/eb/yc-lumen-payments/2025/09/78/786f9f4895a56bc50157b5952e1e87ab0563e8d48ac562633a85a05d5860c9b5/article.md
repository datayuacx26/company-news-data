# Stripe Open Source Alternative

Billing gets messy fast: free trials, seats for teams, orgs, usage, credits. Stripe Billing helps at the start, but it becomes complex and costly. We built **Lumen Payments** as an open source alternative that gives you control, is easier to integrate, and costs less. It also handles AI usage and credit models in a clean way.

## Why it’s easier


- Fewer steps, clear examples, minimal webhooks.
- Works locally with one command and seed data.
- You can extend flows in code instead of fighting a dashboard.


---


## Cost


- Stripe Billingadds 0.7% on top of payment fees.
- **Lumen self-host** : no billing fee.
- **Lumen cloud** : significantly cheaper than Stripe Billing.


---


## AI usage and credits


Track tokens or calls, price by model, use credit wallets per user or org, define rollover rules in code, and keep an audit trail. Fixes and backfills are simple.
