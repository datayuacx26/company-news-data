# Ennis Announces New General Manager of Eagle Graphics

**Midlothian, TX. June 6, 2025 –** Ennis, Inc. (NYSE: EBF) announced Stephen Colebert as the new general manager of Eagle Graphics, located in Annville, Pennsylvania. Established in 1976, Eagle Graphics has built a reputation for delivering high-quality commercial print solutions tailored to a diverse range of customer needs. Its comprehensive capabilities include 8-color UV web printing, digital and security printing, jumbo roll production, traditional business forms and direct mail services.


“We are excited to welcome Stephen to the Ennis print family,” said Carey McCellan, business unit director at Ennis. “His proven industry expertise makes him a true value-added member of our organization and we are confident he will play a key role in driving continued success.”


Colebert brings more than 34 years of experience in the print industry, including 31 years with the same organization through multiple transitions, including RRD, LSC Communications, KODI and Sheridan. Throughout his career, he has worked across six facilities in three states, gaining hands-on experience in operations, production management and process improvement.


“I have been fortunate to learn from many talented people over the years, and I consider myself a lifelong student of the industry. I’m excited to bring my experience to Ennis and Eagle Graphics while continuing to learn and grow alongside this great team,” Colebert said.

**About Ennis** Ennis, Inc. ([ennis.com](https://www.ennis.com/) ) is primarily engaged in the production and sale of business forms and other business products.
