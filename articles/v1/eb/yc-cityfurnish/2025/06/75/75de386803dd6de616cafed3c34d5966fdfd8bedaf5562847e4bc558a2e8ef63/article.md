**The Ultimate Renting Checklist for Events**


Before you jump into the rental wagon, here’s your not-so-boring but super important checklist:


**Know Your Guest Count** – Don’t guess. Count heads like your decor depends on it (because it does).
**Theme and Color Palette** – Decide on this early so you’re not panic-choosing peach sofas the night before.
**Rental Company Research** – Check reviews, portfolios, and read the return and damage policy.
**Damage Policy & Timelines** – Small kids, beautiful females with bangles and uncles with their drinks, some damages are bound to happen. Be prepared and check your furniture rental policy carefully.
**Delivery and Pickup Logistics** – Who’s handling what, and when. Ask the rental folks to be specific — like, “to the minute” specific.
