##


1. Keep the North-East Open and Clutter-Free


The North-East is often considered the zone of clarity, wisdom, and positive energy. As it is the zone of where Vastu Purush’s head is resting. This area should always be light and open. Do not add any red or yellow colour to it. You can put your temple here, also this area can be used for meditation.


You can add some plants to it but not too much. These are some easy vastu tips for your home’s NE area to help you increase the mind clarity.


###


Home Décor Tip


- Avoid storing heavy furniture or unused items.
- Decorate this space with indoor plants or fresh flowers.
- Allow natural sunlight to enter whenever possible.
- Use light shades like white, cream, or pastel colours.


A bright and organized North-East instantly makes the home feel fresh and welcoming.


##


2. Add Warmth to the South-East


The South-East is associated with the fire element and represents energy, activity, and finances. SE is the best location to make a kitchen. Adding blue, black, white and grey colour here can be harmful to your health and it can cause serious blockages to your cash flow as well. keeping warm colours here is mandatory here. Elemental balance is also mandatory. you can not ignore these vastu tips for home.


###


Home Décor Tip


- Keep it clean and well-organized.
- Use warm colours like light red, orange, or peach in small decorative elements.
- Replace broken appliances promptly.
- Keep the cooking area free from unnecessary clutter.


A neat kitchen naturally becomes one of the happiest and most inviting spaces in the home.


##


3. Create Stability in the South-West


The South-West represents stability, strength, and long-term security. To create a solid base for your relationships, a healthy SW is very important. This is the place your Pitra (ancestros) as well. To get blessing from them you can hang a picture of your ancestors here. Do not add any tree, plant or a picture of any tree or plant here. Yellow is the color here. Avoid all kind of mirror and glass here.


This area works best when it feels solid and grounded.


###


Home Décor Tip


- Place heavier furniture such as wardrobes or storage units here.
- Choose earthy colours like beige, brown, or mustard.
- Avoid unnecessary movement of furniture.
- Keep the area clean and organized.


A well-balanced South-West often creates a feeling of comfort and permanence within the home.


##


4. Keep the North Bright and Organized


The North is commonly associated with opportunities, career, and financial flow. A blocked or cluttered North can make the room feel smaller and less inviting.


A clean, well-organized North creates a sense of openness and movement within your home. Keep this area free from unnecessary clutter, allow natural light to enter whenever possible, and choose furniture that maximizes space without making the room feel crowded. Adding a healthy indoor plant or keeping your work essentials neatly arranged can make this area feel fresh, functional, and visually appealing.


##


5. A Comfortable North-West for Better Living


The North-West is associated with movement, communication, and social connections. It is the ideal zone for creating spaces where people naturally come together, whether it’s a family sitting area, guest room, or informal lounge.


Design this space to feel light, airy, and welcoming. Comfortable seating, soft furnishings, and good ventilation can make the North-West an inviting place for conversations and relaxation. Avoid overcrowding the area with bulky furniture, and instead opt for functional pieces that promote comfort while maintaining a sense of openness. A well-designed North-West can make your home feel more lively and welcoming for both family members and guests.
