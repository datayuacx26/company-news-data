# Think Outside the Box: Bold Furniture Color Combinations to Transform Your Home

If you reading this article, it means that you are searching for information that can help you furnish your home in a bold and unique style. Choosing a color scheme for your home can be an exciting and daunting task. On one hand, you’ll feel exhilarated at all the possibilities of what style would look like in your space. But on the other hand, choosing poorly can leave you feeling underwhelmed with no style at all!

We all know about the everlasting impact colours leave on us. our brain is coded to relate colours to particular things without thinking.


For Example: when we say blue, we think of ocean and when we say red, either its blood or danger.


Thats why, after doing a through research we have compiled this information for you. We hope that this article helps you find answers to some of your queries related to right yet personal colour combinations for your furniture. When[decorating your home](https://cityfurnish.com/blog/top-10-popular-home-decor-trends-in-2020) , furniture color combinations can make a huge impact. A bold color combination can instantly bring life and excitement to a room.


Now the question is, how do you navigate the maze of furniture color options to find the perfect theme for your home?


Here are some tips to help you incorporate bold furniture colors into your[home decor](https://cityfurnish.com/blog/modern-home-decor-trend-you-should-know-about) :


####


Choose a color palette:


Choosing more colours would help. select many combinations, shortlist them as per the size of the space, mood of the room or utilization of the room. Pick 2-3 colors that you love and want to use in your room. You can use a color wheel to determine which colors complement each other. For example, blue and orange are complementary colors while blue and green are analogous colors.


####


Benefits of Using a statement piece:


Start with a bold-colored sofa or armchair as your focal point and build the rest of the room around it. this method will help stay anchored and you won’t wander away from your theme. Also, a statement piece is a symbol of uniqueness. its your personal choice, around which you are creating a stunning world for yourself. A unique piece helps you stand out in a crowd.


####


Finding the right balance between bold with neutral:


Bold colors can be overpowering, so it’s important to balance them with neutral furniture pieces such as a[natural wood table](https://cityfurnish.com/things/3838/athena-coffee-table) or white curtains. This way, your bold piece will be the focal point of the room without taking over every inch of it. When purchasing a neutral piece, which balances well with another furniture. some good example of perfect mixing are here:


Pairing a bright red sofa with natural wood coffee table.


A bold yellow armchair with a natural rattan accent chair.


Mixing a bright blue accent wall with natural wood bookshelves.


A statement green velvet ottoman with a natural jute rug.


Pairing a bright orange accent chair with a natural bamboo side table.


This will ensure that you can use it in any[room without feeling](https://cityfurnish.com/blog/make-your-living-room-feel-more-cozy) like it no longer fits there.


####


Adding accents to make it comfortable and classy:


Use accent pieces like throw pillows, curtains, and rugs to introduce new colors and bring your color scheme together. To make the whole process more dynamic, you can create an accent wall to behave like support background which will later highlight every piece you bring in. For example:


Pieces made of natural wood, such as a coffee table or end table, will complement the blue wall by adding warmth and texture to the space.


Gold and Brass: Gold or brass accents, such as lamps or picture frames, can add a touch of glamour and sophistication to the room.


White Furniture: White furniture, such as an armchair or sofa, can create a crisp, clean contrast to the blue wall and keep the space feeling fresh and bright.


Bold Contrast Colours: If you want to create a more dynamic look, consider adding furniture pieces in bold, contrasting colors, such as a red armchair or a yellow sofa.


Textured fabrics: Adding textured fabrics, such as a shag rug or woven throw pillows, can add depth and texture to the space and help the blue wall stand out.


####


Experiment:


Trying new ideas is the key here. Don’t be afraid to play with different color combinations and have fun with your choices. If you love it, then it will work! For example, if you have a traditional living room with lots of greys, whites and creams, adding a bright blue sofa can create an unexpected contrast. The bold colour will help to draw the eye in and make it stand out from the rest of the room. The same goes for a more modern living room. If you have lots of blacks, whites and greys, adding a pop of blue can provide the perfect bit of colour to offset your neutral palette.


####


Designing as per the size and architecture of your space:


When choosing[furniture](https://zior.in/) for your home, consider the design style of your space and the size of your room. Bold color combinations may not be appropriate for traditional or formal spaces. But it doesn’t mean that you cannot bring in some colours in these spaces. a textured wall, a classy carpet and one or two beautiful paintings can add a lot to any space.


In smaller rooms, it’s important to balance bold colors with lighter or neutral colors otherwise your small space can look overstuffed and chaotic. Good lighting can also bring out the best in your furniture and enhance your bold color choices.


####


Don’t let go of your personal style:


Before you start[shopping for furniture](https://zior.in/) , think about your personal style and the vibe you want to create in your home. Do you prefer a calm and relaxing atmosphere or an energetic and playful one? Your personal style should be the starting point for your furniture color theme. this consideration will give a sense of ownership once you are done decorating and you will find pride in every praise you will receive for your beautiful home.


####


Designing around the colors of your walls:


The color of your walls can have a big impact on the overall look and feel of your home. Take into account the color of your walls when choosing a furniture color theme. If your walls are a neutral color, you have more freedom to choose bold and bright furniture. If your walls are already bold and bright, a more neutral furniture color theme may be a better choice. You can also have two different colours wall in one room. In this scenario try to follow the brighter colour mostly but don’t forget the lighter colour fully. Here are some examples:


Neutral colors: Furniture in neutral colors, such as beige, grey, or white, can create a cohesive look and allow the two colored walls to stand out.


Complimentary colors: If the two colored walls are complementary, such as blue and orange, consider choosing furniture in the other two colors on the color wheel, such as yellow or red, to create a harmonious look.


Matching one of the wall colors: If one of the colored walls is dominant, consider choosing furniture in that color to create a cohesive look.


Using patterns: Furniture with patterns, such as a patterned armchair or a printed sofa, can help tie the two colored walls together and create visual interest in the room.


####


Your Mood Your Choice:


Furniture color themes can help to create a specific mood or atmosphere in your home. Cool colors like blue and green can create a calming and relaxing atmosphere, while warm colors like red, yellow, and orange can create a more energetic and inviting atmosphere. Natural colours can turn the entire feel classy and


Additionally, when using bold furniture color combinations, it’s important to keep in mind the overall aesthetic of your home. If your home has a more minimalist style, using bold colors sparingly on just one piece of furniture may be more appropriate. On the other hand, if your home has a more eclectic style, you can mix and match bold colors in different pieces of furniture and accessories to create a lively and playful atmosphere.


Bold furniture color combinations can showcase your unique style and creativity, and bring excitement and personality to your home. Whether you want to create a calming and relaxing atmosphere or a bold and energetic one, there are endless possibilities with furniture color combinations. So have fun and let your creativity guide you!
