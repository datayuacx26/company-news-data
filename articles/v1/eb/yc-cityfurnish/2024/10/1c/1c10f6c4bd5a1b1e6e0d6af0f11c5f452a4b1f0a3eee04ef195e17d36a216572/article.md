# Home Decor Ideas for Rented Homes: Redesign in Budget

Living as a tenant a lot of times we are forced to compromise on style and comfort due to the high cost and lack of permanence of our situation. We accept it and make do with it, but it doesn't necessarily mean that we like it. But there are easy home decor ideas we have been working on to solve this problem for you. These 10 sneaky hacks to transform your rented homes from drab to fab, all without losing much flexibility, money, time or energy.


## 1. Removable Wallpaper


Removable wallpaper is an easy and temporary home decor idea if you want to change the entire look of your home. Not to forget the unlimited options in colors and themes. Either you are a nature lover, or love geometric patterns, wither you only want some beautiful lines on your wall or just some metallic touch, wallpapers are the best solution for most of your problems. It's like temporary tattoos for your walls - all the fun, none of the commitment. Perfect for indecisive decorators or serial movers!


## 2. Area Rugs for Personality


Rented homes almost never has exciting flooring, if they even have any flooring. Area rugs are here to save the day. These magical floor-covering superheroes add plushness under your feet, but they're also perfect for hiding those mysterious stains left by previous tenants. Additionally they are a treat for your eyes, as well as bring in the required elegance we all want to explore now and then.


## 3. Temporary Backsplash


Stick-on tiles and peel-and-stick backsplash panels are your secret weapons for instant kitchen glam. These home decorating ideas for rented apartments let you unleash your inner designer without unleashing your landlord's wrath. Rented home decorating ideas never looked so slick!


## 4. Stylish Lighting Fixtures


Those standard-issue light fixtures in your rented home are about as exciting as watching paint dry. It's time to light up your life (and your apartment) with some dazzling lights. Some wall lights, a few tall and short lamps here and there, specially in the corners, and some string lights in balcony and bedroom has the potential to blow you away. Every time they come to light, you would wonder, is it my home or a resort?


## 5. Add Plants to Your Home Decor


Greenery not only purifies the air but also adds a touch of nature to your concrete jungle. From resilient succulents to dramatic fiddle leaf figs, there's a plant for every black thumb and sun-starved corner. Just don't name them—it makes eviction day harder.

## 6. Command Hooks and Strips


Command hooks and strips are your sneaky allies in the battle against boring walls in rented homes. These magical adhesives let you decorate without losing your security deposit. Just don't go overboard - your landlord might get suspicious if every inch is covered!


## 7. Upgrade Curtains


Experimenting with curtains is the easiest home decor idea to jazz up your home, permanent or temporary. It's like giving your windows a makeover without the commitment. Plus, it's a sneaky way to inject personality and texture into your space. Home decor ideas for rented houses has never been so fun!


## 8. Stylish Furniture Covers

Let's face it, your landlord's taste in furniture might be stuck in the '70s. How to solve this? Here is the perfect home decor idea. Slip on some stylish furniture covers and voila - instant home decor for rented houses that won't break the bank or your lease agreement. It's like giving your sofa a facelift without the plastic surgery drama.


## 9. Wall Decals and Stickers


Wall decals are your sneaky accomplice in crime! These stick-on wonders come in a dizzying array of designs, from sassy quotes to psychedelic patterns. Perfect for spicing up your rented home decorating ideas without leaving a trace. Stick 'em up, peel 'em off – no harm, no foul!


## 10. Floating Shelves


These magical home decor ideas for rented houses contraptions let you display your prized knick-knacks without angering your landlord. Just don't overload them with your vintage brick collection, or you might be in for a surprise redecorating session!


## Conclusion

But seriously, with these hacks, you can transform your temporary digs from drab to fab faster than you can say "security deposit." Remember, your rental is like a Tinder date – it might not be forever, but that doesn't mean you can't have some fun while it lasts. So go forth and decorate, you rebellious rule-bender, you.
