The finest smart TVs for streaming are immediate gateways to the world of apps and can instantly transport you to services, including Netflix, Prime, Hotstar, and more. The best smart TVs not only have quick response times and intuitive navigation, but they also have powerful search features that can help you discover specific TV shows and movies. Additionally, they frequently have features like built-in Chrome cast or Apple Airplay that let you stream content from your phone directly to your smart TV.


Although some of these functions may be available on streaming devices like the Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K, it's frequently more convenient for customers to have those smart TV platforms built-in. By doing so, switching between live TV and streaming services will only require one remote control.
