# 7 Bedroom Decor Trends And Ideas That You Should Not Miss!

Sophistication, comfort and practicality – your bedroom requires and deserves it all!


Table of Contents


Toggle


One-third of our precious lives are spent sleeping and relaxing in the warmth of our bedroom. It’s this room that you are most likely to unwind, reset and re-energize your brain. This room will allow you to set the tone for the next day. Thus, it’s no surprise that it’s inherently essential to cultivate some serenity and peace while showing off elegance and your personal style in your bedroom.


When creating a haven for yourself, you must inculcate peace in every corner, so all the stress-inducing factors are left at the threshold of this room. It doesn’t only need to look good but also makes you feel like you can drop all your inhibitions right away.


Designing this room, though, may come with a set of worries and confusion. After all, it’s your bedroom, and you cannot let even one thing go wrong!


So, if you are switching houses or simply re-designing your bedroom for the better, you have come to the right place. We list the top 7-bedroom decor ideas and trends that have taken the world of interior designing by storm!


Feel free to pick the ideas you like and incorporate them into your bedroom.


##


Throw in some colour!


Have you heard about the term colour drenching? If not, let’s acquaint you with it!


It’s simply the act of painting all the surfaces of your bedroom, and not just the four walls. The idea behind it is simple – immerse your room in colour to make it appear larger than it is.


Now there are many ways to do so!


If you want your bedroom to look thoughtful, you could paint the walls, doors, cupboards and window frames the same colour. You can even pick different shades of the same colour. It would work especially well with soft greens, greyed blues, or any other neutral tone.


On the other hand, if you want to make a dramatic statement, you can pick a bold colour like yellow, orange or red. It will bring life to the room with that essential ‘pop,’ thus adding more character than before.


You can also paint the woodwork and ceilings to create a more cohesive aesthetic bedroom decor.


Pro tip: Do not go for spotless white since it has become quite the norm for every other house.


##


Soft lighting


It’s a known fact that if you want to sleep better, you must switch off all the bright and harsh overhead lighting at least an hour before you hit the hay. It ensures that your eyes get ample time to adjust to the darkness and puts you in a sleepy zone.


Clearly, the lighting of your bedroom is necessary to help attain good sleep, so, unsurprisingly, it becomes a focal design point, too.


You can invest in smart lighting solutions that can be well controlled with just a touch or come with a remote dimmer that can gauge the passing of time and gradually lower the light saturation. It will serve as a great aid to your senses.


Try to make ambient lighting the primary light source for your bedroom. Pick warm-tone lamps for your bedside tables to get a flood of soft lighting on both sides of the bed. Additionally, you can also get under-lighting for wardrobes, dressing tables and the bed for that beautiful hotel-like atmosphere.


##


Maximalism vibe


Most people believe that the concept of curated maximalism or ‘clutter core,’ as named by the millennials, is not for the bedrooms. That’s not true!


2022 has been all about stepping out of the Covid-19 shadows that brought alienation and distance between us and everyone else. This pandemic also allowed most people discovered the depths of their personalities. Thus, ensuring that the different facets of your personality shine through your bedroom design has become essential.


You can do this by repurposing and showing off your personal belongings with a bit more flair. Incorporate the varying tones and put textured and opulent materials with a contrasting structure to ensure layered richness in your room.


You can do so by keeping a bamboo or nickel-plated table lamp on a trolley table with a heavily structured design. Or you can pair eclectic and bright-coloured cushion covers on plush cushions to express yourself vividly.


How about the palette of burnt orange with a splash of cool blue, alongside some delicate floral work, including pinks and red?


Simply put, channel your entire personality in your room and don’t worry about it being too much – it’s trendy!


##


Multifunctional furniture


Sure, we have left Covid-19 behind, for it to hopefully never return. But it has integrally changed the way we operated and lived. For example, most people’s ‘work’ shifted to the four walls of their houses. This is why you need multifunctional furniture to fulfil your necessity while also saving both money and floor space.


Here’s a little something to give you a fair idea – you can get a wide-top dressing table which can double up as your work desk if need be. This way, you won’t need to create a dedicated workspace. Plus, as the day comes to an end, you can keep your work paraphernalia away, ensuring a good work-life balance.

##


Statement headboards


Gone are the days of minimalism (for most people, if not all)! Today’s bedroom decor trends if dominated by varied textures, floral designs and snug patterns. Statement headboards have become the real deal – with over 88% of the crowd scouting Pinterest for ideas to get beds that have gravitas and colour.


Today, the trend demands that you make a luxurious statement with your bedding. For the same, you can get an upholstered headboard, which is both practical and comfy and will allow you to put your personality on bare display.


Opt for headboards with curvy and tall silhouettes and couple them with rich colours and vivid patterns that tenaciously bring together all the elements of your room.


As per 2023 interior design predictions, scalloped designs and organic-shaped silhouettes will be a huge rage. Why? They are easy on the eyes, feel cosier and warmer on touch, and evoke a feeling of safety which will help make you feel restful.


You can find beautiful headboard designs even in the furniture on rent, so make sure to check out your options.


##


Curated art


Creating a gallery wall is one of the most popular trends of all time, and it’s gaining excessive admiration from tons of people everywhere.


A person’s bedroom says a lot about them – it’s the reflection of their personality, and this can very well include art pieces. You have an opportunity to show your unique taste and individuality with the art that you must carefully curate for your room.


Since the bed is the focal point of the bedroom, it allows you to play around with the empty walls. So, you can make the bed the main feature by putting one statement piece or several different artwork prints around the bed like a halo. It will draw more attention to the bed, putting it in the eyes of everyone!


##


Biophilia


For those unversed, a biophilic design indicates creating a mini garden or nature corner in your bedroom. The demand for this design trend has been at an all-time high this year and is likely to stay here for a long time!


First and foremost, bringing greenery to your bedroom will have a positive impact on both your mental and physical health. It will eliminate all pollutants from your surroundings, such as Carbon Dioxide.


Next, if you don’t want to put in a lot of effort, pick some low-maintenance house plants that can survive, grow and thrive even in indirect sunlight. Rubber plants make for the perfect indoor plant!


Keep in mind to get a plant that doesn’t produce too much pollen if you have any allergies.
