**Building a Safer Tomorrow: Sparsh’s Journey at Ingram Micro**


Meet Sparsh Bhatia, a Security Engineer based in India, who has been part of Ingram Micro for over three years. His story reflects what makes our culture unique—growth, purpose, and the power to make an impact every day.


**Why Ingram Micro?**


“When I joined Ingram Micro, I was drawn to its strong global presence and the opportunity to work with innovative technologies in a collaborative environment,” Sparsh shares.


What keeps him motivated? “Continuous learning, a supportive culture, and the ability to contribute to impactful projects driving real value.”


**Purpose in Every Action**


For Sparsh, meaning comes from knowing his work matters:


“What gives meaning to my work as a Security Engineer is knowing the measures I put in place to protect people, systems, and critical data directly. Contributing to a safer and resilient environment, and seeing the real impact of that protection, keeps this role genuinely fulfilling.”


His team plays a critical role in safeguarding the organization:


“We proactively strengthen security, respond to threats, and ensure the organization remains resilient to emerging risks. By working closely with each other and supporting other teams, we help create a safer, more reliable environment for all.”


**Culture of Growth and Inclusion**


Sparsh emphasizes the collaborative spirit that defines Ingram Micro:


“It’s a place where you can make a difference while working with talented, supportive teams.”
