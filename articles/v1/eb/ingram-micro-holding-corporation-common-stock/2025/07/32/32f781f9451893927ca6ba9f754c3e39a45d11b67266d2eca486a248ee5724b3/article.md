# From Campus to Career: Nithyasree’s Start at Ingram Micro


Nithyasree I Trainee Software Developer India


04 July 2025


When Nithyasree first heard about Ingram Micro during campus placement presentation, she was instantly captivated. “


*Everyone in my peer group wanted to join the company based on the presentation we saw—the culture, the events, the way they care for people. It was inspiring,* ” she recalls.


Now, nearly a year into her internship, Nithyasree is a Trainee Software Developer on the Product Search team within the Discovery Department of Engineering. Her journey has been nothing short of transformative.


**A Launchpad for Growth**


“


*I was driven by Ingram’s focus on digital transformation. I wanted to be part of that journey, however much I could contribute,* ” she says. And contribute she did. From bug fixes to feature development, Nithyasree has worked across both front-end and back-end technologies, including enhancements to the Xvantage search page and API health checks.


But it wasn’t just the technical exposure that stood out. “


*The Campus to Corporate Launchpad helped me set my goals. It gave me clarity on where I want to take my career,* ” she explains. Through 15–20 sessions, she learned about growth mindset, communication, and professionalism—skills that have shaped her both personally and professionally.


**A Culture of Connection**


One of the most surprising aspects of her experience? The accessibility of leadership. “


*We had the opportunity to meet top leaders within our first month at Ingram. I can walk into their office and have a conversation—that’s what I love about Ingram,* ” she shares.


Her team, too, has been a pillar of support. “


*They make sure I get the right amount of work and guide me through any blockers—even if it takes hours of debugging together,* ” she says. With mentors both inside and outside her team, Nithyasree never felt alone in navigating her new environment.


**Advice for Future Interns**


To those just starting out, Nithyasree offers this advice: “


*Don’t be scared. Be calm. Embrace everything that comes your way. Ingram will make you feel comfortable—it’s like a family.* ”


When asked to describe her internship in one word, she doesn’t hesitate: “


*Spectacular.* ”


**Looking Ahead**


“


*If I had to do it again, I’d join Ingram again. The exposure and growth potential here are huge. I can see myself growing into a successful professional,* ” she says with confidence.
