# Building Growth Through Trust

# Building Growth Through Trust


Teemu Vihervaara Cybersecurity Lead Nordics


07 August 2026


For Teemu Vihervaara, success starts with surrounding yourself with great people, ambitious goals, and a willingness to keep pushing forward.


Six years ago, he joined Ingram Micro because of the unique combination of industry leading technology vendors and high performing teams.


"A great mix of the world's leading vendors paired with trailblazing teams who are striving for better each and every day."


Today, as


**Cyber Security Lead Nordics** ,Teemu helps partners across the Nordic market grow their business and profitability through cybersecurity solutions, while continuing to play a key role in strengthening Ingram Micro's position in one of the industry's most dynamic sectors.


**Supporting Partners in a Rapidly Evolving Industry**


Based in Espoo, Finland, Teemu leads cybersecurity initiatives that help partners navigate an increasingly complex technology landscape.


Together with his team, he works closely with partners across the Nordic region to identify opportunities, develop expertise, and build profitable cybersecurity practices.


"Me and my team help partners in the Nordic market to grow their business and profitability through leading cyber security products."


His role combines business strategy, relationship building, and technical expertise, allowing him to help partners stay ahead of emerging challenges while unlocking new opportunities for growth.


**Proud of How Far the Team Has Come**


When asked about his proudest accomplishment, Teemu finds it difficult to choose just one.


Instead, he points to the remarkable evolution of the cybersecurity business throughout his time at Ingram Micro.


"I'm particularly proud of our tremendous growth from basically a not known value distributor to one of the most recognized value distributors in the Nordics."


That transformation didn't happen overnight. It was the result of sustained effort, strong partnerships, and a shared commitment to delivering value to customers and partners.


Seeing the team's impact grow year after year is something Teemu considers one of the most rewarding parts of his journey.


**Growing Through Trust**


One of the defining aspects of Teemu's experience at Ingram Micro has been the trust placed in him from day one.


Rather than feeling constrained by fear of failure, he felt empowered to take ownership, try new approaches, and continuously challenge himself.


"I've been fortunate that Ingram Micro and our leadership have given me full trust from the first day."


That environment helped him stretch beyond his comfort zone, develop new capabilities, and build confidence as a leader.


For Teemu, growth isn't just about gaining technical knowledge. It's also about having the freedom to take initiative and learn through experience.


**A Culture That Stands Apart**


Ask Teemu what truly differentiates Ingram Micro from other companies, and his answer comes back to the people.


"It's really the thing that's setting Ingram Micro apart."


He describes the culture as authentic, supportive, and focused on helping customers and partners succeed.


"Our teams are authentic, caring but most importantly very customer and business oriented."


This combination of collaboration, accountability, and customer focus creates an environment where individuals are encouraged to contribute, learn, and make a difference.


**The Value of Connections**


One of the things Teemu enjoys most about his role is the variety it offers.


Every day brings different opportunities to work with partners, engage with vendors, and collaborate with people across the technology ecosystem.


"The variety of the things we get to do with our partners and vendors, and the people I have the chance to meet, is really a thing that I appreciate a lot."


Those connections not only make the work interesting but also help drive innovation, strengthen partnerships, and create new opportunities for growth.


**Advice for Future Talent**


For anyone considering a career at Ingram Micro, Teemu's advice is simple but powerful:


"Be brave, be passionate and make sure to set high aspirations."


He believes success comes from being willing to challenge yourself, pursue ambitious goals, and approach opportunities with energy and determination.


At Ingram Micro, people who are curious, motivated, and ready to make an impact will find plenty of opportunities to grow.


> "Working at Ingram Micro means that you're one of the fortunate ones, having an employer who's genuinely caring about you and your professional development."
