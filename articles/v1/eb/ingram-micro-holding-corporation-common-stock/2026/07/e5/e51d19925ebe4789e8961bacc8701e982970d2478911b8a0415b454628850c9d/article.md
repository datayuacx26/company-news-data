# Ingram Micro to Report Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on July 30, 2026

IRVINE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Ingram Micro Holding Corporation (NYSE: INGM) (“Ingram Micro” or the “Company”) announced today that it will hold a conference call to discuss second quarter 2026 financial results on Thursday, July 30, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. PT (5:00 p.m. ET). The Company plans to release its financial results after market close the same day.


The call can be accessed at 877-407-9781 or 201-689-8796. A live webcast of the call can also be accessed at the Ingram Micro investor relations website at[https://ir.ingrammicro.com](https://ir.ingrammicro.com/) .


A telephonic replay will be available through Thursday, August 20, 2026, at 877-660-6853 or 201-612-7415, access code 13761702. **** A webcast replay will also be available at[https://ir.ingrammicro.com](https://ir.ingrammicro.com/) .


**About Ingram Micro**
