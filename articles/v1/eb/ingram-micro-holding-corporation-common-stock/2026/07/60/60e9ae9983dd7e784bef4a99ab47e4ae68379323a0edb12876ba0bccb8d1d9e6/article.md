# Growing with Trust: Priya's IAM Journey at Ingram Micro

# Growing with Trust: Priya's IAM Journey at Ingram Micro


Priya Kulothungan Senior Professional, IT security India


14 July 2026


In the world of Identity and Access Management (IAM), precision, reliability, and continuous learning are essential. For Priya Kulothungan, these qualities have shaped a rewarding career journey at Ingram Micro India.


Over the past five years, Priya has grown from an individual contributor within the IAM Operations team to a people leader, overseeing daily operations, driving process improvements, and mentoring team members.


### A Journey Built on Trust and Opportunity


*"Over the years, I've had the opportunity to take on new challenges, expand my responsibilities, and grow into a leadership role. Ingram Micro has provided great exposure and learning opportunities, but what I value most is the flexibility, collaborative culture, and supportive leadership."*


Priya's career progression reflects a workplace that encourages ownership, development, and continuous growth.


### Opportunities That Spark Growth


*"I've had the chance to work on cross functional projects, automation initiatives, and process improvements. These experiences have helped me strengthen both my technical expertise and leadership capabilities."*


From supporting day to day operations to contributing to strategic initiatives, Priya has embraced every opportunity to learn, improve, and make an impact.


### Support That Makes a Difference


*"The biggest factor in achieving my career goals has been the support from my managers. They've recognized my potential, encouraged me to step outside my comfort zone, and trusted me with greater responsibilities."*


She also credits internal training opportunities and knowledge sharing sessions for helping her build confidence and expand her expertise.


### Growing Into Leadership


*"My role has evolved significantly over the years. What started with supporting operational activities has grown into leading a team, ensuring quality, and helping drive efficiency improvements across our operations."*


Today, Priya combines technical expertise with leadership, helping both her team and the business succeed.


### Advice for Future Talent


*"Ingram Micro truly values its people. If you're eager to learn, take ownership, and grow your career, you'll find plenty of opportunities here. The exposure to different technologies, teams, and projects helps build a strong foundation for long term success."*


Priya's journey is a testament to the power of trust, continuous learning, and a growth mindset. Her story reflects how the right opportunities and support can help individuals build meaningful, rewarding careers at Ingram Micro.
