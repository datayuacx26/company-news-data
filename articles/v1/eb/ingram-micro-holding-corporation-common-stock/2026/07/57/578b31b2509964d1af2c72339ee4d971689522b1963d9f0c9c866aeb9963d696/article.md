# Coming Back Stronger: Deepa's Leadership Journey at Ingram Micro


Deepa Dhalwalkar Deputy Manager India


15 July 2026


Some career journeys come full circle and become even more meaningful the second time around.


For Deepa Dhalwalkar, returning to Ingram Micro wasn't just a career move. It was an opportunity to continue growing in a place she already knew valued learning, collaboration, and people.


After first joining Ingram Micro in 2015 and returning in 2023, Deepa has grown from a technical contributor into a leader, guiding teams, supporting others, and helping drive business success.


### A Journey That Came Full Circle


*"My story with Ingram Micro began in 2015 when I joined as a Senior Software Engineer. Those early years were an important learning experience that helped me better understand my strengths and shape my career path."*


After stepping away for a period of time due to personal commitments, Deepa found herself drawn back to the company she had always remembered positively.


*"When I rejoined, it felt like coming back to a familiar place, but with new opportunities to grow and make an impact."*


Returning gave her the chance not only to continue building her expertise, but also to pursue a new professional goal.


### Growing Into Leadership


*"When I came back, I was given the opportunity to lead a team, which aligned perfectly with my aspiration to move into a leadership role."*


Over time, her responsibilities evolved from hands on technical work to mentoring team members, supporting strategic initiatives, and helping shape the success of her team.


Her journey reflects the trust, ownership, and growth opportunities available to associates who are ready to take the next step in their careers.


### Opportunities That Empower Growth


*"I've had the chance to work on challenging projects, explore new technologies, and collaborate with supportive leaders and colleagues. Every experience has helped me grow, not only professionally but personally as well."*


By embracing new challenges and continuously learning, Deepa has developed both the technical expertise and leadership skills needed to thrive in a constantly evolving industry.


### A Culture That Supports Success


One of the things Deepa values most about working at Ingram Micro is the supportive environment and flexibility that allows associates to succeed both professionally and personally.


*"The healthy work life balance makes a real difference. The environment is flexible, supportive, and understanding, which helps you perform at your best while also making time for yourself and your family."*


For Deepa, that balance has been a key part of her long term success and overall well being.


### Advice for Future Talent


*"If you're considering joining Ingram Micro, go for it. Be open to learning, embrace new challenges, and don't be afraid to share your ideas. With curiosity, dedication, and a positive attitude, you'll discover endless opportunities to grow and succeed."*


Deepa's story demonstrates that careers are rarely linear. Sometimes the best opportunities are the ones that bring you back to where your journey began, giving you the chance to grow even further than before.
