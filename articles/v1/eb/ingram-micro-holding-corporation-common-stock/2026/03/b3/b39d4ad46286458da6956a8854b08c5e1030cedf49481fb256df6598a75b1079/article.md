# From Associate Engineer & Software Developer to Impact, Growth, and Collaboration


02 March 2026


When Cedric joined Ingram Micro as an Associate Engineer & Software Developer on the Victors team, he was excited by the opportunity to build solutions that truly matter.


Working in an environment that’s pushing the boundaries of B2B engineering and AI means he’s constantly exposed to new technologies, innovative approaches, and teammates who value fresh ideas.


What stands out most in his journey is the culture of openness. “I feel heard here. When I share a suggestion, it gets thoughtful consideration and that makes the work really rewarding,” he shares. Being part of a team that encourages curiosity, and continuous improvement has accelerated both his technical and professional growth.


Every day brings a new challenge, a new question to ask, and a new opportunity to learn. He’s energized by the impact his work creates and by seeing how software can transform the way teams operate and deliver value.


His advice to future talent?


*Ask the obvious questions and meet people across teams, it accelerates your growth.*
