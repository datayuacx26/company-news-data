## What is Workflow Automation Software for Accounting Firms


Accounting workflow automation refers to software that takes repetitive, rules-based tasks off your team's plate and handles them automatically. Think client onboarding checklists, document requests, deadline tracking, and recurring bookkeeping entries.


For accounting firms, you get less time chasing clients for missing documents and more time on expert-level work.

## Dext


Dext focuses on document capture, using OCR and machine learning to extract vendor names, amounts, and tax details from receipts, invoices, and bank statements before pushing that data into your accounting software.


Here's what's included:


- Connects with 11,500+ banks, e-commerce services, and payment systems, with sync across 30+ accounting tools including QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage
- Mobile app for receipt scanning, mileage tracking, and expense claims on iOS and Android
- Multi-currency support and line-item splitting for complex expense scenarios
- 99.9% data extraction accuracy via AI and OCR (per Dext)


Pricing starts at $34/month for 5 users and 300 documents, scaling to $100/month for 30 users and 4,000 documents. Close management, reconciliations, accruals, and client portal features are not included, so firms needing those will have to bring in additional tools.


Dext works well if receipt OCR is your main gap. For anything beyond that, you're looking at a multi-tool setup.


## Karbon


Karbon is a practice management tool built exclusively for accounting firms. It brings together work management, client communication, and team collaboration in one place, which makes it a strong fit for firms that want visibility across their entire book of work.


The workflow features in Karbon are genuinely well-regarded. You can build out templated workflows, assign tasks, and track job status without hunting through emails or spreadsheets. Client communication is threaded directly into work items, so context stays attached to the right job.


That said, Karbon sits at a higher price point, which can be a hurdle for smaller firms or solo practitioners weighing the cost against the features they'll actually use day to day.


## Jetpack Workflow


Jetpack Workflow has focused on internal[task management and deadline tracking](https://doublehq.com/task-management/) for accounting firms since 2014, helping thousands of firms organize recurring client work.


### What They Offer


- Over 70 pre-built templates covering monthly bookkeeping, 1040 tax returns, payroll processing, and quarterly financials
- Automatic job generation based on recurrence settings, from weekly to custom schedules
- Time tracking that integrates with QuickBooks Online


Pricing starts at $30 per user monthly.


It works well for small firms that want simple deadline tracking without the weight of a full practice management system. The gaps are worth knowing, though. Jetpack is one of the few practice management tools without a client portal, and it has no native integrations beyond QuickBooks. Bank feed automation, reconciliations, invoicing, and AI-powered close features are all absent.


For firms wanting consolidated workflow automation, expect to piece together several other tools alongside it.
