# Introducing Recommended Solutions

This post is about **Solutions** — recommended approaches to our projects.


## Why Solutions?


When we started out with CodeCrafters, we were focused on the *challenge* aspect of the learning experience.


As we signed up new learners and paid attention to feedback (especially critical feedback) — we realised that we had the opportunity to not only challenge our learners, but also share best practices, alternate approaches, and maximise the return on the time invested in solving a stage.


As a starting point, we've now added a **Solution** button on all CodeCrafters stages, for all customers.


## The Solutions Popup


The button launches into a popup which contains a recommended **Diff** that gets you through the stage, as well as an **Explanation** describing the approach.


We recommend initially attempting without referring to the solution. Once you've solved a stage (or attempted a few times), you'll find the Solutions tab a great way to validate your approach or discover an alternate perspective.


Solutions is a new feature currently available on select courses. We're working on incrementally expanding our catalog and bringing it to all courses.


## Get started


You can access the Solutions feature by visiting any stage on a[CodeCrafters course](https://app.codecrafters.io/tracks) . They're live on select courses, and will be live for all over time.
