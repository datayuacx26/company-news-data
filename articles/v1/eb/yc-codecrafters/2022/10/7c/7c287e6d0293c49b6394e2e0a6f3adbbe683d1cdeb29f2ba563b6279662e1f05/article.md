# Vote for new courses

Today we're releasing the[CodeCrafters Vote Page](https://app.codecrafters.io/vote) .


Submit new ideas, vote on existing ones, and help us decide what to launch.


## How it works?


The[CodeCrafters Vote](https://app.codecrafters.io/vote) page already has a few course ideas added, based on requests we've gotten from customers, as well as our own interests. Everyone is allowed to vote — we do require a login for quality control.


Once you complete any course, you receive a supervote. Supervotes carry more weightage, since they come from customers that are most invested.


## Submit new ideas


Have an idea for a *Build your own X* style course? We want to hear them.


If it's not already listed on the vote page, please let us know your idea by filling out the form linked at the top of[the vote page](https://app.codecrafters.io/vote) (takes < 5 min).


On the form, we also have an optional field where you can provide more context, feedback, or expand on the idea. We'll contact you if necessary.


Like with voting, you need to be logged in to submit new ideas.


## Get started


You can access the CodeCrafters Vote page at[its dedicated page](https://app.codecrafters.io/vote) . Anyone can vote, you just need to be logged in. Course completers get supervotes.


Got feedback? Pleasewrite us an email !


We can't wait to listen to your ideas and ship them.
