# Introducing Repo Banners

Your repos just got prettier. Introducing **Banners** .


Show off your progress — in style.


A customer repo[featuring](https://github.com/ugonight/codecrafters-docker-rust) the new banner.


### How it works


When you start a new CodeCrafters project, the` README` automatically includes an animated banner that tracks your progress in real-time.


Each project's banner has a different theme. Your old projects are also eligible to get a banner, you only need to update your README to render it.


Banners are available for both new and old projects


You can grab your banner link from within the **Project Settings dropdown** , for including into your README, or anywhere else.


Hundreds of developers are already showcasing[banners on their repos](https://github.com/search?q=codecrafters&type=repositories&s=updated&o=desc) .


## Get started


You now have something to collect every time your commit passes.


New projects automatically get a banner. To add banners to your existing projects, grab the link from the **Project Settings Dropdown** .
