Today, we're excited to share news of our $1.8M seed fundraise, with participation from some of the world's top developer experts.


Arash Ferdowsi (Dropbox), Mike Krieger (Instagram), and many more.


### Our Investors


We've built the CodeCrafters community one developer at a time.


Our earliest adopters came from niche developer subreddits, programming-focused Slack groups, and our launches on Hacker News.

Where CodeCrafters members work


It's fitting that for our seed fundraise, we partnered with technologists who deeply understand our audience and mission.

### Conclusion


Over the past year, we've experienced healthy growth in all aspects. Most notably, 13x more users completed our core stages.


We'll continue investing in our platform and catalog, to ensure that our customers—whether old or new—can grow more confident in their craft.
