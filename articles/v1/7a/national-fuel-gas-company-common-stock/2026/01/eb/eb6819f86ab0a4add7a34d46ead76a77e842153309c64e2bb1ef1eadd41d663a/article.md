# DDQ Averaging System Alert – Pennsylvania


#### January 28, 2026


DDQs will not run on Friday, January 30th, because of gas purchase considerations due to the end of month occurring on Saturday, January 31st. As a result, DDQs will be running on, Thursday, January 29th, for Friday, January 30th through Monday, February 2nd. During this period, DDQs will be averaged together for January 30th and 31st. February 1st and 2nd will be averaged separately. The next DDQ run will be made on Monday, February 2nd for Tuesday, February 3rd and beyond.
