# Checkr and Intuit partner to streamline background checks for millions of growing businesses

*A new multi-year partnership brings background screening directly into Intuit QuickBooks Online Payroll and Intuit Enterprise Suite, streamlining trusted hiring for millions of small and mid-sized businesses*


[Checkr](https://checkr.com/) , the data platform powering safe and fair decisions, today announced a multi-year, preferred partnership with Intuit, the global financial technology platform that


makes


[Intuit TurboTax](https://turbotax.intuit.com/) ,


[Credit Karma](https://www.creditkarma.com/) ,


[QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/) , and


[Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/) , embedding Checkr’s background check technology directly into


[QuickBooks Online Payroll](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/oa/payroll/) and


[Intuit Enterprise Suite](https://www.intuit.com/enterprise/blog/) , saving businesses time with a more seamless, integrated hiring process. The partnership marks a significant expansion of Checkr’s mission to modernize trusted hiring at scale, and Intuit’s commitment to deliver an integrated, all-in-one platform that provides businesses with everything they need to compete and grow.


As small- and mid-sized businesses face intensifying hiring pressures, from labor shortages to increased compliance complexity, fragmented systems create delays, friction, and risk. With millions of businesses relying on Intuit’s financial technology platform, this partnership represents a significant step in building a unified hiring and


[onboarding](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/hiring/employee-onboarding/) tech stack for US businesses. By bringing background checks into the core


[payroll and HR workflow](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/payroll/hr-and-payroll/) , Intuit and Checkr are helping businesses move faster, stay compliant, and compete for talent in an increasingly complex labor market.


“Small- and mid-sized businesses today are expected to hire with the same speed and sophistication of large enterprises, yet many still rely on fragmented systems and limited resources,” said Kristen Faris, senior vice president of Sales Solutions at Checkr. “Our partnership with Intuit changes that. By embedding Checkr’s background check technology directly into QuickBooks Online Payroll and Intuit Enterprise Suite, we’re making it easier for millions of businesses to hire safely, confidently, and efficiently.”


“Growing businesses want tools that work together seamlessly, without adding complexity to already stretched teams,” said Olivier Bartholot, vice president, segment leader, Intuit Workforce Solutions. “By embedding Checkr’s background check technology directly into QuickBooks Online Payroll and Intuit Enterprise Suite, we’re streamlining the hiring process and increasing trust among employers who are expanding their workforce.”


## Key benefits and integration details


With this integration, scaling businesses will experience a more streamlined hiring process that includes:


- **Seamless hiring workflow** : Background checks are embedded directly into employee profiles within QuickBooks Online Payroll and Intuit Enterprise Suite for real-time status visibility.


- **Operational efficiency** : Automated, faster screening allows businesses to hire quickly and reduce manual administrative work.


- **Trust and compliance** : Checkr’s fair, accurate background check system supports safer hiring decisions and helps businesses navigate complex


[regulatory environments](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/manage-employees/business-compliance/) .


- **Unified experience** : Screening, payroll, and HR tools now live within a singular platform, reducing friction for resource-constrained teams.


## Availability


Checkr’s embedded background check integration is now available to all QuickBooks Online Payroll and Intuit Enterprise Suite customers. Checkr and Intuit will continue to work together to simplify and streamline employer operations, helping businesses compete, stay compliant, and scale.


To start running background checks directly within QuickBooks Online Payroll or Intuit Enterprise Suite, visit the


[Checkr-Intuit integration page](https://help.checkr.com/helpcenter/s/article/Intuit-Checkr-Integration-User-Guide) for activation steps, pricing, setup guides, and to schedule a demo.
