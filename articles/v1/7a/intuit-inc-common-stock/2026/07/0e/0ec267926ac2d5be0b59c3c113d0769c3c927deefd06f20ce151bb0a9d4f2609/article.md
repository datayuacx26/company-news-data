# Double named a platinum partner in Intuit's App Partner Program

[Double](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/app/apps/appdetails/keeper/en-us/?queryID=ef8e5bb775a092920a81be9b61ab9f66) (formerly Keeper), a close and practice management platform built for client accounting services (CAS) teams and bookkeeping firms, today announced its status as a Platinum partner, the highest tier of partnership offered in the new


[Intuit App Partner Program](https://developer.intuit.com/partners) . The designation reflects Double’s deeply embedded integration with


[QuickBooks Online](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/oa/get-quickbooks/) on the Intuit platform.


## A partnership built on ledger-level execution


Double was built around a simple premise: managing and doing the close in the same place. Its two-way integration with QuickBooks connects task execution, client communication, and journal-level work directly to the general ledger. The designation as a Platinum Partner formalizes a partnership that has long been foundational to how Double customers operate.


The Intuit App Partner Program recognizes partners delivering differentiated, high-impact experiences on the Intuit platform. As one of a small group of Platinum partners, Double gains early access to modernized APIs and expanded collaboration with Intuit, supporting deeper ledger-level execution and tighter operational alignment for shared QuickBooks customers.


“We built Double around the ledger from the start, because that’s where the close actually happens,” said Ben Stein, CEO at Double. “Being named a Platinum partner reflects that focus.”


Turning deep integration into real execution


For accounting teams, the difference they feel is structural. Tasks are completed in the same


[workflow](https://www.intuit.com/enterprise/blog/operations/automate-workflows/) as the financial data they affect. Transaction reviews, client questions, and journal entries sync automatically through a two-way connection. When the task list is finished, the ledger is already updated and the close is truly complete.


When close execution and


[ledger](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/bookkeeping/accounting-ledger/) data live in the same system, new capabilities become possible. Double’s recent reconciliation enhancements use full close and ledger context to help teams resolve discrepancies faster. Instead of stopping the workflow to investigate a variance, accountants can see likely causes and reasoning directly within the reconciliation task, then complete and document the close in one place.


“Using Double on top of QuickBooks Online, we cut our final review time by 85%, saving hundreds of hours each month,” said Mike Greenblatt, partner and vice president of operations at


[MCO Bookkeeping](https://www.mcobookkeeping.com/) &


[BeanSquad](https://beansquad.com/about/) . “Because the work and the ledger live together, we’re able to scale without adding complexity.”


For more than two decades, Intuit’s developer ecosystem has supported applications extending QuickBooks for specialized workflows. The new App Partner Program reflects a deeper investment in partners whose products are tightly aligned with how accounting work is performed.


As a Platinum partner, Double reinforces its commitment to ledger-connected close management as a foundation for scale, visibility, and operational confidence for modern accounting teams.


“By building ledger-level execution directly into their workflows, Double is helping accounting teams move faster, reduce friction, and complete the close with greater confidence,” said Josh Hofmann, vice president, Global Partner Ecosystems, Intuit. “Their Platinum partner designation reflects the depth of their integration and the real impact they’re delivering for QuickBooks customers.”


Double rebranded from Keeper in late 2025, reflecting the company’s expanded focus on ledger-connected close execution.
