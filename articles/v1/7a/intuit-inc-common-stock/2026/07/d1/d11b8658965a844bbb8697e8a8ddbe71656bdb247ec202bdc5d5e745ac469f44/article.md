# How Intuit QuickBooks helped the Tooth Fairy find her pixie dust again

The Tooth Fairy may have wings, but she also has a massive business to run. Beyond the magic, she’s the CEO of a global powerhouse managing millions of nightly asset-in, cash-out transactions, a high-stakes fulfillment operation where every minute counts and there’s no room for error. For the modern entrepreneur, even one with wings, doing it yourself can be a surefire way to ground your business. That's why to truly soar to new heights, the Tooth Fairy turned to QuickBooks on the Intuit platform. With Intuit Intelligence, the Tooth Fairy can ask any question and get the benefit of an AI-powered team and trusted experts who respond based on data and insights that are specific to her business. With Intuit Intelligence, she's got her pixie dust back, is outdoing her financial best and kids are getting paid faster. Now that's a different kind of magic!


## From magical realism to modern results


The Tooth Fairy’s whimsical journey from doing it all herself to outdoing it with QuickBooks is the centerpiece of our latest brand campaign created with BBDO New York. Introduced to the stage during the 2026 Winter Olympics Opening Ceremony on February 6, our creative vision for this campaign reimagines the Tooth Fairy as a modern CEO, because even icons have overhead. By using “magical realism” to personify the chaos of administrative backlogs, our new campaign illustrates a universal truth: When you’re sidelined by the business of doing business, you can’t focus on the magic that started it all.


In a comedic turn, the television spot features NHL star Brady Tkachuk, who anchors the Tooth Fairy’s cultural status, proving that when the Tooth Fairy is sidelined by paperwork, everyone (even a 6’4” power forward) notices. Because every legend needs a back office, the campaign also features athletes Alex Morgan and Jamie Anderson, entrepreneurs who trade the world stage for the boardroom as they scale their own ventures. Their involvement reinforces another truism: to find your “pixie dust” again, you have to outdo the manual tasks that can pull you in a dozen directions.


Our goal has always been to multiply what our customers are capable of, and with Intuit Intelligence, we are taking that further; moving beyond simple data to provide the real-time answers and actionable guidance business owners need to scale. Whether you’re an elite athlete or a folkloric icon, outdoing your best requires moving past the manual and into a future where the work is done for you. Part of Intuit QuickBooks’ Outdo brand
