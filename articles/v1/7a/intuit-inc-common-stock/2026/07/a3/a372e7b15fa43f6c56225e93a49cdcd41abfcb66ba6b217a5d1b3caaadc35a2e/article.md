# QuickBooks expands into Claude and ChatGPT with new features | QuickBooks

## This is what purpose-built AI for small business looks like


AI is increasingly where people go first for financial decisions, and those decisions carry real consequences for cash flow, teams, and livelihoods. That's why it matters that the AI you're using is grounded in real, current data from the system that actually runs your business.


As a QuickBooks customer, you can continue to power your Claude and ChatGPT experiences with the right context to your data and your business, and also take action to make informed financial decisions with confidence.


The benchmarks, patterns, and insights built into QuickBooks reflect your real-world financial behavior at scale, turned into practical guidance you can act on immediately via the QuickBooks connector in Claude and plugin in ChatGPT.


And because QuickBooks is a native connector, it works alongside everything else you've plugged into Claude and ChatGPT: draft the proposal, turn it into an estimate, send the invoice, and follow up on payment from your customer, all in the same session where you run the rest of your day.


This is what purpose-built AI for small business looks like.


## Your data, your control


Trust is the foundation of everything we build and financial data remains yours. When you connect the QuickBooks connector in Claude or plugin in ChatGPT, we selectively apply your data to power trusted, accurate responses at your request. Your data stays within our Intuit systems and is never used to train foundation models. You retain full control over your business, and every action remains reviewable and human-verified.


## What's coming next


This is only the beginning. Upcoming features include:


- Payroll done-for-you experiences and actions: Run payroll, manage compensation changes, and import payroll data from other AI platforms and tools.


- Personalized lending offers: Surface your own Term Loan or line-of-credit options, not just peer benchmarks.


- Creditworthiness intelligence: Deeper insight into the factors that can affect your eligibility for financing.


With the QuickBooks connector in Claude and plugin in ChatGPT—now expanded with sales, payroll, and capital capabilities—you can move beyond simple answers to actionable results for your business, right inside your conversation.
