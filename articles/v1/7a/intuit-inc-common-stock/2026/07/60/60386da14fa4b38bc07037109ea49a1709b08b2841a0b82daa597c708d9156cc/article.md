# G-Accon joins Intuit App Partner Program as Platinum Partner to drive automated financial reporting at scale

*Google Sheets-native platform earns top-tier status, giving accounting firms earlier access to QuickBooks API updates and deeper reporting capabilities*


[G-Accon](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/app/apps/appdetails/g_accon/en-us/) , a Google Sheets-native platform for financial reporting and accounting automation, today announced its participation in Intuit’s new


[App Partner Program](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/news/app-partner-program-drives-growth/) as a Platinum-tier member. The designation reflects G-Accon’s standing as one of the top-performing


[QuickBooks Online](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/) integrations available to accounting firms, bookkeepers, and finance teams worldwide.


As a Platinum partner, G-Accon gains early access to Intuit’s updated APIs before changes roll out publicly. For accountants and finance teams who depend on G-Accon daily, that means fewer disruptions, faster fixes, and a more reliable connection between QuickBooks and


[Google Sheets](https://workspace.google.com/products/sheets/) . The company also receives co-marketing support from Intuit, and access to usage analytics that will shape future product development.


“Platinum access means we're able to stay closely aligned with the new capabilities and features QuickBooks delivers to its customers,” said Andrey Kustarnikov, CEO and founder, G-Accon. “This means we can proactively ensure a


[seamless experience](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/gaccon_for_quickbooks/652300792306) for the firms running critical reports at scale. Having worked with QuickBooks since 2017, this deeper collaboration helps us deliver even more reliable, uninterrupted reporting for our shared customers.”


The Intuit App Partner Program is designed to help third-party partners grow faster, scale smarter, and deliver more value to the millions of small- and


[mid-market businesses](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/news/intuit-enterprise-suite-new-era-of-growth/) and accountants on the Intuit platform. Platinum partners receive early access to new and modernized APIs, co-marketing opportunities, and analytics to better understand app usage. Intuit has supported a


developer ecosystem


for more than two decades, and the new


[Apps Partner Program](https://developer.intuit.com/partners) reflects a deeper investment in the partners behind the most-used integrations.


G-Accon already handles two-way sync, multi-entity consolidation, live dashboards, and automated report delivery for more than 10,000 businesses worldwide. G2 ranked the company #1 in several categories in its Spring 2026 reports, including the Momentum Grid and Grid Report for Google Workspace for Finance, awarding it 29 badges across 92 reports. These rankings were finalized March 17, 2026. The Intuit Platinum partnership gives those capabilities a stronger foundation going forward.


“G-Accon’s participation in our program as a Platinum Partner reflects our


shared commitment to helping accountants deliver value to their clients,” said Joshua Hofmann, vice president, Global Partner Ecosystems, Intuit. “And as a Platinum Partner, our shared accounting customers can have confidence that G-Accon meets the highest platform integration standards and requirements.”
