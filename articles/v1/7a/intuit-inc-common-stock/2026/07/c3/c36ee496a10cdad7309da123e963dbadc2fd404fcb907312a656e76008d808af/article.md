[Intuit TurboTax](https://turbotax.intuit.com/) ,

[QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/) , and


[Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/) ,


today the launch of


****[Intuit Accountant Suite](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/accountants/intuit-accountant-suite/) , a new AI-native platform that provides accounting


firms with everything they need to scale and


manage their clients, firms, and teams, all in one place.

Intuit Intelligence, a revolutionary


**system of intelligence,** that in the hands of accountants will allow them to harness the power of Intuit’s embedded


[AI agents](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/news/virtual-team-of-ai-agents/) and connected data to access insights and recommendations that scale their own businesses and deliver advisory services that fuel their clients’ success.

## Intuit Accountant Suite: The power of the Intuit platform in the hands of accounting firms to fuel their efficiency and growth


Accounting firms play an important role in helping businesses succeed. Yet, like many businesses, they face complexity as they juggle multiple tools, disconnected systems, and time-consuming compliance work. These challenges often limit their ability to deliver higher-value advisory services and scale effectively.


Intuit Accountant Suite eliminates these challenges with the power of the Intuit platform and system of intelligence in the hands of accounting firms.


With


Intuit Intelligence and access to embedded AI agents, accountants will have access to done-for-you experiences,


insights, and recommendations


that drive confident, faster decision-making for their clients, firms, and teams


.


Accountants will


get immediate and accurate answers to any business question, from who’s my most profitable client to which staff is underutilized.


They also will be able to complete tasks automatically, from running a client’s payroll to creating firm-wide and client-specific reports and dashboards because of the secure access to Intuit and client data and data from third-party systems.


And when added capacity is needed, Intuit Accountant Suite will allow firms to tap into Intuit’s trusted experts who can serve as an extension of their virtual team.


Intuit Accountant Suite is tailored to meet firm needs with the seamless integration of five core capabilities powered by Intuit’s platform: client management, client collaboration, service delivery, business planning, and team management.


Intuit Accountant Suite provides:


- **Consolidated client management:** Manage all client files in one place with a single sign-in, providing better insight across services provided and eliminating data exports or multiple account logins.


- **Custom dashboard:** Ensure nothing falls through the cracks with each team member’s ability to customize their home base, clearly surfacing critical tasks, reminders, and AI-powered insights.


- **Role-based access controls:** Delegate tasks with customizable roles and permissions, enabling compliance and efficient management of accounting, bookkeeping, payroll services, and more.


- **Unique client identification:** Assign a firm-specific Client Number to each client for streamlined searching, tracking, and billing, reducing manual effort.


- **Optimized performance:** Experience fast and responsive performance, regardless of client volume, with a modern infrastructure designed for scalability.


- **Books close at scale (Beta):** Standardize the review and close process with customizable templates and progress tracking to ensure ontime client delivery.


- **AI-powered Client Insights (Beta):** A new Client Insights feature provides firm-wide client analytics and benchmarking for more-informed business decision making.


"One of the hardest parts of running a firm is staying connected to the pulse of the business beyond day-to-day client work,” said


**Dan Luthi** , partner,


[Ignite Spot](https://www.ignitespot.com/) . “The foundation being built with Intuit Accountant Suite has the potential to reshape how we visualize and respond to what’s happening in our practice. The promise of fast, intuitive insights could truly be a game-changer."


## Coming Soon: More AI-powered workflows and insights to help firms scale


****


In the coming months, Intuit Accountant Suite will continue to evolve. With the power of Intuit’s platform and system of intelligence,


**AI-powered tools and automations** designed to improve capacity planning, productivity, accuracy, decision making, and collaboration will be added, helping firms scale and run more smoothly with less overhead.


- **Capacity planning:** A new capacity planning module will deliver data insights into firm-wide staffing utilization and make recommendations to help ensure no one is over-subscribed or under-utilized.


- **Collaboration tools: Soon, document sharing capabilities, integrated web-conferencing, meeting scheduling, and AI note-taking capabilities will enhance firm efficiencies.**
- **AI-powered analysis and anomaly detection:** Monitor client portfolios, proactively identify key financial trends, and flag anomalies across payroll, accounts payable, and bill pay data.


- **Seamless books to tax integration:** Easily sync client tax data with


[Intuit ProConnect Tax](https://accountants.intuit.com/tax-software/tax-online/) , saving time and reducing data entry errors.


## Availability


[Intuit Accountant Suite](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/accountants/intuit-accountant-suite/) is currently available to US-based accounting firms at no cost, with plans to expand into markets outside of the US in the near future. Future pricing for Intuit Accountant Suite will be based on functionality and determined by market.
