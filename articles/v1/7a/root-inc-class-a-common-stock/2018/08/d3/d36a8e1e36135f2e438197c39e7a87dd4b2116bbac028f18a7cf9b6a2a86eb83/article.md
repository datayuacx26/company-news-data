# Filing a claim is easy with Root

#### First things first


Getting into an accident is distressing for everyone. We provide a guide in the app about what to do after an accident— *including practical things like safety measures and not admitting guilt.* Take a look to get prepared ahead of time.


Remember,[Comprehensive and](https://www.joinroot.com/coverage#comprehensive-overview)[Collision coverages](https://www.joinroot.com/coverage#collision-overview) are optional, while[Liability](http://blog.joinroot.com/how-to-choose-liability-bodily-injury-property-damage) is mostly required everywhere. But, if you cause an accident—or someone else does, but they leave the scene—or you or Mother Nature cause damage to your car, Comprehensive and Collision are coverages you’ll likely be grateful to have.


Check out our post on[how to choose Comprehensive and Collision coverages](https://www.joinroot.com/blog/how-to-choose-comprehensive-and-collision-coverage/) to learn more.


#### What happens after you file a claim?


After you[submit your claim](https://www.joinroot.com/claims/) , a claims expert will get in touch to review it and discuss next steps.


Hopefully, you won’t ever need to file a claim, but being insured is your safety net when the unexpected happens and things do go wrong. And when and if that happens, we’ve got you covered.


Do you have[Rental coverage](https://www.joinroot.com/coverage#rental-overview) to help you get a rental car after a covered accident?


#### If the time comes, and you need to file a claim


We hope you never have to use the claims feature. But, if you do, hopefully you’ll feel better knowing how simple and quick it is to file your claim online or in the app.
