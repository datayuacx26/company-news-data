# Reach Selected for Panasonic Accelerator Program

#### Program Advances Wireless Power Energy Solutions for Industrial and Smart Infrastructure Applications


March 4, 2025 – Redwood City, Calif –[Reach Power](http://www.reachpower.com/) (Reach) announced today it has been selected as one of five companies to join the[Panasonic Accelerator by Electric Works Company](https://panasonic.regacy-innovation.com/) , an exclusive innovation program led by[Panasonic Corporation](https://www.panasonic.com/global/about.html) and[ReGACY Innovation Group Inc](https://regacy-innovation.com/) . Launched in 2021, this initiative fosters co-creation with cutting-edge startups to transform “Life Infrastructure” and build a more sustainable future.


Yoshinori Hadama, Manager of Electric Works Company, Panasonic Corporation, said, “Panasonic has long seen wireless power transfer as a key driver of future commercial innovation. After reviewing industrial use cases, we see tremendous potential in partnering with Reach to develop high-power wireless solutions for the Japanese market. By combining Reach’s innovation with Panasonic’s expertise, we can redefine power delivery and enhance efficiency across industries.”


Chris Davlantes, Founder and CEO of Reach, added, “We are honored to be selected for this prestigious program. Collaborating with Panasonic, a global technology and electronics leader, provides an invaluable opportunity for our team to develop real-world applications that solve critical challenges.”


As part of the program, Reach is collaborating with Panasonic to develop Factory 4.0 solutions, with a primary focus on wirelessly powering cameras and sensors in smart manufacturing environments to enhance automation and operational efficiency. This initiative aims to eliminate power constraints in industrial settings, enabling seamless data collection, monitoring, and control without the need for wired connections or frequent battery replacements. Additionally, Reach and Panasonic plan to explore future applications for wireless power in smart meters and persistent drone operations, expanding the impact of this technology across various industrial and infrastructure sectors.


Throughout the program, Reach will collaborate with Panasonic to refine, validate, and explore the commercialization and large-scale deployment of these concepts.


#### About Reach


Reach is the industry leader in Wireless Power-at-a-Distance and a pioneer in future force energy technology, revolutionizing power delivery much like Wi-Fi transformed data transmission. Our patented innovations—high-efficiency power beaming, network optimization, and adaptive electromagnetic surfaces—enable a scalable, safe, and flexible power platform. Designed for government, defense, public safety, manufacturing, and logistics, Reach eliminates wired infrastructure and battery constraints, enhancing efficiency and enabling on-demand power for mission-critical applications.
