## **Post-Quantum Cryptography: Building the Foundation for Long-Term Security**


The quantum threat is no longer theoretical. Organizations operating in regulated and high-security sectors need to act now and BlackBerry UEM is already on the path forward. BlackBerry is advancing its post-quantum cryptography roadmap with a significant upgrade to cryptographic libraries directly on devices, aligned to the NIST post-quantum standards framework (NIST 203–205). These protections are being embedded at the device level across BlackBerry® UEM Server and BlackBerry® Dynamics™ applications, supporting progress toward FIPS 140-3 post-quantum accreditation.


For organizations in regulated environments, this is a clear, credible, and accelerating path to long-term cryptographic assurance. Quantum-resilient security is becoming a near-term requirement, and organizations need a clear path to prepare.


## **Sovereign File Sharing: Absolute Control Over Your Most Sensitive Content**


When it comes to sensitive information, organizations operating in sovereign environments cannot afford to rely on conventional enterprise file sync and share (EFSS) solutions or legacy managed file transfer (MFT) tools to secure data in motion. BlackBerry® Workspaces™ is purpose-built for this challenge and has established itself as the sovereign EFSS platform of choice for organizations that demand uncompromising control over their most critical files.


What sets BlackBerry Workspaces apart is its sovereign-grade architecture: digital rights management security is embedded directly into every file. This means your data remains under your control regardless of where it goes, whether it's shared internally, with partners, or across organizational boundaries. This approach provides persistent, file-level protection that maintains control as files move across environments. Shaped by real sovereign and non-sovereign customer requirements and with further significant updates in progress for later this year, BlackBerry Workspaces is a powerful sovereign-ready file control solution.
