# BlackBerry Secure Communications Solutions Are Zebra Validated

## What Is Zebra Validation Testing?


Zebra's testing program offers two levels of application verification: Compatible and Validation. Validation is the more rigorous of the two.


Achieving Validation status means an application has passed interoperability testing that confirms intended functionality under common customer usage scenarios on specific Zebra devices. The testing is designed to show that software and device features work together reliably in realistic conditions.


For organizations that depend on mission-critical communications, tested performance under realistic operating conditions provides a stronger basis for deployment decisions than vendor assurances alone.


## How Validation Supports Frontline Operations


BlackBerry AtHoc enables organizations to send and receive critical alerts during emergencies and operational events. BlackBerry SecuSUITE delivers secure voice, messaging, and communications capabilities for organizations with stringent security requirements.


When deployed on Zebra mobile computers, these solutions support workers in environments where communication reliability directly affects safety, coordination, and operational effectiveness. A delayed alert or dropped secure call can interrupt emergency response or create gaps in situational awareness.


Zebra Validation confirms that BlackBerry AtHoc and BlackBerry SecuSUITE have been tested with Zebra hardware, reducing the risk of compatibility issues in the field and supporting more consistent deployment and communications experiences.

Zebra Validation provides several advantages for organizations evaluating or deploying these solutions:

Zebra positions validation testing as a way to strengthen customer confidence, support solution differentiation, improve customer satisfaction, and confirm interoperability between software solutions and Zebra devices.
