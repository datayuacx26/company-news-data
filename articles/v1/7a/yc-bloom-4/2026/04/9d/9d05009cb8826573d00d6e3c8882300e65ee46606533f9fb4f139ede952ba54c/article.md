# Refer Friends, Earn Free Credits


Invite friends with your unique referral link and earn free credits as they build with Bloom.


You can now earn free Bloom credits by inviting friends to build with Bloom.


Open the new


**Rewards**


tab in


**Settings**


to find your unique referral link. Share it with a friend, and you’ll both earn credits as they complete milestones in Bloom.


It’s a simple way to bring more builders into Bloom and get extra credits for your next app.
