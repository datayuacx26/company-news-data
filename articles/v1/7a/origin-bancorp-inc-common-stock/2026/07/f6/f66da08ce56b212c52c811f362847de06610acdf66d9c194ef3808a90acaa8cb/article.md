# Origin Bank Hires Brad Young as Vice President, | Origin Bank

Origin Bank announced today it has hired Brad Young as Vice President, Commercial Relationship Banker at its Highland Colony Financial Center in Ridgeland, Mississippi.


“Origin is thrilled to welcome Brad to our team,” said Larry Ratzlaff, Origin’s Mississippi state president. “His extensive industry knowledge and dedication to relationship-driven commercial banking will be a major asset to our customers, while his collaborative spirit makes him a perfect fit for our culture.”


Young has more than twenty years of experience in the financial industry, specializing in commercial banking, business development, lending, treasury management services and relationship-focused banking across a wide range of industries.


In his new role, Young will be responsible for developing commercial banking relationships with deposit and lending solutions that suit individual customer needs.


A graduate of The University of Alabama, Young is actively involved in the Mississippi business community. He supports the American Heart Association and is a longtime advocate for St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital. He and his wife Cyndi have been married for fourteen years and enjoy an active lifestyle together.


Young is based at Origin’s Highland Colony Financial Center located at 1063 Highland Colony Parkway in Ridgeland, Mississippi. For more information or to schedule an appointment, please contact the banking center at (601) 856-3380.


**About Origin**


Origin Bancorp, Inc. is a financial holding company headquartered in Ruston, Louisiana. Origin’s wholly owned bank subsidiary, Origin Bank, was founded in 1912 in Choudrant, Louisiana. Deeply rooted in Origin’s history is a culture committed to providing personalized relationship banking to businesses, municipalities, and personal clients to enrich the lives of the people in the communities it serves. Origin provides a broad range of financial services and currently operates more than 55 locations in Dallas/Fort Worth, East Texas, Houston, North Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama and the Florida Panhandle. For more information, visit www.origin.bank. Member FDIC. Equal Housing Lender.
