# Origin Bank Promotes Zach Ervin to Vice President, | Origin Bank

Origin Bank announced today it has promoted Zach Ervin to Vice President, Commercial Relationship Banker. Ervin has served Longview and surrounding areas as a commercial banker at Origin Bank since 2022.


“In his four years at Origin, Zach has embodied our culture of excellence,” said Lori Sirman, president of Origin Bank’s East Texas region. “This well-deserved promotion reflects not only his achievements, but the positive impact he continues to make on our customers, team and community.”


Ervin has more than eight years of experience in the financial industry, starting his career as a credit analyst before moving to commercial lending. In his new role, he will focus on providing solutions for small and large businesses including commercial and industrial lines of credit and commercial real estate loans.


He earned a Master of Business Administration and Bachelor of Arts in Finance and Economics from East Texas Baptist University. He is currently a board member of Junior Achievement of East Texas and plans to expand his community service as part of his new role. He and his wife Abbey share twin girls, Nora and Hazel.


Ervin is based at Origin’s Longview Financial Center located at 3805 N. Spur 63 in Longview. For more information or to schedule an appointment, please contact the banking center at (903) 212-9000.


**About Origin**


Origin Bancorp, Inc. is a financial holding company headquartered in Ruston, Louisiana. Origin’s wholly owned bank subsidiary, Origin Bank, was founded in 1912 in Choudrant, Louisiana. Deeply rooted in Origin’s history is a culture committed to providing personalized relationship banking to businesses, municipalities, and personal clients to enrich the lives of the people in the communities it serves. Origin provides a broad range of financial services and currently operates more than 55 locations in East Texas, Dallas/Fort Worth, Houston, North Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama and the Florida Panhandle. For more information, visit www.origin.bank. Member FDIC. Equal Housing Lender.
