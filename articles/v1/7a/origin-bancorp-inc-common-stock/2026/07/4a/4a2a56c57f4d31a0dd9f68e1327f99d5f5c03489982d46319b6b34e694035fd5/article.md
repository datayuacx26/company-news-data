# Origin Bank Welcomes Klein, Merritt and Reily | Origin Bank

Origin Bank today announced it has hired three banking professionals to join its team in North Texas. The addition of John Klein, Caroline Merritt and Mark H. Reily further strengthens Origin's ability to deliver excellent service, build relationships and meet the ever-changing financial needs of its customers.


“John, Caroline and Mark each bring valuable expertise to our growing team and strengthen our ability to serve our customers in the Dallas market," said Warrie Birdwell, North Texas regional president at Origin Bank. "Investing in exceptional talent is a key part of our strategy to ensure we remain responsive to our customers’ needs today while helping them achieve their goals for the future."


**John Klein** joins Origin as a Relationship Manager at Origin’s Preston Financial Center in Dallas. Klein has more than 21 years of experience in banking, specializing in real estate, middle market and small business lending. In his role, he will develop and manage commercial banking relationships, helping clients with financial solutions that support their businesses while also contributing to the Origin team as a resource and mentor. Klein earned a Master of Business Administration from Southern Methodist University and a Bachelor of Arts in Economics from The University of Texas. He is an active member of the Church of the Incarnation, where he serves on its Foundation Board of Directors. He and his wife Emily share two children, John and Martin.


**Caroline Merritt** serves as a Commercial Portfolio Manager II at Origin’s Dallas Financial Center in Dallas. She brings more than five years of experience in the financial industry to her role, specializing in middle market commercial and industrial lending. At Origin, Merritt will assist team members with managing and growing commercial portfolios through credit analysis, risk management, client support and personalized consultative services. A certified public accountant, she earned a Master of Business Administration from Mercer University and a Bachelor of Business Administration from The University of Mississippi. She is an active member of the Cary Council non-profit organization as well as the Dallas/Fort Worth chapter of the Association for Corporate Growth.


**Mark H. Reily** is a Regional Credit Officer at Origin’s Dallas Financial Center in Dallas. He has more than 46 years of banking industry experience specializing in working with high net worth individuals and families, producing and managing financial portfolios, and evaluating credit risks for customers. At Origin, he will provide personalized credit risk analysis for high net worth individuals, cultivating relationships to identify loan options that best suit customer needs. He and his wife Jane share four children and two grandchildren.


To learn more, please visit Origin.bank or call 888-292-4037 for 24/7 customer service.


**About Origin**


Origin Bancorp, Inc. is a financial holding company headquartered in Ruston, Louisiana. Origin’s wholly owned bank subsidiary, Origin Bank, was founded in 1912 in Choudrant, Louisiana. Deeply rooted in Origin’s history is a culture committed to providing personalized relationship banking to businesses, municipalities, and personal clients to enrich the lives of the people in the communities it serves. Origin provides a broad range of financial services and currently operates more than 57 locations in Dallas/Fort Worth, East Texas, Houston, North Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama and the Florida Panhandle. For more information, visit www.origin.bank. Member FDIC. Equal Housing Lender.
