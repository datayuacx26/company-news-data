# September 08, 2025 BlackLine Celebrates Top Companies Driving the Future of Financial Operations Honorees include American Express Global Business Travel, Wendy's, and Cavco, recognized for achieving significant transformational outcomes

**LOS ANGELES – September 8, 2025** –[BlackLine](https://www.blackline.com/) (Nasdaq: BL), the future-ready platform for the Office of the CFO, today announced the winners of its 2025 Modern Accounting Awards. This year’s recipients, including


American Express Global Business Travel, Cavco Industries, and The Wendy’s Company, represent some of the world’s most respected brands and have each driven remarkable outcomes through strategic finance transformation.


The awards celebrate organizations that have fully embraced future-ready financial operations, demonstrating exceptional leadership, rapid time to value, and lasting impact using BlackLine’s market-leading platform.


**2025** **Modern Accounting Award Winners**


-


**American Express Global Business Travel**
American Express Global Business Travel (Amex GBT) has strategically leveraged a broad suite of BlackLine solutions to enhance automation, strengthen its SOX control environment, and drive global efficiency. Central to this success is its Blackline Centre of Excellence with measurable and significant results delivered year-to-date including 6 FTEs redeployed through reconciliation enhancements, journals automation and reporting dashboards. Amex GBT has already achieved 60% auto-certification of reconciliations and over 90% auto-matching in high-volume transactional reconciliations, with ongoing journals automation projected to automate 50% manual journal entries. These outcomes represent a milestone in Amex GBT’s multi-year transformation roadmap, with full results expected to deliver enterprise-wide efficiencies aligned with long-term strategy. .


-


**Cavco Industries**
[Cavco Industries](https://www.cavco.com/) , a valued BlackLine customer for over a decade, exemplifies a journey of continuous growth and finance transformation. By consistently leveraging BlackLine's ongoing innovations, Cavco has progressively expanded its footprint, moving from foundational financial close automation to embracing our latest solutions, including the recent addition of Financial Reporting Analytics. This strategic evolution has enabled them to modernize and automate once-manual consolidation processes, resulting in accelerated reporting, eliminated errors, and providing their leadership with crucial real-time visibility. This successful, ongoing partnership continues to inspire broader adoption across additional business units, serving as a testament to how a long-term, collaborative commitment fuels continuous innovation and creates scalable, lasting transformation.


-


**The Wendy’s Company**
As a global enterprise,[Wendy’s](https://www.wendys.com/) rapidly replaced its legacy systems with BlackLine to deliver both immediate and sustained value. The swift deployment of Account Reconciliations and Transaction Matching streamlined operations, reinforced compliance, and unlocked new efficiencies at scale, all while seamlessly integrating with their Oracle ERP.


“We’re proud to recognize these organizations not only for what they’ve achieved—but for how they’ve led,” said Owen Ryan, co-CEO and Chairman of the Board at BlackLine. “They exemplify the mindset of the future of finance: bold, technology-enabled, and relentlessly focused on strategic value. Their results reinforce what we know to be true—BlackLine is the platform of choice for finance & accounting leaders committed to lasting transformation.”


All winners will be honored at BlackLine’s annual global conference, **BeyondTheBlack™** , where they will share their transformation journeys with thousands of finance and accounting professionals. The North America 2025 event will be held in Las Vegas on September 8 -10, 2025.


To learn more and register, visit[beyondtheblack.com](https://beyondtheblack.com/) .
