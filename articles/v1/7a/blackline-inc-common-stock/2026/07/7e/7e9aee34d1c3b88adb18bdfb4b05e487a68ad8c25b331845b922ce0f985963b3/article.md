## Why the Traditional Month-End Close Stifles Enterprise Strategy


Historically, accounting teams have worked in intense, cyclical bursts. The end of each period initiates a manual "fire drill" of balance sheet reconciliations, journal entries, and data consolidation. This reactive posture means that by the time the CFO receives financial statements, the data is already weeks old.


To break this cycle, organizations must transition to[continuous accounting](https://www.blackline.com/resources/glossaries/continuous-accounting/) . Instead of saving all verification tasks for period-end, activities like[transaction matching](https://www.blackline.com/products/financial-close/transaction-matching/) and balance sheet reconciliations are performed continuously. This approach distributes the workload evenly throughout the month, paving the way for an on-demand close.


By eliminating process latency, the CFO moves from reporting on past results to shaping future outcomes. The finance function becomes a strategic partner at the executive table, utilizing real-time financial indicators to guide strategic planning, mergers and acquisitions (M&A) preparation, and corporate governance.
