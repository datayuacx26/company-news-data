## What are Accruals in Accounting?


At its core,[accrual accounting](https://www.blackline.com/solutions/accruals/) is a fundamental concept that ensures revenues and expenses are recognized in the period in which they are earned or incurred, regardless of when cash is exchanged. This adherence to the matching principle provides a more accurate and transparent picture of a company's financial performance and is a strict requirement under Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP).


The process involves identifying and accounting for a wide range of financial activities, including vendor accruals for unpaid invoices, payroll accruals for earned wages, accrued revenue for services completed but not yet billed, and various interest and tax accruals.


However, the manual process of identifying unrecorded transactions, calculating estimates, and creating journal entries is often inefficient and prone to error, which can prevent the accurate and timely closing of the books.


## Why Manual Accruals Processes Are Unsustainable


The traditional, manual approach to accruals is unsustainable in the modern business environment, creating significant risk and inefficiency. The core challenges are deeply rooted in outdated processes and a lack of intelligent technology:


• **Fragmented Systems** : In most organizations, critical data is siloed across multiple, disconnected systems like procurement tools, HR platforms, and ERPs. This fragmentation forces accountants to manually consolidate information, which is not only inefficient but also significantly increases the risk of error during data transfer.


• **Inaccurate and Incomplete Data** : Manual work is inherently prone to costly errors, where even a misplaced decimal in an accrual calculation can lead to material misstatements. Teams also struggle to collect timely information from other departments, resulting in delays and incomplete data that further compromise accuracy.


• **Time-Consuming Manual Work** : The process often relies on spreadsheets, which forces teams to spend significant time on repetitive, low-value tasks like data entry and reconciliation. This manual effort diverts focus from strategic analysis and contributes to a high-stress close period that stretches for a week or more.


• **Difficult Estimations** : Estimating expenses without a formal invoice is a major challenge that requires significant and often frustrating follow-up with vendors and internal budget owners. This makes it difficult to calculate accruals with consistency and confidence each period.


• **Compliance and Audit Risks** : Manual processes often lack a clear, easily accessible audit trail. An inability to quickly produce supporting documentation, like a purchase order or email confirmation for an accrual, can challenge audit approval and increase the risk of non-compliance with regulations like the[Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)](https://www.blackline.com/resources/glossaries/sox-compliance/) .
