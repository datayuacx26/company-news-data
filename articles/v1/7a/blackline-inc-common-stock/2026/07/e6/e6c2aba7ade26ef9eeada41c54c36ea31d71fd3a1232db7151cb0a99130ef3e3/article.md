# BlackLine Announces Date for Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call

LOS ANGELES, July 21, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[BlackLine, Inc](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=74uyYfXHBX1qDdR8ul2CSBWOtmW0iWKTl98UwUee7Dw4jWxAPspg9RsYOpKNeX168ohauxiOetKPU6GyQZgUYV8BRSVddzKG3Hbwsh-zT_c=)[.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=J_tuW46PI6Gps1m4o44voqjgipbl6jFYC1sYN2HbPZdCYaqP0tTchHmsjn3ChDy5t_Rz58KdgyXMu_BmdwRd6Q==) (Nasdaq: BL) announced today that it will release financial results for the first quarter ended June 30, 2026 after market close on Tuesday, August 4, 2026 followed by a conference call hosted by management at 2:00 p.m. PT / 5:00 p.m. ET. A live webcast and replay will be accessible on BlackLine’s investor relations website at[https://investors.blackline.com/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=hD1xQGB-ig_2pz61Ostx-_AlnQCFneZxVtTT3Fv9UPsdFVrO_YUTRLlj89IJWjzTfq0p1JnzXmi7cF2AUdN6k2y4VXqHMeppd8XzAGjQ0SfGDflsiZlS-4G-GVPKfvCh) . To access the conference call by phone, please register[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=qoD5T3qDEg4WwVz-NSdXQNLII39cNrdsD6wvUJXe42418A_pp7eGOJgAGMfxG5xGvKhyHujjYMSKfvjDUzfio5GV3OvBvu_Hd7BGg26BVSfBPVbU7u73_JoyeoQzbAB0aoTUJAvkymHAu88AB5Kvohwc45tDVIBD4ga3NHR2CoY=) , and dial-in details will be provided.
