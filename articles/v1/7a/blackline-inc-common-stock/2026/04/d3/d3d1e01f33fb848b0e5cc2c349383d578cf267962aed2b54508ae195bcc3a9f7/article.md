# BlackLine Announces Date for First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call

LOS ANGELES, April 21, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[BlackLine, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=8jNyxGMOwjZu7X0BBSOKV67ly4J4MgD1vyfRg0HYgyuudm6Q0PZsXl9xIT8druBm3W-6ZKcEUMw0fUYDTwSJfeOTBIZvJZTNtjwuyXz4foU=) (Nasdaq: BL) announced today that it will release financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026 after market close on Tuesday, May 5, 2026 followed by a conference call hosted by management at 2:00 p.m. PT / 5:00 p.m. ET. A live webcast and replay will be accessible on BlackLine’s investor relations website at[https://investors.blackline.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=2ANwln62OOB_4x3eJ-EqKEmncdZVhrUJyenqPrnDtZXfkpeoiGpKUnhk1eeYq1KLEiAmPvnvV1Qu7ZCLKG3bn7hT3OPuQ6WqTeuSkUWEw5xvwvjLYkzyLx4DFP3qzu8M) . To access the conference call by phone, please register[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=lhrm2gK_dS_2x0MbTiCrP_DQEkH4yOgp2ThWBC8ujNuSjcSDMvA1jCNgTjpkhlBi9jocJQL6l2hCUTMusXxQJZiJFHGkLyGcedDD7sraFBqMCujygQ8O21D-zcXhY9BEM9ri2X82tqrKrEmZzMCAvKFQ05WXSIih6cUwS06obi4=) , and dial-in details will be provided.
