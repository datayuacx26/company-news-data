# Mercury Insurance Named in U.S. News & World Report's 2024-25 Best Companies to Work For

LOS ANGELES


, Sept. 25, 2024


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / --[Mercury Insurance](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=2477010571&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mercuryinsurance.com%2F&a=Mercury+Insurance) has been named by U.S. News & World Report as one of the 2024-25 Best Companies to Work For. The recognition by the national publication is a first for Mercury.


"We are deeply honored to be recognized as one of the top companies in the country by U.S. News & World Report. It's a reflection of our dedication to evolving with our team members' needs," stated Heidi Sullivan Lopez


, Vice President, Chief Human Capital Officer for Mercury. "We prioritize our people's growth and well-being, ensuring they have the resources to thrive both personally and professionally. Seeing so many of our team members choose to stay with us for years is a testament to the supportive environment we've built—one that feels more like a family than just a workplace."


U.S. News' ratings reflect the evolving sentiments that factor into employee decision making when choosing the ideal company. The ratings take into account a variety of factors, including pay, and benefits, work-life balance and flexibility, job and company stability, physical and psychological comfort, a sense of belonging and esteem, and career opportunities and professional development.


This year, U.S. News' ratings expanded to encompass 549 companies across the overall best company list, 24 industry lists and four regional lists. To calculate the U.S. News Best Companies to Work For list, U.S. News only considered companies that were a part of the Russell 3000 index as of June 2023


and had more than 75 Glassdoor reviews written between 2020-23. The[methodology](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=3328416336&u=https%3A%2F%2Fmoney.usnews.com%2Fcareers%2Fcompanies%2Fmethodology&a=methodology) , developed with the support of a panel of[six experts](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=1318787221&u=https%3A%2F%2Fmoney.usnews.com%2Fcareers%2Fcompanies%2Fadvisors&a=six+experts) , also factors in data, including employee sentiment, gathered from U.S. News partners[Revelio Labs](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=3794963942&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.reveliolabs.com%2F&a=Revelio+Labs) ,[Good Jobs First](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=3853950588&u=https%3A%2F%2Fgoodjobsfirst.org%2F&a=Good+Jobs+First) and[QUODD](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=643961229&u=https%3A%2F%2Fquodd.com%2F&a=QUODD) .


For more information on the[Best Companies to Work For](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=190185176&u=https%3A%2F%2Fmoney.usnews.com%2Fcareers%2Fcompanies%2Frankings%3Fsrc%3Dusn_pr&a=Best+Companies+to+Work+For) , review the[FAQs](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=1005015966&u=https%3A%2F%2Fmoney.usnews.com%2Fcareers%2Funderstanding-the-badge&a=FAQs) and explore[Facebook](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=3044506193&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.facebook.com%2Fusnewsandworldreport&a=Facebook) and[X (formerly Twitter)](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4262994-1&h=3514106919&u=https%3A%2F%2Ftwitter.com%2Fusnews&a=X+(formerly+Twitter)) using #BCTWF.
