# Deutsche Bank expands escrow services in Korea, Vietnam and Malaysia

Deutsche Bank today announced the expansion of its escrow services in South Korea, Vietnam and Malaysia, strengthening its Trust and Securities Services offering in Asia Pacific and enabling clients to execute increasingly complex transactions with greater confidence.


As cross-border activity accelerates and deal structures become more sophisticated, clients are placing greater emphasis on safeguarding assets throughout the lifecycle of a transaction. Escrow arrangements play a critical role by holding funds or assets with a neutral third party until predefined conditions are met, helping to mitigate counterparty, financial and regulatory risks.


With this expansion, Deutsche Bank will provide on-the-ground escrow support in three high-growth markets that are attracting strong foreign investment and corporate activity. The move enhances the bank’s ability to support clients navigating mergers and acquisitions, project finance, capital raising and other transaction types where secure asset separation and conditional fund release are essential.


Deutsche Bank’s escrow solutions combine global scale with local expertise. The bank supports escrow arrangements across multiple jurisdictions and currencies, offers standardised documentation to enable fast execution, and provides integrated access to foreign exchange, custody, cash management and reporting services.


The service is designed to provide assurance to all parties in a transaction that funds are secured and will only be released when agreed milestones are achieved. This reduces settlement risk, supports smoother deal execution and builds trust between counterparties, particularly in cross-border or multi-party transactions.


> Expanding our escrow capabilities in South Korea, Vietnam and Malaysia reflects rising client demand for secure, transparent and efficient transaction structures across Asia Pacific. Our global platform, combined with deep local expertise, enables us to support clients confidently throughout the transaction lifecycle. Daniel Clark, Head of Trust and Securities Services for Asia Pacific and the Middle East & Africa


The addition of South Korea, Vietnam and Malaysia builds on Deutsche Bank’s established escrow capabilities across Asia Pacific and globally, where it supports clients with dedicated specialists, efficient know-your-client processes and a wide range of investment options for escrow balances.


By expanding into these markets, Deutsche Bank is further reinforcing its position as a trusted partner for secure escrow solutions, supporting clients as they pursue growth opportunities across the region.
