# Five themes the Deutsche Bank Research Institute is tracking through the rest of 2026

What happens in one region no longer stays there. Renewed conflict in the Middle East and the ongoing war in Ukraine have underscored how events in one part of the world can quickly reverberate across economies, markets and industries.


For investors, corporates and policymakers alike, questions around energy security, supply chains, inflation and regional stability are once again converging with other major structural themes. Those include artificial intelligence, the resilience of the US economy and ultimately the future direction of global markets.
