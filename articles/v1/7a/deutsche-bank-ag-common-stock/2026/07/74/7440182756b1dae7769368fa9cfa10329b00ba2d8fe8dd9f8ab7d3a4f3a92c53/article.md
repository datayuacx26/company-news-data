# Deutsche Bank recognised by Euromoney for M&A leadership across APAC

Deutsche Bank has secured four major wins at the 2026 Euromoney Awards for Excellence, with the bank named Best Investment Bank for M&A in China, Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore.


Widely regarded as one of the banking industry's most prestigious awards programmes, Euromoney's Awards for Excellence recognise institutions delivering outstanding results, innovation and client impact.


### Why did Deutsche Bank win these awards?


The awards highlight the continued momentum and market-leading capabilities of Deutsche Bank’s regional M&A franchise, building on earlier recognition from FinanceAsia, where Deutsche Bank was named Best M&A House in China and Korea.


The awards recognise Deutsche Bank's role in a number of significant transactions across the region, including:


- Air Liquide’s 2.8-billion-euro acquisition of DIG Airgas
- Actis’ acquisition of Singapore’s leading environmental management company, 800 Super
- Sampoerna’s billion US dollar sale to POSCO
- Advising JD.com on its 4-billion-euro strategic investment partnership with Ceconomy


> Winning across multiple markets in Asia reflects the strength and consistency of our regional platform, and the differentiated expertise we offer clients through deep sector knowledge and seamless collaboration across markets and products. Joe Lai, Head of Investment Banking and Capital Markets for APAC


### Global Hausbank in action


These wins alongside several other global awards underscore Deutsche Bank's continued investment in delivering comprehensive solutions from across the Bank’s entire suite of capabilities for clients.
