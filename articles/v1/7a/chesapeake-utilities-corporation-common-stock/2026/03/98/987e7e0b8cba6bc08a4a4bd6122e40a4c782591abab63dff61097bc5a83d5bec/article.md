## Sharp Energy Named Best Propane Supplier for 2026 by BusinessRate


##### ***BusinessRate Best of Award reflects the trust of customers across the Wilmington community.***


*DOVER, Del* **.** – Sharp Energy, a subsidiary of Chesapeake Utilities Corporation (NYSE: CPK) has been recognized as Best Propane Supplier in Wilmington, North Carolina, for 2026 as part of the BusinessRate Best of 2026 Awards, which highlight top local businesses for customer satisfaction, brand reputation and service excellence.


The BusinessRate Best of 2026 Awards celebrate businesses that demonstrate consistent excellence and strong customer relationships, helping consumers identify trusted local service providers in their communities. The awards are determined using real Google reviews from customers, analyzed through BusinessRate’s scoring system to compare businesses within the same local market.


“This recognition reflects the trust our Wilmington customers place in us every day,” said Bill Hancock, vice president, non‑regulated business at Chesapeake Utilities. “Our team is committed to delivering reliable propane services and excellent customer experience. Being recognized as the best propane supplier in the Wilmington market based on our Google Review history demonstrates that our customers appreciate the dedication of our front-line professionals to do just that.”


Sharp Energy serves residential and commercial customers throughout the Wilmington area, providing propane solutions that support homes, businesses and local operations. The Company’s focus on dependable delivery, safety and responsive customer care helped earn recognition among Wilmington’s top service providers.


**About Chesapeake Utilities Corporation** Chesapeake Utilities Corporation is a diversified energy delivery company, listed on the New York Stock Exchange. Chesapeake Utilities Corporation offers sustainable energy solutions through its natural gas transmission and distribution, electricity generation and distribution, propane gas distribution, mobile compressed natural gas utility services and solutions, and other businesses. For more information, visit **[www.chpk.com](http://www.chpk.com/)** .


**About Sharp Energy** Sharp Energy, a wholly owned subsidiary of Chesapeake Utilities Corporation (NYSE: CPK), delivers propane to customers across multiple states, providing energy solutions for homes, businesses and fleets. The company is committed to safety, reliability and customer-focused service in the communities it serves. For more information, visit[www.sharpenergy.com](https://www.sharpenergy.com/) .
