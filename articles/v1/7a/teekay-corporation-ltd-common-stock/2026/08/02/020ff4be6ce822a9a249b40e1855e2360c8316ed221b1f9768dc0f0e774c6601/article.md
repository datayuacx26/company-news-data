# Exploring the Maritime Industry Through Play and Learning

*“Had the opportunity to step onboard Kanata Spirit for my first vessel visit and it was truly an eye-opening experience.*


*Jasdeep Anand, Fleet Training Officer and Chief Engineer Shas hikant Waghmare gave us a thorough tour of the vessel. Getting to see everything up close really brought what I’ve been learning in school to life. Even seeing how provisions are stored onboard was quite fascinating! The food prepared by the galley crew was really good too.*


*Beyond the technical side, it was great to get a better sense of the human side of shipping and how the crew work together to keep daily operations running smoothly. Really appreciated the warm welcome from Captain Krishna Mohan and the rest of the crew.*


*As everything was new to me, the team’s reassurance, company, and insights made my visit a lot less intimidating.* *Looking forward to more vessel visits and learning opportunities ahead in my maritime journey!*
