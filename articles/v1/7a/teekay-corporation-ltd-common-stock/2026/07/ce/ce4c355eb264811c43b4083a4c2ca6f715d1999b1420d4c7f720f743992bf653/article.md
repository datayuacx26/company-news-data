# Two Decades of Joy with Boat for Hope

July 27, 2026


## Wherever we operate, we strive to strengthen the places we call home by giving our time, sharing our resources, and creating opportunities that make a lasting difference.


Since 2006, Teekay has proudly partnered with[Variety – The Children’s Charity](https://www.variety.bc.ca/) , creating meaningful moments and brighter futures for children with special needs and their families.


[Boat for Hope](https://www.boatforhope.ca/) brings together colleagues from our Vancouver office alongside local skippers, maritime industry supporters, first responders, and volunteers for a memorable day on the water. What started as a grassroots initiative within Vancouver’s boating community has grown into a beloved annual tradition, welcoming more than 1,000 participants each summer.


What began as a shared commitment to giving back has grown into a tradition our teams look forward to each year.


For the past two decades, we’ve had the privilege of helping create moments of joy, connection, and lasting memories for children and families in our community. This is a commitment we are proud to carry forward, year after year.
