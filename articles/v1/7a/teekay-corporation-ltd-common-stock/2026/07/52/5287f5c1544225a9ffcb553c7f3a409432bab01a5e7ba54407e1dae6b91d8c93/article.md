# Celebrating Our Culture of Care

July 22, 2026


## Recognizing the culture of care that drives our safety performance.


We’re proud to be recognized with the **Caring Collaborator Award** at the Shell Maritime Partners in Safety Awards.


This award reflects the strong safety culture our teams have built together and reflects the progress we’ve made, from maintaining a strong LTI-free record to advancing our Goal Zero commitment across the fleet.


A heartfelt thank you to our global colleagues across sea and shore. Your dedication, professionalism, and care for one another are what make our safety culture so strong.
