# Celebrating Environmental Progress

July 6, 2026


## We’re proud to be part of a port community driving sustainability forward across marine operations and global trade.


Teekay has been recognized with the 2025 Blue Circle Award from the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority for our commitment to environmental stewardship at the Port of Vancouver.


This award reflects our #TeekaySPIRIT and our continued focus on operating responsibly and sustainably.
