# Award-Winning Comedy Podcast “Scam Goddess” Joins iHeartMedia and Will Ferrell’s Big Money Players Podcast Network, Launches Full-Length Video Episodes For the First Time

*Listen to today’s new episode featuring Langston Kerman,*[HERE](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1119-scam-goddess-49639260)


*Video episodes now available,*[HERE](https://www.youtube.com/@ScamGoddess)


**WHAT:** “


[Scam Goddess](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1119-scam-goddess-49639260) ”, the award-winning comedy podcast dedicated to fraud and all those who practice it, joins iHeartMedia and Will Ferrell's Big Money Players podcast network (BMP) and expands into video, allowing fans to watch the full-length episodes for the first time.


Named one of TIME Magazine's 10 Best Podcasts of 2022, “Scam Goddess” has grown from an acclaimed podcast into a bestselling book, a Hulu television series and a sold-out national live tour.


In every episode, the Scam Goddess herself, Laci Mosley (A Black Lady Sketch Show, iCarly, Going Dutch), covers the hottest scams on the streets today while also giving in-depth breakdowns of the wildest robberies, rackets and capers throughout history. It's like true crime, but without all the death—aka True Con! Plus, Mosley is always joined by hilarious guests like Conan O'Brien, Miranda Cosgrove, Jameela Jamil, Lamorne Morris, Ayo Edebiri, Gayle King, Nicole Byer, Matt Rogers and more. Join the Scam Goddess "Con-gregation" to experience the funny side of fraud. And as Mosley always says, “stay schemin'!”


The first video episode, “F.R.A.U.D. is Dope the Jonathan Dupiton Con with Langston Kerman,” premieres today and will feature comedian and “My Momma Told Me” host Langston Kerman. He will join the show to break down AI ads, beach ab shots and shoddy merch Jonathan Dupiton released for Dupiton’s podcast "Rich and Unemployed."

**About Laci Mosley**


Comedian, actress, producer, writer, bestselling author, and self-proclaimed "Queen of Scam,"


[Laci Mosley](https://www.instagram.com/divalaci/?hl=en) has been at the forefront of the true crime and scam storytelling phenomenon since launching Scam Goddess in 2019. Over the last seven years, she has transformed her original concept into the Scam Goddess empire; a multiplatform franchise spanning podcasting, publishing, television, and live entertainment. Named one of TIME Magazine's 10 Best Podcasts of 2022, Scam Goddess has grown from an acclaimed podcast into a bestselling book, a Hulu television series, and a sold-out national Live Nation tour, with Laci performing her one woman show at iconic venues including House of Blues Chicago and Gramercy Theatre in New York City, as well as Los Angeles, San Francisco, Boston, and Dallas.
