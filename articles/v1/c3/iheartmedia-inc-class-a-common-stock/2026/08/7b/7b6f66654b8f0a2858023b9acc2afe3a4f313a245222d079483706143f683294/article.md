# iHeartMedia Spotlights New Music from Beyoncé and Ariana Grande; Names STELLA LEFTY and FLO as Latest ‘On The Verge’ Artists

**NEW YORK – July 31, 2026 –** iHeartMedia highlighted new music and artists alike recently with special programming.


On Friday, **Beyoncé** ’s new single “[MORNING DEW (DONK)](https://www.iheart.com/artist/beyonce-89266/songs/morning-dew-donk-414224867/) ” was featured as an iHeartRadio Spotlight across all iHeartRadio R&B stations while several songs from **Ariana Grande** ’s new album “[petal](https://www.iheart.com/artist/ariana-grande-678625/songs/petal-421089822) ” were featured in New Music Friday slots across all iHeartRadio CHR stations.


This summer, iHeartMedia named STELLA LEFTY and FLO as the latest ‘On The Verge’ artists, with singer-songwriter **STELLA LEFTY** featured across iHeartCountry stations with her single “[Boston](https://www.iheart.com/artist/stella-lefty-38677833/songs/boston-389124783) ” and hit British girl group **FLO** featured across iHeartRadio Rhythm CHR stations with their single “[Don’t Break Her Heart](https://www.iheart.com/artist/flo-44746331/songs/dont-break-her-heart-406060546) .” iHeartMedia’s ‘On The Verge’ program helps build awareness for up-and-coming artists through its radio airplay and historically, all ‘On The Verge’ artists have charted in the top 30, with more than half landing in the top 10 and over a third claiming the No. 1 chart slot.
