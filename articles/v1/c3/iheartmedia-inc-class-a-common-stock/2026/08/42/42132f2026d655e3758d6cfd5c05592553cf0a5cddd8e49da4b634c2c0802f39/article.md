# “iHeartRadio and TikTok LIVE Premiere with Alex Warren” Featuring a Performance and Exclusive Premiere of His New Single “RESCUER” on TikTok and iHeartRadio Stations August 6

*Alex Warren will perform his new single, premiere the official music video for “RESCUER” and share inspiration behind the music with fans ahead of the single’s official release on August 7*


*Fans can visit iHeartRadio’s digital station Hit Nation and use the talkback feature to submit questions to Alex Warren*


New York, N.Y. – July 31, 2026 – iHeartMedia and TikTok announced today that on August 6 at 7 p.m. PT/ 10 p.m. ET the “iHeartRadio and TikTok LIVE Premiere with Alex Warren” will stream LIVE on,[@iheartradio](https://www.tiktok.com/@iheartradio) ,[@tiktok](https://www.tiktok.com/@tiktok) , and[@alexwarren](https://www.tiktok.com/@alexwarren?lang=en) . Hosted by Alex Warren at the iHeartRadio Los Angeles Theater, the preview will also broadcast on iHeartRadio CHR stations nationwide, select Hot AC stations and stream on iHeartRadio’s digital station[Hit Nation](https://www.iheart.com/live/hit-nation-4422/) .


In celebration of Alex Warren’s new single “RESCUER,” set to be released on August 7, fans will get an exclusive first listen to the single with a special performance, see the premiere of the official music video for “RESCUER” and Alex will take the host chair for a fan-driven talk show style conversation about the inspiration behind the music and his upcoming album, *WILDCHILD* . Beginning today, fans can visit iHeartRadio’s digital station Hit Nation and use the talkback feature to submit their questions to Alex Warren. Submissions will be accepted through August 6 and select responses will be featured during the event. Additionally during the event, fans can interact directly on TikTok through interactive poll questions and custom likes.


Alex Warren is a Grammy nominated artist who has also received Breakthrough Artist of the Year, Song of the Year and Best New Pop Artist awards at the 2026 iHeartRadio Music Awards and received the Breakthrough Artist of the Year[TikTok Award.](https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZP8tnmGu6/)


“I’m so excited to premiere ‘RESCUER’ with iHeartRadio and TikTok LIVE before its official release,” said Alex Warren. “Music has always been about connection for me, and together with iHeartRadio and TikTok I’ll be able to directly connect with my fans, answer their questions and experience this moment together. It’s going to be really special for everyone to hear ‘RESCUER’ and be part of this launch with me.”


Alex Warren’s highly anticipated new album *WILDCHILD* is set to be released on August 28, fans can pre-order[HERE.](https://alexwarren.lnk.to/wildchild)


Support for the “iHeartRadio and TikTok LIVE Premiere with Alex Warren” event provided by Atlantic Records.
