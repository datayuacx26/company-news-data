# iHeartPodcasts and Real Ones Media Debut “REAL DEAL with Tony Reali” a Sports Culture Podcast Built Around One Irresistible Sports Moment at a Time

*Each week, Tony Reali and special guests will unpack one unforgettable moment in sports through humor, nostalgia, compelling storytelling and thoughtful conversation*


**WHAT:** iHeartPodcasts today debuts “


[REAL DEAL with Tony Reali](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1119-real-deal-with-tony-reali-338598967) ,” a sports culture podcast built around one unforgettable moment at a time. A play. A clip. A photo. A legend. The moments that shaped sports and continue to resonate long after the final whistle. Every episode begins with an iconic image, using it as a starting point to explore why the moment still matters and why it continues to move fans years later.


**WHO:** Hosted by longtime ESPN personality Tony Reali, “REAL DEAL” features conversations with athletes, broadcasters, comedians, journalists, superfans, creators, and the unexpected people connected to each moment. Blending humor, nostalgia, compelling storytelling, and genuine insight, Tony and his guests relive the moment, uncover the story behind the story, and reveal the lasting impact it had on sports and culture.


“REAL DEAL” isn't a debate show or a recap show. It's a show about memory, emotion and the sports moments we still can't stop replaying and sharing years, and sometimes decades, later.


**WHEN:** The first episode debuts July 14, 2026 with new episodes releasing weekly on Tuesdays.


**WHERE:** “REAL DEAL with Tony Reali'' is distributed by iHeartPodcasts and is available on the iHeartRadio app and everywhere podcasts are heard. Listen now


[HERE](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1119-real-deal-with-tony-reali-338598967) .


**About Tony Reali**


Tony Reali is one of the most recognizable and trusted voices in sports media, best known for hosting ESPN’s Around the Horn for more than two decades and appearing on Pardon the Interruption as the beloved “Stat Boy.” Across nearly 7,000 episodes, Tony became known for bringing intelligence, humor, empathy, energy, and humanity to sports television.


REAL DEAL with Tony Reali is Tony’s next chapter: a digital-first sports culture show built around unforgettable moments, emotional storytelling, curiosity, play, and the beautiful chaos that makes sports matter in the first place.
