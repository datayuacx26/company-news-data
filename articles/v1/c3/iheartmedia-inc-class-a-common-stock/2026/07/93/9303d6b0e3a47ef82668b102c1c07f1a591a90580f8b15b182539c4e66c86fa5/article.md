# iHeartMedia Spotlights New Music from Nickleback, Cage The Elephant, Tucker Wetmore; Celebrates Gracie Abrams and Madonna

**NEW YORK – July 17, 2026 –** iHeartMedia highlighted new music and artists alike recently with special programming, including new World Premiere debuts and more.


This month, iHeartMedia highlighted new music with World Premiere debuts across several genres, including **Nickleback’** s “[Rattle The Cage](https://www.iheart.com/artist/nickelback-34750/songs/rattle-the-cage-416676766) ” on July 14 across all iHeartRadio Rock stations, **Cage The Elephant’** s “[Beaches In Tennessee](https://www.iheart.com/artist/cage-the-elephant-117804/songs/beaches-in-tennessee-414572234) ” on July 10 across all iHeartRadio Alternative stations and **Tucker Wetmore** ’s “[Who Told You That](https://www.iheart.com/artist/tucker-wetmore-33601651/songs/who-told-you-that-414567745) ” across all iHeartCountry stations.


Additionally, iHeart celebrated two highly-anticipated albums in the first half of this month with New Music Friday features played across stations nationwide. On July 3, **Madonna** ’s “[Confessions II](https://www.iheart.com/artist/madonna-35393/albums/confessions-ii-413605288) ” was featured in New Music Friday slots with multiple songs from the 16-track album played across all iHeartRadio CHR stations. On July 17, **Gracie Abrams** ’ “[Daughter from Hell](https://www.iheart.com/artist/gracie-abrams-33523798/albums/daughter-from-hell-417432420) ” was featured in New Music Friday slots with several songs from her third studio album played across all iHeartRadio CHR stations.
