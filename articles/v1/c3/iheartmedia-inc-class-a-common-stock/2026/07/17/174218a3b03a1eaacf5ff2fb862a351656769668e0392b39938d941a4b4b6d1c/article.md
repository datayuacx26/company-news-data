# iHeartMedia Announces the Debut of the New iHeartRadio Digital Station “Marky Ramone Radio” Launching July 15

NEW YORK- July 15, 2026 – iHeartMedia announced the debut of the new iHeartRadio digital station “Marky Ramone Radio” launching today, July 15, 2026. The station will be hosted by the longest-serving drummer of the legendary punk band, Rock and Roll Hall of Famer and Grammy Lifetime Achievement Award recipient Marky Ramone. The station is available now online and on the free iHeartRadio app.


“Marky Ramone Radio” will broadcast a mix of music from a variety of genres chosen by Marky with behind the scenes stories and insights into the songs. In addition to the Ramones renowned discography, the station will also feature a variety of genres including rock, motown, blues, British invasion, and more.


“I’m happy to be joining iHeartRadio to host my own station. I will play a variety of music that I like and some that I love…rock, motown, British invasion, punk, soul, new wave, jazz and a lot of surprises. I’ll also talk about my musical career, my times with the Ramones and touring around the world. I’ll also will be talking to some cool people. So check it out and Hey Ho, Let’s Go,” said Marky Ramone.


“Punk rock has always been about authenticity, individuality, and breaking the rules. No one embodies that spirit better than Marky Ramone. As we celebrate the 50th anniversary of punk, Marky Ramone Radio gives fans unprecedented access to the music, stories, and artists that built one of the most influential genres of all time. We're proud to partner with Marky to create a destination that both lifelong fans and new listeners will love, exclusively on the iHeartRadio app.” said Jon Zellner, President of Programming Operations & Digital Music for iHeartMedia.


“Rock ‘n’ roll has always been free and Marky Ramone intends to keep it that way. Coinciding with the 50th anniversary of punk rock as well as Marky Ramone’s birthday, Marky Ramone Radio is now available to everyone thanks to the free iHeartRadio app, where fans will be able to enjoy the sounds that revolutionized rock ‘n’ roll. Gabba Gabba hey,” said Harvey Leeds, CEO Headquarters Media.


Fans can tune in to the new station by searching “Marky Ramone” on the iHeartRadio app and at[iHeartRadio.com](https://www.iheart.com/live/classic-rick-dees-top-40-10600/) .
