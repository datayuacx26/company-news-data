# iHeartMedia Announces “iHeartRadio Album Preview with Gracie Abrams Featuring Daughter from Hell Presented by Capital One,” Airing on iHeartRadio Stations July 16

*Gracie Abrams will take over iHeartRadio’s studio to give fans an exclusive first listen to her new album Daughter from Hell ahead of its July 17 release*


*Fans can visit iHeartRadio’s digital station Hit Nation and use the talkback feature to share lessons they learned in their 20s and questions for Gracie for a chance to be featured in the special*


*Additionally, iHeartRadio New York’s Z100 will host an Album Preview Party for fans on July 15*


**NEW YORK- July 10, 2026-** iHeartMedia today announced the “ **iHeartRadio Album Preview with Gracie Abrams featuring Daughter from Hell Presented by Capital One** ” will broadcast on July 16 at 9 p.m. ET/6 p.m. PT. GRAMMY®-nominated singer-songwriter Gracie Abrams will take over iHeartRadio’s studio for a one-hour radio special, giving fans an exclusive first listen to music from her highly anticipated new album, Daughter from Hell, ahead of its official release on July 17. The event will broadcast across iHeartRadio Contemporary Hit Radio (CHR) and select Hot AC stations nationwide and stream on iHeartRadio’s digital station[Hit Nation](https://www.iheart.com/live/hit-nation-4422/) .


Hosted by Abrams, the takeover will feature exclusive stories behind the music, personal reflections on the making of the album and previews of select unreleased tracks. Throughout the broadcast, Abrams will connect directly with listeners by responding to fan questions and reacting to lessons fans learned in their 20s.


On Friday, July 17, iHeartRadio’s New Music Friday program will feature select tracks and audio from the special across participating stations.


“I’m so excited to share Daughter from Hell with everyone,” said Gracie Abrams. “This album means so much to me, and every song tells a story. Hosting my own radio show with iHeartRadio gives me the chance to introduce this music in a way that feels personal. I love that fans can be part of the conversation and share their experiences.”


Beginning today, fans can visit iHeartRadio’s digital station[Hit Nation](https://www.iheart.com/live/hit-nation-4422/) and use the talkback feature to share the biggest lessons they learned in their 20s. Submissions will be accepted through July 16 and select responses will be featured during the special.


Additionally, iHeartRadio New York’s Z100 is giving fans the chance to hear the new album in its entirety at an exclusive Album Preview Party on July 15, ahead of its release. Z100 listeners will have the opportunity to win access on-air, while Capital One cardholders will have additional chances to win by visiting z100.com/gracieabrams.
