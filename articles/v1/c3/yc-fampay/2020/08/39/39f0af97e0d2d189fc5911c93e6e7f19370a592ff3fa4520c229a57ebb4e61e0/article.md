Since you've got all the necessary details on Aadhar KYC now, let's try to solve some of the issues surrounding it 💪🏼


## **I'm not sure which mobile number📱 is linked to my Aadhaar**


Follow the steps below to clear the confusion!


Step 1: Visit the[Aadhaar Website](https://resident.uidai.gov.in/verify?ref=blog.famapp.in) 📲


Step 2: Enter your Aadhaar number 🤳🏽


Step 3: The page will show 'Verification Complete!', and the last 3 digits of the number linked to your Aadhaar. 💯


## **My Aadhaar is linked to a mobile number which is no longer in use**


No worries! Here is what you can do:


Step 1: Visit your nearest[Aadhaar Centre](https://appointments.uidai.gov.in/easearch.aspx?ref=blog.famapp.in) 🛺
( Tip: Use the search by ' **State** ' option to get a list of all centres in your city)


Step 2: Ask for Update or Correction form 🔖 (or download from[here](https://uidai.gov.in/images/aadhaar_enrolment_correction_form_version_2.1.pdf?ref=blog.famapp.in) )


Step 3: Carry your original Aadhaar Card for verification. Yes, no other documents required!


Step 4: Submit the form and verify your biometric details. (Yes, the thumb impression!) 👍


Step 5: Collect the acknowledgment slip and wait for 10 days for it to get updated


Step 6: Treat yourself to an ice-cream 🍦 You deserve it 😉


**Tip:** You can also visit your nearest telecom operator store 🏪 . Please check with your nearest store if they have the facility to get this done :)
