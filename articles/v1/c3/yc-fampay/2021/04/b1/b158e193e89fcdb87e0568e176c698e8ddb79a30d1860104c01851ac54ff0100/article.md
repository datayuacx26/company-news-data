**Why choose Instagram as your marketplace? 🤷🏻‍♀️**


Now that you have a fair idea of what our young businesspersons are selling on Instagram, you might wonder why you should set up shop at Instagram. 👀


Well, long story short:[Instagram is a gold mine of opportunities for a business.](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/1554245014870700/?helpref=hc_fnav&bc%5B0%5D=Instagram%20Help&bc%5B1%5D=Instagram%20for%20Businesses) 🌟 We'll tell you why.


1. **Ease of usage** : If you're not a big IG fanatic, you don't have to worry about a steep learning curve. **Setting up your business page on Instagram is easy** , and makes your social media handling accessible! Business pages can link their accounts from other social platforms to Instagram, and publish content for all of them at the same time. 👌🏼
2. **Great features** : With Instagram, GenZs have been able to **channel their creative abilities to create a beautiful catalog-like experience for customers.** Use stories and highlights to shed light on great topics such as your business story, customer reviews, customization and teasers for new releases, and get on top of the your business game! 🤩
3. **Analytics tools:**[Instagram Insights](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/788388387972460) is a truly commendable feature. Decided to post something and can't gauge how fire your post is? **Analytics will give you easy and cohesive stats to look at** , which makes it so easy to understand which posts are pushing your business forward. 🔎
4. **Creative Freedom:** *Instagram lets young business owners paint their business canvas however they like.* **There are pages with different themes, making them look intriguing and fun.** If you're inclined towards a particular theme, you can incorporate a cohesive feed, definite color scheme and tone of your page! This is your chance to be unique and experimental: do what you enjoy and have fun doing it! 🌈


🔥 *Fam tip: Instagram has been a big supporter of businesses during the pandemic, even showing solidarity and introducing a sticker called "Support Small Business". Instagram Users can share their favorite small brand, improving the brand's visibility!*


**Is there a blueprint you can follow?** 📘


While we strongly believe in being unique and doing things your way, there are some baselines that will help you establish your Instagram Business smoothly. 📊 Let's throw light on the things that ensure a prepared entry of your business in the IG marketplace. 💪🏽


1. **Find your niche:** The most important step would be to find what you'd like to monetize. It can be **products, services** or a mix of both. Out of the box ideas are always great, but even if your product is simple, it can turn out to be simply amazing! ✨
2. **Market Research:** We know, heavy word alert! 😓 Don't worry, it's not as tricky as it sounds. Doing some **basic market research** would involve looking at other businesses that might sell similar products, noting their prices and customer demographic. 📝
3. **Making samples** : To launch your business, it would be a great idea to make some samples beforehand. This will give you an idea about the **time, material, and cost involved in making your product** . You can get even get feedback from family or squad and decide on the prices and range that you'd like to offer. 🤑
4. **Shoot creatives:** Channel your photography and editing skills to **create online content around your products!** If you have a theme in mind, follow it. If you're experiencing some creative block, draw inspiration from other pages and sync it with your uniqueness. 📸
5. **Build a following:** Once you've made your Instagram Business Account and posted some introductory posts, it's time to get a customers! Woohoo! Use that market research to follow relevant people, and engage with them. 🔥 *Fam tip: What if we told you there's a **[community of 7K+ teens](https://blog.famapp.in/blog/when-building-for-genzs-build-it-on-discord/) ,** waiting to hear about your business?
