# Quantum-Si Announces the Expansion of the Proteus™ Roadshow

**Multi-City Roadshow is Driving Awareness of Proteus and Developing Customer Funnel for Commercial Launch**


BRANFORD, Conn.–(BUSINESS WIRE)–Jun. 3, 2026–
[Quantum-Si Incorporated](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.quantum-si.com%2F&esheet=54546349&newsitemid=20260602203644&lan=en-US&anchor=Quantum-Si+Incorporated&index=1&md5=8ac17a4945581e4968d4a03357f9a61d) (Nasdaq: QSI) (“Quantum-Si,” “QSI” or the “Company”), a proteomics technology company redefining protein analysis through single-molecule protein sequencing, today announced the expansion of a roadshow designed to accelerate the education of the market on its Proteus platform and applications for protein sequencing.


The roadshow includes information about the Proteus platform and application focus as well as data on applications for protein sequencing utilizing the Company’s first-generation Platinum® Pro instrument. During the second quarter of 2026, the roadshow has visited Seattle, Houston, Washington D.C., and multiple cities across Germany, France and the U.K. During the month of June, the roadshow is scheduled to stop at multiple institutions in North Carolina’s Research Triangle Park, St. Louis and multiple cities in Italy in collaboration with the Company’s local channel partner.


“We are excited to continue to expand the Proteus roadshow to more cities in both the United States and key European markets,” said Jeff Hawkins, President and Chief Executive Officer of Quantum-Si. “Roadshows have proven to be an effective way to expand the breadth and depth of customer awareness of Proteus and our proprietary single-molecule protein sequencing technology and we look forward to continuing with this initiative as we prepare the market for Proteus launch.”


**About Quantum-Si Incorporated**


Quantum-Si is transforming proteomics with a benchtop platform that brings single-molecule protein analysis to every lab, everywhere. The Company’s platform enables real-time kinetic-based detection and allows researchers to move beyond traditional, multistep workflows and directly access dynamic, functional protein insights with unparalleled resolution. By making protein analysis simpler, faster, and more informative, Quantum-Si is accelerating proteomic discoveries to improve the way we live.
