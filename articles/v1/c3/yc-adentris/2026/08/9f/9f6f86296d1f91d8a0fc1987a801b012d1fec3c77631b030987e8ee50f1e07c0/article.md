# Adentris and Grant Global & Associates Partner on Revenue Integrity

Adentris is a healthcare compliance platform that reviews charts and claims on top of a provider's existing EHR. Today it is announcing a signed partnership with Grant Global & Associates (GGA) to resell and implement its 5 revenue integrity modules for private-equity-backed behavioral health operators.


## Key takeaways


- What: a signed reseller and implementation partnership between Adentris and GGA.
- Who it helps: private-equity-backed behavioral health operators and their investors.
- Why it matters: it moves compliance checks earlier, from month-end review toward the point of care.
- What is new: GGA will resell and implement all 5 Adentris revenue integrity modules, with joint onboarding.


## What the partnership includes


Under the agreement, GGA will resell and implement Adentris across its client base. The two teams run rollouts and staff training together, so operators are supported through onboarding rather than left to deploy on their own.


Adentris reviews charts and claims inside the provider's current EHR, with no system replacement. Its 5 modules cover code verification, clinical necessity review, prior authorization, denial management, and executive financial reporting.


## Why revenue integrity matters for behavioral health


The partnership targets private-equity-backed behavioral health platforms, where clean documentation and accurate claims drive both regulatory standing and financial performance. Denials and documentation gaps are a well-documented drain on provider revenue, a problem tracked by industry bodies such as[HFMA](https://www.hfma.org/?ref=blog.adentris.com) and reflected in federal guidance from[CMS](https://www.cms.gov/?ref=blog.adentris.com) .


For an operator, the goal is simple: catch a coding or documentation issue before the claim goes out, not 30 days later in an audit. In behavioral health, a single denied claim can tie up 1000s of dollars for weeks.
