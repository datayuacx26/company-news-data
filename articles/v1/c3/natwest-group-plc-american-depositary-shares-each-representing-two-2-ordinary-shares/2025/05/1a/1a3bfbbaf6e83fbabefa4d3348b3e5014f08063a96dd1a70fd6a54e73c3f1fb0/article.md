## 2026 publications


[Hierarchical Reason-of-Contact Detection in Retail Banking Customer Interactions via LLM-Driven Taxonomy Induction](https://openreview.net/forum?id=KfcWaTdGVi)
ACL 2026 Industry Track (Oral)
*Yoages Kumar Mantri, Yagneswaran Sriraja, John Hartley, Greig A. Cowan, Raad Khraishi, Abhinav Gupta*


Introduces a deployed, hierarchical Reason-of-Contact detection system for retail banking conversations. The approach combines clustering, LLM-driven taxonomy induction, and rapid retrieval to identify customer intent across channels, supporting real-time performance, auditability, dynamic taxonomy updates, and improved detection of out-of-distribution contact reasons.
ㅤ


[Helping Customers in Distress: An LLM-powered Agent that Converses, Probes, and Routes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16268) **** ICLR 2026 Workshop: Advances in Financial AI
*Alankar Atreya, Stefan Sylvius Wagner, Devesh Batra, Robert Hankache, Cristovão Iglesias Jr, Patrick Sinclair, Giulio Pelosio, Michael McMillan, Greig A. Cowan, Raad Khraishi*


Presents a customer-facing LLM triage agent for fraud, scam, and dispute journeys. The agent conducts multi-turn conversations, asks targeted follow-up questions, and routes cases in line with policy, with evaluation using historical cases and synthetic customer digital twins.
ㅤ


[Large-Scale Chatbot Validation Through Customer Digital Twin Simulations](https://openreview.net/forum?id=fOolwJFk25) **** ICLR 2026 Workshop: Advances in Financial AI **** *Cristovão Iglesias Jr, Devesh Batra, Alankar Atreya, Stefan Sylvius Wagner, Robert Hankache, Patrick Sinclair, Giulio Pelosio, Michael McMillan, Greig A. Cowan, Raad Khraishi*


Introduces a scalable validation framework for customer-facing chatbots using synthetic customer agents grounded in transactional and conversational data. The framework enables large-scale testing through automated evaluation, expert review, and adversarial probing before deployment.
ㅤ


[AutoQA: An Interpretable Automation Framework for CDD Quality Assurance in Financial Services](https://openreview.net/forum?id=VJHTDYqq4T)
ICLR 2026 Workshop: Advances in Financial AI
*Iman Zafar, Andrea Bassani, Alexandros Anatolakis, Greig A. Cowan, Raad Khraishi*


Presents an interpretable automation framework for customer due diligence quality assurance. AutoQA combines deterministic rules with targeted LLM-assisted reasoning, keeping analysts in the loop while improving consistency, interpretability, and review efficiency in regulated financial-services workflows.
ㅤ


[Evaluating Performance Drift from Model Switching in Multi-Turn LLM Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03111) **** ICLR 2026 Workshop: Catch, Adapt, and Operate: Monitoring ML Models Under Drift
*Raad Khraishi, Iman Zafar, Katie Myles, Greig A. Cowan*


Introduces a switch-matrix benchmark for measuring performance drift when LLM systems switch models mid-conversation. The paper shows that handoffs between models can affect reliability in multi-turn settings, motivating explicit monitoring and evaluation of model switching in production LLM systems.
ㅤ


[MOSAIC: Modular Orchestration for Structured Agentic Intelligence and Composition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00708)
arXiv preprint
*Yifan Bao, Xinyu Xi, Xinyu Liu, Wen Ge, Lei Jiang, Kevin Zhang, Raad Khraishi, Yihao Ang, Anthony K. H. Tung, Lukasz Szpruch, Hao Ni*


A memory-grounded agentic framework that treats automated data science as structured model selection. MOSAIC retrieves prior cases and code modules to build a reusable blueprint specifying the chosen modelling components and how they compose, then validates candidates by execution and refines them with feedback. On financial time-series forecasting and generation, it improves task performance, execution success, and decision traceability over AutoML and agentic baselines.
