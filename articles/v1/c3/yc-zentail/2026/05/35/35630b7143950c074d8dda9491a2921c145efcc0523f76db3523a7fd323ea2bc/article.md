## Amazon updates: Amazon SP-API


### **What is Amazon SP-API?**


Amazon’s Selling Partner API (SP-API) is a comprehensive set of tools that allow sellers to better manage their Amazon accounts. One major change is the upgrade to the backend schema and taxonomy, which affects the attributes available to sellers during the item setup process. This means that sellers must now adhere to new item setup requirements to ensure their listings remain active, compliant, and discoverable on Amazon.


### **Why should you upgrade?**


With the release of SP-API, upgrading is critical for sellers to maintain smooth operations, optimize listing performance, and keep up with Amazon’s new standards. A significant part of this upgrade is the revamped item setup process, which includes updated templates and new required attributes for listings. Sellers must now use these updated templates to meet Amazon’s new product type and attribute requirements, or they risk having their listings become inactive or undiscoverable.

## Walmart updates: Omnispec 5.0


### **What is Walmart Omnispec 5.0?**


Walmart’s Omnispec 5.0 is the latest update to its product listing specifications. It brings a host of improvements to how sellers provide product data, including more granular details and enhanced accuracy. This update is designed to make product listings more standardized, ultimately leading to better searchability, higher conversion rates, and a more seamless customer experience.


### **Why should you upgrade?**


Sellers who upgrade to Walmart Omnispec 5.0 will have access to more relevant data fields, allowing for richer, more informative product listings. This can significantly improve product discoverability and lead to higher sales. If you’re still using an older specification, you might miss out on crucial opportunities for enhanced visibility and better performance in search results.


### 5 things to know about Walmart Omnipec 5.0


1. **Expanded attributes** : Omnipec 5.0 introduces more detailed attributes, allowing you to provide deeper product information that boosts relevance in search results.
2. **Better data accuracy** : The new spec reduces the risk of data errors by introducing more stringent validation rules, ensuring cleaner product information across Walmart’s marketplace.
3. **Optimized search performance** : By using richer product data, Omnispec 5.0 helps Walmart’s algorithm display more relevant products to shoppers, improving the likelihood of purchase.
4. **Simplified data entry** : While there are more attributes to manage, Omnispec 5.0’s structure simplifies the data entry process, helping sellers stay compliant with less effort.
5. **Improved customer experience** : With more accurate and detailed product information, shoppers can make better-informed decisions, decreasing returns and increasing satisfaction.
