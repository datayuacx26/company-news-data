# What's new in Zentail? Highlights from H1 2026

We've spent the first half of the year focused on giving sellers better visibility into their business and making it easier to identify opportunities for growth. This release introduces a redesigned Dashboard and a completely rebuilt Analytics experience, giving you better visibility into sales performance, listing health, profitability, and channel performance – all from within Zentail.


## See the health of your business at a glance


Your[Dashboard](https://help.zentail.com/en/articles/1079002-dashboard-overview) is now built around the metrics that matter most to marketplace sellers. Monitor revenue, buyable listings, publishing health, and channel performance from a single view so you can quickly identify problems, prioritize fixes, and uncover opportunities to grow sales.


Instead of jumping between reports, you can see how your business is performing the moment you log into Zentail. You'll also find revenue projections based on your buyable listings, helping you understand the potential impact of getting more products live and available for purchase across your marketplaces.


Everything is designed to help you spend less time finding information and more time acting on it.


Learn more in our[Dashboard help article](https://help.zentail.com/en/articles/1079002-dashboard-overview) .


## Turn your sales data into smarter decisions


We've completely rebuilt[Analytics](https://help.zentail.com/en/articles/1404426-business-analytics-overview) to give you faster, more flexible reporting and better visibility into what's driving your business.


Instead of piecing together reports or exporting data into spreadsheets, you can quickly answer questions like:


- Which marketplaces are driving the most profitable sales?
- Which brands or products are growing fastest?
- How much are channel fees impacting margins?
- Which warehouses or fulfillment providers are driving your results?


The new experience loads significantly faster, gives you more ways to analyze your data, and makes it easier to compare performance across every part of your business. Flexible filters, custom date ranges, and downloadable reports help you find the answers you need without digging through spreadsheets.


Learn more in our[Analytics help article](https://help.zentail.com/en/articles/1404426-business-analytics-overview) .


## Improvements & fixes


- **Zentail now supports Amazon’s**[new Item Highlights](https://www.zentail.com/blog/amazon-is-cutting-product-titles-to-75-characters-heres-what-sellers-need-to-know) **field,** making it easier to manage product highlights as Amazon rolls out its latest listing requirement.
- **Eligible Target Plus listings are now more consistently shown as buyable** , helping keep products available for shoppers.
- **Bulk price updates for grouped products are now more reliable** , reducing the chance that some product variants are skipped during marketplace price updates.
