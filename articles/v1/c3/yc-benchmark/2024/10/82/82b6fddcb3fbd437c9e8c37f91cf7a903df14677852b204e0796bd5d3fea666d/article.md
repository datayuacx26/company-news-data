# Introducing Benchmark

## TL;DR


Benchmark's AI-powered platform allows investment teams to screen deals in minutes while automatically aggregating all of their valuable deal data for future analysis. With Benchmark, investors can focus on high-probability opportunities and build permanent institutional knowledge, saving them thousands of hours per year and giving them a significant edge over the competition.


## The Challenge: Navigating a Competitive Landscape


The private markets have experienced substantial growth over the past decade, intensifying competition and making it increasingly challenging for firms to differentiate their strategies and generate outsized returns.


Today, the investment process is highly inefficient:


- Teams spend thousands of hours reviewing low-probability opportunities that ultimately fail.
- Valuable data from these opportunities is often neglected due to the manual work required to harvest and analyze it.


In order to succeed in this competitive landscape, investment firms need to find a low-cost way to increase efficiency and utilize their valuable proprietary data to help generate outsized returns, which is where we come in.


## Benchmark's Solution: AI-Powered Deal & Data Analysis


Benchmark revolutionizes the investment process by automatically extracting, normalizing, and summarizing deal data from any source. Our platform enables investment teams to:


1. **Rapidly Screen Deals:** Quickly assess opportunities, answer due diligence questions, extract key data points, and build comps in minutes, not hours.
2. **Optimize Time Allocation:** Minimize time spent on low-probability deals and focus resources on high-potential opportunities.
3. **Leverage Proprietary Data:** Utilize historical deal information to improve investment outcomes and drive data-informed decision-making.


## How it Works


1. **Upload Deal Documents:** Securely parse documents from any data source in minutes regardless of format or complexity.
2. **Instantly Screen your deal:** Review comprehensive deal summaries, key metrics, internal comps, and due diligence question answers.
3. **Dig Deeper with Real-Time Q&A:** Ask any question about the deal and receive accurate, verifiable answers with comprehensive citations.
4. **Analyze All of Your Unstructured Data:** Transform previously unstructured data into structured, API-ready information enabling unprecedented analysis and insight.
