*Headquartered in Neenah, Wisconsin, Global Company Reaffirms Dedication to Community with Multi-Year Relief Pledge*


**NEENAH, WI, August 11, 2026** — Plexus Corp. (NASDAQ: PLXS) today announced a $1 million commitment dedicated to supporting tornado relief and recovery efforts across Menasha and surrounding Fox Cities communities in Wisconsin. Administered through the Plexus Community Foundation (PCF), the multi-year pledge will be distributed across calendar years 2026 and 2027 to support both immediate aid and sustained long-term recovery for affected local families and infrastructure.


This commitment directly aligns with Plexus’ vision to help build a better world—a purpose that extends beyond the products it designs, manufactures and services to actively support the communities where its team members live and work.


Founded in Neenah in 1979, Plexus has grown into a global enterprise with more than 20,000 team members, while remaining deeply anchored in its home community. Today’s announcement reflects the company’s ongoing commitment to care for its people, neighbors and local businesses during critical times of need.


“The Fox Cities isn’t just where Plexus was founded—it’s our home and the community that supported our growth into a global company,” said Todd Kelsey, President and Chief Executive Officer of Plexus Corp. “Living our vision of building a better world starts right here in our own backyard. As our team members and neighbors face the difficult path of rebuilding after this devastating tornado, Plexus is committed to stand alongside our community as we recover together.”


Funds will be disbursed to non-profit partners supporting essential basic needs, such as food, shelter and mental health wellbeing. To ensure every dollar creates maximum long-term impact, Plexus will continuously assess the areas with the greatest ongoing need as recovery efforts evolve.


In addition to community funding, Plexus is providing resources and support to team members directly impacted by the storm. To complement these financial contributions, Plexus team members have also been actively participating in volunteer efforts on the ground.


For more information about Plexus and its community engagement initiatives, visit[www.plexus.com](https://www.plexus.com/) .
