**Title:** USA | MISSION READY: Accelerating Defense Tech from TRL 3 to Volume Production
**Description:** As the US Department of Defense pivots toward rapid fielding and the “Replicator” initiative, the challenge for innovative OEMs has shifted. It is no longer just about *if* a technology works (TRL), but *how fast* it can be produced at scale without sacrificing mission-critical quality (MRL).


This webinar addresses the “Valley of Death” between TRL 3 and 6, where promising prototypes often stall. We will explore how to de-risk product development through strategies that allow disruptors to meet DoD requirements and transition into Programs of Record with the speed of a startup and the reliability of a Prime.
