The global semiconductor industry is in a high-stakes race against time. As the demand for advanced chips in AI, IoT, and automotive continues to skyrocket, a critical constraint is emerging: a significant engineering talent shortage for semiconductor manufacturing equipment.


In Japan, this challenge is particularly acute. Analysts predict the industry will require over a million additional skilled workers globally by[2030](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/Industries/tmt/articles/global-semiconductor-talent-shortage.html) , a demand the current pipeline simply cannot meet. This shortage, coupled with the retirement of highly experienced veterans, is straining in-house teams and slowing the pace of innovation.


For semiconductor capital equipment manufacturers (OEMs), this is more than a staffing problem; it’s a risk to market entry and a limit on core intellectual property (IP) development at a time when the industry demands speed. You must innovate faster than ever, yet system integration, routine testing and lifecycle management drain your most valuable engineers’ time.


## Beyond Hiring: The Strategic Partnership Model


Traditional hiring is no longer a sufficiently rapid solution to bridge the engineering shortage. The time required to recruit and ramp individual resources can result in missed market windows in an industry where throughput and uptime are critical. The solution is a strategic partnership. At Plexus, we act as an immediate, expert extension of your team.


This model allows your internal specialists to remain focused on the Master-to-Partner philosophy: you retain your core IP, while Plexus provides the engineering services horsepower to execute complex system-level work. By leveraging a full product lifecycle partner with a global footprint, you gain a partner with the expertise needed to serve as an extension of your team who values long-term, strategic relationships, enabling you to reallocate your internal resources to next-generation IP development.

## Solving Bottlenecks: Four Pillars of Support


As a technical partner, Plexus offers targeted support that overcomes common engineering and manufacturing bottlenecks.


We build the skill set and stay in sync with your product roadmaps to minimize start up delays. Our teams can easily flow in or out of the product lifecycle to meet the needs of the customer.


While we have deep experience in the listed product areas, we couple that with broad expertise across many engineering disciplines, which allows us to be flexible in serving customers.


1. Concept and Feasibility to Rapid Prototyping: Shortening the Learning Cycle


Intentionally engaging early in technical development and supply chain, our integrated approach accelerates development by supporting Rapid Prototyping, directly from our Engineering design centers. Instead of lengthy, sequential design cycles, we help you quickly create, test and iterate physical models of your wafer fabrication equipment components in our lab, as an extension of yours.


This fast-turn capability allows your design team to engage in rapid learning, validating concepts and identifying design flaws in days or weeks, rather than months, ultimately bringing a superior, de-risked product to market faster. Feedback from our team can provide early input for manufacturability, cost and longevity prior to[NPI](https://www.plexus.com/solutions/new-product-introduction/) transition.


2. Product Development and Commercialization: Manufacturing Readiness


Many internal engineering teams get stuck in the engineering first phase. They have a brilliant concept but lack the bandwidth or specific[Design for Excellence](https://www.plexus.com/solutions/design-and-development/design-for-excellence/) (DFX) expertise to finalize the design for efficient, high-volume production. We step in to take the final design to a manufacturing-ready state, optimizing for:


- Testability: Integrating functional test requirements from the start
- Cost: Selecting components and streamlining assembly for cost-conscious volume
- Quality: Ensuring full compliance with strict industry standards like Copy Exact


3. Proactive Manufacturing Process Improvement


The best designs still need continuous refinement once they enter high-volume production. We contribute to the feedback loop by proactively sharing opportunities for improvement identified in manufacturing. Our manufacturing floor engineers leverage real-time production data to flag changes that can enhance yield, improve efficiency, and reduce long-term costs. This continuous communication model uses real-world manufacturing intelligence to optimize your next product revision.


4. Strategic End-of-Life (EOL) Management


Managing product obsolescence is a massive time drain for highly skilled internal engineers. We streamline the End-of-Life business by performing critical Bill of Materials (BOM) reviews. We assist with accelerating the process for EOL boards by grouping them based on levels of complexity, identifying alternative components, and clearly equating the cost and risk of replacement, freeing your core engineering team from tedious parts management.
