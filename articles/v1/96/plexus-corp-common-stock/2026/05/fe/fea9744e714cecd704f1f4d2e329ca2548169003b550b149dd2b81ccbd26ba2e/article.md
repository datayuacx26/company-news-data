Following record-breaking Q2 earnings, Plexus Corp. President and CEO, Todd Kelsey, joined CNBC Fast Money on May 30 to discuss how Plexus is leaning into technology to drive strong results.


In the interview, Todd highlights how we are integrating AI and advanced automation to deliver value across our market sectors. This includes leveraging AI-driven insights to enhance our global supply chain and manufacturing floor.


We are also capitalizing on surging demand for semiconductor capital equipment and data center power solutions—the critical infrastructure behind AI.


With a 19% year-over-year revenue increase and a raised outlook for fiscal 2026, we are excited to continue this growth alongside our partners, shareholders and team members as we deliver our vision to help create the products that build a better world.
