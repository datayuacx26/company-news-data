Launching a complex electronic product in highly regulated sectors like healthcare, defense and aerospace is a monumental undertaking. The pressure to innovate rapidly while meeting stringent regulatory standards and navigating volatile global supply chains is immense. For Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), the path from concept to market is often fraught with risk, ranging from intricate technical requirements to a web of uncoordinated providers.


Traditionally, many companies have relied on a multi-provider model—engaging one firm for electronic product design, another for prototyping and yet another for production. However, this approach introduces significant drawbacks, as each handoff creates opportunities for miscommunication, misinterpretation of technical specifications and a dangerous diffusion of accountability.


At Plexus, we provide a powerful alternative: the competitive advantage of a trusted, turnkey product development partner. We act as a single, accountable partner that integrates a full value stream of solutions—from initial[Product Strategy](https://www.plexus.com/solutions/design-and-development/product-strategy/) through[product development](https://www.plexus.com/solutions/design-and-development/product-development/) ,[new product introduction (NPI)](https://www.plexus.com/solutions/new-product-introduction/) and volume manufacturing. This integration eliminates the friction of handoffs, allowing your team to focus on innovation while we provide the comprehensive engineering services and advanced production resources to bring your vision to life.


## Building the Right Foundation with Product Strategy


Many product failures are not rooted in technical flaws, but in a systemic failure to establish a solid strategic foundation that aligns the product direction with the business case. Reworking a product late in the cycle is exponentially more expensive than getting it right at the start. At Plexus, our[Product Strategy](https://www.plexus.com/blog/product-strategy-the-foundation-for-product-success/) process ensures we help you confidently answer two critical questions: “Are we making the right product?” and “Are we making the product right?”


Our engagement includes thorough steps to understand user needs, technical requirements and business guardrails together:


- **Immersion:** We seek to understand the full stakeholder ecosystem, rooted in the user’s experience. Through site visits or simulations, our experts gain genuine insight into the technical context, usability and true cost of existing pain points.
- **Tech Exploration and Stakeholder Interviews:** Our multi-disciplinary teams identify critical knowledge gaps and explore emerging technologies. We prioritize stakeholder needs to illustrate the possible solution spaces for your product.
- **Archetype Development:** We explore Archetypes—mental models and initial architectures—to test assumptions rapidly without the expense of hardware prototypes.
- **Synthesis:** The engagement culminates in a comprehensive development roadmap. We document a single authoritative framework that informs the product design phase, linking every subsequent engineering decision back to original business goals and validated user needs.


## Driving Product Development through Product Maturity Levels (PMLs)


In complex product development, managing the integrity of the product is as vital as managing the schedule of the project. To drive clarity and ensure the right level of rigor, Plexus utilizes a framework known as Product Maturity Levels (PMLs).


While traditional project management tracks activities (e.g., “Is the design complete?”), PMLs seek to drive a clear, objective understanding and communication of the product’s status from initial concept to production-ready. This provides an objective state of truth for informed business decisions.


- **The 7-Level Progression:** The product progresses from PML 1 (Discovered), where the team identifies core technologies, to PML 7 (Qualified), where the team verifies the production process and prepares for volume ramp.
- **Driving Clarity and Depth:** Each level demands a specific depth of detail and stability. This rigor ensures that by the time you reach verification, the product has had the necessary engineering checks to pass quality audits and regulatory hurdles.
- **The PML Champion:** To maintain objectivity, we utilize PML Champions. These are experienced experts who provide an independent assessment of the product maturity, ensuring projects stay on track and meet the high standards required for market success.


## Optimizing for Production: Simultaneous DFX and Test Engineering


The key differentiator of our integrated model is that manufacturing and testing are never afterthoughts. We leverage our experience in volume manufacturing to incorporate[Design for Excellence (DFX)](https://www.plexus.com/solutions/design-and-development/design-for-excellence/) and test engineering as a part of the[design and development](https://www.plexus.com/solutions/design-and-development/) process.


By engaging early in the lifecycle, our team of DFX engineers validates that designs are fully manufacturable from the outset. This approach ensures that your product is optimized for production without the need for costly late-stage redesigns. By embedding manufacturing intelligence directly into the design phase, we ensure that every technical insight matures into a high-yield production roadmap.


Our comprehensive service offerings provide a rigorous defense against launch delays and quality risks across every level of your product:


- **Rule-Driven PCBA and HLA Analysis:** We provide expert analysis at both the PCBA level and for full system High-Level Assembly (HLA). For PCBA, our services include Design for Fabrication (DFF) and Design for Assembly (DFA), all backed by a proprietary ruleset derived from over 45 years of manufacturing best practices.
- **Precision Modeling with VPL:** To ensure absolute accuracy, we utilize the Valor Parts Library (VPL) to create digital models of off-the-shelf components built directly from manufacturer mechanical drawings. This allows us to verify the physical fit and spacing of your design before the first board is ever fabricated.
- **Strategic Test and Access Analysis:** We ensure a sound test strategy is baked into your design, focusing on maximum test probe accessibility to optimize in-circuit testing. This allows us to create products that are tested easily and comprehensively, tailored specifically to your business needs and industry standards.
- **Lifecycle and Compliance Protection:** Our team performs Electronic Component Lifecycle (ECL) and RoHS compliance analyses. By identifying components nearing obsolescence or those with regulatory risks early, we protect your design from immediate supply chain disruptions.


By identifying potential hurdles during the design phase, we align on risk-mitigation strategies that finalize the roadmap for production. This proactive collaboration ensures that when your product reaches the factory floor, it is ready for high-yield, reliable manufacturing and scalable success.


## Accelerating the Finish Line: Integrated NPI Transition


The bridge between a verified design and a scalable product is the New Product Introduction (NPI) phase. When moving to a new partner for[manufacturing](https://www.plexus.com/solutions/manufacturing/) , this is often a major choke point. With Plexus, moving into production is an “ease of transition” because the design, engineering and manufacturing teams function as a single, cohesive entity.


To ensure superb execution, a dedicated[Transition Manager](https://www.plexus.com/blog/transition-management-for-successful-contract-manufacturing/) acts as your single point of accountability, coordinating all stakeholders globally. The Transition Manager navigates between teams to maintain strong, open communication while managing complex launch dynamics. By identifying one leader with expert teams behind them, we overcome the hurdles to turn your complex innovation into a market-ready reality.


The unity of our teams stems from our shared global values and vision to create products that build a better world. Instead of simply passing files across departments, our teams work toward a collective goal: delivering high-quality, reliable products to the people who need them. This cultural and operational alignment eliminates “cold handoffs” and knowledge gaps. Because we share the same data, rigorous quality standards and an unwavering commitment to your success, we preserve the intent of the design through every stage of the build.
