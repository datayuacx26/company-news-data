# Range Resources Statement on Shell Petrochemical Announcement

June 14, 2016 12:42 pm


[Jobs & Economy](https://www.rangeresources.com/topics/jobs-economy/)


[Press](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/press/)


Range Resources applauds Shell’s decision to move forward with their western Pennsylvania petrochemical facility. This is great news for workers and consumers and another example of the positive benefits of shale in Pennsylvania. With continued responsible development of the area’s abundant natural gas and with smart policies in place the industry can continue to create family-sustaining jobs in the region.
