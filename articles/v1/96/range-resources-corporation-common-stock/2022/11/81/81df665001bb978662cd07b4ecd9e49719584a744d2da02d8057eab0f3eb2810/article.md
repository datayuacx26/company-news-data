# Range Hosting 2000 Turkeys Drive

November 10, 2022 9:43 pm


[Uncategorized](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/uncategorized/)


Join Range employees and our community friends to support Washington County families this Thanksgiving. We’re hitting the streets in downtown Canonsburg and Washington to collect monetary donations for the **Greater Pittsburgh Food Bank** and **Food Helpers** . Range is matching all donations up to $25,000 to make sure every family has access to a Thanksgiving meal this year.


Check out our Facebook Event Here for all the details.


**[Facebook: Range Resources](https://www.rangeresources.com/range-hosting-2000-turkeys-drive/Range%20Resources%202000%20Turkeys%20Drive)[2000 Turkeys Drive](https://fb.me/e/2tzy9G5ae)**


We’ll be on street corners adjacent to Arc Human Services & Chicco Baccello in Canonsburg (111 West Pike Street) and the Farmers Market Pavilion in Washington (139 South Main Street). We look forward to seeing you there!


You can also donate online any time from now until the event here.
