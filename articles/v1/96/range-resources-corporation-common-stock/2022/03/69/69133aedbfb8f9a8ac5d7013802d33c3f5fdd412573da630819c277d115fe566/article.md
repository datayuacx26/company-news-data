# Range Announces $125k in 2022 Good Neighbors Fund Grants through Washington County Community Foundation

March 7, 2022 9:32 am


[Uncategorized](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/uncategorized/)


Range recently announced its dedication of $125,000 in 2022 first responder grants through its Good Neighbors Fund. The fund, which was established in 2018 and is made possible through its partnership with the Washington County Community Foundation (WCCF), has provided roughly $460,000 to date to police, fire, and emergency management services organizations in Southwestern PA, where we operate.


From left to right: Smith Township Police Department Chief Winford LaRue, Range Resources Community Relations Specialist Christina Kramer, and Smith Township Police Department Sergeant Bruce Carlton.


Range’s Christina Kramer says, “It is incredible to consider that since we began this initiative, the Range Resources Good Neighbors Fund has awarded nearly $460,000 to help local first responder organizations within our region.”


“Dedication, skill, and sacrifice are demonstrated by these local heroes on a daily basis, and these grants have become a special way for us to show our appreciation as well as support them in their service,” she said.


Eligible organizations can apply for up to $10,000 in grant funding through March 31, 2022, with recipients announced this spring.
