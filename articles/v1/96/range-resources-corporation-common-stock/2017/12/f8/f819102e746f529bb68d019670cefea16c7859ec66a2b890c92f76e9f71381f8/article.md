# Toys For Tots: Range Resources Donates $50K Worth Of Toys

December 27, 2017 1:26 am


[Community](https://www.rangeresources.com/topics/community/)


[Press](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/press/)


With help from employees, vendors and the[Observer-Reporter](https://observer-reporter.com/community/community_notices/toys-for-tots-campaign-goal-exceeded/article_bb1769fa-df78-11e7-b493-0f2951867b03.html) , Range volunteers stuffed a box truck full of donations for the Toys for Tots campaign. All in, Range was able to donate more than[$50,000 worth of toys this](https://observer-reporter.com/community/community_notices/toys-for-tots-campaign-goal-exceeded/article_bb1769fa-df78-11e7-b493-0f2951867b03.html) year.


Range’s 2017 Toys For Tots campaign was the Company’s most successful one yet. Watch a quick video of Range volunteers kicking off this year’s campaign below.
