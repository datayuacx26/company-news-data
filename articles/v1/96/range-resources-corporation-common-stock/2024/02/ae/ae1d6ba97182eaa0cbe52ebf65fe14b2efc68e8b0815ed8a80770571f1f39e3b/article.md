# Range Resources Named Among America’s 2024 Most Just Companies

February 6, 2024 2:33 pm


[Press](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/press/)[Uncategorized](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/uncategorized/)


We are excited to share that Range was again named to the annual[Just Capital & CNBC’s America’s Most Just Companies list](https://nam02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https://justcapital.com/rankings/&data=05%7c02%7cnpompeani%40rangeresources.com%7c5df4e04e582143d22d0f08dc2734bd85%7c1a68cf95d1ee493e8f7b44ecf728efdf%7c0%7c0%7c638428354676873355%7cUnknown%7cTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7c0%7c%7c%7c&sdata=AKbF9A4YzfiK0E8pKaNxbMXTbBHEbFNGSRQTnbeWLJc%3D&reserved=0) **.**


The rankings assess performance of the largest U.S.-based corporations and help differentiate companies across five key categories: workers, communities, customers, environment, and shareholders & governance.


[Range Resources Company Performance Page](https://nam02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https://justcapital.com/companies/range-resources-corporation&data=05%7c02%7cnpompeani%40rangeresources.com%7c5df4e04e582143d22d0f08dc2734bd85%7c1a68cf95d1ee493e8f7b44ecf728efdf%7c0%7c0%7c638428354676884157%7cUnknown%7cTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7c0%7c%7c%7c&sdata=hynNsmLjklOchg1jXK4%2BKvF07ihnmu9f0Avaigde9SI%3D&reserved=0)


Range ranked 271st overall out of 937 companies, and 7th out of 23 qualifying oil and gas industry. Importantly though, Range ranked **first among Appalachian producers.**


Range outperformed industry averages in four of the five categories and **is the “Industry Best” in the Environmental category.** Range will also be included in the JUST ETF / JULCD Index by performing in the top half of the Oil & Gas industry.
