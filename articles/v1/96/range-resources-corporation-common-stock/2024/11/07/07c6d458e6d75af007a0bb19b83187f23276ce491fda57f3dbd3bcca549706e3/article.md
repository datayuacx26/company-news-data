# 2000 Turkeys Food Drive Kicks Off on Wednesday, November 20, 2024

November 14, 2024 12:46 pm


[Uncategorized](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/uncategorized/)


The annual 2000 Turkeys Food Drive is well underway, but Range volunteers, along with Washington County and W&J students, will be hitting the streets in downtown Canonsburg and downtown Washington on November 20, 2024, to raise funds for food insecure families in Washington County to enjoy a traditional Thanksgiving meal this holiday season.


From 8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., volunteers can be found at the following locations:


**Washington**
Farmers Market Pavilion
(139 S. Main Street, Washington, PA 15301)
and surrounding street corners


**Canonsburg**
Chicco Baccello/Arc Human Services
(111 W Pike St, Canonsburg, PA 15317)
and surrounding street corners


[Click here to donate online.](https://secure.qgiv.com/event/2000turkeys24)


Range is matching all donations made online, cash or checks collected in person on November 20 up to $25,000! Checks can be made out to 2000 Turkeys.
