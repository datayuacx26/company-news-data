# Range Named One of America’s 2026 Most Responsible Companies for Fifth Consecutive Year

December 15, 2025 11:38 am


[Press](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/press/)


For five years running, Range has been named to Newsweek’s list[of America’s Most Responsible Companies](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-most-responsible-companies-2026) , including the recently released 2026 list.


The recognition comes from an evaluation of the nation’s top companies displaying responsibility in the categories of environmental, social, and governance (ESG).


[America’s Most Responsible Companies 2026](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-most-responsible-companies-2026)


Much of the data evaluated by Newsweek comes from Range’s annual[Corporate Sustainability Report (CSR)](https://www.rangeresources.com/sustainability/) .


This report provides insight into Range’s approach, achievements, and goals on environmental, social, and governance topics.


For several years now, Range remains the only Appalachian producer to make it on the list, and shares this recognition with companies like NVIDIA, General Motors, Dell, and Apple.


This recognition supports our sustainability goals, most notably our recent achievements of reaching Net Zero Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions, expanded “A” grade MiQ certification for all Pennsylvania production, and 83% reduction in methane emissions intensity since 2019.


> “We are guided by our Core Values,” said Range CEO & President, Dennis Degner. “They anchor our culture and shape how we operate responsibly, support our people, and deliver long-term value. As a team, our commitment to sustainability has grown into a meaningful competitive advantage.”
