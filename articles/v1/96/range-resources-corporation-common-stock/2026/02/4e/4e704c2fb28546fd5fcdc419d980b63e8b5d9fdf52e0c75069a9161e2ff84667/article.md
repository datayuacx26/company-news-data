# Range Resources Receives 2026 Pittsburgh Excellence in Ethics Award

February 17, 2026 1:10 pm


[Press](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/press/)[Stories](https://www.rangeresources.com/resource/stories/)


Range is proud to be the recipient of the 2026 Pittsburgh Excellence in Ethics Award hosted by the Southwestern Pennsylvania Rotarians together with the Allegheny Conference on Community Development, University of Pittsburgh Institute for Entrepreneurial Excellence, Robert Morris University, The Pittsburgh Rotary Club Foundation, and The Pittsburgh Business Exchange.


This event celebrates Pittsburgh area organizations setting the bar for ethical practices. Along with this prestigious local award, Range has also been named to[Newsweek’s list of America’s Most Responsible Companies](https://www.rangeresources.com/range-named-among-americas-2026-most-responsible-companies-for-fifth-consecutive-year/) for five consecutive years.


Laural Ziemba, Director of Public Relations, received the award on behalf of the Company and recognized that Range’s employees exemplify ethical standards each day.


While Range’s employees are at the heart of our practices, here are some practical ways Range has committed to ethical operations:


**Environmental Stewardship:** Range was the first company to voluntarily disclose hydraulic fracturing fluids in 2010. Since then, Range has continued to lead the industry in transparency and best practices, including ambient air monitoring on a variety of well pad sites and internationally recognized MiQ “A” grade certified (low methane emissions) with all Pennsylvania production. Range also recycles approximately 100% of flowback and produced water generated from our operations.


**Charitable Giving:** In 2025 alone, Range partnered with more than 500 unique nonprofits spanning Pennsylvania and Texas, including over 100 new partners, totaling $1.36 million.


**Community Impact:** In addition to charitable donations, Range employee volunteers had a record year spending over 3,400 volunteer hours in the communities where we live and work to give back to nonprofits and worthy causes.


**Net Zero Emissions:** Since 2024, Range Resources has reached Net Zero Scope 1 & Scope 2 GHG emissions through direct emissions reductions and verified carbon offsets.
