# 5 Free Webflow Cloneables Worth Your Attention Right Now

## **5 Free Webflow Cloneables Worth Your Attention Right Now**


Based on a[YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EZIY1IpQyDY) by Magnaem @ Memberstack


**TL;DR:** The Made in Webflow page is still producing great work. Here are five free cloneables, from scroll-driven transitions to full landing page templates, that you can grab and use in your projects today.


The Made in Webflow page does not always get the attention it deserves, but there are creators on there doing genuinely impressive work. Here are five cloneables that stood out recently and are worth checking out..


**[SVG Mask Blinds Transition](https://webflow.com/made-in-webflow/website/hero-blinds) — Richard Segura -** A scroll-driven transition between images using SVG masks. The effect is smooth and distinctive, not something you see often in the Webflow community. Clone it and use it as a starting point for your own scroll interactions.


**[Accordion Parallax Carousel](https://webflow.com/made-in-webflow/website/elastic-carousel) — Richard Segura -** A parallax carousel where scrolling separates the images and clicking any one of them expands it to a full view. Clean, well thought out, and ready to clone.


**[Minimal Image Tabs](https://webflow.com/@robingranqvist) — Robin Granqvist -** Tab navigation where the tab headers sit directly over the images and clicking each one transitions to a different image. The layout approach is creative and adaptable, not just for images, but for any content you want to present in a tabbed format.


**[Aurelia](https://webflow.com/made-in-webflow/website/aur3lia) — Muhamad Fadli -** A free e-commerce landing page template with bold overlaid typography and one of the cleaner navigations you will find built in Webflow. The whole template is well structured and free to use as a base for your own store.


**[Dwellix](https://webflow.com/made-in-webflow/website/dwellix) — Ink Studio -** A property listing template with a strong hero header, clear content hierarchy, and generous whitespace throughout. You do not have to use the whole template, even just individual sections are worth pulling into your own builds.


**FAQ**


**Q. Are all of these actually free?** Yes. All five are free cloneables available directly on the[Made in Webflow](https://webflow.com/made-in-webflow) page.


**Q. Can I use parts of a template without cloning the whole thing?** Absolutely. Webflow cloneables let you pull in the full project and use only the sections or components you need.


**Q. Do these work for non-design use cases?** Some of them, yes. The minimal image tabs clonable in particular is built in a way that adapts well beyond just images — you can swap in whatever content fits your project.
