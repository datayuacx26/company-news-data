## **What is an Externship?**


Simply put, an Externship is remote work experience delivered in the format of a class.


It is a structured, short-term, virtual experience that blends academic learning with real-world business projects.


Unlike internships, which typically require full-time commitments, Externships last 8-12 weeks with just 10 hours per week, making them ideal for students who need flexibility. Externships are designed to be completed alongside coursework or even another internship, allowing students to test-drive multiple career paths and industries before committing to a specific job or graduate program.


At Extern, students can choose from a range of Externships in industries like finance, consulting, strategy, product and marketing—all with mentorship and training built-in.

## **What an Externship is NOT: Common Misconceptions**


We hear these misconceptions all the time, so we’re here to set the record straight. To clear things up, here’s what an Externship **is NOT** :


❌ **Externships are NOT case studies or simulations.** Every Externship is a *real* business project, often designed to generate insights for an actual company.


❌ **Externships are NOT busy work.** Externs receive structured training and apply job-ready skills to meaningful projects.


❌ **Externships are NOT job shadowing.** Instead of watching others work, Externs actively participate in business projects and problem-solving.


❌ **Externships are NOT long-term commitments.** They’re designed to be short, flexible, and remote, making them accessible for students with busy schedules.


❌ **Externships are NOT internships.** While they provide real professional experience, externships are different from internships that are hosted directly by companies. See below for the fill list of the differences! However, some **Externships are Internship-Track** , meaning top performers may be considered for paid internships or full-time roles directly with the host company.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Externships


### What is an externship?


An externship is a short-term, project-based professional experience that lets you work on real business challenges for a company. Unlike internships, externships are typically remote, flexible, and range from one to four weeks. They're designed to help you build portfolio-ready work without needing to relocate or commit to a full summer.


### What is the difference between an externship and an internship?


Internships are usually 10 to 12 weeks, often require relocation, and come with a formal application and interview process. Externships are shorter (one to four weeks), remote, and focused on completing a specific project deliverable. Both give you professional experience, but externships are more accessible since they don't require a competitive hiring process or time away from school.


### Are externships worth it?


Yes. In Extern's post-program survey of 1,800+ students, 93% rated their experience positively and 88% said the live mentorship sessions were valuable. Externships give you a tangible project to put on your resume and talk about in interviews. Hiring managers care about what you can do, not how long you spent somewhere. A four-week externship where you delivered a strategy deck or data analysis carries more weight than a vague summer role with no clear output.


### How do externships work?


You choose a company and project track, then complete structured deliverables over one to four weeks. You work at your own pace with guidance from mentors and a clear brief. At the end, you have a finished portfolio piece and a credential you can add to LinkedIn and your resume.


### Can an externship help me get a job?


Absolutely. Externships give you two things employers look for: relevant experience and proof of initiative. They fill resume gaps, give you specific stories for behavioral interviews, and show that you sought out real-world work on your own. Many externs report getting interview callbacks after adding their externship to their resume.


### Who can do an externship?


Anyone. Externships are open to college students at any year, recent graduates, and career changers. There's no GPA requirement, no application essay, and no interview. If you're looking to build experience in consulting, marketing, data analytics, finance, or other fields, you can start immediately.
