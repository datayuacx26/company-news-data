# Rig 439 Hosted the U.S. Department of Energy In Partnership With Exxon and PSP


---


Today, Rig 439 in West Texas gave representatives from the Department of Energy a firsthand look at how technology and expertise are shaping modern drilling operations.


Hosted in partnership with[ExxonMobil](https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/) and the[Permian Strategic Partnership](https://permianpartnership.org/) , the visit showcased H&P’s FlexRobotics® technology in action and highlighted the innovation happening every day in the field. The tour highlighted automated pipe-handling systems operating on the rig floor, real-time data supporting operational decisions and the seamless coordination required to keep everything running safely and efficiently.


[FlexRobotics®](https://www.hpinc.com/technologies/flexrobotics) is working on rigs today, helping advance drilling performance while supporting safer, more efficient operations.


Thank you to the Rig 439 crew for representing H&P so well and demonstrating the leadership, expertise and commitment to excellence that continue to set our company apart.
