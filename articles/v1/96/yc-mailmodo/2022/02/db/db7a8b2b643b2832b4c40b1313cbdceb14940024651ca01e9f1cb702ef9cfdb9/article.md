# How to Use Your Brand Logo in Your Email Campaigns

## Where can you add a logo to your email?


Now that you know the benefit of a logo in emails let's find out where you can add your company logo in emails.


### Sender ID photo


You can add it as the profile photo of the email address that you use to send your[email campaigns](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-marketing-campaign/) . It is one of the first elements your subscribers will see in their inbox, and the logo in the sender's profile photo helps influence whether they open the email or not.


Here,[Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/) has put its logo in the sender's profile photo with its signature yellow brand color. The logo with the brand color immediately lets the people know exactly who the email is from, encouraging them to open and read the email.


### Email header


A logo in the[email header](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-header/) is one of the more subtle but still impactful ways to establish your brand's identity within the email.


As you can observe from the example below,[Nike](https://reallygoodemails.com/emails/forget-fomosee-whats-waiting-for-you) put their logo in the email header and nothing else, but still, we can remember and recognize the brand with just that logo.


### Email footer


The footer is the last thing a person sees when reading your email before leaving. So, you have to add your logo there to finish the email with a bang and reestablish your brand's identity like how[H&M](https://www.hm.com/entrance.ahtml?orguri=%2F) has done here.


However, most people don't use this precious real estate in their emails. Check out our guide on[email footers](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-footer/) to find out how to optimize the footer at the bottom of your emails.


## How to add an email logo to email marketing campaigns


Here we'll discuss the steps you can take to add your[brand logo](https://www.zilliondesigns.com/blog/type-of-logo-for-brand/) to the email campaigns.


### Sender photo


To add your logo to your sender profile photo, you can set up a BIMI record. By implementing BIMI, you signal your email providers that your messages are authenticated, which can improve deliverability rates and reduce the likelihood of your emails being marked as spam.
If you have implemented BIMI, your logo will appear in the subscriber's inbox provided that the email client supports BIMI. To use BIMI, you must first fulfill the[email authentication](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-authentication/) processes listed below:


1.


Set up a Sender Policy Framework ([SPF](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/spf/) ), which specifies which IP addresses can send emails to the domain.


2.


Implement Domain Keys Identified Mail ([DKIM](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/dkim/) ), which adds a digital signature to emails and confirms that they haven't been tampered with while in transit.


3.


Implement[DMARC](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/dmarc/) , a protocol built on SPF/DKIM that instructs the receiving server what to do if SPF/DKIM authentication fails.


4.


After you've completed your login, create a Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG) file and upload it to the server.


5.


The Verified Mark Certificate (VMC) certification process (applicable exclusively to non-Gmail participants) entails a specific identity and trademark verification.


6.


To implement Domain Name System (DNS) records for BIMI, go to your DNS hosting provider and create a new record.


Your logo will be reviewed and displayed in the recipient's inbox once all initial processes have been accomplished. You can use the[BIMI Inspector](https://bimigroup.org/bimi-generator/) to see if your brand conforms with BIMI standards.


### Header


If you are using an[Email Service Provider (ESP)](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-service-provider/) , here are some steps you need to take to add your logo to your email header.


We are using[Mailmodo](https://www.mailmodo.com/) to demonstrate the steps, but these steps are more or less similar for all ESPs.


1.


Go to the[template builder](https://www.mailmodo.com/features/email-template-builder/) in your ESP.


2.


Click on the insert image button.


3.


Upload your logo and resize it to your preferred size to fit your email. If you don’t already have branded assets ready, an[AI logo](https://www.adobe.com/express/create/ai/logo) generator can help you quickly create a polished design optimized for email headers and footers.


4.


Place it at the top of your email in the header region.


### Footer


The steps are the same as the header for adding your logo in the footer. You will have to add it as an image in the footer and resize its width to fit properly amongst other elements in the footer.


## How to add an email logo to your email signature


Adding a logo to an email signature isn’t difficult at all. The steps vary based on the email client you use. Let’s see how you can add a logo to your email signature using Gmail and Outlook. You can easily create a professional logo using a[logo maker](https://www.design.com/logo-maker) before inserting it into your signature.


### Gmail


1. Sign in to your Gmail account.
2. Click on the gear icon in the top right corner, then select "See all settings."
3. Scroll down to the "Signature" section.
4. If you don’t have a signature, click “Create new” and create one. If you have an existing signature, click on it to edit.
5. Place your cursor where you want the logo to appear. Click the image icon in the formatting toolbar. You can upload an image from your computer or insert an image URL.
6. Click on the inserted image to resize it if needed.
7. After you finish editing your signature, scroll down and click “Save Changes” at the bottom of the settings page.


### Outlook


1. Launch the Outlook application on your computer.
2. Click on the gear icon in the top right corner to open settings.
3. Click the “Signatures…” button. To create a new signature, click “New” and name your signature. To edit an existing signature, select the signature you want to edit from the list.
4. Place your cursor in the signature editor where you want the logo to appear. Click the image icon to insert an image. Browse to find your logo file and click “Insert.”
5. You can resize the image by clicking on it and dragging the corners.
6. Choose your new signature as the default for new emails and/or replies.
7. Click on save and then exit the settings.


## Best practices to follow when placing the logo in emails


While adding a logo to your emails might seem straightforward, there are a few tips that can help you make it more effective in the email.


### Use a transparent PNG


Images without a background are known as transparent PNGs, and they are commonly identified by a checkerboard pattern, as depicted in the example below.


When email clients change the email from light to[dark mode](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/dark-mode-email/) , a transparent PNG logo ensures that the image will not have a jarring background. And, when your subscribers view your email in dark mode, they will not be exposed to bright backdrops, which will improve their user experience.


However, if you're using transparent PNGs in your email, remember to shrink the file size before sending it, as they tend to be larger.


### Have a white border around your logo


In the dark mode, if the transparent picture of the logo or text is black, it has a high possibility of the logo disappearing into the backdrop, as shown in the image below.


So, you can add a white border around the black elements to make the logo or text apparent even in dark mode as shown in the image below. The white element will just merge into the background when seen in light mode.


### Add an alt text to the images


Alt text tags are text snippets that explain an image, in this case, your logo, and help make[emails accessible](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-accessibility/) . When the email client cannot load an image file, they come in helpful. If the email doesn't load for your readers, they'll see the alt text instead of the image. So, even if your logo isn't visible, it helps readers recognize it, as shown in the following image.

### Test your emails to check if the logo renders well


Send yourself a[test](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-testing/) email after adding your logo to your[email signature](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-signature/) area to ensure the logo is properly placed. Make sure your logo is noticeable but not so enormous that it overwhelms the remainder of your email.


Experiment with the positioning, font and sizing of your logo to ensure that it is visually consistent with the rest of your email signature and fits comfortably within your[email template](https://www.mailmodo.com/email-templates/) .
