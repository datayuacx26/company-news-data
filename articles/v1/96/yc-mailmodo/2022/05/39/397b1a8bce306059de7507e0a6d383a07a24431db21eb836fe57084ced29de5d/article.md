# A Guide to Email Outreach for Link Building

In 2020, there were an estimated[306.4 billion emails](https://www.statista.com/statistics/456500/daily-number-of-e-mails-worldwide/) sent worldwide per day. So, how do you make your email stand out so that the site owner opens it?


The key is to create an attention-grabbing and personalized[subject line](https://www.mailmodo.com/email-subject-lines/) .[A/B testing](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/ab-testing/) can help you find the best one for your webpage and link-building campaign. You should test different subject lines to check metrics such as[open rates](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/good-email-open-rate/) ,[click-through rates](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/ctor-vs-ctr/) , and response rates.

### 3. Write a strong hook


The subject line is your first step. Don't waste the recipient's time with a mediocre introduction. Keep it brief and create credibility.


Your email's opening lines should be no more than a sentence or two long. Introduce yourself, stating your position and the company you represent.


### 4. Have a strong pitch


A pitch or link request is an important part of the outreach email. A playful and customized subject and introduction can draw the viewer's interest. A successful link request should include the six components listed below:


-


#### Ensure minimalism


Make your request as straightforward as possible. Site owners get a massive number of link requests daily. Most of them do not have the time to check them all. The pitch should be clear, precise, and scannable to increase the chances of a response.


Each line should contain no more than three sentences. Include a link to the page you're referring to and the resource you're utilizing. To improve outreach success,[link building services](https://saynine.ai/blog/best-link-building-agencies/) can help craft targeted, well-structured pitches that increase acceptance rates and strengthen your backlink profile.


-


#### Customize your pitch template


One of the most common mistakes made during outreach is sticking to one template. Using the same template throughout the link-building campaign reduces the chances of obtaining a backlink. This is because the email will appear generic, something top[SaaS link building agencies](https://rankwizards.co/articles/saas-link-building-agencies) avoid by personalizing every pitch.


-


#### Make it personal


Your outreach should sound genuine, from the subject line to the salutation.[Personalization](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-personalization/) requires an understanding of your[target audience](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/target-audience/) . It is important to maintain authenticity. It is an essential component of cold email outreach.


-


#### Create shareable content


The most essential element of a link-building campaign is content.[Link building](https://linkdune.com/) and content go together. You can create the best[content strategy](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/content-marketing-strategy/) in the world, but it will be basically worthless if no one knows about it.


If you don't regularly share in-depth content, you'll have to rely on[other methods](https://www.singlegrain.com/seo/effective-seo-techniques-that-work/) to expand your SEO strategy. You will need to pinpoint the assets on your website that are valuable.[Linkable assets](https://editorial.link/linkable-assets/) are useful, in-depth, non-promotional, top-of-funnel, and evergreen content.


**Related guide** :[6 Tips to Write Cold Email That Gets You Response](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/how-to-cold-email/)


-


#### Bring forth a unique value proposition


In the email, illustrate the value of your content piece. This is about the site offering useful insights to its own audience. In the outreach email, you should discuss how the asset will benefit the prospect's audience.


**Related guide** :[How To Write a Unique Selling Proposition for Your Brand](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/unique-selling-proposition/)


-


#### Have a memorable CTA


Your email should end with a clickable call to action. Make sure that there is no ambiguity. Ask the site admin if they are willing to take part in including this resource on their website.


To make it easier for them, include a mailto link in your email. This reduces their workload, allowing them to reply with a pre-filled subject line, CC, and body content. To create these types of links, use[Mailto Link Generator](https://www.mailmodo.com/tools/mailto-link-generator/) .


### 5. Follow up


After sending an email, the next thing you can do is to wait it out. If you haven’t received a reply the next day, don't worry. It’s not because your email outreach is a failure. You have to remember that everyone is busy.


Even if you have sent out the perfect email, it can get buried under the hundreds of emails they receive. You can send them a[follow-up email](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/follow-up-email/) after a couple of days, but don't bother them too much if they don't respond. Give them enough time to respond.


Allow your pitch to lapse after one of two follow-ups. You can, then, contact them with another pitch later if you still haven’t gotten any response.
