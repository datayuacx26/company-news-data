# How To Do Salesforce Email Automation: Benefits & Alternative

## Why choose Salesforce email automation?


Here are some of the key advantages of email automations available in Salesforce:


### 1. Readily available in all native solutions**


You can create an email right away if you're signed in to Salesforce and any native platform solutions. This feature is embedded into every Salesforce-based product, including Accounting Seed.


### 2. Messaging streamlining


Send emails straight from personal accounts or from any type of data you have access to, such as leads, contacts, opportunities, case records, and campaigns. Send an email with attachments from within Salesforce to any functional email address. Many organizations also rely on[salesforce customization services](https://www.avenga.com/salesforce-customization/) to tailor these capabilities to their specific workflows and communication needs.


### 3. Attachment of file


You can securely include file notes or pertinent information in your email, such as a bill. Of course, you can also transfer and upload files that are not on Salesforce or organize them more efficiently using[Salesforce Files](https://titandxp.com/article/data/files-salesforce/) .


### 4. Complete security


Securely send emails containing important financial or personal information. To prevent people from viewing attachments, employ page layouts and Salesforce permissions. Additionally, you can keep track of every email action for your records. You may see the correspondence's times, senders, and other details. Managers can also impose internal limitations as needed.


### 5. Targeted messaging


Need to send messages for a specific event or at a specified time? Schedule Salesforce emails to notify personnel of tasks to be completed or information to be updated. Similarly, implementing[Salesforce case assignment](https://kubaru.io/salesforce-case-assignment/) ensures incoming support requests are automatically directed to the appropriate team member for faster resolution. Sales representatives, for instance, can plan emails to prospects and contacts as a follow-up to calls.


### 6. Send group emails


Send a copy of an email to a list of people to speed up communication and maintain consistency in content and updates. Salesforce provides templates with merge fields that you may use to customize each copy of the email in addition to generating email lists.


## How to set up email automation in Salesforce


To set up email automation in Salesforce, you can utilize Salesforce Flows, which provide a powerful and customizable automation tool. Here is a step-by-step guide to configuring email automation using Salesforce Flows:


### 1. Create a flow


Navigate to Flows in Salesforce and click on the "New Flow" button. Give your Flow a name and select the "Autolaunched Flow (No Trigger)" type.


### 2. Design the flow


Add elements such as loops, assignments, and decisions to configure the Flow according to your automation requirements. These elements allow you to run logical operators and define the conditions for triggering automated emails. In many organizations,[Salesforce admin support](https://www.synebo.io/services/salesforce-administration-services/) can be used at this stage to ensure workflows are properly configured and aligned with business requirements.


### 3. Add email actions


Include the "Send Email" action element in your Flow. Customize the element by selecting the email template, recipients, subject line, and any other necessary details. You can utilize merge fields to personalize the emails based on customer data.


### 4. Test and debug


Use the Flow Builder menu to debug your Flow and test different scenarios. Verify the email recipients and content with test data to ensure everything is functioning as intended.


### 5. Activate the flow


Once you are satisfied with the configuration and testing, activate the Flow to start the email automation process. The Flow will now automatically send out emails based on the specified triggers and conditions.
