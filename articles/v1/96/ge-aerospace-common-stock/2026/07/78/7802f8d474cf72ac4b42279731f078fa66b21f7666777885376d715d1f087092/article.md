# Austrian Airlines Selects GE Aerospace FlightPulse® to Expand Flight Data Analytics to the Cockpit

*New capability builds on Austrian Airlines’ long-standing use of GE Aerospace’s EMS for safety and fuel analytics, giving pilots personalized, flight-specific insights*


**FARNBOROUGH, U.K. —** GE Aerospace today announced that Austrian Airlines, part of the Lufthansa Group, has signed for FlightPulse®, a GE Aerospace, Software as a Service (SaaS) mobile application that connects operational flight data directly to pilot decision-making before and after each flight. The announcement follows a signing at the Farnborough International Airshow.


Austrian Airlines is a long-standing GE Aerospace customer for Safety Insight and Fuel Insight, using GE Aerospace’s EMS platform to process flight data analytics today. With FlightPulse, Austrian Airlines can extend the value of those insights beyond historical reports by integrating flight-specific feedback into pilot workflows to support more informed decision-making in the cockpit.


“Airlines have a wealth of flight data available today, and we commend the team at Austrian Airlines for wanting to get valuable insights directly into the hands of the pilots at the point of decision,” said Andrew Coleman, President and General Manager, GE Aerospace, Software as a Service. “Austrian Airlines knows the value of flight data analytics powered by the EMS platform. By adopting FlightPulse, they are broadening access to fight-specific insights, helping pilots make more calculated choices aligned with safety, fuel awareness, and sustainability objectives.”


Together, Fuel Insight and FlightPulse help teams move from data collection to data-driven action by delivering accessible insights that can be used to inform priorities and measure progress. Across GE Aerospace, SaaS, customers have seen a ~15% increase in fuel-saving procedure adoption and ~20% increase in sustainability procedure success rates. By incorporating FlightPulse and Fuel solutions into their workflows, customers have averaged around 5.71M kg CO2 emissions avoided and 1.88M kg of fuel saved in their first year.


“This signing reinforces our focus on giving pilots access to practical insights they can use before and after each flight,” said Oliver Cantele, Capt. and Project Lead, Austrian Airlines/Lufthansa Group. “We already see how powerful flight data is through the analytics we process using GE Aerospace’s EMS platform. With FlightPulse, that insight becomes available to pilots in a more direct way, supporting decisions tied to safety, fuel-saving procedures, and our sustainability goals.”


**About Austrian**


Austrian Airlines is Austria's leading airline with a worldwide route network of more than 120 destinations. With its hub at Vienna Airport, Austrian Airlines connects Austria with Europe and the whole world. As Austria's flag carrier, Austrian Airlines embodies highest service quality and acts responsibly to society and the environment. Austrian Airlines aims to achieve a neutral CO2 balance by 2050 through reduction and compensation measures. Already by 2030, Austrian wants to halve its net CO2 emissions compared to 2019 through reduction and compensation measures. Austrian Airlines' environmental management has been validated according to the EMAS standard since 2023.


Austrian Airlines is part of Lufthansa Group, Europe’s largest airline group, and a member of the Star Alliance, the first global group of international airlines.
