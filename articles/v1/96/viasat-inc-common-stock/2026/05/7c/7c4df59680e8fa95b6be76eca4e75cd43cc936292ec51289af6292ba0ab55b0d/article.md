# Viasat Sets May 28, 2026 for Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Results Conference Call and Webcast


[PDF Version](https://investors.viasat.com/node/27641/pdf)


CARLSBAD, Calif., May 22, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Viasat, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Zf8at1FBMLWHEh-Vvj79kxgs41CB59GclWBcINHOGtZ4Q1ZhFOlkt2HZqQEwLGyv8lDv-s6ktNH54SuuDQ63tA==) (NASDAQ: VSAT), a global leader in satellite communications, today announced it will release its fourth quarter and fiscal year 2026 financial results on Thursday, May 28, 2026 after market close. Results will be provided in a letter to shareholders, which will be posted to the[Investor Relations](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=2C9SBsnrxXm5SAW2Rdcd3Liq7pja6UDwYzXFEDJlbo_FZOI-jH1GZWzrm6dknZH8kaqUWOCSKGBZw87qgFWYv6pHrXfrQK5Dz9itw9W2PKk=) section of the Company’s website.


Viasat will also host a conference call and webcast on Thursday, May 28, 2026 at 2:30 p.m. Pacific Time / 5:30 p.m. Eastern Time to discuss results.


To participate on the live conference call, please dial (800) 715-9871 (toll-free in the U.S. and Canada) or (646) 307-1963 (international), and reference conference ID 2206055.


A live webcast will be available in Viasat’s[Investor Relations](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=2C9SBsnrxXm5SAW2Rdcd3A6Rn7ZlBoUPlwQFDd1aVd_sjkevY4uurdyOZ3IOrrz5Gm-NiXy_H_IyJTwtV8T4sqp0ZDnjUSx-GvWKeQ6cJd8=) section of Viasat’s website. A replay of the webcast will be archived immediately following the conference call.


**About Viasat**
Viasat is a global communications company that believes everyone and everything in the world can be connected. With offices in 24 countries around the world, our mission shapes how consumers, businesses, governments and militaries around the world communicate and connect. Viasat is developing the ultimate global communications network to power high-quality, reliable, secure, affordable, fast connections to positively impact people's lives anywhere they are—on the ground, in the air or at sea, while building a sustainable future in space. In May 2023, Viasat completed its acquisition of Inmarsat, combining the teams, technologies and resources of the two companies to create a new global communications partner.
