# MasTec Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Conference Call

## MasTec Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Conference Call


April 17, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.mastec.com/node/20251/pdf)


CORAL GABLES, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 17, 2026-- MasTec, Inc. (NYSE: MTZ) will release its first quarter financial results on Thursday, April 30, 2026 after the market close. In addition, MasTec’s senior management will host a webcast to review these results on Friday, May 1, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. ET.


The event will be broadcast live and can be accessed through the MasTec Investor Relations website at[https://investors.mastec.com/events-presentations/events](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.mastec.com%2Fevents-presentations%2Fevents&esheet=54514157&newsitemid=20260417472501&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.mastec.com%2Fevents-presentations%2Fevents&index=1&md5=b1b80877420f40e8c2dcf4e441551488) . A replay link, along with the earnings release and supporting materials, will also be posted to the website.


**About MasTec** : MasTec, Inc. is a leading North American infrastructure engineering and construction company operating across a range of end markets. MasTec’s primary activities include the engineering, building, installation, maintenance and upgrade of communications, energy and utility and other infrastructure, such as: power delivery services, including transmission and distribution, wireless, wireline/fiber and customer fulfillment activities; power generation, primarily from clean energy and renewable sources; pipeline infrastructure, including natural gas pipeline and distribution infrastructure; heavy civil; and industrial infrastructure. MasTec’s customers are primarily in these industries. MasTec’s corporate website can be accessed at[www.mastec.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.mastec.com&esheet=54514157&newsitemid=20260417472501&lan=en-US&anchor=www.mastec.com&index=2&md5=b6017fd5c9553d7e3852fc3545af81a5) .
