# Hidden revenue killer no one talks about at Money TLV

It sits in the backend. Hidden on the payment side. "The payment provider will fix it, right?" 🤑


Here's what most don't realize:


##### Failed payments happen to your GOOD customers


##### Often, the cards are still valid (banks just decline anyway)


##### In most cases, customers don't even know their subscription failed


#####


For many AI and SaaS companies, it's still a mystery — but the impact on[churn](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23churn&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED) is very real.


## Involuntary churn


Involuntary churn= losing subscribers due to payment failures, not active cancellations. Your customers aren't choosing to leave; they don't even know there's a problem.


There's no single root cause. Dozens of reasons exist: insufficient funds, fraud flags, expired cards, AVS issues, SCA requirements, invalid card data... Each failure type needs its own targeted solution.


When a failed payment becomes unrecoverable, you lose more than immediate revenue: you lose significant LTV potential.


The math is compelling: If your average customer stays 6-12 months, increasing recovery rates from 45% to 55% can boost ARR by 6%-10%.
