# Adecoagro Acquires Best in Class Urea Producer

##### Adecoagro Acquires Best in Class Urea Producer.


We are pleased to announce the acquisition of Profertil, the largest and lowest-cost producer of granular urea in South America. By becoming the controlling shareholder with 90% ownership, this transaction represents a transformative milestone for Adecoagro.


We believe it is a unique opportunity to position the company as a key player in the region, strengthening Argentina’s competitive advantages and production capabilities while further diversifying our portfolio.
