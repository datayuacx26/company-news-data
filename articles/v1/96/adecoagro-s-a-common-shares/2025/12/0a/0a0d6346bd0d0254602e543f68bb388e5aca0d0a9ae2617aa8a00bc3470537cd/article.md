# Adecoagro Announces Submission of Binding Offer to Acquire the Remaining 50% of Profertil S.A.

Adecoagro, a leading sustainable production company in South America, announces the submission of a binding offer to acquire YPF’s 50% stake in Profertil S.A., the largest producer of granular urea in South America.
