## Understanding how Agentforce works


Agentforce works by bringing together several core components to ensure efficient task execution. Here’s how these pieces fit together to deliver results.


- **Agents:** The[AI agent](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/ai-agent-for-small-business/) that interacts with users, interpreting their natural language queries and providing responses.
- **Topics:** Predefined areas of focus or expertise that guide the agent on what it can handle (like sales inquiries, customer support, or task automation).
- **Actions:** The specific tasks agents can carry out, such as answering a customer query, generating a lead, or updating a sales record.
- **Reasoning engine:** This is the logic that processes user inputs, analyzes data, and decides the best course of action based on the situation.
- **Large language model (LLM):**[LLM](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/what-are-large-language-models/) is the AI model that powers the agent’s ability to understand and respond in natural language, making interactions intuitive and human-like.


The agentic layer is seamlessly integrated into the Salesforce Platform. It uses data, AI, and the full Salesforce suite of[CRM apps](https://www.salesforce.com/crm) to create a unified experience. This deep integration empowers agents to take proactive actions across every role and channel, offering a complete context of each customer interaction. Here’s a simple breakdown of how it works.


### Access to your data


Agentforce connects to your existing business data (like customer details,[sales records](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/data/) , and product information) and uses AI to analyze this data as it updates in your systems.[Data 360](https://www.salesforce.com/data) gives Agentforce unified access to customer data from all sources, including emails, transcripts, and PDFs. With real-time insights, agents can quickly adapt to customer needs and take actions like automating follow-ups or finding relevant knowledge articles. For example, if a customer’s order status changes, the agent can immediately pull that updated info and provide an accurate response.


### AI-powered reasoning


The[Atlas Reasoning Engine](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/what-is-a-reasoning-engine/atlas/) is what makes Agentforce so smart. It breaks down tasks into smaller steps and continually adapts its actions based on the data and goals. For instance, if a customer asks a question, the agent not only looks for an answer but evaluates multiple options and refines its response until it finds the best one. This process ensures that tasks are completed accurately, even if the situation changes.


### Executing actions


Once equipped with the necessary data and reasoning, the[autonomous agent](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/autonomous-agents/) takes action. This might involve answering a customer query, automating a workflow, or creating a new sales lead. Every step is designed to save time and improve efficiency.
