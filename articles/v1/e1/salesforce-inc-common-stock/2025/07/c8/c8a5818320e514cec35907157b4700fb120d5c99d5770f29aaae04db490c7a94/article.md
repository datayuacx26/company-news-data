# Become an Agentic Enterprise: A Refreshed Step-By-Step Guide

### How we did it


Recognizing the challenges of measuring agent impact at scale, Salesforce built a transparent, structured system for tracking performance and outcomes. At first, managing just a few agents seemed manageable, but as the number of agents — and agents managing agents — grew, it became clear that manual tracking would not suffice. This led to the creation of a centralized platform for monitoring agent activity, quality and business value in real time.


The system incorporates our **Agent Scoring Rubric** , which provides a comprehensive, data-driven view of each agent’s performance. The rubric tracks overall health, efficacy, adoption, satisfaction, and impact on business outcomes, ranking agents against peers and offering suggested goals for continuous improvement. Leadership uses this insight to make informed decisions about which agents to expand, optimize, or retire, and to ensure that every agent is aligned with strategic objectives. Dashboards and scorecards also enable teams to visualize trends, identify gaps, and spot opportunities for improvement quickly.


One example of the power of measurement comes from our Sales Agent. Early on, we observed high rates of negative or missing information responses, which limited its usefulness. By systematically tracking performance metrics, analyzing business impact, and iterating based on these insights, we were able to reduce error rates from 30% to 17%, significantly improving both adoption and the agent’s ability to generate meaningful results.


The key lesson from our experience is that measurement is not optional — it’s foundational. Transparent metrics create trust, guide continuous refinement, and demonstrate the real-world value of agents. By building robust tools, organizations can scale this practice, ensuring that their agents are delivering both operational efficiency and measurable business impact.
