# Agentforce is Now Built Into Salesforce Suites

**What is Agentforce for Small Business?**
Agentforce for small business is the AI platform from Salesforce that powers the new built-in artificial intelligence features within the Salesforce Suites (Free, Starter, and Pro) designed specifically for small businesses and startups. It connects your customer data across sales, service, and marketing to provide instant summaries, draft personalized communications, and automate tasks.


**Which Salesforce Suites include these new AI features?**
The built-in AI capabilities, including instant record summaries and ‘Draft with AI’ for email copy, are now available in the Free, Starter, and Pro Suites. The Employee Agent is included in the Starter and Pro Suites.


**Is there any extra cost or setup required to use the built-in AI?**
No. These AI features are built directly into the Salesforce Suites and require no additional setup, configuration, or extra cost. They are designed to be ready to use right away.


**How does Agentforce ensure my customer’s data is secure?**
Agentforce 360 is built on the[Salesforce platform](https://www.salesforce.com/) , which includes built-in security, trust, and data governance. Your data stays protected within your Salesforce environment; it is not shared with third-party AI models, ensuring your customer information remains secure and private.


**How does the Employee Agent help my small business team?**
The Employee Agent acts as an AI assistant that can quickly handle tasks like finding and summarizing account details for meeting prep, logging activities (like call notes) into records, and drafting contextual email copy, allowing your team to focus on customer relationships and selling instead of administrative work.
