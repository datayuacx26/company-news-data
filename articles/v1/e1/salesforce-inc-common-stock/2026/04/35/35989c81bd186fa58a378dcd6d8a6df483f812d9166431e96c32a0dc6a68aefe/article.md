# Salesforce Agentforce Life Sciences to Unify Moderna’s Global Commercial Operations

**Tuesday, April 28, 2026 –** Salesforce today announced that Moderna has selected[Salesforce’s Agentforce Life Sciences for Customer Engagement](https://www.salesforce.com/life-sciences/cloud/customer-engagement/?d=cta-body-promo-8) to unify the biotechnology company’s global commercial operations. The collaboration supports Moderna’s vision to enable a single, connected platform to engage customers, streamline field and customer service operations, and deliver the best customer experience based on a 360-degree understanding of their customers.


The connected platform will integrate data, AI, and customer engagement across commercial users spanning multiple regions, allowing for:


- **Unified Customer 360 with Trusted Data:** Moderna will consolidate customer data from SAP, ecommerce, and multiple regional systems to create a single source of truth for HCP interactions. Fueled by[IQVIA OneKey](https://appexchange.salesforce.com/appxConsultingListingDetail?listingId=26eb9e4e-0b89-416f-94af-4e2c164820d7) , the company will have access to trusted, real-time reference data and private, secure master data management.
- **AI-Powered Commercial Excellence:** Moderna’s field teams will use[Agentforce](https://www.salesforce.com/healthcare-life-sciences/life-sciences-artificial-intelligence/?d=cta-body-promo-8) to deliver next best actions, automate cycle planning, and streamline call reporting, empowering commercial users globally to engage HCPs more effectively with real-time insights and intelligent recommendations.
- **Global Omni-Channel Marketing at Scale:** As Moderna expands its marketing efforts globally, this enables unified cross-channel marketing execution, supporting both commercial engagement and customer service with personalized, data-driven campaigns.
- **Platform Built for Unification:** Salesforce’s[open ecosystem](https://www.salesforce.com/compare/salesforce-vs-veeva/) and integration-first architecture connects commercial, medical, patient services, and contact center operations to support Moderna’s unified platform. This helps the company to scale rapidly without vendor lock-in while building on its existing investment in[Agentforce Life Sciences](https://www.salesforce.com/healthcare-life-sciences/life-sciences-software/?d=cta-body-promo-8) .


“Moderna is a company that has always embraced innovation, and this collaboration reflects their commitment to building a modern, AI-powered commercial organization,” said **Joe Ferraro, General Manager of Agentforce Life Sciences** . “With Agentforce Life Sciences, Moderna gains a unified platform that brings together data, AI, and automation — empowering their teams to deliver more meaningful customer engagement on a global scale.”
