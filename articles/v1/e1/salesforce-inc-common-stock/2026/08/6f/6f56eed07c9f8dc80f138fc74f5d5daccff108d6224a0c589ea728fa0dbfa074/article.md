# Salesforce Named a Leader in IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Software Platforms for National Civilian Government AI-Enabled Case Management

Salesforce today announced it has been named a Leader in the 2026 IDC MarketScape for Worldwide Software Platforms for National Civilian Government AI-Enabled Case Management[2026 Vendor Assessment](https://www.salesforce.com/government/resources/idc-salesforce-public-sector-case-management-vendor-summary/) .*


As AI reshapes how governments manage and deliver citizen services, agencies need technology that can support their missions from the start. Salesforce brings together purpose-built applications, trusted AI, and a unified platform across critical civilian use cases, helping agencies deploy faster, support security and compliance requirements, and serve constituents with greater efficiency and responsiveness.


Salesforce was recognized for the following strengths:


- **Mission-specific case management templates:** Salesforce has a broad portfolio of out-of-the-box civilian case archetypes for benefit, grants, licensing and permitting, social benefits, courts, and emergency response. These blueprints can reduce design risk for cross-program civilian workloads such as integrated eligibility, regulatory enforcement, and grants-to-outcomes traceability.
- **Agentic AI investments:** Agentforce ships with prebuilt public sector agent templates wired into the Public Sector Toolkit (guided flows, dynamic assessments, decision tables, intelligent document reader). Agents for complaint-to-case conversion, regulatory compliance, candidate sourcing, and benefit eligibility are usable out of the box rather than requiring greenfield build, and the Agent Fabric supports model choice and MCP/A2A interoperability support agencies pursuing multivendor AI strategies and seeking to avoid lock-in to a single foundation model.
- **Global compliance and sovereignty footprint:** Salesforce’s combination of FedRAMP High, DoD IL5, and 13 country-specific authorizations span the EU, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea, UAE, and Latin America, supported by Hyperforce EU Operating Zone for in-region data residency.

#### **About IDC MarketScape:**


IDC MarketScape vendor assessment model is designed to provide an overview of the competitive fitness of technology and service suppliers in a given market. The research utilizes a rigorous scoring methodology based on both qualitative and quantitative criteria that results in a single graphical illustration of each supplier’s position within a given market. IDC MarketScape provides a clear framework in which the product and service offerings, capabilities and strategies, and current and future market success factors of technology suppliers can be meaningfully compared. The framework also provides technology buyers with a 360-degree assessment of the strengths and weaknesses of current and prospective suppliers.
