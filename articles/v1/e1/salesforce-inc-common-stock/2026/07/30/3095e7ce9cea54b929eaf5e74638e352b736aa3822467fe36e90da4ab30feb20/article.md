# Why Data Privacy and Archiving Are the New Marketing Growth Engines

## Share article


-
-
-
-


## Key Takeaways


- Moving beyond basic legal requirements to embrace data privacy builds customer trust and protects brand health.
- Systematically clearing out stale records ensures that your active database remains fast and accurate.
- Tools like Salesforce Privacy Center and Archive turns compliance into a scalable competitive advantage rather than a manual burden.


Modern marketing is built on a simple promise: deliver the right message to the right customer at the right moment. To fuel that personalization, brands have spent years accumulating as much customer data as possible. Unfortunately for marketers, these massive, unwieldy data volumes are starting to present a challenge.[45% of marketers say their data is incomplete, inaccurate, or out of date](https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/report-finds-nearly-half-of-marketing-data-is-inaccurate/50390/) , while[65% of organizations](https://captaincompliance.com/education/cisco-2026-data-and-privacy-benchmark-study-the-stats-signals-and-what-they-mean-for-governance-in-the-age-of-ai/#:~:text=Data%20discipline%20is%20strained%3A%2065,customer%20data%20for%20AI%20training.) struggle to access high-quality consent data and maintain compliance.


Regulations are at the heart of the issue. Globally,[6.6 billion people are now covered by data privacy laws](https://iapp.org/news/a/notes-on-the-updated-global-privacy-law-and-dpa-directory-and-major-privacy-developments) , with regulations around AI evolving regularly. Many companies fail compliance audits simply because their systems can’t sync opt-out requests at scale. Standard consent tools rarely have the immutable history tracking needed to prove compliance during audits.


But compliance is only one aspect to consider.[Data privacy](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/data-privacy-compliance/what-is-data-privacy/) and data management are also core to customer trust, campaign precision, and brand health.


Outdated records dilute targeting accuracy and create awkward moments. Imagine a customer getting a promotional email addressed to a name they haven’t used since a divorce. Or a buyer getting summer recommendations based on 2018 purchase history, when they bought a shirt just last week. Campaigns built on stale data don’t just underperform, they actively push customers away.


## The “Golden Trio”: Privacy, data, and marketing


To thrive today, organizations need Privacy, Data, and Marketing — what Salesforce calls the “Golden Trio — working in lockstep. When they work together, privacy signals feed directly into campaign execution, and customers get the experience they actually consented to.


Proactive[data archiving](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/data-archiving-solutions/guide/) plays a vital role here. Archiving isn’t about throwing away valuable records, but[clearing the clutter](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/data-archiving-strategy/) so your active database stays fast, fresh, and useful. If you keep your active database clean, you can glean more relevant insights.


And for marketers using agentic AI like[Agentforce](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/) , this matters even more: clean, consented data is what allows AI agents to personalize at scale without introducing compliance risk.


## Elevate trust with Privacy Center and Archive


With[Privacy Center](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/privacy-center/) and[Archive](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/data-archiving-solutions/) , you can bring advanced data privacy and management capabilities into your Salesforce org, so you don’t have to bolt on tools or manage it from outside.


### 1. Automate privacy management with Privacy Center and Global Consent Manager


With Privacy Center, you can anonymize personal data to reduce risk, delete unnecessary records, and automate customer privacy requests like Right to be Forgotten (RTBF) or Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR).


To help customers even further, the new Global Consent Manager (GCM) gives your team a centralized system of record for consent, with a scalable, event-driven architecture built for enterprise scale. Key benefits of GCM include:


- Centralized consent powering visibility and enforcement across CRM and[Data 360](https://www.salesforce.com/data/) .
- Automated audit trail to track consent changes, ensuring compliance with global privacy laws.
- Share consent signals instantly across your entire enterprise architecture via seamless API and events.


### 2. Boost precision and compliance with Archive


Archive systematically removes stale records to boost campaign precision and compliance. Key benefits include:


- Automatically clear data clutter so every campaign segment is built on accurate, up-to-date records.
- Access legacy profiles in the background so your live journeys keep running without a hiccup.
- Keep your org storage clean and your historical data organized, so regulatory inspections don’t catch you off guard.


## Embrace the future of trusted marketing


Trust is the ultimate currency. Marketers who respect customer preferences and keep their data clean build stronger, more loyal relationships and a brand worth coming back to. Privacy Center and Archive can help you get there.
