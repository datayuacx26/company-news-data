## What is a business operating system (OS)?


At its core, a business operating system (OS) is a connected set of tools that manages the essential functions of how your business runs day-to-day. It’s where your team sends marketing emails, tracks leads, closes deals, and resolves customer issues — without having to jump between a dozen disconnected apps.


For founders, the most popular form of a business operating system is[customer relationship management (CRM)](https://www.salesforce.com/crm/what-is-crm/) — yes, the platform to does it all. A modern CRM is a living system that connects your people, your data, and your processes in one place. It quite literally, houses your business — your home base.
