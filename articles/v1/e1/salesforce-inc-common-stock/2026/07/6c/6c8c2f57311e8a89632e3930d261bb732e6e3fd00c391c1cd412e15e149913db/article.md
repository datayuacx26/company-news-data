## How to get started with Foundations


Activating Foundations takes just a few steps inside your existing[Salesforce Setup](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=platform.setup_home_page.htm&language=en_US&type=5) . You choose which features to enable, and you can move at your own pace — turning on marketing tools first, then exploring commerce, then AI agents as your team gets comfortable. The goal isn’t to overhaul everything at once. It’s to start getting more value from the CRM you already use, on the timeline that makes sense for your business.


To activate Salesforce Foundations, follow these steps within your Salesforce account:


1. Log in to your Salesforce account.
2. Navigate to “Setup” and search for or select “Salesforce Foundations”.
3. Click “Add to my Contract”.
4. Add the Salesforce Foundations products to your cart.
5. Complete the checkout process.
6. Return to the “Setup” menu to begin enabling and configuring your new features.

**Is Salesforce Foundations really free?**
Yes — Foundations is a free add-on for existing Enterprise Edition customers or higher. There are no additional licensing fees to activate it, though some usage-based features (like Pay Now transaction processing and Agentforce overages) may incur fees if you exceed the included limits.


**Do I need to be a Salesforce developer to activate Foundations?**
No. You activate Foundations directly in Salesforce Setup, and many features are designed to get your team up and running quickly — in some cases in just a few minutes. The Trailhead module[Grow Your Business with Salesforce Foundations](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/salesforce-foundations-quick-look/get-to-know-salesforce-foundations) walks you through the setup step by step.


**What’s the difference between Salesforce Foundations and Salesforce Starter Suite?**
Starter Suite is a standalone product for businesses just getting started with a CRM. Foundations is a free upgrade layered on top of an existing Agentforce Sales or Service Enterprise subscription — it’s designed for businesses that already use Salesforce and want to expand into marketing, commerce, AI, and unified data without purchasing separate products.


**Can I use Foundations to send marketing emails?**
Yes. Foundations includes a drag-and-drop email builder and up to 2,000 email sends per month per org, along with built-in analytics to measure how your campaigns perform. It’s a great entry point for small teams that want to run basic email marketing from within their CRM.


**What happens when I’ve used up my Agentforce Flex credits?**
Foundations includes 200,000 Agentforce Flex credits to get you started. Once you’ve used those,[additional Flex credits are available for purchase](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/small-business/smb-success-stories-with-salesforce/) . The included credits are intended to give you enough runway to test meaningful use cases and see the value before committing to more.
