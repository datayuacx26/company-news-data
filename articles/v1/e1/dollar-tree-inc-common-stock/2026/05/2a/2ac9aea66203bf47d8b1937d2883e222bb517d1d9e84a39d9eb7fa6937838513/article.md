# Dollar Tree, Inc. to Host First Quarter Earnings Conference Call

## Dollar Tree, Inc. to Host First Quarter Earnings Conference Call


May 07, 2026 4:15pm EDT[Download as PDF](https://corporate.dollartree.com/_assets/_89a553c8e35d4fe0832b90f86cfe1d95/dollartreeinfo/news/2026-05-07_Dollar_Tree_Inc_to_Host_First_Quarter_Earnings_304.pdf)

(NASDAQ: DLTR), will report financial results for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 ended on May 2, 2026, before the stock market opens on Thursday, May 28, 2026, followed by a conference call for investors and analysts at 8 a.m. ET. Chief Executive Officer Mike Creedon and Chief Financial Officer Stewart Glendinning will discuss the company’s results and lead a question-and-answer session.
