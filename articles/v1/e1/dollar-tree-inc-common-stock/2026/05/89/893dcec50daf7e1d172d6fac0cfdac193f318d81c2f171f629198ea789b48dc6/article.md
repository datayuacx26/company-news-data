# Dollar Tree Celebrates Opening of New Distribution Center with Ribbon Cutting

## Dollar Tree Celebrates Opening of New Distribution Center with Ribbon Cutting


May 14, 2026 7:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://corporate.dollartree.com/_assets/_89a553c8e35d4fe0832b90f86cfe1d95/dollartreeinfo/news/2026-05-14_Dollar_Tree_Celebrates_Opening_of_New_305.pdf)


- *Litchfield Park, AZ, distribution center expected to service nearly 700 stores across the West and Southwest*
- *Dollar Tree is actively recruiting for distribution center roles*


CHESAPEAKE, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Dollar Tree, Inc. (NASDAQ: DLTR) celebrated the opening of its 1-million-square-foot distribution center in Litchfield Park, AZ, with a ribbon-cutting ceremony yesterday.


The company announced it purchased the turnkey facility[last October](https://corporate.dollartree.com/news-media/press-releases/detail/297/dollar-tree-announces-new-distribution-center-in-litchfield) . Outbound deliveries begin next month, shipping to approximately 700 stores across Arizona, Colorado, Nevada, New Mexico, and Utah.


“The ribbon cutting is a celebratory moment for our teams and strengthens our supply chain in the Southwest and West. This facility will help us move product closer to our stores and serve customers more quickly,” said Roxanne Weng, Chief Supply Chain Officer, Dollar Tree. “We are proud to be part of the local community and bring nearly 400 jobs to the area.”


The new facility is part of Dollar Tree’s broader effort to enhance its distribution network, increase capacity, improve speed to stores, and support continued growth across its store footprint.


Dollar Tree plans to open its[next distribution center](https://corporate.dollartree.com/news-media/press-releases/detail/296/dollar-tree-to-rebuild-marietta-distribution-center) in Marietta, Oklahoma, in spring 2027, with a return to the community after the prior facility was destroyed by a tornado in April 2024.


To learn more about employment opportunities with Dollar Tree, please visit:[https://careers.dollartree.com](https://careers.dollartree.com/) .
