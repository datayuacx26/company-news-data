# Tradeweb Announces Partnership with Professional Golfer James Nicholas

#### Multi-year partnership reflects a shared belief that long-term success is built through discipline, preparation, and transparency


#### Nicholas makes British Open debut this week wearing Tradeweb logo


NEW YORK — July 15, 2026 — Tradeweb Markets Inc. (Nasdaq: TW), a global leader in electronic trading across asset classes, today announced a multi-year partnership with professional golfer James Nicholas.


This week, Nicholas makes his debut competing at The Open Championship – also widely known as the British Open – one of the four major men’s golf championships. With a growing list of career milestones, Nicholas has established himself as one of professional golf's emerging talents. A two-time Ivy League Player of the Year at Yale University, he claimed his first Korn Ferry Tour victory earlier this year and competed in his second consecutive U.S. Open in June 2026.


Nicholas is recognized for his disciplined and analytical approach to competition, as well as for bringing fans along on his journey through authentic, behind-the-scenes storytelling. His commitment to preparation, continuous improvement, and transparency resonates closely with Tradeweb’s approach to transforming global financial markets through technology, innovation, and data-driven analytics.


Billy Hult, CEO at Tradeweb, said: "What drew us to James wasn't just his talent – it was his ambitious and disciplined commitment to improving every single day. Success in golf, much like success in financial markets, is built on preparation and consistent execution. James has also given fans an authentic look at the work behind his success, and we value that commitment to transparency. At Tradeweb, we share that same drive to continually push boundaries, innovate, and pursue excellence for our clients, making James a natural partner for us.”


Nicholas said: "What resonates with me about Tradeweb is its commitment to always looking ahead. Whether you’re preparing for a major championship or building technology that helps power global markets, success comes from continuously learning, adapting, and finding ways to improve. That’s a mindset I try to bring to my game every day, and I’m proud to partner with a company that shares that same level of dedication to excellence and innovation.”


**About Tradeweb Markets**


Tradeweb Markets Inc. (Nasdaq: TW) is a leading global operator of electronic marketplaces for rates, credit, equities and money markets. Founded in 1996, Tradeweb provides access to markets, data and analytics, electronic trading, straight-through-processing and reporting for more than 50 products to clients in the institutional, wholesale, retail and corporates markets. Advanced technologies developed by Tradeweb enhance price discovery, order execution and trade workflows while allowing for greater scale and helping to reduce risks in client trading operations. Tradeweb serves more than 3,000 clients in more than 85 countries. On average, Tradeweb facilitated more than $2.8 trillion in notional value traded per day over the past four fiscal quarters. For more information, please go to[www.tradeweb.com](https://www.tradeweb.com/) .
