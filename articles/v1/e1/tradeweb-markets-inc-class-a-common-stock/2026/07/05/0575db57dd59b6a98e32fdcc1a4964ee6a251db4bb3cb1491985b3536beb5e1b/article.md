# Tradeweb Exchange-Traded Funds Update – June 2026

The following data is derived from trading activity on the[Tradeweb Markets institutional European- and U.S.-listed ETF platforms](https://www.tradeweb.com/our-markets/institutional/equities/etps_funds/) .


#### EUROPEAN-LISTED ETFs


**Total traded volume**


Trading activity on the Tradeweb European-listed ETF marketplace reached EUR 84.3 billion in June and nearly EUR 240 billion in the second quarter of the year, the platform’s second highest quarterly volume on record. The proportion of transactions and notional volume completed via[Tradeweb’s Automated Intelligent Execution (AiEX) tool](https://www.tradeweb.com/our-markets/institutional/equities/etps_funds/) amounted to 95% and 34%, respectively.


Adam Gould, Global Head of Equities at Tradeweb, said: "June continued the strong momentum we’ve seen across our ETF business throughout the year. Growth was fuelled by both new clients onboarding to the platform and unrelenting adoption of our rules-based automated RFQ protocol.”


**Volume breakdown**


Equity ETFs saw net buying in June, representing 73% of the overall platform flow, five percentage points higher than the previous 12-month rolling average. Similarly, ‘buys’ in fixed income ETFs surpassed ‘sells’ by 16 percentage points; in contrast, commodity ETFs were mostly sold during the month.


North America and Global Equities remained the most actively traded ETF categories, with a combined EUR 37.2 in total traded volume.


**Top ten by traded notional volume**


There were six equity products among the ten most heavily traded European-listed ETFs in June, led by the iShares Core EURO STOXX 50 UCITS ETF. Commodity ETFs with agriculture and gold exposures also featured prominently, while the iShares $ Treasury Bond 0–1yr UCITS ETF was the sole fixed income fund in the list.


#### U.S.-LISTED ETFs


**Total traded volume**


Total consolidated U.S. ETF notional value traded in June reached USD 104.2 billion, bringing the Q2 2026 total to just shy of USD 300 billion, the platform’s second-best quarterly performance since launch. During the month, AiEX transactions represented 60% of all U.S. ETF tickets and accounted for 16% of U.S. ETF notional volume on the platform.


Adam Gould, Global Head of Equities at Tradeweb, said: " Globally, clients executed more than 454,000 fully automated ETF trades in June, up 107% year-over-year, while automated notional volume surpassed USD 50 billion, an 87% year-over-year increase. As institutions continue to incorporate automation into their trading workflows, we expect this trend to further accelerate and scale.”


**Volume breakdown**


As a percentage of total notional value, equities accounted for 62% and fixed income for 30%, with the remainder comprising commodity and specialty ETFs. ****


****


**Top ten by traded notional volume**


Broad U.S. equity products occupied half of June’s rankings, with the Vanguard 500 Index Fund ETF holding on to the top spot for the eighth consecutive month. The remaining five fixed income ETFs reflected continued demand for corporate bond, aggregate bond and short-duration Treasury exposures.
