# Expanding Our Operational Capabilities in Abu Dhabi

### **Bigger facility. Broader capabilities. Ready to deliver.**


Nabors UAE has successfully opened its newly expanded Abu Dhabi facility, marking a significant step forward in strengthening operational capabilities across the region. More than a physical expansion, the facility signals a strategic shift toward integrated service delivery, operational efficiency, and deeper customer partnerships.


Mohamad Arafa, Nabors Abu Dhabi Area Manager, noted that the transition to the new facility was completed safely and efficiently, with zero lost-time incidents, reflecting Nabors’ strong safety culture and leadership. Along with the local management team, the opening ceremony was also attended by Subodh Saxena, Senior Vice President of Canrig and Nabors Drilling Solutions, highlighting the strategic significance of this expansion.


Spanning **42,800 square meters** , the new facility houses advanced[machine shops](https://www.nabors.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/BRCH_Machine-Shops-Capabilities_Eastern-Hemisphere.pdf) ,[fishing services and tools](https://www.nabors.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/BRCH_Fishing-and-Remedial-Tools.pdf) , **rentals** , **tubular running services** (CDS and conventional), and[pressure control equipment .](https://www.nabors.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/BRCH_Pressure-Control-Equipment_2025.pdf) The site also supports a wide range of specialized services, including **drill pipe rental** , **automated catwalk** and **offline bucking units** , **drilling tool rentals** (stabilizers, jars, and more), **high-torque wrenching** , **subassembly** , **cold cutting** , and **nitrogen testing services.**


### **Integrated Delivery, Tangible Customer Value**


International Tubular Services (ITS), now operating under the Nabors umbrella, is already serving customers across the Middle East from this expanded base. By consolidating multiple services through a single provider, the facility enables **fewer interfaces, lower overall costs, and greater operational certainty** for drilling operations.


For customers, this integrated model simplifies execution and enhances coordination, reducing downtime and improving overall efficiency. For Nabors, it strengthens how we deliver services, better resource utilization, and long-term, strategic partnerships rather than transactional sourcing.


### **A Regional Platform for Growth**


This expansion reinforces Nabors’ commitment to delivering reliable, integrated solutions that enhance operational performance and create measurable value for customers across the region. It also positions Abu Dhabi as a key operational hub supporting both current demand and future growth.


Nabors extends its appreciation to the Abu Dhabi team for their outstanding teamwork and disciplined execution in bringing the facility online safely and efficiently
