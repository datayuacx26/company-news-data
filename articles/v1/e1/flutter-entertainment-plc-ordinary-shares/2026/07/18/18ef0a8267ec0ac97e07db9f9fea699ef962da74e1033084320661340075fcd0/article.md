# How Flutter's tech leaders are putting AI to work

**6. Growing AI capability**


For **Sara Sousa, General Manager at Blip** , Flutter's sports betting center of excellence in Porto, the starting point isn't the tool, it's the people using it. "AI is here to supercharge our engineering teams," **she says** . "It takes on repetitive tasks, it helps us write and review code faster, and it gives teams more time to focus on the hard problems: design, logic, and product value."


That focus on people over hype shapes how Blip is rolling AI out. The hub's goals are to maximize AI tool usage, track real efficiency gains, and partner with Flutter's brands to help define the future of product engineering, all built on the belief that "the best ideas start from within."


The practical expression of that is "earn your license": the idea that a tool is only as powerful as your ability to use it well. The AI Hub, an internal learning platform with paths from basic to advanced topics, launched with its first modules already live, built by Blip's own trainers. That upskilling showed up at the hub’s first Leap AI Day this year, a flagship event combining a keynote, real project stories and hands-on workshops, which teams put into practice in a competition focused on building working AI-powered solutions.


"This is the momentum we want to keep building," **Sousa says** , "not on slides, but in real work, real products, and real people."
