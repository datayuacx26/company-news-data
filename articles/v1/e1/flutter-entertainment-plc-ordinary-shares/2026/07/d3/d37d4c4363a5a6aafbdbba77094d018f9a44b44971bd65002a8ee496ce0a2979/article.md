# Big names, big matches and big bets: Flutter’s 2026 World Cup wrapped

**Record-setting performances**


Despite ultimate disappointment for Argentina, this was Messi's tournament and, given his experience and talent, it's unsurprising that he set[new records](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/schjelderup-lands-roma-radar-spurs-150000427.html) in 2026. He finishes having played more World Cup matches ([34](https://www.espn.co.uk/football/story/_/id/49085479/lionel-messi-world-cup-history-results-stats) ) than any other player, and broke Ronaldo's[record](https://www.espn.co.uk/football/story/_/id/49085479/lionel-messi-world-cup-history-results-stats) as the oldest player to score a World Cup hat-trick.


But he was far from the only player to set records. Ronaldo became the first player to score in[six consecutive](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/cristiano-ronaldo-portugal-goal-record) World Cups, and Spain's goalkeeper finally saw his record run of World Cup games without conceding a goal ended at 650 minutes by Belgium's Charles[De Ketelaere](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/spain-belgium-match-report-highlights) .[Kane](https://www.englandfootball.com/articles/2026/Jun/17/harry-kane-england-men-world-cup-goals-record-20261706) became England's leading World Cup scorer, overtaking Gary Lineker, and Mbappé became the Frenchman with the most World Cup matches (21), surpassing Hugo[Lloris](https://www.englandfootball.com/articles/2026/Jun/17/harry-kane-england-men-world-cup-goals-record-20261706) .[One random stat](https://fcbayern.com/en/news/2026/07/england-fall-to-argentina-in-world-cup-semi-final-despite-taking-the-lead) : the 2026 final was the first since 1978 not to feature a Bayern Munich player.


**Bigger and better?**


With 48 teams, this World Cup was always going to deliver more goals and more storylines than any before. And growing rumors suggest that the 2030 tournament, to be hosted in Morocco, Portugal and Spain, with early matches in South America, could be expanded yet again to 64 teams. If so, there will be even more chances for the underdogs to win hearts and for the big stars to break records. And, of course, for Flutter's customers to bet on it all.
