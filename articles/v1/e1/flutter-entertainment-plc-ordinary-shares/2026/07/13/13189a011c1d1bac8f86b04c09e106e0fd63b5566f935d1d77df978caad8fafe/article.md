# Customers wanted full-game injury protection. FanDuel launched Bet Protect+

#### **What this tells you about Flutter's product approach**


While Bet Protect+ is a product of FanDuel, Flutter's leading US sportsbook, it further demonstrates how Flutter brands prioritize the customer experience, with similar offerings available in other sports in other key sports betting markets.


Across its global portfolio – which includes Paddy Power and Betfair in the UK and Ireland, Sisal in Italy, and Sportsbet in Australia – Flutter has consistently invested in proprietary product features that create differentiation through personalized offers and tailored generosity, rather than purely promotional spend.


In the U.S., FanDuel has stated it will not profit from Bet Protect+ directly. The 3% premium covers the cost of providing the protection, not margin. By making the feature opt-in and self-funded, FanDuel avoids a trade-off common across the industry: funding promotional offers through less competitive odds or reduced generosity elsewhere on the platform.


Customer research drove the design. More than half of surveyed users chose paid full-game protection over free partial coverage, with the preference strongest among experienced bettors who evaluate wagers in terms of expected value. The product was built around what customers wanted, not what was cheapest to offer.


***The feature is available in most US states where FanDuel operates. Customers in Massachusetts, Connecticut, North Carolina, Tennessee, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey currently retain free first-quarter injury coverage with bonus bet refunds.*
