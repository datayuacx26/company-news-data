# Tech4Good: meet the innovators expanding access to physical activity

##### **Civic Dollars - Northern Ireland**


Bridging the gap between people and outdoor spaces, promoting physical and mental wellbeing while strengthening local communities through a community currency.


---


*“Civic Dollars is a community currency app that allows people to earn rewards by being outside, incentivising them to get outside and do some exercise. Being active and being healthy should be something that’s easy and accessible — not something that’s expensive, exclusive, or off-limits.” Dr ‑Gráinne McAnee, Researcher at Civic Dollars*


**


##### **Bonito Foundation - Sweden**


Building a digital hub that connects blind football coaches and Sport for Development practitioners, increasing the availability and quality of programs.


---


*“We’re building a platform to centralize resources, leading to better trained, more impactful and connected coaches who can enable more visually impaired people to play football.” Jaime Elovsson, Co-Founder and CEO at Bonito Foundation*


**


##### **Xiborg - Japan**


Developing advanced prosthetic technology while expanding ‘Blade for All’ clinics globally to improve access to movement and rehabilitation in underserved regions.


---


*“Our mission is to help people move more, and we can track meaningful improvements — not just in performance, but in day-to-day wellbeing — by turning rehabilitation and training into something people can stick with.” Ken Endo, Founder and Engineer at ‑Xiborg* **


##### **Social Innovation Sports - Portugal**


Designing inclusive sports programs that support migrant and refugee communities to access physical activity and build social connections.


---


*“If we can use technology to lower the barrier to movement — to make activity more motivating and more personalized — we can help more people build healthier habits that last.” Ricardo Carvalho, Founder and President at Social Innovation Sports*
