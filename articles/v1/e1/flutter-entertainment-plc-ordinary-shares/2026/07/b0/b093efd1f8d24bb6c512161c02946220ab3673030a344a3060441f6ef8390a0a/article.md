# Flutter's newest market gets the Flutter Edge playbook

### Pacey platform integration


Betnacional exemplifies the Flutter Edge in action, pairing the Group's technology, expertise and scale with local knowledge and reputation. Flutter became a market leader in the US, Italy and Australia by acquiring trusted local brands and combining them with global capabilities. It is now repeating that playbook in Brazil.


When Flutter acquired Betnacional, its sportsbook ran on third-party trading technology, so Carroll set an ambitious target: move onto Flutter's main risk and trading platform before the World Cup kicked off, in what would become the fastest such integration achieved by any brand within the Group. This new platform brings deeper markets, fewer and shorter in-play suspensions, faster bet placement and a stronger Bet Builder experience. Player markets are a major driver of Bet Builder activity across Flutter's established markets. The Group's UK and Ireland brands were first to offer bets on player fouls, which became the most popular player market in bet builder. "What we see today on Betnacional, in terms of the mix of bet builder and player markets, represents a real opportunity for growth," **according to Carroll** .


Betnacional's World Cup pricing is compiled by Flutter's UK and Ireland soccer trading team, but a tournament being followed by millions globally is being supported by specialist teams across several Flutter hubs, including São Paulo, combining local market understanding with some of the most experienced soccer trading operations in the industry. "We want to invest in local talent in Brazil," **Carroll says** .
