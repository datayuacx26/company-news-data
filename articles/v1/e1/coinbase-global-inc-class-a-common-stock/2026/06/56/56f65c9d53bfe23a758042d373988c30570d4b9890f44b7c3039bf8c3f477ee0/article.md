# System Update: Take Control of Your Money with Coinbase

Since our last System Update, we have been hard at work building upon the product foundations that make the Everything Exchange possible. More assets to trade, more ways to trade them, and a more robust financial suite that enables you to take full control of your money in one place. Today, we are launching even more new features and services that expand on each of these three pillars. Coinbase is building the future of finance, where you can manage your money with just one interface, and one login. We’re saying “no” to financial fragmentation, and the new products we’re introducing today take us several steps closer to that future.


## **Trade more on Coinbase**


We are dramatically expanding what you can trade – and how you trade – on Coinbase, bringing every type of asset and market under one roof.


For years we’ve laid the groundwork, and now we’re bringing the benefits of crypto to stock trading. Next month, we will introduce **tokenized stocks** for our non-U.S. customers.¹ Backed 1:1 by the underlying asset, Coinbase tokenized stocks will represent true equity ownership, including dividend payouts and complete shareholder rights, alongside the programmatic utility of the onchain economy. For the first time, you can trade U.S.-based stocks 24/7, lend your shares to earn yield, use them as collateral for a loan, or even gift them directly to someone else as easily as sending a text message.


In the coming months, we’ll also be rolling out **options trading for crypto and stocks** , directly on Coinbase.² This unlocks a new level of sophistication for trading strategies on Coinbase with a full range of motion in both your traditional and digital assets.


On the equities front, you can now **transfer your existing stock portfolios** from other platforms directly to Coinbase, and trade nearly every major stock, index, and ETF you can think of in the U.S. And now, traders can complement their crypto portfolio with **access to equities on Coinbase Advanced** . This includes benefits like zero commission fees, TradingView charting (e.g. indicators), fractional shares and up to 3.5% rewards on trade-ready USDC balances.³


Beyond traditional public equities, we are introducing **real world asset (RWA) perpetual futures** , granting targeted exposure to thematic equity indices like AI, China, Defense, and the Tech 100. Introduced earlier this month, our **new Pre-IPO perps** also offer early exposure to high-interest companies before they hit public exchanges, starting with SpaceX plus Anthropic and OpenAI coming soon.⁴


Since launching prediction markets access earlier this year, we’ve seen incredible demand.² And we’re now bringing you more contracts with even faster and richer trading experiences. Today, we’re introducing:


-


**Crypto binaries:** Trade time-boxed “up or down” markets on assets like BTC, ETH, SOL and more. This allows you to trade predictions on outcomes across 15-minute, hourly, daily, monthly, and annual intervals.


-


**Combos:** Bundle multiple predictions into a single trade, across any category on the platform. That means combining trades on economic indicators alongside global political events, or tapping into your read on sports game outcomes and player performance all in a single position.


On the onchain frontier, we’re making your path to DEX trading even more direct with more tokens and faster access. Tomorrow, you will be able to trade any of the tens of millions of Base or Solana tokens the instant they become available onchain with our **New Launches** tab in the Coinbase app.⁵


We’re also **giving Coinbase Advanced a major upgrade** . Modular, institutional-grade, and built for cross-asset execution, we redesigned our sophisticated trading platform to meet you where you are. Your strategy, your set up, your stocks and crypto side-by-side on a single interface. Just choose a template or drag and drop widgets to build a custom trading layout that meets your needs. And in the next few months we are bringing together our U.S. spot exchange, international derivatives exchanges, and Deribit into **a unified, regulated global liquidity pool** to give customers the most liquid spot, perpetuals, and options access in the world. And we are giving everyone access – Coinbase is the first approved by the CFTC to offer global regulated crypto derivatives, including options, to Americans.


## **Trade smarter with Coinbase**


With the broadest range of assets available in the market, there's even more opportunity if you know what you're looking for. So we are delivering the tooling required for intelligent trading in a global, cross-asset marketplace.


Today, we’re launching **Coinbase Advisor** – rolling out first to Coinbase One members in the U.S. It’s one of the world’s first AI-powered, SEC-registered in-app investment advisors designed to bring professional-grade investment advice to everyone. From helping you design complex tax-loss harvesting to turning breaking news into multi-asset trade recommendations, it handles the heavy analytical lifting so you can optimize your wealth-building strategies with ease. Access to professional grade investment advice now sits in the Coinbase app, available 24/7 without the traditional cost barriers.⁶


For those looking to dive even deeper, we’re making Coinbase the most powerful platform for agentic trading with **Coinbase for Agents.** Now you can enable any AI agent to execute sophisticated strategies on your portfolio around the clock. Using natural language, you can define your agent's exact tasks and boundaries, whether assigning it to execute a market thesis across spot and derivatives or utilizing the[x402 protocol](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/x402/welcome?country=US&currency=USD&mobile=false&japan_bespoke_content=false&logged_in=false&null=) to pay for premium, paywalled financial research. You can confine your agent to an isolated sub-account and set precise limits around capital allocation, asset permissions, and trade sizes – ensuring you grant it a clearly defined budget and trading parameters.⁷


## **Your primary financial platform**


On Coinbase, your money never sits still. It earns when you spend it. It stays in the market, even when you need cash. And it grows the moment your paycheck lands. We continue to evolve our financial services products to work even harder for you, providing flexible, capital-efficient alternatives to legacy banking constraints.


We are expanding the utility of the Coinbase One Card today with the launch of the first travel platform that gives you 5% Bitcoin back on every booking, sent straight to your account. The new **Travel Portal** eliminates the stress of expiring or devaluing traditional loyalty points. No games, just Bitcoin – with access to all the travel protections and benefits offered through the American Express Network. This is the first of many boosted rewards for cardholders coming this year.⁸


We want the Coinbase One Card to be available to everyone, no matter your credit history. Today, we're introducing **USDC-backed access to the Coinbase One Card** , built for everyone who is overlooked by traditional credit bureaus. This update allows users who aren't approved for a traditional line of credit to secure a Coinbase One Card using USDC as collateral. So you can unlock Bitcoin rewards on everyday purchases while continuing to earn rewards on the underlying USDC deposit, paid out every single week. All while being able to help build your credit with every on-time payment.⁹


With so many ways to earn and use your crypto, staking your assets shouldn’t mean they stay locked up. Starting today, you can **borrow against your staked Solana** on Coinbase, powered by JitoSOL, via our ongoing integration with Morpho on Base. In addition to borrowing against BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA, and LTC, you can now unlock liquidity from more of your yield-generating assets: cbETH (up to $1mn) and now JitoSOL (up to $100k).¹⁰


Making Coinbase the ultimate hub for managing your entire financial life means you need comprehensive security controls over all your outgoing funds. So we’ve begun rolling out **Transfer Protection** – a new defensive framework including time-delayed withdrawals, providing a vital review window to verify transactions before they execute, alongside customizable daily limits that prevent unauthorized, large-scale outflows. You can also implement multi-party approval workflows, creating a collaborative authentication process for outbound money movement. These rigorous guardrails ensure that as you store, save, and grow your wealth on Coinbase, it remains entirely under your command.


## **Coinbase infrastructure for your business**


Coinbase has spent nearly 15 years building crypto infrastructure at scale to power our own products, and today our **all-new Coinbase Developer Platform (CDP)** makes it simple for any company to participate in the onchain economy. One access point unlocks access to our wallet infrastructure, payments capabilities, trading systems, and stablecoin issuance.


Where we're really seeing crypto use cases take off is around stablecoin payments, with nearly a trillion dollars in stablecoin volume last year on Coinbase products alone. That’s where **Coinbase Payments** comes in – it’s a complete stablecoin payments solution within CDP built on USDC, Base, institutional-grade custody, and a powerful API layer. Companies like Checkout.com are already using it to move money faster, cheaper, and more globally than ever before.


For businesses that want to offer crypto to their customers without the complexity of licensing, we're announcing a **fully custodial infrastructure solution** , powered by 80 global regulatory licenses and our stress-tested compliance infrastructure. We’re launching with partners like Klarna and Webull to bring launch timelines down from years to weeks.


CDP is also built for the agentic economy. We’re partnering with **AWS to unlock native agent payments on AWS AgentCore** . And Coinbase Payments APIs are now **agentic-enabled out of the box** . That means companies like OpenRouter – the largest AI model exchange and a Coinbase Payments customer – will be able to accept payments seamlessly from both humans and agents.


The future of finance won’t be split between a bank, a brokerage, and a crypto wallet. It is unified, intelligent, onchain, and on Coinbase. Explore all these new features today.
