# Strada’s AI in ‘Profiles in Risk’ Podcast: Why Insurers Trust Us With Their Calls

## Why phone AI? Why now?


You’ve probably heard of AI chatbots. But phone AI? That’s newer and more powerful than you might think.


Why? Because the insurance industry still runs on phones. Policyholders call in. Carriers call out. The phone is where trust gets built and business gets done.


But humans can only do so much:


-


Calls get missed.


-


Hold times frustrate customers.


-


Teams don’t have time to make proactive calls.


That’s where Strada steps in. Their AI agent can call, talk, follow up via email or SMS, and even schedule appointments, without anyone on your team lifting a finger.


## The power of specialization: Why insurance?


Strada doesn’t try to be everything to everyone. It’s focused solely on the insurance space, where Amir and his team saw a clear opportunity.


Amir’s own father is an insurance agent. He grew up seeing how often his dad was on the phone. That real-world insight led him and his co-founder to zero in on insurance as the ideal place for voice AI.


That insight paid off. Strada now works with top carriers, MGAs, and brokers to help them scale their outreach, reduce wait times, and drive growth.


## So, how accurate is it?


Tony, the host, asked the question on every listener’s mind: *How do you make sure the AI doesn’t say the wrong thing?*


This is a big deal. If the AI misquotes a premium or gives incorrect advice, you’ve got a problem.


Amir’s answer: testing, testing, and more testing.


Here’s how Strada keeps things on track:


-


**Structured instructions.** Every AI assistant follows a custom script or “prompt” with strict boundaries.


-


**Built-in testing tools.** They simulate hundreds of conversations to check for errors before anything goes live.


-


**Real-time monitoring.** Conversations are evaluated continuously to flag anything that needs tweaking.


And because it’s all digital, Strada can test and improve at scale, faster than any human QA team ever could.


> “ *It’s like having one AI test another AI,” Amir said. “We simulate the top 20 questions and make sure the system responds correctly every time.* ”
