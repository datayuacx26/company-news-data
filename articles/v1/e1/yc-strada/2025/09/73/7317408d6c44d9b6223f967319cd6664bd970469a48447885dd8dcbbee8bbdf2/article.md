# Introducing Strada Workflows: Beyond the Phone Call

### Real Use Cases in Insurance


Strada Workflows transforms how insurance teams operate by automating critical post-call processes:


**Abandoned Quote Recovery:** When prospects don't complete their application on the first call, Strada Workflows automatically schedules follow-up outreach, creates agent reminders, and updates lead status in your CRM. Every warm lead gets proper attention without manual tracking.


**Payment Recovery Automation:** When customers promise to pay during collection calls, Workflows automatically checks your billing system three days later. If the balance is still unpaid, it triggers another reminder call, and notifies the account owner. Preventing policy lapses becomes proactive instead of reactive.


**Renewal Risk Management:** When a policyholder mentions they’re shopping around or unhappy during a renewal call, Workflows instantly flags the account as at-risk, creates a save task for the retention team, and triggers a personalized follow-up sequence. Renewals become proactive rather than last-minute scrambles.


**Claims Intake:** When customers report new claims, Strada automatically creates a file in your claims system, assigns adjusters, sends required documents to customers, and alerts all relevant teams, reducing a 2-hour manual process to minutes.


**Certificate Issuance:** When a COI is requested, Workflows pushes the request to your AMS to validate policy terms and generate the correct form. Once issued, it’s sent to the certificate holder by email or SMS, while Workflows records the activity and syncs updates across your AMS and CRM for full visibility.
