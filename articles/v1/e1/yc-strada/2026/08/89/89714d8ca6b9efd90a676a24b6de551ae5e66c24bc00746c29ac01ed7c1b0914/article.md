# Announcing Multi-Channel FNOL: Intake Anywhere

## The four channels


**Voice**


Voice remains the dominant channel for FNOL. When a policyholder calls to report a loss, Strada's AI agent answers, identifies the caller’s policy, and guides them through the intake process conversationally. The agent follows the carrier's existing FNOL process, asks only the questions that apply based on previous answers, and adapts the conversation naturally rather than following a rigid script. There is no IVR maze, no hold queue, no form to fill out after the call.


For carriers, this means every inbound FNOL call is handled consistently, at any hour, without routing to a live representative unless the situation genuinely calls for it.

**Chat**


Some policyholders prefer not to call. Chat gives them a way to report a loss on their own terms through a web widget, a mobile app, or a messaging interface the carrier is already running. Because the same AI workflow powers both voice and chat, carriers don't maintain separate intake processes for different channels.


*Chat support for FNOL*


**Email**


Email can be used to start a claim, but it is more often where supporting documentation arrives: photos of damage, police reports, third-party information. Strada automatically associates incoming emails with the correct claim, extracts structured information from attachments, and appends both documents and metadata to the claim record. For carriers running high-volume operations, this eliminates a significant amount of manual document handling.


*Email interaction handled by Strada agent*


**SMS**


SMS has two applications. It can support full claim intake for policyholders who prefer to file entirely over text, or serve as a follow-up layer after a claim is initiated by phone, chat, or email, like sending confirmations, next steps, or requests for additional information. Policyholders can respond via SMS to provide missing details or answer follow-up questions. It closes the loop for policyholders who want a record of what was filed without having to log into a portal.
