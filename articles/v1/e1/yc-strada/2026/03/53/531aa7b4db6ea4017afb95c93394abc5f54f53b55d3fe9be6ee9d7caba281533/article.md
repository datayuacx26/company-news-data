# Strada Named a 2026 Insurance Luminaries Finalist

Strada has been[selected as a finalist](https://www.event.propertycasualty360.com/insurance-luminaries/2026-finalists) in the 2026 Insurance Luminaries Awards, presented by PropertyCasualty360. This recognition places the company among a small group of organizations reshaping the property and casualty insurance landscape through measurable impact, not just technical novelty.


## What this recognition actually signals


The[Insurance Luminaries Awards](https://www.event.propertycasualty360.com/insurance-luminaries/10064873) highlight companies shaping the future of the industry. Being named a finalist reflects:


-


Recognition for innovation and leadership


-


Alignment with key industry trends


-


Demonstrated impact across insurance operations and customer experience
