# Propane Tank Refill vs. Exchange: Which Option Is Right for You?

If you use propane for


[outdoor living](https://www.suburbanpropane.com/blog/enhance-outdoor-living-propane-guide/) , grilling,


[RV travel](https://www.suburbanpropane.com/blog/propane-rv-travel-efficiency-comfort-reliability/) , and other portable propane-powered applications, you may wonder whether it makes more sense to exchange your tank or have it refilled. Understanding the differences between propane tank refill and tank exchange can help you choose the option that best fits your needs.


## **Propane Tank Refill vs. Exchange: What’s the Difference?**


The primary difference between propane tank refilling and propane tank exchange is what happens to the cylinder. With a propane tank refill, propane is added to your existing cylinder, allowing you to keep and continue using the same cylinder. With a tank exchange, you trade in your empty tank for a pre-filled replacement tank that has already been prepared for use.


## **How Propane Tank Refills Work**


Propane tank refilling allows you to bring your existing cylinder to a refill location, where certified Suburban Propane technicians or Authorized Resale Partners safely refill the tank Once properly filled, the same cylinder is returned to you for continued use.


### **Benefits of Refilling Your Propane Tank**


Here are the benefits of refilling your propane tank with Suburban Propane versus exchanging.


- Refilling gets you a full 20-lb cylinder of propane, as much as 25% more than an exchanged cylinder.


- You not only save money but time as well – your cylinder lasts longer so you make fewer trips to refill or exchange it.


- A full cylinder provides more burn time so you can keep grilling.


- Refilling allows you to keep your own personal cylinder.


## **How Propane Tank Exchange Works**


Propane tank exchange is commonly used for 20-lb portable cylinders that power grills,


[patio heating](https://www.suburbanpropane.com/blog/extend-your-patio-season-this-autumn-with-propane/) , fire pits, and other outdoor equipment. It involves bringing your empty or partially used tank and exchanging it for one that is pre-filled.


### **Benefits of Tank Exchange**


- Convenient for occasional or seasonal propane users


- No waiting while a tank is refilled


- Straightforward tank swap process


## **Is It Better to Exchange or Refill a Propane Tank?**


Whether you prefer tank exchange or refilling largely depends on how often you use propane, the types of appliances you power, and the services available in your area.


### **Tank Exchange May Be a Good Option If:**


- You don’t own your own propane cylinder


- Your propane needs are limited or infrequent


- A tank swap better fits your needs


- You prioritize convenience over maximizing propane capacity


**Tank Refilling May Be a Good Option If:**


- You use propane regularly for


[outdoor cooking and entertaining](https://www.suburbanpropane.com/blog/enhance-outdoor-living-propane-guide/)
- You prefer to keep your existing cylinder


- You want the flexibility to refill before the tank is empty


- You are looking to maximize value from your propane purchases


## **When Should You Refill Your Propane Tank?**


Many propane users choose to refill their tank before it is completely empty. Planning ahead can help reduce interruptions during peak


[grilling](https://www.suburbanpropane.com/blog/the-great-grill-debate-propane-vs-charcoal/) season or tailgating, outdoor events, and other times of increased propane use.


Common signs it may be time to refill your propane tank include:


- Weak or uneven flames


- Reduced heat output


- Longer cooking or heating times


- The tank feels noticeably lighter

## **Propane Tank Refill vs. Exchange FAQs**


### **Does Suburban Propane offer propane tank refills and exchanges?**


Yes. Many Suburban Propane locations offer propane cylinder refill and tank exchange services. Because availability varies by location, we recommend contacting your nearest Suburban Propane office to confirm the offerings available in your area.


### **Can I refill a propane tank that still has propane in it?**


In many cases, yes. A propane cylinder can often be refilled before it is completely empty, provided it meets current safety and condition requirements.


### **Is it cheaper to refill a propane tank or exchange it?**


A propane tank refill may provide greater value than exchanging a cylinder because you pay for the propane added to your tank rather than receiving a replacement cylinder. With Suburban Propane, refilling gets you a full 20-lb cylinder of propane, as much as 25% more than an exchanged cylinder.


### **Is it safe to transport a propane tank for a refill?**


Yes. Propane tanks can be safely transported for refilling when proper safety precautions are followed. Secure the tank upright in a well-ventilated area of your vehicle, such as a trunk, cargo area, or truck bed, and take steps to prevent it from shifting during transit. For more information visit


[Propane Education & Research Council](https://propane.com/safety/safety-articles/handling-and-transporting-small-cylinders/) .


### **Is it safe to refill my own propane tank?**


No. Portable propane cylinders should be refilled by certified personnel using proper equipment and procedures. Professional refilling helps support safe handling and compliance with applicable safety standards.
