# Workday to Announce Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Financial Results on August 27, 2026

PLEASANTON, Calif.


, Aug. 3, 2026


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / --[Workday, Inc.](https://www.workday.com/) (NASDAQ: WDAY), the enterprise AI platform for HR, finance, and IT, will announce its fiscal 2027 second quarter financial results after market close on Thursday, August 27, 2026. The company will host a conference call at 1:30 p.m. Pacific Time / 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time to review its financial results and business outlook.


A live webcast of the event will be available on the[Workday Investor Relations site](https://events.q4inc.com/attendee/644225303) . The replay of the webcast will be available for a minimum of 90 days after the conference call.


**About Workday**
[Workday](https://www.workday.com/en-us/homepage.html) operates at the heart of the enterprise – HR, finance, and IT – where the margin for error is effectively zero. By tightly coupling AI with the context, guardrails, and trusted processes that run the business, Workday goes beyond AI that assists work to agents that do the work and drive measurable outcomes. More than 11,500 organizations worldwide, including more than 65% of the Fortune 500, trust Workday to deliver. For more information about Workday, visit[workday.com](http://workday.com/) .
