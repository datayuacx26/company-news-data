*Over the years, Workday has sent delegates to the Grace Hopper Celebration Conference, one of the largest technology leadership gatherings in the world. This documents the was workmates have shown up to share their expertise and connect with other technology leaders.*


*Last update: April 2026*


## 2025: Technology Leadership in the Age of AI


*Our delegation of 71 technologists represented Workday’s technology leadership at the*[Grace Hopper Celebration (GHC) November 2025](https://ghc.anitab.org/) ***.***


GHC is the world’s largest conference for technology leadership organized by[AnitaB.org](https://anitab.org/) . It’s a space where attendees learn from industry experts and tackle the most pressing challenges in computing through technical sessions, inspiring keynotes, and hands-on workshops. The conference is named after **Rear Admiral Grace Hopper** , a pioneer in computer science.


Our Workmates shared their expertise and sharpened their skills across engineering, cybersecurity, design, product, AI, and leadership while building connections that will strengthen our products and push our thinking forward.

Key Insights:


**We’re building community across the tech pillars that matter:** We hosted our second annual AI, Machine Learning, and Cloud Computing Reception, bringing together technologists, partners, and innovators to connect and share ideas.


**Our Workmates are thought leaders:** Software development engineers **Deeheem Ansari** and **Adam Darcy** led a hands-on threat modeling workshop that was filled to capacity, giving attendees practical frameworks to secure their products. Additionally, **Kathy Pham** , Vice President of AI, presented on socially responsible tech and introduced this year’s Technical Leadership Awardees, having been awared the Technical Leadership Award last year.


**We gained critical insights into the tech industry’s trajectory:** The sessions and workshops — spanning AI, product, engineering, design, leadership, accessibility, and more — provided a firsthand look from speakers representing[leading technology companies](https://ghc.anitab.org/speakers) at how organizations across the tech landscape are building and adopting technologies. The networking receptions also gave attendees opportunities for candid conversations about what’s working and what’s next.


**Our commitment extends globally:** Our partnership with AnitaB.org continues as we sponsor the upcoming Grace Hopper Celebration India, supporting the next generation of technology leaders, and building our Workday presence and community, in another key region.

## 2024: Technology


*Fifty delegates and five speakers from Workday participated in technology and innovation sessions at Grace Hopper 2024. As part of its sponsorship, the Workday hosted the*[AI/ML and Cloud Computing Reception](https://tiny.cc/workday-ghc) *on October 9, 2024 at 5:30pm ET at the Marriott Downtown Philadelphia.*


Taking place October 8–11 2024, both virtually and in person in Philadelphia,[GHC 24](https://ghc.anitab.org/) served as one of world’s largest computing and technology leadership conferences. The Celebration provided a diverse and vibrant platform for this community to connect, collaborate, and step into their collective power.


> “Our sponsorship of GHC 24 reflects Workday’s deep commitment to fostering a more inclusive tech industry,” said Kathy Pham, Vice President, AI, Workday and 2024 ABIE Technical Leadership Award recipient. “We stand alongside Grace Hopper Celebration and AnitaB.org in championing diversity, and we’re proud to help empower the voices of women and nonbinary technologists — this week, and beyond.”
