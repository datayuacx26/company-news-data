## Why do people look for a G2 Buyer Intent alternative?


The signal is good. The coverage is narrow. Those two facts drive most of the searching. When a buyer is comparing you against competitors on a review page, they are close to a decision, which makes review-site intent one of the more actionable signals in the category. The problem is that it only fires for buyers who research on that one platform. Anyone evaluating you through analyst content, community threads, a competitor's site, or a quiet internal build never shows up, so you are watching a single doorway into a building with many entrances.


The second issue is that it stops at identification. G2 Buyer Intent tells you an account is in-market. It does not resolve the buying committee, write anything, or reach out. This work lands back in your data tool, your sequencer, and your reps' queues, and the review-site window closes while the handoff happens.


So teams look for alternatives for one of two reasons: they want a wider net of signals so they catch buyers the review site misses, or they want the signal to actually trigger outreach instead of a task. The alternatives below cover both, plus the second review-site source for teams that want more of the same kind of intent.
