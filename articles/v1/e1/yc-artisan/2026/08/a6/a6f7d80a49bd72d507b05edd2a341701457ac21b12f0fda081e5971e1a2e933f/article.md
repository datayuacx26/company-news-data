## How we chose


Four criteria, weighted for a buyer who is already tired of doing outbound by hand:


1. **How much of the job the tool does.** Sending infrastructure is table stakes. The differentiator is whether the tool writes and replies, or just moves your words.


2. **Deliverability at scale.** Mailbox provisioning and management, inbox placement testing, sender reputation monitoring, and automatic pausing of unhealthy mailboxes.


3. **Pricing transparency.** Published tiers beat quote-on-request, and we flag add-ons that inflate the sticker price.


4. **Deployment fit.** Whether one operator can run it, or whether it needs a dedicated cold email specialist to keep it healthy.


## Why do teams look for an Instantly alternative?


Most switch because Instantly's model is infrastructure-first, and infrastructure is only half the job. Per its own positioning, Instantly gives you sending volume, mailbox rotation, and a lead database. The copywriting, the targeting logic, and the reply-handling stay on your side of the line. This is a fine trade if you have people to do that work. It is a poor one if the reason you bought software was to stop doing it yourself. For a closer look, see our[Instantly AI review](https://www.artisan.co/blog/instantly-ai-review) .


The common triggers we hear:


-


**The manual load doesn't shrink.** You still open the inbox every morning to write, qualify, and reply. Instantly's AI features arrived later than its sending tooling and are still catching up to that core.


-


**Deliverability management is your job.** Instantly gives you the levers; keeping mailboxes healthy across a large sending footprint is ongoing work.


-


**Higher tiers add up.** The jump from entry to hypergrowth pricing is steep once you add mailboxes, lead credits, and seats, per its pricing page.


None of this makes Instantly a bad product. It makes it the wrong product for a team that wants outbound to run without a person steering it. For a related comparison, see[Apollo vs. Instantly](https://www.artisan.co/blog/apollo-vs-instantly-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) .

### 2. Smartlead: The agency and API workhorse


Smartlead is the closest direct rival to Instantly for high-volume senders, and it is often the better pick for agencies. Unlimited mailbox rotation, a deep API, and a master inbox for managing many client accounts make it the tool of choice for shops that run dozens of sending domains at once. If your business is sending on behalf of other businesses, Smartlead's architecture was built for you.


The honest caveat is add-on math. Smartlead's core tiers run $39 to $379 per month per its pricing page, but placement testing is billed separately at $49 to $599, so the effective cost climbs once you turn on the features a serious sender needs. And like Instantly, Smartlead is infrastructure: you still write and reply. See[Instantly vs. Smartlead](https://www.artisan.co/blog/instantly-vs-smartlead-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) , and for agency picks, our roundup of[outbound sales tools](https://www.artisan.co/blog/outbound-sales-tools) .


**Best for:** agencies and API-first teams that run many inboxes across clients.


**Who should pick something else:** teams that want the copy and replies handled, or that dislike surprise add-on costs.


**Pricing:** $39-379/mo core; placement testing $49-599 extra, per Smartlead's pricing page.


### 3. Lemlist: Personalization-first multichannel


Lemlist is the alternative for teams whose whole thesis is standing out in the inbox. It pioneered custom images and dynamic variables inside cold email, and it runs multichannel sequences that combine email with LinkedIn steps. Bootstrapped to roughly $40M in revenue and having acquired the video tool Claap in October 2025, it is a serious, independent company with a strong personalization pedigree.


The trade is that Lemlist's personalization is powerful but manual: you build the tokens, the images, and the logic. It scales your creativity, not your headcount. Pricing runs $69 to $109/user/month, per its pricing page. See[Instantly vs. Lemlist](https://www.artisan.co/blog/instantly-vs-lemlist-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) .


**Best for:** teams that want highly crafted, visually personalized sequences and will invest the time to build them.


**Who should pick something else:** teams that want the personalization generated for them at scale rather than assembled by hand. For that, see our roundup of[the best cold email tools](https://www.artisan.co/blog/the-best-cold-email-tools) .


**Pricing:** $69-109/user/month, per Lemlist's pricing page.


### 4. Woodpecker: Simple usage-based sending


Woodpecker is the pick for small teams that want straightforward pricing and unlimited inboxes without a per-seat model. It charges by usage, about $7 per 100 prospects contacted, which is friendly to teams whose volume is spiky or seasonal. Deliverability tooling is solid and the interface is uncomplicated.


Woodpecker is deliberately narrower than Instantly. It is a clean email sequencer rather than a sprawling growth suite, and its AI and multichannel capabilities are lighter. For a small team that just wants reliable, affordable sending, this focus is a feature. For more options, see our guide to[outbound sales software](https://www.artisan.co/blog/outbound-sales-software) .


**Best for:** small teams that want predictable usage pricing and unlimited inboxes.


**Who should pick something else:** teams that need heavy volume infrastructure, multichannel depth, or the work done for them.


**Pricing:** usage-based, about $7 per 100 prospects contacted, per Woodpecker's pricing page.


### 5. Saleshandy: The budget entry point


Saleshandy is the value pick for teams starting cold email on a tight budget. It bundles a lead finder with sending and sequencing at published, low per-tier prices, so you can prospect and send from one tool without stitching together a lead source. For a first outbound motion, that consolidation at a low price point is useful.


As with the others in this tier, the ceiling is the manual model. Saleshandy sends what you write and leaves the replies for you to handle. It is a fine starting rung; teams tend to outgrow it as volume and personalization needs rise.


**Best for:** teams launching a first cold email motion on a tight budget.


**Who should pick something else:** teams that have outgrown a starter sender or want autonomous writing and replies.


**Pricing:** published per-tier pricing, per Saleshandy's pricing page.


## Stay with Instantly if...


Instantly is the right call for a specific, real profile. Keep it if:


-


You have strong copywriters and a team that enjoys managing the inbox, so the manual work is a fit rather than a burden.


-


Your priority is cheap, reliable, high-volume sending, and you want to operate the deliverability levers yourself.


-


You are running an agency motion and Instantly's lead database plus its sending footprint already cover your workflow.


Switching tools to solve a copy-and-reply problem that Instantly was never meant to solve is a mistake. If the manual load is the problem, this is the signal to move to an autonomous[AI BDR](https://www.artisan.co/blog/ai-bdr) instead.


## How Instantly compares to an AI BDR


The category distinction matters for the buying decision. Instantly, Smartlead, Woodpecker, and Saleshandy are sending platforms: they move messages you create. Lemlist adds personalization craft on top of the same manual model. An[AI sales agent](https://www.artisan.co/ai-sales-agent) like Ava is a different category. It does the sourcing, the writing, the sending, and the replying, and it manages deliverability as part of the job rather than as a set of levers you pull.


If your bottleneck is sending capacity, an Instantly-class tool solves it. If your bottleneck is the human hours spent writing and replying, more sending capacity does not help. This is the fork in the road for most teams evaluating Instantly in 2026. ## Frequently asked questions


**What is the best alternative to Instantly?** For teams that want cold email written, sent, and answered for them, Artisan is the strongest alternative, because Ava runs the full outbound job autonomously with managed deliverability. For teams that want a direct like-for-like sending platform, Smartlead is the closest rival, especially for agencies.


**What is the best enterprise alternative to Instantly?** Artisan. It adds what enterprise buyers need on top of sending: central sales-ops deployment for large AE teams, cross-channel do-not-contact governance, CRM owner routing, an audit trail, and SOC 2 Type II compliance. Instantly's model is built for operators who run their own sending rather than for governed, at-scale deployment.


**Is Smartlead better than Instantly?** For agencies that run many client inboxes, often yes: unlimited mailbox rotation and a deeper API fit that workflow. Both are infrastructure tools with the same limit, though. You still write and reply, and Smartlead's placement testing is a $49 to $599 add-on on top of its $39 to $379 core tiers.


**How much does Instantly cost?** Instantly's public tiers run $47 to $555 per month per its pricing page, scaling with mailboxes, lead credits, and seats. The effective cost depends heavily on your sending volume and how many contacts you enrich.


**Does Instantly write cold emails for me?** Instantly offers AI writing assistance, but the platform is built around sending infrastructure, and those AI features are newer than the core sending tools. The writing, targeting, and reply-handling remain primarily your responsibility, which is the main reason teams look for an alternative.


**Can I replace Instantly with an AI SDR?** Yes, if what you want is the job done rather than the sending enabled. An autonomous AI sales development representative (SDR) like Ava sources leads, writes personalized email, manages deliverability, and answers replies, which covers Instantly's function plus the manual work Instantly leaves to you. Teams that only want a cheap sending layer will not need to switch.


**Which Instantly alternative is cheapest?** Woodpecker's usage model (about $7 per 100 prospects contacted) and Saleshandy's low published entry tiers are the most budget-friendly for small volumes. Cheapest per message is not the same as cheapest per meeting booked, which is where autonomous tools change the math.
