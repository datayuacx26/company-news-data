# 10 Best RocketReach alternatives for 2026

RocketReach is primarily a contact lookup tool, designed to help prospecting sales reps find and verify email addresses and phone numbers of target leads.


If you need an all-in-one outbound solution with detailed account data and advanced multichannel outreach functionality, you’ll need to look elsewhere.


## Why look for a RocketReach alternative?


RocketReach has the following limitations:


-


Contact data, especially mobile numbers, is often incorrect or outdated, according to[user reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/rocketreach/reviews) on G2.


-


The outreach sequencing feature is limited, so most teams will need to invest in another solution to handle[outreach automation](https://www.artisan.co/blog/outreach-automation) .


-


The inflexible “per-month” pricing structure doesn't suit pay-as-you-go needs.


-


There are fewer AI features than in other outbound solutions, limiting the tool’s scalability.


## How to choose the right alternative


When selecting a RocketReach alternative, ask the following questions:


-


**Database size and contact types:** Do you need emails only or also direct dials and mobile numbers?


-


**Data accuracy and verification methodology:** Are you willing to pay steeper prices for higher-quality data that is human-verified?


-


**Intent data availability:** Do you need software that tracks and uses buying signals and online behavior **** to identify in-market accounts?


-


**GDPR and compliance:** If you’re reaching out to European companies, you need to ensure the B2B contact data is compliant with relevant regulations.


-


**Built-in outreach features:** Are you looking to find a tool that also offers outreach automation, CRM sync, and email campaign tools?


-


**Pricing model fit:** Would you prefer per-user subscriptions or a pay-as-you-go or custom enterprise contract?
