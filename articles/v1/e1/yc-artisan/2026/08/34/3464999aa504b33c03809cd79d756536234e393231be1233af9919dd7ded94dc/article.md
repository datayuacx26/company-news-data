## How we chose


Four criteria, weighted for a buyer who has outgrown a starter sender:


1. **How much of the job the tool does.** A bundled lead finder plus sending is a start. The differentiator is whether the tool also writes and replies.


2. **Deliverability at scale.** Inbox placement testing, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC checks, sender reputation monitoring, dynamic send limits, and automatic pausing of unhealthy mailboxes.


3. **Pricing transparency.** Published tiers beat quote-on-request, and we flag add-ons that inflate the real cost.


4. **Deployment fit.** Whether one operator can run it, or whether it needs a dedicated cold email specialist.


## Why do teams look for a Saleshandy alternative?


Most switch because Saleshandy is a starter tool, and starter tools have a ceiling by design. The low price and bundled lead finder make it easy to launch a first motion, but the model assumes a person does the actual selling work: writing every message, choosing every target, and answering every reply. As a team's volume and personalization needs rise, the cheap software becomes the thing capping them.


The triggers we hear most:


-


**Growth outpaces the tool.** A bundled lead finder is convenient early, but its data depth and deliverability tooling have poorer coverage than a dedicated stack, and volume exposes that.


-


**The manual load never shrinks.** Saleshandy sends what you write. The human hours spent writing and replying are exactly the same at 10,000 emails as at 1,000.


-


**Personalization stays basic.** Starter senders lean on light token-swapping, so as reply rates fall industry-wide, teams want truly per-recipient messaging the tool cannot generate.


None of this makes Saleshandy a bad first rung. It makes it the wrong long-term home for a team that wants outbound to scale without scaling the manual work. For fundamentals, see our guide to[cold emailing](https://www.artisan.co/blog/cold-emailing) .

## Stay with Saleshandy if...


Saleshandy is the right call for a specific profile. Keep it if:


-


You are launching a first outbound motion and want the cheapest way to prospect and send from one tool.


-


Your volume is modest and a bundled lead finder covers your list-building needs.


-


Your team writes and replies happily, so the manual model is a fit rather than a burden.


Switching tools to solve a writing-and-reply problem that Saleshandy was never meant to solve is a mistake. If the manual load is the bottleneck, that is the signal to move to an autonomous[AI BDR](https://www.artisan.co/blog/ai-bdr) instead.
