# ZoomInfo vs. Cognism: Which should you choose in 2026?

ZoomInfo is the better fit for teams whose pipeline centers on North American accounts and who want the deepest US data plus intent signals. Cognism is the better fit for teams selling into Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA), where its compliance-first sourcing and phone-verified Diamond Data are the differentiators. The short version is US depth versus EMEA compliance.

## What is the core difference between ZoomInfo and Cognism?


Geography and compliance philosophy. ZoomInfo optimizes for depth in North America; Cognism optimizes for compliant reach across Europe.


ZoomInfo is the incumbent US data platform, now positioned as the GTM Intelligence Platform and trading under the ticker $GTM. Its edge is the accuracy and richness of US enterprise data plus a signals layer, WebSights for visitor identification and Scoops for human-verified intelligence, that most rivals cannot match in this market. See[ZoomInfo vs. Apollo](https://www.artisan.co/blog/zoominfo-vs-apollo-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) for a head-to-head view.


Cognism was built for a different problem: selling into Europe without stepping on the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It sources data with a notification-based model designed for EU compliance, scrubs against do-not-call and Telephone Preference Service registers, and centers its pitch on Diamond Data, mobile numbers verified by a human team actually dialing them. Where ZoomInfo's gravity is North America, Cognism's is EMEA, and its whole architecture reflects this. It depends entirely on where you sell. This is the rare comparison where the winner flips by territory.

## Which is better for GDPR and compliance?


Cognism, and it is not especially close for EMEA outbound. Compliance is the reason the product exists.


Cognism markets GDPR-aligned sourcing with a notification-based model, scrubs contacts against DNC and TPS registers, and positions compliance as a core feature rather than a checkbox. For a team dialing and emailing across Europe, this posture reduces real legal exposure and is often the deciding factor.


One honest note to weigh is that Cognism's subsidiary Kaspr was fined 240,000 euros by France's data-protection regulator, the CNIL, in December 2024, per the regulator's own reporting. It is a data point about a subsidiary, not a verdict on Cognism's core sourcing, but a careful buyer should ask about it directly. ZoomInfo offers GDPR-related features too, but its center of gravity and sourcing model are US-first, which is why compliance-heavy EMEA teams tend to prefer Cognism.


## How do ZoomInfo and Cognism pricing compare in 2026?


Both are gated and annual, so neither offers the transparency of a self-serve tool. Budget for a real evaluation, not a sticker price.


ZoomInfo is quote-based and annual, with buyer-reported entry points around $15K per year that climb with seats, data volume, and add-ons like intent and WebSights. Its contract terms have a reputation buyers should go in aware of. For a deeper look, see[ZoomInfo pricing and reviews](https://www.artisan.co/blog/zoominfo-pricing-and-reviews) .


Cognism's pricing is also gated, with reported ranges of roughly $15K to $50K per year depending on package, seats, and data access. Cognism has historically leaned on unrestricted-view or generous-credit models in some packages, which can appeal to teams that hate per-record credit anxiety, but confirm the current terms directly. Because both hide pricing behind sales, the honest advice is the same: scope your seats and data needs, then negotiate, and do not anchor on the lowest reported figure.

## Frequently asked questions


### Is ZoomInfo or Cognism better for European data?


Cognism, in most cases. It was built for EMEA outbound, with GDPR-aligned sourcing, DNC and TPS scrubbing, and Diamond Data mobile numbers verified by a human team. ZoomInfo's data is deepest in North America, so European-focused teams usually find Cognism's coverage and compliance posture a better fit. Teams selling on both continents sometimes run both, using each where it is strongest.


### What is Cognism's Diamond Data?


Diamond Data is Cognism's phone-verified dataset: mobile numbers checked by a human team that actually calls to confirm them, rather than relying on inference alone. The aim is higher connect rates and lower wasted dials, which matters most in EMEA, where mobile numbers are harder to source. It is Cognism's signature differentiator against providers that verify phones less rigorously.


### How much do ZoomInfo and Cognism cost?


Both are gated and annual. ZoomInfo is quote-based with buyer-reported entry points around $15K per year, rising with seats and add-ons like intent and WebSights. Cognism's reported ranges run roughly $15K to $50K per year depending on package and data access. Neither publishes self-serve pricing, so scope your needs and negotiate rather than anchoring on the lowest reported figure.


### Is Cognism GDPR compliant?


Cognism positions GDPR-aligned, notification-based sourcing and DNC and TPS scrubbing as core features, which is central to its EMEA appeal. One data point to weigh is that its subsidiary Kaspr was fined 240,000 euros by France's CNIL in December 2024, per the regulator. This concerns a subsidiary rather than Cognism's core sourcing, but a careful buyer should raise it directly and confirm current practices as part of due diligence.


### Which has better intent data, ZoomInfo or Cognism?


ZoomInfo has the more established signals layer for the US market, including Scoops human-verified intelligence and intent data, alongside WebSights visitor identification. Cognism offers intent capabilities too, often through partnerships, but its primary differentiators are compliant sourcing and phone verification rather than a deep native intent engine. For US intent depth, ZoomInfo leads; for EMEA compliant reach, Cognism leads.
