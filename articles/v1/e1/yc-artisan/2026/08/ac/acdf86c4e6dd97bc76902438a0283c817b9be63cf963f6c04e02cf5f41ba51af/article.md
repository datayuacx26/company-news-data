# ZoomInfo vs. LinkedIn Sales Navigator: Which should you choose in 2026?

ZoomInfo is a bulk B2B data platform: deep, verified, exportable contact records with intent signals, sold on annual enterprise contracts. LinkedIn Sales Navigator is a social selling tool that lives inside the network, built for finding, saving, and warmly engaging prospects, priced from around $99 per month. Pick ZoomInfo for exportable data at scale, Sales Navigator for relationship-led selling.

## How we compared them


We looked at four things a buyer actually feels: how you extract contact data and use it, how well each finds and qualifies the right prospects, what it costs, and which sales motion it fits. ZoomInfo and Sales Navigator both help you find people to sell to, but they do it in opposite ways: one exports data for volume outreach, the other keeps you inside a network for relationship selling. The right pick follows from how your team actually sells.


## What is the core difference between ZoomInfo and LinkedIn Sales Navigator?


Exportable bulk data versus in-network social selling. ZoomInfo hands you contact records to act on anywhere; Sales Navigator keeps you inside the platform and hands you relationships.


ZoomInfo is a data platform first. It claims over 321 million profiles (a marketing figure) and is built to give you verified business emails and direct dials you can export into your CRM and sequencer, then run outreach at volume. On top of this data sit signals most rivals cannot match: WebSights for website visitor identification and Scoops for human-sourced sales intelligence. It repositioned around a broader go-to-market intelligence story and now trades publicly under the ticker $GTM. For a related matchup, see[ZoomInfo vs. Clay](https://www.artisan.co/blog/zoominfo-vs-clay-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) .


LinkedIn Sales Navigator is a selling tool that lives inside the professional network. Its power is advanced search across the network's constantly updated profiles, saved leads and accounts, and real-time relationship signals like job changes and shared connections, all aimed at warm, personalized engagement. It deliberately does not give you bulk exportable contact data; the value is staying inside the network where the relationship happens.


## Which has better data, ZoomInfo or LinkedIn Sales Navigator?


Different kinds of "better." ZoomInfo wins on exportable, verified contact detail at scale. Sales Navigator wins on freshness and relationship context inside the network.


ZoomInfo's advantage is depth you can take with you: verified emails, direct dials, firmographics, and intent, all exportable into whatever system you run outreach from. For teams that email and call at volume, this portability is the whole point. Sales Navigator's advantage is that its profile data is maintained by the members themselves, so titles and job changes are often fresher than any third-party database, and it surrounds each person with relationship context. But you cannot bulk-export this context into a sequencer, and it gives you no verified personal email or direct dial. If you need contact data to act on elsewhere, ZoomInfo leads. If you need current profiles and warm context inside the network, Sales Navigator leads. For a related matchup, see[ZoomInfo vs. Apollo](https://www.artisan.co/blog/zoominfo-vs-apollo-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) .


## How do ZoomInfo and LinkedIn Sales Navigator pricing compare in 2026?


Sales Navigator is transparent and per seat; ZoomInfo is opaque, annual, and enterprise-priced. This gap alone decides it for many smaller teams.


Sales Navigator publishes seat pricing openly, from around $99/seat/month for the Core tier, with an Advanced tier around $150/seat/month and an Advanced Plus tier sold as a custom enterprise contract. A rep can start on a single seat month to month.


ZoomInfo is quote-based and sold on annual contracts, with buyer-reported entry points around $15K per year that climb steeply with seats, data volume, and add-ons like WebSights and intent. Its contract terms, including auto-renewal and negotiation dynamics, have a reputation buyers should go in aware of. For an individual rep or small team, Sales Navigator is a fraction of the cost; for an enterprise running high-volume outbound off exportable data, ZoomInfo's price can be justified by the motion it enables. ## Which fits your sales motion better?


It depends on whether you sell by volume or by relationship. ZoomInfo fits high-volume outbound; Sales Navigator fits considered, relationship-led selling.


If your motion is email and call at scale across many accounts, you need exportable, verified data feeding a sequencer and dialer, and this is ZoomInfo's design. If your motion is fewer, higher-value relationships built through warm engagement, shared connections, and social touches, Sales Navigator keeps you where these relationships live and reveals the signals that make outreach timely. Many enterprise teams run both: ZoomInfo for exportable data and volume, Sales Navigator for the relationship layer on strategic accounts. They are complementary as often as they are alternatives.


## What is the shared limitation of both ZoomInfo and LinkedIn Sales Navigator?


Neither one writes or sends your outreach. Both are prospecting tools, and this is the honest limit they share.


ZoomInfo gives you exportable data and signals, but a person still writes the messages, sends them, dials the numbers, and works the replies. Sales Navigator identifies the right people and the warm context, but a person still crafts each message and manages every conversation inside the network. Both help you find and prioritize who to contact. Neither owns the contacting. If your real bottleneck is the human time it takes to message and follow up with everyone you have identified, adding either tool sharpens the input without touching this constraint.
