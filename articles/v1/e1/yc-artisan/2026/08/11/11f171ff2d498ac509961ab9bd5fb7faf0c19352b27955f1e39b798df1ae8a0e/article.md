# Artisan vs. Instantly: Which fits your team?

Instantly is a volume cold-email platform: it gives you sending infrastructure, a large lead database, and deliverability tooling, and you run it. Artisan is an AI business development representative (BDR) named Ava who writes the cold email, sends it, manages deliverability, and answers the replies for you. Choose Instantly to run a cheap sending engine yourself. Choose Artisan to have the outbound done.


## What each tool is for


Instantly built its reputation on cold-email volume at a low price. You connect or buy mailboxes, run sending ramp-up on them, load leads (its database claims over 450 million), build sequences, and send at scale with rotation across inboxes. Its deliverability tooling and price are the reason it has one of the largest user bases in the category. It is a strong engine. The catch is that you are the operator. You write the copy, choose who to target, decide the sequence logic, and personally answer everyone who replies. For a closer look at the platform, see[instantly AI review](https://www.artisan.co/blog/instantly-ai-review) .


Artisan works differently: Ava is an AI BDR who runs the motion herself. She finds best-fit leads, writes individually personalized messages, sends them, manages the deliverability underneath, and then does the part Instantly never touches: she reads each reply, qualifies it, answers objections from your knowledge base, and books the meeting. You supervise as much or as little as you want, from approving every message to full autonomy with plain-language escalation rules and an audit trail.

## Prospecting: Targeting and lists


Instantly gives you a large lead database and lets you filter it, but you decide the ideal customer profile (ICP), pull the list, and load it. Targeting quality depends on your operator time and judgment. There is no signal engine deciding who to reach and when. For a sender-to-sender comparison, see[Instantly vs. Smartlead](https://www.artisan.co/blog/instantly-vs-smartlead-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) .


Ava sources and prioritizes for you. She searches over 250 million B2B professionals across over 200 countries, plus Artisan Local Data with over 250 million local businesses, using natural-language search, ICP filters, and intent signals: funding rounds, new executive hires, and active hiring, along with custom signals you write in plain English, each returned with confidence scoring and evidence URLs. Waterfall enrichment across roughly a dozen providers verifies emails and phones, charged only on success. She ranks leads by likelihood of converting before a single message goes out.


## Writing the cold email


This is the core split. In Instantly, you write. Its AI features have grown, but the platform assumes a human authors the copy and the sequence, then sends at volume. Personalization at scale usually means spintax and merge fields, which is why high-volume cold email so often reads generic. For a comparison with another sequencer, see[Instantly vs. Lemlist](https://www.artisan.co/blog/instantly-vs-lemlist-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) .


Ava writes each message. Two prospects in the same campaign receive completely different emails, not one template with a swapped first name and company. Personalization runs from light touches to research-agent swarms that dig for hard criteria. She runs dozens of variations, shifts volume toward what converts, and optimizes at the component level. This is closer to one-to-one personalization at scale than to volume spintax.


## Deliverability: Managed vs. operated


Both platforms take deliverability seriously, and this is where Instantly is strong for an operator. It offers sending ramp-up, inbox rotation, and placement tooling. But you configure and monitor it. If a mailbox starts bouncing, you notice and react.


Artisan manages deliverability for you. It provisions and manages mailboxes, both Artisan-provisioned and connected, tests inbox placement, checks SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, runs mailbox-health dashboards with alerts and dynamic send limits, and auto-pauses unhealthy mailboxes when it detects bad bounce patterns. Email also respects each lead's local timezone and business hours. The outcome arrives without you running the control panel.


SumUp's Karlo Biuk, its growth lead, reported a $52 cost per lead and 8 to 15 positive replies a week from previously unreachable local SMBs, off hundreds of thousands of emails, with Ava handling the send and the deliverability underneath.


## Handling replies: The part Instantly leaves to you


Instantly stops at delivery and tracking. When a prospect replies, a human on your side reads it, decides, and responds. At volume, this inbox becomes a real job, and it is often where high-send campaigns stall: plenty of replies, no one to work them well or fast.


Ava works every reply. She qualifies, answers objections from your knowledge base, chases follow-ups, escalates on rules you write in plain language ("if the lead asks about pricing, escalate"), and books meetings on the right rep's calendar with CRM-owner routing. The calendar fills without a person living in the reply queue. See how it runs on[Ava the AI sales agent](https://www.artisan.co/ai-sales-agent) .


## Pricing: Mailboxes vs. meetings


Instantly is priced to be affordable, which is a real strength. Its all-in-one bundles run Starter $85 to $94, Scale $175 to $194, and Agency $500 to $555/month, with standalone sending plans from Growth at $47 up to Hypergrowth at $358. For an operator who wants cheap volume, that is hard to beat on raw cost.


Artisan is usage-based. Ava's work runs on credits, so you pay for the work she actually performs, and you can try her for free. See[Artisan pricing](https://www.artisan.co/pricing) . The right way to compare them is not price per mailbox. Instantly's low sticker assumes you supply the labor: the copywriting, the targeting, the deliverability monitoring, and the reply handling. Artisan's price includes that labor because Ava does it. Measured on cost per meeting booked, or against a US BDR's base salary of over $60K and a three to six month ramp, the comparison shifts. Teams that use Ava report pipeline at roughly a fifth of the cost of a human BDR.

## FAQ


### Is Instantly better than Artisan for high-volume cold email?


For raw sending volume at the lowest cost, Instantly is purpose-built for the job, provided you supply the copy, targeting, deliverability monitoring, and reply handling. Artisan sends at scale too, and its advantage is that it does all of that work for you and personalizes each message individually instead of relying on spintax. If you want cheap volume you operate, Instantly fits. If you want volume that is written and answered for you, Artisan does. ### Does Artisan handle deliverability the way Instantly does?


Artisan goes further by managing it for you. It provisions mailboxes, tests inbox placement, checks SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, monitors mailbox health with alerts and dynamic send limits, and auto-pauses mailboxes on bad bounce patterns. Instantly offers strong deliverability tooling, but you configure and watch it. Artisan delivers the outcome without you running the panel.


### How much does Instantly cost versus Artisan?


Instantly's bundles run Starter $85 to $94, Scale $175 to $194, and Agency $500 to $555/month, with standalone sending from $47 (Growth) to $358 (Hypergrowth). Artisan is usage-based, with Ava's work running on credits and a free trial available. The honest comparison is not price per mailbox: Instantly's low cost assumes you provide the writing, targeting, and reply labor, while Artisan's price includes that work because Ava does it. ### Who answers the replies in each tool?


In Instantly, you do. It delivers and tracks, and a human on your side works every response. In Artisan, Ava does: she qualifies replies, answers objections from your knowledge base, chases follow-ups, escalates on plain-language rules, and books meetings with CRM-owner routing. For teams without spare SDR capacity, this is often the deciding difference.


### Can Artisan run an agency-style, multi-client motion?


Artisan is built to run outbound for a team, with org-level and campaign-level controls, do-not-contact enforcement, and CRM owner mapping. Instantly's per-mailbox, multi-account model is more tailored to agencies that juggle many clients on a tight tooling budget. If low-cost multi-client sending infrastructure is the need, Instantly fits that shape better; if the need is the outbound work done per client, Artisan does more of it.


### Is Artisan just a cold-email tool?


No. Cold email is one channel Ava runs. She also works social media outreach and dialer call steps inside the same multichannel campaigns, plus signals, enrichment, reply handling, and meeting booking, consolidated into one platform. Instantly is centered on email sending. Artisan is an AI BDR that happens to send excellent cold email as part of a wider motion.
