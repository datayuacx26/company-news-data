## Why do people look for a Bombora alternative?


Two reasons, and they are related. The first is that Bombora sells a signal, not an outcome. Company Surge tells you an account's research activity spiked on a topic, and that is useful, but it is only the beginning of the work. You still have to decide which accounts matter, find the right contacts, write something worth reading, and send it. Bombora feeds the top of that funnel and leaves the rest to your stack. For the wider set, see[intent signals](https://www.artisan.co/blog/intent-signals) .


The second is timing and resolution. Topic intent is account-level and it moves on a lag: a surge reflects aggregated research behavior building over weeks, so by the time a topic crosses the threshold, the account may be well into its evaluation. It also cannot tell you which person at the company is doing the researching. For teams that want to reach a buyer while the window is open, an account-level signal on a multi-week delay is a soft trigger, not a sharp one. For the basics, see[what is intent data](https://www.artisan.co/blog/what-is-intent-data) .


None of this makes Bombora a bad product. It is the topic-intent layer under a large chunk of the category, and Artisan resells it too. The point is that "topic intent" and "acting on a signal" are two different jobs, and Bombora owns the first. The alternatives below split into the ABM suites that wrap intent in scoring and ads, the narrower intent add-ons, and one platform that fires outreach off the signal automatically.
