# The best Supersonik alternatives in 2026

The best Supersonik alternative is Artisan's AI account executive (AE), which runs live, personalized product demos as part of a full AI sales team rather than as a standalone demo agent. 1mind is the enterprise live-agent option, and demo software like Consensus, Navattic, and Storylane covers self-serve demos.


## Why do teams look for Supersonik alternatives?


Usually because a demo agent is one piece of a bigger motion. Supersonik does its job well, as a live AI sales engineer that screen-shares the actual product, answers in real time with synthetic voice (no avatar), and runs around the clock in over 70 languages, with customer logos including Salesforce, HubSpot, and Lemlist and SOC 2 Type II plus GDPR compliance. But it starts and ends at the demo. You still need something to source the pipeline, book the meeting, catch inbound traffic, and progress the deal after the demo ends.


The other reasons are stage and fit. Supersonik is early, a Barcelona company with a $5M seed led by a16z in September 2025, and its pricing is gated on monthly demo volume, so buyers who want a published number or a longer track record shop around. Some teams want the demo folded into a full sales motion rather than bolted on as a point tool. Some want an enterprise-grade live agent that also closes. And some do not want a live agent at all, just a self-serve interactive demo a buyer can click through. This page covers all three paths honestly. ## 2. 1mind: An enterprise live agent that demos and closes


1mind is the closest live-agent alternative if your requirement is enterprise scale. Its photorealistic named agents qualify, demo, handle objections, join live calls, and drive closes across web, product, and calls, and it is backed by $40M in total funding ($30M Series A led by Battery Ventures, public launch December 2025) with a founder, Amanda Kahlow, who previously founded 6sense. It was named the official Drift successor through a Clari and Salesloft referral partnership in March 2026, and its customers include HubSpot, ZoomInfo, and Seismic.


The catch is who it is for. 1mind is enterprise-only and gated, with a reported floor around $100,000 a year, ranging up toward $400,000, and a six-figure average contract per TechCrunch. It is a serious tool for large revenue teams with the budget for it. Smaller teams, or teams that want the demo as one part of a broader consolidated platform rather than a six-figure standalone, will find it out of reach.


**Best for:** large enterprises that want a photorealistic live agent across the full funnel and can clear a six-figure contract. **Who should pick something else:** teams without a six-figure budget, or teams that want the demo consolidated into a broader platform. **Pricing:** enterprise, gated; reported roughly $100K/year floor up to roughly $400K.


## 3. Consensus: Buyer-led demo automation


Consensus is worth a look if what you actually want is scalable demo automation rather than a live conversational agent. It packages interactive product demos that route themselves to the right stakeholder and let buyers self-qualify before a human ever joins, which suits teams with high demo demand and limited sales-engineering hours. Its pricing is published on its site.


The difference from Supersonik is fundamental. Consensus is buyer-led, so the demo runs at the buyer's own pace with no agent in the conversation adapting on the fly. For a repeatable, asynchronous demo experience it is strong; for a live, conversational demo it is a different thing entirely.


**Best for:** teams that want scalable, buyer-led demo automation routed by stakeholder. **Who should pick something else:** teams that specifically want a live, conversational demo agent. **Pricing:** published on its site.


## 4. Interactive demo software: Self-serve product tours


If your goal is a self-serve demo a buyer can click through on your website, interactive demo software (Navattic, Storylane, Walnut, Reprise) is the honest category to shop. These tools let you build guided product tours and embed them on landing pages, so prospects explore the product at their own pace without booking a call. They are well established, with published tiers on their sites.


This is the biggest departure from Supersonik. There is no live agent and no synthetic voice, just a curated click-through of the product. For top-of-funnel education and product-led motions this is exactly right. For a real-time demo where a prospect asks questions and receives answers, it is not a substitute.


**Best for:** product-led and marketing teams that want self-serve, embeddable product tours. **Who should pick something else:** teams that need a live agent to run the demo and answer questions in real time. **Pricing:** published tiers per each vendor.

## FAQ


### What is the best alternative to Supersonik?


Artisan's AI AE, because it runs live, personalized demos grounded in your knowledge base like Supersonik does, but as part of a full AI sales team where Ava, the AI BDR, also prospects, books, and works inbound on one platform. 1mind is the enterprise live-agent option at a reported six-figure floor, and Consensus and interactive demo software cover self-serve demos.


### Is Supersonik a full sales platform?


No. Supersonik is a focused live-demo agent: it joins calls and embeds on sites, navigates the real product interface with synthetic voice, and runs 24/7 in over 70 languages. It does the demo well, but it does not source pipeline, book meetings, or work inbound traffic. Those steps sit with other tools unless you choose a consolidated platform where the demo is one job among several.


### How is Artisan's AI AE different from Supersonik?


Both run live, personalized product demos. The difference is context. Supersonik is a standalone demo agent you add to a motion you assemble yourself. Artisan's AI AE is the close-and-progress layer of a full AI sales team, fed by Ava's pipeline, so prospecting, booking, inbound chat, and the demo run on one platform with one shared knowledge base rather than as separate tools.


### Is 1mind a good Supersonik alternative?


For large enterprises, yes. 1mind's photorealistic agents demo, handle objections, and drive closes across web, product, and calls, backed by $40M in funding and named the official Drift successor in March 2026. The constraint is price: it is enterprise-only and gated, with a reported floor around $100,000 a year. Smaller teams, or teams that want a consolidated platform, will find it out of reach.


### What is the difference between a live demo agent and interactive demo software?


A live demo agent (Supersonik, Artisan's AI AE, 1mind) talks to the prospect in real time, answers questions, and adapts on the fly with synthetic voice. Interactive demo software (Navattic, Storylane, Walnut, Reprise) and buyer-led tools like Consensus give the prospect a self-serve, click-through product tour with no live conversation.
