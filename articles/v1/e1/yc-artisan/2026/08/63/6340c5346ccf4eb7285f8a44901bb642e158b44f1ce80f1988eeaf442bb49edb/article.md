## Why do people look for a ZoomInfo Intent alternative?


Two reasons come up most: cost, and the fact that you are often paying a premium for a signal you can buy cheaper at the source. ZoomInfo Intent is an add-on to a platform reported from around $15K per year, and its contract terms, including renewal and true-up clauses, are worth reading closely. A good part of the topic signal is Bombora-derived, so for teams that mainly want topic intent, paying the ZoomInfo premium on top of a Bombora co-op that they could license directly can feel like a markup.


The second reason is the same execution gap that runs through this whole corner of the category. ZoomInfo Intent enriches a record and flags that an account is showing interest. It does not resolve the full buying committee, write a personalized message, or reach out. This work lands in your sequencer and your reps' queues, and the signal ages while the handoff happens. The intent lives inside the data platform; the selling still happens somewhere else.


None of this makes ZoomInfo a weak platform. Its contact data depth is a real strength, and consolidating intent with enrichment is a genuine convenience for teams already in that ecosystem. The point is that "intent add-on" and "acting on the signal" are two jobs, and the add-on owns the first. The alternatives below split into the source data underneath it, the ABM suites that wrap intent in scoring and ads, and one platform that fires outreach off the signal automatically.
