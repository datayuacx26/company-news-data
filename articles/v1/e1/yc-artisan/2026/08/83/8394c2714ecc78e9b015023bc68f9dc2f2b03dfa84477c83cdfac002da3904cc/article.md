# 8 email prospecting tools for faster pipeline

Building a prospecting stack too often means signing annual contracts and *hoping* that data quality, email deliverability, and outreach personalization hold up.


We’ve curated this list based on what B2B sales teams actually use and recommend. The eight tools below cover every vertical in the category, from all-in-one platforms to dedicated database providers and CRM-native solutions.


## How to choose an email prospecting tool


Prospecting tools generally fall into one of two categories: all-in-one solutions and point solutions. Whether you opt for a consolidated platform will depend on your feature needs, compliance requirements, and, of course, budget.


Use the following criteria when comparing vendors:


-


**Database size and data freshness:** Check the vendor's B2B data coverage in your target regions and ask for bounce rates in your specific segment, especially outside the US.


-


**Deliverability infrastructure:** Look for automatic domain warm-up, mailbox rotation, real-time bounce detection, and spam complaint monitoring across major providers.


-


**Personalization depth:** AI-powered personalization that references prospect activity is the best means of scaling tailored outreach.


-


**Intent data and buyer signals:** Ask which signals the vendor tracks (first-party website activity, content consumption, G2 activity, job changes, funding events) and how they appear inside your workflow.


-


**Compliance:** This is non-negotiable for European prospecting, so look for GDPR-compliant data collection, automatic suppression of opted-out contacts, and EU data residency options.


-


**CRM integration:** If the vendor doesn't include a built-in CRM, confirm native two-way sync with yours, as support beyond Salesforce and HubSpot varies by platform.


-


**Pricing model:** Compare per-seat, per-credit, unlimited sending, and enterprise contracts based on your team size, volume, and compliance needs.

## Best lead database and enrichment tools


If you already have an outreach tool you're happy with, these dedicated data providers will complete your[sales toolkit](http://rtisan.co/blog/sales-toolkit) with verified contact data. They focus on verified contact and company data that feeds directly into your outreach tool or CRM.


### 4. ZoomInfo


ZoomInfo is a data platform that provides deep contact and company intelligence. It's one of the largest B2B databases on the market, with particularly extensive coverage of US mid-market and enterprise companies.


#### Key features


-


420 million contact profiles and 145 million company profiles, with 120 million verified phone numbers


-


In-depth technographic data, including tech stack, software installs, and cloud infrastructure


-


Intent data covering regulatory filings, real-time org chart changes, executive moves, and website visitor activity


-


Org chart mapping for identifying buying committees and navigating complex accounts


-


Native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Snowflake via GTM Studio


-


AI-powered account scoring and prioritization through ZoomInfo Copilot


#### Who is ZoomInfo best for?


ZoomInfo makes sense for enterprise teams selling high-value deals to a defined list of target accounts. It's harder to justify for smaller teams, high-volume prospecting motions, or anyone selling primarily outside North America (data accuracy drops off noticeably in EMEA and APAC).


#### Pricing


ZoomInfo doesn’t publish its pricing.


### 5. Cognism


[Cognism](https://www.artisan.co/blog/cognism-alternatives) is a B2B sales intelligence platform with a focus on GDPR-compliant contact data and phone-verified mobile numbers.


#### Key features


-


Global contact database with the strongest coverage across the UK, DACH, and Nordic regions


-


Manually phone-verified mobile numbers with reported connect rates 2 to 3 times higher than standard databases


-


Do not call list screening across 15 countries


-


Intent data via Bombora integration


-


Chrome extension for pulling verified contact data directly from LinkedIn profiles


-


Native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Salesloft, and Outreach


#### Who is Cognism best for?


Due to mobile data quality and GDPR compliance deeply embedded in the product, Cognism is a go-to for SDR teams doing high-volume cold calling to the UK and European markets.


#### Pricing


Cognism’s pricing starts at $14,000/year for pure lead discovery and list-building functionality. CRM enrichment can be layered as an add-on. At $18,750/year, on-demand data validation is available.
