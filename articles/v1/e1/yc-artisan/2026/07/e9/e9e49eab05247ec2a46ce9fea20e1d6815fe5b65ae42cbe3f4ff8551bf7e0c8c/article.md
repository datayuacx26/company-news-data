## Why do teams look for a Lusha alternative?


Three reasons dominate. The credit model comes first, since users regularly report that credits deplete faster than they planned, which turns a cheap tool into an unpredictable one. Data depth and coverage come next, because Lusha is fine for quick lookups but has poorer coverage than enterprise providers when you need breadth, verified phones, or intent signals. And then there is scope. Lusha stops at the contact. It does not write your emails, run your sends, work your replies, or book your meetings, so the actual outbound work is still on you and whatever other tools you bought.


Which alternative fits depends on the specific pain. Predictable pricing and more scope point one way; compliance depth points another; wanting the entire motion handled points to a platform.

## Stay with Lusha if...


Some users should keep it.
