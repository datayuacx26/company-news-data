This transparency and quick setup are what reviewers praise most. The trade-off is scope. AiSDR's center of gravity is the small and midsize business (SMB) and lower mid-market segment, and its enterprise governance and controls are thinner than on heavier platforms.

## What does AiSDR actually cost in practice?


The published rate is close to the real cost, which is part of the appeal. Even so, a few practical factors shape the total.


The tiers are tied to sending and contact volume, so a team that ramps outbound quickly can outgrow Solo and land on Explore or Scale sooner than the entry price suggests. Annual billing saves about 20%, but it also asks for a longer commitment, so weigh the discount against the option to test first on a monthly plan. Data and enrichment quality affect outcomes as much as the software fee, because an AI SDR that sends to bad contacts wastes spend regardless of tier. And as with any single-purpose agent, if you also need a dialer, deeper CRM controls, or a data platform, these live outside AiSDR and add to the true stack cost. For a smaller team, none of this is prohibitive; the model is deliberately accessible. For the wider toolset, see[SDR tools](https://www.artisan.co/blog/sdr-tools) .


## What do reviewers say about AiSDR?


Reviewers report that the two things AiSDR does best are transparency and speed. The public pricing removes the usual guessing game, and teams say they can have Ami running outbound and inbound quickly, instead of a weeks-long onboarding. For a small team that wants to be sending in days, this responsiveness lands well.


The critiques concern depth more than function. Reviewers report that message personalization can feel templated on harder segments and benefits from human editing, and that the enterprise side of the product, meaning governance, granular controls, audit depth, and security assurances, is lighter than what larger organizations expect. This fits a younger, SMB-focused product built to bring a small team live fast. A revenue operations (RevOps) team that runs a thousand reps under strict controls needs more than it offers. Reviewers who match this smaller profile tend to be satisfied; those who bring enterprise requirements report hitting the ceiling.


## Who is AiSDR best for, and who should look elsewhere?


AiSDR is a strong fit for founders, solo go-to-market (GTM) hires, and SMB or lower mid-market teams that want transparent pricing, a fast start, and a single agent that handles outbound and inbound. If you want to know exactly what you will pay and be live quickly, few tools make it as easy.


It is a weaker fit for enterprises and larger mid-market teams that need deep governance, detailed audit trails, granular autonomy controls, and a heavyweight security posture. Teams whose motion depends on high-volume dialing or a consolidated data-plus-execution platform will also find AiSDR narrower than an all-in-one system. If message depth on complex segments is critical and you do not want to edit outputs, budget for oversight or evaluate platforms with stronger personalization engines.
