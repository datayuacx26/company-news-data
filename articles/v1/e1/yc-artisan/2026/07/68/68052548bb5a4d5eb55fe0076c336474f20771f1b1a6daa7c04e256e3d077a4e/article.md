## What does 1mind do well?


1mind is ambitious, and it's built for the top of the market. Its "AI superhumans" are photorealistic, named agents that show up across your website, inside your app, and on live calls to qualify, demo, handle objections, and push toward a close. Where Supersonik and others screen-share the app, 1mind leans into a human-presenting agent as the face of the buying experience. For a large enterprise that wants a single, polished AI persona spanning multiple buyer touchpoints, this is a coherent bet. For the wider category, see[the best AI GTM platforms](https://www.artisan.co/blog/the-best-ai-gtm-platforms) .


The pedigree is real. Founder Amanda Kahlow previously founded 6sense, so the team knows enterprise GTM. 1mind raised $40M total, including a $30M Series A led by Battery Ventures, and launched publicly in December 2025. Its customer list skews enterprise (HubSpot, ZoomInfo, Nutanix, Coupa, Owner, Seismic), and it was named the official successor for Drift, whose retirement was announced in March 2026 through a referral partnership. If you're an enterprise that wants a strategic AI agent and you have the budget, 1mind is a serious option.


The catch is the price floor and the scope. 1mind is enterprise-only and gated, with reported contracts starting around $100K/yr and running up to around $400K, and a six-figure average annual contract value (ACV) per TechCrunch's reporting. And like Supersonik, the agent is the demo-and-engage layer; the prospecting engine that fills the funnel is a separate purchase. For a fuller breakdown of the vendor, see[1mind pricing and reviews](https://www.artisan.co/blog/1mind-pricing-and-reviews) .


## How is Artisan's AI AE different?


Artisan's AI AE covers the same close-side work. It runs live, personalized product demos grounded in your knowledge base, answers questions and handles objections in real time, and drives the next step, either starting a trial or qualifying and booking a human AE. What's different is that it isn't the whole purchase. It's the last layer of a platform whose main job is to run outbound and inbound autonomously. For a primer on the sales development representative (SDR) category, see[AI SDR](https://www.artisan.co/blog/ai-sdr) .


This platform is Ava, the autonomous AI BDR (business development representative). She finds best-fit leads across a database of over 400M contacts, enriches and prioritizes them with waterfall email and phone data, launches personalized multichannel campaigns across email, social outreach, and dialer steps, handles every reply from a knowledge base, and books meetings on your reps' calendars. Ava Inbound greets and qualifies visitors on your website from the same knowledge base and can route a demo-ready visitor to the AI AE. So the AI AE isn't a standalone six-figure agent; it's the close layer on top of an engine that already generates the pipeline. For the wider field, see[the best AI BDRs](https://www.artisan.co/blog/the-best-ai-bdrs) .


The practical effect is one system and one knowledge base for the entire funnel. A prospect who receives an outbound email, replies, chats on your site, and then watches a demo is handled by coordinated AI employees who share context. With a single premium agent, you still need the rest of the stack around it. For the broader tooling options, see[BDR tools](https://www.artisan.co/blog/bdr-tools) .


## What's the real cost difference?


This is the sharpest contrast. 1mind is a six-figure commitment: reported floors near $100K/yr, ceilings around $400K, and a six-figure average ACV. This is a strategic line item that a large enterprise signs off at the executive level, and for some buyers the polish justifies it.


Artisan's model is a different kind of commitment. The core outbound platform is usage-based. You pay for work performed instead of seats, with no platform fee and a free trial. The AI AE is priced separately, so you talk to sales for it, but there's no six-figure floor that gates your entry to the AI. A team can start with Ava running outbound and add the close layer as it makes sense, instead of committing to a six-figure contract before results are in. Teams that use Ava generate pipeline at roughly a fifth of the cost of a human BDR, and this economics argument holds from startup through enterprise. ## Which scales without a top-down enterprise contract?

## FAQ


### Is 1mind better than Artisan for enterprise AI agents?


For a large enterprise that wants a single, photorealistic, named agent as its buying-experience face and has six-figure budget, 1mind is purpose-built and well-funded. Artisan's advantage is scope and entry cost. The AI AE is one layer of a platform that also prospects and runs inbound, with no six-figure floor and usage-based pricing.


### How much does 1mind cost?


1mind's pricing is gated and enterprise-only. Per TechCrunch's reporting, contracts start around $100K/yr and reach roughly $400K, with a six-figure average ACV. It's a strategic, top-down purchase, so you request a quote directly.


### Does Artisan's AI AE do everything 1mind does?


On the close side, both run live personalized demos, handle objections, and drive the next step. 1mind leans harder into a photorealistic named avatar persona. Artisan's AI AE trades this for integration. It shares one knowledge base and one lead timeline with the prospecting and inbound agents, so it walks in with full context.


### Is 1mind really the Drift successor?


1mind was named the successor for Drift, whose retirement was announced in March 2026 through a referral partnership. If you're migrating off Drift, 1mind is one path. Artisan is a different shape. Instead of a single conversational agent, it's a full platform where the same AI employees prospect, run inbound, and close.


### Which is safer for a buyer who wants to start small?


Artisan. You can start with Ava running outbound on usage-based pricing with a free trial and no platform fee, then add the AI AE close layer when it makes sense. 1mind is enterprise-only with a reported six-figure floor, so there's no small-start path.


### Do I still need my human sales team with either tool?


Yes. Both handle volume and repetitive work and route the hard, high-intent deals to humans. Artisan's framing is explicit. The AI removes the grunt work so reps sell. Neither is meant to take your existing sales team off the field.
