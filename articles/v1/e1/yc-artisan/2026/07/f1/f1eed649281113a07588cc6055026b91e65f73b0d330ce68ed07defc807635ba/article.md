## What does Qualified actually cost in practice?


Qualified's real cost extends well past the platform fee, because it is a premium product built to sit inside a Salesforce estate. Budget with that context.


First, the Salesforce dependency. Qualified is Salesforce-first, so the true cost assumes you already run Salesforce, and its value is tied to that investment. Second, the premium positioning. This is enterprise software with annual contracts and a sales-led motion, so expect a meaningful commitment and implementation effort rather than a quick self-serve start. Third, the acquisition backdrop. With Qualified folding into Agentforce after the April 1, 2026 close, buyers should ask how packaging, pricing, and the roadmap will evolve, and obtain answers in writing. Fourth, scope. Qualified is inbound-focused, so if you also need outbound prospecting, that is a separate system and a separate cost. For an enterprise already committed to Salesforce, these are manageable; for everyone else, they add up.


## What do reviewers say about Qualified?


Reviewers report that Qualified is the premium inbound specialist, and they credit it for exactly that. The inbound conversion playbook, meaning the way Piper and its chat experience engage website visitors, route high-intent traffic, and book meetings for a Salesforce-run sales org, receives consistent praise. Enterprise buyers report a polished product with the maturity and support you expect at the top of the market.


The critiques track the positioning. Reviewers report that the price is premium, which puts it out of reach for smaller teams, and that the Salesforce requirement is a hard dependency rather than a nice-to-have. Scope is the other recurring theme: Qualified is inbound-only, so teams that also need outbound prospecting have to look elsewhere for that half of the motion. The newest concern is the acquisition. With Salesforce closing its purchase on April 1, 2026 and folding Qualified into Agentforce, reviewers raise reasonable questions about how the standalone product and its roadmap will change, which is worth pressing on before a multi-year commitment.


## Who is Qualified best for, and who should look elsewhere?


Qualified is a strong fit for enterprise teams that run Salesforce, treat inbound as a priority, and want a mature, polished tool to convert website traffic into booked meetings. If your motion is inbound-heavy and Salesforce is your system of record, few tools match its depth in that lane.


It is a weaker fit for teams that are not on Salesforce, for smaller organizations where the premium pricing is hard to justify, and for anyone who needs outbound prospecting in the same system. Buyers wary of acquisition uncertainty may also want to understand the Agentforce roadmap before signing. And a team that wants both inbound and outbound handled by one AI, rather than a dedicated inbound point tool plus a separate outbound stack, should weigh a consolidated platform instead.
