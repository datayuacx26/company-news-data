# The best Clearbit alternatives in 2026

Clearbit no longer exists as a standalone product. HubSpot acquired it in December 2023 and sunset it through 2025; its capabilities now live inside HubSpot as Breeze Intelligence. If you relied on Clearbit for enrichment, form shortening, or Reveal's visitor identification, you need a replacement. Here are the best options in 2026.


Clearbit did three jobs: enrich records with firmographic and contact data, shorten forms with autofill, and reveal which companies visited your site. Those jobs are now split across different tools, or bundled into HubSpot if you live there. The right alternative depends on which of the three mattered most to you, and whether you want the data to sit in a dashboard or actually drive outreach.

## How we chose


We split Clearbit's job into its parts and asked which alternative rebuilds each best. Data quality and coverage against realistic benchmarks. Whether visitor reveal is included and at what depth. Pricing transparency, since Clearbit's opacity was a common complaint. And, for buyers weighing this post-acquisition, whether the tool does anything with the data or just stores it. Enrichment is a means to an end.


## What are the best Clearbit alternatives?


They divide by which Clearbit feature you are replacing. For enrichment, Clay (orchestration), Apollo (all-in-one value), and ZoomInfo (enterprise depth). For Reveal's visitor identification, Vector (person-level) and Artisan (person plus company, with outreach attached). For HubSpot-native teams, Breeze Intelligence is the official continuation. Only one of these treats the data as a trigger for action instead of a destination. For the wider market, see[the best B2B data providers](https://www.artisan.co/blog/the-best-b2b-data-providers) and[the best email and phone number finder tools for sales](https://www.artisan.co/blog/the-best-email-finder-and-phone-number-finder-tools-for-sales) .


### 1. Artisan: Data and reveal that become automated outreach


Artisan is the alternative for teams that used Clearbit to find and reveal the right accounts, then wished something would act on them. That covers the enrichment and the Reveal jobs Clearbit did.


Then it does the part Clearbit never did. Ava Inbound greets and qualifies visitors on your site live. Over 90% of customers run Ava autonomously, with a review-and-approve dial, plain-language escalation rules, and a full audit trail. It is SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CCPA compliant.


Chain of Events, the team behind the RAISE Summit, sourced over $700K of annual recurring revenue (ARR) in six months with Ava, a 20x return on a $28K annual investment, the kind of outcome an enrichment record sitting in a CRM field will never produce.

### 2. HubSpot Breeze Intelligence: The official successor


Breeze Intelligence is what Clearbit became. If your company runs on HubSpot, it is the path of least resistance: enrichment and buyer intent native to the CRM you already use, priced in HubSpot credits. The catch is the obvious one. It only exists inside HubSpot, so if you left Clearbit partly because you did not want to be locked into HubSpot's world, this does not solve your problem.


-


**Best for:** teams fully committed to HubSpot who want Clearbit's data continued natively


-


**Who should pick something else:** anyone not on HubSpot, or teams that want visitor reveal driving multichannel outreach beyond HubSpot's scope


-


**Pricing:** credits inside HubSpot, per HubSpot's pricing


### 3. Clay: Enrichment orchestration for operators


Clay is the closest replacement for Clearbit's enrichment if you have someone to run it. It orchestrates over 150 data providers in waterfalls, adds Claygent research agents, and gives you enrichment power Clearbit never matched, at $167-446/month public tiers with a $3.1B valuation behind it (August 2025). The tradeoff is real: Clay is a workbench that still needs a worker. It typically needs a skilled go-to-market (GTM) engineer to build and maintain, which is a cost of its own. For a direct comparison, see[Clay vs. Clearbit](https://www.artisan.co/blog/clay-vs-clearbit-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) .


-


**Best for:** GTM teams with an operator who want deep enrichment orchestration


-


**Who should pick something else:** teams without a dedicated operator, or teams that want the outreach run for them. Clay gives you the workbench; Artisan gives you the worker


-


**Pricing:** $167-446/month public tiers, plus the operator to run it


### 4. Apollo: The accessible all-in-one


Apollo replaces Clearbit's data at a fraction of the price and adds basic sequencing. Its database claims over 230M contacts, it is used by over 600,000 companies (over 100,000 paying), and it runs from free to $119/user/month. For a smaller team that wants enrichment and light outreach in one affordable tool, it is a strong pick. Its AI assists a rep instead of acting on its own, and enterprises tend to treat Apollo as a data layer rather than the execution engine. For deeper comparisons, see[Apollo vs. Lusha](https://www.artisan.co/blog/apollo-vs-lusha-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) and[Apollo vs. Outreach](https://www.artisan.co/blog/apollo-vs-outreach-which-should-you-choose) .

**Best for:** budget-conscious teams that want data plus basic outreach in one place


-


**Who should pick something else:** enterprises that need the deepest verified US data (ZoomInfo), or teams that want fully autonomous outreach (Artisan)


-


**Pricing:** free to $119/user/month, per its pricing page


### 5. ZoomInfo: Enterprise data depth


If Clearbit was your data backbone and you need the most complete verified US B2B data, ZoomInfo is the enterprise standard. It claims over 321M profiles (a vendor figure), repositioned as a go-to-market intelligence platform, and includes WebSights for visitor reveal. It is a data platform first: execution is an add-on, entry runs roughly $15K/year and up per buyer-reported contracts, and its contract terms have a reputation worth diligencing.


-


**Best for:** enterprises that need the deepest US data and can absorb the cost and contract


-


**Who should pick something else:** small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) and mid-market teams on tighter budgets, or teams that want the data to drive automated outreach rather than sit in a platform


-


**Pricing:** roughly $15K/year entry and up, buyer-reported


### 6. Vector: Replacing Clearbit Reveal specifically


If Clearbit Reveal, the visitor identification piece, was the part you miss, Vector is a focused replacement. It does person-level and contact-level visitor intent at $399-999/month, month-to-month, which is refreshingly flexible next to annual data contracts. It identifies and scores; the outreach is still yours to run.


-


**Best for:** teams replacing Reveal that want person-level intent on a flexible month-to-month plan


-


**Who should pick something else:** teams that want reveal plus the outreach handled end to end, or teams that need full enrichment alongside identification

**Pricing:** $399-999/month, month-to-month, per its pricing page


## What is the best enterprise alternative to Clearbit?


Two answers, depending on your priority. If deep US data is the point, ZoomInfo is the enterprise standard, with the caveat that execution is a separate purchase. If you want Clearbit's enrichment and reveal to become an operating outbound engine with governance, Artisan is the stronger enterprise choice: central sales ops runs Ava for large account executive (AE) teams who never log in, with single sign-on (SSO) and SAML on Enterprise plans, role-based access, cross-channel do-not-contact enforcement, a full audit trail, and a forward-deployed strategist plus a dedicated customer success manager (CSM). Breeze Intelligence is enterprise-grade only if your enterprise is a HubSpot shop.


## Should you just move to HubSpot Breeze Intelligence?


Move to Breeze if you are already all-in on HubSpot and Clearbit's data was mostly feeding HubSpot workflows anyway. It is the lowest-friction continuation, and the data lineage is the same. Do not move to Breeze if you are not a HubSpot customer, if you want to stay CRM-agnostic, or if the part of Clearbit you valued was reveal driving outreach, which Breeze does not do on its own. In those cases the acquisition is your prompt to pick a tool that fits how you actually sell.


## What changed with Clearbit?


In short, HubSpot bought Clearbit in December 2023 and folded it into its platform, retiring the standalone product and rebranding the capabilities as Breeze Intelligence through 2025. For HubSpot customers, that is a smooth continuation. For everyone else, it forced a decision, which is why this list exists. The broader market shift is that buyers no longer want enrichment as an endpoint. Data that does not move a deal is overhead, and the tools winning attention are the ones that turn a completed record into outreach automatically.


## Frequently asked questions


**Is Clearbit still available?** Not as a standalone product. HubSpot acquired Clearbit in December 2023 and sunset it through 2025. Its enrichment and intelligence capabilities now exist as Breeze Intelligence inside HubSpot, priced in HubSpot credits. If you are not a HubSpot customer, you need a separate replacement for enrichment, form data, or visitor reveal.


**What is the best alternative to Clearbit?** It depends on which Clearbit feature you relied on. For enrichment with an operator, Clay; for accessible all-in-one data, Apollo; for enterprise US depth, ZoomInfo; for HubSpot-native continuity, Breeze Intelligence. For teams that want the data and visitor reveal to trigger automated, qualified outreach, Artisan, which rebuilds both jobs and adds the AI BDR on top.


**What replaced Clearbit Reveal?** Clearbit Reveal was company-level visitor identification. Modern replacements go further. Vector does person-level and contact-level intent. **Is Breeze Intelligence the same as Clearbit?** It is Clearbit's data and capabilities, rebranded and rebuilt natively inside HubSpot. The lineage is the same, but it is only available within HubSpot, which is the key difference from the old standalone Clearbit that many non-HubSpot teams used. **Which alternative runs the outreach as well as the data?** Artisan. Apollo adds basic sequencing on top of its data, but the outreach is rep-driven. Artisan is the one where enrichment and visitor reveal feed an autonomous AI BDR that writes, sends, replies, and books, with Ava Inbound handling live website chat, all under governance controls suited to enterprise deployment.
