## How we chose


We weighed four criteria against how Regie.ai buyers work. First, how much of the job the tool runs on its own, since the augmentation-versus-autonomous gap is why most people are shopping. Second, sequencing and dialer maturity, because RegieOne bundles both and any alternative has to match this. Third, pricing transparency, given Regie publishes its tiers and buyers expect the same from a switch. Fourth, deployment fit from a lean team through a large, governed sales org. For the wider toolset, see our roundup of[SDR tools](https://www.artisan.co/blog/sdr-tools) .


## Why do people look for a Regie.ai alternative?


They look because RegieOne is built to augment reps, and augmentation has a ceiling. Regie is one of the more credible AI-native engagement platforms out there. Its sequencer, dialer, and Auto-Pilot agents live together, its rep-support philosophy is coherent, and its pricing, reported from $180 to $499/user/month, is relatively transparent for the category. For teams that want reps equipped with strong AI assistance, it is a legitimate pick.


The ceiling is structural. Auto-Pilot drafts, suggests, and speeds up steps, but a rep still owns the motion: deciding, approving, sending, and working replies. That means output per rep improves while pipeline stays bolted to headcount. To double pipeline you roughly double the team. Teams that hoped AI would break this link find that a rep-augmentation platform, however modern, keeps the rep at the center. Per-seat pricing that climbs toward $499/user/month reinforces it. The alternatives below either bring deeper enterprise sequencing governance, lower the cost of the rep-run model, or remove the requirement that a rep run it at all.
