Some BDRs manage the entire outbound demand generation process, while others focus purely on lead generation, leaving engagement to sales development reps (SDRs) and account executives (AEs). This variability in roles highlights the need for flexible, scalable solutions in modern sales processes.


When we say “BDRs,” we mean the team members responsible for the strategic side—developing sales playbooks, defining and researching customer personas—but who also often end up handling more repetitive tasks like cold email outreach and follow-ups.


But wouldn't it be great if your BDRs could focus on the work that really matters?


Well, they can—with the help of AI BDRs.

## Human BDRs vs. AI BDRs


With[65% of US employees](https://www.ey.com/en_us/newsroom/2023/12/ey-research-shows-most-us-employees-feel-ai-anxiety) worried that AI might make their jobs obsolete, it’s only natural to wonder: If AI can manage sales processes, what role is left for human BDRs?


### Human BDRs


First off, human BDRs aren’t going anywhere, and here’s why:


-


**They have the unique experience and vision needed to shape**[outbound strategies](https://www.artisan.co/blog/outbound-sales-strategy) **.** AI needs directions to act on, and human BDRs don’t. Human BDRs can think strategically and spot intent signals that AI might overlook, helping them create new, effective playbooks.


-


**Humans are also better at building trust and rapport with prospects, which is crucial for long-term business relationships** . When it comes to pushing deals forward or securing high-ticket sales, human BDRs still outperform AI.


-


**Good BDRs can read between the lines and adapt in real time during complex interactions.** They excel at understanding buyer psychology and handling objections—something AI, which can only rely on data, simply can’t match.


### AI BDRs


The thing is, human BDRs can only play to their strengths when they're not bogged down with micromanagement. That’s where AI BDRs come in:


-


**AI BDRs can handle a massive volume of tasks in a fraction of the time it would take a human.** From researching leads to sending personalized messages and scheduling follow-ups, AI operates around the clock without getting tired or needing breaks.


-


**AI BDRs excel at analyzing huge amounts of data.** They can track and use intent signals, historical performance, and real-time behavior to prioritize leads and tailor messaging.


-


**AI BDRs don’t require onboarding, training, or additional headcount costs,** making them the perfect addition to any BDR team looking to grow quickly.

And for small startups that don’t have a dedicated BDR team yet, AI BDRs can take on the key BDR responsibilities to get outbound sales operations up and running fast.


No matter how you structure the collaboration, **the key is balancing automation** *(i.e. AI BDRs)* **with a personal touch** *(i.e. human BDRs).*

2.

Throughout most of the outbound demand generation process, the human BDR acts more as a supervisor, overseeing the AI's work while focusing on the high-value aspects of their role.

## Implementing Artificial Intelligence BDR Tools in Your Organization


Which AI BDR tool is right for you? Should you use it for your entire outbound sales process or just specific areas? And how do you know if AI BDRs are actually working? Let’s break it down and tackle these questions step by step.


### 1. Craft a Strategic Vision for AI Integration


AI BDRs can help with a lot of things, from boosting lead quality to creating a smoother experience with personalized messages and timely follow-ups. But before deciding where to use them, take a step back and **look at your business goals—or, more specifically, what’s holding you back from reaching them.**


*Are you consistently missing your lead quota? Maybe you're hitting it but dealing with poor lead quality? Or are you trying to enter a new market but don’t have enough manpower?*


AI BDRs can help with any of these, but you need to be clear on which goal you’re focusing on.


Mind that AI BDRs won’t deliver overnight results. You’ll usually need 3-6 months to see real progress and decide whether to stick with your current approach or make adjustments. And to track that progress, you’ll need to set *measurable* objectives for your AI BDRs.


If you already have a BDR team and know where your gaps are, your goals might look like "automate 70% of follow-up emails next quarter" or "increase lead conversion rates by 10% in 6 months." Or, if you’re just launching a go-to-market (GTM) strategy, you could aim for "booking 100 sales calls in the first three months" or "forwarding 200 sales-qualified leads to sales reps."


The choice is yours, just make sure your objectives align with and support your broader business goals. Setting clear, measurable targets will help keep your AI BDR implementation on track and contribute to long-term success.


### 2. Conduct a Comprehensive Needs Assessment


Once you’ve nailed down your goals, it’s time to figure out **how you’ll achieve them.**


For example, if you aim to forward more engaged leads to your AEs, you’ll want to concentrate on increasing lead volume. But, if you’re looking to boost the number of sales calls through AI, simply increasing lead quantity may not be the best approach—instead, you might need to focus on crafting highly personalized emails.


Depending on your needs, you may decide to implement AI BDRs across any of these functions:


-


**Lead generation:** If your team spends too much time researching leads, an AI BDR can quickly scan databases and qualify prospects based on your ICP.


-


**Outreach:** If your BDRs struggle with scaling outreach, AI BDRs can automate personalized email or social media sequences across thousands of leads.


-


**Data management:** If your lead data is a mess, AI BDRs can handle that, too. They will enrich lead profiles by pulling in additional data points, organize the information for easier tracking, score leads based on engagement, and provide analytics to help you prioritize high-value prospects.


Identifying the specific areas where you want to use AI in BDR will help you define the features you’ll be looking for in your AI BDR software. *On that note…*
