## What is AI in Cold Email?


AI in cold email is simply the use of generative AI to manage parts of your outbound email cycle. It has a range of applications, from initial research of potential customers all the way through to when the time comes to close deals.


AI in cold email automates three main areas:


-


**Prospecting.** AI will use your ICP to filter and enrich leads in your database.


-


**Qualification.** AI will use your scoring criteria to find the most promising leads.


-


**Personalization.** AI will craft personalized opening lines, tailored offers, and calls to action (CTAs).


-


**Follow-up.** AI will send personalized follow-up emails at the right time.
