## What is Outbound Lead Generation?


[Outbound lead generation](https://www.artisan.co/blog/outbound-sales-strategy) is the *proactive acquisition of new prospects* . It’s typically executed by a business development representative (BDR) and involves initiating contact with potential customers who have not interacted with your brand before.


Inbound lead generation, on the other hand, focuses on facilitating the *discovery of your brand* through awareness campaigns, organic content distribution, and referral incentives. It’s also called “attraction” or “permission” marketing. Sales development representatives (SDRs) are responsible for nurturing inbound leads, although the terms BDR and SDR are sometimes used interchangeably.


The key difference between outbound and inbound lead gen is that outbound is targeted more broadly and encourages potential customers to initiate contact with your brand rather than the other way around.

## What Does an Outbound Lead Generation Team Look Like?


Outbound lead generation teams tend to have five well-defined roles:


-


**Marketer:** While marketers are more associated with inbound campaigns, they often play a role in outbound lead generation campaigns. Marketing teams can assist BDRs in their outbound efforts, supporting outreach and targeted advertising.


-


**Business development representative (BDR):** BDRs are the engines of the[outbound sales team](https://www.artisan.co/blog/how-to-build-an-outbound-sales-team) . They are responsible for prospecting, filtering, and reaching out to leads and will often nurture potential customers right up until they become sales-qualified leads (SQLs), at which point account executives (AEs) take over.


-


**Sales development representative (SDR):** Where BDRs do outbound, SDRs do inbound. SDRs are top-of-funnel and pass leads onto AEs for nurturing.


-


**Account executive (AE):** AEs are the “closers.” They are responsible for closing deals with SQLs, negotiating contracts, and communicating with account managers to onboard new customers.


-


**Sales team leader:** The leader oversees the team, sets lead gen targets, facilitates enablement activities, conducts performance reviews, and tracks KPIs. They are also usually responsible for training programs, maintaining team morale, and looking after sales reps more broadly.

### 5. Attending Industry Events and Conferences


Industry events and attendance at conferences and tradeshows are among the most underused lead gen strategies. Yet the data shows they’re highly effective, with[68% of B2B marketers](https://www.teamtecna.com/blog/30-stats-proving-events-are-worth-the-investment) saying they generate the most leads.


People attend events expecting to network and form new partnerships. They’re also an excellent opportunity for smaller companies and startups to put themselves in front of influential leaders and decision makers in the industry.


Here are some tips for making the most out of events:


-


Perfect your elevator pitch to communicate your value proposition to attendees moving between stands.


-


Collect phone numbers and email addresses and put together personalized messages when following up with prospects.


-


Encourage attendees to promote your booth, presentation, or discussion on social media during the event.


-


Create bold, attention-grabbing messaging if you’ve purchased a booth and run ads around that, especially locally.


-


Use traditional print media, like business cards and flyers, to pass on your contact details during the event.

### 6. Create Referral Programs


Referral marketing sits on the boundary between outbound and inbound marketing. Some consider it inbound because it involves leads initiating contact with you. Others think it’s best described as an outbound strategy because you’re indirectly contacting cold prospects.


In any case, referral marketing has a conversion rate[over three times as high](https://www.annexcloud.com/blog/42-referral-marketing-statistics-that-will-make-you-want-to-start-a-raf-program-tomorrow/) as other channels and results in highly loyal customers. It’s also a very cost-effective B2B lead generation strategy.


Here are five tips for running a successful referral program:


-


Offer rewards to *both* existing customers and new ones to incentivize participation.


-


Use referral software to monitor performance and conduct A/B optimization tests.


-


Reduce friction for referrers with shareable links and hands-off compensation (ideally straight to their bank accounts).


-


Differentiate your incentives by offering original and attention-grabbing rewards like VIP experiences and personalized items.


-


Promote broadly but focus on loyal existing customers and individuals with strong, large networks.


It’s also essential to promote your program across *all* your channels—social media, newsletters, organic content, and so on. It’s not just established customers who are able to refer their friends and colleagues. It’s an opportunity for your whole audience, and a multi-channel campaign helps you reach as many people as possible.

### 8. Account-Based Marketing (ABM) Lead Generation


Account-based marketing involves attempting to win one account with very specific advertising and outreach. It’s more common at the enterprise level, where single accounts can justify the budget of an entire campaign.


ABM lead gen campaigns are holistic in nature, targeting companies through advertising, outreach, referral marketing, and events.


Here’s an overview of the main ABM strategies:


-


**Cold email:** This involves identifying key decision makers at a company and sending personalized messages until a connection is made. Traditional direct mail is also often used alongside email campaigns.


-


**Cold calling:** Best done in conjunction with email, cold calling aims to reach key decision makers at target accounts.


-


**Paid ads:** Specialized ad platforms are capable of targeting ads at individuals at specific companies.


-


**Retargeting:** Retargeting tools identify which companies have visited your site. You can then target them with outreach and ads.


-


**Events:** Events are often the easiest way of connecting directly with representatives of a target account. Many will expect to receive pitches at events.


ABM is often sidelined because it isn’t a strategy that results in large quantities of new leads. However, it’s worth remembering that one high-value account can be worth hundreds of smaller ones.
