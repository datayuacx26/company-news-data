# Ridgepost Capital and Stellus Capital Management: Acquisition Discussion with Luke Sarsfield and Robert Ladd

Ridgepost Capital Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Luke Sarsfield and Stellus Capital Management Managing Partner Robert Ladd join Ridgepost Vice President, Investor Relations Brian McKenna for an in-depth discussion on Ridgepost Capital’s acquisition of Stellus Capital Management.


The conversation explores the strategic rationale for the transaction, the complementary strengths of the two organizations, opportunities across Ridgepost’s private credit platform, Stellus’ underwriting approach, and the opportunities created by integrating Stellus into the broader Ridgepost platform.
