## Finding Common Ground with the FORD Framework


Using a reliable structure helps take the pressure off your daily English conversations. The FORD framework is a helpful guide for choosing themes that keep dialogue positive: Family, Occupation, Recreation, and Dreams. Try commenting on shared local events, weekend plans, or even the food and weather to bridge the gap between strangers.


- **Family:** Ask about general interests or shared local activities.


- **Occupation:** Discuss work or studies in a neutral, friendly way.


- **Recreation:** Focus on hobbies, sports, or favorite movie genres.


- **Dreams:** Chat about upcoming travel plans or future career goals.

## Listen Actively and Respond Naturally
