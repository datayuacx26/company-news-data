# Stilta raises $10.5 M, led by Andreessen Horowitz

Stilta builds agentic AI software to enforce, defend, and commercialize patents. Its agents reason across 180 million patents, 250 million scientific publications, and over a trillion archived web pages to surface evidence that legacy tools and manual review consistently miss. The platform helps companies defend against patent assertions, clear paths to commercialization, and uncover hidden value in their portfolios — with every finding traced to a specific document.
