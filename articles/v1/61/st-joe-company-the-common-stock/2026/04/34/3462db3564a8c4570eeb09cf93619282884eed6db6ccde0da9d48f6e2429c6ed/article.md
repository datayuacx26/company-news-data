# THE ST. JOE COMPANY SETS DATE FOR ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS

[PDF Version](https://ir.joe.com/node/20746/pdf)


**April 1, 2026**


**Panama City Beach, Florida** The St. Joe Company (NYSE: JOE) has announced that shareholders of record at the close of business on Wednesday, March 18, 2026 will be entitled to vote at the 2026 Annual Meeting of the Shareholders (the “Annual Meeting”). The meeting will be held on Tuesday, May 12, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Central Daylight Time at the Camp Creek Inn, 684 Fazio Drive, Inlet Beach, FL 32461.


The Company will webcast the Annual Meeting live, in-listen-only format, on the Company’s investor relations website at[https://ir.joe.com.](https://ir.joe.com/events-and-presentations) An archive of the Annual Meeting will be posted to the Company’s investor relations website following the Annual Meeting and will be available until Friday, August 28, 2026.


It is important that shares be represented at the Annual Meeting. Whether or not you plan to attend the Annual Meeting, we urge you to vote and submit your proxy in advance of the meeting. You may vote by Internet, telephone or mail, by following the instructions in the proxy materials.


Shortly after the conclusion of the Annual Meeting, a reception will be held at the Watersound Beach Club for shareholders who attend the Annual Meeting. Space for the reception is limited and advanced registration is required. Please emailJOE2026@joe.com no later than April 27, 2026 to register for the reception or to request additional details regarding the reception.


**About The St. Joe Company**


The St. Joe Company is a real estate development, asset management and operating company with real estate assets and operations in Northwest Florida. The Company intends to use existing assets for residential, hospitality and commercial ventures. St. Joe has significant residential and commercial land-use entitlements. The Company actively seeks higher and better uses for its real estate assets through a range of development activities. More information about the Company can be found on its website at[www.joe.com.](http://www.joe.com/)
