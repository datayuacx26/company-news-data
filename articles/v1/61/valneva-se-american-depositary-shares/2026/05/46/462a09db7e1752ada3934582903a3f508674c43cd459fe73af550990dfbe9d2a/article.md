# Valneva Reports First Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Updates

- **Total** **product sales of €30.5 million**
- **Cash position of €105.3 million as of end March 2026, excluding proceeds from successful reserved offering completed in April 2026**
- **Program launched in April to further reduce operating expenses**
- **Pfizer expected to file regulatory submissions for Lyme disease vaccine candidate**
