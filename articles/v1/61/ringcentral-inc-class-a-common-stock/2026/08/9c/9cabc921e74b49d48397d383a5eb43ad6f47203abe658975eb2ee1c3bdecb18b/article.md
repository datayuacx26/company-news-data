# Inside RingCentral’s Summer Health Day

There’s nothing like stepping away from the screen and spending a summer day together! Recently, our RingCentral Bulgaria team headed out in Sofia for a day of sunshine, games, and time to recharge.


# Sun, games and good company


The day kicked off the morning with coffee and refreshments before jumping into the activities. Teammates played water volleyball, tried out a few pool dives, and joined in on some casual sports. With good food and cold drinks on hand, it was a relaxing,fun, way to catch up with colleagues outside the workday.


# Uniting the team


Events like Summer Health Day are about more than taking a break — they’re about our community. We value the connections we build with one another, and creating opportunities to spend time together outside the office is an important part of our culture. Whether we’re working on a project or cheering each other on in a game of water volleyball, those connections help make us a stronger team.
