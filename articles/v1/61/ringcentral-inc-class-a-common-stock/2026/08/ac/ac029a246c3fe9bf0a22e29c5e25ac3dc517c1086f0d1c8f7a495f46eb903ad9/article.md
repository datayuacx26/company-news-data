## What does AIR Pro’s expansion mean for RingCX users?


Now available in RingCX, AIR Pro unifies voice and digital channels into a single system, eliminating the need to manage separate tools. It automatically detects and switches languages mid-conversation, responding directly in the customer’s spoken language. Rather than relying on simple bot flows, the system reasons through complex, multi-step workflows independently. Organizations manage the entire setup through a centralized studio featuring skill templates, safety guardrails, real-time ROI tracking, and built-in integrations.


### Three core pillars of AIR Pro


- **Radical simplicity: no code agent building for any team member**


AIR Pro lets team members build and deploy AI agents without engineering support. Users state their operational needs, and the platform automatically generates complete specifications. This approach accelerates setup and reduces IT overhead.


- **Vertical readiness: pre-built agents tailored for specific industries**


AIR Pro includes pre-built agents and workflows tailored for specific industries. Teams deploy these ready-made setups immediately to automate common customer interactions without starting from scratch.


- **Full autonomy: AI agents complete end-to-end tasks across connected systems**


AI agents perform complex, multi-step workflows from start to finish rather than just answering questions. Agents adapt through every customer interaction to increase operational efficiency over time. Built on an open architecture, AIR Pro connects with an organization’s existing software stack.


### AIR Pro for Healthcare with pre-built workflows and 100+ native EHR integrations


RingCentral introduces healthcare ready accelerators in AIR Pro, offering pre-built workflows for patient verification, appointment management, records access, billing inquiries, prescription updates, and post-visit follow-ups. The system automates routine administrative tasks securely, allowing medical staff to focus on direct patient care. AIR Pro connects natively with over 100 electronic health record systems including Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, and eClinicalWorks to manage patient data across existing healthcare systems.


“AIR Pro expands what’s possible with AI automation and setting AIR Pro apart is its ability to reason and plan.” Sara Hoyt, Sr. Enablement Manager, Customer Success, RingCentral


## RingCX AI quality management: New unified reporting for human and AI interactions


Now updated AI quality management capabilities in RingCX, extending automated evaluations to virtual receptionist conversations. Supervisors can now analyze virtual agents and human staff in a single reporting view using shared scorecards, trackers, surveys, and dashboards. Combined customer satisfaction analytics benchmark human and AI performance side by side across customer intent, issue type, and satisfaction drivers to pinpoint operational gaps.


### Automated report catalog:


- Raw tracker output report: Displays every tracker hit with its corresponding transcript snippet, speaker identity, and location hit. This breakdown provides direct visibility into the interactions that trigger each tracker.
- Power skills report: Scores per-interaction performance across empathy, listening, tone, and clarity to measure communication quality across every conversation.
- Coaching received report: Delivers an agent-centric view of coaching activity so managers can verify that representatives absorb guidance.


### AI interaction analytics: New Compliance tab


RingCentral enhances its AI interaction analytics platform with a built-in Compliance tab that automatically flags sensitive data under HIPAA, PCI DSS, and GDPR regulations. Built-in severity filters prioritize high-risk interactions so teams can review urgent issues immediately. Compliance warnings attach directly to call details, data tables, and share dialogs, giving administrators visibility to resolve risk before audits occur.


## RingCX admin updates: Screen recording alerts, unified portal, smarter callback previews


#### Screen recording alerts and agent notifications


Agents will now receive clear notifications directly in the application when screen recording is active. Administrators can configure these notifications within general settings to manage in-app visibility across voice and digital agent views, ensuring compliance with organizational policies and regional regulations.


#### Digital interaction preservation on agent disconnect


RingCX holds active chat, SMS, email, and digital sessions for a set duration defaulting to one minute if an agent loses connectivity or refreshes a browser. Reconnecting within this window lets the representative resume the conversation seamlessly. If the timer expires, the interaction returns to the queue to protect customer wait times. Administrators can customize this interaction reservation timeout period for each queue.


#### Unified admin portal for RingEX and RingCX


Administrators can now manage RingEX and RingCX users, channels, and routing from a single unified interface. Centralizing administration across the communications ecosystem streamlines setup workflows and provides one-click access to integrated platform analytics.


#### Enhanced RingCX callback and campaign analytics


RingCX analytics now introduces a previewing state for voice callback interactions, allowing agents to review customer details before accepting a callback request. Providing pre-call context prevents blind acceptances, ensures smoother digital-to-voice transfers, and improves customer interactions.


The system also distinguishes between voicemail left and automated answering machine connections to deliver precise campaign metrics. Isolating actual message delivery reveals true outbound outreach effectiveness and accurate performance data.
