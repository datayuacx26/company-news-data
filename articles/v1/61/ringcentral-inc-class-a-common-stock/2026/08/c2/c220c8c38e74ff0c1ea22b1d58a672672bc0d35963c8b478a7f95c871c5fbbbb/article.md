# RingCentral named a Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ Leader for the 12th consecutive year

We’re proud to share RingCentral’s 12th consecutive year as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Unified Communications as a Service. This acknowledgment reflects the strength of our RingEX solution, and our continued innovation across business phone, meetings, messaging, SMS, contact center, and of course, AI.


Business communications is undergoing its most significant transformation yet—evolving from solutions that connect people, to platforms that help organizations understand what is happening across conversations, and ultimately to intelligent systems that turn those conversations into insights, actions, and outcomes.


The Gartner report highlights how “UCaaS platforms are rapidly evolving with embedded generative AI features such as real-time transcription, translation, meeting summarization, and sentiment analysis, significantly enhancing meeting productivity and reducing administrative workload.” This is where business communications becomes more than connectivity.


It becomes intelligence.


RingCentral is building for this evolution and going beyond the basics of transcription and summarization toward a focus on Agentic Voice AI at the center of what we do. Because RingCentral’s Agentic Voice AI is built on our own reliable, global business voice network and integrated natively across our portfolio, we can bring AI and human interactions together seamlessly for organizations of every size.


We believe RingCentral continues to differentiate itself through its recognized strengths focused on:


- An open platform approach and extensive application marketplace, featuring nearly 600 prebuilt integrations—which maps well with businesses that have customization requirements.
- An intuitive setup, ease of use, and intuitive interface that empowers both users and administrators alike to operate effectively without extensive technical expertise.
- A robust set of capabilities including Customer Engagement Bundle (CEB), AI Receptionist (AIR), and AIR Pro, which align well with small business requirements, while also providing an expansive telephony feature set that supports large enterprise requirements.


The foundation of what RingEX is delivering is a carrier-grade platform that serves approximately 600,000 businesses across 45 countries and supports international numbers in nearly 100 countries. We carry roughly 40 billion voice minutes annually and billions of text messages. This infrastructure and scale are what RingCentral has built upon to develop its robust suite of AI innovations including:


- **AI Receptionist (AIR)** – An always-on virtual front desk AI agent that answers calls and texts, handles routine inquiries, captures leads, books appointments, and routes customers with full context.
- **AIR Pro** – A configurable agentic AI platform that goes beyond answering questions to reason through requests, coordinate workflows, and take action across connected business systems.
- **AI Virtual Assistant (AVA)** – A real-time assistant that captures notes, surfaces relevant context and recommendations, and automates follow-up across calls, meetings, and messages.
- **AI Conversation Expert (ACE)** – A conversation-intelligence solution that analyzes interactions at scale to uncover trends, evaluate performance, provide coaching, and turn dialogue into actionable business insights.


Our ultimate goal goes beyond connecting businesses with their customers. It’s to provide organizations with a single platform that can connect and orchestrate across every customer interaction, whether to a human or an AI agent, and help resolve customer needs more efficiently and effectively.


The next decade of business communications will be defined by intelligent customer engagement and a system of outcomes. Organizations won’t ask whether AI can summarize meetings. They’ll ask whether AI can resolve customer issues, improve customer retention, accelerate sales, or complete business workflows.


We’re proud that Gartner has once again recognized RingCentral as a Leader, and we’re even more excited about what’s ahead and the opportunity to continue raising the bar.
