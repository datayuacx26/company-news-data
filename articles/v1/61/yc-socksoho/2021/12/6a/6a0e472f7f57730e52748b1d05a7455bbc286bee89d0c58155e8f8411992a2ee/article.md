# How we created Revolutionary No-Show Socks? Hear from Pritika Mehta, founder of SockSoho

Last summer, my friend introduced me to the concept of no-show socks. I thought, let me give it a try. And I did not try just one brand, I ordered no-show socks from 78 different brands from across the world!!!


I wanted to wear them with my ballerinas but it was SO ANNOYING to wear these .... **No-show socks slipped down every time I walked** and sometimes, even while I was sitting. Can you imagine the feeling when your socks are crumpled in your shoes and after every 5 mins you have to pull them back to their right position!!


I thought that maybe there was some issue with my feet or shoes that those no-show socks were slipping down 😖


I wanted to find out what's wrong!! So whenever I saw anyone wearing these no-show socks I asked them on why they liked wearing these, which brand they wore and if there was something they didn't like about no-show socks.


**Can you imagine, I heard only one statement," I like wearing no-show socks but I wish they could stick and don't fall again and again."**


We thought that if people want to wear no-show socks but are unhappy with its current features, let us take the matters in our hands. We then set out to make the best possible no-show socks one could ever wear! 💪


After 4 months of intense research and 68 iterations, we came up with uniquely designed no-show socks that won't slip even if you wear them for 18+ hours. We guarantee this. ⭐️


You can sit, walk, run or dance in them without bothering about pulling them up again and again.


IF IT SLIPS, IT'S FREE .... 😈


**- Pritika Mehta (CEO, SockSoho.com)**
