# What do the colors you wear reveal about your personality?

The right use of color can be very powerful as every color exudes a certain feeling and leaves a certain impression on the person looking at it. We only need to look at the world around us to see and feel its impact. For example, we see a soothing sky blue color or pale yellow color, the first image that comes to our mind is of a bright sunny day with a clear refreshing blue sky, we see someone in the sharp violet shade and a royal, noble and extravagant feeling engulfs us, similarly, we see any color or hue, automatically a scenario, a mood, a person or a memory comes to our mind. You may not know it but the color palette of your wardrobe has already thrown an impression about you on others. So why not learn more about how to choose colors on your wardrobe wisely and make a perfect impression.


## **THE BOLD SCARLET**


Scarlet is one of the most powerful and expressive colors. It radiates sweet and innocent vibes when mixed with white to create pastel shade or pink tones or bring about sharp and aggressive responses when used in pure form.


Being exposed to or wearing scarlet with black gives off a powerful vibe. The "power tie" worn by businessmen across the globe is, traditionally, scarlet. And don't forget the "red carpet" that is only rolled out for the most prestigious celebrities and influencers. So, if today you wanna grab attention and leave a powerful impression, scarlet is the color for you.


## **THE CALM GREEN**


Green is one of the most calming and neutral hues. Green represents stability and peace and is often used to portray nature and hope. Pastel green is a bit more cheery and light and has some warm associations. Darker greens represent safety and affluence – think money; American bills are dark green in color. With the addition of blue, greens become more teal and take on cooler tones. Green hue fills you with joy and confidence. In summers, pastel green is a must for your wardrobe for it depicts the harmony and the vast nature out there. Shades of green leaves an everlasting joyous impression.


## **THE SOOTHING BLUE**


This is the color of truth and wisdom. It also has a calming effect and is linked to intellect. It’s also the most stable hue. Blue is a great color to wear to a workplace as it is the color associated with perfectionists who can get the job done, even in stressful situations. Shades of blue depict your loyalty, commitment and creativity. Pastel blue has a conservative, structured and soothing effect. In other words, pastel blue is a refreshing treat for summers.


## **PEPPY YELLOW**


Yellow is a radiant color that exudes warmth and inspiration. It is the happiest of hues and signifies communication, enthusiasm and sunlight. It is the color of high energy, enthusiasm, hope, fun, and cheerfulness. So stop thinking that if you wear a yellow outfit, it will make you look absurd or flashy, It will only mean great joy and will definitely brighten your day.


Pastel yellow or pale yellow provides the most soothing and calming effect to eyes. Illuminating Yellow and Ultimate Gray, a combination of bright yellow of lemon skin and the dull, familiar gray were chosen as the colors of the year 2021 by Pantone. According to Pantone's press statement, Illuminating Yellow is associated with optimism and vivacity, while Ultimate Gray encourages "feelings of composure, steadiness and resilience."


## **ALL TIME WHITE & BLACK**


White and black are the purest forms of colors. Each represents simplicity and formality. It is almost impossible to create anything without the addition of white or black. White is associated with purity, cleanliness and perfection. Black is the color of power, elegance and mystery. Each of the colors has a stark look when used without accentuation. The combination of black and white leaves an ultimate strong and fearless impression . Black and White outfits can never look out of style or season.
