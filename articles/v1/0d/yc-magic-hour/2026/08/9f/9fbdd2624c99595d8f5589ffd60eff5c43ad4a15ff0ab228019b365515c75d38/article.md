## **Character Mode vs Scene Mode**


Magic Hour includes two generation modes designed for different creative goals.


**Character Mode** replaces only the main subject while keeping the original background and environment intact. This is the best choice when you simply want to transform a person into another character without changing the rest of the video.


**Scene Mode** goes a step further by regenerating both the character and the surrounding environment. This makes it possible to create entirely new scenes rather than simply replacing the subject.


For most beginners, **Character Mode** is the easiest place to start because it preserves the original setting and produces more predictable results.
