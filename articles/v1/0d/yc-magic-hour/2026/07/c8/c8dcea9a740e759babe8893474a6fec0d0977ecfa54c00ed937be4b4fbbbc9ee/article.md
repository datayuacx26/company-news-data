## **What Is Unboring AI?**


[Unboring AI](https://reface.ai/unboring) is a browser-based AI platform developed by **Reface** , combining face swapping, photo animation, AI avatars, and video restyling in one place. Its biggest strength is a constantly updated collection of movie scenes, memes, and trending social media templates, making it easy to create entertaining content with just a few clicks.


During our testing, face swaps looked natural on high-quality photos, and the platform also offered more than **40 AI video styles** , including anime, cartoon, clay, and Lego-inspired effects. Unlike editors that let you build everything from scratch, Unboring is primarily designed around its template library, so it's best suited for casual creators rather than users producing original video content.


One thing to keep in mind is its pricing model. Earlier versions used a token system that scaled based on the number of faces and video complexity, while the current version emphasizes subscription plans with different feature limits. As the platform continues to evolve, it's worth checking the latest pricing page before subscribing.


Privacy is another consideration. Unboring processes biometric facial data for face swapping, and its privacy policy grants broad content licensing rights, with some data reportedly retained for up to three years.


## **Where Unboring AI Falls Short**


Unboring AI produces convincing face swaps and has one of the largest template libraries available, but it's not the right fit for every creator.


- **Free access isn't as straightforward as it used to be.** Earlier versions reportedly gave new users five free tokens to try the platform. However, **during our latest testing in July 2026, we were taken directly to the pricing page after signing up** , so we couldn't verify that free trial on a new account. If you're hoping to test the platform before paying, your experience may differ depending on when you sign up.


- **It's still built around templates.** Unboring's biggest advantage is its library of movie scenes, memes, and trending videos, but that's also its biggest limitation. If you want to upload your own footage for YouTube, TikTok, or client projects, you'll need higher-tier features, while competitors like Magic Hour let you start with your own content immediately.
- **Credits and features can be confusing.** Earlier versions used a token system where usage depended on video length and complexity, making costs difficult to predict. The current version focuses on subscription plans instead, but available features still vary significantly between Basic and Premium, so it's worth checking what's included before subscribing.
- **The best features are reserved for Premium.** Basic plans are suitable for casual use, but advanced tools like unrestricted custom content, faster processing, and additional AI features are designed to encourage an upgrade. Frequent creators will likely outgrow the entry-level plan fairly quickly.
