## **Intro**


If you searched for **Swapface** , there are actually **two different products** with nearly identical names, and most review articles never explain the difference. **Swapface** is a desktop application for live face swapping that relies on your GPU, while **SwapFaces.ai** is a browser-based video face swap platform that uses a credit system. ## **Swapface (swapface.org): What It Is and Who It Is For**


[Swapface](https://www.swapface.org/) is a desktop application designed primarily for **real-time AI face swapping** . Instead of uploading videos to the cloud, everything is processed locally using your computer's GPU, which keeps your footage on your own machine.


Its biggest strength is live performance. The software integrates with platforms like OBS, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Discord, and TikTok through a virtual camera, making it popular with streamers, VTubers, and content creators who want to swap faces during live broadcasts.


For smooth performance, Swapface recommends a mid-range dedicated GPU such as an RTX 2070 or better. While the software now supports Apple Silicon Macs alongside Windows, Intel-based Macs are not supported, and performance still depends heavily on local hardware.


The free version allows **10 face swaps per day** , but exported results include a watermark. Paid plans start at $39/month (Pro), offering 50 face swaps per day, unlimited live streaming, and watermark-free exports. A pay-as-you-go option is also available at $19 for 100 credits, though these credits can only be used for offline image and video processing, not live streaming.


**Main limitation:** If you don't have a compatible GPU or you want to face swap a video directly in your browser without installing software, Swapface is not the right tool for that workflow.


## **SwapFaces.ai: What It Is and How It Differs**


[SwapFaces.ai](http://swapfaces.ai/) is an entirely separate product despite its similar name. It runs completely in your browser, so there's nothing to install and no GPU requirements.


Instead of subscriptions, it uses a **credit-based payment model** with one-time purchases. Credits never expire, making it attractive for occasional users who don't want another monthly subscription.


The platform automatically deletes uploaded content from its servers within **24 hours** , which is a useful privacy feature for most users.


There is limited free access to explore the platform before purchasing additional credits, although most serious video generation requires paid credits.


One thing worth knowing: the platform includes tools beyond creative editing that may not be appropriate for professional or workplace environments. Review the full feature list before using it in a business context.
