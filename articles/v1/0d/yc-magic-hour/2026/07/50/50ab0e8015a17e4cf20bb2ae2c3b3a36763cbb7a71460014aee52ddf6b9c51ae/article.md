## **Tips for Better Results**


The quality of your output depends heavily on the quality of the audio you upload. These tips helped produce the most consistent results during testing.


- **Use clean audio whenever possible.** Magic Hour analyzes dialogue, music, Foley, ambience, and sound effects to generate visuals. Background noise or heavily distorted audio can reduce how accurately the scenes match your content.
- **Add a prompt if you have a specific look in mind.** The tool works without one, but a simple prompt like *"cinematic mountain landscape at sunset"* or *"dark futuristic city with neon lights"* helps steer the style.
- **Generate multiple versions from the same audio.** Magic Hour recommends creating **three to five cuts** because each generation interprets the audio differently. It's an easy way to compare visual styles before downloading your favorite.
- **Start with a shorter clip.** A 20–60 second sample lets you evaluate the results quickly before committing to a longer generation.
- **Use a starting image for consistency.** If you're creating content around a specific person, product, or location, uploading a starting image helps lock in that subject across the generated video.
