## **What Is Remaker AI?**


[Remaker AI](http://remaker.ai/) is a browser-based AI editing platform that offers[face swapping](https://magichour.ai/products/face-swap) ,[AI image generation](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-image-generator) , background removal, image upscaling, AI headshots, and dozens of other creative tools.


The platform is operated by **ZINGDECK INTL PTE. LTD.** , a Singapore-based company. Instead of charging a monthly subscription, it uses a **pay-as-you-go credit system** , so you only purchase credits when needed.


Everything runs in the browser, making it accessible on both desktop and mobile devices without downloading software.


According to traffic estimates from Similarweb, Remaker receives roughly **5 million monthly visits** , with much of its audience coming from India, the United States, and Indonesia.


## **How Remaker AI Face Swap Works**


### **1. Create a free account**


Visit[Remaker.ai](http://remaker.ai/) and sign up using your email or Google account. New users receive **30 free credits** immediately after registration.


### **2. Open the Face Swap tool**


From the **AI Tools** menu, choose **Face Swap** . Remaker also offers separate tools for multiple-face swaps, video face swaps, and AI portraits.


### **3. Upload your photos**


Upload the source face you want to copy, then upload the target image where the face should appear.


Remaker automatically detects facial landmarks before processing.


### **4. Choose V1 or V2**


Remaker offers two processing engines. **V2 is described by Remaker as "Faster & more realistic, better with face obstructions. May be unstable - use V1 if it fails."** If V2 doesn't complete successfully, simply switch back to V1 and try again.


**This V1/V2 choice is one of Remaker's most practical features and something most competing tools do not offer. V2 handles face obstructions and partial profiles better. V1 is more stable for straightforward swaps. Start with V2 and fall back to V1 if it fails**


### **5. Generate and download**


Click **Swap** and wait for processing to finish.


On the free plan, downloaded results do not include a watermark.

- **Daily credits expire.** The 5 bonus credits earned each day disappear if they are not used before the day ends.
- **Video projects consume credits quickly.** Video face swaps require substantially more compute than photo swaps, so purchased credits can disappear much faster during longer projects.
- **Quality can vary on longer videos.** While short clips usually look convincing, longer videos are more likely to show blending issues around facial movement, expressions, or lighting.
