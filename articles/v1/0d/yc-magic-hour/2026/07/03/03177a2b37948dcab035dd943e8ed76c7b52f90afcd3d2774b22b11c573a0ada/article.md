# 7 Best Reface Alternatives in 2026 (For Video Face Swap Without Watermarks)

## **TL;DR**


- Reface's free plan limits you to its own template library. You can't upload your own video without paying.
- The seven tools below let you face swap your own videos instead of relying on pre-made templates.
- **Magic Hour** is the strongest free option if you want browser-based face swaps with no watermark and no credit card required for your first swaps.


## **Intro**


Reface is one of the most downloaded face swap apps available. It is also one of the most frustrating to use for free.


The free plan limits you to Reface's own template library. You cannot upload your own video without a paid subscription. Free exports include watermarks and ads. And the subscription pricing (particularly the weekly plan that quietly converts to yearly) has generated significant user complaints.


We compared seven alternatives that solve at least one of these problems, ranked them by how useful they actually are for free, and noted which is best for each type of use case.


## **Why People Look for Reface Alternatives**


People usually don't leave Reface because of poor AI quality. They leave because the free experience becomes limiting once they want to create something beyond meme templates.


- **You can't upload your own videos on the free plan.** Free users are limited to Reface's template library unless they upgrade.
- **Free exports include watermarks and ads.** Many users find the experience interrupted by advertisements between swaps.
- **Subscription pricing causes confusion.** Reviews frequently mention forgetting to cancel weekly plans that convert into much higher yearly costs.
- **Privacy concerns.** Reface processes biometric facial data and states that certain facial information may be stored temporarily, which some users aren't comfortable with.


If any of these sound familiar, the tools below solve one or more of these problems.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **Is there a free Reface alternative with no watermark?**


Yes. Magic Hour lets you face swap your own photos and videos without adding a watermark to free exports. You can get started without a credit card, and the free plan includes **up to 5 image face swaps per day** , making it easy to test the tool before upgrading.


### **Can I use Reface on a computer instead of my phone?**


Yes. Reface's browser version is called **Unboring** . It works on desktop browsers, although many advanced features still require a paid subscription.


### **Which Reface alternative works best for my own videos instead of templates?**


Magic Hour and DeepSwap are both good options because they support uploading your own videos instead of forcing you to use template libraries. Magic Hour is the better choice for beginners thanks to its simpler workflow and watermark-free free exports, while DeepSwap stands out if you need to replace multiple faces in the same clip.


### **Do these tools require an app download?**


No for most of them. Magic Hour, DeepSwap, Vidnoz, Akool, MioCreate, and Unboring all work directly in your browser. FaceMagic is the only tool on this list that requires downloading an iOS or Android app.


### **Are AI face swap tools legal?**


Yes, when they're used responsibly. Creating face swaps for personal projects, entertainment, or content where everyone involved has given permission is generally legal in many countries. Using AI to impersonate someone, spread misinformation, or create non-consensual deepfakes may violate local laws and the terms of platforms like YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.


### **What is the difference between Reface and Unboring?**


Unboring is Reface's official browser-based platform. The Reface app is mobile-only on iOS and Android. Both are made by the same company and share similar template libraries. Unboring adds browser access and some additional web-specific features. Both require paid plans for full access, with Unboring starting at $7.79/month annually.


### **Can I face swap a YouTube video without downloading it?**


Yes. Magic Hour lets you paste a YouTube URL directly into the video face swap tool without downloading the file first. The AI imports the clip, lets you trim it to the section you want, and processes the swap in the cloud. No download or installation required.
