## **Tips for Better Results**


- **Start with the highest-quality scan possible.** Even a sharp phone photo of a printed picture usually performs much better than a blurry or poorly lit image.
- **Always restore before colorizing.** Removing scratches and faded areas first prevents colors from bleeding into damaged parts of the photograph and gives the AI more accurate visual information.
- **Generate a second version if skin tones look slightly off.** Because AI predictions vary slightly between generations, a second attempt often produces more natural-looking faces.
- **Portraits and outdoor photos usually deliver the best results.** Images with clear faces, natural lighting, and recognizable scenery tend to receive the most convincing colors.
- **Crop unnecessary borders before uploading.** During testing, removing large white borders and damaged photo edges helped the AI focus on the actual subject and produced cleaner restoration results.
