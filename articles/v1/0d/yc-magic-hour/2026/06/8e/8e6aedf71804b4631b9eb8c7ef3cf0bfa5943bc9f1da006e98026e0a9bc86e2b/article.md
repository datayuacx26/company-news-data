## **Tips for the Best Results**


- Use a clear, high-resolution photo with good lighting. Images where the clothing is fully visible typically produce more consistent results.
- Choose photos where the subject is facing the camera or standing in a natural pose. This helps the AI better understand how the outfit should fit the body.
- Try multiple clothing styles before downloading your final image. Small changes in outfit selection can create noticeably different results.
- Avoid blurry photos, heavily cropped images, or photos where clothing is hidden behind objects, as these can reduce accuracy.
