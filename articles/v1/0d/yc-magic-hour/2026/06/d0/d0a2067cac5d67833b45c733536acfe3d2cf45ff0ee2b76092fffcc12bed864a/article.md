## **How Fashion and Beauty Creators Build an AI Workflow**


The most successful creators do not rely on a single AI tool. Instead, they build a workflow where each platform handles a specific stage of production.


A typical workflow might look like this:


1. Generate campaign concepts in Midjourney.
2. Create visual variations and marketing assets in Magic Hour.
3. Turn static images into motion using Kling.
4. Refine and edit videos inside Runway.
5. Produce tutorial or educational content through HeyGen.
6. Publish and distribute assets using Canva.


This approach allows creators to move from concept to finished campaign significantly faster than traditional production methods.


Some creators also experiment with specialized workflows such as clothes swapper demonstrations, talking photo content, or virtual product showcases. These formats can help increase engagement while reducing production costs.


As AI continues to improve, fashion creators are gaining access to tools that would have been reserved for major brands only a few years ago.
