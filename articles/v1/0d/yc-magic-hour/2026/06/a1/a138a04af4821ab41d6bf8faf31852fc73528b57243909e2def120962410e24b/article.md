## **Tips for Getting the Most Realistic Result**


The AI handles most of the work automatically, but photo selection still makes a big difference.


- Match the camera angle between both photos whenever possible. A side-profile subject usually won't blend naturally into a front-facing scene.
- Choose photos with similar lighting conditions. Bright outdoor images and dark indoor portraits often create noticeable inconsistencies.
- Avoid photos where the original person's body is blocked by furniture, other people, or large objects.
- Pick source photos with a similar distance from the camera. If one subject fills the frame while the other appears far away, proportions may look less realistic.
- If the first result isn't convincing, try replacing either the target photo or the person photo instead of generating the same pair repeatedly. Small changes often produce much better results.
