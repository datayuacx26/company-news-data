# Greif Recognized as One of America’s Most Responsible Companies

## What This Means for Our Customers


At Greif, responsibility isn’t just a value; it’s a promise to our customers. We are proud to share that this commitment has been recognized once again. For the seventh consecutive year, Greif has been named to[Newsweek’s America’s Most Responsible Companies list](http://google.com/search?q=newsweeks+americas+most+responsible+companies+2026&rlz=1C1GCEB_enUS1189US1189&oq=newsweeks&gs_lcrp=EgZjaHJvbWUqBggAEEUYOzIGCAAQRRg7MgYIARBFGDkyCwgCEAAYChgLGIAEMgsIAxAAGAoYCxiABDILCAQQABgKGAsYgAQyCwgFEAAYChgLGIAEMgsIBhAAGAoYCxiABDIGCAcQRRg80gEIMjYzOGowajeoAgCwAgA&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8) .


This recognition reflects our daily efforts to implement ESG initiatives that directly support our customers’ sustainability goals, such as reducing environmental impact and enhancing supply chain resilience.


## A Recognition Rooted in Customer Commitment


Since Newsweek launched the ranking for 2020, Greif has appeared on the list every year. That consistency matters. It shows customers that when they partner with Greif, they’re working with a company that delivers not just quality packaging solutions but also responsible practices that align with their ESG goals.


The Newsweek/Statista ranking analyzes:


1. Publicly available ESG performance data
2. A large-scale consumer survey focused on responsibility and reputation


Out of thousands of companies evaluated, only 300 earn a spot—making this recognition a meaningful measure of Greif’s long-term progress, transparency, and our commitment to responsible practices that stakeholders can trust.


“Being named to this list for the seventh year in a row is a testament to the dedication of our global teams,” said Ole Rosgaard, President and CEO of Greif. “We remain committed to operating with integrity, making our operations smarter and more efficient, and creating long-term, positive value for our customers, our communities, and our colleagues.”


For our customers, more intelligent and more responsible operations translate into:


1. A dependable global supply chain
2. Continuous innovation in sustainable packaging
3. Responsible sourcing and production practices
4. Reduced risk and greater supply-chain transparency


## Helping Customers Achieve Their Sustainability and Circularity Goals


Greif’s sustainability strategy is designed with customers at the center. Across our global footprint, we are innovating and investing in solutions that help partners advance their own ESG commitments.


## How We’re Supporting Customer Success


1. Innovative, lower-carbon packaging options
2. From fiber to steel to plastics, we’re developing products that reduce environmental impact without compromising performance.
3. Expanded reuse and recycling capabilities
4. Our circular systems help customers close the loop, reclaiming materials and reducing waste.
5. Continuous investment in operational efficiency
6. More efficient operations mean reduced emissions, lower resource use, and stronger performance for our customers’ supply chains.


## A Sustainable Future Built Together


“Receiving this recognition reflects the passion and perseverance of our colleagues around the world who bring our sustainability commitments to life every day. I’m incredibly proud of our teams and grateful for their dedication to building a more responsible and resilient future together,” said Aysu Katun, Greif’s Vice President of Sustainability.


## What This Means for You


Partnering with Greif means you’re working with a company that:


1. Shows measurable progress year over year
2. Upholds rigorous ethical and environmental standards
3. Invests in the future of sustainable materials and technologies
4. Operates with transparency, integrity, and global accountability


Our recognition is not just an internal milestone—it’s a reminder of the value we strive to deliver to every customer, every day.
