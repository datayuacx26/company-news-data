# PPWR Implementation Roundtable

This session is designed to move the conversation past policy intent and into the practical work of delivery. As EU packaging rules begin to reshape industrial systems, organizations across the value chain are confronting uncertainty not about *what* the regulation says, but about *how* it will function in real operating environments. The aim of this discussion is to surface where ambition and execution currently collide, explore how companies are navigating those tensions, and identify the decisions that must be made now to avoid structural dead‑ends later. Held under Chatham House rules, the session prioritizes candid exchange, shared problem‑solving, and a clear-eyed view of what responsible implementation will actually require.


**PPWR Implementation Roundtable Details**


Monday, 11 May 2026, 10:00AM-12:00PM CET


interpack 2026, Düsseldorf, Germany


**What We’ll Examine**


- Challenges for Industrial Packaging
- Reuse – Practical Feasibility
- Recycled Content – Filler and End-User Perspective
- Implementation and Risk Management
- Data Readiness and the Digital Product Passport
- EPR and the Allocation of Responsibility


**Who Should Attend**


A deliberately small group of senior practitioners from across the PPWR implementation ecosystem, including representatives from packaging associations, EPR and system infrastructure bodies, multinational manufacturers, and policy experts. Attendance is limited and by invitation, based on fit with the session’s scope and attendee composition.
