In the food industry, safety is not just a requirement, it is a responsibility that spans the entire supply chain. From raw materials to the final product reaching consumers, every step matters. At Greif, we recognize that industrial packaging plays a critical role in protecting food quality, preventing contamination, and ensuring compliance with global standards.


As a global leader in industrial packaging, Greif is committed to delivering solutions that uphold the highest levels of food safety. Our packaging is designed, manufactured, and handled with strict hygiene controls, ensuring that our customers can rely on us as a trusted partner in safeguarding their products.

**FSSC 22000: A Key Pillar of Greif’s Food Safety Commitment**


At Greif, food safety is embedded in our operations. Our commitment to food safety is anchored by FSSC 22000 certification, an internationally recognized standard for food safety management systems in packaging production.


This certification ensures that Greif facilities adhere to stringent requirements, including risk management, contamination control, hygiene practices, and full traceability. It also reflects our culture of continuous improvement, with regular audits and ongoing enhancements to our processes.

- **Market confidence:** Strengthening brand trust through high-quality, certified packaging solutions.
