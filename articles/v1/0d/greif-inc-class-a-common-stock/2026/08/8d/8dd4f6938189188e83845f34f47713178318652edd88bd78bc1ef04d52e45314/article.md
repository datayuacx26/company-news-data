# Choosing the Right Steel Drum: Why Size Matters

The 55-gallon (~220 liter) steel drum is about as close to a universal standard as industrial packaging gets. It shows up in chemical plants, food and beverage lines, lubricant blending, anywhere bulk liquids need to move. For a lot of applications, it’s still the right call.


### But what if your product doesn’t need that much room?


Shipping a partially filled 220L drum means paying to move empty space, plus higher freight costs and more packaging than the job requires. Whether you’re filling specialty chemicals, food ingredients, laboratory reagents, or high-value formulations, matching drum size to actual volume tightens up your supply chain instead of just filling a container.


A drum that’s too large can mean:


- Paying to transport unused capacity
- Higher packaging material consumption
- More storage space required
- More product residue left behind after dispensing


While a drum that’s too small cuts the other way: more containers, more handling time, more labor, and even more shipping runs.
Matching the drum to the product is what keeps operations lean either direction.

Greif’s small and intermediate steel drums span 15L to 160L, open head or tight head, giving manufacturers real flexibility instead of rounding up to the nearest standard size.
