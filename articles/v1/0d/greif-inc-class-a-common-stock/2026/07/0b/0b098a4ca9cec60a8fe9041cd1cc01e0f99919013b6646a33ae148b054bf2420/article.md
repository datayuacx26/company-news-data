# Why Food and Beverage Manufacturers Choose FSSC 22000-Certified Packaging

Food safety is a priority throughout the[food and beverage supply chain](https://www.greif.com/es/mercado/comida-y-bebidas/) . While manufacturers invest heavily in ingredients, processing, and quality control, packaging also plays an important role in maintaining product integrity from production to final delivery.


Whether transporting edible oils, liquid food ingredients, flavorings, concentrates, or other food products, packaging must be manufactured under controlled conditions to help minimize contamination risks and support product quality. This is why many food and beverage manufacturers increasingly look for packaging suppliers that operate under internationally recognized food safety management systems.


### The Growing Importance of Food-Safe Packaging


Consumer expectations, regulatory requirements, and global supply chains have raised the bar for food safety. Today’s manufacturers are expected to demonstrate robust controls not only within their own operations but throughout their supply networks.


Packaging is a critical part of that equation. Containers used to store and transport food products must be produced with consistent quality standards, effective hygiene controls, and rigorous manufacturing processes. A strong food safety culture at the packaging supplier level helps support the overall integrity of the supply chain.


### Understanding FSSC 22000


FSSC 22000 (Food Safety System Certification 22000) is one of the most widely recognized food safety management system certifications in the world and is benchmarked by the Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI).


The certification provides a structured framework for managing food safety risks through documented procedures, hazard analysis, process controls, employee training, and continuous improvement. Rather than relying solely on product inspections, FSSC 22000 helps food packaging suppliers implement preventive controls and food safety management processes throughout the manufacturing process.


For packaging manufacturers, certification demonstrates a commitment to producing packaging solutions under internationally recognized food safety standards.


### What Is FSSC 22000 Certification and Why Is It Important for Food Packaging Suppliers?


FSSC 22000 is a globally recognized food safety management system certification that helps organizations identify and control food safety risks through documented processes, training, and preventive controls. It is benchmarked by the Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI), making it a trusted standard across the food industry.


For food packaging suppliers, FSSC 22000 certification demonstrates a commitment to manufacturing packaging under controlled conditions that support food safety, hygiene, quality, and product integrity. For food and beverage manufacturers, partnering with certified suppliers can help reduce supply chain risks, support compliance efforts, and strengthen confidence in packaging quality.


### Why Certified Packaging Matters


Choosing packaging manufactured in an FSSC 22000-certified facility provides additional assurance that food safety considerations are integrated into daily operations.


For food and beverage manufacturers, this can help:


- Strengthen confidence in packaging quality and consistency
- Support food safety and compliance requirements
- Reduce supply chain risks
- Protect product integrity during storage and transportation
- Safeguard brand reputation and consumer trust


As food supply chains become increasingly complex, manufacturers are placing greater importance on partnering with suppliers that share their commitment to quality and food safety.
