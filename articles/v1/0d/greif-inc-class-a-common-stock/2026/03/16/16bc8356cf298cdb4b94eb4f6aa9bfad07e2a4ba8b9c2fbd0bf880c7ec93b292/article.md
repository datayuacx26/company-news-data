# When Performance Can’t Be Compromised: Why Greif Leads in Small Plastic Bottles

Greif offers a diverse range of small plastic containers, including bottles, jerrycans and tight heads that are highly customizable to meet the unique needs of customers.


In today’s market,[small plastic bottles](https://www.greif.com/de/flaschen-kanister-enge-kopfe/) are more than packaging, they are critical components of brand reputation, consistency, product safety and compliance. As more suppliers enter the conversation, it’s important to ask a simple question:


Is your packaging provider simply making containers or engineering solutions?


At Greif, we believe leadership in small plastic bottles is earned through performance, innovation and partnership.


### Built for Industrial Performance


Greif’s small plastic bottles, jerrycans and tight head containers are manufactured using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) and designed specifically for real-world industrial use.


We focus on delivering containers that perform consistently across:


- Crop protection and specialty chemicals
- Automotive fluids
- Animal health
- And More


This means prioritizing durability, chemical resistance and operational reliability ensuring containers hold up under demanding conditions from filling line to end use. Because in these industries, packaging isn’t just a container, it’s part of the product system.


### Advanced Barrier Technology That Protects What Matters


For sensitive or hazardous formulations, standard packaging is not enough.


Greif offers a range of versatile and robust[Barrieretechnologie](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLEKCLZE_0roq3AUfdEeXNJgfccolSgGGR&si=aoMt32Pxp50DlFeZ) solutions designed to:


- Mitigate risks of permeation and chemical migration


- Reduce the potential for environmental exposure


- Extend product shelf life


- Protect content from external environmental factors


- Contain hazardous gases and volatile components


These multi-layer barrier solutions provide an added layer of protection where product integrity, safety and compliance are critical.


### Designed for the Real World


A container that works in theory but fails in practice is not a solution.


Greif integrates venting technologies, optimized neck finishes and ergonomic handle designs to ensure:


- Controlled, glug-free pouring


- Safer dispensing


- Improved operator experience


- Reduced workplace incidents


These details matter on the production floor, in warehouses and at the point of use.


### Customization Backed by Expertise


Many suppliers offer standard containers. Greif offers partnership.


Our team collaborates with customers to tailor packaging to exact specifications including:


- Custom heights and weights


- Neck sizes and closure compatibility


- A portfolio of barrier solutions


- Color matching and branding support


- Labeling and sleeving services


With UN approvals we help simplify regulatory pathways while accelerating speed to market.


### Scale, Reliability and Long-Term Partnership


In a volatile supply chain environment, dependability matters as much as design.


Greif brings global manufacturing capabilities, technical expertise and decades of industry experience to ensure consistent quality and reliable supply. We are not reacting to market trends, we are shaping them.


When competitors begin talking about capabilities, Greif customers can point to proven performance. Because leadership isn’t about entering the category. It’s about elevating it.
