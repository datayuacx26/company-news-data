# Greif Keeps Agriculture Moving with Trusted Jerrycans

From the moment agricultural inputs leave production to the moment they’re applied in the field, performance matters. Greif delivers that assurance with[Jerrycans and plastic packaging](https://www.greif.com/es/botellas-bidones-cabezas-apretadas/) built for reliability, protection, and efficiency. Because stronger packaging doesn’t just support agriculture, it helps it thrive.


### Why Packaging Matters in Agriculture


Agricultural chemicals, fertilizers, crop protection products, and liquid inputs require packaging that can withstand demanding transportation, storage, and field conditions. Durable HDPE packaging helps protect product integrity, improve safety, and support regulatory compliance throughout the agricultural supply chain.


### Designed for Safety, Durability & Sustainability


Greif’s jerrycans and small plastics are primarily manufactured from high-density polyethylene (HDPE), offering a strong yet lightweight solution for handling a wide range of agricultural liquids. Whether used for fertilizers, herbicides, pesticides, or crop protection products, these containers are engineered to perform in the toughest conditions.


**Performance vs. Design**


- Durable construction for long-term use
- Leak-resistant closures to prevent spills
- UN-certified options for hazardous materials
- Ergonomic handles for ease of handling
- [Sustainable designs](https://www.greif.com/es/sostenibilidad/) that reduce plastic usage


These features help ensure that agricultural products are protected throughout the supply chain, from production to field application.


**Five Greif Product Key Benefits for Agriculture**


1. **Maintain Product Integrity with Superior Chemical Resistance**
Greif jerrycans are engineered to withstand aggressive agricultural formulations, preventing chemical corrosion that can compromise both container strength and product quality. This means your fill goods remain stable, effective, and uncontaminated, so they perform exactly as intended when it matters most in the field.
2. **Minimize Product Loss with Durable Transport and Storage Solutions**
Built to handle long supply chains and harsh environmental conditions, Greif containers resist impacts, temperature fluctuations, and stacking pressure. This durability reduces the risk of leaks, breakage, and costly waste, helping ensure more product reaches its destination safely and profitably.
3. **Improve Operator Efficiency with Safer, Easier Handling**
Designed with ergonomic grips and controlled dispensing features, Greif jerrycans make handling more precise and less physically demanding. This not only enhances safety for workers but also reduces spillage and application errors, saving time, lowering waste, and improving overall productivity in the field.
4. **Simplified Compliance with Trusted, Certified Packaging**
Greif’s UN-certified plastic packaging is built to meet stringent global regulations for hazardous materials. By using solutions designed for compliance from the start, you reduce regulatory risk, streamline approvals, and gain confidence that your packaging meets industry standards wherever you operate.
5. **Sustainable Farming with Responsible Plastic Packaging**
Greif designs lightweight, recyclable packaging that reduces material use and environmental impact without sacrificing performance. This helps agricultural businesses advance their sustainability goals while maintaining the durability and protection needed to operate efficiently in demanding conditions.
