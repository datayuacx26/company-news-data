# Advancing Sustainability Across Greif’s Nine Plants in Brazil

With nearly 150 years of history, Greif is a global leader in industrial packaging products and services, recognized worldwide for quality, reliability, and innovation.


In Brazil, Greif’s nine manufacturing plants play a critical role in advancing the company’s sustainability commitments. These facilities, which serve customers across Brazil and Latin America, have collectively purchased International Renewable Energy Certificates (I-RECs) equivalent to their 2025 electricity consumption. Through this initiative, Greif is able to connect the electricity used across its operations to renewable energy sources.


The I-REC purchases support the offsetting of Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions across all nine Brazilian plants. By linking electricity consumption to renewable and traceable sources, Greif is taking meaningful steps toward reducing emissions and operating more responsibly throughout the region.


As highlighted by leadership within the Brazil operations, this achievement represents an important milestone for both the company and its customers:


*“This certification reinforces our commitment to more sustainable operations while also delivering value to our customers. I would like to congratulate the entire Tri-Sure team for their strong focus on customers and sustainability.”*


This initiative goes beyond internal impact, it creates direct value for customers. By partnering with Greif, customers can trust that the company’s sustainability actions are credible, measurable, and aligned with supporting their own environmental goals.


More than delivering product quality, Greif provides solutions that meet today’s industry expectations, combining global leadership with strong local responsibility.
