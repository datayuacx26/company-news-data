# Custom Dual-Chamber Plastic Bottles Designed Around Your Product

When one formula isn’t enough, custom dual-chamber plastic bottles from Greif give brands the flexibility to deliver more, without complicating the customer experience. Designed to keep two contents separate until use, these bottles help protect performance, extend shelf life, and elevate how your product is experienced.


Whether you’re launching an innovative formulation or refining an existing one, Greif partners with you to design packaging that fits your product, your process, and your customers’ expectations. From chamber ratios to dispensing systems, every detail is engineered to support ease of use, reliability, and brand differentiation.


## Designed to Keep Two Contents Separate Until the Moment of Use


Many products work best when their components are kept apart until the exact moment they’re needed. Greif’s custom dual-chamber plastic bottles are engineered to do just that, protecting product performance while simplifying how end users apply, mix, or activate the contents.


In industrial and specialty chemical applications, dual-chamber bottles are often used to separate a base material from an activator or catalyst. This ensures consistent performance, reduces the risk of premature reaction, and allows operators to mix only what they need, when they need it, minimizing waste and downtime.


For agricultural products, dual-chamber packaging allows concentrated actives to be packaged separately from stabilizers or additives. Farmers and applicators benefit from extended shelf life, safer handling, and accurate dosing at the point of use, even in challenging field conditions.


In cleaning and maintenance products, one chamber holds a powerful cleaning agent, while the other contains a fragrance, booster, or neutralizer. The components combine only during dispensing, delivering maximum effectiveness while maintaining stability during storage and transport.


Dual-chamber bottles are also well-suited for formulations that require freshness or activation, such as adhesives, coatings, or specialty lubricants. By keeping ingredients isolated until dispensing, manufacturers can preserve product quality while providing customers with a more reliable, repeatable outcome.


### Why customers choose Greif dual-chamber bottles
