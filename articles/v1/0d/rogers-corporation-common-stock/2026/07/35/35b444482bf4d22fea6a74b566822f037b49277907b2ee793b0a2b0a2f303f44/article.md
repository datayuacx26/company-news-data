# Rogers Corporation Releases Its 2026 Sustainability Report

##### Release Date: 07/16/2026


Chandler, Arizona, July 16, 2026: Rogers Corporation (NYSE: ROG) today released its 2026 Sustainability Report, outlining Rogers’ approach to managing environmental impacts, supporting employees, maintaining strong governance practices, and developing advanced materials that help customers improve efficiency and enable next-generation technologies.


“We are pleased to share our 2026 Sustainability Report,” said Ali El-Haj, Rogers President and Chief Executive Officer. “The report reflects our ongoing commitment to sustainability and the role it plays in how we operate, innovate, and create long-term value.”


The report is available on the Company’s website at[www.rogerscorp.com](https://www.rogerscorp.com/about-us/corporate-responsibility/sustainability-report) .


### About Rogers Corporation


Rogers Corporation (NYSE:ROG) is a global leader in engineered materials to power, protect and connect our world. Rogers delivers innovative solutions to help our customers solve their toughest material challenges. Rogers’ advanced electronic and elastomeric materials are used in applications for EV/HEV, automotive safety and radar systems, mobile devices, renewable energy, wireless infrastructure, energy-efficient motor drives, industrial equipment and more. Headquartered in Chandler, Arizona, Rogers operates manufacturing facilities in the United States, Asia and Europe, with sales offices worldwide. For more information, visit www.rogerscorp.com.
