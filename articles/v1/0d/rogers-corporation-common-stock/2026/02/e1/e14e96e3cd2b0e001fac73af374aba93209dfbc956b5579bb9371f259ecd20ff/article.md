# Rogers Corporation Launches PORON® ReSource30 Polyurethane Foam

##### Release Date: 02/25/2026


### A new material solution designed to help companies achieve their climate pledges and contribute to a cleaner future.


Chandler, Arizona, February 26, 2026: Rogers Corporation (NYSE:ROG) (“Rogers”) is pleased to announce the launch of PORON® ReSource30 polyurethane material.


An innovative formulation containing post-industrial recycled and renewable bio-based content, PORON ReSource30 polyurethane is engineered for customers for whom sustainability is a key part of their mission statement. Engineered to provide the same reliability and long-term performance as other members of the PORON® product portfolio, PORON ReSource30 foam leverages a renewable supply chain and reduces reliance on finite, petroleum-based products.


“PORON ReSource30 polyurethane is a highly durable product that supports companies progressing toward their climate commitments, ESG objectives, and investor expectations by incorporating more sustainable raw materials”, states Justin Chretien, Senior Product Manager, Global Polyurethanes for Rogers. “It achieves higher firmness levels at reduced density, thus reducing material consumption and emissions as compared to legacy PORON products.”


To learn more about[PORON ReSource30 polyurethane](https://www.rogerscorp.com/elastomeric-material-solutions/poron-industrial-polyurethanes/poron-resource30) please visit the Rogers Corporation website.


### About Rogers Corporation
