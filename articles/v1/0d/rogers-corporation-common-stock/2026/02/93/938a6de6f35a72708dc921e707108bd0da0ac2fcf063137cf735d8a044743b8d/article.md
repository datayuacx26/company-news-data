# Beyond the Server Rack: Where Soft UL 94 V-0 Foams Solve Real Design Challenges


*Published by Rogers Corporation [Elastomeric Material Solutions](https://www.rogerscorp.com/blog?qsBlogSection={951CE41D-76AB-4377-9060-5F437C222DD7})*


UL 94 V-0 compliance is often associated with server housings and IT infrastructure. But engineers across many industries face the same design challenge: how to fill uneven gaps, protect sensitive components, and still meet flame safety requirements within compact electronic assemblies.


This is where soft, compressible UL 94 V-0 foams make a practical difference. PORON® 40V0 polyurethane foam was developed for a wide range of applications where flame safety and mechanical reliability must work together.

### The Assumption That Causes Design Headaches


A common assumption in electronic design is that achieving UL 94 V-0 compliance requires materials that are relatively stiff, dense, or mechanically unforgiving. This perception is rooted in the historical availability of flame-rated materials that prioritized structural rigidity over compliance.


As a result, teams often compensate by tightening housings, increasing fastener loads, or accepting vibration and noise as unavoidable tradeoffs for flame safety. While effective in meeting compliance requirements, these approaches can introduce new challenges, including connector stress, air leakage, and inconsistent compression after thermal or environmental cycling.


Soft UL 94 V-0 foams offer an alternative approach by addressing flame safety requirements and providing mechanical compliance that helps accommodate tolerances, vibration, and long-term performance in tightly packaged systems.


### Where Flame Safety Collides with Real-World Assembly Constraints


Soft UL 94 V-0 foams are increasingly used in applications where flame safety must be balanced with tolerance absorption, vibration control, and long-term stability.

EV Charging & Automotive Electronics Compact housings for modules and chargers are exposed to vibration and variable tolerances across assemblies Cushions connectors and circuit boards without transferring vibration or overstressing components, maintains UL 94 V-0 compliance


Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) Enclosures evaluated under UL 9540 and 9540A protocols must manage mechanical, thermal, and safety risks simultaneously Soft UL 94 V-0 foam helps fill uneven module gaps without introducing excess force that could complicate qualification or reliability

Across these applications, the challenge is rarely just meeting a standard. It is meeting the standard without compromising mechanical performance over time.


### A Practical Decision Gate for Soft UL 94 V-0 Foams


Before diving into material properties or datasheets, it helps to determine whether a soft UL 94 V-0 foam is the right class of material to evaluate. In many electronic assemblies, flame safety is addressed early in the design process. Mechanical behavior often becomes a concern later, during qualification testing or early production. A soft UL 94 V-0 foam may be worth evaluating when flame-rated materials are contributing to downstream issues such as:


- Difficulty maintaining consistent contact due to tolerance variation
- Increased fastener load or housing stiffness used to compensate for rigid sealing materials
- Sensitivity to vibration affecting connectors, solder joints, or board-level components
- Performance drift after thermal or environmental cycling


If none of these conditions are present, a firmer flame-rated material may be sufficient. When they are present, soft UL 94 V-0 foams offer a way to address flame safety while improving performance. ### Key Design Considerations for Soft UL 94 V-0 Foams


Once a soft[UL 94 V-0](https://www.rogerscorp.com/blog/2026/~/link.aspx?_id=B1DB9EA0C4AB4155B812328275460573&_z=z) foam is deemed appropriate to evaluate, engineers typically focus on several critical factors:


- Tolerance absorption: The ability to compress and conform to uneven gaps without transferring stress into sensitive components
- Safety compliance: Verified UL 94 V-0 performance
- Clean performance: Free from halogens and siloxanes to help minimize contamination risks for sensitive electronics
- Long-term reliability: Resistance to compression set and stress relaxation to maintain sealing force, cushioning, and positional stability over time

### Key Takeaway


Flame safety is rarely the only design constraint. While UL 94 V-0 compliance is often addressed early in the design process, many performance issues emerge later when flame-rated materials are subjected to vibration, tolerance variation, and long-term compression.


Soft UL 94 V-0 foams allow engineers to meet flame safety requirements while maintaining mechanical compliance over time. This helps reduce stress transfer, preserve sealing performance, and support long-term reliability in compact electronic assemblies beyond the server rack.
