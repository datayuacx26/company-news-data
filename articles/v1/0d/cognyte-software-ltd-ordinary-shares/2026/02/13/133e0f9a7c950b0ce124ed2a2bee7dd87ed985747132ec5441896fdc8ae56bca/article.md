# Cognyte Customer Wins Ministry of Defense Innovation Award for Operational Success Countering Terror Financing

**HERZLIYA, Israel, February 24, 2026** –[Cognyte Software Ltd](https://www.cognyte.com/) . (NASDAQ: CGNT) (“Cognyte”), a global leader in software-driven technology for investigative analytics, today announced that a Tier-1 military intelligence agency customer in the Europe/Middle East/Africa (EMEA) region was recognized with a National Ministry of Defense Innovation Award for operational excellence in countering terror financing.


The Innovation Award recognizes proven success in defense and security operations that advance innovation, demonstrate measurable operational impact and address complex national security challenges. This year’s competition included more than 40 submissions from across the country’s Ministry of Defense, with winners selected following an in-depth evaluation by a senior judging panel comprised of experts from academia, industry, the defense sector and government.


The award reflects the customer’s new ability to apply advanced analytics and artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities to address the evolving challenge of terror financing. As funding mechanisms become more sophisticated and increasingly leverage digital platforms, encrypted communications, blockchain, cryptocurrency and open-source channels, investigative teams face growing difficulty connecting fragmented data, identifying blind spots and uncovering the financial networks that enable terrorist activity.


The solution supporting the awarded operation is built on Cognyte’s AI-driven investigative analytics and decision intelligence platform. By connecting the customer’s diverse open-source, financial, and contextual data sources within a single analytical workspace, the platform helped investigators eliminate blind spots and establish a more complete view of financial activity. Advanced analytics and AI were applied to surface hidden patterns, resolve entities across datasets, automate repetitive tasks and queries and generate actionable intelligence that investigators could prioritize and act upon with greater speed and confidence.


“This recognition highlights the customer’s innovation and operational leadership in addressing a critical national security challenge,” said Lior Kohavi, General Manager at Cognyte. “By using Cognyte’s AI and advanced analytics platform to connect fragmented data and expose hidden relations, investigative teams transform information into actionable intelligence, so they are better equipped to identify, follow and neutralize complex money trails and financing networks.”
