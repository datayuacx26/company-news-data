# Cognyte Announces $10+ Million Expansion with Tier-1 National Security Agency, Scaling Capacity and AI-Driven Intelligence

**HERZLIYA, Israel, March 4, 2026** –[Cognyte Software Ltd](https://www.cognyte.com/) . (NASDAQ: CGNT (“Cognyte”), a global leader in software-driven technology for investigative analytics, today announced a $10+ million expansion agreement with a long-standing, Tier-1 national security customer in the Europe/Middle East/Africa (EMEA) region. The agreement expands and upgrades the agency’s deployment of Cognyte’s investigative analytics platform to support significant data growth, extend operational coverage and embed AI-powered predictive analytics to support proactive cyber threat investigations.


This latest expansion builds on a relationship spanning more than a decade, during which the agency has generated significant operational value and investigative performance gains from Cognyte’s solutions, leading the agency to progressively institutionalize the company’s advanced, AI-powered analytics platform as a core investigative infrastructure for combating terror and serious crime.


Operating within a secure, on-premises environment designed to process vast volumes of multi-source intelligence data, the agency continues to expand its reliance on Cognyte’s solutions, increasing infrastructure capacity to meet accelerating analytic demands and sustain mission-critical intelligence performance. Reflecting a multilayer expansion of its investigative analytics environment, the agreement broadens deployment across additional operational units while further embedding advanced data fusion and AI-driven analytics across the agency’s investigative workflows.


In addition, the expansion delivers a new AI-powered cyber threat investigative analytics use case, extending the agency’s operational coverage into an additional intelligence domain and reinforcing the platform’s cross-domain applicability.


“This continued expansion reflects how proven operational performance and new use case deployments drive broader adoption with measurable value,” said Efi Nuri, Chief Revenue Officer at Cognyte. “Expansion at this scale reflects long-term operational trust. When agencies confront increasing data volumes and escalating sophisticated threats, they standardize on solutions that have proven their ability to deliver actionable intelligence at scale. Over many years, this agency has deepened its reliance on our investigative analytics and AI capabilities, while continuously expanding deployments across units.”


“This latest expansion with a long-standing customer demonstrates the durability of demand for our analytics platform,” said David Abadi, Chief Financial Officer at Cognyte. “Sustained expansion within national security environments reflects the foundational role our solutions play in customers’ core operational infrastructure. As agencies increase capacity, extend deployments across units and integrate advanced AI capabilities, they reinforce long-term platform standardization and institutional reliance – strengthening visibility into future growth.”
