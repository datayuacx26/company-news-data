# Securing LA28 and Mega-Events From Attacks in the Era of Data Overload

In a recent[Homeland Security Today](https://www.hstoday.us/subject-matter-areas/cybersecurity/securing-la28-and-mega-events-from-attacks-in-the-era-of-data-overload/) article, Omer Frenkel covers the technologies, operational intelligence and collaboration needed to handle security at major events like the the Los Angeles Summer Olympics in 2028 (“LA28”) and major international sporting events like the 2026 World Cup. With the events fast approaching, there’s opportunity for intelligence and law enforcement agencies to evolve from a reactive response mindset to an approach that emphasizes proactive prevention of security breaches leveraging open-source intelligence (OSINT), Signal and visual intelligence, cyber and other tools.
