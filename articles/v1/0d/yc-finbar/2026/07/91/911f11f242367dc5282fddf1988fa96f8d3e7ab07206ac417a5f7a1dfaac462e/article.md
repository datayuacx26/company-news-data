##


Why Perth Property Remains Attractive for Investors


Despite uncertainty around potential changes to capital gains tax and negative gearing policies, Perth continues to demonstrate strong property market fundamentals.


Low vacancy rates, rising rental prices and ongoing tenant demand are supporting the appeal of Perth investment properties, particularly well-located new developments that offer modern amenities, quality construction and long-term value.


The panel discussed how newly built properties may become increasingly attractive for investors looking for assets that align with evolving tax considerations, tenant expectations and lifestyle preferences.


##


The Growing Appeal of Perth Apartments


As Perth continues to experience population growth and increased demand for quality housing, apartments are playing an important role in meeting the needs of homeowners, downsizers, professionals and investors.


With more than 30 years of experience delivering premium apartments in some of Perth’s most desirable locations, Finbar has seen first-hand the changing needs of the market and the importance of creating developments that combine location, quality and long-term appeal.


From established suburbs to emerging precincts, well-designed apartment developments continue to provide opportunities for those looking to invest in Perth property or secure a home in a thriving Western Australian market.


##


What’s Next for the Perth Property Market?


The future of Perth real estate presents both challenges and opportunities. While market conditions continue to evolve, limited housing supply and sustained demand continue to support confidence in Perth’s property sector.


The panel highlighted the importance of staying informed, understanding market trends and seeking advice from experienced property professionals when making investment decisions.
