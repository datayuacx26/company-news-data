# Everything you need to know about Riverbank Residences

Are you chasing waterfront living with premium amenities, urban convenience, and modern sophistication?


Look no further than Riverbank Residences, a landmark new development rising on the banks of the Swan River in the flourishing Rivervale Springs precinct.


With 143 thoughtfully designed one, two, and three-bedroom apartments across 19 storeys, Riverbank represents a new benchmark in Riverside living, where everyday life feels effortless, and every view is a moment to savour.


###


**An Iconic Riverside Location**


From lazy riverside strolls to lively local eats, Rivervale blends the best of both worlds.


Start your day with coffee at a cosy café, wander the scenic Swan River foreshore, or enjoy a sunset drink at a nearby bar. Just minutes from the Perth CBD, Crown Perth, Optus Stadium, and key transport links, this well-connected yet peaceful pocket offers convenience without the crowds.


It’s an underrated gem, and it’s all right on your doorstep.


###


**A Lifestyle Rich with Amenities**


Riverbank brings resort-style living into daily life with a range of amenities purpose-built to enhance residents' lifestyles.


The Ground Level features:


-


A 20m heated infinity pool with poolside lounges and gazebo — perfect for weekends in the sun


-


A fully equipped residents-only gym with cardio gear, strength equipment, and free weights


-


Sauna to unwind after a long day


-


Lounge & games room — ideal for socialising with family, friends or neighbours


-


Private dining room for intimate dinners or celebrations


-


Nature play area & community garden (‘The Farm’) — a tranquil spot to grow things and meet neighbours


-


Watercraft storage so you can fully embrace your riverfront address


-


Cycling and bike share facilities for effortless exploration


In addition to all of the above amenities, residents can ascend to the 19th floor to enjoy the rooftop entertaining deck. Featuring sunset views over the river and city skyline, BBQ facilities, and stylish lounging areas, the roof deck is the perfect backdrop for drinks with friends, gatherings or quiet moments above the city.


###


**Stylish and Functional Interiors**


At Riverbank, everyday living is elevated by intuitive technology and understated elegance, creating a sense of ease, comfort and flow.


Welcome guests to your apartment through a discreet intercom system, granting access remotely via your smartphone, wherever you are. Your private sanctuary awaits behind secure, keyless entry, accessible by fingerprint, personal PIN, or sleek swipe fob.


Stepping inside your sanctuary, comfort and style coexist intuitively. Exquisite interiors flaunt high-end appliances, stone benchtops, and premium fittings, opening out to expansive balconies that frame tranquil tree canopies and sweeping river and city views.


Whether you’re enjoying your morning coffee or unwinding in your living room after a busy day, every detail reflects contemporary elegance and liveability.


###


**Designed to Benefit You, and the Environment**


Designed to achieve an 8-Star NatHERS energy rating, Riverbank is built for tomorrow. Sustainability is embedded in every facet of design, construction and operation, with a clear path to carbon-neutral certification through innovation, efficiency and offsetting.


Some of the key sustainability features at Riverbank include:


-


Double-glazed windows and external doors reduce the need for excessive heating and cooling


-


The installation of a centralised hot water system to reduce energy consumption


-


Solar PV installed on the roof to reduce running costs


-


Recessed LED downlights throughout for maximum energy efficiency and light quality


-


Rainwater capture for irrigation.


-


Waste recycling, low-impact materials and circular practices minimise lifecycle impacts.


-


BubbleDeck slab system reduces concrete volume and removes transport-intensive formwork
