# 50% Sold: The Success of the Romeo Applecross Launch

The recent launch of Romeo Applecross attracted significant attention, with 50% of apartments sold during the release period. This milestone represents approximately $84 million in presales, marking a strong start for the final stage of Finbar’s Applecross precinct. The success reflects the high demand for quality apartment living in one of Perth’s most sought-after riverside suburbs.


##


Romeo Applecross Completes a Landmark Precinct


Romeo Applecross is the third and final stage in Finbar’s Applecross precinct, following the successful delivery of Sabina and Aurora. Together, these three stages form a cohesive and evolving community that has reshaped apartment living in Applecross.


This final release offers 152 thoughtfully designed residences, including one, two, and three-bedroom apartments. The collection balances luxury design with functional living spaces, appealing to a wide range of buyers looking to elevate their lifestyle or add to their portfolio.


##


Resort-Style Amenities Designed for Lifestyle


Romeo Applecross offers a curated collection of resort-style amenities designed to elevate everyday living.


Residents can unwind on the Level 24 rooftop entertaining deck with panoramic views, or enjoy the 25-metre heated swimming pool, cabana, and BBQ areas. Wellness is supported with a fully equipped gym, sauna, steam room, and a dedicated massage space.


For entertaining and connection, a private dining room, residents’ lounge with games area, and a private cinema provide spaces to gather or relax. A business centre with work pods supports working from home, while a music and recording studio adds a creative edge.


Practical touches include a guest apartment, pet play area and wash station, concierge services, smart parcel lockers, and car share facilities, bringing ease and convenience to everyday life.


Together, these amenities create more than just a home; they deliver an elevated lifestyle experience for residents.


##


The Lane: A New Public Space for Applecross


A key feature of the precinct is The Lane, a landscaped pedestrian link connecting Canning Highway and Kintail Road. This space is designed to encourage community interaction and includes public art installations and gathering areas.


Once complete, suspended light sculptures will illuminate The Lane, transforming it into a lively public destination. This addition will bring new energy and identity to the Applecross streetscape, benefiting both residents and visitors.


##


Strong Demand Driven by Location and Quality


Finbar CEO Ronald Chan highlighted the strong interest from buyers during the launch. He noted that the appeal of well-located, high-quality apartments continues to grow, especially with the added incentive of stamp duty concessions available before construction begins.


The location of Romeo Applecross offers residents easy access to the


Perth CBD, quality schools, transport links, vibrant café and retail precincts, parks, and the Swan River


. This combination of convenience and lifestyle makes it an attractive choice for a diverse group of buyers.


##


What Buyers Can Expect Next


With 50% of apartments sold at launch, the momentum is expected to continue as buyers benefit from off-the-plan apartment stamp duty concessions and fixed-price contracts, adding further appeal to the investment.


Finbar’s careful planning and delivery of the Applecross precinct ensure that Romeo Applecross will maintain the high standards set by Sabina and Aurora. This final stage completes the vision for a connected, vibrant riverside neighbourhood.


The success of the Romeo Applecross launch shows that quality, location, and lifestyle remain top priorities for apartment buyers in Perth. With strong sales and a well-rounded offering of residences and amenities, this final stage promises to be a valuable addition to the Applecross community.
