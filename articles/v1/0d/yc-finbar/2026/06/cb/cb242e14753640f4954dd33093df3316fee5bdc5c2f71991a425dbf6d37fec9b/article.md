# Bel-Air Apartments Reaches Completion: Finbar's 80th Development

Finbar is proud to announce the completion of our Belmont development, Bel-Air Apartments, thanks to our builder


Hanssen Construction


, Chang Architects and the many consultants and contractors who have contributed to the construction of this project.


Marking Finbar's 80th completed development, Bel-Air Apartments represents another significant milestone in our commitment to delivering quality apartment living in well-connected locations across Western Australia.


Commenting on the project's completion, Finbar CEO Ronald Chan said:


> "The completion of Bel-Air is a clear example of our objective to deliver mid-level apartment stock that responds to genuine market demand. By focusing on affordability, quality and well-connected locations, we are providing new homes that are accessible to more people without compromising on design, amenity or liveability."


The $114 million development comprises 194 one- and two-bedroom apartments, a ground-floor commercial precinct, and an extensive range of resort-style resident facilities designed to support a vibrant and connected lifestyle.


Located in the heart of Belmont, Bel-Air Apartments offers residents convenient access to major transport links, employment hubs, retail amenities and recreational destinations, which made it an attractive option for owner-occupiers and investors alike.


To celebrate the completion of the development, Finbar hosted the Grand Opening of Bel-Air Apartments on Saturday, welcoming project partners, buyers and special guests. The occasion was marked with a ribbon cutting by Finbar CEO Ronald Chan, Finbar Chairman John Chan, Minister for Housing and Works the Hon John Carey MLA, Hanssen Construction's Gerry Hanssen and Darren Linton, and Chang Architects Director Sin Chang.


The completion of Bel-Air highlights Finbar's continued focus on delivering thoughtfully designed developments that meet the evolving needs of Western Australians.


We extend our sincere thanks to our valued purchasers, and the dedicated teams from Hanssen Construction and Chang Architects for their contribution to the successful delivery of Bel-Air Apartments.


As residents begin moving into their new homes, we look forward to seeing the Bel-Air community flourish and become an integral part of Belmont's growing urban landscape.
