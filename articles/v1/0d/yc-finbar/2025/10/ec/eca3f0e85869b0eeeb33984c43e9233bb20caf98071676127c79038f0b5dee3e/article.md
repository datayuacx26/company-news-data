# Finbar Commences Construction at Riverbank Residences

Construction has now commenced at our almost sold-out development, Riverbank Residences.


We marked this milestone over the weekend with a Turning of the Soil ceremony, where our buyers joined us to celebrate the official start of construction.


Launched at the end of August, Riverbank Residences saw immediate strong interest with 70% sold during the initial release period. Now, just over a month later, construction has commenced, and the project has only 10 apartments left for sale.


> **Finbar’s brand equity, built up through 30 years of successful developments, played a key role in this project’s success, with almost half of the sales coming from repeat buyers.**


The $115m project is set to comprise 143 one, two and three-bedroom apartments across 19 storeys, and features a range of intentional resort-style amenities set to enhance residents’ lifestyles, including a roof deck offering all residents river and city views.


Sustainability is central to the design of the development, with Riverbank set to achieve an 8-star average NatHERS rating and Greenstar Design certification, embedding efficiency and environmental responsibility throughout construction and operation.


A huge thank you to our builder, Hanssen Construction, Chang Architects, and every buyer who made this milestone possible.


We look forward to delivering Riverbank Residences in mid-2027.
