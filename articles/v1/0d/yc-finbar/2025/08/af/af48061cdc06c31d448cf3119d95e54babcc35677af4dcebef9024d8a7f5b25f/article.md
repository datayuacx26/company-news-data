# Bel-Air Apartments is sold out!

We’re pleased to announce that our Bel-Air Apartments development has officially sold out during the construction phase and well ahead of its anticipated completion in mid-2026.


Located in the heart of the vibrant hub of Belmont, Bel-Air was designed to meet a clear need in the market for high-quality, affordable new apartments. Buyers responded to that vision, drawn to the quality of the homes and the location’s proximity to key amenities like the Perth CBD, airport and Swan River.


The $113 million project will comprise 194 one- and two-bedroom apartments across 9 levels, a ground-floor commercial zone, and a range of resort-style facilities purpose-built to enhance the lifestyles of Bel-Air residents.


“The successful conclusion of our marketing campaign for Bel-Air, the first time in over ten years that Finbar has sold out a project prior to construction completion, represents the current strong demand for mid-tier apartment product in Western Australia from both owner-occupiers and increasingly investors.” - Finbar CEO,


[Ronald Chan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ronald-chan-00973a38/)


A huge thank you to our builder, Hanssen Construction, Chang Architects, and every buyer who made this milestone possible.


We look forward to delivering Bel-Air in 2026 and welcoming its new residents home.
