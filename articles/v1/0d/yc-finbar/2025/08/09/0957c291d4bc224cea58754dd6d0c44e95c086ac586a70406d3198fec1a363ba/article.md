# The Most Exclusive Address in Perth: An Inside Look at Civic Heart's $9.5 Million Penthouse

**Inside an opulent penthouse above the clouds — complete with panoramic river views, custom joinery, and one seriously dreamy kitchen.**


Set high above South Perth’s leafy streets, with 360-degree views stretching across the Swan River, city skyline and Kings Park, this breathtaking penthouse is the crown jewel of Civic Heart, Perth’s most iconic new residential address.


Spanning a generous 400m² of indoor–outdoor living, this home at the top of Perth's tallest residential building doesn’t just promise luxury; it delivers it with warmth, considered design, and a distinctly Western Australian sense of space.


###


Every Detail, Elevated


From the moment you step through the keyless entry, the apartment feels both expansive and inviting. There’s a soft, quietness to the space, thanks to acoustic insulation and double glazing, that makes the spectacular views all the more serene.


The open-plan living and dining area is anchored by a sculptural gas fireplace and flows effortlessly out to a generous terrace, complete with a built-in BBQ, sink, and bar fridge. From here, you can watch the sun melt behind Kings Park or the city lights shimmer across the water, wine in hand.


###


A Kitchen That’s Truly a Showpiece


The kitchen is the beating heart of the home. Designed for serious entertainers and weekend grazers alike, it features Gaggenau appliances, dual ovens, both gas and induction cooking, and 50mm stone benchtops that feel as substantial as they look.


A hidden scullery adds a second sink and dishwasher, keeping things tidy during long dinners with friends or quiet nights in.


There’s even a pair of Vintec wine fridges and an integrated Liebherr fridge/freezer, thoughtful touches that speak to the home’s balance of form and function.


###


Space to Live, and Breathe


The penthouse offers three oversized bedrooms, each with a private ensuite and walk-in robe. The master suite feels more like a boutique hotel: think freestanding bath, twin rain showers, high-tech bidet toilet (yes, really), and a walk-in robe worthy of a fashion editor.


A separate study offers flexibility to create a fourth bedroom, home office or personal studio, a welcome touch for those working or creating from home.


And tucked behind the main hallway is a spacious laundry and enclosed drying room, ensuring practicality never gets sacrificed for beauty.


###


Design Meets Daily Ritual


This home is filled with the quiet luxuries that make everyday life feel like a retreat: soaring 3-metre ceilings, rich engineered timber floors, Clipsal C-Bus system for smart home automation, and full-height windows that dissolve the boundary between inside and out.


You’ll also find a statement powder room for guests, ample storage, and access to a rare 65m² private garage, big enough for three cars.


###


Life at Civic Heart


Living at Civic Heart means more than just a beautiful apartment; it’s an entire lifestyle precinct.


Residents enjoy world-class amenities, including a 25m heated pool, multiple gyms, massage rooms, a library, games room, theatres, guest suites for when family or friends come to stay, and so much more.


There’s also a rooftop Sky Deck on Level 37 with private dining rooms and panoramic views, available to all residents.


Outside, landscaped laneways and piazzas spill into the surrounding neighbourhood, connecting the building with nearby cafés, the South Perth foreshore, and the ferry to the city.


This penthouse at Civic Heart isn’t just a home, it’s a place of intention and imagination; a rare opportunity to own the highest private residence in Perth.
