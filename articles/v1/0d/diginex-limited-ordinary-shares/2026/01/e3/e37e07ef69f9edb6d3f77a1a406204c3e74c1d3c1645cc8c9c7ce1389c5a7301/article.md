### **Expanding Diginex’s Ecosystem of Companies and Products**


This year represented a turning point in Diginex’s evolution.


On 5 March 2025, Diginex Limited (NASDAQ: DGNX) rang the Nasdaq Closing Bell, marking a significant milestone following our successful listing in January 2025.


Over the course of the year, Diginex advanced its strategy to identify high-potential opportunities in the RegTech and data intelligence sectors. In October 2025, we completed the acquisition of **Matter** , a Copenhagen-based ESG company provides granular sustainability data, portfolio-level analytics, and flexible API integrations used by major financial platforms such as Nasdaq eVestment.


In December, Diginex announced the acquisition of **The Remedy Project** \[[Corporate Accountability Strategies | Responsible Business Growth](https://www.remedyproject.co/) \], a specialist in human-rights risk, grievance handling, and supply-chain remediation. This collaboration combines Diginex’s AI-powered platforms with Remedy’s expertise in operational-level grievance mechanisms, forced-labor remediation frameworks, and access-to-remedy programs.


In December, we also signed a definitive agreement to acquire **Plan A** \[[Carbon Accounting Software by Plan A](https://plana.earth/) \], one of Europe’s leading AI-powered carbon accounting and decarbonization platform. It is our ambition of delivering an integrated, end-to-end ESG and carbon management solution for our customers.


What began as a focused ESG and RegTech company is rapidly evolving into a broader, AI-enabled platform spanning sustainability/ESG and carbon data management and, human-rights risk and supply-chain compliance.
