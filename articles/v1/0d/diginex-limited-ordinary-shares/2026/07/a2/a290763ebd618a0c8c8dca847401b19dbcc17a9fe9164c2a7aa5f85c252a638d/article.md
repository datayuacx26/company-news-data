## **What is ESG Consulting?**


**Environmental, social, and governance (ESG)** cover the strategies and models that companies use to align their operations with the growing demand for sustainability. The interest by companies has risen over the years, ushering in a series of positive impacts not just for the respective companies but also for their partners, communities, and the entire planet. About 89% of business executives indicate that adopting ESG helps them win a competitive advantage in the market.


Operating sustainably can also help your company to cut down the cost of production and get more support from investors. But even with these notable benefits, many companies struggle with ESG policy formulation and reporting. As a result, concerns have been raised about the rising **problem of greenwashing** .


**Environmental, social and governance (ESG) consulting for corporate clients** comes as a reliable solution for guiding companies in planning and correctly implementing ESG strategies. The agencies are run by professionals who understand the process of ESG implementation and help companies to maximise the benefits that come from making their operations sustainable.


## **Roles of ESG Consulting for Corporate Clients**


So, what can ESG consultants do for your company or corporate clients? Below are some of the main responsibilities and duties of **ESG consultants** and how they add value to your enterprise:


- **Preparing ESG Policy**


For a company to operate sustainably, it needs to have a clear **ESG policy** . This is a clearly defined outline to guide a company’s sustainability efforts. An agency can help your company to review its operations and craft the best guidelines to ensure all the goals for sustainability are achieved. For example, the policy might set guidelines for zero-waste and zero-carbon emissions from all the units of the company or business.


- **Performing ESG Risk Analysis**


**ESG consultants** can help advise you on the risk issues and how they should be addressed. In many cases, managers only focus on the positive impacts of ESG, such as cutting down emissions to address climate change, but this does not give the true outlook of the company to stakeholders. **ESG consulting for corporate clients** helps to change this by ensuring that all impacts, including the negative ones, are highlighted.


- **ESG Reporting**


One of the crucial tasks of an ESG process is communication to stakeholders. Because **ESG reporting** is a process that involves a lot of planning, data gathering, and analysis, most companies find it pretty challenging. An ESG consultant can help you to understand the entire process of disclosure, from company assessment and materiality assessment to publishing of the **ESG report** . Next, they will work with your corporate team to craft a good strategy for ESG reporting and develop management insights for reducing sustainability risks.


A good ESG consultancy can also come in handy in helping you to optimise the benefits of your ESG reports. For example, you can use the **ESG report and ESG rating** to drive the marketing of your company. This might be all you need to win a bigger market and profits.
