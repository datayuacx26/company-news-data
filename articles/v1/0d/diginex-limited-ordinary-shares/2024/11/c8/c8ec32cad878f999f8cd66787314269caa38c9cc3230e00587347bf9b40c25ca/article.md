# HSBC Sustainable Business Accelerator Program x diginex

**Driving Real-World Impact: diginex’s Evolved Approach to the Sustainable Business Accelerator Program with HSBC**


During the first HSBC Sustainable Business Accelerator Program, we realized that while our program was effective, the experience was theoretical. This inspired a program redesign to make our offerings more hands-on and impactful. Diginex continued the partnership with HSBC, bringing this new, dynamic approach to HSBC’s Commercial Banking clients from diverse sectors like manufacturing, retail, and technology.


****


**Enhancing Learning with hands-on Workshops**


To make the experience more practical, we introduced interactive workshops. These sessions allow participants to engage with one another, gain live demos of the diginexESG platform, and tackle real-life case studies. This blend of theoretical learning with practical exercises helps companies apply sustainability concepts directly to their own materiality assessments, carbon data, and ESG strategies.


****


**Key Benefits of the Program**


The program offers a comprehensive structure, blending modules, personalized one-on-one sessions, and workshops. This format supports participants in both understanding and practically applying ESG principles. The focus is on setting sustainability goals, KPIs, and materiality assessments, all aligned with each participant's business objectives.


Through our partnership with HSBC, diginex is expanding its reach to support even more companies on their sustainability journeys. Clients value the opportunity to learn from other organizations, gain fresh insights, and share best practices across industries.
