## What should you test before trading a crypto algo?


The biggest mistake is choosing a platform based only on features.


The more important question is whether the strategy works for you under realistic conditions.


Before going live, test:


-


Historical performance across different market conditions.


-


Maximum drawdown, not just total returns.


-


Win rate and average profit or loss per trade.


-


Entry and exit logic.


-


Stop loss and other risk controls.


-


[Slippage](https://algotest.in/blog/what-is-the-impact-of-slippage-on-an-algo) and execution behaviour.


-


Required capital and margin.


-


Performance during sharp market moves.


-


Forward or paper trading results using live market data.


This is where[backtesting](https://algotest.in/blog/guide-to-backtesting-trading-strategies) and forward testing become important.
