### What Bookmap offers

Liquidity heatmaps.

-

-

-


DOM.

### Bookmap vs GoCharting


Choose Bookmap if liquidity visualisation is your main priority.

Bookmap is more focused on global futures and US markets, so check NSE data support before switching.
