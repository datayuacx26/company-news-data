## What is BTST Trading?


BTST stands for Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow.


As the name suggests, you buy a stock today and sell it on the next trading day. The goal is to benefit from overnight price movements without holding the stock for several days or weeks.


BTST trading is popular among traders who want to capture short term momentum without trading intraday.


For example,


You buy 100 shares of XYZ Ltd. at ₹500 just before the market closes.


The next morning, the stock opens at ₹520 after positive market news.


You sell your shares at ₹520.


Your profit is ₹20 per share, or ₹2,000 before charges.


This simple approach is why many traders use BTST during strong market trends.


## How Does BTST Trading Work?


BTST trading follows a simple process.


1.


Identify a stock showing strong momentum before market close.


2.


Buy the stock near the end of the trading session.


3.


Hold the position overnight.


4.


Sell the stock the next trading day based on your target or exit plan.


Unlike intraday trading, you carry the position overnight. This means you can benefit from gap up openings, but you are also exposed to overnight risks.

## Benefits of BTST Trading


BTST trading offers several advantages when used correctly.

### Quick Profit Opportunities


You do not need to wait for weeks to book profits. Most BTST trades last only one trading day.


### Less Screen Time


Unlike[intraday trading](https://algotest.in/blog/best-intraday-trading-strategies-rules-tips) , you do not have to monitor charts throughout the day.


### Lower Capital Commitment


Your money is not locked for a long period because trades usually close the next day.


## Risks of BTST Trading


Every trading strategy has risks, and BTST is no different. Since you hold your position overnight, it's important to understand what could go wrong before placing a trade.


### 1. Overnight Risk


Unexpected news, company announcements, or global events can move the market before it opens the next day. Even if your setup looks strong, overnight developments can quickly change market sentiment.


### 2. Gap Down Openings


A stock can open much lower than your buying price due to negative news or weak market conditions. This may result in losses before you even get a chance to exit the trade.


### 3. High Volatility


Stocks suitable for BTST often move quickly, which creates both opportunities and risks. Sharp price swings can hit your stop-loss or reduce profits if the trade moves against you.


### 4. Short Delivery


In some cases, settlement issues can lead to short delivery, where the shares are not delivered on time. While this doesn't happen often, it's a good idea to understand your broker's BTST policy and settlement rules before trading.


## Best BTST Trading Setups


Choosing the right setup is more important than simply buying any stock before the market closes.


Here are some of the most reliable BTST trading setups.


### 1. Breakout Setup


This is one of the most popular BTST strategies.


Look for stocks that close above an important resistance level with strong buying volume.


The breakout often attracts more buyers on the next trading day.


What to look for:


-


Price closes above resistance.


-


Strong bullish candle.


-


Higher than average volume.


-


Positive market sentiment.


### 2. High Volume Breakout Setup


Volume confirms whether a breakout is genuine.


If a stock breaks resistance with significantly higher trading volume, it usually indicates strong institutional participation.


This setup often provides better follow through the next day.


### 3. Moving Average Pullback Setup


This setup works well in trending markets.


Look for stocks trading above the 20[EMA](https://algotest.in/blog/automate-ema-indicator-without-coding) or 50 EMA. Wait for a small pullback during the day and enter when the stock recovers before market close.


This allows you to trade in the direction of the existing trend.


### 4. Gap Continuation Setup


Some stocks gap up because of earnings, company announcements, or positive sector news.


If buying continues throughout the day and the stock closes near its high, the momentum may continue the next day.


### 5. Momentum Setup


Strong stocks often remain strong.


Look for stocks making fresh highs with consistent buying throughout the session.


These stocks usually have better chances of continuing their move the following day.

## Profit Booking Strategies for BTST Trading


Entering a trade is only half the job. Knowing when to exit is equally important.


### 1. Set Realistic Profit Targets


Avoid waiting for unrealistic gains.


Many BTST traders aim for small but consistent returns based on the stock's volatility.


### 2. Use a Trailing Stop Loss


A[trailing stop loss](https://algotest.in/blog/trail-stop-loss-to-break-even-price-when-backtesting) helps lock in profits while allowing the trade to continue if the stock keeps moving higher.


### 3. Move Your Stop Loss to Break Even


Once your trade moves in your favor, shift your stop loss to your entry price.


This protects your capital if the market suddenly reverses.


### 4. Book Partial Profits


If the stock reaches your target quickly, consider booking part of your position while letting the remaining shares run with a trailing stop loss.


### 5. Follow the Trend


If momentum starts weakening or selling pressure increases, do not hesitate to exit.


Protecting your profits is often better than waiting for a larger move.
