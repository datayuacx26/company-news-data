## 1. Options Made Easy


**Author:** Guy Cohen


**Why read it?**
If you're completely new to options trading, this is one of the easiest books to start with. It explains calls, puts, premiums, and basic strategies in simple language without assuming prior knowledge.


**Best for:** Beginners and traders moving from equity investing to F&O.


**Level:** ⭐ Beginner


## 2. Options as a Strategic Investment


**Author:** Lawrence G. McMillan


**Why read it?**
Considered one of the most comprehensive books on options trading, it covers everything from covered calls to advanced spreads, along with the risk management behind each strategy.


**Best for:** Traders who know the basics and want a complete reference guide.


**Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Intermediate to Advanced

## 3. Option Volatility & Pricing


**Author:** Sheldon Natenberg


**Why read it?**
A classic for understanding implied volatility, option pricing, and the Greeks. It explains *why* option prices move, not just how to trade them.


**Best for:** Experienced options traders looking to deepen their understanding of volatility.


**Level:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Advanced


Related:[Implied Volatility Explained: How to Read and Compute IV](https://algotest.in/blog/implied-volatility-and-how-to-read-and-compute-iv/)


## 4. Trading Options Greeks


**Author:** Dan Passarelli


**Why read it?**
Instead of simply defining Delta, Theta, Vega, and Gamma, this book shows how to use them to make better trading decisions in live markets.


**Best for:** Intermediate traders ready to apply the Greeks to trade management.


**Level:** ⭐⭐ Intermediate


**Practice it on AlgoTest:**
Track live Greeks on Nifty and Bank Nifty option chains inside[AlgoTest](https://algotest.in/) and see how these concepts play out in real market conditions.


## 5. How to Make Money in Derivatives


**Author:** Ashwani Gujral


**Why read it?**
One of the few books written specifically for Indian traders, using NSE examples to explain futures, options, and technical analysis.


**Best for:** Traders who want an India-focused introduction to derivatives.


**Level:** ⭐ Beginner to Intermediate


Related:[Options Trading: A Comprehensive Guide with Algorithmic Strategies](https://algotest.in/blog/options-trading-a-comprehensive-guide-with-algorithmic-strategies/)


## 6. Everything About Trading Options


**Author:** Prashant Shah


**Why read it?**
A practical guide to reading option chains and open interest—two topics that many international books don't cover in depth.


**Best for:** Indian traders who want to make decisions using option chain analysis.


**Level:** ⭐⭐ Beginner to Intermediate


**Practice it on AlgoTest:**
Use[AlgoTest's Option Simulator](https://algotest.in/feature/simulator) to analyse open interest and test your market view before placing a trade.


## 7. Trading in the Zone


**Author:** Mark Douglas


**Why read it?**
Trading success isn't just about strategy—it's about discipline. This book focuses on psychology, helping traders overcome fear, greed, and emotional decision-making.


**Best for:** Traders who know the strategies but struggle with execution and consistency.


**Level:** ⭐ All Levels


Related Read:[ITM vs ATM vs OTM Options: How to Choose the Right Strike Price](https://algotest.in/blog/itm-atm-otm/)


## Bonus Pick for Algo and Quant Traders


**Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives, John Hull**


Hull's book is the academic standard for options pricing theory and shows up often in NISM and NSE Academy coursework.


It's denser than the other picks on this list, but for traders who eventually want to move from discretionary to systematic or algo trading, it builds the pricing intuition that supports that shift.
