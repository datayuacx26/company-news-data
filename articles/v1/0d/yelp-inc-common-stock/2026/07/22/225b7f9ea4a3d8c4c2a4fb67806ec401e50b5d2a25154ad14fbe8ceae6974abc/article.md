# Awesome Women in Engineering

## About Awesome Women in Engineering


AWE provides support for women engineers at Yelp. As an official Yelp Employee Resource Group, we are committed to organizing activities targeted towards professional growth for our members, helping them maximize their potential at Yelp and beyond.


AWE started in April 2013 as a small grassroots organization aimed at connecting women engineers at Yelp. It has since expanded into a large structured organization, offering many programs such as a mentorship ring, brown bag lunches to discuss the latest events in tech, public speaking workshops, self defense classes, book clubs, and many more!
