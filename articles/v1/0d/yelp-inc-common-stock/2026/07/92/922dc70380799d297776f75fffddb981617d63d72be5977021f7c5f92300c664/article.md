# Gen Z does their research and wants the receipts

From the[matcha craze to the growth of grandma hobbies](https://trends.yelp.com/2026-yelp-trend-forecast.html) , we’ve seen Gen Z driving a number of the biggest trends both online and off. As a generation projected to command[$12 trillion](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2025/unlock-the-unmatched-spending-potential-of-gen-z-what-brands-need-to-know/) in spending power by 2030, they’re also reshaping how purchases are made, whether it’s finding the hottest new restaurant or deciding on a new nail salon. Having grown up with the internet at their fingertips, they’re navigating an increasingly noisy digital landscape flooded with AI-generated content.


To learn more about how Gen Z is making purchasing decisions, Yelp partnered with Angus Reid Group on new consumer research. What we found: Gen Z does their homework. They dig deep into research from multiple sources, lean heavily on written reviews, and approach AI-generated summaries with a dose of skepticism. **At the core, Gen Z wants authentic, human content.**


We also spoke with Gen Z members of our[Yelp Elite Squad](http://yelp.com/elite) , the highly engaged locals who regularly contribute reviews, photos, and videos on Yelp, about how they find the hottest businesses in their communities and the information they need to know before they go.


**The myth of the impulsive Gen Z spender**


Gen Z has a reputation for spontaneous spending, but the data tells a different story. Across every category we surveyed, the overwhelming majority of Gen Z researches on at least one platform before making a purchase decision: restaurants (97%), beauty (94%), fitness and wellness (93%), home services (92%), and professional services (84%). Having grown up with endless information, Gen Z naturally cross-references, compares, and digs into the details before choosing where to spend.


Quynh-Nhi N., a Yelp Elite Squad member from Dallas, said, “I use TikTok just to watch the video, but I go over to Yelp to see a little bit more detail on what everyone else is saying about the new places.”


**Gen Z relies more on written reviews than influencers when deciding where to spend**


Yelp is one of several platforms Gen Z uses to find their new favorite business, as it has the detailed reviews they need to make an informed decision. The survey found Gen Z favors detailed written reviews over influencer recommendations for both dining out (78% vs. 59%) and service professionals (81% vs. 61%).


The survey also found that 77% of Gen Z respondents trust platforms with detailed written reviews over those with only star or numerical ratings when researching services, and 76% feel the same for restaurants. A star or point rating without review text can’t tell you where to park, what to eat, or if the professional arrived on time, and Gen Z needs “the receipts” from platforms like Yelp to prove that they should take a chance on a new business.


Our Gen Z Elites broke down why they appreciate Yelp’s authentic reviews and what makes a review useful to them:


- “I like the honest reviews. I want to hear… how long did you have to wait for something? Did you go back to this place again? Was this service the best service you ever had?” — Angelina E., Gen Z Yelp Elite, Orlando
- “I feel like people tend to explain more on Yelp. On Google, it’ll show when someone leaves a five star rating and like there’ll be no detail. I want to see words or pictures and more about the experience, not necessarily just individuals and their ratings. I am looking to pick a good place that’s going to fit the vibe for everyone.”— Miranda M., Gen Z Yelp Elite, Brooklyn


**Takeout and restaurants are top priorities for Gen Z**


For Gen Z, the perfect meal is a top priority. Our survey found that 78% reported spending money on takeout or delivery and 72% reported dining out in person in the past month. Gen Z is also spending more on food than they were a year ago, with 45% increasing their takeout or delivery spend and 45% spending more at restaurants. When a little extra cash comes their way, like a bonus or a gift, the survey found that 74% said they’d spend it on dining out and 70% would put it toward takeout or delivery.


**Price is key for Gen Z, and likely getting more important as inflation creeps higher**


When looking for a new restaurant, our survey found the menu (89%) and price (87%) were the most influential factors in deciding where to eat. As they continue to navigate a[high-inflation environment](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/inflation-rises-to-a-3-year-high-on-spiking-gas-prices-highlighting-affordability-challenges) , Gen Z is likely to keep prioritizing budget over nice-to-have restaurant aspects like ambiance, which 73% said was influential when deciding where to eat.


Our Gen Z Elites highlighted how Yelp has helped them find the best dish for their budget:


- “A lot of hole-in-the-wall restaurants don’t have a website that has a menu. So I’ll go to Yelp and look into the photos; sometimes people will post a menu and that gives me a good idea. I’ll send it to my friend group and say, ‘Does this price point meet everyone’s expectations?’”— Miranda M., Gen Z Yelp Elite, Brooklyn
- “I really value when people on Yelp post when the happy hours are and what that menu looks like, because a lot of times I feel like I’m digging a little bit for those little details.” — Quynh-Nhi N., Gen Z Yelp Elite, Dallas


The same held true for deciding on a service pro like a hair stylist or pet groomer, with 85% saying price was influential in their decision-making. Beyond price, 81% said up-to-date business information and 80% said ease of booking were key factors in their decision-making, highlighting this generation’s expectation for instant information and seamless experiences at the touch of a button.


**Gen Z isn’t sold on AI**


While Gen Z may be using AI frequently, they’re not quick to trust the results. Our survey found only 48% of Gen Z trusts that AI-generated summaries for service pros are accurate and an even lower number (43%) trust that they are accurate for restaurants. This data aligns with[previous research](https://blog.yelp.com/news/americans-use-ai-but-dont-trust-it-thats-a-problem-worth-solving/) Yelp conducted with Morning Consult, which found Gen Z was the generation most likely to want proof of trusted sources when using AI search. Yelp’s approach to AI is powered by our authentic, in-depth community content, with hundreds of millions of reviews, photos, and detailed business information contributed by real people.


Whether Gen Z is hunting for the perfect coffee shop or booking a facial, one thing is clear: they need all the details from sources they can trust. On Yelp, Gen Z can read authentic reviews, compare their options, and book with confidence.


**Methodology**


*This survey was created by Yelp employees and distributed via email by Angus Reid Group to a sample of participants on their panel across the USA in Urban and Suburban areas from February 2, 2026 to February 19, 2026. Participants were incentivized with points provided to their Angus Reid Group member accounts. When we refer to “Gen Z” throughout this blog post, we are referring to a subset of this audience that we surveyed, where participants are 18-29 years old (n=1,323 unweighted, n=1,232 weighted). Rim weighting was applied based on region, generation, and gender.*
