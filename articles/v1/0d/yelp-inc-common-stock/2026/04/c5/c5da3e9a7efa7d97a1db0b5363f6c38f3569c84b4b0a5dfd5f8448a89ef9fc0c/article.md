# Search less and do more with the new AI-powered Yelp Assistant

Looking for photos of a specific dish? Now, you can use natural language when searching within a business’s media gallery, and we’ll surface the most relevant images—no need for perfect keywords. We expanded[Popular Offerings](https://blog.yelp.com/news/fall-product-release-2025/#popular-offerings) , which leverages LLMs to highlight the most frequently mentioned services, items, or experiences, to even more categories and across all platforms—giving you a snapshot of what each business is known for at a glance. Plus, in the coming weeks, AI-powered[before and after photos labels](https://blog.yelp.com/news/fall-product-release-2025/#popular-offerings:~:text=pros%20even%20easier.-,Before%20and%20after%20photos,-are%20now%20grouped) for services businesses will be available on iOS for more than 100 visually-driven services, including home, auto, beauty, pet, and real estate categories. These labels help you quickly evaluate a pro’s work to make hiring decisions.


Additionally, you can now upload longer, 90-second videos to business pages or your reviews, letting you share richer, more dynamic experiences. Plus, our new passwordless login streamlines the review writing process, so whether you’re a longtime Yelp Elite or just leaving your first review, contributing valuable feedback is easier than ever.
