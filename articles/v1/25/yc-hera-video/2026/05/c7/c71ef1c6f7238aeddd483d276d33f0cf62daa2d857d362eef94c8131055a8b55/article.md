## A practical storyboard


Use this sequence as a starting point, then tighten the timing around the one action you want the viewer to take.


- 0-2s: Open with the specific before-state, such as scattered feedback, manual reporting, or a blank campaign calendar.
- 2-6s: Reveal the product name and the one job it performs better than the old workflow.
- 6-12s: Animate three UI or benefit beats, each with a short label and one visual proof point.
- 12-18s: Show the transformation, such as a clean dashboard, completed video, or approved launch asset.
- 18-22s: End with a direct CTA and a product URL or waitlist action.

## Channel cutdown plan


- Homepage: 16:9, 30 to 45 seconds, focused on the full product launch videos story.
- LinkedIn: 1:1 or 4:5, 20 to 30 seconds, silent-first with a strong first frame.
- Reels, Shorts, and TikTok: 9:16, 12 to 20 seconds, one hook and one proof point.
- Email or sales follow-up: 15 to 30 seconds, direct CTA and minimal animation noise.


## Production checklist


- Write the launch promise before designing any scene.
- Keep each on-screen line under nine words so it works on mobile.
- Use one motion language for all callouts, such as slide, scale, or count-up.
- Create 16:9 first, then adapt to 1:1 and 9:16 for launch channels.
- Export a silent version with readable captions for autoplay feeds.


## Quality bar before publishing


- The first frame explains the topic without audio.
- Every text element is readable on a phone screenshot.
- Each motion beat has a job: reveal, compare, emphasize, transition, or close.
- The final frame tells the viewer what to do next.
- The video still makes sense if exported as a silent autoplay asset.


## What to measure


- CTA click-through rate from the video frame.
- Scroll depth or watch time on the landing page.
- Qualified signups, demo requests, or waitlist joins.
- Reuse rate across launch channels.


## Common mistakes to avoid


- Opening with a logo animation before the viewer understands the problem.
- Listing every feature instead of sequencing the three strongest proof points.
- Using cinematic stock-style AI video when the product needs sharp UI and text clarity.

## FAQ


### How long should an AI product launch video be?


For a landing page or Product Hunt launch, 20 to 45 seconds is usually enough. Paid ads and social teasers can be shorter, while a full homepage explainer can run closer to 60 seconds.


### Can I create a product launch video without footage?


Yes. Motion graphics can carry the launch with product screenshots, interface cards, charts, icons, animated text, and brand visuals.


### What should the first scene show?


Show the customer problem, not the company logo. A clear problem frame gives the rest of the launch video a reason to exist.
