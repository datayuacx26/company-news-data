# AI Brand Video Generator for Startups: Build a Reusable Motion System

## The short answer


A startup brand video is strongest when it defines reusable motion rules: type, color, transitions, pacing, and CTA treatment.


The searcher wants more than a one-off brand video. They need a motion system that can be reused without hiring a motion designer for every asset.

## Who this workflow is for


This is for startup teams that need a consistent motion identity across launches, social videos, webinars, product demos, and investor updates.


## What to prepare before generating


- A specific product launch videos goal tied to agencies.
- One sentence that names the viewer, the problem, and the promised outcome.
- Any source assets: screenshots, charts, brand colors, logo files, fonts, or data points.
- The target channel and aspect ratio before you write the prompt.
- A final CTA that matches the viewer's intent.


## When to use this motion format


- Your team creates frequent public-facing assets.
- Your visual brand exists in static form but not motion.
- You want all launch videos to feel connected.


## A practical storyboard


Use this sequence as a starting point, then tighten the timing around the one action you want the viewer to take.


- 0-3s: Open with brand symbol, category, or product promise.
- 3-10s: Show three motion principles through product scenes.
- 10-18s: Demonstrate color, type, and transition behavior.
- 18-25s: Apply the system to a real use case.
- 25-30s: End with a reusable brand lockup.


## Prompt recipe to start in Hera


> Create a 30 second startup brand motion system video. Use \[brand colors\], \[font style\], logo placeholder, product UI cards, reusable transitions, and a final lockup. The style should feel modern, confident, and product-led.


A good first prompt should name the audience, product category, visual style, aspect ratio, duration, brand colors, and the one message that cannot be missed. After the first generation, refine timing, hierarchy, labels, and transitions in smaller prompts instead of asking the model to solve everything at once.

## Channel cutdown plan


- Homepage: 16:9, 30 to 45 seconds, focused on the full product launch videos story.
- LinkedIn: 1:1 or 4:5, 20 to 30 seconds, silent-first with a strong first frame.
- Reels, Shorts, and TikTok: 9:16, 12 to 20 seconds, one hook and one proof point.
- Email or sales follow-up: 15 to 30 seconds, direct CTA and minimal animation noise.


## Production checklist


- Define two or three motion principles before generating.
- Use brand colors intentionally, not on every element.
- Create reusable intro, lower third, proof card, and CTA scenes.
- Document timing and easing choices for future assets.
- Make sure the system works on light and dark backgrounds.


## Quality bar before publishing


- The first frame explains the topic without audio.
- Every text element is readable on a phone screenshot.
- Each motion beat has a job: reveal, compare, emphasize, transition, or close.
- The final frame tells the viewer what to do next.
- The video still makes sense if exported as a silent autoplay asset.


## What to measure


- CTA click-through rate from the video frame.
- Scroll depth or watch time on the landing page.
- Qualified signups, demo requests, or waitlist joins.
- Reuse rate across launch channels.


## Common mistakes to avoid


- Creating one beautiful brand video with no reusable rules.
- Overusing the logo instead of building product recognition.
- Letting the AI choose random colors and typography each time.

## FAQ


### What is a motion brand system?


It is a set of reusable rules for how your brand moves: transitions, type animation, logo behavior, colors, timing, and layout.


### Do startups need brand videos?


Not always, but teams that publish often benefit from a consistent motion system.


### Can AI follow brand guidelines?


It can if you provide colors, fonts, logo assets, examples, and specific rules in the prompt.
