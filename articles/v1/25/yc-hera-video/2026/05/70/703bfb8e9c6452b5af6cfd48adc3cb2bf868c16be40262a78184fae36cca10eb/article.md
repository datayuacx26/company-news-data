# Event Promo Videos With AI Motion Graphics

## The short answer


An event promo video should make the date, audience, value, and registration action impossible to miss.


The searcher wants event assets that look professional and can be updated quickly as speakers, dates, sponsors, or agenda details change.

## Who this workflow is for


This is for event marketers, conference organizers, community teams, and founders promoting webinars, launches, summits, and live events.


## What to prepare before generating


- A specific product launch videos goal tied to startup launch teams.
- One sentence that names the viewer, the problem, and the promised outcome.
- Any source assets: screenshots, charts, brand colors, logo files, fonts, or data points.
- The target channel and aspect ratio before you write the prompt.
- A final CTA that matches the viewer's intent.


## When to use this motion format


- You need speaker announcements, countdowns, agenda teasers, or registration reminders.
- The event has multiple channels and formats.
- Details may change and the creative needs to stay editable.


## A practical storyboard


Use this sequence as a starting point, then tighten the timing around the one action you want the viewer to take.


- 0-2s: Show the event name and date.
- 2-7s: Name the audience and reason to attend.
- 7-14s: Animate speakers, sessions, or topics.
- 14-20s: Add location, format, sponsor, or time details.
- 20-24s: End with registration CTA and URL.


## Prompt recipe to start in Hera


> Create a 24 second event promo motion graphics video for \[event\]. Include event name, date, audience, three agenda highlights, speaker card placeholders, sponsor area, and a final register CTA. Style: premium conference design.


A good first prompt should name the audience, product category, visual style, aspect ratio, duration, brand colors, and the one message that cannot be missed. After the first generation, refine timing, hierarchy, labels, and transitions in smaller prompts instead of asking the model to solve everything at once.

## Follow-up prompts that improve the first draft


- Make the first 3 seconds more specific to this is for event marketers, conference organizers, community teams, and founders promoting webinars, launches, summits, and live events.
- Reduce on-screen text by 30 percent and keep every line readable on mobile.
- Make the CTA frame work as a static thumbnail.
- Create a second version with slower pacing and more whitespace.


## Channel cutdown plan


- Homepage: 16:9, 30 to 45 seconds, focused on the full product launch videos story.
- LinkedIn: 1:1 or 4:5, 20 to 30 seconds, silent-first with a strong first frame.
- Reels, Shorts, and TikTok: 9:16, 12 to 20 seconds, one hook and one proof point.
- Email or sales follow-up: 15 to 30 seconds, direct CTA and minimal animation noise.


## Production checklist


- Keep date and time visible in more than one frame.
- Create speaker-card templates for repeat announcements.
- Make a countdown variant for the final week.
- Use brand-safe sponsor placement.
- Export square, vertical, and presentation-screen formats.


## Quality bar before publishing


- The first frame explains the topic without audio.
- Every text element is readable on a phone screenshot.
- Each motion beat has a job: reveal, compare, emphasize, transition, or close.
- The final frame tells the viewer what to do next.
- The video still makes sense if exported as a silent autoplay asset.


## What to measure


- CTA click-through rate from the video frame.
- Scroll depth or watch time on the landing page.
- Qualified signups, demo requests, or waitlist joins.
- Reuse rate across launch channels.


## Common mistakes to avoid


- Making a beautiful teaser that hides the date.
- Using tiny speaker titles or sponsor logos.
- Forgetting timezone or event format details.

## FAQ


### What should an event promo video include?


Include event name, date, audience, value, key sessions or speakers, and a clear registration CTA.


### How long should an event promo video be?


Most event promos work well between 15 and 30 seconds.


### Can I create speaker announcement videos with AI?


Yes. Use a reusable speaker card template with headshot, name, title, topic, and event branding.
