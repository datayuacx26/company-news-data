# SPS Hosts GoMN this Wednesday, May 18 at 6pm

This Wednesday, May 18, at 6:00 pm, the[GoMN Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/golangmn) is welcoming[John Murphy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/murphybytes/) who will present **Debugging Go Programs in Kubernetes** . A brief summary of the topic can be found[here](https://www.meetup.com/golangmn/events/285834006/) , where you can also register to attend!


The event is sponsored by[SPS Commerce](https://www.spscommerce.com/) , is BOTH in-person AND virtual, and everyone is welcome to[join](https://www.meetup.com/golangmn/events/284836111/) - this is a free event :).
