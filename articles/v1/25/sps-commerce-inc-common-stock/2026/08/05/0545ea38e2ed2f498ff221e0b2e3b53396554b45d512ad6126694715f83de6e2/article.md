# Amazon Grade and Resell: How It Works and When It Makes Sense

In this article, learn about:


-


What Amazon Grade and Resell is and why Amazon built it


-


How the inspection, grading, and relisting process works


-


Which products qualify, and which don't


---


Not every returned product needs to become a loss. Amazon's Grade and Resell program gives eligible third-party sellers a way to recover value from certain customer-returned inventory instead of having it disposed of or sent back to the seller. Rather than treating every return as unsellable, Amazon inspects eligible products, assigns a condition grade, and relists qualifying items at a discounted price.


For many sellers, the program is an alternative to disposal or liquidation that recovers a portion of a product's value while cutting down on unnecessary waste. The exact recovery amount depends on the item's condition, demand, and resale price, and Amazon manages the inspection, grading, listing, and fulfillment for qualifying inventory.


## Why Did Amazon Create Grade and Resell?


Customer returns are a normal part of ecommerce, but they carry real costs. When a product comes back to an Amazon fulfillment center, sellers typically have several options:


-


Return the inventory to themselves


-


Dispose of the product


-


Liquidate the inventory


-


Seek reimbursement when appropriate


-


Participate in Grade and Resell if the item qualifies


Grade and Resell was introduced to keep recoverable inventory from being discarded, giving sellers another way to offset the financial impact of returns. Instead of treating every opened package as unsellable, Amazon evaluates whether it can still provide value to another customer.


## How Does Amazon Grade and Resell Work?


The process starts after an eligible FBA item is returned by a customer. Amazon inspects the returned product to determine whether it meets the program's requirements. If it does, the item gets a condition grade and is listed for sale as a pre-owned product.


Throughout the process, Amazon manages:


-


Product inspection


-


Condition grading


-


Discounted pricing


-


Marketplace listing


-


Order fulfillment


-


Customer service


Sellers receive proceeds from the resale, less applicable program fees, without needing to inspect or relist the item themselves.


### How Amazon Grades Returned Products


Eligible products are inspected against Amazon's established condition standards before they're relisted.


**Condition grade**


**What it means**


Like New


Nearly identical to a new item. Packaging may have been opened, but the item shows little or no evidence of use.


Very Good


Limited use, with minor cosmetic imperfections that don't affect functionality.


Good


Fully functional but shows visible signs of previous use, such as light scratches or worn packaging.


Acceptable


Functional but may have more noticeable cosmetic wear or replacement packaging.


Products that fail Amazon's inspection standards aren't resold through the program. Instead, they follow the seller's selected inventory disposition preferences, such as removal or disposal.

### Which Products Are Eligible?


Not every return can participate in Grade and Resell. Eligibility depends on several factors, including:


-


Product category


-


Product condition


-


Safety requirements


-


Regulatory restrictions


-


Inventory eligibility


-


Marketplace availability


Certain products, including many consumables, personal care items, hazardous materials, and products with safety concerns, are generally excluded. Amazon periodically updates eligibility requirements, so sellers should review the latest program guidance within Seller Central.


## What Are the Benefits of Grade and Resell?


For many businesses, the biggest advantage is recovering value from inventory that might otherwise generate little or no return.


-


**Recover revenue:** Instead of absorbing a complete inventory loss, sellers may recover a portion of the product's value through resale.


-


**Reduce disposal costs:** Items that can be resold may avoid disposal fees while cutting down on unnecessary waste.


-


**Improve sustainability:** Extending the life of usable products supports circular commerce by keeping eligible items in the marketplace longer.


-


**Simplify operations:** Amazon handles the inspection, grading, listing, fulfillment, and customer service, reducing operational overhead for sellers.


## What Are the Limitations To Consider?


Grade and Resell isn't the best fit for every product. Sellers should consider that:


-


Resale prices are discounted


-


Not every returned item qualifies


-


Recovery values vary based on market demand


-


Certain categories remain ineligible


-


Amazon determines the final condition grade


For some products, liquidation or removal orders may provide a better financial outcome depending on inventory value and secondary market demand.

## What Are the Best Practices for Maximizing Recovery Value?


Businesses participating in Grade and Resell often see the best results when they focus on reducing preventable returns while maximizing recovery opportunities.


-


**Monitor return trends:** Recurring return reasons may reveal opportunities to improve listings, packaging, or product quality.


-


**Improve product detail pages:** Accurate descriptions, dimensions, images, and specifications help customers make informed purchasing decisions and can reduce avoidable returns.


-


**Review recovery metrics:** Use Amazon's Returns & Recovery reporting tools to understand how much inventory is successfully being recovered through resale.


-


**Evaluate recovery options regularly:** Grade and Resell is only one inventory recovery strategy. Compare recovery values against liquidation, removal orders, and reimbursements to determine the best outcome for each product.


## Is Grade and Resell Worth It?


For sellers with consistent return volume, Grade and Resell can be a practical way to recover value from inventory that might otherwise generate little financial return.


The program doesn't eliminate the costs associated with returns, but it does offer an additional recovery path that can support profitability, reduce waste, and simplify inventory management. Businesses that regularly review return trends and recovery performance are often better positioned to determine when Grade and Resell is the right choice.


## Frequently Asked Questions


### What happens to items that don't qualify for Grade and Resell?


Products that fail Amazon's inspection standards follow the seller's selected inventory disposition preferences, such as removal or disposal, rather than being relisted.


### Does Amazon charge a fee for Grade and Resell?


Sellers receive proceeds from the resale minus applicable program fees. Exact fee structures should be double-checked in Seller Central, since Amazon updates program terms periodically.


### How is Grade and Resell different from liquidation?


Grade and Resell inspects and grades individual items for resale directly to consumers on Amazon. Liquidation moves inventory in bulk to business-to-business pa artners, typically faster but at a lower per-unit recovery.
