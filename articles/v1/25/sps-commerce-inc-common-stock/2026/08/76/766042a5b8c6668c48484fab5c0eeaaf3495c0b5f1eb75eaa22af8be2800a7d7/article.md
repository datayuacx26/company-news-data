# Amazon Seller Fulfilled Prime (SFP): Requirements, Benefits and How to Qualify in 2026

In this article, learn about:


-


What Amazon Seller Fulfilled Prime is and who it fits


-


The prequalification and trial process required to enroll


-


The ongoing performance standards sellers must maintain to keep the Prime badge


---


Amazon[Seller Fulfilled Prime (SFP)](https://sell.amazon.com/programs/seller-fulfilled-prime) lets merchants display the Prime badge on products they ship themselves, instead of routing inventory through[Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA](https://sell.amazon.com/start/fba?ld=SEUSSOAGOOG-C100-D-INC&gad_campaignid=23897460875&gbraid=0AAAAACcYLw4oUSVXnw2QmZTSEJZnKhBu9&gclid=Cj0KCQjw-MDTBhCgARIsAKAkdlQY_BYEcsDnky2ZkEKmcgRTuJVAI9GVqQrK7qxvTC2fh56x46lhlnQaAsLBEALw_wcB) ). For sellers with mature warehouse operations, it's a way to earn Prime visibility without giving up control of inventory. But it comes with a real bar to clear, and an even higher bar to keep clearing.


## What Is Amazon Seller Fulfilled Prime?


Seller Fulfilled Prime allows merchants to earn the Prime badge on listings they fulfill from their own warehouse rather than through FBA. Inventory stays under the seller's control while Amazon customers still get the fast, free shipping they associate with Prime.


For many brands, SFP is a middle ground between full self-fulfillment, otherwise known as[Fulfilled by Merchant (FBM)](https://sell.amazon.com/programs/fulfilled-by-merchant) , and outsourcing inventory to Amazon's fulfillment centers.


Amazon reopened SFP enrollment to new sellers in late 2023 after redesigning the program with stricter performance standards aimed at protecting the Prime customer experience, and those standards have continued to evolve since.


## Who Should Use Seller Fulfilled Prime?


SFP tends to work best for sellers that:


-


Already run sophisticated warehouse operations


-


Can consistently meet one- and two-day delivery promises


-


Want to keep inventory in their own facilities


-


Sell oversized or specialty products


-


Have negotiated carrier contracts in place


-


Need more inventory flexibility than FBA allows


Smaller sellers without mature fulfillment operations are generally better served by FBA, since Amazon handles storage, picking, packing, shipping, returns, and customer service on their behalf.


## What Are the Seller Fulfilled Prime Requirements?


Qualifying for SFP happens in two phases: prequalification, then successfully completing the SFP trial. Amazon reviews both historical account health and real-world fulfillment performance before granting Prime eligibility.


### Have a Professional Seller Account


Sellers need:


-


An Amazon Professional selling account


-


A default domestic U.S. shipping address


-


Active self-fulfilled inventory


Individual seller accounts can't enroll in SFP.


### Meet Prequalification Metrics


Before the trial begins, Amazon evaluates the seller's recent fulfillment history against a set of minimum standards. Double-check these figures against current Seller Central policy before publishing, since Amazon revisits them periodically:

Meeting these thresholds shows Amazon that a seller already operates at a consistently high service level before Prime branding enters the picture.


### Complete the Seller Fulfilled Prime Trial


After prequalification, sellers enter a required trial that generally runs 30 days, or four full performance weeks depending on enrollment timing. During the trial, sellers must consistently meet Prime delivery expectations on real customer orders, which are evaluated against Prime service levels rather than standard FBM benchmarks.


Only after passing the trial does Amazon activate the Prime badge on eligible products.


## The Ongoing Performance Requirements


Enrollment is only the starting line. Amazon reviews SFP performance weekly and falling short of required metrics can mean Prime offers get disabled or the seller is removed from the program entirely.


### On-Time Shipping


Orders need to ship when promised. Late shipments affect Prime eligibility quickly, since Prime customers expect predictable delivery windows.


### On-Time Delivery Rate (OTDR)


Amazon has put growing weight on whether orders actually arrive by the promised date. As of 2026, sellers generally need to maintain at least a 90% On-Time Delivery Rate. Amazon has also moved toward more targeted enforcement, meaning a poor-performing listing may be deactivated individually rather than disabling every seller-fulfilled listing at once.


### Valid Tracking


Tracking information needs to be uploaded promptly and stay valid. Amazon expects over 95% valid tracking for qualifying shipments.


### Delivery Speed


SFP exists because of fast shipping. Amazon evaluates how often customers viewing Prime listings actually receive same-day (where available), one-day, or two-day delivery, with expectations that vary by product size tier: standard-size, oversize, and extra-large.


## What Are the Shipping Requirements?


Amazon gives sellers flexibility in how they fulfill orders, but not in whether they meet customer expectations. Sellers may:


-


Ship from their own warehouse


-


Use a 3PL provider


-


Operate multiple warehouse locations


-


Use Amazon-integrated shipping carriers


Many sellers also lean on tools like Amazon Buy Shipping and Shipping Settings Automation to improve delivery accuracy, which can also provide protection for certain on-time delivery metrics when used correctly.


## How Does Prime Order Volume Management Work?


One feature many sellers overlook is Prime order volume management. Rather than accepting unlimited Prime orders, Amazon lets sellers set daily capacity limits, which helps warehouses avoid taking on more same-day, one-day, or two-day orders than they can realistically fulfill. The goal is to protect both operational performance and customer satisfaction.


## What Are the Customer Service Responsibilities?


One advantage of SFP is that Amazon handles much of the post-purchase customer experience, including returns, refunds, customer inquiries after purchase, and return authorizations.


Sellers stay responsible for inventory accuracy, fulfillment speed, and shipping performance.


## Why Do Sellers Lose Seller Fulfilled Prime Status?


Common reasons sellers get disenrolled include:


-


Late shipments


-


Declining on-time delivery rates


-


High cancellation rates


-


Inconsistent shipping volume


-


Failure to meet Prime delivery promises


Amazon also monitors whether enrolled sellers consistently fulfill Prime orders throughout the month. Sellers with very low monthly SFP shipping volume may see their daily Prime order limits reduced until they demonstrate sustained performance.


## Is Seller Fulfilled Prime Worth It?


For the right business, SFP offers real advantages: Prime badge visibility, higher conversion rates, stronger Featured Offer (Buy Box) competitiveness, inventory control, custom packaging opportunities, and less dependence on Amazon's warehouse capacity.


The tradeoff is operational load. Maintaining Prime-level service takes disciplined warehouse processes, reliable carrier performance, accurate inventory management, and continuous monitoring of fulfillment metrics. Businesses without mature logistics operations may still find FBA the easier path to the Prime badge.


## Frequently Asked Questions Regarding Amazon SFP


### Can any seller enroll in Seller Fulfilled Prime?


No. Enrollment requires a Professional selling account, a default U.S. shipping address, and a recent fulfillment history that clears Amazon's prequalification thresholds before the trial can begin.


### How long is the Seller Fulfilled Prime trial?


The trial generally runs 30 days, or four full performance weeks depending on when a seller enrolls and requires meeting Prime-level delivery performance on real customer orders throughout that window.


### What happens if a seller falls below the performance requirements after enrolling?


Amazon reviews performance weekly. Falling short can lead to Prime offers being disabled on specific listings or, for sustained issues, removal from the program.
