Lidl has overtaken Morrisons to become Britain's 5th largest supermarket. For 35 consecutive months, Lidl GB has held the title of Britain's fastest-growing bricks-and-mortar retailer.

## Why Trading with Lidl Is Different


Lidl operates a lean, efficient retail model, and every part of the supplier relationship is built to support it. Traditional grocery accounts give suppliers room for incremental process improvement over time. Lidl expects operational readiness from day one.


The buying team evaluates suppliers across 4 areas: supply reliability, logistics capability, product quality, and price competitiveness. Each carries equal weight, and a gap in any one of them can stall or end the commercial relationship.


That lean model also shapes what a listing is worth. Lidl stores carry a limited, curated range compared to a traditional supermarket, which makes getting on the range more competitive. Suppliers who do secure a listing tend to see higher volume per SKU as a result.


## What Lidl Requires From Suppliers


### Logistics


Deliveries must arrive on Euro pallets (1,200mm x 800mm) or half pallets (600mm x 800mm), using 1 of 3 approved pallet types: Chep, LPR, or standard stamped Euro pallets. Shrink-wrapped outer cases are not accepted. Products must be shelf-ready and fully shoppable on arrival at the distribution centre.


All warehouse deliveries are booked through Lidl's online **TSM** (Time Slot Management) system, which suppliers are expected to use consistently.


### Quality


Lidl requires a minimum of 75% remaining shelf life at the point of manufacture. Unannounced audits are part of the relationship from the start, and biannual testing continues throughout the listing period. Suppliers are expected to adhere to UK government salt, sugar, and calorie reduction programmes.


### Listing Types


There are 3 routes to getting your products on Lidl shelves:


1.


**Permanent listings** are year-round placements decided at the annual range review. These require ongoing price commitments and shelf-ready, mixed-case packaging.


2.


**Seasonal listings** cover key trading periods, Christmas, Easter, summer, and winter, and suit products with strong seasonal relevance.


3.


**Food Specials** are one-week promotional placements. Suppliers engage with the Food Specials team 10 to 13 weeks in advance and confirm they can fulfil the agreed volume in full 1 to 2 weeks before the promotion goes live.


## EDI and Compliance


Lidl uses EDIFACT for electronic document exchange, transmitted via a Value Added Network (VAN). The 4 core messages suppliers need to support are:


Message


Purpose


ORDERS


Purchase orders sent from Lidl to you


ORDRSP


Your confirmation response to each order


DESADV


Dispatch advice sent when goods leave your site


INVOIC


Invoice submission


EDI invoicing is not formally mandated, but it is Lidl's preferred approach. Suppliers without it in place before their 1st transaction carry significantly more manual overhead, and retrofitting EDI mid-relationship is harder than getting it right at the start. Having it ready before onboarding begins puts you in a stronger position from day one.
