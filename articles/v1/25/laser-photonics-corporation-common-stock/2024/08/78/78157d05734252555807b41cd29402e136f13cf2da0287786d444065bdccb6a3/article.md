# Laser Photonics to Showcase Its Next-Generation Laser Solutions at FABTECH 2024

**ORLANDO, Fla., Aug. 20, 2024** –[Laser Photonics Corporation](http://laserphotonics.com/) (LPC) (NASDAQ:[LASE](https://invest.laserphotonics.com/) ), a leading global industrial developer of laser systems for laser cleaning and other material processing applications, announces its participation in FABTECH 2024, set to take place from October 15 to 17 at Orlando’s Orange County Convention Center.


FABTECH is North America’s largest metal forming, fabrication, welding and finishing event. Florida is now[ranked](https://www.floridajobs.org/docs/default-source/communicationsfiles/2023-florida-manufacturing-report.pdf) the 10th largest state for manufacturing employment – making it an ideal location for FABTECH 2024. The event promises to attract not only attendees from Florida’s flourishing manufacturing industry but also participants from around the globe, offering exhibitors access to a diverse and expansive audience.


“We’re looking forward to showcasing our innovative laser technologies at FABTECH 2024,” said Wayne Tupuola, CEO of Laser Photonics. “Our booth will feature live demonstrations of our advanced laser systems, illustrating how our technology can enhance productivity and efficiency in manufacturing operations. In today’s competitive market, embracing cutting-edge technology is crucial for staying ahead, and FABTECH is an excellent platform for presenting and discovering such innovations.”


With over 800,000 square feet of exhibits, FABTECH is the only event that makes it possible to see the latest metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing equipment in one place, where equipment innovations can be explored by application and industry. It offers industry decision-makers a space for connection, inspiration and discovery of technologies that are changing the future of every type of manufacturing.


At FABTECH, LPC will showcase two of its industrial-grade semi-automated systems for roughening, conditioning and finishing: the[CleanTech Robotic Cell (CTIR-6040)](http://laserphotonics.com/semi-automatic-laser-systems/robotic-cell-enclosure) and the stand-alone[CleanTech MegaCenter (CTIR-5040)](http://laserphotonics.com/laser-cleaning/cleantech-megacenter) , each with IOT capabilities and Generation 4 compact scan heads. LPC will also feature its[MarkStar 20-MSH-Pro](http://laserphotonics.com/handheld-laser-marking-systems/markstar-pro) marking solution.

**CleanTech Laser Blasting Technology**
[CleanTech Laser Blasting](https://www.cleantechlaser.com/) products by Laser Photonics provide industry professionals with eco-friendly, cost-effective and time-efficient systems that are utilized for industrial cleaning. Applications include rust removal, paint removal, surface preparation and more. This technology is an excellent alternative to traditional cleaning methods like sandblasting, dry ice blasting and other abrasive blasting techniques.


**About Laser Photonics Corporation**
Laser Photonics is a vertically integrated manufacturer and R&D Center of Excellence for industrial laser technologies and systems. Laser Photonics seeks to disrupt the $46 billion, centuries-old sand and abrasives blasting markets, focusing on surface cleaning, rust removal, corrosion control, de-painting and other laser-based industrial applications. Laser Photonics’ new generation of leading-edge laser blasting technologies and equipment also addresses the numerous health, safety, environmental and regulatory issues associated with old methods. As a result, Laser Photonics has quickly gained a reputation as an industry leader in industrial laser systems with a brand that stands for quality, technology and product innovation. Currently, world-renowned and Fortune 1000 manufacturers in the aerospace, automotive, defense, energy, industrial, maritime, space exploration and shipbuilding industries are using Laser Photonics’ “unique-to-industry” systems.
