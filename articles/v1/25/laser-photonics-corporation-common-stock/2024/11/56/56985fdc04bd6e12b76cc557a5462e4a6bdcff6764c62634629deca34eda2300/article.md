# Laser Photonics and Fonon Technologies to Exhibit With NCMS at DoD Maintenance Symposium 2024

**ORLANDO, Fla., Nov. 12, 2024** – For this year’s Department of Defense Maintenance Symposium,[Laser Photonics Corporation](http://laserphotonics.com/) (LPC) (NASDAQ:[LASE](https://invest.laserphotonics.com/) ), a leading global developer of industrial laser systems for cleaning and other material processing applications, is partnering with its sister company,[Fonon Technologies](https://www.fonon.us/) . Together, they will join the National Center for Manufacturing Sciences (NCMS) in the NCMS exhibit booth at the symposium from December 10-13, 2024.


The Maintenance Symposium, conducted by the Office of the Secretary of Defense, is the only DOD event focused entirely on the maintenance and sustainment of weapon systems and equipment. This year, over 2,300 maintenance professionals from the military, government, industry, and academia are expected to attend, making it a top forum for exchanging ideas on national security maintenance and logistics.


“The DOD Maintenance Symposium is an event linking innovative technology with federal funding opportunities,” said Wayne Tupuola, CEO of Laser Photonics. “Laser Photonics offers maintenance professionals the[CleanTech](http://laserphotonics.com/laser-cleaning-introductory) and[DefenseTech](https://www.fonon.us/defensetech-laser-cleaning-products) laser blasting systems, which are state-of-the-art solutions for combating corrosion—a major challenge for all Defense sectors.“


**What:** DOD Maintenance Symposium 2024
**When:** December 10-13, 2024
**Where:** Salt Palace Convention Center, Salt Lake City, UT
**Booth:** 433


NCMS will host **Booth 433** in Salt Lake City and has invited LPC, Fonon Technologies, and other NCMS Industry Partners to co-host. At the Maintenance Symposium, LPC and Fonon Technologies will showcase a range of laser systems. They will feature the[DefenseTech MRLS Portable Finishing Laser](https://www.fonon.us/products/defensetech-mrls-portable-finishing-laser-1010) (DTMF-1010),[DefenseTech MRLS Finishing Laser](https://www.fonon.us/products/defensetech-mrls-finishing-laser-1030) (DTMF-1030), and[DefenseTech MRLS Roughening Laser](https://www.fonon.us/products/defensetech-mrls-portable-roughening-laser-3040) (DTMR-3040) cleaning systems. These systems are manufactured by LPC and marketed by Fonon Technologies to the defense sector. LPC will also provide insights into its comprehensive portfolio of industrial-grade laser systems for material processing, including the[WeldTech](http://laserphotonics.com/laser-welding-systems) ,[MarkStar](http://laserphotonics.com/laser-marking) , and other products.

**About NCMS**
The National Center for Manufacturing Sciences is a cross-industry technology development consortium, dedicated to improving the competitiveness and strength of the U.S. industrial base. As a member-based organization, it leverages its network of industry, government, and academia partners to develop, demonstrate, and transition innovative technologies efficiently, with less risk and lower cost. NCMS enables world-class member companies to work effectively with other members on new opportunities – bringing together highly capable companies with providers and end users who need their innovations and technology solutions. NCMS members benefit from an accelerated progression of idea creation through execution. Through NCMS, companies with innovative technologies can collaborate with end users and develop solutions to meet their requirements. Coupled with their collaborative power to partner small R&D companies with top-tier OEMs, the results are innovations and opportunities to develop and refine and provide user-centric solutions.
