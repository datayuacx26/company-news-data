# Laser Photonics Announces Successful Exhibition Booth at FabTech 2024

**ORLANDO, Fla., Oct., 21, 2024** –[Laser Photonics Corporation](http://laserphotonics.com/) (LPC) (NASDAQ:[LASE](https://invest.laserphotonics.com/) ), a leading global industrial developer of laser systems for laser cleaning and other material processing applications, is excited to announce a successful year of attendance at the FABTECH 2024 tradeshow that took place from October 15 to 17.


The event resulted in Laser Photonics generating 137 new leads from fabricators, metal formers, welding managers, finishing managers and business owners from industries around the world. During the show, Laser Photonics demonstrated two of its industrial-grade semi-automated systems for roughening, conditioning and finishing: the[CleanTech Robotic Cell (CTIR-6040)](http://laserphotonics.com/semi-automatic-laser-systems/robotic-cell-enclosure) and the Class I[CleanTech MegaCenter (CTIR-5040)](http://laserphotonics.com/laser-cleaning/cleantech-megacenter) .


This was the first time the two laser systems were brought to a tradeshow by LPC, and both proved to be popular among booth visitors who got to see the technology in person. LPC also showcased its[MarkStar IM-1020](http://laserphotonics.com/handheld-laser-marking-systems/markstar-pro) marking solution.


“The significant interest we garnered in our new systems at FABTECH this year is a testament to our continuing growth and the success of our solutions,” said Wayne Tupuola, CEO of Laser Photonics. “We are thrilled to continue introducing our laser systems to industry professionals as we participate in more trade shows in the future.”


FABTECH is North America’s largest metal forming, fabrication, welding and finishing event. This year’s event attracted not only attendees from Florida’s flourishing manufacturing industry but also participants from around the globe, offering LPC access to a diverse and expansive audience.


Laser Photonics continues to supply world-renowned and Fortune 1000 companies with cost-effective, time-efficient and operator-safe laser systems. For more information, visit Laser Photonics at[http://laserphotonics.com/](http://laserphotonics.com/) .


**CleanTech Laser Cleaning Technology**
[CleanTech Laser](http://laserphotonics.com/laser-cleaning)[Cleaning](http://laserphotonics.com/laser-cleaning) products by Laser Photonics provide industry professionals with eco-friendly, cost-effective and time-efficient systems that are utilized for industrial cleaning. Applications include rust removal, paint removal, surface preparation and more. This technology is an excellent alternative to traditional cleaning methods like sandblasting, dry ice blasting and other abrasive blasting techniques.


**About Laser Photonics Corporation**
Laser Photonics is a vertically integrated manufacturer and R&D Center of Excellence for industrial laser technologies and systems. Laser Photonics seeks to disrupt the $46 billion, centuries-old sand and abrasives blasting markets, focusing on surface cleaning, rust removal, corrosion control, de-painting and other laser-based industrial applications. Laser Photonics’ new generation of leading-edge laser blasting technologies and equipment also addresses the numerous health, safety, environmental and regulatory issues associated with old methods.
