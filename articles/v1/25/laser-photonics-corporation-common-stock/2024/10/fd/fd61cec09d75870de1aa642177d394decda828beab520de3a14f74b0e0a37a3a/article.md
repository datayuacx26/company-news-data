# Laser Photonics and Fonon Technologies To Showcase Laser Solutions at 2024 F-15 TCP Worldwide Review

**ORLANDO, Fla., Oct. 29, 2024** –[Laser Photonics Corporation](http://laserphotonics.com/industries/defense) (LPC) (NASDAQ:[LASE](https://invest.laserphotonics.com/) ), a leading global developer of industrial laser systems for cleaning and other material processing applications, joins its sister company, Fonon Technologies, a laser-based development and systems integration company for state-of-the-art components, material processing and power-generating equipment, in announcing that the two companies are co-hosting an exhibit booth at this year’s[F-15 TCP Worldwide Review](https://www.f15tcpwwr.com/) .


The F-15 TCP Worldwide Review, organized by the F-15 Technical Coordination Program (TCP) under the F-15 Division of the[Fighters and Advanced Aircraft Directorate](https://www.aflcmc.af.mil/WELCOME/Organizations/Fighters-and-Advanced-Aircraft-Directorate/) (FAAD), is an annual event that brings together foreign partners with the United States Air Force and industry stakeholders. This event is a platform for discussing issues, improvements, and modifications related to the F-15 platform.


“For Laser Photonics, the F-15 TCP Worldwide Review is a great opportunity to present our innovative solutions to professionals in the defense industry,” said Wayne Tupuola, CEO of Laser Photonics. “We plan to use this opportunity to educate attendees on how our laser solutions can streamline logistics and MRO processes. We look forward to connecting with key industry players, strengthening existing relationships, exploring new markets, and developing new opportunities.”


Bryan Lee, Vice President of Fonon Technologies, added, “We are thrilled to bring our cutting-edge laser systems to the F-15 Program. Our eco-friendly solutions have been increasingly adopted in the defense industry due to their effectiveness and ease of use. We appreciate the F-15 TCP and the F-15 Division of FAAD for providing a forum where innovators like Fonon Technologies and LPC can showcase technologies that are beneficial to the U.S. Air Force.”


**What:** 2024 F-15 TCP Worldwide Review
**When:** Nov. 18 – Nov. 22, 2024
**Where:** Booth 112 at Caribe Royale (8101 World Center Drive, Orlando, FL 32821)


At the Review, LPC and Fonon Technologies will showcase a range of laser systems. They will feature the[DefenseTech MRLS Portable Finishing Laser 1010](https://www.fonon.us/products/defensetech-mrls-portable-finishing-laser-1010) (DTMF-1010),[DefenseTech MRLS Portable Finishing Laser 1030](https://www.fonon.us/products/defensetech-mrls-finishing-laser-1030) (DTMF-1030), and[DefenseTech MRLS Roughening Laser 3040](https://www.fonon.us/products/defensetech-mrls-portable-roughening-laser-3040) (DTMR-3040) cleaning systems. These systems are manufactured by LPC and marketed by Fonon Technologies to the defense sector.


**DefenseTech Laser Cleaning Technology**
The DefenseTech product line provides professionals in the defense industry with eco-friendly, cost-effective and time-efficient systems for industrial cleaning, rust removal, coating removal and surface preparation. Laser cleaning equips industry professionals with the technology they need to maintain equipment integrity and enhance traceability. DefenseTech laser cleaning systems effectively accomplish this without damaging underlying surfaces, ensuring prolonged equipment life and operational readiness. **About Laser Photonics**
Laser Photonics is a vertically integrated manufacturer and R&D Center of Excellence for industrial laser technologies and systems. Laser Photonics seeks to disrupt the $46 billion, centuries-old sand and abrasives blasting markets, focusing on surface cleaning, rust removal, corrosion control, de-painting and other laser-based industrial applications. Laser Photonics’ new generation of leading-edge laser blasting technologies and equipment also addresses the numerous health, safety, environmental and regulatory issues associated with old methods. As a result, Laser Photonics has quickly gained a reputation as an industry leader in industrial laser systems with a brand that stands for quality, technology and product innovation.
