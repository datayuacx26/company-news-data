**About Hallador Energy Company**


Hallador Energy Company (Nasdaq: HNRG) is a vertically-integrated Independent Power Producer (IPP) based in Terre Haute, Indiana. The Company has two core businesses: Hallador Power Company, LLC, which produces electricity and provides accredited capacity at its one-Gigawatt (GW) Merom Generating Station, and Sunrise Coal, LLC, which produces and supplies fuel to the Merom Generating Station and other companies. To learn more about Hallador, visit the Company’s website at[www.halladorenergy.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=MdlBLVnsBmZZ8Bb8ed69iy-CaMSLdhry8xjhQNOHabJCiOSN_sMOZ3QiehBXlX03uOn4bCMYBSG30vjRRnQg6zIcFwxlktPx2oI69rxtXfg=) .
