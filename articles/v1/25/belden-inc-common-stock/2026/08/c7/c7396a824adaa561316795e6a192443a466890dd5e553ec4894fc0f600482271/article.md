# How IT can build secure, scalable infrastructure for OT data

### Step 2: Extend and scale connectivity across OT


Outdoor assets, remote cabinets and exposed work areas need reliable access and strong backhaul performance, but many OT environments quickly outgrow what copper and conduit were designed for.


**Hirschmann IT DAP849 Wireless Access Points** add[Wi-Fi 6](https://www.belden.com/blog/wi-fi-6-benefits-and-supporting-it) capabilities in an IP67-rated enclosure for harsh environments, with two fiber uplinks to support long-distance connectivity and reliable performance in environments with high electromagnetic interference.


And we not only help connectivity reach the field, but also scale the backbone behind it. **Hirschmann’s GREYHOUND GRS2000 L2/L3 Switches** provide high-density rugged switching with backbone connectivity up to 10 Gb/s to support network expansion and future-ready industrial infrastructure.


Together, these solutions address **volume** and **velocity** by extending reach, increasing capacity and supporting faster movement of operational data across the OT environment.
