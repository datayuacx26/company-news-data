# Solving ERP problems by rethinking enterprise connectivity

Walk into almost any enterprise office right now, and you’ll see the same picture: sales, finance, legal, administration and operations teams all working from enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems that are supposed to keep the business aligned and informed. And these platforms have been able to do that reasonably well—until recently.


To move faster and make better decisions, organizations are starting to layer agentic AI and other tools on top of their existing ERP platforms to get faster answers to questions, uncover business opportunities and automate analysis. But **this setup can be unstable and lead to ERP problems** if the network wasn’t designed to support anything more than traditional office traffic.


## ERP and AI demand more from your network


The amount of data flowing through ERPs and other business applications is significant, but it pales in comparison to the amount of[data required by AI](https://www.belden.com/blog/why-building-a-data-foundation-matters-in-ai-for-manufacturing) and agentic tools as they combine and evaluate information from multiple systems.


As these technologies transform what ERP can do, **they also expose weaknesses in the networks that support them** .


Data must move reliably from the plant floor to the office and out to[data centers](https://www.belden.com/solutions/industries/data-centers) or cloud services to deliver accurate and timely results to ERP and AI tools. When it doesn’t, users experience bottlenecks.
