# Belden Announces 2023 Distributor Value Award Winners

The Belden Distributor Value Awards recognize our distributors who demonstrate one of Belden's six core values consistently across the globe, emphasizing the importance of providing exceptional customer service through ongoing enhancement and collaborative effort.


**Belden Core Values**


Belden continues to push the industry forward by working with distributors who share these values across the globe. “Our distributors are key partners in delivering Belden’s solutions to the global market,” says Ashish Chand, CEO and President of Belden. “The annual Distributor Value Awards gives us the opportunity to celebrate and honor the distributors that have best exemplified the Belden values over the past year.”


To determine the selections, Belden sales teams nominate partners based on the past year’s activities. The Belden Partner Alliance Committee then carefully considers and selects the winners from these nominees. The top 6 most outstanding partners in each region are recognized and celebrated for their remarkable accomplishments.


Congratulations to the Distributor Value Award Winners of 2023!
