# Celebrating the Distributors Driving Belden’s Values Forward

###


---


## EMEA award winners


### PRODUS - Continuous Improvement Is Our Way of Life


PRODUS S.A. has been a trusted Belden partner for 30 years, demonstrating exceptional long-term commitment. What truly sets them apart is their proactive approach and deep market engagement. They operate as a true extension of Belden in Poland—continuously sharing insights, identifying opportunities, and ensuring we remain aligned with local market trends and customer needs.


Thanks to their dedicated efforts, a significant portion of the public transport sector in Poland relies on Hirschmann solutions, alongside a wide base of industrial and discrete customers. For their continued commitment and continuous improvement mindset, PRODUS S.A. is a deserving award recipient.


#### About PRODUS S.A.


PRODUS S.A., in collaboration with Belden, utilizes the latest technologies to provide its clients with reliable and modern solutions. The company continually develops its competencies, ensuring high-quality services and strengthening its position in the Polish market as a trusted partner in industrial data transmission systems.


### Fegemu Solutions - We Play To Win


The “We Play to Win” award, reflects Fegemu’s consistency, perseverance, and strong drive to achieve success. Above all, it highlights the commitment and trust they have shown in Belden from the very beginning - a commitment that has clearly translated into accelerated growth and strengthened results throughout 2025.


#### About Fegemu Solutions:


Fegemu Solutions has worked closely with industrial companies since 1996, delivering advanced technology solutions that enhance operational processes. Their team comprises highly skilled professionals with expertise across a wide range of industrial sectors, enabling it to provide clients with consistently high-quality service.


### We Reach For Greatness – Routeco UK


Routeco UK is awarded the 2025 Distributor Value Award "We Reach for Greatness" in recognition of an exceptional performance that truly sets the bar high. With an impressive 65% growth in 2025, Routeco has demonstrated what is possible when ambition meets execution.


Leveraging their extensive customer base, they have successfully positioned Belden deeper into their accounts - unlocking new opportunities and expanding Belden's footprint where it matters most. On top of that, Routeco has won customers through their genuine dedication to outstanding service, fast and reliable supply, and making it easy to do business with them. It is this combination of outstanding results, strategic customer positioning, and a customer-first attitude that makes Routeco a truly deserving recipient of this award.


#### About Routeco UK:


Routeco UK is a leading technical distributor of industrial automation and control solutions and is now part of the Sonepar Group. In addition to an extensive range of high-quality components, they support their customers with customized solutions, technical expertise, and its own on-site warehouse. Customers from a wide range of industries - from pharmaceuticals and food to the automotive industry, energy, metal, and mechanical engineering - benefit from a comprehensive range of services and personalized consulting tailored specifically to their needs.


### Infinigate Group – We Invest In Talent


The Infinigate Group is awarded the 2025 Distributor Value Award "We Invest in Talent" in recognition of their outstanding commitment to technical excellence across the Belden channel. Built on a long-standing partnership, Infinigate has consistently demonstrated a willingness to grow alongside Belden - including taking on products outside their traditional portfolio.


With a team of highly skilled professionals, they act as a true extended arm of Belden's team - delivering product updates and hands-on training to System Integrators while also providing valuable support to other Belden channel partners. Their investment in talent strengthens the entire partner ecosystem, and it is precisely this dedication and versatility that makes them a deserving recipient of this award.


#### About the Infinigate Group:


The Infinigate Group, the leading technology platform and trusted advisor in Cybersecurity, Cloud & Network Infrastructure sets itself apart for its deep technical expertise, delivering locally tailored solutions and services to SMB and enterprise customers across EMEA and ANZ. Relying on a strong central supply chain and an extensive portfolio of leading-edge solutions, The Infinigate Group sparks growth for vendor and channel partners.


### Wesco Anixter CSS MEA - We Succeed Through Teamwork


Wesco Anixter CSS MEA is recognized for exemplary collaboration with the Belden MEA team, consistently translating joint planning into strong commercial results in structured cabling across 2025 and 2026. Through disciplined account engagement, transparent communication, and a shared commitment to customer success, they have helped accelerate project momentum, strengthen stakeholder confidence, and expand Belden’s presence in priority opportunities.


This performance reflects a shared focus on adding value, strengthening the partner ecosystem, and driving mutual growth. For these reasons, Wesco Anixter CSS MEA is a worthy recipient of the 2025 Distributor Value Award for “We Succeed Through Teamwork”.


#### About Wesco Anixter:


Wesco Anixter is a Fortune 500 company and a leading global distributor of Network & Security Solutions, Electrical & Electronic Solutions and Utility Power Solutions. Following the 2020 acquisition of Anixter, they offer full-line solutions, and intelligence, that create reliable, resilient systems that sustain businesses and communities. Through our unmatched global distribution network along with our supply chain and technical expertise, we help lower the cost, risk and complexity of our customers’ supply chains.


### MICAS Group - Customers Define Our Success


MICAS Group has been awarded “Customers Define Our Success” for consistently putting customer outcomes at the center of how they plan, sell, and deliver Belden solutions across the Middle East. By combining deep expertise in building automation and power infrastructure, MICAS has helped customers achieve reliable performance, faster project delivery, and dependable support across key verticals such as hospitality, healthcare, data centers, and industrial automation.


With broad regional coverage and a reputation for prompt responses and effective project support, MICAS has strengthened confidence in Belden’s portfolio and delivered record performance driven by repeatable customer success. This commitment is why MICAS is a deserving recipient of this award.


#### About MICAS:


Established in 1995, MICAS Middle East for Intelligent Connectivity and Systems L.L.C – O.P.C (formerly Al Mazroui ICAS) has grown steadily, specializing in the latest building automation technologies as well as cables and low-current systems. They supply state-of-the-art equipment and solutions, with a particular strength in premium cables and accessories.


With offices across the U.A.E. and the wider Gulf region, and supported by substantial stock inventory, MICAS has successfully met a broad range of project requirements throughout the GCC. Prompt delivery and responsive customer support have been key to maintaining high levels of client satisfaction.
