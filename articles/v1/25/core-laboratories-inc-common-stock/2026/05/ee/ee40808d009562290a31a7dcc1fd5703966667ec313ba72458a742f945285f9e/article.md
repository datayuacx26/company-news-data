# Core in Action: Turning Measured Data into Intelligent Insight with RAPID™

As the energy industry accelerates toward digital platforms and AI, Core Lab is advancing its leadership through[RAPID™](https://www.corelab.com/products/rapidtm/) , the Company’s proprietary, cloud-based platform for reservoir characterization data. Designed as a secure, structured, and searchable database environment, RAPID™ enables clients to access high-fidelity reservoir data in real time, transforming how information is delivered, integrated, and applied.


RAPID™ replaces fragmented file delivery with connected, real-time data access, modernizing global laboratory workflows and significantly enhancing the client experience. By providing highly structured geological, petrophysical, and engineering datasets in formats that integrate seamlessly into client systems, RAPID™ supports advanced analytics and machine learning applications, ensuring that digital workflows are built on reliable, laboratory-derived inputs.


“What makes RAPID™ unique is that the data isn’t just digital; it’s measured data delivered in a structured environment that was architected by geoscientists and designed to make complex reservoir data actionable. As our clients adopt more advanced digital workflows and AI tools, the accuracy and quality of the underlying data become even more critical. RAPID™ ensures those decisions are built on a reliable and accessible foundation. *”*


**Lindsay Roe, Director of Technology**


## The Foundation of RAPID™


Core Lab’s data is not modeled or inferred; it is measured. Backed by nearly a century of reservoir description expertise, the Company has assembled one of the industry’s deepest datasets through Joint Industry Projects, including information from nearly 10,000 wells, more than 75,000 data points, and extensive analyses from some of the world’s largest and most prolific reservoirs. This depth is further strengthened when client’s trust RAPID™ as their proprietary system of record, forming a uniquely robust and integrated data repository.


As digital platforms scale and AI-driven workflows become more prevalent, the importance of high-quality, accurate data becomes increasingly critical. In a world of expanding data and accelerating automation, Core Lab™ remains focused on what matters most: delivering the precision, reliability, and scientific insight that underpin confident reservoir decisions.
