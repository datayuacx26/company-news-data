## What to look for in a wallet pass platform


A wallet pass platform should do more than mint a pass file. That is table stakes. The real question is what happens after the pass is installed.


**Apple Wallet and Google Wallet support**


The platform needs reliable support for both ecosystems, including loyalty cards, offers, generic passes, coupons, and update flows.


**Dynamic updates**


Balances, rewards, referral status, and offer details should update without forcing the customer to reinstall anything.


**Marketing triggers**


The best programs use wallet passes as part of a broader retention flow, not as a static barcode sitting on a phone.


**Attribution**


If a pass drives a referral, store visit, coupon redemption, or lead, the brand needs to know. Otherwise it is just pretty plumbing.


> A wallet pass without attribution is just a nicer coupon. Useful, sure. But not enough for a serious growth team.

## Where the other platforms fit


[PassKit](https://passkit.com/guide/get-started/) is a strong choice when the brief is mostly wallet infrastructure. It is wallet-native and has a long history in the category. If a brand wants a flexible Apple Wallet and Google Wallet system without building from scratch, it deserves a serious look.


[Vibes](https://www.vibes.com/blog/mobile-wallet-loyalty-cards) makes sense for companies already thinking in mobile engagement, SMS, and loyalty. Its wallet work is credible, especially for large consumer brands with app-based loyalty programs.

## FAQ


What is a wallet pass platform?


It is software for creating, distributing, updating, and managing passes in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.


Are Apple Wallet and Google Wallet the platforms?


They are the native wallet ecosystems. Most brands still use a software platform to manage pass creation, updates, distribution, and campaign logic.


What is the best wallet pass platform for ecommerce?


For referral-led ecommerce, Talkable is the strongest fit because the wallet pass connects to referral tracking, rewards, fraud controls, and revenue reporting.


Can wallet passes support referrals?


Yes. A pass can carry referral access, discount details, store credit, QR codes, and offers. The important part is connecting those actions to attribution.


Do wallet passes replace email or SMS?


No. They work best as another owned channel. The advantage is persistence: the pass stays on the phone and can update over time.


Should every brand use wallet passes?


No. They make the most sense when customers have a reason to reuse the pass, redeem a benefit, refer friends, or check a reward balance.
