Referral marketing in 2026 is not a side bet anymore. It is one of the few acquisition channels that can still scale on trust instead of rented attention.


Ad costs are still high, cookies are less useful, and shoppers are more skeptical of polished brand claims. That puts referrals in a stronger position: people trust other people, especially when the recommendation comes with context and a real reason to act.


We pulled together 50 referral marketing stats that show why referrals deserve a real seat in your 2026 growth plan, from customer trust and CAC pressure to mobile sharing, rewards, retention, and ROI.


### Consumer trust and behavior


- According to[Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org/2024/06/research-customer-referrals-are-contagious) , referred customers generate 30-57% more referrals than non-referred customers
- [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/a-new-way-to-measure-word-of-mouth-marketing) research shows that marketing-induced consumer-to-consumer word of mouth generates more than twice the sales of paid advertising
- [Nielsen](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2024/maximizing-roi-in-a-fragmented-world-nielsen-annual-marketing-report/) reports that 92% of consumers trust recommendations from friends and family over all other forms of advertising
- [Deloitte’s](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/retail-distribution/digital-consumer-trends) Digital Consumer Trends report shows that referred customers have a 37% higher retention rate
- According to[Forrester’s](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2024-us-customer-experience-index/) Customer Experience Index, companies with strong referral programs see 27% higher customer satisfaction scores


### Business impact


- [Gartner’s](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/5140631) research shows that customer acquisition costs increased by 29% in 2024, a cost base many growth teams are still working around in 2026
- According to[McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/a-new-way-to-measure-word-of-mouth-marketing) , businesses with formalized referral programs report 71% higher net promoter scores
