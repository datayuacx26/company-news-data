## A referral offer customers can keep.


Use Wallet to make BFCM referral offers easier to save, find again, and send when the sale is live.


Black Friday and Cyber Monday referral campaigns have a familiar problem: customers see an offer, mean to come back, then lose it in the noise. A digital Wallet pass gives the friend offer, earned reward, and next action a home on the phone. It is there when a customer is ready to share or shop.


This is a practical guide for teams planning BFCM referral offers with[Talkable Wallet](https://www.talkable.com/wallet-pass) , Apple Wallet, and Google Wallet.


A Wallet pass keeps the active referral offer and reward close at hand.


## The idea: make the offer feel kept, not chased


Inbox placement is fragile during BFCM. A referral offer that gets saved to Wallet has another way back to the customer when they are ready to shop.


The point is simple. Give the offer a home on the phone, then give people a reason to reopen it. Wallet works best when it gives the customer a usable offer, not another reason to open an email.


**The sale is loud. The offer should be easy to keep.**


## The customer motion


### A Wallet offer has a job at each point in the sale.


01 / Save it


Give people something worth holding onto before BFCM starts: early access, a live referral reward, or a friend offer with a real end date.


02 / Find it again


Put the current offer and customer reward where they can see them in a second. Wallet should be the fast route back to value.


03 / Send it


Prompt customers who saved but have not shared. Tie the reminder to the sale, not to an automated follow-up cadence.


04 / Use it


Bring earned rewards back when the deadline actually matters. If there is no value left, leave the customer alone.


Wallet can carry the customer from saving an offer to sharing and reward use.


Message direction


## Pass updates for Black Friday and Cyber Monday


Every update needs a reason to exist. Keep the copy close to the customer’s actual moment. The pass is useful when it says what changed and what to do next.


01 / AFTER SAVE


### Confirm the offer is there.


“Your BFCM referral offer is saved. Share it with a friend and keep your reward ready for the sale.”


02 / BLACK FRIDAY


### Make the live offer obvious.


“Black Friday is on. Open Wallet to see your referral offer and reward.”


03 / FINAL HOURS


### Bring an earned reward back.


“Your reward is still in Wallet. Use it before the offer expires.”


**Use pass updates for live offer changes, referral prompts, and expiration messages. Skip generic reminders.**


## Build the pass as campaign creative


A Wallet pass is a small campaign asset, not a tiny website. The pass needs to make the offer clear, keep the next action close, and give customers a reason to come back.


- **Put the value first.** Lead with the reward or the friend offer. No decoding required.
- **Make BFCM visible.** Give the pass event-specific creative, language, and a real end date.
- **Keep the action close.** Sharing, referral information, and useful links should be easy to find.

## The BFCM operating rhythm


Plan around the sale, not around the feature.


01


BEFORE BFCM


### Earn the save.


Introduce Wallet as a place to hold early access or a live referral offer before the event begins.


02


FRIDAY


### Make the offer unmistakable.


Update the pass so the current reward and customer action are clear at a glance.


03


WEEKEND


### Make sharing timely.


Talk to customers who saved but have not shared. Make the live event the reason to act.


04


LAST CALL


### Make reward use easy.


Bring active, unused rewards back before the deadline. Do not create urgency where there is no value left.


**The filter for every update: does the customer have a real reason to act right now?**


## What to measure in a Wallet referral campaign


An install is the start. The next action is the signal. Watch how customers move from install to share, reward use, and Wallet-attributed sales. That is where the campaign earns its place in the BFCM plan.


- Install rate
- First referral share
- Reward redemption
- Wallet-attributed sales

**If Wallet makes the offer easier to keep and easier to use, it is doing its job.**


## Frequently asked questions about BFCM referral offers


### What is a Wallet referral offer?


It is a referral offer or reward that a customer saves to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. The pass can show the current value and give the customer a direct route to share or shop.


### When should a brand ask customers to save a Wallet pass?


Ask when the pass holds a specific benefit worth keeping, such as early access, a referral offer, a reward, or an event-related incentive.


### How often should brands update a pass during BFCM?


Only when there is a real change the customer can use. Black Friday launch, a distinct Cyber Monday offer, and a valid reward deadline are sensible moments. Repeated generic reminders are not.


### What should brands measure?


Measure movement from install to share, reward use, referral conversion, and attributed sales. Review profitability and quality signals alongside volume.
