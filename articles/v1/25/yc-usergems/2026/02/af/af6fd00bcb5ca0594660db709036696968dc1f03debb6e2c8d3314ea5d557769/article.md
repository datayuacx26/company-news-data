# UserGems wins 6 RepVue Reppy Awards, ranks in the top 5% of all sales organizations

‍ **\[San Francisco, CA\] —** UserGems, theAI Command Center for B2B revenue teams, won awards in six of seven categories in[RepVue's](https://www.repvue.com/awards) biannual Reppys program, which recognizes sales organizations based on voluntary, anonymous feedback from sales professionals about compensation, culture, leadership, and overall employee experience.


The recognition comes from RepVue, the world's leading sales organization ratings platform, which collects millions of data points annually from B2B sales professionals. Companies that rank in the top 20 for their category or top 5 in a metro area become eligible for[Reppy Awards](https://www.repvue.com/awards) , with winners selected from nearly 20,000 tracked sales organizations.


UserGems received Reppy Awards in the following categories:


- [Culture & Leadership](https://www.repvue.com/awards/winners/2026/culture-and-leadership)
- [Product-Market Fit](https://www.repvue.com/awards/winners/2026/product-market-fit)
- [Inbound Lead Flow](https://www.repvue.com/awards/winners/2026/inbound-lead)
- [Incentive Compensation](https://www.repvue.com/awards/winners/2026/incentive-comp-structure)
- [Diversity & Inclusion](https://www.repvue.com/awards/winners/2026/diversity-and-inclusion)
- [Small Companies](https://www.repvue.com/awards/winners/2026/small)
- [Top Overall Sales Organization](https://www.repvue.com/awards/winners/2026/overall)


The awards reflect UserGems' commitment to creating an environment where sales professionals can build long-term careers, not just hit quota. RepVue measures organizations on factors including culture, leadership, professional development, compensation, diversity, and overall employee sentiment.


"The winners of the Reppys represent organizations fully committed to the success and development of their employees," said Ryan Walsh, CEO of RepVue. "In the modern workforce, attracting and retaining top sales talent is critical, and these companies have created cultures where sales professionals can not only succeed but build long-term careers."


UserGems' recognition across multiple categories, from culture and leadership to product-market fit and compensation, demonstrates alignment between what the company promises to customers and how it operates internally.


RepVue's Reppy Awards are issued biannually and based entirely on data voluntarily submitted by B2B sales professionals who rate their organizations across compensation, culture, overall employee experience, and other factors. The anonymous, socially-sourced ratings drive transparency in sales organizations and help sales professionals make informed career decisions.


UserGems' top 5% ranking and six category wins position the company among elite sales organizations recognized for providing environments where sales employees have the highest potential to achieve career success.
