## ShipBob Summer 2026 Release FAQs


Here are answers to some of the top questions merchants have about ShipBob’s Summer 2026 Release.


#### What is ShipBob’s MCP server?


ShipBob’s MCP (Model Context Protocol) server is an open connection layer that gives AI tools direct, read-and-write access to your fulfillment data. Unlike most fulfillment integrations that only let you pull information, ShipBob MCP lets your tools both retrieve and act on orders, inventory, and shipments in a single step (for example, pulling up an exception order and rerouting it without switching tabs).


#### What is Bobby, ShipBob’s AI agent?


Bobby is ShipBob’s embedded AI agent, built directly into the ShipBob merchant dashboard. It lets you manage your fulfillment operation using plain-language requests.


You can ask Bobby questions, and it reads your live operational data, then carries out the work: reshipping orders, creating warehouse receiving orders (WROs), updating inventory records, and more. It confirms every change before executing and logs each action so nothing happens without your knowledge.


#### Is Bobby available now?


Bobby is currently in beta, and the waitlist is open. Merchants can join the waitlist directly from the ShipBob dashboard at[web.shipbob.com](https://web.shipbob.com/) . ShipBob has indicated that Bobby and additional AI capabilities will continue shipping throughout 2026.


#### What is the ShipBob Promise?


The ShipBob Promise refers to the delivery promise ShipBob sets at checkout on behalf of its merchants. ShipBob’s Promise Engine is a component of the broader Fulfillment Engine, one of the two core AI engines that run across the ShipBob platform.


The Promise Engine uses real-time data — inventory positions, carrier performance, warehouse capacity, and network conditions — to calculate delivery dates that your operation can actually keep. It works alongside AI-driven inventory placement, order routing, carrier selection, and packaging optimization to ensure that the date shown to your customer at checkout is one ShipBob can reliably hit.


#### What is TrackBob?


TrackBob is ShipBob’s branded post-purchase tracking experience. It provides customers with a branded, merchant-customized tracking page that replaces generic carrier tracking links. The goal is to keep the post-purchase experience on-brand, reduce WISMO support tickets, and give customers a tracking touchpoint they actually open and engage with.
