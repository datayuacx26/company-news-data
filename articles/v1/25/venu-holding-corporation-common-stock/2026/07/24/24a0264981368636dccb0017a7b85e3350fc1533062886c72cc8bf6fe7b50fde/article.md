# VENU Hosts Special Event for Ford Amphitheater Investors Featuring VENU Shareholder Dierks Bentley

### VENU Hosts Special Event for Ford Amphitheater Investors Featuring VENU Shareholder Dierks Bentley


July 7, 2026


COLORADO SPRINGS, Colo.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Venu Holding Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fbit.ly%2F3KaUj1X&esheet=54565970&newsitemid=20260707400116&lan=en-US&anchor=Venu+Holding+Corporation&index=105&md5=77d4300a3501a90096acd27cd8181553) ("VENU" or the "Company") (NYSE AMERICAN: VENU), an owner, operator, and developer of premium live entertainment destinations, today announced it hosted a dedicated evening for Colorado based Ford Amphitheater Luxe FireSuite® and Owners Club investors, featuring GRAMMY-nominated, multi-platinum recording artist and VENU® shareholder Dierks Bentley.


Dierks Bentley Unplugged Pre-Party in Colorado Springs, CO | Photo Credit: Keni Mac


The event took place Thursday, July 2nd, at Phil Long Music Hall at Bourbon Brothers, ahead of Bentley's performance on the Company's Ford Amphitheater main stage as part of his[Off the Map](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fdierks.com%2Fpages%2Foff-the-map-tour&esheet=54565970&newsitemid=20260707400116&lan=en-US&anchor=Off+the+Map&index=106&md5=4d05a0671c5f87222fc927ac72e33525) tour, marking his return to Ford Amphitheater this summer. The evening included a curated question and answer session with VENU Chief Operating Officer, Vic Sutter, and a live acoustic performance of several of Bentley's most beloved hits, giving shareholders a rare, close-up look at the artist whose stake in VENU, and role as a founding member of the Company's celebrity advisory council, have helped shape VENU's approach to venue design and fan experience.


The advisory council curates VENU's development, the growth of its premium entertainment campuses, and the elevation of its hospitality experiences, bringing together industry leaders who have built and shaped the live entertainment landscape.


"Experiences like this are one in a million," said J.W. Roth, Founder, Chairman, and CEO of VENU. "Dierks represents everything we're building toward: authenticity, excellence, and an unwavering commitment to the fan experience. I'm grateful he could spend this time with fellow shareholders and for the opportunity to host him and his entire team.”


VENU continues to grow its national footprint, with venues operating and in development or entitlement across Colorado, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Texas, and additional markets in pre-construction phases. The Company's approach of aligning artists, fans, and shareholders around its venues remains central to its long-term growth strategy.
