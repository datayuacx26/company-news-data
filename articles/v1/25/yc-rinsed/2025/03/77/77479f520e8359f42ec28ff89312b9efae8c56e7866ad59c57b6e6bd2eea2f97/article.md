# Rinsed Introduces New Fleet Plan Solution

Rinsed has introduced a new Fleet Plan solution, making it easier for car washes to manage large accounts. This feature simplifies setup for operators and provides fleet owners with a straightforward way to handle membership management themselves.


“We’re excited to deliver a solution that works easily for both car wash operators and fleet owners,” said Hank Fitchner, Head of Product at Rinsed. “This feature is designed to simplify processes, provide flexibility, and ultimately help our customers grow their business.”


The Fleet Plan feature offers:


- **Easy Account Management:** Fleet owners can manage their own accounts in the Customer Portal, including adding and removing vehicles.
- **Multiple Billing Options:** Supports card-on-file and auto-bill payments for added convenience, as well as non-credit card billing methods.
- **Automated Barcodes:** Barcodes are automatically generated and sent directly to fleet owners for easy setup.
- **Tax-Exempt Support:** Easily handles tax-exempt fleet customers.
- **Flexible membership types:** Fleet owners can decide if they want to pay per wash or have an unlimited membership.


The Fleet Plans feature is now available to current DRB customers, and is included with The Car Wash CRM platform at no additional cost.
