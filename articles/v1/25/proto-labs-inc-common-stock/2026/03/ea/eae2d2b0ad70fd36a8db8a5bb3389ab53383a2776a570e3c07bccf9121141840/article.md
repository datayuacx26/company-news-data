Tariffs remain the subject of constant headlines. In a climate shaped by frequent policy moves, including recent Supreme Court and administration actions, it’s easy to lose sight of what actually changes your landed cost and project lead time. Here’s the bottom line: prototypes were and will be duty free, while rates on commercial goods saw only a small decrease after the latest rounds of actions. ## Navigating Ever-Changing Tariff Policy


Amid shifting rules, three factors consistently determine tariff exposure: country of manufacture, the raw materials (including quantity and origin), and the end use of the parts. Prototypes are generally exempt from duties, whereas production parts and assemblies may incur tariffs depending on classification and origin.
