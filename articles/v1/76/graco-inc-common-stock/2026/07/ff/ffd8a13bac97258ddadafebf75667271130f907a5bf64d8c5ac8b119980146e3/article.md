## A Summer Tradition That Continues to Bring People Together


From its beginning, Graco has focused on building a work environment and culture that focuses on its people. Long before the company became a global leader in the fluid handling industry, that culture started taking shape through annual events that brought employees and their families together. One example of this is the company’s annual picnic.


Since 1929, Graco has hosted an annual summer picnic, a gathering that began as a small event and has grown alongside the company and is a defining part of its culture.


#### A legacy that has grown alongside the company


The first picnic was held at the home of founder Leil Gray and his wife, Clarissa, along Lake Minnetonka in Minnesota. In its early years, employees gathered in a setting that reflected the company’s close-knit dynamic at the time. As Graco grew, the picnic outgrew its original location and evolved into a large-scale event that continues to connect employees.


*In 1931, Gray Company employees gathered at the Lake Minnetonka home of Leil and Clarissa Gray for the third annual picnic.*


Over the decades, the picnic has welcomed hundreds and, at times, thousands of employees, retirees and family members. While its location and activities have changed, the purpose of the event has remained the same: providing a space for people to connect beyond work and recognize the shared contributions that have moved Graco forward.


#### Activities that foster collaboration and connection


The event has featured a mix of friendly competition and family entertainment. In the early years, the picnics included slow-pitch softball games, often matching factory teams against office teams or external competitors. Over time, the event expanded to include volleyball, basketball, and other activities that encouraged participation.


As the company grew, so did the picnic experiences. Organizers introduced family-friendly attractions such as carnival games, pony rides, petting zoos and face painting.


*An aerial view of the picnic and games at the 2013 picnic in Rogers, Minnesota.*


The impact of the annual picnic extends beyond just one Graco location. Over time, the tradition has been seen across multiple Graco locations in the United States and internationally. This expansion shows that Graco’s strength comes from its people and the connections that they have with one another both domestically and internationally.


#### A culture created from coming together


As Graco celebrates its centennial in 2026, the annual picnic is a reminder to all employees of where the company started and the importance of bringing everyone together.
