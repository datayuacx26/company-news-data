## Innovating for Life: Graco's STEM Journey


To say Graco is firmly committed to STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics) is an understatement. After all, STEM isn’t just a buzzword. It’s literally what we do, how we set the bar and move what matters. At its core, STEM is about problem solving, and Graco has carved out a legacy of solving problems through innovation.


It’s a core philosophy, an integrated approach to analytical thinking that animates our purpose, vision and values. For us, that extends to partnering with our customers, employees and communities to support STEM at every stage, from the classroom to careers.


#### STEM begins in the classroom


Our employees recognize that today’s education lays out the groundwork for tomorrow’s innovation. In 2025,[The Graco Foundation](https://www.graco.com/us/en/about-graco/foundation.html%20-%20grant-requests) awarded $461,500 in scholarships to students attending local four-year colleges and technical schools.


Beyond simply providing financial resources, we partner with initiatives like[FIRST Robotics](https://www.firstinspires.org/) , the world’s leading youth robotics community, where high school students design, build, and program robots for competition. Graco employees volunteer their time as mentors, sharing expertise in coding, CAD design, and mechanical systems to ensure students have access to real-world learning opportunities that spark innovation and confidence.


These experiences teach problem-solving, teamwork, and technical skills that prepare students for future careers in manufacturing and engineering at companies like Graco. If STEM is the lifeblood of invention, it is simply in our best interest to make it accessible to all students. When we partner with schools and nonprofits, we empower the very students who will be contributing to Graco’s innovations down the road.


#### STEM continues in the workplace


In STEM fields, technology is always changing, and ongoing education ensures Graco is at the forefront of changing it. Graco supports and invests in local colleges and universities as well as programs like Achieve Twin Cities’ Step Up. Interns don’t just observe, instead they take an active role in learning and doing the work of creating and innovating.


For existing employees, Graco has invested over $900,000 in tuition reimbursement, and in 2025 contributed $1.59 million to nonprofits supporting education and workforce development. Graco has also committed hundreds of thousands to employee families through scholarships to help children follow in their parents’ impressive footsteps.


#### Looking toward a STEM-based future


Graco innovations don’t happen through theoretical learning alone. There’s no equation for perfecting plural-component proportioning systems or airless spray technology. These breakthroughs come from hands-on problem-solving and collaboration. They’re born of hands-on problem solving and interacting with other problem solvers.


As we plan for a STEM future, a commitment to life-long, experiential learning remains essential to developing solutions that move materials that matter, no matter what.


If you want join us on our STEM journey,[learn more about our internship program](https://www.graco.com/us/en/careers/internships.html) and take a look at some of the organizations[we have supported](https://www.graco.com/us/en/about-graco/foundation.html%20-%20grant-requests) through the Graco Foundation.
