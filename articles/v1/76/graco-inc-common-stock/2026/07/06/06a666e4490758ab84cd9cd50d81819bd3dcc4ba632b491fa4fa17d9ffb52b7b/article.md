## A Century of Collaboration: How Distributors Help Bring Graco Innovation to Customers


When a company reaches a century in business, longevity alone isn’t the story. What matters is how that longevity was built and the people and organizations that helped make it possible.


As Graco celebrates its centennial, one constant stands out: the critical role distributors play in turning innovation into real-world results. For decades, these relationships have helped ensure Graco technologies are not only engineered, but clearly understood, adopted and relied on where the work happens. From hands-on demonstrations in the company’s earliest years to today’s global network, Graco’s distributor model has remained foundational to supporting customers and moving the materials that matter.


That dynamic is especially visible in long-standing relationships like Graco’s work with Spray Equipment & Service Center, a distributor that has grown alongside Graco as finishing, lubrication and automation technologies have evolved.


*Early photo of Graco and Spray Equipment & Service Center's relationship captured at a tradeshow booth.*


#### Innovation thrives through collaboration and strong relationships


Over the past 100 years, Graco has continued to move fluid handling technology forward. But innovation alone doesn’t solve problems. Customers also need confidence that a new solution will work in their environment, integrate into their processes and deliver long-term value.


That’s where distributor expertise becomes essential.


Distributors serve as a bridge between new technology and practical applications, helping customers evaluate options, test solutions and implement change with clarity and support. This role has become increasingly important as manufacturing, finishing and automation systems grow more complex.


#### Growing together: Spray Equipment & Service Center


Founded in 1970 in Wichita, Kansas, Spray Equipment & Service Center is one of Graco’s long-standing distributors. The company began with a focus on industrial and aerospace spray equipment, but its capabilities expanded as customer needs and Graco’s portfolio changed.


Over time, Spray Equipment & Service Center invested in advanced technologies such as two-component mixing, electrostatic spray and engineered finishing systems, aligning closely with Graco’s innovation roadmap. That shared evolution positioned the distributor to guide customers through new approaches rather than simply supplying equipment.


“As technology has evolved, so have customer expectations,” said Rick Mott, Spray Equipment & Service Center's vice president of marketing, strategy and vendor relations. “Working closely with Graco allows us to help customers understand what’s possible and move forward with confidence.”


#### Turning innovation into confidence


In 2000, Spray Equipment & Service Center strengthened its customer support by opening an in-house testing and demonstration facility. The space allows customers to see Graco technologies in action, allowing them to test processes, evaluate outcomes, and reduce uncertainty before making operational changes.


By expanding its Graco offerings to include automation, dispensing and powder finishing, the distributor has continued to meet customers where they are, providing hands-on expertise that extends well beyond installation.


This approach reflects a shared belief across Graco and its distributor network: innovation is most effective when customers are supported at every stage.


#### A model that has stood the test of time


As Graco looks ahead to its next century, relationships like the one with Spray Equipment & Service Center illustrate why the distributor model remains foundational to the company’s success.


By working closely with experienced distributors, Graco can continue to focus on what it does best, like engineering reliable solutions, while customers benefit from local expertise that helps ensure those solutions deliver value in the real world.


It’s a model built on shared knowledge, mutual growth and a commitment to helping customers move materials that matter—one application at a time.
