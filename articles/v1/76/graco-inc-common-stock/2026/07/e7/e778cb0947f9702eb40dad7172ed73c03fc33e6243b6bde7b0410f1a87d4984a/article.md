## Powered by Patents: How Graco Drives Innovation Forward


Innovation and continuous improvement have shaped Graco since its founding. From the earliest days, the company set out to solve real-world challenges with practical solutions.


That commitment began with founder Russell Gray, who developed the first air-powered grease gun. His invention did more than launch a business. It established a mindset of innovation that continues to define Graco today.


#### A foundation built on solving problems


Gray received Graco’s first patent from the United States Patent Office in 1930. Over his lifetime, he secured 33 patents focused on improving lubrication and maintenance processes across industries.


These innovations included grease guns, lubricating systems, lubricating dispensers, paint sprayers, apparatus to clean auto engines and lubricant dispensing apparatus.


Each patent reflected a clear goal: make work more efficient and reliable. That same goal continues to guide Graco today.


#### Created from innovation, lasting from continuous innovation


Since those early years, Graco has continued to expand its portfolio of technologies and solutions. Engineers and employees across the company focus on developing products that improve performance, reduce downtime and simplify complex processes for customers.


That work has resulted in more than 7,600 patents worldwide. While a single innovation may generate multiple patents across different countries, the total reflects the consistency and importance of Graco’s innovation strategy. Today, nearly 3,000 of those patents remain active.


“Some patents represent meaningful advancements in technology, while others are true breakthroughs,” said Paul Sherburne, associate general counsel and chief intellectual property council at Graco. “At the heart of every patent is an inventor who contributes to our legacy of leading through technology.”


#### Recognizing the people behind the progress


At Graco, innovation starts with its people. The company celebrates and recognizes the employees who develop new ideas and bring them to life.


At the Contractor Equipment Division facility in Rogers, Minnesota, a patent display highlights hundreds of innovations created by Graco inventors. The display serves as a visible reminder of the impact employees make every day.


*Patents from the Contractor Equipment Division are displayed at the Rogers, Minnesota facility.*


Each spring, Graco hosts a patent recognition luncheon to honor Minnesota-based inventors. Similar events take place across global locations.


Inventors can choose how they are recognized for their contributions. Options include a commemorative plaque featuring their name, invention, patent number and issuance date, along with a 1926 Peace silver dollar that marks the year Graco was founded.


Additional recognition options include a patent ring, with a diamond added for each subsequent invention. The ring features the inventor’s name on one side and a patent recognition symbol on the other. Inventors may also choose a cash award.


#### Looking ahead


Innovation remains central to how Graco moves forward. By combining engineering expertise with a strong focus on customer needs, the company continues to develop solutions that help industries operate more efficiently and reliably.


This commitment extends beyond products. Graco fosters a collaborative environment where employees contribute ideas, improve processes and help strengthen the company’s long-standing culture of excellence.
