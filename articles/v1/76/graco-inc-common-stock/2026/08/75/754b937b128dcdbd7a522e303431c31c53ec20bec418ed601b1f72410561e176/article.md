## Graco in the News: Coating and Painting Tips and Expertise


Graco continues to be recognized by leading trade publications for its innovation, expertise and customer-focused approach to solving real-world challenges. Recently, Graco subject matter experts shared insights across several publications.


#### CoatingsPro: Why Preparation Matters: Creating a Foundation for Coatings Success


In *CoatingsPro* , Graco Senior Category Marketing Manager for Protective Coating Equipment Bob Gundersen discusses how ensuring that everything is ready for a coating job is an important part in setting companies up for success.


“Protective coating sprayers are no longer just another tool for the jobsite. They’re an extension of the crew. They can help ensure that prep work is completed consistently, that mixing and conditioning meets specifications, and that less experienced crew members can apply coatings accurately and safely.”


[Click here](https://content.ampp.org/coatingspro/article/26/3/16/108056/Why-Preparation-Matters-Creating-a-Foundation-for) to read the full article.


#### American Painting Contractor (APC): Paint Trends 2026


As each year passes, there are new trends that emerge in the painting industry. In *APC* , Graco Senior Global Category Marketing Manager for Paint Tyler Sterner shared their thoughts on formulation trends. Sterner discussed how low- or zero-VOC formula options have now become an expectation.


“A key macrotrend we’re seeing—especially in cabinetry—is the transition to more durable, faster-drying coating systems. These products reduce time between coats and transform how contractors plan, manage spray space, and schedule reinstallation,” said Sterner. “As coatings advance more quickly, there’s less margin for error, increasing the importance of precise pressure control, consistent atomization, and repeatable.”


[Click here](https://paintmag.com/paint-trends-2026/) to read the full article and learn about other paint trends.


#### Construction Business Owner: A New Workforce Playbook


In *Construction Business Owner* , Graco Senior Category Marketing Manager for Protective Coating Equipment Bob Gundersen reflects on the company’s 100-year legacy and how it has evolved through shifting markets, workforce changes and evolving jobsite demands.


Although tools have changed throughout the past century, one thing remains the same: adaptability and productivity are key for businesses to be successful.


[Click here](https://www.constructionbusinessowner.com/management/new-workforce-playbook) to read the full article.
