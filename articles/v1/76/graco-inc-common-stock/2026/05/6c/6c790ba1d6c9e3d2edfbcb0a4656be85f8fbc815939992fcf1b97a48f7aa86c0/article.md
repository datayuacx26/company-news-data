# A Northwoods Tradition Rooted in Graco Culture


For more than 70 years, a company-owned retreat has reflected Graco’s long-standing commitment to connection, community and its people.


At Graco, culture extends beyond the workplace. One of the company’s most enduring examples is Graco Oakes, a Northwoods retreat that has brought employees, retirees and families together for generations.


Located on a quiet peninsula in Minnesota’s Chippewa National Forest near Remer, Graco Oakes spans over 40 acres and offers something rare: space to slow down, connect and build lasting relationships. For more than seven decades, the retreat has served as a place where Graco employees can step away from day-to-day routines and experience the company’s people-first culture in a meaningful way.


“Not many companies invest in something like this for their employees,” said Josh Waddell, Graco Oakes manager. “There’s a sense of continuity here that you don’t find many places. People come back year after year, and over time it becomes part of their own Graco story.”


#### A retreat built for connection


Graco Oakes blends classic Northwoods tradition with amenities designed for shared experiences, with opportunities for bonfires, fishing, yard games and time outdoors. For many, Graco Oakes has become synonymous with an authentic “up north” experience.


Beyond recreation, the retreat also supports focused collaboration. Teams regularly use the space for meetings and work retreats, taking advantage of the quiet setting to strengthen relationships and align around shared goals.


#### A legacy that continues


Graco Oakes traces its roots to 1955, when Graco founder Russell Gray purchased the property with a clear purpose: to create a getaway for Graco leaders and employees. At the time, the retreat included three cabins. Over the decades, it has grown to seven, evolving with the company while staying true to its original intent.


Today, Graco Oakes remains available to Graco employees, retirees and their families, and underscores the company’s long-term commitment to the people who help move its business forward.


#### Shaped by the people who use it


Graco’s continued investment in Graco Oakes reflects a belief that success is measured by more than products and performance. It is also defined by how a company supports its people.
