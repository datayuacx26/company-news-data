## Ultra® QuickShot™ Recognized in Popular Mechanics Tool Awards 2026


Some tools gain attention because they offer something new. Others, like the Ultra® QuickShot™, earn it because they change the way work is done.


In May 2026, Popular Mechanics recognized Ultra QuickShot in the finishing and fabrication category of its[annual Tool Awards](https://www.popularmechanics.com/home/tools/a71025423/tool-awards-2026/) . The program highlights products that deliver real value to professionals in the field. For Graco, the recognition reflects the company’s long-standing commitment to building solutions that make a difference in the work professionals do.


#### Designed for the moments that matter most on the job


Small jobs often come between larger projects, yet they demand the same level of quality, care and finish. The Ultra QuickShot was designed for these moments. Its lightweight, cordless, and compact design helps professionals complete tasks faster and spray in tight spaces.


The purpose of Ultra QuickShot is simple: to help professionals complete small jobs with the same confidence and quality they expect for larger projects.


#### Recognition rooted in real-world performance


The Popular Mechanics Tool Awards are known for spotlighting solutions that perform in real-world conditions. That is what makes this recognition meaningful. It highlights how the Ultra QuickShot provides real benefits to the people who use it and reinforces Graco’s belief that innovation works best when shaped by customer feedback and experience.


As Graco continues to innovate, recognition like this reinforces that moving materials that matter starts with listening to and supporting the people who move them every day.


#### Delivering on customer feedback


Building on QuickShot’s foundation, Graco recently introduced the new Ultra QuickShot™ II, expanding the lineup with additional capabilities designed to support professionals and evolving jobsite needs.
