# Celebrating 100 Years of Graco: What Else Happened in 1926?


In 1924, Russell Gray invented the[world’s first air-powered grease gun](https://www.graco.com/us/en/about-graco/news/articles/2026/q1/the-invention-of-the-air-powered-grease-gun.html) —a revolutionary tool that transformed vehicle service stations, oil companies and farms. That breakthrough became the foundation for Graco Manufacturing. When the company was founded in 1926, the Graco brothers continued pushing boundaries, creating new products that solved real-world challenges. With Russell at the helm of engineering and[his brother Leil Gray](https://www.graco.com/us/en/about-graco/news/articles/2026/q1/founding-brothers-of-graco-leil-russell.html) serving as president and CEO, **their ingenuity ignited a lasting culture of problem‑solving, craftsmanship and innovation that propelled the business from a small Minneapolis start‑up to a global leader in fluid‑handling technology.**


This year, Graco turns 100—a milestone few companies in manufacturing achieve. Now as a global manufacturer of pumps, sprayers and fluid-handling solutions used across countless industries, our people continue to advance technology and build with the same spirit that started it all.


The year 1926 wasn't just the beginning of Graco—it was a moment of worldwide change and discovery. As we honor our centennial, we’re looking back at other notable moments that shaped the era in which we began.


### Events in the U.S. that Happened in 1926


- The U.S marked its sesquicentennial.
- Calvin Coolidge was president of America’s 48 states.
- The Prohibition era was in full swing during “The Roaring ’20s.”
- The beginnings of the Golden Age of radio.
- The New York Yankees lost to Rogers Hornsby and St. Louis Cardinals 4-3 in the World Series, despite having Babe Ruth and Lou Gehrig.


### What Happened Around the World in 1926


- Joseph Stalin was the leader of the Soviet Union.
- Hirohito became the emperor of Japan.
- The book “Winnie-the-Pooh” by A.A. Milne was published.
- The Charleston dance craze took the world by storm.
- Fire destroyed the Shakespeare Memorial Theater in Stratford-upon-Avon in England
- Harlem Globetrotters basketball team was introduced.


### Business Breakthroughs in 1926


- The National Broadcasting Co. (NBC) became America’s first national radio network, debuting in New York with a broadcast that featured musical and comedic acts.
- The Ford Motor Co. established a 40-hour work week in its automobile plants, becoming one of the first U.S. companies to do so.
- The U.S. Bureau of Public Roads established the first federal highway system.
- Scottish inventor John Logie Baird demonstrated the first television.
- American physicist and inventor Robert H. Goddard successfully tested the first rocket using liquid fuel.
- The first Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT) was administered to more than 8,000 students.
- Automotive antifreeze became commercially available, allowing people to drive their vehicles year-round.
- Aboard a dirigible, Norwegian explorer Roald Amundsen and his crew become the first party recognized to fly over the North Pole and Arctic Ocean.


Graco’s anniversary reflects a century of resilience, innovation and leadership. From the Great Depression to digital transformation, Graco has adapted and thrived through every era. Our story spans global challenges and technology revolutions—emerging stronger every time. Today, we celebrate a legacy built on ingenuity and look forward to shaping the future.
