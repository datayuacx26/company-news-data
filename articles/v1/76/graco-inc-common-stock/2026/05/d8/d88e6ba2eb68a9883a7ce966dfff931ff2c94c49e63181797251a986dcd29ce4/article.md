# Graco Breaks Ground on New Headquarters


A century ago, Graco began with a simple idea and a belief in building things the right way, for the long term. Today, that same mindset continues to guide the company forward, as Graco takes another meaningful step in its journey of growth with the groundbreaking of its new corporate headquarters in Dayton, Minnesota.


The ceremony, held Tuesday, May 12, 2026, marked more than the start of construction. It signaled how Graco is continuing to evolve, bringing people together, investing in collaboration and positioning the company for the next chapter of innovation and progress.


*The Graco executive team breaks ground at the company's ceremony.*


From its roots in Minneapolis to its growing presence across the Twin Cities metro area, Graco’s footprint has expanded alongside its business and global reach. With two facilities already operating in Dayton and others nearby, relocating the company’s headquarters is a natural next step. The move brings leadership teams closer to operations and strengthens day-to-day collaboration across the organization.


But this move is about more than proximity. The new headquarters represents how Graco is intentionally shaping the way it works today and in the future. Designed to support collaboration, decision-making and innovation, the space will serve as a central hub where teams come together to move the business forward with purpose. In an ever-changing environment where digitization is accelerating the flow of information and global competition continues to intensify, this investment positions the business for long-term growth and supports Graco’s ability to quickly adapt without compromising its commitment to quality.


As Graco looks ahead to its next century, the Dayton headquarters stands as a symbol of momentum, honoring a legacy built on reliability and innovation, while creating space for what’s next. It reflects a company that continues to move forward, together with its people, its customers and its communities.
