## A Century Together: One Milestone Celebrated Worldwide


#### A milestone worth celebrating


100 years of successful operations is something that should be celebrated. Graco officially marked its 100-year anniversary on Sunday, April 26, 2026, and made sure to recognize its milestone accomplishment with those who made it possible.


#### Powered by people, performance and purpose


Graco’s ongoing support of the people and industries that move materials that matter is made possible by its employees, expertise, performance and commitment to quality. To mark this milestone, Graco brought teams together across its[global locations](https://www.graco.com/us/en/about-graco/locations.html) to celebrate a century of ingenuity and look ahead to a future of possibility.


#### Honoring the past and looking ahead


The gatherings recognized a shared legacy and a common purpose, while also looking toward what’s to come. Teams marked the milestone in ways that reflected local cultures while reinforcing what connects them all: shared values, respect for the past and confidence in the future. These moments created meaningful opportunities to connect with others and show how far Graco has come and acknowledge the collective effort behind its century of innovation.


Together, these global celebrations reflected the strength of Graco’s people and relationships. Whether through employee-focused events, distributor engagement or moments of recognition, the centennial served as a good reminder that Graco’s success was not built alone, but rather built on collaboration, commitment and a shared belief in moving materials that matter.


#### Learn more about Graco’s century of innovation


As the company enters its next century, that same sense of connection and purpose will be what continues to move the company forward in its next 100 years.
