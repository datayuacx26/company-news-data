## Making What Matters: Steve Wrobel


You know you’ve had a remarkable career when your inventions are being used to help create new inventions. For Graco engineer Steve Wrobel, innovation has never been about novelty for its own sake. For two decades, Wrobel has helped turn creativity into solutions that solve real problems for Graco customers, and he has over 290 granted patents worldwide to prove it.


If engineering lives at the intersection of art, science and business, Wrobel is comfortable at the crossroads.


**Turning creativity into customer-driven innovation**


That mindset took shape long before his name appeared on a Graco patent. As a child, Wrobel watched his father take apart a tractor and rebuild it piece by piece. That hands-on approach to curiosity left a lasting impression. “He could fix anything mechanically,” Wrobel recalls. “There’s that history of using the creative side of your brain.”


**Built to design**


Wrobel’s path into engineering started early. In high school, Wrobel took independent study classes in design before attending technical school for drafting and design. His first job out of school was with an engineering firm where clients often arrived with little more than an idea to be made into a solution.


That variety helped shape his approach. One of his first design projects was the Z-plate, the essential platform component for 3D printing systems, in the early years of additive manufacturing. Other projects had Wrobel designing charging carts for an airline and even a machine to make shave ice. While some innovations varied, Wrobel credits the variety for his success. “I can think outside the box because I’ve got knowledge in a wide variety of areas,” he says, a perspective that continues to shape his work at Graco.


**Engineering with purpose at Graco**


Wrobel joined Graco after a former coworker described the opportunity to build products that would be used every day on job sites. His first Graco patent began with a simple idea: turning a paint sprayer into something more than a paint sprayer.


To reach inaccessible areas, contractors often stood on five-gallon buckets, a solution that was unsafe and inefficient. Wrobel designed a step that attaches directly to the sprayer frame, adding functionality without adding complexity. “It was about adding more utility,” he explains. “A step stool and a sprayer in one.”


Seeing his name on that first patent brought pride, but more importantly, validation. “It’s kind of a stamp of approval that you’re able to do something nobody else has ever done.”


**From customer need to patented solution**


In line with Graco’s philosophy of customer-driven innovation, many of Wrobel’s patented ideas begin with a pressing customer need. During the COVID-19 pandemic, demand surged for sprayers capable of applying disinfectants. The unique challenge: protecting battery-powered sprayers from corrosive chemicals.


Wrobel designed an enclosure to shield batteries from moisture and chemical exposure. The idea was not only quickly patented but later adopted by longtime Graco partner DeWalt for use in pressure washers. This innovation is a clear example of how customer needs drive innovation beyond a single application.


Behind every patent is collaboration. Wrobel credits Graco’s intellectual property team for helping turn ideas into defensible innovation. “You have to research the history. Has someone done it before, and is there a way around that?”


**Prototyping at the speed of innovation**


Rapid experimentation plays a critical role in that process. The same 3D printing solutions Wrobel helped design early in his career now help bring prototypes of new ideas to life under time constraints. Additive manufacturing allows Wrobel to print, assemble, test and refine concepts before investing in tooling.


While smart technologies, artificial intelligence and advanced materials continue to evolve, Wrobel believes in the human side of innovation. “It’s still a person-based process,” he says. “You need that inventive mindset.”


And when innovation builds on innovation, creativity becomes a legacy.
