## Graco Marks 100 Years with Monumental Land Art by Saype


Reaching 100 years is more than a milestone for Graco. It is an opportunity to reflect on the company’s legacy, the partnerships that shaped its success and the purpose that continues to guide its future.


As part of its centennial celebration, Graco partnered with world-renowned artist[Saype](https://en.saype-artiste.com/) to create a monumental artwork in Dayton, Minnesota, the future home of Graco’s global headquarters. The installation marks Saype’s first land art project in Minnesota and stands as a visual expression of connection, collaboration and shared purpose.


#### Art designed to connect people and places


Saype’s work is known for its scale and meaning. Designed to be experienced from above, the art transforms landscapes into meaningful messages temporarily, fading back into the environment over time.


Creating the artwork required extensive planning, precise measurement and precise execution. Using Graco paint sprayers, Saype brought the artwork to life.


*The Ultra 795 XT that was used to create the land art. Photo credit: Saype*


*The Powershot XT that was used to create the land art. Photo credit: Saype*


#### “By Our Hands,” a global message from Minnesota


The finished piece, titled *By Our Hands* , spans 210-by-182-feet. It depicts two hands stretching a single thread across a world map, weaving connections across continents. From the air, the image reflects both the scale of the artwork and the idea that progress is achieved through shared effort and collaboration.


“The creation of art does not rest solely in the hands of the artist,” Saype said. “It depends on an entire chain of expertise. It is together that we weave something on the scale of the world.”


*Land art titled By Our Hands created by artist Saype. Photo credit: Saype*


#### A reflection of Graco


That message is a reflection of how Graco operates. For a century, the company has grown by working with customers, distributors and employees across the globe. Today, Graco serves customers in more than 100 countries on six continents, supporting industries that keep the world moving.


Placing the artwork in Dayton carries added meaning for Graco. Minnesota is where Graco’s story began, and it remains central to the company’s future. As Graco prepares for its next 100 years, the land art serves as both a tribute to its roots as well as a guide to what lies ahead.


#### Looking ahead to the next century


The centennial is not only about celebrating past achievements, but about recognizing the responsibility that comes with a global presence. Graco’s products, solutions and expertise extend far beyond any one location and help customers solve challenges.


*By Our Hands* offers a visual reminder of that shared commitment. It reflects the belief that success is built together and that moving materials that matter depends on strong connections across people, industries and borders.


As Graco looks enters its second century, the artwork stands as a lasting expression of the company’s purpose and its continued commitment to helping move materials that matter.
