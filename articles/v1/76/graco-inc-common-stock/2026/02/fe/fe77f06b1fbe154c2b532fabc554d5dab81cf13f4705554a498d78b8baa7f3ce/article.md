## The Graco Foundation: Partnerships That Matter


Investing in our communities to move the world forward isn’t just something we do to give back. From continuing education to workforce training, Graco understands cultivating relationships within our communities lays the groundwork for tomorrow’s innovations. For 70 years, the[Graco Foundation](https://www.graco.com/us/en/about-graco/foundation.html) has supported institutions and programs that align with our vital work. They create opportunities, strengthen communities and empower individuals.


Since its inception, the Graco Foundation has given away more than $50 million to organizations in the communities we serve. Over time, the foundation has refined its approach, focusing on recipients who align with our[Purpose, Vision and Values](https://www.graco.com/us/en/about-graco/purpose-vision-values.html) . To this end, the Graco Foundation focuses on three specific areas: STEM education, workforce development and youth empowerment.


From supporting ethics in business education to funding critical health initiatives and earning national recognition for corporate philanthropy, Graco’s values have shaped decades of impact. Some highlights include:


**Summit Academy OIC:** Free job training and GED prep for unemployed or underemployed individuals, with 1,000+ students trained annually **.**


**Minnesota Diversified Industries:** Employment opportunities for people with disabilities through manufacturing and packaging services. ****


**East Side Neighborhood Services:** Child care, after-school programs, food distribution and job training for families and older adults.


**Twin Cities Rise:** Assistance for individuals facing employment barriers to secure stable, well-paid jobs. ****


**Achieve Twin Cities:** Guides youth toward post-secondary education and career success


**University of St. Thomas:** $500,000 gift to University of St. Thomas to create a business ethics chair (matched by CEO David Koch).


**United Way:** Earned Spirit of America Summit Award for nearly $600,000 in contributions across nine cities.


The foundation has also expanded its reach by empowering employees, matching donations of up to $2,500 annually. Our Dollars for Doers program contributes up to $1,000 to organizations where our employees volunteer.


On top of that, the foundation supports family members of Graco employees seeking to further their education. For the 2025-2026 academic year, the total scholarship donation for employee children this year is $244,000. Every grant, scholarship, and volunteer hour represents a commitment to lives that matter.


Success is a joint effort, and partnerships that matter are the foundation of our future. Through the Graco Foundation, we are investing in education, workforce readiness and community programs that move lives forward. When companies and communities thrive together, everyone wins.[Learn more about the Graco Foundation](https://www.graco.com/us/en/about-graco/foundation.html) .
