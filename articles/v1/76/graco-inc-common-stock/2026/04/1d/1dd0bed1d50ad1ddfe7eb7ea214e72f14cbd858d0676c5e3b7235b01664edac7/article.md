## Graco Centennial Products Celebrate Century of Ingenuity


From a garage start-up in 1926 to a global leader in fluid handling solutions, Graco’s 100-year journey has been defined by our commitment to moving materials that matter. It isn’t often a company gets to celebrate 100 years of innovation, reliability, and partnership, so we want to give you a chance to celebrate with us.


We’re honoring our legacy with a special lineup of centennial-branded products that symbolize our commitment to excellence and our vision for the future. These limited-edition products aren’t just a new look. They reflect a reputation earned over a century of performance.


For manufacturing professionals and contractors alike, Graco equipment has always stood for durability, precision, and efficiency. We aren’t just celebrating our products, but the industries and people who have relied on Graco to move the world forward.


The centennial collection includes some of our most trusted solutions:


**Ultra Electric Airless Paint Sprayers** : Ultra 390, 395, 490 XT and 695 XT models deliver the speed and finish contractors demand.


**Mark V XT HD 3-in-1 ProContractor Sprayer** : Built for heavy-duty jobs, a cornerstone for professionals tackling large-scale projects with texture and other tough coatings.


**King PC 70:1 Sprayer** : Engineered for industrial applications where power and reliability are non-negotiable.


**LineLazer 3400 Gas Airless Line Striper** : A favorite for precision striping in road and utility work.


Stellair Manual Guns: A paint gun built for small-batch jobs that require superior finishes.


**Oil King:** Portable, heavy-duty oil drain designed to keep up with service shops and increase productivity. ****


**Coolant King** : Portable antifreeze drain with corrosion-resistant design for long service life.


Our centennial isn’t just about honoring the past, it’s about shaping the next 100 years of customer-focused innovation. As your needs evolve and new challenges emerge, Graco remains committed to engineering solutions that are made to move what matters.
