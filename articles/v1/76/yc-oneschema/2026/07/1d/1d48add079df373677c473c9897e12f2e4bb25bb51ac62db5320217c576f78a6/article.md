# Announcing OneSchema FileFeeds: Automate your CSV Imports

Our flagship product,[OneSchema Importer](https://www.oneschema.co/embeddable-importer) , is now powering millions of file imports across high-growth startups to multinational enterprises. We are excited to launch our newest product,[OneSchema FileFeeds](https://www.oneschema.co/filefeeds) , to expand our product offering for recurring CSV import use cases.[See the demo in action](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s22ZcDI3cQk) .


## **What is OneSchema FileFeeds?**


OneSchema FileFeeds allows you to automate recurring CSV imports. Launch an integration with any business system in 15 minutes, as long as your customer can export a CSV to an SFTP folder.
**‍**


## **How does it work?**


- To create a file feed, build a validation template and record yourself fixing errors in a sample input file. We’ll replay the transformations on all future files.
- Errors get sent directly to your customer to correct, instead of having to involve your engineering team.


‍


## **What pain does FileFeeds solve?**


- Make your SFTP/file integrations stable and reliable
- Get your eng team out of building/maintaining hacky custom integrations
- Make it easier for your customer to integrate with you (no more asking for their IT resources or security approvals for their core systems)


‍


## **What are top use cases for OneSchema FileFeeds?**


- **Tricky integrations** Imagine you’re a benefits tech company that is onboarding a multinational enterprise. They don’t have IT resources to allow you to integrate with their HRIS to sync employees data, so you can only integrate by exporting a CSV to an SFTP folder.
- **Recurring CSVs to an email** You’re a marketplace and many of your vendors send you updated prices as a CSV to an email address. Your team is currently manually detaching CSVs from the emails and inputting the data into your system
- **Aggregating 3P data sources** For companies where data is their primary service, you’ll receive 3P data as CSV to an SFTP folder. OneSchema FileFeeds allows you to monitor the uploads to make sure they are received on-time and with the correct data.
