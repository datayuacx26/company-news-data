# Blackstone Announces Investment in FUTRONIC, Korea’s Leading Actuator and Motion Control Technology Provider

SEOUL – July 20, 2026 – Blackstone (NYSE:BX) announced today that private equity funds affiliated with Blackstone (“Blackstone”) have entered into a definitive agreement to make a significant investment in FUTRONIC (“FUTRONIC” or “the Company”), a leading supplier of high-precision actuators serving the global automotive and industrial robotics industries. This transaction is in partnership with FUTRONIC’s Founder, Jin-ho Ko, who will remain in his role as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer and work with Blackstone to accelerate the Company’s global growth and end-market expansion.


Founded in 1993, FUTRONIC has successfully evolved into a trusted R&D and supply chain partner for many of the world’s most recognized automotive OEM platforms and their networks. Today, it plays a critical role in early-stage engineering with its customers across broader mobility end-markets, including humanoids and general robotics. With primary operations in Korea and the United States, the Company continues to actively expand its international footprint.


Jin-ho Ko, Founder, Chairman & Chief Executive Officer, FUTRONIC, said: “This partnership with Blackstone marks a special moment in FUTRONIC’s journey and is testament to our highly talented engineers and the growth potential for our company. Blackstone shares our vision of delivering advanced mechatronic solutions to our valued customers and partners, and their unique scale and global platform will help drive our expansion and solidify our leadership in actuation and motion control solutions.”


Eugene Cook, Head of Korea for Blackstone Private Equity, said: “We are honored to partner with Chairman Ko and FUTRONIC’s leadership team who have spent more than 30 years building a leading position in the global actuator industry. FUTRONIC represents the very best of Korea’s mechatronics industry, with impressive R&D and engineering capabilities that position it well for rapid growth across automotive and robotics markets. This partnership reinforces Blackstone’s commitment to backing visionary entrepreneurs and family-owned businesses, using our scale, operational expertise, and expert networks.”


Kyungmin Song, Principal, Blackstone Private Equity, said: “Humanoids and broader automation are still in the early innings of exponential growth, and FUTRONIC can play an important role for its world-renowned current and future customers. The convergence of Artificial Intelligence and the physical world is a key investment theme for Blackstone’s Private Equity business, and we believe FUTRONIC’s motion control solutions will be an important enabler of this megatrend.”
