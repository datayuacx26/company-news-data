As your startup grows beyond its scrappy beginnings, your compensation needs evolve, too. While Market Data Lite supports early-stage companies, there comes a point when growth requires more sophisticated tools.


Here are seven clear signals that indicate 2026 is the right time to switch to[Market Data Pro.](https://pave.com/products/market-data-pro)


## 1. You're Expanding Into Multiple Markets or Countries


​​When your team was based in a single city, a single-location benchmark cut was sufficient. But as you hire across new cities or internationally, compensation becomes significantly more complex.[Geographic pay differentials](https://www.pave.com/blog-posts/what-are-geographic-pay-differentials) —the variations in compensation for the same job across different locations—require careful analysis and administration.


As companies globalize and embrace remote or hybrid working models, understanding location-based pay strategies becomes critical. Pave's research shows that[88% of companies take location into account](https://pave.com/blog-posts/compensating-distributed-employees-how-a-location-based-pay-strategy-can-help) when determining compensation. Market Data Pro includes geographic pay-differential tools that provide job-by-job data, helping you maintain consistency while adapting to local labor markets.


## 2. You're Competing for New and Emerging Talent


The talent landscape is shifting rapidly, particularly in technology. Pave research reveals that between January 2023 and July 2025, the percentage of employees under the age of 25 at large public tech companies was cut in half as[AI reshaped workforce composition](https://pave.com/blog-posts/ai-is-disrupting-the-workforce-from-the-bottom-up) . Meanwhile, new roles, such as[Prompt Engineer](https://app.pave.com/pave-data-lab/prompt-engineer) and[AI/ML jobs](https://app.pave.com/pave-data-lab/ai-ml-titles-over-time) , are emerging rapidly.


You need real-time compensation data to compete for sought-after talent and keep up with new roles that may not appear in traditional surveys. Market Data Pro equips you with timely insights into how competitors are paying for these positions, so you can confidently make strong offers and remain ahead in fast-changing talent markets.


## 3. You're Building Executive and Leadership Teams


Hiring your first C-suite and executive leaders is a pivotal moment. Executive compensation packages vary widely across companies, typically comprising base salary, variable pay tied to business goals, equity grants, and specialized benefits. Companies in Pave's dataset spend an average of 21% of compensation costs on[executive base salary alone](https://pave.com/blog-posts/what-is-executive-compensation-getting-started) .


Effective executive compensation benchmarking ensures you attract and retain leaders while remaining competitive. Market Data Pro enables you to[create tailored peer groups](https://pave.com/blog-posts/executive-compensation-peer-groups-why-they-matter-how-to-build-them) and access precise executive compensation data, empowering you to build well-structured, attractive leadership packages.

## 4. You're Implementing Variable Compensation Programs


Transitioning from base salary to structured bonus programs requires robust market intelligence. Design short-term incentive plans that balance driving performance with managing risk, since poorly designed programs can encourage the wrong behaviors or demotivate employees if payouts are unattainable.


The[best bonus plans are built from business strategy](https://pave.com/blog-posts/key-considerations-for-designing-effective-bonus-programs) , focused goals, and transparent communication. Market Data Pro enables you to benchmark target bonuses by job level, function, and location, ensuring you design programs that both motivate employees and prevent overpaying.


## 5. You're Establishing Your Equity Refresh Philosophy


As employees near vesting cliffs, shift retention strategy from new-hire grants to refresh programs. What works for a 10-person startup often does not sustain you as you approach IPO. The median IPO journey now stretches to 13 years—well beyond typical four-year vesting schedules.


Pave data reveals that about half of[private companies with 500–3,000 employees use RSUs](https://pave.com/blog-posts/equity-that-grows-with-you-how-to-design-grant-strategies-that-scale-from-seed-to-pre-ipo) as their main equity award. Market Data Pro delivers clear[equity program benchmarks](https://pave.com/blog-posts/equity-compensation-trends-uncovering-the-data-on-equity-program-design) that help you model dilution, plan equity burn, and build compelling refresh programs that keep high performers engaged longer.


## 6. You Need Sophisticated Equity Modeling


As your employee equity gets more complex, understanding your equity position relative to the market is essential. Companies such as[Upstart have leveraged Pave's Market Data for real-time insights](https://pave.com/case-studies/upstart) that support confident transformation of equity programs.


Market Data Pro includes advanced equity tools beyond basic benchmarking: unvested equity holdings, equity burn rates, vesting structures, and vesting schedules. These capabilities help you model dilution scenarios and ensure your equity program remains competitive without excessive dilution.


## 7. You Require Integrated Planning Tools


If your compensation cycles still rely on spreadsheets, you're sacrificing efficiency and potentially accuracy. Companies such as[Databricks have leveraged Pave to build](https://pave.com/blog-posts/implementing-new-compensation-management-system-lessons-from-databricks) flexible, scalable, and transparent pay programs that keep pace with hypergrowth.


Participants at[Total Rewards Live noted that](https://pave.com/blog-posts/trends-impacting-compensation-insights-from-total-rewards-live) pay transparency is about more than just ranges; it’s about fostering understanding among employees. Market Data Pro integrates into your existing planning process, simplifying transitions and increasing your team’s trust in your pay strategies with clear, effective tools.


## Conclusion


The transition from Market Data Lite to Pro isn't just about accessing more data—it's about aligning your compensation infrastructure with your company's evolving needs. With enhanced tools and insights, you can attract, retain, and motivate the talent propelling your next stage of growth. By upgrading, you position your organization to stay competitive and responsive in a dynamic market, ensuring your rewards strategy scales seamlessly with your business.
