# From Offer to Vesting: How Pave Ensures Compensation Value Is Always Clear

Recent research from Pave shows candidates review their offer an average of 10 times before signing, but engagement drops to only a few times per year afterward. Only[32% of employees believe their pay is fair](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/11-28-22-gartner-hr-research-finds-only-thirty-two-percent-of-employees-believe-their-pay-is-fair) , and just 38% understand how it is determined. The issue is not the compensation itself, but that its value is no longer communicated after the offer is signed.


This post outlines our product solution. It details[Visual Offer Letter](https://pave.com/products/visual-offer-letter) ,[Total Rewards](https://www.pave.com/products/total-rewards) , and[Team View](https://pave.com/products/team-view) , and explains how[The Pave Agent](https://www.pave.com/products/pave-agent) supports each stage to ensure compensation value is consistently communicated from the initial offer through every vesting, raise, and promotion.


## Visual Offer Letter: Secure Decisions and Set Expectations


The offer stage is when candidates are most engaged, with 56% revisiting their offer on multiple days.[Visual Offer Letters](https://pave.com/products/visual-offer-letter) are designed to support this engagement:


- **Comprehensive package visualization.** Base salary, target bonus, new hire equity, and benefits are presented in a single interactive view, providing a clear total compensation overview. This transparency reduces follow-up questions and accelerates offer acceptance.
- **Equity modeling tool** . The interactive equity explorer enables candidates to assess the potential value of their new hire grant over time and under various company growth scenarios, without requiring specialized knowledge.
- **Transparent benefits breakdown.** Each benefit includes a name, description, and annual value, tailored by employee group and location, and incorporated into total compensation. This ensures all benefits, such as 401(k) matches and wellness stipends, are clearly communicated.
- **Custom branding.** Organizations can use custom colors, imagery, and messaging, along with an About Us section to highlight mission and values, ensuring employer branding begins before the first day.
- **Integrated recruiting workflows.** Native integrations with Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby allow recruiters to generate personalized offers quickly and efficiently.


## Total Rewards: Extending the Offer Experience Throughout Employment


While most companies revert to static documents after hiring,[Total Rewards](https://www.pave.com/products/total-rewards) maintains a personalized, quantified experience beyond the initial offer:


- **Continuous access portal.** Employees can view their up-to-date total compensation, including salary, bonus, equity, and benefits, at any time. **‍**
- **Clear equity insights.** Employees can view both vested and unvested equity, examine individual grants, and model future value, reinforcing the ongoing value of their compensation. **‍**
- **Reward letters.** When compensation changes due to merit, promotion, or refresh, employees receive the same visual, personalized communication as with their initial offer, ensuring transparency behind each change. **‍**
- **The Learning Hub.** Employees can explore their job ladder and competencies, view previous, current, and next levels with target compensation, and access your compensation philosophy. Compensation teams can assess understanding through anonymous polling and feedback. **‍**
- **Aligned with employee timelines.** Engagement data shows 75% of rewards views occur within 30 days of the merit cycle. Total Rewards encourages ongoing engagement between pay events, such as vests, enrollment, and promotions.


## Team View: Equip Managers with Comprehensive Compensation Insights


Eighty-four percent of employers rely on managers to communicate pay; only 38% believe they've trained them well (WTW).[Team View](https://pave.com/products/team-view) closes that gap with context, not scripts:


- **Centralized compensation records.** Team View consolidates compensation data from HRIS, cap tables, and previous cycle spreadsheets, allowing managers to view each employee’s complete compensation history on a unified timeline. This ensures managers and employees share the same perspective.
- **Detailed equity insights.** The updated equity view provides managers with clear, grant-level information on each employee’s current and potential equity value, enabling specific and credible discussions.
- **Compensation band visibility.** Managers can see each employee’s position within the compensation band, providing context for pay decisions and helping identify retention risks early.


The payoff shows up in the data: employees whose managers communicate about compensation frequently view their total rewards at nearly double the rate—98% vs. 47%—and companies that added manager-led communications saw engagement grow 25% more year-over-year.


## Across All Three: The Pave Agent and Pave AI


[The Pave Agent](https://www.pave.com/products/pave-agent) is the AI compensation analyst within the platform and serves as the central resource for communication:


- **On-demand analysis with transparency.** When a manager or compensation partner needs to explain a raise, market positioning, or grant value, the Agent provides analysis with supporting data and rationale. It offers recommendations and supporting details, while your team makes the final decision and communicates the outcome.
- **Priority Talent Review, powered by Pave AI.** Rather than requiring managers to review every record, Pave consolidates compensation signals into targeted assessments, highlighting key individuals, issues, and recommended actions. Managers review and make informed decisions.
- **Rapid scenario exploration.** Compensation teams use the Agent to test hypotheses and evaluate scenarios across multiple data sources, significantly reducing manual analysis time.


All AI capabilities in Pave are advisory, not autonomous. The Agent provides defensible recommendations for human review and never sets or changes compensation independently.


## A Unified Experience, Not Separate Tools


These products deliver a consistent, personalized, and quantified compensation view to candidates, employees, and managers. While each can address specific needs, their value increases when used together, ensuring a seamless experience from offer to ongoing employment.
