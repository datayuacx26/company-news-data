# LemonSlice x ElevenLabs

## **Why ElevenLabs**


Lemon Slice partnered with ElevenLabs to handle the entire voice interaction layer for their avatars - including voice activity detection, interruption handling, and low-latency speech synthesis - without needing to develop voice infrastructure internally.


“ElevenLabs had the rare combination of an agent platform that was easy to start with but could also handle production complexity: tool calling, custom MCP servers, knowledge bases. The developer experience was seamless. And the voices are obviously great.” - Lina Colucci, CEO, Lemon Slice


## **Accelerating time to market**


With the ElevenLabs[Agents Platform](https://elevenlabs.io/agents) , Lemon Slice shipped its platform to production in a fraction of the time it would have taken to build in-house. The integration provides advanced functionality out of the box, including tool calling, custom MCP servers, and knowledge base connections.


“We’re proud to partner with ElevenLabs and launch this new interactive avatar model to the world. It’s a streaming diffusion transformer model that plugs directly and exclusively into ElevenLabs Agents to give[voice agents](https://elevenlabs.io/voice-agents) a ‘face’” - Lina Colucci, CEO, Lemon Slice
