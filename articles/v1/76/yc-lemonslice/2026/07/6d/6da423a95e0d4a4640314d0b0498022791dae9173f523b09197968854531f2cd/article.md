# Shipping Log - Week 27 - POTUS talks to our avatar

Hi guys,


What a week! Here are some of the highlights:


1. **The President of the United States talked to our avatar!** This was wild. President Trump was supposed to talk to AI Teddy Roosevelt for 1 minute, but ended up talking to it for nearly 15 minutes.[Here's a clip](https://x.com/MargoMartin47/status/2072393375244804320) .

Brad Smith, President of Microsoft, said to me during his visit, “\[The avatar\] you created is amazing. It *is* state of the art. It has been such a pleasure for us at Microsoft to work with LemonSlice.” See coverage in[Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lesliekatz/2026/07/01/ai-powered-theodore-roosevelt-is-ready-to-answer-your-questions/) ,[Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/president-trump-chats-with-ai-teddy-roosevelt-about-the-panama-canal-2000780299) ,[CNET](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/teddy-roosevelt-ai-avatar-donald-trump-interaction/) , and more.
