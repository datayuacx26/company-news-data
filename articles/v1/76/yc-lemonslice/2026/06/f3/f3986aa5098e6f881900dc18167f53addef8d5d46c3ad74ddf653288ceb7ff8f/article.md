# Shipping Log - Week 26

Hey all,


Here’s what we shipped this week:


**1. We dramatically reduced avatar connection times!** Many of you asked for this. The median connection time from pressing “start call” to getting an avatar to appear is now <3 sec! And I’m especially proud that our p99 is <10s, which is a nearly 30s improvement!


Based on our testing, users are fine waiting 10s as long as you have a ringing sound and “ringing screen UI” implemented (saying “loading” or “connecting” is not as good).


If you’re seeing connection times that are longer than this: 1) let us know and 2) it’s likely other parts of your pipeline.


Read the full details:[https://lemonslice.com/blog/connection-time](https://lemonslice.com/blog/connection-time)


**2. Bring your avatars into a zoom call (beta).** Do you want to invite your avatars as participants in a zoom call? We just released a closed beta version of this feature. Email me if you want to try it out. I had a lot of fun chatting with this little alien -[check it out](https://www.loom.com/share/a511ff85340a4800a19e343b915e0ee6) :)
