# Shipping Log - Week 32

Hey all,


Here’s what we shipped this week.


**1. Make horizontal avatars.** We shipped a new greenscreen playground that allows you to easily make your avatars horizontal (or any other aspect ratio). Once you’re happy with the look of it, we provide the exact code to paste into Claude or Cursor to get it deployed into production. It’s fun to play with -[check it out](https://lemonslice.com/docs/reference/green-screen#no-code-green-screen-editor) !


**2. New usage page.** It’s now way easier to spot your usage patterns and dig into specific sessions. This redesign was long overdue and I’m so happy we finally did it.[Take a look](https://lemonslice.com/avatars/usage) and LMK what else you’d want to see.


**3. Rate your calls.** After a call on our web app, you’ll now see a thumbs up / down button allowing you to rate it. Please use it! We work hard every day to make the best possible avatars and I read every piece of feedback to determine what we ship next.


Happy building!
- Lina
