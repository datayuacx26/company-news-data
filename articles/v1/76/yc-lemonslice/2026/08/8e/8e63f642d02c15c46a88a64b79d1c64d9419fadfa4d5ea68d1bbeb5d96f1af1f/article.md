# LemonSlice x Agora

We’re excited to announce a partnership between Agora and LemonSlice. Agora is a global leader in real-time engagement and Conversational AI, providing the technology and infrastructure behind ultra-low-latency voice, video, interactive livestreaming, and human-to-AI experiences at global scale. They power global customers like Whatnot (#1 live selling platform in the US) and Talkspace (#1 rated online therapy with 1M+ users). Its infrastructure supported the[largest live shopping stream in U.S. history](https://medium.com/whatnot-engineering/scaling-whatnot-behind-the-largest-live-shopping-stream-in-us-history-040a458f538c) —bringing 582K+ concurrent viewers together for Whatnot’s MrBeast livestream.


‍


LemonSlice is the leader in[interactive avatars](https://www.lemonslice.com/) : digital characters that can talk, listen, and react in real-time during a video call. LemonSlice powers Microsoft’s AI Teddy Roosevelt hologram and Y Combinator’s AI Office Hours. Using LemonSlice’s Character World Models, developers can add an interactive character of any style into their products.


‍


Today, you can use LemonSlice avatars directly within Agora. Agora’s[Conversational AI Engine](https://www.agora.io/en/products/conversational-ai-engine/) allows developers to easily build AI chatbots and voice agents by combining any LLM, TTS, and STT models. With the new LemonSlice-Agora integration, developers can now add LemonSlice avatars to their conversational AI experiences and then deploy them across Agora’s robust, global infrastructure.


‍


Live Play Mobile is already using the two platforms to build avatars that host 24/7 live gaming entertainment and has achieved comparable engagement metrics to real human hosts.


‍


### **What developers can build**


Together, Agora and LemonSlice give developers the building blocks to create interactive, face-to-face AI experiences that can see, speak, listen, and respond in real time.


**Gaming** — Bring AI-powered characters and NPCs to life with expressive avatars that players can naturally talk to in real time.


**Live Selling** — Create AI shopping hosts and brand ambassadors that can showcase products, answer questions, and engage shoppers through interactive video experiences.


**Live Entertainment** — Build virtual hosts, creators, and characters that can interact with audiences in real time 24/7, opening up new formats for live shows, social experiences, and interactive content.


**Sales & Product Demos** — Deploy always-on AI sales reps and product experts that can deliver personalized demos, answer questions, qualify prospects, and guide customers through products face-to-face.
