# LemonSlice Actions

LemonSlice avatars can now do any action in real-time. Our avatars have always had natural, expressive hand gestures when they speak. Actions allow you to trigger more specific things like pointing, waving, cheering, or jumping.

### Try it for yourself


The photorealistic character on our homepage has several built-in actions. Chat with her and see if you can spot these actions:


- Waving at the start of the call
- Swaying
- Head tilt during listening


These all trigger automatically during a conversation.


Separately, you can trigger 3 other actions - dance, turn, wave - by pressing a button on the side panel or asking the avatar to “dance for me.”

### How to create actions


Actions must be onboarded specifically for a character. The process involves sending LemonSlice 2-5x videos demonstrating the action. It can be a human doing the action even if your character is an animal, which makes onboarding quite easy. Each action has a cost for both onboarding and continued use.
