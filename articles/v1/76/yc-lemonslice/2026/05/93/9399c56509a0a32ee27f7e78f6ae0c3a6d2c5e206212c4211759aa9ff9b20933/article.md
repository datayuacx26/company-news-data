# Shipping Log - Week 22

Hey all!


The team has been working hard to improve your experience with LemonSlice across the board: better model quality, better documentation, new integrations, and more.


I wanted to share a few quick updates you can take advantage of today.


**1. Cookbook with open source demos.** There are so many applications of interactive avatars, it can be hard to know where to get started. To provide a bit of inspiration, we've released a[Cookbook](https://d13291b44c837fdb81cedf9f335c7b6b.us-east-1.resend-links.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flemonslice.com%2Fdocs%2Fguides%2Fcookbook/1/0100019e756b9384-9d4a1714-4e77-4fed-aaad-9b7e4d2540e5-000000/rvj5AsAYv9BW7ZpPXMYv85KYmmx2b0_IGtTZjACazos=452) where you can find recipes to help you build all sorts of products with LemonSlice avatars. The code is completely open source, and we'll be adding many more recipes in the coming weeks. Check it out!


**2. Better Guides for advanced features.** For developers who want to bring their experiences to the next level, we offer customized actions you can trigger programmatically during a real-time call. Check out our latest Guide to learn about the many ways you can integrate actions into your application:[How to Use LemonSlice Actions](https://d13291b44c837fdb81cedf9f335c7b6b.us-east-1.resend-links.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Flemonslice.com%2Fdocs%2Fguides%2Factions-guide/1/0100019e756b9384-9d4a1714-4e77-4fed-aaad-9b7e4d2540e5-000000/aq-IwieM7jGMA9FjUheW9GnzQkDSEwJVkqxFVfATGEM=452)


**3. Improved video quality.** We have been quietly shipping model updates that will improve the overall quality of your avatar call. Teeth in particular should look noticeably better.


**4. Better transcript alignment with LiveKit.** Developers who use LemonSlice with LiveKit will now see better time alignment for streamed transcriptions / subtitles. Upgrade to the latest LiveKit SDK to start taking advantage of this today.


Have a great weekend, and please reach out with issues, questions or feature requests!


Thanks!


- Lina
