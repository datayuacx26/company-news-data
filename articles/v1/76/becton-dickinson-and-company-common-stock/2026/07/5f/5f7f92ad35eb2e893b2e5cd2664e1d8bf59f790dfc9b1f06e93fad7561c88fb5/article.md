# BD Named to TIME’s 2026 List of America’s Best Companies

**FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J.** **(July 9, 2026)** – BD (Becton, Dickinson and Company) (NYSE: BDX), a leading global medical technology company, has been named among the top-performing U.S.-based companies by *TIME.* The recognition reflects a data-driven evaluation of thousands of companies across employee satisfaction, financial performance and corporate sustainability.


“This honor validates the powerful connection between our people and the performance we deliver to customers, clinicians and ultimately, patients,” said Manish Sinha, executive vice president and chief people officer for BD. “Our 60,000 global associates bring a shared commitment to excellence that drives innovation and empowers BD to advance the future of healthcare.”


Organized by *TIME* and Statista, a statistics portal and industry ranking provider, top organizations like BD were named to the ‘America’s Best Companies 2026’ list based on three primary dimensions:


1. **Employee Satisfaction** : Approximately 217,000 employee surveys assessing workplace culture and employer reputation.
2. **Financial Performance** : Revenue growth, profitability and asset performance using multi-year financial data.
3. **Corporate Sustainability** : Environmental impact, social responsibility and governance practices using standardized metrics.


This accolade demonstrates progress toward BD’s 2030+ corporate sustainability goals and commitments, including programs to maintain a healthy and thriving workforce that cultivates our culture of inclusion, safety and well-being.
