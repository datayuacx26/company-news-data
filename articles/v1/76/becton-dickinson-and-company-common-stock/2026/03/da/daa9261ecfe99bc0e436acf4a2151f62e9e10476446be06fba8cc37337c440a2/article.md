# BD Named a Top 100 Global Innovator by LexisNexis

*2026 recognition underscores the strength of BD’s innovation engine and its contributions to sustainable growth, margin expansion and competitive advantage*


**FRANKLIN LAKES, N.J.** **(March 6, 2026)** – BD (Becton, Dickinson and Company) (NYSE: BDX), a leading global medical technology company, today announced that it was named a Top 100 Global Innovator by LexisNexis for 2026. This is the fourth consecutive year that BD has been recognized. The distinction reflects the strength of BD’s innovation portfolio and highlights the company’s momentum in advancing technologies that support long‑term growth and elevate the standard of care for healthcare providers and patients.


The LexisNexis Top 100 Global Innovators list evaluates companies based on improvements in patent quality rather than the number of patents filed. The analysis uses the Patent Asset Index methodology to measure the strength, influence and global relevance of patent assets across more than seventeen million global patent families. The 2026 list and accompanying Innovation Momentum report also examines global inventor collaboration trends and emerging innovation dynamics.


"Being named a Top Global Innovator validates the creativity and impact of our teams who are solving healthcare's toughest challenges through innovations that simplify and personalize patient care,” said Elizabeth McCombs, executive vice president and chief technology officer at BD. “We have sharpened our focus by partnering closely with customers to advance innovative platforms and products that help their businesses thrive, improve patient outcomes, and deliver stronger returns to our shareholders.”


BD’s innovation strategy remains closely aligned with the company’s long-term growth priorities. The company continues to advance platforms in burgeoning areas such as smart connected care, AI-enabled solutions, robotics and automation, medication management, and high growth therapeutic categories. These technology investments support BD’s ability to expand in higher margin, higher growth segments and accelerate the company’s evolution into the world’s premier MedTech leader.


“Innovation can sometimes feel like an abstract concept that is hard to measure and even harder to improve,” said Marco Richter, Senior Director of IP Analytics and Strategy for LexisNexis Intellectual Property Solutions. “Happily, patent portfolios provide a relevant and impactful gauge for evaluating innovation across companies across all industries, technologies, and sizes. LexisNexis® Patent Asset Index provides an objective apples-to-apples framework for analyzing innovation leadership based on changes in the strength of each company’s patent assets, highlighting the companies who are driving innovation forward. The Top 100 Global Innovators have demonstrated their leadership in advancing the world’s knowledge, while creating technologies and tools that will build a better world.”


Recent progress includes meaningful advancements in digital health ecosystems, expanded automation capabilities in pharmacies and clinical settings, and continued development of platforms that improve efficiency, safety and treatment outcomes. These innovations contribute to the company’s ability to drive margin expansion, strengthen its competitive differentiation and support consistent sustainable growth.
