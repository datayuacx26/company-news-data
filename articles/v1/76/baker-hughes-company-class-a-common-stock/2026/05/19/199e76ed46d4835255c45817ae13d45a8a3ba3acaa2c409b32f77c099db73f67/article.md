As ultrasonic testing technology becomes more advanced, within hundreds of different types available, probe selection has become more complex. That is exactly why we launch this free mobile app: UT – Probes Krautkrämer.


Image


#### How the new Krautkrämer Probes App helps


The newly released Krautkrämer probe app focuses on simplifying one of the most time-consuming parts of ultrasonic inspection workflows: finding the right probe quickly. It is not only a probe search app, but also a smart filtering tool and knowledge library. What to expect on this app:


- **Browse 300+ Probes** : Search the complete Krautkramer UT probe catalog by name, family, frequency, diameter, application, and more.


- **Smart filtering** : Narrow results using multi- criteria filters including probe category, MHz, contact face diameter, beam angle, and application type.


- **Quick comparison** : Add robes to your comparison tray and view side-by-side specifications to make informed decisions.


- **Detailed probe specifications** : View pulse shape diagrams, spectrum images, sketch drawings, case dimensions, and full technical specifications for every probe.


- **Accessories & wedges** : Discover compatible cables, wedges, calibration blocks, and coupling for each probe.


- **Guide Me** : Not sure which probe you need? The Guide Me function will help you select the right probe based on your industry, application, and inspection requirements.


- **Personal probe collection** : Save your frequently used probes for quick access


- **Multi-language support** : Available in English, German, and Chinese. Easily switching from different languages.


- **Fully offline ready** : All probe data is stored locally on device. No internet connection is required after the initial setup. Perfect for remote inspection.


- **Knowledge support** : UT booklet, brochure PDF, FAQ and material library are ready to support.


- **Contact the sales team** : Once you find the right product, you can submit directly your request to our sales team on the product page.


Whether you are on-site conducting inspections or back at the desk planning your next project, the right UT probe is now at your fingertips!
