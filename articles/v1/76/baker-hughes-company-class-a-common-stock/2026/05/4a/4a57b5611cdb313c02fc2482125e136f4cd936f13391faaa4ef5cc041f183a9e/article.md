## A glossary for film radiography, digital radiography (CR & DDA), and computed radiography (CR) focused on the BS EN ISO 17636-1:2022, BS EN ISO 17636-2:2022, and BS EN ISO 16371-2:2017 standards


.


### A


**Area of Interest (AoI)** – Minimum area evaluated on the radiograph containing the weld, heat affected zone (HAZ) on both sides, and all markers, lead letters and IQIs.


**Aliasing** – Image artefacts occurring when the spatial frequency of the object exceeds the detector’s sampling capability, typically appearing as jagged edges or moiré patterns.


### B


**Basic Spatial Resolution (SRᵦ)** – Half of the measured image or detector unsharpness, representing the smallest geometrical detail that can be resolved.


- SRᵦ detector: measured with duplex wire IQI directly on detector
- SRᵦ image: measured with duplex wire IQI on the object (source side)


**Bad Pixel** – Underperforming detector element of a digital detector array (DDA).


### C


**Computed Radiography (CR)** – Digital radiography technique using storage phosphor imaging plates instead of film.


**Contrast to Noise Ratio (CNR)** – Ratio between the difference in mean signal of two image areas and the noise level; a key component of digital image quality.


**Normalized Contrast to Noise Ratio (CNRₙ)** – CNR normalized to basic spatial resolution, enabling comparison of detection sensitivity across systems.


### D


**Digital Detector Array (DDA)** – Electronic detector converting incident radiation into a digital image via discrete detector elements (pixels).


**Double Wall Double Image (DWDI)** – Radiographic technique where both walls of a component are penetrated and both wall images are evaluated.


**Double Wall Single Image (DWSI)** – Radiographic technique where both walls are penetrated but only the wall adjacent to the detector is evaluated.


### G


**Geometric Magnification (v)** – Ratio of source to detector distance (SDD) to source to object distance (SOD).


**Geometric Unsharpness (uᴳ)** – Image blur caused by finite source size and inspection geometry.


**Grey Value (GV)** – Numeric value representing pixel intensity in a digital radiographic image.


**Linearized Grey Value** – Grey value directly proportional to radiation dose, required for quantitative image evaluation (SNR, CNR).


### H


**Heat Affected Zone (HAZ)** – Material region adjacent to the weld affected by the welding thermal cycle; minimum width considered is weld width or at least 10 mm.


### I


**Image Quality Indicator (IQI)** – Device (wire, step hole, duplex wire) used to verify radiographic image quality and sensitivity.


**Imaging Plate (IP)** – Photostimulable phosphor plate used in computed radiography to store a latent radiographic image.


### N


**Nominal Thickness (t)** – Thickness of the parent material, excluding manufacturing tolerances.


### P


**Penetrated Thickness (w)** – Total material thickness in the direction of the radiation beam, accounting for all penetrated walls.


### R


**Region of Interest (RoI)** – Defined group of pixels used for measurement of SNR, CNR or other image metrics.


### S


**Signal to Noise Ratio (SNR)** – Ratio of mean signal level to noise level in a selected region of a digital image.


**Normalized Signal to Noise Ratio (SNRₙ)** – SNR normalized to basic spatial resolution, used as a primary acceptance metric in digital radiography.


**Source to Detector Distance (SDD)** – Distance between radiation source and detector; equivalent to SFD in film radiography


**Source to Object Distance (SOD)** – Distance between radiation source and the surface of the test object.


**Source Size (d)** – Effective size of the radiation source or focal spot, influencing geometric unsharpness.


### W


**Weld Area to Evaluate (WAE)** – Area on the radiograph containing the weld and adjacent HAZ on both sides to be assessed for imperfections.
