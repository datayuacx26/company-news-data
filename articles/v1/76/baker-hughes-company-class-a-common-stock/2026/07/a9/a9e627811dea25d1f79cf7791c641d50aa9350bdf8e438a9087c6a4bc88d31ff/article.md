## Baker Hughes Declares Quarterly Dividend


07/26/2026


[Download (opens in new window) PDF 1.02 MB](https://s21.q4cdn.com/548121662/files/doc_news/Baker-Hughes-Declares-Quarterly-Dividend-9c424fae0-2026.pdf)


HOUSTON and LONDON, July 26, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Baker Hughes (NASDAQ: BKR) announced today that the Baker Hughes Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.23 per share of Class A common stock payable on Aug. 17, 2026, to holders of record on Aug. 7, 2026.


Baker Hughes expects to fund its quarterly cash dividend from cash generated from operations.
