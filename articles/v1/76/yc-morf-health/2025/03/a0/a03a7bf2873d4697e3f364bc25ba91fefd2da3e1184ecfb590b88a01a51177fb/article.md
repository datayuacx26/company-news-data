# How to Streamline Patient Onboarding with Automation

A smooth, thoughtful onboarding experience is one of the best ways to increase conversion, reduce no-shows, and set patients up for success.


But for many healthcare providers, onboarding is still a mix of disconnected tools, manual follow-ups, and operational overhead. That’s where automation can make a huge difference—not just for your team, but for your patients too.


Here are some of the best ways we’ve seen teams streamline onboarding using automation:


## Branded, Patient-Centered Signup Flows


The signup experience is your first impression. A branded, mobile-friendly form makes your practice feel more polished and trustworthy—and gives patients confidence they’re in the right place.


Even better: let patients **save their progress** and return to where they left off. Whether they get interrupted during intake or need time to find their insurance card, allowing them to come back avoids frustration and drop-off.


At Morf, we’ve seen that even just giving patients a persistent link or reminder to finish signup improves completion rates significantly.


## Self-Service Scheduling That Actually Works


Letting patients book appointments themselves is great—but only if it’s smart enough to account for the realities of healthcare.


For telemedicine practices especially, it's critical to show appointment availability that matches:


- The patient’s **insurance**
- Their **state** (based on provider licensure)


If you don’t support self-scheduling (yet), you can still automate key pieces:


- 🔔 Notify your operations or admin team when someone is ready to be scheduled
- 📅 Let the patient **book a time** with your **intake coordinator** so they can quickly get on the phone and get everything sorted


## Sign-Up Abandonment Outreach


Most patient journeys don’t happen in one sitting.


Send automated SMS or email nudges to people who start signup but don’t finish. These don’t need to be pushy—a simple reminder with a link back to where they left off goes a long way.


## Automated Insurance Eligibility Checks


Verifying insurance eligibility manually is slow, error-prone, and often leaves the patient hanging.


Instead, as soon as someone submits their insurance details, trigger an **automated eligibility and benefits check** so you know whether they’re covered—and they know what to expect.


You can even combine this with follow-up automation if a plan isn’t accepted or if clarification is needed.


## Intake Form Reminders


Patients often forget to complete forms before their visit—and your team ends up scrambling to chase them down.


Set up automated reminders via **SMS or email** to prompt patients to complete their intake, especially in the 24–48 hours before their appointment. We’ve found SMS to be more effective for urgent nudges, especially with mobile-first patients.


## Appointment Reminders (That Actually Reduce No-Shows)


No-shows aren’t just frustrating—they’re expensive.


Simple, well-timed reminders—especially via SMS—can significantly reduce no-shows and last-minute cancellations. Personalizing the message with the patient’s name, provider name, or visit type can also make a big difference.


## Conversion Tracking (So You Know What’s Working)


Want to optimize your onboarding process? You need visibility into what’s actually happening in your funnel.


In our[last blog post](https://www.morf.health/) , we talked about tracking the full journey:


- Did someone complete signup?
- Did they book?
- Did they show up?


By tracking these milestones—and tying them back to marketing sources—you can make better decisions about what’s working and where patients are getting stuck.
