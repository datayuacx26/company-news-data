# Meet Flo AI: The Future of Healthcare Workflow Automation

Healthcare teams spend countless hours each week on manual, repetitive work including chasing intake forms, updating spreadsheets, re-entering data across disconnected systems. It’s invisible labor that takes time away from what matters most: caring for patients.


At Morf Health, our mission has always been simple: make the infrastructure disappear so providers can focus on people, not paperwork.


Today we’re excited to introduce Flo AI - our intelligent workflow assistant that makes automating everyday tasks faster, easier, and fully self-service.


## **Why We Built Flo**


When we founded Morf Health in 2022, we started as an *integration platform* that helped practices keep patient data in sync across all their tools — EHRs, scheduling software, billing systems, and CRMs.


Over time, customers began using Morf to automate more of their operations: onboarding, reminders, follow-ups, and lab notifications. We saw practices reduce administrative hours, cut no-shows, and improve patient preparedness.


But one consistent challenge kept surfacing: automation still felt intimidating **.** Even with our visual builder, teams often leaned on our customer-success team to help configure workflows. That reliance limited how many automations a small practice could realistically manage.


**Flo AI changes that.** It puts the power of automation directly in your hands.


## **What Flo AI Does**


Flo AI is your guided workflow builder — a friendly layer on top of Morf’s automation platform that lets anyone create or edit workflows without technical training.


Here’s how it works:


- **Describe your goal.** Type something like, *“When a new patient books an appointment, send them an intake form, remind them 48 hours before their visit, and alert my staff if it’s incomplete.”*
- **Flo builds it for you.** Triggers, timing, and conditions are pre-configured automatically inside your Morf dashboard.
- **You review and launch.** Adjust steps, connect your tools, and go live — in minutes, not days.


Flo responds instantly, works 24/7, and follows the same HIPAA- and FHIR-compliant standards that power the rest of Morf Health.No coding. No waiting for a support ticket. No extra vendor fees.


## **How Flo Helps Your Practice**


Flo AI isn’t about replacing staff; it’s about making every hour more valuable.


- **Save time.** Reduce back-and-forth around reminders, forms, and scheduling updates.
- **Improve consistency.** Every patient gets the same timely communication and follow-up.
- **Cut no-shows.** Automated reminders keep appointments on track.
- **Ease team burnout.** Less admin work means more energy for patient care.


Early customers using Morf for similar automations have seen measurable improvements in show rates, engagement, and staff satisfaction — and Flo AI makes achieving those results simpler than ever.


## **Built on Morf’s Core Strengths**


Flo AI isn’t a standalone experiment; it’s built directly on Morf Health’s proven infrastructure:


- **HIPAA and SOCII compliance** for secure data handling across every system.
- **Library of integrations** with leading EHR, scheduling, and communication tools.
- **A transparent dashboard** to monitor profiles, workflows, and outcomes in real time.


Flo AI brings all of that capability into a self-service experience anyone on your team can use.


## **A Simpler Way Forward**


Healthcare technology doesn’t need to be louder or more complex — it needs to *work quietly in the background.* Flo AI was designed to do exactly that: remove the friction between ideas and implementation.


If you can describe your process, you can automate it.
