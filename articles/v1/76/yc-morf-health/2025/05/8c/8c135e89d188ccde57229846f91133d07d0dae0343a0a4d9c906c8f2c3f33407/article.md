# Automating the most time-consuming step in the patient journey

As Nema’s patient volume grew, so did the operational complexity of managing a patient’s next steps after each care milestone.


When a patient finished the “intensive care” phase of treatment, the care‑team needed **30‑45 minutes** to transition that patient into the next phase—review forms, send reminder emails, and update the EHR & CRM so nothing fell through the cracks. Dozens of those hand‑offs happened every week.


Three months after building workflow automations in Morf, the same hand‑off takes less than **5 minutes** and patient engagement in the “recovery” phase is up 75%.


### **The Challenge**


Every growing practice has a moment where the care plan changes gears. During these junctures, two things are always true:


1. **Patients still need guidance.** If reminders and resources don’t arrive on time, adherence drops, outcomes may decline, and churn creeps up.
2. **Staff time is scarce.** Manually tracking patient status, reviewing forms, and sending follow-up emails doesn’t scale.


For Nema, the admin grind added up to **≈ 5 hours a week** and delayed critical outreach when patients were most likely to drift away.


### **The Morf Solution**


In a just a few days Nema implemented workflows in Morf to create an automated “discharge” playbook:


- **Auto‑detect phase completion** – Morf listens to the EHR for a specific signed visit note.
- **Route the patient —** automatically tag the appropriate care group based on treatment completion, with personalized branching logic for special use cases, like insurance coverage or self-pay
- **Send sequenced nudges —** Kick off a patient-portal conversation to boost engagement and follow up with timed resource emails and assessments at key milestones
- **Sync the systems —** Updates CRM records to keep patient data aligned across the EHR and CRM for downstream automations


No more duplicate entries, or “Did we email her yet?” Slack threads.


### **Results After 3 Months**


- **Admin time:** 5 hrs/week → **45 min/week** (‑84 %)
- **On‑time follow‑ups:** 62 % → **96 %**
- **Patient‑portal engagement:** **+75 %** opens & replies


Staff reclaimed nearly half a work‑day every week—and used it for proactive patient care rather than admin.


### **Why This Applies Beyond Nema**


Whether your clinic calls it *discharge* , *post‑op* , *maintenance* , or *ongoing care* , you still face the same hand‑off risks: missed reminders, stale data, and disengaged patients. Morf:


- Connects natively to the major EHRs you already use.
- Handles branching logic—insurance quirks, comorbidities, age—without a line of code.
- Keeps outreach in your own voice, so automation never feels robotic.


“Morf unleashed our care team’s capacity—what took 5 hours a week now takes less than 45 minutes, and our patients never miss a critical check-in.” — Chipper Stotz, Head of Product


For fast-growing practices like Nema, automating patient transitions with Morf doesn’t just lower operating costs—it keeps patients supported and engaged at a critical hand-off in their care.
