# Corporate Giving

Allegiant’s Corporate Giving program is focused on supporting three core areas that make a difference in communities we serve and prioritize causes that are important to us as a company. These areas include:


- Making travel possible for those facing serious medical challenges
- Enabling community safety, preparedness and recovery
- Promoting STEM education and access to careers in the aeronautical sciences


We are proud to provide a robust charitable giving program that allows us to impact even further the areas we serve and call home. If you would like to request a monetary or in-kind donation, please send a detailed email tocorporate.giving@allegiantair.com .


We recommend at least a month’s lead time for requests and appreciate your patience in our response, as we receive many submissions. Please know that it would be impossible to support every worthy cause, as much as we’d like to.
