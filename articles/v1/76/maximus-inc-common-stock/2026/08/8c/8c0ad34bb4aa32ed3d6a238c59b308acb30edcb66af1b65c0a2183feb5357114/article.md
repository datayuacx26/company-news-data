# Bruce Caswell joins Elizabeth Dole Foundation board of directors

The[Elizabeth Dole Foundation](https://www.elizabethdolefoundation.org/news/elizabeth-dole-foundation-convenes-national-leaders-to-launch-blueprint-for-caregiving-in-america) , the nation’s leading organization serving America’s 14.3 million military and veteran caregivers, announced the appointment of[Bruce Caswell](https://maximus.com/leadership/bruce-caswell) to its Board of Directors. Caswell brings decades of public-sector leadership focused on advanced technology and innovative solutions that improve outcome-based government program and service delivery to meet the nation’s evolving needs for service members, Veterans, and caregivers.


“At Maximus, our mission is to deliver government services with excellence to the people who depend on them — at scale, and without fail. With that same level of urgency and focus, I look forward to helping deepen the Foundation’s positive impact on the lives of millions of Veterans, military families, and their caregivers.”


— **Bruce Caswell, President & CEO, Maximus**


This appointment reinforces Maximus’ commitment to improving outcomes through high-impact, integrated whole-health ecosystems and advances the Foundation’s[National Blueprint for Action](https://www.elizabethdolefoundation.org/news/elizabeth-dole-foundation-convenes-national-leaders-to-launch-blueprint-for-caregiving-in-america) , a solutions-driven roadmap guiding programs, partnerships, and advocacy.
