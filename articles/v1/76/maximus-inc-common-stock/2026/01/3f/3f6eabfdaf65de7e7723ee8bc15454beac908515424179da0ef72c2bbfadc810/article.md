# GovTech spotlights SNAP solution

## AI-backed tool can help states reduce costly mistakes that can lead to cuts in federal assistance


As states navigate significant federal changes to the[Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program](https://maximus.com/state-and-local-government/snap) (SNAP), a recent Government Technology article highlights how Maximus is supporting state agencies with smarter tools to improve accuracy and reduce payment error rates.


The piece explores[Accuracy Assistant](https://maximus.com/news/maximus-launches-snap-accuracy-assistant) ™, Maximus’ AI-powered solution that identifies and prevents errors in SNAP eligibility and benefit determinations. By using predictive analytics and real-time data analysis, the tool helps state teams flag inconsistencies early and make corrections before benefits are issued.


This innovative solution comes at a pivotal time, as states face growing operational pressure from federal policy changes that shift more financial responsibility for payment error rates, while program demand and complexity remain high. Accuracy Assistant reflects Maximus’ commitment to combining advanced technology with human expertise to strengthen program integrity, support frontline workers, and ensure eligible individuals receive the benefits they depend on.
