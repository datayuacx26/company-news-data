# Maximus Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend of $0.33 per Share

# Maximus Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend of $0.33 per Share


April 06, 2026 6:30am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_56a8934dde57489ccfe4f2cde0dc116a/maximus/news/2026-04-06_Maximus_Declares_Quarterly_Cash_Dividend_of_0_33_582.pdf)


TYSONS, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Maximus](https://maximus.com/) (NYSE: MMS), a leading provider of government services, announced today that its Board of Directors has approved a quarterly cash dividend of $0.33 per share, payable on June 1, 2026, to shareholders of record on May 15, 2026.


**About Maximus**


As a leading strategic partner to government, Maximus helps improve the delivery of public services amid complex technology, health, economic, and social challenges. With a deep understanding of program service delivery, acute insights that achieve operational excellence, and an extensive awareness of the needs of the people being served, our employees advance the critical missions of our partners. Maximus provides tech-enabled services to government agencies, including innovative business process management and technology solutions, that provide improved outcomes for the public and higher levels of productivity and efficiency of government-sponsored programs. For more information, visit[maximus.com](http://www.maximus.com/) .
