# SOCOM's human performance edge

In a recent interview with Fed Gov Today, Frank Wakeham, Maximus’ Senior Managing Director, Defense and National Security Lead, discusses how decision advantage and sustained readiness require advanced innovative models that extend operational longevity, support resilience, and help elite teams maintain decision superiority in the moments that matter most.


Hear how U.S. Special Operations Command can operationalize at speed and scale through an ecosystem-driven innovation approach that translates emerging technologies into secure, mission-aligned capabilities that support readiness and human performance objectives.


The result is a more adaptive, resilient model for advancing human performance and strengthening mission readiness.


This perspective has implications well beyond SOCOM. As agencies modernize, the question isn’t just about technology investments, but how technology is part of a mission-ready ecosystem.
