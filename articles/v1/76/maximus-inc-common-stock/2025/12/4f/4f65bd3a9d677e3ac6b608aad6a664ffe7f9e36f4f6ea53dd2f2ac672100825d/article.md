# Illinois' award-winning health exchange

## Award-winning digital tool simplifies enrollment for Get Covered Illinois customers


We recently supported a major milestone in Illinois as the state transitioned from the federal health insurance marketplace (HealthCare.gov) to a state-based marketplace (SBM). We helped design, build, and launch the[Get Covered Illinois website](https://getcovered.illinois.gov/) through a new contract with the state.


We designed the new website to help residents easily compare health plans, check for financial assistance, and enroll in coverage tailored to their needs. Our human-centered design approach resulted in a modern, accessible, and user-friendly experience.


In just five months, we delivered a fully functional, accessible website — ahead of open enrollment, and within the state-managed platform. The new website serves as the digital front door to access health and dental plans tailored to the needs of Illinois residents, helping them transition smoothly between Medicaid and Qualified Health Plans.


> Drawing on our expertise in applying digital solutions to public health insurance programs that meet the diverse needs of their populations, we planned, built, tested, and launched a customer-friendly website.


****[Rick Sankey](https://maximus.com/employees/rick-sankey) | Senior Managing Director, Maximus


The successful launch builds on our decades-long partnership with the State of Illinois, where we currently serve as the[Medicaid](https://maximus.com/state-and-local-government/medicaid) enrollment broker and manage the state’s award-winning[Medicaid managed care enrollment website](https://enrollhfs.illinois.gov/en/enroll) . Our familiarity with the needs and preferences of residents, combined with our deep expertise in[digital accessibility](https://maximus.com/customer-experience/digital-government/state-and-local/digital-accessibility) and health insurance literacy, shaped the design and development of the new Get Covered Illinois site.
