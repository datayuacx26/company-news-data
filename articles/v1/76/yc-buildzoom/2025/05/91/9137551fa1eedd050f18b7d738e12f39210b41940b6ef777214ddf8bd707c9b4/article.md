Rory is the Director of Enterprise Sales at Buildzoom


### **Q: Rory, for business owners unfamiliar with preconstruction, what does this phase entail, and why is it so important?**


**Rory:** **** Preconstruction is essentially the planning phase before any physical construction begins. It involves activities like site analysis, design development, budgeting, permitting, and contractor selection. This phase sets the foundation for the entire project. A well-executed preconstruction process can identify potential challenges early, optimize costs, and streamline the construction timeline. Neglecting this phase often leads to unforeseen issues, budget overruns, and delays.

### **Q: What are some common pitfalls business owners face during preconstruction, and how does BuildZoom help mitigate them?**


**Rory:**


One common pitfall is underestimating the complexity of the permitting process, which can vary significantly by location and project type. Delays in obtaining permits can derail timelines. We help by leveraging our knowledge of local regulations to expedite this process. Another issue is selecting contractors without thorough vetting, leading to quality or reliability problems.
