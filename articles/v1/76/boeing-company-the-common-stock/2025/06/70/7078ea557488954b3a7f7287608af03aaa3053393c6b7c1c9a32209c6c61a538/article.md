# 200 Students Dive into Robotics Workshops in Qatar

**DOHA, Qatar, June 3, 2025** — INJAZ Qatar and Boeing \[NYSE: BA\] have successfully conducted STEM and robotics workshops at the Doha British Schools, helping students in grades five to eight build and customize their own robots.


Kuljit Ghata-Aura, president of Boeing Middle East, Türkiye, Africa, and Central Asia, said: "Boeing has partnered with INJAZ Qatar on this program since 2019, reaching over 1,000 students across various schools in Doha over the years. These workshops provide an interactive and enjoyable way for children to apply their STEM knowledge while inspiring them to explore future careers in aerospace, software engineering, robotics, and other in-demand fields."


Emad Al Khaja, INJAZ Qatar CEO, added: “At INJAZ Qatar, we are committed to empowering youth through meaningful, experiential education that bridges the gap between school and the workforce. Our long-standing partnership with Boeing reflects our shared vision to inspire the next generation of STEM leaders. Through initiatives like ‘Robotics Program’ we provide students with an engaging platform to explore real-world applications of science, develop essential skills, and ignite their curiosity for the future.”


This year, the program has provided 200 students at the Doha British Schools with hands-on experience in building and testing robotic systems, with a focus on navigation, movement, and motor integration. Students have also practiced important skills such as critical thinking, problem solving, creativity and teamwork.


Over the years, Boeing’s collaboration with INJAZ Qatar has positively impacted more than 5,000 students through a diverse array of entrepreneurship and STEM initiatives.


**About INJAZ Qatar**


INJAZ Qatar, in partnership with the business community, educators, and corporate volunteers, is dedicated to inspiring and preparing young people in Qatar for success. As a member of Junior Achievement Worldwide, the non-profit organization focused on workforce readiness, entrepreneurship, and financial literacy, INJAZ Qatar has reached over 350,000 students across more than 100 schools and 14 universities since its establishment in 2007. With the support of over 8,000 corporate volunteers and teachers, INJAZ Qatar is part of the INJAZ Al-Arab network, which spans 14 countries in the Middle East and North Africa and shares a common mission to empower young Arabs for success in the global economy.


More information:[here](https://www.injaz-qatar.org/)


**About Boeing** :


A leading global aerospace company, Boeing develops, manufactures and services commercial airplanes, defense products and space systems for customers in more than 150 countries. Since 2006 when Qatar Airways placed its first order with Boeing, the company’s relationship with Qatar and commitment to supporting Qatar National Vision 2030 have strengthened. Boeing contributes to the economic diversification and ambitious growth of Qatar by supporting the development of the local aerospace ecosystem and advanced technologies, and by building the skills and capacity of future talent.
