# AEROSENS Receives TACFI Award from the U.S. Air Force

**DUBAI, UAE, JULY 22, 2026** — At the Farnborough International Airshow 2026, Boeing and the UAE’s Tawazun Council for Defence Enablement announced that AEROSENS, a company supported through the Al Sadeem Program, has been awarded a Tactical Funding Increase (TACFI) grant by AFWERX, the innovation arm of the U.S. Air Force. The funding will support the continued development of AEROSENS’ AeroISU platform, enhancing in-transit visibility and operational readiness.


The Al Sadeem Program is a joint initiative between Tawazun and Boeing designed to support high-potential technology companies through technical mentoring, investment and strategic partnerships, accelerating the development of advanced technologies and strengthening innovation across the aerospace and defence sectors.


“We prioritise investments that deliver tangible outcomes for the UAE by strengthening national capabilities, developing local talent and building a sustainable defence industrial base,” said H.E. Shareef Hashim Al Hashemi, Director General of Industry Development at Tawazun Council for Defence Enablement. “Through the Al Sadeem Program and our strategic partnerships, we continue to support high-potential companies and accelerate the development of technologies that contribute to national capability and industrial growth.”


“Boeing is dedicated to fostering innovation and collaboration in the aerospace sector,” said Vince Logsdon, vice president, International Business Development and Strategic Partnerships for Boeing Defense, Space & Security. “This milestone reflects Tawazun’s leadership in advancing strategic national capabilities. Through our long-standing partnership, we are proud to support the development of next-generation technologies like those of AEROSENS, while contributing to the growth of the UAE’s defense ecosystem and local industry.”


“This funding represents an important milestone in the advancement of our technology and reflects confidence in our vision to deliver innovative, data-driven solutions that strengthen defence logistics,” said Maria Martínez, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of AEROSENS. “We are proud to have the continued support of Tawazun and Boeing as we advance our mission.”


**About Tawazun**


Tawazun Council for Defence Enablement serves as a national enabler focused on translating defence and security priorities into targeted institutional, industrial, and economic outcomes across the UAE ecosystem. The Council plays a pivotal role in supporting defence procurement by ensuring acquisition programmes are strategically aligned with long-term national capability requirements whilst adhering to value-for-money principles and industrial localisation objectives.


**About Boeing**


A leading global aerospace company and top U.S. exporter, Boeing develops, manufactures and services commercial airplanes, defense products and space systems for customers in more than 150 countries. Our U.S. and global workforce and supplier base drive innovation, economic opportunity, sustainability and community impact. Boeing is committed to fostering a culture based on our core values of safety, quality and integrity.


**About AEROSENS**


AEROSENS Inc. is a dual-use aviation technology company developing advanced IoT solutions for both defense and commercial aviation markets. The company focuses on improving security, asset visibility, operational safety, and mission efficiency through secure, connected networks and intelligent data analytics. AEROSENS’ innovations drive the digitalization and automation of inspections, tracking, safety, and security processes, enhancing efficiency and readiness across aviation and defense logistics operations. The company’s technology roadmap spans connected logistics, aviation safety and security and situational awareness systems designed to make complex aviation and defense environments safer, smarter, and more resilient. Learn more at www.aerosens.com.


**About AFWERX**


AFWERX, the innovation arm of the Department of the Air Force and a division within the Air Force Research Laboratory, partners with small businesses, startups and nontraditional vendors to address challenges facing Airmen and Guardians. Headquartered at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, AFWERX strengthens the defense industrial base by expanding access to innovators, building partnerships and accelerating the transition of critical technologies to operational capability. In fiscal year 2025, AFWERX awarded more than 1,000 contracts totaling $1.37 billion and achieved 438 Phase III transitions valued at $8.1 billion. For more information, visit afwerx.com.
