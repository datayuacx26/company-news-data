# Navient to announce second quarter 2026 results, host earnings webcast August 6

[PDF Version](https://news.navient.com/node/13396/pdf)


HERNDON, Va., July 02, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Navient (Nasdaq: NAVI) will host an audio webcast to review its second quarter 2026 results on Thursday, August 6, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time.


The webcast will be available on[Navient.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=cAuT8lodM3r_R9OttAHHxZDbSayqFiRpRQ-6qdHtrQx9MOQp3l2BOyCvRLXpQLvV5qM51AoVu_1LAUxvJwa7C7qfmXOzVJ47UgBD-fHbENA=) ; a replay of the webcast will be available on the site.


The results will be posted the same day on[Navient.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=cAuT8lodM3r_R9OttAHHxZDbSayqFiRpRQ-6qdHtrQxDbt-ygRattzvQd0dQdZriDxKdTGdknYommGZXBAD7sTN7oz3dMSC8k3kqFygIL6Y=) after 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time before the call begins.


In addition to being available on the company’s investor website, the results will be filed with the SEC on a Form 8-K available at[SEC.gov](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=BH_n2oE0bZ1ZXskLj820wcIQCZ7adFJDzmgIirB4SvLwt81-dPen_J9fZXJRXjcX1gBjVwAToUmYX7fsPYVj8g==) .
