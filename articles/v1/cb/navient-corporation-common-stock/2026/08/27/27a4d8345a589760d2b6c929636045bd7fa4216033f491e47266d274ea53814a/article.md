# Navient posts second quarter 2026 financial results

Complete financial results are available on the company’s website at[Navient.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=wQWjF5ThAjWSZwIeChqxorHDO4UppUefyjftbqnZbbCYxLjbl-SfQJDTJ35MN7iCCYq4MqBo1qijOmFbQg3RN6IBFhpVBYQcFsSqcSOsAXIiwsZWTCq6CVJ9fnJGOgEH) . The webcast will be available on[Navient.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=wQWjF5ThAjWSZwIeChqxorHDO4UppUefyjftbqnZbbDi_AkIQ5diNL43xmTvKUbqTv9_PepmiDdXa9_rB8EtFyT4XT9aUFQFeq8nZzOtOHY0ppohcNS-6LY9nTelRnBG) .
