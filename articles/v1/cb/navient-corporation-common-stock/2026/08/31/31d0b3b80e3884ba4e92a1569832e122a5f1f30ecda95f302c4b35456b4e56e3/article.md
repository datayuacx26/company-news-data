# Navient declares third quarter common stock dividend

[PDF Version](https://news.navient.com/node/13416/pdf)


HERNDON, Va., Aug. 13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Navient (Nasdaq: NAVI) announced that its board of directors approved a 2026 third quarter dividend of $0.16 per share on the company's common stock.


The third quarter 2026 dividend will be paid on September 18, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on September 4, 2026.
