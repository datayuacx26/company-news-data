# Navient to announce first quarter 2026 results, host earnings webcast April 29

[PDF Version](https://news.navient.com/node/13331/pdf)


HERNDON, Va., April 03, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Navient (Nasdaq: NAVI) will host an audio webcast to review its first quarter 2026 results on Wednesday, April 29, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time.


The webcast will be available on[Navient.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Bk19DJ4jrQhq3iPL5dVy1lnJt_2L2vY4qBU7TySi7uewoK1Fc86CJBguYWtwJN2zcAjooWs7d6ze8e-beZn2MbCFt0S0lM6uGn41XNklsiA=) ; a replay of the webcast will be available on the site.


The results and presentations slides will be available the same day by 7:00 a.m. on[Navient.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Bk19DJ4jrQhq3iPL5dVy1lnJt_2L2vY4qBU7TySi7ufJA3829ovjxgjXAjrh87gDZnb7wk14_JJUT-bSV0VRuqoOiO-hsDUA99HnPFNQwN8=) .


In addition to being available on the company’s investor website, the results will be filed with the SEC on a Form 8-K available at[SEC.gov](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=fJBeGPvrE-w-HQ0Ig8YRXTV88ICj3WlRCBS4-atOD3MgooPtKJpI2Az-jtRfa-uumAz99iWY4GSk1-QVJFB9NQ==) .
