# Navient posts first quarter 2026 financial results

Complete financial results are available on the company’s website at[Navient.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=7RIASqxgh7Q-RXX-dVvWrWjkE2-p_OYrFXZRZaDoTYoNaMPuxKvEoC_QUwg6D78pcKOsB7ZheVvSSchCgk8qexu8xmzqrehTeyt1e_Afsw0HP27_7cz9UrgGj4kQylXd) . The webcast will be available on[Navient.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=7RIASqxgh7Q-RXX-dVvWrWjkE2-p_OYrFXZRZaDoTYoOxDpbcFysZQNe_3pzuLhf2DWdcIFTQv7wdoixu36BBXM9Ioxdfiwqe05m_3SH5l9nsyGwf390qr98zRR00n06) .
