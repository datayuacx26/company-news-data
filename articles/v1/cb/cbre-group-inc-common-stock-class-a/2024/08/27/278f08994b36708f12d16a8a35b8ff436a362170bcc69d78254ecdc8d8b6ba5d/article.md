CBRE Deal Flow has been named the “Deal Management Software of the Year” by[PropTech Breakthrough](https://www.proptechbreakthrough.com/) , an independent market intelligence organization.


[CBRE Deal Flow](https://www.cbredealflow.com/) is CBRE's proprietary digital marketplace and global listing platform for property and portfolio sales. It hosts the industry’s largest and most diverse for-sales inventory, providing access to a growing database of more than 1.5 million global investors.


"CBRE Deal Flow is part of the driving force behind our intelligence-driven strategies, providing CBRE with a competitive advantage in commercial real estate acquisition, disposition and financing," said[Kevin Aussef](https://www.cbre.com/people/kevin-aussef) , Americas President of Investment Properties at CBRE.


Part of an integrated ecosystem of technology tools tailored for CBRE’s Capital Markets professionals, CBRE Deal Flow is complemented by the recent introduction of[CBRE Capital AI](https://www.cbre.com/press-releases/cbre-unveils-capital-ai-unlocking-the-power-of-real-estate-investment-property-data) . This innovative technology uses artificial intelligence to analyze billions of proprietary data points from CBRE Deal Flow and other sources to unlock a wider pool of capital.


“CBRE Deal Flow is an integral part of our comprehensive e2e technology platform for Capital Markets, delivering top-tier market intelligence to our professionals and clients, streamlining transaction execution, and automatically capturing thousands of key data points in real-time to generate proprietary insights unique to CBRE’s scale,” added[Sandeep Davé](https://www.cbre.com/people/sandeep-dave) , Chief Digital & Technology Officer for CBRE.


About CBRE Group, Inc.


CBRE Group, Inc. (NYSE:CBRE), a Fortune 500 and S&P 500 company headquartered in Dallas, is the world’s largest commercial real estate services and investment firm (based on 2024 revenue). The company has more than 140,000 employees (including Turner & Townsend employees) serving clients in more than 100 countries. CBRE serves clients through four business segments: Advisory (leasing, sales, debt origination, mortgage servicing, valuations); Building Operations & Experience (facilities management, property management, flex space & experience, digital infrastructure services); Project Management (program management, project management, cost consulting); Real Estate Investments (investment management, development).
