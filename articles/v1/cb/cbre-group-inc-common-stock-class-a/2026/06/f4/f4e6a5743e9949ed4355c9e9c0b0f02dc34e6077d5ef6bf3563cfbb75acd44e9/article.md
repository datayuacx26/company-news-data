CBRE announced that Nishant Bagadia has joined the company as Global Head of Partnerships for Energy & Sustainability.


In this role, Bagadia will oversee the global partner ecosystem across CBRE’s energy and sustainability services, helping to structure and scale partnerships that align with client needs and net zero ambitions.


Bagadia has more than 15 years of partnerships experience at the intersection of commercial growth, technology, energy and sustainability. As a leader of global partnerships at Breakthrough Energy, he worked across clean tech deployment, ecosystem development, and technology scale-up. Prior to that, as Vice President of Business Development at Salesforce, he unlocked commercial growth through large-scale partnerships and market expansion.


“Having the right ecosystem of technology, capital and service partners helps us deliver differentiated value for our clients. Nishant will work closely across our energy and sustainability teams to lead a partnership strategy grounded in client needs,” said Rob Bernard, Chief Energy & Sustainability Officer, CBRE.


CBRE has worked with several partners in recent years to expand its capabilities in areas of growing importance to its clients. This includes agreements with global water solutions and services leader[Ecolab](https://www.cbre.com/press-releases/cbre-partners-with-ecolab-to-improve-water-use-in-cre) , climate risk analysis platform[Climate X](https://www.cbre.com/press-releases/cbre-expands-climate-risk-assessment-capabilities-with-climate-x-agreement) , and sustainability data intelligence platform[Deepki](https://ir.cbre.com/press-releases/detail/197/cbre-property-management-forges-strategic-partnership-with) .


About CBRE Group, Inc.


CBRE Group, Inc.
