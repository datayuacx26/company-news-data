## **Audience Segmentation: Send the Right Message to the Right People**


Not every recipient engages the same way—so why treat them all alike?


**Audience Segmentation**


automatically groups recipients based on how they respond to your messages. This helps you send follow-ups that make sense, instead of blasting the same message to everyone.


Here’s how segmentation works:


✔


**Engaged Users (Hot Leads)**


– These are people who clicked your link. They’ve shown interest and are more likely to take action. Follow up with a personalized message or offer.


✔


**Unreachable Numbers (Dead Ends)**


– If messages repeatedly fail to deliver, these numbers may be outdated, inactive, or have activated the Do-Not-Disturb (DND) feature. Remove them to avoid wasting resources.


✔


**Missed Opportunities (Potential Leads)**


– Users who opened your message but didn’t click may need a nudge. Consider sending a follow-up with a different approach.


### **Strategic Advantages**


Instead of sending mass messages and hoping for the best, segmentation allows you to:


✔ Prioritize engaged users and drive meaningful interactions.


✔ Clean up your contact lists to avoid sending messages to inactive numbers.


✔ Fine-tune future campaigns based on real engagement patterns.


When you segment your audience, your messages become


**more relevant**

**better engagement**


and


**higher response rates**
