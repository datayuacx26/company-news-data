# TERMII LAUNCHES GLOBAL ESIM API SERVICE IN PARTNERSHIP WITH SIU TELECOMS FOR SEAMLESS DATA ROAMING ACROSS 180+ COUNTRIES

Lagos, Nigeria – **Termii** (www.termii.com), a leader in communication technology servicing over 20,000 businesses and 10M end-users monthly, is proud to announce the launch of **Sotel** (www.sotel.com), a new global eSIM API service, in partnership with **Siu Telecoms** (www.siutel.ng). The cutting-edge API solution is designed to revolutionize how businesses across various sectors - banking, fintech, travel, tourism, and aviation - provide seamless global connectivity.


The service enables businesses to launch eSIM services for seamless data roaming in over 180 countries as a value-added service to its upward mobile customers


. This service is delivered in partnership with Siu Telecoms (www.siutel.ng), a newly licensed Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) in Nigeria, marking a significant step forward in digital communication and global data connectivity.


**A New Era of Global Connectivity for Businesses**


As global data consumption increases, businesses across the tech spectrum are seeking ways to offer seamless connectivity to their clients. Termii’s Sotel eSIM API provides an innovative and scalable solution that allows companies to integrate global data roaming services into their offerings without the hassle of dealing with multiple vendors or establishing complicated network agreements. The API supports rapid deployment, enabling businesses to offer global connectivity quickly and giving them a competitive edge in their respective markets.


Businesses such as banks and fintech companies can now provide their customers with a secure global eSIM solution to stay connected while travelling abroad, making financial transactions easier and more secure. For businesses in travel and aviation, the solution opens the door to enhanced customer experiences by providing reliable, uninterrupted data services to international travellers.


**End User Experience and Benefits**


For end users, the benefits of Termii’s Sotel eSIM API are unparalleled. Customers no longer need to switch between multiple SIM cards as they cross borders. Instead, they can activate and manage their eSIMs remotely, enjoying high-quality, cost-effective data services across over 180 countries. Whether travelling for business or leisure, users benefit from seamless connectivity, ensuring they stay connected with family, colleagues, and business networks, no matter where they are.

### **Why Sotel and the Need to Enter the eSIM Space**


With the increasing reliance on data-driven services and the rise of remote work, Termii recognised the urgent need for businesses to offer global connectivity without the traditional hassles. **Sotel eSIM** provides a future-forward solution, enabling businesses to extend their reach and enhance customer satisfaction on a global scale.

Siu Telecoms, Termii’s strategic partner, brings its expertise as a licensed MVNO, providing the underlying infrastructure that powers this groundbreaking solution. This collaboration leverages the strengths of both companies—Termii’s deep expertise in communication technology and Siu Telecoms’ robust telecommunications network—to deliver a world-class eSIM service.
