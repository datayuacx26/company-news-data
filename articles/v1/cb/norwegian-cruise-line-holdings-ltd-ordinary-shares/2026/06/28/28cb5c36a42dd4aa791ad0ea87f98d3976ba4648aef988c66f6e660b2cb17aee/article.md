# Regent Seven Seas Cruises® Announces First-Ever Wellness Cruise with ELEMIS in 2027 Spotlight Voyage Collection

# Regent Seven Seas Cruises® Announces First-Ever Wellness Cruise with ELEMIS in 2027 Spotlight Voyage Collection


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_c2df6b2254110bfec5507f078bada635/nclhltd/news/2026-06-11_Regent_Seven_Seas_Cruises_Announces_First_Ever_807.pdf) June 11, 2026 4:24pm EDT


***Seven Specially Curated Sailings will also Feature Culinary Aficionados Mark and Kate Bittman, Journeys with*** ***Prestigious Vineyards and Winemakers, and a Fascinating Genealogy Cruise with Ancestry.com***


**MIAMI, June 11, 2026:**[Regent Seven Seas Cruises](https://www.rssc.com/) **®** , the world’s leading ultra-luxury cruise line, has announced its[2027 Spotlight Collection](https://www.rssc.com/Spotlight-Voyages) , featuring seven sailings that will inspire guests with total immersion into their passions and interests. The collection spans voyages aboard *Seven Seas Grandeur®* , *Seven Seas Splendor®* , *Seven Seas Voyager®* , and the brand’s newest ship, *Seven Seas Prestige™* , set against some of Europe’s most captivating destinations. Together, they offer a diverse range of themes, from wellness and genealogy to gastronomy and fine wine, providing guests distinct opportunities for discovery. Each voyage will feature thoughtful programming tailored to the theme of the sailing.


“Spotlight Voyages represent the evolution of luxury travel - guests are looking not only to see the world, but also to engage through their personal interests and passions,” said Wesley D’Silva, president of Regent Seven Seas Cruises. “Our 2027 collection has been crafted to bring together world-class experts and iconic luxury brands in a way only Regent can deliver. From exclusive culinary and wine experiences to our inaugural wellness and longevity voyage, each sailing offers a unique perspective while delivering the unrivaled comfort and service of The World’s Most Luxurious Fleet.”


Highlights of the 2027 Spotlight Collection include Regent’s inaugural Spotlight Voyage on Wellness & Longevity with ELEMIS, reflecting the growing demand for holistic well-being, longevity, and transformative travel. Developed in partnership with luxury skincare and wellness brand ELEMIS, the sailing will explore nutrition, mindfulness, movement, and healthy aging through expert-led sessions and wellness-focused activities. The Spotlight Voyage will also take place aboard Regent’s newest ship, Seven Seas Prestige™, the brand’s first new ship class in a decade, setting a new standard for ultra-luxury cruising, including four new suite categories and new dining experiences that further enhance the all-inclusive luxury offering.


The collection also features an epicurean Spotlight Voyage with culinary experts Mark and Kate Bittman who will explore food, culture, and storytelling through live podcast discussions and culinary experiences inspired by regional cuisine. Meanwhile, a series of wine-led Spotlight Voyages will invite guests to delve deeper into the world of viticulture alongside acclaimed hosts and winemakers, offering curated tastings, expert insights, and immersive discussions that celebrate craftsmanship, heritage, and innovation across some of the world’s most distinguished wine regions.


The collection is further enhanced by a unique Spotlight on Heritage in partnership with Ancestry.com, providing guests with the opportunity to explore their personal histories through expert-led genealogy research, bespoke consultations, and deeply meaningful, place-based discoveries.


For more information, please visit[RSSC.com/Spotlight-Voyages](https://www.rssc.com/Spotlight-Voyages) , call 1-844-4REGENT (1-844-473-4368), or contact a professional Travel Adviser.


**Regent’s 2027 Spotlight Voyage Collection**


[Spotlight on Wine with Foley Family Wines & Spirits](https://www.rssc.com/cruises/VOY270324/summary?source=CruiseSearch&m=2027-3&sh=VOY) ****


*March 24, 2027 - 10 Nights*


*Seven Seas Voyager Rome (Civitavecchia), Italy - Cinque Terre (La Spezia), Italy - Portofino, Italy - Cannes, France - Mahón (Menorca), Spain - Palma de Mallorca, Spain - Ibiza, Spain - Valencia, Spain - Barcelona, Spain*


Sailing from Rome to Barcelona, guests will explore the world of luxury winemaking. Hosted by Mari Wells Coyle, VP of Winemaking for Foley Family Wines & Spirits, and Jay Gifford, Managing Director, Internation for Foley Family Wines & Spirits, the voyage features curated tastings, expert-led seminars, and intimate discussions highlighting the craftsmanship behind some of California’s most acclaimed wines. Guests will sample selections from Foley’s premium portfolio, including Silverado, Chalk Hill, Ferrari-Carano, and Merus, while gaining insight into the wider Foley lifestyle portfolio spanning wineries, spirits, hotels, restaurants, and sports franchises.


[Spotlight on Heritage with Ancestry.com](https://www.rssc.com/cruises/VOY270702/summary?source=CruiseSearch&m=2027-7&sh=VOY) ****


*July 2, 2027 - 12 Nights*


*Seven Seas Voyager*


*London (Southampton), England - Fowey, United Kingdom - Cork (Cobh), Ireland - Waterford, Ireland - Holyhead, Wales - Douglas, Isle of Man - Liverpool, England - Bangor, Northern Ireland - Glasgow (Greenock), Scotland - Kirkwall (Orkney Islands), Scotland - Edinburgh (Leith, overnight in port), Scotland*


Tracing the stories that shape us, this unique voyage aboard *Seven Seas Voyager* invites guests to explore their histories alongside leading genealogists from AncestryProGenealogists. Sailing from London to Edinburgh, the journey combines expert insight, personalized consultations, and opportunities for bespoke research into guests’ own family histories and heritage. Luxury travelers can also take part in optional ancestral home visits and customized genealogy research linked to the itinerary. Together, these tailored experiences offer a deeper understanding of familial lineage and identity, creating opportunities for highly personal discoveries throughout the voyage.


[Spotlight on Wine with Mercante Wine Merchants](https://www.rssc.com/cruises/GRA270711/summary?source=CruiseSearch&m=2027-7&sh=GRA) ****


*July 11, 2027 - 8 Nights* ****


*Seven Seas Grandeur*


*Rome (Civitavecchia), Italy - Salerno, Italy - Messina (Sicily), Italy - Catania (Sicily), Italy - Kotor, Montenegro - Dubrovnik, Croatia - Koper, Slovenia - Venice (Fusina), Italy*


On board *Seven Seas Grandeur* , guests will join James Mercante, founder of Mercante Wine Merchants, for an enriching sailing from Rome to Venice exploring the heritage, craftsmanship, and traditions of Italian wine. A retired U.S. Navy Captain and distinguished maritime attorney, Mercante combines a lifelong connection to the sea with a deep passion for preserving his family’s Italian winemaking traditions. Inspired by his ancestral roots in Puglia, Mercante and his wife, Annie, returned to southern Italy to source exceptional wines that reflect the region's character. Throughout the voyage, guests will enjoy exclusive tastings, educational seminars, engaging discussions, and culinary pairings that showcase how premium Italian wines pair with regional flavors. The voyage will leave guests with a deeper appreciation of the intersection of wine, culture, family legacy, and maritime tradition both onboard and ashore.


[Spotlight on Wine with Palmaz Vineyards](https://www.rssc.com/cruises/SPL270711/summary?source=CruiseSearch&m=2027-7&sh=SPL) ****


*July 11, 2027 - 14 Nights*


*Seven Seas Splendor*


*Copenhagen, Denmark - Berlin (Warnemunde), Germany - Ronne (Bornholm), Denmark – Stockholm (overnight in port), Sweden - Tallinn, Estonia - Helsinki, Finland - Liepaja, Latvia - Gdansk (Gdynia), Poland - Amsterdam, Netherlands - Bruges (Zeebrugge), Belgium - London (Southampton), England*


Guests sailing from Copenhagen to London aboard *Seven Seas Splendor* will enjoy an immersive wine experience hosted by Jessica and Florencia Palmaz of Palmaz Vineyards, one of Napa Valley’s most innovative family-owned wineries. Through tastings, educational seminars, and engaging conversations, guests will discover the Palmaz approach to estate winemaking, sustainable vineyard practices, and the integration of technology and precision within luxury wine production. Rooted in a philosophy that great food and wine create meaningful human connection, the Palmaz family has built a brand centered on craftsmanship and shared moments. Hosted personally by members of the Palmaz family, the voyage will offer guests a deeper insight into the traditions and expertise shaping the culture and character of the estate.


[Epicurean Spotlight with Mark and Kate Bittman](https://www.rssc.com/cruises/VOY270714/summary?source=CruiseSearch&m=2027-7&sh=VOY) ****


*July 14, 2027 - 12 Nights*


*Seven Seas Voyager*


*Edinburgh (Leith), Scotland - Amsterdam, Netherlands - Copenhagen, Denmark - Berlin (Warnemunde), Germany - Ronne (Bornholm), Denmark - Gdansk (Gdynia), Poland - Klaipeda, Lithuania - Riga, Latvia - Tallinn, Estonia - Stockholm, Sweden*


Award-winning journalist, bestselling author, and podcast host Mark Bittman, alongside producer and editorial director Kate Bittman, will host a culinary and storytelling experience through the Baltic region on board *Seven Seas Voyager* . Sailing from Edinburgh to Stockholm, guests will enjoy live podcast discussions, intimate conversations, and interactive cooking demonstrations inspired by regional culinary traditions and local flavors. Widely regarded as one of the most influential voices in modern food culture, Mark Bittman has spent decades shaping conversations around food, sustainability, and home cooking through his acclaimed writing, bestselling books, and podcast, while Kate brings a thoughtful, creative approach to content development. Together, they will explore the intersection of food, travel, and community throughout the voyage, while also capturing content for their acclaimed podcast, Food with Mark Bittman.


[Spotlight on Wellness & Longevity with ELEMIS](https://www.rssc.com/cruises/PRT270821/summary?source=CruiseSearch&m=2027-8&sh=PRT) ****


*August 21, 2027 - 10 Nights*


*Seven Seas Prestige*


*Trieste, Italy - Argostoli (Cephalonia), Greece - Gythion, Greece - Athens (Piraeus), Greece - Mykonos, Greece - Santorini, Greece - Ibiza, Spain - Barcelona, Spain*


Regent’s first-ever Spotlight Voyage dedicated to wellness will offer guests a transformative ten-night Mediterranean journey aboard *Seven Seas Prestige* , sailing from Trieste to Barcelona. Developed in partnership with luxury skincare and wellness brand ELEMIS, the experience will center around four key pillars of well-being: Diet & Nutrition, Mind & Stress, Skin & Longevity, and Movement & Exercise. Designed to reflect the growing global interest in longevity and holistic well-being, the voyage will feature expert-led wellness sessions, interactive BeMoved classes, mindfulness enrichment, and Blue Zone-inspired culinary programming influenced by the lifestyle habits of the world’s longest-living communities. Blending wellness experiences with destination discovery, the sailing offers guests a unique opportunity to explore practical and aspirational approaches to living well.


[Spotlight on Wine with Honig Vineyard & Winery](https://www.rssc.com/cruises/VOY271011/summary?source=CruiseSearch&m=2027-10&sh=VOY) ****


*October 11, 2027 - 11 Nights*


*Seven Seas Voyager*


*Rome (Civitavecchia), Italy - Tuscany (Livorno), Italy - Monte Carlo, Monaco - Toulon, France - Sete, France - Palamos, Spain - Mahón (Menorca), Spain - Palma de Mallorca, Spain - Alicante, Spain - Barcelona, Spain*


Hosted by Steven Honig, guests will embark on an unforgettable Mediterranean journey aboard *Seven Seas Voyager* with Honig Vineyard & Winery. The voyage from Rome to Barcelona will feature curated tastings, hosted dinners, educational seminars, and intimate conversations focused on Napa Valley winemaking, sustainability, and food-and-wine pairing. Raised in San Francisco with weekends spent at his family’s Napa Valley vineyard, Steven Honig developed an early connection to wine country and hospitality before building an international career in cycling, sports marketing, and business development. Since returning to the family winery in 2000, he has helped shape its approach to sales, guest experience, and sustainable vineyard stewardship. Throughout the voyage, guests will enjoy hearing anecdotes and gaining insight into the passion and traditions behind one of the region’s most respected wineries.
