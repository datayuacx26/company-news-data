MIAMI, July 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Ltd. (NYSE: NCLH) (together with NCL Corporation Ltd., “Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings” or the “Company”) announced today it will report second quarter 2026 financial results on Thursday, July 30, 2026 at 6:30 a.m. Eastern Time with a conference call and webcast to discuss results at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time.


The conference call will be webcast via the Company’s Investor Relations website,[https://www.nclhltd.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=C9M705hLGMmn5fMxa4pvKFLW1EaKm8-Kg5crDkINVIyrb5DRRXKicN7xA9ZuchL2chNZMOE2GmwM_LoSrOl4-DB3FYJeLXJ0agl7XHE-q3uiXQD3zKS6F6enwDse6MqL) . A replay of the webcast will be available[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=eIRfOepwlxRKn0Qv6owYtB9zl870GXFlbt867PMp3NVeNjo3e_ZuXoEwolW5M8f6FJ4w9HEdRxF6tnFyd9Crr5-31lmsxCeWyqeJON_UB7o=) on the Company’s website for 30 days following the call.


**About Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Ltd.**


Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings Ltd. (NYSE: NCLH) is a leading global cruise company that operates Norwegian Cruise Line, Oceania Cruises and Regent Seven Seas Cruises. With a combined fleet of 35 ships and ~75,000 berths, NCLH offers itineraries to approximately 700 destinations worldwide. NCLH expects to add 16 additional ships across its three brands through 2037, which will add ~43,000 berths to its fleet.
