# Phreesia’s Megan Turco Named to MM+M 40 Under 40

-
-
-
-


**ALL-REMOTE COMPANY/WILMINGTON, Del. — Dec. 4, 2025 —** Phreesia today announced that Megan Turco, Director of Client Experience for Phreesia Network Solutions, has been named to[MM+M’s 2025 40 Under 40 list.](https://www.mmm-online.com/news/mmm-reveals-2026-40-under-40-honorees/)


MM+M’s annual 40 Under 40 list celebrates standout talent across healthcare marketing and communications. This year’s honorees were chosen for their impact in shaping the future of medical marketing through creativity, leadership and a commitment to a more inclusive, patient-centered industry.


“Megan has this rare ability to make operational excellence feel energizing,” said Danielle Lynch, Vice President of Client Experience at Phreesia. “She brings empathy, creativity and a whole lot of fun to the work—and that combination has been truly transformational for our business. Her passion for improving patient experiences and outcomes shows up in everything she does, and it’s exactly what makes her such an exceptional leader at Phreesia.”


Since joining Phreesia in 2018, Turco has progressed through five increasingly strategic roles, helping transform the Client Experience team from a group of four people into a mission-critical organization of more than 50 professionals. Today, Turco leads a dedicated sales team responsible for strategy, execution and growth for more than 80 life sciences and agency clients. Under her leadership, the team has exceeded its target for four consecutive years, achieving double-digit annual revenue growth year over year while launching programs that have reached millions of patients.


“Megan personifies our core values and displays incredible authenticity every day, it’s part of what makes her an exceptional leader and an inspiration to me and others,” said David Linetsky, President of Phreesia Network Solutions. “Our sales organization has a unique culture that’s deeply collaborative: we win as one. The entire team is celebrating today and is tremendously proud of this well-earned recognition of Megan’s accomplishments.”


Turco’s leadership has consistently delivered strong, measurable results. Internal survey data shows that 95% of clients rate their Client Experience representative as “very good” or “excellent,” while 94% say they receive great service from Phreesia. Turco is also widely regarded inside and outside the company for building culture, mentoring rising talent and strengthening long-standing client relationships.


“I’m incredibly honored to be included in MM+M’s 40 Under 40,” said Turco. “This recognition really speaks to the talent and heart of the team I get to lead. They show up every day with creativity, urgency and purpose. I’m so proud of what we’ve built together.”


For more information on Phreesia, visit[phreesia.com](https://www.phreesia.com/) .
