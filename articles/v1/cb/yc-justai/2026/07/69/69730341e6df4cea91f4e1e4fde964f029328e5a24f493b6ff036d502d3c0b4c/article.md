# The DNA of a founding team: Grit, chaos, and the right first hires

8 years ago, I made an impulsive decision overnight. quit my cushy job at Goldman to join Sensorflow, a 2-month-old company then, as their founding engineer. It meant a 50% pay cut, long hours, and endless chaos. Looking back, I know it was the best decision I made and Just Words wouldnt be the same without that founding experience.


6 months ago, I was on the other side of that leap, searching for someone who had the same fire I felt back then—the grit to dive headfirst into uncertainty, the passion to build something from nothing, and the drive to make it all work. Jeff and I screened hundreds of resumes, conducted over 50 interviews, and came up empty. It felt like we were hunting for a unicorn.


Then we found Rodrigo.


It didn’t happen through the usual process. Thanks to Jeff’s outbounding (which beat my sales outbounds any day) and our Tweep connections, Rodrigo popped onto our radar.


After his first call with Rodrigo -


Jeff: “I think you’d really like him”


Me: “Should we meet him for a coffee tonight?”


Jeff: “I am texting him right now”


Within an hour, in true founder-mode fashion, we were sitting by the painted ladies with Rodrigo, and within a week, we made him an offer.


We had the PMF feeling with Rodrigo, and here’s why we were right. In the last few months -


Since then, Rodrigo’s been everything we hoped for and more. He’s led late-night hackathons, consistently beats us to the office by 9 AM, helped us organize our chaos (we’re now Linear power users), and even brought board games to roast Jeff at our first happy hour.
