# Tumblr boosts conversions by +4.7% with JustAI's messaging engine.

### 🎯Tumblr Taps Into the Future of Messaging with Just Words


[Tumblr](https://tumblr.com/) , one of the world’s largest creative communities, set out to reimagine how it communicates with millions of users through push notifications. With daily touchpoints like “Recommended Topics” and “Intro Trending,” even fractional lifts could shift the trajectory of engagement and long-term retention.


But Tumblr’s team wasn’t just looking to A/B test copy.
They wanted to **evolve how messages are created, selected, and scaled** —automatically.


### 🧠 The Old World: Static Tests. Gut-Driven Copy.


-


**Slow Cycles** : Testing was gated by setup time and engineering priorities, leading to limited experimentation.


-


**Fixed Variants** : At most, a few handpicked notifications could be tested in parallel. And once the test ended, so did the learning.


-


**Low Insight Density** : Sequential, single-channel tests made it hard to extract clear insights or transfer them across journeys.


### ⚡ The New World: Always-On Message Intelligence


With **Just Words** , Tumblr unlocked:


-


**Instant Velocity** : Copy variants were auto-generated, plugged in, and tested without waiting on new briefs or dev cycles.


-


**Dynamic Optimization** : Instead of static A/B tests, messages were continuously refreshed, ranked, and improved across campaigns.


-


**Learning Loops** : Tumblr didn’t just learn *which* message won, but *why* , and *for whom* , unlocking personalization at scale.


### Approach


1.


**Building a self-improving Message Pool**


Just Words created push notification content across 6 different notification types, according to Tumblr’s brand and tone.


2.


**Auto‑Tune Optimization**


Just Words dynamically reallocated send volume toward top‑performing variants in real time—surface champions early, maximize impact. The system auto-archived variants in this pool based on past performance and recommended new variations from learnings over time.


3.


4.


**Transparent Reporting**


Every week, we reported the performance of winners and consistency in lift week over week.


5.


**Extended Rollout**


After identifying winners in each test, we consolidated those champion variants into a single, larger experiment spanning all five notification types.
