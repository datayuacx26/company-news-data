# JustAI turns 1 today!

### Here’s Our 2024 Wrapped: Full of Growth 📈, Grit 💪, and Gratitude 🙏


It feels surreal to say this, but we’re turning one 🎂 in just four days! A milestone like this calls for a moment of reflection, so let’s take you on a journey through our incredible first year.


#### 🏢 Company Highlights


-


Reached profitability while achieving significant revenue growth.


-


Expanded from zero to serving multiple enterprises with personalized communications.


-


Grew into a strong unicorn team.


#### 🤖 Product Impact


-


Enabled over $5M in revenue retention for our customers.


-


Scaled from zero to over 10 million personalized emails sent through our platform.


-


Delivered 300+ AI-powered message variations to drive engagement.


### Our Journey


We officially incorporated on January 4th, starting with nothing but an idea on paper. Our first product version? Let’s call it “rough.” Despite that, an early partner agreed to pilot it with their email notifications. By February, we had iterated to a slightly better version.


#### 💡 The First Insight


Our first breakthrough came during a conversation with a growth leader. After a demo filled with buggy features, they cut through the noise: “My problem is I don’t know which notification works best when I’m rotating 20 different ones in an experiment. I don’t know how to make a decision.” That insight hit home. We pivoted to solve exactly that—testing hundreds of variations in one experiment with clear insights for each.


This shift led to our first paid pilot, marking a turning point for our company.


Around the same time, a conversation with another growth leader gave us our tagline: “A state of continuous experimentation.” This encapsulated our mission and resonated deeply with our customers.


#### 💰The First Check


Our initial pilot showed promising results. After weeks of negotiations and meetings, we signed our first annual contract. Soon after, another champion brought us into their new role, securing another major deal.


#### 🤝 Building the Team


Realizing we were understaffed, we prioritized hiring. In the summer, we welcomed our founding engineer, whose contributions were transformative. By mid-year, we onboarded another customer in an unforgettable 2 a.m. call while attending a wedding abroad.


### 🎉 What a Year!


2024 has been a year of relentless learning—from building a team to mastering sales, customer success, and negotiations. We’re ending the year with a clearer understanding of our customers and their pain points. As we look ahead to 2025, our focus will be on building stickiness and driving re-engagement in a category that’s still new to many marketers.


Here’s to the next chapter and all the possibilities it holds. Thank you for being part of our journey!
