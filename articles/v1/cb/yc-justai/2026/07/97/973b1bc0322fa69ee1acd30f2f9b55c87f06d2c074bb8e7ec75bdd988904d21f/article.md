# Coursera scales personalization with JustAI decisioning

###### From bandwidth bottlenecks to AI-powered breakthroughs - Coursera drove growth, efficiency, and insights without writing a line of code.


> *”Don’t have to use external reporting tools for readouts”*

#### 🚀 The AI-Forward Collaboration


Coursera and Just Words share a bold vision: build AI-native systems that make personalization effortless, scalable, and ROI-positive.


Coursera has long led the charge in applying AI across learning, using it to create courses, coach learners, and assess progress. Just Words brings that same intelligence to lifecycle marketing, making it easy for teams to run hundreds of tests, personalize at scale, and surface insights automatically, without relying on engineers or analysts.


Together, they set out to answer a big question:


**What if Lifecycle personalization could be as fast as the internet it runs on?**


#### 🎯 Goals


Optimizing lifecycle emails at scale. Success meant hitting three objectives:


-


📈 Lift in revenue and email KPIs from improved performance


-


⚡️ 10x greater testing efficiency


-


💡 Faster, proactive insights to inform product and marketing


#### 📌 The Problem


Before Just Words, personalization Lifecycle messaging was slow and expensive:


-


Some cohort-level test required engineering lift and coordination


-


Performance readouts took hours of manual analysis across tools


-


Creative team bandwidth did not allow for multiple iterations of copy and limited to sprint cycles.


Despite having strong hypotheses and plenty of content ideas, bandwidth - not creativity - was the blocker.


#### 💡 The Just Words Solution


In under 2 weeks, Coursera was live on Just Words, testing, optimizing, and learning across dozens of messages with no engineering help.


📩 Here’s what changed:


-


**Scale without the sprawl** : **147** email variants tested in just one month, **452,000+ messages** personalized and optimized through Just Words


-


**AI powered** : **65** **AI decisions** made by Just Words' AI


-


**Hyper-targeting** : We personalized lifecycle messaging by degree, domain, and learner interest


-


**KPI optimizations** : We employed greedy optimizations to pick winning variants among many variants in real time, diverting resources to high performers based on true KPIs like enrollment and subscription rates


-


**Content Prediction:** We used performance indicators to predict the next best content


-


**Zero manual reporting** : Dashboards tracked every test and surfaced weekly insights


In essence: Coursera got the performance of a 3 person experimentation team, without hiring a single one.
