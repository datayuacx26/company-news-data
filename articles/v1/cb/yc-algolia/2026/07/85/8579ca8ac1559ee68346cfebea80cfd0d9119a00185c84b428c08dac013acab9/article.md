# Algolia Named a Leader in IDC MarketScape for General Purpose Knowledge Discovery Software

SAN FRANCISCO – December 9, 2025 –[Algolia,](https://www.algolia.com/) the[AI retrieval platform](https://www.algolia.com/products/ai-search) trusted by more than 18,000 businesses and millions of developers worldwide, today announced that it has been named a Leader in the[IDC Marketscape: Worldwide General-Purpose Knowledge Discovery Software 2025 Vendor Assessment (doc # US53011225, November 2025)](https://my.idc.com/getdoc.jsp?containerId=US53011225&pageType=PRINTFRIENDLY) . We believe this recognition underscores the company’s rising influence in the rapidly expanding Search and Knowledge Discovery Software market.


Algolia was also ranked 5th for 2024 market share in the[IDC Worldwide Search and Knowledge Discovery Software Market Shares, 2024 (doc # US53660925, August 2025)](https://my.idc.com/getdoc.jsp?containerId=US53660925&pageType=PRINTFRIENDLY&algoliaQueryID=&indexName=) .


According to the IDC Market Share report, the Search and Knowledge Discovery market grew 28.9% year over year, with Algolia capturing 4.2% global market share and outpacing nearly every major competitor as the fastest-growing independent vendor. The company achieved a 38.5% year-over-year growth in 2024, outpacing the total market by nearly 10%, fueled by enterprise adoption and AI-native innovation.


[Amy Machado, Senior Research Manager for Content and Knowledge Discovery Strategies, IDC](https://my.idc.com/getdoc.jsp?containerId=PRF003779&_gl=1*103p3kn*_ga*MjEyMjA3NjQ0MS4xNzUwMDk3OTQz*_ga_541ENG1F9X*czE3NjQ2MjM4NjQkbzM4JGcxJHQxNzY0NjIzOTU1JGo0NiRsMCRoMA..*_gcl_au*OTYwNDM0MDMuMTc2MDAyMjU4OS4xNDY5NzA3NjQ1LjE3NjQ2MjM5MTQuMTc2NDYyMzkyMQ..) , commented, “Search and knowledge discovery is the catalyst for AI innovation. The ability to find and connect the right information at the right time is the driving force behind modern AI-powered workflows and experiences.”


The MarketScape report states, “Algolia users can tailor their applications, from simple discovery to integrated operational workflows (i.e., the "buy online, pick up in-store" functionality). Algolia customers tell IDC that they appreciate the user experience and the flexibility to build solutions specific to their needs and goals without extensive development work.”


As enterprises continue to modernize commerce, content, media and knowledge ecosystems, Algolia’s platform continues to expand its global footprint.
