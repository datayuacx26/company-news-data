# Triumph Payments Reaches 100B+ in Total Payment Volume

Our mission is to provide best-in-class services and solutions for our customers and at the heart of that goal is to grow network transactions.


In 2016, TriumphPay created its network to bring real-time data to our customers. Finishing that year with just under $500,000 in network transaction volume, we began to see the power of secure, structured payment data exchange. Soon, our customers did as well. By 2020, we had over 5.7B in total payment transaction volume.


Year over year since, we have seen substantial growth and, in Q1 of 2025, we surpassed the milestone of $100 billion in payment volume. Triumph Payments has become the largest database of real-time transportation transactions in the world.


As we continue to drive forward, we celebrate the growth and success we have seen thus far, which would not have been possible without our customers, partners, and investors. Collectively, they saw the vision and what was possible.


This is a significant achievement for the whole Triumph organization, empowering us to provide unrivaled efficiency, secure and transparent transactions, and working capital to the transportation industry. We believe this is just the beginning of what’s possible for transportation.


Data will be at the core of the services and solutions we bring to market in the near future and long term, leveraged for our Payments and Intelligence divisions, giving us the opportunity to solve some of transportation’s toughest challenges.


With even more on the horizon, we look forward to continued growth, partnership, and value for transportation.
