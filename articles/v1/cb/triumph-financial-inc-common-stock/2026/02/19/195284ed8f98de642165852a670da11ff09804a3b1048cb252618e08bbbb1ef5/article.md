# Triumph to Present at the Raymond James 47th Annual Institutional Investors Conference on March 3, in Orlando, FL


February 23, 2026 7:00am EST


DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Triumph Financial, Inc. (NYSE: TFIN) today announced that Aaron P. Graft, founder, vice chairman & chief executive officer, will present at the Raymond James 47th Annual Institutional Investors Conference on March 3, 2026, in Orlando, Florida. Triumph’s presentation is scheduled to begin at 7:30 a.m. EST. The presentation will be webcast live and may be accessed through this direct link,[https://event.summitcast.com/view/VSr8zRPFYu9jT7Rm69ptdC/8GvvBN2YpJhqcXCxrHf6HM](https://event.summitcast.com/view/VSr8zRPFYu9jT7Rm69ptdC/8GvvBN2YpJhqcXCxrHf6HM) or via the Company's IR website at[ir.triumph.io](https://ir.triumph.io/) through the News & Events, IR Calendar links.


**About Triumph**


Triumph (NYSE: TFIN) is a transportation-focused financial and technology company that delivers payments, factoring, banking, and intelligence solutions designed to simplify and modernize freight transactions for brokers, carriers, shippers and factors. The company develops technology and financial products that improve operational efficiency, increase transparency and security in transactions, and expand access to working capital across the transportation industry. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, Triumph’s portfolio includes Triumph, LoadPay and TBK Bank. Learn more at[www.triumph.io](http://www.triumph.io/) .
