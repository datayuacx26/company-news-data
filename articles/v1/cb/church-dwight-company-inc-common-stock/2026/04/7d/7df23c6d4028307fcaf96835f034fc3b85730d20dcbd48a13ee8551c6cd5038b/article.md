# Church & Dwight Co., Inc. Declares 501st Regular Quarterly Dividend

### Church & Dwight Co., Inc. Declares 501st Regular Quarterly Dividend


April 29, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/233583214/files/doc_news/Church--Dwight-Co--Inc--Declares-501st-Regular-Quarterly-Dividend-2026.pdf)


EWING, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Church & Dwight Co., Inc. (NYSE:CHD) today reported that its Board of Directors declared a regular quarterly dividend of $0.3075 cents per share.


This quarterly dividend will be payable June 1, 2026, to stockholders of record at the close of business on May 15, 2026. It is the Company's 501st regular consecutive quarterly dividend.


Church & Dwight Co., Inc. manufactures and markets a wide range of personal care, household and specialty products, under the Arm & Hammer brand name and other well-known trademarks.
