# XTI Aerospace to Participate in Needham's 15th Annual Virtual Industrial Tech, Robotics & Power Conference

*Management to Discuss Second Quarter Results, Strategy and Growth Opportunities with Institutional Investors*


DALLAS


, July 27, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- XTI Aerospace, Inc. (Nasdaq: XTIA) ("XTI Aerospace," "XTI," or the "Company"), an aerospace and advanced technology platform and parent company of Drone Nerds, LLC, ("Drone Nerds"), a leading drone solutions platform serving commercial, enterprise and government customers, today announced that members of its executive leadership team will participate in the 15th Annual Needham Virtual Industrial Tech, Robotics & Power Conference on August 17–18, 2026.


XTI Aerospace will be represented by Scott Pomeroy, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer; Brooke Turk, Chief Financial Officer; and Jeremy Schneiderman, Chief Executive Officer of Drone Nerds.


During the virtual conference, XTI's leadership team will participate in one-on-one meetings with institutional investors to discuss the Company's second quarter results, the continued execution of its strategic transformation, operating performance, long-term strategy and growth opportunities across the commercial, enterprise and government drone markets. Management will reference the Company's second quarter 2026 earnings presentation, which will be available in the Investor Relations section of the Company's website prior to the conference.


"Our second quarter earnings results and presentation will provide investors with an updated view of the continued execution of our strategic transformation, our operating and financial performance and our long-term strategy," said Scott Pomeroy, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of XTI Aerospace. "We look forward to discussing the continued growth of the Drone Nerds platform, our disciplined approach to executing our strategy, and the opportunities we see to create long-term shareholder value."


Institutional investors interested in scheduling a meeting with XTI Aerospace during the conference should contact their Needham & Company representative.


**About XTI Aerospace, Inc.** XTI Aerospace, Inc. (Nasdaq: XTIA) is an aerospace company providing unmanned aircraft systems ("UAS") solutions through its commercial drone solutions division, operated through Drone Nerds, LLC and two development-stage divisions focused on autonomous defense systems and domestic manufacturing of unmanned systems components designed to support federal procurement and sourcing requirements. XTI's commercial drone solutions business provides hardware distribution, training, service, repair, and lifecycle support to enterprise, public safety and government customers.


XTI Aerospace is headquartered in Dallas, Texas.
