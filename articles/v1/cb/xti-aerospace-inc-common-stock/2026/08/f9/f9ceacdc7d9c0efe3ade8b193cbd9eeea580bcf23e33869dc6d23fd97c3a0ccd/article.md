# XTI Aerospace Postpones Release of Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results

DALLAS, Aug. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — XTI Aerospace, Inc. (Nasdaq: XTIA) (“XTI Aerospace,” “XTI,” or the “Company”), an aerospace and advanced technology platform and parent company of Drone Nerds, LLC, (“Drone Nerds”), a leading drone solutions platform serving commercial, enterprise and government customers, today announced that it is postponing the release of its second quarter 2026 financial results.


The Company’s second quarter 2026 earnings conference call, previously scheduled for August 12, 2026, is also being postponed. The Company will provide additional information regarding the timing of its second quarter 2026 earnings conference call once the revised timing has been finalized.


The revised schedule will provide the Company and its advisors with additional time to complete certain financial review procedures associated with the quarter-end reporting process, while also accommodating related scheduling requirements. The Company believes the additional time is appropriate to allow these procedures to be completed in a thorough and orderly manner prior to the release of its second quarter financial results.


**About XTI Aerospace, Inc.**


XTI Aerospace, Inc. (Nasdaq: XTIA) is an aerospace company providing unmanned aircraft systems (“UAS”) solutions through its commercial drone solutions division, operated through Drone Nerds, LLC and two development-stage divisions focused on autonomous defense systems and domestic manufacturing of unmanned systems components designed to support federal procurement and sourcing requirements. XTI’s commercial drone solutions business provides hardware distribution, training, service, repair, and lifecycle support to enterprise, public safety and government customers.


XTI Aerospace is headquartered in Dallas, Texas.
