# XTI Aerospace Seeks Accelerated Development of TriFan 600

This FlightGlobal article discusses several recent developments concerning XTI Aerospace, including:


- Accelerated development initiatives for the TriFan 600 fixed-wing VTOL aircraft.
- Mesa Airline’s investment and conditional purchase order for up to 100 TriFan.
- LOI with AVX Aircraft Company to expedite the TriFan 600’s development and certification.
- Submission of new patent applications.
