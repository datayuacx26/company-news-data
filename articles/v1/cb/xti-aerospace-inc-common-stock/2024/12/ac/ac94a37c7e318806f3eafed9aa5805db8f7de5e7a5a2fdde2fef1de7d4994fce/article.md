# XTI Aerospace Conducts “General Familiarisation” Presentation of TriFan 600 VLCA to FAA

XTI Aerospace recently announced it has conducted a General Familiarisation (Gen Fam) presentation of the TriFan 600 VLCA (Vertical Lift Crossover Aircraft) with the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), reports a press release. This presentation is an important milestone towards FAA Type Certification for the TriFan.


More than 60 FAA representatives including members from the Central Certification Office as well as aviation experts from across the U.S attended the event.
