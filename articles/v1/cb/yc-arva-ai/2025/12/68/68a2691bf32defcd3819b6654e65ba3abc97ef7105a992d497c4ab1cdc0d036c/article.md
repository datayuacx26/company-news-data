# Arva AI is delighted to announce that Dajana Marsollek has joined as CRO (Chief Revenue Officer) | Arva AI - Blog

Arva is delighted to announce that **Dajana has joined the company full-time as Chief Revenue Officer (CRO).**


Dajana has already played a key role in Arva’s journey, working closely with the team as an advisor to shape go-to-market strategy and support some of the company’s earliest wins. She brings significant experience from her time at **Taktile** , where she was an early hire and helped build and scale their commercial function.


At Arva, Dajana will lead all revenue and go-to-market activities as the company continues to scale its platform, which **deploys agentic AI to automate financial crime compliance operations** and transform how teams work in this space.


“Dajana has been instrumental in our early growth, and she has a deep understanding of both our market and our mission,” said Rhim, CEO of Arva. “We’re thrilled to have her on board full-time to lead our commercial strategy and help us scale Arva’s impact.”


“Arva is solving one of the hardest problems in financial services, and doing it in a way that transforms how financial crime compliance teams work. After seeing the impact firsthand as an advisor, I knew I wanted to go all in and help build a world-class GTM organization and team.”


Dajana’s appointment marks an important milestone in Arva’s growth as the company continues to partner with financial institutions to modernise and automate their financial crime compliance operations.
