# Closing Out 2025 at Arva AI | Arva AI - Blog

As we wrap up 2025, it’s a moment to pause and reflect on just how far Arva AI has come in a single year.


This was a year defined by focus, execution, and an unwavering commitment to building something that genuinely works. With a small but mighty team, we didn’t just ship features—we built a product that delivers real outcomes for compliance teams operating at massive scale.


And the results speak for themselves.


#### 2025 by the Numbers


This year, Arva AI achieved milestones that would normally take teams many times our size:


-


**32× ARR growth**


-


**$XM in Annual Recurring Revenue**


-


**1M+ compliance reviews handled**


-


**12.4 years of analyst time saved**


Behind each of these numbers are real people—compliance analysts freed from repetitive manual work, teams scaling without burning out, and institutions finally able to focus their expertise where it matters most.


#### Highlights from the Year


Beyond the metrics, 2025 was packed with meaningful progress across every part of the company:


-


**Crossed seven-figure revenue with zero churn**
A clear signal that customers don’t just try Arva—they stick with it.


-


**Welcomed an exceptional group of advisors**
Seasoned operators and thinkers who’ve helped us sharpen our strategy, raise our ambition, and execute with more precision.


-


**Went deeper into research and accuracy**
We invested heavily in advancing our models and investigative capabilities, because in financial crime, precision isn’t a nice-to-have—it’s everything.


-


**Delivered step-change efficiency for leading banks and fintechs**
Working with some of the largest institutions in the world, we repeatedly heard the same feedback:
*“You’re more accurate than teams of humans and any other product.”*
It’s the best compliment we could receive—and a responsibility we take seriously.


#### Thank You


None of this happens in isolation.


To our customers, advisors, investors, partners, and everyone who has supported Arva AI along the way: thank you for believing in us, backing us, and trusting us with some of the most critical workflows in financial services. We don’t take that trust for granted for a second.


#### Looking Ahead to 2026


Next year is about going deeper, moving faster, and raising the bar—again.
