##


Tough Commerce Cost Breakdown: What You Need to Know


Tough Commerce offers a range of pricing options designed to meet the needs of construction businesses of all sizes. The cost depends on several factors, including the features you need and the level of support you want.


Here’s a simple breakdown of the typical pricing components:


-


**Base subscription fee** : This covers access to the core platform and essential features.


-


**Yard licenses** : Costs increase with the number of primary locations (Yards) where your equipment is used.


-


**Add-on modules** : Optional features like advanced reporting, equipment tracking, or custom integrations may come at an extra charge.


-


**Implementation and training** : Some plans include self-serve onboarding, while others may require additional fees for setup and additional training.


-


**Support packages** : Premium support options can be purchased for faster response times and dedicated assistance.


Understanding these components helps you estimate your total cost and avoid surprises.
