# Quest Diagnostics to Present New Research at the 2026 ADLM Annual Meeting

Experts from Quest Diagnostics will highlight data from 13 studies on a range of laboratory topics at the 2026[Association for Diagnostics & Laboratory Medicine’s](https://nam04.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmyadlm.org%2Fmeetings-and-events%2Fannual-meeting&data=05%7C02%7CMichael.J.Wagner%40questdiagnostics.com%7Cf07a2df40b334a76da0508dee9b4df37%7Cb68c6481b22b46b38c4c0024bb9b9b1f%7C1%7C0%7C639205160240265819%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=yPDeFRgxWCVKooFeNiH5uBHOhXeGmuE%2Be9pWJ0aoU58%3D&reserved=0) (ADLM) Annual Meeting, July 26-30, 2026 in Anaheim, California. The studies cover a range of the company’s clinical focus areas, including cardiometabolic and endocrinology, infectious disease and immunology, neurology, prescription drug monitoring and toxicology, and oncology.


“At Quest Diagnostics, we believe that laboratory testing is at the center of healthcare, providing insights to make health more proactive, personal, and connected,” said board-certified oncologist, pathologist and internist Dr. Yuri Fesko, Senior Vice President and Chief Medical Officer, Quest Diagnostics. “ADLM is the lab industry’s premier conference, and we look forward to sharing our findings based on our innovations and unique visibility into the nation’s health.”


In addition to the new research, Wen Dui, Associate Director of R&D Toxicology, Quest Diagnostics, will host a roundtable discussion titled “Emerging Environmental Toxins: PFAS Forever Chemicals and Beyond” on July 27 from 7:30 – 8:30 am and 12:30 – 1:30 pm P.T. Posters being presented by Quest researchers:

**Neurology**

**Oncology**
