Arch was founded in 2001 as a company that promotes progress and inspires our clients, employees, investors and communities to achieve their greatest potential.


With some 25 years of steady growth, we’ve expanded both organically and through strategic acquisitions. We’ve grown to more than 8,000 employees across four continents and surpassed $30 billion in capital.


This strong history has built a resilient foundation for future growth, while preserving our entrepreneurial, winning culture. Through it all, our[Purpose](https://www.archgroup.com/about/our-purpose-and-values/?utm_source=growth-story&utm_medium=link) has remained the same: **We Enable Possibility** .
