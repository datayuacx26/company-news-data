# What Is USM and Why Are More Airlines Choosing It?

In today’s highly competitive and cost-sensitive aviation environment, airlines are under constant pressure to reduce maintenance expenses without compromising safety or performance. One of the most effective strategies gaining traction across the industry is the use of Used Serviceable Material (USM).


But what exactly is USM, and why are more airlines incorporating it into their maintenance programs?


Understanding USM in Aviation


Used Serviceable Material (USM)


refers to aircraft components that have been removed from in-service aircraft, inspected, tested, and certified as airworthy according to stringent industry and regulatory standards. These parts can include everything from landing gear and APUs to avionics and flight controls.


Rather than purchasing new OEM parts, which can be costly and sometimes have long lead time airlines can source cost-saving aviation parts through reputable aftermarket providers like AerSale. These USM components deliver equivalent performance at a fraction of the price.


**Key Benefits of Aircraft USM**


**1. Significant Cost Savings**


On average, USM parts can offer savings of 30% to 60% compared to new parts. For airlines operating on thin margins, these savings can translate to millions of dollars annually in reduced maintenance costs.


**2. Faster Turnaround Times**


By sourcing from an established USM inventory, operators can avoid lengthy OEM lead times. This helps keep aircraft flying and reduces costly downtime—a key operational advantage.


**3. Proven Performance and Reliability**


All USM components are subjected to comprehensive inspections and overhauls, often by FAA- or EASA-certified repair stations. When sourced from trusted providers, USM offers performance and safety equivalent to new parts.


**4. Environmental Sustainability**


Using recycled and recertified parts supports sustainability in aviation by reducing waste and lowering the environmental impact of manufacturing new components.
