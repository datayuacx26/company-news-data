# How Predictive Maintenance and Digitalization Are Reshaping the Industry

The aviation aftermarket is entering a new era. As operators face increasing pressure to improve reliability, control costs, and minimize downtime, the world of **Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul (MRO)** is being transformed by **predictive technologies, digital platforms, and engineered solutions** .


With 2025 on the horizon, understanding these shifts is critical for fleet managers, lessors, and airline executives alike. Here’s a look at the top **aviation MRO trends for 2025** —and how forward-looking providers like **AerSale** are helping drive the industry forward.


### 1. Predictive Maintenance Is No Longer Optional


In the past, operators relied on scheduled maintenance and reactive repairs to manage fleet health. In 2025, **predictive maintenance** is taking center stage. Leveraging real-time data and analytics, airlines can now anticipate component failures **before they happen** , reducing AOG events and improving dispatch reliability.


Key benefits include:


- Lower unscheduled maintenance costs
- Improved parts planning and inventory management
- Increased aircraft uptime and availability


### 2. Digital MRO Solutions Are Accelerating Efficiency


Digitalization is impacting every step of the MRO lifecycle—from maintenance tracking to records management to quality assurance. In 2025, expect increased investment in:


- **Mobile MRO tools** and paperless work orders
- **Digital twins** for aircraft and components
- **AI-driven task scheduling** and workforce optimization


These technologies not only reduce human error and improve traceability but also **streamline compliance and regulatory reporting** , saving time and money across the board.


At AerSale, we are integrating digital tools across our MRO network—from mobile inspection systems to digital customer portals that keep operators informed in real-time.


### 3. Used Serviceable Material (USM) Is Gaining Ground


With supply chain bottlenecks and rising OEM lead times, **used serviceable material (USM)** is emerging as a key pillar in modern MRO strategies. By incorporating **certified USM** into maintenance plans, operators benefit from:


- Lower material costs
- Shorter turnaround times
- Sustainable reuse of high-value assets


As one of the industry’s leading providers of USM, **AerSale pairs MRO services with in-house parts support** , giving customers a one-stop solution for cost-effective, high-quality repairs.


### 4. Engineered Repairs and DER Capabilities Are in High Demand


OEM parts pricing continues to rise, and operators are increasingly seeking **engineered alternatives** to expensive replacements. In 2025, **DER (Designated Engineering Representative) repairs** will play a major role in cost containment strategies.


AerSale’s **in-house engineering team** is actively developing custom repair solutions and FAA-approved modifications that help our customers extend component life without compromising performance or safety.


Our approach blends **innovation with regulatory expertise** , helping airlines navigate today’s economic challenges with smarter, more sustainable MRO strategies.


### 5. ESG and Sustainability Are Driving Decisions


Sustainability isn’t just a buzzword—it’s a core consideration for airlines in 2025. From reducing carbon emissions to embracing circular economy practices, the MRO sector is being reshaped by environmental, social, and governance (ESG) goals.


Digital maintenance platforms, USM integration, and predictive monitoring all contribute to a more **resource-efficient** MRO operation. AerSale’s focus on **asset reuse, green disassembly, and life-extension engineering** is helping customers meet ESG benchmarks while improving their bottom line.


### **Looking Ahead**


As 2025 unfolds, the convergence of **digital innovation, predictive intelligence, and engineered efficiency** will continue to redefine the MRO landscape. Operators that embrace these trends will not only stay competitive—they’ll lead the next wave of aviation excellence.
