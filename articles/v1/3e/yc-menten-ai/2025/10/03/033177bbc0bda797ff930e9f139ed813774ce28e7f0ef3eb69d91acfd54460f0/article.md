# Andrea McNeal Joins Menten AI as VP of Business Development

🎉 Big news at[Menten AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/menten-ai/) — we’re thrilled to welcome[Andrea McNeal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andreamcneal/) as our new Vice President of Business Development! Andrea brings a stellar track record of building transformative partnerships across biotech and pharma, including at[AbbVie](https://www.linkedin.com/company/abbvie/) and[Genentech](https://www.linkedin.com/company/genentech/) /[Roche](https://www.linkedin.com/company/roche/) , from multi-target oncology collaborations to platform licensing deals that bridge cutting-edge science with real-world impact. She knows how to turn complex ideas into relationships that move programs — and industries — forward. At Menten, Andrea will lead our efforts to expand strategic collaborations and help scale our AI-driven peptide design platform to new heights. We couldn’t be more excited to have her on board. Welcome, Andrea — let’s unlock the potential of peptides macrocycles to create tomorrow's life-changing medicines! 🚀
