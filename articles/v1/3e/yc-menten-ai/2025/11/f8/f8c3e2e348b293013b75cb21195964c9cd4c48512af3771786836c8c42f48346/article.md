# Menten AI presents at BioEurope 2025

Menten AI was proud to have our CEO, **Hans Melo** , and VP of Business Development attend and present at **BioEurope 2025** , held November 3–5. The event brought together global leaders in biotechnology and pharma to explore collaboration and innovation in drug discovery. Our team shared Menten AI’s latest advances in AI-driven peptide design and connected with partners shaping the future of biotech.
