# Menten AI Attends the Swiss Peptide Therapeutics Forum

#### Menten AI Invited by the Swiss Chemical Society to Discuss its Platform


Menten AI was invited to speak at this year's Peptide Therapeutics Forum.[Hans Melo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hansmelo/) represented the company and highlighted recent advancements to our platform for designing cell permeable and orally bioavailable cyclic peptide therapeutics.


‍


##### About the SCS


The[Swiss Chemical Society](https://scg.ch/) (SCS) promotes chemical sciences in Switzerland through conferences, networking, and education. Its support for peptide research is highlighted by the annual[Peptide Therapeutics Forum](https://ptf25.scg.ch/) , which "provides an innovative platform to exchange on the various aspects of drug development with a focus on peptides." (SCS, 2025)
