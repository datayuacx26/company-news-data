# Veriff achieves Cyber Essentials Certificate


We are glad to announce that Veriff is one of the first IDV companies that has been awarded with Cyber Essentials Certificate.


In today’s increasingly interconnected world, cybersecurity is more important than ever before. Cyberattacks are becoming more sophisticated and widespread, requiring businesses to have protective measures in place in all fields. That’s why we’re especially proud to be one of the first IDV companies to have obtained the certification.


## Effective government-backed standard


Cyber Essentials is an effective government-backed standard that protects against the most common cyber attacks. Obtaining this certification demonstrates that Veriff takes cybersecurity seriously and has taken steps to protect its data and systems.


The Cyber Essentials Certificate sets a new security standard giving Veriff a competitive advantage in the identity verification field. This achievement is a testament to the company’s unwavering commitment to cybersecurity and our dedication to protecting our customers’ data.


“Cyber Essentials is a valuable asset to showcase the dedication to cybersecurity at Veriff. It serves as an essential resource for retaining our customers while drawing in new ones as we extend our reach to broader global markets.”


**Fanny Lamp** InfoSec Compliance Manager,


Veriff


## The key benefits of Cyber Essentials Certification


- **Enhanced Security** : The certification ensures that Veriff has implemented fundamental cybersecurity measures, enhancing the overall security posture
- **Customer Trust:** It builds trust with our customers, assuring them that Veriff takes data security seriously and has implemented best practices to protect against cyber threats.
- **Competitive Advantage:** In an era where cybersecurity is a critical consideration for businesses, the Cyber Essentials certification sets Veriff apart, giving it a competitive edge.
- **Risk Mitigation:** By adhering to the Cyber Essentials framework, Veriff actively mitigates the risk of common cyber threats, safeguarding both internal operations as well as the interests of those we serve.
