# CommodityAI Partners with Pyxis Group and Principia Consulting | Agentic AI for Commodity Operations

*Why we've partnered with Pyxis Group and Principia Consulting to bring agentic AI to commodity operations.*


Walk onto any commodity trading floor and watch where the work actually happens. A surprising amount of it isn't in the CTRM. It's in the inbox.


Contracts arrive as PDF attachments. Nominations come in over email. Confirmations, shipping documents, and counterparty back-and-forth all move through email, documents, and chat — unstructured, inconsistent, and written for humans to read.


Then someone has to get that information into the systems that run the business. CTRM and ERP platforms need structured, accurate data to do their job: every position, every settlement, every invoice depends on it. So operations teams spend their days as a human bridge between the two worlds — reading messages, keying in fields, chasing missing details, and reconciling what the email said against what the system shows.


That gap, between how trade information arrives and how systems need to consume it, is where the manual work lives. It's where reconciliation errors creep in. And it's where operational risk compounds as volumes grow and teams stay flat.


## A partnership built around a real operational problem


Today we're announcing a partnership aimed squarely at that gap. CommodityAI is partnering with **Pyxis Group** , a global advisory firm serving the commodities industry, and its joint venture partner **Principia Consulting** , a specialist implementation firm and one of ION Commodities' integration partners. Together, the three firms will help commodity traders, producers, and industrial consumers modernize their operations and put AI to work in a way that lasts.


Here's how the pieces fit.


CommodityAI brings the technology. Our AI agents sit at the intersection of communications and systems — reading the unstructured information that arrives by email, document, and chat, reconciling it, and writing structured records back into the CTRM and ERP platforms where trade and operations data lives. The result is cleaner data, faster processing, and fewer hands touching every transaction.


Pyxis brings the advisory depth. The firm works with asset-backed traders and trading companies across business transformation, M&A, CTRM and ERP implementation, AI adoption, and organizational change.


Principia brings the implementation muscle — deep, hands-on expertise across the major CTRM, ETRM, and ERP platforms the industry actually runs on.


Put together, that's a rare thing in this market: a single path from strategy, to implementation, to intelligent automation. "Together we can take clients from operational pain to intelligent, automated workflows," said Philip Koenig, CEO and Co-Founder of CommodityAI — "and do it in a way that sticks."


## Build the data foundation first


There's a reason the sequence matters.


A lot of commodity firms come to AI with an analytics ambition — predictive models, machine learning, automated risk monitoring. Then they hit the wall every data team knows well: the models are only as good as the data underneath them. And in commodity operations, that data is scattered across inboxes and documents, rarely clean enough to trust.


So this partnership is built in the right order. First, establish a reliable structured-data foundation by capturing what arrives in unstructured channels and writing it cleanly into the systems of record. Then layer on the advanced analytics and AI as a firm's program matures. Foundation first, ambition second. It's the unglamorous step most teams skip — and the one that decides whether everything built on top of it actually works.


That sequencing reflects where most operations teams really are today: high transaction volumes, legacy workflows, and steady pressure to do more with leaner teams.


## Built for the major trading hubs


Across the three firms, the partnership has a presence in the United States, Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia — covering the major commodity trading hubs where clients operate and where demand for operational modernization is accelerating.


It's a combination clients have been asking for. As Kunal Ramtri, Managing Director at Pyxis Group, put it: the partnership is "genuinely end-to-end," supporting clients "from strategy through to live deployment, across every major market where we operate."


## The takeaway


Commodity operations are at an inflection point. The firms that pull ahead won't be the ones with the boldest AI roadmap on a slide — they'll be the ones who fix the data foundation first, then build. That's exactly what this partnership is designed to do.


If your operations team is still the bridge between the inbox and the CTRM, we'd like to show you what closing that gap looks like.
