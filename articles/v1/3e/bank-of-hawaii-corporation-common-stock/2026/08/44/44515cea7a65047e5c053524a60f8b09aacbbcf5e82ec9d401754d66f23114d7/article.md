# Bank of Hawai‘i to Temporarily Close Branches on Hawai‘i Island and Maui County Due to Hurricane Lala

Due to Hurricane Lala, Bank of Hawai‘i will temporarily close all branch operations on Hawai‘i Island and Maui County **tomorrow, Saturday, August 15** .


Bank of Hawai‘i will continue to assess the status of its branch network, and will update the public on branch closures and reopenings as the storm continues on its path.


Bank of Hawai‘i’s Contact Center operations remain open to assist customers and can be reached at (808) 643-3888. Online and mobile banking and ATM services will continue to operate unless power interruptions are experienced.


Bank of Hawai‘i will also place public updates on all of its social media platforms (Facebook:[www.facebook.com/bankofhawaii](https://www.facebook.com/bankofhawaii) , Instagram:[www.instagram.com/bankofhawaii](https://www.instagram.com/bankofhawaii) and X (formerly Twitter:[www.twitter.com/bankofhawaii](https://www.twitter.com/bankofhawaii) ), as well as its website:www.boh.com .
