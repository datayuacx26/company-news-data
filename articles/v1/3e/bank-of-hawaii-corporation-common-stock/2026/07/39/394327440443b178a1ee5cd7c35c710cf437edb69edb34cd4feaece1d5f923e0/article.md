# Bank of Hawai‘i to Reopen Guam and Saipan Branches Following Super Typhoon Bavi

Following Super Typhoon Bavi, Bank of Hawai‘i will reopen its Tamuning, Harmon, and Micronesia Mall branches on Guam, and its Gualo Rai Branch on Saipan tomorrow, Wednesday, July 8, ChST during the following business hours:


Guam Branch hours:
• Tamuning and Harmon:


o Monday - Friday: 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.
o Saturday: 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.


• Micronesia Mall Kiosk:


o Monday - Friday: 10 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.
o Saturday: 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.


Gualo Rai Branch hours on Saipan:
• Monday - Friday: 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.
• Saturday: closed on Saturdays until further notice


Given the power outages across the West Pacific region, some Bank of Hawai‘i ATMs on Guam and Saipan are not functioning. For a current list of ATMs that are operating on Guam and Saipan, please visit the Bank of Hawai‘i website at:[www.boh.com/locations](https://www.boh.com/locations) .


Call Center operations remain open to assist customers. West Pacific region customers may continue to contact Bank of Hawai‘i through its Call Center at 1-877-553-2424 for Guam and Saipan. Bank of Hawai‘i’s Call Center on Guam will be automatically re-routed to Hawai‘i.


Online and mobile banking services will continue to operate. Bank of Hawai‘i will also place public updates on all of its social media platforms (Facebook:[www.facebook.com/bankofhawaii](http://www.facebook.com/bankofhawaii) , Instagram:[www.instagram.com/bankofhawaii](http://www.instagram.com/bankofhawaii) and X (formerly Twitter:[www.twitter.com/bankofhawaii](http://www.twitter.com/bankofhawaii) ), as well as its website:www.boh.com .
