# Tendo Achieves Key Data Security Certifications, Strengthening Commitment to Privacy and Protection

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE


*Tendo Updates PCI, SOC 2 Type 2, and HIPAA Certifications, Reinforcing Its Trusted Standards in Data Protection and Security*


\[ **Philadelphia, PA, January 28, 2025**


\]


—[Tendo](https://tendo.com/) , a software company committed to creating seamless healthcare experiences for patients, clinicians, and caregivers, has reinforced its commitment to data privacy and security by successfully completing multiple third-party audits. By renewing and updating its PCI, SOC 2 Type 2, and HIPAA certifications, Tendo demonstrates its dedication to maintaining and exceeding industry standards for safeguarding sensitive healthcare data.


> “The security of our customers’ data is critically important. As a result, we continue to focus on proactively monitoring, updating, and enhancing our data security programs and certifications,” says Tendo InfoSec Manager, Ken Armstrong. “These certifications reflect our dedication to safeguarding both our clients’ and their patients’ sensitive information, and ensure we remain at the forefront of healthcare data security.”


Tendo recently renewed several key security certifications as part of its commitment to maintaining the highest standards. This included the successful completion of a third-party PCI audit with Insight Assurance (QSA), ensuring full compliance with Payment Card Industry (PCI) standards. Additionally, the SOC 2 Type 2 certification was renewed, and HIPAA requirements were completed, further enhancing data protection measures. To validate its security controls, Tendo’s systems underwent rigorous internal and external penetration testing, with ongoing compliance across all platforms using industry-leading management tools.


> “By leveraging third-party auditors like Insight Assurance, Tendo is demonstrating a dedication to transparency and security that goes above and beyond basic compliance requirements,” says Matt Ernst, VP of Technical Operations and Support at Tendo. “I am proud to be a part of a team that takes data security so seriously at a time when it has become such a prevalent topic.”
