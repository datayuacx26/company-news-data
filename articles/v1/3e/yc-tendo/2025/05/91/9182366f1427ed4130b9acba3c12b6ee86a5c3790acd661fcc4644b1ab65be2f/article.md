# Driving Improved Hospital Quality Ratings: Tendo Insights Featured at ACDIS Annual Conference

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE


**\[Philadelphia, PA — May 1, 2025\]** —[Tendo](https://tendo.com/) , a leading healthcare software company focused on providing advanced data-driven insights, today announced its participation in the[2025 ACDIS Annual Conference](https://acdis.org/acdis-conference) , taking place May 4–7 in Orlando, Florida. Tendo will host an exhibit at Booth #801, connecting with clinical documentation (CDI) leaders to showcase how Tendo Insights helps hospitals and health systems get the quality credit they deserve.


Attendees can learn how Tendo’s advanced analytics and streamlined workflows support smarter documentation, enhanced quality ratings, and operational performance. From surfacing AI-identified documentation gaps to delivering real-time insights across CMS, USNWR, Leapfrog, and other quality programs, Tendo equips CDI and quality teams to improve documentation accuracy, safeguard revenue, and elevate organizational rankings.


**Key benefits of Tendo Insights include:** ****


-


Smarter, data-driven documentation workflows
-


Real-time visibility into quality program performance
-


AI-powered gap identification and opportunity insights
-


Enhanced hospital reputation, higher ratings, and reduced penalties


In addition, Deb Jones, Senior Director of Insights at Tendo, will deliver an educational session titled “Finding Meaningful Purpose in CDI: Connecting CDI Work to *Patient* Advocacy, Quality, and Professional Fulfillment”. The presentation will explore how CDI specialists can link their daily work to broader organizational goals, patient care outcomes, and personal career satisfaction.


> ***“CDI teams play a critical role in clinical quality, financial health, and patent advocacy,” said Deb. “I’m excited to be part of the conversation at ACDIS and share how Tendo Insights is helping CDI leaders elevate their work and their organization’s performance.”*** ******


To learn more about Tendo Insights and schedule a meeting at ACDIS, visit[tendo.com](https://tendo.com/) .
