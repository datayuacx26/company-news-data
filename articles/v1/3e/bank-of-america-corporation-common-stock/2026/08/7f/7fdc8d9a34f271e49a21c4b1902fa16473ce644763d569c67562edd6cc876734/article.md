# U Mass UMass Memorial Health Receives two hundred fifty thousand dollars $250,000 Grant from Bank of America to Support the North Pavilion

August 3, 2026 at 1:00 PM Eastern


#


UMass Memorial Health Receives $250,000 Grant From Bank of America to Support the North Pavilion


## Key points


- Bank of America is providing a **two hundred fifty thousand dollar $250,000 grant** to support **expansion of UMass Memorial Medical Center’s North Pavilion**
- Funding will help complete **24 additional patient rooms** , expanding access to high-quality care in Worcester
- The gift marks the **first corporate donation to the campaign**
- Bank of America continues its **longstanding commitment to Worcester** , including local philanthropic giving, volunteerism and support for economic opportunity


## Key points


- The gift marks the **first corporate donation to the campaign**


Rendering of the new UMass Memorial Health North Pavilion exterior


WORCESTER, Massachusetts


M.A.


– UMass Memorial Health today announced it has received a two hundred fifty thousand dollar


$250,000


Anchor Grant from Bank of America. This significant funding will advance expansion of the state-of-the art North Pavilion, part of UMass Memorial Medical Center. This marks the first corporate gift toward the North Pavilion philanthropic campaign.


*“We are grateful to Bank of America for this generous contribution,”* said **Justin Precourt, President of UMass Memorial Medical Center** . *“This investment helps support the expansion of the North Pavilion, enabling us to provide high-quality care to more patients while addressing growing capacity needs.”*


The North Pavilion currently has 72 private patient rooms for acute care, with a focus on chronic disease management. The facility was designed with shell space to accommodate 24 additional patient beds, and this expansion will complete those rooms.


*“At Bank of America, we know growing communities need access to high-quality medical care,”* said **Michael Garand, President of Bank of America Worcester** . *“We’re proud to partner with UMass Memorial Health to help expand the North Pavilion to better serve our community.”*


The new rooms are expected to open in summer 2027.


## Frequently asked questions


### Question: Why is Bank of America investing in healthcare infrastructure like the North Pavilion?


**Answer:** Bank of America is committed to strengthening the communities where it operates by supporting access to essential services, including healthcare. Investments like this help ensure Worcester residents have access to high-quality care while supporting long-term community growth.


### Question: How does this grant reflect Bank of America’s broader commitment to Worcester?


**Answer:** Bank of America has a strong and ongoing presence in Worcester, with approximately **170 local employees** , **14 financial centers** , and a long history of community investment. Since 2020, the company has contributed about **two point nine million dollars $2.9 million in philanthropic giving** , delivered more than **10,500 volunteer hours** , and provided significant lending to local businesses to support economic growth.


### Question: What areas does Bank of America prioritize in Worcester?


**Answer:** Bank of America works with local partners to advance **economic opportunity, stable housing and community development** , while also supporting arts and culture organizations that help Worcester thrive.
