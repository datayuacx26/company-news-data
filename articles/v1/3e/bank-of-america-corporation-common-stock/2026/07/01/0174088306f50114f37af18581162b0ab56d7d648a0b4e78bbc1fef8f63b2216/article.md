## Methodology


Sparks Research conducted a national online survey on behalf of Bank of America from April 13 to May 10, 2026. A total of 2,000 surveys (1000 homeowners and 1000 renters) were completed with adults 18 years old or older, who make or share in household financial decisions, and who currently own a home/previously owned a home or plan to own a home in the future. Select questions allowed respondents to choose more than one answer, resulting in responses that may equate to more than 100 percent.
