# Bank of America brings together Sir David Beckham and a new U2 song for Street of Dreams , a short film highlighting how support, belief and sport can change a young person’s future.

July 8, 2026 at 8:00 AM Eastern


#


Bank of America brings together Sir David Beckham and a new U2 song for *Street of Dreams* , a short film highlighting how support, belief and sport can change a young person’s future.


LONDON – Powered by Bank of America, the official bank of the FIFA World Cup twenty twenty six


2026


™ and released during the tournament is a new short film *[Street of Dreams](https://youtu.be/RHBopni_xTo) ,* which tells the story of a young girl whose life has been upended through homelessness but refuses to give up on her dream of playing soccer. Set to the new U2 track, also titled 'Street of Dreams', the film features Bank of America’s[Sports with Us Global Ambassador](https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/company-overview/initiatives/sports-with-us.html) Sir David Beckham to tell an uplifting story about resilience, hope and the transformative power of sport.


The short film tells the story of a young girl 'Calle' played by newcomer Nevaeh Derricks. After her life is disrupted, Calle is seen holding on to soccer as a source of purpose and possibility. As she navigates uncertainty and self-doubt, she is guided by Sir David Beckham who appears as a personification of her inner voice, a guardian only she can see. He's a powerful symbol of belief and opportunity, standing with her and showing up when it matters.


The film highlights Bank of America’s recent commitment to grow their '[Soccer with Us](https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/company-overview/initiatives/sports-with-us/soccer0.html) ' platform, which is showcased in the film. By the end of twenty twenty six
