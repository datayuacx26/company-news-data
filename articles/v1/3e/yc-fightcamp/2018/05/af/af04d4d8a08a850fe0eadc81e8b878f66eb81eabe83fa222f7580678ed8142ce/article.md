# FightCamp App Update

In this blog post, we run you through what's new in the latest version of the FightCamp app.


From a totally new user for both iPad and iPhone to filters that will make it easier to select a workout, the latest update to the app makes FightCamp a lot more user friendly.


### New Workout Selection Screen


It is a thing of beauty. A feast for the eyes. The new workout selection screen makes it easier to go through the workouts.


### New iPad User Interface


It is a thing of beauty. A feast for… Alright! you get it. For all iPad owners out there, we’ve redesigned the user interface so you have a better experience interacting with the FightCamp app.


### Filters


Looking for the next workout you want to do will be easier than ever using the filters. You now can sort out workouts by trainers, length and difficulty level. That being said, it’s always good to mix things up a little.


### More Information on the Workouts


There is now a way to avoid getting blindsided by a round of nasty plank punches. You now have access to a workout overview. This will all you to walk into your workout with a clear idea of the challenge you’ll be facing.


While we were at it we also squashed a couple of tiny little bugs here and there. All in all, we were able to make the app better for you thanks to your feedback. Please keep it coming.


Thank you from the FightCamp Team
