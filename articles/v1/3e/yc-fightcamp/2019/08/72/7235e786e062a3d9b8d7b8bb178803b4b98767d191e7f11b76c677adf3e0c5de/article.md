# FightCamp Introduces The Contender Path

Are you looking to follow a workout program all laid out for you? A path that will bring your boxing skills and physical conditioning to the next level? FightCamp is proud to introduce


**the Contender Path**


, now live in the app.


## What Is The Contender Path?


The Contender Path is made for those who are coming to FightCamp with previous boxing experience or who have completed the Prospect Path.


The


**Contender Path**


is your entry point to intermediate workouts and intermediate skills. You will learn how to step and punch, how to slip, how to roll and how to mix all these movements into challenging combinations. This intermediate path will build up your endurance and cardio by having take on 10-round workouts. New skills, new endurance, new fitness level, NEW YOU!


FightCamp Trainers Tommy Duquette and Aaron Swenson will guide you through the Contender Path. Tommy is a former US National Boxing Team member with 136 fights (over 100 wins) and over 15 years of experience as a trainer. Aaron is a former 2-time National Kickboxing Champion with 27 wins and years of experience as a personal trainer. Who said you couldn't learn from the best, from the comfort of your home?


Connect with other FightCamp users, share your progress on the Contender Path and find the extra motivation you're looking for on the FightCamp Community.
