# FightCamp App Update: Introducing the Leaderboard

Ever wondered how you compared to the rest of the FightCamp community? Well today is the day the guessing stops. In the latest version of the FightCamp app, you'll be able to know exactly where you stand thanks to the leaderboard.


You can now take your fight outside your house and engage in a little friendly competition with the rest of the FightCamp community. How cool is that?


We hope you are as excited as we are about the leaderboard. We'd love to see you share how you rank in our community.
