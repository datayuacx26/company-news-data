# FightCamp Launches a New Kickboxing Path

Kickboxing workouts are now available on the FightCamp app. In addition to the Prospect Path and Contender Path, you will now be able to take the reins of the all-new Kickboxing Path!


## What Is the Kickboxing Path?


This 8-10 week path is led by two-time National Champion and professional kick-boxer, Aaron Swenson and includes a mixture of tutorial videos, drills and workouts. In this path, you'll learn the kickboxing basics, including the proper stance, the round-kicks, front-kicks, side-kicks, and basic defensive moves.


The Kickboxing Path is perfect for those with little-to-no experience. For those coming in with prior experience, this path is perfect for reinforcing what you have learned in the past and will provide you some structure for continuing your skills in the future.
