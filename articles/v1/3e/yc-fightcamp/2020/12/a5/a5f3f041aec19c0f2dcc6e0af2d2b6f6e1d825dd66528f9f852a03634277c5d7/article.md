# Start With a Bang 2021 At-Home Fitness Challenge

Join FightCamp and thousands of FightCamp community members for our Start With a Bang 2021 Challenge! It's the perfect at-home fitness and boxing challenge to start the year off right!


## What Is The Start With a Bang 2021 Challenge?


In the FIRST 21 days of January, we challenge you to PUNCH with us.


We’ve heard that it takes an average of 21 days to form a habit, so let’s start the New Year strong, and start punching together!


From January 1-21st, we invite you to join us. Together, we are setting the bar high--but we KNOW you can do it!


We want to donate $10,000 to


[Boston Boxing’s](https://www.bostonboxing.com/)


after-school boxing program,


Fit Students For Life, so these are the punch goals and donation amounts we’ve set:


-


**50 MILLION punches unlocks the first $5K**


-


**60 MILLION punches unlocks another $2.5K**


-


**70 MILLION punches unlocks the final $2.5K**


## Why Should I Join?


If you hit the punch goals we’ve set, we will take $10,000 and donate it to the Fit Students For Life after school program in Boston. This after school boxing program is run out of the Boston Boxing Gym and provides under-privileged, inner-city youth with a fighting chance.


\[FightCamp trainer, Tommy Duquette, participated in this program as a kid.\]


## How Can I Join The Start With a Bang 2021 Challenge?


1.


If you're already a FightCamp Member, update your app to see the new feature and the challenge.


2.


Everyone with Punch Trackers is already entered!


3.


Download the Pledge Card and commit to a BIG GOAL


4.


Share your Pledge Card on your social media accounts. This helps you to stay accountable!


5.


On January 1st, 2021, start punching and unlock badges while hitting your personal best.


6.


Share FightCamp with your friends on and off the app so we can continue to share the gift of boxing with those in need.


Not a member yet? You can still come out swinging with us in 2021 by downloading the pledge card and committing to working out with us using free FightCamp workouts sent to your inbox. The rest of the FightCamp Community wants to cheer you on, too. Use the hashtag #startwithabang2021 on your posts. Search for it and cheer each other on.


## Challenge Details


In the app, you’ll be able to see the real-time punch stats of the entire community as you contribute to the goal as well as your own individual contribution. Not only will you be boxing for something greater than just your own goals, but you’ll be changing lives with every punch.


Still need more convincing? You will be able to unlock special badges in the app that haven’t ever been released before.


## Start With a Bang 2020 Challenge


Last year, we hit our punch goal and donated $10K to Boston Boxing Gym.


The FightCamp community threw 14,260,518 punches in the first 20 days of 2020. Our Top 3 collectively threw 503,781 punches and our Top 20 threw almost 2 million punches collectively.


More than anything, we want to shoutout to the strongest, fiercest fitness community on the planet! Even if we erase the insane numbers put up by the Top 20, we still would have surpassed our 12 million punch goal. This was an amazing community effort that proves we truly are stronger together.
