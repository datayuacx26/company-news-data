# How To Use Traditional Wraps with FightCamp

Did you know that you can use your FightCamp[Punch Trackers](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/boxing-equipment/improve-your-boxing-training-with-punch-trackers) with traditional boxing hand wraps too? If your quick wraps are in the wash, or you simply prefer using traditional wraps for your boxing and kickboxing workouts, then our easy[step-by-step hand wrapping guide](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/boxing-equipment/step-by-step-hand-wrapping-traditional-boxing-wraps/) will help teach you how you can keep tracking your punch output performance with traditional hand wraps.


## 7 Steps For Wrapping Your Hands With Punch Trackers


### Step 1


Reverse roll your wraps with the text facing inside


### Step 2


Place the loop over your thumb and wrap your wrist 3-4 times


### Step 3


Wrap between each finger, wrapping your wrist in between each finger


### Step 4


Wrap your knuckles 3-4 times, depending on your hand size (large hands 3 times, small hands 4 times)


### Step 5


Wrap your thumb, then wrap your wrist


### Step 6


Place the red punch tracker on your right wrist with the rounded edge towards your knuckles


### Step 7


Wrap the punch tracker onto your wrist, then finish wrapping your wrist and close velcro


Repeat the same steps for your left wrist with the blue punch tracker and get ready to do some punching!
