## How Do Boxers Workout?


Boxers do a series of high intensity circuit workouts so it’s never the same sequence, keeping you fully engaged and never knowing what’s next.


Boxers tend to have super lean bodies with defined muscles because there’s not a part of the body they don’t use. Boxing workouts typically consist of core and leg workouts since punching power comes from the base of the body upward.


Boxing is much more than punching. It also requires the boxer to be light and quick on their feet so boxing workouts also include drills like jumping rope which helps get the heart rate up.
