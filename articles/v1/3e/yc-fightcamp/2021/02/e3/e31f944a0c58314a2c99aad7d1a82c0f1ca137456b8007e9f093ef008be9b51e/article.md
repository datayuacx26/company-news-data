# 7-Day High Intensity Cardio Training Workout Program

This week’s workout focuses on building cardio endurance. Cardio endurance is essential for a healthy heart. It will also increase your daily performance and boost your energy. All levels can do this workout.


Don't forget, each FightCamp workout includes a quick warmup and a stretch but you can always add on an extended cool down for good measure.
