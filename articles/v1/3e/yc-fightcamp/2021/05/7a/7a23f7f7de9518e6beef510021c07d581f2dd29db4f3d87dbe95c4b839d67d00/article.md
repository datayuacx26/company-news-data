# 4 Essentials For Creating Your At-Home Boxing Gym

You want to start a boxing workout routine and make training part of your lifestyle, great! There are a few things you will need before you begin your exercise regimen. Here are the four (4) essentials:


## 1. Workout Space


You’ll need to set up a space to perform your workouts. Luckily, boxing workouts take up a minimal footprint in your home, garage, basement, or your living room. We recommend a training space that’s about 8 feet by 2 feet that you can designate for your punches and kicks.
