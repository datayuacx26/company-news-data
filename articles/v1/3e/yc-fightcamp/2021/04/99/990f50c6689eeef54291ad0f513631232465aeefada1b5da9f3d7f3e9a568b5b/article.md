# FREE 15-Minute At-Home Kickboxing Workout | Infographic

Join FightCamp Trainer Shanie Smash for this 4-round at-home kickboxing workout perfect for all levels. In just 15 minutes, practice boxing punch combinations and basic kickboxing moves, including the jab, the cross, the lead and rear kicks, and plank punches.


Each round consists of three (3) 1-minute drills, followed by 1 minute of rest. Get ready to break a sweat!


* Don't forget to add a


[warm-up](https://youtu.be/AvxRR1IDWvk)


and a


[cool down](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/8-minute-cool-down-static-stretching-routine-for-boxing)
