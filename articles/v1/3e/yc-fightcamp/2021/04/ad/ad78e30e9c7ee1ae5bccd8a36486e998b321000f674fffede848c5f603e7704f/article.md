## What Is a Free-Standing Punching Bag?


A free-standing (also known as "floor standing") punching bag is a punching bag that does not require mounting. Typically, it is attached to a heavy base that is weighted down with sand or water.


On the other hand, the traditional hanging punching bag requires mounting for installation. To install a hanging punching bag at home, you have a few available options, none of which is ideal. You either need to:


-


drill holes into your ceiling/walls


-


or hang it on a metal frame (which takes up space and can scratch your floor)


A free-standing punching bag not only doesn’t require professional installation, but it is also portable, so it can be moved around the house easily for storage and training. It’s a great option for practicing your punches and kicks at home.


## Is A Free-Standing Punching Bag A Good Workout?


In short, the answer is - yes. The free-standing heavy bag is an excellent alternative to a traditional hanging heavy bag, but there are two drawbacks with many free-standing punching bags on the market today: (1) their bases move a lot while you train, and (2) they are too light and tip over when you hit them. This is often the case for less expensive products in this category. Newer bags on the market have worked to address these problems, but some bags are still better than others.


## Are Free-Standing Punching Bags Better Than Traditional Heavy Bags?


Free-standing punching bags and traditional heavy bags are both great for boxing training. They both have pros and cons. As we mentioned before, a certain downside with the traditional heavy bag is that it requires permanent and professional installation for at-home use which is an additional expense. The best free-standing punching bag is portable and doesn’t require permanent or professional installation.


The only differences are the weight of the bag (the best free-standing heavy bag provides good resistance, but usually is lighter) and the swinging motion. ## How Tall & How Heavy Should a Free-Standing Punching Bag Be?


The proper height for a free-standing punching bag depends on the person who uses it. Typically, it is recommended that the bag be at least your height, if not taller. Since most of the shots and punches you throw will be on the same level as your arms and towards an opponent’s head, you need a bag that is at least that tall.


Additionally, if you are going to throw low kicks (e.g. as in Muay Thai and kickboxing), make sure that the bag has adequate padding. Many free-standing punching bags on the market are only padded partway down and do not have padding towards the base.


A free-standing punching bag should have a sturdy base for all of your punches and kicks. We recommend[filling the base](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/for-members/step-by-step-how-to-fill-your-heavy-bag-base/) of your free-standing bag with sand as it will weigh more, but a bag base filled with water will still give you a great workout. The base can fit 350 lbs. of sand or 250 lbs. of water.
