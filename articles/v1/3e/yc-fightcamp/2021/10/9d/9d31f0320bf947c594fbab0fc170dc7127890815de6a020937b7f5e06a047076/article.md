# Heavy Bag Review: Century Versys Bag Vs. FightCamp Bag

Selecting the perfect punching bag shouldn’t be overwhelming. While there are a lot of options available, a little bit of research can give you a clearer idea of what you need and what to look for in a boxing bag.


Comparing some of the more popular heavy bags on the market can be a great start. Here, FightCamp Trainer Aaron Swenson takes a look at some of the key features of the **Century Versys bag** and the **FightCamp Free-Standing bag** to see how they measure up.


## CENTURY VERSYS BAG


### STRAPS


- Has straps on the top so you can push and pull the bag as needed (great for drills like knee hits)


### LIGHTWEIGHT


- Easy to pick up and lay on the floor, ideal for MMA-style fighting


### MULTIUSE


- Good prop for plyometrics like jumps or toe touches


### SOFT


- Softer hitting surface makes it great for kids


## FIGHTCAMP FREE-STANDING BAG


### DURABLE


- Great for a more intense boxing or kickboxing workout and putting all your power behind your hits


### STABLE


- With a sand-filled base and a bag ring specially designed for FightCamp, this bag won’t move at all during your workouts


### TALL


- Excellent height for greater hitting surface and long enough for plank punches and low kicks


### STYLE POINTS


- Aesthetically pleasing for in-home use


## What Is The Best Punching Bag To Buy?


The[best punching bag](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/blogs/home-boxing-equipment/what-punching-bag-should-i-buy-for-at-home-use) to buy is the bag that fits your workout needs. Different bags are ideal for different uses, so it’s a very individual decision. Doing your research is the most important thing you can do to determine which bag is best for you.


## What Should I Look For In a Heavy Bag?


You should look for a heavy bag that is suited to your workout style, falls within your price range, and is made of decent quality. Unlike many other punching bags, a heavy bag should be sturdy enough to endure high impact punching and kicking. You will also want to decide if you prefer[a free-standing bag or a hanging bag](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/blogs/home-boxing-equipment/how-to-choose-the-right-punching-bag-for-your-workout) , whether you need it to be firmer or softer to hit, and how big you need it to be.


## What Is The Best Heavy Bag?


The best heavy bag is dependent on you as a boxer--your needs and your preferences. Doing your research is key to choosing the best heavy bag for you.
