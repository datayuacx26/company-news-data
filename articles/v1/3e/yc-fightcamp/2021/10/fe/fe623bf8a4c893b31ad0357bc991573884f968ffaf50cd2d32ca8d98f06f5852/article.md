Selecting the perfect punching bag shouldn’t be overwhelming. While there are a lot of options available, a little bit of research can give you a clearer idea of what you need and what to look for in a boxing bag.


Comparing some of the more popular heavy bags on the market can be a great start. Here, FightCamp Trainer[Aaron Swenson](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/fightcamp-trainer-spotlight-aaron-swenson) takes a look at some of the key features of the

**FightCamp Free-Standing bag**

## CENTURY VERSYS BAG REVIEW


### STRAPS


-


Has straps on the top so you can push and pull the bag as needed (great for drills like knee hits)


### LIGHTWEIGHT


-


Easy to pick up and lay on the floor, ideal for MMA-style fighting


### MULTIUSE


-


A good prop for plyometrics like jumps or toe touches


### SOFT


-


Softer hitting surface makes it great for kids


## FIGHTCAMP HEAVY BAG REVIEW


### DURABLE


-


Great for a more intense boxing or kickboxing workout and putting all your power behind your hits


### STABLE


-


With a sand-filled base and a bag ring specially designed for FightCamp, this bag won’t move at all during your workouts


### TALL


-


Excellent height for greater hitting surface and long enough for plank punches and low kicks


### STYLE POINTS


-


Aesthetically pleasing for in-home use

## What Should I Look For In a Heavy Bag?


You should look for a heavy bag that is suited to your workout style, falls within your price range, and is made of decent quality. Unlike many other punching bags, a heavy bag should be sturdy enough to endure high-impact punching and kicking. You will also want to decide if you prefer


[a free-standing bag or a hanging bag](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/blogs/home-boxing-equipment/how-to-choose-the-right-punching-bag-for-your-workout) , whether you need it to be firmer or softer to hit, and how big you need it to be.
