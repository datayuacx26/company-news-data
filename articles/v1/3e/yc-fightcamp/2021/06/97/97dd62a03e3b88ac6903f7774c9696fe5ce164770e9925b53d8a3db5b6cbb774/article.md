**punching bag** . There are several


**free-standing punching bags**


and


**home punching bags with stands**


to choose from.

**free-standing punching bags**

**BEST heavy bag for home gyms**


for at-home boxing workouts:


-


Everlast Elite Series


-


FightCamp Bag

Everlast Powercore

**boxing bags for home**


were tested by three (3) boxers:

**home punching bags**

## Is a Home Punching Bag Worth It?


The short answer is yes, and here is why. Boxing bags for home allow you to practice almost every move that you would use in a traditional boxing or kickboxing match. Practicing against air can only get you so far--this is known as shadowboxing. A home punching bag gives you resistance and the illusion of an opponent. This way, you can figure out your own style of fighting. That, combined with the stress relief that comes from hitting the bag for a while, makes finding the best heavy bag for a home gym well worth the cost.


## Is a Standing Punching Bag Better Than a Hanging Punching Bag?


This is mostly up to the individual. It depends on your training needs and the space you have available. For example, if your training needs are to simulate a real fight, a hanging bag might be more appropriate for you since the swinging motion of the bag after you hit it is more like a real fight. If your space doesn’t allow for a hanging bag to be permanently installed to the ceiling or you don’t have space for a hanging bag rack, then you might want to get a free-standing heavy bag. Both boxing bags for home, hanging punching bags and free-standing punching bags, have advantages and disadvantages.


## Hanging Punching Bags


### Pros


-


More resistance


-


Multiple workout variations


-


Do not take up a lot of floor space


-


Able to swing which simulates a real fight more than a bag that doesn’t swing. This can be helpful for training.


### Cons


-


Can be tricky to install


-


Require a lot of room to accommodate the swinging motion


-


Not portable


## Free-Standing Punching Bags


### Pros


-


Portable


-


Do not take up much open space


-


Offer a different level of resistance and weight


-


Easy to set up, not requiring hooks or brackets for installation


### Cons


-


Typically not be as stable and can topple over


-


Typically not as durable


-


Not able to swing


When choosing the best heavy bag for home gyms, make sure to evaluate what you will be using it for. This will help you when it comes to selecting and purchasing the right punching bag for you.


## Purchasing Your First At-Home Punching Bag


There are a few things to consider when buying your first boxing bag for home. First--the durability and quality. You do not want a home punching bag that will fall apart after the first training session.


Next, think about price. Consider your budget and the reviews of the boxing bags for home you are looking at. Ask yourself the following questions:


-


Does the punching bag fit your needs?


-


Do you plan on upgrading your heavy bag once you become more experienced?


-


Is this a good home punching bag for beginners?


These questions and considerations will lead you down the path to getting the best heavy bag for a home gym at a good value that will suit your needs.


## What is the Best At-Home Punching Bag to Buy?


Over the years many companies have released all sorts of punching bags, some are high quality and well worth the money, while others are better left…at the store. Buying your first punching bag can be intimidating, especially if you are new to combat sports.


It can be overwhelming when trying to decide what punching bag to buy. But once you decide on your needs, your space, and the price you’re willing to pay, a punching bag will be a valuable investment for your at-home boxing space and will help you train better and more effectively.
