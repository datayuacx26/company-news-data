# Best Punching (Heavy) Bag Boxing Gloves

## Do I Need Boxing Gloves For a Punching Bag?


The short answer is yes. Punching a heavy bag with boxing gloves increases punch power and endurance--the added protection of the boxing gloves allows you to deliver stronger punches. Heavy bag boxing gloves also provide additional support to your wrist and protect your knuckles, minimizing the risk of injury.


## What Weight Boxing Gloves Should I Get?


This is a common question that many beginner boxers have. Boxing gloves are sized using ounces. For specific details on which size boxing gloves you should purchase, read more[here](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/boxing-equipment/how-to-choose-the-best-boxing-gloves-for-beginners/) .


Hanging punching bags can weigh anywhere between 70 to 150 pounds, while freestanding punching bags can weigh 350+ pounds. Workouts on punching bags are usually focused on strength training as the heavy bag resists your strikes more than a normal bag.
