# NEW FightCamp Release: The Customer Dashboard

FightCamp members can now easily manage their accounts using the NEW FightCamp Customer Dashboard.


To give more convenience to the FightCamp members, account changes can now be done in just a few clicks instead of reaching out to our support team, giving FightCamp customers direct control on the status of their accounts.


## What can customers control in the Customer Dashboard?


The Customer Dashboard empowers FightCamp customers to be able to have EASY ACCESS to their personal information. In the new Customer Dashboard members will be able to:


-


Easily access their Personalized


***Referral Code***


-


Update their


*Credit Card*


Information or


*Billing Address*


-


*Pause*


or


*Manage*


their Subscription


## How does the Customer Referral Code work?


FightCamp customers now have an EASY way to access their personal referral code. With the FightCamp Referral code, members get to GIVE two months and GET two months FREE!


To EASILY access your personalized FightCamp referral code:


-


**LOGIN** our Customer Dashboard using this link


[HERE](http://account.joinfightcamp.com/)


-


**ENTER** the


*email address*


that is linked to your account.


*NOTE*


:


*This must be the account user’s email that was initially sent the activation code upon sign up)*


-


When you login, there is a


**REFERRAL CODE** section -


**COPY** it and send it to a friend!

If you’re a current FightCamp member AND the Account holder, the Customer Dashboard is easily accessible through your member account


[login](http://account.joinfightcamp.com/)


. If you are a FightCamp member with a prepaid membership you have access, but some features may be limited.
