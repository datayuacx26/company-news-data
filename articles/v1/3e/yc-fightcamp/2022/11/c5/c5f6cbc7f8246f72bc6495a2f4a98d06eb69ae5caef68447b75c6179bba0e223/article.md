# The Ultimate Boxing Gift Guide

Boxing is a big sport with a very unique culture around it, which makes buying gifts for boxers a one-of-a-kind experience. So this holiday season, FightCamp has taken the guesswork out of buying gifts for boxing lovers with this awesome list of gift ideas put together by[FightCamp's own coaches](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/why-i-became-a-trainer-stories-behind-the-fightcamp-trainers) !


### [Coach PJ](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/fightcamp-trainer-spotlight-coach-pj) - Jump Rope


Image Credit:[CROSSROPE](https://www.crossrope.com/products/slim-jump-rope-handles/)


Jumping rope is a core part of any boxer’s routine, and since we tend to leave them everywhere having extras is never a bad thing. Getting a modular rope that you can clip different thickness handles to is a great gift idea too because it lets you adjust the speed of the rope to level and intensity.


### Flo Master - Boxing Slip Ball


Image Credit:[TITLE Boxing](https://www.titleboxing.com/title-boxing-professional-slip-ball)


Slip balls are an awesome way to practice shifting our center of gravity to avoid punches and proper slipping technique, and it's also one of the more fun training tools boxers have available, so this Christmas or Hanukkah be sure to pick one up for the boxer in your life (or yourself)!


### [Flo Master](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/fightcamp-trainer-spotlight-flo-master) - Double-End Bag


Image Credit:[Rival](https://www.titleboxing.com/rival-next-generation-double-end-bag-1)


Double-end bags are awesome training tools for both hand-eye coordination and punch endurance, and the fact that they take up way less space than a heavy bag makes them awesome boxing gifts for any athlete!


### Shanie Smash - Egg Weights


Image Credit:[Egg Weights](https://eggweights.com/products/egg-weight-cardio-max)


Egg weights are another huge space saver that can enhance any workout, and they’re super convenient to take with you on the go as well. If you don’t have a pair, you should definitely pick a pair up for yourself when you’re grabbing gifts for your boxing friends.


### [Shanie Smash](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/fightcamp-trainer-spotlight-shanie-smash-rusth) - Foam Roller


Image Credit:[TRIGGERPOINT](https://triggerpoint-therapy.ca/products/grid-foam-roller?variant=40788196917400)


I remember the first time I actually used a foam roller it completely changed my outlook on recovery. I had always been more of the traditional macho ‘walk it off’ type when it came to soreness, but recovering properly really does make a huge difference. Warm-ups, cooldowns, and recovery are just as important as the workout itself, so make sure you or the boxer in your life have the right tools available this holiday season!
