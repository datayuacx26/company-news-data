## The Art of 8 Limbs


“Muay Thai is more than just a sport – it’s a way of life,” says Sean.


Many liken Muay Thai to kickboxing, but movement and striking have very noticeable differences. One of the main differences is also the source of the moniker “Art of Eight Limbs” – which refers to the number of weapons a Muay Thai fighter has at their disposal:


-


2 Fists


-


2 Shins


-


2 Elbows


-


2 Knees


Kickboxing makes primary use of four striking points, 2 fists and 2 feet. Muay Thai fighters also use different techniques, timing, and rules in combat – some might argue they even have a bit more creative license in the ring.


The rich tradition of Muay Thai as a discipline is steeped in cultural history, tracing its origins as far back as the 16th century. This remarkable sport has since gained increasing popularity worldwide, and Sean Fagan has quickly risen to the ranks as one of its finest trainers.
