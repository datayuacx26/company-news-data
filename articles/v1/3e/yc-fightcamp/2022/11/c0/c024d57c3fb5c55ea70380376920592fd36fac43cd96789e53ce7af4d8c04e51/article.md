# The Making of Performance Nutrition Path with Chris Algieri

World champion fighter, ringside analyst, author, and certified nutritionist[Chris Algieri](https://www.instagram.com/chris_algieri) helps professional athletes reach their full potential. He has authored “The Fighter’s Kitchen” and “So You Want to Be a Fighter,” worked with top fighters including 2-time world champion “Miracle Man” Danny Jacobs, and served as the Performance Nutrition Consultant to the Division I Stony Brook University Seawolves.


Now, Chris is bringing his expertise to the FightCamp community with his exclusive Performance Nutrition Path available only on the[FightCamp App](https://joinfightcamp.com/?utm_source=organicBlog&utm_content=homepage) . He is going to be covering everything from fasting and fueling to recovery, sleep optimization, supplements, and more. He’ll even bust some of the biggest myths when it comes to health, fitness, and nutrition so you know what really matters when it comes to optimizing your performance.


Chris reminds us that, “If you’re a professional athlete, a weekend warrior, or an enthusiast, performance matters. Being fit, sharp, and energetic is essential to everything you do in life.”


## What Performance Means


“You want to be able to perform, and I don’t just mean physically. You have to perform whether you’re at work or with your family – being energetic and being fit are all important for everyday life,” Chris explains. “The food that we eat directly affects how our bodies feel. When you feel good, you do good.”


The goal of Chris’ Performance Nutrition Path is to help you make a positive change in your life that will increase your own performance. He is sharing the knowledge and tips you need to make it happen.


Here’s a taste of what to expect:


### Chapter 1: Fasting – Not What You Eat, But How


Chris explores the emerging trend in intermittent fasting. He will explain what it means, the different types of fasting, and what impact this has on your body.


### Chapter 2: Fueling – Food as Fuel


This chapter is all about shifting the paradigm in your mind to look at food as fuel and how to give your body what it needs to perform at its best.


### Chapter 3: Recovery – Food as Medicine


As Chris says, if you’re going to train hard, you have to recover hard. Here he will teach you about optimal nutrition that supports your body’s recovery process.


### Chapter 4: Supplements – Maximum Your Performance


When it comes to nutrition, it is always food first. In this chapter, Chris will cover everything you wanted to know about supplements with a whole food approach.


### Chapter 5: Sleep – More Sleep, More Gains


The importance of sleep cannot be overstated. Chris will answer real questions from the FightCamp community about the role of sleep in maximizing performance.
