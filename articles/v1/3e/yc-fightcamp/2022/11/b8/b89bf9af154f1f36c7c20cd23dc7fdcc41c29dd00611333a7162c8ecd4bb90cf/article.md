# Challenge Yourself With FightCamp Solo Milestone Challenges

## What’s New on the FightCamp App


Hey FightCamp Fam, we have something new for you, and just in time to get a head start on those fitness resolutions! Starting December 1st, 2022 on the[FightCamp App](https://joinfightcamp.com/?utm_source=organicBlog&utm_content=homepage) , we are adding a Challenges feature with a gamified twist for your workouts.


Challenge yourself each month with a solo challenge. Earn points for your output and reach milestones to receive exclusive challenge badges.


## How Do the Solo Challenges Work


-


Each month a new challenge will be released on the FightCamp App.


-


See Challenge rules and progress under the Challenges tab on the app.


-


Every challenge will have a timeframe for you to gain points through your activity in FightCamp workouts.


-


Challenge points go towards earning a monthly Challenge badge.


-


Keep track of your challenge progress in the Progress Report to see how close you are to earning a challenge badge.


-


At the end of completing a challenge, if you earned enough points based on your activity in your workouts, you can qualify for one of the following badge levels: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum.
