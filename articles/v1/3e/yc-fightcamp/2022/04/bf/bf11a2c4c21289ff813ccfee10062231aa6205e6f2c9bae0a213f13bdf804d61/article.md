# Best Jump Ropes For Beginner Boxers

When it comes to being in tip-top shape, especially for boxing and kickboxing training, a[jump rope](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/how-to-jump-rope-like-a-boxer-for-beginners/) is one of the best pieces of equipment you can invest in. With the numerous benefits of[jumping rope](https://youtu.be/XD6zAV6NZaQ) and the affordable options, it’s no surprise that most fighters use jump ropes to increase their endurance and boost their boxing training.


Here, FightCamp Trainer[Jess Evans](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/fightcamp-trainer-spotlight-jess-evans) will outline how to choose the right jump rope, the benefits of jumping rope, and the different types of jump ropes. Jess is a 3-time amateur Muay Thai champion and an avid roper (she has a jump rope in her gym bag at all times). With her tips and top key factors, you’ll be training like a pro boxer!


## How To Choose The Right Jump Rope


### 1. Fitness Goals


Before purchasing a jump rope, ask yourself the following questions:
Are you...


-


Starting to jump rope to lose weight?


-


Trying to build strength?


-


Looking to improve endurance?


-


Trying to master boxing footwork?


### 2. Rope Length


-


Golden Rule: Measure and cut the rope to your shoulder height


-


Stand on the middle of the rope with one foot


-


Pull up on the handles


-


The handles should come up to shoulder height


-


Some people prefer the jump rope to be a little longer, while others prefer it a little shorter


-


Jess’ Preference: Shoulder-height to avoid rope lagging, tripping, and hitting the toes


### 3. Durability


-


What material is the jump rope made of?


-


Is it made of cheap materials?


-


Do the handles spin freely?


-


Jess’s Preference: Free rotating handles which help with speedwork and tricks


### 4. Cost


-


You get what you pay for


-


If you want a quality jump rope that will last, you may need to invest more


## 4 Benefits of Jumping Rope


The reasons are endless when it comes to adding jumping rope into your boxing routine. Here are Jess’ top four (4) benefits:


### 1. Increases Stamina and Endurance


-


Use it as a warm-up for your workout


-


Use it as a full-body workout


-


An excellent way to improve conditioning, increase stamina, and build endurance


### 2. Improves Coordination


-


Challenges your brain to do two things simultaneously


-


Move both hands and feet together


### 3. Improves Explosiveness and Quickness


-


Serves to help build and improve fast-twitch muscle fibers


-


Helps you move around more freely, in an explosive manner


### 4. Improves Footwork


-


Go-to training exercise for most fighters


-


Helps you remain active and light on your feet


-


Utilize different patterns (jumps) to mimic ring movements


## Different Kinds of Jump Ropes


Here are four (4) of the main types of jump ropes and their features:


### 1. Weighted Jump Rope


-


Great for conditioning grip, shoulders, and forearms


-


Weighs more than your average jump rope


-


Average weighted jump rope: 1-2 lbs.


-


Average standard jump rope: 6-10 oz.


-


Rope can do damage when hitting toes, not ideal for beginners


-


Price: Typically $15 - $30


### 2. Speed Jump Rope


-


Ideal for improving jump rope explosiveness


-


Improves cardio, coordination, footwork


-


Best rope when it comes to mastering double-unders


-


Double-unders: Jump rope passes under feet twice per single jump


-


Price: Typically $15 - $40


### 3. Performance Jump Rope


-


Ideal for mastering jump rope tricks


-


Best for general jump rope purposes


-

### 4. Tracking Jump Rope


-


Similar to the FightCamp Punch Trackers, this jump rope tracks your jump count


-


Also, tracks calories burned and time spent jumping


-


Coach Aaron’s Take: Anytime you use equipment that is connected (tracking) it makes you work that much harder to challenge yourself


-


Price: $15 - $90
