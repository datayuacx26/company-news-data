# 15 Minute At-Home Boxing Workout For Beginners

Join FightCamp Trainer[Aaron Swenson](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/fightcamp-trainer-spotlight-aaron-swenson) for this 4-round at-home boxing workout for beginners. In just 15 minutes, practice boxing punch combinations and basic boxing moves, including the jab, the cross, and uppercuts.


Each round consists of three (3) 1-minute drills, followed by 1 minute of rest between rounds. Get ready to break a sweat!


Be sure to[warm-up](https://youtu.be/dR0Nx5SycKQ) and[cool down](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/8-minute-cool-down-static-stretching-routine-for-boxing) for this boxing combos for beginners workout.


## 15 Minute Boxing Workout


### Combo 1 (1 Minute)


-


Jab - Jab - Cross | 1-1-2


### Combo 2 (1 Minute)


-


Jab - Cross - Lead Uppercut | 1-2-5


### Combo 3 (1 Minute)


-


Lead Uppercut - Rear Uppercut | 5-6


Rest for 1 minuite
