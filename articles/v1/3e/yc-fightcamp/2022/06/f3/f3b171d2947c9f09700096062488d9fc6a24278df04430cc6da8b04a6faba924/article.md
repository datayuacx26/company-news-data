# How To Get Workout Results In 30 Days With Boxing

Boxing is the ultimate workout for achieving a[strong, lean physique](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/why-training-like-a-boxer-gets-you-in-amazing-shape/) . It takes discipline and consistency to get results, and those results can start to reveal themselves quickly.


With a combination of[healthy eating](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/a-boxer-s-diet-how-to-eat-like-a-boxer-beginner-s-guide/) and focused training, even beginner boxers can start to appear more toned in 30 days. What’s more, these first 30 days are essential to setting you up for future success. It’s believed that it takes 21 days to form a habit – so committing just 21 days to your workout routine is all you need to make it part of your lifestyle. Get to the 30-day mark and its second nature.


Here we’ll delve into how to make the most of these 30 days, and what tricks can help ensure your success.


## Making It Count


Boxing is a full-body workout that combines cardio and[strength training](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/what-is-strength-conditioning-boxing-training) . Training like a boxer will build muscle, increase your endurance, and diminish excess body fat over time. If you want to see workout results in 30 days, boxing is a great choice.


Still, it’s not just the efficacy of the workout that matters when your goal is to get leaner. A boxer’s lifestyle is all about healthy choices that support overall fitness. Here are three (3) tips to follow that will make these 30 days count.


## Three (3) Tips To Getting Results


### 1. Add Workout Variety


Switching up your workout not only challenges different muscle groups but also helps keep it interesting. Boxing training is perfect for adding variety because there is such a wide range of exercises in a boxer’s repertoire.


Trying[HIIT boxing](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/hiit-get-in-shape-with-a-10-minute-daily-workout/) 2-3 days a week will fire up your metabolism, while 1-2 days of[strength and conditioning](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/t/strength-and-conditioning) will boost muscle growth. Don’t forget to add some[yoga](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/blogs/boxing-workouts-drills/10-yoga-poses-for-boxers-and-kickboxers) on your active rest days to stretch and tone your whole body.


### 2. Stick to a Healthy Diet


No matter how much you sculpt your muscles, you won’t see them until you change what you eat. As FightCamp Trainer Flo Master says “It starts in the kitchen”. A[boxer’s diet](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/boxing-nutrition-101-a-dietitians-guide-to-a-boxers-diet/) plays a huge role in how boxers and athletes stay lean and fit. Regardless of whether you are aiming to drop a size or see more muscle definition, everyone should pay closer attention to the fuel they are giving their bodies.


[Eating like a boxer](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/a-boxer-s-diet-how-to-eat-like-a-boxer-beginner-s-guide/) means cutting out junk, including overly processed foods, artificial sugars, and saturated fats. A boxer’s diet contains plenty of whole grain[carbohydrates](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/importance-of-carbs-for-boxers-explained-by-a-dietitian/) , ample lean[protein](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/boxer-s-diet-protein-requirements-and-best-protein-sources/) , nutrient-dense fruits and vegetables, and[healthy fats](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/healthy-fats-an-essential-part-of-a-boxers-diet/) like nuts and avocado.


### 3. Prioritize Rest


This may seem counterintuitive, but your body needs proper rest to perform efficiently. Good sleep hygiene helps regulate your metabolism and allows your muscles to recover and grow stronger.


Make sure that you are getting at least 7 hours of sleep each night and take[rest days](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/what-you-need-to-know-about-rest-days-q-a-with-coach-pj/) when you need them. You’re asking your body to transform itself in 30 days, make sure it has the energy for the task.


## Setting Yourself Up For Success


Committing to a fitness routine isn’t easy. Remember, if you can get through the first three weeks, you have conquered the hardest part. Make it the full four weeks, and you have the healthier habits locked in.


The most important thing is to not get discouraged. Here are a few tips that can help you make it through 30 days and jumpstart a new fit-focused lifestyle.


## Three (3) Tips For Success


### 1. Set Realistic Goals


Depending on your fitness level at the start of the 30 days, you may not be able to work out as intensely or as often as you’d like. It is perfectly okay to take it slow as you build strength and endurance. You will have days when you are sore – take it easy, take a day off if you need to, just don’t give up.

### 3. Have Fun With It


Assimilating a workout routine into your life should be enjoyable. Boxing is much-loved as one of the most fun,[stress-busting](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/how-to-use-at-home-kickboxing-training-to-knockout-stress/) workouts you can do in your own home. Choosing a good boxing program will introduce you to a world of healthy habits that support physical and[mental health](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/boxing-your-way-to-better-mental-health/) .
