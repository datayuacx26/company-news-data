# Female Celebrities Who Swear By Boxing

Boxing training is a high cardio, strength building, and conditioning workout that can completely transform your body. The great thing about boxing is that you are learning a combat skill, but you don’t necessarily have to fight competitively to see the changes in your physique. These workouts not only make you physically strong but are great for


[mental growth](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/blogs/news/boxing-your-way-to-better-mental-health) . Here are some popular female celebrities who have added boxing to their workouts.


## 5 Female Celebrities Who Do Boxing Workouts


Whether it’s for a role, to get out some stress, or to learn a new skill, these female celebrities have all taken up boxing.


### 1. Jennifer Lopez


Image Credit:


[People.com](https://people.com/health/jennifer-lopez-boxes-alex-rodriguez-workout-video/)


Lopez is one of the most recognizable women of our time. Even at age 50, J-Lo is known for her curvy, yet toned physique. Starting as a professional dancer, moving to center stage and then onto the silver screen, J-Lo has used boxing to[keep up her shape](https://people.com/food/jennifer-lopez-boxing-exercise-tracy-anderson/) and stamina for filming and going on tour. According to interviews, J-Lo has used her boxing training in many films and


[shows](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/entertainment/hollywood/110916/jennifer-lopez-films-boxing-scenes-for-tv-show.html) : Money Train (1995), Enough (2002), and Shades of Blue (2016-2018). In between her busy schedule, Lopez can sometimes be spotted at a gym practicing combos while hitting focus mitts.


### 2. Gigi Hadid


Image Credit:


[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/xYCKu1jCSy/)


Gigi Hadid is an American supermodel who has worked with nearly every major fashion house across the globe. From walking the runways, modeling, and becoming a new mom in 2020, Gigi can also be seen


[training](https://www.womenshealthmag.com/fitness/a37807900/gigi-hadid-abs-crop-top-runway-paris-fashion-week/)


at NYC’s Gotham G boxing gym. Her


[trainer](https://www.insider.com/how-does-gigi-hadid-workout-2018-6)


comments that health and strength are top priorities for Hadid, and she keeps up with her workouts whether she is traveling or back in the Big Apple. Gigi often promotes the physical and


[mental benefits](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/boxing-your-way-to-better-mental-health/)


of a consistent boxing routine.


### 3. Khloe Kardashian


Image Credit:


[TheCut.com](https://www.thecut.com/2017/04/keeping-up-with-the-kardashians-season-13-episode-4-recap.html)


Khloe Kardashian was consistently portrayed by the press as being the “unfit” sister of the Kardashian family. Using the shade to motivate her, Khloe hired a personal trainer and completely changed her diet plan. Khloe’s transformation and her new love of fitness granted her a spin-off show,


*Revenge Body* , apart from her famous family. Khloe has an extensive home gym and has added boxing into her training program. She’s been exposed to the benefits of boxing and loves how it helps to relieve stress and build mental toughness. In one of her


[YouTube workout videos](https://youtu.be/PA0a9A-uBGY) , she notes that her boxing workouts allow her to “sweat out the stress.”


### 4. Shay Mitchell


Image Credit:


[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/eJnk7Iwr9k/?hl=en)


Actress and model Shay Mitchell, best known for her role on “Pretty Little Liars,” has always been vocal about how fitness is vital to her daily life. She’s been featured as the “Sexiest Woman Alive” and Maxim’s Hot 100 throughout her career. Not only are her genetics a considerable contributor to her beauty, but she also loves to work out. Shay has even used her love of fitness and celebrity status to launch new business ventures. In an interview with


[Business Insider](https://www.insider.com/shay-mitchell-swears-by-boxing-to-get-in-shape-2017-8) , she is quoted saying, "Boxing, boxing, boxing! That's my favorite. It's just a workout where you have to be in the moment — you can't think about anything else." On her Instagram, which has over 31 million followers, you can see her in her life as a new mom and in countless boxing training videos.


### 5. Jennifer Garner


Image Credit:


[Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/tvshowbiz/article-5804409/Jennifer-Garner-works-sweat-kickboxing-Santa-Monica-gym.html)


Another celeb that loves the total body aspect of a boxing workout is Jennifer Garner. Garner’s acting career has taken her from starring in heartwarming films like “13 Going On 30” to thrillers like “Peppermint.” Even with children and a demanding movie career, Garner can still be seen boxing training in Santa Monica, California. Depending on which production she joins, her schedule can be anywhere from


[5 to 6 days a week](https://www.womenshealthmag.com/fitness/a27180144/jennifer-garner-birthday-workout/) . During the summer of 2021, Jennifer showed off her


[at-home workout routine](https://www.eatingwell.com/article/7907944/jennifer-garner-and-her-mom-share-exercise-routine/)


with her 83-year-old mother. Garner's trainer has said that she loves


[cardio](https://youtu.be/H9nXi2u8t1M) , strength training, and focusing on the upper body, which are all key elements to any boxing routine.
