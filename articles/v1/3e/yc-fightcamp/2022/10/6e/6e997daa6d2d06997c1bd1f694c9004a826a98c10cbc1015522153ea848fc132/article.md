# The Making of the Basic Nutrition Path with Tyler Minton

Professional MMA fighter and top nutritionist[Tyler Minton](https://www.instagram.com/tylermintonnutrition) knows “you can’t out train a poor diet.” Recognized by MMA Junkie as one of the leading nutritionists in combat sports, Tyler has coached champion fighters like Khabib Nurmagomedov, Stephen “Wonderboy” Thompson, Max Holloway, Daniel Corier, and more. Now he brings his experience to FightCamp members with his expert series on nutrition.


Tyler covers everything you wanted to know about fueling for peak performance. From how calories are burned to real-world meal prep and even insider tips on how pro fighters cut weight, Tyler shares his secrets on optimizing your health and improving your training. You can now watch this series on all things nutrition only on the[FightCamp App](https://joinfightcamp.com/?utm_source=organicBlog&utm_content=homepage) .


Here’s a sneak peek of what to expect in this cutting-edge series, as well as a Q&A with Tyler about the making of the Nutrition Path.


## Bringing it All to the Table


Tyler is bringing 8 years of education and over 15 years of industry experience to this series. He’s covering everything from scientific formulas to real-world approaches to help the FightCamp community understand the importance of nutrition in reaching their goals.


Here’s what to expect from each information-packed chapter in this 10-part series:


### Chapter 1 - Energy Balance


Energy balance is the relationship between the calories we consume and the calories we burn. If you’re working out, you probably have a goal involving either losing or gaining weight. In this chapter, you’ll learn how each of these goals is achieved through manipulating calorie intake.


### Chapter 2 - How Calories Are Burned


When most people hear ‘calories burned’ they immediately think of working out. There are actually 4 different ways we burn calories in a day, and you may be surprised at just how this process works. Here Tyler takes you through the fascinating science behind how calories are burned.


### Chapter 3 – Macronutrients


Macronutrients are nutrients our body needs to supply it with energy in the form of calories. Most of us are familiar with carbohydrates, proteins, and fats, but few people know much about them. This chapter breaks down the role of essential macronutrients in your diet.


### Chapter 4 - Quality Focused Approach Eating Strategy


Although the number of calories you eat will determine your weight, the types of food you eat directly impact the way you look and feel. Tyler dispels the myth of ‘good’ and ‘bad’ foods, explaining that there are simply some foods that better support our goals and health than others. Here you’ll learn how to choose an eating strategy that best aligns with your goals.


### Chapter 5 - Quantity Focused Approach Eating Strategy


This is a breakdown of calorie estimation techniques, as well as how to adjust for weight maintenance, loss, or gain. Tyler details some of the formulas you can use to choose the right number of calories for your specific goals. If you like details and precision, this chapter is for you.


### Chapter 6 - The Importance of Hydration


Hydration is critical, but most people don’t know just how important it is. Water protects our tissue, spinal cord, and joints while optimizing body temperature, digestion, and even brain function. In this chapter, Tyler teaches you how something as simple as drinking the right amount of water can mean success or failure toward your goals.


### Chapter 7 - Real World Application Meal Prep


Here you’ll start applying everything you’ve learned. Tyler explains what it looks like to take all the nutritional science and formulas to your plate, demonstrating quality and quantity methods with the perfect balance of macronutrients. If you weren’t hungry before this chapter, you will be by the end.


### Chapter 8 - Post Workout Smoothie


In this chapter, Tyler teams up with FightCamp Trainer[Aaron Swenson](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/fightcamp-trainer-spotlight-aaron-swenson) to whip up a customizable recipe for a post-workout smoothie. This is one of Tyler’s favorites and is much-loved by his professional clients.


### Chapter 9 - Question and Answers


FightCamp Trainer Aaron Swenson hits Tyler up with real questions from the FightCamp community. Watch nutritionist powerhouse Tyler as he flexes his knowledge with off-the-cuff responses in this enlightening Q & A session.


### Chapter 10 - How Fighters Cut Weight


Tyler has helped top fighters prepare for their moment in the ring, and now he shares insider tips on how the pros cut weight. He explains that weight cutting is not the same as fat loss and that the health of the athlete always takes priority over unrealistic goals. Find out the real story behind weight cutting in pro combat sports and hear Tyler’s thoughts on weight classes.


## The Making of The Nutrition Path


When FightCamp reached out to Tyler Minton about leading an expert series, he was on board immediately. Many of his clients already use FightCamp and he was excited to offer up his insight to help fighters reach their training goals.


Hear about his experience on set with the FightCamp team:


### Why is this series important for FightCamp members or anyone?


Working out is only one part of the fitness puzzle. You can only get so far with workouts alone and when you pair a great workout with the right nutrition program, you really start to see the proof of all your hard work.


### Why are you the perfect person to lead this series?


I have 8 years of college education and 15 years of industry experience. I've helped hundreds of professional athletes and thousands of the general population with their nutrition goals.


### How did you get in contact with FightCamp?


FightCamp reached out to me, and I knew immediately who they were as I've seen a lot of the fighters that I work with use their products.


### What was your experience like on set?


I had so much fun! The FightCamp team was extremely professional but kept things fun. I'm really hoping to film another path in the future as I feel like we only scratched the surface of the information I can give to benefit the FightCamp community.


### What was your favorite part of the experience?


My favorite part was just talking with the team between takes. Everyone in the company has their own interesting fight or film experiences and it was really great to get to know everyone.


### What is something you learned while you were on set filming with FightCamp?

I would really like to help people understand what it is about their physiology that makes dieting hard and how to use this information to make things easier.

They're a lot more enthusiastic! Their energy is unreal!

### If there is one point from your series that you would underline in bold for the FightCamp Members, what would it be?


You can't out train a poor diet.

The way we eat literally has the power to heal you or to kill you and I have given all the information needed for the[FightCamp Community](https://www.facebook.com/groups/joinfightcamp/) to heal.
