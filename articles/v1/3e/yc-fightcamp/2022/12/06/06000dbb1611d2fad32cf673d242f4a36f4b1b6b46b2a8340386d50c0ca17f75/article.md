# How to Make S.M.A.R.T. Goals for the New Year

## Are You Ready to Make the Most of the New Year?


Don't let the new year be like last year, where you set goals and expectations and then end up disappointing yourself by not meeting your resolutions. If you fail to plan, you might as well plan to fail, and FightCamp is not the company that wants you to fail at anything. Consider taking this structured resolution *path* , which is a way to stay organized as you achieve your goals.


A popular and effective way to create a *path* to follow is by setting S.M.A.R.T. goals. S.M.A.R.T. is an acronym that stands for Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, and Time-bound. By setting goals that follow this framework, you can increase your chances of achieving your objectives and progressing toward your long-term vision.


## How to Make S.M.A.R.T. Goals for the New Year


### 1. Make Your Goals Specific


The more specific your goals are, the easier it will be to focus on what needs to be done to achieve them. For example, instead of saying, "I want to get in shape," try, "I want to drop 10 pounds by March 1st by exercising for at least[30 minutes per day](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/get-your-30-minutes-of-exercise-a-day-with-boxing) and following a[healthy meal plan](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/best-boxing-diet-plan-for-the-new-year/) ." When your goals are specific, you know what to do to achieve them and can take targeted action.


### 2. Make Your Goals Measurable


It's essential to have a way to track your progress toward your goals. This could be a numerical value, like the number of pounds you want to drop, or a tangible action, like the number of days per week you wish to exercise. By measuring your journey, you can see your progress and how much further you have to go. This can help you stay motivated and adjust your strategy if needed.


### 3. Make Your Goals Attainable


Setting challenging, realistic, and achievable goals is important. If your goal is too difficult, you may become discouraged and give up. On the other hand, if your goal is too easy, you may not see the progress you desire. To determine if your goal is attainable, consider your current resources and limitations and any available support to help you reach your goal.


### 4. Make Your Goals Relevant


Your goals should be meaningful and aligned with your values and long-term vision. This will help you stay motivated and committed to achieving them. Suppose your goal is not relevant to your overall objectives. In that case, you may lose interest or find it challenging to stay motivated. Make sure your goals are aligned with what you truly want to achieve in your life.


### 5. Make Your Goals Time-Bound


Setting a deadline for your goal helps you stay focused and motivated. It also enables you to track your progress and make any necessary adjustments along the way. When you have a deadline, you can create a plan with specific milestones and action steps to help you stay on track.
