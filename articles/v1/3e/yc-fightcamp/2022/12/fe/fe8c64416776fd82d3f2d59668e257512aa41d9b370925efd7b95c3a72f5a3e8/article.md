# Get Your 30 Minutes of Exercise a Day With Boxing

If you have time to scroll through Instagram and TikTok, you have time for a workout. The average person spends[over two hours each day](https://www.broadbandsearch.net/blog/average-daily-time-on-social-media) on social media. The[CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physicalactivity/basics/adults/index.htm) recommends we include 150 minutes of physical activity each week. It sounds like a lot at first, but when you break it down, that's only 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week. When it comes to finding an effective workout that can fulfill that half hour, boxing packs a lot of punch.


Boxing training is intense. You don’t need to commit to a whole hour to get phenomenally fit. In just 30 minutes, you can get a calorie-scorching cardio workout that strengthens and tones your whole body. The benefits of boxing go beyond improving your physical health. Just a half hour of boxing can improve your mood and your lifestyle, making it easy to happily commit to a fun fitness routine that makes you look and feel better.


Here we’ll delve into the top five (5) reasons why a 30-minute boxing session is your best bet for staying active.


## Benefits of a 30-Minute Boxing Workout


### One-Two Punch: Cardio & Strength Combo


A 30-minute boxing session gives you an incredible workout that combines both cardio and strength training. Boxing training which can include shadowboxing, mitt work, and punching on a[heavy bag](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/how-to-train-on-a-heavy-bag-boxing-tips/) , is one of the most effective ways to reach your fitness goals and get in[fantastic shape](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/why-training-like-a-boxer-gets-you-in-amazing-shape/) . While your heart rate is increased, your muscles are fighting the resistance of the bag with every punch, improving endurance and strength at the same time.


### Maximum Efficiency


Most high-intensity workouts should be around 30 minutes, including a short warm-up and cool down. This allows enough time for your body to reach peak performance during the session before overdoing it. Your heart rate is up, blood is pumping, and muscles are firing just long enough to have an excellent full-body blast without pushing it to the point of causing strain or injury.


### Stress Relief


When you are fighting feelings of stress and frustration, there are few things more cathartic than[unleashing that stress](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/bettering-yourself-through-boxing/) onto a punching bag. Just 30 minutes of boxing exercises can release some of that pent-up pressure by allowing you to transfer it from your mind into motion. Boxing training is a[safe and healthy outlet](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/how-to-use-at-home-kickboxing-training-to-knockout-stress/) for anyone who has ever felt the need to just get it all out.


### Mental Wellness


Boxing is a brain booster, helping to clear the mind and elevate emotions. Executing complex boxing combinations stimulates brain activity and challenges your memory, which research says can greatly[prevent age-related cognitive decline](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/how-boxing-boosts-brain-health-and-prevents-dementia) . Studies have also shown that as little as[15 minutes per day](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/how-exercise-improves-mental-health/) of exercise can help relieve symptoms of depression. A 30-minute boxing session can leave you feeling better immediately and help[regulate your emotional well-being](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/wellness/boxing-your-way-to-better-mental-health/) over time with consistent workouts.


### Convenience


It is easier to be consistent with a ‘workout schedule’ if you can actually fit a workout into your schedule. Just a half hour is easy to pencil in somewhere during the day, whether it’s a quick 30-minute morning wake-me-up, a lunch break bag beatdown, or an evening de-stress session. You don’t need to find a whole hour of your time to have an amazing full-body boxing workout, and you can make it even more convenient by doing your[boxing training at home](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/boxing-equipment/top-6-reasons-to-invest-in-an-at-home-fitness-system/) . Boxing exercises are easy to do at home with or without equipment, freeing up even more time in your day.
