# Track Your FightCamp Workouts From Your Apple Watch

## What’s New


That’s right FightCamp members, you asked and we listened. As of September 23rd, 2022[FightCamp iOS app](https://fightcamp.app.link/srbn9hdzSlb) users will now be able to track FightCamp workout sessions straight from their Apple Watch via the FightCamp Apple Watch app.


We know a lot of the FightCamp members have Apple Watches, so we wanted to make tracking your boxing and kickboxing sessions more easily accessible for you all.


## How to Track Your Workouts on Your Apple Watch


Once you start a FightCamp workout from your iPhone (Apple currently doesn’t allow this integration with an iPad or Android), the FightCamp Apple Watch app will open and start tracking your workout stack automatically. The Apple Watch app will track your whole workout stack, time, and total calories burned.


To see your workouts appear on your Apple Watch be sure you have the FightCamp iOS app version 4.6.0 or greater and that your Health Kit is enabled. Here are 2 ways to manually enable your Health Kit:


1.


FightCamp app → More tab → Health button.


2.


Settings → Health Kit → Data Access & Devices → FightCamp. Allow all.
