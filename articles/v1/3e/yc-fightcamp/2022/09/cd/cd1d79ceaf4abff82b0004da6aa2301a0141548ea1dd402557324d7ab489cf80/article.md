# First Time Hit By A Body Punch | FightCamp’s Tommy Duquette

FightCamp Co-Founder and Trainer[Tommy Duquette](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/fightcamp-trainer-spotlight-tommy-duquette) is sharing his experience about his first time ever on the receiving end of a body shot. Similar to FightCamp Trainer[Aaron Swenson’s](https://youtu.be/oMc52QTWo1A) first time, Tommy recalls the moment he was truly hurt by a punch. Check it out as he describes this boxing milestone.


## Sixteen and Innocent


One of the deadliest shots a boxer can take is a body blow. This is definitely a lesson Tommy learned the hard way. When he was about sixteen years old Tommy was drastically improving his fight game and it just so happened to catch the eyes of one of the adults in his gym. Impressed by his improvements, he asked Tommy if he wanted to spar a little bit, just to practice.


Up until then, Tommy hadn't done a lot of[sparring](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/how-to-start-sparring-beginner-boxing-tips-drills) and definitely not any against an adult boxer. Slightly nervous, Tommy agreed since his opponent said he was going to go light and take it easy on him.


The bell rang and the two began to spar. Tommy utilized his jabs, keeping his opponent at bay, and not allowing him to get inside on him. Frustrated, his opponent turned on the aggression.


## The Liver Shot


Tommy’s opponent was eventually able to close the[distance](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/learn-to-control-distance-and-dictate-the-boxing-match) and get Tommy against the ropes. Boom! Before he knew what hit him, his opponent landed a body shot to the right side of his body, square on his liver. Little did Tommy know at the time just how painful a blow to the liver could be. A properly executed liver shot can essentially “shut down the entire body.”


That is exactly what happened to Tommy. The room changed a shade of color and his ears began to ring. He was done for. The punch never made him drop, but it did indeed end their sparring session. Tommy’s lesson learned that day:[body shots](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/6-basic-body-shot-punches-to-use-in-boxing/) are the true punch “secret sauce.” A lesson FightCamp teaches their athletes.
