# Introducing Drills: Build Your Own Path to Progress

## Revisiting Our Roots


More than a decade ago FightCamp began, as *Hykso* , with a mission to revolutionize boxing and martial arts training through technology.


We believed fighters deserved a better way to measure their performance. So we built one of the most reliable punch tracking systems in combat sports. We earned investor support from legends like Mike Tyson, Floyd Mayweather, Georges St-Pierre, and many others who shared our vision for the future of training.


As our technology evolved, so did our ambition.


We expanded beyond tracking and launched FightCamp with a new goal: make boxing training accessible to anyone, anywhere. By combining world-class coaching with performance tracking, we built a community of tens of thousands of members who discovered the power of training like a fighter at home.


When COVID accelerated the shift toward home fitness, our community grew rapidly. And when the world returned to gyms, our members stayed with us.


Through it all, we continued building new workouts, new features, and new technology.


But we never lost sight of where we started.


## Freedom Without Guesswork


Some training sessions call for a trainer.


Others call for freedom.


Sometimes you want expert coaching guiding every round. Other times you want to work on a specific technique, test your conditioning, chase a personal record, or simply get a quick session in on your own terms.


Both are valuable.


And both are more effective when they're measured.


## New Way To Train With FightCamp Drills


Our latest feature, **Drills** , gives you the freedom to train without a trainer while still tracking every punch.


Choose from:


-


**Freestyle Mode** for open-ended training


-


**Classic Drills** designed around specific performance goals


-


**Custom Drills** that let you create and save your own formats


Whether you're practicing a technique, testing your physical capacity, or squeezing in a quick bag session when you're short on time, Drills give you the flexibility to train your way while capturing the data that keeps you motivated and accountable.A Live Workout Experience Like Never Before


This release marks a major evolution to the live in-workout experience. For the first time, FightCamp users can see the quality of each individual strike as they train, offering a new layer of insight into technique and performance.


Want to beat your last workout’s output? Now you’ll see exactly how each strike is helping you get there.


## FightCamp Work Membership


Drills are included with every FightCamp All-Access membership alongside our full library of 4,000+ trainer-led workouts and programs.


But we also know that many athletes prefer to train independently. They have their own routines, train in local gyms, or simply don't need instruction.


What they still value is measurement.


That's why we're introducing **[FightCamp WORK](https://joinfightcamp.com/work/memberships)** .


A new membership built for people who want the motivation, accountability, and performance insights that come from tracking their training.


When you're training at home, in a gym, or on your own schedule, FightCamp WORK turns every punch into data, helping you measure your effort, track your progress, and stay accountable to your goals.


**Put in the work.**


We'll help you measure it.
