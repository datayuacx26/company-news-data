# Meet Justin Hughes: A Real Fighter In Your Corner

You're going to know within the first round.


Not because someone tells you Justin Hughes is different. Because you'll feel it. The tempo. The voice. The way he calls combos.


Justin is a professional MMA fighter based in Las Vegas. He's been fighting and coaching for over 10 years. He's trained competitive fighters and everyday athletes. And now, he's bringing that same energy to FightCamp as the platform's newest trainer.


But here's what matters more than the resume: Justin coaches like someone who has earned every word he says. No scripts. No motivational filler. When he tells you to dig in on Round 3, it's because he's been in rounds where digging in was the only option.


## WHAT MAKES A JUSTIN WORKOUT DIFFERENT


Justin's MMA background shapes everything about how he coaches. He doesn't live in one discipline. He draws from boxing, kickboxing, and years of cage experience, and that shows up in how he structures combinations, how he coaches movement, and how he pushes you to think about your body as a complete weapon, not just your hands.


His pacing is relentless in the best way. He keeps intensity high from bell to bell, but it's controlled. There's structure underneath the sweat. You're not just throwing punches to throw punches. You're building something: sharper timing, cleaner footwork, stronger instincts.


And then there's the energy. Justin's coaching style is loud, direct, and real. He'll catch you off guard between rounds and then push you harder than you expected in the next one. It's the kind of energy that makes you forget you're training alone in your garage, because it genuinely feels like someone is in the room with you.


## WHO IS JUSTIN HUGHES


Justin came up in MMA. He's fought professionally, trained under seasoned coaches, and spent years building his craft across multiple disciplines. He's coached everyone from competitive fighters to first-timers, and he treats both with the same directness and respect.


Justin came to FightCamp through Aaron Swenson. Aaron trained with him, saw what he brought to the table, and backed him fully. That alone says something. Fighters vouch for fighters. And from his first day in the FightCamp studio, Justin brought an energy that was impossible to ignore: raw, gritty, technically sharp, and completely himself.


He's based in Las Vegas. He still actively competes. And he shows up to coach the same way he shows up to fight: like it matters.


## WHAT TO EXPECT


If you've been craving workouts that feel earned, not handed to you, Justin is your trainer. His sessions are intense but structured. His technique breakdowns are clear without being watered down. And his presence on screen carries the kind of authority that only comes from someone who has actually done the work.


Whether you're coming back to training after a long break or you've been at this for years, Justin meets you where you are, and then pushes you past where you thought you'd stop.
