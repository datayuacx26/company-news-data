# Meet Your New Boxing Trainers: Charlotte & Selina

## From the World Stage to Your Corner


You’ve seen them bring the heat in kickboxing, now they’re stepping into the boxing workouts arena. Starting today, Charlotte Craig and Selina Flores have released their first-ever boxing workouts on FightCamp.


Get ready to elevate your training - FightCamp is proud to introduce new boxing workouts led by two champions from the world stage!


Known for bringing authenticity to every training session, FightCamp has always featured real fighters with real experience—and now, Selina and Charlotte are stepping into the ring to share their elite skills.


As part of our commitment to offering variety in both trainers and training styles, their addition brings even more ways to learn, grow, and challenge yourself, no matter where you are in your fitness journey.


## Charlotte


Charlotte Craig is a lifelong martial artist and former **Olympian** , having represented Team USA in Taekwondo at the 2008 Beijing Olympics at just 17 years old. She’s a **World Championship bronze medalist** , a Pan American Champion, and has competed in over **60 international fights** , earning podium finishes across global competitions like the **Taekwondo World Cup, U.S. Open, and Grand Prix** . Trained from a young age under Olympic gold medalist Jimmy Kim, Charlotte's journey from a determined five-year-old to a world-class competitor is a testament to her dedication, discipline, and elite skill. Now, she brings that same intensity and precision to FightCamp—infusing every workout with Olympic-level focus and martial arts mastery.


## Selina


Selina Flores is the reigning **WBC Muay Thai World Champion** and one of the most accomplished fighters in the sport today. She’s an **11-time Amateur National Muay Thai Champion** , a former **North American Champion** , and only **the third American woman in history** to win the prestigious WBC World Title. Her technical skills have earned her global recognition, and she’s showcased her talent on some of the biggest stages in the world—including the **Rajadamnern World Series** in Thailand and the **Muay Thai Super Series** in Perth. Beyond competition, Selina is also a passionate coach and mentor, regularly leading clinics and seminars to train the next generation of fighters. With years of elite-level experience and a deep dedication to martial arts, she brings both technical mastery and championship grit to every class.
