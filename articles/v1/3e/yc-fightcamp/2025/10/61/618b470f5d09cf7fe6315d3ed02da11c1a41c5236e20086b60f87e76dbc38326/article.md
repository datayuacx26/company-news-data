# Turning Competition Into Progress with FightCamp’s New Bots

## Progress, Personality, and Punches with Purpose


There’s a well-known phenomenon in sprinting: runners perform better when they’re racing alongside strong competitors. When there’s someone just ahead of you, pushing the pace, your body responds—you dig deeper, run faster, and break through your limits. At FightCamp, we believe that with an appropriate level of challenge, the same applies to all areas of competition, including boxing training.


We’re excited to launch **10 new characters in the ‘Vs Bot’ mode** , each representing a different difficulty level—and each with a distinct personality inspired by nostalgic pixel art and classic boxing culture.


## More Than Just Opponents


These Bots are more than just digital punching bags. They’re crafted to bring a sense of challenge, story, and boxing culture into your workouts. Each Bot is themed around iconic boxing archetypes, giving you a chance to learn a piece of the sport’s history while you train.


## Progress That You Can Feel


With 10 clearly defined difficulty levels, you now have a built-in way to measure your growth vs opponents that push you to be better.


## More Fun, More Personality


Training should be engaging. With their retro-inspired designs and unique identities, these bots make it fun to push through and unlock the next level of your progress.


## Boxing Culture


Each Bot carries the name of a classic boxing term or phrase, giving you bite-sized pieces of the sport’s rich cultural heritage.


## Climb the Ranks


You don’t have to beat the toughest Bot on Day 1. Start where you are. As your technique sharpens and your stats improve, move up a level and keep the momentum going.


FightCamp’s new Bots are here to add more meaning, motivation, and mastery to your workouts. They’re more than digital rivals—they’re a reflection of your journey.


**Pick your Bot, lace up your gloves, and let’s get to work!**
