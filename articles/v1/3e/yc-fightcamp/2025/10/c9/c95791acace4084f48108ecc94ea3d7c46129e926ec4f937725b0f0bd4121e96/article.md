# Inside the First-Ever FightCamp House

We just came back from Las Vegas, where we hosted the very first **FightCamp House** during the Canelo Álvarez vs. Terence Crawford fight weekend.


### What is the FightCamp House?


It’s where we bring creators and athletes together, immerse them in real boxing culture, and give them a taste of the grind that fighters like you know well.


For our first event, we handpicked four creators—each at a different stage of their boxing journey—and put them through the real thing:


With coaches Jess Evans and Flo Master in their corner, they got a workout that left no doubt about the respect this sport demands. They even tested a new beta drill feature coming soon to the FightCamp app that we think you'll love, so keep an eye out for that.


We also invited some notable friends of FightCamp to the experience: pro boxer[Ashton Sylve](https://www.instagram.com/ashtonsylveh2o) , Muhammad Ali’s grandson[Nico Ali Walsh](https://www.instagram.com/nicoaliwalsh) , the #1 boxing designer[I Got My Own Back](https://www.instagram.com/igotmyownback_fighter/) , and even UFC legend[Chuck Liddell](https://www.instagram.com/chuckliddell/) . Stories were shared, hands were wrapped, and everyone walked into fight night with excitement and anticipation.
