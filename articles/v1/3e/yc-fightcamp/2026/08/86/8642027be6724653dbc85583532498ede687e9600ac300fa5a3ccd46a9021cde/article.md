# FightCamp Introduces New Strike Power Feature

# Introducing Strike Power: Dynamic Feedback to Fuel Your Workouts


At FightCamp, we’re always looking for new ways to make your workouts more motivating, engaging, and effective. Today, we’re excited to roll out a major update to how you experience your Power.


Every strike counts. Hit harder, faster, and sharper to push your Total Output higher, with every strike. Your Output score represents the sum of your strikes and their power, and now you can see your effort broken down by both metrics.


## What's New


This dynamic update brings your Output score to life by displaying real-time visual feedback for every punch and kick, turning each strike into a satisfying, gamified moment. FightCamp has multiplied your Output score by 10 and layered in dynamic strike visuals. Now, every punch or kick your throw triggers a floating Strike Power value, like +1, +6, or +12, right on the screen, in real-time. These values instantly merge into your total Output, giving you immediate strike-by-strike feedback as you train.


FightCamp is bringing the arcade-style Punch Power machine to your at-home workouts with its latest feature. The calculations powering Output haven't changed but how you experience your performance has.


## Powered By Community Feedback


Early testers overwhelmingly shared that the new feature has had a noticeable impact on their motivation and technique. “My output is higher. I did find myself pushing harder, even as a beginner. You can see the count difference. For me, the constant strike acknowledgement was motivational—like a video game.” Another noted, “The visual feedback is helping me make technical adjustments live, instead of just grinding through rounds blindly. The output numbers don’t lie—when my technique is clean, the scores reflect it immediately.”


Since the launch of the second-generation console, FightCamp has introduced innovative features like Partner Workouts and Kick Tracking, all designed to unlock new ways to train, compete, and grow. With even more enhancements on the way, the Strike Power feature marks another major step in FightCamp’s mission to deliver the most immersive and motivating connected boxing and kickboxing experience available today.


## A Live Workout Experience Like Never Before


This release marks a major evolution to the live in-workout experience. For the first time, FightCamp users can see the quality of each individual strike as they train, offering a new layer of insight into technique and performance.


Want to beat your last workout’s output? Now you’ll see exactly how each strike is helping you get there.
