# VideoGen 3.4: AI Video, AI Avatars, API Access, and More Included


VideoGen 3.4 is now live with broader AI feature access, the VideoGen API, clearer credits, project themes, and stronger script-to-video results.


VideoGen 3.4 is now live, making more AI creation tools available across paid plans and adding new ways for teams and developers to build with VideoGen.


The release brings AI video, AI avatars, AI images, and other AI features into a simpler plan structure. It also introduces the VideoGen API, expands credit visibility, and adds more brand and editing controls for teams creating video at scale.


## More AI Creation in Every Paid Plan


Paid teams now get broader access to VideoGen's AI creation features without needing to upgrade feature by feature. Business plans also include Premium stock and more monthly credits, making it easier to plan around repeat video production.


## A Bigger Platform for Teams and Developers


VideoGen 3.4 also opens up API access for generating videos, images, voices, avatars, and full workflows programmatically. For teams working in the editor, project-wide themes help keep every section on brand without manually restyling each scene.


Credits are now easier to understand, with plan allotments, a visible rate card, usage history, credit purchases, and automatic top-ups.


## Better First Drafts, Faster Finishing


The script-to-video workflow has improved defaults, more style options, better AI image generation, smoother transitions, and built-in effects. The editor also gets more finishing controls, from inline image cropping and SVG color editing to better sharing and export reliability.
