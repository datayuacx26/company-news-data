# VideoGen 3.2.0: AI Video Generation & Editing Update for Creators and Teams


VideoGen 3.2.0 rebuilds the editor for smoother performance, introduces 7 guided workflows, adds line/arrow annotations, improves text editing and animations, and makes voiceovers and sharing more reliable for creators and teams.


VideoGen 3.2.0 is centered on a simple goal: make it easier to go from an idea to a publishable video without fighting the editor. We rebuilt the editor to reduce lag and improve responsiveness, especially on longer timelines, and added full support for mobile playback in previews. We also improved reliability in areas that tend to break flow, including voiceovers and project sharing, so creators and teams spend less time troubleshooting and more time finishing.


## Guided Workflows for Faster First Drafts


This release introduces Workflows, seven guided ways to start a new project based on what you already have: an idea, a script, a voiceover, a recording, or existing assets. Instead of requiring manual assembly from scratch, VideoGen helps generate a first draft automatically, then allows refinement inside the same editor. Influencers creating Shorts or Reels, startups building product explainers, and marketing teams producing ad variations can choose the path that fits their workflow. Media style presets in select workflows, including Free stock, AI image, and Premium iStock, help shape early creative direction without adding complexity.


## Smoother Editing and Finishing Tools


Version 3.2.0 also improves the finishing layer, where most editing time tends to go. Updates include faster inline text editing with bold, italics, and bullet support, more performant animations with a duration slider, and four text animation modes: all-at-once, line-by-line, word-by-word, and character-by-character. New tools such as line and arrow annotations, duplicate section, and box select are designed to speed up common tasks, especially for tutorials, demos, and repeatable content series.


Combined with built-in voiceovers, auto subtitles, and generation-first workflows, these improvements support creators and marketing teams looking for AI video software that handles editing more automatically while keeping outputs ready for social media content.


## Designed for Creators, Influencers, and Teams


Whether you are an individual creator looking for AI video platforms for content creation, a startup building brand awareness, or a marketing team testing social and ad concepts, VideoGen 3.2.0 focuses on reducing manual steps between idea and publish. As AI video generation becomes more common, the tools that stand out are the ones that combine generation, editing, and reliability in one place.
