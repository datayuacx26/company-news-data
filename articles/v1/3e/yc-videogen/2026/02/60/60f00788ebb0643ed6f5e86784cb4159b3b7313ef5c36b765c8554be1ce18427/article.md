# VideoGen CEO Anton Koenig interviewed on NapierPR podcast


VideoGen CEO Anton Koenig joins the Marketing B2B Technology podcast to share insights on AI-powered video creation, discussing how combining AI-generated content with professional editing helps marketers produce high-quality videos at scale.


VideoGen CEO Anton Koenig joined Mike Maynard on Napier's Marketing B2B Technology podcast for "Harnessing AI for Video Editing: Insights from VideoGen's CEO Anton Koenig."


Thank you to the Napier team for the thoughtful conversation on AI video, editing workflows, and how marketers can create better video content without slowing down their production process.
