# EMCOR Group, Inc. Announces Participation In Upcoming Investor Events


NORWALK, CONNECTICUT, November 3, 2025 – EMCOR Group, Inc. (NYSE: EME) announced today that Tony Guzzi, Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer, Jason Nalbandian, Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, and Lucas Sullivan, Director, Financial Planning & Analysis, will participate in the following investor events.


**William Blair Fireside Chat**
**Date** : Wednesday, November 5, 2025
**Fireside Chat Discussion Time** : 2:00 p.m. EST
[Click here for live webcast](https://williamblair.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_f_XW9lqZSv6t59mr3HFgEA#/registration)
The fireside chat will be available via live audio webcast on the home page of the Company's website at[http://www.emcorgroup.com](https://emcorgroup.com/) and archived for replay through November 19, 2025.


**Baird 2025 Global Industrial Conference**
**Date** : Thursday, November 13, 2025
**Location** : Chicago, Illinois
**Fireside Chat Discussion Time** : 9:30 a.m. EST
[Click here for live webcast](https://wsw.com/webcast/baird81/register.aspx?conf=baird81&page=eme&url=https://wsw.com/webcast/baird81/eme/1742525)
Interested investors should contact their Baird representative directly to schedule a meeting. The fireside chat will be available via live audio webcast on the home page of the Company's website at[http://www.emcorgroup.com](https://emcorgroup.com/) and archived for replay through December 13, 2025.


**UBS Global Industrials and Transportation Conference**
**Date** : Thursday, December 4, 2025
**Location** : Palm Beach, Florida
Interested investors should contact their UBS representative directly to schedule a meeting.


A copy of the Company’s most recent investor presentation will be available under “[Presentations](https://emcorgroup.com/investor-relations/presentations) ” in Investor Relations section of the Company’s website,[www.emcorgroup.com](https://emcorgroup.com/) , prior to each event.
