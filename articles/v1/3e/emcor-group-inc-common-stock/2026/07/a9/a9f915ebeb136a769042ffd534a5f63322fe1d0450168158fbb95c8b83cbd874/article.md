# EMCOR Group, Inc. Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend


NORWALK, CONNECTICUT, July 1, 2026 – EMCOR Group, Inc. (NYSE: EME) today announced that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.40 per common share. The dividend will be paid on July 31, 2026 to stockholders of record as of July 15, 2026.


A Fortune 500 company and a member of the S&P 500, EMCOR Group, Inc. is a leader in mechanical and electrical construction services, industrial and energy infrastructure and building services.
