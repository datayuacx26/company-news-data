## Smart Ways to Make the Most of Your Summer Getaway


Summer road trips are a great way to explore new places, create lasting memories and enjoy the freedom of the open road. But between fuel, food, lodging and entertainment, costs can add up quickly if you don’t have a plan.


“A road trip doesn’t have to derail your finances,” says Rebecca Guardiola,[Community President](https://www.wsbonline.com/about/teams/community-presidents/) at WaterStone Bank. “The key is treating your vacation like any other financial goal—plan ahead, set a budget and track your spending along the way.”


Before you hit the road, consider these strategies to help keep your trip on budget.


### **5 Ways to Keep Your Road Trip Budget on Track**


#### **1. Start with a realistic budget**


Many travelers budget for gas and hotels but overlook smaller expenses such as tolls, parking, attraction fees, snacks and souvenirs.


Before your trip, estimate your major expenses and then add a cushion of 10% to 15% for unexpected costs. Building those expenses into your plan upfront can help prevent surprises later.


#### **2. Create a daily spending allowance**


Rather than tracking one large vacation budget, divide your spending into daily targets for meals, activities and extras.


“A daily spending limit helps you make better decisions in the moment,” Rebecca says. “It also prevents overspending early in the trip and trying to make up for it later.”


#### **3. Make food a planned expense**


Food is one of the easiest categories to underestimate on a road trip. Multiple convenience store stops, coffee runs and restaurant meals can quickly exceed expectations.


Packing snacks, drinks and simple meals can reduce costs while leaving more room in your budget for experiences and attractions you truly want to enjoy.


#### **4. Be strategic about fuel costs**


Fuel is often one of the largest road trip expenses. Before traveling, map out your route and use fuel-price comparison tools to identify lower-cost options along the way.


If you use a rewards credit card responsibly, consider taking advantage of fuel rewards or cash back programs to help offset some of your travel expenses.


#### **5. Save for your trip before you leave**


One of the best ways to avoid vacation-related financial stress is to save in advance.


“If your vacation money is sitting in the same account as your everyday spending, it’s easy to dip into it,” Rebecca says. “A dedicated savings account can help you stay focused on your goal and arrive at your trip knowing the money is already set aside.”


### **Bonus: Iconic American Road Trips**


Looking for inspiration for your next adventure? These classic routes offer memorable scenery and unique experiences:


- **Route 66** – The historic route from Chicago to Santa Monica remains one of America’s most iconic road trips.
- **Blue Ridge Parkway** – A scenic drive through Virginia and North Carolina with stunning mountain views.
- **Great River Road** – Follow the Mississippi River through 10 states and experience the culture, history and communities that line its banks.
- **Pacific Coast Highway** – One of the country’s most famous coastal drives, featuring spectacular views of California’s shoreline.
- **Columbia River Highway** – A picturesque Oregon route known for dramatic gorge views and waterfalls.


With a little preparation and thoughtful budgeting, you can spend less time worrying about money and more time enjoying the journey.
