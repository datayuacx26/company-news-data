# Welcome Jonathan Reznack, New Business Development Officer


2 minutes read


**Posted:** June 16, 2026


**Wauwatosa, Wis. – June 16, 2026** – WaterStone Bank is pleased to announce the appointment of Jonathan Reznack as Business Development Officer.


In his new role, Reznack will strengthen connections with local businesses, supporting customers with tailored financial solutions, and expanding WaterStone Bank’s presence throughout its markets.


Reznack brings extensive banking and business development experience. Prior to joining WaterStone Bank, he served as Small Business Relationship Manager, Vice President, at Associated Bank where he worked closely with local companies to identify financing options and support long-term growth. Earlier in his career, he held branch leadership roles that built a strong foundation in customer service, sales, and team leadership.


“Continuing to invest in how we support business banking and the communities we serve remains a key priority,” said Julie Glynn, Chief Retail Officer and Executive Vice President. “Jonathan brings strong experience and a commitment to helping local businesses succeed, and he will help advance that effort across our footprint.”


Reznack is an active supporter of the Wisconsin Chinese Chamber of Commerce and was recognized with its Strategic Partner Award for his commitment to local organizations and community engagement. A former professional angler, he also serves on the board of Fishing for the Heart, where he supports outreach efforts through the sport of fishing.


Outside of work, Reznack enjoys hunting, fishing, traveling, and spending time with his family.


## Jonathan Reznack


“I’m excited to join WaterStone Bank and contribute to an organization that is focused on helping local businesses grow and thrive. I look forward to building new connections and supporting customers with solutions that meet their needs.”


**About WaterStone Bank**


WaterStone Bank, established in 1921, is a leading financial institution committed to serving the communities of Milwaukee, Waukesha, and Washington Counties. With a proud history of innovation, integrity, and community service, WaterStone Bank offers a wide range of banking and financial solutions to both individual and business customers. Headquartered in Wauwatosa, Wisconsin, the bank operates 14 branches across the region and is recognized for its commitment to customer service and community involvement.
