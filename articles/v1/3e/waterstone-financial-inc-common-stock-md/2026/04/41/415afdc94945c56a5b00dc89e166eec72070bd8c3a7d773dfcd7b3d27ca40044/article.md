# Welcome Kelly Entringer, Fox Point’s New Community President


2 minutes read


**Posted:** April 29, 2026


**Wauwatosa, Wis. – April 29, 2026** – WaterStone Bank is pleased to announce the appointment of Kelly Entringer as Community President of its Fox Point branch. Entringer brings nearly 30 years of experience across retail management, banking, and operations, along with a strong customer-focused leadership approach.


She earned a bachelor’s degree in accounting from Lakeland University and began her career with Rogan’s Shoes in 1996, where she advanced into leadership roles across multiple locations in the Fox Valley. Entringer later gained banking experience with Athens First Bank & Trust in Georgia before returning to Wisconsin, where she worked for Chase while completing her degree.


“Kelly brings a strong combination of leadership experience and customer focus that aligns well with our community-first approach,” said Megan Weigand, Assistant Vice President, Regional Manager. “We are excited for her to strengthen relationships with customers and businesses in the Fox Point community.”


Entringer resides in Menomonee Falls and enjoys staying active through sports, camping, and biking the Bugline Trail. She is also an avid fan of the Brewers and Bucks and enjoys exploring local sushi restaurants.


## Kelly Entringer


“Customer service has always been at the forefront of my work. My goal is to ensure customers feel supported, valued, and confident in their experience.”


**About WaterStone Bank**


WaterStone Bank, established in 1921, is a leading financial institution committed to serving the communities of Milwaukee, Waukesha, and Washington Counties. With a proud history of innovation, integrity, and community service, WaterStone Bank offers a wide range of banking and financial solutions to both individual and business customers. Headquartered in Wauwatosa, Wisconsin, the bank operates 14 branches across the region and is recognized for its commitment to customer service and community involvement.
