# Taiv ranks #4 on Deloitte Fast 50 | Taiv Blog

We’re incredibly proud to share that Taiv has been ranked #4 on Deloitte’s Technology Fast 50™ list for 2025, recognizing Canada’s fastest-growing technology companies.


This milestone is a signal that the technology and network we’re building is working. The Fast 50 celebrates companies that are driving innovation, leadership, and growth across Canada’s tech ecosystem, and landing in the top five is a powerful validation of what we’ve been building.


At Taiv, our mission has always been to make business TV more fun, engaging, profitable, and easier to manage. Our AI-powered hardware and software are transforming how bars, restaurants, and retailers use their TVs, turning them into dynamic, revenue-generating tools that improve guest experiences and help venues grow.


Ranking #4 is a reflection of the dedication and creativity of our entire team. From developers to sales, operations, account management, onboarding and installers across North America, every person at Taiv has played a part in this achievement.


Over the past year, we’ve expanded our network across the U.S., launched in Canada, and surpassed 3000+ locations. We’ll continue to push the boundaries of what’s possible in the business TV space, and prove that every venue, no matter the size, can take control of what plays on their screens. This recognition from Deloitte reinforces that we’re on the right path, and it motivates us to keep pushing further.


We’re deeply grateful to our customers, partners, and investors for believing in us. This win is yours too.
