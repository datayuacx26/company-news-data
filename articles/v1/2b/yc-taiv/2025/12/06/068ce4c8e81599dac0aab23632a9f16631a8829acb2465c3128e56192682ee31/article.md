# Taiv co-founders named Forbes 30 Under 30 | Taiv Blog

We have some exciting news to share! Taiv co-founders Noah Palansky (CEO) and Jordan Davis (CTO) have been named to the **2026 Forbes 30 Under 30** list in Marketing & Advertising.


Our journey started off with a bold idea: make venue TVs smarter. Fast forward a few years & Taiv is now in nearly **5,000** **venues** , helping **150+ brands** connect with audiences at the exact moments they’re paying attention. We’re reaching **15 million people** a month and continuing to grow. What hasn’t changed is our core mission: Developing technology that makes businesses more fun, engaging, profitable, and easier to manage for patrons, business owners, and advertisers.


Forbes 30U30 is a big achievement for Noah and Jordan, but it’s also a reflection of the **incredible team** behind the scenes. We couldn’t do this without our talented developers, sales reps, account managers, installers, and everyone else pushing our vision forward every day. Without them, none of this happens. We also owe this success to our **customers, partners, and investors** for believing in the vision.
