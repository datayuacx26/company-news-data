# Sports bar TVs as a marketing tool | Taiv Blog

Walk into any sports bar on game night and you’ll see the same thing: fans glued to the screens, soaking in every play. The TVs set the tone - they’re why people come. But while most bars use them only to show the game, those screens can also be one of the most effective marketing tools you already own.


## Using your Captive Audience to Lift Sales and Reducing the Wait-Time Drag


Game nights create a captive audience. Fans are watching, waiting through timeouts and commercials, and already in the mood to order. That’s the perfect moment to promote your own specials.


Picture this: during halftime, instead of guests zoning out to national ads, they see your **bucket-of-beer deal** or a teaser for **Wednesday Trivia Night** . Many will act on it because the timing feels natural.


Busy nights mean guests wait for food, drinks, or a table. Normally, that feels like downtime, but your TVs can turn it into opportunity. Showing the promo card for an **upcoming UFC fight night** or highlighting **signature cocktails** keeps customers engaged and thinking about what else to order.


Fact: Digital signage has been shown to **reduce perceived wait times by up to 35%,** making wait feel shorter and improving overall customer satisfaction. **(**[risevision.com](https://www.risevision.com/blog/reduced-perceived-wait-times-and-perceived-queuing-with-digital-signage) )


## Upselling Every Product, to Every Guest


Servers are busy, and guests don’t always want to hear a sales pitch. But an on-screen reminder feels effortless. A table that came for beer and nachos might suddenly decide to join your **$5 shot promo when the home team scores,** because they saw it on screen at the right moment.


And the numbers back it up! According to[dotsignage.com](https://www.dotsignage.com/blog/32-digital-signage-statistics/) :


- Promoted items on digital signage see a **30%+ lift in sales** .
- Displays can increase the **average purchase amount by nearly 30%.**


## Reinforcing Your Bar’s Identity


Sports bars thrive on community and personality. Use your screens to show **staff picks for the week** , highlight **fan photos from last night’s game** , or share **behind-the-scenes kitchen clips** . Guests remember these small touches, and it makes them feel like they belong, turning one-time visitors into regulars.


Using TVs is like having a dozen free billboards within your four walls. Updating promotions is simple and inexpensive, which means you can stay timely, plugging specials as soon as the weather changes, advertise seasonal menus, or push ticket sales for your next event right when demand is highest.
