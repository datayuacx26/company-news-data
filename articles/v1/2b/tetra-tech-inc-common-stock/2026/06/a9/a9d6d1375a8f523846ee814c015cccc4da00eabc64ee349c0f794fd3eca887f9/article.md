# Strengthening ASEAN Integration and Cooperation through Aus4ASEAN Futures

The Aus4ASEAN Futures program is a strategic partnership developed to bolster the three community pillars of ASEAN: Economic, Socio-Cultural, and Political-Security.


Managed by a dedicated Program Management Team and the Program Planning and Monitoring Support Unit (PPMSU) within the ASEAN Secretariat, the initiative enhances ASEAN’s responsiveness and alignment with member states’ priorities.


## Our role and impact


As the Managing Contractor of the Technical Services and Support Program (TSSP), Tetra Tech International Development provides critical technical, logistical, and project support, facilitating integration into ASEAN systems. Key responsibilities include:


- **Long-Term Advisers:** Recruitment and management of experts in monitoring and evaluation, GEDSI, and strategic communications to ensure the effectiveness and inclusivity of the initiative.
- **Technical Expert Panel:** Maintenance and coordination of a panel of technical experts and partner organisations to provide timely and specialized support for ASEAN projects.
- **Logistics for ASEAN Activities:** Management of travel and logistics for participants from ASEAN Member States and other dialogue partners, enhancing engagement and collaboration in DFAT-funded initiatives.
- **Project and Activity Management:** Utilization of a flexible fund to support the dynamic needs of ASEAN-Australia projects, ensuring adaptability and responsiveness to regional priorities.
