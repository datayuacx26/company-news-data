# Healthcare Analytics ROI: How Modern Infrastructure and Domain Expertise Deliver Real Results

Healthcare organizations invest millions in data analytics and AI tools, yet many struggle to demonstrate value. With[77 percent of health systems](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12202002/) citing immature AI tools as a barrier, the problem isn't the absence of technology—it's the approach to deploying it.


## The Problem: Three Flawed Approaches Sabotage Healthcare Analytics


Most health systems fall into one of three traps:


1. **Generic platforms** offer flexibility but lack healthcare-specific logic, making time-to-value difficult to realize.
2. **Point solutions** deliver depth but fail to scale enterprise-wide.
3. **Mega-platforms** provide broad functionality but prove too rigid for healthcare's variability.


These approaches force healthcare leaders to choose between speed, specificity, and scalability, leaving organizations with expensive platforms but still depending on manual reports for critical decisions.


## Our Answer: Modern Infrastructure + Healthcare Intelligence


Rather than forcing healthcare into generic technology tools, our approach starts with the healthcare problems that need solving—then Health Catalyst leverages[advanced infrastructure](https://www.healthcatalyst.com/use-cases/clinical-outcomes) and AI to accelerate impact.


In one integrated ecosystem, Databricks + Health Catalyst IgniteTM delivers:


- **Databricks** : Unified, scalable data platform; lakehouse architecture; enterprise-wide governance; and AI-ready foundation.
- **Ignite** : Healthcare-specific logic models, clinical and financial benchmarks, regulatory workflows, enrichment pipelines, and prebuilt dashboards.


This combined foundation closes the implementation gap that undermines most analytic programs: Databricks ensures technical power and scalability; Ignite ensures healthcare applicability, trust, and measurability.


Unlike organizations that begin with technology and struggle to adapt it to healthcare, Ignite began with healthcare improvement—and layered AI on top using proven methodologies.


## Proof: Clinical Cost Management in Healthcare in Action


Our solution, Clinical Cost Intelligence (CCI), is an example of this model working end-to-end.


CCI applies Ignite healthcare intelligence and Databricks computing power to[identify and validate cost opportunities](https://go.healthcatalyst.com/WB-250710-PowerCosting_LPOn-Demand.html) across providers, procedures, and supplies. In one case study, this AI-powered solution supported a clinician-led,[7-step improvement process](https://www.healthcatalyst.com/learn/ebooks/7-guiding-questions-a-framework-for-outcomes-improvement) that delivered measurable savings, drove horizontal optimization, and helped healthcare leaders reinvest in service line growth.


How it works:


- AI agents normalize and classify supplies using clinical ontologies.
- Statistical and machine learning models identify cost variation patterns.
- Additional AI agents score opportunities using[our quality-improvement framework](https://www.healthcatalyst.com/use-cases/quality-improvement) to ensure recommendations balance cost impact with clinical appropriateness.


Results:


- $150,000 to $300,000[annual savings](https://go.healthcatalyst.com/WB-250710-PowerCosting_LPOn-Demand.html) on disposable medical equipment.
- $1.2 to $2.5M[potential savings](https://go.healthcatalyst.com/WB-250710-PowerCosting_LPOn-Demand.html) through supply standardization.


## The Proof at Scale: Solving the Hardest Barriers to Healthcare Analytics


The Ignite-Databricks ecosystem solves three barriers generic platforms cannot:


1. **Siloed and rapidly evolving data** . Healthcare data comes from EHRs, claims, labs, pharmacy systems, and more. Databricks unifies enterprise data; Ignite applies healthcare-specific logic, normalization, and validation.
2. **Regulatory and compliance complexity** . HIPAA requirements, CMS submissions, and quality reporting demand workflows tailored to healthcare. Ignite embeds regulatory guardrails and submission workflows directly into the platform.
3. **Trustworthy, clinically grounded AI** . AI in healthcare must be transparent, validated, and clinically relevant. Ignite provides healthcare logic; Databricks provides the AI infrastructure; together, they produce insights clinicians can trust.
