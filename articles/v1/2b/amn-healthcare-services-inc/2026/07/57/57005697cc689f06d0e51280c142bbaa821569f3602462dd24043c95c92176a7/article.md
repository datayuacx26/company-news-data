# Your Career, Your Map: The Ultimate Guide to Starting Your Allied Health Travel Journey

You didn't get into[allied health](https://www.amnhealthcare.com/careers/allied/) to sit still. Whether you're a[physical therapist](https://www.amnhealthcare.com/careers/allied/travel-therapy/physical-therapy/) , a[CT tech,](https://www.amnhealthcare.com/careers/allied/imaging-radiology/ct-tech/) a[respiratory therapist](https://www.amnhealthcare.com/careers/allied/imaging-radiology/Sonography/) , or a[sonographer](https://www.amnhealthcare.com/careers/allied/imaging-radiology/Sonography/) , you chose a career built on skill, adaptability, and impact. So why settle for the same four walls, the same commute, and the same routine year after year?


Travel allied health isn't just a career move. It's a lifestyle upgrade. And right now, the market couldn't be more in your favor.


Here's everything you need to know to get started and why 2026 might be the best time to make the leap.


## The Demand Is Real, and It's Growing


Open allied jobs are up 10% in the last 30 days alone and 8% higher than this same time last year, with OR Tech demand up nearly 19%, MRI Tech demand up over 15%, and Laboratory demand surging by 39%.


Healthcare facilities across the U.S. face persistent staffing shortages driven by an aging population, rising rates of chronic conditions, and ongoing workforce gaps. Travel allied health professionals fill those gaps on short-term contracts, moving where the need is greatest and earning a premium for the flexibility.


The bottom line? Travel allied health jobs are a top priority for facilities to fill, and that means opportunity for you.


## What Does "Travel Allied" Actually Mean?


Travel allied health professionals take 13-week assignments at hospitals and clinics across the country, stepping in where specialized support is needed most. You gain exposure to new equipment, teams, and care settings while staying in control of your career path.


AMN Healthcare places physical therapists, occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, respiratory therapists, radiologic technologists, MRI technologists, CT technologists, sonographers, cardiovascular technologists, and more. Whatever your specialty, there's a travel assignment with your name on it.
