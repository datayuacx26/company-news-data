# Medical Lab Scientist Vacancies Open Now

The medical laboratory workforce is facing a shortage unlike anything the industry has seen in decades, and that means healthcare jobs for medical laboratory scientists and medical technologists are wide open across the United States. Whether you're an experienced MLS exploring travel assignments for the first time or a credentialed MLT ready for your next contract, the market is firmly in your favor.


Here's what you need to know about the current landscape, where the openings are, and how to take the next step.


### The Medical Laboratory Workforce Shortage: Why Vacancies Are Everywhere


The United States is currently short an estimated 20,000 to 25,000 laboratory professionals, creating a sustained wave of open healthcare jobs that shows no signs of slowing down. This isn't a temporary staffing blip, it's a structural crisis driven by a shrinking talent pipeline, a retiring workforce, and rising diagnostic demand.


[Search Medical Lab Scientist Jobs Now](https://www.amnhealthcare.com/jobs/jobs/c/Allied/?filters=JobType:Travel&filters=Discipline:Medical+Technologist/Clinical+Lab+Scientist,Medical+Laboratory+Technician)


The Bureau of Labor Statistics has projected a need for approximately 10,000 new medical laboratory professionals annually, but academic programs are producing only about 5,000 graduates each year. More than 60 percent of current laboratory professionals are approaching retirement age, and accredited training programs have declined by 15 percent over the past decade.


The 2024 ASCP Vacancy Survey confirms that while vacancy rates have declined somewhat relative to the acute peaks of 2022, they remain elevated compared to pre-pandemic levels. Retirement rates continue to climb, with 10 out of 17 laboratory departments reporting increases. For job seekers, this shortage translates directly into leverage: more open positions, more geographic options, and stronger market conditions than the profession has seen in a generation.


### Are Medical Lab Scientists in High Demand?


Yes, and that demand is projected to persist. The Bureau of Labor Statistics projects about 22,600 openings for clinical laboratory technologists and technicians each year through 2034. While employment growth is projected at 2 percent, the sheer volume of annual openings, fueled by replacement needs, tells the real story. The workforce is turning over faster than it can replenish itself.


Federal policymakers have taken notice. The Medical Laboratory Personnel Shortage Relief Act of 2025 provides grants to train more clinical laboratory staff and adds lab professionals to the National Health Service Corps. U.S. News & World Report recognized clinical laboratory technician as one of the best jobs of 2026, citing strong career outlook and job satisfaction.

### Where Are the Most Openings?


The highest concentrations are in states with large populations and extensive hospital networks, including Texas, California, New York, and Florida. However, some of the most urgent vacancies are in rural and critical access settings where permanent recruitment pipelines are thinnest. Facility types hiring now include short-term acute care hospitals, critical access hospitals, independent laboratories, and outpatient clinics.
