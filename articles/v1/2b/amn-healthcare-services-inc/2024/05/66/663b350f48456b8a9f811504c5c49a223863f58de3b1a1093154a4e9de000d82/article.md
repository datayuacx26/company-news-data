# Webinar: Enabling a Tech-Centric, Data-Driven Healthcare Workforce Strategy

Are you ready to explore the innovative solutions currently shaping the future of healthcare? In our latest Becker's Hospital Review webinar, leaders from both Bayhealth, Southern Illinois Healthcare and AMN Healthcare discussed how technology-driven total talent solutions can ensure organizations are equipped to navigate the evolving healthcare staffing landscape more effectively.


**This webinar provides invaluable insights, including:**


- Understanding current challenges and gaining a roadmap for navigating the complex healthcare landscape.
- Practical strategies for achieving control, visibility, and cost comparison while managing both internal and external contingent staff.
- Leveraging root cause analysis, holistic strategies and a range of MSP options (vendor-neutral, hybrid, master supplier) to effectively attract, retain and optimize talent, ensuring the delivery of quality care to communities.
- Exclusive insights into current Associate Chief Nursing Officer and Vice President of Patient Care Services at Bayhealth, Christine Keithly's experiences with implementing technology-driven total talent solutions.
- How Southern Illinois Health leveraged data-driven tech to achieve significant labor cost reductions.
