# Kodak to Feature Advanced Print and Packaging Solutions at PAMEX 2026

Kodak will participate in PAMEX 2026, India’s premier exhibition for print and packaging professionals. From January 27–30, 2026, attendees can visit Kodak at Stall M41, Hall 1, where they can learn about Kodak’s latest innovations designed to help printers achieve higher productivity, sustainability, and profitability.


Kodak will spotlight its advanced prepress technologies, including[computer-to-plate (CTP)](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/page/offset-ctp/) solutions and[KODAK SONORA Process Free Plates](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/page/sonora-process-free-plates/) , focusing on how these innovations empower printers to streamline workflows, enhance quality, and reduce environmental impact. At the booth, Kodak will also conduct presentations of the[KODAK PRINERGY Platform](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/page/prinergy-platform/) tailored to customer requests and requirements. PRINERGY offers advanced automation and cloud-enabled capabilities to simplify production, integrate seamlessly with existing processes, and deliver consistent quality across analog and digital print.


In addition, samples produced on[Kodak’s Inkjet Imprinting Systems](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/products/digital/imprinting/) will be showcased at the booth. These samples demonstrate the exceptional print quality, vibrant color reproduction, and versatility that Kodak’s inkjet technology delivers across a wide range of applications from commercial print to packaging. Visitors will have the opportunity to see firsthand how Kodak’s inkjet solutions can help them expand their offerings and meet evolving customer demands.


“The solutions we’re featuring at PAMEX 2026 demonstrate Kodak’s commitment to innovation and customer success. We look forward to helping print professionals unlock new efficiencies and opportunities,” said Bhalchandra Nikumb, Kodak Print Solutions Sales Director, India.

**About PAMEX**
PAMEX is a major national event held once every two years, bringing together leading industry professionals and showcasing the latest advancements in print and packaging technology. It serves as a hub for innovation, networking, and knowledge sharing across the entire print ecosystem.
