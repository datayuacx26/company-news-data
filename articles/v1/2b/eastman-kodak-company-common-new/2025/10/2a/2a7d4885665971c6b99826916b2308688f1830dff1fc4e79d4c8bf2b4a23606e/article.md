# All-new KODAK PRINERGY webinar series focuses on enhanced automation, color management, and integration

Kodak is launching the second installment of its KODAK PRINERGY webinar series in October, offering an in-depth exploration of the platform’s core capabilities for both digital and offset print workflows. This new series will take a deeper dive into how PRINERGY drives automation and operational efficiencies, empowering printers to streamline production, reduce costs, and enhance overall performance. The topics and dates are:


- *Understanding COLORFLOW Pro & Ink Optimization* − October 7, 2025. Participants will learn how Kodak’s advanced, intelligent color management tools can enhance color precision, cut ink costs and optimize workflow efficiency across their entire print operation.
- *Robust Automation & Digital Print* − November 6, 2025. Explore how PRINERGY delivers powerful automation for both digital and traditional print environments. Learn how its intelligent workflow tools streamline production, reduce costs, and boost operational efficiency.
- *The Power of Extended Integration* − December 16, 2025. Discover how PRINERGY’s expanded integration streamlines connectivity with MIS systems and a wide range of third-party tools. This webinar highlights how unified workflows and automated data exchange increase efficiency and control.
- *Barcode Automation & 3D Rendering* − January 15, 2026. Uncover how the KODAK PRINERGY Platform uses barcode automation and new 3D rendering to simplify tracking, reduce manual steps, and visualize packaging with precision.


The webinars are aimed at both existing PRINERGY customers and prospective users from the global print community. In addition to demos and practical tips, the 45-minute sessions will give attendees the opportunity to have their questions answered by Kodak experts. The webinars will take place twice each day − once for European and once for North American participants. The full schedule and registration is available at[kodak.com/go/webinars](https://www.kodak.com/go/webinars) .
