# New Kodak Sustainability Report highlights ongoing environmental initiatives and achievements

At Kodak, sustainability is not just a goal, it is a core


value that drives the company’s innovation and global operations. A clear testimony to this


is the new edition of Kodak's Sustainability Report entitled “One World, One Kodak.” The


report provides a comprehensive summary of ongoing sustainability initiatives and


achievements across Kodak's global business activities in 2023 and through Q3 2024.


In addition to an update on Kodak's sustainability strategy, the new publication


highlights the significant milestones that Kodak has achieved on its sustainability journey.


These include a 56% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions and a 31% reduction in water


withdrawal, both ahead of the company’s 2025 targets. The accomplishments reflect


Kodak's proactive approach to sustainability, as well as a long-term vision for a more


sustainable future. Kodak's sustainability strategy also emphasizes caring for its employees


and communities. Kodak has made further strides in enhancing employee health, safety,


and well-being, and its community impact initiatives.


The 44-page report underlines how Kodak products and technologies are enabling


customers in the printing industry to become more sustainable by reducing energy, water


and chemical consumption and avoiding waste. Innovative solutions, such as KODAK


SONORA Process Free Plates, KODAK PROSPER Presses and Imprinting Systems as well as


water-based KODACHROME and KODAK EKTACOLOR Inks, play a crucial role in helping


printers reduce their environmental footprint. Consequently, the report features various


printers from around the world who explain the positive impact of Kodak products on their


sustainability efforts.


The 2024 report also contains updated information aligned with the Global Reporting


Initiative (GRI), ensuring that Kodak’s customers, partners and suppliers have access to


comprehensive and reliable information about the company’s sustainability performance.


“Our sustainability strategy is rooted in the belief that we can make a positive impact on


the world while delivering value to our customers and shareholders,” commented Jim


Continenza, Executive Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Kodak. “Sustainability is an


integral part of our growth strategy and a reflection of our corporate values.”


Kodak’s Sustainability Report for 2024, “One World, One Kodak,” can be downloaded at


[https://www.kodak.com/go/sustainability](https://www.kodak.com/en/company/page/sustainability-old/) .
