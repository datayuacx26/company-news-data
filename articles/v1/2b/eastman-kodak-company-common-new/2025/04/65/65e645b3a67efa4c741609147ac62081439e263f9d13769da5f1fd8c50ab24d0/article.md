# Kodak to showcase cutting-edge prepress and print technologies at China Print 2025

Kodak's unmatched portfolio of offset, inkjet and software


solutions will be featured at China Print 2025 taking place from May 15 to 19 at the New


China International Exhibition Center in Beijing. At China's largest and most influential trade


show for the printing and packaging industry, Kodak will showcase cutting-edge solutions at


its booth E2-115 in hall E2.


The comprehensive range of high-performance Kodak CTP solutions will be represented


by the KODAK MAGNUS Q800 Platesetter and the KODAK TRENDSETTER Q800 Platesetter,


both with Single Cassette Unit (SCU) automation for automated plate loading. Offering the


most complete portfolio of smart prepress solutions, Kodak is ideally positioned to provide


any printer with a customized digital platemaking solution. The Kodak team will


demonstrate live imaging of KODAK SONORA XTRA Process Free Plates,* which offer fast


imaging speeds, strong image contrast and optimized handling robustness.


Also on display will be the KODAK PRINERGY Platform including the KODAK PRINERGY


INSITE Portals, an integrated workflow solution that streamlines production across digital


and analog printing processes with collaboration between printers and their clients.


PRINERGY version 11.0 is now available, bringing significant advancements to the


functionality, performance and connectivity of Kodak's industry-leading workflow software.


Show attendees will see the capabilities of high-speed KODAK PROSPER Imprinting


Systems with a wealth of print samples from customer jobs and KODAK PROSPER Print Bar's


high-quality wider printing width samples. KODAK PROSPER Print Bar incorporates KODAK


PROSPER Plus Imprinting Systems to bring unprecedented flexibility and color-to-color


registration accuracy to hybrid printing.


“We look forward to welcoming printers and prepress professionals to our booth at


China's premier trade show on printing and packaging technologies,” said Jenine Rose-


Johnson, VP, Ops/Alt Channels & APR Sales, Kodak. “China Print visitors will see that Kodak


is committed to leading the way with innovative, highly productive solutions that enable


printers to profitably serve their customers and grow their business.”
