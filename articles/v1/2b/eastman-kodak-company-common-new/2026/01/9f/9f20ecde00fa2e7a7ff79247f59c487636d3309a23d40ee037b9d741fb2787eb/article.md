# Kodak to feature advanced print solutions at page2026 in Tokyo

Kodak will participate in page2026 held at the Sunshine City Convention Center, Tokyo, from February 18 to 20. At booth C-13 (hall C, Bunka Kaikan 3F), show visitors will learn about Kodak’s innovative CTP, plate, workflow software and inkjet technologies.


[KODAK PROSPER Imprinting Systems](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/page/imprinting-systems/) add digital capabilities to analog printing processes and enable hybrid production without compromising throughput.[PROSPER Plus](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/product/digital/imprinting/prosper-plus/) and[PROSPER S-Series Imprinting Systems](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/product/digital/imprinting/prosper-s-series/) and the[PROSPER Print Bar](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/product/digital/prosper-print-bar/) are designed for integration into offset, flexo or gravure presses, converting lines, and finishing equipment. Kodak will present a range of print samples, allowing attendees to experience the application versatility and quality of PROSPER Imprinting solutions.


Kodak leads the market with innovations in highly efficient and sustainable process-free plate technology with[KODAK SONORA XTRA Plates](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/product/offset/plates/sonora-xtra/) made in Japan. Kodak will demonstrate imaging of SONORA XTRA Plates on a[KODAK TRENDSETTER Platesetter](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/product/offset/ctp-systems/trendsetter-q1600-platesetter/) . The platesetter will also image TRF-IR830 Thermal Laser Film from Mitsubishi Paper Mills. This dry film offers an efficient computer-to-film solution for flexo/letterpress printing, screen printing and printed circuit board applications.


Kodak will showcase the[KODAK PRINERGY Platform](https://www.kodak.com/en/print/page/prinergy-platform/) , an integrated workflow automation solution that streamlines and optimizes production across analog and digital printing. Supporting industry standards such as Japan Color and G7, KODAK PRINERGY COLORFLOW Software helps printers achieve consistent and accurate color across various devices and substrates.


"We eagerly anticipate participating in page2026, where we will demonstrate how Kodak empowers printers to successfully and profitably tackle the challenges of today’s dynamic print market with efficient solutions for analog, digital, and hybrid printing," said Takeshi Nakagawa, Sales Director Japan, Kodak.
