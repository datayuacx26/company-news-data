# Xunlei Limited Schedules 2025 Unaudited Third Quarter Earnings Release on November 13, 2025


SHENZHEN, China, Nov. 06, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Xunlei Limited (“Xunlei” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: XNET), a leading technology company providing distributed cloud services in China, today announced that it plans to release its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2025 on November 13, 2025 before market open.


The earnings press release will be available on the Company's investor relations page at[http://ir.xunlei.com.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=2cygJtlskxHdcmNtG1drv2On4K4aOPl7tKnE7__9rg-A6nTNcQCxU4m1DR3ZHcKPWQM_Fu21-MftJJev5OuMYK5c4Aqkg2dh5a-cVBMMecs=)


**Conference Call**


Xunlei's management will host a conference call at 7:00 a.m. U.S. Eastern Time on November 13, 2025 (8:00 p.m. Beijing/Hong Kong Time), to discuss the Company's quarterly results and recent business developments.
