# Deepnote is now open-source

## [November 5, 2025](https://deepnote.com/changelog/2025-11-05)


###


[Deepnote is now open-source](https://deepnote.com/changelog/2025-11-05#deepnote-is-now-open-source)


After seven years of building on the notebook, we're opening it up. Deepnote is now open-source under the Apache 2.0 license. That includes the new` .deepnote` format — a human-readable, project-based standard built for collaboration, multiple languages, and AI agents working alongside you. Your existing work comes with you: convert any` .ipynb` with` npx @deepnote/convert` and open it in VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf, or JupyterLab. Star us on[GitHub](https://github.com/deepnote/deepnote) and build on it with us.


Read more about why we built a new notebook format[here](https://deepnote.com/blog/why-we-built-a-new-notebook) .
