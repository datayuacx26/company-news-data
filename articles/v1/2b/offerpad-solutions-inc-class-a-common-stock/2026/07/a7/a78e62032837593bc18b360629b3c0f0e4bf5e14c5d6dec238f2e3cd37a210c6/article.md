# DIY Fixes to Help Your Home Sell Faster

Does your home need a little TLC before it goes on the market? Plenty of simple, budget-friendly DIY fixes can help it show better and sell faster. Hit up YouTube and Pinterest for how-tos and inspiration, or start with a few tips from Offerpad’s own renovations crew. Not the handiest? Skip to the end, because there are easier options too.


## Start with fresh paint


Repainting is one of the easiest ways to appeal to more buyers. Cover faded or bold colors with soft, warm neutrals for a clean, updated feel, and remember there is more to neutrals than gray and beige. One tip: avoid painting the walls and ceiling the same color, especially in smaller rooms, so the space does not feel boxed in.


## Swap dated hardware and fixtures


After you put down the paintbrush, pick up a screwdriver. Cabinet hardware seems like a small detail, but new handles and knobs in the kitchen and bathrooms can refresh the whole look of a room for very little money or effort.


## Handle the small repairs


You have probably learned to overlook the minor repairs that never made the to-do list. Buyers will not. Start simple: swap old incandescent bulbs for LEDs and replace dated light fixtures. Then work through the easy safety and wear items, like uneven flooring, loose carpet and railings, torn screens, sticking windows, clogged drains, leaks under sinks, and cracked caulk. Tighten or replace loose hinges so doors and cabinets open smoothly and closet doors stay on track.


## Do not forget the curb


You know the saying about judging a book by its cover. A lot of drive-by buyers do exactly that, and if the outside does not appeal to them, they may never come see the inside. Keep the grass trimmed and weeded, tidy the yard, and take down any leftover holiday lights. Prune overgrown bushes and trees so the landscaping adds to the home instead of hiding it. Clean the deck, porch, and outdoor furniture, and draw the eye to your front door with a few potted plants or flowers along the walkway.
