## What is a cold email service?


A cold email service is any solution that helps businesses find prospects, launch outreach campaigns, improve inbox placement, and generate meetings or revenue through outbound email. In practice, “cold email service” is a broader and more accurate term than “agency” because the market now includes both service providers and software platforms. Some companies handle everything for you, while others provide the infrastructure, data, automation, and workflow tools your team needs to run outbound internally.


## The two types of cold email services


### 1. Done-for-you cold email services


These companies handle campaign setup, targeting, messaging, and execution for you.


### 2. Cold email platforms


These tools give your team the software needed to manage prospecting, sending, automation, or deliverability internally.


Both can work extremely well. The right choice depends on your team, your budget, and your growth stage.

# Why deliverability is the foundation of cold email


Cold email success depends on sender reputation and inbox placement. Domain health, authentication, engagement signals, and sending behavior all influence whether a campaign lands in the inbox or vanishes into spam. That is why deliverability is no longer a technical detail to fix later. It is the foundation that determines whether copywriting, targeting, and sequencing can work at all. Even the best service provider or platform will underperform if the setup is weak.
