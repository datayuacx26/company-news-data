# SCDNA26: Storage and Computing with DNA Conference in Rome

# SCDNA26: Storage and Computing with DNA Conference in Rome


Join the global community driving the future of DNA-based data storage and computing at SCDNA26! Taking place May 27-29, 2026 at Roma Tre University in Rome, Italy, this conference is co-organized by DigNA and the DNA Data Storage Alliance. Kilobaser is proud to be part of this initiative through the DNAMIC project.


### [DigNA Portfolio](https://kilobaser.com/digna-dna-data-storage) & DNA Data Storage Alliance present:


## Storage and Computing with DNA 2026 (SCDNA26)


**Date:** May 27-29, 2026


**Location:** Aula Magna, Rectorate of Roma Tre University, Via Ostiense 133, Torre A, 00144 Rome, Italy


**Website:**[scdna26.day-one.biz](https://scdna26.day-one.biz/)


The SCDNA26 conference unites researchers, industry experts, and startups working on DNA-based data storage and computing. It provides a forum for sharing ideas, discussing the latest developments, and identifying opportunities for collaboration between research and commercial sectors.


The event is co-organized by the[DNA Data Storage Alliance](https://dnastoragealliance.org/) (a SNIA community), the[DigNA portfolio](https://kilobaser.com/digna-dna-data-storage) (European Innovation Council), and[Day One](https://www.day-one.biz/) .


Kilobaser is proud to be part of the DigNA portfolio through the[DNAMIC project](https://kilobaser.com/dnamic-dna-data-storage) , contributing decentralized[DNA synthesis technology](https://kilobaser.com/technology) for data storage applications.


## Conference Topics


The conference brings together experts across the full spectrum of DNA-based information technology:


-


**DNA Data Storage** — Solutions for long-term data preservation using DNA as a storage medium
-


**DNA Computing** — Novel computing paradigms using DNA molecules as computational substrates
-


**Networking & Collaboration** — Opportunities for cross-sector partnerships between research and commercial initiatives


Whether you are a researcher, industry professional, or startup founder working on[DNA data storage](https://kilobaser.com/post/dna-data-storage) or computing technologies, SCDNA26 is the place to connect with the global community shaping the future of these deep technologies.


## Confirmed Keynote Speakers


The conference features keynote presentations from leading figures in DNA data storage and computing:


-

-


**Jeff Nivala** — Assistant Professor, University of Washington
-


**Damien Woods** — Professor, Maynooth University
-

More speakers and the full program will be announced on the[conference website](https://scdna26.day-one.biz/) .
