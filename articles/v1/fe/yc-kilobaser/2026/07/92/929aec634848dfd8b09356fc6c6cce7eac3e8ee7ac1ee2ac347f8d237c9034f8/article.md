# Introducing the New Hexynyl Cartridge: Revolutionary Click Chemistry at Your Fingertips

### What is Hexynyl Modification?


The Hexynyl modification introduces a terminal alkyne group to your oligonucleotides, creating a powerful platform for further modifications through click chemistry. This allows you to attach azide-labeled molecules to your DNA or RNA sequences, opening doors to a wide range of applications in research, diagnostics, and therapeutics.

## About Click Chemistry


### The principle of CuAAC


In click chemistry, two functional groups bind to each other, each on a molecule. We use Cu(I)-catalyzed azide-alkyne click chemistry (CuAAC). As the name implies, the binding reaction is catalyzed by Cu(I) ions. Thus, the DNA-bound 5’-Hexynyl Phosphoramidite anchor binds in a Cu(I) environment with the azide conjugated dye to form a stable alkyne.
