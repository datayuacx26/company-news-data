# Kilobaser N₂Go Nitrogen Generator

# Kilobaser N2Go Nitrogen Generator


Say goodbye to gas cylinders. The Kilobaser N2Go is our new compact, portable nitrogen generator — designed to supply your Kilobaser synthesizer with N2 gas, right at your benchtop.


## No more gas cylinders


Running a DNA or RNA synthesizer requires a reliable supply of inert gas — typically nitrogen — to drive the pneumatics of the synthesis process. Until now, that meant managing pressurized gas cylinders: scheduling deliveries, handling heavy bottles, and dealing with the associated logistics and safety considerations. The **Kilobaser N₂Go** changes that. It generates nitrogen on demand, directly from ambient air, and stores it in an integrated 2-liter tank — ready to supply your synthesizer whenever you need it, wherever you need it.


## Designed for the Kilobaser platform


The N₂Go was developed as the ideal gas supply companion for the[Kilobaser One](https://kilobaser.com/product/kilobaser-one) and[Kilobaser One-XT](https://kilobaser.com/product/kilobaser-one-xt) synthesizers. It delivers output pressure up to 6.5 bar with a flow rate of 0.8–2 l/min — precisely matched to the requirements of the Kilobaser synthesis workflow.


The unit regulates pressure automatically. Once the tank reaches its target pressure, it switches off. When pressure drops below the lower threshold, it restarts — all without any manual intervention.


## Compact and portable


At **400×270×315 mm** and **15 kg** , the N₂Go fits comfortably beside your synthesizer on the benchtop. No dedicated gas storage room, no cylinder racks, no regulator assembly.


**Key specifications:**


-


Output pressure: max. 6.5 bar
-


Flow rate: 0.8–2 l/min (adjustable)
-

-

-
