# The Rocketable Manifesto

# The Rocketable Manifesto


### Thoughts on building single person, billion dollar software companies


[Alan Wells](https://substack.com/@rocketable)


Jan 23, 2025


There will be a moment in time, in the not too distant future, when it will be possible to replace every human in the org chart of a software company with an AI agent.


Today's foundation models already possess enough raw intelligence to automate most of the tasks required to run a profitable software business.In the functions where the models aren't yet intelligent enough, they will be soon.


When every human function required to operate a software product has an AI agent equivalent, the next step will be within reach: operating a company entirely with AI.


But AI agents within each function won't be enough. What’s missing?


To operate the single person, billion dollar software companies of the future, agents will need to be:


-


directed towards common goals


-


integrated to collaborate across functions


-


tuned with feedback loops connected to product metrics


-


trained to make resource allocation decisions


At


[Rocketable](https://www.rocketable.com/) , we are creating the tools that let us replace the entire org chart of a software company with a team of AI agents. Our strategy is to develop these tools by owning the products that we automate, because we know from experience that integration is the key to unlocking superhuman performance.


Our process is simple to explain but difficult to execute:


-


acquire an existing software product


-


replace the human org chart with AI agents


-


iterate until agents consistently deliver superhuman performance


-


repeat (at increasingly larger scales)


We believe that when AI agents are properly crafted, coordinated, and applied to the business of operating software products, superhuman outcomes will follow: higher quality products that customers love, developed more quickly, at orders of magnitude lower cost


This is how single person, billion dollar software companies will be built in the age of intelligence.
