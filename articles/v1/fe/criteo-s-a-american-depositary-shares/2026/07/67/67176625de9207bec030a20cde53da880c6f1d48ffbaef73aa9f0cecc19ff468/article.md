The best growth marketing strategies aren’t built on theory. They’re built on results. While every business has unique goals, the challenges tend to look remarkably similar. Teams want to attract more customers, improve return on ad spend (ROAS), reduce the time spent managing campaigns, and continue growing without dramatically increasing advertising budgets.

## What makes a successful growth marketing strategy?


Growth marketing is often associated with experimentation, but sustainable growth requires more than simply testing new channels.


The strongest growth strategies combine customer acquisition, retention, creative optimization, and performance measurement into a system that continuously improves over time. Increasingly, AI is becoming an important part of that process by helping marketers identify high-intent shoppers, optimize budgets automatically, personalize creative, and adapt campaigns in real time.


The case studies below demonstrate how those ideas translate into measurable business outcomes.


## Agape Diamonds: Turning abandoned carts into higher-value customers


Luxury purchases often involve careful consideration, making cart abandonment one of the biggest challenges for jewelry retailers.


Agape Diamonds, a U.S.-based brand specializing in ethically sourced lab-grown and simulated diamonds, wanted to go beyond traditional retargeting. The company needed a way to attract new high-intent shoppers while encouraging existing visitors to return and complete their purchases.


Rather than adding more manual optimization, Agape upgraded its existing approach with Criteo GO. AI-powered campaigns automatically delivered personalized product ads across the open web and supported social channels based on real-time browsing behavior and purchase intent. At the same time, the company launched a dedicated new customer acquisition campaign, creating a full-funnel strategy that reached shoppers from discovery through conversion.


The results demonstrated the impact of combining automation with commerce-focused advertising:


- 20% increase in ROAS
- 20% increase in conversion rate
- 13% increase in average cart value

## Wine Country Gift Baskets: Scaling holiday growth without sacrificing efficiency


Holiday shopping creates enormous opportunities for ecommerce brands, but it also creates intense competition.


Wine Country Gift Baskets had reached a point where increasing advertising spend risked reducing efficiency. The company needed to continue growing during its busiest season while maintaining strong ROAS, even as competition intensified.


Using Criteo GO as the foundation of a full-funnel strategy, Wine Country expanded beyond acquisition and retention campaigns by incorporating online video and social advertising into a coordinated cross-channel approach. AI continuously optimized bidding, audience targeting, and placements, allowing the company to scale without introducing unnecessary complexity.


The performance gains were impressive:


- 28% year-over-year improvement in ROAS
- 10% increase in average cart value
- 13% increase in advertising spend while maintaining efficiency

## What these growth marketing reveal about modern advertising campaigns


Looking across these examples, several consistent themes emerge. Successful brands aren’t simply increasing advertising budgets. They’re improving how those budgets are used by combining AI, automation, and commerce intelligence throughout the customer journey.


Common characteristics include:


- AI-powered optimization that continuously adjusts bidding and delivery.
- Cross-channel campaigns that reach shoppers beyond a single platform.
- Personalized creative based on shopping behavior and product interest.
- Faster campaign setup with significantly less manual management.
- Performance improvements measured through business outcomes such as ROAS, conversions, revenue, and customer acquisition.


Taken together, these capabilities help businesses create marketing programs that are both easier to manage and better equipped to grow over time.


## Why growth marketing is evolving


Growth marketing has always been about finding sustainable ways to improve performance. What’s changing is how marketers accomplish that goal. As customer journeys become more fragmented and advertising channels continue to multiply, manually managing every campaign becomes increasingly difficult. AI is helping close that gap by automating repetitive work, identifying high-intent shoppers, generating creative assets, and optimizing campaigns continuously.


For growing businesses, that means sophisticated advertising capabilities are becoming accessible without requiring enterprise-sized teams or endless hours of manual optimization.


The brands highlighted here represent different industries and different business goals, but they all demonstrate how combining AI with a cross-channel strategy can produce meaningful, measurable results.
