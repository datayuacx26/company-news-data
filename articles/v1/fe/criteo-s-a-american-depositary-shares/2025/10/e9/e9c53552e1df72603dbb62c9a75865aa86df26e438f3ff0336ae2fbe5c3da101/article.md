# Introducing Programmatic Guaranteed Deals on Commerce Grid

Table of contents


1. Why this matters
2. What’s new?
3. Where Programmatic Guaranteed deals shine
4. How to get started with PG deals on Commerce Grid


The way buyers and sellers trade premium inventory is always evolving.


Today, we’re taking another step forward.


Criteo Commerce Grid now supports


**Programmatic Guaranteed (PG) deals** , giving both publishers and buyers the best of both worlds. You’ll get the certainty of direct sales and the efficiency of programmatic.


Beginning with DV360 Direct, this launch opens a fresh chapter for deals-based trading, especially for high‑demand formats like video and CTV where guaranteed delivery and predictable performance matter most.


## Why


this matters


In a crowded and unpredictable programmatic ecosystem, certainty is a sought-after commodity.


Buyers want guaranteed impressions, fixed pricing, and outcomes they can count on.


Publishers want predictable revenue, stronger partnerships, and the confidence that deals will deliver as promised.


With Programmatic Guaranteed deals on Commerce Grid, everybody wins:


- Guaranteed delivery with fixed pricing


- Priority in auctions when enabled by the publisher, ensuring PG deals win first


- Cross-device frequency capping, powered by the Criteo Shopper Graph


- Seamless activation via the Commerce Grid user interface


The result


is


smarter trading, less risk, stronger relationships between buyers and sellers


– and a happier ecosystem for everyone.


## What’s


new?


PG deals on Commerce Grid is a very big deal, if you’ll pardon the pun.


For the more technically minded, here’s exactly what’s changing under the hood:


- **DV360 Direct with bidirectional syncing:** Google DV360 is currently the supported DSP for full PG deal integration, so deals sync automatically between Commerce Grid and DV360 for streamlined activation.


- **Flexible PG trading workflows across DSPs:** Programmatic Guaranteed is also supported with other DSPs, though setup and activation mechanics may vary depending on DSP capabilities.


- **Cross-device efficiency:** Frequency capping works across users and devices to reduce waste and improve reach without over-serving.


- **Simple activation:** Publishers can enable Programmatic Guaranteed in the Commerce Grid UI and configure the deal based on the buyer’s DSP workflow. Buyers then activate the deal according to the relevant setup for their DSP.

PG deals are designed for environments and situations where predictability is what matters most. That includes:


- **Premium Video and CTV:** Secure high-value inventory with guaranteed outcomes.

## How to get started with PG deals on Commerce Grid


Activating PG deals is remarkably simple.


In fact, we can boil it down to just two core steps: one for the sell side, and the other for the buy side:


1. Publishers create a supply package, set flight dates, budget, and pacing, then configure the deal based on the buyer’s DSP workflow.
2. Buyers activate the deal according to the setup required by their DSP and confirm campaign readiness with their publisher or Commerce Grid contact.


With PG deals on Commerce Grid, both sides of the supply chain get a streamlined workflow


*and* the confidence that campaigns will deliver as planned.
