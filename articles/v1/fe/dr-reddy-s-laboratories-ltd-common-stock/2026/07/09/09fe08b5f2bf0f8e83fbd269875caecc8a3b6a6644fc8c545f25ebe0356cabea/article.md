# Dr. Reddy's API team at CPHI Milan 2024

#### Dr. Reddy's API team at CPHI Milan 2024


We are thrilled to share the incredible success of Dr. Reddy's API team at CPHI Milan 2024, held from October 8th to 10th at Fiera Milano, Italy. This prestigious event brought together over 62,000 attendees and 2,400 exhibitors from 168+ countries, making it a global hub for pharmaceutical innovation and collaboration1.


CPHI Milan provided an excellent platform for networking. Our experts engaged with industry leaders, fostering new partnerships and strengthening existing relationships. The event was a testament to our collaborative spirit and dedication to advancing global health. Sustainability remains at the core of our operations. Our participation at CPHI Milan emphasized our commitment to sustainable practices, aligning with our goal to reach over 1.5 billion patients globally by 20301.


We extend our heartfelt thanks to everyone who visited our booth and engaged with our team. Your support and collaboration are vital as we continue to innovate and deliver high-quality pharmaceutical solutions worldwide.
