# Dr. Reddy's AI Day: Igniting the Future of Healthcare with Innovation

Dr. Reddy's AI Day: Igniting the Future of Healthcare with Innovation - May 8th, 2024, marked a pivotal moment as Dr. Reddy’s hosted a dynamic AI Day, bringing together luminaries from across the industries and technology spectrum to delve into the transformative power of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in our world today. Kicking off with a thought-provoking session led by our esteemed leadership team, the event sparked passionate discussions on the intersection of AI with drug discovery, data sciences, co-pilots, automation, and more. It was a forum where ideas flowed freely and insights were shared eagerly, laying the groundwork for a future were innovation reigns supreme. Our keynote talks are featured by Vishal Sikka, CEO, Vianai (former CEO of Infosys), Dr. Rohini Srivathsa, CTO of Microsoft India, Ekhlaque Bari, CEO, XdotO, Ashwin Sugavanam, head of data, Thought works and Dr. Srikanth Velamakanni, Co-Founder, Group Chief Executive & Executive Vice-Chairman of Fractal.


Our esteemed partners —[Amazon](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amazon/) ,[AuxoAI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/auxoai/) ,[Cognizant](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cognizant/) ,[Google](https://www.linkedin.com/company/google/) ,[Microsoft](https://www.linkedin.com/company/microsoft/) ,[Salesforce](https://www.linkedin.com/company/salesforce/) ,[Thoughtworks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/thoughtworks/) , and more—actively contributed to the event, showcasing their innovative AI solutions and use cases across industries, with a special focus on healthcare. Their stalls, dashboards, and demo videos vividly depicted AI’s potential, sparking imaginations and igniting conversations, and their active participation underscored the collaborative nature of the event. Our internal team proudly presented the AI-generated applications, models, and dashboards that are already revolutionizing processes within[Dr. Reddy's](https://api-events.drreddys.com/zh-hans) . These innovations, deeply rooted in our business processes, are making a tangible difference in our operations, streamlining workflows and enhancing efficiency. The lively exchanges that followed underscored the collaborative spirit driving our journey into the future, where knowledge sharing is paramount, and innovation knows no bounds.


As we reflect on Dr. Reddy's AI Day's success, one thing is abundantly clear: AI’s transformative power is shaping the future of healthcare. Together, we stand at the forefront of this revolution, poised to drive meaningful change and usher in a new era of possibility.
