# Ultra 2.0: Loading…

*The next-generation gaming platform built by gamers, for gamers, is coming!*


Seven years. That’s how long we’ve been grinding together, building, testing, failing, winning and learning alongside you. From the very first member of the Ultra community, to those who just discovered us, and everyone who believed in us along the way… you’re the reason Ultra exists.


Now, it’s time to level up.


We’re almost ready to drop Ultra 2.0. This is not just a reskin, or extra features, but a whole new way of thinking about what a gaming platform should be. This one’s for you!


---


## Why It Hits Different?


We’ve all seen this cycle: no content means no users, no users means no content. That’s a death spiral for platforms. With Ultra 2.0, we’re flipping it on it’s head. We’re going big on **bringing the next wave of gamers** with an onboarding tool we think you’ll love.


But there is more than that:


- We’ve put our design team to the test to create a fresh, modern look and feel.
- Gaming that is social again. There is no better feeling than joining your best friend, and jumping into your favorite game. We’re here to create those memories.
- Rewards for just being a gamer? Hell yes. Play games, complete quests, and progress.
- Fast updates, built with you, tested with you.


This isn’t corporate talk. This is us saying that Ultra 2.0 is our chance to take everything we’ve built, and finally bring it all together.

## What’s Under The Hood?


Ultra 2.0 runs on top of a backbone we’ve been perfecting for years: the game store, the wallet, the marketplace, the dev tools, and our blockchain. Everything is battle-tested and now we’re upgrading it into something that brings everything you need as a gamer into one place, and makes it more rewarding than ever.


You don’t need to care about “blockchain” to enjoy it. No fees, no friction, no wallets to set up. Just log in, play, earn, and own. If you’ve been with us since day one, you already know, this has been our mission from the start. Built for Gamers. Built for You.
