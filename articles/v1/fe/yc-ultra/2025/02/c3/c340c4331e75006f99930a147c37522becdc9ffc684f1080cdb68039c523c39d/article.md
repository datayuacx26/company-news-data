# Ashes of Mankind: Roadmap Reveal

The future of Ashes of Mankind is here and it’s packed with ambitious updates, groundbreaking innovations, and a whole lot of action. During a special roadmap reveal, **Adrien** , **Rod** , and **Doug** broke down everything that’s coming in 2025, from Citadels and Empires to new monetization mechanics and a game-changing ranked system.


---


Let’s get to it.


---


---


---


---


## **Q2 2025: Combat, Customization & Economy Tweaks**


### **Citadels: Combat Overhaul & Dynamic Missions**


Q2 is all about getting the Citadels experience right, making combat tighter, looting more rewarding, and the overall gameplay smoother. Here’s what’s coming:


- **Combat & Customization Overhaul** : Expect an additional stance (pruning), improved animations, and more realistic weapon handling.
- **Health & Armor System Finalization** : Balancing survival mechanics for a more immersive experience.
- **Gear & Loot System Revamp** : New loot mechanics, expanded gear options, and item rarity tiers.
- **Dynamic Missions & Hostage Rescues** : More mission variety to keep things fresh.
- **Optimization Improvements** : A continued effort to make the game run better for everyone.


---


### **Empires: Monetization & Tokenomics**


On the Empires side, things are ramping up as the economy takes shape:


- **Monetization & Balancing** : Fine-tuning resource earning, trading, and spending.
- $ **ASH Tokenomics** : Release tokenomics detailing how the in-game economy functions and how players can earn and use $ASH.
- **Deeper Citadels-Empires Integration** : The first step toward a fully connected AoM universe.


---


---


## **Q3 2025: Persistence & Ranked Play**


### **Citadels: Persistent Sessions & Ranked Play**


We’re taking things to the next level with persistence and competitive gaming:


- **Persistent Sessions** : The Citadel becomes an ongoing, living world.
- **Character Progression & Advanced Crafting** : Upgrade weapons and fine-tune your playstyle.
- **Ranked Play** : A never-before-seen system where survival, loot quality, and strategy determine your rank.
- **Leaderboard System** : Track your progress and prove you’re the best.


---


### **Empires: Corporations, Freelancers & Staking**


Big moves for the Empires economy:


- **Corporations & Freelancers Soft Launch** : Corporations dominate the economy, freelancers take on jobs, both shaping the in-game market.
- $ **ASH Staking System** : Lock $ASH tokens for rewards and in-game benefits.


---


---


## **Q4 2025: Open World & Full Empires Launch**


### **Citadels: Open World Begins**


By the end of 2025, Ashes of Mankind will truly begin its evolution into an open-world experience:


- **Open World Exploration** : Move beyond the Citadel into the wild.
- **Final Optimization Pass** : Ensuring smooth performance for all players on more devices.


---


### **Empires: Full-Scale Economic Warfare**


The Empires experience reaches its full form:


- **Final Empires Launch** : all systems go. Fully operational in-game economy with production, trading, and staking.
- **Seamless Player-Driven Economy** : Empires degens, Citadels players, all interacting without even realizing they’re using Web3.


---

## **Final thoughts**


This Ashes of Mankind roadmap isn’t just a list of updates, it’s the blueprint for a gaming revolution, something that the space hasn’t seen before. Whether you’re diving into Citadels combat, shaping the economy in Empires, or just staking $ASH for rewards, 2025 is shaping up to be a defining year.
