# Republic Airways® Plane Pull 2026 Recap

The 2026 Republic Airways® Plane Pull presented by Lucas Oil marked the 14th time we’ve gathered together with our community to raise funds and awareness for charities whose mission is to support and grant hope to children suffering from life-threatening or terminal illness.


The Plane Pull began as a celebration of Tyler Frenzel, a 9-year-old boy from Carmel, IN, who passed away from leukemia in 2004. Many of the organizations we champion today supported Tyler during his treatment. What started as[a grassroots giving movement founded by our Associates](https://careers.rjet.com/blog/careers/turning-grassroots-passion-into-corporate-giving/) has grown into an annual commemoration of what we can accomplish together.

## 2026 Plane Pull by the Numbers


“Thank you to everybody for coming out and making this the best plane pull yet.” *— Republic Airways President and Chief Commercial Officer Matt Koscal*


This year’s Plane Pull saw 52 teams participate, and combined with the 345 guests at the Plane Pull Gala at the Lucas Oil Estate, raised over $850,000 for A Kid Again, Indiana Wish, Peyton Manning Children’s Hospital, Riley Children’s Foundation and several other children-focused charities.


This figure brings the 14-year total to nearly $7 million. An extraordinary number that is only possible thanks to our Associates, beneficiaries, partners, community members and especially our sponsors.
