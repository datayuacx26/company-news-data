# What Makes Republic Airways a Top Workplace?


Putting our people first has always been our mission at Republic Airways. Because of that, we’ve been named as one of Central Indiana’s Top Workplaces by IndyStar. We want to thank all our Associates for being recognized as one of Central Indiana’s Top Workplaces.


“The success we’ve had all comes down to our people,” says President and CEO Matt Koscal. “The culture they create and the passion they bring is what pushes us forward every single day. We know that when our people are growing, our business is growing.”


Here are some of the ways Republic Airways ensures our Associates are always at the heart of everything we do.


## Our Culture


At Republic Airways, we know that we win together. “Success at Republic Airways isn’t a zero-sum game,” said[Republic Airways Instructor and Captain Jimmy Deneef](https://rjet.com/blog/how-a-world-class-culture-creates-a-world-class-training-environment/) . “One person succeeding doesn’t mean someone else isn’t.”


Across all departments, Republic Airways Associates are confident they have the support they need to succeed. “That’s the culture that’s always been here,” explained[Barry Confer, a Republic Airways Pilot for 41 years](https://rjet.com/blog/41-years-at-republic-airways/) . “If you show up, put in the effort and do a good job, Republic is going to have your back. They’ll give you all the tools and support you need; they just expect you to make the most of them.”


## Growth Opportunities


When our people grow, our company grows. Through dedicated services like our[Mentorship Program](https://rjet.com/blog/career-growth-through-mentorship/) or just through the assistance of willing colleagues, Republic Airways Associates have a clear career path forward and the backing they need to follow it.


“It’s a tree with many branches,” said[Republic Airways Manager of Maintenance Aaron Spear](https://rjet.com/blog/a-25-year-career-at-republic-airways/) . “It seems like there are always opportunities if you’re willing to reach out and grab them.”


Learn more about the[award-winning culture and opportunities](https://rjet.com/about/) at Republic Airways.
