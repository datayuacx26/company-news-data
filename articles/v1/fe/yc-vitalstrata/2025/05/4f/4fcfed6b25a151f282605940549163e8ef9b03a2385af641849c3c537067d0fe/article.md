# Announcing VitalStrata's $1.2M pre-seed round

## **Why We Founded VitalStrata**


Risk adjustment for Medicare Advantage is at an inflection point. MAOs face rising clinical complexity, larger volumes of unstructured data, and increasing regulatory scrutiny — all while operating under tight operational timelines. Traditional manual coding workflows and generic NLP tools are insufficient in capturing the full picture of an MAO's member population health with high-precision.


We founded VitalStrata to supercharge MAOs with their retrospective and prospective risk adjustment operations. Our belief is simple: MAOs need a platform capable of understanding longitudinal clinical context, detecting subtle prospective signals, and generating audit-defensible evidence — all with the accuracy, scalability, and transparency modern operations demand.


## **Announcing Our Pre-Seed Round**


We’re proud to share that VitalStrata has raised **$1.2 million in pre-seed funding** from an exceptional group of investors, including **Y Combinator** , **Kleiner Perkins** , and **Global Founders Capital** , among others.


This investment strengthens our ability to build deeply technical infrastructure for MA risk adjustment — and to accelerate the development of a platform purpose-built for the growing demands of MAOs.


## **What We’re Building**


At VitalStrata, we are building RAF Precision AI, an end-to-end AI platform engineered for both retrospective and prospective risk adjustment. Our approach is rooted in three core technical pillars:


### **1. Longitudinal Chart Ingestion**


RAF Precision AI processes multi-year clinical documentation, enabling richer context and more reliable coding decisions compared to episodic or single-document approaches.


### **2. Complex Prospective Signal Detection**


Built on top of the longitudinal chart ingestion capabilities, RAF Precision AI identifies subtle, multi-factor clinical signals that can inform care interventions and capture member risk earlier — going beyond simple suspecting heuristics.


### **3. Best-in-Class Coding Accuracy with Audit-Ready Evidence**


Every identified code is paired with MEAT-based, audit-defensible evidence generation, designed to support compliance and withstand increasing regulatory scrutiny.


Together, these capabilities form a unified risk adjustment engine designed to elevate accuracy, operational efficiency, and financial performance for MAOs.


## **What’s Ahead**


With this funding, we are expanding our engineering and clinical foundations, deepening partnerships within the MA ecosystem, and preparing to bring our platform to more organizations seeking higher accuracy, efficiency, and compliance in their risk adjustment operations.


We’re also growing the team. For those who want to help define the future of MA risk adjustment, we invite you to explore open roles on our Careers page.
