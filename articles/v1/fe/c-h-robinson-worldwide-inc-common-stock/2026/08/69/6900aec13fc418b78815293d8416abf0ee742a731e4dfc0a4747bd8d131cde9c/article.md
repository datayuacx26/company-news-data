Read by more than 14,000 logistics leaders each month, the C.H. Robinson Edge Report brings together market data, expert analysis, and actionable guidance to help shippers stay ahead of volatility and plan with confidence.


[This month’s edition](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/) examines how tightening capacity, rising transportation costs, and evolving trade and regulatory policies are influencing freight markets around the world. From the new Section 301 tariff framework and Middle East volatility affecting global supply chains to the increased scrutiny of carrier vetting practices, the report highlights key trends shippers should monitor and strategies to help navigate them.


## What insights are inside the August 2026 Edge Report?


- **Freight market:** Get updated insights across[truckload](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/na-truckload/) , less-than-truckload (LTL), intermodal, ocean shipping,[air freight](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/air/) , and ports and drayage. Learn how tightening truckload capacity and rising[LTL](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/na-ltl/) costs are creating new planning challenges for shippers, and explore strategies to help manage transportation spend. See where softer[ocean](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/ocean/) demand is creating more flexibility and where constraints remain, and how air freight pressure is becoming more localized across specific origins, commodities, and time-sensitive cargo flows.
- **Canada, Mexico, and cross-border:** See how stricter B-1 visa enforcement, English-language requirements, and increased scrutiny of freight documentation are tightening[cross-border](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/cross-border/) capacity in Mexico. Find out how the cross-border market is evolving, how the Gordie Howe International Bridge could improve the resiliency of U.S.-Canada freight flows, and why new tariffs on Canadian goods may soon have an impact.
- **Trade policy and government regulations:** Explore the new Section 301 tariff framework and what continued uncertainty around U.S.[trade policy](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/customs/) and USMCA negotiations could mean in the months ahead. Understand how the Supreme Court’s broker liability decision and[evolving requirements](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/government-regulations/) for carrier safety, driver qualifications, and transportation infrastructure are reshaping the freight landscape.
- **Industry insights:** Get sector-specific perspectives for[retail](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/industry-insights/retail/) , automotive,[energy](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/industry-insights/energy/) , and healthcare supply chains. Learn what the latest Consumer Price Index data means for retail supply chains, how connected-vehicle code restrictions are affecting automotive sourcing, what the nuclear energy comeback could mean for energy logistics, and how the growing rural hospital crisis is creating new challenges for healthcare supply chains.
- **Diesel fuel:** Learn how shifting global fuel markets are creating different levels of exposure across Europe, Asia, and North America, and what renewed[diesel volatility](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-us/resources/insights-and-advisories/north-america-freight-insights/aug-2026-freight-market-update/diesel/) could mean for freight costs and planning.
