# How To Avoid Being at the Mercy of the IVF Postcode Lottery in England 🇬🇧

Let’s imagine that your chances of conception depended on a coin flip. That would definitely cause most people to panic.


Unfortunately, couples in England who are trying to get pregnant face a similar problem in the form of the


IVF postcode lottery


.


According to the


[United Kingdom’s National Health Service (NHS)](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/ivf/availability/) , people who ovulate are eligible for three cycles of IVF treatment under the nation’s healthcare system if they meet certain criteria.


However, not every part of the country follows this guideline, hence the phenomenon “


IVF postcode lottery.


”


That’s because each area in England has its own NHS integrated care board (ICB), which decides who can receive NHS-funded IVF treatment. This includes the number of treatment cycles and whether these cycles are full or partial.


Read on to learn more about the


IVF postcode lottery


situation in England, and more importantly, how you can take charge of your fertility journey to overcome it!

## **Understanding the** **IVF Postcode Lottery** **📖**


The eligibility guidelines for NHS-funded IVF treatment in every U.K. constituent country are clear-cut and published on the NHS’ official website. Naturally, this includes England.


Previously, the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) crafted


[the following recommendations](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg156/chapter/Recommendations#access-criteria-for-ivf) :


### **People Under 40 Years Old**


If you’re below 40 years old at the time of application, you should be offered three NHS-funded IVF treatment cycles if:


- You have been trying to get pregnant for a minimum of two years via regular unprotected sex.


**OR**


- You have not conceived after 12 artificial insemination cycles, with at least half of them using intrauterine insemination (IUI).


If you reach 40 years old while undergoing IVF, you can complete your current cycle. But do note that additional cycles will not be offered.


### **People Who’re 40-42 Years Old**


You should be granted one NHS-funded IVF treatment cycle if you meet all four criteria below:


1. You’ve tried to get pregnant through regular unprotected sex for two years, and you’ve not conceived after 12 artificial insemination cycles, with at least six of them using IUI.


2. You’ve never undergone IVF treatment.


3. Your ovarian reserve is not low.


4. You’ve been clearly told the additional implications of IVF treatment and pregnancy at your age.


Although these recommendations leave almost no room for interpretation, the fact that each area in England is governed by its own NHS ICB complicates matters greatly.
