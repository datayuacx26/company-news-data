## Perinatal Mental Health: Manage Your Well-Being During and After Pregnancy


The anxiety, worry, and stress surrounding pregnancy can get overwhelming at times, resulting in perinatal depression. This includes prenatal depression (which begins during pregnancy) and postpartum depression (which begins after pregnancy).
