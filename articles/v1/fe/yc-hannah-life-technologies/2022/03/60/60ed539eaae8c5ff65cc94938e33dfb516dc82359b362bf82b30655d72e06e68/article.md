## **All about the twoplus Applicator**

### **Using the Applicator**


Using the Applicator is straightforward and only requires 3 simple steps:


- **Step 1:** Have your partner or donor ejaculate into the collection cup.


- **Step 2:** Dip the Applicator into the collection cup and tilt it at an angle. Then, pull the plunger towards you to transfer the semen from the collection cup into the Applicator.


- **Step 3:** Lie on your back or get into a sit-up position. Next, insert the loaded Applicator into the vagina (at least 3/4 of it should be inserted, but stop whenever strong resistance or discomfort is felt) and push the plunger to release the semen.
