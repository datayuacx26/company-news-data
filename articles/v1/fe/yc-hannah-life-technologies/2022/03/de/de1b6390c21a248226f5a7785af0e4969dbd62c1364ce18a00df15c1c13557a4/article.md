## **What Is Ovulation?** **Plus, Its Links to Your Fertile Window & Menstrual Cycle**


Before we get into the nitty-gritty, let’s get the basics right 📚


### **Ovulation 101: Sex-Ed (Adulting Version) 👄**


Ovulation happens when hormonal changes within your body signal the release of a mature egg from the ovaries. The egg then travels down the fallopian tube so that it can be


[fertilized by the sperm](https://blog.twoplusfertility.com/blogs/fertility/how-long-can-sperm-live) .


This monthly phenomenon typically occurs around the middle of your menstrual cycle, roughly 14 days before your next period begins.


Understanding how ovulation works and when it happens is thus essential knowledge for anyone trying to conceive (TTC). It’s basically the prime time for making a baby.


By tracking ovulation signs and timing intercourse accordingly, couples like you and your partner can increase their chances of achieving pregnancy.

### **The Relationship Between Ovulation and the Menstrual Cycle 🔗**


Ovulation isn’t separate from the menstrual cycle, but a part of it — the midpoint of it, to be specific. If your cycle is 28 days long, your ovulation day is around days 14-15.


Contrary to popular misconception, most women-identified folks do not have the “perfect” 28-day cycle — we know, right?! 🤯 In fact, science shows that a normal menstrual cycle actually lasts anywhere


[between 26 and 35 days](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20869180/) .


If your cycle is fewer than 26 days, it’s considered short, with ovulation happening earlier. When the menstrual cycle lasts more than 35 days, it’s seen as a long cycle, with ovulation happening later. This obviously affects the timing of your fertile window (more on that later).

## **When Is Ovulation** **and Your Fertile Window? 🤔**


You’ve probably encountered phrases like “fertile window” and “fertile period” when Googling how to get pregnant faster. And your gut instinct is correct in telling you these are key terms to add to your TTC vocabulary.


### **The Fertile Window — Is It the Same As the Fertile Period?**


The fertile window is a scientific term for the short window of time when you’re at peak fertility in each menstrual cycle. It consists of ovulation day + the five days leading up to it. In human-speak, some of us refer to it as “the fertile period” 💅


### **How Long Does Ovulation Last?** **You’d Be Surprised at the Answer**


Some of us may mistake our ovulation period for our fertile period when that’s not the case.


As mentioned earlier, your fertile period (or fertile window) is much longer, lasting 5-6 days. It’s also inclusive of your ovulation day. Meanwhile, ovulation is only a one-day event that typically lasts 12-24 hours.


No matter if you’re scheduling sex according to your ovulation day or fertile period, time is of the essence if you want to take full advantage of your peak fertility and increase your chances of getting pregnant 😉

### **How Many Days After Ovulation Can You Get Pregnant?**


If you want to know how many days after ovulation you are still fertile, the answer is a mere one day or so.


That’s because ovulation only takes place for 12-24 hours. Any longer than that, the mature egg would have disintegrated in the fallopian tube, resulting in no fertilization.


The best time for conception begins five days before ovulation until the day after. While more research is needed, a scientific review highlighted that


[timed intercourse during your fertile window may improve your pregnancy success rates](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25775926/) .


Remember, it’s a numbers game when it comes to conception!
