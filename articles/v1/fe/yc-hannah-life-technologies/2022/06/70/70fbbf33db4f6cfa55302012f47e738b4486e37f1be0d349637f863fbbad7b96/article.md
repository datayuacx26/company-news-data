## Considering[In-Vitro Fertilisation (IVF)](https://twoplusfertility.com/blogs/fertility/ivf-iui-ici-ivi) options but not sure where to start? Here’s your essential guide to IVF and the IVF clinics in Singapore.


If you’ve arrived at this article, chances are, the baby-making journey has been all but an easy ride. Indeed, successfully conceiving can be a process that takes not just weeks, but months or even years. For some, it requires medical procedures in order to successfully conceive, and one such method couples can opt for is IVF.


If you’re considering IVF to help you and your partner conceive, here’s a handy guide to IVF and finding an IVF clinic in Singapore.


## **What is In-Vitro Fertilisation (IVF)?**


In-vitro quite literally means, outside the living body. Hence, unlike natural conception, IVF refers to the fertilisation of the egg with the sperm, outside of the human body. More specifically, IVF is an[Assisted Reproductive Technique (ART)](https://twoplusfertility.com/blogs/fertility/fertility-acronyms-abbreviations) that consists of a series of procedures, in order to help a couple conceive, or to prevent genetic problems \[1\].


It is at times seen as a last resort for couples trying to conceive, having exhausted all other means such as adjusting their lifestyle, using conception tools,[Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)](https://twoplusfertility.com/blogs/fertility/iui-vs-icsi) and more.


For couples considering IVF, here are the procedures that make up IVF, in a nutshell \[1, 2\]:


- Egg stimulation via hormone injections


- Assessment of eggs via ultrasound and blood tests


- Retrieval of mature eggs


- Sperm retrieval, typically through masturbation


- Fertilisation of egg by sperm in a lab


- Freezing of embryos


- Transfer of embryos into womb


While a full IVF cycle takes around 3 weeks, it could be prolonged as these procedures are split into parts, and this also does not factor in the screening and consultation timeline \[1\].


## **What do IVF clinics offer?**


Unlike signing up for an exercise class and simply showing up, there are many layers to successfully enrolling into an IVF program.


Firstly, there will be an assessment ordered by the IVF clinic to check on your fertility and potential causes of infertility. This will help the doctor to make a better recommendation on the most suitable form of treatment, as well as ensure that you meet the IVF criteria. It is possible that after a fertility check, you could be recommended other conception methods besides IVF \[2\].


Besides executing the IVF procedure shared above, IVF clinics also offer other additional services including:


- Fertility evaluation


- Other reproductive services such as Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)


- Semen analysis


- Egg freezing


- Egg donation


- Support services such as counselling

## **Costs of IVF**


IVF treatment does not come cheap. However, that also doesn’t mean that it is only reserved for the wealthy ones among us.


The average cost of an IVF cycle comes up to around S$13,000 to S$15,000 in the private sector, and this typically includes the following fees \[2\].


- Fees charged by IVF laboratories (S$8,000 to S$10,000)


- Medication fees (S$2,000 to S$5,000)


However, do note that there is no one-size-fits-all, and the costs could differ from couple to couple, depending on the individual needs. To add, costs would also typically be subjected to prevailing government taxes.


It is also important to keep in mind that IVF is not a sure-fire way of getting pregnant. Success rates depend on factors such as age, weight, cause of infertility and more \[3\]. Hence, not every IVF cycle will be a success and it could require multiple IVF cycles for a couple to successfully conceive.

**Government subsidies and MediSave to tap on**


Are there any government subsidies for couples trying to conceive via IVF? Yes, since 1 January 2020, couples can get up to 75% of the costs co-funded by the Ministry of Health (MOH) for Assisted Conception Procedures (ACPs) in public Assisted Reproduction (AR) centres \[4\].


For IVF couples, this co-funding will cover their ART treatment, up to a total of 3 fresh and 3 frozen ART cycles. 2 of the key requirements for you to receive this co-funding include having to be below 40 years of age at the start of the ART cycle (with the exception of the last 2 cycles), and being evaluated by a doctor to meet the requirements necessary for ART \[4\].


For full details on this co-funding, refer to


[this page](https://www.moh.gov.sg/cost-financing/healthcare-schemes-subsidies/marriage-and-parenthood-schemes#:~:text=Eligible%20couples%20undergoing%20Assisted%20Conception,effect%20from%201%20Jan%202020) .


### **Can one use CPF or MediSave to help offset the costs?**


Yes, you can opt to use MediSave to pay for ACPs such as IVF. However, there is a limit to the amount you can use from your Medisave for these procedures.


Medisave limits for ACPs are capped at S$15,000 per patient, and broken down as such \[5\]:


- First cycle: S$6,000


- Second cycle: S$5,000


- Subsequent cycles: S$4,000
