# USPS Overnight Shipping Cost and 2026 Priority Mail Express Rates

USPS overnight shipping, sold as Priority Mail Express, starts at $35.90 at retail and $31.11 with commercial pricing as of July 12, 2026. That is the Flat Rate Envelope. Weight-and-zone parcels start around the same point for a half-pound in Zone 1 and climb from there based on how heavy the package is and how far it travels.


The service commonly called “overnight” now delivers in one to three days by 6 p.m., depending on the origin and destination ZIP Codes. True next-day delivery is only available between eligible ZIPs. So the first decision isn’t whether to use Priority Mail Express. It’s whether you’re actually paying an overnight price for a two-day outcome.


The other decision hides in that $35.90-versus-$31.11 gap. Same service, same box, different price, based entirely on whether you buy at the retail counter or through commercial pricing. Across a few hundred shipments a month, that spread stops being a rounding error.

##


USPS overnight shipping isn’t always overnight


Priority Mail Express delivers one to three days by 6 p.m., 365 days a year, to most U.S. addresses and PO Boxes. Next-day delivery happens only between eligible ZIP Codes. If your origin and destination don’t support it, you’re buying the fastest USPS service, but the package still takes two or three days.


This matters for one common misconception: the “6 p.m. cutoff.” Six p.m. is the delivery deadline on the scheduled delivery day, not a universal deadline for dropping off your package. Mailing acceptance times are set by each local Post Office, and they vary. If you’re timing a shipment to go out today, check your local acceptance time, not a blanket 6 p.m. rule.


Priority Mail Express is also the only USPS domestic service with a money-back guarantee. If it misses the guaranteed delivery window, you can request a refund. That guarantee, not raw speed, is the real reason to pay for it.


##


What changed in 2026, and why your rate went up


If your overnight costs climbed this year, it wasn’t one increase. It was three separate moves, and only two of them were price hikes.


- **January 18, 2026:** Priority Mail Express prices rose 5.1% on average as part of USPS’s regular competitive price change.
- **April 26, 2026:** USPS added a temporary 8% transportation-related increase on base postage for Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail, Ground Advantage, and Parcel Select. It’s time-limited and set to expire January 17, 2027.
- **July 12, 2026:** No new base-price increase. Instead, USPS changed how it calculates dimensional weight and added new fees.


That last date is the one catching shippers off guard, because it doesn’t show up as a higher sticker price. It shows up as a heavier billable weight.


##


The DIM weight change that hits more shippers than the fees


Effective July 12, 2026, USPS lowered its dimensional-weight divisor from 166 to 139 and now rounds every package dimension up to the next whole inch. For any package larger than one cubic foot (1,728 cubic inches), USPS charges based on actual weight or dimensional weight, whichever is greater. The lower divisor raises that dimensional weight by roughly 19%.


In plain terms: if you ship anything bulky but light, a pillow, a lampshade, an oversized-but-empty-feeling box, your billable weight just went up even though the scale didn’t move. The fix is on your side of the transaction. Right-size the box, cut the empty space, and you cut the dimensional weight you’re paying for.


USPS also added two hazmat fees on July 12. A $7.50 handling fee applies to Priority Mail Express and Priority Mail hazardous-materials shipments. And a $50 noncompliance fee applies per package when undeclared hazmat is found in a commercial shipment that wasn’t properly labeled at the time of label creation. If you ship anything with a battery, an aerosol, or a flammable, declare it correctly. The $50 fee is entirely avoidable and entirely painful.

##


Frequently asked questions


### Is USPS overnight shipping the same as Priority Mail?


No. Both are fast, but only Priority Mail Express carries a money-back delivery guarantee and the one-to-three-day committed window. Priority Mail does not guarantee its transit time.


### What is the cutoff time for USPS overnight shipping?


There’s no single national cutoff. The 6 p.m. figure is the delivery deadline on the scheduled day, not a drop-off deadline. Acceptance times are set by each local Post Office, so check yours.


### How much does it cost to overnight a letter or envelope?


The Priority Mail Express Flat Rate Envelope is $35.90 at retail and $31.11 with commercial pricing as of July 12, 2026. It fits documents, and it’s usually the cheapest overnight option.


### What happens if my Priority Mail Express package is late?


Priority Mail Express includes a money-back guarantee. If it misses the guaranteed window, you can request a refund from USPS.


### Does USPS deliver overnight on weekends?


Priority Mail Express delivers 365 days a year to most addresses. Sunday and holiday delivery is available in many markets for an added $12.50.
