# How Shopify Sellers Get Big Brand Shipping Without the Big Brand Volume

Right now, your shipping probably lives in two or three different places. Labels in one tab, orders in another, billing somewhere you’d rather not look at. And every time you buy a label, there’s a good chance you’re paying retail rates, because no one ever set you up with anything better.


It’s how shipping works for most Shopify sellers. Default carriers and default rates that leave you with limited options and higher shipping costs than you should be paying. Today that changes with EasyPost Nexus.


##


**Introducing EasyPost Nexus**


[Nexus](https://www.easypost.com/nexus/) puts EasyPost’s shipping platform – the same one that supports 100,000+ shippers and moves over 1 billion packages a year – in reach of any Shopify seller.


Through Nexus, you can connect your Shopify store, import orders, and buy and print labels from a single dashboard. No more copying order numbers between tabs. One login. One place to see what’s going out today and what it’s costing you. That’s the whole point.


##


**Order management in one place**


Once your store is connected, Nexus pulls your Shopify orders in automatically, with the shipping address, items, and order details already attached. You’re not spending hours retyping addresses or exporting a spreadsheet to upload somewhere else.


Your orders show up in the Nexus dashboard, you pick a carrier and rate, and you print. It’s that simple. The order will be marked as fulfilled and its status will even sync back to your Shopify’s dashboard as well.


##


**Shipping rates the big brands get, without the big brand volume**


Most Shopify sellers overpay on shipping, due to stiff retail pricing and limited carrier options. Nexus solves that with EasyPost Wallet Carriers.


[EasyPost Wallet Carriers](https://www.easypost.com/wallet/) are leading carriers that EasyPost has pre-negotiated contracts with, allowing you to access the following without volume minimums, contracts, or fees.


- Create your own shipping account credentials without having to talk to the carrier


- Get discounted rates of up to 88% off retail


- Centralize your carrier billing to one platform


- Free to use, up to 3,000 labels a month


Wallet is expanding with innovative and highly sought after carriers like GOFO, UniUni, and Veho. These next-gen carriers are providing emerging sellers with more ways to save, deliver faster, and strengthen their overall shipping strategy, especially during the peak holiday season. Coverage varies by carrier, so they won’t fit every order, but where they do, they’re some of the most cost-effective options in the network.


That’s where Wallet and Nexus work together. Wallet provides one-click access to top-tier carriers and Nexus is the centralized dashboard to manage all your orders.


##


**Carrier diversification made simple, with lower surcharges and faster delivery times**


Through Shopify your carrier options today are whatever they picked for you. There’s no room to shop around or try other carriers that might serve your customers better or cheaper. And you’re not alone.


In our 2026 peak season shipping survey, 30% of shippers ranked costs and surcharges as their top concern ahead of the holidays. 21% of shippers said managing multiple carriers was their second biggest headache. Carrier flexibility and management is a concern impacting all shippers.


To ease these burdens, EasyPost offers Luma AI and an


[expansive carrier network](https://www.easypost.com/carriers/) with over 100+ national, regional, and hyperlocal carriers ready to serve you. Luma AI automatically recommends the best label for your package based on historic data from 1B+ shipments, so you can add your carrier lineup to reduce costs without feeling overwhelmed at rating. If you want to be a savvy shipper and diversify your carrier mix, Nexus has the flexibility to help you get started**.
