# Ship Biologics With Minute-by-Minute Condition Data

For most shipments, “delivered” is the end of the story. The package arrived, the customer is satisfied, and the tracking number goes quiet. For pharmaceuticals or a tray of diagnostic specimens, “delivered” tells you almost nothing. The box can land on time, at the right address, and still end up ruined because its temperature was not maintained.


UPS® Premier was created for healthcare and life sciences operations who need flawless service: pharmaceutical manufacturers, biotech firms, clinical trial organizations, hospitals, and laboratories, who suffer from an estimated[$35 billion in losses a year](https://veratrak.com/resources/pharma-cold-chain-disruption-challenge) due to damage in transit.


If you move sensitive packages that need consistent sensor-based monitoring, then UPS® Premier is for you.


##


UPS® Premier is now available through EasyPost


UPS® Premier is a contract-only U.S. domestic service level designed to deliver packages on-time and in the right condition. It pairs priority handling across the UPS network with condition monitoring that does not stop at location, and it is built for high-stakes, highly regulated, and sensitive materials. UPS® Premier ensures your parcel arrives at its destination in the right condition by providing:


- **White-glove priority handling** throughout the UPS network, allowing these packages to move ahead of the general package flow.
- **Minute-by-minute, sensor-based visibility** into location and environmental conditions, including temperature, light, and humidity.
- **Proactive intervention and recovery** for shipments that show signs of distress, while there is still time to act on them.
- **Detailed data logs** for regulatory compliance and audit, so the chain of custody is documented rather than reconstructed after the fact.


UPS® Premier supports packages up to a maximum weight of 150 lbs and a maximum length of 108 inches. You can select from three different service tiers based on the level of visibility desired.


- **Silver (01):** Basic monitoring with standard handling instructions.
- **Gold (02):** Enhanced monitoring with expanded handling options.
- **Platinum (03):** Premium monitoring with full handling instruction support.


##


Better shipping without adding a separate platform


Because UPS Premier is part of EasyPost’s UPS integration, you select it the same way you select any other service level, through the same API and the same multi-carrier workflow you already run. There is no additional platform to learn and integrate.


To get started, contact your UPS Account Execute to obtain your contract and UPS 6-digit account number*. You can then use those credentials to activate UPS® Premier through your EasyPost account and access rates.


*Once the contract is secured, customers should prepare for the specific hardware requirements—such as RFID-capable printers and specific sensor technology—associated with their chosen service level.
