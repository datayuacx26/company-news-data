# Bulk Create


You can now generate hundreds of video variations at once with CSV import. Define your text and image overrides in a spreadsheet, drop it onto the canvas, and Jitter will create a scene for every row based on your original artboard.


1. Right-click your scene and choose *Copy as* → *Download as CSV* to get a pre-filled template with your layer names as columns.
2. Fill in a row per variation. Text layers take the replacement copy directly, and image layers take a filename (images must sit in the same folder as the CSV).
3. Back in Jitter, make sure the scene you'd like to create variations for is selected.
4. Drag the CSV and images onto the canvas to generate the new scenes. If your file has any blank cells, Jitter will keep the original content for respective layers.


Bulk Create is perfect for producing big ad campaigns, localized versions of your content, or any other type of animations at scale in a fraction of the time.


## Other improvements


- Stock media in AI chat: Jitter AI can now search and pull in stock images and videos (via Pixabay and Pexels) right from the design chat. Simply describe the media you're looking for, and the agent will find it for you and add it to your scene.
