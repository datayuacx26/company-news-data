# Jitter AI: Superagents

You can now ask Jitter to create anything with Superagents. Great for remixing templates, animating complex scenes from scratch, or turning one asset into a full campaign with a variety of sizes, formats, and localized versions.


Here's how to use agents:


1. Select a scene on the canvas and click the ✨ icon in the top right corner.
2. Pick a shortcut (like *Resize* , *Translate* , *Rename layers* , or *Clean up timeline* ) for quick actions.
3. Or open AI chat and describe anything you want: from small design tweaks to complex animations.


Everything you create with agents stays fully editable, so you can refine and iterate until it looks exactly the way you imagined.


When your animation is ready, ask Jitter for more sizes, formats, languages, or copy variations, and batch export everything in one click.
