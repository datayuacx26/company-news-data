# Custom text effects


Jitter now supports custom text effects! Build fully custom text animations and reuse them across files to keep your motion consistent and on-brand.


Here’s how to get started with custom text effects:


1. Select a text layer on the canvas.
2. In the right-hand sidebar, make sure you're in the *Animate* tab.
3. Depending on the kind of animation you're building, go to the *In* or *Out* tab and select *Custom* .
4. Create your custom effect by defining its movement, rotation, scale, opacity, acceleration, duration, and more.
