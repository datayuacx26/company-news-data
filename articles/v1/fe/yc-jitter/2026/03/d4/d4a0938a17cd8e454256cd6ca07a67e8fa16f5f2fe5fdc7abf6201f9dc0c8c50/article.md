# Prompting: Image to video


Prompting is now part of Image to Video in Jitter! You can now tweak the auto-generated prompt or replace it completely to get exactly the video you want.


Here's how you can write your own Image to Video prompt:


1. Open your Jitter file and select the image you'd like to generate a video from.
2. In the sidebar on the right, go to the *Animate* tab.
3. Click *View and edit prompt* .
4. Edit the generated prompt (or write your own) and click *Generate video* .
