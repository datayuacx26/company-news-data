# Multi-layer hide and lock


Managing Jitter files with many layers just got a little smoother. You can now quickly hide (or show) and lock (or unlock) multiple layers in one sweep.


Here's how it works:


1. In the layer list (the left-hand panel in your Jitter file), click and hold the hide or lock icon next to any layer.
2. Drag down across other layers.
3. Release to hide/show or lock/unlock all selected layers in one go.
