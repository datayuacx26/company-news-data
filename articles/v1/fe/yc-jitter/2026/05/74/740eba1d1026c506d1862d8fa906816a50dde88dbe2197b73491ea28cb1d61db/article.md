# Batch export


If you're working with multiple artboards (design variations, different sizes, or several languages), exporting just became a lot faster. You can now select the artboards you need and export them all at once in a click.


Here's how to use batch export:


1. In your Jitter file, select the artboards you'd like to export.
2. Click the *Export* button in the top right corner.
3. Choose your export format.
4. You'll land on the export page with all of your selected files exported. From here, you can further adjust the export settings, choose to download some of the scenes individually, or simply hit the *Download* button to save them all to your device.


Batch export is available on the Jitter Max plan.
