# Components · Jitter

You can now create and reuse components across your file! Perfect for keeping logos, CTAs, and other recurring elements consistent across format and design variations, or composing complex multi-scene montages where every scene stays easy to edit.


Here's how to use components:


1. Select any element (or scene) on the canvas, right-click it, and hit *Create component* .
2. Copy your component and paste it into any scene in your file.
3. That's it! Whenever you edit the component, all instances will update automatically.


This is the first version of components, and for now they're file-level. Next up: components you can reuse across your entire workspace.


## Other improvements


- Staggering: You can now apply staggered timing across layers directly from the timeline by selecting several operations and right-clicking them.
- Drag to adjust inspector values: You can now click and drag on any number field in the right-hand sidebar to adjust its value, instead of typing or using arrows.
- Live font preview: Hover over any font in the font picker to preview it live on the canvas.
