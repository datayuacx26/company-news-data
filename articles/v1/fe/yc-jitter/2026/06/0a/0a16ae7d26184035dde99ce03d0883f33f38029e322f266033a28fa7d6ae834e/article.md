# Counters


You can now animate numbers as counters in Jitter: great for countdowns, growing stats, and any moment where numbers need to stand out. Here's how to use counters:


1. Add a text layer with a number on the canvas — that number will be the initial state of your counter.
2. In the *Animate* tab, click *Custom* and find *Counter* in the *Text* section.
3. Set the *From* (where the counter will start), *To* (what it will count up or down to), and *Step* (the interval it will counts at) values, choose the effect for the transition, and adjust the duration of your animation if needed.
4. That's it — you've just created a counter!


Counters handle just about any number format: integers, decimals, negatives, percentages, and values with units (like $42 or 1,234.50kg).
