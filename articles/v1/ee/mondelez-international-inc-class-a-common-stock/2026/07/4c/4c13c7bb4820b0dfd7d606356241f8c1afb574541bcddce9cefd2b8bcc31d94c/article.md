## Format of the Annual Meeting of Shareholders


The Board of Directors has determined we will hold a virtual Annual Meeting of Shareholders conducted via webcast. We have designed the format of the Annual Meeting so that shareholders have the same rights and opportunities as they would have at a physical meeting. Shareholders will be able to submit questions during the meeting using online tools, providing our shareholders with the opportunity for meaningful engagement with the Company.


## Electronic Delivery of Materials


Mondelēz International[offers our shareholders electronic delivery of proxy materials](https://enroll.icsdelivery.com/MDLZ/2387) , including the[Annual Report on Form 10-K](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/assets/PDFs/MONDELEZ-INTERNATIONAL-INC_10K_2025.pdf) ,[proxy statement](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/assets/Investors/ASM/2026/MDLZ_Mondelez-International_2026-Proxy-Statement.pdf) ,[proxy card](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/assets/Investors/ASM/2026/MDLZ_Mondelez-International_2026-Proxy-Card.pdf) and[related materials](https://www.mondelezinternational.com/assets/Investors/ASM/2026/Notice-of-Internet-availability.pdf) . Electronic delivery provides a variety of benefits to the company, our shareholders and the environment.


By choosing electronic delivery, you can help us conserve natural resources and reduce printing and mailing costs.


Shareholders who hold Mondelēz International stock directly with our stock registrar and transfer agent, EQ Shareowner Services, are Registered Shareholders.


Shareholders who hold our stock indirectly through an account with an institutional or other nominee holder of our stock, such as a broker or bank, are referred to as Beneficial Shareholders or shareholders “in street name.”
