### Horace Mann Launches Exclusive Program that Offers Fitness, Financial Wellness, and Exclusive Discounts to Support Educators Nationwide


February 17, 2026


SPRINGFIELD, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Horace Mann has launched the Horace Mann Club™ that offers a robust set of perks designed to help educators save money, reduce stress, and prioritize their well-being.


Through the Horace Mann Club, educators can access a wide range of exclusive offerings, including fitness and wellness partnerships, financial tools, classroom resources, professional development programs, and more. This new program expands Horace Mann’s support of educators as a way to give back to those who give so much to their students and their communities.


“For more than 80 years, Horace Mann has supported educators with resources they need to thrive and succeed,” said Steve Chauby, Chief Marketing and Distribution Officer for Horace Mann. “The Horace Mann Club is our way of thanking and continuing to support educators with benefits, discounts, and resources designed specifically for them.”


Educators who register can explore unique offerings including:


- Professional growth opportunities, such as webinars and professional development credit courses
- Preferred gym memberships and wellness programs through national and regional fitness partners
- Identity and credit protection tools
- Financial wellness resources, including student loan guidance and budgeting tools
- Classroom support resources, such as lesson plan templates and grant opportunities
- Plus additional benefits as new partners are added to the Horace Mann Club


The program is available at no cost to educators and school employees. Registration only takes a few minutes and unlocks immediate access to nationwide benefits.


Educators can learn more and register by visiting[https://club.horacemann.com.](https://club.horacemann.com/)


** The Horace Mann Club program is provided through Horace Mann Service Corporation (HMSC), a wholly owned subsidiary of Horace Mann Educators Corporation. HMSC contracts with companies not affiliated with Horace Mann to offer their products and services to Horace Mann Club members. HMSC may receive compensation from these companies based on the sale of these products or services.*


**About Horace Mann**


Horace Mann Educators Corporation is the largest multiline financial services company focused on helping America's educators and others who serve the community achieve lifelong financial success. The company offers individual and group insurance and financial solutions tailored to the needs of the educator community. Founded by Educators for Educators® in 1945, the company is headquartered in Springfield, Illinois. For more information, visit[www.horacemann.com](http://www.horacemann.com/) .
