## **Series Matchup Overview**


The New York Knicks enter this series as favorites, though not by as wide a margin as the seeding gap might suggest. New York finished the regular season 53–29, earning the No. 3 seed in the Eastern Conference and securing home-court advantage for this first-round matchup. The Knicks were one of the East's most physical and well-coached teams all season, grinding out wins through defensive toughness, strong rebounding, and a half-court offensive system that punishes undisciplined opponents. Madison Square Garden adds another layer, as there’s no louder or more intimidating home environment in professional basketball.


The Atlanta Hawks, seeded sixth in the East with a 46–36 record, arrive as a dangerous opponent. Atlanta's offense is built around pace and playmaking, and when their shot creation is clicking, they can light up any defense in the league. The Hawks closed the regular season strong and carry confidence into this series. With that said, matching up with New York's physicality on the road in Game 1 is a tall order, and Atlanta will need to weather the early MSG atmosphere before they can impose their style of play.

**New York's physicality up front.** The Knicks impose their will in the paint, and Atlanta will need to match that intensity from the opening tip. If New York controls the boards and limits second-chance opportunities for the Hawks, they can grind this game into the kind of low-possession battle that favors the home team.

If Atlanta can generate early offense in transition and get their guards into rhythm, they have the offensive weapons to put real pressure on New York's defense throughout the night.
