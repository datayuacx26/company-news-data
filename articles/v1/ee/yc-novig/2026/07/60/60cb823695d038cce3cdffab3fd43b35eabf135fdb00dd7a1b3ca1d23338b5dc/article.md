*Written by: Jacob Fortinsky (Founder & CEO)*


I’m so excited to announce that Novig, the first prediction market for sports, has launched nationwide!


Players have been fed up with having to play on apps where the house always wins and the odds are stacked against them. On Novig, 43% of users are profitable — compared to an industry average of 3% — and we’re the first app to offer a commission-free sports prediction market. We’re absolutely thrilled to now be able to offer our revolutionary app to sports fans nationwide.


The Novig app has two modes: Novig Coins and Novig Cash. Novig Coins are used to play for fun and test new trading strategies, whereas Novig Cash can be played to redeem real cash prizes.


Novig is available now on iOS with our Android release coming soon. All new users get 1,000 Novig Coins and 5 Novig Cash preloaded to their wallet to start trading. We currently offer moneyline, spread, total markets, and player props in NFL, NBA, WNBA, NCAAF, NCAAB, and MLB.
