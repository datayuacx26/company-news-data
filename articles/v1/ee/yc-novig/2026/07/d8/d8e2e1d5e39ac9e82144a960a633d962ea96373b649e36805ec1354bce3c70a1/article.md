## **Series Matchup Overview**


The Denver Nuggets enter this series as clear favorites, and their regular season record makes the case. Denver finished 54–28, earning the No. 2 seed in the Western Conference and cementing themselves as one of the West's premier title contenders. The Nuggets were a model of consistency throughout the year, anchored by their elite half-court offense and one of the deepest rosters in the league. Ball-handlers and two-way wings fill out a rotation that gives opposing coaches headaches, and at altitude in Ball Arena, Denver is as difficult a road environment as there is in professional basketball.


The Minnesota Timberwolves, meanwhile, finished 49–33 to secure their playoff spot as a lower seed in the Western bracket. It wasn't always pretty for Minnesota, but their defensive identity and physicality kept them competitive against top competition throughout the season. The Timberwolves enter this series battle-tested and will need to lean on that toughness to slow down a Nuggets offense that can score from anywhere on the floor.
