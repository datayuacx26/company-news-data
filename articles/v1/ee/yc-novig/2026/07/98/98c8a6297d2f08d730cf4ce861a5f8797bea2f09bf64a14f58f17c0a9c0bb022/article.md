## **Series Matchup Overview**


The Houston Rockets enter this series as favorites and have earned that distinction through one of the more impressive regular seasons in the Western Conference. Houston finished 52–30, securing their playoff spot as a competitive mid-tier seed in a loaded West. The Rockets are built around youth, athleticism, and defensive intensity, which is a combination that made them one of the more difficult teams to score on all season. Toyota Center provides a genuine home-court advantage, and Houston's crowd and energy figures to be a factor from the opening tip.

The Lakers closed the regular season with purpose and will be looking to impose their will early in what shapes up to be one of the more intriguing first-round matchups in the West.
