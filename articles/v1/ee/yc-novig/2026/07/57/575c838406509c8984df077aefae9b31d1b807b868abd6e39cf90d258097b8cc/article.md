## **Series Matchup Overview** ‍


The San Antonio Spurs enter this series as overwhelming favorites, and the numbers make it difficult to argue otherwise. San Antonio finished the regular season 62–20, earning the No. 2 seed in the Western Conference and establishing themselves as one of the most dominant teams in the league this year. The Spurs were a relentless force all season long, combining elite offensive execution with a defensive scheme that gave opponents fits night after night. Frost Bank Center has been one of the loudest and most energized home environments in professional basketball this season, and the Spurs will be feeding off that crowd from the opening tip.


The Portland Trail Blazers, meanwhile, finished 42–40 to secure their playoff spot as a lower seed in a competitive Western bracket. Portland's path to the postseason was hard-fought, and the Trail Blazers arrive with plenty of motivation as significant underdogs. Making the playoffs in the loaded Western Conference is no small feat, and Portland will look to use that chip on their shoulder to compete in what most expect to be a lopsided series on paper.
