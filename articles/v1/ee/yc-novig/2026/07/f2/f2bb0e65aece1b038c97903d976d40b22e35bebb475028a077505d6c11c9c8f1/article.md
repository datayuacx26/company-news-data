# Florida Sports Betting: Latest News & Updates (2025)

TL;DR: Florida does have legal online and retail sports betting as of October 2025, but it’s exclusive to Hard Rock Bet via the Seminole Tribe compact. **Is sports betting legal in Florida?**


Yes. Hard Rock Bet relaunched statewide mobile betting on Dec 5, 2023, and it remains the only legal sportsbook in Florida in 2025. Retail sportsbooks are also open at Seminole casinos.
