## **How Sports Betting Became Legal in Massachusetts**


### **Years of Debate After PASPA (2018-2021)**


When the federal sports betting ban was struck down in 2018, many states moved quickly. Massachusetts took a slower route.


Between 2018 and 2021, multiple sports betting bills were introduced, but none actually passed. The debate focused less on whether to legalize sports betting itself and more on how to structure the system. Lawmakers couldn’t agree over:


- How many online licenses should be issued
- Whether professional sports teams should have direct access to licenses
- How tax revenue should be distributed
- Whether to allow betting on college sports


Those disagreements delayed progress while many neighboring states moved forward.


### **The Breakthrough: The 2022 Sports Wagering Act**


In August 2022, the legislature passed a sports wagering bill authorizing both retail and mobile betting. Charlie Baker, the governor at the time, signed the bill into law, which officially legalized sports betting statewide.


Unlike some states, Massachusetts did not require a voter referendum. The law moved forward through the standard legislative process.


### **Regulatory Rollout and Market Launch**


After the bill passed, the Massachusetts Gaming Commission led implementation. Through late 2022 and early 2023, the commission:


- Drafted sports betting regulations
- Opened licensing applications
- Reviewed operators for approval
- Established advertising and responsible gambling rules
- Built compliance oversight systems


Retail sportsbooks launched in January 2023 and mobile sportsbooks followed quickly after in March 2023. This structure has remained steady through 2026.
