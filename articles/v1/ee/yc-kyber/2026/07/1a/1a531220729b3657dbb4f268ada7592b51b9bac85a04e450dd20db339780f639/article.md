# Kyber Achieves SOC 2 Type 1 Certification, Strengthening Data Security Commitment

Kyber, a Y Combinator backed AI startup purpose-built for the insurance sector, is proud to announce it has achieved SOC 2 Type 1 certification. This milestone underscores Kyber’s commitment to data security and regulatory integrity, ensuring the highest levels of protection for the sensitive information handled by insurance carriers, MGAs, and TPAs.


Kyber provides an AI-powered platform that automates the drafting, review, and delivery of claims notices and letters. By leveraging advanced AI, Kyber drastically reduces the time claims adjusters spend on manual tasks, allowing them to auto-draft regulatory-compliant notices, collaborate seamlessly in real-time, and ensure consistent, compliant communications. Insurance carriers, MGAs, and TPAs use Kyber to streamline their claims operations, saving time, reducing errors, and improving overall transparency across their teams.


‍


## Significance of Achieving SOC 2 Type 1 Compliance


For an insurance-focused platform like Kyber, achieving SOC 2 Type 1 certification is more than a badge of honor—it’s a critical step in demonstrating our adherence to industry regulations and our commitment to safeguarding sensitive data. In today’s digital landscape, insurance teams must prioritize security, and by achieving compliance, Kyber ensures that our customers can trust us to manage their data with integrity.


Achieving SOC 2 Type 1 certification, overseen by Insight Assurance, required a rigorous and comprehensive auditing process. Through this audit, Kyber demonstrated its compliance with the stringent requirements of SOC 2, affirming our dedication to protecting sensitive information and maintaining operational resilience. This certification assures our partners that Kyber upholds the highest security standards for data handling and process integrity.


‍


## A Platform Built with Data Security at Its Core


Data security is a central tenet of Kyber’s platform. As a solution purpose-built for insurance claims teams, we understand the importance of protecting both policyholder information and business-critical data. Our platform incorporates multiple layers of security, from encrypted data transmissions to robust access controls, ensuring that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive information. Additionally, Kyber provides detailed audit trails for all actions taken on the platform, offering complete transparency into the handling of claims notifications.
