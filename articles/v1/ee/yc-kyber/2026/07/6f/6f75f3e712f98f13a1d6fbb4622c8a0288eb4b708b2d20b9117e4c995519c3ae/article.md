# Snapsheet and Kyber Partner to Transform Insurance Claims Communication with AI-Powered Document Generation

**CHICAGO, IL \[November 8th, 2025\]** – Snapsheet, a leader in cloud-based claims management technology, is excited to announce a new strategic partnership with[Kyber](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ask-kyber?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-mention) , an innovative provider of AI-driven document generation and delivery solutions tailored for the insurance industry. This collaboration aims to streamline and enhance the claims communication process, benefiting insurers and policyholders alike.


Kyber, founded in 2022 and backed by Y-Combinator and leading AI investors, has rapidly gained traction in the insurance sector, serving prominent carriers and MGAs. Their AI-driven platform applies automation to the drafting, review, and distribution of essential claim documents like Reservation of Rights and Declination Letters, significantly reducing time spent on administrative tasks.


Through this partnership, Snapsheet and Kyber have developed an API-based integration that seamlessly connects Kyber’s document generation capabilities with Snapsheet’s claims management platform. This integration enables insurers to automate document-based tasks and workflows, enhancing speed, accuracy, and compliance within their claims processes.


> “By integrating Kyber's advanced document generation with Snapsheet’s claims management platform, we’re creating a seamless experience that will transform communication workflows in the insurance industry,” said[Arvind Sontha](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arvind-sontha?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-mention) , Co-Founder & CEO of[Kyber](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ask-kyber?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-mention) . “This partnership empowers insurance carriers to accelerate their digital transformation journey, setting a new standard for operational excellence.”


## Transforming Claims Communication Efficiency


This partnership addresses a critical pain point in the insurance claims process: the time-consuming task of document drafting and delivery. With Kyber’s platform, insurance adjusters can achieve a 65% reduction in drafting time, allowing them to focus on more complex claims tasks. By automating document workflows, turnaround times have been reduced from hours to minutes, leading to faster response times and improved customer satisfaction.


## Key Benefits of the Snapsheet and Kyber Partnership:


- **Reduced Drafting Time:** A 65% reduction in time spent on document preparation, allowing adjusters to focus on high-priority tasks.
- **Enhanced Compliance:** Automated templates and AI-driven language extraction reduce errors, improving compliance and ensuring consistent, high-quality communications.
- **Accelerated Response Times:** Faster document turnaround, reducing response times from hours to minutes.


> “Snapsheet’s focus has always been on delivering innovative solutions that enhance efficiency and customer experience,” said[Brad Weisberg](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bradweisberg1?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-mention) , CEO of[Snapsheet Inc](https://www.linkedin.com/company/snapsheetinc?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-mention) . “Our partnership with Kyber allows us to offer insurers a modern solution for document management, helping them streamline workflows and meet compliance standards effortlessly.”


## A Vision for Modernizing Insurance Communication


Both Snapsheet and Kyber share a commitment to modernizing the insurance industry through advanced technology. By leveraging AI, this partnership provides a dynamic solution for claims communication that can grow with the needs of insurance carriers.


The integrated solution is already being used by Branch Insurance, a mutual client of Snapsheet and Kyber, with positive results. Moving forward, Snapsheet and Kyber plan to market this solution to other insurers while exploring deeper integration opportunities that enhance functionality within Snapsheet’s claims platform.
