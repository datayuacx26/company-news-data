# YXT Recognized for ESG Innovation Practice Excellence, Highlighting Long-Term Value Creation Through AI

## YXT Recognized for ESG Innovation Practice Excellence, Highlighting Long-Term Value Creation Through AI


July 13th, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.yxt.com/node/7261/pdf)


**YXT Recognized for ESG Innovation Practice Excellence, Highlighting Long-Term Value Creation Through AI**


YXT.com Group Holding Limited, a provider of AI-enabled enterprise productivity solutions, today announced that it was recently recognized as an “ESG Innovation Practice Excellence Enterprise” at the Gelonghui Mid-Year Strategy Summit 2026.


The award recognizes listed companies that use technology and business model innovation to advance ESG practices and translate sustainability principles into commercial value. YXT’s recognition reflects the Company’s continued progress in AI transformation, intelligent productivity, responsible AI, green operations and long-term value creation.


**Key Highlights**


• YXT was recognized as an “ESG Innovation Practice Excellence Enterprise” for its efforts to integrate AI innovation with sustainable business practices.


• Monthly recurring revenue from AI-related products grew nearly fourfold year over year in 2025.


• YXT currently serves more than 2,300 large enterprise customers, with subscription-based enterprise training solutions accounting for 94% of total training business revenue in 2025.


• Direct carbon emissions declined from 97.2 tons in 2022 to 45.8 tons in 2025.


• YXT increased server resource utilization from below 10% to over 40%, while reducing overall cloud resource energy consumption and operating costs by approximately 20%.


• The Company has maintained zero major data security incidents for several consecutive years and has obtained certifications including ISO 27001, ISO 27701 and Level 3 data security maturity certification.


**AI Innovation and Intelligent Productivity**


YXT has continued to advance its intelligent productivity strategy by applying AI across knowledge operations, talent development and sales enablement. Through product capabilities such as intelligent knowledge bases, AI-powered course creation, AI role-play and learning maps, the Company helps enterprises capture, structure and continuously manage knowledge assets across employee experience, business processes and organizational practices.


The Company believes that successful enterprise AI adoption depends not only on model capabilities, but also on whether AI can understand an organization’s knowledge, workflows and business scenarios. YXT’s solutions are designed to help enterprises turn knowledge into capability, experience into assets, and organizational capability into sustainable productivity.


**Responsible AI and Data Security**


YXT has made responsible AI and data security a core part of its ESG practice. The Company has maintained a record of zero major data security incidents for several consecutive years and has obtained certifications including ISO 27001 for information security, ISO 27701 for privacy information management, and Level 3 data security maturity certification.


The Company has also introduced digital watermarking and content traceability technologies to help ensure that AI-generated content remains secure and controllable.


Green Operations and Infrastructure Efficiency


On the environmental side, YXT has continued to improve the efficiency of its operations and AI infrastructure. The Company’s direct carbon emissions declined from 97.2 tons in 2022 to 45.8 tons in 2025.


Through its self-developed multi-model gateway and intelligent scheduling system, YXT increased server resource utilization from below 10% to over 40%, while reducing overall cloud resource energy consumption and operating costs by approximately 20%.


**Business Quality and AI Growth Momentum**


YXT’s ESG progress is also supported by continued improvement in business quality. In 2025, the Company generated total revenue of RMB 340 million, representing year-over-year growth of 2.7%. Revenue from subscription-based enterprise training solutions reached RMB 317 million, up 5.2% year over year, accounting for 94% of total training business revenue.


The net revenue retention rate of subscription customers remained stable at 101.4%, and the Company currently serves more than 2,300 large enterprise customers.


YXT’s AI business is beginning to show clear growth momentum. In 2025, monthly recurring revenue from AI-related products grew nearly fourfold year over year. The Company’s profitability also improved, with gross margin reaching 68.3%, up 6.5 percentage points from 2024. Adjusted net loss narrowed from RMB 200 million in 2024 to RMB 147 million in 2025, representing a year-over-year reduction of 26.4%.


**Technology Investment and Governance**


YXT has continued to invest in core AI technologies. The Company has accumulated multiple patents and more than 100 software copyrights. Its enterprise-grade AI simulation training technology uses a multi-role collaboration mechanism involving an AI executor, analyzer and decision-maker, helping improve stability in complex training scenarios and reduce the risk of large language model hallucinations.


Since establishing its “AI First” strategy in 2022, YXT has continued to integrate AI into its own organization and workflows. In a recent AI coding practice, an eight-person team completed a preview version of a new product in approximately 20 days, generating around 340,000 lines of code and more than 1,600 files, with coding completed by AI. The Company has also established regular AI transformation training to support employees in transitioning toward human-machine collaboration roles.


YXT has built a governance framework to support long-term development. The Company’s board consists of six members, including two independent directors, representing 33.3% of the board. In cybersecurity management, YXT has adopted an active vulnerability management program, including regular vulnerability scanning, penetration testing and security testing throughout the software development lifecycle.


**Long-Term Value Creation**


The ESG Innovation Practice Excellence recognition reflects YXT’s continued efforts to integrate AI innovation, sustainable operations and corporate governance into its long-term growth strategy. As AI continues to enter real enterprise workflows, YXT remains focused on helping organizations improve knowledge operations, employee capabilities and business execution through intelligent productivity solutions.


**About YXT.com**


YXT.com (NASDAQ: YXT) is a technology company focusing on enterprise productivity solutions. With a mission to "Empower people and organization development through technology," the Company strives to become the supreme provider in building and boosting enterprise productivity by combining over a decade of experience in tech-enabled talent learning and development and with AI-augmented task copilots and unleashing the power of knowledge and synergy. Since its inception, YXT.com has supported and received recognition from numerous Global and China Fortune 500 companies.
