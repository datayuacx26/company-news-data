# Your Employees Already Watch Everyone Else in Short-Form Videos. It’s Time They Watch You.

Your employees already watch hundreds of short videos a day. From friends, favourite stars, even politicians. Why not from you?


Ten years ago, the idea that simple, “talking-head” videos would be the primary way we get information would have been laughable. Yet, right now, someone on your team is watching a 30-second TikTok video or Instagram Reel on their phone.


In the last hour, they’ve scrolled past friends, favorite stars, and yes… even politicians. This week, they will spend an[average of 19 hours](https://www.statista.com/statistics/611707/online-video-time-spent/#statisticContainer) consuming content in that exact format.


Meanwhile, that critical, company-wide update you wrote is sitting in their inbox, likely unread. You're trying to lead in paragraphs while your entire organization is thinking in 30-second story arcs.


This isn’t a generational quirk, there’s been a fundamental shift in how information is consumed. Dismissing this as a consumer trend is a massive strategic error. As a leader, you have to ask yourself a tough question: **Why are you not using the format your employees clearly prefer?**


By sticking to the old playbook of text-heavy emails, you are leaving an incredible amount of value on the table. Here’s what you're missing:


### **1. True Engagement, Not Just "Open Rates"**


The battle for attention is fierce, even inside your own company. An email "open" is a vanity metric. True leadership is about impact and comprehension.


Your team can smell - and tune out - a pre-written, PR-approved script from a mile away. They are craving authenticity. Short, dynamic video messages are optimized to capture attention and cut through the noise, delivering your message with a human touch that text simply cannot replicate.


### **2. Authentic Leadership at Scale**


Your team doesn't just want to hear *what* you have to say; they want to see and feel *why* you're saying it.


This is where video becomes a tool for leadership at scale. It’s not about being perfect in fact, being imperfect is the whole point. That’s not a bug; it’s a feature. It’s you, sharing a 30-second video from a site visit. It’s you, celebrating a team win on the fly. It’s you, explaining a tough strategic shift in your own words.


This format forces clarity and encourages a personal style that[humanizes your leadership](https://executive.berkeley.edu/thought-leadership/blog/importance-authenticity) . It proves you're a real person, not a corporate robot, and it’s what builds massive trust. That is the transparency that builds an unbreakable culture.


### **3. Digestible Message with a Lasting Impact**


Think about your last major strategy rollout. Was it a dense PDF? An hour-long, 'click-next' all-hands video that employees muted in the background?


That’s not learning or alignment. That’s a compliance checkbox.


This is where the "TikTok model" isn't a toy; it's a tool. Its true power is in delivering hyper-contextual, digestible information. Because the "teacher" isn't a random actor, it's you. It’s your leadership team. It’s your subject matter experts, sharing knowledge from a trusted, proven source: their own team. This is how you dramatically speed up alignment and build a real learning culture.


### **This Isn't About Dance Trends. It's About Connection.**


Let's address the elephant in the room. When you hear "video for the workplace," you might picture your CFO doing a viral dance challenge. That's not it.


This isn't about replicating consumer trends. It's about adopting the principles that make them so powerful:


- **Brevity:** Respecting your team's time.
- **Authenticity:** Showing the real person behind the title.
- **Consistency:** Creating a steady, visible drumbeat of leadership.
