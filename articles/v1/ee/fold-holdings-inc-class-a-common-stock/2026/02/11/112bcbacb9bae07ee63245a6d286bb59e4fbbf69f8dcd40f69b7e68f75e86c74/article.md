# How spins, rewards, and referrals work now

Spins, rewards, and referrals are some of the favorite ways Fold customers engage with the app, so the new experience is designed to make each of them easier to find and use. Instead of spreading these features across multiple tabs, they now live in predictable places that match how you naturally navigate the app.​


## **Where to find your daily spin**


Your daily spin wheel now lives in the notification center, which you can open by tapping the bell in the upper right of your home screen.​ This change is designed so that spins live where alerts already live, making it easy to see when you have a spin waiting and to act on it right away.​

## **Rewards live under bitcoin**


Rewards now live under your bitcoin holdings. From the home screen, tapping bitcoin takes you to a view where your rewards are clearly displayed along with your current bitcoin balance.​ Here you can see how many sats you have available, a history of previous reward payments, and a simple redemption flow that lets you add those sats to your stack.​


Housing rewards with your bitcoin balance helps you see the connection between everyday actions and long‑term stacking.​ We made a quick video so you can see how redeem your rewards and put them in your bitcoin wallet in just a few taps.

## **Referrals live in settings**


Referrals are now located in the top‑left settings menu. When you open the menu, look for the yellow box that links to your referral program.​ From there, you can copy your referral link or share it through your favorite apps, making it easy to invite friends and track when they join.​
