# Shop smarter: Using the new Shop tab and gift card tools

The new Shop tab brings your merchant gift cards, bonus offers, and bitcoin gift redemptions into a single, organized space.​ Instead of bouncing between separate lists and detail screens, you can now search, filter, buy, and manage gift cards from one hub.​


## **A better way to browse brands**


Gift card search now lets you quickly find your favorite merchants by name so you do not need to scroll through long alphabetical lists.​ You can also filter by redemption method and categories, helping you zero in on the brands and formats that fit how you actually shop.​


This makes it easier to plan purchases around higher rewards rates or limited‑time promos, which can meaningfully increase the sats you earn on everyday spending.​

## **Managing active gift cards**


Once you buy a gift card, it appears in an organized gift card section that shows your active cards and remaining balances.​ Keeping this information in one place reduces the odds of forgetting a card or leaving a small balance unused.​


From this section you can tap into details like barcodes, redemption instructions, and past redemption history where available.​ This structure is designed to make gift cards feel like part of your overall money picture rather than something separate or hard to track.​


## **Smoother bitcoin gift card redemption**


Bitcoin gift card redemption now uses a clearer, step‑by‑step flow that walks you from entering your code to confirming sats added to your account.​Each step is explained on screen, reducing confusion and helping you feel confident that your gift is being converted correctly.​
