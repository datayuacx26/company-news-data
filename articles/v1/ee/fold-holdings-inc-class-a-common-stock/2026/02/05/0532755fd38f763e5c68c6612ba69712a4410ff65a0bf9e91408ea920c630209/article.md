# Inside your new Fold app

Your Fold app just leveled up. The new experience is designed so the first thing you see answers three questions:


- How much money do you have?
- How are your sats are growing?
- What other ways can I earn?


Instead of jumping between screens, the new layout puts your bitcoin, cash, and key actions within a few taps so the app feels more like a control center than a collection of menus.​


## **Your new home screen**


The home screen now puts your cash and bitcoin front and center so you always know where you stand when you open the app.​ From here you can move straight into your bitcoin view, access your debit card activity, or tap into other tools without hunting through hidden sections.​

## **All‑new transaction center**


The new transaction center shows every transaction across all of your money in one place, including card swipes, gift cards, and bitcoin activity together.​ Seeing everything in a single timeline makes it easier to match your spending with the rewards and sats you are earning along the way.​


Filters and detail views help you drill into specific types of activity, like bitcoin buys or gift card redemptions, without losing the big picture of your account.​ This structure supports better tracking for budgeting, tax time, or simply understanding how Fold fits into your everyday cash flow.​


## **Smarter notification center**


The notification center is now your hub for promos, rewards alerts, security updates, and your daily spin. ​Instead of digging through email or scattered messages, you can open the app, tap the bell in the top right, and see what needs your attention.​
