# What Fold actually does for your money

[Fold](https://go.foldapp.com/xtBZ/0v57lres?ref=blog.foldapp.com) is a money app built around one idea: the things you're already doing, spending, saving, paying bills, can also earn bitcoin. Here's the plain version of what it actually does, no jargon required.


**The high level version**


- Fold is a money app. Everything you already do when it comes to spending, or paying bills, earns bitcoin.
- You don't need to understand bitcoin to benefit from it. You just need to spend the way you already do.


**Earn on what you're already spending**


- The[Fold cards](https://go.foldapp.com/xtBZ/ihjcivol?ref=blog.foldapp.com) earn bitcoin on eligible everyday purchases, groceries, gas, bills, and more.
- That's not a bonus tacked onto a card you already had. It's the whole point of our card offerings.
- You can boost your earning rate further by setting up[recurring bitcoin purchases](https://foldapp.com/buy-bitcoin?ref=blog.foldapp.com) or paying your[credit card](https://go.foldapp.com/xtBZ/ihjcivol?ref=blog.foldapp.com) * statement with bitcoin already in your Fold account.


**Buy bitcoin without the usual overhead**


- If you already buy bitcoin somewhere else,[Fold is built to be one of the cheaper places to do it.](http://btcpricetool.com/?ref=blog.foldapp.com)
- Set it up once as a recurring purchase using[Auto-Stack](https://foldapp.com/buy-bitcoin?ref=blog.foldapp.com) and it happens automatically. No need to time the market or check a chart every day.


**Turn gift cards into bitcoin**


- [Buy gift cards](https://go.foldapp.com/xtBZ/z2a91x21?ref=blog.foldapp.com) for stores and restaurants you already use, and those purchases earn bitcoin back too.
- Want to give bitcoin to someone who's curious about it?[Fold's bitcoin gift cards](https://foldapp.com/bitcoin-gift-card?ref=blog.foldapp.com) let you gift bitcoin directly, no wallet setup required on their end.


**Bitcoin back on your bills too**


- Most rewards programs stop at your card. Fold[extends into bill pay](https://support.foldapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/46036177124763-ACH-Bill-Pay-Rewards?ref=blog.foldapp.com) , so recurring payments you're making anyway can also earn.
- The idea is simple, the things you're already paying for shouldn't be dead weight. They should be doing something.


**You don't have to "believe" in bitcoin to use Fold**


- You don't need a wallet you understand, a strong opinion on where the price is headed, or any interest in the white paper that started it all.
- You just keep spending the way you already do. The app handles the rest.


If any of this sounds like something you'd use,[signing up takes about three minutes.](https://go.foldapp.com/xtBZ/0v57lres?ref=blog.foldapp.com)
