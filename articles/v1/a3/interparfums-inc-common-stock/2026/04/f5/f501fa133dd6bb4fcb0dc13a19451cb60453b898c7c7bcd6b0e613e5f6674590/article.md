## Alessandro Trotta: Vice President – Support Functions


A graduate of Bocconi University in Milan, Alessandro Trotta began his career in the beauty sector in 2002 at Beauté Prestige International, first as CFO of the Italian subsidiary and later as Group CFO in Paris.


In 2018, he became CFO EMEA at Shiseido and, in 2021, joined Byredo as Chief Operating Officer overseeing Finance and Operations.


He joined Interparfums in 2023 as Chief Financial Officer, assuming responsibility for the overall financial management of the Group.


Today, as Support Functions Executive Director, he oversees all Group support activities, including Finance, Legal, IT and HR.


He is also a member of the Board of Directors of Middlenext, the French association representing mid‑cap listed companies.

## Emmanuelle Thellier : Vice President – International Sales Director


A graduate of the European Business School in 1990 and of Sup de Luxe in 1993, Emmanuelle Thellier began her career in 1990 as Export Manager for the fragrance brands Weil and Molyneux.


She joined Interparfums in 1994, to develop the brand Burberry in Europe and Russia. In charge of Spain, she managed the opening of the Spanish subsidiary and the takeover of the brand Rochas from 2015.


Responsible for the US market from 2020, she supported the IPLB subsidiary in its growth on Jimmy Choo and Kate Spade.


In 2025, Emmanuelle Thellier joined the Executive Committee and became Vice President international sales in charge of Interparfums' international commercial strategy for Jimmy Choo, Montblanc, Karl Lagerfeld, Kate Spade, Rochas, Moncler, Boucheron, Solférino et Off-White.
