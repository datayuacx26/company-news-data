# Zinc 2.0: Universal Ecommerce API to Buy from Any URL (Beta)

**"We are making an API for buying things."** That was the first line of Zinc's YC application nearly 12 years ago. Today we're announcing three updates that take us closer to that original vision — starting with **Zinc 2.0** , a beta of our universal ecommerce API that can buy from any URL with a checkout.


Since founding, Zinc has programmatically purchased and shipped over $600 million in products — more than 20 million orders fulfilled for customers. For this next decade, we're expanding from a curated list of retailers to[buying from any site that has a checkout](https://www.zinc.com/docs) , with[MPP agent payments](https://www.zinc.com/blog/mpp) ,[transparent per-call pricing](https://www.zinc.com/pricing) , and infrastructure built for[AI-native commerce](https://www.zinc.com/solutions/ai) .


Ecommerce UX is broken: search is noisy, ads are everywhere, and checkout flows bury you in cookie banners and popups. Zinc gives builders — whether you're shipping an[AI shopping agent](https://www.zinc.com/blog/how-to-build-ai-shopping-agent) , a gifting platform, or something we haven't imagined yet — the API layer to complete purchases programmatically instead of sending users back to a retailer checkout. Already on Zinc v1? See the[v1 → v2 migration guide](https://www.zinc.com/docs/v2/migrating-from-v1) .


**To that end, here are 3 things we're excited to announce today:**


#### 2️⃣ Zinc 2.0


We're debuting a beta of our universal buying agent — *any* URL that has a checkout — Zinc can buy it for you. Explore the[v2 API docs](https://www.zinc.com/docs) or . We'll onboard customers gradually over the next several weeks and months as we expand capability.


#### 💅 Zinc's New Look


We were *long* overdue for a facelift, so we came up with a new identity that celebrates what Zinc helps automate the purchasing & shipping of: Products! Our new website & brand show off the products we all buy online and emphasizes Zinc's role in building the pipes that move these physical products from the warehouse to your customers' doorstep.


#### 📘 Zinc's New Docs


In addition to the new brand, we've refreshed our[documentation](https://www.zinc.com/docs) to make it much easier to get started with the API — including an interactive playground you can try right in your browser. Upgrading from v1? Start with the[migration guide](https://www.zinc.com/docs/v2/migrating-from-v1) .
