# Student Shows Bottle with Water Business

# Student Shows Bottle with Water Business


by


Apr 12, 2010


12 April 2010


A University of Manchester Student has started a new business based on converting bottled water drinkers to tap water. Edwin Broni-Mensah, who is doing a PhD in Mathematics, devised a branded GiveMeTap bottled and persuaded 26 cafes and restaurants to offer users free refills.
