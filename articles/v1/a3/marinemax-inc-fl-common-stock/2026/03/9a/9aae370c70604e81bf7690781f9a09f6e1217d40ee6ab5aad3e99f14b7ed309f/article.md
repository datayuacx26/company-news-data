# MarineMax Expands Operations At Treasure Island Marina, Enhancing Service And Support For Bay County Boaters

PANAMA CITY BEACH, FL (January 9, 2025)— MarineMax Panama City Beach is excited to announce the strengthening of its long-term partnership with Treasure Island Marina. This cherished family-owned business has served Bay County for nearly 50 years.


Under the new agreement, MarineMax has acquired the service and Parts Departments, further solidifying its commitment to delivering exceptional service and support to boaters. Additionally, pursuant to the agreement, MarineMax added multiple renewals to its existing lease, ensuring continued collaboration to support the boating community in the region. Under this renewed agreement, MarineMax will remain the premier boat dealer on Treasure Island Marina’s 12-acre waterfront property.


“Our values, work ethic, and love for promoting the boating lifestyle in Bay County align perfectly,” said Will Fleming, family member and COO of Treasure Island Marina. “We couldn’t be happier to have a partner like MarineMax working with us here at Treasure Island Marina.”


Treasure Island Marina has been a cornerstone of the boating community, and this strengthened partnership with MarineMax reflects a shared dedication to enhancing customers' boating experiences. Together, the two organizations look forward to building on their legacy of excellence and continuing to support the vibrant boating lifestyle in Bay County for years to come.


"We are thrilled to welcome the talented service team from Treasure Island Marina to MarineMax Panama City Beach," said Robert Stirling, General Manager. "This acquisition strengthens our commitment to providing exceptional service to our customers right here in Panama City Beach. By combining the team’s expertise and dedication to customer satisfaction with MarineMax's values, we can ensure our customers and local boaters stay on the water, enjoying their boating experiences. With our in-house service team, we provide convenient and efficient service, minimizing downtime and keeping our customers’ boats in top condition."


MarineMax Panama City Beach is the leading boat dealer and brand expert serving the Panama City Beach area. Since 2011, the dealership has been committed to delivering unmatched service and sales, catering to all boating needs. As a trusted boating resource, MarineMax Panama City Beach proudly offers a range of boats for sale from premium brands such as Azimut, Cruisers Yachts, Galeon Yachts, Ocean Alexander, MJM Yachts, Harris, Aquila, Sea Ray, Boston Whaler, and Grady-White. With this extensive selection of boats and yachts, customers will find the right boat to align with their boating lifestyle.
