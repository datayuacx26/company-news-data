# Going Pink with a Purpose

October is National Breast Cancer Awareness Month, and MarineMax is proud to stand with this important cause.


October is National Breast Cancer Awareness Month, and MarineMax is proud to stand with this important cause. On Wednesday, October 22, we’re joining the American Cancer Society’s Power of Pink Day to #GoPinkWithAPurpose. This initiative aims to raise awareness about breast cancer and promote the importance of regular screenings. We encourage everyone to take a moment to educate themselves and support those who have yet to be screened.


## Breast Cancer Facts


- In 2024, an estimated **310,720 people** will be diagnosed with breast cancer.
- About **2,790 cases** are expected in men.
- There are currently **over 4 million survivors** in the U.S. living with a history of breast cancer.
- Approximately **165,000 patients** are managing metastatic breast cancer.
(Source:[American Cancer Society Breast Cancer Facts & Figures 2024–2025](https://www.cancer.org/research/cancer-facts-statistics/breast-cancer-facts-figures.html) )


### Saving Lives Through Early Detection


Research proves that early detection saves lives. MarineMax is committed to the health and wellness of our team members, customers, and community.


- Regular screenings are recommended starting as early as age 40.
- Talk to your healthcare provider about the screening options that are best for you.


We're proud to support the American Cancer Society by donating and participating in the #GoPinkWithAPurpose initiative at our store locations. Join us in raising awareness, wear pink on October 20, and explore more ways to get involved through the[American Cancer Society](https://www.cancer.org/) or[Making Strides](https://secure.acsevents.org/site/SPageServer/?pagename=strides_msabc) website.


### Support from the American Cancer Society


The American Cancer Society provides comprehensive resources for patients and families, including:


- Guidance and education
- Free transportation to treatment
- Hotel accommodations to ensure access to care


Since 1913, the Society has invested **over $6.9 billion in cancer research** . As of June 2024, more than **$80 million** has gone toward breast cancer treatment innovations.
Learn more at the[American Cancer Society](https://www.cancer.org/) .
