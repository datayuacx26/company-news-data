# MarineMax is Proud to Honor our Veterans

November 11 marks an important day—Veterans Day. On this day, America celebrates and gives thanks to the brave men and women who have served in the United States Armed Forces, and MarineMax would like to do the same.


To all those who have served, and continue to serve, we salute you. Thank you for your service and sacrifice.


Join us in celebrating the remarkable individuals within the MarineMax family who have selflessly served our country. Their stories of resilience, courage, and camaraderie inspire us, reminding us of the profound value of service. At MarineMax, we are privileged to have veterans as an integral part of our team, bringing unique skills, leadership, and steadfast values to our community. Together, we reflect on the significance of their dedication and express our heartfelt gratitude to those who have served, currently serve, or will wear the uniform of the United States military.


One way MarineMax proudly honors our veterans is through our annual Lake Minnetonka Wakesurf Open, which goes far beyond trophies—it directly supports[Wake for Warriors](https://www.wakeforwarriors.org/) , a remarkable organization dedicated to creating a unique path to healing for wounded veterans and their families. Wake for Warriors harnesses the power of water sports like wakeboarding and wakesurfing to help veterans recover physically and emotionally, transforming these thrilling activities into therapeutic experiences. By participating in Barrel Boss, you’ll raise awareness and contribute to Wake for Warriors' mission of providing life-changing experiences for our nation’s heroes. It’s more than a competition—it’s a chance to make a real difference.


If you’d like to take part in showing your appreciation, below are a few suggestions to show gratitude and honor those who served:


- Support a local veteran-owned business by making a purchase or sharing a message of gratitude on their social media page.
- Volunteer or donate to your local[American Legion](https://mylegion.org/PersonifyEbusiness/Donate#legion-org) or other charities such as the[Wounded Warrior Project](https://www.woundedwarriorproject.org/) .
- Surprise a friend, family member, coworker, or neighbor with a coffee or lunch.
- Visit or show you care by spending quality time with a veteran.
- Help injured veterans get out on the water with[Wake for Warriors](https://www.wakeforwarriors.org/) and let them have an experience of a lifetime!
- When recognizing a veteran or someone in uniform, thank them for their service.
- If possible, take a veteran out on the water. Go fishing, waterskiing, or even enjoy a leisurely cruise along the shore.


MarineMax would like to wish all veterans, military members, and their families a safe and wonderful Veterans Day. We truly appreciate all you have done and still do for this great country!
