# MarineMax’s Heather Mess Appointed to AMI Board of Directors

OLDSMAR, FL (February 10, 2025)—MarineMax is proud to announce that Heather Mess, National Marina Manager, has been appointed to the Association of Marina Industries (AMI) 's Board of Directors. As a Board Member, Heather will occupy one of the corporate marina seats and contribute her extensive expertise to advancing the industry.


Heather has earned the esteemed Certified Marina Manager (CMM) designation through AMI's rigorous training program. This certification underscores her commitment to excellence in marina management, emphasizing safety, regulatory compliance, best practices, leadership, and the development of clean and resilient facilities. Several MarineMax Marina Managers have also achieved this certification, with many others currently in the process, ensuring that the team is well-equipped to deliver exceptional service and uphold the highest industry standards.


Reflecting on her journey and new role, Heather stated, "The Association of Marina Industries has been instrumental in advancing my career through its comprehensive training programs, courses, conferences, and extensive resources. The networking opportunities and industry insights it provides are highly valuable to the initiatives we pursue at MarineMax Marinas. I am truly honored and excited to serve on the Board of Directors for AMI. This opportunity allows me to collaborate with industry leaders and contribute to impactful initiatives that will shape the future of the marine industry."


In addition to her board appointment, Heather recently participated in a Women's Leadership Panel at the AMI Conference & Expo, where she discussed collaborative efforts within the industry. Her involvement underscores MarineMax's dedication to fostering leadership and promoting diversity within the marine sector.
