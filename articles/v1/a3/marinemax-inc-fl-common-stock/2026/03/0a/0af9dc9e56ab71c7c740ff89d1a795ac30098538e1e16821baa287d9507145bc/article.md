# MarineMax Dallas to Host Special Events During North Texas Boat Week, July 17 – 20

DALLAS, TX (July 8, 2025)— North Texas Boat Week is back and bigger than ever, and MarineMax Dallas is your premier destination for an unforgettable experience on the water. From July 17–20, boating enthusiasts are invited to explore the latest models, enjoy great food and live entertainment, and take advantage of exclusive offers during this four-day celebration.


Visitors can discover exceptional offers on top boat brands and models, from luxury yachts to family-friendly runabouts. Throughout the show, guests will enjoy delicious food and drinks from local favorites. On Thursday, July 17, Our Blended Table will be serving up catfish and burgers from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Friday, July 18, features The Secret Society Company with refreshing snow cones and smoothies from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. On Saturday, July 19, Cousins Maine Lobster joins the fun from 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., and the event wraps up on Sunday, July 20, with Texas Tacoholics offering authentic tacos from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.


In addition to great food, guests can look forward to exciting live music performances, with the full lineup to be announced soon. Each day will also feature engaging activities and entertainment for attendees of all ages.


MarineMax Dallas will showcase a wide selection of boats from Harris, Sea Ray, and Boston Whaler, while the MarineMax Dallas Yacht Center will display larger vessels from Cruisers Yachts and Sea Ray.


Whether shopping for your dream boat or soaking in the lively atmosphere, MarineMax is your first and last stop of the weekend. For more event details, visit[here](https://www.marinemax.com/pages/locations-dallas) .
