# MarineMax Somers Point Honored with Dual Awards from Sailfish

SOMERS POINT, NJ (July 17, 2025)— Sailfish Boats has recognized MarineMax Somers Point with two prestigious honors for the 2025 Model Year: the Sales Achievement Award and the Customer Service Achievement Award.


The Sales Achievement Award reflects the organization’s passion and dedication to the brand, as well as its recognition as a leader in its respective market(s).


The Customer Service Achievement Award is based on feedback from new Sailfish owners through Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) surveys, specifically their likelihood to recommend the dealership. This recognition highlights the team's dedication to delivering an exceptional ownership experience from sale to service.


John Mondelli, General Manager of MarineMax Somers Point, shared, “We truly believe in the Sailfish brand. These boats offer incredible value—something our customers immediately recognize in both pricing and the high-quality craftsmanship Sailfish consistently delivers.”


TJ Rose, Regional President at MarineMax, added, “Our team in Somers Point enjoy a great partnership with Rob and the entire Sailfish team. Their support makes it easy to provide a top-tier ownership experience to our customers. They deliver an outstanding product, and that makes all the difference.”


MarineMax Somers Point remains committed to serving as a trusted destination for boaters in the region, offering expert guidance, premium brands like Sailfish, and best-in-class service.
