# MarineMax Islamorada Joins The Island Boat Show To Support The Community

ISLAMORADA, FL (January 16, 2025)—MarineMax Islamorada is excited to announce its participation in the highly anticipated Island Boat Show, which will take place from January 24 to 26, 2025, at Island Community Church in Islamorada, Florida.


At this three-day event, MarineMax will showcase the latest boat models from leading manufacturers, including Sea Ray, Boston Whaler, and Scout. It will also feature exciting boating accessories, unique merchandise, stunning art, and delicious food.


The Island Boat Show offers attendees the chance to explore new boats, discover exciting accessories, shop for unique merchandise, and enjoy beautiful art. The event will also feature delicious food and a fun-filled atmosphere, making it the perfect outing for boating enthusiasts and families.


In addition to its exciting exhibits, the Island Boat Show is committed to giving back to the community. All proceeds from the event will support Island Community Church's local and global missions and ministries. Furthermore, funds raised will go toward higher education scholarships for local high school students, furthering the community’s growth and future.


“We’re thrilled to be part of the Island Boat Show again this year,” said Tommy Brashares, Sales Manager of MarineMax Islamorada. “It’s a wonderful opportunity to connect with fellow boating enthusiasts while supporting a great cause that impacts the community and beyond. We look forward to seeing everyone at the show and celebrating the spirit of giving back.”


Don’t miss out on this incredible opportunity to see the best in boating while giving back to the community. Join MarineMax Islamorada and other local businesses at the Island Boat Show for a weekend of fun and philanthropy.
