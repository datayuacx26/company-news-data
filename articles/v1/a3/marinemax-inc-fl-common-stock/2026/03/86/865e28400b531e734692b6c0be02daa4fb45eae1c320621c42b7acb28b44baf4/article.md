# MarineMax’s Bridget Pinsonneault Named a 2025 Boating Industry Woman Making Waves

OLDSMAR, FL (June 23, 2024)— MarineMax proudly announces that Bridget Pinsonneault, Director of People Operations, has been named one of Boating Industry’s 2025 Women Making Waves, recognizing women making a significant impact across the marine industry.


Since joining MarineMax three years ago, Bridget has played a key role in transforming the company’s culture. Her leadership has improved onboarding, increased employee engagement, and strengthened cross-functional collaboration across the organization. She’s also led initiatives focused on inclusion, effective project management, and internal communication—efforts that have improved retention and team morale company-wide.


Bridget’s passion for people and fresh perspective have made her a powerful advocate for change. She leads with empathy, humor, and a people-first mindset that resonates at every level of MarineMax. Though her work is largely behind the scenes, the results are felt across the company and by customers alike.


“Bridget brings authenticity and energy to everything she does,” said Beth Garland, SVP of People Strategy. “Her influence is undeniable, and we’re proud to celebrate this well-deserved recognition.”


Bridget holds a B.S. from the University of South Florida and enjoys boating around Siesta Key and Longboat Key with her family.
