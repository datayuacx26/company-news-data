# MarineMax Charleston Celebrates Grand Opening of New Showroom

## MarineMax Charleston Celebrates Grand Opening of New Showroom


CHARLESTON, SC (August 12, 2025)— MarineMax Charleston is set to celebrate the grand opening of its all-new, state-of-the-art showroom on Friday, August 22, located in the heart of Daniel Island. The community is invited to join the celebration from 4:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. for an evening of upscale hospitality, live music, premium vendors, and an exclusive look at the latest models from world-class boat brands.


This highly anticipated event marks a significant milestone for MarineMax Charleston as the team unveils a modern retail space designed to elevate the customer experience and deepen the company’s connection to the local boating community.


“As we open the doors to our brand-new showroom, MarineMax is proud to deepen our roots within the Daniel Island and Charleston community,” says TJ Rose, MarineMax Regional President. “This milestone is more than just a grand opening; it’s the start of an exciting chapter where we grow together with the Lowcountry boating community.”


“Through our expanding partnership with Scout and our premium lineup of brands like Sea Ray, Boston Whaler, Saxdor, and Cruisers Yachts, we are committed to providing an elevated, full-service experience that supports every aspect of our customers’ boating needs.”


“Our new showroom offers a welcoming and sophisticated space for our customers to explore the latest models, attend educational events, and connect with our dedicated team. We are honored to call Daniel Island home and look forward to building lasting relationships with boaters throughout Charleston for years to come.”


The new location reflects MarineMax’s continued investment in the Charleston area and its commitment to providing best-in-class service, sales, and support. Guests at the grand opening event will enjoy hors d’oeuvres and refreshments, live entertainment, and one-on-one opportunities to connect with MarineMax team members and vendor partners.


For more information and to RSVP, visit[here](https://www.marinemax.com/pages/locations-charleston) .
