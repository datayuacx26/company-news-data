# 'Tis the Season for Giving

At MarineMax, we believe in the power of community and the joy of giving back. That’s why we’re proud to partner with Toys for Tots and local charities through our annual Fill the Boat Donation Drives. Together with our team members, customers, and local communities, we strive to make the holiday season brighter for children and families in need.


The mission of[Toys for Tots](https://www.toysfortots.org/) is simple yet impactful: to collect new, unwrapped toys and distribute them to less fortunate children, spreading hope and joy during the holiday season. By sharing the magic of the holidays, Toys for Tots inspires children to grow into responsible, productive, and caring members of society.


Beyond toy drives, many of our MarineMax locations are teaming up with local charities to host food drives, clothing collections, and other initiatives tailored to their communities' unique needs. Whether it’s contributing a toy, a non-perishable food item, gently used clothing or a monetary donation, there are countless ways to make a meaningful impact this season.


Check with your[nearest MarineMax location](https://www.marinemax.com/events?q=toys%20for%20tots&location=&types=&brand=&date=&format=) to see how they’re participating and join us in making a positive impact on the community this holiday season. Every contribution counts. Together, we can bring smiles, hope, and joy to families and children this holiday season.
