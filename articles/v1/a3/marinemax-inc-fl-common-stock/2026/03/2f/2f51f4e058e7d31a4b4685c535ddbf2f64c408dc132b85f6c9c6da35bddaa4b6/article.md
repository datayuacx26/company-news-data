# MarineMax Sponsors Lake Minnetonka Wakesurf Open July 17–19

ROGERS, MN (July 1, 2025)— MarineMax is proud to sponsor the Lake Minnetonka Wakesurf Open, taking place July 17–19 in partnership with Nautique Boats and the Spirit of the Lakes Festival. The three-day event combines wakesurfing, community celebration, and support for veterans.


Event Schedule:


- Thursday, July 17 – Wake For Warriors Charter Cruise
Veterans and their families are invited for a therapeutic experience on the water, focused on connection and healing.
- Friday, July 18 – Nautique Demo Night
Open test drives of the latest Nautique boats on Lake Minnetonka.
- Saturday, July 19 – Wakesurf Open Competition
Riders of all ages and skill levels compete behind Nautique boats in grassroots divisions. Registration is open now.


Held alongside the Spirit of the Lakes Festival, the Wakesurf Open adds to a weekend filled with live music, food trucks, a vendor marketplace, fireworks, and more, drawing families and watersports enthusiasts from across the region.


Proceeds from the event benefit Wake For Warriors, a nonprofit that provides recreational therapy through watersports for veterans and their families.


For event details, registration, or to book a demo, visit[here](https://www.marinemax.com/events/activities/lake-minnetonka-wakesurf-open) .
