# MarineMax Charleston Honored as Daniel Island’s Best Boat Dealership

CHARLESTON, SC (April 27, 2026)— MarineMax Charleston is proud to announce it has been named Daniel Island’s Best Boat Dealership, a testament to the dealership’s unwavering commitment to delivering an exceptional boating experience to the Charleston community.


"We are excited and honored to be recognized as Best Boat Dealership for Daniel Island," said Karl Anderson, General Manager of MarineMax Charleston. "This is a culmination of a huge team effort by our entire staff and something we take tremendous pride in. We aim to deliver not only an exceptional product but also a first-class experience. A huge thank you to our colleagues at Scout, Boston Whaler, Sea Ray, Saxdor, Cruisers Yachts, Aquila, Galeon, Azimut, Mercury, and Yamaha, among others, who are on mission with us in supporting the Charleston boating community."


As the trusted boat dealer for the Charleston area with over 30 years of legacy, MarineMax Charleston carries one of the region's most comprehensive lineups of recreational and luxury vessels. Whether a customer is searching for a sleek cruiser, a versatile fishing boat, or a luxurious yacht, the dealership's experienced team is committed to guiding every buyer toward the perfect vessel to match their boating lifestyle and supporting them well beyond the sale. Backed by a service and maintenance department staffed with highly skilled professionals, MarineMax Charleston is a true partner on and off the water.


This award underscores MarineMax’s broader mission to help people enjoy the water and build lifelong memories with family and friends.
