# MarineMax Locations Earn Top Honors at 2026 Sea Ray Dealer Meeting

OLDSMAR, FL (April 28, 2026)— MarineMax, the nation’s largest recreational boat and yacht retailer, today announced that numerous company locations earned prestigious recognition at the 2026 Sea Ray Dealer Meeting, underscoring MarineMax’s continued commitment to delivering an exceptional boating experience to customers across the country.


MarineMax locations were honored in three award categories: Unrivaled Support, Market Leader, and Overall Excellence, spanning all regions of the United States.


**Unrivaled Support** , the following MarineMax locations were recognized for their dedication to customer service and support:


Northeast: MarineMax Kent Island, MarineMax Norwalk, MarineMax Somers Point, and MarineMax Westbrook


Central: MarineMax Excelsior, MarineMax Lake Ozark, MarineMax Nisswa, and MarineMax Rogers


Mid-Atlantic: MarineMax Charleston, MarineMax Greenville, MarineMax Lake Norman, and MarineMax Savannah


Southeast: MarineMax Fort Walton Beach, MarineMax Jacksonville, MarineMax Jupiter, MarineMax Lake Lanier, MarineMax Naples, MarineMax Ocean Reef, MarineMax Orlando, MarineMax Pensacola, MarineMax Sarasota, MarineMax St. Petersburg, MarineMax Venice, and MarineMax Wrightsville Beach


**Market Leader** , recognized for their outstanding sales performance and market presence:


Northeast: MarineMax Boston, MarineMax Brick, MarineMax Danvers, MarineMax Huntington, MarineMax Norwalk, MarineMax Somers Point, and MarineMax Westbrook


Central: MarineMax Excelsior, MarineMax Nisswa, and MarineMax Rogers


Mid-Atlantic: MarineMax Lake Wylie


Southeast: MarineMax Clearwater, MarineMax Cocoa, MarineMax Fort Myers, MarineMax Jupiter, MarineMax Miami, MarineMax Palm Beach, MarineMax Panama City, MarineMax Pensacola, MarineMax Pompano, MarineMax Sarasota, MarineMax St. Petersburg, MarineMax Stuart, MarineMax Venice, and MarineMax Wrightsville Beach


**Overall Excellence** , the highest tier of recognition, was awarded to locations demonstrating exceptional performance across all aspects:


MarineMax Excelsior, MarineMax Lake Lanier, MarineMax Lake Norman, MarineMax Nisswa, MarineMax Venice, and MarineMax Wrightsville Beach


"We are incredibly proud of the teams at each of these locations," said Kyle Langbehn, President of Retail Operations at MarineMax. "This recognition from Sea Ray is a direct reflection of the passion and dedication our people bring to work every day. From sales to service to the overall customer experience, these locations are setting the standard."
