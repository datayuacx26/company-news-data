# Beth Garland Named Chief People Officer

OLDSMAR, FL (June 17, 2026)— MarineMax, Inc. (NYSE: HZ0), the world's largest recreational boat and yacht retailer, marina operator, and superyacht services company, today announced that Beth Garland has been named Chief People Officer.


Since joining MarineMax nearly five years ago as Senior Vice President of People Strategy, Garland has helped lead and develop many of the people-focused initiatives that support teams across the organization. Her leadership has been instrumental as the company has expanded its business, added new divisions and brands, and continued building a strong culture centered around its mission, values, and people.


"Beth has played an essential role in shaping our culture and strengthening team member engagement as MarineMax has grown," said Brett McGill, Chief Executive Officer and President of MarineMax. "Her passion for our people and her leadership make her the right choice to lead our people strategy as Chief People Officer, and we're excited to see the continued impact she'll have across the organization."


In her new role, Garland will continue to lead people strategy across MarineMax, helping the company attract, develop, and support great talent while strengthening the culture that makes MarineMax such a special place to work. Her passion for people, leadership development, and team member engagement continues to make a meaningful impact throughout the organization.
