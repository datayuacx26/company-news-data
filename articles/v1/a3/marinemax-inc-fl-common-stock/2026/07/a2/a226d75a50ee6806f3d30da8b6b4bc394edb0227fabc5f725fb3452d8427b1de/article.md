# MarineMax Launches First Certified Pre-Owned Boat Program

OLDSMAR, FL (July 8, 2026)— MarineMax (NYSE: HZO), the nation’s largest recreational boat and yacht retailer, today announced the launch of the MarineMax Certified Pre-Owned Program. A first-of-its-kind initiative in the marine industry that redefines what customers can expect when purchasing a pre-owned vessel. Designed to bring a new level of transparency, confidence, and value to the pre-owned marketplace, the program reflects MarineMax’s commitment to delivering an exceptional ownership experience at every stage of the boating journey.


As the pre-owned boat market continues to grow and evolve, buyers are seeking more than just a good deal; they want assurance, quality, and a trusted partner behind the purchase. The MarineMax Certified Pre-Owned Program answers that call. Every certified vessel undergoes a rigorous multi-point inspection and reconditioning process performed by factory-trained MarineMax technicians, ensuring that each vessel meets the highest standard before it ever reaches a customer.


“For years, MarineMax has applied the same meticulous standards to every pre-owned vessel that comes through our doors,” said Kyle Langbehn, MarineMax President of Retail Operations and EVP. “This program puts a name and a promise to what we’ve always believed: that buying a pre-owned boat from MarineMax should feel just as confident and exciting as buying a brand-new one. We’re proud to be the first in the industry to formalize that commitment.”


The program is available on brands MarineMax has extensive expertise and experience representing, ensuring buyers have access to a curated selection of premium pre-owned inventory. Key program highlights include:


- **Limited One-Year Warranty Coverage:** Every certified boat comes with a one-year limited warranty, giving buyers confidence that extends well beyond the day of purchase.
- **Rigorous Multi-Point Inspection:** Each boat or yacht is thoroughly inspected and reconditioned by trained MarineMax technicians to meet strict quality standards before earning its certified designation.
- **Strict Eligibility Requirements:** Only select brands that MarineMax has extensive expertise and experience representing qualify for the program, helping ensure consistently high-quality inventory with a clean history.
- **Enhanced Resale Value:** Certification supports strong long-term resale value, making a MarineMax Certified Pre-Owned boat a smarter investment for buyers.


The launch of the MarineMax Certified Pre-Owned Program represents a meaningful evolution in how the marine industry serves its customers. They are offering a structured, warranty-backed certification program for pre-owned inventory, bringing a level of confidence and consistency that stands apart in the marine retail space.


Customers can explore available Certified Pre-Owned inventory at any MarineMax location or online[here](https://www.marinemax.com/collections/certified-pre-owned) . MarineMax team members are available to answer questions and guide buyers through the certified buying experience.
