# Advancing Healthcare Innovation: Segmed’s Impact at Scale

Segmed has been advancing and conquering milestones: building a nationwide, privacy-first imaging data network spanning all 50 U.S. states, supporting more than 150 million imaging studies, and enabling over 35 FDA-cleared AI technologies.


Our new[Impact Report, Advancing Healthcare Innovation with Segmed](https://www.segmed.ai/resources/advancing-healthcare-innovation-segmed-impact-report) , captures what this progress looks like in practice, across customers, research, healthcare providers, and the broader ecosystem.


## **From Vision to Infrastructure**


Segmed was founded with a clear mission: unlock high-quality, diverse medical imaging data for research and innovation, without compromising patient privacy.


Today, that vision has become infrastructure:
‍


• A network covering **all 50 U.S. states**


• **150M+ de-identified imaging studies**


• Support for **35+ FDA-cleared AI solutions**


• Customers across **20 countries**


• Industry-leading certifications, including **ISO27001, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA Safe Harbor** , and **Expert Determination**


This foundation enables healthcare innovators to build AI that is not only powerful, but representative, reliable, and regulatory-ready.


‍


## **Real-World Impact Across the Ecosystem**


Segmed’s impact spans the full innovation lifecycle:


**AI & Product Development**
From traditional AI validation to large-scale foundation models, Segmed datasets power training, validation, and FDA submissions across modalities including CT, MRI, X-ray, PET, pathology, and EHR-linked data.


**Real-World Evidence & Clinical Research**
Researchers leverage longitudinal, multimodal datasets to develop predictive imaging biomarkers, external control arms, and outcome-driven insights, accelerating discovery while preserving rigor.


**Healthcare Equity & Representation**
Before Segmed, deep learning models were often trained on data from just a few states. Today, AI models can be trained on patient cohorts from **all U.S. states** , improving generalizability and equity.


## **Trusted by Industry and Academia**


Segmed partners with leading enterprises, startups, and academic institutions, supporting global research, peer-reviewed publications, and invited presentations at major conferences including RSNA, ECR, SIIM, and PMWC. Our customer feedback consistently highlights:
‍


• Speed and scale of data access


• Legal and regulatory simplicity


• Diversity and clinical relevance of datasets


• Professionalism and trust
