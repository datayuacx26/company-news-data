# Segmed Renews ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certification, Strengthening Its Leadership in Secure Medical Data Innovation

*Palo Alto, CA – August 26, 2025* - Segmed, Inc., a leading provider of regulatory-grade medical imaging data for life sciences, MedTech, and AI innovation, today announced the successful renewal of its **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification** , the world’s most recognized standard for information security management.


This achievement, validated through an independent audit conducted by **BARR Certifications** , reaffirms Segmed’s unwavering commitment to **data protection, security, and trust** - all essential pillars for advancing medical research and device innovation in an era of increasingly complex cybersecurity threats.
‍


> “At Segmed, data security isn’t a compliance checkbox, it’s a foundation for innovation,” said **Darcy Connors, Vice President of Operations at Segmed** . “Our customers, ranging from AI developers to global MedTech leaders, and our data partners, ranging from imaging clinics to large healthcare systems - trust us with sensitive, high-value imaging data. Renewing our ISO certification demonstrates that we consistently hold ourselves to the highest security and governance standards, year after year.”


Since its initial certification, Segmed has maintained a **comprehensive information security management system (ISMS)** that aligns with international best practices. The renewal reflects Segmed’s ability to not only meet but exceed rigorous requirements for risk management, data confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
‍


> “Real-world imaging data is shaping the future of healthcare innovation, but its value depends on trust,” said **Dr. Jie Wu, Chief Data Officer and Cofounder of Segmed** . “By maintaining ISO 27001 certification, we reinforce our role as a trusted partner to healthcare providers, biopharma, and MedTech organizations who rely on us to deliver both innovation and security.”


‍


##### **A Standard for a Data-Driven Future in Healthcare**


The medical device and pharmaceutical industries are under increasing pressure to demonstrate regulatory compliance, ensure patient privacy, and reduce risks associated with sensitive data. By holding ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, Segmed ensures that its partners have access to diverse, de-identified, and regulatory-grade imaging data, without compromising security or patient trust.
