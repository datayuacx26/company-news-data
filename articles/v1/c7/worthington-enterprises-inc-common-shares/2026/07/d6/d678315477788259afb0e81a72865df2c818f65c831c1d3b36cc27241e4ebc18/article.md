# Worthington Earns 2026 Top Workplaces Honors

Worthington Enterprises is one of the[Top Workplaces in central Ohio for 2026](https://www.columbusceo.com/story/business/awards/2026/03/26/columbus-ceo-top-workplaces-2026-these-89-employers-made-winners-list/89326308007/) . This marks the 14th consecutive year Worthington Enterprises (12 as Worthington Industries) has been included since the inaugural list.


Energage, in partnership with Columbus CEO magazine, bases the rankings on employee responses to a confidential survey. Approximately 1,200 Worthington Enterprises employees across three locations in central Ohio were offered the chance to participate. Employees evaluate five “positivity measures,” ranging from company values to whether they feel included, appreciated and a part of something meaningful. Employees also rate various facets of company leadership, performance, feelings of connection and salary and benefits.


“We’re grateful to everyone on our team for the roles we all play in making Worthington a place where people want to be, can do their best work and be their best selves,” said Worthington Enterprises President and CEO Joe Hayek. “We continue to believe – and witness daily – that our people-first culture is a meaningful differentiator for Worthington Enterprises.”
