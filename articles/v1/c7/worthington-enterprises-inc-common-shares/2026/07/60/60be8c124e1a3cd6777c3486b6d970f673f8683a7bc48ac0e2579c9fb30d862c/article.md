# Worthington Is One of the World's Most Productive Companies

Worthington Enterprises is one of the[World’s Most Productive Companies™](https://www.lnsresearch.com/worlds-most-productive-companies) , according to global research and advisory firm[LNS Research](https://www.lnsresearch.com/) . This places Worthington Enterprises among the Top 100 publicly traded manufacturers worldwide, achieving the strongest industrial productivity growth since 2019.


According to LNS Research’s analysis, the World’s Most Productive Companies™ excel through key differentiators such as:


- Aligning strategy, operating models, transformation and supply networks as one integrated system.
- Investing in people and decision intelligence as deliberately as capital and automation.
- Simplifying architectures so innovation scales beyond pilots.
- Running disciplined, enterprise operating models grounded in proven principles.
- Sharing information across the value chain to reduce variance and increase speed.
- Deploying AI within guardrails that protect stability, trust and performance.
- Treating productivity as the primary driver of profitable growth.


Colin Souza, vice president and chief financial officer, Worthington Enterprises, said, “It’s an honor to be recognized among these companies for our approach to industrial productivity. The differentiators highlighted by this report reflect how our employees are successfully leveraging the Worthington Business System of innovation, transformation and acquisition to guide our growth.”


LNS Research’s Industrial Productivity Index™ (IPI) is a comprehensive benchmark of operational performance across 10 major manufacturing sectors and is the foundation for this recognition. Fewer than 15% of the more than 700 companies analyzed qualified for this distinction.


A measure of true enterprise-wide performance, the IPI shows that the World’s Most Productive Companies™ are outperforming peers by aligning leadership, strategy and execution across the entire value chain—from product innovation and manufacturing to supply networks and service delivery.
