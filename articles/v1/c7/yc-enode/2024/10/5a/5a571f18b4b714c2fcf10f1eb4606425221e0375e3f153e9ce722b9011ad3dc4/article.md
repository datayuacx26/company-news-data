# NeoCharge and Enode partner to help EV drivers in the US get more value from their vehicles

[NeoCharge](https://getneocharge.com/) is putting power in the hands of EV drivers – literally.


With the launch of the new[NeoCharge app](https://getneocharge.com/pages/app) , EV drivers can accurately track their EV charging costs, and set smart schedules that cut their utility bills by as much as 70%.


Here at Enode, we’re proud to be part of the digital infrastructure that makes the NeoCharge app possible. We're excited to collaborate on even more innovative use cases as NeoCharge makes strides into the US home energy management space.


## **Cutting the friction from home EV charging**


NeoCharge’s mission is to accelerate the transition to an all-electric future by making EV charging and home electrification more accessible.


For EV owners, home charging can often be a headache. Tracking charging costs, aligning charging with the cheapest energy price hours, and switching between OEM and third-party apps is frustrating, and means they’re not able to realize the full value of their EV.


The NeoCharge app solves those problems. It’s a powerful tool for optimizing EV charging, tracking home energy usage, and helping drivers effortlessly cut their energy consumption and costs.


## **Seamless EV connectivity and smart scheduling**


The NeoCharge app connects to utilities and EVs, so that users can accurately track their EV charging costs, and set smart schedules that save them money.


Thanks to Enode’s market-leading API for EV connectivity, NeoCharge customers can connect over 15 major EV brands (including Tesla, BMW, Ford, Chevrolet) to the app in just a few taps. The app also syncs with their exact utility rates to find the cheapest charging hours, and enables seamless smart charging that slashes EV charging costs by up to 70%.


By providing detailed tracking of charging sessions and energy costs in one place, the NeoCharge app delivers greater visibility and control for EV drivers, helping them become active participants in the energy transition.


## **Holistic home energy management solutions**


And the app’s about to get even more useful. Users with more than one home energy device will soon be able to integrate them into the NeoCharge app to get a holistic view of their home energy consumption, and make devices work in harmony.


We’re working with NeoCharge to integrate smart EV charger such as ChargePoint Home, Wallbox Pulsar Plus, and the Enel X Juicebox. This will help enable optimized EV charging for more homeowners and renters.


There are plenty more home energy use cases on the roadmap, and we’re delighted to be working with a US leader to accelerate them to market. Together we’re making the energy system more interoperable, more sustainable, and more customer-centric. Watch this space!
