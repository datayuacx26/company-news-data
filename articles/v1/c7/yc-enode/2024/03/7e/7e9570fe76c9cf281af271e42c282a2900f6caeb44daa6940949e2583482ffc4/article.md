# The beginner's guide to EV data

You already know it: electric vehicles (EVs) are taking over, and there’s no going back. And as more and more EVs hit the roads each year, the opportunity for innovation and potential value they can offer also grows.


The way we see it, the use of EV data – and by extension[EV APIs](https://enode.com/blog/electric-vehicle-api) (Application Programming Interfaces) – is key to unlocking that value, and taking EV user experiences to the next level.


If you are new to the world of EV APIs, or want a refresher, we’ve created a simple guide on the topic 👉[The beginner's guide to EV data](https://enode.com/pdfs/beginners-guide-to-ev-data.pdf)


Dig in to learn more about how EV data can be accessed, what kind of data is available via EV APIs and what value-adding solutions they can help build.
