# Fuse Energy delivers super cheap EV charging with Enode

[Fuse](https://www.fuseenergy.com/) is the fastest growing energy retailer in the UK for a reason. They offer some of the most competitive fixed and time-of-use (ToU) tariffs on the market, alongside a seamless switching experience through the Fuse app and web.


Now, Fuse is partnering with Enode to take that experience even further, bringing intelligent EV charging and public charging features to EV owners across the UK.


> “Partnering with Enode means we’re able to deliver the best-possible experience to the widest-possible market of EV owners,” said Alan Chang, Founder and CEO at Fuse. “Enode’s API is an integral part of our digital infrastructure, empowering us to launch smart use cases that drive real value for customers in record time.”


## **Keeping control of EV charging costs**


With Fuse, customers can be confident they’re getting the most competitive energy prices, and contributing to a more sustainable energy system at the same time. The Fuse app lets customers track their consumption and spending in real-time, as part of making electricity cheaper, greener, and more accessible for everyone.


Now, with their new EV products, the Fuse experience gets even better.


Managed charging is the next step, with Fuse's smart EV tariff. Moving energy use from expensive periods to cheaper ones cuts energy costs for customers and helps keep the energy system in balance. Using Enode’s API, Fuse lets customers seamlessly connect their EVs and home chargers to the Fuse app with just a few clicks. Customers can then create charging schedules that automatically optimize for cost, taking advantage of Fuse’s smart EV tariff, the cheapest in the UK.


> “There’s a huge opportunity to make electric the cheapest and most enjoyable way to drive,” said Thorvald Thorsnes, CRO and co-founder of Enode. “Optimizing when and how EVs charge, planning routes to avoid range anxiety, and packaging it up with a seamless app experience for consumers is the way forward. We’re delighted to partner with Fuse, who are already making it happen. ”


## **Superior energy services for consumers**


Partnering with Enode lets Fuse connect to a wide range of EV brands and models, instantly access their data, and effortlessly send commands to control charging. This makes the Fuse app simpler to use, and more valuable to customers.


Through the new managed charging solutions, Fuse can offer even cheaper energy tariffs to EV drivers, and help them get more lifetime value out of their vehicles. And, instead of having to switch between home energy management and EV apps, customers will be able to seamlessly and holistically manage energy consumption through the Fuse super-app.
