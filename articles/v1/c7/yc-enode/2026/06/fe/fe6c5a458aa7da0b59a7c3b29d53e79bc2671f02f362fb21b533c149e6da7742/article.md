# SMA partners with Enode to unlock solar and battery flexibility for the next grid

[SMA](https://www.sma.de/) , a leading provider of solar solutions, partners with Enode to help energy companies connect and optimize SMA assets through a single, reliable integration.


As households adopt more distributed energy resources, including solar panels, home batteries, electric vehicles and heat pumps, energy companies need reliable ways to connect these assets and deliver seamless customer experiences.


Through this partnership, energy retailers, utilities and energy platforms using Enode, can integrate SMA-connected assets into energy products and flexibility programs without building and maintaining their own integration.


The partnership supports a range of use cases, including solar self-consumption optimization, home battery optimization, dynamic tariff automation, demand response programs and virtual power plant participation.


For households, this means more value from their SMA assets and greater freedom to choose the energy apps and services that work best for them. They can connect to a growing ecosystem of energy apps and flexibility programs that help optimize consumption, automate savings and unlock the full potential of their solar and battery systems.


Solar energy in combination with battery storage are becoming increasingly important parts of the modern energy system. By making SMA assets accessible through Enode, the partnership helps energy companies build services that create value for both consumers and the grid.


The addition of SMA further expands Enode's ecosystem of connected energy devices, strengthening interoperability across the growing landscape of distributed energy resources.


> *"We're excited to welcome SMA to the Enode ecosystem," says Morten Strom, Senior Partnership Manager at Enode. "As solar and battery adoption continues to grow, energy companies need simple and reliable ways to connect these assets and create great customer experiences. Together with SMA, we’re making it easier to unlock the value of distributed energy assets for both users and the grid."*


Together, SMA and Enode are helping energy companies unlock more value from distributed energy resources while supporting a more connected, flexible and sustainable energy system.
