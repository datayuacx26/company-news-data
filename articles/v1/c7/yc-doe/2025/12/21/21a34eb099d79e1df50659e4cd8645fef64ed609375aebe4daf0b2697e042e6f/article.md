# Introducing Credits: Simple, Predictable Usage for AI Work

Today we're rolling out a new credits system that makes it simple to understand and control how you use Doe. Whether you're running a quick query or automating complex multi-step workflows, you'll always know exactly what you're paying for.


## How Credits Work


Credits are the universal currency for work in Doe. Every time Doe processes a request on your behalf - whether that's querying your CRM, analyzing a dataset, or compiling a report from multiple sources - it consumes credits based on the complexity of the task.


Simple queries use fewer credits. Complex multi-step tasks that involve multiple AI models or large datasets use more. This means you're always paying proportionally to the value you're getting.


**And here's the important part: you only pay for results that work.** If Doe's response fails or doesn't meet your expectations, no credits are consumed. We want you to feel confident experimenting and iterating - you shouldn't be penalized for exploring what's possible.


## What's Included


Paid plans come with a monthly credit allocation:


Each plan includes credits sized to the team and the work Doe is expected to run. Higher-volume teams can work with us on custom allocations, usage analytics, and support.


New users can start with trial credits before choosing a paid plan.


Credits refresh at the start of each billing cycle. If you're evaluating Doe for a larger team,[contact sales](https://doe.cal.com/sales/enterprise?overlayCalendar=true&utm_source=doe.so&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=blog&utm_content=introducing-credits) for plan details.


## Transparent Usage


You'll always know where you stand. Doe shows your remaining credits in real-time, and you'll receive a notification when you're running low. We'll never cut off access mid-task - your work will always complete.


If you need more capacity, Standard and Pro plans offer additional credits at predictable rates. Or you can upgrade to a higher tier for better per-credit economics.


## Designed for Teams


The credits system is built with teams in mind:


- **Per-user allocations** - Each team member gets their own credit pool on Standard and Pro plans, so heavy users don't impact everyone else
- **Shared visibility** - Admins can see usage patterns across the team with Pro's usage analytics
- **Predictable costs** - Know exactly what your monthly bill will look like before it arrives


## Why Credits?


We explored many commercial models before landing on credits. The goal was simple: align credit usage with the value Doe delivers.


Flat subscription fees work for predictable software, but AI assistants are different. The work they do varies enormously - from a 30-second lookup to a 15-minute deep research task. Credits let us price fairly for both, while giving you complete control over how you allocate your budget.


## Getting Started


The credits system is live now across the product. If you're an existing user, you'll see your credit balance in the dashboard. New users can start with trial credits, and a credit card is required to activate the trial.
