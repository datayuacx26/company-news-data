# Introducing the Trace Panel: Full Visibility Into Your AI Agent

When you delegate work to an AI agent, you want to know exactly what it's doing on your behalf. Today, we're introducing the **Trace Panel** - a new feature that gives you complete visibility into every action Doe takes.


## Why Transparency Matters


AI agents are powerful. They can research topics, gather data from multiple sources, analyze information, and compile comprehensive reports-all in minutes rather than hours.


But power without visibility creates uncertainty. How do you know the agent checked the right sources? How do you verify its methodology? How do you catch potential errors before they compound?


The Trace Panel answers these questions by showing you exactly what's happening, step by step.


## What You'll See


The Trace Panel displays every phase of the agent's work:


- **Planning** - Watch the agent break down your request into concrete steps. You'll see the full plan before execution begins, giving you a chance to understand the approach.


- **Execution Timeline** - Each action is logged with timestamps, so you know how long each step takes and where the agent is spending its time.


- **Source Tracking** - When Doe searches the web or fetches information, you see exactly which sources it's accessing. No black boxes.


- **Analysis Summaries** - As the agent processes information, you'll see its reasoning and intermediate conclusions-not just the final output.


## Meet Your Agents


Every agent in Doe gets assigned a friendly name like Adrian, Richard, Anna, and more. These aren't just labels; they help you track who did what across your session.


When you're reviewing traces, you'll see which agent handled each task. In fact it's a searchable record in itself, so you can ask what each agent has done independently.


Want to find all the research Adrian did on competitor pricing? You can simply ask for it. Need to pull up every source Anna cited in her market analysis? It's all there.


## Built for Auditability


The Trace Panel isn't just about curiosity. It's about trust.


For teams working in regulated industries or handling sensitive decisions, being able to audit AI-assisted work is essential. The Trace Panel creates a complete record of how conclusions were reached, which sources informed them, and what logic connected the dots.


This means you can:


- **Verify accuracy** - Check that the agent used authoritative sources and interpreted them correctly.


- **Debug issues** - If something looks wrong in the output, trace back to find where things went sideways.


- **Build confidence** - Show stakeholders exactly how the work was done, not just the result.


## Available Now


The Trace Panel is live for all Doe users. Open any task and you'll see the panel alongside your results, showing the full journey from request to completion.


We believe AI agents should be partners you can trust-and trust requires transparency. The Trace Panel is our commitment to keeping you in control.


Try it out and let us know what you think.
