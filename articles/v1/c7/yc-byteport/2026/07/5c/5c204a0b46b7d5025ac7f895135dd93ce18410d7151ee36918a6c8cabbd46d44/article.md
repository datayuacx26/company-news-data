## The Problem


Nearly every file uploaded to the internet still crawls through TCP, a **50-year-old protocol** that treats 4K video the exact same way it treats email packets.


TCP's reliability scheme limits its throughput inversely with network delay and packet loss. When network loss is detected, TCP reduces its congestion window **exponentially** . This aggressive back off and slow ramp up limit the TCP window size, severely limiting its long-term throughput and bandwidth utilization. On lossy cellular or satellite networks, this can limit throughput to well under **1% of the network capacity** .


In addition to this throughput limitation, traditional file transfer protocols fail to provide the **security and predictability** required by business-critical data transfers. Uploads, downloads, and transfers must be **fully resumable** and **protected from interception** .
