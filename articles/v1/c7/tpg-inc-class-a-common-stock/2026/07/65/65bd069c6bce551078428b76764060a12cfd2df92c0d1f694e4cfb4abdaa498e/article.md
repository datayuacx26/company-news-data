# TPG Launches Arcura Medical Properties Following Completion of Acquisition of Premier German and Dutch Medical Outpatient Portfolio

***Purpose-built medical outpatient properties platform suited for long-term growth through backing by experienced real estate investor***


***Simon Betty appointed CEO of Arcura***


**London, Berlin & San Francisco –** TPG Real Estate ("TPG") today announced the launch of Arcura Medical Properties **** (“Arcura”), a new, purpose-built European healthcare real estate platform designed to deliver high-quality clinical infrastructure and pursue growth opportunities driven by increasing demand for medical properties. The formation of Arcura follows the completion of TPG’s approximately €400 million acquisition of a portfolio of leading medical outpatient properties located across Germany and the Netherlands (the "Portfolio") from Vital Infrastructure Property Trust (formerly Northwest Healthcare Properties REIT).


Arcura will operate as an independent, specialized owner and operator of medical outpatient buildings (“MOBs”) and outpatient clinical infrastructure. Today, the platform consists of 30 properties across more than 180,000 square meters, serving more than 400 healthcare tenants in key regions including Berlin, Hamburg, and The Randstad. Arcura will manage the existing Portfolio, focusing on delivering premier tenant experiences through standards-led management, and targeted capital investment, while also pursuing disciplined growth opportunities in core European markets.


Simon Betty, who led Vital Infrastructure Property Trust’s Europe organization, will serve as Arcura’s CEO. Betty has more than two decades of real estate investment and leadership expertise across several global markets, and will oversee an established, local executive team leading all asset management, leasing, and growth initiatives. Richard Mully, a tenured real estate investor and operator with more than three decades of experience, will serve as Chairman.


Simon Betty, Chief Executive Officer of Arcura Medical Properties, said: “Arcura is purpose-built to meet growing demand for high-quality medical real estate, serving as a partner of choice to tenants and as a long-term steward of premier medical outpatient properties in attractive and growing markets. Our focus is on delivering bespoke, high-performing environments for medical practitioners, and infrastructure that supports specialist practices, diagnostics, and clinical services across the full acuity spectrum of care. We are very constructive on the outlook for the outpatient medical sector and are delighted to partner with TPG.”


Ben Deeg, Business Unit Partner at TPG, said: “As healthcare delivery across Europe increasingly shifts toward outpatient and ambulatory settings, demand for well-located, professionally managed medical infrastructure continues to grow. This opportunity aligns TPG’s focus on partnering with great management teams to build and enhance platforms that provide differentiated value to the tenants they serve. We are pleased to partner with Simon and Arcura’s experienced team to scale the platform and bring institutional capabilities to a real estate sector benefiting from significant growth, and as we look ahead, see a clear pathway to building the leading pan-European medical outpatient platform through targeted acquisitions in attractive markets.”


**About Arcura Medical Properties**


Arcura Medical Properties is a leading European healthcare properties platform focused on the full spectrum of medical outpatient real estate. Arcura owns and manages multi-tenant medical buildings that support providers to deliver clinical and surgical care closer to the community. The platform is built for long-duration performance, combining disciplined acquisition with a standards-led approach to leasing and asset management that helps connect tenants to high-quality, well-located medical outpatient offerings.
