# Bridgford Foods Corporation Announces Director Resignation

ANAHEIM, Calif., March 24, 2014 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Bridgford Foods Corporation (Nasdaq:BRID) today announced that Allan Bridgford Jr. resigned as a member of the Board of Directors of Bridgford Foods Corporation. The resignation was not the result of a disagreement with management regarding operations, policies or practices of the Company.


The Registrant intends to begin a search for a new member of the Board of Directors as soon as practicable.
