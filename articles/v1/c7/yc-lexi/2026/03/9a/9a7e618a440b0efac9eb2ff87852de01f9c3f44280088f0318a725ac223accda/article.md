A solo lawyer took on one of the world’s largest hospitality groups **Accor** in a consumer dispute involving unfair practices and financial claims.


The complaint was formally admitted before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, marking the beginning of what would typically be a long, resource-intensive process.


But this case unfolded differently.


Instead of months of fragmented research and manual drafting, the lawyer relied on **Lexi** .
