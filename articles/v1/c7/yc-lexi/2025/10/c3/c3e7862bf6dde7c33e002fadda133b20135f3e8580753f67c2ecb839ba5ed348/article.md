# Lexi Takes the Stage at TechCrunch Disrupt: Showcasing the Future of AI-Powered Legal Work


Lexi Team


October 28, 2025


2 min read


This week, Lexi joined the spotlight at the Global Founders Panel, an official TechCrunch Disrupt side event hosted by the Swedish-American Chamber of Commerce in San Francisco and Silicon Valley. Held at FOCUS Innovation Studio in downtown San Francisco, the evening gathered investors, founders, and innovators shaping the next wave of intelligent automation.


### Bringing Legal AI to the Global Stage


Representing the **Y Combinator Fall 2025 (F25)** batch, **Lexi** was featured alongside other pioneering startups—Zalos, Koyal, Marselia Robotics and Captain—each tackling industry-defining problems through artificial intelligence.


Lexi’s presentation highlighted how **AI-powered legal associates** can transform the way legal teams operate—helping law firms and in-house departments draft, research, and analyze cases with the same precision and ethical care expected of a human associate, but with exponential speed and scalability.


### Expanding Capacity, Empowering Lawyers


At the event, Lexi’s team demonstrated how its platform streamlines end-to-end legal workflows:


-


**Automated drafting and research** that adapt to firm-specific standards.


-


**Chronology and document management** that understand matter context.


-


**Ethical guardrails** ensuring every output remains confidential, compliant, and verifiable.


As CEO **Harshit Garg** explained, *“We’re not replacing lawyers—we’re giving them leverage. Lexi acts as a dependable teammate that understands context, maintains audit trails, and scales with the workload.”*


### Why This Moment Matters


TechCrunch Disrupt has long been the place where next-generation technologies meet mainstream attention.
For Lexi, being featured in this global forum underscores a key reality: **AI is no longer peripheral to legal practice—it’s becoming the core infrastructure** .


Across jurisdictions like the US, India, Australia and Singapore, legal professionals are turning to Lexi to manage ever-growing workloads while upholding the highest standards of accuracy and client care.


### What’s Next


Following the showcase, Lexi will continue its North America roadshow, meeting with law firms, corporate legal departments and innovation partners to expand pilot deployments and gather feedback from early adopters.


If you’re attending TechCrunch Disrupt 2025 or based in the Bay Area, join the conversation and see how Lexi is redefining what’s possible for modern legal teams.
