# FreeCast Adds Sportoutdoor.TV to Its Free Channel Line-up - Press Release - FreeCast

FreeCast is announcing the launch of Sportoutdoor.TV on its free streaming platform. The outdoor sport FAST channel is the latest addition to the growing service, and is now available to FreeCast users world-wide at no additional cost.


With 30 years of experience producing TV shows in Italy, Sportoutdoor.TV is the first 24/7 HD English-language channel dedicated entirely to the outdoors worlds, sports, mountains, cruises and tourism. All of the channel’s programs feature original content, expertly filmed to deliver thrilling action and unforgettable adventure. The channel covers a wide range of sports—from sailing and boating to mountain biking, an array of winter sports and much more. Besides outdoors sports, the channel also features 12 hours a day of cruise and tourism programs, showcasing the most beautiful destinations in the world, explored onboard the most beautiful cruise ships in the world.


Tracy West, FreeCast’s Executive Vice President of Digital Content, discussed the value of sports-focused channels to the company’s strategy. “Sportoutdoor.TV is a fantastic addition for us because it features the types of sports that average people often participate in. Biking, boating, skiing, and more are popular hobbies, and programming from the perspective of those who participate in them instantly connects with millions. There’s a unique value in that, and we’re able to offer that type of high quality content for free.”


“We're very excited to be launching our Sportoutdoor.TV channel on FreeCast. We know our engaging and high quality TV shows will be a great addition to the FreeCast FAST Channels library, and we're very optimistic about the additional reach that FreeCast can give us.” said Greta Omoboni, FAST Channel Manager for Sportoutdoor.TV.
