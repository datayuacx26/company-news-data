# The Boat Show Channel Launches on FreeCast’s Lineup

FreeCast is announcing a new agreement with Boat Show Television, Inc. to bring the Boat Show Channel to FreeCast’s lineup of free channels. The Boat Show Channel is now live on the service, joining hundreds of free linear channels and hundreds of thousands of on-demand movies and TV episodes.


The Boat Show Channel is a nautical lifestyle channel, with a spotlight on yachting. The channel features tours of luxury yachts with deep dives into the interiors and technologies that make them so special, and the latest innovations and trends in high performance boating.


FreeCast’s Executive Vice President of Digital Content, Tracy West, talked about the value of lifestyle channels to FreeCast. “The Boat Show Channel has broad appeal; all kinds of viewers can enjoy a glimpse into the world of yachts and luxury travel. In many parts of the country, particularly coastal areas, boating is such a part of the culture. With value to such a diverse group of audiences, this channel a great addition to our offering.”


“The launch of the Boat Show channel represents a significant step in bringing our premium nautical content to a broader audience. This collaboration with FreeCast allows us to showcase the best of the yachting world, from incredible reviews to exclusive insights, directly to passionate viewers worldwide.” Said Federico Cozza, Founder & CEO of Boat Show Television, Inc.
