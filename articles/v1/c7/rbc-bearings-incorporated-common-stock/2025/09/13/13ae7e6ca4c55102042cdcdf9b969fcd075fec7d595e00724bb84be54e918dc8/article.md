# RBC Bearings Announces Participation in Upcoming Investor Conferences

OXFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Sep. 3, 2025-- RBC Bearings Incorporated (NYSE: RBC), a leading international manufacturer of highly engineered precision bearings, components and essential systems for the industrial, defense and aerospace industries, today announced that it will participate in the Alembic Global Advisors 3rd Annual Torrey Pines Conference in La Jolla, CA on September 9, 2025, and the Morgan Stanley 13th Annual Laguna Conference on September 10, 2025 in Dana Point, CA. The Company will be represented by Chief Operating Officer, Daniel Bergeron, and Chief Financial Officer, Robert Sullivan. Materials shared during the conference will be available online at:[https://investor.rbcbearings.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.rbcbearings.com&esheet=54318744&newsitemid=20250903317712&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.rbcbearings.com&index=1&md5=cbd9dbc4102b722cca42a6484616ae90) .


**About RBC Bearings**


RBC Bearings Incorporated is an international manufacturer and marketer of highly engineered precision bearings, components and essential systems. The Company is primarily focused on producing highly technical or regulated bearing products and components requiring sophisticated design, testing, and manufacturing capabilities for the diversified industrial, aerospace, and defense markets. The Company is headquartered in Oxford, Connecticut.
