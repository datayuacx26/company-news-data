# Poop Tracker vs Aidy for Crohn's: Stool Log or Full Picture?

Poop Tracker's listing emphasizes Crohn's stool detail, while Aidy connects bowel entries to broader monitoring and treatment roles documented in a recent[IBD app review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39807416/) .

Poop Tracker's claimed exception is maximum bowel-movement detail, custom health fields, or native Android access.

## Poop Tracker Wins on Bowel-Movement Detail


This Poop Tracker Stool IBS Crohns review starts with its clear strength. The[Apple listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/poop-tracker-stool-ibs-crohns/id1613642833) claims Bristol type, shape, color, smell, volume, pain, urgency, blood, mucus, food, gas, straining, notes, and bathroom time. It also advertises 30-plus charts, Apple Health, exports, caregiver sharing, and Apple device support. That is a detailed Crohn's bowel movement app.


That claimed depth suits someone whose gastroenterologist has requested a[blood mucus stool log](https://aidyhq.com/learn/blood-in-stool-with-crohns-when-its-expected-vs-when-its-a-red-flag) or a precise account of urgency and bathroom time. The listing also advertises optional symptom, medication, nutrition, activity, sleep, mood, and measurement trackers that can widen the record.[CareClinic's feature page](https://careclinic.io/features/) describes appointments, labs, documents, family profiles, reminders, and correlations. Users must decide which modules matter and consistently complete them. Granularity has value only when the resulting record remains sustainable and readable.


## A Stool Description Cannot Measure Inflammation


The Bristol scale gives stool form a shared vocabulary. A[validation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27492648/) found substantial accuracy and reliability overall, while also finding classification difficulty around several adjacent types. Poop Tracker's listing claims it adds context that Bristol alone misses, including urgency, bleeding, pain, and frequency. Those fields can help a patient describe change clearly.


Stool observations still answer a limited question. Research comparing Crohn's stool frequency and abdominal pain with endoscopic activity found only moderate correlations overall ([study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31644790/) ). The[AGA Crohn's biomarker guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37981354/) recommends[combining symptoms and biomarkers](https://aidyhq.com/learn/treat-to-target-ibd-what-patients-need-to-know) in several monitoring situations.[NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/crohns-disease/diagnosis) likewise describes blood tests, laboratory stool tests, endoscopy, and imaging as tools used to assess Crohn's. Photos, colors, and app charts cannot substitute for those tests. Both products are self-tracking aids, so worsening or concerning changes belong in a clinical conversation.


## Aidy Connects Stool to Crohn's Treatment


Aidy connects Bristol type to[meals, food-symptom associations, symptoms, adherence, and doctor-ready reports](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) . It supports biologic induction and maintenance, dose countdowns, reminders, injection-site rotation, post-dose check-ins, and route or interval changes. Poop Tracker claims medicine logging and reminders but does not describe a comparable biologic workflow.


Aidy calculates a modified[Harvey-Bradshaw Index](https://aidyhq.com/learn/crohns-harvey-bradshaw-index-what-your-hbi-means) . HBI is symptom-oriented and has an imperfect relationship with the more complex Crohn's Disease Activity Index ([validation research](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16633052/) ). It cannot prove inflammation, but it organizes bowel, pain, and wellbeing into a consistent trend.[NIDDK's treatment overview](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/crohns-disease/treatment) describes medicines and surgery among Crohn's options. Aidy follows that timeline directly.


## Sharing, Privacy, and Logging Burden Differ


Poop Tracker claims exports, caregiver access, Apple Health imports, labs, and modules beyond digestion, an advantage for multiple conditions. Aidy's narrower reports present meals, symptoms, stool, activity scores, medication, and biologic history for an IBD visit. A broad health record favors Poop Tracker; a concise Crohn's story favors Aidy.


Privacy disclosures also differ. Apple's Poop Tracker label says health, contact, user-content, identifier, usage, sensitive, and diagnostic data may be collected and linked to identity. CareClinic claims health data uses encrypted storage and that records are encrypted locally ([security explanation](https://start.careclinic.io/knowledgebase/privacy-and-security/health-data-protected-encrypted/296/) ). Aidy data can be exported or deleted, is encrypted, carries no ads, and is not sold ([Aidy website](https://aidyhq.com/) ). Users should review both current policies before sharing caregiver access or longitudinal health data.

Poop Tracker is the better specialist for people who truly need every claimed stool attribute, Apple Watch entry, or a broad caregiver record. Aidy is the better Poop Tracker alternative for everyday Crohn's management because bowel movements remain connected to diet, adherence, biologic logistics, HBI trends, and GI-ready reporting. For the full disease picture, Aidy wins.
