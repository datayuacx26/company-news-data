# Tolly vs Aidy for UC: Flare Risk or IBD Scores?

Tolly's listing centers food-first “flare risk,” while Aidy connects meals to broader monitoring, education, and treatment roles documented across digital care in an[IBD app review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39807416/) .


**Quick answer:** Choose Aidy as the primary UC tracker because its free workflow connects bleeding, urgency, stool, meals, adherence, biologic schedules, SCCAI trends, and doctor-ready reports. Tolly is the genuine exception for someone who primarily wants its claimed food scanning, nutrition scoring, cross-condition diary, or native Android experience.

## What Tolly currently does well


Tolly's[current Apple listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) shows an iPhone and iPad app at version 1.4.4, dated August 7. It advertises unlimited free barcode scanning as the[standout feature](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) . The listing claims Tolly[checks packaged-food ingredients](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) against its digestive-trigger database, assigns a 0-to-100 score, and labels ingredients green, yellow, or red with explanations.


The listing claims[Premium adds](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) a restaurant tool. A user photographs a menu, then[Tolly claims to estimate likely ingredients](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) , score dishes, and suggest possible modifications. The same[listing describes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) meals, drinks, alcohol, sleep, stress, menstrual cycles, Bristol stool type, urgency, frequency, color, medicines, supplements, and more than 15 symptoms. It also claims to[offer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) 7-, 30-, and 90-day PDF reports, a hydration-oriented flare mode, breathing exercises, and artificial-intelligence pattern summaries. For quick decisions in a grocery aisle or restaurant, Tolly has the sharper proposed workflow.


## Tolly's flare risk and Aidy's SCCAI answer different questions


As a UC flare risk app,[Tolly claims to generate a daily score](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) from 0 to 100 using the previous three days of food, sleep, stress, alcohol, bowel, and menstrual-cycle entries. Its listing claims that product-defined forecast comes with specific guidance. The same[listing classifies Tolly](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) as general education and personal tracking and disclaims medical-device, diagnosis, advice, and treatment status. The number therefore functions as a[proprietary wellness prompt](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) .


Aidy[automatically calculates the Simple Clinical Colitis Activity Index](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) , or SCCAI, from UC logs over time. The[original SCCAI study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9771402/) evaluated a[defined symptom index](https://aidyhq.com/learn/sccai-score-for-ulcerative-colitis-what-the-questions-mean) covering daytime and nighttime bowel frequency, urgency, blood, general well-being, and extracolonic features, finding strong correlations with established complex assessments. A[patient-completed SCCAI study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23269224/) found substantial agreement with clinician scoring. Separate[patient-reported UC research](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24810138/) validated a measure built from stool frequency, rectal bleeding, and general well-being. These[studies establish clinical lineage](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9771402/) for structured symptom scores.[Aidy remains a self-tracking tool](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) .


## Food scores are useful context for UC


For an ulcerative colitis food scanner, Tolly's[listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) claims unlimited barcode checks for quick packaged-food research and a premium menu camera for restaurant choices. Its claimed restaurant results rely on estimated ingredients, while the color-coded food ratings are Tolly's judgments across a broad population that includes IBS, IBD, reflux, stomas, and food intolerances.


That scope matters.[National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases guidance](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/ulcerative-colitis/eating-diet-nutrition) says research has not established specific foods as causes of UC symptoms or worsening symptoms. The[agency still suggests](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/ulcerative-colitis/eating-diet-nutrition) that clinicians may recommend a food diary to[identify personal symptom associations](https://aidyhq.com/learn/ibd-food-triggers-vs-food-intolerances-what-tracking-can-and-cannot-show) . Tolly's scans can generate hypotheses for meal choices. Aidy's[meal-symptom correlations](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) can preserve what happened afterward alongside the rest of a UC record. Neither type of association[establishes intestinal inflammation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36822736/) .


## Treatment tracking gives Aidy the stronger UC core


Tolly's listing claims its[treatment layer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) records daily medicines and supplements, compares symptoms on days they are taken, and places the results in a shareable report. That is useful for a broad gut journal. Aidy's[IBD-specific workflow](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) adds configurable[biologic induction and maintenance schedules](https://aidyhq.com/learn/what-to-track-during-induction-and-maintenance-treatment) , reminders, injection-site rotation, infusion and injection check-ins, next-dose countdowns, adherence trends, SCCAI trends, and doctor-ready reports.


The distinction has clinical relevance.[NIDDK explains that UC treatment](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/ulcerative-colitis/treatment) depends on disease severity and the amount of colon affected, and medicines are used to reduce inflammation and induce or maintain remission. An[American Gastroenterological Association guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36822736/) recommends[combining symptoms with biomarkers](https://aidyhq.com/learn/treat-to-target-ibd-what-patients-need-to-know) for monitoring established UC. Aidy[organizes the treatment and symptom side](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) of that picture more completely. SCCAI trends remain patient-reported context, while[biomarkers and clinical assessment address inflammation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36822736/) .


## Tolly premium price and the overall verdict


Tolly's[published pricing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) claims free unlimited barcode scanning. Apple lists Premium at $7.99 monthly or $29.99 yearly and says it adds[restaurant scans, flare predictions, advanced tracking, reports, AI insights, flare mode, and saved foods](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tolly-ibs-ibd-companion/id6761734618) .


[Aidy is free with no paid subscriptions](https://aidyhq.com/) , so it supplies the more complete free primary UC record.


Tolly is the better food-scanning complement for grocery and restaurant choices. Aidy is the stronger primary Tolly alternative because it[connects symptoms and meals](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) with biologics, adherence, SCCAI trends, education, and reports. Tolly's claimed free scanner has value; Aidy's whole-condition structure wins the Tolly vs Aidy IBD app decision.


That distinction matters.
