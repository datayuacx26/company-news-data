# Tract vs Aidy: Which IBD Tracker Are You Comparing?

Two separate Tract gut apps appear in Apple's store, so choosing against Aidy starts with identifying which listing you actually mean before comparing their features.


**Quick answer:** Choose Aidy as the primary UC tracker because its free workflow clearly connects symptoms, stool, meals, adherence, biologic schedules, SCCAI trends, and doctor-ready reports under one established product identity. A Tract app is the genuine exception when its specific claimed feature, such as ostomy support, Apple Health, or guided food reintroduction, is essential.

Aidy is included in the Crohn's & Colitis Foundation's[Helpful Apps and Digital Health Services directory](https://www.crohnscolitisfoundation.org/helpful-apps) . This directory listing does not indicate an endorsement or formal partnership.


## First identify which Tract app you found


The first IBD listing is[Tract: IBD & IBS Tracker by USMAN DAWOOD MUHAMMAD](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tract-ibd-ibs-tracker/id6751367224) , App Store ID 6751367224. Apple lists an iPhone app whose description claims coverage for inflammatory bowel disease, irritable bowel syndrome, low-FODMAP tracking, and ostomy care.


The second is[Tract: IBD, IBS & Gut Health by RORY BOKSER](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tract-ibd-ibs-gut-health/id6759877270) , App Store ID 6759877270. Apple lists iPhone and iPad support. The developer's[Tract Health press kit](https://www.tract.health/press) describes a Montreal-built food-plan app founded by Bokser, who lives with ulcerative colitis.


A third current listing,[Tract: IBS Food Tracker by Ankur Kansal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tract-ibs-food-tracker/id6761203554) , App Store ID 6761203554, focuses on meal photos, bowel logs, and ingredient correlations for IBS. Its listing positions it as an IBS tracker, outside the two IBD-oriented comparisons. A useful Tract IBD app review should therefore name the developer and store ID before discussing features.


## What the two IBD-focused Tract apps do well


The Usman Dawood Muhammad app has the widest specialist tracking menu. Its[listing documents](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tract-ibd-ibs-tracker/id6751367224) more than 30 symptoms, Bristol Stool Chart entries, customizable dashboard cards, a FODMAP database, medication reminders, appointment scheduling, Apple Health syncing,[ostomy output and skin logs](https://aidyhq.com/learn/after-ibd-surgery-what-to-put-in-a-daily-recovery-log) , plus PDF and CSV exports. That makes it a strong option when granular FODMAP or ostomy records are decisive.


Rory Bokser's Tract takes a food-first approach. Its[listing documents](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tract-ibd-ibs-gut-health/id6759877270) meal and stool photos, voice logging, diet scores, weekly AI summaries,[structured food reintroduction](https://aidyhq.com/learn/a-two-week-food-reintroduction-plan-after-an-ibd-flare) , tolerated-food lists, generated recipes, and history exports. It supports low-FODMAP and several other structured eating approaches. This specialization has a legitimate use case: a[peer-reviewed review of FODMAP research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11103764/) reports that low-FODMAP diets have improved overall gut symptoms in studies of people with quiescent IBD and IBS-like symptoms. The scores describe logged food and symptoms.[Assessing intestinal inflammation requires clinical monitoring](https://gi.org/journals-publications/ebgi/alkazzi_aug2025/) .


## Where Aidy gives ulcerative colitis a clearer workflow


Aidy narrows its scope to Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis. It includes[meal photos, symptom and treatment tracking, trend analysis, educational material, and physician-friendly reports](https://aidyhq.com/) . Aidy also includes[biologic dose countdowns, configurable reminders, injection-site rotation, post-dose check-ins, medication adherence summaries, food-symptom correlations, and Simple Clinical Colitis Activity Index trends](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) .


That combination fits what many people want from an ulcerative colitis tracker. Diarrhea, rectal bleeding, abdominal pain, and urgency are among the[symptoms identified by the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/ulcerative-colitis/symptoms-causes) . Aidy brings those daily observations together with treatment timing and reporting. Its[SCCAI trend](https://aidyhq.com/learn/sccai-score-for-ulcerative-colitis-what-the-questions-mean) is based on a[published clinical symptom index](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9771402/) , giving the log a UC-specific structure. For someone seeking a Tract app alternative centered on treatment continuity and appointment preparation, Aidy's workflow is clearer and requires fewer choices during setup.


## Price, privacy, and the practical choice


The Tract IBS app price depends on which listing you mean. The[Usman Dawood Muhammad app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tract-ibd-ibs-tracker/id6751367224) lists a free download and Pro at $4.99 monthly or $39.99 yearly.[Rory Bokser's Tract](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tract-ibd-ibs-gut-health/id6759877270) claims a subscription after any eligible trial and lists $14.99 monthly or $49.99 yearly, with an introductory annual offer.[Ankur Kansal's IBS-only app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tract-ibs-food-tracker/id6761203554) is listed as free. Store prices can change.


[Aidy is free, has no paid patient subscription, runs no ads, and does not sell user data](https://aidyhq.com/) . It offers an[iPhone app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) and a text-message interface. Both IBD Tract listings claim that health data is not sold, although their storage and Apple privacy disclosures differ, so each current policy deserves review before use.


Whichever app you choose, symptom and food logs remain one part of[UC monitoring](https://aidyhq.com/learn/uc-monitoring-plan-labs-stool-tests-and-when-colonoscopy-frequency-changes) . The[2025 American College of Gastroenterology guidance](https://gi.org/journals-publications/ebgi/alkazzi_aug2025/) combines patient-reported bleeding, bowel habits, and urgency with biomarkers and endoscopic assessment. The[Crohn's & Colitis Foundation symptom tracker](https://www.crohnscolitisfoundation.org/sites/default/files/legacy/assets/pdfs/ibd-symptom-tracker.pdf) likewise recommends a daily diary that can be brought to appointments. Usman's Tract is compelling for FODMAP, Apple Health, or ostomy depth. Bokser's Tract suits structured food-plan work. Aidy is the stronger free default for an integrated ulcerative colitis treatment and reporting record.
