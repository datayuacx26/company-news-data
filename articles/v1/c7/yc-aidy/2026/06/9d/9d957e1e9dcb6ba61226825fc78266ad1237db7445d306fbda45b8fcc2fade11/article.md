# Ulcerative Colitis Tracker vs Aidy: PUCAI or SCCAI?

Ulcerative Colitis Tracker's listing centers on PUCAI, a score developed for[pediatric UC](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17681163/) , while Aidy supports broader[symptom and treatment tracking](https://aidyhq.com/learn/does-symptom-tracking-actually-help-with-ibd) for adults with UC.


**Quick answer:** Choose Aidy as the primary adult UC tracker because it connects SCCAI trends with symptoms, stool, meals, adherence, biologic schedules, and doctor-ready reports for free. Ulcerative Colitis Tracker is the genuine exception for a caregiver or clinical conversation specifically centered on its claimed pediatric PUCAI workflow.

## Identify the exact Ulcerative Colitis Tracker app


This Ulcerative Colitis Tracker app review covers x11systems LLC.[Apple's listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulcerative-colitis-tracker/id1664896729) shows a June 2024 iPhone and iPad release. A previously indexed Android package returned HTTP 404 when checked in August 2026. Apple is the only verified live native storefront; cached Android results are stale evidence.


The[published interface](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulcerative-colitis-tracker/id1664896729) is deliberately simple. Apple describes PUCAI, symptom, food, medication, and vital-sign tracking that can be shared with a doctor. Its official screenshots show separate Score, Food, Medications, Vitals, and Notes records, plus reminders and a calendar that groups days by activity level. Temperature, respiratory rate, blood pressure, weight, and height appear under Vitals. Neither storefront describes a PDF, CSV, automated food correlation, or detailed biologic schedule.


## PUCAI and SCCAI capture different UC details


[PUCAI combines abdominal pain, rectal bleeding](https://aidyhq.com/learn/pucai-score-pediatric-ulcerative-colitis-activity-explained) , stool consistency, stool frequency, nocturnal stools that cause waking, and activity level, as shown in a[peer-reviewed pediatric IBD review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5470346/) . Current[ESPGHAN-ECCO guidance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40677018/) continues to use PUCAI in pediatric UC management. That makes Ulcerative Colitis Tracker a logical PUCAI tracker app when a child's care team already follows that index.


[SCCAI records daytime and nighttime bowel frequency, urgency, blood, wellbeing, and extraintestinal features](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9771402/) . Its development work compared it with complex clinical and laboratory indices; a later[patient-reported study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4137887/) enrolled adults. That evidence gives Aidy the clearer adult score fit. The[PUCAI components](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5470346/) add pain, stool form, and activity limitation;[SCCAI adds urgency and extraintestinal symptoms](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9771402/) .


Age still needs nuance. A[patient-completed PUCAI study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21593644/) included ages 4 to 29 and found strong agreement with physician scores. Its[intended pediatric context](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40677018/) should still guide interpretation.


## Aidy adds treatment and pattern context


Ulcerative Colitis Tracker's listing claims it covers the basics: a caregiver can record a PUCAI score, meals, medication, vitals, notes, and calendar history in one sparse interface. Its screenshots show a food screen accepting text and a photo. The storefront does not advertise correlations, custom symptoms, education, or a defined report format.


[Aidy adds common and custom symptoms, Bristol Stool Chart entries, meals by text or photo, food-symptom correlations, medication adherence, SCCAI trends, doctor-ready reports, source-backed education, and artificial intelligence chat](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) . It also includes[biologic induction and maintenance schedules, advance reminders, injection-site rotation, and post-dose check-ins](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) . That treatment depth makes Aidy the stronger primary adult tracker.


Food entries remain[observations](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/ulcerative-colitis/eating-diet-nutrition) .[NIDDK says](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/ulcerative-colitis/eating-diet-nutrition) researchers have not found that specific foods worsen UC, although a diary may identify foods that seem related to someone's symptoms. Under[that guidance](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/ulcerative-colitis/eating-diet-nutrition) , neither a field labeled “trigger food” nor a[calculated correlation proves causation](https://aidyhq.com/learn/ibd-food-triggers-vs-food-intolerances-what-tracking-can-and-cannot-show) .


## Free status, privacy, platforms, and updates


As a free colitis symptom tracker, Ulcerative Colitis Tracker has a simple published price story:[Apple marks it Free](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulcerative-colitis-tracker/id1664896729) and shows no in-app-purchase catalog.[Aidy is also free, runs no ads, and has no paid user subscriptions](https://aidyhq.com/) . Aidy therefore matches the competitor on cost while offering a broader adult UC workflow.


Privacy favors Ulcerative Colitis Tracker.[Apple reports “Data Not Collected”](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulcerative-colitis-tracker/id1664896729) , based on the developer's disclosure.[Apple's Aidy label](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) lists data that may be linked to identity. Platform reach is mixed: Apple says Ulcerative Colitis Tracker supports iPhone and iPad, while Aidy's native listing is iPhone-only and[SMS access](https://aidyhq.com/) is available. No live Android listing was verified for either product.


Ulcerative Colitis Tracker's[iOS version](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulcerative-colitis-tracker/id1664896729) remains dated June 2024, and its former Android URL no longer resolves.[Aidy's Apple history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) shows later releases. Update dates establish recency, not quality.


## Which UC score and app fit best?


[Ulcerative Colitis Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ulcerative-colitis-tracker/id1664896729) is better for caregivers whose pediatric teams[use PUCAI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40677018/) . Its listing claims a free, minimal app with developer-reported privacy, iPad support, vitals, and notes for appointment context.


For most adults,[Aidy](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aidy-manage-crohns-colitis/id6747647744) is the better primary[SCCAI tracker app](https://aidyhq.com/learn/sccai-score-for-ulcerative-colitis-what-the-questions-mean) and UC record. Its[adult-validated SCCAI](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4137887/) includes urgency and extraintestinal symptoms, while Aidy connects that score with meals, correlations,[biologic schedules](https://aidyhq.com/learn/ibd-medication-calendar-infusions-injections-labs-refills-and-prior-authorization-dates) , tolerability checks, reports, and education. That wider free toolkit is its decisive advantage.


Neither symptom score diagnoses UC or confirms bowel inflammation;[NIDDK describes](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/ulcerative-colitis/diagnosis) diagnosis using history, examination, blood and stool tests, and endoscopy.[AGA guidance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36822736/) favors combining[symptoms with biomarkers](https://aidyhq.com/learn/treat-to-target-ibd-what-patients-need-to-know) for monitoring. Score fit matters, and Aidy's broader adult workflow wins this comparison.
