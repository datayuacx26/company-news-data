# AI and Automation: Andrew Wells Of Sorting Robotics On How To Effectively Harness AI Technology In People Operations

**Authority Magazine -** 12/23/24


**Ask questions about AI to potential new hires.** We value candidates who make it through our hiring process to our on-site interviews. These onsite interviews typically have a presentation component and it is always a positive for us when someone explains how and why they used AI as a tool to complete the project. Be mindful, however, about having AI do all the work. Trust me we know what to look for.
