# Rolling in Opportunities: The Two Biggest Job Market Catalysts in Cannabis


Jun 17


Written By[Sorting Robotics Inc.](https://www.sortingrobotics.com/press?author=60529097057b3955726f94a9)


**RollingStone -** 3/31/25


Is working in cannabis a cool job now? In 2024, the legal cannabis industry saw a[significant uptick](https://www.vangst.com/2024-jobs-report) in jobs, gaining 22,952 additional positions. Today, there are approximately 440,445 full-time equivalent jobs in the cannabis space.
