# Tech Jam 2024: Powering Up Cannabis Tech Connections During the Industry’s Busiest Conference Week

**\[Las Vegas, NV\]** . For the second year in a row,[Tech Jam](https://partiful.com/e/bfogov83Ufbee6l7nrcj) , the esteemed cannabis technology networking event, hosted by[Green Lane Communication](https://www.greenlanecommunication.com/) ,[Sorting Robotics](https://www.sortingrobotics.com/) ,[Canix](https://www.canix.com/) ,[RollPros](https://rollpros.com/) ,[LeafyPack](https://leafypack.com/) , and[First Citizens Bank](https://www.firstcitizens.com/) , will kick off one of the cannabis industry’s busiest weeks in Las Vegas as a precursor to the[13th annual MJBizCon](https://mjbizconference.com/) , the world’s largest cannabis business conference and tradeshow. Tech Jam will take place on Tuesday, December 3rd, from 5:30 PM to 9:30 PM PST at[Jammyland Cocktail Bar & Reggae Kitchen](https://jammy.land/)
