# Tech Jam 2024: Powering Up Cannabis Tech Connections During MJBizCon

For the second year in a row,[Tech Jam](https://partiful.com/e/bfogov83Ufbee6l7nrcj) , the esteemed cannabis technology networking event, hosted by Green Lane Communication, Sorting Robotics, Canix, RollPros, LeafyPack, and First Citizens Bank, will kick off one of the cannabis industry’s busiest weeks in Las Vegas as a precursor to the 13th annual MJBizCon, the world’s largest cannabis business conference and trade show. Tech Jam will take place on Tuesday, December 3rd, from 5:30 PM to 9:30 PM PST at Jammyland Cocktail Bar & Reggae Kitchen
