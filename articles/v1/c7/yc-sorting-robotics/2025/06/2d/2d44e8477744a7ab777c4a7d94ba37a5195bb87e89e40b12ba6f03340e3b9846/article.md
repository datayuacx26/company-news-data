# Tech Jam 2024: Powering Up Cannabis Tech Connections During the Industry’s Busiest Conference Week

### Tech Jam returns to Las Vegas, bringing together cannabis tech innovators and industry leaders to shape tomorrow’s cannabis landscape.


**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**


Mike Mejer


Mike@GreenLaneCommunication.com


**\[Las Vegas, NV\]** — For the second year in a row,[Tech Jam](https://partiful.com/e/bfogov83Ufbee6l7nrcj) , the esteemed cannabis technology networking event, hosted by[Green Lane Communication](https://www.greenlanecommunication.com/) ,[Sorting Robotics](https://www.sortingrobotics.com/) ,[Canix](https://www.canix.com/) ,[RollPros](https://rollpros.com/) ,[LeafyPack](https://leafypack.com/) , and[First Citizens Bank](https://www.firstcitizens.com/) , will kick off one of the cannabis industry’s busiest weeks in Las Vegas as a precursor to the[13th annual MJBizCon](https://mjbizconference.com/) —the world’s largest cannabis business conference and tradeshow. Tech Jam will take place on Tuesday, December 3rd, from 5:30 PM to 9:30 PM PST at[Jammyland Cocktail Bar & Reggae Kitchen](https://jammy.land/) , located at 1121 S Main St, Las Vegas, NV 89104.


Tech Jam’s blend of laid-back vibes and candid conversations will set the tone for an action-packed conference week. It’s not just a pre-conference happy hour; it’s a unique event where groundbreaking ideas are born, relationships are built, and technology meets vision—Tech Jam focuses specifically on the technology shaping tomorrow’s cannabis landscape.


“We couldn’t be more excited to host Tech Jam 2024. In a complex, ever-changing regulatory environment, it’s critical to have best in class technology providers. We look forward to the conversation with operational leaders on how to support this incredible industry going forward,” said[Stacey Hronowski](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stacey-hronowski-3893a43a/) , Co-Founder & CEO of[Canix](https://www.canix.com/) .


Setting the stage as a relaxed yet high-energy gathering for cannabis industry leaders, tech enthusiasts, and innovators, Tech Jam creates an informal atmosphere where meaningful connections happen naturally and attendees can explore new tech solutions, discuss future AI and automation trends, and share insights.


“After the success of last year’s inaugural Tech Jam, we’re even more excited to bring together leaders from Canix, RollPros, Green Lane Communication, LeafyPack, and First Citizens Bank to elevate this year’s event. Tech Jam is where meaningful connections are made, ideas are sparked, and the cannabis industry’s most innovative technology gets its spotlight. We’re ready to connect, collaborate, and redefine what’s possible in cannabis tech,” said[Nohtal Partansky](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nohtal/) , Co-Founder & CEO of[Sorting Robotics](https://www.sortingrobotics.com/) .


“With cannabis CEOs in attendance and ready for interviews, Tech Jam also provides the media with a rare opportunity to engage with key voices in the industry,” says[Michael Mejer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelmejer/) , founder of[Green Lane Communication](https://www.greenlanecommunication.com/) . “For local media and national cannabis industry press alike, Tech Jam offers direct access to the conversations shaping the industry’s future.”

**About RollPros**


RollPros is the leader in pre-roll joint automation technology for the legal cannabis industry. Based out of Vancouver, WA, the company’s proprietary Blackbird Joint Rolling system mimics the compaction of hand-rolled joints while preserving the integrity of the flower’s essential ingredients. The company’s automation system helps cannabis businesses thrive by facilitating production of the industry’s highest-quality pre-rolls, optimizing production capacity, and freeing up key personnel to focus on critical business activities. RollPros is the first company to create a commercial joint rolling machine that does not require pre-made cones. For more information about RollPros, please visit[www.rollpros.com](http://www.rollpros.com/) .
