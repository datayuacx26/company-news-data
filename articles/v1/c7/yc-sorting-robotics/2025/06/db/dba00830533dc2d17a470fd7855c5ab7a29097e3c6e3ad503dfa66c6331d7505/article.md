# Tech Jam 2024: Powering Up Cannabis Tech Connections During MJBizCon

For the second year in a row,


[Tech Jam](https://partiful.com/e/bfogov83Ufbee6l7nrcj) , the esteemed cannabis technology networking event, hosted by Green Lane Communication, Sorting Robotics, Canix, RollPros, LeafyPack, and First Citizens Bank, will kick off one of the cannabis industry’s busiest weeks in Las Vegas as a precursor to the 13th annual MJBizCon—the world’s largest cannabis business conference and trade show. Tech Jam will take place on Tuesday, December 3rd, from 5:30 PM to 9:30 PM PST at Jammyland Cocktail Bar & Reggae Kitchen, located at 1121 S Main St, Las Vegas, NV 89104.


Tech Jam’s blend of laid-back vibes and candid conversations will set the tone for an action-packed conference week. It’s not just a pre-conference happy hour; it’s a unique event where groundbreaking ideas are born, relationships are built, and technology meets vision—Tech Jam focuses specifically on the technology shaping tomorrow’s cannabis landscape.


"We couldn't be more excited to host Tech Jam 2024. In a complex, ever-changing regulatory environment, it's critical to have best in class technology providers. We look forward to the conversation with operational leaders on how to support this incredible industry going forward," said Stacey Hronowski, Co-Founder & CEO of Canix.


Setting the stage as a relaxed yet high-energy gathering for cannabis industry leaders, tech enthusiasts, and innovators, Tech Jam creates an informal atmosphere where meaningful connections happen naturally and attendees can explore new tech solutions, discuss future AI and automation trends, and share insights.


"After the success of last year’s inaugural Tech Jam, we’re even more excited to bring together leaders from Canix, RollPros, Green Lane Communication, LeafyPack, and First Citizens Bank to elevate this year’s event. Tech Jam is where meaningful connections are made, ideas are sparked, and the cannabis industry’s most innovative technology gets its spotlight. We’re ready to connect, collaborate, and redefine what’s possible in cannabis tech," said Nohtal Partansky, Co-Founder & CEO of Sorting Robotics.


“With cannabis CEOs in attendance and ready for interviews, Tech Jam also provides the media with a rare opportunity to engage with key voices in the industry,” says Michael Mejer, founder of Green Lane Communication. “For local media and national cannabis industry press alike, Tech Jam offers direct access to the conversations shaping the industry’s future.”
