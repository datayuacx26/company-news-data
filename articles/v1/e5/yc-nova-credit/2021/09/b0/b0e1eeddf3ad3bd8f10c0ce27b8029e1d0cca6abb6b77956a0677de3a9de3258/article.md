Welcome to the Nova Credit Engineering blog![Nova Credit](https://novacredit.com/) is the premier cross-border bureau that helps newcomers and other global citizens apply for financial services using their international credit history from their home country. Our mission is to power a fair and inclusive financial system for the world. We are starting this engineering blog to shed some light on the interesting technical challenges that we face at Nova, and to give an inside look at what life is like within our engineering organization.


If you’re interested in joining the team, check out our[careers page](https://www.novacredit.com/careers) or[job board](https://jobs.lever.co/neednova) for current openings. You can also read more about our engineering values[here](https://www.keyvalues.com/nova-credit) .
