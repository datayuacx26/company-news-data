# Dark mode

[Back to All](https://docs.castle.io/changelog)


Added


Castle now supports dark mode.


The interface automatically adapts to your system's color scheme preference. For users who want manual control, you can override the automatic setting from the user menu. Choose between Light, Dark, or System (auto) at any time.
