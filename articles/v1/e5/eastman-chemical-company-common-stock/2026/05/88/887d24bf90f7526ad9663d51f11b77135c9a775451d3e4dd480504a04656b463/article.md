# PepsiCo and Eastman Collaborate on Recycled Bottles

- [Circular economy](https://www.eastman.com/content/eastman/corporate/us/en/media-center/discovery.html?tag=eastman-cms:corporate/media/discovery/circular-economy)
- [Packaging](https://www.eastman.com/content/eastman/corporate/us/en/media-center/discovery.html?tag=eastman-cms:corporate/media/discovery/packaging)
- [Partnerships](https://www.eastman.com/content/eastman/corporate/us/en/media-center/discovery.html?tag=eastman-cms:corporate/media/discovery/partnerships)
- [Recycling](https://www.eastman.com/content/eastman/corporate/us/en/media-center/discovery.html?tag=eastman-cms:corporate/media/discovery/recycling)
- [Renew](https://www.eastman.com/content/eastman/corporate/us/en/media-center/discovery.html?tag=eastman-cms:corporate/media/discovery/renew)


This multiyear agreement with Eastman is now yielding a tangible proof point: bottles made with Eastman PET Renew demonstrate how materials once headed for waste streams can be turned into high‑quality, specially designed beverage packaging.


Read an[in-depth story](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/stories/2026/turning-old-carpets-and-hard-to-recycle-waste-into-recycled-bottles) on PepsiCo.com to learn more about the company’s collaboration with Eastman to deliver high-quality, food-grade recycled beverage bottles — including for Gatorade. Eastman’s molecular recycling makes the new packaging possible and is a step in the right direction for packaging circularity.


*A multiyear agreement between Eastman and PepsiCo repurposes discarded hard-to-recycle polyester plastic waste in an effort to improve packaging circularity.*


Learn more about how[Eastman PET Renew](https://www.eastman.com/en/sustainability/sustainable-solutions/plastics/eastman-pet-renew) helps brands stand out with high-performance, sustainable packaging made from hard-to-recycle plastic waste reborn through molecular recycling.
