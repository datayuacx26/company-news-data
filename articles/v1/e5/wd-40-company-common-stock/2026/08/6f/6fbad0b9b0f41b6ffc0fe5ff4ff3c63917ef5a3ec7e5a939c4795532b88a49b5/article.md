# WD-40 Company to Participate in Water Tower Research Fireside Chat Series

[View All News](https://investor.wd40company.com/investors/press-releases/default.aspx)


### WD-40 Company to Participate in Water Tower Research Fireside Chat Series


August 17, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s201.q4cdn.com/722056013/files/doc_news/WD-40-Company-to-Participate-in-Water-Tower-Research-Fireside-Chat-Series-2026.pdf)


SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- WD-40 Company (NASDAQ:WDFC), a global marketing organization dedicated to creating positive lasting memories by developing and selling products that solve problems in workshops, factories and homes around the world, today announced that Steve Brass, president and chief executive officer, and Sara Hyzer, vice president, finance and chief financial officer, will participate in the Water Tower Research Fireside Chat Series, on Wednesday, August 19, 2026 at 8:00 a.m. PDT.


The discussion will be hosted by Linda Bolton Weiser, managing director, consumer, at Water Tower Research. The discussion is expected to focus on business performance, strategic initiatives, operational execution, and long-term growth opportunities. Other forward-looking or material information may also be discussed.


The event is open to the public. Interested participants may register through Water Tower Research at[https://www.watertowerresearch.com/event/view/491](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.watertowerresearch.com%2Fevent%2Fview%2F491&esheet=54590830&newsitemid=20260817979507&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.watertowerresearch.com%2Fevent%2Fview%2F491&index=1&md5=f3abc8868464c63edd3082d3ca65ec2d) .


**About WD-40 Company**


WD-40 Company is a global marketing organization dedicated to creating positive lasting memories by developing and selling products that solve problems in workshops, factories, and homes around the world. The Company owns a wide range of well-known brands that include maintenance products and homecare and cleaning products: WD-40® Multi-Use Product, WD-40 Specialist®, 3-IN-ONE®, GT85®, 2000 Flushes®, no vac®, Spot Shot®, Lava®, Solvol®, X-14®, and Carpet Fresh®.


Headquartered in San Diego, California, USA, WD-40 Company recorded net sales of $620.0 million in fiscal year 2025 and its products are currently available in more than 176 countries and territories worldwide. WD-40 Company is traded on the NASDAQ Global Select Market under the ticker symbol “WDFC”. For additional information about WD-40 Company please visit[http://www.wd40Company.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wd40Company.com&esheet=54590830&newsitemid=20260817979507&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wd40Company.com&index=2&md5=74ccdd27dbec8f36cd0d0f545167b021) .
