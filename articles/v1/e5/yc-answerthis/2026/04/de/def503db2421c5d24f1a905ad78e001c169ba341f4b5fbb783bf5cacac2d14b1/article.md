# **AnswerThis vs Consensus: Which AI Research Tool Wins in 2026?**


The academic research field has experienced fundamental shifts in recent years due to the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools within academic research. AI technology is now helping researchers conduct literature reviews and write papers at a much faster pace than ever before. Two great examples of AI powered tools are *AnswerThis* and *Consensus* . Even though both products help researchers, they have slightly different applications.


In this article, we will compare AnswerThis to Consensus in full detail so that you have some insights as to which product may be a better fit for your research workflow. If you are an academic, PhD candidate or young researcher, this article can assist you in selecting the right tool.


## **What is AnswerThis?**


[AnswerThis](https://answerthis.io/) is an advanced, AI-based research assistant for academics. It allows users to upload numerous research papers and to automatically produce literature reviews, identify research gaps, compare studies and support the development of structured academic content.


Compared to generic AI tools, AnswerThis provides significant depth in addressing research-specific issues. It automates the synthesis and analysis tasks that typically require weeks of manual effort down to a few hours of work. AnswerThis is especially useful for thesis writing, systematic reviews and academic publishing.


*Key strength:* Covers all areas of research automation


## **What is Consensus?**


Consensus, an AI-driven academic search engine, enables researchers to discover answers based on scientific literature. Consensus uses an intuitive interface for users to quickly search and find answers to their questions using natural language queries. The platform will return summarized responses from research papers, including citations to the actual studies used.


Consensus can be especially helpful when a researcher needs to get quick, evidence-based answers to questions, such as "Is intermittent fasting good for cognition?". Consensus will provide an evidence-based answer. Consensus primarily focuses on search/discovery rather than providing the entire research workflow.


*Key strength:* Fast and evidence-based response to your inquiry.


## **Core Difference: Workflow vs Search**


There is a key difference between AnswerThis and Consensus based on how they are used. The goal of Consensus is to provide you with an answer, while the goal of AnswerThis is to help you do your research. Consensus can be used for providing fast insights, but once you have gone past the initial exploratory stage (i.e. to[create a literature review](https://answerthis.io/literature-review) , to create a synthesis of multiple papers), the usefulness of Consensus diminishes. On the other hand, AnswerThis is a tool used to enable researchers to go beyond just reading papers and create publishable output.


In summary:


-


Consensus = Google Scholar + AI-based summaries


-


AnswerThis = the complete research assistant


## **Literature Review Capabilities**


The literature review is one of the most important and time-consuming components of the research process. Consensus provides users with summaries of the individual research articles contained in several sources. However, it does not provide the ability to create a structured literature review using multiple sources.


AnswerThis excels here as it allows users to upload multiple papers into the platform, which then automatically generates an integrated and structured literature review from the uploaded documents. In addition, it identifies trends, inconsistencies, and research gaps.


*Winner:* AnswerThis


## **Research Gap Identification**


One of the most difficult tasks faced by many researchers is identifying a gap in existing research. While Consensus can identify existing literature and provide evidence from the literature, it does not specifically identify a gap in research or an area where new research is needed.


AnswerThis does more than this by combining the results of multiple studies to[identify a gap in research](https://answerthis.io/ai/research-gap-finder) or an area for further research. This is particularly useful for PhD students who are working to define their research question.


*Winner:* AnswerThis


## **Writing and Academic Structuring**


As previously stated, Consensus only provides the user with summarization of their answers and does not help with writing or structuring their work. Therefore, users will still be responsible for manually writing their own papers and structuring their work.


AnswerThis not only helps with writing but also helps with writing in an academic format. In addition to being able to complete academic papers and dissertations, AnswerThis also helps the user to write academic-style content as well as maintain consistency, clarity, and the proper academic style.


Winner: AnswerThis


## **Ease of Use and User Experience**


The user experience with Consensus is very simplistic and consists of simply asking a question and receiving an answer. Therefore, it is particularly well-suited to new users or for users looking for quick answers to questions.


AnswerThis is more advanced and loaded with features. However, it provides users with an excellent level of functionality. Once the user learns how to use the program, they will find that it replaces multiple types of software and will provide significant value.


Verdict:


-


Ease of use → Consensus


-


Power & depth → AnswerThis


## **When Should You Use Each Tool?**


Early in the research process, Consensus is great for generating ideas and quickly identifying what is already known and researched. AnswerThis can be used throughout every step of the research (i.e., literature review, data analysis, and writing), making it especially useful for serious academic work (theses, journal articles).


*Best approach:* Using Consensus for idea generation and then using AnswerThis for implementation is the best way to utilize the two tools. However, if you had to select one tool, AnswerThis would be the better choice because it has a broader application area.


## **Final Verdict: AnswerThis is the Clear Winner**


Consensus provides rapid and verifiable responses but has a limited application area (won't support complete research). AnswerThis provides a comprehensive AI-driven research environment which allows finding, processing, and producing high-quality academic materials.


If your goal is to:


-


Write a thesis


-


Conduct a literature review


-


Publish research


Then **AnswerThis** is the clear winner.


## **Conclusion: The Future of Research is Integrated AI**


The next generation of academic research tools will do more than simply provide you answers, they help you think, analyze, and create. AnswerThis is an evolutionary advancement of AI research tools. Researchers need not to switch between platforms but rather utilize one platform (AnswerThis) for a complete and comprehensive research workflow.
