# **Top 10 AI Tools for Researchers in 2026**


The role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has changed considerably in the way in which research takes place. No longer do researchers spend days upon days going through papers and organizing their information into notes or formulating ideas to draft written materials in 2026. Instead, AI-based research tools allow for quicker literature reviews, more thorough analysis, and better productivity when writing research papers. The use of AI has become a complete partner in conducting research - from conception to publication.


This article discusses the 10 most popular AI-based research tools such as AnswerThis, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and so on.

## **2. Claude – Best for Long-Form Academic Writing**


Researchers now favour[Claude](https://claude.ai/) as the ideal tool for managing longer form types of writing such as academic writing. By being able to summarize long PDF files while keeping track of all the details, it is easy to produce a long form document with coherent answers that could also be used in thesis writing or a literature review synthesis with more fluidity than other tools available today.


*Best for:* Long-form writing, document analysis


## **3. ChatGPT – The Most Versatile AI Assistant**


[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) remains the leading AI resource in flexibility for academic purposes. It works for developing ideas to create drafts, summaries, coding, and explaining complex academic subjects. If you are developing research topics or drafting paragraphs, ChatGPT is a very helpful tool. Although it is a general-purpose tool and may require some guidance / support for the specifics of deeper academic structure to be provided.


*Best Uses:* Writing support, idea development, providing fast explanations


## **4. Perplexity – AI-Powered Research Search Engine**


[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) fills the void that exists between a Google Scholar and AI chatbot, providing users with answers to research questions that are real-time, cited sources. It is suited for anyone doing searches for literature due to the speed at which they're able to find literature. The results are summarized and there are direct answers provided along with citation references.


*Best for:* Quick literature discovery, cited references


## **5. GitHub Copilot – Essential for Technical Researchers**


The[GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/copilot) application is revolutionary for researchers in the field of programming, machine learning, and/or data science. The Copilot will work with you to write code, debug code, or provide algorithm suggestions. It will help cut down the time it takes to develop something greatly when developing experimental research set ups.


*Best for:* Writing code, ML model development


## **6. NotebookLM – Smart Research Notebook**


[NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/) functions as an individualized research assistant that collaborates with your documents directly. You can upload papers, notes, and sources, so it will generate summaries, insights, and answers based on these materials.


*Best for:* Personalized knowledge management


## **7. Elicit – AI for Literature Review Automation**


Elicit is designed specifically for academic research and facilitates the automation of literature reviews, data extraction, and summarizing papers; It is particularly helpful for systematic reviews.


*Best for:* Structured literature reviews.


## **8. Scite – Smart Citation Analysis Tool**


Scite provides information beyond just citations. It indicates whether a study has been supported, contrasted against, or discussed/mentioned in other studies.


*Best for:* Analyzing citation quality.


## **9. Research Rabbit – Visual Literature Mapping**


Research Rabbit provides a visual connection between research papers allowing you to find relationships among studies and explore different topics.


*Best for:* Discovering new areas of research.


## **10. Grammarly – AI Writing and Editing Assistant**


Grammarly continues to be an essential resource to help refine your academic writing. Grammarly improves the correctness, clarity, and tone of your writing making sure that your research documentation is presented in a professional manner.


*Best for:* Editing and proofreading
