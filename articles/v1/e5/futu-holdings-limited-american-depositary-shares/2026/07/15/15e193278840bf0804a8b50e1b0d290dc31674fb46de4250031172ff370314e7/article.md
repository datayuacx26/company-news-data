# Futu Partners with J.P. Morgan to Host AR-Powered FX Warrants Interactive Game Show, Jointly Advancing Financial Inclusion


Events


--


(Hong Kong, June 12, 2025) Futu Securities International (Hong Kong) Limited ("Futu") today announced a collaboration with J.P. Morgan, the world’s largest bank by market capital, to host an interactive game show event themed around “Foreign Exchange (FX) Warrants”. The innovative game features a cutting-edge Augmented Reality (AR) experience, offering participants an opportunity to enhance their understanding of the FX market through AR technologies while also winning popular Futubull merchandise.


The event will take place at the Atrium on the ground floor of K11 Art Mall in Tsim Sha Tsui from June 14 to June 22. New customers who open a Futubull account on-site will receive an exclusive HK$200 supermarket coupon, with total welcome rewards of up to HK$2,000 available.


Pioneering AR Experience on FX Warrants: Learning Through Innovation


Futu and J.P. Morgan have been committed to promoting investor education on listed derivative products. For this event, J.P. Morgan developed its first-ever AR game to help demystify derivatives products and price movements. By integrating games, learning about listed structured products has become more interactive and accessible, enhancing investor understanding and engagement.


Exclusive On-Site Rewards: Welcome Package Worth Up to HK$2,000 from Futu


Throughout the event, Futu will offer an enticing array of Futubull merchandise, including both popular classics and new releases. New customers who open an account on-site will be rewarded with an HK$200 supermarket coupon. Furthermore, they can unlock additional rewards by completing multiple tasks, such as making their first deposit with Futubull. These welcome rewards can accumulate to an impressive total of up to HK$2,000. For full terms and conditions, please refer to[https://www.futuhk.com/en/support/topic2_2030](https://www.futuhk.com/en/support/topic2_2030) .


Mr. Daniel Tse, Managing Director of Futu Securities, said: “We are pleased to partner with J.P. Morgan on this innovative initiative. We aim to promote investor education in a creative and engaging approach, particularly in the area of FX derivatives, which are less commonly explored by retail investors. As a leading tech brokerage in Hong Kong, Futu remains committed to financial inclusion and continues to invest in investor education. Looking ahead, we will roll out diverse initiatives to enhance financial literacy and support the development of the local retail investment market.”


Mr. Cedric Cheung, Head of Listed Structured Products Sales, Asia, J.P. Morgan, said: “Both Futu and J.P. Morgan strive to promote digitalization among Hong Kong investor across the financial market through innovative and efficient initiatives. We are excited to collaborate with Futu to provide investor education that resonates with investors. Our team has thoughtfully designed an interactive game using AR technology, which helps investors gain a clearer understanding of the possible returns and risks associated with listed structured products."
