# Futu Teams Up with UBS to Host Investment Event and Seminar


Events


--


(Hong Kong, October 28, 2025)


Futu Securities International (Hong Kong) Limited ("Futu")


is pleased to announce its collaboration with UBS to launch a series of offline investment events aimed at enhancing financial literacy, empowering investors with innovative strategies, and promoting the development of Hong Kong's financial market.


A Gateway to Smart Trading Trends, and a Chance to Win Big


From 29 October to 12 November 2025, Futu will kick off an investment experience event, held at the Ground Floor Atrium of K11 Art Mall in Tsim Sha Tsui.


At the center of the event is interactive zone where both seasoned investors and beginners can experience Futu’s cutting-edge trading platform firsthand. Visitors will explore innovative features, including AI-powered tools designed to help beginners gain a solid foundation in investment knowledge across diverse asset classes, encouraging them to start investing with confidence.


The event also offers a unique chance to maximize trading potential, giving traders a shot at winning big. Guests can register onsite for Futu’s 2nd Global Paper Trading Competition, where participants will compete against investors worldwide for a share of the US$200,000 prize pool in stock vouchers and exclusive rewards— an opportunity to boost returns and showcase trading skills on a global stage.


Joint Investment Seminar: Expert Insights on Hong Kong’s Market Future


UBS and Futu will be teaming up to host a featured investment seminar on 22 November at the HKEX Connect Hall. Titled "Hang Seng Index Gearing Up for 27,000: Tips for Future Market Strategies", the seminar will feature leading industry experts discussing how global economic shifts and macroeconomic policy trends influence Hong Kong’s stock market. Attendees will gain insights into emerging industry trends and practical asset allocation strategies to navigate future market opportunities. Speakers include Ms. Winni Cheuk, Head of Sales, APAC Public Distribution at UBS; Mr. Martin Wong, Senior Vice President, Global Client Development, Markets at HKEX; Ms. Anna Fung, Senior Analyst at Futu Securities; and Mr. Alex Wong, renowned stock commentator and financial expert.


This thematic seminar is part of Futu’s ongoing initiative to help investors seize market opportunities and achieve wealth growth in today’s unpredictable and complex environment. It also offers attendees the chance to reflect on past market trends for deeper insights, with an exclusive visit to the Museum of Financial History inside HKEX Connect Hall. Here, guests can explore compelling, historically significant exhibits, as well as interesting photo opportunities.


Mr. Daniel Tse, Managing Director of Futu Securities


, said: "Futu hopes to provide Hong Kong investors with more comprehensive and considerate support, enabling them to make informed decisions more easily in an ever-changing market. We are committed to building an investment platform that is not only educational and resource-rich but also engaging and accessible, both online and offline. Our goal is to provide investors with the tools they need while creating an enjoyable experience that makes them feel truly welcomed into the world of finance.”


From Market Insights to Mouthwatering Delights: Discover Exciting Rewards


Beyond expert investing insights, visitors at the investment experience event can indulge in special perks, like complimentary ice cream, simply by scanning the UBS QR code and completing email registration. What’s more, new customers who successfully open an account on-site will receive a HK$200 supermarket voucher, and upon completing fund deposits and designated tasks can enjoy welcome rewards totaling up to HK$2,000. For detailed terms and conditions, please visit:[https://www.futuhk.com/support/topic2_2245](https://www.futuhk.com/support/topic2_2245) .
