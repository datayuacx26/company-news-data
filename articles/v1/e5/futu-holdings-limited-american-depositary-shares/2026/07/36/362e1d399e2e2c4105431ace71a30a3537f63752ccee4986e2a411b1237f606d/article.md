# Futu to Hold Hong Kong's Largest Investment and Wealth Expo for the Third Consecutive Year


Events


--


(Hong Kong, 10 February 2026) – Futu Securities International (Hong Kong) Limited ("Futu") today announced that the third edition of the Futu Investment Exhibition ("Futu Expo"), Hong Kong's largest investment and wealth expo\[1\], will take place on 13-14 March at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre. Since launching in 2024, the Expo has become the city's premier annual investment event designed exclusively for retail investors, and the only free-admission exhibition of its scale in Hong Kong. This year's edition will spotlight Futu's comprehensive suite of services spanning securities trading, wealth management, and private wealth management, while gathering international financial institutions, industry pioneers, corporate leaders, and investment experts to decode wealth-building strategies for 2026. The Expo will kick off with a major product launch on Day 1, unveiling enhanced platform features designed to empower investors with superior tools and seamless investment experiences.


Three Themed Zones for Every Investor Profile


Recognizing that investors have evolving needs, Futu has designed three specialized zones this year: Trading, Wealth Management, and Private Wealth Management – ensuring comprehensive coverage from first-time investors to high-net-worth individuals. In addition to interactive product experiences, the Trading Zone will feature a dedicated crypto area highlighting the latest digital asset products and advanced trading tools. The Wealth Management Zone offers personalized one-on-one consultations with wealth advisors who craft bespoke portfolio strategies. This dual approach, combining cutting-edge digital capabilities with high-touch personal service, reinforces Futu's positioning as a holistic wealth partner.


36 Premier Investment Seminars with Market-Moving Insights


The Expo will deliver 36 investment seminars and workshops featuring C-suite executives, global institutional experts, and veteran market strategists. Headliners include Tom Lee, Chairman of the Board, BitMine, alongside senior officials from CME Group, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX), Japan Exchange Group (JPX), and Nasdaq. Discussion topics will span 2026's most compelling investment themes, such as global equities outlook, AI-driven opportunities, precious metals dynamics, Web3.0 innovation, cryptocurrency strategies, and more, equipping attendees with actionable intelligence to navigate the year ahead.


Interactive Learning Meets Over 1 Million Giveaways Featuring 24K Golden Bulls


True to its mission of "Making investing easier and not alone," Futu has curated engaging activities across all three themed zones, blending financial education with interactive entertainment. Participants may win for over HK$1 million in prizes, including 24K golden bulls worth HK$200,000, plus wealth starter packages, various coupons, exclusive Futu collectibles, and over 10,000 premium giveaways.
