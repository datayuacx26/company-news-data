# Futu Investment Exhibition Attracts Over 12,000 Investors Announced AI-Powered Intelligent Decision-Making and Trading Insights at Hong Kong’s Largest


Events


--


(Hong Kong, March 17, 2026)


The third Futu Investment Exhibition ("Futu Expo"), organized by Futu Securities International (Hong Kong) Limited ("Futu")


, concluded successfully at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre on March 13–14. The two-day event drew more than 12,000 investors and featured 36 investment strategy seminars and workshops, inviting over 100 internationally renowned financial and economic leaders, listed companies, and corporate partners to participate. Since its debut in 2024, the Futu Expo has become Hong Kong’s largest free annual investment exhibition tailored for retail investors.


Futu Expo attracts over 12,000 investors and brings together more than 100 internationally renowned financial and economic leaders, listed companies, and enterprises. It is an annual investment and wealth expo tailored specifically for retail investors.


This year's exhibition demonstrated the powerful financial ecosystem and capabilities of the Futu Group. The exhibition featured three themed zones: Trading, Wealth Management, and Private Wealth Management, offering targeted products for a wide range of investors. Zones showcased solutions ranging from stock trading and options strategies to crypto assets, personal finance, and private wealth services.


### AI Fully Upgraded: Launching Exclusive AI Agent Features to Lower the Barrier for Quantitative Trading


On the first day of the Futu Expo, Futu announced a major series of AI and automated trading upgrades on its platform. With the fully enhanced Futubull AI and the upcoming dedicated AI Agent features, Futu significantly lowers the entry barrier for quantitative trading—enabling everyday investors to conduct end-to-end automated processes from strategy development to live trading execution without any coding. This marks the beginning of a new era of AI-driven investing. At the same time, Futu is actively integrating investment, virtual asset ecosystems, and everyday financial management scenarios to build a comprehensive and holistic financial services system.


In addition, executives from various markets representing Moomoo, overseas independent brand by Futu Holdings Limited, also convened in Hong Kong to share investment opportunities, risk assessments, and allocation strategies for international markets, including the United States, Australia, Singapore, and Japan. This collaboration reflects Futu's mission since its inception: to provide Hong Kong investors with an efficient, one-stop, cross-market, and cross-asset class investment platform.


Moomoo's global market executives join hands in Hong Kong to participate in seminars and share in-depth insights on global investment opportunities.


### Record-Breaking Collaboration Among Financial Institutions: with 36 Premier Investment Seminars Featuring Global Financial Leaders


This year's Futu Expo has set a record for the highest number of collaborative financial institutions, bringing together 19 leading international partners, including financial institutions and global exchanges. The two-day event featured 36 investment seminars and workshops covering topics spanning global market outlook, cryptocurrency trends, in-depth industry analysis, and practical trading strategies.


The lineup of distinguished speakers includes Tom Lee, the Chairman of the Board at BitMine and renowned "Wall Street Oracle," provided insights into cryptocurrency market cycles and the virtual asset prospects. Senior representatives from leading global exchanges from CME Group, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX) and Nasdaq, will dissect new investment opportunities from various market perspectives.


The Expo has also specially invited C-suite executives from multiple listed companies—Mao Geping, CHICMAX, XtalPi Holdings Limited , Dobot Corp, UBTECH Robotics, MINISO, and Newborn Town （ordered by company stock code）—alongside prominent Hong Kong stock commentators and financial experts, who shared insights on trending sectors and hot market topics through interactive sessions, helping retail investors identify potential investment opportunities.


This year's Futu Expo has set a record for the highest number of collaborative financial institutions, bringing together 19 leading international partners


Tom Lee shared forward‑looking insights on the cyclical nature of the crypto market and the macro outlook for digital assets.


### Over HK$1 Million in Prizes Awarded Through Interactive Finance‑Themed Games


The two-day event was vibrant and well-received, with all seminars and workshops filled. Three themed zones showcased diverse product experiences, interactive games, and wealth management education workshops designed for participants of all ages and experience levels. Rewards worth over HK$1 million were given away, including 24K golden bulls valued at HK$200,000, the latest electronic products, and more than 10,000 premium giveaways.

Mr. Daniel Tse, Managing Director of Futu Securities


, stated: "Thanks to the enthusiastic participation and support of our industry partners and investors, the third Futu Expo has been a major success. As a leading fintech company based in Hong Kong with a global outlook, Futu is committed to promoting financial inclusion. From hosting the Futu Expo, expanding our diverse products and services, to investing in AI-powered financial service innovation, our mission is to help investors achieve their wealth goals and become their long-term, trusted wealth partner. We’re committed to deepening our financial platform ecosystem to help investors seize opportunities in a dynamic global market".
