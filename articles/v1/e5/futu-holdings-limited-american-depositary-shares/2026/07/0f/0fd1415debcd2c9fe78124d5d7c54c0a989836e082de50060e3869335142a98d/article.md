# Futu Concludes Largest Retail Investors Event in Hong Kong, Looking into the Future of Smart Investing with 12,000 Participants


Events


--


(Hong Kong, March 24, 2025) Futu Securities International (Hong Kong) Limited ("Futu") successfully concluded its second Futu Investment Exhibition ("Futu Expo") at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre from March 21-22, 2025. As the largest retail investors event in Hong Kong, this year the Expo was doubled in size, attracting over 12,000 participants with over 25 investment strategy seminars and workshops .


Mr. Leaf Hua Li, Futu's Founder and Chairman, inaugurated the event by announcing the integration with DeepSeek and the launch of new features and artificial intelligence (AI)-powered tools on the company's investment app Futubull, reinforcing Futu's commitment to continuously leveraging cutting-edge technologies to empower retail investors.


Mr. Leaf Hua Li, Futu's Founder and Chairman inaugurated the expo and product launch event and took photos with the audience.


Gathering Over 100 Financial Leaders to Explore Diverse Opportunities and 2025 Investment Strategies


This year’s Futu Expo, themed "Invest 2025: Find Unlimited Triumphs", convened over 100 global financial leaders, including senior executives from three major exchanges across the world — Japan Exchange Group (JPX), Nasdaq, and Singapore Exchange (SGX) — alongside industry elites and Hong Kong’s most renowned stock analysts and financial experts.


The Expo featured a dynamic agenda. In the "Futu x Exchange Talk" session, senior representatives from Japan Exchange Group (JPX), Nasdaq, and SGX Group engaged in in-depth discussion on global economic opportunities, addressing the potential and challenges of AI in investment, and strategies for diversified portfolios. Listed AI cloud service provider - Kingsoft Cloud, enterprise management AI provider - Kingdee International, and AI-focused venture capital－3C AGI Partners, conducted an insightful session on the development of the AI sector. Prominent financial elites from Hong Kong, including Hao Hong, Alex kwok Ying Wong, Oscar Kam Keung Choi, Louie Chun Ying Shum, Francis Sze Chi Kwok and Wing Luk Chan, led interactive keynote seminars, dissecting 2025’s emerging trends. Topics spanned U.S. and Hong Kong equities, forex tactics, and virtual asset allocation, offering attendees with inspirations and actionable insights to navigate the dynamic markets in 2025 and ahead.


Mr. Leaf Hua Li unveiled a suite of pioneering product features, including Futubull AI designed to be investors' intelligent assistant.


Innovative Features Debut: AI Technology Powers a New Era of Investing


On the opening day of the Expo, Futu unveiled a suite of pioneering product features, including the DeepSeek R1 powered intelligent chatbot function Futubull AI, and the AI Algo Trading, a leading smart solution for frequent short-term trading. These powerful tools will be integrated into the Futu platform. As a responsible fintech innovator, Futu prioritizes security, compliance, and effectiveness in its AI offerings designed to redefine investment experiences. By leveraging AI technology, Futu aims to translate technological innovations into tangible customer values, taking financial services to the next level.


A 6-meter-tall inflatable Futubull mascot was at the event, creating the best social media moment for participants.


Supported by 19 Partners and Sponsors, Distributing Over 10,000 Gifts


The two-day Expo was filled with a vibrant atmosphere, where every sharing session and seminar was packed to capacity, radiating excitement throughout the venue. Beyond the insightful sessions, the Expo featured three specially designed interactive gaming zones, creating unforgettable experiences for investors of all levels. Through these engaging activities, participants learned and exchanged ideas in a relaxed and enjoyable environment, deepening their investment knowledge while immersing in a dynamic community of Futubull users and fans. A 6-meter-tall inflatable Futubull mascot was at the event, creating the best social media moment for participants. Attendees also experienced the excitement of ringing the bell in a simulated IPO ceremony.


With the support from 19 partners and sponsors, Futu distributed over 10,000 gifts, including sought-after Futubull merchandise. In addition, participants had the opportunity to win the latest electric car, Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra smartphones, tablets, wireless earbuds, and other electronic products valued at over 180,000 HKD. Social media buzz surged as attendees shared highlights, approximately 1,500 Futubull fans shared their event experience on Futubull platform and their social media platforms, reflecting Futu’s mission to promote financial literacy and financial inclusion.


Mr. Daniel Tse, Managing Director of Futu Securities, said: “


“The overwhelming success of the second Futu Expo underscores Hong Kong retail investors’ growing appetite for intelligent tools and market insights. As a fintech pioneer, Futu remains dedicated to advancing financial inclusion, simplifying investing, and empowering users to navigate dynamic markets with confidence.”
