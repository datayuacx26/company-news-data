# Sample availability for electronic components and design intent conversion

Most design projects need to go through a series of prototyping processes before going into a mass production stage and final release to consumers. These prototypes are made in different stages of the design process to ensure that the functionality of the finished product is the desired one.


In the electronics space, components suppliers help engineers in their prototyping stages by providing free samples of their components. At SnapEDA, we want to help engineers get those free samples, in addition to the ECAD and MCAD models, to expedite the design process. The image below shows how engineers can find free samples from the components they’re looking at including in their designs.


‍


Figure 1. Part page of component with free samples available for request on SnapEDA


‍


But is having free samples on suppliers part pages on SnapEDA something that might influence the design intent of engineers?


We analyzed the data from hundreds of thousands of downloads happening on our site to understand if sample availability is affecting download conversion among our engineering community, and what we found is very interesting.


The chart below shows the likelihood of an engineer to design-in an electronic component evaluated on[SnapEDA](http://www.snapeda.com/) . As shown, for every component evaluated, about 30% of parts get designed-in.


‍


Figure 2. Average part evaluation conversion to download on SnapEDA, in the last 7 weeks


We had previously shown how the[availability of MCAD models was one of the strongest variables that affected the design intent](https://insights.snapeda.com/blog/why-every-electronic-component-supplier-needs-to-provide-3d-models) of engineers downloading on SnapEDA. However, we also found that **having free samples available for request, right when the engineers are browsing for the components they need in their design, also greatly affects their design intent** . Below, you’ll find a chart showing that parts with free samples available for request, have a 54% higher probability of being designed-in.


‍


Figure 3. Average conversion to download with sample availability


‍


We understand how prototyping is crucial for product design and development. We also now understand that providing the samples needed for this prototyping in early stages of the design can also influence engineering design intent in the electronic space.
