# How to grow the influence of your products across the industry

‍


With the digital transformation boom accelerated by the pandemic, multiple studies have shown[that consumer behavior has changed towards the use of digital channels](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/a-global-view-of-how-consumer-behavior-is-changing-amid-covid-19#:~:text=4.%20Flight%20to%20digital) . 67% of the respondents from a[consumer survey](https://www.zendesk.com/blog/searching-for-self-service/) conducted by Nuance Enterprise said they prefer self-service experiences over in-person interactions, that’s why component suppliers must adapt to these disruptive changes and come up with better ways to engage with customers and grow their design-in influence digitally.


Even before the pandemic, the trend of the new generations was to “Google” the information and purchase from online retailers. So you might wonder how to better engage with your customers to thrive in the digital space. Here are some tips:


‍


**1. Be empathetic by providing your customers with everything they need**


Our nature as human beings is to choose services that make our lives easier. By digitizing your product catalog, you’d be able to offer more relevant information so that your customers choose your products over your competitors’. But how exactly do you do this?


A simple way to do it is by **adding more interactive content** such as CAD models on your website. CAD models are a good resource for engineers as they ease the use of your components in their final products.


Studies show that[50% of engineers’ time can be spent sourcing and managing data for component models](https://www.rsgroup.com/media/press-release/2017/02022017#:~:text=Studies%20have%20shown%20that%20up%20to%2050%25%20of%20an%20electronics%20design%20engineer%E2%80%99s%20time%20can%20be%20spent%20on%20sourcing%20and%20managing%20data%20for%20component%20models%20used%20within%20design%20software%20tools.) . By adding this content to your site, you’d be able to speed up their design process and drive more loyalty.


SnapEDA offers a CAD viewer that displays symbols, footprints, and 3D models. Engineers can examine and interact with the CAD models by rotating the 3D models to see the form factor or hovering over pins to see relevant details.
