# Hamilton to Report First Quarter 2025 Financial Results on May 7, 2025

Hamilton to Report First Quarter 2025 Financial Results on May 7, 2025


**PEMBROKE, BERMUDA, March 26, 2025** — Hamilton Insurance Group, Ltd. (NYSE: HG) (“Hamilton” or the “Company”) will issue its first quarter 2025 financial results after the market closes on Wednesday, May 7, 2025.


Hamilton will host a conference call to discuss its financial results on Thursday, May 8, 2025, at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time. The conference call dial-in can be retrieved by completing the registration form available at[https://registrations.events/direct/Q4I6483782606](https://url.us.m.mimecastprotect.com/s/6NfTCADmP7tlZQLyf9i6UGf86e) .


A live,[audio webcast](https://events.q4inc.com/attendee/523086056) of the conference call can be accessed through the Investors portal of the Company’s website at[investors.hamiltongroup.com](https://investors.hamiltongroup.com/overview/default.aspx) where a replay of the call will also be available.


For access to either the conference call or webcast, please dial in/login a few minutes in advance to complete any necessary registration.

**About Hamilton Insurance Group, Ltd.** Hamilton is a Bermuda-headquartered specialty insurance and reinsurance company that underwrites risks on a global basis through its wholly owned subsidiaries. Its three underwriting platforms: Hamilton Global Specialty, Hamilton Select and Hamilton Re, each with dedicated and experienced leadership, provide access to diversified and profitable business around the world.
