# Hamilton Appoints Mike Mulray as Chief Underwriting Officer of Hamilton Select

Hamilton Appoints Mike Mulray as Chief Underwriting Officer of Hamilton Select


**PEMBROKE, BERMUDA, October 13, 2025** — Hamilton Insurance Group, Ltd. (NYSE: HG) (“Hamilton” or the “Company”) announced today the appointment of Mike Mulray as Chief Underwriting Officer at Hamilton Select, its US excess and surplus (E&S) lines insurer, effective October 20, 2025.


In his new role, Mulray will lead the underwriting operations of Hamilton Select, ensuring continued underwriting excellence in the market, and report to Anita Kuchma, Chief Executive Officer of Hamilton Select.


“With over 25 years of underwriting experience and a distinguished reputation in the insurance industry, Mike brings exceptional expertise that will be instrumental in driving our underwriting strategy as we continue to grow our US E&S platform,” said Kuchma. “The market’s enthusiasm for what we’re building has been rewarding and it is a testament to our talented team and their well-established broker relationships and focused marketing efforts. I am delighted to welcome Mike to Hamilton and look forward to collaborating with him to continue to build on the strong foundation we’ve established in what remains an attractive E&S marketplace.”


Mulray comes to Hamilton following over nine years with Everest where he most recently served as EVP and President of North America Insurance. Before that, he was Global Chief Operating Officer, having joined Everest as Chief Underwriting Officer. Prior to Everest, Mulray spent a decade with General Electric Company where he held several executive positions with responsibilities in Enterprise Risk Management and Corporate Risk & Insurance, including oversight of one of the largest and most expansive insurance programs in the world. He began his career with Aon, serving in a variety of roles in the US and Bermuda markets.
