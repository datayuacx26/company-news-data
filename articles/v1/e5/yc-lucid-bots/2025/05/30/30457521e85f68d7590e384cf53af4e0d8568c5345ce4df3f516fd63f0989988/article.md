## Core Competencies for Facility Management


Facility managers oversee various critical aspects like maintenance, cleaning, security, catering, landscaping, technology, and more. To effectively manage such diverse domains, facility managers need well-rounded competencies.


Some key skills and proficiencies modern facility managers should possess include:


- **Critical Thinking:** As facilities adopt smart systems and technologies, facility managers need analytical skills


to evaluate options, solve problems innovatively, and make optimal decisions. Critical thinking enables balancing costs and benefits for the right solutions.
- **Adaptability:** Continuous innovation in building infrastructure and systems requires facility managers to adapt quickly. Being flexible to implement new processes and technologies is essential.
- **Decision Making:** Facility managers make many high-stakes decisions related to operations, vendors, budgets, resource allocation, and more. Strong decision-making skills lead to increased productivity and cost savings.
- **Leadership Skills:** Managing diverse teams and stakeholders needs effective leadership skills. Being able to motivate and inspire teams ensures seamless facility operations.
- **Interpersonal Skills:** Facility managers regularly interact with occupants, vendors, maintenance crew, and other stakeholders. Strong verbal and written communication skills create effective collaboration.


## Technical Expertise and Operational Knowledge


Modern buildings feature complex mechanical, electrical, plumbing, security, and technology systems. With sustainability goals and smart infrastructure


becoming the norm, facility systems are getting more sophisticated. Effectively running such intricate systems necessitates specialized technical know-how and operational skills.


Facility managers must up skill in technical aspects like building automation, energy management, electrical systems, and more. They should understand daily operations to make informed maintenance and vendor decisions.


With rapid digitization, managers need the expertise to assess and adopt smart building technologies. Staying updated with the latest advancements in property management technologies is essential.

Developing specialized technical facility management skills reduces expenses through preventative maintenance while meeting tenant expectations. With buildings getting more advanced, technical expertise and operational knowledge will only grow more vital for facility managers.
