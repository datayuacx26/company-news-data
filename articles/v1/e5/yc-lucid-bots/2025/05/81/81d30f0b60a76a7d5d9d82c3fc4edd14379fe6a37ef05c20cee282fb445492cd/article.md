## Integrating Technology into Your Cleaning Strategy


Incorporating cutting-edge software solutions and technology, such as the[Sherpa Drone](https://knowledge.lucidbots.com/sherpa-drone) and[Lavo Bot](https://knowledge.lucidbots.com/lavo) , into existing cleaning protocols requires a strategic and seamless approach to your asset tracking.


Here are essential tips for effectively integrating the best facility management software and tools into your cleaning strategy:


- **Assess Your Current Cleaning Protocols**


- Determine specific tasks or areas in your current cleaning workflows where the Sherpa Drone or Lavo Bot could offer significant advantages over traditional methods.


- **Training and Familiarization**


- Provide comprehensive training sessions for staff members to familiarize them with operating and maintaining these advanced cleaning technologies. Proper training ensures that staff can utilize the tools effectively, optimizing their capabilities for efficient cleaning.


- **Gradual Implementation**


- Initiate the integration gradually to minimize disruptions to ongoing cleaning operations. Start with pilot programs or specific areas to assess the tools’ effectiveness and allow staff to adapt gradually to the new technologies.


- **Develop Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)**


- Detail step-by-step guidelines for incorporating the Sherpa Drone or Lavo Bot into routine cleaning processes, ensuring consistency and maximizing their potential.


- **Monitoring and Feedback**


- Regularly monitor the performance of these tools and gather feedback from staff involved in their use. This feedback loop helps identify areas for improvement, fine-tune cleaning protocols, and maximize the efficiency of technological integration.
