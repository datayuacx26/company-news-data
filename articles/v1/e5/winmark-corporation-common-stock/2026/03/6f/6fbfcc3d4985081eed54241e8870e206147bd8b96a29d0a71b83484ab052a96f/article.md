# What Is Winmark? How Its Resale Retail Franchise Model Strengthens Communities

March 18, 2026 |


By Winmark


Since 1988, Winmark Corporation has built one of the most recognized names in resale retail franchising. Through five consumer brands — Once Upon A Child®, Plato's Closet®, Play It Again Sports®, Style Encore®, and Music Go Round® — Winmark creates circular, sustainable local economies that benefit families, entrepreneurs, and the environment.


The concept is straightforward: locally owned Winmark stores buy gently used clothing, equipment, and gear directly from community members, then resell those items at a fraction of the original retail price. The result? Cash in customers' hands, quality goods at accessible prices, and billions of items diverted from landfills — all anchored in the neighborhoods where people live.


**Who Owns and Operates Winmark Franchise Stores?**


Every Winmark retail location is independently owned and operated by a local franchisee — not a corporate entity. This is by design.


Winmark offers prospective franchise owners with the brand best suited to their passions, ensuring that store owners are invested in the same community they serve. These owners are neighbors, parents in local school organizations, members of sports leagues, participants in civic and charity events.


This community-first model creates real advantages for shoppers:


- **On-the-spot cash payments** give sellers an immediate incentive to return when items are outgrown or no longer needed — no shipping hassles, no waiting for online marketplace buyers.
- **Local hiring** means store staff understand the tastes, trends, and needs of the people walking through the door.
- **Repeat business by design** — Winmark's buy-sell model naturally encourages customers to come back, making each store a trusted neighborhood fixture rather than a one-time stop.


The results speak for themselves: **98.7% of Winmark franchisees renew their agreements after 10 years** , and 53% expand to own more than one brand location.


**How Does the Winmark Resale Model Benefit the Environment?**


Winmark's business model is inherently sustainable — not as a marketing position, but as a structural reality of how resale retail works.


When a family sells outgrown children's clothes to Once Upon A Child, or drops off old hockey equipment at Play It Again Sports, those items re-enter circulation in the local community instead of heading to a landfill. Since 2010, Winmark stores have collectively kept **more than 1 billion items out of landfills** , reducing toxic runoff and off-gassing in the communities where stores operate.


Beyond landfill diversion, the resale model sidesteps many of the most damaging aspects of conventional retail:


- **No new manufacturing required** for resold goods, meaning fewer carbon emissions and no new raw material extraction.
- **No long-haul supply chains** , keeping economic activity local and reducing transportation-related emissions.
- **An alternative to fast fashion** , bypassing the workforce and environmental ethics issues embedded in low-cost, high-volume apparel production.


**Does Resale Retail Perform Well During Economic Downturns?**


Yes — and Winmark's 35+ year track record demonstrates it.


Resale retail is widely recognized as **resilient in any economy.** When household budgets tighten, value-oriented shopping accelerates. Families who might have bought new gear or clothing at full retail price turn to resale options; sellers who need extra cash bring in items they no longer use. Both sides of Winmark's business model strengthen in economic uncertainty.


This durability makes Winmark franchise ownership an attractive proposition for entrepreneurs evaluating long-term business stability. 100% of Winmark's retail outlets are franchisee-owned, meaning every dollar of local economic activity stays within the community — creating jobs, circulating cash among families, and building wealth for local business owners.


**Is a Winmark Franchise the Right Business Opportunity for You?**


For entrepreneurs looking to build a business with measurable community impact, Winmark's franchise model offers a distinct combination of advantages:


- **Proven system:** More than 35 years of operational refinement across five established consumer brands.
- **Resilient market:** Resale retail continues to grow in popularity across all demographic groups, driven by value-consciousness and sustainability awareness.
- **Community integration:** The buy-sell-trade model makes Winmark stores natural neighborhood institutions — not just retail outlets.
- **Franchisee support:** Winmark's 98.7% renewal rate reflects strong system satisfaction and franchisor support.
- **Scalable model:** More than half of Winmark franchisees own multiple locations, a sign of confidence in the brand's long-term performance.
