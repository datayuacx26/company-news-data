# How Winmark Resale Stores Strengthen Local Communities

December 03, 2025 |


By Winmark


Winmark’s resale brands — Once Upon a Child, Plato’s Closet, Play It Again Sports, Style Encore, and Music Go Round — are locally owned franchises committed to supporting families and communities. Our business model is designed to improve lives at the neighborhood level while contributing to a global circular economy.


## The Winmark Franchise Model: Built on Community and Sustainability


Being an active, positive force in the community is central to Winmark’s mission. Our resale stores offer an affordable, eco-friendly alternative to big-box retail and online shopping. Beyond saving customers money, our brands help strengthen the social fabric of the towns and cities where they operate. How Winmark-brand stores make a difference:


- **Meeting the Needs of Active Families:** Local owners understand that today’s purchase will probably be outgrown or unused next season. That’s why they continuously pay customers for gently used clothing and equipment, then sell it at a fraction of the cost of what a consumer would pay for brand new.


- **Putting Cash Back in Customer’s Pockets** : Winmark’s[industry-leading business model](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/why-winmark-/) pays customers for merchandise, on the spot. This simple, transparent process builds trust and makes seasonal closet clean-outs easy.


- **Empowering Local Entrepreneurs** : Winmark seeks values-driven store owners who want to build businesses that matter.[Our ideal franchisee partners](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/why-winmark-/ideal-candidate/) are deeply connected to their communities and thrive on creating local impact.


- **Connecting People and Passions:** We hear so many[inspiring stories](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/testimonials/) from our franchisees about building meaningful businesses with a Winmark store. From supporting youth sports leagues to empowering women entering the workforce, our franchisees create meaningful connections every day.


- **Affordable Sustainability for All** : Our resale system has kept[over 2 billion items out of landfills](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/blog/2025/august/winmark-franchisees-demonstrate-impact-as-compan/) , reducing environmental impact while making sustainable shopping accessible to everyone.


## **Franchise Owners Embody Our Mission**


From the latest teen fashions to sporting goods and gear to musical instruments and equipment, Winmark’s franchisees offer value-driven, socially responsible shopping options for their communities. The average Winmark franchise returns more than $400,000 per year to its customers for their gently owned goods. Our franchisees are making a real difference in the lives of their neighbors:


- Mikki Fitzer, owner of a Plato’s Closet, supports local charitable organizations and[opens her store to the community](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/blog/2025/june/how-winmark-franchisees-give-back-to-their-commu/) with an annual “Take What You Need” Day.


- Chantelle Harder, who recently added a Style Encore to her resale retail portfolio (she also owns a Plato’s Closet and a Once Upon a Child),[credits the community’s enthusiasm](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/blog/2025/may/owner-on-a-mission-provide-resale-for-everyone-/) for her continued success.


- Alain Villafranca puts his corporate business management and economics skills to use[to bring joy](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/blog/2024/may/from-the-corporate-world-to-music-go-round-this-/) to his Music Go Round customers.


## **Thriving Businesses Built on Purpose**


With over 30 years of experience, Winmark has proven that purpose-driven franchising works. Our model benefits communities and the planet — keeping 180+ million items out of landfills each year.


Imagine owning a business that succeeds financially while uplifting your community. If you’re an entrepreneur driven by purpose, Winmark offers the opportunity to make an impact that matters.
