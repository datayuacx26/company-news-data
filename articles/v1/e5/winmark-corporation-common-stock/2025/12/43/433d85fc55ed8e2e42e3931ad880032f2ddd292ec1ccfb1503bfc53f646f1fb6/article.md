# Entrepreneurs, Get Ready for a Great 2026!

December 23, 2025 |


By Winmark


As 2025 comes to a close, Winmark and its five franchise brands are gearing up for an even more successful year ahead. The resale retail category is booming, and our 100% franchisee-owned stores — Plato’s Closet, Once Upon A Child, Play It Again Sports, Style Encore, and Music Go Round — are perfectly positioned to take full advantage of this growing market.


## **Why Motivated Entrepreneurs Should Consider Franchising**


Winmark leads the way in creating a resale ecosystem that delivers[top clothing brands and gently-used sports and musical equipment](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/our-brands/) to consumers. For more than 35 years, we’ve pioneered retail concepts that:


- **Help the planet** through sustainable practices
- **Reward customers** with value and convenience
- **Empower entrepreneurs** with purpose-driven business ownership


Franchising offers a[structured, proven path to building a thriving business](https://www.winmarkfranchises.com/testimonials/) — especially in high-growth categories like resale retail.


## **New Year, New Goals: Key Considerations for Entrepreneurs**


- **Evaluate and adjust:** Review what worked and what didn’t. Learn from mistakes and replicate successes.
- **Understand the “why”:** Analyze operational breakdowns or surprise wins to refine your strategy.
- **Self-assess:** Define your life stage, goals, and passions. Let these guide your entrepreneurial decisions.
- **Do your homework:** Stay informed about consumer trends (Gen Z, Gen Alpha), economic forecasts, and industry shifts.
- **Research franchise options:** Compare opportunities, costs, and support systems. Speak with current franchisees for real-world insights.
- **Network and share ideas:** Talk about your goals with friends and community members. Their feedback may reveal untapped opportunities.


## **Winmark is Different**


We’re on a mission to make resale accessible for everyone while staying true to our core values:


- **Sustainability:** Our model reduces waste and pays customers cash for gently used items. Winmark and its franchisees have kept over 2 billion items out of landfills since 2010.
- **Purpose-driven partnerships:** We seek entrepreneurs who share our community-focused values and ambition.
- **Best-in-class training and support:** From business planning and site selection to marketing and operations, we provide ongoing training and support to equip you for success.


## **Proven Results You Can Trust**


Our franchise model delivers results:


- **98.7% renewal rate** after 10 years
- Consistently ranked in **Entrepreneur’s Franchise 500**
- A strong track record of environmental impact and owner success


## **Make Winmark Part of Your 2026 Plans**


The New Year is the perfect time to start fresh or pivot toward a purpose-driven business. If you’re ready to explore opportunities in the booming resale retail sector, Winmark is here to help.
