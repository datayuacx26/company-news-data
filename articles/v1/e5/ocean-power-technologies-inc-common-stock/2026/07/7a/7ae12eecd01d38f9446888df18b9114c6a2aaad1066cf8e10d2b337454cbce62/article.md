# Ocean Power Technologies to Announce Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Results on July 23, 2026

MONROE TOWNSHIP, N.J., July 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- **Ocean Power Technologies, Inc. ("OPT" or "the Company") (NYSE American: OPTT),** a leader in innovative and cost-effective low-carbon marine power, data, and service solutions, today announced that it will release earnings for its fourth quarter and fiscal 2026 year ended April 30, 2026 on Thursday July 23, 2026. The Company currently expects to file its Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended April 30, 2026 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on or about July 29, 2026.
