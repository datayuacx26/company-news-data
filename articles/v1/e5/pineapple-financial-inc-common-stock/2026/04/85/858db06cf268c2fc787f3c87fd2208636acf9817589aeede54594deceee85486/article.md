Toronto, Ontario--(Newsfile Corp. - April 9, 2026) - **Pineapple Financial Inc.** (NYSE American:[PAPL](https://www.stocktitan.net/overview/PAPL/) ) ("Pineapple" or "the Company"), a leading fintech platform, today announced that it will host a virtual fireside chat on Thursday, April 16, 2026, at 11:00 A.M. **About Pineapple Financial Inc.** Pineapple Financial Inc. (NYSE American:[PAPL](https://www.stocktitan.net/overview/PAPL/) ) is a publicly traded company with a digital asset treasury strategy focused on accumulating INJ tokens through disciplined open-market purchases. The Company is committed to delivering shareholder value through strategic capital allocation in the dynamic digital asset landscape.


In addition to its digital asset treasury, Pineapple is an award-winning fintech and leading Canadian mortgage brokerage network, focusing on both the long-term success of agents and brokers as well as the overall experience of homeowners. With hundreds of brokers within the network, Pineapple creates cutting-edge cloud-based tools and AI-driven systems to enable its brokers to help Canadians realize their dream of owning a home. Pineapple is active within the community and is proud to sponsor charities across Canada to improve the lives of fellow Canadians.
