# Marex expands institutional digital assets offering through Deribit

Marex today announced that it has been onboarded as a broker on Deribit, one of the leading platforms for digital asset derivatives trading.


The onboarding further expands Marex’s institutional digital assets offering and enhances client access to global crypto derivatives markets.


## Expanding institutional access to crypto derivatives markets


Through integration, Marex will provide institutional clients with access to liquidity across crypto options, futures and perpetuals products.


The offering combines Marex’s global infrastructure, risk management framework and institutional-grade service model with Deribit’s digital asset derivatives marketplace.


The onboarding reflects growing institutional demand for digital asset trading solutions that combine operational standards and regulatory oversight of traditional finance with the liquidity and innovation of crypto-native markets.


## Supporting institutional participation in digital assets


As a highly regulated financial services platform, Marex provides clients with:


- a trusted counterparty


- robust risk management capabilities


- institutional-grade service and execution


- dedicated coverage across global markets


The firm’s digital assets offering is designed to support both:


- traditional financial institutions entering the crypto space


- crypto-native participants seeking high-touch execution and tailored trading solutions


## Strengthening Marex’s digital asset capabilities


The onboarding forms part of Marex’s broader strategy to continue expanding its digital asset capabilities and strengthen its position as a global, technology-enabled liquidity and execution partner across both traditional and digital markets.


Jonathan Issan, Co-Head of Crypto Trading at Marex, commented:


“We’re delighted to be onboarded as a broker on Deribit, a leading platform in the digital asset derivatives space. This partnership reflects Marex’s commitment to bridging traditional finance and crypto markets, bringing our deep liquidity expertise and agency execution model to a broader client base. As a regulated and trusted counterparty, we aim to deliver tailored solutions that meet the evolving needs of both institutional and crypto-native clients.”


Luuk Strijers from Deribit added: “The integration with Marex marks another important step in connecting traditional finance with digital asset markets. By combining Marex’s global distribution and risk management expertise with our deep liquidity and institutional-grade derivatives platform, we can offer both established financial institutions and crypto-native clients more efficient, reliable access to the digital asset derivatives space.”
