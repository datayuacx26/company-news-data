# AI demand, grid constraints and the next phase of US power markets | The Marex Market Pulse podcast

In this episode of Marex Market Pulse, we explore the key forces shaping US power markets today, from accelerating AI and data centre demand to evolving weather patterns, natural gas dynamics and regional infrastructure constraints.


Featuring Mindy Yajko, Head of US Power Sales at Marex, the discussion examines how market participants are navigating a rapidly changing landscape where long-term load growth, generation development and transmission limitations are increasingly influencing pricing and volatility.


The conversation looks at the growing impact of data centres on load forecasts, the challenges utilities and ISOs face in meeting future demand, and why location-specific dynamics are becoming increasingly important across power markets. The episode also explores weather-related risks, the role of natural gas as the primary marginal fuel, and how Henry Hub continues to shape US power pricing despite diverging global gas benchmarks.
