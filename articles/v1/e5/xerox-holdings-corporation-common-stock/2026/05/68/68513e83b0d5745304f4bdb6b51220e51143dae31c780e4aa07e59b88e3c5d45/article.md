# Xerox Named a Leader in Quocirca’s 2026 ACT Assessment


Xerox has been positioned as a Leader in Quocirca’s 2026 Automation & AI, Cloud and Technology (ACT) assessment, recognizing the breadth, maturity, and integration of capabilities across Automation and AI, Cloud, and Technology Ecosystems.


The ACT assessment provides a robust evaluation of the capabilities required to modernize operations, simplify work, and support today’s increasingly distributed workforce. This recognition highlights how Xerox brings together[automation](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services/workflow-automation) ,[AI](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services/it-solutions/data-solutions) ,[cloud platforms](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/office/software-solutions/xerox-workplace-cloud) , and ecosystem integration across a broad[digital](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services) ,[IT](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services/it-solutions) and[print](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/office) portfolio to deliver practical, integrated solutions that reduce complexity and support real operational needs.


## Delivering Integrated Automation, AI, and Cloud at Scale


Building on a long heritage of simplifying work and modernizing workflows, Xerox now applies AI as a foundational capability across its portfolio. From predictive maintenance and[intelligent document processing (IDP)](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services/data-information-capture/intelligent-document-processing) to[workflow automation](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services/workflow-automation) ,[production optimization](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/digital-printing/workflow) , and service delivery, AI is embedded across devices, cloud platforms, and managed services—enabling practical innovation at scale.


Xerox brings together on‑device intelligence, cloud‑based orchestration, and ecosystem‑driven services to help organizations reduce complexity, improve resilience, and accelerate transformation across hybrid and distributed environments whatever their stage of digital maturity.


### Recognition Across the ACT Framework


#### Xerox was recognized as a Leader based on strengths including:


-


Automation and AI. Foundational AI embedded across workflows, services, and platforms to drive efficiency, insight, and improved service outcomes.


-


Cloud. Secure, cloud‑native and hybrid platforms that support modern work models while reducing infrastructure and operational complexity.


-


Technology Ecosystems. Strong partner orchestration across cloud, security, analytics, workflow, and IT services, delivering integrated, outcome‑focused solutions.


This leadership reflects not only technology capability, but also proven execution and global scale, aligning with Quocirca’s definition of ACT Leaders as market pacesetters enabling client transformation and innovation.


### A Services‑Led Platform for the Next Phase of Growth


The ACT Leader position also reflects continued momentum across[Digital Services](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services) and[Xerox IT Solutions](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services/it-solutions) , alongside the ongoing integration of Lexmark. Together, these capabilities are strengthening Xerox’s ability to deliver higher value, recurring digital and IT services, while building a more unified, scalable platform for sustainable growth.


By combining workflow automation, cloud services, AI‑enabled platforms, cybersecurity, and managed IT, Xerox helps organizations move from siloed technologies to end‑to‑end solutions that modernize operations and support long‑term success.

For organizations navigating automation, cloud adoption, and workplace transformation, the ACT assessment provides clear direction. Xerox continues to focus on helping clients simplify work, modernize workflows, and adapt with confidence in a rapidly changing technology landscape.
