# Xerox Holdings Corporation Declares Dividend on Common and Preferred Stock

**Norwalk, Conn.** —


[Xerox Holdings Corporation](http://www.xerox.com/) (NASDAQ: XRX) announced today that its board of directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.025 per share on Xerox Holdings Corporation Common Stock. The dividend is payable on April 30, 2026, to shareholders of record on March 31, 2026.


The board also declared a quarterly dividend of $20.00 per share on the outstanding Xerox Holdings Series A Convertible Perpetual Preferred Stock. The dividend is payable on April 1, 2026, to shareholders of record on March 15, 2026.
