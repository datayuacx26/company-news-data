## Your Data Center Has More Decisions Than Ever.


From evolving virtualization platforms to AI initiatives and rising infrastructure costs, IT leaders are facing no shortage of data center challenges.


Join[Xerox IT Solutions](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services/it-solutions) and Lenovo for a live discussion on how organizations are building more resilient, flexible, and future-ready data center environments.


### Modern Data Centers in an Unpredictable World: Hybrid Multicloud, AI & Infrastructure Strategies with Lenovo, July 15, 2026 at 11:00 AM CST


45-Minute Live Session + Q&A

**Navigating today's infrastructure challenges** including supply chain concerns, availability, and Lenovo's Top Choice Express program Presented by Pawan Misra-NA Think Agile Product Manager


-
