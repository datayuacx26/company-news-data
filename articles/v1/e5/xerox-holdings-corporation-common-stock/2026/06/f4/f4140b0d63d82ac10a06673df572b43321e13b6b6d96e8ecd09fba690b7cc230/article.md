## Your Firewall Won't Last Forever.


With Cisco ASA retirement approaching and Cisco year end on the horizon, now is the time to evaluate your security strategy and prepare for what's next.


Join[Xerox IT Solutions](https://www.xerox.com/en-us/services/it-solutions) and Cisco for a practical discussion on firewall replacement options, secure networking best practices, and opportunities to strengthen your security posture while planning for the future.


### Cisco Security Updates & Year-End Planning: Preparing for Cisco ASA Retirement, Secure Networking & What's Next, July 16, 2026 at 11:00 AM CST


45-Minute Live Session + Q&A


#### In this webinar, we'll cover:


-


Cisco ASA requirements and replacement options


-


A brief introduction to Splunk and its role in security visibility


-


How Xerox IT Solutions and Cisco Customer Experience teams can help maximize technology investments


-


Cisco year-end support considerations and security best practices


Featured speakers include Cisco and Xerox IT Solutions experts, including Z Alsalihi, Ryan Larson, Christopher Heinrich, Jason Kennedy, and Jon Zemrac.


Whether you're actively planning a firewall refresh or looking to better understand your options, this session will provide practical guidance to help inform your next steps.


We look forward to seeing you there!
