# State of Vermont Judiciary Case Study

# Keeping Justice Moving: How Vermont Modernized Its Court Technology


Modernizing Vermont Judiciary's network infrastructure enables seamless court operations, ensuring fair access to justice while significantly reducing operational costs.


### About the Client


The Vermont Judicial Branch is committed to providing timely and equitable justice for all residents. As a crucial government branch, it guarantees access to legal recourse through the Supreme Court and the Vermont Superior Court, managing a variety of cases across civil, criminal, environmental, family, and probate divisions, as well as the Judicial Bureau.


### The Challenge


The integrity of Vermont's Judiciary hinges on effectively resolving disputes while ensuring every Vermonter has access to a fair process. With two courts and 20 divisions handling individual rights, public safety, and commercial concerns, the state's Judiciary plays a crucial role in the broader U.S. legal landscape, where state courts manage around 66 million, roughly 95%, of domestic cases annually. However, the onset of the pandemic disrupted the Court’s essential functions. Despite this challenge, maintaining operations—whether virtually or in-person—was non-negotiable. As the pandemic evolved, it became clear that consistent administration of justice required a reliable and secure framework for conducting court proceedings, both remotely and onsite. Efficient technology was crucial for both scenarios. While the transition to hybrid court operations appeared straightforward in theory, the complexities arose from the limitations of an overburdened and aging network.


Relentlessly focused on their mission, the leaders of the State of Vermont Judiciary moved swiftly to ensure the uninterrupted delivery of justice, implementing solutions for seamless transitions between in-person and remote court operations.


### The Solution


The Vermont Judiciary leveraged a pivotal moment to modernize its network and overhaul its assets and infrastructure for secure and consistent access to court proceedings. Our decade-long partnership fostered a deep understanding of the judicial mission and enabled us to support the Court's technological needs, essential for serving the state's nearly 650,000 citizens.


Serving as the virtual Chief Information Officer (CIO), we focused on:


-


Understanding requirements


-


Selecting the right solution providers


-


Offering integration expertise


-


Delivering high-quality experiences on time and within budget.


The results were evident.


### The Results


By deploying Palo Alto Networks' Prisma SD-WAN and Access, the Vermont Judiciary unified its cloud networking and security for remote and in-court personnel. The integration of Aruba Central and Aruba ClearPass facilitated simplified both device management and access control across wired and wireless networks. Consequently, the Vermont Judiciary amplified capacity by 50 times, cut its budget by 10%, maintained constant uptime, and reduced network restoration from weeks to just three hours.


-


**Empowering the Remote Workforce:** The transition to hybrid operations required secure Wi-Fi for employees, with Microsoft Intune reducing device setup time by 75%.


-


**Cost-effective Transitions:** A thorough assessment revealed existing Microsoft 365 licenses, allowing for a shift to a cost-saving hypervisor and a 25% reduction in data center costs.


-


**Right-sizing for the Future:** We also provide insights on emerging technologies, ensuring the Judiciary adopts the right technology solutions that are tailored to its current and future needs—all while remaining agnostic in our approach.


### The Challenge


-


The Vermont Judiciary’s mission is to provide equal access to justice, protect individual rights, resolve disputes fairly, and ensure everyone has their day in court.


-


The pandemic forced a rapid transition to online and hybrid court operations, exposing limitations of an aging, oversubscribed network across 31 locations.


-


An independent review found the infrastructure prevented judges from issuing orders, staff from accessing filings, and citizens from joining hearings.


-


A new, independent Zero Trust network and broader infrastructure modernization were required to deliver reliable, accessible proceedings and support due process.


### The Solution


-


We applied our expertise in integration to design and implement a secure, modern network environment capable of supporting reliable court operations.


-


Solutions were fully customized and vendor agnostic, focused on delivering the most effective outcomes rather than promoting specific Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) or one-size fits all approaches.


-


The team prioritized initiatives that delivered maximum value for the State of Vermont Judiciary, on time and within budget.


-


The engagement included deploying a new independent Wide Area Network (WAN), implementing identity and access management tools, strengthening security controls, expanding data storage capacity, and positioning the Court for future technology needs.


### The Results


-


The new network has achieved 100% uptime, enabling the State of Vermont Judiciary to make meaningful progress on its caseload.


-


With Microsoft Intune supporting court personnel devices, laptop deployment and update management time decreased by 75%.


-


A thoughtful modernization approach also reduced costs, including a 25% decrease in data center compute expenses through migration to a more cost-friendly hypervisor.


-


By maximizing existing assets and agreements, the team leveraged current Microsoft 365 licensing and previously purchased storage to migrate the court’s file server to an improved Microsoft 365 environment.
