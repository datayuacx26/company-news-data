# Xerox Named a Leader in Quocirca’s 2026 Cloud Print Services Landscape


Xerox has been positioned for the fifth consecutive year as a Leader in Quocirca’s Cloud Print Services Landscape 2026. Xerox was also ranked strongest in the market for both strategy and completeness of offering, reinforcing the strength of its cloud-first vision and ability to deliver secure, scalable services for modern work environments.


As organizations modernize print infrastructure and support increasingly distributed workforces, cloud print is becoming an important part of broader workplace transformation. Quocirca recognized Xerox for its strengths in platform depth, security, workflow integration, analytics, and cloud delivery.


## Cloud Print Built for the Modern Workplace


Built on a multi-tenant SaaS architecture aligned with zero trust principles, Xerox cloud solutions help organizations reduce IT complexity while enabling secure, scalable, and flexible operations. Quocirca highlighted the Xerox cloud-first approach, which supports organizations of all sizes—from SMBs to large enterprises—as they transition to hybrid cloud environments.


The Xerox cloud-first model also makes enterprise-grade capabilities more accessible to SMB and mid-market organizations while enabling channel partners to deliver higher-value cloud services to a broader client base.


### Extending Beyond Print


Today, cloud print is about more than managing devices. Organizations are increasingly looking beyond print management toward integrated, intelligent document workflows.


Quocirca recognized Xerox capabilities that extend beyond traditional print management into workflow automation, analytics, and AI-enabled document processing. These capabilities help organizations streamline how information is captured, processed, and managed across the business.


### A Stronger Cloud Platform


The integration of best-of-breed Xerox and Lexmark cloud technologies is further strengthening the Xerox cloud platform, enhancing connectivity, analytics, and device intelligence across distributed environments.
