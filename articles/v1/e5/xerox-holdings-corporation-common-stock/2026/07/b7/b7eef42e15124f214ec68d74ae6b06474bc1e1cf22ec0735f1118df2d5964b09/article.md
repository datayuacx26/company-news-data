# A rebranded coffee shop and updated building signage create a warmer welcome at Northumbria University


## When the university needed new signage for its coffee shop and campus buildings, it knew it could count on us to deliver.


"We're a university, not a printing and signage company: outsourcing to Xerox gives us access to a large global organization that can meet our diverse needs. Whatever we ask our Xerox DAOP for help with, the answer is always 'yes'. He's the guy with the keys to the Xerox kitbag."


— David Jackson, Director of Campus Experience, Northumbria University


### About the Client


A modern, inclusive university based in Newcastle upon Tyne, Northumbria University has a diverse community of around 35,000 students from than 140 countries, and some 3,000 academic and professional staff on campus. Its three dynamic faculties cover society and culture, science and environment, and health and wellbeing.


Northumbria has a global reputation for academic excellence, a wealth of partnerships with higher education establishments worldwide, and close relationships with major employers in the public and private sectors. It aims to change lives through world-leading research and transformative education, enabling people from all backgrounds to succeed.


### The Challenge


In the spring of 2025 Northumbria University started two significant signage projects. The first was to rebrand the on-campus coffee shop, run by the university's catering contractor. "You'd walk down the street and not really notice the coffee shop was there," says David Jackson, the university's Director of Campus Experience. "We wanted to make it brighter and more welcoming, to encourage passing trade and increase footfall."


Planning started in February and the project had to be completed within two months, in time to serve people who would be attending a major conference hosted in the nearby sports center. This timing would also ensure the coffee shop was ready for the return of staff and students after the Easter break.


In parallel, David needed to overhaul the university's building signage to reflect a major academic restructure.


Departments were being renamed as 'schools' and the number of faculties was reducing from four to three, with some schools therefore switching buildings. To reflect these changes, signage on the outside and inside of all buildings and throughout the campus had to be updated in time for the new academic year.


### The Solution


For many years Northumbria University has worked with Xerox as its outsourced partner for design and print. The service covers everything, including:


-


On-demand printing of templated items (like business cards) via a self-service portal.


-


Secure printing and delivery of exam papers.


-


Graphic design and artworking services for materials like prospectuses and brochures.


-


Fulfilment of bespoke requests.


Having extended the initial five-year contract with Xerox several times, Northumbria issued a new tender in 2023 using the Crescent Purchasing Consortia (CPC) Framework, Lot 2 — Digital Transformation Solutions. The university chose Xerox to continue providing the outsourced service based on competitive pricing, outstanding quality, and its status as a trusted advisor.


A critical element of the service is the onsite Xerox Delivery Account Operations Professional (DAOP), Jeff Workman. Working exclusively with the university, Jeff provides consultancy, creative services, project management and quality assurance. Through his collaboration with Northumbria's brand team and his in-depth knowledge of the brand itself, Jeff ensures all the design and print he manages showcases the university's branding to best effect.


#### Coffee shop rebrand


For the coffee shop rebrand, Jeff collaborated with David Jackson's Campus Experience team and the catering contractor to design the new look. He organized delivery of the full range of elements needed, including interior and exterior paintwork, planter graphics, canopies, bin stores, and illuminated signs, and ensured color matching on all the substrates.


Use of the coffee shop's new logo – a steaming coffee cup – was extended to the glass manifestations (that increase glass awareness and comply with health and safety regulations) on the door and windows.


#### Building signage project


The first step in the building signage project was a survey to understand the extent of the project. The survey, which covered 12 buildings (internal and external), identified 96 types of sign to be updated. These ranged from vinyl, stretched fabric and foam board signs to vinyl and brushed steel lettering and building directory boards.


"I was surprised by just how much signage we had in some buildings. Jeff's knowledge of our campus and our branding helped speed up the survey and reduce snagging at the other end of the project."


— David Jackson, Director of Campus Experience, Northumbria University


Jeff created the artwork for the new signs and specified the materials. "I trusted Jeff as the expert to get the details right," says David. "For example, he took care to ensure that, where a school was relocating to a new building, the brushed steel lettering of its name exactly matched the existing school names on the building."


David and the operations director of each faculty worked through the proofing process with Jeff in a timely way, ensuring the artwork was sent to production on the agreed date. In parallel, Jeff liaised with the university's maintenance officer to prepare the campus for the installation of the new signs by removing outdated ones and repainting surfaces.


As planned, the 145 new signs were ready in early September, and Jeff oversaw the fitting teams to install them in time for the start of the academic year. "Staff and students had to be able to find their way around on day one of the new term," says David. "I knew the schedule for the signage project was tight, but I never doubted it would get done in time, because Xerox always delivers."
