## What Is a Niacin Flush?


A


**Niacin Flush is a temporary reaction caused by vasodilation**


- the widening of blood vessels near the skin’s surface.


When certain forms of Niacin are absorbed by the body, they can stimulate the release of compounds called prostaglandins, which increase blood flow close to the skin. This can create sensations such as:


-


Warmth,


-


Tingling,


-


Redness,


-


Itchiness,


-


Or a sudden “rush” sensation.


The flush most commonly appears:


-


On the face,


-


Neck,


-


Chest,


-


Ears,


-


Or upper body.


For many people, it begins approximately 10–20 minutes after consumption and fades naturally shortly afterward.


-


**Importantly:**


**A Niacin Flush is not an allergic reaction.**

## Why Does the Flush Feel Stronger for Some People?


This is where things become highly individual.


No two Resurrections are exactly the same because no two bodies are exactly the same.


Several factors can influence how noticeable the flush feels, including:


**Alcohol Consumption**


Higher alcohol intake can sometimes amplify the sensation.


**Food Intake**


Taking Resurrection on an empty stomach may increase the intensity of the flush for some users.


**Individual Sensitivity**


Some people naturally react more strongly to Niacin than others.


**Timing**


The timing between drinking, eating, hydration, and taking the protocol can affect the experience.


**Niacin Adaptation**


People who use Niacin-containing supplements regularly often report that the flush becomes milder over time as the body adapts.


This variability is completely normal.


As we like to say:


*Sometimes it purrs. Sometimes it roars.*


For most users, the flush


**fades within approximately 10–30 minutes.**


The sensation typically peaks quickly and then gradually disappears as circulation normalizes.


While the experience can feel dramatic if unexpected, it is temporary and self-resolving.


## Is the Niacin Flush Dangerous?


For healthy individuals, a standard


**Niacin Flush is generally considered harmless**


and temporary.


The warmth, redness, and tingling are the result of increased circulation near the skin’s surface - not damage occurring inside the body.


That said, if you have underlying medical conditions, sensitivities, or concerns regarding supplementation, it is always best to consult a healthcare professional before use.
