# Is ChatGPT Actually Getting Your Tax Return Right?

### Tax Rules Change Every Year and AI Often Doesn’t Know That


Tax law isn’t set in stone. Every year brings new legislation, updated IRS guidance, inflation adjustments to brackets and deductions, and changes that affect your specific situation. General AI tools have a “knowledge cutoff” – a point in time after which they stop learning – and they don’t always flag when they might be behind.


‍


That means you can get advice based on rules from a year or two ago without even knowing it. The consequences are the same as acting on a hallucination: penalties, surprises at filing time, and refunds you didn’t get because no one told you the rules changed.


### Your Tax Situation Is Unique to You


No two people’s taxes look the same. Whether you got married this year, had a baby, sold your home, started freelancing on the side, or just moved to a new state – each of those events changes what you owe, what you can deduct, and how you should file. General AI is designed to give answers that work for the widest possible audience, which often means the answer doesn’t really fit anyone’s specific situation.


‍


Think of it this way: someone who freelanced part of the year, bought their first home, and got married all in the same year has a completely different tax picture from a salaried employee who rented all year. A generic answer rarely serves either of them well.
