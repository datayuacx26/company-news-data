# TaxGPT vs Black Ore: Beyond 1040 Automation

## Platform Analysis


Both companies are solving the same underlying problem, accountants drowning in manual 1040 work, but the solutions are in different shapes. Black Ore went narrow and deep, building a system that tries to run the entire individual return end to end with minimal human intervention. TaxGPT, on the other hand, went wide, building a platform that sits underneath the whole engagement: research, prep, review, drafting, planning, and the institutional memory that ties a firm’s client relationships together across years.


### Black Ore


Black Ore pitch is specific and well earned: automate the individual return from intake through the finished audit package, with as little human touch as possible along the way. Black Ore has built automation across data extraction, workpaper assembly, and diagnostic checks. The tradeoff behind this is that Black Ore’s depth is concentrated on one form type. A firm that runs mixed engagements such as corporate returns, advisory work, multi-year planning conversations with clients will need a second system to cover what Black Ore doesn’t touch.


### TaxGPT


TaxGPT starts from a different premise: a firm’s AI shouldn’t stop at a return. It should be built to move with the work, pulling authority for a research question in the morning, helping draft a client letter at noon, flagging a planning opportunity from last year’s file in the afternoon, all within a single system that remembers who a client is and what has already been discussed. The breadth shows up in two directions, first across form types which the range includes the forms firms actually file. The second is across engagements itself: research, preparation, review, client communication and planning all run through the same platform instead of living in four disconnected tools that don’t share context


## The Gap Worth Noting


Black Ore’s depth when it comes to 1040 preparation is good and should not be underestimated. The main differentiator between good AI tax preparation software and great is the ability to offer full service to firms.


### Agent Andrew vs Tax Autopilot’s Anomaly Detection


Black Ore’s review layer runs anomaly detection and an AI copilot inside its own prep pipeline, quality control built to catch errors in what Tax Autopilot itself produced. Along with this, the main supported forms are W-2s/1099s/K-1s in any format.


Agent Andrew reviews work regardless of where the work originated. Whether it's a return prepared in Black Ore's own Autopilot system, a different system, or by a human preparer. It is a judgement-layer review of completed returns for audit risk and missed savings via its Red/Green/Cleared flag system.


This independence is a differentiator between Black Ore and TaxGPT: a firm can adopt Agent Andrew without changing prep at all. For further reading see[Manual Tax Review vs Agent Andrew](https://www.taxgpt.com/taxgpt-insights/manual-tax-review-vs-agent-andrew) .


### Closing the Ecosystem


Tax preparation is one piece of a much longer workflow. Research, client and IRS correspondence, forward-looking planning, and the institutional memory that carries a client relationship across tax years all sit outside the return itself, and that's where the platform gap between the two shows up most clearly.


### Research


Every position taken on a return needs to trace back to an IRC section, a Treasury regulation, or case law, and that lookup work doesn't happen inside Black Ore’s Tax Autopilot. TaxGPT builds research into the same system as prep, and TaxGPT Matrix adds multi-state authority lookups for firms handling returns across jurisdictions. A Black Ore firm still needs a separate research subscription (Checkpoint, CCH, BNA, or similar) to support the positions Autopilot's output relies on.


### Drafting


Client letters, notice responses, and engagement or opinion letters are a recurring cost of doing business, and Autopilot doesn't generate any of them. TaxGPT's Writer handles this range natively, memos, IRS notice responses, and engagement or opinion letters, inside the same workspace as the return. Black Ore firms still need to draft these by hand or route them through a separate tool.


### Planning


Autopilot stops at the completed return; it has no agent for identifying planning opportunities or modeling strategy based on what it just prepared. TaxGPT Cowork's tax planning agent picks up where prep ends, surfacing strategy options directly from the filed return. A Black Ore firm has to run planning as a separate, manual exercise disconnected from what Autopilot produced.


### Client Memory


A return prepared in isolation loses everything a firm learned about that client the year before, prior elections, recurring issues, planning conversations. TaxGPT's Client Intelligence carries that context forward automatically, so each year's engagement builds on the last instead of starting cold. Black Ore firms have to reconstruct that history manually from files and notes each season.


### Canada Support


U.S. only coverage means firms with cross-border or Canadian clients hit a wall immediately, Autopilot has no CRA, ITA, or provincial tax logic. TaxGPT extends its coverage north of the border, supporting Canadian federal and provincial rules inside the same platform. A Black Ore firm serving Canadian clients needs an entirely separate system to cover that work.


### Which Should You Choose?


The decision boils down to what your firm does. If your firm strictly runs 1040 returns then you should choose Black Ore, they handle the volume at enterprise level scales. Choose TaxGPT if everything around the tax season is what matters to you. This includes 1040 preparation but also business returns, engagement with clients, or you are handling multi-jurisdictional work. All of this, handled on one platform.


### FAQ


#### Q: Does Black Ore handle business returns?


Black Ore automates 1040 individual returns. Partnership and fiduciary support, the 1065 and 1041 products, are still listed as "Coming Soon" on Black Ore's own site. TaxGPT already covers business entities today. Agent Andrew reviews the 1040, 1065, 1120, and 1120-S, and Cowork prepares individual and corporate returns inside your existing tax software.


#### Q: Does Black Ore do tax research?


No. Black Ore is "built for firms where the main problem is volume and production speed, not answering complex research questions." Research sits at the center of TaxGPT instead. It answers federal, state, local, territorial, and Canadian tax questions with citations to the IRC, Treasury Regulations, court cases, and rulings in every response.


#### Q: What’s the key difference between TaxGPT and Black Ore?


Black Ore takes source documents like W-2s, 1099s, brokerage statements, and bank records and turns them into a prepared 1040, dropping the finished return into your existing tax software for review. TaxGPT starts earlier in the process and covers more ground. It handles the research question first, reviews the prepared return for errors and missed savings through Agent Andrew, and runs the workflow end to end with Cowork, from individual and corporate return preparation to amended returns, tax projections, and bookkeeping through month-end close. Black Ore replaces one step in one return type. TaxGPT covers research, review, and workflow across the firm.
