# Form 1099-DA Checklist for Tax Professionals

## Deadlines and Processing Timelines


Milestone Deadline Notes


Furnish recipient statements (Form 1099-DA) February 17, 2026 The general instructions specify this as the due date for furnishing statements for Forms 1099-B, 1099-DA, 1099-S, and 1099-MISC (if boxes 8 or 10 are used); confirm current-year updates in the Guide to Information Returns.


File 1099-DA with IRS (paper) Generally February 28 (for many 1099s) Paper filing is discouraged and subject to e-file mandate (≥10 returns); check the Guide to Information Returns for current-year due dates and consider IRIS e-file.


File 1099-DA with IRS (electronically) Generally March 31 E-file required if you file 10 or more information returns in aggregate; use IRIS (FIRE retiring for TY 2026/FS 2027). Confirm exact date each year in the Guide to Information Returns.

Correction window reduced for penalties Within 30 days after due dates Correcting within 30 days reduces per-return penalties; confirm rates and caps under IRC § 6721 and § 6722.


Form 945 (backup withholding annual return) January 31 (following year) Report annual backup withholding on nonpayroll payments; make deposits via EFTPS per Instructions for Form 945.


2025 transition relief (penalties) Applies to 2025 filings IRS will not impose certain penalties for failure to file/furnish 1099-DA for 2025 transactions if brokers make good-faith efforts; backup withholding relief also applies under specified conditions.

### FAQs


#### Who must file form 1099-DA?


Brokers that take possession of digital assets being sold or exchanged by customers (including custodial platforms, certain hosted wallet providers, kiosks, and payment processors) must report gross proceeds on Form 1099-DA starting with transactions on or after January 1, 2025.


#### What information is reported and when does basis reporting begin?


Gross proceeds must be reported starting in 2025; basis reporting on certain transactions begins for transactions on or after January 1, 2026.


#### What are the key deadlines for 1099-DA?


Furnish recipient statements by February 17, 2026; file with the IRS by the applicable paper/electronic deadlines (generally February 28 for paper, March 31 for e-file; confirm current-year dates in the Guide to Information Returns). E-file is mandatory if you file >= 10 information returns.


#### Is there penalty relief for 2025 transactions?


Yes. For transactions occurring in 2025 (reported in 2026), the IRS will not impose penalties for failure to file or furnish forms 1099-DA if brokers make a good-faith effort. Backup withholding obligations are also relieved in certain situations for 2025-2026.


#### What penalties apply for incorrect/missing filings?


IRC § 6721 and § 6722 impose per-return penalties with tiered reductions for timely corrections, lower caps for small businesses, and increased penalties for international disregard.


#### How can I mitigate penalties if issues arise?


Establish reasonable cause by showing due diligence and responsible actions (such as TIN solicitations, prompt corrections, addressing impediments). Follow correction procedures and retain documentation.


#### How do backup withholding and annual reporting work?


Apply backup withholding when required and report the withheld tax annually on Form 945; make deposits via EFTPS. See the IRS backup withholding overview and Form 945 instructions.
