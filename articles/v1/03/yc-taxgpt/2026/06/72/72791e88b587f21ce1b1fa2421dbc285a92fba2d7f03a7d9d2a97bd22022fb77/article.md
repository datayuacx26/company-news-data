# Form 1065 Checklist for Tax Professionals

Form 1065, the U.S. Return of Partnership Income, is filed by domestic and foreign partnerships doing business in the United States. It reports a partnership’s total income, deductions, liabilities, and distributions to partners, ensuring that each partner’s tax obligations are correctly calculated and documented through Schedule K-1.


Whether you are preparing a two-member LLC or a multi-jurisdictional partnership with foreign owners, accuracy is critical. This checklist gives you a structured, CPA-reviewed workflow to prepare, review, and file a compliant Form 1065 package while reducing errors, penalties, and IRS correspondence.


[Ask TaxGPT Research](https://www.taxgpt.com/tax-research-software) any technical questions that come up during preparation, including K-2/K-3 applicability, basis rules, foreign partner requirements, and updated thresholds.


## Form 1065 Checklist


### 1. Filing requirements and due date


☐ Confirm the entity meets the definition of a partnership required to file Form 1065.


☐ Due date: 15th day of the third month after the end of the tax year (March 15 for calendar-year partnerships).


☐ File Form 7004 for a six-month extension.


☐ Qualified Opportunity Funds must file Form 8996 even with no income or expenses.


If the partnership’s filing status or entity classification is unclear, you can use[TaxGPT Research](https://www.taxgpt.com/tax-research-software) to verify whether Form 1065 is required for the year.


### 2. Electronic filing rules


☐ E-filing is mandatory for partnerships filing 10 or more returns in aggregate for the year.


☐ Hardship waivers may be requested under IRS instructions.


☐ Partnerships subject to the BBA Centralized Audit Regime must designate a Partnership Representative or attach Schedule B-2 to elect out if eligible.


### 3. Core Form 1065 preparation


☐ Review entity information (EIN, business code, accounting method, total assets).


☐ Report gross receipts and complete Form 1125-A if applicable.


☐ Include other income such as:


1. Form 4797 (asset sales)
2. Form 6252 (installment income)


☐ Deduct allowable expenses (salaries, rent, depreciation via Form 4562, retirement contributions, etc.).


☐ Complete Schedule K for distributive share items.


☐ Prepare Schedules L, M-1, and M-2 unless the small-partnership exception applies.


### 4. Schedule B: Other information


☐ Attach Schedule B-1 if any partner owns 50 percent or more of capital or profits.


☐ Report related entities, including corporations, partnerships, and trusts.


☐ Disclose basis adjustments such as Sections 754, 743(b), and 734(b).


☐ Address foreign partner activity and related forms (8805, 8938, K-2, K-3).


☐ Answer the digital asset question.


☐ Complete Schedule B-2 if electing out of the BBA audit regime.


[Ask TaxGPT Research](https://www.taxgpt.com/tax-research-software) to walk you through foreign partner disclosures, basis adjustment rules, and Schedule B-1/B-2 requirements.


### 5. Schedules K-1, K-2, and K-3


☐ Prepare accurate Schedule K-1s for each partner showing:


- Income and loss items
- Deductions and credits
- Capital account changes
- Share of liabilities


☐ Determine if Schedules K-2 and K-3 apply (based on current-year exceptions).


☐ Provide at-risk (Form 6198) and passive activity information (Form 8582).


☐ If property distributions occur, ensure partners receive data needed to complete Form 7217.


When the return is complete, use[Agent Andrew](https://www.taxgpt.com/agent-andrew) to review the Form 1065 package to detect errors, mismatched partner data, incorrect capital account movements, and issues affecting partner-level tax reporting.


### 6. Capital accounts and basis support


☐ Maintain tax-basis capital accounts as required since 2020.


☐ Apply IRS Notice 2021-13 relief if corrections are needed in good faith.


☐ Supply partners with data required to compute outside basis, including liabilities and 743(b) adjustments if applicable.


### 7. Common attachments and statements


☐ Attach forms as applicable:


- Forms 1125-A, 4562, 4797, 6252, 8996, 7205
- Schedules B-1, B-2, K-2, and K-3
Section 754 election and supporting 743(b)/734(b) adjustments
- Foreign withholding and reporting forms (1042, 8805, 8938)


### 8. Partner deliverables


☐ Furnish Schedules K-1 (K2, and K-3 if required) by the return’s due date, including extensions.


☐ Correct any errors promptly to avoid information-return penalties.


☐ Penalties increase with delay; reasonable cause relief may apply under Publication 1586.


### 9. Penalties and relief provisions


- Failure-to-file penalty under Section 6698:


- $235 per partner per month for 2024, increasing to $245 after December 31, 2024.


- Small partnership penalty relief: Rev. Proc. 84-35 for partnerships with 10 or fewer qualifying partners.
- First-Time Abate may apply via Form 843.
- K-1 accuracy errors may trigger Notice 972CG; respond timely.
