# Form 1040 Schedule C Checklist for Tax Professionals

### Schedule C doesn't have to break your workflow


Preparing Schedule C should be straightforward but with undocumented expenses, missing mileage logs, incorrect NAICS codes, outdated depreciation schedules, and incomplete substantiation, it often becomes one of the most error-prone components of[Form 1040](https://www.taxgpt.com/blog/form-1040-preparation-checklist) .


A single mistake drives IRS notices, understated income, missed deductions, faulty SE tax calculations, and QBI errors that ripple into the rest of the return. And when clients hand you a box of receipts, three bank accounts, and a mixed pile of business and personal transactions? Your prep time multiplies and billable hours turn into cleanup hours.


This checklist removes uncertainty. It gives you a clear path for completing Schedule C with full compliance, cross-form accuracy, and defensible substantiation.


## Before You Start: Pre-Engagement Intake


Gather these items before opening Schedule C:


☐ **Prior-year return + carryforwards** Depreciation, Section 179, listed property, QBI losses, suspended losses.


☐ **Confirm entity structure** Sole proprietorship or single-member LLC taxed as disregarded entity.


☐ **Determine Section 162 trade or business status** Ensure the activity is profit-motivated and meets the “ordinary and necessary” standard.


☐ **Business profile details** Business name, address, EIN, start date, accounting method.


☐ **NAICS classification** Verify correct business activity code from Schedule C instructions.

## Schedule C Preparation Checklist (Line-by-Line)


### Business Information


☐ Enter business name, address, EIN (if any), accounting method
☐ Confirm new business start date if applicable
☐ Select accurate Principal Business Code (NAICS)


### Income: Gross Receipts and Other Income


☐ Collect all invoices, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, platform statements, cash receipts, etc.
☐ Reconcile to books/prepare a bank deposit analysis
☐ Adjust for returns, allowances, and other income
☐ Document non-cash or digital payments (PayPal, Stripe, Venmo)


### Cost of Goods Sold (If Applicable)


☐ Determine if inventory exists (property held for sale, not depreciable)
☐ Beginning/ending inventory values
☐ Purchases and materials
☐ Inventory valuation method (cost, lower of cost or market, other)
☐ Classify materials & supplies -


## Deductible Expense Checklist


Every expense must be ordinary, necessary, documented, and business-related.


### Car and Truck Expenses


☐ Choose actual vs. standard mileage (70¢ for 2025)
☐ Verify mileage logs: date, miles, business purpose
☐ Apply listed property rules
☐ Ensure no depreciation if standard mileage is used first year


### Travel Expenses


☐ Must be away from tax home
☐ Substantiate transportation, lodging, meals (50%)
☐ Business purpose must be clear and documented


### Meals


☐ Record amount, place, business purpose, and attendees
☐ Verify no entertainment component included


### Home Office


☐ Exclusive and regular use test
☐ Principal place of business or client meeting space
☐ Choose actual vs. simplified method
☐ Attach Form 8829 if using actual method


### Supplies, Tools, Office Expenses


☐ Deduct when first used/consumed (non-incidental)
☐ Deduct when paid (incidental) if no consumption records
☐ Allocate personal vs. business use as needed


### Other Common Expense Categories


☐ Advertising
☐ Insurance (business)
☐ Rent or lease payments
☐ Utilities☐ Professional fees
☐ Bank charges
☐ Postage
☐ Education (if maintaining/improving skills)


## Depreciation & Section 179 Checklist


Most Schedule C errors occur due to incorrect depreciation entries.


### Form 4562: Depreciation and Amortization


☐ Report new assets placed in service
☐ Include basis, date, method, recovery period, convention
☐ Apply listed property rules for vehicles/equipment


### Section 179 Expensing


☐ Confirm property eligibility
☐ Apply annual §179 limit + business income limitation
☐ Election made on Form 4562
☐ SUV limitations apply
☐ Verify Pub 946 for current-year limits


### Bonus Depreciation


☐ Confirm eligibility for tax year
☐ Ensure basis and business-use percentage support the claim

### Qualified Business Income (QBI) Deduction: Section 199A


☐ Determine if Schedule C income qualifies
☐ Exclude non-qualifying items (wages, guaranteed payments, capital gains)
☐ Use Form 8995 (simple) or 8995-A (complex)
☐ Check taxable income thresholds
☐ Apply aggregation rules if used
☐ Track suspended QBI losses


### Self-Employment Tax (Schedule SE)


☐ Compute SE tax based on Schedule C net profit
☐ Calculate above-the-line SE tax deduction
☐ Ensure correct flow to Form 1040 (lines 1–15)


## Substantiation & Recordkeeping


☐ Maintain receipts, invoices, proof of payment
☐ Mileage logs must be contemporaneous
☐ Document purpose for travel/meals
☐ Home office square footage worksheets
☐ Asset ledgers and depreciation schedules
☐ Keep records for minimum IRS retention period


### Required Attachments & Cross-Forms


- **Form 4562** — Depreciation, §179, listed property
- **Form 8829** — Home office (actual method)
- **Forms 8995 / 8995-A** — QBI deduction
- **Schedule SE** — Self-employment tax


## Final Quality Control


☐ Verify Schedule C totals flow correctly to Form 1040
☐ Confirm SE tax and QBI deductions integrate correctly
☐ Attach all required forms
☐ Cross-check income against forms and bank statements
☐ Confirm Section 179 elections are accurate
☐ Ensure no personal expenses are included
