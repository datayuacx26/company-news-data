# Sales Tax Nexus: When & Where You Must Collect

### Multi-State Complexity


Managing sales tax compliance across multiple states is one of the biggest challenges for businesses, especially after the Wayfair ruling. With each state setting its own tax rates, economic nexus thresholds, and filing deadlines, businesses must stay vigilant to avoid penalties and ensure accurate tax collection.


#### 1. Navigating State-Specific Tax Requirements


Each state enforces unique sales tax rules based on the Wayfair ruling, requiring businesses to track:


- Varying economic nexus thresholds.
- Tax-exempt product classifications, as some states tax digital goods, services, or shipping fees differently.
- Local tax variations, since cities and counties may impose additional taxes beyond the state rate.


#### 2. Keeping Up with Legislative Changes


Sales tax laws frequently change, with states updating nexus thresholds, reporting requirements, and exemptions. Businesses should:


- Subscribe to state tax agency updates to stay informed on law changes.
- Review legislative changes quarterly to ensure ongoing compliance.
- Adjust internal tax policies accordingly to reflect new regulations.


#### 3. Risk Management & Liability for Past Sales


Businesses that previously did not collect sales tax in states where they now have nexus may be at risk for retroactive tax liability for uncollected sales tax or state[audits or penalties for non-compliance](https://www.taxgpt.com/blog/irs-tax-audit) .


To mitigate risks, businesses should:


- Conduct a nexus review to assess past exposure.
- Consider voluntary disclosure agreements (VDAs) in states where tax obligations were previously overlooked.
- Implement a clear compliance strategy to prevent further liabilities.


#### 4. Using Marketplace Facilitators for Sales Tax Compliance


Many states require marketplace facilitators (e.g., Amazon, eBay, Etsy) to collect and remit sales tax on behalf of third-party sellers. Businesses selling through these platforms can:


- Reduce their direct sales tax collection burden, as the marketplace often handles it.
- Verify tax collection policies of each marketplace to ensure compliance.
- Understand reporting obligations, as some states still require sellers to report facilitated sales.


While marketplace facilitators simplify tax collection, businesses must still track where they have nexus for non-marketplace sales.


### Tools & Automation


With the growing complexity of multi-state sales tax laws, businesses must adopt automation tools to maintain compliance, reduce errors, and streamline reporting. Relying on manual calculations is no longer practical, especially for businesses selling across multiple states.


Modern sales tax automation tools can handle real-time tax calculations, multi-state filings, and ongoing compliance monitoring. Businesses use these tools to:


- Automatically apply the correct tax rates based on the buyer's location.
- Track economic nexus thresholds to determine when new tax registrations are required.
- Generate and file sales tax returns with state authorities.
- Reduce audit risks by ensuring transactions are accurately taxed and documented.
