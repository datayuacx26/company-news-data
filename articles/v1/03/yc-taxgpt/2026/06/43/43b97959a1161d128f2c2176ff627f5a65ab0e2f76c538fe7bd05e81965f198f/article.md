# Form 1040 Preparation Checklist for Tax Professionals

Filing Form 1040 doesn’t have to be a multi-week marathon


Preparing an accurate Form 1040 shouldn’t mean sorting through dozens of schedules, thresholds, and mismatched client details. But too often, it still does.


Between mismatched dependents, missing 1099-K adjustments, and unclaimed credits buried in Schedule 3, one oversight can mean rework, client calls, and delayed refunds.


A single unchecked box on Schedule 8812 or an outdated Form 1098 can trigger notices that derail your workflow. For multi-state taxpayers, verifying residency and allocation adds even more complexity.


This checklist simplifies every step of the Form 1040 process, helping you prepare and review returns efficiently, accurately, and right the first time.


## Before You Start: Filing Requirement, Intake, and Due Date Controls


☐ **Confirm filing requirement** by status, age, and gross income. Note special cases: self-employment ≥ $400, marketplace APTC reconciliation, net investment income, and HSA/IRA additional taxes.


☐ **Set the due date** : generally April 15; track weekend/holiday shifts, disaster relief postponements, and automatic 2-month extension for qualifying taxpayers abroad (interest still accrues).


☐ **Extension planning** : prepare Form 4868 with a pay-to-zero target; document how the estimate was computed.


☐ **E-file strategy** : plan to e-file for acknowledgment, faster refunds, and reduced schema errors; confirm software year and schema version.


☐ **Identity protection** : check for an IP PIN requirement; retrieve or renew as needed.


☐ **Authorizations** : verify POA/Tax Information Authorization (Form 2848/8821), CAF updates, and client e-signature method.


☐ **Transcripts** : pull wage and income transcripts (W-2/1099/1098), account transcripts for carryforwards, and prior-year AGI for e-file PIN.

### Step 1: Identity, Filing Status, Household, and Dependents


☐ **Legal name/SSN/ITIN** must match SSA/IRS; resolve name changes and hyphenation exactly.


☐ **Digital assets question** : answer accurately; confirm taxable events and Form 8949 routing if applicable.


☐ **Filing status** : determine Single, MFJ, MFS, HOH, or QSS using support, residency, and qualifying person tests. Document HOH/QSS evidence.


☐ **Dependents** : verify SSNs/ITINs, relationship, residency, support, tie-breaker rules, and adoption/ITIN timing. Note effects on CTC/ODC and education credits.


☐ **Kiddie tax** : identify unearned income exposure; route to Form 8615 as needed.


☐ **Residency profile** : full-year vs part-year, dual-status, and foreign residency (treaty/FEIE/FTC handled in linked content).


### Step 2: Income Completeness (Form 1040 Lines + Schedule 1)


☐ **Wages/withholding** (W-2): reconcile to transcripts; check RSUs/ISOs reporting, pre-tax deductions, and state allocations.


☐ **Interest/dividends** (1099-INT/DIV): identify tax-exempt interest, qualified dividends, and foreign tax paid.


☐ **Retirement** (1099-R): compute taxable amounts, check rollover codes, early distribution penalties/exceptions (route to Form 5329 where applicable).


☐ **Social Security** (SSA-1099): apply inclusion worksheet; coordinate with other income for taxable portion.


☐ **Capital assets** : confirm whether **Form 8949** is required before Schedule D (basis reported/no adjustments exceptions). Track wash sales and crypto disposals.


☐ **Other income via Schedule 1** : business/farm, rents/royalties, unemployment, prizes, cancellation of debt, and the **1099-K personal-loss adjustment** entry (when applicable).


☐ **Foreign items** : wages, bank interest, dividends; route to FEIE/FTC as appropriate (details in linked schedules/credits content).


☐ **State tax refunds** : include when itemized in prior year and tax benefit rule applies.


☐ **Nontaxable vs taxable settlements** : classify correctly (physical injury vs punitive, back pay, etc.).

### Step 3: Adjustments to Income (Schedule 1, Part II)


☐ **Educator expenses** : cap and eligible costs.


☐ **HSA deduction** : confirm eligibility, contributions vs. distributions, and Form 8889 attachment.


☐ **Self-employed** : deductible half of SE tax, SEP/SIMPLE/qualified plan contributions, and **self-employed health insurance** (Form 7206).


☐ **Alimony paid** : only for pre-TCJA instruments or properly grandfathered; include recipient TIN.


☐ **IRA deduction** : coverage status and phase-out checks; Roth vs traditional interactions.


☐ **Student loan interest** : income limits and lender statements.


☐ **Moving expenses** : Armed Forces on active duty only.


### Step 4: Deductions: Standard vs Itemized (Schedule A)


☐ **Compare** standard deduction to itemized totals; document your selection logic.


☐ **Medical** : apply the 7.5 percent AGI threshold; coordinate S-corp reimbursement plans and HSA usage.


☐ **SALT** : apply the $40,000 cap; consider sales tax election where beneficial.


☐ **Mortgage interest/points** : verify Form 1098 details, acquisition debt limits, refinances, and tracing of home equity interest.


☐ **Charitable** : cash vs noncash substantiation, Form 8283 thresholds, and carryovers.


☐ **Casualty/theft** : federally declared disaster requirements; route to Form 4684.


### Step 5: Credits, Reconciliations, and Payments (Schedules 8812 & 3)


☐ **Child & dependent credits** : confirm ages, residency, and SSN/ITIN rules; compute via **Schedule 8812** and verify refundable portions.


☐ **Education credits** : 1098-T reconciliation, scholarship adjustments, and double-benefit guardrails (no double dipping with 529/HSA).


☐ **Foreign tax credit / other nonrefundable credits** : verify source and limitation mechanics.


☐ **Premium tax credit** : reconcile **Form 1095-A** via **Form 8962** ; track APTC repayment caps.


☐ **Withholding and estimates** : reconcile W-2/1099 withholding and 1040-ES payments to transcripts; apply extension payments and prior-year credits.


### Step 6: Taxes and Additional Taxes (Schedule 2 and SE)


☐ **Tax computation** : qualified dividends/capital gains worksheet where applicable; coordinate with NIIT.


☐ **Self-employment tax** : **Schedule SE** for net earnings ≥ $400, include partnership guaranteed payments.


☐ **Additional Medicare Tax** : Form 8959 thresholds; coordinate with SE income and wages.


☐ **NIIT** : Form 8960 exposure on net investment income; basis/tracing and passive activity interactions.


☐ **Household employment** : household employer taxes and W-2 filing confirmations.


☐ **Retirement plan additional taxes** : Form 5329 exceptions and recapture.


### Step 7: Refunds, Balance Due, and Safe Harbors


☐ **Estimated tax safe harbors** : 100/110 percent prior-year rule or 90 percent current-year; annualize for uneven income.


☐ **Refunds** : use direct deposit; **Form 8888** to split refunds; verify routing/account numbers.


☐ **Balance due** : set up IRS online payments, EFTPS, or payment plans; disclose first-time abatement eligibility where relevant.

### Step 8: Attachments and E-File Assembly Quality Control


☐ **Include required schedules** (A–E, SE, 1–3) and all **W-2/1099/K-1** attachments.


☐ **PDF statements** : attach brokerage detail or statements when schema requires or summaries are used.


☐ **Name controls** and **TIN formats** : confirm per e-file rules; validate prior-year AGI/self-select PIN.


☐ **Digital assets** and foreign disclosures: ensure internal consistency across answers and attached forms.


☐ **Bank info** : routing number checksum and account type.


### Step 9: Due Diligence, Documentation, and Carryforwards


☐ **Working papers** : identity, filing status, dependents, and residency proof.


☐ **Income substantiation** : W-2/1099s, consolidated 1099-B, SSA-1099, 1099-K with notes on personal vs business.


☐ **Basis records** : stock lots, crypto cost basis, real property improvements, depreciation schedules (linked content).


☐ **Business records** : books, mileage logs, home office calculations.


☐ **Carryforwards** : capital loss, charitable, education, QBI, passive losses, NIIT basis adjustments, document rollovers.


☐ **Signatures & acknowledgments** : retain e-sign logs, engagement letters, and organizer responses.

## Rejection Triggers to Watch (And How to Avoid Them)


- **SSN/ITIN mismatches or name control errors** : match SSA/IRS records; watch hyphens and suffixes.
- ‍ **Out-of-date forms or schema** : wrong-year form versions, unsupported attachments.
- **Unlinked or missing schedules** : 1–3, SE, or D omitted when referenced on 1040.
- **Digital assets answered “No” despite activity** : ensure 8949 or other reporting where needed.
- **Incorrect refundable credit reconciliations** : Schedule 8812 and 8962 math or eligibility mismatches.
- **Bank info errors** : invalid routing numbers or account type.
