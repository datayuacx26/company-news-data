# Form 944 Checklist for Tax Professionals

Simplified filing doesn’t mean simple errors


Form 944 was created for small employers, those with $1,000 or less in annual federal employment tax liability, to ease the quarterly burden of Form 941. But “simpler” doesn’t mean risk-free.
Every year, tax professionals see penalties triggered by one small oversight: missing IRS authorization, mismatched W-2 totals, or incorrect deposit timing.


Precision still matters. From verifying eligibility to reconciling wage data and filing electronically, Form 944 demands the same diligence as any quarterly filing.


This checklist breaks down the full process so you can help clients stay compliant, avoid costly notices, and maintain clean year-end reconciliation records.


## Form 944 Checklist


### Step 1: Confirm Filing Eligibility


☐ Ensure the client received written IRS authorization to file Form 944.


☐ If no authorization exists, default to Form 941.


☐ Use IRS opt-in/opt-out procedures if filing status changes.


☐ Verify EIN and business information using Form 8822-B.


☐ Confirm no specialized returns (Form 943 or 945) are required.


Ask[TaxGPT Research](https://www.taxgpt.com/tax-research-software) to instantly verify Form 944 eligibility, opt-in/opt-out procedures, and IRS authorization references before you begin.


### Step 2: Prepare Payroll Systems Before the Year Starts


☐ Configure systems to calculate FITW, FICA (6.2% SS + 1.45% Medicare), and Additional Medicare Tax (0.9%).


☐ Select the correct deposit schedule (monthly or semiweekly).


☐ Ensure all deposits are made electronically via EFTPS.


☐ Review electronic filing thresholds, employers filing 10+ returns must e-file.


### Step 3: Withholding and Deposits During the Year


☐ Calculate withholdings using Form W-4 and Pub. 15-T.


☐ Match and remit FICA contributions electronically on time.


☐ Track employee tips and report them accurately.


☐ Keep FUTA (Form 940) separate from Form 944 reporting.


### Step 4: Year-End Reconciliation


☐ Reconcile wages, withholdings, and deposits across payroll records.


☐ File Forms W-2 and W-3 with the SSA by early February.


☐ Verify EIN, business name, and totals match across filings.


Let[Agent Andrew](https://www.taxgpt.com/agent-andrew) review your Form 944 totals against payroll and W-2/W-3 data, flagging mismatches before you e-file!


### Step 5: Prepare and File Form 944


☐ **Due Date:** January 31 (following the tax year).


☐ Confirm 944 eligibility for the current year.


☐ Include:


- Total wages and federal income tax withheld
- Employer/employee FICA and Additional Medicare Tax
- Adjustments for sick pay, tips, or rounding
- File electronically if you meet e-file thresholds.


### Step 6: Make Payments (If Balance Due)


☐ Pay electronically via EFTPS or IRS Direct Pay.


☐ Confirm total deposits and W-2 wages reconcile with final 944 totals.


### Step 7: Amended or Adjusted Returns


☐ Use **Form 944-X** to correct prior-year errors.


### Step 8: Penalty and Interest Prevention


☐ Monitor for late filing (FTF), payment (FTP), and deposit (FTD) penalties.


☐ Apply reasonable cause relief where applicable.


### Step 9: Recordkeeping and Reconciliation Controls


☐ Maintain payroll records, EFT confirmations, and FICA computations.


☐ Reconcile:


- Form 944 vs. Payroll summaries
- Form 944 vs. W-2/W-3 totals
- Deposits vs. EFT records
