### AI Models


#### TaxGPT's Multi-Model Approach


TaxGPT is built to be model-agnostic, running on a combination of the latest frontier models and a proprietary model fine-tuned specifically for tax. This architecture ensures customers automatically benefit from every advancement in AI performance, accuracy, and reasoning, regardless of which model leads the pack.


#### Blue J's Approach


Blue J runs on frontier large language models (notably OpenAI) layered over its curated database of primary tax authority, Tax Notes, and IBFD commentary. It's a strong, defensible setup for research and prediction. What it isn't built for is broad workflow integration, Blue J is a research engine, not an end-to-end platform, so firms still hand off prep, review, and client management to other tools.


Independent benchmarking shows what that architecture delivers: see[how TaxGPT outperforms general-purpose models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google on tax accuracy and hallucination rates.](https://www.taxgpt.com/blog/taxgpt-is-state-of-the-art-tax-llm)

#### Blue J's Document Upload Feature

Where the two diverge is depth and scale: TaxGPT is built to ingest and reconcile large document sets across entities in a single client context, while Blue J's document features are oriented around grounding individual research questions rather than driving downstream prep and review workflows.

### Client Intelligence and Memory


#### TaxGPT's Client Storage


With TaxGPT, managing client data goes beyond static folders. Our[intelligent system](https://www.taxgpt.com/tax-crm) creates secure, AI-enhanced client records where you can store everything, from prior-year returns and supporting documents to notes, jurisdiction info, and more. Each time you upload a file or start a conversation, it’s automatically linked to that client’s profile, allowing TaxGPT to provide smarter, more personalized answers.


This creates powerful workflow advantages:


- Contextual Memory: TaxGPT doesn't just answer questions, it remembers who you're asking them for. Every time you research, review, or write, it pulls in your client's full context from entity structures, filing history, and more.
- Relationship Mapping: Connect related entities to reveal ownership structures, inter-company relationships, and compliance dependencies, all in one view.
- Historical View: See the full timeline of a client’s filings, documents, and past tax decisions.


#### Blue J's Storage Features


Blue J organizes work through Workspaces and Folios and lets you attach client files for context within a research thread. What it doesn't offer is a persistent, cross-session client profile — there's no client record that remembers entity structures, prior-year positions, and filing history across every interaction. That's the gap TaxGPT's Client Intelligence fills: today's client-focused firms increasingly want research, writing, and review that carry full client context automatically, not just fast standalone answers.


### Tax Return Review and Quality Control


#### TaxGPT's AI-Powered Return Review Features


Most AI research platforms stop at answering questions, but TaxGPT goes beyond that, reimagining the tax workflow from end to end. At the center of this shift is Agent Andrew, our[AI-powered return review engine](https://www.taxgpt.com/agent-andrew) , purpose-built to improve the review process.


Here’s how it works:


Agent Andrew analyzes tax returns to source documents and instantly generates a detailed review report filled with actionable insights. Each item is prioritized using a color-coded system designed for tax professionals:


- 🔴 Red: Identifies high-risk issues like transcription errors, omissions, misclassifications, and mismatched figures, all of which significantly increase a client's audit risk.
- 🟢 Green: Surfaces missed opportunities for tax savings, from home office deductions to retirement contributions and bonus depreciation elections.
- ⚪ Cleared: Represent resolved or rejected red & green flags. These items require no further action and support.


With Agent Andrew, you’re not just answering questions; you’re reviewing smarter, faster, and with built-in tax intelligence.

#### Blue J's Traditional Approach


Blue J does not offer AI-driven tax return reviews. Its platform is focused exclusively on research, meaning tax professionals must rely on traditional, manual quality control methods or bring in third-party tools to check for errors, risks, and opportunities.


### Agentic Workflows


Research and review are table stakes. The real divergence between these platforms is what happens after the research question is answered.


[TaxGPT Cowork](https://www.taxgpt.com/cowork) runs agentic workflows that execute real firm work end to end —[AI tax return preparation](https://www.taxgpt.com/ai-tax-preparation) inside your existing software (ProConnect, CCH Axcess, Drake, UltraTax), 1065 K-1 extraction and partner memos, R&D tax credit studies, entity-structure reviews, amended returns, bookkeeping close, and interactive tax-planning projections. These aren't chat answers; they're completed deliverables.


Blue J has no equivalent. As a research-and-prediction engine, it stops at the answer — prep, review, and client management happen in other systems. For a firm trying to consolidate its stack rather than add another point tool, that's the deciding difference.
