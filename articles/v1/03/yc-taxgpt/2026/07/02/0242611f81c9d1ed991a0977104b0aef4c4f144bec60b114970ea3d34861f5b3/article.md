# Why "That's the Way We've Always Done It" No Longer Works: Embracing AI in the Tax Profession


For many seasoned tax professionals, the mantra "That's the way we've always done it" feels like a badge of honor—a testament to tried-and-true processes that have worked for years. I first encountered this phrase prominently displayed on a whiteboard in the conference room of my first public accounting job. My boss, Steve, used it as a gentle reminder to challenge the status quo, and it has stayed with me ever since.


Fast forward to today, and the tax profession faces a transformative opportunity: artificial intelligence (AI). The question isn’t whether AI will reshape the industry but how tax professionals will respond. For those reluctant to explore this new frontier, let’s unpack why it’s worth taking the leap—and why clinging to the old ways may hold you and your firm back.


## The Case for AI in Tax Practices


### 1. **Inexpensive and Accessible**


AI tools, including TaxGPT, have disrupted the market by offering cost-effective alternatives to traditional software. While a tool like TaxGPT costs $1,000 annually, legacy systems can run into the tens of thousands per year—and often come with steeper learning curves and limited adaptability.


For small firms or independent practitioners, the cost savings alone justify a closer look. Many AI platforms also offer free or freemium models, allowing you to test the waters before committing. Even for larger firms, adopting AI can optimize spending and redirect resources toward higher-value activities, such as client relationship management.


### 2. **Easy to Learn**


You don’t need a degree in computer science to use AI effectively. Most modern AI tools are designed with user-friendliness in mind. With basic knowledge of how to prompt AI and a willingness to experiment, both new hires and seasoned partners can quickly integrate these tools into their workflows.


For example, you can use AI to automate routine tasks like document drafting, tax code research, or summarizing new legislation. The ease of use makes AI an equalizer—helping professionals across skill levels achieve better outcomes faster.


### 3. **Flexible and Adaptable**


Unlike traditional, rigid software, AI operates like a "choose-your-own-adventure" tool. You interact with it in natural language, guiding it to meet your specific needs. Whether you’re drafting a client communication, analyzing financial data, or brainstorming strategies to reduce a client’s tax liability, AI adapts to your workflow—not the other way around.


This adaptability allows tax professionals to start small and scale usage as confidence grows. Perhaps you begin by automating meeting notes or data entry before moving on to client analysis or scenario modeling. You can implement AI at your own pace without overhauling your entire practice.


### 4. **Modular Integration**


One of AI’s greatest strengths is its modularity. It doesn’t have to take over your practice all at once. Instead, you can use AI selectively for tasks like:


- **Tax research:** Summarizing IRS rulings or identifying changes in state tax codes.
- **Communication:** Drafting engagement letters, client emails, or marketing materials.
- **Client or team meetings:** Automating notetaking and meeting recording.


By starting small, you minimize disruption while reaping measurable benefits almost immediately.


### 5. **Rapid Innovation**


AI evolves at a pace unmatched by any previous technology. Tools that seemed rudimentary six months ago may now offer advanced functionalities. For example, recent iterations of TaxGPT include features like real-time legislative updates, document upload, and personalized recommendations tailored to client profiles.


If you’ve tested AI tools in the past and found them lacking, it’s worth revisiting them. Chances are, they’ve already improved—or new tools have emerged that address your specific needs.


## Why Now Is the Time to Act


As we approach the next tax season, the urgency to explore AI grows. Waiting too long risks falling behind in a profession increasingly shaped by technological advancements. The benefits—cost savings, improved efficiency, enhanced accuracy—are too compelling to ignore.


By experimenting with AI today, you gain:


- **Experience:** Familiarity with the tools, strengths, and limitations.
- **Insight:** A clearer understanding of where AI fits in your workflow.
- **Advantage:** A competitive edge in a market that values innovation.
