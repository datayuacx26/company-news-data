# 🚩 Red Flag | AI Prompt Engineering 101 | TaxGPT's Newsletter

Examples of Bad Prompting and Good Prompting


‍


Bad Prompting


‍


1. Vague or Ambiguous Prompts:


Example: "Tell me about income tax."


This prompt lacks specificity and context, making it difficult for the AI to determine the user's intent. The response may cover too broad a range of topics, leaving the user unsatisfied.


‍


2. Prompts That Are Too Short:


Example: "Income tax."


This prompt is too brief and doesn't provide enough information for the AI to generate a meaningful response. The user might receive a generic answer that doesn't address their specific needs.


‍


3. Overly Complex Prompts:


Example: "I need to file my income tax return for the year 2022. I have a few questions about deductions and credits that I can claim. Can you please provide me with a detailed explanation of the different types of deductions and credits available, and how to determine which ones I'm eligible for based on my specific situation?"


This prompt is convoluted and may overwhelm the AI, leading to a response that misses key points or fails to address the user's actual concerns.


‍


Good Prompting


‍


1. Clear and Specific Prompts:


Example: "What are the most common deductions and credits available for individual income tax filers in the United States for the 2022 tax year?"


This prompt is direct and focused, allowing the AI to provide a relevant and concise answer.


‍


2. Contextual Prompts:


Example: "I am a single filer with a total income of $50,000 for the 2022 tax year. I have student loan interest, charitable donations, and medical expenses. What deductions and credits should I consider when filing my income tax return?"


By including specific details about their situation, the user helps the AI tailor its response to their unique needs.


‍


3. Step-by-Step Requests:


Example: "Can you provide a step-by-step guide on how to determine which deductions and credits I'm eligible for based on my income, filing status, and expenses?"


This prompt breaks down the user's request into manageable parts, making it easier for the AI to deliver a structured and informative response.


‍


Conclusion


‍


Effective AI prompt engineering is a valuable skill for users looking to maximize their interactions with AI systems. By crafting clear, specific, and context-rich prompts, users can enhance the relevance and accuracy of the information they receive, particularly when navigating complex topics like income tax. With the right approach, users can empower themselves to get the answers they need efficiently and effectively.
