# 🚩 Red Flag | Offseason Tech Edition! | TaxGPT's Newsletter

**Supercharge Your Firm During the Holiday Offseason: Unlock and Integrate Artificial Intelligence Into Your Workflows**


‍


As usual for tax firms, the time spent between October 15th and January usually involves some combination of vacation, retrospectives on what went well and not too well during the prior 9 months, and preparing for the next busy season. This offseason, however, may be the most critical for tax professionals. In my opinion, it is **the** most important few months that you, as a decision maker or decision influencer, will experience - potentially in your career.


‍


While this sounds like pure hyperbole, let me explain: the revolution in technology that is Artificial Intelligence is converging with the tax industry at a time when the industry itself is in dire straits. While the most recent technological revolution for the accounting industry, the internet and paperless workflow boom from the early 2000s through the mid 2010s, was indeed a huge boon… this time, it’s different.


‍


The internet-fueled paperless workflow revolution was more of an “a la carte” transformation. Firms were afforded the flexibility to choose what to digitize and what to leave SALY. Some firms opted to digitize workpapers when received, while others back scanned the completed files after deliverables were completed. Some firms made client portals mandatory, while others were happy to print returns and mail 8879s.


‍


The difference now, however, is multifaceted. Overall, the concepts and technologies behind the paperless workflow were fairly static. E-signatures, client portals, even Optical Character Recognition in the early 2000s compared to just a year ago saw little measurable advancement. If you were late to adopt any specific tool or technology, you could always catch it next year.


‍


Now? If you blink, the landscape can shift underneath your feet. The velocity at which AI moves is hard to overstate - from 2020 to 2022, the computational power of AI grew **27,000,000x** . Yes, twenty-seven million times more powerful. In two years. As we entered 2023, the technology became inexpensive enough for business and household use. Enter 2024, and there are hundreds of companies capitalizing on providing AI tools to the tax industry alone, either directly or indirectly. The proliferation of AI brings me to the next section.


‍


**On the other side of the coin, we have the state of the tax industry.**


Client expectations: the hype surrounding AI has also skewed expectations of clients in many cases. Clients (and even tax professionals) have expectations that aren’t quite congruent with the current offerings of AI. It’s expected to be perfect, instant, and free.


‍


Floundering pipeline: face it, the old saying of “good talent is hard to find” is being amplified. CPA exam enrollment is plummeting, there’s a massive shortage of accounting talent, and the long hours combined with low pay aren’t exactly enticing.


‍


Employee expectations: the good talent you **do** find will most likely already have experience with AI tools, and putting them back in the stone age won’t land very well.


‍


To get ahead (or even just maintain) it is critical to use the time available to understand and implement AI tools. Here are some tools that myself, our team, and our users are using to make an impact.


‍


**Automating Team and Client Meetings**


Team and client meetings are the backbone of any tax firm, but they come with administrative demands like notetaking, action item tracking, and follow-up scheduling. AI can streamline these processes to ensure more efficient and productive interactions. Tools like[Fathom](https://fathom.video/home) and[Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/) are great options. They sit in your meetings, recording and/or transcribing the conversations, and provide summaries along with action items.


‍


It did seem strange at first not having a Google or Word doc open (or a legal pad out!) during the first few meetings, but these tools are now indispensable for us. They recognize who is speaking and let you share the recordings and summaries via links or email.


‍


This really lets the participants focus on paying attention to each other, flesh out ideas, and be completely effective during the time together.


‍


**Streamlining Communications**


The importance of communications among teams or with clients is paramount. While being such a critical skill, it’s also extremely difficult. Team members and clients alike have varying degrees of knowledge and sophistication regarding tax concepts. Emails and phone calls are time consuming and tedious. What’s worse? Time spent communicating with clients is notoriously difficult to properly track and bill for.


‍


TaxGPT in particular excels at a few aspects of communication. You can convert a client or coworker question into a research query, and that response into a memo, all in mere seconds. What’s more, you can scan a document such as an IRS notice and ask TaxGPT to not only explain it - but write a response for it.


‍


Beyond email, chat programs such as[Slack](https://slack.com/) provide exceptional communication platforms for teams of any size. Slack also features a host of integrations with other workflow software that really supercharge the value, such as calendars, email, project management utilities, and more.


‍


**Simplifying Scheduling**


While not necessarily AI-powered, or as reliant on AI as some of the other tools mentioned, scheduling tools eliminate the back-and-forth commonly associated with arranging meetings and calls. These tools offer intuitive solutions for tax firms:


- Calendar Integration: AI scheduling assistants can sync with all team members’ calendars, finding optimal meeting times without conflicts.
- Automatic Reminders: These tools can send reminders to clients (and you!), reducing no-shows and improving time management for tax professionals.
- Time Zone Adaptation: For firms with clients across regions, AI tools automatically adjust for time zones, streamlining coordination and preventing missed meetings.
- Autonomous Rescheduling: Missed meetings or last-minute-declines can automatically suggest rescheduling for times that fit into attendee calendars.


By automating scheduling, tax firms can ensure seamless client meetings and maintain an organized workflow.


‍


**Enhancing Project Management**


Effective project management is essential in ensuring deadlines, filing requirements, and compliance milestones are met. AI-driven project management solutions help track tasks and maintain accountability within a tax firm. Practice management solutions such as[TaxDome](https://taxdome.com/) and[Canopy](https://www.getcanopy.com/) offer varying degrees of intelligence that can assist with:


- Workflow Automation: AI can automate task assignments based on workload, expertise, or priority, ensuring deadlines are met without overloading team members.
- Milestone & Status Tracking: AI tools offer a bird’s-eye view of project progress, identifying bottlenecks and sending reminders for pending tasks.
- Real-Time Updates and Reporting: For firms juggling multiple clients, AI can provide updates on project status, generate reports, and notify stakeholders of critical issues in real-time.
