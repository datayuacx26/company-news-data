# 🚩Red Flag | New Year's Resolution: Tech, Part 1 | TaxGPT's Newsletter

**In my first role at a public accounting firm** , many many years ago, I had the privilege of being the edgy, young techno-wiz-kid. I knew next to nothing about taxes or accounting, but I was hungry and eager to please. The one thing I knew more than any of my colleagues, was how to be lazy.


Not lazy in the sense that I didn’t like to work, on the contrary, I loved to work. I still do. What I don’t enjoy is being “busy”, being weighed down by archaic processes, and knowing there’s a better way but not being empowered to do anything about it.


I also have handwriting that would make a doctor physically ill, which is not conducive to writing important notes and documentation on client workpapers.


Another shout out to my first boss, Steve, he had a vision for the firm that involved adopting technology. I was given the keys to the kingdom to explore ways to improve processes. I worked with our consulting firm to vet and implement various technologies throughout our workflow. During my tenure, the firm transformed into a completely paperless office. In a not-so-weird twist of fate, here I am on the bleeding edge of the marriage between taxes and artificial intelligence.


I can’t stress enough how important it is to remain on top of current and upcoming trends in technology - if you’re a seasoned veteran in the tax game, or an up-and-comer in college - radical value can be achieved through the implementation of technology. Steve always said “the more you know, the more valuable you are” and I certainly can’t refute that.


With that introduction out of the way, let’s explore various technologies in their current iterations that can transform your processes.


### **1. Reminder! Go Paperless: The Year to Fully Digitize Your Practice**


**Why Go Paperless?** It’s almost surreal to say this, as this was the origin story for my professional career: physical paperwork not only slows down operations but also increases the risk of data loss or errors. Going fully paperless allows for faster document access, efficient collaboration, streamlined workflows, improved security, and greater profits.


Additionally, paperless workflows can make every process easier for you, your staff, your clients, and other stakeholders. Digitizing your workflow also defeats geographic barriers - not just for your clients, but for you and your colleagues. Expand your clientele across the nation - or world, if you’d like. Offer work-from-home for your staff. Even you, and I do mean **you** , can work from anywhere. My tax season in 2021 was spent on a beach in Costa Rica - certainly beat staring at an office wall.


**Resolution Tip:** Identify cumbersome workflows that can be positively impacted by conversion to paperless. Empower your staff to explore and vet technology offerings, demo software, and implement solutions. Some ideas on where to start?


Client Portals: Portals offer online collaboration and document storage for client projects. Most major tax software vendors offer client portals as part of their software package. 3rd party vendors also add standalone portal platforms that generally integrate well with tax software.


Benefits: No more mailing documents back and forth, no more “I lost my copy of… can you send me another?”. Documents are accessible anywhere with an internet connection, and available in real-time.


Electronic Signatures: Electronic signing is one of the easiest solutions, and weirdly not used as much as I’d think it would be in tax firms. Many federal and state tax, and tax-related forms can be electronically signed.


Benefits: Like the portal systems, e-signatures eliminate the need to mail signature pages back and forth, or inconvenience clients to visit the office in person to sign. E-signature programs also database your documents, send automatic reminders, and can integrate with tax software to automatically send signature pages when a project is marked finished.


Tax Research: Turns out they still print tax research publications. Converting to electronic resources for tax research is the most painless unlock for firms, and also does not require the cooperation of clients.


Benefits: Control + F. I can’t imagine trying to page my way through a physical publication to locate information. Layer in AI and boom, not only is relevant information located, it’s also synthesized and written in a manner you can actually understand. Use a tool such as TaxGPT and do something you’d be proud of.


### **2. AI Integration: Boost Accuracy and Efficiency**


**Why Integrate AI?** Artificial Intelligence (AI) is revolutionizing the tax industry by enhancing accuracy, streamlining workflows, and unlocking new efficiencies. In an environment where precision is paramount, even the smallest human errors can lead to audits, penalties, and client dissatisfaction. AI-powered tools act as a safeguard by cross-referencing returns, identifying anomalies, and flagging inconsistencies that might otherwise slip through the cracks.


Beyond error detection, AI's ability to process vast amounts of data in seconds opens doors to new insights. Firms can leverage AI to analyze historical filings, financial statements, and market trends to uncover tax-saving strategies and optimize deductions. This level of data-driven intelligence empowers firms to provide higher-value advisory services, positioning them as strategic partners rather than just compliance specialists.


AI doesn't just refine tax preparation—it redefines how firms operate. By automating repetitive, time-consuming tasks such as data entry, document review, and compliance checks, AI liberates teams to focus on more profitable, client-facing activities. The time saved can be reinvested into expanding service offerings, strengthening client relationships, or pursuing continuing education and professional growth.


**Resolution Tip:** Adopt AI tools that align with your firm's specific needs and goals. Start with solutions that enhance core functions—such as audit risk prediction, tax optimization recommendations, and client engagement analytics. For example, AI platforms can predict which clients are more likely to face IRS scrutiny, allowing firms to proactively address potential issues before they escalate.


Consider expanding AI integration to client interactions. Tools that can join virtual meetings, record video, generate transcripts, and create actionable follow-ups help ensure no detail is overlooked. These AI assistants streamline communication and reduce administrative burdens.


Moreover, AI-driven platforms like **TaxGPT** can supercharge tax research, draft client correspondence, analyze complex financial statements, and even craft responses to IRS notices. By automating these labor-intensive tasks, tax professionals can respond faster and with greater precision, enhancing both productivity and profitability.


AI isn't just a tech upgrade—it's a strategic advantage. Firms that embrace AI-driven solutions today will be better equipped to navigate the evolving tax landscape, stay ahead of competitors, and deliver exceptional value to their clients.
