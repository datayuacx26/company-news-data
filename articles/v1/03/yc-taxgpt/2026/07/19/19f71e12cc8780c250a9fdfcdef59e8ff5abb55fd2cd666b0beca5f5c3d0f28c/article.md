# 🚩 Red Flag | AI Has Entered the Workflow | TaxGPT's Newsletter

One of most common issues facing tax professionals with regards to technology is the absolutely overwhelming number of solutions that exist for a given need.


Known as paralysis by analysis, this can be caused by a number of reasons: fear of making mistakes, information overload, and complexity of choices, to name a few.


Delayed decision making, or even complete indecision, can lead to reduced efficiency, missed opportunities, and even irrelevance! For those that tend to adopt a wait-and-see approach with emerging technology, I can say from personal experience that AI is something you just have to try for yourself.


I've personally been transformed into an AI-first mindset. There's almost no area of my workflow that AI can't have some positive impact on. Let's explore this idea a bit!
‍

Workflow: tax research
Old model: painstaking, time consuming manual research using outdated search methods on Thomson Reuters, Bloomberg, CCH, etc.

New model:[TaxGPT](https://www.taxgpt.com/) , of course, parsing hundreds of thousands of datapoints in seconds, delivering you a concise, verifiable response.


Workflow: correspondence composition
