# Free-Stand Single or Dual Access Enclosures Enhancement

Enhanced to support more applications, our A30 enclosures feature sturdy unibody construction and versatile internal mounting options for panel and component mounting flexibility.


Now with a universal rating of **UL type 12, 3R, and 4,** customers can use their **single-door** enclosures inside or outside, with protection from dirt, dust, oil, and water. The **double-door** version is rated for **UL type 12/3R.**


What’s new with both **single-door and double-door** enclosures?


- Stainless steel hinges and hinge pins for durability
- Powerglide handle to provide UL Type 3R and 4 protection
- Updated door trough design to support sealing


What’s new with the **single-door** enclosures?


- Foam-in-place gasket for better sealing
