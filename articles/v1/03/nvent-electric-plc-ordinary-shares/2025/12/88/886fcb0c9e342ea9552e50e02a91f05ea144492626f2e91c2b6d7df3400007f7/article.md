# Continuous Hinge with Clamps, Type 4 Enclosure Enhancement

Building on previously communicated updates, we have made additional updates to the Continuous Hinge with Clamps LP, Type 4 Enclosure (A12) portfolio. This expansion includes:


- 10 additional enclosure size combinations with the standard ANSI 61 gray finish
- 94 SKUs of RAL 7035 light gray finish enclosures as a non-stock standard product
- Additional panel size options for component mounting


To recap the enclosure design enhancements, below is what has changed:
The single-door enclosure solution has been redesigned to achieve a universal rating, with the enclosure suitable for both indoor and outdoor applications. All hardware is stainless steel, including the screw-down door clamps and heavy-gauge hinge pin. The door clamp count and position have been re-defined to achieve optimal compression of the seamless, foam-in-place gasket. In addition, the solution is finished both inside and out in a premium, polyester powder paint that enhances corrosion protection, like many of our nVent HOFFMAN enclosures.


As a reminder, we have streamlined our portfolio by phasing out the Bulletin A4 product family and replaced with the Bulletin A12 enclosure solutions. For more about these changes, please visit our[product lifecycle page.](https://www.nvent.com/en-us/hoffman/a4-product-lifecycle-timeline)


Additional information can be found on the Continuous Hinge with Clamps, Type 4 Enclosure[Product Page.](https://www.nvent.com/en-us/hoffman/products/continuous-hinge-clamps-lp-type-4)
