# Safer, Faster and Easier Cable Support with nVent CADDY

##### The nVent CADDY Single-Arm Cable Pressure Clip is a smart way to support cables—strong, reliable, sustainable, quick to install, and safe. This solution was designed to eliminate common issues contractors face every day: brittle plastic clips that fail over time, wasted materials from nail gun misfires, and the hassle of managing multiple product types for different applications.


##


### **Sustainability and Durability**


Plastic pressure clips have long been a popular choice for cable support, but evolving job site demands call for solutions that deliver greater durability and sustainability.


The nVent CADDY Single-Arm Cable Pressure Clip improves productivity on job sites by securely supporting and organizing small cable bundles on the building structures. Its robust steel design won’t be subject to fatigue or become brittle over time—like plastic does—which ultimately prevents cables from falling and time-consuming rework. Compatible with direct fastening, it can be reused in case of misfire and is 100% recyclable, ensuring you meet sustainability goals and reduce waste.


## **Gangable/Two-in-One Solution**


Our patent-pending design allows installers to connect two clips in seconds using only their hands, doubling cable support capacity per single fixing point. This means fewer part numbers to manage on the job and no more juggling between standard plastic, fire-retardant plastic, single or double clips.


In addition, the nVent CADDY Single-Arm Cable Pressure Clip profile has been engineered for smooth cable insertion with one hand, improving installation speed and overall productivity


### **Install Faster, Work Smarter**


Efficiency matters. The nVent CADDY Single-Arm Cable Pressure Clip speeds up installation with two versatile options: a version compatible with traditional anchoring and one with direct fastening. For added safety and convenience, clips can be installed from the ground using a nail gun and a pole—avoiding the need for ladders and saving installation time, which equates to lower project costs and safer crews.

Job site safety is not optional. That’s why the nVent CADDY Single-Arm Cable Pressure Clip is engineered to protect both your crew and your cables.

These features are critical for protecting people, equipment, and systems by helping to reduce toxic smoke and preventing spreading flames in case of a fire.
