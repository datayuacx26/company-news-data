# nVent SCHROFF Network and Colocation Cabinets Now Available as Customizable Varistar CP Solutions

As part of our ongoing commitment to innovation and customer-centric solutions, nVent SCHROFF is transitioning its Network and Colocation cabinet offering to the Varistar CP platform. Network cabinets and Colocation cabinets are now available as customizable configurations within the Varistar CP platform.


The Varistar CP cabinet system is engineered for performance, flexibility, and durability. Built on a robust welded frame structure, it supports static loads up to 1600 kg and meets stringent shock and vibration standards (IEC 61587-1 DL6 level), making it ideal for demanding environments such as data centers, telecom facilities, and industrial applications.


Now, with the integration of Network and Colocation cabinet options, customers can benefit from a unified, scalable solution that simplifies deployment and enhances protection for critical IT assets.


Cabinet configuration is at the heart of this offering. From layout and airflow management to cable routing and security features, the Varistar CP cabinets can be tailored to meet your needs for Networking cabinets and Colocation cabinets.
