# Accelerate Innovation in Real-Time Testing with Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL)

**nVent SCHROFF Launches Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Campaign to Accelerate Innovation in Real-Time Testing**


**Straubenhardt, Germany – \[July 4th, 2025\] — nVent SCHROFF** , a global leader in electronic systems protection and integration, is proud to announce the launch of its **first-ever Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Campaign** — a multi-channel initiative focused on showcasing advanced infrastructure solutions for real-time simulation and testing environments.


**Powering the Future of Embedded System Validation**


As industries like automotive, aerospace, defense, and industrial automation continue to evolve with increasing complexity and digitalization, Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) testing has become essential for ensuring safety, performance, and time-to-market efficiency.


nVent SCHROFF’s HIL Campaign brings the spotlight to its robust, modular, and scalable enclosures and platform solutions that meet the demanding needs of HIL systems. These systems are used to test embedded control units (ECUs) and other hardware components in simulated environments that replicate real-world conditions — without the risks and delays of physical field testing.


**What the Campaign Includes**


The HIL Campaign is a comprehensive program that includes:


- A case study generated in support of our customer IPG.
- Whitepaper: nVent SCHROFF explores the evolving role of HiL in modern system design, with a focus on essential infrastructure that supports reliable, scalable, and high-performance test environments.
- Hardware in the Loop background and applications.


**Built for Precision, Protection, and Performance**


“nVent SCHROFF brings decades of experience in providing tailored infrastructure solutions for mission-critical electronics. With this campaign, we want to highlight how our systems provide not only physical protection but also optimized integration for HIL environments — helping our customers accelerate innovation with confidence.” said Lorena Lopez, Vertical Marketing Manager Test & Measurement, nVent SCHROFF.


From rugged test racks for automotive labs to high-performance backplanes for aerospace simulators, SCHROFF platforms offer industry-grade protection, thermal management, EMC shielding, and system flexibility — essential for reliable HIL operation.


**Join the Movement**


This campaign is an open invitation to engineers, designers, and system integrators to explore how **HIL + nVent SCHROFF = smarter, faster, safer testing.**
