# nVent SCHROFF Expands High-Power Rack Safety Plus (RSP) Portfolio with 32 A and 63 A 3-Phase Versions

nVent SCHROFF has expanded its Rack Safety Plus (RSP) product line with new 3-phase versions specifically developed for the European Market. These enhancements bring higher power versions and 3-phases to outlets.


As demands for higher power density and advanced power distribution grow across Europe’s industrial and technology sectors, nVent SCHROFF is meeting the challenge with the latest evolution of its Rack Safety Plus (RSP) product line. Now available in newly developed **3-phase versions** , these enhancements bring higher power inputs and 3-phase outlets, delivering greater efficiency, flexibility, and performance tailored specifically for the European market. Fit for test labs, edge sites, or validation environments where flexible, high-current rack-level power is needed, the RSP stands apart from standard data center PDUs, offering a specialized solution designed for dynamic and demanding applications.


**What’s New?**


**1. New Input Options: 32 A and 63 A for 3-Phase RSP**


The updated Rack Safety Plus is now available with 32 A and 63 A 3-phase input options, offering solutions for high-power applications. These new variants complement the existing 16 A 1-phase and 3-phase options, giving system designers the flexibility to meet demanding power needs.


**2. Expanded Outlet Panel Options for 3-Phase 16 A and 32 A**


To support different output configurations, the new versions include **enhanced outlet panels** on panel 2. These allow multiple configurations, such as:


- 12x C13
- 6x C13
- 3x C19 (including lockable versions)
- 3x Schuko (Type F, CEE 7/3)
- 1x IEC 60309 (3L+N+PE, 6h) available in both 16 A and 32 A versions


**3. Additional DC Outputs Now Available**


Another major improvement is the integration of additional DC outputs, including:


- 24 V DC output (350 W)
- Optional 5 V DC (32.5 W) and 12 V DC (54 W) outputs at the rear panel


This integrated functionality reduces the need for external power supplies and simplifies cable management within the rack.


**4. Form Factor Update for 63 A Models**


Please note: the 63 A Rack Safety Plus version requires 4U of rack space, compared to the 3U height of the 16 A and 32 A models. This change accommodates higher power handling and enhanced safety mechanisms.
