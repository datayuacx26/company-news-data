# Employee Story: Jasmine, HR Business Partner

#### ***“Don’t be afraid to speak up, get involved, and bring your ideas to the table.”***


For Jasmine, an HR Business Partner in Hutchins, TX, joining nVent was a chance to make a meaningful impact in HR while working for a company that values people. “The opportunity to work in a fast-paced, hands-on environment where employee experience and operational excellence go hand in hand was exactly what I was looking for,” Jasmine shared.


Jasmine began her journey at nVent in October 2024 as an HR Coordinator and was promoted to HR Business Partner just before her first anniversary. Her role focuses on listening, problem-solving, and connecting employees with leadership.


Jasmine appreciates nVent’s inclusive culture, describing it as a family where everyone belongs and can grow both personally and professionally. “Each step of the journey has sharpened my skills and reinforced that showing up with purpose and integrity truly makes a difference,” she reflected. Her role centers around people—listening, problem-solving, and building bridges between employees and leadership.


A highlight of her career has been co-chairing the Conductors Employee Resource Group (ERG), where she has merged her HR strengths with her passion for engagement. Collaborating with leadership to shape direction and ensure alignment with the company’s mission reminded her why she loves this work—helping people feel seen and supported. “It’s about being empowered to build something better for both the business and the people,” she emphasized.


Jasmine describes nVent’s culture as professional yet supportive, a place that celebrates individuality while encouraging personal growth. The company’s investment in employee development through programs like tuition reimbursement and leadership training stands out to her.


Additionally, the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) has been an invaluable resource for maintaining balance and mental well-being. “Having access to confidential support when life gets heavy has made a real difference. It shows that nVent doesn’t just talk about caring for employees; they actually mean it.”


Jasmine encourages others to embrace opportunities to be bold. “Don’t be afraid to speak up, get involved, and bring your ideas to the table,” she advised. She believes that growth happens when you stay curious and connected.


Her journey at nVent has helped her grow professionally while finding a supportive community that values authenticity. Jasmine’s story exemplifies the importance of purpose, integrity, and a culture that truly prioritizes its people.
