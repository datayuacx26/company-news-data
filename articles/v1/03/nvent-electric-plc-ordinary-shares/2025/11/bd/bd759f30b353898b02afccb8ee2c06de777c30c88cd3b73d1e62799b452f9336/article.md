# Commerce Controls delivers reliable protection to public utility service

### **IEC Modular Enclosures**


**SUCCESS STORY**


Commerce Controls is a systems integrator that cities trust with their most precious resource: water. Since 1977, Commerce Controls has been helping water and wastewater management facilities with integrated solutions that maximize control and minimize disruption.


The company’s reliable, timely, and high-quality service has helped build deep relationships in both the private and public sectors. When a water treatment plant in Austin, TX, needed to update its infrastructure, the plant hired Commerce Controls to design and install new electrical control panels.


But there was an issue: The electrical enclosures required by the city of Austin were no longer in production. Updating the facility would require making an informed decision on which enclosures on the market offered the most reliable protection.


Upon consulting with its trusted distributor, Commerce Controls recommended replacing the previous generation of enclosures with nVent HOFFMAN IEC Modular Enclosures.


Based on their demonstrated durability and flexibility, the city accepted the proposal and incorporated the IEC Enclosures into its framework for standardized, city-approved products for future use.


**Delivering reliable protection to the next generation of critical infrastructure**
