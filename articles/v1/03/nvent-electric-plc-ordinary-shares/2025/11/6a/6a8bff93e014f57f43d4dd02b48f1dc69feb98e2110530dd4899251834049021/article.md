# Veteran Voices: Honoring Our Veteran Employees

We believe our culture is a differentiator and fosters an environment where our employees can bring bold ideas, be authentic and build the future of a more sustainable and electrified world. nVent is proud to have several veterans on our team who have bravely served in all branches of the United States Armed Forces. Each of them brings with them unique talents and principles of their service that they incorporate into what they do daily at our company. We are grateful for their service and their willingness to share their stories.


______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________


#### **Sean, Design Specialist in Tempe** **, Arizona (US Navy Veteran)**


> ###### ***“Serving in the Navy shaped my management and organizational skills. It also taught me to maintain a proper attitude and to prioritize integrity. Having integrity means being accountable for your actions and owning them, which is also connected to earning respect and giving it. The teamwork that comes from this is incomparable to anything without integrity. ”***


**What is your former branch of service and military specialty (MOS)?**


US Navy, hull maintenance technician.


**Why did you choose to join nVent?**


I was looking for a position in the electrical field that would not require me to be on a construction site day in and day out.


**How did you discuss your military experience and the transferable/technical skills you possess when you interviewed at nVent?**


Supervisor and management skills, organization, attitude and past experiences.


**Which nVent Win Right value resonates with you most and why?**


Absolute Integrity resonates with me. Having integrity means being accountable for your actions and owning them. Being accountable resonates back to earning respect and giving it. The teamwork that comes from this is incomparable to anything without integrity.


**How does nVent’s culture appeal to specifically veterans?**


Our values and diverse culture.


**What is one piece of career advice you would like to share with others?**


Be yourself and don’t sacrifice your personal values. Everything will fall into place with a little time.


#### **Calvin, Machine Operator in** **Centralia, Illinois (US Army Veteran)**


> ###### **“The military taught me accountability and responsibility. That is what I bring to nVent every day and why I connect to our value of Accountability for Performance.”**


**What is your former branch of service and military specialty (MOS)?**


US Army, 19K – M1 (armor crewman).


**How do you incorporate your military service into your personal and professional brand currently?**


Being accountable and responsible.


**Which nVent Win Right values resonates with you most and why?**


Accountability for Performance is the value that really sticks out to me. nVent pays me money to perform, same as the military, so accountability has always been a strong suit for me.


**What is one piece of career advice you would like to share with others?**


Be loyal, work hard – and definitely do 401K. Always take pride in your work, it will pay off.
