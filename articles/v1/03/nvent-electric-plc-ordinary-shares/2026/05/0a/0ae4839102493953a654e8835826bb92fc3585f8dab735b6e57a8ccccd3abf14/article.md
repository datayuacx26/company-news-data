# HM Cragg partners with nVent to drive quality and scalability

### CONSISTENT PERFORMANCE FOR CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE


### SUCCESS STORY


For almost 60 years, HM Cragg has equipped customers with power solutions that keep the world running. Ranging from Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS) and generators to backup battery applications, HM Cragg solutions keep data centers, core utilities, and other critical infrastructure online through difficult conditions and unexpected disruptions.


To maintain this combination of speed and quality, HM Cragg needed a climate control partner that could offer comprehensive partnership.


That meant responsive, rapid, and deep support that eliminated friction and contributed to HM Cragg’s reputation for exceptional customer outcomes. This search led straight to nVent HOFFMAN.


### Enhanced support. Better outcomes.


Having already established a relationship with nVent as its vendor of choice for CEL lights, HM Cragg decided to expand the partner relationship to include climate control solutions and IEC modular enclosures.


*“We touch every piece of power, from where it enters the building to where it’s being used, and everywhere in between. It’s important that our enclosure and climate control solutions can keep up with our comprehensive approach."*
**Kody Schultz Business Manager** **(Custom Solutions Group) HM Cragg**


### Customized, industrial-grade power solutions: simplified ****


In a short time, HM Cragg’s partnership with nVent HOFFMAN has generated measurable results for both the company and its customers. The modular design of the nVent HOFFMAN IEC enclosures vastly simplifies the task of meeting customer demand at scale, without compromising on the individualized details that make HM Cragg solutions special.


An enduring commitment to support lies at the core of this business value. Combined with the flexibility and consistent performance of nVent HOFFMAN climate control and IEC modular enclosure solutions, this support guarantees the hands-on problem-solving HM Cragg needs in order to provide customers with the same level of bespoke service.
