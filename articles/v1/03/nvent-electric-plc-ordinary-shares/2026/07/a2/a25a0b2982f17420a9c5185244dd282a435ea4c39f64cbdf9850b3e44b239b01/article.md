# nVent Earns 2026 VETS Indexes Recognized Employer Award for Veteran Support and Employment

NEWS ARTICLE


nVent Earns 2026 VETS Indexes Recognized Employer Award for Veteran Support and Employment


*Award highlights nVent's strong commitment to recruiting, hiring, retaining, developing, and supporting veterans, National Guard members and Reservists.*


nVent, a global leader in electrical connection and protection solutions, has earned the Recognized Employer designation in the 2026 VETS Indexes Employer Awards. This recognition highlights nVent’s demonstrated commitment to veteran employment, including recruiting, hiring, retaining, developing, and supporting veterans, as well as members of the National Guard and Reserves.


The VETS Indexes Employer Awards program evaluates employers on their veteran-focused policies, practices, and outcomes, making it one of the most objective and comprehensive assessments of military-friendly employers in the United States.


“nVent has demonstrated meaningful and measurable support for veterans and the military-connected community through its commitment to building opportunities for those who served,” said Nicholas Antaki, President of VETS Indexes. “Employers like nVent continue to set the standard and help drive the future of veteran employment forward.”


**Recognized for Excellence Across Key Veteran Employment Categories**


The VETS Indexes evaluation process assessed participating organization across five core categories of veteran and military-community support:


- Veteran job candidate recruiting and hiring
- Veteran employee development and retention
- Veteran-inclusive workplace policies and culture
- Support for National Guard members and Reservists
- Military spouse and family support


Earning the Recognized Employer designation reflects nVent’s strong performance across these categories and its continued focus on creating meaningful career opportunities for military-connected talent.


**Supporting Veterans at Every Stage of Their Career**


Veterans at nVent bring unique leadership, technical expertise, and problem-solving skills to the workplace. Many employees actively give back to their local communities through participation in the nVent Veterans Employee Resource Group, which supports connection, advocacy, and professional development.


nVent is committed to helping veterans transition successfully from military service to civilian careers by recognizing and leveraging the skills, experience, dedication, and values they bring. The company is honored that so many veterans have chosen to build their post-military careers at nVent.
