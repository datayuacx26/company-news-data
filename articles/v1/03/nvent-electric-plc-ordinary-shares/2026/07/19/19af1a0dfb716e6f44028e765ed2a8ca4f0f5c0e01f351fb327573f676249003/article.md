# nVent Recognized at Rail Analysis Innovation & Excellence Summit 2026

NEWS ARTICLE


nVent Recognized at Rail Analysis Innovation & Excellence Summit 2026


Recently, our team was recognized at the annual[7th Rail Analysis Innovation & Excellence Summit 2026](https://conference.railanalysis.com/) in New Delhi, India. Rail Analysis India is a magazine and networking portal focused on the railway, metro, semi and high speed, stations and infrastructure sector of India. nVent earned a spot in the “Achievement in Import Substitution – Achieving Make in India” category, recognizing our contributions to India’s rapidly growing metro railway industry.


nVent is a trusted partner for railway projects all over the world, including India. We combine deep industry and application expertise, with innovative solutions as a global brand. Our focus on localization enables strong collaboration and support to each project. We’re driven by our purpose to build a sustainable and electrified world.


This year’s Summit brought together more than 500 decisionmakers from across the rail and metro ecosystem. The event included a conference, exhibition and awards ceremony, and celebrated innovation, advancements and collaborative opportunities within the industry.


We are honored to receive this recognition. Congratulations to[all the awardees](https://conference.railanalysis.com/list-of-winners-2026/) !
