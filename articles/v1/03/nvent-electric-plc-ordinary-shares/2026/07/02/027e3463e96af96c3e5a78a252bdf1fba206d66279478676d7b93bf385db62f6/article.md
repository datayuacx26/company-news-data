The nVent HOFFMAN Indoor Side-Mount Global Air Conditioner provides a cooling performance of 2,000 BTU/Hr. This eco-friendly air conditioner offers a 30% improvement in energy efficiency, resulting in a reduction of operational costs, and its new controller gives you more autonomy than ever over your cooling system. It is easy to service and offers the reliability and durability needed to meet necessary standards and requirements.​

Delivers up to 30% improved energy efficiency
- **Environmentally Compliant and Future-Ready**

- **Faster Upgrades with Less Disruption**
