# nVent Earns Most Innovative Award from McNaughton McKay & The Reynolds Company

NEWS ARTICLE


nVent Earns Most Innovative Award from McNaughton McKay & The Reynolds Company


Innovation and Adaptability is one of our[Our Values](https://www.nvent.com/en-us/about-nvent/nvent-values) . Last week, the nVent CADDY team earned recognition for using this value to serve our customers. During the McNaughton McKay & The Reynolds Company (MMG) annual meeting, the team learned the good news that it earned the company’s “Most Innovative Award”. This recognition is a testament to our team's dedication to delivering inventive solutions to our customers.


MMG is a wholesale electrical distributor serving markets in North America, offering a full line of products for everyone from electrical contractors to large-scale manufacturers. MMG is an employee-owned company focused on creating long-term value that contributes to the success and profitability of its customers.


Together, nVent and MMG have served customers for more than 45 years. Our latest work to improve the customer experience includes developing more agile responses, consistent communication and collaboration and optimizing the sales process.


Congratulations, nVent CADDY team! Thank you to MMG for its ongoing support and collaboration. We look forward to our continued success together.
