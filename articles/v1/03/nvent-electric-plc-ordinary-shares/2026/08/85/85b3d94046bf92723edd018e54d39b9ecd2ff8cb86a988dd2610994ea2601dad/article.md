# New HyShed Sanitary Hole Seals

Expand sanitary enclosure solutions with the new nVent HOFFMAN HyShed Sanitary Hole Seals. Designed to cover unused knockouts and pushbutton openings, these hole seals help maintain Type 4X and IP69 sealing performance while supporting the strict hygiene requirements of sanitary environments.


Featuring an FDA-grade silicone gasket, HyShed Sanitary Hole Seals provide reliable protection against water ingress and help preserve enclosure integrity in demanding washdown applications.


Key benefits include:


- Helps prevent water ingress through unused or modified openings
- Hygienic design ideal for food and beverage washdown environments
- Enables future enclosure modifications and expansion without costly redesigns
- Maintains Type 4X and IP69 protection when properly installed
