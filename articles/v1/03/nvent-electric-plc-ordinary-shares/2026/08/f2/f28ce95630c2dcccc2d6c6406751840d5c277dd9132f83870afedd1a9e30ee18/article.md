# Electrical Interlock Now IEC Modular-Compatible

The nVent HOFFMAN IEC Electrical Interlock is equipped with a newly designed mounting bracket to support the IEC Modular Enclosure platform. The core functionality of the interlock remains untouched. When energized, the electrical interlock provides a positive internal safety lockout that prevents the door from being opened.


With the new IEC Electrical Interlock, we now have a solution for customers moving from ProLine to IEC Enclosures that ensures compatibility without compromising safety. This supports our goal of strengthening our global offering and simplifying transitions to new product portfolios.
