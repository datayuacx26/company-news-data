# Commercial Wireway Type 1 with New Snap Connect Design

The Lay-In Flat-Cover Type 1 wireway in both galvanized and painted finishes has been redesigned to feature a convenient “snap” fit connection, making installation quicker and easier. At this time, the feature enhancement is limited to the 4.00 x 4.00 inch and 6.00 x 6.00 inch solution sizes, present on all wireway Straight Section lengths as well as the joining U-Connectors. The “snap” fit design brings the following:


- Straight Section retention and self-alignment of U-Connector
- Elimination of 4 internally fastened nuts per connection
- Multi tool screw head(s) for flexible installation
- Backwards compatibility with legacy design


This update is an example of our commitment to product innovation and customer experience enhancement.
