# Disconnect Wall-Mount Enclosure Type 12 Enhancements

The Disconnect Wall-Mount Enclosures within the A25 Bulletin are being updated with two new features:


- Stainless-steel hinge pin in place of the original mild steel pin
- Standardized ANSI 61 gray premium, polyester powder paint finish both inside and out


These changes ensure we continue to deliver premium products our customers expect, within the timeframe required of today’s fast-paced markets.


We will continue to communicate as these changes become effective, but we expect these updates to be complete by the end of July. For customers requiring a white interior we maintain the ability to provide such a finish, but it will require a modified solution request.


Refer to the product page for more information, including the catalog numbers in the A25 bulletin:
