# New Polycarbonate Type 4X/6P Enclosures


Our non-metallic enclosure line just got more innovative. Introducing the new nVent HOFFMAN Polycarbonate Type 4X/6P Enclosures, for demanding industrial applications that require a high degree of protection and extreme durability.
These enclosures are equipped with various features, including:


- **Corner Elevator System:** Slotted shelf technology that provides solid hinged front surfaces that are easy to install, align and keep level
- **Latches:** Non-metallic and metallic latches and hinged screw cover are all padlock-able providing superior protection. An additional lift-off screw cover also allows easy access to components.
- **Polycarbonate Material:** The material is lightweight yet durable, offering resistance to impact and fire
- **Modification Options:** Multiple options to support versatility, including holes & cutouts, threads & countersinking, and silk-screening
