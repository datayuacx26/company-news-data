# nVent Launches Smart DC Distribution Unit to Power and Protect Next-Generation Telecom Infrastructure

*Improved monitoring, cybersecurity, and compact design enable reliable power distribution for 5G, edge computing, and outdoor network deployments*


nVent Electric plc (NYSE: NVent), a global leader in electrical connection and protection solutions, today announced the launch of the nVent SCHROFF Smart DC Distribution Unit (Smart DCDU)—a next-generation intelligent DC power distribution solution engineered for modern telecom networks, edge data centers, and mission-critical infrastructure.


As 5G and future 6G networks expand and move closer to the edge, network operators and system integrators face increasing pressure to deliver **r** eliable, secure, and efficient DC power distribution—often in remote or outdoor environments. Traditional solutions lack visibility, flexibility, and proactive diagnostics, increasing the risk of network downtime and costly service disruptions.


**Intelligent DC Power Distribution for Modern Networks**


The nVent SCHROFF Smart DCDU addresses these challenges with advanced monitoring, remote management, and cybersecurity-focused design, helping customers maintain uptime and performance across distributed network environments.


With real-time monitoring and billing-grade measurement at the circuit level, operators gain immediate insight into voltage changes, breaker status, and system performance, enabling proactive intervention before failures occur.


Key capabilities include:


- Remote monitoring of circuit breakers and power usage
- Real-time alerts to prevent downtime and power disruptions
- Dual power feeds to eliminate single points of failure
- Billing-grade outlet-level monitoring for accurate power tracking


**Proactive Monitoring and Remote Management**


Designed to meet the needs of both network operators and system integrators, the Smart DCDU provides flexible local and remote management via TCP/IP connectivity, enabling seamless integration into existing network management systems.


Operators benefit from:


- IP-based access for centralized control
- Proactive alerts for breaker trips and anomalies
- Improved visibility across distributed sites


System integrators gain a reliable, field-ready solution that simplifies deployment and reduces operational complexity.


**Built-In Cybersecurity and Industry Compliance**


To support critical infrastructure, the Smart DCDU is designed with advanced cybersecurity features, including secure firmware updates and ongoing compliance with global standards such as UL 2900-1, IEC 62368, CE, RoHS, and REACH.


These capabilities help customers meet strict regulatory requirements while ensuring long-term product security and support—a top concern for telecom operators deploying large-scale networks.


**Compact, Modular Design for Easy Deployment**


The Smart DCDU features a compact 3U footprint, making it ideal for retrofit installations in existing telecom cabinets without requiring redesigns. Its modular architecture and hot-swappable circuit breakers simplify maintenance and enable scalability as network demands grow.


Additional new design benefits include:


- Touch-safe push-in connectors for faster installation
- Flexible circuit breaker configurations
- Balanced load distribution across circuits and phases
- Durability for outdoor and harsh environmental conditions


**A Complete Telecom Power and Enclosure Solution**


Unlike competitors that offer standalone components, nVent delivers a fully integrated telecom infrastructure solution. With its established portfolio of enclosures, cooling systems, and accessories, nVent enables customers to streamline deployment by working with a single, trusted vendor.


This approach reduces complexity, improves system compatibility, and accelerates time to deployment—especially critical as networks scale to support edge computing, cell sites, and high-density data transmission.


**Supporting the Future of Edge Power**


“With the continued expansion of critical networks and the increased reliance on renewable energy to power these remote cell sites networks, dependable and intelligent DC power distribution is more critical than ever,” said Anthony Palmer, global business development manager. “The nVent SCHROFF Smart DCDU gives operators the visibility, control, and security they need to prevent downtime and confidently scale their infrastructure.”
