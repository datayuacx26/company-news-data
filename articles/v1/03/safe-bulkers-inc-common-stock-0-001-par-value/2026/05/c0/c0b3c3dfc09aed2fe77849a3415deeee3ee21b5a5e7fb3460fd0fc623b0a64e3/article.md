# Announcement of availability of the Prospectus in relation to the parallel listing on Euronext Athens

This announcement does not constitute a prospectus or an offer of securities in any jurisdiction.


The company “SAFE BULKERS, INC.” (the “ **Company** ”), (NYSE: SB), an international provider of marine dry bulk transportation services, hereby announces that as of 27 May 2026, it makes available to investors in Greece the prospectus, as approved by the Board of Directors of the Hellenic Capital Market Commission (the “ **HCMC** ”), in its meeting held on 27 May 2026, which was drafted in English and includes a Greek translation of the Summary (Περιληπτικό Σημείωμα) thereof, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 (the “ **Prospectus Regulation** ”), the applicable provisions of Greek law 4706/2020, the Delegated Regulations (EU) 2019/979 and (EU) 2019/980, and Resolution 1/892/13.10.2020 of the HCMC (the “ **Prospectus** ”), in relation to the parallel listing and admission to trading of all 101,826,580 dematerialized, registered voting shares of common stock, of a par value $0.001 each, issued by the Company (the “ **Common Stock Shares** ”) on the Main Market of the Regulated Securities Market of Euronext Athens by virtue of the resolution of the Board of Directors of the Company dated 1 April 2026.


The Company’s outstanding Common Stock Shares are primarily listed and traded on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE).


The Listings and Market Operation Committee of Euronext Athens, at its meeting held on 26 May 2026, approved the admission to trading of the Common Stock Shares on the Main Market of the Regulated Securities Market of Euronext Athens (the “ **Admission** ”).


Additional information regarding the Admission is set out in Sections 4.3 “TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF THE ADMISSION”, 4.4 “ADMISSION TO TRADING AND DEALING ARRANGEMENTS” and 4.5 “PROCEDURE FOR ADMISSION TO TRADING” of the Prospectus.


## Indicative timetable


Set out below is the timetable for the admission of the Common Stock Shares to trading on the Main Market of the Regulated Securities Market of Euronext Athens:


Date Event


26 May 2026 Ascertainment of the fulfilment of all listing prerequisites and approval of the admission to trading of the Common Stock Shares by the Euronext Athens Listings and Market Operation Committee, subject to the approval of the Prospectus by the HCMC.


27 May 2026 HCMC approval of the Prospectus.


27 May 2026 Publication of the Prospectus on the Company’s, the Listing Advisor’s, and Euronext Athens’ websites.


27 May 2026 Publication of announcement regarding the availability of the Prospectus in the Daily Statistical Bulletin of Euronext Athens and on the Company’s website.


1 June 2026 Publication, on Euronext Athens’ website and the Company’s website, of the announcement regarding the date of the commencement of the Common Stock Shares trading.


2 June 2026 Publication, on Euronext Athens’ website and the Company’s website, of the announcement regarding the opening price of the Common Stock Shares prior to the opening of Euronext Athens.


2 June 2026 Commencement of trading of the Common Stock Shares on Euronext Athens.


It is noted that the above timetable depends on many unpredictable factors and may be subject to change. In such case, the investing public shall be informed through a relevant announcement on the Daily Statistical Bulletin of Euronext Athens and on the Company’s website.
