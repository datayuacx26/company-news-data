## Business Introduction


Operating in a rural area where storms can knock out internet and cell service without warning, Bob is the owner of a small, family-owned tow truck company that works around the clock.


The company’s city contracts demand a 24/7 response, and every missed call puts their reputation and livelihood on the line. For them, phone reliability isn’t a nice-to-have. It’s how they stay in business.


Before switching to Community Phone, Bob and his team relied on an internet-based phone system for their office line. On paper, the setup looked fine. Calls were set to forward from the office to whoever was on duty.


## The Challenge


But in practice, it failed them when they needed it most:


- Calls were dropping without warning, especially during bad weather, when internet signals were weakest.
- Voicemails went unnoticed, meaning staff had to group-text drivers asking, “Did someone call this person back?”
- Customers were left waiting in stressful situations, hurting trust and the company’s reputation.
- No clear way to track calls meant confusion over who was handling what, leading to delays and missed follow-ups.
- Late-night calls slipped through the cracks, putting their city contracts and steady income at risk without them even knowing.


> “We were losing at least one call a day, and at $150 a tow, that adds up fast. That’s over $4,000 a month, just gone. And that’s not even counting the wasted staff time or the risk of losing contracts.”

## The Solution


When the company switched to Community Phone, the difference was clear from day one.


Calls no longer dropped during bad weather, voice quality improved, and the team could finally rely on their phone system. And the best part was for the first time, they had full visibility into every call.


This revealed a critical gap: some late-night calls were going completely unanswered. These were the exact calls their city contracts required them to respond to. Without Community Phone, they would’ve never known.


### Key Solution Components:


- **Reliable call routing without internet** , ensuring their phones worked even when the Wi-Fi didn’t
- **A shared inbox for all missed calls and voicemails** , so everyone on the team could see what needed follow-up, both day and night
- **Mobile app access for dispatch and drivers** , so team members could check call status and respond on the go
- **Full visibility into who was (and wasn’t) answering calls** , including during nights and weekends
