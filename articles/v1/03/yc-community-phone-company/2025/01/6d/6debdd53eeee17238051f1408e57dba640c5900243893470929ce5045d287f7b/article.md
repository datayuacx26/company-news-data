## **How To Port Number Away From Verizon**


If you are looking to port your number away from Verizon, there are a few things that you will need to do to guarantee a seamless transition. Below, we have outlined the steps you will need to take to port your number away from Verizon successfully.


### **1. Get Verizon Number Transfer PIN**


The first step is to request a Verizon number transfer PIN from customer service. This PIN is required to initiate the porting process with your new carrier.


Once you have the PIN, you can provide it to your new carrier, and they will begin the process of porting your number away from Verizon. A few days are usually enough to complete the process.


#### **Method 1 - Dialing PORT**


To request your Number Transfer PIN, dial #PORT from your smartphone. Verizon will text you the PIN number needed to transfer your phone number to another carrier. This is a quick and easy way to get your transfer pin and begin porting your phone number.


#### **Method 2 - Generating Number Transfer PIN Online**


You can also generate a Number Transfer PIN online by logging into your My Verizon account.


Once logged in, click on the "My Services" tab and select "Number Transfer" from the drop-down menu. You can enter the 10-digit phone number you want to port and generate a PIN.


### **2. Provide Relevant Information To New Carrier**


When you switch carriers, your new carrier will need information from your old carrier to port your number. This includes:


- Your account number.
- Your PIN number.
- The service address that is associated with your account.


Your new carrier will use this information to start the porting process. Once your old carrier has confirmed that the request is valid, they'll begin the process of transferring your number to the new carrier. This can take up to a few days, but once it's complete, you'll be able to use your phone number with your new carrier.


### **3. Check Verizon Port Status**


Have you been thinking about switching your phone service to Verizon? Or perhaps you already have Verizon service, and you're considering porting your number from another carrier. Either way, you'll want to check the status of your port to see if everything is on track.


The port number for Verizon is 1-888-844-7095. You can use this number to contact the Port Department at Verizon for assistance with your account or any other questions you may have.


When you call, you should have the following information:


- Name.
- Current address.
- The last four digits of your Social Security number or date of birth.
- Your account number with your current carrier (if you have one).
- The phone number you want to port.
- The billing ZIP code for the account.


With this information, the customer services department will be able to pull up your account and confirm the status of your port. They can also help you with any troubleshooting that may be needed. So don't hesitate to call them if you have any questions or concerns.


### **4. Cancel Verizon Plan**


Once your number has been successfully ported to the new carrier, you must cancel your Verizon service. This can be done by calling customer service at *611 from your Verizon phone or 1-800-922-0204 from any other phone.


You must provide your account information and the 10-digit phone number for a successful port. Once your service is canceled, you will no longer have an active account with Verizon.

## **Common Verizon Porting Issues**


One of the great things about Community Phones is that you can keep your same phone number when you switch over to us. This process is called porting, and it's generally pretty simple. However, sometimes there can be issues with porting numbers from Verizon. Here are some common issues and what you can do about them:


### **1. Transfer PIN Not Working**


Porting a landline number from Verizon requires a transfer PIN.


If you don't have the PIN, you can try calling the customer service department to see if they can provide it. The PIN might be incorrect or expired if it is not working.


In this case, you'll need to contact customer service to confirm the PIN. Once you have the correct PIN, you should be able to port your number to Community Phone successfully.


### **2. Verizon Port Service Says "OK"**


Suppose you've verified that your number is eligible for porting and submitted a porting request. Still, the Verizon port service says "OK" instead of giving you a case number. In that case, this usually means that there's already an existing porting request for your number. In this case, you'll need to cancel the existing request before submitting a new one.


### **3. Verizon Keeps Sending Me PINS**


If you're trying to port your phone number from Verizon to another carrier, you may have been asked for a "porting PIN." This PIN is generated by Verizon and is used to verify your identity when porting your number.


However, some people have reported that they keep receiving these PINs even after successfully porting their number. If this happens to you, don't worry - it's likely just a Verizon glitch.


You can try contacting Verizon customer service to let them know about the problem, and they will help you resolve it.


### **4. My Verizon Number is Locked**


If your Verizon number is locked, it may be because you have an outstanding balance with Verizon. To unlock your number, you'll need to pay any outstanding balances. Once you've done that, try porting your number again.


If you don't have an outstanding balance, try contacting Verizon customer service. They may be able to help you unlock your number.


### **5. There's a Basic Data Mismatch**


If you're seeing a message that your Verizon number can't be transferred because of a basic data mismatch, it's likely due to one of two things:


1. The PIN you're using to request the transfer is either incorrect or no longer valid. You can get a new PIN by contacting customer service.
2. There may be a problem with the account information the company has on file for you. In this case, you'll need to contact customer service to update your account information.


Once you've updated your information with Verizon, try requesting the transfer again. Don't hesitate to contact our support team for help if you continue to have trouble.

No, porting your Verizon number to another carrier will not cancel your service with Verizon. You will still be responsible for paying any outstanding charges on your account.


### **Is there a fee to cancel a line with Verizon?**


There may be a fee to cancel your line with Verizon, depending on your plan type. If you have a contract plan, you may be subject to an Early Termination Fee (ETF). For more information on service cancellation, visit[Verizon site](https://www.verizon.com/support/residential/account/manage-service/cancel.)


### **Can Verizon refuse to port my number?**


According to FCC guidelines, Verizon must port your number if you are switching to another carrier. However, there are certain circumstances where Verizon may refuse to port your number, such as if you have an outstanding balance on your account or are canceling service before the end of your contract.
