# Why Does My Landline Keep Beeping? 5 Causes and Solutions

If you're struggling to get rid of the voice message notification from your landline or are wondering why your landline keeps beeping, this article is for you.


This article will discuss 5 common causes and solutions for landline beeping, from VoIP issues to faulty cables to how to get your phone working again.


## Why Does My Landline Keep Beeping?


Here are some common reasons behind landline beeps and their solutions. Keep reading to find out what works best for you!


### 1. You Have a New Notification


When you receive a new voicemail on your landline, the phone will usually give off a distinctive ring or beep to alert you.


### 2. The Battery is not Charged


If you're wondering why your landline keeps beeping, you may not have charged the battery. Check the battery to see if you need to charge it.


### 3. An Issue with the Phone Jack


Sometimes the problem likely lies in the wiring of your telephone jack. If the wires are not connected correctly, they can come loose and create a poor connection. It can cause many problems, including a beeping sound from your phone.


### 4. AC Power Adapter Issue


If you hear constant beeping during a phone call, there's likely an issue with the AC power adapter. The beeping noise is usually caused by a power surge or voltage fluctuation, which can damage the adapter. If your AC power adapter is damaged, you must fix it.


### 5. Multiple Devices on a Single Line


When you have several devices connected to your home phone line, each one will emit a beep when someone tries to call you.


## 10 Steps to Fix Landline Beeping


Follow these 10 steps to troubleshoot and fix the landline beeping:


1. Remove the batteries in your phone.
2. Remove the phone cord.
3. Remove the AC power adapter.
4. Wait for 15 minutes after you have unplugged everything from your phone.
5. Put the batteries back into the phone.
6. Plug the phone cord back in
7. Reconnect the AC power adapter
8. Wait for 60 seconds for the system to reboot
9. Test each handset one by one
