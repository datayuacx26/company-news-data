## 1stDibs to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conference


08/13/2026


[Download (opens in new window) PDF 71 KB](https://s203.q4cdn.com/756296962/files/doc_news/1stDibs-to-Participate-in-Upcoming-Investor-Conference-a39c7f80a-2026.pdf)


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


1stdibs.com, Inc. (Nasdaq: DIBS), a leading online marketplace for luxury design products, announced today that CEO David Rosenblatt and CPO & CMO Bradford Shellhammer will present at the Midwest IDEAS Investor Conference in Chicago on Wednesday, August 26 at 10:15 AM ET.


A live webcast and replay of the presentation will be available on the company's Investor Relations website ([investors.1stdibs.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.1stdibs.com&esheet=54589074&newsitemid=20260813360496&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.1stdibs.com&index=1&md5=297087a1b807a2acf8457b3518fe148a) ).


About 1stDibs
1stDibs is a leading online marketplace for connecting design lovers with highly coveted sellers and makers of vintage, antique, and contemporary furniture, home décor, art, jewelry, watches and fashion.
