## 1stDibs to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conference

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


1stdibs.com, Inc. (Nasdaq: DIBS), a leading online marketplace for luxury design, today announced participation at the following investor conference.


1stDibs executives will participate at the East Coast IDEAS Investor Conference in New York on June 11, 2026. There will be a webcast presentation at 11:15am ET, which investors can listen to on our investor relations website at[investors.1stdibs.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.1stdibs.com&esheet=54539484&newsitemid=20260520880258&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.1stdibs.com&index=1&md5=99cc667d084f395dfbc2bbac3d8d011f) .


About 1stDibs
1stDibs is a leading online marketplace for connecting design lovers with highly coveted sellers and makers of vintage, antique, and contemporary furniture, home décor, art, jewelry, watches and fashion.
