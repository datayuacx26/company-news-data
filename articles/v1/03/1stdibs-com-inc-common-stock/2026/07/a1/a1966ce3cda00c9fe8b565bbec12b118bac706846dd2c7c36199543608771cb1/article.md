# 12 Items That Will Enliven Your Summer Table

# 12 Items That Will Enliven Your Summer Table


Alfresco entertaining calls for lighter fare — and we don't mean just the menu. Set the table (and the tone) with these colorful, quirky pieces.


by[Colleen Egan](https://www.1stdibs.com/blogs/the-study/contributors/colleen-egan/)

[Summer gatherings](https://www.1stdibs.com/collections/garden-party/furniture/dining-entertaining/) are all about loosening up. Entertaining shifts from white cloths and fine china to more eclectic tableware, like cabbage-ware plates and maybe an ice bucket in the shape of a giant strawberry. In the spirit of low-key, playful dinners, here are a dozen items that will get the party — or at least the conversation — going.


---


[Set of six French Art Deco dachshund knife rests, 1930s](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/tableware/6-french-art-deco-dachshund-basset-hound-knife-rests-light-blue-glass-1930s/id-f_46036072/)


True, no one *needs* Art Deco glass knife rests in the shape of dachshunds. But now that you know they exist, can you imagine another meal without them?


---


[Carl Auböck set of six corn-cob skewers with leather holder #3679, 1930s](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/serving-pieces/carl-aubock-6-forks-leather-holder-corn-cob-skewers-3679-vienna-1930s/id-f_50495822/)


For a designer as quirky as Carl Auböck, these small forks are pretty utilitarian, though incredibly chic options for eating corn off the cob. Dating from the 1950s, the six nickel-plated brass skewers have rattan-wrapped handles and fit into a leather holder.


---


[Mondays oyster platter, new](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/ceramics/blue-hill-at-stone-barns-oyster-platter/id-f_29615112/)


Handcrafted by Brooklyn-based ceramics studio Mondays, this free-form oyster platter was designed for chef Dan Barber’s acclaimed Blue Hill at Stone Barns restaurant.


---


[Set of six goto Murano glasses, new](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/decorative-objects/sculptures/figurative-sculptures/set-of-6-handmade-unique-goto-murano-drinking-glasses/id-f_13404271/)


*Goto* is the Venetian word for the tumblers from which glassmakers used to drink water as they worked in front of hot furnaces. These Murano *goto* glasses by Roberto Beltrami are available in 12 colors and customizable sets.


---


[Robert Picault ceramic pitcher, 1950s](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/pitchers/french-ceramic-pitcher-robert-picault-1950s/id-f_49567642/)


This pitcher, made by French ceramist Robert Picault in the 1950s, feels both rustic and elevated.


---


[Set of four Sharland England rattan placemats, new](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/tableware/rattan-placemats-set-of-4/id-f_46478962/)


Start your table-setting game strong with this set of vintage-inspired rattan placemats from[Louise Roe](https://www.1stdibs.com/introspective-magazine/louise-roe-sharland-england/) ’s brand, Sharland England.


---


[French hand-decorated fish plates, 1970s](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/dinner-plates/french-hand-decorated-fish-plates/id-f_50475192/)


Who knew fish had so much personality? These 1970s dinner plates are hand-painted, and the back of each is signed with the name of the fish depicted.


---


[Mauro Manetti strawberry ice bucket, 1960s](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/barware/strawberry-ice-bucket-mauro-manetti-firenze-circa-1960-70/id-f_45019182/)


Just try not to smile while scooping cubes from this strawberry-shaped ice bucket by Italian designer Mauro Manetti.


---


[Bertrando Di Renzo hand-painted ceramic radicchio aperitivo bowl, 2020](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/ceramics/radicchio-hand-painted-ceramic-aperitivo-bowl-made-italy/id-f_20867212/)


With the[cabbage-ware trend](https://www.1stdibs.com/blogs/the-study/cabbage-ware/) going strong, this delicate radicchio-shaped *apertivo* bowl by Bertrando Di Renzo would be at home among all manner of lettuce-related dishes.


---


[Set of three silver-and-glass swan-form salts, 1940](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/sterling-silver/silver-glass-swan-form-salts/id-f_38918992/)


With their grace and beauty,[swans have long fascinated designers](https://www.1stdibs.com/blogs/the-study/swan-trend/) , who have incorporated their forms into a variety of homewares. These German silver-and-crystal examples from the 1940s can be filled with salt, pepper or sweets.


---


[Creart set of six Londra flutes with pink bowls and orange satin bases, new](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/glass/set-of-6-londra-pink-orange-satin-base-flutes/id-f_50181372/)


Whether it’s champagne or sparkling cider, pour something bubbly into these citrusy-hued flutes by Creart, and the occasion will instantly feel more festive.


---


[Carl Auböck brass Hand bottle opener model #4224, new](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/dining-entertaining/barware/carl-aubock-model-4224-hand-brass-bottle-opener/id-f_11621553/)


Need help opening your drink? Carl Auböck can give you a hand.
