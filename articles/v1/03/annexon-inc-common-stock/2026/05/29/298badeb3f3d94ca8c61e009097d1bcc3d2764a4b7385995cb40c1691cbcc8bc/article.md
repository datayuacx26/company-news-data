# Annexon to Participate in Upcoming June Investor Conferences

[PDF Version](https://ir.annexonbio.com/node/10991/pdf)


BRISBANE, Calif., May 27, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Annexon, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=QSIXGrJbOI0lyikBTqWQ_ZY3DNd31fXCxC-uMxfa4hJoVBTmqq4UNbPF2hMCrnVE0m_4gWhn-sD5tJ3HHADz1S4PAsB9d-0GAL39oDeqFhbbpzzMjMJZkTjacF1FZrNHbnqGs2WEg6xsF49hc7FFlubXWayBntGQy-B_QXZSryEWVMusDFsFz0BeTD0CPfTY8RsD1Iqajqmnba07Y2A4MzczxQAuK3rPJwgEAx1dd6k=) (Nasdaq: ANNX), a biopharmaceutical company advancing the next generation platform of targeted immunotherapies aimed at neuroinflammatory diseases that impact nearly 10 million people worldwide, today announced that Douglas Love, Esq., president & chief executive officer, will participate in fireside chats during the following June investor conferences:


- Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, at 11:40 a.m. ET in New York, NY; and,


- Goldman Sachs 47th Annual Global Healthcare Conference on Tuesday, June 9, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. ET in Miami Beach, FL.

Replays of the webcasts will be archived on the Annexon website for 30 days following the presentations.


**About Annexon** Annexon Biosciences (Nasdaq: ANNX) is advancing the next generation platform of targeted immunotherapies for nearly 10 million people worldwide living with serious neuroinflammatory diseases. Our founding scientific approach focuses on C1q, the initiating molecule of a potent inflammatory pathway that when misdirected can lead to tissue damage and loss of function in a host of diseases. Our targeted therapies are designed to stop classical complement-driven neuroinflammation at its source to provide meaningful functional benefit and alter the course of disease. Annexon’s mission is to deliver game-changing therapies to patients so that they can live their best lives.
