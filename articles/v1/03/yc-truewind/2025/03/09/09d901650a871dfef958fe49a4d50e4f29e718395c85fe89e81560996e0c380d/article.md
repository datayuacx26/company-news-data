# Announcing Truewind in the 2025 AICPA/CPA.com Startup Accelerator

The AICPA and CPA.com Startup Accelerator helps grow startups throughout the accounting ecosystem so that innovators and cutting-edge solutions can help transform the profession. Their aim is to strengthen the ability of individual accountants and finance teams to be the go-to analysts, strategists and consultants that are indispensable to organizations across the globe.


This mission aligns with Truewind, where we are building a digital staff accountant to elevate accountants into more advisory roles, instead of back-office bookkeeping. Powered by AI, Truewind is a digital staff accountant that never sleeps, never gets sick, and always does its work. Completely predictable capacity planning.


This week, we are announcing that Truewind is a member of the 2025 AICPA/CPA.com Startup Accelerator. The accounting and finance profession of the future will look very different than it does today. Firms are already having to adapt to changes related to regulatory compliance, task automation, AI, blockchain, and more. By working with the advisors at AICPA and CPA.com, we will align closer to the product and technology capabilities most valuable to accountants.


Looking ahead, we will work with the AICPA and CPA.com advisors, leveraging their wide array of knowledge and expertise. We are grateful to the leaders of AICPA and CPA.com for their ongoing support and assembling a world-class panel of advisors from throughout the profession.
