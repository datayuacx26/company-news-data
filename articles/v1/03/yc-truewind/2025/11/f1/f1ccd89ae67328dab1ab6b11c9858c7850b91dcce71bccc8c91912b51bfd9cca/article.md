## How the Integration Works


Truewind's integration is built using Sage Intacct's APIs, ensuring secure, real-time synchronization of your accounting data.


1. **Two-Way Sync**
Truewind connects directly to Sage Intacct's general ledger, bank transactions, and entity structure. Any updates made in Truewind like reconciliations, journal entries, or notes - sync seamlessly back to Sage Intacct.
2. **Secure Data Handling**
All data stays within a secure, encrypted environment. Truewind is a Sage-approved Marketplace Partner, meeting Sage's data privacy and API usage standards.
3. **Auto-Redact PII**
Truewind embeds an auto-redaction system, where we automatically redact any sensitive personal information from the data before it gets processed by an AI. For example, if you upload a brokerage statement, we will redact the family name, before using AI to complete the work, protecting you or your clients' anonymity.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop Design**
Automation doesn't mean loss of control. Accountants are great at what they do when given the control. In Truewind, accountants can review, approve, and override AI suggestions at every step. This is done with full audit trails and version history.
5. **Entity-Aware Automation**
For multi-entity environments, Truewind automatically detects intercompany transactions and maintains reconciliations at both the entity and consolidation level.
