# Using AI to Reconcile Payments to Invoices in 80% Less Time

Hundreds of controllers and CFOs note this as a top five reason that slows the close. Payments hit the bank before invoices are marked paid. Deposits bundle multiple customers. Refunds, credits, and intercompany transactions blur the trail.


Even in Sage Intacct, the process often ends up in spreadsheets. Accountants have to manually match deposits to invoices, line by line.


That's where **Truewind's AI-powered AR reconciliation** comes in: it replaces manual matching with a continuous, intelligent process that runs on top of Sage Intacct and gets smarter every month.


Traditional AR reconciliation involves:


- **Parsing deposits** that combine multiple invoices or partial payments.
- **Tracking timing differences** between customer payments, bank clears, and invoice updates.
- **Managing multiple entities** and intercompany settlements.
- **Handling exceptions** like overpayments, short pays, and unapplied credits.


For most accountants, this means long nights in Excel, pivot tables, and color-coded comments. This is costly. It costs time, accuracy, and visibility into the true cash position.


## The Truewind Approach Using AI


Truewind's AR agent reconciles your invoices to the deposits in your bank account. Whether you use a 3rd party system or a proprietary home-built system, we can structure the raw data into a GL format.


### Here's how it works:


### Continuous Data Sync


Truewind pulls real-time data from Sage Intacct: open invoices, customer receipts, deposits, and credit memos.


### AI-Powered Matching


Using machine learning, the system identifies probable matches between invoices and deposits - even when names, amounts, or references don't align perfectly.


Example: $9,950 deposit linked to two invoices ($7,200 + $2,750) from the same customer, detected via remittance memo and pattern learning.


### Exception Handling


The AI flags anomalies such as partial payments, duplicate receipts, or missing AR entries and routes them for review.


### Human-in-the-Loop Review


Accountants stay in control. Suggested matches can be confirmed, edited, or overridden directly from the Truewind interface.


### Two-Way Sync to Sage Intacct


Once approved, reconciled transactions and adjustments automatically post back to Sage Intacct, ensuring the GL and subledger remain perfectly aligned.


Truewind connects directly to Sage Intacct. Our AR agent automates this reconciliation from end to end.
