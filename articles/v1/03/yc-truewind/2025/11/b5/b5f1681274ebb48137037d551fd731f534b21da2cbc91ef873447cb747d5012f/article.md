# Accelerating the Future of Accounting Automation

At Truewind, our mission has always been simple: **help accountants close faster, with confidence, and love their work again.**


Today, we're thrilled to announce that **Truewind is officially a Sage Intacct Marketplace Partner** .


This partnership brings our AI-powered digital staff accountant directly to Sage Intacct users - giving finance teams a seamless way to automate reconciliations, accelerate the month-end close, and deliver higher-quality financials with less effort.


But this isn't just an integration. It's a shared vision for what accounting can become.


## A Partnership to Build the AI-Native Month End Close, on Sage Intacct


For decades, finance teams have worked in cycles: collect, reconcile, review, repeat.


Even with powerful systems like Sage Intacct, so much of the close still depends on human pattern recognition: matching deposits, amortizing prepaids, explaining variances.


We believe that's about to change.


Hundreds of teams are using Truewind's AI agents that sit on top of Sage Intacct as a **digital layer of intelligence;** one that learns your workflows, automates repetitive accounting tasks, and keeps your books continuously up to date.


With this partnership, Sage Intacct users can now:


- **Categorize account and dimensions** for as many banks and credit cards as you have; thousands of transactions classified in minutes.
- **Automate reconciliations** for accounts receivables with AI matching and real-time syncing.
- **Generate prepaid and fixed asset schedules** automatically, with amortizations posted to the GL.


This empowers accountants. Let AI handle the rote work faster. We want our accountants to raise the bar on being a value-added partner; this is made possible with Truewind + Sage Intacct.


## Why Sage Intacct + Truewind


Sage Intacct has long been the platform of choice for **modern, multi-entity accounting** , trusted by firms, family offices, and mid-market finance teams who demand precision and scalability.


Truewind extends that strength with **AI-native workflows** built specifically for Sage Intacct's architecture.


Our engineering team works hand-in-hand with Sage APIs to ensure secure, real-time, two-way synchronization - so every automated reconciliation or AI-generated entry remains perfectly aligned with your books.


Together, we're delivering:


- **Speed:** Close your books in days, not weeks.
- **Accuracy:** Eliminate manual errors with AI-assisted review.
- **Visibility:** Know where every account stands, every day.


### Our Vision: Continuous Close, Continuous Trust


We believe the future of accounting isn't about "AI replacing accountants." It's about **AI amplifying accountants.**


Whether you're an accounting firm or a corporate accountant, we're all committed to the same calling: to guide financial decisions, ensure compliance, and provide strategic advice. Companies and economies do better because you do your work well.


With Sage Intacct as the system of record and Truewind as the system of intelligence, we're moving from a world of month-end to a world of continuous close. One where your books are always ready, your data always reconciled, and your team always in control.


## What Comes Next


This is just the beginning.


Over the coming months, you'll see deeper integrations between Truewind and Sage Intacct: from expanded reconciliation modules to new AI agents that automate flux analysis, review commentary, and client deliverables.


Our goal is to make the close so seamless that it disappears - replaced by a continuous, confident accounting workflow built on Sage Intacct.


If you're a Sage Intacct user, accounting firm, or controller looking to modernize your close, now's the time. We'd love to meet with you and hear about the bottlenecks to your close.
