# Truewind Integrates with Sage Intacct to Unlock AI-Native Close Automation

**SAN FRANCISCO, CA - November 18, 2025** - Truewind, an AI-powered digital accountant designed to help finance teams close the month-end process faster, today announced an integration with Sage Intacct as an approved Marketplace partner.


The collaboration combines Truewind's automation technology with Sage's cloud financial management platform, enabling finance teams to streamline operations, reduce manual work, and deliver real-time visibility into financial performance.


"Our relationship with Sage is an important step in our mission to make accounting simpler and more intelligent," said[Alex Lee](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexlee611/) , Co-Founder and CEO of[Truewind.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/truewindai/) "Sage is trusted by leading finance teams worldwide. Together, we're enabling customers to close their books faster, identify issues earlier, and strengthen confidence in their data."


The native integration syncs financial activity with Sage Intacct's general ledger, ensuring accuracy and alignment across entities. This enables finance teams to automate reconciliations, accelerate the month-end close, and maintain accuracy across entities through continuous, real-time synchronization.


Key benefits of using Truewind via the Sage Intacct integration include:


- Continuous synchronization of transactions, accruals, and deposit activity
- Automated reconciliations and journal entry posting directly to Sage Intacct
- Improved accuracy through AI-driven categorization and validation
- Reduced manual data entry and faster month-end close cycles
- Integration requiring minimal configuration and setup


Truewind's AI-driven platform automates routine accounting work, so teams can focus on analysis and strategy. By embedding automation into daily workflows, finance teams reduce errors, improve efficiency, and operate with always up-to-date financial data.
