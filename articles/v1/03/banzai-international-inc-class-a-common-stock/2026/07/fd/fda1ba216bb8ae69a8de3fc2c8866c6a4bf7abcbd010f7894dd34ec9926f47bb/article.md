# 6 Best Video Marketing Tools for Increasing Sales

Most video tools weren’t built with sales and marketing teams in mind, making it hard to find platforms that support both creating content and driving revenue.


**That disconnect wastes budget, slows down follow-ups, and makes it harder to prove the impact of your strategies on pipeline.**


This article breaks down **six video marketing tools that help you create, share, and track videos that directly support sales** —from outreach to lead nurturing to product demos.


We also cover the **key features to prioritize** and **how to choose the right solution for your team.**


With this clear **decision-making framework** and **breakdown of the top platforms,** you’ll be able to select a tool that:


- **Fits your process**
- **Connects video to revenue**
- **Helps your team close more deals**


## Key features to look for in video marketing software


Choosing the right video marketing tools to drive sales means focusing on features that help you:


- **Create content efficiently**
- **Get it in front of the right audience**
- **Track performance**
- **Use that data to move leads through your funnel**


Here are the essential features to prioritize:


### Video creation and editing capabilities


Your video software should **make creating professional-quality content as fast and flexible as possible** —even if your team has limited production resources; the right tools and workflows play a key role in any effective video content creation strategy.


Look for options that support **remote video capture, simple editing workflows, and the ability to brand your content** for different audiences.


This flexibility helps you **produce content that fits each sales stage** , from quick explainer clips to in-depth demos.


### Hosting and distribution options


Once your videos are ready, you need **flexible ways to host and distribute them.**


The best tools support **live events, on-demand hosting, and direct sharing through email, landing pages, and social channels** —allowing you to reach leads where they’re already active and capture meaningful engagement data.


### Analytics and performance tracking


Without clear performance data, it’s impossible to know which videos contribute to sales. Strong video tools give you detailed analytics on:


- **Views**
- **Completion rates**
- **Drop-off points**
- **Individual viewer behavior**


The most valuable platforms don’t just track views—they connect engagement patterns to buying signals, so you know which content resonates and which leads are actively considering your solution.


### Personalization and interactivity


Static, generic videos rarely help close deals. Your platform should make it easy to **personalize video content for different industries, personas, or even individual leads.**


Interactivity is also key—whether that’s adding **clickable CTAs, in-video forms, or polls and live Q&A** in webinar settings.


These interactive elements capture signals of interest that help **qualify leads faster.**


### CRM and marketing automation integrations


Videos don’t exist in a vacuum—they need to work within your existing sales and marketing workflows.


Tools that **connect directly to your CRM** or marketing automation platform allow you to **automatically log video engagement data, trigger follow-ups, and build video into lead scoring models.**


This integration helps turn video from a standalone asset into a key part of your sales system.

## How to choose the right video marketing tool for your team


The best video marketing tool for impacting your sales performance depends on:


1. **How your team uses video** and
2. **What role video plays** in your sales process.


As you prioritize features, keep these factors in mind:


- **Team size and resources:**


If you need to create high-quality video but have limited production capacity, tools with remote capture and easy editing save time and budget.


- **Sales cycle length:**


For longer, consultative sales, you may need tools that support live demos and webinars, while fast-moving sales teams benefit more from quick, personalized outreach videos.


- **Content strategy:**


If video is central to your lead generation and nurturing efforts, look for platforms that combine video creation, on-demand hosting, lead capture, and analytics into a single workflow.
