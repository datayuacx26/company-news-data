# Taking the Stage at Index™ 26


**Charlotte, N.C., May 7, 2026** — Magnera is gearing up for a dynamic presence at[INDEX™26](https://www.indexnonwovens.com/) , the world’s leading global exhibition for the nonwovens industry. Alongside showcasing its extensive portfolio of material solutions, Magnera will participate in the INDEX™ Exhibitor Product Presentations, spotlighting progress in fluid barrier innovation.


As part of the event’s official program, two members of Magnera’s innovation leadership team will share perspectives on shifting market demands and the technical considerations shaping the next generation of protective materials.


**What to Expect**


On May 20 at 14:30, in Room R, Hall 3,[Robert Garcia](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robertgarciapano/) and[Sebastian Pordomingo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/spordomingo/) will present Magnera’s[award‑nominated Next‑Generation Liquid Barrier Technology.](https://magnera.com/news/magneras-next-generation-liquid-barrier-solution-nominated-for-index26-award/)


The session will address the changing regulatory and commercial landscape surrounding PFAS, including global phase‑out initiatives, pressure on supply chains, and rising cost challenges. The speakers will also outline how Magnera’s latest liquid barrier development offers a practical and scalable alternative designed to meet evolving performance expectations.


**Visit Magnera at INDEX™26**


Stop by Booth 2127 to learn more about Magnera’s material solutions, built to support performance, consistency, and application‑driven innovation across the nonwovens industry.
