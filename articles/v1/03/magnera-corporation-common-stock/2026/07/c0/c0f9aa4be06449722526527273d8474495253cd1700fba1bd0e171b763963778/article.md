## Carles Moix Elected to EDANA Board of Governors


Magnera is proud to announce that Carles Moix, Vice President of Finance for EMEIA and Asia, has been elected to the EDANA Board of Governors for a three-year term running from July 1, 2026, to July 1, 2029.


**Key Highlights**


- Carles Moix elected to the EDANA Board of Governors
- Three-year term (2026–2029)
- Represents Magnera within the global nonwovens industry
- Reinforces Magnera’s industry leadership


The appointment was confirmed during EDANA’s 2026 Annual General Meeting in Brussels, where five new board members were elected to help guide the association’s strategy and priorities for the global nonwovens industry. As a member of the Board of Governors, Carles will contribute to shaping the future direction of EDANA and supporting initiatives that foster growth, profitability, innovation, sustainability and collaboration across the industry.


Carles leads Finance for EMEIA and Asia at Magnera and brings extensive international leadership experience across manufacturing, packaging and industrial sectors. Throughout his career, he has demonstrated expertise in:


- Global operations
- Financial transformation
- Organizational leadership
- Strategic growth initiatives
- Cross-border mergers and acquisitions.


“Carles’ appointment is a reflection of both his leadership capabilities and Magnera’s commitment to advancing our industry,”[Paul Harmon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paul-harmon-492527a/) , Chief Innovation and Marketing Officer at Magnera.”EDANA plays a critical role in bringing together industry leaders to address challenges, share knowledge and drive innovation across the nonwovens value chain. Carles will be an outstanding representative for Magnera and our industry as EDANA continues its important work.”


On June 17-18, EDANA members came together for the Annual General Meeting (AGM) and Board Meeting.


As a leading manufacturer of specialty materials serving[filtration](https://magnera.com/market/filtration/) ,[healthcare](https://magnera.com/market/healthcare/) ,[hygiene](https://magnera.com/market/hygiene/) ,[wipes](https://magnera.com/market/wipes/) ,[construction](https://magnera.com/market/building-construction/) and industrial markets:


- Magnera recognizes the importance of active participation in industry organizations. Through engagement with associations such as[EDANA](https://www.edana.org/) ,[INDA](https://www.inda.org/) , and the[Global Nonwovens Alliance](https://www.globalnonwovensalliance.org/) .
- Magnera collaborates with industry peers, contributes to dialogue around emerging market and regulatory trends, and helps advance innovation that strengthens the nonwovens industry worldwide.


Carles’ election further reinforces Magnera’s commitment to industry leadership. By actively participating in organizations that shape the future of nonwovens, Magnera helps drive conversations around sustainability, product innovation, operational excellence and the evolving needs of customers and consumers. These collaborations create opportunities to share expertise, influence industry priorities and support the development of solutions that move the industry forward.


“We congratulate Carles on this well-deserved appointment and look forward to the insight and leadership he will bring to EDANA and the broader nonwovens community.”

**About EDANA**


[EDANA](https://www.edana.org/about-us/who-we-are) is the international association and voice of the nonwovens and related industries. Since 1971, the association has provided for its[270+ members](https://www.edana.org/membership/members?hsLang=en) , a comprehensive range of services with the information and data necessary to enhance the industry’s goals and performance. The association has a global reach with a focus on Europe, the Middle East and Africa. All activities reflect the changing dynamics of the nonwovens industry with three areas of particular focus:


- Advocate free and fair trade


- Foster product stewardship


- Create an environment beneficial to innovation and to sustainable and profitable growth


**About Magnera**


Magnera Corporation (NYSE: MAGN) serves 1,000+ customers worldwide, offering a wide range of material solutions, including components for absorbent hygiene products, protective apparel, wipes, specialty building and construction products, and products serving the food and beverage industry. The distinct scale and comprehensive portfolio of Magnera’s products bring customers more materials and choices. Magnera builds personal partnerships that withstand an ever-changing world.
