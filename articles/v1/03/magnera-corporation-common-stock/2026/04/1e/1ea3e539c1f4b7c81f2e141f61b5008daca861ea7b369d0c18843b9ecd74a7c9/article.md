# Magnera LAUNCH Internship Program 2026 | Apply Now


Magnera’s Global Headquarters based in Charlotte, NC along with sites across the United States are opening the door for the next generation of talent through its **2026 LAUNCH Internship Program:** an immersive, hands‑on experience designed to help emerging professionals take meaningful steps toward a purpose‑driven career.


Built for individuals who are eager to learn, explore, and contribute, the LAUNCH Internship Program offers far more than a traditional internship. Participants gain the opportunity to work on real business projects, collaborate with experienced professionals, and gain exposure to global operations across Magnera’s organization.


**What LAUNCH Stands For**


The LAUNCH Program is centered on six core pillars that guide every intern experience:


- **Leadership** – Developing the confidence to lead through collaboration and accountability
- **Ambition** – Encouraging curiosity, initiative, and growth
- **Understanding** – Learning how different teams and functions work together across the business
- **Networking** – Building meaningful professional relationships with peers and leaders
- **Curiosity** – Exploring ideas, asking questions, and challenging the status quo
- **Hands‑on Experience** – Solving real-world challenges that make a tangible impact


Through this framework, interns are empowered to deliver meaningful solutions while learning directly from subject‑matter experts across Magnera’s global operations.


**A Real‑World Learning Experience**


Magnera’s LAUNCH interns don’t just observe, they contribute. The program is designed to help students strengthen their skills, broaden their perspectives, and gain confidence by tackling real challenges that matter to the business. Along the way, interns receive guidance, feedback, and mentorship to support both personal and professional development.


For those who are motivated, curious, and ready to make an impact, the LAUNCH Internship Program provides the foundation needed to grow a career with intention.


**Applications Now Open**


Applications for **Summer 2026** are officially open, and Magnera invites driven candidates to apply.


*At this time, internship applications are being accepted from candidates located in the* ***United States.*** **
