# A Brand New Record: Cinemark Achieves Biggest Domestic Weekend Box Office of All Time as Spider-Man: Brand New Day Delivers the Company’s Highest Grossing Single-Title, Three-Day Domestic Opening

## A Brand New Record: Cinemark Achieves Biggest Domestic Weekend Box Office of All Time as Spider-Man: Brand New Day Delivers the Company’s Highest Grossing Single-Title, Three-Day Domestic Opening


August 03, 2026 7:30am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_f50920fdc9d372f34826b9f982cf8788/cinemark/news/2026-08-03_A_Brand_New_Record_Cinemark_Achieves_Biggest_647.pdf)


*Cinemark also achieved all-time records across premium formats, with the best-ever weekend for Cinemark XD, ScreenX panoramic screens and D-BOX motion seats*


*Demand for Cinemark’s food, beverage and merchandise offerings reached new heights, driving a record food and beverage revenue weekend*


PLANO, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Cinemark Holdings, Inc.](http://www.cinemark.com/) (NYSE: CNK), one of the largest and most influential theatrical exhibition companies in the world, today announced that July 31 through August 2 was the biggest domestic weekend box office of all time for the company, propelled by the sensational opening of *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* , with additional contribution from *The Odyssey’s* strong holdover performance *.* Additionally, the web-slinging success of *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* delivered Cinemark’s best-ever domestic box office for a three-day opening weekend, surpassing the previous record set by *Avengers: Endgame* .


In addition to the all-time box office highs, Cinemark’s continued investments in premium entertainment experiences with its expansive portfolio of enhanced viewing options drove the best-ever three-day U.S. weekend results for Cinemark XD, the world’s No. 1 exhibitor-branded premium large format, ScreenX 270-degree panoramic screens and D-BOX motion seats. As moviegoers swung into theaters for Spider-Man's latest adventure, they also indulged in food, beverage and merchandise, delivering Cinemark’s highest weekend food and beverage revenue of all time.


“Cinemark’s outsized performance for *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* across our entire global circuit once again underscores how great films in immersive, shared theatrical settings have the ability to create must-see cultural events on a heroic scale – especially at Cinemark,” said Sean Gamble, President and CEO of Cinemark. “Hats off to our global team for making such a significant impact in enabling these special moments for our guests, and congratulations to our partners at Sony and Marvel on this exceptional weekend.”

**About Cinemark Holdings, Inc.**


Cinemark Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: CNK) provides extraordinary out-of-home entertainment experiences as one of the largest and most influential theatrical exhibition companies in the world.
