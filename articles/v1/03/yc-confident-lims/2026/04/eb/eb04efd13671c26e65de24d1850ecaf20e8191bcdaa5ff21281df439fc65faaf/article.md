# Confident LIMS & QuantumLeaf Optimize Your Operations


Confident LIMS + QuantumLeaf Integration: Optimize Cannabis Operations and Compliance.


FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: 8/29/23


**The Complete Cannabis Enterprise Software Solution**


Connecting Every. Single. Aspect. Of Your Cannabis Enterprise


San Francisco, 8/29/23:[Confident LIMS](https://www.confidentlims.com/) , a trusted provider of technology and services for cannabis testing and trade, and[QuantumLeaf Solutions](http://quantumleafsolutions.com/) , a leading provider of comprehensive seed-to-sale business management solutions for the cannabis industry, are thrilled to announce a new integration. This strategic partnership aims to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of cannabis operations by providing seamless connectivity between QuantumLeaf’s ERP platform and Confident LIMS’ testing and trading platform.


[Get Started](https://get.confidentcanna.com/confident-quantumleaf-integration/)


The integration between QuantumLeaf and Confident LIMS brings together two industry-leading platforms to create a seamless experience for cannabis companies. By connecting QuantumLeaf’s comprehensive seed-to-sale management capabilities with Confident LIMS’ efficient testing and trading platform, businesses can optimize their operations, enhance compliance, and accelerate growth.


Key features and benefits of the integration include:


- **Streamlined workflow:** Eliminate the need for manual data entry and simplify the transfer of information between QuantumLeaf and Confident LIMS platforms, reducing the risk of errors and saving valuable time for cannabis businesses.
- **Enhanced compliance:** Seamlessly integrate lab results data from Confident LIMS into QuantumLeaf’s ERP platform, businesses can ensure compliance with regulatory requirements across multiple jurisdictions, minimizing the risk of penalties and maintaining a strong reputation within the industry.
- **Improved inventory management:** Allow for real-time inventory tracking, enabling businesses to optimize stock levels, reduce waste, and ensure timely product availability.
- **Efficient trade execution:** Print compliance labels out of actual costed production runs with synced test results. You can also choose to streamline logistics by selling based on batch test results and automatically attaching and emailing required COAs on shipments.


## How To Get It


To enable the integration, all you have to do is[subscribe to a Data Plan](https://orders.confidentcannabis.com/settings/client/integrations) for your various Confident accounts. Once you sign up, you will have access to your Confident LIMS API Key and Webhook for one flat monthly rate. Simply connect your Confident LIMS account to QuantumLeaf to start seeing your test results.
