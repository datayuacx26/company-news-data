## Why We Built Client Dashboards


When you submit samples for testing, you already generate a steady stream of valuable data: how much you test, what you test for, how fast results come back, and what it all costs. But that data lived inside individual orders and Certificates of Analysis (COAs), useful for viewing one result at a time, hard to see as a trend.


Clients told us they wanted answers to practical questions. Are my potency values drifting? Which assays fail most often? Is my turnaround time getting better or worse? What am I actually spending on testing this quarter?


Manual spreadsheets or custom-built tools can answer those questions. They just should not have to.


‍


## New for Everyone: Volume Dashboards, Free


Your Analytics tab now includes a Volume collection, available to every client at no cost:


- **Samples** : see how many samples you have submitted month over month, so you can plan around your busy and slow seasons.
- **Sample-Assays** : break that volume down by assay, so you can see what you order most.


‍


## New for Client Pro: Six Advanced Analytics Collections


Clients on the Pro plan get everything in Free, plus 19 additional dashboards across six collections:


- **Test Results** : analyte values, averages, and standard deviations over time, plus failure rates by assay and by analyte.
- **Velocity** : your turnaround times, with a clear view of which samples ran late.
- **Expenses** : your testing spend at the order and sample level.
- **Payables** : outstanding balances, with line-item detail when you need it.
- **Prices** : what you pay over time, by sample, test package, and sample-assay.
- **Market Share** : fully anonymized data on how your volume compares to similar trends on the network.

### Filters That Actually Narrow Things Down


Every dashboard shares the same filter set: date range, lab, matrix category and type, assay, analyte, test package, industry, compliance status, and rush. The default view shows your last 12 months. Need only the rush samples from one lab, for a single assay, in Q1? Three clicks.

### Export Anything


Every dashboard and individual chart downloads as CSV, JPG, or PDF. Pro clients can also create and save configurable CSV export templates: choose the exact sample fields, assays, and analytes you want, save the template, and reuse it on every export.

## FAQ


### Why do not my dashboard numbers match my own spreadsheet?


Dashboards refresh once a day between midnight and 6am PT, and they only count samples and orders processed through Confident. If you track outside testing in your spreadsheet, the totals will differ.


### Can I download the dashboards?


Yes. Every dashboard and individual chart has a download button supporting CSV, JPG, and PDF formats.


### Do I need technical skills to use the dashboards?


No. Dashboards live in your existing Analytics tab and work with point-and-click filters. No API, no query language, no setup.


### What does Market Share show?


Fully anonymized comparisons between your sample volume and similar trends across the Confident network, under the same filters you apply to your own data.
