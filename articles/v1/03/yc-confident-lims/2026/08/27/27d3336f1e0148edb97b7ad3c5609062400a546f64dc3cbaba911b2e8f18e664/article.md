# One Lab Account. Every Industry You Serve.

Most analytical labs test more than one kind of sample. Testing clients change, so sample matrices change. Regulations change, so compliance requirements change.


As your lab's business changes, your LIMS should change too.


That's why Confident LIMS now supports your full mix of industries — Agriculture, Cannabis & Hemp, Environmental, Food & Beverage, Nutraceuticals, Cosmetics & Personal Care, Industrial Chemicals, Oil & Gas, and more — natively, from a single lab account. No second system. One login. Industry-specific configurations.

## What multi-industry LIMS support actually means in Confident


Confident LIMS began by serving analytical testing labs in the highly regulated legal cannabis industry a decade ago and expanded support for labs in hemp and nutraceuticals over the years. Today, Confident is announcing new-and-improved functionality that seamlessly and dynamically adapts lab workflow and configurations according to each industry the lab serves. This means that a single lab account can operate across multiple industries natively, with automated, industry-specific workflows that adapt to each industry to make your lab move faster than ever.


Your menu, order forms, table views, calculations, action limits, and report templates dynamically reflect the actual work your lab is doing — in whatever industry it's doing it. Cannabis & Hemp workflows, including METRC and BioTrack integrations, continue to function exactly as before.


‍


## What's new in Confident LIMS


The release is organized around three practical changes to how labs configure and run their testing operations.


### Industry-specific test packages


Each supported industry has its own test packages, built around its sample matrix categories, fields, analytes, and reporting requirements. No generic templates retrofitted to cover everything.


### Cross-industry ordering


Your lab and your clients can now place orders across all your supported industries from a single account. Intake forms, Chain of Custody, COA templates, and LIMS configurations automatically adapt to the industry selected on each order. Cannabis & Hemp orders, including regulator integrations like METRC and BioTrack, work the same as before.


### Filter and organize by industry


Table views across the platform now include an Industry column. Sort, filter, and group your work by the real industry of each sample — the way your lab actually operates.

## The industries Confident LIMS supports today


Confident is used across eight analytical testing industries.


### With native industry configuration (this release)


**Agriculture.** Soil analysis, pesticide residue testing, and crop quality workflows. USDA and EPA compliance tools, configurable templates, and automated reporting.


**Cannabis & Hemp.** Purpose-built for cannabis and hemp testing labs. METRC and BioTrack integrations, seed-to-sale compliance tracking, and a branded client portal for real-time results.


**Environmental.** Water, soil, and air quality testing. NELAP accreditation support, EPA method templates, and automated chain-of-custody tracking.


**Food & Beverage.** Pathogen, nutritional, and contaminant testing workflows. FDA and FSMA compliance with built-in audit trails and configurable reporting.


**Nutraceuticals & Supplements.** Potency, contaminant, and identity testing. cGMP and FDA compliance with configurable assay templates and full traceability.


**Cosmetics & Personal Care.** Stability testing, preservative efficacy, heavy metals screening, and FDA compliance — all in one configurable platform.


### Also supported on the platform


**Industrial Chemicals.** Raw material QC, batch release, and ASTM/ISO method workflows with configurable templates, instrument integrations, and full traceability.


**Oil & Gas.** Crude analysis, refinery QC, and fuel quality testing with configurable workflows, API/ASTM method templates, and automated reporting.


Don't see your industry? Adding a new industry configuration now takes days, not months. Tell us what you need and your customer success team will scope the rollout with you.

## Frequently asked questions


### Can one lab account run multiple industries in Confident LIMS?


Yes. A single Confident LIMS lab account can now operate across all supported industries with a single login. Test packages, sample matrices, order forms, Chain of Custody, COA templates, compliance workflows, third-party integrations, and LIMS configurations automatically adapt to the industry selected on each order.


### Which industries got native configuration in this release?


Agriculture, Cannabis & Hemp, Environmental, Food & Beverage, Cosmetics & Personal Care, and Nutraceuticals have native configurations in this release. Industrial Chemicals, and Oil & Gas testing are also supported on the platform today, with native configurations rolling out as demand grows.


### Can existing customers migrate to multi-industry support?


Yes. The new industry designation applies to new orders going forward. Talk to your Account Manager or email support@confidentlims.com for a quick walkthrough of how to configure new industry settings in your account. New industry configurations now take days to deploy, not months.


### How do I request a new industry?


Talk to your Account Manager or email support@confidentlims.com for a quick walkthrough of how to configure new industry settings in your account.
