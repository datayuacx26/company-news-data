# One Platform, Every Industry You Serve: Confident Beyond Cannabis

Yes — one LIMS can run cannabis, food, environmental, nutraceutical, and more from a single account. A multi-industry LIMS is a Laboratory Information Management System whose test packages, COA templates, chain-of-custody records, and compliance workflows reconfigure by the industry of the sample, so one login serves every service line your lab offers.

## Built in cannabis. Ready for what's next.


Confident built its platform in cannabis — and cannabis was a hard place to build. Few industries have such a complex web of strict, state-specific regulations, where the rules shift by jurisdiction and the documentation has to keep up in real time. Conquering that was the point: a system that handles cannabis compliance, state by state, is a system ready for nearly any other regulated testing industry.


That's why we're investing in adjacent verticals now. The instrumentation carries over — an HPLC doesn't know whether it's analyzing a cannabis extract or a dietary supplement — and the software is ready for it. One area we're especially excited about is peptide testing. Part of the nutraceutical and supplement space, peptides are one of the hottest growth areas in analytical testing right now, and Confident already processes hundreds of peptide samples every day.


## What a multi-industry LIMS actually means


It's not a marketing label for a system with several industry pages. It's an operational capability: one account, many verticals, configured — not rebuilt — for each. Test packages and analyte lists change by industry. COA templates carry the right fields, units, and references for each matrix. Chain-of-custody rules adapt per vertical. And compliance documentation follows the framework that applies to each sample — without forcing the rest of your lab to follow it.


## The cost of running separate systems per vertical


Adding a service line is a growth decision; bolting on a second LIMS to support it is a tax on that growth. Every extra system means another license, another integration, and another interface your analysts context-switch between. Reporting fragments — you can't see throughput, turnaround time, or rework across the whole lab when half your data lives in another tool. And every handoff between systems is a place where a sample ID gets mistyped. One platform keeps the gain where it belongs: in your margin.


## How configuration adapts per industry


This is where “configurable” earns its keep — you adjust the system to each vertical without writing code or waiting on a developer. Test packages and matrices, COA templates, chain-of-custody rules, and compliance workflows (ISO 17025, NELAP, METRC, FSMA, cGMP) each reconfigure by sample type. A configurable LIMS helps you comply with the framework that applies to each sample, building the documentation into the daily workflow instead of leaving it for the week before an audit.


## Where labs expand next


The move from one vertical to many tends to start where the instrumentation overlaps:


- **Food and beverage.** Potency and contaminant methods share with food-safety panels — pathogen, nutritional, and shelf-life testing, with COAs and traceability that help you comply with FSMA and HACCP.
- **Environmental.** Water, soil, and PFAS work draw on existing chromatography and spectrometry, with documentation aligned to NELAP/TNI accreditation.
- **Nutraceutical, including peptides.** Supplement and peptide testing lean on the same potency and contaminant chemistry, with cGMP-oriented documentation for identity and label verification.
- **Cosmetics and personal care.** Stability, microbial, and ingredient testing extend naturally, with reporting shaped to emerging MoCRA expectations.


Confident already runs eight analytical verticals from a single account and serves more than half of the licensed cannabis labs in North America — backed by a 15,000+ client network and more than 5M samples processed each year.
