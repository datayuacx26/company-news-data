# Confident LIMS Integrates with Databis for Verified CoA QR Transparency

Confident LIMS and Databis are partnering to help cannabis brands turn verified lab results into consumer-facing product trust—without the spreadsheets, downloads, and re-uploads that create errors. This integration connects Databis directly to Confident LIMS, so brands can sync structured lab results and the matching Certificate of Analysis (COA) PDF from the source system. No copying values by hand. No "which PDF is the latest?" confusion. Just a clean data path from lab verification to QR-driven consumer experiences.

## How Databis gets COA data from Confident LIMS


Databis integrates with Confident LIMS through a secure API connection to import verified COA data at the source. That eliminates manual data entry and keeps lab results consistent everywhere they appear. Once synced, Databis creates durable, blockchain-backed digital records for COAs and products—so brands can publish verifiable product information through serialized QR codes, with real-time updates as new results become available.


## Why verified COA data breaks down after the PDF


A Certificate of Analysis is more than a document. It's a compliance artifact, a retailer requirement, and often the only thing standing between a trusted product and doubt.


The problem is what happens after the lab finalizes results. Many teams still move lab results downstream by downloading PDFs, copying key numbers into label systems or web pages, re-uploading COAs to QR platforms, and repeating the process every time a result changes.


That workflow invites transcription errors, delays, and version mismatch. One typo on a cannabinoid value can become a labeling issue. One outdated PDF can become a consumer-trust issue.


## What the Confident LIMS + Databis integration does


Databis connects directly to Confident LIMS to pull verified data from the lab's system of record. That means Databis receives the same approved information the lab produced—without intermediate steps that can distort it.


### Source-level sync (structured results + COA PDFs)


Databis can automatically sync structured result fields, so values are machine-readable, and the matching COA PDF, so the consumer download matches the numbers shown.


### Real-time updates as results change


If new results are posted or corrected in Confident LIMS, Databis can reflect those updates so consumer-facing pages stay current. That reduces stale-QR-code problems, especially for brands scaling SKUs and batches.


### Durable records for traceability


After sync, Databis creates blockchain-backed digital records for COAs and products as part of its platform. The goal: an immutable, long-lived record that stays accessible over time.


## Why this matters for QR programs and labeling workflows


When a brand puts a QR code on packaging, they're making a promise: scan this and you'll see what's actually in the product.


That promise breaks if the QR page shows one set of numbers and the PDF shows another, if the posted COA is an older version, or if someone updates a value in one system but not the other.


Pulling results directly from Confident LIMS helps keep product information auditable, consistent, and defensible across packaging, e-commerce, and consumer QR experiences. That's especially useful for serialized QR packaging programs where brands need repeatable accuracy batch over batch.


## Benefits for brands, operations, and consumers


### For brands and operations teams


Fewer manual steps mean less time spent downloading, editing, and re-uploading COAs. Structured results and matching PDFs reduce mismatch and labeling headaches. And because downstream product information traces back to the LIMS audit trail, your audit posture improves.


### For consumers


Consumers get instant access to verified product information via QR codes, more confidence that the data shown matches lab-approved results, and a more transparent experience that supports loyalty and repeat engagement.


## How Databis connects brands and consumers


Databis connects cannabis brands and consumers through unique, serialized QR codes placed on products. When consumers scan a Databis QR code, they can access detailed product information and collect a digital version of the product in their account—helping brands build direct, ongoing relationships with consumers.


Databis also provides brands with real-time, verified feedback on product performance batch over batch, creating a direct feedback loop with consumers.
