## **Why Your Testing Lab and Labeling System Must Work Together**


In highly regulated industries like cannabis and manufacturing, labeling is not a standalone process. Businesses such as producers, manufacturers and distributors are often required by law to label packaging with the unique test results directly associated with the specific batch of inventory within the packaging. This compliance requirement presents a monumental challenge for vendors, as every SKU and every production batch invariably receives a unique set of lab test results that must be correctly identified on the label. Furthermore, labels depend on accurate, validated lab data—and regulators expect that data to be traceable, consistent, and controlled.


Without tight integration between lab testing systems and labeling software, businesses face risks such as:


- Manual transcription errors between lab testing platform and labeling systems


- Inconsistent test results appearing on Certificates of Analysis (COAs) and product labels


- Delays caused by reprints, corrections, or failed inspections


- Increased audit findings due to weak data traceability


- Widespread product recalls or loss of permits and licenses


A compliance-focused lab testing platform paired with a purpose-built labeling platform helps eliminate these risks.
