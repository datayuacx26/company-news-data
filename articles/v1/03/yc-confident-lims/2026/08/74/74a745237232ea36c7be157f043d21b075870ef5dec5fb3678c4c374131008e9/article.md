Labs don’t just *use* **Confident LIMS** — they rely on it. Respondents included a representative sample of Confident’s analytical testing laboratory users in the United States and Canada.


Below, those survey responses are presented in precise stats to illustrate how much labs depend on Confident to run their business every day.

## **Who at the Lab Uses Confident LIMS?**


When we asked ***“* What role(s) do you have at your lab? *”,*** we saw that Confident users span across **all roles at the lab** — not just the core **Lab Team (43%)** — including **Leadership (25%), Front Office (21%), Back Office (6%), and Other roles (6%)** . Respondents could select multiple roles given that, reflecting how many people wear more than one hat.


*Pie chart showing that 43% of role selections are Lab Team, 25% Leadership, 21% Front Office, 6% Back Office, and 6% Other, illustrating that nearly every part of the lab organization relies on Confident LIMS.*


**What this tells us:** Confident isn’t a niche tool used by one department; it’s *the* shared system-of-record across leadership, lab operations, front office and back office teams — exactly where strategy, operations, compliance, and customer experience intersect.

## **Daily Usage: Confident LIMS as the Operational Hub**


When we asked *“How often do you typically use Confident?”,* the results were decisive: **89% of respondents log in every day, underscoring that Confident is a daily operational system, not mission-critical tool for every day work.**


*Pie chart showing how often users access Confident LIMS: 89% use it every day, 7% a few times per week, 2% a few times per month, and 2% rarely, highlighting that most users rely on Confident daily.*


With that level of daily usage, Confident effectively becomes the operational hub of the lab. If Confident isn’t available, work slows or stops. That’s why reliability, audit-ready data, and a workflow that makes sense to analysts, QA, and operations **teams are so critical—and why our customers say they *depend* on Confident, not just “use” it.**


For most labs, Confident is:


- The system where **samples are accessioned and tracked**
- The **source-of-truth for all data and calculations**
- The source of **COAs and regulatory records**
- The platform used to **communicate results to clients** (often via integrated client portals)


Without Confident, labs can’t operate — which is why we prioritize reliability (+99% uptime), cloud availability (work from anywhere), and fast human support (same day responses, <2 day median request-to-completion).

## **Long-Term Relationships: Labs Stay with Confident for Years**


We then asked how long labs have used Confident LIMS. The answer wasn’t surprising, but it did make us proud: **82% of respondents have been on the platform for more than a year, and 40% for over three years.**


*Pie chart showing user tenure with Confident LIMS: 11% less than six months, 7% six to twelve months, 42% one to three years, and 40% more than three years, highlighting that 82% of users have been on the platform for over a year.*


For a LIMS — where switching costs are high, training is intensive, and compliance is on the line — that length of tenure is a strong indicator that labs see Confident as a **long-term operational partner** , not a temporary tool.


This aligns with broader market data: Confident LIMS is the **#1 LIMS** in the legal cannabis industry for **10 years in a row** , trusted by **100+ labs** , **15,000+ testing clients** , and more than **50% of licensed cannabis labs** in North America.

When labs build their daily workflows around Confident, they’re betting on more than software. They’re betting on:

For QA leaders, that means:


- Every result and revision is **traceable**

Confident’s industry-standard Client Portal and Testing Network enables customers to order tests, track their status, and securely access test results, chain of custody forms, and invoices in one place — a key reason labs stay and expand their usage over time.


For lab managers and front office teams, this translates to:
