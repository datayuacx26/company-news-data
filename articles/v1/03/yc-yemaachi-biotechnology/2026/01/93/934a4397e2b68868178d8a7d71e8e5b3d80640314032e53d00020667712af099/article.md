# Yaw Bediako Recognized Among The 100 Most Influential CEOs in Oncology in 2025.

**Yaw Bediako** is an immunologist and the CEO of **Yemaachi Biotech,** an Accra-based immunogenomics company focused on diversifying precision oncology. Under his leadership, Yemaachi has positioned itself as a key contributor to African cancer research by leveraging the continent’s genetic diversity to identify novel biomarkers and therapeutic targets.


A landmark achievement of his tenure is the 2025 launch of the **African Cancer Atlas (TACA)** in partnership with **Roche.** This consortium aims to sequence **15,000 genomes from 7,500 African cancer patients,** creating one of the world’s most comprehensive genomic datasets to address the historic underrepresentation of African populations in global drug discovery and diagnostic development.


Dr. Bediako’s leadership has attracted global support, including a **$25 million Cancer Grand Challenges award** as part of the **SAMBAI consortium** to investigate cancer inequities. He has also been recognized as one of the **100 Most Influential Africans and a Bloomberg New Economy Catalyst,** highlighting his role in advancing precision medicine across the continent.


Through strategic partnerships with organizations such as Bio Usawa, Dr. Bediako is helping bridge the gap between genomic research and the development of locally relevant oncology solutions, ensuring that the future of precision medicine is inclusive and accessible.


The 100 CEOs featured here reflect the diversity and complexity of modern oncology leadership. In 2025, some lead the world’s largest cancer institutions; others guide specialized societies or emerging innovators. What unites them is responsibility: for millions of patients, for the integrity of science, and for the future direction of cancer care globally.
