## How to choose PPC tools


Choose PPC management software based on channels and workflow. If you mainly manage Google Ads and Microsoft Ads, prioritize search advertising management features such as search term analysis, keyword management, PPC audits, PPC bid management software depth, budget pacing, alerts, and reporting.


- If you mainly manage Google Ads and Microsoft Ads: prioritize search term analysis, keyword management, PPC audits, bid management, budget pacing, alerts, and reporting.
- If you manage search alongside Meta, Amazon, LinkedIn, TikTok, or retail media: choose a cross-platform PPC management platform.
- Agencies comparing PPC tools should prioritize account monitoring, client reporting, alerts, audits, automation, and PPC workflow software features.
- Ecommerce teams comparing PPC software should look for Shopping support, product feed workflows, retail media activation, and revenue-based reporting.
- Enterprise teams: check scalability, integrations, permissions, support, security, and pricing.
- Creative-led paid media teams: prioritize creative analysis, fatigue detection, campaign recommendations, and first‑party performance data.
