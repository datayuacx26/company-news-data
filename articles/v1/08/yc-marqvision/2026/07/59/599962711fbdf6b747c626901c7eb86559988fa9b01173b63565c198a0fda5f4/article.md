# Introducing the Marq AI Slack App

Today we're introducing the Marq AI Slack App: file takedowns and pull performance reports directly in Slack, without opening the dashboard. Paste a URL, and a takedown is filed. Marq AI handles the rest — and reports progress in the same thread your team is already reading.


‍


## The cost of the detour


Brand protection is interrupt-driven work. A channel partner forwards a suspicious link. A customer complains about a fake. Leadership asks "why are we seeing so many fakes online?" in a channel, twenty minutes before the meeting.


‍


None of that happens in a dashboard. It happens in Slack, where your team already runs the day.


‍


So acting has always meant a detour: open the platform and file the request there, or message your account manager and wait while someone re-enters it by hand. The threat doesn't get worse because nobody saw it. It gets worse in the gap between seeing it and acting on it.


‍


The Marq AI Slack App closes that gap. Self-serve and two-way: your team acts directly, and the platform answers back — no login, no handoff, no re-entry.


‍


## File a takedown at the speed of a message


### > \`/enforce {URL}\`


Paste a suspicious link, and enforcement starts. A confirmation step verifies the brand and channel, the request is created in your Marq AI dashboard, and the Slack bot posts progress in the thread as the takedown moves across domains, web content, social media, and marketplaces.


##
Your brand's numbers now gets reported where the questions get asked


### > \`/summary\`


A daily or weekly digest of your brand protection performance, delivered to the Slack channel you choose: activity across every enforcement channel (e.g., scanned, flagged, reported, removed), current takedown status, and an AI summary that flags the anomalies worth your attention.


‍


## The first surface of something bigger


The Slack App is the first product built on our Action-Enabled API. It's the layer that makes every action in the MarqVision platform run by agents. Anything your team can do in the dashboard is becoming something they can do from anywhere.


‍


Slack is the first surface and it won't be the last! And the two commands are just the start: next comes plain language — type what you want done, and the agents handle the rest.


‍


## Getting started


We've rolled out to a first group of customers, who are working with us to shape how brand protection can be run truly agentic, inside Slack — from the commands themselves to how summaries and anomalies get surfaced and shared across the team. The Marq AI Slack App rolls out to all customers over the coming weeks.
