# Plug to Participate in Roth London Conference

Plug Power will participate in the Roth London Conference runing from June 16–18, 2026, in London, United Kingdom. Jose-Luis Crespo, Chief Executive Officer, and Benjamin Haycraft, Chief Strategy Officer, will meet with institutional investors to discuss Plug’s strategic direction, global growth opportunities, and progress executing on the Company’s long-term profitability and hydrogen infrastructure objectives.


As demand for large-scale energy resilience and industrial decarbonization solutions continues to grow globally, the conference provides an opportunity for Plug to highlight its expanding international presence, integrated hydrogen ecosystem, and leadership across electrolyzers, hydrogen production, storage, delivery, and fuel cell technologies.


**Event Details**


- Dates: June 16–18, 2026


- Event: Roth London Conference


- Location: London, United Kingdom


- Participants: Jose-Luis Crespo, Chief Executive Officer; Benjamin Haycraft, Chief Strategy Officer


Additional information on Plug’s investor conference participation can be found in the Investor Resources section of the Company’s website:[HERE](https://www.ir.plugpower.com/events-and-presentations/default.aspx)
