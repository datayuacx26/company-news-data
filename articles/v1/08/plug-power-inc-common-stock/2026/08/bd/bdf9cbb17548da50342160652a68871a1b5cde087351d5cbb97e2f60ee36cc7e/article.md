# Plug Power to Meet with Investors at the Canaccord Growth Conference

Plug Power will participate in the Canaccord Growth Conference on August 12, 2026, in Boston, Massachusetts. Jose-Luis Crespo, Chief Executive Officer, and Roberto Friedlander, Vice President of Investor Relations, will meet with institutional investors to discuss Plug’s strategic priorities, commercial execution, capital allocation strategy, and progress advancing the Company’s long-term growth and profitability objectives.


As industries continue to invest in resilient energy infrastructure and low-carbon technologies, the conference provides an opportunity for Plug to share updates on its integrated hydrogen ecosystem, expanding commercial deployments, and disciplined approach to scaling the business while strengthening financial performance.


### **Event Details**


- Date: August 12, 2026


- Event: Canaccord Growth Conference


- Location: Boston, Massachusetts


- Participants: Jose-Luis Crespo, Chief Executive Officer; Roberto Friedlander, Vice President of Investor Relations


Additional information on Plug’s investor conference participation can be found in the Investor Resources section of the Company’s website:[HERE](https://www.ir.plugpower.com/events-and-presentations/default.aspx)
