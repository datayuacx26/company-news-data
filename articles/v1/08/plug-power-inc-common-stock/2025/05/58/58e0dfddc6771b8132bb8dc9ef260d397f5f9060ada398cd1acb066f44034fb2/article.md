## **Why Electrolyzers—and Why PEM Wins**


Electrolyzers use electricity to split water into hydrogen and oxygen—no fossil fuels required. The cleaner the electricity, the greener the hydrogen. For industrial users with access to low-cost, renewable power, on-site electrolyzers are the most efficient and resilient way to secure hydrogen supply long-term.


Among available technologies, PEM electrolyzers like GenEco offer unmatched performance for intermittent energy inputs, compact footprint, and rapid response times. Compared to alkaline systems, PEM units produce higher purity hydrogen, require less maintenance, and perform better under fluctuating power—making them a smarter, lower-risk investment for decarbonization.

The world of electrolyzer innovation is evolving fast. Here are the key trends—and how GenEco is ahead of the curve:
