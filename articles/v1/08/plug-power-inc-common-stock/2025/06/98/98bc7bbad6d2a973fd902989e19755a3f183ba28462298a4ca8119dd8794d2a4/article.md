# A Message to Shareholders: Help Plug Power Take the Right Path Forward

On May 30, 2025, Plug Power Inc. (the “Company”) filed its preliminary proxy statement, which includes two important proposals that it is asking stockholders to support. Proposal 2 seeks approval to increase the number of our authorized shares. Proposal 3 seeks approval to permit our Board to implement a reverse stock split, but only if needed. If our stockholders do not approve Proposal 2, then we may not have the additional shares that may be needed to support the Company’s business. Shares are critical to a company’s ability to operate—whether it’s raising capital, supporting partnerships, issuing equity to employees, or pursuing strategic initiatives. Without an increase in the number of authorized shares of common stock, we may be constrained in our ability to address ongoing needs and to pursue opportunities that support long-term growth. This could adversely affect our financial performance and limit our flexibility moving forward.


We strongly encourage our stockholders to vote in favor of both Proposal 2 and Proposal 3. Increasing our authorized shares through Proposal 2 is the most straightforward way to ensure the Company has the tools it needs to execute on its strategy. If Proposal 2 is approved, we will not implement a reverse stock split to increase the number of our authorized shares. Proposal 3 gives the Board the optional authority to implement a reverse split—but only if necessary and only if Proposal 2 is not approved.


It’s also important to understand the differences in voting thresholds between the two proposals. Proposal 2 requires approval by a majority of all outstanding shares entitled to vote. Proposal 3, on the other hand, may be approved if, assuming we achieve a quorum and we use our “blank check” authority to participate in the vote, a majority of such shares present or represented by proxy at the annual meeting vote in favor of the proposal. That means Proposal 3 could still pass even if Proposal 2 fails. That’s why it is important to vote on—and **vote for** —both proposals.


**One of the biggest challenges we face is that roughly 40% of Plug Power’s shares are currently out on loan, which can negatively impact both voting and stock performance. If your shares are on loan, please consider recalling them by June 9, the record date, so you can vote your shares at the upcoming annual meeting.**


***It is imperative that you vote your shares, no matter how many shares you own.***
