# Fuel Cells vs. Batteries: Powering the Future of Warehousing

Warehouses are the unsung heroes of modern commerce. From keeping store shelves stocked to ensuring your latest online order arrives on time, they’re the engine behind the global supply chain. But even the most reliable engines need an upgrade, and that upgrade is already underway.


Warehousing is evolving. The next big decision for operations leaders? Choosing how to power their fleets. And increasingly, the answer isn’t batteries, it’s hydrogen fuel cells.


At Plug, we’re seeing more businesses make the shift to hydrogen fuel cells for material handling. Why? Because the performance difference is real, and the operational advantages are impossible to ignore.


## **Why Fuel Cells for Material Handling Make Sense**


### **Refuel in Minutes, Not Hours**


Plug’s GenDrive hydrogen fuel cells slash downtime with refueling that takes under 3 minutes. No more waiting on battery charging or coordinating complex battery swaps. Operators stay on the floor, and your facility keeps moving.


### **Consistent Power from Start to Finish**


Batteries fade over time. Fuel cells don’t. Plug’s systems deliver steady, full-shift power that keeps productivity high and worker frustration low. That’s a win for throughput and for morale.


### **Reclaim Valuable Warehouse Space**


Battery charging areas take up valuable square footage. With fuel cells, that space can be repurposed for what truly matters: moving goods, not parking and charging. It’s a smarter footprint for high-efficiency operations.


### **Who’s Already Made the Switch?**


Plug is powering some of the world’s largest and most recognized brands, including **Walmart** , **Amazon** , and **Home Depot** . These companies aren’t just early adopters; they’re leaders who understand the strategic edge that comes from embracing clean, scalable, and high-performance energy solutions.


With over 72,000 fuel cells deployed and a growing hydrogen infrastructure across North America and Europe, Plug isn’t just keeping up; we’re leading the way.


### **The Time for Hydrogen Is Now**


Fuel cells for material handling aren’t a “someday” idea. They’re proven, operational, and scaling today. From reduced emissions to improved fleet uptime and lower total cost of ownership, Plug’s hydrogen solutions are helping warehouses meet today’s demands while preparing for tomorrow’s opportunities.
