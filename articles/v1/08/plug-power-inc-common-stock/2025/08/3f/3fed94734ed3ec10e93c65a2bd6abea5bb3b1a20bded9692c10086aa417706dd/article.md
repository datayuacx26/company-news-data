# H2@Work Employee Spotlight: Don Boyajian

Don brings a precise lawyering approach to Plug’s government affairs work. He navigates legislative and regulatory matters at every level of government. His legal expertise and policy insight ensure that Plug’s voice is heard in key debates.


## From Intern to Leader


Don first joined Plug as a college intern in 2004. Seventeen years later, he returned, driven by his admiration for Plug’s vision in the clean energy economy. His journey reflects both his passion for hydrogen technology and his desire to make a meaningful impact.


## People and Purpose


What kept Don at Plug is the team and the mission. He values the dedication of his colleagues and believes hydrogen will play a vital role in decarbonizing hard-to-abate sectors. Troubleshooting policy challenges fuels his work each day.


## Shaping Landmark Legislation


Don has helped secure major policy wins, including the Inflation Reduction Act and the One Big Beautiful Bill. His prior experience working for the New York State legislature gives him a unique perspective on both sides of the policy process.


## Championing Hydrogen’s Niche


Hydrogen appeals across political lines but risks slipping through policy cracks. Don and his team work tirelessly to keep hydrogen issues front and center. Their clear messaging and proactive outreach ensure that hydrogen stays on the agenda.


## Living with Purpose and Positivity


Don lives by purpose, passion, and authenticity. He strives to spread empathy and positivity in every interaction. That philosophy guides both his professional work and his personal life.


## Adventure and Music


When he’s not shaping policy, Don heads to the mountains of Utah to ski or hike. He also plays guitar in a bluegrass or country band. Music and the outdoors keep him energized and balanced.
