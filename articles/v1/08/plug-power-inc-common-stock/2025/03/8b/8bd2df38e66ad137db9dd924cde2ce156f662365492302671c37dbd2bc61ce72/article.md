# Southwire Chooses Plug Power to Advance Supply Chain Sustainability

## A Smarter, More Sustainable Alternative for Material Handling


For years, Plug has helped some of the biggest names in logistics, retail, and e-commerce eliminate downtime and maximize efficiency with hydrogen-powered solutions. Now, industrial suppliers are following suit.


Southwire, a leading wire and cable manufacturer, is embracing hydrogen to drive sustainability and performance at its new Dallas-Fort Worth distribution center. By integrating Plug’s fuel cells, fueling infrastructure, and hydrogen supply, Southwire is reducing emissions and streamlining operations.


## Why Southwire Chose Plug


As part of its commitment to a cleaner supply chain, Southwire is deploying more than 50 hydrogen-powered forklifts along with a Plug-designed fueling station. This shift is expected to cut over one million pounds of CO2 emissions annually.


By partnering with Plug, Southwire benefits from:


- A reliable hydrogen supply sourced from Plug’s growing U.S. production network.
- Custom fueling infrastructure designed for fleet efficiency.
- Proven hydrogen fuel cells that keep equipment running nonstop.
- Ongoing service and support for long-term reliability.


## Plug’s Hydrogen Ecosystem in Action


Plug is leading the way in hydrogen adoption, delivering an end-to-end ecosystem that includes production, storage, delivery, and power generation.


- Over 72,000 fuel cells deployed in material handling and industrial applications.
- More than 275 hydrogen fueling stations across North America.
- 39 tons of daily hydrogen production from facilities in Georgia, Tennessee, and Louisiana.
- The largest user of liquid hydrogen globally.
- Trusted by industry leaders including Walmart, Amazon, Home Depot, and BMW.

Investment in hydrogen technology reflects a larger shift toward cleaner, high-performance energy solutions.
