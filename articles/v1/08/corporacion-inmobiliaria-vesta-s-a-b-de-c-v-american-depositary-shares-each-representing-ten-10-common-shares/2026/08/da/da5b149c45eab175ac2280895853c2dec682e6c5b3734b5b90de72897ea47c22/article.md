# Vesta to Host 2026 Investor Day on November 11, 2026

# Vesta to Host 2026 Investor Day on November 11, 2026


August 12, 2026 9:00 AM EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_9e8fd18cc64d266596fca78e407bdcea/vesta/news/2026-08-12_Vesta_to_Host_2026_Investor_Day_on_November_11_152.pdf)


MEXICO CITY--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Corporación Inmobiliaria Vesta, S.A.B. de C.V. (NYSE: VTMX; BMV: VESTA) ("Vesta" or the "Company"), a leading industrial real estate company in Mexico, today announced that it will host its 2026 Investor Day on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, in New York, NY.


At the event, Vesta's management team will discuss the Company's business performance, capital allocation priorities, and long-term growth outlook with important updates related to the Company's Route 2030 strategic plan, including recent development projects across key markets and other initiatives driving long-term value creation.


On-site registration and networking breakfast with management will begin at 8:00 a.m. ET, followed by management presentations at 9:00 a.m. ET and a live Q&A session with members of the management team.


The event is open to institutional investors and sell-side analysts and will be available both in person and via live webcast. Pre-registration is required by November 3, 2026.


To register, please visit[vestaday.com](http://vestaday.com/) . Due to limited capacity, in-person attendance is subject to confirmation, and confirmed attendees will receive additional event details. A live webcast, replay and presentation materials will be available through Vesta's Investor Relations website at[ir.vesta.com.mx](http://ir.vesta.com.mx/) following the event.


For further information, please contact InspIR Group atinspir@inspirgroup.com .


**About Vesta** Vesta is a leading real estate owner, developer and asset manager of industrial buildings and distribution centers in Mexico. As of June 30, 2026, Vesta owned 232 properties located in modern industrial parks across 16 states in Mexico, totaling 43.3 million sf (4.0 million m2) of gross leasable area (GLA). Vesta serves a diversified base of world-class clients across a range of industries, including automotive, aerospace, retail, high-tech, pharmaceuticals, electronics, food and beverage and packaging. For additional information, please visit:[www.vesta.com.mx](http://www.vesta.com.mx/) .
