**About Vesta**


Vesta is a leading real estate owner, developer and asset manager of industrial buildings and distribution centers in Mexico. As of March 31, 2026, Vesta owned 231 properties located in modern industrial parks across 16 states in Mexico, totaling 43.0 million sf (4.0 million m2) of gross leasable area (GLA). Vesta serves a diversified base of world-class clients across a range of industries, including automotive, aerospace, retail, high-tech, pharmaceuticals, electronics, food and beverage and packaging.
