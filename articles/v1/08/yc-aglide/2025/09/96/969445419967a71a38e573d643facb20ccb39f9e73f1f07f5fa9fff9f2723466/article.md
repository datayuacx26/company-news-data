# Aglide is SOC 2 Compliant

Aglide has achieved SOC 2 compliance.


When you're trusting a vendor with identity infrastructure, compliance is a reasonable baseline to expect, and we're glad to meet it.


That said, we don't treat SOC 2 as the definition of good security. It's one signal among many. A clean audit report reflects good process, but security is broader than that.


Security is a first-order consideration in everything we build. When we design a new integration or ship a new feature, the questions we ask from the start are about access patterns, credential handling, and data exposure. Our architecture is built on zero trust principles with end-to-end encryption throughout.


Learn more about our architecture on our[security page](https://aglide.com/security?ref=blog.aglide.com) , or explore our compliance programme on our[trust centre](https://trust.aglide.com/?ref=blog.aglide.com) .
