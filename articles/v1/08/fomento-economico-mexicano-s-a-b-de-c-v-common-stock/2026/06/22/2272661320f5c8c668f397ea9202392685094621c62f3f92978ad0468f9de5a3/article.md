# Suppliers Guiding Principles

The Suppliers Guiding Principles shall be complied by all those who seek to be FEMSA’s suppliers and current FEMSA’s suppliers participating in the different operations and supply chains of FEMSA. The term Supplier includes individuals and legal entities that provide goods, render services, are distributors, agents, intermediaries, representatives, as well as any third party acting on behalf of and/or representing FEMSA.
