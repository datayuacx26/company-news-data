# Nu Skin Enterprises Announces Quarterly Dividend

[PDF Version](https://ir.nuskin.com/node/24386/pdf)


PROVO, Utah--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 10, 2026-- Nu Skin Enterprises, Inc. (NYSE: NUS) today announced its board of directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.06 per share, which will be paid on Sept. 9, 2026, to shareholders of record on Aug. 28, 2026.
