# Setting Up and Using a Cash Drawer with Yezza Clinic System

Welcome! This guide will walk you through the steps to set up and use a cash drawer with Yezza Clinic System. If you don’t have a cash drawer yet, you can purchase one \[[here](https://s.shopee.com.my/4AfyXE8Zti) \]. (Note: Need to purchase together with USB Trigger)


## **Step 1: One-Time Configuration**


Before you begin, make sure both your **MyKad reader** and **Cash Drawer** are properly connected to your PC.


#### 1. Download and Install the Required Software


- Head to the PC that is **connected** to the Cash Drawer and download the necessary software: \[[Click to download](https://cdn.yezza.io/desktop-client/yezza-hardware-setup-v240822.exe) \].
- Open the downloaded file and install it on your PC.


#### 2. Configure Your Yezza Admin Account


- Log in to your **Yezza admin account** .
- Navigate to the **Cash Drawer** section.
- Set the **Trigger Code** to **0 (zero)** .


#### 3. Set the Correct COM Port


- Identify which **COM Port** is assigned to your Cash Drawer.
- Test each available **COM Port** by selecting one and clicking **“Open”** .
- If the correct **COM Port** is selected, your **Cash Drawer will open automatically** .
