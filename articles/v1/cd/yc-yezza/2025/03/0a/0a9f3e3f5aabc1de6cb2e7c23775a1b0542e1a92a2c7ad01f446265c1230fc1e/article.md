# How to Access the Malaysian MyInvois Portal and Set Up Yezza as an Intermediary

If you need to access **MyInvois**


via **MyTax**


and configure your profile to include Yezza as your intermediary, follow these steps:


---


**Step 1: Log In to MyTax** ****


1. Visit the **MyTax portal**


:[https://mytax.hasil.gov.my](https://mytax.hasil.gov.my/) .
2. Click **“Login”**


and enter your credentials.
3. Once logged in, navigate to the **MyInvois portal**


from the dashboard.


---


**Step 2: Switch to Your Company Tax Profile** ****


1. After logging in, click on your **profile name**


(usually in the top-right corner).
2. Select **“Switch Taxpayer”**


from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose **your company’s tax profile**


to ensure you are managing the correct business account.


---


**Step 3: Edit Your Profile to Add Yezza as an Intermediary** ****


1. From the


**MyInvois Home** page , go to


**“Manage your Profile”** .


2. Navigate to **“Taxpayer Profile”**


under **“Profile”**


to update your business details.
3. Locate the **“Representatives”**


section to


**Add Intermediary** .


4. Add **Yezza**


as your intermediary by entering the following details:


1. **TIN:**


C26519995060
2. **BRN:**


202001010294
3. **Name:** YEZZA TECH SDN. BHD.


5. Set your **“Representation From”**


date to today, and leave **“Representation To”**


date **empty** to prevent expiry date error (you can always remove Yezza as your intermediary in the future).
6. Click **“Save”**


to confirm the changes.


---


**Step 4: Verify Your Settings** ****


1. Navigate back to the **dashboard**


to ensure the intermediary details are reflected correctly.
2. If everything is in order, you are now set up to process **e-invoicing through Yezza.**


---


**Need Help?** ****


If you encounter any issues during the setup, you can contact **MyTax customer support**


or reach out to **Yezza’s support team**


via whatsApp for further assistance.


By following these steps, you’ll ensure that your **e-invoicing process is correctly linked to Yezza**


, making your tax submissions smoother and compliant with **LHDN requirements**


.
