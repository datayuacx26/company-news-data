# E-Invoicing Made Easy with Yezza Clinic Systems

With Malaysia’s push towards digitalization, **e-invoicing**


is now essential for clinics to ensure seamless transactions and compliance with regulations. If you’re using **Yezza Clinic Systems**


, you can generate and manage e-invoices effortlessly—without complicated setups or lengthy training. This FAQ will help you understand what e-invoicing is and how it works within the system.


---


### **What is E-Invoice?** ****


An **E-Invoice**


is a digital version of a traditional invoice that is **electronically generated, submitted, and stored**


. It ensures accurate, seamless, and tax-compliant transactions while reducing paperwork.


---


### **Why E-Invoicing?** ****


E-invoicing eliminates the hassle of manual paperwork, improves accuracy, and ensures a more professional approach to billing. It also helps clinics comply with regulatory requirements while streamlining financial management.


---


### **How to Set Up E-Invoicing in Yezza Clinic Systems**


You don’t need any additional setup—just ensure you select the correct **ID type**


(either IC or Passport) during registration and submission, and Yezza will handle the rest!


If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to reach out to our support team.


---


### **How does E-Invoicing work in Yezza?**


Yezza’s e-invoicing process is fully integrated into your clinic’s workflow:


#### **1. ID-Based Submission**


- E-Invoices in Yezza require **an identification number (IC or Passport)**


for submission.
- During **registration and submission**


, users must **select the ID type**


for validation before proceeding.


#### **2. Invoice Generated at Checkout** ****


- E-Invoicing happens **automatically at checkout**


when patients make payments at the clinic.
- The system records the transaction and submits the invoice digitally.


#### **3. Making Amendments or Cancellations** ****


- Users can **delete**


an e-invoice via **MyInvois Portal**


within **72 hours (3 days)**


after submission.
- After **3 days**


, any changes must be processed using a **debit or credit note via MyInvois Portal**


.


---


### **Can I make corrections to an E-Invoice after submission?**


Yes, but it depends on the timing:


✔


**Within 72 Hours (3 Days)**


- **** You can **delete**


an e-invoice directly via **MyInvois Portal**


.


✔


**After 72 Hours (3 Days)**


- **** Changes must be made through a


**debit note (to increase charges)** or


**credit note (to reduce charges)** via **MyInvois Portal.**
