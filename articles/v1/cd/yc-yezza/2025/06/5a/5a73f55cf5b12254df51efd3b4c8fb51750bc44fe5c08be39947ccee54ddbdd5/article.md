# Yezza Data Migration Guide: Process, Timeline, and What to Expect

# Migration Scope


## What is Yezza Migration?


-


Yezza Migration is the process of transferring clinic data from your existing system to the Yezza platform.


-


Typically includes patient records, services, inventory, and visit history.


-


Performed in 3 stages: **Extraction** , **Import** , **Verification** .


-


Customised per system—guided in WhatsApp support group.


## Before You Begin


- You will be required to provide your **login credentials** if you’re using **Cmagsys Cloud** , or Yezza will assist in extracting the latest **backup file** if you’re using **Cmagsys Server** .
-


Migration is available to clinics with an active Yezza account.


-


Our team manages most of the process, but your review is essential.


-


Communication is handled through your dedicated Support WhatsApp group.


## Migration Timeline


Stage Timeline


Data Extraction Within 3 business days after account creation


Data Import 5–7 business days after extraction


Data Verification Right after import is complete


Data Re-Migration Once your clinic goes live on Yezza

## What Data Can Be Migrated to Yezza?


### Included Data Types


-


**Medications:** Name, medication details, stock quantity, selling price, stock cost.


-


**Procedures, Investigations, Services:** Name, selling price.


-


**Patient Information:** Name, ID, phone number, address


- **Patient Medical Records:** Visit date, case notes, diagnosis, medications and treatments


###


### Limitations of Migration


-


System structure incompatibilities


-


Data format issues


-


Platform-specific limitations


🚫 Data not currently supported for migration:


- **Medical files/documents:** Medical certificate, Referral letters, X-ray file, blood test file, ultrasound files.
- **Outstanding invoices:** Yezza will display all invoices regardless the status.
- **Payment details:** Either Cash/Card/Panel


💡 *Yezza is continuously improving its migration script to support more formats in future updates.*


## Recommended Backup Strategy


-


Always keep a **backup of your latest data** before starting migration.


-


This ensures you have access to the full dataset in case of any migration issues.


## After Migration: What’s Next?


-


Verify your data with guidance from our team.


-


Start using Yezza for daily clinic operations.


-


Reach out if any data needs rechecking—we’re here to help!
