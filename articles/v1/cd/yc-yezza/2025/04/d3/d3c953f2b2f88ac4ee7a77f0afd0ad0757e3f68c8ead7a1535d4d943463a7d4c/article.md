# Ultimate e-Invoice FAQ for Clinics in Malaysia (2024-2025)

---


## **1. What is e-Invoice?** ****


- A digital invoice submitted to LHDN for validation.
- Includes a Unique Identification Number (UIN) and QR code.
- Ensures tax transparency and compliance.


🔗[LHDN Reference](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/e-invoice)


---


## **2.** **When will e-Invoice be fully implemented?**


- **1 August 2024**


: For turnover > RM100 million.
- **1 January 2025**


: For turnover RM25M–RM100M.
- **1 July 2025**


: Mandatory for all businesses.


🔗[LHDN Timeline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/e-invoice/implementation-of-e-invoicing-in-malaysia/e-invoice-implementation-timeline)


---


## **3.** **When do clinics need to implement e-Invoice?**


-


By **1 July 2025**


, or earlier if turnover > RM25 million.


---


## **4.** **What if clinics don’t implement e-Invoice?**


- Risk of LHDN penalties.
- Difficulty in tax audits and filings.
- Legal non-compliance.


🔗[PwC e-Invoicing](https://www.pwc.com/my/en/services/tax/e-invoicing.html)

## **6.** **Can I submit e-Invoices without a business registration number?**


-


Yes. Individuals can submit using their **IC Number**


.

## **11.** **My patient claims via Company or TPA. Who gets the e-Invoice?**


- Only the **patient**


is considered the buyer (even if company/TPA pays).
- e-Invoice must be issued to the **patient**


, not the company/TPA.


🔗[LHDN Healthcare FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/media/atse5ojz/lhdnm_healthcare_faqs.pdf)
