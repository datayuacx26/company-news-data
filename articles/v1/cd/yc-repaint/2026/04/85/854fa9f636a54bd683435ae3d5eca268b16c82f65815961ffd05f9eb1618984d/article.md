# Introducing Repaint Website Styles

Repaint has a new tab on the left, which shows a gallery of website styles. Each one has unique layouts, typography, colors, and personality. You can pick one, add it to your chat, and have Repaint use it to build your site.


We added styles because Repaint was hit-or-miss. Some sites looked great, but a lot came out generic or broken. Most people can't describe exactly what they want in words, so it's hard to fix a site you don't like. But everyone can find something they like in a gallery, so it's a much better way to get started.


Repaint uses styles for visual direction, and customizes the content to fit your business. So a style for a marketing agency could easily be adapted to a law firm, financial advisor, or architecture studio.


We think styles are a huge improvement to the average Repaint site. You can try them live in[Repaint](https://repaint.com/) .
