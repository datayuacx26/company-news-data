# Smarter Website Generation in Repaint

Today, we released a major update to how Repaint generates websites. It can follow more specific instructions, and it's more flexible when redesigning sites.


**Major improvements:**


-


It can rebuild a website in its original style.


-


It's better at using other sites as references.


-


It can generate custom styles, outside the premade gallery.


-


It uses your brand colors, fonts, and images more consistently.


-


It imports large websites gradually, so your usage isn't spent all at once.


Repaint was originally built to fully redesign sites in a new style. But many people wanted to do something more specific: *clone this site, keep that section, use this reference, match my brand,* *make something custom...*


We realized redesigning a website is rarely all-or-nothing. Sometimes you want to preserve the original site, sometimes you want a full redesign, and often you want something in between. With this update, Repaint is much better at handling all of it.


You can try the new system live in[Repaint](https://repaint.com/) today.
