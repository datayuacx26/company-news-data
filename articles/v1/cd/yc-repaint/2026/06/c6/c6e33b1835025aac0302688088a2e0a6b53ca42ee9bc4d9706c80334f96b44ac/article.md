# Expanded Editing Limits for Everyone

We just released an update that makes Repaint much more efficient at making edits with AI. As a result, you can make roughly two to three times as many edits before reaching your usage limit.


We made this update to make Repaint better for large websites and heavy editing. Before, many people needed to buy extra usage credits to finish their website. Also, if you had Repaint rebuild a website with a dozen pages or more, you'd use nearly the entire free tier just to make the first version. These issues should be much less common now.


We achieved this by adjusting how our AI agent works and mixing in cheaper AI models. With these efficiency gains, we're able to raise editing limits for everyone. It applies to free tier limits, Plus limits, and extra usage credits. If you already purchased credits, they'll automatically go two to three times as far.


The chat now runs on new AI models, so you may feel a slight shift in conversation style. We did extensive testing first, and from what we can tell the new agent is just as capable as the old one. If you run into any issues, reach out to support and we'll look into it.


Expanded limits are live for everyone today in[Repaint](https://repaint.com/) .
