# Importing Social Content into Repaint

We just released an update that lets Repaint use content from external profiles like Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business.


We made this update to make Repaint better at using existing digital content. It was already great at using existing websites, but it couldn't read other platforms without getting blocked. That was inconvenient if your business lives on social media, because the only way to use your existing info was to re-upload everything to Repaint.


With this update, Repaint can read your profiles directly. All you have to do is share a link. Here are some examples of what Repaint can read:


-


**Instagram** — profile photo, bio, posts, comments.


-


**Facebook** — page info, contact details, posts, images.


-


**Google Business** — hours, address, reviews, photos.


It should also have better access to other platforms, like YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Yelp.


Once imported, Repaint can use the content on your website, based on your instructions. You can pull your reviews onto your homepage, turn your posts into a gallery, or add your hours and address to your footer.


This update is live today. You can use it on an existing website in[Repaint](https://repaint.com/) , or[create a new website directly from your profile](https://repaint.com/social-import) .
