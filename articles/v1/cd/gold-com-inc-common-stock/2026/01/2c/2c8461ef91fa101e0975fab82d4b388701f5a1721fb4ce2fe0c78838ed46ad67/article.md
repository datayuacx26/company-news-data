**About Gold.com, Inc.**
Gold.com builds on gold’s storied history and heritage to define the future of alternative asset management. Founded in 1965, Gold.com offers a comprehensive solution for all aspects of the precious metals and collectibles value chain. Its vertically integrated platform combines market expertise in gold, silver, platinum, and palladium and collectibles that include rare coins and currency with state-of-the-art logistics, financing, and minting capabilities to serve consumers, collectors, and institutional clients globally.


Gold.com’s direct-to-consumer marketplace, anchored by flagship brands[JMBullion.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=V4pYF826XqNbwCItYDONwEMKqu5ol8ICi-5sLK9CvBbdI5X_wc5qbf0oeZ_TWflKf_NVSmg6R65w4d_72dNfMwp-DGP8mp-rv_ALNRsJw4Q=) ,[Stack’s Bowers Galleries](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=RdvyXIW6LDeRMi5Q9X8LHjIDoci-mEBdHieib9FMqtCOSGLyU7ROFOTUFVziPUrd5QbWnwotWVFY5tcQ2LEEmxsafBnQHi5z53lJGuFAvQ8=) ,[GovMint.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=QctS1XAulwXzpAnR9ZM0b_OXVsrMlrXdaT-ewXwcjCQz7ocvQAAEk3IR_EYj-ETOSaEawS0BMOhkpt1DI3aViA==) , and[Goldline](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Uc8ZEHfcvSmMzzA7gdLttEw9A6ymfAGWvlLC1Ki-0io_EbgnkkgN2Tr5Yc7igDBFEl3dmpXG_j7Dx7VAPmEvjg==) , has served millions of customers. The Company’s trading and wholesale sales platform, which operates under A-Mark Precious Metals, maintains distribution and finance focused relationships with a network of sovereign and private mints and has been an “authorized purchaser” of the United States Mint since 1986. Gold.com’s Collateral Finance Corporation secured lending subsidiary,[CFCGoldLoans.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=1gurt0xatMWCzIcL1HQ6yAuSvEfMKG29XmsvcTJWlIWW4-JORyGcagH_RP8BRHvHvIhQ49PAvZQYILtYxoD48pfEniTQLhtGW8kF3quRzFl-E3R_ZTAmJayJMniUUP6TeKX7hE55L5n8yreuW40A4ouF9bdOEGdG_j5RMBromMvaQsbsp3MTF9EMKeXohilzfEyYilHDl8L9eyuhxhWynbx9QnbYve-mYb9j1aCWHcu6aVl_3zHwPWOjiIM0gimY22lJ-5IelyKiGk-0Xs-mhM8P0teCiGAzSHgzVPH1R0Ec_IppKMwbEKo4bS25Krfb24It5UFrqUHp52H_HtXhQw==) , extends bullion, numismatic, and sports card loans while A-Mark Global Logistics supports the Company’s operations with airport-adjacent distribution centers and IRA-approved storage depositories.
