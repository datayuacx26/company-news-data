# Herman O. West Foundation Scholarship Awarded to Twenty-One Students Across the Country


**West employees’ students received $5,000 each year over four years, totaling $20,000 for higher education. While the selected student for the Chairman’s scholarship received $10,000 a year, totaling $40,000 over the four years.**


West is committed to their culture of giving back through their initiatives, and one way we implement that is through supporting our employees through the Herman O. West Foundation. The Herman. O. West Foundation has been awarding U.S. and Puerto Rico West employees who have a dependent that is pursuing a post-secondary education.


This year, students from Arizona, Florida, Pennsylvania, Nebraska, North Carolina and Michigan were picked through an independent Scholarship Selection Committee based on the following criteria: character, maturity, leadership, extracurricular activities, motivation, interest and desire, predicted success in college, and academic achievement.

Established in 1972, the Herman O. West Foundation honors Herman O. West, one of the founders of West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc. One of the scholarships awarded, the William S. West scholarship, is made possible through a gift to the Foundation from Mr. West’s family in memory of West’s former Chairman & CEO. This year’s chairman’s scholarship was awarded to Matthew Sweeney from Exton, Pennsylvania, son of Matthew Sweeney. In addition to providing scholarships to the children of team members, the Herman O. West Foundation supports team members through matching gifts and emergency relief programs. Donations from U.S. full-time employees to organizations that hold 501(c)(3) designation are matched by the Foundation up to $1,500 per employee. The Employee Emergency Relief Fund was launched to support employees during times of unexpected hardship. It is funded by employees, for employees and matched by the Herman O. West Foundation.


West is proudly committed to giving back to our team members to make a difference in them and their families lives. We appreciate all the hard work from our One West Team and are delighted to help our team members and their families as they enter the new chapter of their lives.
