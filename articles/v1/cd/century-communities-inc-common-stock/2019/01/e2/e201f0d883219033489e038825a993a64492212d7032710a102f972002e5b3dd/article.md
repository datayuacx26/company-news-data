# Century Communities announces new paired homes in Parker and celebrates model grand opening Jan. 19

DENVER


, Jan. 16, 2019


/PRNewswire/ --[Century Communities](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=2349197-1&h=1197003360&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.centurycommunities.com%2F&a=Century+Communities) , a leading national home builder, announced today that new paired model homes will open to the public for viewing at Alder Village in Parker


at 8899 Birch Run Drive. The grand opening is Saturday, Jan. 19


from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.


and includes tours of 2 model homes and a complimentary waffle food truck for refreshments.

Alder Village's new paired homes in Parker offer 4 floorplans ranging from 1,450 - 3,044 sq. ft. with 2 to 4 bedrooms and include 2-bay attached garages. Personalize with an optional gourmet kitchen or unfinished basement.


Conveniently located in northwest Parker


, Alder Village is a new neighborhood developed by Century Communities offering ranch and two-story paired homes starting in the upper $300s. Homes range in size from 1,450 to 3,044 square feet, with 2-4 bedrooms, 2-3.5 baths, and include 2-bay garages. These unique paired homes include covered front porches, open kitchens with island and pantry, great rooms with yard access, owner's suite with 4-piece bath and walk-in closets, laundry rooms and unfinished basements. Homeowners can also personalize one of four floorplans with an optional gourmet kitchen or finished basement.


Colorado's


Urban Division President, Brian Mulqueen


, said, "We are delighted to present this exciting new community in desirable Parker


. Alder Village is ideal for young couples, families, and empty nesters looking to live in a vibrant community with a low maintenance lifestyle."


Alder Village's location is close to a wide array of restaurant and shopping choices, including Park Meadows Mall, and provides a short commute to the Denver Tech Center and easy access to Denver


International Airport. The town of Parker


is rated by CNN Money as "one of the best places to live in the United States


." In the 1800s, Parker


became a junction for stagecoach routes and is located 20 miles southeast of downtown Denver


. Parker


is served by the Douglas County


School District, one of the highest rated school districts in the state. Residents of Alder Village can also take advantage of Parker's


27 miles of trails, 250 acres of developed parkland, 900 acres of open space, and planned activities coming soon to the Rueter-Hess Reservoir for fishing, hiking and non-motorized boating.


At the model grand opening celebration, homebuyers and brokers can visit with a New Home Sales Associate and learn more about the low maintenance lifestyle in this flexible and distinguished community.
