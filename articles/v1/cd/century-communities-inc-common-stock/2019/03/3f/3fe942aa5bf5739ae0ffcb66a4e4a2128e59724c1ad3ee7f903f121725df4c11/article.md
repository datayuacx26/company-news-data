# Century Communities Inc. announces national open house weekend March 23-24

DENVER


, March 22, 2019


/PRNewswire/ --[Century Communities](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=2411890-1&h=349279683&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.centurycommunities.com%2F&a=Century+Communities) Inc., a top 10 national home builder, announced today it is opening all of its showcase and new model homes for viewing during its National Open House Weekend. The nationwide event opens to the public March 23-24


at all participating Century Communities Divisions.


The National Open House Weekend offers interested homebuyers the opportunity to tour Century Communities' existing inventory of showcase quick move-in or new model homes located in 15 states across the West, Mountain, Texas


and Southeast U.S. regions.


"We're excited to host interested homebuyers at our National Open House Weekend. This is an exciting event for Century Communities and allows us the opportunity to feature our quality craftmanship and extra attention to detail on new homes throughout the country. Plus, it's a great time for potential homebuyers to save money on their new dream home with interest rates being at a 12-month low," said Liesel Cooper


, western regional president.


For more information on the National Open House Weekend visit[www.centurycommunities.com](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=2411890-1&h=142046230&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.centurycommunities.com%2F&a=www.centurycommunities.com) .


**About Century Communities:**


Century Communities, Inc. (NYSE:CCS ) is a top 10 national home builder. Century Communities is engaged in all aspects of homebuilding, including the acquisition, entitlement and development of land, along with the construction, innovative marketing and sale of quality homes designed to appeal to a wide range of homebuyers. The Colorado


-based Company sells its Century Communities and Wade Jurney Homes


in 15 states across the West, Mountain, Texas


and Southeast U.S. regions and offers title, insurance, and lending services in select markets through its Parkway Title, IHL Insurance Agency, and Inspire Home Loan subsidiaries.
