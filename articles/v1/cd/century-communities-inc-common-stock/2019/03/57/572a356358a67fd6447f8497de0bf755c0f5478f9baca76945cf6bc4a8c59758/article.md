# Century Communities announces grand opening celebration and ribbon cutting at Westwood Crossing in Poulsbo on Saturday, March 9th

SEATTLE


, March 7, 2019


/PRNewswire/ --[Century Communities](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=2396185-1&h=772530484&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.centurycommunities.com%2F&a=Century+Communities) , a top 10 national home builder, announced today that Westwood Crossing, a new community in Poulsbo


, will host a grand opening celebration on Saturday, March 9


, 2019. Mayor Rebecca Erickson


will begin the festivities with a ribbon cutting at noon, followed by a catered lunch and some special giveaways. The festivities will be held at 21735 Urdahl Road NW in Poulsbo


, and the model home tours will continue throughout the weekend.

Century Communities at Westwood Crossing


Westwood Crossing features 37 new single-family homes starting in the mid $400s with sizes ranging from 2,207 to 3,094 square feet. Interested homebuyers will find three affordable and high-quality open plans which feature gourmet kitchens, bright bedrooms, covered patios and more in this quiet, tree-lined community. In addition, homeowners can choose from 2-3 bedrooms and 2-car garages in rambler-style or two-story homes. As a bonus for families, the community is within walking distance of Vinland elementary school.


"This vibrant new community fits a variety of lifestyles and offers convenience for homebuyers as Westwood Crossing is conveniently located across the Puget Sound from Everett


, Edmonds


, Lynnwood


and Seattle


. Visitors will have a chance to meet Mayor Erickson, join our celebration and take advantage of grand opening offers," said Century Communities' Washington Division President John Merlino


.


Nicknamed "Little Norway," Poulsbo


offers residents the best in Puget Sound living nestled between Liberty Bay


and Squamish Harbor. Homeowners at Westwood Crossing will enjoy Poulsbo's


quaint downtown, thriving arts district, waterfront restaurants, shops, marina and waterfront park. Outdoor enthusiasts will enjoy biking, kayaking, bird watching, fishing, boating, camping and more on and around the peninsula.


Commuters are only 15-minutes away from Seattle


by riding one of the famous Washington State


Ferries; yet are close to Seattle-Tacoma International Airport via a short drive across the Tacoma Narrows Bridge.
