# Century Communities hosts grand opening this Saturday in Tuscany Village

HENDERSON, Nev.


, Nov. 9, 2018


/PRNewswire/ --[Century Communities](http://www.centurycommunities.com/) , recently named the fastest growing public builder by *Builder* magazine, announced today the public is invited to a grand opening celebration for Fiori Townhomes at Tuscany Village


this Saturday from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m.


The festivities will be held at 1056 Via Panfilo Ave in Henderson


.


Fiori Townhomes'


grand opening features a new collection of single and-two-story townhomes in Century Communities' guard-gated golf community of Tuscany Village


. Visitors will be treated to VIP model tours, light refreshments and gift card giveaways.


Continue Reading


Fiori Townhomes, located at the guard-gated golf community of Tuscany Village, feature single and two-story townhomes up to four bedrooms with courtyards, patios, attached two-car garages and golf course views.


"We are excited to add this new collection of classic and elegant townhomes into our master-planned community of Tuscany Village


. This community is set near the golf course with views of the mountains. It's a great location for homebuyers seeking resort-style living with luxury amenities at affordable prices," said Robb Beville


, Nevada


division president for Century Communities.


Fiori's new townhomes start in the low $200s and will appeal to a wide array of homebuyers. The homes come in three flexible and welcoming floor plans ranging in size from 1,563 to 1,931 square feet. They also include 2-car attached garages, 2-4 bedrooms and 2-3 bathrooms. Homebuyers will also enjoy state-of-the-art stainless-steel appliances and granite countertops, and views of the par 72 championship Chimera Golf Club.


Residents at Fiori Townhomes will have access to La Vita Tuscana, a 35,000-square-foot recreation center which includes a clubhouse, lagoon pool, lap pool, fitness center, aerobic and billiards room, plus racquetball, basketball and tennis courts.


Developed by Century Communities, Tuscany Village


is located in Henderson


, a 20-minute drive from McCarran Airport and the Las Vegas Strip, 10 minutes from Lake Mead National Park and within a short drive of Red Rock Canyon.


Homebuyers interested in learning more about the model grand opening savings at Fiori Townhomes in Tuscany Village


can visit Century Communities' website at[https://www.centurycommunities.com/find-your-home/nevada/las-vegas-metro/henderson/fiori-townhomes](https://www.centurycommunities.com/find-your-home/nevada/las-vegas-metro/henderson/fiori-townhomes)
