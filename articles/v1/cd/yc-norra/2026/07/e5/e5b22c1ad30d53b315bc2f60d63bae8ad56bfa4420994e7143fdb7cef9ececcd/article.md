# Norra vs AssetTiger for Nursing Home Equipment

For a skilled nursing facility, Norra is the SNF-native pick and AssetTiger is the better free catalog. AssetTiger is genuinely free for up to 250 assets and good for fixed office and IT gear. But it only knows where an item was last scanned, so on a busy nursing floor its location data drifts. Norra reports room-level location automatically, with no scanning.


Both names come up when an operator wants something lighter and cheaper than hospital tracking, so this piece compares them honestly: what each is built for, where AssetTiger genuinely wins, and why a skilled nursing buyer usually lands on Norra. The equipment question in skilled nursing is not really a technology question. It is a margin question. A typical 110-bed nursing home loses $155,000 to $500,000 a year to equipment waste: rentals that keep billing past their return date, owned wheelchairs nobody can find, and duplicate purchases of gear already sitting in a closet one floor up. The median skilled nursing facility runs on a 1.8 percent operating margin, so that waste can equal most of what the building earns in a year. We break the full model down in the[2026 SNF equipment waste report](https://www.norra.io/blog/2026-snf-equipment-waste-report) . Any tool you pick has to move that number, not just produce a tidier list.


## What AssetTiger is, and where it fits


AssetTiger is a well-built, genuinely free asset catalog from the maker of MyAssetTag, which is how the product stays free: the business sells the physical barcode and QR labels you print and stick on your gear. You create a record for any asset, add custom fields, attach warranty and contract documents, and check items in and out from a phone by scanning a label. The free plan covers up to 250 assets with unlimited users and unlimited cloud storage, and paid annual plans start in the low hundreds of dollars a year for larger counts. It carries email alerts for expiring warranties, maintenance schedules, depreciation, audit history, and reporting.


The honest credit: for a small list of fixed assets, AssetTiger is one of the best free tools in its class. IT departments, universities, and construction and transportation companies use it to catalog laptops, tools, and office equipment at essentially zero software cost. If your requirement is a searchable, low-cost inventory of things that mostly sit still, and you have staff whose job includes keeping that catalog current, AssetTiger does that job well and cheaply.


AssetTiger is also candid about what it is not. It has no GPS, no geofencing, no in-motion alerts, and no automatic location layer of any kind. It tracks fixed assets, not equipment in constant motion. Those are reasonable limits for the buyer it was built for. They become the whole problem on a nursing floor.


## Why a free barcode catalog breaks in skilled nursing


The catch is structural, and it is not a knock on the software. A barcode catalog only knows where an item was the last time a human scanned it. The map is exactly as accurate as staff discipline: every item, every move, forever. On a nursing floor that discipline does not hold. Nurses and aides already lose 30 to 60 minutes per shift hunting for equipment. Asking them to stop and scan a wheelchair every time it rolls to a new room is added work during the exact moments they have none to spare. Within a few weeks the database drifts from reality, and a location system you cannot trust is one you stop opening. This is the core reason skilled nursing operators keep bouncing off general asset apps, and we lay out the full case in[why skilled nursing should not use general asset software](https://www.norra.io/blog/why-skilled-nursing-should-not-use-general-asset-software) .


The 250-asset free ceiling compounds it. A single skilled nursing facility's durable medical equipment fleet, once you count wheelchairs, hospital beds, specialty mattresses, patient lifts, IV and feeding pumps, oxygen concentrators, and monitors, can pass 250 taggable items fast. When it does, AssetTiger asks you to either delete assets to stay free or jump to a paid annual plan, and users report that the free-to-paid step arrives abruptly. So the exact moment a facility gets serious about tracking its whole fleet is the moment the free plan stops being free.


The bigger gap is where the money hides. The single largest equipment leak in skilled nursing is rentals that should have gone back: a facility keeps paying a daily rate on a rented mattress or therapy pump long after the resident stopped needing it, because nobody can confirm the unit is sitting unused in a storage room. A scan-based catalog cannot catch that, because the catch depends on knowing the live location and status of every billable item without asking anyone to do anything. That is the workflow Norra automates, and we walk through it in[how software stops duplicate rentals](https://www.norra.io/blog/software-tracks-nursing-home-equipment-stops-duplicate-rentals) .

**Norra** is the SNF-native choice. **Best for:** skilled nursing facilities and chains that want equipment waste eliminated, not just inventoried.


2.


**AssetTiger** is the free, general-purpose catalog. It is a flexible barcode app that can log any asset with custom fields at essentially zero software cost, and it is a strong pick for fixed office and IT gear. It has no live location layer, no SNF workflows, a 250-asset free ceiling, and it depends on staff scanning every item on every move, which is where it struggles on a busy floor.


**Best for:** a small fixed-asset list, back-office and IT gear that rarely moves, or a facility with an unusually disciplined scanning routine and a near-zero software budget.

It helps to name the axes that separate SNF-fit tools from the rest, because they apply here too: room-level location (you need the room, not sub-meter precision), no wiring buildout, an operating expense instead of a capital install, and live in days instead of months. Norra is built to all four. A free barcode catalog clears the cost axis but misses the one that matters most on a nursing floor, which is location you can trust without human effort.
