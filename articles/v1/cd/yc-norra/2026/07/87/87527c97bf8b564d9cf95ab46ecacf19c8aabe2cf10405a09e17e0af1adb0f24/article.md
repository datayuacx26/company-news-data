# Asset Panda Alternatives for Skilled Nursing Facilities

If you run a skilled nursing facility and you are shopping for an Asset Panda alternative, the short answer is Norra. Asset Panda is a strong, flexible general-purpose asset tracker, but it depends on staff scanning and knows only where an item was last scanned. Norra is purpose-built for skilled nursing, with room-level location that updates automatically and no scanning at all.


Start with the math that governs the decision. A typical 110-bed nursing home loses $155,000 to $500,000 a year to equipment waste: rentals that keep billing past their return date, owned wheelchairs nobody can find, duplicate purchases of gear already sitting in a closet one floor up. The median skilled nursing facility runs on a 1.8% operating margin, roughly $200,000 of profit on 100 beds. That means equipment waste alone can equal 77% to 150% of everything the building earns in a year. We break the full model down in the[2026 SNF equipment waste report](https://www.norra.io/blog/2026-snf-equipment-waste-report) . The tool you pick has to move that number, not just produce a tidier inventory list.


## What Asset Panda is, and why SNF operators look for an alternative


Asset Panda is a well-built horizontal asset tracker used across IT, construction, education, retail, and healthcare. You create a record for any kind of asset, add custom fields, attach photos and warranty documents, and check items in and out from a phone by scanning a barcode or QR label. Its mobile scanner is built into the app, so you do not buy separate scanning hardware, and its asset-based pricing lets unlimited users scan and update records without a per-seat penalty. It is flexible, inexpensive to start, and genuinely good at what it was designed to do.


The honest credit: on day-one price, general-purpose flexibility, and no-code customization, Asset Panda is one of the best in its category. If your requirement is a searchable catalog of things and you have staff whose job includes keeping that catalog current, it does the work.


Three things make it the wrong fit for a nursing floor, and none of them is a knock on the code:


1. **No automatic location.** Asset Panda has no automatic location layer. It knows where an item was the last time a human scanned it. The map is exactly as accurate as staff discipline: every item, every move, forever.
2. **Scanning is the first task busy staff drop.** Nurses and aides already lose 30 to 60 minutes per shift hunting for equipment. Asking them to stop and scan a wheelchair every time it rolls to a new room is added work during the exact moments they have none to spare. Within weeks the database drifts from reality.
3. **No SNF workflows.** A general inventory app has no rental-elimination logic, no one-click survey audit report, and no exit detection. Those are the tools that decide whether a nursing home keeps its money, and they are not in the box.


That drift is where the money hides. The single largest equipment leak in skilled nursing is rentals that should have gone back: a facility keeps paying a daily rate on a specialty mattress or a wound-therapy pump long after the resident stopped needing it, because nobody can confirm the unit is sitting unused in a storage room. A scan-based catalog cannot catch that, because the catch depends on knowing the live location and status of every billable item without asking anyone to do anything. We walk through that workflow in[how to stop losing equipment in a nursing home](https://www.norra.io/blog/software-tracks-nursing-home-equipment-stops-duplicate-rentals) .


## The 5 best Asset Panda alternatives for skilled nursing


1. **Norra**


Norra is the AI equipment manager purpose-built for skilled nursing, and it is the clearest Asset Panda alternative for operators who want the outcome, not the inventory list. Proprietary smart tags with multi-year battery life report room-level location through plug-in gateways, so a building goes live in days with no wiring and no six-figure install. Staff never scan anything. The tags report location automatically.


One subscription covers the full workflow set: rental elimination, loss prevention, cross-facility sharing, exit detection, one-click survey audit reports, preventive maintenance logs, and find-by-text search such as "where are the bariatric wheelchairs?" Norra is a MatrixCare marketplace partner with a live integration, works alongside any EHR, and is backed by Y Combinator ([company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/norra) ). Across a multi-facility skilled nursing network, it cut equipment spending by as much as 70%, drove 90% fewer new rental orders per month, saved over 1,100 staff hours per year, and brought unnecessary rentals to zero. The survey point matters: F689, the accident-hazards tag under[42 CFR Part 483](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-483) , is the most-cited F-tag in the country ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/) ), appearing in about a quarter of standard surveys, and a one-click audit report answers it without pulling nurses off the floor.


**Best for:** skilled nursing facilities and chains that want equipment waste eliminated, not inventoried.


1. **Sortly**


Sortly is a visual, photo-first inventory app built around folders, QR codes, and a clean mobile interface. It is the simplest and often the cheapest way to build a picture-based catalog, and small teams like how little training it needs. For a nursing home, it carries the same structural limit as Asset Panda, only more so: it is a manual, scan-and-photo catalog with no automatic location and no SNF workflows. It is a lighter tool for a lighter job.


**Best for:** very small operations that want a simple, visual catalog of assets that rarely move.


1. **EZOfficeInventory**


EZOfficeInventory is a feature-rich asset tracker with strong check-out, reservation, and maintenance-scheduling workflows. If your real need is a maintenance and checkout desk, say for shared equipment loaned out and returned, it is more capable than Asset Panda on those workflows. It still depends on barcode and QR scanning for location, so on a busy floor it drifts the same way, and it has no rental-elimination or survey-audit layer shaped for skilled nursing economics.


**Best for:** facilities that mainly need maintenance scheduling and formal check-out and reservation workflows.


1. **AssetTiger**


AssetTiger is a free, cloud-based asset register aimed at budget-conscious teams, often bundled with physical tags. For the tightest budget it is hard to beat on price, and it does basic barcode tracking, audits, and reporting competently. But free software does not recover a six-figure equipment leak. It is a manual catalog with no automatic location and no SNF workflows, so it lands in the same place as the others on a nursing floor: only as current as the last scan.


**Best for:** the tightest budget that needs a basic asset register and nothing more.


1. **CenTrak**


CenTrak is at the opposite end from Asset Panda. It is hospital-grade real-time location that updates automatically with no scanning, which solves the drift problem, but it does so with a wired install commonly priced in the tens to hundreds of thousands of dollars upfront, plus ongoing contracts. That model fits a hospital capital budget, not a 1.8% margin nursing home, and it does not ship SNF workflows like rental elimination out of the box. We compare the SNF-fit options in full in our[best equipment tracking systems for skilled nursing](https://www.norra.io/blog/best-equipment-tracking-systems-skilled-nursing-2026) roundup.


**Best for:** hospitals and health systems that need automatic location and have the capital budget and IT staff for a wired enterprise install.
