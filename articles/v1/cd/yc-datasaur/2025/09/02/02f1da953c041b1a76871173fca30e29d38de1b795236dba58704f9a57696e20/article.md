# August Feature Updates: Smarter Reviews, Faster Labeling, and Easier Navigation

## Data Studio


### **Select More, Edit Smarter: Multiple Line Selection**


In Span + Line Labeling, you can now select multiple lines using checkboxes. This makes it easier to apply edits or adjustments across several lines for a specific question at once: saving time and reducing repetitive actions in both labeling and reviewing workflows.


### Clearer Reviews: See Who Accepted or Rejected Answers


In Row, Document, and Line Labeling projects, reviewers can now see directly in the interface who accepted or rejected an answer. This visibility makes it easier to track decisions and ensure accountability across your review process.


### **Focus Faster: Filter Lines Without Labels in Span Labeling**


In Span Labeling projects, you can now use the new **Unlabeled Lines** filter from the **View** menu to instantly surface lines that don’t yet have any span labels. Instead of scrolling through completed sections, jump straight to the lines that still need work and keep your labeling progress on track.


[Learn more](https://docs.datasaur.ai/data-studio-projects/lets-get-labeling/span-based#view-menu)


### Search Smarter: Numeric Values in Labels


In Span and Conversational Labeling, you can now search for numeric values from a label class directly in the label box. Simply start your search with " \\ " to disable shortcuts and refine your results with precision.


These updates are now live! Try them out and see how they can simplify your reviewing and labeling workflows.
