# Juventus Football Club Announces Fortinet as Its Official Cybersecurity Partner

**About Fortinet**


[Fortinet](http://www.fortinet.com/) (NASDAQ: FTNT) is a driving force in the evolution of cybersecurity and the convergence of networking and security. Our mission is to secure people, devices, and data everywhere, and today we deliver cybersecurity everywhere you need it with the largest integrated portfolio of over 50 enterprise-grade products. Well over half a million customers trust Fortinet's solutions, which are among the most deployed, most patented, and most validated in the industry. The[Fortinet Training Institute](http://www.fortinet.com/nse-training) , one of the largest and broadest training programs in the industry, is dedicated to making cybersecurity training and new career opportunities available to everyone. Collaboration with high-profile, well-respected[organizations](http://www.fortinet.com/trust?tab=trusted%20partners) from both the public and private sectors, including CERTs, government entities, and academia, is a fundamental aspect of Fortinet’s commitment to enhance cyber resilience globally.[FortiGuard Labs](http://www.fortinet.com/fortiguard/labs) , Fortinet’s elite threat intelligence and research organization, develops and utilizes leading-edge machine learning and AI technologies to provide customers with timely and consistently top-rated protection and actionable threat intelligence. Learn more at[https://www.fortinet.com](http://www.fortinet.com/) , the[Fortinet Blog](https://www.fortinet.com/blog) , and[FortiGuard Labs](http://www.fortinet.com/fortiguard/labs) .
