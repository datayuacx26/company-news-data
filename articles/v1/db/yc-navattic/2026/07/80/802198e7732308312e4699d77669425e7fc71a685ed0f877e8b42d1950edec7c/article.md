# Best Demo Software for Sales and Presales Teams

[Sales demo software](https://www.navattic.com/blog/sales-demo-software) is a category of tools that lets sales and[presales teams](https://www.navattic.com/blog/presales-software) build, personalize, and share interactive product demonstrations without requiring engineering support for every demo.


The goal is to scale personalized demos across the full funnel, reduce repetitive SE work, and give buyers a self-serve way to explore your product.


### TL;DR: Best Sales Demo Software


- **Navattic** – Best for sales teams that want to use demo automation, agentic demos, and sandbox demos across their sales cycle
- **Consensus** – Best for personalized video and click-through demo flows by buyer role
- **Storylane** – Best for quick screenshot or video demos with AI-assisted creation
- **Walnut** – Best for high-level demo customization for ABM campaigns


### Who is demo software for?


- **AEs (Account Executives):** need self-serve leave-behinds and personalized follow-ups that keep deals moving between calls
- **SDRs:** need async, top-of-funnel demos to include in outbound sequences before a rep ever gets on a call
- **[Solution Engineers](https://youtu.be/6Ln3p4zS7B4?si=9R3os7UxIW8PlYxx) (SEs):** need sandbox or live-call environments and repeatable demo builds that don't require engineering support
- **Sales and Presales Leaders:** need analytics, CRM integration, and a platform that scales across a growing team


### What is the difference between interactive demos, demo automation, and agent demos?


[Demo software](https://www.navattic.com/blog/what-is-demo-software) can encompass a few different types of tools. These three terms get used interchangeably, but they mean different things:


### Interactive demo


A self-guided, clickable walkthrough of your product that a prospect explores on their own.


Primary use cases: website embeds, demo centers, and email links.


### Demo automation


The broader practice of using interactive demos throughout the sales motion to remove manual steps from the buying process.


Primary use cases: pre- and post-call leave-behinds, async follow-up, and partner enablement.


Read more in our full[guide to demo automation](https://www.navattic.com/blog/demo-automation) .


### Agent demo


An[AI demo agent](https://www.navattic.com/product/agent-demos) that interacts with prospects, understands their intent, interprets questions, and navigates through your product and content in real time.


Primary use cases: always-on website demos and qualifying inbound traffic without a rep.


Most sales demo software today spans more than one of these categories; the tools below vary in how deep they go on each.


### What to look for in sales demo software


1. **Ease of demo creation:** Look for intuitive capture tools and no-code editing that let you build demos in minutes.
2. **AI-assisted features:** Evaluate AI capabilities like auto-generated copy, voiceovers, and avatars.
3. **Analytics depth:** Prioritize tools with engagement tracking, viewer identification, and CRM integration for actionable insights.
4. **CRM and tech stack integrations:** Ensure the platform connects with your existing tools (Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, etc.).
5. **Demo type flexibility:** Consider whether you need interactive click-through demos, sandbox environments, video demos, or a combination.
6. **Team scalability:** Make sure the platform supports permissions, roles, and demo libraries that stay organized as your presales team grows.
7. **Support for agentic demo formats:** Check whether the tool supports AI-driven, conversational demos in addition to static click-throughs.


### Where is sales demo software most valuable?


According to our[State of Demo Automation report](https://www.navattic.com/report/state-of-demo-automation-2026) , sales demo software works best to:


- **Reduce repetitive harbor demos:** SEs spend more than 10 hours/week on repetitive, early-stage demos. 94% of SEs report conducting repetitive demos at least sometimes
- **Expand the buyer circle:** Multiple stakeholders need to see the product before a purchase decision, with deals taking an average of 3 demos to close. By sending an async demo, you can see who is involved earlier in the sales cycle.
- **Shorten sales cycle:** Demos need to work async (leave-behinds, outbound) to speed up sales. The average sales cycle is 6 months, while deals with automated demo touches close 19 days faster.
- **Maintain demo environments:** Live demo environments are unstable or require engineering to maintain, and only 39% of SE teams have dedicated demo engineering resources
