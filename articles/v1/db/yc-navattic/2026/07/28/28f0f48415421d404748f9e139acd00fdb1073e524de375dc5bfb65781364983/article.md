# 5 Ways to Identify Demo Visitors without a Form

An overwhelming majority of marketers opt for ungated product tours.


According to our[State of the Interactive Product Demo 2025](https://www.navattic.com/report/state-of-the-interactive-product-demo-2025) research,[~70% of the top 1% of interactive demos](https://www.navattic.com/report/state-of-the-interactive-product-demo-2025#What-should-I-know-about-interactive-demos-in-2025) did not begin with a form gate.


That stat has hovered around 70% for the past two years, likely because ungated demos have a 10% higher engagement rate than gated demos.


While that’s a win for buyers, sales teams may feel otherwise. How do they know who to nurture?


Below, we share a few different ways to track and personalize demos without a gate, outlining when to use them and how to put them into practice.


5 Ways to Identify and Nurture Prospects Without a Form


These form-free tracking methods are an effective way to engage, personalize, and convert demo visitors — without bogging them down:


1. **Outbound & Query Strings** (mass outreach personalization)
2. **Demo Share Links** (1:1 personalized follow-ups)
3. **Account-Based Engagement** (location-based tracking and LinkedIn retargeting)
4. **Navattic JS x HubSpot Integration** (capturing visitor data post-form submission.
5. **Query Strings** (track prospects’ behavior).

Option #2: Share Launchpad links with active prospects


**Best for:** Personalized 1-to-1 demos


Maybe your sales team has already chatted with a prospect and knows their email address.


After the call, they may want to send a follow-up demo of a feature that a potential customer was interested in using[Launchpad](https://www.navattic.com/product/launchpad) .


Launchpad allows reps to customize the demos they send by manually adding prospect details (name, company name, role) at various predetermined points in the demo.


All the reps have to do is:


1. Click **+ Share demo** from the[Launchpad extension](https://docs.navattic.com/launchpad/invite/launchpad-extension) , or straight from your Gmail, Outlook, Outreach, SalesLoft, or Gong inbox.
2. Choose the demo type: a **1-1 demo** personalized to a specific company (recommended for most deals), or a **1-many demo** for outbound campaigns.
3. Select the content: assemble specific flows or choose an existing standard demo based on what the prospect cares about most.
4. Customize the experience by adding a personal intro video, downloadable resources, or a custom URL.
5. Add invites for the prospect and any other stakeholders in the deal, so each person's engagement is tracked individually.
6. Copy the link (with an optional image preview) and send it.

3. **Real-time engagement alerts.** Get notified by email or Slack the moment a prospect opens or shares a demo, so reps can prioritize follow-up and tailor the live demo to what the prospect cared about most.


Option #3: Account-Based Engagement


**Best for:** Prospecting or LinkedIn Ads


Even without a form, Navattic tracks demo engagement at the account level. Here’s how to find out who is visiting your demos:


1. Head to[Navattic’s Account-Based Engagement](https://docs.navattic.com/identify#RPJfe) dashboard.
2. Click into an account and scroll down to the Anonymous Visitors section.
3. Click into a visitor.


- You should see the visitor’s city and state under their anonymous ID.
- You can also go to “Attributes” → “Other” to see any additional identifying information Navattic picked up, such as “Seniority.”


Then, from within the account view, go to the Prospector tab. Prospector will help you find relevant contacts to reach out to by role at the account.


### 2 Bonus features


1. **Playbooks.** Use Navattic’s Account-Based Engagement and[Playbooks](https://www.navattic.com/product/playbooks) to send accounts to your Slack, email, or CRM to alert your team.
2. **Hubspot integration for LinkedIn retargeting.** Read more about how to drive further engagement with demo visitors in our[Interactive Demo Accounts for LinkedIn Retargeting](https://www.navattic.com/blog/interactive-demo-linkedin-retargeting) blog.


Option #4: Navattic JS for HubSpot Integration


**Best for:** Tracking website visitors who fill out forms later


If a visitor engages with an ungated demo and later fills out a HubSpot form, Navattic syncs their activity.


#### Here’s how it works:


1. Install the[Navattic JS](https://www.navattic.com/product/integrations#navattic-js) on your site.
2. Navattic captures HubSpot cookies and links demo activity to HubSpot profiles. Once they fill out a form, you’ll see their demo activity inside HubSpot.
3. From there, you can set up a HubSpot Dashboard Report to see demo views, step views, conversions, and the number of Contacts created by each demo.


Check out this[interactive demo](https://navattic-website.navattic.com/wb120h0u) for a deeper dive.


Option #5: Query strings for prospect or customer activity


**Best for:** Tracking prospect’s behavior


[Navattic Query Strings](https://docs.navattic.com/identify#6kxih) can help you monitor a specific prospect’s behavior by adding attributes to the end of a demo share link.


Here’s how to implement them:


1. Pull up a Navattic demo share link.
2. Figure out what attribute you want to track (full name, email, company name) and how those variables are listed in your CRM. *For step-by-step instructions, scroll back up to Option #1, step 1.*
3. Then, add that attribute to the end of the demo link using this format: navattic-demo-link **?attribute=value**


1. Example: Say you **** wanted to track Jane Doe’s behavior. Your link would look like this:[https://training.navattic.com/rd3v0zhg? user.email=jane.doe@navattic.com](https://training.navattic.com/rd3v0zhg?user.email=jane.doe@navattic.com)


4. Add additional attributes using this format: navattic-demo-link?user.email=value **&** user.fullName=value **&** company.name=value


1. Example: Here’s the URL of a demo that references a company’s domain, a user’s first and last names, and the company they work for:[https://capture.navattic.com/cl8a8zz4j3300709js91gpy7pk?g=cl89bcxfz3956309jth6qtmmxi&s=0 &company.domain ={{domain}} &user.firstName ={{first_name}} &user.lastName ={{last_name}} &cmpany.name ={{company](https://capture.navattic.com/cl8a8zz4j3300709js91gpy7pk?g=cl89bcxfz3956309jth6qtmmxi&s=0&company.domain=%7B%7Bdomain%7D%7D&user.firstName=%7B%7Bfirst_name%7D%7D&user.lastName=%7B%7Blast_name%7D%7D&cmpany.name=%7B%7Bcompany) }}
