# The 6 best LiveAgent alternatives compared | Pylon

LiveAgent is an[omnichannel support tool](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/practical-guide-to-omnichannel-support) that brings all customer communications into one workspace. It includes useful features like knowledge bases, live chat, and[AI agents](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/how-to-set-up-ai-agents) that can automate a lot of conversations.


But some B2B support teams struggle with certain limitations, like LiveAgent’s limited customer context. This platform also lacks full Slack integration, and its AI and[automation features](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/customer-support-automation) aren’t as thorough or accessible as some competitors.


In this article, we’ll talk about why companies move away from LiveAgent, and explain what to look for in a new solution. Then we’ll introduce six leading LiveAgent alternatives for B2B support teams.


## Why do companies switch away from LiveAgent?


Here are the main reasons to look for alternatives to LiveAgent:


- **No account-level context.** LiveAgent organizes support around individual tickets, not accounts. This is useful for B2C teams that handle lots of independent requests, but it’s a roadblock for B2B and SaaS customer support teams that need to manage chats with multiple stakeholders across[long-term customer relationships](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/how-to-build-relationship-with-customers) .
- **Lack of native integrations.** B2B customers tend to expect support through Slack Connect or Microsoft Teams. LiveAgent doesn’t have a native Teams integration, and its Slack app only offers one-way notifications.
- **B2C-focused AI and automations.** LiveAgent's automations handle basic interactions well, but fall short for multi-step B2B support workflows. For example, this platform’s AI agent can answer basic questions, but it can’t independently fix complex issues or use broad customer context to[create personalized replies](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/conversational-ticketing-streamlining-personalized-support) . LiveAgent also restricts the number of automation rules you can set up at each pricing tier.


## What to look for in a LiveAgent alternative


Chat Integrations in Pylon


If you want B2B customer support software to replace LiveAgent, here’s what to focus on:


- **Omnichanel support.** Look for native integrations with platforms your support team uses, so they can easily work with customers across channels.
- **Account intelligence.** Your team needs shared access to detailed customer context and[account health data](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/b2b-customer-health-score) as they write replies to customers.
- **B2B-ready AI.** Choose a support platform that offers not just AI assistants and agents, but also powerful automation for the workflows B2B teams use daily. That could include routing, escalation,[customer sentiment analysis](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/customer-sentiment-analysis) , and SLA management.
- **Self-service tools.** A great[knowledge base](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/best-free-knowledge-base-software-for-support) gets customers the answers they need fast and cuts down on support tickets. Look for a built-in knowledge base that’s easy to update, with AI-powered tools that help your team find gaps and generate draft articles.
- **CRM and engineering stack sync.** Your teams should be able to work smoothly across the ticketing system, CRM,[customer success tools](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/tools-for-customer-success) , and engineering apps. When these systems are fully integrated, they stay in sync and update in real time.
- **Scalable and transparent pricing.** Watch out for pricing models that offer cheap basic plans, but force you into a cycle of constant upgrades and add-ons to get the features you need as you scale. Good support platform pricing is clear, straightforward, and tiered to meet your growing needs.

### 1. Pylon


[Pylon’s omnichannel support platform](https://www.usepylon.com/) lets your team work directly with customers across Slack, Teams, email, and other channels from a single workspace. This platform brings together customer context from places like CRM records and support tickets, so your team can offer rich, personalized[customer experiences](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/effortless-customer-experience) . **Best for:** Pylon is built for B2B and SaaS customer care teams that want powerful but streamlined custom support workflows.


### 2. Intercom


[Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) ’s standout feature is its Fin AI agent, which handles a lot of customer issues on its own. This platform also offers analytics and reporting tools, plus integrations with third-party apps like Jira and Instagram.


**Limitations:** Although Intercom’s basic plan is affordable, some features B2B teams need (like AI assistants and workflow automation builders) are locked to more expensive plans or costly add-ons.


**Best for:** Intercom works well for large consumer-facing companies with high volumes of repeat tickets.


### 3. Zendesk


[Zendesk](https://www.usepylon.com/competitors/zendesk) offers an advanced[ticketing system](http://www.usepylon.com/blog/help-desk-software-examples) , with automated routing and workflows you can customize to deal with common scenarios. You also get real-time dashboards that track customer support metrics like resolution times and team performance.


**Limitations:** This platform's costs grow fast as you add AI features and move to higher pricing tiers, and it’s more complicated to set up and configure than some competitors.


**Best for:** Zendesk is a good choice for enterprise B2C companies that want a flexible, highly customizable support platform.


### 4. Freshdesk


[Freshdesk](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/) ’s ticketing system integrates well with other Freshworks apps, like the Freshsales CRM and Freshservice IT management system. This platform offers omnichannel support, workflow automations, and[AI-powered issue resolution](http://www.usepylon.com/blog/ai-customer-service) through its Freddy AI agent. The AI agent can also spot patterns in metrics and alert you about problems like SLA breaches.


**Limitations:** Freshdesk offers a low starting cost, but you need access to its Pro or Enterprise pricing plan for important features like custom dashboards and AI insights.


**Best for:** Freshdesk suits small teams, especially those that already use other tools in the Freshworks ecosystem.


### 5. Front


[Front](https://front.com/) ’s main strength is its shared inbox, where teams can work together on[customer messaging](http://www.usepylon.com/blog/customer-messaging-service) using internal notes and shared drafts. This tool integrates well with CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot. It also brings customer context together with conversations, for shared visibility across pre and post-sales teams.


**Limitations:** Front’s automation rules and workflows can be complicated to set up.


**Best for:** Front is a solid choice for B2B teams that need a support platform built around collaboration.


### 6. Help Scout


[Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/) ’s main strength is its intuitive design, which makes it easy to set up and start using quickly. This support platform offers a shared inbox where your team can manage conversations from email, chat, phone calls, and social media in one place. There’s also a knowledge base feature that helps you build a branded self-service help center in minutes.


**Limitations:** Help Scout’s simplicity is also its limitation; it offers only basic reporting and few options for workflow automation.


**Best for:** Help Scout offers good value for small teams that need simple, streamlined ticket management.

## FAQ


### What’s the most affordable LiveAgent alternative for a small SaaS team?


Freshdesk and Help Scout both offer free tiers or low-cost entry plans, which can work for early-stage teams getting off the ground. That said, neither is purpose-built for B2B SaaS; Freshdesk's AI and account-level visibility are limited, and Help Scout lacks native Slack support and enterprise workflow depth. As your team scales, the gaps will start to show.


### Which LiveAgent alternative has the best AI features for SaaS support?


Intercom's Fin AI is strong at deflecting high-volume, repetitive queries, but it was designed for product-led and B2C teams, not complex B2B account management. For SaaS teams managing multi-stakeholder enterprise accounts, Pylon's AI is built around account context and multi-step workflows, making it a better long-term fit.


### Can I migrate my data from LiveAgent to another platform easily?


Most major alternatives offer native import tools or CSV-based migration. The complexity depends on ticket volume, custom fields, and integrations in your current LiveAgent setup.
