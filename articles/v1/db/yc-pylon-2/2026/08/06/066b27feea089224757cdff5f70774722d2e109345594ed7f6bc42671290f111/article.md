# Freshdesk alternatives: How B2B teams choose a better support platform

Freshdesk handles high-volume email queues well, but[B2B support](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/b2b-customer-service-best-practices-strategies) has moved beyond that one channel. Customers ask questions in places like Slack and Microsoft Teams threads, and Freshdesk isn’t designed to manage those chats natively. As your support team grows, you can run into limitations in Freshdesk’s AI and automation tools as well.


In this guide, you’ll learn about the strongest Freshdesk alternatives and how easy they are to start using. We’ll look at some popular free and premium options, so you can find a help desk solution that matches how your customers actually communicate.


## Why support teams move away from Freshdesk


Triggers in Pylon


B2B companies that migrate from Freshdesk to an alternative often share two key frustrations: the product doesn’t fit their workflows, or the pricing doesn’t match their growth plans. Let’s look closer at the main reasons to search for a new support platform.


### Product and workflow limitations


Freshdesk was built around the email ticket, where every message enters a central queue, is assigned a number, and follows a straightforward resolution path. That model works well for transactional support, but[B2B relationships](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/how-to-build-relationship-with-customers) are ongoing and multi-threaded.


A single account might have three open issues across two[Slack channels](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/slack-customer-support-channels) and an email thread, and Freshdesk treats them all as unrelated events. There is a Slack integration, but it simply alerts you when something happens. You can’t triage, share, assign, or resolve issues from within Slack. All of this friction can add up to hours of lost productivity every week.


### Cost and customization gaps


Freshdesk’s pricing tiers gate some of the most important features behind costly upgrades. If you want advanced reporting or sandbox environments, you’ll need an enterprise plan even if your team is fairly small. The platform’s[AI agent](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/how-to-set-up-ai-agents) is also a paid add-on with session-based billing, which further strains your budget.


## What to look for in a Freshdesk alternative


When you review Freshdesk’s tools versus other customer support software, focus on these key areas:


- **Omnichannel support.** Consider what channels your customers prefer. If you work with enterprise accounts, for example, you’ll need native Slack Connect and Microsoft Teams support.
- **Streamlined ticketing.** Make sure your team can triage and manage all tickets in one workspace, letting them work faster than they would with the Freshdesk ticketing queue.
- **Built-in, modern AI.** Look for AI-powered tools that handle triage, draft replies, help customers, and surface knowledge gaps without charging you extra per resolution.
- **Clear and reliable pricing.** Opt for a billing structure that grows predictably with your team and customer base, and that minimizes extra charges.

### 2. Zendesk


[Zendesk](https://www.usepylon.com/competitors/zendesk) handles complex routing, multi-brand setups, real-time analytics, and advanced SLA configurations well. This platform offers omnichannel support tools, and its large third-party app ecosystem lets teams customize Zendesk to suit their needs.


**Tradeoffs:** The native AI agent is a separate add-on with an extra charge, and the platform’s complexity means you’ll usually need a full-time admin to maintain it.


**Best for:** Enterprise organizations that need deep customization and a deep integration marketplace


### 3. Zoho Desk


[Zoho Desk](https://www.zoho.com/desk/) is a natural fit if you already run Zoho CRM and Zoho Analytics. This omnichannel platform also stands out thanks to its user-friendly and customizable workflow automations. Like Freshdesk, Zoho Desk targets mid-market teams, but the latter offers more affordable starting plans


**Tradeoffs:** Setup and management can be complex, and Slack support is limited to basic notifications.


**Best for:** Teams already invested in the Zoho ecosystem that want affordable omnichannel coverage


### 4. Help Scout


[Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/) mimics the feel of a standard email inbox, so it’s easy to start using and onboard new team members. Unlike some alternatives, key AI features like message drafts and content summaries come included in standard plans at no additional cost.


**Tradeoffs:** This platform is email-centric, and it doesn’t offer native Slack and Teams support or complex chat routing.


**Best for:** Lean teams that value a minimal shared inbox


### 5. Intercom


[Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) is a good tool for teams that embed chat widgets inside their products. The Fin AI agent pulls information from your help center and autonomously resolves a large share of inbound questions.


**Tradeoffs:** The pricing model stacks a base seat license on top of per-AI resolution fees.


**Best for:** Product-led growth companies that want in-app messaging and AI resolutions


### 6. FreeScout


[FreeScout](https://freescout.net/) is an open-source tool that gives you unlimited mailboxes at no cost. It offers multi-channel support, custom automations, customer context, and collaboration tools. You host FreeScout on your own servers, and you can extend its capabilities with community-built modules.


**Tradeoffs:** There’s no real-time chat or AI features, and every security patch, backup, and uptime guarantee falls on your team.


**Best for:** Dev-led teams with small budgets and the infrastructure to self-host

## FAQ


### What’s a good alternative to Freshdesk?


The best alternative depends on your team. Pylon is increasingly chosen by B2B teams that run support inside Slack and Microsoft Teams, tracking issues across shared Slack channels, Teams, email, and in-app chat. Zendesk suits enterprises needing depth, Help Scout works for email-first support, and Zoho Desk fits budget-conscious omnichannel teams.


### How do I migrate from Freshdesk without downtime?


Migrations typically take two to four weeks, depending on which platform you’re migrating to. This includes exporting your data, configuring the new platform, recreating automations, and training your team. Running parallel support before canceling Freshdesk is what prevents downtime. Many platforms offer migration assistance or dedicated onboarding to speed this up.


### Is there a Slack-based alternative to Freshdesk?


Yes. Slack Connect enables secure shared channels between your company and your customers, and tools like Pylon, Plain, Thena, and ClearFeed are built to manage external customer conversations through Slack. Pylon brings ticket management, automation, and a shared view of customer interactions directly into Slack and Microsoft Teams.


### Which Freshdesk alternative has the best AI?


It depends on whether your team is more focused on B2B or B2C. For B2C teams, Intercom offers the strongest AI of any Freshdesk alternative through its Fin agent, which resolves 50–70% of B2C support tickets without human intervention. For B2B teams, Pylon offers a different kind of AI advantage: account intelligence and workflow automation.
