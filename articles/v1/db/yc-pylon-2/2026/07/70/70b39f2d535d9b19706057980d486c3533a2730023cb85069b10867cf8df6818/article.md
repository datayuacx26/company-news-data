# The 8 Best Kayako Alternatives Compared | Pylon

When it burst on the scene as a bootstrapped startup in 2001, Kayako was a pioneer in unified customer support. But the company has stagnated since its acquisition by a private equity firm in 2018. Product development slowed, AI capabilities took too long to materialize, and many modern B2B support teams have outgrown it.


In this article, we’ll look at why teams are switching from Kayako, and profile the eight best Kayako alternatives for B2B customer support.


## Why teams are switching from Kayako


While Kayako still works as a basic[help desk](http://www.usepylon.com/blog/help-desk-software-examples) and ticketing system, growing B2B teams are limited by its lack of integrations, AI features, and advanced reporting.


Here are the main reasons companies switch from Kayako:


- **Post-acquisition product stagnation.** After the 2018 acquisition, new development stalled. While competitors advanced rapidly, Kayako customers were stuck with a legacy framework that didn’t provide as many new features. As teams scale, they often outgrow Kayako’s limited offering.
- **Absence of modern AI automation.** Although Kayako does offer AI agents and assistants, its automation capabilities are limited compared to other platforms. That makes it harder for companies to scale without increasing headcount, and Kayako users miss out on the benefits of the latest AI customer support software.
- **Fragmented workflows and Slack-blind architecture.** Kayako does offer basic notifications via Slack and other apps, but it’s not possible to create the deep workflow integrations that are common in a modern omnichannel ticketing system. Teams struggle to collaborate across channels and departments as they need to in B2B customer support, and there’s more manual effort necessary than with other platforms.


## What to look for in a Kayako alternative


Issue view from Pylon


Here are a few features to look for when making the switch from Kayako:


- **Active product development.** Look for a company that listens to customer feedback and invests in new features like[AI customer service tools](http://www.usepylon.com/blog/ai-customer-service) .
- **Native Slack and Teams support.** Your customers likely use Slack and Teams all the time, so you need native integrations with those apps to help your team collaborate and communicate with customers without constantly switching tools.
- **Account-level context.** As your team answers customer inquiries, they need access to the full context, including ticket history, open issues, key stakeholders, and account health.
- **B2B-ready AI capabilities.** The best modern platforms use AI-driven tools to summarize complex issues and conversations,[identify churn risk](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/churn-prediction) , generate draft responses, give customers useful[self-service options](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/customer-self-service) , and help your team manage customer relationships.
- **Integration depth with your existing stack.** Look for a support platform that connects with your existing customer relationship management (CRM) system, as well as[customer success tools](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/tools-for-customer-success) , analytics, billing systems, and any other apps you regularly use.
- **Scalable and transparent pricing.** Watch for costly add-ons or prices that change dramatically when the user count increases. You need predictable pricing structures that remain affordable as you grow.

### 1. Pylon


AI Agent runbook view from Pylon


[Pylon](https://www.usepylon.com/) is the best choice for B2B SaaS teams who want omnichannel support built around native integrations for Slack and Microsoft Teams.


Designed specifically for complex B2B workflows, Pylon’s omnichannel support platform takes customer messages from different stakeholders across multiple channels and automatically creates support tickets with all the customer context included. Your team can work from a single platform and track issues in one place, instead of jumping between Slack, email, and live chat. Pylon also lets you integrate your ticket management system with dozens of other apps, like your CRM, call recorder, product ticketing platform, and data warehouse.


Pylon’s strong B2B focus means that it’s not a good fit for consumer-facing companies looking for basic help desk software.


### 2. Intercom


[Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) is great for large B2C companies looking for AI-powered customer engagement.


Intercom’s[customer messaging tools](http://www.usepylon.com/blog/customer-messaging-service) help you respond to high volumes of inquiries by using AI agents to answer simple queries and routing more complex ones to a shared inbox where your team can answer them. Its self-service options can reduce ticket volume, saving troubleshooting time and giving customers instant answers.


Although the starter plan is affordable, costs rise quickly as you migrate to higher plans with more capabilities, and there’s a separate charge for each outcome its AI agent achieves.


### 3. Zendesk


[Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/) is a strong choice for enterprise-scale B2C companies with high ticket volumes.


When comparing Zendesk and Kayako, Zendesk has a broader feature set covering ticket management, live chat,[knowledge bases](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/best-free-knowledge-base-software-for-support) , and automated workflows. Its advanced reporting capabilities and extensive set of integrations make it a popular choice for enterprises with complex support operations.


Zendesk can be difficult to manage, especially for smaller teams, and because it relies heavily on custom workflows and integrations, it can be time-consuming to migrate to the platform.


### 4. Freshdesk


[Freshdesk](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/) is best for small and mid-sized companies, especially those already using other products in the Freshworks ecosystem.


Freshdesk offers simple omnichannel ticket management, with automated workflows and an[AI agent](http://www.usepylon.com/blog/how-to-use-ai-agents) and assistant. It includes many of the features of larger platforms, like multichannel support, a knowledge base, and a self-service portal, and because it has an intuitive interface, it’s quick to learn.


Freshdesk gets expensive for teams with complex customer support needs. Key features B2B teams want, like custom dashboards and AI-powered sentiment analysis, require higher-tier plans.


### 5. Front


[Front](https://front.com/) is a strong choice for B2B startups and smaller teams looking for a shared inbox and multichannel communication.


Front’s platform places a strong emphasis on collaboration. With internal notes and shared drafts, different team members can work together on an issue in real time, without letting the customer see the details until the response is finalized. You can bring in additional team members with @ mentions and assign tickets to named individuals to preserve accountability. Its omnichannel platform brings messages from channels like email, SMS, and live chat into a single conversation thread. You can also integrate the platform with many CRMs, like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive, and see all your CRM information directly in the Front workspace, without switching apps.


Larger enterprise teams may prefer a more traditional ticketing system built for higher volumes.


### 6. Help Scout


[Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/) is ideal for smaller teams looking for streamlined tools without unnecessary features.


Help Scout’s strength is its ease of use. Your team sees a shared inbox showing the latest customer messages, and when they compose a reply, a sidebar pops up to show them important customer information. Its AI assistant helps them summarize issues and generate draft responses.


Because Help Scout is built for simplicity, it doesn’t offer the advanced ticket management and workflow customization options of more robust platforms on this list, like Pylon.


### 7. Zoho Desk


[Zoho Desk](https://www.zoho.com/desk/) is best for small to mid-sized businesses looking for a simple help desk alternative to Kayako. It offers a more intuitive interface than Kayako and lets your team automate key workflows like routing and escalation.


As part of the broader Zoho product suite, Zoho Desk provides strong integration options to connect your customer service software with your Zoho CRM, sales, and operational systems. It offers decent automation capabilities and a built-in AI assistant.


Although Zoho Desk integrates easily with other apps in the Zoho ecosystem, it doesn’t offer as many third-part integrations as many other platforms on this list.


### 8. Tidio


Choose[Tidio](https://www.tidio.com/) if you’re running customer support for a B2C retail or e-commerce company.


Tidio’s main strength lies in its live chat system, which is supported by an AI-powered chatbot. It’s ideal for handling large volumes of routine inquiries without increasing headcount.


However, Tidio is less suited to complex B2B support environments because it has limited integrations, few automation options, and only basic analytics.

## FAQ


### What’s the best Kayako alternative?


There is no single best alternative; it depends on what your team actually does. Enterprise teams who need deep workflow customization tend to land on Zendesk. Budget-conscious teams choose Freshdesk or Zoho Desk. B2B SaaS teams supporting customers in Slack and Teams choose Pylon, because it treats account-level context and shared channels as the core of the product rather than an add-on. The right way to decide is to start from your support model and primary channel, then match the tool to it.


### What features should I prioritize in a Kayako alternative?


Five things separate a modern platform from a legacy one: active product development, native Slack and Teams support, account-level context, B2B-ready AI (resolvers and triage, not just canned macros), and transparent pricing that scales with you. Kayako falls short on most of these post-2019. Pylon was built around the first three: it is actively developed, Slack and Teams are native channels, and[Account Intelligence](https://www.usepylon.com/account-intelligence) ties every conversation to the customer relationship behind it.


### Which Kayako alternative is best for B2B teams supporting customers over Slack?


Pylon is purpose-built for Slack Connect and Microsoft Teams support, which makes it the strongest fit for B2B SaaS teams whose enterprise customers expect support in those channels rather than a ticket portal.


### What’s the most affordable Kayako alternative for a small SaaS team?


Freshdesk and Zoho Desk both offer free or low-cost tiers that work for early-stage teams, but neither is built for B2B SaaS. Freshdesk gives you limited account-level visibility and less mature AI, and Zoho Desk is most powerful only if you’re already in the Zoho ecosystem. They are reasonable starting points, but teams managing complex customer relationships usually outgrow them and move to a purpose-built platform like Pylon as they scale.


### How difficult is it to migrate from Kayako to a new platform?


Most major alternatives support CSV import or offer dedicated migration help. Difficulty depends on your ticket volume, custom fields, and active integrations. Smaller teams usually finish in a few weeks. Pylon offers guided migration so account history and context carry over rather than starting from a blank inbox.
