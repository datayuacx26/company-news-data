# Pylon vs. Freshdesk: Which Fits Your Support Team? | Pylon

Pylon is built for B2B teams who support high-value customers natively within Slack and Microsoft Teams, combining real-time messaging with built-in AI and account intelligence. Freshdesk is a traditional, email-first help desk designed for teams who need broad omnichannel coverage across social media, phone, and web portals at a low entry price.


When comparing Freshdesk versus Pylon, the core difference comes down to architectural philosophy: Freshdesk is built around a traditional email queue, and Pylon is built around real-time messaging channels where your customers already work.


## Why B2B teams switch from Freshdesk


Account custom fields view from Pylon


Most teams leave Freshdesk because their support model has outgrown the traditional ticketing queue. The platform works well when every customer issue can be neatly packaged into an email, but the moment your high-value accounts start expecting real-time collaboration, the architecture becomes a bottleneck.


### Support model and channel limitations


Freshdesk pulls every conversation into a centralized, disconnected queue. If you support enterprise accounts, you know that the most critical conversations happen in shared Slack channels. Freshdesk’s Slack integration is primarily built for internal notifications, not for conducting full, bi-directional support conversations natively within the channel. This forces your team to manually update ticket statuses/each other, reducing productivity. Every context switch adds minutes to your response time, and those minutes compound across hundreds of conversations per week.


Pylon’s Slack integration, on the other hand, treats the shared channel as the primary workspace. When a customer messages you in Slack, your team replies from a unified inbox, and that reply appears instantly back in the customer’s Slack thread. There’s no portal to log into and no context lost in translation.


### AI and cost gaps at scale


Freshdesk’s AI capabilities run through Freddy AI, which is offered as a paid add-on with session-based billing. If you want automated resolution, you pay extra for every session the AI handles; these costs can spiral out of control during high-volume months when you need automation most.


Plus, the core Freshdesk plans don’t include any advanced AI features, meaning you have to upgrade your base tier just to unlock the privilege of paying for the AI add-on. For teams scaling quickly, this unpredictable pricing model makes it impossible to forecast your software budget accurately.


## What to look for in a Freshdesk alternative


When reviewing features lists, focus on how those features map to your daily operations.


Start by looking hard at where your team spends the most time. If they’re spending two hours a day just copying and pasting context between different browser tabs, a platform with a slightly cheaper per-seat license is actually costing you a massive amount of money in lost productivity. The goal is to eliminate friction instead of swapping one clunky interface for another.


Also look closely at how the platform handles the channels your customers use. If you support enterprise accounts, native Slack Connect and Microsoft Teams integrations are non-negotiable. You want a tool that lets your customers message you in Slack, while your team replies from a unified inbox that tracks SLAs and assigns ownership.


Don’t settle for a tool that just pings a Slack channel when a new email arrives. The integration needs to be bi-directional. When your team replies in the help desk, the response needs to appear instantly in the customer’s Slack channel, properly threaded and formatted. If the integration forces the customer to click a link and log into a portal to read the reply, it defeats the entire purpose of offering Slack support.


You also need to evaluate how the platform handles account context. In B2B support, you’re not answering isolated tickets. You’re often managing ongoing relationships, and your team needs to see[a customer’s health score](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/b2b-customer-health-score) , ARR, and full interaction history before they type a single word. If your support platform can’t sync natively with your CRM, your team is flying blind with all of this data. They might treat a[churn-risk enterprise account](https://www.usepylon.com/blog/customer-churn) with the same urgency as a lower-tier user because they lack the right context.

## FAQ


### Is Pylon better than Freshdesk for B2B support?


For B2B teams who support customers inside Slack and Microsoft Teams, Pylon is the stronger choice. It treats shared channels as the primary support surface and includes AI-native workflows, account health scoring, and B2B-specific features out of the box. Freshdesk is better suited for teams who need B2C channel coverage and a traditional ticket queue model.


### Can I migrate from Freshdesk to Pylon without downtime?


Migrations typically take one to two weeks. This includes exporting your data, configuring the new platform, recreating automations, and training your team. Running parallel support before canceling Freshdesk is what prevents downtime. Many platforms offer migration assistance or dedicated onboarding to speed this up. If you want to make the switch, look for a vendor with a dedicated onboarding team, like Pylon.


### Does Freshdesk have AI built in or is it an add-on?


Freshdesk's AI capabilities are available through Freddy AI, which is offered as a paid add-on. Freddy Copilot assists agents with suggestions and summaries, while Freddy AI automates resolution through session-based billing. The core Freshdesk plans don’t include advanced AI features. Pylon, by contrast, includes AI triage, routing, and draft replies as part of the core platform offering.
