# Barclays Renews Long Standing Credit Card Partnership with Barnes & Noble


## Renewal extends nearly two decades of collaboration and continues exclusive rewards program for readers nationwide


Wilmington, Del. | June 09, 2026


Barclays US Consumer Bank today announced the long‑term renewal of its exclusive co‑branded credit card partnership with Barnes & Noble, the nation’s largest retail bookseller. The renewed agreement reinforces a collaboration that has delivered meaningful value to book lovers since 2006 and continues to provide cardmembers with rewards designed specifically for Barnes & Noble customers.


The Barnes & Noble Mastercard®, exclusively issued by Barclays, offers tailored benefits for dedicated readers, including 5% back on Barnes & Noble and Paper Source purchases, automatic Barnes & Noble Gift Card rewards, dining rewards, and premium membership incentives. The renewed partnership ensures the continuation of these valuable benefits for Barnes & Noble shoppers across physical stores and online at bn.com.


“Barnes & Noble is an iconic American brand with a deeply loyal customer base, and we are proud to extend one of Barclays’ longest-standing co-branded partnerships” said Doug Villone, head of cards and partnerships, Barclays US Consumer Bank. “For two decades, we’ve worked together to design a card program that rewards customers for doing what they love most – discovering books, authors, and ideas – while delivering meaningful, everyday value.”


Since its launch, the Barnes & Noble Mastercard has focused on rewarding frequent shoppers with a simple and compelling structure that aligns closely with how customers engage with the brand.


“Barnes & Noble is very pleased to continue to work with Barclays to offer its customers such a compelling credit card proposition,” said James Daunt, CEO of Barnes & Noble. “The Barnes & Noble Mastercard®, exclusively issued by Barclays, has proved its enduring popularity with our bookstore customers.”


Key features of the Barnes & Noble Mastercard include:


- 5% back on purchases made at Barnes & Noble stores and on bn.com, as well as Paper Source purchases.
- Customers who become new cardmembers by opening accounts online can earn a $25 Barnes & Noble Gift Card once they complete their first purchase.
- Customers who become new cardmembers at the Barnes & Noble store may qualify for a complimentary year of Premium Membership, worth $39.99, and will get $10 off their first eligible purchase.
- An additional $25 Barnes & Noble Gift Card every time cardmembers earn 2,500 points, with points earned at a rate of five points per dollar on eligible purchases.
- Restaurant rewards on eligible dining purchases.
- A complimentary 12‑month Barnes & Noble Premium Membership for qualifying existing cardmembers who spend $7,500 annually on eligible purchases.


Barclays has been the exclusive issuer of the Barnes & Noble credit card since 2006. The relationship was most recently extended in 2022.
