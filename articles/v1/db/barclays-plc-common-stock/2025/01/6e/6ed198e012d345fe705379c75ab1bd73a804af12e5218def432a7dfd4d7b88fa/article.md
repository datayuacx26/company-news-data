# Barclays to Launch New Banking Products with Exclusive offers for AARP Members


## Set to launch in 2025, AARP members will earn rates on savings equal to the bank’s highest APR for savings products. Future offerings, in 2026 exclusively for AARP members will include competitive interest rates for personal loans


WILMINGTON, Del. | January 23, 2025


Barclays US Consumer Bank announced today that it will launch a new suite of banking products with exclusive offers for AARP members across the United States. Starting in spring 2025, Barclays will introduce high-yield savings accounts and CDs, and in 2026, personal loan products that will feature competitive interest rates exclusively for AARP members.


Through Barclays’ banking program, AARP members will have an easy way to open an online savings account, deposit funds remotely, view account balances, and link to other financial institutions. Barclays personal loans will offer AARP members a fast and convenient way to finance some of life’s larger purchases with a fixed low interest rate for the life of the loan.


“Over the past four years, Barclays has offered credit card products tailored to deliver benefits exclusively for AARP members,” said James Capolongo, Head of Banking, Barclays US Consumer Bank. “Through Barclays’ innovative savings and personal loan programs, we will provide AARP members unparalleled value, convenience and security to help them take charge of their financial futures.”


Since 2021, the bank has been the exclusive issuer of AARP Credit Cards from Barclays. The[AARP® Essential Rewards Mastercard® from Barclays](http://www.aarpcreditcard.com/) and the[AARP® Travel Rewards Mastercard® from Barclays](https://cards.barclaycardus.com/banking/credit-card/aarp/mastercard-world/bcus-storefront/608fcbcb-3f02-49ef-a7e6-f2950853d9b2/) have made it easy for cardmembers to earn cash back and travel rewards for everyday spend in categories that are important to AARP members related to health and wellness, as well as travel, medical expenses, drug store purchases, gas, restaurants, hotel, airfare, and more.


AARP members will be provided with more details about these savings and loan programs in the coming months. More information about savings products currently provided by Barclays can be found at[Banking.Barclays.com](https://www.banking.barclaysus.com/index.html) , Member FDIC.
