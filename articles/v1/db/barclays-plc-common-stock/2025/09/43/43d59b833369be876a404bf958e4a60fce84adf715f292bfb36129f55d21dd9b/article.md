# Luxury Card Stands Out with Reimagined Product Suite Featuring Elevated Lifestyle Benefits and Travel Rewards


##


Wilmington, DE | September 30, 2025


Luxury Card **®** , **** a premium credit card provider, today introduced an enhanced suite of credit cards, issued by Barclays, offering customers straightforward rewards, concierge services, and elevated lifestyle benefits with seamless redemptions that come standard. The refreshed portfolio of cards also delivers enhanced travel benefits and access to exclusive experiences unique to the Luxury Card brand. **


The new cards retain their signature heavy metal construction, now reimagined in a contactless, vertical design. Each card offers direct access to global 24/7 concierge services via a sleek mobile app. Cardmembers can enjoy straightforward rewards: 2% value on airfare redemptions, and 1% to 2% cashback with no rotating categories or complicated reward redemption structures. Rewards go further as 50,000 points will earn a $1,000 airfare ticket with no blackout dates or seat restrictions.


A notable feature of the refresh is the debut of LuxuryCardTravel.com, an exclusive booking tool, offering 2X accelerated earn on all hotel and airfare purchased on the site, making it even easier to accumulate rewards. Cardmembers can enjoy up to $750 in annual hotel travel benefits on items such as room upgrades, spa treatments, and amenities reserved through the site or by calling the Luxury Card Concierge. Cardmembers also receive unlimited access to over 1,700 Priority Pass™ airport lounges worldwide, along with enhanced airline statement credits.


The refreshed lineup includes tiered access to premium benefits and services. The total annual value of the cards now ranges from $1,500 to $2,500, depending on the card selected.


- **Mastercard® Gold Card™** – Made with 24-karat Gold ($1,199 annual fee)
- **Mastercard® Black Card®** – Black-PVD-Coated Metal Card ($699 annual fee)
- **Mastercard® Titanium Card®** – Brushed Metal Card ($299 annual fee)


"The new Luxury Card suite is the culmination of extensive customer research and feedback," said Doug Villone, head of cards and partnerships at Barclays US Consumer Bank. "We are introducing a simpler rewards strategy along with program enhancements aimed to delight cardmembers. All three cards provide exceptional services and meaningful benefits that deliver compelling value for travelers who want the most out of life."


For more information, please visit[luxurycard.com](http://luxurycard.com/) .
