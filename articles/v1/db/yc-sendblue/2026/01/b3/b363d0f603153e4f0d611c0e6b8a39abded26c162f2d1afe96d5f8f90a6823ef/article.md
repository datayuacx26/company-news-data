# Sendblue vs Sendinblue: What's the Difference?

### Is Sendblue the same as Sendinblue?


**No.** Sendblue and Sendinblue (now Brevo) are completely different companies with no affiliation. Sendblue is an iMessage API for sales teams, founded in 2023. Sendinblue is an email marketing platform founded in 2012 that rebranded to Brevo.


### Did Sendinblue change its name to Sendblue?


**No.** Sendinblue rebranded to **Brevo** in May 2023—not Sendblue. Sendblue is a separate company that was coincidentally founded the same year. The similar names are purely coincidental.


### Can Sendblue send emails?


**No.** Sendblue only sends iMessages and SMS. It is not an email platform. If you need email marketing, use Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) or another email service provider.


### Can Brevo (Sendinblue) send iMessages?


**No.** Brevo cannot send iMessages. Brevo offers email, SMS, and WhatsApp marketing—but not iMessage. Only Sendblue provides iMessage API capabilities for businesses.


### What is Sendblue used for?


Sendblue is used by B2B sales teams to send iMessages and SMS to prospects directly from their CRM systems like HubSpot and Salesforce. It is designed for sales engagement, not marketing campaigns.


### What happened to Sendinblue?


Sendinblue rebranded to **Brevo** in May 2023. The company still exists and offers the same email marketing and automation services—just under a new name. It did not merge with or become Sendblue.


### Are Sendblue and Brevo competitors?


Not directly. They serve different purposes: Sendblue is for sales teams using iMessage, while Brevo is for marketing teams using email. A company could theoretically use both for different use cases.


### Which is better: Sendblue or Sendinblue?


Neither is better—they solve different problems. Choose Sendblue if you need iMessage API for sales. Choose Brevo (Sendinblue) if you need email marketing automation. They are not interchangeable alternatives.
