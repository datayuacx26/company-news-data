# CRM Messaging Is Broken. Business Text Messaging Is the Fix.

Most CRM messaging implementations are layered on top of SMS marketing software. The CRM sends instructions. The SMS platform sends messages. The conversation lives somewhere else. This architecture creates predictable problems:


- Messages logged without context
- Conversations split across inboxes
- Automation firing without awareness of replies
- Sales and support teams working from partial histories


Even when CRM messaging is technically integrated, it often feels disconnected because SMS itself is a poor conversational medium for long-term relationships. Carrier filtering, A2P messaging restrictions, and short code limitations all interfere with continuity. The result is CRM data that looks complete but feels inaccurate to the people using it.
