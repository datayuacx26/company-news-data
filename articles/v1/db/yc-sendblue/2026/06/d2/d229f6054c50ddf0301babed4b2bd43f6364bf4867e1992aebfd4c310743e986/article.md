# Speed to Lead Is a Messaging Problem, Not a Sales Problem

On the surface, SMS marketing software looks built for speed. Trigger fires, message sends, lead engaged. In practice, carrier-based SMS introduces friction at exactly the moment speed matters most:


- New campaigns wait on A2P messaging registration before a single message can go out
- Carrier filtering flags normal, conversational follow-ups as promotional
- Throttling delays delivery during the high-volume periods when leads cluster
- Shared numbers and short codes signal automation, so customers respond defensively


The message may technically arrive in seconds. But if it arrives looking like a broadcast, the customer treats it like one. Fast delivery into a low-trust channel does not produce a fast reply. That is the hidden tax of SMS marketing software. It optimizes the part you can measure and ignores the part that drives the outcome.
