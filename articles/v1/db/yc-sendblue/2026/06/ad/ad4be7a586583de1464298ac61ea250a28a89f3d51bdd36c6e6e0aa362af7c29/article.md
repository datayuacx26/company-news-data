# Texting Software for Business Has Changed (Most Platforms Haven't)

Sendblue replaces SMS with Apple iMessage, and that decision fundamentally alters how business text messaging performs. iMessage is not filtered by carriers. It does not require short codes. It does not rely on shared sending infrastructure. Messages are delivered through Apple's data network and appear inside the native Messages app.


For customers, this means messages arrive where trust already exists. For businesses, it means:


- More consistent delivery
- Faster replies
- Longer conversations
- Less compliance overhead


This is why iMessage-based texting software consistently outperforms SMS marketing software in real-world usage.
