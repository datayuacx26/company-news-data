# Even Android Users Are Using iMessage in Their CRMs (And Why)

Here is the part that surprises most people. Teams using Android phones and Windows desktops are not "using iMessage" in the traditional sense. They are using CRM messaging platforms that deliver messages as iMessage to customers while providing a web-based interface for internal teams.


In practice:


- Customers receive messages as iMessages on their iPhones
- Teams respond from a browser-based CRM inbox
- No Apple hardware is required internally
- Conversations sync directly to CRM records


From the team's perspective, it feels like modern business text messaging. From the customer's perspective, it feels like a personal conversation. This is why Android-first organizations are adopting iMessage-based CRM messaging at increasing rates.
