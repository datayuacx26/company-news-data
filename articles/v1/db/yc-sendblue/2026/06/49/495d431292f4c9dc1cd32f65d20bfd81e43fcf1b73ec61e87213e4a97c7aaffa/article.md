# Why Sendblue Is the Best SMS Marketing Platform (It's Not SMS)

Traditional SMS marketing software is built on infrastructure that predates modern mobile usage. Carrier networks were designed for person-to-person communication, not high-volume, two-way, business-driven conversations. As businesses adopted SMS for marketing and operations, the system buckled under pressure.


The consequences are familiar to anyone who has used text messaging software at scale. Messages are throttled or delayed. Deliverability fluctuates without warning. Carriers apply aggressive spam filtering. A2P messaging rules become stricter every year. Short codes, shared numbers, and templates add complexity rather than clarity.


Even the most polished texting software for business still relies on the same fragile, carrier-controlled foundation. When carriers update policies or enforcement, businesses absorb the disruption. A2P messaging regulations exist because carriers are trying to protect consumers — but that protection often comes at the expense of legitimate businesses forced into rigid frameworks. Sendblue made a fundamentally different choice. Instead of optimizing within a broken system, it stepped outside of it.
