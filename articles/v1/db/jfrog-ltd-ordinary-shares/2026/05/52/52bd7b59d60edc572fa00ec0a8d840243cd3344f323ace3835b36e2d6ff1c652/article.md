# JFrog Brings India’s Enterprise Leaders Together at EveryOps Day Mumbai to Tackle AI’s Biggest Problem: Trust

### *GitHub, HDFC Bank, Infosys, Tata Motors and more join JFrog to confront the security and governance challenges of enterprise software delivery in the AI era*


**Sunnyvale, Calif., and Mumbai, India – May 7, 2026 –**[JFrog Ltd](https://jfrog.com/) (Nasdaq:


[FROG](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/FROG/) ), the creators of the


[JFrog Software Supply Chain Platform](https://jfrog.com/platform/) , the system of record for trusted software artifacts and AI assets, today announced the dates and speaker line-up for its


[EveryOps Day Mumbai](https://events.jfrog.com/everyops-day-mumbai-india/) event taking place Friday, May 15, 2026, at the JW Marriott Mumbai Juhu. As Indian enterprises race to deploy AI agents across their software pipelines, a critical que


stion remains: how do you move faster without losing control? JFrog’s EveryOps Day Mumbai will help surface the answers with business leaders, software security, and AI experts from some of India’s leading enterprises.


“AI is unlockin


g extreme velocity in software delivery, but it is also introducing new risks at scalethat traditional DevOps and security controls were never designed to handle,” said **Sunny Rao, SVP APAC, JFrog.** “EveryOps Day is where India’s leading enterprises come to solve these problems together, exploring practical ways to unify development, security, and AI into a single, trusted software delivery practice.”
