# JFrog Announces Inclusion in Russell 3000 Index

**Sunnyvale, Calif., June 2, 2026** –[JFrog Ltd](https://jfrog.com/?utm_source=businesswire&utm_medium=investor&utm_campaign=russell3k&utm_content=pr) . (“JFrog”) (Nasdaq: FROG), the Liquid Software Company and creators of the[JFrog Software Supply Chain Platform](https://jfrog.com/software-supply-chain-platform/?utm_source=businesswire&utm_medium=investor&utm_campaign=russell3k&utm_content=pr) , the system of record for trusted software artifacts, binaries, and AI assets, today announced it has been selected to be included in Russell 3000 Index as part of the 2026 Russell indexes reconstitution. This inclusion will become effective when the U.S. market closes on June 26, 2026.


Inclusion in the Russell 3000 Index means automatic inclusion in the large-cap Russell 1000 Index or small-cap Russell 2000 Index as well as the appropriate growth and value style indexes. Investors can follow updates expected to be provided by FTSE Russell on May 29, June 5, June 12, and June 18.
