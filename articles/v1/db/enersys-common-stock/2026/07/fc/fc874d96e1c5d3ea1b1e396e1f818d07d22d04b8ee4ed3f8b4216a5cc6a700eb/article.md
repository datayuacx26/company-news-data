# EnerSys Surpasses 4 Million Hawker® ARMASAFE™ Plus 6TAGM Batteries Sold Worldwide, Marking Two Decades of Proven Field Performance

# EnerSys Surpasses 4 Million Hawker® ARMASAFE™ Plus 6TAGM Batteries Sold Worldwide, Marking Two Decades of Proven Field Performance


**READING, PENNSYLVANIA — March 23, 2026 —**[EnerSys](https://www.enersys.com/en/) **** (NYSE: ENS), a global leader in stored energy solutions for industrial applications, has surpassed 4 million Hawker® ARMASAFE™ Plus 6TAGM batteries sold worldwide. Engineered with proprietary Absorbed Glass Mat (AGM) Thin Plate Pure Lead (TPPL) technology, the maintenance-free battery delivers higher starting power, long service life, deep cycle capability, and superior reliability across demanding industrial and military applications. EnerSys will celebrate the innovation behind this state-of-the-art battery solution at the 2026 AUSA Global Force Symposium & Exposition in booth #722 starting Tuesday, March 24, 2026.


“Since 2002, Hawker® ARMASAFE™ Plus batteries have delivered dependable starting power and resilient energy storage to warfighters around the globe,” said Mark Matthews, Chief Technology Officer and President Specialty Global at EnerSys. “Reaching 4 million batteries sold reflects over two decades of proven reliability in the field. Our exclusive TPPL technology continues to set the standard for rugged durability, fast recharge, and long service life, helping soldiers operate with confidence when it matters most.”


Hawker® 6TAGM batteries are ideal for a wide range of military vehicles and auxiliary equipment including tactical vehicles and generators, All Terrain Vehicles (ATVs), controlled remote weapon stations, and renewable energy storage. Backed by an industry-leading warranty and tested to meet MIL-PRF-32143C requirements, additional benefits and features of the Hawker® ARMASAFE™ Plus 6TAGM battery include:


- 1,225 Cold Cranking Amps (CCA)
- 240-minute Reserve Capacity (RC)
- Up to 5-year service life
- 30-month shelf life at 77°F (25°C)
- Operating temperature range from -40°F (-40°C) to 176°F (80°C)
- Shipped fully charged
- Non-spillable design
- Easy installation
- Air transportable


The milestone underscores EnerSys’ continued investment in advanced energy storage solutions that support mission-critical operations in defense and industrial markets worldwide.


For more information on Hawker® ARMASAFE™ Plus 6TAGM batteries, visit[https://hawkerbattery.com/](https://hawkerbattery.com/) .


**About EnerSys**


EnerSys is a global leader in stored energy solutions for industrial applications and designs, manufactures, and distributes energy systems solutions and motive power batteries, specialty batteries, battery chargers, power equipment, battery accessories and outdoor equipment enclosure solutions to customers worldwide. The company goes to market through four lines of business: Energy Systems, Motive Power, Specialty and New Ventures. Energy Systems, which combine power conversion, power distribution, energy storage, and enclosures, are used in the telecommunication, broadband and utility industries, uninterruptible power supplies, and numerous applications requiring stored energy solutions. Motive power batteries and chargers are utilized in electric forklift trucks and other industrial electric powered vehicles. Specialty batteries are used in aerospace and defense applications, portable power solutions for soldiers in the field, large over-the-road trucks, premium automotive, medical and security systems applications. New Ventures provides energy storage and management systems for various applications including demand charge reduction, utility back-up power, and dynamic fast charging for electric vehicles. EnerSys also provides aftermarket and customer support services to its customers in over 100 countries through its sales and manufacturing locations around the world.
