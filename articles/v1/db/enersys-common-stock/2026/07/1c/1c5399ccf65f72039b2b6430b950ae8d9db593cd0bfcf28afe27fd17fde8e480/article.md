# EnerSys Introduces DataSafe® HX530T and DataSafe® HX600T Batteries for Modern Data Center UPS Applications

# EnerSys Introduces DataSafe® HX530T and DataSafe® HX600T Batteries for Modern Data Center UPS Applications


**READING, Pa., — February 10, 2026 —**[EnerSys](https://www.enersys.com/en/) **** (NYSE: ENS), a global leader in stored energy solutions for industrial applications, today announced the introduction of the **DataSafe® HX530T** and **DataSafe® HX600T** batteries, new additions to the DataSafe® HX battery range designed for high-rate uninterruptible power supply (UPS) applications in data centers and other mission-critical environments.


The DataSafe® HX battery range has been trusted for more than a decade in backup power systems that require dependable performance and consistent high-rate capability. The new **DataSafe® HX530T** **and** **HX600T** **batteries** extend this portfolio by incorporating true **Thin Plate Pure Lead (TPPL) technology** , providing customers with additional performance options and higher power density within the same familiar footprint and installation approach of equivalent DataSafe® HX batteries.


*“Data centers are experiencing new operating conditions as power density increases, temperatures rise, and cycling patterns become more dynamic,” said John Gagge, VP Business Development at EnerSys. “Our DataSafe® HX battery range has performed reliably in these environments for many years. The new TPPL-based models give customers an additional choice for applications that may require a higher level of resilience while keeping the installation process consistent with the rest of the DataSafe® HX battery family.”*


The DataSafe® HX530T and DataSafe® HX600T batteries are engineered to support short-duration, high-rate UPS discharge requirements and maintain strong recharge performance between utility disturbances. Key features include:


**• True TPPL plate construction** to support consistent high-rate discharge


**• Lower internal resistance** , enabling improved recharge characteristics


**• Stable performance across a broad temperature range**


**• Full compatibility with equivalent DataSafe® HX battery footprints and top-terminal layouts**


**• >99% recyclable materials** , supporting circular manufacturing initiatives


The new models are designed to integrate into existing UPS architectures without changes to cabinets, racks, cabling, or installation procedures, offering a practical path for customers seeking additional performance margin in more demanding environments.


“By expanding the DataSafe® HX battery range, EnerSys is giving operators flexibility to choose the solution that best aligns with their runtime expectations, temperature profiles, and sustainability objectives,” said John Gagge.


The DataSafe® HX530T and DataSafe® HX600T batteries are available now in North America through authorized EnerSys distributors and partners.


For more information about EnerSys and its full line of products, systems, and support, visit[www.enersys.com](https://www.enersys.com/en/) or contact your local EnerSys representative.
