# Listen to Shania Twain's SiriusXM Concert at Racket NYC

[Shania Twain](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/shania-twain) returned to where it all began for an intimate, one-night-only SiriusXM concert at Racket NYC, and now you can hear the exclusive performance on[Little Miss Twain Radio](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/little-miss-twain-radio) .


[Shania Twain Concert Exclusive show at Racket NYC Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogLittleMissTwainRadio)


The special concert premieres Monday, July 27 at 6pm ET on Little Miss Twain Radio (Ch. 105), with additional rebroadcasts on the channel and streaming on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogLittleMissTwainRadio) .


- **Related:[Listen to Lainey Wilson’s Exclusive Small Stage Series Concert](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/lainey-wilson-small-stage-series)**


Recorded in front of a packed crowd of SiriusXM subscribers and fans, the performance featured beloved hits including “Man! I Feel Like A Woman!,” “Come On Over,” and “You’re Still the One,” alongside songs from Shania’s new album, “Little Miss Twain,” including “Dirty Rosie” and “Stranger Things.” During the show, the five-time GRAMMY winner also shared the personal stories behind several of her new songs, which were inspired by her early years growing up as an aspiring musician in Ontario, Canada.

The concert is part of Little Miss Twain Radio, SiriusXM’s exclusive pop-up channel celebrating the release of Shania’s seventh studio album. The channel features music spanning her legendary career — from country classics and crossover pop hits to memorable collaborations — along with songs by artists who inspired her and exclusive stories behind the music. Little Miss Twain Radio is available on channel 105 in vehicles through August 6 and on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogLittleMissTwainRadio) through August 22.
