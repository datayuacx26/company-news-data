# Shania Twain Launches Little Miss Twain Radio & Performs SiriusXM Concert

Join[Shania Twain](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/shania-twain) for a celebration of her seventh studio album, “Little Miss Twain,” on Little Miss Twain Radio, a pop-up channel on SiriusXM full of new and classic hits, exclusive stories, and more. Plus, join Shania and SiriusXM for an exclusive album release and channel pop-up concert at Racket NYC on Thursday, July 23.


[Little Miss Twain Radio Shania’s hits, stories & influences Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogLittleMissTwainRadio)


## Little Miss Twain Radio


### How to Listen


SiriusXM listeners can find Little Miss Twain Radio on satellite channel 105 from July 23 to August 6, and streaming on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogLittleMissTwainRadio) from July 23 to August 22.


### What You’ll Hear


Little Miss Twain Radio plays all the tracks from the new album, as well as the songs that made Shania a global legend. From country classics to crossover pop hits to her most memorable collaborations, tune in to hear it all, plus music from artists Shania admires, her favorite tracks from her friends, and the stories behind them all.


### About the New Album


“Little Miss Twain” (out July 24) is Shania’s seventh studio album and features 15 new songs:
