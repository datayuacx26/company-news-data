# WWE Radio Features Premium Live Events, ‘Busted Open,’ and More Wrestling Shows

[WWE Radio](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/wwe-radio) — the new official audio home of WWE — on SiriusXM channel 156 is the ultimate destination for WWE fans. Available anytime on car radios and the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogWWERadio) , the year-round channel brings together SiriusXM’s popular daily programming with live audio broadcasts of the biggest WWE events and premier podcast content.


[WWE Radio (Ch. 156) Live WWE events, daily shows & exclusive interviews Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogWWERadio)


## WWE Radio


### How to Listen


WWE Radio is available to SiriusXM listeners nationwide on satellite channel 156 and[on the SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogWWERadio) .


### What You’ll Hear


#### Premium Live Events


WWE Radio delivers live play-by-play audio coverage of all of WWE’s biggest Premium Live Events (PLEs), including:


- WrestleMania®
- SummerSlam®
- Royal Rumble®
- Survivor Series®


#### Flagship TV Programming


The channel features select audio simulcasts of WWE’s weekly flagship television programming:


- [“Raw”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/wwe-raw)
- [“SmackDown”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/wwe-smackdown)
- [“NXT”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/wwe-nxt)


#### Daily Wrestling Programming


WWE Radio is home to the popular daily wrestling programming that SiriusXM has delivered for years:


- [“Busted Open”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/busted-open) hosted by[Dave LaGreca](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/dave-lagreca) , Bully Ray, Mark Henry, and Nic Nemeth
- “Notsam Wrestling Live” hosted by Sam Roberts
- “Cheap Heat Live” with Peter Rosenberg
- “The Hart Beat with Natalya”
- “Off the Ropes” with Raj Dhesi, Mark Henry, and Tommy Dreamer.
- “Busted Open After Dark”


#### Podcasts


Several popular WWE podcasts produced in partnership with Fanatics are a part of the WWE Radio schedule, with episodes airing every day at 4pm ET:


- “What Do You Want to Talk About?” hosted by Cody Rhodes
- “Six Feet Under” with The Undertaker
- “What’s Your Story?” with Steph McMahon
- “WWE Now, Raw Recap” hosted by Sam Roberts and Megan Morant


### SummerSlam


The launch of WWE Radio comes ahead of SummerSlam weekend, when SiriusXM will produce WWE Radio’s first live event broadcast of the two-night event on-site in Minneapolis on Saturday, August 1, and Sunday, August 2. Lead play-by-play Vic Joseph will be joined by WWE Hall of Famer Booker T, as they call the action in and around the ring to SiriusXM listeners.


WWE Radio will broadcast several live shows directly from Minneapolis in the days leading up to SummerSlam. Dave LaGreca, Bully Ray, Mark Henry, Tommy Dreamer, Sam Roberts, and Peter Rosenberg will host from the Minneapolis Convention Center, analyzing upcoming storylines, interviewing WWE personalities and Superstars, and speaking with fans in Minneapolis and across the country.
