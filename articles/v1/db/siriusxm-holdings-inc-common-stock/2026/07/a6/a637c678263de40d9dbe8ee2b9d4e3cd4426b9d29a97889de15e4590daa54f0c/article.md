# LL Cool J Recalls Ol' Dirty Bastard Breaking & Peeing on His Plaques

[LL COOL J](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/ll-cool-j) revisited one of the wildest stories from his storied hip-hop career: the time Ol’ Dirty Bastard broke and urinated on his plaques.


During an episode of “Salute The Sample” on[LL COOL J’s Rock The Bells Radio (Ch. 43)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/rock-the-bells-radio) , Greg Nice shared a memorable quote from the late[Wu-Tang Clan](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/wu-tang-clan) member about creating his unique style.

Then, LL recalled the story of how Chris Lighty called him with shocking news from Chung King Studios.


“For some reason, Ol’ Dirty Bastard just came here, broke all your plaques, and pissed on them,” the “I Can’t Live Without My Radio” rapper said.


LL admitted he had no idea what prompted the incident.


“I said, ‘What’d I do?’ I didn’t even understand it. I couldn’t understand it,” he said. “Loved \[ODB\] ever since.”
