# Hear The Odyssey Cast Event with Matt Damon & Anne Hathaway on Radio Andy

Oscar winners[Matt Damon](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/matt-damon) and[Anne Hathaway](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/anne-hathaway) join[Andy Cohen](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/andy-cohen) for a special “The Odyssey” Cast Event on[Radio Andy (Ch. 102)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/andy-cohen) , sharing behind-the-scenes stories from Christopher Nolan’s epic film, updates on some of Hollywood’s most anticipated projects, and plenty of laughs along the way.

During the conversation, Andy even introduces Matt Damon and Anne Hathaway to Bravo’s newest hit,[“The Real Housewives of Rhode Island,”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/real-housewives) joking that its cast is “as close as we’ve gotten to a Boston Housewives.”


### Matt Damon Shares Behind-the-Scenes “The Odyssey” Stories


Matt Damon recalls filming inside the iconic Trojan Horse, revealing that Christopher Nolan’s plan was simply to “cram into that horse and figure it out.” He also laughs about the internet’s long-running joke that Hollywood keeps casting him in movies where someone has to rescue him, from “Saving Private Ryan” and “The Martian” to “Interstellar,” and now “The Odyssey.”

### Anne Hathaway Shares a “Princess Diaries 3” Update


Anne Hathaway also offers a promising update on “Princess Diaries 3,” revealing the team has had a “story breakthrough” and is moving in a new direction with the script. She adds that she’ll film a new movie before turning her attention to the beloved franchise.
