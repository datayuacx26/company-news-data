# Elizabeth Hurley on Dating Billy Ray Cyrus and Meeting Miley Cyrus

Elizabeth Hurley is opening up about why her relationship with Billy Ray Cyrus works and sharing what it was like meeting[Miley Cyrus](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/miley-cyrus) .


[Elizabeth Hurley Andy Cohen Live Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogElizabethHurleyRadioAndy)


During her appearance on[Radio Andy’s “Andy Cohen Live,”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/radio-andy) Elizabeth said she and the country star have more in common than many people realize.


### Why Elizabeth Hurley and Billy Ray Cyrus Are a Good Match


“We are remarkably similar in strangely different ways,” the actress told Andy.


Elizabeth explained that although they come from different backgrounds, they’ve both spent their lives in show business and are happiest in the countryside. She said they enjoy birdwatching together while she gardens and Billy Ray sits under a tree playing guitar.


### Elizabeth Hurley Opens Up About Miley Cyrus


The “Austin Powers: International Man of Mystery” star also revealed she has met Miley Cyrus.


“She’s adorable,” Elizabeth said.


Although she’d never met Miley before dating Billy Ray, Elizabeth said her son was a huge “Hannah Montana” fan and introduced her to Miley’s music years earlier.
