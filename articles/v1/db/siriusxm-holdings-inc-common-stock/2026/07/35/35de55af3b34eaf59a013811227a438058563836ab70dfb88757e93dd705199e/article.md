# Celebrate Rita Marley’s 80th Birthday with Special Tributes on Bob Marley’s Tuff Gong Radio

[Bob Marley’s Tuff Gong Radio (Ch. 19)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/tuff-gong) is celebrating Rita Marley’s 80th birthday with the “Thank You Jah 80” celebration, airing all weekend on Tuff Gong Radio and the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogRitaMarleyBirthdayTuffGongRadio) .


[Bob Marley’s Tuff Gong Radio Bob’s hits, family music & more Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogRitaMarleyBirthdayTuffGongRadio)


The special programming honors Nana Rita with exclusive new music, live performances from members of the Marley family and acclaimed reggae artists, specialty shows, and highlights from previous birthday celebrations.


### Exclusive premiere tracks


- Rita Marley — “Strength of Adam’s Rib”
- Sheldon Shepherd — “Thank You Jah” (dub poetry tribute to Nana Rita)


### Live performances from the “Thank You Jah 80” Celebration Concert in Jamaica


- Tessanne Chin
- Nadine Sutherland
- Romain Virgo
- Alaine
- McKada


### Additional Live Performances


- Sharon Marley
- Skip Marley
- Soul-Rebel Marley
- Zion Marley


### Specialty programming


- In My House
- Tuff Classics


Listeners can also hear highlights from previous Rita Marley Birthday Concerts and birthday tributes throughout the weekend.
