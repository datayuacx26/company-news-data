# Poker Pro Nick Schulman to Launch New SiriusXM Show


July 16, 2026 1:50pm EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_a3996f3278f85544c6b059350c137974/siriusxm/news/2026-07-16_Poker_Pro_Nick_Schulman_to_Launch_New_SiriusXM_2280.pdf)


***“Poker Weekly with Nick Schulman” debuts Saturday, July 18*** ******


***Six-week series will air exclusively on Mad Dog Sports Radio channel***


**NEW YORK – July 16, 2026 –** SiriusXM announced today that World Poker Tour champion and 8-time World Series of Poker bracelet winner Nick Schulman will host a new poker-focused show – “Poker Weekly with Nick Schulman” – exclusively on SiriusXM.


The six-episode weekly series will debut this Saturday, July 18 (8:00 pm ET) and air exclusively on SiriusXM’s[Mad Dog Sports Radio](https://sxm.app.link/MDSRonSXM) (channel 82). Listeners across North America can tune in to hear Schulman deliver tips for players of various levels, tell inside stories from his 20+ year career as a pro, and share his insights into the big poker events happening around the world.


"I’m honored to be on SiriusXM talking about poker, a game that means so much to me and so many of us,” said Schulman. “Having a platform like this helps legitimize poker being a game of skill, and it means a lot to me to be a part of that."


Schulman entered the world of professional poker at age 19 and two years later, he became the youngest ever winner of a World Poker Tour (WPT) event when he claimed the 2005 WPT World Poker Finals. The victory earned him a $2,167,500 prize, then a record for a regular season event on the World Poker Tour.


Schulman has won eight World Series of Poker bracelets, considered the most coveted non-monetary prize a poker player can win. His total career live tournament winnings to date exceed $26 million. In 2025, Schulman was inducted into the Poker Hall of Fame.
