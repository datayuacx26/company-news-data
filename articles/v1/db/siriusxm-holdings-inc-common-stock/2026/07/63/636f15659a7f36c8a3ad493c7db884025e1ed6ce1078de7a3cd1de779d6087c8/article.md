# Grateful Dead Channel Celebrates Jerry Garcia’s ‘Days Between’ With Bobby Weir’s Final Performance and More

[Grateful Dead Channel (Ch. 23)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/grateful-dead-channel) celebrates the life and legacy of Jerry Garcia during “The Days Between,” August 1–9, marking the time between Jerry’s birth and his passing.


[Grateful Dead Channel Live concerts, exclusive shows & more Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogTheDaysBetweenGratefulDeadChannel)


This year’s celebration features special programming throughout the week, highlighted by a special encore of Dead & Company – 8/3/25: Bobby Weir’s Final Performance on its one-year anniversary. Dead Heads can also enjoy guest DJs, exclusive specials, classic Jerry Garcia concerts, and a preview of the 2026 Grateful Dead Meet-Up at the Movies. See details below.


### Dead & Company Encore


Monday, August 3 at 7pm ET


Celebrate the one-year anniversary of the final Dead & Company performance with a special encore broadcast of Dead & Company – 8/3/25:[Bobby Weir](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/bob-weir-grateful-dead) ‘s Final Performance.

Airs August 1, 4, 6 & 8

Airs August 3, 4, 5, 7 & 9

### Grateful Dead Meet-Up at the Movies Preview


Airs August 4, 5, 7 & 8


Grateful Dead Channel host David Lemieux previews the 2026 Grateful Dead Meet-Up at the Movies, featuring the complete June 17, 1991, concert from Giants Stadium plus an exclusive sneak peek at “Shakedown Street” from the upcoming *Merriweather 6/30/85* release.


### 2026 Grateful Dead Meet-Up at the Movies


August 8 & 10 at more than 500 theaters nationwide


Celebrate the 35th anniversary of an iconic Grateful Dead show as it returns to the big screen. Join Dead Heads in your neighborhood—and around the world—for the 2026 Grateful Dead Meet-Up at the Movies, featuring the complete June 17, 1991, Giants Stadium concert.


As a bonus, the screening also includes an exclusive sneak preview of the legendary “Shakedown Street” from the upcoming *Merriweather 6/30/85* release and a special introduction by Grateful Dead Legacy Manager, audio archivist, and Grateful Dead Channel host David Lemieux.
