# Hear Michelle Branch's The Spirit Room 25th Anniversary Performance on SiriusXM

Michelle Branch celebrated the 25th anniversary of her breakthrough album, “The Spirit Room,” with an exclusive performance in front of a live audience on[PopRocks (Ch. 6)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/poprocks) .


[Michelle Branch LIVE Celebrating 25 years of “The Spirit Room” Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogMichelleBranchSpiritRoom-PopRocks)


Recorded at the SiriusXM Los Angeles studios, “Michelle Branch LIVE” featured unforgettable performances of “All You Wanted,” “Breathe,” “Everywhere,” and “The Game of Love.”


Between songs, Michelle reflected on the enduring impact of “The Spirit Room” and shared the story behind collaborating with The New Radicals on a new version of “The Game of Love.” She also explained why revisiting the hit alongside one of her longtime musical inspirations made the project especially meaningful.
