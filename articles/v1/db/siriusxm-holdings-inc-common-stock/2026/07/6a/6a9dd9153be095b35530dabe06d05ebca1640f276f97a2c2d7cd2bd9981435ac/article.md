# Hear Morgan Wallen’s New Song 'Been By Now' First on SiriusXM

[Morgan Wallen](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/morgan-wallen) is giving SiriusXM listeners an exclusive first listen to his brand-new single, “Been By Now,” before it’s released, along with exclusive commentary about the song.


[Morgan Wallen Radio Curated by Morgan Wallen Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogMorganWallenBeenByNowPremiere?source=streaming_banner)


### Tune In for the Exclusive Premiere


#### How to Listen


Beginning Thursday, July 23, at 11pm ET, tune in to[Morgan Wallen Radio (Ch. 64)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/morgan-wallen-radio-siriusxm) in your car and on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogMorganWallenBeenByNowPremiere?source=in_text) to hear the exclusive premiere of “Been By Now,” plus Morgan’s commentary about the track, for one hour ahead of the song’s release on Friday, July 24.

The premiere continues SiriusXM’s ongoing partnership with Morgan, whose year-round channel features his biggest hits and fan favorites, plus hand-picked songs from his musical influences, live performances, and exclusive shows curated by the country superstar.
