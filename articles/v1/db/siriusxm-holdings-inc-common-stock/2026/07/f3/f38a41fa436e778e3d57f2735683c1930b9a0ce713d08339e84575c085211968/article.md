# John Mayer to Perform SiriusXM Concert at Stephen Talkhouse in the Hamptons

[John Mayer](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/john-mayer) will perform an exclusive SiriusXM concert at Stephen Talkhouse in Amagansett, New York, on Wednesday, August 26, brought to you by Uber. The special performance continues SiriusXM’s tradition of bringing music’s biggest artists to the intimate Hamptons venue for unforgettable live shows.

### How to Listen to John Mayer’s Concert


During this can’t-miss show, John Mayer, joined by his full band, will perform a career-spanning set featuring the songs that made him a global superstar. Before the performance,[Andy Cohen](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/andy-cohen) will sit down with Mayer for an exclusive conversation, giving listeners a behind-the-scenes look before he takes the stage.


- **Related:[Andy Cohen Hosts John Mayer’s ‘Grateful Dead Listening Party’ on SiriusXM](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/andy-cohen-grateful-dead-listening-party)**


The concert will air on[Life with John Mayer (Ch. 4)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/life-with-john-mayer) on Saturday, August 29, at 8pm ET and be available on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogJohnMayerConcertLifeJohnMayer) . Before John Mayer takes the stage, Andy’s exclusive pre-show interview will air on[Radio Andy (Ch. 102)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/radio-andy) on Friday, August 28, at 10am ET and will also be available on the SiriusXM app.


The performance is the latest in SiriusXM’s celebrated series of exclusive concerts at Stephen Talkhouse. Previous performers at the iconic Hamptons venue include[Brandi Carlile](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/listen-to-brandi-carliles-intimate-small-stage-series-performance-on-the-spectrum) ,[Coldplay](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/coldplay-secret-show-siriusxm) ,[Dave Matthews](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/hear-dave-matthews-exclusive-small-stage-series-set-on-dave-matthews-band-radio) ,[Ed Sheeran](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/ed-sheeran-hamptons-concert-photos) ,[Jelly Roll](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/jelly-roll-hamptons) ,[The Killers](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/hear-the-killers-perform-an-exclusive-concert-live-in-the-hamptons-for-siriusxm) ,[Metallica](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/maximum-metallica) , and[Mumford & Sons](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/mumford-sons-to-perform-exclusive-concert-in-the-hamptons-for-siriusxm-pandora) .
