# Hear Dwayne Johnson's Moana: Voices Across the Ocean Special on Disney Hits

Go behind the music of *Moana: Voices Across the Ocean* when an exclusive track-by-track special premieres Monday, August 10 at 12pm ET on SiriusXM’s[Disney Hits (Ch. 133)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/disney-hits) .


[Moana: Voices Across the Ocean Track-by-Track Featuring Dwayne Johnson Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogMoanaVoicesAcrossNation-DisneyHits)


Hosted by Dwayne Johnson, the special features executive producers Lauren Hashian, Tiana Nonosina Liufau, and Kayla Faʻamaligi as they guide listeners through the companion album inspired by Disney’s live-action *Moana* . Along the way, they introduce each artist, explore the meaning behind every song, and celebrate the Pacific voices and cultures that shaped the project.


During the special, Johnson also shares how the album began with an idea he pitched to Disney: to create a companion record that would shine a global spotlight on talented Polynesian artists who “sing from the heart and… from the soul.” The result is a collection featuring more than 25 artists performing in languages from across the Polynesian Triangle, including Samoan, Tongan, Tahitian, and more.


Listeners will also hear the creative team discuss how music preserves genealogy, storytelling, and cultural traditions, making *Moana: Voices Across the Ocean* more than an album — it’s a celebration of Polynesian heritage and the artists bringing those stories to audiences around the world.


Listen to the premiere of “ *Moana: Voices Across the Ocean* Track-by-Track” on Monday, August 10 at 12pm ET, with rebroadcasts throughout the week. The full special is also be available to stream anytime on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogMoanaVoicesAcrossNation-DisneyHits) .


Plus, starting Wednesday, August 12, watch the exclusive interview from the special, featuring Dwayne Johnson, Lauren Hashian, Tiana Nonosina Liufau, and Kayla Faʻamaligi as they discuss the inspiration behind the album, the artists, and the stories that shaped *Moana: Voices Across the Ocean* . Then tune in to Disney Hits (Ch. 133) on car radios and the SiriusXM app to hear the complete track-by-track special, featuring every song from the album.
