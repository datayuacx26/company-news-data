# Celebrate Elvis Week 2026 with Exclusive Specials on SiriusXM

[Elvis Radio (Ch. 76)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/elvis-radio) is honoring the life, music, and enduring legacy of Elvis Presley during Elvis Week 2026 with exclusive countdowns, unforgettable concerts, behind-the-scenes stories, the annual Candlelight Vigil from Graceland, and more — all celebrating The King of Rock & Roll.


[Elvis Radio Broadcasting live from Graceland Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogElvisWeek)


Now through August 16, listen to the special programming below throughout the week on Elvis Radio, or stream it all anytime on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogElvisWeek) .
