# Megyn Kelly’s ‘Get Closer’ Returns to SiriusXM With Special Guests and Listener Call-Ins

Megyn Kelly, host of[“The Megyn Kelly Show,”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/the-megyn-kelly-show) is bringing back her exclusive SiriusXM series “Get Closer with Megyn Kelly” this August. Each week, listeners will hear the show’s standard one-hour podcast episode followed by an exclusive second hour available only to SiriusXM subscribers, featuring special guests, live conversations, and listener call-ins.

## Get Closer with Megyn Kelly


### How to Listen


“Get Closer with Megyn Kelly” features candid conversations that go beyond the headlines, exploring politics, media, culture, current events, and the personal stories shaping today’s biggest debates.


Tune in live on SiriusXM’s The Megyn Kelly Channel (Ch. 111) on car radios and the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogMegynKellyChannel) each week in August (see full schedule below). Episodes will also be available on the SiriusXM app after they premiere.
