# Little Steven’s Underground Garage Honors Vinny Pastore With a Special Tribute

[Little Steven’s Underground Garage (Ch. 21)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/underground-garage) is remembering the life and legacy of Vinny Pastore with a special tribute celebrating his love of music, unforgettable sense of humor, and lasting impact.


[Vinny Pastore Tribute Exclusive special on Underground Garage Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogVinnyPastoreUndergoundGarage)


Best known to many as “Big Pussy” Bonpensiero from *The Sopranos* , Vinny was also a beloved member of the SiriusXM family.


Credit: Renee Niebylski


He hosted SiriusXM’s first-ever live series, *The Wise Guys* , on Raw Dog Radio, and frequently stopped by channels across the platform as a guest DJ, sharing stories, songs, and plenty of laughs with listeners.


Now, Underground Garage invites fans to spend an hour paying tribute to one of the sweetest guys to ever step behind a SiriusXM microphone. The exclusive special premiered Friday, August 7 at 3pm ET and will rebroadcast on Saturday, August 8 at 7pm ET and Sunday, August 9 at 11am ET.


The special is also available to stream now on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogVinnyPastoreUndergoundGarage) . In the meantime, enjoy a memorable clip from *The Wise Guys* below.
