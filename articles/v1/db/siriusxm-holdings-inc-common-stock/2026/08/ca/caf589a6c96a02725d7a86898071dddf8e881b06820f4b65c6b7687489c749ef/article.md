# Harry Jowsey Defends Alex Cooper’s Marriage, Talks Nick Viall Tension, and More

During an appearance on SiriusXM’s[“Page Six Radio,”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/page-six-radio) reality star Harry Jowsey praised[Alex Cooper](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/alex-cooper) and Matt Kaplan’s marriage and pushed back on online speculation about their relationship, saying those who question it don’t know them personally.

“Those people suck that talk about them,” Harry said. “If you know them on a personal level, they are genuinely the greatest people and they’ve been nothing but perfect to me.”


Jowsey also shared that watching Alex and Matt work together has been inspiring.


“Alex will have the idea, ‘I’m going to go to the moon,'” he said. “And he’s like, ‘Cool. We’re going to build the rocket ship.’ That’s how we go together. They’re the best working dynamic possible.”


He added that he even encouraged Alex to share more of her relationship with fans, saying he wishes people could see “how much he loves her.”


During the interview, Harry also spoke out about the advice Alex gave him while filming his new reality show (“Let’s Marry Harry”), his tension with[Nick Viall](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/the-bachelor-nick-viall-defends-corinne-i-like-to-be-surprised) , his infamous appearance on “The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives,” getting penis botox.
