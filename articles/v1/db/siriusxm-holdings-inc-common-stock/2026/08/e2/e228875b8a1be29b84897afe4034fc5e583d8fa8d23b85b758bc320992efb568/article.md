During her return to the show, the Oscar-winning actress opens up about privately caring for her late partner, Bryan Randall, during his battle with ALS in a deeply personal conversation.


Sandra reveals that Bryan asked her to keep his illness private, leaving her unable to confide in even some of her closest friends as she navigated his diagnosis during the pandemic. Bryan died in 2023 following a private three-year battle with ALS.
