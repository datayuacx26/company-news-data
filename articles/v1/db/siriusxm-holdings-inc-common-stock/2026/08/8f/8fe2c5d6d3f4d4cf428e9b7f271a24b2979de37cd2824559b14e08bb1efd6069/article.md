## About *NIMRODS*


Written and directed by Lee Kirk, *NIMRODS* is a coming-of-age road comedy inspired by Green Day’s early years touring in a van before the band’s breakthrough success. The film follows three teenage friends who mistakenly believe their garage band has been invited to open for Green Day on New Year’s Eve, sending them on a wild cross-country adventure from Kansas City to Los Angeles.


The film stars Mason Thames, Kylr Coffman, Ryan Foust, Jenna Fischer, Angela Kinsey, Fred Armisen, Bobby Lee, Sean Gunn, and Mckenna Grace, while Billie Joe Armstrong, Mike Dirnt, and Tré Cool appear as themselves and also serve as producers.
