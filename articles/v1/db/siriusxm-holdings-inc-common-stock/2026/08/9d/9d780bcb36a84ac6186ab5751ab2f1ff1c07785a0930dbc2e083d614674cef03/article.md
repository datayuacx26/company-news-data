# Listen to Phoebe Bridgers’ Lost Weekend Album + Exclusive Stories

Spend a *Lost Weekend* with[Phoebe Bridgers](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/phoebe-bridgers) on[SiriusXMU (Ch. 35)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/siriusxmu) . In celebration of the release of *Lost Weekend* , her first solo album in six years, Phoebe is taking over the channel for a special weekend featuring music from across her career and exclusive stories.


[Phoebe Bridgers’ Lost Weekend on SiriusXMU Tune in from August 14-16 Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogPhoebeBridgersLostWeekendSiriusXMU)


### How to Listen


Tune in to SiriusXMU (Ch. 35) from Friday, August 14 through Sunday, August 16 in your car or on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogPhoebeBridgersLostWeekendSiriusXMU) to hear *Phoebe Bridgers’ Lost Weekend* .


Plus, listen to previous episodes of[Phoebe Bridgers’ exclusive SiriusXMU series, Saddest Factory Radio ,](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/saddest-factory-radio) anytime on the SiriusXM app.


### What You’ll Hear


Throughout *Phoebe Bridgers’ Lost Weekend* on SiriusXMU, hear songs from the new album alongside favorites from her solo discography and side projects, including[boygenius](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/boygenius) and Better Oblivion Community Center.


Phoebe will also play DJ, sharing handpicked songs from some of the artists she’s listening to right now, including This Is Lorelei, ear, Alex G, and more.


### Phoebe Bridgers Returns With *Lost Weekend*


Out Friday, August 14, *Lost Weekend* marks Phoebe’s first solo album since 2020’s acclaimed Punisher. See the full 16-song tracklist below.


The album follows a six-year stretch that included Phoebe’s work with Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus in boygenius. The trio’s 2023 album, *the record* , earned boygenius three GRAMMY Awards.
