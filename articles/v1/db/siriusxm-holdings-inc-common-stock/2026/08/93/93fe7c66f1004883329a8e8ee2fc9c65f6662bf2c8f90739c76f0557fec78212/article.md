# Kurupt Reveals Eminem Paid for His Rehab During Sobriety Journey

During an appearance on[Shade 45’s “Sway in the Morning,”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/sway-calloway) legendary West Coast rapper Kurupt revealed that[Eminem](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/eminem) covered the cost of his rehab after learning Kurupt was struggling with addiction.


[Sway in the Morning Shade 45 (Ch. 45) Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogTagPage-Shade45)


Kurupt explained that it all began with a call from[Dr. Dre](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/dr-dre) , who reached out to offer encouragement and let him know he wasn’t alone.


- **Related:[Dr. Dre Opens Up About Eminem, His Upbringing, and More on SiriusXM](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/dr-dre-james-corden)**


“It was Dr. Dre that called me, literally, and was just giving his support,” Kurupt said. “It was Em who came to the table like, ‘I’m going to put Kurupt with my folks and put some money into getting him into a rehab situation.'”


Kurupt said Eminem wanted him to receive treatment privately, away from the spotlight.


“You know these things cost bread, so Em put the cheese up for it,” Kurupt said. “He put me with Em’s folks, so that was real special to me.”


The rapper said he was deeply moved by the gesture, especially because it came without hesitation.
