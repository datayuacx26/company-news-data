# Stream Sebastian Maniscalco's We Got Company with Jerry Seinfeld

[Sebastian Maniscalco](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/sebastian-maniscalco-siriusxm-comedy-channel) is getting some very good company. The record-breaking comedian is launching *We Got Company* , a new monthly SiriusXM series featuring intimate, long-form conversations with some of the world’s most fascinating people.


[We Got Company Sebastian’s long-form conversations with guests Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogWeGotCompanySebastianManiscalcoComedyRadio)


The series premieres Thursday, August 20 at 12pm ET with comedy icon[Jerry Seinfeld](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/jerry-seinfeld) as Sebastian’s first guest. Watch the trailer for the show above now.


### Sebastian Maniscalco’s *We Got Company*


On each episode of *We Got Company* , Sebastian will sit down one-on-one with leading entertainers, musicians, chefs, athletes, and other notable guests for conversations that go beyond the surface.


Sebastian will explore his guests’ roots, their paths to success, and what they’re pursuing next.


Following the premiere with Jerry Seinfeld, upcoming guests include Ray Romano, Robby Hoffman, David Chang, and more.


*We Got Company* joins the growing lineup on[Sebastian Maniscalco’s Comedy Radio (Ch. 99)](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/sebastian-maniscalcos-comedy-radio) , SiriusXM’s exclusive comedy destination curated and headlined by Sebastian. The 24/7 channel also features his weekly flagship program, *The Sebastian Maniscalco Show* , alongside stand-up from some of today’s biggest names in comedy.


### How to Listen to *We Got Company*


Hear the premiere of *We Got Company* with Jerry Seinfeld on Thursday, August 20 at 12pm ET on Sebastian Maniscalco’s Comedy Radio (Ch. 99) and on demand on the[SiriusXM app](https://sxm.app.link/BlogWeGotCompanySebastianManiscalcoComedyRadio) .


Full video episodes will debut one week later on Sebastian Maniscalco’s[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@sebastiancomedy) . Audio episodes will also be available through *The Sebastian Maniscalco Show* feed wherever podcasts are available.
