# FIFA World Cup 2026™ To Air Live on FOX Sports on SiriusXM and SiriusXM FC

# FIFA World Cup 2026™ To Air Live on FOX Sports on SiriusXM and SiriusXM FC


June 08, 2026 12:00pm EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_a3996f3278f85544c6b059350c137974/siriusxm/news/2026-06-08_FIFA_World_Cup_2026_To_Air_Live_on_FOX_Sports_on_2273.pdf)


*SiriusXM listeners get live broadcasts, powered by FOX Sports, of every FIFA World Cup 2026™ match*


*Former USMNT coach **Bob Bradley** , USMNT member **Walker Zimmerman** and former England player **Wes Brown** sign on with SiriusXM as analysts; They join former USMNT FIFA World Cup* ™ *players **Tony Meola** , **Eric Wynalda** , **Omar Gonzalez** and others on the 24/7 SiriusXM FC channel* **


*More than a dozen current and former players from the U.S. and abroad to be Guest DJs on SiriusXM music channels during the tournament*


**NEW YORK – June 8, 2026 –** SiriusXM will offer listeners across the United States extensive coverage of FIFA World Cup 2026™ - featuring live broadcasts of FOX Sports’ match commentary for all 104 matches happening across the U.S., Canada and Mexico - from the opening match on Thursday, June 11, through the FIFA World Cup Final™ on Sunday, July 19. Additionally, SiriusXM will offer fans extensive coverage on its dedicated soccer channel, SiriusXM FC, as well as special programming on SiriusXM music channels, throughout the tournament.


English-language broadcasts of every match from the tournament will be provided by FOX Sports, with broadcasts airing primarily on[FOX Sports on SiriusXM](https://sxm.app.link/83) (channel 83). When two matches are occurring simultaneously, one match will air on[SiriusXM FC](https://sxm.app.link/SiriusXMFC) (channel 157). All FIFA World Cup 2026™ programming will be available to subscribers on the radios in their cars and on the SiriusXM app.


The U.S. Men’s National Team (USMNT) open their FIFA World Cup 2026™ campaign with their first Group D game against Paraguay on Friday, June 12 at 9:00 pm ET. The USMNT then takes on Australia on Friday, June 19 at 3:00 pm ET and Türkiye on Thursday, June 25 at 10:00 pm ET. All three matches will air live on FOX Sports (SiriusXM channel 83). For a schedule of all upcoming matches and their SiriusXM channels, please visit:[SiriusXM.com/Sports](http://siriusxm.com/Sports) .


In addition to carrying live match broadcasts, SiriusXM will offer listeners in-depth coverage daily throughout the tournament on its 24/7 soccer channel, SiriusXM FC. The SiriusXM FC channel offers live programming morning through night - hosted by an unparalleled team of experts and insiders - that will give fans a way to follow all the latest FIFA World Cup 2026™ news and results every day, whether they are in the car, at home, in the office or on the go.


Joining the SiriusXM FC team as analysts for this year’s tournament are former USMNT head coach **Bob Bradley** , who led the U.S. squad to the Round of 16 in the FIFA World Cup South Africa 2010™, USMNT player **Walker Zimmerman** , who started for the U.S. in every match of their FIFA World Cup Qatar™ campaign, and **Wes Brown** , who played for England in the 2002 and 2010 FIFA World Cup™ tournaments.


Bradley, Zimmerman and Brown join a SiriusXM roster that includes National Soccer Hall of Famers **Tony Meola** and **Eric Wynalda** , who were teammates for the United States in the 1990 and 1994 FIFA World Cup™ tournaments; **Omar Gonzalez** , who played for the U.S. in the FIFA World Cup Brazil 2014™; former U.S. international players **Brian Dunseth** and **Dax McCarty** , and former coaches, executives and journalists from the U.S. and abroad including **Rodney Marsh** , **Adrian Heath** , **Charlie Stillitano** , **Tommy Smyth** , **Tom Rennie** , **Luis Miguel Echegaray** , **Max Bretos** , **Keith Costigan** , **Jessica Charman** , **Melissa Reddy** , **Jack Collins** and **Jason Davis** .


In addition to SiriusXM FC’s soccer coverage, SiriusXM will celebrate the tournament across its many music channels, with more than a dozen current and former players from the U.S. and abroad taking over the mic for special “Global Soccer Guest DJ” sessions. Guest DJs will include current USMNT members **Mark McKenzie** , **Miles Robinson** , **Matt Freese** , **Matt Turner** and **Max Arfsten** , as well as international players **Leonardo Campana** (Ecuador), **Miguel Almiron** (Paraguay), **Kamal Miller** (Canada), **Derrick Etienne Jr.** (Haiti) and several others, including FOX Sports lead studio analyst and National Soccer Hall of Famer **Alexi Lalas** . They will play their favorite tracks and share stories from on and off the pitch on channels including[Hip Hop Nation](https://sxm.app.link/PRlinkHipHopNation) (channel 44),[Caliente](https://sxm.app.link/PRlink-Caliente) (channel 152),[90’s on 9](https://sxm.app.link/90son9PR) (channel 9),[The Heat](https://sxm.app.link/PRlink-TheHeat) (channel 46),[10s Spot](https://sxm.app.link/PRlink-The10sSpot) (channel 11) and more. The Guest DJ sessions will air multiple times on each of these channels throughout the tournament.


The FIFA World Cup 2026™ is set to be the first FIFA World Cup held across three host nations - the United States, Mexico, and Canada - as 48 nations will compete in 104 matches throughout 16 North American venues. In the United States, 11 cities will host matches - Atlanta, Boston, Dallas, Houston, Kansas City, Los Angeles, Miami, New York New Jersey, Philadelphia, San Francisco Bay Area and Seattle.


For more info about FIFA World Cup 2026™ on FOX Sports, visit[Fox Sports Press Pass](https://www.foxsports.com/presspass/) .


For more info on SiriusXM FC’s daily soccer programming, go to[SiriusXM.com/FC](http://www.siriusxm.com/FC) .
