# Watch Stella Lefty Cover Miley Cyrus’ 'The Climb' at SiriusXM

Rising singer-songwriter[Stella Lefty](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/stella-lefty) stopped by SiriusXM Studios for a special performance and conversation, treating fans to live renditions of her breakout songs “Boston” and “I Know I Know,” along with a heartfelt cover of[Miley Cyrus](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/miley-cyrus) ’ “The Climb.”


[Stella Lefty Exclusive performance and interview on SiriusXM Hits 1 Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogStellaLeftyHits1on1?source=streaming_banner)


During her appearance on “ *Hits 1 on 1* ,” Stella sat down with[SiriusXM Hits 1](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/siriusxm-hits-1) host Mikey Piff to discuss her whirlwind rise over the past year. She reflected on growing up in the Chicago suburbs, writing songs inspired by crushes and real-life experiences, and how[Taylor Swift](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/taylor-swift) ‘s debut album first inspired her to pursue music.


Additionally, Stella shared the story behind her viral hit “Boston,” revealing she wrote the chorus in just 15 minutes while getting ready to meet the guy she’s now dating. She admitted she posted the song to an alternate TikTok account “just for fun” before fans quickly embraced it.
