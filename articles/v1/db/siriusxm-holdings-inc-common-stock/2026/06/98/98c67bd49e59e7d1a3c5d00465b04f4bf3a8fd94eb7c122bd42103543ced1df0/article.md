# ‘Toy Story 5 Front Row’: Tom Hanks, Tim Allen, and More Discuss the New Movie

In a special SiriusXM[“Front Row”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/town-hall) conversation hosted by[Julia Cunningham](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/julia-cunningham) , “Toy Story 5” cast members Tom Hanks, Tim Allen, Conan O’Brien, and Greta Lee reflect on the enduring legacy of Disney and Pixar’s beloved series, share stories from the new film, and discuss everything from early Oscar buzz to[Taylor Swift’s new song for “Toy Story 5.”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/taylor-swift-new-song-toy-story)


[Toy Story 5 Front Row Featuring the stars of the movie Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogToyStoryFrontRow?source=streaming_banner)


During the “Toy Story 5 Front Row,” Tom calls the franchise’s success “crazy humbling” and credits the enduring friendship between Woody and Buzz.


“We found out that there was wicked importance in the friendship between Woody and Buzz,” he says.

Tim also recalls that he immediately recognized the original film’s potential because “this friendship could be stick figures. This is such a wonderful story.”


(From L to R) Julia Cunningham, Tom Hanks, Tim Allen, Greta Lee, and Conan O’Brien.


Additionally, the cast reminisces about favorite childhood toys, explains Woody’s famous “bald spot,” and shares why Pixar continues to find new reasons to revisit the world of “Toy Story.”
