# Taylor Sheridan Talks ‘Sons of Anarchy’ and His Hit Shows in ‘Howard Stern Show’ Debut

Taylor Sheridan made his[“The Howard Stern Show”](https://www.siriusxm.com/blog/tag/howard-stern) today (June 30) to discuss his new book, “How To Not Die In Prison: A Survival Guide,” look back at his hit series like “Yellowstone,” “Landman,” “Tulsa King,” and more.


[Taylor Sheridan The Howard Stern Show Listen on the App Listen on the App](https://sxm.app.link/BlogTaylorSheridanHSS?source=streaming_banner)


During the episode, Taylor also opens up about how his contract negotiation after Season 2 of “Sons of Anarchy” led him to quit acting and pursue screenwriting:


“I’m an actor on this show, making scale and I’m literally would leave the set of that show and go to my other job because I didn’t make enough on that show to pay my rent and and live. So, after season two, I told them, I said, guys, I’m not coming back and doing this again for this price. I’m just not doing it.”
