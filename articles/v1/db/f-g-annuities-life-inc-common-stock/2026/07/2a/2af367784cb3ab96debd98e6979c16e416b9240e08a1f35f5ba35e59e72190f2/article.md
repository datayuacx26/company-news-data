# F&G Annuities & Life Announces Ron Barrett as Chief Distribution Officer

F&G has recently aligned under a single sales and distribution strategy for our life and annuity products. This important step strengthens our ability to work together with our distribution partners, supporting the financial aspirations of their clients with products for protection and retirement.


As part of this alignment, we are happy to announce that Ron Barrett has been promoted to Chief Distribution Officer. Ron has served as an executive in the financial services and insurance industry for more than 25 years, developing sales organizations for some of the largest carriers before joining F&G in 2014. His experience managing distribution, sales, and marketing, with a focus on fixed annuities and life insurance, provides a strong foundation for his new role.


Conor Murphy, F&G’s President and Chief Financial Officer, commented “Ron is a seasoned distribution leader who has spent the past 11 years at F&G building the critical relationships essential to supporting our growing business. Known as a collaborative leader who is passionate about F&G’s partners and policyholders, he brings deep expertise and a proven track record to this expanded role.”


As Chief Distribution Officer, Ron will advance our distribution strategy, strengthening relationships and empowering our partners to deliver F&G’s financial products to consumers across the country.


## About F&G


Established in 1959, F&G is headquartered in Des Moines, Iowa, and offers unique life insurance and annuity solutions to meet individual financial security needs through a national network of financial professionals. Our products have protected over 1,000,000 people across the United States.
