# Dan Hobbs to Speak at the Business Intelligence and Analytics Summit!

[Dennis van Bregt](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dennis-van-bregt-2a868b1/) , Head of Data and AI at[allnex](https://www.linkedin.com/company/allnex/) will be co-presenting a keynote session with[Dan Hobbs](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dan-hobbs-258998ab/) , CEO at[Protex AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/protex-ai/) , at the Business Intelligence and Analytics Summit!


📅 Join us on July 10th-11th at the[Radisson Blu Hotel Amsterdam Airport](https://www.linkedin.com/company/radisson-blu-hotel-amsterdam-airport/) .💡


Keynote Topic: Implementing NLP in BI and Analytics.


ℹ️ More about Dennis- He has a robust background in applying AI/ML while also focusing on the data foundation, enabling AI/ML, and deploying it at scale. He is a recognized leader in data, governance, and analytics, known for building successful, engaged, and committed teams. Dennis studied data science at the University of Rotterdam and has accumulated extensive experience working in finance, logistics/supply chain, ERP, and master data management. For the past decade, he has specialized in data, governance, analytics, and AI/ML, with a primary focus on the digital side of the business.


ℹ️ More about Dan- Recently named on the Forbes 30 under 30 list, serial technology entrepreneur Dan Hobbs co-founded Protex AI with the vision of harnessing the best in AI technology to help EHS teams be more proactive and contribute to a zero-harm work environment. Protex AI uses camera software to identify areas of risk in an organisation before accidents occur. Using computer vision, the platform enables companies to effectively identify and correct the non-compliant behaviour trends that lead to accidents. Dan is an advocate for all things big data and EHS tech innovation, regularly contributing at a number of international online and offline events on these very subjects.


🚀 Don't miss this opportunity to harness the power of Natural Language Processing in Business Intelligence! Learn from top industry experts how to transform complex data into meaningful insights that drive user engagement and decision-making.
