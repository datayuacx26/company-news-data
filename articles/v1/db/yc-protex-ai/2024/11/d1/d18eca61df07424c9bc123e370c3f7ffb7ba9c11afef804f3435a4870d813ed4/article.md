# Protex AI Named a Cool Vendor in the 2024 Gartner® Cool Vendors™ in Logistics and Robotics Technology Research

Protex AI, a leader in[AI-driven workplace safety solutions](https://www.protex.ai/product) , has been recognized as a Cool Vendor in the 2024 Gartner Cool Vendors in Logistics and Robotics Technology research *.*


We believe that Protex’s inclusion underscores its innovative application of AI-powered computer vision and generative AI (GenAI) to support proactive safety management, addressing a critical need for safer and more productive workplaces. Protex’s safety solution integrates seamlessly with existing CCTV infrastructure, allowing enterprise organizations to reduce safety incidents and foster a culture of compliance without compromising privacy.


“We hold the view that being named a Gartner Cool Vendor is a testament to the transformative potential of our technology,” said Ciaran O’Mara, Co-founder and CTO of Protex. “Our mission is to leverage AI to create safer workplaces where technology serves as a proactive partner in safety management. We think that this recognition from Gartner further validates our commitment to pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in workplace safety and automation.”


Protex has formed strategic partnerships with global leaders in logistics, wholesale, and manufacturing, including DHL, Amazon, and Berry Group. By equipping these organizations with real-time monitoring, alerting, and proactive safety measures, Protex empowers teams to maintain safety standards effectively. Recent advancements in Protex’s generative AI capabilities have also introduced seamless natural language reporting and actionable insights, simplifying safety management and making it more accessible for organizations worldwide.
