# Announcing Protex AI Predictive Analytics

**July 14, 2025** – Protex AI, a leader in innovative safety and operational AI solutions, announced the official launch of **Predictive Analytics** , a groundbreaking new product designed to empower EHS and operations teams to identify, understand, and address emerging risks *before* they escalate into incidents.


In today's dynamic operational environments, proactive risk management is paramount. Traditional reactive approaches often fall short in preventing incidents. Protex AI's Predictive Analytics transforms this paradigm by leveraging existing organizational data to forecast potential risks, enabling truly preventative measures and fostering a safer workplace.


**Key Benefits of Protex AI Predictive Analytics:**


- **Proactive Risk Identification:** Move beyond reactive incident response. Predictive Analytics analyzes historical incident, observational, behavioral, and operational data to pinpoint emerging risk patterns and potential hazards before they manifest as incidents.
- **Simplified Insights, No Data Science Required:** Our intuitive platform transforms complex data into easily understandable insights, eliminating the need for specialized data science expertise within your EHS and operations teams. Focus on action, not analysis.
- **Data-Driven Decision Making:** Gain a clear, data-backed understanding of where and how risks are developing. This empowers teams to make informed decisions and allocate resources effectively to mitigate the most critical threats.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Utilize follow-up reports to assess the impact of implemented safety measures, confirm their effectiveness, and maintain continuous focus on critical risks, driving a cycle of ongoing safety enhancement.
- **Leverage Existing Data:** Seamlessly integrate your existing safety and operational data to unlock its full predictive potential. Protex AI's platform is designed to work with the data you already have.


Protex AI Predictive Analytics is now available.
