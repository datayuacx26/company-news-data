# Make UK Partners With Protex AI

*Make UK has partnered with Protex AI, a leader in artificial intelligence solutions for workplace safety, to promote proactive safety management using AI-powered technology.*


A new partnership with Protex AI will build on[Make UK](http://www.makeuk.org/) ’s existing health and safety expertise to provide Make UK members with direct access to AI-powered technology to enable businesses to gain greater visibility of unsafe behaviours in their facilities, helping them make informed safety decisions. By working together, Protex AI and Make UK aim to ensure that manufacturers not only meet regulatory requirements but also foster workplaces where employees feel valued and protected.


Protex AI’s privacy-focused platform seamlessly integrates with existing CCTV infrastructure, harnessing advanced computer vision technology to identify and capture unsafe events for leading global manufacturers, including Berry, Ferrero, Stryker, and others. This information helps health and safety teams to spot trends, understand behaviour, and prioritise the implementation of corrective actions.


Dan Hobbs, Protex AI CEO, comments: “Through this collaboration, UK manufacturers will gain access to powerful AI-driven tools that identify and mitigate risks before they lead to incidents, ultimately supporting a safer, more productive work environment.”


Together, Protex AI and Make UK aim to harness cutting-edge AI technology to elevate safety standards across manufacturing sectors and help organisations achieve a proactive, data-driven approach to health and safety.


Chris Newson, Environment, Health and Safety Director at Make UK says: “By combining Make UK's industry expertise with Protex AI's innovative technology, we are paving the way for a safer, smarter manufacturing future.”
