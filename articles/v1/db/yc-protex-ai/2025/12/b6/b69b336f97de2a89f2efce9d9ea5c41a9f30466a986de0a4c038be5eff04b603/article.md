# Protex AI Wins Prestigious Deloitte Technology Fast 50 Rising Star Award

Protex AI is honored to announce that we have been named the **Rising Star Award** winner at the 2025 Deloitte Technology Fast 50 Awards, in association with Enterprise Ireland.


The Rising Star award recognizes companies that demonstrate exceptional innovation, impressive growth, and strong potential for future inclusion in the main Fast 50 ranking. This accolade highlights Protex AI’s success in leveraging Artificial Intelligence to drive safer workplaces and unlock meaningful operational efficiencies for our clients globally.


Speaking about the win, Protex AI CEO Dan Hobbs stated, "We are thrilled to celebrate receiving this award. The recognition is a testament to the growth of our team and client base, confirming that our mission to enable organizations to use data-driven insights to drive safety is resonating strongly in the market."
