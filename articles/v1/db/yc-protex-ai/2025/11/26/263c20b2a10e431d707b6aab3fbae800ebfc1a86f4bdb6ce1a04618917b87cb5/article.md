# Protex AI Highlighted as an Irish Start-Up 'Living the American Dream' by Silicon Republic

Protex AI has been featured by Silicon Republic in its list of "10 Irish start-ups living the American dream this Thanksgiving," recognizing our successful expansion in the US market.


The article praises Protex AI for developing a computer vision-powered platform that plugs into existing CCTV systems to autonomously capture unsafe events in warehouses, significantly improving safety and efficiency for large organizations. The feature also highlights Protex AI's recent achievement of winning the **Outstanding Achievement in International Growth** award at the Technology Ireland Ibec Industry Awards 2025.
