# AI-powered safety solutions: DHL partners with Protex AI to drive safety innovation in logistics

In a major move towards achieving their Group Strategy 2025, DHL Denmark has joined forces with Protex AI to enhance safety measures and optimize logistics operations. With safety being a top priority for DHL, this collaboration is a significant step towards creating a safer and more efficient working environment.


‍


**Safety First: A Core Element of DPDHL Group Strategy 2025:**


As outlined in the DPDHL Group Strategy 2025, safety holds paramount importance. The organization constantly seeks innovative technologies to revolutionize the logistics industry. With AI emerging as a prominent tech trend, DHL recognizes the potential of deploying[AI and computer vision to enhance safety](https://www.protex.ai/product) in their operations.


‍


**Partnering with Protex AI for Proactive Safety Decision Making:**


In a recent proof of concept conducted in collaboration with DHL Global Forwarding in Denmark, Protex AI showcased their AI-powered technology, enabling proactive safety decision making based on actionable insights. Protex AI's cutting-edge solution provides a comprehensive 24/7 view of sites, enabling teams to identify potential risks and help prevent accidents or injuries before they occur.


‍


**The Functionality of Protex AI's Solution:**


Protex AI's system utilizes computer vision and AI to analyze data and generate proactive safety events. These events can include use cases like near misses or non-compliance with procedures. The technology actively observes and assesses various parameters such as forklift speed, adherence to predefined walkways, proper use of personal protective equipment (PPE), and identification of obstructed pathways.


‍


**Maximizing Workplace Safety Through Data Insights:**


By deploying Protex AI's technology, DHL aims to enhance their understanding of workplace dynamics and potential hazards. Regular reports generated by the system provide valuable insights that enable DHL to make direct changes to improve safety conditions. Importantly, the approach respects individual privacy, as the data collection is fully anonymous and complies with GDPR regulations. The reports obtained help DHL in formulating effective strategies to enhance safety without requiring extensive manual monitoring.


‍


The partnership between Protex AI and DHL signifies a significant step towards creating a safer and more efficient logistics environment. By harnessing the power of AI and computer vision, DHL aims to prevent accidents and promote proactive safety decision making. This collaboration exemplifies DPDHL Group's commitment to implementing cutting-edge technologies to drive growth, enhance safety, and deliver excellence in the digital era.
