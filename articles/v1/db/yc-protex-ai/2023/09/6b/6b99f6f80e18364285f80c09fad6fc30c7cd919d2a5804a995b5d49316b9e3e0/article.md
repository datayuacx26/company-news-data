# Protex AI Announces Strategic Partnership with Benchmark Gensuite

## About the Partnership


Protex AI, the Dublin-based pioneer in AI-driven safety solutions, is thrilled to unveil its latest collaboration with Benchmark Gensuite®, a leading provider of digital Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) and Sustainability solutions.


Benchmark Gensuite® empowers organizations to implement robust, cross-functional digital systems for EHS, Sustainability, and ESG Reporting through a unified digital platform, transcending geographical boundaries and diverse operational profiles.


With its intuitive, best-practice-based process functionality, flexible configurations, and potent extensions, the Benchmark Gensuite® platform has been the go-to choice for companies worldwide, enabling them to manage EHS, Sustainability, Quality, Operational Risk and Compliance, Product Stewardship, and Supply Chain Risks for over two decades. It has now organically integrated cutting-edge ESG disclosure reporting and management solutions.


The collaboration is centered around Protex AI's automated safety risk identification capabilities, driven by computer vision AI and facility CCTV cameras. Paired with Benchmark Gensuite's industry-leading EHS software solutions, boasting impeccable workflows and interfaces, this partnership offers our clients unparalleled value and acceleration. Together, we are poised to revolutionize the landscape of workplace safety.


Dan Hobbs, Co-Founder and CEO of Protex AI, expressed his enthusiasm, stating, "Protex AI is elated to join forces with Benchmark Gensuite's comprehensive solutions platform. Our shared mission to provide proactive and innovative safety technologies aligns seamlessly. This integration empowers Benchmark clients to attain company-wide visibility into risks and make data-informed safety decisions using the marvel of computer vision."

## About Protex AI


Protex AI harnesses the power of cutting-edge computer vision technology to proactively identify and mitigate potential safety hazards within manufacturing facilities, warehouses, and ports. Computer vision is a field of AI that trains computers to comprehend visual information from images and videos, enabling them to “see” as humans do. Through seamless integration of 24/7 camera surveillance, event logs, and safety reports, Protex AI quickly identifies trends in unsafe behaviors, facilitating the prioritization of corrective actions. The result? A safer work environment for all.
