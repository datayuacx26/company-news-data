## Three Distinct Styles


The model homes at Aspire at Holladay Hills were designed in collaboration with **design expert Emmy-winning TV host and author**[Bobby Berk.](https://www.tripointehomes.com/blog/bobbyberk.com) **** Bobby’s designs in Holladay feature three intentional styles to inspire your journey at Aspire from serene escapes to standout spaces.


Timeless and chic, **Textured Traditional** showcases a soft color palette of serene sage and creams with light wood accents, plenty of textiles, and bright finishes. This style offers a modern take on a classic look that turns the home into a peaceful retreat.


Artsy and bold, **Graphic Nouveau** boasts clean lines, rich color, and organic silhouettes. Bobby’s rendition of this style at Aspire features deep green throughout the house accompanied by accents and finishes in black, white, tan, and gold for a modern look.


Playful and retro, **Desert Bauhaus** uses terracotta and earthy blues to bring Southern Utah’s red rock and blue sky to your Holladay interior. Upbeat shapes, statement features, and geometric details bring the home to life with a modern twist on old-school style.
