# The Final Chapter Begins: Discover Timber Trails at Snoqualmie Ridge

# The Final Chapter Begins: Discover Timber Trails at Snoqualmie Ridge


Tri Pointe Homes Washington


•


August 3, 2026


Share this article


Nestled within the breathtaking beauty of Snoqualmie Ridge,[Timber Trails](https://www.tripointehomes.com/wa/greater-seattle-area/timber-trails) offers a rare opportunity to experience a refined Pacific Northwest lifestyle where modern elegance meets effortless comfort. Surrounded by sweeping mountain vistas and just minutes from the iconic Snoqualmie Falls, this intimate enclave invites homeowners into a world where outdoor adventure and small-town charm coexist beautifully.


More than just a new neighborhood, Timber Trails represents something far more significant-it is the final opportunity for new construction within the celebrated Snoqualmie Ridge community, marking the closing chapter of one of the Eastside’s most influential planned communities.


For over two decades, Snoqualmie Ridge has set the standard for connected living, blending thoughtfully designed homes with parks, trails, schools, and retail. Now, with the debut of Timber Trails, Tri Pointe Homes® introduces a limited collection of just 46 homes, an exclusive chance to be part of a legacy that has shaped how people live in the Pacific Northwest.


## **Elevated Living, Designed for Today**


Timber Trails features a collection of three-story townhomes and paired homes designed for the way people live today, open, connected, and effortlessly functional. With floorplans ranging from approximately 1,900 to 3,100 square feet, these homes offer three to four bedrooms, expansive great rooms, and seamless indoor-outdoor living spaces ideal for entertaining or unwinding.


Homebuyers can choose from **to-be-built opportunities** , allowing for personalization through Tri Pointe’s curated design studio, or **move-in ready homes** that reflect the most popular design selections, each thoughtfully inspired by the model homes and the natural beauty of the surrounding Pacific Northwest.


## **Design That Inspires: Two Model Homes With Interiors by Bobby Berk**


At the heart of Timber Trails are two stunning model homes with interiors brought to life by **design expert, Emmy-winning TV host, and author Bobby Berk** . Known for his ability to create deeply personal, functional, and beautiful spaces, Berk’s designs at Timber Trails capture both the spirit of the region and the essence of modern living.


[Plan 1: Alpine Modern](https://www.tripointehomes.com/wa/greater-seattle-area/timber-trails/plan-1-timber-trails)


A seamless blend of luxury and nature, *Alpine Modern* celebrates organic materials and contemporary design. Rustic woods, stone accents, and warm plank flooring create an inviting atmosphere anchored by a striking stone fireplace. Expansive windows frame tranquil scenery, bringing the outdoors in. Inside, modern elegance meets natural texture—plush neutral furnishings, reclaimed wood elements, and refined metal finishes create a layered, sophisticated look with rustic charm. The open kitchen features chef-inspired appliances and a minimalist aesthetic, while spa-like bathrooms offer serene retreats with natural wood and stone finishes. Every detail is intentionally designed to create harmony between home and nature.


[Plan 2: Nordic Nest](https://www.tripointehomes.com/wa/greater-seattle-area/timber-trails/plan-2-timber-trails)


Inspired by alpine surroundings, *Nordic Nest* is a cozy, design-forward retreat rooted in Scandinavian simplicity. Warm oak tones flow throughout the home, complemented by soft textiles and a rich yet calming palette of ochre, burgundy, and evergreen.


This model embraces comfort and connection, an open-concept layout featuring see through fireplace connecting the outdoor living to interior comforts. Whether enjoying a quiet evening indoors or taking in sunsets from the deck, Nordic Nest offers a sense of calm and belonging, designed for slowing down and savoring each moment.


## **A Premium Lifestyle, Rooted in Place**


Timber Trails reflects Tri Pointe Homes’ commitment to creating not just homes, but **elevated lifestyle experiences** . Every residence is designed with intention, blending architectural sophistication, innovative design, and a deep respect for the surrounding environment.


Here, homeowners enjoy:


-


Immediate access to miles of trails, parks, and preserved open space


-


Proximity to major employment hubs via I-90


-


A vibrant, established community with schools, retail, and recreation


-


Homes that reflect both timeless design and modern functionality


As opportunities for large-scale communities on the Eastside become increasingly rare, Timber Trails stands apart, not only for its design and location, but for what it represents: **the last chance to own a brand-new home in Snoqualmie Ridge** .


## **Your Place in the Legacy**


Timber Trails is more than a new community, it’s a meaningful milestone. A culmination of decades of thoughtful planning, design excellence, and community building. Whether you’re drawn to the personalization of a to-be-built home or the convenience of a move-in ready residence inspired by Bobby Berk’s vision, Timber Trails offers a unique opportunity to step into a lifestyle defined by beauty, connection, and enduring value.
