## **Welcome to**[Landmark](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/dallas-fort-worth/landmark-dfw) **: A New Chapter of North Texas Living**


North Texas continues to grow quickly, but what makes it special is how each area develops its own rhythm and identity. In the Denton–Fort Worth corridor, that mix shows up clearly—open land, expanding neighborhoods, and easy access to both small-town charm and big-city convenience.


In this part of the region, modern living blends naturally with wide-open space, making it a place where daily life feels more relaxed without being disconnected from everything happening around it.


---


**A Location Connected to Everything That Matters**


This area sits in a fast-growing pocket of North Texas, giving residents access to Denton, Fort Worth, and the broader DFW metroplex.


It’s the kind of location where you’re close to major job centers, retail hubs, and entertainment—but still surrounded by quieter residential space and open Texas landscape.


Whether it’s commuting, running errands, or planning a weekend out, everything stays within a reasonable drive without feeling overly busy day to day.


---


**Outdoor Space That Shapes Everyday Life**


One of the strongest parts of living in this area is how easy it is to get outside. Parks, lakes, and trail systems are a big part of the lifestyle here, and they’re used year-round. Some of the most popular nearby outdoor spots include:


●[Clear Creek Natural Heritage Center](https://www.discoverdenton.com/listing/clear-creek-natural-heritage-center/146/) - A protected nature area with boardwalk trails, wetlands, and wildlife viewing just minutes from central Denton.


●[South Lakes Park / Eureka Park system](https://www.cityofdenton.com/facilities/facility/details/South-Lakes-Park-Eureka-Playground-26) - One of the city’s largest parks, featuring trails, sports fields, and open green space.


●[Ray Roberts Lake State Park](https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/ray-roberts-lake) - A major regional lake destination north of Denton for boating, fishing, hiking, and camping—commonly used by Denton-area residents.


These areas make it easy to step away from daily routines and enjoy time outdoors without having to travel far.


---


**Downtown Denton Square: Local Energy and Culture**


Just a short drive from the area,[Downtown Denton Square](https://www.discoverdenton.com/things-to-do/attractions/downtown-square/) is one of the most active and recognizable spots in North Texas. Built around the historic courthouse, it’s a walkable district filled with local businesses, live music, and a strong community feel. It has a relaxed, creative energy that makes it easy to spend time there without needing a plan. Around the Square, you’ll find:


● Live music almost any night of the week


● Local coffee shops and casual cafés


● A mix of restaurants from quick bites to sit-down dining


● Vintage stores, boutiques, and small local shops


● Seasonal events, markets, and community festivals


What makes Denton stand out is its personality—it feels local, creative, and always active without being overwhelming. It’s a place where people naturally gather, whether for food, music, or just walking around.


---
