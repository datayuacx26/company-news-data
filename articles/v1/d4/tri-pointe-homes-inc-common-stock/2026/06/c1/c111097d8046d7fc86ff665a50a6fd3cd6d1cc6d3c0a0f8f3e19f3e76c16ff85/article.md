# Quiet, Comfortable, Connected: How Whispering Hills Balances Serenity and Convenience


Tri Pointe Homes Arizona


•


June 30, 2026


Share this article


There’s a difference between being away from it all and feeling removed. At[Whispering Hills by Tri Pointe Homes®](https://www.tripointehomes.com/az/phoenix-metro/whispering-hills) , you don’t have to choose between calm and convenient. Tucked in the scenic foothills of Southwest Phoenix in Laveen, this gated community offers quiet streets, open space, and intimate pockets of nature within three miles of Loop 202 and a short drive to downtown Phoenix. Here, ease and opportunity go hand in hand.


## A Setting That Feels Like a Retreat


Whispering Hills lives up to its name. This 122-acre planned community was designed to feel like a true retreat with plenty of room to breathe. Each of the three single-level home collections –[Ridge](https://www.tripointehomes.com/az/phoenix-metro/ridge-collection-at-whispering-hills) ,[Summit](https://www.tripointehomes.com/az/phoenix-metro/summit-collection-at-whispering-hills) , and[Atlas](https://www.tripointehomes.com/az/phoenix-metro/atlas-collection-at-whispering-hills) – is situated within the gated community, contributing to a sense of peace and security in the bustling Phoenix metro. Across the community and upon buildout, residents are expected to have access to approximately 60 acres of open space and four pocket parks. These outdoor escapes will make it easy to enjoy daily walks, playtime, or moments of stillness right outside your door.


## Location Without the Commotion


Whispering Hills offers unmatched proximity to where life happens. The community is only minutes from Loop 202, which means faster commutes to downtown Phoenix, Chandler, Tempe, and Sky Harbor Airport. Situated in the[Laveen Elementary School District](https://laveenschools.org/) and the[Phoenix Union High School District](https://www.pxu.org/) , Whispering Hills is also close to shopping, dining, and major employers, so you can spend more time at home and less time on the road.


## Elevated Everyday Comforts


The homes at Whispering Hills are designed to match the lifestyle of this uniquely balanced setting: quiet and comfortable, yet full of flexibility. Across each collection, buyers can choose from thoughtfully designed, energy-efficient homes ranging from approximately 2,346 to 4,304 square feet. Depending on floorplan, options like[Flex Design®](https://www.tripointehomes.com/blog/personalizing-your-new-home-with-flex-spaces/) spaces,[GenSmart Suites®](https://www.tripointehomes.com/blog/gensmart-suite/) , executive kitchens, and accessory garages help homeowners tailor their space to fit changing needs. And with Tri Pointe’s[LivingSmart®](https://www.tripointehomes.com/livingsmart/) program at the core, these homes are designed to support both environmental sustainability and long-term cost efficiency.


## Nature as Your Neighbor


Living near South Mountain Park and Preserve,[one of the largest municipal parks in the country](https://www.visitarizona.com/places/parks-monuments/south-mountain-park-preserve) , means hiking, biking, and horseback riding are always within reach. With over 16,000 acres and 51 miles of trails, Whispering Hills residents can maintain an active outdoor lifestyle without straying far from home. Whether you’re winding through rugged terrain to discover panoramic city views at Dobbins Point, spotting ancient Hohokam petroglyphs along the Mormon Trail, or taking a scenic drive to marvel at the preserved beauty, South Mountain Park and Preserve delivers recreation at your pace.


## In the Middle of it All – Without All of the Fuss


Whispering Hills offers an increasingly rare opportunity to experience quiet, comfort, and connection all in one place. It’s a chance to live close to what matters while sidestepping the congestion and commotion that often comes with living in a city like Phoenix. From restorative mornings in your ideal home, to days in the city that spark inspiration, to relaxing nights under the desert stars, Whispering Hills is where it all comes together.
