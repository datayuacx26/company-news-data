# Why Living in Apex is a "Peak" Decision

As a charming suburb of Raleigh, Apex’s name reflects its elevated position in more ways than one. This North Carolina town sits at the highest point along a historic stretch of the Chatham Railroad and on a natural watershed divide where rainfall flows toward either the Neuse or Cape Fear River. It also sits at the top of a few different lists.[WalletHub ranked it the #3 best small city in America in 2026](https://wallethub.com/edu/best-worst-small-cities-to-live-in/16581) and in[Niche's 2026 rankings, Apex was named the #1 Best Place to Raise a Family in North Carolina](https://www.niche.com/places-to-live/search/best-places-for-families/c/wake-county-nc/) and the[#4 Best Place to Live in the state](https://www.niche.com/places-to-live/search/best-places-to-live/s/north-carolina/) . Apex has experienced remarkable growth over the past several years. According to[Apex Economic Development](https://www.apexnc.org/918/Population-Characteristics) , as of May 31st, 2026, nearly 86,000 residents call the town home. This is a 4.5% increase that reflects the steady growth trend that Apex has been seeing since 2020. This increase in population should come as no surprise. Apex is perfectly positioned between some of the state’s most beloved destinations and the thriving Research Triangle, while still offering its residents a charming, vibrant town to call home. **A Central Location with Big Advantages** Apex’s prime location in central North Carolina makes it the perfect place to relocate. Being just south of the Research Triangle Park, Apex gives residents easy access to local employment hubs. Close by are major universities like Duke University, North Carolina State University, and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Proximity to these schools makes it easy for students, alumni, faculty, and staff to commute and stay connected. Adding to its appeal, the town is just 20 minutes from Raleigh-Durham International Airport and downtown Raleigh, providing convenient access to travel, employment opportunities, and big-city amenities without sacrificing its welcoming small-town feel. Another perk of Apex’s location is its temperate climate that gives residents a comfortable experience through all four seasons without the hassle of dangerously hot summers or brutal winters. In fact, summer temperatures rarely reach above[95 degrees Fahrenheit and winter temperatures rarely drop below 20 degrees Fahrenheit](https://weatherspark.com/y/20142/Average-Weather-in-Apex-North-Carolina-United-States-Year-Round) . Winters in Apex offer the perfect balance of seasonal charm and practicality. With an average of just four inches of snow per year, residents can enjoy a dusting that turns their world into a winter wonderland without the months of shoveling, icy roads, and harsh conditions common in colder climates. **Community, Culture, and Connection** What truly sets Apex apart is its strong sense of community, which is brought to life through year-round events, local traditions, and many opportunities to connect. Whether you're looking for your next outing, a volunteer opportunity, or a local event, Apex makes it easy to get involved through its[robust community calendar](https://www.apexnc.org/calendar.aspx) and extensive[online resources](https://www.apexnc.org/) . Downtown Apex was named a historic district in 1994 and since then has grown into a bustling social district with unique restaurants and coffee shops, historic homes and buildings, and charming streets. Downtown is also home to the[Halle Cultural Arts Center](https://www.apexnc.org/1442/Halle-Cultural-Arts-Center) ; Apex's hub for arts, culture, and community events. Whether you're enrolling in a visual or performing arts class, attending a live performance, or simply enjoying an evening of local talent, the center offers something for residents of all ages and interests. Looking to get outside and work on your physical and mental well-being? Apex has incredible recreation facilities with numerous[parks](https://www.apexnc.org/710/Parks) ,[greenways](https://www.apexnc.org/464/Greenways) ,[youth athletic programs](https://www.apexnc.org/423/Youth-Athletics) ,[adult athletic programs](https://www.apexnc.org/403/Adult-Athletics) , as well as[specialized recreation](https://www.apexnc.org/520/Specialized-Recreation) for people of all abilities and ages. The town also loves to celebrate! The vibrant event scene makes it easy to build connections and feel at home, with celebrations throughout the year showcasing the people, businesses, and culture that make Apex special. From town-centered celebrations like Founders' Day and Pig Fest to cultural events like the Latino Arts Festival and beloved holiday traditions such as Fourth of July Frenzy and Christmas on Salem Street, Apex is no stranger to bringing the community together for memorable celebrations. **Discover Life at “The Peak of Good Living”** Apex has earned its tagline, “The Peak of Good Living,” by creating a community where residents can truly enjoy a high quality of life.
