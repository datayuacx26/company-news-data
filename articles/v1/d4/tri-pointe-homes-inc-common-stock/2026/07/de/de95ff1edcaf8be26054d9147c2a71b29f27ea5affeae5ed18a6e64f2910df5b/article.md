Many people dream of a retirement that includes sandy beaches, salty air, and an overall slower pace of life. The idea of beach life is undeniably appealing, but is true coastal living everything you imagine it will be?


Retirement is more than just where you live; it’s about how you spend your time. It’s about being close to the people you love, exploring the places you’ve always wanted to visit, and slowing down to enjoy life and its every moment. *Altis at Serenity™ by Tri Pointe Homes®* offers the retirement lifestyle you imagined without some of the often-overlooked challenges of coastal living. You get the comfort and convenience of Raleigh-area living with the luxuries of the beach still just a short drive away.


[Altis at Serenity](https://www.tripointehomes.com/nc/raleigh/altis-at-serenity) is a resort-style 55+ active adult community in Fuquay-Varina that offers residents the best of both worlds and a chance to discover their *New Next.*


**Stay Connected to Family, Travel, and Adventure**


Retirement should expand your world, not shrink it. Staying in a central location puts you in the middle of everywhere, perfect for spontaneous weekend adventures and making travel plans even easier.


Located in the heart of North Carolina, Fuquay-Varina offers retirees easy access to any part of the state, whether that’s the grandkids’ house, the mountains, or the beach. Fuquay-Varina is a short two-hour drive from prime North Carolina shores like Wrightsville Beach and Carolina Beach, and a three-hour drive to the breathtaking North Carolina mountains. It is also just a 30-minute drive to the[Raleigh–Durham International Airport](https://www.rdu.com/) (RDU) for any other national or international travel plans. Let every sunrise of your retirement contain infinite possibilities. Imagine waking up one morning with a car packed and ready for a quick drive down to the beach, and the next setting off to enjoy the mountains. By the end of the week, you could be boarding a direct flight from RDU to your next adventure abroad.


**Explore New Interests Without the Tourist Crowds**


The Triangle is a thriving area that offers endless chances to stay active and engaged. From museums to fine dining, there is always something new to experience.


As home to major universities such as North Carolina State University, the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, and Duke University, the Triangle provides opportunities for lifelong learning. North Carolina residents age 65 and older are eligible to audit courses through NC State’s[Senior Auditors Program](https://nds.registrar.ncsu.edu/apply/eligibility/#senior-auditors) , making it easy to continue exploring new interests and expand your mind in retirement.


Unlike many coastal communities that experience surges of seasonal visitors, the Triangle offers these amenities year-round without the traffic, crowds, and lines that come with tourism. This way, you can enjoy a fulfilling lifestyle while maintaining a sense of comfort and community that makes retirement truly relaxing.


While living on the beach sounds appealing, full-time coastal living can come with[added maintenance](https://acentria.com/beach-house-maintenance-preventive-tips-for-coastal-properties/) ,[increased exposure to severe weather,](https://dukechronicle.com/article/duke-university-coastal-erosion-north-carolina-outer-southeast-barrier-island-house-collapse-nicholas-school-of-the-environment-coastlines-martin-smith-betsy-albright-beach-nourishment-hurricanes-20240612) and[higher insurance costs](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/insurance/homeowners-insurance-north-carolina-increase-lmandp5/) . In recent years, homeowners along North Carolina’s coastline have seen insurance premiums rise, with some coastal properties experiencing increases between 50% and 99% from 2019 to 2024, according to Forbes. Salty air and humidity can take a toll on homes and vehicles over time, adding unnecessary emotional and financial stress to your retirement.


Retiring in Fuquay-Varina means enjoying a welcoming community with easy access to the excitement and opportunities of the surrounding Triangle area. Closer to home, residents can enjoy Fuquay-Varina’s charming[downtown district](https://www.fuquay-varina.org/1755/Downtown) , locally owned shops and restaurants, and community events. This creates the perfect balance of convenience, relaxation, and adventure, all while maintaining that peaceful small-town charm. In fact, Fuquay-Varina has consistently been ranked among the top small towns in America by experts at[Homes.com](https://www.newsobserver.com/news/state/north-carolina/article250971809.html) and[WalletHub](https://www.cbs17.com/news/local-news/wake-county-news/which-wake-county-towns-rank-among-the-best-small-cities-in-america/) . It has also been ranked as one of the[best places for remote workers](https://www.homes.com/learn/best-suburbs-in-america/) - perfect for those active adults still going strong in their careers or those recent retirees finally chasing an entrepreneurial dream.

Enjoy amenities like the pool, yoga studio, and fitness center while building connections with other adults your age.
