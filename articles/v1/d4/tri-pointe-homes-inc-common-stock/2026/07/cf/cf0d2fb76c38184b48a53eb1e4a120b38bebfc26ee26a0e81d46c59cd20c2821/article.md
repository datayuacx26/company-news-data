Wellness at home is no longer limited to a single room, routine, or design feature. It is shaped by morning light in the kitchen, effortless gathering spaces, quiet places to recharge, and design that supports comfort, calm, and connection throughout the day.


Wellness in every detail is the idea behind[LivingWell™ by Tri Pointe Homes®](https://www.tripointehomes.com/livingwell-home) , a next-generation whole-home wellness concept designed around the way life feels every day. Building on the proven performance of[LivingSmart®](https://www.tripointehomes.com/different-by-design) , LivingWell brings architecture, interiors, landscape, systems, and everyday spaces together with a deeper focus on well-being.


For[Pavilions at Holladay Hills](https://www.tripointehomes.com/ut/salt-lake-city/pavilions-at-holladay-hills) in Utah, this philosophy comes to life through a limited collection of luxury estate homes designed to show how intentional design can support the natural rhythms of home. Intentional design blends luxury with the outdoor lifestyles Utah is famous for, from the accessible home courtyard to grounding mountain vistas.


## **What Is LivingWell?**


LivingWell is not a room, feature package, or passing design trend. It is a whole-home philosophy centered on the way people cook, gather, rest, move, work, recharge, entertain, and connect.


Through light, flow, comfort, adaptability, natural materials, smart systems, and indoor-outdoor living, LivingWell creates a home that feels considered from the inside out. The result is a residence designed not only for how homeowners live, but for how they want to feel.


## **The Five Pillars of LivingWell**


-


**Nutrition & Gathering**


-


The kitchen has always been the heart of the home. In a LivingWell home, it becomes a place where everyday rituals feel more meaningful. Chef-inspired kitchens, prep spaces, generous storage, and welcoming gathering areas support everything from a quiet breakfast to a lively evening with friends. At Pavilions at Holladay Hills, these spaces are designed to make cooking, hosting, and connecting feel natural.


-


**Social Interaction & Movement**


-


A home feels better when it moves with ease. Open living areas, thoughtful circulation, and seamless indoor-outdoor living help create a natural rhythm from one space to the next. At Pavilions at Holladay Hills, large openings and inviting transitions encourage movement, conversation, and connection, whether homeowners are entertaining outdoors, gathering inside, or simply enjoying a more open way to live.


-


**Mindfulness & Renewal**


-


Restoration begins with spaces that invite pause. Peaceful bedrooms, spa-like bathrooms, and quiet zones help create moments of calm within the flow of daily life. Soothing materials, thoughtful lighting, and private retreats tailored to your lifestyle make it easier to reset, recharge, and feel at home in a deeper way.


-


**Balance & Adaptability**


-


Life rarely stays the same, and a home should be ready for what comes next. LivingWell emphasizes flexible rooms that can shift with changing needs, from work and fitness to hobbies, guests, creativity, or rest. A space may serve as a home office today, a wellness room tomorrow, and a guest retreat. This adaptability helps the home remain useful, personal, and supportive over time.


-


**Light & Spatial Experience**


-


Light influences the mood of a home from morning to night. LivingWell considers natural light, spatial flow, material selections, automated lighting, roller shades, and circadian-friendly benefits as part of the overall living experience. These choices help create a calmer atmosphere, a stronger connection to the outdoors, and a more balanced sense of place throughout the day.


## **Wellness Engineered From the Inside Out**


A LivingWell home is designed for everyday comfort both on the surface and behind the walls. At Pavilions at Holladay Hills, systems, materials, and construction details are selected with a clear purpose: to help the home feel healthier, quieter, more efficient, and easier to live in.


**Cleaner, More Comfortable Air**


Indoor comfort starts with the air homeowners breathe every day. An HRV system recycles up to 90% of heating and cooling energy, while MERV 13 filtration helps capture allergens and fine particles. A whole-home humidifier supports balanced indoor comfort, and an active radon mitigation system adds another layer of protection.


Low-VOC paint and flooring throughout the home further support air quality, helping create interiors that feel fresh, comfortable, and thoughtfully finished.


**Smarter Everyday Living**


LivingWell also brings comfort into the daily routines of home. Home control automation, automated lighting and roller shades, a Wi-Fi thermostat with remote monitoring, structured whole-home wiring, and conduit for future solar installation help make the home more responsive and convenient.


These features are designed to simplify everyday living, from adjusting the temperature remotely to creating lighting that better supports the rhythm of the day.


**Built for Efficiency, Quiet, and Resilience**


Behind the walls, thoughtful construction helps support long-term comfort. Blown-in insulation, inter-floor sound insulation, advanced framing techniques, engineered ducting and duct sealing, tankless water heaters, high-efficiency HVAC, generator prewiring, and a projected HERS Score of 59 all contribute to a home designed for performance and peace of mind.


The benefit is simple: a home that works quietly in the background, helping support efficiency, durability, and ease.
