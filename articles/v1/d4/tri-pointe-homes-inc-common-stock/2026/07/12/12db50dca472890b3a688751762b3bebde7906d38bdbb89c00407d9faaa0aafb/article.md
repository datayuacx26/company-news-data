# Your Guide to Outdoor Living Near Austin

## New Home Communities with Trails, Parks, and Open Spaces Near Austin


In Central Texas, the outdoors is part of everyday life. Abundant with shaded neighborhood trails, riverside parks, Hill Country views, fishing ponds, playgrounds, and wide-open gathering spaces, the Austin area makes it easy to step outside and enjoy more room to roam.


For homebuyers looking for a new home near Austin with access to trails, parks, and open spaces, Tri Pointe Homes communities offer more than beautiful home designs. They connect residents to the kind of outdoor lifestyle that makes Central Texas feel so inviting. A peaceful morning walk, weekend picnics, and communities designed around fresh air and connection are everywhere you look.


## [Lariat](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/lariat) : Hill Country Living with Room to Explore


Set in Liberty Hill,[Lariat](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/lariat) brings Hill Country charm together with thoughtfully planned amenities and outdoor-focused living. The community is designed around connection, with places to gather, play, unwind, and enjoy the landscape around you.


Residents can enjoy planned amenities like a pool and splash pad, dock and fishing pond, outdoor fitness center, dog park, trails, ponds, and nearby ranchland. It’s the kind of setting where an afternoon walk, a fishing trip, or a casual conversation with neighbors can all happen close to home.


For those who want a lifestyle that feels relaxed, scenic, and rooted in Hill Country character,[Lariat](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/lariat) offers a strong sense of place just outside the pace of the city.


## [Flora](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/flora) : Nature Trails and Gathering Greens in Hutto


Located in Hutto,[Flora](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/flora) is designed for families who want outdoor fun woven into daily life. This sprawling community features nature trails, parks, gathering greens, and resort-style amenities that make it easy to spend time outside throughout the year.


[Flora](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/flora) ’s community design places trails, pools, parks, and gathering spaces throughout the neighborhood, creating natural opportunities to move, play, and connect. Market Square adds another layer of community life, with space for farmers markets, food truck festivals, and neighborly events.


For homebuyers who want a community that feels active, social, and naturally comfortable,[Flora](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/flora) offers a lifestyle where fresh air is always close by.


## [Park Central](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/park-central) : Georgetown Convenience with a Park at the Center


[Park Central](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/park-central) in Georgetown brings together modern home designs, neighborhood amenities, and a connected location surrounded by local charm. True to its name, the community centers around outdoor spaces that encourage residents to come together.


Amenities include a resort-style pool, sports courts, playground, trike track, pond, and walking path. The community is also planned around a 10-acre park and amenity center, creating a natural hub for everyday activity, weekend relaxation, and neighborly connection.


[Park Central](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/park-central) also places residents near some of Georgetown’s most-loved outdoor destinations. San Gabriel Park offers a scenic setting along the river with open green space, sports fields, picnic areas, and hike-and-bike trail access. Blue Hole Park, with its limestone bluffs and South San Gabriel River setting, offers another nearby place to cool off and enjoy the natural beauty of Georgetown.


For those who want a new home near parks, trails, local shops, and Georgetown’s award-winning town square,[Park Central](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/park-central) makes outdoor living feel easy and connected.


## [Wolf Ranch](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/wolf-ranch) : Scenic Trails Near the San Gabriel River


[Wolf Ranch](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/wolf-ranch) in Georgetown is surrounded by Hill Country beauty and designed with nature as a backdrop. This award-winning community blends modern living with outdoor serenity, offering amenities and open spaces that help every day feel a little more extraordinary.


The community’s location near the San Gabriel River adds to its outdoor appeal, with natural scenery, trails, and water views all helping create a peaceful Central Texas lifestyle. The city’s hike-and-bike trails run along the North and South San Gabriel Rivers, and San Gabriel Park connects to other outdoor destinations, including Blue Hole Park, Rivery Park, Chautauqua Park, and trails toward Lake Georgetown. Garey Park is another standout, with hiking trails, equestrian trails, fishing ponds, a dog park, playground, splash pad, and wide-open space to enjoy.


For homebuyers looking for a scenic Georgetown community with elevated amenities and access to the outdoors,[Wolf Ranch](https://www.tripointehomes.com/tx/austin/wolf-ranch) delivers a balance of comfort, connection, and Hill Country calm.
