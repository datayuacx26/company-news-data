# Claude Voice Mode Gets Smarter: Opus, Sonnet, Your Email and Calendar, and More Languages (2026)

### What is Claude voice mode?


Claude voice mode is a hands-free way to talk with Claude out loud and hear it respond, built into the Claude mobile, desktop, and web apps. As of the July 23, 2026 update, it runs on Claude's more capable Opus and Sonnet models and can reach connected tools like your email and calendar in the middle of a conversation.


### What models does Claude voice mode use?


Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku. Before the July 2026 update, voice mode ran only on Haiku, the fastest and lightest model. Now you can switch to Sonnet or Opus mid-conversation using the model selector for deeper reasoning. Claude's Fable model is not available in voice mode.


### Can Claude voice mode read my email and calendar?


Yes, if you have connected them to Claude. Mid-conversation, voice mode can pull from connectors like your email and calendar to summarize a message, read back your schedule, or, with your confirmation, act on what it finds, such as moving an appointment.


### What languages does Claude voice mode support?


At this update, voice conversations are supported in 11 languages: English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and Spanish for both Latin America and Spain. You can switch languages mid-conversation.


### How do I turn on Claude voice mode?


On desktop or the web, open a new chat and click the sound-wave icon in the lower-right corner, then start speaking. On mobile, tap the voice icon in the message box and choose a voice. Switch models with the model selector, and set the spoken language under Settings > General > Voice > Language.


### Is Claude voice mode free, and does it work on desktop?


Yes on both. Voice mode is available on every plan, including the Free plan (and Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise), and it works on mobile, desktop, and the web. Usage counts toward your normal plan limits.


### Can I use my voice to work across all my apps, not just chat with Claude?


That is a different job. Claude voice mode keeps the conversation inside the Claude app. To dictate into any field and take actions across your other apps, Gmail, Slack, Notion, your editor, you need a system-wide voice layer. VoiceOS does exactly that on Mac and Windows, and it connects to Claude as one of its 20+ integrations.
