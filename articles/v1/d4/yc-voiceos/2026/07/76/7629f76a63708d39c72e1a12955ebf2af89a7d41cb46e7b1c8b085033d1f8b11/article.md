# ChatGPT Voice on the Desktop: Control Your Computer and Direct AI Agents by Voice (Mac & Windows)

### What is ChatGPT Voice on the desktop app?


ChatGPT Voice on the desktop is a voice mode inside OpenAI's Mac and Windows ChatGPT app, launched on July 23, 2026. Powered by GPT-Live, it lets you talk to ChatGPT to control your computer and direct multiple agents running in ChatGPT Work or Codex, using just your voice. It can speak, listen, and coordinate work in the app at the same time.


### What does "control your computer with ChatGPT Voice" actually do?


It means you can start a chat or task in voice mode, then ask ChatGPT to start, check, or steer work running in other threads, all by talking. It uses OpenAI's Computer Use to take actions on screen, can access local files and plugins, and on Mac uses Appshots to reference the window you have in focus for context.


### Which platforms and plans support ChatGPT Voice on desktop?


ChatGPT Voice on the desktop runs on macOS and Windows. It is rolling out to the Plus, Pro, Business, Edu, and Enterprise plans. The Appshots feature, which references the focused window, is a Mac capability at launch.


### What is GPT-Live, and how does it relate to this?


GPT-Live is OpenAI's full-duplex generation of ChatGPT Voice, launched on July 8, 2026, that can listen and speak at the same time. It is the engine behind the desktop launch, which is why the desktop voice can coordinate work and hold a natural, interruptible conversation instead of taking strict turns.


### What are Appshots in ChatGPT?


Appshots is a Mac feature that lets ChatGPT reference the app window you currently have in focus, giving it real context about what is on your screen when you talk to it. It makes requests like "summarize this" or "use what's on screen" work against whatever window you are looking at.


### What is the difference between ChatGPT Voice on desktop and VoiceOS?


ChatGPT Voice lives inside the ChatGPT app and is built to direct ChatGPT's own agents, Work and Codex. VoiceOS is a system-wide voice layer that works in every app on your Mac or Windows computer. It dictates and edits text wherever your cursor is and takes actions across apps you already use, like Gmail, Slack, and Google Calendar, with confirmation before anything important is sent.


### How can I control my computer with my voice on Mac and Windows?


You install a voice app, grant microphone access, and start talking. With VoiceOS you press your trigger key and speak to dictate text into any app, rewrite existing text, ask questions about what is on your screen, and use Agent Mode to draft emails, send Slack messages, and create calendar events by voice, with a confirmation step before important actions.
