# Hyster H40-70A DSS Alert: Intervention or Service Fault? Capture the Conditions

## Capturing Operating Context During System Alerts


Because DSS produces both an audible alert tone and a visual indicator on the truck display during Corner Control, High-Lift Traction Control, and High-Lift Tilt Control interventions, capturing operating context at the moment of the alert is essential. Recording whether the truck was actively cornering, traveling with an elevated carriage, or tilting an elevated load establishes whether the event aligns with documented DSS functions.


When an audible warning tone sounds continuously, or when truck performance is restricted without corresponding cornering or elevated load conditions, the behavior is abnormal. Documenting the specific operating state, mast position, and display status provides clear evidence for supervisor review.
