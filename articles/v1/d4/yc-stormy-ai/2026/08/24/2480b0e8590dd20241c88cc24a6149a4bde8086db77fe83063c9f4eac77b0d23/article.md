# Crown RC 5700 Entry-Bar Alarm and Slow Stop: Capture the Safety Response

## OSHA Compliance, Service Boundaries, and Dispatch Protocol


When an Entry Bar alarm sounds or a slow stop occurs in the absence of foot contact, the truck is not working correctly. The Crown manual gives a clear instruction: 'Don't use the truck if it's not working correctly. Notify your supervisor.' Facility staff and operators must never bypass, wedge, shim, or open the entry bar assembly.


Under federal regulation OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178(p)(1), any powered industrial truck found to be defective or unsafe must be immediately taken out of service until restored to safe operating condition. Section 1910.178(q)(1) mandates that all repairs be performed exclusively by authorized personnel. Submitting a clear dispatch record with the truck model, serial number, hour meter reading, and detailed observation allows qualified service technicians to conduct proper diagnostic procedures.
