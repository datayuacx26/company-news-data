# Crown C-5 Coolant, Transmission, and Oil-Pressure Lights: What the Operator Should Do

## OSHA Removal From Service and Maintenance Standards


Federal regulation 29 CFR 1910.178 sets strict requirements for powered industrial truck safety. Under 1910.178(p)(1), any truck found to be in need of repair, defective, or in any way unsafe must be taken out of service until restored to safe operating condition. Section 1910.178(q)(1) mandates that all repairs be made by authorized personnel.


Furthermore, 1910.178(q)(9) mandates that when the temperature of any part of any truck exceeds its normal operating temperature, creating a hazardous condition, the vehicle must be removed from service until the cause of overheating is eliminated. Reporting defects promptly ensures compliant and safe fleet management.
