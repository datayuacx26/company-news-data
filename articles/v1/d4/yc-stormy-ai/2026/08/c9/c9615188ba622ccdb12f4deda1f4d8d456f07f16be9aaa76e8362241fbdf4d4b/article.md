# Crown WP 3200 Display Status: Flashing Low-Battery Indication vs. Service-Required Event Code

## Understanding Low Battery Indication and Raise Inhibition


The Crown WP 3200 walkie pallet truck message panel includes a battery charge meter showing how much charge remains in the battery. When the battery charge reaches a low level, the battery icon flashes.


Crown documentation explicitly states that when the battery icon flashes, you will not be able to raise the forks. Operators should note the low-battery state and consult the manual's approved charging instructions or notify a supervisor.


- Battery icon flashes when the battery is low
- Fork raising is disabled during a low-battery indication
- Power source charging must follow manufacturer specifications and authorized area rules
