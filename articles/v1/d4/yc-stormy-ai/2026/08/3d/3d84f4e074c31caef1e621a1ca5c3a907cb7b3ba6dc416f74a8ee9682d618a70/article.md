# Crown SC 5700 Battery Gate Open: What the Retainer Light Actually Means

## What a remote image cannot certify


A remote image cannot prove that a retainer is correctly engaged, that a switch or sensor works, that the battery is undamaged, or that the truck is safe to operate. It also cannot establish why the warning appeared. Those findings require the exact configuration, the current procedure, and an authorized inspection.


The correct remote contribution is narrower and more valuable: keep the condition from being mislabeled as a charging complaint, preserve the exact display state, and route a complete packet to the person authorized to inspect and release the truck.
