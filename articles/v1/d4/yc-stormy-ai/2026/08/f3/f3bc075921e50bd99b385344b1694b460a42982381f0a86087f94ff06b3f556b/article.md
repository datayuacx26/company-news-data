# Raymond 7000 Deep-Reach Will Not Travel: Check the Compartment Sensor Evidence

## Stationary Travel Inhibit Evidence


Official Raymond product documentation states that if the truck is stationary, it will not travel if the light sensor is blocked. The combination of an audible tone, display message, and travel inhibit reinforces proper operating procedures at the compartment entry.


When an equipped stationary truck will not travel, check for the sounding tone and display message indicating a blocked light sensor spanning the compartment entry.
