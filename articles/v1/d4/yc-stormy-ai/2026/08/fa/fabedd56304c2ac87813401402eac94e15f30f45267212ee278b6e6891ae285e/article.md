# Crown SC 6200 Attachment Does Not Match the Capacity Plate: Stop Before the Next Load

## OSHA 1910.178 Regulations on Truck Modifications and Attachment Markings


Federal OSHA standard 29 CFR 1910.178 sets mandatory requirements for powered industrial truck modifications and front-end attachments. Under 1910.178(a)(4), modifications and additions which affect capacity and safe operation shall not be performed by the customer or user without manufacturer's prior written approval. Capacity, operation, and maintenance instruction plates, tags, or decals shall be changed accordingly.


Under 29 CFR 1910.178(a)(5), if the truck is equipped with front-end attachments other than factory installed attachments, the user shall request that the truck be marked to identify the attachments and show the approximate weight of the truck and attachment combination at maximum elevation with load laterally centered. Under 1910.178(a)(6), the user shall see that all nameplates and markings are in place and are maintained in a legible condition.
