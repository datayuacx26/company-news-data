# Toyota 8BPUE15 Harness and Tether: What the Specification Confirms—and What It Does Not

## The Specification Boundary: What Official Documentation Establishes


The published Toyota 2025 Order Picker Specification (brochure document 007228BPUSS) serves as a baseline engineering and procurement reference. It explicitly details that the 8BPUE15 24-volt model includes standard support equipment comprising an Operator Presence Sensing System (OPSS), orange forks, a spring-applied/electric release parking brake, and a full body harness paired with an energy-absorbing tether rated for operator weights up to 310 lbs.


Additionally, the specification details the physical operating compartment: a standard 39-inch wide operator platform, a full-width operator tether bar mounted overhead, a low 7.75-inch step height, and an emergency manual lowering valve located beneath no-tools service access covers. Optional configurations include a retractable tether assembly and extended platform widths ranging from 44 to 67 inches.


- Standard Support Equipment: Full body harness and energy-absorbing tether (for operator weights up to 310 lbs).
- Platform Architecture: Full-width operator tether bar across the standard 39-inch compartment.
- Serviceability Access: Emergency manual lowering valve accessible without tools under high-impact covers.
