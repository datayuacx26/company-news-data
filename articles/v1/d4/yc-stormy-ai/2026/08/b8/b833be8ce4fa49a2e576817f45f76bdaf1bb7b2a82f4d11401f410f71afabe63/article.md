# Crown SP 1500 Harness and Anchorage Bar: The Checks That Come Before Elevation

## Operator Weight Limits, Derating, and Jurisdictional Rules


Page 57 of the Crown SP 1500 manual provides a specific Fall Protection / Prevention Selection Chart based on operator weight. All components of the fall protection system must be rated for the operator's weight. When an operator's weight exceeds 100 kg (220 lbs), the truck capacity must be reduced by the weight in excess of 100 kg (220 lbs).


The chart specifies device suitability by operator weight class. For operators under 141 kg (311 lbs), full body harnesses, energy absorbers up to 1.8 m (6 ft), and self-retracting lanyards up to 2.4 m (8 ft) are listed; however, energy-absorbing lanyards are prohibited by CAL-OSHA in the State of California. For operators between 141 kg and 181 kg (311 to 400 lbs), self-retracting lanyards (max 2.4 m / 8 ft) and full body harnesses are specified. If operator weight exceeds 181 kg (400 lbs), the manual directs operators to contact Crown. In addition, pregnant women must not use fall protection devices.
