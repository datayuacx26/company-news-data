# Hyster H40-70A Pre-Shift Defect: Build a Defensible Service Handoff

## Defect Isolation: Tag-Out and Key Removal


If any defect affecting the safe use of the forklift is discovered during the pre-shift walkaround, the operator is required to take the truck out of operation immediately. The mandatory isolation protocol requires fastening a DO NOT OPERATE tag to the controls and removing the key (Hyster Guide 8000 SRM 2689, PDF pages 2, 30).


Tag-out and key removal ensure the machine remains out of operation until proper repair is completed. Hyster safety instructions note that permitting untrained or improperly equipped personnel to work on lift trucks creates unreasonable risk of serious injury or death. If facility personnel lack the required knowledge, tools, or equipment, the repair must be referred to an authorized Hyster lift truck dealer (SRM 2689, PDF page 26).
