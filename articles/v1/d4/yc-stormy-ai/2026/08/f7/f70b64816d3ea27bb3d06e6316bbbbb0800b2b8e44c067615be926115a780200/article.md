# Crown PE 4500 COAST ON Light and Beep: Why Releasing the Handle Does Not Apply the Brake

## Manual Coast Control vs. Electronic QuickCoast Behavior


Crown PE 4500 Series end-control pallet trucks offer two optional walkalong coast configurations. When Coast is selected, releasing the control handle does not apply the brake; the truck continues to coast to make it easier to pick loads while walking short distances.


Option 1 uses a manual selector lever on the control arm: move the handle to the coast position and shift the lever toward the control arm to engage Coast, or move handle down and shift the lever away from the control arm to turn Coast off. Option 2 uses an electronic QuickCoast button on the back of the grab bar: move the handle to the coast position and press the button. QuickCoast selection illuminates the no-brake symbol on the display and sounds a single warning beep, followed by a continuous beep whenever the handle is held in a brake position.


- Option 1 (Manual Coast): Requires moving handle to coast position and shifting lever toward control arm; yellow COAST ON indicator lights. Move handle down and shift lever away from control arm to turn Coast off.
- Option 2 (Electronic QuickCoast): Requires moving handle to coast position and pressing grab-bar button; yellow COAST ON indicator and no-brake symbol light; single warning beep sounds, and continuous beep sounds while handle is in a brake position.
- Disengaging QuickCoast: Turn QuickCoast off by pressing the high travel speed button, turning the key switch to OFF, or disconnecting the battery. QuickCoast is also disabled if the reversing button is accidentally hit while the handle is in the Coast position.
