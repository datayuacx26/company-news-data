# JCB 930, 940, and 950 Warning Lights: Capture the System Before Calling for Service

## Quick Reference Guide Scope and Operator Manual Authority


The JCB Quick Reference Guide Revision 1.0 explicitly states: 'This Quick Reference Guide DOES NOT replace the Operators Manual. You MUST read ALL the disclaimers and safety and other instructions in the Operators Manual before initially operating this product.'


Because the Quick Reference Guide is an indicator layout index rather than an operational manual, it supplies no diagnostic flowcharts, fault thresholds, or mechanical repair procedures. All operating responses and safety directives remain governed by the official Operator's Manual.
