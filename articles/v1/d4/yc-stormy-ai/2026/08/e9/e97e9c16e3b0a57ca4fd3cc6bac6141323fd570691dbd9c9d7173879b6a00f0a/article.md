# Crown RR 5700 Reduce Lift Height: Read the Fork Zone Before You Raise Again

## The Five Essential Capacity Verification Fields


When an overload warning occurs, operators must systematically verify five key physical conditions before attempting to move or store the pallet. First, verify actual load weight against known pallet manifests or scale data. Second, verify the horizontal load center from the vertical face of the forks to the load center of gravity. Third, verify that weight is evenly distributed across both forks.


Fourth, confirm the specific lift zone and target rack elevation against capacity plate limits. Fifth, check total overhead clearance to ensure the mast and load backrest clear building structures. Recording these five blank fields provides the necessary factual record whether adjusting the load or initiating service intake.
