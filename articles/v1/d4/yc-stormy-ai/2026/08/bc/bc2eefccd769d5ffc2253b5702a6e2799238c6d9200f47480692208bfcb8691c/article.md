# Crown RR 5700 Daily Safety Check: When the Truck Must Not Be Driven

## OSHA Compliance, Removal from Service, and Supervisor Notification


Federal regulation 29 CFR 1910.178(q)(7) mandates that industrial trucks be examined before being placed in service, at least daily or after each shift in continuous operations. If examination reveals any condition adversely affecting vehicle safety, the unit cannot be placed in service.


Under 29 CFR 1910.178(p)(1), any powered industrial truck found defective, in need of repair, or unsafe must be immediately removed from service until restored to safe operating condition. Operators must immediately report defects to supervisory personnel and remove the truck from service; only authorized, qualified maintenance technicians may perform adjustments, diagnostic testing, or repairs.
