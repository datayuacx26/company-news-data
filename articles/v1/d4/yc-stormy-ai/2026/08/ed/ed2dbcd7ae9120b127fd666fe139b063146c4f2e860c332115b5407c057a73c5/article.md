# Hyster J30-40XNT Battery Fit: Why Voltage, Compartment, Connector, and Weight All Matter

## Electrical Ratings, Connectors, and Charging Requirements


The J30-40XNT series supports 36-volt and 48-volt electrical systems. On the J30XNT, 36V configurations use 18 cells (17 plates per cell) with a maximum 6-hour capacity of 880 Ah (30.6 kWh), while 48V configurations use 24 cells (13 plates per cell) with a maximum capacity of 660 Ah (30.5 kWh). On the J35XNT and J40XNT, 36V configurations use 18 cells (21 plates per cell) with up to 1,100 Ah (38.2 kWh), and 48V configurations use 24 cells (15 plates per cell) with up to 770 Ah (35.8 kWh). The source notes that battery amp-hour capacity is the maximum allowable per UL and that commercially available lead-acid batteries may not necessarily reach these limits.


For conventional charging (BCSSBC), the 36V system specifies a gray SB 350 connector (Anderson Power Products P/N 6320G1 or equivalent) and the 48V system specifies a blue SB 350 connector (Anderson Power Products P/N 6321G1 or equivalent), both with 20-inch (508 mm) 2/0 AWG leads at position 'B'. For rapid/fast charging (BARFBC), the technical guide states that a site survey is required to confirm candidate applications and determine battery and charger specifications; the connector requires dual cabling terminating in two Female EBC-320 DIN connectors (Anderson P/N A32503-0009 or equivalent) with 1 red conductor to (+) and 1 black conductor to (-), with 23-inch (585 mm) minimum 3/0 AWG leads at position 'B' on XNT models (26-inch minimum 3/0 AWG leads on XN models).
