## Understanding MER: The Only Metric That Actually Matters


[0:48 Learn how MER provides a comprehensive view of your overall Shopify marketing efficiency.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHg-jDelQ3Y&t=48) Comparison of MER and ROAS formulas and business use cases.


If you've ever felt that your ad metrics don't match your store's financial reality, then **Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)** is the number that finally connects those two worlds. While ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) measures the performance of a specific campaign, MER gives you the big picture of how efficiently your overall marketing spend turns into revenue.


The formula is simple: **Total Revenue / Total Ad Spend = MER** . For example, if your store makes $100,000 in total revenue and you spend $20,000 on ads across[Meta Ads](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) , Google, and TikTok, your MER is 5. You are earning $5 for every $1 spent. This 'blended' view is essential because it accounts for the 'assists' that don't show up in a single dashboard.


> "ROAS tells you how a campaign performed; MER tells you if your business is actually making money."
