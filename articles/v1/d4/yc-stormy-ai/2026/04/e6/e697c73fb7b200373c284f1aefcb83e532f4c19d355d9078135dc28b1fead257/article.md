## The 30-Video Affiliate Test


[32:27 Why posting high volumes of content is the key to unlocking affiliate success.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRYfA1WjY0k&t=1947) The four-step process for identifying and scaling high-performing affiliates.


To scale rapidly, you need to find "winners" quickly. A proven 2026 tactic is the **30-video trial** . You offer a creator a $1,000 minimum monthly retainer or a 15-20% commission—whichever is higher—on the condition that they provide **one video per day for 30 days** .


This high-frequency approach does three things:


1. It forces the creator to get comfortable with your product and hooks.
2. It provides the algorithm with enough data points to find an audience for your SKUs.
3. It generates a massive library of creative assets that you can then repurpose for[Google Ads](https://ads.google.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) ,[Pinterest](https://business.pinterest.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) , and[Snapchat](https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) .


If a creator hits a viral winner, they might make $5,000 to $10,000 in a single week from commissions. At that point, the retainer becomes irrelevant, and you have a **long-term, motivated partner** who is essentially a commission-only salesperson.
