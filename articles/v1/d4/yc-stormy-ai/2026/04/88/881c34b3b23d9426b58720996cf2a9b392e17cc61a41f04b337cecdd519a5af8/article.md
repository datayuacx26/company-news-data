## The Support Breaking Point: Why Comfrt Hired 40 Reps in 30 Days

While this kept the queue moving, it highlighted a massive risk: human scaling is expensive, slow, and prone to error.


In 2026, the strategy has shifted from "hiring bodies" to **Shopify customer support automation** . By leveraging an AI teammate, you can maintain the "family feel" that Hudson credits for Comfrt's success. Even at 1.5 million orders, a customer shouldn't feel like a number.[Stormy AI](https://stormy.ai/) summarizes recurring ticket problems, allowing founders to fix root causes—like a specific warehouse delay or a manufacturing defect—before the negative feedback loops destroy your ROAS on[Meta Ads](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) .

## Inventory Monitoring: The 'Flash Sale' Kill-Switch


[24:32 Managing inventory levels and projecting revenue to handle massive peaks in customer demand.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRYfA1WjY0k&t=1472) Automated inventory kill switch logic for high-volume flash sales.


One of the most counter-intuitive lessons from Comfrt's growth is knowing when to **stop selling** . During their October flash sale, the brand was trending so hot that they did $1.8 million in a single day. The problem? If they sold through every unit, their[Google Ads](https://ads.google.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) and Meta campaigns would have nothing to point to in December, leading to a total account reset and months of["learning phase"](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700?utm_source=stormy.ai) hell.


Hudson’s team had to manually put items "out of stock" on the website even though they still had physical units in the warehouse. In 2026, this is handled by **ecommerce inventory management 2026** protocols. **Stormy AI** monitors inventory levels across[Amazon](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) and Shopify in real-time. You can set a "Kill-Switch" rule: if stock for a Hero SKU drops below 500 units, Stormy automatically triggers an alert to the growth team to pause ad spend or update the store status.

## How to Automate Your Shopify Back Office with Stormy AI

### Step 1: Connect Your Data Silos


Start by giving[Stormy AI](https://stormy.ai/) access to your[Shopify](https://shopify.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) and ad platforms. Stormy pulls revenue from your store and spend from[Meta Ads Manager](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) and[TikTok Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) , creating a dynamic spreadsheet that updates every hour. Connect Stormy to your support email or[Gorgias](https://gorgias.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) . Ask Stormy to monitor for specific keywords like "Refund," "Broken," or "Late." Create row-level reminders for your top SKUs. If a product’s lead time is 45 days, tell Stormy to **wake up 50 days before** your projected stockout date. It will check the current sales velocity on[TikTok Shop](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/?utm_source=stormy.ai) and Shopify, calculate the reorder quantity, and draft a[PO (Purchase Order)](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/purchaseorder.asp?utm_source=stormy.ai) to your supplier.
