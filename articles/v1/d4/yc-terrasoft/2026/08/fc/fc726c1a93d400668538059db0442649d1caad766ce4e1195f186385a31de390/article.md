# Creatio CEO Named to the Global 100 Women in AI List

Creatio, an AI CRM and workflow platform where people and AI agents work together — with no limits on users, agents, or scale, today announced that Creatio CEO Katherine Kostereva has been named to the[100 Women in AI List](https://www.100womeninai.com/) , a global recognition celebrating women who are shaping the future of artificial intelligence through innovation, leadership, and impact.


The 100 Women in AI List recognizes influential leaders across technology, research, entrepreneurship, policy, and creative industries. Honorees are selected based on their contributions to advancing AI adoption, innovation, and positive societal impact, highlighting the diverse voices helping define the next generation of intelligent business.


Katherine's recognition reflects her leadership at a pivotal moment in enterprise software, as organizations move from AI experimentation to AI-native transformation. Under her leadership, Creatio has been at the forefront of this shift, championing a new enterprise model where people and AI agents work together on a single AI-native platform. Innovations such as AI Twin bring this vision to life, enabling every business user to create personal AI agents without technical skills and putting the power of AI directly into the hands of people across the enterprise.


At the heart of this approach is the company’s vision for the Unlimited Enterprise - a new operating model designed for the AI era, where organizations can scale work across people and AI agents with no limits on users, agents, workflows, or scale. By removing barriers to scale, businesses can unlock 10x productivity, 10x speed, and 10x business impact while empowering human creativity and AI-driven execution to work toward shared outcomes. This vision continues to resonate with organizations around the world as enterprises increasingly invest in AI-native platforms designed to replace legacy systems and accelerate business transformation.


" *This recognition reflects not only the incredible work of the Creatio team, but also a much broader transformation taking place across the enterprise software industry,* " said Katherine Kostereva, CEO of Creatio. " *The future of enterprise automation lies in bringing two worlds together on a single platform - autonomous agents that execute workflows end-to-end, and human-led workflows accelerated by AI. This requires a fundamentally new enterprise model, the Unlimited Enterprise, a model introduced by Creatio to help organizations scale without limits in the AI era.* "


To learn more about Creatio's vision for the Unlimited Enterprise and how organizations can achieve 10x business outcomes with AI, explore Creatio 10x “Unlimited” release:[https://www.creatio.com/page/10x-release](https://www.100womeninai.com/) .
