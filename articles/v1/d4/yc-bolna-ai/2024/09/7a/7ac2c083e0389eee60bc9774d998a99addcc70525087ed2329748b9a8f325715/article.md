# Bolna Debuts on Product Hunt with Voice AI Agents for Automated Conversations

**Bolna** , **Voice AI** platform that enables businesses to build human-like AI agents to handle customer calls, recruitment outreach, and front-desk operations, today announced its official launch on Product Hunt. Bolna’s intelligent voice agents are now featured as a top innovation in AI-powered communication, offering companies a fast way to automate conversations that feel natural and responsive.


Bolna empowers teams to go from idea to live deployment in minutes without writing a single line of code. Its voice agents deliver sub-second conversational responses, handle interruptions, and carry out real tasks such as booking appointments, scheduling interviews, sending emails, and updating databases. This human-like interaction significantly improves customer experience while reducing operational costs and freeing teams to focus on higher-value work.


**Key Features of Bolna’s Voice AI Platform** • **Instant Agent Creation** Build and configure voice agents in less than five minutes using intuitive templates or custom GPT prompts. • **Human-like Conversations** Agents understand pauses, interruptions, and cues, making conversations feel natural and engaging. • **Multilingual and Regional Support** Bolna supports multiple languages and dialects, crucial for diverse markets like India. • **Flexible Integrations** Connect with existing telephony systems, CRM platforms, ATS tools, and workflow automation solutions. • **Action-Driven Capabilities** Beyond simple call handling, Bolna’s AI can schedule events, send follow-ups, fetch data, and integrate with backend systems.


Bolna’s launch on Product Hunt has already attracted strong community interest with hundreds of upvotes and positive feedback from early users and makers. Businesses across industries, including small and medium enterprises, edtech, hospitality and recruitment, are finding voice AI agents a game changer for scaling communication without increasing headcount.


> “Phone calls remain at the core of business communication, but traditional IVRs and robo-callers fail to deliver meaningful interactions,” said the Bolna team. “With Bolna, companies can deploy voice AI agents that truly sound human and drive real outcomes - whether that’s answering inbound calls, screening candidates, or launching outbound campaigns.”
