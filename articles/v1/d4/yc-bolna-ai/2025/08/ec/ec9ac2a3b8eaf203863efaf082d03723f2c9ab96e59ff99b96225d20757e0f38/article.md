# Bolna AI Raises Funding from Y Combinator to Revolutionize Voice AI for India's Next Billion

**Bengaluru, India** – Bolna AI, a Y Combinator Fall 2025 company, is transforming how Indian enterprises handle voice communications at scale. The Voice AI orchestration platform enables businesses to automate millions of customer calls across sales, support, collections, and recruitment with intelligent agents that understand India's linguistic diversity.


### The India Challenge: Beyond Translation


India's business landscape presents unique challenges that global voice AI solutions struggle to address. With over 20 languages and 200+ dialects, conversations frequently switch mid-call from Hindi to Hinglish to regional languages. Traditional solutions require massive human teams that cannot scale economically or handle this complexity.


"In India, phone calls remain the backbone of business operations – from banks managing loan collections to e-commerce companies confirming deliveries," said Prateek Sachan, Co-Founder of Bolna AI. "We're enabling enterprises to automate these conversations while maintaining the natural, adaptive intelligence that Indian customers expect."


### Purpose-Built for Indian Enterprises


Bolna AI addresses three critical gaps in the market:


**Linguistic Complexity at Scale** : The platform handles natural conversations across 10+ Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, and Hinglish, with understanding of 50+ regional accents and seamless mid-conversation language switching.


**Smart Model Routing** : Rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all approach, Bolna automatically selects optimal AI model combinations for each context – whether it's a delivery driver in Bangalore requiring low-latency Kannada support or an engineering recruitment call demanding higher reasoning capability.


**Cost-Efficiency Engine** : Built for India's price-sensitive market, Bolna's platform intelligently routes calls using pre-recorded inserts for common responses, smaller vertical-focused models where appropriate, and dynamic provider selection to minimize costs without sacrificing quality.
