# Flexsteel Honored as One of Newsweek’s World’s Most Trustworthy Companies 2024

Flexsteel Industries, Inc., a leading furniture manufacturer, is proud to announce that it has been recognized as one of Newsweek’s World’s Most Trustworthy Companies 2024.


This esteemed recognition is a testament to Flexsteel’s dedication to quality, transparency, and ethical business practices. In collaboration with Statista, the award was meticulously chosen by Newsweek based on stringent criteria, including customer trust, investor confidence, and employee satisfaction.


Flexsteel has been a staple in the furniture industry for over a century, known for its innovation, quality craftsmanship, and customer-centric approach. With a commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, Flexsteel remains a trusted name in the furniture industry. This accolade reaffirms the company’s dedication to building lasting relationships with its stakeholders through trust and transparency.


“We are honored to be selected as one of Newsweek’s World’s Most Trustworthy Companies 2024,” said Derek Schmidt, President, and soon-to-be CEO of Flexsteel, effective July 1st. “This recognition reflects our ongoing commitment to building customer confidence and fostering a company culture that retailers can proudly promote. We will continue prioritizing innovation, quality craftsmanship, and trustworthiness in all aspects of our business.”


Flexsteel’s recognition as one of the World’s Most Trustworthy Companies in 2024 underscores its role as a leader in the industry and sets a benchmark for others to follow. The company remains committed to delivering exceptional products and services to its customers while maintaining the trust built over the decades.
