## TELA Bio to Announce Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results


July 20, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.telabio.com/node/11266/pdf)


MALVERN, Pa., July 20, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- TELA Bio, Inc. ("TELA Bio") (NASDAQ: TELA), a commercial-stage medical technology company focused on providing innovative soft-tissue reconstruction solutions, today announced that the Company will report second quarter 2026 financial results on Monday, August 10, 2026. TELA Bio’s management will host a conference call and webcast at 4:30 p.m. ET that day to discuss the financial results and provide a corporate update.

**About TELA Bio, Inc.**
TELA Bio, Inc. (NASDAQ: TELA) is a commercial-stage medical technology company focused on providing innovative technologies that optimize clinical outcomes by prioritizing the preservation and restoration of the patient's own anatomy. The Company is committed to providing surgeons with advanced, economically effective soft-tissue reconstruction solutions that leverage the patient's natural healing response while minimizing long-term exposure to permanent synthetic materials. For more information, visit[www.telabio.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=KJaM5fpidPuNd5ndXLTY9OthIjRbgw9g2y62bhjY7g0ekazLEk0EZunTkuSFkF4QyVDcGKsY5t8yTMJi7W8NN2S5K2EK9HMu9WzxaPQh7cvwBDERlvYwoonBIhJFbx4Wbtbtr3ZeaVmq670XWwbMdviToTI-r2FN6wm6kP42R5LsY_dGNZTP0CcQjoaNB30BRwI_q2TIEHLSlkkkYerubX7t2W3P7CLJtqt4-tSgKxpswGdvcf2YhItkulT4GYRy) .
