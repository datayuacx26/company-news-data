# Dylan Tornay Joins Evercore as Senior Managing Director in the Equity Capital Markets Group

Evercore announced today that Dylan Tornay has joined the firm as a senior managing director in its equity capital markets group, where he will focus on the industrials and infrastructure sectors. He will be based in New York.
