# Getty Images Photographers Recognized at 2026 World Sports Photography Awards

Getty Images Photographers Recognized at 2026 World Sports Photography Awards


Jan 30, 2026


|[PDF](https://newsroom.gettyimages.com/en/getty-images/getty-images-photographers-recognized-at-2026-world-sports-photography-awards/pdf)


**New York – January 30, 2026:**[Getty Images](https://www.gettyimages.com.mx/) (NYSE: GETY), a preeminent global visual content creator and marketplace, has taken home 16 prizes at the World Sports Photography Awards, including staff photographers Steph Chambers and Buda Mendes who were recognized with Gold in Baseball and Gymnastics, respectively.


In addition, a larger group of Getty Images staff photographers from around the globe were honored across a variety of categories, including:


- Al Bello: awarded Bronze in American Football for “Touchdown Backflip”; Special Merit in Basketball for “Eye On The Ball”.
- Alexander Hassenstein: awarded Special Merit in Football for “Champions”.
- Bruce Bennett: awarded Special Merit in Ice Hockey for “Jubilation”; Special Merit in Ice Hockey for “Snowstorm”.
- Buda Mendes: awarded Gold in Gymnastics for “Rhythmic Gymnastics World Championships, Rio de Janeiro 2025”.
- Carl Recine: awarded Special Merit in Venues & Views for “Hackney Marshes”.
- Christian Petersen: awarded Silver in Athletics for “Above The Rest”.
- Emilee Chinn: awarded Special Merit in Athletics for “Perseverance”.
- Ezra Shaw: awarded Bronze in Venues & Views for “Racing Through Lava”.
- Lars Baron: awarded Special Merit in Gymnastics for “Under The Lights”.
- Patrick Smith: awarded Special Merit in Baseball for “Game Seven”.
- Richard Heathcote: awarded Bronze in Golf for “Beating The Odds”.
- Ryan Pierse: awarded Special Merit in Venues & Views for “Sumo Royal Albert Hall”.
- Steph Chambers: awarded Gold in Baseball for “Bubble”.
- Yong Teck Lim: awarded Special Merit in Swimming & Diving for “UFO”.


**Michael Heiman, Vice President of Global Sports Photography at Getty Images, said:** “Our photographers continue to amaze us, and we are thrilled to see their artistry celebrated at this year’s World Sports Photography Awards. Their ability to distill the essence of sport—its passion, tension, and humanity—is unmatched. This recognition showcases their extraordinary skill, deep dedication, and limitless creativity. For three decades, we have been committed to telling the stories that unfold on and off the field, and we remain inspired to share the emotion and beauty of sport well into the future.”


Getty Images has the most comprehensive sports collection available today, covering more than 50,000 sporting events annually, and partnering with many of the most significant sports leagues and governing bodies in the world.
