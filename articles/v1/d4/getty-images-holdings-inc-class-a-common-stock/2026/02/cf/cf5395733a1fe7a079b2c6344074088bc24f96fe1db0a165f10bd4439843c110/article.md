# Getty Images and Shutterstock Receive Unconditional Antitrust Clearance from U.S. Department of Justice for Proposed Merger

[BACK TO NEWSROOM](https://newsroom.gettyimages.com/)


[Getty Images](https://newsroom.gettyimages.com/en/getty-images)


Getty Images and Shutterstock Receive Unconditional Antitrust Clearance from U.S. Department of Justice for Proposed Merger


Feb 23, 2026


|[PDF](https://newsroom.gettyimages.com/en/getty-images/getty-images-shutterstock-doj/pdf)


**NEW YORK, February 23, 2026** –[Getty Images Holdings, Inc](https://www.gettyimages.com/) . (NYSE: GETY) (“Getty Images”) and[Shutterstock, Inc](https://www.shutterstock.com/) . (NYSE: SSTK) announced today that the United States Department of Justice (DOJ) has concluded its review of the companies’ proposed merger of equals and the applicable waiting period under the Hart‑Scott‑Rodino Act has expired, without conditions.


“We are very pleased with the DOJ’s decision in recognizing the merits of this transaction,” said Craig Peters, CEO, Getty Images. “With today’s DOJ clearance, we take a significant step forward in bringing together these two companies and unlocking opportunities to strengthen our financial foundation and invest in our future.”


Added Paul Hennessy, CEO, Shutterstock, “By combining our complementary strengths, we will be able to deliver even better service and support for customers and contributors over time. We will continue to work alongside Getty Images to secure the remaining necessary approval for this transaction.”


The merger transaction is expected to deliver substantial synergies across SG&A and CAPEX following close.


Prior to the DOJ’s decision, in the United Kingdom, the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) issued its interim report on February 19 as part of the ongoing Phase 2 review.[Getty Images](https://newsroom.gettyimages.com/en/getty-images/getty-images-cma-report) and Shutterstock are actively engaged with the CMA ahead of the CMA’s final decision due by April 19, to further the CMA’s understanding of the Editorial market and how the merger enhances the combined company’s ability to serve customers, contributors, and partners in a highly competitive marketplace. Based on the merits of the transaction and market realities, Getty Images and Shutterstock remain hopeful that the CMA will reach a conclusion consistent with the DOJ and other regulators around the globe.
