# Daltile’s Green Squared Certification

****


We’re thrilled to announce that all of our North American made products have earned the prestigious Green Squared Certification! This means our tiles meet the highest standards for sustainability, from start to finish. Whether you’re choosing tiles for your home or a large-scale project, you can trust that our certified products are as eco-friendly as they are beautiful.


Green Squared Certification is the tile industry’s top sustainability standard, covering everything from raw material extraction to product disposal. Developed by the Tile Council of North America (TCNA), this certification ensures that our products are environmentally responsible and socially ethical. It’s not just about looking good—it’s about doing good too.


### The Green Squared® Certification Process


#### Manufacturer Applies for Certification


The manufacturer seeking to use the Green Squared Certified mark applies directly to a certification body approved under the Green Squared program by submitting detailed information about the product and practices.


#### Product Evaluation by Certification Body


The certification body evaluates if the product meets ANSI A138.1 through a thorough review of the product, organization, and facility, based on Green Squared Certification Criteria.


#### Certification & Use of Green Squared Mark


Once the certification body determines that the product fully complies with the ANSI A138.1 standard, it authorizes the manufacturer to use the green squared certification mark on the product.


#### Why Green Squared® Matters


Green Squared is the industry’s comprehensive sustainability certification for tiles and tile installation materials. It simplifies green marketing by offering a single standard and recognizable mark, ensuring products meet rigorous, performance-based thresholds.


Certified by international third-party evaluators, Green Squared provides credible assurance that products comply with the stringent sustainability standards outlined in ANSI 138.1, making them ideal for green building projects.
