# Mohawk Group Unveils Shape Theory: A Carpet Tile Collection Inspired by Optimism and Sustainability

**Calhoun, GA.** — Mohawk Group, a leading global flooring manufacturer, unveils Shape Theory, a new carpet tile collection that embodies the manufacturer’s Culture of Care design theme by seamlessly blending optimism and environmental responsibility to create dynamic and collaborative environments.


**A Fusion of Geometry and Nature**


Shape Theory features two distinct yet complementary styles: Corresponding Angle and Cartesian Plane. These styles work together to create a geometric tapestry grounded in nature- inspired elements. Offered in a vibrant palette of 14 colorways, Shape Theory empowers designers to create spaces that foster community and authentic experiences.


**Customization Made Easy**


Mohawk Group's Personal Studio empowers designers to take their vision even further. This online platform allows users to experiment with Shape Theory colorways, visualize them in different settings, and order samples to ensure a perfect fit for their projects.


**Sustainability at the Core**
Shape Theory goes beyond aesthetics, prioritizing human and environmental well-being. The collection features several key sustainability features:


- **Red List Free and Green Label Plus Certified:** Promoting healthy indoor air quality.


- **UL-Certified Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs):** Providing transparency on environmental impact.


- **Living Project Challenge Petal Certified:** Meetin rigorous sustainability standards.


- **Net-Zero Carbon Emissions by 2040 Pledge:** Demonstrating Mohawk Group's commitment to the environment.


**Designing for a Circular Future**


Shape Theory embodies Mohawk Group's commitment to a circular economy. Design for reusability and salvaging, the collection aligns with their Take Back program, further promoting sustainable practices. Shape Theory strives for a net-positive impact by minimizing its footprint on ecosystems and waterways.


**Performance Meets Sustainability**


Shape Theory utilizes EcoFlex Matrix and Colorstrand Fiber, ensuring environmental responsibility without compromising performance. FlexLok+ Adhesive Tabs provide a worry-free installation, while seamless transitions and exceptional durability make Shape Theory a sustainable choice for any space.


"Shape Theory reflects our commitment to fostering a Culture of Care through design innovation and environmental responsibility," said Mark Page, vice president of design for Mohawk Group. "This collection empowers designers to create spaces that inspire collaboration, well-being, and a lasting positive impact."
