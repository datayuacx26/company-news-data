# Durkan Mohawk Group Hospitality Debuts ReBloom Collection at HD Expo 2024

## Unveiling Sustainability Leadership at HD Expo 2024: RevWood Contract Now Carbon Negative!


**Las Vegas, April 29, 2024** Durkan, the hospitality brand for Mohawk Group, is debuting the ReBloom carpet collection at the 2024 HD Expo (booth 4119). Inspired by the restorative power of nature, ReBloom's organic patterns evoke a calming and stress-relieving atmosphere and foster a sense of care for guests, staff, and the environment.


**Immerse in Tranquil Beauty**


ReBloom leans into abstract garden aesthetics, reminiscent of native North American plants, creating a serene escape within your space. Subtle nods to nature like Goldenrod, Texas Bluebonnet, and Milkweed effortlessly connect guests to their surroundings, while the warm and inviting color palette fosters a sense of comfort and belonging. ReBloom’s easy-to-personalize color scheme allows hospitality design professionals to tailor the vibe of the space to perfectly match their vision.


**Beyond Aesthetics: Sustainable Design Solutions**


ReBloom isn't just beautiful, it's kind to the environment. Several Mohawk Group innovations minimize the collection's footprint. Select patterns utilize Color Pulse™ yarn, a breakthrough that delivers stunning visual texture with a low environmental impact – the first of its kind for hospitality carpets. Furthermore, Color Pulse production uses zero water for dyeing, conserving precious resources. Built with lasting performance in mind, these carpets require fewer replacements, further reducing environmental impact. ReBloom goes even further, achieving 100 percent carbon neutrality and an additional 5 percent offset to become "Beyond Carbon Neutral." For those seeking transparency, EPD, HPD, and Declare Label certifications ensure informed material selection for a healthier planet.


**Unprecedented Versatility for Diverse Spaces**


ReBloom's adaptable design ensures a cohesive and visually pleasing floor plan throughout your entire hospitality property. Offered in both Definity and Pattern Perfect qualities, ReBloom combines superior performance with stunning aesthetics. Definity caters to high-traffic areas with its exceptional durability, while Pattern Perfect offers a luxurious feel without sacrificing functionality. This thoughtful range ensures you can create a beautiful and long-lasting foundation for any room size or layout, from cozy guest rooms to expansive ballrooms. ReBloom seamlessly integrates across your space, fostering a sense of visual continuity and tranquility.


**Care for Community: A Partnership with ArtLifting**


ReBloom goes beyond beautiful design to foster a sense of community within hospitality spaces. Through a collaboration with ArtLifting, the collection incorporates the perspectives of diverse artists, like ArtLifting participant Dale Wayne. These artistic influences weave a richer tapestry, reflecting a wider range of experiences and backgrounds. This partnership aligns perfectly with Mohawk Group's Better Together Initiative, which champions inclusivity and social responsibility. Specifying ReBloom is much more than selecting a stunning floor covering, it’s making a statement about the kind of environment hospitality designers cultivate – one that's welcoming and inspiring for all.
