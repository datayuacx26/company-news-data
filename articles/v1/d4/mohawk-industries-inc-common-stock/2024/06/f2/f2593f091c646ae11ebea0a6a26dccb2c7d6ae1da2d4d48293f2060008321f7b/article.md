# Mohawk Group Unveils the Silvius National Forest Collection

**Calhoun, GA.** — Mohawk Group, a leading innovator in commercial flooring solutions, presents the Silvius National Forest Collection, a stunning new line of engineered hardwood flooring that brings the essence of the natural world indoors.


**A Symphony of Nature for Commercial Interiors**


Manufactured from renewable materials, the Silvius National Forest Collection is meticulously crafted to evoke the serenity and beauty of a woodland landscape. Each plank features intricate and authentic grain patterns and organic textures, telling a unique story and creating a calming atmosphere in any space.


This collection boasts a rich and varied color palette, inspired by the natural tones found within a forest. From light browns that exude tranquility to moody mid-tones that add depth and dark hues for a touch of sophistication, Silvius National Forest offers a diverse range to suit any design aesthetic. With a variety of cut lengths, the versatile collection enhances visual appeal and reinforces the authenticity of natural hardwood floors.


**Sustainable Beauty**


Mohawk Group prioritizes environmental responsibility and indoor air quality. A great alternative for designers looking for PVC-free options, the Silvius National Forest Collection is FloorScore® Certified and fully compliant with the Lacey Act. This ensures the use of healthy, sustainable materials and protects global forests by preventing illegal logging and promoting sustainable forest management. Crafted with health impacts in mind, the Silivius collection complies with TSCA and CARB 93120 regulations, ensuring safe chemical management and low formaldehyde emissions for a healthier indoor environment. Furthermore, the collection boasts a 'Beyond Carbon Neutral' designation, meaning it delivers a net-positive impact on the climate.


“The Silvius National Forest Collection offers the perfect blend of aesthetics and environmental consciousness. Each plank is a unique masterpiece, showcasing the timeless beauty of nature while ensuring a healthier planet for generations to come.” said Jackie Dettmar, vice president of marketing, design, and product development for Mohawk Group.
