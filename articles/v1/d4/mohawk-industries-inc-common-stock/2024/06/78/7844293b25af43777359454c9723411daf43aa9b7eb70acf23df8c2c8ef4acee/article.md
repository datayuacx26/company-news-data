# Mohawk Group Buzzes into Design Days in Chicago

**Calhoun, GA. June 10, 2024** — Mohawk Group, a leading supplier of commercial flooring, is thrilled to announce its participation in Design Days Chicago, taking place June 10-12, 2024. While their stunning new Chicago showroom in the Fulton Market Design District is slated for a Fall 2024 opening, design enthusiasts won't have to wait to experience Mohawk Group's commitment to "Culture of Care."


**Immerse Yourself in "the Hive"**


Step inside "the Hive," Mohawk Group's captivating pop-up space located at 817 W. Fulton. This vibrant hub will showcase the essence of Culture of Care, the company's guiding design theme for 2024. Explore this multi-sensory brand immersion and discover how Mohawk Group prioritizes human well-being and environmental responsibility.


**Unwind, Recharge, and Connect**


The Hive is open for exploration Monday and Tuesday, June 10-11, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Wednesday, June 12, from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Fuel your mornings with a complimentary coffee bar available from 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM all three days. On Monday and Tuesday afternoons, unwind with colleagues during a cocktail reception from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM.


**Mohawk Group Beyond the Hive**


Mohawk Group's commitment to the A&D community extends beyond the Hive. Look for them at several exciting activations across Chicago:


- **RECharge Lounge at Metropolis Sustainability Lab at NeoCon:** Mohawk Group sponsors the RECharge Lounge at the 11th Floor of the Merchandise Mart during NeoCon. Take a break, recharge your devices, and learn about innovative products and resources for sustainable design.
- **Lunch at Parked Food Truck:** Join Mohawk Group for lunch on Tuesday, June 11th, at the Parked Food Truck event outside the Daltile Studio/Mohawk Group PS Studio located at 304 W Hubbard Street.
- **IIDA Revel and Afterparty:** Mohawk Group proudly supports the IIDA Revel event, celebrating the design community's accomplishments. Mohawk Group products are also on display at the IIDA Revel Raise the Roof Afterparty.
- **Collab with Turf:** : See Creative Spark and Shape Theory at Turf’s activation on the first floor of the Merchandise Mart during NeoCon (Merchandise Mart space 140.)
- **Collab with Momentum:** Creative Spark and Chromatic Cadence set the stage for Momentum’s celebration of color during NeoCon (Merchandise Mart space 323.)


**Experience the Buzz with Mohawk Group**


"We're thrilled to be buzzing with activity at Design Days Chicago! Our pop-up space, 'the Hive,' is a vibrant exploration of Culture of Care and innovative design solutions,” said Jackie Dettmar, vice president of marketing, design, and product development for Mohawk Group. “We invite everyone to visit the Hive, explore our other activations around the city, and discover how Mohawk Group is shaping the future of flooring.
