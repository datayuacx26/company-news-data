# Mohawk Group Introduces Link Step LVT: Bringing Beautiful Design to BudgetConscious Spaces

**Calhoun, GA.** — Mohawk Group, a leading commercial flooring manufacturer, announces the launch of Link Step LVT, a new collection designed to bring beautiful, high-quality flooring to budget-conscious projects.


**Artful Design, Accessible Price**


Link Step offers a stylish and affordable solution for a variety of settings, including dorm rooms, market-rate housing, tenant improvement projects, and more. Inspired by Mohawk Group's renowned Taking Root collection, Link Step exemplifies the company's commitment to social equity and innovation.


“Link Step LVT is a game-changer for budget-conscious projects,” said Jackie Dettmar, vice president of marketing, design, and product development for Mohawk Group. "By offering beautiful and durable flooring at an accessible price point, we're ensuring more people can experience the positive impact of well-designed spaces."


**Design Flexibility and Durability**


Available in two versatile plank sizes (6" x 48" and 18" x 36"), Link Step offers flexibility for creating a variety of installation patterns and design aesthetics. Its 2mm thickness provides a sleek, modern look while maintaining durability. Featuring a robust 12 mil wear layer and an enhanced urethane finish, Link Step can withstand daily wear and tear in multi-family and dormitory residential spaces.


**Three Styles, Seven Colorways**


Link Step offers three distinct styles and seven stunning colorways, including:


- Solid Bonds: A Classic wood visual that effortlessly integrates with traditional and modern interiors.


- Shared Stride: A striated wood visual that adds texture and visual interest, creating sophisticated and dynamic spaces.


- Harmonic Waves: An eye-catching design featuring a unique, naturally inspired abstract pattern, ideal for those seeking a hard surface with a bold but soft textile visual.


**Applications**


Ideal for a variety of budget-conscious projects, Link Step LVT is a perfect solution for:


- Multifamily Housing


- Student Dormitories


- High-Rise Apartments


- Tenant Improvement Projects
