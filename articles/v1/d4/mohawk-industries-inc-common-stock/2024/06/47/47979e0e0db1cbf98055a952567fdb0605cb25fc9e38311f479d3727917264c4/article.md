# Mohawk Group Unveils ReWilded Refuge Carpet Tile Collection, Celebrating Harmony Between Nature and Urban Design

**Calhoun, GA.** — Mohawk Group introduces ReWilded Refuge, a new carpet tile collection inspired by the growing movement of rewilding – the restoration of natural ecosystems within urban environments.


**Peaceful Coexistence**


ReWilded Refuge reflects the delicate balance between human innovation and the untamed beauty of nature. Drawing inspiration from iconic rewilding projects like New York City's High Line, the collection creates a sense of place that resonates with those who value sustainability and design excellence.


**Two Distinct Expressions**


This collection offers two unique styles:


- **Hidden Landscapes:** Evokes the tranquility of untouched natural environments with serene organic visuals. These titles promote a sense of calm and connection with nature within interior spaces.
- **City Canopy:** Captures the structured elegance of urban architecture, where nature and man-made elements coexist in a dynamic and vibrant way.


**Biomimicry for Depth and Realism**


Each ReWilded Refuge tile features a captivating biomimetic color gradient. These subtle color shifts mimic natural variations found in the wild, adding depth and realism to the collection.


**More Than Aesthetics: A Commitment to Sustainability**


ReWilded Refuge goes beyond aesthetics. The collection is designed to be accessible and versatile for a variety of spaces, reflecting Mohawk Group's commitment to creating inclusive environments that celebrate the interconnectedness of built environments and the natural world.


**A Statement on Sustainability and Design**


"ReWilded Refuge is more than just a collection of carpet tiles – it's a statement about our commitment to sustainability, accessibility, and design excellence," said Mark Page, vice president of design for Mohawk Group. "We invite you to explore ReWilded Refuge and experience the essence of rewilding where each tile tells a story of harmony, beauty, and care for the environment."
