# Mohawk Group Unveils Wild Horizons Carpet Tile Collection, Inspired by Untamed Beauty and Sustainable Design

**Calhoun, GA.** — Mohawk Group introduces Wild Horizons, a new carpet tile collection that captures the essence of untamed landscapes. Inspired by the rich biodiversity of ungardened spaces, Wild Horizons brings the beauty of nature indoors while promoting sustainability and biophilic design.


**A Connection to the Wild**


Wild Horizons draws on the organic patterns and textures found in untouched environments. Earthy tones are complemented by vibrant accent colors reflecting the hues of wildflowers, fostering a sense of connection to the wild within interior spaces.


**Design Versatility**


The collection offers two distinct 12” x 36” plank styles. **Botanic Frame** provides a foundational texture with neutral accents, creating a versatile backdrop for any space, while **Botanic Twist** introduces pops of color inspired by North American wildflowers. Both styles can be used independently or seamlessly combined. When used together, Botanic Twist adds subtle color striations reminiscent of wildflowers scattered across a horizon.


**Sustainable Innovation**


Wild Horizons goes beyond aesthetics, prioritizing both human and environmental well-being. The collection’s sustainability features include:


- **Red List Free:** Ensures no harmful chemicals are present.


- **Living Product Challenge Petal Certified:** Meets rigorous sustainability standards.


- **Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) and Health Product Declaration (HPD):** Provides complete transparency on materials and health impacts.


- **Duracolor Tricor**


**™ Fiber with EcoFlex™ ONE Backing:** Diverts plastic waste from landfills and promotes circular use of materials.
- **100 percent Carbon Neutral + 5 percent Offset:** Aligns with The Climate Pledge and Mohawk Group's goal to go Beyond Carbon Neutral.
- **64 percent Reduced Embodied Carbon Emissions:** Achieved through innovative manufacturing processes.


"Wild Horizons is a testament to our commitment to biophilic design and environmental responsibility. By drawing inspiration from untamed landscapes and incorporating sustainable innovations, Wild Horizons allows us to bring the beauty and vitality of nature indoors, fostering a connection to the wild while ensuring a healthier planet for all," said Mark Page, vice president of design for Mohawk Group.
