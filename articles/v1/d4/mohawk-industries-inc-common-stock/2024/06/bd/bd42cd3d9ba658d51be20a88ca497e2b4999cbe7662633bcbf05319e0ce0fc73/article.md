# Mohawk Group Unveils Hot and Heavy II: A Bold Evolution in Loose Lay LVT

**Calhoun, GA.** — Mohawk Group, a leading provider of commercial flooring solutions, announces the launch of Hot and Heavy II, a dynamic and versatile collection of loose-lay luxury vinyl tiles. (LVT). Building upon the success of the original Hot and Heavy line, this new collection offers a refreshed take on popular visuals and introduces innovative features to cater to a wider range of design needs and budgets.


**Elevated Design Options for All Projects**


Hot and Heavy II presents a suite of updated visuals, including the industry-favorite Bolder, featuring deep taupe and gray in a polished concrete visual, and Secoya, with warm and cool tones in richly grained wood.


**A Commitment to Inclusivity**


Hot and Heavy II also includes connectD, the first-ever fractal design in resilient flooring. Using the research and science of fractals that are known for their stress-reducing properties, Mohawk Group developed connectD to create calming and neurodiverse spaces in education, healthcare, and workplace environments.


**Versatile Formats, Faster Installations**


All Hot and Heavy II styles are available in both the popular 5.0mm loose lay format and a new 2.5mm glue-down option. This multi-tiered approach offers greater price flexibility and allows for seamless coordination with soft-surface collections of different thicknesses and backings. Hot and Heavy II offers large format options for Bolder and connectD (18″ x 36″ planks) and Secoya (10″ x 59″ and 7″ x 48″ planks), contributing to a faster installation process and a more impactful visual experience. Each design features a 20 mil commercial wear layer with Mohawk Group’s M-Force Ultra performance technology, ensuring superior resistance to scratches, scuffs, stains, and dents.


“With Hot and Heavy II, we’ve expanded upon a proven collection, offering designers and architects a wider range of design possibilities without compromising on performance,” said Jackie Dettmar, vice president of marketing, design, and product development for Mohawk Group. “We’re particularly excited about connectD, the first-ever commercial resilient flooring that utilizes fractals to push the boundaries of design and well-being in commercial spaces.”
