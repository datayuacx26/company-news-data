# Mohawk Group Ignites Creativity with the Launch of Creative Spark Carpet Tile

**Calhoun, GA.** — Mohawk Group, a leading innovator in flooring solutions, introduces Creative Spark, a vibrant new carpet tile collection designed to empower designers and architects to bring spaces to life through the power of color.


**Expression Through Color**


Creative Spark celebrates the profound impact color has on shaping the human experience. With an expansive palette of 32 captivating hues, this collection sets the stage for unique stories and cultivates diverse, inclusive environments. Each color seamlessly integrates across Mohawk Group's product lines, encouraging endless creativity and playful exploration.


**Cultivate a Culture of Care**


Beyond aesthetics, Creative Spark fosters a culture of care. By thoughtfully incorporating color into commercial interiors, designers can create spaces that celebrate individuality and inspire a sense of belonging, encouraging collaboration and a more vibrant and dynamic work environment.


**Sustainable Brilliance**
Creative Spark goes beyond aesthetics to deliver a positive environmental impact. Manufactured in Glasgow, Virginia using Mohawk Group's Living Site practices, Creative Spark features solution-dyed nylon fiber and EcoFlex™ Matrix backing for a lighter environmental footprint. The collection is also Living Product Challenge Petal Certified, Beyond Carbon Neutral, Declare Red List Free, and holds certifications for indoor air quality.


**Design Versatility**


Creative Spark offers a single, solid style that can be used for a variety of applications. Install it across entire floors for a bold statement or utilize it as accents and borders to add pops of color and define different areas.
