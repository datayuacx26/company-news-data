# Mohawk Group Opens New Showroom in Chicago

## Mohawk Group Opens New Showroom in Chicago


### *A New Era of Inspiration in the Heart of Fulton Market Design District*


**Chicago, IL. November 18, 2024** — Mohawk Group, a leading provider of commercial flooring, is excited to announce the grand opening of its new Chicago showroom in the flourishing Fulton Market Design District. This spacious, dynamic environment is a place for designers and architects to tap into their creativity and explore Mohawk Group’s extensive range of flooring solutions. Mohawk Group's newest showroom in Chicago marks a significant milestone in the company's commitment to providing exceptional service for clients across the country.


Mohawk Group's 10,000-square-foot showroom is located at 811 W. Fulton Market, joining a burgeoning community of leading commercial interior design brands. The company is excited to serve the Chicago design community year-round and looks forward to participating in Fulton Market Design Days in June 2025.


**A Hub for the Design Community**


Mohawk Group’s new showroom will serve as a hub for inspiration, education, and collaboration, fostering connections between designers, architects, and industry professionals. The new showroom features a range of engaging experiences, including:


- Personal Studio: A cutting-edge space for experimentation and design exploration, equipped with the latest design technology and tools.
- Hospitality Zone: A welcoming space for events, meetings, entertainment and relaxation, featuring a glam room.


“We are thrilled to be a part of the vibrant Fulton Market community,” said Jackie Dettmar, vice president of marketing, design, and product development at Mohawk Group. “This new showroom will provide our clients with an unparalleled design experience and allow us to connect with the local design community on a deeper level.”


Mohawk Group’s commitment to innovation, sustainability, and design excellence is evident in every aspect of the new showroom. The space embodies the company’s “Believe In Better” philosophy, inspiring designers and architects to create spaces that are not only beautiful but also sustainable and functional. By providing a platform for creativity and collaboration, Mohawk Group is empowering the design community to build a better future.
