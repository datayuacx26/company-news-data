# Mohawk Group Unveils Fractal Findings

## Mohawk Group Unveils Fractal Findings


**Dalton, Ga. December 20, 2024** — Mohawk Group, a leading commercial flooring provider, is excited to introduce the Fractal Findings collection, a groundbreaking range of carpet tiles inspired by the intricate patterns found in nature.


**A Scientific Approach to Design**


Found in everything from snowflakes to coastlines, Fractals are repeating patterns that occur throughout our natural world. Human beings are fluent at perceiving fractals in nature and receiving their benefits. In collaboration with 13&9 Design and Dr. Richard Taylor of Fractals Research, Mohawk Group has harnessed the power of fractal design to create spaces that promote well-being and productivity. Dr. Taylor’s research has shown that fractal patterns can significantly reduce stress and improve cognitive function. By incorporating mid-D value fractal patterns into the Fractal Findings collection, Mohawk Group has created a collection that promotes relaxation, reduces stress, and enhances overall well-being.


**Harnessing the Power of Fractals**


The Fractal Findings collection offers a range of neutral base colors and vibrant accent colors, allowing for customization and personalization. The collection features two captivating 24” X 24” carpet tile styles:


- **discoverD:** Inspired by the intricate patterns of animal migration paths, this style evokes a sense of calm and tranquility.
- **exploreD:** Inspired by the dynamic fractures of sea ice, this style creates a sense of wonder and exploration.


**A Commitment to Sustainability**


Mohawk Group’s Fractal Findings collection is not only beautiful but also sustainable. It is Living Product Challenge Petal Certified, ensuring a positive impact on the environment. Manufactured at the company’s Glasgow, Virginia Living Site, these carpet tiles are made with ColorStrand® solution-dyed nylon fiber and EcoFlex™ Matrix backing, minimizing environmental impact.


“Science has shown us the power of fractal patterns to reduce stress and promote well-being,” said Jackie Dettmar, vice president of product, design, and marketing at Mohawk Group. “With Fractal Findings, we’re bringing this knowledge to life in a tangible way, creating spaces that are not only beautiful but also beneficial for people who use them.”
