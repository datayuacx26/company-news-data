# Mohawk Group Enhances MohawkGO Room Visualizer with Expanded Content and Innovative Features

**Calhoun, GA. March 6, 2024** — Mohawk Group, a leading global flooring manufacturer, announced today significant enhancements to its award-winning MohawkGO room visualizer app. Building upon its existing capabilities, MohawkGO now offers an even richer and more immersive experience for architects, designers, and consumers alike.


MohawkGO harnesses the power of augmented reality (AR) to transform how users envision flooring in their spaces. With just a few taps on their mobile device, users can select from Mohawk Group's expansive collection of flooring products and visualize them directly within their chosen room. Whether it's luxurious carpet, resilient hard surfaces, or custom-designed broadloom, MohawkGO enables users to experiment with colors, patterns, and textures in real time, bringing their design vision to life before installation.


**Expanded Content:**


The most notable update is the comprehensive integration of Durkan products within the app. This allows users to seamlessly visualize the full spectrum of Mohawk Group offerings, including the renowned hospitality flooring from the Durkan brand, in any setting. This expanded content empowers designers to explore a wider range of aesthetic possibilities and create truly cohesive design solutions for their projects.


**Custom Broadloom Visualization:**


MohawkGO now features the innovative ability to visualize custom broadloom products in real-time. Users can upload their custom broadloom designs allowing them to instantly see how these bespoke flooring choices will translate within a specific space. This functionality offers designers and clients greater flexibility and control over the design process.


**"We are thrilled to expand the capabilities of MohawkGO and provide users with even greater access to our comprehensive flooring portfolio,"** said \[Name\], \[Title\] at Mohawk Group.


**"The inclusion of Durkan products and the ability to visualize custom broadloom options further empower designers and consumers to explore their creative vision and make informed decisions about their flooring needs."**


**MohawkGO is available for free download on the App Store and Google Play.**
