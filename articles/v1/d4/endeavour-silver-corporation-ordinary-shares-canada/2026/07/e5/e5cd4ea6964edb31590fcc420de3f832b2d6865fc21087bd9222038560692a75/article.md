# Endeavour Silver | Women in Mining

**The Importance of Women in Mining**


The mining industry has long been shaped by innovation, resilience, and collaboration, and women are playing an increasingly vital role in its continued success.


At Endeavour Silver, we recognize that diverse perspectives strengthen our operations, our communities and our future.


Beyond stepping into leadership and technical roles, women contribute across all areas of mining, from geology, plant operations, engineering, and environmental management to leadership, finance, community relations and beyond.


Endeavour Silver aims to support career development, mentorship and inclusive workplaces to help create economic opportunities and a positive social impact in the regions we operate.


**Honoring Recent Promotions**


**** We are thrilled to acknowledge several well-deserved promotions earned over the past year through dedication, hard work, and exceptional leadership:


- Allison Pettit – Vice President, Investor Relations
- Adriana Gomez – Director of Finance
- Alejandra Hincapie – Corporate Secretary
- Yesica Diaz – Finance Manager


These promotions reflect a continued commitment to recognizing talent and supporting the advancement of employees through Endeavour Silver.


As we celebrate their accomplishments, we also acknowledge all the women across the mining sector whose contributions continue to progress the industry in meaningful ways.
