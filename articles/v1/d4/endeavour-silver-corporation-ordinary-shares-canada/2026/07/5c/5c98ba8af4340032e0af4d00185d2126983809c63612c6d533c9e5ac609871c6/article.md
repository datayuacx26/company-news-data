# Endeavour Silver | Socially Responsible Company Distinction 2025

We are proud to announce that our Guanaceví and Bolañitos mines have once again received the *Socially Responsible Company Distinction* , a recognition that highlights our ongoing commitment to ethical practices, community engagement, and sustainable development. This marks 11 consecutive years for Guanaceví and 13 consecutive years for Bolañitos of being recognized for our dedication to responsible mining and the positive impact we strive to make every day.


Born in Mexico as an initiative of the Mexican Center for Philanthropy A.C. (CEMEFI) and the Alliance for Corporate Social Responsibility in Mexico (AliaRSE), the ESR Distinction is an annual recognition granted to organizations that incorporate socially responsible practices and commitments into their business operations. These include areas such as quality of life within the company, business ethics, community engagement, environmental care and preservation, and corporate social responsibility (CSR) management. These distinctive awards are given annually in Mexico by the CEMEFI and Aliarse.


The ESR Distinction is not a one-time commitment; companies must renew it annually to maintain a strong and evolving corporate responsibility culture. Ultimately, the ESR Distinction guides companies along the path of social responsibility.


Since first earning this award in 2012, we have worked diligently to uphold the highest standards in environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance. This award reflects the dedication of our entire team to operate responsibly and support the communities where we live and work.


From everyone at our Guanaceví and Bolañitos mines, to our entire team – thank you. Your dedication, integrity, and passion have driven 13 years of continuous recognition, and it’s your daily actions that define who we are as a responsible mining company. Together, we’ve built something that goes far beyond silver: a legacy of responsibility, trust, and positive impact.


As we celebrate this milestone, we reaffirm our commitment to being a socially responsible company—not just in recognition, but in action, every day. We look forward to continuing this journey with the same passion and purpose that has guided us from the start.


Alejandro Ontiveros, Superintendent of Human Resources, and Roberto Cossio, Superintendent of Community Relations, attended the event in Mexico City on behalf of our Guanaceví operations.
