# Launching nao Stories


Stories turn a chat with your data into a shareable, editable, versioned report. Watch the launch demo and read how it works.


24 March 2026


By Claire Gouzé


Founder @ nao


Today we're launching **nao Stories** .


## What Stories does


Stories turn a chat with your analytics agent into a persistent, shareable document. Ask the agent to write a story on any KPI, and you get a structured report with live charts, tables, and narrative text, ready to edit and share.


- **Create** from any chat: "Write a story on new user growth since the last release."
- **Edit** in a Notion-style block editor. Reorder, rewrite, delete.
- **Version** every save, so you can iterate without losing prior states.
- **Share** with your whole project or specific people. Recipients can continue chatting from the story.
- **Go live** : turn any story into a live report that refreshes on a schedule, with the narrative regenerated to match the new numbers.
- **Export** to PDF or HTML when you need a fixed-layout deliverable.


## Why we built it


We kept hearing the same thing from users of the analytics agent: they could get to the answer fast, but sharing it still meant screenshots, copy-paste, and slides. Stories closes that loop. Exploration, reporting, and collaboration all happen in one place, on top of the same governed context your agent already uses.
