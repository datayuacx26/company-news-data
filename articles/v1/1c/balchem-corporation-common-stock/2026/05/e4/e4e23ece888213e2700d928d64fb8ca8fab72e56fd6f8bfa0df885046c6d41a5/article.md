# MSM for Knee Health Study: Clinical Research Supporting Knee Comfort and Quality of Life with OptiMSM®

## **Clinical Evidence Supporting Knee Health with OptiMSM®**


A 12-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial investigated the effects of oral methylsulfonylmethane supplementation using OptiMSM® in healthy Japanese adults experiencing mild knee pain. 88 participants were randomized to receive either 2,000 mg of OptiMSM® daily or a placebo.


Knee-related quality of life was evaluated using the Japanese Knee Osteoarthritis Measure, a validated questionnaire assessing knee condition and daily function. After 12 weeks, participants supplementing with OptiMSM® demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in total JKOM scores compared to placebo, supporting measurable outcomes related to MSM knee health.
