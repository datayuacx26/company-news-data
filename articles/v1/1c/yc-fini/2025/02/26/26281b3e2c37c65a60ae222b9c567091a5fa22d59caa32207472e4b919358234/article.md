# Fini AI vs Agentforce: Which Salesforce AI Wins?

### Overview and Core Comparison


**1. What is the main difference between Fini and AgentForce?**
Fini is an AI-native support agent that takes real actions like issuing refunds or resolving tickets autonomously, while AgentForce is an assistant tool built into Salesforce that only retrieves knowledge and supports human agents.


**2. Is AgentForce a true autonomous AI agent?**
No, AgentForce acts more like a search assistant by surfacing help articles from Salesforce. It does not take any actions or learn from data over time like Fini does.


**3. How does Fini's AI agent differ in functionality from Salesforce AI?**
Fini doesn’t just answer questions, it completes tasks end-to-end—like canceling a subscription or issuing a refund—based on live data and user context, making it a full agent rather than just a helper.


**4. Which platform supports real-time actions—Fini or Salesforce AI?**
Only Fini supports real-time actions like refunds, cancellations, and status checks. Salesforce AI does not initiate or perform such actions autonomously.


**5. Is Fini limited to Salesforce environments?**
No, Fini is platform-agnostic. It can work seamlessly across Zendesk, Intercom, Slack, APIs, and many others, making it more flexible than Salesforce’s tightly coupled solution.


### Learning and Knowledge Management


**6. Can Fini learn from past tickets and evolve over time?**
Yes, Fini’s AI learns continuously from ticket history, internal documentation, and user feedback, improving over time without needing manual re-training.


**7. Does Salesforce AI auto-update its knowledge base?**
No, Salesforce AI requires manual effort to update knowledge base content, which can lead to stale or outdated responses.


**8. How does Fini auto-update its understanding of new support topics?**
Fini identifies patterns from incoming tickets and internal sources like product updates, filling knowledge gaps automatically with reinforcement learning.


**9. What kind of knowledge sources can Fini ingest?**
Fini can learn from Google Drive, Notion, Confluence, past tickets, raw documents, product changelogs, and APIs, offering broader context than Salesforce AI.


**10. How does Fini help identify knowledge gaps?**
Fini’s analytics layer detects where the AI struggled to respond accurately and suggests areas where documentation or training data is insufficient.


### Action Automation


**11. Can Fini complete tasks on behalf of support agents?**
Yes, Fini can take real actions such as canceling subscriptions, processing refunds, updating customer info, or triggering workflows based on your business rules.


**12. What kinds of actions can Salesforce AI perform?**
Salesforce AI does not take autonomous actions. It only provides knowledge or directs customers to help articles.


**13. Can Fini pull real-time data from APIs?**
Yes, Fini connects to APIs and internal systems to pull live data—like order status, payment confirmation, or policy updates—to deliver accurate answers.


**14. How does Fini prevent incorrect actions from being executed?**
Fini uses confidence thresholds, strict guardrails, and predefined action flows to ensure it only performs tasks within safe parameters.


**15. Is it possible to monitor what actions Fini is taking?**
Yes, Fini includes step-by-step action logs with explainability, so teams can see what the agent did and why.


### Platform Compatibility and Vendor Lock-In


**16. What happens if I move away from Salesforce—can I still use AgentForce?**
No, Salesforce AI only works inside Salesforce. If you switch platforms, you’ll lose all your AI progress and must start over.


**17. How does Fini eliminate vendor lock-in?**
Fini is designed to work across multiple platforms. If your company switches from Salesforce to Zendesk, for example, Fini retains all training and performance history.


**18. Can I deploy Fini on multiple tools simultaneously?**
Yes, Fini can run on Zendesk, Intercom, Slack, Front, WhatsApp, Teams, or even a custom widget—all from one AI model.


**19. How fast can I migrate Fini to a new platform?**
Fini can be redeployed in minutes to a new tool using existing training and configurations without requiring a fresh setup.


**20. Does AgentForce integrate with external support tools?**
No, AgentForce is native to Salesforce and does not natively extend to other tools like Zendesk or Intercom.


### Setup and Maintenance


**21. How difficult is it to set up Fini?**
Fini offers zero-code setup options, a prompt builder, and prebuilt AI flows, making it easy for non-technical teams to deploy and configure.


**22. Is Salesforce AI easy to configure?**
Salesforce AI setup can be complex, especially if custom workflows are needed. It lacks the no-code automation capabilities Fini offers.


**23. Does Fini require ongoing training by admins?**
No, Fini learns continuously from user feedback and support data, reducing the need for manual maintenance.
