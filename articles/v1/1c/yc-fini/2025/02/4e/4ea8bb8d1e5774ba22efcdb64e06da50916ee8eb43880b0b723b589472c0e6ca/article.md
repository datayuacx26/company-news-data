# Fini AI vs Ada

### Platform Capabilities


**1. What makes Fini a native AI agent instead of just a chatbot?**
Fini is designed as a native AI support agent that behaves like a real teammate. Unlike chatbots that only respond with pre-written answers, Fini can fetch live data, trigger actions, break down complex queries, and operate naturally across platforms like Zendesk, Intercom, Slack, and more.


**2. How does Fini handle real-time data fetching compared to Ada?**
Fini connects directly to your internal tools and databases via APIs, allowing it to pull live data on-the-fly. This enables contextual, accurate, and timely responses. Ada, on the other hand, relies heavily on static flows or pre-programmed responses.


**3. Can I use multiple AI agents in Fini for different departments?**
Yes, Fini allows you to create and manage multiple AI agents, each with distinct knowledge and tone. This means you can tailor agents for sales, support, billing, or tech—something Ada doesn’t offer out of the box.


**4. Does Fini support complex workflows and escalations within Zendesk?**
Absolutely. Fini natively integrates with Zendesk and respects your ticketing rules, macros, and triggers. It can also loop in human agents for sensitive or unclear queries through intelligent confidence-based routing.


**5. Is tone configuration possible in Fini?**
Yes, you can configure Fini to respond in different tones—professional, friendly, or humorous—based on your brand’s personality. This ensures a consistent voice across customer interactions.


**6. What is the zero-code setup advantage of Fini?**
Fini offers plug-and-play deployment with intuitive UI and automatic data syncing, so you can go live in days—not weeks—without writing code. Ada also offers zero-code setup, but lacks the same native depth of automation.


**7. How does Fini ensure long-term AI learning?**
Fini uses a reinforcement learning loop powered by live agent feedback and outcome tracking. It continues to refine responses and improve performance across use cases—whereas Ada is more flow-based and less iterative.


**8. How does sentiment analysis in Fini work compared to Ada?**
Fini analyzes customer tone and urgency in real-time to prioritize tickets and customize escalation. Ada lacks native sentiment analysis, which limits its contextual response accuracy.


### AI Capabilities


**9. What is Fini's AI accuracy compared to Ada?**
Fini consistently delivers 95%+ resolution accuracy thanks to multi-layered LLMs, fine-tuned on client-specific data. Ada averages around 80%, making it less reliable for complex queries.


**10. How does Fini break down complex questions?**
Fini decomposes long queries into logical sub-questions and routes each to specialized agents, ensuring accurate resolution even when customer messages contain multiple topics.


**11. Can Fini generate a knowledge base from raw documents?**
Yes, Fini automatically parses documents like PDFs, Notion pages, and Confluence articles to build structured, searchable knowledge bases. Ada does not support this natively.


**12. Can Fini build a knowledge base from past chats and emails?**
Absolutely. Fini’s Chat-to-Knowledge Base feature turns your historical support data into training material and FAQs. This allows you to bootstrap support automation even without a formal help center.


**13. What LLM infrastructure powers Fini?**
Fini uses a multi-LLM architecture to combine reasoning, retrieval, and generation. It dynamically routes queries across GPT-4, Claude, and open-source models for optimal performance.


**14. Does Fini support automated model evaluation?**
Yes. Fini’s evaluation engine scores response accuracy, confidence, and alignment using real conversation data. This helps track performance by topic and identify improvement areas.


**15. What makes Fini’s reinforcement learning better than Ada’s?**
Fini learns from real agent feedback, QA scores, and customer satisfaction metrics—feeding this into model retraining pipelines. Ada offers limited feedback-based learning and relies more on flow optimization.


**16. Does Fini support LLM reasoning and filtering?**
Yes. Fini filters hallucinated responses, performs logic checks, and uses reasoning modules to make answers more accurate. These capabilities are missing or limited in Ada.


### Security and Privacy


**17. What security certifications does Fini comply with?**
Fini is certified for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 42001. These certifications ensure top-tier data protection and operational integrity.


**18. How does Fini handle sensitive data like PII?**
Fini includes an advanced PII shielding layer that automatically redacts sensitive user data like SSNs and credit card numbers before processing, ensuring compliance and privacy.


**19. What data processing agreements does Fini offer?**
Fini has a strict DPA with OpenAI that ensures customer data is never used for model training. All data is automatically purged from OpenAI systems after processing.


**20. Is Fini GDPR compliant?**
Yes. Fini offers full GDPR compliance, including data export, deletion requests, and secure data handling for EU-based customers.
