# VirTra to Exhibit at the 2026 SHOT Show

**VirTra (Nasdaq: VTSI)** will exhibit at the 2026 SHOT Show in Las Vegas from **January 20–23, 2026** . At **Booth #7550** in Caesars Forum the company will demonstrate its V-300 immersive simulator, V-One portable simulator, and V-XR extended-reality platform with live hands-on sessions.


The demonstrations target law enforcement, military, and security training for judgmental use-of-force, firearms proficiency, de-escalation, and scenario-based decision making.
