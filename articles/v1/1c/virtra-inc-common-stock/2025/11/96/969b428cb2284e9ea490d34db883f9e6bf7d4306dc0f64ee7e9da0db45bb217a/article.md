# VirTra Appoints Grant Barber to Advisory Board

**VirTra (Nasdaq: VTSI)** announced the appointment of **Grant A. Barber** to its Advisory Board on November 4, 2025. Barber brings more than **35 years** of international financial and operational leadership across technology, telecommunications, and industrial technology markets.


His background includes serving as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer at Hughes Communications, leading that company’s transformation into a public company and overseeing regulatory reporting, public filings, investor engagement, and multinational governance. Barber also held senior roles at Acterna and nearly two decades at Nortel Networks.
