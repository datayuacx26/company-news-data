## Introduction to Pre-Calculus


When you start any new class, it’s always a good idea to have a basic understanding of the subject before diving into the details. This is especially true when it comes to more advanced math courses. You’ll move from subjects like algebra, geometry, and trigonometry to even more complex topics like pre-calculus and calculus.


## What is the Definition of Pre-Calculus?


Pre-calculus is like a preview of what's to come in calculus. It’s a course that brings together everything you’ve learned in algebra, geometry, and trigonometry and pushes it up a level.You’ll be able to confidently tackle topics like: functions, shapes, and how things move and change.


## Key Elements of Pre-Calculus


Pre-calculus covers several critical topics that lay the groundwork for future math courses.


### Functions and Graphs


**Linear, Quadratic, and Polynomial Functions** : Students learn to identify, graph, and solve these types of functions.


**Exponential and Logarithmic Functions** : Pre-calculus introduces exponential growth/decay and their inverse, logarithmic functions.


**Rational and Piecewise Functions** : Students explore the behavior and characteristics of these more complex functions.


### Trigonometry


**Unit Circle** : Understanding angles and radian measure.


**Trigonometric Identities** : Students memorize and apply key identities like sine, cosine, and tangent.


**Graphs of Trigonometric Functions** : Learning how to graph and analyze trigonometric functions and their transformations.


### Complex Numbers


**Imaginary Unit (“i”)** : Understanding the definition of the imaginary unit (“i”) and how to solve equations with complex solutions.


**Operations with Complex Numbers** : Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of complex numbers.


### Sequences and Series


**Arithmetic Sequences** : Finding common differences and sums of terms.


**Geometric Sequences** : Understanding ratios and calculating sums of geometric series.


### Conic Sections


**Parabolas, Ellipses, and Hyperbolas** : Students analyze the graphs and properties of these conic sections.


**Equations of Conic Sections** : Students learn how to write and transform equations for parabolas, ellipses, and hyperbolas.


### Vectors and Parametric Equations


**Vectors** : Introduction to vector addition, scalar multiplication, and vector applications.


**Parametric Equations** : How to represent curves using parametric forms.


### Limits (Introductory Concepts)


While limits are a central part of calculus, pre-calculus introduces basic limit concepts to prepare students for the more rigorous calculus definitions.


## How Does Pre-Calculus Prepare You for College & Beyond?


Pre-calculus plays a vital role in the academic journey of students, especially those pursuing STEM careers.


-


**Builds a Strong Math Foundation:** Mastering pre-calculus concepts like functions, limits, and trigonometry will prepare you for future math courses.


-


**Supports Real-World Applications:** Concepts like exponential growth, trigonometry, and limits are used in fields like physics, engineering, and computer science.


-


**Prepares You for College Entrance Exams:** Standardized tests like the SAT and ACT often include questions on pre-calculus concepts, so understanding them can boost your test scores.
