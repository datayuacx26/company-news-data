# Welcome to Alaska

Check out these Alaskan views![Landstar BCOs](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/owner-operators/) David Johnson and Lloyd Tubbs snapped photos of their trucks upon arrival at the Alaskan border. The BCOs had just finished a 10-day and 4,400 mile transport from the southeast corner of the “lower 48” to Fairbanks, Alaska.


Landstar Agent Clyde Carey, owner of 49th Freight in Anchorage, Alaska, shared the photos via social media calling Johnson and Tubbs “rock star BCOs” for making the very long haul and essential delivery for his customer.


Carey says the owner-operators are among his agency’s, “year-round, go-to BCOs, for delivering on-time in Alaska, with no claims, no matter how long the haul.”
