# Landstar Selected as a Top 100 Trucker by Inbound Logistics

The editors at *Inbound Logistics* have selected Landstar System, Inc. as a[Top 100 Trucker for 2022](https://www.inboundlogistics.com/top-100-truckers/) . Landstar appeared in the alphabetical list of winners published by the magazine in September.


Landstar provides customers with[customized trucking services](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/) in North America including truckload, LTL, heavy/specialized, cross-border, expedited, hazmat and temperature controlled.


According to *Inbound Logistics* , several hundred companies submitted credentials for this year’s list. The magazine’s editors evaluated surveys and questionnaires, conducted online research, and spoke with shippers and truckers before making their selection. *Inbound Logistics* says this list benchmarks the types of services transportation buyers value when evaluating current partners or searching for new ones.

[Inbound Logistics](https://www.inboundlogistics.com/) is a magazine and website targeted toward business logistics and supply chain managers.
