# Landstar Ranks No. 8 on the Commercial Carrier Journal 2022 CCJ Top 250 List

Landstar System, Inc. ranked No. 8 in the *Commercial Carrier Journal*[2022 CCJ Top 250](https://www.ccjdigital.com/ccj-top-250) list of the largest for-hire carriers in North America with a U.S. operating authority. The company moved up one spot from the 2021 list.


Customers have access to more than 11,800 leased owner-operator trucks plus more than 90,000 approved independent third-party capacity providers and a wide array of trailing equipment through[the Landstar network](https://www.landstar.com/why-landstar/) .


*Commercial Carrier Journal* has ranked the top active, for-hire trucking companies since the late 1960s. The rankings are comprised of a blended data set used to calculate the annual list, including revenues and counts on power units, trailers, drivers and other company information.


[Commercial Carrier Journal](https://www.ccjdigital.com/) is a multi-channel (website, printed publication and e-newsletters) brand for freight transportation business news targeted toward fleet executives and managers.
