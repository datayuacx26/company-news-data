# Chief Executive's Circle - Class of 2021

The Class of 2021 Chief Executive’s Circle (CEC) members includes agents representing 23 independent Landstar agencies, plus 10 agencies elevated to the Gold Level.


Each new member of the CEC has been an agent-of-record with Landstar for a minimum of 10 consecutive years and has generated a minimum of $40 million in Landstar revenue.


The Gold Level honors long-term performance and commitment to excellence in safety and customer service. Each Gold Level inductee has been the agent-of-record for a minimum of 15 consecutive years, starting on or after Landstar’s inception in 1988, and must have generated a minimum of $125 million in Landstar revenue.
