# Landstar Named a Top 100 3PL Provider by Inbound Logistics

The editors at *Inbound Logistics* named Landstar System, Inc. among the best 3PL providers again in 2022. Landstar appeared in the alphabetical list of the[2022 Top 100 3PLs](https://www.inboundlogistics.com/articles/2022-top-100-3pls/) published by the magazine in July.


Landstar provides customers with[customized transportation solutions](https://www.landstar.com/services/) in North America and across the globe.


“Each year, *Inbound Logistics* editors select the best logistics solutions providers by carefully evaluating submitted information, conducting personal interviews and online research, and comparing that data to our audience’s burgeoning global supply chain and logistics challenges,” said Felecia Stratton, editor of *Inbound Logistics.* “ *IL* editors selected this year's class of Top 100 3PLs from a pool of more than 300 companies. The service providers we selected are companies that, in the opinion of the editors of *Inbound Logistics* , offer the diverse operational capabilities and experience driving efficiency and operational excellence for today’s demand-driven enterprises.”


*Inbound Logistics* is a magazine and website targeted toward business logistics and supply chain managers.
