# Landstar Recognized with Logistics Management 2022 Quest for Quality Award

Landstar Transportation Logistics, Inc. has ranked eighth in the Dry Freight Carriers category of the *Logistics Management*[2022 Quest for Quality Award](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/quest_for_quality_2022_truckload) . The awards were announced in the publication’s August issue.


Landstar provides[truckload services](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/truckload-van/) with safe, on-time, claim-free capacity at a cost-effective price.


Since 1993, *Logistics Management* has surveyed a representative sample of its readers, who are qualified buyers of logistics and transportation services, to evaluate the transportation logistics service providers they use. The annual[Quest for Quality survey](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/39th_annual_quest_for_quality_awards_2022_service_prevails_through_trying_t?oly_enc_id=8808C9454678A3S) asks readers to rank companies on the following criteria: on-time performance, value, information technology, customer service, and equipment and operations. In order to be an award winner, a company must receive at least 5% of the category vote. According to *Logistics Management* , the survey produced more than 3,800 total responses this year.


*Logistics Management* is a magazine and website targeted toward logistics professionals and buying influencers of logistics services, technology and equipment.
