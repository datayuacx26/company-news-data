# Landstar Recognized by NSC for Online Safety Programs

Landstar was honored with a[2021 Excellence in Driver Safety Training Center Award](https://www.nsc.org/safety-training/defensive-driving/become-a-ddc-instructor/ddc-awards) by the National Safety Council (NSC) during the 2022 NSC Safety Congress & Expo in San Diego, California. NSC recognized Landstar for its online defensive and safe driving modules provided to independent owner-operators leased to the company.


##### Michael Slevin, Jr., supervisor at Landstar’s Field Operation Center in Mesa, Arizona, received the award on behalf of the company from NSC’s Ryan Pietzsch.


---


According to the NSC, the annual awards recognize excellence in advancing progress on the road to zero deaths by delivering defensive driving programs.


As a[safety-first company](https://www.landstar.com/why-landstar/safety-security/) , Landstar is committed to safety initiatives and awareness programs for owner-operators leased to the company.


The[National Safety Council](https://www.nsc.org/) is a nonprofit, public service organization promoting health and safety in the United States since 1913.
