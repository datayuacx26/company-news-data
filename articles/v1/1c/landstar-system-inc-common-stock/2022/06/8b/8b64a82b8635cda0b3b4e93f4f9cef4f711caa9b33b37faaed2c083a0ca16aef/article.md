# Landstar ranks No. 6 on Transport Topics’ Top 100 For-Hire Carriers List

Landstar System, Inc. has ranked No. 6 on the[2022 Transport Topics Top 100 For-Hire Carriers list](https://www.ttnews.com/for-hire/rankings/2022) . The company moved up three spots from the publication’s 2021 list after generating a record $6.5 billion in revenue during fiscal year 2021.


Landstar also ranked in several sub-sectors of the For-Hire Carriers list, including:


- [Flatbed/Heavy Specialized](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/heavy-haul/) Ranked No. 2
- [Truckload/Dedicated](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/truckload-van/) Ranked No. 2 (up one spot from the 2021 list)
- [Intermodal/Drayage](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/rail-intermodal/) Ranked No. 12 (up three spots from the 2021 list)
- [Less-Than-Truckload](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/less-than-truckload-ltl/) Ranked No. 27


In addition, Landstar ranked No. 18 on the[2022 Transport Topics Top 100 Logistics Companies list](https://www.ttnews.com/logistics/rankings/2022) , up from No. 22 in 2021. Landstar also ranked No. 7 in the Freight Brokerage sub-sector of the Logistics Companies list.


Customers can access more than 102,000 capacity providers through[the Landstar network](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/) , including 11,800+ leased owner-operator trucks, and 1,200 independent Landstar agents.


##### Companies on the *Transport Topics* Top For-Hire Carriers list are common and contract freight carriers operating in the United States, Canada and Mexico, and are ranked on the basis of annual gross revenue. Companies on the *Transport Topics* Top 100 Logistics Companies list provide logistics services, including freight brokerage, freight forwarding, warehousing and dedicated contract carriage in North America and are ranked on the basis of annual gross revenue.
