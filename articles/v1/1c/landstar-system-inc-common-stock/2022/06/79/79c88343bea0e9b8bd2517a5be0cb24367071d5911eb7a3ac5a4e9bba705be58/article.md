# 2022 Sponsor Spotlight – Pilot Flying J

Landstar would like to thank Pilot Flying J for providing assistance throughout the year to support Landstar independent owner-operators and our programs including LCAPP and Landstar’s annual events – the BCO All-Star Celebration and our convention for independent freight agents.


[Pilot Flying J](https://pilotflyingj.com/) , offers Landstar independent owner-operators exclusive fuel rebates at its owned and operated travel centers in the United States.


Pilot Flying J is one of the dozens of companies to participate in the[Landstar Contractors’ Advantage Purchasing Program](http://lcapp.com/fuel/fuel-purchasing-details/) (LCAPP®) – a unique, cost savings program available to Landstar owner-operators offering discounts on products and services to help lower their operating costs. Through LCAPP, Landstar owner-operators receive a 25 cents per gallon discount at all Pilot or Flying J owned and operated travel centers in the United States.


In addition to an exclusive fuel rebate, Landstar owner-operators receive 10 points per gallon purchased on their Pilot Flying J Professional Driver myRewards® card. Landstar owner-operators also have access to more than 75,000 truck parking spaces, 5,300 deluxe showers, and 6,200 diesel lanes in Pilot Flying J’s North America network of travel centers.


#### About Pilot Flying J:


Pilot Travel Centers LLC, founded in 1958 and headquartered in Knoxville, Tennessee, has grown its network to more than 800 retail and fueling locations and as the third largest tanker fleet in North America. Pilot supplies more than 14 billion gallons of fuel per year to the market. Pilot Company serves 1.5 million guests per day and provides over 70,000 fleet customers with solutions for fuel, credit, factoring, services and rewards. More information on locations and rewards are available in the myRewards Plus™ app.


Pilot Company is currently ranked No. 7 on Forbes' list of America's Largest Private Companies. For additional information about Pilot Company, its 28,000 team members and commitment to giving back, visit[www.pilotcompany.com](http://www.pilotcompany.com/) .
