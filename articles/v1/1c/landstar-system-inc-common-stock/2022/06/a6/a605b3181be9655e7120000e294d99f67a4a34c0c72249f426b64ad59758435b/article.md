# 2022 Sponsor Spotlight – Insurance Office of America

Landstar would like to thank 2022 diamond-level sponsor Insurance Office of America (IOA) for its support and sponsorship of this year’s annual events: the Landstar Agent Convention and the Landstar BCO All-Star Celebration.


[IOA](https://www.ioausa.com/industries/transportation/) offers Landstar’s independent owner-operators workers’ compensation insurance coverage plus a unique high-value trip transit cargo insurance policy for Landstar’s high-value cargo shipments. IOA has the knowledge of the transportation and logistics industry that enables their team to help clients plan for current and future liabilities.


> “In the U.S., vehicles carrying people and goods cover over 3 billion miles a year on the road, according to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. What part of our economy isn’t impacted by the transportation industry? Whether you operate a fleet or you’re a single owner-operator, having a clear understanding of the specific regulations, duties of care, risks, and criminal activity your operation may be vulnerable to is imperative.” – IOAUSA.com


### About IOA


IOA is among the fastest-growing independent insurance agencies in the United States – ranked 28th on Business Insurance’s 2021 100 Largest Brokers of U.S. list. The company was named a National Underwriter Agency of the Year. Headquartered in Longwood, Florida, IOA has more than 1,300 associates located in over 60 office locations in the U.S. and the United Kingdom.
