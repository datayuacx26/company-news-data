# A Message of Appreciation from Landstar

## Thank You Landstar BCOs!


We’re celebrating Landstar BCOs this week - National Truck Driver Appreciation Week (NTDAW). Each year during this week, America salutes professional truck drivers for their commitment to one of the economy’s most demanding and important roles.


From Sept. 13 -19, Landstar has planned a virtual celebration for its nearly 10,000 business capacity owners (BCOs), the term used for independent owner-operators exclusively leased to Landstar.


To kick off our celebration, please take a moment to watch this message from Landstar.


Be sure to follow Landstar’s virtual celebration throughout NTDAW on our official social media channels, and join in by using the hashtag #ThankYouBCOs in your own social media posts.
