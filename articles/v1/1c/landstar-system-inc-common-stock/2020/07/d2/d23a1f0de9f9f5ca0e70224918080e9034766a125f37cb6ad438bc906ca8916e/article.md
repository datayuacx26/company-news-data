# A New Truck for an Essential Worker

This year truck drivers have been recognized as essential workers and there has been a renewed awareness of how their deliveries can impact our daily lives. At Landstar, we’ve always known this. To reward Landstar’s safest owner-operators, the company gives away a new truck each year in the annual[All-Star Truck Giveaway](https://www.landstar.com/blog/2020-bco-all-star-truck-giveaway/) . Landstar Executive Vice President of Capacity Development Rocco Davanzo says it’s all about appreciation.
