# Landstar Fuel Rebates Top $50 Million

The annual fuel rebate savings for independent business capacity owners (BCOs) through the[Landstar Contractor’s Advantage Purchasing Program (LCAPP)](https://lcapp.com/) is at **$54,956,293 and counting!**


Each year, Landstar BCOs save millions of dollars on fuel purchases through LCAPP. The previous LCAPP fuel savings record was $47,312,132, set in 2019.


When it comes to fuel savings and fuel tax charges, the LandstarOne® mobile app helps owner-operators optimize their fuel savings to find the best prices wherever they travel. LandstarOne is available for download via Android:[http://bit.ly/2IkkQ9H](https://bit.ly/2IkkQ9H?fbclid=IwAR0I2vF_xpg7qQxSEitGmCH2ZqvM1AO_D-twy07rPA-F5eRicVuhjq87opQ) or iPhone:[https://apple.co/2XnHlk9](https://apple.co/2XnHlk9?fbclid=IwAR25xEBJFKAk4KdX8NJNq60WFtaVQLgKQ2a0bq2JK6142nKq5FOk2hZTTrM) .


**Landstar’s total fuel rebate information is calculated weekly based on information provided to Landstar from its LCAPP vendors. The year to date total, $54,956,293 was calculated from fuel savings information during the week of Nov. 2-8, 2020.*
