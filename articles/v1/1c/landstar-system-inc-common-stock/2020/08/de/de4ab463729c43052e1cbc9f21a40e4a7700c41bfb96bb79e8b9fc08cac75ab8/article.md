# Landstar Continues to Receive Industry Recognition

Landstar’s ongoing commitment to service excellence has led to recognition as an industry leader by *Inbound Logistics, Logistics Management* and *Transport Topics* .


### ***Inbound Logistics*** **| Top 100 3PL Providers**


[Inbound Logistics](https://www.inboundlogistics.com/cms/article/2020-top-100-3pls/) honored Landstar as one of the Top 100 3PL providers. Companies on the annual list were selected based on survey submissions.


“When choosing the 2020 Top 100 3PL Providers, *Inbound Logistics* editors looked for providers who offer the visibility, flexibility, speed and control that drive the supply chain solutions our audience needs to achieve their goals and meet customers’ evolving needs. *Inbound Logistics* is proud to honor Landstar for innovative solutions empowering logistics and supply chain excellence in 2020,” said Felecia Stratton, *Inbound Logistics* editor.


### ***Logistics Management*** **| Top 50 U.S. and Global 3PLs**


Landstar holds the number 23 spot on the Top 50 U.S. 3PLs list and number 40 on the Top 50 Global 3PLs list.[Logistics Management](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/top_50_us_and_global_third_party_logistics_2020/3pl) placed 3PL providers on their respective lists according to 2019 revenue.


### ***Transport Topics*** **| Top 100 For-Hire Carriers**


Landstar ranked ninth overall on the[Transport Topics Top 100 For-Hire Carriers list.](https://www.ttnews.com/top100/for-hire/2020) These rankings, based on 2019 revenue, also placed carriers into sub-categories divided by transportation mode. Landstar’s placement within these additional categories included:


- No.2 on the Flatbed/Heavy Specialized list
- No.3 on the Truckload Dedicated list
- No.5 on the Air/Expedited list
- No.16 on the Intermodal/Drayage list
- No.31 on the Less-Than-Truckload list
