# December 2017 Star of Quality Awards Announced

When members of the Landstar family go above and beyond the call of duty, their extra efforts are recognized with Landstar’s Star of Quality Award. Deserving[business capacity owners (BCOs)](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/owner-operators/) and[agents](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/agents/) receive bronze, silver, gold or platinum lapel pins recognizing their dedication to the best in transportation services.


Our thanks to the following Landstar owner operators and agents who are among the most elite in the freight transportation industry.


**Star of Quality Award Winners of December 2016**


#### **Platinum:**


Preston S. Nale, Vicki M. Nale.
