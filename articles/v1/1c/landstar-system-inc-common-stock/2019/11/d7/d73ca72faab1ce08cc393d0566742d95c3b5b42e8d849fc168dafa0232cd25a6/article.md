# Landstar Receives Fleet Safety Award

Landstar Inway Inc., a Landstar operating company, earned first place in the Florida Trucking Association’s (FTA) Fleet Safety Awards for outstanding achievement in the Truckload Division over 15 million miles.


Award winners represent the best in the industry for both fleet operations and drivers with little or no history of accidents on the road. Landstar Transportation Logistics Vice President of Safety and Compliance Mike Cobb accepted the award on Landstar’s behalf during the FTA annual conference in Palm Beach, Florida, this summer.
