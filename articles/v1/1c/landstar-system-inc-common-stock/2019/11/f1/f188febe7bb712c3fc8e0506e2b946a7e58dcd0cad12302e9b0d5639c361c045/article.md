# 4 Ways to Strengthen Your Freight Agency

Freight agents looking to build a stronger agency need more than activity—they need a clear plan, strong relationships and consistent use of the right tools.


This article outlines four practical ways agents can grow their agency by focusing on customer needs, using available resources and tracking performance over time. Insights are drawn from Landstar agent development leadership and the experience of an independent Landstar agent who scaled his business early in his career.


## 1. Understand customer requirements before recommending solutions


Customers understand their products, timelines and service expectations. A freight agent’s role is to identify the transportation requirements behind those needs and recommend solutions that fit.


When agents take the time to listen and ask the right questions, they are better positioned to support reliable service and build repeat business.


### What to focus on:


- Ask detailed questions about freight type, timing and handling requirements
- Align solutions to customer priorities, not assumptions
- Follow up consistently to strengthen relationships


## 2. Build a strong support network


No freight agency grows in isolation. Early momentum often comes from relationships and the ability to engage the right support at the right time.


### For many agents, growth is shaped by collaboration with:


- Other agents
- Landstar corporate employees
- Capacity providers


A strong network provides guidance, referrals and operational support that can help agents navigate challenges as their business grows.


## 3. Use the tools available to you


Running a freight agency requires balancing sales, operations and customer communication. Practical tools can help agents stay organized and make informed decisions.


Landstar provides resources designed to help agents operate efficiently and compete more effectively.


### Examples of how tools support agency growth:


- Use analytics to track revenue and freight volume
- Monitor trends to evaluate performance over time
- Apply pricing tools to develop faster, more informed quotes


Consistent use of business tools helps agents stay aligned with their goals and identify areas for improvement.


## 4. Know what differentiates your agency


Freight agents who grow consistently often understand what sets their agency apart. That value may come from responsiveness, industry knowledge, pricing discipline or the ability to manage complex shipments.


Tracking the right metrics helps agents stay focused and make adjustments as needed.


### Key metrics to monitor:


- Revenue and freight volume
- Customer retention
- Quoting activity
- Overall business performance


Staying focused on these indicators helps agents evaluate what is working and where to refine their approach.

## Frequently asked questions about building a freight agency


### What helps a freight agency grow?


Freight agency growth often comes from a combination of customer retention, steady sales activity, strong relationships and consistent use of performance data.


### Why is a support network important?


A strong network provides practical guidance, referrals and operational insight that can help agents build momentum and address challenges.


### What tools support freight agents?


Tools such as analytics dashboards, pricing resources and customer tracking systems help agents make more informed decisions.


### What should agents measure?


Agents should monitor revenue, freight volume, quoting activity and customer retention to evaluate performance.


### Ready to build a stronger agency?


Focus on understanding your customers, strengthening your network and using the tools available to you. Consistent effort in these areas can help support long-term agency growth.
