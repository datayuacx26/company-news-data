# Landstar Participates in 2025 CleanPower Expo

As an industry-leading carrier committed to environmental stewardship and actively involved in the clean energy sector, Landstar participated in the[2025 CleanPower Conference](https://cleanpower.org/expo/) in Phoenix, showcasing its expertise in the supply chain. The premier industry event, which took place May 19 - 22, brings policy leaders and experts together for a week of learning, networking and innovation.


At the expo, attendees stopped by the Landstar booth to learn more about how Landstar customizes transportation solutions within the clean energy supply chain - from solar energy systems and battery units to wind power turbines and more.


"From[Less-Than-Truckload](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/less-than-truckload-ltl/) to heavy/specialized loads, we haul it," said Landstar Corporate Account Executive Randy Cose. "The Landstar network has the professional drivers, loading capacity and the experience to handle anything the clean power industry has."


Landstar is a recognized leader in over-dimensional and[heavy-haul transportation](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/heavy-haul/) that has provided capacity for clean energy solutions for more than 20 years. The company specializes in clean energy over-dimensional freight, including:


- Wind towers
- Blades
- Internal components for the erection of wind towers
- Nacelles (machine heads)
- Solar panels
- Solar battery units
- Green generators


Additionally, Landstar implements environmentally responsible business practices in conjunction with its capacity and expertise in handling clean energy freight. Since 2008, Landstar has been a Responsible Care certified company, ensuring compliance with the requirements set forth by the Responsible Care Management System. In line with these efforts and the company’s commitment to fostering environmentally sustainable practices, Landstar's Environmental, Health, Safety, and Security (EHS&S) pledge asserts that the company aims to positively influence the transportation and logistics industry. With this commitment, Landstar meets or exceeds regulatory agencies' environmental requirements applicable to its operations to address greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions.
