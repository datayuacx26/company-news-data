# Honoring Those Who Served

### A Veterans Day Message from Landstar President & CEO Frank Lonegro


This Veterans Day, Landstar honors the men and women who have served in our nation’s Armed Forces, including the veterans within our own network—independent agents, owner-operators, and employees.


Their service and sacrifice protect the freedoms we value and the opportunities we enjoy. President and CEO Frank Lonegro shares his gratitude to all who have served and continue to inspire us with their dedication.
