# Landstar Ranks No. 9 on AJOT’s List of Top 50 Trucking Companies

Landstar System, Inc. placed within the top 10 of the[American Journal of Transportation’s (AJOT)](https://www.ajot.com/) ranking of the Top 50 North American Trucking Companies. The ranking was published on Jan. 27, 2025.


As with AJOT’s Top 100 North American Trucking Companies list, the publication uses various factors in determining the rank of eligible companies, including revenue, capacity and range of services provided. Landstar placed No.9 on this year’s list.


AJOT has published over a century of news, information, and insights for the transportation industry. The publication’s annual ranking considers more than 500 companies for inclusion.
