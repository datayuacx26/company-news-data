# LCAPP Saves BCOs Millions in 2024

Landstar business capacity owners (BCOs) once again saved millions of dollars with the Landstar Contractors’ Advantage Purchasing Program (LCAPP) in 2024.


Last year, Landstar BCOs pumped more than 111 million gallons of fuel, adding up to over $68 million in fuel savings with LCAPP.


LCAPP also helped BCOs purchase 32,883 new tires in 2024. Every Landstar BCO saves an average of $125 off typical retail pricing when they purchase through LCAPP. That adds up to well over $4 million in savings on tires!


Additionally, LCAPP offers savings on more than fuel and tires. Visit www.LCAPP.com today to see discounts on other business purchases.


**Landstar’s total fuel savings information is calculated weekly and compiled based on information provided to Landstar from its LCAPP vendors. The annual total savings amounts for fuel was calculated from data complied throughout 2024.*
