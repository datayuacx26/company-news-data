# Landstar Honored With 2025 Quest for Quality Award

Landstar Transportation Logistics, Inc. has once again been recognized in multiple categories of[Logistics Management magazine's 2025 Quest for Quality Awards](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/42nd_annual_quest_for_quality_awards) , specifically in the categories of:


- [Third-Party Logistics (Transportation Management)](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/42nd_annual_quest_for_quality_awards_third_party_logistics_3pl)
- [Dry Freight Carriers](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/42nd_annual_quest_for_quality_truckload)
- [Industrial & Heavy-Haul Carriers](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/42nd_annual_quest_for_quality_truckload)


The awards were announced in the publication's August issue.


The publication’s quality awards survey, which is now in its 42nd year, has been regarded in the transportation and logistics industry as the "most important measure of customer satisfaction and performance excellence," according to the magazine's release.


Since 1993, Logistics Management has surveyed its readers on an annual basis to evaluate the transportation logistics service providers they use. The sample is representative of qualified buyers of logistics services, technology and equipment. This year's survey garnered over 3,000 responses, ultimately selecting the top industry performers in transportation and logistics services.


The Quest for Quality survey asks readers to rank companies on the following five criteria:


- On-time performance
- Value
- Information technology
- Customer service
- Equipment and operations


Landstar is a recognized leader in heavy/specialized transportation services thanks to its network of experienced owner-operators and a wide array of equipment. The network includes more than 78,000 truck capacity providers and nearly 1,050 agents.
