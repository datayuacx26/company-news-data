# 5 Reasons to Trust Landstar with Your Pharma Freight

For pharmaceutical manufacturers, every shipment is more than freight; it's a lifeline for patients relying on your products.


Landstar delivers peace of mind with temperature-validated equipment, proven reliability, and round-the-clock support to safeguard sensitive pharmaceuticals at every stage.


## Our rigorous processes ensure your shipments reach customers safely and securely every time.


### 1. Unmatched Quality


Our operational protocols, including GDP compliance, are designed to meet the rigorous standards of the healthcare and biotech industries. Every shipment follows a strict checklist, ensuring consistency and compliance. Agents and owner-operators complete regular required training, including biennial cold chain recertification and hazmat training every three years.


### 2. Proactive Equipment Validation


Imagine never having to worry about temperature swings or compliance slips. We rigorously validate our equipment to ensure all set points are precisely maintained, enabling us to proactively anticipate and prevent excursions and deviations. This approach protects product integrity, safeguarding your cargo and your peace of mind.


### 3. Comprehensive Visibility


From pickup to delivery, shipment temperatures are tracked and monitored in real time. This ensures product stability and gives you peace of mind.


### 4. Unmatched Safety & Security


We know the difference. Landstar drivers are vetted for safety and are Hazmat-certified. We tailor layered security measures that meet the needs of modern supply chains whether it is door sensors and alarm settings or having confidence that details about your shipments are only shared with those that need to know, we protect your high-value, temperature-sensitive shipments and information every mile of the way. Rely on the experience of our over 1,060 million-Mile Safe Drivers to ensure your cargo’s safety and security from start to finish.


### 5. Unwavering Service and Communication


We’re available 24/7/365 to coordinate, respond, and resolve any issues that arise. You’ll receive personalized service and operations expertise from our network of independent sales agents and truck owner operators, who in turn receive resources and back-office support from Landstar.


## Why It Matters:


Pharmaceuticals demand more than just transportation; they demand a partner like Landstar who understands the stakes.
