# BCO Walter Jackson Represents Owner-Operators at TCA's Call to Washington

## From the Cab to Capitol Hill


Landstar Vice President of Safety & Compliance Shelly Seaton (left), Landstar BCO Walter Jackson (middle), and Landstar Vice President and Chief Safety & Operations Officer Matt Miller on Capitol Hill


Our network's most inspiring voices are taking center stage in Washington, D.C., and we couldn't be prouder. Landstar Business Capacity Owner (BCO) Walter Jackson, a celebrated[Truckload Carriers Association](https://truckload.org/) (TCA) 2025 Professional Driver of the Year, is on Capitol Hill this week for the TCA's Fall Business Meetings and Call on Washington.


For more than two decades, Walter has been an integral part of the Landstar community. His career is a testament to resilience, dedication and the power of a safety-first mindset. From rebuilding his life after Hurricane Katrina to delivering disaster relief for others, Walter’s story showcases the profound impact that owner-operators have on our country. Now, he's bringing his invaluable perspective directly to policymakers.


Walter is pictured here alongside Landstar's Matt Miller, Chief Safety and Operations Officer, and Shelly Seaton, Vice President of Safety and Compliance. Together, they are advocating for the issues that matter most to our network of owner-operators.


The conversations in Washington are focused on a wide range of critical industry topics, including:


- **Truck Parking** : Pushing for solutions to expand safe and accessible parking options for professional drivers.
- **Regulatory Reform** : Addressing issues like hair follicle testing and contractor status to ensure fair and accurate practices for BCOs.
- **Cargo Fraud / Theft** : Expanding efforts to combat cargo theft and fraud.
- **Recruitment and Retention** : Helping to shape programs that will attract and retain the next generation of trucking professionals.


Landstar is actively involved with industry organizations like the TCA to advocate for policies that support the trucking community and empower owner-operators. Walter Jackson's presence on Capitol Hill this week elevates this advocacy, ensuring that the unique experiences and insights of the owner-operator are front and center in these crucial discussions.


His trip to Washington is a powerful reminder that our BCOs are not just moving freight—they are also driving progress and shaping the future of our industry. We are proud to have Walter represent the Landstar network and to have our leaders, Matt Miller and Shelly Seaton, furthering these important conversations.
