# 2025 BCO Benevolence Fund

The Landstar BCO Benevolence Fund made a meaningful difference in 2025, providing **$125,000** in financial assistance to 37 independent owner-operators and their families during times of unexpected need. Landstar BCOs, as well as truck operators employed by BCOs, and their immediate family members are eligible to apply for support, with all applications carefully reviewed by a dedicated selection committee.


In 2025, assistance was primarily directed toward unforeseen medical expenses (60%), families coping with loss (27%), and urgent, critical needs (14%).


Donations are raised through events hosted nationwide by agents, BCOs, and employees. Last year, these events generated more than $41,000, with an additional $19,700 raised solely during BCO Appreciation Days. Since its establishment in 2021, the Benevolence Fund has raised a total of $484,286 to support BCOs and their families in need.


To learn more about eligibility, apply for assistance, or make a donation to the Landstar BCO Benevolence Fund, emailbcofund@landstar.com .
