# Landstar Earns Top Rankings from Transport Topics

Landstar System, Inc. earned the No. 25 spot on[Transport Topics’ 2026 Top 100 Logistics Companies](https://www.ttnews.com/logistics/rankings/2026) list, which ranks North America’s largest providers by 2025 gross revenue. The annual list highlights industry leaders across the continent.


Landstar also achieved a No. 8 ranking on the publication’s[Top 100 Freight Brokerage Firms](https://www.ttnews.com/logistics/freightbrokerage/2026) list, reflecting its standing in brokerage services.


Landstar’s success reflects the strength of its network-based model, which includes nearly 1,000 independent[agents](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/agents/) and access to over 70,500[truck capacity providers](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/carriers/) . By leveraging this network, along with advanced technology and industry experience, the company delivers flexible and scalable transportation solutions.
