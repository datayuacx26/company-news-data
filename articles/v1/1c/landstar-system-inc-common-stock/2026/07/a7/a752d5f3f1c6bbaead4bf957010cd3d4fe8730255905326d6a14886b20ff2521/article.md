# Landstar Recognized as Top 3PL Provider for 2026

Landstar System, Inc. has once again been recognized as one of the transportation industry's top third-party logistics providers, earning a spot on Inbound Logistics magazine's 2026 Top 100 3PL list. The company’s inclusion marks more than two decades of recognition by Inbound Logistics, reflecting the company's longstanding commitment to delivering excellence in safety, security, and service across the transportation industry.


“This year, the competition was fiercer than ever,” said Felecia Stratton, editor of Inbound Logistics. “Hundreds of 3PLs stepped up to showcase how they navigate today’s supply chain landscape. Our editorial selection team looked past mere size to focus on true industry leadership. We recognize the innovators who provide real-world value, drive headline-worthy performance and keep their customers ahead of the curve.”


Inbound Logistics serves logistics and supply chain professionals through its print publication and digital resources, delivering insights on transportation, warehousing and supply chain management trends.


The annual Top 100 3PL list highlights logistics providers that demonstrate operational excellence, innovation and the ability to meet the evolving needs of shippers. Landstar’s continued recognition underscores the strength of its network, unique business model, and unwavering focus on helping customers move freight safely and efficiently.
