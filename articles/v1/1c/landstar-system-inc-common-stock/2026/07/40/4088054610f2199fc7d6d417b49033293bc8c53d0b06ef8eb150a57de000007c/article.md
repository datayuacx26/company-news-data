# Gregg Nelson Honored with Lifetime Achievement Award

Landstar recognized longtime employee Gregg Nelson earlier this month with one of the company's highest honors during the 2026 Landstar BCO All-Star Celebration in Orlando, Florida. Nelson, Vice President of BCO Retention, received the Jeffrey C. Crowe-Robert E. Zonneville Lifetime Achievement Award for his enduring contributions to the Landstar network.


Established 30 years ago, the award celebrates employees whose influence extends beyond their own achievements and helps shape the success of others. Named in honor of two of Landstar's founding leaders, the recognition highlights exceptional commitment to the company, its people and its culture.


"Gregg's impact on Landstar extends far beyond his role and responsibilities," said Landstar President and CEO Frank Lonegro. "For more than four decades, he has built meaningful relationships throughout the Landstar network and earned the respect of coworkers and independent owner-operators alike. His commitment to supporting others makes him a deserving recipient of this honor."


Since joining Landstar in 1983, Nelson has fostered trusted relationships across the organization while helping independent owner-operators succeed. He began his career with the company as Director of Special Projects and was promoted to Vice President in 2000. Today, he continues to focus on business capacity owner (BCO) retention and engagement.


Nelson is also a familiar face to many independent owner-operators who attend Landstar’s signature events, including the BCO All-Star Celebration and BCO Appreciation Days. Often taking the stage to welcome attendees or leading event highlights such as the company’s biannual truck giveaways, Nelson brings his humor and positive energy into the spotlight — making a memorable experience for BCOs and their families.
Nelson said he was both surprised and deeply honored to receive the prestigious award, noting that the recognition reflected the many people who’ve impacted his career over the years.


“While I am humbled and blessed to receive this award, I do believe that this honor is an accumulation of everyone who has helped shape my journey here at Landstar,” he said. “I also consider it a privilege to have worked with Mr. Crowe and Mr. Zonneville early in my Landstar career. I’m truly thankful for all Landstar has done for me.”
