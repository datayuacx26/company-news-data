# Landstar Earns Three 2026 Quest for Quality Awards

Landstar Transportation Logistics, Inc. has continued to earn recognition among the transportation industry's leading service providers, receiving honors in three categories of *Logistics Management* magazine's 2026 Quest for Quality Awards:


- Third-Party Logistics (Transportation Management)
- Dry Freight Carriers
- Industrial & Heavy-Haul Carriers


According to[Logistics Management](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/43rd_annual_quest_for_quality_setting_the_standard_for_service_excellence) , the Quest for Quality Awards are considered one of the transportation and logistics industry's most respected benchmarks for customer satisfaction and service excellence. Now in its 43rd year, the publication continues to survey transportation and supply chain decision-makers on the service providers they rely on most.


For this year’s survey, more than 2,500 responses were collected, with participants rating providers in key areas, including on-time performance, value, information technology, customer service, and equipment operations.


Companies with the highest overall scores earn Quest for Quality recognition, making the awards a reflection of customer trust and satisfaction.


Landstar's continued success highlights the strength of its extensive network of independent agents and capacity providers. Through a network of nearly 1,050 independent agents and over 78,000 truck capacity providers, Landstar delivers flexible transportation solutions for a broad range of freight needs.


The recognition underscores Landstar's focus on safety, security, and service while helping customers navigate increasingly complex supply chain challenges through customized transportation solutions.
