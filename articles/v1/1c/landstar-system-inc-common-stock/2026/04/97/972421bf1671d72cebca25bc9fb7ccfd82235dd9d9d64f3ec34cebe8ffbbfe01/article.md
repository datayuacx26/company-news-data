# A Closer Look at How Landstar Delivers—Responsibly

At Landstar, sustainability isn’t a standalone initiative—it’s embedded in how our network operates every day. The newly released 2025 Landstar Sustainability Report highlights how safety, operational discipline, environmental stewardship, and community engagement continue to shape our business.


Inside the report, you’ll find examples that reflect the strength of our agent based model, the commitment of business capacity owners (BCOs), and Landstar’s ongoing investments in technology, safety, and responsible operations. From risk reduction and efficiency improvements to longstanding safety programs and meaningful community involvement, the report outlines how we continue to approach sustainability.


The report also provides insight into how we deliver safety, security, and service for our customers, communities, and each other—every load, every day.
