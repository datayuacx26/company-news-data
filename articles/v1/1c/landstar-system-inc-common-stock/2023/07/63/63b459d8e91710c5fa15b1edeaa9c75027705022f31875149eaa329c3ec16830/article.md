# Landstar Celebrates 133 New Million Mile Safe Driver and Roadstar® Honorees

The Landstar Class of 2022 Roadstar® and Million Mile Safe Driver inductees in attendance at the 2023 Landstar BCO All-Star Celebration.


Landstar inducted the newest class of Million Mile Safe Drivers and Roadstar® honorees. The Class of 2022 is comprised of 98 new One Million Mile Safe Drivers, 19 new Two Million Mile Safe Drivers and one new Three Million Mile Safe Driver. Each of the owner-operators has driven at least one million consecutive miles with Landstar without a preventable accident.


Of Landstar’s 1,090 active Million Mile Safe Drivers, including those inducted in this class, 15 individuals were recognized for earning the Roadstar distinction. The Roadstar designation is one of Landstar’s highest honors for truck owner-operators awarded to the “best of the best” for their high levels of safety, productivity and excellence in customer service. Landstar began presenting the Roadstar award in 1990. Currently, there are 174 active Roadstar honorees.


“Landstar takes great pride in the achievement of these independent owner-operators. Driving a million miles or more without a preventable accident is not an easy feat. We are proud to celebrate their safety records and professionalism behind the wheel, and their skill and dedication to their profession makes the roads safer for all of us,” said Landstar President and CEO Jim Gattoni. “Landstar Roadstar honorees set the benchmark for the entire trucking industry and their unparalleled dedication to service excellence has earned them the right to be called the most elite independent owner-operators at Landstar.”
