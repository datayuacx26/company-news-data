# Working Against Human Trafficking

Landstar is committed to working with industry groups in the fight against human trafficking. We recognize that truckers are one of the first lines of defense to identify, report on and help prevent human trafficking. On this episode of The Road to Success® podcast, our guest, Laura Cyrus with[Truckers Against Trafficking](https://truckersagainsttrafficking.org/) shares with us the red flags to watch for, how to report potential human trafficking and where human trafficking can happen.


To report possible human trafficking, call the human trafficking hotline: 1-888-3737-888
