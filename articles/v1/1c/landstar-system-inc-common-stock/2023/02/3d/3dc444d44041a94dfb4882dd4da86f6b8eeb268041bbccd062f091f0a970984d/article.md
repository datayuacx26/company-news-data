# Landstar ranks on AJOT’s 2022 List of Top 100 Trucking Companies

Landstar System, Inc. maintains the eighth spot on the[American Journal of Transportation’s](https://www.ajot.com/ajot_digital/749/?page=6) (AJOT) ranking of the 2022 Top 100 North American Trucking Companies published Jan. 23, 2023.


Landstar has held the No. 8 ranking since AJOT’s[inaugural list](https://ajot.com/premium/ajot-top-100-trucking-companies) published in Dec. 2019. The publication takes many factors into consideration when determining the rank of over 500 eligible companies including revenue, capacity as well as the range of services provided. Landstar made the list published in 2019 as a[truckload](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/truckload-van/) and[LTL provider](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/less-than-truckload-ltl/) , but is now also recognized by AJOT as a leading[3PL provider](https://www.landstar.com/why-landstar/) , according to their list classification.


The[Landstar network](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/) has over 1,100 agents and more than 108,000 capacity providers. Take advantage of Landstar’s experience, technology and capacity to manage your next shipment.
