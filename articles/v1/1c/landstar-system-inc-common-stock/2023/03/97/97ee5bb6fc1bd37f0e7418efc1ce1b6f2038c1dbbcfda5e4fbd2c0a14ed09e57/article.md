# Technology Solutions

## Leveraging Technology to Create the Right Customer Solutions


Landstar Executive Vice President of Business Intelligence and Strategy Jim Applegate sat down with *Inbound Logistics* to talk about the role of technology in transportation logistics.


> “Customers really started expecting it, it was no longer a competitive advantage just to have technology. It became table stakes of just being able to service customer needs.” - Jim Applegate


Applegate discusses the types of technology Landstar has invested in to support its network of independent freight agents, and how access to technology and data has strengthened the traditional customer-agent relationship.
