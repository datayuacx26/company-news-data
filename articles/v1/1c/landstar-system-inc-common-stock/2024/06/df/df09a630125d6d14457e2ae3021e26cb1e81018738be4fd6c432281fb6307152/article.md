# Landstar Named Among Top 100 For-Hire Carriers in 2024 by Transport Topics

Landstar System, Inc., ranked No. 10 on the 2024 *Transport Topics*[Top 100 For-Hire Carriers list](https://www.ttnews.com/for-hire/rankings/2024) . Landstar also ranked in several sub-categories of the For-Hire Carriers list, including:


- Flatbed/Heavy Specialized — Ranked No. 2
- Truckload/Dedicated — Ranked No. 3
- Intermodal/Drayage — Ranked No. 15
- Less-Than-Truckload — Ranked No. 27


Additionally, Landstar ranked No. 26 on the 2024 Transport Topics[Top 100 Logistics Companies list](https://www.ttnews.com/logistics/rankings/2024) and No. 6 in the Freight Brokerage sub-category of the list.
