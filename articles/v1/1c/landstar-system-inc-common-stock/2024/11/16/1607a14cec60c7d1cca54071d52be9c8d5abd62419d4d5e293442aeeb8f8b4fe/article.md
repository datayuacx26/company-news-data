# 2024 Sponsor Spotlight

## Thank You for Supporting Our Signature Events


Landstar would like to thank our 2024 sponsors, whose contributions helped to enhance our two main events for our independent agents and business capacity owners (BCOs).


Our diamond-level sponsors include Comdata, Gallagher Insurance, Insurance Office of America (IOA) and Pilot Flying J. These businesses supported our annual agent convention and the BCO All-Star Celebration.


These events aim to honor our accomplished independent agents who met revenue milestones and owner-operators who've gone above and beyond in their commitment to safety and service excellence. With support from our sponsors, we can highlight their outstanding performance every year.


Sponsor[Comdata, Inc.](https://www.comdata.com/) is a leading provider of innovative payment and operating technology that drives actionable insights from spending data, builds enhanced controls and positively impacts its clients' bottom lines. Comdata's technical capabilities in credit card issuing and transaction processing have helped independent businesses with electronic payment programs for decades.


For over 30 years,[Gallagher Transportation Services](https://www.ajg.com/us/industries/transportation-insurance/) has offered clients insurance and risk management products. Their assistance gives valuable coverage for freight solutions. Gallagher is one of the world's largest insurance brokerage, risk management, and consulting firms that has met client's needs for over 30 years.


Event sponsor,[IOA](https://www.ioausa.com/) , offers unique insurance coverage like high-value trip transit cargo insurance policies, which are instrumental in helping clients plan for current and future liabilities. IOA has knowledge of the transportation and logistics industry, enabling its team to help clients plan for current and future liabilities.


[Pilot Flying J](https://pilotflyingj.com/) offers Landstar’s independent owner-operators exclusive fuel rebates at locations across the country. As North America's largest network of travel centers with 750 locations, Pilot Flying J supplies more than 14 billion gallons of fuel annually to 1.5 million guests with trusted rewards services.


Again, thank you to all of the sponsors that helped support this year's events and continue to support Landstar entrepreneurs every day through discounted services.
