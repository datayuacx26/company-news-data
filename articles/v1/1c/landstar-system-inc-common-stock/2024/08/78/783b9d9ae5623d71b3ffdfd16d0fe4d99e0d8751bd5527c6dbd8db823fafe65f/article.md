# Landstar Recognized with 2024 Quest for Quality Awards by Logistics Management

Landstar Transportation Logistics, Inc. has been recognized in the Dry Freight Carriers and Industrial & Heavy-Haul Carriers categories of *[Logistics Management](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/quest_for_quality_2024_truckload)* magazine’s 2024 Quest for Quality Award. The awards were announced in the publication’s August issue.


Landstar is a recognized leader in[heavy haul](https://www.landstar.com/services/north-american-services/heavy-haul/) transportation services both nationally and globally, thanks to its equipment and network of experienced[owner-operators](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/owner-operators/) .


Logistics Management has surveyed a representative sample of its readers on an annual basis since 1993 to evaluate the transportation logistics service providers readers use. This representative sample is comprised of individuals who are qualified buyers of logistics and transportation services.


According to the publication, “Now in its 41st year, the ‘Quest for Quality’ Award has been regarded in the transportation and logistics industry as the most important measure of customer satisfaction and performance excellence.”


The Quest for Quality survey asks readers to rank companies on the following five criteria:


- On-time performance
- Value
- Information technology
- Customer service
- Equipment and operations


*Logistics Management* is a magazine and website targeted toward logistics professionals and buying influencers of logistics services, technology and equipment.


The[Landstar network](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/agents/) has approximately 1,100 agents and more than 85,600 capacity providers. Take advantage of Landstar’s experience, technology and capacity to manage your next shipment.
