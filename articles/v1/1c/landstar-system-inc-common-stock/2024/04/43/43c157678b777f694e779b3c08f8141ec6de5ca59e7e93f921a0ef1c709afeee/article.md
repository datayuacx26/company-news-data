# Landstar Awards Overall M.U.S.T. Customer of the Year to DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations

From left to right: Landstar agents Roxanne and Joel Lemoine join Brandon Dubroc, representing DIS-TRAN on stage to accept the Overall M.U.S.T. Customer of the Year award from Landstar President and CEO Frank Lonegro.


At its 2024 Agent Convention, Landstar announced[DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations](https://www.distransubstations.com/) of Pineville, Louisiana, as its 2023 Overall Landstar M.U.S.T. Customer of the Year.


A collaboration between Landstar and its customers, Landstar’s Mutual Understanding of Safety Together, or[M.U.S.T., program](https://www.landstar.com/blog/customer-benefits-are-a-must/) involves an extensive on-site review of a customer’s facility, followed by discussion and analysis of loading, unloading and securement, in addition to other safety practices.
Landstar Vice President of Safety and Compliance Shelly Seaton announced DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations as the overall winner during a ceremony held April 5, 2024. Also recognized were independent Landstar Agents Joel and Roxanne Lemoine of[ASE Transportation and Specialized Services](https://www.asetransportation.com/) , LLC, the agency that works with DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations.


Upon a visit to DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations in 2023, Landstar representatives witnessed the safe and smooth load out of 25 different trailers, bound for three separate job sites. Afterward, DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations invited Landstar back for a safety tour — which included the company’s participation in Landstar’s Safety Thursday Conference Call — as well as a vendor fair event.


“Landstar moved a total 663 shipments for DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations last year with no claims,” said Seaton at the award presentation. “This relationship demonstrates a clear and consistent commitment to safety by the customer, which has earned the company Landstar’s Overall M.U.S.T. Customer of the Year award.”


## DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations


DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations is an industry leader in the design and supply of high voltage, electrical substations; the structures the supply power to communities, neighborhoods, and businesses across the United States. DIS-TRAN Packaged Substations is a Crest Industries company. Crest Industries is the operating company of nine world-class businesses serving the electric utility, commercial and industrial electrical contractor and forestry industries.
