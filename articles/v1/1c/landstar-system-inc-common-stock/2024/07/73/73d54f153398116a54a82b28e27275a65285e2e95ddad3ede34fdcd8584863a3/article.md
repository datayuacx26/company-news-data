# Landstar Recognized by Inbound Logistics Top 100 3PLs List

Landstar System was recognized by *Inbound Logistics* magazine on the publication’s 2024 list of[Top 100 Third-Party Logistics Providers](https://www.inboundlogistics.com/articles/top-100-3pls/) . The editors of the industry magazine, which focuses on transportation and technology in the supply chain, compiles and publishes the list annually. Providers operating at both the national and global levels were featured among the Top 3PLs listed in the *Inbound Logistics* July edition.


The[Landstar network](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/) has approximately 1,100 agents and more than 85,600 capacity providers. Take advantage of Landstar’s experience, technology and capacity to manage your next shipment.
