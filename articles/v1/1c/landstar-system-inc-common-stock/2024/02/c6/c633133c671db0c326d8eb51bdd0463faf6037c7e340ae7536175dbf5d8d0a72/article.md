# Landstar System Named a Recipient of the Responsible Suppliers Award

Landstar System, Inc. has been recognized by global chemistry leader The Chemours Company as a recipient of the company’s[2023 Responsible Supplier Awards](https://www.chemours.com/en/news-media-center/all-news/press-releases/2024/chemours-recognizes-its-2023-responsible-supplier-awards-recipients) . This award recognizes four suppliers and their corporate commitment to sustainability.


> “Chemours’ annual Responsible Supplier Award acknowledges the companies that have distinguished themselves by driving quality, innovation and sustainability improvements across Chemours’ supply chain,” the company said in a press release in February 2024.


The Chemours Company selected its winners from among 9,000 supplier companies worldwide. Landstar System, Inc. won the 2023 award for the logistics category.


Those interested in learning about Landstar’s dedication to responsible business practices, including sustainability, can learn more by visiting the[corporate responsibility](https://www.landstar.com/corporate-information/corporate-responsibility/) page.
