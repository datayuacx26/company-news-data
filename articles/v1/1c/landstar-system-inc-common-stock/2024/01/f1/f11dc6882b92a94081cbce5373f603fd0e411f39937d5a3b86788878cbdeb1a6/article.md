# 2023 Sponsor Spotlight: Insurance Office of America

Landstar would like to thank 2023 diamond-level sponsor Insurance Office of America (IOA) for its support and sponsorship of this year’s annual events: the Landstar Agent Convention and the Landstar BCO All-Star Celebration.


[IOA](https://www.ioausa.com/industries/transportation/) offers Landstar’s independent owner-operators workers’ compensation insurance coverage plus a unique high-value trip transit cargo insurance policy for Landstar’s high-value cargo shipments. IOA has knowledge of the transportation and logistics industry that enables its team to help clients plan for current and future liabilities.


“In the U.S., vehicles carrying people and goods cover over 3 billion miles a year on the road, according to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. What part of our economy isn’t impacted by the transportation industry? Whether you operate a fleet or you’re a single owner-operator, having a clear understanding of the specific regulations, duties of care, risks, and criminal activity your operation may be vulnerable to is imperative.” –[IOAUSA.com](https://www.ioausa.com/business-insurance/transportation/)


**About IOA**


Insurance Office of America (IOA) is the fourth largest privately held insurance brokerage in the United States. Founded in 1988, IOA is a recognized leader in providing property and casualty, employee benefits, and personal lines insurance and risk management solutions as well as insurtech innovation. Headquartered in Longwood, Florida, part of the greater Orlando community, IOA has more than 1,300 associates located in over 60 offices in the U.S. and United Kingdom. For more information, visit[www.ioausa.com](http://www.ioausa.com/) .
