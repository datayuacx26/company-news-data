# Landstar Named Masonite's National Carrier of the Year 2023

Masonite® — a leading global designer, manufacturer, marketer and distributor of interior and exterior doors — named Landstar the winner of its National Carrier of the Year 2023 award in late April.


The winner was determined via a graded, qualitative scale across 12 categories based on customer service, operational success, pricing and business relationships.


For several years, Landstar has collaborated with Masonite®, during which it has developed strong connections with the customer’s corporate logistics team and key operational personnel at the plant level.


Specifically, independent Landstar Agent Bobby Hicks, owner of PRM Agency in Caldwell, Idaho, and Landstar Corporate Account Executive Randy Cose led the way for this award. Thank you, Bobby and Randy!
