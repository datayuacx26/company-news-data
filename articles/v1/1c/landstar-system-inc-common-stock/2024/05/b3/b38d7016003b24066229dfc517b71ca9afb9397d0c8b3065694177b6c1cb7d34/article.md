# Landstar Ranks in Top 10 on FleetOwner’s 2024 List of Top 500 For-Hire Carriers

Landstar System, Inc. maintains the eighth spot in the annual[FleetOwner 500: For-Hire list](https://issuu.com/docs/0c2b8157b9b194d5d1ece2ed0b9eabae/22) published Feb. 12, 2024.


FleetOwner bases its rankings on the number of commercial power units reported to the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration’s (FMCSA) Safety and Fitness Electronic Records (SAFER) system.


The Landstar network has approximately 1,100[agents](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/agents/) and access to more than 85,600 truck[capacity providers](https://www.landstar.com/the-landstar-network/owner-operators/) .
