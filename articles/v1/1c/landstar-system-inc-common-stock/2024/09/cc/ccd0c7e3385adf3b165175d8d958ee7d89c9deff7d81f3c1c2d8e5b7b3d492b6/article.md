# Inbound Logistics Top 100 Truckers

Landstar is on the 2024 Top 100 Truckers list compiled annually by Inbound Logistics magazine. According to the publication’s editor Felecia Stratton, “Several hundred companies submitted credentials this year, presenting a challenge for the selection team to choose only 100 trucking leaders among them.”


*Inbound Logistics* editors evaluated surveys and questionnaires, conducted online research, assessed historical data, and spoke with shippers and truckers before making their selection. According to the publication, the list benchmarks the types of services transportation buyers value when evaluating current vendors or searching for new ones and is a snapshot of the trucking segment in 2024.


The Top 100 Truckers list appears in the September 2024 editions - magazine, digital, web and apps.
