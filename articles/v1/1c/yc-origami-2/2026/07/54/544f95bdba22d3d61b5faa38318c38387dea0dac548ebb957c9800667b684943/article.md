## What's the core difference between Origami and Seamless.AI?


Origami is an AI agent that builds lists from a natural-language prompt. You tell it something like "find owners of family-owned paving companies in Texas with 10–50 employees," and it searches the live web, scrapes public data, and returns a structured table with emails, phone numbers, and LinkedIn profiles. It's built for prospecting at scale — defining an ICP once and getting a fresh, verified list.


Seamless.AI is primarily a browser extension and contact lookup engine. You open a LinkedIn profile or company website, and it surfaces email addresses, direct dials, and other contact details in real time. Its strength is on-the-fly research: you already know who you want to reach, and you just need their contact info. It's less about generating a list from a loose description and more about enriching individual records you've already identified.


Sales leaders familiar with both often describe Origami as "the tool you use when you don't know exactly who to contact yet," while Seamless.AI is "the tool you use when you're looking at a specific person and need their email." A founder in the virtual dining space who tested multiple tools told us, "I got a lot of data I don't know what I don't have a lot of ways to make use of it" — Origami solves that by delivering a structured, ready-to-use list instead of raw search results.


## Which tool provides more accurate, up-to-date data?


Data freshness is where the architectural difference matters most. Origami performs a live web crawl for every search, meaning it pulls from current public sources — LinkedIn profiles, company websites, news mentions, job postings, and even niche directories like state contractor registries. This is particularly valuable for industries with high turnover (e.g., education, sales roles) or for targeting SMBs that rarely appear in traditional B2B databases. A sales leader in the EdTech space recently told us that "30% turnover year over year" means any static list is instantly obsolete; Origami's live search reflects today's reality.


Seamless.AI combines a proprietary database with real-time search; it's known for good accuracy on common enterprise contacts and decision-makers. However, like any database-backed tool, its coverage can thin out for local businesses, sole proprietors, or roles that don't have a strong LinkedIn presence. Sales teams in construction, home services, and healthcare have told us they often "spend hours upon hours" manually scraping Google Maps because static databases simply don't index painters, plumbers, or clinic owners well. A private equity professional recently described his frustration with traditional tools for SMB roll-ups: "I had them build a list of paving companies, and it was totally not a list of paving companies, like it was landscape, I mean total junk."


Origami's live approach also means it can surface signals invisible to a stored database — for example, finding a company that just posted a job for a role you target, or identifying a business that recently obtained a new contractor's license. For sales teams working in regulated industries like telecom, where "people don't just switch for fun, they switch because their contract's up," finding the right timing signal can be the difference between a meeting and a deleted email.


## How does each tool handle niche verticals and SMBs?


This is where Origami shines. Because it's not constrained by a pre-built index, the agent can follow digital breadcrumbs across the web. Need independent insurance agency owners with more than 30 employees? It can filter by agency type, location, and employee count from a combination of web sources. A prospect selling to such agencies found competitors "pretty unimpressive" for that niche; Origami delivered leads that turned into real conversations within a week.


Seamless.AI is strongest when the contact has a robust LinkedIn footprint and works for a mid-market or enterprise company. For local service businesses — landscapers, roofers, boutique retailers — the data often thins out because those contacts either don't maintain LinkedIn profiles or their information isn't aggregated in Seamless.AI's database. One founder in the medical aesthetics space noted that his target doctors "don't exist on LinkedIn" and live on Instagram; Origami can identify those doctors through medical directories and practice websites, while a database-centric tool might return nothing.


A healthcare staffing professional highlighted the gap: "We don't get enough clients where we are looking for healthcare as of now, but there are more for marketing and more for other kinds of stuff — not for healthcare as much as we want." When a vertical isn't well-represented in a general-purpose database, a live-crawling agent that checks hospital directories, job boards, and clinic websites becomes the only scalable path.


## Which is easier to use for non-technical sales teams?


Origami's interface is a chat. You type what you need, and the agent asks clarifying questions only if necessary — then delivers a table you can edit, export, or push to your CRM. There's no learning curve to navigate filters, boolean strings, or credit waterfalls. As one prospect put it, "I don't have to find my Marcel with the filters." A team lead can train an SDR to generate a targeted list in under five minutes without building Clay-style workflows or understanding data enrichment waterfalls.


Seamless.AI offers a Chrome extension that's straightforward to install: click it while on a profile and see the suggested contact info. For one-off lookups, it's hard to beat the convenience. But building a targeted list of 100 CFOs at manufacturing companies? That's less intuitive. You'd typically need to gather company profiles first, then use the extension on each one, which becomes time-consuming.


If your team already uses Seamless.AI as a side tool while browsing, it fits naturally into a manual research workflow. If you want to offload the entire list-building step to an agent, Origami reduces the task from hours to minutes. A RevOps leader who had been using multiple tools described the ideal as "one platform that can help me do LinkedIn and email campaigns both" — and Origami's unified approach gets closer to that vision than a standalone browser extension can.


## How do pricing and credits compare?


Origami offers a genuinely free plan with 1,000 credits — no credit card required — which lets you generate several small lists or enrich a handful of leads. Paid plans start at $29/month (2,000 credits) and scale up to $499/month for 40,000 credits. Enterprise plans are custom-priced. Their Pro and Enterprise plans include unlimited exports, but the cost can be a surprise — and many users report that the free tier's monthly limit runs out quickly if you're doing any volume.


For a startup or lean team testing the waters, Origami's free plan is more generous and transparent. For a large organization that can negotiate an enterprise deal, Seamless.AI's enterprise features (like team-wide access and CRM sync) might justify the conversation. But as one founder evaluating tools for his 3D animation studio noted, "I don't have 25 grand to spend on a lead list" — for budget-conscious teams, knowing the price upfront matters.


## Does Origami replace the need for a tool like Seamless.AI entirely?


Not necessarily. Many sales teams use both for different stages of their workflow: Origami for top-of-funnel list building — "who are the right people at these 200 companies I've never heard of?" — and Seamless.AI for mid-funnel research when a specific person's email isn't immediately visible on LinkedIn. The tools complement rather than compete if your sales motion involves both building net-new lists and quickly looking up contacts during a call or while drafting an email.


However, Origami's enrichment capabilities are strong enough that once you've built your list, you may not need a separate lookup tool. Its agent automatically appends emails and phone numbers to each contact row. Teams focused solely on sequencing might find Origami a more natural fit because the list and the contact data arrive together — no uploading CSVs to yet another enrichment tool.


## Where is Seamless.AI genuinely the better choice?


Seamless.AI still holds an edge for specific, high-velocity use cases. If you're an SDR who lives in LinkedIn Sales Navigator and needs to pull a direct dial or email address the moment you land on a profile, the browser extension is fast and unobtrusive. For enterprise sales organizations that have already trained their teams on Seamless.AI and negotiated a favorable annual contract, the switching cost may not be worth it.


Seamless.AI also offers robust CRM sync and team permission controls within its paid plans. If your workflow is strictly reactive — you already have a CRM full of account names and you simply need to enrich them one by one — Seamless.AI's Chrome extension may feel more natural than opening a separate chat interface. An SDR at an IT services firm described her workflow as opening a profile, clicking the extension, and immediately having the number — that kind of speed in the moment is valuable.


For AEs managing 10–200 accounts per patch who need enrichment by functional area (finance, HR, legal), Seamless.AI's extension approach can be perfectly adequate. But for the SDR or founder who needs to generate 500 net-new contacts this week from an ICP they can describe but haven't yet researched, Origami is the faster path to a working list.
