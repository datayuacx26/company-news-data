## How Does the Underlying Technology Differ?


The core difference is *how* each tool builds a prospect list. SalesIntel maintains a curated, human-verified database of B2B contacts and companies, with researchers calling into organizations to confirm titles, phone numbers, and email addresses. This database is refreshed on a periodic cycle—not in real time—and tends to excel for larger, well-established companies that are part of the formal business ecosystem.


Origami, by contrast, is a prompt-driven AI agent. You type a description of your ideal customer (e.g., "chief financial officers at US-based logistics firms that use SAP and have recently raised Series B funding"), and the agent crawls the live web in real time to find matching companies and people. It pulls from public sources, company websites, job postings, news, and social signals, then enriches the records with email and phone data from multiple waterfall providers. The result is a list that reflects what exists *today* , not what a database captured months ago. For fast-moving or non-traditional ICPs (local contractors, niche SaaS startups, newly funded companies), this architectural difference is significant: a live web search can surface businesses that have no entry in a static database, while SalesIntel’s strength lies in the depth and accuracy of its manually curated records for common enterprise titles.


## Which Tool Finds More Contacts for Hard-to-Reach Verticals?


SalesIntel’s verification process gives it high accuracy for contacts at companies where a formal org chart exists and someone is available to answer the phone. For selling into large corporations, software firms, and other established B2B accounts, SalesIntel’s human-verified data often includes direct dials that automated enrichment alone might miss. However, this method has a natural ceiling: if a company isn’t in the database—or if the contact you need doesn’t appear in the standardized hierarchy—you won’t find them.


That’s where Origami’s live web approach takes over. Because it isn’t limited to a pre-indexed list of companies, it can surface leaders at family-owned manufacturing plants, independent insurance agencies, digital marketing studios, or even solopreneurs who run local service businesses like roofing or landscaping. A static database that primarily tracks larger employers will simply not have these records, whereas a live web agent can pull names, roles, and contact details from online directories, membership associations, news articles, or even a company’s own "About Us" page. For any ICP that doesn’t live inside a traditional B2B database, Origami typically provides broader coverage.


## Which Is Easier to Use and Faster to Get Started?


Origami was built to eliminate the learning curve. You don’t need to know boolean logic, build complex filters, or config multi-step enrichment tables. You type a sentence—"find HR leaders at Nordic healthtech companies that are hiring salespeople"—and the agent returns a table of matches in minutes. There’s no training required, and the free plan (1,000 credits, no credit card) lets you test it immediately. For teams where time-to-first-list matters, this is a drastic reduction in the effort needed to go from idea to outreach.


SalesIntel offers a traditional dashboard with filters and search parameters for building lists, plus integrations with tools like Salesforce and Outreach. While powerful, it does require familiarity with the platform to navigate the filtering options effectively. The initial setup—uploading your account lists, configuring intent signals, and training your team—can take days or weeks. If you have a dedicated RevOps function, that investment might be justified; if you’re a founder or a solo sales rep trying to generate leads today, Origami’s prompt-driven simplicity wins hands-down.


## How Do Pricing and Value Compare?


Origami leads with a genuinely free plan (1,000 credits, no credit card required) and paid plans starting at $29/month. Even the $29 plan includes 2,000 credits, and you can scale up to 23,000 credits/month for $299 before moving to custom enterprise pricing. The credit model lets you control spend: a simple list build might cost a handful of credits, while a deeper search with email and phone enrichment uses more. This makes Origami accessible for early-stage startups and solo operators, as well as larger teams that want to track costs per lead.


SalesIntel does not publish pricing publicly—you have to contact sales for a quote. That’s typical for enterprise-focused data platforms with human verification and intent data. While the final price depends on your user count and desired features, it is generally positioned for companies with dedicated sales budgets and a need for high-confidence contact data. For organizations that have exhausted the capabilities of self-serve tools, SalesIntel’s fully managed research can deliver strong ROI. But for teams that are still proving out their ICP or that need to prospect into lower-cost segments, the opacity and likely higher entry point make Origami the more predictable and affordable option.


## Where SalesIntel Still Wins


This isn’t a one-sided story. SalesIntel genuinely excels in a few specific areas:


- **Human-Verified Direct Dials.** If your sales motion relies heavily on cold calling C-suite executives at Fortune 2000 companies, the manually validated phone numbers in SalesIntel can be worth their weight in gold. Origami uses waterfall enrichment from multiple providers, which is accurate for most emails but can’t match the direct-dial hit rate of a researcher who has called the switchboard.
- **Integrated Intent Data.** SalesIntel bundles visitor identification, technographic insights, and news alerts directly into the platform. If you need a single dashboard that tells you which accounts are currently researching your category, SalesIntel’s all-in-one approach removes the need to stitch together multiple tools.
- **Compliance-Ready Data.** For regulated industries that require data lineage and strict sourcing, SalesIntel’s manual verification process provides a clean audit trail. Origami’s web-derived data is sourced from public records and third-party enrichment providers, which is adequate for most businesses but may not satisfy the most stringent compliance departments.


## Who Should Choose Origami?


Origami is the natural fit for:


- **Teams that value speed and flexibility over deep account planning.** You have an ICP in mind, and you want a list now—not after a day of building filters.
- **Companies selling into non-traditional verticals** (local services, independent contractors, specialized agencies, industrial SMEs) where static databases have poor coverage.
- **Founders, solo sales reps, and lean teams** who need a tool that works out of the box, with no training and a free entry point.
- **Organizations that want to augment their existing database** by searching the live web for contacts that Apollo, ZoomInfo, or SalesIntel miss. Using Origami alongside a static database often gives the best of both worlds.
- **Anyone who prefers to pay only for what they use** rather than committing to a large annual contract.


## Who Should Choose SalesIntel?


SalesIntel is likely the better choice if:


- **Your primary use case is high-volume cold calling into enterprise accounts** , and you absolutely need verified direct dials.
- **Your company already uses intent data heavily** and you want it integrated into the same platform as your contact records.
- **You work in a highly regulated industry** (finance, healthcare, government) that mandates auditable data sourcing.
- **You have a dedicated sales operations team** that can manage the platform, build reports, and optimize usage—and can justify the enterprise price tag.


## Verdict


For most sales teams—especially those selling into SMB, local, or niche B2B markets—Origami is the better starting point. Its free plan, prompt-driven interface, and live web search make it the more accessible and adaptable tool for building prospect lists quickly. You can find contacts that traditional databases miss, and you can do it without a RevOps team.


SalesIntel remains a powerful platform for enterprise-focused organizations that need human-verified data and integrated intent signals. If your team lives in Salesforce, relies on cold calling as a primary channel, and has the budget for a custom contract, SalesIntel’s accuracy and compliance features are strong selling points.


But if you’re reading this because you want to pull a list of, say, independent optometrists in Texas who accept Medicaid—or engineering managers at German auto suppliers that recently posted job openings—you’ll likely have better luck starting with Origami. And with a free plan that requires no credit card, there’s no reason not to try it first.
