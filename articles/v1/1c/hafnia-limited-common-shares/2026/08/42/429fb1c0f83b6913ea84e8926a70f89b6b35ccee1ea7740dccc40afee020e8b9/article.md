# Hafnia Supports 25 Newly Graduated Cadets at Regional Maritime University's 20th Graduation

The Regional Maritime University (RMU) recently held its 20th Congregation graduation ceremony marking the end of years of study for hundreds of cadets.


Hafnia’s **** ties **** with **** RMU **** date **** back **** to **** March **** 2023, **** when **** the **** two **** signed **** a **** Memorandum **** of **** Understanding **** to **** bring **** top **** cadets **** from **** West **** Africa **** aboard **** Hafnia **** vessels **** for **** their **** cadetships **** and **** first **** roles **** at **** sea. **** Since **** then, **** the **** partnership **** has **** grown **** through **** guest **** lectures, **** mentoring, **** and **** a **** steady **** pipeline **** of **** graduates **** joining **** the **** fleet **.**


Of the 391 graduates in 2026, 25 cadets have been shortlisted by Hafnia and Marlin Marine Crewing Services (the crewing company managing crewing for Hafnia in Ghana) – in which the cadets are set to join Hafnia vessels following the graduation.


During the graduation ceremony, Hafnia also supported three awards for academic excellence at this year’s ceremony. Each recipient received a monetary prize, Hafnia Certificate of Excellence, alongside confirmations of commencing their professional careers aboard Hafnia vessels.


Congratulations to the award recipients: Abayateye Desmond – Best in Navigation (BSc Nautical Science), Akpakpli Marcellina Awovi – Best in Marine Electrical and Electronic Systems III (BSc Marine Electrical and Electronic Engineering) and Agyeman Dorothy Nana Akua Darkua – Best in Marine Engineering (BSc Marine Engineering)


The work between MMCS, Hafnia, and the Regional Maritime University continues to open and drive career paths for the next generation of seafarers – and to build the skilled crews our fleet depends on.
