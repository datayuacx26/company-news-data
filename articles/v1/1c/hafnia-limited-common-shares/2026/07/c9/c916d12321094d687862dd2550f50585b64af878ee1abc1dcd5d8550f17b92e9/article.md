# Pankaj Porwal: Speaker at Headway Maritime Decarbonization Seminar

Pankaj Porwal, General Manager Fleet Sustainability at Hafnia, recently participated as a panel speaker at the Maritime Decarbonizations Seminar organized by Headway Maritime.


The discussion focused on how the maritime industry can translate decarbonization ambitions into practical actions across fleets and operations.


Topics included operational efficiency, data-driven decision-making, crew engagement, and the role of collaboration in supporting the industry’s sustainability objectives.


During the session, Pankaj shared Hafnia’s approach to advancing its decarbonization ambitions through fleet optimization initiatives, operational performance improvements, and the practical implementation of sustainability measures across the organization.


The panel also explored the opportunities and challenges associated with future fuel pathways, including considerations related to infrastructure, cost, and regulatory developments. The speakers highlighted the importance of a pragmatic and flexible approach as the industry continues its transition towards lower-emission operations.


The discussion provided valuable insights into how industry stakeholders are approaching decarbonization and reinforced the importance of collaboration, innovation, and practical execution to support long-term progress.


Fellow panelists included Gurpreet Singh of Berge Bulk and Caroline Tan of Hong Lam Marine.
