# Simon Gilles Fassot Presented Hafnia's Data and AI Initiatives at Databricks Data + AI Summit 2026

Hafnia’s Data Analytics & AI team, represented by Simon Gilles Fassot, General Manager and Head of Global Data Analytics & AI, recently presented at the Databricks Data + AI Summit 2026 in San Francisco, one of the world’s leading conferences dedicated to data and artificial intelligence, attended by more than 32,000 industry professionals.


The session explored how Hafnia is bringing together data, analytics, and AI within a unified environment to support business operations and decision-making across the organization. Through practical examples, Simon shared how trusted and governed data can be securely leveraged to help employees access insights, automate workflows, and enable faster, more informed decisions.


The presentation showcased Hafnia’s continued focus on building scalable digital capabilities and applying data and AI in practical ways that create value across the business. It also highlighted how technology can support operational excellence by connecting data, analytics, and workflows in a secure and governed framework.
