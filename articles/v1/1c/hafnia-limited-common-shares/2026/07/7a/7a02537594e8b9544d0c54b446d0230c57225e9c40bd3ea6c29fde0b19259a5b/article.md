# Suneha Gadpande Featured on SMOU's Knot Your Average Podcast

Suneha Gadpande, Marine and HSEQ Superintendent, was recently featured on *Knot Your Average Podcast* as part of a special series marking the International Day for Women in Maritime.


Presented by the Singapore Maritime Officers’ Union / Wavelink Group in collaboration with the Singapore Shipping Association’s (SSA) Young Executive Group (YEG), the series brings together young maritime voices to share perspectives shaping the future of the industry.


Across the episodes, Suneha and fellow guests share perspectives on their career journeys, the challenges and trade-offs they have faced, and the realities of building and sustaining a career in maritime. Suneha also reflects on her experience at sea and her transition ashore to Hafnia.
