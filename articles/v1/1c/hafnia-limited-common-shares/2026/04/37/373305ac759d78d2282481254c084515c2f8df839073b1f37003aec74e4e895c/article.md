# Ray Lee: Panel Speaker at Singapore Maritime Week

Ray Lee Tong Chok (Marine, Security) represented Hafnia as a panel speaker at the Safeguarding Lives at Sea event during Singapore Maritime Week on 23 April 2026.


The event, hosted by the Foundation for Humanitarian Action at Sea (FHAS) in partnership with the S. Rajaratnam School of International Studies (RSIS), brought together operational and policy stakeholders to strengthen humanitarian responses at sea and address pressing operational and legal challenges, with the aim of building trust and advancing practical, coordinated solutions.


As part of the panel, Ray shared operational insights on how seafarers prepare and respond to distress situations. The discussion reinforced that the obligation to assist always takes priority, with safety of life at sea remaining the guiding principle.
