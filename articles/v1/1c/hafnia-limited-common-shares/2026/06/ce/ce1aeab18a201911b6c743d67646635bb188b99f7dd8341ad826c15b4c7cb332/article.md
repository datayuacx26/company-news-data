# Hafnia Hosts Pool Board Meeting in Greece during Posidonia 2026

Hafnia recently hosted its bi-annual Pool Board Meeting in Greece during Posidonia 2026, welcoming over 50 attendees representing our 24 Pool Partners.


The meeting offered an important platform for dialogue, bringing Partners together to discuss market developments, exchange perspectives and strengthen alignment across the Hafnia Pools.


Key topics included the current geopolitical landscape, ongoing market volatility, and Hafnia’s spot market approach and strategy.


Other highlights included the development of our MR Pool, which amongst others is welcoming eight newbuilds this year, reflecting the continued renewal and competitiveness of Hafnia’s modern fleet. Additional topics in the program included market presentations and dedicated pool meetings across MR, LR1 and Chemicals, alongside various social engagements.


The meeting underlined Hafnia’s continued commitment to close partner engagement and in creating a strong forum for commercial dialogue, insight sharing, and long-term value creation.
