# Operations Event 2026 Brings Together Tanker Operations Teams Across the Industry

Last week, Hafnia hosted a **Copenhagen** Operations Event alongside TORM, NORDEN, Stena Bulk, Maersk Tankers and Weco Tankers.


The event brought together more than 110+ operations representatives from oil majors, traders, customers and brokers from all over Europe together with the operations teams from Hafnia, TORM, NORDEN, Stena Bulk, Maersk Tankers and Weco Tankers for a day focused on networking, collaboration and strengthening industry relationships.


The program included a cruise through Copenhagen Harbour aboard MS Bjørnsholm, followed by team activities at Langelinie Park designed to encourage interaction and cooperation across organizations.


The day concluded with an evening gathering, providing further opportunities for discussions and relationship building among participants.


We thank all participants for a successful event and look forward to future gatherings!
