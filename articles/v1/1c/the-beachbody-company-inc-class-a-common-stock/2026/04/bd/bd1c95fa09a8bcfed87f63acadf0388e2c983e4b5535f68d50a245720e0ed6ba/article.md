# The Shakeology Reviews Are In: Over 1 Billion Servings and Counting:

It’s a number that stops you in your tracks: **over one billion servings sold** . That’s an impressive number for a product that’s never been on a store shelf (so far). But this protein-superfood shake has been serving satisfied customers for more than 15 years, and unlike many nutrition brands who will build momentum on a message of quality and potency but gradually dilute that quality with eventual profit-taking, Shakeology has maintained if not improved its standard of excellence and efficacy even to this day.


## **The Big Picture: What the Numbers Show**


---


According to a roundup by BrandRated.com, Shakeology holds an **average rating of 4.2 out of 5 stars on Amazon** , where the most popular product — the Chocolate Vegan 30-serving bag — consistently generates enthusiastic feedback. On Trustpilot, the picture is similarly warm among fans: a review analysis at HonestBrandReviews.com found that **66% of Shakeology reviews were rated “Great” or “Excellent.”**


It’s worth noting upfront — as multiple review sites flag Shakeology was sold through a network of independent “Beachbody Coaches,” many of whom earned commissions on sales. In 2024, BODi closed the network and moved to a multi-channel sales model which will include retail.


Where many reviews come from former Beachbody Coaches, plenty of non-affiliated reviewers have also weighed in positively, and their voices make up much of what follows.

## **So, Why Did Shakeology Sell Over a Billion Servings?**


---


A billion servings is a remarkable figure for a premium product. So what explains that kind of sustained demand for over 15 years?


**It fills a real nutrition gap.** When Shakeology launched in 2009, the category of “superfood nutrition shake” didn’t even exist. BODi’s own blog states that *“Shakeology created the category of superfood nutrition shakes 15 years ago.”* Particularly for its own CEO, who is a self-proclaimed “veggie hater”, he commissioned the creation of Shakeology for people who couldn’t — or wouldn’t — eat the recommended daily servings of fruits, vegetables, and fiber.


**It pairs incredibly well with a fitness ecosystem.** Shakeology doesn’t exist in isolation. It is sold alongside over 140 fitness programs such as P90X, Insanity, 21-Day Fix, and other high-engagement home workout programs, and often a Shakeology monthly subscription comes with a free trial of the BODi fitness platform. People who want real lifestyle change find the combination of starting a new fitness program plus Shakeology to be the combination that got them the best results.


**It tastes good enough to be a habit.** This point can’t be overstated. A healthy product that people hate drinking eventually gets abandoned. Shakeology’s consistent praise for taste helped turn it into a daily ritual rather than a chore for millions of customers.


**It keeps evolving.** BODi publishes updates to the Shakeology formula on an ongoing basis, with co-creators Isabelle Daikeler and Darin Olien meeting monthly with an R&D team to review ingredients and testing standards. This team is demanding. And the 2024 launch of the 0g Added Sugar line of Shakeology is one example of the brand responding to changing consumer priorities without sacrificing taste or nutrition.


## **A Fair Note**


---


It would be incomplete not to acknowledge that Shakeology some reviews across the internet are mixed. Critics point to the high price – although their comparisons tend to be to simple protein shakes or straight greens powders, which lack the comprehensive nature of Shakeology. Some users said they had digestive issues, but solved them by cutting the serving size in half. The EatProteins.com review notes that the product has complaints related to subscription cancellation issues, a separate matter from the product itself and one which BODi believes has been solved with their move to Shopify and it’s simple to use subscription management software.


What the positive reviews reflect is a real and loyal customer base for whom Shakeology genuinely works — for taste, nutrition, convenience, and healthy habits. That’s a meaningful story and supports that the first billion servings sold will not be its last.
