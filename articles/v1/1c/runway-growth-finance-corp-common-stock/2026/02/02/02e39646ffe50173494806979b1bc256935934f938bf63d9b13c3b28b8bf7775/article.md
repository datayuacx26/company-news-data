# Runway Growth Finance Corp. Announces Date for Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Financial Results and Conference Call

## Release Details


## Runway Growth Finance Corp. Announces Date for Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Financial Results and Conference Call


February 10, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.runwaygrowth.com/node/10531/pdf)


MENLO PARK, Calif., Feb. 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Runway Growth Finance Corp. (“Runway Growth” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: RWAY), a leading provider of flexible capital solutions to late- and growth-stage companies seeking an alternative to raising equity, today announced that it will release its fourth quarter and full year 2025 financial results after market close on Thursday, March 12, 2026. Runway Growth will discuss its financial results on a conference call that day at 2:00 p.m. PT (5:00 p.m. ET).


To participate in the conference call or webcast, participants should register online at the[Runway Growth Investor Relations website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=6vxjR_EU2vbHAig77JYjbLXsRrFRuewXfla-fADQ4_SDWEg5CjDAT6bMmC6EEf2RIsa6KaF65iHTws4CGWtgcCcmXh4Gb-aDjSKUnrUX90cwsoLyXlndPkAMxV5XEAYN93-Ba-DdmuRulnSJix2fug==) . Participants are requested to register a day in advance or at a minimum 15 minutes before the start of the call. The earnings call can also be accessed through the following links:


- [Conference Call](https://register-conf.media-server.com/register/BIcebfaa0e6390423cad4217fede352eae)
- [Webcast](https://edge.media-server.com/mmc/p/chn278vy/)


A replay of the webcast will be available two hours after the call and archived on the same web page for 90 days.


**About Runway Growth Finance Corp.**


Runway Growth is a specialty finance company focused on providing flexible capital solutions to late- and growth-stage companies seeking an alternative to raising equity. Runway Growth is a closed-end investment fund that has elected to be regulated as a business development company under the Investment Company Act of 1940, as amended. Runway Growth is externally managed by Runway Growth Capital LLC, an affiliate of BC Partners Advisors L.P., and led by industry veteran David Spreng. For more information, please visit[www.runwaygrowth.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Vj7tP4e8x94sG9yFhXnSzkbX7ml9aalEnF6KF5YnUTT24WQQ1WLtpXBZccdK-vc4xsHvPko-QELjGboY34G8TafPzoyG2Upqw6WVSBzNxWc=) .
