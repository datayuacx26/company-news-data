# WeRide | WeRide Announces Strong Q1 2026 Financial Results: Total Revenue Hit US$16.5 Million, Driven by Robotaxi and ADAS Commercialization

**WeRide (NASDAQ: WRD, HKEX: 0800)** , a global leader in autonomous driving technology, announced its financial results for the **first quarter of 2026** on May 13, 2026.


In Q1, the company reported total revenue of **US$16.5 million, up 58% year‑over‑year (YoY).** Driven by improved operational efficiency and optimized resource allocation, operating losses continued to narrow YoY. **Gross profit rose 56% YoY** to **US$5.7 million** , with a **gross margin of 35%** , leading the industry.


Robotaxi commercialization continued to scale. **Registered Robotaxi users in China** roughly **doubled YoY** , with **average daily orders** per vehicle exceeding **17** and reaching **28** during peak periods. WeRide’s global Robotaxi fleet has now reached **about 1,300** vehicles, with plans to deploy **200,000** Robotaxis and other autonomous vehicles globally over the next five years.


Meanwhile, mass production of WeRide's L2++ one‑stage end‑to‑end ADAS solution, **WeRide Driving (WRD 3.0),** accelerated during the quarter, securing nearly 30 vehicle design wins. The first mass‑produced passenger vehicle co‑developed with GAC Aion, the Aion N60, officially launched. In addition, WRD 3.0 also became the first and only solution in the history of the China Urban Intelligent Driving Competition to achieve **four consecutive wins** , underscoring WeRide's industry-leading ADAS capabilities.


As the world's only company globally to achieve large‑scale commercial deployment of both L4 fully driverless and L2++ ADAS solutions, WeRide will continue to leverage its technology strengths to advance the global commercialization of autonomous driving across diverse global scenarios.
