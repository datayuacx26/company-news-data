# WeRide | Italian Ambassador to China Experiences WeRide Robotaxi, Praises China’s Leading Autonomous Driving Technology

**GUANGZHOU, China – March 30, 2026** – Massimo Ambrosetti, the Ambassador of Italy to China, visited Guangzhou and experienced WeRide’s fully driverless Robotaxi during a visit to the company's office. Representatives from WeRide’s government relations and global marketing teams accompanied the visit and held discussions with the Ambassador on potential applications of autonomous driving technology in Italy.


*From left to right: Third - Massimo Ambrosetti, Italian Ambassador to China; Second - Valerio de Parolis, Consul General of Italy in Guangzhou; Fourth - Huang Yehua, Deputy General Manager of WeRide; Fifth - Maeve Zhang, Senior Director of PR & Marketing at WeRide*


During the weekend afternoon peak traffic period in Guangzhou, WeRide’s Robotaxi navigated complex urban traffic smoothly and confidently. The ride offered Ambassador Ambrosetti firsthand insight into the safety, comfort, and stability enabled by WeRide’s Level 4 (L4) autonomous driving technology.


*Ambassador Massimo Ambrosetti experiencing WeRide Robotaxi GXR*


The Ambassador rode in WeRide’s Robotaxi GXR, a mass‑produced model specifically designed for urban mobility scenarios. The GXR features the world’s first hidden B‑pillar design in a Robotaxi and supports seating for up to five passengers. It offers one of the largest entry/exit spaces and interior space in the industry, delivering an exceptionally spacious and comfortable rider experience.


Equipped with WeRide’s self‑developed HPC 3.0 high‑performance computing platform, the Robotaxi GXR delivers advanced L4 autonomous driving capabilities on public roads. The platform processes over 20 quadrillion (20 PFLOPS) complex calculations per second, enabling the vehicle to handle dense traffic flows, intersection merges, and pedestrian interactions with high decision‑making efficiency and smooth driving performance.


At the end of the ride, Ambassador Ambrosetti praised the Robotaxi GXR's overall performance, describing the ride as “very comfortable and safe.” He also exchanged views with WeRide on potential deployment scenarios and development plans for autonomous driving technology in Italy.


The Ambassador’s recognition reflects WeRide’s deep technical expertise built through years of autonomous driving R&D and large‑scale global deployment. As a global leader in autonomous driving, WeRide leverages its proprietary WeRide One universal autonomous driving platform to deploy products across **12 countries and over 40 cities** . The company’s autonomous fleet has reached **2,113** vehicles, including **1,125 Robotaxis.**


WeRide’s autonomous driving services are now deployed across four European markets: **France, Belgium, Switzerland, and Slovakia.**


- **France** : WeRide partnered with Renault Group and beti to launch Europe’s first fully driverless commercial autonomous bus project in Drôme.
- **Belgium** : the WeRide Robobus has entered regular commercial operations in Leuven.
- **Switzerland** : WeRide obtained the country's first fully driverless Robotaxi permit and will roll out driverless Robotaxi services in the first half of 2026.
- **Slovakia** : WeRide signed a national‑level strategic partnership with ELEVATE Slovakia to launch the country’s first autonomous driving project.


*WeRide Robotaxi operating in Furttal, Switzerland*


Looking ahead, WeRide will continue accelerating its global strategy to enable large‑scale deployment of autonomous driving technology across Europe and worldwide. The company aims to deploy more than **10,000 Robotaxis by 2030** , delivering safe, reliable, and accessible autonomous mobility services to more regions around the globe.
