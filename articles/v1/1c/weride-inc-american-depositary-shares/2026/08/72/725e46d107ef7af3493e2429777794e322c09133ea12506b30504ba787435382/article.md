# WeRide | WeRide Announces 2026 Q2 and H1 Financial Results: Quarterly Revenue Increases 82% YoY and Overseas Revenue Increases 164% YoY in Q2 2026

**WeRide (NASDAQ: WRD, HKEX: 0800),** a global leader in autonomous driving technology, announced its financial results for the **second quarter and first half of 2026** on August 12, 2026.


In the first half of 2026, WeRide reported a total revenue of **US$51.0 million, up 73% year‑over‑year (YoY).** In the second quarter of 2026, total revenue reached US$34.2 million, representing an increase of 82% YoY and an increase of 103% QoQ. Gross margin rose to 37.5%, reflecting an increase of 9.4 percentage points compared with Q2 2025 and continued improvements in operating efficiency.


International expansion has become WeRide's strongest driver of growth. Overseas revenue increased 154% YoY in the first half of 2026, and increased 164% YoY and 169% QoQ in the second quarter. With Robotaxi deployments launching across Europe, fully driverless operations expanding in the Middle East, and right-hand-drive Robotaxis planned for deployment in Singapore and Hong Kong, WeRide's proven asset-light model is being rapidly replicated across additional markets. To date, WeRide has expanded its autonomous driving footprint across 13 countries and over 60 cities globally, establishing commercially viable business models in multiple overseas cities. WeRide's international business is expected to generate recurring cash flow and become an important profit center as it continues to be supported by attractive gross margin profile and strong incremental economics.


Commercialization of both WeRide's L4 and L2++/L3 businesses continued to accelerate and gain momentum. In the second quarter of 2026, revenue from WeRide's L4 business reached US$18.5 million, representing a 131% quarter over quarter (QoQ) growth, while number of registered users of the WeRide Robotaxi service in China increased 35% QoQ. As of July 31, WeRide's global L4 fleet comprised approximately 3,400 vehicles, including more than 1,800 Robotaxis. Second-quarter revenue of WeRide's L2++/L3 business also increased 219% QoQ. Similarly in the second quarter of 2026, total deliveries of WRD 3.0, our one-stage end-to-end L2++/ L3 solution, were approximately 30,000 units. To date, we have secured L2++ production design wins for over 30 vehicle models.


Autonomous driving is emerging as one of the most commercially viable applications of Physical AI. WeRide has established a strong foundation for long-term growth with its Physical AI technology platform. It is also among the first in the industry to scale Physical AI from technology validation to commercial deployment as it continues to advance the commercialization of Physical AI through real-world operations at scale.
