### **Preparing Your Space for Virtual Staging**


Before virtual staging, it's crucial to lay the groundwork for success. Begin by decluttering and organizing each space.


Then determine how you can optimize lighting. Natural light is essential; open curtains, let sunlight flood the rooms, and ensure each space is well-lit. This not only creates a more appealing atmosphere but also provides a clearer canvas for the virtual elements.
