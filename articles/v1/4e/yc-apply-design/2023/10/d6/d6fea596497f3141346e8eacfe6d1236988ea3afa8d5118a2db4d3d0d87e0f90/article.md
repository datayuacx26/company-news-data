### **12. Is Virtual Staging Ethical in Real Estate Marketing?**


Virtual staging is ethical as long as it is used responsibly. It should enhance the presentation of a property without misleading potential buyers. It's important to accurately represent the space and avoid altering fundamental aspects like size, structural features, or significant property flaws. Transparency is key; realtors should disclose that the property has been virtually staged.
