# The Best Virtual Staging Plan

Virtual staging has become a cornerstone of effective real estate marketing. As the industry grows even more competitive and digital-first, having a reliable, fast, and creative staging strategy isn’t just nice to have — it’s essential.


Whether you're an agent, photographer, or broker, this guide will help you build a scalable virtual staging plan that delivers consistent results across every listing.


**1. Standardize Your Staging Workflow**


Consistency saves time and builds brand credibility. Start by defining a simple checklist:


- Upload all listing photos to ApplyDesign immediately after the shoot.
- Identify vacant or under-staged rooms — these are priority candidates for virtual staging.
- Use saved room templates to apply consistent design styles that fit your market.


Tip: ApplyDesign's real-time editor lets you adjust furniture, colors, and layouts in seconds, which means no back-and-forth emails with outsourced designers.


**2. Choose Styles That Fit Your Market**


Buyers in different regions respond to different aesthetics. A beach condo in Miami shouldn’t look like a loft in Brooklyn.


With ApplyDesign, you can switch between styles like **Scandinavian, Modern Farmhouse, and Urban Industrial** to test what resonates.


- **Suburban homes** : Opt for warm, family-friendly layouts with soft textiles and wood accents.
- **Downtown condos** : Lean into clean lines, metal, and monochromatic palettes.


**3. Design for Versatility**


Every buyer has a different vision for a space. Go beyond "just staged" and offer multiple perspectives when needed:


- Stage a bedroom as a **nursery** or a **home office** .
- Show an open floor plan with **two different furniture layouts** .
- Offer “with and without” versions to let buyers mentally test-drive the home.


ApplyDesign’s drag-and-drop tools and reusable design templates make this fast and simple.


**4. Use Mobile-Friendly Export Formats**


With over 70% of real estate browsing happening on mobile, it’s critical that your images look great in portrait format. Export vertical-friendly visuals directly from ApplyDesign to use in:


- Instagram and Facebook Stories
- Short-form video walkthroughs
- Email marketing campaigns


**5. Integrate Virtual Staging Into Your Listing Presentations**


Sellers expect tech-savvy agents. Show before-and-after virtual staging examples during your pitch and explain how it helps homes sell faster.


With ApplyDesign, you can generate high-res exports and web-ready galleries that impress on the spot.


**6. Track What’s Working**


Every home is different, but your staging performance doesn’t have to be a mystery.


Track:


- **Time on market** before/after staging
- **Click-through rates** on MLS and Zillow
- **Feedback from showings**


ApplyDesign lets you A/B test different styles and layouts — empowering you with data, not just design.
