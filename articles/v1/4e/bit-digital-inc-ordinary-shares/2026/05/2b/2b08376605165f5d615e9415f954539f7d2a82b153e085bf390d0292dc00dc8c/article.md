NEW YORK, May


7


, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — Bit Digital, Inc. (Nasdaq: BTBT) (“Bit Digital” or the “Company”) today announced that it will release its financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026, after the market closes on Thursday, May 14, 2026.
