# AlTi Global, Inc. Reports Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results

**About AlTi Global, Inc.**


AlTi is a global wealth and investment partner to families, family offices, foundations and institutions, helping clients activate capital with clarity, bring structure to complexity, and plan with purpose across borders and generations. AlTi combines the breadth of a global firm with the service offering of a family office to deliver solutions designed to meet the full complexity of wealth and capital. We currently manage or advise on approximately $96 billion in combined assets and have an expansive network of approximately 465 professionals globally.
