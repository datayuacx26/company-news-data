# Piper Sandler Strengthens Healthcare Investment Banking Team with the Addition of Jason Arnold

MINNEAPOLIS & LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Feb. 23, 2026--[Piper Sandler Companies](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.pipersandler.com%2F&esheet=54428841&newsitemid=20260223201916&lan=en-US&anchor=Piper+Sandler+Companies&index=1&md5=ded8306bef0f8360aaf296f69f7f4c42) (NYSE: PIPR), a leading investment bank, is pleased to announce the addition of[Jason Arnold](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.pipersandler.com%2Fabout%2Fpeople%2Fjason-arnold&esheet=54428841&newsitemid=20260223201916&lan=en-US&anchor=Jason+Arnold&index=2&md5=012e7f91b7c7a49661cec2795b47ed8a) as a managing director in the healthcare investment banking group. Arnold will be initially based in London while serving clients globally. He will be advising life science tools and diagnostic companies on mergers and acquisitions, strategic partnerships and capital raising transactions.


“We’re delighted to welcome Jason to the healthcare investment banking team. He brings extensive experience advising executives and boards across the tools and diagnostics landscape, both in Europe and globally. His perspective and relationships will further strengthen our ability to deliver strategic advice to clients worldwide,” said[Peter Day](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.pipersandler.com%2Fabout%2Fpeople%2Fpeter-day&esheet=54428841&newsitemid=20260223201916&lan=en-US&anchor=Peter+Day&index=3&md5=22afd0d6cd4a3896c6c89836c75e29f7) , global group head of healthcare investment banking at Piper Sandler.


"I’m excited to join the global healthcare investment banking team, given its deep sector expertise and broad engagement across the healthcare ecosystem. I look forward to working with my partners to provide global clients with unique market insights and access to the firm’s leading platform, building on its strong foundation in life science tools and diagnostics to help clients execute complex strategic and capital markets decisions with confidence," said Arnold.


Prior to joining Piper Sandler, Arnold was a partner and managing director at William Blair, where he spent 16 years in its healthcare investment banking group. He began his career at Ernst & Young. Arnold received a bachelor’s degree and a master’s degree from the University of Colorado, Boulder.
