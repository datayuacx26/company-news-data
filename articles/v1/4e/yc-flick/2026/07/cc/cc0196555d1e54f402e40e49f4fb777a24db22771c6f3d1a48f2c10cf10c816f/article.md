# How to Keep a Character Consistent in GPT Image

## HOW IT WORKS


1.


---


STEP 1


LOCK A REFERENCE IMAGE


Generate or upload one clear image of your character. This is the design every later prompt points back to.


2.


---


STEP 2


WORK IN ONE THREAD


Keep all generations in a single chat thread so the model retains context about the character between prompts.


3.


---


STEP 3


RE-FEED THE REFERENCE EACH TIME


For every new image, point to the reference and say to use the exact same character, same face and outfit.


4.


---


STEP 4


RE-ANCHOR AND DESCRIBE DETAILS


Feed the best output back as the new reference and spell out the identity details the model tends to drop, like accessories and colors.


5.


---


STEP 5


BRING IT TO LIFE IN FLICK


GPT Image makes stills only. Drop your locked character into Flick and animate it with Veo 3, Kling, or Seedance.
