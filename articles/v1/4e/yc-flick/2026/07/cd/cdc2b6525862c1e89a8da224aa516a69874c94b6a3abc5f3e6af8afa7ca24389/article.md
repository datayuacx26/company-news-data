# How to Keep a Character Consistent in Nano Banana

## HOW IT WORKS


1.


---


STEP 1


PROVIDE REFERENCE IMAGES


Give Nano Banana one or more clear images of your subject, a person, pet, mascot, or product, to define the identity.

2.


---


STEP 2


PROMPT THE NEW SCENE


Ask in natural language to place that same subject in a new scene or pose, telling it to keep the face and outfit.

3.


---


STEP 3


BLEND REFERENCES WITH FUSION


Combine multiple references, such as the character plus a wardrobe or background, into one image while preserving identity.

4.


---


STEP 4


REFINE WITH CONVERSATIONAL EDITS


Iterate with edits like changing the lighting while keeping the face. Identity holds across edits, though it can soften after many changes.

---


STEP 5


BRING IT TO LIFE IN FLICK
