# How to Keep a Character Consistent in Sora

## HOW IT WORKS


1.


---


STEP 1


OPEN CREATE CAMEO


In the Sora app, go to your profile and start a new Cameo. This is where Sora builds the reusable identity.


2.


---


STEP 2


RECORD THE REFERENCE VIDEO


Record a few seconds of the subject. For a self-cameo you turn your head and count aloud, which also confirms consent. For a pet or persona, upload short footage.


3.


---


STEP 3


NAME IT AND SET PERMISSIONS


Give the Cameo a name and choose who can use it. Save it so it becomes a reusable character.


4.


---


STEP 4


INSERT THE CAMEO INTO PROMPTS


Tag the saved Cameo in any new generation and Sora re-renders that identity into the scene, with synced voice for self-cameos.


5.


---


STEP 5


KEEP CONTINUITY ACROSS SHOTS


Reuse the same Cameo for every shot in a sequence. For finer art direction, lock a character in Flick and carry it across other models too.
