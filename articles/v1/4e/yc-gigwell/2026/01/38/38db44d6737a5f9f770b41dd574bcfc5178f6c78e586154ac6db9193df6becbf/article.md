# Answers to common questions about Talent Buyer Pro, the dedicated software by Gigwell for venues, promoters, and talent buyers.

## **FAQ: Talent Buyer Pro for Venues & Promoters**


**What is Talent Buyer Pro?**
Talent Buyer Pro is live music management software built for venues, promoters, and talent buyers. It streamlines artist bookings, contracts, settlements, and team workflows to save time and maximize revenue.


**How does Talent Buyer Pro simplify the booking process?**
Talent Buyer Pro provides a smart holds calendar, branded offer sheets, automated task workflows, and centralized task views. These features keep teams in sync and allow venues to manage offers, contracts, and settlements efficiently.


**Can Talent Buyer Pro help manage multiple shows at once?**
Yes. The platform’s smart calendar tracks multiple holds, confirmed shows, on-sale dates, and announcements, all with color-coded entries and customizable privacy controls.


**How does Talent Buyer Pro improve team productivity?**
Automated sequential workflows delegate tasks to the right team members at the right time. For example, signing a contract can trigger marketing and logistics actions, keeping everyone aligned and events on track.


**Does Talent Buyer Pro provide reporting and insights?**
Absolutely. The platform offers powerful, real-time reports to track efficiencies, monitor growth, identify bottlenecks, and make informed financial decisions for your venue or festival.


**How are offer sheets and contracts handled?**
Talent Buyer Pro allows venues to generate professional, branded offer sheets with customizable terms. Real-time tracking shows receipt, opens, and forwards to ensure nothing slips through the cracks.


**Can Talent Buyer Pro manage my venue’s contacts?**
Yes. It includes a centralized contacts database where you can tag and manage artists, promoters, and venue contacts. Integration with Gmail ensures all communication is tracked and organized.


**Is Talent Buyer Pro suitable for international venues?**
Yes. The platform supports international currency and multiple venue workflows, making it scalable for festivals, large venues, and multi-location operations.


**How is Talent Buyer Pro different from Productivity Suite?**
Talent Buyer Pro is built specifically for venues, promoters, and talent buyers to manage bookings, offers, settlements, and team workflows. Productivity Suite is designed for booking agents to manage artist rosters, tour schedules, and agency workflows. Each product serves a different role in the live music ecosystem.


**How is Talent Buyer Pro different from Artist Essentials?**
Talent Buyer Pro is for venues and promoters, while Artist Essentials is designed for artists to manage profiles, press kits, tour logistics, and communications with agents and venues. Each product is tailored to its user’s workflow.


**What is the pricing for Talent Buyer Pro?**
Talent Buyer Pro starts at $99 per month and scales up based on your venue’s size and needs. For a custom quote or enterprise pricing, contact our sales team to find the best plan for your workflow.


**What makes Talent Buyer Pro different from other venue software?**
Talent Buyer Pro combines real-time holds, branded offers, automated workflows, settlements, and actionable reporting into a solution built specifically for live music venues and talent buyers. It’s optimized for team productivity and revenue tracking.


**How can I get started with Talent Buyer Pro?**
You can request a demo through the Talent Buyer Pro landing page, explore the whitepaper, or contact the Gigwell team to see how the software fits your venue’s workflow.
