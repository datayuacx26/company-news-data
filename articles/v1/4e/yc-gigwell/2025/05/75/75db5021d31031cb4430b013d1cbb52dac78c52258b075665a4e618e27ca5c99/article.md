# **Gigwell vs Prism: Booking Software Comparison for Venues**

## Overview of Both Platforms


### Gigwell Talent Buyer Pro


**Gigwell Talent Buyer Pro** is a cloud-based platform developed by industry professionals who spent the past ten years perfecting booking software trusted by global agencies, managers, and artists.


Now for venues, Gigwell’s platform offers a more user-friendly interface and full customization to tackle booking challenges. It also has unique advanced features like artist advancing with flight and hotel lookups, integrated e-sign, a mobile app, a central talent hub for storing artist assets, and integrated email tracking so buyers and agents never lose a thread. Though currently in beta, Talent Buyer Pro already rivals most of Prism’s core features—and stands out for its legendary customer support and more affordable pricing without annual up-charges.


### Prism for Venues


**Prism** is a versatile booking platform for venues and promoters that focuses on show settlements and task tracking. It offers many core features needed to manage holds, contracts, advancing, and reporting. Prism integrates with your accounting and marketing tools but lacks mobile access, automated offer creation, and some of the deeper workflow features available in Gigwell Talent Buyer Pro.


Prism is widely used and has a strong feature set for independent venues, it does not connect natively with artist-side booking software or provide the same level of onboarding, automation, or customization options as Gigwell.
