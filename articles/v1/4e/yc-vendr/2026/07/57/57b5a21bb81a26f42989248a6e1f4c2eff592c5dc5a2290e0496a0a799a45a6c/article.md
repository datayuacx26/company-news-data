# Announcing Vendr’s New Partnership with Brex

Since launching in 2018, Vendr has sought to transform the way companies buy software and we’ve saved our customers more than $400M in the process. Today, we’re excited to share the next step in our journey to help you save: a partnership with Brex, the AI-powered spend platform known for its best-in-class corporate cards and expense management.


From now through the end of 2024, active Brex customers are eligible for 12 months of free access to Vendr Premium Procurement.


This partnership is all about helping more organizations tap into the power of both platforms, creating a seamless procurement process that helps you manage spend from intake to renewal.


**Save from Intake to Renewal with Brex and Vendr**


By using Brex and Vendr, you can streamline your procurement process for intake-to-procure and procure-to-pay to help you save time and money on software purchasing.


Here’s a peek at how the Vendr + Brex workflow looks:


1. Submit intakes in Vendr Procurement Platform
2. Get internal purchase approvals in Vendr Procurement Platform
3. Purchase the software
4. Submit invoices in Brex
5. Get internal payment approvals in Brex
6. Pay for the software in Brex
7. Stay ahead of the renewal in Vendr Procurement Platform


If this seems too easy, that’s because it is. Brex and Vendr not only empower you to create a delightful procurement process, they do so with the most advanced features on the market.


**For Active Brex Customers**


If you’re already a Brex customer, start experiencing the benefits of this powerful partnership today.[Create your Vendr account on this exclusive page](https://www.vendr.com/signup/brex) . Once you sign up, you'll receive an email to schedule your complimentary onboarding session and begin taking advantage of this powerful collaboration.


**For Prospective Brex Customers**


If you’re not already a Brex customer and are interested in learning more about Brex, you can speak with their team[here](https://www.brex.com/book-a-demo) . If you’re ready to buy, skip the negotiation and[request a private offer](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/brex/) in Vendr’s Marketplace. Once you purchase Brex, we’ll turn on your free year of Vendr Premium Procurement.


Welcome to the future of procurement. Your stakeholders are going to love you.
