## What is supplier management software?


Supplier management software refers to software solutions that automate every step of the supplier lifecycle, from onboarding to supplier performance management and offboarding. The right supplier management software can increase supplier participation, streamline spend management, simplify supplier data management, and mitigate supply chain risks.


## Benefits of supplier management automation


A supplier management system has its own set of capabilities that streamline and simplify day-to-day supplier management operations. If done right, supplier management automation can offer a ton of benefits. Listed below are five major benefits that you can enjoy by implementing good supplier management software.


### 1. Improve supplier onboarding process


Manual supplier onboarding is such a hassle. Procurement managers end up spending most of their time chasing after purchasers and supplier contacts to complete the onboarding documentation.


A supplier management system will eliminate the need for manual follow-ups and establish automated workflows to facilitate document collection and enrollment. What's more, when there is a self-service supplier portal, suppliers can keep their data up-to-date without seeking help from the procurement team.


### 2. Enhance supplier collaboration


In a manual supplier management environment, collaboration is always a challenge. If your supplier has not alloted a key account manager for your account, you will be left at the mercy of their customer support team. In both cases, you may have to wait at least 24 hours for a proper response from the supplier's team.


The collaboration happens in real time with a supplier management solution, as most platforms have built-in chat functionality. In addition to facilitating communication, some platforms provide a marketplace for suppliers and buyers.


### 3. Mitigate supplier risks


Supplier risk management is a necessary evil. In a world where even reputed software providers are at risk of having[multi-day outages](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=000320234&type=1) , it is critical to keep a close watch on your supplier's compliance and risk environment.


If you don't have a dedicated[vendor risk management software](https://www.vendr.com/blog/vendor-risk-management-software) , then a vendor management system (VMS) is a good place to start. Today, most supplier management systems come with a risk management module that takes care of supplier risk assessments and offers pre-built questionnaires to perform necessary due diligence.


### 4. Streamline performance management


There's much more to supplier performance management than merely keeping (service level agreements (SLAs) in line. Supplier performance management is the process of tracking, measuring, and analyzing an external supplier's performance at regular intervals.


Manual performance management is subject to bias and favoritism. Automation streamlines the end-to-end process, keeps it free from bias, and helps make insightful decisions. Additionally, supplier scorecards capture both qualitative and quantitative vendor performance metrics. Insights acquired through performance management help identify and weed out underperforming suppliers, thus facilitating quality management.


### 5. Control spend management


Manual spend management is fraught with characteristics that foster maverick spend. These characteristics include poor spend visibility, incomplete supplier data, vague specifications, and more.


Vendor management software helps you spot redundant purchases and overspending but also prepare ahead for SaaS renewals so that you can negotiate a better deal.


## Features to look for in a supplier management solution


Freely available product ratings and comparisons are often generic and vague. Making purchase decisions based on incomplete data can lead to undesirable complications. To make the right decision, businesses need to cross-check whether their desired supplier management solution comes with the right set of features.


Listed below are some indispensable features that an ideal supplier management software must have.


### 1. Supplier self-service


This functionality empowers suppliers to manage their profiles, update catalogs, complete risk assessments, and track payments without getting in touch with the procurement team.


### 2. 360-degree performance


Most supplier management platforms come with an intuitive dashboard that offers a bird's eye view of vendor performance data like SLA adherence, contractual compliance, and more.


### 3. Corrective actions


Top supplier management solutions have a SCAR module that initiates a supplier corrective action plan every time a supplier’s performance slips up. Some solutions even offer the feature to offboard an underperforming supplier directly.


### 4. Risk management


The power of dynamic rules-based workflows enables businesses to streamline their[risk management](https://www.vendr.com/blog/vendor-risk-management) process by ensuring policy, regulatory, and legal compliance. Some supplier management software helps to retrieve and verify supplier information like business reports, financial data, security certifications, and credit risk using the supplier's unique identification like a[D-U-N-S number](https://www.dnb.com/duns-number.html) .


### 5. Real-time collaboration


Instant collaboration functionalities help businesses collaborate with their suppliers across geographies in real time to communicate feature requests, to notify about a bug in the SaaS tool, and more.


### 6. Spend analysis


The spend analysis feature can help capture all spend categories (across suppliers, departments, and more) with automated notifications, handy dashboards, and configurable reports for informed decision-making.


### 7. Paperless processing


Supplier management processes are always a lot of paperwork. Automated supplier management solutions offer a centralized database to store all procurement documents from RFPs to invoices.


### 8. Seamless integration


Businesses that implement supplier management software can improve their end-to-end procurement process's speed, productivity, and performance from requisitioning to invoicing by effortlessly integrating with ERP, accounting systems, and more.
