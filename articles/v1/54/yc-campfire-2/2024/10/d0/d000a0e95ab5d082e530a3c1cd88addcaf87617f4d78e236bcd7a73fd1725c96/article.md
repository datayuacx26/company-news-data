Campfire


Team

[Accounting](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=Accounting)[Finance](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=Finance)


Introducing the feature no one asked for, but everyone loves. Meet modern close management - the first ledger with built in end to end close management.


Close management tasks can now be performed directly from an integrated close management checklist, accelerating your close process even further.


Our close checklist allows you to:


- Automatically perform close tasks such as drafting revenue accounting and elimination entries.
- Manage any close task such as reviewing payroll and confirming that bank reconciliations are complete.


# Here’s what to expect from our new close management feature:


# **Save time with a modern close**


Get out of spreadsheets and into software. Easily and efficiently perform tasks directly from your integrated checklist.


# **Automated user tracking by task**


Maintain audit-ready status with automated timestamp tracking for who performed and reviewed each close task. You can also require linked journal entries for easy reviewing.


# **Simplify close tasks across all systems**


Easily manage close tasks across systems. Whether in Campfire or an external system, run close tasks directly from your new Campfire close checklist.
