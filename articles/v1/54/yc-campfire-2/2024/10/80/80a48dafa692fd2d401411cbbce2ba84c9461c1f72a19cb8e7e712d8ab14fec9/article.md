# Campsites - The Community For Accounting / Finance Leaders


# Campsites mission


Campsites was founded on the premise of providing a place for accounting and finance professionals that work at tech companies to have a community focused on modernizing the role and moving forward together.


**What's included?**


1. Slack workspace that provides access to other accounting and finance professionals to ask questions to your peers. Members also post open roles and find their next role amongst the community.
2. Access to virtual and in-person events. We host dinners, lunches and other events in major cities throughout the United States and Canada, and also host virtual events such as workshops, virtual happy hours and other thought leadership and networking discussions.


**Our core values are outlined below**


**Thought-leadership** - Sharing the collective knowledge of the group to grow together through thought leadership.


**Career advancement** - **** Campsite members are high caliber and source talent amongst the group. We're building the highest caliber group of accounting and finance leaders at mid-size tech companies.


**Community** - Build your network and trade best practices with your peers - that's Campsites.
