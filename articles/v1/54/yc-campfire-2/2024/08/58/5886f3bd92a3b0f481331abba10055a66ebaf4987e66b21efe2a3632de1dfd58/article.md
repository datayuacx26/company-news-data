**Company Profile**


- **Company Name:** Revela

# **About Revela**


[Revela](https://www.revela.co/) develops software for real estate management companies that helps users streamline and automate operations. The business is primarily SaaS with usage-billing as well.


# **The Problem**


Revela was on QuickBooks and found invoice reconciliation, 606 revenue accounting, and SaaS reporting to be taking an outsized amount of time.


- **ARR reporting** : Managing all of the customer contracts in spreadsheets was becoming an increasingly large part of the monthly close - QuickBooks had no concept of ARR, so reporting was entirely in spreadsheets.
- **Deeper insights were lacking:** Revela was looking to measure more metrics such as department spend by category and vendor to further optimize the business, which was not available in QuickBooks.
- **Time-consuming reporting:** QuickBooks did not support their board or multi-entity financial reporting, which created siloed data across different segments of the company.
- **Scaling the company without scaling the team** : Everything was manual on QuickBooks, but the team was looking to 1) stay lean, 2) improve reporting while, 3) supporting tremendous growth.


# **The Solution**


Moving to more sophisticated accounting software was the only option for Revela to meet its objectives. They turned to Campfire for its accounting platform with end-to-end revenue automation for SaaS companies.


> “We went through a competitive evaluation and selected Campfire for our next stage of growth because it’s very scalable, handles complex 606 revenue accounting, and automates invoice reconciliation for high-volumes of transactions. Unifying our accounting and revenue reporting in a single place is a big win for our team.” Richard Atkins, CPA, Director of Accounting @ Revela


**Fully automated 606 revenue accounting**


Campfire feature: **** Revenue reporting and recognition


- Automated revenue accounting and reporting, such as ARR waterfalls, deferred revenue by customer and other KPIs removed the need for spreadsheets
- Rule-based revenue recognition schedules reduced time spend on repetitive tasks
- Revenue reporting by customer segment and product line for improved Board reporting and internal decision-making


> “The US GAAP compliant revenue recognition functionality has been one of my favorite features of Campfire.”Richard Atkins, CPA, Director of Accounting @ Revela


**Stronger foundation of accounting data has improved budgeting and planning**


Campfire feature: Granular forecasting dimensions


- Analyses like department-level vendor spend by category, identified opportunities to reduce and consolidate spend with ease


**Enhanced & more frequent reporting**


Campfire feature: **** Advanced SaaS reporting


- Customizable dashboard for key SaaS metrics (ARR, MRR, churn rate, ACV) and cohort analysis for customer behavior reduced time spent on reporting
- Real-time financial performance visualization & automated generation of board-ready financial statements allowed for more consistent reporting


**Scalable solution with a fast-growing business**


Campfire feature: Advanced feature access and reliable customer support


- Manual process automation reduced the need for additional headcount
- Software integrations, with partners like Ramp, allowed for end-to-end automation
- Quick turn around times for requested features supported rapid company growth


# Results


By implementing Campfire, Revela was able to transform its financial operations, shaving 3 days off their monthly close process while improving accuracy, and providing deeper insights into their financial performance.


The finance and accounting team can now focus on strategic initiatives such as improving profitability rather than manual data entry and reconciliation, allowing the team to play a greater role in the company’s efficient growth.
