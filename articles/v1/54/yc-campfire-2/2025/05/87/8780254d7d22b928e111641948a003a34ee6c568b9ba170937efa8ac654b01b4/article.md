ChatGPT is an incredible tool. Like any platform, it has its strengths and limitations—and knowing when to use it vs consider product-integrated AI is key to building an efficient, modern finance workflow.


There’s never been a better time to explore AI in finance. The tools are powerful, the use cases are expanding, and the upside is huge. But not all AI is created equal. Some tools are designed for flexibility and brainstorming. Others are purpose-built for structured workflows. Understanding the difference can help you get the most out of both.


# What AI Is Actually Good At Right Now


But before we get into when to use what, let’s take a moment to ground ourselves in what AI can actually do today. The hype is loud, but underneath it, the progress is real.


AI is already strong at handling structured, rule-based tasks—especially when there’s clear context. That includes:


- Coding transactions
- Surfacing anomalies
- Drafting word-based responses (e.g. policies, memos, commentary)
- Reviewing human work for consistency


Where it still struggles:


- Complex judgment or interpretation
- Tasks requiring deep business context and consistency
- Full autonomy in sensitive workflows (e.g. audit signoff, policy enforcement)


That’s where understanding how to work best with AI, and keeping a human in the loop is important—and where choosing the right type of AI matters just as much.


# ChatGPT vs. Product-Integrated AI: What’s the Difference?


### 🧠 ChatGPT, Claude, and similar tools are flexible, open-ended interfaces that excel at:


- One-off projects
- Brainstorming ideas
- Writing first drafts
- Quick, unstructured research


They shine when you need a fast starting point or a creative jumpstart. But they rely on you to provide context and ensure quality, making them less suited for high-volume, high-risk workflows.


### ⚙️ Product-Integrated AI (like Campfire) integrates AI directly into your financial systems. It understands your up to date data, structure, and policies—because it’s operating within them.


At Campfire, we operate as a modern general ledger for mid-market finance & accounting teams—embedding AI & automation directly into your workflows so you can close fast, and scale faster.

# Why It Matters


You wouldn’t reconcile cash in a text editor. And you wouldn’t run your board reporting process in a Slack thread.


ChatGPT can be a powerful partner in ideation, policy writing, and ad hoc analysis. But when the workflow repeats, the data matters, and collaboration or compliance is on the line—that’s where product-integrated AI like Campfire shines.


Campfire’s AI doesn’t just generate responses. It understands your business context, operates with audit trails, and helps your team scale without adding headcount.


# TL;DR


- Use ChatGPT for one-off tasks, brainstorming, and drafts.
- Use product-integrated AI like Campfire for workflows that are structured, sensitive, or repeatable.
- The best finance teams use both—strategically.


ChatGPT is a great place to start—whether you’re drafting a policy, exploring a new idea, or building a one-off analysis. But when you’re ready to execute those ideas at scale, tools like Campfire can help bring structure, context, and efficiency to your workflows.
