Finance teams deserve better than spreadsheets, late journal entries, and last-minute payroll surprises. That’s why we’re excited to announce our **partnership with Rippling** —bringing even more automation and precision to Campfire’s modern accounting platform.


The partnership includes a native integration between platforms, making it easier than ever for high-growth teams to centralize financial data, streamline payroll categorization, and accelerate close timelines with confidence. Check out Campfire on the **[Rippling App Store](https://www.rippling.com/app-shop/app/campfire) .**


# A Smarter Way to Manage Payroll Accounting


With Campfire x Rippling, you can now connect employee and compensation data directly to your accounting workflows.


# Here’s what you can expect:


**1. Automated Payroll Categorization**
No more manual spreadsheets. Campfire automatically tags payroll line items by department and expense type—down to the most granular detail. If a role changes or a misclassification slips through, Campfire flags and corrects it, ensuring clean, audit-ready reporting every time.


**2. Continuous Data Sync for Employee Info**
As your org evolves, so does your financial data. This integration keeps employee roles, departments, and payroll splits up-to-date—automatically provisioning or suspending accounts in Campfire as changes happen in Rippling.


**3. Seamless Single Sign-On (SSO)**
With Rippling SSO, employees can securely access Campfire without extra credentials or admin overhead. One login, full visibility.

With Campfire and Rippling, your financial operations are no longer playing catch-up. You’re ahead—automated, accurate, and audit-ready.
