# Background


Fooji powers some of the world’s most exciting brand experiences, including campaigns at the 2024 Paris Olympics. However, behind the scenes, Fooji’s finance team faced a mounting challenge: their accounting systems weren’t keeping up with their scaling business.


Fooji was previously operating on NetSuite but struggled with growing complexity around inventory, procurement, and campaign reporting. Their NetSuite setup, despite heavy investment, was no longer aligned with the business — slowing down close times and forcing manual workarounds to get basic reporting done.


With a lean team and a fast-growing business, Fooji needed a financial platform that could evolve with them.


# The Problem


# **Trapped in Complexity and Manual Work**


For Fooji’s finance team, the challenges were stacking up:


- **Slow Closes:** Closing the books took 15+ days.
- **Fragmented Systems:** Inventory, procurement, and financial reporting were disconnected.
- **Missed Insights:** Building product- and campaign-level P&Ls required painful, manual spreadsheet work.
- **NetSuite Limitations:** Even after significant consulting spend, their NetSuite instance couldn't deliver the custom reporting they needed.


"Our CEO made it clear: We needed true campaign profitability insights—and we couldn’t get there with the tools we had," said Heather Redka, Fooji’s Controller.


The team needed not just a better system, but a better way of working.


# The Solution


# **A Platform Built for Modern Finance Teams**


Fooji ran a rigorous procurement process and chose Campfire over retooling their existing legacy ERP. They moved away from an "all-in-one" approach and toward a “best-in-breed” finance stack, pairing Campfire with procurement automation and native Salesforce integrations for campaign management.


Campfire offered exactly what Fooji needed:


- **Unified Financial Data:** All procurement, inventory, and campaign revenue data flowed directly into Campfire.
- **Automated Close Management:** Reconciliations, revenue recognition, and eliminations were no longer manual tasks.
- **Native Salesforce Integration:** Campaign and contribution margin reporting became self-service.
- **Platform Scalability:** Campfire’s flexible architecture could evolve alongside Fooji’s scaling, dynamic operations.


Despite Fooji’s complex, multi-entity setup, the migration to Campfire was smooth—and impactful.


# The Result


# **Faster Closes, Greater Visibility, and Strategic Transformation**


Fooji’s move to Campfire unlocked immediate and dramatic improvements:


- Time to close dropped from 15 days to just **3 days** .
- Campaign P&L reporting became **self-serve, in real time.**
- Accounting team **productivity dramatically increased** —with no headcount expansion.
- Finance shifted from transactional work to **strategic initiatives** .


"Campfire didn’t just speed up our close—it changed the role of finance at Fooji," said Heather. "We’re now business partners, not just record keepers."


With Campfire’s automation, Fooji’s finance team spends less time reconciling transactions and more time helping leadership drive better decisions—like tightening vendor management, improving campaign profitability, and optimizing working capital.


# Summary


- Migrated off NetSuite to Campfire to better support growth and complexity
- Reduced monthly close from 15+ days to 3 days
- Empowered lean finance team without adding headcount
- Gained real-time contribution margin reporting across campaigns
- Increased strategic focus across finance, sales, and operations


Fooji’s story showcases how modern finance teams are evolving—and how Campfire is helping them lead that change.
