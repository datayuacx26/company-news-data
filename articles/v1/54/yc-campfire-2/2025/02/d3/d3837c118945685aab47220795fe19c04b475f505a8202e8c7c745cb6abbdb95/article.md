​ **From CPA to CFO** highlights the journeys of top tech CFOs who began their careers in accounting. These intimate, one-on-one conversations, offer insight into candid stories about career transitions, challenges, and lessons learned.


​Our inaugural episode features **Ron Wangerin, Chief Financial Officer at Trust & Will** , who brings over 30 years of financial leadership experience. Starting his career at Deloitte and rising to a Senior Manager principally in their aerospace and defense group, Ron transitioned to industry roles where he consistently drove exceptional growth. As CFO of Viasat (NASD: VSAT), he helped scale revenue from $180M to $1B+ and market cap from $150M to $2B+.


​His expertise spans public and private companies across technology, software, communications, medical technology, and consumer products. Ron will share his journey on how he translated his technical accounting expertise into strategic financial leadership, including thoughts on AI & automation, organizational transformation, M&A and more.
