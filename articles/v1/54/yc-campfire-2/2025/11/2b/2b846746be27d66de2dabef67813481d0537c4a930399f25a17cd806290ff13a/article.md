# Campfire and Anrok Partner to Simplify Sales Tax Automation for Modern Companies


Campfire


Team


November 13, 2025


[General](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=General)[Accounting](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=Accounting)[Finance](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=Finance)


### **Automate sales tax from transaction to filing**


Campfire has joined forces with **Anrok** , the modern sales tax automation platform built for SaaS and digital-first businesses.


Together, we’re removing one of the most manual parts of accounting, sales tax compliance, by connecting Anrok’s intelligent automation directly into Campfire’s core accounting platform.


The result: finance teams can manage compliance automatically, without leaving Campfire.


### **Fast setup, seamless sync**


Connecting Anrok to Campfire takes minutes - no code required. Once linked, transactions flow securely between systems, keeping both your financial data and compliance records in perfect alignment.


With the integration enabled, you can:


- **Connect Campfire and Anrok in minutes** - import transactions and monitor sales automatically.
- **See exposure instantly** - view where your business is liable with real-time, interactive mapping.
- **Register with confidence** - manage filings and new jurisdictions right from Anrok.
- **Calculate tax accurately** - Anrok applies the correct rates to every invoice created in Campfire.


### **Always current, always compliant**


Sales tax rules evolve constantly. Manual tracking or spreadsheets can’t keep up. By combining Campfire’s centralized financial data with Anrok’s automation, teams can stay compliant automatically - with less effort and zero guesswork.


This connection ensures every transaction is captured, categorized, and reported correctly - across all entities and locations.


### **Designed for the modern finance stack**


As finance teams adopt more specialized tools, integrations like Campfire + Anrok ensure data stays synchronized and processes stay efficient.


- **Controllers** get audit-ready data without the manual prep.
- **Finance leaders** gain visibility into compliance exposure in real time.
- **Accounting teams** eliminate repetitive, error-prone tasks.


It’s a smarter, more connected way to manage compliance - built for speed and accuracy, not busywork.


### **The future of accounting is connected**


Our partnership with Anrok is a step toward our vision of automating your financial operations end-to-end - from your general ledger to tax compliance.


Modern companies shouldn’t have to choose between control and simplicity. With Campfire and Anrok, you can have both.
