### **Challenge**


# **Consolidating Financial Operations During Hypergrowth**


When[Brian Lese](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brianlese/) joined TwelveLabs as Head of Finance in September 2024, he set out to build a lean, high-output finance function to manage operations across the US and Korea.


The problem? While Brian's team had a robust finance stack at its disposal (encompassing Ramp, Sequoia, Bamboo, and Stripe), it was being actively bottlenecked by their accounting system, QuickBooks. Not only did QuickBooks lack native integrations with the rest of TwelveLabs' stack, but it also couldn't facilitate multi-entity reporting, a considerable shortcoming for a company with divisions across the globe.


The lack of interoperability slowed the team considerably. Routine tasks, such as bank reconciliations, would take days, revenue recognition required hours of manual updates, and month-end closes often stretched into weeks. Every day brought more manual tasks that drained the team's already limited bandwidth.


What TwelveLabs needed was a solution that would unify their finance stack and streamline day-to-day operations rather than act as a perpetual blocker. So, Brian began evaluating various ERP systems. He also knew the team needed dedicated accounting leadership, so he brought on[Nikki Pasamic](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nipasamic/) as Senior Accounting Manager


When he discovered[Campfire](https://campfire.ai/) , an efficient team obsessed with their customers, he knew he'd found the perfect fit.


> *"We were growing fast, but our finance team was drowning in manual workflows. Without Campfire, we easily would have had to triple our headcount to stay afloat." — Nikki Pasamic, Senior Accounting Manager at TwelveLabs*

### **Solution**


# **An AI-First ERP That Scales Alongside Lean Teams**


Campfire delivered an AI-first ERP that transformed TwelveLabs' finance team into the value driver Brian had envisioned from day one.


Thanks to Campfire's vast library of 200+ native integrations and competitive APIs, the onboarding process was seamless. Within days, the platform was already connected across Ramp, Sequoia, Bamboo, HubSpot, and Stripe, which eliminated the fragmented and manual workflows that previously plagued the team. Campfire also facilitated multi-entity and multi-currency consolidation, enabling unified financial management across all geographies and subsidiaries.


Brian and Nikki watched as internal efficiencies began to compound. Bank reconciliations dropped from days to hours. Month-end close times were slashed by over 50%. Once cumbersome day-to-day operations, such as revenue recognition, commentary writing, and matching uncategorized transactions, were now being handled autonomously.


But what keeps both Brian and Nikki most engaged is Campfire's iteration speed. Whether it be a request for a new feature or a slight change to their UI/UX, their feedback never falls on deaf ears. "Product changes get rolled in seemingly the next week," Brian shares. "They show us new features on our weekly calls before we even ask."


> *"The migration to Campfire was the definition of seamless. Within days, we had integrated it across the entire stack and automated workflows. Earlier, categorizing transactions took an hour per line item but with Campfire we can clear the entire list in 10 minutes." — Brian Lese, Head of Finance at TwelveLabs*
