# Campfire Teams Up with CBIZ Team to Empower Next-Gen Finance Teams


Campfire


Team


October 20, 2025


[General](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=General)[Accounting](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=Accounting)[Finance](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=Finance)


We’re extremely excited to announce Campfire’s strategic partnership with CBIZ, one of the largest accounting and advisory firms in the U.S. This collaboration brings together Campfire’s next-generation core accounting platform with CBIZ’s deep expertise in technology, accounting, tax, advisory, benefits, and insurance - helping high-growth companies scale with clarity, speed, and confidence.


At its core, this partnership is about delivering both modern infrastructure and trusted guidance. Campfire is purpose-built for today’s finance leaders, who are expected to deliver board-ready insights with lean teams and compressed timelines. By centralizing financial data, automating workflows, and accelerating the monthly close, Campfire enables finance teams to move faster and unlock strategic insights. CBIZ, with its national reach, expansive client base, and decades of experience, is the ideal partner to deliver these solutions at scale.


> “CBIZ is a powerhouse in the accounting industry, and we couldn’t ask for a better partner to bring Campfire to more finance teams. Together, we’re bridging the gap between modern software and expert advisory to help companies scale smarter and faster.” — **John Glasgow, CEO & Founder at Campfire**


CBIZ, following its 2024 acquisition of Marcum LLP, has emerged as one of the largest accounting firms in the nation, with over 10,000 professionals across 160 offices. Known for pairing data-driven insights with tailored client service, CBIZ helps middle-market businesses unlock opportunities and achieve their financial goals.


> “Campfire represents the future of financial software. By pairing their AI-native ERP with CBIZ’s technology, advisory and accounting services, we can give clients the tools and insights they need to operate with speed, accuracy, and strategic foresight.” — **Ada Kong, Technology Transformation Practice Co-Lead at CBIZ**


Campfire is not just another ERP - it’s a new category of financial infrastructure, designed to replace legacy systems that no longer meet the demands of today’s businesses. From multi-entity consolidation to automated revenue recognition, Campfire equips finance teams with real-time visibility into cash flow, contracts, and performance.


With this partnership, companies will have access to a fully integrated solution: Campfire’s modern ERP as the system of record, alongside CBIZ’s trusted guidance as the system of advice. Together, the firms are helping businesses eliminate inefficiencies, improve compliance, and unlock the strategic insights that fuel growth.


We’ve already rolled out joint solutions to select clients, with broader availability expected later this year.
