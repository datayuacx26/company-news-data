AI is reshaping how teams work. Large language models (LLMs) have changed the way we communicate, analyze, and automate. But without access to a company’s detailed financial data, even the best models can’t deliver the accuracy finance teams require.


That’s why we built something new.


# **Meet LAM - Campfire’s Proprietary “Large Accounting Model”**


LAM (Large Accounting Model) is the first ERP-native foundational model trained entirely on accounting data.


It’s designed specifically for finance teams that need accuracy, reliability, and transparency - helping them close faster, gain real-time insight, and scale with confidence.


Unlike generic AI models, LAM learns directly from your ledger as it grows, adapting to your company’s unique structure while maintaining full attribution of its work. The result is consistent, explainable performance, where every prediction and classification can be traced back to its source.


Already, LAM is achieving over 95% task accuracy, giving teams the confidence to automate work that once demanded hours of manual review.


# **How it Works**


Accurate AI requires massive amounts of structured, granular data. Developed in the 90’s, legacy ERPs weren’t built to process information at this scale, forcing data to be summarized and stripping away the context AI relies on.


Campfire was built differently. Our modern architecture supports immense amounts of granular financial data - without summarization. That foundation allows LAM to learn from trillions of data points and deliver precise, context-aware automation.


#
**What LAM Can Do**


LAM enhances the accuracy and reliability of core accounting workflows within Campfire, including:


- Transaction matching and categorization across systems like billing, spend management, and payroll.
- Automated reconciliations that identify variances before they impact reporting.
- Audit-ready attribution for every action taken by the model, ensuring transparency and trust.


Operating entirely within Campfire’s SOC-certified environment, LAM keeps data secure and private without the need for third-party exposure or external processing.
