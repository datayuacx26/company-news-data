# Campfire and DOSS Partner to Connect Real-Time Operations with AI-Native Accounting


Campfire


Team


February 24, 2026


[Finance](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=Finance)[Accounting](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=Accounting)[General](https://campfire.ai/blog?category=General)


Campfire has partnered with DOSS, the AI-native operations platform for scaling consumer goods, food & beverage, and health & beauty businesses.


Together, we're closing the gap that's plagued inventory-based businesses for years: ops and finance running in separate systems, with month-end reconciliation as the only moment they meet.


The result: one source of truth that flows automatically from your warehouse to your general ledger.


# One system from operations to close


For over a decade, inventory-heavy businesses have been stuck with the same bad options — a legacy ERP that tries to do everything and does most of it poorly, or a patchwork of point solutions that never actually talk to each other.


DOSS and Campfire were both built from scratch to replace that. DOSS is the operational system of record: inventory, procurement, orders, warehouse. Campfire is the financial system of record: your GL, revenue recognition, prepaids, and close. Together, they give scaling businesses something they've never had before — a fully integrated, AI-native stack where operations and finance actually work as one.


# What the integration delivers


With DOSS and Campfire connected, every transaction on the ops side flows directly into your accounting layer — structured, validated, and ready to work with. No manual exports, no spreadsheet handoffs, no chasing numbers at month-end.


Joint customers can now:


- **Automate revenue from real COGS** — DOSS consolidates landed costs and live COGS across channels; Campfire uses that operational truth to drive accurate revenue, margin, and close.
- **Close faster with cleaner data** — Because data from ops arrives structured and reliable, finance teams spend close week closing — not cleaning up records.
- **Connect quote-to-cash to compliant reporting** — DOSS automates order-to-invoice and AR flows; Campfire turns those into automated journal entries, revenue schedules, and financial reporting.
- **Scale without adding headcount** — DOSS handles multi-channel, multi-entity operational complexity; Campfire centralizes the accounting controls as you grow.
- **Get answers with AI** — Ask questions and solve problems through Dossbot in DOSS, or Ember AI in Campfire. Both AI assistants work with your live data.


# Built on the same philosophy


Both companies share a foundational belief: modern business software should be built for automation from the ground up — not patched together from legacy systems or customized into something unrecognizable.


> "Real-world businesses have lived with a painful tradeoff: inventory and operations live in one world, finance lives in another, and month-end is where everyone fights about who is 'right.' With DOSS for operations and Campfire for finance, teams can have one source of truth that flows from the warehouse floor all the way to the general ledger." — Wiley Jones, CEO & Co-Founder of DOSS


> "With a live two-way sync between DOSS and Campfire, every inventory movement, order, and warehouse transaction flows directly into your general ledger in real time. Finance teams get faster closes, cleaner reporting, and less manual work — because the data from ops is already structured and reliable the moment it hits Campfire. Your GL, your prepaids, your close process — all of it stays current without anyone having to chase numbers." — John Glasgow, CEO & CFO at Campfire


# Designed for inventory-driven businesses


This partnership was built for companies that have outgrown their current stack — or are tired of fighting with it. If your ops and finance teams are working from different data, running separate reports, and reconciling the gap every month, this is for you.


- Operations teams get full visibility into inventory, orders, and fulfillment — with the confidence that every transaction is syncing to finance in real time.
- Finance teams get accurate, structured data flowing directly into the GL — so the close is faster and the numbers are right the first time.
- Leaders get one source of truth across the entire business.


# The future of accounting is connected


Our partnership with DOSS is part of a bigger vision: automating your financial operations end-to-end, from the first transaction to the final close.


Modern businesses shouldn't have to choose between operational speed and financial control. With Campfire and DOSS, you get both.
