- Holistic preventive practices include dietary modifications, Yoga, meditation, herbal supplements, and personalised wellness plans that are tailored to an individual's unique needs and constitution.


- This proactive approach encourages lifestyle changes and habits that support physical, mental, and emotional well-being.


Nirva's approach exemplifies the benefits of preventive care by integrating the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda and Yoga into modern health routines. By addressing not only symptoms but also the root causes of health imbalances, Nirva's treatment plans aim to create long-term harmony in the body.


- In contrast, conventional medicine often leans toward a reactive model—intervening after symptoms appear or when a condition becomes critical.

Holistic preventive care, as practised by Nirva, shifts the Focus toward long-term health maintenance.
