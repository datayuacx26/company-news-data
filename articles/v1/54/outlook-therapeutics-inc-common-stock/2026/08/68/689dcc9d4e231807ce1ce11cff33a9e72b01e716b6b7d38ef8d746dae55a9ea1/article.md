ISELIN, N.J., Aug. 06, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Outlook Therapeutics, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=W_Yj5S30bzpnaffqG6p7WxMFUwCc-WRm29CZ_YCtUwUzDSLEDwBaycE0t8XSLb-FG2GZQBNlekyO_qbLrp_4pmWJyI0B60D_HoBCnt2AInZb7XT_HKW9b7BQbM4ciDMH) (Nasdaq: OTLK), a biopharmaceutical company focused on the development and commercialization of LYTENAVA™ (bevacizumab-vikg, bevacizumab gamma) for the treatment of retinal diseases, today announced that it will report its financial results for the third quarter fiscal year 2026 and will host a corporate update conference call and webcast on Friday, August 14, 2026, at 8:30 AM ET.

**About Outlook Therapeutics, Inc.**


Outlook Therapeutics is a biopharmaceutical company focused on the development and commercialization of LYTENAVA™ (bevacizumab-vikg (U.S.), bevacizumab gamma (E.U.)). LYTENAVA™ is the first ophthalmic formulation of bevacizumab to receive U.S. FDA approval and European Commission and MHRA Marketing Authorization for the treatment of wet AMD. Outlook Therapeutics commenced commercial launch of LYTENAVA™ (bevacizumab gamma) in Germany, Austria, and the UK as a treatment for wet AMD.
