## Commercial hand soap vs. sanitizer: Which is better?


Maintaining a clean and safe environment for your employees and guests starts with a robust commercial hand hygiene program. For any business prioritizing workplace hygiene, the question often arises: Should I invest primarily in commercial[hand soap or hand sanitizer](https://unifirst.com/facility-services/soaps-sanitizers-skin-care/) ? Each option plays a vital role in your overall facility service program, providing complementary benefits that help prevent the spread of germs and maintain high standards of business hygiene supplies. Relying on just one of these solutions leaves a noticeable gap in your building’s health standards. A comprehensive approach requires understanding the unique benefits of both options. By integrating soap and sanitizer effectively, you help ensure good hygiene for your staff and visitors throughout their day.


##### The role of commercial hand soap


Washing your hands with commercial hand soap and water is the most effective way to remove all types of germs and chemicals. While hand sanitizer is a convenient backup, it does not eliminate all types of pathogens and is less effective when hands are visibly dirty or greasy, according to the Center for Disease Control. Commercial hand soap works by breaking down the oils on our skin that trap dirt, grime, and germs. When combined with the motion of scrubbing and the flow of clean water, soap physically removes these unwanted particles from the hands and washes them down the drain.


Soap is absolutely necessary in specific environments. Restrooms, kitchens, and employee breakrooms require reliable soap dispensers to handle visible soil and food preparation needs. Providing high-quality soap and dispensers demonstrates your commitment to the fundamental health and comfort of everyone in your building, while supporting a culture of effective workplace hygiene.


##### The power of hand sanitizer


While commercial hand soap is highly effective, people do not always have immediate access to a sink. This is where commercial hand sanitizer proves its value for good hygiene in the workplace. Alcohol-based sanitizers are formulated to kill germs directly on contact without the need for water or towels, making them an excellent supplement to hand cleaning routines.


Sanitizers provide a fast, convenient way for people to disinfect their hands as they move through your building.[Touchless dispensers](https://unifirst.com/products/automatic-soap-sanitizer-dispenser/) , a staple of UniFirst hand hygiene solutions, are perfect for high-traffic areas where cross-contamination is common. Providing sanitizer through strategic dispenser placement shows that you proactively care about the continuous safety of your guests, even outside the restroom.


## Choosing the right dispensing systems


Having the right products is only half the battle. Your business also needs dependable[dispensing systems](https://unifirst.com/facility-services/commercial-restroom-washroom-supplies/) to deliver them. Manual pump bottles can quickly become messy and run out of product when you need it most.


Commercial-grade dispensers, especially touchless automatic models, offer a superior experience. They provide a controlled, measured dose of soap or sanitizer, reducing waste and cutting down on operational costs. Touchless systems also prevent the dispenser itself from becoming a shared touchpoint, further limiting the spread of germs.


## Strategic placement for maximum protection


To get the most out of your products, you must decide where to place them in your industrial hygiene plan. Consider places where they will be used most frequently.


Hand soap dispensers belong near every sink in your facility. Ensure they are mounted securely and kept full. Hand sanitizer stations require a more strategic approach. You should locate sanitizers at main entrances, near elevator banks, outside of conference rooms, and at employee workstations. By placing these dispensers in the natural flow of foot traffic, you encourage regular use and build a culture of cleanliness.
