# 4 high-visibility uniform essentials for safe construction work

When it comes to[high-visibility work clothes](https://unifirst.com/uniforms-workwear/high-visibility/?filter&filter_cat=92&sort_by=featured&product_cat=92) , you might find yourself pondering many options: What specific workwear actually complies with industry regulations? Would a standard reflective vest be enough, or should we look for a specific design? These are vital questions for any business owner who prioritizes the well-being of their crew. This article covers the four high-visibility uniform essentials that combine industry-leading safety standards with the comfort your workers deserve.


## Table of Contents


It’s important to understand the foundational philosophy of hi-visibility clothing. The American National Standards Institute (ANSI) and the International Safety Equipment Association (ISEA) developed the ANSI/ISEA 107 standard, which outlines the requirements for high-visibility safety apparel. This standard ensures the garments meet specific visibility guidelines, including the use of fluorescent materials and reflective stripes to enhance the wearer’s visibility in different lighting conditions.


High-visibility garments that meet the ANSI/ISEA 107 standards are rigorously tested for visibility and durability, providing an added assurance of safety for workers in hazardous environments.


The visibility of a worker in a demanding construction site is a non-negotiable factor in helping to prevent accidents. Over the years, studies have reaffirmed that hi-vis clothing significantly reduces the risk of workplace injuries. One such study concluded that the odds of a vehicle accident decreased by almost 25% when workers at roadside construction zones wore hi-vis clothing.


Every element of hi-vis workwear—from reflective[high-vis shirts](https://unifirst.com/uniforms-workwear/high-visibility/?filter&filter_cat=92&sort_by=featured&product_cat=92&style=3857%2C3855) and[high-visibility hoodies](https://unifirst.com/uniforms-workwear/high-visibility/?filter&filter_cat=92&sort_by=featured&product_cat=92&style=3856) to year-round essentials like high-vis vests, hi-vis jackets, high visibility coveralls, and hard hats—play a crucial part in the overarching strategy for workplace safety.


Let’s look at the five hi-vis uniform essentials every construction worker should have:


## High visibility work shirts – first line of protection


A[hi-visibility shirt](https://unifirst.com/uniforms-workwear/high-visibility/?filter&filter_cat=92&sort_by=featured&product_cat=92&style=3855%2C3857) is likely to be the first layer of high-vis protection for most construction workers. These light, breathable garments are often worn over regular clothing, providing a convenient and highly visible layer without impeding movement or comfort.


When choosing a high-visibility shirt, consider the sleeve length, which is often determined by your workplace environment and the season. A hi-vis long sleeve shirt provides more coverage against the elements and abrasions, while short-sleeved options might be preferred where heat and arm mobility come into play. Visibility-wise, make sure the ANSI/ISEA 107 standard is met, as this ensures a certain level of retroreflective material that bounces light back to its source.

## High visibility outerwear and hard hats – year-round safety centric solutions


High-visibility jackets, vests, coveralls, and hard hats offer additional protection throughout the year. When picking a high-visibility jacket, consider the insulation level and waterproofing. You want a jacket that offers protection without reducing your maneuverability or comfort.


For those on the site who need more extensive and consistent coverage, high-visibility coveralls offer an all-in-one solution.


Equally critical in safety gear are high-visibility hard hats, offering dual benefits of head protection and high visibility. Choosing a hard hat that meets both ANSI head protection standards and visibility requirements is essential for ensuring the utmost safety.
