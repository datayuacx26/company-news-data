# L3Harris Receives $170 Million Radio Order From Central European Nation

**ROCHESTER, N.Y., June 9, 2026** — L3Harris Technologies (NYSE: LHX) has received a $170 million order from a Central European nation for its[Falcon® IV](https://www.l3harris.com/all-capabilities/falcon-iv-family-radios) family of communication systems. The technology will support ongoing force modernization initiatives and will enhance secure, seamless interoperability with U.S. and NATO partners.


The new systems will provide next‑generation voice and high-speed data communications, which L3Harris designed to strengthen the command‑and‑control architecture. The Falcon IV radios will enhance mission effectiveness and coalition coordination through improved situational awareness, connectivity and performance in contested environments.


“Central Europe is currently undergoing a major modernization of its capabilities, and L3Harris radios are a force multiplier in that transformation,” said Chris Aebli, President, Mission Critical Communications, Communications & Spectrum Dominance, L3Harris. “Our Falcon IV systems provide Europe with assured communications to remain connected regardless of their operating environment.”


L3Harris is supporting NATO modernization with programs underway in[Germany](https://www.l3harris.com/newsroom/press-release/2025/05/l3harris-receives-214-million-orders-support-german-armed-forces) , the[Netherlands](https://www.l3harris.com/newsroom/press-release/2025/03/l3harris-dutch-ministry-defence-sign-foxtrot-long-term-agreement) and other allied nations. The adoption of Falcon IV radios ensures full interoperability with these efforts, strengthening network cohesion, enhancing coalition readiness and supporting long‑term mission success.
