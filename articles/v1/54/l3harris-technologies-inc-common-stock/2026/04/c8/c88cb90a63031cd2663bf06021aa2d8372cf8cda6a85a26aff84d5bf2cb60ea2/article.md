# L3Harris Announces Quarterly Dividend

**MELBOURNE, Fla., April 23, 2026** — The Board of Directors of L3Harris Technologies (NYSE: LHX) has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $1.25 per common share, payable June 26, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on June 5, 2026.
