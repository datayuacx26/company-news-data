# L3Harris Sets Date for Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Release

**MELBOURNE, Fla., July 6, 2026** — L3Harris Technologies (NYSE: LHX) will release its second quarter 2026 financial results after the market closes on Wednesday, July 29, 2026.


The company will then host an earnings call on Wednesday, July 29, 2026, at 5 p.m. ET. Participants are encouraged to listen via webcast at[L3Harris.com](https://www.l3harris.com/events/shareholder/second-quarter-calendar-2026-earnings-call) . A replay of the call will also be available on[L3Harris.com](https://www.l3harris.com/events/shareholder/second-quarter-calendar-2026-earnings-call) following the event.
