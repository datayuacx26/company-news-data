# L3Harris Demonstrates Multi-Domain Supremacy at Annual Project Convergence Exercise

**MELBOURNE, Fla., Aug. 18, 2026** — L3Harris Technologies (NYSE: LHX) recently showcased a variety of interoperable, multi-domain capabilities at the Army’s Project Convergence-Capstone 6 at Fort Irwin, California.


The company successfully linked decision makers with multi-domain sensors and effectors in unified networks for rapid interdiction of threats in complex and contested environments. L3Harris’ key capabilities included[interoperable, multi-domain assured communications](https://www.l3harris.com/all-capabilities/falcon-iv-family-radios) ,[Link 16](https://www.l3harris.com/all-capabilities/link-16-tactical-data-links) integration onto UH-60 Black Hawks, and the[Deceptor™](https://www.l3harris.com/all-capabilities/deceptor) modular electronic warfare payload.


“Our wide range of open system defense technologies have modern, high-volume manufacturing and resilient supply chains to support their rapid delivery into the hands of soldiers,” said Christopher Aebli, President, Communications & Spectrum Dominance, L3Harris. “Many of the capabilities we demonstrated at Project Convergence show our technology is ready to meet the demands of tomorrow, providing additional value to the U.S. government’s investments into software-defined and open-systems solutions.”


The company also teamed with ARX Robotics to provide an integrated, counter-unmanned aerial system capability leveraging L3Harris’[CORVUS-RAVEN](https://www.l3harris.com/all-capabilities/corvus-raven-counter-drone-capability) .


L3Harris is making continued progress in support of new Army requirements after its selection[as a Next Generation Command and Control transport layer provider](https://www.l3harris.com/newsroom/press-release/2025/10/us-army-selects-l3harris-support-ngc2-program) . The company also recently showcased its capabilities at Ivy Mass, Lightning Surge and Operation Jailbreak, where its[Wraith®](https://www.l3harris.com/newsroom/editorial/2022/07/wraith-waveform-provides-truly-resilient-communications-tactical-edge) waveform and[WESCAM® MX®-Series](https://www.l3harris.com/all-capabilities/wescam-mx-series) of electro-optical/infrared sensor systems were validated as “jailbroken.”
