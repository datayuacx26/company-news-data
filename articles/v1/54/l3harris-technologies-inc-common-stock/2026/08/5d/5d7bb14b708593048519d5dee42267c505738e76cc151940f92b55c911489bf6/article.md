# L3Harris Completes Sale of Majority Stake in Commercial Space Propulsion, Power and Electronics Businesses to AE Industrial Partners

**MELBOURNE, Fla., Aug. 4, 2026** — L3Harris Technologies (NYSE: LHX) has completed the sale of a majority stake in its commercial space propulsion, power and electronics businesses to AE Industrial Partners at a total enterprise value of $845 million.


The company expects to use the proceeds to invest in facilities and advanced technologies to meet unprecedented demands for growth.


L3Harris’ RS-25 rocket engine operations were excluded from the sale, and the company will maintain a ~40% ownership stake in AE Industrial Partners’ new space technologies business, which will be named Rocketdyne.
