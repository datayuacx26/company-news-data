# L3Harris to Present at Upcoming Investor Conference

**MELBOURNE, Fla., May 13, 2026** — L3Harris Technologies (NYSE: LHX) Chairman and CEO Christopher Kubasik and Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer Ken Sharp will present at the Bernstein 42nd Annual Strategic Decisions Conference at 2:30 p.m. ET on Wednesday, May 27, in New York City. The presentation will stream live on[L3Harris.com](http://www.l3harris.com/events/shareholder/bernstein-42nd-annual-strategic-decisions-conference) , with a recording available following the event.
