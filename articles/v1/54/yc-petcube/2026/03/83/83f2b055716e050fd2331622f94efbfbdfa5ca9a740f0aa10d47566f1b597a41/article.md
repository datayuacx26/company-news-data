# Petcube Launches All-in-One Intelligent Pet Care App

*Meet the updated Petcube App—your all-in-one pet-first platform that brings personalized AI guidance, 24/7 certified vet chat, and your complete pet health management into one unified experience.*


For years, Petcube helped pet parents stay close with cameras, trackers, and smart devices. Today we’re taking the next step: helping you care smarter—with or without hardware.


**The updated Petcube App brings your pet’s world into one place:**


- A redesigned pet-centered architecture
- A personalized AI assistant
- Unlimited 24/7 chats with certified vets
- Improved tools for managing pet health, activity, and routines
- Seamless integration with Petcube devices for deeper insights


*“Pet parents are doing the most—but they’re still managing care with a patchwork of notes, screenshots, PDFs, and late-night searches,”* **said Anastasia Kukhar, CEO of Petcube.** *“With the Petcube App, we’re introducing a new way of being a pet parent—powered by intelligence instead of guesswork—by building one reliable source of truth for every pet.”*


## What you do in the app


**Start with your pet**


- Create a profile for each pet (age, breed, habits, photos, and health background). Run a guided Health Check to set a baseline and get a clear summary of what looks good and what to watch.


**Build a real record**


- Store medical records, meds, treatments, vet visits, and a Pet Medical ID for emergencies—with build-in reminders and expense tracking.


**Track everyday life**


- Log feedings, walks, meds, activity, and key moments in your pet journal—so patterns are easier to spot over time.
- If you have Petcube devices, connect them for live streaming, smart alerts, emergency monitoring, and activity insights.


**Get answers that actually know your pet**


- Ask the pet-focused AI assistant about health, behavior, food, and training. It uses your pet’s profile, Health Check, and history to give tailored guidance—not generic tips.


**Talk to a specialist anytime**


- Chat with certified vets 24/7, with the ability to share a specific pet’s profile, AI-chat history, and photos/videos for faster, clearer decisions.


The Petcube App is available now on[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/petcube/id720151500) and[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.petcube.android) . No hardware required—just smarter pet care, with optional device integration for deeper insights. Personalized AI guidance and unlimited 24/7 vet access start at $9.99 per month or $99.99 per year, including a free two-week trial.


Better pet care isn’t about more tabs, more tools, or more guessing—it’s about having the right context in one place. Download the Petcube App to build your pet’s living record, get personalized guidance, and tap a vet anytime—for better decisions for them, and more peace of mind for you.
