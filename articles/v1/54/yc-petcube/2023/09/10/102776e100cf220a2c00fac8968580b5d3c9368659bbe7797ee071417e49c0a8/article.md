# Exciting New Features for Petcube Care Subscribers

*We're delighted to introduce several exciting improvements to our[Petcube Care](https://petcube.com/care/) service, designed to elevate your experience. Our latest update brings new features that will enhance your connection with pets.*


- **Better Recognition:** We've made significant improvements to our recognition capabilities, resulting in a sharper ability to identify people and animals in your recorded videos. It means that with the improvements, you'll receive more precise notifications when your pets and loved ones are within the camera's field of view.
- **Reduced False Notifications:** Many of you had concerns about inconsistent motion alerts, and we've taken steps to minimize these occurrences. Your experience should now be smoother and more reliable.


We encourage everyone to enable[Petcube Care](https://petcube.com/care/) notifications and try the update feature. We remain dedicated to your human-pet moments and will continue to make your Petcube experience more enjoyable.
