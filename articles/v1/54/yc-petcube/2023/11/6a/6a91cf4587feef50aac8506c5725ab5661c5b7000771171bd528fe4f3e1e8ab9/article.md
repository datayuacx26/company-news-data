# All-New Care Settings & New User Onboarding Released

*With great excitement, we introduce a set of significant updates designed to elevate your Petcube experience.*


Petcube has diligently prepared a new user onboarding process, ensuring that users receive a comprehensive introduction to the core features and valuable insights immediately following the device setup.


In addition to this, our[Petcube Care](https://petcube.com/care/) subscribers will find a range of refined options available within their Care settings. These new features provide the ability to enable motion-based video recording for pets while also allowing for the deactivation of notifications about this specific video and detection method. To observe these settings in action, we invite you to enable Care video recording within your device settings.


Furthermore, our newly updated Care settings and introducing a Care free 2-week trial are seamlessly integrated into the setup flow. We have streamlined the user experience for your convenience and benefit.


If you have any questions about these updates or any feedback regarding the Petcube app or your Petcube camera, please don't hesitate to reach out to us atsupport@petcube.com . We're excited to hear from you and look forward to enhancing your Petcube experience.
