# Petcube Partners with FluentPet to Let Your Pet Talk with Real-Time Video

*Bonding with dogs and cats has reached a whole new level. With this new integration, pet parents can receive real-time video clips from[Petcube](https://petcube.com/) cameras every time their pets press a[FluentPet](https://fluent.pet/) Connect button—capturing every ‘talking’ moment right on their smartphones.*


Petcube’s mission has always been to strengthen the bond between pets and their families, even from miles away. FluentPet, backed by cognitive science, has transformed pet communication with its recordable sound buttons, used in over 100,000 homes and with more than 1 million units sold. Together, this integration adds richer context to every word, bringing pets’ voices to life—to be truly seen, heard, and understood.


> “By pairing FluentPet’s buttons with Petcube’s real-time video, we’re giving pet parents more than a notification of what button was pressed,” shared *Anastasia Kukhar, CEO of Petcube* . “It’s not just what pets say, but how they say it—showing emotions, body language, and intent. With Petcube’s two-way audio, pet parents can respond instantly, creating a dialogue that builds trust and strengthens connection.”


Starting today, the Petcube and FluentPet integration empowers pets to express themselves with clarity, eliminating the guesswork. Paired with real-time video, every button press creates a memory stored for up to 120 days in the FluentPet App, helping pet parents deepen their understanding and feel closer to their pets, no matter the distance.


Revisit those “talking” moments and start building a shared language with your pets today—get your FluentPet button bundles with Petcube cameras at[flnt.pt/fluentpetcube](http://flnt.pt/fluentpetcube) .
