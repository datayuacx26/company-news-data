# Petcube Bites 2 Lite and Play 2 Cameras are Now at Target

*Staying connected to your pets just got easier. You can now find our interactive cameras in over 1,900 Target stores across the US.*


Petcube is dedicated to giving pet parents more ways to keep their pets safe and comfortable, even when they're not home. From today, available to Target customers, Petcube elevates the pet care routine by offering real-time monitoring and interaction with pets, and now you can buy it on the spot—in the nearest[Target store](https://www.target.com/store-locator/find-stores) .


**Daria Prylipko, Sales Director at Petcube** , shared her excitement about the company’s launch on Target:


> “With Target reaching nearly 8 out of 10 U.S. shoppers and about 66% of American households having pets, this partnership allows us to connect with so many pet parents and their pets across the country. We're excited to bring smart pet tech to one of the world’s top retailers, making advanced pet care simpler and more accessible. Now, at Target, pet parents can easily find everything they need—from food and toys to the latest pet tech—all in one place.”


At all 1900+ Target locations across the U.S., you can find two Petcube devices:[Petcube Play 2](https://petcube.com/play-2/) and[Petcube Bites 2 Lite](https://petcube.com/bites-2-lite/) . Both are available for purchase in-store or online at[Target.com](https://www.target.com/) , with free 2-day shipping on orders over $35 for Target Circle 360™ members or when using the Target Circle card.


Additionally, 1,548 store locations are stocked with real products on display—for you to get a closer look before bringing one home. With Petcube products built to last and backed by Target’s quality guarantee, you can be confident in their durability and optimal performance.


Petcube has ambitious yet clear goals: to lead the pet tech category at Target and be there for every pet parent. We’re committed to making pet care smart, simple, and more accessible to everyone. Bringing Petcube to Target is another step toward turning that vision into reality.


Find[Petcube](https://petcube.com/) at your nearest Target today and stay connected to what matters most.
