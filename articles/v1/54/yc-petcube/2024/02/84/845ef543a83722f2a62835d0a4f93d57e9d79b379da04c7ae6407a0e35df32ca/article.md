# Petcube Partners with Stupid Pet Tricks on TBS for Pet Talent Showcase

*Grab your furry friends and tune in, as TBS'[Stupid Pet Tricks](https://www.tbs.com/mystupidpettrick) officially partners up with Petcube. Hosted by the comedian Sarah Silverman, the show premieres on Sunday, February 11 at 5:00 pm ET/ 2:00 pm PT across TBS, Discovery, truTV, and Animal Planet.*


A modern take on the iconic David Letterman show, Stupid Pet Tricks promises a half-hour of laughter, heartwarming moments, and a star-studded lineup featuring Jon Hamm, Will Ferrell, and more. Viewers at home can join the fun by posting their pets’ tricks on social media using #mystupidpettrick.


> Turner Networks President Jason Sarlanis welcomes the show to TBS' comedy lineup, noting, "Only Sarah Silverman’s comedic mastery can do justice to David Letterman’s celebrated segment."


TBS is also taking the Stupid Pet Tricks tour to cities: Tampa, Dallas, Austin, Longmont, and Brevard. This multi-city tour offers pet parents the chance to showcase their pets on the pink carpet and enjoy pet-friendly photo moments.


Weekly winners from the audience video submissions, selected by judges, will be featured on air and receive the prize —[Petcube Bites 2 Lite](https://petcube.com/bites-2-lite/) camera. Here's how to participate online:


- Capture your pet's trick on camera
- Share the video on Instagram or TikTok
- Tag @TBSNetwork and use #mystupidpettrick


If selected, TBS’ team will contact you to arrange getting your video on air and reward you with the Bites 2 Lite.


> “I can’t think of a better fit for a creative partnership, — says Andrey Klen, CMO at Petcube, — Pet parents use Petcube cameras all the time to train animals remotely, capturing cute funny moments in the process. With the current state of the world we could use this wholesome content and take a little comfort in our beloved pets”.


Submit your videos and mark your calendars for the show's premiere. Seize the chance for you and your pet to stand out, virtually or on the pink carpet. We can’t wait to see your pets’ performances.
