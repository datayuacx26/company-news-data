# Petcube Puts Water Fountains in NYC's Cat Café

*From now on, the Petcube Fountain will be part of[Meow Parlour](https://www.meowparlour.com/) —the first cat café and nonprofit rescue in New York City. Built into the café’s daily care, it helps keep adoptable cats hydrated, healthy, and comfortable as they wait to meet their future families.*


This partnership brings Petcube into cat- and dog-friendly cafés—community spaces where people and animals connect, and many pets take their first steps toward adoption. Starting with Meow Parlour, we bring our mission of smart, connected care to cats still waiting for families of their own.


The partnership launched with an event celebrating[Shop Cats of New York](https://nypost.com/2025/09/09/lifestyle/shops-cats-of-new-york-book-spotlights-the-citys-best-kitties/) , Tamar Arslanian’s newly revised and expanded book, featured in The New York Times, USA Today, and the New York Post.


**Meet Petcube Fountain**


[Petcube Fountain](https://petcube.com/fountain/) is our first smart hydration solution that prioritizes pets’ urinary and kidney wellness. Beyond delivering fresh, clean, and safe water, the fountain is designed to entice pets to drink more, keeping them healthy and hydrated.


**Key features of Petcube Fountain:**


- UV-C antibacterial light and 4-layer filtration system (filters last up to 30 days)
- Dishwasher-safe, food-grade tank, 7+ days of water supply
- Whisper-quiet cordless magnetic pump, motion-sensor adjustable water flow
- Rechargeable 5200 mAh battery for up to 3 weeks of wireless use
