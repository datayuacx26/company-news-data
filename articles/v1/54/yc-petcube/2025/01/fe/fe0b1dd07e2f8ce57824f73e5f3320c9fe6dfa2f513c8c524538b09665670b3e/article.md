# Petcube Launches Water Fountain

*Introducing our first hydration solution that prioritizes pets’ urinary and kidney wellness. Beyond delivering fresh, clean, and safe water, the fountain is designed to entice pets to drink more, keeping them healthy and hydrated.*


Built for cats and small dogs, the fountain combines UV-C antibacterial light and a 4-layer filtration system to deliver nearly 100% water purification. Its wireless magnetic pump mimics a natural water stream, adding to the water quality while simplifying cleaning and maintenance.


Crafted from food-grade, BPA-free materials, the fountain offers a ceramic basin for allergy-prone pets and sensitive chins or stainless steel for durability and easy upkeep—helping protect against bacteria, cat acne, and infections.


Starting today, the[Water Fountain](https://petcube.com/fountain/) is available for pre-order at $89.99 with a crafted ceramic basin and $69.99 with a stainless steel bowl on petcube.com, with orders shipping from March 1, 2025.


> “With Water Fountain, we’re moving beyond monitoring to actively improving pets’ health, starting with hydration—the foundation of well-being,” said **Anastasia Kukhar, CEO of Petcube** . “Our new fountain ensures water is not just clean—free from particles, chlorine, and pollutants—but it is also specifically designed to entice pets to drink more. Dehydration negatively impacts 10 million cats annually and we are strongly committed to bring that number down.”


**Some key product features:**


- UV-C antibacterial light and 4-layer filtration system (filters lasting up to 30 days)
- 1.7L dishwasher-safe, food-grade plastic tank for 7+ days of water storage
- Whisper-quiet magnetic pump (<30 dB) with motion sensor for adjustable water flow
- Rechargeable 2600 mAh battery for up to 20 days of wireless use
- Chew-proof cable, rubber feet for safety, and ambient LED light for status notifications
- Petcube App integration to track filter change cycles


Mitigating risk of urinary and kidney diseases isn’t just about clean water—it’s about making pets want to drink more, and Petcube’s Water Fountain does exactly that. Pre-order yours on[petcube.com](https://petcube.com/fountain/) to upgrade hydration for your pets—and peace of mind for you.
