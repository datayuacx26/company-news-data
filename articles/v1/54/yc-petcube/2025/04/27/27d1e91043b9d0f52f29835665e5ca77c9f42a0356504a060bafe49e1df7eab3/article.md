# Petcube Invites NYC’s Pet Parents to Craft Ceramic Pet Fountains

*To celebrate the launch of our first hydration product, we teamed up with Doclay NYC to host a ceramic basin painting workshop. At the Petcube Fountain Ceramics Studio, New York pet parents can design their own ceramic basin—adding a personal, creative touch to the tech that’s built to keep pets healthy and hydrated.*


> “Petcubers are truly guiding the product development here, we are merely helping out with some engineering parts. Fountain was on top of the wish list, and now it’s fresh out of the assembly line,” shared **Andrey Klen, Co-Founder at Petcube** . “The only logical thing now is to play with it some more by inviting Petcubers to add the final touches. It’s a tribute to our community and a perfect way to keep creating together.”


Petcube Fountain Ceramics Studio will be hosted at Doclay NYC, a pottery studio known for its mindful creation philosophy. Pet parents are invited to join open-door sessions during April 24-26. Attendees can select the time slot that works best for them and RSVP[here](https://forms.gle/ZsTSuNhpxJ6bRCGi6) .


> “This collaboration is a wonderful fit for both Doclay and Petcube, reflecting our shared values of care, creativity, and connection. At Doclay, we believe in crafting meaningful art that brings people closer to what they love. Petcube does the same for pets and pet parents. Together, we’re bringing about a space for creativity and thoughtful design that strengthen the bond between pets and their families,” said **Soo Lee, Founder of Doclay NYC** .


[Petcube Water Fountain](https://petcube.com/fountain/) was officially launched at CES 2025 in Las Vegas this January and is available for pre-order at $89.99 with a crafted ceramic basin and $69.99 with a stainless steel bowl on[petcube.com](https://petcube.com/fountain/) , with orders shipping in April 2025.


### Key features of Petcube Water Fountain:


- UV-C antibacterial light and 4-layer filtration system (filters last up to 30 days)
- Dishwasher-safe, food-grade tank, 7+ days of water supply
- Whisper-quiet cordless magnetic pump, motion-sensor adjustable water flow
- Rechargeable battery for up to 3 weeks of wireless use


Create your one-of-a-kind fountain at the Petcube Fountain Ceramics Studio or order now at[petcube.com](https://petcube.com/fountain/) —and provide your pet with better hydration, by design.
