Alpine Adventures Await: Vail Resorts Unveils Summer 2026 Season Dates in Colorado and Utah


Epic Pass Holders Unlock Summer Perks Including Scenic Lift Access and 20% Savings on Dining, Lodging, Bike Rentals and Golf

Apr. 28, 2026 – Keystone, Colo. – With the 2025/26 ski season officially in the rearview, Vail Resorts’ Colorado and Utah mountains are trading snow-covered slopes for sun-soaked adventure and summer energy. As the seasons change, resorts are gearing up to welcome guests back for a vibrant summer lineup of hiking, mountain biking, scenic lift rides, and elevated alpine experiences designed to keep the energy high long after the snow melts.


Summer excitement begins in early May, led by Keystone Resort, which kicks off the season with golf opening May 8 at The River Course. Additional resorts will follow soon after, launching a lineup of experiences featuring breathtaking alpine views, signature events, and family-first activities.


2026/27[Epic Pass](https://www.epicpass.com/) Holders can take advantage of exclusive summer[benefits](https://www.epicpass.com/benefits/summer.aspx) , including unlimited scenic lift and gondola rides with select Passes, and 20% savings on dining, lodging, bike rentals, and golf at Keystone and Park City. For guests who haven't yet locked in on their Pass, eligible skiers and riders who visited any of the company’s 37 North American resorts with a lift ticket during the 2025/26 season can save up to $175 on the price of select Epic Pass products. With the new pricing, that means teens and young travelers (ages 13–30) could lock in an Epic Pass for as low as $694 or an Epic Local Pass for just $474.


More details will be announced as summer opening dates approach.


**RESORT SUMMER DATES**


[Beaver Creek Resort](https://www.beavercreek.com/explore/experience-beaver-creek-in-summer.aspx)


- June 19 – Sept. 27: Open Daily through Sept. 7, then Saturday-Sunday only through Sept. 27
- Golf opens at Beaver Creek Golf Club on May 16, and at Red Sky Ranch on May 18


[Breckenridge Ski Resort](https://www.breckenridge.com/explore-the-resort/activities-and-events/summer-activities.aspx)


- June 12 – September 7: Open Daily


[Crested Butte Mountain Resort](https://www.skicb.com/explore-the-resort/activities-and-events/summer-activities.aspx)


- June 13 – Sept. 7: Open Daily through Sept. 7, then Saturdays & Sundays through Sept. 27


[Keystone Resort](https://www.keystoneresort.com/explore/experience-keystone-summer.aspx)


- June 25 – Sept. 20: Open Thursday – Monday through Labor Day Weekend, then Friday - Sunday through Sept. 20
- Golf opens at The River Course on May 8, and at Keystone Ranch on May 15


[Vail Mountain](https://www.vail.com/explore/experience-vail-summer.aspx)


- June 12 – Sept. 27: Open Daily through Sept. 7, then Friday-Sunday only through Sept. 27
- Gondola One will open on June 12 and Eagle Bahn Gondola will open on June 19


[Park City Mountain Resort](https://www.parkcitymountain.com/explore/experience-parkcity-summer.aspx)


- June 6 – Sept. 27: Open daily through Sept. 7, then Thursday-Sunday only through Sept. 27
- Crescent Lift (bike haul), Town Lift and Red Pine Gondola will open June 13
- Golf Opens at Canyons Golf Course on May 16


Certain activities and trails may open at later dates depending on conditions. For more details, be sure to follow resort social media channels and visit resort websites closer to the start of summer.
