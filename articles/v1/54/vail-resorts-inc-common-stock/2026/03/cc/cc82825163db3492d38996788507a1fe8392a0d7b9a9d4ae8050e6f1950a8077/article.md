- New website debuts with official branded merchandise from 15 world-class resorts, including Vail Mountain, Whistler Blackcomb, Breckenridge and Park City Mountain

**BROOMFIELD, Colo., March 19, 2026** – Vail Resorts today announced a new online retail destination for official resort and Epic-branded apparel and accessories. Available now at[EpicShop.com](http://epicshop.com/)


, guests can shop exclusive merchandise from 15 world-class resorts to stay connected to the mountains year-round.


Epic® Shop features up to 40 styles per resort across 15 legendary destinations: Vail Mountain, Breckenridge, Park City Mountain, Beaver Creek, Keystone, Whistler Blackcomb, Heavenly, Stowe, Okemo, Northstar, Kirkwood, Stevens Pass, Crested Butte, Mount Snow and Hunter Mountain. The collection includes apparel, headwear, drinkware, souvenirs, stickers and more.


“Our guests have an incredible connection with the mountains they ski, ride, and enjoy in any number of ways—and we want to make it easier for them to shop their favorite mountain merchandise from anywhere,” said Greg Sullivan, Vail Resorts Executive Vice President of Retail/Rental, Hospitality & Guest Services. “Ski culture is more vibrant than ever, extending well beyond the slopes and into daily life. Whether guests want a souvenir from their first time on snow, memento from a milestone trip, or simply a way to represent their favorite resort, they can now shop official gear without waiting for their next mountain visit.”


The launch collection is only the start. Epic Shop will continue to expand with more resorts and fresh collections coming next season. As Epic Shop evolves, guests can also look forward to co-branded collaborations and exclusive limited-edition pieces designed to bring mountain style into everyday life.


Shipping is available across the United States, with plans to expand internationally in the future. Explore the launch collection now at[EpicShop.com](http://epicshop.com/)


, or through any of the 15 resort websites. 2026/27 Epic Passes are now available at[EpicPass.com](http://epicpass.com/)


.
