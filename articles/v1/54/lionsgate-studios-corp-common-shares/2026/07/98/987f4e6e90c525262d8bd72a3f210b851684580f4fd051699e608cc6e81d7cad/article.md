### LIONSGATE TO RELEASE FIRST QUARTER EARNINGS FOR FISCAL 2027 AND HOLD ANALYST AND INVESTOR CONFERENCE CALL AFTER MARKET CLOSE ON THURSDAY, AUGUST 6

SANTA MONICA, Calif. and VANCOUVER, BC


, July 22, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Lionsgate (NYSE: LION) today announced it will release its financial results for the fiscal 2027 first quarter ended June 30, 2026 after market close on Thursday, August 6.


Lionsgate senior management will hold its analyst and investor conference call to discuss its fiscal 2027 first quarter at 5:00 P.M. ET/2:00 P.M. PT on Thursday, August 6. Interested parties may listen to the live webcast by visiting the events page on the Lionsgate Investor relations[website](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4736592-1&h=3558662528&u=https%3A%2F%2Furl.us.m.mimecastprotect.com%2Fs%2FPlPQC9r2Ejf080QjHoflfqJuES%3Fdomain%3Dinvestors.lionsgate.com&a=website) or via the following[link](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4736592-1&h=760126124&u=https%3A%2F%2Furl.us.m.mimecastprotect.com%2Fs%2FwXMhC0R9rMHxjxVzuDh9f93RmE%3Fdomain%3Devent.choruscall.com&a=link) . A full replay will be available starting Thursday evening, August 6, by clicking the same link.


**About Lionsgate:** Lionsgate (NYSE: LION) is one of the world's leading standalone, pure play content companies. It brings together diversified motion picture and television production and distribution businesses, a world-class portfolio of valuable brands and franchises, a premier talent management and production powerhouse at 3 Arts Entertainment and a more than 20,000-title film and television library, all driven by Lionsgate's bold and entrepreneurial culture.
