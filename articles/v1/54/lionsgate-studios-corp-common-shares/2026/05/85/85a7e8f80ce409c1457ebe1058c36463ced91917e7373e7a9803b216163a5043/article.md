### LIONSGATE TO RELEASE FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR EARNINGS FOR FISCAL 2026 AND HOLD ANALYST AND INVESTOR CONFERENCE CALL AFTER MARKET CLOSE ON THURSDAY, MAY 21, 2026


May 7, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/746463231/files/doc_news/LIONSGATE-TO-RELEASE-FOURTH-QUARTER-AND-FULL-YEAR-EARNINGS-FOR-FISCAL-2026-AND-HOLD-ANALYST-AND-INVESTOR-CONFERENCE-CALL-AFTER-MARKET-80XTZ.pdf)


SANTA MONICA, Calif. and VANCOUVER, BC


, May 7, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Lionsgate (NYSE: LION) today announced it will release its fourth quarter and full year financial results for the 2026 fiscal year ended March 31, 2026 after market close on Thursday, May 21.


Lionsgate senior management will hold its analyst and investor conference call to discuss its fiscal 2026 financial results at 5:00 P.M. ET/2:00 P.M. PT on Thursday, May 21. Interested parties may listen to the live webcast by visiting the events page on the Lionsgate Investor relations[website](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4682969-1&h=1672855711&u=https%3A%2F%2Furl.us.m.mimecastprotect.com%2Fs%2FnNm1CZ6W9gHwp44OhzfgUBlCwA%3Fdomain%3Dinvestors.lionsgate.com&a=website) or via the following[link](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4682969-1&h=2266946265&u=https%3A%2F%2Furl.us.m.mimecastprotect.com%2Fs%2F8x2uC1w9vMc09zzvuGhMUVDLLn%3Fdomain%3Devent.choruscall.com&a=link) . A full replay will be available starting Thursday evening, May 21, by clicking the same link.


**About Lionsgate:**
Lionsgate (NYSE: LION) is one of the world's leading standalone, pure play content companies. It brings together diversified motion picture and television production and distribution businesses, a world-class portfolio of valuable brands and franchises, a premier talent management and production powerhouse at 3 Arts Entertainment and a more than 20,000-title film and television library, all driven by Lionsgate's bold and entrepreneurial culture.
