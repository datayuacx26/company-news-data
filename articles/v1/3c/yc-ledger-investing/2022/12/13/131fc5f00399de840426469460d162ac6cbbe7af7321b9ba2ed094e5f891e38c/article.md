Ledger Investing Opens Office in NYC


Ledger Investing’s New HQ at 75 Rockefeller Plaza. Source: RXR Realty


We are excited to announce the opening of our new headquarters in the heart of New York City. Located on the 24th floor of 75 Rockefeller Plaza, this office space will provide a central hub for our team members to collaborate and work together, as well as a place for us to host our customers as we continue to drive innovation in the insurance industry.


“We are thrilled to open our new headquarters office in NY and to be a part of such a vibrant and dynamic financial hub. Our team is dedicated to driving innovation in the insurance industry and we believe that our new location will help us better serve our clients and employees. We look forward to continuing to grow and succeed in this exciting new chapter for Ledger Investing.” Brad Fischtrom, COO of Ledger Investing


As a[remote-first company](https://medium.com/ledger-investing/reflections-on-remote-work-d19492c7af88) , we have always been committed to providing our team members with the flexibility and support they need to succeed in work and home life. With the opening of our new headquarters, we are excited to offer our team the opportunity to work in person and collaborate with their colleagues face-to-face. We believe that this will help foster a sense of community and collaboration among our team members, and allow us to work more efficiently and effectively.


In addition to providing a space for our team members to work and collaborate, this new location will allow us to better serve our clients and expand our global reach. Our new headquarters will be a place for us to host meetings and events with our customers. We are committed to building strong, long-lasting relationships with our customers and partners, and we believe that having a physical space where we can meet and engage with them will be invaluable.


We are excited to be part of the growth and dynamism of one of the world’s most vibrant financial hubs, and we are eager to start connecting with all of the other innovators and partners in the space.


In the coming months, we have some exciting projects and initiatives in the works. We are committed to driving change and improving the insurance landscape, and we believe that our new headquarters will help us achieve these goals.


Thank you for your continued support and we look forward to seeing you at our new headquarters soon!

About Ledger Investing


Ledger is a technology company disrupting the insurance industry’s financing. Ledger Investing and Ledger Capital Markets were formed to enable insurers to create substantial value by securitizing insurance risk while providing investors with an opportunity to invest directly in a new and growing asset class. We are backed by a combination of leading tech VCs and strategic insuretech investors.
