Ledger Company Updates


**What's in this post?** We have accomplished a lot over the past year. Let's fly through together on what happened in our journey to disrupt insurance financing.


Ledger opens for investors


In an effort to make Insurance Investing a mainstream asset class, we launched Ledger Capital Markets, our Broker-Dealer arm. Read more about it[here](https://medium.com/ledger-investing/insurance-startup-becomes-licensed-us-broker-dealer-248f751a34a0) .


Insurance partnerships on all continents


We have gone out of our way to promote securitization with carriers and brokers in Asia, Europe, Africa, and America. Although regulations and data reporting vary by country, we are hard at work building a standard that will make insurance investing mainstream for the rest of the world.


Groundbreaking risk forecasts


In 2018, we were deeply invested in researching solutions to forecast insurance underwriting loss ratio distributions. Looking at the US market over the past 30 years, we have benchmarked cutting-edge industry models and proprietary models across dozens of performance metrics.


Our new forecasting models offer several advantages over other models in the actuarial literature. Read more about it[here](https://medium.com/ledger-investing/intuitively-predict-insurance-chaos-8ea589ca206e) ,[here](https://medium.com/ledger-investing/visualizing-insurance-losses-1b2e3517b88b) and[here](https://medium.com/ledger-investing/developing-stochastic-loss-reserving-models-10f48f9039ce) .


Investing platform launch


Our team focused a lot on making our findings and apps easily accessible on our platform. Request access[here](https://ledgerinvesting.typeform.com/to/l9xKaM) and start exploring and investing insurance portfolios previously inaccessible on the capital markets.


Read more about it[here](https://medium.com/ledger-investing/fresh-design-and-new-features-3fbcc560b0ef) .


Successfully financed an auto business


Texas-based insurance broker, AmWINS and Redpoint Insurance accessed capital from with AIG's AlphaCat Managers using Ledger securitization standards and analytics technology.


Read more about it[here](https://medium.com/ledger-investing/ledger-securitization-to-enable-auto-insurance-to-access-capital-markets-1f9abf988cdd) or directly on[Artemis](http://www.artemis.bm/news/ledger-closes-non-standard-auto-insurance-securitization-with-alphacat/)


Growing team


The Ledger Family has expanded in 2018 to take on new challenging projects. We will continue to onboard Data Scientists, Engineers, Designers, Capital Markets and Insurance Experts to make things people love.
