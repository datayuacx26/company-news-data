# Ledger Data Science Team Releases BayesBlend

Ledger Investing is proud to announce the release of BayesBlend, an innovative open-source model averaging library developed by our very own Nathaniel Haines and Conor Goold.


At Ledger, we use BayesBlend to combine predictions from multiple mathematical models, where each model reflects a unique hypothesis about how casualty insurance portfolios will perform into the future. This "data-driven hedging" gives us a better view than any given model in isolation. We continue to develop cutting edge analytics and commit to publishing our work to make it available to you.


Check out[BayesBlend](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00158) and see how it can enhance your data science projects:[GitHub Link](https://github.com/LedgerInvesting/bayesblend/) .


More on Ledger’s modeling approach[here](https://www.ledgerinvesting.com/insurance-library) .
