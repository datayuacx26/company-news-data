# Ledger Investing Completes $250M Casualty ILS Secondary Transaction

“The increasing demand from sophisticated financial institutions for casualty ILS affirms its multi-pronged value proposition within private credit, absolute return, and other areas of client portfolios. We recognize the immense potential casualty ILS holds in providing both stability and diversification to our investors' holdings. We’re excited to be at the forefront of this emerging and highly attractive asset class.” said Alex Freiberg, CEO, Ledger Capital Markets, LLC.


About Ledger Investing


Ledger Investing, Inc. is a tech-enabled financial services company that has led the development of casualty insurance-linked securities (ILS) since its inception in 2016. Recently named to CB Insights Insurtech 50 (2023), the company has raised more than $90M from leading VCs and strategic insurance industry investors. Ledger's $75M Series B funding round was completed in June 2022 and was led by WestCap with participation from Teachers’ Venture Growth, Intact Ventures and previous investors, SignalFire, MassMutual Ventures, Allegis Capital and Accel. In 2021, Ledger created the first dedicated casualty ILS fund. To date, $1B gross premium has been securitized through primary and secondary transactions for General Liability, Workers' Compensation, Commercial Auto and Private Passenger Auto through Ledger Marketplace.
