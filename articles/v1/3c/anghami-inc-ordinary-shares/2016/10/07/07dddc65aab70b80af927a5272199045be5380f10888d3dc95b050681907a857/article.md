# Anghami Reaches the 30 Million Downloads Milestone

BEIRUT


, October 11, 2016


/PRNewswire/ --


Anghami - the leading on demand music platform of the Middle East


, today announced that it has surpassed 30 million downloads of its iOS and Android mobile applications, as well as 33 million registered users.


Anghami reaches the 30 million downloads milestone (PRNewsFoto/Anghami)


(Photo:[http://photos.prnewswire.com/prnh/20161010/426957](http://photos.prnewswire.com/prnh/20161010/426957) )


These figures mark a significant milestone for Anghami - highlighting that while Anghami is a mobile-first service, its traction and growth is still accelerating and a growing segment of users listen daily using the web and the recently launched Windows & Mac desktop apps.


While Anghami's main focus is the Middle East


, over 5 million of its users use its products from far over the oceans to keep up-to-date with the music back home as well as to reminisce to the childhood tunes.


The success of Anghami reaffirms its market leading position, and its major role in driving music and entertainment in the Middle East


. Anghami is available through a variety of platforms including smartphones, web, tablets, desktops, smart watches, Apple TV, Chromecast, Sonos and more to come.


"The 30 million milestone is yet another indication that Anghami is the real connection between users and artists in the Middle East


," said Eddy Maroun


, Anghami CEO. "The product updates we've been doing the past year and our offerings with the artists and the various telcos in the region have paid off and we plan to work harder to connect the music lovers as well as the availability of our premium offerings in the months to come."


Anghami's growth mirrors the success of streaming as well as mobile growth with consumers seeking easy to use applications that offer music, videos and entertainment in one product.


About Anghami: Anghami, Arabic for 'my tunes', is the first music streaming platform for the Middle East


and North African (MENA) region. Launched in 2012, the freemium service uses music to bring artists and fans together. With over 30 million users, it is the most prominent music venture in the region.
