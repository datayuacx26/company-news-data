# Anghami Expressions: The New User-Generated Music Video Feature by Anghami

The Middle East's


number one Music Entertainment Platform just introduced a new engaging feature, " **Anghami** **Expressions** **"** .


(Photo:[http://mma.prnewswire.com/media/448809/Anghami.jpg](http://mma.prnewswire.com/media/448809/Anghami.jpg) )


Anghami Expressions is a vertical video feature that allows users to create, share and discover music through instantly user-generated videos. Anghami is excited to see its millions of users express themselves through Expressions by singing, dancing, having fun, and lip-syncing.


Anghami Expressions allows users to create instant music videos by choosing any 15 second part/extract from Anghami's 20 million Arabic, and International songs. Creativity knows no limit! This feature allows the consumer to pause the recording to change scenes, alternate between front and back cameras and spice up the video with stickers!


More options are offered by Expression Videos such as filters, slow motion effect, and setting up a timer so not to get stuck with video selfies all the time and fit more people in.


From the artists and brands' perspectives, Anghami Expressions is a fun and exciting way to reach fans and customers.


Anghami believes that the technology behind bringing the camera and music together on mobile empowers people to express themselves, live in the moment, and have fun together. Living up to Anghami's mantra, Add Music to Your Day!


Learn more about expressions:[http://www.anghami.com/expressions](http://www.anghami.com/expressions)
