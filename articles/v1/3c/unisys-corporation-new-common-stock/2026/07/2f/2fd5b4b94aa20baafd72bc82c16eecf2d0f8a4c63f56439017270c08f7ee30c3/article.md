# Get faster, more accurate answers to your “What if” questions

#### Propelling a company forward takes imagination, with “What if” questions often sparking innovation. Quantum computing gives you more accurate answers to your simulation exercises.


Quantum computing leverages quantum mechanics to solve problems that would be impossible for classical computers to solve. Just as[quantum computing eases workload challenges](https://www.unisys.com/blog-post/ecs/quantum-computing-can-save-you-years-of-workload-frustration/) , it can also add tangible value to your simulation exercises, especially when combined with AI and other advanced technologies such as high-performance computing.


### The crucial role of simulation


A simulation, or business scenario analysis, provides answers to complex questions through dynamic modeling. It’s the go-to tool when teams seek clarity on the critical “what if” questions that shape their future. A simulation serves as a prediction, a discovery, and a validation. Consider what that looks like in practice:


- Determine whether to adjust the price of a product or service. Sample query: What if we adjust our product price by fifty cents?
- Anticipate the ripple effects of a supply chain disruption. Sample query: What if a supply chain hiccup in the Middle East disrupts our operations?
- Assess the business impact of a natural disaster. Sample query: What if a tornado wipes out key infrastructure?
- Test assumptions about which actions will best serve your organization. Sample query: Is high client attrition linked to bandwidth bottlenecks or response delays?


By running these virtual experiments, organizations confidently explore solutions without costly risks. Blend curiosity with caution and you land on the biggest advantage of simulations.


### How quantum computing refines business scenario analysis


Running a business scenario analysis starts with crafting a model — this might be as simple as a single equation or as intricate as multiple interacting systems that operate collaboratively or competitively. From there, you code the model onto a platform designed to run countless iterations. This process often takes significant time and resources, but quantum computing can help.


Quantum is especially effective for simulations when combined with other technologies, such as AI, high-performance computing, and advanced machine learning. When advanced AI, such as graph neural networks and neurosymbolic AI, integrates with quantum computing, you gain deeper insights and capabilities.


While classical simulations often require simplifying assumptions to fit hardware constraints, quantum computing enables faster and richer model exploration. Think of it like having X-ray vision in a shifting maze. With quantum technology, you can speed through the map and see changes in real time, revealing insights that classical methods can’t capture.


In addition, quantum computing can process an exponentially large number of possibilities simultaneously, enabling more advanced simulations. Instead of running just one or two scenarios sequentially, quantum systems can explore thousands of outcomes in parallel. This accelerates discovery and decision-making.


### Gaining quantum advantages


Quantum-powered simulations deliver several business advantages that can advance your organization.


- **Expanding model availability** : Quantum allows your organization to create models that were previously out of reach. That includes those that adapt dynamically as the environment shifts, much like having a map that updates in real time as you move through a maze. This flexibility enables teams to understand processes that classical methods can't handle.
- **Getting a big picture perspective** : Quantum’s power to process countless scenarios simultaneously means organizations can explore a richer set of possibilities all at once. Picture reading an entire library of books at the same time instead of one chapter after another. This leads to faster insights, greater accuracy, and a competitive edge.
- **Preparing for the future:** The talent shortage and rapid advances in quantum technology make acting now a strategic imperative. Invest in quantum-powered simulations to position yourself well to address future complex challenges.
