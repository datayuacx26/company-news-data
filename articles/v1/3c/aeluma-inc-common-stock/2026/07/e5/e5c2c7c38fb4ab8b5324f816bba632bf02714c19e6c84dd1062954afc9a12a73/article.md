# Aeluma CEO to Chair Optica Advanced Photonics Congress Industry Program on AI and Quantum

# Aeluma CEO to Chair Optica Advanced Photonics Congress Industry Program on AI and Quantum


July 07, 2026 7:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_7ba91a8b631de84379a01f3b6b111173/aeluma/news/2026-07-07_Aeluma_CEO_to_Chair_Optica_Advanced_Photonics_109.pdf)


*Leading Industry Experts to Participate in Interactive Panel Sessions*


*Company to Present Technical Paper on Scalable Quantum Dot Lasers for AI Datacom*


GOLETA, Calif., July 07, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Aeluma, Inc. (NASDAQ: ALMU), a semiconductor company specializing in high-performance, scalable technologies for mobile, AI, defense and aerospace, robotics, automotive, AR/VR, and quantum, announced today that CEO and Founder Jonathan Klamkin, Ph.D., will serve as Chair of the[Industry Program](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=R7uui1XsQpU8YkbuF-5nWir_R3a1BVS5R7mPsmlzB6NopeScZzcNbVMmYohL9Zy4ZHCJl-QGVRlXs46lFFzoTBYB7QoM2u4cISwQcPpLWwfnwScUw5rEQWkD_OhniOV6LyBigMDtDdZY3wTKLbfVIWkdwN0HismXC-59FdCrdq6sIK6IkKWYE_LheT-WdFa1) at the Optica[Advanced Photonics Congress](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=MzGD7IxzEZ-KlFnnU-jbLzI-efkGd-1687MntmQY8-jPPIaqLTKs0cPXK6rUYa-5i04_bcx6IHGCZVCBcBKUTFjea2EUJtSd8kA_vu8pw2WACE-XtVuwn_BSDxWcwHIlis6gaddgcBUb3KxrXZ6PnaqjVtcj8xtyFyiZ1l-h5bU=) . Aeluma will also present a paper on its scalable quantum dot laser technology as part of the[Technical Program](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=aeYJ_tbTl_wyn23DRNCoz-XSAyjXoEZId6mXisSLrvmZ1BnZB_mfmRpkPYF3AAxxiq3W5V376AWMyEArfyp5NtEmE4E5uG692GSS9qcPkOtni4RCWr1hARMZPquqD9gzDibfcjjM07kk2A5X1pUGb4N_v5ArixlZTMRGpgAK9Y8=) .


[Optica](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=AcZshouSbQHCpUnOrrAu-TQpnRiR4YOAX7axTegsedXiD-q9o6HORc1HGmOY3m_Xy-nRsItnZEi3wF0fYPVjEA==) is the leading global professional society for optics and photonics, and its conferences are among the industry’s premier forums for emerging photonics technologies.


The Industry Program will take place on July 28, 2026, at 3:30 – 7:30 PM PST. The topics to be discussed during panel sessions include:


- Next-Generation Scale-Up and Scale-Out Interconnects for AI: Photonic Component Requirements
- Quantum and Low-Temperature Photonics: Component and Integration Requirements


Industry leaders will participate in these panel sessions to describe the work their companies are conducting to address demanding requirements and the challenges associated with scaling.


Also, Aeluma’s Director of Technology Enablement, Phillip Skahan, Ph.D., will present the paper titled “High-Performance Datacom Quantum Dot Lasers by Production-Ready 300mm MOCVD,” on July 27, 2026.


To meet with the Aeluma team at the event, attendees are encouraged to visit Booth 108 at the[Exhibit](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=bWqfbbV8GGBgdG93Xm8FYOMU74iPtlkGbpi3banW2RNibhLzfypQkbfHnXdKs4JbudBqe0dWelxVjSm_z4GsYu9dpPGeI8Ke-tPUgSjqddc2rItfzLQxvXT_z89DVVG2KBeVLB85TAnJmAakzePJkw==) during July 27 – 29 at the Hilton Long Beach, Long Beach, California. To schedule a meeting, contact the company at[info@aeluma.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=ezfQqF3YcVnhRmjElvHUGR1WGVXdNzS4u_we9xNvt5AZG8sP4129HPQYgo5-UbpE_woLiNIu6FI1NBE19mixNQ==) .

**About Aeluma**


Aeluma (NASDAQ: ALMU) is a transformative semiconductor company specializing in high-
performance photonic and electronic technologies that scale. The company’s proprietary platform combines compound semiconductors with scalable manufacturing used for mass market microelectronics to enable volume production and large-scale integration. Applications for Aeluma’s technology include mobile, AI, defense and aerospace, robotics, automotive, AR/VR, and quantum. Headquartered in Goleta, California, Aeluma operates state-of-the-art R&D and manufacturing capabilities for semiconductor wafer production, quick-turn chip fabrication, rapid prototyping, test and validation. Aeluma also partners with production-scale fabrication foundries, packaging, and integration companies.
