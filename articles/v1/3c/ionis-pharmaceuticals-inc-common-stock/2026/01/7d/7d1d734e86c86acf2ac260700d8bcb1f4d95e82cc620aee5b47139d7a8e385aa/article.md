Description


ION775 is an investigational apoC-III siRNA being evaluated for people with severe hypertriglyceridemia (sHTG). ION775 is designed to maximize durability, with the potential for semiannual dosing. ION775 is Ionis’ first clinical-stage siRNA medicine.


**About Severe Hypertriglyceridemia (sHTG)**


sHTG is defined by very high triglyceride (TG) levels of 500 mg/dL and above and characterized by an increased risk of acute pancreatitis (AP), atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) and other comorbidities. More than 3 million people are living with sHTG in the U.S., including ~1 million who are considered high-risk (TG levels ≥880 mg/dL or TG levels ≥500 mg/dL with AP history or other comorbidities).


*Safety and efficacy have not been evaluated by any regulatory authorities for the indication described.*
