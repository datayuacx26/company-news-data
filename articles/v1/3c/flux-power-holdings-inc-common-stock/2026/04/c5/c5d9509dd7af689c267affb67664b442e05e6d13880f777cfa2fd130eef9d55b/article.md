# Flux Power Advances Industry Leadership at MODEX 2026 with SkyEMS Enhancements, Sustainability Award Finalist Recognition, and Expert Panel Participation

### Company to showcase advanced lithium-ion solutions, debut enhanced SkyEMS® capabilities, and contribute to industry electrification strategy discussions


VISTA, Calif., April 07, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Flux Power Holdings, Inc](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=_e57hXWUdwqGbpNSDt0w_YcWe0hnRppW2aBBf5WEjJt5TCAQ3WfR6raR3ps5chOKV5gnoLxxsCyrWI_xWBedy2V16kCE0lbu9GYxHrMpZTo=) . (NASDAQ: FLUX), a leading developer of advanced lithium-ion energy storage solutions for material handling, airport ground support equipment (GSE), and industrial applications, today announced its participation at **MODEX 2026** , taking place April 13–16 at the Georgia World Congress Center in Atlanta.


At **Booth B8108, Hall B** ,[Flux Power](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=_e57hXWUdwqGbpNSDt0w_Q9fXsbqZchX_9rW-Sy7eDpfMOOsQWPkoVAM4ENq78N6rp8DaO0TXtQ_uEqcdUP2lB2sA6ku5PXux8akA5q_OL9oDwADQnR1YyMGuAz90rlCZPXNw4BED2S_KCWzmPWV9yQPpCBZ94AAGZCHUoaUd-I-YHHAPTxy9DW47tgxfVsLw4UUUHbQLeglMfemBKe-yQ==) will showcase its portfolio of lithium-ion battery solutions engineered to deliver superior performance, enhanced safety, and a lower total cost of ownership for fleet operators. Attendees will also experience the latest advancements to the company’s[SkyEMS®](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=itpCU4n4qkFg76GOsCrUMTvO_oSDG8FmjtBr7r4HG2l3iy_HdYGuN_TPZ5PXj2kyOd60onfz_yFYq9d1Wtyftkjr0m5YTPmg6HoltkDIMTF3tiOhsXD-AjvT41msqANd5ia2mxDbrSFu2LEC6W5peOjdwtHP8RQVl_wSkWzRdX0_n-vlg9idfIwVEmbPfh4m1N8fOdKmIIXkU703adF7qw==) **fleet intelligence platform** , designed to provide real-time visibility, predictive insights, and actionable analytics that help optimize fleet performance and maximize uptime.


**Enhanced SkyEMS® Platform Delivers Deeper Fleet Intelligence**


The latest[SkyEMS](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=itpCU4n4qkFg76GOsCrUMaJHobpRQ20RIZlAXvZrRz1ZROZSktIl_ujXmCcKB5ucCA1d_WTw8OHT_1UrKQeDAnI1v3jja7d4qJfBLlH-io87ue3FZLuAhDwlR8tNbhtBpnyHw_HM3-PddKDoXaSC8wpKUbT4pAqy5Hz2s0ttlQkM50RKTRT22MjMcvg1xBJVdTLQ_3AL-NGgt4J6x6bchA==) enhancements introduce a significantly upgraded **mobile-first user interface** , enabling operators to access critical fleet data anytime, anywhere. New capabilities include:


- **Intuitive mobile dashboards** for at-a-glance fleet health and performance
- **Enhanced real-time alerts and notifications** to proactively address issues before they impact operations
- **Expanded data integrations and API connectivity** for seamless interoperability with WMS, ERP, and OEM systems
- **Advanced reporting and analytics tools** to support faster, data-driven decision-making across operations


Together, these enhancements empower customers with greater control, improved operational efficiency, and measurable cost savings.


**Patented State-of-Health (SOH) Technology Sets New Standard**


Flux Power will also highlight its newly patented[State-of-Health (SOH)](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=LBHG6DKQQVQoY73MYhlAUJH2C3wh6HdYtfG-dao0adMlys29j8V8VmcI_580lzQWq8Rt54r7KlkNV6mNMWIs5FUQj0UqEGar2d2_iyj7d0CFElpfjRvWgoe6HBwWqpS71eMp8Dw333nas1ZGorg7MpKrlFkFzl_5gSszV3PUwWABISu96sKMDIkGz2Id30iHf_9zHUBHhbiXLxJqQ9tIXtnG3qv_5_6frAsFfLOc8sk=) technology, representing a significant advancement in battery lifecycle management. This innovation delivers a more precise, dynamic assessment of battery condition—enabling operators to accurately forecast performance, optimize maintenance intervals, and extend overall asset life.


By integrating advanced battery analytics with intelligent software, Flux Power continues to push the boundaries of performance, reliability, and sustainability in energy storage solutions.


**Finalist for MODEX 2026 Innovation in Sustainability Award**


Flux Power has been named a finalist for the **MODEX 2026 Innovation in Sustainability Award** , recognizing the company’s leadership in advancing clean energy solutions and helping customers reduce emissions through electrification.


**Industry Leadership at Advanced Energy Council Panel**


Flux Power also announced that **Lucia Salcido, Senior Product Manager** , will participate in the *“Partners in Your Electrification Strategy”* panel hosted by the Advanced Energy Council (AEC). The session will bring together industry leaders to explore how collaborative, data-driven approaches are accelerating electrification across material handling and logistics operations.


The session will take place on **Tuesday, April 14, 2026, from 10:30 a.m. to 11:15 a.m. ET in Theater I** at the Georgia World Congress Center.


“MODEX is a key opportunity to connect with customers and partners accelerating the transition to electrification,” said **Jeff Mason, Chief Operating Officer of Flux Power** . “Our continued innovation, from advancements in SkyEMS and our patented SOH technology to recognition as a sustainability award finalist reflects our commitment to delivering intelligent, reliable energy solutions that power the future of material handling.”


**Visit Flux Power at MODEX 2026**


Attendees are invited to visit **Booth B8108, Hall B** to learn more about Flux Power’s lithium-ion battery solutions and see firsthand how SkyEMS® is transforming fleet management through actionable, data-driven insights.

**About Flux Power Holdings, Inc.**
Flux Power (NASDAQ: FLUX) designs, manufactures, and sells advanced lithium-ion energy storage solutions for electrification of a range of industrial and commercial sectors including material handling and airport ground support equipment (GSE). Flux Power’s lithium-ion battery packs, including the proprietary battery management system (BMS) and telemetry, provide customers with a better performing, lower cost of ownership, and more environmentally friendly alternative, in many instances, to traditional lead acid and propane-based solutions. Lithium-ion battery packs reduce CO2 emissions and help improve sustainability and ESG metrics for fleets. For more information, please visit[www.fluxpower.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=oz-2J2kxy-VEMNxfHqvhrZ0MYSWNA9UHxQnS83PdhkHPHMeLIjLPt2gOOnAI15CzHkU0OWVKtBMGchH_64MRxLF6kKFfYbL_pcKFNCVMKaY=) .
