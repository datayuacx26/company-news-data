# Power Integrity Testing for EV Chargers & High-Voltage Converters: Tools That Drive Precision

As EV and HEV platforms scale rapidly, ensuring stable and efficient power delivery in high-voltage converters and charging systems has become mission-critical. Power integrity testing - verifying voltage stability, signal fidelity, and electromagnetic compatibility under dynamic loads - presents unique challenges, including fast-switching transients, wide bandwidth requirements, and the need for precise synchronization across test instruments. Advanced signal generation, waveform creation, and high-resolution measurement tools are essential to simulate real-world conditions and accurately diagnose issues. This is where high-performance signal generators, arbitrary function generators, oscilloscopes, and power meters come together as a complete validation solution.


**Challenges in EV Charging & High-Voltage Power Testing**


EV charging systems and onboard converters operate under increasingly demanding conditions: higher voltages, faster switching frequencies (SiC/GaN devices), and tighter efficiency constraints. Engineers must validate power electronics performance across transient loads, ensure compliance with EMI/EMC standards, and troubleshoot signal distortions that can cause inefficiencies or failures.


Capturing these events requires instruments capable of wide bandwidth, high sampling rates, and deep memory. For example, identifying switching noise or ripple artifacts demands oscilloscopes with high acquisition rates and advanced triggering, while replicating real-world RF interference conditions requires flexible signal generators with precise modulation control. Additionally, power measurement must be accurate and responsive to ensure efficiency and safety compliance.
