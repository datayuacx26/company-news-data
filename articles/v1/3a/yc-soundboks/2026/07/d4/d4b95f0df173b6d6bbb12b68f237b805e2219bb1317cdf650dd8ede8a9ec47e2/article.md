# 10 hours and 36 minutes at Roskilde Festival 2026

Eight years have passed since[SOUNDBLOKS](https://soundboks.com/blogs/in-tune/experience-soundboks-at-roskilde-festival-2026) last partied at Roskilde Festival’s infamous campsite. A lot has happened in that time.


The COVID pandemic. This century’s best film Parasite. Queen Margarethe’s abdication. Olympic and World champions crowned. Countless scandals, feel-good stories, and everything in between.


Seismic moments like these have shaped the world and rewritten history. It felt like time to add another to the list.


We sent our in-house roving reporter to cover the reopening of a Roskilde Festival icon. Here’s what he saw with his own two eyes.
