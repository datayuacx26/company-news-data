# Experience Soundboks at Roskilde Festival 2026

[Roskilde Festival’s](https://www.roskilde-festival.dk/) campsite means a lot to SOUNDBOKS. This is our birthplace, our testing ground, and the scene of countless Soundboks-powered parties.


We’re expecting over[12,000 Soundboks speakers at the campsite](https://soundboks.com/blogs/in-tune/returning-to-our-roskilde-roots) . Veteran Soundboks 1s and Soundboks 2s who have survived a decade of Roskilde Festival. Brand new[Soundboks 4s](https://soundboks.com/products/soundboks-4) ,[Soundboks Mixes](https://soundboks.com/products/soundboks-mix) , and[Lightboks](https://soundboks.com/products/lightboks) ready for their first experience. This is where our story comes to life.


This year, we’re bringing some seriously special activities to the campsite. Whether we’ll see you there or not, here’s how you can get involved.


## Join SOUNDBLOK: our official campsite base


For the first time since 2018, SOUNDBLOK returns to the Roskilde Festival campsite. Part-repair shop, part-DJ booth, this is the home of all our campsite activities.


SOUNDBLOK revolves around five principles:


- Build: a new friendship or your dream customization
- Find: the song that defines summer
- Fix: your Soundboks gear or your social battery
- Play: get silly, dance wild, have fun
- Watch: the campsite come alive and future memories


This is where you step into our world.


Expect a massive party during the warmup days. The winners of our DJ competition bring their skills to this dancefloor. When they’re not playing, we’ll make sure to have a few Soundboks turned up and some beers on ice to keep the vibes going.


All the activities below take place here. If in doubt, check SOUNDBLOK out.


## Repair any SOUNDBOKS product for free


All festival long, our repair shop is there to make sure your Soundboks gear works perfectly. Our dedicated team have seen it all before and know your product inside-out. Whether you own a S[oundboks 4](https://soundboks.com/products/soundboks-4) ,[Lightboks](https://soundboks.com/products/lightboks) ,[Soundboks Mix](https://soundboks.com/products/soundboks-mix) , or anything older–we’ll get your party back up and running in no time.


All you have to do is head to SOUNDBLOK and talk to a member of the team. They’ll book you in, work their magic on your party speaker or party light, and let you know when to pick it back up.


#### Opening hours:


**27/6:** 14:00 - 23:00


**28/6 - 04/7:** 09:00 - 23:00


## Tune into the party on Repair TV


Anyone can experience Roskilde Festival 2026 thanks to Repair TV. Raw, unedited, and brand new for this year, we’ll capture the real culture of the campsite.


Repair TV is inspired by the chaos and realness of early-2000s MTV and 2010s VICE. Our roaming reporter will have their boots on the ground across the entire festival. Messy interviews, behind the scenes content, repair shop coverage, daily recaps–Repair TV has it all.


Attending this year? Keep a lookout for our cameras. Can’t make it? Get dirty without the mud.


## Exclusive merchandise only available at SOUNDBLOK


Who doesn’t love festival merchandise? You get to relive those memories every time you wear that hat or T-shirt. This year, we’ve teamed up with Roskilde Festival to create some exclusive designs you can’t buy anywhere else.


Every day, we’ll drop super limited quantities of a new piece of merch. Act fast–these are only available on the same day. Once they sell out, they’re gone forever.


Only available at SOUNDBLOK.


## Get creative at our customization station


Haven’t had the chance to personalize your Soundboks? Old design looking a bit tired? You’re in luck. For two hours every warmup day, we’re opening our exclusive customization station.


Stickers, stencils, magnets, and more–there are plenty of ways to add some style to your Soundboks. You don’t need to book a time–simply rock up and get customizing. The best bit? It’s completely free.


## Discover new music from our DJ competition


Bedroom DJs all over the world dream of playing at a festival. Thanks to our DJ competition, here’s your chance to live out that fantasy.


We’ve got three one-hour sets up for grabs at SOUNDBLOK. Every warmup day, a new DJ shows off their skills. Using a top secret criteria, we’ll pick our ultimate DJ to play one final time. As well as this final set, they win two tickets to Roskilde Festival 2027 and SOUNDBOKS gear for their camp.
