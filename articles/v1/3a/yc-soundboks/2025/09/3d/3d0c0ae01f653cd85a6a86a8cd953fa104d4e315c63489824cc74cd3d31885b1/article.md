# Why Lightboks is a Must-Have for Every Party

In this article, we want to show how Lightboks delivers the goods in every situation. Looking at its benefits for indoor use, outdoor partying, and pro-level performances, we’ll break down the key features for all three use cases.


Here's why you need a Lightboks in your life and how good party lighting makes a world of difference.


### Create indoor party experiences that rival clubs and venues


Lightboks transforms everyday spaces into designated party zones. At the push of a button, energy is injected into the room. This isn’t a one-size-fits-all approach, either: it’s super simple to adjust the settings to fit your vibe.


In the early stages of your party or for your post-game, you probably want to have more ambient lighting. With its smooth transitions, Chill mode allows you to create intimate and relaxed vibes. This creates Instagram-worthy experiences that people want to attend and share.


When your party calls for it, you can use the app to remotely change your settings and control the ambiance. This is where Dance and Rave modes come into play. More strobes, quicker transitions, and greater audio reactive response adds energy that compliment your music. Since Lightboks is silent, you get to generate more immersion and atmosphere without cranking your music to the max. We bet your neighbors appreciate this.


### Outdoor lighting levels up the vibes, whatever the weather


When you want to light up your backyard party with a backdrop or a centerpiece for your outdoor session, Lightboks comes into its own. Not only does it compliment sunsets and interact with the nature around you, but it also guarantees pro-level performance wherever you are.


This audio reactive party light has been designed specifically with portability in mind. Just look at its grab & go handle–perfect for carrying to your party or hoisting into the air on the drop. Plus, its soccer ball size means it can fit into pretty much any backpack–there’s no excuse not to pack this for your outdoor party.


Once you do get to your spot, you can rely on its weatherproof build and extensive battery life to keep the vibes going. Its IP65 coating and rubber bumper corners are well equipped to deal with bad weather, drink spills, or accidental knocks. Combine that with 14 hours of battery life on Rave mode or 10 on Chill mode and your outdoor party can keep going until the sun comes up.

From DJ performances to jamming with the band, Lightboks adds an extra dimension to the atmosphere. Not only does it emphasize your music, highlighting power moments, drops, and breakdowns, party lighting creates a stage–the focal point of any memorable performance.

Thanks to the simplicity of Lightboks’ setup and its customization capabilities in the app, its super easy to transition from performance to performance.
