### GameSquare Repurchases Over 1 Million Shares in July 2026


07/23/2026


**FRISCO, TX /[ACCESS Newswire](https://www.accessnewswire.com/) / July 23, 2026 /** GameSquare Holdings, Inc. ("GameSquare" or the "Company") (NASDAQ:GAME), a next-generation media, entertainment, technology and digital native treasury company, today announced it repurchased over 1 million shares of its common stock during the month of July 2026 for less than $0.4 million, representing an average price of approximately $0.38 per share. Following this transaction, the Company has approximately $10.9 million remaining under its current authorization, which was expanded on April 14, 2026.


Consistent with its capital allocation priorities, GameSquare intends to continue to opportunistically repurchase its common stock. Since October 2025, the Company has repurchased a total of over 8.8 million shares of its common stock for nearly $4.1 million, representing an average price of approximately $0.46 per share.


"We continue to believe GameSquare's shares trade at a significant discount to the underlying value of our business, and we intend to aggressively capitalize on that disconnect through continued share repurchases," said Justin Kenna, CEO of GameSquare. "Our strong balance sheet, including capital generated through opportunistic ETH sales, provides us with the financial flexibility to pursue a highly active and aggressive repurchase strategy."


**About GameSquare Holdings, Inc.**
GameSquare (NASDAQ:GAME) is a cutting-edge media, entertainment, and technology company transforming how brands and publishers connect with Gen Z, Gen Alpha, and Millennial audiences. With a platform that spans award-winning creative services, advanced analytics, and FaZe Esports, one of the most iconic gaming organizations, we operate one of the largest gaming media networks in North America. As a digital-native business, GameSquare provides brands with unparalleled access to world-class creators and talent, delivering authentic connections across gaming, esports, and youth culture. Complementing our operating strategy, GameSquare has developed an innovative treasury management program designed to generate yield and enhance capital efficiency, reinforcing our commitment to building a dynamic, high-performing media company at the intersection of culture, technology, and next-generation financial innovation.
