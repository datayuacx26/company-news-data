# Metal Projects: The First Team Intelligence Workspace

Private equity teams operate through structured deal processes: target screening, due diligence, investment execution, and post-investment monitoring. Each stage spans scores of documents, multiple team members, and weeks or months of evolving analysis across deals.


Yet until now, most AI tools and generalist vertical AI leaders like ChatGPT have forced teams to work in disconnected chats. The workflow is fragmented: generate insights in an AI chat, manually search SharePoint for documents, cross-reference with DealCloud in another window, and copy-paste outputs into memos.


Metal Projects is designed to fix the fundamental issues with this pain point


### Why We Built Projects: Context Without a Home


When we spoke with PE deal teams about their AI workflows, the frustration was consistent:


> *"AI is helpful for quick answers, but finding that one insight requires scrolling through pages of chat history. There's no way to build on prior work."*


The core problem is that existing AI tools weren't designed for how deal teams actually operate: PE work builds knowledge over time. It requires shared context. It demands that insights persist and compound, not disappear.


**Teams need a workspace that mirrors how they think: organized around deals, research themes, or key initiatives—with all relevant documents, analysis, and team knowledge in one place.**


### Workspace Designed for How Deal Teams Operate


Metal Projects create dedicated collaborative spaces for each deal, target company, or conference prep. Instead of treating AI as a disconnected Q&A tool, Projects become the central workspace where your team's diligence actually happens.


**Organized by Deal Logic** Create Projects for active deals, sector research, or deal archives. Each maintains its own organized context and history.


**All Files Live Together** Upload or select CIMs, expert calls, management presentations once. Every team member can work together in that Project space.


**Shared Analysis, Unified Intelligence** Ask about a company’s revenue quality via Metal Chat and Metal synthesizes across your CIM, expert calls, and all relevant files simultaneously—understanding your full project context.


**Context Persists and Builds** Every question, insight, and analysis stays within the Project. When your analyst flags customer concentration risk, your VP sees it during IC prep. Knowledge compounds instead of resetting.
