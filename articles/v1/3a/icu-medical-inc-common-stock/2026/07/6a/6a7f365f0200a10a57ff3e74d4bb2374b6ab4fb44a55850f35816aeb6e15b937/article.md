# ICU Medical’s Plum 360™ Infusion System Once Again Receives Best in KLAS Honor as Top-Performing Smart Pump EMR-Integrated

#### Plum 360 recognized by KLAS Research as 2024 Best in KLAS Smart Pump EMR-Integrated, continuing a seven-year track record of award-winning clinical excellence.


**San Clemente, Calif. February 7, 2024** —ICU Medical, Inc. , a leader in the development, manufacture and sale of innovative medical products used in infusion therapy, vascular access, and vital care applications, today announced its[Plum 360](https://www.icumed.com/products/infusion-therapy/infusion-pumps-and-software/plum-large-volume-pumps/plum-360/) smart infusion system has once again been recognized as the top-performing Smart Pump EMR-Integrated by[KLAS Research](https://klasresearch.com/) , a leading global health care research firm.


This Best in KLAS designation is based on feedback from thousands of healthcare providers throughout the United States and Canada and results from in-depth side-by-side comparisons of IV smart pump performance in multiple categories, including culture, loyalty, operations, product, relationship, and value. The[Best in KLAS Software and Services](https://klasresearch.com/report/2024-best-in-klas-awards-software-and-services/3413)


report recognizes these companies.


"At KLAS, we firmly believe that the voice of healthcare providers and payers is paramount. The Best in KLAS awards are based on extensive feedback and evaluations from healthcare professionals across the nation." Said Adam Gale, CEO of KLAS Research. "Winning a Best in KLAS award, therefore, is not just about recognition; it shows the trust and confidence that healthcare providers place in the winning vendors. It also helps validate each vendor's commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction. We are proud to recognize 2024's Best in KLAS award winners! Their unwavering dedication to improving patient outcomes is wonderfully inspiring."


The Plum 360 has won a Best in KLAS award every year that it has been eligible and is the only smart infusion system to win the Smart Pump EMR-Integrated category. This category award underscores how closely aligned the Plum 360 is with the latest industry guidelines for interoperable infusion systems, including the Institute for Safe Medication Practices (ISMP). Most importantly, this prestigious award is a direct reflection of ICU Medical’s customers’ success in reducing medication errors and improving the quality of care.


ICU Medical deployed the first interoperable IV system in 2008 and has helped lead the way in developing standards and products to enable healthcare systems to optimize the very significant safety and efficiency benefits that interoperable IV delivery offers. This foundation of interoperable excellence is being leveraged and enhanced in the new[ICU Medical IV Performance Platform](https://www.icumed.com/landing-pages/iv-performance-platform/)


.


"The honor of being recognized as Best in KLAS for a seventh year in a row speaks to ICU Medical’s core values." Said Dan Woolson, corporate vice president and general manager of ICU Medical Infusion Systems. "We are proud that our commitment to enhancing patient safety and care is reflected in our products, and look forward to continuing this commitment through our next-generation IV systems."


ICU Medical recently launched the IV Performance Platform consisting of the next-generation Plum device – the[Plum Duo™ infusion pump](https://www.icumed.com/products/infusion-therapy/infusion-pumps-and-software/plum-large-volume-pumps/plum-duo/)


, and[LifeShield™ IV safety software](https://www.icumed.com/products/infusion-therapy/infusion-pumps-and-software/infusion-safety-software/lifeshield/)


. This highly intuitive infusion system builds on the unique delivery capabilities of the Plum 360 and leverages more than 15 years of interoperability experience to bring healthcare facilities into a new era in infusion therapy.
