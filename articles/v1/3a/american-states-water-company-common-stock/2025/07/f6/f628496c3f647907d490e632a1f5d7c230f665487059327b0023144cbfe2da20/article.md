# Golden State Water Company Welcomes Gino DiCaro as Public Policy and Government Affairs Manager

Post


July 8, 2025


FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
*July 8, 2025*


**San Dimas, Calif.** – Golden State Water Company (Golden State Water) is pleased to announce the appointment of Gino DiCaro as Public Policy and Government Affairs Manager. DiCaro will lead strategic advocacy efforts and strengthen relationships with federal, state and local government agencies.


“I’m honored to join Golden State Water and contribute to its mission of delivering safe, reliable and high-quality water service to communities across California,” said Gino DiCaro. “I look forward to working with policymakers, regulators and community leaders to support sustainable water solutions and advocate for the needs of our customers.”


With more than 30 years of experience in public affairs, communications and policy advocacy, DiCaro brings a deep understanding of California’s legislative and regulatory landscape. He previously served as Vice President of Communications at the Wine Institute and held senior leadership roles at the California Manufacturers & Technology Association, where he advanced key policy initiatives and stakeholder engagement strategies.


“We’re pleased to welcome Gino DiCaro to Golden State Water as our new Public Policy and Government Affairs Manager,” said Paul Rowley, Senior Vice President at Golden State Water Company. “DiCaro brings nearly three decades of experience in public policy and communications, along with a deep understanding of California’s legislative environment. He will play a key role in representing Golden State Water on issues that impact our operations locally and statewide.”


In his new role, DiCaro will represent Golden State Water in advocacy efforts with elected officials and regulatory agencies, while providing strategic insight on evolving policy landscapes. He will also lead the implementation of the company’s public policy strategy and strengthen relationships with key government partners.


DiCaro’s appointment reflects Golden State Water’s continued commitment to proactive engagement with policymakers and community leaders across California.

About Golden State Water Company


Golden State Water is a wholly-owned subsidiary of American States Water Company (NYSE: AWR) and provides water service to approximately 1 million customers across ten counties in Northern, Coastal, and Southern California. American States Water Company also owns a contracted services subsidiary, American States Utility Services, Inc. (ASUS), which provides operations, maintenance, and construction management services for water and wastewater systems located on military bases throughout the country under 50-year privatization contracts with the U.S. government.
