# 908 Devices to Present at the Sidoti Small Cap Conference

BURLINGTON, Mass.–(BUSINESS WIRE)–


908 Devices Inc. (Nasdaq: MASS), a core small-cap growth company focused on purpose-built handheld chemical analysis tools for vital health, safety and defense tech applications, announced that CEO and Co-founder Kevin J. Knopp and CFO Joe Griffith will virtually present and host investor meetings at the Sidoti Small Cap Conference on Wednesday, March 18, 2026.


The Company presentation is scheduled to begin at 1:45 p.m. Eastern Time. A live audio webcast of the event with accompanying slides can be accessed here:[https://sidoti.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_rb94-xr9QPyFewc5E6MmJQ](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fsidoti.zoom.us%2Fwebinar%2Fregister%2FWN_rb94-xr9QPyFewc5E6MmJQ&esheet=54448476&newsitemid=20260316329537&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fsidoti.zoom.us%2Fwebinar%2Fregister%2FWN_rb94-xr9QPyFewc5E6MmJQ&index=1&md5=7fbcb5dbf3891907b3730b0dae62df96) . Interested parties may access an archived webcast of the presentation on the “Investors” section of the company website at:[www.908devices.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.908devices.com&esheet=54448476&newsitemid=20260316329537&lan=en-US&anchor=www.908devices.com&index=2&md5=c0c9af58e71560411c5eb91bd9045bd3) .


**About 908 Devices**


908 Devices is revolutionizing chemical analysis with its simple handheld devices, addressing life-altering applications. The Company’s devices are used at the point-of-need to interrogate unknown and invisible materials and provide quick, actionable answers in vital health, safety and defense tech applications, addressing the fentanyl and illicit drug crisis, toxic carcinogen exposure, and global security threats. The Company designs and manufactures innovative products that bring together the power of complementary analytical technologies, software automation, and machine learning.
