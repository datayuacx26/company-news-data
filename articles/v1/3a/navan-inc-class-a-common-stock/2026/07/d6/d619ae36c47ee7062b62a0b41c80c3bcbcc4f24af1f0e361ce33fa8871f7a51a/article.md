# Microsoft Ignite 2026: Complete travel guide

## Key takeaways

- San Francisco's federal lodging per diem for November often falls well below conference-week hotel rates near the venue, so budget for the gap or consider neighborhoods slightly farther out.
- Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART) from San Francisco International Airport (SFO) to downtown takes 30–40 minutes, making it the most reliable transfer for most solo arrivals.

- Cap your agenda at three to four sessions per day and leave gaps for hallway conversations, where partner deals and product evaluations actually happen.
- Following up within 48 hours of the conference gives you the best chance of turning new connections into lasting business relationships.


Microsoft Ignite 2026 will bring tens of thousands of IT professionals, developers, and business leaders to Moscone Center for four days of product roadmaps, hands-on labs, and partner conversations.\[1\] The sessions are why you register, and careful trip planning gives you the best chance of leaving with a clear Azure strategy and a full calendar of follow-ups. This guide walks through the full attendee lifecycle for Microsoft Ignite 2026, from booking your hotel to filing expenses after you return home.


## What is Microsoft Ignite and who is it for?


Microsoft Ignite is Microsoft's flagship technical conference for IT professionals, developers, business leaders, and partners. The 2026 edition runs November 17–20 at Moscone Center in San Francisco, with badge pickup available November 16.\[1\]\[2\]


The attendee mix skews enterprise: Cloud architects evaluating Azure migrations, security admins assessing Copilot and Defender roadmaps, developers building on Microsoft 365 and Power Platform, and ISV partners looking for co-sell opportunities. Microsoft reported more than 200,000 registrants and more than 20,000 in-person attendees at Ignite 2025, with more than 400 sessions, demos, and expert-led labs across the program.\[3\] The 2026 catalog will publish closer to the event, but the scale and technical depth are consistent year to year.


If you're deciding whether the trip is worth approving internally, frame it around outcomes: The specific product evaluations you need to complete, the certifications you plan to earn, or the partner meetings you'll hold on-site. The trip is easiest to justify when it's tied to concrete business outcomes.


### What's the value of attending Microsoft Ignite 2026


Ignite is where Microsoft shows what's shipping next, not what shipped last quarter. For IT leaders, that means direct access to product teams, hands-on labs, and the context you can't get from a webinar. For developers and architects, it means seeing Copilot, Azure, and security features in production-ready demos before your organization commits budget.


Beyond the official agenda, Ignite week includes key programming:


- **November 16:** Badge pickup and pre-day labs for early arrivals
- **November 17:** Opening keynote and core session tracks
- **November 18:** Welcome reception and partner networking in the Hub and Meeting Center
- **November 19:** Celebration event and evening programming
- **Throughout the week:** Hub sessions, Meeting Center appointments, and unofficial partner happy hours (lists can be found on community sites)


The business case is not attendance for its own sake. It's walking away with a prioritized adoption roadmap, three to five partner conversations that advance a deal, and the technical clarity to brief your team when you return. That outcome is much more likely when the logistics are handled before you arrive.

### Microsoft Ignite hotel pricing


Navan analyzed anonymized hotel booking patterns near Moscone Center during the November 2025 Microsoft Ignite conference week (check-ins November 15–23), which can be helpful for 2026 planning: \[8\]


- **Median hotel rate:** During conference week, the median nightly rate near Moscone was $687 — about 70% higher than a typical November week in the same area ($405).
- **Stay length:** Business travelers stayed an average of 3 nights in 4.1-star hotels.
- **Checking in and out:** Most attendees checked in on Monday and checked out on Friday, suggesting a Monday-to-Friday conference stay.
- **Late bookings:** 40.7% of hotel bookings were made within 2 weeks of check-in, including 20.4% booked within the final 7 days.


When to book (median nightly rate by lead time):


- **2+ months ahead:** $481 median
- **1–2 months ahead:** $437 median (lowest rate)
- **2–4 weeks:** $856 median (highest)
- **1–2 weeks:** $799 median
- **Last minute (within 7 days):** $664 median


Booking one to two months before check-in is the most reliable strategy based on Navan data. Although last-minute bookings may cost less than the highest-priced booking windows, inventory is usually low and presents a risk. If Microsoft offers an official room block, compare that rate against open inventory before you commit, because the block isn't always the cheapest option.

If you're crossing multiple time zones, consider arriving the evening before sessions begin.

## Set a realistic San Francisco budget before you go


San Francisco is one of the most expensive U.S. cities for business travel, and a flagship Microsoft event pushes lodging higher still. Build your budget before finance asks for it. Use the federal per diem as your baseline, then adjust for conference-week pricing.


### GSA per diem for San Francisco


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. For San Francisco in November 2026, the figures are:


- **$364 per day:** Includes $272 per night for lodging and $92 per day for meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** $23 for breakfast, $26 for lunch, $43 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (deduct conference-provided meals from your meal claim as applicable)
- **First and last travel days:** M&IE is typically reimbursed at **75%** of the daily rate when you're in transit\[6\]


Note: GSA updates rates each October, so verify the current figure at gsa.gov before you travel.


Conference-week hotel rates near Moscone routinely exceed the federal lodging cap. Navan booking data from the November 2025 Ignite week showed a $687 median nightly rate near the venue, roughly $415 above the $272 GSA lodging rate.\[8\] Budget for that gap or plan to stay in a neighborhood where rates are lower and commute via Muni or BART.


### Other budgeting considerations


Before you fly, set a daily spend target so you're not hunting down receipts on your flight home.


- **Conference registration:** Conference passes start at $2,325, with Hub-only costing $1,625 and a free Digital pass. (Verify current prices at ignite.microsoft.com before you book.)\[1\]
- **Conference-provided meals:** Reduce your M&IE claim for these days, tracking which lunches or receptions are included in registration.\[4\]
- **Airport transfer:** Taking the BART from SFO to downtown San Francisco costs about $11, and rideshares run $35–$50+ with surge pricing during peak arrival windows.\[5\]\[6\]
- **Client dinners:** Meals near Moscone typically run $75–$150 per person at sit-down restaurants in SoMa and the Financial District.
- **After-hours events:** These may require cover charges, hosted-bar minimums, or last-minute rideshare surges, so budget for the unofficial partner circuit.
- **Merchandise and swag:** Although easy to forget in a per diem spreadsheet, swag and merch show up on personal cards by day three.


## Build your agenda before you go


The best Microsoft Ignite itineraries balance structure and flexibility. Plan your priority sessions before you arrive, but leave room for the Hub, the Meeting Center, and the conversations that happen between them.


Before you fly to San Francisco:


- **Build your session schedule:** Star your must-see talks in the Microsoft Ignite app and at ignite.microsoft.com, and leave lower-priority sessions for on-demand viewing later.
- **Book meetings early:** Reach out one to two weeks ahead with a short LinkedIn or Ignite message and a specific 15-minute meeting request.
- **Prepare your logistics:** Download the app, set your out-of-office message, and confirm your calendar includes Moscone locations for each meeting and session.
- **Make networking easy:** Decide how you'll exchange contact information (QR code, NFC, or LinkedIn), and add a quick note after each conversation, such as “Met at Ignite, discussed Copilot rollout.”

## What to wear and pack for Microsoft Ignite


Microsoft Ignite skews sharper than a startup hackathon but softer than a banking conference. Think chinos or dark jeans with a collared shirt or blouse, optional blazer for client dinners, and comfortable shoes you can stand in for hours. If you're presenting to executives or hosting partners, pack one notch dressier than you would for expo-floor browsing.


San Francisco in mid-November runs cool, with highs around 61–64°F, lows around 49–51°F, and a one-in-four chance of rain on any given day.\[7\] Layers beat a single heavy coat, and a light rain jacket fits easily in a day bag.


### Conference essentials


Microsoft Ignite means long days on your feet, back-to-back sessions, and plenty of time away from your hotel. These essentials can help you stay comfortable and focused throughout the conference:


- **Rotate your shoes:** Bring two comfortable pairs to reduce foot fatigue after long days at the convention center.
- **Pack a high-capacity battery:** A 20,000mAh (or larger) power bank keeps your phone charged for your badge, schedule, maps, and networking.
- **Carry a reusable water bottle:** The air in conference venues is infamously dry, so prioritize staying hydrated during back-to-back sessions.
- **Bring hand sanitizer:** Thousands of attendees share the same spaces all week, so it's an easy addition to your conference bag.
- **Don't forget earplugs:** Whether you're attending loud evening events, are a light sleeper, or end up in a noisy hotel, you'll be glad you packed them.
- **Pack a light layer:** San Francisco evenings cool down quickly, especially once the fog rolls in, so layers are essential.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for the conversations that matter


The most valuable moments at Microsoft Ignite aren't always on the agenda. Plan your priority sessions, but leave time for product demos, partner events, and the conversations that happen between them.


- **Use the Ignite app:** Check session capacity, room changes, and navigation across Moscone in real time.
- **Attend the official events:** The Welcome Reception and Celebration\[1\] are two of the easiest places to meet attendees outside the session schedule.
- **Watch for partner events:** Many exhibitors host demos, networking events, and happy hours throughout the week, with details announced closer to Ignite.


### Networking that turns a conference into a pipeline


Treat networking as a before-during-after system, not something that happens spontaneously on the expo floor.


Before the conference, build your target list, research recent announcements, and send personalized outreach two weeks out with a specific request. During the conference, ask meaningful questions and take notes after you engage with a contact covering what you discussed.


Within 48 hours of the conference ending, send a short message referencing what you discussed, connect on LinkedIn if you haven't yet, and suggest a follow-up meeting. Block two hours on your calendar for follow-up before you leave San Francisco — reply rates drop sharply after 48 hours.\[11\]


## What and where to eat during Microsoft Ignite 2026


Long conference days are easier when you plan meals ahead instead of defaulting to the closest food line. Eating a nutritious breakfast before you arrive, carrying a refillable water bottle, packing a protein-rich snack, and planning lunch or dinner ahead can help you stay energized without missing a beat.


Here are a few reliable options within walking distance of Moscone:


- **Client dinner:** PAZZIA on 3rd Street in SoMa handles business dinners with Italian entrees and private-table options a short walk from Moscone.\[9\]
- **Team meal:** SoMa Social on 7th Street works for group dinners without the formality of a white-tablecloth room.\[10\]
- **Quick fuel:** Yerba Buena Gardens cafés are located steps from the venue for coffee and lunch between sessions. In-venue options exist, but lines spike at peak lunchtime.

## Keep your energy up and avoid conference burnout


While you're at Ignite, your goal shouldn't be attending every session. Instead, focus on remaining engaged from the opening keynote through the final day. Give yourself time to recharge so you can make the most of the conversations and opportunities that matter.


- **Build in downtime:** Even a 15-minute break between sessions can help you reset.
- **Choose evening events selectively:** Save your energy for the meetings and sessions that matter most.
- **Keep water within reach:** Staying hydrated throughout the day is one of the easiest ways to avoid the afternoon slump.


## Departure day: Late checkout, lounges, and making your flight


A little planning on your final night can make departure day much less stressful. Pack before you go to bed and charge your devices so you can focus on checking out, getting to the airport, and catching your flight.


- **Ask about late checkout:** Request it when you check in and confirm it again the night before. Loyalty status can help, and Navan Edge's 24/7 human travel experts can request late checkout if you have an evening flight.
- **Take advantage of lounge access:** Both Terminal 1 and the International Terminal at SFO offer lounges where you can work or relax before boarding.
- **Leave extra airport time:** Conference-week traffic and security lines are often busier than usual, so aim to arrive at least two hours before a domestic flight and three hours before an international departure.


## After Microsoft Ignite: Expenses and re-entry


A few minutes of follow-up before you settle back into your regular routine can save time later and help you get more value from your conference.


- **Submit your expenses promptly:** Rideshares, meals, and conference purchases are much easier to reconcile while they're still fresh in your mind.
- **Explain any exceptions:** If your hotel exceeded your company's per-diem or travel policy, include a brief note for your approver when you submit your expenses.
- **Keep receipts organized:** If you're traveling internationally, save receipts in their original currency and follow your employer's reimbursement policy for currency conversion.
