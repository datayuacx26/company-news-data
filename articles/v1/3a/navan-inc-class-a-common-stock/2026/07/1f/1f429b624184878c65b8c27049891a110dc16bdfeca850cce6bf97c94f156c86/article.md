# EDUCAUSE 2026 guide to travel, hotels and on-the-ground tips

EDUCAUSE 2026 brings campus IT leaders, higher ed executives, and edtech vendors to the Colorado Convention Center for four days of sessions, exhibits, and community events.\[1\] The difference between attending and getting real value often comes down to what happens before, between, and after the sessions. A well-planned week can leave you with clearer architecture decisions, stronger campus partnerships, and concrete next steps — without the reimbursement backlog and conference fatigue that often follow a rushed trip.


## What is EDUCAUSE and who is it for?


EDUCAUSE Annual Conference is the flagship gathering for higher education technology professionals and solution providers, hosted by EDUCAUSE.\[1\] The 2026 event is in-person from Sept. 29–Oct. 2 at the Colorado Convention Center in Denver.\[1\]\[2\]


The audience includes campus leadership and educators, including CIOs and CTOs evaluating enterprise systems, CISOs assessing identity and security roadmaps, instructional technology directors planning AI and Learning Management System (LMS) upgrades, and vendor teams working the exhibit floor toward signed agreements. EDUCAUSE anticipates that more than 4,000 technology leaders and professionals will attend.\[3\]\[4\]


If you're pitching your organization's leaders on the trip, tie attendance to outcomes you can measure. Think validating a student information-system migration path before the next academic year, meeting vendors in the EDUCAUSE Commons with a scored evaluation rubric, or aligning your security team on identity standards before locking in budgets.


## What makes EDUCAUSE 2026 worth the trip


EDUCAUSE is where higher ed technology strategy gets stress-tested against peer institutions, not just vendor slide decks. For campus leaders, that means direct access to solving the problems you face when it comes to an LMS, identity, and data governance. For IT directors, it means walking the exhibit floor with a short list instead of rebuilding one from webinar replays.


The official schedule is only part of the value. Plan time for these onsite moments:


- **Explore the EDUCAUSE Commons:** Browse sponsor booths, poster sessions, refreshments, and lounge spaces for informal meetings.\[5\]
- **Attend the general sessions:** Hear the opening keynotes, including the EDUCAUSE Top 10 and featured speakers.\[1\]
- **Join the Welcome Reception:** Meet fellow attendees on Tuesday, Sept. 29.\[5\]
- **Stop by the networking receptions:** Connect with peers in the Commons on Wednesday and Thursday afternoons.\[5\]
- **Take advantage of offsite community events:** Attend evening programming across Denver on Sept. 30 and Oct. 1.\[5\]
- **Join a Community Group meetup:** Connect with EDUCAUSE member communities during in-person gatherings alongside the official schedule.\[6\]


What you accomplish at EDUCAUSE — a vendor short list validated against peer feedback, an identity roadmap your CISO can defend, relationships with campus colleagues you'll call all year — is what justifies the travel budget. That value only shows up if the logistics are handled before you land.

## Set your Denver budget before traveling


EDUCAUSE week spending spans lodging, meals, DEN transfers, client dinners, and offsite community events. Be sure you compare your budget to federal per diem and what downtown Denver actually charges in late September.


### Federal per diem for Denver


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, the following October rates for FY2026 applied to Denver (Adams, Arapahoe, Denver, and Jefferson counties):


- **$307 per day:** Includes $215 per night for lodging and $92 per day for meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** Includes $23 for breakfast, $26 for lunch, $38 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (deduct conference-provided meals from your meal claim as applicable)
- **First and last days of travel:** An M&IE allowance of $69 (i.e. 75% of the daily M&IE rate) for travel days


EDUCAUSE straddles the 2026/2027 federal fiscal year, so verify current figures at[gsa.gov](http://gsa.gov/) before traveling. Updated FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) rates are expected to be published in mid-August 2026.\[7\]


During EDUCAUSE week, conference hotels near the Colorado Convention Center often exceed the federal lodging rate for Denver ($215 per night), so budget for the difference or confirm your organization's reimbursement policy before booking.


### Other expenses to consider


Build these costs into your travel budget before booking so approvals, reimbursement, and expense reporting go more smoothly.


- **Conference registration:** A full conference pass for EDUCAUSE members starts at $1,185 and nonmember pricing starts at $1,739; verify current tiers at the official event website before booking.\[8\]
- **Preconference programming:** Separate registration is required for preconference sessions on Tuesday, Sept. 29.\[5\]
- **Online conference access:** Access to the Oct. 14–15 online event is included with a full in-person conference pass.\[1\]
- **Airport transfer:** A rideshare from Denver International Airport (DEN) runs $50–$80 one-way. Regional Transportation District (RTD) University of Colorado A Line fare is $10.50 and requires a short walk or free MallRide connection downtown.\[9\]\[10\]
- **Client dinners:** Downtown steakhouses and private dining rooms near the convention center typically run $75–$175 per person.
- **After-hours community events:** Offsite meetups and partner receptions may carry cover charges or hosted-bar minimums, so reserve a small budget buffer.


## Plan your agenda with flexibility


EDUCAUSE's multi-track agenda makes it tempting to schedule every hour of the day. Prioritize the sessions that matter most, but leave time to explore the Commons, stop for an unscheduled vendor conversation, or simply take a break. Even a short walk along the 16th Street Mall or a quiet coffee between sessions can help you recharge before the next round of meetings.


Before EDUCAUSE begins:


- **Logistics:** Install the EDUCAUSE Events app, turn on session alerts, set out-of-office, and pin the Colorado Convention Center address on your calendar.
- **Sessions:** Favorite must-see sessions in the EDUCAUSE Events mobile app; popular time slots fill once the full agenda is published in late July.\[4\]\[11\]
- **Meetings:** Email campus colleagues, vendors, or community peers roughly two weeks out with a concrete 15-minute meeting request.
- **Contacts:** Pick QR, NFC, or LinkedIn with a note and capture one line of context per person while you're still in the hallway.

## What to pack and wear at EDUCAUSE


EDUCAUSE attendees tend toward higher-ed IT business casual. Plan to wear collared shirts or polished blouses, chinos or slacks, comfortable closed-toe shoes, and an optional blazer if you're presenting or hosting a vendor dinner. Campus leaders often dress one notch sharper for general sessions, while exhibit-floor browsing is more relaxed.


Denver in late September and early October is typically sunny, dry, and highly variable. Expect daytime highs in the low-to-mid 70s°F and overnight lows in the 40s°F, with cooler mornings, warm afternoons, and the occasional early-season cold snap.\[12\] You'll spend much of the day inside the convention center, but Denver's mile-high elevation, dry climate, and wide temperature swings make layers, sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle worthwhile.


### Conference essentials


If you're traveling from lower elevations, begin hydrating well before your trip. Denver's mile-high altitude and dry climate can contribute to fatigue, headaches, and dehydration during your first day or two, making water, sunscreen, and layers just as important as comfortable shoes.


- **Rotate footwear:** Downtown blocks and convention-center floors are tough on your feet, so pack two comfortable pairs of walking shoes.
- **Stay hydrated:** Bring a refillable water bottle and drink more water than you normally would — the combination of elevation and dry air catches many visitors off guard.
- **Carry a high-capacity power bank:** Badge scanning, scheduling, note-taking, and networking apps can drain your phone before the day ends.
- **Protect your skin:** Pack lip balm, moisturizer, and sunscreen. Even on mild days, Denver's high elevation makes UV exposure stronger than many visitors expect.
- **Pack for temperature swings:** Bring light layers for cool mornings, warm afternoons, and heavily air-conditioned breakout rooms.
- **Pack basic wellness items:** Hand sanitizer and throat lozenges can come in handy after long days in crowded session rooms.


## Getting from the airport to the Colorado Convention Center


Most attendees fly into Denver International Airport (DEN), about 25 miles from the Colorado Convention Center.\[9\]\[10\] Despite the distance looking manageable on a map, the drive to downtown typically takes 30–45 minutes, so choose your transportation before you land rather than deciding at baggage claim.


If your schedule allows, arrive the evening before the opening general session. You'll avoid the morning rush for badge pickup, have time to adjust to Denver's altitude, and start the conference rested instead of rushing from the airport to your first session.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for the EDUCAUSE Commons and hallway conversations


Some of the most useful EDUCAUSE conversations happen between sessions — in the Commons lounges, at poster presentations, and during evening community events. Build those opportunities into your schedule instead of treating them as leftover time.


- **EDUCAUSE Events mobile app:** Track room changes, session capacity, and your personal schedule across the convention center.\[11\]
- **General sessions:** Plan around the Wednesday and Thursday morning anchor sessions before locking in your breakout schedule.\[5\]
- **EDUCAUSE Commons:** Explore exhibits, poster sessions, refreshments, and networking receptions throughout the main program days.\[5\]\[6\]
- **Evening programming:** Offsite community events run Sept. 30–Oct. 1; watch the app and community channels for locations and timing.\[5\]


### Network with a follow-up plan


Treat EDUCAUSE networking as a process that starts before you arrive and continues after you leave.


- **Before Denver:** Identify 10 to 15 people worth meeting — campus peers, vendors on your shortlist, speakers on your must-see list — and send a short, specific meeting request about two weeks out.
- **On the floor:** Lead with institutional context (“We're evaluating LMS migration options this fiscal year”) rather than a vendor pitch. Capture one memorable detail per contact in your notes app before you move to the next booth.
- **Back at your hotel each night:** Draft quick follow-ups with next steps while names are fresh and send them within 48 hours.
- **Before leaving Denver:** Block time on your Friday calendar for follow-ups before your flight. Reply rates fall once you're back in back-to-back campus meetings.


## What to eat at EDUCAUSE 2026


Whether you're grabbing breakfast before the opening keynote, fitting in lunch between sessions, or meeting colleagues for dinner, planning your meals helps you stay energized throughout the conference. Pack a reusable water bottle and a protein snack so you're prepared if your schedule runs long.


When you need a sit-down meal, these Denver spots fit different EDUCAUSE moments:


- **Client dinner:** Guard and Grace handles business dinners with private areas for groups up to 70, a few blocks from the convention center on 14th Street.\[13\]
- **Team meal:** Stout Street Social works for group dinners with shareable plates and private dining for up to 100.\[13\]
- **Private dining/executive lunch:** Water Grill in LoDo offers customizable private rooms for groups of 10–200 and is steps from the convention center.\[14\]
- **One-on-one or small group:** Located in the historic Colorado National Bank building, range restaurant offers vault rooms for intimate meetings and chef-driven New American menus.\[15\]

## Finish strong on departure day


The conference doesn't end when you leave the Colorado Convention Center. Before heading to DEN, take a few minutes to organize your notes, submit any urgent expenses, and prepare for a smoother return to campus.


- **Pack before bed:** Charge devices, lay out travel clothes, and check drawers and outlets for badges and chargers.
- **Request late checkout:** For evening flights, ask at check-in and reconfirm the night prior, or let Navan Edge's human travel experts request it on your behalf.
- **Build airport buffer:** DEN is generally busy, but more so after major conferences, so allow at least two hours for domestic departures and three for international.
- **File expenses early:** Upload receipts while line items are fresh; note any lodging above your institution's per-diem ceiling when you submit.
- **Use lounge time:** If your card includes airport lounge access, sort notes and receipts there instead of at the gate.
- **Block debrief time:** Reserve an hour on your calendar to email new contacts and brief your team on EDUCAUSE takeaways before regular work resumes.


## Make your EDUCAUSE 2026 trip count


A successful week in Denver at EDUCAUSE 2026 is more than the sessions you attend. Stay close to the convention center, budget ahead, make time for networking, and follow up before you head home. Those small decisions can make it easier to return with stronger relationships, clearer priorities, and ideas you can immediately share with your team.
