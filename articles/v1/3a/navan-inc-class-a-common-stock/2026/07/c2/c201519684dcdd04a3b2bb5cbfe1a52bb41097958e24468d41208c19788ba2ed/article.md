# How to plan and book a successful trip to Workday Rising 2026

## Key takeaways


Workday Rising 2026 runs Oct. 12–15 at The Venetian Resort and Caesars Forum in Las Vegas. This guide walks through Strip hotels, LAS transfers, GSA budgeting, Expo strategy, and departure logistics for the four-day program.


- Compare hotel availability on The Venetian campus, central Strip properties, and north Strip options before you default to the closest or cheapest room rate.
- Conference-week Strip rates often run well above the federal per diem lodging rate, so budget for the gap or book through Workday's official housing portal.
- [Navan Edge](https://navan.com/product/navan-edge) coordinates flights, hotels, and restaurant reservations in one chat when Rising room blocks fill up or there's less availability near The Venetian.
- Choose high-priority breakouts and preserve white space for Expo demos, Braindates, and Hands-On Labs.
- Send follow-up notes within two business days while product demos and tenant questions are still fresh.


For four days in mid-October, Workday Rising 2026 will bring HR, finance, and IT leaders to Las Vegas for keynotes, product sessions, hands-on learning, and conversations with peers and Workday experts.\[1\] The choices you make before the conference, including where you stay and how you structure your agenda, will shape what you take home from Workday Rising. With a thoughtful plan, you can return with clearer priorities, stronger professional connections, and practical next steps.


## What is Workday Rising and who is it for?


Workday Rising is Workday's flagship U.S. conference for human capital management (HCM), finance, and IT leaders exploring cloud ERP, analytics, and AI on the Workday platform.\[1\] The event runs Oct. 12–15 at The Venetian Resort and Caesars Forum in Las Vegas.\[1\]\[2\]


The conference typically attracts around 30,000 attendees, bringing together executives and practitioners from across the Workday ecosystem.\[3\] Expect to see CHROs and HRIS directors planning workforce and talent strategy, CFOs and controllers evaluating financial close and planning modules, CIOs and integration leads assessing platform extensibility, and partner teams supporting customer deployments. Workday Rising welcomes existing customers as well as invited prospects, sponsors, analysts, and accredited media.\[3\]


The Workday Rising 2026 Session Catalog is live, with hundreds of sessions available to browse before the conference.\[18\] You can finalize your agenda and reserve eligible sessions in September once the Session Scheduler opens.


If you're building the business case for attending, identify the outcomes you want to achieve before you arrive — whether that's evaluating new AI capabilities, comparing partner solutions, or meeting with peers to validate your roadmap.


## What makes Workday Rising 2026 worth the trip


Workday Rising is where the company's product direction meets the practitioners who run it daily — not a replay of release notes you could read online. For HR and finance leaders, that means keynotes, roadmap sessions, and customer-led stories you can take back to your steering committee. For technical teams, it means Hands-On Labs and Express Labs where you test features in a guided tenant environment.\[1\]\[3\]\[6\]


Some of the highest-value experiences happen outside the breakout rooms:


- **Workday Rising Expo:** Explore partner booths, product demonstrations, and sponsor exhibits throughout the Venetian Expo Center.
- **Keynotes and product strategy sessions:** Hear Workday executives share product updates, customer stories, and the company's long-term vision for HCM, Financial Management, and AI.
- **Braindates:** Join peer-led conversations to exchange ideas and learn how other organizations are approaching similar challenges.
- **Hands-On Labs and Express Labs:** Test new features in guided and self-paced technical environments with support from Workday experts.
- **Welcome Reception:** Connect with other attendees over drinks and light refreshments on Monday evening.
- **Customer Appreciation Party:** Continue networking at Wednesday's evening event with food, drinks, and entertainment.
- **Official hotel shuttles:** Take advantage of complimentary transportation between participating conference hotels and the Venetian Expo Center.


The value of Workday Rising comes from what happens after the conference. A clear plan, stronger relationships with partners and peers, and answers to your biggest questions can all help move projects forward once you're back at work. Planning your travel, agenda, and meetings before you arrive makes it much easier to spend your time on those outcomes instead of conference logistics.

### Workday Rising hotel pricing


October falls within Las Vegas's lower federal lodging tier, but a flagship enterprise conference still drives demand around the Venetian Expo Center. Hotels within walking distance often sell out or raise rates as the event approaches, so it's worth comparing conference hotel blocks with current market prices before you book.


Navan analyzed anonymized hotel booking patterns near the Venetian Expo Center during October 2025 — the most recent October conference week in Navan booking data. Although Workday Rising is making its debut in Las Vegas for 2026, these figures provide a useful benchmark for conference-week pricing on the Strip.\[16\]


- **Median hotel rate:** During conference week, the median nightly rate near the venue was $329 — about 60% higher than a typical October week in the same area (median of $206).
- **Stay length:** Business travelers stayed an average of 2.8 nights.
- **Check-in pattern:** Monday was the most common arrival day, and Thursday the most common departure day.
- **Late bookings:** Roughly 36% of bookings were made within two weeks of check-in.


What stands out: According to Navan data, travelers who booked 1–2 months before check-in paid a median of $286 per night, while waiting until the final 1–2 weeks pushed the median to $360.\[16\] That's a difference of about $74 per night, making early booking a safer strategy.


### Workday Rising flight trends


The main airport serving Workday Rising attendees is Harry Reid International Airport (LAS). Navan flight data from a comparable October 2025 Las Vegas conference week shows 3,691 business flights into the city, with nearly 10% arriving from international destinations.\[16\] The busiest origin cities were San Francisco, New York, London, Newark, and Boston.


Consider arriving a day early, to give yourself time to settle into your hotel, pick up your badge, and ease into the week. You'll start Monday focused on the conference instead of worrying about flight delays, luggage, or racing across the Strip.

## Set a realistic budget for Las Vegas before you go


Your conference pass is only one part of the cost of attending Workday Rising. Budget for conference-week hotel rates, meals outside conference programming, airport transportation, client dinners, and other business travel expenses before you book.


### GSA per diem for Las Vegas


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, the following FY2026 rates apply to Las Vegas:


- **$212 per day:** Includes $126 per night for lodging and $86 per day for meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** $21 for breakfast, $23 for lunch, $36 for dinner, and $6 for incidentals (deduct conference-provided meals from your meal claim as applicable)
- **First and last travel days:** M&IE is typically reimbursed at 75% of the daily rate ($64.50) when you're in transit


Updated FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) rates will be published in mid-August 2026, so verify the current figures at gsa.gov before traveling.\[7\]


Conference-week hotel rates within walking distance of the Venetian Expo Center often exceed the federal lodging cap. Navan booking data from a comparable October 2025 Las Vegas conference week showed a median nightly rate of $329 near the venue — more than $200 above the FY2026 GSA lodging rate of $126.\[16\] Budget for that difference or consider staying farther along the Strip and using the official conference shuttles.


### Other budget considerations


Before you travel, estimate your daily spending so there are fewer surprises when it's time to submit your expense report.


- **Conference registration:** Full Experience Passes start at $2,395; verify current pricing at rising.workday.com before registering.\[8\]
- **Conference-provided meals:** Registration includes the Welcome Reception, breakfast bites and lunch on Oct. 13–14, the Customer Appreciation Party, and brunch on Oct. 15. Adjust your meal reimbursement accordingly.\[3\]
- **Airport transportation:** Taxi fares to The Venetian are roughly $29 plus the airport pickup fee, while rideshares typically cost $15–$40 depending on demand and surge pricing.\[9\]\[10\]
- **Client dinners:** Restaurants on the Strip commonly run $85–$200 per person for business dinners.
- **After-hours networking:** Partner receptions, team outings, and other unofficial events can add to your travel budget beyond the meals included with registration.

## What to pack and wear at Workday Rising


Workday Rising calls for business casual: collared shirts or polished blouses, slacks or chinos, comfortable closed-toe shoes, and a light layer for over-air-conditioned ballrooms. Presenters and client-dinner hosts often dress one notch sharper, and Expo-floor browsing is more relaxed.\[3\]


Las Vegas in October runs warm in the afternoon with highs around 85°F (29°C) and nights that can dip to 55°F (13°C).\[3\] You'll move between climate-controlled halls and outdoor Strip walks, so pack breathable daytime fabrics and a sweater or blazer for evening events.


### Conference essentials


Everyone remembers their computer and charger, but don't forget to bring these must-pack items:


- **Two pairs of comfortable shoes:** Convention flooring and Strip distances punish single-shoe days.
- **Portable charger:** Session Scheduler, Braindate notes, and badge scans drain batteries by mid-afternoon.
- **Snacks:** Pack a few protein bars or other easy snacks for the gaps between sessions, long Expo visits, or client meetings that run through lunch.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Desert air dehydrates faster than you notice between sessions.
- **Throat lozenges and hand sanitizer:** Crowded keynotes and Expo aisles add up over four days.
- **Light layer:** Breakout rooms run cold even when the Strip is warm.
- **Sunscreen:** Outdoor walks between properties add up under strong afternoon sun.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for the Expo and networking


Some of the most valuable conversations at Workday Rising happen outside the breakout rooms. Leave time to explore the Expo, attend product demonstrations, and connect with other attendees between sessions.


- **Workday Rising Expo:** Explore sponsor booths, product demonstrations, and innovation showcases throughout the Venetian Expo Center.\[1\]\[6\]
- **Braindates:** Join peer-led discussions with other attendees to exchange ideas and compare experiences; reservations open with the Session Scheduler in September.\[3\]
- **Hands-On Labs and Express Labs:** Try new features in guided or drop-in technical sessions led by Workday experts.\[3\]
- **Meetups and product demos:** Take advantage of smaller sessions and informal conversations between keynotes and breakouts.\[3\]
- **Session reservations:** Some sessions require advance reservations, while others are first come, first served; check the Session Scheduler for details when it opens.\[3\]


## Make networking part of your plan


A few thoughtful conversations can be more valuable than squeezing in one more breakout session.


- **Before you travel:** Make a short list of customers, partners, colleagues, or peers you'd like to meet, and reach out about two weeks before the conference to schedule time together.
- **During the conference:** After each conversation, jot down a quick note about what you discussed and any agreed-upon next steps. You'll thank yourself when it's time to follow up.
- **At the end of each day:** Spend a few minutes drafting follow-up emails while the conversations are still fresh, then send them within a day or two of returning home.


Finally, leave yourself some breathing room. A packed agenda looks productive on paper, but a short break between sessions often leaves you with more energy for the conversations and ideas you'll remember long after the conference ends.


## What to eat at Workday Rising 2026


Workday provides meals throughout the conference — from the Welcome Reception and breakfast bites to conference lunches, the Customer Appreciation Party, and the closing brunch — so you're not paying for every meal on the Strip.\[3\]


Client dinners and team outings usually fill the remaining gaps. For sit-down meals outside the venue, try these options on the Strip:


- **Client dinner:** Delmonico Steakhouse at The Venetian handles executive dinners with private rooms and classic steakhouse service steps from the Expo halls.\[12\]
- **Team meal:** Fogo de Chão in the Grand Canal Shoppes works for group dining with Brazilian table service and private rooms for larger teams.\[13\]
- **Private dining / executive lunch:** Cañonita near the Venetian Expo Center offers private and semi-private rooms for groups of 10–250 with customizable menus.\[14\]
- **One-on-one or small group:** Sugarcane on Restaurant Row is great for intimate meetings, thanks to its dedicated private dining room near the convention center.\[15\]


If a six-person partner dinner is needed mid-week, Navan Edge can reserve Las Vegas restaurants in the same chat as your hotel and LAS flights.


## Finish strong on departure day


Your time at Workday Rising isn't over until you're through LAS and back to your normal day-to-day work. Here's what to do before checkout:


- **Pack the night before:** Charge your gear, lay out your travel clothes, check the room for swag, chargers, and anything else you may have left behind.
- **Confirm late checkout:** Whether or not you remembered to request late checkout when checking in, check with the front desk or have Navan Edge coordinate on your behalf.
- **Arrive early at the airport:** After the conference you can expect long lines at LAS, so allow at least two hours for domestic flights and three hours for international.
- **Submit expenses early:** Upload receipts before your time at the symposium blurs together, and flag lodging above your per-diem cap when you file.
- **Visit the airport lounge:** Get quiet time to sort notes and receipts before boarding if your airline ticket includes lounge access.
- **Calendar a debrief:** Reserve an hour to message new contacts and summarize conference outcomes before regular work resumes.

Workday Rising 2026 rewards attendees who arrive with a plan. Book the right hotel, set a realistic budget, prioritize the sessions that matter most, and leave room for the Expo and networking. Those choices make it easier to head home with stronger connections, clearer priorities, and ideas you can put into practice right away.
