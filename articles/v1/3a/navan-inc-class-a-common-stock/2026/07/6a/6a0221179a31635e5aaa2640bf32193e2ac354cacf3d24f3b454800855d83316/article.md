# Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo 2026: Hotels, travel, and attendee tips

## Key takeaways


Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo 2026 runs Oct. 19–22 at the Walt Disney World Swan and Dolphin Resorts in Lake Buena Vista, Orlando. This guide offers guidance on hotel blocks, MCO transfers, federal per diem, IT Xpo floor time, and departure logistics for the four-day program.


- Rooms at the Swan and Dolphin fill up quickly during Symposium week, so compare the official conference hotels with nearby Disney Resort and Marriott properties.
- With conference-week rates often exceeding Orlando's federal lodging per diem in October, you need to budget for the gap or book through Gartner's public-sector hotel options if eligible.

Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo brings together CIOs, CISOs, and senior IT leaders for four days of analyst insights, technology strategy, and executive networking.\[1\] The right hotel, a thoughtful meeting schedule, and enough space between sessions can make the difference between simply attending the conference and returning with clearer priorities, stronger vendor relationships, and practical ideas you can put into action.


## What is Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo and who is it for?


Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo is Gartner's flagship North American conference for CIOs and senior technology leaders. The program focuses on AI, cybersecurity, data and analytics, infrastructure, and the strategic decisions shaping enterprise IT over the coming year.\[1\]\[2\]


Past Gartner symposiums in Orlando have drawn more than 9,000 attendees; this year's program features more than 350 sessions with more than 140 Gartner experts and 180 solution providers.\[1\]\[2\] Attendees include CIOs and CTOs shaping technology roadmaps, CISOs evaluating security strategy, IT directors assessing platforms and infrastructure, and technology vendors connecting with enterprise buyers throughout the week.


If you're making the case to attend, define what success looks like before you arrive. Schedule analyst meetings around your organization's biggest technology questions, identify the vendors you want to evaluate in IT Xpo, and leave with recommendations you can confidently share with your leadership team.


## What makes Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo 2026 worth the trip


The Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo brings Gartner's research to life through conversations with the analysts behind it and the technology leaders putting those ideas into practice. For CIOs, that means direct access to analysts on AI strategy, cybersecurity, and business transformation. IT directors attend the conference for IT Xpo demos and roundtables, where they test assumptions against peer institutions and vendor roadmaps.


Block time for these onsite moments:


- **IT Xpo:** Explore 180-plus solution providers, live product demonstrations, and technology presentations.\[2\]
- **Gartner analyst one-on-ones:** Reserve a private 30-minute meeting through Gartner Conference Navigator before appointments fill up.\[4\]
- **Roundtables and workshops:** Register early for interactive sessions with limited seating, including “Ask the Expert” meetings.\[4\]
- **Keynotes and featured sessions:** Hear Gartner's latest research on AI, cybersecurity, customer experience, data and analytics, and technology leadership.\[2\]
- **Peer Community meetings:** Connect with leaders facing similar technology and business challenges through role- and topic-based discussions.\[4\]
- **Special Event:** Join the evening networking event, with return shuttle service available to participating partner hotels.\[4\]
- **Official hotel shuttles:** Travel between Gartner partner hotels and the Swan and Dolphin campus every 10–20 minutes during conference hours.\[4\]


The value of the Symposium/Xpo comes from what you bring back: analyst guidance that shapes your roadmap, a shortlist of vendors worth pursuing, and new relationships that continue after the conference ends. Those outcomes are much easier to achieve when your hotel, analyst appointments, and IT Xpo priorities are planned before you arrive.

### Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo hotel pricing


Navan analyzed anonymized hotel booking patterns for October near the Walt Disney World resort campus during Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo 2025 in Orlando.\[11\] Use these benchmarks to put hotel pricing into context:


- **Median hotel rate:** During conference week, the median nightly rate was $248 near Swan and Dolphin and adjacent properties — about 11% higher than a typical September week near the venue ($223).
- **Stay length:** Business travelers stayed an average of 2.9 nights.
- **Check-in pattern:** Sunday was the most common arrival day and Thursday the most common departure day.
- **Late bookings:** Roughly 38% of bookings were made within two weeks of check-in.
- **Booking window:** Travelers who booked 1–2 months ahead paid a median nightly rate of $211, while those who booked more than two months in advance paid a median nightly rate of $301.


Although October is generally quieter for travelers in Orlando, the Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo increases demand around the Swan and Dolphin campus. Conference hotels often sell out early, and rates typically climb as the event approaches. Booking through Gartner's hotel block gives you access to negotiated rates and the official conference shuttle network.


If you're booking through Gartner's hotel program, keep these points in mind:


- **Book a hotel when registering:** Conference-rate reservations are available only through Gartner's registration process; don't contact hotels directly.\[3\]\[4\]
- **Don't miss the deadline:** Discounted hotel rates end at 5 p.m. ET Friday, Sept. 18, 2026.\[3\]\[4\]
- **Be aware of minimum stays:** The Swan and Dolphin require a four-night minimum stay. Other Gartner partner hotels require a minimum three-night stay that includes either Oct. 18–21 or Oct. 19–22.\[4\]
- **Consider other costs:** Resort fees, parking charges, cancellation policies, and deposit requirements vary by property.\[4\]


### Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo flight trends


The primary airport serving Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo 2026 attendees is Orlando International Airport (MCO). Navan flight data from the 2025 conference week shows 1,305 business flights into Orlando, with 5.8% from international origins.\[11\] The busiest origin cities were Newark, Chicago, Atlanta, New York, and Dallas-Fort Worth.


If you're crossing time zones, consider arriving the day before the conference so registration and opening day keynotes run more smoothly.

## Set your Orlando budget before traveling


The cost of attending GitHub Universe goes well beyond airfare and accommodations. You have to account for your conference pass, MCO transfers, client dinners, and incidentals beyond what Gartner provides onsite, so build a realistic budget before requesting approval.


### Federal per diem for Orlando


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, the following October rates for FY2026 applied to Orlando (Orange, Osceola, and Seminole counties):


- **$220 per day combined:** Includes $140 for nightly lodging plus $80 for daily meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** Includes $20 for breakfast, $22 for lunch, $33 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (reduce your meal allowance for any catering at the event)\[5\]
- **First and last travel days:** Includes M&IE allowance of $60 (i.e. 75% of the daily M&IE rate)\[5\]


Updated rates for FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) are expected to be published in mid-August 2026 at gsa.gov. During conference week, hotels near the Swan and Dolphin campus often exceed the federal lodging rate ($140 per night), so budget for the difference or confirm with finance before booking.


### Other expenses to consider


Budgeting for these expenses before you travel can help you stay within policy, avoid last-minute surprises, and make your expense report easier to complete when you get home.


- **Conference registration:** Standard registration starts at $8,200, and public-sector attendees may qualify for a $6,125 rate. Gartner clients should check with their representative for available discounts or ticket options.\[4\]\[6\]
- **Conference meals:** Breakfasts, breaks, and some onsite catering are included; don't forget to adjust your M&IE reimbursement accordingly.\[4\]
- **Airport transfer:** Expect to pay about $35–$65 for a rideshare from MCO, $65–$85 for a taxi, or $17–$18 per person for a shared shuttle.\[7\]\[8\]
- **Client dinners:** Private dining and Disney Springs restaurants typically run $85–$200 per person.
- **After-hours networking:** Leave room in your budget for partner receptions, team dinners, or other informal networking events.

## What to pack and wear at Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo


Attendees typically dress in business casual attire, such as collared shirts, slacks or chinos, comfortable closed-toe shoes, and layers for chilly session rooms. If you're presenting, meeting with executives, or hosting client dinners, consider stepping up to business professional attire.


Orlando in October runs warm and humid, with daytime highs around 85°F (29°C) and overnight lows near 65°F (18°C).\[9\] You'll move between climate-controlled ballrooms and outdoor walks between resort buildings, so pack breathable daytime fabrics and a light sweater or blazer for evening events.


### Conference essentials


A few small packing choices can make long days of sessions, analyst meetings, and networking much more comfortable. Add these items to your suitcase before you leave:


- **Comfortable shoes:** You'll cover plenty of ground between keynotes, breakout rooms, analyst meetings, and IT Xpo.
- **Portable charger:** Gartner Conference Navigator, note-taking, messaging, and badge scans can drain your battery before the day ends.
- **Business cards or a digital contact card:** Exchanging contact information is still common during analyst meetings, executive networking, and vendor conversations.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Florida's warm, humid weather makes it easy to get dehydrated, even when you're moving between air-conditioned buildings.
- **Light sweater or jacket:** Session rooms and meeting spaces are often much cooler than the outdoor temperature.
- **Compact umbrella:** Brief afternoon showers are common in central Florida, even in October.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for IT Xpo and hallway conversations


Some of the most valuable conversations happen outside the scheduled sessions. Build time into your agenda for IT Xpo, analyst meetings, and networking opportunities instead of filling every hour with presentations.


- **IT Xpo:** Explore 180-plus solution providers, live demonstrations, and technology presentations.\[2\]
- **Gartner Conference Navigator:** Build your agenda, register for analyst meetings, and receive real-time schedule updates.\[4\]
- **Analyst one-on-ones and roundtables:** Reserve your spot early, as popular analysts and sessions fill quickly.\[4\]
- **Peer Community meetings:** Connect with technology leaders facing similar business and IT challenges.\[4\]
- **Special Event:** Take advantage of the evening networking event and plan your return shuttle if you're staying at a partner hotel.\[4\]


Don't forget: Long conference days are more productive when you leave yourself time to recharge. A short walk around the resort or a quiet break between meetings can leave you more focused for your next analyst session or customer conversation than trying to attend every event on the agenda.


### Make networking intentional


The best networking starts before you arrive. Reach out to customers, partners, Gartner analysts, and vendors you'd like to meet about two weeks before the conference, and schedule time for the conversations that matter most.


During IT Xpo and networking events, focus on the business challenges you're trying to solve rather than collecting brochures or business cards. After each conversation, jot down a few notes while the details are still fresh.


Before you head home, send follow-up emails with a clear next step — whether that's scheduling a product demo, sharing requirements with a vendor, or introducing a colleague. Those conversations are much easier to continue when you follow up within a day or two of the conference.


## Where to eat at Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo 2026


Gartner provides networking breakfasts, refreshment breaks, and some onsite catering throughout the conference, so you won't need to plan every meal in advance.\[4\] Even so, long days of sessions, analyst meetings, and client conversations can make it easy to skip lunch or push dinner later than expected. Eat breakfast, keep a protein bar or other easy snack in your bag, and carry a reusable water bottle so you're prepared if meetings run long or you find yourself walking straight from IT Xpo to your next session.


When it's time for a client dinner, team outing, or a meal away from the conference, these Lake Buena Vista restaurants are convenient options near the Swan and Dolphin campus:


- **Client dinner:** Todd English's bluezoo at the Dolphin Resort handles executive dinners with private dining options and award-winning seafood steps from session halls.\[10\]
- **Team meal:** Rosa Mexicano at the Dolphin welcomes group events from 15–200 guests with customizable menus and private dining rooms.\[10\]
- **Private dining / executive lunch:** Il Mulino New York Trattoria at the Swan offers private rooms for important dinner meetings with classic Italian service.\[10\]
- **One-on-one or small group:** Kimonos at the Swan suits intimate meetings for up to 100 guests with authentic Japanese cuisine and a traditional atmosphere.\[10\]

## Finish strong on departure day


Your time at Gartner IT Symposium/Xpo isn't over until you're through MCO and back to your normal day-to-day work. Here's what to do before checkout:


- **Pack the night before:** Charge your gear, lay out your travel clothes, check the room for swag, chargers, and anything else you may have left behind.
- **Confirm late checkout:** Whether or not you remembered to request late checkout, ask at the front desk or let Navan Edge coordinate on your behalf.
- **Arrive early at the airport:** After the conference, you can expect long lines at MCO, so allow at least two hours for domestic flights and three hours for international.
- **Submit expenses early:** Upload receipts before your time at the symposium blurs together, and flag lodging above your per-diem cap when you file.
- **Visit the airport lounge:** Get quiet time to sort notes and receipts before boarding if your airline ticket includes lounge access.
- **Calendar a debrief:** Reserve an hour to message new contacts and summarize conference outcomes before regular work resumes.
