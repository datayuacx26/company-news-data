# Money20/20 USA 2026: Your complete attendee travel guide

## Key takeaways


Money20/20 USA 2026 takes place Oct. 18–21 at The Venetian Expo and The Venetian Resort in Las Vegas. Use this guide to choose the right Strip hotel, budget for conference week, plan your agenda, and make the most of networking opportunities.


- Rooms on The Venetian campus fill up quickly during Money20/20 week, so compare The Venetian and Palazzo with mid-Strip and Upper Strip hotels.
- Conference-week rates on the Strip typically run well above federal lodging per diem caps in October; budget for the gap or book early through Money20/20's official room block portal.

## What is Money20/20 USA and who is it for?


Money20/20 USA is the flagship U.S. gathering where banks, payments companies, fintech startups, retailers, and investors negotiate partnerships that shape how money moves.\[1\] The in-person event is Oct. 18–21, 2026, at The Venetian Expo and The Venetian Resort on the Las Vegas Strip.\[1\]\[2\]


Many attendees arrive with meetings and partnership conversations already on their calendars. Expect bank and card-network executives evaluating infrastructure partners, fintech founders meeting investors, payments engineers comparing real-time rails, retail and commerce leaders scouting checkout innovation, and venture investors scanning the Startup Hub for the next breakout.


Money20/20 USA expects more than 11,000 attendees this year, with more than 380 sponsors on the Show Floor and roughly one in three attendees holding a C-suite role.\[1\]\[2\]\[3\] Programming spans hundreds of talks in various formats, including fireside chats, panels, roundtables, and meetups, with the following four content pillars:


- **Borderless Financial Architecture:** Explore how payments, banking, and financial infrastructure are evolving to support seamless global commerce across markets and currencies.
- **Technology as the Great Equalizer:** Discover how AI, automation, and emerging technologies are helping organizations improve access, efficiency, and innovation across financial services.
- **Partnerships as the Engine to Scale:** Learn how banks, fintechs, enterprises, and technology providers are collaborating to accelerate growth, launch new products, and expand into new markets.
- **Brave New World: Setting Guardrails:** Examine how regulation, fraud prevention, cybersecurity, and responsible AI can help the industry innovate while maintaining trust and resilience.


If you're building the business case for attending, define success before you book your flight. That might mean evaluating real-time payments infrastructure before a product launch, comparing embedded finance partners on the Show Floor, or scheduling investor meetings through SmartMeet before October arrives.

The value of Money20/20 lies in what happens next. That could mean advancing a pilot agreement, moving investor conversations forward, or returning with a fraud-prevention short list your security team can assess. Booking your travel and priority meetings early gives you more time to focus on those outcomes once you arrive.

### Money20/20 hotel pricing


Navan analyzed anonymized hotel booking patterns near The Venetian during Money20/20's October 2025 Las Vegas week.\[18\] Use these benchmarks to put Money20/20 2026 pricing into context:


- **Median hotel rate:** During conference week, the median nightly rate was $253 — about 18% higher than a typical September week near the venue ($215).
- **Stay length:** Business travelers stayed an average of 2.5 nights.
- **Check-in pattern:** Sunday was the most common arrival day and Wednesday the most common departure day.
- **Late bookings:** Roughly 40% of bookings were made within two weeks of check-in.


According to Navan data, travelers who booked 2–4 weeks out paid a $249 median rate, and those waiting until 1–2 weeks before the event saw median rates of $273. Don't forget to take into account resort fees, parking, and cancellation fees, as they also vary widely on the Strip.


Money20/20 negotiates exclusive rates at The Venetian for attendees, and many attendees book through the event portal for the best available inventory.\[2\] Compare the official conference hotel block with public rates before booking, because the lowest price isn't always inside the block.

## Set your Las Vegas budget before traveling


Conference registration is only one part of your total travel budget. Hotel rates, airport transportation, client dinners, and incidental expenses can easily rival the cost of your pass during Money20/20 week, so build a realistic budget before requesting approval.


### Federal per diem for Las Vegas


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, the following FY2026 rates apply to Las Vegas:


- **$212 per day combined:** Includes $126 for nightly lodging plus $86 for daily meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** Includes $22 for breakfast, $23 for lunch, $36 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (reduce your meal allowance for any catering at the event)\[7\]\[9\]
- **First and last travel days:** Includes M&IE allowance of $64.50 (i.e. 75% of the daily M&IE rate)\[9\]


Updated rates for FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) are expected to be published in mid-August 2026 at gsa.gov. During conference week, hotels on the Strip often exceed the federal lodging rate ($126 per night), so budget for the difference or confirm with finance before booking.


### Other expenses to consider


Build these costs into your travel budget before booking so approvals, reimbursement, and expense reporting go smoothly.


- **Conference registration:** A Standard Pass starts at $2,899, an Early Stage Startup Pass starts at $1,545, and a Growth Stage Startup Pass starts at $1,855. Both startup passes require an application; verify current pricing at the official event website.\[10\]
- **Conference food and drink:** Complimentary food and beverage are included with your registration, so reduce your M&IE allowance accordingly.
- **Airport transfer** : The taxi zone fare from LAS to The Venetian is roughly $29 plus a $2.40 airport pickup fee. A rideshare costs $15–$40, depending on surge pricing.\[11\]\[12\]
- **Client dinners:** Fine-dining and private options on the Strip typically run $85–$200 per person.
- **After-hours networking:** Plan a small budget for Money Row evenings, Industry Night, and partner-hosted receptions.

## What to pack and wear at Money20/20 USA


Money20/20 calls for business casual: collared shirts or polished blouses, slacks or chinos, comfortable closed-toe shoes, and a light layer for chilly ballrooms. If you're presenting on the mainstage or are hosting a client dinner, dress one notch sharper.\[2\]


Las Vegas in October runs warm in the afternoon, with highs around 85°F (29°C) and nights that can dip to 55°F (13°C). You'll move between air-conditioned expo halls and warm outdoor walkways, so pack breathable daytime fabrics and a sweater or blazer for evenings on Money Row.


### Conference essentials


You probably won't forget your laptop or phone, but be sure to throw these into your suitcase:


- **Comfortable shoes:** You'll spend hours on convention floors and walking between The Venetian properties, so rotate between two pairs if possible.
- **Portable charger:** Between the conference app, SmartMeet notifications, QR code exchanges, and messaging, your battery won't last all day.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Las Vegas's dry climate catches up quickly, especially when you're talking nonstop.
- **Hand sanitizer and throat lozenges:** Four days of meetings, handshakes, and conversations are tough on both your immune system and your voice.
- **Light sweater or blazer:** Expo halls stay cool even when it's hot outside.
- **Sunscreen and sunglasses:** You'll likely spend more time walking outdoors between meetings and dinners than you expect.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for the Show Floor, The Intersection, and hallway conversations


Partner evaluations and investor conversations accelerate on the Show Floor — in booth demos, Intersection lounge meetings, and queue-side chats you won't find on the printed agenda.


- **Show Floor:** Sponsor booths and solution showcases across The Venetian Expo halls\[1\]\[5\]
- **The Intersection:** TradFi–DeFi convergence programming with dedicated stage and lounge\[5\]
- **Startup Hub:** Startup pods, stage content, and investor networking lounge for founders and VCs\[3\]\[6\]
- **SmartMeet:** Pre-scheduled hosted meetings for qualified deal conversations\[7\]
- **Money Row:** Restaurant takeover for networking across The Venetian dining venues\[7\]
- **Connections Lounge:** Open meeting space for badge-to-badge appointments throughout show days\[7\]


### Network like you're building pipeline


Approach Money20/20 like an investor roadshow week, not a general-attendance conference.


- **Before Vegas:** List high-value accounts like banks on your integration shortlist, VCs investing at your stage, and retail partners evaluating your checkout stack. Send meeting requests a few weeks out.
- **On the Show Floor:** Open with the business problem instead of a feature pitch. Log one specific detail per contact before you walk to the next booth.
- **Each evening:** Draft two-sentence follow-ups while demos are fresh. Send within 48 hours with a specific next step — a technical diligence call, shared integration requirements, or intro to your product lead.
- **Before you travel:** Reserve time for replies before your flight, because response rates drop quickly the further you get away from the conference.


It's tempting to schedule every hour of the conference, but back-to-back meetings can leave you too drained for the conversations that matter most. Build short breaks into your calendar to recharge, review notes, or step outside before your next meeting. Protecting a little white space helps you stay sharp through four long days of networking.


## What to eat at Money20/20 USA 2026


Money20/20 offers complimentary food and drink as part of your pass fee, and Money Row turns The Venetian restaurants into networking venues across show days.\[7\]\[10\] Still, client dinners and team outings fill the gaps around provided service. If you want to book a special sit-down meal, try these Strip restaurants:

- **Team meal:** Bouchon at The Venetian works for group dining with Thomas Keller's bistro fare and semi-private options for larger teams.\[15\]

## Finish strong on departure day


Your time at Money20/20 isn't over until you're through LAS and back to your normal day-to-day work. Here's what to do before checkout:


- **Pack the night before:** Charge your gear, lay out your travel clothes, check the room for swag, chargers, and anything else you may have left behind.
- **Confirm late checkout:** Whether or not you remembered to request late checkout when checking in, check with the front desk or have Navan Edge coordinate on your behalf.
- **Arrive early at the airport:** After the conference you can expect long lines at LAS, so allow at least two hours for domestic flights and three hours for international.
- **Submit expenses early:** Upload receipts before your time at Money20/20 blurs together, and flag lodging above your per-diem cap when you file.
- **Visit the airport lounge:** Get quiet time to sort notes and receipts before boarding if your airline ticket includes lounge access.
- **Calendar a debrief:** Reserve an hour to message new contacts and summarize conference outcomes before regular work resumes.
