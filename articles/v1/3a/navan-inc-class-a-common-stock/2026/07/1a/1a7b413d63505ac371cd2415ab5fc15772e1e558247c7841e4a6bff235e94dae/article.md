# TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 travel guide and attendee tips

- Hotels within walking distance of Moscone West fill up quickly for Disrupt week, so compare SoMa, the Financial District, and Union Square before booking.
- Conference-week hotel rates often exceed San Francisco's federal lodging per diem, so budget for the difference or stay a little farther from the venue.

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 brings founders, investors, operators, and startup teams to Moscone West for three days of product launches, fundraising conversations, and practical sessions on building and scaling companies.\[1\]\[2\] Your conference pass gets you into keynotes, Startup Battlefield, and the Disrupt Expo Hall. The decisions you make before and between sessions shape the value you take home. A well-planned week can leave you with stronger investor relationships, clearer product direction, and actionable next steps instead of conference fatigue and missed opportunities.


## What is TechCrunch Disrupt and who is it for?


TechCrunch Disrupt is TechCrunch's flagship startup and technology conference, bringing founders, investors, and operators together for three days of pitching, deal-making, and product insights.\[1\] The 2026 event runs Oct. 13–15 at Moscone West in San Francisco.\[1\]\[2\]


TechCrunch expects more than 10,000 attendees, with the audience spanning early-stage founders pitching in Startup Battlefield, growth-stage CEOs scaling their companies, investors sourcing new opportunities, and operators looking for practical sessions on hiring, AI infrastructure, and go-to-market strategy.\[1\] Disrupt also welcomes corporate innovators, sponsors, and accredited media.\[1\]\[3\]


The agenda includes more than 200 sessions across six stages, with more than 250 speakers covering startups, AI, venture capital, and emerging technology.\[4\] If you're building the business case for attending, focus on measurable outcomes: scheduling investor meetings before you arrive, evaluating potential partners on the Expo floor, or refining your fundraising narrative before your next funding round.


## What makes TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 worth the trip


TechCrunch Disrupt is where founders, investors, and operators come together to share ideas, evaluate new technologies, and build the relationships that shape the next stage of a company. For founders, that means Startup Battlefield, investor Q&A, and opportunities to refine a fundraising story. Investors come to discover emerging companies and build new relationships, while operators come for practical sessions on hiring, AI, go-to-market strategy, and scaling a growing business.


Some of the highest-value experiences happen away from the main stages:


- **Disrupt Expo Hall:** Explore sponsor booths, product demonstrations, and startup showcases across the show floor.\[3\]
- **Startup Battlefield:** Watch startups compete live for equity-free prize money and industry recognition.\[1\]\[5\]
- **Deal Flow Café:** Meet founders and investors through curated networking available to qualifying pass holders.\[3\]
- **Braindates:** Join one-on-one and small-group peer conversations scheduled through the event app.\[3\]
- **Side events:** Attend partner-hosted networking events, receptions, and meetups throughout Disrupt week.\[1\]
- **Six conference stages:** Choose from sessions across the Builders, Industry, Breakout, Roundtable, and other programming tracks.\[4\]


The value of TechCrunch Disrupt comes from what happens after the conference. Stronger investor relationships, clearer product direction, and new partnerships can all help move your business forward once you're back at work. Planning your travel, agenda, and meetings before you arrive makes it much easier to spend your time on those outcomes instead of conference logistics.

### TechCrunch Disrupt hotel pricing


Navan analyzed anonymized hotel booking patterns near Moscone West during TechCrunch Disrupt week in October 2025.\[13\] Use these benchmarks to put pricing into context:


- **Median hotel rate:** During conference week, the median nightly rate near the venue was $493 — about 27% higher than a typical October week in the same area (median of $387).
- **Stay length:** Business travelers stayed an average of 3.9 nights.
- **Check-in pattern:** Sunday was the most common arrival day, and Friday the most common departure day.
- **Late bookings:** Roughly 35% of bookings were made within two weeks of check-in.


What stands out: According to Navan data, travelers who booked 1–2 months before check-in paid a median of $409 per night, while waiting until the final 1–2 weeks pushed the median to $535.\[13\] That's a difference of about $126 per night, making earlier booking the safer strategy if your travel policy allows.


### TechCrunch Disrupt flight trends


The primary airport serving TechCrunch Disrupt attendees is San Francisco International Airport (SFO). Navan flight data from Disrupt week 2025 shows 3,406 business flights into San Francisco, with 17.6% arriving from international origins.\[13\] The busiest origin cities were New York, Newark, Chicago, Seattle, and Denver.


Consider arriving on Oct. 12, if your travel policy allows. You'll have time to settle into your hotel and ease into the week before the conference begins.

## Set your San Francisco budget before traveling


The cost of attending TechCrunch Disrupt goes well beyond airfare and accommodations. Factor your conference pass, hotel, meals outside conference programming, airport transportation, client dinners, and other business travel expenses into your budget before you book.


### Federal per diem for San Francisco


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, the following October rates for FY2026 to San Francisco (city and county):\[6\]


- **$364 per day:** Includes $272 per night for lodging and $92 per day for meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** $23 for breakfast, $26 for lunch, $38 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (deduct conference-provided meals from your meal claim as applicable)
- **First and last travel days:** Includes M&IE allowance of $69 (i.e. 75% of the daily M&IE rate)


Updated rates for FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) are expected to be published in mid-August 2026 at gsa.gov. During conference week, hotels within walking distance of Moscone West often exceed the federal lodging rate ($272 per night), so budget for the difference or confirm with finance before booking.


### Additional expenses


Before you travel, estimate your daily spending so there are fewer surprises when it's time to submit your expense report.


- **Conference registration:** Early Bird Attendee Passes start at $559, Founder Passes at $579, and Investor Passes at $799, with additional discounted tiers available for students, nonprofits, and Expo+ attendees. Verify current pricing before registering.\[3\]
- **Airport transportation:** BART from SFO to Powell Street or Montgomery Street costs about $11, while rideshares typically run $35–$50 depending on traffic and surge pricing.\[7\]\[8\]
- **Client dinners:** SoMa steakhouses and private dining rooms typically run $75–$175 per person.
- **After-hours networking:** Partner receptions, investor events, team outings, and other unofficial gatherings can add to your travel budget beyond conference-provided meals.


Map these line items before purchase, so approvals and your post-trip expense report stay clean.


## Plan your TechCrunch Disrupt agenda


TechCrunch has published the Disrupt 2026 agenda, giving attendees time to explore sessions across six stages before the conference begins.\[4\]\[14\] The program includes more than 200 sessions covering startups, AI, venture capital, product development, and scaling businesses.\[1\]\[4\]


- **Tuesday (Oct. 13):** Startup Battlefield rounds, Industry Stage sessions on payments and fintech, and Builders Stage panels including “What Happens When OpenAI Ships Your Roadmap” and “Winning Pre-Seed Without a Product”\[14\]
- **Wednesday (Oct. 14):** Additional Startup Battlefield sessions, AI-focused Industry Stage programming, and Builders Stage discussions, including “M&A Is Now an Early-Stage Strategy”\[14\]
- **Thursday (Oct. 15):** Closing Startup Battlefield rounds and Builders Stage sessions including “The 90-Day GTM: Why $0–$10M ARR Is the New Baseline” and “How to Create Viral Growth and Capitalize On It”\[14\]\[15\]


Featured programming spans pre-seed fundraising, product-market fit, AI, hiring, compensation, and go-to-market strategy, with additional speakers and sessions announced closer to the event.\[15\]


### Build flexibility into your schedule


Start by reviewing the published agenda and identifying your highest-priority sessions.\[14\] Leave room between sessions for Startup Battlefield, the Disrupt Expo Hall, Braindates, Deal Flow Café meetings, and the conversations that often become the most valuable part of the conference.


Here are some tips to prepare for Disrupt:


- **Sessions:** Star your must-attend sessions in the event app and check for updates as additional programming is announced.
- **Meetings:** Reach out to investors, partners, or fellow founders about two weeks before the conference to schedule short meetings while everyone is in San Francisco.
- **Logistics:** Download the Disrupt app, enable session alerts, and set your out-of-office message.
- **Networking:** Decide how you'll exchange contact information and add a quick note after each conversation to make follow-up easier.

## What to pack and wear at TechCrunch Disrupt


Business casual is the norm at TechCrunch Disrupt. Think clean blazers over tees or button-downs, dark jeans or chinos, comfortable closed-toe shoes, and a light layer to manage chilly session rooms. If you're pitching, meeting investors, or attending client dinners, consider dressing one step sharper than you would for a typical workday.\[3\]


San Francisco in October runs mild in the afternoon, with highs around 68–72°F (20–22°C) and evening fog that can drop temperatures into the mid-50s°F (13–15°C).\[2\] You'll move between climate-controlled halls and outdoor SoMa walks, so pack breathable daytime fabrics and a sweater or blazer for evening events.


### Conference essentials


While most business travelers remember their tech, they often forget these must-pack items:


- **Two pairs of comfortable shoes:** Convention flooring and SoMa distances can be tough on your feet, and switching up shoes can help.
- **Portable charger:** Session alerts, Braindate notes, and badge scans can drain batteries by mid-afternoon.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Dry convention air dehydrates faster than you notice between sessions.
- **Throat lozenges and hand sanitizer:** Crowded keynotes and Expo aisles could mean a lot of exposure to germs over three days.
- **Light layer:** Breakout rooms might run cold even when SoMa is mild, so bring layers so you're ready for anything.
- **Earplugs:** These are useful for evening events with music and if you're sleeping near noisy areas.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for the Disrupt Expo Hall and hallway conversations


Some of the most valuable conversations at TechCrunch Disrupt happen outside the session rooms. Plan plenty of time to explore the Disrupt Expo Hall, attend Startup Battlefield, and connect with founders, investors, and partners between sessions.


- **Disrupt Expo Hall:** Explore sponsor booths, product demonstrations, startup showcases, and technology activations throughout the show floor.\[3\]
- **Startup Battlefield:** Watch startups pitch live, hear feedback from judges, and see emerging companies compete throughout the conference.\[1\]\[5\]
- **Braindates:** Join one-on-one and small-group conversations with other attendees through the event app.\[3\]
- **Side events:** Attend partner-hosted receptions, dinners, demos, and networking events throughout Disrupt week.\[1\]


### Make networking part of your plan


As enticing as back-to-back sessions can be, a few thoughtful conversations can be more valuable than an overflowing schedule.


- **Before you travel:** Make a short list of investors, founders, partners, or customers you'd like to meet, then reach out before the conference to schedule time together.
- **During the conference:** After each conversation, jot down a quick note about what you discussed and any agreed-upon next steps. You'll thank yourself when it's time to follow up.
- **At the end of each day:** Spend a few minutes drafting follow-up emails while conversations are still fresh, then send them within a day or two of returning home.


Finally, leave yourself some breathing room. A quieter moment between sessions or a short walk away from Moscone West can leave you with more energy for the conversations and ideas you'll remember long after the conference ends.


## What to eat at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026


Conference days at TechCrunch Disrupt can stretch from early sessions through evening networking events, so plan your meals before you arrive. We recommend starting each day with a filling breakfast, carrying water and a high-protein snack in your bag, and not relying on grabbing food between sessions when the Disrupt Expo Hall is busy. If you're hosting clients or meeting investors over dinner, reserve restaurants using Navan Edge in the same chat thread where you booked your hotel and flights.


For sit-down meals beyond quick coffee breaks and convention snacks, these SoMa restaurants work well for different Disrupt moments:


- **Client dinner:** Fogo de Chão on 3rd Street can be good for executive dinners with private rooms and AV for larger groups steps from Moscone.\[9\]
- **Investor one-on-one:** MKT Restaurant & Bar at the Four Seasons on Market Street suits polished business lunches and dinners near the venue.\[10\]
- **Team meal:** Kaiyo on 3rd Street offers Nikkei cuisine with semi-private dining for mid-size founder teams.\[11\]
- **Upscale close:** Alexander's Steakhouse serves wagyu-focused steaks for high-stakes conversations within a short rideshare of SoMa.\[12\]


## Finish strong on departure day


Your Disrupt outcomes don't fully count until you're through SFO and back in your normal workflow. Tackle these before checkout Wednesday or Thursday:


- **Pack Wednesday night:** Charge gear, stage travel clothes, sweep the room for badges and chargers.
- **Confirm late checkout:** If you have an evening flight, organize during check-in at the front desk, and double check a day before.
- **Leave extra time at SFO:** Post-conference SFO can get busy — allow at least two hours for domestic flights and three for international.
- **Submit expenses while details are live:** Upload receipts before SoMa blurs together, and flag lodging above your per-diem cap when you file.
- **Use lounge buffer:** Airport lounge access buys quiet time to sort notes and receipts before boarding.
- **Schedule a debrief:** Reserve an hour to message new contacts and summarize Disrupt outcomes for your team before regular work resumes.
