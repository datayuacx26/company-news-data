# Adobe MAX 2026: Travel guide for Miami Beach

Adobe MAX 2026 brings designers, creative leaders, photographers, video professionals, marketers, and other creatives to Miami Beach for three days of keynotes, sessions, hands-on labs, Creative Park, and networking.\[1\] With popular sessions and labs requiring advance scheduling, hotel options spread across Miami Beach, and major events like MAX Sneaks and MAX Bash competing for time, planning ahead can help you get more from the conference and bring useful ideas and new skills back to your team.


## What is Adobe MAX and who is it for?


Adobe MAX is Adobe's flagship creativity conference, bringing designers, creative directors, marketers, photographers, video professionals, and other creatives together for keynotes, sessions, hands-on labs, MAX Sneaks, Creative Park, and networking.\[1\] The 2026 in-person event is at the Miami Beach Convention Center and offers optional preconference courses Nov. 8–9 with the main event taking place Nov. 10–12.\[1\]\[2\]


The program includes more than 200 sessions and labs across seven tracks covering topics such as AI, branding, creative leadership, the creator economy, design and illustration, photography, and video and motion.\[1\]\[4\] Attendees can also take part in photowalks, explore Adobe products and partner technologies in Creative Park, and attend evening events including the Welcome Reception and MAX Bash.\[1\]\[4\]


If you're trying to get buy-in to attend, identify the most relevant skills, tools, and ideas you can walk away with. Prioritize hands-on labs and sessions that support your current projects, make time to explore new workflows and technologies in Creative Park, and look for opportunities to connect with other creative professionals facing similar challenges.


## What makes Adobe MAX 2026 worth the trip


Adobe MAX combines product announcements and previews with hands-on learning, access to Adobe experts, and opportunities to connect with other creative professionals. Keynotes and MAX Sneaks offer a look at what Adobe is developing, while sessions and labs give attendees opportunities to build skills across AI, branding, creative leadership, design, photography, video, and other areas.\[1\]\[3\]\[4\]


Plan around these in-person experiences:


- **Keynotes and MAX Sneaks:** Hear Adobe announcements and see experimental technologies and projects from Adobe Labs.\[1\]\[3\]\[4\]
- **Hands-on labs and photowalks:** Build practical skills in 90-minute labs using MAX-provided equipment or join two-hour photography experiences around Miami Beach.\[4\]
- **Creative Park:** Meet Adobe experts, explore sponsor demonstrations and activations, and attend Creative Park theater programming between sessions.\[1\]\[4\]
- **Welcome Reception and MAX Bash:** Make time for two of the conference's major evening events and additional opportunities to connect with other attendees.\[3\]
- **Preconference courses and certification:** Add a full-day course before MAX or take one complimentary Adobe Certified Professional exam during conference week.\[1\]\[3\]\[4\]


What you learn at MAX doesn't have to stay in Miami Beach. Focus your schedule on sessions, labs, and conversations that can inform your current projects, introduce new tools or workflows, and give you practical ideas to share with your team when you return.

### Adobe MAX hotel pricing


Navan looked at anonymized October hotel booking patterns during Adobe MAX 2024 in Miami Beach to provide context for hotel pricing.\[13\] Use these benchmarks while planning your trip:


- **Median hotel rate:** During conference week, the median nightly rate near the event venue was $378, roughly 6% higher than a typical October week in Miami Beach ($356 median).\[13\]
- **Stay length:** Business travelers stayed an average of 2.7 nights at 4.1-star hotels.\[13\]
- **Late bookings:** Roughly **57%** of bookings were made within two weeks of check-in, including nearly **32%** during the final week before arrival.\[13\]


Adobe offers negotiated rates at official MAX hotels across Miami Beach, with the 2026 hotel block available through Oct. 16 or until rooms sell out.\[17\]


If you're booking through Adobe's official MAX hotel program, keep these points in mind:


- **Book through MAX:** Negotiated hotel rates are available through MAX registration or the Attendee Dashboard rather than by contacting hotels directly.\[17\]
- **Watch the deadline:** MAX hotel rates are available through Oct. 16, 2026, or until rooms sell out.\[17\]
- **Check transportation:** Some official hotels are within walking distance of MBCC, while properties farther away offer MAX shuttle service.\[17\]
- **Compare the full stay cost:** Resort fees, parking charges, and cancellation terms vary by property.\[17\]


### Adobe MAX flight trends


Miami International Airport (MIA) is the primary airport serving Miami Beach.\[8\]\[9\] Navan flight data from MAX week 2024 shows 295 business flights into Miami, with 21.7% from international origins.\[13\] The most common origin cities were New York, Atlanta, London, San Francisco, and Newark, with additional volume from Phoenix, Los Angeles, and Boston.\[13\]


If you're attending the main conference, consider arriving Monday, Nov. 9, so you have time to get to Miami Beach and settle in before Tuesday's programming begins. If you're attending a preconference course Nov. 8 or Nov. 9, plan your arrival around your course schedule instead.

## How to budget for conference travel to Miami Beach


Your Adobe MAX travel budget should account for the full cost of the trip, from registration and lodging to transportation, meals, and other expenses. Planning for those costs upfront can help you set a realistic budget before requesting approval.


### Federal per diem for Miami Beach


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, GSA had not yet published FY2027 rates, which will apply to Adobe MAX 2026.


For planning purposes, FY2026 Miami-Dade County rates were:


- **$237 per day combined:** Includes $145 for nightly lodging plus $92 for daily meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)\[7\]
- **M&IE breakdown:** Includes $23 for breakfast, $26 for lunch, $38 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (reduce your meal allowance for any catering at the event)\[7\]
- **First and last travel days:** Includes an M&IE allowance of $69 (i.e., 75% of the daily M&IE rate)\[7\]


Updated rates for FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) are expected to be published in mid-August 2026 at gsa.gov.


### Other expenses to consider


Planning for these expenses before you book can make it easier to stay within budget, secure travel approval, and avoid surprises when it's time to submit your expense report.


- **Conference registration:** A full conference pass costs $1,795 through Sept. 15, with standard registration increasing to $1,995 afterward. Check the event website for official rates and pricing tiers.\[5\]
- **Preconference courses:** Optional full-day courses on Nov. 8–9 are priced separately from the full conference pass.\[3\]
- **Conference meals:** Full conference registration includes breakfast and lunch Tuesday through Thursday, so adjust your M&IE reimbursement accordingly.\[3\]
- **Airport transfer:** The Miami Beach Airport Express costs $2.25 each way, while rideshare fares vary based on traffic and demand.\[8\]\[9\]
- **Client dinners:** Private dining and upscale restaurants in Miami Beach typically cost $75–$150 per person.
- **After-hours networking:** Leave room in your budget for team dinners, partner-hosted gatherings, and other events beyond official MAX programming.


## Plan your Adobe MAX agenda


With more than 200 sessions and labs on the MAX schedule, deciding what not to attend can be just as important as choosing what makes your list.\[4\] Start with your highest-priority sessions and hands-on labs, then leave room for Creative Park, photowalks, and conversations with other attendees.


Adobe has published the schedule on the official MAX 2026 in-person agenda. Registration opens on Tuesday at 8 a.m., and here is a quick look at the overall day-by-day calendar:\[4\]


- **Sunday–Monday (Nov. 8–9):** Optional preconference courses\[4\]
- **Monday–Thursday (Nov. 9–12):** Adobe Certified Professional exams\[4\]
- **Tuesday (Nov. 10):** Day One Keynote, sessions, labs, photowalks, Creative Park, and the Welcome Reception\[3\]\[4\]
- **Wednesday (Nov. 11):** Day Two Keynote, sessions, labs, photowalks, Creative Park, MAX Sneaks, and MAX Bash\[3\]\[4\]
- **Thursday (Nov. 12):** Labs, photowalks, Creative Park, and the final session ending at 3 p.m.\[4\]


### Build flexibility into your schedule


Add your highest-priority sessions and labs to your MAX schedule first, particularly hands-on experiences with limited capacity. Full conference attendees can initially schedule up to three labs and one photowalk, with the lab limit potentially lifted three weeks before MAX if space remains.\[3\]


Keep these priorities in mind as you build your schedule:


- **Sessions and labs:** Prioritize the topics and hands-on learning opportunities most relevant to your work, and add limited-capacity sessions early.\[3\]\[4\]
- **Creative Park:** Set aside time to meet Adobe experts, explore product demonstrations and sponsor activations, and attend Creative Park theater programming.\[3\]\[4\]
- **Keynotes and MAX Sneaks:** Plan around major stage programming before filling the rest of your schedule with sessions and labs.\[3\]\[4\]
- **Logistics:** Check your schedule and session locations throughout the day so you have enough time to move between activities.

## What to pack and wear at Adobe MAX


Adobe MAX doesn't require formal business attire, so comfortable, polished casual clothing works well for most conference days. Pack breathable clothes for warm Miami Beach weather, comfortable shoes for long days at MBCC, and a light layer for air-conditioned session rooms. If you have client dinners or other business meetings planned, bring an outfit that works for those occasions as well.


Miami Beach is typically warm in November, with daytime highs around 80°F and overnight lows around 70°F.\[6\] November is also drier than the summer months, although rain is still possible.\[6\] You'll likely move between warm, humid conditions outdoors and cooler conference spaces throughout the day, so lightweight layers are useful.


### Conference essentials


A few practical extras can make long conference days more comfortable, from navigating Miami's warm weather and cool session rooms to staying prepared during November cold and flu season. Add these essentials to your packing list:


- **Two pairs of comfortable shoes:** Alternate pairs during the conference to give your feet a break after long days at MBCC and walking around Miami Beach.
- **Portable charger:** Using the MAX schedule, messaging, maps, and other apps throughout the day can drain your battery.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Keep water with you during long stretches of sessions, labs, and Creative Park.
- **Light sweater or jacket:** Bring an extra layer for air-conditioned keynotes, labs, and session rooms.
- **Compact umbrella or light rain jacket:** November is relatively dry in Miami Beach, but showers are still possible.
- **Snacks:** Keep a protein bar or another easy snack in your bag in case sessions or labs overlap with your usual meal times.
- **Hand sanitizer and cough drops:** Keep both in your day bag for long days in crowded conference spaces.
- **Earplugs:** Pack a pair if you're a light sleeper, especially if your hotel is near South Beach nightlife.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for Creative Park and networking


Some of the most useful parts of Adobe MAX happen outside scheduled sessions and labs. Leave time in your agenda for Creative Park, conversations with Adobe experts and other attendees, and networking events instead of filling every hour with scheduled programming.


- **Schedule meetings before you arrive:** Reach out to colleagues, partners, and other attendees you want to meet about two weeks before MAX to arrange a coffee or time to connect at Creative Park.
- **Leave time for Creative Park:** Set aside dedicated time to meet Adobe experts, explore sponsor demonstrations and activations, and attend Creative Park theater programming rather than squeezing it between sessions.
- **Make room for evening events:** The Welcome Reception and MAX Bash offer additional opportunities to meet other attendees outside the conference's daytime programming.
- **Take notes after conversations:** Jot down useful ideas, resources, and next steps while the details are still fresh.
- **Follow up within 48 hours:** Send a short email or LinkedIn message with a clear next step, whether that's sharing a resource, continuing a conversation, or making an introduction.
- **Build in short breaks:** Long days of sessions, labs, Creative Park, and walking around Miami Beach can be tiring. Leave enough space in your schedule to grab water, have a snack, or take a short break before your next activity.


The best networking starts before you arrive and continues after you leave. Focus on conversations that connect to your work and interests, and make a note of any follow-up you want to make before moving on to your next session. A few useful conversations you continue after MAX can be more valuable than trying to meet as many people as possible.


## What to eat at Adobe MAX 2026


Full conference registration includes breakfast and lunch Tuesday through Thursday, so you won't need to plan every meal during MAX.\[3\] Remember to adjust your M&IE reimbursement for conference-provided meals, and keep a snack in your bag in case sessions or labs overlap with your usual meal times.

## Finish strong on departure day


A little preparation the night before departure can make your final MAX day easier, especially if you're checking out before Thursday's programming ends.


- **Pack the night before:** Set aside your travel clothes, charge your devices, and check the room for chargers, adapters, and other easy-to-miss items.
- **Ask about late checkout:** If you have an afternoon or evening flight, check with your hotel about late checkout or luggage storage so you don't have to carry your bags through the final day.
- **Allow enough time at MIA:** Plan to arrive at least two hours before a domestic flight and three hours before an international flight, and leave extra time for traffic between Miami Beach and the airport.\[8\]
- **File expenses promptly:** Upload receipts and note any expenses that require additional documentation while the details are still fresh.
- **Use airport downtime:** If you have lounge access or time before boarding, use it to organize notes, receipts, and follow-up tasks.
- **Plan your follow-up:** Set aside time after the conference to share useful takeaways with your team and reconnect with people you met at MAX.
