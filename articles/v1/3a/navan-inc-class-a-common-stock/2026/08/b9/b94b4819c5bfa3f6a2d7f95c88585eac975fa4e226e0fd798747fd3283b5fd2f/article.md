# IT Nation Connect Global 2026: Flights, hotels, restaurants and travel tips

IT Nation Connect Global 2026 brings the ConnectWise partner ecosystem together at Rosen Shingle Creek.\[1\] With a little planning before you arrive, you can head home with new ideas to improve your business, stronger vendor relationships, and practical advice from peers who have solved the same challenges. The right travel plan also makes it easier to focus on the conference instead of hotel logistics, expense reports, and other distractions.


## What is IT Nation Connect Global and who is it for?


IT Nation Connect Global is ConnectWise's flagship gathering for managed service providers (MSPs) to share growth strategies, product roadmaps, and real-world experiences.\[1\] Whether you're evaluating new tools, refining service delivery, or looking for practical ways to grow your business, the conference combines product announcements, technical education, and peer networking in one place.


The 2026 conference is expected to attract 4,000+ attendees for its 180+ sessions, 150+ speakers, and 130+ exhibitors.\[1\] You'll meet MSP owners evaluating acquisitions, operations leaders refining service delivery, sales and marketing leaders building pipeline, engineers evaluating security and automation platforms, and vendor partners meeting buyers on the show floor.


IT Nation Connect Global is designed for the people who make technology and business decisions for MSPs. Role-based tracks span sales, marketing, service delivery, finance, and leadership alongside hands-on workshops and community programming throughout the event.\[1\]


## What makes IT Nation Connect Global 2026 worth the trip


IT Nation Connect Global is as much about the conversations between sessions as the sessions themselves. Alongside ConnectWise product updates, you'll hear how other MSPs are approaching AI, cybersecurity, service delivery, hiring, and business growth. It's an opportunity to compare notes with peers, explore new vendors, and ask questions that don't always get answered in a webinar.


Some of the highest-value experiences happen outside the breakout rooms:


- **Solution Pavilion:** Meet ConnectWise partners, compare products, watch live demonstrations, and connect with vendors serving the MSP community.\[1\]
- **Workshops:** Dive deeper into technical and business topics through hands-on sessions held throughout the conference.\[1\]
- **Community Gathering:** Meet other MSP professionals and connect with peers facing similar business and technology challenges.\[1\]
- **PitchIT Competition:** Watch emerging technology companies pitch their solutions to a panel of judges and the IT Nation community.\[1\]
- **IT Nation Gives:** Join the conference's community service event and network with other attendees while supporting a local cause.\[1\]
- **Evening networking events:** The Welcome Reception, IT Nation After Hours, the Pub Crawl, and the IT Nation Celebration provide opportunities to meet customers, partners, vendors, and fellow attendees outside formal sessions.\[1\]


Getting the most out of IT Nation Connect Global starts before you arrive in Orlando. Planning your travel and conference schedule early leaves more time to focus on learning, networking, and the conversations that make the trip worthwhile.

## Set your Orlando budget before traveling


The cost of attending IT Nation Connect Global goes well beyond airfare and accommodations. You have to account for your conference pass, MCO transfers, client dinners, and incidentals beyond what ConnectWise provides to conference attendees.


### Federal per diem for Orlando


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, the following November rates for FY2026 applied to Orlando (Orange, Osceola, and Seminole counties):


- **$220 per day combined:** Includes $140 for nightly lodging plus $80 for daily meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** Includes $20 for breakfast, $22 for lunch, $33 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (reduce your meal allowance for any catering at the event)\[5\]
- **First and last travel days:** Includes M&IE allowance of $60 (i.e. 75% of the daily M&IE rate)\[5\]


Updated rates for FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) are expected to be published in mid-August 2026 at gsa.gov. During conference week, rooms at and around Rosen Shingle Creek often exceed the federal lodging rate, so budget for the difference or confirm with finance before booking.\[11\]


### Additional expenses


Budgeting for these expenses before you travel can help you stay within policy, avoid last-minute surprises, and make your expense report easier to complete when you get home.


- **Conference registration:** An All-Access Pass starts at $900, with early-bird pricing of $700 through Sept. 25, 2026. Check the official event website for current rates.\[4\]\[5\]
- **Keynote & Solution Pavilion Pass:** A pass starts at $350 and includes access to keynote sessions and the Solution Pavilion.\[4\]
- **Social Guest Pass:** A Social Guest Pass costs $350 and allows a non-attendee guest to join conference social events.\[4\]
- **Team discount:** Register two or more colleagues from the same company in one transaction to receive 25% off additional All-Access Passes.\[2\]
- **Conference meals:** Dinner at the Wednesday Welcome Reception plus breakfast, lunch, and dinner during conference events on Thursday and Friday are included with an All-Access Pass, so adjust your M&IE reimbursement accordingly.\[2\]
- **Airport transfer:** Expect to pay about $30–$50 for a rideshare from Orlando International Airport (MCO), $40–$60 for a taxi, or $15–$30 per person for a shared shuttle.\[6\]\[7\]
- **Client dinners:** Private dining at A Land Remembered or Cala Bella typically costs $85–$200 per person before taxes, gratuity, and group minimums.
- **After-hours networking:** Leave room in your budget for partner receptions, team dinners, or other informal networking events beyond the conference schedule.


Add up these costs before you buy so approvals and your post-trip expense report stay clean.

## What to pack and wear at IT Nation Connect Global


Business casual is the norm at IT Nation Connect Global. Comfortable shoes are a must, especially if you'll be spending time in the Solution Pavilion, attending networking events, or walking across Rosen Shingle Creek's large resort campus.


Orlando in November is mild and humid, with daytime highs around 80°F (27°C) and overnight lows near 60°F (16°C).\[8\] You'll move between air-conditioned ballrooms and outdoor walks across the resort grounds, so pack breathable daytime fabrics and a light layer for cold session rooms.


### Conference essentials


A few small packing choices can make long days of sessions, meetings, and networking much more comfortable. Add these items to your suitcase before you leave:


- **Two pairs of comfortable shoes:** You'll spend a lot of time on your feet between sessions, the Solution Pavilion, networking events, and meetings across the resort.
- **Portable charger:** The conference app, note-taking, and networking can drain your phone before the day is over.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Staying hydrated is especially important if you're moving between outdoor walkways and warm November weather.
- **Notebook or digital note-taking app:** Capture ideas, vendor comparisons, and follow-up tasks while they're still fresh.
- **Light sweater or zip-up:** Meeting rooms are often much cooler than the outdoor temperatures.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for Solution Pavilion and networking


It's tempting to fill every hour with breakout sessions, but some of the best conversations at IT Nation Connect Global happen in the Solution Pavilion, at networking events, and between sessions. Leave room in your schedule to explore, meet with vendors and partners, and connect with other MSP professionals.


A few ways to plan your time:


- **Set aside dedicated Solution Pavilion time:** Don't try to squeeze vendor visits between sessions. Blocking time for demonstrations and conversations makes it easier to compare solutions and ask thoughtful questions.
- **Schedule important meetings before you arrive:** Reach out to customers, partners, or vendors a week or two before the conference so you're not coordinating calendars onsite.
- **Leave room for networking:** The Welcome Reception, IT Nation After Hours, the Pub Crawl, and other evening events offer additional opportunities to connect with peers and industry partners.\[1\]
- **Take breaks throughout the day:** A short walk, a coffee break, or a quiet moment between sessions can help you recharge and stay engaged through afternoon meetings and evening events.
- **Capture your next steps:** Jot down a few notes after each conversation so it's easier to follow up once you're back at the office.


## Where to eat at IT Nation Connect Global 2026


Most of your conference meals are covered with an All-Access Pass, including the Wednesday Welcome Reception and meals served during conference events on Thursday and Friday.\[2\] Packing a protein bar or other easy snack is also a good idea in case you find yourself heading straight from a breakout session to the Solution Pavilion without much downtime.


You'll also want to plan for client dinners, coffee meetings, and the occasional meal between sessions. Here are a few good dining options at Rosen Shingle Creek:


- **Client dinners:** A Land Remembered offers Florida-inspired cuisine and handles executive steakhouse dinners with the MacIvey private dining room for up to 24 guests.\[9\]
- **Team meals:** Cala Bella serves Italian and Mediterranean fine dining, with a private room that seats up to 42 for group celebrations and partner dinners.\[10\]
- **Quick fuel:** Café Osceola offers a full American buffet for breakfast and lunch, while Tobias Burgers & Brews and 18 Monroe Street Market are faster casual options on property.\[12\]
- **One-on-one or small groups:** Banrai Sushi and Mi Casa Tequila Taquería are great for smaller meetings, with flexible seating and shorter service times than formal private dining.\[12\]

## Finish strong on departure day


Your time at IT Nation Connect Global isn't over when the closing reception ends. Before you head home, take a few minutes to wrap up the week and set yourself up for an easier return to work.


- **Pack the night before:** Charge your devices, set aside your travel clothes, and check the room for chargers, conference swag, and anything else you may have left behind.
- **Confirm your checkout time:** If you need a little extra time before heading to MCO, request a late checkout at the front desk or let Navan Edge coordinate it for you.
- **Leave extra time for the airport:** MCO is a busy airport, so plan to arrive at least two hours before a domestic flight or three hours before an international departure.
- **Submit your expenses while they're fresh:** Upload receipts before they disappear into your backpack, and complete your expense report before you're back in the flow of everyday work.
- **Follow up with new contacts:** Send a quick email or LinkedIn message to the people you met while the conversations are still fresh in everyone's mind.
- **Schedule a team debrief:** Set aside time to share what you learned, compare notes with colleagues, and decide which ideas or vendor conversations deserve a closer look.
