# Ai4 2026 guide: Travel, food, sessions and everything in between

## Key takeaways


Ai4 2026 runs Aug. 4–6 at The Venetian Resort in Las Vegas. This guide walks through Strip hotels, LAS transfers, GSA budgeting, Exhibit Hall strategy, and departure logistics for the three-day program, as well as the optional Aug. 3 pre-conference programming.


- Venetian-campus inventory is tight during Ai4 week, so compare the Palazzo-connected towers, the Treasure Island–Wynn corridor, and the Harrah's–LINQ–Flamingo cluster before you default to the closest room rate.
- Las Vegas federal lodging per diem is $126 per night in August (FY2026 GSA rate; verify at gsa.gov before traveling), but conference-week Strip rates often run well above that cap. Budget the gap or book through Ai4's Registered Attendees Portal room block.
- Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) is located roughly three miles from The Venetian, and taxis or rideshares usually beat public transit on door-to-door time (and are easier with luggage).
- Build your agenda around high-value sessions, Exhibit Hall demos, and summits, but leave room for white space and valuable hallway conversations.

Las Vegas becomes the center of the enterprise AI world during Ai4 2026, bringing together researchers, executives, founders, technology leaders, and an expected 400 exhibitors for three days of keynotes and technical sessions.\[1\] Your hotel choice, daily schedule, and meeting strategy have as much impact on the value of the trip as the conference itself. Planning ahead is the best way to head home with a stronger vendor shortlist, clearer implementation priorities, and relationships that continue long after the event ends.


## What is Ai4 and who's it for?


Ai4 is one of North America's largest conferences focused on the applied use of artificial intelligence, according to its organizers, bringing together enterprise leaders, researchers, founders, and government decision-makers. Ai4 2026 runs Aug. 4–6 at The Venetian Resort in Las Vegas and is expected to attract more than 12,000 attendees, 1,000 speakers, and nearly 400 exhibitors.\[1\]\[2\]\[3\]\[4\]


Programming covers enterprise AI strategy, scaling AI, data infrastructure, machine learning operations, responsible AI, and generative AI. The audience includes executives evaluating AI investments, technical teams comparing deployment approaches, policymakers shaping governance, and founders meeting prospective customers and partners.\[2\]\[4\]\[15\]\[16\]


If you're making the business case for attending, tie the trip to a specific outcome, such as validating a governance framework, shortlisting vendors, or aligning your team on a production roadmap before Q4 planning.


### What makes Ai4 2026 worth the trip


Ai4 is where applied AI roadmaps meet the practitioners who deploy them. For technical teams, maximizing value involves attending sessions on scaling AI, generative AI deployment, and responsible governance that can't be replicated remotely. For executives, it involves walking the Exhibit Hall with a prioritized shortlist and enough context to defend next year's AI budget before it's locked.


Plan for more than just the breakout sessions. In addition to the Exhibit Hall — where nearly 400 exhibitors will showcase their latest AI technologies, including AMD, AWS, Cisco, NVIDIA, Google Cloud, and IBM — don't miss:


- **Aug. 3 (pre-conference):** Kick off the week with Technical and Business Summits, hands-on AI training courses, the Applied AI Research Conference, and industry meetups. RSVPs are required for the summits.\[5\]\[16\]
- **Aug. 4–6:** Attend opening keynotes, six content tracks, the Exhibit Hall, and daily networking over breakfast, lunch, and happy hour.\[1\]\[4\]\[15\]
- **Aug. 5:** AI pioneers Geoffrey Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Andrew Ng are scheduled to share the main stage in a joint keynote.\[3\]\[5\]
- **Aug. 6:** Waymo co-CEO Dmitri Dolgov and founder Sebastian Thrun are scheduled to discuss the future of autonomous mobility.\[3\]\[5\]
- **Aug. 6 (evening):** Wrap up the conference at the official afterparty at TAO, sponsored by 1mind, Dell Technologies, Lightning AI, and Optimizely.\[16\]

## Set your Las Vegas budget early


Registration is only part of your Ai4 budget. Hotel rates, meals outside the conference, airport transfers, client dinners, and evening networking can add up quickly in Las Vegas. Compare your company's travel policy with federal per diem rates and expected conference-week pricing before you book so there are fewer surprises later.


### Federal per diem for Las Vegas


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. For Las Vegas (Clark County), the August 2026 rates are:


- **$212 per day combined:** $126 nightly for lodging, plus $86 for daily meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** $22 for breakfast, $23 for lunch, $36 for dinner, $5 for incidentals — reduce your meal allowance for any catering Ai4 provides\[15\]
- **Transit-day M&IE:** 75% of the daily rate ($64.50) on your first and last travel days\[6\]


Conference-week Strip hotels near The Venetian often exceed the $126 lodging cap by a wide margin — Navan's median was more than $100 above the federal figure during the proxy week — so discuss expected lodging with finance before you book.


### Other expenses to consider


Registration and hotel costs are only part of the equation. These are the other expenses most attendees should factor into their Ai4 budget.


- **Standard pass:** Pricing starts at $2,595, with a VIP Pass running $3,895; verify current tiers at the official event website before you register.\[15\]
- **Reduced passes:** The conference offers reduced rates for startup, government, academic, and nonprofit tiers; apply on the official event website.\[15\]
- **Conference-provided meals:** Breakfast, lunch, and daily happy hours are included with paid passes.\[15\]
- **Pre-conference add-ons:** Technical and Business Summits, AI training courses, and the Applied AI Research Conference on Aug. 3 may have separate fees.\[5\]\[16\]
- **Airport transfer:** Taxi fares to The Venetian run roughly $29, with a $2.40 airport pickup fee; rideshares typically cost $15–$40, depending on surge pricing.\[7\]\[8\]
- **Client dinners:** Strip steakhouses and private dining rooms can range from $75–$150+ per person, depending on the venue and selections.
- **After-hours networking:** Official happy hours are included, but unofficial vendor dinners and meetups still carry rideshare costs.


Budgeting for these expenses before you travel makes it easier to stay within policy, avoid unexpected costs, and submit your expense report without scrambling afterward.


## Plan your agenda with flexibility


It's tempting to fill every minute of your time at Ai4 2026, but the busiest schedule isn't always the most productive one. Build your week around your highest-priority sessions, then leave room for Exhibit Hall demos, summits, and hallway conversations.


Block 30 minutes before travel week for:


- **Sessions:** Star must-see keynotes and track sessions in the Ai4 app once login arrives (typically two to three weeks before the event); popular summits require an RSVP.\[2\]\[16\]
- **Meetings:** Ping customers, partners, or internal stakeholders about two weeks out with a specific 15-minute Exhibit Hall or coffee slot.
- **Logistics:** Download the Ai4 app, enable session alerts, set out-of-office, and save The Venetian address in your calendar.\[2\]
- **Contacts:** Choose QR, NFC, or LinkedIn — capture one context line per conversation before you leave the booth.

## What to pack and wear at Ai4


Ai4 calls for enterprise AI business casual: collared shirts or quarter-zips, chinos or dark jeans, comfortable closed-toe shoes, and a light layer for over-air-conditioned ballrooms. Presenters and client-dinner hosts often dress one notch sharper; Exhibit Hall browsing is more relaxed.\[2\]


Las Vegas in early August is brutally hot, with highs around 102°F (39°C) and lows near 78°F (26°C).\[10\] You'll move between climate-controlled halls and outdoor Strip walks, so pack breathable daytime fabrics and a sweater or blazer for evening events.


### Conference essentials


Most attendees remember the laptop and charger. These are the items people wish they'd packed by day two:


- **Two pairs of comfortable shoes:** Convention flooring and Strip distances can be punishing.
- **Portable charger:** Session alerts, app messaging, and badge scans can drain batteries by mid-afternoon.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Desert air dehydrates you faster than you'll notice, so stay hydrated.
- **Hand sanitizer:** Shaking hands at crowded keynotes and Exhibit Hall aisles adds up over three days.
- **Light layer:** Breakout rooms run cold even when the Strip is warm.
- **Sunscreen:** Protect yourself during outdoor walks between properties.

Most attendees arrive via Harry Reid International Airport (LAS), roughly three miles from The Venetian Resort.\[2\]\[7\] Decide your transfer before you land.


If your schedule allows, arrive Sunday, Aug. 2, or Monday, Aug. 3. You can collect your badge ahead of Tuesday keynotes and hit pre-conference programming without a same-day arrival crunch. Ai4 doesn't require a printed ticket — show up to registration with your confirmation email or photo ID and staff will print your badge on-site.\[2\] If you land early, ahead of Tuesday's programming, Navan Edge specialists can request early check-in on your behalf (subject to hotel availability).

## Beyond sessions: Make time for the Exhibit Hall and hallway conversations


Vendor evaluations and peer conversations often happen on the Exhibit Hall floor — in booth demos, summit circles, and queue-side conversations you won't find on the printed agenda. Here's where to meet people:


- **Exhibit Hall:** Nearly 400 exhibitors across enterprise AI infrastructure, models, and deployment tooling\[4\]
- **Aug. 3 summits:** Technical Summit for practitioners; Business Summit for executives (RSVP required)\[5\]\[16\]
- **Daily programming:** Breakfast, track sessions, lunch, Exhibit Hall receptions, and pass-included happy hours\[15\]\[16\]
- **Joint keynote (Aug. 5):** AI pioneers Geoffrey Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Andrew Ng share the main stage\[3\]\[5\]
- **Official after-party (Aug. 6):** TAO afterparty with live music and networking\[16\]
- **Industry meetups:** Healthcare, finance, engineering, and product function gatherings are listed in the Ai4 app closer to August\[16\]


### Network like you're building pipeline


Treat Ai4 like three days of customer discovery, not a badge-collection sprint. Use these habits before, during, and after the conference to get more value from every conversation:


- **Before Vegas:** List 10 to 15 accounts worth face time — vendors on your shortlist, customers exploring AI pilots, other execs — and send tight meeting requests about two weeks out.
- **On the Exhibit Hall floor:** Open with the business problem (“We're scoping responsible AI governance for Q4”) instead of a feature pitch. Log one specific detail per contact before you walk to the next booth.
- **Each evening:** Draft two-sentence follow-ups while demos are fresh. Send within 48 hours with a specific next step — a pilot review call, shared requirements doc, or intro to your ML lead.\[11\]


Don't forget to leave breathing room. Three days of dense AI programming reward pacing over volume. A 20-minute walk through the Grand Canal Shoppes or a quiet reset in your room often produces a sharper afternoon vendor conversation than chaining sessions without a break. Hydrate deliberately, eat real meals between keynotes, and protect sleep when you've got an early summit.


## What to eat at Ai4 2026


Ai4 provides breakfast, lunch, and daily happy hours across the program, so you don't have to buy every meal on the Strip.\[15\] If you need a quick bite or are planning client dinners and team outings, you have options:


- **Client dinner:** CUT by Wolfgang Puck at The Palazzo handles executive dinners with private dining space, steps from the convention floor.\[12\]
- **Team meal:** Yardbird Southern Table & Bar sits on Restaurant Row inside The Venetian, with shared Southern plates built for group meals without white-tablecloth formality.\[13\]
- **Quick fuel/business meal:** Grand Lux Cafe on The Venetian's casino level runs 24 hours, which works for an early breakfast before keynotes or a late bite after a long Exhibit Hall day.\[14\]

## Finish strong on departure day


Your Ai4 outcomes don't fully count until you're through LAS and back in your normal workflow. Tackle these before checkout Thursday, Aug. 6:


- **Pack Wednesday night:** Charge gear, stage travel clothes, sweep the room for badges and chargers.
- **Confirm late checkout:** Evening flights warrant a front-desk request at check-in and a reminder Wednesday night. Navan Edge specialists can request late checkout on your behalf.
- **Pad LAS time:** Post-conference Thursday and Friday departures lead to wait times at LAS — allow at least two hours for domestic flights and three for international.
- **Submit expenses while details are live:** Upload receipts before the Strip blurs together; flag lodging above your per-diem cap when you file.
- **Use lounge buffer:** Airport lounge access buys quiet time to sort notes and receipts before boarding.
- **Schedule a Monday debrief:** Reserve an hour to message new contacts and summarize Ai4 outcomes for your team before regular work resumes.

Ai4 2026 rewards attendees who arrive with a plan. Choose the right hotel, build a realistic budget, save time for the Exhibit Hall, and start your follow-up before you leave Las Vegas.
