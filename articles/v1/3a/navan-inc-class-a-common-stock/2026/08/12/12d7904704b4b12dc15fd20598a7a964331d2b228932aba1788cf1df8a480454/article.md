# Full travel guide for MAICON 2026, and how to get the most out of the trip

MAICON is designed to be hands-on, with workshops, Build Sessions, and conversations focused on applying AI in real marketing teams. Planning your hotel, budget, and schedule ahead of time leaves more room to explore new ideas, connect with peers, and return with practical takeaways instead of a notebook full of unfinished notes.


## What is MAICON and who is it for?


Hosted by the Marketing AI Institute, the Marketing Artificial Intelligence Conference (MAICON) focuses on helping marketing teams apply AI in practical ways through keynotes, breakout sessions, workshops, and an expo hall.\[1\] The seventh annual conference takes place from Tuesday through Thursday, Oct. 13–15, 2026, at the Huntington Convention Center of Cleveland.\[1\]\[2\]


Attendees include CMOs and marketing executives shaping AI strategy, content and demand generation leaders evaluating new tools, marketing operations professionals improving workflows, and agency teams looking for practical ideas they can bring back to clients. Rather than a broad technology conference, MAICON is designed specifically for marketers exploring how to use AI across campaigns, operations, and team processes.\[1\]\[3\]


If you're making the case to attend, define measurable goals before you arrive. Plan to compare AI tools you're actively considering, gather ideas you can apply to current marketing initiatives, and leave with recommendations you can confidently share with your team.


## What makes MAICON 2026 worth the trip


MAICON will focus on practical ways to use AI across modern marketing teams. From executive strategy sessions to hands-on workshops, the conference is designed to help attendees evaluate new tools, improve workflows, and learn from organizations already putting AI into practice.\[1\]\[3\]


Block time for these onsite experiences:


- **Optional preconference programs:** Choose from the AI for CMOs Summit or hands-on workshops covering tools like Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, ChatGPT, and Claude with an All-Access or Summit pass.\[3\]\[6\]
- **Opening Night Party:** Connect with fellow attendees at the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame on Tuesday evening while experiencing one of Cleveland's best-known attractions.\[5\]
- **Applied AI track:** Explore practical sessions that showcase real-world AI applications, marketing workflows, and lessons learned from organizations already using AI.\[4\]
- **Strategic AI track:** Hear marketing leaders discuss AI strategy, governance, organizational change, and long-term planning.\[4\]
- **Build Sessions:** Create a practical AI workflow, agent, automation, or other hands-on project during working sessions designed for active participation. Bring your laptop.\[4\]
- **AI Transformation Stage sessions:** Learn how organizations are approaching executive buy-in, governance, adoption, and measurable business outcomes.\[4\]
- **MAICON expo hall:** Compare AI marketing platforms, watch product demonstrations, and speak directly with solution providers throughout the conference.\[2\]\[3\]


The most valuable takeaways often come from combining sessions with conversations in the expo hall and networking events. Leave time to compare vendors, ask detailed questions, and connect with peers facing similar challenges so you return with ideas you can apply long after the conference ends.

## Set your Cleveland budget before traveling


Before you book your trip, estimate the total cost of attending MAICON, including your conference pass, meals outside conference programming, transportation, and other business travel expenses.


### Federal per diem for Cleveland


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. As a benchmark, the following October rates applied to Cleveland (Cuyahoga County) for FY2026:


- **$239 per day combined:** Includes $159 nightly lodging, plus $80 daily meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** Includes $20 for breakfast, $22 for lunch, $33 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals; reduce for meals provided during the conference\[9\]\[3\]
- **First and last travel days:** Includes an M&IE allowance of $60 (75% of the daily M&IE)\[9\]


Updated FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) rates are expected to be published in mid-August 2026 at gsa.gov. During MAICON, hotel rates near the Huntington Convention Center of Cleveland often exceed the federal lodging rate, so budget for the difference or confirm with finance before booking.


### Other expenses to consider


Include these expenses in your budget before you book so you can estimate the full cost of the trip and avoid surprises during conference week.


- **Conference registration:** Regular Main Conference passes cost $1,999 online and $2,099 if you purchase your pass onsite at the event. Verify current pricing before registering.\[3\]
- **Preconference passes:** Workshops and the AI for CMOs Summit require the appropriate All-Access or Summit registration package. An All-Access pass costs $2,499, and an All-Access + On-Demand pass costs $2,899. The AI for CMOs Summit + Main Conference package costs $2,899.\[3\]\[6\]
- **Conference-provided meals:** Breakfasts, lunches, and snacks are included during the main conference, so adjust your meals and incidental expenses (M&IE) reimbursement accordingly.\[3\]
- **Airport transportation:** The RTA Red Line from Cleveland Hopkins International Airport (CLE) to Tower City costs $2.50 one way or $5 for a day pass, while rideshares typically cost $25–$40.\[8\]\[10\]
- **Client dinners:** Expect to spend about $75–$150 per person at downtown Cleveland steakhouses and seafood restaurants.
- **After-hours networking:** Registration includes the Opening Night Party at the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, but you may also want to budget for additional happy hours, partner events, or informal networking gatherings.

## What to pack and wear at MAICON


Business casual is appropriate for MAICON, including blouses or collared shirts, slacks or chinos, and comfortable shoes. Bring a blazer or polished layer for executive programming, client dinners, and the Opening Night Party at the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame.


Cleveland weather in mid-October can be cool, windy, and variable, with daytime highs often in the low 60s°F and evening temperatures falling into the upper 40s°F.\[11\] Pack layers you can add or remove as you move between convention spaces and outdoor walks. A water-resistant jacket is also useful for rain and wind coming off Lake Erie.


### Conference essentials


A few practical additions to your suitcase can make long days of sessions, meetings, and networking events more manageable:


- **Two pairs of comfortable shoes:** Alternate between pairs during the conference to stay comfortable while walking downtown and spending hours on convention center floors.
- **Portable charger:** Session alerts, notes, messages, and badge scans can drain your phone before the day ends.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Keep water with you during long blocks of sessions, Build Sessions, and expo hall visits.
- **Hand sanitizer and throat lozenges:** These can be useful during three days of crowded sessions and conversations.
- **Water-resistant jacket and compact umbrella:** Cleveland's October weather can shift quickly, and lakefront wind may make temperatures feel colder.
- **Laptop and charger:** Bring both for Build Sessions, where attendees can create a workflow, agent, automation, or other practical AI application onsite.\[4\]

## Beyond sessions: Make time for the MAICON expo hall and networking


Some of the most valuable MAICON conversations happen outside scheduled breakout sessions. Leave time in your agenda for the expo hall, Build Sessions, conference meals, and informal conversations with marketers working through similar AI questions.


Here are a few ways to make the most of your time onsite:


- **Join the Opening Night Party:** The event at the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame gives attendees an early opportunity to connect before the main conference begins.\[5\]
- **Explore the MAICON expo hall:** Compare AI marketing tools across content, analytics, operations, and customer experience, and ask vendors how their platforms fit your team's current workflows.\[2\]\[3\]
- **Take part in Build Sessions:** Use these hands-on sessions to create a practical workflow, agent, automation, or other AI application you can continue developing after the conference.\[4\]
- **Attend AI Transformation Stage sessions:** Hear how marketing leaders are approaching adoption, governance, executive support, and measurable business outcomes.\[4\]
- **Use conference meals for conversation:** Networking breakfasts, lunches, and the official happy hour offer natural opportunities to meet peers and continue discussions from earlier sessions.\[5\]
- **Schedule important meetings before you arrive:** Reach out to speakers, peers, agency partners, and vendors one to two weeks before the conference while calendars are still flexible.
- **Leave room for unexpected conversations:** Avoid filling every hour with sessions. Some of the most useful introductions may happen over coffee, between expo booths, or after a Build Session.
- **Follow up while details are fresh:** Send a brief message within two business days with a clear next step, such as sharing requirements, scheduling a demo, or introducing a colleague.


MAICON is designed around practical application as much as presentations. Giving yourself time to compare tools, exchange ideas with peers, and continue conversations outside the session rooms can be just as valuable as the talks you planned in advance.


## What to eat at MAICON 2026


Conference breakfasts and lunches are included with most MAICON registrations, so you won't need to plan every meal in advance.\[3\] Even so, long days of sessions, Build Sessions, and networking can make it easy to skip meals or delay dinner. Eat breakfast, keep a protein bar or other easy snack in your bag, and carry a reusable water bottle so you're prepared if conversations or workshops run longer than expected.


When it's time for a client dinner, team outing, or meal away from the conference, these downtown Cleveland restaurants are convenient options near the Huntington Convention Center of Cleveland:


- **Client dinner:** The Burnham inside the Hilton Cleveland Downtown offers upscale American cuisine and is just steps from the convention center, making it a convenient choice for business dinners.\[13\]
- **Team meal:** Blue Point Grille in the Warehouse District serves seafood in a setting that's well suited to group dinners, about a 10-minute walk from the venue.\[14\]
- **Executive lunch or one-on-one:** Fahrenheit on Public Square offers downtown views, private dining options, and a comfortable setting for business lunches or smaller meetings.\[15\]
- **Quick meal between sessions:** Café options at the Hilton Cleveland Downtown and nearby restaurants around Public Square and Tower City make it easy to grab a quick bite without straying far from the conference campus.
