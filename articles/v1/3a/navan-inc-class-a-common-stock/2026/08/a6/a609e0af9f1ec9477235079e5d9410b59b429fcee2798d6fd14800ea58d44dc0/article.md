## What is ANA Masters of Marketing and who is it for?


ANA Masters of Marketing is the Association of National Advertisers' flagship conference for senior marketing leaders to share growth strategies, brand leadership insights, and real-world experiences from some of the world's largest organizations.\[1\] The 2026 conference takes place Oct. 20–23 at Rosen Shingle Creek in Orlando.\[1\]\[2\]


The conference is tailored towards CMOs, chief brand officers, marketing VPs, brand directors, agency leaders, and senior marketers from consumer and B2B companies. You'll hear how leading brands are approaching AI, measurement, media, creativity, customer experience, and organizational growth while comparing ideas with peers facing many of the same challenges.


Confirmed speakers include marketing leaders from Procter & Gamble, Verizon, The LEGO Group, AB InBev, GEICO, and other global brands, with additional speakers announced leading up to the conference.\[1\]


If you're building the business case to attend, define what success looks like before you arrive. Schedule meetings around your organization's biggest marketing priorities, identify the peers and partners you want to meet, and leave with practical ideas, new relationships, and strategies you can confidently bring back to your leadership team.


## What makes ANA Masters of Marketing 2026 worth the trip


ANA Masters of Marketing is where senior marketing leaders step away from day-to-day execution to hear how some of the world's biggest brands are approaching growth, AI, creativity, measurement, and customer engagement. Alongside General Assembly keynotes, you'll have opportunities to compare ideas with peers, exchange perspectives with agency partners, and take part in smaller discussions focused on putting those ideas into practice.


Some of the highest-value experiences happen outside the keynote hall:\[1\]\[3\]


- **General Assembly:** Hear keynote presentations and leadership discussions from CMOs and senior marketing executives shaping the future of brand growth.
- **Breakout sessions:** Explore focused discussions on marketing strategy, creativity, media, measurement, AI, and leadership throughout the conference.

- **Networking events:** Build relationships during receptions and optional Golf and Pickleball Tournaments throughout the week.
- **Virtual access:** Revisit select sessions on demand and continue networking through the ANA Events app and Grip platform before and after the conference.


Getting the most out of ANA Masters of Marketing starts before you arrive in Orlando. Planning your travel, scheduling key meetings, and leaving room for conversations between sessions gives you the best chance of returning home with practical ideas, stronger professional relationships, and a clearer vision for your organization's marketing strategy.

### ANA Masters of Marketing hotel pricing


Navan analyzed anonymized hotel booking patterns during October 2025 ANA Masters week to provide context for hotel pricing. Use these benchmarks while planning your trip:


- **Median hotel rate:** During conference week, the median nightly rate was $245 near Rosen Shingle Creek.\[12\]
- **Stay length:** Business travelers stayed an average of 2.5 nights.\[12\]
- **Check-in pattern:** Monday was the most common arrival day, with most travelers checking out Friday after the conference concluded.\[12\]
- **Late bookings:** Nearly 48% of bookings were made within two weeks of check-in.\[12\]
- **Booking window:** Travelers who booked one to two months ahead paid a median nightly rate of $209, compared with $260 for those booking one to two weeks before arrival.\[12\]


October is a busy month for conventions in Orlando, and hotels near Rosen Shingle Creek tend to fill quickly. Booking through ANA's official hotel block gives you access to the negotiated conference rate and keeps you within walking distance of every keynote, breakout session, and networking event.


If you're booking through ANA's hotel program, keep these points in mind:


- **Book after registering:** Hotel booking information is included with your conference registration confirmation.\[1\]
- **Don't miss the deadline:** The discounted conference rate ends Sept. 28, 2026.\[1\]
- **Watch for scams:** ANA doesn't use third-party housing companies, so ignore unsolicited calls or emails offering discounted conference rooms.\[1\]

## Set your Orlando budget before traveling


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, the following rates applied to Orlando (Orange, Osceola, and Seminole counties) for FY2026:


- **$220 per day combined:** Includes $140 for nightly lodging plus $80 for daily meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** Includes $20 for breakfast, $22 for lunch, $33 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (reduce your meal allowance for any catering at the event)\[4\]
- **First and last travel days:** Includes M&IE allowance of $60 (i.e. 75% of the daily M&IE rate)\[4\]


Updated rates for FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) are expected to be published in mid-August 2026 at gsa.gov. During conference week, rooms at and around Rosen Shingle Creek often exceed the federal lodging rate, so budget for the difference or confirm with finance before booking.


### Other expenses to consider


Budgeting for these expenses before you travel can help you stay within policy, avoid last-minute surprises, and make your expense report easier to complete when you get home.


- **Conference registration:** Platinum passes start at $1,999. Gold, Silver, Nonmember, and Virtual Platinum tiers are also available. Check the official ANA website for current pricing and eligibility requirements.\[1\]
- **Client-side ANA members:** Qualifying client-side members may attend in person at no cost. Be aware of ANA's cancellation policy, including applicable no-show fees.\[1\]
- **Optional networking events:** The Golf Tournament costs $200, and the Pickleball Tournament costs $100 for attendees who want additional networking opportunities outside the conference schedule.\[1\]
- **Conference meals:** Many meals and networking receptions are included throughout the conference, so remember to adjust your M&IE reimbursement accordingly.\[1\]
- **Airport transfer:** Expect to pay about $30–$50 for a rideshare from Orlando International Airport (MCO), $40–$60 for a taxi, or $15–$30 per person for a shared shuttle.\[6\]\[7\]
- **Client dinners:** Private dining at A Land Remembered or Cala Bella typically costs $85–$200 per person before taxes, gratuity, and any group minimums.
- **After-hours networking:** Leave room in your budget for sponsor receptions, team dinners, or informal networking beyond the official conference schedule.


Add up these costs before you book so approvals and your post-trip expense report stay straightforward.

## What to pack and wear at ANA Masters of Marketing


Business casual is the norm at ANA Masters of Marketing, with many attendees dressing a little more formally for keynote presentations, executive meetings, and client dinners. Comfortable shoes are still essential, especially if you'll be moving between General Assembly sessions, ANA Amplify Labs, networking events, and meetings across Rosen Shingle Creek's large resort campus.


Orlando in October is warm and humid, with daytime highs around 85°F (29°C) and overnight lows near 65°F (18°C).\[8\] You'll move between air-conditioned ballrooms and outdoor walkways throughout the resort, so pack breathable daytime fabrics and a light layer for cooler meeting rooms.


### Conference essentials


A few small packing choices can make long days of sessions, meetings, and networking much more comfortable. Add these items to your suitcase before you leave:


- **Two pairs of comfortable shoes:** You'll spend plenty of time on your feet between General Assembly sessions, networking events, and meetings across the resort.
- **Portable charger:** The conference app, note-taking, messaging, and networking can drain your phone before the day is over.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Staying hydrated is especially important if you're moving between outdoor walkways and warm October weather.
- **Notebook or digital note-taking app:** Capture ideas, speaker insights, and follow-up tasks while they're still fresh.
- **Light sweater or blazer:** Meeting rooms are often much cooler than the outdoor temperatures, and an extra layer works well for evening networking events.
- **Compact umbrella:** Brief afternoon showers are common in central Florida, even in October.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for General Assembly and networking


It's tempting to fill every hour with General Assembly sessions, but some of the best conversations at ANA Masters of Marketing happen during networking events, ANA Amplify Labs, and between sessions. Leave room in your schedule to connect with peers, agency partners, and fellow marketing leaders instead of rushing from one presentation to the next.


A few ways to plan your time:


- **Set aside dedicated time for ANA Amplify Labs:** These interactive sessions go beyond keynote presentations, giving attendees practical frameworks and hands-on discussions they can apply back at work.\[3\]
- **Schedule important meetings before you arrive:** Use the ANA Events app and Grip to reach out to peers, agency partners, customers, or sponsors a week or two before the conference so you're not coordinating calendars onsite.\[1\]
- **Leave room for networking:** Receptions, sponsor events, and optional golf and pickleball tournaments offer additional opportunities to build relationships outside the formal conference program.\[1\]
- **Take breaks throughout the day:** A short walk around Rosen Shingle Creek or a coffee between sessions can help you recharge and stay engaged through afternoon discussions and evening events.
- **Capture your next steps:** Write detailed notes after each conversation so it's easier to follow up once you're back at the office.


## What to eat at ANA Masters of Marketing 2026


Many meals and networking receptions are included throughout ANA Masters of Marketing, so you won't need to plan every breakfast, lunch, or evening event in advance.\[1\] Even so, long days of keynotes, meetings, and networking can make it easy to skip a meal or push dinner later than expected. Eat breakfast, keep a protein bar or other easy snack in your bag, and carry a reusable water bottle so you're prepared if conversations run long or you head straight from a General Assembly session to your next meeting.


You'll also want to plan for client dinners, coffee meetings, and the occasional meal between sessions. Here are a few good dining options at Rosen Shingle Creek:


- **Client dinners:** A Land Remembered offers Florida-inspired cuisine and handles executive steakhouse dinners with the MacIvey private dining room for up to 24 guests.\[9\]
- **Team meals:** Cala Bella serves Italian and Mediterranean fine dining, with a private room that seats up to 42 for group celebrations and partner dinners.\[10\]
- **Quick fuel:** Café Osceola offers a full American buffet for breakfast and lunch, while Tobias Burgers & Brews and 18 Monroe Street Market are faster casual options on property.\[11\]
- **One-on-one or small groups:** Banrai Sushi and Mi Casa Tequila Taquería are great for smaller meetings, with flexible seating and shorter service times than formal private dining.\[11\]
