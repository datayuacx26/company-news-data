# AWS re:Invent 2026: Hotels, travel, and on-the-ground tips

AWS re:Invent 2026 will take place across six Las Vegas Strip venues, which makes hotel location, transit time, and agenda planning especially important. With sessions, partner meetings, Expo time, and evening events spread throughout the week, a little planning before you arrive means you can spend more of the week learning, meeting with AWS experts and partners, and bringing useful ideas back to your team.


## What is AWS re:Invent and who is it for?


AWS re:Invent is the annual cloud and AI conference from Amazon Web Services, bringing together developers, engineers, architects, technology leaders, and AWS Partners for five days of technical learning, product announcements, and networking.\[1\]\[2\] The 2026 event takes place Nov. 30–Dec. 4 across six Las Vegas venues: Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, Encore, MGM Grand, The Venetian, and Wynn.\[2\]\[3\]


AWS expects more than 60,000 attendees and offers more than 2,200 sessions covering AI, cloud architecture, security, data, developer tools, and other technical topics.\[1\]\[2\]\[3\] The program includes keynotes, breakout sessions, workshops, chalk talks, hands-on labs, and other interactive learning experiences designed for attendees at every experience level.\[2\]\[3\]


If you're seeking buy-in from your leadership to be able to attend, define what success looks like before you arrive. Identify the sessions most relevant to your role, decide which AWS experts and partners you want to meet, and leave with practical ideas, technical guidance, and new connections you can share with your team after the conference.


## What makes AWS re:Invent 2026 worth the trip


AWS re:Invent is where Amazon Web Services unveils new products, shares its technology roadmap, and gives attendees direct access to the engineers, architects, and leaders building its cloud platform.\[1\]\[2\] Whether you're evaluating new AI capabilities, modernizing your infrastructure, or building cloud-native applications, the conference offers opportunities to learn from AWS experts and see new services in action before they're widely adopted.


Plan your week around experiences you can't easily replicate remotely:


- **Keynotes:** Hear major AWS product announcements, technology strategy, and customer stories from AWS executives and industry leaders.\[2\]\[3\]
- **Hands-on labs and workshops:** Build, test, and troubleshoot with AWS services in guided technical sessions led by AWS experts.\[3\]\[4\]
- **Chalk talks and Builder Sessions:** Join interactive discussions and small-group learning experiences where you can ask questions and work through real-world technical challenges.\[3\]\[4\]
- **re:Invent Expo:** Explore AWS Partner solutions, watch live demonstrations, and compare products across hundreds of exhibitors.\[2\]\[4\]
- **Ask the Experts:** Meet one-on-one with AWS engineers and solution architects to discuss architecture, migration, security, and implementation questions.\[3\]\[4\]
- **Networking events and re:Play:** Connect with peers, AWS Partners, and AWS employees throughout the week, then wrap up the conference at re:Play, the event's signature closing celebration.\[3\]\[4\]


The value of re:Invent isn't measured by how many sessions you attend. It's what you bring back: practical solutions to technical challenges, new skills, stronger relationships with AWS experts and partners, and ideas your team can put into practice. Planning your hotel, agenda, and transportation before you arrive gives you more time to focus on those opportunities once the conference begins.

### AWS re:Invent hotel pricing


Navan looked at anonymized hotel booking patterns during AWS re:Invent week in November 2025 to provide context for hotel pricing. Use these benchmarks while planning your trip:


- **Median hotel rate:** During conference week, the median nightly rate near the re:Invent venues was $396, roughly 19% higher than a typical November week.\[13\]
- **Stay length:** Business travelers stayed an average of 3.4 nights in 4.3-star hotels.\[13\]
- **Late bookings:** More than 35% of bookings were made within two weeks of check-in, including nearly 18% during the final week before arrival.\[13\]
- **Booking window:** Travelers who booked more than two months ahead paid a median nightly rate of $381, compared with $406 for those booking one–two weeks before arrival.\[13\]


Although November is generally a quieter time for tourism in Las Vegas, AWS re:Invent brings tens of thousands of attendees to the Strip, and hotels near the conference venues often fill up well before the event begins. Booking through AWS's official hotel program gives you access to conference rates and keeps you closer to the venues where you'll spend most of your time.


If you're booking through the official AWS hotel program, keep these points in mind:


- **Book after registering:** Access to conference hotels is available through the AWS re:Invent attendee portal after you register.\[3\]
- **Don't miss the deadline:** Official hotel reservations close Nov. 5, 2026, subject to availability.\[3\]
- **Compare total cost:** Resort fees, parking, and cancellation policies vary by hotel, so look beyond the nightly room rate before booking.
- **Choose your location carefully:** Staying closer to the venues where you'll spend most of your day can save more time than simply booking the lowest available rate.


### AWS re:Invent flight trends


Most attendees arrive through Harry Reid International Airport (LAS). Navan flight data from re:Invent week 2025 shows 3,621 business flights into Las Vegas, with 13.2% arriving from international origins.\[13\] The busiest departure markets were San Francisco, the New York metropolitan area, Boston, Denver, and Austin.

That gives you time to check into your hotel, pick up your badge, and get settled before Monday's opening sessions. AWS also offers airport badge pickup at Harry Reid International Airport's Terminals 1 and 3 on Sunday, so you can avoid one more stop before the conference begins.\[4\]

## How to budget for conference travel to Las Vegas


Plan your AWS re:Invent 2026 budget around the full cost of the trip, including registration, lodging, transportation, meals, and other travel expenses. Accounting for those costs upfront can help you set a realistic budget before requesting approval.


### Federal per diem for Las Vegas


The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) sets federal per diem rates by city and season. At the time of publication, the following November/December rates applied to Las Vegas (Clark County) for FY2026:


- **$212 per day combined:** Includes $126 for nightly lodging plus $86 for daily meals and incidental expenses (M&IE)
- **M&IE breakdown:** Includes $22 for breakfast, $23 for lunch, $36 for dinner, and $5 for incidentals (reduce your meal allowance for any catering at the event)\[5\]
- **First and last travel days:** Includes M&IE allowance of $64.50 (i.e. 75% of the daily M&IE rate)\[5\]


Updated rates for FY2027 (October 2026–September 2027) are expected to be published in mid-August 2026 at gsa.gov. During conference week, rooms on the Strip often exceed the federal lodging rate — especially those outside the AWS block — so budget for the difference or confirm with finance before booking.


### Other expenses to consider


Planning for these expenses before you book can make it easier to stay within budget, secure travel approval, and avoid surprises when it's time to submit your expense report.


- **Conference registration:** Early bird registration starts at $1,299. Verify current rates on the event website before you register.\[3\]
- **re:Play guest pass:** Guest passes for Thursday night's re:Play celebration are available during registration or through the attendee portal for attendees bringing a guest.\[3\]
- **Airport transfer:** Expect to pay roughly $20–$35 for a rideshare from LAS. The RTC bus typically costs $6–$10, depending on your route.\[6\]\[7\]
- **Transportation between venues:** Most conference venues are connected by walking routes or complimentary conference shuttles, although rideshares between north and south campus locations can cost $12–$20 during busy periods.\[4\]
- **Client dinners:** Private dining and upscale restaurants near the conference venues typically run $75–$175 per person.
- **After-hours networking:** Leave room in your budget for partner receptions, team dinners, and other informal networking events.


## Plan your AWS re:Invent agenda


With more than 2,200 sessions planned across six venues, it's impossible to do everything at AWS re:Invent. Start by identifying your highest-priority sessions, then leave time for the Expo, hands-on learning, and conversations between meetings. You'll also want to build extra time into your schedule for walking between venues across the Las Vegas Strip.\[2\]\[4\]


Here's a high-level look at the week:


- **Sunday (Nov. 29):** Badge pickup, registration, and conference kickoff activities\[4\]
- **Monday (Nov. 30):** Campus-wide sessions begin, followed by the Welcome Reception in the re:Invent Expo\[4\]
- **Tuesday (Dec. 1):** Opening keynote, Expo hours, and a full day of technical sessions and networking\[3\]\[4\]
- **Wednesday (Dec. 2):** Full conference programming, Expo activities, and evening networking events\[4\]
- **Thursday (Dec. 3):** Final Expo hours, technical sessions, and the re:Play closing celebration\[3\]\[4\]
- **Friday (Dec. 4):** Closing sessions and conference wrap-up\[4\]

### Build a plan around your priorities


Review the session catalog before registration opens and build your schedule around the sessions that matter most to your role. Then leave room for the conversations and hands-on experiences that often become the most valuable parts of the week.


Here are a few ways to prepare before you arrive:


- **Sessions:** Favorite your must-attend sessions in the re:Invent event catalog or mobile app, and register for popular workshops and labs as soon as reservations open.\[3\]\[4\]
- **Meetings:** Reach out to AWS Partners, customers, or colleagues about two weeks before the conference to schedule Expo meetings or coffee chats.
- **Logistics:** Download the re:Invent mobile app, enable session notifications, set your out-of-office message, and save your hotel and conference venues before you travel.\[3\]
- **Contacts:** Whether you exchange QR codes, LinkedIn profiles, or digital business cards, add a quick note after each conversation so you'll remember the context when you follow up.


## What to pack and wear at AWS re:Invent


Most attendees dress in casual business or tech casual attire, such as jeans, polos, sweaters, lightweight jackets, and comfortable sneakers. If you're meeting with customers, executives, or AWS Partners outside the conference, consider packing a blazer or other business-casual layers for client dinners and meetings.


Late November in Las Vegas is generally mild, with daytime highs around 60–65°F (16–18°C) and overnight lows near 40–45°F (4–7°C).\[8\] You'll spend the day moving between air-conditioned conference venues and outdoor walks along the Strip, so pack layers that are easy to add or remove as temperatures change.


### Conference essentials


A few small packing choices can make long days of sessions, hands-on labs, and networking much more comfortable. Add these items to your suitcase before you leave:


- **Two pairs of comfortable shoes:** You'll cover plenty of ground between conference venues, the Expo, and networking events.
- **Portable charger:** The re:Invent app, session schedules, messaging, and note-taking can quickly drain your phone's battery.
- **Reusable water bottle:** Las Vegas's dry climate makes it easy to become dehydrated, especially during long conference days.
- **Throat lozenges and hand sanitizer:** Five days of crowded sessions, keynotes, and Expo conversations can take a toll.
- **Light sweater or jacket:** Session rooms are often much cooler than the weather outside.
- **Small backpack or laptop bag:** Carry your laptop, charger, water bottle, and conference swag comfortably throughout the day.

## Beyond sessions: Make time for expo floors and networking


Some of the most valuable conversations at AWS re:Invent happen outside scheduled sessions. Leave time in your agenda for the Expo, Ask the Experts, and networking events instead of filling every hour with technical sessions.


- **Schedule meetings before you arrive:** Reach out to AWS Partners, customers, and colleagues about two weeks before the conference to lock in Expo meetings or coffee chats.
- **Leave time for the Expo:** Block dedicated time to explore partner booths, attend live demonstrations, and visit Ask the Experts rather than squeezing them between sessions.
- **Allow time between venues:** The conference spans six properties, so leave enough time to walk or rideshare between meetings and sessions.
- **Take notes after each conversation:** Jot down one or two key takeaways or next steps while the discussion is still fresh.
- **Follow up within 48 hours:** Send a short email or LinkedIn message with a clear next step, whether that's scheduling a demo, sharing a resource, or introducing a colleague.
- **Build in short breaks:** Five days of walking, sessions, and networking can be exhausting. A quick coffee or quiet break between meetings can help you stay focused for the rest of the day.


The best networking starts before you arrive and continues after you leave. A handful of meaningful conversations with clear follow-up actions will usually be more valuable than collecting dozens of business cards you'll never revisit.


## What to eat at AWS re:Invent 2026


Long days of sessions, hands-on labs, and networking can make it easy to skip meals or grab something on the go. Start the day with a filling breakfast, keep a protein bar or other easy snack in your bag, and stay hydrated so you're not running on coffee alone by mid-afternoon.


When it's time for a client dinner, partner meeting, or team meal, these restaurants are convenient options near the conference venues:


- **Client dinner:** CUT by Wolfgang Puck at The Venetian offers private dining and an upscale steakhouse experience just steps from the Expo.\[9\]
- **Partner meeting:** Bouchon Bistro at The Venetian is a great choice for business lunches or dinners in a quieter setting near the conference campus.\[10\]
- **Team dinner:** Nobu at Caesars Palace accommodates group dinners with its signature Japanese-Peruvian cuisine a short walk from Caesars Forum.\[11\]
- **Executive dinner:** Hell's Kitchen at Caesars Palace pairs a central location with an elevated dining experience for important customer or partner meetings.\[12\]

## Finish strong on departure day


Your time at AWS re:Invent isn't over until you're through Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) and back to your regular work. Before you check out, take a few minutes to wrap up the week so you're not sorting through receipts, business cards, and session notes after you get home.


- **Pack the night before:** Charge your devices, lay out your travel clothes, and double-check the room for chargers, badges, and anything else you may have left behind.
- **Confirm late checkout:** If you have an evening flight, ask the front desk about late checkout or have Navan Edge coordinate it on your behalf.
- **Arrive early at the airport:** After the conference, LAS can be especially busy, so allow at least two hours for domestic flights and three hours for international departures.\[6\]
- **Submit expenses early:** Upload receipts while the week's meals, transportation, and hotel charges are still easy to remember, and note any lodging above your per diem allowance when you file.
- **Visit the airport lounge:** If you have lounge access, use the quiet time to organize your notes, respond to emails, or catch up on follow-ups before boarding.
- **Schedule a debrief:** Block time early the following week to share key takeaways, reconnect with new contacts, and turn conference ideas into action items for your team.
