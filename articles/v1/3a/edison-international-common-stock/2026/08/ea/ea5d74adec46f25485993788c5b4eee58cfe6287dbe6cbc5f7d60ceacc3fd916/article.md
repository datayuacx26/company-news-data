# Save Money on Energy-Efficient Tech

[Rebuilding or repairing a home after a wildfire](https://energized.edison.com/disaster-recovery) often means balancing priorities and a budget.


Southern California Edison’s new[Simplified Wildfire Incentives for Transforming Customer Homes (SWITCH) program](https://energized.edison.com/disaster-recovery/simplified-wildfire-incentives-for-transforming-customer-homes) helps eligible homeowners stretch those dollars further when they choose to go green.


SWITCH offers incentives on qualifying energy-efficient technology customers choose to install when rebuilding or repairing within the Eaton and Palisades fire-affected areas. The program helps reduce out-of-pocket costs while improving home energy performance.


**Eligible technologies include** :


- Heat pump HVAC systems
- Heat pump water heaters
- Heat pump clothes dryers
- Induction ranges or cooktops
- Smart thermostats


**How much can you save?**


- Incentives range from $100 to $2,400
- Customers can combine upgrades to increase savings


**Who is eligible?**


SCE customers and owners of single-family and/or multifamily residential properties within the Eaton and Palisades wildfire impacted areas are eligible. Single-family and multifamily properties (i.e., townhomes and condos) must be individually owned.


Funding is limited and distributed on a first-come, first-served basis through 2028 or until exhausted. Equipment must be new, purchased and installed on or after Aug. 1.


***For more information, visit***[sce.com/switch](https://energized.edison.com/disaster-recovery/simplified-wildfire-incentives-for-transforming-customer-homes) ***.***
