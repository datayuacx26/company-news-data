# Cool Your Home and Bill This Summer

Summer in Southern California usually means two things: triple-digit forecasts and finding new ways to stay cool. What if you could handle the heat without breaking the bank?


Before you resign yourself to sweating it out, consider upgrading your thermostat.


Unlike a conventional thermostat, a[smart thermostat](https://energized.edison.com/stories/smart-thermostats-gift-year-round-savings) can connect to your phone, letting you adjust your home’s temperature remotely.


“The biggest money-sucking mistake people make is forgetting to adjust their thermostat when they leave their home — wasting energy and money on an empty house,” said Robert Hagstrom, a Southern California Edison advisor in Customer Energy Programs. “Being able to make those changes from anywhere can save customers up to 30% on their bills.” The technology can even learn your daily patterns and use your phone’s location to know when you’ve left the house, automatically adjusting the thermostat.


When you upgrade to a smart thermostat and enroll in[SCE’s Smart Energy Program](https://www.sce.com/save-money/savings-programs/enroll-in-savings-programs/smart-energy-program) , you can get up to $125 in bill credits.


Check out[SCE Marketplace](https://marketplace.sce.com/) to compare brands, prices and energy efficiency ratings to find the model that best fits you.
