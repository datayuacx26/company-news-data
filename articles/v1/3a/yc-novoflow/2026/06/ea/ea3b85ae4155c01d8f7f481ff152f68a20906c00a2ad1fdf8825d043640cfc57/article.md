## The Challenge of High-Volume Multilingual Intake


During peak hours, front desk staff in busy clinics are often managing in-person patients while inbound calls go unanswered. Missed calls represent direct revenue loss, and the patients who do get through may face long hold times and inconsistent service, particularly if they are not fluent in English.


Even when clinics deploy voice tools to address call volume, a critical gap often remains: the voice tool handles the conversation, but a human staff member still has to enter the collected data into the EHR. This means the administrative burden is only partially relieved. True intake automation requires a system that connects the patient conversation directly to the EHR, completing the full workflow without staff follow-through.


## Why API-Dependent Tools Fall Short


Most tools that attempt to bridge voice AI with EHR data entry rely on API connections to transfer information from the call into the scheduling system. This approach fails for clinics using legacy, on-premise, or Citrix-hosted EHR systems, which either lack bidirectional APIs or run in environments where API-based tools cannot access the underlying application.


Even for clinics with API-capable EHRs, building and maintaining those connections requires significant development resources. API connectors are prone to breaking during software updates, creating ongoing maintenance overhead that many clinics cannot sustain.


Novoflow bypasses this entirely. Rather than connecting to the EHR at the data layer, its visual AI interacts with the EHR interface at the screen level, the same way a human intake coordinator would. No API development required.
