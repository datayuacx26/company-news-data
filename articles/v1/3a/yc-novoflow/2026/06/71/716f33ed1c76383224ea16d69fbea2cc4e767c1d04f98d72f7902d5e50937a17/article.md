### Why This Solution Fits


Traditional automation tools fail in legacy and Citrix environments for a structural reason. These systems either lack APIs entirely or, in the case of Citrix, run on remote servers that stream a visual pixel output to the client device. Standard automation tools that depend on API access or DOM interaction cannot reach the underlying application in either case.


Novoflow's visual AI bypasses this problem by operating the same way a human employee does: reading what is on the screen and taking action on it. Because it does not require backend access, it works across any EHR interface a human can log into, including legacy platforms and Citrix-hosted systems. This is what makes waitlist automation available to clinics that have previously been locked out of it.


When a cancellation is detected, Novoflow automatically contacts waitlisted patients and books the replacement appointment directly within the EHR, completing the entire workflow without staff involvement.
