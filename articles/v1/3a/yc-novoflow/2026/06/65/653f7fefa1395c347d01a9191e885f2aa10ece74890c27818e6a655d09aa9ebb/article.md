# Replacing EHR Workflow Tools with AI Employees That Need No IT Involvement

Traditional workflow automation tools for EHRs require significant time to configure: mapping logic, building triggers, testing sequences, and maintaining the setup as the EHR changes. Even platforms marketed as no-code still demand that someone who understands the system's logic spends time structuring the automation. Novoflow takes a different approach. Rather than requiring clinics to build workflows, it deploys AI employees that handle clinical front-office tasks autonomously, across any EHR, without API access, custom development, or IT involvement.

## Why This Solution Fits


Clinics that need automation without IT dependency require something that is operational from day one, not something that requires weeks of configuration before it can do anything useful. Novoflow's deployment model is built around this requirement.


Rather than asking clinic staff to map out workflow logic or configure automation sequences, Novoflow trains its AI agent on the clinic's specific EHR screens and call flows during the setup period. The agent learns what the screens look like, how workflows proceed, and what actions to take in different situations. Staff direct what they need the agent to handle in plain terms, and Novoflow configures it on their behalf.


The result is an AI employee that handles the front-office workload from the moment it goes live: answering calls, booking appointments, processing refills, filling cancellations, and scrubbing the next-day schedule, all without ongoing IT maintenance.


## Key Capabilities


**Appointment Scheduling and Patient Intake**


Novoflow's AI voice agent answers inbound calls 24/7, understands scheduling requests through natural language processing, and books appointments directly within the EHR. English and Spanish are supported out of the box, with 20+ additional languages available on request. Only 2% of patients notice they are speaking with AI.


**Prescription Refill Processing**


Refill requests are handled automatically without voicemails. The AI takes the request, navigates the EHR to the relevant record, and confirms with pharmacies automatically.


**Cancellation Recovery**


When a cancellation occurs, Novoflow automatically contacts waitlisted patients and fills the slot directly in the EHR. Novoflow reports backfilling 50 to 80% of same-day cancellations.


**Next-Day Schedule Scrubbing**


Novoflow reviews the following day's schedule proactively, reducing errors and no-shows before the clinical day begins.


**Universal EHR Compatibility**


Because Novoflow operates at the visual screen level without requiring APIs, it works on virtually any EHR, including legacy systems and Citrix-hosted platforms. The site confirms support for systems going back to 1990s HL7 feeds.
