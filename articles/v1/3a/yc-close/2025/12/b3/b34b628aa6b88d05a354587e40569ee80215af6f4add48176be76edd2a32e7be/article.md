# Close 2025 Product Roundup: Automation, AI, and Your New Sales Command Center

At the beginning of 2025, the Close team set our sights on two big outcomes for our customers:


1. Help our customers manage their **whole inbound lead flow** **in one place** : Close
2. And make Close **fast and magical** in setup and customization (<30 minutes to set up a CRM from scratch)


These goals help Close customers move faster in a simpler, more focused setup.


- Fewer tools to juggle = saving money and troubleshooting time
- Inbound lead flow in one tool = faster speed to lead, more closing deals, and nothing slips through the cracks
- Faster set up = getting to the *wow* experience with Close in minutes


Here’s a round-up of the many ( *many* ) powerful new features in Close that paved the way for a successful *check, check* of these two goals, all in pursuit of helping *you* sell faster and easier.


## **1. Automation that runs your sales ops (not just your emails)**


Workflows graduated from “follow‑up machine” to **full‑blown sales ops engine** this year. Now, you can cut your Zapier costs, reduce wasted troubleshooting time, and run more reliable automations directly in Close.


### **Smarter triggers & actions**


You can now run far more of your revenue process directly in Close:


- **Event‑based triggers**


- Kick off Workflows from **Custom Activities, Opportunities, and Contacts** (e.g., when an Opportunity is marked Won or a specific custom event is logged).
- Trigger Workflows when an **Opportunity stalls** and “Needs Attention,” so aging deals don’t go ice-cold.
- Launch Workflows off **Call events** , with filters for Outcomes, direction, or duration—perfect for automating follow‑ups, reassignment, or tasks.


- **Operational actions, not just outreach**


- **Update Lead** as a Workflow step, mapping trigger data into any standard or Custom Field.
- Use **AI Enrich inside Workflows** to automatically research and enrich new or high‑value Leads as they come in.
- Automatically create Opportunities, update Tasks, or assign work to the right rep as it moves through your pipeline.


### **Better timing & guardrails**


We also gave you tighter control over *when* automation fires:


- **Minute‑level delays** for near‑instant reactions to form fills and other key events.
- **Email drafts in Workflows** so you keep a human in the loop on important outreach steps. Automation + human review = perfect custom communication.
- [Runs View](https://www.close.com/changelog/workflow-blackout-dates) to help you review every Run of every Workflow in one place
- **Conversion goals** that automatically remove people from a Workflow when they book a meeting or their status changes.
- [Blackout Dates](https://www.close.com/changelog/workflow-blackout-dates) so email/SMS steps skip holidays or company closures.


[Explore new Workflows features now](https://app.close.com/workflows/) or catch the replay of our automation webinar below.


## **2. AI that does the busywork for you**


We built AI into Close to reduce your admin tasks and[automate low-value work](https://www.close.com/blog/the-no-bs-ai-voice-automation-buyers-guide-for-teams-that-hate-wasting-money) . This up-levels sellers to do their best work and focus on real, juicy sales work that matters. No fluff, just real AI features that let you focus on selling more.


### **Close Notetaker \[Beta\]**


Kill deal drag.[Close Notetaker](https://close.com/demo-library/intro-to-notetaker) helps small, scaling businesses grow sales velocity.[Close Notetaker](https://www.close.com/notetaking) transcribes, summarizes, and records all your sales meetings––so you stay focused on the human you’re selling to. It logs everything back to Close automatically.


You get:


- Call summaries and action items without typing
- Shareable links for key moments you want to coach on
- One less external AI tool to manage and pay for


[Try Notetaker now](https://app.close.com/settings/notetaker/)


### **Automated Lead Enrichment**


**AI Enrich** now fills in missing Lead and Contact data in one click or in bulk—from titles to company details—right inside Close. In Q3, we expanded AI Enrich so you can also:


- Bulk‑enrich records directly from Smart Views
- Enrich key fields like Contact titles without leaving Close
- Use AI Enrich inside Workflows to enrich new inbound or “high‑intent automatically” leads as they arrive


[Read more about AI Enrich here](https://help.close.com/docs/ai-enrich)


### **Close + Claude (and MCP)**


We introduced a Close MCP Server that helps your team find insights and take action without switching tabs or losing momentum. It connects Close to Claude, ChatGPT, or n8n now. We recommend[trying the Close + Claude integration](https://www.close.com/blog/close-claude-crm-integration) first.


### **AI that nudges the right deals**


Two more AI helpers shipped this year:


- [Pipeline Guidance](https://help.close.com/v1/docs/en/opportunities#pipeline-guidance) **:** An AI‑powered “Needs Attention” view that surfaces stuck or at‑risk deals and suggests next actions (follow up, call now, or close out).
- [Email Draft Assist](https://help.close.com/docs/emailing#email-draft-assist) **:** When an email reminder comes due, Close’s AI drafts a follow‑up suggestion directly in your Inbox so you can send or tweak in seconds.


Together, they mean less time deciding *what* to work on—and more time actually doing it.


## **3. Tools to communicate faster (and keep everything in one place)**


Your reps live in email, WhatsApp, calendars, and calls. In 2025, Close improved at pulling all of that into a single view with our revamped Inbox.


### **Native Close Forms \[Beta\]**


Instantly capture website leads directly into Close. Use Close’s no-code[forms](https://www.close.com/blog/introducing-close-forms) with instant routing, spam protection, and clean CRM data.


You can now ditch your third-party form tool and save time + money on integrating multiple systems.


- Easy, **no-code editing** to match your brand
- [Automatic field mapping and Workflow enrollment](https://www.close.com/blog/how-to-use-close-forms-with-workflows)
- Automate **deduplication** and **compliance**


[Read more about Forms here](http://close.com/forms) and emailsupport@close.com for access to the Beta.


### **A more powerful Close Inbox**


The new Close Inbox is the sales command center you’ve been waiting for. If you’re still bouncing between Gmail/Outlook and your CRM, you’re leaving speed on the table.


- **Potential Contacts** for handling messages from people *not yet* in Close
- **Lead & Opportunity context in every row** (status, value, due dates), with inline status updates
- **Hover insights** so you can see key lead details without leaving the Inbox
- **Richer filters** by lead status, pipeline, opportunity status, and Smart Views
- **Lead status colors** and **High Priority tasks** so your most important work pops visually


[See the new Inbox now](https://app.close.com/tasks/inbox/)


### **WhatsApp inside Close**


We took WhatsApp from duct-tape-workarounds to fully in the loop. Never let a deal slip through the cracks because your conversations are in multiple different tools.


- **Native WhatsApp integration links** let you jump from Close to WhatsApp Web with a single click.
- The **TimelinesAI integration** syncs WhatsApp conversations (including attachments) as native-looking Close activities, auto‑creates Leads when new chats come in, and matches messages by phone number.


[Read more about the WhatsApp integration](https://www.close.com/whatsapp) or watch our video below.


### **Updated Calendly integration**


The **new and improved Calendly integration** makes sure booked meetings actually turn into clean CRM data. You can now:


- Auto‑create Contacts when meetings are booked (with duplicate detection)
- Use **Round Robin** event types with your team
- Force a refresh with a **Sync** button if you’ve made changes in Calendly


That means faster handoffs from “booked” to “tracked in pipeline,” with less manual data entry.


[Read more about the updated Calendly integration](https://www.close.com/changelog/new-and-improved-calendly-integration?utm_source=chatgpt.com)


### **Quality‑of‑life wins**


A few smaller—but mighty—communication upgrades:


- **Docked SMS:** Keep text threads visible while you work elsewhere in Close.
- **Dark Mode** on web & desktop.
- **Pinned Notes & Custom Activities** so the most important context is always at the top of the activity feed.


## **4. Features to enable better sales coaching & reporting**


We invested heavily in surfacing the *right* data for managers and revenue leaders.


### **Activity Smart Views you can share**


[Activity Smart Views](https://www.close.com/changelog/activity-smart-views) let you build and share views specifically on Calls, Emails, SMS, WhatsApp messages, Meetings, Notes, and Custom Activities.


Use them to:


- Create coaching queues (e.g., “all calls over 10 minutes this week”)
- Track specific moments like onboarding calls or renewals
- Give SDR/AE/CS teams activity dashboards tailored to their workflows


### **Cleaner reports = meaningful data**


We refreshed key reporting surfaces to make trends easier to spot:


- A **Saved Views** page to manage all Smart Views, Conversations, and Reports from one place
- **Report sharing** for Activity Overview, Funnel, and more—update once, share with everyone
- An improved **Activity Comparison Report** with a cleaner table layout and drag‑and‑drop columns


Add in[Customizable Call & Meeting Outcomes](https://help.close.com/docs/outcomes) , and you get more consistent logging plus more actionable call data for reviews and coaching.
