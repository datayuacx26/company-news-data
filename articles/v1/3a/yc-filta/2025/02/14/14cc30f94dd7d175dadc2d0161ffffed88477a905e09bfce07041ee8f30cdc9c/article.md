# From Layoff to Leadership: Keri Gardner’s Million-Dollar Filta Success Story

Life can throw unexpected curveballs. For Keri Gardner, a corporate layoff in her 50s became the catalyst for an incredible entrepreneurial journey with Filta Environmental Kitchen Solutions. Facing a crossroads, Keri decided to bet on herself, turning a moment of uncertainty into a million-dollar success story.


Initially, entrepreneurship wasn’t in Keri’s plans. However, a pivotal phone call ignited her determination to take control of her future. Investing $100,000 from her 401(k), she purchased two Filta territories on Long Island, launching her business amidst the challenges of the 2020 pandemic.


What followed was a testament to resilience and strategic growth. Leveraging the slower pace of business, Keri focused on securing corporate accounts and refining her operations. Within six months, her husband joined her, and together, they expanded to six territories.


Keri’s story highlights the power of seeking advice and embracing collaboration. By connecting with other Filta franchisees, she learned valuable insights and adapted best practices to her own business. She also emphasizes the broader impact of Filta’s services, understanding that maintaining cooking oil quality directly influences food quality and customer satisfaction.


As a woman in a traditionally male-dominated industry, Keri faced her share of challenges. However, she focused on her strengths, outworking the competition and proving that success is defined by hard work and seizing opportunities. Today, her Filta business has surpassed a million dollars in gross revenue, and she’s excited to explore the vast potential ahead.


Keri’s journey is an inspiring reminder that setbacks can lead to remarkable achievements. If you’re facing a turning point, take her advice: bet on yourself. You might just discover your own path to success with Filta.
