# Good News for Oklahoma City Commercial Kitchens: Filta is Expanding!

Great news for restaurants, schools, hospitals, and other commercial kitchens in Oklahoma City! As recently reported by The Journal Record, Filta Environmental Kitchen Solutions is targeting Oklahoma City as a prime market for expansion. This means local businesses will soon have easier access to Filta’s innovative services for filtering cooking oil, cleaning fryers, and recycling waste oil.


Filta’s arrival in Oklahoma City promises a range of benefits for commercial kitchens. Their core service, complete deep fryer management with on-site microfiltration, helps extend the life of cooking oil, potentially cutting oil consumption in half. This not only leads to significant cost savings but also contributes to a more sustainable operation.


Furthermore, Filta takes care of the messy and often hazardous task of fryer cleaning, eliminating the need for staff to handle this chore and addressing related safety concerns. When it’s time for an oil change, Filta guarantees that 100% of the used oil will be recycled into eco-friendly biodiesel, supporting environmental responsibility.


According to The Journal Record, Filta’s Vice President of Franchise Development, Rob Totten, noted that Oklahoma City is considered one of their top 10 prime markets for growth this year. While a Filta franchise previously operated in the city, its closure left a demand for these valuable services, with the Tulsa-based franchise even temporarily serving locations like the Paycom Center.


Filta serves a wide array of industries, including restaurants, amusement parks, colleges and universities, hospitals, hotels, and even stadiums and arenas. This demonstrates the broad applicability and value of their services for various types of commercial kitchens.
