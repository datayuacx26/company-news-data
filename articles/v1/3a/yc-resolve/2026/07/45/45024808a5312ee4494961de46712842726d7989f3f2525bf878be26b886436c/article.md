# How M3G Achieves Safety & Schedule Certainty with Resolve Coordination

### Project Types:


**Food & Beverage, Chemical Processing Facilities**


[M3G](https://www.m3gprojetos.com.br/) specializes in engineering design for food, beverage, pharmaceutical, and chemical facilities across South America for clients like Proctor & Gamble, Nestle, Mondelez, Unilever, and other top brands. Their goal is to help clients design functional facilities and get them to production as fast as possible.


They recently turned to Resolve to accelerate their project delivery and improve confidence in their coordination process. **One issue alone helped prevent 3 weeks of startup delay at a food production plant.**


### Key Challenges Faced:


- Difficult to understand 2D drawings resulted in overlooked design issues
- Past coordination and VR workflows required IT, exports, and hours of setup per model review
- Delays in production startups due to rework cause project overruns and client frustration


Before Resolve, M3G struggled with coordination done via 2D screens and when they tried other VR solutions they were time consuming and required IT support every time. With Resolve, model reviews became simple and portable—enabling engineering teams to collaborate directly with owners onsite and remotely, without IT support or model prep.


> “If you have a one-month project deadline, Resolve helps you finish within the month and with fewer issues.”
> — Mauricio Gouvea, CEO, M3G


## **MEP Coordination with Clarity and Predictability**


Resolve’s platform has transformed M3G’s coordination process. Engineering teams can perform virtual inspections with owner O&M teams accelerating approvals, eliminating rework, and reducing costly delays. Previously, setting up a VR session required days of preparation, file conversions, and technical expertise.


Now, M3G is able to leverage Resolve’s desktop and VR viewers to collaborate with owners and catch issues early. From equipment layouts to safety hazards, teams are able to stay on schedule and avoid redesigns.


### Key Issue Avoided:


**Insufficient visibility for food safety monitoring**


In one facility walkthrough, Resolve revealed that there was insufficient visibility for monitoring process areas. This would have blocked inspections by the safety and quality team, requiring major late redesigns.


Instead, M3G was able to update the design to include more visibility windows **preventing up to a 3 week delay in startup!**


### Stay on Schedule with Confidence


Resolve helps M3G ensure that when a model is approved, it’s final. With Resolve, M3G has been able to more quickly identify designs that don’t align with food safety specifications or client maintenance requirements. This helps avoid production delays.
