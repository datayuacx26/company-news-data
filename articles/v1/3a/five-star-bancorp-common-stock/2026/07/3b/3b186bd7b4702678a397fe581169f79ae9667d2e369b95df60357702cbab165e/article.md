# Five Star Bancorp Announces Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Release Date and Webcast

[PDF Version](https://investors.fivestarbank.com/node/10021/pdf)


RANCHO CORDOVA, Calif., July 09, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Five Star Bancorp (Nasdaq: FSBC) (“Five Star” or the “Company”), a holding company that operates through its wholly owned banking subsidiary, Five Star Bank (the “Bank”), expects to report its financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, after the stock market closes on Wednesday, July 22, 2026.


Management will host a live webcast for analysts and investors to review this information at 1:00 PM ET (10:00 AM PT) on July 23, 2026.


The live webcast will be accessible from the “News & Events” section of the Company’s website under “Events” at https://investors.fivestarbank.com/news-events/events. Please pre-register for the event using this link. The webcast will be archived on the Company’s website for a period of 90 days.


**About Five Star Bancorp** Five Star is a bank holding company headquartered in Rancho Cordova, California. Five Star operates through its wholly owned banking subsidiary, Five Star Bank. The Bank has nine branches in Northern California. For more information, visit https://www.fivestarbank.com.
