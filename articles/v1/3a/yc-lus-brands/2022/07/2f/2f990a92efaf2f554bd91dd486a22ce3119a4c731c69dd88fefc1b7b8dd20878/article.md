# Why You Need An Easy Curly Hair Routine

Allow us to paint a picture that we’re sure every curly girl knows all too well. You’ve just stepped out of the shower after washing and conditioning your hair. Now comes the myriad of styling products. Section by section, you slop on a sticky leave-in conditioner, mousse and/or cream, perhaps topped off with a serum or spray—all in the name of a wash-and-go routine. But what about layering on four or five products says “wash and go,” anyway?


In the end, you’re left with a bathroom cabinet that looks like a beauty supply store, a whooole lot of time and money spent and…well, that’s it. Rarely does this more-is-more approach actually give you the curls that we want.


That’s because what you need is an easy curly hair routine. That’s us LUS (Love Ur Self)! 😁


Since our launch, we’ve refused to accept that curly hair has to be complicated. We knew there had to be an easier way, but since we couldn’t find the perfect formulas for a truly easy curly hair routine that also delivered on results, we created it. And that’s how LUS (Love Ur Self) was born. 🙌


The foundation of our brand is our easy[3-Step System,](https://loveurcurls.com/pages/3-step-system) which takes the guesswork out of maintaining beautiful and healthy curls, saving you both time, money and energy.

##### Steps 1 & 2: Shampoo and Conditioner


Our[shampoo](https://loveurcurls.com/products/love-ur-curls-shampoo) and[conditioner](https://loveurcurls.com/products/love-ur-curls-ultra-hydrating-detangling-conditioner) make up the first two steps of our beloved 3-Step System. There’s an old (but persistent) myth in the curly hair community that it doesn’t actually matter which products you use in the shower, and that styling products are the only place that good formulas count. But that couldn’t be farther from the truth! Selecting good-for-you shampoos and conditioners that are specifically formulated with curly hair in mind, is crucial for achieving soft, bouncy and defined curls.


Our shampoo is formulated to be gentle on your hair, something that’s extremely important for curls. It's free of sulfates and harsh detergents to ensure that while your scalp and hair are being cleansed of excess oil, debris and build-up, your hair isn't being stripped of its much-needed natural oils and moisture.


Then, our ultra-rich, silicone-free conditioner slides through your hair like butter, binding moisture into your hair and leaving it soft and hydrated. It’s infused with shea butter and moringa oil to repair dry, damaged hair, and is totally colour-safe and suitable for all curl types.


Together, these two steps ensure your hair is primed and ready for styling.

##### How to Use Our All-In-One Styler


Start with freshly washed, wet hair. If your hair is already dry, use a[spray bottle](https://loveurcurls.com/collections/accessories/products/lus-flairosol-spray-bottle) to re-wet your curls. You'll want your strands to be pretty wet for our products to work effectively, since LUS (Love Ur Self) formulas are water-activated.


First, part your hair into sections. Pump the All-In-One into your hand, using 1-2 pumps per section, rub your hands together and rake the product through your hair, from root to ends.


Um…that’s it. No, seriously!


When we say we believe in an easy curly hair routine, we really mean it.
