# How To Maintain A Healthy Scalp With Curly Hair

What comes to mind when you think of healthy curly hair full of volume and shine? Like so many of us, you might think that having healthy locks all comes down to the products you use. This is partly true, but we’ll let you in on a little secret; healthy hair actually starts with a healthy scalp.


If you’ve noticed that your scalp isn’t feeling its best and your hair is feeling a little rough around the edges, it’s time to get to the root of the problem. Whether you’re looking for the low-down on how to grow curly hair faster or just want to give your scalp the makeover it deserves, we’re here to help! Our top scalp care tips will have you waving bye-bye to an unhealthy scalp and hello to longer, healthier, and shinier curls.


Ready? Let’s dive in!


## Common Causes and Symptoms Of An Unhealthy Scalp


When our scalp isn’t at its healthiest, we’re prone to experiencing some pesky symptoms, including dandruff, dryness, dermatitis—but what are the causes?


If you’ve been battling one of these symptoms and can’t quite put your finger on why, here are some of the most common reasons below:


Too many hair products: While giving your curls some TLC with your favorite hair products can go a long way every now and again, using too many products on your hair can lead to a build-up of excess oils and chemicals, which irritates our scalp and stunts hair growth.


Chemicals: If you regularly bleach, perm or relax your hair and your scalp isn’t feeling great, you may have found the culprit! Chemicals in bleach and hair colors like sulfates and hydrogen peroxide can cause some major stress on the scalp and, in some cases, can even induce hair loss.


Heat products: When we use flat or curling irons, they can strip the hair of its natural oils, leaving our curls and scalp extra dry and thirsty.


Genetics: There’s not too much we can do to prevent genetics from affecting our hair, but it’s good to be aware that some people are simply born more prone to dry scalps. Don’t worry, though - there are lots of things that can help! Stick around, and we’ll tell you how!


Your diet: Just as your diet can have a huge impact on your skin, it can also have an affect on your scalp. In terms of scalp issues tied to diet, lack of hydration and essential fatty acids are particularly to blame.


The environment: No matter how much TLC you give your scalp, heat, cold weather, pollution, climate change, and chemicals in the air can all lead to an unhealthy scalp.


Reactions to ingredients: If you’ve recently switched hair products and notice that your scalp looks sore and irritated, you might be experiencing an allergy to one of the ingredients. Speak to your doctor to identify which ingredients could be causing your scalp to react.


Hormones: Various life stages can bring a surge of new hormones that can have an affect on your scalp health. When going through menopause, for example, our natural levels of estrogen drop, causing the amount of sebum our scalp and hair produces to drastically reduce, leading to a dry scalp.


Although having a little dandruff and a slightly dry scalp every now and again is more of an annoyance than anything else, a dry scalp that won't go away can be uncomfortable and irritating. Getting to the bottom of why these symptoms occur is a great start, but how can you make sure your scalp stays healthy moving forward?


To keep your curls healthy and hydrated, make sure you’re not only using the right products but also making the necessary lifestyle changes to ensure your scalp is as happy as it can be!


Let’s look at a few ways to keep your scalp in top-top shape.

Whether you want to learn how to grow curly hair faster or how to maintain a healthy scalp with curly hair, we can’t stress how important it is to nourish your scalp from the inside out—aka be mindful of what you eat and drink.
