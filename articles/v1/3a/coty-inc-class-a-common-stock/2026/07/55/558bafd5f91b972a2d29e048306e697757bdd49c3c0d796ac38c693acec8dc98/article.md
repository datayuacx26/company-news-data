# Megan Thee Stallion Enters Fragrance Industry & Introduces New “Hot Girl Summer Eau de Parfum” with Coty


SHARE WITH :


Press release IMAGES


**NEW YORK – July 2, 2026** -- Coty Inc. (NYSE: COTY) (Paris: COTY), one of the world’s largest beauty companies with a portfolio of iconic brands across fragrance, color cosmetics, and skin and body care, is partnering with Megan Thee Stallion to launch Hot Girl Summer Eau de Parfum. Available Summer 2026, the fragrance marks latest expansion of the three-time, Grammy award-winning superstar and entrepreneur’s business portfolio.


The limited-edition product is designed as a unisex offering, reflecting Megan’s inclusive approach to self-expression across her entrepreneurial endeavors. Hot Girl Summer Eau de Parfum will be available in 90 ml and 10 ml, exclusively at selected retailers in the US, UK, and Canada and[MeganTheeStallion.com](http://megantheestallion.com/) .


The fragrance is crafted with a rich blend of creamy coconut milk, orchid blooms and warm vetiver oil, delivering a scent that is both radiant and sensual. The result is a captivating composition that unfolds smoothly on the skin and lingers with lasting presence.


Encased in a sculptural, flame-shaped bottle, the design draws inspiration from Megan’s Hot Girl Summer lifestyle, capturing its bold, fiery spirit. Hot Girl Summer Eau de Parfum transitions seamlessly across seasons, offering a luminous profile that works year-round, from summer to winter.
