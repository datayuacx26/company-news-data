# GitOps Launch Week Announcement

At Nullstone, we believe developers should be able to submit a single pull request that contains everything needed to deliver a feature or bug fix. No manual configuration in another system. No manual steps at deploy time. Instead, fully automated and identical across environments.


GitOps has become a powerful way of working for infrastructure teams. As part of an effort to empower developers with self-service, we believe GitOps should be usable for developers to configure their infrastructure, architecture, and much more. Instead of being just an infrastructure management tool like ArgoCD/FluxCD, we believe that GitOps can be used to build, deploy, and operate cloud-native applications with less fuss.


All week, we’re launching Nullstone GitOps to achieve this goal. Stay tuned to learn more.
