# Justin Jefferson and Under Armour Elevated JJets Flight School to New Heights in its Second Year

Throughout the weekend, athletes stepped directly into Justin’s blueprint for success. On-field sessions emphasized route precision, footwork, and competitive execution, while film study alongside Justin and his performance team provided athletes with deeper insight into preparation and mental processing at the highest level. Each athlete received hands-on coaching designed to sharpen both technical skills and football IQ. Beyond the field, Flight School athletes participated in a curated Under Armour Campus Brand Store shopping experience with Justin, connecting product innovation with personal expression


As part of Under Armour’s mission to support local athletes, JJets Flight School opened its doors to the Baltimore community. Student athletes from Baltimore City Public High Schools who are a part of Under Armour’s Project Rampart Initiative, attended a live training session and witnessed elite competition firsthand. Project Rampart plays a key role in Under Armour’s local impact, supporting Baltimore City schools through uniform outfitting, facility enhancements, coach training, and academic programming.
