# Introducing the UA Flare

At its core is Liquid Baselayer technology, crafted to deliver the same comfort and feel as UA's iconic Baselayer apparel. Combined with a DESMA direct-injection construction that seamlessly bonds the upper and sole, it creates a fit so frictionless and adaptive it feels like a second skin.


A two-tone knit adds visual depth with a softened, almost blurred effect that enhances dimension and texture, while a high-resiliency PU foam underfoot delivers responsive energy return with every step. The result is a shoe that moves differently, feels different and looks unlike anything else. For athletes who move instinctively and express themselves just as boldly off the field as they do on it, the UA Flare is built to keep up and stand apart. By design.


The UA Flare first appeared on the runway of Feng Chen Wang during the label’s spring-summer 2027 show at Paris Fashion Week in a special collaborative colorway releasing later this year.
