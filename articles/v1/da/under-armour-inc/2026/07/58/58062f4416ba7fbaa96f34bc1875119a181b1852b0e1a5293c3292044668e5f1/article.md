# Under Armour Celebrates the Off-Season in New "Rest Less" Sportswear Campaign

Under Armour launches “[Rest Less](https://www.underarmour.co.uk/en-gb/t/restless/) ”, its latest Sportswear campaign and the brand’s boldest expression of UA Sportswear to date, blurring the lines between performance, style, and contemporary culture. Fronted by World Cup-winning goal scorer[Ferran Torres](https://www.instagram.com/ferrantorres/) , football stars and Under Armour athletes[Fermín López](https://www.instagram.com/fermin/) , and[Ollie Watkins](https://www.instagram.com/olliewatkins/) , alongside artist[Rilès](https://www.instagram.com/0riles/?hl=en) , social creator[Sharky](https://www.instagram.com/ohnosharky/?hl=en) , and actor[Nick Mohammed](https://www.instagram.com/nickmohammedy/) , the campaign brings together a unique cast of cultural tastemakers spanning sport, music, and entertainment.


Rest Less builds on the brand’s evolving Sportswear vision with a social-first campaign that reimagines the off-season. Set within the fictional world of the five-star Hotel Armouré, the campaign brings to life the ultimate summer destination where athletes, artists, and entertainers check in but never truly switch off. At Hotel Armouré, resting is an extreme sport. Everyone arrives with major baggage, and relaxation doesn’t stand a chance.
