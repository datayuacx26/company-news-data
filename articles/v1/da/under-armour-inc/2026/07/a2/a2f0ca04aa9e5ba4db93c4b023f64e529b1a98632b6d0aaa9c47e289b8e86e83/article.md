# Under Armour Advances Its T-Shirt Legacy with the Launch of The Bouncy Tee

That vision comes to life in Under Armour’s new campaign for The Bouncy Tee, featuring 2026 ACM Album of the Year Award winner Parker McCollum, who the brand officially welcomed to its growing roster of athletes and artists; and Grammy-nominated artist, multi-platinum global superstar, and entrepreneur Gunna. Both artists bring established musical careers and distinct creative perspectives that align naturally with Under Armour’s continued evolution across entertainment and culture. They also represent how performance today extends far beyond the field and gym to the stage, studio or a night out.


At the core of The Bouncy Tee’s innovation is a blend of ultra-smooth Pima cotton and NEOLAST™, an advanced fiber designed to provide stretch, resiliency, and long-lasting shape retention while addressing sustainability challenges. The result is a fabric that responds to movement with a subtle “bounce,” maintaining its structure, softness, and fit wear after wear. Rapid-dry technology accelerates evaporation and moisture management, allowing the shirt to dry quickly and keep pace with whatever the day demands.
