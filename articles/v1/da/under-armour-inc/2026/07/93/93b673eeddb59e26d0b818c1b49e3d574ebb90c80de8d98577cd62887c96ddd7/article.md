# Under Armour Welcomes Tinx as New Brand Ambassador

Under Armour today welcomes Christina Najjar, better known as Tinx, as its newest brand ambassador, representing both UA footwear and apparel.


A creator, entrepreneur, bestselling author, and one of the most influential voices shaping modern conversations around confidence, wellness, and self-worth, Tinx joins the UA family as the brand continues to broaden its definition of performance.


The partnership begins with[Summer of Outside ,](https://events.underarmour.com/summer-of-outside/) a new event series inspired by the movement Tinx created and her annual commitment to spend less time on her phone, and spend more time outdoors, prioritizing human connection, and doing the things that make her feel her best.


The series will make stops in San Francisco, Chicago, and New York City, bringing together movement, community, and the rituals Tinx has become famous for. Each meet up will feature movement, product giveaways, Christina’s Café pop-up experience, and moments designed for attendees to disconnect, recharge, and leave feeling better than when they arrived.
