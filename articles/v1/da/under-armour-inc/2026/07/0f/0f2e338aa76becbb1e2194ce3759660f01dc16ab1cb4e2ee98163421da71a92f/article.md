# Under Armour Welcomes Li Yueru

Under Armour today announced the signing of WNBA player Li Yueru, one of the most accomplished figures in Chinese basketball and a proven competitor on the international stage. With this partnership, Li becomes Under Armour’s first female Chinese professional basketball athlete.


The partnership marks an important milestone in Under Armour’s continued commitment to the basketball, further strengthening the brand’s presence in China and across the global game. Together, Under Armour and Li will champion performance, inspire the next generation of athletes and help drive the continued growth of basketball.


One of the leading centers in the global game, Li has established herself among the sport’s most respected players. Her career highlights include a silver medal at the 2022 FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup and a championship title at the 19th Asian Games. In recent years, she has continued to elevate her game through competition in Europe’s top professional leagues, WNBA and Unrivaled, earning recognition for her consistency, resilience and impact on the court.
