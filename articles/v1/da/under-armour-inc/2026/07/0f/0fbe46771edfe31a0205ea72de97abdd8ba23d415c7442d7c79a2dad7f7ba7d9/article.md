# Under Armour and Lapstone & Hammer Celebrate MLB All-Star Week with Exclusive Sneaker Release and Launch Party

As Philadelphia prepares to welcome MLB All-Star Week, Under Armour and Lapstone & Hammer are honoring the city with a limited-edition footwear release. The UA HB-Lo “All Lines Lead Home” transforms Under Armour’s new basketball-inspired sportswear silhouette into a tribute to the movement and energy that pulse through the city every day.


The release will make its debut at a special launch event at Lapstone & Hammer’s storefront on July 12, giving locals and visitors first access before the shoe becomes available on UA.com beginning July 14.


Philadelphia has long served as a cultural crossroads, a city built on creativity, individuality and pride. At the center of that creative community is Lapstone & Hammer, the renowned premium lifestyle brand founded by Brian Nadav, and a local destination for streetwear culture and connection. The brand’s commitment to craftsmanship and community mirrors Under Armour’s own belief that great products are built with purpose.
