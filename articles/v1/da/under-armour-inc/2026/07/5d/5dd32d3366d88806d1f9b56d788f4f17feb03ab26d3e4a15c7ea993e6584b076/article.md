# Under Armour and Bryce Harper Celebrate Philadelphia with the Launch of the Harper 11

Bryce Harper and Under Armour introduce the Harper 11, the latest evolution of Harper's signature cleat franchise and the next chapter in one of baseball's longest-running athlete partnerships. The Harper 11 "All Lines Lead Home" serves as the debut colorway and first retail launch of Bryce's 11th signature cleat with Under Armour.


Earlier this year, Harper announced the extension of his partnership with Under Armour, continuing a relationship that began in 2011 when he became the face of Under Armour Baseball. In 2015, Harper and Under Armour signed a 10-year extension that was the largest endorsement deal for a baseball player at the time. More than a decade later, the Harper 11 represents the latest milestone in a partnership built on innovation and a shared commitment to the game.


Designed around the idea of futuristic protection for baseball, the Harper 11 draws inspiration from Harper's experience as a catcher, catcher's gear and motorcycle protective equipment. The result is a cleat engineered to deliver lightweight protection, breathability and stability while meeting the demands of today's game.
