# Jordan Thompson and Under Armour Host First Collegiate Volleyball Camp

Jordan Thompson and Under Armour hosted its fifth-annual Jordan Thompson (JT) Rise Academy volleyball camp, which focused on the development and mentorship of collegiate athletes for the first time. The first collegiate-focused academy provided a highly-curated experience and a premier development platform for some of volleyball’s top female athletes preparing to take the next step toward the professional level.


Since launching in 2022, the JT Rise Academy has provided young athletes with access to volleyball training, coaching, and performance insights. Over its first five years, the camp annually provided 40 high school athletes with the opportunity to receive hands-on instruction, skill development, and direct mentorship from Jordan.
