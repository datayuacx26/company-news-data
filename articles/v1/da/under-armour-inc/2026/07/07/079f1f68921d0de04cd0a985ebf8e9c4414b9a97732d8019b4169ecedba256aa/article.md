# Under Armour Names BOYNEXTDOOR as Global Brand Ambassadors

Since its founding, Under Armour has supported those driven to become better, recognizing performance not only in competition but in the everyday commitment to pursue what matters. BOYNEXTDOOR reflect that same mindset, having built a distinct identity by growing at their own pace and forging their own path.


'Commit' marks a shift in how Under Armour talks about performance, moving the focus away from wins and results toward the motivation that keeps people showing up in the first place. The campaign explores that idea through the people who live it, starting with BOYNEXTDOOR as its first ambassadors.
