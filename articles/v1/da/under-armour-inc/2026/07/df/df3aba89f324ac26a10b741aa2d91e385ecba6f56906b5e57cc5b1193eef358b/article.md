# Under Armour to Host UA Next: City Classic Baltimore

Building on the legacy of premier basketball events, Under Armour announced today that it will host its first-ever City Classic Baltimore later this summer. This showcase is designed to put the next generation of stars on center stage in a city known for its deep basketball roots, passion, and culture. This year, UA is bringing 20 of the best boys and girls high school basketball players each from all over North America to its hometown to compete in its marquee showcase event of the summer.


The City Classic is set for Saturday, August 15 at St. Frances Academy in Baltimore, MD with the girls' game set for 12 p.m. ET. and the boys’ game to follow at 2 p.m. ET. The games will be open to the public, with tickets available for purchase[HERE](https://events.underarmour.com/ua-next-city-classic/) .
