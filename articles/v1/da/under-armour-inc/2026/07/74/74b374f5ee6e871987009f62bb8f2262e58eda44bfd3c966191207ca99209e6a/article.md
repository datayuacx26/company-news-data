# Marine Serre and Under Armour Introduce a New Vision of Performance

As Under Armour celebrates 30 years of redefining athletic performance, the brand enters a new chapter with French fashion house Marine Serre. In a debut capsule collection, Marine — inspired by Under Armour’s iconic 2000s sportswear archive and its most famous innovation, the baselayer — reframes sports as a universal language of the body.


The limited-edition capsule launches June 5 exclusively on[marineserre.com](https://www.marineserre.com/) , at select Marine Serre retail locations, and through an immersive pop-up experience in Paris running June 5–7, before expanding globally later this summer on[UA.com](https://www.underarmour.com/en-us/t/collaborations/marine-serre/) . Located at Rue de Turenne, 75003, the pop up will feature an environment that brings the collection into dialogue with space — where the raw energy of sport meets the refinement of craftsmanship and art, inviting visitors into the world of Marine Serre x Under Armour.
