# UA Next All-America Game Returns to Camden Yards for Second Straight Year

The All-America Baseball Home Run Derby and Game will both be open to the public. Tickets are available for purchase for $10 each, with proceeds benefiting the[Baltimore Urban Baseball Association](https://www.bubabaseball.org/) (BUBA), which serves youth across Baltimore using baseball as a catalyst for positive programming, community connection and empowerment. GameChanger will serve as the official streaming and content partner, providing live coverage and event content throughout the weekend so that fans nationwide can have access to the action from Camden Yards. Tickets can be purchased at


[https://mlb.tickets.com/?agency=MLB_MPV&orgid=56069&pid=9772913](https://mlb.tickets.com/?agency=MLB_MPV&orgid=56069&pid=9772913) .


Additional brand partners will help create a first-class experience for players, families and baseball fans alike. Gatorade will provide dugout hydration for the athletes as well as an on-site activation area, while pMotion will conduct performance assessments and provide hands-on treatment for participating players. The presenting partner of the Home Run Derby, Rawlings, will showcase their latest product throughout the event, alongside player equipment experiences and branded integrations. Zebra Pen will gift premium writing products, support player signing sessions and fan zones, and present the "Signature Moment of the Game", while Bat Around will engage fans and players alike with its one-of-a-kind gamified batting experience.


The UA Next All-America Game has become one of the nation's premier amateur baseball events, showcasing future collegiate and professional standouts as they compete against the best talent in the country. The event's return to Camden Yards underscores its continued growth and prestige, giving participants the chance to perform on one of baseball's most celebrated stages.


**About UA Next**


UA Next is Under Armour's youth sports platform designed to empower the next generation of athletes through elite competition, training and development opportunities. Through premier events and experiences across multiple sports, UA Next helps provide young athletes with opportunities to showcase their talents and pursue their goals on and off the field.
