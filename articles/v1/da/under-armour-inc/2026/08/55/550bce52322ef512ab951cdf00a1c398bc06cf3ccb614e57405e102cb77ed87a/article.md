# Your Softest Legging Just Got Better: UA Meridian

Now, the Meridian Legging is back with its most refined update yet. An upgraded fabric delivers the same buttery feel you know and love, now even softer and slightly thicker for enhanced coverage. Better for the planet, it's engineered to shed fewer microfibers than traditional performance materials, helping reduce environmental impact without sacrificing the premium comfort and performance that made the Meridian a standout.


Thoughtful design updates further elevate the experience. A no-front-seam construction creates a cleaner silhouette, while additional all-around coverage provides a more secure, body-hugging fit. A SmoothSculpt™ waistband stays comfortably in place, delivering support without digging in or rolling down.
