# Under Armour Basketball Introduces the Fox 3

De’Aaron Fox plays at a pace few can match. His game is defined by a lightning-quick first step, elite end-to-end speed and the ability to stop on a dime, turning full-speed attacks into controlled finishes, kickouts and game-changing plays. The Under Armour Fox 3 was built to meet that exact demand: a lightweight performance basketball shoe engineered for players who move fast, stop hard and strike when the opportunity presents itself.


De’Aaron’s third signature basketball shoe, the Fox 3 is tailored to enhance his style of play: explosive speed with the control to brake, plant and attack again. That balance between speed and control is the foundation of this sneaker, and every element is designed to support the dynamic nature of his game.


Lighter than its predecessor at just 11.9 ounces, the Fox 3 has evolved in design and construction to give athletes like De’Aaron an edge. A lightweight mesh upper helps secure the foot without adding unnecessary weight, giving players the containment they need through hard cuts and lateral movement. Across the upper, a textured fox-tail-inspired graphic nods to De’Aaron’s name and captures the sense of speed and motion that defines his game.
