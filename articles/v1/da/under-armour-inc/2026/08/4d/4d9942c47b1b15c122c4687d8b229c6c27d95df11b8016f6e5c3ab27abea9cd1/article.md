# Under Armour Signs Rising Canadian Hockey Star Jade Iginla

Off the ice, Iginla is passionate about creating greater access and opportunity for women and girls of color in the sport. She serves as a board member of the Women of Colour Hockey Collective, helping support a more inclusive future for the game.


Iginla also comes from one of hockey's most storied families. The daughter of Hockey Hall of Famer Jarome Iginla, she is the third sibling to be drafted into professional hockey. Her brother Tij was selected sixth overall by the Utah Mammoth in the 2024 NHL Draft, while her brother Joe was selected 65th overall by the Calgary Flames in the 2026 NHL Draft.


Under Armour has maintained a strong presence in hockey since 2001, equipping athletes across Canada and the U.S. with performance apparel, HeatGear® baselayers, footwear and training gear designed to help them perform at their best.
