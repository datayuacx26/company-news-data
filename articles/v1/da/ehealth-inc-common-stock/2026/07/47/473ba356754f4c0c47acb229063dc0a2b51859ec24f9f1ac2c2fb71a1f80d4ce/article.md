# GLP-1 drugs and Medicare: How beneficiaries can save on these blockbuster medications

# GLP-1 drugs and Medicare: How beneficiaries can save on these blockbuster medications


*By [Whitney Stidom](https://news.ehealthinsurance.com/experts/whitney-stidom) , Vice President of Consumer Enablement, [eHealth](https://www.ehealthinsurance.com/)*


For the


**[over 60%](https://www.prb.org/resource/tra-rising-obesity-in-an-aging-america/)** of Americans 65 and older who are overweight or obese, GLP-1 drugs are a potential game changer. But for many Americans, the


high cost


of these medications is a barrier to use, including limited


access


through Medicare plans


.


That


is


starting to change


, especially with the


July 1


launch


of the


**[GLP-1 Bridge program](https://www.ehealthinsurance.com/medicare/coverage/medicare-glp-1-bridge-program/)** .


Originally developed to help treat type 2 diabetes, GLP-1 drugs have proven


**[effective at promoting weight loss](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9877131/)** , at least for as long as the medications


remain


in use.


And more older people want to use them:


A


new


**[eHealth survey](https://news.ehealthinsurance.com/_ir/68/20266/2241301e46429cff33aa6eac30dd6024/Topline%20Results%20-%20Healthcare%20Pulse%20Survey%202026.pdf)** found that


28


% of Medicare beneficiaries sa


id


they have used a GLP-1 medication at some point, while


39


% sa


id


they


would be


interested in trying one of these drugs


if


they were covered by Medicare


.


While Medicare


health plan


coverage for these drugs varies depending on the type of insurance plan and what these drugs are being prescribed for


,


recent changes are starting to make these drugs more accessible for more Americans


.


Here is a look at how access to GLP-1 drugs is changing for people with Medicare:


**Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage**
If you have Original Medicare or a Medicare Advantage plan, GLP-1 drugs are generally covered for the treatment of certain chronic conditions. For instance,


**[98% of Medicare Advantage plans](https://news.ehealthinsurance.com/ehealth-analysis-of-glp-1-drug-coverage-among-medicare-advantage-and-affordable-care-act-plans)** cover at least one GLP-1 drug for the treatment of diabetes.


Coverage for weight loss is far less common, with fewer than


**[1% of Medicare Advantage plans](https://news.ehealthinsurance.com/ehealth-analysis-of-glp-1-drug-coverage-among-medicare-advantage-and-affordable-care-act-plans)** offering that


.


**GLP-1 Bridge p rogram**
As of


July 1, eligible Medicare beneficiaries with Part D coverage may be able to access certain GLP-1 medications for $50 per month through a temporary program running through 2027


. E


ligible beneficiaries must be at least 18 years old and fall into one of the following groups:


1.


Have a


body mass index (


BMI


)


of 35 or higher


.


2.


Have a BMI of 30 or higher along with certain health conditions, including heart failure, uncontrolled high blood pressure, or chronic kidney disease


.


3.


Have a BMI of 27 or higher along with conditions like pre-diabetes,


a previous


heart attack or stroke, or peripheral artery disease


.


Given how new this program is,


**[most Medicare beneficiaries](https://news.ehealthinsurance.com/_ir/68/20266/2241301e46429cff33aa6eac30dd6024/Topline%20Results%20-%20Healthcare%20Pulse%20Survey%202026.pdf)** (73%) are unaware of it.


Importantly,


eligibility depends on meeting specific prior-authorization criteria, your doctor’s approval and enrollment in a standalone prescription drug plan or Medicare Advantage (MA) coordinated care plan that offers prescription drug coverage in 2026.


**Paying out of pocket without insurance**
For people without Medicare or insurance coverage, including from any state, government or federally funded program, the Trump Administration is taking steps to make


GLP-1


more affordable through


**[TrumpRx.gov](http://www.trumprx.gov/)** .


The site offers certain types of GLP-1 drugs with monthly prices that ****


**[average $350 and as low as $199](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-major-developments-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/)** , depending on dosage.


That compares to


previous


monthly prices of over $1,000. If you


don’t


have insurance, this is among the most affordable ways to access a GLP-1 for weight loss or the treatment of a chronic condition such as diabetes


.


If you or a loved one is considering a GLP-1 drug, it is important to review your health benefits carefully to understand when these drugs may be covered and at what price, especially with Medicare


coverage


policies


, qualifying criteria and plan benefits


continuing to evolve.
