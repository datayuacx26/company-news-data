# Who is eHealth?


**[Learn more about eHealth](https://news.ehealthinsurance.com/_ir/68/20267/c42156f59e3f7f5db818bb79185612b4/eHealth_Narrative_August_2026.pdf)**


For nearly 30 years, **[eHealth](https://www.ehealthinsurance.com/)** has helped millions of Americans find the healthcare coverage that fits their needs at a price they can afford, using data, artificial intelligence and a consumer-first approach to help people quickly and effectively compare insurance options.


As a leading independent licensed insurance agency, eHealth offers access to plans from more than 180 health insurers, including national and regional companies, supporting consumers during their working years and retirement.


eHealth’s advisors build life-long relationships with consumers and help match them with the insurance plans, services, and support they need to live healthier, more financially secure lives.
