# Alight honored as one of TIME’s Most Sustainable Companies for 2026

Alight, Inc. (NYSE: ALIT), a leading benefits administration provider of health, wealth and leave solutions, has been listed by TIME and Statista as one of the[World’s Most Sustainable Companies for 2026](https://time.com/article/2026/06/23/worlds-most-sustainable-companies-2026/) . The award program recognizes companies that prioritize sustainability in their business practices.


“The long-term success of Alight is built upon a commitment to ethical practices, integrity and transparency in how we serve clients,” said Christine Terborg, Vice President of Corporate Sustainability and Impact at Alight. “We are guided by a purpose to help millions of people achieve better health and financial security, which is why we champion sustainability. We’re honored to receive this distinction from TIME as one of the world’s most sustainable companies, recognizing our commitment to responsible and transparent business practices as we help build a future where health and financial security are within reach for all.”


For this award, TIME and Statista evaluated companies in more than 20 key performance indicators related to sustainability, including compliance with international reporting standards, greenhouse gas emissions and progress toward sustainability goals and initiatives. Out of over 5,800 of the world’s largest and most influential companies assessed, the top 750 were awarded based on their transparency, accountability and impact on the environment.


Alight’s Corporate Sustainability and Impact program is focused on building a more sustainable future. Initiatives include:


- Receiving Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) validation for Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions reduction targets, reinforcing Alight’s commitment to reaching net‑zero emissions by 2050.
- Publishing Alight's fifth annual Global Impact Report, providing transparent disclosure aligned with leading sustainability reporting frameworks and stakeholder expectations
- Advancing governance of sustainability issues through oversight by the Board's Nominating and Governance Committee and cross-functional sustainability working groups
- Expanding supplier sustainability engagement to encourage emissions measurement, target setting and responsible business practices across Alight’s value chain


Statista publishes hundreds of worldwide industry rankings and company listings with high-profile media partners. This research and analysis service is based on the success of statista.com, the leading data and business intelligence portal that provides statistics, relevant business data and various market and consumer studies and surveys.


This recognition follows several other awards for Alight in 2026, including[USA Today America’s Climate Leaders,](https://www.alight.com/about/newsroom/alight-listed-as-one-of-usa-today-americas-2026-climate-leaders)[Newsweek America’s Most Responsible Companies of 2026](https://www.alight.com/about/newsroom/alight-named-to-newsweek-2026-americas-most-responsible-companies) and the[Gold Hermes Creative Award](https://enter.amcpros.com/hermes/winners/) for leave communications.
