# Alight earns top 5 rankings for User Experience and Vendor Satisfaction in 2025 Sapient HR Systems Report

“Alight stands out as a top performer in the 28th Annual Sapient Insights HR Systems Survey. With strong adoption among large enterprises and healthy momentum in new RFP activity, Alight has clearly made a name for themselves among their current users and potential buyers,” said Stacey Harris, Chief Research Officer at Sapient Insights Group. “Comments from Alight’s users highlighted the platform’s combination of compliance reliability, advanced analytics and personalized engagement tools. Alight’s expertise in navigating complex benefits strategies, its robust wellness resources and its ability to drive better business outcomes show that Alight’s users value their partnership with a vendor that is clearly working with them to achieve long-term success.”


According to[Alight’s 2025 Employee Mindset Study](https://www.alight.com/thought-leadership/2025-alight-employee-mindset-study) , 64% of employees with comprehensive workplace benefits report an exceptional employee experience—20 points higher than those without comprehensive benefits, highlighting the critical role of integrated support systems in helping employees navigate healthcare needs and benefits.


"We are honored to be recognized by the Sapient Insights Group for our commitment to delivering exceptional user experiences and being a trusted partner to our clients," said Rob Sturrus, Chief Client Officer at Alight. "These top 5 rankings are a testament to the dedication of our teams and the ongoing innovation behind the Alight Worklife® platform, as we empower organizations to create meaningful benefits experiences that support employee wellbeing and drive lasting business outcomes."
