# Validation Institute confirms Alight Healthcare Navigation delivers measurable savings

Alight Healthcare Navigation combines engagement, data and clinical guidance to steer employees toward efficient, high-quality care through the Alight Worklife® platform. By connecting patients with the experts and information they need, employees and employers can see better outcomes.


The Validation Institute found that cost reductions are primarily driven by connecting members with providers who demonstrate a consistent ability to deliver high‑value care. These providers typically:


- Use less intensive care pathways, helping members avoid unnecessary surgeries, imaging and other high‑cost interventions.
- Make higher‑quality referrals and maintain privileges at facilities known for strong outcomes.
- Emphasize preventative care to better manage chronic conditions and reduce downstream complications.
- Diagnose and treat in‑office whenever appropriate, minimizing avoidable specialist visits or procedures.


This rigorous methodology ensures that reported savings reflect real, validated financial impact for employers while highlighting the role of high‑performing providers in improving both cost and care quality.


“Alight’s savings claims are both substantial and valid, backed by our $100,000 ERISA Immunity guarantee,” said Al Lewis, founder of the Validation Institute and a leading authority on measuring employee benefits vendor outcomes.
