# Alight achieves Gold Hermes Award for valuable work on leave of absence communications

Alight’s leave communications prepare employees in advance of key leave milestones to improve confidence and outcomes. At the same time, the materials empower managers with clear action items, guidance and resources to support employees effectively. For both employees and managers, Alight’s leave communications materials increase awareness and understanding of leave benefits, processes and services. The program also delivers a consistent, transparent communication experience aligned to the client’s specific leave program.


Key features of Alight’s leave communications include:


- **Launch-related information with FAQs:** Emails are sent to employees when a company launches Alight’s Leave Solutions offering, explaining how Alight will support them before, during and after a leave of absence. FAQs help reduce anxiety and call volume by providing answers to common employee questions about eligibility, job protection, documentation requirements, pay during leave and return-to-work expectations.
- **Flyers, newsletters and guides:** These communications contextualize leave of absence in various formats, including digestible visual references, embedded communication in employee newsletters and an overview of what employees can expect when requesting and taking a leave of absence.
- **Manager/HR FAQs and information sessions:** A more detailed FAQ set tailored to HR teams, addressing policy alignment, compliance considerations, escalation paths and how Alight integrates with existing absence programs. Additionally, HR teams receive a structured live session to answer questions about the transition, a step-by-step checklist and a quick guide on how to manage employee leave of absence and workplace accommodations.


Hermes Creative Awards honor the messengers and creators of the information revolution, recognizing the creative industry’s best publications, branding collateral, websites, videos, advertising, marketing, and communication programs.
