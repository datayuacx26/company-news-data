# Alight named a Leader across all market segments in the 2026 NelsonHall NEAT assessment for Benefits Administration

**CHICAGO** — **April 21, 2026** — Alight, Inc. (NYSE: ALIT), a leading benefits administration provider of health, wealth, leave and point solutions, today announced it has been positioned as a Leader in each of the six market segments evaluated in NelsonHall’s Benefits Administration: Employee Wellness Reimagined NEAT vendor evaluation.


This marks the second consecutive year Alight has been recognized as a Leader by NelsonHall across the following benefits administration market segments:


- Overall
- Digital Focus
- Health & Welfare Focus
- Marketplace Focus
- Total Benefits Outsourcing (TBO) Focus
- Geographical Reach


NelsonHall’s NEAT assessments evaluate vendors based on their ability to deliver immediate value to clients as well as their capacity to meet future client requirements. Alight’s positioning as a Leader reflects its strengths across digital innovation, client outcomes and its ability to support complex benefits programs at scale.


In the 2026 NEAT assessment, NelsonHall recognized Alight for its:


- **Comprehensive benefits administration capabilities** , supporting large, complex global organizations.
- **Advanced digital platform and analytics** , enabling personalized guidance, automated decision support and conversational experiences.
- **Strong focus on experience and year-round engagement** , using AI-enabled health navigation, wellbeing guidance, and personalized communications to move beyond event-based interactions to continuous, context-aware support.
- **Ongoing investment in innovation and its ecosystem partnership** , including AI-driven tools that improve efficiency, insights and service delivery.


“Alight has been recognized as a Leader in NelsonHall’s 2025 and 2026 Benefits Administration NEAT evaluations, specifically for its Health & Welfare (H&W) capabilities, including administration, clinical advocacy and decision support,” said DeeAnna Warrington, Principal Analyst, HR & Talent Transformation at NelsonHall. “This is delivered through the scalable Worklife platform, enabling employers to address benefits complexity, improve cost management and support employee health outcomes. Ongoing investment in interoperability and AI-enabled capabilities, combined with strategic partnerships, positions Alight to meet rising demand for more integrated, personalized and outcome-oriented benefits delivery across the employee lifecycle.”


Alight supports many of the world’s largest organizations by delivering integrated benefits administration solutions through the Alight Worklife platform and decades of service delivery experience, serving as a mission-critical partner to clients. By bringing together data, insights and human expertise, Alight helps organizations navigate complexity, improve decision-making and deliver better outcomes for their people.


“Being recognized as a Leader by NelsonHall highlights our unwavering focus on delivering meaningful outcomes for organizations and their people,” said Rohit Verma, CEO at Alight. “At Alight, we’re focused on building an integrated benefits experience that harnesses our scale, user base and strong partnerships. Through purposeful use of AI, we’re able to create differentiated experiences and drive impactful results, ensuring organizations can confidently support their people across health, wealth and leaves.”
