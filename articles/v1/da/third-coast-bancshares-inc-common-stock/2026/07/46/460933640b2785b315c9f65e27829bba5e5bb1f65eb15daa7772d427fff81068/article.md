# Third Coast Bancshares, Inc. Announces 2026 Second Quarter Earnings Release and Conference Call Schedule

**About Third Coast Bancshares, Inc.**


Third Coast Bancshares, Inc. is a commercially focused, Texas-based bank holding company operating primarily in the Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth, Greater Houston, and San Antonio markets through its wholly owned subsidiary, Third Coast Bank. Founded in 2008 in Humble, Texas, Third Coast Bank conducts banking operations through 21 branches encompassing the four largest metropolitan areas in Texas.
