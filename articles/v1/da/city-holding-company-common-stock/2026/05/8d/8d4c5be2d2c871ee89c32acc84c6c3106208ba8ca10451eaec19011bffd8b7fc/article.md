# City National Bank’s Tim Whittaker promoted to Executive Vice President of Commercial Banking

Charleston, West Virginia (May 20, 2026) – City National Bank and City Holding Company are pleased to announce the promotion of longtime employee and commercial banking leader Tim Whittaker to Executive Vice President of Commercial Banking.


Whittaker joined City in 1998 as Credit Officer. He served as Senior Vice President and Chief Credit Officer since 2004. In this new role, Whittaker manages City’s commercial lending department.


“Tim’s leadership and dedication have helped drive City’s commercial lending strategy for more than 20 years,” said President and CEO Skip Hageboeck. “We appreciate his influence in building a culture of excellence, and we’re excited to see him move into this expanded role.”


Whittaker is a graduate of West Virginia State University and a Certified Public Accountant. He and his wife Amy reside in Teays Valley and their two daughters, Emma and Abby, are students at WVU. An active member of his community, he serves on the Board of Directors of the Charleston Area Alliance and is a member of Dunbar United Methodist Church.


**About City National Bank**


Headquartered in Charleston, W. Va., City National Bank serves consumers and businesses in more than 95 locations across West Virginia, Kentucky, Virginia and Ohio. Nationally recognized for its customer service and as one of the most highly rated banks in America, City has been delivering innovative banking products and supporting its local communities for more than 60 years. City ranked #1 in Customer Satisfaction for Retail Banking in the North Central Region of the country in the 2026 JD Power U.S. Retail Banking Satisfaction Study, its sixth such award in nine years.
