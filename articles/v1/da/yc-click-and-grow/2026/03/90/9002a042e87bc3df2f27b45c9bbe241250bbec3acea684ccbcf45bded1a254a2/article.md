# Click & Grow Welcomes New CEO

Click & Grow is delighted to welcome Johan Schepers as our new Chief Executive Officer, effective as of last month. He


joins us at a pivotal moment as we continue expanding our mission to help people everywhere experience the joy of growing their own foods and plants, effortlessly and sustainably.


****


*“Click & Grow is uniquely positioned at the intersection of innovation and accessibility, shaping how people reconnect with fresh food and gardening in their everyday lives. I’m excited to build on this strong foundation and help shape the company’s next phase of growth.”*


— Johan Schepers, CEO of Click & Grow


With leadership experience at SodaStream (PepsiCo) and other industry innovators, Johan has a proven track record of purposeful growth and meaningful consumer impact that perfectly complements our vision for a greener future.


As we embark on this next phase, we look forward to the fresh perspectives and energy Johan brings to our journey.
