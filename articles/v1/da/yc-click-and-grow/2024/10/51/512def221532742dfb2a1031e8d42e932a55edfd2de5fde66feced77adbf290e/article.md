# Click & Grow partners with Ermonix to extend its portfolio of indoor gardens

*Hydrovase by Ermonix*


Great news for indoor gardening enthusiasts! You can now enjoy Click & Grow's simple-to-use plant pods in a wider variety of garden designs, thanks to our new partnership with Ermonix. This collaboration gives you the best of two worlds – Click & Grow's proven plant pod technology combined with Ermonix's distinctive indoor garden designs.


*Garden Cube by Ermonix*


Through this partnership, Ermonix becomes another official Click & Grow partner brand, meaning their indoor gardens are fully tested and certified to work seamlessly with Click & Grow plant pods. Check them out


[here](https://ermonix.com/) !


In their own words: “Click & Grow plant pods have been a great asset to our product range. We wanted to provide our customers with an effortless gardening journey, with a guarantee that their plants will sprout. Click & Grow’s extensive range of high quality plant pods gives us peace of mind that our customers will have the best possible outcomes while using our indoor gardens.”


*Ed and Hanan from Ermonix*


## **Click & Grow Partnership Program**


Since 2009, we've been perfecting the art of indoor gardening, becoming one of the most trusted names in the industry. Our proprietary pre-seeded plant pods have proven so successful and easy to adapt that other brands such as Urban Cultivator and now Ermonix are choosing to use them in their garden designs, skipping the need to develop their own plant pod technology.


Through our Partnership Program, we're making it easier than ever for companies to enter the world of indoor gardening. Partners get instant access to Click & Grow's proven technology, extensive plant portfolio, and customer engagement solutions. It provides a cost-efficient way for companies to create their own indoor gardens with a guarantee that their customers receive an excellent growing experience from day one.
