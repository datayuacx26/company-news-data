# Click & Grow and Eugeria Working Together to Support Dementia Care

**We’re proud to share that Eugeria, a Canadian, women-led company advancing dementia care, is now including Click and Grow smart gardens as part of their curated selection of evidence-based care.**


Eugeria is a trusted source for caregivers, senior living communities, and healthcare providers across North America. Their mission is to improve the quality of life for people living with dementia through thoughtful, non-pharmaceutical solutions, ranging from sensory tools to therapeutic activities. By adding Click and Grow smart gardens, Eugeria is expanding access to meaningful, nature-based experiences for older adults.


Our smart gardens are designed to bring the calming and restorative benefits of nature into any space, no green thumb required. For individuals living with dementia, this connection to nature can promote sensory stimulation, cognitive engagement and emotional well-being.


We’re honored that Eugeria has chosen to include our smart gardens in their efforts to support dignified, person-centered care. Together, we’re helping more people live well, every day.
