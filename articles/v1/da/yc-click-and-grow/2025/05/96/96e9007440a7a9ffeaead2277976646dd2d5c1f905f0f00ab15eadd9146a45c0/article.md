# Click & Grow Partners with Little Kitchen Academy as their Exclusive Indoor Garden Provider

### ****


We are thrilled to partner with[Little Kitchen Academy](https://littlekitchenacademy.com/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=22558273086&gbraid=0AAAAA_padZ5d-FxiSJ-G1V92lNtP_qZrx&gclid=Cj0KCQjwotDBBhCQARIsAG5pinOrU7BPusWtDYmU5BfQK_1n9tP5OkkvtAIAMSsfB80F63oume3onnQaAgdjEALw_wcB) as their exclusive indoor garden provider across all U.S. and Canadian locations.


Starting this summer, Little Kitchen Academy's living food walls will feature our Smart Gardens—bringing hands-on, garden-to-table learning to students in the most meaningful (and delicious) way.


"This partnership is a perfect fit," said Felicity Curin, Founder of Little Kitchen Academy. "Our Living Food Walls now powered by Click & Grow bring nature to life for our students, allowing them to see, smell, touch, and taste fresh herbs, fruits, and vegetables. It's a hands-on garden-to-table experience.”


We’re proud to support Little Kitchen Academy's mission to help children build confidence, independence, and healthy habits—one fresh green at a time. 👩🍳 🥬


### **Click & Grow Partnership Program**


The[Click & Grow Partnership Program](https://www.clickandgrow.com/pages/partnerships) is a great fit for companies looking to enter the indoor gardening segment but lack experience with plant products. Whether you're a startup or a global brand, we'll help you build a gardening product your customers will love using for years to come.


Our expertise and mature technology make it possible for more and more people around the world to grow their own food indoors, no matter where they live. With Click & Grow, users can grow plants 30% faster than they would using traditional methods. Our pre-seeded plant pods allow users to grow the fresh, vitamin-rich produce quickly and hassle-free.


The partnership program offers an easy way for partners to enter the world of indoor gardening with instant access to our technology, extensive plant portfolio, and customer engagement solutions.
