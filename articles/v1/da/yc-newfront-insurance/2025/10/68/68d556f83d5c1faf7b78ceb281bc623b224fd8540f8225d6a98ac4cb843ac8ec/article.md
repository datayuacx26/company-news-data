# Beyond Automation: AI's Strategic Role in Transforming Business Insurance

At the Ai4 2025 Conference in Las Vegas, Lin Yuan, Chief AI Officer and EVP of Engineering at Newfront Insurance, explored how artificial intelligence is redefining the business insurance landscape. The technology is not just automating processes but also transforming the way brokers, underwriters, and clients collaborate.


Lin encouraged attendees to think beyond automation when it comes to AI. Instead of focusing solely on speeding up simple tasks like form-filling or document routing, forward-thinking organizations are using AI to enhance risk management, client service, and business insights.


Newfront has several examples of AI-driven solutions that are tackling long-standing industry pain points:


-


[Coverage Gap Analysis](https://www.newfront.com/cga) uses machine learning to review policies, detect coverage gaps, and recommend improvements.


-


[D&O Predictive Modeling](https://www.newfront.com/predictive-modeling) uses 25 years of litigation data to forecast potential exposure and optimize limits.


-


[Contract Review](https://www.newfront.com/contract-review) instantly parses third-party agreements to flag mismatches with client coverage.


-


[RiskPulse](https://www.newfront.com/riskpulse) delivers real-time visibility into loss trends and risk mitigation opportunities.


-


[Benji](https://www.newfront.com/total-rewards/technology) , Newfront’s AI assistant for HR teams, simplifies benefits navigation through Slack and mobile apps.


Lin’s conversation emphasized that not only building valuable point solutions, but also using integrations, not point solutions, is what unlocks exponential value. By layering AI across systems like Salesforce, AMS, and its own proprietary platform, Navigator, Newfront ensures that data flows seamlessly, enabling smarter and faster decisions across the organization.


Internally, Newfront’s engineers leverage AI for productivity gains, from automated code reviews to context-aware development tools, amplifying creativity and collaboration. Lin urged attendees to think of AI as a strategic co-pilot, one that helps teams manage risk more intelligently and deliver faster in an industry long defined by complexity.
