# Why Experimentation is Essential to Understanding Generative AI’s Potential and Challenges

### Introduction


In the rapidly evolving landscape of generative AI, curiosity and hands-on experimentation are some of the most powerful tools we have to unlock its potential. At Artos, we’ve seen how experimentation not only builds an intuitive understanding of what AI can achieve but also reveals the often-underestimated complexities involved in building robust, scalable AI solutions. Here, we’ll dive into why experimentation with generative AI is vital, from uncovering AI’s unique strengths and limitations to understanding the true challenges of scaling these technologies effectively.


### Part 1: Experimenting with AI to Understand Its Potential (and Limits)


When working with generative AI, there’s an early temptation to assume it can handle a broad range of tasks without issue. However, as we’ve seen in both internal testing and through our collaborations with companies across life sciences, the value of experimenting in various AI modalities—text, image, and data processing—cannot be overstated.


### Why Experimentation Matters


Generative AI is not sentient, nor is it a magic bullet. Experimentation reveals specific contexts where AI performs particularly well, as well as areas where it struggles. For example:


-


**Analysis Exploration** : Exploring nuances in how AI systems do analyses differently can help users find valuable use cases rather than expecting uniform, high performance across all tasks.


-


**Modality Exploration** : AI models also vary in their strengths across modalities—image generation might deliver compelling visuals, but when handling structured data analysis, those same tools often lack precision. Testing across these modalities highlights differences and lets users tailor applications to their specific strengths.


By experimenting within these modalities, teams learn faster where generative AI’s utility lies and how to leverage it effectively without overestimating its capabilities. Experimentation here translates to building confidence in applying AI in a meaningful, realistic way.


### Part 2: Recognizing Challenges as AI Applications Scale


Exploring generative AI’s potential on a small scale—whether using ChatGPT for basic Q&A or Perplexity for contextual responses—gives a fairly limited view of the capabilities and limitations of AI. The true complexity of generative AI becomes apparent when it’s scaled up for automated, interconnected processes, especially in complex industries like life sciences.


### The Difference Scaling Makes


When businesses move from standalone generative AI tasks to orchestrating multiple AI-driven steps, challenges emerge that experimentation at scale helps expose:


-


**Edge Cases** : Increased usage surfaces edge cases that were not visible in one-off scenarios. AI may handle a large volume of standard requests well, but scaling reveals a multitude of fringe cases where models might require recalibration.


-


**Complex Workflows** : Beyond single-step interactions, it’s in multi-step workflows where limitations become clear. For instance, AI’s need for precise instructions and handling nuances often means that scaling requires additional layers of human oversight or secondary AI to achieve desired results.


Testing AI applications at scale allows for discovering and preemptively addressing these challenges, which is where companies realize that additional investments in workflow design, error handling, and model fine-tuning are essential.


### The Role of Experimentation in Real-World Applications


From our work with clients who explore both general-purpose tools like ChatGPT and specialized vertical-specific AI systems, we’ve observed the broad applicability of generative AI but also the need for a nuanced approach to deployment. Experimentation provides an essential pathway for businesses to move beyond surface-level AI interactions and strategically apply AI where it truly makes a difference.


Whether it’s identifying optimal use cases or navigating scaling hurdles, a commitment to consistent experimentation reveals insights that no amount of theoretical planning could provide. As companies invest in this hands-on process, they gain the knowledge needed to build AI applications that are not just functional but truly impactful.


### Conclusion


For businesses eager to embrace generative AI, starting with experimentation is essential. It’s in testing and scaling where you’ll discover AI’s hidden strengths, areas for improvement, and the knowledge needed to make these tools work in your favor. Generative AI’s potential is vast, but only through ongoing exploration can we fully harness it—and mitigate the challenges of scaling and complexity.
