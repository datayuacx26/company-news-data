## What is Profound?


Profound is primarily a data product. It tracks how brands show up across[AI answer engines](https://www.maintouch.com/blogs/what-is-answer-engine-optimization-complete-guide) like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, and that's what most customers use it for. The pricing reflects the depth of that analytics layer. They're landing in the same camp as Semrush and Ahrefs: sell the data, sell to agencies, let someone else do the work.


They've raised real money. Per public reporting, $35 million Series B led by Sequoia in August 2025, then $96 million Series C in February 2026 at a $1 billion valuation. That puts them firmly in the enterprise tier of[the AEO category](https://www.maintouch.com/blogs/best-aeo-tools) .


Late 2025, Profound launched a Zapier-style workflow tool called Agents with pre-built templates for common jobs:


- Content Refresh
- AEO FAQ Generation
- Competitive Research
- Net-New Content


It's a secondary product layered on top of the core analytics. You string together steps in a workflow builder rather than having purpose-built agents that know exactly what to do. After you get your data, Profound leaves you to go coordinate the rest: separate tools for backlinks, technical fixes, content publishing. Profound connects to WordPress, Sanity, and Contentful for publishing.
