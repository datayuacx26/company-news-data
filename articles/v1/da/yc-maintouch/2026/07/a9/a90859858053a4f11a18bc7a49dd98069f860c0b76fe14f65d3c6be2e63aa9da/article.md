## How Profound Works


Profound launched as one of the first dedicated AI visibility products. It monitors ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, tracking which brands get cited in AI-generated answers, how often, and in what sentiment context.


The core mechanics:


- Prompt tracking across the major AI engines
- Competitive share-of-voice reporting
- Sentiment analysis on how your brand gets framed
- A workflow builder called Agents for configuring content and optimization steps


It slots into your existing content workflows without replacing them. The scope is AI search visibility only, not general SEO, not social.


The standout feature is Prompt Volumes: panel data from opted-in consumers showing what people actually ask AI tools, with demographic breakdowns attached. Think of it as keyword research rebuilt for the[answer engine optimization](https://maintouch.com/blogs/what-is-answer-engine-optimization-complete-guide) era, built into the same product instead of bolted on separately.


The Agents workflow builder is real, but it has a learning curve. User feedback is consistent on this: you need a bit of technical comfort before it pays off.


## Why Consider Profound Alternatives?


Profound earns its keep for a specific buyer: enterprise brands with real analytics budgets, a dedicated AI search team, and a genuine need for deep citation intelligence. If that's you, the tool holds up.


The gaps show up the moment your situation drifts from that profile.


Pricing is the first wall. The Growth plan at $399/month monitors three engines: ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Want Claude or Grok? That's a custom-priced Enterprise conversation. Independent reviews flag this consistently as the primary friction point.


But the deeper issue is architectural. Profound tells you where the gaps are and leaves the room. Someone on your team still has to write the content, ship the technical changes, and chase the backlinks. The tool was built to inform decisions, not to execute them.


A few more limits worth naming:


- One workspace per account, so agencies, holding companies, and multi-brand teams hit a hard ceiling
- CMS integrations cover WordPress and Sanity, per the knowledge base
- The Zapier-style workflow builder was built for agency setups, not lean in-house startup teams


That's the real reason teams start looking elsewhere: not the price, but the handoff. For a full feature breakdown, see the[Maintouch vs Profound](https://maintouch.com/blogs/maintouch-vs-profound) head-to-head.
