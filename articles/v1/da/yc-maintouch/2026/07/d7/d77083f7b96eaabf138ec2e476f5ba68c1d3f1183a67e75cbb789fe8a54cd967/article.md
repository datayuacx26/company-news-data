## What Are AI Visibility Tracking Tools?


Traditional rank tracking answers one question: where does your page sit on Google for a given keyword? Position 3, position 11, page two. Clean, numeric, easy to chart.


AI visibility tracking measures something else entirely. These tools watch how often, and in what context, your brand shows up inside the answers AI engines generate. The five that matter right now: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Claude.


There's no ranked list to check. Ask Claude or Perplexity a buying question and the engine assembles an answer from multiple sources: it either names your brand or it doesn't. You're measuring citation frequency and share of voice against your competitors, across engines, for the prompts your buyers are actually typing. Your rank on Google tells you nothing about whether you show up in that answer.

### What does "share of voice" actually mean in AI visibility tracking?


It's the percentage of relevant prompts where your brand gets cited, measured against your competitors. If AI engines answer 100 prompts about your category and your brand appears in 22 of them while your top competitor appears in 40, your share of voice is 22%. Tracking this across engines, not just Google, shows you where you're winning the citation game and where a competitor is quietly taking the answer instead.


### How is AI visibility tracking different from traditional SEO rank tracking?


Traditional rank tracking gives you a position number for a keyword on Google. AI visibility tracking measures citation frequency, how often your brand is named inside AI-generated answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Claude. There's no ranked list to check. You're measuring presence in the answer itself, not position on a results page. A brand can rank #1 on Google and still get skipped entirely in AI Overviews if its content isn't structured for citation.

### What is schema health and why does it affect AI citation?


Schema is structured markup, JSON-LD code on your page that tells AI engines and search engines exactly what the page is about. It acts as a binary qualifier: pages without clean, accurate schema are deprioritized before content quality is even evaluated. Schema health refers to whether your markup is present, error-free, and matches what's actually on the page. A price change that isn't reflected in your schema, for example, creates a mismatch that can suppress citations. Maintouch monitors schema coverage and automates fixes via CMS webhook on every publish or update.

Yes, and citation share is more about structure than volume. AI engines prefer self-contained passages that answer one question completely, formats like FAQ sections and comparison tables, and clean schema markup. A small team that fixes schema, restructures existing content into passage-level chunks, and tracks which prompts competitors are getting cited for can move the number without publishing dozens of new posts. ### How do fan-out queries affect AI visibility, and how do I use them?


Fan-out queries are the sub-questions an AI engine generates internally before assembling an answer. When someone asks Perplexity "what's the best tool for tracking AI citations," the engine breaks that query into related sub-questions and pulls answers for each. If your content answers those sub-questions, you get cited.
