## What Is AirOps and How Does It Work?


AirOps is a no-code AI workflow tool for content operations. You wire up data sources, AI models, and CMS destinations inside a visual builder called Grids, and the pipeline runs at scale without custom code.


On top of Grids sits Page360, an analytics layer that pulls Google Search Console data, engagement metrics, conversion tracking, and content freshness into a single view. In late 2025 AirOps shipped an Insights product adding Sentiment Tracking and Query Fan-outs to surface[AI search visibility data](https://maintouch.com/blogs/llm-visibility-complete-guide-tracking-ai-search-rankings) alongside GA4 and Search Console signals.


The product fits best when your bottleneck is execution speed, not strategy. It assumes you already know what to publish and just need it out the door faster. The teams it works for:


- E-commerce brands, SaaS companies, and agencies investing in the[best AEO tools](https://maintouch.com/blogs/best-aeo-tools) to protect discoverability as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews reshape how people search
- Teams publishing 20 or more articles a month
- Teams with a working content playbook already in place
- Teams with technical fluency on staff, since Grids assumes comfort with pipelines


## Why Consider AirOps Alternatives?


AirOps earns its keep when the real problem is pushing more on-brand content out the door faster. The Grids builder, Brand Kit, and CMS connections do that job well, for teams that already have a settled strategy and enough volume to warrant the setup time. The gaps show up once your needs run wider than production speed.


### Steep learning curve and developer dependency


Building basic workflows takes hours to weeks; real proficiency takes months. G2 clustering shows 25+ reviews flagging the learning curve, with 17+ calling it a genuine barrier, per ContentMonk. Marketing teams end up leaning on developers to iterate, and even then, generic output still needs 2-3 hours of editing per article before it's usable.


### Task-based pricing with a sharp cliff


AirOps prices on tasks instead of seats. One article in a serious workflow burns 500-800 tasks, as Slate documents. Want multi-engine insights or team collaboration? You jump straight to Pro at $2,000/month. No mid-tier stepping stone. That's a 10x price jump with nothing in between.


### AI visibility tracking gated by tier


The Solo plan only covers ChatGPT Insights. Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Gemini require Pro. The "Answer Engine" diagnostic grades content readiness instead of showing whether you're actually cited today.


### No closed-loop execution


AirOps stops at generation and publishing. No automated backlink building, no strategic keyword discovery. The post-publish work that actually decides ranking lands back on your team. You end up with pages that exist and don't climb.
