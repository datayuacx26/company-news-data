# Overcoming Labor Shortages with AGVs and AMRs

## The Current State of Labor Shortages in U.S. Manufacturing


The American Manufacturing sector is grappling with a severe labor shortage that threatens to undermine its growth and competitiveness. By 2030, the manufacturing skills gap could result in[2.1 million unfilled jobs](https://nam.org/2-1-million-manufacturing-jobs-could-go-unfilled-by-2030-13743/?stream=workforce) , potentially costing the industry $1 trillion in 2030 alone.
