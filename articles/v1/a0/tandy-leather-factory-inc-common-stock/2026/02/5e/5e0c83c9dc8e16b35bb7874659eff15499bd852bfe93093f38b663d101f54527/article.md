# Introducing Floral Charms: Leather Keychains with a Creative Touch

We’re excited to introduce **Floral Charms** , our newest leather crafting inspiration designed to add handcrafted beauty to everyday essentials. These decorative floral shapes are cut from durable leather, making them both visually striking, highly customizable and practical for daily use.


To support makers of every skill level, **Floral Shapes** are available as **veg-tan leather cutouts** , both in-store and online, and include a[free tassel pattern download](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0063/5997/3970/files/Tassel.pdf?v=1770069834) . This makes it easy to assemble and customize your own leather floral charm using your preferred dyes, finishes, and hardware. Whether you’re an experienced leatherworker or just getting started, these shapes are simple, versatile, and enjoyable to work with.


Download the[free tassel pattern](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0063/5997/3970/files/Tassel.pdf?v=1770069834) , choose from **[daisy shapes](https://tandyleather.com/products/half-dozen-daisies-kit) ,[rose shapes](https://tandyleather.com/products/half-dozen-roses-kit) , or both** , and craft a leather floral keychain that adds a personal, handmade touch to keys, bags, or gifts. We can’t wait to see how you bring these Floral Charms to life in your next leather project.
