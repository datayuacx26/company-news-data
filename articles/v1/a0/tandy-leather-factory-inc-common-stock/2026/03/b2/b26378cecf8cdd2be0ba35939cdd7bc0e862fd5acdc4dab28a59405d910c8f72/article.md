# Free Pattern: Embroidered Egg Charm Accessory

Add a touch of Easter or springtime in under an hour with these adorable embroidered leather egg accessories. Using veg-tan or bright chrome-tan leather, along with a bit of dye and colorful thread, you can create a festive egg that’s guaranteed to last.


To give it some extra depth, lightly stuff each egg with a bit of fluff before closing the final stitches of the exterior stitch line. The result is a soft, slightly raised shape that gives the finished piece a nice 3D look.


This project is perfect for a DIY project just in time for Easter and the springtime season ahead. Download our FREE pattern below and give it a try!
