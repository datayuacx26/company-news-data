**Instructions:**


**Step 1** **:** Wet your leather circles with an ample amount of water using either a[sponge](https://tandyleather.com/products/cellulose-sponge) or a[spray bottle](https://tandyleather.com/products/fine-mist-sprayer) . (User discretion just ensure it's wet enough to tool and not too wet where it becomes difficult) ****


- Maker Tip! Grab a 6-7oz piece of veg-tan and trace one of the circles onto the thicker leather if you'd like to tool one or both the sides


**Step 2:** Grab a[wing divider](https://tandyleather.com/products/wing-divider) and set it to the size of the outside border you'd like. This project used 2 lines one which was 2cm from the edge and another .5cm away (2.5cm total).


**Step 3:** Stamp the outside border of your circle with the[brass sunburst stamp](https://tandyleather.com/products/sunburst-arch-border-brass-stamp) .


**Step 4:** Fill the inside circle with a pattern of your choice - like a basketweave pattern.


- Maker Tip! If you'd like to tool a design now is a good time and then work it into the basketweave design!


**Step 5:** Stamp the inside border between the basketweave pattern and the edge using a[flower border brass stamp](https://tandyleather.com/products/flower-border-brass-stamp) to blend in the edges.


**Step 6:** Use another[camouflage stamp](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-crescent-camoflage-stamp?variant=45233339924611) between these two border stamps to help add a little extra detail. Repeat for the opposite side and allow to dry.


**Step 7:** To make the gusset,[cut a 4" rectangular strip](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-strap-cutter) of 4/5 oz veg-tan that's 22" long


- Maker Tip! Use Tandy's new[regalia belts](https://tandyleather.com/products/regalia-belt-4x50) to save time strapping!


**Step 8:** Strap out a 1" wide and 50" strip using 4/5oz leather or use an Economy Veg-Tan Strip


**Step 9 (OPTIONAL):** Wet this strap and stamp any designs you'd like into it and allow to dry.


**Step 10:** Apply[pure neatsfoot oil](https://tandyleather.com/products/dr-jacksons-pure-neatsfoot-oil?variant=45233567498371) to the all the dry pieces of leather and allow the oil to dry.


**Step 11:** Dye the gusset and strap with[Fiebing's light brown pro dye](https://tandyleather.com/products/fiebings-pro-dye?variant=45233630019715) on both sides (follow antique steps below if you stamped it). Allow to dry.


**Step 12:** For stamped and tooled pieces of leather first apply[Fiebing's pro-resist](https://tandyleather.com/products/fiebings-pro-resist?variant=45233577197699) and allow to dry then apply[Fiebing's dark brown antique paste](https://tandyleather.com/products/fiebings-antique-finish?variant=45233593745539) on the inside and outside. Allow to dry.


**Step 13:** Apply[Fiebing's tan kote](https://tandyleather.com/products/fiebings-tan-kote?variant=45233560092803) on the inside and to seal the leather pieces and allow to dry.


**Step 14:** Set the wing divider again to 5mm and trace down both the long sides of the gusset to mark your stitching holes


**Step 15:** Punch out your stitching holes using the 3mm chisel on the line you just marked


**Step 16:** Place the[solid clip d-ring](https://tandyleather.com/products/solid-clip-dees) 1.5" down from the top of the d-ring in the center on both ends of the gusset and mark both the spots for holes. (Ensure the solid d-ring is as straight as possible.)


**Step 17:** Once the holes have been marked punch out the holes ensuring to keep them inline with each other.


**Step 18:** Set the solid d-ring with rapid rivets on both sides


**Step 19:** Begin[saddle stitching](https://tandyleather.com/blogs/tandy-blog/tandy-skills-saddle-stitching) one circle to the gusset once you've finished repeat for the opposite side


**Step 20:** Take your strap and put it through the strap hole on the[trigger snap](https://tandyleather.com/products/trigger-snaps) . Measure out 3" on the interior and center the strap.


**Step 21:** Punch a hole in the center of the strap towards the top of the 3"


**Step 22:** Line up the holes and set your rivets


**Step 23:** Put your trigger snaps on your solid clip d-ring and you've finished!
