# Gift Idea: Tote Insert with Painted In Blue

Tony Gonzalez, founder of Painted In Blue, created the perfect way to organize your tote bag with his custom organizer insert. Follow his step-by-step instructions below to get started and bring some order to your everyday bag!

**Instructions:**


**Step 1:** Print out, assemble, and place paper pattern on your leather.


**Step 2 (OPTIONAL)** : If you would like a liner use a thin leather of your choosing and glue it to your cut out piece. Once dry cut away the excess liner from the main piece.


**Step 3:** With a[wing divider](https://tandyleather.com/products/wing-divider) set the distance to 5MM, then lightly trace around the exterior of the pockets ensuring you leave one of the longer 8" sides without a mark.


**Step 4:** Using[tape](https://tandyleather.com/products/2535-650-tanners-bond-adhesive-tape?variant=45233583882371) go around the edges of the pockets and then adhere the pocket to the interior of the pattern.


**Step 5:** Following the marked lines punch out the stitching holes with your chisel.


**Step 6:**[Saddle stitch](https://tandyleather.com/blogs/tandy-blog/tandy-skills-saddle-stitching) the interior pockets together.


**Step 7:** Using your wing divider again set the distance to 5MM, then lightly trace around the exterior of the bag.


**Step 8:** Using the optional[positioning needles](https://tandyleather.com/products/t-positioning-needles-8-pack) you can begin saddle stitching your edges together.


**Step 9:** Insert and organize your tote bag!
