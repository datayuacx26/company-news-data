**Instructions:**


**Step 1:** Place the pattern on leather and begin cutting pieces B, and D out with a rotary cutter.


**Step 2 (OPTIONAL):** Cut out lining pieces A & C (This ensures no flesh side is exposed except around the crease, but does expose the magnetic rivets.)


**Step 3:** Mark the stitching lines with an[awl](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-scratch-awl) or[leather pen](https://tandyleather.com/products/leather-marking-pen) .


**Step 4:** Using a stitching chisel punch out the stitching holes. (Be sure to fold over piece D and use tape to stick the leather together while punching out those stitching holes.)


**Step 5:** Mark and punch out the holes for the magnets on piece B & D (if you're using a liner do not punch holes on B - only on A).


**Step 6:** Set magnets onto piece B & D (if you're using a liner do not set on piece B only on piece A).


**Step 7 (OPTIONAL):** Saddle stitch pieces A & B together.


**Step 8:**[Saddle stitch](https://tandyleather.com/blogs/tandy-blog/tandy-skills-saddle-stitching) the fold on piece D.


**Step 9:**[Tape](https://tandyleather.com/products/2535-650-tanners-bond-adhesive-tape?variant=45233583882371) together pieces C & D and sew together the pockets (Don't sew the outside yet!).


**Step 10:** Take the combined C & D piece and stitch onto the combined to A & B piece.
