**Instructions:**


Phone Case


**Step 1:** Trace **** the interior part of your phone case on paper then transfer it to veg-tan and trace it out. **(This pattern fits an iPhone Pro Max 16 or similar phone size - resize the pattern in printer settings for other phone sizes!)**


**Step 2:** Follow your traced pattern as you begin to cut it out ****


- Maker Tip! Cut it out slightly larger than your traced pattern so you can slowly cut it down to fit perfectly on your phone case!


**Step 3:** Wet your leather with an ample amount of water using either a[sponge](https://tandyleather.com/products/cellulose-sponge) or a[spray bottle](https://tandyleather.com/products/fine-mist-sprayer) . (User discretion just ensure it's wet enough to tool and not too wet where it becomes difficult) ****


**Step 4:** Place the tooling design on top of the leather and begin tracing the pattern with your[stylus](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-pro-modeling-tools?variant=31977877110915) onto the leather.


**Step 5:** Begin carving into the leather with your swivel knife.


**Step 6:** Tape the back of your project with painter or masking tape to prevent it from widening or stretching out while you tool on it


**Step 7:** Tool the pattern using the following stamps in order:


1. [Craftool® Ballpoint Stylus](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-ballpoint-stylus) (SKU: 8059-00) QTY: 1


2. [B935 Craftool® Stamp Beveler Smooth](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-smooth-beveler-stamp?variant=45233326063747) (SKU: 6935-00) QTY: 1


3. [B203 Craftool® Stamp Beveler Smooth](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-smooth-beveler-stamp?variant=45233326030979) (SKU: 6203-01) QTY: 1


4. [S631 Craftool® Stamp Seeder Smooth](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-seeder-stamp?variant=45233327603843) (SKU: 6631-00) QTY: 1 (use this for the spots!)


5. [S931 Craftool® Stamp Seeder Smooth](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-seeder-stamp?variant=45233327538307) (SKU: 6931-00) QTY: 1 (use this for the spots!)


6. [U58 Craftool® Stamp Mulefoot Serrated Pointed](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-pointed-mulefoot-stamp?variant=45233330028675) (SKU: 6858-00) QTY: 1


7. [A098 Craftool® Stamp Background Teardrop Checked](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-checkered-pear-background-stamp?variant=45233322688643) (SKU: 6098-00) QTY: 1


8. Add decorative cuts


**Step 8:** Use a[#3 Edge Beveler](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-edge-bevelers?variant=45233546526851) to round the edges of your leather piece (just the grain side).


**Step 9:** Apply Tokonole to the edges of your leather piece and burnish the edges for a smooth finish.


**Step 10:** Oil both leather using a


[Round Sponge](https://tandyleather.com/products/leathercraft-sponge-round-2-pack) and


[Dr. Jackson's Pure Neatsfoot Oil](https://tandyleather.com/products/dr-jacksons-pure-neatsfoot-oil?variant=31977747153027) .


**Step 11 (OPTIONAL):** Paint the armadillo with the maul and opossum with the swivel knife with[Angelus Paints](https://tandyleather.com/products/angelus-leather-paint) to give your case an extra pop!


**Step 12:** Coat the phone case with[Fiebing’s Pro-Resist](https://tandyleather.com/products/fiebings-pro-resist?variant=45233577197699) . Let fully dry for at least 24
hours before moving forward.


**Step 13:** Coat the front of both straps with[Fiebing's Antique Paste](https://tandyleather.com/products/eco-flo-gel-antique-8-floz?variant=45233571692675) and wipe off any excess. Let dry for at least 24 hours.


**Step 14:** Add a coat of[Fiebing’s Tan Kote](https://tandyleather.com/products/fiebings-tan-kote?variant=45233560092803) to add a protective finish to the phone case. Allow this to fully dry before continuing.


**Step 15:** Using[contact cement](https://tandyleather.com/products/tanners-bond-contact-cement) apply an ample amount to both the case and the flesh side of the leather (fuzzy side). Flip the phone case upside down and apply weight to the inside of the case and allow to set.


- Maker Tip! Scuff up the exterior of the phone case with a coarse piece of sandpaper to help the contact cement adhere better.


Hair Clip


**Step 1:** Trace **** the exterior part of your hair clip on paper then transfer it to veg-tan and trace it out.


**Step 2:** Follow your traced pattern as you begin to cut it out.


- Maker Tip! Cut it out slightly larger than your traced pattern so you can slowly cut it down to fit perfectly on your hair clip!


**Step 3 (OPTIONAL):** Give this hair clip extra depth and follow the tooling steps above with the same tools.


**Step 4:** Paint the your design with your choice of[Angelus Paints](https://tandyleather.com/products/angelus-leather-paint) to help your hair clip pop. Allow this to fully dry before continuing.


**Step 5:** Add a coat of[Fiebing’s Tan Kote](https://tandyleather.com/products/fiebings-tan-kote?variant=45233560092803) to add a protective finish to the hair clip. Allow this to dry before continuing.


**Step 6:** Use a[#3 Edge Beveler](https://tandyleather.com/products/craftool-edge-bevelers?variant=45233546526851) to round the edges of your leather piece (just the grain side).


**Step 7:** Apply[Tokonole](https://tandyleather.com/products/tokonole-burnishing-agent) to the edges of your leather piece and burnish the edges for a smooth finish. ****


**Step 8:** Using[contact cement](https://tandyleather.com/products/tanners-bond-contact-cement) apply an ample amount to both the case and the flesh side of the leather (fuzzy side). Apply pressure to the hair clip with clips or other tools to allow the contact cement to set. (Be careful not to glue something to this while waiting!)


- Maker Tip! Scuff up the exterior of the hair clip with a coarse piece of sandpaper to help the contact cement adhere better.
