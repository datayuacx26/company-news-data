**Instructions:**


**Step 1:** Cut out a 6.5" x 6.5" piece of embossed leather and utility leather (cutting the embossed slightly smaller).


**Step 2:** Dye, antique, or paint your 6.5" x 6.5" embossed veg-tan piece!


**Step 3:** Using the utility leather cut out the interior with a design of your choice.


**Step 4:** Carefully skive down the exterior edges at least 5CM from the edges.


**Step 5:** Bevel the interior edges from what you just cut out and the exterior of the utility leather.


**Step 6:** With both flipped over center the embossed leather on top of the utility.


**Step 7:** Using[Eco-Weld®](https://tandyleather.com/products/eco-flo-leather-weld-adhesive?variant=45233563762819) glue these pieces together and let them set until dry.


- Maker Tip! Place them under a heavy book or weights with a paper between them to help them set.


**Step 8 (OPTIONAL):** Dye, stamp, tool or take this time now to design anything on the leather tray.


**Step 9:** Using a wing divider set the distance to 5MM and the two pieces right side up. Trace the interior design and around the exterior.


**Step 10:** Using your[stitching chisels](https://tandyleather.com/products/88043-535-pro-line-diamond-stitching-chisels) punch out the interior design.


**Step 11:**[Saddle stitch](https://tandyleather.com/blogs/tandy-blog/tandy-skills-saddle-stitching) the interior pieces together.


**Step 12:** Now glue this piece to the leather tray (whichever side you glue it to will be the interior - so choose carefully). Allow to dry.


**Step 13:** Following your exterior wing divider lines punch out your stitching holes.


**Step 14:** Saddle stitch these pieces together.


**Step 15:** Pinching the closest corners together, saddle stitch each side of the tray ensuring to keep it tight for best appearance.


**Step 16:** Add your loose items and enjoy!
