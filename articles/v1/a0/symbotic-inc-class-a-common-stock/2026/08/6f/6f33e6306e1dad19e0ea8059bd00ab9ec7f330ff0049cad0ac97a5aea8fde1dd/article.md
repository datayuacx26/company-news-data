# Steve Pagliuca

## Steve Pagliuca


Director


Steve Pagliuca has been a member of Symbotic's Board of Directors since August 2026.


Steve is the Founder and CEO of PagsGroup, a growth capital investment firm with expertise in biotech, technology, media and sports. He is also a Senior Advisor to Bain Capital, a global investment firm managing approximately $180 billion in assets, where he previously served as Co-Chair. Earlier in his career, Steve founded the Information Partners Fund for Bain Capital and Dun & Bradstreet, one of the first technology-focused investment funds, which specialized in telecom, media and technology investments and achieved top-quartile returns. He began his career as a partner at Bain & Company, focused on the healthcare and technology practice.


Steve is currently a Chairman and Principal Owner of Atalanta B.C., the Serie A football club based in Bergamo, Italy, and the winner of the 2024 UEFA Europa League. He previously served as a Managing General Partner and Co-Owner of the Boston Celtics, where he was also Chairman of the Basketball Committee and Founder and President of the Boston Celtics Shamrock Foundation, leading programs to provide underserved children with access to housing, healthcare and educational resources. Through the Pagliuca Family Foundation, Steve established the Pagliuca Harvard Life Lab in 2016, a shared lab space supporting Harvard University students and faculty in biotech and life sciences research.


Steve holds a B.A. from Duke University and an M.B.A. from Harvard Business School.
