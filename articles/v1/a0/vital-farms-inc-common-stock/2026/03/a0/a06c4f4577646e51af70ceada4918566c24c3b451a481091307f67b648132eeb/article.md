# Salted Butter Cookies

## Salted Butter Cookies


Celebrate every baker's favorite ingredient with rich salted butter cookies — golden and snappy with just the right amount of salt. These cookies are easy to make and only need a few key ingredients to really sing.


---


Makes


24


cookies


[Print Recipe](https://vitalfarms.com/wprm_print/salted-butter-cookies)[Pin Recipe](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/create/bookmarklet/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fvitalfarms.com%2Fsalted-butter-cookies%2F&media=https%3A%2F%2Fvitalfarms.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F02%2FSalted-Butter-Cookies-6.jpg&description=Salted+Butter+Cookies&is_video=false)


### Ingredients


- 1


cup


Vital Farms Salted Butter,


room temperature


- 1


cup


powdered sugar


- 4


Vital Farms egg yolks,


room temperature, separated


- 3


cups


all-purpose flour


- 2


teaspoons


flaky salt


### Instructions


- Add the butter and powdered sugar to the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment. Mix on medium speed until the butter and sugar are well incorporated and light and fluffy, about one minute.


- Add 3 egg yolks to the butter mixture and mix again until combined.


- Add the flour and salt and mix again until fully incorporated.


- Place the dough between two large sheets of parchment, spreading as evenly as you can. Gently roll the dough out into about a 1/4-inch thickness. Place the dough on a baking sheet and chill in the fridge for at least one hour, until firm.


- Preheat the oven to 350 °F. Use a 2-inch round cookie butter to cut out rounds. Place them on a parchment-lined baking sheet about 2 inches apart. Repeat with the rest of the dough, re-rolling the scraps as necessary.


- Add the remaining egg yolk to a small bowl along with a splash of water. Whisk to combine. Brush the egg wash on each cookie. Take a fork and drag the tines lightly through the surface of the cookie in one direction, then again in the opposite direction to make a crosshatch pattern. Bake the cookies for about 15 minutes, until golden-brown. Cool completely before enjoying.
