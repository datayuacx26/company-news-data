# Cottage Cheese Turkish Eggs

## Cottage Cheese Turkish Eggs


A brunch dish worthy of company and easy enough for a weekday, inspired by the Turkish dish çilbır, with a protein boost from cottage cheese.


---


Makes


2


servings


[Print Recipe](https://vitalfarms.com/wprm_print/cottage-cheese-turkish-eggs)[Pin Recipe](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/create/bookmarklet/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fvitalfarms.com%2Fcottage-cheese-turkish-eggs%2F&media=https%3A%2F%2Fvitalfarms.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F02%2FCover-Photo-1-scaled.jpg&description=Cottage+Cheese+Turkish+Eggs&is_video=false)


### Ingredients


#### Cottage Cheese:


- 1


cup


cottage cheese


- 1


garlic clove


- 2


tablespoons


olive oil


- 1


tablespoon


fresh lemon juice


- 1


tablespoon


fresh dill,


chopped, plus more for garnish


- 1/4


teaspoon


ground cumin


- salt and pepper,


to taste


- 1-2


tablespoons


water


#### Chili Butter:


- 2


tablespoons


Vital Farms Unsalted Butter


- 1


teaspoon


ground Aleppo pepper


- 1/2


teaspoon


paprika


#### Eggs:


- 4


Vital Farms Hard Boiled Eggs,


halved


- bread of choice,


for dipping


- fresh mint,


for garnish


### Instructions


- Add the cottage cheese, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, dill, cumin, salt, and pepper to a blender or food processor. Blend or pulse until the ingredients are combined. Add water as needed to reach desired consistency.


- Make the chili butter by heating a small sauce pan over low heat. Add in the butter and melt then stir in the Aleppo pepper and paprika. Gently cook for around one minute. Remove from the heat and set aside.


- Spread the whipped cottage cheese onto a shallow bowl. Arrange the hard boiled eggs on top.


- Generously spoon the chili butter over the eggs. Garnish with fresh dill and mint.


- Serve with bread of choice and enjoy!
