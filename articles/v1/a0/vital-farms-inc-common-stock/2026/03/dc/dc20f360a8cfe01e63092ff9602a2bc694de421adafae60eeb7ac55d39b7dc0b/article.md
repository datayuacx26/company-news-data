# How To Build The Perfect Snack Plate

## The Perfect Snack Plate


More of a formula than an exact recipe, with just a few of your favorite bites, this snack plate comes together in a snap!


---


Makes


1


serving


[Print Recipe](https://vitalfarms.com/wprm_print/the-perfect-snack-plate)[Pin Recipe](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/create/bookmarklet/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fvitalfarms.com%2Fhow-to-build-the-perfect-snack-plate%2F&media=https%3A%2F%2Fvitalfarms.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F03%2FIMG_4630-2.jpg&description=The+Perfect+Snack+Plate&is_video=false)


### Ingredients


- 2


Vital Farms Eggs


- flaky salt


- pepper


- tinned fish


such as sardines, mackerel, or salmon


- your favorite crackers or bread


- veggies


such as tomatoes, sliced watermelon radish, and cucumbers


- sliced cheese


such as manchego, gouda, or sharp cheddar


- cured meat


such as salami, summer sausage, or ham


- pickled items


such as olives, pickles, or pepperoncinis


- condiment or spread


such as grainy mustard, fig jam, or honey


### Instructions


- Bring a large pot of water to a boil then gently add in the eggs using a slotted spoon. Turn heat down to medium so the water is simmering but no longer at a rapid boil and cook eggs for 8 minutes.


- Meanwhile, prepare an ice bath by filling a large bowl with ice and water. When done, remove the eggs with a slotted spoon and transfer to the ice bath. Set aside to cool.


- When ready to assemble your plate, peel and slice the eggs in half and season with flaky salt and pepper. Add the rest of your snacks of choice and enjoy!
