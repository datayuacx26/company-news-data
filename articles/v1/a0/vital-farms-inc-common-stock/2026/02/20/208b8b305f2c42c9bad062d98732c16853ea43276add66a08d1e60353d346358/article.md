# Butter Masala: The perfect Sauce Pairing For Any Protein

## Butter Masala Sauce


*Need a head start on dinner? This versatile sauce made with salted butter and warm spices is the perfect compliment to any protein. Think grilled salmon, crispy chickpeas, or juicy meatballs! Keep a batch on hand when you need to throw a quick yet flavor-packed meal together!*


**


---


Makes


2


cups


[Print Recipe](https://vitalfarms.com/wprm_print/butter-masala-sauce)[Pin Recipe](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/create/bookmarklet/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fvitalfarms.com%2Fbutter-masala-a-perfect-sauce-pairing-for-any-protein%2F&media=https%3A%2F%2Fvitalfarms.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2025%2F11%2FCover-Photo.jpg&description=Butter+Masala+Sauce&is_video=false)


### Ingredients


- 3


tablespoons


Vital Farms Salted Butter


- 1/2


small white onion,


finely diced


- 2


teaspoons


fresh ginger,


finely grated


- 2


cloves


garlic,


finely grated


- 1/2


teaspoon


ground cumin


- 1/2


teaspoon


ground coriander


- 1


teaspoon


garam masala


- 2


tablespoons


tomato paste


- 3/4


cup


canned crushed tomatoes


- zest of 1 large lime


- 1/2


cup


heavy whipping cream


- 3


tablespoons


greek yogurt


- 1


tablespoon


fresh lime juice


- salt and pepper,


to taste


### Instructions


- In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium-high heat. Add the onion and stir occasionally, until golden.


- Add the ginger and garlic and cook until fragrant. Next add the cumin, coriander and garam masala. Stir to combine.


- Add the tomato paste and cook for about two minutes. Add the crushed tomatoes, lime zest, and heavy cream. Turn the heat down to low and simmer for about 5 minutes. Stir in the yogurt, lime juice, salt, and pepper.


- Pair the sauce with any protein you'd like such as baked salmon, crispy baked chickpeas, or even meatballs.


> > > This sauce can be used immediately or stored in the refrigerator in an airtight container for up to three days.
