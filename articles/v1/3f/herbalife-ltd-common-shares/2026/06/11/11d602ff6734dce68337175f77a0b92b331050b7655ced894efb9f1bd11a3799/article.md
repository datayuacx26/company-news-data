# Herbalife Chief Financial Officer to Present at East Coast IDEAS Conference on June 11


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_d56a2baebdc024835f9ac27c3baef943/herbalife/news/2026-06-02_Herbalife_Chief_Financial_Officer_to_Present_at_946.pdf) Jun 2, 2026 • 7:05 am ET


LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Herbalife Ltd.](https://ir.herbalife.com/) (NYSE: HLF), a premier health and wellness company, community and platform, today announced John DeSimone, Chief Financial Officer, will present at the East Coast IDEAS Conference on Thursday, June 11, 2026 at 10:35 a.m. ET (7:35 a.m. PT).


The live webcast will be available at the following link:[https://event.summitcast.com/view/GAjb2PfaSPbv9ViGy3AtzN/ZC5vwyKUXDsTKS4G6iXFet](https://event.summitcast.com/view/GAjb2PfaSPbv9ViGy3AtzN/ZC5vwyKUXDsTKS4G6iXFet)


A link to the webcast will also be available under the Investor Relations section of Herbalife's website at[https://ir.herbalife.com/](https://ir.herbalife.com/) . A replay of the webcast will be available at the same website following the completion of the event and for six months thereafter.


Additional information regarding the East Coast IDEAS Conference in New York City is available at[www.IDEASconferences.com](http://www.ideasconferences.com/) .


**About Herbalife Ltd.**


Herbalife (NYSE: HLF) is a premier health and wellness company, community and platform that has been changing people's lives with great nutrition products and a business opportunity for its independent distributors since 1980. The Company offers science-backed products to consumers in more than 90 markets through entrepreneurial distributors who provide one-on-one coaching and a supportive community that inspires their customers to embrace a healthier, more active lifestyle to live their best life.


For more information, visit[https://ir.herbalife.com](https://ir.herbalife.com/) .
