# Herbalife CFO to Participate in Maxim Group “Health, Wellness & Longevity” Virtual Conference on July 22

LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Herbalife Ltd.](https://ir.herbalife.com/) (NYSE: HLF), a premier health and wellness company, community and platform, today announced John DeSimone, Chief Financial Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at the “Health, Wellness & Longevity” Virtual Conference presented by Maxim Group LLC on Wednesday, July 22, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. ET (11:00 a.m. PT).


The live webcast will be available at the following link:
[https://digital.maximgrp.com/events/health-wellness-longevity-07222026](https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdigital.maximgrp.com%2Fevents%2Fhealth-wellness-longevity-07222026&data=05%7C02%7Csamanthagou%40herbalife.com%7Ceb709f5e8903408c555608dee436aa75%7C101f87a76d6b4c6c9d9c223592a2ba50%7C0%7C1%7C639199120623531812%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=%2Bjt0UXFh6QqDuess4j%2BWLM7x7K5M3k41wD9B6iFzYuw%3D&reserved=0)


A link to the webcast will also be available under the Investor Relations section of Herbalife's website at[https://ir.herbalife.com/](https://ir.herbalife.com/) . A replay of the webcast will be available at the same website following the completion of the event and for five days thereafter.


Additional information regarding the “Health, Wellness & Longevity” Virtual Conference presented by Maxim Group LLC is available at[https://digital.maximgrp.com/events/health-wellness-longevity-07222026](https://digital.maximgrp.com/events/health-wellness-longevity-07222026) .


**About Herbalife Ltd.**


Herbalife (NYSE: HLF) is a premier health and wellness company, community and platform that has been changing people's lives with great nutrition products and a business opportunity for its independent distributors since 1980. The Company offers science-backed products to consumers in more than 90 markets through entrepreneurial distributors who provide one-on-one coaching and a supportive community that inspires their customers to embrace a healthier, more active lifestyle to live their best life.
