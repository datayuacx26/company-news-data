# Northspyre alternatives for merchant builders

If you are a merchant builder evaluating Northspyre, you are looking for software to manage the financial and budget side of development: project budgets, costs, draws, and reporting across the homes or projects you are building to sell. This guide explains the real alternatives, how they differ, and how to choose the one that fits how your team works.


## The short answer


For financial and budget management built for merchant builders, the practical choice comes down to two platforms: Rabbet and Northspyre. Both are designed for the development side of the business, where the work is setting budgets, managing draws, and reporting to lenders and investors across several projects at once.


The other names you will see, Procore and IngeniousBuild, solve a different problem. They are construction management platforms, built mainly for general contractors and for vertically integrated developers who run their own construction. If your team already works in Procore to manage the build, you are covered on field operations, but that is a separate job from managing project finances and budgets.


## Financial and budget management is not construction management


It helps to separate two categories that often get grouped together.


Financial and budget management is the development side: setting and tracking project budgets, managing budget adjustments and contingency, packaging and tracking draws, and reporting cash and exposure across your portfolio. This is the work a merchant builder does to keep projects funded and profitable.


Construction management is the field side: submittals, RFIs, daily logs, scheduling, and subcontractor coordination. This is the work a general contractor does to build the project.


Some developers need both. But if your core need is controlling budgets and draws across projects you are building to sell, you are looking for financial and budget management, and that narrows the field to two.


## The alternatives at a glance


### Rabbet


[Real estate development management software](https://rabbet.com/developers/product-overview) for developers. Rabbet brings budgets, documents, draw reviews, and portfolio insights into one connected workspace, with real-time forecasts and change order tracking. It is built for the financial and budget side of development.


### Northspyre


Development management platform for real estate developers. Northspyre focuses on automating project cost data, budgeting, and forecasting to support development decisions. Like Rabbet, it is built for the financial and budget side.


### Procore


Construction management platform for general contractors and owners. Procore is strong on field operations such as submittals, RFIs, daily logs, and subcontractor coordination. It is a fit for teams that self-perform or closely manage construction.


### IngeniousBuild


Project and construction management platform. IngeniousBuild is geared toward teams managing project execution, often general contractors or vertically integrated developers who handle their own construction.


## How to choose


A few questions usually settle it.


Do you self-perform construction, or do you hire general contractors? If you run your own construction, a construction management platform like Procore may already cover the build, and you are choosing a finance platform to sit alongside it. If you hire it out, your main need is financial and budget management.


Is your priority field operations or financial control? Submittals and daily logs point to construction management. Budgets, draws, and portfolio reporting point to financial and budget management.


How many projects are you running, and who do you report to? Merchant builders managing several projects and answering to lenders and investors benefit most from a platform that rolls budgets and draws into one portfolio view.

Rabbet is the real estate development management system for developers. For a merchant builder, that comes down to three things:
