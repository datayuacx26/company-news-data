# September 2025 Product Releases

This month, we are excited to introduce SLA Tracking, designed to help you stay compliant and confident in meeting commitments by tracking Days to Process, setting proactive alerts, and customizing notifications.


‍


‍


With SLA Tracking, you can:


- Store SLA commitments for Days to Process directly in Rabbet
- Set proactive alerts so you’re notified before deadlines are at risk
- Customize notifications or turn them off when not needed


By tracking Days to Process SLAs, Rabbet makes it easier for you to avoid last-minute scrambles, reduce compliance risks, and deliver a smoother experience for borrowers.


Check out our[help article](https://help.rabbet.com/en/articles/12369170-sla-tracking) to learn how to enable SLA Tracking and more.


## Other Releases:


**Proof of Payment & Site Image Tracking:** You can now[track proofs of payment and site images](https://help.rabbet.com/en/articles/12294684-upload-and-track-proof-of-payment-documentation) with invoices/pay apps, making it easy to confirm payments and verify completed work.


**Draw Package Ordering:** Automatically orders draw package documents for easier draw package creation.


**Enhanced PDF Annotations:** Improved and expanded annotation (signatures, comments and more) of PDF documents.
