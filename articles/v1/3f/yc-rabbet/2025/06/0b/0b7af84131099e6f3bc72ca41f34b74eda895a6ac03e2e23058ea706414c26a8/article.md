# Product Update: Timeline Management

This month, we’re introducing a Timeline Management feature to enhance visibility and foster collaboration, ensuring your projects stay on track efficiently and responsibly.


‍


‍


## Key Highlights


- **Visual Gantt Chart Interface** : You can view your project timeline in a clear Gantt chart format, helping you easily understand task durations, dependencies, deadlines, and how everything fits together.


- **Create and Manage Tasks** : Add tasks with all the essential details—name, planned and actual dates, completion targets, and assigned owners—so everyone knows what they’re responsible for.


- **Set Task Dependencies** : Link tasks that rely on one another. If one task can’t start until another finishes, you can define that relationship.


- **Collaborate Through Comments** : Communicate directly on tasks by leaving comments, using @ mentions to loop in teammates. All historical comments are stored in one central location for easy reference.
- **Stay Informed with Weekly Email Summaries** : Opt in to receive a weekly summary email from Rabbet that keeps you updated on tasks and alerts you to any overdue items.


- **Standardize Workflows Across Projects** : As an admin, you can define standard task templates for different types of projects—saving time and ensuring consistency across your entire organization.


- **Get Portfolio-Wide Visibility:** Reports let you analyze tasks across multiple projects. Use filters and saved views to zero in on key insights fast.
