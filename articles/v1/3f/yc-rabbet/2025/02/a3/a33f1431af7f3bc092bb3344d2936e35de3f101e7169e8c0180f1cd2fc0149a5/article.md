# Automate 81% of Manual Tasks: How Rabbet Transforms Productivity in Construction

Real estate professionals spend a staggering[35% of their time](https://www.autodesk.com/blogs/construction/process-productivity-construction/) on non-productive activities, amounting to about 14 hours per week, according to Autodesk. These manual tasks, ranging from administrative paperwork to chasing down approvals, slow down progress, increase costs, and heighten the risk of errors. Rabbet is changing this industry-wide issue by automating 81% of manual workflows, freeing real estate leaders to focus on high-value activities that drive project success. Here’s how Rabbet’s centralized data foundation revolutionizes construction project management.


## The Challenge: Inefficiency in Manual Tasks


Tasks like document handling, budget tracking, and invoice management are critical yet time-consuming. Without automation, these processes can bog down productivity, cause delays, and lead to miscommunication among teams. The result? Wasted time, higher costs, and project inefficiencies.


Rabbet addresses these challenges with a user-friendly platform designed to streamline workflows, centralize information, and automate repetitive tasks. Below are the key areas where Rabbet delivers transformative results:


1. Budget Management
Rabbet automates budget tracking by integrating data from invoices, contracts, and other sources directly into a centralized dashboard. This eliminates manual calculations and ensures that budget data is always up-to-date and accurate. Construction teams can quickly identify discrepancies, track progress, and make informed financial decisions in real time.
2. Document Management
Forget about sifting through emails and scattered folders. Rabbet’s document management feature allows you to upload and categorize documents effortlessly. The platform reads and extracts critical information from uploaded files, automating data entry and ensuring nothing gets lost in the shuffle.
3. Team Collaboration
Rabbet fosters team collaboration by providing a single source of truth for project data. Real-time updates, notifications, and configurable workflows keep everyone aligned, reducing the time spent on back-and-forth communications. Teams can focus on execution rather than chasing updates.
4. Benchmarks & Insights
Rabbet doesn’t just centralize data; it turns it into actionable insights. By automating performance tracking and benchmarks, the platform highlights trends, risks, and opportunities, allowing teams to make proactive adjustments and stay ahead of potential issues.
5. Contract Tracking
Managing contracts manually is a recipe for delays and errors. Rabbet connects contract details with cost-tracking features to provide full context when reviewing invoices. Teams can quickly access contract details and stay compliant without the administrative overhead.
6. Draws & Reports
Rabbet simplifies draw requests by automating the process from data collection to reporting. The platform integrates budget and invoice data, generating accurate, customized reports that keep stakeholders informed and aligned.
7. Loan Administration
Managing construction loans is a complex, multi-step process. Rabbet automates disbursement tracking, compliance checks, and document collection, ensuring smooth interactions with lenders and reducing the time spent on administrative tasks.
8. Cash Flow Forecasting
By automating cash flow analysis, Rabbet provides teams with clear visibility into upcoming expenses and funding needs. This feature helps prevent cash flow bottlenecks and ensures projects remain financially stable.
9. Lien Waiver Management
Rabbet simplifies lien waiver management to reduce data entry errors and prevent payment delays. Teams can verify, manage, and store lien waivers with minimal manual intervention, ensuring smooth project workflows.
10. Invoice Management
Rabbet automates the invoice review and approval process, eliminating the need for manual data entry and back-and-forth communications. The platform integrates seamlessly with accounting systems, speeding up payment workflows and improving accuracy.
11. Schedule Management
Stay on top of project timelines with Rabbet’s automated schedule tracking. Real-time updates ensure that teams can monitor milestones, identify potential delays, and take corrective action promptly.
12. Funding Source Management
For projects with multiple funding sources, Rabbet simplifies the process by automating the organization and tracking of contributions. This reduces errors, enhances transparency, and simplifies compliance reporting.
