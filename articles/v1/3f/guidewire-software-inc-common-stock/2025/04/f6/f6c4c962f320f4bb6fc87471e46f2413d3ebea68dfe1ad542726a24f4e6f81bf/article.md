- The Future of Property and Contents Claims: Embracing an Ecosystem-Driven Approach


Guest author - Bryn McKay, Chief Operating Officer, Codafication
April 28, 2025


With an evolving environmental and regulatory landscape, traditional property and contents claim systems and processes are proving inadequate. Insurance carriers, adjusters, and property owners are facing more regular and severe weather events and increasing regulatory complexities that demand a shift toward a more comprehensive, ecosystem-driven solution.


## The Digital-First Demand


Modern policyholders expect a self-service, digital-first approach with real-time updates and direct communication with the people servicing their claim. The rise of mobile technology and data-driven automation has set new standards for service delivery. Customers no longer tolerate prolonged processing times, manual documentation, or lack of transparency. They want:


- **Instant access** to real-time claim statuses through online portals.
- **Real-time communication** with adjusters and service providers.
- **Automation** to accelerate approvals and payouts.


## The Regulatory Shift


Regulatory bodies across the globe are introducing new policies aimed at improving claim services and ensuring better customer experiences. These changes require insurance carriers to enhance transparency, reduce claim processing times, and leverage digital tools to provide more efficient services. Key regulatory updates include:


- **Stricter Timelines for Claim Resolution** : Insurers must process and settle claims within a defined period, minimizing delays that often frustrate policyholders.
- **Improved Communication Requirements** : Carriers must ensure real-time updates and clear communication channels between claim handlers and policyholders.
- **Enhanced Data Security and Compliance** : Regulations now mandate stronger data protection measures to secure sensitive customer information in digital claims processing.


## The Ecosystem-Driven Solution


To meet these demands, insurers must embrace an interconnected ecosystem that brings together policyholders, adjusters, and contractors with data-driven automation.


A modern claims ecosystem includes:


**Cloud-Based Claims Management Systems** for scalability and seamless integration.


**Digital Collaboration Tools** to connect stakeholders and enhance communication.


**Structured Data** leveraged with historical claim data and industry data to automate the next step in the claim process.


## The Benefits of a Modernized Approach


Adopting an ecosystem-driven model improves efficiency, reduces claim processing times, enhances accuracy, and ultimately improves customer satisfaction. By leveraging technology, insurers can move beyond reactive claim processing to proactive risk management, offering more value to policyholders and strengthening trust in the process.


“The insurance industry is at a critical juncture where digital transformation is no longer optional - it is essential. Companies that modernize their claims process by embracing automation, self-service capabilities, and interconnected ecosystems will not only stay competitive but also provide an exceptional customer experience. The future of property and contents claims lies in an agile, tech-driven approach that prioritizes speed, transparency, and policyholder empowerment” - Bryn Mckay, Chief Operating Officer, Codafication.
