- Empowering Transformation with BOLT


Guidewire has maintained a strong and expanding presence across Australia and New Zealand (ANZ) that highlights a strategic focus: enabling insurers to modernise core systems, enhance operational efficiency, and innovate within a competitive market. Historically, core system replacement projects present significant hurdles to digital transformation within the insurance sector. These initiatives frequently encounter complex integration challenges, scope creep, and unexpected delays. Misalignment among vendors, partners, and insurers often complicates projects, alongside compliance, regulatory hurdles, and inadequate change management. Such undertakings also carry high costs, often yielding low returns on investment, leading to data fragmentation, poor data governance, and heightened cybersecurity risks. Ultimately, these issues can negatively affect the customer experience. Insurers consistently seek solutions to make these essential projects faster, more predictable, and more affordable.


In response to these persistent challenges, the Guidewire APAC team introduced **BOLT (Business-line Optimised Launch Templates)** in May at the Guidewire Insurance Forum in Sydney. This new program aims to redefine how insurers implement Guidewire solutions. BOLT integrates Guidewire’s platform, products, and pre-packaged content, specifically tailored for particular countries and lines of business. This is combined with a prescriptive implementation approach designed to reduce the time needed to realise value significantly. BOLT departs from traditional implementation models, which typically involve extensive requirements gathering, complex scoping, and multi-phased deployments. Instead, BOLT enables insurers to accelerate their launch processes with greater efficiency.


With BOLT, customers achieve several operational advantages:


- **Rapid Deployment** : Insurers can achieve go-live status in as little as three to four months. This accelerated timeline is facilitated by a curated, prescriptive implementation methodology that streamlines deployment steps. The structured approach minimises variables, ensuring a focused and efficient rollout.
- **Optimised Costs and Ownership** : BOLT significantly reduces technology implementation and ongoing ownership expenses. This is accomplished through the provision of pre-packaged configurations, specialised extensions relevant to specific regions and lines of business, and ready-to-use integrations. These integrations are sourced from the growing list[Guidewire Marketplace](https://marketplace.guidewire.com/) Technology partners, ensuring proven compatibility and performance.
- **Enhanced Regulatory Alignment** : The program incorporates built-in features and robust reporting capabilities designed for direct alignment with local regulations and prevailing industry standards. This proactive approach helps insurers maintain compliance, mitigating risks associated with evolving regulatory environments and reducing the burden of manual adjustments.


BOLT’s "out-of-the-box" delivery model is foundational to its effectiveness. By minimising customisation requirements and emphasising pre-tested, validated configurations, BOLT empowers insurers. They can then concentrate on their distinct competitive differentiators, whether it’s fostering superior customer experiences, driving innovation in risk management practices, or proactively adapting to new regulatory demands. The development of BOLT for APAC leverages years of Guidewire's implementation experience, deep insights into local market needs, and a collaborative[PartnerConnect ecosystem](https://www.guidewire.com/partners) . This collective effort ensures that integrations are thoroughly built, tested, and refined, enabling customers to deploy quickly and with confidence.


BOLT will soon be available for the Australian motor line of business, with additional lines of business such as Home, Workers’ Compensation, and Small-to-Medium-Enterprise (SME) (BizPack) to follow, marking a significant step toward making insurance system transformation more agile, efficient, and scalable. We are enthusiastic about BOLT and its potential to accelerate transformation across the insurance industry. For insurers seeking faster technology modernisation, reduced implementation risks, and new opportunities for growth,[connect with us](https://www.guidewire.com/about/get-in-touch/contact-us) to explore how BOLT can enhance your operations.
