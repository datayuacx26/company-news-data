- Guidewire Home: A unified Guidewire Cloud Platform experience


We’re excited to announce that Guidewire Cloud Console is now fully migrated into Guidewire Home, so all your Guidewire applications are now available seamlessly as one Guidewire Cloud Platform experience. With Guidewire Home as the entry point to all your Guidewire applications, you have a unified view into the status of your applications, can quickly find information, and perform tasks that are most important to you.


## An informative, actionable landing page


The Guidewire Home landing page is designed to help you manage your Guidewire Cloud Platform experience. With an actionable summary view into your builds, deployments, and applications, the new landing page lets you quickly assess the status of your applications and take necessary actions. For example, from the Guidewire Home landing page, you can:


- Link directly to your builds to check on and resolve build issues
- Link into Lifecycle Manager to commit and deploy changes that are available
- Redeploy any apps that aren’t running


You can also pin the apps and user documentation you use most often so that you can get to them directly from the landing page, without having to open the navigation panel or the Help menu.

## Intuitive and unified navigation


In the application navigation panel, you can find all your Guidewire applications, including the apps that have recently migrated from Guidewire Cloud Console. (These migrated apps are highlighted in yellow in the following image.) In the navigation panel, applications are grouped by functional area to enhance your user experience. When you pin apps from the navigation panel, they’ll appear in the My applications section of your landing page.


Remember, Guidewire Cloud Console will no longer be available by March 26, 2025, so start using Guidewire Home to access all your Cloud applications right away.


## What’s next

- Enhanced landing page experience for business users
- Visibility into updates that are recommended for you
- Intuitive and standardized access management
