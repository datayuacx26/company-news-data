## Meet Mamata Behera from CastleBay Infotech


Name: Mamata Behera
Job Title: Guidewire Architect – ClaimCenter
Company: CastleBay Infotech


### How long have you been working with Guidewire products?


With over 14 years of experience working with Guidewire products, I have primarily served in Architect roles on Property & Casualty (P&C) transformation initiatives. My experience spans multiple platform versions, starting from version 7.0, and I hold certifications across all technical versions up to 10.0, including the Guidewire ACE Professional Certification in PolicyCenter, ClaimCenter, BillingCenter, and Integration.


### What do you enjoy most about your role?


I enjoy leading both development and implementation efforts and have worked with more than six P&C clients across regions including North America, the UK, Switzerland, and Australia.


### What advice would you give to someone who is starting out in development or who is new to using Guidewire?


A strong and in-depth understanding of the insurance domain is key to excelling in Guidewire development. While it is important to build technical expertise in areas like Cloud, AI, and low-code platforms, true success comes from combining these skills with a deep knowledge of P&C insurance processes. To further strengthen your journey, I recommend completing the Guidewire training curriculum, keeping certifications current, and focusing on configuration over customization. This approach enables the community to build robust, scalable, and upgrade-friendly solutions.


### What are some recent projects or initiatives you’re most proud of?


I have developed strong expertise in handling complex projects, integrating third-party systems, and tailoring Guidewire solutions to meet specific business requirements. I am proud of having contributed to a variety of implementations, including ClaimCenter and PolicyCenter, gaining comprehensive experience across the full project lifecycle—from initial concept and solution design to production deployment, including end-to-end implementations and core system modernization.


### How did you first get involved with Guidewire and the developer ecosystem?


My journey with Guidewire began as a developer, and over time, I progressed into roles such as lead and architect. I started my career at TCS, where I built a strong foundation in Java and J2EE before transitioning into the Guidewire ecosystem.
