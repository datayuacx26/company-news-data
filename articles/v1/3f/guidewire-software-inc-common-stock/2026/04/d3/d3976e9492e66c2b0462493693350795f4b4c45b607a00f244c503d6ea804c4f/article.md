- Building Beyond: What Will Be Inspired at DEVSummit 2026


Guidewire DEVSummit 2026 is scheduled for May 13-16 in Bengaluru, India. We are hosting more than 3,000 InsuranceSuite developers, full-stack engineers, and technical architects for four days of intensive engineering.


*Want to stay in the loop about what’s happening for Guidewire developers? Subscribe to our[developer newsletter](https://www.guidewire.com/developers/developer-resources/developer-newsletter) for a monthly update on events, news, and release highlights delivered directly to your inbox.*


## Shifting from configuration to composition


The theme "Building Beyond" marks a fundamental pivot in our technical roadmap. This year, DEVSummit will prioritize hands-on experience for real-world implementation-based learning.


We are focusing on the mechanics of how you write code, the patterns required to architect extensions for long-term compatibility, and the deployment of agentic AI within the Guidewire Cloud platform.


*“This year is about turning Building Beyond from a theme into code you can take back to production.”* - Mohammed Anzy S, Guidewire Vice President, Engineering and Managing Director


## Framework for technical growth


To maximize your time in Bengaluru, DEVSummit follows a structured engineering framework:


1. **Learning** : Engineering-led tracks building expertise in the latest InsuranceSuite capabilities
2. **Innovation** : Translating abstract architectural concepts into production-ready code
3. **Community** : Exchanging proven implementation patterns with 3,000 global peers


## Customized 3-block learning journey


The learning journey will follow a stream-based progression in which participants commit to one single, curated path composed of **three modular building blocks** . Each block builds on the previous one, prioritizing depth over breadth for a sequential and cumulative experience.


Each block represents a distinct capability and will include a:


1. **Conceptual deep dive** – Focused on principles, patterns, and context
2. **Hands-on application** – Focused on implementation and practice


This ensures a tight loop of learn → apply → internalize.


## The capstone “wrapper”


The learning culminates for every stream in a capstone “wrapper” experience where participants:


- Integrate multiple building blocks
- Apply concepts in a unified scenario
- Build an end-to-end solution


This shifts the focus from understanding to creation.


## Validating production-ready proficiency


The Capstone project is the core of this experience. By completing the Deep Dive, Hands-on, and Capstone sequence, you demonstrate the ability to ship production-quality code using the latest Guidewire tools. Participants who successfully finish their Capstone project will receive an **official Certificate of Completion** .


This certification validates your proficiency in critical technical domains, such as the new InsuranceSuite Extension Layer or the Guidewire AI Framework. You leave the summit with a verified record of your skills and a working prototype of your solution.


## Secure your technical roadmap


The event agenda includes detailed tracks for InsuranceSuite developers and full-stack engineers. For the first time, we have also introduced dedicated streams for QA engineers and Business Analysts to ensure full-team alignment on the Guidewire Cloud journey.
