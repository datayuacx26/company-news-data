- Three Steps to Get Your App Listed in the Guidewire Marketplace


The[Guidewire Marketplace](https://marketplace.guidewire.com/s/?r=4&_gl=1*se4l6n*_gcl_au*MTUwMTA3Nzg0NC4xNzI1MDQ1MDUz*_ga*MTMxNjc5NjMxNi4xNzIyNjIxMTAz*_ga_LN5WM89V7V*MTczMjEyNjc2OC43MC4wLjE3MzIxMjY3NjguNjAuMC4w) enables insurers to accelerate the pace of innovation with apps, content, and more. With the largest and most comprehensive marketplace of P&C solutions, it’s never been easier for insurers to discover new solutions to accelerate success and build an ecosystem to differentiate policyholder experiences. App developers publishing content in the Marketplace get exposure to the largest customer community in the P&C industry.


## Eligibility


Are you an insurtech developer interested in having your solution integrated with Guidewire InsuranceSuite or InsuranceNow? Do you have a Guidewire customer? If yes, perform the following steps to publish your content in the Marketplace. We’ve curated this insurtech ecosystem to deliver key integrations — validated by Guidewire — to our customer community of more than 520 insurers.


### Step 1: Application


To apply, go to our PartnerConnect site. In the form, you can submit details about your solution, its traction in the P&C industry, and the proposed integration with InsuranceSuite.


[Apply via PartnerConnect](https://www.guidewire.com/partners/for-customers-find-a-partner/technology-partners-and-marketplace)


### Step 2: Assessment and Review


Guidewire completes a technical assessment along with a business case review to evaluate the proposed workflow of the integration. An ideal integration decreases an insurer’s deployment efforts and improves the overall user experience. The business case review includes verifying existing P&C customer adoption of your solution.


### Step 3: Approval


If we approve your submission, we then invite you to join the Guidewire PartnerConnect program — the largest and most experienced partner community in the P&C industry. When the partnership is contracted, you’ll have access to Guidewire products, training, and documentation.


To ensure project success, you and Guidewire will collaboratively manage the integration development project.
