# Cincinnati Financial Schedules Webcast to Discuss Third-Quarter 2025 Results

CINCINNATI


, Oct. 7, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- **Cincinnati Financial Corporation** (Nasdaq:CINF ) plans to release its third-quarter 2025 results on Monday, October 27, 2025, after the close of regular trading on the Nasdaq Stock Market.


The company will hold a conference call to discuss third-quarter 2025 results on Tuesday, October 28, at 11 a.m. ET. To access the call webcast, please visit *[investors.cinfin.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4526599-1&h=3373485260&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.cinfin.com%2F&a=investors.cinfin.com)* . A replay will be available approximately two hours after the event's completion.


**About Cincinnati Financial Corporation:** Cincinnati Financial Corporation offers primarily business, home and auto insurance through The Cincinnati Insurance Company and its two standard market property casualty companies. The same local independent insurance agencies that market those policies may offer products of our other subsidiaries, including life insurance, fixed annuities and surplus lines property and casualty insurance. For additional information about the company, please visit *cinfin.com* .
