# Flex LNG – Notice of Annual General Meeting 2026

Hamilton, Bermuda


March 20, 2026


FLEX LNG Ltd. (NYSE: FLNG) (“FLNG” or the “Company”) advises that the 2026 Annual General Meeting of the Company will be held on 5 May 2026. The record date for voting at the Annual General Meeting is set to 24 March 2026. A copy of the Notice of Annual General Meeting and associated information, including the Company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F, will be distributed and made available on the Company’s website at www.flexlng.com prior to the meeting.


The Board of Directors
