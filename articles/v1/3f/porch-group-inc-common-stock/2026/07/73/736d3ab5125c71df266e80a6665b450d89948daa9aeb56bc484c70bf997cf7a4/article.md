### Porch Group to Release Second Quarter 2026 Earnings on July 29, 2026


July 20, 2026


SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Porch Group, Inc. (“Porch”) (Nasdaq: PRCH), a new kind of homeowners insurance company, announced today it will report financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, after markets close on Wednesday, July 29, 2026.


**Q2’26 Earnings Conference Call**


Porch management will host a live webinar on Wednesday, July 29, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern (2:00 p.m. Pacific) to discuss financial results and outlook, with a Q&A session to follow. Supporting materials, including a presentation, press release, and additional financial info, will be available on the company's website.


All are invited to listen to the event live by registering for the webinar[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F673727847&esheet=54573879&newsitemid=20260720615221&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=1&md5=f6bdca296ed26257ce622b56219e6481) . A webinar replay will also be available in the Investors section of Porch’s corporate[website.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.porchgroup.com%2F&esheet=54573879&newsitemid=20260720615221&lan=en-US&anchor=website.&index=2&md5=32aa34f75cced6d87d71f6eb0255736f)


**About Porch Group**


Porch is a new kind of homeowners insurance company. Porch's strategy to win in homeowners insurance is to deploy leading vertical software solutions in select home-related industries, provide the best services for homebuyers including important moving services, leverage unique data for advantaged underwriting, and provide more protection for policyholders.


To learn more about Porch, visit ir.porchgroup.com.
