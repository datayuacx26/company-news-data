### Porch Group to Release Fourth Quarter 2025 Earnings on February 11, 2026


January 27, 2026


SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Porch Group, Inc. (“Porch”) (Nasdaq: PRCH), a new kind of homeowners insurance company, announced today it will report financial results for the fourth quarter ended December 31, 2025, after markets close on Wednesday, February 11, 2026.


**Q4’25 Earnings Conference Call**


Porch management will host a live webinar on Wednesday, February 11, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern (2:00 p.m. Pacific) to discuss financial results and outlook, with a Q&A session to follow. Supporting materials, including a presentation, press release, and additional financial info, will be available on the company's website.


All are invited to listen to the event live by registering for the webinar[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F252616362&esheet=54404754&newsitemid=20260127272102&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=1&md5=798edf82df148980ab788531ff19fc96) . A webinar replay will also be available in the Investors section of Porch’s corporate[website.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.porchgroup.com%2F&esheet=54404754&newsitemid=20260127272102&lan=en-US&anchor=website.&index=2&md5=57bd467238f3aada63fd9f025e95f09a)


**About Porch Group**


Porch is a new kind of homeowners insurance company. Porch's strategy to win in homeowners insurance is to deploy leading vertical software solutions in select home-related industries, provide the best services for homebuyers including important moving services, leverage unique data for advantaged underwriting, and provide more protection for policyholders.


To learn more about Porch, visit ir.porchgroup.com.
