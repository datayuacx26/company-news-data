### Porch Group Announces Attendance at Upcoming Investor Events


August 11, 2026


SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Porch Group, Inc. (“Porch”) (Nasdaq: PRCH), a new kind of homeowners insurance company, today announced its upcoming investor event attendance.


Porch Management will be attending and participating in one-on-one and group investor meetings at these upcoming events:


- August 12, 2026: 29 th Annual Technology, Internet & Communications Conference (Virtual)
- August 13, 2026: 8 th Annual Needham FinTech & Digital Transformation Conference (Virtual)
- August 18-19, 2026: Craig Hallum Non-Deal Roadshow (San Francisco, Denver)
- August 21, 2026: Three Part Advisors Investor Meeting (Virtual)
- September 1, 2026: Stephens Inc. Non-Deal Roadshow (Boston)
- September 9, 2026: KBW Insurance Conference (New York City)
- September 10, 2026: Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference (San Francisco)
- September 17, 2026: StoneX 13 th Annual Tech, Media & Telecom Conference (New York City)
- September 22-23, 2026: Oppenheimer Non-Deal Roadshow (Toronto/Montreal)
- September 24, 2026: Cantor Non-Deal Roadshow (Midwest)


These investor events and future events can be found on our website[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.porchgroup.com%2Finvestors%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54587899&newsitemid=20260812967357&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=1&md5=1e74b650c312cd6228bfd0395a9ecd62) .


**About Porch Group**


Porch Group, Inc., ("Porch") is a new kind of homeowners insurance company. Porch's strategy to win in homeowners insurance is to deploy leading vertical software solutions in select home-related industries, provide the best services for homebuyers including important moving services, leverage unique data for advantaged underwriting, and provide more protection for policyholders.
