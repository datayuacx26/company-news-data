## **What are the key features of Anima’s Care Orchestration?**


### 1) Remove the administrative burden from routine tasks


Using a **simple drag and drop interface** , anyone in your clinic can create workflows in minutes. All you need to get started with a workflow is a trigger (e.g. a new document, lab report, or a collection of patients) then create your collection of the actions needed to deal with the trigger (e.g. filtering lab report results automatically, clinical coding using AI, checking patient demographics, or sending a patient a self-book link).


**Each workflow can be completely customised to your own clinic’s needs** . Here are a couple of use cases, but this is just a small selection of what Anima can do (book a short demo[here](https://calendly.com/d/455-yx2-cb2) if you’d like a full tour!)


-


**Long Term Condition recall:** Build workflows for vascular conditions, respiratory conditions, or Diabetes - for example, automatically send 2-week BP reading questionnaires to patients and have them self-book with Nursing depending on the results.


-


**Lab Reports** : File lab reports such as Pathology in a specific way. Based on report outcomes, send patients questionnaires, create tasks to action internally, or send patients appointment booking links. No longer will you need to review every report.


### 2) Automated document processing


No more digging through records. Our AI assistant, Annie, can read documents, and match them to the right patient instantly. Digest a 5-page letter in a single paragraph with all the key information summarised.


Want to ensure you always consistently code a diabetes letter with specific QOF codes? Create a workflow that adds your chosen codes automatically every time that letter type is processed.


### 3) Automate workflows for ‘abnormal’ results - on your terms


Anima’s care workflows don’t just deal with normal results. You can set your own criteria for an abnormal result, and if the criteria is met, trigger a series of automated actions & tasks to ensure the patient receives the right outcome at the right time.


### 4) Instant task creation


Directly assign tasks to the right person, automatically send appointment booking links, send messages to patients via SMS/email, change medications and much more. Then, automatically save to record.


### 5) Never miss a QOF point again


With thousands of cases to deal with a week, it’s easy to see how teams can become fatigued and human error can start to creep in - you might have seen things missed in your coding, or patients not called back in for a review when they should have done. With Anima, workflows runs automatically in the background and your AI assistant Annie automatically scans uploaded documents to find or suggest the most relevant codes. This can save you up minutes **per document** when it comes to tagging and ensuring accurate coding.
