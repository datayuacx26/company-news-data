# 2024 in Review, Part I: A Winning Formula

We're bringing you a brand new series as we look back on the last 12 months - our **2024 in Review** . We'll be looking back at the highlights from this year, celebrating the achievements of the teams that we've been working with, and looking forward to what's coming in 2025. And where better to start than with some of our award winning practices?


In a landmark achievement highlighting the transformative power of digital healthcare solutions, Brookside Group Practice in Reading won 2024's prestigious HSJ Digital Award for Improving Primary Care through Digital. Being one of the only solo GP practices to be nominated for any award across each of the 22 categories, Brookside's win is even more remarkable.


Read on to find out more about the groundbreaking model that the Brookside group have implemented to revolutionise how they deliver patient care, supported by Connected Care and Anima.


## The winning formula: Patient Health Teams Model


At the heart of this success story lies the way in which Brookside have implemented their non-clinician traige model, integrating the Johns Hopkins ACG System patient segmentation model within the Connected Care population health management platform, and Anima.


The result is a RAG rating for both patient conditions and individual requests, as well as overall patient needs. The system enables more targeted and personalised care delivery while facilitating better resource allocation and patient outcomes.


The model implements specialised teams that work in harmony to deliver comprehensive care. Each team comprises partners, associates, physician associates, social prescribers, and patient services liaisons. The roles are carefully defined and integrated into the overall care delivery system, ensuring optimal utilisation of skills and resources while maintaining clear communication channels and accountability.


## Measureable impact and benefits


Brookside's model has led to several benefits for the practice team and patients:


-


Improved patient access


-


Enhanced continuity of care


-


Reducing unnecessary appointments


-


Improving equity for patients


-


Efficient use of practice resources
