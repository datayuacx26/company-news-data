# WellBN Practice's Same-Day Document Processing Without Additional Hires

**Location:** Brighton & Hove, Sussex ICB


**Patient List Size:** ~27,000


**Go-Live With Anima Documents:** February 2025


## Overview


WellBN’s three practices went live with Anima in February 2025 to address their document processing challenges. The practices previously handled around 6,000 documents per month with frequent two-week backlogs. Since integrating Anima, the median processing time has drastically reduced, enabling same-day completion of documents, significantly improving patient follow-up times. Anima's onboarding provided dedicated support, transitioning smoothly from SystmOne with seamless routing from sources like paper scanning, NHSmail, and NHS MESH.


## The Challenge


Before adopting Anima, WellBN faced significant challenges in document processing. Using SystmOne directly, they managed about 6,000 documents per month, spending 2-5 minutes on each document. However, this often resulted in delays, with a typical backlog stretching over two weeks.


## Key Outcomes


### Faster Document Processing


The practices have significantly accelerated their document processing speed. Anima’s features such as automatic patient matching, document summarisation, and clinical codes suggestions have played a vital role. These advancements, along with automated letter type prediction and AI-enhanced coding preferences, have reduced time spent per document and expedited overall workflow.


### Reduced Backlog


The reduction in backlog at WellBN has been noteworthy. Documents that previously took weeks are now handled within a day, thanks to the accurate and timely updating of patient care plans enabled by Anima.


### Ability to Manage Volume Without Hiring


With the integration of Anima, WellBN has managed to significantly increase their document handling capacity **without needing to hire additional staff.** The practice no longer relies on external agencies, reducing costs while efficiently managing a higher volume of work.
