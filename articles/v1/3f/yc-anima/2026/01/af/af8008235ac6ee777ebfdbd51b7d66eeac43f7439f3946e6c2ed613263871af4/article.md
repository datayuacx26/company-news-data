# How Anima helped The Lawson Practice to remove their document backlog forever

**Location:** North East London ICB


**Patient List Size:** ~17,000


**Go-Live With Anima Documents:** May 2024


## Overview


The Lawson Practice, located in North East London ICB, integrated Anima Documents in May 2024 to improve their document processing workflow. Before Anima, processing 3,500 documents per month was time-consuming, often creating a backlog of 1-2 weeks. Now, documents are typically processed within 1.74 days (median), ensuring timely patient follow-ups and reducing clinical risks. The practice, guided through the transition by their dedicated Anima account manager, now experiences faster processing and improved consistency, allowing greater focus on patient care.


## The Challenge


Prior to implementing Anima, The Lawson Practice relied on EMIS to manage its document workflow, handling around 3,500 documents each month. Processing each document took 5-10 minutes, and same-day processing was rarely achievable, often resulting in a 1-2 week backlog. This system limited efficiency and sometimes delayed patient care.


## Key Outcomes


### Faster Document Processing


With Anima, The Lawson Practice enjoys quicker document processing. Features like automatic patient matching and document summarisation help reduce time spent on each document. The inclusion of clinical code suggestions ensures accurate processing, which was not possible with the previous system.


### Reduced Backlog


Implementing Anima has minimized the backlog from a week or more to processing within two days. This consistency in handling documents reduces clinical risks and keeps patient care timely and accurate.


### Improved Coding Consistency


Anima's automated coding tools offer suggestions based on the document content, learning the team's preferences. This feature not only improves coding consistency but also helps in capturing essential QOF codes, securing potential funding for the practice.


### Greater Staff Confidence


The intuitive user interface of Anima has increased staff confidence. Bringing all patient data into a single platform avoids the hassle of managing multiple systems, allowing staff to focus on patient care rather than navigating technical barriers.
