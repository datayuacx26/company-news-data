# Channel3 recognized in the Awin Power 100

Channel3 commended for value and innovation after beating competition from over one million partners.


NEW YORK, NY — May 12, 2026


— Channel3, the company building a universal, AI-ready product graph and APIs for agentic shopping, today announced that it has been named in the[Awin](https://www.awin.com/) Power 100: a globally recognized index of the 100 most innovative affiliate partners.


Channel3 was one of just 100 companies selected from Awin's network of over one million active partners across 35 countries, including AI shopping tools, influencers, price comparison sites, YouTubers, niche bloggers, global media powerhouses, FinTech pioneers, and coupon sites.


This year's Power 100 is the most diverse yet, and includes organisations of all shapes and sizes, from billion-dollar enterprises and Series-A backed start ups. This reflects how affiliate partnerships are evolving beyond traditional models and offering advertisers greater opportunities to scale and grow. Brands on Awin are increasingly able to work with a wider range of partners to reduce dependency on other channels and support discovery, customer acquisition, brand awareness and long-term growth.


Awin CEO Adam Ross comments:


“The Awin Power 100 reflects how quickly the affiliate channel is evolving. Today, advertisers can work with a far broader mix of partners than many people realize, from AI shopping tools and creators to technology partners, publishers, loyalty partners and even other brands.


That shift means affiliate programs can now support far more than just sales at the point of conversion. They're helping brands drive discovery, influence purchase decisions and reach new audiences in ways that simply weren't possible a few years ago.”


Channel3 CEO Alexander Schiff comments:


“We're thrilled to have been recognized in the Awin Power 100. Being recognized among the most innovative affiliate partners globally is a testament to the hard work of our team and the strength of the partnerships we've built. The affiliate channel is evolving faster than ever, and we're excited to be at the forefront of that change. We look forward to continuing to drive value for advertisers and consumers alike as we grow together with Awin.”


See the full Awin Power 100 list at[Awin.com](https://www.awin.com/) .
