# How to earn commission with affiliate product data

If you're building an app or agent that recommends products, you can turn those recommendations into revenue. Affiliate product data lets you surface real products with trackable links — when a user buys, you earn a commission. Here's how it works and what to look for.


## Why affiliate product data?


Not all product APIs are affiliate-enabled. Some return product info but no monetizable links; others tie you to a single network or require per-brand signups. An API that combines broad product coverage with built-in affiliate rails means you integrate once and earn across many merchants.


## What you need


- **Attribution** — Links that track when a user came from your app and attribute the sale to you.
- **Coverage** — A large catalog so your recommendations are relevant and varied.
- **Simplicity** — One API, one relationship, no need to manage hundreds of affiliate programs.


## Use cases


Shopping assistants, gift finders, style advisors, and comparison tools can all surface product results with affiliate links. When users click through and purchase, you earn. Channel3's catalog is affiliate-ready for most products via partnerships with major networks — so you can focus on the experience and let the data layer handle monetization.
