# Why real-time product data matters for AI and apps

Product data goes stale fast. Prices change, items go out of stock, and new variants appear. For AI assistants and recommendation apps, showing outdated information isn't just wrong — it erodes trust and sends users to dead or misleading links.


## The cost of stale data


When an agent recommends a product that's no longer available or has a different price, the user loses confidence. In the worst case, they click through to a 404 or a cart that doesn't match what they were promised. Real-time (or near real-time) data keeps recommendations accurate and actionable.


## What "real-time" means in practice


Different APIs update at different cadences. Some are batch-refreshed daily or weekly; others are updated as catalogs change. For shopping and AI use cases, you want freshness that reflects current availability and pricing — so the product your user sees is the product they can buy. Channel3's pipeline keeps data fresh so your app or agent can show results that match reality.


## Building on a solid base


Choosing a product data provider with a focus on freshness and coverage means you spend less time debugging wrong prices or broken links and more time improving the experience. Your users and your conversion rates will thank you.
