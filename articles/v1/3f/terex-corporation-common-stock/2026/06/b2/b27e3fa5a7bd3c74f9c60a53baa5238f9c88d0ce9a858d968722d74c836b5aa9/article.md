# Terex Utilities and James A. Kiley Company Unveil Custom America 250 Bucket Trucks at EUFMC 2026

Terex Utilities and James A. Kiley Company, an authorized and long-time Terex dealer in Boston, Massachusetts, are proud to unveil two custom-wrapped bucket trucks celebrating America’s upcoming 250th anniversary. The patriotic units will make their debut at the Electric Utility Fleet Managers Conference (EUFMC) in Williamsburg, Virginia, this week. The trucks will be showcased among industry-leading utility equipment and innovations.


Terex Utilities and Kiley partnered to create the trucks as a tribute to 250 years of American freedom, service, and pioneering. A custom red, white, and blue wrap was made and inspired by all the hardworking utility crews that keep America connected every day. Kiley’s truck is an Optima HRX55 bucket truck.


“As a Terex dealer in the Northeast we are proud of the equipment we build for our customers and we are proud of our country,” Jim Kiley of James A. Kiley Company said. “This custom truck embodies that pride and patriotism that we pour into our equipment.”


Terex Utilities wrapped a TRX52 telescopic bucket truck with a similar design that also celebrates 250 years of America’s independence. The truck will be used for demonstrations for utility crews throughout the United States this summer.


“Williamsburg, Virginia, was one of the first locations where America’s independence was celebrated 250 years ago and we thought EUFMC would be the perfect location to show off our American-made bucket trucks and this tribute to the nation’s 250th birthday,” Terex Utilities Product Marketing Manager Tyler Schwingler said.


Following its debut at EUFMC, Terex Utilities’ TRX52 will be making stops at events in Minnesota, Texas, and Nebraska through the end of July.


Other equipment that Terex Utilities will be showcasing at EUFMC is the new Terex TRX Series, the cutting-edge 3rd Eye 360° camera system, and a hands-on demonstration of the Terex Flex Pro digger derrick controls.
