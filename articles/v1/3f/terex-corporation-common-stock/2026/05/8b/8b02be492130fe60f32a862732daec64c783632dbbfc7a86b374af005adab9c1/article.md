# Terex Showcases Innovation and Customer Focus at Tennessee Demo Day

Terex Services recently brought together a wide range of Terex brands and solutions for a “Demo Day & Customer Experience” event in White House, Tennessee. More than 100 customers attended to see live demonstrations from Environmental Solutions, Materials Processing, and Specialty Vehicles—highlighting the breadth of Terex capabilities.


Attendees from industries including waste management, utilities, infrastructure construction, and fire and emergency response had the opportunity to explore equipment first-hand, connect with Terex experts, and learn more about the services and solutions available to support their operations.


Across the event site, guests experienced more than a dozen pieces of equipment in action—from bucket trucks and refuse collection vehicles to digital solutions and fire apparatus. Customers were able to walk the lot, engage directly with Terex team members, and gain insights into product capabilities, service support, and integrated solutions.


The event demonstrated the value across Terex operating segments to deliver comprehensive solutions that meet diverse customer needs.


Equipment on display included solutions from Environmental Solutions such as bucket trucks, digger derricks, refuse collection vehicles, and 3rd Eye® digital technology; Materials Processing equipment including ProAll, Advance Mixer, and Green-Tec solutions; and Specialty Vehicles offerings such as fire apparatus and emergency vehicles supported by leading partners.


“Not only do we expect the event to generate strong commercial outcomes, it also sparked valuable dialogue on how we can further align across our businesses to create greater value for customers,” said Dave Young, Vice President of Digital, Environmental Solutions. “There was significant interest in our 3rd Eye technology, and it was especially rewarding to engage with customers across a wide range of industries.”
