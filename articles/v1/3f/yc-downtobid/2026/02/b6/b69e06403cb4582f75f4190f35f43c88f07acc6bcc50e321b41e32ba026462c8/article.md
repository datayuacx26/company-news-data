## Key Features


Here’s what makes Procore Construction Network unique.


### Expansive Subcontractor List


Procore Construction Network lists subcontractors from every major US city, helping general contractors easily recruit subs from any part of the country. This approach speeds up subcontractor recruitment, ensuring general contractors spend less time searching for subs and more time getting to know them.


Unfortunately, the majority of construction companies listed on Procore Construction Network have outdated contact details, making it difficult for general contractors to get bid responses and participation.


### Collaboration Tools


Procore Construction Network uses several tools that enhance team collaboration. They have a built-in messaging tool that allows the team to share information on the go. This tool comes in handy during construction, especially to on-field teams. Users can share information, upload reports, and issue updates, ensuring everyone is on the same page.


However, the platform’s messaging tool is messy, and users have to dig through many messages to find a specific email. This approach slows down the response time, forcing the team to work overtime to share information and updates.


### Subcontractor Recruitment


Procore Construction Network has an extensive subcontractor network from different US cities and towns. General contractors can connect with different subcontractors from any part of the country. With close to a million subcontractors registered to the network, there is always someone to help out with your project.


Unfortunately, most of the subcontractors’ information is outdated, and many are also inactive, making it challenging to recruit readily available subs.


### Pricing Plan


Procore Construction Network is free for any users. Anyone can create a free account and start using the database to connect with new subcontractors or advertise their construction businesses. Specialty contractors can list all the previous projects on the business profile to help them easily connect with potential clients.


### Pros


- **User-friendly interface** - the platform’s design is intuitive, making it easy to use even for new clients.
- **Expansive subcontractor network** - You can reach subcontractors offering different services and in different geographical locations.
- **Strong integration capabilities** - users can integrate the platform with other construction management platforms and streamline the construction management process.


### Cons


- **Poor customer support** - although the platform has a dedicated customer support team, the response time is slow, which is problematic for time-sensitive projects.
- **Limited mobile functionality** - the platform’s mobile app is limited and cannot handle tasks as the browser model.
- **Unverified subcontractors** - the database has a larger user base, but most of them are unverified, making it difficult to trust their capabilities.
