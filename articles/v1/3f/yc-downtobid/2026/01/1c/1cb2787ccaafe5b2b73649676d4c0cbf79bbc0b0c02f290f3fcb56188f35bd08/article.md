## How Much Does Procore Cost?


Procore doesn’t offer standard pricing for its services. Instead, it offers a tiered pricing model, where the overall costs depend on the construction size and additional features you need. The customization approach is **why the platform doesn’t have public pricing** since every project is different.


[Is Procore worth it](https://downtobid.com/blog/is-procore-worth-it) ? Well, it depends on what you need and your situation. Maybe their Annual Construction Volume pricing doesn't seem fair to you if you are closing big projects and you find that the software is not worth it.


For instance, a construction remodeling project would cost less than a complete construction project. This is because of differences in their sizes and the project requirements. Luckily, Procore doesn’t use a per-user pricing module, meaning you can onboard unlimited users. This approach is beneficial to larger construction companies with many users. You must contact customer support for a quotation.


Based on industry data, it suggests annual fees typically fall between 0.1% and 0.2% of hard construction costs. Though this seems like a small percentage, it can represent significant dollars on major projects—an expense that has to be factored into someone's budget.


Someone on[Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Construction/comments/1giwlxy/procore_cost_question_as_an_owner/) mentioned: *"The cost for my last Procore project was around $80,000 for a $59MM project, but that was without the financial module which is one of the big ones for Procore"*


Procore offers a demo option to help you familiarize yourself with the platform. However, there’s a catch. You must request a demo, as it’s not readily available, and must be within the supported regions. The sales team takes a few hours to set up your demo account. Although a free demo is a nice gesture, having to wait for hours is a bit frustrating.
