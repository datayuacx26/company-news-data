## What Is BidPlanroom?


BidPlanroom is a full‑service bid‑management hub that lets contractors create a branded portal for every project. After a GC uploads drawings, the system builds a digital plan room and issues invitations by email or fax. Each project can be "private" (invite‑only) or "public" (listed in BidPlanroom's marketplace so any registered sub can request access).


Subcontractors log in—free of charge—to view or download files, and BidPlanroom records who looked at what. Estimators see a dashboard of responses (Bidding, Not Bidding, Undecided) and can send batch follow‑up emails, distribute addenda, or collect final quotes through an online bid form. Because everything happens inside one portal—contact management, file storage, bid tabulation—BidPlanroom appeals to GCs who value a structured, end‑to‑end repository. ## Frequently Asked Questions


### What is the key difference between BidPlanroom and Downtobid?


BidPlanroom offers branded online plan rooms with structured document controls and requires subcontractors to create accounts for access. Downtobid focuses on AI-driven automation with one-click, no-login sub access, personalized bid invites, and verified subcontractor databases for faster and easier bidding.


### How do the follow-up and subcontractor engagement processes compare?


Downtobid automates follow-ups with polite, scheduled reminders and tracks real-time bid coverage, significantly reducing manual effort. BidPlanroom relies on manual batch follow-ups and depends on subcontractors actively using the portal, which can reduce responsiveness.


### Which platform offers better bid document management and control?


BidPlanroom excels in providing a comprehensive bid management hub with deep version control, addenda tracking, prequalification forms, and detailed bid tabulation inside a centralized portal. Downtobid streamlines access with a planroom design focused on immediacy and ease for subcontractors but with fewer granular document controls.


### Based on price and ROI, which is more cost-effective?


Both platforms have similar subscription pricing, but Downtobid’s automation and higher subcontractor engagement often yield faster ROI by saving time and increasing bid coverage, whereas BidPlanroom’s structured feature set suits GCs prioritizing organization and control.
