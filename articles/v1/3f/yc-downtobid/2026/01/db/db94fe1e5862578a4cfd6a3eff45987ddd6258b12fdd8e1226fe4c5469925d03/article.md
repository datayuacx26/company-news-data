## 2. ConstructConnect (formerly iSqFt)


ConstructConnect is the consolidated home of iSqFt, BidClerk, and Construction Data, forming one of the largest preconstruction ecosystems in North America. GCs use it to post ITBs, access a massive subcontractor directory, manage prequalifications, and even do digital takeoffs under one umbrella. Since SmartBid is also being integrated into ConstructConnect, this effectively makes ConstructConnect the “official” successor for those wanting a broader solution with the same corporate backing.


## Key Features


### Huge Subcontractor Network


By merging iSqFt, BidClerk, etc., ConstructConnect boasts a massive database. If you want broad coverage in multiple states or specialized trades, their directory can be a big plus.


### Prequal & Digital Takeoffs


Beyond simply emailing invites, GCs can prequalify subs using built-in forms and run digital takeoffs or cost analytics. Essentially, it can be your one-stop precon tool.


### Coverage Tracking


ConstructConnect offers real-time coverage views and reminders so you can see who’s opened invites or downloaded plans.


**Pros**


- **Potentially Largest Reach:** Ideal if you frequently need new subs in new markets.
- **Integration:** Ties into other ConstructConnect products for leads, analytics, and beyond.
- **Familiar to Many Subs** : iSqFt was widely used, so many subcontractors already have accounts.


**Cons**


- **Data Accuracy:** A huge directory can also mean stale or duplicated listings.
- **Pricing: **Typically not cheap—likely starting around $3,600/year.
- **Spray-and-Pray Risk:** GCs who rely on this big network might accidentally spam subs outside the correct scopes.


### How It Compares to SmartBid


SmartBid is literally being folded into ConstructConnect, so if you were comfortable with SmartBid’s brand or style, upgrading to ConstructConnect is the logical corporate path. You get a bigger directory, extra features like digital takeoffs, and the same overarching support. But watch out for potentially messy data and higher subscription costs.

BuildingConnected, acquired by Autodesk, is a network-centric bidding platform. It allows subcontractors to maintain a single login where they see all invites from different GCs. This solves one of SmartBid’s biggest sub complaints: multiple logins across multiple GCs. BuildingConnected’s robust planroom, bidding, and prequal features, combined with Autodesk’s ecosystem, make it a strong alternative for those wanting a polished network approach. ### Prequalification & Bid Leveling


BuildingConnected includes built-in prequal modules and side-by-side bid comparisons for quick leveling.

## 4. PlanHub


PlanHub is a free-to-post platform connecting GCs and subs. GCs upload project details; subs see them in a unified bid board—[somewhat like BuildingConnected](https://downtobid.com/blog/planhub-vs-building-connected-vs-downtobid) , but simpler and more budget-friendly. It’s particularly attractive for GCs who want an easy start (no big fees) and for subcontractors who are tired of multiple logins or complicated doc sharing.


## Key Features


### Free GC Posting


Most GCs can post projects at no cost, and advanced features are in premium tiers.


### Sub-Centric “Bid Board”


Subcontractors track all open invitations from various GCs in one place. This alone[may make PlanHub worth it](https://downtobid.com/blog/is-planhub-worth-it) .


### Built-In Viewer & Takeoff


Premium plans allow you to do basic on-screen takeoffs, so subs don’t need extra software.


**Pros**


- **Open, Modern UI:** Straightforward navigation for GCs and subs alike.
- **Low Barrier to Entry:** Great if you’re budget-limited or want quick results.
- **Increases Sub Engagement:** Minimal friction or new logins fosters better coverage.


**Cons**


- **Smaller Database:** Still scaling user base, so coverage depends on region/trade.
- **Limited Advanced Features:** GC-level analytics or custom forms might be lacking.
- **Upsells for Premium:** Real takeoff or advanced plan manipulation might require upgrading.


### Contrast with SmartBid


[PlanHub is much cheaper](https://downtobid.com/blog/how-much-does-planhub-cost) ) and extremely sub-friendly—two big friction points for SmartBid. If you want a simpler approach that draws subs in rather than annoying them with repeated logins, PlanHub’s open concept may drastically improve your response rates.

Procore is big on integrations (QuickBooks, DocuSign, etc.) and appeals to mid-to-large contractors wanting a single source of truth.

## 7. PipelineSuite


PipelineSuite aims for a direct, budget-friendly approach to bidding. It’s basically a web-based system where you maintain your own sub list, send out mass ITBs, track statuses, and share plan files via an online planroom. No big networks or advanced AI—just an efficient, private solution for GCs who prefer controlling their own contacts. Find out[more here](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/pipeline-suite) .


## Key Features


### Your Own Sub Database


You import and edit sub contacts (by trade, region), with no external directory forced upon you.


### Mass Email/Fax Invites


Create projects, attach docs, and invite subs. PipelineSuite logs who clicked or responded.


### Document & Addenda Management


Keep all project drawings in one place, notify subs of updates with a couple clicks.


**Pros**


- **Straightforward & Affordable:** Usually cheaper than SmartBid. Minimal training needed.
- **You Control the Data** : Not reliant on subs updating profiles in a huge directory.
- **No Extra Frills:** Focused on the basics to keep overhead low.


**Cons**


- **No AI or Big Network:** You won’t find automatically suggested subs or a large public directory.
- **Limited Integrations:** Lacks advanced reporting or deep tie-ins to other PM software.
- **UI Might Feel Basic:** Doesn’t have the slick interface or analytics of bigger platforms.


### Why Choose It Over SmartBid


If you dislike subscription costs or the complexity of SmartBid, PipelineSuite can be a leaner, cheaper solution for sending out invites and tracking them. It suits GCs who rely on local, established subs and just need a simpler system.


## Final Thoughts: Pick the Tool That Solves Your Biggest Headaches


Each alternative tackles a different SmartBid shortcoming:


- Downtobid emphasizes AI-driven coverage, ensuring no sub is overlooked and no invite goes unanswered.
- ConstructConnect offers a bigger directory plus integrated data—perfect if you already rely on their ecosystem.
- BuildingConnected addresses sub frustration with a single login and robust network.
- PlanHub fosters a free, straightforward approach that unifies invites for everyone.
- Pantera Tools is cost-effective and brand-friendly for GCs wanting custom labeling.
- Procore merges bidding with broad project management for those wanting a single platform.
- PipelineSuite keeps it lean, private, and budget-friendly if you just need the basics.


Ultimately, the “best” solution depends on your team size, budget, and whether you prioritize automation, sub engagement, cost, or integration with broader tools. If your main gripe is manual drudgery, something like Downtobid may deliver fast results. If you want maximum sub coverage, ConstructConnect or BuildingConnected might suit you better. By zeroing in on your biggest pain points with SmartBid and picking a tool that directly solves them, you’ll see less chaos in your next bid cycle—and more time to focus on securing profitable work.
