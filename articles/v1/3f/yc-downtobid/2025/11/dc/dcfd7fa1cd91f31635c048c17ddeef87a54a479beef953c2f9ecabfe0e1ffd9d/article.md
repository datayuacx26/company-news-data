## Master the Art of Competitive Bidding


Industry standard for hit ratio in construction bidding is around 5:1. That means for every five jobs you bid, you should win one. If your ratio is worse than this, you need to refine your approach.


Smart GCs get bids from at least three different companies for each trade. This means you're always competing against at least two other plumbing contractors. Your bid needs to stand out for the right reasons.


Start with accurate scope evaluation. Read every spec sheet carefully. Walk the job site when possible. Ask questions about unclear requirements. The contractor who catches potential issues early often wins the job.


Price competitively but don't race to the bottom. Factor in all costs including materials, labor, permits, and a reasonable profit margin.


Your[bid board](https://www.downtobid.com/bid-board) organization matters too. Track which types of projects you win most often. Focus your energy on similar opportunities where you have proven success.

## Perfect Your Follow-Up and Communication Strategy


Taking ownership of the follow-up process proves you're easy to work with and on top of your game. This simple step separates professional contractors from the pack.
