## iSQFT - Best for Project Leads


[iSQFT](https://downtobid.com/blog/isqft-vs-downtobid) has been operational since 1993, making it one of the oldest construction management software. It has been growing ever since, accommodating many players in the commercial construction sector.


iSQFT is best known for helping subs identify project leads in the North American market, thanks to its extensive project database. It mostly focuses on commercial construction projects with thousands of active projects.[Learn more here](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/construction-software-technologies) .


The construction manager platform entered into a partnership with Construction Market Data, Construction Data, and BidClerk to form ConstructConnect. Although the move improved the platform, it also means the older iSFT platform doesn’t receive any updates. Read more[iSQFT substitutes](https://downtobid.com/blog/isqft-alternatives) here.


## Key Features


Here are a few standout features of iSQFT.


### Extensive Subcontractor Network


General contractors appreciate iSQFT’s huge subcontractor network within the North American market. This approach helps the contractors easily recruit new subcontractors for upcoming projects. The platform has spent years promoting its networking capabilities, resulting in an ever-growing subcontractor database.


Unfortunately, the platform relies on subs to update their data, such as name, qualification, and availability. Although some users appreciate this freedom, profile updates usually take a back seat, especially when subs are busy with other contracts.


Most of the subs have outdated information, such as invalid emails or website addresses. Some are no longer active on the platform despite still being listed. Bid participation is very low compared to Downtobid.


### Centralized Bid Management


iSQFT has a dedicated bid board where subs can review bid documents and submit proposals. General contractors can create and manage projects. They can share project documents, invite subcontractors, and set project timelines.


However, iSQFT has a few notable drawbacks. The generic ITBs are boring to read and are identical due to a lack of customization. The platform also doesn’t have an automated bidding schedule, meaning the bid invites are sent randomly, resulting in poor response and bid participation.


Every bid invite is shallow and lacks crucial project information, which leaves the subcontractor in the dark. They have to figure things out on their own. The platform sends out addenda as separate emails, which can be overwhelming for subs to track.


### Collaborative Tools


iSQFT improves team collaboration thanks to centralized communication management and document management. Users can share updates and feedback, which improves collaboration. Document management allows them to upload and store documents on the platform. They can also edit and share documents, ensuring everyone works on the latest information. Building Connected has the following features.

Generic bid invites don’t cut it anymore. Building Connected has eliminated this problem by creating branded bid invites to make them stand out. The documents contain your name, company logo, and even a specific color.


Subcontractors can identify your ITBs and submit proposals. This approach increases bid participation, improving the chances of connecting with the right subcontractors.

Building Connected integrates perfectly with Autodesk Docs, helping you easily move construction documents between the two platforms. You can also use cloud storage as a backup to keep your files safe.


Since Autodesk Docs integrates with most construction management software, it’s now easier to transfer documents from these platforms into Building Connected.

With over 1 million vetted subcontractors on their database, it’s safe to say that Building Connected is one of the best places to recruit subs.
