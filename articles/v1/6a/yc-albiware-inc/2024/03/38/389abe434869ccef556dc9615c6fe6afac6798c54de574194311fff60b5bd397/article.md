## **A Revolution in Property Restoration Services for Water Damage**


There are four conventional steps involved in restoring a water-damaged property. Restoration services must first assess and document the damage, which usually involves taking photos and/or providing video footage of the damaged property. The next steps involve removing the water so that there won’t be further damage. After that, the entire area requires drying. Finally, the damaged area must be monitored to ensure no residual moisture left that can lead to mold development.


Yet new technology now helps deal with the challenges of water damage in property restoration. Services provided by professional restorers can now help mitigate the impact of water damage that previously proved difficult to handle. This includes technology that eases documentation of water damage, which ensures policyholders can claim the actual cost of property restoration. ### **Infrared Technology for Tracking Water Damage**


Increased accuracy in identifying where moisture exists has been transformed by infrared technology, which reveals temperature variations. This, in turn, allows restorers to detect areas where water damage may not be readily apparent by pinpointing water damage with greater accuracy.


### **More Efficient Dehumidification Technology**


Combined with apps that show where moisture is centered and identify water-damaged areas, modern dehumidification equipment does the actual work of drying a water-damaged area. These machines greatly reduce the chance of mold taking hold. Their faster drying times also minimize the risk of further damage to structures due to water damage.


### **Supporting Controlled Demolition Techniques**


Those areas deemed beyond repair by moisture mapping and infrared technology can be targeted for reconstruction. This targeted demolition saves much time, especially regarding water-related property restoration. Services for rebuilding and repairing the structure can then be done more efficiently and with greater precision.

Moisture meters are integral for any water-damaged property. Keeping track of these devices’ real-time data helps guide a restorer on the best action for mitigating and repairing a water-damaged structure.
