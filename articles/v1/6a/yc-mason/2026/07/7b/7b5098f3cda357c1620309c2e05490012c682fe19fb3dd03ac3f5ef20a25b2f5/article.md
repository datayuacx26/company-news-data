# Dr. Chet Robson Joins Mason as Advisor, Bringing Healthcare Expertise from Walgreens, Adventist Health, Jasper Health, and More

*By Nancy Xiao, President of Mason*


We’re delighted to share that Dr. Chet Robson has joined Mason as an advisor to help us further our healthcare and digital health solutions.


Dr. Robson brings a wealth of experience from his impactful twenty-year tenure as a senior physician, including his work as the Chief Clinical Officer at Walgreens. While there, Dr. Robson and his team evaluated thousands of companies, programs, devices, products, and services, ensuring that each offered maximum clinical benefit while minimizing risk for millions of customers. His evidence-based review process, grounded in evidence-based medicine and rigorous assessment methodologies, has set a standard in evaluating healthcare products and services for clinical integrity and raising patient care standards.


Mason’s integrated hardware and software platform has enabled the reliable collection of critical healthcare data across the globe. Today, healthcare companies have been stuck using broken manual or paper and pencil processes to collect, manage, and distribute their most valuable asset: real-time patient and clinician data.Dr. Robson's expertise will play a crucial role in guiding Mason’s next strategic initiatives, including ongoing development of cutting-edge connected solutions that meet the evolving needs of healthcare providers and patients alike.


***To quote Dr. Robson: “With so many dynamic changes, healthcare has become disjointed and siloed. What excites me about Mason is that the company combines the skills, knowledge, and experience to deliver comprehensive, integrated mobile infrastructure solutions. Creating connections from an individual to an enterprise level from clinical trials to healthcare delivery. Utilizing every aspect of hardware, software, and systems to create value-based solutions. I am excited to support Mason as they enable connected healthcare."***


Beyond his professional achievements, Dr. Robson embodies a passion for improving healthcare accessibility and quality—a vision that resonates deeply with Mason's core values. His commitment to evidence-based medicine, preventive care programs, and patient-centered approaches mirrors our dedication to creating impactful, inclusive solutions that empower healthcare providers and improve patient outcomes.


We’re thrilled to work with Dr. Robson on shaping the future of healthcare delivery through more comprehensive, creative, and efficient solutions with the power of the Mason platform.
