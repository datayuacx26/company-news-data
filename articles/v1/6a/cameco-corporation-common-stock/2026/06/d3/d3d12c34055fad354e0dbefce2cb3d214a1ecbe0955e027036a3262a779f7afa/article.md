It's an exciting time in the nuclear industry - and we'll need skilled leaders to achieve its full potential. That's why we're proud to be a program partner with the Canadian Executive Nuclear Energy Management School, hosted by the[University of Saskatchewan](https://facebook.com/31912359596) in cooperation with the[International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)](https://facebook.com/96699782061) . As part of the program, Cameco is also hosting a tour of Cigar Lake mine. During the Leadership Panel, Cameco Senior VP & Chief Corporate Officer Rachelle Girard joined other distinguished guests in sharing insights on leading through complexity, driving innovation, and shaping the future of critical industries. Moderated by Dr. Baljit Singh (USask) the other panelists included Janna Switzer ([Denison Mines Corp](https://facebook.com/1012137182154439) ), Sheldon Wuttunee (Saskatchewan First Nations Natural Resource Centre of Excellence), John Sowagi (Conexus Nuclear Inc.) and Doug Opseth ([SaskPower](https://facebook.com/9226212436) ).
