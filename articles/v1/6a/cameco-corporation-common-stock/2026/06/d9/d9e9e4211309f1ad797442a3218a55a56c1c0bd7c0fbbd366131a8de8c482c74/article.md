Skill, courage and dedication took centre stage at the[Saskatchewan Mining Association](https://facebook.com/122772681099567) ’s Emergency Response Mine Rescue Skills Competition in Saskatoon. Cameco crews from Cigar Lake, Key Lake and McArthur River joined a record field of 19 teams, and we couldn’t be more grateful for their commitment to being ready when it matters most. Special congratulations to McArthur River’s Marty Ledoux for placing second in the bench technician event which tests troubleshooting and repair skills for life-support equipment.
