# How Dojah Helped Flex Onboard 20,000+ Users with Its Identity Suite

[Flex](https://www.yourflexpay.com/) is a peer-to-peer payments platform that enables users to send and receive money instantly using a PayTag instead of traditional account numbers. Built to simplify everyday transactions, Flex set out to give Nigerians a faster and more convenient way to move money.


As the team prepared to launch, they needed an identity verification partner to help onboard users securely from day one. This was important to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and to verify that every user signing up was legitimate, reducing the risk of fraudulent signups that could affect the platform's growth. Flex wanted a partner that would be easy to integrate and support both compliance and user conversion as the platform scaled.


This case study explores how Dojah filled that gap and helped Flex effectively verify users, stay compliant, and successfully onboard over 20,000 users.


### **The Challenge: Verifying Users Accurately Without Slowing Down Growth**


As a peer-to-peer payments platform, Flex needed every user on the platform to be who they said they were. With money moving directly between individuals, a single fraudulent signup or unverified account could put real users at risk and undermine the trust the platform depended on.


At the same time, Flex also had to meet Nigeria's regulatory requirements around identity verification, making accurate onboarding a compliance necessity, not just a safety measure.


Because Flex was building its verification process from scratch, and it was a critical step in user conversion, the team needed an identity partner that could get onboarding right from day one, without adding friction to a product built on speed.


*"We needed a verification partner that could keep us compliant and help us onboard genuine users from the very start"*


— ***Arinze Ihemedu, CTO, Flex***


That search for a dependable identity partner led Flex to Dojah.


### **The Solution: Dojah's Identity Suite for Fast and Reliable Onboarding**


Flex chose Dojah for its identity verification needs after finding the


[EasyOnboard](https://dojah.io/all-products/easy-onboard) widget easy to integrate, with documentation clear enough to get the team up and running quickly.


*"Integration with the EasyOnboard widget was easy, and the documentation was clear."*


— ***Arinze Ihemedu, CTO, Flex***


With


[Dojah's Identity Suite](https://dojah.io/all-products/easy-onboard) in place, Flex built a verification process around BVN and NIN checks to verify each user's identity, alongside image checks to confirm the person signing up matched the government ID being submitted. To secure the authentication process, the team added OTP verification across email, WhatsApp, and SMS, giving users multiple reliable ways to confirm their identity during onboarding.


Among these, email OTP stood out for its consistent uptime, giving Flex a dependable channel it could count on as more users signed up.


Together, these services enabled Flex to accurately verify users from day one, maintaining the seamless experience the platform was built to deliver.


### **The Results: 20,000+ Users Onboarded and 99% Verification Uptime**


Since integrating Dojah's Identity Suite in 2024, Flex has onboarded over 20,000 users, verifying each one accurately while keeping the sign-up experience fast and friction-free.


Dojah’s BVN Advance played a key role in this, helping Flex confirm user identities quickly and supporting the onboarding process as more users joined the platform. Alongside this, Dojah's KYC service maintained a 99% uptime, giving Flex a dependable foundation to expand its user base while keeping growth on track.


*"BVN Advance has been really good for onboarding, and we've maintained about 99% uptime with Dojah."*


— ***Arinze Ihemedu, CTO, Flex***


With reliable verification running behind the scenes, Flex could focus on moving money instantly for users, without compromising on compliance or accuracy.
