# PolicyFly Partners with Green Shield Risk Solutions

## PolicyFly Partners with Green Shield Risk Solutions to Help Specialty Insurers Confidently Underwrite in Wildfire Zones


**New York, NY — May 29, 2025** — PolicyFly is excited to announce a new integration with Green Shield Risk Solutions, a wildfire intelligence company redefining how insurers assess and underwrite high-risk properties. This partnership brings Green Shield’s Property Guardian structure-level wildfire risk data directly into PolicyFly’s underwriting workflows—giving MGAs and carriers the ability to instantly triage, evaluate, and price risks in wildfire-prone areas. “The demand for coverage in wildfire zones is surging, but the tools underwriters have relied on are no longer enough,” said Cory Crosland, CEO of PolicyFly.


> ##### “By embedding Green Shield’s data into our platform, we’re empowering underwriters to filter the noise, find the right risks, and quote them faster than ever.” - Cory Crosland


**Tackling the Wildfire Underwriting Gap**


Traditional wildfire scores often fail to capture the full picture—ignoring key factors like vegetation around a structure, parcel-level characteristics, and real-time fire propagation models. Green Shield’s Property Guardian was built to close that gap. With over 200 data points and wildfire simulation models powered by historical weather, Property Guardian gives underwriters the detail they need to understand not just if a property is exposed, but how and why it’s vulnerable—and what can be done about it. “This integration puts that power exactly where it needs to be—inside the underwriting process,” said Brian Bastian, Head of Product at Green Shield.


> ##### “We’re not just giving underwriters a score, we’re giving them the context, science, and structure-level insights that lead to confident decisions.” - Brian Bastian


**Real-Time Risk Insight, Built into the Workflow**


The integration allows underwriters to process large volumes of submissions—including those coming in via broker portals, agency uploads, or even unstructured email—while automatically enriching those submissions with Green Shield’s wildfire data.


> ##### “We’re not trying to replace underwriter judgment—we’re giving them the clarity to act decisively. Whether it’s a quote, a decline, or a mitigation strategy, we equip underwriters with the context they’ve always wanted but could never get from a black-box score.” – Brian Bastian


Underwriters can now:


-


Instantly prioritize submissions based on wildfire exposure


-


See detailed risk profiles at the structure and parcel level


-


Understand fire propagation scenarios and mitigation opportunities


-


Quote only the risks that align with appetite and aggregate strategy


> ##### “In today’s market, insurers aren’t pulling out of wildfire zones because of lack of opportunity—they’re pulling out because they don’t trust their data, we’re changing that.” - Cory Crosland


“Together, PolicyFly and Green Shield make writing in wildfire zones not just possible—but sustainable and successful,” said Cory Crosland.


**Expanding Market Access, Responsibly**


As climate events grow more severe and underwriting grows more complex, this integration enables insurers to take a data-forward approach—without slowing down. Green Shield’s expertise, including team members with deep wildland firefighting experience, ensures insurers aren’t just relying on historical scores, but forward-looking, dynamic models that evolve with the environment.


For specialty insurers navigating shifting appetites, regulations, and capacity constraints, the PolicyFly <> Green Shield partnership offers a path forward: scale submission intake, reduce manual review, and grow confidently in markets once deemed uninsurable.


> ##### “Speed wins in specialty, this partnership gives our customers the speed, accuracy, and clarity they need to grow their business—even in the riskiest zones.” – Cory Crosland
