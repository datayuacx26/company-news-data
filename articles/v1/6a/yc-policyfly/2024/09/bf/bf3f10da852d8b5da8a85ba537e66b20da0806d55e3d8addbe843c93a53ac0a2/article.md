# SOC 2 Type II Certified

#### **PolicyFly Announces SOC 2 Type II Certification**


At PolicyFly, we’ve always taken security and trust seriously, and we are thrilled to announce that we have achieved SOC 2 Type II certification. This certification, verified by Prescient Security, emphasizes PolicyFly’s ongoing commitment to data security.


#### **What is SOC 2 Type II Certification?**


System and Organization Controls 2 (SOC 2) is a comprehensive compliance standard for service organizations developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA). A SOC 2 Type I certification evaluates whether controls are designed properly, while a SOC 2 Type II certification goes further and evaluates whether those controls are consistently functioning as intended.


#### **Why Does This Matter?**


Achieving SOC 2 Type II certification demonstrates PolicyFly’s commitment to keeping data secure. Our customers can rest easy that we are fully committed to safeguarding and protecting sensitive information today, and in the future.
