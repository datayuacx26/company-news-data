# Shipper and Inabuyer EV Expo: Collaborating for a Sustainable Future in Indonesian Mobility

[Shipper](https://shipper.id/) has recently made significant strides in supporting the progress of the electric vehicle (EV) industry in Indonesia by becoming one of the sponsors of


[Inabuyer EV Expo 2023](https://inabuyer.com/) , an event focusing on the electric vehicle (EV) industry.


This event, taking place in Jakarta, Indonesia, from 28-30 November 2023, brings together key stakeholders and enthusiasts from the EV sector, providing a platform for knowledge exchange, networking, and showcasing the latest innovations in the field. This significant event aims to showcase the latest advancements in the electric vehicle (EV) industry and promote sustainable transportation solutions in Indonesia. Shipper’s support for the expo highlights its commitment to driving progress and innovation in the EV sector.


#### **Shipper Commitment to Support the EV Industry**


The Indonesian government has been actively involved in promoting the adoption of EVs in the country. Through various policies and incentives, the government aims to accelerate the shift towards eco-friendly transportation.


[Shipper’s support](https://shipper.id/) for the Inabuyer EV Expo aligns with the government’s efforts to promote EVs. The company’s involvement in the event demonstrates its


**commitment to supporting the development of the Indonesian EV market and providing the necessary logistics infrastructure to facilitate its growth** .


#### **A Catalyst for Industry Collaboration**


The Inabuyer EV Expo, hosted by the Ministry, offers a groundbreaking platform for global EV industry players. The event will feature various exhibitions, seminars, and workshops to foster knowledge sharing, collaboration, and business opportunities. The expo aims to propel the growth of the EV market in Indonesia and contribute to the country’s sustainability goals.


#### **Revolutionizing Indonesia’s Electric Motorcycle Landscape**


Inabuyer EV Expo has successfully garnered attention as the largest electric motorcycle and ecosystem exhibition in Indonesia. This event is not just a showcase, it offers various activities including test rides of electric motorcycles. Inabuyer EV Expo aims to meet the needs of Indonesian society while positively impacting the development of the electric vehicle industry in the country. This remarkable contribution is expected to accelerate the transition towards more environmentally friendly mobility in Indonesia.


#### **Driving Sustainable Transportation Solutions in Indonesia**


As a sponsor of the Inabuyer EV Expo,


[Shipper](https://shipper.id/) recognizes the immense potential of the EV industry in Indonesia. The company firmly believes that sustainable transportation solutions are essential for the country’s economic and environmental future. By supporting this significant event, Shipper aims to raise awareness, foster collaboration, and accelerate the adoption of EVs in Indonesia.


#### **Shipper’s Technology Supports the Sustainability Commitment**


As a leading technology company in the logistics sector,


**Shipper** believes in the potential of sustainable transportation solutions and actively contributes with its commitment to supporting the progress of the EV industry in Indonesia. Through its advanced technology, extensive network, and commitment to sustainability, Shipper plays a role in accelerating the adoption and growth of the EV sector in Indonesia, ultimately contributing to a more sustainable future for the country.
