# Navigating the Dynamic Retail and E-commerce Landscape: Insights from the Indonesia Retail and E-commerce Summit 2024

The 2nd edition of the Indonesia Retail and E-commerce Summit took place at the prestigious Hotel Indonesia Kempinski in Jakarta on February 28, 2024. This highly anticipated event served as a gathering of industry leaders, professionals, and enthusiasts, all converging to explore the transformative journey of the retail sector in Indonesia. Shipper, represented by Lexy Ardhyan as our VP E-commerce, was a prominent panelist at this esteemed event, offering insightful perspectives on the strengths and challenges of Indonesia’s retail and ecommerce sector. As the discussion delved into topics such as rapid urbanization and the embrace of modern retail formats, Shipper emphasized the pivotal role of digitalization across diverse business models. Moreover, the discourse scrutinized Indonesia’s diverse topography and its impact on establishing a seamless supply chain.


However, our involvement at the event wasn’t the sole highlight. Shipper had the honor of receiving two prestigious awards: Supply Chain Innovation of The Year and Top 10 Leader of The Year, which recognized Budi Handoko, as Shipper’s COO & Co-Founder. These accolades underscore our commitment to innovation and leadership in the industry, reinforcing


our reputation in the retail and e-commerce sector in Indonesia.


At the summit, the focus was on showcasing the dynamic and ever-evolving landscape of Southeast Asia’s retail and e-commerce sector. This evolution is driven by the changing consumer behaviors and the rapid advancement of technology within the industry. With Indonesia poised to dominate over 45% of the Southeast Asian e-commerce sector by 2025, the consistent annual growth and the rapidly expanding user base present an enticing landscape for industry players looking to engage with this vibrant market. These factors underscore the immense potential and opportunities available for businesses operating within the retail and e-commerce space in Indonesia.
