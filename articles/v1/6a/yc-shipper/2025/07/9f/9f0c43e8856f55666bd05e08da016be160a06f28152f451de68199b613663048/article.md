# Shipper Plugin Now Available on Shopify: Smarter Shipping for Your Store

Exciting news for Shopify store owners! The


**Shipper Plugin is now officially available on the Shopify dashboard** , giving you direct access to powerful logistics tools without ever leaving your store’s backend.


This new plugin helps e-commerce businesses


**save time, reduce manual work** , and improve order fulfillment—all with just a few clicks.


## **Why It Matters for Shopify Merchants**


Managing logistics manually—checking shipping rates, creating shipments, tracking orders—can be overwhelming, especially for growing stores. That’s why Shipper created this plugin: to bring


**end-to-end logistics support directly into your[Shopify](https://accounts.shopify.com/) dashboard.**


With the


**Shipper Plugin on Shopify** , you don’t need to open multiple tabs or work with separate tools. Everything you need is now in one place.


## **What Can You Do with the Shipper Plugin?**


### **1. Check Real-Time Shipping Rates**


Get instant rate comparisons from trusted 3PL partners like


**JNE, J&T, SiCepat, Grab, Ninja Xpress** , and more—


**right from your Shopify dashboard** .


### **2. Create Shipments Directly**


Orders placed on your Shopify store can be


**processed instantly through Shipper** . Skip the manual entry—just sync and ship.


### **3. Track Packages in Real-Time**


No more copying tracking numbers into separate websites. Use the plugin to monitor your packages in one centralized view, and update your customers in a few clicks.


### **4. Generate Air Waybills Instantly**


Need a shipping label? The Shipper Plugin helps you


**create AWBs automatically** —ready to print and attach.
