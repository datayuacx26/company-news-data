# Profound vs AirOps: Which Tool is Better for GEO in 2026?

## Key Takeaways


- **Profound and AirOps perform different core functions.** Profound is built for AI-search monitoring and measurement, while AirOps pairs AI-search insight with content production and publishing workflows. The right choice depends on whether your team needs to measure visibility or execute content changes at scale.
- **Profound leads over AirOps on monitoring depth.** Profound is the broader option for multi-engine AI visibility tracking, prompt-volume data, and executive-level reporting, with coverage across eight AI surfaces including Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, and Google AI Overviews.
- **AirOps beats Profound on content execution.** AirOps connects AI-search insight to content operations, with page-level reporting that ties AI citations to Search Console and GA4 data, making it the stronger fit for teams that already know their content priorities and need a repeatable production workflow.
- **Pricing information is limited.** AirOps doesn’t offer publicly available pricing, while Profound ranges from $99/month for small teams and $399/month for growing (only 3 answer engines tracked), with estimates up to $2,000/month for the full package. Plan-specific quotes should be confirmed directly with sales for both platforms.
- **AthenaHQ offers a third alternative.** AthenaHQ combines deep AI engine monitoring and insights with content execution in one single platform, with agents turning citation data into prioritized next steps to improve your AI search visibility. It also offers a seat-unlimited pricing model built for teams that want to run GEO across the whole organization, not just a single analyst.


With over[61% of consumers](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/new-front-door-to-the-internet-winning-in-the-age-of-ai-search) turning to AI search to evaluate products and services, brands are turning away from traditional SEO tactics to earn attention. Generative engine optimization (GEO) helps your brand get in AI-generated answers, prioritizing mentions and citations over page rankings.


Two platforms in this category include Profound and AirOps, but there are some critical differences between them. While Profound leans toward AI visibility monitoring and measurement, AirOps connects AI-search insights to content operations. This guide breaks down each tool in more detail, and introduces a third option, AthenaHQ, for teams that do not want to choose between measuring and acting.


## Comparison Criteria: What Matters When Choosing a GEO Platform


Before evaluating Profound vs AirOps specifically, it helps to define the key capabilities for GEO platforms. Primary criteria include:


- **Monitoring and measurement:** This covers leading indicators that your brand is actually showing up in AI answers, including LLM coverage, brand mentions, citations, prompts, demand signals, competitor intelligence, reporting, and data freshness.
- **Execution:** This means content creation, workflow automation, publishing integrations, and repeatable processes that enable you to take insights and put them into action.
- **Enterprise readiness:** Implementation, integrations and API options where verified, governance and security evidence, team workflows, regional coverage, and pricing transparency.

## AI Engine Coverage and AI Search Visibility Tracking


Profound’s full plan includes Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, and Google AI Overviews, while AirOps reportedly spans ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. However, their coverage serves different purposes. Sources primarily position AirOps around content workflow automation and publishing, while Profound is more strongly suited for AI visibility analytics and strategic insight.


## Prompt Data, Citations, and Competitive Intelligence


Profound lists Prompt Volumes among its monitoring and insight features, along with prompt-volume data, advanced competitor benchmarking, and AI-visibility analytics. External sources compare Profound more favorably in this area, with deeper insights, including real-time reference rates and sentiment analysis. AirOps, by comparison, offers more basic citation alerts. AirOps also provides page-level data combining AI citations, Search Console, and GA4 traffic .


Before you commit, run through a short validation checklist. Good questions to ask on a demo include:


- Which prompts are tracked, and how is a citation defined?
- What competitor set is included?
- Which engines and countries are covered?
- What reporting exports are available, and how often does data refresh?
- Can data be segmented by product line, page, or region?


### Which Tool Is Better for Enterprise AI-Search Monitoring and Reporting?


It’s also important to consider whether your team needs executive and competitive intelligence or reporting tied directly to content-production workflows. If security is a factor, one comparison notes that Profound offers API, CMS plugins, secure SSO, SOC 2, GDPR, and 15-minute refreshes, while AirOps provides a Power Steps builder, GDPR compliance, and daily batch updates . Be sure to validate current terms directly with each vendor, since these details can change by plan and date.


### How Much Implementation Effort Should You Expect?


Choose a measurement-first rollout if you need to establish a baseline across prompts, engines, competitors, and citations before acting. Choose an execution-first rollout if you already have clear content priorities and need a repeatable production workflow. Either way, request a live walkthrough of onboarding, data connections, roles, CMS or analytics integrations, review stages, and export or API requirements before you commit. Do not assume unverified integrations are available on your plan.

Build a total-cost checklist that covers:

- Engines and regions required
- Prompt or response allowances
- Implementation and data integrations
- Content-production volume and review labor
- Support and enterprise governance needs


## How to Evaluate GEO ROI Over Time


Establish pre-launch baselines first. Track visibility and citation indicators by priority engine and prompt, connect content releases to those changes, then evaluate downstream traffic, conversion, and pipeline metrics over a consistent reporting period. This sequence keeps you from crediting a content push for changes that were already in motion, and it gives you a clearer perspective when you report results to leadership.

## Frequently Asked Questions


**How does Profound compare to AirOps for generative engine optimization?**


Profound and AirOps approach GEO from different angles. Profound focuses on enterprise AI-search monitoring, prompt-volume data, and competitive reporting across a broad set of AI engines. AirOps focuses on turning AI-search insight into content operations, with page-level reporting that ties AI citations to Search Console and GA4 data. Teams that need to measure visibility across many engines tend to prefer Profound, while teams that need to act on that visibility through content workflows tend to prefer AirOps.


**Which tool is better for enterprise AI search monitoring, AirOps or Profound?**


Profound is the stronger option for enterprise AI search monitoring based on supplied evidence, with named coverage across eight AI surfaces, dedicated prompt-volume data, and reporting of real-time reference rates, SOC 2 compliance, and secure SSO. AirOps offers monitoring as well, but sources describe its citation tracking as more basic and its reporting as more tightly coupled to content and page performance than to enterprise-wide competitive intelligence.


**Does Profound or AirOps offer better AI search visibility tracking?**


For the broadest supplied evidence of multi-engine visibility tracking, Profound is the stronger pick, with named coverage across Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, and Google AI Overviews. AirOps tracks brand appearance across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity and pairs that tracking with page-level data connecting citations to Search Console and GA4 traffic, which is useful when the priority is tying visibility to specific pages rather than tracking the widest possible set of engines.

There is no single best GEO software for every team; the right choice depends on whether the priority is measurement, execution, or both.
