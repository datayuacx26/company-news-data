# Digital Tech Show 2026: How to Unify Orchestration and Governance in the AI Era

The digital acceleration driven by generative AI has brought critical challenges for technology leaders: scattered scripts, unowned automations, fragmented operations, and no real visibility into what’s running in production.


What should be a smooth automation operation often turns into a collection of isolated silos, with decentralized executions and a lack of visibility into what’s actually happening in production.


On **May 5th and 6th** , BotCity will be at **Digital Tech Show 2026** to demonstrate how major Brazilian players have regained control of their operations through end-to-end governance.

## What to Expect from the Event


At **Booth 30** , the focus is on the complete journey: from isolated script to governed ecosystem, with full control over the automation lifecycle. This means end-to-end orchestration, production execution visibility, RPA and AI Agents working together, and real data to report value to the board.


To make this happen, we’ve prepared unmissable activations:


### 1. Exclusive Talks with BotCity Clients


Three real case studies from companies that have already made the transition. Each solved the problem from a different angle — direct ROI for HR, breaking free from lock-in with greater autonomy, and a federated model with a centralized CoE. Together, they cover the full spectrum of organizations trying to scale automations in production with real control.


-


##### **Pague Menos | May 5th · 2:00 PM · AI + Low Code & No Code**


Auditorium *“From manual spreadsheets to intelligent bots: how we generated over R$3M in avoided costs for HR”* , with **Daniel Miguel Arcanjo** — Technology/Hyperautomation Manager.


[Pague Menos](https://www.paguemenos.com.br/) will share how they automated the analysis and correction of time-clock entries based on labor regulations and scheduled shifts. The system identifies inconsistencies — such as overtime, rest-day pay, and invalid clock-ins — and applies or suggests adjustments, generating real productivity for HR and avoiding unnecessary payroll costs.


Daniel will open up about how they went from manual spreadsheets to a model that generated more than R$3 million in avoided costs.


-


##### **QuintoAndar | May 5th · 3:30 PM · RPA & AI Tech Auditorium**


*“From lock-in to hyperautomation: QuintoAndar’s journey with Python, AI, and business-unit autonomy”* , with **Larissa Soares** — Automation & AI Tech Lead.


[QuintoAndar’s](https://www.quintoandar.com.br/) journey is about a structural decision: moving away from low-code lock-in and building a Python-based model with BotCity. But the evolution didn’t stop there.


From execution to decision-making, the team advanced with visibility and scale until reaching hyperautomation with AI and a new role for the business in building automations. Larissa will share how this shift was made without losing operational speed.


-


##### **Stone |** ****May 6th · 10:30 AM · RPA & AI Tech Auditorium****


******** *“Federated agility: how a Stone operational team became an automation factory in months, not years”* , with **Vinícius Salazar** — Group Product Ops Manager.


How do you transform a purely operational area into a cost-saving engine in less than a year?[Stone](https://www.stone.com.br/) adopted a federated RPA model that allowed the operational team to take on a leading technology role, with a centralized CoE, FTE reduction, and improved quality, security, and governance.


Vinícius will show that, with the right methodology, tools, and support, a business can automate safely and quickly — eliminating IT bottlenecks.
