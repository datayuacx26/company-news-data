## Peakstone Realty Trust Announces Date for Third Quarter 2025 Earnings Release and Webcast


10/22/2025


Download (opens in new window)


EL SEGUNDO, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Peakstone Realty Trust (the "Company") (NYSE: PKST), a real estate investment trust that is executing a strategic transition to an industrial REIT, targeting growth in the industrial outdoor storage (“IOS”) sector, announced it will release its third quarter 2025 financial results after the market closes on Wednesday, November 5, 2025. Peakstone will host a webcast to present the results on Wednesday, November 5, 2025 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time.


To access the webcast, which will be available in listen-only mode, please visit[https://investors.pkst.com/investors/events-and-presentations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.pkst.com%2Finvestors%2Fevents-and-presentations&esheet=54343820&newsitemid=20251022045044&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.pkst.com%2Finvestors%2Fevents-and-presentations&index=1&md5=2f914ed6bcf716137937ac8cf13bee91) at least ten minutes prior to the scheduled start time to register and install any necessary software. A replay of the webcast will be available on the Company’s website shortly after the conclusion of the live broadcast.


To access by phone, please use the following dial-in numbers, as applicable:


1-844-825-9789 (for domestic callers)
1-412-317-5180 (for international callers)


**About Peakstone Realty Trust**


Peakstone Realty Trust (NYSE: PKST) is a real estate investment trust that is executing a strategic transition to an industrial REIT, targeting growth in the industrial outdoor storage sector. As part of this strategy, PKST is actively reshaping its portfolio to drive value creation.
