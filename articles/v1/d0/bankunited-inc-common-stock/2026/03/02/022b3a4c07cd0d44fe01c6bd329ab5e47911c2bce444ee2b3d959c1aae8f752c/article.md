# BankUnited, Inc. to Announce First Quarter 2026 Results

MIAMI LAKES, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 31, 2026-- BankUnited, Inc. (NYSE: BKU) announced today that financial results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 will be made public in a news release on Wednesday, April 22, 2026 prior to the market opening.


A conference call to discuss quarterly results will be held at 9:00 a.m. ET that day with Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer, Rajinder P. Singh, Chief Financial Officer, James G. Mackey and Chief Operating Officer, Thomas M. Cornish.
