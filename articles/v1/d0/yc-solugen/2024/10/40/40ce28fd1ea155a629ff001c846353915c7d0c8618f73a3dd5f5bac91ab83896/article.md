# Solugen Welcomes New Chief Operating Officer

We are thrilled announce the addition of an exceptional leader to our executive leadership team. With their diverse expertise and proven track record, they will play a pivotal role in driving our continued growth and enhancing our ability to serve our customers.


**Chuck Shehadi joins as Chief Operating Officer.**


A former Marine and Naval Officer, Chuck brings a hands-on approach with over 15 years of leadership experience at Apple, Tesla, Nike, and Zoom.


At Tesla, he built the global purchasing organization and supported the scale-up of Tesla’s Fremont manufacturing operations, along with international expansions into Europe and APAC.


At Zoom, he led global procurement during the company’s hyper-growth phase amid the pandemic.


His deep expertise in operations, manufacturing, and supply chain management will ensure Solugen’s operational excellence and drive rapid scaling across all assets.
