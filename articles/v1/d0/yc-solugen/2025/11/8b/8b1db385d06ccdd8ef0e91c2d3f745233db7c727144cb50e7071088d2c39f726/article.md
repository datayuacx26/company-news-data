# Solugen Global at Organic Grower Summit 2025

We’re heading back to Monterey for


[Organic Grower Summit 2025](https://www.organicgrowersummit.com/en/home.html) , and are looking forward to catching up with our growers, retailers, blenders, partners, and more.


This year, we’re bringing fresh field insights and data focused on


[Azogen 5-0-0](https://www.solugenglobal.com/copy-of-products)[— our](https://www.solugenglobal.com/copy-of-products)[OMRI-Listed, CDFA-Registered ammonia nitrogen](https://www.solugenglobal.com/copy-of-products)


product that continues to deliver precise, predictable nitrogen availability for organic growers.


**Ryan, Andre, and Bernard will be arriving December 1–2.** You can book a meeting with us


[here](https://www.solugenglobal.com/contact) .

**Here’s what we’ll be sharing at OGS 2025:**


-


New 2024–2025 field trial results showcasing how Azogen 5-0-0 helps bridge the nitrogen gap with consistency and accuracy. Full transparency, too; we're more than open to digging into those plots where results were different than anticipated.


-


Real-world grower stories from operations integrating Azogen into their fert programs.


-


Field photos and application insights from on-farm trials across multiple regions.


-


Practical nitrogen management conversations for organic growers looking to improve yield reliability/predictability.
