# Webflow Community Challenge: Build with MCP 2.0

**Build something with the Webflow MCP and show us what you made.**


It's open-ended on purpose. Connect the Webflow MCP to Claude, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible AI tool. Then use it to design, build, analyze, or govern your Webflow site in a way that would have taken you longer before.


Share a Loom recording, screenshot, or written walkthrough of what you built, and tell us about the use case it unlocked for you.


**Dive into our**[MCP Starter Kit to get up to speed](http://webflow.com/resources/mcp-starter-kit?utm_source=bettermode&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=fy27-MCP-Launch-Announcement) **or use of of these ideas to jump right into building:**


-


Build or update a component using your design system and CMS data


-


Ask Claude to analyze your site's performance and propose improvements


-


Set up agent instructions to keep AI-generated content on-brand


-


Build a page agentically from a Figma design


-


Use branching to safely iterate on changes before pushing live


**Bonus track — Prompt Library:** Share the exact prompt you used to accomplish your task. The best submissions will be featured in a publicly available Webflow MCP Prompt Library.


🏆 **Prizes** 🏆


Share what you built with the Webflow MCP and be entered to win $2,500 in cash gift cards and opportunities to be featured on Webflow.


**To participate in this Challenge and be entered to win a $500 prize:**


-


Connect the Webflow MCP to an AI tool (Claude, Cursor, or similar)


-


Complete a task or build something using MCP 2.0 capabilities


-


Share at least one of the following:


-


A Loom recording of your build or workflow


-


A screenshot of what you created


-


A written description of the use case and outcome


-


Post your submission in this thread below by **August 31st, 2026.**


**For a bonus raffle entry:**


-


Share your submission on social (LinkedIn, X, or Threads) with #WebflowMCP


**To be considered for the Prompt Library:**


-


Include the specific prompt(s) you used to accomplish your task


-


Prompts should be reusable and clearly scoped to a specific MCP use case


Made it this far? We can't wait to see what you create. Happy building!


## **FAQs**


**Who can participate?**


Anyone with a Webflow account. Webflow’s MCP is available today on all site plans, at no extra cost.


**How does the raffle work?**


Every valid submission earns one entry. Share your build on social and tag @webflow for a bonus entry. Submit by August 18th, 2026.


**When are winners announced?**


September 8th, 2026 here in the Webflow Community homebase.
