## What Is a Pet Tracker?


The first step in comparing PetPhone and traditional devices is to define what a pet tracker is and how it functions.


A pet tracker is a device that can usually be worn on a collar and helps determine the pet’s location through the use of GPS technology. Most of these pet trackers send the information straight to your smartphone, allowing you to locate your pet live or see the history of their movements.


Such devices can prove very useful for adventurous pets and provide a worry-free experience for their owners who are concerned about their pets going missing. Traditional pet wearables, however, usually only go that far—they do not provide any health monitoring, communication, or emotional engagement features.


They might be the go-to option when it comes to tracking pets, but their functionality is limited, thereby creating demand for more advanced and integrated gadgets like the PetPhone.


## What Is a Pet Phone?


A pet phone is a modern device designed to go beyond traditional tracking. In contrast to conventional GPS pet trackers or standard pet wearables, a pet phone is an innovative device that not only monitors a pet’s location but also facilitates interaction and health monitoring.


The PetPhone, as the currently first smart phone specially for pets, incorporates features like real-time tracking, two-way communication, and activity monitoring. It can also notify owners of unusual behavior or health issues, enhancing pets’ safety and overall well-being. As an innovation in pet technology, it goes beyond basic tracking, offering proactive care and engagement, making it a growing favorite among responsible pet owners.
