## Why Do You Need a Portable Mobile WiFi Device?


Although it’s easy to share the network with other devices by simply turning on the smartphone’s hotspot function, its drawbacks are clear. Firstly, using a phone as a hotspot will not only rapidly drain its battery but also cause overheating. Additionally, using the phone’s hotspot can severely consume the data of the phone's SIM card. Moreover, it often fails to guarantee a stable network signal. Whether it is the phone's performance or its vulnerability to interference from nearby devices, both can affect the network connection experience and work efficiency.


Unlike the phone’s hotspot, the portable mobile WiFi devices are specifically designed for sharing networks among multiple devices, and their signals are specially optimized to provide a more stable and smooth network connection. Additionally, using the portable mobile WiFi hotspot device does not consume the phone's data plan or battery power, offering longer-lasting network service. Compared to using a phone (as a hotspot), it can provide network connections for more devices simultaneously. For instance, GlocalMe's Numen Air can offer simultaneous, high-quality network connections for up to 16 devices. Most importantly, for frequent travelers or business people, it eliminates the hassle of researching local data plans and purchasing SIM cards. No matter where you are, a portable mobile WiFi device ensures your network connection remains uninterrupted. It can be said that a portable mobile WiFi device is like a global network pass, always safeguarding your global travels.


## How to Choose the Best Portable Mobile WiFi Device for Travel?


There are so many options available in the market. But which one is more suitable for you? Choosing the right mobile WiFi hotspot depends on your specific usage needs and scenarios. You can consider your typical office or travel situations, or think about moments when you would say, "It would be great if I had a mobile WiFi hotspot right now." In simple terms, by figuring out these core factors, you can easily select the best portable mobile WiFi device for your needs.


### Network Speed and Coverage


4G devices are more affordable, with the technology being well-established and offering a broader coverage area. In contrast, although 5G has a relatively limited coverage area, it can offer faster network speeds and lower latency, making it suitable for users who need to conduct high-definition live streaming or multi-person online video conferences. Consider whether the countries you frequently visit offer 5G coverage and whether your work has specific requirements for the high network speed and low latency of 5G.


### Balance between Portability and Battery Life


As portable devices, the weight, size, and overall design of mobile WiFi hotspot devices significantly impact the carrying and usage experience. When we are on the go, we prefer to avoid adding any extra burden. However, a lightweight design may come at the cost of compromising on battery life, which does actually affect your activity range and work efficiency. Selecting an excellent device that can achieve a perfect balance between long-lasting power and lightweight design is undoubtedly the best option.


### Stability of Multi-Device Connection


Whether it's traveling with family and friends or working remotely, we all need to share the network among multiple devices. A portable mobile WiFi device with a powerful chip and a specially optimized signal processing system can ensure smooth connections for multiple devices simultaneously without compromising stability.


## How to Choose the Most Suitable Data Plan?


Similarly, when choosing the most suitable data plan, it’s important to consider your actual usage situation, such as the duration of travel and expected data consumption.


### Travel Duration


Whether for a business trip or a vacation, if the travel duration is relatively extended or you need to move among multiple countries and regions, choosing a longer-term and higher-consumption package will provide greater reliability and security.


### Data Amount


If you only occasionally use mobile WiFi hotspots to send and receive emails or browse text and image-based content, the demand for data volume will likely be low. However, activities like frequent video conferences, high-definition live streaming, large file uploads or downloads, etc., will significantly increase your data consumption needs. Therefore, a larger data package is necessary to meet the requirements.
