## How to Connect Your Laptop and Other Devices


### Step 1: Insert a SIM card


Prepare a SIM card suitable for your mobile WiFi device and insert it into the card slot. For devices that support CloudSIM or eSIM, such as Numen Air, this step is not necessary.


### Step 2: Power on your mobile WiFi


Press and hold the power button to turn on your mobile WiFi device. The device's indicator light or screen usually shows the network status.


### Step 3: Find the network name


Use your mobile phone, laptop, tablet, or other devices to search for WiFi signals. Find the network name (SSID) of your mobile WiFi hotspot and enter the password to connect to the network. Usually, the default network name and password are marked on the back or screen of the device.


### Step 4: Verify connection


After connection, some devices may automatically switch to the browser for further verification or login operations to ensure the normal availability of the network.


### Step 5: Manage settings for best performance


The network settings can be managed through the device's screen or the accompanying APP, including modifying the SSID, password, the number of connected devices, and viewing the data usage, etc.
