## How to Select Your Best Portable WiFi Device?


Now, it's time to shift from “why do I need it” to “which one do I need”. With so many choices available on the market, which one is the best portable WiFi device for you? Don't worry. ### Network Options: Stick with 4G or Jump to 5G


It depends on your requirement for speed.


-


4G is mature and stable, with a wide coverage area, which is already sufficient for daily use (watching videos, responding to emails, browsing the web, etc.) and offers better cost-effectiveness.


-


5G, on the other hand, is for the future and can provide extremely high speeds and extremely low latency. For people who pursue the ultimate speed to handle large files or conduct high-definition live streaming, 5G is a more suitable option. However, the 5G network coverage in the areas you frequently visit is an indispensable factor that must be taken into account.


In summary, if you mainly live in areas with strong 4G coverage and have a limited budget, a 4G portable WiFi device is the practical choice. But if you want to enjoy the latest technology and ultimate speed, 5G is undoubtedly the superior choice.


### Coverage: Urban Routine or Global Travel?


The coverage of your device depends on your activity footprint. If you only use it domestically, most devices will suffice. But if you frequently travel globally, it is necessary to consider devices that support global frequency bands. Taking brands like GlocalMe as an example, it collaborates with hundreds of global operators and leverages core intelligent technologies, such as CloudSIM, allowing seamless roaming with no need to swap SIM cards.


### Battery & Weight: Lasting Power or Lightweight Portability


As a portable device, the battery life and weight of the portable WiFi router critically shape the user experience. Battery life is a tangible metric that directly determines your range of movement. Portability affects comfort. A lighter device is an everyday carry-friendly option.


Actually, an excellent product achieves a perfect balance between long-lasting power and lightweight design.
