## What Is Portable WiFi and How Does It Work?


[Portable WiFi](https://eu.glocalme.com/collections/portable-wifi)


is a compact device that gives one the possibility of using the internet wirelessly while on the move. Its working principle is like that of a mini WiFi hotspot that connects to cellular networks (4G or 5G) and then transmits a WiFi signal for your mobile phone, tablet, or laptop.


In contrast to conventional SIM-based internet, a portable WiFi device usually incorporates eSIM or cloudSIM technology. When you go on international trips, you can get the benefit of local data access without the need to buy or swap physical SIM cards.


To put it simply, it is your personalized internet bubble — and it moves with you wherever you go.
