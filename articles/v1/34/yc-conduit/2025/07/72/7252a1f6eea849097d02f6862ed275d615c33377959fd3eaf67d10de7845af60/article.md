# Cargo Theft Prevention Starts at Warehouse Driver Check-In

Cargo theft is at an all-time high. According to[CargoNet](https://www.overdriveonline.com/equipment/article/15750961/cargonet-cargo-theft-continuing-to-rise-at-blistering-pace) , incidents of theft rose 13% in Q2 2025, with reported losses exceeding $128 million. The top target? Warehouses and distribution centers.


That’s no coincidence—today’s theft schemes increasingly rely on document fraud and identity theft to bypass traditional safeguards.


These aren’t opportunistic crimes—they’re sophisticated operations that exploit outdated or manual driver check-in processes. And when it comes to cargo theft prevention, securing your gate is just as important as locking the trailer.


As the threat evolves, facility teams are being asked to do more with the same tools.


That’s why Conduit has added two new features to[Driver Check-in](https://www.helloconduit.com/driver-sign-in) that close common security loopholes—and make the process faster and easier for your team. Let’s take a closer look at what’s new.


[Take a tour of Conduit's Driver Check-in solution.](https://www.helloconduit.com/conduit-tour?ft_tour_entry_point=blog_theftfeatures)


## Driver Check-in Location Validation: Real-Time Location, Real Security


Early or remote driver check-ins create confusion, dock congestion, and—in some cases—open the door to fraud. With this new feature, Conduit now verifies a driver’s physical location before check-in is allowed.


**What’s Included:**


-


**On-site presence verification:** Confirm drivers are physically at your facility during check-in by capturing and validating their real-time GPS location.


-


**Automatic location validation:** Flag attempts to check in from outside the yard—or refusals to share location.


-


**Remote check-in prevention:** Ensure drivers can only log arrival when they're truly on-site.


**Result:** Better schedule accuracy, fewer disruptions, and enhanced operational security.


## Driver ID Verification: Fast, Frictionless, Fully Secure


Driver fraud is a growing risk—from stolen commercial driver’s license (CDLs) to impersonators using doctored images. Our new feature uses biometric tech and real-time validation to confirm that the person at the gate or in your yard is exactly who they say they are, while eliminating manual processes.


**What’s Included:**


-


**Automated CDL authentication** : Instantly verify CDLs without manual review.


-


**Biometric driver matching** : Confirm that the driver checking in matches the photo and information on their CDL using advanced facial recognition technology.


-


**Liveness detection** : Ensure drivers are physically present during CDL verification with anti-spoofing technology that prevents photo or video impersonation attempts.


-


**Compliance-ready logs** : Meet regulatory requirements and maintain audit trails with easily accessible driver credentials and tamper-proof verification records.


**Result:** Stronger security, faster processing, and reduced risk exposure at check-in.
