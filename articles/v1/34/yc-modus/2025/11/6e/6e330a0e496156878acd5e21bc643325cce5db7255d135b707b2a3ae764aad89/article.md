# Modus joins the Workday Partner Network - Modus | AI-powered headcount management

We’re proud to share that Modus is now a Workday Silver Innovation Partner — officially recognized for our certified integration, enterprise-grade reliability, and alignment across the Workday ecosystem.


For customers, this partnership delivers immediate value:


## Certified Integration Through the Workday Partner Network


Our placement in the Workday ecosystem ensures Modus customers receive verified integration, dedicated support, and smoother deployment across Workday environments.


## Enterprise-Grade Trust and Scalability


Silver partner status reflects Modus’ continued investment in security, reliability, and scale—key requirements for organizations running global sales and financial operations on Workday.


## Stronger Ecosystem Alignment


Modus can now integrate more deeply across shared systems and data, allowing teams to unify headcount planning, capacity modeling, and workforce intelligence directly with Workday’s platform.


As AI-driven sales planning becomes core to GTM operations, alignment between Modus and Workday ensures customers benefit from a connected, secure, and future-proof foundation.


**We’re proud to partner with Workday to help teams plan more accurately, move faster, and operate with a unified source of truth.**
