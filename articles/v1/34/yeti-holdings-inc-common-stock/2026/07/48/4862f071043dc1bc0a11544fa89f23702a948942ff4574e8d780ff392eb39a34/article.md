# YETI x Commonwealth Games 2026 | YETI Australia

Australian Team Chef de Mission Petria Thomas OAM OLY said the partnership reflects the Australian Team’s focus on performance and sustainability.


*“YETI’s commitment to durability and reusability aligns perfectly with our commitment to sustainability, and these products will play a meaningful role in supporting our athletes and officials at the Games,” Thomas said.*


*“Hydration is a critical part of performance, and YETI’s high‑quality products will give our Team one less thing to worry about as they prepare for Glasgow 2026 – we’re very grateful for YETI’s support and to be able to provide our team members with their world leading products, items that will be used long after the Games.”*
