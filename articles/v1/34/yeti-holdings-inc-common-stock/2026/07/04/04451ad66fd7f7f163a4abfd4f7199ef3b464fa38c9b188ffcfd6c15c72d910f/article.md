# Your Mission's Accomplice

"This bag is essential because first and foremost, everything stays dry until I need it — hooks can rust and I don’t want my flies getting wet until I choose. So I know it’s all safe in this gear case. I prefer to wear it as a crossbody bag using the[Sideclick™ Strap](https://au.yeti.com/products/sideclick-strap) and with this larger size, I can fit everything I need for a day walking the flats. I also like how the 6L pack has a mesh pocket, so that’s where I keep my leader wallet. This pack is impressive because it’s large enough that I can fit a[Yonder® 600 mL Bottle](https://au.yeti.com/products/yonder-6l-bottle) in it, depending on how much other gear I have.


When I am on the river guiding in Montana, I use the bag similarly: I leave bigger, heavier cargo boxes on the boat and use this gear bag to help me to be better prepared and organised while guiding clients."
