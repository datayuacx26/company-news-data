# Knowledge is Key | YETI Australia

**Trout:** Royal Wulff, Size #14 - arguably the world’s most popular dry fly, and with good reason.


**Murray Cod:** Double-Barrel Surface popper, Size #6/0 - Cod hit hard, and the top-water fishing is addictive.


**Barramundi:** Beechies Barra-Bomber, Size #2/0 - I’ll never tire of this Northern Australian favourite.


**Giant Trevally:** Black Brush Fly, Size #6/0 - One of the craziest fish to chase on a fly rod.


**Blue Bastard:** Spawning Shrimp, Size #2 - yep that’s it’s name, a favourite for the shallow water of Northern Australia.


**Black Spot Tuskie:** Brown Crab, Size #1/0- these aqua-blue tractors go from 0-100 in 2 seconds, hold on tight!


**Permit:** White Alphlexo Crab, Size #2 - the ‘Holy Grail’ of fly fishing, and luckily for us, Australia is full of them.


**Yellow-Tail Kingfish:** Squid Fly, Size #2/0 - visual, sleek, & hard fighting.


**Queenfish:** Absolutely anything that swims - Queenies are generally not fussy, but fight hard and put on an epic show.


**Aussie Bass:** Gurgler, Size #2 - a Southern freshwater favourite of mine!
