# Take Five with Tony Vandemore | YETI Australia

I’m in my[Cabela's waders](https://www.cabelas.com/shop/en/cabelas-northern-flight-front-zip-breathable-hunting-waders-for-men?ds_e=GOOGLE&ds_c=Cabelas%7CShopping%7CPMax%7CProprietary%7CGeneral%7CNAud%7CNVol%7CNMT&gad_source=1&gclid=Cj0KCQjwhb60BhClARIsABGGtw9iGtv97VY9iinXraWiDXQBbV4rehuMOJLREGM8QWb-IuVaUDhZkhwaAkL1EALw_wcB&gclsrc=aw.ds) every single day. Going through the wetlands, they’re essential. I’m also pretty lucky that I don’t get cold easily, so I usually just wear a[sweater](https://www.cabelas.com/shop/en/cabelas-special-ops-half-zip-fleece-long-sleeve-pullover-for-men) and that keeps me warm enough for the hunt. But if I need an extra layer for those extra cold days, I’ll throw on a vest.[The Redhead Elite](https://www.cabelas.com/shop/en/redhead-windshear-elite-vest-with-scentinel-for-men) one is windproof and is made to help conceal your scent.


Next to the[Makers](https://www.makerscallcompany.com/) calls on my lanyard, I keep bird bands. They’re little metal pieces that a federal or state agency puts on a bird’s leg. They’re actually the longest standing data set for helping waterfowl conservation nationwide. You report to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service where you got this bird with this band, and they use it to track harvest data and migration trends across decades. But they’re really few and far between. A lot of people hunt their whole lifetime and never get one. I’ve been pretty fortunate to have a lot of them and for me, they’re a sentimental thing. I’ll sit in a tree and I’ll look at one of my old bands that I shot with my grandfather 35 years ago. It’s a good way to track memories.
