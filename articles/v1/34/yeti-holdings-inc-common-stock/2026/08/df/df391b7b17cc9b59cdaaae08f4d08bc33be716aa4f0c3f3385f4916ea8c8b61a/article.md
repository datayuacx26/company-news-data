# Passing It On

For chef & ambassador Mark LaBrooy, fatherhood has made the outdoors even more meaningful.


*"The places and pursuits that shaped my life now have a whole new purpose as I share them with my children Whether it's teaching them where food comes from, passing on skills, or exploring the bush together... Creating memories through these shared experiences is one of the greatest privileges of fatherhood."*


We spent a day in the wild with Mark and his kids. Together, they got their hands dirty, started a roaring fire, and got to cooking up a feast. Empty bowls, and the peace and quiet of the wild - the surest sign of a meal well made.
