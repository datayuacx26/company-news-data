# Custom Tundra For You

Sometimes standard isn't good enough...


Previously only available to the YETI Flagship stores in North America, Custom Tundra Kitting is now available throughselected YETI retailers across Australia.


Tundras come standard with black latches, rope handles and badge, now you can add on a colour trim kit of your selected colour that best fits your personal preference.


-


-


These selected dealers will have a Custom Tundra Kitting station, where the store staff can update your new Tundra with new coloured latches, rope handles and matched badge on the spot.
