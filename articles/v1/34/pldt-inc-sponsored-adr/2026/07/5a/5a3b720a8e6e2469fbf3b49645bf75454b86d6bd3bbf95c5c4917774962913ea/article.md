# PLDT Enterprise Advances Resilience-First Thinking Across Digital Transformation, Cybersecurity and Agentic AI Journey of Philippine Enterprises

PLDT Enterprise Advances Resilience-First Thinking Across Digital Transformation, Cybersecurity and Agentic AI Journey of Philippine Enterprises


Manila, Philippines — As artificial intelligence changes how organizations work—and how cyber threats move—business leaders are being asked to think beyond adopting the newest technology. The more urgent question is whether their people, systems, and operations can continue functioning when disruption happens.


PLDT Enterprise is helping move that conversation forward through a series of speaking engagements, workshops, and executive discussions focused on digital and cyber resilience, preemptive defense, data protection, and the emerging role of Agentic AI in business operations.


Through the participation of Alexis Bernardino, Assistant Vice President and Head of the CXO Advisory Group at PLDT Enterprise, the organization has brought its perspectives to major industry platforms, including the Gov Excellence Summit 2026, Cyber Resilience Summit 2026, Digital Next Philippines 2026, and the upcoming Philippines Agentic AI Forum.


These engagements build on PLDT Enterprise’s continuing work through its Preemptive Cyber Defense discussions, Data Exposure Defense Workshop and CXO Cyber and Digital Resilience Masterclass—bringing complex technology conversations closer to the real concerns of business leaders across the public sector, conglomerates, banking and financial services, key industries, and SMEs.


**Moving from digital transformation to resilience**


For years, digital transformation has largely been associated with modernization: moving to the cloud, connecting more systems, and introducing new platforms. But as businesses become more digital, they also become more exposed to disruptions that can affect customers, employees, revenue, and public trust.


PLDT Enterprise is encouraging organizations to broaden the conversation—from simply transforming operations to building operations that can withstand, respond to, and recover from disruption.


“In a landscape being reshaped by AI, transformation alone is no longer enough. Organizations must also ask whether their business operations can remain secure, trusted, and available when disruption happens,” said Bernardino. “Digital and cyber resilience means building that readiness across the whole enterprise anticipating the worst case scenario—from connectivity infrastructure, network, cloud, cybersecurity, data, and ultimately to AI.”


GovXcellence 2026 Summit showcased a powerful shift toward a unified, data-driven ecosystem, highlighting breakthrough innovations in cybersecurity and enterprise mobility. From high-level plenary sessions to cutting-edge tech exhibits, GovXcellence 2026 has officially set a new benchmark for digital governance, proving that the roadmap to a modernized Philippines is not just a vision but an active, accelerating reality.


At the Cyber Resilience Summit 2026, industry leaders examined how organizations can remain operational and trusted even during an attack. The gathering placed resilience at the center of business strategy, highlighting the need for stronger governance, practical response frameworks, and readiness for AI-driven threats.


Digital Next Philippines 2026 widened the discussion to the country’s evolving digital economy, bringing together leaders from government, banking, public services, retail, telecommunications, and other industries. The summit explored how organizations can pursue innovation while protecting data, customers, and institutional trust.


PLDT Enterprise’s participation across these platforms reflects a consistent message: cybersecurity cannot be treated as an isolated technical function. It must be connected to business continuity, customer experience, leadership decisions, and long-term growth.


**Preparing for threats before compromise**


One of the key ideas PLDT Enterprise is advancing is the shift from a purely reactive security posture toward preemptive cyber defense—helping organizations identify exposure and strengthen defenses before an incident becomes a business crisis.


This approach is supported by what the CXO Advisory Group describes as data exposure defense and an end-to-end view of enterprise architecture. Rather than looking at connectivity, networks, cloud, cybersecurity, and AI as separate layers, organizations are encouraged to see how these components work together throughout the transformation journey.


The goal is straightforward: help businesses neutralize risks before compromise.


For decision-makers, this means asking practical questions. Where are the organization’s most critical exposures? Which services must remain available during an attack? How quickly can teams detect, respond, and recover? And as AI becomes embedded in more workflows, how can it be used not only by attackers, but also by defenders?


**Bringing Agentic AI into real operations**


The next stage of this conversation centers on Agentic AI—systems designed to move beyond providing recommendations and toward planning, deciding, executing, and optimizing workflows with greater autonomy.


At the upcoming Philippines Agentic AI Forum, the focus turns to how enterprises can move from AI experimentation toward production-ready applications that create measurable business outcomes. For PLDT Enterprise, this creates an opportunity to advance an Agentic AI Operations practice that brings together infrastructure modernization, networks, cloud, cybersecurity, data, and AI.


“AI is turbo charging the speed and scale of change on both sides of cybersecurity,” Bernardino added. “The same technology that introduces new risks can also help organizations detect unusual activity earlier, respond faster, and operate more intelligently. The challenge is to adopt it with the right architecture, controls, and governance from the beginning.”


This integrated approach allows PLDT Enterprise to support organizations at different stages of their journey—from enterprises strengthening foundational connectivity and cloud environments to leaders preparing for more advanced AI-enabled operations.


**Turning expertise into practical guidance**


Across its industry engagements, PLDT Enterprise aims to make resilience more understandable and actionable for executives and operational teams.


The value of thought leadership lies not only in discussing future technologies, but in helping organizations make better decisions today: protecting essential services, safeguarding customer trust, preparing people for disruption, and ensuring that innovation does not come at the expense of security.


By bringing together its fixed, wireless, and ICT capabilities with advisory perspectives on digital and cyber resilience to Agentic AIOps Practice, PLDT Enterprise is helping organizations see a more complete path forward—one where transformation, protection, and continuity move together.


As AI adoption accelerates and cyber risks continue to evolve, resilience will increasingly define which organizations can adapt with confidence. Through its growing presence in major industry conversations, PLDT Enterprise is helping Philippine businesses prepare not only for the technology ahead, but for the people, operations, and trust that must remain protected along the way.
