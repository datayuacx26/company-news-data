# PLDT Enterprise strengthens SKYGO’s digital transformation with IoT and managed connectivity solutions

PLDT Enterprise and Skygo Marketing Corp. leaders come together to mark their growing partnership, aimed at strengthening Skygo’s digital transformation through smarter connectivity, managed network solutions, and future-ready technologies. (L-R): Hazel Amoyan, Head of CRM - Visayas, PLDT Enterprise; Jay Lagdameo, Vice President and Head, Domestic Enterprise Business, Regional & Commercial, PLDT Enterprise; Shyrwinsteel Sia, Chief Technology Officer, Skygo Marketing Corp.; Waldo Layese, Software Manager, Skygo Marketing Corp.


---


PLDT Enterprise is strengthening its strategic partnership with SKYGO Marketing Corporation, supporting the motorcycle retailer and distributor’s digital transformation journey through a more reliable, secure, and scalable network foundation. The collaboration highlights how integrated connectivity, IoT, and cloud-ready solutions can help businesses modernize operations, improve efficiency, and prepare for long-term growth.


SKYGO Marketing Corporation is focused on empowering Filipinos with mobility through affordable world-class motorcycles. Operating nationwide in the Philippines, the company is pursuing a broader digital transformation strategy aimed at building a fully integrated, secure, and high-performing digital ecosystem that enhances operational efficiency, enables data-driven decision-making, and supports scalable business growth.


At the center of this effort is PLDT Enterprise’s MY Only WAN offer, bundled with Fiber 100MB, wireless internet backup, Meraki SD-WAN, and Managed Network Services, along with postpaid plans and IoT solutions across fixed and wireless. Together, these solutions are designed to provide SKYGO with business continuity, centralized visibility and control, stronger network security, and improved application performance across its growing operations.


**A commitment to enabling digital transformation in retail and distribution**


As a company in the retail and distribution – motorcycle space, SKYGO’s business relies on stable and high-performing digital infrastructure to support operations across branches and users nationwide. The company has been working toward a more connected environment where platforms such as Microsoft O365, Microsoft Dynamics, and IoT solutions can operate seamlessly.


To support this direction, the partnership with PLDT Enterprise is intended to address key business challenges, including inconsistent connectivity, lack of network redundancy, limited visibility across sites, and rising demands from cloud-based and IoT-driven systems, as these issues can affect critical applications, productivity, and overall operational efficiency if left unaddressed.


With the MY Only WAN setup, SKYGO is expected to benefit from automatic failover, uninterrupted connectivity, centralized network management, and intelligent traffic routing that helps critical applications such as Microsoft O365 and Microsoft Dynamics perform with lower latency and better user experience. The solution also supports standardized security policies across branches and users, helping strengthen compliance and protection against cyber threats and unauthorized access.


“PLDT Enterprise remains committed to helping businesses build the secure and scalable digital foundations they need to grow,” said Jay Lagdameo, Vice President and Head of Domestic Enterprise Business, Regional & Commercial at PLDT Enterprise. “Through solutions that combine connectivity, centralized management, and resilience, we support organizations like SKYGO as they modernize operations and move toward a more integrated digital environment.”


**Built for continuity, visibility, and growth**


The business impact expected from the partnership includes improved operational efficiency through centralized network management, faster issue resolution, proactive monitoring, and better overall network performance. By reducing downtime risks and strengthening business continuity, the solution is also expected to support stronger productivity and more consistent service delivery.


The partnership is also positioned as a foundation for future initiatives, particularly around IoT and Microsoft O365 deployment. From SKYGO, Shyrwinsteel Sia, Chief Technology Officer said that “this partnership is framed around a shared direction toward innovation, stronger digital infrastructure, and long-term business growth.”


“As SKYGO continues to modernize its business processes and expand its digital capabilities, the partnership with PLDT Enterprise highlights how integrated connectivity, IoT, cloud-ready infrastructure, and managed services can work together to support a more secure, resilient, and future-ready business environment,” he concluded.
