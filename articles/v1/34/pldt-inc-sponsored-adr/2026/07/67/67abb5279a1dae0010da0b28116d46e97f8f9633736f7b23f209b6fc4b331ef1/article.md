# News | PLDT Enterprise | Billing Enhancement Advisory

We are rolling out updates to the **PLDT Bills Online (BOL)** platform to deliver a more **secure, convenient, and transparent** experience for customers accessing their billing information. Target Implementation: **July 24, 2026**


**What's Changing?**


- **Simplified Login Experience** - More intuitive and user-friendly login interface for easier navigation for customers accessing their billing information.
- **Stronger Security Measures** - Enhanced password protection requirements, email-based verification for added account security.
- **Improved Self-Service Support** - Self-service password reset and account recovery for faster access when needed.
- **Updated Terms & Privacy** - Refreshed Terms & Conditions and Data Privacy provisions for greater transparency on customer data handling and protection.


**What Customers Need to Do on First Login**
Upon their first login after go-live, customers will be asked to complete the following steps before they can fully access the platform:


- • Update their password to meet the new security requirements.
- • Review and accept the updated Terms & Conditions and Data Privacy provisions.
- • Confirm that their registered contact details are up to date to support email-based verification and account recovery.
