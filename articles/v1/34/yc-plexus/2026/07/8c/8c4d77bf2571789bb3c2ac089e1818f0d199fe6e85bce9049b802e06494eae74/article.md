We are excited to highlight our continued partnership with @Greater Fox Cities Habitat for Humanity to construct our fourth home through the Plexus Community Foundation. Our team recently marked a significant milestone as we gathered to raise the first wall at the construction site.


At Plexus, our vision extends far beyond the transformative products we help create—it’s also about the lasting, positive impact we make in our communities. Seeing our volunteers come together to support this build is a powerful reminder of how we live our vision and values every day.


Thank you to every volunteer who has contributed to this meaningful project so far, helping to advance our commitment to building a better world. Stay tuned for further updates as we prepare to hand over the keys in August.


At Plexus, manufacturing and engineering in today’s evolving landscape requires more than technical skill; it demands innovative solutions for the world’s most complex challenges. We turn bold ideas into real-world applications, and the industry is taking notice.


Plexus was recognized as a finalist at the 2026 Manufacturing Leadership Awards, presented by the Manufacturing Leadership Council (part of the National Association of Manufacturers (NAM)).


These prestigious awards celebrate organizations that shape the future of manufacturing. For Plexus, being named a finalist recognizes how we turn innovation into results that drive extraordinary outcomes for our team members, customers and the world.


We are incredibly proud to be recognized across two competitive project categories that define modern advancements:


**AI Vision and Strategy:** honoring our project, *“Innovating Responsibly: Driving Enterprise Value Through Strategic AI Adoption.”* We have democratized cutting-edge technology by deploying enterprise-wide self-service AI tools to over 20,000 global team members. From implementing machine learning-driven self-healing supply chain parameters that reduced component lead times to leveraging “Gemini code assist” to boost software developer velocity, we are embedding AI into the fabric of our culture.


**Sustainability and the Circular Economy:** recognizing our project, *“Zero Waste to Landfill: Powering a Circular Economy for Local Industry.”* Led by our manufacturing team in Neenah, Wisconsin, we’ve built an environmental framework that achieves zero waste to landfill.


Through a circular strategy, we feed paper directly back to local mills, process hard-to-recycle plastics into high-density fuel pellets and route remaining residuals to power a local cheese factory. This project achieved a massive reduction in waste-related Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions and inspired other global Plexus sites to work toward achieving zero waste to landfill status.


## Under Consideration for Other Top Industry Honors


Beyond the individual project categories, Plexus caught the attention of the judging panel, placing us under consideration for these additional distinctions:


- **The Manufacturer of the Year Award:** chosen from the high-scoring project award winners across all categories and recognizes the companies that have demonstrated outstanding, holistic success in driving digital transformation, innovation and operational excellence.
- **The Future of Manufacturing Award:** a unique accolade reserved exclusively for projects identified by the judges as exceptionally innovative, disruptive and futuristic.


## Driven by Our Global Team


These recognitions are only possible because of our people. Every day, our 20,000 global team members are driven by our shared vision to help create the products that build a better world.


At Plexus, we believe that designing, manufacturing and servicing some of the world’s most transformative products, from life-saving medical devices to advanced aerospace systems, begins with fostering a workplace where every individual feels deeply valued, heard and supported.


That is why we intentionally cultivate a culture of innovation, belonging, trust and growth. We invest in our people, providing the foundation for them to continuously expand their skills, so they can realize their full potential through hands-on leadership development and continuous learning programs.


By prioritizing their well-being and professional growth, we empower their collaborative spirit and passion to push the boundaries of what is possible in electronics manufacturing.
