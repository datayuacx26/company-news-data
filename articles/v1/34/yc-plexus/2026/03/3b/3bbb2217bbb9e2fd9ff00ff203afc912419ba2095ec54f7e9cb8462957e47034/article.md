**Title:** EUROPE | MISSION READY: Accelerating Defence Tech from TRL 3 to Volume Production
**Description:** In a new era of European security, the UK and EU are demanding a step-change in procurement speed—moving from years to months. For non-traditional defence partners and established OEMs alike, the hurdle is navigating complex regulatory landscapes while scaling production within regional borders.


This session focuses on the journey from TRL 3 to 6 through the lens of Sovereign Readiness. We will discuss how OEMs can de-risk their transition to volume production, ensuring that next-generation sensors, comms, and autonomous systems are qualified, compliant, and ready for the Euro-Atlantic theater.
