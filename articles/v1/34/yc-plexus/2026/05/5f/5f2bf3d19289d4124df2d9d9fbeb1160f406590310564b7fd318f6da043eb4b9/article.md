**Description:** Join Plexus to explore the fundamental shift in medical device development as the industry moves from traditional, rules-based programming to data-driven machine learning (ML) architectures. We will examine the core opportunities presented by ML, alongside the technical hurdles of implementation.


The session will address opportunities, from managing the complexities of data labeling to the transition from cloud-based models to efficient, on-device inference. This webinar aims to equip developers and engineers with a clear roadmap to enable medical devices with the latest machine learning capabilities.
