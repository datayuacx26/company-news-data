# The PIM Space Is Evolving At The Speed Of AI

Earlier this year, I had the opportunity to take over coverage of the product information management (PIM) space from my colleague


[Chuck Gahun](https://www.forrester.com/analyst-bio/chuck-gahun/BIO20107) , and it has been a fascinating journey. I’ve immersed myself in myriad vendor briefings, dug into


[historical](https://www.forrester.com/report/the-forrester-wave-tm-product-information-management-q4-2023/RES179976) and more


[recent](https://www.forrester.com/report/demystifying-the-technical-functions-of-product-information-management/RES186480) Forrester research, and educated myself on the history of the space and its evolution. The PIM space has transformed well beyond its roots as a back-office data repository. Today, it plays a central role in how organizations manage, govern, and activate product data across increasingly complex digital ecosystems. The market has evolved to meet the needs of commerce leaders who are looking for vendors that effectively enable controlled, scalable product data in an AI-driven world.


### **What Are Some Common Themes?**


- **AI continues to redefine how PIM creates value.** To no surprise of anyone remotely connected with the commerce space, AI continues to reshape the direction of the PIM market. Yet the conversation has evolved beyond simply adding AI capabilities. Organizations increasingly seek to balance speed and scale with human oversight, reliability, and accountability.


- **Rising expectations are redefining product information management.** Commercial and technology forces are raising expectations for how product information is managed. Regulatory requirements necessitate increased transparency, traceability, and control. At the same time, segment


boundaries are blurring as platforms, partners, and surrounding technologies grow more interconnected. These realities impact how organizations think about product information, with the focus shifting from intrinsic capabilities to broader ecosystem information flow.


- **PIM value is shifting from features to orchestration.** While the foundational functionality of PIM is still key, differentiation is increasingly moving toward how effectively platforms support coordination, governance, and interoperability. Also, competitive pressure is evidenced not only in AI capabilities but also from adjacent technologies, evolving buyer expectations, and changing product optimization approaches. The market is being redefined around adaptability, control, and effective operation across expanding networks of systems, data, and stakeholders.


### **What To Watch For**


In the landscape report, I’ll highlight the major trends, but what stands out is the broader pattern. There is evidence of an industry shift where there is a blend of innovation with control, expansion with discipline, and ambition with caution — with variations between the players. There’s a lot more beneath the surface of these signals, and the full landscape report will unpack the latest on the space and its vendors.
