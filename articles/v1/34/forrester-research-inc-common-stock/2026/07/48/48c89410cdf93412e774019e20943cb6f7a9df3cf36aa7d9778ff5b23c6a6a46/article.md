# Answer Engines’ Next Act: From Visibility To Growth

Treating answer engine optimization (AEO)[best practices](https://www.forrester.com/report/best-practices-for-answer-engine-optimization-aeo/RES188745) like checklist activities ignores answer engines’ impacts on brand strategy and interdisciplinary workflows. Effective, differentiated AEO requires marketers to understand answer engines’ role in the funnel and the new ways they orchestrate influence.


## Answer Engines Are More Like Billboards Than Search Engines


Answer engines act like brand media rather than search results, which harvest demand generated upstream. Answer engines create demand to lift branded search activity, confer salience and energy on brands, and build memory structures that buyers retain throughout their journeys.


Answer engines are the primary audience that marketers must influence because they determine when and how buyers form preferences. Engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini turn curiosity into guided, branded exploration by inducing buyers to ask branded questions early in their journeys. For example, a consumer who asks, “What are the best high-yield savings accounts?” now quickly follows up with questions like “Is Marcus by Goldman Sachs worth it for someone with $50,000 in savings?” Engines don’t just answer questions; they contextualize brands and foster deep evaluation.


## Cross-Functional Coordination Is The New Key To Orchestrating Influence


Influencing when and how answer engines mention and cite brands is a thorny process problem. Because answer engines’ opinions are determined by a vast array of sources, influencing answer engines is an omnichannel, cross-functional effort. AEO entails[several roles working together](https://www.forrester.com/report/answer-engine-optimization-aeo-role-connections-tool/RES189795) to shape visibility and sentiment across highly cited sources like Forbes, LinkedIn, Reddit, and YouTube. The key to influence orchestration is converging search and social.


Social listening data becomes a critical input into AEO because it helps marketers measure how ideas circulate, not just where queries occur. Social media’s aggregation of FAQ-style conversations featuring vulnerable, authentic voices (rather than brand and product marketing speak) makes it highly visible across answer engines. Viral narratives supply the language, examples, and sentiment that answer engines rely on, making social media the primary influencer of AEO outcomes. SEO and social media practitioners must sync operationally around shared goals such as greater visibility and better sentiment.
