# The Architecture And Delivery Leader’s Guide To Technology & Innovation Forum Central

Architecture and delivery leaders are under pressure from every direction. They are expected to modernize platforms, reduce technical debt, improve delivery speed, support AI initiatives, strengthen governance, and prove business value — all while keeping systems running and teams engaged.


The challenge is no longer identifying what needs to change. Most leaders already know that. The challenge is figuring out how to make meaningful progress in an environment where complexity keeps growing and expectations keep rising.


**Technology & Innovation Forum Central** , taking place in Austin on September 14–15, 2026, is designed to help leaders navigate those realities. But there’s a packed agenda, and success comes down to making intentional choices about where you spend your time.


If you’re responsible for architecture, application development, infrastructure, platforms, engineering, or technology delivery, here are the sessions you should prioritize.


Review the[agenda](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/agenda/) and register for Technology & Innovation Forum Central to start building your plan for September.


## Start Monday By Focusing On The Foundation


Many organizations are eager to accelerate AI adoption, but architecture and delivery teams know the real work happens underneath the surface. Data quality, platform readiness, integration complexity, operating models, and governance often determine whether an initiative succeeds or stalls.


Monday’s certification sessions provide a strong starting point, particularly if you’re looking to assess your organization’s readiness and identify gaps in strategy, delivery, or AI adoption.


**From there, prioritize:**


- Build The Technical Foundation For Business Outcomes In The AI Era


This workshop tackles one of the most important challenges facing architecture leaders today: connecting technology decisions to business results.


Technology leaders are increasingly expected to demonstrate impact, not just deploy systems. This session explores how to create the technical foundations that support measurable outcomes and sustainable growth.


**You should also consider:**


- Changing The Nature Of Work: Redefining The IT Operating Model In The Age Of AI


Many organizations discover that delivery bottlenecks are not caused by technology alone. Team structures, governance models, and decision-making processes can become just as limiting as outdated platforms. This session examines how technology organizations must evolve as AI transforms how work gets done.


Before wrapping up the day, make time for the Planview case study. Real-world examples often provide practical lessons that are difficult to gain from frameworks alone.


## **Start Tuesday With Enterprise Architecture**


Tuesday begins by shifting the conversation from experimentation to outcomes. The opening keynote focuses on how organizations move beyond AI pilots and toward meaningful customer and business impact.


For architecture leaders, the first major decision point comes during the 10:30 a.m. breakout sessions.


- Enterprise Architecture: The Cutting Edge


This should be a priority session for anyone responsible for architecture strategy.


Enterprise architects are being asked to do more than create technology standards. They are helping organizations navigate AI adoption, guide modernization efforts, enable business transformation, and provide governance without creating unnecessary friction.


As organizations embrace composable architectures, platform thinking, value streams, and AI-powered experiences, architecture’s role is becoming more strategic than ever.


## Focus On Platform Transformation


The next breakout session may be one of the most relevant for architecture and infrastructure leaders.


- Reimagining I&O: From Infrastructure Operator To Capability Platform


Infrastructure teams are evolving beyond traditional operational responsibilities. Increasingly, they are expected to provide reusable capabilities that help teams deliver value faster and more consistently.


This session explores how infrastructure organizations can support agility, scalability, and business outcomes while remaining reliable and secure.


If data readiness is a major priority for your organization, consider extending the morning with:


- Build A Plan For AI-Ready Data


Many of the most ambitious AI initiatives are limited by fragmented data, inconsistent quality, and weak governance.


This workshop focuses on creating the foundation necessary to support AI at scale, making it particularly valuable for leaders responsible for architecture and platform strategy.


## Design For Scale Rather Than Experiments


Tuesday morning’s agenda also provides the opportunity to shift the insights toward execution.


- The Cognitive Operating Model


Organizations are beginning to rethink how people, processes, technology, and AI work together. This keynote explores how operating models may evolve as AI becomes more deeply embedded across the enterprise.


For architecture and delivery leaders, this provides valuable context for many of the decisions they will be making over the next several years.


**Later on Tuesday, check out:**


- Design For Capabilities: The New Infrastructure Foundation For AI


This may be one of the most important sessions on the agenda for architecture leaders.


Many organizations are moving away from treating infrastructure as a collection of technical assets and toward viewing it as a set of business-enabling capabilities. For leaders focused on platform modernization, resilience, scalability, and long-term agility, this session is likely to provide some of the most practical guidance available at the event.


## **Learn From Organizations Doing The Work**


Toward the end of the day, the focus shifts from architecture strategy to operational execution.


- Cloud And Infrastructure: Lessons From The Front Lines Of Modern IT


Architecture and delivery leaders constantly face the tension between speed and stability: The business wants faster delivery, while teams must maintain security, quality, and reliability. Finding the right balance has become one of the defining challenges of modern technology organizations. This session promises practical lessons from organizations navigating those realities today.


## **Close With What Comes Next**


Tuesday afternoon provides the opportunity to close the Forum with two forward-looking keynotes:


- The Future Of Software: From Packaged Apps To Generated Business Capabilities At Scale
- AI Governance For The GOOOOOAL!


Together, these sessions explore how software delivery, governance, and technology operating models are evolving as AI capabilities continue to mature. For architecture and delivery leaders, these topics are no longer future concerns. They are becoming active planning priorities.


## **Don’t Underestimate The Value Of Peer Conversations**


Some of the best insights at Technology & Innovation Forum Central will come from conversations between sessions. Use the networking breaks, lunch, welcome reception, and closing reception to compare modernization approaches, discuss delivery challenges, validate assumptions, and learn how peers are approaching similar problems. Whether you’re tackling technical debt, platform modernization, operating model transformation, or AI adoption, chances are you’ll find someone facing the same challenge from a different angle.


## **What To Do Next**


To get the most from Technology & Innovation Forum Central:


- Prioritize architecture and infrastructure sessions aligned to your biggest challenges.
- Balance strategic conversations with practical workshops.
- Focus on platform modernization, delivery effectiveness, and operating model evolution.
- Spend time networking with peers facing similar transformation efforts.


The most successful architecture and delivery leaders won’t leave Austin with unanswered questions. They’ll leave with a clearer understanding of how to modernize their technology foundations, improve delivery outcomes, and build the capabilities their organizations will need to thrive in an AI-driven future.


Ready to put these insights into action? Register for[Technology & Innovation Forum Central](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/) and join fellow architecture and delivery leaders in Austin this September.
