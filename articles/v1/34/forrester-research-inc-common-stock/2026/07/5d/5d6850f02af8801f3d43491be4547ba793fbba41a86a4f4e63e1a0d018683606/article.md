# How Technology & Innovation Forum Central Turns Conversations Into Strategic Connections

In technology leadership, progress rarely comes from strategy alone. It comes from conversation — comparing approaches with peers, sharing lessons learned, and discussing how other organizations are navigating the accelerating impact of AI on technology, operations, and business transformation.


That’s why networking at[Technology & Innovation Forum Central](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/) is not treated as a side benefit. It’s built into the event experience, creating multiple opportunities for technology leaders to connect with peers, analysts, and industry experts around the challenge of taking control of AI. Taking place in Austin on September 14–15, 2026, the Forum turns moments into opportunities for lasting connections.


## Build Connections From The Very Start


[Technology & Innovation Forum Central](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/) starts fostering meaningful conversations from the moment attendees arrive. On Monday afternoon, attendees can[participate in certification sessions](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/agenda/?filter=51904,51905) focused on high-performance IT strategy and data and AI readiness, gaining opportunities to work alongside peers tackling similar technology transformation priorities.[Deep-dive sessions](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/agenda/?filter=51956) focused on operationalizing AI strategies and evolving operating models provide additional opportunities for meaningful discussion.


Leaders participating in the[Forrester Women’s Leadership Program](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/special-programs/#womens) can begin making connections even earlier, joining peers for focused discussion and relationship building before the main conference programming begins.


For prospective attendees, this early momentum matters. Day-one conversations set the stage for more meaningful learning alongside peers who share similar challenges.


## Structured Experiences Create Natural Opportunities To Connect


Some of the most valuable networking happens when participants are actively solving challenges together.[Technology & Innovation Forum Central](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/) includes[hands-on workshops](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/agenda/?filter=51735) on topics such as building an AI workplace strategy, creating technical foundations for business outcomes in the AI era, developing AI-ready data plans, and driving AI literacy and fluency throughout the organization.


These collaborative learning environments naturally bring together leaders working through similar initiatives. Rather than relying on chance encounters, attendees engage in discussions with peers who are confronting many of the same questions around AI adoption, data readiness, IT operating models, and technology strategy.


## Networking Is Woven Throughout The Agenda


The event intentionally includes multiple touchpoints for networking. Attendees can connect during[dedicated coffee and networking breaks](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/agenda/?filter=51733) on both days, providing time to discuss keynote insights, compare experiences, and continue conversations sparked during breakout sessions.


The agenda also includes a networking lunch, giving attendees dedicated time to exchange ideas and expand their professional networks among fellow technology leaders. These recurring opportunities are important, because meaningful professional relationships often develop through multiple interactions rather than a single conversation.


## Analyst-Led Roundtables Bring Purpose To Networking


For attendees seeking more focused peer engagement, the Forum offers[analyst-led roundtables](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/agenda/?filter=51803) designed specifically for discussion and collaboration. Roundtables include industry-focused conversations for government, insurance, banking, and healthcare leaders, allowing participants to connect with peers facing similar sector-specific challenges.


Additional roundtable discussions explore topics such as “ *Architecting The Autonomous Enterprise: The Questions We Need To Be Asking* ,” creating opportunities for attendees to exchange perspectives with both analysts and fellow technology executives. These sessions help transform networking from casual introductions into productive conversations centered on shared business priorities.


## Executive And Leadership Communities Foster Deeper Engagement


[Technology & Innovation Forum Central](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/) recognizes that some of the most valuable connections happen among professionals with shared responsibilities and career aspirations. The agenda includes the invitation-only[Executive Leadership Exchange Program](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/special-programs/#ele) , where a like-minded cohort can connect with peers to discuss operationalizing AI and other strategic leadership goals. Facilitated networking sessions enable attendees to benchmark strategies, exchange best practices, and explore the challenges pressuring technology leaders.


The event also features[Forrester’s new Future Leaders Program](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/special-programs/#futureleaders) , designed for emerging technology professionals nominated by senior leaders within their organizations. In addition to specialized content, the program includes dedicated networking opportunities that allow participants to build relationships with peers and gain exposure to Forrester frameworks early in their careers. These community-based experiences create environments that encourage meaningful discussion, stronger professional relationships, and valuable knowledge sharing among both current and future technology leaders.


## Receptions Turn Conversations Into Lasting Relationships


Networking continues beyond the formal agenda through receptions designed to help attendees deepen the connections they make during the day. The[welcome reception on Monday evening](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/agenda/?filter=51729) provides the first opportunity for attendees to gather as a community, while Tuesday’s closing reception offers a final chance to reconnect with peers, analysts, and industry experts before departing. These experiences help transform introductions made during sessions, workshops, and roundtables into lasting professional relationships.


## The Real Networking ROI For Technology Leaders


[Technology & Innovation Forum Central](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/) brings together leaders responsible for technology strategy, architecture, infrastructure, operations, data, and AI transformation. The networking experience is intentionally designed to reflect those priorities.


By attending, technology leaders gain access to:


- Peers tackling many of the same AI, data, and technology transformation challenges.
- Analyst-led discussions that encourage practical knowledge sharing and collaboration.
- Multiple opportunities to reconnect throughout the event through certifications, workshops, networking breaks, lunches, roundtables, and receptions.


The result is an experience where relationships develop through shared learning and ongoing conversation, not simply exchanged business cards.


For technology leaders evaluating whether to attend[Technology & Innovation Forum Central](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/) , look beyond the session list. From certifications and workshops to analyst-led roundtables, networking lunches, leadership communities, and evening receptions, networking is woven throughout every phase of the event experience.
