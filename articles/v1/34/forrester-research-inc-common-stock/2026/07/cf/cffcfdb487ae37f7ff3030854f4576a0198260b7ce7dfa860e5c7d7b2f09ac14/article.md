# Five Key Findings From The Forrester Wave™: Workday Services, Q2 2026

The Workday services market has entered a new phase. Buyers are no longer looking solely for implementation partners that can deliver projects on time and on budget. Increasingly, they want strategic partners that can help them drive continuous transformation, operationalize AI, and maximize platform value long after go-live. In[The Forrester Wave™ Workday Services, Q2 2026](https://www.forrester.com/report/the-forrester-wave-tm-workday-services-q2-2026/RES196449) , we evaluated 14 of the most significant Workday services providers to help organizations identify the right partners for their needs.


Here are five themes that stood out during our research:


1. **Workday services has become an AI-led transformation market**


The strongest providers are positioning Workday as a platform for enterprise transformation rather than simply an HR or finance system. Leaders increasingly differentiate themselves through AI-enabled consulting, proprietary accelerators, industry-specific AI solutions, agent-based automation, and innovation programs aligned to Workday’s product roadmap.


1. **Continuous value has replaced the traditional implementation mindset**


Workday customers are looking beyond deployment. Leading providers are expanding their focus to optimization, adoption, release management, managed services, and ongoing business transformation. Many firms now emphasize long-term value realization and continuous improvement as core elements of their strategy.


1. **Industry expertise is becoming a key differentiator**


As Workday expands deeper into finance, planning, payroll, workforce management, and industry-specific use cases, buyers increasingly value providers that bring deep industry knowledge. Healthcare, financial services, public sector, retail, and utilities customers often seek specialized operating models, regulatory expertise, and prebuilt industry solutions that accelerate time to value.


1. **Talent and delivery models matter more than ever**


The most successful providers have invested heavily in Workday-certified talent, global delivery capabilities, governance frameworks, and quality controls. In an increasingly competitive labor market, the ability to attract, develop, and retain Workday expertise remains one of the strongest predictors of execution success.


1. **Boutique firms continue to play an important role**


While global systems integrators dominate large-scale enterprise transformations, specialized boutique partners remain highly relevant, particularly for midmarket organizations and customers seeking deep functional expertise. Many boutiques compete effectively through governance, customer intimacy, innovation, and specialized Workday capabilities. This trend first emerged in our earlier evaluation and continues to shape the market today.


**What This Means For Buyers**


Selecting a Workday partner is no longer simply about implementation capacity. Organizations should evaluate providers based on their ability to support business transformation, leverage AI responsibly, deliver industry-specific expertise, and provide a roadmap for continuous value realization. The best partner for a global finance transformation may not be the same provider best suited for a midmarket human capital management deployment or a highly regulated industry environment.


As Workday expands its footprint across HR, finance, planning, payroll, and AI-enabled workflows, the distinction between technology implementation and business transformation continues to blur. Buyers that align partner selection with their long-term transformation objectives will be best positioned to realize the full value of their Workday investment.
