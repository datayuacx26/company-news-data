# The Real Promise Of Synthetic Personas Is Better Activation

For years, B2B marketers have invested heavily in buyer personas, interviewing customers, commissioning research, synthesizing insights, documenting motivations, and mapping buying roles. Yet too often, those valuable insights end up buried in PDFs, slide decks, or shared drives, referenced occasionally but rarely used to shape day-to-day decisions.


Traditional buyer personas remain essential. They serve as the compass that helps organizations understand who their buyers are and what matters to them. But a compass alone doesn’t help teams navigate every turn of an increasingly complex go-to-market journey. In today’s AI-powered marketing environment, organizations need a more scalable way to put audience knowledge to work.


That’s where synthetic personas come in. Forrester defines synthetic personas as digital representations of specific job roles that simulate buyer attitudes, motivations, and behaviors to support AI workflows. Rather than replacing traditional buyer personas, they extend their value by making audience understanding available in a format that AI systems can use across workflows.


### **From Compass To GPS**


If traditional buyer personas are the compass, synthetic personas are the GPS. They help marketing, product, customer success, and sales teams apply buyer insight in the moments when decisions are being made.


The real power of synthetic personas is not faster persona creation. It’s better persona activation.


Imagine a portfolio marketing team preparing to launch a new solution. Instead of relying solely on internal debate and limited feedback cycles, marketers can use synthetic buyer personas to test positioning, surface likely objections, and refine messaging before it reaches the market. Teams can pressure-test value propositions, explore alternative approaches, and identify opportunities for personalization earlier in the process.


The benefits extend far beyond launch planning. Synthetic personas can help marketers improve audience targeting, create more relevant content, support sales enablement, and simulate likely buyer responses before campaigns go live. By embedding audience insight directly into AI-enabled workflows, organizations can move from occasional persona reviews to continuous audience-informed decision-making. Synthetic personas help organizations scale insight across marketing, sales, and product workflows faster and more efficiently than ever before.


### **Better Directions Require Better Data**


Of course, even the best GPS is only as reliable as the data behind it.


Organizations cannot simply ask generative AI to create buyer personas from a prompt and expect meaningful results. Effective synthetic personas must be grounded in proprietary data, primary research, validated customer insights, and organizational context. Without those foundations, teams risk creating generic profiles that appear useful but fail to reflect the complexity of real buyers and opportunities to differentiate your offering. Effective synthetic personas are grounded in research and contextual data, not AI-generated shortcuts.


Strong governance requires human oversight, centralized ownership, and clear standards for how synthetic personas are developed and applied. AI can help teams execute faster, but strategy should remain grounded in research, expertise, and human judgment.


### **Turning Insight Into Action**


Traditional buyer personas are still essential as the compass that provides direction. But synthetic personas add the GPS layer that helps teams navigate situations in real time, from launch planning, messaging development, content creation, campaign execution, or sales readiness, with greater confidence and consistency. Together, they transform persona research from a static resource into a dynamic decision engine that helps teams reduce risk, move faster, and stay relentlessly focused on their customers.
