Technology leaders are under pressure to deliver on multiple fronts at once: Scale AI initiatives, modernize technology operations, optimize spending, develop talent, and prove business value. The most effective leaders don’t solve those challenges alone. They learn from peers, test ideas against proven frameworks, and invest in capabilities that create lasting impact. That’s where the[special programs at Technology & Innovation Forum Central](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/) can help.


Designed for technology, AI, and data leaders at every stage of their careers, these exclusive programs provide direct access to peers, proven frameworks, certification opportunities, and leadership development experiences that extend far beyond the mainstage. If you’re looking to maximize your Forum experience and leave with actionable strategies you can apply immediately, start here.


## Connect With Executive Peers Facing The Same Challenges


The most valuable insights often come from conversations with leaders tackling the same issues you are. The[Executive Leadership Exchange (ELE)](https://www.forrester.com/event/technology-innovation-central/special-programs/#ele) is an invitation-only experience for qualified technology and data executives seeking candid discussions, meaningful networking, and practical perspectives on the issues shaping leadership agendas today.


Participants gain access to exclusive executive programming, including an “Ask Me Anything” session with Forrester CTO Michael Facemire. Learn how Forrester has operationalized AI across the business, hear lessons learned, and engage in open discussions about turning AI investments into measurable value. The program also includes executive networking opportunities, a private luncheon, and leadership-focused conversations with peers navigating similar transformation challenges.


Best for:


- CIOs, CTOs, and technology executives
- Data and technology C-suite leaders
- Senior leaders driving transformation and AI strategy
