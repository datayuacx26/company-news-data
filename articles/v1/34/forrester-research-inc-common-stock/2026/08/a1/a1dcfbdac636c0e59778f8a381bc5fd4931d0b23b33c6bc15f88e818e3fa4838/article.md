# Forrester’s 2026 Technology Award Winners For APAC

Technology leaders across Asia Pacific are under pressure to deliver more than resilient systems and efficient operations. They are expected to help their organizations grow, adapt, earn trust, and turn emerging technologies, especially AI, into measurable business value. Forrester’s Technology Awards recognize organizations that demonstrate how technology can become a true business catalyst. This year, Forrester is recognizing winners across three award categories: **Technology Strategy Impact** , **Enterprise Architecture** , and **Data and AI Impact** .


The


**Technology Strategy Impact Award** **** recognizes IT organizations that drive measurable business results through technology strategy. The award evaluates how IT leaders align technology investments with business goals and deliver outcomes that matter, using Forrester’s High-Performance IT lens of alignment, trust, and adaptivity.


The


**Enterprise Architecture Award** recognizes organizations whose enterprise architecture practices help the business make better technology decisions, strengthen strategic alignment, and create sustained business value. Winning teams


demonstrate


that enterprise architecture is not just a governance


function


,


it is a strategic capability that connects business outcomes, technology choices, operating models, and future-state transformation.


The


**Data and AI Impact Award** recognizes organizations that use data, analytics, and AI to deliver measurable business, customer, operational, or innovation outcomes at scale. Winning organizations show how data and AI capabilities can move beyond experimentation to create trusted, repeatable, and differentiated impact across the enterprise.


Congratulations to the 2026 APAC winners:


- **Technology Strategy Impact:** Singtel


- **Enterprise Architecture:** CLP Power Hong Kong and Nan Shan Life Insurance Co., Ltd.


- **Data and AI Impact:** DBS Bank and Grab


Each winner demonstrates a different dimension of technology leadership, from high-performance IT strategy to outcome-focused architecture and scaled data and AI impact.


## Technology Strategy Impact: Singtel


Singtel wins the 2026 Technology Strategy Impact Award for APAC for its multi-year IT transformation,


Xcelerate


, which turned IT from a traditional support function into an AI-native capability that drives the business.


Recognising


AI as a catalyst for business transformation, Singtel aligned technology with enterprise priorities by


modernising


its digital core, building a unified data and AI foundation, and embedding AI across engineering, operations, and customer experiences.


Xcelerate


reduced the application landscape by 44%, lowered technology obsolescence to below 10%, and created reinvestment capacity by redirecting traditional IT spend into AI, digital platforms, and next-generation technologies. The transformation also strengthened system resilience,


maintained


service availability above 99%, and accelerated product launches from weeks to days. Customer experience improved through best-in-class NPS, simpler digital


journeys


and AI-enabled customer engagement. Singtel’s transformation shows how a technology organization can build trusted, adaptive, and strategically aligned capabilities that enable growth, resilience, and competitive differentiation in an AI-driven market.


## Enterprise Architecture: CLP Power Hong Kong


CLP Power Hong Kong won the 2026 Enterprise Architecture Award for transforming enterprise architecture from a traditional governance function into an outcome-driven capability that orchestrates business transformation at scale. As CLP simultaneously advanced major Customer Experience, Smart Grid, and ERP programs while


maintaining


99.999% electricity reliability for approximately 2.9 million customer accounts, its architecture team played a pivotal role in aligning strategy, investment priorities, technology decisions, and digital delivery across the enterprise. Through shared platforms, reusable architecture patterns, and architects embedded within transformation programs, CLP delivered measurable business outcomes, including a 20% increase in digital adoption across mobile app and web channels, reaching approximately 1.5 million customer accounts; a 90% reduction in data latency, enabling near real-time decision-making; increased operational efficiency by 30% in ERP processes; and improved technology investment efficiency by 15% across major transformation initiatives. CLP’s approach


demonstrates


how enterprise architecture can evolve from a governance function into a strategic driver of enterprise-wide transformation and business value.

achieved

The judges were particularly impressed by Nan Shan’s ability to combine business architecture, reusable technology assets, and outcome-focused governance to create measurable improvements in revenue growth, operational efficiency, customer experience, and risk management

## Data


&


AI Impact: Grab


Grab wins the 2026 Data & AI Impact Award for developing an internal-facing, self-service platform to help business and corporate employees build and run their own AI-driven automations. Grab’s central AI team, Grab Technology Solutions (GTS), developed Valet, a governed agentic workspace that empowers business functions to build and own agents and automation workflows. It empowers business teams to own process logic and outcomes, while GTS enables them to deliver better outcomes through governing platform, security, and production controls. Process redesign precedes automation, with human approval required for write actions and high-risk operations. The ongoing human-in-loop model builds trust and ensures effective oversight of the agents. Early results demonstrate measurable improvements in workflow speed and functional capacity.
