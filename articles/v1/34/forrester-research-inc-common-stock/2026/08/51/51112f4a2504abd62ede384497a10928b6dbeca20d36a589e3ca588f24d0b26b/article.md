# Your Processes Are The Weakest Part Of Your AEO Strategy

Many marketers now know the[best practices](https://www.forrester.com/report/best-practices-for-answer-engine-optimization-aeo/RES188745?ref_search=3520107_1785462092485) they must implement to get mentioned and cited by ChatGPT, Google, and Claude but still struggle to implement answer engine optimization (AEO). Marketers sync with web developers about the technical prerequisites of visibility: server-side rendering, Bing’s IndexNow protocol, monitoring for indexation issues, and more. They’re well aware of the weaknesses of SEO’s KPIs and are beginning to benchmark new KPIs like citation attempt and recommendation share. However, despite internalizing best practices, most marketers haven’t mastered the cross-functional collaboration AEO requires.


## AEO Works When Every Team Reinforces Authority


AEO is a marketing discipline and organizational imperative. Copywriters can create deeply distinctive, quotable content, but if customers are badmouthing a brand on Reddit and the brand’s site promotes products rather than answers FAQs, that content won’t be cited in ChatGPT.


Web developers can ensure pages load in less than three seconds, are responsive on mobile, and have schema markup but, if the content on those pages doesn’t convey the trust signals answer engines reward, they won’t be included in AI Mode’s answers.


AEO is a fragile, sprawling process that must be greater than the sum of its parts. It requires coordinating content, IT, social media, PR, paid media, brand marketing, information security, and legal to monitor and augment authority across engines.
