# Marti Technologies, Inc. Files Annual Report on Form 20-F for Fiscal Year 2025

## Marti Technologies, Inc. Files Annual Report on Form 20-F for Fiscal Year 2025


April 15, 2026 4:15pm EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_10b1b65ce713776670ba3f356bbfdb30/marti/news/2026-04-15_Marti_Technologies_Inc_Files_Annual_Report_on_106.pdf)


ISTANBUL--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Marti Technologies, Inc. (“Marti” or the “Company”) (NYSE American: MRT), Türkiye’s leading mobility super app, announced that it has filed its annual report on Form 20-F, including its financial results, for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 (the “2025 Annual Report”) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. The 2025 Annual Report can be accessed under the “Financial Info” section of the Company’s investor relations website at[https://ir.marti.tech/](https://ir.marti.tech/) .


In addition, Marti shareholders may receive, free of charge, a hard copy of the Company’s complete financial statements by requesting a copy from the contact below.


**About Marti:**


Founded in 2018, Marti is Türkiye’s leading mobility app, offering a wide variety of transportation services. Marti operates a ride-hailing service that matches riders with car, motorcycle and taxi drivers; offers delivery services; and operates a large fleet of rental e-mopeds, e-bikes, and e-scooters. All of Marti’s offerings are serviced by proprietary software systems and IoT infrastructure. For more information, visit[www.marti.tech](http://www.marti.tech/) .
