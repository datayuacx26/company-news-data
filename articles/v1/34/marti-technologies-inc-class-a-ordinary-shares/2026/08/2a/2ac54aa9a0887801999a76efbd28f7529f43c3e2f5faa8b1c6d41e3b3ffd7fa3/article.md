# Marti Technologies, Inc. to Report 2026 Second Quarter Results on August 19, 2026

## Marti Technologies, Inc. to Report 2026 Second Quarter Results on August 19, 2026


August 17, 2026 6:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_c96eae98c54b11e62e9cd005b3a970d7/marti/news/2026-08-17_Marti_Technologies_Inc_to_Report_2026_Second_115.pdf)


ISTANBUL--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Marti Technologies, Inc. (“Marti”) (NYSE American: MRT), Türkiye’s leading mobility super app, will announce its financial and operational results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 (“2026 Second Quarter Results”) before the U.S. markets open on Wednesday, August 19, 2026.


**Conference Call and Webcast Details**


Marti’s management will host an analyst and investor conference call and live webcast to discuss its 2026 Second Quarter Results at 3:30 p.m. Istanbul / 1:30 p.m. London / 8:30 a.m. New York time on Wednesday, August 19, 2026.


**Live webcast** can be accessed via:


[https://event.choruscall.com/mediaframe/webcast.html?webcastid=Hrk0obuY](https://event.choruscall.com/mediaframe/webcast.html?webcastid=Hrk0obuY)


**Participant Dial-In:** +1 877-485-3103 / +1 201-689-8890


A replay will be available on the Marti Investor Relations website[https://ir.marti.tech/](https://ir.marti.tech/) following the call.


**Marti’s 2026 Second Quarter Results presentation** will be available on the Marti Investor Relations website[https://ir.marti.tech/](https://ir.marti.tech/) on August 19, 2026.


**About Marti:**


Founded in 2018, Marti is Türkiye’s leading mobility app, offering a wide variety of transportation services. Marti operates a ride-hailing service that matches riders with car, motorcycle, and taxi drivers; offers delivery services; and operates a large fleet of rental e-mopeds, e-bikes, and e-scooters. All of Marti’s offerings are serviced by proprietary software systems and IoT infrastructure. For more information, visit[www.marti.tech](http://www.marti.tech/) .
