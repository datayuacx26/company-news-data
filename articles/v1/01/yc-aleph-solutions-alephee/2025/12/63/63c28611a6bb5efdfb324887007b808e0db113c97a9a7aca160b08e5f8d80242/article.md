# Alephee and Stellantis together in the future of automotive e-commerce

Alephee participated in the Business Partner Day, an event promoted by Stellantis that brought together clients, suppliers, and strategic partners with the goal of strengthening ties and collaboratively driving the digital transformation of the automotive sector.


The event, which has already established itself as one of the main points of connection between the automaker and its partners, was a space for knowledge exchange, innovation, and inspiration — reaffirming Alephee's role as a key player in the evolution of automotive e-commerce in Latin America.


###


Connection with the transforming automotive sector


Under the **“Value”** pillar of Stellantis' global **Dare Forward 2030** strategy — which prioritizes digitalization, entrepreneurial spirit, and regional roots — the company announced that **one third of global sales is expected to occur through online channels by 2030** .


In this context, Business Partner Day consolidated itself as an essential space to present new paths, share best practices, and strengthen ties between the automaker, distributors, and technology providers like Alephee, which has been supporting the automotive sector in digitizing its operations for years.


###


### Inspiration, dialogue, and new opportunities


During the two-day event, Alephee's team participated in an intensive agenda of learning and networking.


The lectures covered the challenges and opportunities of digitalization in the sector, emphasizing how e-commerce is now an indispensable part of the automotive chain.


The team was also present at the **Business Roundtable** , presenting solutions already operating in the network that enable infrastructure, integration, and scalability for dealerships and distributors.


The event also served to strengthen connections with major partners who share the same mission: **driving a more inclusive, efficient, and connected automotive e-commerce.**


According to **Marcela Arantes Ribeiro** , Customer Success Manager at Alephee Brazil:


> *“We were energized by the event and are thrilled to be part of this moment of inspiration and showcasing the many opportunities for network expansion. We are committed and excited about our partnership, which provides the necessary structure for business scalability!”*


###


Why is this event strategic for Alephee?


- **Strengthening partnerships**


Being alongside a global automaker like Stellantis reinforces Alephee's credibility and role as a strategic partner in the digitization of the sector.


- **Visibility in the automotive ecosystem**


The event was an opportunity to present solutions and tangible results, showing Alephee as a company that not only follows changes but accelerates the digital transformation of the market.


- **Integration and collaboration**


Direct contact with clients and partners allowed for identifying new synergies and opportunities to expand the reach of Alephee's solutions in Latin America.


### Looking to the future


With strengthened connections and new collaboration fronts on the horizon, Alephee leaves Business Partner Day with a renewed commitment to continue driving digitalization and scalability in the automotive business.


More than just a networking event, the meeting was a milestone for the sector and proof that the evolution of automotive e-commerce is just beginning.
