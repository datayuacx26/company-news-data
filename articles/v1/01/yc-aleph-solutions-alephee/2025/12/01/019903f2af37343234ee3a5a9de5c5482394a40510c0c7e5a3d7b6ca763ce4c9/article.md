# Stellantis Parts & Services Convention brings together partners and dealerships

The **Alephee** team had the opportunity to participate in the **Stellantis Parts & Services Commercial Convention 2025** , the last major event of the year promoted by Stellantis, held at the Royal Palm Plaza in Campinas (SP).


The event brought together dealerships, partners, and strategic suppliers in an intense schedule focused on relationship-building, training, and business generation. Over the two days, participants had access to lectures on sales and performance, technical workshops, business rounds, and a practical immersion in e-commerce—including a standout innovation: the E-commerce Game, a gamified experience for hands-on learning about the digital environment.


### Strengthening relationships with clients and partners


For Alephee, participating in the event was a strategic moment to present its solutions and services, strengthen relationships with clients and partners who were with us throughout the year, and especially, listen closely to the dealerships.


The convention also opened space for relevant conversations about the main challenges faced by companies when it comes to digitalizing operations, especially in the context of digital platforms and automotive e-commerce.


### Looking toward the future of digital operations


In addition to celebrating the year’s results, the event was an opportunity to begin outlining the next steps. Conversations with dealerships made it possible to understand specific needs, operational challenges, and expectations for the coming years—essential insights for building increasingly efficient solutions.


### According to Jônatas Lenotti, dedicated account manager for Stellantis at Alephee:
*“These events are crucial for us to stay close to the dealership network and the brands, aligning strategy and needs. This direct contact with each client and partner is extremely important, as it gives us the opportunity to redirect our next steps toward exponential and consistent growth—refining, together with this successful partnership between Alephee, Stellantis, and the sales channels!”*

Commitment to the evolution of the market


Alephee’s presence at the Stellantis Parts & Services Commercial Convention reinforces its commitment to acting as a strategic partner—connecting technology, market knowledge, and close relationships with clients to drive the digital evolution of the automotive sector.
