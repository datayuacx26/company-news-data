# Research Agent

# The rep spent 40 minutes on Google before the call. Every call.


Here's what a Tuesday afternoon used to look like for one of our early customers.


3:12pm. A demo is scheduled for 3:30. The AE pulls up the lead in HubSpot. Company name. Email. Not much else.


They open a new tab. LinkedIn, check title, headcount, recent posts.


Another tab, the company's website, About page, blog, jobs board.


Crunchbase, funding, investors, when they raised.


Another tab, their competitors. G2 for any product mentions.


18 minutes in, they've got a rough picture. 22 minutes later, they're on the call, half-prepared, going in with a script that doesn't actually fit.


Multiply that by 20 calls a week. That's most of a working day. Per rep. Per week.


---


## What the Research Agent does


You point Surface at a lead. It goes and does what your AE would do, except it does it in seconds, at every lead, whether or not a meeting is booked.


For every lead in your Lead Data Platform, Surface adds a new column: **Company Research** . Structured, current, deep. Recent funding, hiring signals, tech stack, competitor context, product mentions, buying triggers.


It's the difference between walking into a demo with a script and walking in with intelligence.


## The 5 hours a week claim


We didn't make that number up. It's what our early customers hit consistently, usually more.


The savings come from three places:


- **Pre-call prep** disappears. The rep opens Surface and it's done.
- **Cold outbound** gets sharper. Reps can send a message that references something specific because Surface already surfaced it.
- **Handoffs** stop losing context. When an SDR passes a lead to an AE, the research is already in the record.


## What it's *not*


It's not a data enrichment tool. Clearbit and Apollo already do that. The Research Agent is doing what a *human* would do with those data points, reading, synthesizing, deciding what matters for this specific lead in your specific ICP.


It's not a chatbot. You don't ask it questions. It just fills in the column, and it's ready before your rep even opens the record.


## Where it fits in the platform


The Research Agent runs on top of the Lead Data Platform. Every lead that enters Surface, via form, ad landing page, enrichment sync, or CSV upload, passes through it. The output flows into scoring, into routing, into content agents, into your CRM.


Because it's on the shared context layer, the research doesn't die inside one tool. It's available everywhere.
