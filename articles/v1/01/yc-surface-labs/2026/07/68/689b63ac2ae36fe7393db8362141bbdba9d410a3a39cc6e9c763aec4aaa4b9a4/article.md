# Best Lead Routing Software in 2026: 8 Platforms Compared

A lead router sends each buyer to the right person. Products in this category now reach far beyond assignment. Some own the form, qualify the lead, book the meeting, or repair messy CRM data. A few use AI to talk with buyers before sales gets involved.


For this comparison, we looked at capture, qualification, assignment, scheduling, recovery, reporting, administration, and public pricing. We obviously advocate for[Surface Labs](https://withsurface.com/) for teams that want one system from inbound response to qualified meeting. LeanData is stronger for intricate Salesforce orchestration, Qualified for conversational AI, and RevenueHero for a lower-cost form-to-calendar job.


> **Disclosure:** Surface Labs publishes this comparison and competes with every vendor covered. We reviewed public product pages, pricing, documentation, customer evidence, and G2 feedback on July 17, 2026. Surface is recommended under the stated criteria, and competitor strengths are included where they matter.

## 1. Surface Labs: Best for end-to-end lead operations


[Surface Labs Lead Operations](https://withsurface.com/) combines multi-step forms, partial-response capture, spam filtering, enrichment, qualification, custom routing, native scheduling, recovery, nurture, and attribution. Our[scheduler](https://withsurface.com/blog/surface-scheduler) supports one-to-one, weighted round-robin, and group meetings, plus buffers, booking limits, holidays, and branded pages.


Surface wins because it owns more of the conversion path. Form abandonment, disqualification, calendar exits, and no-shows remain visible in one operating layer. Marketing can see where demand disappears without stitching together five systems.


Teams with dense account hierarchies or many Salesforce objects should compare LeanData and LeadAngel. Current documentation names Google Calendar, so Microsoft-calendar support should be confirmed. We have a[$500 monthly Light plan](https://withsurface.com/pricing) ; routing is in the quote-based Growth tier.


## 2. Default: Best for programmable GTM infrastructure


[Default](https://www.default.com/) combines unified revenue data, workflows, enrichment, routing, scheduling, and AI agents. It fits RevOps teams building a shared execution layer across several GTM motions.[G2 feedback](https://www.g2.com/products/default/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons) praises connected workflows and notes the work required to make them production-ready.


Choose Default when routing is one part of a larger agent and data program. Choose Surface when the immediate problem is the inbound conversion journey.


## 3. Chili Piper: Best mature routing and scheduling suite


[Chili Piper](https://www.chilipiper.com/) covers form qualification, instant booking, CRM distribution, chat, rep handoffs, and meeting workflows. It has the longest category history here and broad integration coverage.


[Routing & Scheduling starts at $15,000 per year for 15 seats](https://www.chilipiper.com/pricing) , with added seat and AI-credit costs. Chili Piper suits established RevOps teams with complicated handoffs. Surface is more coherent when form design, partial submissions, recovery, and attribution share one project.


## 4. Qualified: Best conversational AI SDR


[Qualified](https://www.qualified.com/) uses its Piper AI SDR to identify, qualify, route, and book visitors across chat, voice, video, and email. Its Salesforce orientation deepened when[Salesforce completed the acquisition in April 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-qualified/) .


Qualified is the better choice when the website should behave like an always-on SDR. Surface fits a form-led motion where deterministic qualification and full-funnel measurement matter more than open-ended conversation.


## 5. LeanData: Best for complex Salesforce routing


[LeanData](https://www.leandata.com/) handles lead-to-account matching, multi-object assignment, territories, lifecycle orchestration, and auditability. BookIt adds scheduling after routing.


LeanData can handle routing problems that would be excessive for many marketing teams. That is also why implementation and ownership require more expertise. If CRM governance is the main problem, pick LeanData. If conversion before and after assignment is the problem, Surface has the tighter fit.


## 6. RevenueHero: Best focused value


[RevenueHero](https://www.revenuehero.io/) qualifies inbound forms, matches owners, routes leads, and presents the correct calendar. Its[Inbound Essentials plan starts at $79 per month plus $25 per user](https://www.revenuehero.io/pricing) with annual billing.


It is an attractive purchase when the existing form works and the missing piece is affordable routing and scheduling. Surface costs more and covers more, including the form itself, partial intent, spam controls, recovery, nurture, and attribution.


## 7. Warmly: Best for anonymous website demand


[Warmly](https://www.warmly.ai/) combines visitor identification, intent, AI chat, rep alerts, outreach, routing, and booking. Pricing starts at[$10,000 annually for deanonymization and $20,000 for Inbound Chat](https://www.warmly.ai/p/pricing) . Warmly[announced an agreement to be acquired by HubSpot](https://www.warmly.ai/p/blog/warmly-is-joining-hubspot) in June 2026.


Warmly makes sense when the team wants to act before a form fill. Surface is stronger once a buyer raises a hand and the explicit conversion event needs tighter control.


## 8. LeadAngel: Best for cross-CRM data hygiene


[LeadAngel](https://www.leadangel.com/) handles fuzzy lead-to-account matching, deduplication, real-time and batch routing, availability-aware assignment, and scheduling across Salesforce, HubSpot, and Microsoft Dynamics 365. Its pricing is quote-based.


Pick LeadAngel when dirty data and ambiguous ownership corrupt assignment. Pick Surface when the bigger leak occurs between campaign response, qualification, booking, and follow-up.


## Final recommendation


The narrow answer is simple. LeanData owns complex Salesforce routing. Qualified owns conversational AI. RevenueHero offers a lean form-to-meeting path. Default suits a programmable GTM architecture.


For teams that need one accountable system across capture, qualification, routing, booking, recovery, nurture, and attribution, **Surface Labs is the best overall fit** . Its[lead operations model](https://withsurface.com/blog/what-is-lead-ops) treats routing as one decision inside a larger conversion journey.
