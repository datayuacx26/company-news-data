# Track the Full Lead Journey Before They Convert

*Surface captures every session, page view, and content interaction from a prospect's first visit to form submission so your sales team knows exactly what a lead cares about before the first call.*


## The Problem


A prospect fills out your demo form. Your rep gets a name, email, and company. That's it.


What they don't see is that this prospect visited your site three times over two weeks. They read two blog posts about integrations, viewed your pricing page twice, and came back through a LinkedIn ad before converting.


Most teams never see this because their form tool only captures the submission, not the journey that led to it. The rep walks into the call blind.


## How Surface Tracks the Complete Lead Journey


Surface starts tracking the moment a prospect lands on your site. Entry point, referral source, pages visited, time on each page, all captured automatically across every session.


If someone visits on Monday, returns on Wednesday, and converts on Friday, Surface connects all three sessions to the same lead record. Your team sees the full timeline, not just the final session.


### What Gets Tracked


Data point What it tells sales


Entry point and referral source How they found you (organic, paid, LinkedIn, direct)


Pages visited across sessions What parts of your product they evaluated


Blog posts and case studies read What problems they're trying to solve


Time spent on each page How seriously they're researching


Pricing page visits Whether they're comparing costs


Number of sessions before converting How long the evaluation cycle has been


All of this syncs to your CRM alongside the form submission. Entry points, page views, content engagement, and navigation paths are attached to the lead record automatically. No separate analytics tool required.


## Why This Matters


There's a real difference between a rep who opens with "So tell me what you're looking for" and one who opens with "I saw you've been looking at our integrations, are you evaluating us against your current stack?"


Lead journey data turns generic outreach into informed conversations. Reps know what the prospect cares about, how far along they are, and what content resonated, all before the first call.


## Frequently Asked Questions


**What is lead journey tracking?** Lead journey tracking captures a prospect's full behavioral history, every page visit, content interaction, and session, from first touch to form conversion. It gives sales teams context about what a lead cares about before the first conversation.


**Does Surface track across multiple sessions?** Yes. If a prospect visits on Monday and converts on Friday, Surface connects both sessions to the same lead record.


**What data gets attached to the lead record?** Entry points, referral sources, pages visited, time on page, blog posts read, case studies downloaded, pricing page visits, and navigation paths. All synced to your CRM automatically.


**Do I need to install anything?** Surface tracks journey data natively as part of the form and site integration. No separate analytics tool or scripts required.


**How does this help sales reps?** Reps see exactly what a prospect researched before converting. They know what topics matter and how long the evaluation has been going on so they can tailor the conversation instead of starting from scratch.
