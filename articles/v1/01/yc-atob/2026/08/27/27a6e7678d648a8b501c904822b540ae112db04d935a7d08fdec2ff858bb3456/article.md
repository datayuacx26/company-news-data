## **What to Look for in a Construction Fleet Fuel Card**


### **Fuel Acceptance Near Job sites, Suppliers, and Routes**


Construction crews fuel wherever the current job site demands. Closed-network fuel cards that limit purchases to specific brands force costly detours when sites shift weekly.


Fuel cards that work anywhere Mastercard is accepted provide broad network coverage for crews moving between projects, material suppliers, and equipment yards. AtoB's[business fuel cards](https://www.atob.com/company-fuel-cards) solve this problem by offering a wide network of Mastercard acceptance, eliminating the restrictions that brand-specific cards impose.


### **Spend Controls for Crews, Vehicles, and Project Budgets**


Construction companies issue fuel cards to foremen, equipment operators, and laborers. This requires controls beyond what standard[fleet fuel card programs](https://www.atob.com/blog/fleet-fuel-card-program) offer.


Essential control features include:


- **Per-transaction dollar limits -** Cap fuel spend based on vehicle type or crew role
- **Fuel-type restrictions -** Limit purchases to diesel only for heavy equipment, or allow unleaded across different[types of construction vehicles](https://www.atob.com/blog/types-of-construction-trucks) and light-duty vehicles
- **Time-of-day controls -** Prevent after-hours fuel transactions
- **Merchant category blocks -** Restrict non-fuel purchases entirely


Gallon-based limits that match vehicle tank capacity help prevent over-fueling and misuse. Features like Secure Card Unlock ensure only authorized personnel can activate fuel purchases through a driver app.

Construction job sites present elevated risk, making[construction fraud](https://www.atob.com/blog/construction-fraud) prevention a top priority for operators. Equipment sits unattended overnight, multiple crews share card use, and remote locations complicate oversight.
