# Top 5 Fleet Fuel Card Programs for Businesses

Fuel is one of the largest controllable expenses across fleet operations, yet many fleets still rely on generic payment cards that lack the controls needed to manage fuel spending, driver purchases, and operating costs.


A fleet fuel card program is built to solve exactly these challenges by combining payments, reporting, and controls into one system.


This guide explains how to evaluate the right[fleet fuel card](https://www.atob.com/#atob-for-fleets) for your business, compares leading providers, and outlines how modern platforms help fleet managers track fuel, improve fuel efficiency, and reduce overall fleet fuel expenses.


## **Why Your Fuel Card Program Can Make or Break Fleet Margins**


Small per-gallon differences compound quickly across thousands of fill-ups each year, significantly impacting overall[fleet management costs](https://www.atob.com/blog/fleet-management-costs) . ATRI's annual operational cost survey found that fuel averaged[48.1 cents per mile in 2024](https://trid.trb.org/View/2570650) 1, roughly 21% of the $2.26 total cost per mile for motor carriers.


For fleet owners managing tight operating margins, the right fuel card program can improve cost visibility, reduce fraud exposure, and support stronger trip-level profitability.


Meanwhile, standard payment tools lack the visibility needed to manage fuel spend effectively. Here's the difference:


- Business credit cards typically capture only total transaction values
- Fleet fuel cards capture detailed data, including gallons, location, and driver usage


Beyond discounts, the right fleet card program protects margins by helping to[prevent fuel card fraud](https://www.atob.com/blog/how-to-prevent-fuel-card-fraud) , enforce spend controls, and streamline accounting. These are benefits that generic payment cards never offer.


## **What to Look for in a Fleet Fuel Card Program for Businesses**


Several critical factors separate the best[fuel cards for business](https://www.atob.com/company-fuel-cards) from programs that create more problems than they solve. The right program for your fleet depends on network access, cost structure, controls, and reporting capabilities, especially when evaluating[the best fuel cards for truckers](https://www.atob.com/blog/fuel-cards-for-truckers) operating across different routes and fueling patterns.


### **Network Coverage and Acceptance**


A key consideration is where cards can be used. Some programs restrict purchases to independent gas stations or branded fuel stations, while others provide broader access.


Open-loop cards backed by Mastercard or Visa work at nearly any truck stop nationwide. Closed-loop cards restrict purchases to a specific brand.


Fuel cards are accepted at a wide range of locations, including major truck stops and independent fuel stations, helping fleets avoid detours and maintain efficiency.


### **Spend Controls and Fraud Prevention**


Fuel cards offer built-in controls that standard payment tools do not.


Look for features such as:


- **Dollar limits -** Set maximum transaction amounts per purchase
- **Gallon caps -** Prevent excessive fuel purchases beyond tank capacity
- **Time-of-day restrictions -** Block purchases during off-hours
- **Merchant category filtering -** Limit purchases to fuel-only transactions


Meanwhile, advanced programs add[telematics](https://www.atob.com/blog/what-is-telematics) integration to verify vehicle location at the point of sale and confirm purchases do not exceed tank capacity. Preventing misuse ensures that fuel savings are not offset by unnecessary expenses.


### **Reporting, Fees, and Credit Requirements**


The best fuel card for your business should provide clear reporting and transparent pricing.


Make sure to evaluate:


- Monthly account fees, a small fee per card, and setup costs to avoid the[hidden costs of fuel cards](https://www.atob.com/blog/hidden-costs-of-fuel-cards-in-fleet-management)
- Transaction or processing charges
- Reporting tools for tracking fuel receipts and usage


Confirm whether a personal guarantee or credit check is required, as many fleets prefer[net 30 fuel cards](https://www.atob.com/blog/net-30-gas-cards-no-personal-guarantee) to limit liability.


Understanding the type of card you choose is important, as it determines both flexibility and cost.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **What is a fleet fuel card program?**


A fleet fuel card program is a payment system built for businesses operating vehicles, providing spend controls, fuel discounts, fraud prevention, and detailed transaction reporting that standard credit cards do not offer.


### **How much can a business save with a fleet fuel card program?**


Savings depend on fleet size and fuel volume. AtoB customers receive average truck diesel savings of 42¢ per gallon (up to $2.00)* at participating truck stops.


### **Can I use a fleet fuel card at any truck stop?**


Acceptance depends on card type. Open-loop cards backed by Mastercard work at nearly any truck stop nationwide. Closed-loop cards are limited to their respective networks.


### **Do fleet fuel card programs require a credit check?**


Not all programs require credit checks. Prepaid options like AtoB Unlimited do not require a credit check, making them ideal for small businesses.


### **What spending controls do fleet fuel card programs offer?**


Common controls include dollar limits, gallon caps, time restrictions, and fuel type filtering. Advanced programs add telematics-based location and fuel-level validation.
