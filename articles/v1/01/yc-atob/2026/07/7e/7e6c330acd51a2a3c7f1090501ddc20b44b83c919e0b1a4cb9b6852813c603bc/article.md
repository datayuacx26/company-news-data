# 7 Best Business Gas Only Credit Cards

A gas only business credit card restricts employee purchases to fuel and approved vehicle expenses at the transaction level. Unlike standard business payment cards that only list the fueling merchant on a statement, these specialized fleet cards capture detailed information, including gallons purchased, fuel type, driver ID, and vehicle assignment.


This guide compares the best fuel-only card options for businesses, explains what to evaluate before selecting a provider, and outlines how modern fleet[fuel cards](https://www.atob.com/product-fuel-cards) combine controls, reporting, and fuel rewards into one system.


## **Why Gas Only Cards Are Important for Businesses**


A fuel-only business credit card gives fleet operators more visibility and control over driver spending than a traditional business credit card or consumer gas card.


Instead of reviewing transactions after they occur, businesses can manage purchases proactively through restrictions and reporting tools. This is especially important for businesses with growing fuel expenses or multiple drivers using company vehicles. For many operations, a dedicated[fleet fuel card program](https://www.atob.com/blog/fleet-fuel-card-program) provides stronger controls and reporting than standard payment cards.


Understanding how[fuel cards save you money](https://www.atob.com/blog/fuel-cards-save-you-money) reveals that the benefits for fleet operations extend beyond simple restrictions:


- **Spend control -** Set limits on purchases to fuel and approved vehicle categories, helping fleets reduce unauthorized fuel purchases and driver misuse
- **Transaction visibility -** Capture detailed data, including driver name, vehicle ID, gallons purchased, fuel type, and location for every fill-up
- **Per-gallon savings -** Access discount networks that reduce fuel costs at thousands of truck stops nationwide
- **Streamlined accounting -** Eliminate paper receipt chasing with digital records, automated[IFTA fuel tax reporting](https://www.atob.com/blog/ifta-fuel-tax-reporting) , and direct accounting integrations


Failing to[prevent fuel card fraud](https://www.atob.com/blog/how-to-prevent-fuel-card-fraud) and unauthorized purchases drains fleet profits every year. Gas only cards address that problem directly by enforcing purchase rules at the point of sale, helping fleets identify suspicious activity before it affects broader[fleet expense management](https://www.atob.com/blog/fleet-expense-management) and operational efficiency.


## **What to Look for in a Gas Only Business Card**


Before selecting a card, fleet operators should evaluate several operational and financial factors.


### **Acceptance and Discounts**


Open-loop cards backed by Mastercard or Visa provide broader acceptance at truck stops and retail fuel locations nationwide. Closed-loop cards may offer deeper discounts, but they limit where drivers can fuel.


Some providers focus on branded fuel networks, while others prioritize independent truck stop partnerships and broader fuel acceptance.


Different providers structure savings differently by offering any of the following:


- **Flat per-gallon savings -** Fixed discount amount on every gallon purchased
- **Cash back percentages -** Percentage-based rebates on total fuel spending
- **Rebate programs -** Delayed savings credited back to your account


The size of the discount network matters because savings only help when drivers can access it along their actual routes.


The right program depends on where your drivers fuel and how frequently they purchase diesel or gasoline.


### **Controls, Fees, and Approval**


Strong spend controls let managers restrict purchases by type of fuel, set daily or weekly limits, enforce time-of-day rules, and whitelist specific merchants for each driver or vehicle.


Review the complete fee schedule first before applying, including:


- Monthly account fees
- Per-card charges
- Transaction fees
- Setup costs
- Out-of-network penalties


These charges can quickly erode any per-gallon savings, so look for a card company offering transparent pricing. Approval structures and underwriting requirements vary by provider, particularly for newer businesses or fleets with limited operating history.


Meanwhile, prepaid or prefunded options like a[fuel prepaid card](https://www.atob.com/secured-gas-card) may offer more flexible approval structures for newer businesses.


Understanding the differences between[secured and prepaid fuel cards](https://www.atob.com/blog/secured-vs-prepaid-fuel-cards) and understanding[how prepaid gas cards work](https://www.atob.com/blog/how-do-prepaid-gas-cards-work) can help businesses choose the right balance of flexibility, control, and approval requirements.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **What is a gas only credit card for businesses?**


A gas only credit card for businesses restricts employee purchases to fuel while capturing detailed transaction data, including gallons, fuel type, price per gallon, driver ID, and vehicle assignment. These cards are available in both credit-based and prepaid formats.


### **Can I get a business fuel card that works only at truck stops?**


Yes, closed-network fleet cards exist for truck stop-only use and often provide deeper diesel discounts. Universal cards like AtoB combine truck stop discounts with the flexibility to fuel at any location that accepts Mastercard.


### **Is a prepaid fuel card better than a credit-based fleet card?**


Prepaid fuel cards require no credit check and provide exact budget control. Credit-based cards offer net payment terms, higher spending limits, and help build business credit through bureau reporting.


### **Do gas only business credit cards offer rewards or discounts?**
