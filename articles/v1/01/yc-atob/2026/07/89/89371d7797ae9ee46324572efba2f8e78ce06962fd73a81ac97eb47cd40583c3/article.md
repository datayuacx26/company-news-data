# 5 Best Fuel Cards for New Businesses

Most fuel card providers are built for established operations with strong business credit profiles. New businesses can still qualify through prepaid options and cash flow-based underwriting.


We cover the best fuel cards for early-stage carriers, what to evaluate, and how to apply with limited documentation. Providers like AtoB offer both credit and prepaid[fuel cards](https://www.atob.com/product-fuel-cards) designed for businesses at every stage, including startups with no established credit history.


## **Why New Businesses Need a Fuel Card**


New businesses that rely on vehicles face a critical decision: use personal credit cards for fuel or invest in a dedicated fuel card.


While business credit cards are available, they often mix personal and business expenses, offer limited controls, and do not provide the tools needed for fleet management. A fuel card, by contrast, is designed specifically for operational use.


Here is what a fuel card offers:


- Centralize fuel expenses across drivers and vehicles
- Track and manage fuel purchases through a single portal
- Control spending with built-in limits and restrictions
- Simplify[IFTA fuel tax reporting](https://www.atob.com/blog/ifta-fuel-tax-reporting) and reduce administrative work
- Improve fuel efficiency by supporting fuel-efficient driving habits


If you are a new business owner, separating fuel expenses early creates better financial visibility and reduces the need for reimbursement tracking across your employees.


## **What to Look for in a Fuel Card as a New Business**


New businesses have different needs than established fleets. The right solution should align with early-stage operations while still scaling as the business grows.


### **Acceptance Network and Spending Controls**


Cards with universal acceptance provide the most flexibility. Solutions that work nationwide allow drivers to fuel at major truck stops, independent gas stations, and locations near job sites.


Spending controls matter more when processes are still forming. Look for features that allow you to:


- Restrict spending to fuel-only purchases
- Set limits per transaction or per driver
- Block purchases at non-fuel merchants
- Track activity in real time


These tools help control spending and reduce the risk of misuse.


### **Credit vs. Prepaid and Fee Transparency**


Choose between different[fuel card types](https://www.atob.com/blog/fuel-card-types) based on your business stage:


- **Credit cards -** Function like charge cards with a credit line and billing cycle
- **Prepaid cards -** The[best prepaid fuel cards](https://www.atob.com/blog/best-prepaid-fuel-cards) are ideal for brand-new operations or those with limited credit history


Evaluate fees carefully. Common charges to watch for include setup fees, monthly per-card charges, per-transaction fees, out-of-network penalties, and late payment costs. Some cards advertise no monthly fee but charge per transaction.


Understanding the full cost helps ensure the card is a fit for your business.


### **Business Credit Reporting and IFTA Compliance**


A fuel card that reports to business credit bureaus helps build a financial profile over time. This is important for businesses that plan to apply for financing later.


New carriers also need IFTA-ready reporting. Look for these features:


- **Itemized transaction data -** Detailed purchase records with location and gallons
- **Digital receipt capture -** Automatic receipt storage and organization
- **Automated IFTA exports -** Pre-formatted reports for quarterly filing
- **Accounting software integration -** Direct sync with QuickBooks or similar platforms


These features reduce administrative work and improve accuracy.

## **How to Apply for a Fuel Card as a New Business**


Preparation improves approval odds even with limited documentation and credit history, especially for businesses exploring easy approval business gas cards as an entry point into fleet financing.


### **What You Will Need**


Most card issuers require:


- **EIN -** Your Employer Identification Number from the IRS
- **Business formation documents -** LLC articles of organization or corporate registration
- **Valid driver's license -** Government-issued ID for the primary account holder
- **Business bank account -** Required by some providers for verification
- **Bank statements -** Showing recent revenue history for credit-based applications


### **Strengthening a Thin Application and the Approval Timeline**


Steps to improve your application:


1. Register as an LLC or corporation rather than a sole proprietorship
2. Open a dedicated business bank account separate from personal finances
3. Ensure business information matches across all registrations and documents
4. Compare[secured vs prepaid fuel cards](https://www.atob.com/blog/secured-vs-prepaid-fuel-cards) and consider starting with a prefunded option to establish payment history first


Prepaid cards typically receive approval within one to two business days. Credit-based fleet cards may take several days, depending on underwriting requirements.


## **Fees and Costs New Businesses Should Watch For**


Fee structures vary significantly across issuers and can erode savings for businesses on thin margins.


### **Common Fee Types**


Watch for these charges:


- Setup and onboarding fees
- Monthly account charges
- Transaction fees for fuel purchases
- Fees for out-of-network fueling


Evaluate savings carefully. Even small discounts on fuel prices can add up, but only if fees do not offset those benefits.


### **Calculating True Cost vs. Savings**


Calculate net savings: monthly fuel volume multiplied by per-gallon discount, minus total monthly fees.


Cards advertising large discounts within small networks often deliver less real-world savings than cards with broad acceptance and moderate discounts. This is a key factor when evaluating fleet fuel cards with discounts across different operating routes.

## **Fuel Cards for New Businesses FAQs**


### **Can a brand-new business get a fuel card?**


Yes. Prepaid fuel cards require no credit check, credit history, or personal guarantee. Credit-based options may require several months of revenue history, but often use cash flow-based underwriting.


### **What is the best fleet fuel card for a small business?**

Prepaid fuel cards typically receive approval within one to two business days. Credit-based fleet cards may take several days, depending on underwriting requirements.
