## **What a Fleet Card Is and Why Small Companies Use One**


A fleet fuel card is a payment tool designed for businesses that regularly purchase fuel. Unlike traditional business credit cards or business charge cards, fleet cards are built specifically for company vehicles and help businesses manage operating costs more effectively.


Key benefits include:


- Fuel discounts and fuel rebates that help reduce fuel costs
- Driver spending controls and purchase restrictions
- Automated reporting and expense management tools
- Greater visibility into fuel purchases and fleet spending


Some fleet cards extend beyond fuel purchases by combining reporting, spend controls, fraud prevention, and fleet expense management within a single platform. ## **What You Need Before Applying for a Fleet Card**


Different cards use different qualification standards. Some providers evaluate fuel volume, while others may consider business history or credit score as part of the application process.


Most fleet fuel card applications require the following:


- EIN or business tax ID, legal business name, and business address
- Estimated monthly fuel volume
- A business bank account for billing and payments
- Your social security number (SSN)


Some fleet card programs also focus on established businesses, while others offer[fuel cards for new businesses](https://www.atob.com/blog/fuel-cards-for-new-business) managing fleet spending for the first time.


For a smaller business evaluating its first fuel card, gathering this information in advance can help streamline the application process.
