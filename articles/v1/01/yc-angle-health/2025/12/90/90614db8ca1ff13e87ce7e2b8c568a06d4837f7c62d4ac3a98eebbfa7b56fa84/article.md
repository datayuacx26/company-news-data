# Skip the Wait: Smarter Ways to Get Care Before the Holidays

The holidays are busy enough without adding a long wait at urgent care or the ER. Between packed schedules, travel, and seasonal illness, it’s easy for small health issues to turn into major disruptions. The good news? You can skip the wait and get the care you need faster with the tools already included in your Angle Health plan.


## **1. Start with Telehealth**


‍


**Get care anytime, anywhere.**


**‍**
If you wake up with a cough, rash, or flu symptoms, skip the waiting room. Through the[Angle Health app](https://www.anglehealth.com/members) , you can connect with a doctor 24/7 for many common issues without an appointment or commute.


‍


**Best for:**


‍


- Cold, flu, sinus, or allergy symptoms
- Minor injuries or infections
- Prescription refills or medication questions


****


**Pro tip:** Download the[Angle Health app](https://www.anglehealth.com/members) and confirm your profile before you need care. That way, you are ready to see a provider in minutes when something pops up.


## **2. Know When to Choose Urgent Care vs. the ER**


‍


Not every situation needs an emergency room visit. Choosing the right level of care saves time, money, and stress.


‍


**Go to urgent care if:**


‍


- You have a minor injury, cut, or sprain
- You need X-rays or labs, but it is not life-threatening
- Your doctor’s office is closed


‍


**Go to the ER if:**


‍


- You have chest pain, difficulty breathing, or sudden numbness
- You are experiencing severe bleeding or broken bones
- Your think it could be life- or limb-threatening


‍


If you are unsure,[use the in-app chat](https://member.anglehealth.com/login) **** or reach out toAngle’s Care Team . They can guide you to the right place.
