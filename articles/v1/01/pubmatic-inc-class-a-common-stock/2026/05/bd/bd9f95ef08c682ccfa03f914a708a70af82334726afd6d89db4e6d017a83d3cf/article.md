# Programmatic Promised Transparency. Agentic Is Built to Deliver It: Introducing the Detailed Reasoning Agent

Agentic media buying is the most significant structural shift in advertising since programmatic. The efficiency gains are real: buyers using PubMatic’s AgenticOS are seeing dramatic reductions in campaign setup time by up to 98%, measurable CPM improvements and up to 50% total fee efficiency, and direct access to premium inventory at open-internet scale. Every buyer that has launched an agentic campaign through PubMatic has come back to run more.


But as autonomous systems take on more consequential decisions — identifying audiences, optimizing inventory, adjusting campaigns in real time — a new question has become central to every buyer conversation: why did the agent make that choice?


Today, PubMatic is introducing the **Detailed Reasoning Agent on AgenticOS** — a new capability that gives traders a complete, auditable record of every consequential decision an agent makes, accessible at any time.


#### We’ve Been Here Before


Programmatic advertising promised efficiency and accountability when it scaled in the early 2010s. What followed was a generation of black-box optimization: platforms that drove performance metrics up while making it impossible to answer the most basic questions — where did my ads run, and why did the algorithm do what it did? The technology worked, but accountability lagged.


The question for agentic advertising is whether we repeat that pattern, or build the accountability layer from the beginning.


At PubMatic, we’ve made a deliberate architectural choice: transparency is infrastructure, not a feature you add after the fact.


Some in the industry have argued that agentic buying, if built as a series of direct bespoke agent-to-agent interactions, simply recreates the fragmentation and opacity of early programmatic at machine speed.


It’s a legitimate warning, and it points to why an independent marketplace layer matters now more than ever. AgenticOS provides a full agentic marketplace: buyer agents submit briefs and match against more than 100,000 sites and apps through shared, auditable infrastructure — open internet scale, not a closed loop.


#### Introducing the Detailed Reasoning Agent


Today, we’re introducing the Detailed Reasoning Agent on AgenticOS, a new capability that gives traders a complete, auditable record of every consequential decision an agent makes, accessible at any time. When an agent acts on audience selection, inventory choice, or a campaign adjustment, it now logs a structured reasoning chain that traders can access at any time. It observes the full context of the objective: inputs, constraints, available data, prior campaign state. It generates multiple candidate action paths before committing to any. It evaluates each path for feasibility, coverage gaps, and risk. It commits to a decision with explicit written rationale, surfacing every assumption it made along the way. And it verifies its own outputs, cross-checking every action against live data before logging the result.


For every decision that directly touches spend, audiences, and outcomes, the Detailed Reasoning Agent is required to document its full reasoning chain — the action paths it considered, why each alternative was rejected, the assumptions it made that were not independently verified at runtime, and any knowledge gaps that remained unresolved at the time of response. At each step, it records a trust signal: a verifiable record that the agent acted correctly and completely.


The result is something traders have been asking for since the dawn of programmatic: the ability to walk into a client meeting, pull up the agent’s reasoning, and explain every significant decision without guessing.
