# PubMatic to Participate in Upcoming Financial Conferences

## PubMatic to Participate in Upcoming Financial Conferences


May 11, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.pubmatic.com/node/13351/pdf)


NO-HEADQUARTERS/REDWOOD CITY, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 11, 2026-- PubMatic, Inc. (Nasdaq: PUBM), the leading AI-powered ad tech company delivering digital advertising performance, today announced that members of its management team are scheduled to participate at the following upcoming financial conferences:


- 21st Annual Needham Technology, Media & Consumer Conference in New York, NY on May 13, 2026. Management will participate in a webcasted fire-side chat at 10:15 a.m. ET and host 1x1 meetings.
- 2026 Jefferies Software, Internet & AI Conference in Newport Coast, CA on May 27, 2026. Management will host 1x1 meetings.
- Evercore TMT Global Conference in San Francisco, CA on June 3, 2026. Management will participate in a webcasted fire-side chat at 2:10 p.m. PT and host meetings.
- Rosenblatt’s 6th Annual Virtual Technology Summit on June 9, 2026. Management will host 1x1 meetings.


A webcast of the fire-side chat will be available in the “Events” section of PubMatic’s investor relations website at[https://investors.pubmatic.com/news-events/investor-calendar](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.pubmatic.com%2Fnews-events%2Finvestor-calendar&esheet=54533533&newsitemid=20260511363995&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.pubmatic.com%2Fnews-events%2Finvestor-calendar&index=1&md5=e8a51a3444ddb6984bbe362eb4eb2acd) . The webcast replay will be available following the conclusion of the live presentations for 90 days.
