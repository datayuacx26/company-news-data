# PubMatic and Agence 79 Achieve 5x Higher CTR vs Open Market with Programmatic Video Curation in France

**Paris, 11 June 2026** — PubMatic (Nasdaq: PUBM), the leading AI-powered ad tech company delivering digital advertising performance, announces the results of its partnership with Agence 79, part of Havas Media Network, on a programmatic video campaign for a leading French mass-market retailer. Leveraging PubMatic’s curation solutions, the campaign outperformed all its objectives: +16.7% video completion rate (VCR) versus target, +12.5% viewability, and a Click-Through Rate (CTR) five times higher than the same campaign on the open market (Source: Agence 79 internal data).


#### **A Growing Need for Control and Transparency Among French Agencies**


As the French digital advertising market reached €12.4 billion in 2025, according to the 35th Observatoire de l’e-pub ([https://www.sri-france.org/observatoire-epub/35eme-observatoire-de-le-pub/](https://www.sri-france.org/observatoire-epub/35eme-observatoire-de-le-pub/) ), with video now representing 60% of all display investments, agencies are increasingly challenged to access premium inventory while maintaining control over their supply chain. The multiplication of programmatic pathways makes it more difficult to ensure transparency regarding impression quality, data usage, and transaction fees.


Against this backdrop, Agence 79 sought a curation partner capable of meeting three key requirements: measurable video performance, full transparency across the supply path, and complete autonomy over data governance and deal structures.


#### **What the Curation Model Delivered**


After evaluating several market players, Agence 79 selected PubMatic for its ability to combine performance, transparency, and buy‑side control. The campaign was activated through PubMatic’s unified direct-to-supply platform, which brings together data intelligence, advanced optimisation, and curated access to premium video inventory.


In practical terms, Agence 79 was able to build a tailored premium video environment by selecting specific delivery channels, while retaining full control over data segment activation. The entire campaign exceeded Agence 79’s initial target of 80% viewability, ensuring consistent and reliable measurement conditions throughout


.


#### **Results that Validate the Model**


The campaign’s performance exceeded targets across all indicators:


- 5x Click‑Through Rate (CTR) compared to the open market


- +16.7% Video Completion Rate (VCR) versus the set target


- +12.5% Viewability versus the set target
