# Announcing FDX Support and Expanded Coverage for Rutter Bank Feeds

Rutter’s Bank Feeds product enables fintechs, banks, and BaaS platforms to sync transaction data directly into SMB/Mid-market accounting software, helping customers automate reconciliation and keep their books up to date.


By appearing as a connected financial institution within platforms like QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage, you can eliminate manual CSV uploads and deliver a native, real-time experience for your users.


Today, we’re expanding that capability with support for **FDX-based data ingestion** and broader coverage for **Plaid-powered platforms** .


## **What Rutter Bank Feeds Do**


Rutter integrates your company into the **banking section** of leading accounting platforms, enabling your customers to:


- Automatically sync transactions from your financial offerings, including bank accounts, credit cards, and charge cards
- Reconcile payments, deposits, and transfers in a few clicks
- Eliminate manual entry and reduce bookkeeping errors
- Send payments from bank accounts offered by your product


Traditionally, this functionality was limited to large financial institutions with deep integrations. Rutter brings it to fintechs, regional banks, digital banking platforms, and BaaS providers through a single API.


## ‍ **What’s New**


**FDX-Based Data Ingestion** Rutter now supports **FDX (Financial Data Exchange)** transaction data. **Customers can:**


- Push FDX-formatted data directly to Rutter
- Let Rutter extract and standardize it on your behalf


This provides greater flexibility and allows you to leverage existing data infrastructure while accelerating implementation.
‍


**Pre-Built UI Components**


Rutter also now offers pre-built UI components to assist your customers in mapping their bank or card accounts to their GL accounts. This offers seamless syncing for them, and hugely reduced development effort for you.


Functionality includes selecting an account and mapping it back to a general ledger and a date range to pull transactions from


‍


Review and manage your linked and unlinked accounts and add new ones


**Support for Plaid-Powered Accounting Platforms**


Rutter now supports accounting platforms that rely on **Plaid for bank feed connectivity** , including **Wave** , **Zoho Books** , and others. These join our existing coverage:


- QuickBooks Online
- QuickBooks Desktop
- Quicken
- Credit Karma
- Xero
- Sage Intacct
- Sage Business Cloud
- NetSuite
