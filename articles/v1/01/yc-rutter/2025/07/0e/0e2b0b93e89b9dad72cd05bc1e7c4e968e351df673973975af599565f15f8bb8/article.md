# Expanding the Financial OS: Rutter Now Supports Zid and Salla

At Rutter, we're committed to helping businesses building the ultimate financial operating system. Today, we're excited to announce the expansion of our integration capabilities with the addition of two leading Middle Eastern commerce platforms: Zid and Salla.


## **Powering Global Fintech Solutions**


Building the financial operating system for modern businesses requires seamless connections across diverse data sources. As businesses increasingly operate globally, the ability to integrate with region-specific platforms becomes crucial for fintechs.


With the addition of Zid and Salla integrations to our Commerce category, we're strengthening our ability to serve fintech companies that operate in Saudi Arabia and the broader MENA region.


## **Why Zid and Salla are Game Changers**


Zid and Salla represent two of the fastest-growing e-commerce platforms in the MENA region, powering thousands of online stores for local and regional merchants.


For fintech providers, integrating with these platforms unlocks critical commerce data that transforms multiple use cases: from strengthening underwriting models with real-time performance metrics before extending capital, to enabling sophisticated cash flow forecasting that helps merchants optimize their financial operations. These integrations also enhance financial analytics capabilities, providing merchants with consolidated insights across previously siloed sales channels.


By expanding our commerce coverage to include these regional powerhouses, Rutter ensures your products can deliver exceptional experiences to businesses previously underserved by financial technology solutions


## **Unified API, Expanded Reach**


What makes Rutter unique is our Universal API that provides the same level of granularity as a direct integration while utilizing a single schema. With this approach, developers can now access Zid and Salla data using the same endpoints they already use for Shopify, WooCommerce, and our other commerce integrations.


## **Building the Complete Financial OS**


Today's businesses expect a single platform to streamline all financial operations. The era of disparate financial products is giving way to integrated Financial Operating Systems that connect banking, commerce, payments, accounting, and more.


Rutter powers push and pull integrations for leading B2B fintech products across these categories, helping eliminate manual processes and transform financial processes. The addition of Zid and Salla further strengthens our commerce category, complementing our robust integrations across accounting, banking, and payments


## **Getting Started**


These new integrations are now available in beta. Existing customers can begin accessing Zid and Salla data immediately through our standard commerce endpoints. For detailed implementation information, visit our[Commerce API Reference](https://docs.rutter.com/rest/version/orders) .
