# Keurig Dr Pepper to Participate in Deutsche Bank dbAccess Global Consumer Conference

FRISCO, Texas and BURLINGTON, Mass.


, May 20, 2026


/PRNewswire/ –Keurig Dr Pepper Inc. (NASDAQ: KDP) today announced that Tim Cofer, Chief Executive Officer, and Anthony DiSilvestro, Chief Financial Officer, will present at the Deutsche Bank dbAccess Global Consumer Conference on June 3, 2026 at 5:15 AM ET.


Access to a live webcast and replay of the event will be available in the Investors section of the Company’s corporate website,[www.keurigdrpepper.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4693454-1&h=3119746713&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.keurigdrpepper.com%2F&a=www.keurigdrpepper.com) .
