# Tebra EHR Software Review (2024)

Tebra's medical software is a comprehensive platform designed for healthcare practices to manage their administrative and clinical operations effectively.


It integrates various aspects of practice management, including electronic health records (EHR), patient intake, billing, and appointment scheduling, into a single unified system.


Tebra also offers a patient portal, enhancing patient engagement by allowing secure messaging, access to eLab results, and signing treatment plans.


The software aims to streamline workflows, reduce administrative burdens, and enhance the overall efficiency of medical practices by incorporating robust revenue cycle management features.


By providing a cloud-based solution, Tebra ensures that healthcare providers can access essential information and manage their practices from any location, facilitating smoother patient encounters.


The software is particularly beneficial for small to medium-sized medical practices seeking to improve their operational efficiency and patient care delivery through a cohesive and user-friendly interface.


## Tebra Key Features


Tebra is a comprehensive healthcare software that offers a range of features to enhance medical practice management and patient care. Here are four key features of Tebra:


**Electronic Health Records (EHR):** Tebra's EHR system provides a complete view of patient data and histories, including prescriptions and patient charts, to streamline clinical workflows and enhance the quality of care.


**Integrated Billing and Payments:** The software offers integrated billing solutions that allow healthcare providers to manage claims and payments efficiently, reducing administrative burdens and accelerating payment collection within medical software platforms.


**Telehealth Services:** Tebra supports telehealth functionalities, enabling providers to offer remote consultations and expand access to care. This feature is essential for patient convenience and broader healthcare delivery.


**Appointment Scheduling and Patient Communication:** The platform includes robust scheduling tools and enhanced messaging customization, allowing personalized and efficient patient communications through the patient portal. This improves patient satisfaction and operational efficiency.

## **What is Tebra patient intake?**


Tebra patient intake is a comprehensive solution designed to streamline the process of gathering essential patient information before medical appointments.


This feature is part of Tebra's all-in-one solution for medical practices, which includes online scheduling, appointment reminders, patient portals, and text messaging.


The patient intake process allows practices to send intake forms electronically to patients, who can complete them at their convenience. These forms can collect vital information such as personal details, medical history, reasons for visiting, and insurance information.


Tebra's system supports the creation and customization of intake forms, enabling practices to tailor the forms to their specific needs. The intake forms can be printed or attached to patient records, ensuring that all necessary information is readily accessible.


By digitizing and automating the intake process, Tebra enhances efficiency, reduces paperwork, and improves the overall patient experience.


## **Does Tebra have an app?**


Yes, Tebra has several apps available to support different aspects of medical practice management. The primary app, available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, offers features for electronic health records (EHR), practice management (PM), and billing.


This app serves as a mobile companion to the Tebra platform, enabling healthcare providers to manage their practice on the go.


Additionally, there is the Tebra Kiosk app, designed to revolutionize the patient intake process by providing a modern, digital experience for collecting patient information and payments.


These apps help streamline various administrative tasks, improving efficiency and patient experience in medical practices.
