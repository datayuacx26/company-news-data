# Mckesson EMR/EHR Software Review

**McKesson EMR/EHR Software is a robust healthcare solution designed to enhance clinical efficiency and patient care.**


With over 20 years of experience in medical software and a workforce of nearly 80,000 employees, McKesson's systems are **trusted by a wide range of health organizations** .


**The software automates essential clinical functions, including prescription management, diagnostic order entry, and results reporting** , streamlining workflows for healthcare providers.


McKesson's cloud-based platform integrates practice management, patient portals, and medical billing, providing a comprehensive solution for modern healthcare needs.


This integration helps healthcare organizations improve efficiency, accuracy, and patient outcomes, making McKesson a significant player in the healthcare industry.


## McKesson Key Features


- **Comprehensive EMR/EHR Capabilities** : McKesson's software offers robust[electronic medical records (EMR)](https://blog.quadrant.health/what-is-an-emr-a-complete-guide/) and[electronic health records (EHR)](https://blog.quadrant.health/what-is-an-ehr-electronic-health-record/) functionalities, including patient record management, clinical documentation, and electronic prescribing. These features enhance the efficiency and accuracy of clinical workflows, thereby improving patient safety by ensuring accurate and timely access to medical records.
- **Practice Management Integration** : The software integrates practice management tools that streamline administrative tasks such as appointment scheduling, patient check-in, and staff coordination. This integration helps healthcare providers manage their practices more effectively, reducing administrative burdens and improving overall clinical workflow.
- **Patient Portal** : McKesson includes a patient portal that allows patients to access their health records, schedule appointments, and communicate with their healthcare providers. This feature enhances patient engagement and promotes better health outcomes by facilitating easier access to medical information and services, thus ensuring patient records are always up-to-date and accurate.
- **Medical Billing Services** : The built-in medical billing capabilities of McKesson's software support financial management by streamlining the billing process, reducing errors, and improving[revenue cycle management](https://blog.quadrant.health/what-is-revenue-cycle-management-in-healthcare/) . This feature ensures that healthcare providers can efficiently handle billing tasks and maximize their financial performance, contributing to a well-managed practice management system.


## McKesson Pros and Cons


### **Pros**


1. **Intuitive Interface** : McKesson EMR is praised for its easy-to-use and visually appealing layout, making it simple for users to navigate and find information quickly.
2. **Comprehensive Features** : The software offers robust EMR/EHR functionalities, including patient record management, clinical documentation, and electronic prescribing, enhancing clinical workflows.
3. **Integrated Practice Management** : McKesson integrates practice management tools, streamlining administrative tasks and improving overall efficiency.
4. **Patient Portal Access** : The inclusion of a patient portal enhances patient engagement by allowing patients to access their health records, schedule appointments, and communicate with healthcare providers.


### Cons:


1. **Complex Refund Process** : Users have reported that the refund process for McKesson can be complex and time-consuming.
2. **Initial Learning Curve** : While the interface is user-friendly, there might be a learning curve for new users to become fully proficient with all features and capabilities.


## McKesson Pricing


McKesson's pricing varies based on the specific software solution and the needs of the healthcare practice. For instance, McKesson Practice Choice is known for its cost-effectiveness, particularly for small to medium-sized practices.


While exact pricing details are not always disclosed upfront, McKesson provides competitive pricing strategies across its range of products, including EMR/EHR and pharmacy management systems.


Given its comprehensive features, McKesson's software solutions offer significant value by improving operational efficiency, patient engagement, and financial management, making the investment worthwhile for many healthcare providers.


## When McKesson EMR/EHR Software is a Good Choice


McKesson EMR/EHR Software is a good choice for healthcare practices under several circumstances:


1. **Small to Medium-Sized Practices** : McKesson Practice Choice is designed to be cost-effective and suitable for small to medium-sized practices that may not have the budget for more expensive solutions.
2. **Need for Comprehensive Features** : Practices looking for a comprehensive EMR/EHR solution that integrates patient record management, clinical documentation, electronic prescribing, practice management, patient portals, and medical billing will find McKesson beneficial.
3. **User-Friendly Interface** : Practices new to EMR or those seeking a user-friendly interface will appreciate McKesson’s intuitive design and seamless integration with practice management tools, making it easier to navigate and use without extensive training.
4. **Oncology Practices** : McKesson's iKnowMed EHR is specifically tailored for oncology practices, offering specialized features that support oncology workflows and patient management.
5. **Web-Based Solution** : As a cloud-based solution, McKesson Practice Choice is ideal for practices that prefer not to manage on-premises servers and IT infrastructure. This setup can reduce the need for dedicated IT staff and make updates and maintenance easier.
