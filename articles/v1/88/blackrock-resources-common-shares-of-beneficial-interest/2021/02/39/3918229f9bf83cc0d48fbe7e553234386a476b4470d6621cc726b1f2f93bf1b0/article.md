# Solutions for the renewable energy markets!

Canonsburg, PA — By 2040 the Global Energy demand is expected to rise by 60%. The trend to further electrify buildings, cars, buses, along with smart appliances and automation will spur the need for more electricity everywhere. Solar, wind and natural gas contribute the most to meeting the demand, but more will need to be done. A new workforce of renewable energy experts will be needed, and BlackRock Renewables will be prepared to meet the demand.


President and CEO, Michael Parker said “We are very excited to offer our talent and experience to the renewable energy industry. It is a natural progression to the work BlackRock Resources has done in the energy sector.”


BlackRock Resources, a leader in energy staffing, project management and engineering solutions would like to announce a new division aimed at providing workforce development and staffing opportunities to the renewable energy industry! BlackRock Renewables has a clear objective to build on the success of BlackRock Resources and to bring staffing, recruiting, project management, engineering and field resources to the renewable energy markets. We are prepared to take on the most challenging tasks and provide solutions and options in a timely and cost-effective manner. Staffing opportunities include: Project Managers, Project Controls, Engineers, Construction Management, and many more!


The BlackRock Renewables division will provide knowledgeable and experienced talent who’s primary objective is to successfully complete projects on schedule and on budget. BlackRock Resources has the experience and the resources to manage any project regardless of its size, at the corporate level and in the field office. We partner with our customers to provide qualified personnel to some of the largest energy projects in the USA.


BlackRock Resources was founded in Canonsburg, Pa. in 2012 with the mission of providing professional staffing and consulting services to the energy markets. We provide custom workforce solution options that meet today’s demands.
