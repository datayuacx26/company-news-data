## **What are roofing leads?**


A roofing lead is a potential customer who shows interest in your services — from a homeowner requesting a quote, to someone booking a roof inspection, to a pricing request through an[Instant Estimator](https://roofr.com/estimator) .


But here’s the key: **a lead isn’t a job until you nurture it, and not all leads are quality leads.** That’s where roofers often lose opportunities.


In fact, *Roofing by the Numbers 2025* found that **only 28% of roofers currently use a CRM to track lead,** meaning most are leaving money on the table. Roofers who adopted a CRM plus marketing automations reported generating **2x more leads year-over-year** than those who didn’t.


## **Nurturing a lead**


Generating roofing lead is only half the battle. Leads that come in aren't going to automatically equal a sale. Treading your lead properly and nurturing them is what makes them become a customer. Nurturing a lead can look like:

## **The fastest channels for roofing lead generation**


Not all lead channels deliver results at the same speed. Here are the top five ranked by how quickly you can expect to see results:


### **1. Neighbor knocking after a job**


When you finish a roof in a neighborhood, knock on 10 doors around the job site. Homeowners see your truck, your crew, and your work firsthand — making this the fastest way to convert a neighbor into a lead. No ad budget required.


### **2. Google Local Services Ads (LSAs)**


LSAs put your business at the very top of Google search results with a “Google Guaranteed” badge. Once your account is verified, you can start receiving calls within days. You only pay per lead, not per click, making this one of the most cost-efficient paid channels for roofers.


### **3. Referral programs**


A structured referral program turns your happy customers into a sales team. Offer a reward (gift card, discount on future service) for every referral who books a job. Referrals close faster and at higher rates than almost any other lead source because trust is already built in.


### **4. Website instant estimator**


Adding an[instant estimator](https://roofr.com/blog/win-more-referrals-from-realtors-with-roofrs-instant-estimator) to your website captures leads 24/7, even when you’re on a roof. Homeowners who use an online estimator are already price-shopping, meaning they’re much further along in the buying process than a cold inquiry.


### **5. Social proof and review campaigns**


A targeted review campaign — sending past customers a direct link to leave a Google review right after job completion — builds the kind of[social proof](https://roofr.com/blog/using-customer-reviews-to-sell-roofs) that influences new homeowners to choose you over competitors. More reviews also improve your local search ranking, creating a compounding effect.


## **How to convert roofing leads faster with speed-to-lead**


[Speed-to-lead](https://roofr.com/blog/speed-to-lead-in-roofing) is the time between when a prospect submits a request and when you respond. In roofing, it’s one of the most important metrics you can track — because the first roofer to respond typically wins the job.


Studies consistently show that the majority of customers go with the first contractor who follows up. If you’re waiting hours — or days — to respond, you’re handing jobs to your competitors. Here’s a simple 3-step process to fix it:


1. **Enable instant online estimates on your website.** Tools like Roofr’s Instant Estimator let homeowners get a real estimate on demand — so your first “response” happens automatically, before you even pick up the phone.
2. **Set up automated SMS/email follow-ups.** The moment a lead fills out your contact form or requests a quote, trigger an automated text or email confirming receipt and setting expectations.
3. **Track response times in your**[roofing CRM](https://roofr.com/blog/best-roofing-crm-g2) **.** If you can’t measure it, you can’t improve it. A roofing CRM lets you see average response times across your team, identify bottlenecks, and hold your sales process accountable.


## **Advertising for roofers: paid and free channels that work**


Both free and paid channels have a place in a healthy roofing lead generation strategy. But they work very differently. The key is understanding what you’re trading: time vs. money, and long-term compounding vs. immediate results.


### Free channels to drive leads


- SEO (optimized website and blog content)
- Referral programs for past customers
- Google Business Profile optimization ([we've got a guide for that](https://roofr.com/blog/roofers-guide-google-business-profile) )
- Social media (Facebook, Instagram, etc)
- Customer reviews


### Paid channels to drive leads


- Google Ads (pay-per-click)
- Google Local Services Ads (LSAs)
- Lead services (Angi, HomeAdvisor, etc)
- Faebook/Instagram paid ads
- Regargeting ads to website visitors


The critical difference: free channels compound over time. An SEO blog post you publish today can generate leads for years. A referral program grows as your customer base grows. Paid channels stop the moment your budget stops — making them powerful for immediate pipeline, but unsustainable as your only strategy.


The most successful roofing companies invest in free channels for long-term growth while using paid channels to fill short-term gaps. This two-track approach targets both “free roofing leads” and high-intent buyers simultaneously.

## **FAQs: How to generate roofing leads**


### **What is the best way to get roofing leads?**


The most effective roofing lead channels are Google Local Services Ads, a website with an instant estimator, and a referral program for past customers. Together, these three channels cover high-intent search, passive inbound capture, and trust-based word-of-mouth — the three pillars of a sustainable lead pipeline. For most roofers, starting with a referral program (zero cost) and Google LSAs (fast results) provides the fastest ROI.


### **How much do roofing leads cost?**


Roofing leads typically cost $15–$300 depending on the source — Google Ads leads average $50–$150, while lead services like Angi can reach $200–$300 per lead. Organic channels like SEO and referrals have near-zero cost per lead once set up. The true cost calculation should factor in close rate: a $50 Google Ads lead that closes at 10% costs $500 per job, while a $0 referral that closes at 60% costs almost nothing.


### **How do I generate roofing leads for free?**


Free roofing leads come from optimizing your Google Business Profile, collecting customer reviews, running a referral program, and adding an online estimator to your website. Google Business Profile optimization is the fastest free win — a complete, review-rich profile can appear in the local 3-pack for “roofer near me” searches with no ad spend. Referrals and reviews compound over time, making them the most valuable long-term free channels.


### **How do I follow up with roofing leads quickly?**


Use automated SMS or email triggers the moment a lead submits a form — the goal is a response within 5 minutes. A[roofing CRM](https://roofr.com/blog/crm-for-roofers-everything-you-need-to-know) can automate this entirely, sending a confirmation message instantly while queuing a personal follow-up call for your team. Even a simple automated text saying “We received your request and will call within 30 minutes” dramatically reduces lead drop-off.


### **What is a roofing lead generation tool?**


A roofing lead generation tool is software that captures, qualifies, and follows up with potential customers automatically — examples include website estimators, CRM with automation, and Google Ads integrations. Roofr combines several of these functions in one platform: an Instant Estimator that captures website leads, a CRM to manage and track them, and automation tools to follow up without manual effort.


### **How do I get roofing leads in winter?**


Winter roofing leads come from emergency repair campaigns, targeting storm-damaged areas, and staying top-of-mind with past customers via email. Ice dams, wind damage, and heavy snow loads create genuine urgency — roofers who have an email list and a storm response campaign ready can move faster than competitors. Year-round SEO is essential for off-peak seasons.
