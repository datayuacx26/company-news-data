40% of homeowners go with the first roofer they hear back from. The *first.* Not the cheapest or the most experienced. So the faster you respond, the better your shot. Manually pulling measurements and building quotes one-by-one? That's how you stay busy and broke.


76% of roofers are already using proposals and estimating software. But 28% are still on spreadsheets. That gap is your opportunity.


Itemized estimates — materials, labor, line by line — that you deliver instantly make homeowners trust you more, question you less, and say yes faster. The right tools make these estimates easy to produce, so there's no reason not to.


**Roofr Tip:** Don’t send one-liner quotes. Detailed, line-itemized proposals are the difference between a "sounds good" and a "let me think about it."
