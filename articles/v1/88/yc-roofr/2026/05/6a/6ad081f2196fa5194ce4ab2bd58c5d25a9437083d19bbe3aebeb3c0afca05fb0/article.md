With roofing instant estimates handled automatically, you're not burning time on quotes for folks that aren't serious. Homeowners see a real number tied to their actual roof. That builds more trust than any figure you could quote cold over the phone.

### Frequently Asked Questions


#### **How accurate are Roofr's online estimates?**


Roofr's Instant Estimator tech is powered by the same satellite imagery that puts our[measurement reports](https://roofr.com/measurements) among the best in the industry.


‍ **‍** Quotes are displayed as price ranges based on the materials and pricing you've set in your account, so the numbers reflect your actual business. They're accurate enough to have a real pricing conversation while giving you room to finalize the quote after an inspection.


#### **Do online estimates replace in-person quotes?**


**‍** No, and in most cases they're not meant to. The Instant Estimator is a lead qualification and engagement tool to get homeowners invested and informed. You can still do an inspection and confirm scope later.


#### **Does an instant estimator help me get more leads?**


**‍** It doesn't replace your marketing. No magic shortcuts exist: you still need to drive traffic through ads, referrals, yard signs, or whatever's working for you.


What the Instant Estimator does is convert more of that traffic into actual leads. Instead of a homeowner bouncing, they get a real quote. They get value, they appreciate and trust your brand, you win the job.


#### **Can I use the Instant Estimator outside of my website?**


**‍** Heck yeah you can. Each Instant Estimator has a unique link you can share anywhere: social media,[your Google Business Profile](https://roofr.com/blog/roofers-guide-google-business-profile) , email campaigns, text messages, and so on. You can also generate a QR code from your link and add it to physical marketing materials.


#### **What happens to leads after they submit a quote?**


**‍** Lead details flow directly into your Roofr CRM, where you can see their contact info and the estimate they got. From there, you can follow up, assign the lead to a rep, and track them through your sales pipeline. If you're using Roofr's full platform, you can also move from lead to measurement to proposal without ever leaving the system.
