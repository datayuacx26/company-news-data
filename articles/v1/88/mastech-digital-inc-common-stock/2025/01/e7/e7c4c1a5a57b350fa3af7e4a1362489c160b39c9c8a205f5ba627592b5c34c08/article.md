### **What Are Project Portfolio Management Tools?**


Project Portfolio Management tools are software solutions designed to assist organizations in managing a collection of projects as a cohesive portfolio. Unlike traditional project management tools, PPM tools focus on broader objectives, such as strategic alignment, risk assessment, and portfolio performance optimization.


These tools provide insights into resource allocation, project viability, and potential risks, enabling decision-makers to make informed choices. According to a PMI report, organizations with mature PPM practices complete


[35% more of their programs](https://www.pmi.org/learning/thought-leadership/series/portfolio-management/delivering-on-strategy)


, fail less often, and waste less money. All of these benefits also indirectly result in improved customer satisfaction.
