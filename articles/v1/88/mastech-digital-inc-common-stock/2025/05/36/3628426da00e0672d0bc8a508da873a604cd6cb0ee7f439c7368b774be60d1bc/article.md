## What is Application Portfolio Management (APM)?


APM is a strategic discipline that gives organizations a structured approach to managing and assessing their application inventory. It enables leaders to evaluate each application’s value, cost, risk, and technical fit, providing a comprehensive view of where to invest, improve, or retire.


### Key aspects of APM:


- **Inventory and assessment:** APM begins with a comprehensive inventory of all applications, including their usage, performance, cost, and business impact.
- **Rationalization:** This involves analyzing the application portfolio to identify redundancies, obsolete applications, or underperforming software and then deciding whether to retire, consolidate, or upgrade them.
- **Optimization:** APM aims to optimize the application portfolio for efficiency and effectiveness by aligning applications with business goals, improving performance, and reducing costs.
- **Governance:** Establishing clear policies and procedures for managing the application lifecycle, including acquisition, deployment, maintenance, and retirement. ****
