# Axcelis Announces Timing and Availability of First Quarter 2026 Results and Conference Call

BEVERLY, Mass.


, April 23, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Axcelis Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: ACLS), a leading supplier of enabling ion implantation solutions for the semiconductor industry will release financial results for the first quarter of 2026 after market close on Thursday, May 7, 2026.


The Company will host a call to discuss the results for the first quarter of 2026 on Thursday, May 7, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. ET. The call will be available via webcast that can be accessed through the Investors page of Axcelis' website at[www.axcelis.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4671418-1&h=3814291731&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.axcelis.com%2F&a=www.axcelis.com) , or by registering as a participant here: **[https://register-conf.media-server.com/register/BIabf144ee757c4fccaceea99cf3cea2c9](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4671418-1&h=3786439707&u=https%3A%2F%2Fregister-conf.media-server.com%2Fregister%2FBIabf144ee757c4fccaceea99cf3cea2c9&a=https%3A%2F%2Fregister-conf.media-server.com%2Fregister%2FBIabf144ee757c4fccaceea99cf3cea2c9)** Webcast replays will be available for 30 days following the call.


**About Axcelis:**


Axcelis (Nasdaq: ACLS), headquartered in Beverly, Mass., has been providing innovative, high-productivity solutions for the semiconductor industry for over 45 years. Axcelis is dedicated to developing enabling process applications through the design, manufacture and[complete life cycle support](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4671418-1&h=2484393505&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.axcelis.com%2Fsupport-programs%2Fglobal-support-solutions&a=complete+life+cycle+support) of[ion implantation](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4671418-1&h=1650844618&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.axcelis.com%2Fproducts%2Fion-implant&a=ion+implantation) systems, one of the most critical and enabling steps in the IC manufacturing process. Learn more about Axcelis at[www.axcelis.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4671418-1&h=3814291731&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.axcelis.com%2F&a=www.axcelis.com) .
