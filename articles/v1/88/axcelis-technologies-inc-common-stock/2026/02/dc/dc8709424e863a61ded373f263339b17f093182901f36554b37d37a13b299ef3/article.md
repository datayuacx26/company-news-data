# Axcelis Announces Participation in Upcoming Investor Conferences

BEVERLY, Mass.


, Feb. 23, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Axcelis Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: ACLS), a leading supplier of enabling ion implantation solutions for the semiconductor industry, announced today the company's plans to participate in the following upcoming investor conferences:


- The Susquehanna Financial Group Fifteenth Annual Technology Conference, taking place at the Lotte New York Palace Hotel on February 26th and virtually on February 27th, 2026. Axcelis management will host one-on-one and small group meetings on Thursday, February 26th with interested investors. For more information on the conference, or to schedule a one-on-one meeting, please contact a Susquehanna Financial Group representative.
- The Morgan Stanley Technology, Media & Telecom Conference, taking place at the Palace Hotel, San Francisco, March 2 – 5, 2026. Axcelis management will host one-on-one and small group meetings on Tuesday, March 3rd with interested investors. For more information on the conference, or to schedule a one-on-one meeting, please contact a Morgan Stanley representative.
- The Loop Capital Markets 7th Annual Investor Conference, taking place virtually on March 9th, 2026, at the Lotte New York Palace Hotel on March 10th, 2026. Axcelis management will host virtual one-on-one and small group meetings on Monday, March 9th with interested investors. For more information on the conference, or to schedule a one-on-one meeting, please contact a Loop Capital Markets representative.
- The Stifel 2026 NYC Technology One-on-One Conference, taking place at The Conrad New York Downtown in New York City on March 10th, 2026. Axcelis management will host one-on-one and small group meetings with interested investors. For more information on the conference, or to schedule a one-on-one meeting, please contact a Stifel representative.
- The 2026 Cantor Global Technology & Industrial Growth Conference, taking place at the New York Hilton Midtown in New York City on March 9-11, 2026. Axcelis management will host one-on-one and small group meetings with interested investors on March 11th. For more information on the conference, or to schedule a one-on-one meeting, please contact a Cantor representative.


**About Axcelis:**


Axcelis (Nasdaq: ACLS), headquartered in Beverly, Mass., has been providing innovative, high-productivity solutions for the semiconductor industry for over 45 years. Axcelis is dedicated to developing enabling process applications through the design, manufacture and[complete life cycle support](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4624379-1&h=1894808255&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.axcelis.com%2Fsupport-programs%2Fglobal-support-solutions&a=complete+life+cycle+support) of[ion implantation](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4624379-1&h=2256621396&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.axcelis.com%2Fproducts%2Fion-implant&a=ion+implantation) systems, one of the most critical and enabling steps in the IC manufacturing process. Learn more about Axcelis at[www.axcelis.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4624379-1&h=129881485&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.axcelis.com%2F&a=www.axcelis.com) .
