# Notice to Shareholders Regarding Settlements

## Notice to Shareholders Regarding Settlements


Oct 27, 2025


[PDF Version](https://investors.originmaterials.com/node/10731/pdf)


WEST SACRAMENTO, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Oct. 27, 2025--[Origin Materials, Inc. (“Origin”)](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.originmaterials.com%2F&esheet=54345745&newsitemid=20251027393591&lan=en-US&anchor=Origin+Materials%2C+Inc.+%28%26%238220%3BOrigin%26%238221%3B%29&index=1&md5=c0b73f211b39b488e4ecd1427038e128) (Nasdaq: ORGN, ORGNW), a technology company with a mission to enable the world’s transition to sustainable materials, has entered into binding agreements to settle the shareholder class action lawsuit and the related derivative lawsuit pending in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California (the “Court”).


The proposed settlements resolve all claims asserted against Origin and the other named defendants in the lawsuits, without any liability or wrongdoing attributed to them or the Company. The shareholder class action lawsuit and related derivative lawsuit were initially filed in August 2023 and March 2025, respectively.


Each settlement agreement is subject to preliminary and final approval by the Court. Information regarding the proposed settlements and the approval process may be found in the[Settlement Agreement and Notice of Pendency](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.originmaterials.com%2Fstatic-files%2F7012163f-f472-478e-a457-597fbb4b19a0&esheet=54345745&newsitemid=20251027393591&lan=en-US&anchor=Settlement+Agreement+and+Notice+of+Pendency&index=2&md5=aa1450bf625806a651860ea676e8cce4) and[Proposed Settlement of Derivative Actions](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.originmaterials.com%2Fstatic-files%2F33c225ad-5376-4ee0-b2ea-85873d72744b&esheet=54345745&newsitemid=20251027393591&lan=en-US&anchor=Proposed+Settlement+of+Derivative+Actions&index=3&md5=d99edd70ccdc89a1e19261ae237ea4a8) .


**About Origin Materials**


Origin is a technology company with a mission to enable the world’s transition to sustainable materials. Our innovations include PET caps and closures that bring recycling circularity and enhanced performance to a ~$65 billion market, specialty materials, and our patented biomass conversion platform that transforms carbon into sustainable materials for a wide range of end products. For more information, visit[www.originmaterials.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.originmaterials.com&esheet=54345745&newsitemid=20251027393591&lan=en-US&anchor=www.originmaterials.com&index=4&md5=ffb5bc3876606ccfed61a760f96808d2) .
