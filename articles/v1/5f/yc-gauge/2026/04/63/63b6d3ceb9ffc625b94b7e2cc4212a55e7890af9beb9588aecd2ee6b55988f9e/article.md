# Track Branded Prompts with Gauge

Today we're launching Branded Prompts inside Gauge.


A non-branded prompt is the kind we've always measured. Something like "What are the best AI SEO platforms for reporting, monitoring, and visibility?" The KPI is visibility:[how often your brand gets mentioned](https://www.withgauge.com/blog/aeo-kpis-the-key-metrics-for-measuring-ai-search-performance) when the model decides who to talk about. Example from our own workspace: 16%.


A branded prompt flips that. You tell the model who to talk about and ask for its opinion. "How does Gauge help companies do AI SEO?" Visibility on a prompt like this is always 100% by definition. The model has to talk about you. The interesting question becomes how it talks about you.


##### How they're handled:


Because branded prompts are guaranteed to mention you, they would distort visibility scores if rolled in with everything else. So they're excluded by default from rankings and citations.


##### How to create them:


Adding a branded prompt is the same flow as adding a non-branded one. Click the Branded toggle when you add it — see our docs for[setting up branded prompts](https://docs.withgauge.com/help/branded-prompts) step by step. The[Gauge agent](https://www.withgauge.com/features/ask-gauge) is also fully aware of the distinction and will create branded prompts alongside non-branded ones when you ask it to expand your prompt set.


##### Why they matter:


Branded prompts are the engine behind our new[Sentiment Analysis feature](https://www.withgauge.com/features/sentiment-analysis) , which launches in tandem. They give you a direct read on[how AI describes your product](https://www.withgauge.com/blog/full-guide-to-tracking-and-fixing-ai-brand-sentiment) to buyers who already know your name, and what lens the model takes when those buyers ask about you specifically. Customers using this in beta have already turned it into a source of[content roadmap input](https://www.withgauge.com/features/content-engine) .
