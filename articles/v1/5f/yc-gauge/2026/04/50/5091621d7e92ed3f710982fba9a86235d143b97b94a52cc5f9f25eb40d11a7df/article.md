# Sentiment Analysis: See What AI Actually Thinks About You

Today we're launching[Sentiment Analysis](https://www.withgauge.com/features/sentiment-analysis) inside Gauge. It looks at the actual text of every answer we collect for you and surfaces what AI is saying about your brand, across every model we track.


##### How it works:


Sentiment Analysis runs across the full text of every answer we gather, both[branded and non-branded prompts](https://www.withgauge.com/blog/branded-prompt-tracking) , across every AI model. Not one prompt. Not the same prompt sampled 100 times. The full dataset of every response, every day. See our docs for[how to navigate Sentiment Analysis in Gauge](https://docs.withgauge.com/navigating/sentiment-analysis) , from the sentiment timeline to the model-by-model split.


##### What you'll see:


• Sentiment over time with positive, negative, and neutral distribution


• A model-by-model split, so you can tell which platforms are favorable and which aren't


• Key findings: the actual narrative themes AI is using to describe your brand, surfaced through full-text search across every response


• Pros and cons with direct quotes from the models, so you can see exactly which lines are shaping the narrative


• Two plans set up by default for every customer: Strengths and Weaknesses, and Competitive Positioning

##### What this gives you:


A direct read on[how AI is positioning your brand](https://www.withgauge.com/blog/full-guide-to-tracking-and-fixing-ai-brand-sentiment) , with the receipts to back it up. The actual themes, the actual quotes, and the actual model-by-model differences.
