# Introducing Reddit Citation Tracking

Reddit is one of the most-cited sources inside AI answers. Today we're rolling out Reddit Citation Tracking inside Gauge.


You can now see exactly which subreddits and threads are driving citations in your space, and which ones your brand is missing from.


##### Subreddits view:


The most impactful subreddits leading to citations across the prompts you track, ranked by their contribution to visibility in your category.


##### URLs view:


Every Reddit thread cited in your space, with citation rate, change in visibility over time, and the brands mentioned inside the thread. Click any URL to see every prompt it was cited for.[Compare your coverage against competitors](https://www.withgauge.com/features/competitor-analysis) and filter by topic, date, and AI platform.


##### Two top-line metrics:


• Reddit[citation rate](https://www.withgauge.com/blog/aeo-kpis-the-key-metrics-for-measuring-ai-search-performance) : how often[reddit.com](http://reddit.com/) is cited across your tracked prompts.


• Reddit[brand mention rate](https://www.withgauge.com/blog/ai-brand-mentions-per-answer) : of the times Reddit is cited, what percent of those threads actually mention your brand.


See our docs for[a full walkthrough of the Reddit views and metrics](https://docs.withgauge.com/navigating/reddit) .


Live now at[withgauge.com](http://withgauge.com/) .
