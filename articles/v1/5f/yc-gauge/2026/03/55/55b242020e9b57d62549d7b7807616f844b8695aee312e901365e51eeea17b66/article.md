# Ask Gauge: The Agentic Marketer

With user behavior now spread across traditional and AI search, it’s never been more important to ensure that your brand is the answer. There’s a wealth of data distributed across GA4, GSC, keywords, prompts, ads, and more. This data is incredibly rich, but fragmented and complicated to understand.


Gauge unifies all of these data sources into a single agent that can do the work of an entire marketing team. Let’s take a look at how this works for one of our customers,[Braintrust](https://www.braintrust.dev/) :


We started by building a best-in-class[GEO/AEO platform](https://www.withgauge.com/geo) , with a core focus on getting the right data. We quickly found that data alone isn’t enough. Our users kept asking:


1. How can I make sense of all of this prompt/answer data?
2. How does it relate to my existing data?
3. What are the actual things I need do to move the needle?


Our agent interprets all of your data sources and executes high value work for you — see our docs for[how to work with Ask Gauge](https://docs.withgauge.com/navigating/ask-gauge) day to day. Once Gauge has all the data it needs, it can build and execute plans of attack,[recommending and drafting focused content](https://www.withgauge.com/features/content-engine) . Last but not least, Gauge lives where you already work, with[MCP](https://www.withgauge.com/blog/gauge-mcp) and slack bot support.
