# Google Ads is Now Live in Gauge

Today we're launching the Google Ads integration inside Gauge.


#### How to connect:


Settings → Integrations → Google → Authorize Google Ads. Pick the ad accounts you want to sync and save. Multiple accounts are supported. If the Ads scope wasn't part of your original Google authorization, a one-click reauth covers it. Heads up: disconnecting Google Ads also removes linked GA4 and Search Console scopes under the same Google account, so plan accordingly.


#### What data comes through:


• Campaign level: spend, budget, impressions, clicks, CTR, conversions, cost per conversion


• Ad group level: performance breakdowns, impression share, budget efficiency


• Keyword level: exact keywords triggering your ads, match types, quality scores, search impression share


• Ad copy level: headlines, descriptions, RSA asset ratings (Low / Good / Best), and which combinations are converting


• Device and audience: performance splits by mobile vs desktop and audience segment


• Search terms report: the actual queries people typed that triggered your ads, viewable next to your[AI visibility](https://www.withgauge.com/ai-visibility) data


‍


#### Your Google Ads data now sits alongside AI visibility, citations, and organic search inside the same workspace. That means you can see, in one view:


• Keywords where AI already mentions your brand organically and you're also paying to show up


• Topics where paid is your only presence (no AI visibility, no organic ranking), surfaced as content gap signals


•[Branded vs non-branded](https://www.withgauge.com/blog/full-guide-to-tracking-and-fixing-ai-brand-sentiment) paid efficiency next to your unprompted AI mention rate


•[Competitor keyword bidding](https://www.withgauge.com/features/competitor-analysis) alongside your AI visibility trends on the same terms


• The search terms report as a feed into your[Gauge prompt set](https://www.withgauge.com/features/prompt-tracking) , in real buyer language


Live now. Go to Settings → Integrations → Google to connect your account.
