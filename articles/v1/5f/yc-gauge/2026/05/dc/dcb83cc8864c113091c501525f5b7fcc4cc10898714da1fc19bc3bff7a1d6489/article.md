# ChatGPT Ads Just Went Public

ChatGPT Ads just opened to the public. Any US business can now run paid placements inside ChatGPT directly at[ads.openai.com](http://ads.openai.com/) , without an agency or a minimum spend.


#### What's live:


• Two bidding models: cost-per-impression and cost-per-click


• Ads appear below ChatGPT answers, matched to the intent of the conversation


• Conversion tracking and pixel-based measurement are part of the launch


• Free and Go tier users see ads. Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise remain ad-free


• OpenAI states the ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers and that conversations remain private from advertisers


This opens a new paid surface inside one of the largest AI platforms in the world: 2 billion queries per month, with the first ad placements any business can buy inside generative AI answers.
