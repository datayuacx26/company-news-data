- Google searches are falling for the first time ever (and Google itself is moving to AI answers)
- Gartner predicts AI chatbots will capture 25% of search volume by next year


With traffic leaving search, traditional SEO is dying. Our customers are seeing inbound AI traffic **double every quarter** .
