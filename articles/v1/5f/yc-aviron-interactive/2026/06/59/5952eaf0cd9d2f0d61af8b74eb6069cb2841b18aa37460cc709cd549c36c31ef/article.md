## Why Does Entertainment Drive Retention Better Than Willpower?


Fitness entertainment removes the decision entirely. Standard home cardio asks you to want to exercise — an ask most people lose most nights. Aviron's format doesn't ask.


When the choice on the screen is a class, a race, a scenery ride, or an episode of your show, the decision stops being "do I want to work out." It becomes "what do I want to do for the next 30 minutes." The rowing is just the controller.


This is why the same member who abandoned a treadmill will stay on Aviron. The treadmill was the workout. The Aviron session is the show, the game, the class, or the race. The physical effort is the same. The format around the effort is different, and the format is the reason people come back.
