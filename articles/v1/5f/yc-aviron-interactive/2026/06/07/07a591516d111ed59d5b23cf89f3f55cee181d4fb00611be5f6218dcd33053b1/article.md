# Aviron Membership: What's Included | Aviron

An Aviron membership is $29 a month billed annually and gives you Aviron World: a library of 1,000+ workouts across eight categories, multiplayer and single-player games, live and on-demand classes, guided training programs, scenic rows, monthly team competitions, and streaming from Netflix, YouTube, and Hulu. One membership also covers unlimited family profiles, so everyone in your home trains under the same fee with their own stats.


Here is what comes in the box, so to speak:


- **1,000+ workouts** across eight categories, refreshed over time
- **Games and competitions** built into the workout, not bolted on after
- **Classes and programs** from Aviron instructors and partners
- **Streaming** while the machine tracks your live metrics
- **Unlimited family profiles** under one membership


## **What workouts and content come with the membership?**


The core of the membership is variety, because variety is what keeps a routine from going stale. You get more than 1,000 workouts spread across eight categories, which means you rarely face the same session twice unless you want to. There are coached classes led by Aviron instructors, guided programs with real structure like the 12-week Row Your Fat Off and HIIT Climb, and scenic destinations with Live Terrain that adjusts resistance and incline to match the route.


This is the engine behind consistency. When every workout feels like a fresh choice rather than a chore you already know by heart, you show up more often. And showing up more often is the whole point, because results come from the workouts you actually do, not the ones you planned.


## **What about the games and competition?**


This is where Aviron is genuinely different. The games were built into the platform from day one by Unity developers, so they are real games you play by rowing, not a points overlay slapped on a basic workout. You race, you strategize, and you forget you are exercising, which is exactly the idea.


On the competitive side, the membership includes monthly Team Challenges that span every machine type, live multiplayer racing where you go head to head with other members, and global leaderboards to chase. There is also a streak system with Streak Freeze you earn at milestones, plus achievements and Aviron Coins to collect. It adds up to a reason to come back tomorrow, which is worth more than any single feature.


## **Does one membership really cover the whole family?**


Yes. One membership, unlimited profiles, no per-person fee. Your partner, your kids, and you each get your own profile with your own history, your own streaks, and your own place on the leaderboard, all under the same $29 a month. Most connected fitness brands charge per account or push you toward a second membership. Aviron includes the household.


That changes the real cost per person. Split one membership across three rowers and you are under $10 a month each for the full platform. For a home where more than one person actually uses the machine, this is often the feature that makes the whole thing worth it. We dig into the household angle in our guide on[Aviron for families](https://www.avironactive.com/membership/) .


## **What does the membership cost, and can you cancel?**


The annual plan is $29 a month, billed once a year, which saves you around $60 compared with paying month to month and works out to roughly two months free. Prefer flexibility? The monthly plan is $34 with no commitment, and you can cancel anytime. There is also a perpetual option at a one-time $2,499 if you would rather own the membership outright and never pay monthly again.


One more thing worth saying plainly: you do not lose your machine if you ever stop paying. Without a membership, the rower still tracks your metrics, history, and badges through the MyAviron companion app. The membership is the entertainment on top, not the key to the door. ## **Frequently asked questions**


### **How many workouts does the membership include?**


More than 1,000, across eight categories, with new content added over time.


### **Can I stream shows while I row?**


Yes. The membership includes streaming from Netflix, YouTube, and Hulu on the machine, and it tracks your live metrics while you watch.


### **Is there an extra charge for more family members?**


No. Unlimited family profiles are included in a single membership.


### **Can I cancel the membership later?**


Yes. The monthly plan has no commitment and can be cancelled anytime. The machine keeps its core tracking features even without a membership.


One membership, the whole world of workouts, and room for everyone under your roof. That is the deal.
