# Do You Need a Subscription for Aviron? | Aviron

No, you do not need a subscription to use an Aviron. The machine works without a membership, and you still keep your workout history, live metrics, resistance control, and achievement badges through the MyAviron companion app on iOS and Android. What a membership adds is the entertainment layer on top: the full library of 1,000+ workouts, the games, the live and on-demand classes, the team competitions, and streaming from Netflix, YouTube, and Hulu.


So the real question is not whether the machine still functions without paying monthly. It does. The question is whether you want the full Aviron World experience or a tracked, no-frills row. Both are real options, and you are never locked out of equipment you paid for.


## **What can you do on an Aviron without a membership?**


Quite a lot, actually. Strip away the membership and you still have a fully working connected rower. Through the MyAviron companion app, the free experience includes:


- Your complete workout history, saved and tracked over time
- Live workout metrics while you row, so you can see output, pace, and effort
- Full resistance control, so you set the challenge yourself
- Achievement badges as you hit milestones
- Your own profile, so your numbers stay yours


That is a real rowing setup, not a stripped demo. If your goal is to get on the machine, push hard, and watch your numbers climb week over week, you can do that without paying a cent beyond the hardware. Plenty of people row this way and stick with it. If you want to make those sessions count, our guide on[how to use a rowing machine](https://www.avironactive.com/blog/how-to-use-a-rowing-machine/) covers form and pacing.


## **What does the membership add?**


The membership turns a good rower into Aviron World, the full fitness entertainment platform. This is the part designed to make you forget you are exercising. With a membership you get:


- 1,000+ workouts across eight categories, from coached classes to scenic rows
- Multiplayer and single-player games built into the workout
- Guided programs like the 12-week Row Your Fat Off and HIIT Climb
- Monthly Team Challenges and live races against other members
- Streaming from Netflix, YouTube, and Hulu while the machine tracks your session


This is where the consistency comes from. When the workout is genuinely fun, you show up more often, and showing up more often is what produces results. Aviron reports a 92% one-year retention rate, which is the kind of number you only hit when people actually want to come back. The free tier keeps you rowing. The membership keeps you hooked.


## **Is there a free trial of the membership?**


There is no free trial of the membership itself. What Aviron offers instead is a 30-day risk-free home trial on the hardware. You set the machine up in your own space, row on it for a month, and if it is not for you, you return it with free returns and no restocking or shipping fees.


That structure says something about how Aviron thinks about the free tier. The no-membership experience is deliberately generous, with all your metrics and history included, because a credible free option makes the paid one feel more honest, not less. You are not being nudged toward a subscription to make the machine usable. You are choosing whether you want the entertainment on top.


## **Who should skip the membership, and who should keep it?**


Skip it, at least at first, if you are a self-directed rower who likes setting your own resistance, following your own plan, and tracking your own numbers. The free tier covers all of that. You can always add the membership later if you find yourself wanting more variety.


Keep it if you know you need the workout to be fun to stay consistent, if more than one person in your home will row, since unlimited family profiles come with one membership, or if you want classes, programs, and competition to push you. For most households that actually want to use the machine often, the membership is what makes it stick. Our[full breakdown of what the membership includes](https://www.avironactive.com/blog/aviron-membership-whats-included/) goes through everything in the library, and[the membership page](https://www.avironactive.com/membership/) has current pricing.


## **Frequently asked questions**


### **Will the rower stop working if I cancel my membership?**


No. The machine keeps working with metrics, history, resistance control, and badges through the MyAviron app. Only the membership-only content goes away.


### **Does the free tier cost anything?**


No. The free experience is included with the machine. You only pay for the membership if you want the full workout library, games, classes, and streaming.


### **Can I try the membership before paying for it?**


There is no free membership trial, but the hardware comes with a 30-day risk-free home trial, so you can return the machine within 30 days if it is not for you.


### **Do I need the membership for more than one person to use the machine?**


No for basic tracking, since profiles are part of the free tier. If you want the full library for everyone, one membership covers unlimited family profiles.


You own the machine, so you own the choice. Start free, add the full world when you want it, and keep rowing either way.
