## High protein for muscle gain vs. fat loss


Optimizing your body composition requires distinct caloric strategies depending on your metabolic phase. A successful lean bulk demands a controlled calorie surplus to fuel muscle tissue synthesis, while a disciplined cut requires a calorie deficit to drive fat oxidation. Despite these opposite energy pathways, your underlying demand for high-quality protein remains high to prevent muscle wasting and support recovery.


To protect your hard-earned lean mass, nutrition researchers like Alan Aragon recommend maintaining a daily protein intake of 0.7 to 1 gram per pound of body weight. During a caloric deficit, high protein intake preserves nitrogen balance and shields muscle tissue from being broken down for energy. During a surplus, this same protein intake maximizes muscle protein synthesis. Hitting these precise macronutrient thresholds is critical, but it often becomes a logistical headache of constant meal prep and compromised ingredient quality.
