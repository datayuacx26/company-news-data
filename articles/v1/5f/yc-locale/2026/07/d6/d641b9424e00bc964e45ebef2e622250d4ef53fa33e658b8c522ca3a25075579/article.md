# Under 560 Calories: What Locale's Low-Calorie Option Includes

## What Under 560 Calories Means in Practice


Managing daily energy intake requires exact numbers rather than vague claims. In clinical trials on human energy restriction, such as the NIH CALERIE study, structured moderate calorie reduction of approximately 14% yielded measurable metabolic benefits without compromising tissue function[\[1\]](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/calorie-restriction-immune-function-health-span) . To support targeted energy control, our Certified Clean High Protein Meal Subscription provides two distinct portion tiers across our rotating weekly menu.


While our standard option provides 650 to 850 calories per meal to fuel active energy needs, our lower-calorie option strictly caps total energy under 560 calories


. Rather than shrinking overall food volume or reducing essential protein, we achieve this lower threshold by adjusting macro composition: reducing starchy carb portions and substituting extra fiber-dense, organic vegetables sourced directly from local California partner farms.

Standard Calorie Option 650 - 850 cal Balanced complex carbs, 40-60g protein Moderate to large appetites and fuel for active daily schedules


Lower Calorie Option Under 560 cal Lower carbs, extra fiber-dense veggies, 40-60g protein Targeted calorie deficit and post-prandial blood sugar management


This intentional macro adjustment allows individuals managing a medical calorie deficit or controlling post-meal glucose spikes to maintain satiating meal volume without overshooting daily energy targets.

## How Calories Come Down Without Sacrificing Satiety


Reducing meal energy without leaving you hungry comes down to smart culinary restructuring. In our lower-calorie meal options, we reduce the portion of high-glycemic grains like rice or barley while expanding the portion of fiber-rich organic vegetables. By holding the core protein source steady at 40 to 60 grams per meal, we maintain the essential amino acid intake required for muscle preservation and metabolic support while dropping overall energy content under 560 calories.


- High-Glycemic Grain Trim: Rice or grain portions are scaled back, lowering overall energy density without dropping total food volume.
- Fibrous Vegetable Expansion: Extra volumes of organic kale, roasted cauliflower, or crisp cabbage add physical bulk, key micronutrients, and dietary fiber.
- Sustained Protein Floor: Meals preserve 40 to 60 grams of quality protein from named partners like Mary's Free Range Chicken or wild-caught shrimp.


Fat quality also plays a direct role in how your body processes energy. We never cook with industrial seed oils, preparing every dressing and sauce exclusively with extra-virgin olive oil and avocado oil. Soluble dietary fiber dissolves in water to form a gel-like material in the stomach, slowing down digestion and helping to lower blood sugar[\[3\]](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/nutrition-and-healthy-eating/in-depth/fiber/art-20043983) . This ensures smooth, predictable energy rather than the sharp glucose spikes and crashes common in standard takeaway meals.


Clean preparation requires total transparency from kitchen to table. Every meal recipe undergoes independent third-party lab testing by Light Labs for heavy metals, pesticides, PFAS, and microplastics


. To eliminate microplastic contamination during reheating, we package meals in reusable glass jars for weekly home delivery across the San Francisco Bay Area and Los Angeles. You get precise caloric control and verifiable purity in every jar.
