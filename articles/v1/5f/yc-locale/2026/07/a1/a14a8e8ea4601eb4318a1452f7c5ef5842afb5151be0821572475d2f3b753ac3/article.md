# Standard or Low-Calorie: Which Meal Option Should You Pick?

## Standard vs. Low-Calorie: The Two Options Side by Side


When you[build your weekly plan](https://www.shoplocale.com/pages/select-plan) , you are not locked into a single calorie bracket for the whole order. The Standard and Low-Calorie settings are a per-meal toggle, available on most of the weekly rotating menu. This allows busy professionals across the San Francisco Bay Area and Los Angeles to easily customize their nutrition to fit changing schedules and physical activity levels.

## Who Should Pick Standard: Fueling Active and Demanding Workdays


Maintaining peak cognitive performance during a demanding twelve-hour workday requires clean, reliable fuel. The human brain accounts for only two percent of body weight but consumes twenty percent of the body's energy[\[1\]](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC124895/) , making adequate caloric intake critical for sustained focus. If you regularly engage in deep work, lead lengthy strategy sessions, or transition straight from the office to the gym, your body will burn through low-calorie meals too quickly, leading to afternoon brain fog and fatigue.


Locale's weekly plan is built to flex instead. You can customize your[weekly rotating menu](https://www.shoplocale.com/pages/menu) meal by meal, choosing the Standard calorie option on days when you need robust cognitive and physical stamina. These high-protein meals contain 40 to 60 grams of quality protein and slow-burning carbohydrates like bone broth-infused rice, cooked only with olive and avocado oil, never seed oils. Every meal is packaged in microplastic-free reusable glass jars.


The Standard calorie option is ideal for:


- Professionals managing active daily schedules or high-intensity exercise routines
- Individuals who need sustained mental stamina to handle back-to-back meetings
- Anyone with a larger appetite looking for satisfying, clean, high-protein portions


## Who Should Pick Low-Calorie: Portions Optimized for Clean Weight Management


If you are looking to manage weight cleanly, maintain a steady caloric deficit, or simply prefer lighter evening meals that won't disrupt your sleep, the Low-Calorie option is the one to pick


. You toggle it meal by meal rather than committing to it across your whole order. Every low-calorie meal stays under 560 calories by replacing a portion of the starches with organic vegetables from California partner farms such as Lakeside Organic Gardens in Watsonville


.


This lower energy-density swap keeps you fully satisfied without the typical post-meal slump, relying on whole produce to provide natural, blood-sugar-stabilizing dietary fiber. The packaging is worth a mention too, since a lighter meal is still a reheated one: Locale delivers in reusable glass jars rather than plastic trays, and picks them up, sanitizes and reuses them each week.


- Active weight loss: Effortlessly maintain your caloric deficit while hitting your clean eating targets.
- Lighter dinners: Avoid heavy starches in the evening to improve metabolic rest and overall sleep quality[\[2\]](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7215804/) .
- Sedentary days: Scale down your energy intake on rest days or during long office stretches without sacrificing nutrition.

## Frequently asked questions


What is the calorie difference between the Standard and Low-Calorie options?


Locale's Standard meals contain between 650 and 850 calories per serving, making them perfect for those with moderate to active daily fuel requirements. The Low-Calorie option is designed for weight management and lighter dining, staying under 560 calories per serving by replacing a portion of carbs with nutrient-dense, organic vegetables from California partner farms.


Do Low-Calorie meals contain less protein than the Standard meals?


The Low-Calorie setting reduces the carb portion and adds vegetables, so what changes most is calories and carbs. Protein varies meal by meal rather than following one fixed rule, so check the label on the live weekly menu for the exact macros of the meal you're picking — every meal lists calories, protein, carbs and fiber.


Can I mix both Standard and Low-Calorie options in the same weekly delivery?


Yes. Because the Low-Calorie setting is a per-meal toggle and not a separate plan, you can mix both options in one weekly delivery. Choose a plan size from 4 to 20 meals and apply the low-calorie option to the specific meals where you want a lighter, lower-carb portion.


How does Locale keep meals high-protein and nutrient-dense without heavy calories?


We use functional, whole-food inclusions rather than processed fillers to pack in nutrition. For example, we integrate bone broth-infused rice, edamame noodles, and Greek yogurt-based sauces to ensure every meal delivers 40 to 60 grams of protein and over 8 grams of fiber, stabilizing blood sugar and keeping you full longer.


How does the Locale subscription work, and can I cancel anytime?


Locale operates on a flexible weekly plan with sizes ranging from 4 to 20 meals delivered every Monday in insulated tote bags that stay cold for up to 4 hours. There are no long-term commitments: you can skip a week or cancel entirely at any time through your account dashboard.


Where does Locale source its ingredients, and are they tested for toxins?


We partner with premium California suppliers like Mary's Free Range Chicken, Lakeside Organic Gardens, and Monterey Fish Market. To ensure uncompromising safety, our meals are independently third-party lab-tested by Light Labs for heavy metals, pesticides, phthalates, PFAS, and other toxins, with the results published. We also pack our meals in plastic-free, reusable glass jars.
