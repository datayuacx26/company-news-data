Alab SF is a San Francisco-based culinary service delivering organic, chef-crafted meals that are gluten free and dairy free[\[1\]](https://alabsf.net/) . Delivery days run Sunday from 1 PM to 7:30 PM and Wednesday from 10 AM to 3:30 PM, so clients can receive fresh meals twice a week[\[1\]](https://alabsf.net/) . The kitchen works with trusted farms and states that if an ingredient is not organic or sustainable, it is not used; cooking fats are limited to extra virgin olive oil, coconut oil, and pasture-raised beef tallow, with sesame oil reserved for flavor rather than cooking[\[1\]](https://alabsf.net/) .


### Customizable Macros and Glassware Pickup


For active individuals tracking precise macro intakes, Alab SF publishes a per-portion breakdown of its individual meals, each built from 4 oz of protein, 5 oz of grains, beans, or roasted vegetables, and 5 oz of seasonal sautéed greens[\[1\]](https://alabsf.net/) . A 4 oz portion of salmon provides 25g of protein and 233 calories, 4 oz of beef yields 31g of protein, and 4 oz of chicken delivers 25.9g of protein[\[1\]](https://alabsf.net/) . Carbohydrate components like sprouted brown rice and black rice are listed in the same 5 oz portions, with sprouted brown rice at 7.5g of protein and 5g of fiber[\[1\]](https://alabsf.net/) .


To keep packaging out of the landfill, Alab SF delivers meals in glassware inside reusable insulated bags with ice bottles that the team picks up the following week[\[1\]](https://alabsf.net/) . Clients schedule a pickup and set the bags and glassware by the door, and collection runs Wednesdays from 10:30 AM to 3:30 PM and Sundays from 1:00 PM to 7:30 PM[\[4\]](https://alabsf.net/glassware-pickup-1) . The company states meals stay fresh for up to 4 hours outside with the ice packs or bottles, and the closed-loop return keeps single-use plastic out of the process[\[1\]](https://alabsf.net/) .

## Plentiful Kitchen: Zero Seed Oils and Hormone-Friendly Sourcing


Plentiful Kitchen is a San Francisco Bay Area prepared meal service built on real, nutrient-dense ingredients, with glass-only packaging the founder frames as better for your hormones[\[3\]](https://plentifulkitchen.com/) . Founder Chef Inbal started the kitchen after recovering her own health through whole foods, with the stated intention of helping moms find relief around food and feeding their families, and every meal is built on organic, non-GMO produce from local farms, organic grass-fed and finished meats, and wild-caught fish[\[3\]](https://plentifulkitchen.com/) .


### Glass Packaging and Weekly Delivery Structure


A cornerstone of Plentiful Kitchen's philosophy is an absolute ban on industrial seed oils: the kitchen states "no seed oils, ever"[\[3\]](https://plentifulkitchen.com/) . All warm cooking is performed exclusively with avocado oil, while cold dressings and sauces utilize cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil[\[3\]](https://plentifulkitchen.com/) . Every meal is naturally gluten-free and dairy-free[\[3\]](https://plentifulkitchen.com/) .


Plentiful Kitchen delivers fresh meals every Tuesday across the San Francisco Bay Area[\[3\]](https://plentifulkitchen.com/) . To minimize exposure to toxic compounds and[microplastics](https://www.shoplocale.com/blogs/standards/microplastics-and-heavy-metals-in-prepared-meals-what-to-know) , meals arrive in glass containers with no plastic anywhere, and empties are left out with the next delivery to be collected, washed, and reused[\[3\]](https://plentifulkitchen.com/) . Clients on an ongoing weekly plan save $10 every week, which the kitchen states adds up to $520 per year[\[3\]](https://plentifulkitchen.com/) .
