# Locale's Banned Ingredients

At Locale, we have put hundreds of hours into researching the ingredients that go into our meals, making sure that everything we put into our food was optimized for your performance today and health tomorrow.


Over time, we developed a list of banned ingredients, or ingredients that we would never use in our food. This list is expanded over time with the most up to date research on health and nutrition.


Last Edited: August 2025


##

-


Acesulfame-K (acesulfame potassium)


-


Aspartame


-


Cyclamates


-


Saccharin


-

-


High fructose corn syrup


-

-

-

-

-

Sugar alcohols (sorbitol, xylitol, erythritol, maltitol, mannitol)

Titanium dioxide (TiO₂)
