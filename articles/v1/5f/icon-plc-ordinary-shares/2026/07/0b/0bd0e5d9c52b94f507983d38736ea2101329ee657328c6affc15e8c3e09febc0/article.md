# ICON schedules second quarter 2026 earnings conference call

**Dublin, Ireland, July 22, 2026** –[ICON plc](https://www.iconplc.com/) , **(NASDAQ: ICLR)** a world-leading clinical research organisation, today announced that it will release its financial results for the second quarter 2026 after the market closes on Wednesday, July 29, 2026. The company will hold a conference call and webcast to discuss its financial results and performance on Thursday, July 30, 2026 at 8:00am ET.


Any changes to these events and links to the live webcasts (where available) will be posted on the Investor section of our website under “[Events](https://investor.iconplc.com/events-presentations) ”. A webcast replay of the conference call will be available approximately one hour following the conclusion of the call.


**About ICON plc**


ICON plc is a world-leading clinical research organisation. Offering deep operational and medical expertise we accelerate innovation, driving emerging therapies forward to improve patient outcomes. From molecule to medicine, we deliver integrated consulting, clinical development, commercialisation and post-marketing solutions to pharmaceutical, biotechnology, medical device, government and public health organisations worldwide. With headquarters in Dublin, Ireland, ICON employed approximately 40,350 employees in 97 locations in 55 countries as at March 31, 2026. For further information about ICON, visit:[www.iconplc.com](https://www.iconplc.com/) .
