### Why Europe, not America, may be Chinese sponsors’ smartest move


As Chinese sponsors expand globally, Europe offers a stable, science-driven regulatory environment and broad market access across the region. Planning for EMA requirements from the outset helps accelerate approvals, minimise costly study adaptations and support long-term commercial success.
