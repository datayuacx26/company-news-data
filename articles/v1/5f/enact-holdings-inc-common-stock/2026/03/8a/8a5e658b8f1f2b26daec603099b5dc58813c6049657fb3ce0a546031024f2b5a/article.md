# Enact Releases 2025 Sustainability Report

[PDF Version](https://ir.enactmi.com/node/11861/pdf)


RALEIGH, N.C., March 25, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- **Enact Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: ACT) (Enact)** today announced the release of its 2025 Sustainability Report covering the calendar year 2025.


The 2025 report builds on Enact’s longstanding commitment to transparency and long-term value creation for stakeholders. It provides updated insights into the company’s approach to sustainability, including ongoing efforts related to community impact, employee engagement and development, governance, and risk management. The report also reflects continued engagement with internal and external stakeholders to ensure Enact’s sustainability priorities remain aligned with its business strategy.


“On behalf of everyone at Enact, I am pleased to share our 2025 Sustainability Report,” said Rohit Gupta, President and CEO of Enact. “Our mission to help people responsibly achieve and sustain homeownership goes hand in hand with how we invest in our employees, support our communities, and govern our business. This report highlights how we continue to make progress across these areas while staying focused on long-term, sustainable performance for all our stakeholders.”


Enact’s 2025 Sustainability Report is available on Enact’s Investor Relations website at[https://ir.enactmi.com/sustainability](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=YDeubWarylCyCm8QLfR1McTBmt4a6-dUxnkOstlW8JJCtekcNWYJjiF533gojoAsElFMKwnbkHw__2TSHmTAFO2ltX_J5c2E_5HkdBpJH2mseRCMy1duA4pHlm8Yf2p-oCF_cIKNsVEDcx6kAU5iDg==) .
