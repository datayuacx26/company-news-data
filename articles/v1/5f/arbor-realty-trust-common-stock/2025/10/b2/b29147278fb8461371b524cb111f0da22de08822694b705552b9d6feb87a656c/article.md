# Jean-Laurent Pouliot Named GlobeSt. 2025 Influencer in Multifamily Real Estate

Jean-Laurent Pouliot, Managing Director, Senior Production Officer, was recognized as a GlobeSt. 2025 Influencer in Multifamily Real Estate. Pouliot was selected along with other notable professionals and pioneers in the multifamily industry, including developers, investors, brokers and individuals who introduced new concepts and products.


Pouliot exemplifies Arbor’s entrepreneurial and innovative approach to multifamily lending. Most recently, he led the launch of Arbor’s pioneering[Proprietary Preferred Equity](https://arbor.com/freddie-mac/proprietary-preferred-equity-behind-conventional-loans/) product behind Freddie Mac Conventional Loans. This unique program allows borrowers to simultaneously secure a senior loan and preferred equity in one transaction while working with a single, experienced team all the way through servicing without interruption. Arbor was the first lender to offer this financing option, filling a critical market gap by providing sponsors with a flexible, cost-effective capital solution that complements Freddie Mac’s senior debt.


Through strategic foresight and first-class execution, Pouliot continues to shape the future of multifamily real estate.
