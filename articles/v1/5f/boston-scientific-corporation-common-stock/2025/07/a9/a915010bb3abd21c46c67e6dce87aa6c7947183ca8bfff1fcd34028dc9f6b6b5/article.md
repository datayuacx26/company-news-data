# Interested in our Q2 2025 earnings?

Today, Boston Scientific shared its financial results for the second quarter of 2025.


Overall, we generated net sales of $5.061 billion during the second quarter of 2025, growing 22.8 percent on a reported basis and 17.4 percent on an organic basis1 compared to the prior year period.


We also achieved net sales growth in each of our reportable segments, with 7 percent organic growth1 in MedSurg and 23.2 percent organic growth1 in Cardiovascular compared to the prior year period.


"This was another excellent quarter — marked by exceptional top-line performance — that delivered margin expansion and prioritized investment for future growth,” says Mike Mahoney, chairman and chief executive officer, Boston Scientific. “I am incredibly grateful to our dedicated global team for demonstrating clinical and commercial excellence across the company and positioning us for differentiated long-term performance.”


**Additional Q2 highlights:**


- We received[U.S. Food and Drug Administration approval](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-07-07-Boston-Scientific-receives-FDA-approval-for-expanded-labeling-of-FARAPULSE-TM-Pulsed-Field-Ablation-System) to expand instructions for use labeling to include the treatment of drug refractory, symptomatic persistent atrial fibrillation (AF) with the FARAPULSE™ Pulsed Field Ablation (PFA) System.
- We also received CE mark for the[WATCHMAN FLX™ Pro Left Atrial Appendage Closure Device](https://news.bostonscientific.com/watchman-flx-pro-left-atrial-appendage-closure-implant) , which is optimized for healing and designed to improve visualization during device placement and treat a broader range of patient anatomies.
- We completed the acquisition of[Intera Oncology® Inc.](https://www.interaoncology.com/) , a medical device company that provides the Intera 3000 Hepatic Artery Infusion Pump and floxuridine, a chemotherapy drug.
- We also completed the acquisition of[SoniVie Ltd.](https://sonivie.com/) , the developer of the TIVUS™ Intravascular Ultrasound System, an investigational renal nerve denervation technology designed to treat hypertension.2


For the full Boston Scientific earnings announcement and more business highlights from the second quarter of 2025, see the[press release](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-07-23-Boston-Scientific-announces-results-for-second-quarter-2025) .
