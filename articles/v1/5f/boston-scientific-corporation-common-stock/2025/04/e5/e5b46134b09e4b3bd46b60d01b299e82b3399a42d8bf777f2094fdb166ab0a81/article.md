# Interested in our Q1 2025 earnings?

Today, Boston Scientific shared its financial results for the first quarter of 2025.


Overall, we generated net sales of $4.663 billion during the first quarter of 2025, growing 20.9 percent on a reported basis and 18.2 percent on an organic basis1 compared to the prior year period.


We also achieved net sales growth in each of our reportable segments, with 5.3 percent organic growth1 in MedSurg and 25.6 percent organic growth1 in Cardiovascular compared to the prior year period.


"We delivered an exceptional quarter to start the year, reflecting the effectiveness of our highly engaged global team and the strength of our product portfolio,” says Mike Mahoney, chairman and chief executive officer, Boston Scientific. "We remain well-positioned for the future as we continue to focus on meaningful innovation, clinical science and the execution of our category leadership strategy to drive differentiated growth and performance for the long-term.”


**Additional Q1 highlights:**


- We completed the acquisition of[Bolt Medical, Inc.](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-01-08-Boston-Scientific-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-Bolt-Medical,-Inc) , the developer of an intravascular lithotripsy advanced laser-based platform for the treatment of coronary and peripheral artery disease.
- We announced our agreement to acquire[SoniVie Ltd.](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-03-03-Boston-Scientific-announces-agreement-to-acquire-SoniVie-Ltd) , the developer of the TIVUS™ Intravascular Ultrasound System, an investigational nerve denervation technology designed to treat hypertension — such as renal artery denervation in the kidneys — subject to customary closing conditions.
- *The New England Journal of Medicine* published[results](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2307291) from the investigator-sponsored SINGLE SHOT CHAMPION clinical trial which demonstrated the[FARAPULSE™ Pulsed Field Ablation (PFA) System](https://news.bostonscientific.com/cardiac-ablation-for-afib) achieved superior effectiveness for the treatment of symptomatic paroxysmal AF versus Medtronic’s Arctic Front Advance™ cardiac cryoablation catheter.
- *JAMA Network Open* published real-world data demonstrating that patients with prostate cancer treated with[SpaceOAR™ Hydrogel](https://news.bostonscientific.com/cancer-treatment-options) showed a[25% reduction](https://www.curetoday.com/view/reducing-bowel-complications-following-radiation-in-prostate-cancer) in bowel disorder risk and a 46% decrease in procedures like colonoscopies four years post-radiation therapy.


For the full Boston Scientific earnings announcement and more business highlights from the first quarter of 2025, see the[press release](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-04-23-Boston-Scientific-announces-results-for-first-quarter-2025) .


*1Organic net sales growth excludes the impact of foreign currency fluctuations and net sales attributable to certain acquisitions and divestitures for which there are less than a full period of comparable net sales.*
