# Interested in our Q4 2025 earnings?

Today, Boston Scientific shared its financial results for the fourth quarter of 2025.


Overall, we generated net sales of $5.286 billion during the fourth quarter of 2025, growing 15.9 percent on a reported basis and 12.7 percent on an organic basis1 compared to the prior year period.


We also achieved net sales growth in each of our reportable segments, with 6.5 percent organic growth1 in MedSurg and 16.1 percent organic growth1 in Cardiovascular compared to the prior year period.


For the full year 2025, Boston Scientific generated net sales of $20.074 billion, growing 19.9 percent on a reported basis and 15.8 percent on an organic basis1.


“2025 was another exceptional year for Boston Scientific, with our global teams delivering differentiated innovation and high performance that enabled us to exceed our goals," says Mike Mahoney, chairman and chief executive officer, Boston Scientific. "As a direct result of the dedication, consistency and winning spirit of our team, we have impacted millions of patient lives and are well-positioned to continue on our strong growth trajectory well into the future."


## Additional Q4 highlights:


-


We received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval and CE mark for the[FARAPOINT™ Pulsed Field Ablation (PFA) Catheter](https://www.bostonscientific.com/us/en/healthcare-professionals/products/pulsed-field-ablation/farapoint-pulsed-field-ablation-catheter/fp90000122.html) , a nav-enabled, focal PFA catheter that can create focal and linear-shaped lesions within a single device.


-


We received U.S. coverage of Endoscopic Sleeve Gastroplasty (ESG), using the[OverStitch™ Endoscopic Suturing System](https://prnewswire.webdriverlogin.com/preview/mr5/bostonscientific/struggling-to-lose-weight-these-innovative-solutions-can-help) , by Elevance Health (formerly Anthem) beginning December 18, 2025, and recognition by The American Society for Metabolic and Bariatric Surgery of ESG as an endorsed procedure, expanding patient access to an innovative, less invasive weight-loss solution.


-


We announced agreements to acquire the following companies, subject to customary closing conditions:


- [Penumbra, Inc.](https://prnewswire.webdriverlogin.com/preview/mr5/bostonscientific/2026-01-15-Boston-Scientific-announces-agreement-to-acquire-Penumbra,-Inc) , a publicly traded company that offers thrombectomy products for use in peripheral vascular procedures, minimally invasive peripheral embolization technologies and differentiated neurovascular solutions for access, stroke revascularization and neuroembolization


- [Valencia Technologies Corporation](https://prnewswire.webdriverlogin.com/preview/mr5/bostonscientific/2026-01-12-Boston-Scientific-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-Valencia-Technologies-Corporation) , a privately held company focused on the development and commercialization of the eCoin® System, an implantable tibial nerve stimulation device for the treatment of urge urinary incontinence


-


We completed the acquisition of[Nalu Medical, Inc.](https://prnewswire.webdriverlogin.com/preview/mr5/bostonscientific/2025-10-17-Boston-Scientific-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-Nalu-Medical,-Inc) , developer of the Nalu Neuromodulation System, designed to use peripheral nerve stimulation to deliver targeted relief for adults living with severe, intractable chronic pain of peripheral nerve origin.
