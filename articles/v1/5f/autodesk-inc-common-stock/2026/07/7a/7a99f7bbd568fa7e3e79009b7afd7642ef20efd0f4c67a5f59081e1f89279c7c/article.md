# Four ways AI is changing product development

AI is becoming a critical part of the product development process. With Autodesk Assistant in Fusion, teams can use natural language to streamline workflows, generate content, and quickly access the information they need. Here are four ways AI is used in Fusion:


### Autodesk Assistant 101


Ask questions, get guidance, run commands and customize Fusion using natural language. Autodesk Assistant is built right into Fusion, see how it helps you accelerate your workflow.


### AI-assisted design with Claude


Claude and Autodesk Fusion work together, accelerating concept development while leaving creative decisions in the hands of designers and engineers.


### Generate photo-realistic renders in seconds


Turn simple text prompts into polished visualizations of your designs, making it easier to explore concepts and showcase your work.


### Interrogate complex designs faster


Ask Autodesk Assistant to analyze your design to surface information for purchasing, helping teams move from concept to production with fewer manual steps.
