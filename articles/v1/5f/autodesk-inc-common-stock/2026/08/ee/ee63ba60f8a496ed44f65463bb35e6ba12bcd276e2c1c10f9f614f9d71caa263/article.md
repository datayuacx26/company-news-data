# Autodesk extends invitation to join financial results conference call

Second quarter fiscal 2027 financial results conference call to be held Thursday, August 27, 2026, 2 p.m. PT


SAN FRANCISCO, Aug. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ —[Autodesk](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4743960-1&h=2653756049&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.autodesk.com%2Fpr-autodesk&a=Autodesk) , Inc. (NASDAQ: ADSK) today announced it will share its second quarter fiscal 2027 financial results conference call via its website on Thursday, August 27, 2026, at 2 p.m. Pacific Time (PT). Join the live webcast call here:[autodesk.com/investors](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4743960-1&h=208260280&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.autodesk.com%2Finvestors&a=autodesk.com%2Finvestors) . An audio replay of the webcast will be available after 5 p.m. PT at[autodesk.com/investors](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4743960-1&h=208260280&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.autodesk.com%2Finvestors&a=autodesk.com%2Finvestors) .


More information will be available on[autodesk.com/investors](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4743960-1&h=208260280&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.autodesk.com%2Finvestors&a=autodesk.com%2Finvestors) .
