# Paying Freelancers: Is it Better to Pay in Local Currency or USD?

The modern startup’s blueprint for success relies heavily on accessing world-class talent, often hiring engineers, designers, and marketers beyond US borders. For business owners, managing this global workforce presents a critical challenge when it comes to overseas payments.


When payouts are slow or riddled with hidden fees, it does more than just cause a headache for your contractor, it erodes trust and becomes a major risk to talent retention. The payment experience is no longer just a financial function; it is an essential part of your talent strategy.


## **What currency is better to pay in?**


Have you experienced getting paid in US dollars only to receive a significantly lower amount in your local currency after conversion and transfer fees?


The true cost of paying a contractor in US dollars, only for them to receive less than expected after intermediary banks and conversion markups take their share, can quickly sour any good working relationship. International freelancers are increasingly demanding transparency and reliability when it comes to payments.


Research highlights just how critical the payment experience is to loyalty. A[2023 global hiring report](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.deel.com/library/2023-global-hiring-report-employer-perspective) found that a striking 54% of global contractors reported leaving a job due to a poor payment experience; while 88% prioritize payment reliability and transparency when choosing clients. This is key to retention: providing certainty that the amount invoiced is the exact amount received.


The optimal strategy shifts the power to the contractor. By offering the choice of receiving payment in their local currency, with a transparent conversion rate upfront, business owners ensure the full expected value arrives without surprise deductions. This simple feature transforms a potentially frustrating financial transaction into a positive, respectful experience.


Research shows more contractors prefer getting paid in their local currency. \[Image source: Pexels\]
