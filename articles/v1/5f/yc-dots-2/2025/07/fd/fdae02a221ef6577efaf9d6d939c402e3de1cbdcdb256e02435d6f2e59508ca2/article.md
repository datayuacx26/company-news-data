# Why Teams Switch to Dots

Payouts are often the last thing anyone wants to deal with — until they’re the reason you’re losing top contractors, damaging trust, or spending weekends fixing spreadsheet errors.


And in an age where contract work powers everything, from data labeling to[telehealth](https://dots.dev/solutions/telehealth) , creator platforms to global research, getting payments right isn’t just an ops detail. It’s the difference between scaling smoothly and falling apart.


**We asked our customers: *What made you switch to***[Dots](https://usedots.com/platform/payouts/) ***?***


Here’s what they told us:


## **1) “We needed to stop doing this manually.”**

## **2) “We needed faster contractor payouts.”**


When payouts are delayed, you’re not just sending money late. You’re risking the very foundation of your business: your workers. Late payments invite high churn, lower trust, and stalled momentum. Dots customers told us that speed wasn’t a nice-to-have, it was the reason they stayed competitive.


> *" **Since implementing Dots, just about everything – time, speed, user interface – is better** . At every turn, we were disappointed with Stripe’s performance, especially regarding speed. It was so slow for us that mailing a physical paper check to the customer was faster than the time it took for them to payout on their ACH transactions."*


-[Ryan Glossop](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryan-glossop/) **, Co-CTO at**[Forward Forestry](https://lbmjournal.com/cambium-acquires-forward-forestry/) **(acq. by**[Cambium](https://www.cambiumcarbon.com/) **), Largest network of arborists and tree care companies selling logs in the U.S**


> *"It definitely saves us 50% of our time compared to working with Stripe, and I know our pros would agree that Dots is significantly easier to use and leverage than Stripe. We've reduced the average time for payouts by about 50%. And **the amount of time it takes to get the payout to the pro by at least 50%** . The biggest win there is just being able to payout quickly via a platform like Venmo, which means payments are received by our instructors within a few minutes."*
