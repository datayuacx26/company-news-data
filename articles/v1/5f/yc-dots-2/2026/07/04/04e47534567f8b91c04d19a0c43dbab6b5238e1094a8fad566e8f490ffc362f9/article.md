## What Are Contractor Payment Solutions?


Selecting a contractor payment solution law firm operators trust requires understanding payout infrastructure. For legal and professional services, these API tools collect payee data, route funds across various rails, and handle tax requirements.


Legal practices face specific friction points. Your team must process high contractor volumes while meeting strict compliance rules. A capable system runs KYC (Know Your Customer) identity verification during onboarding, supports[just-in-time funding](https://usedots.com/blog/what-is-just-in-time-funding/) , and files 1099s at year-end without manual input. It also collects W-9s (US tax identification forms) from domestic contractors and W-8BENs (foreign status certification forms) from international payees, then validates each Taxpayer Identification Number against IRS records before any payout clears. Tools that skip these steps push the compliance burden back onto your team.

## Stripe Connect


Stripe Connect routes funds to connected accounts through a widely recognized API. Many legal teams select this option to route standard disbursements, though there are known[Stripe Connect payout gaps](https://usedots.com/blog/why-platforms-are-moving-beyond-stripe-connect-6-payout-gaps-what-to-do/) worth reviewing.


### What They Offer


- Routes payouts across 46 countries via ACH or wire transfer.
- Supports Instant Payouts to eligible debit cards in nine regions for a 1 to 1.5% fee.
- Collects W-9s (US tax identification forms) from domestic payees but does not include 1099 filing.

## Trolley


[Trolley payments](https://usedots.com/blog/trolley-reviews-pricing-alternatives/) operate as a global payouts and tax service routing bank transfers, wires, PayPal, and Venmo across 210 countries in 135 currencies.


### Capabilities and Limitations

- Offers 1099 handling as an add-on module with per-filing fees.
- Charges per-transfer fees that vary by rail and destination country.


## Routable


[Routable](https://usedots.com/blog/routable-reviews-pricing-alternatives/) serves as an accounts payable automation service for finance units handling high volumes of vendor bills.


### Capabilities


- Routes wire, check, and ACH (Automated Clearing House) transfers.
- Connects to FedNow (instant Federal Reserve rail) and RTP (Real-Time Payments) for faster domestic transfers. Both rails settle funds in seconds, not the one-to-five business days typical of standard ACH. However, Routable's core design is built around AP invoice workflows, so real-time settlement is available as a feature within that accounts-payable context, not as a standalone contractor payout rail.

## FAQ


### How do I choose the right contractor payment solution for my law firm or professional services firm?


Start by mapping your actual friction points: if your firm pays contractors across multiple countries, rail coverage and tax automation matter most, making Dots or Trolley stronger fits. If your team runs high-volume vendor invoices through a finance-first workflow, Tipalti or Routable may align better with your AP stack.


### Is Dots better than Stripe Connect for legal firms paying contractors internationally?


Dots covers 190+ countries across 300+ rails, while Stripe Connect supports payouts in 46 countries and charges a 1 to 1.5% fee for instant payouts. For firms paying contractors outside Stripe's supported regions, or those running high payout volumes where instant surcharges compound quickly, Dots removes both the geographic ceiling and the per-transfer fee.


### When should a legal or professional services firm choose Tipalti over Dots?


Tipalti fits best when your firm already runs a finance-team-led AP workflow with ERP software like NetSuite at the center and contractor payments are one line item among many vendor invoices. If contractor disbursements are high-volume, multi-country, or require real-time settlement with automated 1099 filing under a single contract, Dots handles those requirements directly without routing through an AP suite.


### What tax compliance features should a contractor payment solution include for legal and professional services firms?


At minimum, look for automated W-9 collection, IRS TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number) matching before payouts clear, and 1099 generation at year-end. The[ABA's guidance on 1099 rules for lawyers](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/law_practice/resources/law-technology-today/2025/1099-for-lawyers/) confirms that any firm paying a contractor more than $600 in a year must file a 1099, making automated filing a necessity, not a convenience. Firms paying international contractors should also require W-8BEN (foreign status certification form) collection and, depending on contractor locations, country-specific withholding compliance.


### Which contractor payment solution works best for firms just starting to scale their contractor network?


Routable and Stripe Connect offer faster entry points for firms with simpler domestic payment needs and existing AP or developer tooling. For firms expecting to grow contractor headcount quickly, add international payees, or need compliance and tax filing handled from day one, Dots supports that scale without requiring a platform rebuild later.
