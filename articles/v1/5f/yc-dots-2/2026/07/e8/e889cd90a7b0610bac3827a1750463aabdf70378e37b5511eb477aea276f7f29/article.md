We scored each option against six criteria:


- Settlement speed: Real-time money movement versus batch-based ACH and wire rails. Programs paying global advocates depend on instant settlement: funds that clear in seconds keep participation high, while 1-to-5 business day delays erode trust. We weighted providers that route through real-time rails such as RTP, FedNow, and local instant networks over those defaulting to next-day ACH or multi-day wire settlement.
- Rail depth: Number and type of payment networks available, including domestic instant rails, international wires, and local real-time networks.
- Country coverage: Total number of countries and currencies supported for outbound payouts.
- Instant-payout surcharges: Additional fees charged per transfer for real-time or instant settlement.
- Bundled compliance: Whether KYC (Know Your Customer), KYB (Know Your Business), and tax-form automation (W-9s, 1099s) are included under one contract or require separate vendors.
- Payee experience: Quality of the recipient-facing portal, supported payout methods, and onboarding friction for global advocates.

## Stripe Connect


Stripe Connect delivers proven payment infrastructure for a payout solution ambassador program. The service executes domestic transfers dependably, but expanding across borders requires adding outside vendors: a key reason why[platforms are moving beyond Stripe Connect](https://usedots.com/blog/why-platforms-are-moving-beyond-stripe-connect-6-payout-gaps-what-to-do/) .


### What They Offer


Stripe Connect supports accounts in 46 countries. Standard pricing runs 0.25% plus $0.25 per transaction. Instant Payouts are restricted to supported debit cards within nine core countries and carry a 1.5% surcharge per transfer. Standard ACH payouts settle in 1 to 2 business days, while cross-border wires take longer and trigger FX markup fees. Programs paying advocates beyond Stripe's 46 supported payout countries must route through separate providers to reach the remaining geography.


## Tipalti


Tipalti operates as an AP automation suite for finance teams distributing contractor payments internationally. See our[Dots vs Tipalti](https://usedots.com/blog/dots-vs-tipalti/) comparison for a full breakdown.


### System Capabilities


This suite accesses 50 payment rails across 200 countries. ACH and wire settlement takes 1 to 3 business days domestically and 1 to 5 days cross-border. Domestic ACH costs $1 to $1.50, while foreign exchange markups run 1% to 2% on top of the mid-market rate for cross-border transfers. Programs paying global advocates absorb those markups on every international payout, with no real-time rail option to bypass batch settlement delays. Teams that need instant settlement or predictable FX costs across 190+ countries will find Tipalti's AP-focused architecture a structural constraint.


## Trolley


[Trolley payments](https://usedots.com/blog/trolley-reviews-pricing-alternatives/) distribute funds via standard bank transfers, wires, PayPal, and Venmo across 210 countries and 135 currencies.


### What They Offer


The standard plan runs $49 per month. Each transfer adds separate transaction costs, like a $10 wire fee with foreign exchange markups.


- Good for teams wanting bundled tax tools alongside traditional banking.
- Limitations: Trolley charges a $10 wire fee plus FX markups on every transfer above the $49 monthly base, adding costs that compound with payout volume. The service has no real-time rail option, so all settlements clear on standard bank or wire timelines, not in seconds. International payouts carry a 2% FX markup, and programs moving through bank-partner onboarding can expect a 4 to 6 week setup window before payouts begin moving.


## Tremendous


Tremendous distributes rewards primarily through gift cards, prepaid Visa cards, and charity donations instead of direct bank transfers. The service supports more than 1,000 gift card brands and covers over 80 countries, making it a common choice for survey incentive programs and loyalty reward campaigns. However, Tremendous is not designed for high-volume cash disbursements: it lacks native KYC (Know Your Customer) and KYB (Know Your Business) onboarding, W-9 collection, and automated 1099 filing, which means compliance workflows must be handled outside the service. Programs paying ambassadors or referral partners who prefer direct cash payouts over store credit or gift cards face a structural ceiling with Tremendous, since 65% of consumers prefer cash for referral rewards. Teams with advocates spread across regions that require real-time bank settlement, multi-rail routing, or automated tax compliance will need to supplement Tremendous with additional vendors to close those gaps.

Domestic instant payouts route through FedNow and RTP, reaching approximately 85% of US bank accounts without a per-transfer surcharge. Tax workflow automation covers W-8s, W-9s (US tax identification forms), and 1099s, reducing manual compliance work for US-based payee networks. ## FAQ


### How do I choose the right payout solution for my ambassador or referral program from this list?


Start by identifying your payee geography and volume. If your ambassador network spans many countries and requires instant settlement, Dots or Routable fit best; if your advocates primarily receive gift cards and store credit, Tremendous works. Teams with finance-team-led workflows and fewer real-time requirements may find Tipalti sufficient.


### Is Dots better than Stripe Connect for paying global ambassadors and referral partners?


Dots covers 190+ countries versus Stripe Connect's 46 supported payout countries, charges no instant-payout surcharge where Stripe adds 1.5%, and imposes no daily volume cap where Stripe enforces published daily volume limits on fast transfers. For programs paying ambassadors across multiple regions in real time, Dots closes the gaps Stripe Connect leaves open.


### Is Tipalti or Trolley a better fit for ambassador programs that need bundled tax tools?


Tipalti suits finance teams that need AP automation with multi-country reach across 200 countries, though ACH and wire settlement runs 1 to 5 business days and FX markups apply. Trolley bundles tax tools alongside traditional banking rails for $49 per month but adds separate per-transfer fees and FX markups on top. If automated 1099 filing and real-time settlement must sit under one contract, neither matches Dots' end-to-end compliance stack.


### When should I consider Routable over the other payout solutions on this list?


Routable works well for high-growth marketplaces with a mostly US-based payee network that need FedNow and RTP (Real-Time Payments) access with strong tax workflow automation covering W-8s, W-9s (US tax identification forms), and 1099s. Once your ambassador program expands internationally, Routable's cross-border settlement of 1 to 3 business days across 140+ currencies becomes a structural constraint worth weighing against providers with deeper international rail coverage.


### What payout features matter most for running a high-volume ambassador program at scale?


The three capabilities that break down first at scale are settlement speed, compliance automation, and payee onboarding. Programs paying thousands of global advocates need real-time rails without surcharges, automated KYC (Know Your Customer) and KYB (Know Your Business) checks before funds move, and tax-form collection (W-9s and 1099s) built into the same workflow instead of bolted on separately.
