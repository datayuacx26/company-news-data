## What Are Contractor Payment Services?


Contractor payment services execute disbursements to independent workers, managing the complete payout lifecycle. This begins with payee onboarding and KYC (Know Your Customer) identity verification. These tools route funds across local payment rails, offer[just-in-time funding](https://usedots.com/blog/what-is-just-in-time-funding/) for disbursements, and file 1099 tax forms at the end of the year.


As the demand for temporary staffing grows, the volume of contractor disbursements rises with it, and so does the complexity. Agencies must track varying pay schedules, collect the right tax forms from each worker, and route funds through whichever payment method a contractor actually uses. Doing that manually across hundreds of payees creates delays and compliance risk. The IRS defines[independent contractor status](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/independent-contractor-defined) based on behavioral and financial control factors that affect how agencies must classify and pay workers. Contractor payment services exist to automate every step of that lifecycle under a single system.

## Stripe Connect


Stripe Connect routes funds to connected accounts across 46 countries. Built around its card-acceptance ecosystem, this infrastructure service processes contractor disbursements for staffing agencies.


### What They Offer


- Payouts via ACH, wire, and debit card push.
- Instant Payouts in nine regions for an extra 1 to 1.5% fee.

## Trolley


Trolley is a mass payout service supporting contractor disbursements across 135+ countries. It targets marketplaces and platforms paying independent workers via ACH and wire.


- **Good for:** Businesses paying contractors in developing markets who need broad country coverage and self-serve tax form collection.

- **Bottom line:** Staffing agencies that need instant settlement and bundled compliance under one contract will outgrow Trolley's setup timeline and separate billing model.

## FAQ


### How do I choose the right contractor payment service for my staffing agency from options like Dots, Tipalti, and Routable?

If your agency pays hundreds of contractors across multiple countries and needs bundled 1099 filing, Dots or Tipalti fit best; if you process a smaller volume of domestic vendor invoices, Routable works. Agencies with real-time settlement requirements should rule out any service that relies solely on ACH batch timing.

### What is TIN matching and why does it matter for contractor payments?


TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number) matching validates a contractor's tax ID against IRS records before any payout clears. Skipping this step risks IRS CP2100 notices, which trigger mandatory 24% backup withholding on future payments to a flagged worker and require correction workflows that add overhead at tax time. The IRS outlines the full[1099 reporting requirements for contractor payments](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/reporting-payments-to-independent-contractors) on its official guidance page.


### How long does it take to go live with a contractor payment service like Dots compared to Trolley?


Dots targets a go-live timeline of under one week for most businesses. Trolley requires a 4 to 6 week bank-partner onboarding step before disbursements can begin, which delays contractor payments and adds setup overhead for staffing agencies that need to move quickly.
