## What Are Payout Services for Home Services Marketplaces?

When a contractor finishes a job, a[home services marketplace](https://usedots.com/solutions/marketplaces) must reliably disburse their earnings using[payout APIs for gig economy platforms](https://usedots.com/blog/best-payout-apis-gig-economy-platforms/) . Without dedicated payout infrastructure, marketplaces must stitch together multiple providers, manage tax forms manually, and absorb the overhead as contractor volume grows. ## FAQs


### How do I choose the right payout service for my home services marketplace from options like Dots, Stripe Connect, Tipalti, Trolley, Routable, and Tremendous?


Start with two criteria: settlement speed and compliance scope. If your contractors expect same-day or instant earnings and you need built-in KYC (Know Your Customer), tax filing, and multi-rail routing under one contract, Dots fits that profile. If your team runs AP (accounts payable) workflows through an ERP and can accept ACH-batch timing, Tipalti is worth considering instead.


### Is Dots faster than Stripe Connect for paying home services contractors instantly?


Dots routes funds through RTP (Real-Time Payments) and FedNow (the Federal Reserve's instant payment service) at no added surcharge, while Stripe Connect charges an additional 1 to 1.5% fee for instant payouts to debit cards. ### When should a home services marketplace pick Tipalti or Trolley over Dots?


Tipalti suits enterprise finance teams that already run payments through ERP integrations and can tolerate ACH-batch settlement times. Trolley works for teams that need a white-labeled payee portal and can absorb a 4 to 6 week bank-partner onboarding period. Choose Dots if you need sub-week launch speed, real-time settlement, and tax automation without stitching together separate tools.


### What is TIN matching and why does it matter for home services marketplace payouts?


TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number) matching validates a contractor's tax ID against IRS records before any payout clears. Skipping this step risks IRS CP2100 notices, which trigger mandatory 24% backup withholding on future payments to a flagged worker and require correction workflows that slow your payout operations.


### Can Tremendous handle recurring contractor payouts for a home services marketplace?


Tremendous is built for one-off incentive distribution across gift cards and prepaid Visa cards, making it a poor fit for recurring contractor earnings that require KYC onboarding, 1099 filing, and real-time rail routing. For recurring home services payouts with compliance built in, a dedicated payout infrastructure service like Dots is the more appropriate choice.
