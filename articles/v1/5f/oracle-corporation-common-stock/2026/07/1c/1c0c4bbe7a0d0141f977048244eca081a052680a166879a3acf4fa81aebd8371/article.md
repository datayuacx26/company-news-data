# Oracle to Help Improve Construction Visibility and Results for Red Sea Global

Oracle today announced that[Red Sea Global](https://www.redseaglobal.com/) (RSG) has selected Oracle to transform how it manages some of the world’s most ambitious hospitality destinations. Leveraging[Oracle Aconex Cloud](https://www.oracle.com/middleeast/construction-engineering/aconex/) and[Oracle Primavera P6](https://www.oracle.com/middleeast/construction-engineering/primavera-p6/) , RSG will unite more than 23,000 users, from executives to field engineers, providing global stakeholders with the visibility to help streamline and accelerate project collaboration and delivery.


“Red Sea Global is setting a new benchmark for digital transformation in large-scale hospitality destination projects,” said Mark Webster, senior vice president and general manager, Oracle Infrastructure Industries. “By integrating project budgets, schedules, and workflows, Red Sea Global will be able to achieve greater consistency, real-time visibility, and efficiency across its global operations. This unified approach can enable rapid, informed decision-making that helps support better project outcomes.”


As its operations continued to scale, RSG’s fragmented, siloed systems could not keep pace with the needs of its geographically dispersed teams, leading to inefficiencies that threatened project delays. With Oracle Aconex, RSG’s global design and engineering teams will have access to real-time project data, making it easier to collaborate and stay on track with the project goals. RSG will be utilizing all Aconex modules, including Connected Cost and Model Coordination capabilities, to help transform how its project teams manage comments and conduct design and review processes. RSG is also implementing custom workflow automation to integrate document approvals, cost controls, and project correspondence.


“Our vision requires technology that connects thousands of stakeholders across continents while remaining simple and agile to implement,” said Sultan Moraished, Group Head of Technology and Corporate Excellence, Red Sea Global. “With Oracle Aconex and P6, our teams will be able to collaborate in real time to deliver ambitious tourism destination projects more quickly and with greater confidence.”


Oracle Primavera P6 will further enable RSG to drive centralized project scheduling and resource management, helping to minimize risk and support on-time delivery. Additionally, Aconex and Primavera P6 will support RSG with advanced reporting, analytics, and real-time insights. This unified approach can enable faster, more informed decision-making to support RSG’s commitment to delivering exceptional, visionary destinations.
