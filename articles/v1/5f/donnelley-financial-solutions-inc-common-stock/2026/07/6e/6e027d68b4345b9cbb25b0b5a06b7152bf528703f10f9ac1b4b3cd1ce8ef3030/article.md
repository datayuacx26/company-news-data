As Head of Vertical Sales and Market Strategy, Gary works across sales, marketing, service, and product to define and execute strategies that position DFIN to expand market share with third-party administrators. Gary holds a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration with a concentration in Finance from Bryant University and is based in Boston, MA.
