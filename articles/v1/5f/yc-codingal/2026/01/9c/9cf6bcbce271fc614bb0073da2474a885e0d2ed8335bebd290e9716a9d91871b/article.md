# Different Parts of a Computer


Published on January 3, 2026


A computer is like a smart machine that helps us learn, play games, watch videos, and do homework. But just like our body has different parts (eyes, brain, hands), a computer also has many parts that work together.


Let’s learn about different parts of a computer in a fun and easy way!


## 🌟 1. Monitor – The Computer’s Face


The monitor is the screen of the computer. It shows everything — pictures, games, videos, and text.


📌 Think of it like a TV where you can see what the computer is doing.


## 🎹 2. Keyboard – The Computer’s Hands


The keyboard has many buttons called keys. You use it to type letters, numbers, symbols, and commands.


📌 It’s like the computer’s hands that help it write and talk to you.


## 🖱️ 3. Mouse – The Computer’s Pointer


The mouse helps you point, click, drag, and select things on the screen.


📌 It’s like your finger for the computer. Wherever you move the mouse, the arrow on the screen moves too!


## 🧠 4. CPU – The Brain of the Computer


CPU stands for Central Processing Unit. It is the most important part because it:


✔ Thinks
✔ Solves problems
✔ Runs programs
✔ Tells all other parts what to do


📌 Just like your brain controls your body, the CPU controls the whole computer.


## 🗂️ 5. RAM – The Computer’s Short-Term Memory


RAM stores information only while the computer is ON.


📌 Imagine your brain remembering a phone number only for a few seconds — that’s how RAM works!


When the computer turns off, RAM forgets everything.


## 💾 6. Hard Drive / SSD – The Computer’s Long-Term Memory


This is where all your files stay:


- Photos
- Videos
- Games
- Homework
- Music
- Apps


📌 Even when the computer is turned off, it remembers everything stored here.


## 🎧 7. Speakers – The Computer’s Voice


Speakers help the computer make sounds:


- Music
- Video sounds
- Game sound effects
- Voice recordings


📌 They help the computer talk to you!


## 🖨️ 8. Printer – The Computer’s Printer Friend


A printer takes what’s on your screen and puts it on paper:


- Drawings
- Photos
- Homework
- Worksheets


📌 It’s like the computer’s colouring and writing machine.


## 🎤 9. Microphone – The Computer’s Ears


A microphone lets the computer hear your voice.


📌 When you speak into the mic, the computer listens — just like Alexa or Siri!


## 📸 10. Webcam – The Computer’s Eyes


A webcam is a tiny camera that:


- Takes photos
- Records videos
- Lets you join online classes


📌 It works like the eyes of the computer.


## 🌐 11. Wi-Fi / Internet Parts – The Computer’s Connection to the World


These help your computer connect to:


- Websites
- Online games
- Videos
- Google
- Online classes


📌 It’s like giving your computer a magic door to the whole world!


## 🔌 12. Power Supply – The Computer’s Energy Source


Just like you need food, a computer needs power. The power supply sends electricity to all parts.


📌 Without it, the computer cannot wake up!


## 🛠️ 13. Motherboard – The Computer’s Main Body


The motherboard is a big board inside the CPU cabinet.


It holds and connects:


- CPU
- Memory
- Storage
- Graphics
- All other parts


📌 Think of it like the computer’s skeleton that holds everything together.


## 🕹️ 14. Special Computer Helpers (Peripherals)


These are extra tools you can add:


- Headphones
- Touchscreen
- Joystick
- Drawing tablet
- External hard drive


📌 They make the computer even smarter and more fun!

## 🧩 Putting It All Together


A computer works just like a team. Each part has a special job:


Part Job


Monitor Shows pictures and videos


Keyboard Types letters and numbers


Mouse Helps you click and move


CPU Thinks and controls everything


RAM Remembers things for a short time


Hard Drive/SSD Stores things for a long time


Speakers Makes sound


Microphone Listens to you


Webcam Lets you be seen


Printer Puts things on paper


Wi-Fi Connects to internet


Power Supply Gives electricity


Motherboard Connects all parts


## 🎉 Conclusion: Computers Are a Team!


All these parts work together just like the parts of your body. When they cooperate, the computer becomes a powerful, helpful machine — perfect for learning, playing, creating, and exploring.
