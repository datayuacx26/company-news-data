## What is a Jumping Game?


Have you ever played the Dinosaur game anytime you had an internet connectivity issue or your web page was unreachable?


This game is a very famous jumping game developed by Google back in 2014. Now, it’s a standard game available in Google Chrome browser whenever there’s some kind of internet issue.


Essentially, a jumping game is a type of video game where you control any character by performing jumping actions. The main objective is usually to navigate through a series of platforms, obstacles, and challenges by timing jumps and avoiding hazards.


You must have played many jumping games or other video games that have a jumping-character element to it. Sonic Games, or Super Mario Bros games, have a lot of jumping components.
