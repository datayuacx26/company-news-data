### **Our approach to innovation**


From predictive replenishment to intelligent orchestration, we continuously translate macro trends into operational excellence. But innovation is not a solo act. It’s a team sport. To stay ahead of ongoing developments and balance innovation with what already works best, we collaborate across ecosystems to co-create scalable solutions that deliver measurable value.


### **The four pillars of our automation strategy**


Our automation strategy is built on four foundational pillars:


- **Large-scale automation**


High-capex, infrastructure-heavy solutions that enable transformative change. These are designed for new builds or step changes in customer operations, unlocking new capabilities and future-proofing logistics.


- **Adaptive technologies and continuous improvement**


Modular, flexible systems that integrate seamlessly into existing operations. These solutions offer agility without disruption and are supported by commercial models like Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS), OPEX and pay-as-you-go, lowering barriers to entry and opening the door to innovation for a wider range of customers.


- **Artificial intelligence**


AI is the game-changer that enhances every other pillar – from optimizing routes and managing inventory to improving picking accuracy. It’s the brain behind smarter, safer, and more efficient operations.


- **Research and development**


We test and co-develop technologies with partners. This dual approach accelerates innovation and gives our customers early access to game-changing solutions. Dual R&D programs – one for proven technologies and one for early-stage co-development – ensures that innovation is both rapid and risk-free.


The summit highlighted a growing openness to outsourcing logistics and operations, not as a cost-cutting measure, but as a way to scale, innovate, and stay agile. The most successful brands are those that treat their 3PLs as long-term partners, not short-term vendors.


“Technology doesn’t replace people, it empowers them. AI and automation are designed to support frontline teams, reduce fatigue, and improve safety. We see ourselves as a strategic partner. Co-creation with our clients and suppliers is central to our solutions driving long-term success through collaboration.”


**John Wilson– Senior Director, Global Automation R&D, GXO**


### Real-world AI use cases


Our AI-powered automation solutions are already making a tangible impact across operations:


- **Dynamic zone picking:** Autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) help double productive picking time while cutting overall time by 35%.
- **Robotic unloading:** Robotic systems now handle up to 600 cartons per hour, improving both efficiency and worker safety.
- **Tailored picking models:** From Goods-to-Person to Goods-to-Request, we are deploying smart, scalable solutions that are redefining how contract logistics operates.


### **The brain behind smarter warehousing**


We see artificial intelligence as the brain behind automation and operations. While consumer AI like ChatGPT helps people draft emails or organize meetings, mission-critical logistics demand far more. Our AI systems process millions of real-time data points to make split-second decisions that directly impact physical operations — from optimizing pick paths and managing inventory to enabling proactive replenishment.


Just as the human brain coordinates complex movements without conscious thought, our AI coordinates thousands of warehouse tasks simultaneously. In pilot sites across the globe, we’ve seen the results: greater accuracy, fewer manual tasks, and safer, more efficient operations. Whether it’s shortening pick paths, predicting SKU demand, or preventing stockouts before they happen, AI is transforming how we fulfill orders — faster, smarter, and with less fatigue for frontline teams.


“But AI doesn’t work in isolation. It requires a solid data foundation. Many companies are data-rich but insight-poor. We build AI around clear use cases, ensuring every algorithm delivers real value. Because when AI is powered by the right data, it doesn’t just automate — it elevates.”


***Dr. Jana Boerger– Senior Director, Warehouse AI, GXO***


### **The first AI-native operating system for logistics**


To truly unlock the power of AI in logistics, we built something entirely new:[GXO IQ](https://gxo.com/logistics-technology/gxo-iq/) – the first AI-native operating system designed specifically for supply chains. Unlike traditional systems that layer AI on top, our solution is built from the ground up to orchestrate millions of decisions in real time. It runs on four integrated layers: live signals from over 1,000 sites, AI models that act, modular workflows, and one unified interface. Powered by Snowflake and Google Cloud, GXO IQ turns complexity into clarity. The impact is already clear from pilot deployment: the solution delivers a 22% productivity lift and powers more than 120 million decisions per replenishment run helping businesses move faster, smarter, and with greater precision.
