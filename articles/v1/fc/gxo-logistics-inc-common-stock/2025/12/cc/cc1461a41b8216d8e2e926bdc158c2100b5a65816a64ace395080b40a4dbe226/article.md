# Optimizing bottle return operations to cut CO₂

[Case study](https://gxo.com/news-and-insights/?news_type=case-study)


# Streamlined bottle returns help brewery manufacturer cut CO₂


## **Customer challenge**


A leading brewery industry manufacturer faced the challenge of **improving the logistics of bottle returns.** This process, heavily influenced by seasonal sales fluctuations, required a flexible approach and efficient management of variable volumes. Additionally, it was necessary to properly prepare bottles for off-season storage and to sort out bottles originating from other producers. The client, part of a global corporation, placed particular emphasis on **achieving ESG goals—promoting a circular economy, reducing CO₂ emissions, and minimizing environmental impact.**


## **Solution**


GXO, as their logistics partner, designed and **implemented a comprehensive solution that included bottle sorting as a value-added service.** In a specially designated area optimized for ergonomics and efficiency, a manual sorting process supported by technology was introduced. This approach enabled higher productivity, reduced losses from breakages, and ensured precise separation of the customer’s bottles from competitors. The process is continuously improved in line with operational excellence principles.


## Results


Thanks to the collaboration between GXO and our customer, over 120 million returnable bottles were sorted in 2024 alone. This resulted in **savings of more than 0.5 tons of new glass and the elimination of approximately 25,000 trips from the glassworks to the brewery. Consequently,** **CO₂ emissions were reduced by over 805 tons.** A returnable bottle can be reused up to 20 times, and its carbon footprint is five times smaller than that of a single-use bottle. The client, appreciating GXO’s flexibility and effectiveness, extended the partnership for the coming years. Joint innovations are being developed, including process automation, aimed at further strengthening the supply chain and enhancing sustainability.
