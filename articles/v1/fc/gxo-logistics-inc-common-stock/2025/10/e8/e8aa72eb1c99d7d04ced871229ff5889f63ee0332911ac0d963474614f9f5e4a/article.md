## Introduction


In today’s volatile landscape, businesses are facing a critical moment in transport strategy.


With **fleet renewal** decisions looming, whether you purchase or lease, leaders are being asked to commit to solutions that may define their operations for the next five to ten years. But with rapid changes in technology, regulation and customer expectations, the risk of locking into long-term, costly and inflexible transportation models has never been higher. So how do you choose the right route forward? The answer lies in agility, enabled through strategic **partnerships** .


## What’s at stake: The hidden risks of going it alone


Transport decisions made today will define your **operational agility** tomorrow. But with the pace of change accelerating, businesses may not have the full picture to make the right choices alone. Without the right insight, you risk investing in solutions that may not align with future needs.


Internal teams often lack the bandwidth or expertise to manage compliance, optimization and innovation at scale. And external pressures are intensifying, including:


- Volatile fuel prices and inflation
- [ESG](https://gxo.com/Impact/) reporting requirements and emissions targets
- Global supply chain disruptions, from port congestion and extreme weather to geopolitical instability and raw material shortages
- Rising consumer expectations for speed, visibility and flexibility, putting pressure on last-mile and delivery models
