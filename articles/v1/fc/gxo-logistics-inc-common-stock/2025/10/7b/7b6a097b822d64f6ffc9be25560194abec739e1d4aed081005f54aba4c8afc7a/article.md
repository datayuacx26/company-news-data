## Introduction


In today’s volatile landscape, businesses are facing a critical moment in[transport strategy](https://gxo.com/supply-chain-mgmt/transportation-solutions/) .


With **fleet renewal** decisions looming, whether you purchase or lease, leaders are being asked to commit to solutions that may define their operations for the next five to ten years. But with rapid changes in technology, regulation and customer expectations, the risk of locking into long-term, costly and inflexible transportation models has never been higher. ## What’s at stake: The hidden risks of going it alone


Fleet decisions made today will define your **operational agility** tomorrow. But with the pace of change accelerating, businesses may not have the full picture to make the right choices alone, especially when it comes to vehicle type, fuel strategy or tech investment. Without the right insight, you risk investing in solutions that may not align with future needs.


Internal teams often lack the bandwidth or expertise to manage compliance, optimization and innovation at scale. And external pressures are intensifying, including:


- Volatile fuel prices and inflation
- [ESG](https://gxo.com/impact/) reporting requirements and emissions targets
- Global supply chain disruptions, from port congestion and extreme weather to geopolitical instability and raw material shortages
- Rising consumer expectations for speed, visibility and flexibility, putting pressure on last-mile and delivery models

## Sustainability and tech: The backbone of smarter transport


Technology and sustainability are now central to transport strategy, not just for efficiency, but for long-term viability.


AI-powered route optimization, dynamic planning and real-time tracking help reduce mileage, fuel use and emissions. For example, GXO has helped customers remove hundreds of thousands of miles annually through smarter planning, cutting costs and carbon simultaneously.


Meanwhile, alternative fuels like hydrotreated vegetable oil (HVO), liquified natural gas (LNG) and bio compressed natural gas (bioCNG), offer immediate emissions reductions where electric EVs aren’t yet viable. These aren’t just stopgaps; they’re strategic tools that help businesses decarbonize today while preparing for tomorrow.


****“The real value of partnership lies in seamless integration: technologies and fuel strategies tailored to your network, delivering measurable impact and supporting long-term sustainability goals.”****


***Carl Hanson, Managing Director – Transport, GXO***


## Real-world impact: B&Q’s low-carbon transformation


GXO’s work with B&Q, a leading UK-based DIY and home improvement retailer, is a blueprint for a future-focused transport strategy. Together, we’ve developed and delivered a long-term glide path to net zero by 2040, transitioning the entire retail logistics fleet to alternative fuels and introducing electric vehicles across the network.


Highlights include:


- 100% of the fleet now powered by LNG and HVO
- 17% mileage reduction through smarter planning and backhaul
- Launch of electric vans and HGVs, with more planned
- Shared infrastructure enabling other GXO customers to access LNG fueling to encourage greater adoption


These initiatives reflect not just operational excellence, but strategic foresight.
