# A rapid, automated solution for parts distribution


## Customer challenge


A company that sells and services world-class propulsion and power systems for large marine and land vehicles was facing challenges with accuracy and turnaround time in its aftermarket parts distribution.


**Safety:** Handling heavy/bulky parts such as engine blocks and crankshafts requires special equipment and training


**Accuracy:** With 40,000 SKUs, 80% small parts, inventory and order accuracy are challenging to maintain


**Turnaround time:** Using traditional material handling equipment, only 6-8 lines could be processed per hour causing order backlogs and slow turnaround even on emergency orders


## Solution


GXO’s solution introduced a 5,000 square-foot automated storage and retrieval system (ASRS), handling 40 lines per hour and enabling a 5-6x increase in productivity. GXO operates using the customer’s SAP extended WMS to provide inbound inspection, inventory management and order fulfillment to its distributors. The site processes approximately 200 unplanned emergency orders per day.


Value-added services include packing, boxing/crating, carton construction, kitting, ITN processing for international shipping and customer service, helping distributors with returns and quality issues.


## Results


- **Start-up:** The site launched on time and under budget
- **Reduced backlog:** Reduced backorders from $32 million to just over $200,000 in the first six months
- **Key metrics:**


- Emergency orders: Reduced fill time from 24 hours to same-day
- Standard orders: Reduced fill time from 72 to 24 hours
- Inventory accuracy: 99.99%
- Order accuracy: 99.67% or above


- **Continuous improvement:** GXO’s formal customer excellence planning program aligns the operation with the customer’s forecast, budget, productivity goals and labor plan on an ongoing basis.
