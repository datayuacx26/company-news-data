# Enabling omnichannel growth through strategic first-time outsourcing


## Customer challenge


A global apparel leader operates more than 500 stores worldwide, with products available in over 100 countries. As part of its European growth strategy, the company made a strategic decision to **outsource its European logistics operations for the first time** , transitioning from an in-house model to a long-term partnership with a specialist logistics provider.


GXO was selected to take over operations at a highly automated distribution center in Germany, **supporting distribution across wholesale, retail, digital, ecommerce, and marketplace channels** under a 20‑year partnership agreement.


## Solution


The distribution center was established through a **first-time outsourcing** **approach** , enabling a seamless transition from an in‑house operation to a fully outsourced model – without disruption to ongoing business.


**Under this model, GXO assumed responsibility for operating the existing warehouse, rebadged the workforce, and took over the site lease,** while also managing and integrating existing assets such as racking and material handling equipment. This approach provided the customer with immediate operational continuity while unlocking greater flexibility in resourcing, scaling, and long‑term cost management.


The site was designed as a **scalable,** **future-ready logistics platform** to support omnichannel growth across Europe. The facility launched with an initial capacity of approximately 37 million units and is planned to scale to 55 million units and up to 650 employees. From inbound to outbound, the operation integrates state-of-the-art automation and technology, including shuttle and mini-load systems, a fully automated carton opening, closing, and labeling, and a paper bagging machine.


Beyond traditional B2B distribution, the facility also enables direct‑to‑consumer fulfillment as a core element of the **customer** **’** **s omnichannel strategy** . Ecommerce operations were introduced through a structured, phased ramp‑up, allowing additional European markets to be onboarded progressively while ensuring stability, quality, and service excellence from day one.


**Warehouse consolidation into a single, highly automated hub enables the customer to realize large-scale efficiencies** across inventory management, capital deployment, overhead, and operating costs – helping transform logistics from a traditional cost center into a **value creating, performance driven operation** .


## Results


By outsourcing its European logistics operations for the first time, the customer gained access to GXO’s world-class operational expertise, advanced automation capabilities, and data-driven inventory management capabilities – **delivering measurable capital efficiency gains, supporting their long-term growth aspirations and minimizing the risk to ongoing inflationary cost increases.**


The new operating model is designed to balance capital commitments between the customer, GXO, the landlord, and financial institutions to minimize customer balance sheet commitments and the associated opportunity costs of a **multi-million-dollar investment** in a state-of-the-art automated site.


In the long-term, the customer should also realize significant benefits from reduced exposure to inflationary costs increases as well as their ability to leverage GXO’s tools to drive labor optimization, improved inventory efficiency, and economies of scale.


This first-time outsourcing model delivered more than operational efficiency – it established a foundation for sustainable, scalable growth. With continuity for employees, optimized labor management and a clear path to workforce upskilling, the model aligns workforce capability with evolving business demand.


Together, GXO and the customer are building an inclusive, high-performance culture designed not just to meet today’s demands, but to accelerate long-term success for the business, its people, and the communities they serve.
