# Scaling cross-border growth with integrated managed transport


## Customer challenge


A fast-growing UK outdoor and lifestyle brand, needed to redesign its European logistics model to **reduce shipping costs from the UK** and establish **Germany as a central European gateway** . With European demand increasing, the existing UK-centric model no longer delivered the required speed or cost efficiency.


To enable this shift, the company partnered with GXO to launch **ecommerce fulfillment, reverse logistics, and transport operations** from GXO site in Germany – **with a full go-live required in just three months** . ** The ramp-up took place during peak season preparation and in the weeks leading up to Black Friday, combining high volumes, tight timelines, and seasonal pressure into a highly demanding implementation with little margin for error.


**A scalable transport setup** was required that could manage growing order volumes across Europe, seamlessly integrate with the Warehouse Management System (WMS), and ensure reliable carrier performance.


## Solution


GXO focused on delivering a transport solution that was **stable from day one** while remaining flexible and scalable to support future growth. Within a matter of weeks, the joint teams standardized the transport foundation and ensured that all carrier processes were seamlessly integrated into daily warehouse operations at the German site.


A unified Carrier Management System (CMS) was implemented to automate key transport activities, including **label creation, manifest management, and end-to-end tracking data flows** . Selected and preferred carriers were fully embedded into the operation, covering express and standard parcel. The standardized solution also ensures that **future carrier integrations can be handled quickly and easily** , supporting ongoing network expansion.


To provide full transparency, GXO built **clear visibility into key performance indicators** . Insights into carrier performance helped the brand to better understand how their European distribution behaved day to day and enabled proactive improvement where needed.


## Results


Despite the **short and intense ramp-up period** , the project achieved a **very smooth go-live** . Operations started on time, carrier integrations performed as expected, and the transition into peak season was handled without disruption.


The brand benefited from a **transparent and scalable cost model** , improved visibility into key KPIs and faster delivery times to European customers, particularly for express shipments. The successful go-live under peak-season conditions gave the business confidence in the new logistics setup and a solid foundation for continued European growth.
