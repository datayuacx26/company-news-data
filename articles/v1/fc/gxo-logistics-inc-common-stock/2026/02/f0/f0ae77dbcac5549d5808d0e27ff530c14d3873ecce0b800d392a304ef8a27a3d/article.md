# Integrated managed transport drives 99% on‑time delivery

[Case study](https://gxo.com/news-and-insights/?news_type=case-study)


# 99% on time deliveries and 10% cost savings through an integrated transport solution


## Customer challenge


As it expanded quickly across Europe, a fast‑growing apparel brand needed an **integrated logistics model that could orchestrate complex flows** (e‑commerce, retail, wholesale and teamwear) while ensuring speed, reliability and cost control.


In logistics, transport typically accounts for nearly two thirds of total costs, compared with one third for warehousing. Managing both through a coordinated and centralized approach creates significant value through cost reductions, optimized flows and improved overall performance.


## Solution


The company partnered with GXO to design and deploy a Managed Transport solution fully integrated with its warehouse operations. From day one, the teams worked closely together to **align warehouse processes with transport flows, ensuring smooth execution and on‑time performance.**


The solution is built on three key pillars:


- **Flexibility and carrier neutrality** : Agile transport orchestration through an independent partner network adapted to each channel’s needs.
- **Visibility and optimization** : A control tower with dashboards, KPI tracking, proactive exception management and invoice reconciliation to reduce errors and costs.
- **End‑to‑end integration** : Coordination of warehousing and transport operations to maximize efficiency and reduce the risk of disruptions.


## Results


After one year of implementation, the benefits were clear:


- **Cost savings** : Optimization of the e‑commerce flow by shifting from parcel service to letterbox delivery, generating 10% savings for domestic e‑commerce.
- **E‑commerce performance** : On‑time delivery rate above 99% during a high‑volume month (November) despite volumes increasing ninefold during peak.
- **Efficiency and sustainability** :


- Optimization of store deliveries through a specialized transport partner, increasing capacity and reducing unnecessary trips.
- Consolidation of pallet flows for groupage at local hubs, reducing costs, improving truck utilization and lowering emissions.


This integrated approach turns transport complexity into clear value with stronger visibility, greater agility and measurable operational gains.
