# Monopar Announces Abstract Accepted for Oral Presentation at AASLD - The Liver Meeting® 2025

[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_2a3888b22770967f57dee9d4aa201d08/monopartx/news/2025-10-15_Monopar_Announces_Abstract_Accepted_for_Oral_124.pdf) October 15, 2025


WILMETTE, Ill., Oct. 15, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Monopar Therapeutics Inc. (“Monopar” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: MNPR), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing innovative treatments for patients with unmet medical needs, today announced that its abstract on the Phase 2 ALXN1840-WD-204 copper balance study has been selected for an oral presentation at the American Association for the Study of Liver Diseases (AASLD) – The Liver Meeting® 2025, taking place in Washington, D.C., from November 7-11, 2025.


Presentation Details:


- **Title:** Rapidly Improved Cu Balance in Wilson Disease Patients on Tiomolybdate Choline
- **Publication Number:** 0073
- **Session:** Distinct Pathways, Shared Progress: Metabolic/Genetic Disorders & Biliary Physiology
- **Date:** Sunday, November 9, 9:00-9:15 am EST
- **Presenting Author:** Aftab Ala, MBBS, M.D., FRCP, Ph.D.


The abstract is currently available on the[conference website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=BE3OmkbORPlw3HXwq4uKvzcw7_lGtyrXPE9zROqcZ-Kkrwv_FC-rF8d0fjSkjXe45IbG5DGgUsxNOybJGR5Hi3dRHGhfyRIo-HCSTCB0Q2VSyAFyOwCSWsEh5RxfYLGau4eUwuuvJPW36VVPhQu-DBBlvy6qEv6Bn7r0tEdLQmM=) , and the materials for the oral presentation will be available at[www.monopartx.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=87pKgdzqCJQPpN02GlYs9jFr7SUBZfB5Y0YGE_iAB-ZBVtJdrMcdpTgCqFcMKMAHIBY_yoFbkHAjibsY6KViigtwgxZI3cBO6hX2cln2010=) on November 9, 2025.


**About Monopar Therapeutics Inc.**


Monopar Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company with late-stage ALXN1840 for Wilson disease, and radiopharmaceutical programs including Phase 1-stage MNPR-101-Zr for imaging advanced cancers, and Phase 1a-stage MNPR-101-Lu and late preclinical-stage MNPR-101-Ac225 for the treatment of advanced cancers. For more information, visit:[www.monopartx.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=87pKgdzqCJQPpN02GlYs9t3GPSD50nhF1wrCh5t5qUYitv-rRf_hGhR0DW2sFO_B1XC2g5htla6KLz0qWw6R81srhZaldiIZlwkAxe5jEUc=) .
