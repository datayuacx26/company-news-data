# How Automation Improves Local Government Efficiency

# How Automation Improves Local Government Efficiency


August 04, 2026


by Amber Ventimiglia


##


Local governments are under growing pressure to do more with limited staff and resources. AI and automation can improve efficiency, but their greatest value often extends beyond reducing manual work. When implemented thoughtfully, automation changes how information moves across an organization, helping departments make faster decisions, collaborate more effectively, and spend less time waiting for information.


[Pickens County, Georgia](https://www.tylertech.com/resources/case-studies/tea-winner-boosting-efficiency-with-ap-automation) , saw those benefits after modernizing its finance operations with AI-assisted invoice capture and accounts payable (AP) automation. Previously, the county relied on separate applications that didn’t communicate with one another. Finance could see where work stood, but other departments couldn’t. Staff often waited for monthly reports or contacted the finance team for updates before making routine purchasing and budgeting decisions.


Many local governments evaluate automation by asking one question: How much manual work does it eliminate? Pickens County suggests a better one: How much organizational friction does it remove?


## Automation Does More Than Eliminate Manual Work


Like many local governments, Pickens County’s accounts payable process depended heavily on manual effort. Invoice information was entered by hand, departments lacked real-time visibility into financial data, and finance staff spent valuable time answering status questions instead of focusing on financial management.


AI-assisted invoice capture simplified that workflow. Instead of manually entering invoice data, staff could upload invoices directly into the system, where AI extracted much of the information automatically before routing invoices through the approval process. The AP automation system has empowered them to complete disbursements in batches of 60-100 at once.


As Accounts Payable Specialist Hollie Poole explained, “I just grab your invoice off my email and throw it in the capture ... It’s really a huge efficiency time saver. We love it.”


Reducing manual data entry certainly saved time. But it also made the entire approval process more transparent.

## Shared Visibility Accelerates Decisions


Once invoices entered the automated workflow, departments could see where they were in the approval process without contacting finance. Managers could monitor budgets through scheduled reports and dashboards rather than waiting for monthly updates, helping them identify issues sooner.


For Poole, that visibility fundamentally changed day-to-day operations. “At any moment, there’s not a question of, ‘Where’s that invoice?’ or 'Who’s supposed to be looking at that?’”


That shared visibility eliminates countless small interruptions that slow work across local government. Instead of sifting through emails, checking multiple systems, or asking another department for updates, employees spend more time moving work forward.


When automation reduces organizational waiting time, decisions happen faster.


## Efficiency Grows When Information Moves Freely


As AI and automation continue to evolve, local governments will have even more opportunities to streamline routine work. But technology delivers its greatest value when it improves how departments work together — not simply how quickly they complete individual tasks.


Pickens County’s experience demonstrates that lesson well. AI-assisted automation reduced manual effort, but the broader outcome was a more connected financial operation where information was easier to access, approvals moved faster, accountability improved, and departments could make decisions with greater confidence.


For local government leaders, that larger shift is valuable. Sustainable efficiency comes from ensuring the right people have the right information at the right time. By removing delays between people, information, and decisions, AI-assisted automation helps organizations move work forward with greater confidence.


Work moves faster because the organization moves together.
