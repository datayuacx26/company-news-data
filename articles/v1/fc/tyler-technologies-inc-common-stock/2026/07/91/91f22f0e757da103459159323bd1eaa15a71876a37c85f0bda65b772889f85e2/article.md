# Info-Tech: Tyler Public Safety Named Leading Tech Partner


July 02, 2026


by Kate Nadolski


##


Public safety agencies constantly face growing pressure to modernize mission-critical systems while continuing to serve their communities without interruption. From improving interoperability and information sharing to supporting mobile-first operations, compliance requirements, and increasingly complex workflows, agencies need technology partners that can help them navigate both today’s demands and tomorrow’s challenges.


**A recent independent assessment from Info-Tech Research Group highlights why many agencies are turning to Tyler Technologies as a partner.**


## Rising Above the Rest


Conducted for a U.S. county evaluating options for a modern law enforcement platform, the study examined leading public safety technology vendors across several critical criteria, including functionality, market presence, long-term viability, ease of use, affordability, transparency, customization capabilities, and future readiness.


**Following its evaluation, Info-Tech ranked Tyler Technologies highest overall among the vendors assessed.**


**Tyler earned the highest placement on Info-Tech’s vendor quadrant** , which measures both functional breadth and depth alongside vendor footprint. Notably, Tyler was the only vendor positioned in the upper-right quadrant of the analysis, demonstrating leadership in both solution capabilities and organizational strength.


## Why the Ranking Matters


For public safety agencies, selecting technology is about more than just purchasing a single application. Agencies are making long-term investments in systems that must support mission-critical operations, adapt to changing requirements, and remain reliable for years to come.


Info-Tech’s findings reinforce Tyler’s ability to serve as a long-term modernization partner, helping agencies address some of the most pressing challenges facing public safety today:


- Modernizing aging technology infrastructure
- Improving interoperability and data sharing across departments
- Supporting mobile-first field operations
- Meeting evolving state and federal compliance requirements
- Increasing operational resilience
- Managing increasingly complex public safety workflows


As agencies continue to evaluate modernization strategies, vendor stability, scalability, and long-term vision are becoming just as important as product functionality.


## A Connected Public Safety Ecosystem


One of the study’s strongest findings was **the breadth of Tyler’s[public safety portfolio](https://www.tylertech.com/solutions/courts-public-safety/public-safety)** .


Tyler’s solutions span the full public safety operational lifecycle, including:


- Computer aided dispatch (CAD)
- Records management
- Fire and EMS operations
- Mobile operations
- Citation management
- Public safety analytics
- Compliance and reporting capabilities
- Civil process
- Corrections


This breadth allows agencies to move beyond disconnected systems and siloed data, creating a more connected technology ecosystem that supports **better information sharing, improved situational awareness, and more informed decision-making.**


Rather than managing multiple vendors across critical operations, agencies can leverage integrated solutions designed to work together across departments and workflows.


## Scale, Stability, and Long-Term Viability


In public safety, technology decisions are often measured in decades, not years.


Info-Tech’s assessment recognized Tyler’s significant market presence and organizational strength — important considerations for agencies making mission-critical investments. As agencies grow, expand their services, or navigate changing operational demands, they need confidence that their technology partner has the scale, resources, and expertise to support them over the long term.


## Backed by Independent Research


According to Info-Tech, the assessment was developed using analyst expertise, market research, and detailed reviews of publicly available vendor information. Following its evaluation, the firm identified Tyler as one of the vendors best positioned to meet the client’s requirements, ultimately ranking the company highest in the overall assessment.


As public safety agencies continue investing in technology that strengthens operations and improves service to their communities, Tyler remains committed to delivering connected, scalable solutions that empower first responders and public safety professionals now and in the years to come.


### Who is Info-Tech Research Group?


[Info-Tech Research Group](https://www.infotech.com/) is a research and advisory firm that produces unbiased, highly relevant research and provides advisory services to help leaders make strategic, timely, and well-informed decisions. The firm regularly publishes industry insights highlighting technology proven to meet needs and drive measurable results.


Info-Tech’s reports are based on unbiased research studies and data findings from verified user feedback collected through the firm’s SoftwareReviews platform. Findings are informed by data from users and IT professionals who have real-time experience with the software throughout the procurement, implementation, and maintenance processes.


### What’s Next? Building the Future of Public Safety


As public safety agencies continue evaluating modernization initiatives, the need for connected, resilient, and future-ready technology has never been greater. Emerging technologies, advanced analytics, mobile capabilities, and integrated data environments are transforming how agencies operate, collaborate, and serve their communities.


Info-Tech’s recognition reinforces Tyler’s commitment to helping agencies navigate this evolution. By combining proven public safety solutions with a long-term vision for government technology modernization, Tyler helps agencies build a foundation that can adapt to changing operational demands while supporting the mission-critical workflows of today.


Whether your agency is looking to modernize dispatch and records management, improve field mobility, enhance compliance efforts, or strengthen interoperability across departments, Tyler’s Enterprise Public Safety platform, Enforcement Mobile, and Fire & EMS Suite provide connected solutions designed to streamline operations and improve efficiency before, during, and after an emergency call.
