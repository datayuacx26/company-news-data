# CAD-to-CAD Helps Solve Titusville Burglaries

# CAD-to-CAD Helps Solve Titusville Burglaries


June 10, 2026


by Kate Nadolski


##


In modern public safety operations, outcomes often depend less on a single agency’s capabilities and more on how effectively agencies share information in real time.


A case in Titusville, Florida, demonstrates how CAD-to-CAD interoperability between the Titusville Police Department (TPD) and the Brevard County Sheriff’s Office (BCSO) played a critical role in **identifying, tracking, and arresting a repeat offender.**


## Early Encounters Build a Pattern


The case began in June, when Titusville officers responded to a call at a local hotel. A homeless individual was found sleeping in a hallway. Officers made contact, identified the subject, confirmed there were no active warrants, and issued a trespass warning.


The incident was documented and entered into the CAD system. While routine on its own, the entry became important when dispatch personnel later reviewed the subject’s history.


Using multi-agency search tools within the agency's CAD system, Titusville personnel discovered that nearby Brevard County had prior encounters with the same individual. BCSO’s record system, or “global jacket”, included more complete demographic and historical information.


**Through CAD-to-CAD interoperability, Titusville was able to access and integrate that BCSO data into their own system. This allowed them to build a more complete alert record for the subject, ensuring officers were aware of prior trespass activity across multiple locations and agencies.**


Over the following weeks, the same individual repeatedly surfaced at several hotels in Titusville, each time receiving additional trespass warnings. In some cases, interactions escalated to verbal aggression toward staff. With each encounter, the subject’s behavioral pattern became more defined and more concerning.


Because of the shared systems, officers were no longer responding to isolated calls.


**They were building a connected history of repeated activity across multiple locations.**


## The Breakthrough: Linking a Series of Burglaries


The situation escalated in August, when Titusville began receiving multiple burglary calls within a small geographic area. Three separate commercial businesses, including a restaurant, an automotive shop, and a car dealership, were burglarized within a short time frame and a half-mile radius.


Surveillance footage from each location showed a single suspect, but image quality was not sufficient for positive identification. At that point, the investigation risked slowing due to a lack of clear leads.


A key turning point came when an officer mentioned the suspect resembled someone he recently issued a trespass warning to, though he could not recall the individual’s identity.


Using their CAD system, the agency searched all trespass-related calls the officer had cleared over the prior months. Within minutes, they generated a shortlist of possible individuals.


That list was then cross-referenced using multi-server search capabilities across agency systems. One subject stood out, with multiple recent trespass warnings in the same general area as the burglaries.


Titusville’s records contained the subject’s history but lacked a usable photograph. However, through CAD-to-CAD interoperability, analysts were able to pull the matching global jacket from BCSO, which included a clear image.


**What had started as an unidentified suspect in surveillance footage became a confirmed person of interest in less than 10 minutes.**


## From Identification to Arrest


With a confirmed identity and image in hand, Titusville distributed an intelligence bulletin to officers across the area. Patrol units began actively searching locations associated with recent activity.


A short time later, a Titusville supervisor observed a potential match near a commercial area close to one of the earlier burglary scenes. Detectives responded, and the suspect was tracked into a nearby wooded area where he was taken into custody and charged with multiple counts of burglary and theft.


## The Role and Value of CAD-to-CAD Interoperability


This case highlights the operational impact of CAD-to-CAD interoperability between Titusville and Brevard County.


Without shared systems:


- Trespass incidents would have remained isolated reports
- The suspect’s identity would have been difficult to confirm quickly
- Investigators would have lacked access to cross-agency history
- The burglary investigation could have stalled due to limited leads


With interoperable CAD systems:


- Officers were able to recognize patterns across multiple agencies
- Dispatchers quickly connected fragmented information into a single suspect profile
- A usable photograph and full identity were obtained in minutes
- Investigators were able to locate and arrest the suspect within hours


This case demonstrates how interoperability transforms public safety operations from reactive, isolated responses into coordinated, intelligence-driven action.


By allowing agencies like BCSO and TPD to share records, search across systems, and build unified subject histories, CAD-to-CAD connectivity directly improves situational awareness and investigative speed.


In this instance, that connectivity turned a series of disconnected incidents into a solvable case and significantly reduced the time between crime detection and arrest.
