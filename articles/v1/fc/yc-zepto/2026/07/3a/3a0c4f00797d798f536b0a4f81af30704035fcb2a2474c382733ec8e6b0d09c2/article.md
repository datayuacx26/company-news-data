# A Field Guide to My Data Science Internship at Zepto

> ***🎙️ The Observer’s Log: Entry #01*** *“We arrive at the bustling, hyper velocity habitat known as Zepto. Here, time is not measured in hours or days, but in ten minute increments. Today, a new species enters the environment: The Data Science Intern. Notice how they cautiously approach their workstation, armed with Python notebooks and an eagerness to understand the complex mathematical rhythms of quick commerce.”*


When I joined Zepto as a Data Science Intern on the Pricing team, I knew I was stepping into one of India’s fastest moving tech ecosystems. But reading about quick commerce at scale and actually building for it are two entirely different beasts.


Working alongside my manager, Deepak Dhankani, and my buddy, Abhijeet Anand, my mission for the season was clear: dive deep into the world of **inventory based pricing** and build models that could react to dynamic supply and demand in near real time.


Here is a look into my journey navigating the engineering, operations, and culture that make Zepto tick.


### Phase 1: Observing the Physical Backbone (The First 48 Hours)


> ***🎙️ The Observer’s Log: Entry #02*** *“Before the intern can manipulate the digital data streams, they must first understand the physical realm. To build models for the wild, one must step into the wild. And so, the migration to the Dark Stores begins.”*


My onboarding during the first two days was far from a standard presentation slideshow. We were immersed straight into the philosophy and physical reality of the company.


We kicked things off with an interactive session with Co-founder Kaivalya Vohra (KV). What struck me most was his clarity and humility; he emphasized that fast experimentation and deep operational thinking are what truly power the company. We also sat down with CTO Nikhil Mittal, who broke down Zepto’s engineering philosophy: building systems that are fundamentally **observable, scalable, and resilient** .


The theoretical architecture came to life when we visited Zepto’s Super Stores and Dark Stores. Standing inside a Dark Store is like watching a highly optimized relay race, watching inventory being stocked, orders picked, and riders dispatched; all in real time where every second is accounted for *.*


### Phase 2: No “Intern” Labels in the Wild


> ***🎙️ The Observer’s Log: Entry #03*** *“In many corporate ecosystems, the intern is kept in protective captivity, working on simulated data and sandbox projects that never see the light of day. But at Zepto, the environment demands immediate integration. Survival means shipping to production.”*


One of the most refreshing aspects of my internship was that there was no artificial divide between “intern work” and “full time work.” From day one, I was entrusted with real ownership.


Working on inventory based pricing, I was tasked with developing a pricing model that optimizes inventory availability while ensuring alignment with Zepto’s Pricing strategy. Under the guidance of Amit, Deepak and Abhijeet, I went from ideation to deployment at lightning speed. Just **two weeks into my internship, my model was deployed live on a pilot!** To scale the model across Zepto’s massive footprint, I regularly sat in on strategy meetings with Product and Planning teams. Getting out of the data science silo taught me three core skills:


- **Translating Business to Tech:** I learned how to dissect business requirement, ask the right questions about constraints, and frame it into a concrete mathematical optimization and predictive modeling problem.
- **Experimentation Design:** Structured A/B tests in a hyper local delivery environment; selecting control vs. test dark stores, accounting for demand spillover, and defining statistical significance for real-time metrics.
- **Shipping to Production:** Connected the model directly to live dark store inventory and pricing streams using ETL pipelines.


### Phase 3: The Mega Scale of the Mother Hub


> ***🎙️ The Observer’s Log: Entry #04*** *“We now venture into the epicenter of the supply chain: The Mother Hub. Here, the sheer volume of data we process on our screens manifests as towering aisles of physical goods moving in automated harmony.”*


Midway through the internship, our team took a field trip to a Zepto Mother Hub. If the Dark Stores are the sprinters of the ecosystem, the Mother Hub is the beating heart.


Witnessing the warehouse automation in action, a facility efficiently processing millions of products was mind-blowing. But what made me proudest as a data scientist was seeing in-house AI initiatives running live on the ground. A standout moment was seeing **OdinEye** (Zepto’s scalable computer vision face authentication system for attendance) seamlessly processing personnel in real time. Seeing machine learning solve tangible operational friction at scale reaffirmed why we build the way we do.


### Phase 4: Pack Bonding


> ***🎙️ The Observer’s Log: Entry #05*** *“Even the most industrious pack must engage in ritualistic play to maintain social cohesion. Notice how the hierarchy dissolves on the turf, replaced by friendly competition and adrenaline.”*


It wasn’t all algorithms, pilot testing, and deployment metrics. The Data Science team made sure we took time to unwind and bond as a squad.


Our team outing was an absolute blast, featuring a high energy mix of football, go-karting, and an unforgettable game of laser tag. Moments like these, laughing, competing, and collaborating outside the office, made the team feel like a community.


### Evolution of a Data Scientist


> ***🎙️ The Observer’s Log: Final Entry “*** *As the season concludes, we observe a transformed individual. The hesitation of day one is gone, replaced by the confidence of an engineer who has tested their hypotheses in the real world. The intern has successfully evolved.”*


Reflecting on my time at Zepto, I realise I’ve gained much more than just technical proficiency in pricing models and data pipelines. I’ve learned how to build with velocity, collaborate cross functionally with product and planning teams, and design data science models that generate business impact.
