### Ameris Bank Promotes Homeownership with Wealth Watchers in Northeast Florida


06/25/2026


[Download this Press Release (opens in new window) DOCX](https://s205.q4cdn.com/432887073/files/doc_news/2026/Jun/25/Press-Release-Ameris-Bank-Promotes-Homeownership-with-Wealth-Watchers-in-Northeast-Florida.docx)


***$50,000 donation continues multi-year partnership***


ATLANTA (June 25, 2026) – Ameris Bank presented a $50,000 community investment to Wealth Watchers in support of ongoing efforts to expand financial education, economic empowerment, and homeownership opportunities throughout Northeast Florida.


The donation highlights the strong partnership between Ameris Bank and Wealth Watchers, organizations that share a commitment to helping individuals and families build wealth through sustainable homeownership and financial wellness.


“This investment reflects Ameris Bank’s commitment to creating pathways to homeownership and supporting organizations that are making a meaningful impact in the communities we serve,” said Ameris Bank Director of Community Lending Clyde Anderson. “Wealth Watchers has proven successful in helping connect prospective homebuyers to educational resources, down payment assistance opportunities, and trusted guidance throughout their homeownership journey.”


“The Ameris donation allows Wealth Watches to extend financial education and much needed support to our community. We believe it is important to help families create a legacy and build generational wealth through homeownership,” said Carrie Davis, president and chief executive officer of Wealth Watchers.
