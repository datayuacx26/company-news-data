# Texxon Holding Limited Announces Commencement of Operations at Henan Polystyrene Production Facility, A Milestone in Manufacturing Expansion Strategy

**Texxon Holding (Nasdaq:NPT)** began operations at its new Henan polystyrene production facility in June 2026, completing construction and commissioning. The plant in Taiqian County, Henan, has up to **600,000 tons** annual capacity and supports GPPS and HIPS production for appliance and consumer goods markets.


The facility is located within a major manufacturing and logistics corridor, within a 500-km radius of key home appliance hubs, and has passed required safety, environmental and regulatory inspections. Texxon views this as a key step in its shift toward an integrated manufacturing and supply chain platform.
