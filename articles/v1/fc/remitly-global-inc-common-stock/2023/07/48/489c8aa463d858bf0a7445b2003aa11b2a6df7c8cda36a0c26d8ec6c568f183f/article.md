Whether you have roots in India, Bolivia, Sri Lanka, Trinidad or anywhere in between, August likely holds a date that matters to you.

## August 2026 Independence and National Days Around the World


August is a landmark month for national pride. Across every continent, countries pause to remember the moments that shaped them.


### Swiss National Day (August 1)


Swiss National Day 2026 falls on Saturday, August 1. It commemorates the Federal Charter of 1291, the founding pact signed by the cantons of Uri, Schwyz and Unterwalden. Unlike holidays built around revolution, this one honors a peaceful agreement between neighbors. Swiss families mark the day with hilltop bonfires, fireworks over lakes and community gatherings. Learn more about **[Swiss National Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/swiss-national-day-everything-you-need-to-know/) .**


### Yorkshire Day, United Kingdom (August 1)


[Yorkshire Day is celebrated on August 1 in the United Kingdom, honoring the historic county of Yorkshire](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/yorkshire-day-celebrating-the-largest-county-in-england/) . The day promotes its rich heritage, culture and achievements—from sweeping landscapes to contributions in literature, music and sports. Festivities include parades, concerts and community events that showcase Yorkshire’s distinct identity.


### Jamaican Independence Day (August 6)


On August 6, 2026, Jamaica marks 64 years of independence from British rule, achieved in 1962. For Jamaicans at home and across the diaspora, the day is a celebration of resilience and cultural pride, complete with reggae, jerk chicken and the black, green and gold flag. Discover how **[Jamaican Independence Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/jamaican-independence-day/)** is celebrated.


### Bolivia Independence Day (August 6)


Bolivia Independence Day falls on August 6, 2026, marking 201 years since Bolivia declared independence from Spain in 1825. The largest celebrations take place in La Paz and Sucre, with military parades, folk dances like the cueca and morenada, and traditional dishes such as salteñas. Read about the history behind **[Bolivia Independence Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/bolivia-independence-day-history-traditions-how-its-celebrated/) .**


### Singapore National Day (August 9)


Singapore National Day 2026 falls on Sunday, August 9, commemorating the island’s independence in 1965. The National Day Parade features military displays, aerial performances and a sea of red-and-white flags. Singaporeans abroad often watch the livestream and gather over dishes like chicken rice and laksa. Explore **[Singapore National Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/singapore-national-day/)** .


### Ecuador Independence Day (August 10)


Ecuador Independence Day on August 10, 2026, honors the 1809 Grito de Independencia—the first organized uprising against Spanish rule in Quito. Celebrations include military parades, cultural performances in Plaza Grande and traditional foods like llapingachos and ceviche de camarón. Learn more about **[Ecuador Independence Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/ecuador-independence-day-history-traditions-how-to-celebrate/) .**


### Pakistan Independence Day (August 14)


[Pakistan Independence Day, celebrated on August 14, marks the country’s freedom from British rule](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/pakistan-independence-day/) in 1947—a momentous date that symbolizes the birth of a sovereign nation. The day is observed with flag-hoisting ceremonies, parades, cultural events and fireworks displays, fostering a deep sense of patriotism and national unity.


### India Independence Day (August 15)


[India Independence Day on August 15 commemorates the country’s liberation from British colonial rule](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/india-independence-day/) in 1947. It is a momentous occasion that marks the birth of an independent and sovereign India. The day is celebrated with flag-hoisting ceremonies, patriotic speeches, cultural performances and festivities across the country, as Indians reflect on their hard-won freedom.


### South Korea Liberation Day aka Gwangbokjeol (August 15)


Gwangbokjeol falls on Saturday, August 15, 2026, marking 81 years since Korea’s liberation from Japanese colonial rule in 1945. The name means “the day the light returned.” Koreans mark the day by displaying the Taegukgi flag, attending official ceremonies and enjoying evening fireworks. Read about **[South Korea Liberation Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/south-korea-liberation-day-gwangbokjeol/)** .


### Liechtenstein National Day (August 15)


Liechtenstein National Day also falls on August 15, 2026. In one of Europe’s most intimate national celebrations, the reigning Prince opens the doors of Vaduz Castle and invites the entire nation in for a garden party, followed by public fireworks. Discover **[Liechtenstein National Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/liechtenstein-national-day/)** .


### Indonesia Independence Day (August 17)


[Indonesia Independence Day on August 17 commemorates the country’s declaration of independence](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/indonesia-independence-day-history-traditions-and-celebrations/) from Dutch colonial rule in 1945. Indonesians mark the day with flag-hoisting ceremonies, parades, cultural events and sports competitions, coming together to honor their nation’s history and celebrate its sovereignty.


### Gabon Independence Day (August 17)


Gabon Independence Day 2026 falls on Monday, August 17, marking independence from France in 1960. From the capital Libreville to smaller towns, Gabonese people celebrate with military parades, traditional music and dance, and community gatherings in the national colors of green, yellow and blue. Learn more about **[Gabon Independence Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/gabon-independence-day-history-traditions-how-its-celebrated/) .**


### Afghan Independence Day (August 19)


Afghan Independence Day, known as Jashn-e-Istiqlal, falls on August 19, 2026. It marks the signing of the Treaty of Rawalpindi in 1919, which freed Afghanistan from British influence. Afghans celebrate with traditional food like kabuli pulao, music and family gatherings, proudly displaying the black, red and green flag. Read about **[Afghan Independence Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/afghan-independence-day-date-history-traditions/) .**


### National Heroes Day, Philippines (Last Monday of August)


[National Heroes Day in the Philippines is observed on the last Monday of August—August 31, 2026](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/national-heroes-day/) . Different from June’s Independence Day, this observance pays tribute to soldiers and national icons who made significant contributions to Philippine history and independence. Parades, flag-raising ceremonies and wreath-laying events are held nationwide to honor those who fought for freedom and the welfare of the Filipino people.


### Malaysian Independence Day, Hari Merdeka (August 31)


Hari Merdeka falls on Monday, August 31, 2026, marking Malaysia’s independence from British rule in 1957. The word “Merdeka” means freedom. Malaysian-American communities celebrate with cultural festivals, traditional dishes like nasi lemak and rendang, and live broadcasts of the national parade from Kuala Lumpur. Explore **[Malaysian Independence Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/malaysian-independence-day/)** .


### Trinidad and Tobago Independence Day (August 31)


Trinidad and Tobago Independence Day also falls on August 31, 2026, marking independence from the United Kingdom in 1962. The day comes alive with soca and calypso music, steelpan performances and food like doubles and roti. The nation’s motto captures the spirit: “Together we aspire, together we achieve.” Learn about **[Trinidad and Tobago Independence Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/trinidad-and-tobago-independence-day/)** .


### Caribbean Emancipation Day (August 1)


Emancipation Day falls on Saturday, August 1, 2026. It commemorates the abolition of slavery across the British Caribbean on August 1, 1834, which freed roughly 800,000 people. Countries including Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados and Guyana mark the day with cultural performances, remembrance events and family gatherings. Read about **[Caribbean Emancipation Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/caribbean-emancipation-day-dates-history-and-how-to-celebrate/)** .


### Colorado Day (August 1)


Closer to home in the US, Colorado Day falls on Saturday, August 1, 2026. It marks Colorado’s admission as the 38th state in 1876—just weeks before the nation’s centennial, earning it the nickname “the Centennial State.” Many state parks offer free or discounted admission on the day. Discover **[Colorado Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/colorado-day-how-to-celebrate-the-centennial-state/)** .


## Cultural Festivals and Observances Around the World


Beyond independence, August brings vibrant cultural festivals rooted in centuries of tradition.


### Onam, Kerala’s Harvest Festival (August 17–26)


Onam 2026 runs for 10 days, from August 17 through August 26, with the main day of Thiruvonam falling on August 26. Kerala’s most beloved festival celebrates the mythical return of King Mahabali. Highlights include intricate flower carpets called pookalam, the dramatic snake-boat races known as vallam kali, and a grand vegetarian feast, the Onasadya, served on banana leaves. Malayali communities worldwide keep the spirit alive with community events and shared meals. Learn everything about **[Onam](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/onam-everything-you-need-to-know-about-keralas-harvest-festival/)** .


### Obon Day, Japan (Mid-August)


[Obon is a traditional Buddhist festival celebrated in Japan during mid-August, also known as the Festival of Souls](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/obon-day/) . It honors the spirits of deceased ancestors and welcomes them back home. Families gather to pay respects at ancestral graves, light lanterns, and participate in traditional dances called Bon Odori. Obon is a time of deep remembrance and a celebration of the enduring bond between the living and the departed.


### Raksha Bandhan (August 18)


Raksha Bandhan 2026 falls on Tuesday, August 18. This Hindu festival honors the bond between siblings. Sisters tie a sacred thread called a rakhi around their brothers’ wrists as a symbol of love and protection, and brothers offer gifts in return. Indian families abroad often mail rakhis home or celebrate over video call. Read about **[Raksha Bandhan](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/raksha-bandhan-day/)** .


### Nikini Poya (August 21)


Nikini Poya falls on Friday, August 21, 2026. This Sri Lankan Buddhist full-moon holiday commemorates the First Buddhist Council, held after the Buddha’s death to preserve his teachings. Devotees dress in white, visit temples, observe the Eight Precepts and offer food to monks. Discover more about **[Nikini Poya](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/nikini-poya-what-it-is-and-how-its-celebrated/)** .


### Qixi Festival, Chinese Valentine’s Day (August 20)


Qixi Festival falls on Thursday, August 20, 2026. Rooted in the 2,000-year-old legend of two star-crossed lovers, Zhinü and Niulang, it’s celebrated with romantic gestures, gift-giving and star-gazing. Couples across China and the diaspora exchange flowers, jewelry and thoughtful presents. Learn about the **[Qixi Festival](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/qixi-festival-chinese-valentines-day-everything-you-need-to-know/) .**


### Hungry Ghost Festival (August 12)


The Hungry Ghost Festival peaks on August 12, 2026, with the broader Ghost Month running from July 28 to August 25. Rooted in Buddhist and Taoist belief, families honor wandering ancestral spirits through food offerings, the burning of joss paper and communal getai performances. It’s observed widely in China, Singapore, Malaysia and Taiwan. Read about the **[Hungry Ghost Festival](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/hungry-ghost-festival-dates-traditions-and-rituals/)** .


### La Tomatina, Spain (August 26)


La Tomatina 2026 takes place on Wednesday, August 26, in the town of Buñol, Spain. In this famous tomato-throwing festival, tens of thousands of participants hurl over 150,000 tomatoes at each other during a one-hour battle. Tickets and advance planning are essential. Get everything you need to know about **[La Tomatina](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/la-tomatina-everything-you-need-to-know/)** .


## Catholic and Christian Feast Days in August 2026

St. Alphonsus Liguori Feast Day falls on Saturday, August 1, 2026. It honors the founder of the Redemptorists and a Doctor of the Church, known for his compassionate approach to moral theology. ## Awareness Days and Notable Observances


August also carries several days dedicated to reflection, appreciation and connection with the wider world.


### International Day of the World’s Indigenous People (August 9)


[Observed on August 9, the International Day of the World’s Indigenous People](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/what-is-world-indigenous-peoples-day/) recognizes and promotes the rights, cultures and contributions of indigenous communities worldwide. The day shines a light on the challenges facing indigenous populations, advocating for their empowerment, the protection of their ancestral lands, and the preservation of their unique cultural heritage.


### Japan’s Mountain Day (August 11)


[In Japan, August 11 is celebrated as Mountain Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/mountain-day-in-japan/) , a national holiday dedicated to appreciating the beauty and significance of mountains. Established in 2016, it provides an opportunity for people to connect with nature and acknowledge the important role mountains play in Japanese culture, history and ecology. The day aligns with the Japanese concept of *shinrin-yoku* , or forest bathing, which emphasizes the therapeutic benefits of spending time in nature.


### World Elephant Day (August 12)


World Elephant Day falls on Wednesday, August 12, 2026. It raises awareness of the threats facing African and Asian elephants, including poaching and habitat loss, and unites conservationists and everyday people around protecting these iconic animals. Learn how you can help on **[World Elephant Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/world-elephant-day-how-you-can-help-save-elephants/)** .


### International Youth Day (August 12)


[International Youth Day is observed on August 12 and celebrates the power and potential of young people in creating positive change](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/international-youth-day/) . The day recognizes the unique challenges faced by young individuals, promotes their rights and well-being, and encourages their active involvement in social, economic and political life.


### International Left-Handers Day (August 13)


[International Left-Handers Day on August 13 celebrates the uniqueness of left-handed individuals](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/international-left-handers-day/) and raises awareness about the challenges they may face. The day recognizes their contributions across many fields and promotes equal opportunities for left-handed people everywhere.


### World Humanitarian Day (August 19)


World Humanitarian Day falls on Wednesday, August 19, 2026. Designated by the United Nations, it honors aid workers who risk their lives helping people in crisis and raises awareness of humanitarian needs worldwide. Read about why **[World Humanitarian Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/world-humanitarian-day/)** matters.


### National Aviation Day (August 19)


National Aviation Day also falls on August 19, 2026—Orville Wright’s birthday. Established by President Franklin D. Roosevelt in 1939, it honors the history of flight and the pioneers who made air travel possible. Learn about[National Aviation Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/national-aviation-day-history-facts-and-how-to-celebrate/) .


### US Coast Guard Birthday (August 4)


The US Coast Guard Birthday falls on Tuesday, August 4, 2026, marking 236 years since the service’s founding in 1790. It honors the men and women who protect America’s coastlines and rescue those in danger at sea. Read about the **[US Coast Guard Birthday](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/us-coast-guard-birthday-what-to-know/)** .


### Senior Citizens Day (August 21)


National Senior Citizens Day falls on Friday, August 21, 2026. Established in 1988, it recognizes the contributions and wisdom of older adults. It’s a meaningful occasion to call, visit or support the elders in your life. Learn how to honor[Senior Citizens Day](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/world-senior-citizens-day/) .


### Perseid Meteor Shower (August 11–13)


The Perseid meteor shower peaks around August 11–13, 2026. Under dark skies, stargazers can see up to 100 meteors per hour—no telescope needed. It’s one of the most reliable and beloved light shows of the year. Find out when and where to watch the **[Perseid meteor shower](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/when-is-the-perseid-meteor-shower/)** .


## Popular National Days in the United States


Alongside global celebrations, the US observes a colorful mix of lighthearted national days throughout August.


### National Sisters Day and National Friendship Day (First Sunday in August)


Both fall on the first Sunday of August, which is August 2, 2026.[National Sisters Day celebrates the bond between sisters](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/national-sisters-day/) , while[National Friendship Day honors the friends who enrich our lives](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/how-to-celebrate-national-friendship-day/) . Both are occasions to reconnect and spend quality time together.


### National Watermelon Day (August 3)


August 3 celebrates the juicy fruit that defines summer. Host a watermelon-themed gathering or experiment with creative recipes to savor the season’s sweetness.


### National Oyster Day (August 5)


[National Oyster Day on August 5](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/national-oyster-day/) is a culinary celebration of these prized mollusks, enjoyed both raw and cooked for their distinctive briny flavor.


### National Root Beer Float Day (August 6)


August 6 pays tribute to the classic American pairing of vanilla ice cream and effervescent root beer—a nostalgic dessert worth revisiting.


### National Book Lovers Day (August 9)


[National Book Lovers Day on August 9 celebrates the joy of reading](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/celebrate-national-book-lovers-day/) . Curl up with a favorite book, explore a new genre or share a recommendation with a friend.

## Frequently Asked Questions About August National Days and Holidays


### What is the significance of August national days and holidays?


August is a month filled with celebrations, both patriotic and cultural. Countries like India, Pakistan, Indonesia, and Jamaica celebrate their independence in August, while other countries honor their heritage through festivals and events. In addition to these national observances, there are also significant Catholic feast days and global awareness days during this month.


### Why are there so many independence days in August?


Many countries chose to declare their independence from colonizing powers during August due to favorable weather conditions for organizing protests or uprisings. For example, India gained its independence from British rule on August 15th after a long struggle led by Mahatma Gandhi.


### What Are Some Notable Independence Days in August?


Numerous countries celebrate their independence in August. For instance, India commemorates its independence on August 15, Indonesia celebrates August 17, and Ecuador marks August 10. These dates are often celebrated with parades, cultural events, and public gatherings.


### Are There Other Important Events Besides Independence Days in August?


Yes, August also features notable Catholic feast days such as the Assumption of Mary on August 15, as well as global awareness days like International Youth Day on August 12. These events highlight diverse cultural, spiritual, and social themes.
