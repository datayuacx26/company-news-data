# Remitly Business Adds Bulk Payments and Send by Link

Cross-border payments are one of the hardest jobs you take on as a small business owner. Manual payment runs take hours. A single typo in a recipient’s name can hold up your vendor for days. Remitly Business is built to take that work — and risk — off your plate.


Remitly Business is built on the same infrastructure that 9.6 million Remitly customers already trust to move money across borders. It’s designed for an owner running the books, not a corporate treasury team. More than 20,000 businesses already use the product.


Today, Remitly is introducing two new features built to address the specific needs of small businesses like yours: Bulk Payments and Send by Link.


**Bulk Payments Handle Payment Runs Faster** **** With Bulk Payments, you can pay multiple recipients in a single workflow — contractors, freelancers, suppliers, or international team members. Just select your recipients from a list, see delivery speed, fees, and totals for every payment in one view, and approve. If your business runs a monthly pay cycle


, what used to take half a day could now take minutes.


**Send by Link Reduces Payment Errors**


Incorrect recipient details — a misspelled name, a mismatched government ID, an outdated bank account number — are one of the leading causes of failed cross-border payments today. Collecting those details usually means asking a vendor or contractor to share sensitive banking information by email or other less secure channels — an awkward exchange most senders would rather avoid.


With Send by Link, you can initiate a payment using only your recipient’s email and phone number. Your recipient provides their payment details directly through a secure link, in their own time, so you never have to collect or see sensitive information.


*“When you pay your mother, you trust her before you ever hit send. When you pay a contractor on the other side of the world, you’re either handing sensitive details to someone you barely know — or asking them to,”* said Ankur Tiwari, VP, Product, Remitly.


*“We built Send by Link so that never has to happen.”*
