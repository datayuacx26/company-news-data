# GoHealth Named to Newsweek’s List of the Most Trustworthy Companies in America 2025

GoHealth has been named to Newsweek’s list of the Most Trustworthy Companies in America 2025. This award is presented by Newsweek and Statista Inc., the world-leading statistics portal and industry ranking provider. The award list was announced on March 26, 2025, and[can be viewed on Newsweek.com](https://rankings.newsweek.com/most-trustworthy-companies-america-2025) .


The **Most Trustworthy Companies in America 2025** list was built on an innovative methodology consisting of two evaluation components:


1. **Survey Results:** 25,000 U.S. residents evaluated companies on three pillars of trust.
2. **Social Listening Analysis:** Mentions about the companies from various internet segments were gathered and rated according to their sentiment, virality, and reach.


The 700 companies with the highest score have been awarded as the Most Trustworthy Companies in America 2025.


GoHealth is proud to be recognized on Newsweek’s list of the Most Trustworthy Companies in America for the second year in a row.
