# GoHealth to Present at Medicarians 2024: The Home of Senior Health, Wealth, and AgeTech

CHICAGO, March 28, 2024 /Globe Newswire/ —[GoHealth, Inc.](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=3790888-1&h=707344162&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gohealth.com%2F&a=GoHealth%2C+Inc.) (GoHealth) (NASDAQ: GOCO), a leading health insurance marketplace and Medicare-focused digital health company, announced the company will present at Medicarians 2024: The Home of Senior Health, Wealth, and AgeTech on Wednesday, April 10, 2024 in Las Vegas, Nevada. Chief Executive Officer, Vijay Kotte, will speak on the Plenary mainstage at 9:05 a.m. Pacific Time.


More information for the conference can be found at[https://medicarians.com/](https://medicarians.com/) .
