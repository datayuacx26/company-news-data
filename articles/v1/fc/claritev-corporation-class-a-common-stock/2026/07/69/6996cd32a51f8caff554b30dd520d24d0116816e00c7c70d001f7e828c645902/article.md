# The State of IDR in 2025: Trends, Market Dynamics, and Strategic Insights for Payers


Navigating rising dispute volume, provider-favored outcomes, and growing financial exposure in the evolving IDR landscape.


Independent Dispute Resolution (IDR) activity continues to surge, with more than 1.18 million disputes filed in the first half of 2025 alone — **a 39% increase over the prior six months** . At the same time, providers prevailed in approximately 88% of determinations, creating significant financial and operational challenges for health plans.


This white paper examines the evolving IDR landscape, highlighting key trends, outcomes, and market dynamics shaping payer strategy. Learn how a more data-informed approach can help organizations reduce financial exposure, improve defensibility, and navigate increasing dispute volume with greater confidence.


## What You’ll Learn:


- Understand the latest IDR volume, outcome, and market trends
- Identify key drivers behind provider-favored dispute outcomes
- Navigate eligibility challenges and administrative complexity more effectively
- Strengthen reimbursement and offer strategies with data-driven insights
- Improve financial performance through more defensible IDR decisions
