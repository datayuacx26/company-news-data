# The Next Evolution of Payment Integrity


Reducing payment leakage through connected workflows, advanced analytics, and responsible AI.


Improper payments continue to cost health plans billions each year, while many payment integrity programs remain fragmented across pre-pay and post-pay functions. As claims grow more complex, organizations need a more connected approach that prevents recurring errors instead of repeatedly recovering them after the fact.


This white paper explores how responsible AI, advanced analytics, and connected workflows can help health plans transform payment integrity into a continuous learning system — reducing payment leakage, improving accuracy, and driving smarter decision-making across the claims lifecycle.


## What You’ll Learn:


- Why connected payment integrity is the next evolution of payment integrity programs
- How to turn post-pay findings into proactive pre-pay interventions
- High-impact AI use cases that improve efficiency, consistency, and outcomes
- Key governance, data, and interoperability requirements for responsible AI adoption
- How to measure success with KPIs that support continuous improvement
