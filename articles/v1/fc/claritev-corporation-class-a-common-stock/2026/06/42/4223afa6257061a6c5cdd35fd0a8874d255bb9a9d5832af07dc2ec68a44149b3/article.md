# Beyond Recovery: Designing the Next Generation Payment Integrity Program


*For years, payment integrity success has been measured largely by recoveries – but recoveries tell only part of the story.*


In this webinar, experienced leaders from Claritev will challenge the traditional recovery- centric mindset and explain why recoveries alone fall short as a measure of success.


View it to explore more meaningful, forward- looking ways to evaluate impact, maximize program value, and redefine what “success” should really mean in payment integrity.


## In this webinar, you’ll learn how to:


- Understand the critical role prevention should play in a modern payment integrity program
- Effectively evaluate pre-pay programs and their performance
- Balance pre-pay and post-pay approaches for optimal results
- Leverage post-pay findings to strengthen pre-pay solutions
- Build a closed-loop payment integrity program that drives continuous improvement
