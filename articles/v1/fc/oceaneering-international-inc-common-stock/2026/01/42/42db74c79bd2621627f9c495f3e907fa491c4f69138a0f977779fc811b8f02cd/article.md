Houston – Oceaneering International, Inc. announced that its[Integrity Management and Digital Solutions](https://www.oceaneering.com/asset-integrity-management/) (IMDS) business has secured a four-year extension to its inspection support contract for multiple Equinor assets in Norway. The extension period began on January 1, 2026.


The scope includes[In-Service Inspection](https://www.oceaneering.com/asset-integrity-management/inspection/) ,[Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)](https://www.oceaneering.com/asset-integrity-management/inspection/conventional-ndt/) ,[Advanced NDT](https://www.oceaneering.com/asset-integrity-management/inspection/advanced-ndt/) ,[Rope Access](https://www.oceaneering.com/asset-integrity-management/inspection/ensuring-access/) , and the provision of Inspection[Engineers](https://www.oceaneering.com/asset-integrity-management/integrity-engineering/) . Services will cover three onshore installations Hammerfest, Kollsnes, and Kårstø, as well as three offshore assets Aasta Hansteen, Norne, and Johan Castberg, located in the Barents and Norwegian Seas.


Erlend Fremstad, Director of IMDS for Scandinavia, said: “We are proud to continue our collaboration with Equinor across these six assets. This extension reinforces our commitment to operational excellence, with a strong focus on HSE and quality performance. We value Equinor’s trust in our ability to deliver safe, reliable, and innovative inspection solutions.”
