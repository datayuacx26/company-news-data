# Trane Drives HVAC Innovation with New Commercial Solutions for Electrification, Data Centers, and Building Performance


The latest innovations focus on enhancing efficiency, reliability, and sustainability for building owners and operators.


Media Contact


Trane Technologies Media Relations


Trane by Trane Technologies


media@tranetechnologies.com


**DAVIDSON, N.C., Sept. 23, 2025** –Trane ®–by Trane Technologies (NYSE: TT), a global climate innovator, announced a new portfolio of commercial HVAC solutions designed to meet the evolving demands of the built environment, including new electrification of heat technologies, advanced offerings for mission-critical data centers, and redesigned solutions that help enhance overall building performance and serviceability, reinforcing Trane’s commitment to industry-leading innovation.


"Trane is dedicated to pioneering solutions that not only meet today’s challenges but also anticipate the needs of tomorrow," said Oakley Roberts, Vice President of Product Management, Commercial HVAC Americas, Trane Technologies. "Our innovations span the needs of the built environment, from providing the resilient, high-performance cooling required in data centers to developing affordable, electrified heating systems that help our customers achieve their business and sustainability goals. We are delivering the cutting-edge technology our customers require to operate more efficiently and sustainably."


## Advancing Electrification and Sustainability


Trane continues to expand its portfolio of sustainable solutions that support the electrification of heat and help customers reduce their carbon footprint. ****** The[Ascend™ Air-to-Water Heat Pump (ACX)](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/products-systems/chillers/air-cooled-chillers/ascend-air-to-water-heat-pump.html) is built upon the proven Ascend platform, with expanded capacities ranging from 80-230 tons. The ACX offers a cost-effective, all-electric heating solution for smaller commercial buildings, capable of operating efficiently in temperatures as low as -15°F and delivers hot water temperatures up to 145°F. Additionally, the now features a factory-installed partial heat recovery option, which captures and repurposes waste heat to help reduce energy consumption and support bold sustainability goals.


[Ascend™ Air-Cooled Chiller (ACS)](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/products-systems/chillers/air-cooled-chillers/ascend-model-acs-chillers.html) now features a factory-installed partial heat recovery option, which captures and repurposes waste heat to help reduce energy consumption and support bold sustainability goals.


## AI & Smart Building Technology


Designed to help enhance building performance and drive future readiness, Trane is introducing[ARIA](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/services/operate-maintain-repair/aria-ai.html) , an AI-Building Agent. This generative AI solution analyzes equipment and building data to provide actionable insights and recommendations, empowering facility teams with continuously updated intelligence to help streamline operations, support faster decisions, and help improve performance.


Trane is also adding[Nuvolo IWMS](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/services/operate-maintain-repair/nuvolo-asset-management.html) to its smart building lineup. Nuvolo is a comprehensive integrated workplace management system that brings together maintenance and asset management to support customers across the full building lifecycle. These new solutions provide a unified platform for managing diverse building and workplace needs and ensuring long-term operational excellence.


## Innovative Solutions for Data Centers


Trane is[expanding its thermal management system portfolio for data centers](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/trane-expands-high-efficiency-precision-cooling-capabilities-for-data-center-thermal-management-systems.html) with cooling solutions engineered for AI workloads, sustainability, and future growth.


A key component is the new[Trane® Computer Room Air-Handler (CRAH)](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/trane-expands-high-efficiency-precision-cooling-capabilities-for-data-center-thermal-management-systems.html) , designed specifically for the unique cooling challenges of data centers. The[CRAH](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/products-systems/air-handlers/crah-unit.html) helps support temperature control to support critical equipment operation.


To manage the rotation, staging, and sequencing of chillers within thermal energy management systems, Trane is also extending its[chiller plant control](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/products-systems/building-management---automation/building-automation-systems/tracer-sc-plus/chiller-plant-control.html) facility programming application, the through the Tracer® SC+ system controller. Harnessing Trane’s deep systems knowledge and expertise, chiller plant control converts complex programming into a simplified, efficient solution that can be customized to meet the unique needs and conditions of modern data centers.


As data centers continue to evolve with increasing capacity demands, tighter timelines, and zero tolerance for downtime, Trane is introducing significant enhancements to its comprehensive portfolio of data center support[services](https://www.trane.com/commercial/north-america/us/en/services.html) . These advancements underscore Trane’s commitment to delivering end-to-end thermal management support, helping improve uptime, efficiency, and long-term performance for mission critical data center operations.
