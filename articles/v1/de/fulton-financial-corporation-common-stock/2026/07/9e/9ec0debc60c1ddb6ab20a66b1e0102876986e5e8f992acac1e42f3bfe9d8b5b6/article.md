# Ann Mehra Joins Fulton Bank Advisory Board

**Lancaster, Pa. (Feb. 23, 2026)** – Fulton Bank, N.A., a wholly-owned subsidiary of Fulton Financial Corporation (NASDAQ: FULT), today announced that Ann Mehra has joined its Regional Advisory Board.


Mehra has served as an industry advisor to the U.S. Department of Commerc e’s Internet


of Things Advisory Board, having been nominated by the Deputy Assistant Secretary of the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. In this role, she helped develop the bipartisan National Internet of Things (IoT) strategy, a report assessing IoT use in the United States and recommending actions to accelerate the secure and economically beneficial interconnectivity of devices and systems.


“We are thrilled to welcome Ann to our advisory board. Her background in government and the technology sector aligns well with our growing life science and technology banking team,” said Joe Durham, Market President at Fulton Bank. “Ann is a fantastic partner to help us continue to grow and serve as trusted advisors to banking customers.”


Mehra has over 25 years of experience working in information technology and advancing public-private partnerships. Previously, she led public healthcare verticals for CA Technologies (acquired by Broadcom), and Splunk (acquired by Cisco). During her tenure at Sun Microsystems (acquired by Oracle), Mehra helped develop community-based, regional partner ecosystems that led to the birth of SiriusXM Satellite Radio and Neustar for the Washington, D.C. metro region.


Mehra holds a bachelor’s degree in economics from The George Washington University, a master’s degree in biotechnology with a concentration in neuroscience from Harvard University, and an Artificial Intelligence Executive Scholar certification from Massachusetts Institute of Technology.


The Fulton Bank Advisory Board is comprised of members of the business community who provide advice and recommendations to help Fulton fulfill its purpose of changing lives for the better.
