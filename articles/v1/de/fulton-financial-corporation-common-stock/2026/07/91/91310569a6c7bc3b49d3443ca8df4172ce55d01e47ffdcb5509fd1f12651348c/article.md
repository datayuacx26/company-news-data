# Fulton Private Bank Announces New Fiduciary Team Leadership

**Lancaster, Pa. (March 6, 2026)** – Fulton Bank, N.A., a wholly-owned subsidiary of Fulton Financial Corporation (NASDAQ: FULT), today announced the promotion of two of its Fulton Private Bank fiduciary team members.


Greg Latimer has been promoted to Chief Fiduciary Officer of Fulton Private Bank. Latimer will provide strategic leadership across Fulton Financial Advisors in fiduciary governance, risk management and compliance. He was formerly Director of Fiduciary Services at Fulton Previously, he served as a Trust Officer for a multi-family office, providing planning and administrative solutions for ultra-high net-worth families. Latimer began his career practicing law with a focus on estate planning and administration, elder law, and transactional matters. He holds a J.D. from Drexel University School of Law and a Certified Trust and Fiduciary Advisor (CTFA) designation. He also serves as a board member for the Lancaster Bar Association and on the Trust Advisory Committee for the Pennsylvania Bankers Association.


Erin Miller has been promoted to Director of Fiduciary Services, assuming strategic leadership of Fulton Private Bank’s fiduciary offerings and providing management of the team of Fiduciary Advisors and Client Service Specialists. She was previously a Fiduciary Advisor. Before joining Fulton, she worked as a Fiduciary Advisor for Wilmington Trust and, prior to that role, as an attorney in trust and estate planning. She holds a J.D. from the Dickinson School of Law and is a member of the Pennsylvania Bar Association, the York County Bar Association and the York County Estate Planning Council.


[Fulton Private Bank](https://www.fultonbank.com/Investments/Fulton-Private-Bank) delivers comprehensive wealth management solutions to affluent and high net worth individuals, families, executives and business owners. Services provided include investment management, private banking, trust, financial planning, charitable and retirement.


“We want to congratulate Greg and Erin on their recent promotions and acknowledge their dedication to leading our team and serving our clients,” said Dara Bachman, President of Fulton Private Bank. “At Fulton Private Bank, our goal is to provide peace of mind through individualized wealth management services from a team of financial experts who know each of their clients’ unique needs. We look forward to continuing that mission with our new leadership.”

#### ABOUT FULTON BANK


Headquartered in Lancaster, Pa., Fulton Bank is a premier community bank in the Mid-Atlantic region. As a subsidiary of Fulton Financial Corporation (Nasdaq: FULT), a $32 billion financial services holding company, Fulton Bank offers a broad array of products and services at more than 200 financial centers across Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Maryland, Delaware, and Virginia. At Fulton Bank, we seek to change lives for the better by building strong customer relationships, providing significant community support and empowering more than 3,300 employees to do the same.
