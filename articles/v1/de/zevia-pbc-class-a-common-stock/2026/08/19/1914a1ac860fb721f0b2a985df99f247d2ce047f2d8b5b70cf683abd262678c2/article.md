### Zevia Statement Regarding Kanen Wealth Management LLC


August 14, 2026


LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Zevia PBC (“Zevia”) (NYSE:ZVIA), the Company that provides naturally delicious, zero sugar better-for-you beverages, today issued the following statement in response to the press release issued by Kanen Wealth Management LLC earlier today:


Zevia welcomes open communication with all of its shareholders and values their input. Zevia's Board of Directors and management team regularly review the Company's strategic priorities and opportunities towards the goal of enhancing value for shareholders and the Company's other stakeholders.


Zevia plans to continue to constructively engage with its shareholders, and Zevia's Board of Directors intends to review the matters raised carefully and thoughtfully.


**About Zevia**


Zevia PBC, a Delaware public benefit corporation designated as a “Certified B Corporation,” is focused on addressing the global health challenges resulting from excess sugar consumption by offering a broad portfolio of zero sugar, zero calorie, naturally sweetened beverages. All Zevia® beverages are made with a handful of simple, plant-based ingredients, contain no artificial sweeteners, and are Non-GMO Project verified, gluten-free, Kosher, and vegan. Zevia is distributed in more than 41,000 retail locations in the U.S. and Canada through a diverse network of major retailers in the grocery, drug, warehouse club, mass, natural, convenience and ecommerce channels.
