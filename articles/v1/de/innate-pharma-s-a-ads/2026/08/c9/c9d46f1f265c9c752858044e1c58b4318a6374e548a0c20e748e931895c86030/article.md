# Innate Pharma announces conference call and webcast

** Fri, 08/14/2026 - 11:30


Innate Pharma SA (Euronext Paris: IPH; Nasdaq: IPHA) (“ **Innate** ” or the “ **Company** ”), today announces that the Company will hold a conference call on Friday, August 14, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. CEST / 8:00 a.m. EDT.


Participants during the call will be:


- Jonathan Dickinson, Chief Executive Officer
- Yannis Morel, Executive Vice President, Chief Operating Officer
- Frédéric Lombard, Senior Vice President, Chief Financial Officer
- Stéphanie Cornen, Vice President, Investor Relations, Communication
