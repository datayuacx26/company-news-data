# Becoming The #1 Mobile Network Operator in Latam Using Cellular Backhaul Over Satellite

Gilat partnered with TIM Brasil to expand 4G coverage across rural Brazil using satellite backhaul technology, supporting the country’s largest cellular backhaul-over-satellite network. The project connected remote communities and enabled digital transformation initiatives, including smart agriculture and IoT applications.
