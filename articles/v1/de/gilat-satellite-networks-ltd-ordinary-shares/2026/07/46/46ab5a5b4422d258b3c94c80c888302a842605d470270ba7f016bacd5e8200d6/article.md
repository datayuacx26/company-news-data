# Pronatel Bridging the Digital Divide in Peru

Gilat Peru partnered with Pronatel to expand high-speed fiber connectivity across remote Andean regions, bringing reliable internet access to nearly 1,000 schools, healthcare centers, police stations, and public institutions. The project included the deployment of nearly 1,000 km of fiber-optic infrastructure and public Wi-Fi hotspots, helping bridge the digital divide and advance digital inclusion in rural Peru.
