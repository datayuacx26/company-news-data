# CFE TEIT Mexico for Bridging the Digital Divide

Gilat helped CFE TEIT expand internet and mobile connectivity to underserved and remote communities across Mexico, supporting the country’s digital inclusion goals. Leveraging satellite technology and nationwide expertise, the project enabled reliable broadband access for communities where traditional infrastructure was not economically feasible.
