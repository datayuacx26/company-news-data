# Tactical Transportable and Re-Deployable SATCOM Ground Stations

This White Paper explores deployable SATCOM ground station solutions designed to ensure operational continuity in contested environments. It highlights how defense and government organizations benefit from mobile, independent Points of Presence (POP), multi-orbit and multi-waveform connectivity, and cyber-hardened network architectures that deliver resilient beyond-line-of-sight communications for mission-critical operations.
