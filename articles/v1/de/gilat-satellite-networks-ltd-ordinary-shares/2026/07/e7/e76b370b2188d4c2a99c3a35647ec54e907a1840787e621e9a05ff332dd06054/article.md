# Ensuring Connectivity Across Sub-Saharan Africa

Gilat supports Africa Mobile Networks (AMN) in delivering mobile broadband services to remote communities across Sub-Saharan Africa through satellite cellular backhaul. The solution helps connect previously unserved villages, enabling affordable 2G, 3G, and 4G services while reducing deployment costs for mobile operators.
