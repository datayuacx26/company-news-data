# GESAC Program in Brazil for Bridging the Digital Divide

Gilat partnered with Telebras and Brazil’s GESAC program to deliver broadband connectivity to more than 1,500 remote sites, including schools, health centers, libraries, and rural communities. The initiative helps bridge the digital divide and promote digital inclusion across underserved regions of Brazil.
