# Connecting Millions of People in Mexico

Together with Hispasat and AXESS, Gilat helped Altán Redes extend 4G LTE coverage to underserved regions throughout Mexico using satellite-based cellular backhaul. The solution enabled millions of people in remote areas to access reliable mobile broadband where terrestrial infrastructure was unavailable or cost-prohibitive.
