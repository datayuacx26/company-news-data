# SkyEdge IV Case Study: GESAC Program in Brazil for Bridging the Digital Divide

Using its SkyEdge IV platform, Gilat enabled the GESAC program to provide high-speed satellite connectivity to more than 1,500 locations across Brazil’s remote northern regions. The scalable solution delivers reliable internet access, Wi-Fi services, and digital opportunities to rural communities.
