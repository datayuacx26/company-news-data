# HeyCharge × Easee launch CONNECT MagicBox — smart, solar-aware retrofit

The HeyCharge Connect MagicBox: plug-in retrofit adapter for any OCPP-compatible wallbox.


HeyCharge, the Munich-based EV charging infrastructure company backed by Y Combinator and the European Innovation Council, today announced **HeyCharge Connect** — a new product line of retrofit adapters designed to add smart energy management to existing EV chargers. The first product in the line, the **MagicBox** , targets home EV owners and fleet operators, and will be made available to Easee customers across the DACH region as part of a strategic partnership between the two companies.


HeyCharge Connect adapters work as an intelligent bridge between a wallbox and the broader energy ecosystem. The MagicBox connects directly to any OCPP-compatible charger, connecting it to HeyCharge’s SecureCharge platform — no electrician visit, no firmware changes, no downtime. Installation is self-service: plug in, configure, and go.


Critically, the MagicBox operates with local, offline capabilities. Unlike cloud-dependent charging solutions that require reliable Wi-Fi coverage in garages and parking areas, the MagicBox functions independently — managing charging sessions, enforcing energy limits, and communicating with other devices over a local mesh network even when internet connectivity is unavailable.


## What the MagicBox Unlocks


Once connected, the MagicBox transforms a basic wallbox into a fully integrated smart charging system. Key capabilities include:


**Solar and tariff optimization.** The MagicBox connects to rooftop PV systems and dynamic electricity tariffs, automatically scheduling charging sessions to maximize self-consumption and minimize cost.


**Smart home integration.** The MagicBox integrates with Home Assistant today, enabling coordinated energy management across the household — not just the charger. Broader smart home support via Matter, including Google Home and Apple HomeKit, is expected in the second half of 2026.


**Dynamic load management.** In properties with limited grid capacity, the MagicBox coordinates charging in real time — distributing available power automatically to avoid overloading the electrical connection. This works without cloud dependency, and no additional networking infrastructure is required.


The MagicBox's compact form factor with standard EU plug — no electrician required.


**In-vehicle access with Android Automotive OS.** Through integration with Google’s Android Automotive OS, the MagicBox enables drivers to start and manage charging sessions directly from the vehicle’s infotainment system — no RFID card, no smartphone app required. This is particularly valuable for fleet and pool car environments, where individual driver credentials are impractical, and delivers the convenience of Plug-and-Charge without the associated infrastructure cost and complexity.


**Regulatory compliance.** The MagicBox supports compliance with 14a EnWG, Germany’s regulatory framework requiring grid operator control of large consumer devices such as EV chargers and heat pumps — an increasingly important requirement for both new and existing installations.


**Company car reimbursement.** For employees charging company or service vehicles at home, MagicBox enables precise session tracking and automated reimbursement reporting, powered by HeyCharge’s SecureCharge technology. Because SecureCharge operates locally without internet connectivity, it delivers secure, tax- and regulatory-compliant charger management even in garages and parking areas without Wi-Fi or cellular coverage — a use case already proven on the HeyCharge platform in commercial and multi-family residential deployments. Employers get auditable data; employees get paid for the energy they use.


## Partnership with Easee


Easee, the Norwegian smart charging company, is the launch partner for the MagicBox. The device will be made available to Easee customers across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland as part of the partnership, extending the capabilities of existing Easee installations through HeyCharge’s intelligent software and connectivity layer. The collaboration enables customers to unlock additional value from their charging infrastructure, connecting it more seamlessly into the wider energy ecosystem without added complexity. Details on pricing and bundle options will be announced ahead of general availability.

**About HeyCharge**


HeyCharge (YC S21) is a Munich-based EV charging infrastructure company whose SecureCharge technology enables scalable, intelligent, and cost-efficient charging operations for residential, commercial, and fleet environments. The company’s technology combines Bluetooth-based session authentication with a Zigbee mesh network for energy management and inter-charger communication, packaged as both a software platform and embeddable hardware modules. HeyCharge is a recipient of the European Innovation Council Accelerator grant. For more information, visit[heycharge.com](https://heycharge.com/) .


**About Easee**


Founded in Norway in 2018, Easee is a leading provider of smart EV charging solutions, designed to be scalable, intuitive, and future-ready for both residential and commercial use. With over one million chargers installed across Europe, Easee is helping accelerate the transition to electric mobility by making effortless electrification accessible to all. More information is available at[easee.com](https://easee.com/) .
