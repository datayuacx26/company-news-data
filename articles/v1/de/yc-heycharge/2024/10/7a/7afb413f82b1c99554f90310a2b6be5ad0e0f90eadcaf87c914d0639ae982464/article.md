# HUMAX Forms Strategic Partnership with HeyCharge and Announces Joint Development of Next-Generation Charging Stations

Enabling charging within buildings is essential for the success of the energy transition and electromobility in Europe — especially as around 80% of charging occurs at home and the workplace. While there are already numerous initiatives to improve EV charging in single-family homes, charging for residents of multi-family homes in urban areas has been significantly neglected due to high hardware costs and complicated installations. The product also offers the same benefits for charging fleets and employee vehicles in office buildings.


HUMAX and HeyCharge are tackling these challenges by developing a new generation of innovative AC charging hardware. The goal is to create an EV Charger for the mass market that makes installation in underground garages or areas with inadequate cellular coverage more attractive and faster. Both companies bring their core expertise to the development: HUMAX offers many years of experience in manufacturing cost-optimized electronic products and an international sales network. HeyCharge contributes its specially developed offline technology for underground garages and its billing software. This technology is integrated cost-effectively on-board and is automatically included in the charging station without retrofitting.


“In the context of our international expansion strategy, the charging segment is very interesting for us. The right solution offers enormous scaling potential for HUMAX in Germany and Europe,” explains Jeff Kim, President of Humax. “With HeyCharge, we have found a partner to jointly develop a unique, mass-market product for this rapidly growing market. Our goal is to market more than 100,000 charging points, relying on a significant reduction in total operating costs for our customers.”


“We are extremely excited about the partnership with HUMAX. We are confident that the joint solution will achieve an industry-leading combination of best user experience and lowest cost,” adds Chris Cardé, CEO of HeyCharge. “We aim to take charging in areas with poor internet connection, such as underground garages, to a new level by bundling our SecureCharge no-internet EV charging management platform into Humax’s hardware and service offering.”


“Our research demonstrates a potential — in Germany alone — of over 10 million charging stations. The most crucial factors are to enable a rapid scaling of electrification in our core segment along with cost efficiency,” adds Robert Lasowski, CBDO at HeyCharge. “Together with Humax, we plan to significantly expand our market share.”


The partnership pursues a clearly-defined and ambitious goal: the reduction in total operating costs (TCO) of charging infrastructure by up to 50% compared to conventional solutions. The bundle of hardware and software, along with the associated billing functionality, offers a worry-free package for property owners and other charging infrastructure operators.


The new cost-efficient EV Charger is expected to be available in the first quarter of 2025.


## About HUMAX


Founded in 1989, HUMAX is one of the world’s largest manufacturers of innovative technology solutions for Smart Home, Connectivity, and Mobility. In the Mobility sector, the company specializes in all-in-one fleet management systems and charging technology for electric vehicles for both private and commercial use. Other focuses include a mobility services platform, a device and hub platform, parking services, and intelligent building solutions. HUMAX sells its products under its brand name in more than 90 countries and has enjoyed an excellent reputation for innovation, quality, and user-friendliness for over 35 years. For more information, visit[humaxcharging.com/de](https://humaxcharging.com/de) .


## About HeyCharge


HeyCharge is an EV charging technology company providing a platform for the operation and usage of EV chargers without internet access. This technology applies to EV charging globally, but works especially well in underground and other low-connectivity environments. They operate EV charging installations powered by their own innovative technology in the German market, and make turnkey and white-label versions of the technology available to customers in the EV charging industry globally.
