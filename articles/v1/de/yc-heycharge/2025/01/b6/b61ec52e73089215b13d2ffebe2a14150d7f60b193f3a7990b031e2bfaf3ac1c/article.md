# Vonovia and HeyCharge Partner to Deliver Affordable, Scalable EV Charging for Renters Across Germany

Vonovia, Europe’s largest residential real estate company managing approximately 541,600 apartments and 108,000 parking spaces, has partnered with HeyCharge, a Munich-based smart EV charging innovator. Their collaboration aims to provide budget-friendly and scalable charging infrastructure to renters throughout Germany.


## Market Context


The electric vehicle sector has expanded well beyond luxury segments. Urban renters are increasingly adopting EVs, creating surging demand for accessible charging solutions in apartment buildings and residential communities. However, developing cost-effective and widely deployable charging networks has presented significant obstacles — challenges this partnership directly addresses.


## Partnership Vision


Robert Lasowski, HeyCharge’s Co-Founder and CBDO, stated: “Developing affordable and scalable EV charging for residential buildings has always been at the core of HeyCharge’s mission.” He emphasized that their proprietary technology substantially reduces installation expenses, democratizing EV charging access for tenants who have historically been left behind by the charging infrastructure buildout.


## Pilot Results


The companies completed successful pilot installations in underground garages and outdoor parking areas across Germany. User feedback proved overwhelmingly positive, demonstrating clear potential for broader expansion. Data from these trials will inform the development of a tenant-focused, scalable charging infrastructure tailored to Vonovia’s extensive portfolio.


## Impact


This partnership establishes new benchmarks for residential EV charging, positioning sustainable mobility as an accessible, practical option for broader populations. By combining Vonovia’s unparalleled residential reach with HeyCharge’s offline-capable charging technology, the collaboration has the potential to bring EV charging to hundreds of thousands of parking spaces across Germany.


## About HeyCharge


HeyCharge is EV charging — incredibly simple. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Munich, HeyCharge has developed SecureCharge, a patented offline-capable charger management technology that enables EV charging without internet connectivity.
