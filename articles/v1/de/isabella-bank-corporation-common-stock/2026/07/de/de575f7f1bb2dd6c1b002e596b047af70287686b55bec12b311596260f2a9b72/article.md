# Isabella Bank Announces Officer Promotions

Neil McDonnell, president of Isabella Bank, announced the promotion of several team members to officer positions across the organization. The promotions reflect the continued growth and strength of the Bank’s team, as well as its commitment to developing talent from within.


“We are proud to recognize each of these team members for their outstanding contributions,” McDonnell said. “Their leadership, dedication and commitment to our mission are essential to the Bank’s continued success and growth.”


## Team Members Promoted


### Miles Coffland, Vice President and Information Security Officer


Miles Coffland has been promoted to vice president. A member of the Isabella Bank team since 2003, Coffland serves as information security officer and coordinates security awareness, vendor management, business continuity and identity theft education programs.[Learn more about Miles Coffland](https://www.isabellabank.com/about/news/detail/isabella-bank-announces-promotion-of-miles-coffland-to-vice-president) .


### Len Desormeau, Officer and Human Resources Generalist


Len Desormeau has been promoted to officer. Since joining Isabella Bank in 2019, Desormeau has overseen recruiting, onboarding and employee relations efforts. In his role as human resources generalist, he continues to help attract, develop and retain talented employees across the organization.[Learn more about Len Desormeau](https://www.isabellabank.com/about/news/detail/isabella-bank-announces-promotion-of-len-desormeau-to-hr-officer) .


### Amanda Dudek, Officer and Branch Manager


Amanda Dudek has been promoted to officer. Dudek joined Isabella Bank in 2022 and has more than 20 years of banking experience. Based at the Bank’s Saginaw Bay Road branch, she supports employee development while serving customers and communities throughout the region.[Learn more about Amanda Dudek](https://www.isabellabank.com/about/news/detail/isabella-bank-announces-promotion-of-amanda-dudek-to-officer-and-branch-manager) .


### Cathy Lamrouex, Officer and Branch Manager II


Cathy Lamrouex has been promoted to officer. A banking professional with more than 30 years of experience, Lamrouex joined Isabella Bank in 2022. She manages the day-to-day operations at the Bank’s Saginaw State Street branch and supports team members across the organization.[Learn more about Cathy Lamrouex](https://www.isabellabank.com/about/news/detail/isabella-bank-announces-promotion-of-cathy-lamrouex-to-officer-and-branch-manager-ii) .


### Deb Markley, Regional Vice President and Regional Branch Manager


Deb Markley has been promoted to regional vice president. Markley brings 37 years of community banking experience to her role as regional branch manager, where she oversees operations for Isabella Bank’s south region branches and provides leadership and support to branch teams throughout the region.[Learn more about Deb Markley](https://www.isabellabank.com/about/news/detail/isabella-bank-announces-promotion-of-deb-markley-to-regional-vice-president) .


### Dee Powell, Company Officer and Project Manager


Dee Powell has been promoted to company officer. Since joining Isabella Bank in 2013, Powell has held several leadership positions and now serves as project manager. Based at the Bank’s downtown Mount Pleasant office, she oversees the Bank’s project portfolio and supports project owners across the organization.[Learn more about Dee Powell](https://www.isabellabank.com/about/news/detail/isabella-bank-announces-promotion-of-dee-powell-to-company-officer) .


### Harry Steib, Regional Vice President and Regional Branch Manager


Harry Steib has been promoted to regional vice president. Steib joined Isabella Bank in 2012 and serves as regional branch manager for the east region. Based at the Bank’s Freeland branch, he provides leadership to branch teams, supports business development efforts and helps lead the Bank’s community involvement through Great Lakes Bay region.[Learn more about Harry Steib](https://www.isabellabank.com/about/news/detail/isabella-bank-announces-promotion-of-harry-steib-to-regional-vice-president) .
