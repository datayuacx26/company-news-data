# Turing Labs Unveils AI Platform to Help Companies Navigate Evolving Ingredient Regulations


*February 10, 2025 · 3*


SAN FRANCISCO—(BUSINESS WIRE)—Turing Labs Inc. today launched an enhanced AI-powered platform designed to help food and beverage manufacturers swiftly adapt to changing regulations, with immediate application for the upcoming FDA ban on Red Dye No. 3. This solution addresses the growing challenge companies face as regulatory changes accelerate globally.
‍


The platform enables manufacturers to proactively reformulate products affected by current and future ingredient regulations while maintaining product quality and consumer acceptance. This capability is particularly crucial as companies prepare for the Red Dye No. 3 ban, effective January 2027 for food products.


> “Regulatory changes are becoming more frequent and complex, requiring manufacturers to be increasingly agile. Our platform significantly reduces the time and resources needed for reformulation while ensuring compliance and product quality across multiple regulatory scenarios,” said Manmit Shrimali, founder and CEO of Turing Labs.


‍


#### How Turing Labs is Leading the Way


Turing Labs, a platform that combines advanced AI algorithms with domain-intelligence on a vast number of ingredients, has upgraded its platform to help brands reformulate their products seamlessly:


- **Faster Reformulation:** The platform accelerates the research and testing process, identifying optimal replacements for Red Dye No. 3 in record time.
- **Enhanced Safety and Quality:** Turing’s AI analyzes safety profiles, stability, and sensory attributes to ensure replacements align with consumer preferences and regulatory standards.
- **Sustainability Focus:** The platform prioritizes natural, eco-friendly ingredients, helping brands meet growing consumer demand for sustainable products.
- **Cost Optimization:** Turing’s data-driven insights minimize costs associated with reformulation, from ingredient sourcing to production adjustments.


> “Turing Labs has been pioneering AI-driven ingredient reformulation since day one. When we invested years ago, we recognized and aligned with their vision for the future—one where brands could innovate and adapt faster than ever before. Today, with regulatory changes like the Red Dye No. 3 ban and more expected on the horizon, Turing’s platform will be the foundation for companies that don’t just want to keep up, but lead their industries,” said Trace Cohen, Managing Director of New York Venture Partners


‍


#### Why Major Brands Should Act Now


The FDA’s timeline leaves little room for delay, and Turing Labs offers brands a significant competitive edge:


- **Regulatory Compliance** : Avoid penalties and maintain consumer trust by transitioning early.
- **First-Mover Advantage:** Position your brand as a leader in health-conscious innovation.
- **Consumer Loyalty:** Respond to rising demand for safer, natural ingredients in the wake of growing health concerns.


> “Today’s CPG companies no longer need to become a software company to be competitive. We have engineered Turing Labs from the ground up to enable CPGs to focus on their core - understanding and delivering on unmet consumer needs. We’re proud to see leading CPG companies trusting Turing Labs as their innovation partner, choosing proven R&D-specific AI over generic solutions that don’t understand the complexities of product development,” said Shrimali.


‍


#### Proven Track Record


Turing Labs has already helped leading companies across the food and pharmaceutical sectors streamline their reformulation processes. By using Turing Labs, brands can unlock unparalleled speed, precision, and success in navigating the transition away from Red Dye No. 3.
