# Toy Story 5 Soars to Infinity and Beyond with All-Time Highest Total Revenue for a June Opening Weekend at Marcus Theatres

### Toy Story 5 Soars to Infinity and Beyond with All-Time Highest Total Revenue for a June Opening Weekend at Marcus Theatres


June 22, 2026


*The highly anticipated installment in the beloved franchise also resulted in the all-time highest combined concession, merchandise, and food and beverage revenue for a June opening weekend*


MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Marcus Theatres®](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marcustheatres.com%2F&esheet=54558083&newsitemid=20260622705538&lan=en-US&anchor=Marcus+Theatres%26%23174%3B&index=1&md5=f352a1b78ee5c6f058637fa644beceb8) , the nation’s fourth largest theatre circuit and a division of[Marcus Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marcuscorp.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54558083&newsitemid=20260622705538&lan=en-US&anchor=Marcus+Corporation&index=2&md5=ba9739d0d96ea43adc8859623c1856a0) (NYSE: MCS), today announced that the opening weekend of *Toy Story 5* and strong carryover performances from several films delivered record total revenue and record combined concession, merchandise, and food and beverage revenue for a June opening weekend.


Fans of all ages turned out for *Toy Story 5* , from young children meeting Jessie, Woody and Buzz Lightyear for the first time to longtime moviegoers who have followed the franchise for decades. Across Marcus Theatres and Movie Tavern® locations in 17 states, audiences made the film’s opening weekend a success, with highlights including:


- All-time highest total revenue for a June opening weekend
- All-time highest combined concession, merchandise, and food and beverage revenue for a June opening weekend
- Highest opening box office for an animated film since 2019 and the highest opening for the *Toy Story* franchise
- Best three-day opening weekend for a film in 2026


“For more than 30 years, audiences have grown up with Woody, Buzz and the rest of the *Toy Story* gang,” said Jeff Tomachek, president of Marcus Theatres. “What makes *Toy Story 5* so special is that it brings together multiple generations in a way very few franchises can. Parents who grew up with these characters are now sharing them with their own kids, creating a whole new set of movie memories. It also feels fitting that Jessie and Buzz are helping lead the charge for what continues to be a strong year at the box office. To infinity and beyond may be a great catchphrase, but it's also a good description of where moviegoing momentum is headed right now. From the blockbuster success of *The Super Mario Galaxy Movie* to films like *Michael* , *The Devil Wears Prada 2* , *Obsession* and *Backrooms* , audiences continue to show that they want a wide variety of stories and experiences. The common thread is that they're best enjoyed together on the big screen. With an exciting lineup still ahead, there's never been a better time to go to the movies.”


The rest of the summer at Marcus Theatres features more highly-anticipated films, including *Supergirl* (June 26), *Minions & Monsters* (July 1), *Moana* (July 10), *The Odyssey* (July 17), *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* (July 31), *Super Troopers 3* (August 7), *Paw Patrol: The Dino Movie* (August 14), *Insidious: Out of the Further* (August 21), and *Coyote vs. Acme* (August 28).


Looking ahead to the remainder of the year, additional films that are expected to perform well include: *Practical Magic 2* (September 11) *, Resident Evil* (September 18) *, Forgotten Island* (September 25) *, Digger* (October 2) *, Verity* (October 2) *, Other Mommy* (October 9) *, The Social Reckoning* (October 9) *, Street Fighter* (October 16) *, The Cat in the Hat* (November 6) *, Godzilla Minus Zero* (November 6) *, Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* (November 20) *, Hexed* (November 25) *, Focker-In-Law* (November 25) *, Dune: Part Three* (December 18) *, Avengers: Doomsday* (December 18) *, The Angry Birds Movie 3* (December 23) ** and *Jumanji: Open World* (December 25).
