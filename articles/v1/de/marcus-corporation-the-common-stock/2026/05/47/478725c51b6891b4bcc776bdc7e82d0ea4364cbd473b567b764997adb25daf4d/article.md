# Marcus Corporation to Hold Virtual Annual Shareholders’ Meeting May 21, 2026

### Marcus Corporation to Hold Virtual Annual Shareholders’ Meeting May 21, 2026


May 7, 2026


MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[The Marcus Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marcuscorp.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54530719&newsitemid=20260506005935&lan=en-US&anchor=The+Marcus+Corporation&index=1&md5=f21d8bcb1532a489d96749a329b3203b) (NYSE: MCS) today announced it will hold its virtual Annual Meeting of Shareholders on Thursday, May 21, 2026, beginning at 9:00 a.m. Central/10:00 a.m. Eastern time. The business portion of the meeting will be followed by a shareholder question and answer session.


Shareholders of record may vote their shares electronically, online, by mail or by phone prior to the virtual Annual Meeting. Shareholders may also vote their shares online during the meeting. The record date for shareholders entitled to vote at the virtual Annual Meeting is March 24, 2026. The company’s proxy statement was furnished to shareholders beginning on April 7, 2026. Proxy materials are available online at[proxyvote.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.proxyvote.com&esheet=54530719&newsitemid=20260506005935&lan=en-US&anchor=proxyvote.com&index=2&md5=d70655c515d2a9fb32eb2faf9f3f1063) .


Shareholders and interested parties can listen to a live audio webcast and view presentation materials of the meeting by logging onto the investor relations section of the company’s website:[investors.marcuscorp.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.marcuscorp.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54530719&newsitemid=20260506005935&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.marcuscorp.com&index=3&md5=41bf5853a008f86fce03937aad6f134a) or through this link:[virtualshareholdermeeting.com/MCS2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fvirtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FMCS2026&esheet=54530719&newsitemid=20260506005935&lan=en-US&anchor=virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FMCS2026&index=4&md5=7e5470658d2560e5d108d594dd7a19c1) . Only shareholders who log-in to the virtual meeting and register with their control number will be able to vote and ask questions during the meeting. Attendees should log on at least 10 minutes prior to the start of the meeting to download and install any necessary software. The meeting will be available for replay through August 21, 2026.
