# 5 Fall Recipes from Around the Country

Maybe you’ve seen the temps drop, the leaves fall, or the air has finally dried out—fall has arrived. It’s a magical time.


For all of us in the coffee world, that means fall-themed drinks. Regardless of your preferences, (PSL lovers- we support you and hear you) seasonal drinks are a smash hit and we’re absolutely here for it. We took a fall menu deep dive and talked to some barista friends across the country to share their creative and delicious ideas. Each recipe below represents a unique regional approach to the quintessential fall seasonal drink, and we hope that one (or more) of these inspires you to get creative and try something different. Enjoy!


**Denver** **** **** **Kaffe Landskap: Iced Pumpkin Spice Latte**


- Ingredients: pumpkin spice syrup, oat milk


- For the customer who: has warm fall weather


- Flavor notes: subtly sweet with just a hint of ginger, and a rich velvet texture


[@kaffelandskap](https://www.instagram.com/kaffelandskap/?hl=en) |


[www.kaffelandskap.com](http://www.kaffelandskap.com/)


**Atlanta Academy Coffee: The "PSL Must Die"**


- Ingredients: olive oil, grapefruit-infused caramel


- For the customer who: is feeling rebellious


- Flavor notes: At first sip, you'll immediately notice the very smooth texture, it’s denser than a typical latte due to the olive oil. It’s not overly sweet, thanks to the grapefruit. It’s not a typical fall drink, but it still hits the spot for autumn vibes.


[@academy_coffee](https://www.instagram.com/academy_coffee/?hl=en)[|](http://www.kaffelandskap.com/)[www.academycoffeeatl.com](http://www.academycoffeeatl.com/)


**San Francisco Equator Coffee: The Habibi Latte**


- Ingredients: orange blossom, cardamom, clove, vanilla syrup, 2 shots, and 6oz steamed milk


- For the customer who: is feeling nostalgic


- Flavor notes: You’ll immediately be greeted with orange blossom before you taste this latte. The spices undoubtedly evoke the fall vibe—think of scented candles or grandma's cookies. The vanilla leaves a pleasant aftertaste on your pallet.


[@equatorcoffees |](http://www.kaffelandskap.com/)https://www.equatorcoffees.com


**Miami Cali Coffee: Iced Pumpkin Butter Latte**


- Ingredients: pumpkin, cinnamon, white chocolate


- For the customer who: is feeling indulgent


- Flavor notes: This drink is rich with pumpkin flavor, yet light on the palate. The white chocolate adds just the right amount of sweetness. Enjoy with whip cream and a caramel drizzle on top for bonus points.


[@calicoffee](https://www.instagram.com/calicoffee/?hl=en) |[https://www.calicoffee.com](https://www.calicoffee.com/)


**Dallas Merit Coffee Co.: Iced Shaken Maple Pecan**


- Ingredients: maple syrup, dark brown sugar, pecans, oat milk


- Flavor notes: Prepared in a shaker and served over ice, this cold drink is a perfect way to catch a fall vibe even if the weather is still warm. It tastes like a pecan pie but in refreshing drink form. It’s not overly sugary, but still hits that sweet spot.


[@meritcoffee](https://www.instagram.com/meritcoffee/?hl=en) |[https://www.meritcoffee.com](https://meritcoffee.com/)
