# Female-Founded Táche is Making Waves In The Alt-Milk Business

## Pistachios + coffee. A budding friendship with a ton of potential.


At Odeko, we believe in the magic of small business. In honor of Small Business Saturday, we wanted to highlight a vendor partner that is as equally creative as it is ambitious. Roxana Saidi, the founder of Táche, tells a story that inspires us and embodies what we love about working with small businesses.


In 2015 Roxana set out to create a sustainable and delicious product without the added oils or water consumption of other alternative milk options. As a first-generation Iranian-American, pistachios were a key part of Roxana’s culinary upbringing. They were her favorite nut, not only for their cultural relevance but also for their numerous[health benefits](https://www.webmd.com/food-recipes/benefits-pistachios) .


**Doing More with Less**


Being the first true pistachio milk with no filler ingredients like almonds or rice, Táche represents a new category in the rapidly growing alternative-milk segment. Táche has committed to creating a delicious product with no added oils—just pure pistachio goodness.


**Celebrating Small Business Success** ****


In a notably short time, Táche has become a hit. That success goes far beyond steaming for lattes, although it’s exceptional for that too, thanks to the monounsaturated fats. Pistachio milk compliments yet stands out in drinks, grain breakfasts, cereals, and more.
**** With nationwide distribution at Odeko, Táche is sought after by guests of third-wave cafes. Our friends at Verve, Devoción, and Sant Ambroeus stock it, among others. Consumers can get their hands on Táche at specialty and natural grocers across the country, like Erewhon.


**Building on Success** ****


What makes Táche tick? They embody a values-driven company. As part of Táche’s ethos and origin as a female-led and founded brand, the company donates a portion of their profits, product, and time to the Lower East side Girls Club of New York in order to nurture the growth of underprivileged girls in the community.


At Odeko, we proudly support small businesses on a daily basis. If you’re looking to add a new alternative milk from an inspiring small business with a great story to your menu, Táche is it. Find and order them in your supply portal.
