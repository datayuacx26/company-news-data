# Retool Achieves AWS Consumer Goods Competency

Retool, the governed platform where AI development is fast for builders and safe for the business, today announced that it has achieved the AWS Consumer Goods Competency from Amazon Web Services (AWS). This designation recognizes Retool’s proven technical expertise and demonstrated success in helping brands respond to changing consumer demands and heightened competition.


The AWS Consumer Goods Competency differentiates Retool as an AWS Partner Network (APN) member with deep industry experience and technical proficiency in building solutions that address the complex challenges facing consumer brands. To receive the designation, AWS Partners must undergo rigorous technical validation and demonstrate proven customer success in the consumer goods sector.


Today, consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands are under immense pressure to adopt AI to optimize supply chains and respond quickly to consumer demand, but the governance gap remains the primary blocker to production. The rise of AI has enabled teams to vibe code applications in minutes using powerful agents, but when pushed live outside of an enterprise’s governance policies can disrupt entire supply chains.


Retool helps enterprises securely bring AI into critical internal operations by providing a governed environment for building, deploying, and managing internal software. Whether teams are building in Retool, Cursor, Claude Code, Kiro, or another AI code creation tool of their choice, Retool gives organizations a secure-by-default way to operationalize AI at the speed the business demands.


“Retool is proud to achieve the AWS Consumer Goods Competency,” said David Hsu, CEO and Founder of Retool. “As AI mandates intensify, our team remains dedicated to helping our AWS customers harness the power of AI-generated software with enterprise-grade security.”


Retool is available in[AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-cqgbgmrm63i4y?sr=0-1&ref_=beagle&applicationId=AWSMPContessa) making it easy for enterprise customers to procure. As part of the[AWS Independent Software Vendor (ISV) Accelerate Program](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/programs/isv-accelerate/) , Retool works closely with AWS field teams to bring trusted, AWS-validated solutions to enterprise customers.
