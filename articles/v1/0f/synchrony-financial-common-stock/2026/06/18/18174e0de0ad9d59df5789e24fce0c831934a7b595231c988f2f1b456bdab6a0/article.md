# Synchrony to Participate in the Morgan Stanley US Financials Conference

STAMFORD, Conn.


, June 2, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Synchrony (NYSE: SYF) Chief Financial Officer, Brian J. Wenzel, will participate in a fireside chat at the Morgan Stanley US Financials Conference on Tuesday, June 9, 2026 at 7:30 a.m. (Eastern Time).


A live webcast and replay will be made available on the Synchrony Investor Relations website at[www.investors.synchrony.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4698004-1&h=1627954919&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.investors.synchrony.com%2F&a=www.investors.synchrony.com) .
