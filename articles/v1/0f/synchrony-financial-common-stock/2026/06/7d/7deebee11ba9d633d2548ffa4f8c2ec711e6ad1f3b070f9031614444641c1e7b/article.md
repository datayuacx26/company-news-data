# Synchrony to Announce Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on July 21, 2026

STAMFORD, Conn.


, June 23, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Synchrony (NYSE: SYF) plans to report its second quarter 2026 results on Tuesday, July 21, 2026. The earnings release and presentation materials are scheduled to be released and posted to the Investor Relations section of the Company's website,[www.investors.synchrony.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4711713-1&h=2835888742&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.investors.synchrony.com%2F&a=www.investors.synchrony.com) , at approximately 6:00 a.m. Eastern Time. A conference call to discuss Synchrony's results will be held at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time on that day; the live audio webcast and replay can be accessed through the same website under Events and Presentations.
