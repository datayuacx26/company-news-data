## The Manager Engagement Crisis


Here, you’ll find engagement statistics showing **how manager engagement rates have sharply declined** , with disengaged managers directly undermining employee morale, employee satisfaction, and overall business performance.


### 5. Global Manager Engagement Fell[from 30% to 27%](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/349484/state-of-the-global-workplace.aspx) in 2024


**Source:** ***Gallup*** 's *State of the Global Workplace Report 2024*


‍


**Business Implications:** The 3-percentage-point decline in manager engagement is particularly concerning because **managers influence 70% of team engagement variance** . Disengaged managers create cascading negative effects throughout their teams.


**Strategic Application:** Manager disengagement often stems from role ambiguity, insufficient support, and overwhelming responsibilities. Organizations must prioritize manager development and support systems to prevent engagement erosion.

### 6. Engagement[Dropped 5 Percentage Points](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/659279/global-engagement-falls-second-time-2009.aspx) for Managers Under 35

**Business Implications:** Younger managers face unique challenges including role transition difficulties, insufficient support systems, and overwhelming responsibilities that contribute to rapid engagement decline.
