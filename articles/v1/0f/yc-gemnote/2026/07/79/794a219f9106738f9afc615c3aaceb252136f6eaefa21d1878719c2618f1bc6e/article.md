# College Swag and Promotional Items for Recruitment

In the competitive landscape of higher education, attracting the brightest minds and athletes to your institution is vital. Luckily, with eye-catching personalized giveaways for college students you can market your logo with gear that gets noticed.


‍


## Types of College Swag


Universities have lots of options when it comes time to invest in memorable recruiting swag. Best of all, the following college promotional products offer prime ways to show off your college name.


###
Accessories


Accessories cover a wide range of items, and for companies looking for lightweight items for transporting to college fairs, you can’t go wrong with branded items like hats, beanies, scrunchies, totes, keychains, stickers, patches, and the like. With so many options available, discovering the perfect swag in your budget just got easier.


### Decor + Home Goods


For college students who are going away for the first time, having cool decor to show off their college is exciting! Consider items like custom towels, branded game balls, pennant flags, and more.


‍


### Clothing


Custom apparel remains a top gift for universities since recruits look forward to showing off their college spirit. If you’re looking for college swag ideas that won’t disappoint, look at tees, hoodies, jackets, joggers, shorts, socks, and more!


‍


## Tips for designing trendy college swag for recruitment


For swag-worthy promotional items on-brand, professional customization is a must! To get the awe-inspiring designs your students want, consider the following.


‍


**1. Use school colors**


While it may seem like branding 101, having your logo isn’t enough, you want recruiting swag that’s easy to identify.


**2. Include school logo**


It goes without saying but your recipients want to show off their future alma mater. So when it comes to giveaways for college students put your marketing efforts front and center!


‍


**3. Consider Budget**


While being limited to a budget isn't always fun, it does help narrow your selection! And, when it comes to college swag ideas, there are many ways to make your investment go farther. Talk to your Gemnote rep for ways you can save on customization, bulk orders and more.


‍


**4. Research items that are popular among students**


When it comes to knowing what your college students want, just look to your Gemnote rep for the best ideas. They can even create a curated idea board featuring the best in branded college promotional items—all within your budget!
