## How to measure trade show & corporate event giveaways ROI


The right trade show and corporate event giveaways can help generate leads, increase brand awareness, and strengthen customer relationships. To measure ROI, define your goals before the event, then track metrics such as giveaways distributed, qualified leads, QR code scans, website visits, and meetings booked. You can also calculate cost per lead by dividing your total giveaway investment by the number of qualified leads generated.


‍


For higher-value corporate event giveaways and VIP gifts, focus on outcomes such as follow-up meetings, sales opportunities, customer retention and conversions. Comparing these results with your total investment can help identify which giveaways deliver the most value.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Trade Show Giveaways


### What are the best trade show giveaways?


The best trade show giveaways are useful, memorable, and relevant to your audience. Popular options include pens, notebooks, reusable bags, water bottles, branded apparel, portable chargers and premium tech accessories. The right choice depends on your budget, event goals, and the attendees you want to reach.


‍


### What are good trade show giveaways under $1–$100?


Good trade show giveaways are available at nearly every price point. Lower-cost options include stickers, pens and other small branded items, while a larger budget can cover drinkware, apparel, bags, portable chargers and premium gifts. Consider using affordable giveaways for general booth traffic and higher-value products for qualified leads, VIPs or special promotions.


‍


### What are the most popular trade show giveaways?


Some of the most popular trade show giveaways include branded pens, tote bags, notebooks, water bottles, T-shirts, hats, and tech accessories. Products that are practical, easy to carry, and useful after the event tend to have the greatest staying power.


‍


### How much should you spend on trade show giveaways?


How much you should spend depends on your audience, event size, and goals. Inexpensive giveaways under $10 can work well for high-volume distribution, while $10–$50 conference giveaways offer greater perceived value. Premium corporate event giveaways and VIP gifts may cost $50, $100, or more and are typically reserved for high-value prospects, clients, speakers, or executives.


‍


### What are unique trade show giveaways that stand out?


Unique trade show giveaways go beyond basic promotional products and offer something useful, unexpected, or experiential. Ideas include Polaroid cameras, massage guns, premium travel bags, wireless chargers, gourmet snacks and curated gift kits. Creative customization and packaging can also help make a familiar product feel more distinctive.


‍


### What are good conference giveaways for attendees?


Good conference giveaways for attendees include products they can use during the event and afterward, such as reusable totes, water bottles, notebooks, portable chargers, and branded apparel. Consider the audience, event setting, and travel requirements when choosing conference swag that will be useful and easy to take home.


‍


### Can you customize trade show giveaways with your company logo?


Yes. Most trade show giveaways can be customized with your company logo, brand colors, artwork, or event messaging. Depending on the product, customization options may include screen printing, embroidery, engraving, full-color printing, custom packaging, and other decoration methods.
