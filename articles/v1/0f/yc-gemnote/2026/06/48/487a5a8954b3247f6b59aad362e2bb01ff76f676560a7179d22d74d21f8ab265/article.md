# Employee Onboarding Kit Ideas: New Hire Welcome Boxes & AI Employee Swag

An employee onboarding kit sets the tone for a new hire's entire experience at your company. Whether you're building a traditional employee onboarding package or creating AI new hire kits for a fast-growing technology company, the right welcome experience helps employees feel connected from day one.


‍


## What is an employee onboarding kit?


Employee onboarding kits are curated collections of branded items given to new hires on or before their first day. Also called new hire welcome kits or employee welcome packages, they typically include practical work tools, branded apparel, and items that reflect company culture. For AI companies, onboarding kits often include AI employee swag, tech accessories, and[AI personalized gifts.](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/ai-gifts)


‍


The best onboarding kits go beyond novelty. Useful, high-quality items create a strong first impression and extend your brand beyond the office. For remote employees, a thoughtfully assembled kit can provide a tangible connection to the team.

## What is included in a new hire welcome kit?


[Building an employee onboarding kit](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/how-to-create-the-best-employee-onboarding-kit) typically includes two categories: administrative essentials and branded merchandise. Together, these items help new hires get oriented while reinforcing company culture. Many organizations package them into a new hire welcome box delivered before an employee's first day.


‍


**Common onboarding kit contents include:**


- **Company handbook and culture guide:** Policies, values, and expectations
- **Benefits and HR documentation:** Enrollment forms, contacts, and key dates
- **Job-specific resources:** Role descriptions, team org charts, and 30/60/90-day goals
- **Workspace equipment:** Laptops, peripherals, or home office stipends
- **Branded swag:** Apparel, drinkware, bags, office supplies, and other branded items


‍


The best onboarding kits balance both categories. Well-organized documentation signals professionalism, while useful, high-quality merchandise shows employees they're valued.\]

## Why branded merchandise belongs in an onboarding kit:


[Branded merchandise](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/what-is-branded-merchandise) does more than fill a box. Useful, well-chosen items show employees they're valued from day one. For AI companies, custom AI employee swag can reinforce company culture while strengthening brand connection.


‍


### Increases employee engagement


A branded welcome gift signals investment from day one. New hires who feel recognized early are more likely to engage quickly with their role and team.


‍


### Builds a sense of belonging


Shared branded items, like a team polo worn on Fridays, a mug that shows up on video calls, or a water bottle carried to the gym, create a low-key but consistent visual identity across a team. For new hires, especially, these small details help them feel like part of something before they have deep relationships at the company.


‍


### Creates a strong first impression


An onboarding kit arrives before a new hire has attended a meeting or completed training. That early gesture carries weight. It frames the company as thoughtful and organized at the exact moment the employee is forming their first impressions.


‍


### Extends brand visibility beyond the office


Employees who use branded gear in public, like coffee shops, gyms, airports, become organic brand ambassadors. Unlike paid advertising, this visibility is earned through product quality and employee pride.

## How to measure the effectiveness of your employee onboarding kit


Sending a welcome kit is only half the equation. Tracking how well it lands helps you refine future kits, justify the investment, and understand what actually resonates with new hires.


‍


### Onboarding surveys and direct feedback


A short survey sent during the first week or at the 30-day mark is the most direct way to evaluate your kit. Ask new hires which items they've used, what they'd change, and how the kit made them feel coming into the role. Anonymous responses tend to yield more candid answers.


‍


### Social media mentions and hashtag tracking


Including a branded hashtag in your onboarding kit gives you a passive way to track organic engagement. When employees post their welcome kit on LinkedIn or Instagram, it signals both satisfaction and unprompted brand advocacy.


‍


### Employee retention and engagement data


Onboarding experience is one of several factors tied to early retention. If you're tracking 90-day retention rates or engagement survey scores, compare results before and after investing in a more intentional onboarding kit.


‍


### Observed usage and brand visibility


Are employees showing up to video calls with branded drinkware? Wearing the apparel to company events? Visible, repeated use of kit items is an informal but reliable signal that the merchandise was worth the investment.

## Frequently asked questions about employee onboarding kits


### What should an AI employee welcome kit include?


An AI employee welcome kit typically includes branded apparel, drinkware, notebooks, tech accessories, and role-specific resources. Many companies also include AI employee swag such as custom stickers, desk accessories, and premium technology gifts that reflect their brand and culture.


‍


### How much should you spend on a new hire welcome kit?


Most companies spend between $30 and $200 per employee on an onboarding kit. A $30 kit covers a few well-chosen branded basics like a tee, drinkware, and a snack. A $100 kit allows for a more cohesive package with apparel, a bag, and a tech accessory. At $200, there is room for premium materials, a wider product mix, and more sophisticated packaging.


‍


### What is the difference between an onboarding kit and a welcome kit?


The terms are often used interchangeably. In practice, a welcome kit tends to refer specifically to the branded merchandise and[gifts given to a new hire](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/new-job-gift-ideas) , while an onboarding kit may also include documentation, equipment, and other role-specific materials. Both serve the same purpose: helping new employees feel prepared and connected from day one.


‍


### Are employee onboarding kits worth the investment?


Research consistently links positive onboarding experiences to higher early retention and faster time-to-productivity. A thoughtfully assembled kit signals that the company is invested in its people before work begins, which sets a tone that is difficult to replicate through other means. For companies in competitive hiring markets, a strong onboarding kit can be a meaningful differentiator.


‍


### Can onboarding kits be shipped to remote employees?


Yes. Branded onboarding kits can be assembled, packaged, and shipped directly to a remote employee's home address before their start date. This is increasingly common as distributed teams have become the norm. Working with a[fulfillment partner](https://www.gemnote.com/capabilities/kitting-warehousing) that handles kitting and direct-to-employee shipping simplifies the logistics considerably.


‍


### How do you customize an onboarding kit for different roles or seniority levels?


A tiered approach works well. A standard kit covers all new hires with core branded items. An elevated kit for senior hires or executives adds premium apparel, higher-end drinkware, or personalized touches like a handwritten note or custom packaging. Industry or role-specific items, like a field bag for sales roles, a desk kit for office-based employees, can also be layered in without rebuilding the program from scratch.


‍


### What are the best AI new hire kits for remote employees?


The best AI new hire kits combine practical work essentials with branded merchandise that helps remote employees feel connected to the team. Popular items include apparel, drinkware, notebooks, backpacks, and AI new hire merch that employees can use both at home and in the office.
