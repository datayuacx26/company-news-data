# Redefining MLB Professional Sports Merch for Gen Z

Does professional sports merch need an update to resonate with younger audiences?


‍


Generation Z, often called Gen Z or Zoomers, did not grow up collecting jerseys out of loyalty alone. Instead, this cohort, born between 1997 and 2012, gravitates toward pieces that reflect personal style and self-expression. For Major League Baseball, that shift signals a meaningful change in how merchandise is designed, positioned, and experienced across the league.


‍


Today’s fans are not simply buying team gear to show support. They are curating items they would choose to wear in any setting. Custom sports merchandise now serves as an extension of identity, blending team affiliation with individual taste, aesthetic preferences, and point of view, whether worn at a game or as part of everyday style.


‍


*Photo courtesy of Sporty & Rich*


‍


### **MLB Is Gaining Momentum**


According to MLB.com, Major League Baseball is trending in the right direction. The 2025 season drew more than 71 million fans for the third consecutive year, marking the first time league attendance has reached that level since 2005. The Los Angeles Dodgers also became the first franchise in nearly two decades to surpass 4 million in attendance.


‍


On the field, recent rule changes are reshaping the fan experience. The introduction of the pitch clock in 2023 reduced the average game time to approximately 2 hours and 38 minutes, making professional baseball faster, more engaging, and more accessible to modern audiences.


‍


These changes matter. Increased attendance and a faster pace of play signal renewed interest in baseball and create new opportunities to connect with both existing fans and younger audiences.


‍


*Photo courtesy of Sporty & Rich*


‍


### **Another Opportunity: Turning Attention into Identity**


Attendance is only part of the story. The next challenge is what happens after fans engage with the game, and whether Major League Baseball can turn this momentum into long-term relevance with Gen Z consumers.


‍


For Gen Z, engagement does not stop at watching games. It extends into everyday behavior and self-expression, influencing:


- What they wear
- What they share
- How they express identity


‍


This is where MLB merchandise and branded sports apparel take on a larger role. What was once simple team gear becomes a cultural touchpoint, giving fans a way to express who they are, not just which team they support.


‍


*Photo courtesy of Aimé Leon Dore*


‍


### **Why This Matters for a Merch Strategy**


If Major League Baseball wants to build long-term loyalty, its merchandise strategy needs to evolve with[trendy professional sports giveaways and merch](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/sports-giveaways-and-merch) . Gen Z consumers are not asking if they support a team. They are deciding whether that apparel fits into their lifestyle, personal style, and identity.


‍


This shift reshapes the role of sports merchandise. It moves from team-first to identity-first, from mass-produced products to curated and expressive pieces, and from purely functional gear to culturally relevant apparel. The opportunity is not just in selling MLB merchandise. It is in creating custom sports apparel and fan merchandise that people genuinely want to wear in their everyday lives.


‍


## 10 MLB Merch Ideas Gen Z Actually Wants to Use and Wear


For[Gen Z merch ideas](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/merch-ideas-for-your-gen-z-customers) to resonate, it has to feel less like traditional sports team merchandise and more like everyday fashion. Here are the types of custom sports merchandise that actually work.

Tonal caps with logo stitching in the same color family as the fabric create a clean, understated look that feels more refined than traditional high-contrast designs. ## FAQs for creating the best sports team merchandise


‍


### What types of MLB merch appeal most to Gen Z fans?


Gen Z gravitates toward MLB merchandise that doubles as everyday streetwear. Vintage-inspired caps, elevated basics, and limited-edition drops that work beyond the stadium perform best. Creative collaborations with artists or designers they already follow also stand out.


‍


### Why is Gen Z important to MLB’s merchandise strategy?


Gen Z represents the next two decades of consumer spending. Building loyalty now through products they want to wear drives future ticket sales, streaming engagement, and long-term fan connection.


‍


### How are MLB teams using collaborations to reach younger fans?


Teams are partnering with local artists, streetwear brands, and cultural creators, often working alongside MLB apparel suppliers to produce limited capsule collections. These releases feel rooted in the city, generate social buzz, and signal cultural relevance in a way traditional merchandise cannot.


‍


### How does sustainable merch factor into Gen Z purchasing decisions?


Sustainability influences buying decisions. Materials and sourcing matter, and transparency is expected. Brands that can clearly communicate responsible production and work with sports merchandise suppliers **** who focus on sustainability have an advantage.


‍


### How can MLB teams execute trend-driven merch quickly?


Speed to market is critical for staying relevant in professional sports merch. Working with flexible custom merchandise partners allows teams to produce smaller runs, respond to trends faster, and launch products while interest is still high.
