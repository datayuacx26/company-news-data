# 22 Top Swag Ideas for Your Affiliate Marketing Teams

Affiliate marketing teams are built on momentum. For that reason, motivation and community matter. That’s one reason why the right swag plays a bigger role than many brands realize.


‍


Thoughtful, well-designed swag helps affiliates feel connected to your brand, proud to represent it, and excited to stay involved. It reinforces identity, rewards performance, and creates touch points that go far beyond a single campaign or payout.

## 22 Best Products for Affiliate Marketing


Below are 22 of the best products for affiliate marketing that are actually used and appreciated. Explore products and get tips on how to make each one custom pick feel intentional, premium, and on-brand.


‍


### 1. Branded T-Shirts


A classic for a reason. High-quality branded t-shirts offer affiliates a versatile option for wearing on social media, at events, or while creating content. The key is choosing a fabric, fit, and design that feels retail-ready, not promotional. A clean logo placement, subtle messaging, or minimalist design makes the shirt something affiliates reach for regularly, not just once. Branded tees are a terrific option for new affiliate onboarding, milestone rewards, and[content creator kits.](https://www.gemnote.com/products/pr-influencer-kits)


‍


*Photo courtesy of Amié Leon Dore*


‍


### 2. Joggers


Joggers strike the perfect balance between comfort and style, especially for remote-first affiliate teams. They’re ideal for creators who work from home, travel often, or spend long days filming and editing. When done right, joggers feel like a premium lifestyle piece, not merch. Neutral colors and understated branding keep them wearable beyond “work hours,” while bold colors make a statement wherever they go. Either way, your brand will stand out.


‍


*Photo courtesy of Sporty&Rich; Shown: NY Healthclub Sweatpants*


‍


### 3. Hoodies


Few swag items build loyalty like a great hoodie. Affiliates wear them on planes, during livestreams, in coffee shops, and on casual Zoom calls. A well-made hoodie becomes part of someone’s daily routine—and that’s powerful brand exposure. Consider embroidered logos or tonal prints to elevate the look.


‍


*Photo courtesy of Urban Outfitters; Guinness Pint Graphic Hoodie Sweatshirt*


‍


### 4. Jackets


Branded jackets feel like a step up. They work especially well for top-performing affiliates, leadership tiers, or event recognition. Lightweight coach jackets, windbreakers, or insulated layers give affiliates something functional and stylish. An affiliate swag item, like a name-brand custom jacket, feels earned.


‍


*Shown: The North Face jacket*


‍


### 5. Custom Crewnecks


Custom crewneck sweatshirts are an affiliate swag item that offers the comfort of a hoodie and a clean, polished aesthetic. They’re easy to style and one of the best products for affiliate marketing. Want to make a statement with your logo? Try an embroidered patch for a unique, elevated look.


‍


*Photo courtesy of A24 Films; Shown: A24 Light 'A Twenty-Four' Patch Crew*


‍


### 6. Candy or Snack Packs


Small wins can still have a big impact. Branded candy or curated snack packs are perfect for quick thank-you, campaign launches, or holiday touches. They’re affordable, memorable, and easy to ship alongside other items. Custom packaging or inserts help tie the experience back to your brand.


‍


*Photo courtesy of A24 Films*


‍


### 7. Tote Bags


Totes are endlessly useful. From grocery runs to conferences to everyday carry-alls, a durable branded tote keeps your brand visible without feeling promotional. Choose heavier canvas or recycled materials for a premium feel. Great for onboarding kits and[event swag](https://www.gemnote.com/products/event-giveaways) .


‍


*Photo courtesy of OnlyNY*

### 8. Duffel Bags


Duffels are ideal for affiliates who travel, attend events, or balance fitness with work. They feel elevated, practical, and long-lasting. A clean logo on a well-constructed bag reinforces brand credibility every time it’s used.


‍


*Photo courtesy of A24 Films*

### 9. Backpacks


A quality backpack signals seriousness. Affiliates use backpacks daily—for laptops, travel, or content creation gear. When you invest in quality here, it shows. Look for thoughtful details like padded sleeves, smart pockets, and durable materials. This is swag that becomes part of someone’s workflow.


‍


*Shown: Parkland Rio Mini Backpack*

### 10. Hats


Branded hats are an easy, low-commitment, and highly visible option. Whether it’s a classic baseball cap, a beanie, a structured snapback, or more, hats work especially well for social-first affiliates who are often on camera, making them an excellent choice for affiliate marketing digital products or[swag store](https://www.gemnote.com/software/e-commerce-store) picks.

### 11. Socks


Socks are fun, functional, and surprisingly effective. They’re easy to size, easy to ship, and feel like a thoughtful add-on in larger kits. Subtle branding or pattern-based designs keep them wearable, while custom embroidery adds a thoughtful touch.

### 12. Portable Charger Banks


Affiliate work happens everywhere. Portable charger banks are one of the most practical swag items you can offer. They support travel, events, filming days, and long work sessions. When affiliates rely on it, they remember where it came from.

### 13. Throw Blankets


For the best affiliate marketing products, think comfort that builds a connection. Branded throw blankets feel cozy, premium, and personal. They’re perfect for home offices, couches, or chilly travel days. Neutral tones and high-quality materials make all the difference.


‍


*Photo courtesy of JJJJound*

### 14. Beach Towels


Beach towels are great for summer campaigns, retreats, or lifestyle-driven brands. They offer a large branding canvas without feeling intrusive and are often used in highly visible settings. A fun design makes[custom beach towels](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/custom-beach-towels) a fast fav.

### 15. Candles


Candles add an unexpected lifestyle element to affiliate swag. They work especially well for wellness, beauty, or home-focused brands, and feel more like a gift than merch. Custom labels or[packaging](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/candle-packaging) can subtly reinforce brand identity.

*Photo courtesy of Rails*

### 16. Bluetooth Speakers


Portable speakers support content creation, workouts, travel, and downtime. They’re a high-perceived-value item that affiliates genuinely use. Choose compact designs with good sound quality and simple branding.


‍


‍


### 17. Seasonal Favorites


Seasonal swag creates brand excitement and makes for brilliant affiliate incentives. Items like pool floats, picnic blankets, or winter accessories feel timely and fun. They show your brand is paying attention to the moment, not just running a generic program. Try these affiliate marketing products for your limited-time campaigns or launches.

### 18. Calendars or Planners


Planners and calendars support productivity and goal-setting. They’re especially effective when tied to affiliate incentives, launches, or annual planning. A well-designed planner reinforces structure and consistency throughout the year.


‍


*Photo courtesy of Condé Nast Store*

### 19. Notebooks


Notebooks are simple, versatile, and always useful. Whether affiliates use them for content ideas, tracking goals, or daily notes, a quality notebook becomes part of their routine. Try embossed or debossed logos to keep the branding polished, or have fun with colors and patterns.


‍


*Shown: Public Supply notebook*

### 20. Dopp Kits


Dopp kits are ideal for travel-heavy affiliates. They’re compact, practical, and feel premium when made well. Great for event kits, retreats, or top-tier rewards.


‍


‍


### 21. Reusable Bags


Reusable bags align with modern lifestyles. They’re practical, easy to carry, and widely used. Look for sturdy construction and clean branding to avoid the “throwaway” feel.


‍


*Image courtesy of Bon Appétit*

### 22. Branded Drinkware


Branded drinkware is a staple for a reason. Mugs for home offices, tumblers for commuting, and water bottles for daily hydration all offer constant brand visibility. Choosing quality materials and thoughtful designs ensures they’re used daily.

## Why Affiliate Swag Works Better When It’s Done Right

- Reflects your brand’s quality and values
- Reinforces belonging, not obligation


When affiliates feel recognized and supported, they’re more likely to stay engaged, produce better content, and advocate for your brand authentically.
