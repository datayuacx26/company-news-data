## What Makes Swag Pants Effective


The key to swag that sticks is usefulness, quality, and brand alignment. Swag pants check every box:


- **Wardrobe Staple:** Comfy pants, whether for travel, workouts, lounging, or casual office days, are universal and always appreciated. Even swag pajama pants have a place in your recipient’s home.
- **High-Quality:** High-quality joggers or lounge pants don’t just get shoved in a drawer; they become a staple. And, just because they’re high quality, doesn’t mean they come with a high price tag. Talk to your Gemnote rep for ideas.
- **Brand Alignment:** You’ve got plenty of options when it comes to customization. With your logo, color palette, subtle brand message, and more, you can reflect your culture —whether that’s wellness, fun, or a laid-back, high-performance vibe—while promoting your company.


Plus, unlike T-shirts that can blend into the crowd, swag pants feel fresh, fun, and slightly unexpected—traits companies look for to make their brand merchandise memorable and their company top of mind.

## How to Design Swag Pants Everyone Will Love


Here’s how to make sure your swag pants are a hit, not a miss:


1. **Choose high-quality materials:** Opt for soft, breathable fabrics that people can wash and wear on repeat.


2. **Prioritize fit and sizing inclusivity:** Offering a wide range of sizes ensures everyone feels included and comfortable.


3. **Customization is key:** Screen printed, embroidered, custom tag, logo patch—when it comes to showing off your company, Gemnote specializes in keeping your swag on brand and in demand!


4. **Location is everything.** A small logo on the hip or ankle is great for those less is less-is-more vibes. Or, embrace the maximalist trend, with a large logo running down the leg.

## Sustainable Swag Pants: The Future of Branded Apparel


Sustainability matters, and it should influence your swag strategy. Many brands now offer eco-friendly joggers made from organic cotton or recycled polyester blends, aligning with your company’s sustainability goals while giving your employees something they’ll feel good about wearing.


**When selecting sustainable options:**


- Check for certifications like GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard) or OEKO-TEX.
- Don’t forget about[eco-friendly packaging](https://www.gemnote.com/capabilities/packaging-sustainability) and delivery.
- Share your sustainability story when gifting the pants to help recipients understand your commitment to responsible practices.
