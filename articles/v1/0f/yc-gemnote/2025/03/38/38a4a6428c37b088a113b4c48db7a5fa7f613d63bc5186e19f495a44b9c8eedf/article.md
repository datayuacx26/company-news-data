## 5 Tips For Creating an On-Brand New Hire Swag Box


‍


### 1. Define Your Goal


Before you dive in, decide what your new hire swag box should achieve. A free T-shirt is nice, but the real magic happens when your swag has a purpose beyond being another logo-stamped giveaway.


#### Here are some goals to consider:


- **Reinforce Company Culture:** Use swag to reflect your company’s values and personality. If innovation drives your brand, think high-tech gadgets like wireless chargers. If wellness is your thing, toss in a sleek water bottle or a mindfulness journal.
- **Make New Hires Feel Valued:** Thoughtful touches—like a handwritten note, a personalized item, or a nod to their interview—show employees they’re more than a headcount.
- **Encourage Brand Advocacy:** When new hires love their swag, they’ll rock it outside the office. That’s free exposure and a boost to team pride.


‍


The key to nailing your swag box lies in balancing creativity with intention. Start by picking a theme that ties everything together—think “Work-from-Anywhere Essentials” for remote teams or “Eco-Friendly Vibes” for sustainability-focused companies. Then, source high-quality items that new hires will actually use, like a durable tote bag or a[branded notebook](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/branded-notebooks) with a premium feel. Avoid generic fillers; every piece should spark excitement or serve a function. Partnering with a swag expert like Gemnote can streamline the process, ensuring your box is cohesive, professional, and delivered on time—leaving you free to focus on welcoming your new team members.

### 2. Budget Your Swag Boxes For New Hires


Before diving into swag selection, set a budget that fits your onboarding strategy. Swag boxes can range from simple and affordable to premium and polished, depending on item count, customization, and packaging quality.


#### Here’s how to budget smartly:


- **Basic ($25-$50 per box):** Start with a branded T-shirt as the star, then toss in low-cost goodies like stickers, a pen, or a notebook to round it out.
- **Mid-Tier ($50-$100 per box):** Step it up with a custom tee,[branded water bottle](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/custom-water-bottles) , and a small tech item like a phone stand or cable organizer.
- **Premium ($100+ per box):** Go big with high-end apparel (think hoodies or jackets), top-notch gadgets (like wireless earbuds), and eco-friendly packaging.

#### Making It Work:


Crafting a new hire swag box that lands doesn’t need to drain your wallet—it’s about clever moves. Start with a hero item, like a branded T-shirt or a slick water bottle, then layer in affordable extras like stickers or a trusty pen to keep it punchy without overspending. Ordering in bulk trims costs, letting you pack more value into every box. Above all, go for quality over chaos—new hires will cherish a few standout pieces far more than a jumble of forgettable trinkets.


‍


#### Finding the Sweet Spot:


Budgeting is about striking a balance that fits your crew. A basic box can shine with a branded tee as the star, paired with playful touches like a keychain or notepad to keep it light and fun. For a mid-tier vibe, mix a practical water bottle with a handy tech perk like a cable organizer. Going premium? Invest in lasting appeal—think cozy hoodies or earbuds wrapped in eco-friendly packaging. Match it to your team’s world—webcam covers for remote pros, desk gear for office regulars—and lean on a swag partner like Gemnote to maximize every dollar.
