## Tips for Creating Seasonal Swag That Stands Out


With so many options, how do you choose swag that leaves a lasting impression?


‍


**Here are a few tips to guide your spring strategy:**


- **Be functional.** If your swag solves a seasonal problem (like staying cool, packing light, or organizing a desk), it’ll get used—and remembered.


- **Stay on theme.** Align colors, patterns, and messaging with spring. Think florals, bright hues, bold greens, citrusy shades, or notice me patterns in your brand colors.


‍


*Photo courtesy of Only New York*


‍


- **Think sustainable.** Look for eco-conscious packaging, recycled materials, or refillable formats. Spring is the time to lead with values.


- **Add personality.** Include branded messaging cards with humor or inspiration to boost the unboxing experience and highlight your brand voice while connecting with your recipients.


- **Make it Instagram-worthy.** Beautiful design encourages shares and unboxing videos, which means free brand exposure. With the best promotional items for spring you can make sure your company gets noticed!
