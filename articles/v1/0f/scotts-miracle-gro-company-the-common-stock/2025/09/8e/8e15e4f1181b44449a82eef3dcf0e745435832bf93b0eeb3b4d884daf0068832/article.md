# Clover & drought-tolerant mix lead new low-maintenance offerings | ScottsMiracle-Gro US

O.M. Scott & Sons is an innovative brand designed to meet the needs of consumers who desire beautiful, easily maintained outdoor spaces with a focus on natural ingredients and sustainable packaging. As a low-maintenance alternative to traditional grass, clover grass seed is a key player in the initial product line.


[The O.M. Scott & Sons clover seed](https://scottsmiraclegro.com/en-us/brands/scotts/products/grass-seed/18437C.html) is expertly crafted with premium strawberry clover, designed to establish naturally low-maintenance and drought-tolerant green spaces across the U.S. This clover lawn seed is also formulated without any added artificial or pest control ingredients, ensuring it's safe for use around people and pets when applied as directed.


Clover's inherent ability to self-fertilize is a significant advantage. It works in harmony with nitrogen-fixing bacteria to convert atmospheric nitrogen into usable plant fertilizer. Furthermore, this low-growing ground cover requires less frequent mowing and significantly less water compared to most traditional turfgrass lawns, simplifying yard care.


Strawberry clover's deep taproot system allows it to access water reserves deep within the soil, enabling it to maintain its verdant appearance even during short dry spells. Its stolon-based spreading mechanism facilitates rapid establishment compared to many turfgrasses and provides a valuable natural food source for vital pollinators such as bees and butterflies, contributing to a thriving ecosystem.


Complementing this water-wise approach, Scotts recently introduced the[Scotts Grass Seed Drought Tolerant Mix](https://scottsmiraclegro.com/en-us/brands/scotts/products/grass-seed/scotts-turf-builder-grass-seed-drought-tol-mix/18442A.html) , now available to Home Depot and Amazon customers. This blend strategically combines Tall Fescue, known for its deep root system that taps into deep soil moisture, and Kentucky Bluegrass, which utilizes rhizomes for natural spreading and filling in bare areas.


Looking ahead, Scotts is poised to expand its offerings with the introduction of more native grass species, including buffalo grass, dichondra, and blue grama, further catering to environmentally conscious consumers seeking regionally adapted, low-maintenance solutions.
