# A Safety Milestone that Exemplifies Our Safety Culture | ScottsMiracle-Gro US

In order for safety to be a true part of workplace culture, it has to be a consistent commitment each and every day. It must be deeply ingrained into processes and habits across the company.


When it comes to safety and consistency at ScottsMiracle-Gro, one team has achieved a Cal Ripken-like streak that will be hard to duplicate. (Though our teams will certainly try.)


Our Lawrenceville, Virginia, site recently celebrated a 30 year lost time accident (LTA) free milestone. Everyone at Lawrenceville has contributed to this incredible feat not only for our industry, but all industries. To celebrate the achievement, key leaders met with and congratulated associates. There were steaks, a festive spread of food and, of course, cake for dessert. The whole team mingled and reminisced about the outstanding 30 year journey of creating an exceptional safety culture.


Shout out to all of our associates who are a part of our safety culture where we stay safe each and every day, at work and at home.
