# Empowering Columbus City Schools through gardening & fresh foods | ScottsMiracle-Gro US

## The magic that grows as kids discover gardening


Our purpose to GroMoreGood takes many forms. One of those special moments happened with Columbus City Schools in Ohio, where the magic of a single idea blossomed beyond its original intent and became something bigger, something greater. The growth journey of the Columbus City Schools program epitomizes GroMoreGood. A new generation of kids is developing a lifelong connection to the earth.


Origins of the Farm-to-School program


Early in her career, school teacher Katie Young had a writing assignment for her students that was based on a simple question: Do you know where food comes from? Not one of the students answered that it comes from the earth. Katie knew this had to change.


In 2018, the[Columbus City Schools Farm to School program](https://sites.google.com/columbus.k12.oh.us/ccs-farm-to-school/home) was born in partnership with ScottsMiracle-Gro,[The Scotts Miracle-Gro Foundation](https://scottsmiraclegro.com/en-us/corporate/our-impact/the-foundation.html) and[The Ohio State University Extension](https://extension.osu.edu/today/farm-school) . Teachers were given tools and Miracle-Gro supplies to teach kids how to create, tend to and harvest gardens full of fresh vegetables.


Benefits of student mental health and wellness


The school garden is about building community and promoting emotional wellness.


GroMoreGood impact beyond the classroom


Elementary school students discovered that fresh foods are delicious, sometimes on their own and sometimes when added to a smoothie or pizza!
