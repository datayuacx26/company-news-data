# Conversational AI in Healthcare: Benefits and Use Cases

#


Conversational AI is an exciting new technology that is transforming the healthcare industry. New AI models can interact with patients in a natural, conversational manner and help with tasks like scheduling, billing, and customer support.


Implementing conversational AI in healthcare assists busy providers by automating repetitive tasks, freeing up more time to focus on valuable care. Patients also enjoy faster responses and better overall customer service from their healthcare providers.


Here’s how conversational AI works, why it’s so beneficial for healthcare providers, and how it’s changing the industry.


## What Is conversational AI in healthcare?


Conversational AI is a type of artificial intelligence that uses natural language processing to have conversations. Users can talk or type naturally, and the AI model responds similarly to how a human would.


This technology is particularly valuable in healthcare settings. Many healthcare providers struggle to manage a large patient load without compromising the quality of care and service. Conversational AI can handle many repetitive administrative tasks, freeing up time for providers.


With this technology, patients can interact directly with an AI model to set up appointments, handle insurance claims, and more. The model answers the patient’s questions and guides them through the process, referring them to a human healthcare expert when appropriate.


Healthcare AI models are also designed to comply with HIPAA and other important privacy standards.


## How does conversational AI support healthcare?


Conversational AI models can integrate naturally into many different aspects of healthcare operations. It’s particularly helpful for automating routine patient interactions, which gives both providers and administrative staff more time to focus on complex tasks.


For example, a conversational AI model might welcome patients to the facility and guide them through an intake form, freeing front desk staff to address complex insurance issues or scheduling concerns. When patients have questions, the conversational AI can either answer directly or connect them with the appropriate staff member to address their concerns.


Conversational AI tools also support healthcare providers with clinical workflows, such as assisting doctors and nurses with charting and other documentation tasks. These tasks are time-consuming when completed by hand, leading to delays and provider burnout. With AI support, providers can document each appointment faster and stay on schedule.


## Benefits of conversational AI in healthcare


There are many benefits to using conversational AI in healthcare settings, both for patients and providers. Here’s why this new technology is worth the investment for healthcare facilities.


### Better patient experience


When used strategically, conversational AI improves the overall healthcare experience for patients. For example, conversational AI can answer questions about healthcare services, availability, and insurance options. These tools can even follow up with patients after an appointment to check on their symptoms and schedule future appointments if necessary.


Navigating the healthcare system is often overwhelming for patients, but this added support can make the process easier. These positive experiences encourage patients to return when they need care, and routine care often leads to better health outcomes for patients and more financial stability.


### More operational efficiency


Conversational AI tools help providers operate more efficiently, even during busy or stressful periods. They can handle aspects of patient scheduling, check-in, billing, and documentation so appointments move faster. Providers can focus entirely on providing quality care, and appointments are less likely to run late.


### Faster access to care


With conversational AI, patients can access the care they need quickly. When patients start experiencing symptoms, they can talk with an AI chatbot to get more information right away.


The chatbot can help the patient find the right provider for their symptoms and insurance policy and schedule an appointment. In some cases, it can even schedule a telehealth appointment so the patient can get expert care without leaving their home.


While conversational AI tools can’t make diagnoses, they could suggest ways to manage symptoms at home until the patient can see a doctor. This extra support gives patients comfort and peace of mind.


In many cases, fast access to healthcare can have a direct impact on health outcomes. Patients can get a diagnosis and treatment plan before symptoms progress to something more serious.


## Use cases of conversational AI in healthcare


Conversational AI in healthcare has a wide variety of possible use cases. Here are some of the ways that healthcare organizations are using conversational AI to improve their operations.


### Patient scheduling and appointment management


Conversational AI helps make the scheduling process quick and easy for patients. Patients can call your office or visit your website, and an AI chatbot will help them schedule an appointment. It will ask about the patient’s care needs and availability and will use that information to find available time slots.


When a patient books an appointment, it automatically goes on your calendar, which reduces manual work for your administrative team. Conversational AI agents can also send patients reminder messages for upcoming appointments, which helps reduce no-shows. If a patient needs to cancel or reschedule an appointment, AI can handle it in just a few minutes.


### Patient triage


In busy hospitals and urgent care clinics, the triage process can be a significant operational bottleneck. Conversational AI can help patients get care faster.


Patients can talk directly to an AI agent about their symptoms and medical history, and the AI will prioritize care accordingly. Conversational AI kiosks can also guide patients and their families as they complete intake forms. By automating the triage process, high-volume facilities can reduce wait times for patients and reduce stress on staff.


### Billing, insurance, and admin support


Conversational AI is particularly helpful for administrative tasks like billing patients and submitting insurance claims. These AI tools can suggest appropriate insurance codes based on appointment documentation.


If the claim is approved, AI agents can help bill the client for any remaining charges and send payment reminder messages. If the client would like to appeal a denied claim, conversational AI can help walk them through the process.


Conversational AI can also answer patient questions about their insurance coverage and payment options. By talking to an AI agent, patients will develop a better understanding of what is and isn’t covered under their insurance policy to reduce confusion.


### Patient communication


Conversational AI tools can help doctors communicate more effectively with their patients throughout the entire care cycle. Before appointments, conversational AI can answer questions about the facility’s location, estimated appointment costs, and how to prepare, so patients know what to expect.


Immediately after the appointment, an AI agent can send patients a summary of their treatment plan. If the patient has questions, they can ask the AI for clarification. These tools can also send follow-up messages to see how patients are recovering and schedule future appointments.


Personalized communication keeps patients engaged before and after their appointment. Regular reminders can also help patients stay on track with their medication and any lifestyle changes they need to make.


### Self-service support


Medical issues don’t always happen during working hours. Conversational AI gives patients access to 24/7 medical support when they need it, even if their doctor is unavailable.


Patients can tell an AI agent about their symptoms and concerns, and the AI can make recommendations about how to manage those symptoms and whether emergency treatment is necessary. If patients have questions about scheduling or billing, they can ask the AI chatbot when it’s convenient for them, rather than being limited to business hours.


## The future of conversational AI for healthcare


It’s just the beginning of conversational[AI for healthcare](https://www.soundhound.com/voice-ai-solutions/healthcare/) . As this technology continues to develop, large language models will likely get more sophisticated, and AI agents will be able to work with minimal human input.


With these changes, conversational AI models will be able to handle more complex healthcare tasks. Agents will develop more industry-specific knowledge and will be able to support both in-person care and telehealth with less manual input.


Healthcare organizations will need to be vigilant to make sure their AI tools remain compliant with HIPAA and other patient privacy standards. Healthcare staff will also need to continue to oversee AI operations and correct any errors, even when the AI is capable of completing tasks on its own.
