# QueryPie unveils Lingo, an AI real-time interpretation service


Press Release


June 5, 2026


QueryPie, a data security and AI platform company led by CEO Brant Hwang, announced Lingo, an AI real-time interpretation service connected to QueryPie AI Platform.


Lingo recognizes speakers’ voices and provides captions and translations across major meeting environments, including in-person meetings, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom. It supports Japanese, Korean, English, Thai, and Vietnamese, with additional languages planned.


QueryPie explained that it communicates daily with customers, partners, and investors across Korea, Japan, the United States, and other regions. Its teams in Korea, Japan, and Indonesia also collaborate across different languages. After trying existing real-time interpretation services, the company found it difficult to identify a service that satisfied speed, quality, and post-meeting workflow connectivity requirements, so it decided to build Lingo directly.


Real-time captions and translation for in-person meetings, Google Meet, Teams, and Zoom From live interpretation during meetings to post-meeting summaries and workflow automation


Lingo automatically detects the spoken language, transcribes meeting conversations, and translates them in real time. During meetings, it provides fast captions; after the meeting, it refines final captions and translations based on the meeting context.


After a meeting ends, Lingo can connect with Agents and MCP integrated with QueryPie AI Platform to automate follow-up work such as meeting summaries, task organization, CRM record updates, and document generation. For example, a sales representative can automatically upload customer meeting notes to Salesforce CRM, or a quotation Agent can generate a document and store it in Google Drive.


For security, Lingo applies organization-level data isolation, authentication, encrypted storage, and audit controls to process sensitive meeting data safely.


Pricing follows QueryPie AI Platform’s credit-based usage model, with no fixed fees or per-user billing. QueryPie said this enables customers to use Lingo at a lower cost than major real-time interpretation services.


QueryPie plans to evolve Lingo into a meeting intelligence service. As meeting records accumulate, Lingo can help identify organizational roles, decision-making relationships, and post-meeting workflows. In the future, QueryPie plans to expand it into an AI Agent that combines HR, CRM, and ERP data to identify risks and opportunities.


“Lingo started from a problem we experienced firsthand while working as a global organization,” said Brant Hwang, CEO of QueryPie. “Even when we were willing to pay more, it was difficult to find a real-time interpretation service that was fast and accurate enough, so we eventually built one ourselves.”


“The goal of Lingo is to help teams and customers who speak different languages understand meeting content accurately and ensure that what is discussed in meetings leads to real work,” he added. “Together with QueryPie AI Platform, we will build Lingo into a service that turns meeting data into actionable organizational knowledge.”


## About QueryPie


QueryPie AI is an enterprise security company founded in Silicon Valley in 2016. It develops and provides data and system access control solutions optimized for cloud and on-premises environments.


Officially launched in June 2020 through collaboration among engineers from Kakao and Naver and experts from various fields, the Access Control Platform has grown across access control and audit areas for data, infrastructure, and business systems. More recently, QueryPie has added AI capabilities on top of its security foundation to help enterprises use AI tools and AI Agents while consistently maintaining permissions, approvals, audits, and sensitive data protection.


QueryPie has also launched AI Platform, expanding into an AI platform business that connects all SaaS applications, data, and internal systems used by enterprises with AI Agents to enable real work automation and AX execution. AI Platform includes apps directly provided by QueryPie, such as Lingo, a real-time interpretation service, and NotePie, which helps users easily generate materials from internal company data.


QueryPie AI provides a proven security platform to enterprise customers based on global security certifications and compliance capabilities, including SOC 2 Type II, CSA STAR, PCI DSS, ISO/IEC 27001, and ISMS-P. The company has raised a total of USD 36 million from global investors including Y Combinator, Salesforce, and SoftBank-affiliated Z Venture Capital. QueryPie AI serves 80% of Korea’s unicorn companies as customers and is accelerating global expansion across Japan, the United States, and Southeast Asia.
