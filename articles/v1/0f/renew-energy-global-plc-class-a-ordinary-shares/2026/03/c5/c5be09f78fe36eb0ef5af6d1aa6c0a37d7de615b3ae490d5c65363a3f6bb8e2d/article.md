# Can India Power Its Future Without Fossil Fuels? | Sumant Sinha | The India Story

China powered its manufacturing rise on coal.


But India may have a chance to take a very different path.


In the episode of The India Story, host Vikram Chandra speaks with Sumant Sinha, Chairman and CEO of ReNew, about how India’s energy transition could reshape the country’s economic future.


From rapidly falling solar prices to advances in battery storage, electric vehicles, and green hydrogen, the conversation explores how India could reduce its dependence on imported fossil fuels while strengthening energy security.
