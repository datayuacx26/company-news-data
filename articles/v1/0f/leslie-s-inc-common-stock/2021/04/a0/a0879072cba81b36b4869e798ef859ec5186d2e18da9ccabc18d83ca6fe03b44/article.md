# Best Variable Speed Pool Pumps

Your pool pump does a lot of heavy lifting, circulating water, supporting filtration, and distributing chemicals. But if your pump only runs at one speed, it may also be working harder and using more electricity than necessary. That’s where a variable speed pool pump can make a big difference.


Instead of running at full power every time it turns on, a variable speed pump can run slower for everyday circulation and speed up when your pool needs more flow. That usually means quieter operation, better control, and lower energy use.


So, which variable speed pool pump is best? The honest pool-expert answer is: it depends on your pool. Below, we will break down some of the best options available at Leslie's and help you narrow down the right fit.

## How to Choose the Right Variable Speed Pump


Here is the part we always stress in the store: do not choose a pump based on pool gallons or horsepower alone. The right pump must provide enough flow for the pool while working safely with the rest of the equipment.


Before buying, consider:


- **Plumbing size and layout:** Pipe diameter, length, fittings, and elevation all affect resistance and flow.
- **Your filter's maximum flow rate:** The pump should not push more water than the filter is designed to handle.
- **Heaters and salt systems:** These products usually require a minimum flow rate to operate.
- **Spas and water features:** Jets, waterfalls, deck jets, and in-floor cleaners can significantly increase flow requirements.
- **Voltage:** Confirm whether the equipment pad supplies 115V, 230V, or can support either.
- **Automation:** Check compatibility before assuming a new pump will communicate with an existing controller.
- **Replacement footprint:** Measure the plumbing connections, port height, available space, and service clearance.
- **Efficiency rating:** Compare the pump's Weighted Energy Factor, or WEF. A higher WEF indicates more gallons moved per unit of energy under the federal test procedure, but models should still be compared within the context of your pool's needs.
- **Local rebates:** ENERGY STAR certified pumps may qualify for utility rebates in some areas.
