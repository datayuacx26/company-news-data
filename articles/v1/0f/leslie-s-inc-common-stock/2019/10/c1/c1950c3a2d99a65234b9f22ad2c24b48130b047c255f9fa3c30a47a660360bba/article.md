# Leslie's Installation, Repair, and Maintenance Services

With almost 1,000 stores nationwide, Leslie's has been making pool care easy for families like yours since 1963. We're more than just a trusted one-stop shop for pool equipment, chemicals, and supplies; our friendly team brings expert repair and installation services right to your doorstep, so you can relax and enjoy your pool without worries.


At Leslie's, we truly have your back. Our caring, certified technicians are part of one of the industry’s most reliable service teams, ready to repair, install, or maintain your pool, spa, or equipment—all backed by full licensing, bonding, and insurance. Your peace of mind and enjoyment are always our top priority.


Here’s a quick look at everything we do to help you spend less time worrying and more time enjoying your pool or spa. Flip through the **[service highlights](https://lesliespool.com/service)** below and see how Leslie’s makes pool care easy, every step of the way.


## [Installation Services](https://lesliespool.com/service)


- **Automatic Pool Cleaners:** Expert installation ensures thorough and efficient cleaning, keeping your pool spotless with minimal effort.
- **Chlorinators:** Professional setup for automatic chlorine delivery, ensuring balanced water chemistry and safe swimming conditions.
- **Filters:** Installation of sand, cartridge, or DE filters guarantees proper debris removal and clean, healthy water.
- **Gas Heaters:** Safe hookup and calibration for reliable water heating, extending your swimming season.
- **Heat Pumps:** Installation of energy-efficient systems that maintain your pool's temperature cost-effectively.
- **Pool Pumps:** Correct setup to optimize water circulation and enhance filtration, supporting a sparkling clean pool.
- **Safety Covers:** Secure installation of protective covers to safeguard children and pets and reduce debris.
- **Safety Fences:** Proper fence placement for enhanced safety around your pool perimeter.
- **Solar Covers:** Installation to help retain heat and reduce water evaporation, saving energy and money.
- **Spa Blowers:** Set up for improved spa bubbles and water aeration, enhancing your relaxation experience.
- **Vinyl Liners:** Precision installation or replacement for a leak-resistant, visually appealing pool.
- **Handrails and Slides:** Safe and sturdy installation for accessibility and fun features.
- **Time Clocks:** Setup of timer devices to automate your pool equipment operations and save on energy costs.


## [Repair and Maintenance Services](https://lesliespool.com/service)


- **Acid Wash:** Deep cleaning for plaster pools to remove stubborn stains and restore brightness.
- **Filter Sand Change:** Replacement of sand in your filter for optimal debris capture and clear water.
- **Filter Cleaning:** Professional cleaning of all filter types to maintain performance and prolong lifespan.
- **Salt Cell Cleaning:** Removal of calcium build-up in salt cells for efficient chlorination.
- **Clear Plumbing Lines:** Clearing obstructions for smooth water flow and preventing equipment stress.
- **Pool Draining:** Safe and efficient water removal for maintenance or renovation needs.
- **Heater Diagnostic and Tune-Up:** Comprehensive checks and fine-tuning for dependable heating.
- **Pool Opening:** Full reopening services including cleaning, equipment setup, and chemical balancing.
- **Pool Closing:** Preparation of your pool for winter to prevent freeze damage and ease reopening.
- **Test Entrapment Protection:** Safety inspections to ensure all anti-entrapment devices are functional.
- **Tile Cleaning:** Removal of scale, stains, and grime for sparkling pool tile.
- **Winter Watch Program:** Ongoing care throughout the winter months for optimal pool health.
- **Pool School:** Personalized tutorials from experts on pool operation and care.
