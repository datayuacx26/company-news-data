## Less Guesswork with AccuBlue® Water Testing


Pool water balance is where most pool owners’ headaches begin: cloudy water, algae, staining, itchy skin, strong chlorine smell. Leslie’s **[AccuBlue® in-store water test](https://lesliespool.com/accublue.html)** is designed to take the guesswork out of all of that.


### How it works:


When you bring in a water sample, AccuBlue runs a 10-point analysis and shows you exactly what’s going on with your water. Then, our associates walk you through a customized treatment plan that tells you:


- What products your pool needs
- How much of each product to use
- In what order
- And how soon you’ll be back on track!


You get a clear, easy-to-follow plan instead of juggling test strips, charts, and conflicting advice. Prefer to test at home? **[AccuBlue Home®](https://lesliespool.com/accublue-home.html)** offers the same detailed insights from your own backyard, all connected through the Leslie’s app.
