Chlorine tabs slowly dissolve in your pool water, releasing chlorine over time to help keep the water clean and properly sanitized. **[Leslie’s 6-in-1 Tabs](https://lesliespool.com/leslies-6-in-1-chlorine-tablets-30-lbs-bucket/12343.html)** do that while also delivering additional water-care benefits that would often require separate specialty chemicals. ### 1. Simplify your weekly routine


If you like the idea of doing less measuring, pouring, and juggling different products, 6-in-1 Tabs are made for you. Their multi-function formula helps support several common water-care needs in one step, making maintenance easier to keep up with week after week.


### 2. Keep water clean and clear


The most important job of any chlorine tab is sanitizing. Leslie’s 6-in-1 Tabs provide consistent chlorine to help kill bacteria and algae, while also helping clarify the water for a cleaner, clearer pool.

Sunlight can break down chlorine quickly, especially during hot, sunny pool season. 6-in-1 Tabs include UV protection to help your chlorine last longer, giving your pool more consistent sanitizing support between maintenance steps.

In addition to sanitizing and clarifying, 6-in-1 Tabs help soften pool water. Softer-feeling water can make swimming more comfortable and help create a more enjoyable pool experience overall.


### 5. Protect against stains and scale


Stains and scale can make your pool surfaces and equipment look and feel less than their best. 6-in-1 Tabs help prevent stains and scale as part of their all-in-one formula, supporting better-looking water and surfaces.
