## Why Power Powder Plus Shock Stands Out


One of the biggest reasons **[Leslie’s Power Powder Plus](https://lesliespool.com/power-powder/)** stands out is its cal-hypo formula. Cal-hypo, or calcium hypochlorite, is known for being a strong, effective chlorine shock. Power Powder Plus uses that chemistry to deliver serious sanitizing power without adding cyanuric acid, also known as CYA or stabilizer. That is an important difference.


Some shock treatments add stabilizer to the water, and over time, too much can lead to overstabilization, sometimes called chlorine lock. When that happens, chlorine becomes less effective, even when testing shows it is present. In other words, your pool can have chlorine in it, but that chlorine may not be working the way it should.


Because Power Powder Plus does not add cyanuric acid, it helps you avoid that problem while still giving your pool the strong chlorine boost it needs.
