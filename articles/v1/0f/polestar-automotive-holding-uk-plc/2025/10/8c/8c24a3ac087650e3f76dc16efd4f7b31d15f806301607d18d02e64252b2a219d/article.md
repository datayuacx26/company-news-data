# All Polestar 3 vehicles to get new, more powerful NVIDIA core computer

“Polestar 3 is engineered for the future and to provide functional growth over time," says Gerry Yang, Chief Technology Officer at Polestar. "This free upgrade for all existing Polestar 3 vehicles will enable faster performance and unlock new features for customers in the future, reflecting our commitment to continue improving our products and deliver the best possible ownership experience.”


In addition to hardware upgrades, Polestar continuously works with[over-the-air software updates (OTA)](https://www.polestar.com/owning-a-polestar/ota/) to deliver improvements and add new features to its vehicles. Thanks to the centralised core computing system, Polestar 3 offers even greater possibilities for comprehensive vehicle updates.


New features that have already been introduced this way include the smart schedule for charging, an advanced digital key, and the music listening experience Abbey Road Studios Mode for the Bowers & Wilkins audio system. These are just a few examples of how OTA updates help Polestar vehicles evolve over time, with more features and improvements to come.


For those considering a Polestar 3 as their next vehicle, there’s never been a better time to make the move.[Pre-configured Polestar 3](https://www.polestar.com/preconfigured-cars/polestar-3/) vehicles are available for delivery and will offer customers the same cutting-edge core computer technology as the 2026 model, so customers can buy with confidence knowing their Polestar 3 will keep evolving and improving over time.


Note: The complimentary core computer upgrade will cover all existing Polestar 3 vehicles, including pre-configured cars available for delivery. The hardware change will start at the beginning of 2026 and be performed at a Polestar authorized service point. Customers will be contacted to book a time to have the NVIDIA DRIVE AGX Orin processor fitted.
