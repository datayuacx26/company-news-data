# Polestar portraits with professional cyclist Demi Vollering

##### Total training


To do that, Demi needs to look at her training in a 360 degree way. It starts the moment her day begins. Even something like her morning coffee is part of a thoughtful, purposeful routine.


After that, Demi moves into yoga. This is a daily ritual that is as much about flexibility as it is mindfulness.


Then, time on the bike. Rides vary by distance, terrain, and weather, depending on the training cycle. Everything is planned. Everything has a purpose.


It’s this attention to detail that resonates with us at Polestar. Demi’s approach mirrors our own development process: refining, testing, evolving. Always with purpose.
