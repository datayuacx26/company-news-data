# Solventum to Participate in the 2026 BofA Securities Health Care Conference

ST. PAUL, Minn.


, May 7, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Solventum](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4682927-1&h=2857441875&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.solventum.com%2F&a=Solventum) (NYSE: SOLV) announced today that management will participate in a fireside chat at the BofA Securities Health Care Conference on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, beginning at approximately 10:00 a.m. PDT in Las Vegas, NV.


A live and archived replay of the fireside chat will be available on the company's website at[investors.solventum.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4682927-1&h=141579748&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.solventum.com%2F&a=investors.solventum.com) .
