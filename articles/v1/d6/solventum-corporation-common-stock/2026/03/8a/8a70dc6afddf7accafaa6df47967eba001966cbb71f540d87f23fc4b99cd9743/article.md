# Solventum to Participate in the 2026 KeyBanc Healthcare Forum

ST. PAUL, Minn.


, March 11, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Solventum](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4638253-1&h=2233090968&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.solventum.com%2F&a=Solventum) (NYSE: SOLV) announced today that management will participate in a virtual fireside chat at the KeyBanc Healthcare Forum on Wednesday, Mar. 18, 2026, beginning at approximately 10:30 a.m. EDT.


More information about this event, including access to the live webcast and the webcast replay, will be available at[investors.solventum.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4638253-1&h=658181167&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.solventum.com%2F&a=investors.solventum.com) .
