# Your Ambarella Edge at Embedded World 2026

Ambarella will be exhibiting at[Embedded World 2026](https://www.embedded-world.de/en) , taking place March 10–12 in Nuremberg, Germany, where we’ll demonstrate how our AI SoCs, software stack, and developer tools help teams build, integrate, and deploy edge AI solutions at scale.


Our exhibit is organized around the theme “The Ambarella Edge: From Agentic to Physical AI.” Through a series of live demonstrations, we’ll highlight how our technology delivers a competitive advantage across a broad range of AI applications—from agentic automation and orchestration to physical AI systems operating in real-world environments.


**Enabling Scalable Edge AI Development**


At the core of our exhibit is Ambarella’s scalable portfolio of AI SoCs designed to deliver high AI performance per watt across diverse edge AI workloads. Our AI SoCs are complemented by a software platform that enables rapid development and efficient deployment as applications move from prototype to production.


Live demonstrations will showcase differentiation at the stack level, illustrating how our hardware and software platform work together to support next-generation use cases in robotics, industrial automation, automotive, edge infrastructure, security, and AIoT.


**Built for Real-World Deployment**


As edge AI systems evolve, developers are increasingly tasked with delivering applications that must operate under strict power, latency, and reliability constraints, while still achieving high levels of performance. Ambarella has deployed over 40 million edge AI SoCs in Physical AI devices worldwide, out of over 400 million total SoCs shipped to date, giving our company deep insight into the demands and challenges of real-world edge AI workloads.


At the show, we’ll demonstrate how Ambarella’s ecosystem—bringing together performance-efficient AI SoCs with a robust software platform, sample workflows, and development resources—supports engineering teams in addressing these challenges as they develop and scale edge AI solutions across a wide range of industry verticals.


**Supporting Evaluation, Integration, and Deployment**


We’ll also be featuring our[Developer Zone](https://www.ambarella.com/developer/) (DevZone) as part of our Embedded World exhibit, including select partner solutions developed using Ambarella technology. DevZone provides developers, partners, independent software vendors (ISVs), module builders, and system integrators with hands-on access to software tools, optimized models, and agentic blueprints.


By bringing these resources together in a single cohesive environment, DevZone is designed to reduce friction across evaluation, integration, and deployment—providing a unified entry point to help developers move more efficiently from proof-of-concept to production.


**Visit Us at the Show**


Our exhibit will be located in[Hall 5, Booth 5-355](https://www.embedded-world.de/en/exhibitors/ambarella-inc-2487049#floorplan) . If you’re interested in scheduling a guided tour or meeting, please contact your Ambarella representative or visit the page below.
