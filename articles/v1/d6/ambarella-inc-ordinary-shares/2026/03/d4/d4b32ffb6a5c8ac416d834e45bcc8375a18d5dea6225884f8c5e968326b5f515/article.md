# Embedded World Shows Edge AI in Full Force

The Ambarella team has landed back in the US after a week at Embedded World. We’re (more than) a little tired, but it’s been an incredible, exhilarating few days in Nuremberg, Germany.


We were delighted to connect with hundreds of developers, partners, and system architects during the show. It was great to hear how they’re putting the latest edge AI innovations into practice, the challenges they were facing, and how Ambarella’s technology fits into their solutions.


At the Ambarella booth, we showcased how perception, planning, and execution can be implemented at the edge—where power budgets are limited, where conditions can be less than ideal, and where systems can’t easily be replaced if they fail.


Across a series of live demonstrations, we were able to highlight how Ambarella’s AI SoCs, software platform, and developer tools enable intelligent systems that can automate tasks, interpret complex environments, and operate efficiently in the physical world.


These demonstrations included robotics and industrial automation as well as security, automotive, and broader AIoT applications, and helped show how edge AI systems can move from prototype to production and work inside the power, latency and reliability real-world constraints that are placed on them.


## **Ambarella Developer Zone**


Another key demonstration at the Ambarella exhibition was our Developer Zone (Dev Zone). Providing a unified entry point into our technology stack, Dev Zone makes it easier for teams to prototype, build, and deploy next-generation AI solutions at scale.


Within the Dev Zone, we were able to give developers and partners, as well as a wide range of independent software vendors, module builders, and system integrators hands-on access to an array of custom software tools, optimized models, and agentic blueprints—accelerating time-to-value.


Of course, this was also helped by our Developer Networking Social where attendees were able to meet the Ambarella team, connect with fellow developers, discuss the latest edge AI innovations, and take part in our giveaway raffle.


## **Where are we Next?**


For those of you who were at Embedded World and made it to our booth, thank you for your time. It means so much to meet like-minded developers, share our latest developments, and talk about what the future might hold.


For those that were not, our next event will be at our invitation-only exhibit at ISC West in Las Vegas from March 25 – 27. Here we’ll demonstrate how edge AI is enabling the next generation of intelligent security and physical AI systems under the theme, “A Smarter Vision for AI”. If you’d like to meet us there, please contact your Ambarella representative for more information.
