# T-Mobile execs open a door to mmWave FWA

#### T-Mobile execs open a door to mmWave FWA


Posted on November 17, 2022


**WAILEA, Hawaii** – T-Mobile may finally be ready to find a serious use for the millimeter wave (mmWave) spectrum it's had stashed on a shelf. And it doesn't involve phones. Instead, the carrier is now looking at using its mmWave holdings to upgrade its fixed-wireless service in high-demand areas, two T-Mobile executives said in an interview at Qualcomm's[Snapdragon Summit](https://www.qualcomm.com/company/events/snapdragon-summit) here last week.
