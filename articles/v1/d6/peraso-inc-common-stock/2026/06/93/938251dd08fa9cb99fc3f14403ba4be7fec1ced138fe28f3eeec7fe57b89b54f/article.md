# Peraso (PRSO) and Virewirx Achieve Milestone in Drone Communication Technology

#### Peraso (PRSO) and Virewirx Achieve Milestone in Drone Communication Technology


Posted on June 30, 2026


Peraso Inc PRSO and Virewirx announced a successful demonstration of a simulated environment for drone wireless communication, achieving a peak data capacity exceeding 50Gbps. This milestone underscores the collaborative efforts of both companies, with Peraso contributing its innovative 60 GHz wireless semiconductor solutions and Virewirx providing its VX60 High-Performance Wireless Network.
