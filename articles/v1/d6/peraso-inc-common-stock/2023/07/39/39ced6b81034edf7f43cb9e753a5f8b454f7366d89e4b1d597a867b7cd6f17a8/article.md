# On Demand Webinar: Delivering Gigabit Fixed Wireless with 60 GHz mmWave

In this exploration of Fixed Wireless Access in the unlicensed 60 GHz band, Peraso's Mike Hamilton discusses the technology behind mmWave wireless systems, its strengths and weaknesses, and how it is being successfully deployed today to provide gigabit services.


The technology discussion will cover the engineering impact of the applicable standards, including recent updates in the spectrum allocation for the 60 GHz band. The basics of mmWave beamforming, phased array antenna technology and propagation characteristics will be discussed.


Examples of Fixed Wireless Access installations will be shared and the economic benefits of using 60 GHz will be covered in detail.
