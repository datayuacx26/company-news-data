# Mobilicom and Aitech Partner to Deliver Aerospace and Defense-Grade Solutions for Next-Generation Autonomous UAS Platforms

**March 4, 2025** – Mobilicom (NASDAQ: MOB), a leading provider of cybersecure solutions for drones and robotics, has announced a strategic partnership with Aitech Systems, a global leader in rugged computing systems for mission-critical environments.


This collaboration marks the first commercial roll-out of Mobilicom’s OS3 cybersecurity platform, integrated with Aitech’s rugged NVIDIA-based computing systems. The combined solution delivers advanced tools for manufacturers of AI-driven uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS), enabling them to detect, prevent, and respond to cyber threats in real time.


By uniting Mobilicom’s expertise in end-to-end UAS cybersecurity with Aitech’s proven rugged hardware, the partnership addresses the critical need for secure, high-performance mission computing in the rapidly growing UAS defense and aerospace markets.


> *“We believe this partnership is a significant step in advancing mission computing for next-generation uncrewed systems,”* said Oren Elkayam, CEO of Mobilicom. *“We are proud to partner with Aitech, whose solutions are already embedded within Tier-1 defense and commercial programs worldwide.”*
