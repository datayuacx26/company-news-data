# Mobilicom and Palladyne AI Partner to Deliver Cybersecure Autonomy for Drones and Robotics

Palo Alto, California & Salt Lake City, Utah – June 26, 2025 – Mobilicom, a provider of cybersecure solutions for drones and robotics, has entered into a strategic partnership with Palladyne AI, a developer of embodied neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence software. The two companies will offer a bundled solution combining Mobilicom’s OS3 cybersecurity software with Palladyne AI’s advanced autonomy platforms for drones, industrial robots, and unmanned systems.


This integrated offering pairs Palladyne Pilot, which enables persistent object detection, tracking, and classification for UAVs using multi-modal sensor fusion, with Mobilicom’s OS3, a multi-layered cybersecurity system built for autonomous missions. OS3 ensures operational integrity by providing real-time intrusion detection and prevention, automated response, and continuous resilience across complex tactical environments.


In addition to UAVs, the partnership extends to Palladyne IQ, which equips industrial robots and cobots with AI-driven, human-like reasoning to operate autonomously in dynamic settings.


> “A unified solution combining the strengths of our platforms will deliver unprecedented autonomous operations securely, protected from hostile threats—even in the most challenging tactical settings,” said Oren Elkayam, CEO and Founder of Mobilicom. “We have already identified customers interested in putting the bundled product to work.”


> “This collaboration with Mobilicom’s OS3 cybersecurity allows both of our companies to offer a highly differentiated, secure solution for AI-driven autonomous systems,” added Ben Wolff, CEO of Palladyne AI.


The partnership enables integrators and OEMs to accelerate development of autonomous capabilities while meeting demanding cybersecurity and compliance standards in real-world operations.


### **About Palladyne AI Corp.**


Palladyne AI Corp. (NASDAQ: PDYN) develops advanced AI and machine learning software that gives robots and unmanned systems human-like reasoning capabilities. Its technology enables autonomous decision-making based on real-time environmental changes.


Palladyne’s edge-based software simplifies robot deployment and programming, allowing industrial robots, cobots, and UAVs to achieve autonomy even in complex environments. With minimal training data and lower power use, its solutions apply across sectors including defense, manufacturing, logistics, and infrastructure.


By enabling smarter, faster autonomy, Palladyne AI helps machines take on tasks once too complex to automate.
