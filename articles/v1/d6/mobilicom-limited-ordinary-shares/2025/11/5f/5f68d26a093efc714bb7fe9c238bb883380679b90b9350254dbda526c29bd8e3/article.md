# MOBILICOM LAUNCHES SECURED AUTONOMY™ FRAMEWORK: A COMMON STRUCTURE FOR CYBERSECURITY IN AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS

**WASHINGTON, D.C., October 13, 2025 –** Mobilicom (NASDAQ: MOB), a leading provider of cybersecure solutions for drones and robotics, has unveiled the Secured Autonomy™ Framework, a unified cybersecurity structure for autonomous systems.
The announcement was made during the AUSA Annual Meeting & Exposition in Washington, D.C.


The Secured Autonomy Framework introduces a common industry language for describing, designing, and delivering cybersecurity across autonomous drones and robotics. It organizes protection into three core pillars—Secured Autonomous Platforms, Secured Fleets, and Secured Communications—enabling end-to-end protection from onboard mission systems to fleet coordination and external data links.


> “The Secured Autonomy Framework and accompanying technical book give the industry a clear path to design, measure, and validate cybersecurity for autonomous systems,” said Oren Elkayam, CEO and Co-Founder of Mobilicom. “It helps teams design protection that is measurable, interoperable, and adaptable to new threats and missions.”


Mobilicom presented the framework during a dedicated briefing at AUSA, accompanied by the early release of its new publication, *Secured Autonomy: A Cybersecurity Primer for Drones and Robotics* .
The book offers threat models, mitigation strategies, and practical design checklists based on over fifteen years of experience securing autonomous systems across sixteen countries.


To Mobilicom’s knowledge, it is the first comprehensive technical reference dedicated to cybersecurity in autonomous drones and robotics. The publication is available in early-release at AUSA, with ebook reservations open at[mobilicom.com/securing-autonomy-book](https://mobilicom.com/securing-autonomy-book) .


As an open industry framework, Mobilicom invites manufacturers, integrators, and standards bodies to adopt its three-pillar structure when evaluating cybersecurity requirements, designing architectures, or developing procurement criteria.


Building on Mobilicom’s participation in AUVSI’s Trusted Cyber initiative and Blue UAS certification programs, the framework aims to strengthen collaboration and support the evolution of global standards for secure autonomy.
