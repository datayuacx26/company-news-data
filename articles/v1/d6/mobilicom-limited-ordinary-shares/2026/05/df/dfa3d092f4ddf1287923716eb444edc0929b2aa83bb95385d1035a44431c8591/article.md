# Mobilicom Featured in Digital Journal: Discussing the Need for Secured Autonomy

Mobilicom is pleased to share a recent featured Q&A in *Digital Journal* exploring the critical security challenges facing today’s drone and robotics industries.


The article addresses a fundamental gap in modern defense and commercial technology: while autonomous operations are scaling at unprecedented rates, traditional cybersecurity methods are failing to keep pace with the complex, mobile risk models of cyber-physical networks.


In the interview, the discussion outlines the necessity of a practical “Secured Autonomy” framework, designed as a shared industry reference for engineers, OEMs, operators, and procurement teams to evaluate and mitigate risks across the platform, communication, and fleet layers.


The feature also highlights how Mobilicom’s practical implementation such as the OS3 cybersecure software suite and the Immunity, Cybersecurity, and Encryption (ICE) cybersecurity layer, actively addresses link resilience and operational hardening in real-world deployments.
