# Gridware named TIME’s BEST INVENTIONS 2022

Gridware’s unique power-grid monitoring technology was listed as one of 200 life-changing innovations by TIME.


‍


Nominations were solicited from all around the world, and then evaluated on a range of criteria, including originality, efficacy, ambition, and impact. From turning excess carbon into diamonds to the most powerful telescope yet, the list is as diverse as it is revolutionary. To quote TIME’s: “All these inventions are changing how we live, work, play, and think about what’s possible.”


‍
