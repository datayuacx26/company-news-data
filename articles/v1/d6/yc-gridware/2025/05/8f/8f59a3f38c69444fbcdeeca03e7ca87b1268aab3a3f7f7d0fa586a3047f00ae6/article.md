# Duquesne Light Co. 2024 Corporate Responsibility Report Features Gridware

Duquesne Light Company (https://duquesnelight.com/) is making impressive strides in modernizing its energy infrastructure, with a strong focus on leveraging innovative technology to enhance reliability and customer service. According to their recent corporate responsibility report, one notable development is the planned full-scale rollout of **Gridware** later this year.


Gridware's cutting-edge technology, which was first piloted on distribution poles, is designed to quickly detect issues, expedite repairs, and significantly improve outage response times. By integrating Gridware into their operations, DLC is demonstrating a commitment to building a smarter, more resilient grid that can better withstand the challenges of today’s energy landscape.


As this rollout progresses, Gridware is poised to become a vital component in transforming utility management and delivering reliable service to communities. We’re excited to see how this technology continues to innovate and lead the way toward a more resilient energy future.
