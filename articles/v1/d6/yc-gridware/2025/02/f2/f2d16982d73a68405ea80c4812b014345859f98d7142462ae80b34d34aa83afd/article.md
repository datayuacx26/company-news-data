# CBS News Spotlights Gridware's Mission to Protect Communities from Wildfires

At Gridware, we're on a mission to safeguard our communities from the devastating impact of wildfires. As highlighted in a recent CBS News article, our innovative technology monitors power grid systems in real time, helping to identify potential risks before they escalate into disaster.


With wildfires becoming an increasingly urgent issue, our team is dedicated to transforming how utilities manage their infrastructure. By providing actionable insights, we empower utility companies to make informed decisions that enhance safety and prevent fires from igniting. Our approach not only protects lives and property but also promotes a sustainable future for the regions we serve.
