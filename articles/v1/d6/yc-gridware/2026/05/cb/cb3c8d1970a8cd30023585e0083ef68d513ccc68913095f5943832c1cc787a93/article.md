# Powering Connections with Gridware and AFROTECH

On May 19, Gridware and[AFROTECH](https://afrotech.com/) ™ are coming together to host an evening centered on connection, culture, and opportunity.


This special gathering will bring together thoughtful professionals across technology and energy for meaningful conversations about innovation and what it takes to build a more resilient electrical grid. Joining the panel are Gridware Co-Founder and CEO Tim Barat, Senior Manager of Product Management Brittany Edwards, and Software Engineer Kevin Cook, leaders whose careers reflect the power of combining technical excellence.


The conversation will be moderated by Edith Lee, Gridware’s Senior Product Manager of Commercial, whose work focuses on translating cutting-edge technology into operational intelligence for the utility systems communities rely on every day.


Together with AFROTECH™, the premier platform connecting professionals and innovators in tech, this event is designed to build authentic relationships and connect exceptional talent with what’s next in their careers at Gridware.


[Register here](https://luma.com/zenqyff4) for the event to be held at Gridware HQ in San Francisco.


As Gridware continues scaling its mission to modernize critical infrastructure, this event reflects its broader commitment to creating access, expanding representation, and building technology that serves both people and progress.
