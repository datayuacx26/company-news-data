# Tovala Family Meals: How to Feed 4 in Under 25 Minutes

It's 6:14 pm on a Tuesday. Homework is half-done, someone's looking for a permission slip, and the dog is staring at you like he's been forgotten by society. You have, optimistically, 25 minutes before the dinner question turns into a dinner crisis.


Good news: that's all you need.


Tovala Family Meals are fresh, chef-crafted dinners that serve four people and cook together in a single tray in your Tovala Smart Oven.


## **What are Tovala Family Meals?**


Tovala Family Meals are multi-serving meals — sized for four adult servings — that arrive fresh and cook together in the Tovala Smart Oven. Each meal is built around a protein, sides, sauces, spices, and toppings, all designed to cook in one tray on a single chef-programmed cook cycle. They launched this year as a new offering, a new way to bring the convenience of classic single-serving Tovala meals to families.


Now, households with more than one person can enjoy five minutes of prep or less, scan-to-cook-ease, and hands-off, homemade cooking.


Here’s the specifics:


- **Servings:** Four adult servings.
- **Cook time:** Around 20–25 minutes in the smart oven.
- **Prep:** Five minutes of prep or less. Tray in, scan the QR code, hit start.
- **Format:** Fresh, refrigerated meals. No freezer required.
- **How to order:** Choose them when you build your weekly Tovala order. They appear in the menu the same way single-serving meals do, filtered under "Family Meals" or "Multi Serving."


## **How Tovala Family Meals work**


Here's what you can expect when you sign up.


1. **Pick your meal.** In the Tovala app, scroll the Family Meals category in any given week's menu. Add one (or two) to your order.
2. **It arrives fresh.** Family Meals ship in your weekly Tovala delivery in an insulated box. Refrigerate when it arrives.
3. **On dinner night, scan and start.** Slide the tray into the Smart Oven, scan the QR code on the sleeve, and hit start. The oven loads the chef-programmed cook cycle for that specific meal — bake, broil, steam, toast, and reheat modes are toggled automatically.
4. **Cook is hands-off.** No flipping, no rotating, no babysitting. The Smart Oven handles the entire cycle.
5. **Plate and serve.** When the oven beeps, dinner's done — for four.


Total active cooking time on your part: roughly five minutes. The Smart Oven does the rest.

## **Tovala Family Meals FAQ**


**Does Tovala serve 4 people?** Yes. Tovala Family Meals are multi-serving dinners portioned for four people in a single tray. They run on the same weekly menu as Tovala's single-serving meals — choose them based on how you want to eat each week.


**How long do Tovala Family Meals take to cook?** About 20–25 minutes in the Tovala Smart Oven, fully hands-off. There's no flipping, rotating, or babysitting required — the smart oven runs the chef-programmed cook cycle automatically once you scan the QR code on the sleeve.


**Are Tovala Family Meals fresh or frozen?** Fresh. Family Meals ship refrigerated in your weekly Tovala delivery and are designed to be cooked within a few days of arrival.


**Do I need a Tovala Smart Oven to cook Family Meals?** Yes. Family Meals are designed for the Tovala Smart Oven, which runs the chef-programmed cook cycle automatically when you scan the meal. The Smart Oven also doubles as a standard countertop oven for everything else you cook.


**How are Tovala Family Meals different from single-serving Tovala meals?** Same fresh, chef-crafted format and same simple prep — Family Meals are simply portioned for four people in one tray instead of one person in a single-serve tray. Both are available on weekly menus, so you can mix and match meals that fit your schedule.


**How do Tovala Family Meals compare to HelloFresh?** HelloFresh is a traditional meal kit — fresh ingredients plus a recipe card that you cook from scratch, typically 30–45 minutes of active cooking. Tovala Family Meals are fully prepped and oven-ready, with about five minutes of prep and zero active cooking. HelloFresh is the better fit if you want to cook; Tovala Family Meals are the better fit if you want dinner done.


**What if everyone's home at different times?** Cook when most people are home, then portion for the latecomer to reheat. Tovala reheats Family Meal leftovers cleanly on the bake or reheat setting.


**Can I order both Family Meals and single-serving meals in the same week?** Yes. Tovala's weekly menu includes both. You can build a week with, for example, two Family Meals for family dinner nights and three single-serving meals for solo lunches — all in one order.


**How far in advance can I order a Family Meal?** Family Meals are part of your weekly Tovala menu. Pick them when you build your order, and they arrive fresh in your next delivery.
