# How to Check Your Prepaid Meter Balance in Nigeria

Have you ever been in the middle of watching your favourite TV show, working from home, or preparing dinner when your electricity suddenly goes off?


You rush to your prepaid meter only to discover you've run out of electricity units.


It's a situation many Nigerians can relate to.


The good news is that checking your prepaid meter balance is quick and easy. By monitoring your remaining units regularly, you can plan your next recharge and avoid unexpected blackouts.


## What Is a Prepaid Meter Balance?


Your prepaid meter balance is the number of electricity units (measured in kilowatt-hours or kWh) remaining on your meter.


Every time you use electricity, your meter deducts units based on your energy consumption.


Once your balance reaches zero, your electricity supply will automatically disconnect until you purchase and load another token.


# Why You Should Check Your Meter Balance Regularly


Many people only check their meter after the lights go off. Instead, make it a habit to monitor your balance every few days.


Regular checks help you:


### Avoid Unexpected Power Interruptions


You'll know when it's time to recharge before your units run out.


### Manage Your Monthly Budget


Tracking your usage helps you estimate how much electricity you consume each month.


### Detect High Energy Consumption


If your units finish faster than usual, it could indicate increased electricity usage or inefficient appliances.


# Ways to Check Your Prepaid Meter Balance


The exact method depends on your meter manufacturer.


Here are the most common ways.


## Method 1: Check Using Your Meter Keypad


Most prepaid meters have a keypad that allows you to check your remaining balance.


Simply:


1. Wake up the meter if the screen is blank.
2. Enter the balance code recommended for your meter.
3. Press the Enter or OK button.


Your remaining units should appear on the screen.


> **Note:** Meter codes differ by manufacturer. Always refer to your meter's user manual or contact your electricity distribution company (DisCo) if you're unsure of the correct code.


## Method 2: Check the Meter Display


Some modern prepaid meters automatically display important information in rotation, including:


- Remaining units
- Meter number
- Current consumption
- Voltage
- Load information


Simply wait a few seconds for the display to cycle through the available information.


## Method 3: Use Your DisCo's Customer Portal


Some electricity distribution companies provide online portals where customers can:


- Check account information
- View payment history
- Monitor electricity consumption
- Access customer support


Availability depends on your DisCo and meter type.


## Method 4: Check Through Your Smart Meter App


If you're using a smart prepaid meter, you may be able to monitor your electricity usage through the manufacturer's mobile application.


Depending on your meter, the app may display:


- Real-time balance
- Daily usage
- Monthly consumption
- Recharge history


# Common Prepaid Meter Brands in Nigeria


Nigeria has several prepaid meter manufacturers.


Some common brands include:


- Hexing
- Mojec
- Conlog
- Longi
- Holley
- EDMI


Although the process is generally similar, the specific balance codes may vary.


# What to Do If Your Meter Isn't Displaying Your Balance


Sometimes your meter may fail to display your remaining units.


Possible reasons include:


### Low Battery (for CIU Units)


Some Customer Interface Units (CIUs) use batteries. Replace the batteries if the screen won't turn on.


### Faulty Display


If the screen is damaged or blank, contact your DisCo immediately.


### Meter Communication Issues


Some split prepaid meters may temporarily lose communication between the interface unit and the meter.


Restart the interface unit and try again.


### Meter Fault


If the issue persists, report it to your electricity distribution company for inspection.
