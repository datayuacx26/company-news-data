# How to Spot a Faulty Prepaid Meter Before It Fails

Prepaid meters are a lifesaver for controlling electricity expenses, but a faulty meter can cause unexpected power cuts and financial losses. Learning how to detect early signs of a malfunction can save you time, money, and hassle.


### **Signs Your Prepaid Meter May Be Faulty**


- **Unusual Error Codes:** If your meter frequently displays error messages like “30,” “70,” or “Error,” it may be signalling an internal fault.
- **Token Rejection:** A healthy meter should accept valid tokens easily. If your token fails repeatedly, your meter may need attention.
- **Power Fluctuation Despite Adequate Units:** If your lights flicker or go off even when you have enough units, the meter’s relay might be faulty.
- **Display Screen Issues:** A blank, frozen, or flickering screen is a red flag.
- **Burn Marks or Strange Odours:** Physical signs of damage indicate serious internal issues.


### **What to Do If You Notice These Signs**


- Contact your electricity distribution company (DISCO) immediately.
- Report the issue with your meter serial number and error code.
- Avoid tampering with the meter yourself.


### **Conclusion**


Regularly checking your meter and understanding these warning signs will help you avoid unexpected disconnections and costly repairs.
