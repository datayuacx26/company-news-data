# What Happens to Your Electricity When You Travel? Should You Turn Off Your Meter?

Travelling soon? Many Nigerians wonder if they should switch off their prepaid meters when away from home. Let’s break down what happens to your electricity supply and how you can manage it smartly.


### **Does a Prepaid Meter Consume Units When Appliances Are Off?**


- **No Active Appliances = No Consumption:** If all appliances are switched off, your prepaid meter will not deduct units.
- **Standby Power Drain:** Some appliances (like refrigerators, decoders) consume small amounts of power even when off.


### **Should You Turn Off Your Meter When Travelling?**


- **Switching Off at the Source is Safer:** It’s advisable to switch off your main power supply, especially if travelling for a long time.
- **Meter Tampering Warning:** Never tamper with the prepaid meter itself.
- **Consider Surge Protection:** Installing surge protectors can prevent power surges while you're away.


### **Conclusion**


Ensure all appliances are unplugged or switched off, and if possible, switch off your main power source. This protects your home and prevents unexpected power usage.
