# Best Times to Buy Electricity in Nigeria to Avoid Outages

Buying electricity is often seamless until you face network issues or system outages. Knowing the best times to make your purchase can save you stress.


### **When Should You Buy?**


- **Early Mornings (6 am – 9 am):** Lower transaction volumes mean faster processing.
- **Mid-Week (Tuesday – Thursday):** Banks and payment processors experience less congestion compared to weekends or Mondays.
- **Avoid Month-End Rush:** Salaries and bulk payments often clog systems towards the end of the month.
- **Before Public Holidays:** Always buy ahead of holidays to avoid peak loads and downtime.
