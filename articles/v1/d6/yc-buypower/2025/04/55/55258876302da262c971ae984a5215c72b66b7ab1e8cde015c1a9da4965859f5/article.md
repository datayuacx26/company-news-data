# How This BuyPower Merchant Made Over ₦60,000 Without Selling a Single Unit of Electricity

##


Imagine earning over ₦60,000 in one month without making a single sale. It sounds too good to be true, right? Well, it is possible on the **BuyPower Merchant App.**


One of our savvy merchants did just that in **March** , and here’s exactly how he pulled it off.


### **The Secret? Referrals.**


Our **Merchant Referral Program** allows you to **earn passively** by referring new merchants to join the BuyPower platform. For every electricity transaction your referral makes, you earn **0.1% of the transaction value** and trust us, it adds up fast.


This smart merchant referred multiple others, and by the end of the month, his referral network had done enough business to earn him **₦60,000 in commission** all while they kicked back and watched it grow.


### **Here’s How It Works**


**Step 1** : Open Your Merchant App, navigate to your profile and tap on Refer and Earn.


**Step 2:** Share Your Code: Follow the on-screen instructions and **share your unique referral code** with friends, family, or your network who want to become merchants.


**Step 3:** Follow up: Make sure your referrals **use your code** when they fill out the merchant application form. Stay in touch and help them complete registration if needed.


### **How to Track Your Referrals**


You can monitor everything from the **“My Referrals”** tab in your app:


- **Pending:** The person you referred hasn't filled out the application form with your code.
- **Inactive:** They have signed up(filled out the application form), but they haven't transacted yet.
- **Active:** They've completed their first transaction; this is when you start earning.


**Payouts happen on the 1st of every month** , so you get your commissions right on time.
