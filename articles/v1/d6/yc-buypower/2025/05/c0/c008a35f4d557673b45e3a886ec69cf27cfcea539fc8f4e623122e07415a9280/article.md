## **What is a Virtual Account?**


A **BuyPower Virtual Account** is a unique NUBAN bank account linked to a specific electricity meter. When your customers transfer money into this account to pay for electricity, their token is delivered automatically via SMS, and **you earn a commission** for every successful transaction.


You don’t need to be online, make a call, or type anything. Just set it up once and start **making money in your sleep** .

**How to Create Your Virtual Account (Step-by-Step)**


**Step 1: Log in to your BuyPower Merchant Dashboard**
Download the BuyPower Merchant App on the[Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ng.buypower.merchant&hl=en) or[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/buypower-merchant/id6502284876) , or visit[https://buypower.ng/merchant](https://buypower.ng/merchant) and sign in using your merchant account credentials.


**Step 2: Click on the “Virtual Account” Tab**
You'll find this on your merchant dashboard menu.


**Step 3: Enter Required Information**
You’ll need to provide the following:


- Meter Number (of the customer or business location)
- Phone Number (where tokens should be sent)
- DISCO (Distribution Company, e.g., AEDC, PHEDC, etc.)


**Step 4: Submit to Generate Account Details**
Once completed, a unique **BuyPower MFB Virtual Account** will be generated for that meter.


**Step 5: Share the Virtual Account with Customers**
Give this account number to your customers. When they transfer money to it, they’ll get their electricity token instantly, and you get your **commission** right after the transaction is completed.


## **How You Make Money**


Every time a customer makes a payment into your virtual account, **BuyPower processes the transaction** , sends the token to the customer, and **credits your wallet with your commission** .


Your earnings depend on the **volume of purchases** , so the more customers use your virtual account, the more you earn, without being actively involved.
