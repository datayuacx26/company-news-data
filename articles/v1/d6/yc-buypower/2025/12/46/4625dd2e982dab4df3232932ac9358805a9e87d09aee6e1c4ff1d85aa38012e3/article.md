## **The Problem with Traditional Corporate Gifts**


1: **Money Spent, Impact Lost:** Let's be honest about traditional corporate gifting in Nigeria:


**2: Gift Baskets:** Beautiful presentation, forgotten within days. Half the items go unused, and the basket ends up in storage.


**3: Branded Merchandise:** Employees smile politely at the branded mug, notebook, or T-shirt, then rarely use them. Your brand visibility? Minimal.


**4: Generic Gift Cards:** Limited merchant options, expiry dates, and the hassle of physically going somewhere to redeem them reduce their appeal.


**5: Cash Bonuses:** While always appreciated, cash disappears into general expenses without creating a memorable association with your company's generosity.


**6: Holiday Hampers:** Expensive to procure and distribute, with varied reception depending on individual preferences and dietary needs.


The challenge isn't the gesture; employees appreciate being remembered. **The challenge is creating a meaningful impact that aligns with real needs.**

Actually, the opposite is true. Employees appreciate that you understand their real challenges. The personalisation comes from the relevance, not the wrapping paper. Pair it with a thoughtful message about why you chose this gift, and it becomes deeply personal.


**"What about employees who don't pay their own electricity bills?"**


Even employees living in company housing or with family have friends, relatives, or other members they support. The flexibility of electricity tokens means the gift always finds value.


**"Is this really memorable enough for special occasions?"**


Ask yourself: Would your employee rather receive another branded mug or have their December electricity bill covered? Memorable doesn't mean elaborate—it means meaningful. And few things are more meaningful than genuine financial relief.
