# PSI Rings the Bell at NASDAQ

Share


PSI executives were on hand Feb. 24 to ring the bell at the opening of the day’s trading at the NASDAQ exchange in New York City. Our company also featured on NASDAQ’s tower digital billboard in Times Square.


PSI stock has been listed on the iconic stock exchange since late 2024 under the ticker symbol “PSIX.”
