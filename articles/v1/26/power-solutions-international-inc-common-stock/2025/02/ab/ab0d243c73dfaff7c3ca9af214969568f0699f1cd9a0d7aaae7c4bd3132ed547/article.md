# PSI Returns to PowerGen show

Share


PSI representatives attended the annual PowerGen convention Feb. 11 through 13 in Dallas. We displayed our 13-liter propane engine in conjunction with[Propane Education & Research Council](https://www.linkedin.com/company/propane-education-research-council/) and caught up with existing and prospective clients. We look forward to new relationships and projects resulting from the show.
