# PSI Featured During TCI Expo 2024

Share


Several PSI products were featured during TCI Expo 2024 last month in Baltimore, Md.


OEMs spotlighting PSI engines in their products included Dyna Products, who featured a woodchopper powered by our 8.8-liter industrial engine, the most powerful off-road spark ignited engine in the market available today.


PSI’s patented 30kw lithium-ion all electric power unit was displayed by Morbark, who also featured its BVR 10 Model woodchipper, powered by PSI’s 2.4-liter gasoline engine.


Bandit showcased two woodchippers powered by PSI engines: its new 12XC woodchipper, which features the PSI 2.4-liter gasoline industrial engine; and the 12XP, which uses PSI’s 4.3-liter gasoline engine.


Hosted by the Tree Care Industry Association, the TCI Show features more than 250+ exhibitors.
