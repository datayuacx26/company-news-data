# PSI Celebrates Manufacturing Week

Share


PSI celebrated National Manufacturing Week, Oct. 4 through 11, in style thanks to our Human Resources team. The HR team organized cookouts in Wood Dale and Darien, Wis., gave out candy apples in each building, and distributed pink t-shirts to employees to commemorate Breast Cancer Awareness Month.
