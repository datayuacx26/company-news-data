# PSI Products Featured During ACT Expo 2024

Share


PSI representatives attended the Advanced Clean Transportation Expo in Las Vegas May 20 to 23. Several of the vehicle manufacturers attending the show displayed PSI engines in their products including MAFI Terminal Tractors, Mack Trucks, Momentum, Capacity of Texas, Mullen Power Up, Pioneer, Taylor Machine Works and Enviro Charge. Capacity also featured PSI’s 8.8L Propane engine.


The show spotlights efforts in the transportation sector to reduce emissions while driving economic sustainability.
