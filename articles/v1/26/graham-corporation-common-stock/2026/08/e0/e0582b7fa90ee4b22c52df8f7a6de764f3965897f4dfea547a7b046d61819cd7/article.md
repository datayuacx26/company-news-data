# Graham Corporation Awarded Over $43 Million in Defense Industry Contracts for Mission Critical Submarine and Turbomachinery Products

# Graham Corporation Awarded Over $43 Million in Defense Industry Contracts for Mission Critical Submarine and Turbomachinery Products


August 05, 2026 4:30pm EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_18cd8bc9924e07aef72e17d36f43f55d/grahamcorp/news/2026-08-05_Graham_Corporation_Awarded_Over_43_Million_in_407.pdf)


- *Awarded option year for alternators and regulators to support the MK48 Mod 7 Heavyweight Torpedo program*
- *Secured new contract to provide MK19 Mod 2 Air Turbine Pump assemblies in support of the submarine fleet spares*


BATAVIA, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Graham Corporation (NYSE: GHM), a global leader in the design and manufacture of mission critical fluid, power, heat transfer, vacuum, and advanced mixing technologies for the defense, space, energy, and process industries, today announced that it was awarded two contracts for a combined value of over $43 million.


The first contract awarded was a follow-on fourth option year supporting the MK48 Mod 7 Heavyweight Torpedo program, awarded in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended June 30, 2026. The Company will continue to provide alternators and regulators under this option year.


The Company was also awarded a contract in the second quarter of fiscal 2027, ending September 30, 2026, to provide new-build MK19 Mod 2 air turbine pump assemblies in support of the submarine fleet spares. Deliveries under the MK19 contract are expected to begin in 2028 and continue through 2030.


Matthew Malone, President and Chief Executive Officer, Graham Corporation, commented, “These awards reflect the continued demand we’re seeing across our defense platforms, and demonstrate the durability of our customer relationships built over years of consistent execution. Our mission-critical applications remain vital in helping our customers meet high-level performance requirements. We're proud to continue supporting the MK48 and submarine programs and look forward to building on this momentum."


The revenue for the contracts to support the MK48 Mod 7 Heavyweight Torpedo program and the MK19 Mod 2 Air Turbine Pump Assemblies will be reflected in the Company’s first and second fiscal year 2027 backlog.
