BATAVIA, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Graham Corporation](https://url.avanan.click/v2/___https:/grahamcorp.com/___.YXAzOmtlaWFkdmlzb3JzOmE6bzpiODcyOTg2NThhNjM2MWY1M2Y0NWJmOTllY2Y4OGYxZjo2OjlhYzQ6NWE4ZWIxY2E5MzdjNjlmZGQzZjk2NjI0YmU3MWRhMzZhYWE4NzMyZDY3Zjc3MWM2YTIxYjc0MDk2OWIxYzg3NzpwOlQ6Rg) (NYSE: GHM) (“GHM” or the “Company”), a global leader in the design and manufacture of mission critical fluid, power, heat transfer and vacuum technologies for the Defense, Energy & Process, and Space industries, today announced that Matthew J. Malone, President and Chief Executive Officer, and Christopher J. Thome, Vice President - Finance and CFO, will present at the TD Cowen 47th Annual Aerospace & Defense Conference in Arlington, VA on Thursday, February 12, 2026 at 2:05 pm EST, as well as host investor meetings.


A live webcast of the presentation can be accessed by registering for the event[HERE](https://event.summitcast.com/view/RotEFzzzPbFfY9GhHYv4f7/guest_book?session_id=gjurHiPCpZSsmEyrgLYp4u) , or by going to the Events & Presentation section of the Company's investor relations website at[https://ir.grahamcorp.com/news-events/events-presentations](https://ir.grahamcorp.com/news-events/events-presentations) .


Interested investors should contact their sales representative to register and schedule one-on-one or group meetings.


**About Graham Corporation**


Graham is a global leader in the design and manufacture of mission critical fluid, power, heat transfer, vacuum, and advanced mixing technologies for the Defense, Energy & Process, and Space industries. Graham Corporation and its family of global brands are built upon world-renowned engineering expertise, proprietary technologies, as well as its responsive and flexible service and the unsurpassed quality customers have come to expect from the Company’s products and systems.
