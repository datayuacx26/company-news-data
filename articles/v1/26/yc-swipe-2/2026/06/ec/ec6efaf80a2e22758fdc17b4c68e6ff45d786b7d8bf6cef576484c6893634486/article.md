# Agenforce Hits a Billion

Salesforce Q1 revenue: $11.12 billion. Up 13% year over year.


Benioff says agentic AI is the growth engine. Agentforce alone passed $1 billion in annual run rate.


Half of those bookings came from existing customers expanding.


So the people who were already buying are buying more. That tracks.


The U.S. Air Force signed a $72 million deal. Chobani joined. Louis Vuitton too.


I’m not going to pretend I know what Agentforce actually does at this scale. But $1 billion is a number that means something.
