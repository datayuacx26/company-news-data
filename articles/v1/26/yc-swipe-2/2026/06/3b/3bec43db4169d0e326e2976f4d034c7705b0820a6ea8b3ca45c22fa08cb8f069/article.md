# E-Way Bill vs E-Invoice: Key Differences, Applicability & Examples

# **E-Way Bill vs E-Invoice: Key Differences, Applicability & Examples**


> Every growing business shares one common goal, keeping operations smooth while staying compliant with GST regulations. In the world of GST, understanding E-invoices and E-Way Bills is an important part of achieving that balance. When it comes to GST compliance, E-invoices and E-Way Bills are two important requirements businesses should understand. This often creates a dilemma for many businesses. Both of these documents are used during business transactions for GST compliance, and in some cases, a business may even need both for the same sale.


Also, many people think both documents serve the same purpose, but they are used for quite different purposes. An E-Invoice helps businesses digitally authenticate invoice details under GST, while an E-way Bill helps for tracking purposes when goods are being transported from one location to another.


Imagine, a business selling goods to a customer in another state, the business may need to generate an e-invoice for the transaction and an E-Way Bill before transporting the goods. Missing either document can cause compliance issues, delays, or even heavy penalties which every business should understand.


To make things easier, we will explain E-Way Bill vs E-Invoice, their key differences, applicability, & real-life examples without any technical jargon.


## **What is an E-Invoice**


An[E-Invoice](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/e-invoice-gst-instruments-guide) is a digital invoice system introduced under GST to make business transactions more transparent and easier to track. This does not mean businesses have to create invoices from scratch. Instead, businesses can continue to use their existing billing or accounting software and upload invoice details to the Invoice Registration Portal, or IRP. The portal then verifies the details and generates an IRN and QR code. This confirms that the invoice is valid under GST.


The main purpose of an E-Invoice is to reduce manual errors, improve GST reporting, and create a standard process for invoice verification.


‍ **Key Features of E-Invoice:**


- Invoice details are registered electronically
- Generates a unique IRN number
- Includes a QR code for verification
- Help reduce mistakes in invoice reporting
- Making GST compliance easier for businesses


## **What is an E-Way Bill**


An[E-Way Bill](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/new-gst-e-way-bill-rules-2026-mandatory-ship-to-gstin-in-bill-to-ship-to-transactions) is a digital document required for the movement of goods from one location to another under GST. It mentions goods, supplier, recipient, transporter, and vehicle used for transportation.


The main purpose of an E-Way Bill is to keep a record of the movement of goods and ensure GST rules are being followed during transportation.


Businesses and transporters must generate an E-Way Bill before goods movement when the value of the consignment exceeds the prescribed limit.


- Key Features of E-Way Bill:
- Track the movement of goods
- Required during transportation
- Contains vehicle and transporter details
- Helps GST authorities verify shipments
- Reduces paperwork during transit

## **Difference Between E-Invoice and E-Way Bill with Example**


Let us understand the difference more clearly.


Assume that a business in Delhi sells machinery of ₹5 lakh to a customer in Punjab.


### **Step 1: Generate E-Invoice**


The seller prepares the GST invoice and uploads the invoice details on the IRP portal. After checking the details, the system generates an IRN and QR code.


This confirms that the invoice has been successfully registered under GST.


### **Step 2: Generate E-Way Bill**


As the machinery is being shipped from Delhi to Punjab. A business has to[create an E-Way Bill](https://getswipe.in/ewaybill) before the transportation begins.


This helps track the movement of goods and ensures they can be transported as per GST rules.


In this case:


- E-invoice to prove the transaction
- E-Way Bill allows legal movement of goods


## **Applicability of E-Invoice**


E-Invoice applicability depends on the turnover limit and business category notified by the government.


Generally, registered businesses crossing the specified turnover limit must generate e-invoices for their B2B transactions, exports, and other applicable supplies.


Businesses should regularly check the latest updates on the official[GST portal](https://www.gst.gov.in/help/returns) for applicable turnover limits and exemptions.


## **Applicability of E-Way Bill**


An E-Way Bill is mostly required for the movement of goods when their value crosses the prescribed set value.


It may be required for:


- Interstate movement of goods
- Intrastate movement as per state rules
- Supply of goods
- Goods sent for reasons other than supply
- Returns of goods


An E-Way Bill is usually required before the transportation of goods begins.

## **FAQs**


### **1. Is E-Invoice the same as E-Way Bill?**


No, an E-Invoice validates a business transaction under GST, however an E-Way Bill helps track the movement of goods.


### **2. Is E-Way Bill required after generating an E-Invoice?**


Yes, if the goods are being transported and the conditions for E-Way Bill apply, businesses must generate it separately.


### **3. Who needs to generate an e-invoice?**


Businesses covered under GST e-invoice rules need to generate E-invoices for applicable transactions.


### **4. Who can generate an E-Way Bill?**


The supplier, recipient, or transporter can generate an E-Way Bill depending on the transaction.


### **5. What happens if goods are moved without an E-Way Bill?**


Failure to generate the required E-Way Bill can result in penalties and may also result in goods also being stopped during transportation.


### **6. Can E-Invoice and E-Way Bill be generated together?**


Indeed, many businesses generate both documents together through integrated billing and accounting systems.
