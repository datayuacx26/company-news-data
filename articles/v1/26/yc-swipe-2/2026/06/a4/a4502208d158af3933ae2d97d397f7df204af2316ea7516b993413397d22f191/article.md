# E-Way Bill Invalid Without Part B of Form GST EWB-01

# **E-Way Bill Invalid Without Part B of Form GST EWB-01**


The e-way bill system is an essential component of compliance under the GST law that enables tracking the movement of goods by tax authorities, thereby preventing tax evasion. Many taxpayers and transporters ignore an important requirement which is completing Part B of Form GST EWB-01. When an e-way bill is generated without including Part B in many cases it might not be valid and can result in penalties, detention of goods and compliance liability.


## **What is an E-Way Bill**


Goods transportation that exceeds the prescribed limit under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) legal requirement is required to utilize an electronic document known as an E-Way Bill ([EWB](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/gst-e-way-bill-guide-for-transporters-compliance) ). The EWB is generated and stored on a GST E-Way Bill Portal (site) prior to transporting goods and is an important compliance tool within the GST system. The details included in the EWB will be:


- The party sending the goods (the supplier)
- The party receiving the goods (the recipient)
- A description of the goods included in the shipment
- The name of the company that is transporting the goods (common carrier)
- Vehicle Registration Number used to transport the goods


EWB supports transparency in the movement of goods throughout India and enables the tax authorities to track shipments for purposes of preventing tax evasion and assisting with streamlining logistics. The use of an electronic record (EWB) to track/transit the movement of goods promotes accountability during the cycle of an individual shipment, thus increasing the overall compliance with GST.


## **Understanding Form GST EWB-01**


GST EWB-01 will be used for generating e-way bills under GST;[GSTR 1](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/gstr-1-filing-2026) will help determine the delivery location of the goods supplied. It will consist of two parts: Part A and Part B.


### **Part A**


Details about the consignment being shipped as well as the parties involved:


- Supplier's and recipient's GSTIN
- Invoice/Bill/Document number
- Value of goods supplied
- HSN code
- Reason for transporting the goods
- Delivery location


This information will allow the Government to determine the nature of your transaction and verify the goods being shipped. Part A will typically be generated first and provides the basis for generating the e-way bill


### **Part B**


Transportation-related information:


- Vehicle number
- Transport document number
- Transporter
- Mode of transport


Part B is just as important; it allows the Government to verify the vehicle transporting the goods, therefore determining if the vehicle transporting the goods is legally transporting goods at the time of shipment, and tracking the location of the goods while they are being shipped.


## **Why is Part B Important**


Transportation details that are necessary to verify how and where the goods are being transported are provided in Part B of the e-way bill. The information contained in[Part B](https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/version1.03/vehicle-number-update.html) is also required so that authorities will be able to determine whether or not the goods were transported during transit. The main goals of Part B include:


- To track the goods during transport
- To prevent the unauthorized transportation of goods
- To minimize the chance of tax fraud
- To make it easier for the authorities to inspect the goods during transit


If the e-way bill is not completed properly, it is possible that an incomplete e-way bill will not comply with the legal requirements as established in the law.


### **When an e-way bill without Part B is considered invalid**


Generally, an e-way bill becomes invalid when the transporter fails to provide vehicle details in Part B when transporting the goods by road. There will be exceptions to the above rule and an e-way bill will not be treated as a valid e-way bill if:


- Goods are transported on a public highway.
- Transportation must begin before updating Part B.
- No vehicle number or wrong vehicle number is entered on the e-way bill.
- Transporter name is incorrect or not provided on the e-way bill.


If the e-way bill does not contain the information listed above, the e-way bill will not be a valid document for the transportation of goods.


## **Exceptions where Part B may not be required**


In some scenarios, Part B of a transport document may not need to be filled out immediately.


- **Transportation Within a Short Distance**


When moving products a short distance for an operational reason, the GST Act allows for vehicle information to be handled differently.


- **Using Different Means of Transport and Updating Transport Documents**


For transactions utilizing different means of transport (railway, air, vessel), there are variances in how updates will take place.


- **Moving Goods Before Vehicle Registration**


While a Part A may be generated, Part B must exist before any goods can be moved from the place of origin. Taxpayers should refer to the latest GST regulations that are relevant to their particular transaction.


- **Penalties related to transporting goods without filling Part B**


If Part B is not completed, it can lead to significant non-compliance issues.


- **Detention of Goods and Vehicles**


Tax authorities are able to seize/hold goods that are being moved without a valid e-way bill.


- **Monetary Penalties**


Companies can be[penalized](https://portal.ct.gov/dol/knowledge-base/articles/ui-overpayments/what-is-a-monetary-penalty) under the GST for transporting goods without proper documentation.


- **Delayed Delivery**


Goods held up for inspection may result in delays and/or dissatisfied customers with respect to supply chain operations.


- **Higher Scrutiny**


Repeated violations may lead to increased scrutiny from GST Authorities, increased exposure to non compliance risk.


## **Common Mistakes Related to Part B**


Common e-way bill errors committed by companies when creating or modifying e-way bill records are:


- Forgetting to edit vehicle information before shipping.
- Submitting (partial) vehicle numbers incorrectly or incompletely.
- Utilizing (incorrect or out of date) shipping company data.
- Completing only part A of the e-way bill, while ignoring part B.
- Failing to complete part B in addition to using an alternative vehicle during transport.
- Using transportation methods in part B that differ from actual mode of transport.
- Failing to complete part B in a timely manner resulting in non-compliance at time of inspection.


Even small errors in part B could cause an e-way bill to be treated as invalid, resulting in detention of goods, fines, or delivery delays. By avoiding above-mentioned errors, and continuously confirming transportation data, organizations can remain GST compliant, facilitate effective logistics operation, and prevent discontinuity in business operations.

## **How to Update Part B of Form GST EWB-01**


Updating Part B is a simple process:


1. Log into the e-way bill portal;
2. Select "Update Vehicle Number";
3. Input E-Way Bill number;
4. Input Correct vehicle details;
5. Then submit and save changes.


Make sure to always verify these details before starting transport.


## **Conclusion**


Part B of the e-way bill (EWB) system is a very important part of this process. If a vehicle's details are needed as per e-way bill, e-way bill generated without tag B may be invalid as per GST Rules. This could lead to detention of goods, Penalty imposed on GST taxpayers, delays in Delivery and increased compliance checks. Maintaining compliance with e-way bill requirements is key to preventing GST penalties and enables efficient logistics businesses to run smoothly and efficiently.

## **FAQs**


### After forming part A, can part B be revised?


Yes, part A is developed first, but must be sufficiently revised before the actual transporting of goods takes place.


### If goods are transported without part B what can happen?


Authorities could potentially detain the goods and the vehicle and apply penalties in the context of GST.


### How do I change part B in an e-way bill?


By logging into the e-way bill portal, selecting vehicle change and inputting the e-way bill number along with the correct transportation details.


### Can you change truck details during transport?


Yes, you must update part B on your e-way bill if there was a change to the vehicle during transport to comply with regulations.


### Why is part B important in the context of GST?


Part B provides authorities with information about the goods being transferred in-transit the ability to validate transportation details and to prevent tax evasion, thus it is critical to comply with regulations.
