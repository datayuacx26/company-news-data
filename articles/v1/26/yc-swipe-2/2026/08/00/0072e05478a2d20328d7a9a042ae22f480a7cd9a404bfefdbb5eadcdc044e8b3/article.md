# With Swipe, GST Billing Is Extremely Easy | Customer Testimonial by Paint Emporium


## Meet Sudipto & Paint Emporium


> Paint Emporium is a paint, hardware, and decorating commodities business in Kolkata serving its clients on contractors, painters, and homeowners. Under Sudipto Saha's leadership, Paint Emporium had been developing slowly yet surely; nonetheless, with the arrival of GST-type billing, something difficult happened.


## The Challenge: GST Billing Hassles


Sudipto went on to present the following:


1. **Multiple steps** : First, create the invoice, then go to the GST portal to generate the required e-invoice and e-way bill, and every single activity would require separate login credentials or manual keying of data.


2. **Time Wastage:** Even for the simplest of transactions, sales and billing could take minutes, thus holding up the waiters at the counter and causing inefficiency in respect of time.


3. **Where There Were Chances Of Errors:** Manual entry of data in the portal necessarily created opportunities of errors-different data getting entered or repeated, or missing. This could lead to noncompliance and potential penalties.


## How Swipe Changed Billing and Compliance


Sudipto explains, thus, the truly seamless billing and GST compliance process that Sweep has brought out:


1. **One-Click GST & E-Way bills:** Integrated into the GST module, Swipe could now generate invoices and accompanying GST-accepted e-documents with the click of a single button, thereby avoiding the back and forth of documents with the GST portal.


2. **Zero Technical Learning Curve:** "With Swipe, GST billing is extremely easy," he said. And yet, Sudipto appreciated how the interface made billing very easy despite the new regulations.


3. **Faster Checkouts:** Faster billing means shorter queues. The good thing that Sudipto has observed is that the experience of customers at the billing counter has improved a lot.


4. **Innate Accuracy & Compliance:** For all invoices and e-way bills generated through Swipe, there can be no wrong HSN/SAC codes nor mismatched GST details, thereby greatly reducing any audit risk.


5. **Mobile Billing Freedom:** From instant sales floor billing now, Sudipto does not have to go behind the counter or log onto the computers-anything can be done on mobile.


## The Impact: Smooth Billing, Happy Customers, Peace of Mind


Post-integration with Swipe, Sudipto had the following to report:


1. Reduction of billing time was affected, as with just a click, GSTs and e-way bills would get generated.


2. Higher customer satisfaction has been attained using fast checkout.


3. The assurance of errorless digital records.
