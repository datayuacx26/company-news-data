# Google Play Accelerator India 2026: Swipe selected among 20 AI-powered startups


> Google has announced the 2026 cohort of[Google Play Accelerator India](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/announcing-the-2026-cohort-of-google-play-accelerator-india/) , selecting 20 AI-powered startups across sectors including finance, education, healthcare, gaming, agriculture, ecommerce, productivity and entertainment. Swipe, an Indian GST billing and business-management platform, is among the selected companies.


The selection gives Swipe access to a three-month, equity-free program offering hands-on support in responsible AI integration, app quality, security, growth and monetisation. For Swipe and the businesses using its platform, the program represents an opportunity to strengthen the app’s performance and future capabilities.


## What is Google Play Accelerator India?


Google Play Accelerator India is a three-month, equity-free program that provides selected app and game startups with mentorship, technical audits and product-development support from Google specialists.


Startups walk away with support across a handful of areas: AI and Gemini integration, Android app performance, product strategy, security and privacy, user experience improvements, Play Store optimization, and business growth and monetisation.


The goal isn't just polish for polish's sake. Google wants these companies ready to scale, whether that's across India or into international markets down the line.

## FAQs


### What is the Google Play Accelerator India?


It is an equity-free program focused on mentoring, technical help, AI insights, and growth strategy for selected Android startups from India that takes place over the time of three months.


### Why was Swipe selected in the 2026 cohort?


Swipe has been recognized as one of the 20 selected startups for the 2026 cohort as it is known to be a developer of innovative Android solutions, particularly GST billing and business management applications.


### What services are offered by Swipe?


A fintech app created by Swipe serves business owners to manage GST invoices, payments, tracking of expenses, as well as preparing quotations and other financial activities required for daily operations.


### Does Google take equity in accelerator startups?


No. Google is equity-free in this case as it has more focus on mentorship and technical support rather than investing in startups.
