# E-invoice business continuity plan: What to do when an IRP is unavailable

# **E-invoice business continuity plan: What to do when an IRP is unavailable**


> Real-time invoice registration is essential under India's digital tax structure for eligible taxpayers. Creation of an[Invoice Reference Number (IRN)](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/faqs-e-invoice-irn-system-gst-india) via the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) confirms that your B2B sales and e-way bills are valid. But what do you do when technical issues come into play?


Portal shutdowns or overloaded servers could instantly stop you from creating invoices and could even prevent shipment of goods, resulting in possible delivery delays. Having an **E-Invoice Business Continuity Plan** in place helps to ensure that your financial and logistical activities continue uninterrupted and without breaking any tax laws. This detailed guide tells you why IRP downtimes occur, immediate action plans when portals shut down, and ways to ensure smooth billing while adhering to all tax requirements.


## **When can an IRP become unavailable?**


An Invoice Registration Portal is an advanced government platform capable of managing millions of transactions on a daily basis. Though designed to be sturdy and reliable, outages occur from time to time. Awareness of the main reasons allows for quick reaction.


- **Scheduled portal maintenance work**


Sometimes **GSTN or NIC** require upgrading their systems, applying updates or performing other routine work. Such periods are generally announced in advance through GST notices and carried out during off-peak hours. This makes the portal unavailable to generate IRN at this point.


- **Unexpected technical outage**


A system malfunction may occur due to hardware failure, database corruption or other reasons. When this happens, calls to your **ERP** from the portal will receive error codes or timeout messages.


- **High system traffic and peak-hour overload**


Near the end of the month or financial quarter, there are many transactions performed nationwide. As a result of high system traffic, a portal may fail to respond quickly enough and experience connection timeouts.

## **Immediate steps to take during IRP downtime**


In case your billing system raises an **IRP** connectivity error, panicking is never an option. Here are some actionable ways to handle the situation in an organized manner without causing chaos within your finance department.


### **1. Identify the real problem**


Identify if the problem is at your end or if it is in the government portal. You need to check if your **internet connection, API gateway, and ERP integration** are working fine. Alternatively, try accessing the government portal directly or even an alternate multi-IRP portal.


### **2. Review the Official IRP and GSTN advisories**


Go to the **official e-Invoice portal** and **GSTN** advisory portal sites. These government portals will have some official announcements with regard to the downtime of these websites.


### **3. Inform all internal and external departments**


Let the operations and sales teams know about this problem so that the dispatch managers do not try pushing the invoices through and going ahead with the shipment.


### **4. Prioritize and queue your invoice process**


Sort out your invoices by priority. Keep creating the regular tax invoices in your ERP system and keep the important dispatch invoices ready to be pushed.

## **Creating an E-invoice business continuity plan**


Formation of an effective **E-Invoice Business Continuity Plan** enables you to handle unplanned downtime of the portal effectively, and convert it into a systematic process.


### **1. Specify roles within your company**


Clearly define the roles to be played by different people. Specify who will monitor the portal for updates, who will authorize offline dispatches, and who will reconcile IRN after restoration.


### **2. Implement ERP fallback procedures**


Make sure that your ERP or accounting software is capable of issuing regular tax invoices offline. The ERP or accounting software should queue up these offline dispatches in a "IRN Pending" category.


### **3. Switching to multi-IRPs approach**


The GST system provides you with many Invoice Registration Portals **(for example, NIC, eALPS, NSDL, Clear, and others)** . Make sure that your ERP system configuration facilitates switching from one IRP to another in case of failure.


### **4. Customer communication**


Inform your customers about the unplanned downtime of the portal if you have urgent dispatches. Issue standard physical invoices or delivery challans to them wherever it is allowed under tax regulations.


### **5. Monitor and restart batch processing**


Create an automatic alerting system or test periodically for signs of portal services coming back


online. As soon as they resume, perform sequential batch processing of the queued invoices to create their IRN.


## **Maintaining compliance after IRP services resume**


Bringing back the portals online is not enough. Performing the required post-downtime steps will ensure complete e-invoice compliance and tax alignment.


### **1. Submit queued invoices and generate IRNs quickly**


As soon as the portal comes back online, submit your queued invoices on it. Make sure that you meet the reporting deadlines set by the government authorities **(e.g., the 30-day period for specific turnover levels).**


### **2. Verify generated IRNs with your ERP system**


Cross-reference the **IRNs generated, their QR codes** , and time stamps with the records from your ERP system. Make sure that each of your invoices matches the portal records and there is no duplication.


### **3. Check QR code printing in final invoices**


Make sure that the new IRN numbers as well as the signed QR codes are entered into the ERP system and printed with the final PDF invoices mailed out to customers or included with the shipment.


### **4. Conduct GST return reconciliation**


Compare the information filled automatically in the[GSTR-1 & GSTR-2B](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/reconciliation-of-gstr-2b-book-with-gstr-3b) with the books of accounts of your own. Make sure that all invoices filed post-downtime appear accurately in your returns for the month.


## **Conclusion**


The lack of IRP doesn’t mean that you must stop your business activities. Thanks to the development of an **E-Invoice Business Continuity Plan** , a company can continue invoice process flows, handle its dispatches efficiently, and ensure full compliance even in case of a government portal breakdown. With multi-IRP integration, queue handling, and reconciliation after restoring the service, your company will become resistant to any problems.


## **FAQs**


### **1. What occurs when the IRP is not available?**


When the IRP is not available, real-time IRN generation and QR coding stops temporarily. API call failure due to unavailability from ERP causes delayed generation of instant e-way bill generation and physical movement of goods until fallback operating methods are employed.


### **2. Can businesses generate invoices without the IRP?**


Yes, businesses can generate tax invoices offline through their ERP during IRP downtime. But for notified taxpayers, physical movement of goods or issuance of final B2B tax invoices will need generation of IRNs and QR codes upon the availability of portal services as per the prescribed time period.


### **3. How can businesses be prepared for IRP downtimes?**


Businesses can plan for IRP downtimes by implementing multi-IRP configuration, ERP queue management system for offline invoice generation, training of staff on emergency procedures, having customer communication templates, and designation of roles for reconciliation after service availability.


### **4. Does IRP downtime cause GST compliance failure?**


IRP downtimes do not mean GST non-compliance if taxpayers report their pending invoices within the specified time limit. Failing to generate IRNs following service availability can result in penalty, blocked input tax credit, and invalid transport documents.


### **5. What needs to be done by businesses when IRP starts working again?**


As soon as IRP starts working again, companies should upload their pending invoices in batches, check returned IRNs and QR codes, update their ERP system, generate final copies for the buyer, and reconcile transactions with GSTR-1.
