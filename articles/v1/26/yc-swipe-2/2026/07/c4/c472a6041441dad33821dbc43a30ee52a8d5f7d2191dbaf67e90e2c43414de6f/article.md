# How to reconcile e-invoice and e-way bill data before dispatch

# **How to reconcile E-invoice and E-way bill data before dispatch**


> If you work to manage GST-related shipments, knowing how to reconcile e-invoice and e-way bill before dispatch can help you avoid facing problems. At first, it may sound technical, but it’s really just a simple check to prevent delays and mistakes.


Many businesses rush to generate invoices and e-way bills but forget to check if they match. When they don’t, it can lead to confusion during transit and create issues later


The good part is you don’t need a complex system to handle this. A simple and consistent approach is enough.


Let’s understand this in a practical way.


## **Why reconciliation matters before dispatch**


Before dispatch, make sure your e-invoice and e-way bill have the same details. Any mismatch can lead to problems during transport or verification.


Reconciliation helps you:


- Avoid GST compliance issues
- Prevent delays during transport
- Reduce chances of penalties
- Keep your records clean and accurate


Even a small mismatch in values, GSTIN, or item details can lead to unnecessary complications. That’s why many businesses now follow a proper e-invoice reconciliation process before dispatch.


## **What is E-invoice and E-way bill reconciliation?**


Reconciliation simply means the process of cross-checking the information in both e-invoice and e-way bill to confirm consistency.


Your e-invoice includes:


- Invoice number
- GSTIN of buyer and seller
- Taxable value and GST
- Item details


Your e-way bill includes:


- Consignor and consignee details
- Transport details
- Invoice reference
- Value of goods


While reconciling, you check if the details match in both documents. This helps ensure accurate e-invoice and e-way bill data before dispatch.

## **Key details you should always match**


Before dispatching goods, take a few minutes to verify these details:


### **1. Invoice number and date**


Both documents should refer to the same invoice. Any mismatch here can create confusion.


### **2. GSTIN details**


Verify the GSTIN of both buyer and seller, as even a minor error can lead to problems.


### **3. Taxable value and total amount**


The invoice value and e-way bill value should match exactly.


### **4. Product description and quantity**


Ensure item details are consistent in both documents.


### **5. Place of supply**


This affects GST calculation, so it must be accurate.


### **6. Transport details**


Check that the vehicle number and transporter details are entered correctly.


To avoid costly mistakes in future, do a brief check.


## **Common mistakes businesses make**


Experience doesn’t eliminate errors, small mistakes can still happen when handling multiple invoices.


The common mistakes are:


- Different invoice values in e-invoice and e-way bill
- Incorrect GSTIN entries
- Missing or wrong item details
- Manual data entry errors
- Using outdated invoice information


These mistakes can slow down your dispatch or even lead to notices.


That’s why having a proper e-way bill reconciliation process in place is important.
