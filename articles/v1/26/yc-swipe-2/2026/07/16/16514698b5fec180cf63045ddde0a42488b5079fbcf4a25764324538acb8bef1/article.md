# How to calculate consignment value for E-way bill when discounts or freight apply

# **How to calculate consignment value for E-way bill when discounts or freight apply**


> Calculating consignment value for an e-way bill is an essential part of GST compliance whenever goods are transported above the prescribed threshold. The businesses under GST must issue e-way bills whenever there is a movement of goods above the prescribed limit. However, the process can become difficult for the taxmen who have confusions regarding the evaluation of consignment value, especially while taking discounts, freight, and other expenses into account. Should consignment value be calculated after the discounts? Is freight a part of this calculation? What happens when freight is charged separately? These are common questions that can impact GST compliance.


## **What is consignment value in an E-way bill**


Consignment value denotes the total value of the products in transit as stated in the tax invoice, bill of supply, delivery challan, or any other regulatory paperwork under the GST norms. The consignment value is one of the main factors used to determine the need for an[e-way](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/eway-bill-requirements-india) bill for the movement of goods. The consignment value often comprises:


- Taxable value of the items
- GST (CGST, SGST, IGST, or Compensation Cess, if applicable)
- Transport charges (if mentioned in the supplier invoice)
- Packing and packing costs
- Insurance charges, and
- Other charges connected with the sale of goods


It must be noted that when calculating the value of the consignment, the value entered in the invoice after deducting applicable discounts must be taken into consideration and all applicable charges must be added in order to come to the correct value of consignment which will help the businesses in complying with GST regulations and avoid facing any penalties.


## **Why accurate consignment value matters**


It is crucial to determine the correct consignment value when it comes to performing proper GST compliance and smooth transport of goods as it helps in issuing appropriate e-way bills, reduces the chances of having mistakes, and makes sure the data obtained corresponds to invoiced value and GST records. Proper[consignment](https://www.taxwink.com/blog/consignment-value-under-gst) value calculation is also vital for better handling of financial reporting along with minimizing the possibility of disputes related to tax inspections or assessments.The correct determination of the consignment value will allow businesses to:


- Comply with GST requirements
- Avoid penalties during various inspections
- Create accepted e-way bills
- Avoid delays due to misinformation
- Ensure that the accounting and tax records are accurate
- Minimize the issues related to compliance


On the contrary, the wrong calculations will cause unnecessary generation of a lot of e-way bills, compliance problems, and delays in the goods transportation as well unwilling land penalties for providing false data. Therefore it is necessary for businesses to ensure the correctness of invoice value, discounts being applied, and freight charge amount before issuing an e-way bill.


## **How freight charges affect consignment value**


When calculating the value for an e-way bill, discounts can often cause some confusion.


- **Trade discount**


The invoice indicates a trade discount which has either been given prior to or at the time of sale and reduces the taxable value of the product.


Example:


Market price: ₹60,000


Trade discount: ₹10,000


Taxable value: ₹50,000


The e-way bill calculates the value of the product based on the price indicated in the invoice.


- **Cash discounts**


The cash discounts do not form part of the consignment value calculation because they are not deducted if they do not appear in the invoice used for calculating the value of the goods at that time.


- **Promotional discounts**


Promotional discounts or seasonal discounts do appear on the invoice and consequently impact the consignment value.


## **How freight charges affect consignment value**


Pricing for freight affects the components of the consignment value.


### **Freight shown on invoice**


Freight shown separately on the invoice and charged by the supplier adds to the consignment value.


Example


Particular Amount


Goods Value ₹48,000


Freight ₹3,000


Taxable Value ₹51,000


Here, GST will be payable on ₹51,000. The e-way bill will also consider ₹51,000 plus applicable GST.


### **Freight paid independently**


If the buyer makes the arrangements for delivery of goods by themselves and if[freight](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/gst-on-freight-charges) is not charged by the supplier in the invoice, then freight is not included in the supplier’s value of the goods for determining the consignment’s value.


## **Step-by-step calculation of consignment value**


1. **Step 1: Assess the Taxable Value After Any Relevant Invoice Discounts**


Your first task should be to calculate the taxable value of the items after providing for any trade or promotional rebate that is stated in the invoice issued at the time of supply. Only those discounts that feature in the invoice should be factored into the calculation.


1. **Step 2: Include any freight, packing, insuring, and other incidental expenses**


All expenditure incurred in producing the invoice must be included, such as freight,[packing](https://www.taxmann.com/research/gst-new/top-story/105010000000026921/freight-packing-and-other-ancillary-charges-under-gst-for-the-textile-industry-experts-opinion) , insurance, handling, loading or unloading, as well as other incidental expenses. Such expenses must be added to the taxable value of the goods if the supplier charges for them.


1. **Step 3: Assess GST on the taxable amount**


To work out GST that must be charged, the relevant GST rate must be applied (CGST or SGST or IGST) to the taxable amount after including other expenses. Using the correct GST rate depends on the product type and the particular deal.


1. **Step 4: Sum up GST to arrive at the total invoice value**


Once you have worked out the GST amount, it should be added to the taxable value to provide the total value of the invoice.


1. **Step 5: Use total invoice price as the consignment cost in E-way bill**


When generating the e-way bill, the total invoice price including GST and applicable costs should be treated as the cost of consignment. This step allows securing legal compliance and avoiding trouble during cargo transportation.


## **Examples**


### **Example 1: Trade discount applied**


Invoice Particulars


- Cost of goods = ₹75,000
- Discount = ₹5,000
- Transportation charges = ₹2,000
- Insurance = ₹500
- Taxable amount = ₹72,500
- GST = ₹13,050 @ 18%


Points related to consignment amount


₹72,500 + ₹13,050 = ₹85,550


As the freight and insurance cost are included, the invoice value is considered as the consignment value.


### **Example 2: Adding freight separately in invoice**


Particulars of Invoice


- Cost of Goods = ₹48,000
- Tax @ 18% = ₹8,640
- Transportation arranged by buyer:
- Payment of Freight made to transporter = ₹3,000


Points related to consignment amount


₹48,000 + ₹8,640 = ₹56,640


Freight paid separately is not considered typically as it is outside the scope of the supplier's invoice.

## **Best practices for accurate E-way bill calculation**


Companies must:


- Check the amounts in the invoice before creating the electronic waybill.
- Make sure that the discounts are correctly included in the payment invoice.
- Include transport costs only when they constitute a part of the invoice value.
- Keep accurate records of transport payments.
- Match invoices with tax returns and electronic waybills on a regular basis.
- Use invoicing software with GST to make the numbers easier to calculate and avoid mistakes.


## **Conclusion**


Evaluating the value of a consignment for the e-way bill process is simple as long as one knows how discounts and transportation charges are to be regarded. Certain trade discounts reduce the amount subject to taxation on the invoice, whereas transportation, packing, having their own cost of insurance, and similar charges on the invoice should generally be included in the value of consignment. Companies should guarantee that there is consistency between the consignor's account and the e-way bill in order to avoid compliance problems, delays with shipment, or even fines.

## **FAQs**


### Does consignment value include freight?


Freight does form a part of consignment value if it has been charged by the supplier and is included in the invoice.


### Do trade discounts reduce consignment value?


Trade discounts deduct the taxable value from the taxable amount of the consignment.


### Are cash discounts taken into account for e-way bill generation?


Normally, only those discounts which appear on the invoice at the time of supply affect the value of the consignment.


### Is GST a part of consignment value?


Yes. The total value of invoice including GST is generally taken into account for determining the consignment value for e-way bill.


### Is packing and insurance included in the value of consignment?


Packing and insurance costs are included in the value of consignment in case the charges are mentioned in the invoice.
