# The Salesforce Product Name Survival Guide

2020: Customer 360 Audiences
2021: Salesforce CDP
2022: Genie Customer Data Cloud
2023: Data Cloud


Me in every customer meeting:


*“Just to make sure we’re all talking about the same thing…”*


At this point, half of my meeting prep isn’t architecture or demos.


It’s remembering what Salesforce called the product when the customer bought it.


Somewhere out there, there’s a slide deck that still says:


- Pardot
- Einstein Analytics
- Customer 360 Audiences
- Salesforce Anywhere


And honestly?


I respect it.


Because if you’ve been in this ecosystem long enough, you know:


The implementation may last years.
The product name may last a Dreamforce.
