## **What is an E-way Bill?**


An[e-way bill](https://ewaybillgst.gov.in/) is a document to be generated for the transportation of goods of value equal to or more than the threshold limit. The e-way bill shall contain the details of the supplier and receiver of goods, transportation description of goods, vehicle number, invoice and other applicable details. A tax authority may check an e-way bill to ensure that tax is paid on the transported goods. Thus, either the supplier, receiver, or the transporter can generate it.


## **What are SKD and CKD?**


Before getting into the details of the new e-way bill provisions to know the meaning of SKD and CKD. In short, SKD is called semi-knocked down, and CKD is called completely knocked down. In simple terms, SKD can be explained as goods that are partially assembled, and CKD is referred to as completely dismantled goods.


SKD products usually have some major parts assembled while others are detached. On the other hand, CKD products do not have any parts assembled. Industrial machines, elevators, transformers, wind mills, generators, heavy vehicles, and other machinery are usually transported in SKD and CKD condition.

## **What is a multi-vehicle E-way bill?**


A multi-vehicle e-way bill allows businesses to update the details of different vehicles on the e-way bill portal for goods transported using multiple vehicles. Using this facility, businesses do not have to generate separate e-way bills for different vehicles carrying goods.


Instead, the bill is updated separately for every vehicle carrying goods. This facility is mainly useful for transporting large machinery and equipment. This way, businesses can comply with the e-way bill rules without much hassle.


## **When can i use the facility to move goods in multiple vehicles?**

When transporting goods in multiple vehicles, all the goods must be on the same invoice and destined for the same address. ## **FAQs**


### **Is a separate tax invoice required for each SKD or CKD consignment?**


No, a separate invoice is not required for SKD or CKD. A complete invoice is issued for the supply of goods, while subsequent shipments carry a certified copy of the invoice along with delivery challans.


### **Can heavy machinery be transported in multiple vehicles under GST?**


Yes, heavy machinery can be transported in multiple vehicles under GST as long as the provisions of the GST law are complied with.


### **Who is responsible for updating vehicle details?**


The transporter is responsible for updating the vehicle details.


### **Is a delivery challan required for subsequent SKD or CKD consignments?**


Yes, a delivery challan is required for all subsequent SKD or CKD consignments.
