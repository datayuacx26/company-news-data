## Key Features Explained — What Each One Actually Does For You


### GST-Compliant Invoicing


Every invoice that you generate (if your a registered GST paying taxpayer and are rendering services) should have the exact HSN/SAC code, tax rate (usually 18% for professional services)/GSTIN/format. Getting this wrong attracts notices. Apps like[Swipe](https://getswipe.in/) automatically generate GST ready invoices — you just input the amount, and it does everything else, including CGST/SGST vs IGST split which depends on client location.


### Recurring Invoices


Retainer clients should never require manual invoice creation every month. Set it up once, define the billing cycle, and let the software send it automatically. This feature alone saves hours every quarter.


### Payment Tracking & Reminders


The real cash flow killer for consultants isn't invoicing — it's follow-up. Software that sends automated reminders at 7 days, 14 days, and 30 days overdue removes the emotional friction of chasing payments from clients you actually like.


### Expense Management


Consultants regularly incur billable expenses — travel, software subscriptions, printing, third-party services. Good billing software lets you log these directly against a client project and add them to the next invoice with one click.


### Reports That Actually Help


- Outstanding invoices by client
- Revenue by month and project type
- Average days to payment
- GST collected vs. GST payable


These four reports, available in most decent billing tools, give you a complete financial picture in under five minutes.

## Practical Tips to Get More From Your Billing Software


Set up invoice templates once. Your logo, GSTIN, bank details, payment terms — do it once and never touch it again.


Send invoices the day work is delivered. Studies consistently show that invoices sent immediately after project completion get paid faster than those sent at month-end.


Use payment links. Clients pay faster when there's a single tap between them and completing the transaction. Most modern apps, including[Swipe](https://getswipe.in/) , generate a shareable payment link directly from the invoice.


Automate your reminders. Set a 7-day and 14-day overdue reminder and let the software handle it. It's not nagging — it's professional follow-up.


Review your outstanding report weekly. Five minutes every Monday morning on your aging receivables prevents month-end surprises.
