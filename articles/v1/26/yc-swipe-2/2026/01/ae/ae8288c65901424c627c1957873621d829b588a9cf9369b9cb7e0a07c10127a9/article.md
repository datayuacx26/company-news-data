# **From Paper Files to Digital Clarity: How MITU Foundation Transformed Its Billing with Swipe**


Hi, I’m **Reshma** , from **MITU Foundation** , an NGO dedicated to supporting communities through welfare and development programs.


In the context of non-profit organizations, transparency and impact, as well as efficiency and accountability, are equally critical. Each expenditure should be justifiable, every bill verifiable, and every report or catalogue should be lucid. That is why we decided to use Swipe for our digital billing exigencies; it is the finest invoicing tool for small teams and NGOs in India.


## **Why Swipe Became Our Go-To Billing Companion**


In the past, the processes of billing and documentation were highly inefficient, at least from a logistical point of view. Every time I issued bills for donations, sponsorships, or even small supply purchases, the process involved wading through Excel spreadsheets, physical files, and an infinite stream of WhatsApp messages.


However, everything has improved since we adopted Swipe’s automated billing software:


**Easy Billing, Even Easier Catalogues**


Be it producing an invoice for a training session or designing a catalogue for one of our community initiatives, Swipe ensures everything is streamlined and organized. The level of convenience the interface offers is such that even the non-technologically inclined members of our team were able to learn how to use it in a matter of minutes.


**Reports & Analytics That Help**


For an NGO, understanding where the funds are coming from and how they’re being used is critical. Swipe’s **automated reports** give us a real-time snapshot of our expenses, donors, invoices, and GST summaries — no more messy reconciliations at the end of the month.


**One Dashboard, Many Possibilities**


Everything from invoice creation and stock inventory management to catalogue creation and tax return submission, all available with one single billing software. Furthermore, as it is cloud based, our team has 24/7 access from any location.


## **Made for Every Kind of Business (Even NGOs!)**


Although many assume digital billing systems only apply to retail stores and salons, we have witnessed the versatility of Swipe even for nonprofit organizations. It assists us to:


Automatically compute the Goods and Services Tax.


Issue customized invoices bearing our logo.


Maintain digital files for audits and transparency related to donors.


Monitor expenses by projects with minimal effort.


## **Why Swipe is the Best Billing Software in India for NGOs**


Swipe exemplifies a seamless blend of usability, features, and cost-effectiveness. For an emerging, growth-minded organisation like ours, it serves as more than just a tool; it functions as a comprehensive digital assistant.


## **Final Thoughts**


As far as the MITU Foundation is concerned, with Swipe, billing isn’t just easier—it has made our entire operations more accountable, and data-driven. If you belong to a social enterprise, small business, or an NGO and you’re still using paper-based records or spreadsheets, this is a wake-up call.


Swipe is not just the best billing software in India, it is the boldest step one can take toward transparency, digital advancement, and social impact.
