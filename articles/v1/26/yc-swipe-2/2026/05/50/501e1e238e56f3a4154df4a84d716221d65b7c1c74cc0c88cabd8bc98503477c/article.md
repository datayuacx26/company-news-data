# Mobile Accessories HSN Code & GST Rates Explained

# Mobile Accessories HSN Code for GST Purposes


Mobile phones and accessories have become the center of life for everybody. Mobile accessories such as phone cases, power banks, earbuds, chargers, and others are very popular. Yet, it is needed to know the HSN code under GST when purchasing or selling mobile accessories.


This guide will help you, as an operator, whether a manufacturer, trader, or even a customer who wants to understand why we change the price with taxes. The GST rates, HSN codes, and all other details you need will be discussed in a nontechnical language.


As the demand for these products increases, companies that deal with mobile accessories should be acquainted with the tax regulations and HSN codes. Any little mistake in classification or GST filing can result in a penalty, loss of money, or noncompliance.


## What is an HSN Code?


The **Harmonized System of Nomenclature** is referred to as HSN. It is a system that is used globally to systematically classify goods.


- In India, product classification is done using HSN codes under GST.


- The products are given an HSN code, which helps to identify the rate of tax.


- The appropriate HSN to use on mobile accessories ensures that there is proper tax filing, invoicing, and adherence.


## Why is HSN Code important for mobile accessories?


**Correct Tax Calculation** – The GST rate depends on the HSN code.


**Smooth Compliance** —Helps businesses file GST returns without mistakes


**Avoid Penalties** —Wrong codes may lead to penalties or tax notices.


**Transparency** —Builds trust with customers by showing proper tax details.

Mobile accessories fall under different categories depending on their type. Here’s a quick table for better clarity:

> 👉 As per GST rules, **most mobile accessories are taxed at 18%.**


## GST impact on mobile accessories


- **Consumers:** The price that you will pay will be inclusive of 18% GST. In the case of an example of a charger that is ₹500, GST will be ₹90, and then the charger would be 590.


- **Retailers/Wholesalers:** When billing to obtain the[Input Tax Credit (ITC)](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/blocked-and-disputed-itc-credits) , the right HSN code must be used.


- **Manufacturers:** To ensure easy compliance, they need to place under the correct HSN their raw materials and finished products.


## Detailed GST impact on e-commerce sellers


[E-commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-commerce) platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho are some of the biggest marketplaces for mobile accessories. Sellers here must be extra careful about HSN codes because invoices are automatically generated. If you select the wrong HSN code, not only does it lead to wrong GST collection, but it can also block your Input Tax Credit claims. For e-commerce sellers, this directly affects profit margins and can even result in penalties during GST audits.

## GST on imported mobile accessories


In case you are importing accessories, such as earphones, or power banks, imported in China or other countries, GST is applied at the time of import as Integrated GST (IGST). The same HSN codes are used with the difference that the importer has to pay the IGST then refund it later as ITC.


## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Guessing codes:** Never randomly select an HSN code just because it looks similar.

## FAQs


### 1. The HSN code for mobile chargers exists under which classification?


The HSN code 8504 applies to mobile chargers, which become subject to an 18% GST rate. The correct code guarantees appropriate invoicing and a hassle-free payment of GST.


### 2. Are mobile phone covers also taxed at 18%?


Yes, mobile covers fall under HSN code 3926 and are taxed at 18% GST. Even small accessories require correct codes for compliance.


### 3. Are all the mobile accessories charged the same GST?


Mobile accessories are taxed at 18 percent GST, except that the HSNs differ based on the product. Effective classification eliminates billing errors.


### 4. Why is the HSN code important for small businesses?


HSN codes ensure correct tax filing and allow Input Tax Credit. The system enables businesses to avoid penalties while creating a transparent billing system.


### 5. The GST duty rate for earphones and headphones falls under which category?


The GST tax rate for headphones and earphones falls under HSN code 8518, which has an 18 percent rate.


### 6. Is GST tax applied to tempered glass used for mobile devices?


Yes, GST tax applies to tempered glass products under HSN code 7007 at an 18% rate.


### 7. What is the HSN code of mobile data cables and USB cables?


USB charging cables and data cables were put under HSN code 8544. They are also 18 percent GST taxed like any other accessory
