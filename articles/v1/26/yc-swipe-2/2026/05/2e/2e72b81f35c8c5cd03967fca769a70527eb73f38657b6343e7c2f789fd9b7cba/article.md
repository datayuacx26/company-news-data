# Business Ideas in Bihar With Low Investment

# **Business Ideas in Bihar With Low Investment**


For several years now, Bihar has been known to be a region of migration and conventional agriculture. But in 2026, the perception has changed and now, the focus is on the Startup Revolution. As the state boasts an impressive number of 130 million people and excellent infrastructure, Bihar has ceased to be a labor supply. The state has become a consumer market in its own right.


With the advent of the[Startup Bihar Policy 2026](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/state-startup-policies/Bihar-state-policy.html) and the[Mukhya Mantri Udyami Yojna](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/mukhyamantri-udyami-yojana-bihar) scheme, funds of ₹10 lakh to ₹25 lakh are accessible now, which means that it is a good time to create low-budget business. No matter whether you live in Patna, Muzaffarpur, or rural areas, your low-budget yet efficient business can thrive anywhere in Bihar.


In this article, we will look at the top business ideas in Bihar with low investment in 2026.


## **Agritech and Food Processing Business**


According to[Traffictail](https://traffictail.com/business-ideas-in-bihar/#:~:text=Agricultural%20Powerhouse%3A%20Bihar%20is%20one,packaging%2C%20and%20cold%20storage%20businesses.) , Bihar is an agricultural home, producing massive quantities of litchi, makhana, and maize. In 2026, the real money is not in growing the crop, but in processing it.


1. **Makhana Packaging:**


Bihari produces 90% of the world’s makhana. Starting a small-scale roasting and flavoured packaging unit requires minimal machinery but offers high margins in urban markets.


1. **Mushroom Cultivation:**


Mushrooms can be grown indoors in small rooms with very low capital. With the rising health consciousness in cities like Gaya and Bhagalpur, fresh oyster and button mushrooms are in high demand.


1. **Pickle and Honey:**


Leveraging traditional Bihari recipes for “Aam ka Achar” or “Sattu” based snacks and selling them via e-commerce platforms like Amazon or ONDC allows you to reach a national audience from your home.


## **The Education Hub**


Education is deeply ingrained in the Bihari DNA. Even in 2026, as[mycleaners.in](https://www.mycleaners.in/list-blog-post/best-business-ideas-in-bihar#:~:text=5.,space%20in%20a%20market%20area.) , writes, the demand for competitive exam preparation like UPSC, BPSC, Railway, and SSC, remains at an all time high.


1. **Coaching Centres of Rural Areas** :


While Patna is saturated, there is a massive gap for quality coaching in Tier-3 towns.An efficient centre that can accommodate fast internet connectivity and displays is an inexpensive and worthwhile investment.


1. **Study Spaces or Book Cafes:**


The noisy environments of large cities mean students do not have a quiet place to study. Renting a hall, providing comfortable desks, high speed wifi, and a small tea/coffee corner can generate steady monthly subscription income.


## **The E-Rickshaw Service**


As Bihar’s road connectivity reaches almost every village, the “Last Mile” logistics and service sector is exploding.


1. **E-Rickshaw Charging Stations:**


WIth thousands of e-rickshaws replacing traditional cycles, setting up a solar powered charging point or a battery swapping station is a futuristic business with ;pw daily overheads.


1. **Mobile Repair and Accessories:**


As 5G expands into rural Bihar, the need for quick mobile repairs and low-cost accessories (headphones, chargers) in local markets is a goldmine for tech savvy people.

## **Additional Business Opportunities in Bihar**


1. **Fish Farming or Aquaculture:**


Bihar has vast water resources. Utilising “Biofic” technology allows you to grow high density fish populations in small tanks in your backyard.


- **Investments-** ₹1 Lakh to ₹2 Lakh


- **Why it works** : High local demand for[fresh fish](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/gst-rates-hsn-code-dried-salted-brine-fish) and government subsidies for pisciculture.


1. **Cold Press Oil Extraction:**


Consumers in 2026 are wary of[refined oil](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/gst-rates-hsn-code-1510-other-oils) . Setting up a small cold press unit for mustard or sunflower oil provides a premium and healthy product that people are willing to pay extra for. With an investment of just about ₹1.5Lakh to ₹2.5Lakh, an entrepreneur of Bihar can get a huge amount of money. Bihar has a massive mustard growing belt, and hence sourcing raw material is easy and cheap.


1. **Goat Farming or Bakri Palan**


Known as the “poor man’s cow,”[goat](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/code-104-gst-and-hsn-code-for-live-sheep-and-goats) farming requires very little space and fodder. It is highly resilient to Bihar’s climate and has a ready market for meat.


- **Investment:** ₹50,000 - ₹1 Lakh, of about 5-10 goats.


- **Why it works:** High Return on Investment or ROI and government subsides specifically for livestock.

## **Ways to Government Funding**


In 2026, you do not have to use your life savings to start a business in BIhar.


- **Startup Policy for BIhar** : Interest free seed Grant up to ₹10 Lakh for 10 years on Innovative Ideas for Startup Policy of Bihar.


- **Mukhya Mantri Udyami Yojna:** ₹10 Lakh for SC, ST, EBC, Women, and youth Entrepreneurs, with up to 50% grant/ subsidy and remaining 50% as interest free or low-interest loan.


- **Laghu Udyami Yojna of Bihar** : Financial Assistance of ₹2 Lakh in case of very small scale micro enterprises under Bihar Laghu Udyami Yojna.

## **FAQs**


### 1. Do I need a GST number for a small home business in Bihar?


It depends on the circumstances, for example, say if the total revenue from the sale of goods is less than ₹40 Lakhs per annum and that from the sale of services is less than ₹20 Lakhs/yr, then registration under GST is not mandatory. But if you want to sell your goods on Amazon/Flipkart, then GSTIN is said to be very highly mandatory.


### 2. Which Bihar city is ideal for a startup venture?


While Patna is the commercial capital, cities like Muzaffarpur, the trade hub, or Bhagalpur, the silk or agricultural capital, and Purnia for logistics, are emerging as high growth zones with lower rental costs, hence, you should keep in mind these places.


### 3. How do I apply for the ₹10 Lakh Bihar Startup Grant?


You must register your ideas on the[Startup Bihar Portal,](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/state-startup-policies/Bihar-state-policy.html) the government portal. Your ideas will be evaluated by a committee, and if selected, you will receive the first tranche of the seed fund.


### 4. Is a Litti Chokha stall still a good business in 2026?


Yes, but the trend has shifted toward Hygiene and Branding. Modern “Litti Chokha” cloud kitchens that offer online delivery via Zomato or Swiggy are currently outperforming traditional roadside stalls.


### 5. Can female entrepreneurs have additional advantages in Bihar?


Yes, indeed. In the case of women, the amount of ₹5 Lakhs is paid in total through the scheme named Mukhyamantri Madhila Udyami Yojna, and the rest of ₹5 lakhs is a loan that comes without interest charges.
