# Bangalore Gruha Jyothi Scheme: Benefits and Process

# **Bangalore Gruha Jyothi Scheme: Benefits and Process**


Living in a lively city like Bangalore has its own set of expenditures, such as paying rent, education fees and hikes to the grocery bill. When you are already having trouble paying all your bills, one place you do not want to find yourself in is with a large power bill.


That's the purpose of implementing the Gruha Jyothi Scheme Bangalore. The Government of Karnataka introduced this scheme so that it could become a way for many families to save on their electricity costs by having free electricity.


## **What is the Gruha Jyothi Scheme?**


With support from the government's overall initiative for 2023, Karnataka introduced Gruha Jyothi, whose target is providing every individual with 200 units of electricity per month without incurring any expense. Nevertheless, availability is determined by one's geographical location since the manner in which these supplies will be provided differs between urban and rural areas. Due to variation in areas, BESCOM varies the number of units supplied freely to people every month.


## **Advantages of Gruha Jyothi Scheme**


Saving Money is Obvious: While saving money is one of the advantages of this scheme, it does not end there.


- **Saving each month:** Every month, a middle-class family can expect to save about either Rs 1000- Rs 1500 on their monthly electric bill, or about Rs 15,000 for the whole year, which is quite a bit of grocery money.
- **Tenancy Inclusion:** The best thing about this scheme is that it includes tenants who may not own any land or property.
- **Encouraging People to Conserve Energy:** The cap of 200 units is enough to incentivize people to conserve energy.
- **Ease of Applying for the Scheme:** Gone are those days when you had to queue up at the government office to avail of such schemes. Now the application process is largely online.

## **Criteria to Become Eligible for the Gruha Jyothi Scheme Bangalore**


For becoming an eligible applicant of the Gruha Jyothi Scheme Bangalore, one does not require to come from a particular income group, but here are some conditions:


- **Residential Purposes:** It is meant for residential purposes only. If you operate a tailoring business or any other business from your house using commercial electricity, then you will not be able to apply.
- **Only One Connection:** Only one electricity connection with the help of your Aadhaar number is applicable for the benefit.
- **Energy consumption by the applicant:** In the last year, the energy consumption by the applicant should not be more than 200 kWh on average every month.
- **Residency of the state:** The applicant must be a member of the state of Karnataka irrespective of his/her residence being in a rented apartment in Bangalore due to job considerations or otherwise.

## **Documents to be Prepared**


These documents should be prepared before filling out the form. With them, the process of registering for Seva Sindhu will become easy for you.


- **Aadhaar Card:** This is the foremost document required by the utility company.
- **Customer ID/Account ID:** The 10-digit code for your BESCOM Electricity Bill.
- **Mobile Number:** Needs to be verified via OTP with the aid of an Aadhaar number.
- **Rental Agreement:** To be furnished only in case you are a tenant.


## **Steps to apply for Gruha Jyoti Scheme**


In order to initiate your application process for the Gruha Jyothi Scheme, please follow these steps:


### **Online Process to Apply**


1. Go to Seva Sindhu Official Portal :[Seva Sindhu portal](https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/Sevasindhu/English)
2. 2. Click on **"Gruha Jyothi"** after that, then click on **"Click Here for Gruha Jyothi Registration".**
3. Select the language you prefer. (either English or Kannada).
4. Select your **ESCOM** from the drop-down list and enter your Account ID or Consumer ID from your electricity bill.
5. Select whether you are Owner, Tenant or Family Member.
6. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and your **mobile number** (for OTP verification).
7. Accept the declaration and complete the Captcha. Now click on "Submit".
8. You will receive an acknowledgement number. Please print this for your reference later on.

## **FAQs**


### **1. I am a tenant in Bangalore. Am I eligible to apply for Gruha Jyothi?**


Yes, you are! All you need to do is provide your Aadhaar card and the BESCOM Account ID of the rented house. Just choose the "Tenant" option in the application form.


### **2. If I consume 201 units, what will happen?**


In case your consumption exceeds the limit of 200 units even by a single unit, you will be charged for the total consumption (201 units) on prevailing tariffs.


### **3. Should I register for the scheme each month?**


No, it is a one-time registration process only. If your consumption remains below the limit for every month, you get the discount automatically.


### **4. Is it possible to apply for the scheme if I own two houses?**


It is not possible to avail of benefits under the scheme for more than one household. This is because of the Aadhaar linkage.
