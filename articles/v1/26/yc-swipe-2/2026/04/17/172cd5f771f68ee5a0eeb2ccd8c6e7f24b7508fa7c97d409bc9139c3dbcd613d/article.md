# Stranded Copper Wire: Complete GST & HSN Code Guide

# **Stranded Copper Wire: Complete GST & HSN Code Guide**


Stranded copper wire is a commonly used material in India. It is widely used in homes, offices, factories, shops and large industrial projects. From basic household wiring to heavy electrical systems, stranded copper wire plays an important role. Because it is sold and used on such a large scale, it becomes very important to understand how GST applies to it.


Under the GST system, every product is given an HSN code to decide the correct tax rate. Stranded copper wire also falls under a specific HSN code and knowing this code helps businesses make proper bills, follow GST rules and avoid future issues. This guide explains everything about Stranded copper wire GST and HSN code in a simple way so that even first time sellers or small businesses owners can understand it clearly.


You can also refer to the[HSN Code List](https://getswipe.in/blog/article/hsn-code-list) **** to understand how different products are classified under GST.


## **What is Stranded Copper Wire**


Stranded copper wire is made by twisting many thin copper wires together. This design makes the wire flexible and strong at the same time. Because of its flexibility, it is easier to bend and install compared to solid copper wire.


Stranded copper wire is commonly used in:


● Electrical wiring


● Control panels


● Motors and machines


● Extension cards


● Appliances


● Industrial electrical systems


### **HSN Code for Stranded Copper Wire**


Under GST, stranded copper wire falls under HSN Code 7408. This HSN code is used for copper wire products and helps the government identify the product category for tax purposes. Businesses must mention this HSN code on GST invoices while selling standard copper wire.


HSN Code 7408 covers:


● Copper wires


● Standard copper wires


● Copper conductors


● Copper cables(without insulation in some cases)


Using the correct HSN code is very important because it decides the GST rate. Incorrect HSN codes can lead to billing mistakes and even penalties. For correct product classification, you can refer to the[CBIC HSN Code List](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/htdocs-cbec/gst/hsn-code) to avoid any billing mistakes.


### **GST Rate on Stranded Copper Wire**


In most cases, stranded copper wire is charged at 18% GST. This rate applies whether the wire is sold by a manufacturer, wholesaler or retailer. The same GST rate is charged on bulk sales as well as retail sales. Even if the wire is insulated or supplied in coils or rolls, the GST rate generally remains the same.


There is usually no GST exemption for standard copper wire. Only in rare cases, such as special government notification or specific projects, a different rule may apply.

### **Who Needs to Charge GST on Stranded Copper Wire**


Any business that manufactures, sells or supplies stranded copper wire needs to follow GST rules. Manufacturers must charge GST when selling their products if their annual turnover crosses the GST limit. Traders and shop owners selling copper wire also need to add GST to their bills.


Contracts and electrical service providers who supply stranded copper wires as part of their work must also charge GST. Even when the wire is supplied along with installation, GST rules still apply.


### **GST Registration Requirement**


Businesses dealing in stranded copper wire must register for GST if their annual turnover exceeds the GST limit. In most states, GST registration becomes mandatory once turnover crosses ₹40 lakh. In some special category states, the limit is ₹20 lakh.


Many businesses also choose voluntary GST registration even if their turnover is below the limit. This helps them work with large clients, issue proper GST invoices and claim Input Tax Credit.


Even if the sales are lower, registration helps claim ITC and makes billing easier. You can follow the **GST registration process** to get started.


### **Input Tax Credit on Stranded Copper Wire**


Input Tax Credit(ITC) is a major benefit under GST. If a GST registered business purchases stranded copper wire for business use, it can claim GST paid on that purchase as Input Tax Credit.


To claim Input Tax Credit, the purchase invoice must be valid, the supplier should have filed their GST returns and the buyer must file returns on time. When done correctly, ITC helps businesses pay less tax overall.
