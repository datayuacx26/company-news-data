# Safe and Effective Use of LRAD Systems

###### Operational Guidelines for Public Safety and Security Teams


LRAD systems provide powerful, long-range communication, but their effectiveness depends on trained operators, controlled output, and sound operational judgment.


Download **Safe and Effective Use of LRAD Systems** for practical guidance on:


- How distance, direction, duration, and the environment affect sound exposure
- Using minimum effective volume, safe standoff distances, and alert tones appropriately
- Understanding the ranges and operating characteristics of common LRAD models
- Applying LRAD safely during crowd direction, close-range interactions, and volatile situations


Help your team communicate clearly, reduce unnecessary risk, and use LRAD systems as intended.
