# The Critical Infrastructure Response Gap

###### Protecting Power and Data Infrastructure: From Perimeter Threats to Real-Time Response


Power utilities and data centers are well equipped to detect intrusions and physical threats. The challenge comes after detection, when response depends on manual review, delayed escalation, or dispatching personnel to remote sites. In those first critical minutes, the lack of immediate, on-site intervention can turn a security event into damaged infrastructure, outages, and regulatory exposure.


**Download the eBook to see how power and data operators are moving from detection-only security to real-time deterrence and coordinated response.**


- Learn how to prevent damage and theft before responders arrive
- See how active deterrence changes outcomes at substations and data center facilities
- Understand how integrated systems improve coordination across teams and locations
- Explore ways to reduce downtime, operational disruption, and compliance risk
- Review real-world examples from power, energy, and critical infrastructure environments
