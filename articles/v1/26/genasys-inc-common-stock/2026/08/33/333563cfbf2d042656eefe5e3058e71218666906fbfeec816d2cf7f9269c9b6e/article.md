# Genasys Reaches Major Milestone – Over 50M U.S. Residents Covered by Protect Platform

###### ******Company’s Zone-Based Public Safety Communication and Emergency Alerting Coverage Now Extends to 20% of U.S. by Area******


**SAN DIEGO, CA** – **August 10, 2026** –[Genasys](https://genasys.com/) Inc. (NASDAQ: GNSS), the global leader in[Protective Communications](https://genasys.com/protect-platform/) ®, today announced that its Protect software platform, which includes[Genasys Protect](https://genasys.com/genasys-protect/) ® and[Genasys Evertel](https://genasys.com/genasys-evertel/) ® (Evertel), now covers 15% of the U.S. population and 20% of the country by area, making it the nation’s leading platform for zone-based emergency alerting, evacuation management, and secure, real-time communication.


“Covering more than 50 million people is an important milestone that reflects the growing trust communities and public safety agencies place in Genasys life safety solutions,” said Richard Danforth, CEO of Genasys Inc. “Last summer’s deadly flooding in Texas and several recent tragedies illustrate the ineffectiveness of single channel, general warnings. Genasys Protect delivers multi-channel emergency notifications up to 90% faster than other warning systems using data-driven decisions, precise zone targeting, and clear messaging.”


Whether it’s a neighborhood evacuation order for a natural gas leak, an active SWAT operation shelter-in-place advisory for a few city blocks, or a severe weather alert for a county or community, Genasys Protect’s zone-based alerting helps ensure only the people in affected areas receive emergency notifications.


Zone-based alerting provides vital advantages during wildfires, flooding, hazardous material incidents, and other public safety threats. By delivering the right message to the right people at the right time, Genasys Protect reduces alert fatigue, builds public trust, and enhances overall incident response.


In addition to Genasys Protect, Evertel has grown to support mission-critical communications for more than 500 law enforcement agencies in 45 states. Since its acquisition by Genasys less than three years ago, Evertel has doubled its customer base and is now deployed in some of the largest U.S. cities.


“When it comes to encrypted, legally compliant mission-critical communications, we provide agencies access to any other public safety agency with complete security, transparency, and government accountability,” said Chief Jeff Halstead (Ret.), Genasys Director of Strategic Accounts and Evertel Founder.


Evertel is a FOIA, CJIS, HIPAA, and State compliant communications and team collaboration platform for first responders and government agencies. Evertel greatly enhances communications with voice, chat, messaging, video, and images, empowering teams to share critical information and receive agency-wide and cross-agency updates in a secure, real-time environment.
