# Genasys Receives Follow-On $2.4M LRAD CIP Order

###### ****LRAD 950NXTs Providing Large U.S. Utility Substations Remotely Operated Surveillance and First Response Capabilities****


**SAN DIEGO, CA** – **August 5, 2026** –[Genasys](https://genasys.com/) Inc. (NASDAQ: GNSS), the global leader in[Protective Communications](https://genasys.com/protect-platform/) ®, today announced a new $2.4 million critical infrastructure protection (CIP) order for one of the nation’s largest utility companies. The order follows the recent installation of[LRAD](https://genasys.com/lrad-products/) ® 950NXT systems at one of the utility’s major substations, and a[subsequent $2.0 million order](https://genasys.com/press-releases/genasys-receives-new-lrad-order-from-large-utility-following-recent-lrad-installation/) to outfit additional substations with the remotely operated LRADs.


The 950XTs will be fully integrated with the substations’ existing physical security and include multi-sensor perimeter intrusion detection from integration partner, iEnet.


“More and more critical infrastructure operators around the world are trusting Genasys systems and solutions to safeguard vital assets and protect people,” said Genasys CEO Richard Danforth. “The unparalleled surveillance, first response, and on-site intervention capabilities of our LRAD 950NXTs are being deployed by utilities, dams, ports, nuclear energy facilities, power plants, data centers, and other essential infrastructure.”


Sensor-integrated 950NXTs elevate and enhance CIP by turning passive monitoring into immediate security threat responses and interventions. LRAD 950NXT systems fully address the North American Electric Reliability Corporation’s physical security requirements to Detect, Assess, Communicate, Respond, Delay, and Deter electrical transmission and distribution asset threats.
