# Motorola Solutions to Issue Second-Quarter 2026 Earnings Results on August 5

*Webcast presentation by executives to follow earnings release*


**CHICAGO – July 22, 2026** – Motorola Solutions, Inc. (NYSE: MSI) will issue its second-quarter 2026 earnings results after the close of the market on Wednesday, August 5.


Motorola Solutions will host its quarterly conference call with financial analysts at 4 p.m. Central (5 p.m. Eastern) on August 5. The conference call will be webcast live at[www.motorolasolutions.com/investors](http://www.motorolasolutions.com/investors) .
