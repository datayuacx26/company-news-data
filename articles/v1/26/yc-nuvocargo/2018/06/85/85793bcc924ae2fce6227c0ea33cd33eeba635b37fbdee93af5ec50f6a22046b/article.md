# I’m looking for a technical partner (CTO, VP of Engineering, Cofounder, Chief Hacker, or whatever you want to call it)


[Deepak Chhugani](https://medium.com/@deepakchhugani?source=post_page---byline--6f5e8c63fc46---------------------------------------)


5 min read


·


Jun 25, 2018


--


This post is directed towards my future business partner.


*\[Update: April ’19: I found the partner this post was directed towards! This post was a useful tactic in finding the right person, but ultimately it came down to willful luck, serendipity, and persistence in convincing the right person to join.*


*We’ve also changed our business entirely since writing this post to a new market, but I’m leaving it as is in case it helps others.\]*


### **Back then**


Ever since I quit[my banking job](https://medium.com/@deepakchhugani/investment-banking-is-it-worth-it-e4dd4ffa1cbf) a few years ago to pursue startups, all I hear from everyone is “you need a technical cofounder.”


I spent a few months agreeing with them, going to meetups, hackathons, teaching myself to code, and much more, all to try and recruit this mythical person.


No luck whatsoever.


Back then, I was mainly getting rejected because I was the 500th business dude with “the next Facebook” idea and nothing else to show for except words.


### Today


Fast forward to today, and things are different. Since I wasn’t finding anyone impressive to join me, I chose to work hard for a while and prove myself so I could eventually attract the right person.


**For context** , we’re building[The Lobby](https://www.thelobby.io/?utm_source=TechCofounderMedium&utm_medium=Blog&utm_campaign=TechCofounderMedium) . We’re a marketplace where job candidates can instantly book mock interviews, resume critiques, and get insights from company insiders.


Then, we use the data from those interactions for something much more disruptive to the recruiting industry that helps candidates get better jobs and gives companies access to non-obvious and diverse talent.


We’re a[Y Combinator](https://blog.ycombinator.com/meet-12-companies-from-the-winter-2018-batch/) **& VC-backed startup** in NYC with money in the bank to hire the very best people. **But** I’m still a solo nontechnical founder.


**Today** , we have a lot of interest from talented technical people, but it’s still so hard to find the right person. So I’m writing this in hopes that it will attract my future business partner.


Here are some things I’m proud of since our founding:


- We’ve[built a product](https://www.thelobby.io/?utm_source=TechCofounderMedium&utm_medium=Blog&utm_campaign=TechCofounderMedium) that **solves a real problem,** has generated tens of thousands of dollars in revenue (and[had revenue from day 1](https://www.fastcompany.com/40528348/your-startups-mvp-isnt-working-but-heres-what-might) with spreadsheets), and is growing.
- We just went through[Y Combinator’s](https://blog.ycombinator.com/meet-12-companies-from-the-winter-2018-batch/) latest batch (world’s best accelerator, funds ~1% of applicants globally, a personal dream come true) as a solo nontechnical founder.
- We’re venture-backed and raised funding from top-tier Silicon Valley & NYC investors (details to be announced later).
- We’ve built[an amazing early team](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/?facetCurrentCompany=%5B%2211187112%22%5D) mainly comprised of engineers all over the world in different timezones, which I’ve learned how to[lead and manage](https://blog.usejournal.com/7-steps-to-managing-remote-developer-teams-if-youre-nontechnical-a992b966b885) (with many mistakes and lessons along the way).


All this was done without a technical cofounder or a technical partner who is equally invested in The Lobby’s success as I am.


Aside from actually writing the code, I’ve managed product, getting users, customer support, fundraising, hiring, legal work, and more pretty much by myself thus far.


I am extremely proud of what we’ve accomplished thus far despite these limitations. But I also know that if we want to build an amazing technology company, I need an amazing technical leader (you) for the rest of our company’s life.


For me personally, if you’re the right person, this is a lifelong partnership to build businesses together, where we both have complementary skills and are united by a common goal & mission.


For[The Lobby](https://www.thelobby.io/?utm_source=TechCofounderMedium&utm_medium=Blog&utm_campaign=TechCofounderMedium) , that means someone who is passionate about making the hiring process less shitty, more accessible, and ultimately helps people find meaningful work (where humans spend 70%+ of their lives).


Specifically, I’m looking for someone:


- **Technical —** full stack, experience building web products (huge plus if proficient in Django/Python & React.js).
- **A Leader —** has led teams of 3–10 engineers preferrably.
- **Entrepreneurial** — has either started their own companies in college or shortly thereafter, but is not burned out from failure if one of them didn’t pan out. Engineers obsessed with time-consuming side projects are a great fit too.
- **A magnet —** someone other engineers admire and want to learn from, even if they’re just graduating or 1 year out of school, you know these people from afar.
- **A risk taker —** I don’t care what 99% of Techcrunch funding & “I’m crushing it” articles say; joining a seed-stage company means you know there’s a risk, and correspondingly a potentially high reward. This excites you, and you’re more pumped about the impact you could have in making us a success than interrogating me to see if we’re a comparable option to a job at Facebook or a Series B company.
- **Someone with perspective —** at the risk of sounding arrogant, I want someone who understands why they need me as much as I need them. Being a fantastic engineer is incredibly valuable, as evidenced by the little supply and staggering demand today. But if you’re the person I’m looking for, you know that outside of engineering, building a company has many other facets which I believe I provide and are complementary to your skills.
- **Based in NYC or willing to relocate** — This is the only role I’m not willing to have fully remote. This person should be willing to live in NYC, or spend a significant amount of time here.


This is not a “hire” for me; this is essentially a “late” technical cofounder and who will shape the engineering culture & product roadmap.
