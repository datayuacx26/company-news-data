# My favorite part of being a startup founder


[Deepak Chhugani](https://medium.com/@deepakchhugani?source=post_page---byline--9639dd0cccc1---------------------------------------)


1 min read


·


Sep 29, 2018


--


I have read mountains of startup knowledge, blog posts, essays, and more.


Of all those, I feel very few people tend to emphasize what has been the most amazing part of my experience as a first-time founder: *the advice, relationships, and rapport* you can build with insanely successful people because they were once in your shoes.


Before being a founder, I would occasionally get lucky and meet someone that I considered super successful, but our conversation never got past small talk.


When you’re a founder, it’s like the moment they hear what you’re doing they’re trying to help because they know how hard it is or have been in similarly stressful/risky situations.


It’s a totally different dynamic that leads to amazing, genuine, and memorable conversations, and ultimately to building lasting relationships.


Whether it’s


[Y Combinator](https://medium.com/u/cb8adc841a29?source=post_page---user_mention--9639dd0cccc1---------------------------------------)


alumni & partners, our investors & advisors at[Nuvocargo](http://www.nuvocargo.com/) , or even the tons of random introductions I get every week, I feel so lucky to constantly learn from amazing people with much more experience than me who treat me as a peer because I am a founder, despite my young age or inexperience.
