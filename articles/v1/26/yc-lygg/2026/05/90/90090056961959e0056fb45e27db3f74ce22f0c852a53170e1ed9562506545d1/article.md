# LYGG, Groningen Airport Eelde and Groningen Seaports invite companies to discuss new Groningen–Esbjerg route


Jaa artikkeli:


25.05.2026


News


On 9 June, LYGG, Groningen Airport Eelde and Groningen Seaports will host an event about the new **Groningen–Esbjerg** connection launching in August.


The session will bring together companies from the offshore, maritime, energy and logistics sectors to discuss how regional air connectivity can better support operational travel needs across the North Sea region.


During the event, LYGG will present the Groningen–Esbjerg route, including schedules, pricing structure and opportunities to tailor the service for companies with travel needs in the North Sea region.


In addition to the new Esbjerg connection, LYGG has already been operating the Groningen–Norwich route for several months as part of its growing North Sea network.


### Event details


📍 Groningen Airport Eelde
🗓 Tuesday 9 June 2026
⏰ 16:00–17:00


The event is open to regional companies and organizations active in offshore wind, energy, logistics, maintenance and operations.
