The North Sea network continues to grow as LYGG introduces new connections that strengthen business connectivity between key regional hubs.


## Esbjerg and Groningen Connected from 4 August


On **4 August** , LYGG launches its new direct connection between **[Esbjerg](https://lygg.com/esbjerg/) and[Groningen](https://lygg.com/groningen/)** , linking two of the North Sea region’s key offshore, energy and industrial centres.


The route operates every Tuesday throughout August before expanding to **four weekly frequencies from September** , providing companies with a predictable published schedule between the two regions.


## Den Helder Joins the Network


The next phase follows on **2 September** , when **Den Helder** joins the published schedule with two weekly connections.


Together, these additions provide companies with more direct travel options across the North Sea while strengthening connectivity between regional business hubs.


## Growing Alongside Business Demand


The North Sea network develops alongside **verified recurring business travel demand** . Additional frequencies and regional connections are introduced where recurring demand supports them, while the up to date published schedule is available through **LYGG’s booking tool** .


Companies can access LYGG flights through the flexible Serial Ticket model or purchase one-off tickets under a corporate agreement.


## Tailored Operations Continue Alongside the Network


Alongside the published North Sea network, LYGG also develops tailored operations designed around specific company and project requirements. Together, the published network and tailored operations provide businesses with regional air connectivity that matches their operational needs.


Every connection creates the foundation for the next one. That’s how we’re building the North Sea network: step by step, together with the companies using it.
