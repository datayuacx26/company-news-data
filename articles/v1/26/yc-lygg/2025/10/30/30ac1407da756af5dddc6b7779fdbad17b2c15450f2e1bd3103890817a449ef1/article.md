# Introducing the LYGG Calculator


Jaa artikkeli:

News


We talk a lot about saving time, cutting costs, and reducing emissions. Now you can see exactly what that means.


The new LYGG Calculator translates travel data into measurable impact. It helps businesses quantify how much time, money, and CO₂ can be saved when regional travel becomes smart, direct, and demand-based.


Because in modern business travel, the real cost isn’t only in tickets, it’s in time lost on multi-modal travel paths.


Our calculator compares a LYGG route with the fastest current alternative for a crew change: one of the most time-critical travel patterns in the North Sea region.


You decide how many people travel and what their time is worth.


Try it and let us know what you think: lygg.com/calculator


It’s about time!
