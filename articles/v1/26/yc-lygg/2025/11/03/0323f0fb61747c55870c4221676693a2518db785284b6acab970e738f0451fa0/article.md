## **Fixed prices. No surprises.**


At LYGG, we’ve built our pricing around those who travel often: people and companies for whom regional routes simply need to work.


Because when travel is part of your business rhythm, you need predictability, not fluctuating fares. With LYGG, you always know your costs in advance, so budgeting and planning become effortless.


We offer serial ticket packages, one-off tickets, and tailored flights, all with **fixed prices** . That means your travel costs are always clear upfront. No surprises, no hidden fees.


## **Zone-based Pricing**


Our pricing follows a **zone-based model.** A single-zone covers one city-pair connection. A double-zone connects two cities with a quick, seamless stopover: we pick up passengers in between and continue straight on. No plane changes, just a smart connection.


Fixed, transparent prices mean there’s no need to monitor fare changes or **wait for the “right moment” to book** . You just book and go, confident that everyone pays the same fair rate.


**Every LYGG ticket includes:**


- all taxes and passenger fees
- baggage


## **Flexible Serial Tickets for Recurring Travelers**


For frequent travelers, our **serial ticket** is the most flexible and predictable way to fly.


It’s valid for three months from the first trip, and you can cancel, change names, or move your travel date free of charge up to 24 hours before departure.


**Need to switch routes?**


- Zone change: €50 to move to another single-zone
- Upgrade: €100 to move to a double-zone


We’ve designed this model to make regional flying predictable, transparent, and fair, because we know travel shouldn’t be a guessing game. It’s about keeping your operations, budgets, and people moving smoothly.
