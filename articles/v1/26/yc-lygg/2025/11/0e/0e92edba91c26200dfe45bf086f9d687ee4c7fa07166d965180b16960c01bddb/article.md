# Why We Built Serial Tickets – and Why They Matter


Jaa artikkeli:


19.11.2025


Insights


**LYGG’s serial tickets were built for one purpose: to make recurring regional travel easier to manage, no matter how often plans, projects or people change.**


Many of our clients work across regions, juggling sites, projects and shifting teams week after week. The travel patterns stay the same, even if the people don’t.


### **That’s exactly why we built serial tickets.**


They’re made for organisations with recurring travel needs. One package, multiple travellers: simple, predictable and easy to manage whether it’s regular crew rotations or project-based travel.


### **Because plans** *do* **change.** ****


Each ticket covers one-way travel on a selected route and is valid for three months from the first trip. Need to reshuffle? No problem. You can cancel, move or reassign a seat up to 24 hours before departure, free of charge. Flexibility is part of the product.


### **Fixed prices. Always.**


With our fixed pricing, budgeting becomes straightforward. You know exactly what each trip will cost – no surprises, no dynamic pricing, no extras sneaking in. All LYGG tickets always include taxes, passenger fees and baggage.


In the end, the idea behind serial tickets is simple: to make regional flying predictable, transparent and easy for the companies who rely on it every single week.
