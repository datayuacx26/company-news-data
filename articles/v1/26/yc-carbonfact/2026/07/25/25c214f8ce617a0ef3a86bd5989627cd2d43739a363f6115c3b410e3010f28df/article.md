# Carbonfact Completes ZQ+ Wool LCA for Zentera

In Carbonfact customers’ product data, wool regularly represents just 1-10% of a product's weight while driving 35-50% of its total emissions. Over


[75% of that impact comes from the sheep themselves:](https://www.carbonfact.com/blog/knowledge/carbon-wool) methane from enteric fermentation and nitrous oxide from manure, before the fiber ever reaches a mill.


That imbalance is exactly why the underlying farm data matters so much.[Zentera Wool Company](https://www.zenterawool.com/source-wool#source-wool-contact) , formerly The New Zealand Merino Company, is the organization behind the ZQ certification and ZQ+. Zentera works with over 800 growers across New Zealand, Australia, and South Africa, connecting high-quality wool grown with care for animals, people, and land with global brand and supply chain partners.


Carbonfact and Zentera have completed a full life-cycle assessment (LCA) of ZQ+ greasy wool, built entirely on primary data collected directly from 28 ZQ+ farms.

- ZQ+ wool is traceable back to the farm it was grown on and meets the highest level of animal-welfare and regenerative agriculture requirements.

## What Is ZQ+ Greasy Wool


[ZQ+ greasy wool](https://www.zenterawool.com/source-wool#source-wool-contact) comes from farms that meet Zentera's animal-welfare and traceability standard (wool is tracked back to the specific farms it came from), plus additional regenerative agriculture requirements covering soil health, biodiversity, and greenhouse gas sequestration.


Greasy wool is raw wool as it comes off the sheep, still carrying lanolin (a waxy, waterproofing substance sheep secrete), dirt, and vegetable matter, before washing or any further processing. It is the earliest point in the supply chain where a wool footprint can be measured, ahead of variation added later by different washing or spinning facilities.


Brands that use ZQ+ wool are supporting growers to use regenerative agriculture practices to maintain and enhance the environment in which the wool is grown.


## Carbonfact Completes ZQ+ Wool LCA


The LCA for ZQ+ was built by Carbonfact's science team and Zentera based on data across three pillars:


Primary farm data:


operational data collected directly from 28 ZQ+ farms in New Zealand and Australia, over two production years (2023–2025), representing a total of 43 farm-years.


Internationally recognized methods:


a cradle-to-farm-gate LCA using IPCC 2019 Refinement for on-farm emissions, ecoinvent 3.12 for background data, and the PEF / EF 3.1 method across 16 impact categories, modeled in the open-source Brightway framework.


Full impact transparency:


a methodological report documenting how the data was collected, how the inventory was built, including which assumptions and emission factors were applied, and how results were calculated and interpreted.
