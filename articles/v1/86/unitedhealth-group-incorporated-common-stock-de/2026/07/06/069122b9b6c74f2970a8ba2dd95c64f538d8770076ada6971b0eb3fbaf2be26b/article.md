# Explore early careers jobs and internships at UnitedHealth Group

### **Empowering you from the start**


Are you looking for an opportunity to do interesting and challenging work in a role that helps you get ahead in your career? Explore opportunities with UnitedHealth Group, a family of businesses including Optum and UnitedHealthcare.


We offer a wide variety of programs for students, interns and early careers professionals, going far beyond the areas that first come to mind when you think of our business. In addition to the range of opportunities, at UnitedHealth Group you’ll discover a powerful combination of support, teamwork and growth potential.[Explore just some of the exciting development opportunities we offer](https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/careers/en/work/early-careers.html) .


“In early careers, you feel your opinions are valued at the highest level — people really listen.”


**Matthew K. Software engineer and Technology Development Program (TDP) graduate**


### **Our programs**


- Sales Internship and early careers


With options including a 10-week immersive experience and longer full-time programs, you’ll gain an understanding of the sales process, our product solutions and the role of our organization in the market.


- Cybersecurity


Our global talent development program focuses on the skills needed to thrive in our Enterprise Information Security organization.​


- People team


Interested in human resources? You’ll get hands-on experience here, with exposure to talent acquisition, employee relations, organizational development and more.


- Communications


With internships ranging from 10 weeks to two years, you’ll benefit from diverse experiences in communication areas, including a rotation with an agency partner in our longer internships.


- Marketing


Learn about various marketing roles across the enterprise and gain exposure to executives and leaders in this 10-week experience.


- UnitedHealth Group Leadership Experience (ULE)


Designed to prepare the next generation of business leaders, you’ll benefit from a variety of experiences and development opportunities, including learning from and presenting to executives.


- Consulting development


You’ll experience challenging project work, comprehensive training, executive engagement and core competency development in this 1.5-to-2.5-year program.


“The managers I had during my internship still push my growth. Now I get to lead Consulting Development Program recruiting to give that experience back.”


**Makayla M. Senior clinical recruiter and Consulting Development Program (Advisory Services) graduate**


- Actuarial


Develop into a credentialed actuary in this program that will equip you with the skills and experience to be a future business leader at UnitedHealth Group.


- Finance and accounting


In our two-year program, you’ll develop the functional skills needed to succeed in finance and accounting.


“The opportunity to grow here is incredible. You meet people you’ll learn from — and stay connected with — for years.”


**Liam J. Senior accountant and Finance & Accounting Development Program (FADP) graduate**


- Mergers and acquisitions


Combining foundational leadership training with hands-on experience, you’ll learn real-world processes in this 18-to-24-month learning track.


- Medical assistant apprenticeship


In this 18-month program combining classroom learning and clinical training, you’ll gain hands-on experience, mentorship and an industry-recognized credential to launch your long-term health care career.


- Pharmacy


Preparing you as an **** emerging pharmacist, this program will advance your clinical excellence, operational expertise and leadership skills.


- Behavioral health


Supporting you as a behavioral health practitioner on your path to licensure, you’ll build organizational knowledge and gain exposure to the nation’s largest health care system. ****


- Nursing


Designed to support you as a new nurse on your transition into professional practice, our program builds confidence, clinical competency and leadership skills.


- Analytics


Through tracks that include business analysis, data analysis and data science, you’ll benefit from targeted development opportunities that prepare you to be an analytics leader.


- Technology


Channel your curiosity into initiative in this program that encourages you to dive in, explore and ask questions. Work alongside distinguished engineers and developers pursuing innovations that improve the health care experience for everyone.


“The rotations enabled me to skill up and work with meaningful enterprise tech you don’t see in school. And with all the people I worked with, I was able to build a network that helps me find answers fast across a huge company.”


**Carson D. Software engineer and Technology Development Program (data engineering) graduate**


- Product management


In this 12-month program, you’ll learn core management skills in pathways including: strategic thinking, product lifecycle management, customer-centric mindset, communication and influence, and data literacy.


### **Discover your opportunity to grow in a career at UnitedHealth Group**


Interested in learning more about one of these programs — or opportunities in another area? At UnitedHealth Group, we can help you gain experience, build your professional network and start a rewarding career.
