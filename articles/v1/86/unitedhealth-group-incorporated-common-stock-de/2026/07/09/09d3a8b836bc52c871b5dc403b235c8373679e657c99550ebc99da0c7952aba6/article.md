# Embracing your authentic self: Celebrating Pride at UnitedHealth Group

### **Inclusion in action**


Our dedication to inclusivity extends beyond employee celebrations. We have established initiatives and resources to ensure all employees feel supported.


The PRIDE Employee Resource Group (ERG) and LGBTQ+ Inclusion Pods are pivotal in fostering an inclusive workplace. Employee Resource Groups offer a platform for employees to connect, share experiences and contribute to initiatives while Inclusion Pods provide mentorship and leadership guidance to individuals from our ERG communities. Desiree M. notes, “I am so happy here. I feel genuinely happy to come to work every day because of the fact that I am accepted, appreciated and included.”


****


### **Continuous progress**


As we celebrate Pride Month and reflect on our progress, we remain committed to enhancing our workplace culture to ensure every employee, regardless of their background or identity, feels valued and heard.


As part of our ongoing commitment, we invite you to to learn more about our[culture of belonging.](https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/careers/en/life/culture.html)
