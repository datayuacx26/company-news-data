# Culmination Bio and Cofactor Genomics Leverage Largest Biobank in the U.S. for PREDAPT Precision Medicine Clinical Trial

Culmination Bio, a new Intermountain Health Company, and Cofactor Genomics, a predictive diagnostic immunotherapy company, today announced a partnership to leverage samples and data from one of the largest biobanks in the country to fuel the development of Cofactor Genomic’s OncoPrism test in 11 cancers.
