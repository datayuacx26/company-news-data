# BRING A PLUS ONE ALONG FOR THE RIDE WITH THE SOUTHWEST AIRLINES COMPANION PASS

*Sharing adventures with a friend is just one qualifying roundtrip flight away*


DALLAS


, Aug. 3, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Southwest Airlines Co.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744827-1&h=2364222319&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.southwest.com%2Floyalty%2Fmyaccount%2Fpromotion-detail%3Fid%3D2-5M6OM4UA%26oneClickRegister%3Dtrue&a=Southwest+Airlines+Co.) (NYSE: LUV) today launches a special promotional Companion Pass® offer for Rapid Rewards® Members, making it easier than ever to fly with a family member, friend, or loved one for free.1 Eligible Customers can designate their Companion and travel with the promotional Companion Pass, which is valid for unlimited usage on qualifying flights from January 5 through March 3, 2027.1


**To qualify, Rapid Rewards Members must:**


1. [Register for the promotion](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744827-1&h=1013981124&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.southwest.com%2Floyalty%2Fmyaccount%2Fpromotion-detail%3Fid%3D2-5M6OM4UA%26oneClickRegister%3Dtrue&a=Register+for+the+promotion) and purchase a Southwest® revenue flight (one qualifying round trip or two qualifying one-ways) using Rapid Rewards points, a combination of cash and points, or any other eligible form of payment, starting today through August 6, 2026;
2. Travel by November 18, 2026; and
3. Designate a Companion to fly for free ( **does not include taxes and fees from $5.60 one-way** ) with them on qualifying flights from January 5 through March 3, 2027.


"The Companion Pass is one of the most valued benefits in travel, and this promotion makes it easier than ever for both new and existing Customers to experience the value of Rapid Rewards," said[Nandika Suri](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744827-1&h=2462295528&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.swamedia.com%2Fabout-southwest%2Four-leadership%2Fnandika-suri-MC6LG4PUTAORBXLKHLPSHKZWI7FA&a=Nandika+Suri) , Vice President of Rapid Rewards. "Whether you're enrolling for the first time or have been a Member for years, this offer provides a simple and rewarding way to earn a promotional Companion Pass and share your next adventure with someone special."


**One More Reason to Love Rapid Rewards®**


Southwest offers access to free WiFi, including ultra-fast Starlink WiFi, to all Rapid Rewards Members thanks to T-Mobile.2 Customers can[join Rapid Rewards](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744827-1&h=1000781702&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.southwest.com%2Frapid-rewards%2F&a=join+Rapid+Rewards) to unlock access to free WiFi,[tier benefits](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744827-1&h=476255916&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.southwest.com%2Frapid-rewards%2Ftiers%2F&a=tier+benefits) , and points by flying qualifying flights or spending with eligible partners. Members can purchase points to earn a reward to book their favorite destination or a new Southwest route. All Rapid Rewards rules and regulations apply at[Southwest.com/rrterms](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744827-1&h=1385293911&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.southwest.com%2Fabout-southwest%2Fterms-and-conditions%2Frapid-rewards%2F&a=Southwest.com%2Frrterms) .
