# Join Us for Investor Day Los Angeles

Join us for FPA Investor Day Los Angeles, taking place in Santa Monica, California on Wednesday, October 21, and Thursday, October 22, 2026.


This two-day event is designed to offer a comprehensive view of our investment philosophy, current market perspectives, and how we are positioning portfolios for the long term.


- The event will begin on Wednesday, October 21, with a welcome reception at Pacific Terrace at Shutters on the Beach, providing time to connect with fellow clients and the FPA team.


- Thursday, October 22, will feature investment team presentations at Casa Del Mar, along with interactive Q&A sessions. The day will conclude with a cocktail hour followed by dinner. Breakfast and lunch will be provided during the program.
