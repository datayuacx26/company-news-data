INNOVATION & ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE


## AES & AI Fund


AES and AI Fund are transforming the energy sector by co- building AI-powered companies that drive efficiency and accelerate renewable energy solutions. By combining AES’s deep energy expertise with AI Fund’s leadership in artificial intelligence, we’re tackling some of the industry’s most pressing challenges faster and smarter than ever before.


###### Renewables & battery management


Utilizing AI to improve operations across millions of pieces of connected equipment


###### Worksite safety


Improving energy industry worker safety practices to protect our workforce


###### Renewable energy finance


Ensuring energy projects access the financing they need for the sector to grow.


### A partnership built for impact


AES and AI Fund have developed a fast-track incubation


model to identify, fund and launch AI-powered energy


solutions. In just six months, our first company progressed


from concept to funding, with deployment planned for 2025.


This agile, venture-driven approach ensures that the most


impactful ideas get scaled quickly—so we can deliver next-


level results for customers and the energy sector.

### How it works


##### Strategic idea generation


AES sources AI opportunities from its operations and global energy expertise and advances a select few.


##### AI Fund incubation


AI Fund and AES collaborate to develop the most promising ideas into impact-driven viable startups.


##### Rapid deployment


Companies are launched with funding and tested in real-world scenarios.


##### Scaling for impact


The most successful solutions are expanded to drive transformation across the energy sector.
