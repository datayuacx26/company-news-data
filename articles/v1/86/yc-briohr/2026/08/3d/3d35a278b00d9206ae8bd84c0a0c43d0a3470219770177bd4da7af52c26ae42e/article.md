# How a Seamless HR Transition Kept Rental Hygiene’s Operations Running Smoothly

## Meet Rental Hygiene Services


Most people don't think about what keeps a public washroom running. The soap that's always stocked, the dryer that works, the dispenser that doesn't run out mid-shift. Rental Hygiene Services has spent over two decades making sure none of that is left to chance. Established in 2002 and operating as part of the Bidvest Group, Rental Hygiene Services has built its reputation in Singapore around one core promise: total hygiene systems that actually work. From washroom equipment to dust control mats, hand soap to paper rolls, their products serve the general public daily, in offices, buildings, and shared spaces where cleanliness isn't optional. Every product is dermatologist-tested. Every service is designed to run without interruption. Edmund Ong, who runs the Singapore operations, describes their competitive edge simply: stay ahead by going digital, and keep adding what the market needs before the market asks.
