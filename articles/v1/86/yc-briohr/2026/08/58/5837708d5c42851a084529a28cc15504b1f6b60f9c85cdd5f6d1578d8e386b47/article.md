# How Auraciel Medihealth Made More Time for People, as Its Clinic Network Grew

## Meet Auraciel Medihealth


Every clinic visit depends on more than what happens in the consultation room. Behind patient care are the people, schedules, records, and everyday processes that keep each location running.


Based in Penang, Auraciel Medihealth provides occupational healthcare and primary care services across a growing network of clinics. As the organisation expands, its aim is to become more technology-driven while maintaining high standards of patient care. That ambition also shapes how Auraciel thinks about its 100 employees.


*“We believe our employees are our greatest asset.”*


Auraciel wants to build a workplace where employees feel supported, valued, and able to grow professionally. For the team, that means having clear HR processes, fair policies, continuous learning opportunities, and digital tools that reduce administrative work rather than add to it. Employees can then spend more of their time delivering quality healthcare.
