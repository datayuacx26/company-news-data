## Key Metrics to Measure Performance Review Success


A review cycle nobody finishes isn't working, however good the software looks. Track these four alongside whichever employee performance metrics software you choose.


- **Review completion rate** is the share of employees and managers who finish on time. Below 90% usually signals a process problem, not a software one.
- **360 feedback response rate** matters just as much. A multi-rater review with two of five raters responding isn't multi-rater.
- **Goal attainment rate** tracks how many objectives people actually hit. It's a cleaner signal than review scores, which drift upward over time.
- **Calibration variance** shows how far ratings move once managers compare notes. A wide swing usually means bias, not a real performance gap.


Pair all four with[HR reporting dashboards](https://briohr.com/blog/top-hr-reporting-software/) so trends surface in real time. Engagement data from[employee engagement tools](https://briohr.com/blog/best-employee-engagement-software/) adds the missing half of the picture.


Pro Tip


If completion dips below 90%, check whether reminders reach managers, not just employees. Managers are the bottleneck in most stalled cycles, and it's rarely an engagement problem.

#### FAQs


1


** ** What Is Employee Performance Review Software?


It's software that runs goal setting, feedback collection, and review cycles in one place. The best options connect that data to payroll and employee records instead of leaving it stranded in spreadsheets.


2


** ** How Often Should Companies Conduct Performance Reviews?


Most companies run formal reviews once or twice a year, with quarterly or monthly check-ins between them. Continuous feedback is what keeps the formal cycle useful rather than a box-ticking exercise.


3


** ** Can Performance Review Software Support OKRs and Goal Tracking?


Yes. brioHR, HiBob, Darwinbox, and Workday all track objectives natively. Lighter tools like Swingvy and HR2000 stick to basic appraisal cycles without a dedicated goal-tracking module.


4


** ** Is Performance Review Software Suitable for Small Businesses?


Sometimes. Modular tools like brioHR and altHR scale down affordably to around 30 employees. Enterprise systems like Workday and Darwinbox are built for thousands and rarely make sense below that.


5


** ** Is There Free Employee Performance Review Software?
