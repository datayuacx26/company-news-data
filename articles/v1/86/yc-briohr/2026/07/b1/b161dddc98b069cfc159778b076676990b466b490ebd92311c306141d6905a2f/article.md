## Reasons to Find an Alternative to BambooHR


BambooHR is a capable, well-liked HR platform in its home market. The reasons teams in Malaysia and Singapore outgrow it have less to do with quality and more to do with fit. Here are the three that come up most often.


### No Native Malaysian or Singaporean Payroll


This is the dealbreaker for most regional teams. BambooHR has no native MY or SG payroll engine, which means no EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB, CPF, SDL, or LHDN and Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore filing out of the box. You end up bolting on a separate payroll tool and reconciling between two systems every month, which is exactly where errors creep in. brioHR handles all of it natively, with statutory updates rolling out automatically, so payroll and HR live in one place.


### The Cost of Disconnected Systems


When your HRIS doesn't run payroll, you pay twice: once for each subscription, and again in the hours your team loses moving data between them.[Research from Deloitte found that HR professionals spend up to 57% of their time on administrative tasks](https://www.aihr.com/blog/hr-automation/) , much of it re-entering the same information. A connected system removes that duplicate work. Because brioHR ties[time and attendance](https://briohr.com/products/time-attendance/) , leave, and claims directly into payroll, the data only gets entered once, which is also how teams cut the payroll errors that damage trust and[employee retention](https://briohr.com/blog/improve-employee-retention-hr-professionals/) .


### Support and Compliance That Fit Your Timezone


Global platforms rarely offer local support hours, and that gap bites hardest at month-end when a payroll question can't wait until a US morning. Local statutory changes also land faster than a global roadmap can absorb them. brioHR runs on Malaysian and Singaporean support hours with an average response time under three minutes and a 96% positive feedback rate, and its compliance updates track local rules as they change.
