#### FAQ


1


** ** What is the difference between time tracking software and time and attendance software?


Time tracking software logs how long tasks or projects take, mainly used for billing or productivity analysis. Time and attendance software records when employees start and end work, including breaks, overtime, and absences, and typically connects directly to payroll.


2


** ** Can time and attendance software prevent buddy punching?


Yes. GPS clock-in restricts location-based fraud. Geo-fencing goes further by rejecting clock-ins from outside approved zones. Facial recognition eliminates it almost entirely. Employees have to be physically present to clock in.


3


** ** Does time and attendance software work for remote teams?


Yes. Mobile GPS clock-in, geo-fencing, and digital timesheet approvals all work for remote and distributed teams. The key is choosing a tool that supports multi-location configuration rather than a fixed single-office radius.


4


** ** How does time and attendance software connect to payroll?


The best systems link natively: approved hours, overtime, and breaks flow automatically into the payroll engine without manual exports. This removes errors, saves admin time, and ensures employees are paid accurately every cycle.


5


** ** Is time and attendance software worth it for enterprise businesses?


Yes, especially when shift complexity, compliance exposure, and headcount make manual tracking impractical. Enterprise teams typically benefit most from real-time monitoring, automated overtime rules, and analytics that flag attendance issues before they become payroll problems.
