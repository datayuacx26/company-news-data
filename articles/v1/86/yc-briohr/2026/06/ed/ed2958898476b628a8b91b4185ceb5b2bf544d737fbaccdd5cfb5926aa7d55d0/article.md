# Best Pulse Survey Software for Employee Feedback (2026)

## How to Choose the Best Pulse Survey Software


The right tool depends on how you plan to listen, who needs to act on the data, and how the results connect to the rest of your HR. Here are the factors that matter most.


### Survey Frequency and Automation


Pulse surveys work because they’re short and regular, so look for software that lets you schedule recurring surveys and automate reminders without manual effort. Annual-only surveys miss the moments that drive turnover, while a steady cadence catches issues while you can still fix them. The easier it is to set frequency and let the system handle the rest, the more consistent your listening becomes. brioHR does this well by letting HR set frequency and anonymity once, then launching to mobile and desktop in minutes so response rates stay high. If you’re mapping out a program, brioHR’s[best practices for running pulse surveys](https://briohr.com/blog/best-practices-employee-pulse-surveys/) is a practical guide.


Pro Tip


Start with a short, fixed set of recurring questions and add new ones sparingly. Consistency lets you track trends over time, while a long survey every month quickly leads to fatigue and falling response rates.


### Anonymity and Response Rates


Honest feedback depends on trust, so your tool should make anonymity clear and easy to configure. Employees who believe responses are confidential are far more likely to answer truthfully, which is what makes the data worth acting on. There’s a line to respect here too, since listening should never tip into surveillance, a tension brioHR explores in its piece on[whether you can monitor your employees](https://briohr.com/blog/can-you-spy-on-your-employees/) . Equally important is reach: a tool that works cleanly on mobile will capture frontline and deskless staff that desktop-only systems miss. brioHR supports configurable anonymity and a no-training mobile experience, and you can review its approach to[data security](https://briohr.com/security/) if confidentiality is a priority for your team.


### Analytics and Action Planning


Collecting responses is the easy part; the value is in what you do next. Strong pulse survey software organizes results automatically, surfaces trends, and gives managers visibility into their own teams so action doesn’t bottleneck with HR. That manager layer matters more than it looks, since Gallup’s research attributes around 70% of the variance in team engagement to the manager, which is why[one widely cited analysis](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/349484/state-of-the-global-workplace.aspx) puts so much weight on equipping them. The best setups also connect sentiment to outcomes like performance and turnover, rather than leaving engagement as a number with no context. brioHR’s live dashboards and[HR reporting and analytics](https://briohr.com/products/hr-reporting-software/) do exactly that, tying feedback to the rest of your people data.
