## FAQs


### What is the best way to track employee time?


The best way is software that captures hours automatically and syncs them to payroll. Automated clock-in, mobile GPS, and timesheet approvals remove manual entry and disputed hours. For teams in Malaysia and Singapore, an all-in-one platform like brioHR keeps time, overtime, and pay in one connected system.


### Is time tracking good for employees?


Yes, when it's transparent. Clear, verified time records protect honest employees, ensure accurate overtime pay, and remove arguments about hours worked. The key is choosing a tool that's easy to use on mobile, so logging time takes seconds rather than feeling like surveillance.


### How do I track my working hours?


Use a digital time tracking tool with mobile clock-in. Employees clock in and out from a phone or device, the system logs hours automatically, and managers approve timesheets before they feed into payroll. This is far more accurate than paper logs or spreadsheets.


### What's the difference between time tracking and time and attendance software?


Time tracking logs how long work or tasks take, often for productivity or billing. Time and attendance records when employees start, stop, and take breaks, then feeds those hours into payroll. All-in-one platforms like brioHR combine both, so productivity time tracking and pay stay in one system.


### Does employee time tracking software work for remote teams?


Yes. Mobile GPS clock-in, geo-fencing, and digital timesheet approvals all work for remote and distributed teams. The key is choosing a tool that supports multi-location setup rather than a single fixed-office radius.
