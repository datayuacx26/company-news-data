# Aspire Partners with Flagright for AI-Driven Financial Crime Compliance

Flagright, the AI operating system for financial crime compliance, today announced a partnership with Aspire, the Singapore-headquartered global fintech serving over 50,000 businesses, to strengthen AI-enabled compliance across Aspire's business finance workflows.


As business finance platforms scale across products, customers, and transaction types, compliance teams need adaptable infrastructure that can connect transaction monitoring, screening, risk scoring, case management, quality assurance, and governance in one operating layer.


> "As AI makes fraud and financial crime more sophisticated and harder to catch, compliance infrastructure has to get smarter just as fast,” said Jaco Struik, VP of Fraud & Fincrime at Aspire. “Flagright gives our teams transaction monitoring, screening, risk scoring, and case management workflows that help us stay ahead of that curve while maintaining strong governance."


With Flagright, Aspire’s fincrime teams can:


- [Monitor transaction activity with configurable AML controls](https://www.flagright.com/transaction-monitoring)
- [Screen users and support transaction screening workflows](https://www.flagright.com/watchlist-screening)
- [Apply risk scoring to prioritise reviews and strengthen decisioning](https://www.flagright.com/dynamic-risk-scoring)
- Use[case management](https://www.flagright.com/case-management) , quality assurance, ontology, simulation, and advanced analytics to improve compliance operations


> “Business finance platforms need financial crime compliance systems that are fast, flexible, and governable,” said Baran Ozkan, Co founder and CEO at Flagright. “Aspire’s work with Flagright shows where the market is going: unified AML operations, AI assisted workflows, stronger governance, and a compliance layer that can evolve as the business evolves.”


## **About Aspire**


Aspire is on a mission to reinvent centuries-old corporate banking rules for modern businesses. The company is building a unified financial operating system that combines regulated financial infrastructure with enterprise software, helping businesses manage money with greater control, visibility and confidence as they scale globally.


Headquartered in Singapore, Aspire serves more than 50,000 businesses across 30+ markets and employs over 500 people across nine countries. Aspire is backed by leading investors including Peak XV, Lightspeed, Y Combinator and PayPal.


## **About Flagright**


Flagright is the AI operating system for financial crime compliance, trusted by fintechs, banks, and financial institutions in 30+ countries. The platform unifies transaction monitoring, watchlist screening, risk scoring, case management, investigations, reporting, governance, and AI agents in one configurable, audit ready system.
