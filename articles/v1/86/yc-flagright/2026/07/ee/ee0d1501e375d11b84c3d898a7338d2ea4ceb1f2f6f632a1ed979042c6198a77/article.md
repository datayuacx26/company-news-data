# Tipalti Partners with Flagright for AI-Powered AML Compliance Across Global Payables

‍ **San Francisco, Calif. — July 28, 2026 —**[Flagright](https://www.flagright.com/) , the AI operating system for financial crime compliance, today announced that[Tipalti](https://tipalti.com/) has partnered with Flagright as one of the tools supporting its compliance infrastructure.


Flagright’s compliance stack provides AI-native AML compliance workflows with enhanced speed, control, explainability, and auditability in a unified AI operating system. Flagright removes operational friction through standardized global workflows, reduced manual review, and increased visibility from alert creation through case closure and reporting.


> “Compliance has always been core to how we operate at scale,” said Manish Vrishaketu, Chief Customer and Operations Officer at Tipalti. “Flagright complements our existing framework by helping us reinforce the processes that support global growth and navigate an increasingly dynamic regulatory landscape.”


Flagright’s next-gen financial crime infrastructure supports:


- [Real-time Transaction Monitoring](https://www.flagright.com/transaction-monitoring) : Configurable typologies for AP, mass payouts, cross-border payments, and marketplace workflows.
- [Dynamic Risk Scoring](https://www.flagright.com/customer-risk-scoring) : Risk scoring for counterparties and transactions that adapts to behavioral change and contextual signals.
- [Enterprise Case Management](https://www.flagright.com/case-management) : Intelligent triage, queue management, QA workflows, and full audit trails, connecting detection, investigations, and reporting.
- [AI Forensics](https://www.flagright.com/ai-forensics) : Reconstructed complex payment trails, clustered related activity, and support for faster evidence-based investigations.
- Open Data and Reporting: Analyst-led exploration and management dashboards that integrate with existing data and tooling.


> “Financial crime compliance has shifted from monitoring transactions to connecting risk signals, governance, and explainability across payment operations,” said Madhu G. Nadig, Co-founder and CTO of Flagright. “We’re excited to support Tipalti with greater visibility and operational efficiency while continuing to set a high standard for global payments.”


## **About Flagright**


Flagright is the AI operating system for financial crime compliance trusted by more than 100 financial institutions across 30+ countries. Its unified, risk-based platform brings together transaction monitoring, watchlist screening, investigations, and governance in a single audit-ready system that can be deployed in as little as two weeks. Compliance teams can design and refine controls without code using tools such as a scenario builder, sub-second APIs, dynamic risk profiling, and simulation capabilities that help reduce false positives and improve decision accuracy. The platform also integrates auditable AI agents within governed investigation workflows, maintaining human oversight while ensuring transparent and defensible decisions across jurisdictions. Organizations replacing fragmented compliance tools with Flagright report up to[93% fewer false positives](https://www.flagright.com/use-case/reducing-false-positives) and up to 80% lower compliance costs.
