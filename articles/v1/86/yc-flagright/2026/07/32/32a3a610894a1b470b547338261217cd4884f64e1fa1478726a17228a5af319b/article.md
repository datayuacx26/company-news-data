# UniCredit Selects Flagright for Enterprise Risk Monitoring

Flagright, the AI operating system for financial crime compliance, today announced that UniCredit in Poland uses Flagright to support[transaction monitoring](https://www.flagright.com/transaction-monitoring) ,[customer screening](https://www.flagright.com/watchlist-screening) ,[customer risk scoring](https://www.flagright.com/dynamic-risk-scoring) , and[case management](https://www.flagright.com/case-management) across its digital banking operations.


As digital banking operations scale, compliance teams need systems that combine real time visibility, risk-based decisioning, operational control, and audit ready governance. UniCredit is using Flagright to support financial crime prevention workflows with configurable controls built for modern banking operations.


> “Financial crime prevention in digital banking requires controls that are fast, risk based, and explainable,” said Konrad Krupiński, Financial Crime Prevention Owner at UniCredit NV/SA. “Flagright gives our teams configurable transaction monitoring, customer screening, risk scoring, and case management in a unified operating layer, helping us make consistent decisions while maintaining strong governance.”


With Flagright, UniCredit’s fincrime teams can:


- Monitor monetary and non monetary activity with configurable transaction monitoring
- Screen customers and support customer risk scoring workflows
- Manage investigations through case management and clear decision trails
- Use quality assurance, audit logs, notifications, and advanced workflows to strengthen governance


> “Bank grade financial crime compliance requires flexibility, control, and trust in the system,” said Baran Ozkan, Co founder and CEO at Flagright. “UniCredit’s use of Flagright reflects a broader shift among sophisticated institutions toward unified compliance infrastructure that teams can configure, govern, and operate at scale.”


## **About UniCredit**


UniCredit is a pan European commercial bank serving clients across retail, corporate, and institutional banking. Its digital banking operations in Poland support customers through a mobile led banking experience across retail and business banking services.


## **About Flagright**


Flagright is the AI operating system for financial crime compliance, trusted by fintechs, banks, and financial institutions in 30+ countries. The platform unifies transaction monitoring, watchlist screening, risk scoring, case management, investigations, reporting, governance, and AI agents in one configurable, audit ready system.
