# Reap Collaborates with Flagright on Financial Crime Compliance Infrastructure

Flagright, the AI operating system for financial crime compliance, announced today that its unified, risk-based platform is supporting Reap’s financial crime compliance operations as Reap expands its stablecoin-powered payment infrastructure across Mexico and the broader Americas.


Reap recently expanded into Mexico through its Money Transmitter Registry, strengthening its ability to support localized payment services and cross-border connectivity across the Asia-Latin America corridor. As Reap continues building financial infrastructure across fiat and stablecoin rails, financial crime compliance becomes a critical operating layer for responsible scale.


Through Flagright, Reap’s compliance teams can[monitor transactions in real time](https://www.flagright.com/transaction-monitoring) , configure risk-based rules, identify high-risk activity through[automated risk scoring](https://www.flagright.com/customer-risk-scoring) , and investigate alerts in a[centralized, auditable case management environment](https://www.flagright.com/case-management) . Together, Reap and Flagright collaborate in the use of this tool to maintain strong financial crime controls while supporting fast-moving cards and payment use cases.


> “Reap represents the kind of sophisticated financial infrastructure company that Flagright is built for: fast-moving, multi-market, and operating across increasingly complex payment rails,” said Baran Özkan, Co-founder and CEO of Flagright. “As stablecoin-enabled payments move into enterprise use cases, financial crime compliance cannot sit inside fragmented tools or manual workflows. Financial institutions need a unified AI operating system that can monitor, investigate, optimize, and govern risk in real time while remaining audit-ready. That is the infrastructure layer we are building at Flagright.”


The expansion reflects a broader shift in global payments. Stablecoins are moving beyond crypto-native activity into practical financial infrastructure for cross-border payments, treasury movement, card issuing, and embedded finance. But as payment flows become faster and more global, compliance teams need systems that can keep pace with new transaction patterns, jurisdictional requirements, and financial crime typologies.


> “Mexico is a strategic step in Reap’s broader Americas expansion and our mission to make global money movement more accessible for businesses,” said Darryl Wan, Head of Legal and Compliance, Reap. “As we extend stablecoin-powered infrastructure for cards and payments across new corridors, trust and compliance need to scale with speed. We are committed to working with partners like Flagright to deliver card issuing, stablecoin corporate cards and cross border payments.”


Flagright’s AI operating system for financial crime compliance brings together transaction monitoring,[watchlist screening](https://www.flagright.com/watchlist-screening) , investigations, and[governance in a single risk-based platform](https://www.flagright.com/use-case/governance) . Its AI capabilities are embedded across recommendations, system optimization, and alert investigation workflows, helping financial institutions improve decision quality, reduce manual workload, and maintain defensible compliance operations at scale.


The announcement builds on the existing partnership between Reap and Flagright and reinforces a shared commitment to building trusted, compliant, and enterprise-grade financial infrastructure for global businesses.
