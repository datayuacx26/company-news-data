# SME Bank Selects Flagright for Real‑Time Transaction Monitoring and Watchlist Screening

Flagright, the AI-native, no-code platform for transaction monitoring and AML compliance, is pleased to welcome SME Bank as its newest client. Following a successful evaluation of Flagright’s platform, SME Bank has deployed Flagright to centralize real-time transaction monitoring, watchlist screening, and investigations; strengthening its financial crime controls while keeping operations fast and scalable.


SME Bank is a Vilnius-headquartered digital bank built for business clients, with a specialized banking license issued by the European Central Bank. With a mission to remove obstacles to business growth, SME Bank supports small and medium-sized enterprises with digital day-to-day banking services and lending solutions as it continues to expand its footprint across the region.


With Flagright, SME Bank will be able to:


- [Monitor activity in real time with configurable, risk-based rules](https://www.flagright.com/transaction-monitoring) across customer and transaction flows.
- [Screen customers and counterparties against relevant watchlists](https://www.flagright.com/watchlist-screening) , with clear alerts and investigation-ready context.
- [Investigate alerts end-to-end in a unified workspace](https://www.flagright.com/case-management) with structured case management and audit-ready documentation.
- Apply granular roles and permissions, including maker-checker controls, to support consistent decision-making as the team grows.


## **About SME Bank**


SME Bank is a digital bank dedicated to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), rapidly expanding its activities and continuing to implementing innovative solutions tailored specifically to the needs of this sector. The bank is a reliable partner who offers fast and convenient financial services that allow SMEs to focus on their growth and development. The Bank operates in Baltic states, Finland, and the Netherlands.
