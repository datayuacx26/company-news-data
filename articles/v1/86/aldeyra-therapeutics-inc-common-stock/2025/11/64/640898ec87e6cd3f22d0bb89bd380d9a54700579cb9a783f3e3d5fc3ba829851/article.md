# Aldeyra Therapeutics to Participate in the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference in London

[PDF Version](https://ir.aldeyra.com/node/12966/pdf)


LEXINGTON, Mass.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov. 11, 2025--[Aldeyra Therapeutics, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.aldeyra.com%2F&esheet=54355673&newsitemid=20251111355562&lan=en-US&anchor=Aldeyra+Therapeutics%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=fc38fe9283def477df945d4ca105f86c) (Nasdaq: ALDX) (Aldeyra), a biotechnology company devoted to discovering and developing innovative therapies designed to treat immune-mediated diseases, today announced that Todd C. Brady, M.D., Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at the 2025 Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference in London, England.


Dr. Brady’s conversation with Clara Dong, Ph.D., Vice President, Biotechnology Equity Research at Jefferies LLC., is scheduled to begin at 11:30 a.m. GMT Thursday, November 20, 2025. To view the live webcast, log in to the Investors & Media section of the Aldeyra website at[https://ir.aldeyra.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.aldeyra.com&esheet=54355673&newsitemid=20251111355562&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fir.aldeyra.com&index=2&md5=fd6638ff78ec5cdb93dbc626cfbaf74a) . Following the fireside chat, the webcast will be archived for 90 days.


**About Aldeyra**


Aldeyra Therapeutics is a biotechnology company devoted to discovering innovative therapies designed to treat immune-mediated diseases. Our approach is to develop pharmaceuticals that modulate protein systems, instead of directly inhibiting or activating single protein targets, with the goal of optimizing multiple pathways at once while minimizing toxicity. Our product candidates include RASP (reactive aldehyde species) modulators ADX‑248, ADX-246, and chemically related molecules for the potential treatment of systemic and retinal immune-mediated diseases. Our late-stage product candidates are reproxalap, a RASP modulator for the potential treatment of dry eye disease and allergic conjunctivitis, and ADX-2191, a novel formulation of intravitreal methotrexate for the potential treatment of primary vitreoretinal lymphoma and retinitis pigmentosa.
