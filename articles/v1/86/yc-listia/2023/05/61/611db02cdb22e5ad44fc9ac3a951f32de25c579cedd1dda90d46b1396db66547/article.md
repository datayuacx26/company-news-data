# Send Seller Cards outside of Listia

Hi everyone! You can now send your[Seller Cards](https://www.listia.com/account/digital_collectibles) outside of Listia, to any email address. This is a great way to expand your personal brand and **create a custom “digital business card”** that links to all of your social and marketplace profiles – like Instagram, Twitter, eBay, Etsy, etc!


[View Your Digital Collectibles](https://www.listia.com/account/digital_collectibles)


To send a Seller Card to one of your customers or fans outside of Listia, just click the “gift it” button as usual. From there, you can enter the recipient’s email address and they will receive the card via PassCard.ai – so they won’t need to create a separate account on Listia.


Now you can reward all your customers, even the ones outside of Listia! Have fun with it and let us know where you end up using your Seller cards 🙂
