# Earn Cash for the Holidays by Selling on Listia!

You read that correctly!


During the Holiday Season, for higher valued items (items you determine have a value over $20), you can list Get it Now Auctions on Listia’s web version and earn real money for those items!


For now, the ability to LIST items for real money is only available on the Web Version of Listia, but you will be able to BUY both on the App and Web. Selling for real money with the App is in the works and will be released soon!


Do not worry! Everything on Listia, in terms of PTS, will remain the same.


Rewards store, offers, buying and selling, PTS purchases, EXP, badges, and your Feedback will all still remain as they are. We know our community loves the trading aspect of items for PTS, so we will not be taking that away.


All payments are handled through our third party payments processor, Stripe. From your Listia Account, you will need to set up your Stripe account before having the ability to list items for real money.


Please send in a Support Ticket if you have any additional questions!


All Listia listing rules apply and listings that violate these rules will be removed.
