# First of its kind Promo on Listia! 🥳

List a Physical Item that goes for 50,000 or more PTS and receive 20% bonus on the final sale price!


(No Gift Cards or other Digital Items that are normally digital, but can be listed as physical)


🚨 All Listia Listing Rules Apply. Failure to follow them will result in no bonus PTS given.


🔔 Rules: List a Physical Item between Now and the End of Monday September 4th and receive a 20% bonus on the final sale price once the item has been shipped and positive feedback has been given by the Winner.


After receiving positive feedback simply send in a support ticket with the Auction link(s) that qualify.
