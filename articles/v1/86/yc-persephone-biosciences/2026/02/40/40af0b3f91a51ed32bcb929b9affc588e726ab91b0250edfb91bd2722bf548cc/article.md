# Meet the Microbes

The four probiotic strains in Persephone’s synbiotic — and why they matter.


Inside every Persephone packet is something remarkable: a precise blend of beneficial bacteria that help shape your baby’s gut and immune system during the most important window of development. These aren’t generic probiotics — they’re the foundation of a healthy gut.


## Why Bifidobacterium Matter


Bifidobacterium are some of the very first microbes to colonize a baby’s gut. They feed on Human Milk Oligosaccharides (HMOs), the special sugars in breastmilk, and help train the immune system, support digestion, and keep inflammation low. But our[My Baby Biome study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08274-7) — the largest U.S. study of the infant microbiome — revealed that nine out of ten babies are missing key Bifidobacterium species. Without them, the gut can become unbalanced, digestion becomes less efficient, and the immune system receives different developmental signals.


Persephone’s Daily Synergistic Synbiotic is designed to restore these missing microbes using the exact nutrients they’ve evolved to eat.


## The Four Keystone Strains


Persephone’s formulation includes four strains identified through our infant microbiome research. Each plays a distinct role, and together they help rebuild a balanced, resilient gut.


**Bifidobacterium infantis (PB-01 and PB-02)** :


These two infantis strains are specialists in digesting the full range of HMOs found in breastmilk. As they break down HMOs, they produce beneficial compounds that help shape a calm, well-regulated immune system and support smooth digestion. Because every baby’s microbiome is different, using two complementary strains helps ensure this essential species can take hold.


**Bifidobacterium longum (PB-03)** :


As your baby moves beyond breastmilk, this strain helps bridge the early-life microbiome to one that can process a more varied diet. It digests both HMOs and plant fibers, supporting stool regularity, gut barrier strength, and a healthy transition into solids.


**Bifidobacterium breve (PB-04)** :


Like


B. longum


, this strain grows with your little one, processing both HMOs and plant fibers.


B. breve


also plays an important role in immune balance as seen in the[My Baby Biome Study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08274-7) .


## Why Pair Probiotics with HMOs?


Probiotics need the right fuel to thrive. That’s why Persephone pairs these four strains with a blend of four Human Milk Oligosaccharides — nature’s perfect prebiotics. HMOs give these microbes the nourishment they expect in early life, creating a true synergistic synbiotic where probiotics and prebiotics work together to amplify benefits.


## What Makes Persephone Different


Most probiotics for babies use generic strains. Persephone’s strains come directly from infant microbiome research and are paired with nature-identical HMOs and vitamin D for comprehensive gut and immune support. Each sachet contains everything these keystone bacteria need to establish themselves and flourish.


## The Takeaway


Persephone helps replenish the beneficial microbes modern babies are missing. By restoring these four foundational strains — and feeding them the nutrients they depend on — each packet supports a stronger gut, a more balanced immune system, and a healthier start to life.
