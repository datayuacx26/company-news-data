# What Parents Report After One Month of Supporting Their Baby’s Gut

When you’re caring for a baby or toddler, progress isn’t measured in perfection — it’s measured in how the days and nights feel. More comfortable digestion. Calmer moods. Better sleep. Fewer moments where something just feels “off.”


Parents are often the first to notice when those shifts happen, especially when it comes to the gut.


To better understand what families experience after supporting their child’s gut microbiome, we surveyed forty-seven parents who used Persephone’s Synergistic Synbiotic consistently for at least one month. The survey included 21 infants and 26 toddlers, and while every child is different, clear patterns emerged in what parents noticed during those first weeks. We continue to survey families and collect additional data to deepen our understanding of these early experiences over time.

## What these changes can look like in everyday life


### More comfortable digestion and more predictable poops


After one month,


nearly three-quarters of infants and toddlers


were reported to have improvements in digestion and stool consistency. Parents often described bowel movements becoming easier, more regular, and less uncomfortable.


For many families, this is one of the earliest signs that the gut is functioning more smoothly. And when digestion feels better, parents often notice ripple effects beyond diaper changes — including comfort, mood, and feeding.


### Improvements in crying, colic, and fussiness


Among infants,


63% of parents reported fewer episodes of excessive crying or colic


. For families navigating ongoing tummy discomfort, even modest reductions in crying can feel life-changing.


More than half of parents noticed improvements in mood or fussiness


. This reinforces what growing research continues to show: the gut and nervous system are closely connected, and when the gut feels more balanced, kids often feel more comfortable overall.


### Better sleep and more settled routines


After one month,


nearly half of infants and toddlers


were reported to have improvements in sleep quality or duration. Similar numbers of parents noticed positive changes in appetite.


These shifts often go hand in hand. When digestion is more comfortable and the gut is less inflamed, children may settle more easily at night and feel more comfortable eating during the day.


### Fewer diaper rashes


Many parents also reported improvements in diaper rash, particularly in infants. Gut health and skin health are closely linked through what’s often called the


gut–skin axis


. Balanced gut bacteria can help normalize stool pH, which may create a less irritating environment for sensitive skin and reduce the frequency or severity of diaper rash.


## The takeaway: what one month of gut support can mean


This parent survey suggests that


after one month of consistent use


, Persephone’s Synergistic Synbiotic may support digestion, comfort, sleep, and overall well-being in both infants and toddlers.


Results will always vary from child to child. But these real-world experiences reflect something important:


supporting the gut doesn’t just affect digestion, it can influence how babies feel throughout their entire day.
