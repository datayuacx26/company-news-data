# How to Make Persephone’s Synergistic Synbiotic Part of Your Child’s Da

Adding something new to your baby’s routine can feel like one more thing on a busy list, but supporting your little one’s gut health doesn’t have to be complicated. Persephone’s


Synergistic Synbiotic


was designed to fit seamlessly into daily life, helping you nurture your baby’s developing microbiome.


## What Does it Mean to be Part of Daily Life?


Daily use of the synbiotic is important. Parents with consistent use reported notable benefits after a month of use, whereas those that did not reported little to no benefit. Your baby’s gut is home to billions of microbes still learning to work together, and daily use encourages:


-


Steady colonization of good bacteria in the gut


-


Better absorption of nutrients


-


Ongoing immune support


With consistent use, colonization is often observed by one week, and health benefits by one month. To promote daily use, we recommend attaching it to something you already do every day. Try:


-


Keeping the sachets near your baby’s bottles, vitamins, or feeding supplies.


-


Setting a reminder on your phone at your chosen time.


-


Making it part of your “morning milk” or “bedtime bottle” ritual.


All this being said, don’t fret if you miss a day. Our microbes are resilient, and one day off supplementation won’t stop them from doing their job. Just start the next day you are able.


## When to Give the Synergistic Synbiotic


Choose a time that works best for your family and stick with it each day. Consistency helps the microbes establish a natural rhythm in the gut. Many parents find these times easiest:


-


Morning feeding:


Mix the sachet into milk, formula, or a small spoonful of food right after your baby wakes up.


-


For breakfast:


If your baby eats solids, it’s easy to mix into a soft food like yogurt, applesauce, or oatmeal.


-


Evening routine:


Some parents prefer giving it before bedtime feeding to pair gut support with their nighttime wind-down, like with a bottle of milk.


## How to Give It


Each sachet is pre-measured for convenience. Simply:


1.


Open the packet and add it to breast milk, formula, or a small amount of water or soft food.


2.


Stir or swirl gently until it’s fully dissolved.


3.


Feed immediately—probiotics are most active right after mixing.


4.


Quick tips for babies who are exclusively breastfed:


-


- Mix with a small amount of expressed milk and spread on the nipple before latching.


- Offer the dissolved mixture by spoon, oral syringe, or a tiny cup at the start of the feed.
- Place a little on a clean finger for your baby to suck before latching.
- Even if babies don’t consume the whole packet, they are still getting significant exposure to these beneficial microbes.


If it’s your baby’s first time taking a synbiotic, consider starting with half a sachet for the first few days to let their gut adjust. Then move to the full daily amount. A gradual introduction helps prevent minor tummy changes like gas or softer stools while the microbiome finds its new balance.
