# How Virtual Cards Empower Global Teams

#### **Modern Expense Management for a Global Workforce**


Managing expenses, subscriptions, and employee payments in a remote-first world requires more than just physical corporate cards. Traditional cards are slow, costly, and difficult to distribute to a global team. Virtual cards offer a modern solution, providing instant issuance, easy management, and enhanced security for businesses of all sizes.


‍


#### **A Modern Solution with Munify’s USDC Virtual Cards**


Munify’s USDC virtual cards can be generated instantly and assigned to anyone in your organization, no matter where they are located. Whether onboarding a freelancer in Europe or allocating a budget for your marketing team in Asia, you can issue and fund cards in seconds. Each card comes with customizable limits, spending rules, and real-time tracking, giving your finance team complete visibility and control.


Lost a card or notice suspicious activity? Cancel or replace it instantly with no disruption. Virtual cards also reduce the risk of fraud since they are not physical and can be deleted or reissued as needed.


‍


#### **Built for Flexibility and Efficiency**


Virtual cards streamline procurement, simplify expense tracking, and eliminate the need for international wire transfers. They’re ideal for software subscriptions, travel expenses, and remote payroll. Every transaction is logged and categorized, making reporting and reconciliation straightforward for finance teams managing hundreds of cards across multiple regions.
