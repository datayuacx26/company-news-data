Global payouts are essential for today’s businesses, from marketplaces and fintech platforms to global payroll and the gig economy. Yet, traditional banking systems remain slow, expensive, and riddled with red tape, making cross-border payments a major bottleneck for growt. Delays, high fees, and complex compliance requirements often frustrate both payers and recipients.
