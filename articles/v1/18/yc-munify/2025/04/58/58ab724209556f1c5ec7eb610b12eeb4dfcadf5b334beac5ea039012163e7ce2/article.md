# Sending Money to Egypt in Times of Inflation: A Smarter Approach to Supporting Families Back Home

Inflation has become a reality that families in Egypt face every day. Prices of food, housing, and basic goods continue to rise, making it harder for households to stretch their budgets. For Egyptians working abroad, sending money home is more important than ever—but traditional remittance channels make it harder than it should be.


Money transfer services often charge **high fees** and give **unfavorable exchange rates** , meaning families back home receive less than what was intended. Bank transfers can take days to arrive, creating stress when urgent expenses need to be covered.


The USD is becoming increasingly important as a stable way to preserve value. Instead of losing purchasing power through delays and poor conversion rates, families benefit most when transfers arrive quickly and in USD.


Munify provides a modern way to support loved ones in Egypt. With a USD account and instant digital transfers, money sent from abroad reaches family members quickly, safely, and with more value intact. They can spend with virtual cards or withdraw when needed—giving them flexibility in a tough economic climate.


At a time when every dollar matters, sending money home should be **fast, fair, and supportive** . That’s the future Munify is building for Egyptians worldwide.
