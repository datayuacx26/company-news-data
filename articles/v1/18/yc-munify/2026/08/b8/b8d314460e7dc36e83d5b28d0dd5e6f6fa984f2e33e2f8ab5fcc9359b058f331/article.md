# How to Open a US Business Account Without an LLC or US Address

For founders outside the US, a US business account unlocks a lot: paying international vendors, receiving payments from US customers, and holding revenue in a stable currency. The problem is that most US banks require an LLC, a US address, and a Social Security number before they will even start the process.


Here is how founders in emerging markets can open a US business banking account without any of those.


‍


#### Why non-US founders need a US business account


If your business serves international customers or works with US-based platforms and clients, operating in dollars removes friction on both sides. A US business account lets you:


- Receive payments from US customers and platforms without forcing them into an international transfer.
- Pay suppliers, contractors, and software vendors in dollars.
- Hold revenue in USD instead of converting every inflow into a volatile local currency.


The traditional blocker has always been the paperwork. That is what has changed.
