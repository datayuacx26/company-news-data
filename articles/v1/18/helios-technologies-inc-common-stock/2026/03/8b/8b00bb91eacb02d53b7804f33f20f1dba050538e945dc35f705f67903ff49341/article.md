# Helios Technologies Continues Long-Standing Partnership with Jacuzzi® Group, Powering All-New J5™ Collection

# Helios Technologies Continues Long-Standing Partnership with Jacuzzi® Group, Powering All-New J5™ Collection


March 19, 2026 9:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_b5483d7e150128d40b4ca2c6294ca702/heliostechnologies/news/2026-03-19_Helios_Technologies_Continues_Long_Standing_357.pdf)


SARASOTA, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Helios Technologies, Inc.](https://www.heliostechnologies.com/) (NYSE: HLIO) (“Helios” or the “Company”), a global leader in highly engineered motion control and electronic controls technology, continues its long-standing collaboration with[Jacuzzi® Group](https://www.jacuzzi.com/en-us/) through its operating company[Balboa Water Group](https://www.balboawater.com/) , whose display, control, and lighting technologies support the[Jacuzzi® J5™ Collection](https://www.jacuzzi.com/en-us/hot-tubs/browse-by-collection/j5trade%3B-collection) , the newest generation of premium spas from Jacuzzi® Group.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260319021339/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260319021339/en/)


The Jacuzzi® J5™ Collection includes Balboa’s SpaTouch™ 4+ display, spa controller, and custom four-button auxiliary panel, providing an exclusive user interface for the spa.


The Jacuzzi® J5™ Collection includes Balboa’s[SpaTouch™ 4+](https://www.balboawater.com/spatouch-4/) display, spa controller, and custom four-button auxiliary panel, providing an exclusive user interface for the spa. The platform also incorporates Balboa’s[Chromazon3™](https://www.balboawater.com/chromazon3/) lighting system, delivering multi-zone lighting control with millions of colors, adjustable brightness, and programmable lighting experiences. Together, Balboa’s control and lighting architecture helps integrate key spa technologies including hydrotherapy, interior and exterior lighting modes, and the patented Jacuzzi® infrared therapy into a cohesive and intuitive wellness experience.


“Helios continues to invest in technologies that help our customers deliver differentiated experiences in the health and wellness market,” said Billy Aldridge, President, Electronics of Helios Technologies. “Balboa Water Group’s long-standing collaboration with Jacuzzi® Group demonstrates how advanced electronic controls, intuitive user interfaces, and integrated lighting technologies support the next generation of premium spa platforms.”


“Our relationship with Balboa has been built over years of collaboration and an innovation-driven partnership,” said Erica Moir, SVP of Product Development & Engineering at Jacuzzi® Group. “With the J5™ Collection, Balboa’s control, display, and lighting technologies help enable the intuitive functionality and immersive wellness experience our customers expect from the Jacuzzi® brand.”


The collaboration between Balboa and Jacuzzi® reflects more than a decade of partnership focused on advancing spa technology and delivering premium experiences for spa owners worldwide.


**About Jacuzzi® Group**


Founded in 1956, Jacuzzi® Group is comprised of two segments, Jacuzzi® Spa the global leader in premium spa wellness products, offering industry-leading hot tubs, swim spas, saunas, and hydrotherapy solutions and Jacuzzi® Bath offering bath products and remodeling services across the country. With a legacy of innovation spanning decades, Jacuzzi® Spa is committed to fostering joyful well-being through high-quality, performance-driven products designed for relaxation and recovery. Its brands include Jacuzzi®, Sundance®, Dream Maker®, Hydropool®, and other well-known wellness brands. The company's flagship brand and trademark, Jacuzzi®, is the most recognized name in the world for spa and hydrotherapy bathing. With manufacturing operations in the United States, Canada, Mexico, and throughout Europe and Asia Pacific, Jacuzzi's thousands of employees bring joyful wellness to people in more than 100 countries worldwide. For more information, please go to[www.Jacuzzi.com](http://www.jacuzzi.com/) .


**About Balboa Water Group**


Balboa Water Group is a leading and innovative manufacturer of electronic control systems, pumps, and components for the global spa and wellness industry. As an international provider of fully integrated solutions, Balboa Water Group offers a comprehensive portfolio of products designed to enhance the user experience and simplify spa system design. With a globally diverse team, we serve customers worldwide through dedicated sales, engineering, and manufacturing operations. Balboa partners directly with OEMs and supports a broad network of authorized distributors and system integrators.
