# Enovation Controls, a Helios Technologies Operating Company, Named a UK’s Best Workplaces for Women™ for the Fifth Consecutive Year

# Enovation Controls, a Helios Technologies Operating Company, Named a UK’s Best Workplaces for Women™ for the Fifth Consecutive Year


July 28, 2026 9:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_b5483d7e150128d40b4ca2c6294ca702/heliostechnologies/news/2026-07-28_Enovation_Controls_a_Helios_Technologies_368.pdf)


SARASOTA, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Helios Technologies, Inc.](http://www.heliostechnologies.com/) (NYSE: HLIO) (“Helios” or the “Company”), a global leader in highly engineered motion control and electronic controls technology, today announced that its operating company[Enovation Controls](http://www.enovationcontrols.com/) has been recognized by[Great Place To Work®](https://www.greatplacetowork.co.uk/) as one of the[2026 UK's Best Workplaces for Women™](https://www.greatplacetowork.co.uk/best-workplaces/women/2026) for the fifth consecutive year.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260728933114/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260728933114/en/)


Enovation Controls has been recognized by Great Place To Work® as one of the 2026 UK's Best Workplaces for Women™ for the fifth consecutive year


Enovation Controls fosters an environment where women can thrive through professional development opportunities, internal career advancement, mentorship, and an inclusive culture that encourages every employee to contribute, grow, and lead.


The 2026 UK’s Best Workplaces for Women list is made up of employers whose people have told Great Place To Work® UK they work for a place that is inclusive and equitable for all. The 350 companies on the list are committed to ensuring a reasonable balance of women and men across the organization; removing barriers to women’s career advancement; and creating workplaces where all employees, regardless of gender, can flourish. These initiatives are supported by a workplace culture where employee feedback helps shape organizational improvements.


“This year’s UK’s Best Workplaces for Women list celebrates businesses making a genuine difference day to day, not just in what they say, but in how people experience work. What matters most is that this recognition comes directly from women working in these organizations, who tell us they feel supported, valued, and able to grow,” said Benedict Gautrey, Managing Director of Great Place To Work UK. “Our research demonstrates that these organizations creating high-trust environments deliver stronger results, whether in financial outcomes, impact, or service delivery, alongside greater agility and resilience in the face of change. Congratulations to Enovation Controls for creating an environment where inclusion is clearly felt in practice.”


“Being recognized among the UK's Best Workplaces for Women is an honor because it reflects the experiences of our employees," said Billy Aldridge, President, Electronics for Helios. "Across Helios, we are committed to creating a culture where everyone has the opportunity to grow, contribute, and build meaningful careers. This recognition reinforces our belief that diverse perspectives and an inclusive workplace drive stronger innovation, deeper collaboration, and long-term value creation.”


**About Great Place To Work** **®**


Great Place To Work® is the global authority on workplace culture, helping organisations to create exceptional, high-performing workplaces where employees feel trusted and valued. The UK’s Best Workplaces for Women™ enables these outstanding organisations to celebrate their achievements, build their employer brand, and inspire others to take action. For more information, visit[www.greatplacetowork.co.uk](http://www.greatplacetowork.co.uk/) .


**About Enovation Controls**


Enovation Controls is a fast-growing and innovative manufacturer of electronic controls and displays for diverse markets. As an international leader in fully tailored solutions, Enovation Controls offers a broad range of displays, controls, and instrumentation products for various applications. With an internationally diverse team, we serve customers around the world through our global sales, manufacturing, and engineering operations. Enovation Controls partners directly with OEMs and supports a worldwide network of authorized distributors and system integrators. Visit us at[www.enovationcontrols.com](http://www.enovationcontrols.com/) and follow us on[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/enovationcontrols) .


**About Helios Technologies**


Helios Technologies is a global leader in highly engineered motion control and electronic controls technology, providing premium products that ensure safety, reliability, and seamless connectivity to diverse end markets including agriculture, construction, data centers, energy, health and wellness, industrial, marine, material handling, and recreational vehicles. Helios sells its products to customers in over 80 countries around the world. Its strategy is to be a diversified, customer-centric global enterprise distinguished by innovation, operational speed, and a high-performance culture. The Company has paid a cash dividend to its shareholders every quarter since becoming a public company in 1997.
