# Announcing our partnership with Surepoint

# SurePoint and PointOne Partner to Bring AI-Powered Timekeeping to Mid-Market Law Firms


**New York City, New York** — **October 14, 2025** — SurePoint® Technologies, a leading provider of legal software and intelligence solutions to mid-sized law firms, today announced a strategic partnership with PointOne, a leading AI platform automating time capture and compliance for firms. This collaboration empowers firms to eliminate revenue leakage, streamline timekeeping, and ensure adherence to complex client and firm billing guidelines — all with zero disruption to existing workflows.


PointOne’s AI technology passively captures billable activity across devices and applications — including emails, meetings, calls, and documents — and automatically generates time entries that comply with firm and client-specific billing rules. Integrated with the connected technology ecosystem of the SurePoint Legal Suite, firms can deploy PointOne without disrupting workflows or compromising compliance.


“At SurePoint, we seek partners who are leaders in their category,” said **Lee Weinstein, Chief Partnerships Officer at SurePoint** . “PointOne has built a state-of-the-art AI platform for time capture and compliance. Our partnership delivers our customers a seamless way for firms to increase revenue and transform their approach to timekeeping.”


On average, firms using PointOne capture 6–11% more billable time per day, unlocking significant revenue gains without increasing workload. “We’re delighted to partner with SurePoint — the leading practice management solution for mid-market law firms,” said **Katon Luaces, Co-founder and CEO of PointOne** . “Together, we enable SurePoint users to capture every billable hour with zero friction, automate compliance with client and firm guidelines, and drive revenue growth. This is more than an integration; it’s a shared vision of enabling the firms that power the industry’s future.”


The partnership marks a significant step forward in legal technology, enabling mid-market firms to modernize their operations, enhance profitability, and compete on par with large firms — without the cost or complexity of custom AI deployments.


For more information about the partnership, visit SurePoint and PointOne.
