### PointOne


[PointOne](https://pointone.com/) Time captures work passively across email, documents, calls, and web activity, then generates entries that already match firm policy and[client OCGs](https://pointone.com/blog/crafting-legal-bills) . Designed specifically for complex legal billing environments, the platform layers on top of Aderant, Clio, Elite 3E, SurePoint, and QuickBooks, so firms keep their existing billing system without requiring a disruptive rip-and-replace implementation. By turning passive work data into operational intelligence, PointOne enables finance teams to understand utilization and margins while eliminating the friction of[manual time entry](https://pointone.com/blog/what-is-ai-time-tracking) for attorneys.


### Ajax


Ajax reads on-screen content to build elaborate time entries, capturing across many applications without configuring a separate integration for each one. Reviewers note that this screen-based approach produces granular narratives, but it sits at a premium price point and requires a desktop installation for every attorney. It appeals most to firms that rank maximum entry detail above all else, while some compliance and IT teams weigh the trade-offs of a screen-reading approach against integration-based alternatives.


### Laurel


Positioned as a "time intelligence" platform rather than a simple tracker, Laurel captures work activity and layers on[compliance review](https://pointone.com/blog/optimizing-legal-pre-bill-proforma-review) , automated billing-code assignment, and profitability analytics. Analysts note it is among the most heavily funded vendors in the category and has focused largely on accounting firms in recent years, which broadens its scope beyond purely legal workflows.


### Billables.ai


For practices seeking[automated tracking](https://pointone.com/blog/ai-timekeeping-tools-for-litigation-firms) without enterprise-level complexity, Billables.ai provides a streamlined solution. Market evaluations highlight Billables.ai as an affordable passive capture tool serving solo to boutique law firms. The software connects to daily work tools like Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and Zoom via API integrations and uses AI to categorize tasks by client and matter with auto-generated narratives. While it may lack the deep OCG compliance enforcement found in enterprise platforms, its straightforward implementation and cost-effective pricing structure make it an accessible entry point for small firms aiming looking to move off of traditional manual timers.


### Clio Duo


Operating within the broader Clio Manage ecosystem, Clio Duo functions as an intelligent assistant for legal professionals. Industry analysts describe Clio Duo as an active logging tool ideal for fixing[poor billing narratives](https://pointone.com/blog/how-to-craft-better-legal-bills) . Rather than passively monitoring background activity, it prompts users to refine and expand their manual entries using generative AI. This approach ensures that time records meet the stringent requirements of corporate clients, making it a strong option for[solo practitioners and small firms](https://pointone.com/blog/best-legal-billing-software-for-solo-attorneys) already utilizing Clio's[practice management](https://pointone.com/blog/best-legal-billing-software-for-solo-attorneys) infrastructure.


### BigHand SmartTime


BigHand SmartTime offers a robust time capture solution tailored for large, global law firms. The platform combines active timers with passive activity monitoring to create a comprehensive daily timesheet. Its strength lies in its enterprise-grade analytics and reporting capabilities, which provide firm leadership with granular visibility into timekeeper performance and missing time. The system integrates deeply with legacy financial systems, though its interface and deployment process reflect its focus on large-scale, complex organizational structures.


### Intapp Time


Intapp Time is an established enterprise timekeeping product now adding AI capabilities on top of its existing foundation. Its strength is deep integration with large-firm financial and practice systems, making it a familiar option for firms already invested in legacy infrastructure. Because its AI features are layered onto an older architecture, firms often evaluate it against newer AI-native platforms when automated narratives and matter accuracy are the priority.


### Lawgro MagicTime


Lawgro MagicTime is an AI-based automated timekeeping tool built specifically for lawyers. It captures billable minutes in the background across the applications attorneys use — email, Word, court websites, legal research tools — then builds a timesheet that users review and file in a few clicks. Its AI categorizes captured activity and tags it to the correct client and matter, with customizable rules controlling what gets captured. MagicTime integrates with practice management platforms like Clio, PracticePanther, and Rocket Matter, making it a natural fit for solo and small firms already running on those systems.


### Tempello


Tempello focuses on simplifying the[time capture process](https://pointone.com/news/seed-round) through an intuitive, minimalist interface. It connects to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, matches each email to the correct matter using metadata and AI, and automatically creates time entries in Clio or MyCase — no timers and no desktop monitoring. Pricing is per billable email processed rather than per seat, which appeals to small firms with fluctuating headcount. Because it captures email activity only, it serves as a targeted supplement for firms whose missed time hides in their inboxes, rather than a comprehensive capture or billing compliance engine.


### WiseTime


WiseTime runs autonomously in the background, auto-tagging activity to the correct matter and refining its suggestions over time. It recently added AI narrative summaries, but reviewers characterize it as an activity tracker that still leaves attorneys to finalize entries rather than a tool that writes complete, client-ready narratives. Its lower price and strong privacy controls suit budget-conscious firms comfortable with a lighter, previous-generation approach.


### Memtime


Memtime records computer activity and presents it as a private, local timeline for reconstructing timesheets, with all data stored on the user's own device. Users create their own entries from the timeline, optionally assisted by rule-based automation, but the tool is not legal-specific and does not attribute matters or write narratives — it functions as a memory aid rather than a billing tool. Its offline architecture and low price make it attractive for privacy-strict or cost-sensitive users, including non-legal professionals who bill by the hour.

## FAQs about AI time tracking for lawyers


How much does AI time tracking software cost?


It ranges from about $12/user/month for a basic activity logger to custom enterprise pricing. Tools that write full time entries generally start around $40/user/month.


Are cheaper AI timekeeping tools worth it?


The lowest-priced options, like Memtime (~$12/user/month), are activity logs or memory aids — they do not write time entries or attribute work to the right matter. Tools with genuinely advanced AI that draft client-ready narratives and assign entries to the correct matter cost more, but recover far more billable time. For most firms the value is in AI quality, not the lowest sticker price.


What makes an AI timekeeping tool high quality?


The quality of the AI itself — how accurately it attributes work to the correct client and matter, and how well-written and compliant its generated narratives are. The most advanced platforms produce client-ready entries that need little editing and pass billing review the first time, which is what drives real billable-hour recovery.


What is the difference between AI time tacking tools?


Three things: how they capture work (screen, metadata, or activity log), whether they write complete entries or just log activity, and how deeply they enforce billing compliance.


Do these tools integrate with legal billing systems?


The leading tools integrate with Aderant, Elite 3E, Clio, and SurePoint. Integration depth varies by vendor.
