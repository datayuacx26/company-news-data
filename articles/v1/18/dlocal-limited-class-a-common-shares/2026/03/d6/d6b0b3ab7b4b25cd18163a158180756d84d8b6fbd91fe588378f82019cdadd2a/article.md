# dLocal to Report First Quarter 2026 Financial Results

[PDF Version](https://dlocal.gcs-web.com/node/7691/pdf)


MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay, March 31, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- DLocal Limited (NASDAQ: DLO, “dLocal” or the “Company”), a leading financial technology company powering payments across emerging markets, intends to release financial results for its first fiscal quarter ended March 31, 2026 on May 14, 2026 after market close.


The Company will host a conference call and video webcast on May 14, 2026 at 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time.

**About dLocal** dLocal powers local payments in emerging markets, connecting global enterprise merchants with billions of emerging market consumers across APAC, the Middle East, Latin America, and Africa. Through the “One dLocal” concept (one direct API, one platform, and one contract), global companies can accept payments, send payouts, and settle funds globally without the need to manage multiple local entities and integrations.
