#### Make the Most of Spring Break with Affordable Activities Close to Home


Spring break doesn’t have to mean airports, long car rides or juggling an overstuffed itinerary. Sometimes the most memorable moments are created right at home – especially when the days get longer, the temperatures warm up and everything starts to bloom. And after a winter filled with more snow shoveling than anyone asked for, we know how good it feels to hit reset once spring arrives. Whether you’re hoping to keep the kids entertained, reconnect as a family or simply unwind after a long winter, here are fun, affordable ideas to make your spring break feel special without ever leaving town.


**1. Be a Tourist in Your Own City**


You don’t have to travel far to feel like you’re on an adventure. Visit a museum you’ve never explored, try an escape room, wander around a historic district or finally check out that quirky local attraction you’ve always been curious about. Sometimes the best discoveries are right around the corner.


**2. Get Outside**


Spring practically begs you to step outdoors.


- Take a family hike at a nearby state park.
- Enjoy a picnic at your favorite green space.
- Rent bikes or scooters and cruise around the neighborhood.
- Explore botanical gardens or nature preserves.
- Discover wildlife up close at a zoo.


Fresh air is energizing – and almost always free.


**3. Get Crafty**


From DIY birdhouses to painting flowerpots, hands-on creativity gives kids (and adults!) a place to explore their imagination. Simple supplies can spark hours of fun.


**4. Try a New Hobby**


Spring break is the perfect time to dabble in something new – pottery, guitar lessons, gardening, baking or getting your zen on with yoga. A fresh hobby can spark joy long after break is over.


**5. Volunteer Together**


Spring is a season of renewal – and giving back makes it even brighter. Try helping at a food pantry, participating in a park cleanup, donating old toys or making cards for seniors. Kindness has a way of making the whole week feel more meaningful.
