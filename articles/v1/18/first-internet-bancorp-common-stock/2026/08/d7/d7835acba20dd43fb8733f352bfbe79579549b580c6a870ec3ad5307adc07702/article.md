# Turn Everyday Business Banking Questions Into Clearer Answers

#### Summary


Understanding your finances shouldn’t require hours of analysis. With Model Context Protocol (MCP) from First Internet Bank, customers can choose to securely connect their Business Checking, Business Money Market and Business Savings account to OpenAI’s ChatGPT* or Anthropic’s Claude* and simply ask questions about their finances. From cash flow to spending insights, get real answers instantly, helping you make faster, more confident decisions.


#### The Real Value of AI in Business Banking


By now, you’ve probably heard about using AI assistants like OpenAI’s ChatGPT* and Anthropic’s Claude* to save time and get answers faster.


But what happens when those same tools can be connected to securely access information from your First Internet Bank business accounts?


What can you actually ask?


And more importantly, how can those answers help you run your business better?


That’s where[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://www.firstib.com/business/ai-driven-banking/) comes in.


With MCP, you can choose to securely connect your First Internet Bank Business Checking, Business Money Market or Business Savings account to AI assistants like ChatGPT* and Claude*, allowing you to ask questions about your finances using plain language and receive answers based on your real account activity.


At First Internet Bank, we believe technology should be practical. The value isn’t in the connection itself. It’s in turning everyday financial data into answers that help you make decisions with greater speed and confidence.


#### Start with the Questions You Already Have


Most business owners don’t need more reports. They need answers to everyday questions like:


- “Will I have enough cash to cover upcoming expenses?”
- “Which vendors account for the most of my spending?”
- “How has my spending changed over time?”


These are the questions that drive decisions. With MCP, you can ask them directly and get answers based on your real financial data.

Cash flow is one of the most important areas of your business.

With MCP, you can ask ChatGPT or Claude questions like:


- “How much cash do I have available today?”

- “Would a slowdown in revenue affect my cash position?”


Instead of piecing together reports, you get a clearer picture—right when you need it.

These insights help you identify opportunities to reduce costs, renegotiate contracts or adjust your strategy.

This helps you stay proactive so you can respond to changes before they become challenges.

#### Frequently Asked Questions


##### Do I have to connect my First Internet Bank account to ChatGPT or Claude?


**No** . Connecting your account is completely optional. MCP is only available if you choose to set up a connection between your eligible First Internet Bank business account and a supported AI assistant. Your accounts are not connected automatically, and no data is shared unless you decide to establish and use the connection.


##### Which First Internet Bank accounts can be connected using MCP?


MCP currently can connect to Business Checking, Business Money Market and Business Savings accounts, as well as Personal Checking, Personal Money Market and Personal Savings accounts.


##### What kinds of questions can I ask after connecting my account?


You can ask a wide range of questions about your business finances, including:


- “How much cash is available today?”
- “Which vendors account for most of my spending?”
- “How has my spending changed over time?”
- “How much did I spend on marketing last month?”
- “Can I afford a major purchase this quarter?”
- “What large expenses may impact my cash position?”


The goal is to help you turn everyday financial data into clear, actionable answers that support faster, more confident business decisions.
