# Your Business Financial Data Has Answers. Now You Can Simply Ask.

#### Summary


Having access to financial data isn’t the problem—using it effectively is. With[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://www.firstib.com/business/ai-driven-banking/) from First Internet Bank, customers **can choose to securely connect** their[business checking](https://www.firstib.com/business/checking/) ,[business money market](https://www.firstib.com/business/savings/) or[business savings](https://www.firstib.com/business/savings/) accounts to AI assistants and turn everyday financial data into real-time insights. That means faster answers and better decisions.


#### Having Data Is Not the Same as Using It


Most business owners already have access to their financial data. You can:


- Sign in and see balances.
- Review transactions.
- Generate reports.


But having access to information isn’t the same as **understanding what it means or knowing what to do next** .


That’s where many businesses get stuck.


Because the real challenge isn’t collecting data. It’s turning that data into decisions.


#### Why Faster Decisions Matter


Running a business means making constant financial decisions:


- “Can I afford to make this investment right now?”
- “Is my cash flow trending in the right direction?”
- “Should I adjust spending in certain areas?”


When it takes hours – or even days – to answer these questions, you lose critical momentum.


Opportunities can pass.
Problems can grow.
Decisions get delayed.


At First Internet Bank, we believe your financial tools should help you move faster, not slow you down.


#### Turning Insight into Action


Model Context Protocol (MCP) changes how you interact with your financial data through AI assistants like Open AI’s ChatGPT* or Anthropic’s Claude*.


Instead of reviewing static information, you can ask direct questions and get immediate, relevant answers based on your real account activity.


For example:


- “What changed the most in my finances this quarter?”
- “Where can I potentially reduce costs?”
- “Which expense categories are growing the fastest?”


These aren’t just data points. They’re insights you can act on—right away.


#### From Looking Back to Looking Ahead


Traditional financial tools often focus on what has already happened.


You review last month’s report.
You analyze past transactions.
You react after the fact.


With MCP, you can choose to connect your AI assistants to help you take a more proactive approach by spotting trends earlier.


- Identify changes as they happen
- Make adjustments before issues grow


That shift, from reactive to proactive, is what can help businesses stay ahead.


#### Clearer Visibility into Cash Flow


Cash flow is one of the most critical parts of running a business, and one of the hardest to manage without clear insight.


MCP makes it easier to answer questions like:


- “How has my cash flow changed over the past 90 days?”
- “Can my cash flow support hiring another employee?”
- “Do I have enough liquidity to invest in new equipment?”


Instead of piecing together information from multiple sources, you get a clearer picture in one place. And when you understand your cash flow more clearly, you can operate with more confidence.


#### Built for Real-World Decision Making


Business owners don’t need more data. They need better ways to use it.


That’s why MCP is designed to work the way you already do, inside the AI accounts you subscribe to and use every day. There’s no complex process. No extra system to manage.


Just a faster, more intuitive way to get answers using natural language.


#### The Bottom Line


Better decisions don’t come from more data.


They come from **better access to the right insights at the right time** .


That’s what MCP delivers.


Because when you can clearly understand your financial position in the moment, you’re not just reacting to your business, **you’re leading it.**

#### Frequently Asked Questions


##### Do I have to connect my First Internet Bank accounts to an AI assistant?


No. Connecting your accounts is entirely optional. MCP only works if you choose to set up a connection between your eligible First Internet Bank business accounts and a supported AI assistant. No accounts are connected automatically.


##### Which AI assistants are supported?


MCP currently supports eligible versions of Anthropic’s Claude* and OpenAI’s ChatGPT*. Refer to current plan requirements and availability details before connecting.


##### Will the AI make decisions for me?


No. AI-generated responses are intended to provide information and insights to support decision-making. Business owners remain responsible for evaluating the information and making financial decisions.
