# Connect Your AI Assistant and Turn Everyday Money Questions Into Clearer Answers

#### Summary


Understanding your finances should not require hours of analysis. With Model Context Protocol (MCP) from First Internet Bank, **customers can choose to securely** connect a First Internet Bank Personal Checking or Personal Money Market Savings account to OpenAI’s ChatGPT* or Anthropic’s Claude* and ask plain-language questions about approved account information. From spending trends to savings progress, MCP helps turn everyday financial data into clearer answers that support more informed decisions.


#### The real value of AI in banking


By now, you have probably heard about using AI assistants like OpenAI’s ChatGPT* and Anthropic’s Claude* to save time and get answers faster.


But the value grows when those tools can securely use approved information from your First Internet Bank accounts. The experience becomes more useful because the answers are based on your own account activity, not generic assumptions.


That is where Model Context Protocol (MCP) comes in. It empowers you to securely connect your First Internet Bank Personal Checking, Personal Savings and Personal Money Market Savings accounts if you choose, to supported AI assistants, allowing you to ask questions about your finances in plain language and receive answers based on approved financial data.


At First Internet Bank, we believe technology should help you do more without adding unnecessary work. MCP transforms everyday account activity into useful answers, helping you understand your finances with greater clarity and confidence.


#### Start with the questions you already have


Most people do not need more statements or spreadsheets. They need answers to the questions that shape day-to-day financial decisions.


When you decide to connect your account to MCP, you can ask questions like:


- “Will I have enough cash to cover upcoming expenses?”
- “How much did I spend on dining out last month?”
- “Where is most of my money going?”
- “Am I spending more than usual?”


These are practical questions, and they are easier to act on when the answers are based on your actual account activity.


#### Review spending without the extra work


Understanding spending patterns often means sorting through transactions and trying to connect the dots. MCP can make that process more direct.


You can ask questions such as:


- “How much did I spend on groceries last month?”
- “Am I on track to reach my spending goals?”
- “Will I have enough cash to cover upcoming expenses?”


Instead of manually reviewing activity, you can get a clearer view of spending habits when the question is still fresh.


#### Track savings progress more easily


Whether you are building an emergency fund or saving for a future purchase, understanding progress can help you stay focused.


With MCP, you can ask:


- “How much have I saved over the past six months?”
- “How does my savings balance compare to last year?”
- “How much money did I set aside this month?”


Quick access to these insights can help you see whether your current habits are moving you toward your goals.


#### Spot changes over time


Some financial changes are easy to miss when you look at transactions one by one. MCP can help highlight trends across approved account activity, including spending shifts, recurring charges and progress toward savings goals.


That broader view can help you identify what changed and decide whether you want to adjust your plan.


#### Get answers when decisions are happening


Financial questions do not always show up at the end of the month. Sometimes you want clarity before making a purchase, planning a trip or setting a new goal.


With MCP, you can check your financial picture, ask a follow-up question and explore different scenarios in the same conversation. That makes it easier to move forward with confidence.


#### Designed for how you think


You do not think in spreadsheets. You do not ask questions in formulas. You think in plain language.


MCP is designed to work the same way. You can ask questions naturally and get straightforward answers in return, without translating your financial life into rows and columns first.

#### Frequently Asked Questions


##### What can I ask AI about my finances with MCP?


You can ask practical questions about approved account information, such as spending patterns, savings progress, recurring charges and changes in account activity.


##### Can MCP help me understand spending trends?


Yes. MCP can help supported AI assistants identify spending-related patterns based on approved account data.


##### Can I ask follow-up questions?


Yes. One of the benefits of conversational AI is the ability to continue refining your question as you review the answer.


##### Does MCP replace financial judgment?


No. MCP helps make account information easier to understand. You remain responsible for your own financial decisions.
