# Now You Can Connect Your AI Assistant to Online Banking.

#### Summary


Understanding your finances shouldn’t require digging through transactions, searching statements or piecing together information from multiple accounts. With Model Context Protocol (MCP) from First Internet Bank, **you can choose to securely connect** your First Internet Bank accounts to AI assistants like OpenAI’s ChatGPT* or Anthropic’s Claude* to ask questions and get real answers instantly. No spreadsheets. No extra tools. Just clearer insights to help you make more confident financial decisions with your First Internet Bank Personal Checking, Personal Savings or Personal Money Market Savings account.


#### Imagine If Your Finances Could Explain Themselves


No transactions to sort through. No statements to analyze. Just answers and insights when you need them. That’s the future of banking.


At First Internet Bank, we’re introducing a new way to connect your finances to AI assistants like OpenAI’s ChatGPT* or Anthropic’s Claude*. Powered by[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://www.firstib.com/personal/ai-driven-banking/) , this secure technology allows **you to choose** whether to connect eligible accounts, turning your financial data into insights that help you make decisions with greater speed and confidence.


##### What is MCP?


MCP is a secure connection you can establish between your First Internet Bank account ([personal checking](https://www.firstib.com/personal/checking/) ,[personal savings](https://www.firstib.com/personal/savings/) or[personal money market](https://www.firstib.com/personal/savings/) ) and certain versions of ChatGPT* or Claude*. It allows AI to read approved information, like balances and transactions, so it can provide personalized insights based on your actual financial activity.


Key benefits include:


- You stay in control of your data and permissions
- Access is read-only—no changes or transactions
- Your login credentials are never shared


##### From Data to Answers


With MCP, you can simply ask:


- “How much did I spend on dining out last month?”
- “How has my spending changed over the last few months?”
- “How much have I saved so far this year?”
- “What subscriptions do I pay for each month?”


It’s that easy to get immediate, relevant answers. Instead of spending time reviewing transactions and searching for information, you can spend more time focusing on your financial goals.


##### Designed for How You Live


You may already use AI assistants to answer everyday questions. Now your financial data can live there too. There’s no new system to learn, just smarter access to your finances.


##### Designed Around Control


MCP is not about handing over your finances to AI. It’s about giving you a better way to understand the information you already have. You remain in control of what data is available. Access is read-only, which means AI can analyze information but cannot move money or change your account. Your online banking credentials are not shared with ChatGPT or Claude.


The result is a more useful connection between your banking data and the AI tools that are becoming part of everyday life.


Imagine if your bank account could answer your questions. With MCP and First Internet Bank, that future is here.

#### Frequently Asked Questions


##### What is MCP in banking?


MCP in banking refers to a secure connection you establish that allows versions of Claude or ChatGPT to access approved banking information so they can provide insights based on real account data.


##### Can AI make transactions through MCP?


No. The MCP experience described here is read-only, so AI can analyze approved information but cannot move money or make account changes.


##### Which AI tools can use MCP?


AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude, based on supported account and plan availability.


##### Why is MCP useful for personal banking customers?


MCP helps customers better understand spending, savings and account activity without manually reviewing transactions or exporting data.


##### How can MCP help me manage my finances?


MCP can help you answer questions about spending patterns, savings progress and account activity so you can make more informed financial decisions.


##### Do I have to connect my First Internet Bank accounts to an AI assistant?


No. Connecting your accounts is entirely optional. MCP only works if you choose to set up a connection between your eligible First Internet Bank personal accounts and a supported AI assistant. No accounts are connected automatically
