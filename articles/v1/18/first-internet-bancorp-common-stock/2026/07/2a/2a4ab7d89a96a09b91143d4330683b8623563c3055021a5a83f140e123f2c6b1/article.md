# Five Smart Ways to Save Money on Summer Fun for Kids

#### Summary


Summer fun with kids doesn’t have to come with a high price tag. In fact, some of the best family memories are the ones that cost the least. With a few smart money moves, you can enjoy budget-friendly summer activities your kids will love—without the financial stress.


#### Make Summer More Affordable (Without Cutting the Fun)


Whether you’re planning a staycation or looking for fun things to do near you, a little creativity and a few smart financial moves can go a long way.


##### 1. Cut Costs with Season Pass Savings


If your neighborhood or community offers a pool or activities, a seasonal pass can save money fast. If your kids want to go every other day, a pass makes it easy to say yes without adding up daily fees.


Example: Frequent pool visits quickly offset the cost of a summer pass.


##### 2. Take Advantage of Local Discounts and Memberships


Membership programs like AAA can help you save on attractions, travel and events. A quick search for local discounts before you buy tickets can uncover big savings.


Example: A children’s museum ticket could be discounted, turning a pricey outing into an affordable one.


##### 3. Embrace Free (and Fun!) Outdoor Activities


Some of the best activities are free: parks, splash pads and community events. Pack a picnic and enjoy time together outdoors.


Fun idea: Turn a park trip into a mini challenge like spotting birds or building a fort.


##### 4. Use Everyday Spending to Earn Rewards


Summer spending adds up, but using a rewards credit card can help you earn cash back or points on everyday purchases.


Example: Road trip snacks and gas purchases can earn rewards toward future outings.


##### 5. Share Costs and Plan Group Outings


Planning with friends or neighbors can reduce costs by sharing gas, snacks and tickets.


Bonus: Kids enjoy outings even more with friends along.


#### Make the Most of Summer—and Your Money


Your kids will remember the time you spent together more than the money you spent. Smart financial choices help make those memories possible.
