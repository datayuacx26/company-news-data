# Stop Hunting for Business Spend Data. Just Ask.

#### Summary


Business owners spend too much time gathering financial data—and not enough time using it. With Model Context Protocol (MCP) from First Internet Bank, you can securely connect your First Internet Bank business accounts to AI assistants like OpenAI’s ChatGPT* or Anthropic’s Claude* to ask questions and get real answers instantly. No spreadsheets. No extra tools. Just clearer insights to help you make faster, more confident decisions with your First Internet Bank Business Checking, Business Money Market or Business Savings account.


#### Imagine If Your Finances Could Explain Themselves


No reports to build. No dashboards to navigate. Just answers and insights when you need them. That’s the future of business banking.


At First Internet Bank, we’re introducing a new way to connect your business finances to AI assistants like OpenAI’s ChatGPT* or Anthropic’s Claude*. It’s powered by[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://www.firstib.com/business/ai-driven-banking/) , a secure technology that helps your financial data work for you—inside the tools you already use.


#### What is MCP?


MCP is a secure connection between your First Internet Bank business account and ChatGPT or Claude. It allows AI to read approved information, like balances and transactions, so it can provide accurate insights.


Key benefits include:


- You stay in control of your data and permissions
- Access is read-only—no changes or transactions
- Your login credentials are never shared


#### From Data to Answers


With MCP, you can simply ask:


- “Where can I potentially reduce expenses?”
- “Based on current trends, where will we end the quarter?”
- “What are the biggest drivers of our spending?”


It’s that easy to get immediate, relevant answers. For a busy business owner, that means less time organizing information and more time making decisions.


#### Designed for How You Work


You may already use AI assistants to plan, write and analyze. Now your financial data can live there too. There’s no new system to learn, just smarter access to your business finances.


#### Designed Around Control


MCP is not about handing over your finances to AI. It is about giving you a better way to understand the information you already have. You remain in control of what data is available. Access is read-only, which means AI can analyze information but cannot move money or change your account. Your online banking credentials are not shared with ChatGPT or Claude.


The result is a more useful connection between your business banking data and the AI tools that are becoming part of everyday work.


Imagine if your bank account could answer your questions? With MCP and First Internet Bank, the future is here.

#### Frequently Asked Questions


##### What is MCP in banking?


MCP in banking refers to a secure connection that allows AI tools to access approved banking information so they can provide insights based on real account data.


##### Can AI make transactions through MCP?


No. The MCP experience described here is read-only, so AI can analyze approved information but cannot move money or make account changes.


##### Which AI tools can use MCP?


AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude, based on supported account and plan availability.


##### Why is MCP useful for business owners?


MCP helps business owners ask questions about cash flow, spending and account activity without exporting data or building manual reports.
