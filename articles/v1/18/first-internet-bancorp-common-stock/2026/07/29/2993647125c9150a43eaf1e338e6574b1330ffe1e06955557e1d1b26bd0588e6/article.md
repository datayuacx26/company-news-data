# Stop Building Business Reports. Start Getting Answers.

#### Summary


Business owners shouldn’t have to build reports just to understand their finances. With Model Context Protocol (MCP) from First Internet Bank, customers can **choose to securely connect** your First Internet Bank business accounts to OpenAI’s ChatGPT* or Anthropic’s Claude* and get real answers instantly. No spreadsheets, no manual work, just faster insights to help you manage cash flow, track expenses and make smarter decisions with your accounts.

If you’re like most business owners, getting answers about your finances isn’t simple.


And by the time you’ve pieced everything together, the question you started with has already slowed you down.


It’s not that the information isn’t there, it’s that **it takes too much work to use it** .

What if you didn’t need to export anything at all?


What if instead of creating a report, you could just ask:


- “Which expenses have grown the fastest this year?”
- “Which vendors cost me the most?”
- “Am I on track with my cash flow this quarter?”


With[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://www.firstib.com/business/ai-driven-banking/) , that’s exactly how it works.


MCP allows you to choose to securely connect your First Internet Bank business account to ChatGPT* or Claude*, so you can ask questions and get answers based on your real financial data, instantly.


No spreadsheets. No toggling between systems. No guesswork.

Spreadsheets have their place, but they weren’t designed for fast decisions.


They require:


- Manual exports

That creates friction at every step.


And for business owners, friction costs time. Sometimes, it costs opportunities.


At First Internet Bank, our focus has always been on helping you move faster with the right tools and support. MCP builds on that by removing the manual steps between your data and your decisions.

Because decisions don’t wait for spreadsheets.

#### Frequently Asked Questions


##### Do I have to connect my accounts to ChatGPT or Claude?


No. Connecting your accounts is completely optional.


MCP is not enabled automatically. If you choose to use it, you can securely connect your First Internet Bank business accounts to your ChatGPT* or Claude* accounts. If you do not connect your accounts, nothing changes and your banking experience remains the same.
