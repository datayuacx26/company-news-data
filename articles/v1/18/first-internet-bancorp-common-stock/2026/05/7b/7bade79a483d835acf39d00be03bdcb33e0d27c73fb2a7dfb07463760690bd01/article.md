# First Internet Bank to Join Risk Management Panel at Indiana Bankers Association MEGA 2026 Conference

[First Internet Bank](https://www.firstib.com/) announced today that[Anne Sharkey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anne-sharkey-737b185/) , Senior Vice President and Chief Risk Officer, will speak at the[Indiana Bankers Association (IBA) MEGA 2026 Conference](https://indiana.bank/events/mega) in Indianapolis.


Ms. Sharkey will participate in the panel discussion, “[Elevating Risk Management: Building Strong Risk-Business Partnerships and Integrating Risk into Strategic Decision-Making](https://events.rdmobile.com/Sessions/Details/3441010) ,” taking place on Wednesday, May 20, from 10:30 AM to 11:30 AM EST. The session will examine how community bank leaders can position risk management as a source of business value, foster alignment between risk and business objectives and promote risk-informed decision-making across their organizations.


“Effective risk management isn’t just about oversight. It’s about building trust with the business and creating a shared language around risk that supports smarter decisions,” said Anne Sharkey. “I look forward to joining my peers in discussing practical ways risk leaders can strengthen those partnerships and demonstrate real value to the institutions they serve.”


During the discussion, panelists will share best practices for communicating effectively with business lines and support functions, aligning organizational and risk management objectives, and embedding risk thinking across the enterprise. The IBA MEGA Conference brings together banking professionals, industry leaders and community bank executives to explore the challenges and opportunities shaping the future of banking.


More information about the IBA MEGA 2026 Conference is available[here](https://events.rdmobile.com/Events/Details/20323) .
