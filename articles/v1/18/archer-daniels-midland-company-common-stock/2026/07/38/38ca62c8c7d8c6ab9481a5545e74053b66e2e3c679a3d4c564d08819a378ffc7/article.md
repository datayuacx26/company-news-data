# ADM Releases 2024 Corporate Sustainability Report

ADM released its[2024 Corporate Sustainability Report](https://www.adm.com/globalassets/sustainability/sustainability-reports/archer_daniels_2024_corporate_sustainability_report.pdf) covering the company’s activities and progress toward sustainability goals that drive business growth and strengthen global supply chains.


Among the year’s key accomplishments, ADM expanded its global regenerative agriculture program to over 5 million acres, surpassing the company’s 2024 goal and achieving its 2025 target a year early. Regenerative agriculture, which improves soil health and its ability to store carbon, helps increase farmer resilience and meet customers’ sustainability needs.


The report covers a wide range of progress across environmental, social and governance priorities, from operational efficiencies that reduced ADM’s energy use intensity to a year-over-year reduction in safety incidents.
