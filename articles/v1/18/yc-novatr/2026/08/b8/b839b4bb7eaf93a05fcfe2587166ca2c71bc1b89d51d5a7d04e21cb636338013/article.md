# BIM Learning: Cohort-Based vs Self-Paced Learning {{year}}

## **What is the Difference Between Cohort-Based and Self-Paced Learning?**


Cohort-based and self-paced learning offer two different ways to approach professional education. In cohort-based learning, learners progress through a course with a group, usually following a planned schedule and participating in shared learning activities. The experience often includes live sessions, discussions and opportunities to learn alongside peers.


Self-paced learning gives individuals more control over their learning journey. Learners can access course materials when convenient, move through lessons at their own speed and revisit topics as needed. Both approaches can support skill development, but they suit different learning preferences and circumstances. The key difference is that cohort-based learning centres on progressing with a group, while self-paced learning allows individuals to manage their own learning journey.

## **Which Learning Model is Better for Working AEC Professionals?**


There is no single best option for everyone. The right choice depends on work schedules, available time and how much structure a professional needs to stay on track.


-


### **Choose Self-Paced Learning for Greater Flexibility**


Self-paced learning can suit AEC professionals with changing schedules, project deadlines or frequent work commitments. They can study when their workload allows and revisit lessons when needed, without having to follow fixed class timings.


-


### **Pick Cohort-Based Learning for More Structure**


Cohort-based learning can be a better fit for professionals who prefer planned sessions, clear deadlines and regular interaction. A fixed schedule can make it easier to maintain learning momentum alongside a demanding role.


-


### **Consider Your Professional Commitments**


The best format is the one that fits realistically into your working routine. Professionals with unpredictable hours may value flexibility, while those who can set aside dedicated learning time may benefit from a structured programme.


-


### **Focus on What Helps You Progress**


Rather than choosing based only on convenience, consider which format will help you complete the course, practise new skills and apply them to your work.


## **When Should You Choose Cohort-Based or Self-Paced Learning for Career Growth?**


The right format depends on your work schedule, learning preferences and how much structure you need to stay consistent.


-


Cohort-based learning is suitable when you prefer fixed sessions, regular guidance and interaction with peers.


-


Self-paced learning works well when you have changing work hours and need to fit online learning around your commitments.


-


Cohort-based learning can help when deadlines and group participation keep you accountable.


-


Self-paced learning may be better when you want to revisit lessons or spend more time on challenging topics.


-


Cohort-based learning can provide regular feedback and opportunities to discuss practical challenges with instructors and peers.


-


Self-paced learning suits independent learners who are comfortable creating and following their own study routine.
