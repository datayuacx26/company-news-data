## **What Skills Do Top BIM Employers Look For?**


Leading firms expect BIM Architects to do more than create 3D models. They look for professionals who can manage information, improve project coordination, and contribute to efficient construction technology workflows throughout the project lifecycle. Developing the following skills can make you a stronger candidate for BIM roles:


-


### **BIM Modelling and Documentation**


Create accurate architectural models, construction drawings, schedules, and documentation using


[Autodesk Revit](https://www.novatr.com/blog/what-is-revit-software) while maintaining project standards.


-


### **Clash Detection and Model Coordination** ****


Use Navisworks to identify and resolve clashes between architectural, structural, and MEP models before construction begins.


-


### **Knowledge of BIM Standard** **s**


Understand BIM execution plans (BEP), Level of Development (LOD), and ISO 19650 standards to ensure models meet industry requirements.


-


### **Model Review and Quality Control** ****


Validate BIM models, check data accuracy, and maintain consistency using Autodesk BIM Interoperability Tools and established QA/QC processes.


-


### **Project Coordination** ****


Collaborate effectively with architects, engineers, contractors, and consultants to ensure smooth information exchange and coordinated project delivery.


-


### **Visualisation and Design Analysis** ****


Use tools such as Autodesk Forma and Enscape to support early-stage design decisions, site analysis, and design presentations.


-


### **Workflow Automation** ****


Improve productivity by using tools like


[pyRevit](https://www.novatr.com/blog/what-is-py-revit-its-key-features-who-uses-it) , DiRoots, and ProSheets to automate repetitive tasks and streamline BIM workflows.

-


**Engineering and Design Consultancies:** Global consulting firms recruit
