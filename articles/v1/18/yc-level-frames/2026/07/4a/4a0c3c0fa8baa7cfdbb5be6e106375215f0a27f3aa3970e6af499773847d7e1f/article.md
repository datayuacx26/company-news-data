# Personalize Your Framed Photos

A great photo captures a moment. Adding your own words can help celebrate and preserve the story behind it.


Photos are powerful on their own, but over time the details that make them meaningful can fade — where it was taken, who was there, why it mattered. We designed our[Personalized Framed Photos](https://www.levelframes.com/frames/personalized-frames) to solve that problem by creating a dedicated window opening below your picture for custom text, turning a single moment into a lasting, clearly remembered story.


This isn’t a caption layered over your photo or an engraved novelty. It’s a thoughtfully designed framing experience that gives your words the same care and permanence as the image itself.


## Why Add Text to a Framed Photo?


A few well-chosen words can change everything. Names give context, specific dates anchor time, the location brings you back, even a short phrase can unlock an entire memory.


By adding a second mat window beneath your photo, these personalized frames let you include the information your future self, or a gift recipient, will be grateful to have years from now.


It’s subtle, elegant, and intentionally separate from the image so nothing competes for attention.


## Designed to Be Timeless (Not Trendy)


Personalization should elevate a photo, not overpower it. That’s why the text window is clean, balanced, and proportioned to feel like it’s always belonged there.


You can choose the size of the text opening, add up to two lines of custom text, and select from curated fonts and styles.


Our built-in editor shows a real-time preview of your exact layout, spacing, and typography inside the frame. You can even upload your own image file with text if you've customized something using another tool.


The bottom line is that what you see while designing is exactly what arrives at your door.

This framing style works beautifully for many moments, but it’s especially meaningful when context matters most:


**Weddings & anniversaries** — mark the day, place, and beginning


**Graduations & achievements** — celebrate milestones earned over time


**Family memories** — preserve moments that define a season of life


**Travel & adventures** — turn trips into art that brings you back

Whether displayed in your own home or given as a gift, the added text ensures the story never gets lost.

## How to Add a Text Caption to Your Framed Photo


There are two ways to personalize a Level Frames framed photo.


If you begin on our[Personalized Framed Photo](https://www.levelframes.com/frames/personalized-frames) page, you will first upload your main image and select the print size, and then click the "Add Your Text" button to input the lines of text you want to display as well as the font style.


Or, if you have already started[framing a photo](https://www.levelframes.com/photos) with us, look for the Advanced Options section beneath the blue Finalize button. When you expand those options, you will see a link to "Make This a Personalized Frame" which will allow you to open up the built in editor and customize your text.
