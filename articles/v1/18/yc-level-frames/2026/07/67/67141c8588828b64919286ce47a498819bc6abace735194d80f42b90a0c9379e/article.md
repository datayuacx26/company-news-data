We enjoy working with artists, designers and photographers and helping to grow their business.


We're able to assist with a number of touch points including fine art printing, museum-quality framing and shipping frames to your customers and clients. It's all powered by[our online framing experience](https://www.levelframes.com/) that makes custom framing more convenient, affordable and intuitive.

### Selling Your Work Framed


We help artists and photographers sell framed versions of their work, and have[a number of ways to partner](https://help.levelframes.com/en/articles/961863-sell-framed-versions-of-your-work) depending on your needs.


The most seamless way to sell framed prints and artwork is to[set up a storefront on Dot Press](https://www.levelframes.com/blog/introducing-level-press-for-artists-photographers) , our end-to-end solution with printing, custom framing, shipping and customer support built right into the platform.


To name just a few of our partners, we are currently framing for[Invisible Creature](https://www.invisiblecreature.com/shop/visions-of-future/the-grand-tour) , Joey Roth,[2046 Design](https://2046printshop.com/shop/prints/screen-print/supersymmetry/) ,[Susan Lin](https://susan.level.press/) ,[Dan McCarthy](https://danmccarthy.bigcartel.com/product/keep-it-safe) and more.


### Expert Framing


All Level frames are handcrafted in the USA by an expert team of framers using[archival and acid-free ingredients](https://www.levelframes.com/custom-frames-online) to properly protect and beautifully display your photos, artwork and other items.


We offer a selection of solid-wood frames ranging from the modern styles you might find at an art gallery, to the rustic frames that evoke warm feelings of a cozy fireplace, to classic vintage options that provide a regal touch.


Our 4-ply conservation-grade matting will safely preserve and enhance your artwork if you choose to add one. We finish off frames with four different type of framer's grade acrylic including UV-protection and reflection control options.


### Fine Art Printing On-Demand


Using top of the line, color-calibrated inkjet printers, we're able to produce premium[giclée prints](https://www.levelframes.com/fine-art-printing) on your choice of paper type.


Choose a classic look with heavy stock Lustre Photo Paper, or go with the Archival Matte Art Paper for a more artistic approach. We use eco-friendly, archival pigment inks guaranteed not to fade for over a century.


### Exhibitions, Personalized Service & Artist Discounts


Our artist partnership team will work with you to figure out the best framing solution to fit your needs, and will be available to help on any questions or issues that arise. We have helped many photographers and artists[exhibit their work in museums, galleries and art fairs](https://www.levelframes.com/blog/custom-framing-for-art-shows-and-gallery-exhibitions) across the country.
