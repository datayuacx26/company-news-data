# Level Frames Gift Cards

A beautifully framed print or photo brings new life to any space, but it can be tricky to pick something out for others.


Now you can give the gift of[custom framing](https://www.levelframes.com/) with a **Level Frames** gift card that arrives by mail, or send a digital card directly to your recipient's inbox and let them decide what they want to frame up!


### e-Gift Cards


Perfect for last minute gifting, our digital gift cards are also the fastest way to set someone up to frame anything online.


Simply[select an amount and the date](https://www.levelframes.com/gift_cards/new) on which you'd like them to receive it, and we’ll send a delightful email with all of the details. It's a great gift that delivers right on time, even after our holiday order deadlines have passed.


Digital gift cards can be used on any online custom framing projects or framed artwork.


#### Physical Gift Cards


If you prefer to gift in person, we have a physical gift card that arrives in the mail within 3 to 7 days. Note that any physical cards ordered after December 16th will arrive after Christmas.


[Choose the denomination of your gift card](https://www.levelframes.com/directory/gift-cards) and checkout, and we'll mail you the gift card inside a premium envelope.


There is space on the front of the card if you'd like to add your name and that of your recipient, and they can use the code on the back of the card to redeem the amount you purchased through a special section of our site.


If sending directly to your lucky recipient, you can let us the know the "To" and "From" names in our Special Instructions field at checkout or add those details in a gift note.


> Gifting options available at checkout


These cards have no expiration date and can be used for framed photos, custom framing artwork, framed vinyl records, collage frames and everything else we offer.


#### FAQ for Level Frames Gift Cards


**How does the e-Gift Card work and what can you buy with it?**


It's a digital version of a regular gift card and works exactly the same, only there is no physical component. Your recipient receives an email on the date of your choosing, and will click a link inside to use the balance towards anything on our site - to[frame a photo](https://www.levelframes.com/photos) , artwork or vinyl record, and of course for online custom framing their art prints and posters.


You can[purchase e-Gift Cards here](https://www.levelframes.com/gift_cards/new) .


**What amounts can be purchased?**


Physical gift cards come in denominations of $75, $100, $150 and $250.


for e-Gift Cards, select from $50, $75, $100, $150, $200 and $250, or type in an entirely custom amount.


**How and when are e-Gift Cards delivered?**


When purchasing an e-Gift Card, you can select the delivery date. If you select today’s date, an email will be sent to the recipient immediately with the news of your incredible thoughtfulness and all necessary details for using the card.


If a date in the future has been chosen, the email will be sent in the morning on the date selected.


An example of the email that your recipient will receive is at the bottom of the FAQ.


**How and are physical gift cards delivered?**


Within two business days of purchase, we will ship your gift card and envelope inside a separate mailer via USPS or UPS. Allow 3 to 4 days for delivery, or let us know if you have a particular deadline in the Special Instructions field at checkout and we'll let you know if we can accommodate.


Send them to your home address if you want them in hand for gifting in person, or send directly to your lucky recipient and let is know in the Special Instructions if you'd like us to add a "To" and "From" name.


**How do I use my gift card and check its balance?**


If you are the *recipient* of an e-Gift Card, you'll receive an email from Level Frames that contains the sender's name and a special link that brings you to our site where your card will be activated. When items have been added to your cart, your gift card balance will show up and be applied at checkout. You can always go back to the e-mail and click the same link to see your balance, which will show up at checkout once you have items in your cart.


For physical gift cards, you will visit a page listed on the back of the card to enter your code and redeem the credits.


**What happens if the full amount on a gift card is not used during the first purchase?**


The remaining balance will stay on the card and can be used toward any future framed photo or custom framing project. Just **follow the same link in the original email** to access the balance of a digital gift card, or re-enter the code from the physical card on the same page used before.


You can contact us at hello@levelframes.com if you’ve lost track.


**Can a gift card be returned?**


E-Gift Cards and our physical gift cards are non-refundable.


If any refund is due for a purchase made with a gift card, the funds will be credited back to the same gift card.


**What if I purchased my gift card during a sale, or used credits?**


Score! You purchased the gift card for less than the "face value", but the full amount (before discount or credits) is available to use whenever you or your gift recipient are ready to frame.


**Do gift cards expire?**


Nope. They are good forever!


**Are there any bulk or corporate specials on gift cards?**


Yes. If you’d like to purchase physical cards or e-Gift Cards in quantities greater than 10, get in touch with us a hello@levelframes.com and we will set you up.


> Your recipient will receive this email and be custom framing in no time.

We're making it more convenient, intuitive and affordable to custom frame everything you love.
