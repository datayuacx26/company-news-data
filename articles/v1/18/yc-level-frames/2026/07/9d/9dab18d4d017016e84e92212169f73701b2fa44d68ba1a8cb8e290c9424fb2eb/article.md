The other way to go?

### Hanging your Gallery Wall


The framed photos in our gallery wall bundles arrive with a special bracket system on the back called **WallBuddies** , as well as a mini-hanging guide that shows a recommended layout.


The brackets allow for precise placement of the frames on your wall, and come with a handy template to help with spacing. Simply hold up the frame exactly where you would like it to hang, mark the two top corner points with a pencil or tape, and use the template for nail placement:


The frames hang perfectly flush to the wall and are self-leveling, so you're almost guaranteed to get the placement and hanging perfectly on the first go.
