# Age of Empires Mobile: PC Edition now available


By


- [Athima Chansanchai, Writer](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsexperience/author/chansanchai/)


Great news for players of Age of Empires Mobile! The game is now available on Steam and the Microsoft Store for PCs.


Players who’ve started on mobile can continue to play between their phone, tablet and/or PC without losing progress. The PC Edition optimization includes mouse control and full keyboard navigation.


Get all the details on[Age of Empires](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/age-of-empires-mobile-pc-edition-available-now/) .
