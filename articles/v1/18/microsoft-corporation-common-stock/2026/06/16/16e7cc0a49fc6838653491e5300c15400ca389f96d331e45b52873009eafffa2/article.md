# Elder Scrolls Online introduces new in-game event: High Seas of Tamriel


- [Athima Chansanchai, Writer](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsexperience/author/chansanchai/)


Fans of PC favorite[Elder Scrolls Online](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/the-elder-scrolls-online-standard-edition/BRKX5CRMRTC2/) can look forward to a new in-game event: the High Seas of Tamriel, which XBOX Wire’s Mike Nelson framed as one of the franchise’s bigger experiments, especially with naval combat.


Nelson caught up with Game Director Nick Giacomini and Associate Design Director Jason Barnes to learn more about it.


“It is very different from any of the events that we’ve ever done in The Elder Scrolls Online,” Giacomini says. “It is a destination as opposed to amplifying the world…It involves underwater combat. It involves these kinds of scavenger-hunt collection-type things, like messages in a bottle.”


Find out more at[XBOX Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/06/22/the-elder-scrolls-online-prepares-set-sail-for-adventure-high-seas-of-tamriel/) .
