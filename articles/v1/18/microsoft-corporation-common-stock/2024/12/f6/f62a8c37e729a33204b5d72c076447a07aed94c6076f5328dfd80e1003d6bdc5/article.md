# Microsoft Power Platform & Copilot Studio Stories

This blog post includes a set of Power Platform and Copilot Studio stories curated by the[Power CAT team](https://aka.ms/PowerCATLive) . The stories showcase the ingenuity of individuals and organizations that have adopted[Microsoft Power Platform](https://www.microsoft.com/power-platform) and[Copilot Studio](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-copilot/microsoft-copilot-studio) along with Microsoft 365, Azure, and Dynamics 365 to digitally transform their business. We hope these stories serve as inspiration for you as you embark on your own low-code journey with building agents, apps, and automation.
