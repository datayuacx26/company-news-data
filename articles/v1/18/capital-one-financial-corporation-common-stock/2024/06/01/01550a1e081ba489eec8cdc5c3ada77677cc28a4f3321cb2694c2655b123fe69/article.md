# Capital One Software Releases Cost Breakdown Report Feature

**June 4, 2024, McLean, VA** - Today,[Capital One Software](https://capitalonesoftware.com/) , an enterprise B2B software business of Capital One, announced a new feature in[Capital One Slingshot](https://capitalonesoftware.com/slingshot) , Cost Breakdown Report, which offers customers detailed visuals of Snowflake costs that can be tailored to the unique needs of the organization. With this feature, customers can track and label costs based on tags set by the organization, resulting in a comprehensive, yet customized view of their Snowflake spend.


“Scaling in the Snowflake AI Data Cloud requires robust cost management, and the need for these capabilities will only rise in importance as the volume of data grows and use cases evolve,” said Nima Vadiee, Managing Vice President, Customer Engineering & Success, Capital One Software. “We’re excited to launch the Cost Breakdown Report feature to offer granular visibility into Snowflake costs. With this new feature, Slingshot customers will have both holistic and detailed views into their Snowflake spend, providing insights that can be used to improve efficiency, attribute costs across teams, and implement showbacks across the organization."


The Cost Breakdown Report uses Slingshot tagging capabilities to provide an enhanced level of visibility into Snowflake spend across custom categories. Tags can be assigned to objects—like warehouses and databases—or to specific users, which helps customers then zero-in on specific costs in the Cost Breakdown Report. This level of cost visualization provides organizations with more predictability and the ability to confidently navigate Snowflake cost management.


“We’ve heard from customers that there is a need for comprehensive visibility into Snowflake spend that scales with the organization,” said Vadiee. “The Cost Breakdown Report feature addresses this challenge by providing customers with the ability to connect objects or users back to their costs in a way that is unique to the organization.”


Visit the[Capital One Software website](https://capitalonesoftware.com/) for a demo on the latest Slingshot features.
