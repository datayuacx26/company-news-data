# WisdomTree Reports Monthly Metrics for July 2026


August 12, 2026 6:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_4df2ef68c05138751732bc1f815c7306/wisdomtree/news/2026-08-12_WisdomTree_Reports_Monthly_Metrics_for_July_801.pdf)


*Over $10 billion of net inflows year-to-date, an approximately 12% annualized organic growth rate*


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- WisdomTree, Inc. (NYSE: WT), a global financial innovator, today released monthly metrics for July 2026, including assets under management (AUM) and flow data by asset class.


Note: The table and commentary below exclude AUM and flows related to private assets managed by Ceres Partners, LLC, which had approximately $2 billion in AUM at June 30, 2026. This information is reported with our quarterly earnings results.


Commentary:


- Generated over $1.3 billion of net inflows during July and over $10 billion of net inflows year-to-date, a nearly 12% pace of annualized organic growth
- AUM in the U.S. business ended the month at over $100 billion for the first time in firm history
- Net inflows were driven by strong demand for shorter duration fixed income and ongoing traction in global equities across the broad value and quality growth segments, Japanese equities and thematics
