# WisdomTree Recognized at The Future of Finance Awards 2026 for Leadership in Tokenized Assets and Onchain Infrastructure

# WisdomTree Recognized at The Future of Finance Awards 2026 for Leadership in Tokenized Assets and Onchain Infrastructure


June 25, 2026 6:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_1896cf5fd14b9c2ac925f10e9a8beb4a/wisdomtree/news/2026-06-25_WisdomTree_Recognized_at_The_Future_of_Finance_795.pdf)


*Firm Receives Honors for Best Digital Asset Fund Issuer in North America and* *Best Tokenized Transfer Agent*


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- WisdomTree, Inc. (NYSE: WT), a global financial innovator, today announced it has been recognized with two honors at The Future of Finance Awards 2026, receiving Best Digital Asset Fund Issuer in North America and Best Tokenized Transfer Agent for WisdomTree Transfers, Inc.


Presented annually by Future of Finance, the awards recognize organizations advancing innovation across financial services, digital assets, tokenization and next-generation market infrastructure. The program celebrates companies that are helping redefine how financial products are issued, transferred and accessed in an increasingly digital economy.


The award for Best Digital Asset Fund Issuer in North America recognizes WisdomTree's leadership in bringing regulated investment products onchain. Guided by its goal to be the leader in onchain registered funds, WisdomTree currently offers one of the industry's largest lineups of tokenized funds and has established itself as a leader in tokenized real-world assets on public blockchains. Through platforms including WisdomTree Prime® and WisdomTree Connect™, the firm provides retail and institutional investors with access to SEC-registered tokenized funds, stablecoins and other blockchain-enabled financial products.


The Best Tokenized Transfer Agent award recognizes WisdomTree Transfers, WisdomTree's blockchain-enabled transfer agency and registrar service. As transfer agent for the firm's tokenized funds, WisdomTree Transfers maintains official ownership records across both traditional book-entry and blockchain-based records. The platform helps modernize core fund infrastructure and supports new capabilities for regulated assets on public blockchains, including native peer-to-peer transferability.


“Tokenization represents the next evolution of asset management, enabling assets to move with the speed, accessibility and programmability that today's investors expect,” said Will Peck, Head of Digital Assets at WisdomTree. “This recognition reflects our commitment to building the regulated products and infrastructure needed to bring real-world assets onchain while preserving the investor protections that have long been foundational to financial markets. We believe tokenization will transform how investors access, hold and transfer assets, and WisdomTree is focused on leading that evolution through responsible innovation.”


The Future of Finance Awards are judged by a panel of industry experts and recognize organizations demonstrating innovation, measurable impact and leadership across the evolving financial services landscape.


For more information about the Future of Finance Awards and to see the complete list of winners, visit[https://www.futureoffinance.biz/awards-2026](https://www.futureoffinance.biz/awards-2026) .
