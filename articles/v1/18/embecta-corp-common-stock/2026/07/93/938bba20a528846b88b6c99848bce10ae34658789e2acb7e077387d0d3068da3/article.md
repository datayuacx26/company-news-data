## embecta to Report Fiscal Third Quarter Financial Results


July 23, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.embecta.com/node/9496/pdf)


PARSIPPANY, N.J., July 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Embecta Corp. (“embecta”) (Nasdaq: EMBC), a global company that is advancing its 100-year legacy in insulin delivery to become a broad-based medical supplies company, will host a conference call to discuss its fiscal third quarter 2026 financial results, provide an operational update, and host a question and answer session, at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Friday, August 7, 2026.

The live webcast can also be accessed via the company’s website at[investors.embecta.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=szag_JCC-NF-cPkHUBfgKXrKkJIWdmITNwa3gtJJW0LZFSp1xRBStfPWXCAPTIIdFGAhxg18QCjX6DIrQOzNJmyWMV44vipyfxou4FDxgpY=) .
