## embecta to Participate at the 2026 BofA Securities Global Health Care Conference


May 8, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.embecta.com/node/9421/pdf)


PARSIPPANY, N.J., May 08, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Embecta Corp. (embecta) (Nasdaq: EMBC) today announced that management will present at the 2026 BofA Securities Global Health Care Conference in Las Vegas on Wednesday, May 13, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PT / 6:00 p.m. ET.


Audio webcasts of the presentations will be accessible under the “News & Events” section of the Company's investor relations website at[investors.embecta.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=l85q5c78xPJgWenRis0IQQcuKgErYOhAmBH4MOEm8BVb3jU83J8PrNbZMYA5vIbF6YPptGkjxmHddgioFsX6Ja-AeIfFKWtdcw0cS1_uy_U=) .
