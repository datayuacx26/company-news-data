# Depict vs Tagalys: Merchandising, Search and Recommendations for Fashion and Lifestyle Brands

#### **Why Fashion Brands Are Seeking Alternatives to Tagalys**


Tagalys is a visual merchandising platform for Shopify, built for fashion brands wanting programmatic control over collections, search and recommendations - positioning itself as an alternative to Depict. It offers a Smart Ranking Engine, flexible pinning and, more recently, Looks (styled sets holding together across a collection page), Sequences for building visual aisles, and Markets-aware merchandising for brands selling across regions. Still, fashion brands comparing Depict and Tagalys often need capabilities beyond sequencing and pinning, furthering narrative-led merchandising and contextual product discovery.


For fashion ecommerce managers using or evaluating Tagalys, it’s worth understanding how it compares to Depict. Depict is a fashion and lifestyle alternative to Tagalys, working across Shopify, Centra, Salesforce Commerce Cloud and headless setups. It meets the same merchandising, search, and recommendation needs within a unified platform, with LLM-enhanced search and stronger focus on fashion-aware discovery.

**Visual Merchandising Capabilities**


For fashion and lifestyle brands, visual presentation of products is crucial to both brand identity and conversion rates. This is where both platforms take different approaches.


##### **Depict’s Fashion and Lifestyle Approach**


Depict gives merchandisers control to express brand identity as well as the automation to maintain optimisation across every page. Its visual tools include:


- **Content Cards:** Add custom-size images, videos and instagram posts to showcase brand identity, with hover and default images per collection.
- **Pinned Products:** Pin products to specific positions, maintaining editorial control without battling the algorithm.
- **Repeated Products:** Surface the same product across various collection pages, without duplication.
- **Product Image Selector:** Choose the image that best represents each product, as per the collection’s context.
- **Smart Sorting:** Set merchandising rules once, and apply everywhere. Boost bestsellers, burying out-of-stock items, whilst maintaining pinned products’ positions.


##### **Tagalys' Programmatic Control**


Tagalys centres its visual merchandising around rules and performance data:


- **Trending Score:** Each product is ranked by engagement, tag signals, and freshness; updated daily.
- **Pin with Conditions:** Pin any product to any position, with conditions to move it automatically when stock, size or season changes.
- **Looks:** Group 2-8 products into styled sets that stay together when moved on Shopify PLPs, can be reused across different pages via LookBooks.
- **Sequences:** Create visual aisles by colour, drop or narrative, as you would with a physical store.
- **Markets:** Localise sorting and pinning by Shopify Market without duplicating collections.


#### **Search & Discovery Experience**


Fashion and lifestyle shoppers often search with more descriptive and contextual language than a typical query. For example, ‘boho summer dress’, as opposed to a SKU or category name.


****


##### **Depict: Understanding Shopper Language**


Depict’s Search is AI-powered, combining semantic understanding with keyword search and merchandising rules, working less statically and more like an in-store sales associate:


- **Hybrid Search:** Runs each query through AI, keyword matching, merchandising rules, automated synonyms and automated translations in parallel.
- **Search Everything:** One query surfaces products, collections, policy pages and editorial content, reducing bounce rate for answers already on-site.
- **AI Overviews:** Conversational LLM-generated summaries answer queries about sizing directly within the results.
- **Searchandising:** Apply merchandising strategy directly to search results: pin, boost and bury products in alignment with campaigns and business goals.


##### **Tagalys: Rules-Based Search**


Tagalys’ search is built around control and tuning versus understanding natural language:


- **Predictive Search:** Surfaces results as shoppers type, handling typos before they finish.
- **Search Accuracy:** Fixes inaccurate results at index-level, rather than per query.
- **Pinned and Popular Search:** Allows pinning specific results, popular queries or what is trending in search.
- **Search Results:** Enables refinement and customisation for what appears for any given query.


#### **Product Recommendations**


Merchandising, search and recommendations are becoming increasingly synchronised rather than operating as three separate tools. A shopper’s view of a collection page should inform the products they are recommended, and vice versa. Both platforms treat recommendations as a core part of the stack, not just an add-on.


##### **Depict: Style-Aware Recommendations**


Depict meshes products, AOV, and behavioural strategy into a singular recommendation engine, governed by the same merchandising rules across search and collections:


- **Same brand and Similar Products:** AI reads visual and stylistic product similarity, from images and descriptions as well as shared tags and categories.
- **Shop The Look:** Surfaces complementary pieces to complete an outfit.
- **Cross-sell and Upsell** : Increases order value with genuinely fitting pairings.
- **Previously Seen and Search Intent:** Personalises based on search and browse behaviour, blended per shopper.


##### **Tagalys: Data-Driven Recommendations**


Tagalys uses first-party behavioural and performance data to build their recommendations:


- **Similar Products:** Suggests alternatives when products are out of stock, aiding with sales recovery.
- **Trending/Bestsellers:** Surfaces high-performing products to increase cart value.
- **New Arrivals:** Highlights recently added products, in sync with the same trending data powering collection ranking.

#### **Conclusion: Choosing Between Depict and Tagalys**


For fashion and lifestyle brands building visually compelling shopping experiences, both Depict and Tagalys offer real value, with different strengths.


Depict leans into the narrative-driven product discovery experience, LLM-powered hybrid search, style-aware recommendations, and native support for embedding video and social content, across Shopify, Centra, Salesforce Commerce Cloud and headless setups.


Tagalys leans into programmatic, rules-based control: automated ranking, flexible pinning, styled Looks, performance-based recommendations and market-by-market merchandising for primarily Shopify and Shopify Plus brands.


If you prioritise AI-driven search, native content integration, or are looking for a platform that grows with you beyond Shopify, Depict is likely the better fit. If your priority is granular, rules-based control within a Shopify-only setup, Tagalys is worth looking closer at.
