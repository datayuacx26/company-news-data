# Depict vs Kimonix: Choosing the Right Merchandising Platform for your Fashion or Lifestyle Brand

#### Why Fashion and Lifestyle Brands Compare Depict and Kimonix


Both platforms help ecommerce teams merchandise collections more efficiently, but they solve different problems, with Kimonix now positioning itself directly against Depict, having published its own comparison.


Kimonix built its reputation as a profit-driven, no-code solution for merchandising Shopify stores, using signals like sales, margin and inventory to automatically sort collections. It remains largely used by fashion and apparel brands, with smaller footprint across other verticals like beauty, home and sporting goods. In 2026, it expanded further with the addition of AI-powered on-site search and an AI shopping agent.


Depict is built for fashion and lifestyle brands that need to control the visual story of their collection pages, not just the sorting order, whilst also offering hybrid AI search and recommendations. It supports Shopify, Centra, Salesforce Commerce Cloud and headless storefronts, reflecting how many fashion and lifestyle brands have scaled beyond a singular platform.


If you are currently on Kimonix, and your priority is visual storytelling alongside a platform built to grow with you beyond Shopify, Depict is worth a look.

#### Visual Merchandising Capabilities


For fashion and lifestyle brands, the visual presentation of products is crucial to both brand identity and conversion rates. This is an area where the two platforms take contrasting approaches.


****


**Depict’s Fashion and Lifestyle Approach**


Depict gives merchandisers the tools to shape brand identity alongside automation that keeps every page optimised:


- **Content Cards:** Weave custom-side images, video and Instagram posts directly into collection pages.
- **Pinned Products:** Keep specific products in specific positions, maintaining editorial control without fighting the algorithm.
- **Repeated Products:** Feature the same product across multiple positions with different images to show range and versatility.
- **Product Image Selector:** Choose the exact image (front, back, detail) for each collection context.
- **Smart Sorting:** Set merchandising rules once, and apply everywhere, boost bestsellers, bury out-of-stock items, whilst pinned products keep in place.


****


**Kimonix’s Signal-Driven Approach**


Kimonix merchandises collections primarily through automation and data:


- **Collection Sorting:** AI-driven sorting across 100+ signals- including sales, margin, variant-level stock, returns and shopper behaviour.
- **Product Grouping and Pinning:** Build shop-the-look product groupings and pin key items on top of automated rules.
- **A/B Testing and Merchandising Analytics:** Test sorting strategies and measure what lifts conversion.
- **Multiple Markets:** Geo-merchandise collections and recommendations differently power region.
- **No-Code Implementation:** Runs entirely inside Shopify admin, with no frontend changes required.


#### Search and Discovery


The gap between the two platforms has narrowed with search. Kimonix previously had no dedicated search product until 2026, now offering an AI Sales and Shopping Agent- an agentic, conversational search experience with two-way cart sync, support for 50+ languages, and built-in revenue analytics.


Depict’s search combines AI, keyword matching, and merchandising rules into a singular hybrid approach:


- **Hybrid Search:** Runs every query through AI, keyword matching, merchandising rules, automated synonyms and automated translations in parallel.
- **Search Everything:** One query surfaces products, collections, editorial content, and policy pages.
- **AI Overviews:** Conversational, LLM-generated summaries answer questions directly on the results page.
- **Searchandising:** Apply merchandising strategy directly to search results- pin, boost, and bury products to align with campaigns.


Kimonix’s agent, contrastingly, is built to close the sale inside the conversation itself, adding to the cart without leaving the chat, whereas Depict’s search is built to sit across the entire site experience, applying the same merchandising logic used within collection pages and product recommendations.


#### Ease of Implementation


Depict installs natively on Shopify for visual merchandising and can also be deployed to Centra, Salesforce Commerce Cloud or a headless storefront via API/SDK, this is useful for fashion and lifestyle brands that have outgrown a single-platform setup. Collection pages can go live within minutes, cutting up to 70% of routine merchandising and optimization, with multi-market and multi-store tailoring to replicate strategies across regions and storefronts.


Kimonix remains Shopify and Shopify plus only, with a no-code setup inside the Shopify admin. The theme remains the same, making it a low-lift install for teams without dev resources, though not an option for brands running Centra, Salesforce Commerce Cloud or headless architecture.

#### Pricing in 2026


Depict prices in tiers based on monthly site traffic, starting at $152/month for Merchandising or Search, and $88/month for Recommendations (billed annually), with a 10% discount when bundling all three. This structure generally suits brands running at least 50,000 monthly sessions- custom pricing applies for larger volumes or additional features such as multi-market catalogs.


Kimonix offers tiered pricing based on average monthly orders instead, as well as a custom tier for more complex needs.


#### Is Depict Right for Your Fashion or Lifestyle Brand?


Depict is the strongest fit if you:


- Want visually distinctive, high converting collection pages rather than automated grids alone
- Want brand-led storytelling as well as performance metrics
- Want AI-driven search built to sit across your whole site, not just as a bolt-on
- Run on, or are considering platforms other than Shopify- such as Centra, Salesforce Commerce Cloud or headless
- Want to save merchandising time without losing creative control


#### FAQ


**Is Kimonix a good alternative to Depict?**


Kimonix is a strong choice for Shopify and Shopify plus brands wanting automated no-code collection sorting driven by sales and inventory data. Furthermore they offer AI search and an AI shopping agent. Whilst it serves brands across a few adjacent categories, its customer base remains predominantly fashion and apparel and is less specialised for visual storytelling than Depict.


**Does Kimonix offer AI Search?**


As of 2026, yes, in addition to an AI Sales and Shopping Agent.


**Can Depict be used outside of Shopify?**


Yes, Depict supports Shopify, Centra, Salesforce Commerce Cloud natively, and can be deployed to headless storefronts via API/SDK.


**Which platform is better for fashion and lifestyle brands specifically?**


Depict is purpose built for fashion and lifestyle merchandising, with tools like content cards, image selection by view, and AI-driven search built to work across the whole site. Kimonix also serves fashion brands, with smaller presence across a few adjacent categories.


#### Choosing Between Depict and Kimonix


Both platforms have strengths and the right choice for your site depends on what your team optimises for. Depict leads on brand-led visual storytelling, AI-driven search across the whole site and multi-platform flexibility. Kimonix leads on on-code automation and signal driven collection sorting, now paired with AI search and its own shopping agent.


If visual storytelling, AI-powered discovery, and platform flexibility matter most to your brand, Depict is the more specialised option. If you prioritise automation inside Shopify with minimal setup, Kimonix is worth evaluating.
