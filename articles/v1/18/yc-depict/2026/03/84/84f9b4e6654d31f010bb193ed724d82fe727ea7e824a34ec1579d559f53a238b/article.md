If you're a fashion ecommerce manager currently using Tagalys or considering it as an option, you may be wondering how it compares to other specialized solutions in the market. ## Visual Merchandising Capabilities


For fashion brands, the visual presentation of products is crucial to both brand identity and conversion rates. This is an area where both platforms offer strong but different approaches.


### Depict's Fashion-First Approach


Depict was built specifically for fashion merchandising, with visual tools that enable merchandisers to:


- [Content Cards](http://depict.ai/features/shopify#curate-content-cards) : Seamlessly integrate custom-size images, videos, and Instagram posts directly into collection pages to tell your brand story
- [Image Selector](http://depict.ai/features/shopify#curate-image-selector) : Choose the perfect product image view (front, back, detail) for each collection context to showcase apparel effectively
- [Duplicate Products](http://depict.ai/features/shopify#curate-duplicate-products) : Feature the same product in multiple positions with different images to highlight versatility
- Visual Storytelling: Build narrative-driven collection pages that showcase your brand identity while driving conversions


### Tagalys' Programmatic Control


Tagalys offers visual merchandising with a focus on programmatic control:


- Auto-sort with Smart Ranking Engine: Automatically promotes top performers to prominent positions
- Pin Anything, Anywhere: Ability to pin products in specific positions and unpin them when needed
- Create Aesthetic Visual Aisles: Group products by color, style, or theme to create visually appealing collections
- Customizable Results: Precision control over what appears in search results


## Search & Discovery Experience


Fashion shoppers browse and search differently than customers in other ecommerce categories, using more descriptive and contextual language.


### Depict: Understanding Fashion Language


Depict's LLM-powered "agentic" search acts like an in-store sales associate who truly understands fashion:


- Natural Auto-Correct: Understands misspellings of fashion terminology and brand names
- Deep Contextual Synonyms: Recognizes that "evening wear" and "cocktail attire" might be related searches
- Style Understanding: Comprehends nuanced fashion concepts like "boho chic" or "minimalist aesthetic"
- Natural Refinement: Allows conversational refinement of searches, just like talking to a knowledgeable associate


### Tagalys: AI-Assisted Search

- Handle Typos, Synonyms, and Errors: Ensures shoppers find what they want despite search errors

- Standard Synonym Management: Handles basic synonym relationships
