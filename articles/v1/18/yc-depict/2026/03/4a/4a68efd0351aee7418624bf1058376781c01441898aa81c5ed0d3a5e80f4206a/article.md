# Product Collection Layout Checklist for Fashion Brands

A well-designed product collection layout can directly impact sales, with conversion rates varying widely across fashion categories: **7.4% for accessories** , **3.6% for women's fashion** , and just **0.8% for menswear** . Poor layouts lead to abandonment rates as high as **90%** , while clear navigation and smart tools can significantly improve user experience and sales.


### Key Takeaways:


- **Set Goals** : Define clear visual merchandising goals aligned with your brand identity.
- **Create a Style Guide** : Maintain consistency in colors, typography, and photo standards.
- **Organize Categories** : Use clear product categories and add filters like size, color, and price.
- **Optimize Photos** : Use uniform product images and add on-model photos for context.
- **Leverage Tools** : Automate sorting and add[AI-driven recommendations](https://docs.depict.ai/api-guide/recommendations/api-client/fetching) to boost conversions.
- **Mobile Optimization** : Simplify navigation and speed up page loading for mobile users.


### Quick Wins:


- Use **AI tools** to auto-sort collections and highlight best-sellers.
- Add **filters** and **search options** to improve navigation.
- Optimize for **mobile** to reduce bounce rates.


These strategies can cut merchandising time by **40%** , improve conversions by **4x** , and increase average order value by **26%** . Start implementing today to see measurable results.


## How To Create Collections on Shopify Store


## Set Your Visual Merchandising Goals


Define clear goals for your visual merchandising to align with your brand identity and audience preferences. These goals will help you boost sales and create a lasting impression.


### Create Your Brand Style Guide


A brand style guide is essential for maintaining consistency across all visual elements. It outlines the key details that define your brand's look and feel.


Here’s what to include:


- **Visual Elements** : Specify your color palette, typography, and rules for logo usage.
- **Photography Guidelines** : Outline how product photos should look, including lighting, angles, and model positioning.
- **Layout Standards** : Provide rules for grid systems, spacing, and how products should be arranged.


Top brands rely on consistent guidelines to stand out. For example,[Hermès](https://www.hermes.com/us/en/) ensures its iconic orange is present across all platforms through strict color codes. Similarly,[Zara](https://www.zara.com/us/) ’s photography style - featuring neutral backgrounds and natural lighting - creates a look that’s instantly recognizable.


Once your style guide is in place, schedule regular updates to your collections to keep things fresh and engaging.


### Schedule Collection Updates


With your visual standards set, it’s time to plan regular collection updates. These updates keep your store aligned with seasonal trends and customer expectations.


Here’s a sample timeline for seasonal collections:


Collection TypeTypical TimelineKey FocusSpring/SummerDecember – MayLight fabrics, bright colorsPre-FallJune – AugustTransitional piecesFall/WinterSeptember – NovemberWarm materials, layeringResort/CruiseNovember – JanuaryHoliday and vacation wear


To make these updates impactful:


- **Plan Ahead** : Collaborate with your team and suppliers to create a detailed calendar.
- **Use Data** : Review past sales to guide your collection decisions.
- **Stay Flexible** : Consider adopting a "see now, buy now" model for trending items.


A centralized team that oversees collection planning can effectively balance creative ideas with business objectives. For instance, in 2017, brands using this approach saw an 8.2% revenue increase, compared to 3.5% for traditional retailers. Companies like H&M and Zara excel at this by making quick decisions and managing their own retail channels.


## Build Easy-to-Use Collection Navigation


Clear navigation is key to helping customers find what they need quickly. Studies show that improving product navigation can lower abandonment rates from 67–90% to just 17–33%.


### Set Up Product Categories


Organizing your products into clear categories makes browsing easier. Here's a breakdown:


Category LevelExamplePurposeParent CategoryClothingMain product groupSecondary CategoryWomen's ClothingGender/departmentTertiary CategoryWomen's TopsSpecific product typeCollection TagsNew Arrivals, Best SellersSpecial groupings


Some tips for creating effective categories:


- Use simple, familiar names that resonate with customers.
- Limit category depth to 2–3 levels to keep navigation straightforward.
- Highlight featured categories in your main menu.
- Add SEO-friendly descriptions to category pages.
- Use high-quality banner images to make category pages visually appealing.


Pay attention to search trends. For example, if "summer dresses" or "workout tops" are popular terms, create dedicated categories for them.


### Add Search Filters


Filters make it easier for shoppers to narrow down choices. Data shows that visitors who use site search are 80% more likely to make a purchase, while 76% will leave if their search fails.


Helpful filters to include:


- Size and fit
- Color options
- Price ranges
- Material or fabric
- Style or occasion
- Brand (especially useful for multi-brand stores)


"Product filters help visitors quickly sort through your product collection to zone in on products they need." - TheGood.com


A great example is Fully, a DTC brand that revamped its filtering system in 2020. By refining categories and adding material filters, they saw a 5.97% increase in conversion rates across desktop and mobile.


Best practices for filters:


- Make it easy to add or remove filters.
- Allow shoppers to select multiple options within the same filter type.
- Show the number of products available for each filter option.
- Update results instantly as filters are applied.
- Ensure filters work well on mobile devices.


Platforms like Depict offer tools like a custom grid builder, allowing[fashion brands](https://depict.ai/partners) to create user-friendly category layouts and smart filtering systems without needing coding skills. Automated sorting features also help keep collections organized as inventory changes.

## Improve Product Presentation


Make your product collection stand out by displaying items in a consistent and engaging way. Studies reveal that **75% of online shoppers depend on product images** when deciding what to buy.


### Standardize Product Photos


Uniform product photos create a polished and professional look for your collection. Two common issues in e-commerce are inconsistent image sizes and poor alignment.


Here’s a quick guide to photo standards:


ElementSpecificationPurpose **Image Size** Consistent dimensionsCreates a clean, polished look **Background** Neutral and cleanKeeps focus on the product **Alignment** Standardized pointsEnsures visual consistency **Cropping** Uniform spacingBalances the layout


To achieve this, develop an alignment template that includes:


- Clear guidelines for positioning products
- Defined spacing around items
- Key alignment points (e.g., neck, hips, arms)
- File size requirements


Adding on-model photos to your collection can further elevate its appeal.

## Make Collections Work on All Devices


Optimizing your site for mobile users is a must for fashion brands. With 33% of mobile sites making it too hard for users to browse product catalogs, you need to ensure your collections perform well on any device.


### Build Mobile-First Navigation


Mobile users want quick and easy access to your product catalog. Complicated menus can frustrate shoppers, so it's important to simplify navigation. Here's how you can structure it effectively:


Navigation ElementDesktopMobile OptimizationProduct CategoriesMega-dropdownsDisplay as top-level itemsSecondary LinksHeader menuPlace in a clear secondary areaSearch & FiltersSidebarUse collapsible panelsPopular CategoriesMenu itemsDefault display with "More" option


Edward Scott, Research Lead at Baymard Institute, explains: *"Mobile users rely on the main navigation to understand the product catalog and zero in on products"* . Avoid hiding categories under vague labels or creating menus that are too complex.


To improve navigation for mobile users:


- Keep categories visible as top-level items.
- Use collapsible panels for filters to save space.
- Ensure buttons and links are large enough for easy tapping.


Once your navigation is user-friendly, the next step is to make sure your pages load quickly.
