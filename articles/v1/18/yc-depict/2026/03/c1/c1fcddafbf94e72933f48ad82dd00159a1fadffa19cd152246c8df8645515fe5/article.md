Experimenting with collection layouts can lead to better results. Tools like Depict.ai's custom grid builder let you test different elements, such as product sorting, image sizes, grid configurations, filter positions, and page load speeds.


A great example comes from[Socium Media](https://www.sociummedia.com/) , which tested variations in product titles. For accurate results, test one element at a time. Combine these findings with customer feedback to refine your collection strategy further.
