## Search & Discovery Experience


The way customers discover products is fundamentally different in fashion compared to other ecommerce categories. Fashion shoppers often browse with less specific intent, using descriptive and contextual language.


### Depict: Like Having an In-Store Fashion Associate


Our LLM-powered "agentic" search acts like an in-store sales associate who truly understands fashion:


- **Natural Auto-Correct** : Understands misspellings of fashion terminology and brand names
- **Deep Contextual Synonyms** : Recognizes that "evening wear" and "cocktail attire" might be related searches
- **Style Understanding** : Comprehends nuanced fashion concepts like "boho chic" or "streetwear inspired"
- **Natural Refinement** : Allows conversational refinement of searches, just like talking to a knowledgeable associate, as to setting up and navigating filters


### Nosto: Traditional Search Approach


Nosto's search capabilities, while functional, rely on more traditional methods:


- Rule-based synonym matching with common pairs
- Manual tagging for style, color, fit, and theme attributes
- Separate queries or applications of filters rather than natural refinement
- Less understanding of fashion-specific terminology and contexts
