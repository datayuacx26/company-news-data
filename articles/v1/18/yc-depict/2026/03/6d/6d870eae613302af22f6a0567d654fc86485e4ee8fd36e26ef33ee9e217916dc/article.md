# How to sell more than your bestsellers

In the competitive world of e-commerce, it's common for a handful of bestsellers to dominate sales. However, relying solely on these top performers can leave a vast portion of your product catalog underexposed, missing out on potential revenue. This article explores advanced strategies to enhance product discovery, ensuring that your entire range gets the attention it deserves.


## The "Long Tail" Challenge in E-commerce


The "long tail" concept refers to the vast number of niche products that individually sell less but collectively can rival or surpass the sales of bestsellers. In e-commerce, this translates to a small percentage of products generating the majority of sales, while the rest remain overlooked. Addressing this imbalance requires deliberate strategies to bring these hidden gems to the forefront.


## Techniques for Introducing Shoppers to New Products


1. **Personalized Recommendations** : Utilize AI-driven tools to suggest products based on user behavior, browsing history, and preferences.
2. **Dynamic Homepage Displays** : Regularly update your homepage to feature a mix of bestsellers and lesser-known products, encouraging exploration.
3. **Email Marketing Campaigns** : Segment your audience and send tailored emails highlighting new or underexposed products that align with their interests.
4. **Limited-Time Offers** : Create urgency by offering discounts or promotions on specific products for a limited period.
5. **Strategic Bundles:** Pair best-selling products with lesser-known products and offer a bundle discount.


## Creating Discovery-Focused Collection Structures


Organizing your product catalog in a way that promotes discovery is crucial. Consider the following approaches:


- **Thematic Collections** : Group products based on themes, occasions, or trends to provide context and inspire purchases.
- **User-Generated Collections** : Allow customers or influencers to create and share their own collections or wishlists, fostering community engagement.
- **Rotating Featured Sections** : Regularly update sections like "Staff Picks" or "Hidden Gems" to spotlight different products.


## Using Content to Highlight Underexposed Products


Content marketing can be a powerful tool to draw attention to products that don't naturally attract high traffic. Strategies include:


- **Blog Posts** : Write articles that incorporate and link to specific products, providing context and usage ideas.
- **Social Media Campaigns** : Feature underexposed products in creative posts, stories, or reels to reach a broader audience.
- **Influencer Collaborations** : Partner with influencers to showcase lesser-known products, leveraging their reach and credibility.


## Measuring and Improving Product Discovery Metrics


To assess the effectiveness of your strategies, monitor key metrics:


- **Product Views** : Track the number of views each product receives to identify which items are gaining attention.
- **Conversion Rates** : Measure the percentage of views that lead to purchases, helping to evaluate product appeal and page effectiveness.
- **Bounce Rates** : High bounce rates may indicate that visitors aren't finding what they're looking for, signaling a need for improved navigation or content.
- **Time on Page** : Longer durations suggest that users are engaging with the content, which can be a positive indicator of interest.


## Conclusion


Expanding the spotlight beyond bestsellers requires a multifaceted approach, combining personalized recommendations, strategic content, and thoughtful organization. By implementing these advanced strategies, retailers can unlock the full potential of their product range, driving increased engagement and sales across the board.
