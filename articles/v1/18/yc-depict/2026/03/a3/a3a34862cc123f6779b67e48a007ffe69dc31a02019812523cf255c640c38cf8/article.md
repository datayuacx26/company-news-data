## 5 Common Product Grid Problems


Fixing these problems can help you get the most out of your[Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/) or[Centra](https://centra.com/) tools for better visual merchandising. Product grid issues can hurt sales and drive customers away. Here are five key problems that could be holding your grid back.

### Uneven Grid Layouts


Inconsistent product layouts can make your store look unprofessional and reduce customer confidence. Without proper styling, products often default to a vertical arrangement. This is especially common in stores with product images of varying dimensions.

### Poor Mobile Display


Many stores struggle with mobile responsiveness, leading to images being cropped or distorted. This is a common issue for stores using themes like[Dawn](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/themes-by-shopify/dawn) .


> "Regardless of what I select (Adapt to Image, Portrait, or Square), the image stays as a rectangle." - Rampz, Tourist


### Slow Loading Grids


Heavy, unoptimized images can make product grids painfully slow to load, especially on mobile devices. Customers won’t stick around if they’re waiting too long. The trick is finding the right balance between image quality and load speed.


### Basic Sort and Filter Options


Simple sorting tools like price or alphabetical order often aren’t enough. Without more advanced filters - like style tags, size, color, or material - customers are left scrolling endlessly. This can lead to frustration, abandoned sessions, and missed sales opportunities.
