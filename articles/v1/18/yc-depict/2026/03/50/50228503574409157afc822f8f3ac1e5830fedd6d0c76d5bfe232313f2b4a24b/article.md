# Depict vs Kimonix: A Comparison for Fashion Ecommerce Managers

## Why Fashion Brands Are Exploring Alternatives to Kimonix


Kimonix has established itself as a profit-driven merchandising platform for Shopify stores, helping ecommerce businesses balance what sells best with what is best for them to sell. While it offers solid capabilities for collection merchandising and product recommendations, fashion and lifestyle brands often find themselves seeking solutions that provide more specialized features tailored to their unique visual merchandising needs.


If you're a fashion ecommerce manager currently using Kimonix or considering it as an option, you may be wondering how it compares to other specialized solutions in the market. This is where Depict offers a compelling alternative specifically designed for fashion brands' unique merchandising requirements.

## Visual Merchandising Capabilities


For fashion brands, the visual presentation of products is crucial to both brand identity and conversion rates. This is an area where the two platforms take different approaches.


### Depict's Fashion-First Approach


Depict was built specifically for fashion merchandising, with visual tools that enable merchandisers to:


- [Content Cards](https://depict.ai/features/shopify#curate-content-cards) : Seamlessly integrate custom-size images, videos, and Instagram posts directly into collection pages to tell your brand story
- [Image Selector](https://depict.ai/features/shopify#curate-image-selector) : Choose the perfect product image view (front, back, detail) for each collection context to showcase apparel effectively
- [Duplicate Products](https://depict.ai/features/shopify#curate-duplicate-products) : Feature the same product in multiple positions with different images to highlight versatility
- Visual Storytelling: Build narrative-driven collection pages that showcase your brand identity while driving conversions


### Kimonix's Profit-Driven Approach


Kimonix offers merchandising with a focus on profit optimization:


- Smart Collections That Boost Profits: AI merchandising strategy engine (AMS) to create, sort, and automate smart collections
- Sorting Strategies: Execute sorting strategies that increase sales and optimize inventory
- A/B Testing: Integrated, automated testing to evaluate the effectiveness of merchandising strategies
- No Code Implementation: Works through Shopify's admin with no frontend changes required


## Search & Discovery Experience


Fashion shoppers browse and search differently than customers in other ecommerce categories, using more descriptive and contextual language.


### Depict: Understanding Fashion Language


Depict's LLM-powered "agentic" search acts like an in-store sales associate who truly understands fashion:


- Natural Auto-Correct: Understands misspellings of fashion terminology and brand names
- Deep Contextual Synonyms: Recognizes that "evening wear" and "cocktail attire" might be related searches
- Style Understanding: Comprehends nuanced fashion concepts like "boho chic" or "minimalist aesthetic"
- Natural Refinement: Allows conversational refinement of searches, just like talking to a knowledgeable associate


### Kimonix: Focused on Collection Sorting & Product Recommendations


Kimonix focuses primarily on merchandising and does not offer a Search solution. However, they do also provide Product Recommendations, focused on recommending products that balance customer interest with business goals.

For fashion and lifestyle brands seeking to enhance their online merchandising, both Depict and Kimonix offer valuable capabilities, but with different strengths and focuses.


Depict excels in creating beautiful, narrative-driven collection pages with its fashion-first approach, LLM-powered search that understands fashion context, and comprehensive visual merchandising tools that balance brand storytelling with performance.
