# 【DimOrder x Meituan Dianping】Announce formal cooperation to help merchants create a new era

We are delighted to announce a strategic partnership between DimOrder and[Meituan](https://cn.linkedin.com/company/meituan?trk=public_post_reshare-text) Dianping ([3690.HK](http://3690.hk/) ), a leading platform in China that combines the best features of Yelp and TripAdvisor! With a staggering 690 million annual user transactions and a market valuation of $110 billion, Meituan Dianping is one of the largest lifestyle platforms in China.


This collaboration offers our esteemed restaurant partners access to an extensive user base of over 667 million on the Meituan Dianping platform. They can now leverage targeted advertising, run Groupon-style promotions, and much more! The opportunities for growth and exposure are virtually limitless.


We believe this[#partnership](https://www.linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?session_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Ffeed%2Fhashtag%2Fpartnership&trk=public_post_reshare-text) will provide immense value to our restaurateurs and significantly enhance their businesses. As we continue our mission to create a comprehensive food and beverage ecosystem, we are thrilled to bring this powerful alliance to fruition. Join us as we embark on a new era of collaboration and success with Meituan Dianping!
