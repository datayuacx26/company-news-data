# BW Group rings the Opening Bell at NYSE

BW Group has kicked off the week in New York at the NYSE, together with its listed BW-affiliated companies BW LPG, Cadeler and Hafnia, alongside BW Energy and BW Offshore.


On Monday, 15 June 2026, BW Group Chairman Andreas Sohmen-Pao rang the NYSE Opening Bell, celebrating the strength of our ecosystem across maritime, energy and infrastructure. For more than 70 years, BW Group has invested in the assets and infrastructure that underpin global trade, energy security and economic growth. We remain committed to our mission of delivering energy for the world today while developing the solutions needed for tomorrow.
