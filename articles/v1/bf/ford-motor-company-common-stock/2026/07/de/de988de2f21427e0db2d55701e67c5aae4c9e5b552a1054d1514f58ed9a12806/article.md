# Ford's Quality Momentum Continues: JD Power 2026 APEAL Study Shows Sustained Improvement

If you've been following our journey at Ford, you know we’ve been incredibly focused on fundamentally transforming our quality.


Recently, we earned the number one spot among mainstream brands in the JD Power 2026 Initial Quality Study (IQS).SM That was a tremendous milestone for us, proving that our engineering, supply chain, and manufacturing processes are reducing defects.


But building a great vehicle isn't just about what doesn't go wrong. It’s also about what goes incredibly right.


That’s why I am proud to share the results of the JD Power 2026 U.S. APEAL Study,SM which demonstrate that we are making sustained strides in improving the things people genuinely love about their Ford vehicles.


If you aren't familiar with the APEAL (Automotive Performance, Execution, and Layout) Study, it measures a customer's emotional attachment and level of excitement with their new vehicle's design, performance, safety, usability, and comfort after the first 90 days. It asks the question: after the "new car" feeling has worn off and real-life sets in, do you still love what you drive?


For Ford owners, the results are telling. The Ford brand improved 11 points overall to an 849 index score, jumping from 12th to 9th among mainstream brands and positioning us well above the mainstream average.


I’m proud of the consistency we are seeing in our passion products. Two of the nameplates that carried Ford to the APEAL podium last year did it again this year: the Ford Mustang was named the most appealing Sporty Car for the second year running, and the Ford Super Duty was named the most appealing Large Heavy-Duty Pickup for the third consecutive year.


Ford earned four top-three finishes out of 10 award eligible series, including the two segment awards.


When you put our IQS and APEAL results together, they tell a single, powerful story: This kind of consistency doesn’t happen by accident.


We also earned best-in-segment IQS awards for the second straight year for the F-150, Mustang, and Super Duty, with seven of our ten models placing in the top three of their respective segments — the highest percentage of any automaker.


Overall, we are continuing to provide a better experience for our customers by reducing quality issues with new vehicles and simultaneously improving the features our products provide.


As we move forward, quality and customer experience are no longer siloed at Ford. They are a shared, uncompromising discipline running through our design studios, our engineering teams, our manufacturing plants, and our suppliers.


We are building checks and balances that are built to last, and these latest results show that our focus on what you actually experience behind the wheel is paying off.


We still have work to do, but we are absolutely on the right track. Thank you for continuing on this journey with us.


*Josh Halliburton is executive director of quality at Ford Motor Company.*
