## Why Sleep Has Become an Urgent Workplace Issue


Before diving into the mechanics of how Benepass and Sleep Reset work together, it's worth understanding why sleep is no longer a personal problem — it's an employer problem too.


The data is sobering. The CDC reports that one in three American adults doesn't get sufficient sleep on a regular basis. Meanwhile, research continues to pile up connecting sleep deprivation to reduced cognitive function, poor emotional regulation, decreased productivity, and significantly higher rates of burnout, anxiety, and depression. Survey data from Mind Share Partners' 2021 Mental Health at Work Report shows that mental health concerns are on the rise, with 76% of respondents having at least one symptom of a mental health disorder in the previous year, up from 59% in 2019[Benepass](https://getbenepass.com/blog/benefits-101-a-complete-guide-to-developing-employee-wellness-programs) — and disordered sleep is at the root of many of those struggles.


For employers, the math is equally stark. Workers who consistently sleep poorly cost their companies through absenteeism, presenteeism (being at work but not fully functional), and higher healthcare utilization. Offering tools that address root-cause health issues — like sleep — is increasingly seen not as a perk but as a strategic investment in workforce performance and retention.


## What Is Sleep Reset?


[Sleep Reset](https://www.thesleepreset.com/) is a digital sleep clinic designed to treat insomnia and sleep anxiety without relying on pills, supplements, or melatonin. The program is built around Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I), a structured, evidence-based approach that helps people retrain their minds and bodies for healthy sleep by addressing root causes — adjusting sleep schedules, changing unhelpful thought patterns, and using techniques like stimulus control and relaxation strategies.[Sleep Reset](https://www.thesleepreset.com/)


Sleep Reset was developed in partnership with board-certified sleep medicine clinicians, therapists, and sleep coaches, alongside top sleep researchers from Stanford and Yale.[Sleep Reset](https://www.thesleepreset.com/) The American Academy of Sleep Medicine recognizes CBT-I as the gold standard for treating insomnia — outperforming sleeping pills in both short-term effectiveness and long-term sustainability.


Here's how the program works in practice: The program begins with an in-depth sleep assessment to identify root causes of sleep issues. Users are then provided with a structured weekly action plan, along with daily access to a trained sleep coach via text messages for personalized support and guidance. The app also features sleep logging, progress tracking, and a library of meditations and mindfulness exercises.[Mindtools](https://mindtools.io/programs/sleep-reset/)


The results speak for themselves. A peer-reviewed study published in *Frontiers of Sleep* evaluated Sleep Reset's clinical effectiveness across 564 diverse participants, finding that participants slept 85 minutes more every night on average, fell asleep in half the time, and spent 41% less time awake at night.[Sleep Reset](https://www.thesleepreset.com/) Data shows that 75% of Sleep Reset members improve their sleep, gaining on average one and a half more hours of sleep each night, spending 41% less time awake mid-sleep, and needing 53% less time to fall asleep.[Sleep Reset](https://www.thesleepreset.com/blog/sleep-reset-review-shannon-sedwick)


Sleep Reset's mission is to provide a lifelong, sustainable solution to better sleep after just 12 weeks, with no pills or melatonin needed.[Sleep Review](https://sleepreviewmag.com/sleep-diagnostics/screeners/smart-phone/sleep-reset-app-launched-as-a-digital-sleep-lab-for-smartphones/) For employees who have tried everything from white noise machines to melatonin gummies and still can't sleep consistently, it represents something genuinely different: a clinically supervised approach that targets the actual cause of the problem.
