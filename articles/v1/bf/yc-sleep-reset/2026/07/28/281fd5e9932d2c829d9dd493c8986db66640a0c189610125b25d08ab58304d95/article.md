# Sleep Reset Acquires Chorus Sleep, Adding a Personalized Sleep Health Platform to Its Virtual Sleep Clinic

**San Francisco, April 22, 2026 -** Sleep Reset, the leading virtual sleep clinic treating insomnia and sleep apnea through clinician-guided, CBT-I-based care, today announced the acquisition of Chorus Sleep, a Y Combinator-backed sleep health platform. Chorus Sleep offers a wide-ranging suite of sleep support including AI-powered sleep coaching, CBT-I-based educational content, proprietary breathwork and relaxation sessions, sleep tracking, and snoring and sleep apnea support. With the deal, Chorus Sleep's platform and technology, including Luna, its AI coaching agent, join Sleep Reset's clinician-led model, strengthening its ability to deliver personalized, scalable care to the millions of people struggling with chronic sleep issues.


## **The Platform**


Chorus Sleep was built around the belief that effective sleep care requires meeting each person where they are, with the right intervention at the right time. The platform spans AI-powered sleep coaching, CBT-I-based educational content, proprietary guided breathwork and relaxation sessions, sleep tracking, and snoring and sleep apnea support, all designed to work together as a personalized, adaptive sleep care experience.


At the heart of the platform is Luna, Chorus Sleep's AI coaching agent. Luna is a user-facing coach that combines real-time interaction with longitudinal memory to guide users through structured, adaptive sleep improvement journeys, automatically tracking sleep data, identifying health patterns, logging journal entries, setting reminders, and delivering personalized guidance, all without requiring manual input from the user.


Luna becomes more effective with every interaction, building a proprietary data flywheel that grows over time. The result is a care experience that feels personal and responsive at every step, with human expertise applied precisely where it's needed most.


## ‍ **Outcomes**


Chorus Sleep built its platform with a focus on enterprise and employer health deployments, reaching users across 26 countries. In those deployments, the platform achieved approximately 35% program adoption, 80% retention at month one, and over 50% retention at month six, metrics that reflect meaningful, sustained engagement across a global user base.


Across the platform, Chorus Sleep users demonstrated 30% faster sleep onset, 30% fewer nighttime awakenings, and a 20% improvement in daytime functioning, outcomes consistent with the kind of meaningful sleep improvement Sleep Reset aims to deliver at scale.
