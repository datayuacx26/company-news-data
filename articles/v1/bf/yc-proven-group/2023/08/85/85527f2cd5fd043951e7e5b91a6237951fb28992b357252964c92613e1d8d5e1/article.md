## What is Glycerin?


Even if you’re unfamiliar with glycerin, you’ve likely already met this sugar alcohol in your kitchen. Glycerin naturally occurs in a range of plant and animal foods, appearing as a colorless and odorless liquid. In fact, you could find glycerin in any of the following foods and beverages:


- Soy oil
- Coconut oil
- Palm oil
- Beer
- Honey
- Vinegar
- Wine and wine vinegar


When creating glycerin for skin products, manufacturers often form the molecule through the hydrolysis of fats and oils. As of now, glycerin is rated safe for consumption and topical use by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). When you hear oils, you may think that they’re pore-clogging ingredients. But are ingredients like glycerin or[jojoba oil comedogenic](https://www.provenskincare.com/blog/is-jojoba-oil-comedogenic/) ? The answer is no, and they have a plethora of skin benefits.


## How Does Glycerin Work on Skin?


So, what does glycerin do for skin exactly? The bulk of glycerin’s powers comes from its status as a humectant.


**Humectants** are molecules that attract moisture from the surrounding environment. This means that, when applied topically, glycerin acts as a captivating magnet for moisture, drawing water molecules from the air into your epidermis, or skin layers. This hydration keeps your skin cells healthy so that they can more effectively transport essential nutrients and maintain your[skin barrier](https://www.provenskincare.com/blog/what-is-skin-barrier/) .


Even better? Glycerin has a molecular weight of about 92 grams/mole, allowing it to penetrate the deeper layers of your epidermis. This means glycerin can act as a master of balance, bringing moisture to the epidermal layers that need it most. As a result, you get hydrated and radiant skin from the inside out.


## Top Benefits of Glycerin for Skin


It’s hard to oversell the benefits of glycerin for skin. This hydrating ingredient is a multi-factor glow booster, with abilities to moisturize, strengthen, and brighten the skin. Let’s explore the top 6 benefits of using glycerin in your skincare routine:


1. **Moisturization** – Pure glycerin is a gentle yet effective hydration hero, saving the day with its humectant properties. When applied topically, natural glycerin can significantly boost skin hydration for up to 24 hours. In fact, most moisturizers already contain some form of glycerin in their formula for this reason. According to cosmetic scientist Rachael Polowyj, "Cosmetic chemists will use it in most formulations because of how effective it is in terms of moisturizing."
2. **Skin barrier protection** – Your skin’s natural barrier is a vital “shield” against harmful bacteria and harsh elements, formed by a well-moisturized microbiome on the skin’s surface. By moisturizing your skin, glycerin bolsters your skin's natural barrier. So, when you face dry winter air or aggressive products, you can count on glycerin to protect your skin from any signs of damage, like cracking or skin irritation.
3. **Irritation relief** – Dealing with red and itchy patches on your face? Glycerin could be the key to calming your skin. In fact, one study found that a topical 10% glycerin solution effectively relieved acute skin irritation. Whether you're dealing with a skin condition like a sunburn,[eczema](https://www.provenskincare.com/skin-concern/skin-eczema/) , or simply a touch of sensitivity, glycerin could be your knight in *soothing* armor.
4. **Protection against harsh products** – If you’ve recently invited actives (or targeted skincare ingredients) into your skincare routine, you might be facing some side effects during the adjustment period. Fortunately, you may be able to use glycerin to prevent or reduce these effects, which can include dryness, redness, or peeling.
5. **Anti-aging** – Moisture is a key player in the prevention of premature wrinkles and fine lines. Why is that? Moisturized skin better retains its elasticity and volume, resisting the appearance of wrinkles for longer. Since glycerin is known for its moisturizing properties, it could contribute to anti-aging prevention. One study even found that a 20% glycerin solution effectively reduced the appearance of crow’s feet.
6. **Infection prevention** – In large doses, glycerin may even stop infection in its tracks. One study found that glycerin creates a bacteriostatic environment—i.e., it prevents the growth of bacteria. By applying a formula with a high concentration of glycerin, you could help prevent any open wounds on your[sensitive skin](https://www.provenskincare.com/skin-type/what-is-sensitive-skin/) from becoming infected.

## What Skin Types Should Use Glycerin?


If you have sensitive or particular skin, you may wonder if it’s safe for you to incorporate a glycerin product into your skincare routine. The answer is likely yes.


Glycerin is highly tolerable for almost all skin types. In fact, there are zero known side effects of glycerin in skincare. However, that doesn’t mean glycerin can’t suit certain skin types more than others. The moisturizing and soothing properties of glycerin make it highly suitable for dry and sensitive skin types. On the other hand, it doesn’t hurt to try this hydrating ingredient, regardless of skin type, given its incredible benefits.


## How to Bring Glycerin into Your Skincare Routine


Ready to harness the glow-boosting powers of glycerin? Fortunately, there’s more than one way to incorporate this special ingredient into your skincare routine.
