## Alcohol and Dehydration


The first effect you’ll likely notice after consuming two or more alcoholic beverages is[dehydrated skin symptoms](https://www.provenskincare.com/blog/dehydrated-skin-symptoms/) . When asking yourself, *does alcohol cause wrinkles* , the answer lies in the way alcohol intake affects your cell membranes.


Dehydration can cause dry mucous membranes, and alcohol consumption can damage the mucous membranes in your body, including those in your:


- Liver
- Pancreas
- Skin


Prolonged dehydration can present potential consequences on your skin’s health and overall appearance. One clear indication of dehydration is a decrease in skin *turgor* , or elasticity in the skin, which over time can lead to wrinkles, fine lines, and other signs of aging \[2\].


While applying moisturizing products can help treat your skin’s dryness, cutting back on how often you drink alcoholic beverages and increasing your daily water intake can help hydrate your skin at the source.


## Alcohol and Inflammation


After a few cocktails, it’s normal for some people to experience[flushing in the face](https://www.provenskincare.com/skin-concern/flushed-face/) , especially the cheeks. That’s actually because of *inflammation,* a process that occurs when the body throws up a defense against a perceived irritant.


Inflammation is actually part of the body’s immune defenses and when it senses an intruder, like a foreign body, toxin, or pathogen, it releases chemicals that prompt the body’s cells to have an inflammatory response \[3\]. Since alcohol content is a toxin, it can trigger inflammation throughout the body. As a result, symptoms can occur like:


- Dehydration
- Puffiness
- Redness
- Discoloration


Over time, inflammation can have a damaging effect on your skin cells.


Alcohol can cause health problems, such as systemic inflammation, which is particularly noticeable in the appearance of your skin \[4\]. Chronic, or consistent, inflammation as a result of alcohol consumption can cause the inflammatory cells to remain active, causing them to damage healthy tissues like your skin, which can reduce its elasticity and lead to the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles \[5\].

## Tips for Keeping Your Skin Hydrated and Vibrant


Whether you’ve linked your skin aging signs to alcohol or something else, there are a few lifestyle habits you can work into your routine to preserve your healthiest, most hydrated skin.


First, if you do notice more severe or frequent inflammation in your skin after consuming alcoholic beverages, you may want to consider cutting back on your alcohol intake. Consult with your healthcare provider about your specific body’s limits when it comes to alcohol consumption.


Beyond this, work these easy tips into your day-to-day to help your skin repair and breathe:


- **Drink plenty of water** – Especially after a few drinks, but in general, drink plenty of water throughout the day. Women should consume about 11.5 cups of water per day, and men should drink about 15.5 cups \[8\]. In addition, if and when you do partake in happy hour, try cycling a glass of water in between each alcoholic drink.
- **Use a nightly moisturizer** – Nighttime moisturizers are formulated to sink deep into your skin while you sleep, helping your skin restore and repair dead skin cells and hydrate the innermost skin layers.
