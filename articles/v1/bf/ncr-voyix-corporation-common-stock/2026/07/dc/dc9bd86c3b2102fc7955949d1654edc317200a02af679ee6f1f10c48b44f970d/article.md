# NCR Voyix Unveils AI-Accelerated Suite of Applications on the Voyix Commerce Platform

#### *Showcasing a comprehensive portfolio of retail and restaurant solutions—built on a unified, cloud-to-edge architecture*


**ATLANTA — Jan. 7, 2026 —** NCR Voyix Corp. (NYSE: VYX), a platform-powered leader in unified commerce for shopping and dining, today announced the unveiling of its next-generation portfolio of microservices-based applications. Built on the Voyix Commerce Platform, it represents the culmination of NCR Voyix’s software modernization strategy—transforming how retail and restaurant businesses deliver seamless experiences across every channel and format. This marks a major milestone in the Company’s transformation and creates a step change in its ability to deliver innovation at scale to new and existing customers.


Over the past year, NCR Voyix has seen rapid adoption of its first suite of cloud-native, microservices applications, with fully modernized software in live operation across multiple geographies. By combining decades of deep retail and restaurant expertise, its extensive library of existing software applications and the accelerative power of AI tools, NCR Voyix has fundamentally changed the speed and efficiency of its software lifecycle—from development through testing, compliance and deployment.


“Our next-generation portfolio is more than a technology upgrade—it’s a catalyst for acceleration,” said Nick East, Chief Product Officer at NCR Voyix. “After several years of bold, intentional modernization and the supercharging effect of AI on our experienced technology teams, we’ve reached an inflection point. Our combined cloud-to-edge application suite, payments capabilities and robust services offering enable retailers and restaurants to accelerate new possibilities for their operations, experiences and business outcomes.”


NCR Voyix will be showcasing the following set of applications at Booth 4621 on Level 3 at[NRF ’26: Retail’s Big Show](https://nrfbigshow.nrf.com/) on January 11-13, 2026, in New York City:


- **VoyixPOS:** A powerful point-of-sale (POS) solution that combines the cumulative business logic and years of features of the global leader in POS. Designed to meet the dynamic demands of grocery, convenience, fuel, department and specialty retailers across the globe, Voyix POS enables centralized management, real-time insights and rapid innovation across all locations. Its cloud-to-edge design supports mission critical resilience and security, speed and flexibility.
- **Voyix Self-Checkout:** A self-checkout (SCO) solution that empowers guests to checkout independently and enables retailers to minimize friction and control labor costs. Retailers can deploy and manage SCO lanes quickly, reduce queues, and integrate with POS, loyalty and analytics for consistent experiences. Additional SCO features include Picklist Assist, which uses computer vision and machine learning to autosuggest and fill PLU codes, streamline item lookup and improve accuracy.
- **Voyix Back Office:** A unified retail management application that organizes inventory, purchasing, loyalty and analytics. It delivers near real-time visibility and automation to reduce complexity and improve efficiency across multiple locations.
- **Voyix Supply Chain:** An end-to-end application that integrates planning, analytics and execution—including comprehensive warehouse management—to deliver real-time visibility and control of receiving, picking, packing and shipping. Voyix Supply Chain improves efficiency, reduces risk and enables data-driven decisions across manufacturing, distribution and service operations. It supports accuracy, compliance and scalability across multiple warehouses.
- **Voyix Kitchen:** A kitchen display system that accelerates order fulfillment and improves communication between front- and back-of-house. Its offline capabilities help ensure uninterrupted operations.
- **Voyix Insight with conversational AI:** An AI-powered analytics application with conversational search that provides real-time insights, recommended actions, alerts and automated targeting for forecasting, labor and shrink.
- **Voyix Loyalty:** An AI-driven loyalty application for omnichannel personalization, rapid offer deployment and predictive insights that launches promotions in minutes, predicts churn and next‑best actions and keeps earn‑and‑redeem active even offline.


For more information about NCR Voyix’s complete product suite for restaurants and retailers, visit[NCR Voyix Official Website](https://www.ncrvoyix.com/) .


**About NCR Voyix** NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global platform-powered leader in unified commerce for shopping and dining. Combining a flexible, intelligent platform with end-to-end payments capabilities and services developed through its deep industry experience, NCR Voyix empowers retailers and restaurants to accelerate new possibilities for their operations, experiences and business outcomes. NCR Voyix is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and serves customers in more than 35 countries worldwide.
