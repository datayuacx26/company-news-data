# NCR Voyix Maintains Global Leadership in POS Software, Reclaims Top Spot in New Restaurant Deployments

#### Strategy of platform-focused unified commerce drives NCR Voyix’s sustained leadership across retail and restaurant sectors


ATLANTA – December 15, 2025 – NCR Voyix Corp. (NYSE: VYX), a leading global provider of digital commerce solutions, today announced it has again been recognized as the world’s largest supplier of point of sale (POS) software, according to Global POS Software 2025 from Datos Insights. The study, which analyzes POS software installations across retail and hospitality enterprises with 1,000 or more lanes, confirms NCR Voyix’s continued dominance in total installations and renewed leadership in new restaurant deployments.


NCR Voyix retained the No. 1 position in total POS software installations globally in the retail sector. The company also reclaimed the top spot for new restaurant deployments. In the Grocery+ sector — which includes grocery, convenience and drug stores — NCR Voyix continues to lead globally, reinforcing its strategic focus on unified commerce and enterprise grade software innovation.


The report also highlights NCR Voyix’s regional strength, as a leading provider in key regions and a growing footprint among convenience retailers in North America.


“Our continued leadership in global POS software installations reflects the strength of our platform led strategy and our commitment to delivering scalable, resilient solutions for enterprise customers,” said James G. Kelly, President and CEO of NCR Voyix. “As retailers and restaurants accelerate their digital transformation, NCR Voyix is uniquely positioned to support their growth with unified commerce capabilities that span the globe.”


According to Datos Insights, nearly 2.3 million new POS software installations are expected globally through 2030, with significant growth projected in the Grocery+ space and across APAC, Central and Eastern Europe, LATAM and MEA. NCR Voyix is well positioned to support this growth through continued innovation and deep industry expertise.
