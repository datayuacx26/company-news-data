# NCR Voyix to Present at the ICR Conference

*Benny Tadele to share how cloud-native innovation and AI-driven tools are transforming the dining experience in fireside chat*


**ATLANTA —** NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX), a global leader in unified commerce, will present its restaurant technology strategy at the ICR Conference in Orlando, FL on Tuesday, January 13, 2026, at 1:30 p.m. Eastern Time. Benny Tadele, Executive Vice President and President of Restaurants, will share how NCR Voyix is helping operators tackle industry challenges, from labor shortages to rising guest expectations, by delivering smarter, faster and more connected solutions. A live webcast and subsequent replay of the presentation will be available on the NCR Voyix investor relations website at[NCR Voyix Investor Website](https://investor.ncrvoyix.com/) .


NCR Voyix will be introducing its next-generation suite of microservices-based applications at NRF ’26: Retail’s Big Show, marking a major milestone in its modernization strategy. Built on the Voyix Commerce Platform, these cloud-native solutions combine decades of expertise with AI-driven acceleration to help businesses innovate faster and operate smarter.


At the ICR Conference, the focus shifts to restaurants—showcasing how this technology empowers operators to streamline workflows, personalize guest engagement and adapt quickly to dining trends with minimal disruptions. Restaurants today face a complex balancing act: maintaining operational efficiency while creating memorable guest experiences across dine-in, takeout and delivery channels. NCR Voyix is addressing these demands with a portfolio built for agility.


Its latest suite of applications—built on the Voyix Commerce Platform—brings together microservices architecture and AI acceleration to transform restaurant operations. It includes:


- **Aloha Next by Voyix**
A powerful point-of-sale (POS) solution that combines the cumulative business logic and years of features of the global leader in POS. Designed to meet the dynamic demands of restaurants across the globe, Aloha Next by Voyix enables centralized management, real-time insights and rapid innovation across all locations. Its cloud-to-edge design supports mission critical resilience and security, speed and flexibility.
- **Voyix Edge**
A leading edge-native application engine purpose-built for retail and restaurants. It transforms in-store infrastructure into an Agile Runtime Environment, delivering speed and agility with local resilience. This means faster rollouts, improved uptime and the flexibility to adopt advanced technology in-store, like AI and robotics, at scale.
- **Aloha Menu by Voyix**
A centralized menu management application that empowers restaurant operators to manage every menu—from POS to mobile, kiosk, web, and third-party delivery—from a unified, intuitive interface. With a “create once, deploy everywhere” model, it simplifies operations, ensures consistency and enables dynamic pricing and personalized promotions across all channels.
- **Aloha Smart Manager by Voyix**
An all-in-one back-office solution that replaces outdated tools with a single, intuitive application for labor, inventory, scheduling and reporting. With real-time insights and mobile access, managers save time, make smarter decisions and focus on guests.
- **Aloha Consumer Marketing by Voyix**
A unified application across consumer data, marketing and loyalty that empowers restaurants to accelerate the way they identify, understand and motivate their customers. It supports revenue growth by capturing and connecting disparate data points on individual guest experiences and offers that drive incremental changes in guest behavior.
- **Voyix Kitchen**
A kitchen display that accelerates order fulfillment and improves communication between front- and back-of-house. Its offline capabilities help ensure uninterrupted operations.


For more information about NCR Voyix’s complete product suite for restaurants and retailers, visit[NCR Voyix Official Website](https://www.ncrvoyix.com/) .


**About NCR Voyix**


About NCR Voyix NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global platform-powered leader in unified commerce for shopping and dining. Combining a flexible, intelligent platform with end-to-end payments capabilities and services developed through its deep industry experience, NCR Voyix empowers retailers and restaurants to accelerate new possibilities for their operations, experiences and business outcomes. NCR Voyix is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and serves customers in more than 35 countries worldwide.
