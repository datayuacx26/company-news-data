# Restaurant & Retail Tech: Why Cloud‑to‑Edge Architecture Is Becoming the New Standard

As retailers and restaurants accelerate into a more digital, unified commerce future, one thing has become clear: Outdated technology can no longer keep up. Rising labor costs, increasingly complex technology stacks, and customer expectations for seamless digital‑to‑store experiences have pushed retailers and restaurants toward more modern, resilient platforms.


Enter cloud‑to‑edge architecture - the technology framework accelerating new possibilities in restaurants and retail. NCR Voyix is at the forefront of this transformation, with a platform designed to deliver speed, resilience, and scalability across thousands of lanes, formats, and geographies.


In this article, we break down what cloud-to-edge architecture is, why it matters to restaurants and retailers, and how NCR Voyix is helping the restaurant and retail leading innovators modernize with confidence.


#### What Is Cloud‑to‑Edge Architecture?


Cloud‑to‑edge architecture blends two critical components:


- The cloud, which powers centralized management, analytics, unified commerce, and rapid deployment.
- The edge, which ensures in‑store continuity and performance even in the event of network disruptions.


The Voyix Commerce Platform uses this hybrid model to deliver continuous operations, lightning‑fast updates, and real‑time data visibility across the entire enterprise.


This architecture is built on API-first microservices, containerization, and automated orchestration, giving operators a modular, foundation for future innovation.


#### Why Cloud‑to‑Edge Matters More Than Ever for Restaurants & Retailers


1. Uptime Capability Even During Network Outages


Internet outages can halt checkout, frustrate customers, and cost restaurants and retailers significant revenue. NCR Voyix solutions are designed to offer offline mode, so restaurants and retailers can operate entire stores for extended periods during outages with edge‑native processing that keeps all lanes and devices running normally.


Transactions sync automatically when connectivity returns, eliminating manual reconciliation.


**Why it matters:** In high-volume environments, resiliency isn’t a “nice-to-have” it’s essential.


2. Dramatically Faster Innovation Through Microservices


NCR Voyix has transformed decades of industry business logic into flexible, cloud‑to‑edge microservices. This means restaurants and retailers can adopt new workflows and features efficiently , not through years-long upgrade cycles.


3. Reduced Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)


Because the Voyix Commerce Platform is built on a modern microservices architecture that spans cloud and edge environments it lowers infrastructure cost, extends hardware life and enables predictable scaling across any number of sites without compromising performance or availability.


4. Enterprise‑Scale Performance Backed by Real‑World Results


NCR Voyix supports enormous transaction loads and some of the largest footprints in the world.


Real stats as of January 2026 include:


- 8+ billion API requests per month
- 105 million mobile orders processed per month
- $1.4T in annual US transactions processed


5. Unified Commerce Capabilities Built for Today’s Consumer


Cloud-to-edge architecture enables real‑time visibility, unified carts, and omnichannel workflows. Voyix supports:


- BOPIS, BORIS, and ship‑from‑store
- Universal cart mobility across channels
- Mobile POS for line busting and curbside fulfillment
- Integration with digital commerce solutions


As customers demand cross-channel convenience, the Voyix Commerce Platform gives restaurants and retailers the tools to keep up.


#### Advanced Capabilities Operators Gain with Cloud‑to‑Edge


- Real‑time monitoring of store systems – IT teams get full visibility into store systems, device health, traffic load, and performance. Predictive alerts notify operators of potential outages before they occur.
- Zero-downtime updates – Feature flags, remote configuration, and containerized deployment ensure low-risk , fast rollouts across thousands of stores.
- Multi-vertical flexibility – Support for deli, bakery, QSR, fuel, grocery, department and specialty retail, and hybrid store formats all managed from one platform.
- Faster store buildouts – Voyix POS has enabled new stores to be brought online in hours instead of days, significantly reducing IT burden.
