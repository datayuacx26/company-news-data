# Multi-channel Outreach Campaigns for Home Remodelers

**Today, we are happy to announce our multi-day, multi-channel outreach campaign feature.**


Leaping supports fully configurable outbound sequences. For example: call new leads twice upon arrival and send a text on Day 0, call twice daily with texts every other day through Day 7, then taper to once-daily calls and texts every third day through Day 30.


Each text message in the sequence can be uniquely crafted, allowing for personalized and varied outreach throughout the campaign.


Leads can be enrolled into a sequence either one at a time or in bulk via the Leaping API, making it easy to onboard new contacts at any scale.


Outreach can be restricted to business hours — for example, 8am–8pm Monday through Saturday — ensuring contacts are only reached at appropriate times.


The calling and messaging cadence automatically stops when the lead picks up, calls back, or texts back, preventing unnecessary follow-ups once contact is made.


Multiple campaigns can run simultaneously, allowing different sequences to be active across various lead groups or client accounts at the same time.
