# Introducing the Clarivate Trust Center: A new resource for transparency and confidence

Organizations today face growing regulatory requirements, increasingly sophisticated cyber threats and heightened expectations from stakeholders. As a result, cybersecurity has become a shared responsibility across every partnership.


At Clarivate, we place great value in the trust that our customers have in us. We are relied on to provide mission-critical intelligence, trusted content, and innovative technology solutions that support research, innovation, healthcare and intellectual property decision-making worldwide.


In today’s environment, protecting that trust means continually strengthening how we safeguard information, manage risk and support our customers’ security requirements.


Today, we are pleased to announce the launch of our new Trust Center, an online resource designed to:


- Provide practical resources that helps customers complete due diligence faster and with greater confidence
- Provide customers and partners with greater visibility into our cybersecurity and compliance practices
- Help customers find clear, reliable information about our security programs, compliance standards and commitment to protecting customer data.


### Building confidence through transparency


As the cybersecurity landscape continues to evolve, organizations need to understand how their vendors protect data and maintain secure operations. Customers increasingly expect clear, accessible information about security programs, governance frameworks and compliance controls before making purchasing decisions and throughout the customer relationship.


The Clarivate Trust Center has been created to meet those expectations by bringing together key information about our cybersecurity and compliance practices in a single, easy-to-access location.


### What customers can expect


The Trust Center offers an overview of our approach to cybersecurity, compliance and risk management, including:


- Information about our security program and compliance posture
- Certifications, security statements, data protection and governance resources
- Approved materials that support customer due diligence and vendor risk assessments.


The Trust Center also provides a secure mechanism for eligible customers and prospects to request additional security and compliance documentation, helping reduce the time and effort associated with security reviews and due diligence activities.


### Supporting our customers’ security journeys


The launch of the Trust Center reflects our commitment to helping customers navigate complex cybersecurity and compliance requirements. By providing greater transparency into our security practices and investments, we aim to help customers evaluate risk more efficiently and make informed decisions with confidence.


Our cybersecurity program is designed around industry-recognized security principles and supported by dedicated teams that monitor, assess and strengthen our security posture on an ongoing basis. The Trust Center gives customers a clearer view of those efforts and our commitment to continuous improvement.


### Continuing to earn customer trust


As technology continues to evolve and cybersecurity remains a critical priority, we will continue to invest in the people, processes and technologies that help protect our products, services and information assets.


We invite customers and partners to[visit the Trust Center](https://clarivate.com/trust-center/) to explore approved security and compliance information and learn more about our approach to protecting customer data.
