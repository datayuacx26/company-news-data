# ProMat 2025 – It’s a Wrap!

The Honeywell Warehouse


Honeywell was front and center at ProMat 2025, showcasing its solutions to augment, automate, and activate the entire warehouse. The technology on display was from three different divisions – Productivity Solutions and Services, Intelligrated, and Building Automation. The booth demonstrated how Honeywell is among the top solutions providers offering a comprehensive suite of products to cover your needs, from productivity hardware and software to material handling systems to building management and access control systems. Through the integration of various software solutions, Honeywell provides end-to-end operational visibility to all facets of warehouse operations.
