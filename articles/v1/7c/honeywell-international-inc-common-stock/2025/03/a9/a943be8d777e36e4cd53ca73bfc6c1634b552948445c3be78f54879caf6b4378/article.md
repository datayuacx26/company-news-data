# LogiMAT 2025 – It’s a Wrap!

Matthew Sterner


LogiMAT 2025 wrapped up on March 13th at the Messe Stuttgart Convention Center in Stuttgart, Germany. The show hosted over 65,000 attendees from around the globe, where over 40% traveled more than 180 miles to attend. The event featured 1,650 exhibitors from 40 countries showcasing technology and solutions spread across 734,000 net square feet of expo space. With another year of strong attendance and a diverse list of exhibitors, LogiMAT further cemented its reputation as a must-attend show to see the latest technologies and gain insights on the trends shaping the industry.
