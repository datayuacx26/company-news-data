# Meet TaskOS

TaskOS is our biggest update yet. We rebuilt how work flows through Dex so you can plan a task, run it, and track everything in motion without jumping between scattered tabs and tools.


Let me show you around:


Release note feature image


### Plan Mode


Plan Mode is your dedicated workspace for turning scattered tabs into structured progress.


Create Plans by telling Dex what you're working on, or build them manually. Add freeform notes and use` @` to pull in tabs as context. Launch a Plan to reopen all related tabs and pick up right where you left off.


When you're ready to move, Dex reads your notes and attached context and turns it into clear, actionable next steps.


Release note feature image


### Chat Mode


Chat works the way you're used to: natural language, full access to your tabs and tools. Now it does more.


Run multiple workflows in parallel, monitor live activity and pending actions in one place, and launch saved workflows instantly from the shortcuts bar.


Release note feature image


### Control Panel


The vertical tabs and smart grouping you love now live in a lightweight overlay that sits on top of any page.


Keep all your tabs in a clean list, organized by Dex in one click. Find a tab, reorganize, clean things up, and close the panel to jump right back into what you were doing.


Release note feature image


### Automations


Set up recurring tasks that Dex runs on its own. No builders or flowcharts, just plain language.


Set a schedule (every Monday) or trigger conditions (like an email that goes unanswered for two days), tell Dex what to do, and it runs.


Coming soon to the Control Panel.


### Rollout


TaskOS will come to your Dex automatically this week. If your extension isn't updating, head tochrome://extensions and click the update button in the top-left corner.
