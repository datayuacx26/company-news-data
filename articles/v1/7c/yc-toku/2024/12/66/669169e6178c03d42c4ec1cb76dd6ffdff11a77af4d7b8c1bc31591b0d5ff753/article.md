# How ZKsync Distributed Tokens to 400+ Contributors Compliantly

ZKsync partnered with Toku to execute a complex Token Generation Event (TGE) spanning 25+ jurisdictions, hundreds of grants and multi-stakeholder coordination - delivering compliance, tax efficiency and flawless execution under tight deadlines.


## At a Glance


**Customer** ZKsync **Industry** Blockchain Protocol / Token Launch **Scope** 25+ employee & investor jurisdictions; hundreds of grant agreements. **Solution** Toku Token Grant Administration + Multi-jurisdiction Compliance Execution **Impact** Completion of TGE process under deadline, legal & tax-cost savings, full jurisdiction coverage.


## The Challenge


### Launching a Token Generation Event Across Jurisdictions with Zero Margin for Error


ZKsync needed to execute a token generation event that involved hundreds of grant agreements and complex tax and regulatory requirements across more than 25 jurisdictions. The timeline was narrow - it needed to coordinate investor and employee grants, integrate multi-sig wallets, align with existing security systems, and satisfy internal legal teams and external counsel.


Challenge points included:


- Designing tax-efficient structures tailored to each jurisdiction while working with internal legal and tax teams.


- Processing hundreds of grant agreements under tight timelines and evolving requirements.


- Integrating with wallets, security protocols and investor management systems.


- Managing multi-stakeholder coordination (employees, investors, legal, tax, external counsel) across multiple time zones.
Without the right partner, this type of token event carries significant regulatory, legal and timeline risk.


## The Approach


### Toku’s Three-Phase Strategy for Flawless Token Launch Execution


To meet ZKsync’s requirements, Toku implemented a structured three-phase approach covering preparation, execution and post-launch support.


#### **1. Preparation Phase**


Toku created jurisdiction-specific document templates, custom safe-sizing models and a multi-sig wallet strategy using Gnosis Safe to govern both investor and employee wallets. This groundwork ensured the event could proceed without manual bottlenecks or legal uncertainty.


#### **2. Execution Phase**


During the short execution window, Toku processed hundreds of grant agreements simultaneously, coordinated execution across jurisdictions, and managed critical tax elections in major markets including the US, UK and the EU. The team worked across time zones, integrating with internal systems and external legal counsel to maintain flawless execution.


#### **3. Support Phase**


After launch, Toku continued to support ZKsync with contributor education (town halls), 24/7 platform support, staking, voting and governance assistance, as well as integration with Anchorage and ZKsync’s custodian infrastructure. This ensured the launch was not just completed, but adopted successfully by all stakeholders.
