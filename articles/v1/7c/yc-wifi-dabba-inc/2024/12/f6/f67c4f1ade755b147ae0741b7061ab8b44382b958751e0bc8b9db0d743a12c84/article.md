# DePIN to Become the Fastest Growing Sector in Web3 in 2025?

Alvaro Garcia, a partner at the investment firm minced no words talking about their conviction in dipping themselves heavily into DePINs. *“DePIN will become the global standard for deploying physical infrastructure, coordinating human resources, and generating billions in passive income while at the same time providing easy access and lower cost for the users.”*

DePIN projects have already reached a valuation exceeding $33 billion and continue to grow rapidly. This innovative approach enables more transparent, efficient, and equitable deployment of essential services, revolutionizing how infrastructure is managed and consumed.


As cryptocurrency becomes increasingly accessible, the number of real-world use cases for blockchain technology is expanding. DePINs stand at the forefront of this evolution, offering unique models to decentralize critical infrastructure and fostering a tokenized economy. By addressing fundamental infrastructure challenges, DePINs are paving the way for a broader adoption of Web3 protocols.

Institutional investors are increasingly recognizing the disruptive potential of DePINs. Notably, these projects are seen as utility-driven with significant long-term prospects, making them attractive to seasoned investors. Furthermore, DePINs hold the potential to mainstream cryptocurrency by simplifying interactions with Web3 protocols.


Pranav Kanade, a portfolio manager for VanEck’s digital assets alpha fund, articulated this vision in an[interview](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2024/04/16/depin-is-venture-capitalists-latest-crypto-obsession-can-it-match-the-hype) with CoinDesk. These users would be using public blockchains without necessarily realizing they are interacting with a crypto product.”
