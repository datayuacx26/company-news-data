# DePIN Market Size to Hit $3.5 Trillion by 2028?

Beyond the buzz, as DePINs continue to evolve, the[overall DePin market size](https://www.coingecko.com/en/categories/depin#:~:text=The%20DePIN%20market%20cap%20today,24h%20Trading%20Volume) has already surged by over 400% year-on-year to $31.4 billion as of December 2024. With an incoming pro-crypto government in the U.S., multiple reports project this growth trend to persist.


According to Hong Kong-based University of Web3 (UWEB), the cumulative market size of DePINs is forecasted to hit $3.5 trillion by 2028. UWEB’s findings only affirm what Messari, a popular crypto market research and analytics firm, posted in their annual “State of DePIN”[report](https://messari.io/report/state-of-depin-2023) published in 2023.


Messari analysts also predict DePINs to add $10 trillion to the global economy in the next 10 years.


## **DePin Resilience Indicates Investor Confidence**


As the broader crypto market continues to grow – the latest bull run being triggered by the US election results – the growth of DePINs has been relatively consistent, indicating investor confidence in their long-term prospects.


Interestingly, Messari’s “State of DePIN” report, also notes that even during the extended bear period that lasted until Q3 2024, DePIN market size demonstrated a remarkable resilience.


> “While the broader crypto market faced a steep 70-90% decline, DePIN revenues experienced a much milder drop of only 20-60% from their peak values,” it reads.


## **VC Dominance**


The combined valuation of DePIN tokens as of December 2024 is in excess of $31 billion. Notably, the majority of the projects have seen an influx of funds from reputed venture capitals and institutional investors. A[report](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/depin-venture-capitalists-latest-crypto-162009723.html?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAFz-tJgKhLzz3EswWVxKkVoLilcD8QngVcITPYlxDmOmRWzd7nL7wJysj_Qk5ycYptkz8KUAuPlSTm_GKLKxa2SJkSH6eLzOpFdBiQX_HjoC5n9edk-jJwMHzM0KgO0rdOJg-b4hSG7_svO8ZmGojqZ7SiabIbSGrNod7_2A40g6) published by Coindesk, earlier this year cited[Crypto.com](http://crypto.com/) ’s finding that DePin projects have already raised more than $1 billion in funding.


The increasing and dominant institutional investment leaves a lot of ground for retail investors. While the majority of retail investors are focussed on short-term gains from the increasing prices of top cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, DePINs are becoming increasingly popular amongst investors looking for more stable and long-term investment opportunities.


## **Why It Matters**


DePINs focus on creating physical infrastructure—like telecom networks, IoT devices, or renewable energy grids—supported by decentralized crypto models. Unlike speculative crypto projects, DePINs offer tangible value by solving real-world problems, such as expanding internet access or enabling decentralized transportation systems.


As such, the increasing market size of DePINs is a strong indicator of blockchain technology finding real-world use cases. Moving forward, it is likely that we see DePINs leading the movement of mainstreaming blockchain.
