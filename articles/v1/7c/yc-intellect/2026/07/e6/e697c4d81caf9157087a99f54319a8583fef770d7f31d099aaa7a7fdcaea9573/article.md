# How JobAdder Boosted EAP Engagement to 44% with Intellect’s Platform

## The Problem


Operating across complex time zones and multiple geographies, JobAdder’s workforce experiences severe, compounding macro pressures to perform and continuously innovate. Their previous EAP framework followed a passive, legacy model with severe systemic challenges—including heavy administrative loads, upfront out-of-pocket costs, and accidental privacy compromises. This operational friction restricted group-wide utilization to a stagnant 7% baseline, leaving employees to navigate workplace anxiety in isolation.


## The Solution


JobAdder partnered with Intellect to engineer a zero-barrier ecosystem that moves mental healthcare out of corporate administrative channels and directly into the hands of their distributed workforce.


– Radical Privacy: Complete end-to-end user anonymity insulated from internal corporate eyes, entirely removing the “privacy tax” of legacy reimbursement pipelines
– Proactive Stepped Care: Moving away from a purely reactive, break-glass crisis model to embed daily digital sentiment tracking and one-on-one behavioral coaching
– Leadership Enablement: Training organizational managers to hold supportive conversations and confidently frame the platform as a foundational “Culture Gift”


## Results


By aggressively removing psychological and logistical hurdles, JobAdder successfully transitioned their workplace culture from masking burnout to cultivating a visible, positive buzz of mental resilience.


- **44% Workforce Adoption:** A phenomenal jump from the initial 7% baseline within the first seven weeks of launch.
- **60.7% Repeat User Rate:** High month-over-month engagement retention, with active users averaging 4.6 monthly touchpoints.
- **4.88/5.0 Experience Rating:** Consistently exceptional quality marks across both clinical care and behavioral coaching tracks.
