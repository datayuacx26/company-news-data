# Appointment of Chief Marketing Officer, J.T. Starzecki

5E Advanced Materials, Inc. welcomes J.T. Starzecki as Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) to the leadership team.


In his role as CMO, J.T. will initially focus on market development and investor expansion activities in the retail and high net worth channels for the company.


J.T. has extensive global business experience in capital markets and customer/product development in the mining and minerals space. He previously held the position of CMO for Anglo American Crop Nutrients, focusing on food security initiatives around the world, and has been a Board Advisor for various minerals and mining companies. J.T. Holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in Accounting from St. John’s University.


With the new CMO position, we target to accelerate the awareness of our incredible boron story and 5E’s great position in the global supply chain
