# 13 Tips for Building a Standout B2B Ecommerce Marketplace

The growth of B2B ecommerce has been remarkable. According to research,[B2B ecommerce sales](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-us-b2b-e-commerce-will-reach-an-estimated-3-trillion-by-2027/) in the U.S. are projected to hit $3 trillion by 2027, reflecting the shift of business buying to online platforms. For B2B marketplaces, this presents both a unique opportunity and a challenge. To succeed, a B2B ecommerce marketplace need to offer exceptional value to both buyers and vendors—value that extends beyond what buyers could find from direct vendor-branded websites or competing marketplaces.


In this blog, we’re looking at actionable tips that can help[make your B2B ecommerce marketplace a top choice](https://www.getbalance.com/products/marketplace-os/) for buyers and vendors alike.


## Tapping Into a New Frontier of B2B Ecommerce


B2B marketplaces are reshaping traditional business transactions by providing the convenience of B2C ecommerce with the added flexibility and functionality that B2B transactions demand. As businesses increasingly prioritize efficiency, ecommerce marketplaces are becoming central to the purchasing experience. A survey found that[87% of B2B buyers](https://b2bmarketing.wbresearch.com/blog/the-next-generation-of-b2b-purchasing) primarily make purchases through marketplaces, where they can find multiple vendors and products in one place.


For B2B marketplaces, this shift brings an incredible opportunity to build strong buyer and vendor networks, encouraging ongoing transactions on a platform that’s efficient, secure, and reliable.


## The Challenges Faced by B2B Marketplaces


However, operating a B2B ecommerce marketplace comes with unique challenges. While the rewards are significant, navigating these complexities requires a strategic approach. Here are some of the main hurdles you may encounter:


- **Complex transactions.** B2B transactions often involve multiple stakeholders, longer sales cycle, and flexible payment terms. This complexity demands a streamlined system that can handle the nuances of each transaction without delays or errors.
- **High order volumes.** As a B2B ecommerce marketplace grows, the volume of orders can quickly become overwhelming. Handling numerous transactions across multiple vendors requires efficient operations to avoid bottlenecks and maintain buyer and vendor satisfaction.
- **Multi-vendor management.** Managing multiple vendors on one platform is challenging. Each vendor has unique needs, pricing models, and shipping requirements. Providing a cohesive experience for both vendors and buyers is key to running a successful marketplace.
- **Strict compliance standards.** B2B marketplaces handle sensitive financial data and often serve regulated industries. Adhering to compliance standards like PCI-DSS, SOC2, and GDPR is essential to protect data and build trust.
- **Disintermediation.** When buyers discover a product on a marketplace, they may be tempted to bypass the platform and purchase directly from the vendor. This challenge—known as disintermediation—can erode marketplace value if not addressed.
- **Net terms and credit risk management.**[B2B buyers expect access to payment terms](https://www.getbalance.com/post/net-terms-for-business-buyers/) . Evaluating credit risk accurately while minimizing default rates is crucial for maintaining financial health and keeping buyers engaged. Many B2B marketplaces lack the accounts receivable infrastructure to support net terms operations.
- **Vendor and buyer retention.** Retaining vendors and buyers is a priority for any marketplace. Providing a high-quality, reliable experience that keeps them returning to your platform is essential to sustaining growth.


Addressing these challenges requires adopting best practices that build trust, simplify operations, and create a marketplace experience that stands out. By meeting your business customers’ evolving needs and expectations, you establish your platform as a go-to solution for both buyers and vendors.


## 13 Essential Tips for a Thriving B2B Ecommerce Marketplace


### 1. Keep Product Information Accurate and Easy to Discover


Ensuring up-to-date product information and availability is crucial. Buyers need to make purchasing decisions based on accurate stock levels, specifications, and pricing. Offering advanced product discovery tools, such as visual search, filters, and detailed categorization, also creates a more intuitive shopping experience.


Example: Zoro, a B2B ecommerce marketplace for industrial supplies, provides up-to-date product information and filters that make it easy for buyers to find what they need quickly, enhancing the buyer experience and driving repeat purchases.


### 2. Provide Diverse, Frictionless Payment Methods


B2B buyers expect a range of payment options that fit their purchasing needs. In fact, they[abandon purchases](https://www.hokodo.co/resources/the-payment-experience-that-b2b-buyers-expect-in-2024) due to a lack of suitable settlement options (31%) at checkout. Providing ACH, wire transfers, and credit cards, can make transactions more accessible to business buyers and encourage repeat purchases. Automated and secure payment processes also reduce friction, allowing buyers to complete their purchases quickly.


### 3. Offer Net Terms for Flexibility and Retention


Offering net terms—such as net 30, net 60, or net 90—can be a significant advantage for B2B marketplaces. Many business buyers expect access to flexible credit terms, especially for large or recurring orders. By offering net terms, your marketplace can cater to buyer needs for financial flexibility, making it easier for them to complete transactions without upfront payments. This feature also increases buyer retention, as businesses are more likely to return to a marketplace that supports their cash flow needs.


FASHIONGO, a wholesale fashion marketplace, allows approved buyers to purchase on net terms, making it a preferred platform for retailers seeking flexible payment options.


### 4. Implement AI-Driven Credit Risk Management


Already offering net terms to your buyers? Efficient credit risk management is key for eliminating the risk of bad debt and making the most out of the payment flexibility you offer your buyers. AI-powered credit risk assessment can simplify and streamline the process by evaluating buyers more accurately and within seconds. This can boost approval rates, enabling more buyers to access credit terms—a key factor influencing their choice of supplier.


### 5. Ensure Multi-Vendor Management and Support


Buyers on B2B ecommerce marketplaces often prefer browsing a range of vendors in one place. Streamlining multi-vendor management allows you to deliver a seamless experience across various sellers.


This includes offering buyers customized payment terms per vendor and consolidating multiple transactions into one seamless experience. You can provide a frictionless checkout, unifying transactions across vendors, while still managing payments individually, ensuring an organized and efficient flow of funds.


Bay Supply, for example, provides businesses with the ability to purchase products from multiple vendors in one place, with consolidated orders and tracking. This approach creates a consistent experience for buyers, no matter how many vendors are involved in the transaction.


### 6. Enhance Vendor Payouts with Accuracy and Speed


Payouts are a core aspect of B2B ecommerce marketplace-vendor relationships. Vendors appreciate reliable, transparent payouts, which can increase vendor loyalty and reduce the likelihood of them moving to a different platform. Automating payouts ensures timely, accurate transactions, which reduces manual errors and builds vendor trust.


Look for features that let you:


- Split transactions by vendor, customize payouts down to the last detail, and handle manual payouts through a user-friendly dashboard or API
- Batch transactions on a schedule that works for you—daily, weekly, or monthly
- Stay on top of every payout with real-time status updates and clear details about any issues, making it easy to resolve problems on the spot
- Make fund transfers seamless after each buyer payment with easy identification and resolution of any failed payouts


### 7. Build a Unique Marketplace Experience to Discourage Off-Platform Transactions


Exclusivity can add value. By offering features like loyalty rewards, buyer-specific pricing, and marketplace-exclusive promotions, you give buyers reasons to stay. Advanced features, such as product recommendations, reviews, wish lists, and more, make the browsing experience more engaging.


Buyamia offers exclusive buyer discounts, curated packages, helpful tooltips, and transparent pricing for different product quantities, creating a unique experience that keeps buyers on the B2B ecommerce marketplace.


### 8. Self-Serve Vendor Onboarding and Centralized Management


A streamlined onboarding process benefits everyone. Self-service onboarding empowers vendors to join quickly and independently, which lowers administrative burdens on your team and shortens onboarding time. Vendors can also access a centralized dashboard with real-time insights on order activity, payments, and sales trends.


### 9. Strengthen Security and Compliance Standards


Security is paramount in B2B transactions. Both buyers and vendors need to know that their data and payments are secure. Meeting compliance standards helps protect sensitive information and build marketplace credibility. Data security influences the purchasing decisions of[65% of B2B buyers](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0019850124000452#:~:text=A%20survey%20of%20907%20B2B,process%20(TrustRadius%2C%202021).) .


### 10. Optimize the Buyer Journey with Self-Service Capabilities


Just like with vendors, buyers expect control over their marketplace experience. Self-service tools that let buyers track order history, view all of their invoices, pay them at once or schedule different payment dates based on the business needs are valuable additions. According to a[Mckinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/these-eight-charts-show-how-covid-19-has-changed-b2b-sales-forever) survey, 70-80% of B2B decision makers prefer remote human interactions or digital self-service.


eWorldTrade provides buyers with self-service tools including educational materials and dashboards to manage orders, track shipments, and view purchase history. These give buyers control and create a smooth experience that keeps them engaged on the platform.


### 11. Enhance Vendor Relations with Transparent Metrics and Communication


Vendors value clear, accessible metrics. Offering insights on sales data, order statuses, and payments strengthens trust and transparency. Transparent communication channels also foster cooperation, making it easy for vendors to feel like partners in the B2B ecommerce marketplace’s success.


EC21, for example, provides vendors with clear metrics on the traffic, industry trends, buyer inquiries, sales and order status, and more. These not only enhance communication and build trust, but also give vendors the ability to see areas to improve to grow sales.


### 12. Add Post-Sale Support and Services


The buyer experience doesn’t end at checkout. Post-purchase support, such as order tracking, returns management, and vendor-assisted customer service, adds real value. These services make the buyer’s journey more reliable and improve the chances of repeat business. Buyers appreciate knowing they have support available if they need it.


### 13. Emphasize Clarity in Fees


Transparency in fees, shipping costs, and delivery timelines is essential. Clear pricing and policies reduce surprises for buyers, building trust in your marketplace. Direct vendors may not always offer the same level of clarity, making your B2B ecommerce marketplace a straightforward, buyer-friendly option.


## Driving Growth with Best Practices and the Right Tools


Building a standout B2B ecommerce marketplace takes careful planning and attention to buyer and vendor needs. By implementing these tips, you can create a platform that fosters trust, simplifies processes, and enhances the buyer experience at every stage of the transaction. From offering flexible payment options and credit terms to ensuring transparent pricing and up-to-date product information, each of these strategies helps create a valuable, differentiated experience for buyers and vendors alike.


By focusing on these core elements, you can position your marketplace as a trusted destination for business buyers, helping your platform grow sustainably and strengthening its position as a go-to solution in the rapidly expanding B2B ecommerce landscape.
