# Free research tools


Our


[open-source tools](https://github.com/expectedparrot/edsl) let you simulate surveys using AI agent personas and


[popular language models](https://www.expectedparrot.com/models) of your choice. Results are returned to you as formatted datasets specified by the


[question types](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/questions.html) that you choose (free text, multiple choice, linear scale, matrix, etc.), with columns of information about all the components of your survey: agent traits, model parameters, question details, answers, tokens, API costs, raw responses, and more.


The tools come with


[built-in methods for analyzing results](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/results.html) , and also let you


[collect and compare responses from humans](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/humanize.html) as needed. When you run your simulations at Expected Parrot your surveys and


[results are cached automatically](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/remote_caching.html) , so you can retrieve and share them to let others reproduce and build on your work at no cost. You also get free credits for API calls to LLMs.

# Getting started


The EDSL package is available at


[GitHub](https://github.com/expectedparrot/edsl) and


[PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/edsl/) and is free for anyone to use. Our


[docs page](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/) has


[tutorials](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/starter_tutorial.html) for getting started and modifiable demo notebooks for exploring use cases and experimenting with AI agents and LLMs. You can run simulations on your own computer or


[create an account](https://www.expectedparrot.com/login) to run surveys at Expected Parrot and share projects and results with others.

# Example class project


NYU researcher


[James Traina](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-traina-50a8a729b/) recently designed an


[MBA case study](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xVxq5xbiVNjyuYZqlTpYHPfLuhR0X3jV/view?usp=sharing) using EDSL to simulate complex market research scenarios.
