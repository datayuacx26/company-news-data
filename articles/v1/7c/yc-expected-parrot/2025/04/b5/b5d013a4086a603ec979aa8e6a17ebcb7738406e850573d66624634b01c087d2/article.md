# Have an idea for an LLM experiment?


### Here are some reasons to run it at Expected Parrot, and easy ways to get started.


[Expected Parrot](https://substack.com/@expectedparrot)


and[Robin Horton](https://substack.com/@yerkes)


Apr 15, 2025


There are a bunch of reasons to run your LLM-based experiments at Expected Parrot! These include:


1.


**Open source features.** Our platform is built on


[EDSL](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest) , our


[open source package](https://github.com/expectedparrot/edsl) for conducting research with AI agents and language models.


2.


**Designed for researchers.** Our tools are


[designed](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/overview.html) for social science and market researchers who want to conduct complex tasks and experiments with LLMs—and


*not* spend lots of time on software development.


3.


**Easy access to models.** Our tools work with many LLMs from popular service providers that you can connect to with your own API keys or a


[single key managed by Expected Parrot](https://www.expectedparrot.com/getting-started/edsl-api-keys) . You can check current performance and token prices of available models


[here](https://www.expectedparrot.com/models) .


4.


**Reproducibility.** Your LLM prompts and responses are stored automatically, and always available to you. Anyone else can also


[retrieve them for free](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/remote_caching.html) whenever you share your experiments and results.


5.


**Transparency.** Our tools allow you to precisely specify and control all components of your experiments. Results are generated as readily usable, formatted datasets.


6.


**Built-in tools for analysis.** Our tools come with


[built-in methods](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/results.html) for analyzing, visualizing and exporting your results.


7.


**Free storage.** Your account comes with free


[storage](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/coop.html) for your projects so you can access them from anywhere.


8.


**Free sharing.** You can choose whether to share projects and results privately, or post them


[publicly](https://www.expectedparrot.com/content/explore) .


9.


**Free credits for API calls.** Your account comes with $25 in


[credits](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/credits.html) for API calls to language models for getting started. We also have lots of free credits for students and research projects!


## How it works


Expected Parrot tools are designed to make it easy to run complex experiments with LLMs, and to share and replicate research. A typical workflow consists of the following steps:


-


Design an experiment in


[EDSL](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest) , our


[open source Python package](https://github.com/expectedparrot/edsl) .


-


Run your EDSL code in a notebook (e.g., Jupyter, VS Code or Colab).


-


View your results at your account (


[Coop](https://www.expectedparrot.com/content/explore) is our free platform for storing and sharing content you create in EDSL).


-


Iterate on your work using


[built-in methods for analysis](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/results.html) .


-


Publish your work to allow others to


[replicate your research](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest/remote_caching.html) at no cost.

2.

[See instructions](https://www.expectedparrot.com/getting-started) .


## Explore demo notebooks


If your experiment idea is nascent, take a look at our many


[demo notebooks](https://docs.expectedparrot.com/en/latest) of example code for a variety of use cases. These are designed to help you get familiar with how EDSL works and also provide a modifiable template as your starting point.


If you have a use case that you don’t see in our demo notebooks, please send us a message and we’ll create one for you!
