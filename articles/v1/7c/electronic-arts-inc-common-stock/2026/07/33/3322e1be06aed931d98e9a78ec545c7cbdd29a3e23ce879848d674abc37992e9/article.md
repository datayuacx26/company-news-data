# EA SPORTS™ presents Beckham, Zidane and Zlatan in conversation for the first time.

# EA SPORTS™ presents Beckham, Zidane and Zlatan together for the first time.


## Three football icons. One exclusive conversation. Streaming July 18, 2026 on YouTube and Spotify.


### July 13, 2026


### **An unprecedented conversation.**


This July, **EA SPORTS™ Presents Icons of Football** brings together **David Beckham, Zinédine Zidane, and Zlatan Ibrahimović** for a first-ever sit-down conversation between three of the game's most influential figures.


Premiering July 18 on[EA SPORTS YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@easportsfc) and[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/user/easportsaudio) , EA SPORTS™ Presents Icons of Football captures a candid, unscripted and wide-ranging conversation, moderated by Alex Aljoe. For the first time, fans will hear Beckham, Zidane, and Zlatan together sharing stories, insights and perspectives that only they could.


Few players have shaped football culture like Beckham, Zidane, and Zlatan. Individually, they redefined excellence on the pitch and influenced the game far beyond it. Together, through EA SPORTS Presents Icons of Football, they unite for a conversation only EA SPORTS could bring to life, offering fans a rare opportunity to hear three legends reflect on the moments that defined their careers, how football has evolved and what's next for the sport.
