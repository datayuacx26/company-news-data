# Announcing bidirectional syncing with ad platforms

Today we are thrilled to announce full, two-way sync support with the following ad platforms:


- Google Ads ([documentation](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/google-ads-connections) )
- LinkedIn Ads ([documentation](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/linkedin-ads) )
- Facebook Ads ([documentation](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/facebook-ads-connections) )
- Microsoft Ads ([documentation](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/microsoft-ads) )


### Sync your data to ad platforms


You can now sync the following data from your databases, data warehouses, CRMs, spreadsheets, and APIs:


- User list ad audiences
- Company list ad audiences
- Conversion events


### Sync from your ad platforms


You can also sync all your ad and campaign performance data from your ad platforms into your data warehouses, databases, and cloud storage systems like S3.


Yet more functionality that you can now consolidate in Polytomic for peace of mind.
