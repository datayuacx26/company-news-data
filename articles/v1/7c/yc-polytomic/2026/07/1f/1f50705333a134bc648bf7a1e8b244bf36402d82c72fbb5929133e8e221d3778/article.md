# Announcing CDC streaming from Amazon DynamoDB

Change Data Capture (CDC) is a process to capture changes (inserts, updates, and deletes) from a datastore's real-time stream, thus eliminating the need to scan tables to figure out changes.


Today we are thrilled to announce support for CDC streaming from Amazon DynamoDB into your data warehouses, databases, and cloud storage buckets (Amazon S3, Azure Blob Storage, and Google Cloud Storage).


Whether you are syncing five tables to your data warehouse or 500, Polytomic will happily handle your streaming changes at all scale.
