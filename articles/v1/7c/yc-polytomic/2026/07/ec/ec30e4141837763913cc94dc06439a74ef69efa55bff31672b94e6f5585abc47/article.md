# Announcing the Model Sync activity log

Today we are thrilled to release our Model Sync activity log. This is a visual timeline of all changes to a Polytomic Model Sync configuration, whether these changes are to field mappings, filters, error notification settings, or anything else.


Wondering who removed a field mapping to Salesforce? Or who added you to the error-notification list? Wonder no more, as every Model Sync now comes with a full timeline of configuration changes:


Zooming in:


Every Model Sync activity log comes with filters so you can filter your timeline view to particular change events (for example, only field mapping modifications) or to particular people.


For more, see our documentation[here](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/model-sync-activity-log) . And, as always, please email us at support@polytomic.com with any questions!
