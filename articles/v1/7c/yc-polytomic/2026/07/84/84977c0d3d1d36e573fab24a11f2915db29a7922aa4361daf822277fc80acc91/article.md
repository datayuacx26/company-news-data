# Announcing bidirectional syncing with sales engagement platforms

Today we are thrilled to announce full, two-way sync and ETL support with the following sales engagement platforms:


- Apollo.io ([documentation](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/apollo) )
- Outreach ([documentation](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/outreach) )
- Salesloft ([documentation](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/salesloft) )
- Smartlead.ai ([documentation](https://docs.polytomic.com/docs/smartlead-ai) )


### Sync your data to sales engagement platforms


You can now sync the following to your sales engagement platforms from your databases, data warehouses, CRMs, spreadsheets, APIs, and cloud storage:


- Add Contacts to Outbound Sequences
- Companies
- Contacts


### Sync from your sales engagement platforms


You can also sync all contacts, companies, and outbound sequence data from your sales engagement platforms to your data warehouses, databases, CRMs, spreadsheets, and cloud storage systems like S3.


### Full API support


If you need to embed this functionality in your own platform, you can use[Polytomic Connect](https://apidocs.polytomic.com/) , our white-label API. To see an example[click here](https://apidocs.polytomic.com/2024-02-08/guides/code-examples/adding-users-from-snowflake-to-salesloft-cadence) for a full code sample illustrating how to push contacts from Snowflake to Salesloft Cadences.
