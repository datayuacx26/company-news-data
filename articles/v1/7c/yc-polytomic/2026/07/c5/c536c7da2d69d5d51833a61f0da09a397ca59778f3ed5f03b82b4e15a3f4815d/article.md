# Announcing Polytomic Quicksearch

Today we are launching Polytomic Quicksearch, a universal search and command bar.


Hitting **⌘K** on Mac or **Control-K** on Windows will produce a search bar through which you can search for anything in your Polytomic workspace or trigger common commands:


As you start typing you will instantly see search results that include your objects within Polytomic: syncs, connections, models, and even fields within models. If you are a member of multiple workspaces, you will also see matching workspace names that you can immediately switch to.


Our philosophy has always been to minimize the amount of time our customers spend in our product. Get in, make your change, and move on with your life. Quicksearch is yet another step we take to further that.
