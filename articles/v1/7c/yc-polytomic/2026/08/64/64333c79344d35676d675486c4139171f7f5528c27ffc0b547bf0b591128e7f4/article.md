# Announcing our EU data region

We are excited to announce that Polytomic's EU data centre is now live and open to everyone!


Polytomic's customers already span all continents (except Antarctica - for now!) and are constantly syncing many trillions of records between their systems, all through Polytomic.


With this new data centre, EU customers now have the option to ensure that all their Polytomic data is exclusively processed in Europe, thus satisfying any data-residency desires.


For those who want their Polytomic workspaces provisioned on the EU data centre, simply write to us at support@polytomic.com and we shall make it so.
