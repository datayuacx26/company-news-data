### What's actually changed (and why now matters)


For a couple of years, AI in the contact center meant proof-of-concept projects and a lot of promises. That's over. Agentic AI has moved from experiment to enterprise-ready, and the contact center is one of the highest-impact places to put AI to work, where volume is the heaviest, and every extra minute of wait time adds up fast.


Independent analysts are backing that up too. Aragon Research's[2026 outlook on AI agents](https://www.five9.com/resources/report/aragon-research-ai-agent-2026) points to the shift and inflection point we're seeing with customers every day.


That shift is what's behind the rise of Voice AI Agents: AI that doesn't just answer a question, but understands intent, takes action across your systems, and resolves the request. The same way your best agent would, at a scale no human team could ever match on his or her own.

### Let's address the fear directly


I'll say what a lot of people are thinking but not always asking out loud. Does this mean AI is coming for contact center jobs?


No. Five9 Voice AI Agents are built to augment your team, not replace them. Human judgment, escalation handling, and real experience aren't going anywhere; they're as important as they ever were. The seamless Five9 Human + AI collaboration model works because agentic AI can take on the volume and repetitive work, so your people can work smarter, more effectively and more efficiently overall.
