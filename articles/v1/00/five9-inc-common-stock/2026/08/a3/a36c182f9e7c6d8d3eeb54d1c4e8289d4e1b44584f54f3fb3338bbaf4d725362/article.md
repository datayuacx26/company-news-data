# The Right Agent, Every Time: Routing Meets Real Performance with Five9 Agentic QM

For decades, contact centers have relied on a "set it and forget it" approach to routing. You assign agent skills, hope for the best and manually update them when you find the time. But what if your routing could adjust itself in near real-time, based not on static profiles, but on an agent's actual performance in every single interaction?


That's not a future-state promise. It's here now.


Five9 is proud to announce an industry-first integration:[Five9 Agentic Quality Management (AQM)](https://www.five9.com/products/capabilities/contact-center-agentic-management-monitoring-tools) is now directly connected to Five9 Intelligent CX Routing. This breakthrough closes the loop between agent performance and customer assignment, ensuring every customer is connected with the best agent for their specific needs, every time.


### From insight to action, automatically


Legacy[quality management (QM)](https://www.five9.com/blog/better-calls-happier-customers-how-genai-contact-centers-transforming-quality-management) systems provide a limited, backward-looking snapshot of agent performance based on a small sample of interactions. Supervisors listen to a handful of calls, assign scores and schedule coaching weeks later. By then, the moment that mattered is long gone, and the routing logic built on that stale data is already out of date.


Five9 Agentic QM changes the equation. It evaluates up to 100% of interactions, not just what was said, but how it was said. It reads tone, sentiment and outcomes across voice, chat, email and AI agent-led conversations. That complete visibility creates a rich, dynamic profile of every agent’s true strengths and development opportunities.


Now, that intelligence doesn't just sit on a dashboard. It goes to work.


Think of it as an agentic flywheel, where evaluation drives improvement and improvement generates even more intelligence.


### From blind spots to breakthroughs


1.


**Dynamic Agent Profiles:** As Agentic QM evaluates interactions, it automatically updates agent proficiency scores for the attributes that matter most to your business — like technical expertise, sales acumen or de-escalation skill.


1.


**Intelligent Routing Decisions:** The Five9 routing engine uses these dynamic, performance-based scores to guide routing decisions. When a call comes in, the system identifies the customer’s intent and instantly matches them with the agent best equipped to deliver the right outcome, without manual oversight.


1.


**Continuous Improvement:** As agents improve, their proficiency scores update automatically. Routing becomes a living, breathing system that evolves with your team, eliminating skill-mismatch and the manual effort of keeping profiles current.


Each interaction feeds the next, and the contact center gets smarter, not by working harder, but by[learning faster](https://www.five9.com/blog/agentic-future-when-quality-management-becomes-your-strategic-growth-engine) .


### Visibility as a multiplier


This kind of always-on visibility doesn’t just improve individual interactions, it accelerates performance across the entire operation. Leaders gain a holistic view of what's driving key metrics like CSAT, first contact resolution and average handle time. Supervisors can spot trends early and focus coaching where it makes the biggest impact. And agents feel supported instead of scrutinized, because feedback reflects their complete performance rather than a random handful of calls.


That transparency builds trust across the organization. Agents trust the system because it reflects their full effort. Supervisors trust the data because it’s objective. Leaders trust their teams because progress is visible in real time. When trust replaces guesswork, performance naturally follows.


### A self-optimizing contact center


Every contact center leader dreams of an operation that runs smoothly, learns constantly and gets better with every customer interaction. Pairing Agentic QM with Intelligent CX Routing brings that vision to life.


Instead of waiting for a monthly scorecard, supervisors get instant notice when an interaction starts to go off track and can step in before frustration builds. At the same time, the routing engine is already using what it learned from the last thousand interactions to make the next assignment smarter. Traditional quality frameworks create static insight: reports and meetings that happen long after the moment has passed. This model keeps insight alive, connecting what's happening right now to what needs to happen next.


Over time, small optimizations become major transformation. Handle times drop, satisfaction rises and the contact center shifts from a cost center to a genuine growth engine, one interaction at a time.


The result is a more efficient and effective contact center. You’ll see fewer transfers, improved first contact resolution and higher customer satisfaction. For agents, it means they are consistently put in a position to succeed, handling the interactions they are best prepared for, which leads to greater job satisfaction and reduced attrition.
