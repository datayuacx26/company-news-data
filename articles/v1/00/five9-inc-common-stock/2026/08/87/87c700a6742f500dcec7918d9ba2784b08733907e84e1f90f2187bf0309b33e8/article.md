In our last post, we shared[Five9 Fusion for Salesforce](https://www.five9.com/blog/your-ai-your-choice-five9-fusion-salesforce) and the choice it gives joint customers to deploy Five9 AI Agent Assist or activate Einstein for Service via Five9 TranscriptStream. Today, we're going deeper on what choosing Five9 AI Agent Assist looks like in practice with[Salesforce Fusion](https://www.five9.com/products/capabilities/crm-integration/salesforce-cloud-service-byot?link_norewrite=) and why it's a game-changer for contact centers running on Salesforce.


Customers expect faster resolutions, more personalized service, and agents who are prepared from the first word of a conversation.[Five9 AI Agent Assist](https://www.five9.com/products/capabilities/agent-assist) meets that expectation by delivering real-time intelligence directly to agents inside the Salesforce workspace they already use every day.


Here are the key use cases driving results:


### Real-time guidance and checklists


As a customer conversation unfolds, Five9 AI Agent Assist analyzes what's being said in real time and automatically surfaces the most relevant guidance with step-by-step troubleshooting checklists, compliance reminders or product-specific knowledge cards right within the agent's console. This eliminates the need to manually search knowledge bases mid-call. The right information appears at the right moment, reducing agent cognitive load and keeping conversations moving forward. In high-complexity industries like financial services, healthcare and telecommunications where agents must navigate intricate policies and regulatory requirements, real-time guidance is a critical differentiator.


### Objection handling and upsell opportunities


Sales-oriented contact centers face a unique challenge: agents need to handle objections confidently while identifying genuine moments to introduce relevant products or services. When Five9 AI Agent Assist detects conversation patterns associated with common objections or upsell opportunities, it surfaces targeted guidance to help the agent respond effectively.


This moves contact centers beyond reactive service toward proactive, revenue-generating interactions without requiring agents to identify the right moment on their own. The AI handles the pattern recognition; the agent delivers the human connection.


### How does AI agent assist speed up new agent onboarding?


Hiring and onboarding new agents is one of the most significant ongoing costs for any contact center. Traditionally, it takes weeks or months for new agents to reach full proficiency. Five9 AI Agent Assist dramatically compresses that curve by providing real-time coaching and step-by-step checklists during live calls functioning as an always-on training layer embedded directly in the workflow.


New agents can handle more complex calls earlier in their tenure, reducing dependence on supervisor intervention and shortening time to proficiency. In a tight labor market where agent retention is a strategic priority, a better onboarding experience makes a real difference.


### AI-generated call summaries and reduced after-call work


[After-call work (ACW)](https://www.five9.com/faq/what-is-acw-in-call-centers) is a persistent drain on contact center efficiency. When agents spend several minutes manually writing summaries after every interaction, it adds up fast. Resulting in increased average handle time and inconsistency in how interactions are documented in Salesforce.


Five9 AI Agent Assist's summary capability eliminates this bottleneck. Powered by Generative AI, it automatically produces a comprehensive call summary in seconds once an interaction ends. The agent can review and confirm it with a single click, and it is written directly into the Salesforce CRM record ensuring accurate, consistent documentation without the manual overhead. Supervisors gain structured interaction records for quality monitoring and coaching, and agents carry forward the context needed to personalize every future customer touchpoint.


### What supervisor insights does Five9 AI Agent Assist provide?


Five9 AI Agent Assist gives supervisors the visibility they need to continuously improve team performance without listening to every call. Real-time transcriptions and AI-generated summaries surface customer trends, flag interactions for review and identify coaching opportunities at scale. Within the Salesforce environment, those insights connect directly to CRM data, giving leaders a richer view of agent performance and customer sentiment across the entire contact center.


### The bottom line


Five9 AI Agent Assist helps contact centers reduce average handle time while keeping call information consistent and accurate. Within Five9[Fusion for Salesforce](https://www.five9.com/resources/data-sheet/five9-for-service-cloud-voice-byot) , every one of those efficiency gains flows directly into the Salesforce console your team already relies on covering the full lifecycle of an agent interaction so your team can focus on what matters most: delivering an exceptional customer experience.
