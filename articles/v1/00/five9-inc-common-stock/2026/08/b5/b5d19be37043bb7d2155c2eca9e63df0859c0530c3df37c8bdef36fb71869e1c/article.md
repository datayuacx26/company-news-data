# Ending Status Chaos: Achieving True Unity with Five9 & Zendesk

In the pursuit of a perfect customer experience, we often focus on big, flashy innovations. But what if the single greatest source of friction in your contact center isn't a missing feature, but your core systems working in silos instead of in sync?


For too long, agents have been caught in a digital tug-of-war. They toggle between systems, manually update their status in multiple windows, and get hit with new tasks from a CRM that thinks they’re free while they’re actively handling a customer call. This isn’t just inefficient; it’s a quiet operational failure that undermines your entire CX strategy.


Today, we’re fixing the foundation. With the launch of Federated Routing and presence sync for the[Five9 Adapter for Zendesk](https://www.five9.com/resources/data-sheet/five9-adapter-zendesk) , we are eliminating the status chaos and enabling a truly unified agent experience.


### From multi-channel chaos to true omnichannel


Many businesses claim to offer omnichannel support, but in reality, they operate a collection of siloed, multi-channel tools. An agent might be on a voice call through Five9, but their Zendesk status remains “Available,” triggering a new ticket assignment. The result is a distracted agent, a delayed customer, and a blind spot for supervisors trying to manage team capacity.


That’s not an omnichannel experience. It’s a system working against itself. To deliver on the promise of seamless CX, your platforms can’t just share data — they must operate as one.


### Introducing Federated Routing and Presence Sync: one agent, one status


Federated Routing and Presence Sync synchronizes agent presence between Five9 and Zendesk automatically and in real time.


When an agent accepts a call in Five9, their Zendesk status is instantly set to 'busy.' When the agent accepts a chat interaction in Zendesk, the Five9 routing engine knows not to send them a new interaction. The sync is bidirectional and instantaneous. Whether the status change originates in Five9 or Zendesk. This asynchronous process ensures that agent availability is accurately reflected across both systems, creating a seamless and efficient experience.


Federated Routing and Presence Sync in Five9 Adapter for Zendesk means no more manual toggles. No more guesswork. Just one unified source of truth for agent availability. It’s a simple concept with a profound impact.

Frontline teams are facing unprecedented pressure. Asking them to manage their presence across disconnected systems adds unnecessary cognitive load, leading to fatigue, errors, and burnout. Effective workforce management is impossible without accurate, real-time data. When presence is fragmented, supervisors are flying blind. #### 3. Deliver on the promise of seamless service


Modern customers expect speed and consistency across channels. They don’t know or care that you use separate systems for your contact center and CRM. When your platforms are aligned, routing becomes more intelligent, wait times decrease, and the customer’s path to resolution becomes shorter and smoother. This is how loyalty is won.
