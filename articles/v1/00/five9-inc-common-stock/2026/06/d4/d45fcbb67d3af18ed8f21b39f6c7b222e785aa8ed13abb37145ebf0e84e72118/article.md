June 24, 2026


CX leaders know that the contact center is the heartbeat of customer loyalty. As customer inquiries become increasingly complex, agents are regularly tasked with navigating vast amounts of information and multiple systems to deliver the seamless, personalized experiences modern consumers expect. While managing this operational complexity has traditionally been a challenge, AI now presents a powerful opportunity for CX transformation. In fact,[91% of customer service leaders](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-02-18-gartner-survey-finds-ninety-one-percent-of-customer-service-leaders-under-pressure-to-implement-ai-in-2026) are under executive pressure to implement AI this year.


The most successful organizations are moving beyond viewing AI simply as an automation tool to embracing a model of true Human-AI collaboration.[Five9 AI Agent Assist](https://www.five9.com/products/capabilities/agent-assist) is purpose-built to champion this new way of working. By pairing human intuition and empathy with real-time AI assistance, contact centers can unlock unprecedented benefits. This collaborative approach not only helps agents succeed but positively impacts[key metrics](https://www.five9.com/blog/ultimate-contact-center-agent-productivity-checklist-are-you-track) , including increasing Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), optimizing Average Handle Time (AHT), and driving stronger agent retention.


Here are five reasons why fostering Human-AI collaboration using tools like Agent Assist is the ultimate competitive advantage for your CX strategy:


### 1. Help agents prioritize connection over admin tasks


Instead of replacing agents, AI acts as a supportive partner during live customer interactions. Because Agent Assist continuously listens and captures the conversation through live transcription, agents are completely freed from the distraction of manual notetaking while the customer is speaking.


With AI quietly capturing details for automated post-call summaries, agents are free to focus entirely on active listening and genuine empathetic connection.[Five9 2026 Business Leaders CX Report](https://www.five9.com/resources/e-book/business-leaders-cx-report) found that 47% of organizations have already reduced agent workloads through AI-driven documentation and compliance note automation, and 39% through after-call work and summarization — freeing up meaningful time in every shift.


### 2. Unlock faster resolutions by eliminating the ‘please hold’ barrier


Human agents naturally excel at building rapport, but that natural connection is often broken the moment they have to say, "Let me look that up, please hold." A recent[Verint report](https://www.verint.com/press-room/2026-press-releases/nearly-one-third-of-contact-center-agents-plan-to-quit-as-agent-experience-falls-short/) found that a staggering 45% of calls involve mid-call answer searching. When Agent Assist works alongside the agent, those frustrating breaks in conversation disappear.


Powered by LLM-based triggers, Agent Assist intelligently identifies complex customer intents during the conversation and automatically activates suggested reminders, coaching and knowledge-base articles on the agent's screen in real-time. The CX Leaders Report reinforces the need:[46% of organizations already cite knowledge retrieval](https://www.five9.com/resources/e-book/business-leaders-cx-report) as an area where AI has reduced agent workloads, confirming that real-time knowledge surfacing is one of the highest-impact uses of agent assist technology. This instant delivery of information allows the agent to maintain an uninterrupted, natural dialogue, resolving the issue while shortening the time to resolution.


### 3. Deliver confident, consistent experiences


Navigating complex business policies and product details can be overwhelming for even the most experienced agents. AI Checklists act as an ever-present support system, surfacing contextual action items and tracking adherence to company compliance regulations, such as HIPAA disclosures. This shared intelligence translates into a consistently reliable and confident brand experience for every caller.


### 4. Drive measurable business outcomes


Human-AI collaboration isn't just about making work easier; it is a strategic driver of ROI.[Five9 AI Agent Assist](https://www.five9.com/products/capabilities/agent-assist) provides contact center managers and supervisors with AI-powered insights that immediately surface customer trends in the contact center, analyze KPIs and identify opportunities for agent optimization and performance to reduce costs. For example, by proactively guiding agents to cross-sell and upsell opportunities during a conversation, Agent Assist helps companies increase revenue by 17% on average.


### 5. Foster agent empowerment and retention


When agents are actively supported by AI, the[cognitive load](https://www.five9.com/blog/agent-replacement-myth-hiding-real-problem) associated with their roles is lifted. With Agent Assist, agents feel more capable and confident resolving complex issues. In fact,[45% of contact center leaders](https://www.five9.com/resources/e-book/business-leaders-cx-report) say agents are handling more complex inquiries today than they were two years ago — and 40% specifically credit AI with elevating agents into complex, emotionally-charged cases. This collaborative, supportive environment boosts morale, reduces burnout and turns everyday agents into highly motivated, long-term contributors.


The proof is in the numbers:[47% of organizations](https://www.five9.com/resources/e-book/business-leaders-cx-report) have already reduced agent workloads through AI-driven documentation and compliance note automation, and 39% through after-call work and summarization, freeing up meaningful time in every shift.


By seamlessly integrating intelligence into the natural flow of work, we[empower your human workforce](https://www.five9.com/resources/datasheet-five9-agent-assist) to transform your contact center into a modern engine of exceptional, empathetic customer service.
