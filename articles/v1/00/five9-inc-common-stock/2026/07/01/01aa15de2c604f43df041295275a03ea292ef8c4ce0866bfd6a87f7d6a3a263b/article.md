Five9 recently launched Voice AI Agents, a new self-service product built on a completely new agentic architecture. It handles complex, multi-turn conversations and takes real action across business systems, without relying on scripted flows.


This webinar gives channel partners a practical walkthrough of the product. We'll cover what it does, how it differs from Five9's existing self-service offerings, and where it fits in a customer conversation. Expect a clear look at core capabilities, ideal use cases, key differentiators, and positioning guidance. We'll also touch on pricing, availability, and the governance features that matter most to enterprise buyers.


We'll unpack the shift from scripted to fully agentic and what it means for containment, cost, risk, revenue, and customer satisfaction. The session closes with a live demo. Partners will leave with the knowledge and tools to identify good-fit opportunities and bring Voice AI Agents confidently into customer conversations.
