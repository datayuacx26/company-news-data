The contact center AI landscape is evolving fast and so are the expectations of the enterprises navigating it. As AI becomes central to how service organizations operate, one question keeps coming up: who decides which AI powers your agents? For too long, the answer has been dictated by the platforms themselves. Organizations running Five9 with Salesforce deserve better. They want the flexibility to choose the solution that best fits their existing investments, their agent workflows, and their long-term business goals.


That's exactly what[Five9 Fusion for Salesforce](https://www.five9.com/products/capabilities/crm-integration/salesforce-cloud-service-byot?link_norewrite=) delivers, providing choice in which AI powers your agents.


### A partnership built on over 15 years of trust


Five9 and Salesforce have been working together for more than 15 years, supporting over 1,000 shared customers and 160,000+ service agents globally. Five9 Fusion for Salesforce is the natural evolution of that collaboration with a[deeply integrated, AI-elevated solution](https://www.five9.com/news/news-releases/new-five9-fusion-salesforce-deepens-partnership-deliver-integrated-ai-powered) that combines Five9's real-time system of action with Salesforce CRM to create a unified, intelligent foundation for customer experience.


But Five9 Fusion for Salesforce is more than a technical integration. It represents a shared commitment to giving joint customers the tools, flexibility and AI capabilities they need to deliver modern service experiences without forcing them into a one-size-fits-all approach.


At the heart of this integration is a principle every enterprise deserves: choice. Specifically, the choice of which AI solution powers your agents' real-time experience.


### What are your AI agent assist options with Five9 Fusion for Salesforce?


Not every organization is in the same place on its AI journey, and there's no single right answer when it comes to agent assist. Your approach depends on where your team is today. The tools that are already in place, the workflows your agents rely on and the outcomes you're working toward. With Five9 Fusion for Salesforce, customers can choose the path that makes the most sense for them.


#### Option 1: Five9 AI Agent Assist


For organizations that want a best-in-class agent assist[experience within Salesforce](https://www.five9.com/resources/data-sheet/five9-for-service-cloud-voice-byot) , Five9 AI Agent Assist delivers everything agents need to perform at their best in real time, right within their service console.


Five9 AI Agent Assist empowers every role with AI by:


-


Surfacing real-time guidance cards and checklists


-


Automatically generating post-call summaries


-


Providing live in-call transcription


With Five9 AI Agent Assist as their fingertips, contact centers can:


-


Free agents from manual notetaking


-


Reduce average handle time,


-


Accelerate after-call work


-


Improve first contact resolution


-


Enable agents to stay focused on the customer rather than scrambling for the right answer


For new agents, real-time coaching accelerates time to proficiency. For experienced agents, it surfaces the right information at exactly the right moment. The result is a more confident, more efficient and more empathetic agent experience.


#### Option 2: Einstein for Service, powered by Five9 TranscriptStream


For organizations that have already invested in Salesforce Einstein for Service and want to activate its full potential within their contact center, Five9 TranscriptStream is the bridge that makes it possible.


Five9 TranscriptStream is a real-time transcription streaming API that captures live voice call data and streams it directly into Salesforce grounding Einstein for Service AI features including Next Best Actions, Reply Recommendations and automated Call Summaries with live, accurate conversation data. Rather than processing audio after the fact, TranscriptStream delivers transcription as the conversation unfolds, word by word, in real time.


This distinction matters. It means Einstein for Service can act on what the customer is saying right now surfacing contextually relevant guidance mid-call, not in a post-call report. Agents benefit from AI-powered recommendations that are anchored in the live conversation, while supervisors and managers gain a richer, more complete view of interaction quality and customer sentiment across the contact center.


### Here’s why this flexibility matters


In the era of AI, vendor lock-in is a real and growing risk. Enterprises are making significant investments in AI tooling across their CRM, contact center and workforce platforms, and they need those investments to work together, not compete with each other.


Five9 Fusion for Salesforce was built with that reality in mind. Whether your team is standardized on Five9 AI Agent Assist or you're leveraging Einstein for Service as your Salesforce-native AI layer, the integration supports your strategy without forcing you to compromise. You get a future-ready foundation that meets your business where it is today and scales with you as your AI approach evolves.


The right AI for your agents is the one that fits your business. With[Five9 Fusion for Salesforce](https://www.five9.com/fusion-sfdc) , that decision stays yours.
