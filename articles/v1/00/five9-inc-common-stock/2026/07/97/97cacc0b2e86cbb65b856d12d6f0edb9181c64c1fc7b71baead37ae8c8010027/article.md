Today’s healthcare providers face fierce competition for patients’ attention. Between relentless calls, overflowing inboxes, and constant social media buzz, critical healthcare messages often get lost in the noise. Research shows[patients frequently struggle to recall](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC539473/) even basic details like their last physical date, upcoming screenings, or preventive care options offered by their provider, not to mention more involved directions.


But that’s not the only problem. Communication in healthcare has long been only reactive. Appointment reminders sent only the day before; bills that appear months after services are rendered. But innovative communication methods are empowering patients to take charge of their healthcare journeys and transforming patient engagement methods.


### What is patient engagement?


Patient engagement is about shifting the traditionally reactive nature of healthcare communication to proactive interaction and empowering patients to become actively engaged in their healthcare experience. This shift is due, in part, because of healthcare's overall digital transformation via[patient engagement solutions](https://five9.com/solutions/healthcare) .


No longer must providers wait to discuss important information with patients until their next visit. Instead, they can share test results, give directives, refill prescriptions, and even have full conversations — either virtually or asynchronously through chats — to keep patients engaged in their healthcare experience.


### Patient engagement solutions


Fueling the proactive communication revolution are patient engagement solutions, designed to help healthcare providers and contact centers better serve their patients. The platforms that make this possible offer a wealth of features and capabilities, from omnichannel patient communication options (including self-service), automated workflows, patient histories, appointment requests and reminders, and more.


Modern solutions like[Five9](https://www.five9.com/solutions/healthcare) connect directly with key systems such as Epic and other EHRs to streamline engagement and support — while keeping patient information secure and providers compliant. Their value lies not only in the information they collect and the patient engagement strategies they enable, but also the access to new technology such as AI to make patient and provider experiences that much better.


### Using AI to help with patient engagement


In our[2026 Business Decision Maker Report](https://www.five9.com/resources/e-book/business-leaders-cx-report) , we found that 88% of healthcare organizations have either implemented or are piloting using AI in their contact centers. But data security is still the top concern with AI, and because of healthcare's highly regulated nature, providers may struggle to find applicable use cases than AI adopters in other industries.


However, modern patient engagement solutions empower you to use AI while still meeting all the necessary regulations such as HIPAA. Imagine sending targeted patient engagement campaigns that address specific needs and preferences and promote important things like preventative care and healthy habits at home. Intelligent patient engagement solutions make that possible.


AI also helps elevate agent roles, and we found that healthcare CX leaders are finding it especially helpful with complex, emotionally-charged customer cases. In addition to reducing agent burnout, though, AI has the potential to further improve patient engagement strategies across the industry and reap the benefits of an AI-powered healthcare communication strategy.


### The benefits of proactive communication


#### Better patient outcomes


Gaps in patient treatment can come from many things: a missed appointment, a forgotten dosage, improper following of a provider's directions. But when patients actively participate in their care, they're more likely to stick to their treatment plans.


Activities such as automated appointment reminders can help reduce no-shows and reschedules. Reminders to take medication — through automated SMS, push notifications, or even phone calls — can help ensure treatment plans are followed more closely. Regular check-ins over chat can help keep track of progress, without the need for lengthy in-person visits and free up providers' time for more intensive cases.


All this proactive communication and activity leads to better disease management, fewer complications, and, ultimately, improved patient outcomes and safety. And, with a modern patient engagement solution, healthcare providers can meet patient demands for easy appointment scheduling/rescheduling, prescription refills, and automated callbacks. Patients no longer need to navigate complex, outdated phone systems and can instead manage their care right from their cell phones or other devices.


#### Enhanced patient experience


Improving patient outcomes, however, is only part of the puzzle, and encouraging patients to take part in their healthcare requires prioritizing a good patient experience. Meeting them where they are with omnichannel communication and strong patient engagement strategies will help ensure patients feel well cared for.


Even something as simple as proactive reminders show patients that providers care and are invested in their patients' well-being, building trust, reducing stress, and creating a more informed patient journey. By tracking important[healthcare contact center trends](https://www.five9.com/blog/four-healthcare-contact-center-trends-2024) , healthcare CX leaders can ensure they are providing patients with the positive (and proactive) experiences they need for better health.


#### Improved contact center health


In addition to patients' health, healthcare CX leaders must also contend with maintaining (and improving) the health of their contact center. To do this, many track a number of[contact center metrics](https://www.five9.com/blog/15-essential-contact-center-metrics-and-kpis-measure-healthcare) , such as[first call resolution rates](https://www.five9.com/faq/what-is-first-call-resolution-fcr) , talk/listen ratios, and perhaps most importantly, patient satisfaction.


The Hospital Consumer Assessment of Healthcare Providers and Systems (HCAHPS) survey plays a crucial role in healthcare quality metrics. When patients are part of a strong healthcare communication strategy, they are empowered to make more informed decisions regarding their care, and feel heard and valued. This is often reflected in their feedback, contributing to improved patient satisfaction scores.


As an added benefit, engaged patients are more likely to make the most of the healthcare resources available to them. These patients are typically very discerning regarding when to seek medical attention and are less likely to require readmission.


**Increased patient engagement can reduce readmission rates by**[up to 14%](https://www.techtarget.com/patientengagement/feature/Using-Patient-Engagement-Apps-to-Reduce-Hospital-Readmissions) and mitigate healthcare costs thanks to fewer complications.


#### Enhanced treatment adherence


Many patients[don’t take their medications](https://www.synchronyx.com/blog/pqa-2026-reasons-for-nonadherence) as prescribed. However, patients who are actively involved in their care — as well as those that are given reminders through proactive engagement — are much more likely to stick to their treatment plan.


When patients take their medications as directed, it helps them recover more quickly, reduces the likelihood of readmission, and makes it easier for patients and providers to manage their health in the long run. The key is building a trusting relationship — a two-way street where providers and patients work together through in-person and digital patient engagement. In the end, when both parties work together as a team, everyone achieves better results.


#### Reduced AR days


A major pain point for providers is collecting payments promptly, but proactive billing reminders and easy payment portals can be game changers. Imagine if each of your patients received timely, clear messages about upcoming bills, with readily available and easy-to-use online payment options. Patients are far more likely to pay their invoices on time, and providers significantly reduce their Accounts Receivable (AR) days, improving revenue cycle management (RCM), cash flow, and overall financial stability.


#### Potential cost reduction


Improved care management through proactive measures can also lead to lower healthcare costs down the line — for both patients and providers. Reduced no-shows, improved medication adherence, and early intervention can help reduce the need for repeated and expensive treatment plans.


Additionally, when providers can virtually "see" patients for more routine care — prescription refills, dosage alterations based on efficacy or changes in patients' situations, etc. — their schedules are open for more intensive care such as illnesses, surgeries, consultations, and more. With both sides of the healthcare conversation flowing more smoothly, the entire healthcare system can be more efficient — and cost-effective.


### The bottom line


Proactive communication is a powerful tool that can revolutionize healthcare patient engagement strategies.
