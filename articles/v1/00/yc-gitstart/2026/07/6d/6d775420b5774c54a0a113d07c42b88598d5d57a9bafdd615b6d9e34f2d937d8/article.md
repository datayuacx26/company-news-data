# Sitting down with Maria Zhang, GitStart Board Member, at Plato Elevate 2024

In June, we attended Plato Elevate in San Francisco, with over 1,000 engineering leaders in the audience. Maria led a keynote about engineering budgets, which she has extensive knowledge about given her background at Google, Meta, LinkedIn, and Tinder. Now, she’s the co-founder and CEO of[Proactive AI Lab](https://www.proactiveailab.com/) . We discussed her thoughts and experiences as an engineering leader, including finding success, managing tech debt, and much more.
