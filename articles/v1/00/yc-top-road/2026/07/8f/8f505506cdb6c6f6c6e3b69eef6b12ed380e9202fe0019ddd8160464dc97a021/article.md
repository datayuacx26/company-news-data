# Introducing Collaborator Mode: Earn Additional Income by Building GPTs for Your Clients

Until now, we’ve focused on helping creators monetise GPTs they’ve built for themselves.


Today, we’re excited to launch **Collaborator Mode** built for GPT (super)builders who are creating and managing custom GPTs for clients.


If you’re building GPTs for businesses, consultants, agencies, or brands, this feature lets you collaborate transparently while maintaining full control.


When building for clients, you can now:


- Build and manage the GPT using your own ChatGPT account
- Use your Top Road account to install GPT Tools, set and adjust pricing and payment settings
- Add your client as a collaborator on Top Road, so they have **view-only access** to GPT usage, analytics, and revenue data creating a transparent, shared source of truth.


‍


#### Adding Clients as a Collaborator


To add a collaborator:


1. Click **View Details** on the relevant GPT
2. Go to the **Revenue** tab
3. Scroll to the **Collaborator** section and add your client’s email


Add your client as a collaborator so they get view-only access to GPT usage and revenue data


#### What Collaborators Can (and Can’t) Do


Once added, your client will see the GPT in their Top Road dashboard (marked with a circle to indicate it’s shared).


The orange ring around a logo indicates the GPT been shared with you as a collaborator


Collaborator Mode gives them **view-only access** to usage, analytics, and revenue data — creating a transparent, shared source of truth.


Collaborators **cannot** :


- Install GPT Tools
- Change pricing or payment settings
- Withdraw funds (as all revenue is still paid directly to you as the GPT builder; you handle payments or revenue share directly with your client)


In short: your client gets visibility. You keep control.


‍


#### Collaborator Mode vs Whitelisting


Collaborator Mode is our second feature for GPT builders who create GPTs for clients. The first was whitelisting. You can use either — or both — depending on your business model.


‍


**Whitelisting Only**


Whitelisted users won’t be asked to pay to access the GPT.


This is typically used when the GPT is for internal client use.


Example: You’re a marketing consultant offering a “free” copywriting GPT as part of your service package.


‍


**Collaborator Only**


Collaborators get view-only access to track GPT performance.


This is typically used when the GPT is external and revenue-generating, where you act as the technical builder and your client drives distribution.


Example: You build a travel guide GPT for a travel agency. They promote it to their customers, and you both use Top Road’s usage and revenue analytics as a verifiable performance dashboard.


‍


**Whitelisting + Collaborator**


Or you can use both features in conjunction! Perhaps the client wants to use the GPT for both internal and external use. Or it’s purely for internal use, and the client wants to monitor usage within their organisation.


‍


#### Who Can Use It?


Collaborator Mode is included in **all Top Road GPT Tools plans** — Free, Pro, and Business — at no additional cost.


If you’re already building GPTs for clients, start using it today!
