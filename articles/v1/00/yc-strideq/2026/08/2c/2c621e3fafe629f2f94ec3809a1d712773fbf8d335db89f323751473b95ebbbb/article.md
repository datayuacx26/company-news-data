## What are Restaurant Labor Costs?

Restaurant labor costs are a significant expense – up to 30% of total revenue. They include wages, benefits, recruiting, training, and managing employees. The[Bureau of Labor Statistics: Restaurant Industry Employment Trends](https://www.bls.gov/industry-employment) shows the restaurant industry has some of the highest labor costs.


## How Can Automated Phone Answering Reduce Labor Costs?


Automated phone answering systems can reduce labor costs by automating tasks like answering calls, taking orders, and managing reservations. This frees up staff to focus on other tasks – like customer service, food prep, and management. You’ll see significant labor cost savings, increased productivity, and efficiency.


## What are the Benefits of Automated Phone Answering for Restaurants?


The benefits are numerous. Some of the most significant include:


- Reduced labor costs: automating phone calls means fewer staff are needed, leading to labor cost savings.
- Increased productivity: automation frees up staff for other tasks.
- Improved customer service: automated systems provide quick and easy access to info.
- Enhanced customer experience: automated systems offer personalized and interactive service.
