**1. How does an AI phone answering service differ from a traditional IVR?** Traditional IVR (Interactive Voice Response) relies on “press 1 for sales” menus. An AI service uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to understand spoken words, allowing customers to speak naturally and receive intelligent responses.


**2. Can the AI handle appointment scheduling?** Yes. Advanced services like Strideq can integrate with your digital calendar to book, reschedule, or cancel appointments in real-time without human intervention.


**3. Is it difficult to set up an AI answering service?** Not at all. Most platforms are designed for quick deployment, allowing you to upload your business FAQs and scripts so the AI can learn your specific requirements within minutes.


**4. Will my customers know they are talking to an AI?** While the technology is incredibly lifelike, transparency is often best. However, the efficiency and lack of hold times usually result in higher customer satisfaction regardless of the caller knowing it’s an AI.
