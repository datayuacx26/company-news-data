## What Is Smith AI Answering Service?


**Smith.ai** is a cloud-based virtual receptionist platform that uses **AI-powered voice technology and live agents** to manage incoming calls, website chats, and customer inquiries.


The service is designed to help businesses respond to customers faster while improving lead capture and communication efficiency.


Smith.ai can handle tasks such as:


- Answering business phone calls
- Scheduling appointments
- Screening and qualifying leads
- Routing calls to the right department
- Responding to customer questions


By combining automation with human support, Smith.ai helps businesses ensure that no important calls or inquiries are missed.


---


## How Smith AI Answering Service Works


Smith.ai uses advanced **natural language processing and voice AI technology** to understand customer requests and respond naturally during phone conversations.


The process typically works like this:


1. A customer calls your business number
2. The AI receptionist answers the call instantly
3. The system understands the caller’s request
4. The AI provides answers, schedules appointments, or routes the call


If a call requires human support, the system can transfer it to a live receptionist.


This hybrid approach ensures both automation and personalized communication.
