# CRACKER BARREL FISCAL 2026 SECOND QUARTER CONFERENCE CALL

LEBANON, Tenn.


, Feb. 18, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Cracker Barrel Old Country Store, Inc. ("Cracker Barrel" or the "Company") (Nasdaq: CBRL) plans to release its fiscal 2026 second quarter financial results after the market closes on Wednesday, March 4, 2026 with a conference call to follow at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Company management will discuss financial results for the fiscal second quarter ended January 30, 2026.


The live broadcast of Cracker Barrel's quarterly conference call will be available to the public online in the Events and Presentations section on the Company's website at[investor.crackerbarrel.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4621733-1&h=3475237750&u=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.crackerbarrel.com%2F&a=investor.crackerbarrel.com) . An online replay will also be available.


Cracker Barrel Old Country Store, Inc. (Nasdaq: CBRL) was established in 1969 in Lebanon, Tenn. and operates approximately 660 Cracker Barrel locations and owns the fast-casual Maple Street Biscuit Company.
