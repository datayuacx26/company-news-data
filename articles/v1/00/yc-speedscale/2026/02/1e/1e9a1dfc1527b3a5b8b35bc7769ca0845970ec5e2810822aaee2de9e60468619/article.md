## Why Traffic Replay is the Future of Testing


The traditional testing methods of creating test scripts and simulating test data are often unrealistic, considering that “chaos” is an essential part of production. **Speedscale** ’s **Traffic Replay** technology brings “production reality” into your development environment without security risks.


### How It Works: The Speedscale Technology


Speedscale works by capturing, sanitizing, and replaying real-world traffic to guarantee your services are prepared to handle the real-world patterns your services will actually experience in production.


- **Capture:** Speedscale employs lightweight sidecars or agents running inside your Kubernetes clusters to record real-world API calls.
- **Sanitize:** Speedscale automatically removes PII and other sensitive information to guarantee your test data is compliant.
- **Simulate:** Speedscale is a “smart mock” that simulates downstream dependencies to permit you to test your service in isolation.
- **Validate:** Speedscale validates your results against the original production “gold standard.”


### Modern Testing for the AI Era (MCP)


With the advent of the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** , AI agents are calling your APIs autonomously. Speedscale gives you the context you require to permit these agents to function correctly. With Speedscale, you can replay these conversations to guarantee your AI-authored code still complies with its API contracts before it reaches a customer.
