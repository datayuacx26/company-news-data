# Introducing a More Connected, Flexible Path to MongoDB Certifications

Learning something new feels better when you can see your progress.


That idea is at the heart of the latest updates to[MongoDB Certifications](https://learn.mongodb.com/pages/certification-program) . If you’re pursuing the Associate Data Modeler, Database Administrator, or Atlas Administrator certification, your journey is now more connected, more flexible, and more motivating. These[certification learning paths](https://learn.mongodb.com/pages/certification-learning-paths) are now aligned with[Skill Badges](https://learn.mongodb.com/skills) , so you can build practical skills step by step, earn recognition along the way, and move toward certification with more momentum.


And there’s one more benefit: when you complete the full certification learning path, you receive 50% off your certification exam.


## What's changing?


For three certification tracks, including[Associate Data Modeler](https://learn.mongodb.com/pages/mongodb-associate-data-modeler-exam) ,[Database Administrator](https://learn.mongodb.com/pages/mongodb-associate-database-administrator-exam) , and[Atlas Administrator](https://learn.mongodb.com/pages/mongodb-associate-atlas-administrator-exam) , MongoDB is bringing learning and credentialing closer together.


In these updated journeys, you’ll move through a certification learning path and earn Skill Badges as you progress. Instead of waiting until the very end for recognition, you can now celebrate meaningful milestones throughout the experience.


After all, certification can be a big goal, and Skill Badges help make the journey feel more achievable.


By aligning these learning paths with Skill Badges, MongoDB gives you a clearer sense of progress from the moment you begin. You can build knowledge in smaller steps, validate what you’ve learned along the way, and stay motivated as you work toward a larger credential.


This approach also makes the learning experience feel more practical and more personal. You’re not just preparing for an exam. You’re building real MongoDB skills, earning badges that recognize those skills, and moving toward a certification that validates your broader expertise.


## How the journey works


The updated path is simple:


-


Enroll in a[certification learning path](https://learn.mongodb.com/pages/certification-learning-paths)


-


Progress through the training and earn Skill Badges along the way


-


Complete the full learning path


-


Unlock 50% off the certification exam


-


Schedule the exam and take the next step in your MongoDB journey


It’s a more visible, milestone-driven experience that helps you stay engaged from start to finish.


## What you can expect in each track


Each certification path is designed around the skills that matter most for that role.


For the Data Modeler path, you’ll earn badges focused on topics such as data modeling, schema patterns, and schema optimization.


The Database Administrator path includes badges on querying, indexing, monitoring, performance tuning, security, and sharding.


On the Atlas Administrator path, you’ll earn badges focused on database administration as well as security, networking, and data resiliency specific to the Atlas environments.


## Why certification matters


Skill Badges help you build momentum, but certification remains the big milestone.


Skill Badges recognize specific, focused MongoDB skills as you learn. Certification goes further by validating your broader knowledge and ability to apply those MongoDB skills to a defined job role.


Earning a MongoDB certification can help you stand out and strengthen your credibility, and increase your professional visibility. Both Skill Badges and certifications can be shared through Credly.


In short: Skill badges showcase the skills you've built along the way. Certification brings those skills together to validate your expertise.
