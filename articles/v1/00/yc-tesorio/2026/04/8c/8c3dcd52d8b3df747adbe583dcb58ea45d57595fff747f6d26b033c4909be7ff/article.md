## **What Is DSO Reduction Software and How Does It Work?**


DSO reduction software automates the accounts receivable collections process. It[replaces manual outreach, spreadsheet tracking, and binary ERP dunning with AI-driven workflows](https://www.tesorio.com/blog/accounts-receivable-payable-automation-in-2025-how-to-cut-dso-by-33-days-and-triple-productivity) that determine which accounts to contact, when, and with what message, based on payment history, invoice age, and customer relationship data.


The distinction from native ERP functionality matters. ERP AR modules were built to record transactions. They generate aging reports and apply the same reminder schedule to all overdue invoices, regardless of the customer's context. DSO reduction software is built to act on that data intelligently. It segments customers by ARR tier and risk profile, routes dispute resolution to the right owner, applies cash automatically, and updates cash flow forecasts as invoices are paid or aged, all in real time.


The best platforms connect directly to ERPs like NetSuite, Workday, and Intacct without custom development, and they give CFOs a single view of AR health and forecasted inflows without requiring a separate treasury tool.


## **Why Is My DSO High Even When I Send Payment Reminders?**


Before comparing platforms, it is worth understanding what is actually driving the number. The right tool depends entirely on the right diagnosis.


[The 2025 AR Benchmark Report](https://www.tesorio.com/reports/dso-benchmark) points to five root causes that account for most persistently high DSO, and they are not the ones most teams focus on.


****


**Collectors work from aging reports, not risk intelligence.** Aging buckets treat every 45-day invoice identically. AI-driven prioritization distinguishes between a customer who always pays at 50 days and one showing early deterioration, before either invoice goes overdue.


****


**ERP dunning is binary and cannot be segmented by relationship.** Native reminders are either on or off. A $500K ARR account and a $2K transactional invoice receive the same notification. Dedicated AR platforms automatically apply different cadences, tones, and escalation paths by segment.


****


**Billing errors cause more late payments than customer cash flow issues.** Billing errors contribute to roughly six in ten late payments. Sending more reminders does not fix an invoice error. Tracking dispute patterns and flagging recurring error types does.


****


**Cash application backlogs distort AR visibility and slow close.** Manual payment matching delays accurate reporting of what is actually owed. AI cash application with high auto-match rates keeps AR balances current, so the team is always working from real data.


****


**Disputes age silently because no one owns a resolution.** When a dispute sits in an email thread with no system of record, it can go weeks without movement while the invoice ages past the recovery threshold. Centralized dispute routing with assigned ownership and deadlines prevents this at scale.


Knowing which of these is your primary driver determines which platform will move your number the most.

## **How Do You Reduce DSO with AI-Powered Collections Software?**


Choosing the right platform is step one. Realizing the DSO reduction depends on three workflows running in concert.


**The first is prioritization.** Two invoices, both 45 days old, can have completely different collection probabilities. One comes from a customer who consistently pays at 50 days. The other comes from an account showing early signs of payment deterioration. AI identifies which needs immediate intervention and which will resolve without it, before a collector picks up the phone. Without this layer, teams work from gut instinct and aging reports. With it, every action is targeted.


**The second is intelligent sequencing.** Effective collection email sequences do not send the same reminder to every overdue account. They adapt by customer tier, invoice age, relationship history, and payment behavior.[Discovery Education](https://www.tesorio.com/customers/discovery-education) runs nearly 900 segmented dunning messages per day through Tesorio, with different cadences for K-12 districts, enterprise accounts, and transactional customers. Their overall DSO dropped from 128 days to 43 within roughly a year. That result is not possible with ERP dunning. It requires sequencing logic that reflects the customer relationship, not just the invoice date.


****


**The third is dispute management.** Billing errors drive roughly six in ten late payments. A collection workflow that sends more reminders to an account with an unresolved billing dispute is not solving the problem. Platforms that centralize dispute status, route resolution to a clear owner with a deadline, and surface recurring error patterns across accounts address the actual cause of aging, not just the symptom.


When these three workflows operate together, the results compound.

## **How Do I Choose the Right Collections Automation Platform?**


The right platform depends on three variables: the number of active customer accounts you manage, where your DSO problem originates, and how much implementation time and internal IT capacity you can commit to.


**Under 5,000 active customer accounts:** Prioritize ERP integration speed and ease of use. Automated email sequences with accurate sync are the starting point. Avoid over-buying on features your team will not use within the first year.


**5,000 to 50,000 accounts:** AI prioritization and customer segmentation become essential at this volume. Without them, collectors make outreach decisions based on instinct rather than payment intelligence. Ensure any platform can segment by customer tier automatically, not just by invoice age.


****


**Above 50,000 accounts or enterprise ARR:** Dispute tracking, escalation workflows, cash forecasting, and a centralized communication history are non-negotiable. Lightweight tools cannot handle the exception volume that comes with this scale.


Beyond account volume, bring your three hardest real-world scenarios into every demo: a disputed invoice with a partial payment and mismatched remittance, a short-pay from a high-value customer, and an account with erratic multi-year payment history. How the platform handles those edge cases predicts your daily experience far more accurately than a scripted walkthrough.
