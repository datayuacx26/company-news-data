# Honored at ISE Innovation Awards 2025

‍


We are thrilled to announce that gaiia has been awarded Bronze at the ISE Innovation Awards 2025. This recognition is a testament to our commitment to revolutionizing the broadband industry with cutting-edge solutions that empower service providers to deliver seamless, customer-centric experiences.


‍


### Driving innovation in broadband


The ISE Innovation Awards recognize groundbreaking solutions that push the boundaries of the industry, and we’re proud to be part of this prestigious group. The Bronze award places us alongside some of the most innovative companies in the field, further validating the impact gaiia is making in broadband technology.


As a modern BSS/OSS platform, gaiia streamlines the subscriber experience by connecting service providers’ checkouts directly with their workforce. Think of it as offering an Uber-like experience for broadband services, where customers can easily manage their accounts, while providers can optimize operations through automation and real-time integrations.


‍


### Solving key industry challenges


Our platform helps service providers overcome some of the toughest challenges they face today, such as outdated legacy systems, complex integrations, and operational inefficiencies. gaiia is particularly designed to replace in-house stacks that are often stitched together, offering flexibility and scalability through a cloud-hosted solution with an open API.


‍


### Preparing for the future of broadband


The broadband industry is evolving rapidly, with consumer demands for faster speeds and better customer service on the rise. gaiia is positioned to lead this shift, helping service providers stay competitive and scale efficiently. As new technologies like AI continue to shape the industry, we’re ensuring our platform is prepared for the next wave of innovation.


At gaiia, we’re not just building a product; we’re creating a platform that empowers broadband providers to thrive. We have a team of over 50 engineers working tirelessly to improve our product, integrate new solutions, and ensure our customers can offer world-class service to their subscribers.


‍


### Thank you!


Thank you to the ISE Innovation Awards 2025 for recognizing our work, and to our clients and partners for their continued trust in gaiia. The future of broadband is bright, and we’re proud to be leading the way.
