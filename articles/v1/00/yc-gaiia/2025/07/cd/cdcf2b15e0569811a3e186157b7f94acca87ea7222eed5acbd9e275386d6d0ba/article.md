# Joins NCTC as a General Partner

‍


### gaiia joins NCTC as a general partner to support broadband innovation and growth


We are excited to announce that gaiia has joined the[National Cable Television Cooperative (NCTC)](https://www.nctconline.org/) as a General Partner. This is an important step in our mission to support broadband providers by delivering innovative, efficient, and customer-centric solutions.


‍


### What this means for gaiia


As a General Partner of the NCTC, gaiia will have the opportunity to engage with a network of over 750 broadband and video service providers across the U.S. By joining forces with the NCTC, we’re strengthening our commitment to providing small and medium-sized broadband providers with tools that enable them to compete with larger players in the market.


Our partnership with NCTC further aligns with gaiia’s mission to support broadband providers in delivering modern, flexible, and scalable BSS/OSS solutions that streamline operations and improve customer satisfaction. We believe that by working together with the NCTC, we can help broadband service providers enhance their operational efficiency, reduce costs, and drive long-term growth.


‍


### Why NCTC?


NCTC represents a vital ecosystem of broadband service providers who are focused on delivering high-quality, affordable broadband and video services to their communities. By becoming a General Partner, gaiia gains access to a wealth of industry insights and networking opportunities. More importantly, this partnership enables us to collaborate closely with NCTC’s members to understand their needs and challenges, ensuring we continue developing solutions that truly address the unique challenges of the broadband industry.


‍


### What’s next?


We’re excited to be a part of the NCTC community and will be attending The Independent Show conference in Salt Lake City, August 10th–13th, 2025. It’s a great opportunity for us to meet with broadband providers, discuss their challenges, and explore how gaiia can help them achieve their goals. We look forward to connecting with industry leaders and continuing to innovate alongside NCTC’s members.
