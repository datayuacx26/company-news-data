# Introducing User Presence Indicators

We’re excited to introduce a feature that’s been one of the most requested by our customer support teams: **User Presence Indicators.** This real-time feature helps teams stay aligned by showing who’s currently viewing or working on the same page.


‍


‍


## How it works


When someone opens a page, their avatar will appear in real-time for other users to see. Here's what to expect:


- Appears the moment a user opens the page
- Disappears instantly when they leave (e.g. closes the ticket, account, or browser tab)
- Times out after 60 minutes of inactivity (e.g. the user has the tab open but has not interacted with the page for 60 mins)
- Hover over the indicator to see who’s there.


And yes, the feature scales! You'll see avatars for up to three users. After that, we’ll show two avatars plus a count, so your UI stays clean without losing insight into who else is collaborating.


‍


## Where you’ll see it


As of today, avatars are visible on Tickets and Accounts. More pages are coming soon as we continue expanding this feature.
