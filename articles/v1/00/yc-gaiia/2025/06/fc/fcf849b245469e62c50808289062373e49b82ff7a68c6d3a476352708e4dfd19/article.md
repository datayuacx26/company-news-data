# Finalist: Technology Partner of the Year 2025

‍


We are excited to share that gaiia has been named a finalist for the **Technology Partner of the Year** award at the[2025 Broadband Communities Awards](https://bbcmag.com/shortlist-announced-for-2025-broadband-communities-awards/?utm_source=sfmc&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=TT_Broadband+Communities+Newsletter_6.10.2025&utm_id=266515&sfmc_id=38931216&cid=003N200000IAqz8IAD) ! This recognition comes as a testament to our innovative work in the broadband industry, particularly for the launch of our[Properties module](https://www.gaiia.com/products/properties) .


Our cloud-hosted OSS/BSS platform, including the new Properties module tailored for managing multi-dwelling unit (MDU) billing, has been a game-changer for ISPs. By streamlining MDU management with features like hybrid billing, automated provisioning, and seamless integration, we are helping providers tackle complex challenges and enhance operational efficiency. This recognition underscores the value our platform brings to ISPs looking to modernize their operations and improve the customer experience.


We are incredibly grateful to our customers, partners, and team for making this achievement possible. This is just the beginning, and we look forward to continuing our work to empower ISPs with innovative solutions that drive success.


For more information on the 2025 Broadband Communities Awards, check out the full shortlist announcement[here](https://bbcmag.com/shortlist-announced-for-2025-broadband-communities-awards/?utm_source=sfmc&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=TT_Broadband+Communities+Newsletter_6.10.2025&utm_id=266515&sfmc_id=38931216&cid=003N200000IAqz8IAD) .
