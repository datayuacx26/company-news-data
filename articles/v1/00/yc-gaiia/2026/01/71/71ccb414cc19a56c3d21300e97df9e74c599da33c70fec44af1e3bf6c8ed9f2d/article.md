# New Integration: SiFi Networks

‍


Operating on open access networks creates enormous opportunities for CSPs, but only when operations can keep pace. Today, we’re excited to share that gaiia now supports a deep integration with[Sifi Networks](https://sifinetworks.com/corporate/) , enabling faster activations, better customer experiences, and more efficient day-to-day operations for providers operating on Sifi’s open access infrastructure.


This integration was built in partnership with[Anthem Connect](https://anthemconnectus.com/) , a retail ISP delivering service on Sifi Networks across multiple markets.


‍


### About Sifi Networks


Sifi Networks is a leading open access network operator focused on building and operating full-fiber infrastructure in communities across the US. Their model enables multiple CSPs to deliver services over a shared, carrier-grade network, helping accelerate fiber deployment while preserving consumer choice.


For CSPs, Sifi’s open access approach removes the burden of network ownership and allows teams to focus on customer experience, marketing, and growth.


‍


### Why We Built the Integration


Anthem Connect operates on Sifi Networks and needed an OSS/BSS platform that could handle the operational realities of open access at scale. That meant more than basic provisioning support. It required tight, automated workflows that connect customer onboarding, network interactions, and field scheduling into a single operational flow.


Rather than relying on manual processes or fragmented tools, gaiia worked closely with Anthem Connect to build an integration that supports the full service lifecycle on Sifi’s network.


‍


### What the Integration Does


The gaiia–Sifi Networks integration automates the majority of the open access service lifecycle, including:


- Address qualification and service eligibility at checkout
- Order placement and activation workflows with Sifi’s network systems
- Clear order status visibility for internal teams and customer support


By connecting these steps end to end, CSPs can reduce manual work, and provide a more transparent experience for customers from signup through activation.


‍


### Built for CSPs Operating on Open Access Networks


This integration reflects a broader design principle at gaiia: open access operations demand modern, flexible OSS/BSS platforms.


CSPs operating on OANs need systems that can support multiple networks, dynamic service eligibility, wholesale billing models, and event-driven provisioning. They also need the freedom to differentiate on customer experience, not be constrained by rigid systems.


gaiia was built by operators who scaled on open access networks and experienced these challenges firsthand. Our integrations with operators like[Sifi Networks](https://sifinetworks.com/) and platforms like[UTOPIA Fiber](https://www.gaiia.com/newsroom/gaiia-launches-a-utopia-fiber-integration) ensure that CSPs can move fast, operate efficiently, and deliver the customer experience that sets them apart.
