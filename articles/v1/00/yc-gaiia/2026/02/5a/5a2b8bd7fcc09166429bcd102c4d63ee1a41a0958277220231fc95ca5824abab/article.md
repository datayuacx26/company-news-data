# LymeFiber Launches for Community Broadband in NH

‍


We’re excited to welcome[LymeFiber](https://www.lymefiber.net/) to the gaiia family. LymeFiber, a community ISP serving every reachable home and business in **Lyme, NH** with symmetrical fiber internet and optional phone service, is now live on gaiia’s modern OSS/BSS platform.


‍


### A modern platform for local connectivity


Built to serve the town of Lyme and surrounding areas, LymeFiber selected gaiia to deliver a modern, connected experience for subscribers. The platform now powers checkout, billing, CRM, and inventory management, with an integrated customer portal and address-based service qualification.


Subscribers can easily check availability, schedule installations, and manage payments online through a branded checkout built specifically for LymeFiber. Payments are securely handled through IPPay, while back-office workflows are unified for efficient service delivery.


‍


‍


### Seamless integration and network automation


gaiia’s implementation for LymeFiber included automations with Calix SMx and Calix Service Cloud for provisioning, as well as integration with Vetro for address verification. These connections give LymeFiber’s team a complete view of their network and streamline the activation of new subscribers.


The migration process covered everything from customer accounts and billing data to inventory, tickets, and workforce entities, completed through a structured and validated cutover.


> “Connecting Calix and Vetro in a single platform was important for us. gaiia’s integrations and workflows have given our team the visibility to operate efficiently and deliver a better customer experience.” — Adam Gaston, CIO, Mac Mountain LightCraft


‍


### Laying the groundwork for future growth


LymeFiber’s launch reflects the growing collaboration between private broadband investment and modern OSS/BSS technology. gaiia’s flexible architecture allows each[Mac Mountain](https://www.macmtn.com/) portfolio operator to run in its own fully branded environment while benefiting from a shared implementation framework that accelerates go-lives and reduces operational complexity.


‍


### About Mac Mountain


[Mac Mountain](https://www.macmtn.com/) builds, operates, and invests in fiber broadband networks across the U.S., partnering with ISPs, municipalities, rural electric co-ops, developers, homebuilders, and regional providers to expand high-performance connectivity.[LightCraft](https://www.macmtn.com/lightcraft) , Mac Mountain’s operational arm, delivers a shared implementation framework that standardizes design, build, and operations across portfolio networks. This accelerates launches and reduces operational complexity, allowing partners to focus on growth.
