# Solar Maverick Podcast: Jing Tian on Smarter Solar, MLPE, AI, and Energy Intelligence

Tigo Energy’s Chief Growth & Revenue Officer Jing Tian recently joined[Benoy Thanjan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bthanjan/) on the[Solar Maverick Podcast](https://solarmaverick.podbean.com/) to discuss how the solar industry is evolving into a future defined by smarter solutions, software-driven performance insights, and[Energy Intelligence](https://www.tigoenergy.com/monitoring) .


In the episode, Jing shares her journey from earning a PhD in chemistry to becoming a global solar executive, with leadership roles across both Asia and the U.S. She also offers a clear perspective on why the next era of solar growth will depend on technologies that make systems safer, more adaptable, and easier to manage at scale.


‍


##### Why module-level tools matter in the field


Jing explains how[module-level power electronics (MLPE)](https://www.tigoenergy.com/ts4-x) have become increasingly important across residential, C&I, and utility-scale installations - helping improve system flexibility, safety, and overall performance.


‍


##### Small improvements can create massive impact at scale


**‍** A recurring theme is how seemingly modest performance gains at the module-level can translate into significant financial and operational value when deployed across multiple sites.


‍ **‍**


##### AI-powered monitoring is reshaping O&M


Jing also discusses how the industry is shifting toward predictive analytics. AI-driven insights detect performance issues early, reduce truck rolls, and make operations more efficient while improving reliability over the long term.


‍


##### Rapid shutdown and safety requirements remain critical


**‍** With safety standards continuing to evolve, the conversation touches on how rapid shutdown requirements are influencing system design and why smarter solutions are playing a growing role in meeting these needs without adding complexity.


‍


If you’re interested in where solar is headed, this is a great discussion with practical insights for today’s installers and industry leaders.
