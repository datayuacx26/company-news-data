# New Resource: Tigo EI App Homeowner User Guide

Tigo has released an updated[Homeowner User Guide for the Tigo EI App](https://www.tigoenergy.com/downloads/quick-start-guide-tigo-ei-app-homeowner-user-guide) , helping residential customers easily monitor and manage their solar and energy storage systems.


This new guide walks homeowners through every feature of the Tigo Energy Intelligence (EI) App, including:


- Real-time system visibility — See energy production, consumption, and battery usage in one view.
- Battery management modes — Choose between Self-Consumption, Backup, and Time-of-Use (Rate Saver) to match household priorities and utility rates.
- EV Charger integration — Manage the Tigo GO EV Charger directly from the app, including Green Charge and Boost Charge modes. (available in EU)
- Alerts and monitoring — Receive system updates via email or text, and view device-level performance, warnings, and status history.


Installers can share this resource to help homeowners get the most from their Tigo EI Residential System, reducing post-installation questions and improving satisfaction.
