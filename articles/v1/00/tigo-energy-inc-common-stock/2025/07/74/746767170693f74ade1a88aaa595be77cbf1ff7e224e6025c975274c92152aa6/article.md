# Tigo Energy featured on the Energy Channel in Brazil

As Brazil’s solar market expands rapidly, the demand for safer, more efficient, and retrofit-friendly solar solutions is growing just as fast. At Tigo Energy, we’re proud to support this momentum with technology designed to maximize energy yield, reduce operating costs, and make solar more accessible across the region.


A recent feature from[Energy Channel](https://www.energychannel-us.co/post/tigo-energy-bets-on-safety-efficiency-and-retrofits-to-boost-solar-productivity-in-brazil) highlights how Tigo is contributing to this growth—by delivering **module-level optimization and monitoring** , **enhanced safety through rapid shutdown** , and **retrofit solutions** that help breathe new life into older systems.


Whether it’s a new installation or an upgrade to improve system performance, Tigo’s open-architecture approach empowers installers in Brazil to:


- Increase energy production with optimization
- Ensure compliance with rapid shutdown features
- Minimize O&M with module-level monitoring


As the article notes, Brazil is one of the most promising solar markets in the world—and Tigo is committed to making sure solar professionals across the country are equipped for long-term success.
