# Resource: Application Note for EI Inverter (US) IPOC

As solar professionals take on more projects involving repowering and inverter replacements, understanding how to derate an inverter’s maximum output power becomes increasingly important — especially in areas with strict interconnection limits.


Tigo’s latest application note dives into **Inverter Power Output Control (IPOC)** , a built-in feature for the[EI Inverter (US)](https://www.tigoenergy.com/product/ei-inverter) , which allows installers to set system-level output power caps in a simple, reliable way. Whether you’re replacing legacy inverters or installing new systems, IPOC gives you the flexibility to meet site-specific requirements without external components.

- Step-by-step setup of IPOC in the[Tigo Energy Intelligence (EI)](https://www.tigoenergy.com/monitoring) app
