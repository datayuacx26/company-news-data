# Tigo Energy Featured in Solar Builder Coverage on Repowering

There are 1 million residential solar installations in the US that are 10+ years old. Over the next 5 years, 400,000 homes per year will hit the 10 year old mark. As a result, repowering aged solar installations is becoming an increasingly large and critical component of the residential solar ecosystem. Two recent[Solar Builder](https://solarbuildermag.com/) articles spotlight this trend and prominently feature Tigo Energy technologies:


‍


1. [“Selecting Solar Inverters for Repowering Projects”](https://solarbuildermag.com/news/selecting-solar-inverters-for-repowering-projects/) quotes JD Dillon, Tigo's Chief Marketing and Customer Experience Officer, highlighting repowering best practices and the need for reliable equipment and service to be successful in this growing market
2. [“9 Solar Inverter and ESS Upgrades to Know This Summer”](https://solarbuildermag.com/inverters/9-solar-inverter-and-ess-upgrades-to-know-this-summer/) references Tigo’s Inverter Power Output Control (IPOC) feature as a software-driven solution for setting the maximum AC output during commissioning
