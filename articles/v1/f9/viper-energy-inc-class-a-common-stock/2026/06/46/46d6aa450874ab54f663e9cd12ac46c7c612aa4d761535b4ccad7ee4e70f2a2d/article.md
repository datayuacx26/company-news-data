# Viper Energy, Inc., a Subsidiary of Diamondback Energy, Inc., Schedules Second Quarter 2026 Conference Call for August 4, 2026

## Viper Energy, Inc., a Subsidiary of Diamondback Energy, Inc., Schedules Second Quarter 2026 Conference Call for August 4, 2026


June 30, 2026


[PDF Version](https://www.viperenergy.com/node/11366/pdf)


MIDLAND, Texas, June 30, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Viper Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ: VNOM) (“Viper”), a subsidiary of Diamondback Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ: FANG) (“Diamondback”), today announced that it plans to release second quarter 2026 financial results on August 3, 2026 after the market closes.


In connection with the earnings release, Viper will host a conference call and webcast for investors and analysts to discuss its results for the second quarter of 2026 on Tuesday, August 4, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. CT. Access to the live webcast, and replay which will be available following the call, may be found[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=mhuKBIKxpyIowwPv2zGaRt7EmtHvOQJLSaxJYGWckfGp0TkgdvJTDDNP1_LtcEyn_r-9kr5wVTLJZj4cHgA0OFgJ91bP61LgZ75wP1nzDSc=) . The live webcast of the earnings conference call will also be available via Viper’s website at[www.viperenergy.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=EQETdYtc4K8q3rbGKlsNWVt3mM0IJ0is2ZnzdYRz5-d92GlIrMWRGqzFr9QtwPN0B02BlqJN1qpEpjzpu2w7z9-ACLTPUH0QHzVffntSrV8=) under the “Investor Relations” section of the site.


About Viper Energy, Inc.


Viper is a corporation formed by Diamondback to own, acquire and exploit oil and natural gas properties in North America, with a focus on owning and acquiring mineral and royalty interests in oil-weighted basins, primarily the Permian Basin. For more information, please visit[www.viperenergy.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=EQETdYtc4K8q3rbGKlsNWVL86pFS6fLWOdRP4CCRXB-MibAobWwNjq2aTFtwFzd2aMLbNTju_2Xm8WVYMAhdOe7ppTZrjKsY6FJhaXXab5Y=) .
