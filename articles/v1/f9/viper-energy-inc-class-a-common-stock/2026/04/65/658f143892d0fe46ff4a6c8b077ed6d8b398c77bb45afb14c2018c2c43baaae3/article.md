# Viper Energy, Inc., a Subsidiary of Diamondback Energy, Inc., Schedules First Quarter 2026 Conference Call for May 5, 2026

## Viper Energy, Inc., a Subsidiary of Diamondback Energy, Inc., Schedules First Quarter 2026 Conference Call for May 5, 2026


April 1, 2026


[PDF Version](https://www.viperenergy.com/node/11226/pdf)


MIDLAND, Texas, April 01, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Viper Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ: VNOM) (“Viper”), a subsidiary of Diamondback Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ: FANG) (“Diamondback”), today announced that it plans to release first quarter 2026 financial results on May 4, 2026 after the market closes.


In connection with the earnings release, Viper will host a conference call and webcast for investors and analysts to discuss its results for the first quarter of 2026 on Tuesday, May 5, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. CT. Access to the live webcast, and replay which will be available following the call, may be found[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=vEzmnBrBH77Gk50YaI-rPr_AiI4-H7xxinjR9x41t2-XD21WiYyYXCIHl5C07VLwiycD-W_VWwhELjA0huaGsV7CJ7Lj-Xniw5hBJIh6eyo=) . The live webcast of the earnings conference call will also be available via Viper’s website at[www.viperenergy.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=euGQaBUQzrecXkm1FnlPzPTTTd2FQAKnXCSdENpS2UTjQqpH-3G7xQOrKeEvJ_Cr-YTn1kP4ce6545RdiKCNb5MC-5owNklEeApx6o5VheI=) under the “Investor Relations” section of the site.


About Viper Energy, Inc.


Viper is a corporation formed by Diamondback to own, acquire and exploit oil and natural gas properties in North America, with a focus on owning and acquiring mineral and royalty interests in oil-weighted basins, primarily the Permian Basin. For more information, please visit[www.viperenergy.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=euGQaBUQzrecXkm1FnlPzFfAh43KWXr0oGZqKGMhr6jI4VFpSheM1uOqOcI00yhMpkXixETJ785zz0jxNIUJ8k23jP5QIJGg1LJdKJ3lf6Y=) .
