# Best Cash Application Automation Software for B2B SaaS (2026)

**Cash application automation matches incoming customer payments and remittance data to open invoices, routes exceptions, and posts the result to the accounts receivable ledger.** It is the step that turns “money reached the bank” into “the correct customer invoices are marked paid.”


**Updated July 2026:** this guide separates enterprise order-to-cash suites, mid-market AR platforms, and specialized tools. It also replaces generic match-rate claims with the evidence buyers should request from each vendor on their own remittance and invoice data.

## What B2B SaaS teams need beyond matching


A payment mismatch in SaaS may be explained by a mid-cycle amendment, usage credit, prorated upgrade, tax change, consolidated payment, or invoice correction. Amount-only matching cannot resolve that context reliably.


1. Match Stripe, ACH, wire, check, and remittance data to the customer and invoice.
2. Use contract and billing context to explain differences.
3. Route short-pays, deductions, unidentified cash, and many-to-many payments with evidence.
4. Post the approved result to NetSuite, Sage Intacct, QuickBooks, or the connected ledger.
5. Suppress collections and dunning as soon as the payment is confirmed.
6. Keep unapplied cash and exceptions visible until they are resolved.

## How to run a credible cash-application pilot


1. Use a representative historical sample across ACH, wire, check, card, portal, and lockbox channels.
2. Include partial payments, consolidated payments, deductions, credits, missing remittance, and duplicate-risk cases.
3. Measure invoice-line STP, payment-level STP, false-positive rate, exception time, and posting accuracy separately.
4. Verify the ERP record after write-back, not only the match suggested in the vendor interface.
5. Confirm that paid invoices leave collections sequences immediately.


## Frequently asked questions


### What is cash application automation?


Cash application automation captures payments and remittance information, matches them to open customer invoices, routes unresolved exceptions, and posts approved results to the AR ledger.


### How does AI match payments to invoices?


AI combines remittance text, invoice numbers, customer identity, amounts, payment history, open balances, and learned exception patterns to propose matches, while policy controls determine which results can post automatically.


### What is the best cash application software for SaaS?


LedgerUp is a strong fit for mid-market B2B SaaS teams with Stripe, usage billing, and custom contracts; HighRadius, Billtrust, and Esker are stronger starting points for large enterprise AR operations.


### What is the difference between cash application and collections or dunning?


Cash application records which invoices a received payment settles, while collections and dunning try to obtain payment that has not arrived; accurate cash application should suppress further outreach immediately.


### What is a good straight-through processing rate?


A good STP rate is one measured on your own payment mix with a low false-positive rate and accurate ERP posting; a vendor’s headline percentage is not comparable until the sample and calculation method are the same.
