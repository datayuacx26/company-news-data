# Can I get into UX Design with No Experience?

**Social Media Blurb:** Have you been thinking about switching careers and into UX Design? You might be able to break into the field without experience.


If you are considering a career in UX design, you might be asking yourself if you can get a career without any experience. The answer is yes. There are many resources online that can help you learn the basics, or there are bootcamps like Avocademy.


###


### **What Is UX Design And How Can I Get Started With No Experience?**


Many postings for UX design jobs have requirements that might scare some people from applying, like years of experience, but that should not deter people from applying. There are so many resources online that help you learn the basics of UX design. Many people utilize resources such as YouTube videos, or certifications offered by companies such as Google.


### **What Are Some Effective Ways To Learn UX Design On My Own?**


There are many options for learning UX design skills on your own such as online sources like YouTube, or you could enroll in a UX design bootcamp. The path that you choose to take will vary depending on a few factors such as length of time, cost, and quality of program. You have to be realistic with yourself and decide how much time you want to spend learning, how much you can spend, and what you are looking for from a program or bootcamp.


### **Are There Any Entry-Level Jobs In UX Design That I Can Apply For?**


There are quite a few entry level jobs that UX designers can apply for such as Junior UX designer, UX researcher, interaction designer, visual designer, and product designer. Many companies weigh more emphasis on portfolios that potential employers bring, and not just on the number of years of experience in the field. That is why it is important to have a strong portfolio with real-world projects.


### **How Can Networking Help Me Break Into The UX Design Industry?**


Networking can be really useful when you are trying to land a job in tech. Networking helps with targeting the right people in the field, and making connections with them via places like LinkedIn. However, it is not the only way to connect with those in the field. Avocademy’s bootcamp offers mentorship both during the process of completing the bootcamp, and during the process of job searching.


Getting into UX design with no experience is not only possible but also a common occurrence. With the right mindset, a willingness to learn, and the right resources, anyone can transition into the UX design field. Whether it's taking online courses, attending workshops, or working on personal projects, there are many ways to gain experience and build a portfolio. With hard work, dedication, and a passion for design, anyone can succeed in UX design, regardless of their previous experience.
