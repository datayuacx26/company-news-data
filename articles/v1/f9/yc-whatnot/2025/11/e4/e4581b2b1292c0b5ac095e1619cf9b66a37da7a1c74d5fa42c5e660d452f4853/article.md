# Product Spotlight: Smarter Shipping Tools, Personalized Feed, and Captions for All

Here’s how it helps you:


-


**Keep buyers in the loop:** Confirming packages are shipped on time builds buyer confidence in you as a seller.


-


**Ensure your ship time is accurately counted:** Shipments confirmed with the in-app scanner are automatically counted towards your average ship time, just like a scan with the carrier would be.


-


**Avoid unnecessary support requests and cancellations:** Providing proof of dropoff helps you avoid unnecessary questions and cancellation requests from buyers


The shipment scanner is available to a subset of sellers on iOS and Android today. It will be available to all sellers in all markets where Whatnot operates later this month. Learn more in our[Help Center](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/articles/39184750709261-Proof-of-Drop-Off) .


**A More Personalized Home Feed That Helps Buyers Find You**


We’ve refreshed the Home Feed to make it easier for buyers to find shows and categories they care about most, and for you to get discovered by the right audience.


When someone opens the Whatnot app, they’ll now see a cleaner, more visual feed with personalized category tiles at the top that highlight the areas they browse and buy from most. These tiles connect buyers directly to the communities they love, from trading cards and collectibles to sneakers, fashion, and more.
