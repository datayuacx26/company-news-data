### **Holiday Selling Best Practices**


The final stretch of the year brings an influx of motivated buyers looking for gifts, deals, and sellers they can trust. Here’s how to make the most of holiday traffic using the tools already in your Seller Hub:


**1. Use Promote Tools to Boost Visibility**


The holidays bring record engagement across Whatnot. Promote Tools help your shows appear in front of new buyers in key discovery feeds while you’re live. The more visibility your stream has, the more likely new viewers are to join, follow, and purchase.


Use Promote Tools to spotlight your biggest moments:


-


Feature limited-time shows with guaranteed shipping before the holidays.


-


Schedule promotions around peak weekends such as Black Friday or Christmas Eve.


-


Test different durations and budgets to understand what drives the strongest ROI.


Promoting during high-traffic periods puts your show in front of gift seekers who may not already follow you, helping you grow your audience and turn new viewers into loyal buyers.


**2. Run Flash Sales to Increase Sell-Through**


Holiday shoppers respond to compelling deals. Flash Sales let you create timed discounts on listings in your Buy It Now store or during a show. These short windows motivate buyers to act quickly, whether they’re shopping for gifts or collectibles before the year ends.


You can:


-


Set your own discount and duration.


-


Apply sales to specific items or your entire store.


-


Combine a Flash Sale with a promoted show for even greater reach.


Timed discounts keep your audience engaged, drive higher conversions, and create energy around your shows during peak selling weekends.


**3. Optimize Listings for Discovery**


Detailed, accurate listings make it easier for buyers to find your items through search and category filters. The more complete your listing attributes, the more visibility your store gets across Whatnot’s discovery surfaces.


Add details such as:


-


Category, size, color, brand, and price


-


Clear, consistent titles that describe the item


Buyers can filter your live and Buy It Now listings by attributes such as size, color, and brand. Complete, well-organized listings increase trust and help buyers shop confidently, especially when they are making quick holiday purchasing decisions.


**4. Make the Most of Gift Season**


As you prep your shows, don’t forget there are a few other features that can help you get even more out of the season:


-


Referrals: As you gather with friends and family, tell them about Whatnot and earn referral credits when they join.


-


Search: Looking for a hard-to-find gift? Save your search on Whatnot to get notified when new items are listed.
