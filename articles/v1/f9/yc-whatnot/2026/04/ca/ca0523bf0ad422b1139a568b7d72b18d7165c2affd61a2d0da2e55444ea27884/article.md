# Whatnot x Shopify: Grow your Business with Live Selling

Since launching beta, businesses using the integration have driven more than $10 million in sales across nearly 20 categories, from Collectibles and Pets to Fashion and Food.


This includes[Gaines Pet](https://www.whatnot.com/s/mjwrQkLZ) , a brand specializing in all-natural dog treats. Like many businesses, inventory management was a major barrier to scaling. Now, with a fully integrated setup, everything is streamlined – and they’ve been able to grow quickly.


*“This Shopify integration is an absolute game changer for us. Previously, we were manually managing inventory, which capped our streaming time at about 10–12 hours a week. We are now going live nearly 30 hours a week, and by next month, we expect to increase another 30%. Because of this integration, I can now confidently say that Whatnot is our fastest-growing (and arguably most powerful) sales channel.”*


And this is feedback we’re hearing across the board, including the ease of setup. Whatnot luxury reseller[Glow Luxury](https://www.whatnot.com/user/glowluxury) , said: *“I have installed the integration and wow, what an incredibly smooth and intuitive setup. Truly impressed with how seamless the transition is. We are thrilled to be early adopters of this and can already see how impactful this will be for our business.”*


[FashioNica](https://www.whatnot.com/user/fashionica?srsltid=AfmBOoqqqJYn-51FnfMsddWSLlBp2RpMOeL_7Loyyqbt4TQJhE8jdkPp) , a luxury handbag reseller, shared a similar experience: *“Whatnot’s Shopify integration enabled us to unlock a new sales channel without compromising efficiency or quality. The integration allows us to execute drops quickly through bulk product management while seamlessly syncing orders directly into our fulfillment workflows.”*


### **What this means for you**


Every seller’s business is different, but the growing pains are familiar.


If you’re already selling on Whatnot and using Shopify to manage the rest of your business, you can now connect the two and eliminate manual reconciliation, just like GainesPet, FashioNica, and GlowLuxury. Add products in bulk, keep inventory automatically aligned, and let orders flow directly into your existing fulfillment workflow.


If you sell across multiple channels but haven’t tried live commerce because it felt like one more system to manage, this changes that. Live selling becomes an extension of the operation you already have, not a parallel process.


For sellers using third-party logistics integrated with Shopify, you can manage fulfillment outside of Whatnot's Seller Hub. That means no disruptions, just a seamless plug-in to your existing fulfillment operations. While we can't guarantee every third-party logistics provider can support this, many can. We suggest reaching out to your provider to see if it's possible.


And if your Whatnot business is growing quickly and starting to strain your operations, setting up[Shopify](http://shopify.com/) can give you a single, centralized place to manage products and fulfillment as you scale.


The hard part of selling online isn’t usually going live, it’s everything around it. Listing products, tracking inventory, routing orders, keeping fulfillment on track. Every new channel adds more moving pieces. Now, they move together.


### **Getting started**


The Whatnot Shopify integration is available through the Shopify App Store everywhere Whatnot operates, including the U.S., Canada, France, the UK, Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands, Austria, and Australia.


Setup is quick, and your systems stay seamlessly connected, making live commerce a true engine for long-term growth. It’s where passion meets performance, turning energy into real earnings. We’ll keep evolving the experience alongside seller feedback, and we’re excited to see what you build.
