# Product Spotlight: Clearer Signals, Stronger Protections, Smarter Growth

Stronger protections lead to smoother transactions, which is better for buyers, better for sellers, and better for the marketplace as it grows.


It starts with scanning your shipments in the Whatnot app. When you confirm drop-off using the[Shipment Scanner](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/39184750709261-Confirm-drop-off-with-the-in-app-scanner) , you create instant proof right away — and that early scan makes a real difference.


When a shipment receives a drop-off scan, from any method, shipping-related support tickets decrease by up to 60%. That includes a 71% reduction in claims that an order hasn’t shipped and 50% fewer cases where a package was marked delivered but reported missing. Even among packages confirmed to be in transit, a drop-off scan reduces support contacts by about 20%.


Packages scanned with Whatnot’s Scanner see about 10% fewer issues than those relying only on a carrier acceptance scan, and your[Account Health](https://blog.teamwhatnot.com/unitedstates/seller-protection-account-health-improvements) reflects when you actually dropped off the order. When winter storms disrupted USPS earlier this year, sellers who used our Shipment Scanner were still able to show clear proof of shipment and maintain buyer trust — even when carriers deal with major issues.


We’re also introducing another layer of shipping protection. For[shipments over $500](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407442137741-Protect-your-shipments) , a signature will start to be automatically required at delivery, with a $4.15 fee added to your ledger. Signature confirmation helps ensure high-value items land in the right hands, gives sellers solid proof of delivery, and reduces the back-and-forth when an order is marked delivered but later reported missing. Buyers can opt out at the account level, but those deliveries won’t be eligible for refunds if reported as not received.


From drop-off to doorstep, these safeguards work together to reduce disputes, save you time, and protect your business in the long run.


***How to use it:*** *In your Seller Hub on the Whatnot app, tap the menu in the top-right corner and select Fulfillment. Open each order and tap Confirm Drop-Off. You can scan the shipping label directly in the app, which will automatically notify the buyer once the package has shipped. Orders over $500 will start to automatically include signature confirmation and the $4.15 fee, no extra steps from sellers required.*


Happy Selling!
