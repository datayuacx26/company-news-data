# From First-Day Outfits to Must-Have Backpacks, Whatnot has Back-to-School Covered

Back-to-school season comes with plenty of excitement, from fresh starts, new routines, to back-to-school outfits. But it can also be one of the most stressful and expensive moments of the year, especially for families coming off a summer of vacations, camps, and childcare costs. In fact, 58% of parents say back-to-school shopping is more stressful than the holidays*, with parents planning to spend upwards of $900 on related purchases.**


But back-to-school shopping doesn’t have to feel as stressful or expensive. Whatnot gives parents a fun, engaging way to discover a wide variety of products and deals right from their phone, with many live auctions starting as low as $1. Parents can shop directly from sellers they love, ask questions in real time, and see items up close before purchasing — all without setting foot into a crowded mall.


And this shopping activity is showing up across the platform. Within the last month, **“Back to School” search demand is up roughly 70% year over year** . Some of our fastest-growing sales items include school uniforms and clothing (+341% YoY), school supplies (+304% YoY), and kids shoes (+208% YoY). Backpacks and book bags are leading in order volume, with shoppers drawn to a mix of nostalgic characters and recognizable brands. Character and franchise backpacks — including Shopkins, My Little Pony, and Monster High — are among the most popular, while brand-names like Sprayground, JanSport, Lululemon, and Nike are also generating strong search demand.


The back to school shopping momentum is especially evident in Baby & Kids Clothing, one of the top-selling categories within Whatnot's fashion vertical. **Just over halfway through 2026, our Kids Clothing category has already passed its full-year 2025 sales, with sellers moving more than twice as many items as this time last year** .


The category is also welcoming a wave of new sellers who are growing successful businesses on Whatnot. Nearly three out of four sellers are new to the category this year, and they are already driving more than a quarter of sales.


**Behind this growth is a community of parents building real businesses** , some as side hustles and others as full-time jobs. All three sellers featured below joined Whatnot within the last 18 months. Collectively, they have gone from closet clean-outs to designing their own clothing brands, showcasing how anyone can get started and scale a business on Whatnot.
