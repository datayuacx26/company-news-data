# Product Spotlight: Better Listings Before You Go Live

***How to use it:*** *Add the exact brand when scheduling your show, then make sure your title, thumbnail, and inventory all match. The more accurately you describe what you're selling, the easier it is for the right buyers to find you.*


### **Better Listings, Better Shipping**


As you're taking advantage of these new tools, it's also a good time to make sure your shipping setup is ready for your next show.


The more accurately you list your products, the easier it is to choose the right shipping profile and package weight before you go live. That means a smoother experience for you and your buyers from listing to delivery.


That matters even more now that our new[USPS shipping updates are live](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/46768886074765-USPS-shipping-updates-on-Whatnot-starting-July-31st) . Most orders will now cost less to ship, with Ground Advantage rates for 1 to 5 lb shipments dropping nearly 20% to $7.75. With delivery times similar to Priority Mail, Ground Advantage is now the best option for most shipments.


While USPS increased Flat Rate pricing, we're helping cover much of that increase to keep rates closer to what sellers pay today. And we're not stopping there. We're continuing to invest in new shipping options and more competitive pricing to sellers, with more updates to come.


***How to use it:*** *Before your next show, review your shipping profiles alongside your listings. We recommend Ground Advantage for most shipments, and always match your packaging to the shipping label. Using Priority Mail or Flat Rate packaging with a Ground Advantage label can result in USPS fees.*


Happy Selling!
