# The Community Behind the Hobby's Biggest Weekend

In 2021,[Whatnot](https://www.whatnot.com/) showed up to the[National Sports Collectors Convention](https://www.nsccshow.com/) with a 10-foot-by-10-foot booth. One table. Most people walking the floor had never heard of us.


We've been back every year since, each one bigger than the last. This year we're coming with a 20,000-square-foot space. The distance between that first table and where we are now isn't a story about us. It's about the community that made Whatnot the home of the hobby.


### **Where the hobby lives**


The hobby found a home on Whatnot fast, and the scale of it now is hard to overstate. Four cards are sold every second, and the number of shows earning more than $100,000 in card sales has climbed over 80% in the past year.


Behind those numbers are people: the seller who knows every rookie in the set, the collector who finally tracks down the single they've looked for for years, the buyers who show up week after week for the same show. The hobby has always been about the people in it. Live selling opened new ways to connect, and everything we've built, we've built with the community.


### **The hobby became a career**


Not long ago, making real money in cards meant a storefront, a spot on the show circuit, and the overhead of both. Live commerce changed that. Someone with deep knowledge of a player, a set, or an era can now build an audience and a business from wherever they are, across singles, vintage, breaks, and everything in between. Three in five sports card sellers on Whatnot made their first sale on the platform this year, and the seller base has grown by nearly half.
