# Product Spotlight: Find Demand Faster

Markets move fast. Buyer interests move even faster.


Top Trends are one way we're bringing real-time buyer demand directly into discovery. Powered by search behavior on Whatnot, they highlight what buyers are actively looking for across the marketplace in real-time.


On average, Whatnot users perform roughly 900,000 searches on the platform every day. That's hundreds of thousands of signals that help us understand what's gaining traction and where buyer interest is growing.


And they update dynamically based on marketplace activity. Because Top Trends are informed by the same signals that power discovery across Whatnot, they provide sellers with a more current view of what's capturing buyer attention. For sellers, this creates a powerful advantage: visibility into demand before it becomes obvious.


If you're looking for signals of where demand is building, a few trends currently stand out:


-


Buyers can't seem to get enough of NeeDoh, which continues to rank among the top searches on Whatnot.


-


Sports Jerseys are seeing a surge in interest as the FIFA World Cup keeps sports fans engaged and shopping.


-


Coach is back in the fashion spotlight, with their handbags holding strong resale values and growing popularity among younger buyers.


***How to use it:*** *Click “Categories” and under the Search bar, you’ll see what’s Trending Today. You can toggle between “Most Searched” and “Recommended,” which are more specific to you. Think of Top Trends as a daily pulse check on buyer demand. If you see a trend that aligns with your inventory, consider highlighting it in upcoming shows while interest is growing.*
