# The World Cup Fuels Soccer Jersey Boom on Whatnot

A soccer jersey is never just a shirt. It’s a symbol of national pride, of your favorite player, the memory of unforgettable matches and increasingly, of your personal style. This year, as the World Cup dominated group chats and watch parties popped up around the world, soccer jerseys took over Whatnot.


While sports cards remain one of the most popular shopping categories on Whatnot, fashion has grown to be the largest category by order volume. This was especially reflective during the peak of the tournament, where soccer jersey searches grew faster than sports card searches, jumping nearly 230% with about 3x times more people searching than the month before.


### **Host country pride took over**


Throughout the tournament, Mexico and the U.S. teams led jersey demand on Whatnot, a clear sign of just how much host-country energy shaped shopping behavior. For sales, unsurprisingly US soccer jerseys led sales but England landed in second place and Mexico not far behind.


Across the United States, there was strong regional support for particular national teams.


Mexico dominated across the Southwest and West Coast, including California, Arizona, Washington, and Nevada. USA anchored big coastal and eastern markets like Florida, New York, Pennsylvania, Virginia, and New Jersey. And England showed up scattered across the Deep South, including Louisiana, Alabama, Georgia, and others.


### **Demand moved in real time and across nations**


As the World Cup moved into the knockout rounds, search demand shifted from early host-country favorites to the teams still standing.


Jersey searches for Norway roughly quintupled during the knock-outs on the back of Haaland's breakout run, while headline contenders climbed fast, Spain up 114%, France up 96% and Argentina up 53% week over week, as attention followed the teams still standing.


Meanwhile, early tournament favorites cooled from their group-stage peak. Mexico searches fell 58% and USA searches fell 39% from late June, as attention followed the teams advancing toward the final.


Heading into the final weekend, Spain jersey searches nearly doubled week over week, up 98%, while Argentina held flat, down 12%.


### **Messi led the way but Haaland was the breakout star**


Player demand moved just as fast as team demand. Soccer legend Messi drew more search interest than anyone, and together with 18-year old Lamine Yamal accounted for nearly 40% of all named-player searches, as spectators watched the passing of the torch.


But the breakout star was Erling Haaland. Searches for Haaland jumped roughly 17x from before the tournament, and he briefly overtook Messi in early July as Norway's run gained momentum.


Pulisic, the U.S. captain, also saw a major home-tournament bump, with searches up more than 3x.


A breakout goal, a strong tournament run, or a viral match moment quickly turned into search demand for jerseys tied to a particular player.
