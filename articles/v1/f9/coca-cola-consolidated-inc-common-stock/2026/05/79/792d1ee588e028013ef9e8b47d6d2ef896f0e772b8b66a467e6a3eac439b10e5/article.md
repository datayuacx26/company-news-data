# Coca-Cola Consolidated Welcomes Maryland Governor on Tour of Limited-Edition America250 Mini Cans in Baltimore

Coca-Cola Consolidated hosted a special tour at our Baltimore Production Center, offering Governor Wes Moore and his team an exclusive, behind-the-scenes look at the production of its limited-edition America250 Mini Cans. The Governor also presented the facility with a citation celebrating its 60th Anniversary.


Created in partnership with The Coca-Cola Company as part of the nationwide[America250](https://america250.org/) commemoration, these 7.5-ounce, state-themed mini cans feature unique designs honoring each U.S. state and territory. The tour spotlighted the innovation and teamwork powering Coca-Cola Consolidated’s America250 celebration and underscores our longstanding connection to America’s history, heritage and culture.


Produced at four of Coca-Cola Consolidated’s 11 manufacturing facilities, including the Baltimore Production Center, the cans are available at retailers across Coca-Cola Consolidated’s 14-state territory and Washington, D.C. Consumers can take pride in celebrating America while enjoying the refreshing taste of Coca-Cola, Coca-Cola Zero Sugar and Diet Coke.
