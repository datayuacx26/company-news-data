# Coca-Cola Consolidated Announces New Partnership Bringing Coca-Cola Products to Kiawah Island Golf Resort

**KIAWAH ISLAND, SC, August 18, 2026** **–** Coca-Cola Consolidated is thrilled to announce its new beverage partnership, bringing refreshing, iconic and industry-leading Coca-Cola products to Kiawah Island Golf Resort.


> “We are delighted to welcome Coca-Cola Consolidated to Kiawah Island Golf Resort as a beverage provider,” said General Manager, Villa Resort, Missy Wurthmann. “Guest feedback has communicated a strong desire to enjoy Coca-Cola products while visiting the resort, so we are pleased to have this opportunity to serve our guests their beverages of choice.”


Coca-Cola beverages are now available throughout Kiawah Island Golf Resort, complementing the property’s exceptional dining experiences, championship golf, internationally recognized racquet sports, award-winning recreation and nature programming, luxury accommodations and world-class hospitality. Guests can enjoy Coca-Cola fountain beverages at all of the resort’s restaurants, bars and other food and beverage locations, while The Nest general store offers Coca-Cola’s full portfolio of bottled beverages.


Across the resort, guests will also find a wide variety of 20-ounce favorites, including Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, Coca-Cola Zero Sugar and Sprite, along with smartwater, BODYARMOR, Powerade and Monster Energy. For conferences, meetings and catered events, the resort serves Coca-Cola’s 12-ounce bottles and cans, ensuring a broad selection of beverage options for every guest experience.


> “Kiawah Island Golf Resort has been an icon of the Lowcountry for 50 years, creating unforgettable experiences for guests from around the world,” said David Brannen, Area Sales Manager, Coca-Cola Consolidated. “We’re proud to partner with such a renowned destination, bringing together two trusted brands that share a commitment to quality, consistency and exceptional hospitality. Whether guests are enjoying a championship round of golf, relaxing on the beach, dining with family or attending a special event, we’re excited to provide the beverage brands they know and love throughout every part of their Kiawah Island experience.”


As the local Coca-Cola bottler, Coca-Cola Consolidated is proud to partner with an iconic Lowcountry destination where generations of families create lasting memories. Expanding our partnership to Kiawah Island Golf Resort further strengthens our presence across the region, reinforcing our commitment to serving consumers in South Carolina with the beverage brands they know and love.
