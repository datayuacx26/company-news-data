# This Year in History: Dasani Bottled Water Enters the Market

In April of 1999, Coca-Cola Consolidated became the first bottler to launch Dasani, helping introduce The Coca-Cola Company’s newest bottled water brand to consumers as demand for convenient, refreshing water was taking off.


From its crisp taste and innovative packaging to the continued evolution of the brand, Dasani has remained a part of everyday moments for millions of consumers.
