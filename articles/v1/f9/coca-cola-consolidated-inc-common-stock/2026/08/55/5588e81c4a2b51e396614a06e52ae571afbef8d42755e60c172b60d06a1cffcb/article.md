# Behind the Bottle: Coca-Cola Consolidated Partners with Kiawah Island Golf Resort

Coca-Cola Consolidated and Kiawah Island Golf Resort are bringing together two trusted brands with a shared commitment to quality, hospitality and unforgettable guest experiences.


From championship golf and beach days to exceptional dining and special events, guests can now enjoy their favorite Coca-Cola beverages throughout the resort. But this partnership is about more than beverages, it’s about serving the Lowcountry community and helping create memorable moments for generations to come.
