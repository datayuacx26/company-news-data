# Coca-Cola Consolidated and Montgomery Inn Announce New Partnership Bringing Coca-Cola Products to Both Restaurant Locations

Coca-Cola Consolidated and Montgomery Inn are thrilled to announce their new beverage partnership, bringing refreshing, iconic and industry-leading Coca-Cola products to Montgomery Inn restaurants, located at 9440 Montgomery Road and 925 Riverside Drive in Cincinnati, OH.


Coca-Cola products will now appear alongside Montgomery Inn’s signature favorites. Famous for its slow roasted, perfectly caramelized barbecue, guests can now select from Coca-Cola,
Diet Coke, Coke Zero, and Sprite as well as Minute Maid Lemonade, Mello Yello, Mr. Pibb, Barq’s Root Beer and Barq’s Creme Soda to accompany their meal.


Montgomery Inn has been a staple in the Cincinnati community serving ribs and memories for 75 years. Known globally for its “Rib King” status, this partnership represents a major alignment
of two legacy brands.


“We’re honored to bring together two iconic brands to elevate the ultimate Ohio/Northern Kentucky dining experience,” said Jeff Cummins, Local Key Account Manager for Food Service
at Coca-Cola Consolidated. “Pairing Montgomery Inn’s world-famous ribs with Coca-Cola allows us to deliver even more of the special traditions our community loves.”


As Cincinnati’s local bottler, Coca-Cola Consolidated is deeply rooted in the community. Partnering with a legendary family-owned business like the Gregory family’s Montgomery Inn demonstrates a shared commitment to the “Queen City-Porkopolis.”
