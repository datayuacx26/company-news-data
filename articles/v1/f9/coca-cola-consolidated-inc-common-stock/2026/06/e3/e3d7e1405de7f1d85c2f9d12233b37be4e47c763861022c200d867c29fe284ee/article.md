# Coca-Cola Consolidated Blends An Iconic Treat with Military Gratitude in Columbus, Ohio

In celebration of America’s 250th anniversary, Coca-Cola Consolidated and other Coca-Cola bottlers served refreshing Coke, Diet Coke, Coke Zero, Cherry Coke and Barq’s Root Beer floats to elected officials, staff and visitors at the Ohio Capitol as a tribute to an iconic American treat. And in partnership with[Operation Homefront](https://www.linkedin.com/company/operationhomefront/) and[Kroger](https://www.linkedin.com/company/kroger/) , guests could thank military families through handwritten notes of gratitude and gift cards. This meaningful day was met with excitement and thanks while sipping on a delicious Coke Float!
