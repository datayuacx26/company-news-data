# Mascots and Meals: Celebrating America’s 250th Anniversary in DC

In celebration of America’s 250th anniversary, our Coca-Cola Consolidated Teammates joined the **Washington Nationals** , **Harris Teeter** , and the **Capital Area Food Bank** at Nationals Park to pack **50,000 meals** for families across the region. This event brought us closer to the goal of packing 250,000 meals before July 4th. Together, we’re honoring our Purpose by serving our neighbors and strengthening the communities we call home.


America250 also introduced “[George the Star](https://america250.org/news/a-star-is-born-america250-introduces-george-the-star-official-mascot-of-americas-250th-anniversary/) ,” the official mascot of America’s 250th Anniversary at the event.
