# This Year in History: The Development of Coca-Cola’s Iconic Contour Bottle

The Coca-Cola


contour bottle is more than packaging. It’s a symbol of innovation, identity and timeless design. Its story traces back to Terre Haute, Indiana, where a bold idea took shape and forever changed the way the world experiences Coca-Cola


.


What began as a challenge to create a bottle unlike any other resulted in one of the most recognizable designs in history. With its


distinct curves and unmistakable silhouette, the contour bottle quickly became an icon — representing quality, authenticity and connection across generations.


Today, that legacy lives on within Coca-Cola


Consolidated


’s 14-state territory, where the roots of this design continue to inspire pride and possibility.
