# The BODYSIM Vision


BODYSIM is on a mission to reveal your body’s true potential. We believe that even the most educated minds and active bodies are limited by a collective lack of self-understanding. BODYSIM breaks down that barrier and guides you to your physical peak. Here’s our vision for how BODYSIM will transform our users’s lives:


1.


Build a best-in-class, worth paying more for macro-tracking app for data collection and model calibration
☑ Faster entry due to “meal building” fucntion, and easy meal planning for “no log logging”
☑ A validated, evolving food database of common ingredients and purchasable foods
☑ Bar-code scanning for quick and accurate logging
☑ Predictive logging suggestions
☑ Progress tracking and recommendations
☐ Personalized, preference-based recommendations


2.


Build a best-in-class, worth paying more for meal planning app
☑ Personalized onboarding process to set up goals and preferences
☑ Custom or suggested meal plans for logging in advance and planning
☐ Community features for sharing recipes, tips, and progress
☐ Challenges and achievements to gamify the tracking experience
☐ Integrations with food delivery services and meal kits


3.


Build a best-in-class, worth paying more for workout tracking app
☑ Exercise library to build workouts and splits
☑ Muscle balance tracking for targeting the right exercises
☑ Modeled hypertrophy combining diet and resistance training data with potential
☑ 3D scan-based muscle focus highlights
☑ Progressive overload recommendations
☐ Dynamic exercise substitutions and workout suggestions


4.


Incorporate additional bio-markers from third-party partnerships (while saving our clients money)
☑ Integration with fitness wearables (Apple, Fitbit, Garmin, Whoop, etc.)
☑ Partnership with smart scale companies (Withings, etc.) for additional weight syncing and body composition data
☑ Baseline setting from DEXA scan
☑ Enhanced insights from bio-markers via mail-in blood test
☐ Integration with continuous glucose monitoring devices, sleep trackers, and DNA testing companies


5.


Use that to predict body composition
☑ Develop algorithms that predict body fat percentage based on weight, height, age, and other available inputs
☑ Use personalized simulations, unique to each client, to refine body composition predictions based on user input and passive monitoring
☑ Provide visual representations of body composition changes over time (3D scans)
☑ Use predictive analytics to forecast future body composition based on scenarios of current trends and future behavior.
☐ 3D forecast for realistic scenario planning


6.


Use that to guide clients toward their goals with real support from experts
☑ Dynamic scenario planning and goal adjustment
☑ Data collection reminders to keep information gathering on track
☐ Send regular progress updates to keep users motivated and on track.
☐ Employ nudges and reminders aligned with goals.
☐ Provide a community feature for mutual accountability.
☐ Offer virtual challenges and competitions to incentivize goal pursuit.


7.


All the while developing associated products that help our clients achieve those goals
☐ Consider developing a line of macro-balanced meal replacements tailored to different goals.
☐ Consider a line of supplements designed to support specific goals (protein powder, etc.)
☐ Offer a personalized coaching service with one-on-one support from registered experts.
☐ Create goal-specific clothing and accessories (compression gear for recovery, lifting straps for strength).


After that, well, the body is only the start…
