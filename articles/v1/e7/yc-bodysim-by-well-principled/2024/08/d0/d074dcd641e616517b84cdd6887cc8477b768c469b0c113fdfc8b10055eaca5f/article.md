# Gain Muscle and Shed Fat with a Digital Twin of Your Body

Gain Muscle and Shed Fat with a Digital Twin of Your Body


### The Problem


Muscle gain and weight loss journeys are harrowing because you don’t know what nutrition and exercise plans to follow


- Should I feel guilty not training harder, or would that lead to injury?
- Is that scale jump a signal to change, or just noise?
- Will my diet cause workout fatigue, limit my muscle gains, or just add fat?


Current solutions (MyFitnessPal, other apps, and high-end clinics) suffer from three core problems


1. **The right plan is different for every body.** Your unique metabolism and physiology, like maintenance calories, can easily be 50% off from online “calculators”—enough to go from losing fat to gaining it.
2. **Data is too noisy to steer by.** The scale (50% fat error), your fitness tracker (50% calories error), and even DEXA scans (too infrequent) only provide a noisy, fragmented picture.
3. **Body science is scattered and conflicting.** YouTube is full of “bro science,” and even a rockstar team of dieticians, personal trainers, and sports doctors wouldn’t agree with each other. Without those fundamentals, LLMs and correlation models can’t work either.


No existing solution quantitatively synthesizes all the science and your data into coherent, cogent plans.


### The Solution


BODYSIM is an app-based digital twin of your body; here’s what it does:


- Collects your data from food logging, BIA weigh-ins, DEXA scans, at-home blood tests, and wearables
- Learns to “triangulate the truth” of your unique metabolic profile, resistance training age, and more
- Adjusts your plan by simulating what will happen to your body BODYSIM is a science-based simulation of macronutrient balance and muscle hypertrophy. It includes cell turnover, oxidation for energy, lipolysis of fat stores, synthesis of new muscle, glycogen resynthesis, GI latency, and even ketones. We’re continuing to expand BODYSIM to endocrinology and beyond.

### The Product


**Define** : your starting point and goals


- Set your composition (fat, muscle) goal
- Complete your DEXA scan
- Get personalized nutrition (macronutrient) and exercise targets


**Track** : signals and measurements in the app


- Weigh-in daily on included Withings Body Scan
- Log your meals (barcode scanner, label scanner, recipe sharing)
- Connect your own fitness tracker (any in Apple Health)
- Log your workouts (2,200 exercise db)
- Test your blood biomarkers with our monthly at-home kits


**Simulate** : scenarios of your metabolism and physiology that hit your goals


- Make up your deficit and lose X pounds more fat by focusing on what matters
- Gain X pounds more muscle by increasing protein intake by Y grams/day
- Lose X pounds more fat by decreasing fat intake by Y grams/day
- Decrease workout fatigue by increasing carb intake by Y grams/day
- Plus 4 more key recommendations
