# 10 Steps to Buying Your First Home

Posted by[Patrick Begg](https://www.mihomes.com/blog/patrick-begg) in[Home Buying](https://www.mihomes.com/blog/home-buying)


Buying your first home is a big milestone, and while it's exciting, it's completely normal to feel a little overwhelmed. Between mortgages, budgets, and choosing the right home, there can seem like a lot to figure out.


The good news is that you don't have to navigate it alone. Once you understand the homebuying process, each step becomes much more manageable. Whether you're just starting to explore your options or getting ready to buy, this guide will walk you through what to expect from start to finish.


## Step 1: Calculate What You Can Afford


Before you start touring homes or scrolling through listings, it's important to[understand your budget](https://www.mihomes.com/blog/budgeting-for-a-new-construction-home-spreadsheet) . Knowing what you can realistically afford will help you focus your search and make confident decisions along the way.


Take a look at your monthly income, existing debt, savings, and everyday expenses. If you're considering a newly built home, remember to factor in costs that may be unique to new construction.


## Step 2: Check Your Credit Score Before Applying


Your credit score is one of the biggest factors lenders consider when approving a mortgage. A stronger score can help you qualify for better loan terms and lower interest rates, which could save you a significant amount over time.


If your credit needs a little work, that's okay. Many first-time buyers spend time[improving their credit before purchasing a home](https://www.mihomes.com/blog/welcome-home-club-a-credit-enhancement-program) .


## Step 3: Get Pre-Approved for a Mortgage


Getting pre-approved is one of the smartest things you can do before shopping for a home. It gives you a clear picture of your price range and lets builders and sellers know you're a serious buyer.


During the process, your lender will review your income, debts, assets, and credit history to determine how much you qualify to borrow. Once you're pre-approved, you'll be able to shop with much more confidence.


## Step 4: Understand Your Monthly Mortgage Costs


Your monthly mortgage payment is made up of more than just the loan itself. Depending on your loan type and down payment, you may also pay property taxes, homeowners insurance, and private mortgage insurance (PMI).


Understanding these costs ahead of time can help you plan your budget and avoid surprises after you move in.


## Step 5: Find the Right New Home Builder


Choosing a home builder is about more than finding a floorplan you love. You're also choosing a company that will guide you through one of the biggest purchases you'll ever make.


As you compare builders, look for quality construction, strong warranties, transparent communication, and a team that's willing to answer your questions throughout the process. Touring model homes is also a great way to see craftsmanship and included features firsthand.


See our guide:[Best Home Builders for First-Time Buyers](https://www.mihomes.com/blog/the-best-home-builders-for-first-time-buyers)


## Step 6: Start Touring Model Homes and Communities


Now comes the fun part: seeing homes in person.


As you tour model homes and explore different communities, think beyond the layout. Consider your commute, nearby schools, neighborhood amenities, and how the home will fit your lifestyle both now and in the future.


If you're buying new construction, ask about available homesites, Quick Move-In homes, personalization options, and estimated build timelines so you can find the best fit for your schedule.


## Step 7: Sign Your Purchase Agreement


Once you've found the home that's right for you, it's time to make it official.


Your purchase agreement outlines important details like the home's price, included features, financing terms, estimated construction timeline, and other key information. Take your time reviewing everything, and don't hesitate to ask questions before signing.


## Step 8: Complete Your Mortgage Loan Approval


After your contract is signed, your lender will finalize your loan. This usually involves providing updated financial documents, completing an appraisal if needed, and satisfying any remaining loan requirements.


Responding quickly to requests from your lender can help keep everything moving smoothly toward closing day.


## Step 9: Walk Through Your New Build Home


Before closing, you'll have one final opportunity to walk through your new home with your builder.


This is your chance to confirm everything has been completed as expected, learn about your home's features and systems, and ask any last-minute questions. Your builder will also explain warranty coverage and provide maintenance tips to help you care for your new home.


## Step 10: Close on Your Home and Celebrate


Closing day is when you'll sign the final paperwork, receive your keys, and officially become a homeowner.


While the journey may have felt long, every step was designed to help you make an informed decision and feel confident in your investment. Before you know it, you'll be unpacking boxes and settling into your new home.


## Ready to Take the First Step Toward Homeownership?


Buying your first home may seem like a big undertaking, but it becomes much more manageable when you take it one step at a time. By preparing your finances, understanding your mortgage options, and[working with a trusted builder and lender](https://www.mihomes.com/) , you'll be well on your way to finding a home that fits your lifestyle and your future.


Whether you're still saving for a down payment or you're ready to start touring homes, there's no better time to begin planning for homeownership. Every step you take today brings you one step closer to owning a place to call your own.
