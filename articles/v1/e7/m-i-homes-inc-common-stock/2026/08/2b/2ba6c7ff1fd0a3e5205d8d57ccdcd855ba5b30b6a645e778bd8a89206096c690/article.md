# Celebrating 50 Years

Posted by[Will Duderstadt](https://www.mihomes.com/blog/will-duderstadt) in[M/I Homes Culture](https://www.mihomes.com/blog/mi-homes-culture)


)


Fifty years is a remarkable milestone.


It's long enough to watch neighborhoods take shape and grow; and generations build lives within them. It's a moment for us to stop and reflect on our own[company's history](https://www.mihomes.com/about-us/history) and all that's happened along the way.


But while we're celebrating 50 years of homebuilding, for many families, the story is just beginning.


Behind every front door is a lifetime of moments waiting to unfold.


The excitement of move-in day.
The first family meal gathered around the kitchen table.
Holiday traditions that endure year after year.


Birthday celebrations, backyard cook-outs, late-night conversations, and quiet Sunday mornings spent cozied up at home with the people who matter most.


Over the past 50 years, we've had the incredible privilege of helping families find a place to call home.


Some families are buying their first home as they begin a new chapter together. Others are making room for a growing family, hosting grandchildren, or finding a space that better fits the life they've built.


No matter the stage, home is often at the center of it all.


That's what makes this milestone so meaningful.


**Because 50 years ago, we began with a commitment:**


Focus on[quality](https://www.mihomes.com/why-mi/quality) ,
Operate with integrity.
And above all, treat our customers right.


Today, we continue that mission
Doing the right things, the right way.


Built by hand. Made in America.
And[trusted since 1976](https://www.mihomes.com/about-us/overview) .


We build homes.
Where lives unfold,
Families grow, and
Memories are made.


***Half a century behind us. A lifetime ahead of you.***
