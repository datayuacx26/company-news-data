# ACS Fall Chicago is three weeks away. Since we last exhibited, ReactWise has gone from a reaction optimization tool to an end-to-end platform - from HTE hit screening to kinetic modeling for scale-up readiness.

ACS Fall Chicago is three weeks away. Since we last exhibited, ReactWise has gone from a reaction optimization tool to an end-to-end platform - from HTE hit screening to kinetic modeling for scale-up readiness.


At our booth, you’ll be able to test the full platform yourself - including our most recent updates:


**HTE Plate Designer** : now supporting both discovery (library synthesis) and process applications, helping you intelligently design plate layouts in minutes.


**MemoryBO** : our transfer learning Bayesian optimization engine that warm-starts new campaigns using historical data, so you never have to begin from scratch.


**Kinetics360** : first-principles kinetic modeling for mechanistic understanding and scale-up readiness.


**Solvent Selector** : data-driven solvent replacement recommendations.


I’ll also be presenting new results on robust Bayesian optimization for process development on Monday August 24th from 5:00 - 5:20 PM in room W184bc at McCormick Place


Convention Center.


Title: Robust Bayesian Optimization for Accelerating Process Development.


If you’re working in CMC focusing on reaction optimization and want to learn more, come chat with @daniel wigh and me at booth 224.


See you in Chicago.
