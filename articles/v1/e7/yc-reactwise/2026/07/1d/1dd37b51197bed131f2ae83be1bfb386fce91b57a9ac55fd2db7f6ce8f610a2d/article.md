# The best, greenest experiment is the one you never have to run. ZeroShotAPI makes that possible.

**The best, greenest experiment is the one you never have to run. ZeroShotAPI makes that possible.**


Last week I took that message to @Innovate UK's @Sustainable Medicines Manufacturing Link & Learn in Birmingham in a three-minute pitch of ZeroShotAPI.


@ReactWise's platform predicts the right process conditions for active pharmaceutical ingredients before a single experiment is run.


The response and follow-up discussion with the audience confirmed something I deeply believe: the industry is ready for this.


Moreover, I felt a growing consensus that sustainability can no longer be an afterthought in medicines manufacturing but becomes a critical pillar from early development.


For too long, process development has meant thousands of flask-scale experiments, hundreds of litres of solvent, and enormous chemical waste.


That has to change. And it can. And ZeroShotAPI is how we're doing it.


Sustainability and speed aren't a trade-off. They're the same goal.


Huge thanks to Innovate UK and the SMMIP programme for the platform.
