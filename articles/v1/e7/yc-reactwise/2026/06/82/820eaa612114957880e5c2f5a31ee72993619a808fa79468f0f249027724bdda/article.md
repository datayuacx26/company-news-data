# Speaking at the AI4Green External Stakeholder Forum!

Last week, our CTO Daniel Wigh attended the AI4Green External Stakeholder Forum, where he gave a brief talk on our work together.


ReactWise and AI4Green share a commitment to a more connected, sustainable, and data-driven lab. Our two-way API integration links AI4Green's ELN records to optimization workflows - historical data flows in to warm-start campaigns via MemoryBO, and ReactWise results flow back to keep a continuous experimental record. You can read the full case study[here](https://www.reactwise.com/ai4green-for-seamless-data-exchange) .


Looking forward to further collaboration.
