# Five continents. Five collaborations. One mission.

Five continents. Five collaborations. One mission.


At ReactWise, we believe the best science happens in the open and that means working with researchers around the world, not just building behind closed doors.


Here's a small snapshot of where that's taking us right now - just five of the many partnerships we're proud to be part of:


🌎 North America - Oregon State University: flow chemistry and lab automation
🌍 Europe - Fraunhofer IMM: autonomous chemistry and PAT integrations
🌍 Africa - CSIR: multi-objective flow chemistry
🌏 Asia - Amar Flow Laboratories: industrial-scale, multi-step flow chemistry
🌏 Australia - University of Melbourne: electro/photochemistry flow for challenging multi-objective optimization


Each of these partnerships pushes our science in a different direction - different chemistries, different equipment, different problems worth solving. Together, they keep ReactWise grounded in real, diverse, global science.


This matters to me personally too. For the past years I always found myself at the intersection of research between academia and industry - and I've seen firsthand how much faster science moves when knowledge crosses borders instead of staying locked inside one lab or one company.


That's why we're committed to publishing our research in academic journals.


Global collaboration isn't a side project for us. It's how we make sure ReactWise stays grounded in real science, wherever it's happening.


Don’t find your country on the map? If you're a researcher working on something that could benefit from collaboration - we'd love to hear from you, wherever in the world you are.
