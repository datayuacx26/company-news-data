# ReactWise is now SOC 2 Type 2 certified!

ReactWise is now SOC 2 Type 2 certified!


‍


This builds on our ISO 27001 certification earlier this year and reflects our continued commitment to protecting the experimental data our customers entrust to us.


SOC 2 is the standard most US enterprises expect, while ISO 27001 is the more widely recognized benchmark across Europe and the rest of the world. Holding both means we can meet the security expectations of customers wherever they operate.


For us, this reinforces that security, availability, and confidentiality are built into how we handle, store, and process data, not added on afterward.


As with ISO, we worked with Oneleet, who made the process straightforward.


For our existing and new partners: alongside our ISO 27001 certification, this should further streamline IT security and vendor reviews, helping you get up and running on the platform sooner.
