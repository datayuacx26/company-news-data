## Lucky Strike Entertainment Invites Guests to Make the Most of Summer with 25% Off Summer Season Passes


07/29/2026


Download (opens in new window)


*Beginning August 3, passholders will also receive a free lemonade or dirty soda with every visit through September 1*


RICHMOND, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Lucky Strike Entertainment (NYSE: LUCK), one of the world's premier owner-operators of location-based entertainment, today announced a new Summer Season Pass offer. Beginning August 3 through September 1, guests can purchase Summer Season Passes for 25% off, while all passholders will receive one complimentary lemonade or dirty soda with every visit.


As families look to make the most of the final weeks of summer, Lucky Strike Entertainment continues to invest in affordable experiences that encourage repeat visits and time together. The enhanced Summer Season Pass offer delivers even greater value, giving guests more reasons to return throughout the season. The Summer Season Pass includes two games of bowling and shoe rental every day at participating Lucky Strike, AMF and Bowlero locations, while Premium Pass holders also receive daily arcade reloads, discounts on food and non-alcoholic beverages, and additional exclusive benefits.


"For nearly three decades, Lucky Strike has been a place where families come together, and the Summer Season Pass has helped make bowling a summer tradition for guests across the country," said Katie Warner, Chief Marketing Officer of Lucky Strike Entertainment. "As summer winds down, we're continuing to invest in experiences that bring people together while creating even more opportunities for our guests to connect.”


The promotion reflects Lucky Strike Entertainment's ongoing commitment to providing accessible, value-driven entertainment across its portfolio of brands. With more than 360 locations across North America, the company continues to create destinations where families, friends and communities can connect through shared experiences.
