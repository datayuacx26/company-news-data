## Lucky Strike Entertainment Invites America to Celebrate National Bowling Day


08/03/2026


Download (opens in new window)


*Guests Can Celebrate at Lucky Strike, AMF and Bowlero Locations Nationwide with 50% Off Unlimited Bowling on Saturday, August 8*


RICHMOND, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Lucky Strike Entertainment (NYSE: LUCK), one of the world's premier owner-operators of location-based entertainment destinations, is inviting families, friends and bowling fans to celebrate National Bowling Day on Saturday, August 8, with 50% off Unlimited Bowling and shoe rental from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. at participating Lucky Strike, AMF and Bowlero locations nationwide. Guests can also receive 20% off lane reservations and events booked August 3–9 using promo code BOWL for visits through August 31.


In celebration of National Bowling Day, Lucky Strike Entertainment is bringing its Bowling Forever campaign to life through new digital activations and larger-than-life creative moments, including a Times Square billboard takeover. Bowling Forever honors bowling's lasting place in American culture while inviting a new generation to experience one of the country's favorite pastimes.


For generations, bowling has been where celebrations happen, leagues are formed and family traditions begin. That legacy continues today as Americans increasingly seek in-person experiences, with more than 300,000 bowlers participating in leagues across Lucky Strike Entertainment's nationwide portfolio. National Bowling Day also builds on the momentum of the company's recent nationwide AMF relaunch, reinforcing its continued investment in the future of bowling while honoring one of the sport's most iconic brands.


"Bowling has always been about bringing people together, and National Bowling Day is a chance to celebrate the communities that have made the sport such a lasting part of American culture," said Katie Warner, Chief Marketing Officer of Lucky Strike Entertainment. "We're proud to celebrate by making it easier for families, friends and bowlers of all skill levels to spend time together with great offers that create memorable experiences at our centers nationwide.”


National Bowling Day has been celebrated annually since 1956 and is observed on the second Saturday of August. Guests are invited to celebrate at participating Lucky Strike, AMF and Bowlero locations nationwide, where guests can take advantage of National Bowling Day offers and learn more about local leagues and upcoming fall bowling programs throughout the day.
