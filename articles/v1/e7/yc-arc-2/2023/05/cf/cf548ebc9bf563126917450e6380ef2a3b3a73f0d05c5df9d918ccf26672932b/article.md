# WebSummit Rio Panel: Are We Heading for a Global Banking Crisis? | Arc

During the panel on banking at WebSummit Rio 2023, several important questions were raised about the sector. One of the most pressing issues was whether we are heading for a global banking crisis. The panelists, which included:[Don Muir](https://www.linkedin.com/in/don-muir/) , Founder & CEO of Arc;[Taynaah Reis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/taynaah-reis/?locale=en_US) , CEO of Inkluziva,[Juca Andrade](https://www.linkedin.com/in/juca-andrade/?locale=en_US) , Chief Product Officer of B3; and[Mateus Marques](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mateusjmarques/?originalSubdomain=br) , Reporter of Globo News, shared their perspectives on the topic.


When asked about the differences between the 2008 financial crisis and the current situation, the panelists highlighted the role of Fintechs in providing support for startups during the recent crisis, and the role of the government in the former crisis. Looking towards the future, the panelists predicted an accelerated adoption of Fintech solutions in the banking sector.
