# Arc Partners With Digits for Smarter Accounting

## **Arc Partners With Digits for Smarter Accounting and Happy Customers**


We’re excited to announce our partnership with


[Digits](https://digits.com/?utm_source=arc&utm_medium=blog) , the first end-to-end accounting platform built for the AI era. As a leading capital management solution for startups, **Arc** is committed to giving customers the best experience from pre-seed to pre-IPO. Digits created the first


[Autonomous General Ledger](https://digits.com/blog/agl-announcement/) to support early-stage companies and scale with them as they grow, making this a natural match for our mission. The Arc and Digits teams celebrated the launch of this partnership at the 2025 Startup Grind Conference. Read more about why we decided to work together and learn how to activate one year of free bookkeeping with Digits below.*


### **Platforms Built for Scale**


Arc launched in 2021 to modernize the financial landscape for startups, two years before SVB’s collapse. Digits was founded in 2018 to reimagine accounting with machine learning, four years before ChatGPT. Both platforms were built with scale in mind. Arc partners with institutions like Stripe, with funds held at Fifth Third Bank and BNY Mellon, to deliver enterprise ready security and flexible workflows. Digits offers a product-first approach to AI accounting, from founder-led startups to fast-growing companies needing full-service accounting and tax prep.


### **360° View of Your Financial Stack**


By combining Digits with Arc’s Cash Management suite you can get a full view of every dollar in and out of your business. Busy founders don’t have time to stress about finances - Digits and Arc come in to help categorize transactions, offer seamless operating and treasury management, manage invoices and bills, and offer cards to all of your employees with controls. Built to make month-end close simple and stress-free.


###
**One Free Year of Digits Bookkeeping***


Through our partnership, Digits customers get one year free when you open a new Arc Business Account. It’s just one more way we’re supporting startups with trusted financial tools.


- Deposit **$50,000** into a new Arc Business account and receive **$100/month toward Digits software for 12 months** .


- Deposit **$100,000 or more** , and you’ll receive **$350/ month toward Digits software plus full-service accounting** , alongside Arc’s premium capital management platform.


### **How to Activate Your Offer**


Head to our


[partner page](https://www.joinarc.com/landing/digits?utm_source=Arc&utm_medium=Blog&utm_campaign=launch) or speak to your Digits representative to activate your offer.


** Offer available to new Arc customers who apply for an Arc Business account through this offer page within 90 days of being referred by Digits, and meet all the qualifying criteria. Account opening is subject to review and approval.*


*To qualify for the offer, eligible customers must pay for their Digits bookkeeping service using their Arc card and meet the minimum balance requirements. Eligible customers will receive reimbursement of net eligible payments to Digits for bookkeeping subscription services during a given calendar month, up to the maximum amount based on their Business account balance for that month as described below. Offer recipients can receive up to 12 total reimbursements (1 per month) during the 12 months after opening their Arc Business account.*


*Maximum reimbursement eligibility per calendar month is based on the daily average balance of the Arc Business account, calculated at the end of each month:*


*Balance of at least $50,000: up to $100 reimbursed*


*Balance of at least $100,000: up to $350 reimbursed*


*Balance of at least $150,000: up to $550 reimbursed*


*Reimbursement amounts are not cumulative. Customers must have an active Arc Business account in good standing to receive the offer benefits. Void where prohibited. This offer cannot be combined with any other offer or promotion, and may change without notice.*
