# Celebrate World Mental Health Day 2023 this Oct!

In conjunction with **World Mental Health Day** on October 10th, we're excited to present a series of Live Classes and Spaces that are designed to help you gain valuable insights, tools and empowerment to help you nurture your mental health!


**Navigating Conversations Around Mental Health: Are You Okay?**
*6th Oct 2023, 1pm (SGT)*
- Provides helpful tools to start and handle difficult conversations with friends, family, or colleagues


**Mental Health: Bridging the Gap Between a Universal Human Right and Reality**
*11th Oct 2023, 1pm (SGT)*
- Explore the gap between the ideal of mental health as a fundamental human right and global realities
- Gain insights into the global mental health crisis, discover practical ways to promote mental health equity, and inspire awareness, compassion, and action


**Spaces: Decoding the Impact of Social Media on Mental Wellbeing**
*13th Oct 2023, 1pm (SGT)*
- Discover how social media affects mental wellbeing in the digital age, covering both the positives and negatives
- Improve your digital engagement


**The Art of Saying No (Vietnamese)**
*18th Oct 2023, 1pm (SGT)*
- Equip yourself with the tools to establish clear boundaries and make decisions aligned with your own desires and needs


**Be Happy At Work**
*20th Oct 2023, 1pm (SGT)*
- Discover strategies for boosting happiness, managing emotions, and improving work performance
- Reshape job satisfaction to unlock the keys to workplace happiness with our support


**Spaces: Compassionate Conversations: Navigating the Path of Mental Health Support**
*25th Oct 2023, 1pm (SGT)*
- Join others who understand, and learn to provide care & empathy effectively
- Share practical strategies for creating a nurturing environment in this session


**Ask Me Anything: Featuring Miranda Meng**
*27th Oct 2023, 1pm (SGT)*
- Connect with an ICF Certified Coach where you can ask questions about mental health, personal development, career and overall wellbeing
