## What Does "Dermatologist Developed" Actually Mean?


"Dermatologist developed" means a board-certified dermatologist was involved in formulating the product and choosing active ingredients, setting concentrations, shaping the line before it ever reached a lab bench for mass production.


That's a meaningfully **different claim from "dermatologist tested"** (a derm checked it for irritation after formulation) or " **dermatologist recommended** " (a derm likes the finished product enough to suggest it to patients).


The distinction still matters if you're trying to gauge how much clinical thinking actually went into the formula.


Label What It Actually Means Example Brands


Dermatologist Developed A dermatologist helped formulate the product from the ground up CeraVe, Riversol, Dr. Dennis Gross


Dermatologist Tested A dermatologist evaluated the finished product for tolerability Varies widely by brand


Dermatologist Recommended Dermatologists suggest the product to patients, but may not have formulated it Vanicream, many drugstore staples


Dermatologist Approved An informal endorsement, often without disclosed criteria Used loosely across the industry
