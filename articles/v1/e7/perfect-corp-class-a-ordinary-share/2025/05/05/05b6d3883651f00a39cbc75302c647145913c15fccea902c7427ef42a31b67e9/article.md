## Guidelines for Effective Personalization in Beauty


To succeed with personalized marketing, brands must follow these key principles:


- **Know Your Customer:** Collect and analyze data responsibly. Skincare brands should understand their customers’ unique skin concerns and shopping behavior.

- **Deliver Value-Driven Personalization:** Every touchpoint—email, website, in-store, app—should provide useful, relevant content.
