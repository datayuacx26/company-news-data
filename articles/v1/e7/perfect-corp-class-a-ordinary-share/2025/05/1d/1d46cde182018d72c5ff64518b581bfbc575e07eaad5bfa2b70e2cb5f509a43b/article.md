## What Is an AI Skin Type Test?


An **[AI skin type test](https://www.perfectcorp.com/business/products/skin-type-test)** is a digital diagnostic tool that uses artificial intelligence and computer vision to analyze a person’s skin condition through facial imaging. By capturing a photo of the face, the system can assess multiple skin characteristics and determine the user’s skin type and key concerns—instantly and objectively.


#### **Oily T-zone and Dry U-zone**


A key innovation of our solution lies in its precise evaluation of both the T-zone and U-zone, with an emphasis on oil production and skin behavior in these critical areas.


The T-zone, known for its higher sebum activity, plays a vital role in determining oily or combination skin types. Our dermatologically validated technology boasts a **95% test-retest reliability rate** , ensuring consistent results every time.

Absolutely! Sensitivity is one of the most challenging conditions to identify visually.
