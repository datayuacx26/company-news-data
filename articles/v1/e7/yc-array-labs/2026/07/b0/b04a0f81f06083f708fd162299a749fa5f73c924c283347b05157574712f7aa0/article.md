# Array Labs Expands Strategic Partnerships to Accelerate Global 3D Mapping

**PALO ALTO, CA, February 26, 2025 –** Array Labs, developer of the world's first 3D-imaging satellite constellation, today announced several strategic relationships to accelerate deployment of its breakthrough technology platform.


John Deere (NYSE: DE) recently[announced](https://www.deere.com/en/news/all-news/2025-startup-collaborator-program/) that it has selected Array Labs for its prestigious 2025 Startup Collaborator program to explore applications of high-resolution 3D mapping in the ag and heavy industry markets.


Additionally, Array Labs announced today that it has received an investment from In-Q-Tel (IQT), the strategic investor that identifies innovative technologies to support the U.S. national security community and America’s allies. As part of the investment, IQT Managing Partner Tom Gillespie will join Array Labs' board as an observer.


These new relationships build on Array's collaboration with Umbra and Raytheon (NYSE: RTX),[announced](https://www.arraylabs.io/post/site3d-teaming-agreement) in January 2025, which is accelerating the development of Site3D, Array's first commercial product combining state-of-the-art radar with advanced algorithms to create detailed 3D models of Earth's surface.


Array Labs is also proud to be enrolled in NVIDIA’s[Inception](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/) program and the exclusive[Esri Startup Program](https://www.esri.com/en-us/about/partners/our-partners/startups) , which will help expand access to Array's 3D data products across Esri's extensive network of geospatial technology users worldwide.


## Transforming How We See and Understand Our Planet


Array's distributed satellite architecture combines formation flight technologies and advanced radar systems to collect detailed global data through any weather conditions, day or night. The company's space systems and processing pipeline deliver native 3D data, with sub-meter resolution and frequent global updates – achievements previously impossible with conventional satellites.


These capabilities mark a fundamental shift in how governments, Fortune 500 companies, and civil organizations monitor and understand our changing planet, enabling:


- All-weather rapid damage assessment and response planning
- High-precision terrain mapping in challenging environments
- Automated change detection across vast areas
- Continuous monitoring of critical infrastructure
- Enhanced global situational awareness


"This momentum reflects growing recognition of our technology's transformative potential," said Andrew Peterson, CEO and cofounder of Array Labs. "Working with leaders across agriculture, defense, and geospatial, among other sectors, significantly expands our ability to deliver breakthrough capabilities to our customers worldwide."


These announcements come at a pivotal time for Array Labs, following the successful 2024 launch of two demonstration satellites, multiple[contract](https://www.arraylabs.io/post/afwerx-comms-award)[wins](https://www.arraylabs.io/post/afwerx-coating) with the U.S. Department of Defense, and growing commercial interest in the forthcoming Site3D product line.
