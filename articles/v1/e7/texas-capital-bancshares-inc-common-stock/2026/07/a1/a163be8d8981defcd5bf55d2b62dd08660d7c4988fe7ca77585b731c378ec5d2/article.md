# Texas Capital Securities Advises Global Energy Services Alliance


Published on Jul 09, 2026


Texas Capital Securities ("TCS") is pleased to announce that Global Energy Services Alliance ("GESA"), a global provider of full-lifecycle services for turbine and generator systems, has been acquired by Solaris Energy Infrastructure (NYSE: SEI)("Solaris"), a provider of power generation and distribution solutions to clients in the data, energy, commercial, and industrial sectors.
