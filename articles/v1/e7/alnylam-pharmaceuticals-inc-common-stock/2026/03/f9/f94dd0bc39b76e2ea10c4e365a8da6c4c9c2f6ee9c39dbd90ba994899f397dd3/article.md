## Alnylam Named One of Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies for 2026


RNAi pioneer recognized by *Fast Company* for the 3rd time for continued innovation in gene-silencing medicines


March 30, 2026


At Alnylam, we pioneered the medical breakthrough that is[RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutics](https://www.alnylam.com/our-science/the-science-of-rnai) — a completely new class of medicines that silences disease-causing genes. Rather than resting on this achievement though, we continue to push the boundaries of our technology to target new tissues and diseases...because we know that patients are waiting.


This relentless drive has earned us a reputation as a truly innovative company, defined not just by our science, but by a bold, collaborative culture and forward-thinking business practices and it continues to earn us recognition as one of ***Fast Company's* Most Innovative Companies.** Inclusion in this year's list marks our third time being named a Most Innovative Company ([2019](https://news.alnylam.com/leadership/articles/alnylam-named-fast-company-worlds-most-innovative-company-2019) and[2022](https://news.alnylam.com/leadership/articles/alnylam-named-one-fast-companys-most-innovative-companies-2023) ).


> Alnylam's culture is to turn challenges into opportunities, and this recognition from *Fast Company* reflects exactly that. We are proud to be pushing the boundaries of what's possible for patients." **- Pushkal Garg, MD, Chief Research and Development Officer, Alnylam**


In the[Medicines and Therapeutics category](https://www.fastcompany.com/91497260/medicines-therapeutics-pharmaceuticals-most-innovative-companies-2026) , we ranked 3rd joining peers Gilead Sciences and Vertex Pharmaceuticals in the top three. Eligible companies in this category work on "advances in treatments, vaccines, and drugs that help people live longer, healthier lives."


**About this year's list, from *Fast Company***


"In a world marked by rapid technological change, businesses must adapt to survive—and innovate to thrive. *Fast Company* evaluated organizations across every global region and dozens of industries to produce our annual guide to the most dynamic companies today. The result is a definitive look at the products, strategies, and ideas that are shaping the future."


"The 720 honorees in[Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies of 2026](https://www.fastcompany.com/most-innovative-companies/list) have met our high bar for demonstrating innovation and the impact of those innovations on business and industry. They lead their fields and are transforming the world."


**Alnylam also recognized by *Fast Company* as a "Best Workplace for Innovators"**


It's one thing to be recognized as an innovative company but it's another to be recognized as a great place for innovators to work. We've received that recognition from *Fast Company*[five years in a row](https://news.alnylam.com/careers-culture/articles/five-row-alnylam-named-2025-best-workplace-innovators-fast-company) .


See the full list of 2026's *Fast Company* Most Innovative Companies[here](https://www.fastcompany.com/most-innovative-companies/list) and we invite you to learn more about[our science](https://www.alnylam.com/our-science/the-science-of-rnai) ,[our news](https://news.alnylam.com/) and[careers](https://www.alnylam.com/careers) at Alnylam.
