# Pega Helps Federal Government Modernize Legacy IT Systems with Launch of Pega Blueprint for Government


Newly certified FedRAMP High design tool helps U.S. agencies rapidly transform aging applications for more efficient operations


**WALTHAM, Mass. – April 7, 2026 –**[Pegasystems Inc.](http://www.pega.com/) (NASDAQ: PEGA), The Enterprise Transformation Company™, today announced the launch of[Pega Blueprint™ for Government](http://www.pega.com/blueprintgovernment) – a FedRAMP High–authorized AI-powered design tool helping U.S. federal agencies modernize applications and workflows used in sensitive government operations.


Pega Blueprint for Government enables agencies to rapidly reimagine their processes and systems into modern, flexible, and easy-to-use applications. By adapting the proven commercial[Pega Blueprint](http://www.pega.com/blueprint) ™ to meet stringent FedRAMP High and IL5 standards, Pega allows government workers to securely design processes and apps using access-controlled data in full compliance with federal regulations.


**Market context: Old systems, new threats**
Federal agencies are under intense pressure to improve how they perform mission-critical work, yet many still rely on aging, siloed, and inefficient legacy systems that slow productivity and limit their ability to solve modern day problems. The[U.S. Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-107795) reports 80% of IT budget typically goes toward maintaining legacy systems, leaving little room for true modernization. The need to protect sensitive information only compounds the problem, further limiting federal IT workers from leveraging the latest AI solutions.


**A closer look: AI-powered transformation, built for government**
Available today, Pega Blueprint for Government helps close this gap by providing a secure, authorized environment for teams to rethink and redesign their mission‑critical workflows. Key benefits include:


- **Fast, effective legacy transformation done right:** Pega Blueprint for Government uses gen AI to rapidly ingest and analyze documents, screenshots, code files, internal policy manuals, and other artifacts, and generates modern workflows based on proven best practices in minutes. Pega’s enterprise-grade architecture and governance ensure reliable, scalable, and auditable workflows designed for the long term. This helps agencies more quickly retire aging systems and better support mission-critical operations with more modern applications.
- **Secure modernization for high sensitivity missions:** Federal teams can safely analyze and redesign sensitive operational workflows within a FedRAMP High and DoD IL5 authorized environment. Pega Blueprint for Government was assessed against hundreds of federal security controls, ensuring agencies can transform mission critical work without compromising security, compliance, or data governance.
- **Improved effectiveness and operational efficiency:** By eliminating manual bottlenecks and streamlining process inefficiencies, Pega Blueprint for Government helps agencies provide more productive, dependable, and impactful workflows. This helps speed decision making, improve execution, and optimize outcomes for their most high stakes operations.


**Background: Pega Blueprint**
[Pega Blueprint for Government](http://www.pega.com/blueprint) is based on the commercial version of Pega Blueprint, Pega’s groundbreaking AI tool for designing, building, and optimizing workflows. Pega Blueprint helps enable teams and individuals to quickly create reliable and predictable apps for the enterprise. Using the power of AI agents augmented with best practices from Pega and its partner ecosystem, Pega Blueprint helps organizations dramatically accelerate their digital transformation while maintaining the reliability and governance CIOs demand.


**Availability: Accessing Pega Blueprint for Government**
Pega Blueprint for Government is available immediately to authorized U.S. federal agencies and their partners. For more information, visit[www.pega.com/blueprintgovernment](http://www.pega.com/blueprintgovernment)


**Quotes and Commentary**
“Federal employees supporting critical programs spend enormous energy working around outdated technology and processes because their legacy tools simply weren’t built for the modern missions they support,” said Doug Averill, vice president, global industry market leader, government, Pega. “Pega Blueprint for Government now gives them a more effective way to redesign their sensitive workflows with confidence, helping teams move faster, operate more effectively, and deliver the outcomes people count on.”
