# Pega Announces Quarterly Cash Dividend for Third Quarter of 2026


**WALTHAM, Mass. – June 15, 2026 –**[Pegasystems Inc.](https://www.pega.com/) (NASDAQ: PEGA), the enterprise AI software company for mission-critical work, today announced a quarterly cash dividend of $0.03 per share, maintaining the company’s current dividend program. The Q3 2026 dividend will be paid on July 15, 2026, to shareholders of record as of July 1, 2026.
