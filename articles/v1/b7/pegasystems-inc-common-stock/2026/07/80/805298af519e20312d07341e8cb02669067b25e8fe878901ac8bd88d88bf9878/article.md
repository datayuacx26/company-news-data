# Journey from automation to autonomy: How Pega Agentic AI is redefining enterprise operations

Your customer just made a perfectly reasonable request in your customer service messenger. The problem is that it falls just outside your automated process. The system grinds to a halt. The answer is just disturbingly unhelpful. A support ticket is created, and the seamless experience you promised is broken. Sound familiar? This is the ceiling of traditional automation – a ceiling that is cracking under the weight of modern customer expectations.


Enterprises today are at an inflection point. Customers are more demanding and tech-savvy than ever, expecting brands to be agile, flexible, and instantly responsive to their needs. This shift in customer expectation is forcing business leaders to ask a bigger question: How do we move beyond rigid, predefined processes to create an enterprise that can adapt on the fly? While traditional automation and decisioning were milestones in efficiency, they were not designed for this new reality.


This is where **agentic AI** fundamentally changes the game.


[Agentic AI](https://www.pega.com/agentic-ai) unlocks a transformative opportunity, infusing the enterprise with the power to handle both creative and analytical activities, and make your operations inherently more adaptable to changing customer needs and business strategies.


And expectations are high! The[World Economic Forum](https://www.weforum.org/publications/proof-over-promise-insights-on-real-world-ai-adoption-from-2025-minds-organizations/) highlights that enterprises expect, on average, double-digit percentage increases in productivity and revenue growth within 18 months of deployment and scaling agentic AI, plus a similar reduction in costs.


## Why agentic AI matters now


Across industries such as insurance, healthcare, and financial services, work has become more complex, regulated, and time-sensitive. A single customer interaction often spans multiple systems, roles, and decisions. Human workers spend disproportionate time gathering information, validating data, and coordinating next steps rather than applying judgment where it matters most.


The idea of having systems **that can reason, act, and collaborate with predictable precision is just** the ultimate amplification of human ingenuity, freeing human minds from procedural tasks to focus on the strategic problems and human relations that we are uniquely suited to solve.


By orchestrating intelligence across cases, data, knowledge, and automation, agent-based systems **reduce cognitive load, shorten cycle times, and improve consistency** , while keeping humans in control of critical decisions.

## The shift from old way to agentic way


In a traditional claims or service environment, an adjuster often begins their day by logging into multiple disconnected systems. Policy data sits on one platform, medical records in another, claims history in a third, and correspondence in shared inboxes. To assemble a complete picture, the adjuster manually searches for documents, calls providers for missing information, and follows up with customers for clarifications.


This fragmented process can take hours or even days. Decisions are delayed, errors are introduced through manual handling, and customers experience prolonged uncertainty and frustration.


In an agentic environment powered by Pega, the process looks fundamentally different:


- Customers use the 24/7 available self-service to engage with the brand. Pega Conversation Agent smartly understands the context, asks/validates information, and initiates the right case.
- [Pega’s Predictable AI™ Agents](https://www.pega.com/technology/agentic-workflows) then seamlessly gather data from all relevant systems, request additional documents from customers, validate customer and policy information, analyze supporting documents, and apply business and medical rules in real time.
- Within seconds, the platform assembles a complete, verified case file. Potential risks or exceptions are flagged proactively, and recommended next actions are generated.
- The human adjuster receives a “ready-to-adjudicate” case, supported by transparent reasoning, supporting evidence, and recommendations.
- An assignment agent supports the adjuster by providing zero-hallucination access to case details, compliance guidelines and everything else needed to make the final decision.


Instead of spending time on data collection, the adjuster focuses on judgment and decision-making. What previously took days is now completed in minutes, improving both operational efficiency and customer confidence.
