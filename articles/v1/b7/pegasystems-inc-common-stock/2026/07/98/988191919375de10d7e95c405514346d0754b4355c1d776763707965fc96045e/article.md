# Pega Launches AI-Driven Modernization Capability on AWS To Reimagine Legacy Mainframe Apps Into Cloud-Native Systems


AWS Transform now integrates with Pega Blueprint AI to transform aging COBOL code into modern apps


**LAS VEGAS – June 8, 2026 –**[Pegasystems Inc.](http://www.pega.com/) (NASDAQ: PEGA), the enterprise AI software company for mission-critical work, today at[PegaWorld](http://www.pegaworld.com/) ® announced the[Pega Blueprint AI](http://www.pega.com/blueprint) ™ application design agent will be integrated within[Amazon Web Services (AWS) Transform](https://aws.amazon.com/transform/) to help organizations modernize mainframe applications faster and with minimal friction. This integration simplifies enterprise transformation, allowing clients to extract and analyze their legacy COBOL code with AWS Transform and reimagine it with Pega Blueprint AI into new cloud-ready agentic applications – all through one seamless experience.


**Market Context: The slow, costly, complex off-ramp from COBOL**
As enterprises look to adopt AI agents at scale, mainframe modernization has become a top priority so they can take advantage of the agentic future. Yet these initiatives are often slowed by decades of undocumented logic, millions of lines of COBOL code, and fragmented tools that require manual discovery before modernization can begin. As a result, organizations struggle to move beyond costly, time‑consuming lift‑and‑shift migrations that can replicate legacy inefficiencies in the cloud.


**A Closer Look: modernization in a seamless experience**
The unique combination of Pega Blueprint AI and AWS Transform composable solution enables organizations to perform application discovery and business rules extraction from their COBOL systems and reimagine them for the digital world. AWS Transform analyzes COBOL code and generates full documentation that captures time-tested business rules, logic, processes, and data structures. Powered by Gen AI and fueled by industry best practices, Pega Blueprint AI then ingests this output to autonomously generate future‑state, cloud‑ready application designs without leaving the AWS Transform interface.


The seamless integration of AWS Transform and Pega Blueprint AI enables businesses to move fast and break nothing in their digital transformation quests with benefits including:


- **Reimagining dated workflows:** The new Pega Blueprint AI agent in AWS Transform takes this analysis and synthesizes it with the latest industry best practices and regulations to transform legacy mainframe processes into agile and reliable modern workflows. Pega Blueprint AI can also ingest demos, screenshots, BPMN files, SQL files, and more to ensure the final application preserves business context, rules, data models, and integrations.
- **Accelerating time to value:** Organizations can reimagine the outdated processes holding them back with the help of Pega Blueprint AI, which is informed by industry best practices. This rapid, intuitive, iterative methodology gives organizations the digital foundation they need to better compete in the AI economy.


**Background: Pega Blueprint AI**
Pega Blueprint AI is Pega’s groundbreaking AI-driven design platform for reimagining business, helping enable teams and individuals to quickly create reliable and predictable agentic apps for the enterprise. Using the power of AI agents augmented with best practices from Pega and its partner ecosystem, Pega Blueprint AI helps organizations dramatically accelerate their digital transformation while maintaining the reliability and governance enterprises demand.


**Availability: Experience Pega Blueprint AI with AWS Transform**
Pega Blueprint AI is now available within AWS Transform at no additional cost to clients.


The seamless integration of Pega Blueprint AI and AWS Transform is on display this week at[PegaWorld](http://www.pegaworld.com/) ®, the annual user conference at the MGM Grand in Las Vegas. AWS General Manager of Agentic AI Modernization Omri Kessel will deliver a[keynote address](https://www.pega.com/events/pegaworld/agenda/modernization-power-move-aws-transform-pega-blueprint) to discuss how the Pega partnership accelerates the path to enterprise transformation. In addition, Unum EVP and Chief Information and Digital Officer Shelia Anderson will deliver[a keynote address](https://www.pega.com/events/pegaworld/agenda/cobol-meet-cloud%E2%80%AFunum-group%E2%80%99s%E2%80%AFultimate-modernization-glow) to discuss how Pega and AWS helped modernize Unum’s legacy technology. Visit[www.pegaworld.com](http://www.pegaworld.com/) to watch live or for the replay.


**Quotes & commentary**
“Modernizing core systems isn’t just about moving old code to the cloud – it’s about reimagining how work gets done,” said John Higgins, chief of client and partner success, Pega. “By embedding Pega Blueprint AI directly into the time-tested AWS Transform analysis capability, we’re removing friction from the most painful stages of modernization and helping enterprises finally move from legacy COBOL to intelligent, cloud‑ready applications faster and with greater confidence.”


“We recognized the need to modernize parts of our COBOL based claims platforms to support long term growth and flexibility,” said Shelia Anderson, EVP, chief information and digital officer, Unum Group. “This kind of work has traditionally taken years. By taking advantage of tools like AWS Transform and Pega Blueprint AI, we’re accelerating modernization within a key line of business. The focus is on simplifying long standing technical complexity and creating an environment where our teams can move faster and work more efficiently. This foundational work will help position us for what comes next.”


“Organizations modernizing mainframe applications need faster ways to understand legacy systems and move confidently toward the cloud,” said Dr. Asa Kalavade, vice president, AWS Transform, AWS. “By integrating Pega Blueprint AI directly into AWS Transform, customers can turn COBOL insights into modern, cloud-ready applications more efficiently, helping them accelerate transformation while reducing complexity. This integration completes the COBOL transformation cycle, giving customers an end-to-end path from legacy code to modern applications.”
