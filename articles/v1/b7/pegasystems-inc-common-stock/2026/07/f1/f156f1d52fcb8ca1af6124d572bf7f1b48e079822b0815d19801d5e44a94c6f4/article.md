# Pega to Hold Investor Session at PegaWorld 2026


**WALTHAM, Mass. – May 20, 2026 –**[Pegasystems Inc.](https://www.pega.com/) (NASDAQ: PEGA), The Enterprise Transformation Company™, today announced that an in-person Investor Session will be held at[PegaWorld](https://www.pegaworld.com/) , Pega’s annual user conference held at the MGM Grand in Las Vegas, hosted by Ken Stillwell, COO and CFO, Pega.


The Investor Session is scheduled for Monday, June 8, 2026 at 12:00 p.m. PDT (3:00 p.m. EDT). A live audio-only webcast of the Session will be available at[https://app.webinar.net/w7kxZ4g89Jz](https://app.webinar.net/w7kxZ4g89Jz) . A replay will be posted on the[Investors page](https://www.pega.com/investors) of[Pega’s website](https://www.pega.com/) for a limited time.


The Investor Session agenda will include a business and financial update on Pega. To submit your questions in advance, please email[\[email protected\]](https://www.pega.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8ddde8eaecc4e3fbe8fef9e2ffdfe8e1ecf9e4e2e3fecdfde8eaeca3eee2e0) or you may also submit your questions from the live webcast at[https://app.webinar.net/w7kxZ4g89Jz](https://app.webinar.net/w7kxZ4g89Jz) .
