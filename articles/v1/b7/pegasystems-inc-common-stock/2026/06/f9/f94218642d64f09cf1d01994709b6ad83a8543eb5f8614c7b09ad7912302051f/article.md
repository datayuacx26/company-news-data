# Pega Honors Partners at PegaWorld 2026 for Driving AI-Powered Enterprise Transformation


Fourteenth annual awards recognize partners delivering measurable outcomes through Pega Blueprint AI™, GenAI, and intelligent workflows


**LAS VEGAS – June 8, 2026** –[Pegasystems Inc.](https://www.pega.com/?utm_source=emd&utm_medium=pr&utm_content=2026-Pega-Partner-Innovation-Awards) (NASDAQ: PEGA), the enterprise AI software company for mission-critical work, today announced the winners of the 2026 Pega Partner Awards at its annual[PegaWorld®](http://www.pegaworld.com/) user conference in Las Vegas. The awards recognize partners across Pega’s global ecosystem for leading enterprise-grade AI innovation and delivering exceptional client outcomes through high-value industry solutions, personalizing customer experiences, and modernizing critical systems.


Pega collaborates with a global ecosystem of[best-in-class partners](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships?utm_source=emd&utm_medium=pr&utm_content=2026-Pega-Partner-Innovation-Awards) who accelerate clients' journeys to the autonomous enterprise using GenAI. These partners provide services and solutions to address significant challenges faced by leading organizations worldwide, leveraging their deep experience and industry best practices to enhance Pega’s world-class solutions and enable industry-leading business outcomes.


The awards are presented in three categories: Partner Achievement, Pega Offering Impact, and Industry Excellence. Partners are recognized for their differentiated, industry-leading solutions built on Pega. This year marks the introduction of the Blueprint Pioneer Award, recognizing a partner leading the way in the adoption of[Pega Blueprint AI™](http://www.pega.com/blueprint) to accelerate discovery and time to value.


This year’s recipients are as follows:


**Partner Achievement Awards**
**Partner of the Year**


- [TCS](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships/partner-finder/results?f%5b%5d=industry:Government&f%5b%5d=region:EMEA&q=TCS) for outstanding alignment to Pega’s partner strategy, sustained joint business success, and scaled delivery excellence across global enterprises.


**Market Maker**


- [Accenture](https://www.pega.com/partners-spotlight-accenture) for shaping market demand, expanding executive-level engagement, and driving co-sell momentum across industries.


**Delivery Excellence**


- [TCS](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships/partner-finder/results?f%5b%5d=industry:Government&f%5b%5d=region:EMEA&q=TCS) for setting the standard in large-scale Pega delivery through consistent execution, deep expertise, and measurable client outcomes.


**Innovation Award**


- [Aaseya](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships/partner-finder/results?q=aaseya) for demonstrating innovation and technology leadership in adoption of Pega AI capabilities to deliver cutting-edge, AI-driven solutions for customers.


**Blueprint Pioneer Award**


- [Cognizant](https://www.pega.com/xd/ct688/main/spotlight-cognizant?) for leadership in adopting Pega Blueprint AI to accelerate discovery, align business and IT, and dramatically shorten time to value for clients.


**Pega Offering Impact Awards**
**Customer Engagement**


- [Blend (FutureproofAI)](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships/partner-finder/results?q=FutureproofAI) for delivering hyper-personalized, AI-driven customer engagement at scale for a leading US telecommunications company using[Pega Customer Decision Hub](https://www.pega.com/products/decision-hub) .


**Customer Service**


- [EY](https://www.pega.com/xd/ep3418/main/spotlight-ey) for reimagining customer service delivery for a US insurance life insurance company through a unified, scalable Pega-powered service ecosystem.


**Workflow Automation**


- [Credera](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships/partner-finder/results?q=Credera) for delivering end-to-end workflow automation for a global consumer goods supplier, dramatically accelerating product lifecycle execution.


**Legacy Transformation**


- [Accenture](https://www.pega.com/partners-spotlight-accenture) for successfully modernizing workflow‑heavy legacy systems within a US state department, reducing risk and enabling scalable, cloud‑ready transformation.


**Industry Excellence Awards**
**Communications**


- [Infosys](https://www.pega.com/xd/is191/main/spotlight-infosys) for modernizing customer decisioning and service platforms for one of Europe's largest providers of cable broadband services.


**Financial Services**


- [Accenture](https://www.pega.com/partners-spotlight-accenture) for modernizing mortgage operations at a leading UK building society, delivering a future-ready Pega-based processing backbone.


**Insurance**


- [EY](https://www.pega.com/xd/ep3418/main/spotlight-ey) for best-in-class insurance transformation across multiple global carriers, delivering scalable, GenAI-enabled Pega solutions.


**Government and Public Sector**


- [Coforge](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships/partner-finder/results?q=coforge) for transforming urgent care delivery for a leading European healthcare provider, enabling resilient, national-scale digital health services.


**Healthcare and Lifesciences**


- [Virtusa](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships/partner-finder/results?q=virtusa) for modernizing the contact center at a dental insurance administrator through an AI-powered[Pega Customer Service](https://www.pega.com/products/customer-service) platform.


**Manufacturing and Transportation**


- [Tech Mahindra](https://www.pega.com/services/partnerships/partner-finder/results?q=tech%20mahindra) for modernizing global car manufacturers' registration and warranty systems through a successful migration to Pega Cloud.


The partner awards were announced during the annual PegaWorld® conference at the MGM Grand held in Las Vegas this week. The conference brings together industry experts and leaders to help attendees reimagine their technology strategies so they can practically and responsibly turbocharge productivity, unleash innovation, and optimize customer interactions. For more information, please visit:[http://www.pegaworld.com/](http://www.pegaworld.com/) .


**Quotes & Commentary:**
"The success of our clients' digital transformation journeys is fundamentally linked to the quality of our partner network," said Dan Kasun, global head of partner ecosystem, Pega. "This year's Pega Partner Award recipients have demonstrated exceptional ability to embrace the new wave of Pega’s AI capabilities, such as Pega Blueprint AI, to help organizations unlock the full potential of their technology investments and drive meaningful business outcomes."
