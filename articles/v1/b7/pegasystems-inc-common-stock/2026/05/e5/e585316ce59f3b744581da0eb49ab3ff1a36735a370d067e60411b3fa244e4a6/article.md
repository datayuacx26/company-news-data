# What's New on Pega Academy: 8 Missions You Should Know About

A new season is here and so is a fresh batch of learning content on[Pega Academy](https://academy.pega.com/search?q=) . Whether you're just starting your[Pega journey](https://community.pega.com/roles) , working toward[certification](https://academy.pega.com/certifications) , or exploring automation and AI, there’s something new for you. Here’s a quick rundown of what just dropped, with links so you can jump right in:


#### [Blueprint Delivered Methodology Foundations - NEW](https://academy.pega.com/mission/blueprint-delivered-methodology-foundations/v1)


Learn how Blueprint Delivered™, Pega's AI-powered delivery model, connects Blueprinting, Authoring, and Value Activation to take business intent all the way to production. Covers how to deliver working software sooner, reduce delivery risk, and treat go-live as a planned validation step rather than a high-stakes event.


*Skill level: Beginner*


________________________________________


#### [Pega Task Mining — NEW](https://academy.pega.com/mission/pega-task-mining/v1)


A new addition to the Pega Process Mining mission. Learn how to use desktop-level task data to uncover inefficiencies, even when system logs are incomplete. Covers opportunity identification, task metrics, timeline views, and idle time analysis.


*Skill level: Beginner*


________________________________________


#### [Application Signature Tool: Heritage Application Discovery — NEW](https://academy.pega.com/mission/application-signature-tool-heritage-application-discovery/v1)


A brand-new mission for architects driving modernization. Learn how to use the Application Signature Tool to analyze heritage Pega applications and map a path forward with Pega Blueprint™, turning discovery into clear, actionable plans.


*Skill level: Beginner*


________________________________________


#### [Lead System Architect enhancements for Pega Infinity ’25](https://academy.pega.com/mission/lead-system-architect-enhancements-pega-infinity/v2)


Already LSA-certified? This auxiliary mission helps you stay current on Pega Platform™ ’25, with a strong focus on GenAI. Topics include AI-driven enterprise architecture, Pega GenAI architecture, background processing, automated routing, and hands-on challenges with Pega GenAI Agents.


*Skill level: Advanced*


#### [Lead System Architect](https://academy.pega.com/mission/lead-system-architect/v7)


The core path to Certified Pega Lead System Architect, updated for Pega Platform ’25.1.2. Covers Constellation adoption, architecture, planning and design, and implementation and deployment. If CPLSA is on your roadmap, start here.


*Skill level: Advanced*


________________________________________


#### [System Architect](https://academy.pega.com/mission/system-architect/v8)


The foundational mission for new System Architects, refreshed for Pega Platform ’25. Covers core development topics like case management, data modeling, security, DevOps, and Pega GenAI, plus updates on Pega Blueprint and Center-out® architecture.


*Skill level: Beginner*


________________________________________


#### [Pega Digital Messaging](https://academy.pega.com/mission/pega-digital-messaging/v7)


Updated for Pega Customer Service ’25. Learn how to set up digital channels across web chat, WhatsApp, SMS, Facebook, and Apple Messages for Business. Includes new content on agentic messaging, real-time AI suggestions, and multi-app support in a single environment.


*Skill level: Beginner*


________________________________________


#### [Pega Robot Manager](https://academy.pega.com/mission/pega-robot-manager/v6)


Your go-to mission for managing attended and unattended automations at scale, updated for Pega Robot Manager ’25. New content covers tighter integration with Pega Platform to orchestrate work across people and bots.


*Skill level: Beginner*


________________________________________


### Where to start


Not sure which mission to pick first? Here’s a quick guide:


- Delivering with Blueprint Delivered™? Start with[Blueprint Delivered Methodology Foundations](https://academy.pega.com/mission/blueprint-delivered-methodology-foundations/v1) .
- Working on a modernization project? Check out the[Application Signature Tool](https://academy.pega.com/mission/application-signature-tool-heritage-application-discovery/v1) mission.
- Already an LSA staying current? Grab the[LSA enhancements for Pega Infinity ’25](https://academy.pega.com/mission/lead-system-architect-enhancements-pega-infinity/v2) .
- Targeting LSA certification? Head to[Lead System Architect](https://academy.pega.com/mission/lead-system-architect/v7) .
- New to Pega development? Start with[System Architect](https://academy.pega.com/mission/system-architect/v8) .
- Building customer service channels? Explore[Pega Digital Messaging](https://academy.pega.com/mission/pega-digital-messaging/v7) .
- Managing a robotic workforce? Try[Pega Robot Manager](https://academy.pega.com/mission/pega-robot-manager/v6) .


All missions are free and available to all users at[academy.pega.com](https://academy.pega.com/) . Complete the modules and challenges to earn badges and stay up to date on the latest Pega Platform releases.


Happy learning, see you on the leaderboard!
