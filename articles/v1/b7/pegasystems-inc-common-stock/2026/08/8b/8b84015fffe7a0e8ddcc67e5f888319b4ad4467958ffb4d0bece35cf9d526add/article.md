# Pega Named a Leader in AI Platforms by Independent Research Firm


Pega’s AI-powered platform recognized for agent and app development capabilities, with Pega Blueprint excelling in workflow design


**Waltham, Mass. – August 6, 2026 –**[Pegasystems Inc.](https://www.pega.com/) (NASDAQ: PEGA), the enterprise AI software company for mission-critical work, today announced Forrester Research named Pega a Leader in[The Forrester Wave™: AI Platforms, Q3 2026](https://www.pega.com/forrester-ai-platforms-2026) (1). Pega received the highest scores possible in eight of the 19 total evaluation criteria.


The report explains, “Pegasystems offers a comprehensive AI Platform with roots in workflow automation, customer offer personalization using machine learning, and sophisticated decisioning,” and goes on to state that “Pegasystems’ mantra of “Design for people, build for change” extends to helping global enterprises build AI apps that assist employees and customers as well as automate complex, regulated business workflows.”


Forrester’s evaluation explains, “Pegasystems’ agent and app development tools excel, especially for workflows via its Blueprint capability,” and notes that Pega customers “appreciate the platform’s ease of use for technical developers and business users alike in creating hybrid workflows that combine business processes with agents.”


The report’s profile on Pega concludes that “Pegasystems is a good fit for customers that wish to implement complex, regulated workflows using AI agents.”


Pega was among the 15 most significant AI platform providers evaluated and received the highest scores possible in the Current Offering criteria of agent development, AppGen tools, cohesive experience, and governance controls. It also received the highest scores possible in the Strategy criteria of innovation, roadmap, partner ecosystem, and community.


Forrester evaluated[Pega Infinity™](https://www.pega.com/infinity) , Pega’s AI suite of products designed to help enterprises scale AI with confidence. The evaluation included[Pega Platform™](https://www.pega.com/products/platform) , Pega’s application development platform for designing, automating, and optimizing business workflows that improve efficiency and customer experiences, and[Pega Customer Decision Hub™](https://www.pega.com/products/decision-hub) , Pega’s AI-powered decisioning platform that delivers next-best actions to personalize customer engagement at scale. The report also evaluated[Pega Blueprint AI™](https://www.pega.com/blueprint) , Pega’s design agent that transforms operational insights into agentic workflows.


This report is among Pega's recent analyst recognition for its enterprise AI and automation capabilities. Pega was recognized as a Leader in the[Gartner® Magic Quadrant for Process Intelligence Platforms](https://www.pega.com/gartner-process-intelligence-2026) (2),[The Forrester Wave™: Real-Time Interaction Management Software (RTIMS), Q4 2025 report](https://www.pega.com/forrester-rtim-2025) (3), the[Gartner® Magic Quadrant for Business Orchestration and Automation Technologies](https://www.pega.com/gartner-boat-mq-2025) (4),[The Forrester Wave™ for Digital Process Automation, Q3 2025 report](https://www.pega.com/forrester-dpa-2025) (5), the[Gartner® Magic Quadrant for Process Mining Platforms 2025](https://www.pega.com/gartner-process-mining-2025) (6), and a Leader in[The Forrester Wave™: AI Decisioning Platforms, Q2 2025](https://www.pega.com/forrester-ai-2025) (7). For more background and additional analyst reports, visit[www.pega.com/analyst-reports](http://www.pega.com/analyst-reports) .


**Quotes & Commentary:**
“As organizations continue to invest in AI, many are still seeking effective ways to orchestrate AI across people, systems, and workflows to deliver measurable business outcomes,” said Kerim Akgonul, chief product officer, Pega. “We believe this recognition from Forrester further validates Pega’s approach to enterprise AI. By bringing together governance, AI, workflow automation, and decisioning in a single platform, Pega enables organizations to move from AI design to execution faster and with confidence, while its no per-token pricing model helps ensure predictable outcomes, predictable costs, and maximum business value.”
