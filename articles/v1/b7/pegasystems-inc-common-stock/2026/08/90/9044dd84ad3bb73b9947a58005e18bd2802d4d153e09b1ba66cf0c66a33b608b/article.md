# Pega Announces Special Cash Dividend


**WALTHAM, Mass. – August 14, 2026** –[Pegasystems Inc.](https://www.pega.com/) (NASDAQ: PEGA), the enterprise AI software company for mission-critical work, today announced a per-share rate of approximately $0.083 for the special cash dividend of $7 million that Pega previously announced on July 31, 2026, which is payable on September 16, 2026 to shareholders of record as of August 17, 2026, excluding certain shareholders as described below.


As previously disclosed, Pega entered into a settlement agreement to resolve the consolidated shareholder derivative litigation captioned Dwyer et al. v. Trefler et al., Civ. No. 2484CV01734 (Mass. Super. Ct.) and Larkin et al. v. Trefler et al., No. 1:25-cv-10303-WGY (D. Mass.), under which Pega agreed to adopt certain governance reforms and to pay a special cash dividend of $7 million to shareholders, excluding certain shareholders identified in the agreement. The litigation has been dismissed with prejudice, and the appeal period applicable to the settlement agreement expired on July 25, 2026.
