# MOD Pizza and PAR Technology Celebrate Over 10 Years of Partnership, Surpassing 5M Loyalty Members

**MOD Pizza and PAR Technology Celebrate Over 10 Years of Partnership, Surpassing** **5M Loyalty Members**


*New Recovery and Delivery Operations modules reinforce MOD’s commitment to innovation and operational excellence*


**NEW HARTFORD, N.Y. (April 2, 2026)** –[PAR Technology Corporation](https://partech.com/?utm_source=website&utm_medium=press_release&utm_campaign=&utm_content=mod-pizza-announcement&utm_term=) (NYSE: PAR), a leading foodservice technology provider, is proud to celebrate over a decade of partnership with[MOD Pizza](https://modpizza.com/) , a purpose-led fast-casual pizza brand with 450 locations across North America. Since 2014, MOD Pizza has unified its technology stack with PAR’s integrated ecosystem, including[PAR POS™](https://partech.com/solutions/cloud-pos/?utm_source=website&utm_medium=press_release&utm_campaign=&utm_content=mod-pizza-announcement&utm_term=) ,[PAR OPS®](https://partech.com/solutions/back-office-solutions/?utm_source=website&utm_medium=press_release&utm_campaign=&utm_content=mod-pizza-announcement&utm_term=) ,[PAR® Pay](https://partech.com/solutions/payment-services/?utm_source=website&utm_medium=press_release&utm_campaign=&utm_content=mod-pizza-announcement&utm_term=) , and[PAR® Punchh®](https://partech.com/solutions/guest-engagement-platform/restaurant-loyalty-software/?utm_source=website&utm_medium=press_release&utm_campaign=&utm_content=mod-pizza-announcement&utm_term=) , enabling streamlined operations and memorable guest experiences. The partnership has driven major milestones, including the launch of MOD’s loyalty program in 2018, surpassing 5 million loyalty members in 2025.


“Our journey with PAR Technology has been instrumental in MOD Pizza’s growth and ability to deliver exceptional experiences to our guests,” said Tia Wolfe, Director of Restaurant Marketing at MOD Pizza. “We value the reliability and innovation PAR brings to the table, and we’re excited to continue our partnership as we look ahead to the next chapter.”


This milestone marks MOD Pizza’s continued commitment to innovation and operational excellence, now further strengthened by the adoption of[PAR OPS® Recovery](https://partech.com/solutions/insights-and-delivery-solutions/delivery-loss-disputing-recovery/?utm_source=website&utm_medium=press_release&utm_campaign=&utm_content=mod-pizza-announcement&utm_term=) and[PAR OPS® Delivery Operations](https://partech.com/solutions/insights-and-delivery-solutions/delivery-reconciliation/?utm_source=website&utm_medium=press_release&utm_campaign=&utm_content=mod-pizza-announcement&utm_term=) solutions. With access to these modules, MOD Pizza will gain:


- A fully automated delivery revenue recovery solution built for pizza brands and other delivery‑forward restaurants using third‑party platforms, helping operators protect and optimize a business‑critical revenue stream.
- A third‑party delivery management and reconciliation solution that streamlines delivery operations, reduces losses, and improves profitability across pizza’s most important channel.


“MOD Pizza understands that great guest experiences start with strong operations,” said Savneet Singh, CEO of PAR Technology. “By expanding with Recovery and Delivery Operations, they’re deepening their use of a unified PAR platform to run their business more effectively. Bringing our solutions together gives MOD the consistency and visibility needed to perform better every day, and we’re proud to support them as they continue to scale.”


This milestone sets PAR apart from newer competitors, demonstrating the long-term value and trust that comes from partnering with an established technology provider.

**About MOD Pizza**
Founded in 2008 in Seattle, MOD Pizza is a purpose-led fast-casual pizza brand offering artisan-style pizzas and salads made on demand. With more than 460 locations across the U.S. and Canada, MOD is known for its “pizza with a purpose” philosophy—providing customers unlimited toppings for one set price while fostering a culture of opportunity, belonging, and community. MOD prioritizes fresh, high-quality ingredients and partners on initiatives that support local communities and individuals facing barriers to employment. Learn more at modpizza.com.
