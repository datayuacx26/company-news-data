# Complete Guide to ServiceTitan

If you’re a residential or commercial contractor looking for a system to manage your business, chances are you’ve come across[ServiceTitan](https://www.servicetitan.com/) in your research. ServiceTitan is a popular cloud-based platform that supports customer booking, scheduling, dispatch, field work, invoicing, reporting, and several other workflows used by trade businesses.


ServiceTitan’s extensive list of features is its main appeal, but it’s also what makes the buying decision harder. This guide explains what ServiceTitan does, who it’s built for, how pricing works, and what to consider before committing to the platform.


‍


## What is ServiceTitan?


ServiceTitan is cloud-based business management software built for residential and commercial trade contractors. It brings customer relationship management, field service management, project operations, financial tools, and payments into one platform.


Contractors use ServiceTitan to manage work from earning leads with marketing efforts to first customer inquiry through job completion and payment. Information collected during that process feeds into reporting tools that help you track revenue, job performance, marketing results, and technician activity.


ServiceTitan reported approximately 10,800 active customers at the end of its 2026 fiscal year. They primarily serve businesses in HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage door services, pest control, landscaping, and other related trades.

## Who is ServiceTitan for?


ServiceTitan software is built for residential and commercial trade businesses, serving industries including:


- HVAC
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- Roofing
- Garage doors
- Chimney services
- Water treatment
- Pool services
- Septic services
- Pest control
- Landscaping and lawn care


The platform supports service and replacement work as well as construction and remodeling projects.


In practice, ServiceTitan tends to be a better fit for established or growing contractors that have multiple technicians, dedicated office employees, and enough operational complexity to justify a substantial platform.


For example, your company might benefit from ServiceTitan if you need to


- Coordinate several crews
- Manage recurring service agreements
- Track performance by technician
- Understand which marketing campaigns generate booked revenue


Solo contractors or companies with only a few employees should evaluate ServiceTitan more carefully. If you’re a solo operator who mainly needs basic scheduling and invoicing features, you could end up paying for tools you never use. ServiceTitan might also be the wrong fit if you don’t have a dedicated person who can take ownership of setup, training, and ongoing admin.


That said, company size isn’t the only consideration. ServiceTitan could still make sense for you if you’re preparing to add branches, expand into new trades, or replace multiple disconnected systems you’re currently using.

## ServiceTitan pricing


ServiceTitan doesn’t publish exact pricing. Their[pricing page](https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing) indicates that subscriptions are priced per technician, so contractors need to request a custom quote based on their business and goals.


**The company currently lists three packages:**


Package Description


Starter Must-have tools for running the business, including dispatching, scheduling, call booking, invoicing, and pricebook.


Essentials A broader set of operational capabilities. Includes everything in the Starter package plus mobile estimates and payroll management.


The Works The full ServiceTitan suite. Includes everything in the Essentials package plus advanced reporting, commission tracking, and customizable memberships.


‍


ServiceTitan also offers Pro Products for functions like marketing, contact center operations, pricebook management, fleet management, scheduling, dispatch, and field sales. If you’re thinking about investing in ServiceTitan, make sure you confirm which products are included in your quote and which require an additional subscription.


And before comparing ServiceTitan’s quote with another platform, ask for a complete cost breakdown that covers:


- The price for every user or technician type
- Onboarding, configuration, and data migration
- Training and ongoing support
- Pro Products and other add-ons
- Payment processing or usage-based charges
- Contract length and renewal terms


This gives you a better picture of the total investment than the base subscription alone.

## ServiceTitan features


ServiceTitan’s software covers a wide stretch of the contractor workflow, from the first customer call through job completion and payment. Its exact capabilities depend on your package and any add-on products, but the core platform generally supports seven areas:


‍


### CRM and sales management


ServiceTitan’s CRM keeps customer and job information connected so your team doesn’t have to piece together the full story from call notes, inboxes, and spreadsheets.


A customer record can include:


- Contact information and service locations
- Previous jobs and equipment history
- Calls, messages, estimates, and invoices
- Open opportunities and unsold estimates
- Membership or service agreement details
- Custom fields and information


Sales teams can use that information to follow leads, manage opportunities, and see where potential revenue is getting stuck. Managers can also review close rates and sales performance by employee, campaign, or business unit.


**Key benefit:** The customer service rep answering the phone, the dispatcher assigning the job, and the technician arriving on-site can all work from the same history.


‍


### Construction management software


ServiceTitan’s construction management tools are built for specialty contractors who need tighter control over crews, project costs, documentation, and billing. Contractors that handle both service work *and* longer construction projects will benefit most from these features, since both sides of the business can run through the same system.


Project managers can use ServiceTitan to:


- Build project plans and track progress in real time
- Schedule crews based on availability and project demand
- Capture time, materials, and daily field activity
- Create and track RFIs and change orders
- Store plans, estimates, purchase orders, and other documents
- Compare project budgets with actual costs as work progresses
- Monitor labor allocation and identify potential cost overruns
- Generate progress billing applications that follow AIA standards


Field updates flow back into project plans, budgets, and financial reports, which makes it easier to see when labor is running over, materials are pushing a phase over budget, or an approved change order needs to be reflected in billing. Daily logs can also capture activity from the field and generate summaries for the office.


All this said, ServiceTitan *isn’t* a general construction management platform built to coordinate an entire ground-up development. Its construction tools are geared more toward trade contractors that need to manage both service work and larger projects without splitting the business across separate systems.


‍


### Service operations


For contractors who operate with a high volume of appointments, scheduling and dispatch features are crucial to keeping each workday on track.


ServiceTitan provides tools to:


- Schedule and reschedule appointments
- Assign technicians based on availability
- View job status from the dispatch board
- Track recurring service agreements
- Manage customer and equipment records
- Adjust the day as jobs run long or urgent calls come in


Dispatchers can see where technicians are scheduled and make changes as conditions shift. That visibility becomes more valuable as your company adds trucks, locations, or service lines.


Commercial contractors can also use ServiceTitan to manage equipment records, service activity, and more complex billing relationships. Instead of treating each visit as an isolated job, ServiceTitan helps you maintain a longer history for the property and its assets.


‍


### Field operations


ServiceTitan’s field mobile app brings customer and job information out of the office and puts it right into a technician’s hands.


Depending on how you configure the platform, technicians can use the app to:


- Review job details and customer history
- Access forms, checklists, and equipment records
- Document findings with notes and photos
- Build and present estimates
- Record labor and time
- Collect signatures and payments
- Update the job before leaving the site


**Key benefit** : Cut down on calls back to the office and reduce the amount of information that has to be reentered later.


‍


### Accounting and financial insights


ServiceTitan connects operations with the financial side of the job. That gives owners and finance teams more context than they would get from invoices alone.


Its financial tools can support:


- Job costing
- Purchase orders
- Inventory tracking
- Accounts receivable and payable
- Payroll and technician commissions
- Invoice and payment management
- Financial and operational reporting


ServiceTitan also integrates with accounting and ERP systems. Before adopting ServiceTitan for its accounting features, decide which platform will remain as your financial system of record.


‍


### Client experience


ServiceTitan includes tons of customer-facing tools designed to make the service process easier to follow.


Features include:


- Online appointment booking
- Appointment confirmations and reminders
- Two-way text messaging
- Technician arrival updates
- Digital estimates and proposals
- Financing options
- Electronic invoices and payments


These features give customers more clarity without requiring your team to manually send every update.


For example, a customer can effortlessly receive an appointment reminder, see when the technician is on the way, approve an estimate, and pay the invoice electronically. That creates a more consistent experience while reducing routine status calls.


‍


### Marketing


ServiceTitan’s marketing features focus on connecting lead generation with booked work and revenue.


The platform can help you:


- Track where calls and leads came from
- Connect campaigns to booked jobs
- Measure revenue by marketing source
- Review booking and conversion rates
- Follow up on unsold estimates
- Build customer audiences for future campaigns


This helps you actually see whether leads turn into real appointments and paying jobs.


Additional marketing automation is available through Marketing Pro, one of ServiceTitan’s add-on products. Before purchasing it, confirm which tools are included in your core package and which require a separate subscription.

## ServiceTitan user guide: How to get the most out of ServiceTitan


ServiceTitan has a learning curve. Getting value from the platform depends on how well your team configures it, learns the workflows, and keeps the data within it accurate.


**These best practices can help your company get more from its ServiceTitan subscription:**


‍


### Assign clear owners during implementation


Choose one internal leader to oversee the rollout, then assign workstream owners for major areas like dispatch, accounting, and field operations.


ServiceTitan’s onboarding process encourages companies to use workstream leads who understand how each part of the business operates. Those employees can answer configuration questions, make decisions, and confirm that the system reflects the company’s actual workflows.


Without clear ownership, setup decisions tend to fall between departments, which can lead to inconsistent processes that are a pain to fix after launch.


‍


### Clean up your data before moving it


ServiceTitan is only as useful as the information you give it. Before migrating your data (customer records, pricebooks, memberships, employee data, or equipment history), review what you already have.


Look for:


- Duplicate customer records
- Outdated pricing
- Inconsistent job types
- Inactive employees or technicians
- Missing equipment information
- Campaign names that don’t follow a shared format


Taking the time to clean up your data early will make your reports more reliable and reduce confusion once the platform becomes part of your daily operations.


‍


### Train employees around their actual roles


A dispatcher, technician, CSR, and accounting employee won’t use ServiceTitan in the same way, so training efforts should reflect that.


ServiceTitan Academy offers courses, videos, learning plans, and topic-specific catalogs for different roles and business needs. ServiceTitan also provides implementation training, one-on-one sessions, custom programs, and expanded learning programs for certain teams.


‍


### Start with the workflows you need most


Trying to configure every available feature at once can make it harder to implement ServiceTitan than it needs to be.


Start with the workflows that your business uses every day, like:


1. Booking customers
2. Scheduling and dispatching jobs
3. Giving technicians the right information
4. Creating estimates or invoices
5. Collecting payment
6. Sending accurate data to accounting


Once those processes are working consistently, you can introduce more advanced reporting, automation, marketing, inventory, and add-on products.


‍


### Build dashboards around decisions


ServiceTitan gives you access to many different reports and KPIs, but that doesn’t mean you need to monitor all of them.


Choose a focused set of metrics tied to the decisions you make most often, like:


- Booking rate
- Average ticket
- Technician close rate
- Revenue by business unit
- Job completion rate
- Accounts receivable
- Campaign-generated revenue
- Budget versus actual project costs


The goal is to make important changes easy to spot. A dashboard that no one checks won’t move the needle.


‍


## Is ServiceTitan worth it?


ServiceTitan reviews tend to follow a consistent pattern: Users praise the broad list of features, scheduling and dispatch tools, reporting, and the ability to manage more of the company in one system. But common complaints mention the learning curve, customer support experiences, and the time required to configure the platform around your unique business.


ServiceTitan is also regularly described as a premium-priced option. That doesn’t automatically make it a poor investment, it just means you’ll only see ROI if your business can use the platform fully and has someone to manage it internally.


‍


Factor ServiceTitan may be worth it when… Consider a simpler option when…


Company structure You have several field and office roles that need to work from the same information. One or two people handle nearly every part of the business.


Operations Scheduling, dispatch, reporting, and customer data are spread across several systems. You mainly need a calendar, estimates, and invoices.


Budget You can fund software, onboarding, and internal administration. Low-cost solutions are a priority.


Growth You're adding technicians, branches, trades, or commercial services. Your operation is staying small and relatively simple.


Implementation Someone can own configuration, training, and adoption. No one has time to manage a large system change.


Reporting You'll actively use job, technician, revenue, and marketing data. Basic financial reports give you enough visibility.


‍


Before deciding, identify the specific gains you expect ServiceTitan to deliver, whether that’s faster invoicing, more consistent estimates, stronger follow-up, or better job-cost visibility. Then compare those gains with the full cost and the time your team will spend maintaining the system.

## FAQ


### What companies use ServiceTitan?


ServiceTitan is used by residential and commercial contractors in HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage doors, pest control, landscaping, and other field service trades. Their website and case studies highlight customers like Mr. Rooter, Mr. Electric, and the multi-trade Tonkin Group.


The platform serves a range of company sizes, but its broad feature set is generally most relevant to contractors with multiple employees, growing service volume, and dedicated office operations.


‍


### How much is ServiceTitan per month?


ServiceTitan doesn’t publish a standard monthly price. The company uses custom per-technician pricing and offers Starter, Essentials, and The Works packages. Your final cost will depend on technician count, package selection, additional products, and implementation requirements.


Request a written quote that includes the subscription, onboarding, training, add-ons, and any usage-based fees.


‍


### Is ServiceTitan a CRM?


Yes, customer relationship management is one part of the ServiceTitan platform. It stores customer records and interactions while connecting them with scheduling, dispatch, estimates, field activity, invoices, payments, and reporting.


That said, ServiceTitan is more accurately described as an end-to-end business management and field service platform for trade contractors.
