### What ServiceTitan does


ServiceTitan is an all-in-one field service management platform for residential and[commercial contractors](https://www.permitflow.com/solutions/commercial-contractors) . It connects virtually all of the work that happens between generating a lead and getting a customer’s first call through the final payment, including:

- Estimates and proposals
- Invoicing and payments
- Financing
- Pricebook management
- Technician mobile workflows
- Inventory and purchasing
- Payroll and commission tracking
- Marketing attribution
- Reporting and dashboards


ServiceTitan prices its packages per technician and offers different feature tiers. For exact rates, you’ll need to request a quote directly from ServiceTitan.


‍


### Who typically uses ServiceTitan?


ServiceTitan is primarily used by established trade businesses in fields like HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and roofing. Its platform supports residential and commercial service, as well as construction workflows.


Growing contractors typically turn to ServiceTitan when separate scheduling, accounting, sales, and reporting tools can no longer keep up with the business. ServiceTitan gives the office and field a shared operating system, with enough depth to support larger teams and more structured processes.


‍


### Is ServiceTitan a CRM or ERP?


ServiceTitan includes a CRM, but it does far more than just store customer records and sales activity.


Think of ServiceTitan as a field service management (FSM) and business operations platform.


For some contractors, its job costing, purchasing, reporting, payroll, and inventory features can perform many ERP-like functions. Businesses with more complicated financial requirements may still pair it with a dedicated ERP (enterprise resource planning) or[accounting system](https://www.permitflow.com/blog/construction-accounting-software) .

- Integrations

### How hard is it to switch from ServiceTitan?


Your field service platform will be holding *years* of operational history, so replacing it is more than transferring a customer list.


**Keep in mind that the migration might involve:**


- Exporting customer, job, and equipment records
- Cleaning and mapping data
- Rebuilding pricebooks
- Recreating forms and workflows
- Reconnecting integrations
- Retraining dispatchers, CSRs, managers, and technicians
- Running both systems in parallel
- Managing a temporary drop in productivity


The larger the company and the more customized your ServiceTitan account, the more involved the move is likely to be.


‍


### When switching makes sense


Switching deserves serious consideration when:


- Employees consistently avoid the platform.
- Manual workarounds are affecting job execution.
- The business pays for substantial functionality it never uses.
- Another platform clearly fits the operating model better.
- The expected savings or performance gains justify the transition.


Low adoption is especially important. Software can’t produce accurate reporting when the underlying data is missing, late, or inconsistent.


‍


### When staying with ServiceTitan makes sense


Staying may be the better decision when:


- You rely on ServiceTitan’s reporting, marketing, accounting, inventory, or financing tools.
- The platform is deeply embedded in your operations.
- Employees use it consistently.
- The business has invested heavily in configuration and automation.
- Problems can be fixed through training, process changes, or integrations.


The cost of staying is easy to see on a software invoice. The cost of replacing a system that already works can be harder to spot until you’ve started migrating.

## FAQs


### Who are ServiceTitan’s biggest competitors?


Major ServiceTitan competitors include Jobber, Housecall Pro, Service Fusion, FieldEdge, Workiz, FieldPulse, BuildOps, ServiceTrade, Simpro, and Salesforce Field Service.


The closest competitor depends on the business. Jobber and Housecall Pro are common comparisons for smaller residential teams. BuildOps, ServiceTrade, and Simpro are more relevant to complex commercial operations.


‍


### What is the best ServiceTitan alternative for a small business?


Jobber and Housecall Pro are often recommended as the best ServiceTitan alternatives for small residential service businesses because they offer published pricing and cover core workflows without a massive implementation.


Service Fusion may be worth considering if your growing team wants unlimited users and a flat monthly price.


‍


### What is the best ServiceTitan alternative for commercial contractors?


BuildOps, ServiceTrade, and Simpro are three leading options for commercial contractors.


BuildOps supports commercial service and project operations. ServiceTrade is strong for recurring maintenance, assets, and inspections. Simpro fits businesses that need inventory, purchasing, job costing, and multi-stage project management.


‍


### Is ServiceNow the same as ServiceTitan?


No, they are separate companies and products.


ServiceTitan is built for contractors and field service businesses. ServiceNow is a broader enterprise workflow platform known for IT service management, incident management, employee workflows, and other large-company processes.


‍


### Who owns ServiceTitan?


ServiceTitan, Inc. is a publicly traded company listed on Nasdaq under the ticker TTAN. It is owned by its shareholders rather than a private parent company. Ara Mahdessian serves as co-founder and CEO, while Vahe Kuzoyan is co-founder and president.


‍


### What industries does ServiceTitan support besides HVAC and plumbing?


ServiceTitan supports electrical, roofing, garage door, water treatment, irrigation, septic, painting, pool service, landscaping, lawn care, pest control, air duct cleaning, and other residential and commercial trades. Some industries may be served through products or companies within the broader ServiceTitan portfolio.


‍


### When did ServiceTitan become popular with home service businesses?


ServiceTitan was founded in 2012 by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan. The company expanded quickly during the late 2010s as contractors looked for cloud-based alternatives to disconnected office and field systems. By 2019, ServiceTitan reported more than 2,500 client businesses, rising to more than 5,000 by the end of 2020. It’s now a public company serving residential and commercial contractors.
