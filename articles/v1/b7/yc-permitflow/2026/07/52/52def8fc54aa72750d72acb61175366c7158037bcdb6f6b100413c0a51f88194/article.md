## What are construction permit services?


Construction permit services are permitting solutions that help owners, contractors, developers, and other stakeholders pull permits. These services may be provided by an internal permit coordinator, a permit expediter, or a modern permit management platform.


When you seek out building permit services, your primary goal is probably to free up your team’s time by taking tedious, manual work off your plate. But permitting services can also reduce delays, improve compliance, and get permits approved faster. The downstream impacts: increased first-pass approval rates, faster timelines to realizing revenue, more accurate cash flow projections, and greater ability to scale your business.


**Typical construction permit services include:**


- Identifying required permits
- Filling out a building permit application
- Submitting plans for building permits
- Coordinating with building departments
- Managing comments and resubmittals
- Tracking status updates
- Scheduling inspections
- Organizing documentation
- Closing out approved permits


‍


### Permit management services vs. permit expediting services


These terms are often used interchangeably, but they’re not exactly the same.


**Permit management services** focus on full permit lifecycle management across one or many projects in one or many jurisdictions. This includes preparation, submissions, tracking, visibility, reporting, and process standardization.


**Permit expediting services** are usually person-led services focused on helping a project move through the approval process. A[permit expediter](https://www.permitflow.com/blog/permit-expediter) often uses local knowledge and relationships to navigate city processes. Some find them helpful for urgent or highly specific projects.

### Option 2: Permit expediters


[Permit expediters](https://www.permitflow.com/blog/permit-expediter) are third-party professionals who help move a permit application through the approval process for a specific project.


They know the ins and outs of local building departments, understand common pitfalls, and often have established relationships with government bodies that review building or construction plans. A good permit expediter can help reduce friction by understanding local processes, coordinating with departments, and managing follow-up.


**When you might use a permit expediter:**


- In cities with long or confusing permit processes
- For last-minute projects on tight timelines
- If your internal team lacks experience or bandwidth


‍


**Typical pricing structure for construction expediting services:**


- **Hourly rates** : $75 – $150+ per hour depending on market rates
- **Flat fees** : $500 – $2,000+ per permit, depending on project complexity and location
- **Bundled rates** : For multiple simultaneous permits or ongoing relationships


While expediters can save time, they also add a significant cost to your budget. And more importantly, they don’t always provide the necessary[transparency](https://www.permitflow.com/blog/maximizing-transparency-construction) needed throughout the permit process. Worst case scenario: expediters may not expedite the process at all, becoming just another cost center that needs to be actively managed.


‍


#### Pros and cons of permit expediters


**Pros:**


- Deep local knowledge
- May be able to accelerate timelines
- Handles the back-and-forth with city officials


**Cons:**


- Expensive for ongoing or multi-location projects
- Limited visibility into permit status
- No guarantee of approval speed
- Creates a dependency
- Potentially poor communication, especially if the expediter has other clients

## FAQs


### What is expediting in construction?


Expediting in construction refers to helping permits, approvals, materials, or project tasks move through the process faster. In the context of permitting, it often means managing submissions, coordinating with local departments, responding to comments, and reducing avoidable delays.


‍


### What's the best construction permit service for contractors?


For most contractors, permit management software is the best construction permit service because it provides faster workflows, better visibility, and a process that can scale as project volume grows. Unlike one-off permit expediters or manual in-house methods, permitting software helps contractors manage multiple permits, jurisdictions, and deadlines in one system.


‍


### How can I get construction permits faster?


The best ways to get building permits faster are identifying requirements early, submitting complete plans, responding quickly to requests for corrections, tracking permit statuses proactively, and using permit management software to keep the process organized.


‍


### How much do construction permit services cost?


Costs vary based on the provider, project type, and scope of work. Permit expediters may charge hourly or per project, while permit management software often uses more predictable pricing tied to usage or volume.


‍


### Is permit management software worth it?


For teams pulling permits regularly, permit management software is worth it because it reduces administrative workload, improves visibility, and helps prevent delays caused by missed steps or disorganized workflows. It becomes especially valuable for contractors, builders, and developers managing multiple projects or jurisdictions at once.
