## What requires a building permit in Schenectady?


In the City of Schenectady, most construction, repair, and alteration work requires a permit. The city applies this requirement broadly, so even smaller projects can trigger permitting depending on the scope.


**Projects that typically require a building permit include:**


- New construction
- Additions and enlargements
- Alterations and improvements
- Moving or relocating a structure
- Demolition
- Installation of a solid-fuel-burning heating appliance, chimney, or flue in a dwelling unit
- Repairs that affect structure, egress, building systems, or fire protection


‍


### What doesn’t require a building permit?


[Schenectady’s code](https://ecode360.com/33201156) lists these exemptions:


- One-story detached tool sheds, storage sheds, playhouses, and similar structures associated with 1- and 2-family homes or townhouses, if they are 144 sq. ft. or less
- Swings and other playground equipment for 1- and 2-family homes or townhouses
- Above-ground swimming pools for 1- and 2-family homes or townhouses if designed for a water depth of less than 24 inches
- Fences that are not part of a pool enclosure
- Retaining walls, unless they support a surcharge or impound Class I, II, or IIIA liquids
- Temporary motion-picture, television, and theater stage sets and scenery
- Window awnings supported by an exterior wall of a 1- or 2-family home or townhouse
- Partitions or movable cases under 5 feet 9 inches high
- Painting, wallpapering, tiling, carpeting, and similar finish work
- Installation of listed portable electrical, plumbing, heating, ventilation, cooling equipment, or appliances

## How long do building permits last in Schenectady?


Schenectady building permits are valid for 12 months. Work authorized by the permit must begin within 30 days after issuance and must be completed within one year, unless the Building Inspector grants a written extension for good cause.

## How to get a building permit in Schenectady


Building permits in the City of Schenectady are issued by the[City Building Department](https://www.cityofschenectady.gov/177/Building-Inspector-Code-Enforcement) . Residential and commercial construction projects must follow the city’s application, review, and inspection process, which is administered locally and enforced under the New York State Uniform Fire Prevention and Building Code and Energy Code.


‍


### Step 1: Prepare your plans and documents


The City of Schenectady requires a complete submittal before plan review begins. Incomplete applications can delay review or result in rejection.


**Typical submittal requirements include:**


- **Detailed scope of work:** A written description of the proposed construction, including materials, dimensions, and structural changes
- **Construction cost estimate:** Used to calculate permit fees
- **Stamped plans (when required):** Projects involving structural work or requiring design professionals must be prepared and stamped by a New York State Licensed Architect or Professional Engineer
- **Workers’ compensation documentation:** Submit Form C-105.2, U-26.3, or CE-200 exemption form, as applicable
- **Contractor information:** Trade work such as electrical or plumbing must be performed by properly licensed contractors where required by city regulations


‍


### Step 2: Submit your application


The City of Schenectady accepts building permit applications through its permitting system and by mail or fax (518-372-9459) to the Building Inspector’s Office. The city does not accept applications by email.


- [Online form for building permits](https://www.cityofschenectady.gov/698/BUILDING-PERMIT-APPLICATION-ONLINE-FORM)
- [Online form for electrical permits](https://www.cityofschenectady.gov/726/ELECTRICAL-PERMIT-APPLICATION-ONLINE-FOR)


**Contact information:**


- Address: Building Inspector’s Office, City Hall Room 17, 105 Jay Street, Schenectady, NY 12305-1905
- Phone: (518) 382-5050


‍


### Step 3: Plan review


City staff review your application for compliance with local zoning requirements and the New York State Uniform Code.


**This review may include:**


- **Zoning compliance check:** Verifies setbacks, lot coverage, use, and dimensional requirements
- **Code compliance review:** Confirms structural, life-safety, and building system requirements
- **Flood zone review (if applicable):** Required for properties located in mapped flood hazard areas


If corrections are needed, the Building Department will request revisions before approval.


‍


### Step 4: Pay final fees and receive your permit


After approval, the city calculates final fees based on the declared construction value. Once payment is made, the building permit is issued.


The permit must be posted in a visible location at the job site for the duration of construction.


‍


### Step 5: Begin work and schedule inspections


Inspections are required throughout construction to verify compliance and close out the permit.


Permits in the City of Schenectady are typically valid for 12 months. If work is not completed within that timeframe, you may need to request an extension or renewal before the permit expires.


‍


## Permit inspections in Schenectady


In the City of Schenectady, building permit inspections are performed by the City Building Department. Inspections are required at key stages of construction and must be scheduled before work continues to the next phase.


‍


### Common required inspections


Depending on the project scope, Schenectady may require inspections such as:


- Footing or foundation inspection
- Foundation wall inspection
- Framing or rough-in inspection
- Plumbing, electrical, or mechanical rough inspections (when applicable)
- Insulation inspection (when required by energy code)
- Final building inspection


Additional inspections may be required for structural work, life-safety systems, or specialty installations.


‍


### How to schedule an inspection


You can schedule an inspection by calling the City of Schenectady Building Department or submitting the appropriate online request form.


- [General inspection request form](https://www.cityofschenectady.gov/693/INSPECTION-REQUEST-ONLINE-FORM)
- [Electrical inspection request form](https://www.cityofschenectady.gov/789/ELECTRICAL-INSPECTON-REQUEST-ONLINE-FORM)
- [Plumbing inspection request form](https://www.cityofschenectady.gov/790/PLUMBING-INSPECTION-REQUEST-ONLINE-FORM)


To avoid delays, make sure:


- Approved plans are available on-site
- The permit is posted and visible
- Work being inspected is fully accessible
- Required trade inspections are completed before final approval
