NORTHBROOK, Ill., July 16, 2026 – The Allstate Corporation (NYSE: ALL) today announced estimated catastrophe losses for the month of June of $563 million or $445 million, after-tax. Total catastrophe losses for the second quarter were $1.72 billion or $1.36 billion, after-tax.
