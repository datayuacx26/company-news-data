NORTHBROOK, Ill., August 5, 2026 – The Allstate Corporation (NYSE: ALL) has filed a Form 8-K reporting its financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. You can find the Form 8-K, including the earnings release and investor supplement, on the[company’s page at sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000899051&owner=exclude&count=40&hidefilings=0) . These materials are also now available at[www.allstateinvestors.com](https://www.allstateinvestors.com/) , including the[press release](https://www.allstateinvestors.com/static-files/9a7f61ff-f520-4046-8b84-5464fa15c58c) .

About Allstate
