# Lattice to Showcase Industry-Leading FPGA Innovations at FPGA Conference Europe 2026

[PDF Version](https://ir.latticesemi.com/node/26991/pdf)


HILLSBORO, Ore.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 16, 2026--[Lattice Semiconductor](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.latticesemi.com%2F&esheet=54554316&newsitemid=20260616903720&lan=en-US&anchor=Lattice+Semiconductor&index=1&md5=16c87d3364c4d46313cf69ba98a9d6cb) (NASDAQ: LSCC), the low power programmable leader, today announced its exhibition plan for the upcoming[FPGA Conference Europe](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.fpga-conference.eu%2F&esheet=54554316&newsitemid=20260616903720&lan=en-US&anchor=FPGA+Conference+Europe&index=2&md5=6a453a29d0acf8f9b05d55d696b921d0) . Lattice will participate in various speaker sessions to showcase how low power FPGAs are driving innovation across multiple sectors. The company will also demonstrate its latest FPGA technology advancements in physical AI, advanced connectivity, security, and more.


- **Who:** Lattice Semiconductor

- June 30 – July 2: Lattice Presentations & Demo Showcase
