# Lattice to Deliver Keynote at 2026 OCP Global Summit

[PDF Version](https://ir.latticesemi.com/node/27176/pdf)


HILLSBORO, Ore.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 12, 2026--[Lattice Semiconductor](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.latticesemi.com%2F&esheet=54587537&newsitemid=20260812187250&lan=en-US&anchor=Lattice+Semiconductor&index=1&md5=dc6baae24bc35ef8f986147d7d6974e9) (NASDAQ: LSCC), the low power programmable and platform firmware leader, today announced that Lattice will deliver a keynote at the 2026 OCP Global Summit. Ford Tamer, President and CEO of Lattice Semiconductor, will take the stage with Sanjoy Maity, Senior Vice President, AMI Business Unit Leader, Lattice Semiconductor. Together, they will discuss the growing need for a common management and control model for AI and compute infrastructure, and how the OCP Community can work together to bring this vision to life with existing open technologies. The keynote will take place on Tuesday, Oct. 13, 2026, at 10 a.m. PT at the San Jose Convention Center (SJCC), South Hall, San Jose, California.


The OCP Summit is the premier event uniting the most forward-thinking minds in open IT Ecosystem development. The Summit presents a unique platform for the OCP community from around the globe to share their insights, foster partnerships, and showcase cutting-edge advancements in open hardware and software. At the 2026 Global Summit, industry leaders, researchers and pioneers from the open community will engage in dynamic dialogues, enlightening workshops, and interactive engineering sessions.
