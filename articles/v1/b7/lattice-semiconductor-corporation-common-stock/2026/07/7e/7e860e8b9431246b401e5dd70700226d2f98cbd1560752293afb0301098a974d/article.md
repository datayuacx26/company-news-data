# Lattice Semiconductor Schedules Second Quarter 2026 Results Conference Call

[PDF Version](https://ir.latticesemi.com/node/27076/pdf)


HILLSBORO, Ore.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 23, 2026--[Lattice Semiconductor Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.latticesemi.com%2F&esheet=54575568&newsitemid=20260723400664&lan=en-US&anchor=Lattice+Semiconductor+Corporation&index=1&md5=4f18313f0f06a344683148b73b554054) (Nasdaq: LSCC), the low power programmable leader, today announced that it will hold its second quarter 2026 conference call on Tuesday, August 4, 2026. Ford Tamer, Chief Executive Officer, and Lorenzo Flores, Chief Financial Officer, will discuss Lattice Semiconductor’s financial results and business outlook.


The dial-in number for the live audio call beginning on Tuesday, August 4, 2026, at 5 p.m. Eastern Time is 1-877-407-3982 or 1-201-493-6780 with conference identification number 13761016. A live webcast of the conference call will also be available on the investor relations section of[www.latticesemi.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.latticesemi.com&esheet=54575568&newsitemid=20260723400664&lan=en-US&anchor=www.latticesemi.com&index=2&md5=7960dd4902cadd7d890b4d5e34db12cc) .


**About Lattice Semiconductor**


Lattice Semiconductor (Nasdaq: LSCC) is the low power programmable leader. We solve customer problems across the network, from the Edge to the Cloud, in the growing Communications, Computing, Industrial, Automotive, and Consumer markets.
