# Lattice Named to TIME’s America’s Best Companies 2026 List

[PDF Version](https://ir.latticesemi.com/node/27026/pdf)


HILLSBORO, Ore.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 9, 2026--[Lattice Semiconductor](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.latticesemi.com%2F&esheet=54565814&newsitemid=20260709198957&lan=en-US&anchor=Lattice+Semiconductor&index=151&md5=207eb717a4210dcfaa5ab99ac98d3f9b) (NASDAQ: LSCC), the low power programmable leader, today announced that the company was named one of America’s Best Companies 2026 by TIME and Statista Inc. TIME’s annual list is identified based on employee satisfaction, revenue growth, and sustainability transparency amongst companies that operate in the United States and generated at least $100 million in revenue in 2025.


"We are honored to be recognized by TIME as one of America's Best Companies for the second consecutive year," said Ford Tamer, Chief Executive Officer at Lattice. "This achievement reflects our relentless focus on innovation, our commitment to delivering industry-leading low power programmable solutions to our customers worldwide, and the incredible dedication of our global team. We are excited about the tremendous opportunities ahead as we continue expanding our product and solutions portfolio and working hand-in-hand with our customers to define the next era of datacenter and edge computing."


The full award list can be viewed on[TIME](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftime.com%2F&esheet=54565814&newsitemid=20260709198957&lan=en-US&anchor=TIME&index=152&md5=df644eee834402eb9ae73611295e3ece) ’s website. This research and analysis service is based on[Statista.com](http://statista.com/) , the leading data and business intelligence portal that provides statistics, relevant business data, and various market and consumer studies and surveys.
