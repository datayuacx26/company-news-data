## **NMG Participates in the ROTH London Conference**


Nouveau Monde Graphite will be participating in the ROTH London Conference, held June 16–18, 2026 at the Four Seasons Hotel London at Park Lane.


Our President and CEO, Éric Desaulniers, and CFO, Charles-Olivier Tarte, will be on site to engage with institutional investors and partners through a series of pre-scheduled one-on-one and small group meetings.


Organized by ROTH Capital Partners, this invitation-only event brings together executive teams from over 80 companies across sectors, providing a focused setting for meaningful exchanges between company leadership and the global investment community.


Nouveau Monde Graphite executives will share the latest updates on our fully integrated advanced graphite materials platform, from ore to anode material, anchored in two key projects: the Phase-2 Matawinie Mine and the Bécancour Battery Materials Plants. The Final Investment Decision for the Phase-2 Matawinie Mine was confirmed in May 2026, with construction now actively underway. The momentum continues with the Final Investment Decision for our Bécancour Battery Materials Plants targeted for the second half of 2026, positioning NMG to deliver high-value, anode-ready materials in North America.


To request a meeting, please contact your ROTH representative.
