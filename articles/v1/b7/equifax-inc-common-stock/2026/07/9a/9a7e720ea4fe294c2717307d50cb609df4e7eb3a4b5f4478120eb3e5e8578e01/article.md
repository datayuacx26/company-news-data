# June 2026 U.S. National Consumer Credit Trends Report


July 30, 2026


### Highlights:


-


Total U.S. consumer debt reached $18.25 trillion as of June 2026, marking a 2.1% year-over-year increase, with mortgage debt comprising 74.1% of the total landscape.


-


Non-mortgage sectors showed continued growth, with outstanding balances on auto loans rising 2.9% to $1.73 trillion and bankcard balances increasing 3.9% to $1.11 trillion compared to June 2025.


As of June 2026, the monthly Equifax[Consumer Credit Trends Report](https://www.equifax.com/resource/-/asset/report/monthly-national-consumer-credit-trends-report-june-2026-portfolio/) showed that total U.S. consumer debt was $18.25 trillion, up 2.1% from June 2025. Mortgage debt, including home equity loans, accounted for $13.51 trillion, a 74.1% share of total consumer debt, while non-mortgage consumer debt totaled $4.73 trillion, with 36.5% of non-mortgage consumer debt attributable to auto loans and leases, and 27.2% attributable to student loans.


Outstanding balances on auto loans and leases increased 2.9% from June 2025, to $1.72 trillion in June 2026.


Outstanding balances on U.S. bankcards increased versus the prior month to $1.11 trillion in June 2026, up 3.9% from June 2025. Average bankcard utilization stood at 20.4%, which is a slight decrease from 20.8% in June 2025.

### Consumer Credit Observations:


-


As of June 2026, total U.S. consumer debt was $18.25 trillion, up 2.1% from June 2025.


-


Mortgage debt, including home equity loans, accounted for $13.51 trillion, making up 74.1% of total U.S. consumer debt.


-


First mortgages were 94.9% of mortgage debt outstanding.


-


Home Equity Lines of Credit (HELOCs) were 3.3% of mortgage debt outstanding.


-


Non-mortgage debt totaled $4.73 trillion in June 2026 and broke down into:


-


36.5% from auto loans and leases,


-


27.2% from student loans,


-


24.5% from credit card balances, and


-


11.8% from other products.


**Auto Loans and Leases Observations:**


-


Total outstanding balances on auto loans and leases increased 2.9% from June 2025 to $1.72 trillion.


-


The number of outstanding accounts in June 2026 was 87.9 million, a 0.9% increase year-over-year.


-


The severe balance delinquency (60+ days past due) rate in June 2026 was 1.42%, which is two basis points down from June of last year.


**Bankcard Portfolio Observations:**


-


Outstanding balances on bankcards increased 3.9% from June 2025 to $1.11 trillion.


-


The number of outstanding accounts was 598.4 million in June 2026, a 2.9% increase from June 2025.


-


The severe balance delinquency rate (60+ days past due) as of June 2026 was 2.71%, which is an 8 basis point decrease from June 2025 (2.79%).


-


Average bankcard utilization for June 2026 was 20.4%, compared to 20.8% in June of 2025.
