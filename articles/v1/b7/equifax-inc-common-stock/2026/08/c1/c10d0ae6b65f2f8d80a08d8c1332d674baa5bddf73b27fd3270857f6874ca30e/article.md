# Equifax Helps Guide Consumers Toward Their Financial ‘BEST’


July 29, 2026


#### Story highlights:


-


Certified Equifax employee volunteers deliver financial education to universities, nonprofits, and businesses, successfully reaching over 5,000 consumers across 10 countries to help them build and maintain positive credit.


-


In 2025, Equifax expanded its community outreach by partnering with credit unions, including Georgia United Credit Union, to train staff and equip members with actionable financial wellness and credit-building strategies.


-


Consumer knowledge is key to financial inclusion — helping people gain access to affordable, mainstream financial services.


Equifax strives to deliver on its Purpose of helping people live their financial best through a unique blend of data, analytics, and technology that helps financial opportunities become possible for more people.


It also supports this purpose by empowering individuals with credit knowledge and resources, including understanding their credit scores as they prepare for important life events.


#### BEST Credit Training Program Helps Uplift Communities


The Equifax BEST Credit Education program aims to equip participants with practical knowledge and tools to understand and navigate the world of credit effectively. Its mission is to help establish the behaviors that drive better access to credit, enabling informed financial decisions.


Equifax employee volunteers provide this education in local communities. To become a certified facilitator, employees with strong presentation skills receive comprehensive training, including an in-depth content review with subject matter experts, a knowledge quiz, and practical teach-back sessions.


Most sessions range from 30 to 300 participants from nonprofits, universities, and businesses who learn how to create personalized action plans to build and maintain positive credit.


In three years, the BEST Credit Training Program has grown to include 150 employee facilitators, reaching over 5,000 consumers in 10 countries. Equifax will train its newest program cohort in spring of 2027 to help expand financial knowledge where the company operates.


Learn more about the company’s commitment to[financial inclusion](https://www.equifax.com/about-equifax/financial-inclusion/) , including leveraging alternative data, such as rent, utilities, and telecommunications payments, to help more people access new financial opportunities.


#### Credit Union Members Gain New Knowledge


As the BEST Credit Training Program grows, so does the demand for its services. In 2025, the program expanded to credit union employees and customers. The program served 265 employees and members, including educating over 100 team members from Georgia United Credit Union so they could better assist members in achieving financial wellness.


“We always put our members first, and the credit training provided by Equifax volunteers helped our team members find a new way to do exactly that,” said Georgia United Credit Union CEO Laura King. “The BEST Credit Training Program will create a lasting impact for years to come by helping our members strengthen their financial futures.”


The sessions received an excellent rating — over 98.9% of attendees said they gained new knowledge — with praise for Equifax volunteers’ expertise, preparation, and friendliness.


On July 31, Georgia United Credit Union and thousands of others will celebrate “I Love My Credit Union Day,” an annual milestone marking the impact credit unions have on the nation’s economy.
