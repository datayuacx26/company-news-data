## What Is the VSee Robot?


Traditional telemedicine carts require staff to stop, retrieve, wheel, and set up the device before a physician can connect. In understaffed or overnight environments, that delay has real costs.


The VSee Robot eliminates that friction entirely. Powered by LiDAR navigation, it travels hospital corridors autonomously — day or night — and arrives at the patient’s bedside without any staff assist. Physicians connect within moments, in full clinical presence.


**Key capabilities:**


- Autonomous virtual rounding — no cart setup required
- Rapid telestroke & emergency response
- Multisite specialist coverage across ED and ICU
- After-hours and overnight coverage
- Medication and supply delivery via programmable drawers
- Automated patient triage and intake


**Technical highlights:** 30× optical zoom · Infrared night vision · LiDAR navigation · Surgical-grade Full HD display · Secure patient identification · Programmable supply drawers

## Frequently Asked Questions


**What is an autonomous AI telehealth robot?** A self-navigating clinical device that travels independently to a patient’s bedside so a remote physician can conduct a real-time virtual exam without onsite staff setup. The VSee Robot uses LiDAR navigation to move autonomously through hospital corridors, day or night.


**How is the VSee Robot different from a telemedicine cart?** A telemedicine cart requires staff to retrieve, transport, and set it up. The VSee Robot navigates to the patient on its own — no staff assist, less workload, faster time-to-care.


**What clinical use cases does the VSee Robot support?** Virtual rounding, telestroke, tele-ICU, multisite specialist coverage, after-hours care, patient triage, and medication/supply delivery.


**What is the VSee AI Workflow Engine?** A clinical AI middleware platform that lets health systems deploy 100+ AI tools — stroke diagnosis, AI SOAP notes, deterioration alerts, and more — without rebuilding EHR or IT infrastructure.


**Is VSee HIPAA compliant?** Yes. VSee holds FedRAMP High Authorization from HHS — the highest government cloud security certification — plus HIPAA, SOC 2, and GDPR compliance.


**How fast can VSee go live?** LA County onboarded 800 providers in under 7 days. Complex custom deployments typically go live in 11 weeks.
