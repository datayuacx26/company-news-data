# VSee Achieves HHS FedRAMP High ATO

‍


VSee is thrilled to announce that it has received its FedRAMP High Authority to Operate (ATO) from the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). This is the highest level of government security certification for cloud services, which means VSee is cleared to support sensitive healthcare needs across even more federal agencies.


‍


Dr. Milton Chen, our CEO has stated, “This is a major step in expanding our government partnerships and delivering resilient healthcare infrastructure when and where it’s needed most.”


‍


## What Does This Mean?


‍


With FedRAMP High ATO, federal agencies can now rely on VSee to provide secure telehealth and in-person healthcare services anywhere they're needed. Our platform is built for:


- enterprise-grade encryption, HIPAA compliance, and HIE interoperability
- hybrid telehealth and in-person workflows
- rapid multi-site activation


‍


## Proven in Real Emergencies


‍


We recently proved its critical value when a cyberattack crippled the IT systems of the Governor Juan F. Luis Hospital, the sole hospital on the U.S. Virgin Island of St. Croix. The VSee telemedicine disaster EMR system created for HHS ASPR (Assistant Secretary for Preparedness and Response enabled the hospital to stay operational and keep staff from burning out. Using VSee, hospital and ASPR responders swiftly facilitated emergency care and processed over 250 backlogged radiology studies.
