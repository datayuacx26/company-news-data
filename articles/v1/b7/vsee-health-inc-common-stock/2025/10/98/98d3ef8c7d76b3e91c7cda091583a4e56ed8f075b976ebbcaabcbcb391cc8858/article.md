### **2. Real-World Care Delivery**


Extending the reach of clinical training and community health:


‍


Lamar University – Clinical Workflows


- **Provider-to-Provider Consults:** Mobile carts enable collaboration between primary providers and specialists, bringing expert care to campus and beyond.
- **Community Outreach:** Home care kits empower students and clinicians to participate in remote school or home care visits, broadening care access.
- **Chronic Care Management (RPM):**[Real-time patient monitoring](https://vsee.com/remote-patient-monitoring/) allows students and supervisors to proactively manage and intervene on community health needs, supporting better outcomes.


### **3. Telehealth-Enabled Research**


Advancing research inclusivity, reach, and impact:


‍


Remote Interventions


- Studies on kidney disease, pediatric nutrition, and HIV home testing use VSee’s platform to enable secure, reliable, and continuous remote follow-up with research participants. (DOI.org/10.2196/resprot.7341)


Post-Stroke Rehab Clinical Trial


- NIH-funded clinical trial (NCT06682429) leverages VSee to deliver guided, at-home rehabilitation for stroke survivors, including virtual therapy sessions and remote assessment tools.


### **4. Remote Mentorship & Mental Health**


Multiplying the reach of expert mentorship and specialty care:


Psychiatric Nurse Mentoring


- Virtual supervision provides psychiatric nurse trainees with consistent access to specialist mentoring and mental health resources, especially in underserved and rural locations.
