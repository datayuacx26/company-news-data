# Customer Highlight: How a Major Imaging Company Reduced Patient Delays and Costs with VSee Remote Support

‍


‍


VSee is honored to be a telehealth technology partner for one of the world’s top five key players in the diagnostic imaging industry. For the last seven years, we have partnered together to level up the capabilities of medical imaging for improved patient care, workflow agility, and clinical collaboration.


‍


### **The Challenge: Finding the Right Expert at the Right Time**


For this global industry giant, one of the challenges its users face is getting to the right expertise at the right time to prevent imaging delays, rescheduling, and lost productivity. However, sometimes a phone call is not enough, the go-to technician is out sick, or multiple experts are needed to solve the problem. Its users need a quick way to connect to the right experts in real-time and to allow them to easily see a screen display or even to remotely control their scanning equipment.


‍


### **Enter VSee: Making Training and Expertise Instantly Available, Anywhere**


‍


‍
