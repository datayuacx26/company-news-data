# Winning Telehealth Grant Funding: Insights and Best Practices from Industry Leaders

## **How Rural Health Leaders Are Winning Telehealth Grants (and How You Can Too)**


Federal and state grants are fueling a digital health revolution in rural America—connecting clinics, broadband providers, and community partners to close stubborn access gaps.


‍


From the FCC and CMS to the Department of Commerce, billions in funding are being invested in projects that build broadband infrastructure, expand telehealth access, and increase digital inclusion. But turning those dollars into real outcomes takes more than a great idea—it takes partnerships, planning, and a story that funders believe in.


‍


Here’s how some innovators are doing it.


## **Real Stories. Real Results.**


**Chattanooga, TN: Turning Collaboration Into Funding Success**


By teaming up with a public broadband provider and a local hospice program, Chattanooga’s Enterprise Center built a 150‑organization network that shared knowledge and collected community data on everything from palliative care to household connectivity. That collaboration helped secure federal grants and laid the groundwork for a sustainable, region‑wide telehealth model.


***Why it worked:*** *Funders love scale and shared resources. Real partnerships make your proposal more credible and more competitive.*


‍


**Cleveland, OH: Fast‑Track Innovation From an FQHC**


Neighborhood Family Practice moved fast when the pandemic hit—pulling together a multi‑disciplinary team to apply for an FCC grant in record time. By choosing easy‑to‑use technologies and patient‑friendly workflows, they made telehealth accessible for underserved families and multilingual communities.


***Why it worked:*** *Flexibility, speed, and patient‑centered design win points with reviewers—especially during rapid funding rounds.*


‍


## **What Winning Applications Have in Common**


- **Strong local partnerships** – Pair healthcare + broadband + education + community orgs. Shared effort = stronger credibility.
- **A clear story** – Focus on what’s unique about your community—distance, digital skills, chronic disease, or language access.
- **User‑first design** – Grants reward tech that meets real people where they are, not what looks good on paper.
- **Ready-to-go materials** – Keep your proposal data, partner letters, and budget frameworks up to date so you can apply fast.
- **Sustainable plans** – Think beyond the build-out; show how you’ll keep services alive after the grant ends.
- **Start small, prove it, scale smart** – A successful pilot builds your resume for the next, bigger grant.
