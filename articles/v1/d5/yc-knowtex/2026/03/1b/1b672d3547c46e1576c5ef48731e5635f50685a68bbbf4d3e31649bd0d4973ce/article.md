# Knowtex, LACN, and MOASD Partnership

Knowtex, a leader in voice AI workflows for healthcare, is thrilled to announce exciting new partnerships with **Los Angeles Cancer Network (LACN)** and **Medical Oncology Associates of San Diego (MOASD)** . This collaboration underscores Knowtex’s commitment to enhancing **oncology workflows** with innovative voice AI solutions, while also providing the **flexibility to support multispecialty needs** for clinicians in fields such as gastrointestinal, gynecology, and urology care.


LACN and MOASD, highly respected oncology groups affiliated with the OneOncology network, are dedicated to delivering top-tier cancer care. This partnership will further their mission by introducing AI-driven solutions tailored to the complexities of oncology and related specialties.


### **Advanced Oncology Workflows with Multispecialty Adaptability**


The partnership focuses on streamlining oncology workflows, incorporating Knowtex’s ability to capture detailed clinical narratives and seamlessly integrate them into continuous care records. Knowtex also provides specialized templates and workflows that adapt to the specific needs of gastrointestinal, urology, and gynecology and other fields, ensuring a tailored experience for multispecialty groups while robustly supporting oncology documentation.


### **Enhanced Diagnosis and Billing Code Capture**


Knowtex’s AI system captures diagnosis and billing codes directly from physician-patient conversations with unparalleled accuracy. This feature simplifies the documentation process, ensures compliance, and enables clinicians to review and validate billing codes immediately after the visit encounter, saving time and reducing administrative strain.


### **Intelligent Integration with EMR Systems**


All captured notes, diagnosis details, and codes are intelligently integrated into the EMR, enabling a seamless transition from patient interactions to documented care plans. This integration is designed specifically for oncology workflows and is flexible to meet the needs of multispecialty practices, ensuring high accuracy and efficiency across the board.


### **Partner Information**


#### **About Los Angeles Cancer Network (LACN)**


**Los Angeles Cancer Network (LACN)** is one of Southern California’s leading oncology groups, offering comprehensive cancer care services across 20 clinic locations (and growing) in the greater Los Angeles area. The network’s mission is to provide compassionate, state-of-the-art cancer treatment while supporting patients throughout their care journey. LACN’s team of highly skilled oncologists and specialists work collaboratively to ensure patients receive personalized, cutting-edge care. LACN is a partner of the OneOncology network, which enhances their ability to provide access to clinical trials and the latest advancements in cancer treatment.[www.lacancernetwork.com](https://www.lacancernetwork.com/)


#### **About Medical Oncology Associates of San Diego (MOASD)**


**Medical Oncology Associates of San Diego (MOASD)** offers a complete range of oncology services in an atmosphere of professionalism, encouragement and understanding. MOASD provides personalized care for the detection, diagnosis, treatment, support and follow-up of cancer patients and their families. Recognizing the importance of providing all services “under one roof”, MOASD offices house a medical oncology infusion center, physician offices and exam rooms, and on-site registration. MOASD operates satellite facilities adjacent to Alvarado Hospital, Scripps Mercy Hospital, and Sharp Coronado Hospital and is a partner of the OneOncology network. ****
