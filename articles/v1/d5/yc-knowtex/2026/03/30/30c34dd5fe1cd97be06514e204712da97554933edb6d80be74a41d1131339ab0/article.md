# Knowtex and 4D EMR Partnership

## Announcement


Knowtex and 4D EMR, two innovative leaders in medical technology, are thrilled to announce a pioneering partnership that will introduce the first specialty-specific, end-to-end integrated voice AI electronic medical records (EMR) workflow. This collaboration, initiated during their shared journey in the Medtech Innovators 2023 cohort, marks a significant leap forward in clinician-centric technology, enhancing the efficiency and effectiveness of clinical workflows.


Caroline Zhang, CEO of Knowtex: “Our partnership with 4D EMR is a testament to our commitment to revolutionizing healthcare technology. By integrating our advanced voice AI capabilities with 4D EMR’s robust systems, we are setting a new standard for EMR workflows. This collaboration is not just about technology; it's about empowering clinicians to focus on what they do best – providing excellent patient care.”


> "This partnership is the holy grail of doctor ease of use. As a physician, I’ve been eagerly waiting for voice AI workflows like those from Knowtex. It’s a game-changer, allowing doctors to interact with EMR systems in the most natural and efficient way possible. This is the future of medical record-keeping."


Dr. Robert Pollack, CEO of 4D EMR and a physician working for over 34 years, expressed his long-awaited anticipation for such innovation: “This partnership is the holy grail of doctor ease of use. As a physician, I’ve been eagerly waiting for voice AI workflows like those from Knowtex. It’s a game-changer, allowing doctors to interact with EMR systems in the most natural and efficient way possible. This is the future of medical record-keeping.”


The integrated voice AI EMR system by Knowtex and 4D EMR is designed to understand the nuances of different medical specialties, offering tailored solutions that meet the specific needs of each practice area. This breakthrough will not only streamline documentation and data retrieval but also significantly reduce the administrative burden on healthcare professionals.


As part of this strategic partnership, both companies will continue to work closely to further innovate and refine their technologies, ensuring they stay at the forefront of the medtech industry. This collaboration is a shining example of how technological advancements can directly improve clinical workflows and patient care.


## Partner Info


4D EMR is the next evolution of electronic medical record and practice management software for specialty physician practices. Built by a practicing plastic surgeon, the company’s cloud software is designed with an intuitive interface which meets the unique workflow requirements of its customers. 4D EMR allows specialty practices to streamline operations and improve the clinical experience of patients, staff, and doctors.
