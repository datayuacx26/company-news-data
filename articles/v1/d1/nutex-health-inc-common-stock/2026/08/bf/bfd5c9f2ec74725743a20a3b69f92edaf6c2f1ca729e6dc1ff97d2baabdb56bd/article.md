# NUTEX HEALTH OPENS WEST LITTLE ROCK EMERGENCY HOSPITAL IN ARKANSAS

by


[Nutex](https://nutexhealth.com/author/919496pwpadmin/)


on


[August 18, 2026](https://nutexhealth.com/2026/08/18/)


HOUSTON


, Aug. 18, 2026


/PRNewswire/ — Nutex Health Inc. (“Nutex Health” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: NUTX), a physician-led, integrated healthcare delivery system comprised of 28 state-of-the-art micro hospitals and hospital outpatient departments in 12 states and primary care-centric, risk-bearing physician networks, today announced the opening of its 28th hospital nationwide and third facility in Arkansas. West Little Rock Emergency Hospital is located at 17810 Chenal Parkway in Little Rock.


The staff of medical experts at West Little Rock Emergency Hospital brings the community 24/7/365 concierge-level care. The 18,966-square-foot, state-of-the-art facility houses eight emergency room beds, five inpatient suites, and two triage rooms. It features a full-service laboratory and advanced imaging technology, including MRI, CT, X-ray, and ultrasound, with same-day scheduling and rapid results.


“Although emergencies do not occur every day, we stand ready to assist the citizens of Central Arkansas and the surrounding areas today, tomorrow, and for the years to come,” said Justin White, MD, Chief Medical Officer at West Little Rock Emergency Hospital. “We are honored to join this community, and we look forward to providing care to those whom we have the privilege to serve.”


“We are thrilled to welcome patients to West Little Rock Emergency Hospital, located on the west side of Little Rock,” said Thomas Vo, MD, MBA, Chief Executive Officer at Nutex Health. “This new hospital complements our facility Cabot ER & Hospital on the east side of the Little Rock metropolitan area, improving accessibility and bringing exceptional healthcare closer to more families throughout the area. With both locations serving the region, we are well-positioned to provide the Little Rock community with the high-quality care it deserves.”


**About Nutex Health Inc.**


Headquartered in Houston, Texas and founded in 2011, Nutex Health Inc. (NASDAQ: NUTX) is a healthcare management and operations company with three divisions: a Hospital Division, Population Health Management Division, and Real Estate Division


The Hospital Division owns, develops, and operates innovative health care models, including micro-hospitals, specialty hospitals, and hospital outpatient departments. This division owns and operates 28 facilities in 12 states.


The Population Health Management division owns and operates provider networks such as Independent Physician Associations. Through our Management Services Organization, we provide management, administrative and other support services to our affiliated hospitals and physician groups.


The real estate division comprises of real estate entities along with activity related to the development and construction of hospital facilities. The real estate entities own the land and hospital buildings which are leased to our hospital entities.
