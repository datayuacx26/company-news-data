## The quick view


### 1) Sales personalization and demand generation


**What you achieve:**


Turn static outreach into high-velocity, human-feeling video at scale for outbound, ABM, and product-led demos.


**Where Tavus fits in your workflow:**


- Use the[Video Generation APIs](https://www.tavus.io/tavus-vs-heygen) to programmatically create personalized clips from your CRM or event stream.
- Train a personal digital twin for your reps, then generate per-prospect videos with variables pulled from your data.
- Embed interactive follow-ups with the[Conversational Video Interface](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) for live Q&A after a click.


**Features that matter:**


- Ship fast with “train and generate” simplicity:[train replicas with one API call, and generate with another](https://www.tavus.io/tavus-vs-heygen) .
- Start quickly or stay on brand using[100+ hyper-realistic Stock Replicas](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) or your own digital twin.
- Keep content fresh and personalized using[event-driven video generation](https://www.tavus.io/ai-video-maker) tied to prospect actions.
- Maintain full brand control with[white-labeled APIs](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .
- Expect high realism with Phoenix-3 rendering quality - see customer validation of[Phoenix-3](https://www.tavus.io/tavus-vs-heygen) .


**Ship it fast:**


- Train your replica with[rapid training in less than two hours](https://www.tavus.io/tavus-vs-heygen) , then generate your first 100 videos.
- Add a conversational follow-up flow using[CVI real-time interactions](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .
- Reference the[Developer Docs](https://docs.tavus.io/sections/introduction) when you wire up your API calls.


**Proof points:**


- Tavus serves enterprises including Salesforce and Meta, and raised an $18M Series A to scale its platform, per[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/03/12/generative-ai-video-startup-tavus-raises-18m-to-bring-face-and-voice-cloning-to-any-app) .


### 2) In-product onboarding and customer education


**What you achieve:**


Increase product adoption with an interactive, face-to-face guide that explains features, answers questions, and adapts in real time.


**Where Tavus fits in your workflow:**


- Trigger a video agent during onboarding steps or when users appear stuck.
- Let the agent demonstrate actions and answer questions in-session, then route users to the next best step.


**Features that matter:**


- Build lifelike video agents using the[Conversational Video Interface](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .
- Keep it natural with[~600 ms latency](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) for smooth back-and-forth.
- Make it feel human with[empathetic visual perception](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) that reads expressions and context.
- Support global users with[30+ languages](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .
- Embed seamlessly using[white-labeled APIs](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .


**Implementation pointers:**


- Use CVI for real-time Q&A, and fall back to templated[Video Generation](https://www.tavus.io/tavus-vs-heygen) for proactive tips at milestones.
- Start with the[CVI overview in the docs](https://docs.tavus.io/sections/conversational-video-interface/overview-cvi) .


### 3) Recruitment screening with an AI Interviewer


**What you achieve:**


Run consistent, bias-resistant structured screens while giving candidates a respectful, human-feeling experience.


**Where Tavus fits in your workflow:**


- Offer candidates a self-serve link to meet your AI Interviewer.
- Capture structured responses, probe with follow-ups, and share recordings with hiring teams.


**Features that matter:**


- Start from the documented[AI Interviewer](https://docs.tavus.io/sections/conversational-video-interface/conversation/usecases/ai-interviewer) blueprint.
- Maintain natural flow with[transformer-based turn-taking via Sparrow-0](https://www.tavus.io/tavus-vs-heygen) .
- Keep interviews engaging with[real-time, face-to-face conversations](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .
- Prove time-to-value - customers report they[implemented CVI in just two days](https://www.tavus.io/tavus-vs-heygen) .


**Implementation pointers:**


- Map your competency model to a Persona and question set, then let CVI adapt follow-ups in-session.
- Provide candidates options for language and scheduling using[multilingual support](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .


### 4) Healthcare virtual support and coaching


**What you achieve:**


Offer empathetic, multilingual guidance for triage, care navigation, and coaching without long wait times.


**Where Tavus fits in your workflow:**


- Embed a support agent into portals to explain care options and next steps.
- Provide ongoing coaching check-ins with a consistent, approachable presence.


**Features that matter:**


- See the pattern in action with the homepage[AI Therapist](https://www.tavus.io/) example.
- Build adaptive experiences using[empathetic visual perception](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) and real-time responses.
- Design purpose-built flows - “Imagine a therapist, a personal trainer, or a fleet of medical assistants” as part of[building any conversational experience](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .
- Control branding and compliance posture with[white-labeled APIs](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .


**Implementation pointers:**


- Start narrow: a single triage flow, medication FAQs, or discharge coaching.
- Localize early using[30+ languages](https://www.tavus.io/product/conversational-video) .
