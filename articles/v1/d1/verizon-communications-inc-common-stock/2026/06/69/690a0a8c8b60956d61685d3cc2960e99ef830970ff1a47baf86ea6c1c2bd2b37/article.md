Are there hidden fees?


No. Customers can see what is included in their plan and choose optional services based on their needs . Starting at $45 per month, with a promotional offer of $30/mo per line for qualifying switchers, Simplicity gives every customer access to Verizon’s best 5G network.


What is an activation fee?


An activation fee is a one-time charge some wireless carriers apply when starting service on a new device or line. Customers enrolled in the Verizon Loyalty Program can enroll to have their activation fees waived moving forward.


What is Verizon Dollars?


Verizon Dollars is a rewards program that allows eligible customers to earn 3% back every month simply for being a customer. Verizon Dollars can be used toward devices, accessories, merchandise and special offers.


Can I keep my old phone?


Yes. Verizon Simplicity supports bring-your-own-device activation, allowing customers to keep and use a compatible phone they already own.


How do I use Verizon Dollars rewards?


Verizon Dollars can be redeemed at participating Verizon programs and offers through the My Verizon app. Eligible customers can use Verizon Dollars toward devices, accessories, merchandise and other available rewards.


What is Verizon Simplicity?


Verizon Simplicity is a mobile plan that gives every customer access to Verizon's best 5G network experience without needing to choose between network tiers. It is offered at $45, with an initial promotional offer of $30 per line for mobile customers that switch to Verizon.


What is Verizon One?


Verizon One combines mobile and home connectivity into one plan with one bill and one monthly price.


What is Verizon Loyalty?


Verizon Loyalty is an opt-in customer benefits program that provides rewards to eligible new and existing Verizon customers.


What is Verizon Shine?


Verizon Shine is a part of the Verizon Loyalty Program that gives new and existing customers access to weekly prizes, exclusive offers and special experiences.
