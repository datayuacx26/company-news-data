## **Get Found First: Local Listings & Google Business Profile for Electricians**


Customers can’t hire your business if they never see it on Google Maps, in local search results, or in online directories. One of the fastest ways to improve your visibility is by making sure your[Google Business Profile and online listings](https://www.thryv.com/blog/google-business-profile-optimization/) are complete, accurate, and consistent wherever homeowners search.


Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a potential customer sees. Before they visit your website, they’re checking your hours, reading reviews, looking at photos, and deciding whether to call. An incomplete profile or outdated information can send them to another electrician in a matter of seconds.


Take a few minutes to review the basics:


- Choose the most accurate primary business category.
- Define the cities, neighbourhoods, and local areas you serve.
- Add recent photos of completed work, service vehicles, and your team.
- Keep your business hours up to date, especially during holidays or emergencies.
- Make sure your phone number and website are correct.


Your business information should also match across all online listings. If your office number changed but a few directories still show the old one, a homeowner may call, get no answer, and book another electrician instead.

## **Paid Ads & Lead Gen: Google Local Services Ads for Electricians**


Before spending money on advertising, make sure your online presence is working for you. If your business profile, reviews, or website aren’t encouraging homeowners to call, ads will simply send more people to those same gaps. If you’re working with a limited budget, you’ll usually see a better return by improving those areas before increasing your ad spend.


When you’re ready to invest in paid marketing, Google Local Services Ads (LSAs) are often a good place to start. Unlike traditional paid search campaigns in Google Ads, LSAs charge when a potential customer contacts your business.


To qualify for Google Guaranteed, you’ll typically need to verify your business license, insurance, and complete Google’s background check requirements. That badge can give homeowners more confidence when comparing local electricians.


As leads start coming in, keep an eye on the numbers that tell you whether your investment is paying off:


- Cost per qualified lead
- Number of booked jobs
- Revenue generated
- Which services produce the strongest return


For example, you may discover that emergency electrical repairs consistently produce profitable jobs while smaller repair ads rarely convert. That insight helps you invest more of your budget where it generates the best return.
