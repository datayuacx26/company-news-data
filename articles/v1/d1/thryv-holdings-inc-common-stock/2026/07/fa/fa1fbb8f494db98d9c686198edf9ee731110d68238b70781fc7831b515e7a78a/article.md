## What the Thryv + Jobber Integration Does


The Thryv and Jobber integration creates a direct connection between your marketing and operational workflows.


Instead of manually transferring leads into Jobber, qualified leads now sync automatically into your Jobber pipeline in real-time.


That means:


- No copy-paste work
- No duplicate entry

The result is a faster, cleaner workflow that helps businesses turn more leads into booked jobs.


## How the Integration Works Step by Step


The integration acts as an automated handoff between Thryv and Jobber.


### 1. Thryv Captures and Scores Incoming Leads


Leads generated through calls, forms, ads, or campaigns inside Thryv are automatically processed through[AI Lead Insights](https://www.thryv.com/features/ai-for-small-businesses/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=OrganicSocial&utm_campaign=JobberThryvIntegration#ai-leads) .


Each lead receives a score from 1–5 based on:


- Intent
- Engagement signals
- Sentiment
- Likelihood to convert


### 2. AI Filters Out Low-Intent and Null Leads


Only leads scoring 3, 4, or 5 move into Jobber automatically.


Lower-quality leads remain inside Thryv decluttering your Jobber pipeline.


This helps teams focus attention on leads most likely to become paying customers.


### 3. Qualified Leads Sync into Jobber Automatically


Once qualified, leads are created as Client records in Jobber in near real-time.


If the contact already exists, the integration updates the existing record instead of creating a duplicate.


### 4. Your Team Follows Up with Full Context


Each synced lead includes:


- Lead score
- Sentiment tags
- AI-generated call summaries
- Contact details
- Service-related notes


Your team enters every conversation already informed and ready to act.
