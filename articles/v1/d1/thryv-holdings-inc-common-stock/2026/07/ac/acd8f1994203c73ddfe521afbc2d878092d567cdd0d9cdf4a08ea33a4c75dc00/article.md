1. Recent reviews build customer trust. Review recency and volume matter alongside your star rating.

## **Why Reputation Management Is a Bigger Deal Now**


Customers often decide whether to contact your business before they ever visit your website or pick up the phone. They may find you through Google, Google Maps, Facebook, a review site, social media, or even an AI-generated recommendation. Wherever they discover your business, your online reputation helps determine whether you make the shortlist.


A strong star rating is only part of the picture. Customers also notice how many reviews you have, how recently they were posted, and whether you respond. BrightLocal found that[47% of consumers](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) won’t use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, while[74% focus on reviews](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) written within the past three months.


Imagine a homeowner asking ChatGPT, “Who’s the best electrician near me?” or searching Google for panel upgrade services. Two electricians appear with similar experience. One has a dozen older reviews and no responses from the owner. The other has dozens of recent reviews, thoughtful replies, and accurate business information across major directories.
