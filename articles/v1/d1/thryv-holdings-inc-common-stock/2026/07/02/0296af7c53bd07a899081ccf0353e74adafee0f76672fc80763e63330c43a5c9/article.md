# Webinar Recap: AI, Claude, ChatGPT and Your Business

Artificial intelligence has evolved far beyond writing emails or generating social media posts.


During Thryv’s webinar, Gene Marks explained that the businesses seeing the biggest gains aren’t simply asking ChatGPT random questions. They’re integrating AI directly into the systems they already use every day.


Instead of treating AI as another search engine, successful businesses are using it to:


• Analyze customer information
• Review contracts
• Summarize financial data
• Create reports instantly
• Recommend pricing
• Improve operational decisions


That shift from experimentation to implementation is where real productivity gains begin.


## Key Takeaways


1. AI delivers the biggest business value when connected to your own business data, not just public information.
2. Most small businesses already have AI built into the software they use every day but aren’t taking advantage of it.
3. AI agents can automate repetitive tasks such as scheduling, reporting, document reviews, and customer communications.
4. Businesses should identify one high-impact workflow before investing in custom AI solutions.
5. Every business should create an AI policy to guide responsible, secure employee use.


## Understand the Risks Before You Scale


AI offers tremendous opportunity, but every technology investment requires balancing risk with reward.


Gene highlighted several areas every business owner should understand before expanding AI use:


### Hallucinations


AI sometimes provides incorrect information with complete confidence.


Always verify important business decisions before acting.


### Misinformation


AI only performs as well as the information it receives.


Poor inputs often create poor outputs.


### Privacy and Security


Cloud-based AI platforms continue improving security, but businesses should avoid sharing sensitive information without understanding how their chosen platform stores and protects data.


### Bias


Businesses using AI in hiring, HR, or customer decisions should review outputs carefully and stay aware of emerging regulations.


### Deepfakes


As AI-generated content becomes more convincing, verifying information through trusted sources becomes increasingly important.


Gene emphasized that AI should be viewed like any other business investment: understand the risks, evaluate the rewards, and make informed decisions.


## Connect AI to Your Business Data


One of the biggest missed opportunities for small businesses is leaving valuable business data trapped inside disconnected systems.


Instead of asking AI general questions, businesses should connect platforms like ChatGPT or Claude to tools they already use, including:


- Gmail
- Google Drive
- Microsoft OneDrive
- Outlook
- QuickBooks
- PayPal
- Zoom
- Microsoft Teams


Once connected, AI becomes dramatically more useful.


For example, businesses can ask:


- Which customers were most profitable last quarter?
- Compare my freight invoices against contract pricing.
- Review this proposal before I send it.
- Which accounts are consistently paying late?
- Recommend pricing based on previous estimates.


Rather than searching the internet, AI begins analyzing your own business.

## Build AI Around Real Business Problems


Not every business needs a custom AI application.


But many can benefit from solving one specific operational challenge.


Gene encouraged business owners to start with “low-hanging fruit.”


Examples include:


### Inventory Optimization


Forecast inventory needs and automate purchasing decisions.


### Faster Estimates


Generate proposals automatically after customer conversations.


### Sales Recommendations


Suggest complementary products during customer interactions.


### Predictive Maintenance


Monitor equipment using sensors before failures occur.


### Smarter Scheduling


Automatically assign appointments based on technician availability and customer location.


### Internal Knowledge Bases


Allow employees to search manuals, documentation, policies, and product information using natural language.


The goal isn’t to replace people.


It’s to remove repetitive work so employees can focus on higher-value tasks.

## Create an AI Policy Before You Need One


Technology adoption should include clear expectations.


Gene recommended that every business create an internal AI policy covering:


- Approved AI tools
- Acceptable business use
- Data governance
- Employee responsibilities
- Privacy expectations
- Security requirements


The good news?


AI can help draft the first version.


Business owners can ask ChatGPT or Claude to generate a policy, then review it with legal and IT advisors before implementing it.
