## What Is Answer Engine Optimization?


For years, businesses focused on optimizing their websites for search engines like Google. While that is still important, customers are finding businesses in new ways.


Instead of scrolling through pages of search results, many people now ask AI-powered search tools direct questions like:


- Who’s the best plumber near me?
- What accountant works with small businesses?
- Which roofing company has great reviews?
- Where can I find a family dentist accepting new patients?


Rather than returning a list of links, AI tools often summarize the information they find across the web and recommend a handful of businesses that appear to best answer the customer’s question.


This shift has made **[Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)](https://www.thryv.com/blog/optimize-website-for-featured-snippets-ai-answers/)** an important part of a business’s digital marketing strategy.


AEO is the practice of creating clear, trustworthy, and well-organized online information that helps AI-powered search tools understand your business and confidently include it in relevant answers.


Unlike traditional SEO, which focuses on improving rankings in search results, AEO focuses on helping answer engines understand who you are, what you offer, where you serve customers, and why people trust your business.


The good news? Many of the same practices that improve your SEO also strengthen your AEO. A well-maintained website, consistent business listings, customer reviews, and helpful content all work together to improve your online visibility.


## How AI Is Changing the Customer Journey


The customer journey has always started with a question.


Years ago, someone might search for a service, visit several websites, compare reviews, and spend days researching before reaching out.


Today, that process is becoming much shorter.


Imagine a homeowner looking for a local HVAC company. Instead of opening five different websites, they ask an AI-powered search tool:


*“Who installs heat pumps near me?”*


Within seconds, they receive a summarized answer that may include[local businesses](https://www.thryv.com/blog/how-customers-find-local-businesses/) , customer reviews, services offered, and reasons each business stands out.


By the time that customer clicks on your website or calls your office, they’ve already narrowed their options.


That means customers are spending less time researching and more time deciding.


For small businesses, this creates both an opportunity and a challenge.


If your online information is complete and consistent, AI has more confidence in recommending your business. If your website is outdated, your reviews are sparse, or your business information varies across platforms, your business may be overlooked even if you provide exceptional service.


## Why AEO Helps Customers Decide Faster


Customers want answers as quickly as possible.


Whether they’re looking for an emergency plumber or planning a landscaping project, they don’t want to piece together information from multiple sources if they don’t have to.


AI helps simplify that process by bringing together information from websites, reviews, business profiles, and other trusted sources.


Businesses that make their information easy to understand help customers answer important questions like:


- What services do you offer?
- Where do you work?
- What makes your business different?
- What do customers say about you?
- How can someone contact you?


When those answers are easy to find, customers can move from researching to contacting your business much faster.


That’s why AEO isn’t about trying to “game” AI.


It’s about making your business easier for both customers and technology to understand.

## 7 Ways to Improve Your AI Visibility


### 1. Build Service Pages That Answer Customer Questions


Many small business websites list their services on one page.


While that provides a basic overview, dedicated service pages offer much more value.


Instead of simply saying you offer plumbing services, create individual pages for drain cleaning, water heater installation, leak detection, emergency plumbing, and sewer repair.


Each page should answer common customer questions, including:


- What does this service include?
- Who needs it?
- What problems does it solve?
- What should customers expect?
- What areas do you serve?


The more helpful and specific your content is, the easier it becomes for customers to find answers and for AI to understand your expertise.


### 2. Keep Your Business Information Consistent Everywhere


Your website isn’t the only place customers discover your business.


AI also references information from online listings, review sites, maps, directories, and business profiles.


Make sure your business information is consistent across every platform, including:


- Business name
- Address
- Phone number
- Website
- Hours
- Services
- Service areas


Even small inconsistencies can create confusion for customers and reduce confidence in your business information.


Keeping everything updated also makes it easier for customers to contact you no matter where they find your business.


### 3. Publish Helpful Content That Answers Real Questions


Customers ask questions throughout every stage of the buying process.


The businesses that consistently answer those questions often build trust before a customer ever reaches out.


Consider creating content around questions like:


- How much does roof replacement cost?
- How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?
- What’s included in bookkeeping services?
- Do I need professional landscaping before selling my home?


Helpful articles, FAQs, buying guides, and resource pages not only educate customers but also give AI additional context about your expertise.


Focus on creating content that genuinely helps customers make informed decisions instead of writing solely for search engines.

### 4. Earn and Manage Customer Reviews Consistently


Customer reviews have always influenced buying decisions. Today, they also provide[valuable context that helps AI-powered search tools](https://www.thryv.com/blog/why-reviews-matter-for-ai-search/) better understand your business.


Reviews often mention the services you provide, the quality of your work, your responsiveness, and even the neighborhoods or cities you serve. Together, those details paint a clearer picture of your business for both prospective customers and AI.


Rather than asking for reviews only a few times a year, make review generation part of your everyday customer experience.


Here are a few best practices:


- Ask for a review shortly after completing a job or service.
- Make it easy by sharing a direct review link through email or text.
- Respond to every review, whether it’s positive or negative.
- Thank customers for their feedback and address concerns professionally.


Fresh, authentic reviews build trust with future customers while strengthening your overall online presence.

### 5. Organize Your Website So AI Can Understand It


A helpful website isn’t just good for visitors. It’s also easier for search engines and AI to understand.


Instead of creating one long page that covers every service, organize your content into clear sections with descriptive headings.


For example, a landscaping company might have separate pages for:


- Lawn care
- Landscape design
- Irrigation installation
- Outdoor lighting
- Seasonal cleanup


Each page can answer common questions, explain the service, highlight your experience, and include customer testimonials.


This creates a stronger website structure while helping AI connect the right information to the right customer questions.


Simple improvements like descriptive page titles, logical navigation, and easy-to-read formatting also make your content more useful for everyone.


### 6. Keep Your Website Current


One of the easiest ways to[improve your visibility](https://www.thryv.com/blog/optimize-website-for-featured-snippets-ai-answers/) is simply by keeping your website up to date.


Customers want current information, and so do AI-powered search tools.


Review your website regularly to ensure:


- Business hours are accurate.
- Contact information is current.
- Service areas reflect where you work today.
- Pricing information is updated when appropriate.
- Team members and certifications are current.
- New services are added promptly.


An outdated website can create uncertainty for customers and make it harder for AI to confidently understand your business.


Even small updates throughout the year demonstrate that your business is active and engaged.


### 7. Make It Easy for Customers to Take the Next Step


Getting recommended by AI is only part of the journey.


Once someone visits your website, the next step should be obvious.


Every page should include clear opportunities for customers to contact you.


This might include:


- Online scheduling
- Contact forms
- Phone numbers
- Live chat
- Text messaging
- Request-a-quote buttons


The fewer obstacles customers encounter, the more likely they are to reach out.


Fast responses matter, too.


Whether someone contacts you through your website, social media, or online listings, responding quickly can make the difference between winning a customer and losing one to a competitor.

## Common AEO Mistakes Small Businesses Make


AEO doesn’t require a completely new marketing strategy. In many cases, it’s about improving the fundamentals.


Avoid these common mistakes:


### Relying on One General Services Page


Dedicated service pages provide more useful information for customers and help AI better understand your expertise.


### Neglecting Your Online Listings


Incorrect addresses, outdated business hours, or inconsistent phone numbers create confusion and reduce trust.


### Ignoring Customer Reviews


Reviews influence both customer confidence and the information AI uses to understand your business.


### Publishing Content Only for Search Engines


Keyword-heavy articles without useful information rarely help customers.


Focus on answering real questions instead.


### Forgetting About Mobile Users


Most customers research businesses on their phones.


Make sure your website loads quickly, is easy to navigate, and offers simple ways to contact you.


## How to Measure AEO Success


Unlike traditional SEO, there isn’t one report that tells you how often AI recommends your business.


Instead, measure the outcomes that matter most.


Look for improvements in:


- Website traffic from organic search
- Contact form submissions
- Phone calls
- Appointment bookings
- Review volume and quality
- Time spent on key service pages
- Local search visibility
- Customer inquiries mentioning AI or online recommendations


Remember, the goal isn’t simply appearing in AI-generated answers.


The goal is helping more customers discover your business and feel confident choosing you.
