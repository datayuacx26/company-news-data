# Thryv Named to 2026 Top 100 Global Most Loved Workplaces List

*Marks the third consecutive year of recognition from the Best Practice Institute*


**Dallas, TX (July 14, 2026)** Thryv Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: THRY), provider of a leading global, AI-enabled small business marketing platform, announced today it earned a spot on the annual[Top 100 Global Most Loved Workplaces®](https://insights.economistenterprise.com/projects/2026-top-100-global-most-loved-workplaces) from the Best Practice Institute. This marks Thryv’s third consecutive year appearing on the prestigious list that recognizes 100 organizations across 40+ industries and six continents where employees report the highest levels of belonging, career advancement and values alignment. Thryv improved its standing from #66 in 2025 to its current spot at #53.


The ranking measures and validates what employees report experiencing across five key areas: how positive employees feel about their future at the company, career achievement, alignment of employer values with employee values, respect at all levels, and collaboration. Additional factors, including diversity and belonging, leadership, and professional development, were also evaluated in relation to these core sentiment drivers.


“As a remote-first company, our success comes down to building a values-driven culture that connects and empowers our people, wherever they work,” said Joe Walsh, CEO of Thryv. “It’s great to see that our ranking continues to climb. It’s a testament to the strength of our teams across the U.S, Australia, Canada, the Dominican Republic and New Zealand, and their shared commitment to developing accessible, AI-driven marketing solutions that help small businesses grow.”


Now in its fourth year, the Top 100 Global Most Loved Workplaces list is based on research from BPI’s Love of Workplace Index®, drawing data from more than two million employees worldwide. Companies featured demonstrate an extraordinary commitment to building cultures of trust, respect, purpose, and employee connection, regardless of size, location, or industry.


“Thryv employees are proud to be named a Global Most Loved Workplace, and honestly, so am I,” said Lesley Bolger, EVP, HR & Chief Legal Officer, Thryv. “The collective contributions of our employees fuel our innovation and strengthen our impact on small business customers around the world. Thank you to all Thryv employees who add such positive energy and value to our culture and to our customer experience.”


To view the complete 2026 Global Most Loved Workplaces® list, visit[https://insights.economistenterprise.com/projects/2026-top-100-global-most-loved-workplaces](https://insights.economistenterprise.com/projects/2026-top-100-global-most-loved-workplaces) .


**About Most Loved Workplace®**
Most Loved Workplace® is a global certification and recognition platform powered by Best Practice Institute. Organizations certified as Most Loved Workplaces® outperform peers in retention, productivity, and customer satisfaction. Based on the Love of Workplace Index®, the model is featured in the bestselling book In Great Company (McGraw-Hill, 2019). To learn more, visit[https://certcheck.mostlovedworkplace.com/](https://certcheck.mostlovedworkplace.com/) .


**About Thryv**

Thryv software offers SMBs AI-driven lead insights, automated customer follow‑up and an AI-enabled contact list.
