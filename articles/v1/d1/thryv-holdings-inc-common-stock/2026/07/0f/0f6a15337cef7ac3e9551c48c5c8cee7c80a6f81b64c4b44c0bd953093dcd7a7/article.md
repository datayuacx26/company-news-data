# The 5 Website Pages Every Small Business Needs to Rank in AI Search

A few years ago, ranking in search was largely about keywords.


Today, AI search engines are looking for something much more valuable.


They want to recommend businesses they understand and trust.


Platforms like Google’s AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI-powered search experiences don’t simply scan your homepage. They[evaluate your entire online presence](https://www.thryv.com/blog/how-google-ai-search-and-voice-assistants-choose-answers/) to determine whether your business is credible, relevant, and capable of answering a customer’s question.


That means every page on your website serves a purpose.


Instead of asking, “Does my website look good?” small businesses should ask, “Does my website help AI understand exactly who we are, what we do, and where we serve customers?”


The answer often comes down to having the right pages.


Let’s look at the five pages every small business should have to improve AI search visibility.

1. AI search engines prioritize websites with organized, trustworthy, and complete information.

## Service Pages That Clearly Explain What You Offer


Service pages have become one of the strongest signals AI uses to understand your business.


Generic descriptions don’t provide enough context. AI wants[detailed information that explains exactly what customers can expect](https://www.thryv.com/blog/schema-markup-for-small-businesses-aeo-guide/) .


Each major service deserves its own dedicated page that includes:


- A clear explanation of the service
- Who it’s for
- Common customer problems it solves
- Benefits and outcomes
- Frequently asked questions related to that service
- Calls to action

## FAQ Pages That Answer Real Customer Questions


Customers have questions before they make a purchase.


AI search engines know this.


That’s why FAQ pages have become incredibly valuable. They[provide structured information that AI can easily understand and reference](https://www.thryv.com/blog/why-every-small-business-needs-a-faq-page-in-2026/) when generating answers.


Think beyond simple questions like:


- What are your hours?


Instead, answer the questions customers actually ask before hiring someone.


Examples include:


- How much does this service typically cost?
- How long does the project take?
- What should customers expect during the process?
- Do you offer financing?
- Which neighborhoods do you serve?
- What happens after I submit a quote request?


These questions naturally align with conversational searches people ask AI assistants every day.


**** **Pro Tip**


Use the same language your customers use. The phrases customers type into AI tools often become the exact questions your FAQ page should answer. AI systems prefer pages that fully answer a specific question instead of pages that briefly mention multiple topics.


## About Pages That Build Trust and Authority


Your About page isn’t just your company’s story.


It’s a trust signal.


AI systems evaluate credibility before recommending businesses. An informative About page helps establish experience, expertise, and authenticity.


A strong About page should include:


- Company history
- Mission and values
- Team introductions
- Certifications
- Awards
- Community involvement
- Professional memberships
- Years in business


This information helps AI determine that your business is legitimate and experienced.


It also helps customers feel confident choosing you over competitors.


**Why trust matters** ****


AI isn’t simply matching keywords anymore. It’s evaluating whether your business appears credible enough to recommend.

## Location Pages That Help Customers Find You


AI search is highly localized.


When someone asks:


*“Who is the best plumber near me?”*


AI needs confidence that your business actually serves that area.


If you work in multiple cities or neighborhoods, create[dedicated location pages](https://www.thryv.com/blog/how-customers-find-local-businesses/) instead of listing every city on one page.


Each location page should include:


- Services offered in that area
- Local landmarks or neighborhoods
- Customer testimonials from nearby clients
- Relevant images
- Business hours
- Contact information
- Directions or service area details


These pages help AI connect your business with geographically relevant searches.


They also improve your visibility in traditional local search results.
