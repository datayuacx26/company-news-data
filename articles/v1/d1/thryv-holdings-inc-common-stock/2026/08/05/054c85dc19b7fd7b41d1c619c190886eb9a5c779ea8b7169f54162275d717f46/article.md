## **What Is AI Lead Scoring?**


AI lead scoring is the process of using artificial intelligence to evaluate lead information and identify which prospects are most likely to become customers.


Traditional lead management often relies on manual review or simple rules. You might work through inquiries in the order they arrive or try to remember which conversations seemed most promising.


But not every lead has the same level of intent.


Someone asking a general question may still be researching. Another prospect may be comparing prices. A third may be ready to schedule an appointment today.


AI lead scoring helps surface those differences.


[Thryv AI Lead Insights](https://www.thryv.com/features/ai-for-small-businesses/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=OrganicSocial&utm_campaign=AILeadScoring#ai-leads) , for example, helps businesses evaluate incoming opportunities with an AI-powered 1-to-5 lead score. The feature analyzes customer interactions to surface useful insights, including conversation summaries, recommended action items, and smart tags, so businesses can get a clearer view of their leads.


The result isn’t just another number to track. It’s a faster way to understand which leads may need attention and why.


### **Why AI Scoring Is More Than a Number**


Knowing which leads are most likely to become customers is useful. Knowing **why** they’re showing strong buying signals and what to do next is even more valuable.


That’s where AI lead scoring goes beyond a simple number.


With Thryv AI Lead Insights, that score becomes part of a bigger picture. Instead of starting from scratch with every new inquiry, you can quickly see the key details behind a lead, understand what the customer needs, and identify a logical next step. This gives you more context to decide which opportunities deserve your attention first.


Here’s what that can look like with AI Lead Insights:


- **AI Lead Score:** See a 1-to-5 score that helps indicate lead quality and purchase intent.


- **Conversation Summary:** Get a quick overview of what the customer discussed so you can understand their needs without digging through the entire interaction.


- **Recommended Action Items:** See suggested next steps based on the conversation, helping you move from insight to[follow-up](https://www.thryv.com/blog/lead-response-time/) faster.


- **Smart Tags:** Get additional context about the lead, including information about intent and tone.


- **Lead Source Information:** Understand where opportunities are coming from so you can see which channels are contributing to your pipeline.


Together, these insights can help you prioritize your time more effectively.


Imagine a local plumber receiving three new inquiries. One customer is researching water heater prices. Another wants to know whether the business serves their area. A third says their water heater is leaking and asks if someone can come out today.


All three are potential opportunities, but they aren’t at the same stage of the buying journey.


AI-powered insights can help surface the urgency and intent behind those conversations, giving the business owner a clearer starting point for follow-up. Instead of working through every inquiry in the order it arrived, they can focus first on the opportunity that may need immediate attention.


The benefit isn’t simply knowing which lead scored highest. It’s having the context to understand the opportunity and decide what to do next.


That can mean less time sorting through conversations, faster follow-up on promising opportunities, and a more informed approach to turning leads into customers.
