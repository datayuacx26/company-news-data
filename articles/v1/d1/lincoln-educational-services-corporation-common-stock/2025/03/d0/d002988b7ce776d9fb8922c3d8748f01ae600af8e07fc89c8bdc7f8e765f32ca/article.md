# What Trades Careers Are in Demand? Lincoln Tech Has The Answer

When you hear in-demand trades careers, you probably immediately think of electricians and plumbers, and electricians1 definitely are in demand in the U.S. But so are HVAC technicians2. And depending on where you live, other occupations are seeing a surge in demand. For example, while there’s steady demand for automotive mechanics3 throughout the U.S., in New York, demand is nine times the national average4. Before you set out on your career path, consider what you want to do, where you want to live, and whether there will be demand for your skills. Why not check out some trades careers that are in demand today?


## Electricians are in Demand


Employment of electricians is expected to grow much faster than the average through the decade, with an addition of more than 80,000 jobs each year5. Demand is created by the retirement of older workers, employees shifting out of the profession, and the installation of greener power sources that require new workers with new skills. While demand is up throughout the country, states like[Colorado](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=CO) ,[Connecticut](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=CT) ,[Georgia](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=GA) ,[Illinois](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=IL) ,[Maryland](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=MD) ,[New York](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=NY) ,[New Jersey](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=NJ) ,[Pennsylvania](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=PA) , and[Texas](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/47-2111.00?st=TX) are seeing demand significantly above the national average.


### Electrician Training


To become an electrician, you can enroll in an electrician or electronic training program. Your program introduces you to the basics of electricity and how electrical systems operate. It also teaches you how to install, maintain, and troubleshoot those systems. You learn about motor and generator theory, electrical codes, and schematics and blueprints. In the lab, you practice installation methods of lighting, alarm systems, and home-theater equipment with real tools and wiring.


After your training, you work as an apprentice and then go on to earn state licensure. Licensing requirements vary by state. In Colorado, for example, you need to[complete 4,000 hours of work to become a residential wireman or 8,000 hours of work to become a journeyman electrician](https://dpo.colorado.gov/Electrical/Applications) . You must also submit an application, your transcripts, an affidavit of your work experience, and pass a background check and pay a registration fee. And you need to pass a state exam for electricians.


## Demand for HVAC Technicians


Employment for HVAC technicians is also projected to grow at a much faster rate than the average throughout the decade6. Commercial and residential buildings always need HVAC maintenance and repair and with a boom of commercial and residential construction, new systems need to be installed. There is also a greater emphasis on green technology and energy efficiency which requires trained professionals—which should add to demand for HVAC technicians. Demand may be higher nationally, but states like[Colorado](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-9021.00?st=CO) ,[Connecticut](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-9021.00?st=CT) ,[Maryland](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-9021.00?st=MD) ,[New Jersey](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-9021.00?st=NJ) , and[Texas](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-9021.00?st=TX) are experiencing high demand due to their rapidly expanding populations and need for climate control systems in both residential and commercial settings.


### HVAC Skills and Training


As an HVAC technician, you need to learn the tools and technology used to install, maintain, and repair HVAC systems. A training program can teach you the mechanical and electrical skills you need to ensure systems work correctly. This includes electrical components, damaged parts, and heating/cooling components.


To enter the field, you need a solid and diverse skillset. Troubleshooting and problem-solving skills will help you diagnose and repair HVAC malfunctions. And strong customer service and communication skills will help you better interact with a wide range of customers.


[Most HVAC technicians are required to be certified by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/heating-air-conditioning-and-refrigeration-mechanics-and-installers.htm#tab-4) , so you’ll want to obtain this certification. From here, many HVAC shops and more tenured technicians offer apprenticeship programs where you can continue training while earning money. As you gain more experience, you may be called on to assist with more complex jobs and learn more advanced skills of the trade.


## Are Auto Mechanics in Demand?


While auto mechanics continue to see a steady demand for their skills7, some states and regions, such as[Colorado](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-3023.00?st=CO) ,[Georgia](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-3023.00?st=GA) ,[New Jersey](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-3023.00?st=NJ) , and[New York](https://www.onetonline.org/link/localtrends/49-3023.00?st=NY) , see demand well above the national average due to the high volume of vehicles that need service and repair in urban areas. As with other trades careers, older mechanics who retire or leave the profession need to be replaced. And new technologies like electric vehicles need capable workers with new skills to service them.


### Automotive Skills and Training


If you want to break into the automotive mechanic space, you must develop a variety of both soft and technical skills to make sure you provide the best services possible to customers. You need strong attention to detail paired with mechanical, organizational, and troubleshooting skills to succeed in this field. A good auto mechanic school will teach you the ins and outs of engine components, major parts, and how they all work together.


The industry standard certification that most shops require comes from the[National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence (ASE)](http://www.ase.com/) . ASE certified programs show your understanding of the[core competencies of automotive repair and generally lead to higher paying positions](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/automotive-service-technicians-and-mechanics.htm#tab-4:~:text=Licenses%2C%20Certifications%2C%20and%20Registrations) . ASE also offers more than 50 certifications you can test for based on the skills you want to acquire. With tests for diesel engines, collision repair, and even trucking equipment, there is something for every aspiring automotive mechanic.


## Benefits of a Career in the Trades


If you like to work with your hands and hate the idea of sitting behind a desk all day, the Trades might be the right career path for you. Careers as an HVAC technician, electrician, or auto mechanic are physical jobs that can provide honest work and solid job satisfaction.
