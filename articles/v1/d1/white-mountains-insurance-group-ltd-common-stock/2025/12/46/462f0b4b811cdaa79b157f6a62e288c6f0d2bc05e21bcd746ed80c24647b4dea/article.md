# White Mountains Completes Sale of Bamboo

#### White Mountains Completes Sale of Bamboo


December 8, 2025 at 8:00 AM EST


[PDF Version](https://investor.whitemountains.com/node/18186/pdf)


HAMILTON, Bermuda


, Dec. 8, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- White Mountains Insurance Group, Ltd. (NYSE: WTM) ("White Mountains") announced today that it has completed the sale of a controlling interest in Bamboo to affiliates of funds advised by CVC Capital Partners ("CVC"). White Mountains continues to retain an approximately 15% fully-diluted equity stake in Bamboo.
