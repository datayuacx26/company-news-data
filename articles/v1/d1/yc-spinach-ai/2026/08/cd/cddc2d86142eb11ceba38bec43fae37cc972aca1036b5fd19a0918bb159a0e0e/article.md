## What Is tl;dv?


tl;dv is a[meeting intelligence tool](https://thebusinessdive.com/tldv-review) built primarily for sales and customer success teams. Teams comparing[AI meeting notes tools](https://www.spinach.ai/blog/ai-meeting-notes) will find the options vary considerably in scope. It joins your video calls, records them, creates AI-generated summaries, and lets you clip and share key moments from a recording.


The product is well-regarded for its timestamped clip reels, which let salespeople share a 90-second customer clip with a colleague instead of forwarding a full recording. Its integrations with Salesforce and HubSpot let reps push call notes into CRM records without manual copy-paste.


### Where tl;dv Focuses Its Energy


tl;dv is built around the individual contributor workflow, particularly account executives and customer success managers who need to document and share customer conversations quickly. Key capabilities include:


- Meeting recording across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, with AI-generated summaries delivered after the call ends
- Timestamped moment tagging, so users can bookmark key parts of a conversation and share them as short clips
- CRM logging to Salesforce and HubSpot, pushing call summaries and selected clips into contact or deal records
- A searchable library of past recordings, filterable by keyword or speaker


What tl;dv stops short of is company-wide conversation governance. It produces a per-user library of recordings, not a single organizational record accessible across teams with consistent policy, retention rules, and retrieval.


## What Is Spinach AI?


Spinach AI is an enterprise conversation intelligence platform that joins your Zoom, Meet, Teams, or Webex meetings, captures decisions and action items with named owners during the call, and routes structured outputs to your tools when the meeting ends. Deployed org-wide by an admin, it creates a single governed record across every team’s meetings with enforced retention policy.


Where most meeting tools stop at the transcript, Spinach produces governed conversation data that feeds Jira tickets, CRM records, Slack summaries, and connected agents. The organizational buyer gets a single, policy-controlled record across every team’s meetings, not a different tool per person generating uncontrolled, siloed output.


Spinach connects to Jira, Linear, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, and more, with MCP available on Business and Enterprise plans for teams building custom workflows on top of conversation data.
