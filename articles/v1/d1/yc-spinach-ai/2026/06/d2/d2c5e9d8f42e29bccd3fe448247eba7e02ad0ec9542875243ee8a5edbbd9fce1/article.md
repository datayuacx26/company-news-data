## What is Conversation Intelligence Software?


Conversation intelligence software records, transcribes, and analyzes spoken conversations, typically sales calls, customer support interactions, and team meetings, to surface insights that would otherwise disappear the moment a call ends. At its core, it uses AI to identify patterns across conversations: which topics come up most, how deals progress, where reps lose momentum, and what separates high-performing calls from struggling ones. The[global conversation intelligence software market](https://www.theinsightpartners.com/reports/conversation-intelligence-software-market) reached $24.81 billion in 2025 and is projected to grow to $52.54 billion by 2034, while the broader[AI meeting assistant market](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-meeting-assistant-market-report) is forecast to expand from $4.3 billion in 2026 to $21.5 billion by 2033, reflecting rising demand for automated meeting insights.


The category sits at the intersection of call intelligence software, sales intelligence tools, and revenue intelligence, making it useful across sales, customer success, and revenue operations teams.

## Sybill


Sybill is an AI sales assistant that analyzes both verbal and nonverbal cues from sales calls to generate deal summaries, next steps, and CRM updates automatically. It pulls behavioral signals like attention and sentiment into a “magic summary” after each call, giving sales reps a quick read on buyer intent without manual note-taking.


Where Sybill stands out is its focus on the full buyer persona instead of only call transcripts. It tracks engagement patterns across the deal cycle and feeds that context into follow-up emails and CRM fields.


Key capabilities include:


- Auto-generated call summaries with nonverbal signal analysis, so reps understand what was said and how engaged buyers appeared throughout the conversation.
- [CRM auto-fill for Salesforce and HubSpot](https://www.spinach.ai/blog/sync-google-meet-notes-action-items-hubspot-automatically) , reducing the manual data entry that typically follows every sales call.
- Follow-up email drafting that includes deal context and buyer behavior signals captured during the call.
- Deal health tracking across multiple conversations, giving managers a pattern view instead of a single-call snapshot.


Sybill is built squarely for sales teams and lacks the agile meeting support, ticketing integration, or sprint ceremony features that engineering and product teams need. If your use case goes beyond sales calls into cross-functional team meetings with action item tracking and project management output, it stops short of what tools like Spinach AI deliver.


## Attention

It records, transcribes, and analyzes calls, then surfaces deal risks, coaching signals, and next steps without requiring a separate app.


## Chorus.ai


Chorus.ai, now part of ZoomInfo, records and analyzes sales calls to surface deal risks, coaching opportunities, and buyer signals. It captures calls, emails, and meetings using conversation intelligence, then syncs activity data to CRM automatically.


Where Chorus focuses on sales rep coaching and pipeline visibility, it stops at the revenue org. Teams outside sales rarely touch it. Spinach works across every team function,[converting meetings into tickets](https://www.spinach.ai/blog/convert-meeting-transcripts-to-jira-tickets) that land in your project tools before the call ends.


## Gong


Gong is one of the most recognized names in conversation intelligence, built for revenue teams. It records and analyzes calls, emails, and meetings to surface coaching insights, deal risks, and pipeline trends.


Where Gong excels is depth of sales analytics. Teams get visibility into talk ratios, topic tracking, and competitor mentions across every rep’s book of business. Its deal intelligence layer flags at-risk opportunities before they slip, which makes it a go-to for sales managers running large teams.


The tradeoff is cost and complexity. Gong’s pricing is enterprise-tier, often running well into five figures annually, and the setup investment reflects that.


## Fireflies.ai


[Fireflies.ai](https://www.spinach.ai/blog/fireflies-alternatives) records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Its AI search lets you find any moment from past calls, and it generates conversation summaries with action items after each session.


Where it stops: Fireflies produces documentation. It won’t file a ticket, update a sprint, or connect what was said to what gets built. Teams that need outcomes from their meetings instead of records tend to hit that ceiling quickly.


- Free tier available with limited transcription minutes
- Integrates with Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, and major CRMs
- Supports 100-plus languages for transcription


## Otter.ai


[Otter.ai](https://www.spinach.ai/blog/otter-ai-alternatives) focuses on transcription and meeting notes, with real-time captions, automated summaries, and a searchable transcript library. It connects with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, and its free tier covers basic recording and transcription for individual users.


Where it stops: Otter generates documentation, not outcomes. There are no native ticket-creation workflows, no sprint ceremony support, and no action item routing to project management tools. Sales and engineering teams end up re-keying everything manually after the call.


Otter.ai suits individuals who need clean transcripts. Teams that need decisions turned into trackable work will hit its ceiling fast.


## Avoma


Avoma is[an AI meeting assistant](https://www.spinach.ai/blog/ai-meeting-notes) built for revenue teams that records, transcribes, and analyzes calls to surface coaching insights and deal intelligence. It leans more toward sales coaching than pure transcription tools, with features like talk-time ratios, keyword tracking, and topic-based scorecards that managers can use to review rep performance.


Where Avoma earns attention is its meeting lifecycle coverage: scheduling, live note-taking, and post-call CRM sync all run through one interface. Teams using Salesforce or HubSpot can push structured call summaries directly to contact records without manual entry.


The tradeoff is scope. Avoma is designed for customer-facing conversations, so if your use case spans internal planning, standups, or cross-functional syncs, the coaching-centric output feels mismatched. Pricing scales by seat and feature tier, which can add up quickly for larger teams needing full AI analysis across every call.

## Final Thoughts on Conversation Intelligence Software Tools


Your meetings already produce decisions, blockers, and action items. The question is whether your conversation intelligence software turns those into trackable work or just another transcript to skim later. If you want standups that file tickets and retros that update your backlog before the call ends, Spinach is built for that. Most tools stop at documentation; you need one that ships.


**Which conversation intelligence tool works best for agile software teams?**


Spinach AI is purpose-built for agile teams, with ceremony-specific outputs for standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives that connect directly to Jira and Linear. Sales-focused tools like Gong and Chorus.ai optimize for deal coaching and pipeline visibility, while general transcription tools like Otter and Fireflies stop at documentation without ticketing workflows.


**How do I choose between conversation intelligence software that focuses on transcription versus outcomes?**


Start by mapping what happens after the call ends. If your team needs clean records and search, transcription-focused tools work. If you’re spending 15+ minutes per meeting manually creating tickets and routing action items to project management tools, you need an outcomes-focused platform that automates ticket creation and decision capture before the call ends.


**Can conversation intelligence software integrate with project management tools like Jira and Linear?**


Most sales-focused conversation intelligence platforms sync to CRMs but lack native project management integrations. Spinach connects directly to Jira, Linear, Asana, ClickUp, and Trello without Zapier middleware, filing tickets with full meeting context. Transcription tools typically require manual copy-paste or third-party automation to move decisions into ticketing systems.


**What’s the difference between per-seat pricing and usage-based pricing for conversation intelligence software?**


Per-seat pricing charges a flat monthly rate per user regardless of meeting volume, while usage-based models meter minutes or charge based on feature consumption. High-meeting-volume teams often hit $1,500+ per user annually on usage-based platforms, whereas flat per-seat pricing at $4-5 per user per month stays predictable even during sprint cycles with heavy ceremony schedules.


**When should I consider enterprise conversation intelligence features like SOC 2 and HIPAA compliance?**


If your team handles regulated data (healthcare PHI, financial records, legal matters) or your IT department requires record-by-default governance and org-wide deployment controls, prioritize tools with SOC 2, HIPAA/BAA, single-tenant deployment, and compliance agents from the start. Most platforms gate these features behind enterprise tiers or don’t offer them at all.


**What’s the difference between sales conversation intelligence and meeting intelligence for agile teams?**


Sales conversation intelligence focuses on deal scoring, pipeline risks, and rep coaching across customer calls, while meeting intelligence for agile teams extracts decisions, blockers, and action items from internal ceremonies like standups and sprint planning. Tools like Gong and Chorus optimize for revenue metrics; Spinach optimizes for sprint velocity and ticket creation.


**Zoom Revenue Accelerator vs Gong — which one integrates better with Jira?**


Neither Zoom Revenue Accelerator nor Gong offers native Jira integration for ticket creation. Both sync to CRMs for sales activity tracking, but they stop at documentation. Spinach connects directly to Jira and Linear, filing tickets with full meeting context before the call ends.


**Can I use conversation intelligence software without a sales team?**


Yes, but most conversation intelligence platforms are built for sales-only use cases and lack the ceremony-specific outputs agile teams need. Spinach works across sales, support, product, and engineering teams, with purpose-built intelligence for standups, retrospectives, and sprint planning.


**How do I evaluate conversation intelligence software for regulated industries like healthcare?**


Prioritize SOC 2 and HIPAA compliance from the start, along with BAA availability, single-tenant deployment options, and compliance agents that flag high-risk conversations. Most platforms gate these features behind enterprise tiers or don’t offer them at all, so verify coverage before committing to a proof of concept.


**What does Salesloft do compared to standalone conversation intelligence tools?**


Salesloft is a sales engagement platform with conversation intelligence built in, combining cadence automation, email sequencing, and call recording in one interface. Standalone conversation intelligence tools like Gong and Spinach focus exclusively on capturing and analyzing conversations, often with deeper AI analytics and broader integration support.

Otter offers 300 minutes per month with export limits, Fireflies locks export behind paid tiers, and Zoom AI Companion requires a paid Zoom account. Spinach provides a free tier up to 50 users with full functionality and no export restrictions.


**Can conversation intelligence software capture in-person meetings or only virtual calls?**


Most conversation intelligence platforms only join virtual calls on Zoom, Meet, or Teams. Spinach includes mobile Quick Record for in-person and hybrid meetings, running the same AI processing pipeline on audio captured outside video platforms.


**How does a native MCP server change what conversation intelligence software can do?**


A native MCP server connects meeting context directly to AI coding agents like Claude Code, ChatGPT, and Cursor with single OAuth authentication, so architectural decisions and sprint priorities become first-class inputs to development workflows. Most competitors require custom middleware or limit access to meetings you personally attended.


**When should I choose per-seat pricing over usage-based pricing for conversation intelligence?**


Choose per-seat pricing when your team runs high meeting volumes, since usage-based models can reach $1,500+ per user annually for teams with frequent ceremonies. Flat per-seat pricing at $4-5 per user per month stays predictable regardless of how many standups, planning sessions, or retrospectives you run each sprint.


**What’s the difference between G2 conversational intelligence rankings and actual workflow automation?**


G2 conversational intelligence scores measure user satisfaction with analysis and insights, but most top-ranked tools still require manual ticket creation and CRM updates after the call. Check whether the platform offers native project management integrations and automatic ticket filing, not just high ratings for transcription quality.
