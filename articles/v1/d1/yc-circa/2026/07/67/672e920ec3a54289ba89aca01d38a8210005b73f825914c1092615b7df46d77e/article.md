# 5 Top Questions To Ask In Your Event Marketing Software Evaluation

Whether your company is just starting or looking to scale to that next level, the right technology and software will help you take those next steps with confidence. An RFP, or Request for Proposals, is an information-gathering document that you can use as a first step in the softwar e search to help narrow down vendor offerings to find one that fits your team’s unique needs, features, and of course, budget.


In the event marketing world, there are plenty of software options to choose from. From traditional marketing solutions to CRM platforms masquerading as marketing platfor ms, the industry is full of competition. By asking the right questions in your RFP and doing extensive research before you make a decision, your team can find the perfect solution to take your marketing events to t he next level – and allow your team to operate as a more integrated part of your larger marketing team.
