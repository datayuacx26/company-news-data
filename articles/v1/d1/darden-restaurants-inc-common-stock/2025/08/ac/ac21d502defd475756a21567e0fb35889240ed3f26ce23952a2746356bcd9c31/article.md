### Darden Restaurants to Host Fiscal 2026 First Quarter Conference Call on September 18


August 21, 2025


ORLANDO, Fla.


, Aug. 21, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- Darden Restaurants, Inc., (NYSE: DRI) plans to release its fiscal 2026 first quarter financial results before the market opens on Thursday, September 18, 2025, with a conference call to follow at 8:30 am ET. Rick Cardenas, CEO, and other senior management will discuss first quarter results and conduct a question and answer session. For those who cannot listen to the live broadcast, a replay will be available shortly after the call.
