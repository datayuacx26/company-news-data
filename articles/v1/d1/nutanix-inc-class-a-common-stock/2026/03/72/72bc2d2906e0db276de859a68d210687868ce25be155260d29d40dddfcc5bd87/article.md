# Nutanix Agentic AI: Scaling the Next Generation of Intelligence

## Orchestrate and Secure: The Infrastructure Foundation


Our differentiation is rooted in how we orchestrate accelerated computing by running Kubernetes on VM-based nodes, delivering high performance, strong security, and efficient utilization to lower cost per token. Shared infrastructure requires optimized resource orchestration and security. This is why our solution is anchored on our AHV hypervisor for strong workload isolation and simplified day 2 operations. The key is to deliver this without sacrificing performance.


With early access NVIDIA topology-aware AHV, we automatically optimize workload placement across GPU-dense servers for maximum performance without the need for manual tuning. And, by offloading Nutanix Flow virtual networking on NVIDIA BlueField DPUs (data processing units), we shift networking and security overhead off the CPU (central processing unit) to preserve inference cycles while maintaining maximum throughput and robust isolation.[Read to learn more](https://www.nutanix.com/blog/orchestrating-and-securing-ai-factory-infrastructure-with-nutanix-agentic-ai)


## **Accelerate: The Intelligent AI PaaS Layer**


AI engineers and Agentic AI developers rely on a rapidly expanding set of tools and frameworks to do MLOps, fine tune models, build and run agents and connect them to enterprise data. To help them get started, the Nutanix Kubernetes Platform (NKP) now comes with a built-in catalog of the leading open source AI tools and agentic frameworks. We also complement this open catalog with the more advanced services from our Nutanix Enterprise AI (NAI) product. Version 2.6 of NAI extends our mature Model as a Service platform (MaaS), with built-in enterprise controls for local inferencing with models from NVIDIA and Hugging Face, with a new AI Gateway to provide a unified entry point with standardized access, security, and usage governance to both cloud-hosted and private LLMs.


We’ve also added MCP server support, enabling agents to securely connect to enterprise tools and data sources. And with new support for the NVIDIA Nemotron family of open models, datasets, and training tools, developers can build agentic systems designed to reason, use tools safely, and execute complex, multi-step workflows with greater autonomy.[Read to learn more](https://www.nutanix.com/blog/building-enterprise-ai-stack-with-nutanix-agentic-ai)


## **Integrate: Foundational Data Services for AI**


Agentic AI depends on enterprise-grade data services, and is only as good as the data it can access. As an NVIDIA-validated storage solution and a design partner in NVIDIA’s STX program, Nutanix Unified Storage (NUS) addresses data locality by enabling GPU-accelerated transformation and vectorization within the storage cluster, delivering a high-performance data fabric without bottlenecks. With linear scale to thousands of GPU clients, low-latency NFS over RDMA ( S3 over RDMA coming soon), and KV cache offload to free GPU memory, customers can support larger context windows and more concurrent users while lowering overall cost per token.[Read to learn more](https://www.nutanix.com/blog/data-foundation-of-ai-factory-enabling-agentic-ai-with-nus)


## The Bottom Line: AI Complexity as a Business Advantage


The future belongs to the Agentic Enterprise. By orchestrating the interaction between GPU compute, software-defined networking, and enterprise data services, Nutanix is doing more than just racking and stacking hardware. We are providing a running and regulated environment that turns AI complexity into a scalable competitive edge. Through our deep engineering partnerships with NVIDIA and leaders like Cisco, Dell, and Supermicro, Nutanix is ensuring that the next generation of intelligence is not just powerful, but sustainable.
