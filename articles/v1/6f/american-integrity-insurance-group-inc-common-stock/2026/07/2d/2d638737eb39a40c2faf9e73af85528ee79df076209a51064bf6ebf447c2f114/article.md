# American Integrity Vice President of Claims Trevor McDonald Named PropertyCasualty360’s 2026 Claims Leader of the Year

### American Integrity Vice President of Claims Trevor McDonald Named PropertyCasualty360’s 2026 Claims Leader of the Year


July 28, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s206.q4cdn.com/412004638/files/doc_news/American-Integrity-Vice-President-of-Claims-Trevor-McDonald-Named-PropertyCasualty360s-2026-Claims-Leader-of-the-Year-2026.pdf)


*National recognition honors McDonald’s leadership in building one of the property and casualty industry’s most trusted, innovative, and customer-focused claims organizations*


TAMPA, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


American Integrity Insurance Group, Inc. (NYSE: AII) (“American Integrity,” “we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Company”), a Tampa-based property and casualty insurance holding company and one of Florida’s leading providers of residential property insurance, today announced that its Vice President of Claims, Trevor McDonald, has been named PropertyCasualty360’s 2026 Claims Leader of the Year in the Insurance Carriers category. The honor is part of the national Insurance Luminaries Awards, which recognizes claims executives whose leadership, innovation, and commitment to policyholders are helping shape the future of the property and casualty insurance industry.


As Vice President of Claims, McDonald oversees one of the Company’s most critical functions, ensuring policyholders receive timely, compassionate support during some of the most challenging moments they experience as homeowners.


McDonald’s recognition reflects both his personal leadership and American Integrity’s broader commitment to delivering a claims experience that combines innovation, responsiveness, and compassion. As the Company continues expanding throughout the Southeast, his leadership will remain central to fulfilling American Integrity’s promise to policyholders when they need their insurance company most.


Under McDonald’s leadership, American Integrity has built one of the industry’s premier claims organizations by combining operational excellence, data-driven decision-making, catastrophe preparedness, continuous process improvement, and a steadfast commitment to treating policyholders with empathy, fairness, and respect.


From routine homeowner claims to large-scale catastrophe events, McDonald has emphasized rapid response, proactive communication, operational excellence, and compassionate service—ensuring policyholders receive the support they need when they need it most. His leadership reflects the belief that technology should enhance—not replace—the personal relationships that define exceptional claims service. By leveraging technology and automation, McDonald’s team can spend more time focusing on what matters most: communicating with policyholders; solving problems; and helping families recover after a loss.


Beyond operations, McDonald has fostered a culture centered on accountability, continuous improvement, and professional development, equipping claims professionals with both the technical expertise and interpersonal skills needed to navigate complex situations with confidence and compassion. He has also played a significant role in developing future leaders throughout the organization, building a culture of collaboration, ownership, continuous learning, and servant leadership that reflects American Integrity’s mission of always putting people first.


“Trevor has built exactly the kind of claims organization every homeowner hopes they never need—but is grateful to have when disaster strikes,” said Bob Ritchie, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of American Integrity. “He understands that behind every claim is a family experiencing one of the most stressful moments of their lives. His leadership has created a culture that combines innovation with genuine compassion, operational excellence, and accountability. That combination is extraordinarily rare. This recognition reflects not only Trevor’s exceptional leadership but also the respect he has earned throughout our industry. Everyone at American Integrity is incredibly proud of this well-deserved honor.”


“This year, the team behind PropertyCasualty360.com was thrilled to scale up our annual professional recognition program to more accurately reflect the depth and breadth of the property and casualty insurance industry,” said PropertyCasualty360 Editor-in-Chief Elana Ashanti Jefferson. “These awards pay homage to the critical work around making insureds whole after a major loss while also empowering innovation and modernization to address ever more complex risks and consumer demands.”
