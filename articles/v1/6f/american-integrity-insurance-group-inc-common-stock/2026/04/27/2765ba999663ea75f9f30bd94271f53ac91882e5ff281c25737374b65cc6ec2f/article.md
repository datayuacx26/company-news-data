# Don't Get Swept Away: Flood Insurance Options for Florida Homeowners

Flooding is the[most common and costliest](https://flooddefenders.org/problem/) natural disaster in the U.S., and Florida homeowners face some of the highest risks in the country. From hurricanes and storm surges to heavy rainfall and rising rivers, it doesn’t take much for water to wreak havoc on your home.


Yet many homeowners are surprised to learn that **standard homeowners insurance policies don’t cover flood damage** . Without proper protection, you could be left footing the bill for costly repairs and the loss of cherished belongings.


If you live in Florida, understanding your flood insurance options isn’t just smart - it’s essential.


**Why Traditional Homeowners Insurance Doesn't Cover Floods**


Traditional homeowners insurance policies are designed to provide coverage for a wide range of perils, including fire, theft, and certain types of water damage. However, when it comes to floods, these policies typically exclude coverage for damage caused by rising water. This means that if your home is damaged by a flood, your traditional homeowners' insurance policy may not provide the financial protection you need to recover and rebuild.


In fact, basic homeowners insurance policies have not covered flood damage for many decades. According to the Natural Resources Defense Council,[a series of costly flood disasters](https://www.marketplace.org/story/2021/09/16/why-doesnt-homeowners-insurance-cover-flood-damage) in the 1940s, 1950s, and 1960s led private insurers to stop covering flood risk in a standard homeowners policy.
