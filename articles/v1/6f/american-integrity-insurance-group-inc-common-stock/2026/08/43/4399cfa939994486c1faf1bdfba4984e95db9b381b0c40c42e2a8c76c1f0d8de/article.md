# American Integrity’s Ryan Hodges Receives PropertyCasualty360’s Excellence in Risk Management Award

### American Integrity’s Ryan Hodges Receives PropertyCasualty360’s Excellence in Risk Management Award


August 4, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s206.q4cdn.com/412004638/files/doc_news/American-Integritys-Ryan-Hodges-Receives-PropertyCasualty360s-Excellence-in-Risk-Management-Award-2026.pdf)


*Senior Vice President of Risk Management recognized for advancing data-driven risk management, catastrophe analytics, and long-term financial resilience*


TAMPA, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


American Integrity Insurance Group, Inc. (NYSE: AII) (“American Integrity,” “we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Company”), a Tampa-based property and casualty insurance holding company and one of Florida’s leading providers of residential property insurance, announced that Senior Vice President of Risk Management Ryan Hodges received the Excellence in Risk Management Award in the Insurance Carriers category as part of PropertyCasualty360’s 2026 Insurance Luminaries Awards. This recognition honors professionals whose leadership, innovation, and commitment to excellence are helping to shape the future of the property and casualty insurance industry.


In today’s property insurance market, effective risk management is essential to maintaining financial strength, supporting policyholders, and creating sustainable long-term growth.


Through disciplined portfolio management, advanced catastrophe modeling, continuous exposure monitoring, and thoughtful reinsurance planning, American Integrity works to protect its financial strength while maintaining the flexibility to serve policyholders and agency partners in some of the nation’s most catastrophe-exposed markets.


As Senior Vice President of Risk Management, Hodges leads the Company’s risk management portfolio analytics and catastrophe modeling functions while supporting its reinsurance strategy. He provides leadership with timely insights into geographic and peril-specific risks, enabling informed underwriting, portfolio management, and reinsurance decisions that support American Integrity’s balanced portfolio, long-term resilience, and strategy for responsible growth throughout multiple states in the Southeast.


As American Integrity continues to invest in advanced analytics, automation, and modern risk management tools that enhance visibility into its evolving risk profile, Hodges works closely with underwriting, actuarial, finance, product management, and executive leadership to ensure risk insights are incorporated into strategic business decisions across the organization.


Beyond the technology itself, Hodges has helped foster a culture of data-driven decision-making. His ability to translate complex risk analytics into practical business insights has strengthened collaboration across multiple business functions and supports American Integrity’s commitment to disciplined execution and continuous improvement.


“Ryan’s leadership has bolstered the way we approach risk management by combining deep technical expertise with thoughtful business strategy,” said Bob Ritchie, Founder and CEO of American Integrity. “Disciplined risk management is one of the foundations of our long-term success. Ryan’s recognition reflects his expertise and our commitment to innovation, disciplined decision-making, and financial strength. We’re incredibly proud to see him receive this well-deserved honor.”


“This year, the team behind PropertyCasualty360.com was thrilled to scale up our annual professional recognition program to more accurately reflect the depth and breadth of the property and casualty insurance industry,” said PropertyCasualty360 Editor-in-Chief Elana Ashanti Jefferson. “These awards pay homage to the critical work around making insureds whole after a major loss while also empowering innovation and modernization to address ever more complex risks and consumer demands.”


Hodges’ recognition reflects American Integrity’s ongoing commitment to disciplined risk management, responsible growth, and long-term financial strength. These principles remain central to the Company’s mission of protecting policyholders, supporting agency partners, strengthening communities, and creating long-term value for stockholders.
