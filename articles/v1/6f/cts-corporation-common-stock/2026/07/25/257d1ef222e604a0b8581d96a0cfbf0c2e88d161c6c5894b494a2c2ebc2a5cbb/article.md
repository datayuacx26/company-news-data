# CTS to Attend 'Ocean Business' for the First Time

CTS Corporation will make its first ever exhibitor appearance at the **Ocean Business Expo** on 8-10 April, 2025, in Southampton, UK.


Ocean Business is dedicated to showcasing the most recent developments within the field of **marine technology** . To that end, CTS will bring along a range of **piezoelectric products** that enable all manner of **sonar systems** used for underwater navigation, communication, positioning and much more.


CTS' solutions include both piezoelectric single crystal and ceramic components in standard and advanced geometries, piezocomposite materials as well as transducer design and sub-assembly. As one of the world's premier developers and manufacturers of piezoelectric technologies, CTS has built an extensive portfolio of application-specific piezoelectric materials, optimized for particular end-uses.


Ocean Business attendees can find CTS at stand **V1F** where our representatives will be displaying select product samples and offering expert advice.
