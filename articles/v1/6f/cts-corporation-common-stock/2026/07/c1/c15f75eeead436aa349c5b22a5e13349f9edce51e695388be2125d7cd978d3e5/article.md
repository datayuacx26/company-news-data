# Miniaturized Clock Oscillator: Series 616

CTS is introducing the **series 616 small sized clock oscillator** (XO) to the market. Measuring just 1.6 x 1.2 mm, the series 616 represents the lastest step in CTS' efforts to provide system manufacturers with miniaturized frequency control solutions that retain the high performance standards, emblematic of CTS' products.


Joining the series 620 and 625 clock oscillators, the series 616 is a low-cost, low-voltage SMD solution supporting HCMOS output. Employing the latest IC technology, the series 616 has great frequency stability while also offering excellent phase noise/jitter performance.


Customers can choose between a variety of standard customization options including frequency stability, supply voltage and operating temperature range, ensuring that the performance capabilities of the series 616 will match the requirements of its intended use.


With short lead times and a more than resonable price point, the series 616 is intended as an affordable standard timing reference solution, easily acquired and effortlessly implemented in a broad range of systems and end applications.

## Product Features


- Miniature Size: 1.6 x 1.2 mm
- Frequency Range: 1.0 - 60 MHz
- Phase Jitter: 500 (fs typ)
- Temperature Range: -20 to 70°C, -40 to 85°C, -40 to 105°C
- HCMOS Output
- Frequency Stability: ±20, ±25, ±30, ±50 ppm
- Voltage: 1.8V, 2.5V, 3.3V
