# CTS Trade Show Tour - June 2025

## North American Trade Show Dates


June will be a busy month for CTS, as we will be attending a total of three trade shows across the North American continent. You can find our product experts at each of the following events where they will stand ready to discuss how we may help you find the right solution for your application. You will also get the chance to inspect our product solutions first-hand to ensure that you leave with the exact solution you were looking for.


### IMS Microwave


**June 15-20 - San Francisco, CA**


**The IEEE MTT-S International Microwave Symposium** , or simply **IMS** , is a premier venue for exploring the most recent advancements of **microwave technology** and related fields. The event offers an intriguing combination of presentations, discussions and technology exposition with numerous representatives from both the industry and academia. CTS will bring an extensive portfolio of **radio frequency filters** capable of attenuating and passing specific frequency ranges and a comprehensive range of quartz-based crystal oscillators enabling precise frequency control. Among these is our recently launched[series 139 OCXO](https://www.ctscorp.com/Resources/Press-Releases/Series-139-OCXO-Launch) , specifically designed to meet the stability, accuracy and phase noise demands of the Stratum 3E standard. IMS attendees can explore all our solutions at **booth 243** , where our licensed distributor **Richardson RFPD** will have them on display.

### International Symposium for Therapeutic Ultrasound


**June 21-24 - Banff, Canada**


**The International Symposium for Therapeutic Ultrasound (ISTU)** brings to light the latest innovations utilizing **ultrasound in therapeutic medicine** . CTS is a proud sponsor of the symposium and will be attending the event with a range of **piezoelectric solutions** , specifically designed for therapeutic ultrasound applications. Our product experts will set up shop at **booth 2** in the exhibition area, so head on over for a chat in between the sessions and open-forum discussions. From application-optimized **single crystal** and **piezoceramic materials** to advanced component shaping capabilities and transducer designing, we can help your ultrasound technology come to life.

### Sensors Converge


**June 24-26 - Santa Clara, CA**


Sensors Converge is a major trade show for all kinds of sensors, components and electronics. As a leading developer and manufacturer, CTS will make an appearance at the event, showcasing our various sensing and sensor-based solutions. This includes our contactless, non-intrusive **current sensors** , high IP-rated **temperature probes** and **thermistors** , multiple **switch options** such as **DIP** , **tactile** and **rotary** as well as components for **piezoelectric sensors** . You can inspect all of the above at **booth 505** .
