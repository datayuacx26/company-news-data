# MTB/MXB Band Pass Filters with X-Band Capabilities

CTS has added the series MTB and MXB bandpass filters to the company's portfolio of radio frequency (RF) filter solutions.


The series MTB and MXB bandpass filters have been optimized for **X-band operation** in the **6 - 12 GHz frequency range** - which is significantly higher than what conventional ceramic dielectric filters typically provide.


Combined with a **high peak to average power ratio handling** , the MTB and MXB filters are well suited for both **transmit** and **receive path filtering** in **linear satellite communications (SATCOM)** and **short/long pulsed radar applications** .


While highly innovative, the filters are constructed similarly to traditional monoblock filters, and consequently do not require exotic materials or advanced processing in order to achieve the X-band performance. The series MTB/MXB utilizes five and six resonators, respectively, and retains the same footprint, allowing developers to keep the same PCB layout across multiple platforms.


The product family consists of a total of seven models, each optimized for a specific bandpass range. All models can be acquired either by reaching out through our[website contact formular](https://www.ctscorp.com/Contact-Us) or via our global network of[licensed distributors](https://www.ctscorp.com/Contact-Us/Where-to-Buy) .
