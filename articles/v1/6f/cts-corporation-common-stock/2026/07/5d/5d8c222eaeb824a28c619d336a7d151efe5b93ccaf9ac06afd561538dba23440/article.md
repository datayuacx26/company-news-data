# CTS Introduces Busbar Mount Current Sensors

CTS Corporation (NYSE: CTS) is introducing the series CTS-CS-BAX-20 **busbar mount current sensor** as an off-the-shelf current sensing solution for busbar systems in the mid-to-high current range.


The CTS-CS-BAX-20 will provide non-intrusive measurements of AC and DC currents with excellent accuracy and linearity. It can be calibrated to one of four current ranges, but custom configurations can be accomodated as well upon request.


Specifically designed for busbar system integration, the CTS-CS-BAX-20 offers seamless installation and easy maintenance thanks to the non-contacting, Hall-effect sensing method. Available for direct purchase, the busbar mount current sensor can be acquired via the contact formular on www.ctscorp.com and, at a later date, through our global distributor network.
