# Meet CTS at SENSOR+TEST 2025

CTS will be exhibiting at this year's SENSOR+TEST measurement fair in Nuremberg, Germany. The event takes place on 6-8 May, 2025, and attendees will find the CTS representatives at **booth 1-642** in **hall 1** . Stop by for a first-hand look of our high-quality products, and let's discuss how we might partner up to develop a solution that will fit your performance requirements.


[CTS at SENSOR+TEST 2025](https://www.sensor-test.de/en/exhibitors/search-ep/exhibitor/1577)


---


SENSOR+TEST is a leading forum for sensing, measurement and testing technologies, and the annual event brings together experts from all over the world to explore and discuss the recent trends and developments of the industry. As always, CTS will bring to the table a plethora of product solutions that enable and enhance innovative technologies:


##


Piezoelectric Solutions


CTS offers a wide range of materials, components and transducers that utilize the direct and converse piezoelectric effects to turn mechanical energy into electrical energy and vice versa. Our solutions include piezoelectric single crystals, PZT and lead-free piezoceramics and composite materials. Of these, we manufacture piezoelectric components in various shapes and dimensions as well as ultrasonic transducers for both solids, liquids and air measurement.


[CTS Piezoelectric Products](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric)


## Temperature Sensors


CTS Temperature Solutions encompass numerous options for temperature sensing. We offer both PTC and NTC thermistors and configurable temperature probes that can be optimized for specific sensing media, e.g., air, liquids or solids. In addition, many of our temperature sensing solutions can be acquired with high IP-ratings for use in harsh and humid environments.


[CTS Temperature Solutions](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Temperature-Sensors)


## Current Sensors


CTS provides high-end, non-contacting current sensors for performance monitoring of systems across a wide current spectrum. Our solutions range from clamp-on modules over PCB and busbar mount sensors to multi-sensor battery monitoring systems that incorporate voltage and temperature measurements as well. Choose from a vast array of off-the-shelf products, or get in touch with our product experts for custom solutions.


[CTS Current Sensors](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Current-Sensors)
