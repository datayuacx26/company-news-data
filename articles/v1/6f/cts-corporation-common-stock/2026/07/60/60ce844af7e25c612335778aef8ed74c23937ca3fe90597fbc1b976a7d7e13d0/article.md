# Sponsoring the IEEE IUS 2025

## Discover Our Ultrasonic Solutions for the Medical & Industrial Markets


CTS will sponsor and attend the annual IEEE International Ultrasonics Symposium (IUS), held on 15 - 18 September 2025 in Utrecht, Netherlands.


This year marks the 62nd installment of the ultrasound-focused conference where experts from the industry gather to discuss the latest developments in the field of ultrasonic technology, and CTS will participate with representatives from both our R&D and sales divisions. The IEEE IUS 2025 features a tightly-packed schedule of plenary talks, courses, lectures, poster presentations and more. Registration for this industry-leading conference is still open, so be sure to secure your spot now:


[IUS 2025 Program](https://2025.ieee-ius.org/schedule-overview)


---


### CTS at IEEE IUS 2025


In between the IEEE IUS schedule sessions, attendees can explore the ultrasonic solutions of select exhibitors. The CTS delegation can be found by **booth #2** where we are displaying a range of our ultrasound-generating piezoelectric transducers, sensors and components. From application-optimized single crystal, PZT and lead-free materials to standard and custom-shaped piezoelectric components as well as ready-made transducer and sensor assemblies, we can devise a solution, guaranteed to meet the demands of your ultrasound technology. Stop by, and let's discuss how we might work together on a future project.


If you want to set up an appointment with our team in advance, please contact:
