# CTS Will Attend IAA Mobility 2025 in Munich

CTS Corporation will be in attendance at the IAA Mobility 2025 event, taking place on September 9-14 in Munich, Germany.


A leading international forum for mobility, sustainability and transportation technology, the IAA Mobility event brings together premier developers and manufacturers from the mobility space for six days of presentations, lectures, talks and of course innovative product showcasing.


CTS representatives will be on the ground over the course of the event, standing ready to provide expert advice on how to improve and enhance your automotive technology, If you would like to set up an appointment in advance with one of our experts, feel free to contact:


Mischa Keller, Director of Global Sensors Sales:mischa.keller@ctscorp.com


Benjamin Pszczola, Senior Application Engineer:benjamin.pszczola@ctscorp.com


At the event, our product experts will introduce you to the full range of CTS automotive solutions, such as our **Adaptive Driving Sensor Suite** ; a growing portfolio of precision position sensors that enable critical safety and ride comfort functionalities in current and next-gen vehicles. The suite includes our newly introduced single axis[chassis acceleration sensor](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Automotive-Position-Sensors/Accelerometer) with PSI5 output, our l[inear pedal position sensors](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Position-Sensors/Pedal-Position-Sensors) for enhanced, more efficient braking and many more.
