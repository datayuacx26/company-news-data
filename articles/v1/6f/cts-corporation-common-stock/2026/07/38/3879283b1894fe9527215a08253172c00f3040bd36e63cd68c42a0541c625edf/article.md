# Presentating New Piezo Research at IWATMD 2025

CTS Corporation will be presenting new research results at the annual **International Workshop on Acoustic Transduction Materials and Devices (IWATMD)** that takes place May 13-15 at Penn State University in Pennsylvania, US. The workshop is hosted by the Penn State Materials Research Institute with CTS as a main sponsor.


IWATMD provides a forum for reports on, and discussions of, the state-of-the-art in materials and devices to generate, detect and suppress sound. Attendees will enjoy an interactive environment that fosters formal and informal discussions and technical exchanges between scientists.


[IWATMD 2025 Website](https://www.mri.psu.edu/events/2025-international-workshop-acoustic-transduction-materials-devices-iwatmd)
