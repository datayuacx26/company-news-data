## Improving Quality of Life - Solutions for Medical Applications


CTS Corporation offers a variety of advanced materials and electronic components for the medical marketplace. Experienced in applications such as CPAP machines, medical equipment control panels, patient handling equipment, ultrasonic imaging, intra-vascular ultrasonic imaging and therapeutic ultrasound, CTS’ components are trusted by leading OEMs around the world.

### Critical Temperature Sensing


Maintaining the right temperature of bloodworks, DNA samples and organs for transplantation is naturally of the utmost importance, requiring highly accurate and reliable means of temperature monitoring. CTS will showcase our extensive portfolio of temperature sensors at COMPAMED 2025, encompassing high-end thermistor solutions, dedicated temperature probes for air/gas, liquid and contact surface measurements as well as glass-encapsulated thermistors for the highest levels of reliability and ruggedness. Contact our representatives with details of you specific technology and end use, and we will devise a tailor-made solution for you.
