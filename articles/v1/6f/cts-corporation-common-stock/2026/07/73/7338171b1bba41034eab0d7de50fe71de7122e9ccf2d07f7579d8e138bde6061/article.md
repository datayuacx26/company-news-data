# New Miniaturized Clock Oscillator

CTS is releasing a new miniaturized clock oscillator to the markets. The **series 620** is a general purpose clock oscillator with HCMOS output. It comes with a compact 2.0 x 1.6mm footprint, and as such, it is the smallest yet in CTS' product portfolio, constituting the most recent step in the company's efforts to provide miniaturized frequency control solutions.


In addition to its small size, the series 620 clock oscillator is characterized by its low power consumption with 1.8V, 2.5 and 3.3V supply options and its high frequency stability over a wide temperature range.


The series 620 clock oscillator will be ideal as a local timing reference in various telecommuncation, geolocation and network applications - particularly in space-constrained system environments where compact sub-components are required.
