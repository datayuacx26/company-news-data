# Maglab Resources Transitioning to CTS Corporation!

## **maglab Resources Transitioning to CTS Corporation**


We’re excited to announce that maglab’s website and resources are being integrated into the CTS Corporation site! This move brings the expertise of maglab’s innovative current sensor and electrified system specialists directly to CTS, enhancing our shared knowledge base and service offerings. By uniting our teams and resources under the CTS brand, we’re strengthening our collaboration to serve you better than ever.

## **maglab Services Are Here to Stay!**


We remain dedicated to delivering the same trusted services:


- **Design of Magnetic Components and Systems**
We specialize in designing and manufacturing ferromagnetic components such as shields and cores. Each component is optimized for material, geometry, and environmental factors, making them ideal for applications like contactless current and position sensing.


- **Engineering Support, Analysis, and System Optimization**
Using FEM modeling, we analyze and refine your magnetic sensor system, considering factors like compact design, material performance, thermal power dissipation, and high-frequency behavior. Our expert team ensures you achieve the best balance of performance and cost.


- **Prototyping Quality, and Measurements**
From initial design to final characterization, we support your design-in process with customer-specific prototypes and quality control solutions tailored to your production needs.
