# Series 139 OCXO Launch

**Lisle, IL** – CTS Corporation (NYSE: CTS) is expanding its frequency control product portfolio with the **S** **eries 139 oven-controlled crystal oscillator (OCXO)** .


As a quartz-based OCXO, the series 139 inherently offers superior short term stability while still being a cost-effective choice of frequency control compared to e.g., rubidium and cesium oscillators.


What truely sets the series 139 apart, however, is its enhanced phase noise performance that allows it to operate at a greater efficiency without the interference of unwanted signals.


The series 139 also features a widened range of standard operating frequencies with custom configurations available upon request. It comes in either a surface mount or through-hole package and is compliant to Stratum 3E per GR-1244-CORE.

- Multiple Standard Frequencies (Custom Configurations Available)
- 3.3V or 5.0V
