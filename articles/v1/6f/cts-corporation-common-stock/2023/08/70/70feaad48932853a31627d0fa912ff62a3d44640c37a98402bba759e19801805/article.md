# Solutions for Forklifts and Material Handling Applications

## Operator Interface / Control Panels


Forklift’s Human Machine Interface (HMI) systems can include mechanical switches, rotary controls, and digital displays etc. CTS provides a wide range of components for reliable and ergonomic operator interfaces.


### 291 Series Optical Encoder


The CTS[291 Series](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Passive-Components/Encoders) is a compact incremental rotary optical encoder for precise user input in industrial instruments and control panels. It generates clean A/B quadrature signals for accurate direction and speed detection and is available with optional detents and an integrated momentary switch.


Because it uses optical sensing rather than mechanical contacts, the 291 Series offers long operating life, smooth rotation, and reliable digital output, making it well suited for menu navigation, parameter adjustment, and system configuration.


Optical encoders use a light source and detector with a coded or reflective wheel to generate output signals. Series 291 encoder offers resolution up to 64 PPR (pulses per revolution), detent option and IP50 rating for dust and moisture protection. With no contacting elements, they can achieve rotational lifecycles of 1,000,000 to 3,000,000 cycles and offer better long term reliability than contacting encoder technologies.


### 229 Series Pushbutton Switches


[229 Series pushbutton switches](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Switches/Pushbutton-Switches) , also known as key switches, are mechanical devices used to make or break a circuit. They are designed for use in harsh environments, with a robust internal structure and optional IP67 rating for protection against dust and humidity.


Their long operating lifecycle of over 1,000,000 activations and mechanical robustness allow them to outperform traditional tactile switches, supporting dependable operation in demanding forklift and transportation control systems. Series 229 push button switches come with various configuration options of base styles, Circuit SPST or DPST, LED option and activation force.


### Tactile Switches


CTS offers a variety of[tactile switches](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Switches/Tactile-Switches) with multiple footprint options, sizes, and configurations. These cost effective membrane style switches provide excellent lifecycle performance and are suitable for mode selection and auxiliary functions such as horn control.


### Potentiometers / Rotary Controls (282/284/450 Series)


CTS has a long history in potentiometer design and production and offers products with various element types, package sizes, resistance values, rotational life ratings, and terminal configurations.


- **282 and 284 Series:** Use conductive plastic elements, providing long lifecycle and good dust and moisture protection for demanding environments.
- **450 Series:** Legacy CTS product that is robust and customizable, with a carbon composition element known for its consistent torque profile and stable resistance output widely used in industrial applications.
