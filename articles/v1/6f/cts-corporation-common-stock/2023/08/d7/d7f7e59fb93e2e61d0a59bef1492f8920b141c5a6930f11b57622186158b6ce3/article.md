# 5 Features of the New CTS Website

## 5 Features of the New CTS Website that You Need to Experience


We're excited to announce the launch of our redesigned website here at CTS! With a fresh look and enhanced features, we're dedicated to providing you with an exceptional online experience that reflects our commitment to innovation, excellence, and customer satisfaction.


So, what's new?


Sleek Navigation, Seamless Exploration


Navigating our website has never been easier. We've revamped our[navigation system](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products) to ensure effortless exploration and seamless access to the information you need. Whether you're searching for specific products, technical specifications, or[industry insights](https://www.ctscorp.com/Markets) , finding what you're looking for is just a click away. The new website features 8 concise product categories to help you select the product that is right for you.


- Visit our[Piezoelectric](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric) homepage to browse our wide selection of[Bulk Piezoceramics](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Bulk-Piezoceramics) ,[Single Crystals](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Single-Crystals) , Multilayer, and more.

- Are you specifying passive components in your electronics design? Visit the[Passive Components](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Passive-Components) category to view information on everything from[Crystals](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Passive-Components/Frequency-Control-Products/Crystals) to[EMI Filters](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Passive-Components/EMC-EMI-Filters) .
