# Find Your Ideal Piezoceramic Material

# The CTS Piezo Calculator


Quickly determine the resonance frequency, static displacement and signal response of your piezoceramic components with the **CTS Piezo Calculator** .


Using the CTS Piezo Calculator could not be easier. All you have to do is select your preferred piezoceramic material, choose the shape of your component and type in its dimensional measurements. The calculator will then provide a close proximation of the component's piezoelectric behaviour. It is completely free to use, so give it a try here:


[Download the CTS Piezo Calculator](https://www.ctscorp.com/Files/Product-Marketing-Documents/Piezoelectric/CTS-Piezoelectric-Calculator_1.3_Public.xlsx)


The CTS Piezo Calculator encompasses all[piezoceramic materials](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Bulk-Piezoceramics/Ceramic-Material-Properties) from CTS, including our PZT ceramics and our lead-free alternatives. It can determine the piezoelectric behaviour of the following standard component geometries:


-[Plates](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Bulk-Piezoceramics/Plates-wafers-bars-wedges)


-[Discs](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Bulk-Piezoceramics/Discs-rings-and-rods)


-[Rings](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Bulk-Piezoceramics/Discs-rings-and-rods)


-[Tubes](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Bulk-Piezoceramics/Tubes-Cylinders)


-[Shear Plates](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Bulk-Piezoceramics/Shear-Plates-and-Shear-Stacks)


-[Shear Tubes](https://www.ctscorp.com/Products/Piezoelectric/Bulk-Piezoceramics/Tubes-Cylinders)


NB: *The CTS Piezo Calculator is based on idealized formulars and will thus provide indicative performance values. Reach out to CTS for assistance in selecting the right piezoelectric material for your application.*
