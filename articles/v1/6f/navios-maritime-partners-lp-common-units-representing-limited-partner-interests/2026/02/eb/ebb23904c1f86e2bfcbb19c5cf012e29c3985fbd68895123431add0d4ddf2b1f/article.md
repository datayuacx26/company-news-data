# Navios Maritime Partners L.P. Announces the Date for the Release of Fourth Quarter Ended December 31, 2025 Results, Conference Call and Webcast

[PDF Version](https://ir.navios-mlp.com/node/17561/pdf)


**Conference Call & Webcast: Thursday, February 19, 2026 at 8:30 am ET**


PIRAEUS, Greece, Feb. 13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Navios Maritime Partners L.P. ("Navios Partners") (NYSE:NMM) announced today that it will host a conference call on Thursday, February 19, 2026 at 8:30 am ET, at which time Navios Partners' senior management will provide highlights and commentary on earnings results for the fourth quarter and full year ended December 31, 2025. The Company will report results for the fourth quarter and full year ended December 31, 2025, prior to the conference call.
