# Maya Hawke announces U.S. spring tour and artist presale

Maya Hawke announces a spring tour to accompany the release of her fourth album, *Maitreya Corso.*


She has chosen to tour *during* as opposed to after the album release to create a unique listening experience for fans rooted in live performance and designed to allow those fans to access the music before anyone else.


The songwriter and actress is planning a run of small-room shows to keep that connection front and center with a presale for tickets powered by Laylo. Maya shared:


“I learned recently that this is the best way of protecting the experience of concertgoing. I want people to get the best possible price on tickets and not get gouged by big companies or bots. I hope I’m coming to your city and hope to see you there!”


Here’s everything you need to know.


### 1. Register for the presale


Registration for the presale opens Monday, March 2 at 10AM EST and closes as soon as soon as the artist presale begins on Tuesday, March 3 at 10AM EST.


Fans must RSVP during this 24-hour presale window to receive a unique presale code. Registration and 2FA is required.

### 2. Receive your unique code


On Tuesday, March 3 at 11AM EST (one hour before presale begins), fans who successfully registered will receive a text message with instructions to access their unique code.


To redeem your code:


- Enter your phone number on the redemption page
- Receive your one-time presale code via SMS
- Use that code to access tickets when presale begins


Codes are tied to your phone number and cannot be shared or reused.


### 3. Use your code when presale opens


The artist presale begins Tuesday, March 3 at 12PM EST. Tickets are limited and expected to move quickly; the presale period ends at 11:59 p.m. local time on 3/3.


To enter the presale:


- Select your show date
- Enter your unique code
- Proceed to ticketing

## ❓ Presale FAQ


Why is Maya using unique codes?
This system helps protect fans by limiting brokers and resale activity, giving real fans a better chance to attend these shows.


Can I sign up for multiple cities?
Yes. Fans only need to register once. Your unique code can be used for participating shows (subject to availability and ticket limits).


How many tickets can I purchase?
There is a 4-ticket limit per show, except for Bowery Ballroom, which has a 2-ticket limit.


What if I don’t receive a code?
Codes will be sent via SMS at 11AM EST on March 3. If you don’t receive one, it likely means registration was not completed during the window or SMS verification was not finalized. If you believe this is an error, contact contact@laylo.com.


What happens if my code doesn’t work?
Your code will be sent via text message from Laylo and is tied to your phone number. Only that code will work. Codes cannot be reused or shared.
