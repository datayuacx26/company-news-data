Noah Kahan is focused on getting more real fans in the room on his global tour run.


Fresh off a massive run of sold-out shows, Noah just announced new dates across the UK, Europe, Australia, and New Zealand, giving fans early access through an artist presale designed to keep tickets in the hands of real fans.


The presale is powered by[Laylo’s RealFan technology](https://laylo.com/features/anti-broker) , which uses unique, one-time codes to help cut down on bots and resale activity and ensure more tickets go directly to the fans who signed up for the artist presale.


Here’s everything you need to know.


## 🎟️ How to Get Noah Kahan Presale Tickets


Noah’s presale uses **unique codes** , meaning each fan must register in advance and verify to receive access.


### Here’s how it works:


**1. Sign up before the deadline**
Fans needed to register for their city before **Monday, April 13 at 1:00 PM EDT** . If you missed the window, you will not receive a presale code.


**2. Unlock your unique code**
Before presale begins, fans who signed up will receive a message from Laylo with a link to unlock their code; messages will start arriving around 2AM ET.
‍
To redeem your unique code:


- Click the link in the text or email from Laylo
- Input your contact info when prompted
- Receive your unique code to on your device with link to ticket page


**3. Use your code when presale opens**
Once the presale goes live, head to the ticket link and:


- Enter your unique code
- Join the queue
- Purchase tickets while supplies last


Codes are one-time use and tied to your registration.
