# SINAI and GIST Impact Announce Partnership

We’re thrilled to announce our partnership with[GIST Impact](https://www.gistimpact.com/) , a global leader in impact valuation, climate risk, and benchmarking analytics.


At SINAI, our mission is to deliver audit-grade emissions data, finance-ready decarbonization modeling, and enterprise-scale software designed for the complexity of global operations—heavy industry, supply chains, and multi-regional compliance. Our AI-enabled platform helps enterprises stay audit-ready while uncovering ROI-positive decarbonization strategies.


Together with GIST Impact, we’re bringing companies a sustainability solution that unifies precision emissions data, dynamic financial modeling, and robust climate risk insights in one seamless system. The combined offering helps enterprises tackle both sides of the dual pressure they face: regulatory compliance and cost-effective emissions reduction.


SINAI provides the core carbon intelligence platform, automating Scope 1–3 accounting, identifying reduction opportunities, modeling financial impact, and generating investor-ready transition plans. GIST Impact provides deep analytics on physical and transition risk, impact valuation, and benchmarking, enabling scenario analysis, double materiality assessments, and disclosure alignment with frameworks such as the CSRD and CVM (Brazil).


> “We are thrilled to partner with GIST Impact to deliver a unified, enterprise-scale solution that integrates decarbonization intelligence with impact valuation and climate risk analytics. This partnership provides businesses with a unified system for regulatory compliance, strategic resilience, and measurable sustainability performance. Together, we empower companies to meet compliance and execute their reduction strategies with confidence.” — **Jess Waldeck, CEO, SINAI**

## With this partnership, companies can:


- Meet global reporting requirements (CSRD, CBAM, CVM, IFRS, and more)
- Assess and manage climate-related financial risks across facilities and supply chains
- Engage suppliers with granular, AI-driven Scope 3 emissions insights
- Prioritize cost-effective abatement projects using financial and risk metrics
- Deliver compliance-ready, defensible transition plans for investors and regulators


This partnership reflects our shared commitment to scaling profitable decarbonization and defensible sustainability outcomes for enterprise leaders navigating today’s complex climate landscape.
