# Pinch Desktop: real-time translation that works with every meeting app

**Pinch Desktop** is a Mac application powered by our latest voice translation model, **Falcon v1.3** , delivering real-time speech-to-speech translation across **50+ languages -** in *any* meeting app.


No plugins.


No per-app integrations.


No compatibility matrix.


Why build separate plugins for Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet? With **Pinch Desktop** , we solved the integration problem by moving the translation upstream.


## How Pinch Desktop works


Pinch Desktop operates at the system level by routing audio.


When installed on macOS, it creates a virtual input device called **Pinch Microphone** . This device appears alongside your normal inputs (MacBook mic, USB headset, etc.).


The key difference: its audio is generated by Pinch Desktop, not by hardware.


Our approach is simple:


1. Pinch Desktop captures audio from your microphone.
2. It translates the audio internally using the Pinch API.
3. The translated stream is presented to your system as a virtual “Pinch Mic”.


To Zoom, Teams, Meet - or anything else - it’s just another microphone. others in the meeting can hear translated speech in real-time in your voice.


**Getting started takes seconds:**


1. Select **Pinch Microphone** as your input device
2. Hold the shortcut key when you want translation


That’s it.


## Desktop features:


### Voice Clone


Pinch Desktop gives you control over how your translated voice is heard:


- **My Voice:** Your voice is cloned in real time. Same timbre, same tone - just another language.
- **Male Voice:** A neutral, standard male voice.
- **Female Voice:** A neutral, standard female voice.


For maximum personalization, **voice cloning** preserves your vocal identity across languages. The words change. The voice stays yours.


### Translation volume control


Hearing your own translated speech can be helpful or distracting. Pinch Desktop lets you control that.


- Participants always hear translation at full volume
- But users can lower or mute it locally


### Push-to-talk translation


Translation is intentional.


Pinch Desktop uses a **customizable keyboard shortcut** that acts like push-to-talk:


- Hold the shortcut → your speech is translated
- Release it → normal mic passthrough resumes


This keeps conversations clean and avoids unintended translation.


### Automatic transcripts


Every translated session generates transcripts saved locally in a folder.


They can act as: Meeting notes, conversation records or references for follow-ups.
