# Valmont Industries, Inc. to Host Investor Day on June 16, 2026

## Valmont Industries, Inc. to Host Investor Day on June 16, 2026


April 22, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.valmont.com/node/16271/pdf)


OMAHA, Neb.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 22, 2026-- Valmont® Industries, Inc. (NYSE: VMI), a global leader that provides products and solutions to support vital infrastructure and advance agricultural productivity, will host an Investor Day on Tuesday, June 16, 2026, beginning at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time in New York City.


President and Chief Executive Officer Avner Applbaum, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer John Schwietz, and other members of the senior leadership team will present a comprehensive update on the company’s long-term strategy, value drivers and growth outlook. The program will highlight how Valmont is building on a strengthened foundation to deliver sustainable, profitable growth across its Infrastructure and Agriculture segments. Leadership will provide their perspective on the accelerating utility infrastructure investment cycle, including demand tied to grid modernization and increasing power needs, and how these opportunities are expected to be a primary driver of Valmont’s long-term earnings growth, along with an update on the agriculture market and the actions underway to drive higher-margin growth in that segment.


To attend in person, please register in advance at the following link:[Valmont Industries 2026 Investor Day Registration](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fvalmont-2026-ir-day.open-exchange.net%2Fregistration&esheet=54520962&newsitemid=20260422605960&lan=en-US&anchor=Valmont+Industries+2026+Investor+Day+Registration&index=1&md5=5aacc708b511b640db9fcb6c079ba581) . Due to limited capacity, early registration is recommended. The event will include a presentation followed by a question-and-answer session.


A live webcast will be available at the start of the event. Please register for the virtual presentation at the following link:[Valmont Industries 2026 Investor Day Virtual Presentation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fvalmont-2026-ir-day-webcast.open-exchange.net%2Fregistration&esheet=54520962&newsitemid=20260422605960&lan=en-US&anchor=Valmont+Industries+2026+Investor+Day+Virtual+Presentation&index=2&md5=18f3fb4ca4f47be2321c0cc1317fd0aa) . A replay will be accessible following the conclusion of the program.
