[CadenceLIVE Silicon Valley 2026](https://events.cadence.com/event/CadenceLIVESV2026/agenda?RefId=home) has come to a close. What unfolded over the course of[the event](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/the-stage-is-set-design-for-ai-ai-for-design-at-cadencelive-silicon-valley) was a focused exploration of where semiconductor design is headed—one shaped by[AI-driven workflows](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/ai/overview.html) ,[advanced packaging](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/tools/design-excellence.html) , and[system-level complexity](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/tools/system-design-and-analysis.html) . From[keynote sessions](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/key-takeaways-from-cadencelive-silicon-valley-keynotes) to[technical tracks](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/what-to-expect-on-day-2-of-cadencelive-silicon-valley-2026) , the message was consistent: innovation at scale now depends on how effectively the industry integrates AI, heterogeneous integration, and accelerated computing.


The event opened with a[fireside chat](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/key-takeaways-from-cadencelive-silicon-valley-keynotes) between Anirudh Devgan, president and CEO of Cadence, and


Jensen Huang, founder and CEO of NVIDIA. The exchange set the tone for the event—grounded in both vision and execution—and stood out as one of the most engaging moments of the program.


That perspective carried forward through insights from industry leaders, including Charlie Kawwas, president of Broadcom, and Paul Cunningham, senior vice president and general manager of the System Verification Group at Cadence. Across discussions, a shared reality emerged: the rapid expansion of AI is driving unprecedented design complexity, making autonomous, AI-driven workflows not just valuable but necessary.


The momentum carried on into the[technical track presentation](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/day-1-track-summary-cadencelive-silicon-valley-2026) , with sessions on the[Cadence ChipStack AI Super Agent](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/advancing-design-productivity-through-ai-and-super-agents) , audio processing chips, predictive cooling technologies for AI accelerators and GPUs, and chiplet-based architectures, among other topics. These presentations offered detailed insights as speakers skillfully explored the intricacies of multi-die integration while addressing crucial challenges, including interconnect design, thermal management, system-level co-design, and more.


Across the track presentations,[demo booths](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/beyond-the-keynotes-where-innovation-took-a-tangible-form) , and networking sessions, one theme remained consistent: Designing chips is no longer about individual components, but about orchestrating entire systems with precision, speed, and intelligence.


Perhaps the most remarkable was the atmosphere of eagerness and excitement.[Conversations](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/expert-perspectives-from-across-the-physical-ai-ecosystem) extended far beyond the confines of slides and presentations, spilling into hallways and impromptu gatherings. Every corner turned into a spontaneous think tank, with discussions ranging from PCB and system design analysis to AI-driven EDA workflows.


## Making a Difference Through Volunteering


Attendees took a moment to step away from the hustle and bustle and participated in a truly rewarding experience. Together, we recognized that progress extends far beyond electronic systems design and into the heart of our communities. During the event, many attendees came together to assemble geometry kits for local STEM programs for youth. At the dedicated volunteer stations on the event floor, the spirit of teamwork and camaraderie flourished, leaving everyone with cherished memories and a renewed sense of purpose!


## Cadence Live Demo and Customer Booths: Learning Hubs


The[Cadence booth](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/beyond-the-keynotes-where-innovation-took-a-tangible-form) drew steady crowds—and for good reason.[Live demos](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/corporate-news/posts/beyond-the-keynotes-where-innovation-took-a-tangible-form) brought tools to life in a way slides never could. Attendees heard about the capabilities of Cadence tools and saw them in action, interacted with them, and explored them firsthand.


There was a palpable energy—part curiosity, part excitement—as attendees explored real-world applications and exchanged insights that went beyond presentations.


## Conversations That Continued Beyond Sessions


Throughout the event, the learning didn’t stop—it simply changed form.


The networking event became an extension of the technical sessions. Conversations moved fluidly from **AI model scaling challenges** to **chiplet interoperability** , from **EDA toolchains** to **future-ready silicon strategies** .


Sometimes, the next big breakthrough doesn’t start in a presentation; it starts through a casual conversation.


## More Than a Day, It Was Just the Beginning


The event proved that CadenceLIVE is where the future of silicon is engineered in real time.


From AI-driven compute platforms to deep dives into chiplet ecosystems and system-level design, the message was clear: the next era of innovation will be defined by integration, artificial intelligence, and acceleration.


As the event concluded on a high note, a sense of quiet anticipation builds for what lies ahead and how the insights gained will transform into tomorrow's breakthroughs.


Because here, the future of the silicon world isn’t just being discussed; it's being built!
