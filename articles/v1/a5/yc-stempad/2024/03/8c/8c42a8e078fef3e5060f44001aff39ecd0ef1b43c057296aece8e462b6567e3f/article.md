# Get Started With Stempad: Educator Guide

# Get Started With Stempad: Educator Guide


By Jordan Nguyen


Created on April 28, 2025


Great teaching often starts with a great plan. But between lesson prep, grading, and classroom management, finding the time for thoughtful planning can feel impossible. That's why we're excited to announce a new way for educators to lighten the load: **Stempad Copilot** , now integrated directly into the editor.


Copilot acts as your AI-powered teaching assistant inside Stempad. Whether you're drafting tomorrow's lecture, organizing resources for a new unit, or writing feedback for students, Copilot helps you move faster without sacrificing quality.


With Stempad's flexible block-based system and Copilot's AI support, lesson planning becomes less about busywork and more about teaching.


## Ways educators are already using Stempad x Copilot


We've[previously discussed](https://www.stempad.com/blog/stempad-for-educators) how educators can use Stempad's general features, but we've provided some tips here for Copilot specifically. Here's how educators are putting Copilot to work:


**Build lesson frameworks in minutes**
Start with a general topic or rough idea, and Copilot can help expand it into a complete lesson outline with objectives, activities, and discussion prompts.


**Simplify complex readings**
Drop in a dense article or research paper, and Copilot can generate simplified summaries, pull out key points, generate questions for classroom discussion, and more.


**Analyze table data**
You can share[tables](https://www.stempad.com/learn/blocks/table) with Copilot to get help analyzing data. This could be especially helpful if you're looking to analyze trends in student grades.


**Draft assessments and worksheets**
Need a quick quiz, a set of practice problems, or a reflection prompt? Copilot can suggest questions tailored to your content and grade level.


**Improve clarity of your materials**
Copilot can review your handouts, assignment sheets, or rubrics for grammar, clarity, and tone — helping you communicate more effectively with students and parents.


**Work with math equations**
Whether you need similar problems to create new worksheets or want to find a way to explain a specific concept, Copilot in Stempad supports[math typing](https://www.stempad.com/learn/blocks/math) in-chat. Send Copilot your math equations without worrying about clarity or formatting to get instant help!


**Support science writing and documentation**
From lab instructions to project guidelines, Copilot helps draft, revise, and cite technical documents more quickly.


**Streamline communications**
Use Copilot to draft quick, professional emails — whether you're reaching out to parents, administrators, or colleagues about classroom updates or student progress.


**Brainstorm enrichment ideas**
Looking for new ways to extend learning beyond the curriculum? Copilot can suggest project ideas, extracurricular activities, or cross-disciplinary lesson plans.
