# Boost Your Productivity with Stempad: Effective Notetaking Strategies


By Jordan Nguyen


Created on March 4, 2025


Taking notes is a crucial part of the college experience, but it can quickly become tedious and inefficient if you're using the wrong strategies. Traditional pen-and-paper notes can be slow and difficult to organize, while generic digital tools may lack the features needed for complex subjects. That's where[Stempad](https://www.stempad.com/) comes in—a specialized notetaking platform designed for STEM students and researchers.


With the right tool and approach, you can streamline your notetaking process, making it faster, neater, and easier to review. Here are some strategies to help you optimize your workflow with Stempad.


## 1. Use keyboard shortcuts for faster notetaking


One of Stempad's biggest advantages is its extensive set of[keyboard shortcuts](https://www.stempad.com/learn/shortcuts) . Compared to a mouse or stylus, keyboard shortcuts allow you to capture ideas much faster—especially when your professor is lecturing too quickly to write everything down manually.


Instead of manually searching for symbols or using complicated coding syntax, you can type mathematical expressions like square roots, integrals, or Greek letters using these shortcuts. They automatically convert plain text into properly formatted mathematical notation, saving you time and effort.


In subjects that frequently use variables and formulas, mastering a few key shortcuts can significantly speed up your notetaking process.


## 2. Organize notes with headers and lists


Good notes aren't just about writing things down—they need to be structured in a way that makes reviewing easy. Stempad includes headers, bullet points, and numbered lists to help you organize your content logically.


Unlike other notetaking apps that force you to choose between handling complex equations or having neatly structured typed notes, Stempad allows you to do both within the same document. This means you can create clear, structured notes with math symbols and explanations side by side.


Using headers to separate topics and lists to highlight key points makes it easier to scan your notes when reviewing for an exam. A well-organized document can save time when you need to quickly find an important formula or definition.


## 3. Add visual aids with sketches and diagrams


Some concepts are easier to understand visually instead of through text alone. In case you need to sketch something in your notes, Stempad offers[diagramming tools](https://www.stempad.com/learn/blocks/draw) that allow you to use a mix of your touchpad/mouse and keyboard.


Whether it's a physics free-body diagram, an[electrical circuit](https://www.stempad.com/learn/blocks/circuit) , or[bond-line diagrams for Ochem](https://www.stempad.com/learn/blocks/chemistry) (especially for pre-med students and bio/chem majors!), having the ability to[sketch](https://www.stempad.com/learn/blocks/draw) directly in your notes can make a big difference. Using a mix of visuals and typed notes helps reinforce key ideas and makes your notes more comprehensive.


## 4. Keep your notes organized


One of the biggest problems with traditional handwritten notes is that they can be misplaced, lost, or difficult to sort through. Stempad eliminates this issue by keeping all your notes in one place, which you can then organize into folders.


With everything stored digitally, you'll never have to worry about losing papers or carrying a bunch of papers in your bag.


## Conclusion


Effective notetaking isn't just about writing things down–it's about creating materials to help you study and understand concepts. With Stempad's specialized features, you'll be able to take clearer, faster, and more detailed notes without having to switch between multiple tools. Whether you're dealing with equations, diagrams, or text, Stempad provides the tools for practically every academic subject in a single platform.
