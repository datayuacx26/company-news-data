### Key Terms


ebitda


financial


EBITDA stands for earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization. It measures a company's profitability by focusing on the money it makes from its core operations, ignoring expenses like taxes and accounting adjustments. Investors use EBITDA to compare how well different companies are performing financially, as it provides a clearer picture of operational success without the influence of financial structure or accounting choices.


roi


financial


Return on investment (ROI) measures how much money an investor makes or loses relative to the amount they put in, expressed as a percentage. It helps compare the efficiency of different investments—like checking which of several gardens produced the most fruit for the seeds planted—so investors can decide which opportunities deliver the best payoff for the risk and capital they commit.

TYSONS, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Park Hotels & Resorts Inc. (“Park”) (NYSE:


[PK](https://www.stocktitan.net/overview/PK/) ) today announced the reopening of the Royal Palm South Beach Miami, a Tribute Portfolio Resort (“Royal Palm”), following the completion of the iconic oceanfront resort’s more than


$100 million


comprehensive renovation.


"Our investment in the Royal Palm reflects Park's disciplined approach to capital allocation and our ability to create meaningful long-term shareholder value through high-return redevelopment projects," said Thomas J. Baltimore, Jr., Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Park. "


Miami


remains one of the strongest lodging markets in the country, and we expect this investment to nearly double the hotel’s EBITDA upon stabilization.


Since 2018, we have invested over


$430 million


in ROI-driven projects across our core portfolio, consistently generating returns in excess of 15% to 20% once projects stabilize and driving significant EBITDA growth.


Royal Palm is another compelling example of how we are unlocking embedded value while enhancing the quality and long-term earnings growth of our portfolio."


Located on Collins Avenue in the heart of Miami Beach's historic Art Deco District, Royal Palm blends its celebrated heritage with a completely reimagined guest experience. The reopened property features 404 fully renovated guestrooms and suites, including the addition of 11 new guestrooms, across the East and West towers and Lanai bungalow, as well as a redesigned lobby and arrival experience, four new food and beverage concepts, refreshed indoor and outdoor public spaces, an enhanced pool, enhanced beachfront amenities, and modernized and expanded meeting and event facilities spanning approximately 20,000 square feet.


The reopening represents another milestone in Park's investment strategy that is focused on enhancing the quality and earnings power of its core portfolio. In addition to Royal Palm, Park has completed transformative redevelopment projects at the Bonnet Creek complex in Orlando, Casa Marina and The Reach in Key West, and the Hilton Santa Barbara Beachfront Resort, while also significantly elevating the guestroom product at the Hilton Hawaiian Village, Hilton Waikoloa Village and the Hilton New Orleans Riverside.
