# Contract Lifecycle Management: Stages, Benefits & CLM Software | ROGER

###


1. Contract Request


It all begins with a request. A sales rep might need a new customer agreement, or HR might need an NDA for a new hire. In many teams, this step is handled via email or informal chat messages, leading to inconsistent intake.


🛠️ With ROGER, you can standardize the contract intake process to ensure key info is captured from the start.


### 2. Contract Drafting


Next comes drafting. Legal or operations teams pull from templates or previous contracts to create a new document. However manual copy-paste work increases the risk of errors or outdated clauses.


🔁 ROGER’s dynamic templates and smart fields help you draft contracts in minutes, not hours.


### 3. Review and Collaboration


Internal stakeholders like legal, finance, or department heads, need to weigh in. Then, the counterparty may propose redlines. This phase is where version control nightmares happen.


💬 Real-time commenting and change tracking keep everyone aligned and reduce confusion.


### 4. Negotiation


Some contracts are approved as-is, but many require negotiation. This stage includes redlining, counter-proposals, and back-and-forth until both parties reach an agreement.


A modern CLM tool helps you negotiate without losing your place or your patience.


### 5. Approval


Before the contract is sent for signature, it often needs internal approval. Depending on the value or risk level, this could include multiple stakeholders.


✅ Use custom approval workflows in ROGER to route contracts to the right people automatically.


### 6. Signature


Whether you're using traditional wet ink or modern eSignatures, this stage formalizes the agreement. Delays here can derail deals.


✍️ With ROGER’s eSignature tools, your team and your clients can sign from anywhere.


### 7. Post-Signature Management


The lifecycle doesn’t end at the signature. Now it’s time to:


-


Store the contract securely


-


Track key obligations and deadlines


-


Set reminders for renewals or reviews


-


Monitor compliance and performance


📂 ROGER’s centralized contract repository and smart alerts make this easy.
