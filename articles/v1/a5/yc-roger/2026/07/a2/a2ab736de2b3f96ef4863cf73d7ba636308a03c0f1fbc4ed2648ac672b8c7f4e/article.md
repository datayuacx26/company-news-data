# How to Electronically Sign and Send a PDF

##
❓Can you legally sign a PDF electronically?


Yes, you can[legally sign a PDF electronically](https://www.thanksroger.com/blog/feature-update-sign-pdf-contracts) , and it’s recognized in most countries as equally valid as a[handwritten signature.](https://www.thanksroger.com/blog/wet-signatures-faq) In the U.S.,[electronic signatures](https://www.thanksroger.com/features/esignature) are backed by laws like the[ESIGN Act](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/supervision-and-examinations/consumer-compliance-examination-manual/documents/10/x-3-1.pdf) and[UETA](https://www.uniformlaws.org/committees/community-home?CommunityKey) , which confirm that digital signatures have the same legal weight as ink-on-paper ones, provided both parties agree to use electronic methods.


When you use a platform like ROGER, you’re not just placing a visual signature on the document. You're creating a digital record that includes time stamps, signer authentication, and an[audit trail](https://www.thanksroger.com/blog/feature-update-contract-audit-history) . These additional layers of verification make electronically signed PDFs even more secure and reliable than traditional methods in some cases.


So whether you’re signing a contract, a permission slip, a tax form, or a lease agreement, you can feel confident knowing your electronic signature is legally binding. Just be sure the document includes your intent to sign and has fields or tools that meet legal compliance standards.
