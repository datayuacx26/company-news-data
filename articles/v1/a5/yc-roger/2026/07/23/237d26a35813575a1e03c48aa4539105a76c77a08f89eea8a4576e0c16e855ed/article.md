# What Is Contract Management? Guide to Lifecycle Stages & Processes | ROGER

Understanding the contract lifecycle helps you build repeatable, scalable processes. Let’s break down the standard lifecycle stages that every contract goes through:


**1. Request**


It all starts with a need, maybe your sales team lands a new client, or HR is onboarding a new hire. Someone requests a contract to formalize the relationship. At this stage, it’s important to gather all the necessary information upfront to avoid rework later.


🛠️ **Tip** : Create intake forms or request templates to standardize contract data from the start.


2. **Drafting**


The legal or operations team begins drafting the contract. Without templates or tools, this often means copying and pasting from old files, an error-prone and time-consuming process.


Using a tool like ROGER, you can build dynamic templates with smart fields that automatically populate company names, dates, pricing, and more.


This speeds up contract creation while ensuring consistency and accuracy across documents.


3. **Review**


Stakeholders across departments (legal, finance, operations) review the contract. This can involve internal comments, suggested edits, or checks for legal compliance and commercial alignment.


With traditional processes, reviews often happen in email threads or Word docs, which can lead to version control issues. A centralized platform helps ensure everyone is working on the same document and changes are tracked in real time.
