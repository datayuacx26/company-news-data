## What are goal trackers, and do they make a difference?


Goal trackers are like personal assistants—but for your goals. They help you organize your tasks and keep you on track as you work towards achieving what you set out to do.


Whether it’s a DIY method or a digital tracker, they’re all about making tasks easier and helping you reach your goals smoothly. Digital trackers especially help you visually represent your objectives, dependencies between those objectives, and progress, resulting in[increased productivity](https://www.sunsama.com/blog/best-time-management-strategies) and better overall performance.


People have always relied on simple methods like keeping journals and checklists to monitor their goals. However, technology and digital goal-tracking tools make monitoring and achieving your goals more efficient.


For instance, there are dedicated goal trackers like Sunsama, specifically designed to help you and your team members set, track, and visualize goals. Alternatively, you can craft your own goal-tracker using Google Sheets by creating columns for specific goals, setting due dates, and updating progress regularly.


**Goal trackers make a difference by helping you:**


- Stay accountable and on track with your goals
- Share your goals and schedule with your team
- Use your time and efforts more efficiently
- Visualize your goals, increasing the chances of achieving the most important objectives


A[study](https://www.apa.org/news/press/releases/2015/10/progress-goals) published in the American Psychological Association's journal found that individuals who regularly monitored their progress toward goals experienced improved behavioral performance and a better chance of achieving them.


Statistics reinforce the impact of goal trackers. According to a[2022 survey](https://coschedule.com/marketing-statistics) , marketers who actively set goals are nearly four times more likely to have success than those who do not set goals.


## 4 ways to track your goals for best results


### 1. Set clear and specific goals


Before you actually set your goals, you have to ensure they’re specific and measurable, which makes them easy to track.


One popular framework for setting such goals is the SMART framework, which stands for: ****


- Specific
- Measurable
- Achievable
- Relevant
- Time-bound


These goals are quantifiable and precise, so you can track your goal’s progress objectively.


For example, a vague goal like “get healthier in 2024” would be more challenging to track than something more specific and measurable, like “run a 5k race in 10 months.”

### 3. Create a visual representation


The Journal of Neuroscience shows that our brain processes and values visual information significantly more than other stimuli. This clearly indicates just how important visual representation is, especially when you set out to track your goals.


[Visual representation](https://www.sunsama.com/blog/visual-planning) of your goals is not only more memorable but also more motivating and clear to comprehend. Considering the impact of visual cues on our brain, here are some common ways you can visually represent your goals:


- **Goal charts or progress bars:** These visually display progress toward a goal. This gives you a better indication of progress with each milestone or achievement represented by a mark or color.
- **Mind maps or flowcharts:** These show you how different goals or tasks relate to each other, helping you organize thoughts and identify dependencies between them.
- **Gantt charts or timelines:** Useful for tracking progress over time,[Gantt charts](https://blog.ganttpro.com/en/ultimate-guide-gantt-charts/) outline tasks, deadlines, and the overall project timeline. They offer a comprehensive view of goal completion stages.


For task and goal tracking, you can use tools like Sunsama, which offer visual[Kanban boards](https://www.sunsama.com/blog/kanban-planning) you can use to move your goals through different stages. This gives you a complete overview of your weekly objectives, completed goals, and current objectives.

### 4. Implement the Seinfeld Strategy (Don't break the chain)


Jerry Seinfeld is one of the most prolific comedians of our time. Yet, beyond comedic timing and the ability to enrapture the crowd, it’s his consistency and dedication to follow through with the goals that propelled him to the acclaimed status he holds today.


What helps him stay consistent with his goals is the goal-tracking technique often attributed to him:[the Seinfeld Strategy](https://jamesclear.com/stop-procrastinating-seinfeld-strategy) .


Also known as “Don’t Break the Chain,” it’s a straightforward technique that helps you remain consistent and on track with your goals.


The strategy revolves around the simple act of marking an "X" on a calendar each day one works towards a specific goal. The objective is to maintain a continuous chain of X's, encouraging consistency and warding off the bad habit of procrastination while also tracking your goals.


Here’s a quick breakdown of the Seinfeld Strategy:


- Decide on your goal—the more specific, the better.
- Break your goal down into routines.
- Track your progress by marking your goal with an “X” daily.
- Don’t break the chain.


‍


‍ *The Seinfeld strategy for goal-tracking (*[Source](https://www.saturdaygift.com/seinfeld-strategy/) *)*


Jerry Seinfeld used this “don’t break the chain” strategy to write jokes daily. Beyond tracking goals, this strategy helped him remain consistent with his efforts.


The strategy is also a way to represent your goals and daily habits visually—the picture of your visual chain also remains at the back of your head, which becomes a powerful motivator not to break your streak.


## Digital vs. DIY goal trackers: Why analytics matter


Long before the internet, people relied on DIY goal trackers. Even now, DIY goal trackers remain popular, especially for simple goal tracking—–think of the Seinfeld Strategy.


But when you have to track multiple goals every other day, you need something more reliable to keep track of your progress.


That’s where modern-day digital goal trackers come into the picture.


While DIY goal trackers are simpler and more affordable, digital goal trackers come with advanced features like real-time tracking, the ability to visualize your goals, and goal-based analytics.


[Research in behavioral psychology](https://oxfordre.com/psychology/display/10.1093/acrefore/9780190236557.001.0001/acrefore-9780190236557-e-129) alludes to the positive impact of this feedback toward achieving your goals. This level of clarity when it comes to goal feedback won’t be possible with DIY, free goal-tracking, giving digital goal trackers a unanimous edge.
