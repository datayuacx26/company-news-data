Added:


- Two-factor authentication


: Add an extra layer of security to your account. Turn it on from Settings → Account, scan a QR code with your authenticator app of choice, and you're enrolled. Backup codes are provided in case you lose access to your device.


Improved:


- Clicking the focus bar now just brings the Sunsama window forward, instead of also forcing focus mode on whatever task was in the bar.
- Daily highlights email time no longer drifts. Dragging the shutdown line for a single day only changes that day's draft time.
- Microsoft Planner's group picker now shows every group in your organization, not just the first 100, and groups you're actually a member of sort to the top.
- Changing a task's planned time no longer bumps it up or down the list by priority; it stays put and only shifts to keep the day in chronological order.
- Completing a task now syncs everywhere. Checking off a task linked to Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do, or Microsoft Planner updates the source item no matter where you complete it: mobile, Sunny, MCP, or the app.


Fixed:


- Recurring tasks no longer skip an occurrence when you change the repeat frequency after moving the task to a new day.
- [Monday.com](http://monday.com/) board loading no longer hangs indefinitely on a failed connection.
- The global "add task" shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+A → Ctrl+Enter) now correctly switches Focus Mode to the new task.
- Tasks created from Gmail via MCP now include a link back to the source email.
- "Hide declined events" now works for Outlook and iCloud calendars, not just Google.
- Microsoft Planner no longer shows a stale plan selection after switching groups.
