Improved:


- Recurring tasks with a fixed start time: A recurring task with a specific "roughly at" start time now only holds that slot on its originally scheduled day. If it rolls over to a later day, it's sorted normally with your other tasks instead of jumping ahead.
- Sunny now has access to our help center: Sunny can look up help articles directly when answering your questions, and is more careful with new users who are still learning what Sunsama can do.
- Last used sign-in method: The login page now shows a small badge on whichever sign-in method (Google, Outlook, SSO, or email/password) you used last, so it's easy to find your way back in.


Fixed:


- Calendar and desktop app reliability after sleep/wake: Fixed a cluster of issues where calendar integrations could stop refreshing, briefly disappear, or have their settings silently revert after your computer woke from sleep or the app sat idle. Plus more reliable desktop auto-updates, with a single "Relaunch to update" prompt instead of repeated nag notifications and a macOS install fix.
- Onboarding help links: Fixed broken links in the "Set up Sunsama" onboarding task.
- Jira "Done" styling: Tickets marked Done in Jira now correctly show with strikethrough styling in link previews.
- Todoist due dates: Fixed an edge case that could cause a Todoist task's due date to briefly display incorrectly after being changed.
- Sunny chat and voice: Fixed Sunny chat and voice being completely unavailable due to an underlying AI model change.
