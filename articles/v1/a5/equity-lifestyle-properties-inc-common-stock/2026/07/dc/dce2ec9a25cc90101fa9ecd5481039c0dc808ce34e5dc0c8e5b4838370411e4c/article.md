# ELS Declares Third Quarter 2026 Dividend


07/28/26


CHICAGO


, July 28, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- On July 28, 2026, the Board of Directors of Equity LifeStyle Properties, Inc. (NYSE: ELS) (referred to herein as "we," "us," and "our") declared a third quarter 2026 dividend of $0.5425 per common share, representing, on an annualized basis, a dividend of $2.17 per common share. The dividend will be paid on October 9, 2026 to stockholders of record at the close of business on September 25, 2026.
