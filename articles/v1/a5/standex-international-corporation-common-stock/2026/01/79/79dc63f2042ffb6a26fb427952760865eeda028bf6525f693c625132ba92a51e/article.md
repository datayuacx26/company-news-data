# Announcing the KS01 Reed Technology Key Switch for Secure, Reliable Control in Harsh Environments

January 5, 2026


## **Magnetic Reed Switching for Reliable, Long-Life Performance in Extreme Conditions.**


**Fairfield, Ohio – January 5, 2026 – Standex Electronics** , a global leader in precision-engineered electronic components and a business segment of Standex International Corporation (NYSE: SXI), announces the launch of the[KS01 Reed Technology Key Switch](https://standexdetect.com/products/sensors/position-sensors/ks01) , a breakthrough in secure On/Off control for industrial and commercial applications. Engineered for environments where reliability is non-negotiable, the KS01 delivers exceptional durability, long service life, and peace of mind for OEMs and end-users alike.


### A Smarter Solution for Tough Conditions


Traditional mechanical key switches often fail under exposure to moisture, chemicals, and dust, leading to costly downtime and maintenance. The KS01 solves these challenges with magnetic non-contact switching and hermetically sealed reed technology, eliminating corrosion and mechanical wear. This design ensures millions of fault-free operations, even in the harshest conditions.


#### Key Features and Benefits


- Unmatched Reliability: Hermetically sealed reed contacts prevent environmental damage.
- Secure Operation: Key can only be removed in the OFF position for enhanced safety.
- High Performance: Handles up to 100W / 1.5A for power loads and 10W / 0.5A for signal switching.
- Easy Integration: Panel mount design with M16 nut and D-shape cutout for precise installation.
- Built to Last: IP67-rated housing and corrosion-resistant Nylon Ultramid A3K construction.
- Customization Options: OEM branding and keying available for tailored solutions.


### Applications That Demand Dependability


From industrial cleaning equipment and material handling systems to utility vehicles and security panels, the KS01 ensures uninterrupted performance where failure is not an option.


### Why KS01?


Standex Electronics Detect combines decades of magnetic sensing expertise with collaborative engineering to deliver the right design, at the right time, at the optimal cost. The KS01 is more than a component—it’s a solution that reduces downtime, lowers maintenance costs, and extends equipment life.
