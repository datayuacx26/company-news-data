## Solving Critical EV Sensing & Monitoring Challenges


As EV platforms evolve toward higher voltages, faster charging, and increased driving range, monitoring and protecting critical systems becomes increasingly complex.


One EV manufacturer approached Standex Detect with a challenge: accurately measuring isolation resistance between the vehicle chassis and high-voltage battery systems while meeting strict automotive safety requirements. At the time, battery voltages were increasing toward 800V architectures, creating new measurement and protection challenges.


#### The Challenge


To ensure safe operation, EV battery systems must remain electrically isolated from the vehicle chassis. Accurately monitoring that isolation becomes more difficult as:


- Battery voltages increase
- Safety requirements become more stringent
- System capacitance grows
- Measurement accuracy becomes more critical


The customer required a solution capable of supporting high isolation resistance, high breakdown voltage, long-term reliability, and compliance with demanding automotive standards.


#### The Solution


Drawing on experience across automotive, energy, and industrial applications, Standex Detect worked closely with the customer’s engineering team to develop a solution optimized for the application.


A standard platform was customized to improve:


- Breakdown voltage performance
- Shock and vibration resistance
- Coil characteristics
- Long-term reliability


#### Why it Matters


As electric vehicles become more advanced, reliable monitoring and sensing technologies are critical to maintaining safety, protecting high-voltage systems, and ensuring dependable operation.

## Why Engineers Choose Reed Sensing Technologies


Reed-based sensing solutions offer several advantages for EV applications:


- Zero power consumption in passive state
- Long operating life
- Hermetically sealed construction
- Reliable performance in harsh environments
- Resistance to dust, moisture, vibration, and shock
- Compact form factors for space-constrained applications


These characteristics make reed sensors well-suited for transportation and EV systems where reliability and efficiency are critical.

## Frequently Asked Questions About EV Sensing Systems


Engineers designing industrial automation, robotics, material handling equipment, and motion control systems frequently ask how to reduce unplanned downtime, improve position sensing accuracy, and select the best sensor technology for challenging environments.


Below are answers to common questions about reed sensors, Hall effect sensors, proximity sensing, liquid level monitoring, and other precision sensing solutions that help improve equipment reliability and operational uptime.


Click a question below to explore detailed answers.


#### What role do sensors play in electric vehicles?


Sensors provide the critical feedback needed to monitor and protect EV systems. They support battery management, charging systems, fluid monitoring, motion detection, and safety functions that help ensure reliable vehicle operation.


#### Why is sensing important in EV battery management systems?


Battery management systems rely on sensing technologies to monitor isolation, support battery conditioning, verify charging status, and help maintain safe operation of high-voltage systems. As EV architectures move toward higher voltages, accurate monitoring becomes increasingly important.


#### What types of EV applications use reed sensors?


Reed sensors are commonly used for:


- Battery management systems
- Charge-port detection
- Door and compartment monitoring


- Coolant and fluid level sensing
- Position and safety interlocks


Their low power consumption and hermetically sealed construction make them well suited for demanding automotive environments.


#### What role do Hall Effect sensors play in EV systems?


Hall Effect sensors are frequently used for rotational speed and motion-related applications, including wheel speed monitoring, gear tooth sensing, drivetrain feedback, and motor position detection. They provide continuous electronic feedback in dynamic systems and safety functions when an ASIL rating is required.


#### Why are reed sensors popular in EV designs?


Reed sensors offer several advantages for EV applications, including:


- Zero power consumption in their passive state
- Long operating life
- Resistance to shock and vibration


- Reliable operation in harsh environments
- Compact form factors for space-constrained designs


These features help support efficiency, reliability, and long-term vehicle performance.


#### Can Standex Detect provide custom sensing solutions for EV applications?


Yes. While many projects begin with standard products, Standex Detect regularly works with customers to develop application-specific solutions. Customization may include package size, environmental performance, switching characteristics, or application-specific requirements.
