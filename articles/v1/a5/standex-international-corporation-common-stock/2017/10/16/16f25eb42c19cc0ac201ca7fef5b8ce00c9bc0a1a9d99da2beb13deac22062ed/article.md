# Standex-Meder Electronics Partners with Heilind Electronics

October 19, 2017


## **Expanding Global Reach of Sensor Product Line Through New Distribution Partnership**


[Standex-Meder Electronics](https://standexelectronics.com/) , a global components manufacturer announces its partnership with[Heilind Electronics](http://www.heilind.com/) , a global distributor. This alliance focuses on expanding the Standex-Meder Electronics’ sensor product line through Heilind Electronics’ integrated supply chain, warehouse, and customer-centric sales force, as well as its tactical expertise in delivering products in the digital age through its e-commerce platform. This agreement further iterates both companies willingness to drive growth and build relationships with customers.


> “We are excited about the partnership between Standex-Meder Electronics and Heilind Electronics as this aligns with our growth initiatives in creating a value-added supply chain and parts catalog for our customers, specifically for our sensor product solutions,” said John Meeks, President of Standex-Meder Electronics. “Along with Heilind Electronics being a premier global distributor in the industry, their regionally based sales and support teams create an opportune moment for an extension of our already existing sales force.”
>
>
> “At Heilind Electronics, enhancing our sensor products portfolio has been a strategic initiative for some time,” stated Glen MacGibbon Vice President, Business Development, Sensors at Heilind. “Adding the Standex line of sensor products was a natural fit for our ever-expanding global customer base. As a leading supplier of reed switches and packaged solutions, such as customized liquid level sensors, this partnership was the perfect fit for our two companies.”


Headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, Standex-Meder Electronics is a worldwide market leader in the design, engineering, and manufacture of standard and custom electro-magnetic components, including magnetics products, and a comprehensive range of reed switch and sensor-based solutions. Comprised of eight manufacturing facilities in six countries, located in the United States, Germany, China, Mexico, the United Kingdom, and Japan, they leverage their global resources of engineers and manufacturing facilities to solve complex problems through their partner, solve, and deliver® approach.


Based in Wilmington, MA, Heilind Electronics, Inc. is one of the world’s leading distributors of connectors, relays, sensors, switches, thermal management and circuit protection products, terminal blocks, wire and cable, wiring accessories and insulation and identification products. Founded in 1974, Heilind has locations throughout the U.S., Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Germany, Singapore, Hong Kong and China.


For further information about the companies mentioned in this press release, visit Standex-Meder Electronics on the web at[standexelectronics.com](https://standexelectronics.com/) and Heilind Electronics at[heilind.com](http://www.heilind.com/) .
