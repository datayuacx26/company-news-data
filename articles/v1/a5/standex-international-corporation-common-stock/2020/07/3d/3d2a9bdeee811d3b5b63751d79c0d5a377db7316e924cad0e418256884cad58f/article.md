# SMD Reed Sensor Lineup Expands – New 6.4mm Package

Standex Electronics expands its SMD reed sensor lineup with the MK31 series, the industry’s smallest 6.4 mm package. Designed for portable electronics and wearables, the MK31 delivers long life, low power use, and reliable performance in compact applications.


## Standex Electronics has added the[MK31 series](https://standexelectronics.com/products/mk31-series-reed-sensor/) to its[SMD Reed Sensor lineup](https://standexelectronics.com/products/surface-mount-smd-reed-sensor/) !


As portable electronic devices, handhelds, and even wearables continue to get smaller, so does the demand for electronic components! Thus, both smarter and more power efficient designs are also on the rise.


The new MK31 series is the smallest in size and best in quality! When it comes to PCB space, size matters. The MK31 is 20% smaller than the competition. Measuring only L6.4mm x H1.95mm x W2.2mm. Furthermore, the MK31 and entire lineup of SMD reed sensors require no switching load.


Standex surface mount reed sensors can effectively sense from up to 44mm away. With a unique hermetic seal and no wearing parts, the reed switch sensor can outlast mechanical switches. Offering an unusually long life in the millions of operations.
