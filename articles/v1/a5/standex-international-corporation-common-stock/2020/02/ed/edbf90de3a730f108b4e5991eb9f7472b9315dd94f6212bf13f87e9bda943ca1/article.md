# Standex India Achieves ISO 9001:2015 Certification

February 25, 2020


## ******Reinforcing Commitment to Global Standards in Sensor Production******


Standex Electronics India, a leading manufacturer of precise, reliable and long-lasting sensor solutions, received ISO 9001:2015 certification for its quality management system in the production of sensors, such as the MK06 part and others, for their global network of customers. The certificate is valid through February 10, 2023.


Third party certification body Tüv Süd South Asia Private Limited performed the ISO 9001:2015 audit to certify that Standex Electronics India implemented a quality management system that demonstrates the ability to consistently provide products that meet customer and regulatory needs, while aiming to enhance customer satisfaction. It also verifies that there is consistent results in the processes, efficiencies, manufacturing, and continuous improvements that allow for reliable high-quality deliverables.


Located in Chennai, Standex Electronics India offers a 28,000 sq. ft. clean room environment, raw material availability and injection molding. Skilled labor, including testing, tooling and laboratory capabilities, contribute to quality-focused production of molded and potted sensors, such as MK03, MK04 and MK18. This facility can handle mid- to high-volume sensor manufacturing for customers locally and globally.


Standex Electronics is a team of sensors and magnetics experts, who deliver quality engineering solutions through diverse products and dynamic capabilities globally. This team partners with customers to create value-added assemblies that serve any number of applications and markets.
