# Standex Announces the New Optocoupler Series 530

September 15, 2015


## Improved technical parameters are ideal for usage in high voltage safety applications


Standex Electronics, a manufacturer of precise, reliable, and long-lasting miniature reed switches, sensors, relays and magnetic components, announces a new optocoupler series 530, which provide high isolation voltage in a compact housing ideal for high voltage applications.


The improved characteristics of the new 530 series are the longer air-creepage distance, the better resistance against mechanical shocks and humidity as well as the stable position of the pins. This features and the high isolation voltage in a compact housing makes the 530 series to one of the most preferred optocouplers when customers is looking for a long-life and reliable product for the use in high safety environment such as medical applications.


530 series optocouplers for the galvanic separation of intrinsic safety circuits have a isolation voltage from 10kVDC up to 22kVDC, Current Transfer Ratio min. 80%, cutoff 50kHz, a insulation resistance input /output up to 10^12 Ω and the creepage distance between input and output for highest safety requirements is up to 34mm. The operating temperature is is -40°C up to 100°C. Like all Standex Electronics products, they can be customized by using the company’s product design, engineering, machine shop, manufacturing, environmental testing and laboratory capabilities.
