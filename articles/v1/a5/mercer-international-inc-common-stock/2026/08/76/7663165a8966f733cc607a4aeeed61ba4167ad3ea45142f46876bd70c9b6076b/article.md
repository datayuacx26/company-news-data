# Mercer Peace River Announces Scheduled Fall Maintenance Shutdown

### Ten-day planned maintenance outage in September will strengthen long-term mill reliability and safety


PEACE RIVER, Alberta — August 04, 2026 — Mercer International Inc. (Nasdaq: MERC) today announced that its[Peace River](https://mercerint.com/our-operations/mercer-peace-river/) pulp mill will undertake a planned 10-day maintenance shutdown from September 7 to 17, 2026, a standard practice across the pulp industry that allows maintenance and operations teams to complete critical inspections, upgrades and equipment servicing that cannot be performed while the mill is running.


The shutdown is expected to reduce production by approximately 15,000 air-dried metric tonnes (ADMTs), a planned and anticipated impact that has been factored into the mill’s annual production outlook.


> “Planned maintenance shutdowns are one of the most important things we do all year,” said Cal Dakin, Managing Director of Mercer Peace River. “Taking the mill down on our schedule, rather than reacting to an unplanned failure, lets our teams get into the equipment, inspect it inside and out, and make the upgrades that keep Peace River running safely and reliably for our employees, our customers and this community for years to come.”


Annual maintenance shutdowns are standard practice across the global pulp industry, giving mills the chance to service equipment that runs continuously for the remainder of the year and cannot be safely inspected or repaired while operating. At Peace River, the 2026 work will be focused on the digester internal inspection and repairs, pulp machine optimization work, and the recovery boiler inspection.


The shutdown will bring an influx of 475 to 500 contractors and specialized trades to the Peace River area for the duration of the outage, supporting local suppliers, accommodations and services. Mercer’s Peace River workforce will remain fully engaged throughout the shutdown, working alongside contract crews to complete the maintenance program safely and on schedule.


> “Major maintenance shutdowns like this one reflect the same discipline we apply across all of our Canadian pulp operations,” said Bill Adams, Chief Sustainability Officer and Senior Vice President, Canadian Pulp Operations. “Planned maintenance isn’t just about keeping equipment running efficiently — it’s how we protect the environmental and safety performance our communities and customers expect from us. The mill is expected to resume normal production immediately following completion of the maintenance work in September. Mercer will continue to work with customers and logistics partners to manage the temporary reduction in output.


**About Mercer International Inc.**


Mercer International Inc. is a global forest products company with operations in Germany, the United States and Canada, with consolidated annual production capacity of 2.1 million tonnes of pulp, 960 million board feet of lumber, 210,000 cubic meters of cross-laminated timber, 45,000 cubic meters of glulam, 17 million pallets and 230,000 metric tonnes of biofuels. Mercer’s common stock is listed on Nasdaq under the symbol MERC.


**About Mercer Peace River Pulp Ltd.**


Mercer Peace River Pulp Ltd. operates a kraft pulp mill located in the boreal forest region of Peace River, Alberta, Canada, approximately 500 km north of Edmonton. Mercer Peace River’s pulp mill operations began in 1990 and produce both softwood (NBSK) and hardwood (NBHK) pulp, serving customers in global tissue, packaging, and other cellulose-based markets. The majority of the mill’s pulp sales are made to North American customers, while the remainder is shipped to Asian and European markets. The mill operates an on-site electrical energy generation facility with a capacity of 65 MW, and holds the rights to two 20-year government Forest Management Agreements, along with three other deciduous timber allocations in Alberta.
