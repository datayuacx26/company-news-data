# AGCO Announces Quarterly Dividend

DULUTH, Ga.


, July 8, 2026


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / -- AGCO (NYSE: AGCO) today announced its Board of Directors declared a regular quarterly dividend of $0.30 per common share to be paid on September 15, 2026, to all stockholders of record as of the close of business August 14, 2026.


**About AGCO:**
AGCO (NYSE: AGCO) is a global leader in agricultural machinery and precision agriculture technologies. Driven by a Farmer-First strategy, AGCO delivers value through its differentiated leading brands, Fendt™, Massey Ferguson™, PTx™ and Valtra™. AGCO's high-performance equipment and smart farming solutions, including brand-agnostic retrofit technologies and autonomous offerings, empower farmers to drive productivity while sustainably feeding the world.
