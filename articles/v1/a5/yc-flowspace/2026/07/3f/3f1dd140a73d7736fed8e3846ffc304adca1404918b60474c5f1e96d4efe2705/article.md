# Flowspace Announces Free Fulfillment Assessment to Combat Rising Logistics Costs

COSTA MESA, Calif.--([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/) )--[Flowspace](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fflow.space%2F&esheet=54281853&newsitemid=20250625136914&lan=en-US&anchor=Flowspace&index=1&md5=d3d42ffd894f4d4010ab738a2e77b754) , the leading fulfillment operations platform, announced today that it is offering a free fulfillment assessment to identify savings for U.S.-based brands feeling the impact of rising costs.


As shipping fees increase and tariffs impact the cost to produce products, brands across the industry face mounting costs. Flowspace customers, however, benefit from cost stability driven by a fulfillment model purpose-built to resist volatility.


**How Flowspace Delivers Cost Stability**


Flowspace achieves this cost advantage through a combination of network design, technology, and carrier strategy:


- **Distributed Fulfillment Network** Flowspace’s nationwide warehouse network enables brands to store inventory closer to customers, reducing last-mile distances and cutting zone-based shipping costs that would otherwise compound increased costs.


- **Smart Routing & Optimization** The intelligent routing system behind Flowspace’s network is trusted by logistics teams to optimize cost, speed, and performance across complex, multi-node fulfillment strategies.


- **Volume Efficiency & Variable Costs** Flowspace unlocks economies of scale that individual brands cannot achieve alone and enables brands to pay on a variable basis without fixed costs, shielding against volatility.
