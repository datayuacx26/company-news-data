## Ecommerce fulfillment centers FAQ


### What is a fulfillment center for ecommerce?


A fulfillment center for ecommerce is a facility that stores inventory and handles order processing, picking, packing, shipping, and returns for online orders. It supports ecommerce brands by managing the operational work required to deliver products to customers.


### How is ecommerce fulfillment different for DTC brands?


DTC ecommerce fulfillment is more customer-facing than wholesale or marketplace fulfillment. DTC brands are judged directly on delivery speed, order accuracy, packaging, and post-purchase experience, making fulfillment performance a critical part of brand perception.


### Why do ecommerce brands use fulfillment centers instead of in-house warehouses?


Ecommerce brands use fulfillment centers to avoid the cost and complexity of running their own warehouse operations. Fulfillment centers provide scalable storage, trained fulfillment teams, and established processes that are difficult and expensive to replicate internally.


### How does a fulfillment center for ecommerce help with faster shipping?


A fulfillment center for ecommerce enables faster shipping by allowing brands to store inventory in multiple locations and ship orders from the most efficient fulfillment center. This reduces transit time and helps brands meet customer delivery expectations.


### Can fulfillment centers handle kitting, bundling, and subscription?


Yes. Modern ecommerce fulfillment centers support kitting, bundling, and subscription fulfillment. These capabilities allow brands to assemble multi-SKU orders, promotional bundles, and recurring shipments consistently and accurately.
