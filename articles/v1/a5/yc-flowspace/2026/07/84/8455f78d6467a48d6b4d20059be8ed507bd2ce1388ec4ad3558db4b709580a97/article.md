# Best 3PL for Shopify in 2026: Evaluating top providers

At 200 orders a day, your Shopify store runs fine. At 2,000, things start falling apart in ways you didn't anticipate. The inventory spreadsheet tracking two warehouses drifts out of sync by Tuesday. Wholesale and DTC orders compete for the same pick queue, and neither side wins cleanly.


And then customers open tickets about tracking gaps you can't explain because the data hasn't propagated yet.


Most brands hit some version of this between $5M and $50M in revenue. The Shopify integration that felt seamless at launch starts showing cracks, not because Shopify failed, but because the fulfillment layer was never built for multichannel complexity.


Finding the best 3PL for Shopify at this stage determines whether growth feels controlled or chaotic.

## What is a Shopify 3PL?


A Shopify 3PL is a third-party fulfillment provider that stores your inventory, picks and packs orders, and ships them to customers, all while syncing data back to your Shopify store. At minimum, the provider connects to Shopify to pull orders and push tracking numbers.


Better options go further: real-time inventory syncing, multi-location warehouse routing, flexible payment status handling, and gift messaging to streamline and optimize ecommerce operations.


One important distinction: many apps in the Shopify ecosystem are software tools, not fulfillment providers. A WMS or order management app can improve warehouse workflows, but it does not store, pick, or ship your products.


When evaluating a Shopify fulfillment partner, confirm whether the provider actually operates or manages[outsourced fulfillment](https://flow.space/blog/outsourced-fulfillment/) , or whether they are selling software you will need to pair with a separate warehouse operation.

## FAQs


### What is a Shopify 3PL?


A Shopify 3PL is an outsourced fulfillment provider that handles storage, picking, packing, and shipping for orders placed through your Shopify store. The provider syncs with Shopify to pull orders and push tracking data. Flowspace adds order routing, distributed fulfillment, and real-time visibility across channels on top of that core sync.


### How do I choose the right Shopify 3PL?


Match the provider to your order complexity, channel mix, and growth trajectory. Check Shopify integration depth, inventory sync capabilities, and pricing model transparency. Flowspace fits Shopify brands scaling across multiple channels that need flexibility and cost visibility.


### Is Flowspace better than ShipBob?


It depends on your brand's complexity. ShipBob is a strong fit for standardized DTC workflows with fast shipping priorities. Flowspace is a better fit for brands selling across DTC, retail, B2B, and marketplaces that need distributed fulfillment, intelligent order routing, and daily itemized billing.


### How does a Shopify 3PL relate to order management?


A 3PL executes the physical fulfillment: storing, picking, packing, and shipping. Order management controls the routing logic, deciding which location fulfills which order and under what rules. Flowspace combines both in a single platform, so brands do not need to stitch together separate OMS and 3PL systems.


### If Shopify sales are growing, should I invest in a 3PL?


Growth increases fulfillment strain on internal teams. Manual workflows that work at 100 orders per day tend to break at 500 or 1,000. A Shopify 3PL lets you scale fulfillment capacity without replatforming or hiring warehouse staff.


### What is the difference between provider tiers?


Some Shopify 3PL providers are optimized for early-stage DTC brands with straightforward fulfillment needs. Others serve complex omnichannel operations with distributed inventory, retail compliance, and multiple sales channels. Flowspace sits in the mid-market tier, where brands have outgrown simple fulfillment but do not need an enterprise procurement cycle to get started.


### What are the best ShipBob alternatives for Shopify?


The best alternative depends on your business model. Flowspace fits brands that need more flexibility, distributed fulfillment, and unified visibility across channels. ShipMonk offers strong returns support and transparent invoicing. Stord serves larger brands with complex supply chains. Red Stag is the best option for heavy or bulky products.
