# Flowspace Expands Fulfillment Coverage into Canada, Powering Seamless Cross-Border Shipping and Logistics

## Flowspace fulfillment coverage is now active across Canada, enabling delivery reach into major Canadian metros including Toronto, Montreal, and Vancouver.


## The expansion supports direct-to-consumer, parcel, and wholesale fulfillment through distributed fulfillment coverage across Canada.


COSTA MESA, Calif.–([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/) )–Flowspace, a[modern fulfillment provider](https://flow.space/fulfillment) that combines powerful technology with expert operations, announced the expansion of its fulfillment coverage into Canada, enabling seamless cross-border commerce for leading U.S. brands. This strategic move addresses the growing demand from merchants for unified United States–Canada fulfillment coverage, simplifying shipping and logistics through integrated inventory management, order routing, and carrier partnerships.


The expansion strengthens Flowspace’s North American coverage by extending service reach across key Canadian locations, ensuring efficient delivery coverage for major metros like Toronto, Montreal, and Vancouver. Through the Flowspace platform, merchants maintain a single system of record for orders, inventory, and performance across both countries, allowing real-time tracking and intelligent order routing to minimize costs and transit times. Existing integrations with platforms like Shopify and Amazon will continue to work seamlessly.


**Strengthening Flowspace’s North America Fulfillment Coverage**


This initiative empowers U.S.-based brands to expand into Canada—without the burden of managing separate fulfillment operations or adding headcount. With Flowspace, growing brands gain unified performance analytics and SLA transparency across both markets, supporting scalable international growth.


“Expanding service coverage into Canada is a natural extension of our mission: to power fulfillment excellence for leading brands everywhere,” said Ben Eachus, CEO of Flowspace. “With access to Canada, the brands we serve can reach consumers on both sides of the border through one trusted provider.”
