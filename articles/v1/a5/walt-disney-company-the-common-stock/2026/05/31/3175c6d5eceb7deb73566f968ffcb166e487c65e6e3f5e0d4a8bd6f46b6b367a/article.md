## **A Legacy of Disney Celebrating Blue Star Families**


Disney has proudly supported Blue Star Families’ “


**Blue Star Books** ” program since 2011, donating


**more than 370,000 books** to enrich military families at military bases across the nation. Recent events included visits to


[Camp Pendleton Marine Corps Base](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ow272rFWgQc) in Oceanside, CA and


[Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam in Hawaii](https://bluestarfam.org/2025/05/blue-star-families-brings-the-joy-of-disney-books-to-military-families-in-hawaii/) . Through this longstanding relationship, Disney has contributed to programs that strengthen connections and create meaningful experiences for military families.


“Stories have a powerful way of bringing people together — especially for military families navigating time apart,” said Lisa Haines, Senior


Vice President


of Corporate Social Responsibility


at The Walt Disney Company


. “Through our work with Blue Star Families, we’re honored to create moments of connection, comfort and joy, continuing Disney’s longstanding admiration for those who serve and the families who stand beside them.”


Active-duty military families are called a


**“Blue Star Family”** because of the long-standing tradition of displaying a


**service flag** with a blue star for each family member serving in the U.S. Armed Forces — symbolizing honor, service, and sacrifice while a loved one is on active duty.


## **Creating Welcome Kits for Military Families Coast-to-Coast**


During National Military Appreciation Month in May, Disney VoluntEARS at various Disney locations nationwide are helping Blue Star Families by assembling 4,000 “Welcome Kits” for military families.


Blue Star Families and Disney VoluntEARS at Walt Disney World Resort


Blue Star Families and Disney VoluntEARS from the Disneyland Resort


Disney and Blue Star Families developed the Welcome Kits program in 2024. The kits are specifically designed for active-duty military families who move to new duty stations frequently due to the needs of military life.


On May 5, at Disney’s Yacht Club at Walt Disney World Resort, cast members gathered to assemble 2,000 Welcome Kits. On May 13,


Disney VoluntEARS from the Disneyland Resort packaged another 1,000 kits.

## **Full Circle Moment with Presentation of Welcome Kits**


The Welcome Kits prepared on Capitol Hill were distributed to military families during the Library of Congress event on May 17. The remaining kits will be delivered to Blue Star Families Chapters and distributed to their members around the country.


The 4,000 welcome kits created by Disney VoluntEARS this month alone nearly doubled all those made to date, bringing the total to more than


**7,000 kits** assembled in support of military families since the program began.


Additional kits will be created later this year.


The Walt Disney Company’s efforts with Blue Star Families are part of the


[company’s long history of supporting](https://impact.disney.com/social-impact/veterans-and-military-families/) U.S. military service members, veterans, and their families, dating back more than a century to founders Walt and Roy O. Disney, who both served their country during World War I.


This commitment is being highlighted as part of “Disney Celebrates America,” a company-wide initiative spanning programming, storytelling, and experiences across its iconic brands and businesses in honor of America’s 250th anniversary.
