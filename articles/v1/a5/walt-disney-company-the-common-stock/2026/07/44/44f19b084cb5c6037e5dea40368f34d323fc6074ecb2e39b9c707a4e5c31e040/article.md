# ‘Camp Rock 3’ Sets Out on the ‘All Roads Lead to Camp’ Bus Tour, Visiting Cities Across America Starting July 3

# ‘Camp Rock 3’ Sets Out on the ‘All Roads Lead to Camp’ Bus Tour, Visiting Cities Across America Starting July 3


[Entertainment](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/entertainment/)


July 2, 2026


4 min. read


To celebrate the upcoming premiere of[Camp Rock 3](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/camp-rock-3-teaser/) , Disney announced the “All Roads Lead to Camp” bus tour. Following a successful pop-up at[CMA Fest](https://www.hulu.com/series/cma-fest-57900a5e-2088-4ca5-a111-dcc45933cd80) , the coast-to-coast tour is back in Nashville on Friday, July 3, and Saturday, July 4, for “Let Freedom Sing! Music City July 4th” before commencing on the monthlong road trip, which will make stops across the United States before reaching its final destination,[D23: The Ultimate Fan Event](https://d23.com/ultimatefanevent2026/) in Anaheim, California, on Friday, August 14.


Fans across the country can step aboard for an immersive *Camp Rock* experience, featuring a throwback “Disney Knees” photo moment, a *Camp Rock* hat customization station, exclusive photo opportunities in and around the *Camp Rock* bus, special giveaways, and more surprises inspired by the beloved franchise.


The coast-to-coast tour will visit cities across the United States, including the following*:


- July 3–4 – Nashville, Tennessee
- July 16 – Charleston, South Carolina
- July 18 – Atlanta, Georgia
- July 20 – Charlotte, North Carolina
- July 25 – Cleveland, Ohio
- July 26 – Detroit, Michigan
- July 28 – Ann Arbor, Michigan
- July 29 – South Bend, Indiana
- July 31 – Chicago, Illinois
- August 1 – Milwaukee, Wisconsin
- August 1 – Davenport, Iowa
- August 2 – St. Louis, Missouri
- August 3 – Kansas City, Missouri
- August 4 – Denver, Colorado
- August 9 – Los Angeles, California
- August 11 – San Diego, California
- August 14 – Anaheim, California


**Subject to change.*


In collaboration with the[American Camp Association](https://www.acacamps.org/) (ACA), the bus will make special stops at select ACA accredited summer camps along its journey to bring the spirit of *Camp Rock 3* directly to campers. Disney is also teaming up with ACA to offer scholarships (aka “camperships”) to make summer camp possible for underserved kids across the country. As celebrated in *Camp Rock* 3, camp creates a space where youth build lifelong memories, form lasting friendships, gain confidence, discover new interests, and feel a true sense of belonging and community. This initiative reflects Disney’s commitment to providing access and opportunity for kids when it’s needed most.


In *Camp Rock 3* , when Connect 3 loses their opening act for a major reunion tour, they return to their beloved Camp Rock to discover the next big thing. As campers vie for the chance to open for their favorite band, tensions rise and friendships are tested — leading to unexpected alliances, revelations, and romances.


Joining the franchise are[Liamani Segura](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/camp-rock-3-disney-plus-disney-channel/) as Sage,[Malachi Barton](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/milo-manheim-and-meg-donnelly-talk-passing-the-torch-in-zombies-4-dawn-of-the-vampires/) as Fletch, Lumi Pollack as Rosie, Sherry Cola as Lark, Hudson Stone as Desi, Casey Trotter as Cliff, Brooklynn Pitts as Callie, and Ava Jean as Madison. Returning cast members include future[Disney Legend Joe Jonas](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/AP9CBUkSpOQ) as Shane Gray, future[Disney Legend Nick Jonas](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/jonas-brothers-christmas-movie/) as Nate Gray, future[Disney Legend Kevin Jonas](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legends-2026/) as Jason Gray, and Maria Canals-Barrera as Connie.


Directed by Veronica Rodriguez and written by Eydie Faye, *Camp Rock 3* is produced by Disney Kids & Family and features choreography by Jamal Sims. Tim Federle executive produces, along with Joe, Nick, Kevin, Demi Lovato, Betsy Sullenger, Spencer Berman, and Gary Marsh. Special thanks to Alan Sacks for his contribution to the *Camp Rock* franchise.


*[Camp Rock](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-fd9db4af-f471-408d-8978-e408f169f53f)* (2008) and *[Camp Rock 2: The Final Jam](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-60fe8469-d23e-4bff-871e-47b7030b7ff7)* (2010) are among the Top 10 Disney Channel Original Movies of all time and were the No. 1 cable movie premiere in their years of premiere.** With chart-topping music and standout musical numbers, the *Camp Rock* franchise launched superstar talent and continues to permeate pop culture over 15 years after its debut, inspiring viral moments on[social media](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DNM8TkJNQJb/) .
