# The Walt Disney Company Earns 125 Emmy® Award Nominations

# The Walt Disney Company Earns 125 Emmy® Award Nominations


[Entertainment](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/entertainment/)


July 8, 2026


10 min. read


The Walt Disney Company proudly announces 125 nominations for the 78th Emmy® Awards across its content brands and studios, including ABC, Disney Kids & Family, FX, Disney Television Studios (20th Television Animation and 20th Television), Hulu Originals, National Geographic and Onyx Collective; and The Walt Disney Studios, including Lucasfilm, Marvel Television, 20th Century Studios, and Searchlight Pictures. Disney’s streaming platforms Disney+ and Hulu are home to a total of 109 nominations.


**ABC** received 46 nominations, the most of any broadcast network. *Abbott Elementary* (20th Television) earned its fifth consecutive Outstanding Comedy Series nomination, along with acting nods for Quinta Brunson, Janelle James and Tyler James Williams. *Jimmy Kimmel Live* landed its 15th consecutive nod for Outstanding Variety Series and its first nomination for Outstanding Writing for a Variety Series in 13 years. *Dancing With The Stars* earned its first nomination for Outstanding Reality Competition Program in a decade. ABC dominated the game show categories, claiming four of five nominations in both Outstanding Game Show and Outstanding Host for Game Show. *Shifting Gears* (20th Television) scored its first-ever nomination for Outstanding Picture Editing for a Multi-Camera Comedy Series, *Will Trent* (20th Television) picked up its second consecutive nod for Outstanding Choreography for Scripted Programming, and *The Rookie* (20th Television) landed its fifth nomination for Outstanding Stunt Coordination for Drama Programming.


**FX/Hulu** garnered 23 nominations, including three for Outstanding Series: *The Bear* for Outstanding Comedy, *Love Story: John F. Kennedy Jr. & Carolyn Bessette* (20th Television) for Outstanding Limited, and *Welcome to Wrexham* for Outstanding Unstructured Reality. Both *The Bear* and *Welcome to Wrexham* continue their four-year streak, marking an Outstanding Series nomination for every eligible season to date. The first season of *Alien: Earth* was recognized with four nominations.


**The Walt Disney Studios** received 10 nominations, led by five for *Taylor Swift | The Eras Tour | The Final Show* . *Wonder Man* saw Yahya Abdul-Mateen II earn Marvel’s first-ever nomination for a Performance in a Comedy Series, while Lucasfilm’s *Star Wars: Visions (Vol 3)* was nominated for Outstanding Animated Program and *Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord* also picked up recognition. 20th Century Studios’ *Predator: Killer of Killers* and Searchlight’s *In the Blink of an Eye* also received nods.


**Hulu Originals** earned 22 total nominations. The drama series *Paradise* (20th Television) from Dan Fogelman garnered seven nominations, including a second nomination for Outstanding Drama Series and performance nominations for Sterling K. Brown, Julianne Nicholson and Shailene Woodley. Hulu also received three stand-up comedy nominations for *Nikki Glaser: Good Girl* and *Only Murders in the Building* (20th Television) received its fifth consecutive nomination for Outstanding Comedy Series, as well as a performance nomination for Martin Short. Chase Infiniti received her first-ever nomination for Outstanding Lead Actress in *The Testaments* .


**National Geographic** garners 12 nominations, including Outstanding Hosted Nonfiction Series or Special for *Tucci in Italy* , the second consecutive nomination for the series over two seasons, as well as Outstanding Documentary or Nonfiction Special and Outstanding Narrator for *Ocean with David Attenborough* and Exceptional Merit in Documentary Filmmaking for *The Tale of Silyan* . Sir David Attenborough, who celebrated his 100th Birthday this year, holds the record for the most nominations in the Narrator category.


**Disney Television Studios (20th Television & 20th Television Animation)** garnered 59 Emmy nominations, including ten Outstanding Series nominations including *Bob’s Burgers* and *The Simpsons* for Outstanding Animated Program; three Outstanding Comedy Series nominations for *Abbott Elementary* , *Only Murders in the Building* and *Nobody Wants This* ; two Outstanding Limited or Anthology Series nominations for *Love Story: John F. Kennedy Jr. & Carolyn Bessette* and *The Beast in Me* ; Outstanding Variety Special (Pre-Recorded) for *The Muppet Show* ; and Outstanding Variety Series for *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* Additionally, the Outstanding Drama Series nomination for *Paradise* and Outstanding Comedy Series nomination for *Only Murders in the Building* again make creator and executive producer Dan Fogelman a double nominee across both Outstanding Series categories in the same year.
