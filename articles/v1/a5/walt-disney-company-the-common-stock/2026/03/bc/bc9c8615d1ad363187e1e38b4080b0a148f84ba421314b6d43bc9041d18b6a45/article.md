# ‘Zootopia 2’ Roars to No. 1 on Disney+ with 32 Million Views Globally in its Premiere Week

# ‘Zootopia 2’ Roars to No. 1 on Disney+ with 32 Million Views Globally in its Premiere Week


[Entertainment](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/entertainment/)


March 19, 2026


3 min. read


*[Zootopia 2](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-8f08a8df-8b2f-49e1-9130-1e842b90f185)* is racing ahead on Disney+, hopping to **32M views globally** in its first seven days, making it the **No. 1 film on Disney+** during its premiere week. Its arrival on service builds on the enduring popularity of the franchise: together, *Zootopia, Zootopia+* , and *Zootopia 2* have been streamed **over 885 million hours globally** to date.


The film’s strong debut on Disney+ follows in its huge theatrical pawprint. It shattered box office records, becoming the **[highest-grossing animated Motion Picture Association (MPA) release of all time](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/zootopia-2-highest-grossing-animated-film/)** with $1.9 billion worldwide, ranking as the **eighth highest-grossing film ever** and the **No. 1 domestic release of 2025** .


The sequel’s success continues the legacy of the original *Zootopia* , which captivated fans around the globe and[won the Academy Award](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/zootopia-named-best-animated-feature-2017-oscars/) for Best Animated Feature Film in 2017. *Zootopia* ranks as the **No. 7 movie on Disney+ of all time** and has been **one of the most-streamed movies across all platforms** in the U.S. for three out of the last six years, according to Nielsen.


The world of *Zootopia* has expanded beyond the screen as well. In 2023, the Shanghai Disney Resort opened the first *Zootopia* -themed land, introducing guests to the Mammalian Metropolis and bringing the beloved animal residents of the film to life. Its signature attraction, *Zootopia: Hot Pursuit* , is the **highest-rated attraction at Shanghai Disneyland** , with one in four guests stating they came to the park **specifically for the land** . Leveraging cutting-edge technology, the park also rapidly integrated new content inspired by *Zootopia 2* , debuting the film’s latest Gazelle song with one of the newest Audio-Animatronics just weeks after the film’s theatrical release. Plus, guests go wild for the food too — **over 4.8M Disney Zootopia Pawpsicles** (480 tons of ice cream!) have been sold to date.


The animated feature has also sparked strong fan engagement across consumer products and global retail, igniting **viral demand** for collectibles, blind boxes, and collaborations with brands such as Pop Mart, Miniso, Tony Moly, and Uniqlo. On[DisneyStore.com](https://www.disneystore.com/) , the iconic *Zootopia* carrot pen sold out twice — once at launch and again after restocking.
