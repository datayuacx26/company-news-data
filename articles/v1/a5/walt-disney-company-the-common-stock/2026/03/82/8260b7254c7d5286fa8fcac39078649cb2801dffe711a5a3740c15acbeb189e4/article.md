# ‘Hannah Montana 20th Anniversary Special’ Hits Superstar Numbers Driving 6.3 Million Views on Disney+ and Hulu

# ‘Hannah Montana 20th Anniversary Special’ Hits Superstar Numbers Driving 6.3 Million Views on Disney+ and Hulu


[Entertainment](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/entertainment/)


March 27, 2026


3 min. read


*Fans fuel anniversary into full blown celebration from events and activations to massive brand collaborations and nonstop social conversation*


Two decades later, *Hannah Montana* still owns the spotlight as a defining cultural icon, proved by this week’s celebration, with the ***Hannah Montana 20th Anniversary Special* driving 6.3 million views** on Disney+ and Hulu after three days streaming.


The celebration of the anniversary also drove **nearly 1,000%** increase in viewing of the *Hannah Montana* catalog this past week. The show’s longevity is undeniable: fans have streamed **more than half a billion hours globally** on Disney+ to date.


This month, fans also spent thousands of hours rocking out to the show’s most unforgettable music videos on Disney+ and relived their favorite Miley moments via a dedicated non-stop Stream of all *Hannah Montana* content.


## Watch on Disney+


### The Hannah Montana Collection


Fans can relive their favorite Miley moments in this collection, where they can dive even deeper with a dedicated non-stop Stream featuring all four seasons of “Hannah Montana,” “Hannah Montana: The Movie,” and “Hannah Montana and Miley Cyrus: The Best of Both Worlds Concert,” available now, in addition to watching the “Hannah Montana 20th Anniversary Special.”


[Explore the Collection](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/page-e7899f94-c46f-4736-905f-485705a124af)


The **Hannah Montana 20th Anniversary** permeated pop culture through user generated content, events and activations, content collections and consumer products. From a[Starbucks](https://www.instagram.com/p/DWPWfWdknJx/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==) secret Popstar Refresher and a[Google Search](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWRtcl7kco-/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==) easter egg to[Airbnb](https://www.instagram.com/p/DWTuClqkT7p/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==) opening the doors to Hannah Montana’s Malibu beach house, exclusive show–inspired[merchandise](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/lifestyle/shopping/hannah-montana-20th-anniversary-shirt-merch-clothes-shop-1236543932/) ,[Maybelline pop-ups](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWK-Eh9jdOQ/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==) in LA and New York that drew massive crowds and a custom bracelet duo-pack from[Little Words Project](https://www.instagram.com/p/DWH9HyZiRjY/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==) , the partnerships were next level and spawned **over 250 other brands** (and counting) to join in on the fun organically.


In the month of March, owned posts on Disney+ and Disney Channel social for the campaign have amassed an incredible nearly **440 million total impressions** and over **30 million engagements** across TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube and Facebook. The[teaser](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DU3kiw7CWeH/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==) of the special received over **120 million views** in its first 24 hours and Miley’s recreation of the iconic[Disney Channel Wand ID](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWO9XEVAn4n/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==) broke **45 million organic views** .


Miley Cyrus’ original song, “Younger You (From the Hannah Montana 20th Anniversary Special),” is out today via Hollywood Records and available on[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/3tlEOoxO3h3mkLyLgftdz6?si=J-huB95ET_ik2faDgD3i8Q) ,[Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/younger-you-from-the-hannah-montana-20th-anniversary/1887385339) ,[Amazon Music](https://amazon.com/music/player/albums/B0GTN8XW7D?marketplaceId=ATVPDKIKX0DER&musicTerritory=US&ref=dm_sh_6IqbpofSb4LwVi2a2jOSmYyZQ) ,[YouTube Music](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=Ye-G4zx7ykE&si=zkRYOi2Y-Dgnsayc) , and other digital platforms.


“Hannah Montana” Spotify streams have surged with the special — “This is the Life,” the first song Miley Cyrus ever performed as Hannah Montana, saw an impressive **747% boost in streams** on March 24, the day that the special dropped on Disney+ and Hulu, according to Spotify data. “Best of Both Worlds,” which Cyrus performed in the special alongside “This is the Life,” also saw a **607% lift** , while “Wherever I Go” saw a **537% uptick** and “Ordinary Girl” got a **430% boost** .
