# Disney Experiences Showcase Gives Sold-Out Crowd a Closer Look at What’s to Come During D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event

# Disney Experiences Showcase Gives Sold-Out Crowd a Closer Look at What’s to Come During D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event


[Experiences](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/experiences/)


August 15, 2026


6 min. read


*The showcase, hosted by Neil Patrick Harris, included project updates from around the world, plus the announcement of the reimagining of Tomorrowland at Disneyland Park.*


Fans celebrated a second unforgettable day with immersive offerings across the Anaheim Convention Center and exclusive moments only Disney can deliver.


The second day of[D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/d23-ultimate-disney-fan-event/) presented by Visa brought Disney fans from around the world together for another day of unparalleled storytelling, immersive experiences, and exclusive access to the future of Disney. From[the largest show floor in event history](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/d23-show-floor-2026/) at the **Anaheim Convention Center** to a spectacular evening presentation at the **Honda Center** , fans experienced firsthand the imagination, innovation, and creativity that continue to define **The Walt Disney Company** .


Saturday culminated with the[Horizons: Disney Experiences Showcase](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-ef9fbf7b-b947-479e-9438-2fef09dc3a01) , hosted by **Neil Patrick Harris** , as fans packed the arena for[an unforgettable look at the future](https://disneyparksblog.com/disney-experiences/d23-2026-news-updates-disney-experiences/) of **Disney Experiences** . Throughout the evening,[Disney Experiences](https://disneyexperiences.com/) leaders, Imagineers, and storytellers unveiled exciting new details, shared[behind-the-scenes stories](https://disneyparksblog.com/disney-experiences/the-imagineering-story-new-season/) , and offered fans exclusive first looks at what’s on the horizon. The spectacle came to life with Harris’ show-stopping opening[tribute to Disney Audio-Animatronics](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/a-great-big-beautiful-tomorrow-the-carousel-of-progress-history/) , surprise performances from Grammy Award-winning artists including **Sheryl Crow** , **Beck** , **Los Lobos** , and **Gloria Gaynor** , and celebrity cameos that kept the audience cheering from beginning to end.


Disney’s unique ability to create stories and characters that form lasting emotional connections with audiences around the world extends far beyond the screen. Through[immersive destinations](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/spaceship-earth-updates-coming-to-epcot/) ,[groundbreaking attractions](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/encanto-ride-updates-disney-world/) ,[unforgettable vacations](https://disneyparksblog.com/dcl/meet-the-disney-believe-the-next-disney-cruise-line-ship/) , and innovative guest experiences, Disney brings those stories to life in ways only Disney can.


“As we continue expanding and evolving Disney Experiences around the world, our fans remain at the heart of everything we do,” said **Thomas Mazloum** , Chairman of Disney Experiences for The Walt Disney Company. “Events like this allow us to celebrate the incredible connection fans have with Disney while sharing our vision for the future and the stories, adventures, and memories we’re creating next.”


## **All the Magic in Store From Disney Experiences**


Throughout the evening, exciting updates were shared on transformative projects around the world, including[Tropical Americas](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/indiana-jones-ride-updates-disney-world/) ,[Monstropolis](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/monsters-inc-land-monstropolis-walt-disney-world/) ,[the Cars-themed area](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/piston-peak-national-park-builds-on-the-story-of-frontierland/) , and[Villains Land](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/villains-land-rides-details-and-more-walt-disney-world/) at **Walt Disney World Resort** ; the[Coco attraction](https://disneyparksblog.com/dlr/coco-ride-details-disneyland-resort/) and[Avengers Campus expansion](https://disneyparksblog.com/dlr/avengers-campus-updates-coming-to-disneyland-and-beyond/) at **Disneyland Resort** ; and headline-making additions to Disney’s international destinations, from a[Lion King-themed area](https://disneyparksblog.com/dlp/new-details-for-lion-king-ride-coming-to-disneyland-paris/) in **Paris** to new **Marvel** experiences in **Hong Kong** and **Shanghai** , and a reimagined **Space Mountain** in **Tokyo** .


Among the evening’s biggest highlights, fans cheered for the news that[the Yeti will be brought back to life](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/yeti-audio-animatronic-expedition-everest/) in **Expedition Everest** . Looking further on the horizon, Mazloum shared plans for[a reimagined Tomorrowland](https://disneyparksblog.com/dlr/tomorrowland-disneyland-reimagined/) at Disneyland Park and revealed that **Journey Into Imagination** will receive a complete reimagining at **EPCOT** ,[featuring Figment and the return of Dreamfinder](https://disneyparksblog.com/wdw/figment-dreamfinder-imagination-epcot/) .


Full details for all announcements can be found on the[Disney Parks Blog](https://disneyparksblog.com/) .


## **Even More Excitement from the Anaheim Convention Center**


Earlier in the day, the Anaheim Convention Center continued to serve as the epicenter of Disney fandom as attendees explored immersive activations, exclusive pavilions, fan-favorite panels, and extraordinary shopping experiences. Throughout the day, fans also celebrated beloved Disney stories spanning generations while connecting with fellow fans who traveled from around the globe to be part of the weekend’s festivities.


The day started with the fan-favorite **D23 Mousequerade: The Ultimate Disney Costume Contest** presented by AT&T where guests experienced the incredible artistry and beautifully designed costumes created by attendees to celebrate their fandom. As the day continued, fans were able to get a closer look at some of the films and shows they love, including[Camp Rock 3](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-db450dbe-0450-473e-ad79-a89ad39d2aa7) ,[Bluey](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-fa6973b9-e7cf-49fb-81a2-d4908e4bf694) ,[Percy Jackson and the Olympians](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-f1c31566-ec05-4d30-b23f-3177442233f4) , and[Scrubs](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-6ac12491-8b68-4764-9cbf-91f6f1fec18a) . A deep dive into Disney history also brought fans closer to the magic of Marvel’s Spider-Man,[Zootopia](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-ee6e9e33-4b25-443f-8431-9c6eeeca0dc2) (2016),[KINGDOM HEARTS](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/kingdom-hearts-tv-series/) , Tinker Bell, and more.


Several announcements were shared today at the Anaheim Convention Center, including:


- Preschool megahit[Marvel’s Spidey and His Amazing Friends](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-cc992c16-e126-4a2b-a039-0b1e7106b727) has been greenlit for a historic seventh season on **Disney Jr.** and **Disney+** . With nearly **1.3 billion hours streamed** on Disney+ globally, the series is Marvel’s most-watched TV series on the platform and the longest-running Spider-Man series overall. It introduces a whole new generation of young fans to Peter Parker, Miles Morales, and Gwen Stacy, who together form Team Spidey and go on adventures to protect their community. The announcement was made by **Alyssa Sapire** , Head of Disney Jr., Original Programming, and Strategy, during the “Celebrate 65 Years of Marvel’s Spider-Man!” panel at D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event.
- **The Disney Princess Experience** , an all-new immersive adventure, is currently in development. The announcement was made during a panel at **Disney Theatrical Group** ’s showroom floor booth. Inspired by beloved Disney stories, the experience will invite guests of all ages to journey through the magical worlds of Rapunzel, Tiana, Belle, Moana, and Ariel in a magical quest filled with music, interactive discoveries, and unforgettable moments. Additional details will be announced soon at[www.thedisneyprincessexperience.com](https://www.thedisneyprincessexperience.com/) .
- An exclusive first screening of ***LEGO® Star Wars: The Mandalorian*** , an all-new animated special from **Lucasfilm** , which will debut exclusively on[Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/home) **September 2, 2026** . Shelby Young, who has voiced numerous characters in animated *Star Wars* titles over the years, debuted the[trailer](https://youtu.be/NEqBkXeEpkk?si=irpejuypnwX8Tjbp) and poster before introducing the film to an excited crowd at the Anaheim Convention Center. Based on *Star Wars,* created by **George Lucas** , and inspired by the Disney+ series[The Mandalorian](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-422f6dcc-226f-44e7-98d4-22de69b31cf3) and[The Book of Boba Fett](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-3749c03e-8204-46c5-b102-0410e6d51758) , the special is directed by Chris Buckley and written by Michael Price. The executive producers are Michael Price, James Waugh, Josh Rimes, Jacqui Lopez, Jill Wilfert, Jason Cosler, and Keith Malone, and the producers are Daniel Cavey and Dan Langlois.


## **One More Day of Disney Magic**


The celebration continues tomorrow with the final day of D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event, featuring additional programming, shopping, and fan experiences at the Anaheim Convention Center as well as the highly anticipated[Disney Legends Awards Ceremony](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legends-2026/) at the Honda Center, hosted by **Ryan Seacrest** .


The ceremony will honor the newest class of Disney Legends and celebrate the extraordinary individuals whose leadership, creativity, innovation, and passion have helped shape Disney’s enduring legacy. This year’s inductees include **[Chris Berman](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legend-chris-berman/)** , **[Jerry Bruckheimer](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legend-jerry-bruckheimer/)** , **[Susan Egan](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legend-susan-egan/)** , **[Eric Goldberg](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legend-eric-goldberg/)** , **Anne Hathaway** , **[Bob Iger](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legend-bob-iger/)** , **[Kim Irvine](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legend-kim-irvine/)** , **Dwayne Johnson** ,[the Jonas Brothers](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legends-jonas-brothers/) , **[Lin-Manuel Miranda](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legend-lin-manuel-miranda/)** , and **[Alan Tudyk](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/disney-legend-alan-tudyk/)** .
