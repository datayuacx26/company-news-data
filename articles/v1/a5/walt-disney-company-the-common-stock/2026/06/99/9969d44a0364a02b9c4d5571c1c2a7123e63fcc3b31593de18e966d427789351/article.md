# Disney+ and Hulu Created a Nostalgic Mini Mall Based on Fan Favorite Throwbacks

# Disney+ and Hulu Created a Nostalgic Mini Mall Based on Fan Favorite Throwbacks


[Entertainment](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/entertainment/)


June 10, 2026


3 min. read


*Immersive installations and nostalgic storytelling created memorable moments for fans of all ages*


From June 6–7, Disney+ and Hulu transformed Westfield Century City into the Disney+ Hulu Throwbacks Mini Mall, a limited‑time experiential activation that invited fans to celebrate classic titles and anniversaries such as[High School Musical’ s 20th](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/high-school-musical-20th-anniversary/) and *Lizzie McGuire* ’s 25th. The retro‑inspired mini‑mall blended interactive elements and cultural nostalgia to connect audiences with timeless stories.


The experience featured immersive activations highlighting fan‑favorite Disney+ and Hulu titles, stories, and characters. These interactive installations sparked instant, shareable moments, transforming the mall into a communal space where generations could make new memories together.


To celebrate the opening, stars — including[Glee](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-4d0efcb0-960f-426b-9b38-2ff50ae4c9ab) ’s Amber Riley and Josh Sussman, *[High School Musical](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-75101ab0-a99f-40f5-b4b5-743c42dffca9) ’* s Drew Seeley,[The Proud Family: Louder and Prouder](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-081254a4-bc7c-49cc-b027-bdf958ca4a54) ’s Paula Jai Parker and[That’s So Raven](https://www.disneyplus.com/en-gb/browse/entity-86bb14fa-2040-4b25-b53f-732da4657de3) ’s Juliette Goglia — attended a preview of the Disney+ Hulu Throwbacks Mini Mall.

## **Immersive Moments Inspired by Iconic Favorites**


Guests were welcomed by the Throwbacks Fountain, serving as a vibrant centerpiece which set the nostalgic tone of the experience. From there, visitors could explore a series of themed activations, each inspired by a variety of Disney+ and Hulu titles.


The Throwbacks Pin-Vending Machine offered guests a collectible takeaway to share their excitement for the fandom and cultural nostalgia through mementos.[The Princess Diaries](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-8bc7dd86-3b1c-4630-a095-7efadbc47579) Mattress Slide referenced a beloved moment from the sequel — providing a playful and interactive photo opportunity.


A themed Beauty Salon activation in collaboration with MAC Cosmetics rounded out the experience, featuring visual nods to *[Hannah Montana](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-1cffe36e-99a4-40e4-9f2f-2656c6847fab) ,[Aquamarine](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-90a85ada-3a47-470b-8925-293c1ac1597b) ,[The Parent Trap](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-e00985cb-656a-4e5f-9df6-63eb5a46b271) ,[The Cheetah Girls](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-1f8efb78-6923-4c17-9d8c-c7e7a5e2c481) ,* and[Lizzie McGuire](https://www.disneyplus.com/en/browse/entity-a5525471-bb5a-4367-b7a3-52a69dbfbad8) . Visitors had the chance to receive professional touch-ups inspired by these iconic early-2000s looks, further immersing themselves in the aesthetics of the era.
