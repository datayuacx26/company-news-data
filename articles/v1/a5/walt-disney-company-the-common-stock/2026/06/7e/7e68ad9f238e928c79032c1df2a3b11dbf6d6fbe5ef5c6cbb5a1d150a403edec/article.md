# Disney and Pixar’s ‘Toy Story 5’ Brings the Magic of Movies to Kids and Families Through Charitable Initiatives Worldwide

# Disney and Pixar’s ‘Toy Story 5’ Brings the Magic of Movies to Kids and Families Through Charitable Initiatives Worldwide


[Entertainment](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/entertainment/)


June 17, 2026


6 min. read


As


Disney & Pixar’s


*Toy Story 5* arrives in theaters on June 19, The Walt Disney Company


is


continuing to extend the beloved


franchise’s


over


[30-year legacy](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/toy-story-pixar-history/) through impactful initiatives that bring the magic of movies to[children’s hospitals](https://impact.disney.com/social-impact/childrens-hospitals/) and communities


globally


.


“At Disney, we have so many incredible stories and characters that our guests around the world have a deep emotional connection to,” said Lisa Haines, Senior Vice President,[Corporate Social Responsibility](https://impact.disney.com/social-impact/) , The Walt Disney Company. “Moments like the release of


*Toy Story 5* offer an opportunity to turn that connection into hope and joy for kids and their families when they need it most.”


Disney’s efforts surrounding


*Toy Story 5* extend far beyond the screen, including a range of initiatives that are bringing these beloved characters to support the happiness and wellbeing of kids, families, and communities. Those moments will continue to be shared on this page throughout the coming weeks.


## **Sharing** ***Toy Story 5*** **at Children’s Hospitals**


As Disney looks ahead to the release of


*Toy Story 5* , the company is sharing the magic of movies — and the delight of Woody, Buzz Lightyear, Jessie, and the rest of the gang — with young patients in children’s hospitals around the world through first-ever immersive experiences, meaningful moments of joy, and special screenings.


In the United States, Disney is continuing its 25-year collaboration with Starlight Children’s Foundation to help create meaningful moments of imagination and fun[in ways that only Disney can](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/decades-social-impact/) .


Disney is bringing


*Toy Story 5* to over


400 children’s hospitals , including in more than


300 across the United States. Through


Disney Movie Moments , young patients and their families will be able to watch the newly released film while it is still in theaters — alongside Toy Story-themed deliveries of toys from Disney Store, and Woody, Jessie, and Buzz Lightyear-themed Starlight hospital gowns — to help bring comfort and joy to the hospital experience. In addition, Disney incorporated a first-ever red carpet moment at the


*Toy Story 5* Los Angeles premiere with a live pay-off in never-before-seen pop-up immersive experiences for young patients at select children’s hospitals, which you can read more about below.


Across Europe, a


*Toy Story 5* character tour will visit


10 children’s hospitals in 9 countries , bringing the magic of movies into the heart of hospitals.


[A new Toy Story brand spot](https://youtu.be/SAsIN3Fp34I?si=z4Ra9yYLcRLJh6YN) further highlights


[how Disney storytelling creates moments of comfort, imagination, and escape](https://thewaltdisneycompany.eu/the-walt-disney-company-emea-invites-fans-to-nominate-a-hospital-to-receive-disney-and-pixar-magic-through-new-toy-story-5-themed-short-film-the-magic-of-movies-jessie-saves-the-d/) for young patients and their families. In countries across Asia,


*Toy Story* characters are also making children’s hospital visits, including to celebrate the opening of a Pixar-themed care space.


## **From the Red Carpet to Communities Around the World**


### **Star Command Message Transmission**


The children’s hospital initiative kicked off at the film’s Los Angeles premiere on June 9 with the “Star Command Message Transmission,” an interactive digital installation where premiere attendees, including stars of the film, wrote heartfelt messages for children in hospitals. These messages will appear at select hospital activations across the country, connecting young patients directly to the excitement and spirit of the premiere.


### Children’s National in Washington


,


D.C.


On June 16, messages from the red carpet were transmitted to patients and families at


[Children’s National](https://www.childrensnational.org/) in Washington, D.C. as part of an immersive, interactive


*Toy Story 5-* themed experience. The Toy Story Imagination Station featured a life-size interactive Lilypad activation, character meet-and-greets with Woody and Jessie, toy giveaways, drawing sessions with Pixar animators, and an exclusive screening of


*Toy Story 5* .

### **“PlayWell with Disney” in Shenzhen Children’s Hospital**


Toy Story

### 2026 Special Olympics USA Games Opening Ceremonies


Toy Story characters appeared at the **2026 Special Olympics USA Games Opening Ceremonies** in Minneapolis on June 20. Back-to-back *Toy Story 5* screenings will take place during Special Olympics USA Games Family Nights’ Screenings on June 24 and June 25 at the 3M Arena on the University of Minnesota campus, serving approximately **7,000 athletes** and their families.


### **Orlando Health Children’s Arnold Palmer Hospital in Orlando, FL**


On June 30, the Toy Story Imagination Station surprised kids and families at Orlando Health Children’s Arnold Palmer Hospital


in Orlando, Florida.


Families of young patients in attendance shared their impressions:


“When I first came in and saw the red carpet, it was like Hollywood,” shared Tanesha Wilson.


“Today turned into just pure magic,” shared Jennifer Cotto.


## **Bringing Magic to Rady Children’s Health Orange County**


For over 60 years, Disneyland Resort has worked alongside Rady Children’s Health Orange County (formerly CHOC), to bring happiness to patients and their families. In line with the theatrical release of *Toy Story 5* , Disneyland Resort brought the magic of the beloved Toy Story franchise directly to children and their families at Rady Children’s, with a party on the hospital’s second floor. Patients and their families experienced everything from special encounters with Toy Story characters to themed games, crafts, and giveaways.
