# The Walt Disney Company Executives To Discuss Fiscal Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results Via Webcast

BURBANK, Calif., April 13, 2026 – The Walt Disney Company (NYSE: DIS) will host a live webcast to discuss fiscal second quarter 2026 financial results beginning at 8:30 a.m. ET / 5:30 a.m. PT on Wednesday, May 6, 2026.


Disney will release results before the opening of regular trading on May 6, 2026 and post earnings materials at


[www.disney.com/investors](http://www.disney.com/investors) .


To access the webcast, please visit


[www.disney.com/investors](http://www.disney.com/investors)


. The webcast will be archived.
