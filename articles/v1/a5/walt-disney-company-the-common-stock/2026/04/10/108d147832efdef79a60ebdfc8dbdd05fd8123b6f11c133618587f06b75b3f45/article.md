# ‘Wizards Beyond Waverly Place’ Announces Final Chapter, with Selena Gomez Making Her Directorial Debut and Guest Starring in Multiple Episodes

# ‘Wizards Beyond Waverly Place’ Announces Final Chapter, with Selena Gomez Making Her Directorial Debut and Guest Starring in Multiple Episodes


[Entertainment](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/entertainment/)


April 2, 2026


3 min. read


*[Wizards Beyond Waverly Place](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/wizards-beyond-waverly-place-premieres-october-29-on-disney-channel-next-day-on-disney/)* , the continuation of the Emmy® Award-winning *[Wizards of Waverly Place](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-f9cd33a5-678b-45c6-a7b9-607c327962e6)* , will return for one last spell this summer on Disney+, Disney Channel, and Disney Channel On Demand. The four-part event will go into production next week.


Executive producer[Selena Gomez](https://d23.com/selena-gomez-janice-leann-brown-talk-about-the-big-twist-on-wizards-beyond-waverly-place/) will make her directorial debut with the premiere episode. She will also reprise her role as Alex Russo in multiple episodes.


In the four-part event, Billie ([Janice LeAnn Brown](https://d23.com/wizards-beyond-waverly-place-star-janice-leann-brown-takes-fans-behind-the-scenes-of-the-new-series-and-season-1-finale/) ) — still reeling from losing Alex (Gomez) at the end of Season 2 — discovers that the only way to rescue her mother is to reunite with her long-lost father. As her family bands together to find Alex, Billie realizes that their combined power is the only way the Russos can defeat the evil plaguing them.


The revival has been a hit with both new and returning fans. From launch, the series has amassed over 101 million views on Disney Channel YouTube* and emerged as a top streaming series across key youth and young adult audiences at the series’ launch, ranking among the top five titles overall with kids 6–11, teens 12–17, and adults 18–24 in views.**


The series also stars[David Henrie as Justin Russo](https://d23.com/david-henrie-shares-the-magic-with-a-new-generation-in-wizards-beyond-waverly-place-season-2/) , Alkaio Thiele as Roman Russo, Max Matenko as Milo Russo, Taylor Cora as Winter, and Mimi Gianopulos as Giada Russo. Writers Jed Elinoff and Scott Thomas executive produce with Gary Marsh, Jonas Agin, Rick Williams, Gomez, and Henrie. *Wizards of Waverly Place* was created by Todd Greenwald.
