# How ESPN Is Bringing WrestleMania 42 to Life Across Sports, Entertainment, and Culture

# How ESPN Is Bringing WrestleMania 42 to Life Across Sports, Entertainment, and Culture


[ESPN](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/espn/)


April 17, 2026


5 min. read


[WrestleMania 42](https://espnpressroom.com/us/press-releases/2026/04/espn-to-stream-its-first-wwe-wrestlemania-and-provide-comprehensive-on-site-coverage-from-las-vegas-april-16-20/) — one of the biggest events in sports entertainment — takes center stage at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas this weekend and is poised to be one of the most star‑studded editions in WWE history. The two‑night spectacle unfolds Saturday and Sunday with marquee main events, as Cody Rhodes faces Randy Orton (with Pat McAfee in his corner), and Roman Reigns goes head‑to‑head with CM Punk in a highly anticipated showdown that caps months of storytelling.


ESPN is playing an increasingly central role in bringing the scale, spectacle, and storytelling of WrestleMania to fans around the world. With WWE firmly embedded within the ESPN ecosystem —[including WWE’s premium live events available through ESPN’s new direct‑to‑consumer service](https://plus.espn.com/wwe) , and the[first hour of WrestleMania airing on ESPN2 on Saturday and ESPN on Sunday](https://www.espn.com/wwe/story/_/id/48413729/how-watch-wrestlemania-42-espn-matches-schedule) — the network is reimagining how it covers one of the most iconic franchises in sports entertainment. The approach reflects ESPN’s broader strategy: meeting fans where they are while introducing new audiences to the[cultural power of WWE](https://www.espn.com/watch/collections/ed44d44b-6361-46a1-81c4-2599a461dcd8/live-upcoming) .


We spoke with Meghan Burgoon, Senior Director of Content Integration at ESPN, about how WWE fits into ESPN’s long‑term vision, how the network is approaching WrestleMania weekend, and what it means to blend sports, entertainment, and fan culture at scale for this year’s “Showcase of the Immortals.”


**How does the WWE fit into the ESPN ecosystem?**


We are in the business of bringing to life the biggest moments in sports and entertainment, and there is nothing bigger than WrestleMania. We have a commitment to fans to deliver the star power, storytelling, and excitement of this global event across all of our programming. After all, the “E” stands for Entertainment. Bringing the “Grandest Stage of Them All” to fans on ESPN is a natural fit.


**WrestleMania is a global cultural moment, not just a live event. How did ESPN approach content integration to meet fans where they are before, during, and after the weekend?**


We’re celebrating WrestleMania by bringing every aspect of the event to life across all of our platforms in the weeks leading up to WrestleMania 42.


Our content is designed to build anticipation and create real “FOMO” around WrestleMania. WWE Superstars will appear across ESPN programming, and we’ll spotlight celebrity and athlete super-fans making the trip to Las Vegas. You may even see some of our own ESPN talent testing whether they have what it takes to step into the ring.


**Can you take us into the plans around WrestleMania? What is ESPN doing differently for the event?**


The road to WrestleMania is one of the most exciting stretches on the sports calendar, and our goal is to make fans at home feel fully immersed in the experience. That starts with showcasing the massive footprint WWE will have across Las Vegas during the week.


*SportsCenter* will originate from WWE World, highlighting the ultimate fan experience, while *First Take* will broadcast live from Allegiant Stadium, home of WrestleMania’s two-night event. Our content teams will be throughout the city capturing the energy and bringing the moment to life from every angle.


**How did ESPN balance serving core WWE fans while also making WrestleMania accessible to more casual sports audiences?**


This balance is at the heart of what ESPN does best. Our role is to engage core fans while also introducing WWE to new audiences and helping grow the fandom. Everyone starts somewhere.


We’re delivering content for every level of engagement — from dedicated shows like *No-Contest Wrestling* and event preview programming for hardcore fans, to integrations on *SportsCenter* and social-first content that introduce the fun, spectacle, and personalities of WWE to casual viewers. Our strategy reflects a belief that this sport will continue to grow.


**From a long-term perspective, how does ESPN’s WrestleMania coverage fit into the network’s broader strategy for blending sports, storytelling, and fan culture?**


Sports coverage today goes beyond what happens on the field — or in this case, the ring. WWE understood that early, building unforgettable entrances, long‑form storytelling, and an entire culture around its superstars.


That blueprint aligns perfectly with ESPN’s approach. Our job is to continue bringing that energy, emotion, and storytelling to fans in ways that feel authentic, modern, and immersive.
