# Docebo Launches Docebo AgentHub and Unites Skills Intelligence, Enterprise Knowledge, and Agentic AI in a Single Platform | Docebo

*In its largest release to date, Docebo unifies learning, enterprise knowledge, and skills intelligence into a single hub, going native inside Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and ChatGPT.*


MIAMI–([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/) )–Docebo Inc. (NASDAQ: DCBO, TSX: DCBO), (“Docebo” or the “Company”), the enterprise platform for the AI-era workforce, unifying skills intelligence, learning, and knowledge in one closed loop, today unveiled its next-generation platform at its flagship event, Docebo Inspire 2026.


With this release, Docebo steps into a new category. Today, Docebo becomes something bigger: a unified hub where learning, enterprise knowledge, and skills intelligence come together in a single closed loop. This combination is what the market has been missing and defines the next era of Docebo.


In a category long defined by legacy systems and decades of incremental change, Docebo is choosing to lead it forward.


The Company today announced a portfolio of platform innovations designed to bring learning, knowledge, and skills to where work happens, close skills gaps at scale, and give learning and development teams a smarter operational foundation, including:


- **Docebo AgentHub** , moving beyond chatbots to agents that reason, decide, and act autonomously, including turning enterprise knowledge into ready-to-consume learning on demand;
- **Enterprise Knowledge** , connecting Docebo to 20+ enterprise knowledge sources so people can find and use the knowledge that already exists across their organization;
- **Docebo Skills Intelligence** , powered by 365Talents, closing the loop between skills detection, learning delivery, and validation so organizations can see, grow, and measure workforce capability in real time;
- **Docebo MCP (Model Context Protocol) Server** , making Docebo a knowledge source inside Claude, Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, and any other MCP-enabled AI tool;
- **Docebo Companion** , a headless module that brings Docebo’s AI-powered learning into any system of work without leaving the active tab;
- **Docebo AI Tutor,** an on-demand AI study companion that explains concepts, summarizes content, and guides learners through material around the clock.


Together, we expect these innovations will close a loop in a way we do not think the enterprises have experienced before. Skills intelligence tells Docebo who needs what and why. Knowledge flows into Docebo from every system where it lives. Docebo turns that knowledge into learning. And learning reaches people in the tools they already use every day.


**Docebo: From Learning Platform to Learning and Knowledge Hub**


Knowledge inside an enterprise is scattered across Confluence, SharePoint, OneDrive, Google Drive, ticketing systems, CRM, and dozens of other tools. Learning has historically lived in a separate silo, disconnected from the knowledge people actually rely on to do their jobs. Docebo aims to close this gap.


Enterprise Knowledge connects Docebo to 20+ knowledge sources, giving organizations a single system where their knowledge is accessible, searchable, and ready to act on. For the first time, one-off documents, policy pages, product specs, and tribal knowledge buried in shared drives become usable inputs for learning and workforce readiness.


Docebo AgentHub takes that further. Its agents reason, decide, and act on top of the knowledge graph and to turn scattered content into structured learning. An admin can ask an agent to build a 15-minute onboarding micro-course for new sales reps on a specific product, and the agent will pull from Drive, Confluence, SharePoint, and Docebo’s own catalog, generate the outline, the assessment, and the media plan, and deliver a ready-to-review draft. One-off knowledge becomes a course. A scattered wiki becomes a skills campaign. A product release note becomes an interactive podcast.


That is the shift. Docebo is no longer only the place where learning is delivered. It is the place where knowledge is transformed into learning, and both reach people where they already work.


“Docebo AgentHub is the foundation we’ve been building toward. It gives every organization the infrastructure to help make AI work for their people in the flow of daily work,” said Alessio Artuffo, President and CEO of Docebo. “For twenty years, we have delivered learning. Today, we deliver learning, enterprise knowledge, and skills intelligence together, inside a single hub. That combination is what makes Docebo uniquely positioned in this market, and it is what our customers have been asking for. Docebo is at its best when we lead the category. The LMS industry has moved slowly for a long time. With this release, we are taking it into a new era.”


Artuffo added: “There is a narrative in the market that AI will flatten enterprise software. We do not see it that way. Docebo holds and organizes the data that makes AI valuable for workforce readiness: skills, learning, compliance, and now enterprise knowledge. When you pair that data with agents, and open the platform to every major AI assistant, you do not get squeezed by this shift. You become more essential in it. This release is proof that Docebo is a beneficiary of the AI evolution, not a bystander.”


**Docebo Skills Intelligence: The Differentiator, Powered by 365Talents**


Learning and knowledge only create value when an organization knows who needs what, when, and why. That is the job of skills intelligence, and it is the layer that makes the rest of the Docebo platform uniquely powerful.


Through its acquisition of 365Talents, Docebo now embeds a full skills intelligence engine directly into the learning and knowledge hub. Skills intelligence is no longer a separate system bolted on the side. It is the connective tissue that makes every other Docebo capability smarter.


The result is a closed loop we believe no other LMS delivers end-to-end:


- **Detect.** Skills campaigns surface roles, proficiencies, and inferred skills across the workforce. Managers and employees see a real, dynamic picture of capability, not a static spreadsheet;
- **Grow.** Skills gaps trigger targeted learning paths inside Docebo, powered by Enterprise Knowledge and Docebo AgentHub. Recommendations are driven by actual need, not generic assignment;
- **Validate.** Completed learning, manager reviews, and evaluation signals feed updated skill levels back into the system. The organization’s skills picture gets more accurate every day.


This loop is what the market has been missing. According to RedThread Research, 59% of the best-performing organizations integrate skills into their L&D delivery, compared to 36% of everyone else. The pattern is clear: organizations that can see their people and connect that visibility to learning and knowledge perform better. We believe Docebo is the only platform that delivers all three inside one hub.


**Docebo MCP: Opening the Hub to the Enterprise AI Stack**


Docebo MCP (Model Context Protocol) Server makes Docebo a native knowledge source inside LLMs, including Claude, Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, and any other MCP-enabled AI tool. AI assistants now have visibility into learning programs, skills data, and training catalogs. Using Docebo’s secure, supported connection, they can look up learner progress, surface course recommendations, and check certification status without a custom integration or months of development work.


Docebo MCP Server is live today in public beta and launches generally in July 2026.


“Most organizations have already started rolling out AI assistants,” said Riccardo La Rosa, CTO of Docebo. “The problem is that on day one, those tools know nothing about your learning programs, your skills data, or your people. MCP changes that. The AI tools your organization already uses connect natively to Docebo, so the investment you’ve made in learning and skills data finally shows up where your people are working.”


**The Unique Combination**


Taken together, Docebo AgentHub, Enterprise Knowledge, Docebo Skills Intelligence, and Docebo MCP represent something the market has not seen before: a single, connected platform where skills direct what matters, knowledge feeds learning, agents do the work, and AI assistants make all of it available in the tools people already use. Each piece is valuable on its own. The combination is what we believe makes Docebo uniquely differentiated.


**Core Learning Platform Updates**


Docebo also announced a wave of platform improvements built directly in response to what customers have been asking for. These updates, which we expect to roll out through 2026, include:


- Rebuilt navigation experience for admins with streamlined design, modern UI/UX, and improved accessibility;
- eCommerce updates, including improved shared credit management and multi-currency functionality;
- An improved Content Marketplace to make accessing world-class content easier;
- Interactive Podcasts, turning lesson content into dynamic episodes with two AI hosts for more engaging learning;
- Lesson Narrator, instantly adding natural AI voiceover to any lesson so learners can consume content on the move, in their own language;
- Brand/Experience Studio, giving organizations deeper control over platform branding, including colors, fonts, and logos without touching a line of code;
- Other improvements to live training management, certification lifecycles, on-the-job checklists, advanced enrollment rules management, and analytics.


**A New Pace, Powered by AI**


The breadth of this release reflects a deeper shift inside Docebo. The majority of Docebo’s code is now AI-assisted, driving a 50%+ increase in new product releases in the last quarter alone. The investment in AI is not only reshaping what Docebo ships. It is reshaping how fast Docebo ships it.
