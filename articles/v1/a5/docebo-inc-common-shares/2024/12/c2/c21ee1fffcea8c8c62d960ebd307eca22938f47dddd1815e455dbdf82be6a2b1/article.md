# Docebo Recognized with Nine Brandon Hall Excellence in Technology Awards, Reinforcing Leadership in Learning Innovation | Docebo

**Toronto, December 18, 2024 –**[Docebo Inc](https://www.docebo.com/) . (NASDAQ: DCBO; TSX:DCBO) (“Docebo” or the “Company”), a leading learning platform provider with a foundation in artificial intelligence (AI) and innovation, is proud to announce that it has been awarded nine prestigious Brandon Hall Group Excellence in Technology Awards. This remarkable accomplishment underscores Docebo’s unwavering commitment to enhancing both customer experience learning and employee experience learning.


The Brandon Hall Group Excellence in Technology Awards are among the most esteemed in the industry, recognizing organizations that have successfully deployed programs, strategies, and tools that have achieved measurable results. This year, Docebo has been honored with seven Gold and two Silver awards across the Learning and Development Technology, Future of Work, and Sales Enablement Technology categories.


**Learning and Development Technology Awards**


- GOLD: Best Advance in Generative AI Learning Solution
- GOLD: Best Advance in Learning Management Measurement/Business Impact Tools
- GOLD: Best Advance in Learning Management Technology (LMT)
- GOLD: Best Advance in Learning Management Technology for Compliance Training
- GOLD: Best Advance in Learning Management Technology for External Training
- GOLD: Best Advance in Social Learning Technology


**Future of Work Awards**


- GOLD: Best Advance in Business Strategy and Technology Innovation
- SILVER: Best Advance in Business Automation


**Sales Enablement Technology Awards**


- SILVER: Best Advance in Sales Enablement and Performance Tools (SEP)


These recent wins, combined with the seven Human Capital Management awards won by our valued customers earlier this year, highlight Docebo’s position as a versatile, impactful, and forward-thinking leader and our commitment to excellence in learning technology.


Reflecting on the achievement, Alessio Artuffo, CEO of Docebo, shared, “These awards are a testament to the talent and dedication of our team. We’re not just building products; we’re shaping the future of learning with innovative solutions that drive real impact for our customers and empower businesses and learners around the world. This recognition is a vote of confidence in our vision for the future and validates our key investments, especially in customer experience learning.”


Michael Rochelle, Chief Strategy Officer and Principal HCM Analyst at Brandon Hall Group, expressed his confidence in Docebo’s leadership, stating, “Docebo exemplifies what it means to be an innovative market leader in the learning and development space. Their ability to not only anticipate but also shape the future of learning and work is reflected in the breadth and depth of these awards. They continue to set the standard for excellence, and it’s clear that their vision extends well beyond technology—it’s about creating meaningful change for businesses and learners alike.”


Award submissions underwent a rigorous evaluation process led by a panel of seasoned industry experts and senior analysts at Brandon Hall Group. Entries were carefully reviewed for their ability to address critical business challenges with innovative solutions, deliver measurable results, highlight unique differentiators, and showcase clear advancements in technology, thereby ensuring that each award recognizes true excellence in innovation and impactful business outcomes.


Award recipients will be recognized at Brandon Hall Group’s HCM Excellence Conference 2025, scheduled for January 28-30, 2025, in West Palm Beach, Florida. The event will also feature special sessions where winners can showcase their innovative practices and insights.

**About Brandon Hall Group™**


Brandon Hall Group is the only professional development company that offers data, research, insights, and certification to Learning and Talent executives and organizations. The best minds in Human Capital Management (HCM) choose Brandon Hall Group to help them create future-proof employee development plans for the new era.


For over 30 years, we have empowered, recognized, and certified excellence in organizations worldwide, influencing the development of over 10 million employees and executives. Our HCM Excellence Awards program was the first to recognize organizations for learning and talent and is the gold standard, known as the “Academy Awards of Human Capital Management.”


The awards recognize the best organizations that have successfully developed and deployed programs, strategies, modalities, processes, systems, and tools that have achieved measurable results. We are honored to receive applications from organizations worldwide ranging from small, medium, large, and global enterprises to government, not-for-profits, and associations.
