# Docebo Wins Top Recognition – Gold at the Canadian Marketing Association Awards for Leaders in Learning Series | Docebo

**Toronto, December 4, 2024 –**[Docebo Inc](https://www.docebo.com/?utm_medium=ppc&utm_source=googleads&utm_campaign=20447693886&utm_term=docebo) . (NASDAQ: DCBO; TSX:DCBO) (“Docebo” or the “Company”), a leading learning platform provider with a foundation in artificial intelligence (AI) and innovation,won Gold in the highly competitive LinkedIn B2B Marketing Award category at this year’s Canadian Marketing Association (CMA) Awards for its groundbreaking thought leadership series,[Leaders in Learning](https://www.docebo.com/leaders-in-learning?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=organic_pr&utm_campaign=subscribe&utm_medium=ppc&utm_source=googleads&utm_campaign=20447693886&utm_term=docebo) . The win underscores Docebo’s impact on the global learning community and its customers by fostering meaningful conversations, delivering innovative insights, and shaping strategies that drive real business outcomes.


“To have *Leaders in Learning* recognized by the CMA is a testament to Docebo’s role at the forefront of thought leadership in the learning industry,” said Alex Asnovich, Chief Marketing Officer at Docebo. “This program is about more than sharing ideas—it’s about shaping the future of learning. By bringing together the industry’s brightest minds to tackle the challenges and opportunities facing modern organizations, we’re helping our customers elevate their learning programs and achieve measurable results.”


To date, the series has featured notable experts (including Josh Bersin, Taggart McCurdy, Jason Weeman, Maureen Lonergan, Jessica Winder, Daniel Garcia Alfisi, John Zurovchak, and Courtney Sembler), and is distributed through a variety of channels, including YouTube and[LinkedIn’s newsletter platform](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/leaders-in-learning-7166550123076902912/) , where it has gained a significant following. The initiative reflects Docebo’s commitment not only to leading the conversation on learning, but also to actively driving it forward.


“We’re incredibly proud of this recognition and even more excited about the impact the program has had on our audience in such a short amount of time,” added Asnovich. “This award belongs to everyone who has contributed to making *Leaders in Learning* the success it is today—from our team to the learning industry thought leaders who share their expertise with us.”

**About the Canadian Marketing Association**


The CMA is the voice of marketing in Canada and our purpose is to champion marketing’s powerful impact. We are the catalyst to help Canada’s marketers thrive today, while building the marketing mindset and environment of tomorrow. We provide opportunities for our members from coast to coast to develop professionally, to contribute to marketing thought leadership, to build strong networks, and to strengthen the regulatory climate for business success. Our Chartered Marketer (CM) designation signifies that recipients are highly qualified and up to date with best practices, as reflected in the Canadian Marketing Code of Ethics and Standards. We represent virtually all of Canada’s major business sectors, and all marketing disciplines, channels and technologies. Our Consumer Centre helps Canadians better understand their rights and obligations. For more information, visit thecma.ca.
