# Docebo Recognized with 7 Brandon Hall Group Excellence in Technology Awards 2023 | Docebo

*The 2023 Brandon Hall Group Excellence in Technology Awards recognizes Docebo for outstanding achievements in Future of Work, Learning and Development, and Sales Enablement*


**Toronto,** **January 16, 2024** –[Docebo Inc](https://www.docebo.com/) . (NASDAQ: DCBO; TSX:DCBO) (“Docebo” or the “Company”), a leading learning platform provider with a foundation in artificial intelligence (AI) and innovation, announced today its outstanding success in the esteemed Brandon Hall Group Excellence in Technology Awards in categories focused on extraordinary innovations in learning technology and enterprise-wide platforms.


Each year, the Excellence Awards recognize the best organizations that have successfully deployed programs, strategies, modalities, processes, systems, and tools that have achieved measurable results. “In our 30th year, the Excellence in Technology Awards continue to showcase the best innovations in learning, talent management, talent acquisition, HR, workforce management, and sales enablement technologies. We are proud to receive applications from a diverse range of organizations globally, reflecting the ever-evolving landscape of technology solutions,” said Brandon Hall Group Chief Operating Officer Rachel Cooke, leader of the Excellence Awards program.


This year, Docebo has been honored with a total of seven coveted awards, including six Gold awards and one Bronze award in the following categories.


**Future of Work Awards**


- GOLD: Best Advance in Business Strategy and Technology Innovation


**Learning and Development Technology Awards**


- GOLD: Best Advance in AI and Machine Learning
- GOLD: Best Advance in Learning Management Technology for External Training
- GOLD: Best Advance in Learning Management Measurement/Business Impact Tools
- GOLD: Best Advance in Learning Management Technology (LMT)
- GOLD: Best Advance in Unique Learning Technology


**Sales Enablement Technology Awards**


- BRONZE: Best Advance in Unique Sales Enablement Technology


Alessio Artuffo, President and COO of Docebo, proudly accepted these awards on behalf of the Company and stated, “Every time we win an award for our performance, it underscores our commitment to innovation, operational excellence, and customer satisfaction. The fact that we’ve earned such recognition across a broad base of the rapidly evolving learning technology landscape is a testament to our team’s concentrated focus and dedication to delivering cutting-edge solutions that drive positive outcomes for our clients—many of whom were also recognized this year. We are deeply honored to be recognized by Brandon Hall Group as a leader and innovator in the learning technology industry and immensely proud of the successes our platform enables our customers to achieve.”


Entries were rigorously evaluated by a panel of veteran, independent senior industry experts, Brandon Hall Group analysts, and executives based on criteria such as fit the need, program design, functionality, innovation, and overall measurable benefits.


“Our awards program is distinguished by the thorough evaluation process led by industry experts. Judges meticulously score each entry, and our executive leadership team at Brandon Hall Group reviews and validates the judging and scoring. The level of the award is solely determined by the number of points, ensuring a fair and unbiased recognition of excellence in technology,” said Brandon Hall Group Chief Executive Officer Mike Cooke.


Excellence Award winners will be honored at Brandon Hall Group’s HCM Excellence Conference, taking place from February 13-15, 2024, at the Hilton West Palm Beach, Florida. Select winners will also serve as presenters in breakout sessions, sharing leading practices during the conference.
