# GetAccept ranks yet again on G2’s 2024 Best Software Awards for Sales Products

**New York, NY - February 6, 2024** – GetAccept, the Digital Sales Room platform, today announced it has been named in[G2’s 2024 Best Software Awards](https://www.g2.com/best-software-companies/top-sales) , placing 19th on the Sales Product list. As the world’s largest and most trusted software marketplace,[G2](https://www.g2.com/?utm_source=vendor-news&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=best-software-2024) is visited by 90 million software buyers each year. Its annual Best Software Awards rank the world’s best software companies and products based on authentic, timely reviews from real users .


This is GetAccept ’s second time receiving a G2’s Best Software Award recognition, earning its place this year thanks to their mission to radically redefine the way companies run sales.


GetAccept’s Digital Dales Room empowers revenue teams to increase win rates by engaging and understanding buyers from opportunity to customer, removing friction and increasing engagement in the buying process.


The platform leverages sales content management, proposals and contracts, CPQ, tracking and analytics, mutual action plans, electronic signatures, and seamlessly integrates with your CRM, instantly updating all interactions in one place: the Digital Sales Room.


*“We’re immensely proud to yet again be named on G2’s Best Software Awards, on the Sales Product list”* says Samir Smajic, GetAccept’s CEO and co-founder. *“The GetAccept Digital Sales Room platform provides a complete digital selling and buying experience in one, engaging shared space. The B2B buying journey is very complex and we’re honored to be part of this award showing that we are committed to leading the way and showing how modern sales should be done.”*


*“B2B software buyers, just like consumers, start their purchasing journey with research,”* said Sara Rossio, Chief Product Officer at G2. *“As the world’s largest software marketplace, G2 attracts more than 90 million buyers to our site each year – more than any other B2B marketplace – reaching those from companies of all sizes, in all industries. Based on their authentic feedback, we’re proud to announce the 2024 Best Software Award winners. Congratulations to the less than 1% of vendors listed on G2 who made one of our 30+ lists this year, achieving recognition driven by verified data rooted in the source that truly matters — authentic customer voice.”*


G2’s 2024 Best Software Awards feature more than 30 different lists, ranking software vendors and products using G2’s proprietary algorithm, which is based on G2’s verified user reviews and publicly available market presence data. To be eligible for the Best Software Awards, a software company or product must have received at least 50 approved reviews during the 2023 calendar year. Scores reflect only data from reviews submitted during this evaluation period.


To learn more, view[G2’s 2024 Best Software Awards](https://www.g2.com/best?utm_source=vendor-news&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=best-software-2024) and read more about[G2’s methodology](https://documentation.g2.com/docs/research-scoring-methodologies#best-software?utm_source=vendor-news&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=best-software-2024) .

**About GetAccept**


GetAccept was founded in 2015 by Swedish entrepreneurs and Y Combinator alumni to bridge the gap between outdated sales processes and dire buyer experiences. As the creator and frontrunner of the Digital Sales Room category, GetAccept empowers revenue teams to increase their win rates by engaging and understanding buyers from opportunity to customer. Seamless integrations to CRMs like Salesforce, Hubspot and Microsoft Dynamics allow sellers to collaborate and offer the best buyer experience while shortening their sales cycles.


The platform serves 40,000+ users across 4,000+ enterprises within 60+ countries. GetAccept is headquartered in Malmö, Sweden, and has a team of 200+ employees across the US, Sweden, United Kingdom, France, Norway, and Denmark.
