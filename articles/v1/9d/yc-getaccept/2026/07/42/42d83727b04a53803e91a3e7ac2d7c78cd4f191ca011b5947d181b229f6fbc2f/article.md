# GetAccept welcomes Jon Buss as Non-Executive Director to the GetAccept Board

Malmö, July 3, 2023


— GetAccept, the category leader within the Digital Sales Room space, is pleased to announce the appointment of


[Jon Buss](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jonbuss/) as a Non-Executive Director (NED) to its board.


Jon brings a wealth of experience and a strong background in go-to-market (GTM) strategies, operational expertise, and revenue growth. His appointment is expected to significantly enhance the company's growth journey and further support its management team.


Jon Buss has an impressive track record of driving growth and success across various global organizations. He currently serves as Managing Director at Incode Technologies, a pioneer in the identity verification and biometric authentication industry. Jon has over 25 years of sales and general management leadership experience, primarily in the technology industry, with global companies at all stages of growth. Before joining Incode, Jon was Managing Director and EVP at Yext, a $400M revenue NYSE-listed technology company, where he led the EMEA business from $2M to $85M ARR. He’s also held international executive leadership roles at Criteo, Experian, Dun & Bradstreet, and Computacentre.


In addition to his executive roles, Jon Buss has served as a NED and advisor to several organizations. He is currently an Advisor at Roam, an all-in-one Cloud HQ designed to unite distributed companies, and an Investor & Advisor at Kore Labs, a platform for creating and managing financial products. Furthermore, Jon sat as a NED at BREVO (formally SENDINBLUE), an all-in-one marketing platform that grew rapidly during his time on the board from 2018 to 2021.


Commenting on his appointment, Jon Buss said, *"GetAccept is a dynamic and innovative company well-positioned for remarkable growth. I am thrilled to join the board and work closely with the CEO, management team, and my fellow directors to accelerate the company's success. With my experience in driving revenue growth and go-to-market strategies, I look forward to contributing to GetAccept's expansion and helping them achieve new heights."*


GetAccept's CEO and Co-founder, Samir Smajic, is excited about Jon Buss joining the board, *"We are thrilled to have Jon Buss on board! His deep expertise in go-to-market strategies and execution will be invaluable as we continue our growth journey. We believe Jon's addition to our already strong team will enable us to reach new levels of success and drive innovation in our industry."*


**About GetAccept**


GetAccept was founded in 2015 by Swedish entrepreneurs and Y Combinator alumni to bridge the gap between outdated sales processes and bad buyer experiences. As the creator and frontrunner of the Digital Sales Room category, GetAccept empowers revenue teams to increase their win rates by engaging and understanding buyers from opportunity to signed deal. Seamless integrations with CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Microsoft Dynamics allow sellers to collaborate and offer the best buyer experience while shortening sales cycles.


Voted as the 11th best sales product of 2023 by[G2 Best Software Awards](https://www.g2.com/best-software-companies/top-sales) , the platform serves 40,000+ users across 4,000+ enterprises within 60+ countries. GetAccept is headquartered in Malmö, Sweden, with 200+ employees across the US, Sweden, United Kingdom, France, Norway, and Denmark.
