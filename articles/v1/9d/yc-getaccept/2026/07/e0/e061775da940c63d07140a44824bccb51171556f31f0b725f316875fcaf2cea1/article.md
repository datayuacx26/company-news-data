# GetAccept ranks #17 in G2's Best Sales Products of 2025

The best sales tools don’t just help teams close deals – they empower sellers to build real relationships, streamline their workflow, and ultimately, win more. That’s why we’re incredibly proud to share that GetAccept has been ranked #17 on G2’s Best Sales Software Products list for 2025!


Every year, G2 recognizes the top software solutions that help businesses grow and succeed, based entirely on real customer reviews. Making the list is an achievement in itself, but securing a top 20 spot? That’s something we’re incredibly proud of!


## Why this matters


In today’s fast-moving sales landscape, buyers expect more than a PDF proposal and a follow-up email. Deals are won by the sellers who engage, personalize, and create a seamless experience from first pitch to final signature.


That’s where GetAccept comes in.


From trackable proposals to real-time insights and contract approvals, we help sales teams cut the friction and close deals faster.


And this G2 recognition tells us we’re on the right track – because the people who matter most (our customers!) say so.


## What sets GetAccept apart?


So, what makes GetAccept one of the best sales products in the world?


- **Proposals & quotes that engage buyers.** Personalize every proposal, track engagement, and know exactly when to follow up.
- **Real-time deal insights.** Get notified the moment a prospect interacts with your content, so you can take action at the right time.
- **E-signatures that speed up approvals.** Remove friction from the closing process with legally binding, hassle-free e-signing.
- **A better way to manage sales content.** Keep the right documents, videos, and presentations in one place – accessible exactly when you need them.


Sales is a tough game, but the right tools make all the difference.
