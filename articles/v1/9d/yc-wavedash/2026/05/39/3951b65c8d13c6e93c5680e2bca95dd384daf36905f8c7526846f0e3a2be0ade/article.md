# Wavedash Spring Jam '26

Spring Jam 26 starts tomorrow. It's our first game jam, runs Friday through Sunday, and the prize pool is $4,150 across eight winners.


## Schedule


- **Start** — Friday, May 29 at 9:00 AM PST
- **Close** — Sunday, May 31 at 11:59 PM PST
- **Winners announced** — Tuesday, June 16


## Theme


The theme will be announced at 9:00 AM PST tomorrow in the Wavedash Discord.


## Prizes


The prize pool totals $4,150 across eight winners.


- **Grand Prize** — $1,000
- **Honorable Mentions** — $450 each, awarded to seven games


## Judges


The Wavedash team is judging Spring Jam 26.


## Rubric


Earlier this year we hosted a challenge during Gamedev.js Jam, and we got the feedback piece wrong. Developers put in hard work and walked away without a clear sense of how their games were evaluated, what we liked, or what we didn't. That's on us, and we want to fix it.


For Spring Jam 26, every entry gets a written evaluation from the Wavedash team. You'll know exactly what we looked at and how your game scored across each. Win or not, you'll leave with something useful.


## How to enter


Publish your game to Wavedash, then post a publicly playable Wavedash link in the` #spring-jam-26` channel on Discord before the Sunday, May 31 at 11:59 PM PST deadline.


## Additional information


Any engine that targets the web works. Solo and team submissions are both welcome.


## Where to go


Join at[discord.wavedash.com](https://discord.wavedash.com/) for the full rules, the` #spring-jam-26` channel, and to meet the rest of the developers building alongside you.


See you Friday at 9 AM PST.
