# Introducing the Wavedash Creator Fund

Today we're launching the Wavedash Creator Fund... a new program that puts money directly into the hands of the developers building on Wavedash.


The Creator Fund is a recurring monthly pool distributed to creators based on how their games perform on the platform. The more players engage with your games, the larger your share. Playtime is the primary driver, weighted alongside other signals like retention and engagement so that the Fund rewards not just reach, but games people genuinely love to return to.


There's nothing to apply for. Every eligible creator is automatically included, and payouts are distributed at the end of each month.


The Fund is already live. Payouts for July went out this morning. Eligible creators will find the details in their inbox and in their in-app notifications.


Our goal with the Creator Fund is simple: make it possible for creators to earn real money doing what they love. When you build something people want to play, we want you to share in that success.


Keep making awesome games <3
