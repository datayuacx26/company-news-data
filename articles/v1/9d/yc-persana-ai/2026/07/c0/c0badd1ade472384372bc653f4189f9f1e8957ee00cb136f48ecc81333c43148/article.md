# How to Get Leads for Your Business: Expert-Tested Strategies That Actually Work

A striking 6 in 10 marketers make generating business leads their top priority. This makes sense since 22% of CEOs and **CMOs rank lead generation** as their primary focus for the next 12 months – with only customer retention ranking higher.

But many businesses face challenges with **targeted lead generation** . While 71% of customers expect tailored experiences, just 36% of buyers put their trust in company employees. ## **Build a Strong Foundation for Targeted Lead Generation**


Your success in lead generation depends on how well you know your ideal customers. Companies that **use**[buyer personas](https://persana.ai/blogs/what-is-buyer-persona) see 73% higher conversions compared to those who don't. This makes customer understanding crucial to your business growth.


A detailed **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** should outline the perfect company fit for your product or service. Your ICP needs to combine demographic, firmographic, and environmental characteristics. This combination helps you target prospects who are more likely to convert.


Buyer personas represent specific individuals within your target companies. These **semi-fictional profiles combine audience data** into relatable characters. Your team can better understand customers' pain points and motivations. Most companies start with[two to five buyer personas](https://business.adobe.com/blog/basics/how-to-create-buyer-personas) and refine them as their offerings grow.


**Accurate targeting requires** both firmographic data (company size, industry, revenue, location) and technographic information (technology stack and software usage). This data creates a complete view of your prospects. You can then shift from **broad segmentation to hyper-specific targeting** .


Time invested in this foundation brings strong returns. A deep **understanding of existing customers** ' needs helps you address prospects' questions effectively. You can solve their challenges and meet their expectations, which guides more qualified leads to your business.


## **Inbound Strategies to Attract High-Intent Leads**


Content marketing brings in[three times more leads](https://keap.com/small-business-automation-blog/marketing/content-marketing/content-marketing-for-lead-generation-7-tried-and-tested-strategies) than traditional marketing methods. This makes inbound strategies crucial to attract high-intent prospects. SEO has emerged as one of the best ROI-driven lead generation strategies because it keeps **customer acquisition costs low** .


When your bottom-of-funnel content ranks organically, you'll see amazing results. Creating comparison pages that show how you stack up against competitors can **bring in qualified leads quickly** . Your search visibility also improves when you publish long-form content that showcases your expertise.


Your landing pages need the **right optimization strategy** . Pages with multiple offers can make your conversion rates drop by **266%** . The best approach is to stick to one offer per page and use clear, compelling calls-to-action.


**Gated content helps you generate leads effectively:**


-


**Ebooks and white papers** : Over **80%** of B2B content marketing assets need a form fill


-


**Webinars** : Success rate of **70%** in generating conversions


-


**Interactive quizzes** : Get **52.6%** more engagement than static content


-


**Templates** : Users find immediate value in these practical resources


Lead magnets help **50%** of marketers boost their conversion rates. You can encourage prospects to share their contact details by giving them valuable resources like checklists, free tools, or exclusive industry insights.


Progressive profiling techniques work best here. They help you collect information gradually through multiple interactions. This lets you customize engagement without creating friction when someone first converts.


## **Outbound and Automation Tactics That Scale**


Outbound **automation tactics create expandable systems** for lead acquisition alongside inbound marketing.[AI chatbots](https://persana.ai/blogs/ai-lead-generation-chatbots) have become powerful lead generation tools that operate 24/7 and streamline processes through automated lead qualification and data collection. These **conversational AI solutions boost conversion** rates by a lot - companies report their chatbot leads exceed 40% compared to traditional landing pages at 2.35%.


Customer testimonials work as **exceptional lead generation** assets because 85% of people trust online reviews as much as recommendations from friends and family. Video testimonials prove especially effective when 77% of viewers say they influenced their purchasing decisions.


Retargeting previous website visitors produces impressive results. Retargeting ads average[0.7% CTR compared to general display campaigns' 0.07%](https://saleshive.com/blog/lead-generation-retargeting-campaigns-best-practices-success/) . B2B companies benefit from lower costs per high-intent visitor and better quality leads.


Webinars remain a top lead generation source, with 73% of B2B marketers ranking them highest. These interactive sessions generate an average cost-per-lead of $72, which is nowhere near the cost of SEM ($92) or trade shows ($800+).


Your CRM system should combine smoothly with these tactics. This comprehensive approach creates a continuous connection where **AI-identified leads** flow directly to your sales team to follow up quickly. Your lead generation efforts will expand without increasing your workload proportionally.
