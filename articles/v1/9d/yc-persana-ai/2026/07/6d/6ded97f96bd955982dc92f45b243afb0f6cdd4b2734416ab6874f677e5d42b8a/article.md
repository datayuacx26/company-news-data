## **Define and Segment Your Leads**


Let's understand the fundamentals of[lead prioritization](https://persana.ai/blogs/ai-for-lead-qualification) and segmentation that are the foundations of any successful B2B sales strategy before we explore scoring systems and automation tools.


### **What is lead prioritization?**


Lead prioritization helps teams evaluate and **rank sales leads based on their potential value** and conversion likelihood. Sales teams separate quality leads from unnecessary ones to focus on prospects with the highest chance of becoming customers. This approach helps sales representatives use their time, efforts, and resources better while targeting the most promising leads.


### **Why not all leads are equal**


B2B lead generation comes with a harsh reality.[Around 80% of new leads never translate into sales](https://www.theinsightcollective.com/insights/prioritize-b2b-sales-leads) . Despite this fact, 54% of marketers claim that increasing lead volume is their main goal. **Mass-generated contacts** end up diluting your lead pool.


The main difference lies in understanding that genuine leads have an activity context, while simple contacts do not. Clicks don't always mean leads, and leads don't always convert to sales. Some prospects are ready to buy right away, while others might never convert despite your best efforts.


### **How to identify and segment B2B leads?**


Your **ideal customer profile (ICP)** - attributes describing your perfect customer - should be the starting point for lead segmentation. You can then segment leads across multiple dimensions:


-


**Firmographic data** : Industry, company size, location, and revenue


-


**Behavioral data** : Website visits, content involvement, and email interactions


-


[Intent signals](https://persana.ai/blogs/behavioral-intent-signals) : Research behaviors and keywords suggesting buying interest


-


**Buying stage** : The prospect's position in their purchase experience


Most organizations use a **temperature-based classification system** . They categorize leads as hot, warm, or cold based on sales readiness. Hot prospects want to purchase now, warm leads know your company's value, and cold leads have minimal awareness or need.


Proper segmentation enables **tailored outreach** that significantly improves conversion rates and helps sales teams focus on accounts that matter most.


## **Build a Lead Scoring System That Works**


Building an effective scoring system comes next after identifying leads.[Lead scoring models](https://persana.ai/blogs/lead-scoring) rank prospects on a 0-100 point scale based on their sales-readiness and business fit.


### **Use firmographic and behavioral data**


Firmographic data are the foundations of your **lead scoring model** . Your model should include company details like size, industry, revenue, and location. Prospects who match your ideal customer profile (ICP) should receive higher points for these attributes. The model also tracks external triggers such as new leadership, mergers, or investments to enhance your **firmographic scoring** .


Behavioral data shows engagement patterns through:


-


Email interactions (opens, click-through)


-


Website activities (page visits, content downloads)


-


Social media participation (comments, shares)


These implicit indicators give **stronger conversion signals** than simple demographic information.


### **Assign scores based on engagement and fit**


Point values should reflect both fit (right company match) and behavior (showing intent). This creates a dynamic scoring system that distinguishes researchers from serious buyers.


**The system works best when you:**


-


Map scoring criteria to buyer trip stages (Awareness → Consideration → Decision)


-


**Set clear MQL and SQL thresholds**


-


Add negative scoring for disqualifying actions


Your historical conversion data will reveal which actions predict sales success.


### **Incorporate intent signals and buying triggers**


[Intent data](https://persana.ai/blogs/intent-data) raises your scoring by spotting prospects who actively research solutions like yours. The most valuable signals include:


-


Multiple visits to pricing pages


-


Downloads of buyer's guides or competitive comparisons


-


Webinar attendance with post-event participation


Purchase triggers like funding rounds, leadership changes, or rapid hiring in GTM roles signal readiness to buy. These events create ideal moments to reach prospects when their interest peaks.


Note that **Fit +**[Intent + Velocity = Confidence](https://www.unboundb2b.com/blog/how-to-assign-lead-scores/) **in conversion** . The model needs review every 90 days to stay accurate as market conditions change.
