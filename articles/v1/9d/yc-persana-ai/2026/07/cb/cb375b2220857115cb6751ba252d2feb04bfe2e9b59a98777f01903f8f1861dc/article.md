## **What is a Sales Cadence?**


Sales cadence is the backbone of modern sales outreach strategy. It's a predefined[sequence of sales activities](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/blog/sales-cadence) and touchpoints that salespeople use to contact prospects at set intervals. This approach turns random outreach into a strategic, repeatable process that helps teams achieve consistent results.


### **Sales cadence definition and purpose**


A sales cadence **creates a systematic sequence** where salespeople follow specific steps to interact with potential customers. These touchpoints include emails, phone calls, **social media messages, video conferences** , and direct mail. The process does more than just maintain persistence it gives sales teams a clear roadmap that saves time and eliminates guesswork with each prospect. A well-executed cadence helps teams standardize their approach and reduces training time for new team members.


### **Cadence meaning in business context**


The business world's understanding of "cadence" has evolved over time. While it originally meant rhythm in music or marching, business cadence now refers to[how often a regularly scheduled activity occurs](https://www.merriam-webster.com/wordplay/words-were-watching-cadence) . It represents the consistent rhythm of communication and activity within an organization. A structured rhythm helps break down silos, improves communication across teams, and boosts employee retention.


### **Difference between cadence and sales script**


Sales cadences and sales scripts work together but serve different purposes. The cadence determines the "when and how" timing, frequency, and **choice of communication channels** . A script, on the other hand, focuses on the "what to say" the specific content and messaging used during each interaction.


These **elements complement each other perfectly** . Your cadence creates the framework while your script delivers the message. A typical example shows an email scheduled for Day 2 followed by a call on Day 4. The script then provides the exact wording for that email or the perfect opening line for the call.

## **What are the top Sales Cadence Examples That Work?**


Sales professionals rely on proven cadence **structures to maximize their success** . Here are six sales cadence examples that showed consistent results in different scenarios.


### **Cold outbound cadence**


A 13-touch cold outbound cadence runs for 21-27 days and builds recognition through varied messaging. You should start with an introductory email. A phone call with voicemail follows on day 3. The next step adds a[LinkedIn message](https://persana.ai/blogs/how-to-automate-linkedin-prospecting) on day 5, with follow-up emails on days 7 and 10. The key is to alternate channels so prospects don't feel overwhelmed. This approach works best for **outbound prospecting and pipeline activation** .


### **Inbound lead follow-up cadence**


Inbound leads need 8-12 **touchpoints over 10-15 business days** to capitalize on their buying intent. The process starts with a welcome email that acknowledges their action. Make a phone call the next day. Share a case study or testimonial on day 4, then create a personalized video by day 6. The sequence ends with a follow-up email that invites them to schedule a meeting.


### **Enterprise sales cadence**


Enterprise sales need more time because they involve multiple stakeholders. The sequence begins with[personalized emails](https://persana.ai/blogs/email-personalization-tools) to the core team. **LinkedIn engagement with company influencers** comes next. Phone calls should reference previous communications. Multi-department emails highlight organization-wide benefits. The process wraps up with video content that shows specific use cases matching their needs.


### **SMB sales cadence**


Small businesses need structured yet flexible cadences. A proven **SMB sequence starts with LinkedIn profile** visits on day 1. A cold email addressing pain points goes out on day 2. Follow-up calls happen on day 4. Additional emails land on days 8 and 14. Final outreach wraps up between days 18-21. This approach helps smaller organizations sell systematically without overwhelming busy SMB leaders.


### **Content-driven cadence**


Content-driven cadences blend outreach with educational assets. The **strategy combines personalized emails** with article sharing on social media. Short video summaries and social engagement with prospects' content complete the mix. This educational approach helps you become a trusted advisor rather than just another seller.


### **Breakup email example**


Breakup emails help **close the loop with unresponsive prospects** . These messages generate a[33% response rate](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/the-power-of-breakup-emails-templates-to-close-the-loop) according to one sales team. The most effective breakup emails are personal and have clear subject lines. Seven-word subject lines work best with 46.2% open rates. Adding valuable resources either confirms the prospect's lack of interest or creates urgency to restart the conversation.

## **FAQ**


Let's explore some common questions about sales cadences and their broader applications in business settings.


### **What is the concept of cadence?**


Cadence primarily relates to rhythm found in music, marching, voice patterns, or natural sounds. Business contexts have adopted this concept beyond just audible rhythms. Sales teams use cadence as a[methodical strategy](https://persana.ai/blogs/sales-enablement-content) to **orchestrate business interactions** a practice verified through years of experience. Cadence brings essential structure that supports discipline and predictability within organizations.


### **What do cadences mean?**


Business cadences work like an organization's muscle memory. They create rhythmic sequences of routines, processes, and systems at different frequencies. Teams stay connected through regular business cadence touchpoints that help curb isolation in remote settings and keep members arranged. **Strong cadences eliminate** silos, enhance communication, and boost employee retention.


### **What is Salesforce cadence?**


Salesforce cadence creates a predefined series of sales actions for specific prospects. Cadence objects work together as a family to **build sequences and monitor targets** ' progress through steps. Sales teams can use emails, calls, SMS, LinkedIn activities, and trigger internal processes through flows.
