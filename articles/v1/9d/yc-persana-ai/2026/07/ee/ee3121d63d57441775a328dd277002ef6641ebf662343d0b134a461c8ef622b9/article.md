# Exciting News: Our Next Chapter

**Persana AI team joins Rox to accelerate the future of AI-native GTM automation**


Today we have an exciting announcement to share: **we're joining forces with Rox.**


From day one, Persana AI was built to power modern go-to-market teams with intelligence powered by the best data. Go-to-market teams relied on Persana AI to orchestrate how they find, prioritize, and drive revenue faster powered by AI agents.


Now, the Persana team is taking everything we’ve built and going even bigger with Rox.


By combining Rox’s platform with Persana’s agentic GTM intelligence, we’re accelerating a shared vision: **a fully autonomous, AI-native system for go-to-market.**


## **Building the agentic GTM future, together**


From our earliest conversations, the alignment between the Rox team and the Persana team was immediate. We had independently arrived at the same belief: the future of GTM isn’t more tools - it’s **intelligent systems that unify data, decision-making, and execution.**


The Persana team brings deep expertise in signal-driven workflows, enrichment, and AI-powered execution. Rox brings the platform, scale, and ambition to take this even further.


Together, we’re building toward a world where:


-


Systems understand buying intent in real time


-


Workflows execute automatically across the stack


-


GTM teams operate with unprecedented leverage


This is the next phase of AI in GTM and we’re incredibly excited to build it together.


## **What this means**


For our current customers, we will be sunsetting the Persana platform over the coming weeks and work closely with you for a smooth transition to the Rox platform.[Here are the detailed instructions for the transition](https://persana.ai/blogs/persana-ai-migration-guide) .


We’re committed to making this transition seamless and will be sharing more details, timelines, and migration support.
