## **Understand the Sales Process and Its Challenges**


Sales teams succeed when they **understand their sales process** and know where things usually go wrong.


### **What is the sales process?**


A sales process guides sales teams through repeatable steps that turn prospects into customers. Your team needs this roadmap to convert interest into real business. The journey starts with first contact and ends with closing deals and following up. Research proves that companies with well-laid-out **sales processes**[grow revenue faster](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbesbooksauthors/2025/11/11/why-sales-needs-as-much-attention-as-operations-in-manufacturing/) . They also close deals quicker and keep more customers.


Teams usually move through **stages like prospecting, preparation, approach, presentation, handling objections, closing, and follow-up. Each stage needs specific goals met before** moving forward. A clear structure helps new team members learn faster and lets experienced staff build better relationships instead of wondering what comes next.


### **Common bottlenecks in modern sales teams**


Sales teams face major roadblocks even with good processes. They use the rest on paperwork, planning calls (20%), and looking up information (3.6 hours daily).


Bigger buying committees create extra challenges. Enterprise deals now need 11-20 decision-makers who all want different things. This explains why **enterprise deals close 8-10 percentage points** less often than SMB deals.

Poor sales and marketing coordination (only 27.3% feel confident about their teamwork)

Teams become harder to coordinate as they grow larger

## **8 Expert Strategies to Improve Your Sales Process**


Let's get into eight expert strategies that will reshape your sales process in 2025.


### **1. Audit and eliminate non-selling tasks**


Your sales representatives spend just 28% of their time selling. Administrative tasks, follow-ups, and low-value activities take up the rest of their day. You should **identify and remove activities** that don't directly generate revenue. Your team needs to step away from manual data entry, too many internal meetings, and repetitive paperwork that keeps them from talking to customers.


### **2. Automate CRM and data entry workflows**


Your team can break free from tedious data entry by **automating contact profile** updates and task management. The right[CRM automation](https://persana.ai/blogs/workflow-automation-software) reduces the 70% of time reps waste on non-selling tasks. **Tools like Salesmate track communications** and add details to customer profiles automatically. This lets your team build relationships instead of keeping records.


### **3. Use AI tools for smarter selling**


Teams that use AI tools see 50% more leads. AI helps automate prospecting, makes conversations better, and speeds up decisions. Top performers use AI to sell faster and work better. Salesforce shows 33% quicker meeting preparation and 10% better win rates with AI.


### **4. Arrange sales and marketing content**


Businesses lose over $1 trillion yearly when **sales and marketing don't work together** . Marketing should create content that helps your sales process directly. Both teams need regular meetings to speak the same language and work toward shared revenue goals.


### **5. Optimize your sales tech stack**


A well-planned tech **stack makes operations smooth and cuts unnecessary costs** . Companies waste $21M on unused software licenses, and only 47% of licenses see active use. You should check your tools often to remove overlaps, make integrations better, and ensure each tool serves a clear purpose in your sales process.


### **6. Build trust with social proof**


Almost every customer (99.9%) reads reviews before buying. Your sales process should highlight customer testimonials, **case studies, and industry awards** . These trust signals make prospects feel safer, and 72% of consumers trust brands more after seeing positive reviews.


### **7. Use storytelling to connect with buyers**


People remember stories 22 times better than plain facts. Your sales team should learn to tell compelling stories about how your solution helped **other customers with similar problems** . Stories with relatable heroes, clear conflicts, and measurable results create emotional connections that lead to sales.


### **8. Conduct a sales process health check**


Regular checkups help you find **ways to grow and fix problems quickly** . Look at your pipeline's shape (it should look like a funnel with most opportunities at the top), size (usually 3-4x your sales goal), and speed (deals should move at a steady pace). These checks help you manage performance proactively instead of fixing problems after they happen.

## **Measure and Track Sales Productivity**


Sales process health becomes clear when you track results properly. Companies that perform well in sales are[53% more likely](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/unlocking-the-power-of-data-in-sales) to exploit analytics than their **low-performing competitors** . Your improvement efforts will lack direction without proper measurement.


### **Key metrics to monitor**


Picking the right metrics kicks off effective measurement. We focused on these vital sales KPIs:


-


**Pipeline metrics:** Pipeline-to-quota ratio shows how well reps fill their pipeline compared to quota needs. The[pipeline conversion ratio](https://persana.ai/blogs/sales-conversion-rate) shows how smoothly deals progress between stages.


-


**Efficiency indicators:** Sales cycle duration reveals the time needed to complete a sale, while opportunity-to-close time measures how long it takes from creation to closed-won deals. Quick lead response matters a lot, as studies show conversion rates drop by a lot if reps don't respond within five minutes.


-


**Value metrics:** Annual contract value (ACV) helps spot upselling chances, while customer lifetime value (CLV) shows relationship strength and loyalty that guides predictable revenue.


Your team's proactive approach becomes **clear by tracking emails sent** , daily calls, and overdue tasks. On top of that, content usage rates show how well reps employ your sales assets.
