# Persana AI Migration Guide

As announced in our blog post -[here](https://persana.ai/blogs/exciting-news-our-next-chapter) , we're joining forces with Rox and the entire team at Persana will be joining Rox. This guide covers everything you need to know about the platform transition — how to export your data, how to get started on Rox, and answers to common questions.


We're deeply grateful for your trust in Persana AI. Our goal is to make this transition as smooth as possible.


## Timeline


-


**April 1, 2026** — Announcement published. All billing suspended immediately. No further charges will be applied to any account.


-


**April 1 – May 1, 2026** — 30-day access period. Full platform access to export your data and transition your workflows.


-


**May 2, 2026** — Persana platform sunset. All remaining data permanently deleted. This action is irreversible.


## Billing


All active subscriptions have been canceled effective immediately. You will not be charged going forward. If you believe you were charged after April 1, please contact us at contact@persana.ai and we will resolve it promptly.


## How to Export Your Data from Persana


You have until May 1, 2026 to download any data you wish to retain. Here's how:


1.


**Log in** to your Persana AI account.


2.


**Navigate to your tables.** You'll see all your lead lists, enrichment tables, and saved workflows on your dashboard.


3.


**Download each table** by clicking the export/download icon next to the table you want to save. You can export your data as CSV files.


4.


**Save your files** locally and to cloud storage as a backup. We recommend downloading everything you might need — contacts, enrichment data, workflow results, and any reports.


5.


**Verify your exports** by opening the downloaded files to confirm all data is intact.


**Important:** After May 1, 2026, all data on the Persana platform will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Please make sure to export everything you need before then.


## Getting Started on Rox


Many of the core capabilities you used in Persana — signal-driven prospecting, data enrichment, and automated outreach — are now available on Rox.


To get started:


1.


**Export your tables from Persana** as CSV files. Login to your dashboard for your download link: https://www.platform.persana.ai/


2.


**Sign up for Rox** at http://rox.com.


3.


**Import your CSVs** into Rox People lists.


4.


**Enrich and activate** your contacts using Rox's prospecting and enrichment tools.


For full documentation on how Rox People works, visit:[https://docs.rox.com/development/product/people](https://docs.rox.com/development/product/people)

## Frequently Asked Questions


**Will I lose my data?** Not if you export it before May 1, 2026. After that date, all data on the Persana platform will be permanently deleted. We strongly recommend downloading everything you need as soon as possible.


**Will I be charged for anything?** No. All billing has been suspended effective immediately. If you notice any unexpected charges, contact us at contact@persana.ai.


**Do I have to switch to Rox?** No. You are under no obligation to sign up for Rox. You are free to export your data and use it with any platform you choose. That said, many of the features you used in Persana are now available on Rox, and the Rox team is offering 2x actions on self-serve plans to make the transition easy.


**How do I get my data into Rox?** Export your Persana tables as CSV files, then import them into Rox People lists. See the step-by-step instructions above.


**Who can I contact for help?** Reach out to contact@persana.ai for any questions about data export or the transition. For enterprise inquiries at Rox, contact support@rox.com.
