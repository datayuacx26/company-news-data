## **How to build a LinkedIn marketing strategy**


A successful **LinkedIn marketing strategy** needs careful planning and execution. Let's look at what makes it work.


### **Optimize your company's LinkedIn page**


Your LinkedIn page needs professional visuals and relevant information. Pages with complete details get 30% more weekly views. You should update your cover image at least twice yearly.


### **Set goals**


Your LinkedIn efforts need clear objectives. You might focus on brand awareness, lead generation, or employer branding. Each goal should connect to specific metrics that matter to your business.


### **Send messages to a prospect's LinkedIn inbox**


[LinkedIn Messaging](https://persana.ai/blogs/how-to-automate-linkedin-prospecting) gets a 300% higher **response rate than emails with similar content** . Your messages should be brief (under 500 characters) and tailored to each recipient's profile.


### **Create a content strategy**


The 4-1-1 rule works best: share one self-promotional post, one update from another source, and four pieces from others. Your content pillars should cover company updates, industry news, and authority pieces.


### **Stay active**


You need just 5-10 minutes each day for **LinkedIn engagement** . The data shows that[companies posting weekly see twice the engagement](https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/linkedin-pages/5-non-obvious-ways-to-improve-your-linkedin-company-page) versus less frequent posters.


### **Post relevant content**


Your audience wants valuable, **non-promotional content** that meets their needs. Persana.ai experts point out that visual content performs better - carousels (40%) and short videos (25%) get more engagement.


### **Make use of LinkedIn ads**


You can utilize **LinkedIn's precise targeting features** to connect with decision-makers. Your ad formats should match your business goals.


### **Work with subject matter experts**


Talk to your internal experts instead of asking them to write content. Their insights can become multiple content pieces that help **build your brand's authority** .
