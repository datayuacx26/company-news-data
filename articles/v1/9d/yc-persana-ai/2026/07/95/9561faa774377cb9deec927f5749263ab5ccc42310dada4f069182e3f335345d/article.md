# Persana Funding Filter is Here

If you’re doing outbound—whether you run a lead gen agency or you’re part of a **sales team** —there’s one kind of company that always stands out: **recently funded ones** .


Think about it. A company that’s just raised a round of funding is usually in growth mode. That often means they’re hiring, building new systems, investing in tools, and open to trying new solutions to scale faster. In other words, they have **both the money and the motivation** to buy.


Compared to **bootstrapped or stagnant companies** , funded companies are way more likely to take a meeting, **explore new software** , or **outsource services** . But here’s the catch: you can’t just guess who’s been funded. You need real, accurate data to act on.


## **Why clean funding data matters more than ever**


There’s a lot of noise out there. You might stumble across a random “ **Top 50 Startups** ” list or dig through **outdated directories** —but those aren’t reliable. Targeting companies based on old or unverifiable data wastes time and **burns your outreach efforts** .


What you really need is **up-to-date, trustworthy funding data** , ideally combined with other company insights like team size, tech stack, and industry.


That’s where **Persana** comes in.

1.


**Log into your Persana dashboard.**


2.


**Click on “Find Companies”** to start building a lead list.


3.


Scroll down to the **Company Details** dropdown in the filter menu.
