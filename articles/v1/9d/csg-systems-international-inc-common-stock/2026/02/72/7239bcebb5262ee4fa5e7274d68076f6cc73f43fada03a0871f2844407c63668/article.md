### CSG Systems International Announces Its 13th Consecutive Year of Increased Dividend Payout


February 4, 2026


*Board of Directors approves a 6% increase in its quarterly dividend; company approves Q1 2026 dividend*


DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[CSG®](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.csgi.com%2F%3Futm_source%3DPress%2BRelease%26utm_medium%3DPR&esheet=54410518&newsitemid=20260204108740&lan=en-US&anchor=CSG%26%23174%3B&index=1&md5=20bd69202e02fb13d73e70e2a96753e7) (NASDAQ: CSGS) today announced that its Board of Directors approved a 6% increase in the Company’s quarterly cash dividend payment. The new quarterly payment amount has been increased to $0.34 per share of common stock to be paid on April 1, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on March 18, 2026.
