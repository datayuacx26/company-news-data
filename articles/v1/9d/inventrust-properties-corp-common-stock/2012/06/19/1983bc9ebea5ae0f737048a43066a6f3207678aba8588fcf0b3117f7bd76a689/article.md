# Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc. to Present at NAREIT’s REITWeek 2012 Investor Conference


Company Release - 06/08/2012


Download the PDF version PDF Format (opens in new window)


OAK BROOK, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc. today announced that executives from its senior business management team Inland American Business Manager & Advisor, Inc., will be speaking at REITWeek 2012®: NAREIT's Investor Forum. The presentation is scheduled for 4:30 p.m. ET on Tuesday, June 12.


Approximately 2,000 institutional investors, investment banking professionals and other REIT management teams will be attending the conference. The Inland American executives will provide insight into the REIT’s business structure, latest company developments and diverse portfolio of nationwide commercial real estate including retail, multi-family, industrial, lodging, office and student housing properties.


REITWeek 2012: NAREIT's Investor Forum will take place June 12-14 at the Hilton New York located at 1335 Avenue of the Americas, New York City.


**About Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc.**


Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc. focuses on acquiring and developing a diversified portfolio of commercial real estate including retail, multi-family, industrial, lodging, office and student housing properties, located in the United States and Canada. The company also invests in joint ventures, development projects, real estate loans and marketable securities. As of March 31, 2012 Inland American owned, directly or indirectly through joint ventures in which it has a controlling interest, 970 properties, representing approximately 49 million square feet of retail, industrial and office properties, 9,563 multi-family units and 17,899 lodging rooms. Inland American is one of five REITs that are, or have been, sponsored by affiliates of The Inland Real Estate Group of Companies, Inc.
