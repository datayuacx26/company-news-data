# Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc. to Host Second Quarter 2012 Earnings Webcast


Company Release - 08/14/2012


Download the PDF version PDF Format (opens in new window)


OAK BROOK, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc. (“Inland American”) will host an earnings webcast to discuss its second-quarter and year-to-date 2012 results as well as portfolio strategies for the second half of the year on Thursday, August 23, 2012 at 10 a.m. Central time. Speakers will include Brenda Gujral, president of Inland American, Jack Potts, principal financial officer of Inland American, Tom McGuinness, president of Inland American Business Manager & Advisor, and Tom Lithgow, president of Inland American HOLDCO.


The live webcast and presentation will be available on Inland American’s website at[www.inlandamerican.com](http://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.inlandamerican.com&esheet=50377301&lan=en-US&anchor=www.inlandamerican.com&index=1&md5=8dab7ec42c7b82ef6c0a0457cd0885a5) . Listeners should log on approximately 10 minutes in advance to ensure proper setup to receive the webcast. A replay will be available on the Inland American website following the webcast.


**About Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc.**


Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc. focuses on acquiring and developing a diversified portfolio of commercial real estate located in the United States. The company also invests in joint ventures, development projects, real estate loans and marketable securities. As of June 30, 2012 Inland American owned, directly or indirectly through joint ventures in which it has a controlling interest, 938 properties, representing approximately 48 million square feet of retail, industrial and office properties, 9,563 multi-family units and 17,899 lodging rooms. Inland American is one of five REITs that are, or have been, sponsored by affiliates of The Inland Real Estate Group of Companies, Inc. For further information regarding Inland American, please refer to the company website at[www.inlandamerican.com](http://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.inlandamerican.com&esheet=50377301&lan=en-US&anchor=www.inlandamerican.com&index=2&md5=04afecaf0bc5e5f7d81e4c3d01a12810) .
