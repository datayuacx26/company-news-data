## Kevin’s simple at-home air conditioner maintenance tips


A little routine care can go a long way. Here are a few simple steps Kevin recommends:


- **Change your air filter regularly**
Swap out filters every 30–60 days, especially during heavy-use months. A clean filter keeps air moving and reduces strain on your system.
- **Keep vents open and clear**
Closing vents or blocking them with furniture can disrupt airflow and make your system work harder than it should.
- **Check your return vents**
Make sure nothing is covering return vents — restricted airflow can impact performance across your entire system.
- **Give your outdoor unit some space**
Leaves, dirt and debris can build up around the condenser. Keeping it clear helps your A/C run more efficiently.


Even small actions like these can help improve performance and reduce wear and tear on your air conditioner.
