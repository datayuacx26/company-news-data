# **Queens Study Hall: Free CDL Permit Prep in Long Island City**


On Tuesday, August 4, students filled a room at the Ravenswood Community Learning Center in Long Island City from 3:30 to 5:00pm for a CDL Study Hall. Pizza on the table, practice questions on the screen, and a group of people working through the same commercial learner's permit exam at the same time.


This is a recurring Tuesday afternoon slot in Queens, and it runs on purpose. Studying for the CLP alone off a phone is where most people stall out. Studying in a room with an instructor and five other people who are also two weeks out from their exam is a different experience. Orlando Garcia, one of our NYC ambassadors, helped bring people in.


## **What students said**


Every student who filled out a feedback card rated the session 5 out of 5 and said they would recommend it, scoring it a 10 out of 10. The value they named was not the lecture. It was the room.


"It's good to get other people's input," Jalal wrote. Gabriel pointed to "the amount of information given to pass the test." Ginnette, who is early in her studying, said the material "is valuable when you're just starting to learn."


## **Where the room stood on the permit exam**


Study Halls exist to move one number: how many students actually sit for the[CLP written exam](https://www.emergecareer.com/blog/what-is-the-commercial-learners-permit-exam-aka-cdl-written-exam) . Two students walked out with dates on the calendar, one of them testing the very next morning. One more said he could be ready inside a week, another inside two to three weeks. The rest are mid-study and need another few weeks, which is normal and fine.


The only obstacle anyone named was work. "My job timing interferes," Bryan wrote. That is the most common thing we hear, which is why the coursework is self-paced and why this Study Hall repeats every week instead of being a one-time event.
