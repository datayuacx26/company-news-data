# How to Schedule Your CLP Exam (CDL Written Test)


If you’re taking the first step toward a rewarding career in commercial driving, scheduling your Commercial Learner’s Permit (CLP) exam is an exciting milestone. This test, also known as the CDL written test, is your gateway to hitting the road with confidence. Let’s guide you through the process so you can schedule your exam with ease.


## **General Steps to Schedule Your CLP Exam**


1. **Visit Your State’s DMV or RMV Website** : Start by going to your state’s Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) or Registry of Motor Vehicles (RMV) website. These sites are your go-to resource for scheduling and preparing for the CLP exam.
2. **Find the Scheduling Section** : Most DMV or RMV websites provide a dedicated section for scheduling your CDL-related appointments. Look for links that specifically mention "CLP Exam" or "CDL Written Test."
3. **Follow Instructions on Required Documents** : Ensure you have all the necessary paperwork. These usually include:some text


- Proof of identity and residency.
- Your Social Security number.
- Valid Driver’s license
- DOT Physical
- Any other state-specific requirements.


4. **Prepare for the Exam** :some text


- Download or request the CDL Manual from your state’s DMV website.
- Study key sections relevant to the written test.


5. **Check Fees and Payment Options** : Each state sets its own fees for the CLP exam. Check the cost and confirm acceptable payment methods (e.g., credit card, check, or cash).


## **Helpful Resources**


Your state’s DMV website will often provide:


- Tutorials or videos on how to schedule your exam.
- Downloadable resources like the CDL manual.
- A detailed breakdown of fees and test requirements.
