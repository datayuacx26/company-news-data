# Emerge Career Partners With Imran Inc. to Put CDL Graduates on the Road

Three of our CDL graduates are out on the road with Imran Inc. right now, hauling freight across state lines and doing exactly what they trained to do.


That's the result of our new official partnership with[Imran Inc.](https://imraninc.com/) , and we couldn't be more fired up to announce it.


## Here's how this happened


At Emerge, we train people, get them their Class A CDL, and then work overtime to make sure they land somewhere real. Not just *a* job, but the *right* job with a company that's going to treat them like human beings and give them a genuine career.


Imran Inc. is that company. They're a driver-focused trucking company committed to providing top-tier opportunities for drivers, offering reliable and efficient logistics solutions across multiple industries. They're an active interstate freight carrier based out of Lawrence, Kansas, and they run lanes across the entire country.


They've built a culture around their drivers: a driver-first company where you're more than just a number, treated with respect, appreciation, and support every mile of the way. That's not marketing fluff. We've seen it firsthand, because they've already hired three of our graduates.


Three people who came through our program, earned their Class A license, and are now behind the wheel of an Imran truck, crossing state lines and moving freight.


That's not a pilot. That's not a "let's try one and see." That's a real employer who looked at what we're building and said *yes, send us your people.*


And that's exactly why we formalized this partnership.


## What this means for our graduates


Imran Inc. is built for drivers who are ready to work. The model includes 4 weeks OTR and 4 days home time, so our graduates know exactly what they're signing up for. They're not guessing.


Imran's team is available 24/7 to support drivers with anything they need, whether it's a quick question or urgent help on the road. For someone just starting their driving career, that kind of support is everything.


This is a real pathway from our classroom to a moving truck for people who just leveled up their lives with a Class A license.


## What this means for the industry


We hear it constantly: *there aren't enough drivers.* The industry needs more CDL holders on the road, full stop.


What makes Imran stand out is their model. They host their own training to confirm that recent CDL graduates are ready to hit the road before sending them out, then back them with support every mile after that. It's an incredible approach for carriers that want to augment their workforce with new drivers while keeping safety and readiness front and center.


More fleets should be looking at it.
