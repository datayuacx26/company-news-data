# Investor Watch

We recently surveyed investors and business owners in the US to better understand their views ahead of the US presidential election. While both groups are feeling more optimistic than at the height of the pandemic four years ago, concerns about the economy remain. And they differ on which candidate they feel is best equipped to handle it.

##


Investors favor Harris


57% of investors favor Harris.


43% of investors favor Trump.


##


Business owners favor Trump


47% of business owners favor Harris.


53% of business owners favor Trump.


##


The economy is the top election issue


84% of investors say that the economy is their top election issue.


83% of business owners say that the economy is their top election issue.


##


Many investors are considering portfolio changes


### 77%


“I am looking to make changes to my portfolio before the election.”


##


Business owners want help navigating the election


### 82%


“Having expert financial guidance is especially important for me in an election year.”
