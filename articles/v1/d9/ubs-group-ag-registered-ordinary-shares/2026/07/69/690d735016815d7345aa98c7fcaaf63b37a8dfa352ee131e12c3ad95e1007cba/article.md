# CIO market analysis Visit page

Our research helps you invest better. Established in 2011, the Chief Investment Office has been a key part of UBS GWM’s aim to bring institutional quality advice to our private wealth clients. CIO identifies and communicates investment opportunities, as well as market risks to support you in preserving and growing your wealth. Stay up to date with in-depth reports and market commentary.
