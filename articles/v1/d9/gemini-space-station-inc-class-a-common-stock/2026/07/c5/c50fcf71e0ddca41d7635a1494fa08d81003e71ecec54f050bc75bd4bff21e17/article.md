- **(Sports) France leads Argentina as World Cup favorite:** France entered the 2026 World Cup as one of the clear favorites, and their loaded squad has done nothing to dispel that as they face off with Sweden on Tuesday in the Round of 32. - **(Politics) Becerra becomes heavy favorite to be next California governor:** Democrat Xavier Becerra has emerged as the clear frontrunner to become the next governor of California, with traders giving him a 93% chance of winning while Republican Steve Hilton sits a distant second (less than 15%) as of Monday afternoon. Becerra is the state’s former attorney general and served as health secretary under former president Joe Biden. Hilton served as a political aide to UK prime minister David Cameron before moving to California in 2012.
