# Introducing Gemini's Wimbledon Experience: A New Way to Trade The Championships

Wimbledon is the oldest tennis tournament in the world, and arguably the most watched. For two weeks every summer, the sport's biggest names compete on the most iconic courts in the game. Every match carries real stakes. That’s why we built the Wimbledon experience on Gemini Predictions to put you right in the middle of it.


### The Market for Every Match


From the first round through to the finals, we're listing match winner markets for both the men's and women's draws. That means you can trade Carlos Alcaraz's path through the bracket, back Iga Świątek to take the women's title, or find value anywhere the odds don't quite reflect what you're seeing on court. Every match. Both draws. The full tournament.


This is what prediction markets are built for. We are dedicated to becoming the go-to predictions market for major sports moments where information moves fast, outcomes are uncertain, and a sharper read on the field translates directly into an edge.


### A Curated Experience, Built for the Tournament


The Wimbledon interface isn't just a list of markets. It's a purpose-built trading experience designed around how the tournament actually unfolds. It’s organized by round, updated as results come in, and focused on the matches that matter most. We took the strongest elements of our French Open and World Cup launches and refined them into something that fits Wimbledon's fortnight format and global scale.


Whether you're a Gemini trader who followed the French Open markets closely or someone coming to Predictions for the first time, the experience is built to make it easy to find your market, understand your position, and trade with confidence.


### Trade Multiple Outcomes, One Position


We're also launching pre-packaged combo contracts for Wimbledon. These are single tradeable positions that bundle multiple match winner markets into one. If every leg wins, you win. It's a way to express a higher-conviction view across the draw without placing and managing separate trades.


We're starting with two combos built around one of the tournament's biggest storylines: Serena Williams' return to Wimbledon.


**Former Champions Combo:** Back three past champions to all advance through the first round — Serena Williams, Iga Świątek, and Elena Rybakina. If all three win their opening matches, the combo settles YES.


**American Women Combo:** Trade a US-centric slate. Serena Williams, Coco Gauff, and Jessica Pegula all to win R1. If any one of them falls in the first round, the combo settles NO immediately.


Both combos are live and tradeable now on Gemini Predictions.
