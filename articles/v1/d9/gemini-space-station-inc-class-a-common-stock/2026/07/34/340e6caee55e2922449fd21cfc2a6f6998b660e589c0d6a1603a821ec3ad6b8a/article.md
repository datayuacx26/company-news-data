# Gemini Launches $1.5M Takers Rewards for Predictions Traders

Running from July 1 to September 30, 2026, traders can earn rewards based on daily trading activity and a monthly bonus based on monthly volume.


Here’s how it works:


### Daily Reward


The monthly reward is paid daily in USD. The reward rate scales with a participant's trailing 30-day taker volume, updated daily. Daily Rewards begin once a participant has completed 15 Active Trading Days during the Program Term.


- Takers with between $500K – $1,999,999 in trailing 30-day volume receive 0.5% back in USD
- Takers with between $2M – $9,999,999 in trailing 30-day volume receive 1.0% back in USD
- Takers with $10M+ in trailing 30-day volume receive 1.5% back in USD


#### Monthly Reward


The monthly reward is paid in USD within two weeks following the end of each calendar month. Monthly Bonuses are evaluated on calendar-month taker volume. These run from July through the end of September.


- $500K in monthly volume earns a $2,000 reward
- $1M in monthly volume earns a $5,000
- $2M in monthly volume earns a $15,000 reward
- $5M in monthly volume earns a $30,000 reward
- $10M in monthly volume earns a $50,000 reward


### Liquidity Rewards Program


The new suite of taker rewards programs complements Gemini’s existing liquidity programs for market makers. These allow market makers to earn daily USD rewards by providing high-quality resting liquidity on Gemini Predictions While the Maker Rebate rewards completed fills, Liquidity Rewards pay makers daily for the quality of their resting orders, even when those orders don't fill. The program is designed to deepen books, tighten spreads, and bootstrap activity on selected events.
