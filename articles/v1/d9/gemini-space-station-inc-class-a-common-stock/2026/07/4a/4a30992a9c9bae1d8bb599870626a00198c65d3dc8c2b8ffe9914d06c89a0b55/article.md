# Gemini Provides Custody for Landmark VanEck Solana ETF

The crypto ETF ecosystem has surged in size and scope in 2025. VanEck added to the excitement last month when it launched the VanEck Solana ETF (VSOL), the third SOL ETF in the US, which includes Solana staking rewards.


Gemini is proud to serve as the custodian for VSOL, building on an existing relationship with VanEck that also includes providing custody services for the VanEck Bitcoin Trust (HODL) and the VanEck Ethereum ETF (ETHV).


### VSOL Offers Solana Spot Price Tracking With a Twist


As part of the launch, VanEck has waived VSOL’s sponsor fee for the first $1 billion in assets or until February 17, 2026, whichever happens first. VSOL’s third-party staking provider has also waived its sponsor fee during the same timeframe. After that date or threshold is reached, the fee will hit .30%.


VSOL is one of the first spot ETFs to offer staking rewards, a process that gives validators a financial incentive to help secure the network.


“Solana has quickly emerged as a leading proof-of-stake network, offering speed, scalability, and efficiency that continue to attract developers and real-world use cases,” said Kyle DaCruz, VanEck’s director of digital assets product. “We’re excited to be launching VSOL and to build on VanEck’s long history of expanding access to digital assets through thoughtful, investor-focused products.”


### Gemini Provides Custody With Focus on Compliance


Gemini offers custody, clearing/settlement, trading, and other capital markets services for crypto ETFs.


Gemini has completed SOC 1 Type 2 and SOC 2 Type 2 examinations, certified by Deloitte, so we meet the highest safety and compliance standards.


Gemini segregates assets for sole benefit of the fund owners, fully backed 1:1, and separated from other users’ assets. Gemini is fully committed to maintaining the highest regulatory standards as the industry continues to grow.


Supporting the VanEck Solana ETF as its custodian is another vital step on our mission to unlock the next era of financial, creative, and personal freedom.
