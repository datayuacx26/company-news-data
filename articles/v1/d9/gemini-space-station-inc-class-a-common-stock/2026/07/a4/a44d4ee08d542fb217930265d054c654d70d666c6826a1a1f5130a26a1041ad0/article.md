### Predictions Market Pulse


- **(Politics) Clarity Act odds rise:** The odds of Congress passing the CLARITY Act before the end of the year jumped to 57% on Tuesday morning after the White House reportedly agreed to an ethics provision that had held up negotiations. While the full text of the new bill has not been released, lawmakers now could potentially bring the bill to the Senate floor for a vote by the end of the week, with bitcoin rallying past $66,000 Tuesday morning on hopes of a deal. Lawmakers have until the August recess to push the legislation to a floor vote, and it will need to pass the House of Representatives with a simple majority vote before President Donald Trump can sign it into law.
- **(Crypto) Strong ETF inflows bolster ether rally:** The price of ethereum jumped past $1,900 on Monday and continued to rally Tuesday, continuing its move higher as ethereum spot ETFs recorded $105 million in net inflows. The price of ethereum has now increased more than 11% over the past month, surpassing bitcoin’s roughly 3.5% gain as institutional investors showed some renewed interest in altcoins. As of Tuesday afternoon, Gemini Predictions traders had priced in a 29% chance the price of ether would surpass $2,000 by the end of the month.

### Contract Spotlight


**Who will win the Ballon d’Or this year?**


When Spain defeated Argentina 1-0 in the World Cup final over the weekend, it marked the culmination of a 39-day soccer extravaganza that grabbed the sporting world’s attention. But there’s a major individual award that officials have yet to announce: the Ballon d’Or.


The Ballon d’Or is given out annually to the best soccer player in the world across international and club play, with an international collection of soccer journalists voting on the prize. This year’s award will be announced on October 26 in London.


With a little more than three months before the announcement, traders have tabbed England’s Harry Kane to win the prize (45%), followed by Spain’s Lamine Yamal (12%), and France’s Kylian Mbappé (11%).


Kane is coming off a dominant season at Bayern Munich in the Bundesliga league, with 36 goals in 31 games, before leading England to the World Cup semifinals. Yamal had 16 goals and 11 assists in 28 games for FC Barcelona in Spain’s La Liga, and Mbappé was a dominant presence for Real Madrid before scoring 10 goals in the World Cup.
