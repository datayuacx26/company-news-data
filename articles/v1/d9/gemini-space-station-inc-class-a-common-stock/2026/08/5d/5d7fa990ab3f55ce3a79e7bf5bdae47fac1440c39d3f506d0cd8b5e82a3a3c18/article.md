### 📈 Crypto Markets Overview


**SEC to unveil new crypto guidelines:** The new guidelines could reportedly include an innovation exemption that will allow financial institutions to offer 24/7 trading of tokenized securities and a four-year registration exemption for crypto startups. Additionally, the new guidelines could reportedly loosen fundraising reporting requirements and create a framework for some investment contracts for digital assets. The rules are meant to pave the way for TradFi institutions to push further into the tokenization arena.


**Strategy CEO pledges to buy bitcoin again before year-end:** The news comes after Strategy pivoted into a buy-and-sell strategy earlier this summer, rocking the bitcoin market after aggressively building its bitcoin treasury. Le said the company will resume buying after it bolsters its US dollar reserve so it has enough liquidity to continue servicing the 12% bi-monthly dividend for STRC, the company’s preferred stock. Strategy currently owns 840,447 BTC at an average price of $75,385.


**Inflation drops to 3.4% but bitcoin doesn’t budge:** Core CPI, which excludes volatile food and energy prices, jumped 2.5% from the same period last year. The tame readings helped push TradFi markets higher and it lessened the chances of a rate hike later this year, but bitcoin remained subdued as demand for digital assets continued to remain muted even as sellers tired.
