# Welcome To ActiveTrader 2.0

Gemini has launched a revamped ActiveTrader interface built around the simple idea that high-volume traders deserve the fastest, most intuitive features in circulation, bringing them a true super app experience


ActiveTrader 2.0 includes new, interactive charts, a new order flow, and a whole new way to trade. Here's what will be different the next time traders log in to their Gemini account and choose the ActiveTrader experience.


### A Trading Interface That Moves With You


ActiveTrader now gives you a completely flexible way to trade, whether it’s issuing orders directly from the chart, the order book, or from the order form. This trading experience is built to fit your style, giving you the best chance to thrive in any market environment.


- **Trade from the chart:** Customers can now simply tap to place a limit order and drag to adjust the price. Click the chart for live prices, or tap any candle to see the full breakdown for that moment in time.
- **See everything, right where you're looking:** Open positions and orders now live across the UI. For the first time, traders won’t be required to bounce between screens to check where they stand.
- **A clean order book:** Cumulative depth and mid-price are visible at a glance. Tap a price level and it drops straight into your order form.
- **Trade on margin with full visibility:** Positions and borrowing costs are now available to view all in one place. No more switching between tabs to see where a position stands.


### Why We Rebuilt It


The crypto market can be volatile. That means any trading experience must be designed so crypto traders can see everything in one place, assess conditions, and move quickly.


We wanted to make ActiveTrader 2.0 a fully native experience. That means prices update live with no lag and no stutter, with every interaction feeling smooth.


For high-volume traders, every second is valuable. That’s why ActiveTrader 2.0 brings the ability to pick your account right at the top, flexible order flow, and more ways to see what's tradeable.


### What's Next


Gemini engineers are shipping new product updates across business lines every week. And they continue to keep upgrading ActiveTrader in the immediate future. Be on the lookout for more improvements, including adding a full P&L layout so traders can view their performance in real-time.


### Get It Now


ActiveTrader 2.0 is now available for all Gemini customers. Update to version 26.722.0 in the app stores to check it out.
