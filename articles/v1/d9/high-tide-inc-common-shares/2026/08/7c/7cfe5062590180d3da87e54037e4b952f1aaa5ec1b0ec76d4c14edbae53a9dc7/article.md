# High Tide Projects Record Growth for Q3 2026, Led by Canadian and German Operations

This week, High Tide announced it expects revenue of $195.0 million to $200.0 million for the quarter ended July 31, 2026, gross profit of $51.0 million to $53.5 million, and Adjusted EBITDA of $15.2 million to $16.5 million. Each would mark a company record, representing year over year growth of at least 30%, 27% and 43% respectively. Notably, the low end of the guidance range exceeds the highest current analyst estimate across all three metrics, per FactSet as at August 3, 2026.
