# Cypher Has Been Acquired by Nium

Today, we’re announcing one of the biggest milestones in Cypher’s journey.


Over the past four years, Cypher has helped thousands of people around the world use digital assets for everyday payments. Today, that journey enters an exciting new chapter.


Cypher is getting acquired by[Nium](https://www.nium.com/) , the global leader in cross-border payments infrastructure to expand its fiat-to-onchain capabilities and build a new Cypher experience powered by Nium’s global payments platform.


### Why Nium?


At Cypher, we’ve always believed that the future of payments lies at the intersection of traditional finance and blockchain technology. Nium shares that vision.


As the global leader in cross-border payments infrastructure, Nium enables banks, fintechs, and enterprises to move money across 190+ countries and 100+ currencies through a single global platform. With more than 41 million cards issued annually, and strategic partnerships with industry leaders such as Circle and Coinbase, Nium is building the infrastructure that connects stablecoins with real-world payments at a global scale.


Joining Nium allows us to combine Cypher’s expertise in digital assets with one of the world’s most advanced payments networks, creating the foundation for a faster, more scalable, and globally connected customer experience.


### What this means for you


As part of this transition, we’ll begin the gradual wind-down of the current Cypher platform, including the consumer app, business card platform, and the CYPR ecosystem. It will remain available until September 6, 2026


.


## Here’s what you need to know


- Your funds are completely safe and can be withdrawn at any time before September 6, 2026


, with no fees. ([View withdrawal steps](https://cypherhq.io/wind-down-support/how-do-i-withdraw-my-card-balance) )
- Card loading has been disabled
- You can continue using your Cypher Card until August 7, 2026


.
- All earned rewards must be claimed before September 6, 2026


- Your card remains usable until August 7, 2026


, but your account and any remaining card balance stay accessible for withdrawal until September 6, 2026


. If you still have a card balance after Aug 7


, please withdraw it before the platform winds down.
- Our support team will remain available throughout the wind-down period to assist you


### The CYPR Protocol Being Discontinued.


This was not a decision we took lightly. Together with Nium, our legal and regulatory advisors, and all relevant stakeholders, we carefully explored every possible path for continuing the CYPR protocol after the acquisition. After extensive evaluation, it became clear that maintaining the existing protocol wasn’t feasible.


Rather than create uncertainty or make promises we couldn’t keep, we’ve chosen to be transparent about that decision from the outset and focus on delivering a smooth, well-supported transition for every member of our community.


## Looking Ahead

## Here are the key dates you should be aware of throughout the transition.


## Timeline


**July 8, 2026**


- Card loading is disabled
- New card applications and card issuance end


**July 16, 2026**


- Spend rewards earned between July 2–16 are distributed
- This is the final rewards cycle — no further reward distributions


**August 7, 2026**


- Final day to use your Cypher Card for purchases
- All active cards are cancelled
- If you have a remaining card balance, you won’t be able to spend it after this date, you’ll need to withdraw it before Sep 6


(see withdrawal steps above)


**September 6, 2026**


- The current Cypher mobile app, dApp, and business platform will no longer be available.
- The withdrawal window and rewards claims will close
- The CYPR protocol will be permanently wound down, and the CYPR token ecosystem will come to an end.


## Before the shutdown date, please remember to:


- Back up your wallet recovery phrase or private keys
- Download your transaction history for your records
- Withdraw any remaining funds.
- Claim any outstanding rewards
- Avoid sending funds to Cypher deposit addresses
