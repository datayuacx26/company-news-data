## Key Features of Cypher Agent Pay


### One-Time Use Virtual Cards


Every transaction gets its own brand new virtual card number. The moment a payment goes through, that card is destroyed. Even if your agent lands on a malicious site, there is nothing left to steal. This is one of the most secure ways for an AI agent to make payments online.


For agentic AI systems operating at scale, one-time virtual cards are not just a nice-to-have, they are essential infrastructure.


### Hard Spending Limits and Guardrails


Your agent never touches your main wallet. You create a virtual card, set a hard spending limit, and that is the only funds your agent can access. The moment it hits that limit, it stops. Full control stays with you, always.


### Works with Any AI Agent Framework


Cypher Agent Pay is built on open standards. It works with Claude, OpenAI, and any agent framework that supports skills. If your workflow supports skills, it can support Cypher. No custom integrations required.


### Accepted Anywhere VISA Is Online


Your agents can spend on any online platform that accepts VISA globally. No geographic restrictions or platform limitations.


### Fund with Any FIAT Currency


You can top up the Cypher agent card with any FIAT currency and get your agents spending in minutes.
