## What Actually Changes When You Switch


The biggest practical difference founders notice is pricing structure. Pulley’s plans scale with stakeholder count and complexity, so the bill tends to grow alongside your cap table. Mantle’s plans stay flat regardless of stakeholder count, which matters most once you’re past the earliest stage and adding employees, advisors, and investors regularly.


### How easy is migrating from Pulley to Mantle?


Easier than most founders expect, on either path.


On Mantle’s free plan,[Clerk](https://withmantle.com/equity/mantle-clerk?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=pulley-alternative) (Mantle’s AI assistant) reads your source documents directly and builds the cap table around them, so you’re not manually matching CSV columns to vesting-schedule codes.


On a paid plan, concierge onboarding goes further:[one Mantle customer](https://withmantle.com/case-studies/blazel?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=pulley-alternative) described the concierge onboarding process as dropping their agreements into an upload link and letting Mantle’s team build the entire cap table from there (a sharp contrast to the manual CSV imports they’d dreaded with previous tools).
