# Mantle Partners with Dream Payments to Bring Payment Coordination to Private Markets

**Toronto, ON — August 11, 2026** **—**[Mantle](https://withmantle.com/) today announced the launch of payment coordination within[Mantle Portal](https://withmantle.com/portal) , powered by[Dream Payments’](https://www.dreampayments.com/) embedded payments infrastructure. The launch marks the moment private markets infrastructure stops being a collection of tools and becomes a single operating system, one that now spans data, documents, reporting, relationships, and the movement of capital itself, all inside one platform.


For institutional allocators, this changes what a capital call actually is. Instead of reviewing a call in one system, approving it over email, and executing the wire in a bank portal, an LP now moves from decision to execution without ever leaving the platform that tracks the investment in the first place.


Mantle Portal was built to bring private markets into real time. Where the industry has long operated on static, backward-looking updates, PDFs and emails reconciled by hand, Mantle Portal was built to replace that with live data, real-time tracking, and intelligence that runs continuously across a portfolio rather than resetting with every quarterly report. It serves family offices, endowments, pensions, fund-of-funds, and other institutional allocators managing capital across dozens or thousands of fund commitments, giving them a single system of record for their entire private markets portfolio, from raw fund documents to the analytics and decisions built on top of them.


Capital calls, approvals, and payment instructions have been stuck in three disconnected systems: a document platform for reviewing the call, an email inbox for approvals, and a bank’s online portal for executing the wire. That fragmentation ends today. With payment coordination built into Mantle Portal, every step, from authorization through completed execution, happens in one place, with Dream Payments’ rails carrying out execution while Mantle orchestrates the workflow that triggers each payment.


“Institutional LPs sit at the center of private markets, yet the infrastructure they rely on to coordinate capital has remained fragmented for decades,” said[Amar Varma](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amar-varma-8041b9/) , co-founder and CEO of Mantle. “With Mantle Portal, that’s no longer the case. Allocators no longer have to stitch together documents, approvals, and payment instructions across disconnected tools. They can orchestrate the movement of capital from the exact same place they already track their investments.”


“Financial workflows are increasingly moving directly into the software platforms where investment activity already happens,” said[Brent Ho-Young](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brenthoyoung/?lipi=urn%3Ali%3Apage%3Ad_flagship3_search_srp_people%3BBTiWF20BSzGIMFgV4yWyHg%3D%3D) , CEO of Dream Payments. “Our infrastructure enables platforms like Mantle to coordinate financial execution natively into the user experience, allowing money to move seamlessly within the same system tracking the underlying investment data.”


This launch is another piece of a transformation Mantle Portal set out to build: replacing the entire private markets technology stack with one system. That stack has always demanded a different vendor for each layer: a base-layer accounting ledger, a separate system for document ingestion and data extraction, another for analytics and reporting, and a CRM system managed apart from all of it, reconciled manually across exports, imports, and spreadsheets.


Mantle’s core capabilities in document ingestion, data extraction, analytics, and reporting have turned capital calls, capital statements, and fund documents into structured, queryable data since day one. From there, the platform extended access into the ledger layer and into CRM functionality through the same unified system. With payment coordination now added, Mantle spans the full spectrum, ledger, data, reporting, CRM, and payments, as one platform, one data model, with AI inference running natively across the entire system rather than summarizing it from the outside.


Payment coordination will be available to Mantle Portal clients.
