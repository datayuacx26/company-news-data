# Introducing the Aspora Public Product Roadmap | Aspora Blog

## Here's What It Means for You


Three years ago, Aspora started with a single, stubborn belief: that Indians living abroad deserved better than the clunky, expensive, opaque experience of sending money home.


We built. You used it. You told us what worked and what could be better. And slowly, something bigger than a money transfer app began to take shape.


Every feature we've shipped has been shaped as much by your feedback as by our internal roadmap. Every product decision has been informed by your questions, your ideas, and your trust in what we're building together. That relationship — between Aspora and the NRI community


— is the most important thing we have.


And now, we want to bring you even closer to the journey.

## So, Why Are We Doing This?


Transparency


is something we've always valued internally. Our team works in the open — sharing what we're building, what we're prioritising, and why. It made sense to extend that to the people who matter most: our community.


We also want to build better


. We believe that the best products are built with users, not just for them. Opening up the roadmap means your ideas and priorities can directly influence what we work on next. If something is missing that would make a real difference to your financial life across borders — we want to know.


## Does The Roadmap Show Everything?


Not everything. Some work — infrastructure improvements, security upgrades, compliance work — happens in the background and doesn't appear as a named feature. We've kept the roadmap focused on what directly impacts your experience as an Aspora user.


The roadmap is a living document. We'll update it regularly as things move forward, get shipped, or change.


## This is Part of Something Bigger


The public roadmap is the first of several community initiatives we're launching this year. Alongside it, we're publishing a newsletter


dedicated to the most relevant personal finance insights for NRIs — and bringing the community together in-person


through events in London and Dubai.


We're still early in this journey. But we're committed to building Aspora in the open, with your voice and your trust at the centre of everything we do.


This is what building in the open looks like for us.


## One Last Thing


We're still early. There's a lot left to build, and we want to do every bit of it with your input, your ideas, and your trust at the centre of it all.
