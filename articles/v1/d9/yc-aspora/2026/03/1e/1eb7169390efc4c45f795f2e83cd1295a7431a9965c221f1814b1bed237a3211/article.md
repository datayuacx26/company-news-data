### Physical Exchange Houses


Physical exchange house networks like Western Union, Ria, and MoneyGram maintain hundreds of thousands of agent locations globally and offer cash pickup options across India. They suit recipients in areas without reliable banking access, or NRIs who prefer face-to-face service.


The trade-off: higher FX markups and flat fees make physical exchange houses more expensive than digital-first options for most standard transfers.


### Banks and Authorised Dealers


Traditional bank wire transfers from US institutions remain a reliable route, especially for large, high-compliance transactions. RBI-licensed Authorised Dealer (AD) banks handle legitimate foreign exchange operations with full regulatory backing.


Destination options for your recipient in India include NRE (Non-Resident External) accounts at leading Indian banks — SBI, HDFC, ICICI, IDFC FIRST, and DBS all offer NRE savings accounts where funds can arrive tax-free and with full repatriability. These are destination account types, not transfer platforms themselves.

Bank wires typically take 1–3 business days and carry wider FX spreads. For compliance, always use the correct RBI purpose code and retain transfer records for five years under FEMA.


## What are the common challenges NRIs face when sending money home internationally?


The most common challenges are hidden costs, documentation requirements, and keeping pace with evolving regulations. Exchange-rate markups — often 1–3% that providers do not disclose upfront — quietly reduce what your family receives.


Additional friction points include variable KYC timelines, RBI purpose code requirements, and changing compliance rules across jurisdictions. Staying informed about regulatory developments, including any proposed changes to remittance taxation in the US, is advisable for regular senders. Choosing a transparent, regulated provider removes most of this friction.


## What documents or verification do NRIs need to send money home digitally?


Digital platforms typically require: a valid government-issued photo ID (passport), proof of overseas address (utility bill or bank statement), a self-declaration of NRI status, and completed KYC or CFT screening. Most platforms handle this fully online.


Cross-verification with Indian correspondent banks may add a day or two for first-time transfers. Always confirm the correct RBI purpose code for your transfer type before submitting.

## How long does it typically take for NRIs to send money home through an app?


Most digital apps complete transfers in minutes to 24 hours for standard bank-to-bank routes. Express or card-funded options can be near-instant but typically cost more. Traditional bank wire transfers from US institutions average 1–3 business days.


Always check cut-off times — transfers initiated late in the business day may not process until the following morning. For urgent payments, confirm the provider's same-day cut-off before initiating.
