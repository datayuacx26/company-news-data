Sending money from the UK to India is a routine necessity for millions of NRIs and Global Indians — supporting family, covering bills, funding education, or managing property. This review compares Wise, Remitly, and Aspora across the criteria that actually matter: exchange rates, fees, transfer speed, payout options, regulation, and who each service suits best.


## How to Compare Reliable UK to India Money Transfer Services


The most important number in any transfer comparison is the total INR your recipient receives — after the exchange rate margin and all fees have been applied. That single figure reflects the true cost of a transfer more accurately than any advertised fee.


The mid-market exchange rate is the real midpoint between the buy and sell prices on global currency markets — what you see on Google. Any rate a provider offers you will include some margin above this; the smaller that margin, the more your recipient receives.


Key criteria to evaluate:


- Exchange rate margin


— how much above the mid-market rate you're being charged


- Transfer fee


— the flat or percentage fee charged per transaction


- Transfer speed


— guaranteed delivery timeline, not an estimate


- Regulatory standing


— FCA registration in the UK and RBI/FEMA compliance for India


- User experience


— ease of setup, beneficiary management, and customer support

Aspora supports transfers to hundreds of Indian banks and offers direct bill payments as well, making it particularly useful for NRIs who manage ongoing financial commitments in India.


Where Aspora stands out:


- Google-matching exchange rates with zero hidden markup


- Swift transfers to most banks in India


- Integrated bill payment for UK NRIs managing India-side expenses


- FCA-registered with full regulatory compliance and security-first infrastructure


Aspora is the strongest choice for cost-conscious NRIs and Global Indians who want to maximise the rupee value of every transfer and manage India-side financial obligations from one platform.


## Wise


Wise (formerly TransferWise) is well-regarded for its fee transparency. The platform uses the mid-market exchange rate and charges a small, clearly displayed percentage fee on top. Before you confirm any transfer, you see an exact breakdown of what you're paying and what your recipient receives.


Wise is particularly well-suited to high-value, bank-to-bank transfers and provides a polished mobile and web experience with real-time tracking. It is fully licensed by the UK's Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and regulated in multiple jurisdictions globally.


Where Wise stands out:


- Explicit, upfront cost breakdown before every transfer


- Mid-market rate with a small transparent fee


- Globally licensed and FCA-registered


- Live transaction tracking with clear delivery timelines


Wise's primary limitation for UK-to-India transfers is that it supports bank-to-bank delivery only — no cash pickup option for recipients.


## Remitly


Remitly is built around delivery speed and payout flexibility. It offers two distinct transfer tiers: Economy (1–3 business days, lower fee) and Express (minutes, higher fee). Recipients in India can receive funds via bank deposit, mobile wallet, or cash pickup.


Remitly is FCA-registered in the UK and uses standard industry encryption and multi-factor authentication.


Where Remitly stands out:


- Express tier for same-day or near-instant delivery


- Simple, guided interface suitable for first-time senders


- FCA-registered with solid security controls


The main trade-off with Remitly is cost variability. The total cost — fee plus exchange rate margin — can vary between the Economy and Express tiers. Always check the total INR your recipient receives before selecting a speed tier.
