# Christian Dior Rouge Premier Lipstick Necklace

One of the things I admire most about the past is the dedication to craftsmanship. Before mass industrialization, everyday items were often imbued with artistry and personalization.


The House of Dior has captured this spirit through a collaboration between makeup expert Peter Phillips and jewelry artist Victoire de Castellane. They have transformed a functional everyday tool—lipstick—into the epitome of luxury, ornately designed with diamonds, opals, tourmaline, and turquoise. Every detail is dazzling and sumptuous.


Furthermore, this decadent piece can be attached to a chain and worn as an eye-catching statement necklace. It truly takes the term "designer" to a new level. With its nod to nature, extraordinary level of detail, and genius dual purpose, it is a timeless creation.


As Rouge Premier was only presented at a few exclusive events, these rare pieces will undoubtedly become a significant part of fashion history.
