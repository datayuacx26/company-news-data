# Small Light Handbags Truly For Your Wrist

Sometimes, it is a serious drag toting around a bag. It can be hard on the shoulders and your back. And, most handbags that rest on the wrist are just uncomfortable due to their weight. Enter the little wrist bag! The perfect accessory for those who travel very light.


Here are a few personal favorites.


## Chanel Pearl Bracelet Flap Pouch Bags


Introduced in 2021, these bags are seriously classy and cute. 2 of the 3 sizes can even hold some cellphones. The original retail prices range from $2525 to $3625.


## Chloe Small Nile Bracelet Bag


Introduced in 2017, this is one of the larger bracelet bags. However, it is still quite small with a height of 6 inches and a length of 7.5 inches. This stylish little bag can also be purchased in the pre-owned market for well under $1000.


## Gucci Raja Broadway Bracelet Handle Bag


Small but dramatic, and big enough to hold some phones. This satin bag is hard to find. When you do see it, expect to pay around $1000.


## Jimmy Choo Micro Bon Bon handbag


One of the cutest bucket bags ever. Retailing $1200 to $4700, this bag is classically Jimmy Choo. In the resale market, you can even find it for just a few hundred dollars.


## Louis Vuitton Party Palm Springs Bracelet Bag


Retails $790! Measuring 2.25 by 3.25 inches, this bag is teeny tiny. A phone will not fit inside. Not exactly the most practical choice, it sure is cute though.


## Fendi Nano Fendigraphy Wrist Bag


Too small for some phones, this is my personal favorite due to its small but not ridiculously small size and comfort level. The retail price is also not bad at $1290.


## Prada Saffiano Logo Wrist Pouch


Very small, not very functional, it really exists for a statement. This little fella is $850 new.
