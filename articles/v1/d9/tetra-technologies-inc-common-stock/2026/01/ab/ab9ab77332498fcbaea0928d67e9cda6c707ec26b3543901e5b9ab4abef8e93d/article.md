# TETRA Top Highlights of 2025


#### By | January 8th, 2026


#### January 8th, 2026


- Facebook
- LinkedIn
- More


As we reflect on 2025, we’re proud to share our Top Highlights—a testament to the dedication, innovation, and excellence of our employees, the continued support of our customers and partners, and the meaningful impact we’ve made in the communities we serve.


From winning awards for engineering innovation and our rebrand, to advancing sustainable produced water solutions, being ranked the Gulf of America’s top supplier for the fifth consecutive year, and unveiling our “One TETRA 2030” growth strategy at NYSE Investor Day, these milestones highlight how we continued to drive innovation and create value across industries.


Giving back remained a priority in 2025. We supported STEM education, donated to food banks, and contributed to programs that strengthen the communities where we live and work.


Thank you to our employees, customers, and partners for making 2025 a remarkable year. Explore the highlights below and join us in celebrating the moments that helped shape our success.
