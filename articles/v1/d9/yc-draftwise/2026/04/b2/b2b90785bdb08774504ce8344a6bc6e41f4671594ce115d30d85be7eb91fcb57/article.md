# Draftwise Partners with Goodwin on Firmwide Rollout

**New York, NY // April 22, 2026** – Draftwise, the Contract Intelligence platform, today announced that it has partnered with global law firm Goodwin, an industry-built law firm that seamlessly fuses legal excellence with strategic and business advisory, to harness its latest technology to more efficiently provide Goodwin’s clients with the highest quality work in a rapidly changing legal landscape.


"Goodwin is exactly the kind of firm Draftwise was built for–a powerhouse transactional practice whose decades of deal precedent represent an unmatched institutional edge," said James Ding, CEO of Draftwise. "With Draftwise, Goodwin’s teams can surface the most relevant language from millions of prior deal documents in seconds, putting the full depth of the firm’s experience to work for every client, on every deal."


"The firmwide rollout of Draftwise is an important part of how we’re evolving as a firm. Our transactional teams work on some of the most complex, market-shaping deals, and they need tools that match the pace and sophistication of that work," said Eric Tan, Chief Digital & Technology Officer at Goodwin. “By giving our lawyers AI-driven access to curated precedent, clause analytics, and insights drawn from our institutional knowledge, we’re enabling deeper analysis and more consistent quality. This is about empowering our people and strengthening the way we serve clients with advice that is thoughtful, precise, and grounded in real-world experience.”


Draftwise will now give Goodwin lawyers access to negotiated language from comparable transactions, identify client-specific preferences from past deals, apply firm-standard positions and playbooks, and discover market-tested alternatives with full strategic context, all without leaving Microsoft Word.
