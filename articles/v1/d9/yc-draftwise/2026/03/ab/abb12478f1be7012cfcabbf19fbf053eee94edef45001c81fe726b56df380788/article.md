# Draftwise Now Integrates with Docusign

In-house legal teams can now connect their Docusign workflows with Draftwise.


‍


In-house legal teams today use more software than ever. Each one does its job well and is critical to the tech stack. But when they don't talk to each other, the gaps between them become your problem.


‍


A contract is reviewed and redlined on one platform, then manually downloaded, reformatted, and uploaded elsewhere to get signed. Context gets lost. Versions confused. Time is wasted. And for a lean in-house team already juggling competing priorities, those small inefficiencies add up fast.


‍


This is why tool interoperability is foundational to how modern legal teams operate – and why we prioritized integrating with Docusign.


‍


If you rely on Docusign to execute agreements, your contract workflow is now end-to-end.


‍


Redline, markup, and negotiate in Draftwise, powered by the executed agreements and templates stored in Docusign, without leaving your flow, switching tabs, or manually transferring documents.


‍


The Docusign integration is available now. Contactsales@draftwise.com to get started today.
