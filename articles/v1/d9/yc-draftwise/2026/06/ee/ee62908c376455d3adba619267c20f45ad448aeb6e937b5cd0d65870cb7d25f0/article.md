# DraftWise & Egnyte Partner on AI Contract Intelligence | Draftwise

**New York, NY // June 01, 2026 –** Draftwise, the leading contract intelligence platform for legal drafting, review, and negotiation, today announced a strategic technology partnership with Egnyte, a leader in secure content collaboration, intelligence, and governance. The integration enables legal teams to leverage Draftwise's AI-powered contract intelligence directly from Egnyte's secure document infrastructure, eliminating friction between document storage and contract review.


With the Draftwise–Egnyte integration, law firms and in-house legal teams can now access and work on contracts stored in Egnyte without leaving their trusted document workflows. Draftwise applies each organization’s precedents, playbooks, and negotiation history to draft, redline, and review contracts, leveraging its unique institutional knowledge.


> "Legal teams shouldn't have to choose between secure, organized document management and cutting-edge AI," said Emre Ozen, Chief Operating Officer at Draftwise. "Our integration with Egnyte means that the contracts already living in Egnyte are now fueling smarter, faster, and more consistent contract work. This is AI that's powered by your best deals, not generic training data."


> "At Egnyte, we believe that the value of an organization’s data shouldn’t be limited by disparate systems and clunk workflows," said Prasad Gune, Chief Product Officer at Egnyte. "Bringing Draftwise’s contract intelligence directly into Egnyte enables legal teams to leverage the power of their knowledge base where they already work best. It provides a seamless user experience with the security and precision our customers demand."


Legal professionals can use the Draftwise-Egnyte integration to:


• Access Egnyte contracts directly within Draftwise's AI-powered legal environment


• Draft and redline agreements using their organization's own precedent and playbooks


• Surface relevant negotiation history and past deal language from across their contract portfolio


• Maintain Egnyte's enterprise-grade security and governance standards throughout every workflow


The Draftwise integration is available now on the Egnyte App Store. To learn more or request a demo, visit[here](https://www.draftwise.com/partners/egnyte) .
