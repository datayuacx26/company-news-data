# Draftwise Promotes Josh Sandman to Chief Sales Officer

**NEW YORK** — Draftwise, the proprietary legal intelligence platform trusted by over half of the Vault 10 and leading Fortune 500 legal teams, today announced the promotion of Josh Sandman to Chief Sales Officer.


Since joining Draftwise in 2024, Sandman has led the company's global sales organization, helping grow revenue more than sixfold while expanding Draftwise's presence among many of the world's leading law firms and enterprise legal teams. As Chief Sales Officer, he will continue leading Draftwise's global sales strategy and go-to-market organization as the company enters its next phase of growth.


Prior to joining Draftwise, Sandman held several leadership roles at Pendo, where he helped scale the business from zero revenue to more than $200 million. During his tenure, he played a pivotal role for both the company's direct and indirect sales organizations and established Pendo's San Francisco office.


He later joined Gravity Sketch, where he led commercial teams across North America and Europe, bringing deep experience building and scaling international go-to-market organizations selling to some of the largest automotive and footwear brands in the world


"Josh has played a pivotal role in Draftwise's growth over the past two years," said James Ding, CEO and co-founder of Draftwise. "He has built an exceptional sales organization with a culture we’re proud of, earned the trust of our customers, and helped accelerate our growth at a defining moment for both Draftwise and the legal industry. I'm excited to see him lead our next chapter as Chief Sales Officer."


"I'm incredibly grateful to James, Emre, and Ozan for the opportunity and to the clients I have come to know and respect," said Sandman. "We've built an exceptional team and partnered with some of the world's leading law firms and legal departments. As Chief Sales Officer, I remain committed to our founders' vision: helping legal teams turn their firm's knowledge, unique market data, and hard-earned negotiations into their greatest competitive advantage."


As Chief Sales Officer, Sandman will oversee Draftwise's global sales organization and continue driving the company's growth across North America and EMEA.
