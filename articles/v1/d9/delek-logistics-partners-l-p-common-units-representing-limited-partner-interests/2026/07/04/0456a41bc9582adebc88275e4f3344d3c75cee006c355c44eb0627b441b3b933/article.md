# Delek Logistics Partners, LP to Host Second Quarter 2026 Conference Call on August 5th

### Delek Logistics Partners, LP to Host Second Quarter 2026 Conference Call on August 5th


July 17, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/790371638/files/doc_news/Delek-Logistics-Partners-LP-to-Host-Second-Quarter-2026-Conference-Call-on-August-5th-2026.pdf)


BRENTWOOD, Tenn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Delek Logistics Partners, LP (NYSE: DKL) (“Delek Logistics”) today announced that the Partnership intends to issue a press release summarizing second quarter 2026 results before the U.S. stock market opens on Wednesday, August 5, 2026. A conference call to discuss these results is scheduled to begin at 11:30 a.m. CT (12:30 p.m. ET) on Wednesday, August 5, 2026.


The live broadcast of this conference call will be available online by going to[www.DelekLogistics.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.DelekLogistics.com&esheet=54571954&newsitemid=20260717257139&lan=en-US&anchor=www.DelekLogistics.com&index=1&md5=792649aeea7a5e10d3bc665eed95b8c0) and clicking on the webcasts section of the website. The online replay will be available on the website for 90 days.


**About Delek Logistics Partners, LP**


Delek Logistics is a midstream energy master limited partnership headquartered in Brentwood, Tennessee. Through its owned assets and joint ventures located primarily in and around the Permian Basin, the Delaware Basin and other select areas in the Gulf Coast region, Delek Logistics provides gathering, pipeline, transportation, and other services for its customers in crude oil, intermediates, refined products, natural gas, storage, wholesale marketing, terminalling, water disposal and recycling.


Delek US Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: DK) ("Delek US") owns the general partner interest as well as a majority limited partner interest in Delek Logistics and is also a significant customer.


Information about Delek Logistics Partners, LP can be found on its website ([www.deleklogistics.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.deleklogistics.com&esheet=54571954&newsitemid=20260717257139&lan=en-US&anchor=www.deleklogistics.com&index=2&md5=eaba35c4d3ca9d7c06d7680b2d9dc3af) ), investor relations webpage ([https://www.deleklogistics.com/investor-relations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.deleklogistics.com%2Finvestor-relations&esheet=54571954&newsitemid=20260717257139&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.deleklogistics.com%2Finvestor-relations&index=3&md5=5db8160a357c8dd57c38c3625edc40c4) ), and news webpage ([https://www.deleklogistics.com/news-releases](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.deleklogistics.com%2Fnews-releases&esheet=54571954&newsitemid=20260717257139&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.deleklogistics.com%2Fnews-releases&index=4&md5=d52461a50e4b48163d47cea5432f52b4) ).
