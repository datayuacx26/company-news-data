### Delek Logistics Partners, LP 2025 K-1 Tax Packages Available on Website


March 6, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/790371638/files/doc_news/Delek-Logistics-Partners-LP-2025-K-1-Tax-Packages-Available-on-Website-2026.pdf)


BRENTWOOD, Tenn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Delek Logistics Partners, LP (NYSE: DKL) today announced that 2025 K-1 tax packages are now available on our third-party provider’s website,[https://www.taxpackagesupport.com/DelekLogistics](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.taxpackagesupport.com%2FDelekLogistics&esheet=54441064&newsitemid=20260306529816&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.taxpackagesupport.com%2FDelekLogistics&index=1&md5=57d3d90e0be61c1bd317a6344fbfb8c7) . Printing and mailing these tax packages are currently underway.


Questions regarding the 2025 Tax Reporting Package can be addressed by contacting 1-833-263-0144 between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. CST, Monday through Friday.


**About Delek Logistics Partners, LP**


Delek Logistics Partners, LP is a midstream energy master limited partnership headquartered in Brentwood, Tennessee. Through its owned assets and joint ventures located primarily in and around the Permian Basin, including both the Midland and the Delaware Basins, and other select areas in the Gulf Coast region, Delek Logistics provides gathering, pipeline, transportation, and other services for its customers in crude oil, intermediates, refined products, natural gas, storage, wholesale marketing, terminalling, water disposal, and recycling. Delek US Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: DK) (“Delek US”) owns the general partner interest as well as a majority limited partner interest in Delek Logistics Partners, LP, and is also a significant customer.


Information about Delek Logistics Partners, LP can be found on its website ([www.deleklogistics.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.deleklogistics.com&esheet=54441064&newsitemid=20260306529816&lan=en-US&anchor=www.deleklogistics.com&index=2&md5=40efe41580e79720b75ebdb26ff895df) ), investor relations webpage ([https://ir.deleklogistics.com/overview/default.aspx](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.deleklogistics.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54441064&newsitemid=20260306529816&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fir.deleklogistics.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&index=3&md5=ef8f0a7760b0ee85ec4887f691c2d816) ), news webpage ([https://ir.deleklogistics.com/news/default.aspx](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.deleklogistics.com%2Fnews%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54441064&newsitemid=20260306529816&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fir.deleklogistics.com%2Fnews%2Fdefault.aspx&index=4&md5=0bc9e026700519333cdcbbd95c81fe8d) ), and its Twitter account ([@DelekLogistics](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fx.com%2FDelekLogistics&esheet=54441064&newsitemid=20260306529816&lan=en-US&anchor=%40DelekLogistics&index=5&md5=bfd817217c15b04702fc4e58a5652fa8) ).
