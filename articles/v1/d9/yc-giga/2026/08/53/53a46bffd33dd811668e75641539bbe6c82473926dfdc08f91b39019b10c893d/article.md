# Scheduled callbacks for Giga voice agents

Customers are much more likely to convert when you reach them at an optimal time. That’s why with Giga, customers can now request a callback. Giga’s voice AI agents can now schedule and place callbacks on their own—keeping a conversation’s context and memory in place until your customer is ready to chat. Customers never have to repeat their requests or rehash the conversation.


Before today, when customers got interrupted mid-call, it was the end of the conversation. You might document it in their ticket, but in all likelihood, you’ll miss that callback window. It may be after business hours, or perhaps you’re just in a long customer queue, so you need to call back.
