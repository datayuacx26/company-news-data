# BOIL WATER ADVISORY LIFTED

**ESTE INFORME CONTIENE INFORMACIÓN IMPORTANTE ACERCA DE SU AGUA POTABLE, HAGA QUE ALGUIEN LO TRADUZCA PARA USTED, O HABLE CON ALGUIEN QUE LO ENTIENDA**


**Boil Water Advisory Lifted for Customers in Part of Scranton**


**SCRANTON, Pa. (July 29, 2026)** **–** Pennsylvania American Water announced that the boil water advisory for customers in part of Scranton has been lifted. Customers were notified on July 27, 2026, of the loss of positive water pressure due to a water main break, resulting in the issuance of the boil water advisory. We are pleased to report that the issue has been resolved.
