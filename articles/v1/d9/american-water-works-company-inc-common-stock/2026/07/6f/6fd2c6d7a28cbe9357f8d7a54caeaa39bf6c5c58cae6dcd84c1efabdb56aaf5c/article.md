# New Jersey American Water and Mayor Mahler Team Up to Support Wanaque Families Facing Food Insecurity

*Wanaque Feeds the Hungry receives $1,000 donation through New Jersey American Water's "Mayor's Charity of Choice" raffle*


**CAMDEN, N.J. – JULY 22, 2026 –**[New Jersey American Water](https://amwater.com/njaw/about-us) donated $1,000 to[Wanaque Feeds the Hungry](https://form-renderer-app.donorperfect.io/give/wanaque-feeds-the-hungry/wfth_2025) , a nonprofit organization that has spent more than 30 years helping neighbors facing food insecurity, ensuring that no one in the Wanaque community goes hungry.


Wanaque Feeds the Hungry was selected as the beneficiary of this donation through New Jersey American Water's "Mayor's Charity of Choice" contest at the New Jersey Conference of Mayors, where Wanaque Mayor Daniel Mahler won a raffle giving him the chance to choose a local nonprofit to receive $1,000.


*Pictured at the check presentation, from left to right: Jaclyn Veasy, Mayor of Evesham Township and President of the New Jersey Conference of Mayors; Kim Schalek Downes, Vice President of External Affairs, Government Relations, and Business Development, New Jersey American Water; Mayor Daniel Mahler of the Borough of Wanaque; Chelsea Kulp, Director of External Affairs, New Jersey American Water.*
