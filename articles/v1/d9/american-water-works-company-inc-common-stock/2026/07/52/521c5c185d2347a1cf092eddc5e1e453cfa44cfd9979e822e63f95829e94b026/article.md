# New Jersey American Water and Mayor Galate Team Up to Support Hopatcong Animals

*The Hopatcong Pound Project receives $1,000 donation through New Jersey American Water’s “Mayor's Charity of Choice” raffle*


**CAMDEN, N.J. – JULY 22, 2026 –**[New Jersey American Water](https://amwater.com/njaw/about-us) donated $1,000 to the[Hopatcong Pound Project](https://www.facebook.com/HPPSafehouse/) , a nonprofit organization dedicated to rescuing and caring for cats in the Hopatcong community, including feral, unadoptable and FIV-positive cats that many shelters are unable to take in.


The Hopatcong Pound Project was selected as the beneficiary of this donation through New Jersey American Water's "Mayor's Charity of Choice" contest at the New Jersey Conference of Mayors, where Hopatcong Mayor Marie Galate won a raffle giving her the chance to choose a local nonprofit to receive $1,000.


*Pictured at the check presentation, from left to right: Jaclyn Veasy, Mayor of Evesham Township and President of the New Jersey Conference of Mayors; Kim Schalek Downes, Vice President of External Affairs, Government Relations, and Business Development, New Jersey American Water; Mayor Marie Galate of the Borough of Hopatcong; Chelsea Kulp, Director of External Affairs, New Jersey American Water.*
