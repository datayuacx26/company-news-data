# Planned Water Service Interruption Scheduled for Lancaster Township Area in Butler County on August 13

*Planned work will strengthen reliability and support future growth in the State Route 19 corridor*


**LANCASTER, Pa. (Aug. 11, 2026)** – Pennsylvania American Water announced that approximately 380 customers in Lancaster Township, Butler County, will experience a temporary water service interruption beginning at 9 a.m. on Thursday, August 13. Service is expected to be restored by 5 p.m. that day.


The planned outage will allow crews to complete critical work along State Route 19, including installing a new water service connection for a development project and two inline valves that will enhance the reliability, resiliency and flexibility of the water system.


Customers are encouraged to prepare by storing water in advance for essential needs during the interruption. Once service is restored, customers might experience temporarily cloudy or discolored water. If discoloration occurs, it is recommended to let water run for three to five minutes until water runs clear. Pennsylvania American Water crews will work as safely and efficiently as possible to complete the work and restore service as quickly as possible.
