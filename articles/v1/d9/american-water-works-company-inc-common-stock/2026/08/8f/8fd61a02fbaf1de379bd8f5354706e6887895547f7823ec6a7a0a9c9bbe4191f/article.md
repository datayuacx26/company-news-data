# Kentucky American Water to Upgrade Water Infrastructure on Williams Lane and Beach Road in Fayette County

**Lexington, Ky. (August 6, 2026)** – Kentucky American Water is investing approximately $370,000 to upgrade critical water infrastructure in Fayette County along Williams Lane and Beach Road.


The project includes replacing 1,185 feet of aging 1.5‑inch-diameter water main installed between 1950 and 1970 with 3-inch-diameter PVC main. The project includes water main along Williams Lane from the end of Williams Lane to Beach Road, and on Beach Road from Williams Lane to Old Richmond Road.


This investment is part of the company’s ongoing commitment to strengthening water infrastructure to support reliable service and consistent water quality for customers. Planned infrastructure investments help reduce the likelihood of service interruption and improve water flow for homes and businesses. The improvements are designed to deliver long‑term value by reinforcing the system customers rely on every day.


Construction on this project began this week. Crews will typically work Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Access to homes and businesses will be maintained throughout construction, and crews will work directly with customers to accommodate driveway access
as needed.


In areas where excavation is required, temporary surface restoration will be provided. Permanent restoration will be completed once the soil has settled and weather conditions permit.


Work will be performed by Lagco Construction, with traffic control coordinated in partnership with local authorities. Motorists are advised to use caution near work zones, follow posted signage and expect some construction‑related noise during active work hours. Emergency vehicle and local access will be maintained at all times. Daily site cleanup will occur, and driveway, sidewalk and landscaping restoration will be completed upon project conclusion.
