# Houlihan Lokey Strengthens Capital Solutions Group With Senior Real Estate Hire

### Houlihan Lokey Strengthens Capital Solutions Group With Senior Real Estate Hire


March 23, 2026


**Steven Loffman Joins as a Managing Director in New York**


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Houlihan Lokey, Inc. (NYSE:HLI), the global investment bank, announced today that Steven Loffman has joined as a Managing Director in its Capital Solutions Group. Mr. Loffman will be based in New York and will lead the U.S. Real Estate Capital Advisory practice for the firm. This announcement follows the firm’s recent addition of Mellum Capital, a European based real estate capital advisory business bringing the firm’s global real estate capital solutions practice to over 20 professionals.


Mr. Loffman joins the firm following 18 years at Raymond James & Associates, where he most recently served as a Managing Director in its Real Estate Investment Banking Group. During his time with Raymond James, he managed more than 200 transactions, raising over $75 billion across public and private capital markets, served as the lead banker on IPOs across traditional Real Estate, GSA, Mortgage REIT, Timber, and Agriculture sectors, and developed innovative capital solutions and strategic partnerships for public and private real estate companies alike.


“Steven’s deep transaction expertise, creativity in developing capital solutions, and long-standing leadership in the real estate sector make him an outstanding choice to lead our U.S. Real Estate Capital Advisory effort,” said Anthony Martino, Managing Director and Global Co-Head of Houlihan Lokey’s Capital Solutions Group. “As clients navigate an increasingly complex environment, Houlihan Lokey is exceptionally well positioned to provide innovative, impactful solutions that drive meaningful results across the real estate capital solutions landscape.”


“Real estate clients today face an environment that demands sharper insights and more adaptive financing strategies, and Houlihan Lokey is exceptionally well equipped to help them navigate that challenge,” said Mr. Loffman. “I am excited to collaborate with our colleagues across the firm to continue delivering tailored solutions to clients globally.”


Houlihan Lokey’s Capital Solutions Group comprises 200 dedicated professionals across 18 offices in eight countries, including Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, Dallas, Dubai, Frankfurt, Houston, London, Los Angeles, Madrid, Manchester, Miami, Milan, Munich, New York, Paris, San Francisco, and Zurich. In the past 12 months, the group raised and advised on around $39 billion across more than 125 transactions.*
