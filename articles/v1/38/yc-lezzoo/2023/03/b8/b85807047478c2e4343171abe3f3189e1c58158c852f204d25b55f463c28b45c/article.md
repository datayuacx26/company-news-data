# The 2-week Feature That Changed The Fate Of Our Startup! A Lezzoo Story.

The genesis design of Rojana


In the world of startups, growth is the name of the game. As Paul Graham suggests, a startup is synonymous with growth. In the midst of August, our team at Lezzoo was determined to find a way to accelerate our growth. Conventional methods like vouchers, free deliveries, and discounts simply weren’t cutting it for us. These approaches often yield minimal impact on unit economics, and without significant funding, scaling becomes nearly impossible. We yearned for a fresh, unique idea to capture the attention of our target audience. Unfortunately, the legacy tactics used by major food delivery apps don’t work for everyone, especially smaller startups like ours.


### Birth of Rojana


Initially, we aimed to create a raffle and giveaway campaign that would incentivize users to boost their engagement and retention. However, we found that traditional raffles with lengthy timeframes, such as “win a car by the end of the month” or “win a PS5 by the new year,” had lost their appeal. We identified three crucial factors that influence the effectiveness of raffles:


1. The giveaway timeframe: Longer durations often demotivate participants and cast doubt on the campaign’s credibility.
2. Entry criteria: We discovered that users were reluctant to alter their consumer behaviour, such as being required to place multiple orders, spend a certain amount, or share content on social media.
3. The potential for loss: The possibility of not winning anything was a major deterrent for users.


However, traditional raffles with long timeframes felt outdated and were plagued by issues that dampened user enthusiasm. We asked ourselves, “What if we reinvent this old marketing concept by offering daily giveaways with simple entry criteria, like placing one order, and ensuring everyone wins?” Thus, “Rojana” was born, a term that means “everyday” in Kurdish.


After consolidating our data on Google Sheets, we realized that with the support of our partners and sponsors, our ambitious campaign could become a reality. We began by securing commitments from 20 partners and 3 sponsors and launched the campaign with 30–35 daily gifts, including TVs, headphones, smartwatches, and even air fryers. Our goal was to gamify the experience, allowing users to see who won each hour, the brand they ordered from, and the gift they received. We aimed to create a strong network effect to sustain the campaign’s momentum — the more people won, the more people would know about Rojana, ultimately driving more orders and attracting more partners.


The campaign’s execution was swift — so much so that 85% of our team only learned about it three days before the launch. After going live, our initial optimism turned to pessimism as traffic remained stagnant, and some partners expressed doubts about the campaign’s performance. However, by day four, we saw a 2% growth compared to the previous day, followed by another 2% the next day, and 3% growth on the seventh day. Our day-over-day growth averaged 1–2%, and within two weeks, our user engagement and daily active users doubled. At this point, Rojana began to dictate our strategy, taking on a life of its own.


### Hype up the Hype!


As Rojana’s success became apparent, we decided to amplify its impact further by increasing the number of daily prizes, offering high-value weekly prizes, and adopting a meme-driven communication strategy. At one point, we were giving away 260 gifts per day and one car per week, thanks to our partnership with MG Motors.


This bold move catapulted Lezzoo into the spotlight, with our unconventional marketing approach garnering attention from friends, family, and industry peers. Our content attracted more traffic and engagement than that of our competitors, and Rojana’s results exceeded our wildest expectations.


When @justmivan joined our team, he brought his expertise in content strategy and leadership, and his positive energy inspired the whole team. Together, we embarked on a transformative journey in local marketing, moving away from conservative communications and adopting a much more engaging and entertaining approach. Thanks to Mivan’s guidance and our shared courage, we achieved a turning point that propelled our brand to new heights.


### The Aftermath


Our Rojana today ❤


Rojana didn’t just transform our brand identity and company culture; it became a benchmark for both our successes and failures. The feature’s performance was astounding, as evidenced by the following figures:


- 20,000 gifts and 10 cars given away
- 1 million TikTok views and 17K new followers
- 30% growth on Instagram (Q4 vs. Q3)
- 42% revenue growth (Q4 vs. Q3)
- Record-breaking order numbers
- The highest notification click rate
- Highest daily active user count


The journey of Rojana highlights the importance of being open to experimentation and taking bold steps forward in the ever-changing startup landscape. We are proud of our achievements with Rojana, and we hope the Iraqi and Kurdish startup community as well as regional and international can draw inspiration from our experience.


We would like to express our gratitude to the brilliant minds behind Rojana, our incredible team, partners, and sponsors who made this journey possible. And don’t forget to check out our Ramadan Rojana offers — we’re giving away an iPhone every day!” 😉
