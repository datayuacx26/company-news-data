# Elemeno Health Earns Back-to-Back Inc. 5000 Recognition

**OAKLAND, Calif., August 11, 2026:**


[Elemeno Health](https://www.elemenohealth.com/) has been named to the


[2026 Inc. 5000](https://www.inc.com/inc5000) , the annual list of the fastest-growing private companies in America. This marks the second consecutive year Elemeno Health has earned a place on the list.


The recognition reflects continued demand for Elemeno Health’s


[frontline operations platform](https://www.elemenohealth.com/solutions-healthcare) , which helps healthcare organizations prepare, support, and enable frontline teams in the flow of work. To date, the platform has been deployed across more than 100 hospitals and clinics.


“Healthcare organizations are under pressure to accelerate workforce readiness, reduce operational variation, and support teams through constant change,” said Tim Coulter, CEO of Elemeno Health. “Our growth reflects the need for trusted guidance, communications, and operational support embedded in daily work. Elemeno helps health systems establish shared standards while giving each hospital, department, and unit the local context its teams rely on.”


Elemeno helps close the knowledge-to-practice gap by turning policies, procedures, best practices, and operational knowledge into locally relevant guidance and job aids that help frontline staff build skills, reinforce competencies, and apply knowledge with confidence in the flow of work.


Elemeno Health initially focused on just-in-time guidance and microlearning for nurses and other frontline clinicians. As its use has expanded across healthcare organizations, the platform has evolved into a frontline operations hub that brings together locally relevant procedural guidance, job aids, staff communications, onboarding resources, skills development, and competency support.


This progression is moving Elemeno beyond intermittent training and reference use toward daily operational support for managers and frontline teams.


The company’s continued growth reflects several developments:


-


Since its founding, the platform has been deployed across more than 100 hospitals and clinics, including large health systems, individual hospitals, and smaller and rural healthcare organizations.


-


Use has expanded beyond nursing into pharmacy, radiology, laboratory services, and other clinical and operational functions.


-


Elemeno’s Shared Resource Network now includes more than 40,000 assets, giving smaller and resource-constrained healthcare organizations a practical starting point for frontline guidance, skills development, and workforce support.


-


Larger health systems use Elemeno to distribute shared standards and best practices while allowing individual hospitals, departments, and units to adapt resources to their local workflows, staff, and communities.


-


Elemeno is extending its role in product adoption and operational support through relationships with medical device and automation companies. Through its partnership with Swisslog Healthcare, Elemeno gives frontline staff just-in-time guidance for pharmacy automation and robotics.


The Shared Resource Network is particularly relevant to smaller and rural hospitals, which often have fewer dedicated resources for clinical education, informatics, and content development than large health systems. Elemeno enables these organizations to draw from a broad base of existing frontline resources while maintaining control over the information presented to their own teams.


The platform also addresses a growing challenge associated with health system consolidation. As independent hospitals become part of larger systems, leaders need to align policies and practices without losing the local workflows and identity that remain important to employees, physicians, and patient communities. Elemeno supports both systemwide consistency and local adaptation.


Elemeno Health is deploying AI-assisted capabilities that help operational leaders organize, update, and deliver timely, contextually relevant guidance. These capabilities are built around trusted organizational content and local context, keeping a human in the loop.


“Healthcare is ultimately delivered by people, so technology should be built around them,” said Arup Roy-Burman, MD, founder of Elemeno Health. “When frontline staff get the information and skills support they need in the moment, they can work with greater confidence and competence. That, in turn, supports better care, stronger job satisfaction, and a more sustainable healthcare workforce.”


This year’s Inc. 5000 recognizes a new class of companies redefining what growth looks like. From AI and advanced manufacturing to healthcare, consumer products, and professional services, these businesses are expanding their impact, creating jobs, and proving that entrepreneurial ambition continues to fuel the U.S. economy.


Among the 5,000 companies on the list, the median three-year revenue growth rate was 130%, and those companies have collectively added more than 627,208 jobs to the U.S. economy over the past three years.


For the full Inc. 5000 list, honoree company profiles and a searchable database by industry and location, visit


[www.inc.com/inc5000](http://www.inc.com/inc5000) .


“Every company on the Inc. 5000 has a story of perseverance, smart decision making, and a refusal to sit still,” said Mike Hofman, editor-in-chief of Inc. “Their growth reflects more than strong financial performance; it reflects creativity, resilience, and the customer focus required to build companies that make a lasting impact. We congratulate all honorees on this significant achievement.”


Inc. will celebrate the honorees at the 2026 Inc. 5000 Conference & Gala, taking place Oct. 14-16 in Dallas. The top 500 companies will be listed in the fall issue of Inc. Magazine.

Inc. 5000 List Methodology


Companies on the 2026 Inc. 5000 are ranked according to percentage revenue growth from 2022 to 2025. To qualify, companies must have been founded and generating revenue by March 31, 2022. They must be U.S.-based, privately held, for-profit and independent, rather than subsidiaries or divisions of other companies, as of Dec. 31, 2025. Since then, some companies on the list may have gone public or been acquired. The minimum revenue required for 2022 is $100,000, and the minimum for 2025 is $2 million. Inc. reserves the right to decline applicants for subjective reasons.

About Elemeno Health


Founded by clinicians in 2016, Elemeno Health is a Public Benefit Corporation that helps healthcare organizations prepare, support, and enable frontline teams in the flow of work. Its cloud-based frontline operations platform gives staff immediate access to trusted, locally relevant guidance, job aids, communications, onboarding resources, and competency support at the point of need. By replacing fragmented binders, emails, and document repositories with role- and team-specific resources, Elemeno Health helps organizations standardize best practices, accelerate workforce readiness, strengthen operational consistency, and support safer care delivery.
