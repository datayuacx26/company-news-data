# Enhanced West Coast South America Service to the U.S. Northeast

## **New Direct Northbound Call to Gloucester Marine Terminal at the Port of Philadelphia**


**** Seaboard Marine is pleased to announce an important enhancement to its


**West Coast South America (WCSA) service** with the introduction of a direct northbound call to


**Gloucester Marine Terminal in Gloucester City, New Jersey** at the


**Port of Philadelphia.**


Beginning with the voyage


**arriving in Gloucester on Sunday, October 25, 2026** , customers shipping from the West Coast of South America will benefit from a more direct connection to the U.S. Northeast through Gloucester Marine Terminal.


This enhancement further strengthens Seaboard Marine's service between key markets in Chile, Peru, and Ecuador and the Northeast United States, providing customers with an improved routing and greater connectivity for northbound cargo.


**The scheduled rotation for the inaugural direct northbound WCSA call to Gloucester is:**


- **San Antonio, Chile:** Departing Saturday, October 3, 2026


- **Lirquén, Chile:** Departing Monday, October 5, 2026


- **Callao, Peru:** Departing Tuesday, October 6, 2026


- **Paita, Peru:** Departing Thursday, October 8, 2026


- **Guayaquil, Ecuador:** Departing Friday, October 9, 2026


- **Gloucester, New Jersey:** Arriving Sunday, October 25, 2026


- **Brooklyn, New York:** Arriving Wednesday, October 28, 2026


**New Terminal Address for WCSA Northbound Service:**


**Gloucester Marine Terminal**


160 Essex Street


Gloucester City, NJ 08030


The new direct northbound call represents another significant enhancement to Seaboard Marine's West Coast South America network, creating a direct streamlined connection for cargo moving from Callao, Paita, and Guayaquil and improved connectivity from Lirquén and San Antonio, Chile


**** to the U.S. Northeast.
