# Seaboard Marine Expands Regional Service with New Weekly Monday Departures from Costa Rica to Ecuador and Peru

Seaboard Marine is pleased to announce the expansion of our regional network with the introduction of an additional weekly sailing from Moin, Costa Rica, to Guayaquil, Ecuador, and Callao, Peru. The new Monday departure complements the company’s existing Saturday service, providing enhanced flexibility and improved scheduling options for importers and exporters throughout Central and South America.


This service expansion reflects Seaboard Marine’s continued commitment to delivering reliable, efficient, and customer-focused ocean transportation solutions. With two weekly sailings from Moin, shippers gain greater access to key South American dry and refrigerated cargo markets, strengthening regional connectivity.


“Our new Monday sailing from Moin to Guayaquil and Callao demonstrates our dedication to providing greater flexibility, improved connectivity, and consistent service,” said Piero Buitano, Regional Vice President. “It creates additional opportunities for Costa Rican businesses to reach South American markets with speed and reliability.”


Key Highlights of the Enhanced Service:


- **Dual Weekly Sailings:** Monday and Saturday departures from Moin to Guayaquil and Callao.
- **Improved Connectivity:** Expanded access for Costa Rican exporters to key markets in Ecuador and Peru.
- **Refrigerated Cargo Expertise:** Reliable transport of perishable goods, including fresh produce.
- **Regional Network Growth:** This will strengthen Seaboard Marine’s integrated network in Central America, South America, and the Caribbean.
