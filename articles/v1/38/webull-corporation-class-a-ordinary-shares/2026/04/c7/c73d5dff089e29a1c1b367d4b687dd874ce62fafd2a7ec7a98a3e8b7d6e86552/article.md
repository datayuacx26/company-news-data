# Webull Files Annual Report on Form 20-F for the Year Ended December 31, 2025

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla.


, April 9, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Webull Corporation](https://www.webullcorp.com/) (NASDAQ:BULL ) ("Webull" or the "Company") today announced that it has filed its annual report on Form 20-F for the fiscal year ended on December 31, 2025 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC").


The annual report on Form 20-F, which contains Webull's audited financial statements, can be accessed on the SEC's website at[http://www.sec.gov](http://www.sec.gov/) as well as on the Company's investor relations website at[https://www.webullcorp.com/investor-relations/sec](https://www.webullcorp.com/investor-relations/sec) . Shareholders may receive a hard copy of the annual report free of charge upon request.
