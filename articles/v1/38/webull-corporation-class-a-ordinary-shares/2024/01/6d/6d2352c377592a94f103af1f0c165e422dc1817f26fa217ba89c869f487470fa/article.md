# Webull and TradingView Join Forces


01/15/2024


[Webull is now a proud partner of TradingView](https://www.webull.com/tradingview) ! TradingView, a charting and trading platform, provides users with market data access, community content, and analytical features, allowing you to use advanced charts and tools to help you on your investment journey. Now, it also gives you the ability to use your Webull account to trade without switching platforms.


Clients can now connect their Webull brokerage account to their TradingView user profile and trade directly from the industry’s leading charting platform. Immerse yourself in TradingView’s exceptional user experience by trading without the need to separately open your Webull platform interface. With over 100 drawing tools and indicators, your trading experience can be taken to the next level.


Don’t forget to join TradingView’s social network so you can stay connected in a community of like-minded investors and gain access to scripts, trading ideas, and livestreams. Additionally, check out their comprehensive analytical suite featuring heatmaps, reports, advanced screeners, and financial statistics. Seasoned traders can also enjoy their strategy testing, alert system, and proprietary programming language.


At Webull, we are always looking for new ways to make improvements for our users. By integrating with TradingView, we hope to optimize the trading experience for investors of all backgrounds and ability levels.


New to TradingView? Join today to gain access and streamline your trading process!
