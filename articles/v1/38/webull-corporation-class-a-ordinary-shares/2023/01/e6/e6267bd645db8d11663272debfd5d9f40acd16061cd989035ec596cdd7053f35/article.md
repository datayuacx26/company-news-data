# Webull Wins "Best Brokerage App" at Benzinga's 2022 Global Fintech Awards

NEW YORK, NY – Jan. 19, 2022 – Webull, a leading[commission-free trading platform](https://www.webull.com/introduce) , has been recognized as the Best Brokerage App at Benzinga's Global Fintech Awards. The annual awards honor the top companies in the fintech sector that are building the future of financial services and capital markets.


"We are thrilled to have been recognized by the Benzinga Global Fintech Awards as a top company building the future of financial services," said Anthony Denier, CEO of Webull. "Our priority has always been to provide traders with the tools they need to make more informed decisions about their investments, and this recognition truly reflects our continued commitment to our users, particularly during periods of economic volatility. We are excited to continue making advancements that further propel the industry forward."


Webull's achievement was initially acknowledged at the Benzinga Global Fintech Awards ceremony, held on December 8th, 2022 in New York City. The award's independent panel of judges reviewed applicants on impact in the areas of investing, technology, financial literacy, and more.


In 2022, Webull launched its latest version, Webull 8.0, as well as[Index Options Trading](https://www.webull.com/index-options) , and a recurring deposit feature for users, which expanded their coverage of trading tools and provided a more seamless user experience.


Additionally, Webull has been recognized as a top broker for short selling for active traders has been highly reviewed by Benzinga.


The list of Benzinga award winners can be found[here](https://www.benzinga.com/events/fintech-awards/winners/) . For more information, on Webull, please visit[www.webull.com](https://www.webull.com/) .
