# Meet Warp: The Future of Employee Management Software

Nobody goes into HR to call state tax agencies. And no one takes an operations job to reconcile benefit deductions in spreadsheets or search for withholding IDs on government websites.


**Meet Warp:** the only AI-native employee management platform.


Instead of clicking through clunky dashboards, outdated software, or .gov websites for taxes, Warp's AI agents open every state tax account, file every payroll form, and resolve every tax notice. Automatically.


What started as a better way to run payroll has become the first AI-powered employee management platform that truly runs itself: onboarding, payroll, global payments, time tracking, compliance, benefits, and IT, all automated in one place, all tied to a single employee record. Built for speed and enterprise-grade power as you scale from 1 to 10,000 employees.


## From the first day to the last day, automated


When a new hire signs their offer letter on Warp, the coordination work disappears. Agreements, direct deposit, tax forms, and benefits enrollment all kick off automatically. Through[Warp Fabric](https://www.warp.co/it) , every app, account, and device gets provisioned across thousands of integrations simultaneously, without your ops person spending the afternoon on it.


The same logic applies when an employee leaves. A single action in Warp deactivates every account, every app, every device simultaneously.[MDM software](https://warp.co/blog/what-is-mdm) , SSO configurations, and access management are part of the same system as HR, updated from the same employee record in real time.


Both onboarding and offboarding are workflows, not checklists someone has to remember to finish. The same is true for payroll.


## Run payroll in 10 minutes


[Payroll automation](https://warp.co/blog/how-to-automate-startup-payroll-to-save-time-and-reduce-errors) is not a feature on Warp. It is the default state.


Most platforms automate the calculation and leave the rest to your team. Warp automates all of it: the calculations, the withholdings, the filings, and the direct deposits. Payroll runs every cycle on its own, accurately and on time across all 50 states, with employees, contractors, and commission-based roles flowing through the same workflow.


For hourly teams, Warp has built-in time-tracking and attendance software that automatically enforces overtime rules and state labor policies, syncing directly with payroll with no manual reconciliation. For international teams, Warp pays in over 150 countries in local currency, so[contractor payroll](https://warp.co/blog/when-should-startups-hire-independent-contractors) and full-time employees are never managed in separate systems.


Running a full payroll cycle on Warp takes under ten minutes, with no tax compliance requirements piling up in the background.


## Tax compliance, handled


With Warp, **80% of tax notices are resolved without anyone on your team touching them.** The rest go directly to our in-house tax experts.


Hire in a new state, and Warp registers you with every agency automatically. No .gov websites. No tracking down withholding IDs or unemployment insurance numbers. The[multi-state payroll compliance](https://warp.co/blog/multi-state-payroll-compliance-guide-for-startups-2026) work that takes most businesses hours every quarter happens in the background.


When a tax notice arrives, Warp AI reads it, drafts the response, and files it across more than 10,000 jurisdictions. For the edge cases that require a human, our in-house tax team handles it directly with the agency. Not a ticket queue. Not a chatbot telling you to call a number.


Most payroll providers have you handle the compliance work. Warp gives you payroll and compliance automation as a non-negotiable. You will never visit a government website, sit on hold with a tax agency, or pay an accountant $150 per filing again.


And as any HR leader will tell you, managing benefits carries its own version of overhead.
