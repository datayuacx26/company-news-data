# Dive into the Foundations of the Cloud with Mitchell Hashimoto on the Platform Engineering Podcast

At Massdriver.cloud, we are always keen to bring you the latest insights and thought leadership in the world of platform engineering and DevOps. Our latest podcast episode is a must-listen for anyone looking to deepen their understanding of cloud infrastructure and its evolution.


In this episode, Cory O'Daniel sits down with Mitchell Hashimoto, the co-founder of HashiCorp and the visionary behind Terraform. Mitchell shares his journey from creating Vagrant to pioneering infrastructure as code with Terraform, offering invaluable lessons for engineers and teams navigating the complexities of cloud environments.


**Key Highlights:**


- **The Genesis of Terraform:** Mitchell delves into the inception of Terraform, discussing the challenges and inspirations that led to its creation. This segment is a treasure trove of insights for anyone interested in the early days of infrastructure as code.
- **Infrastructure as Code (IaC) Evolution:** Learn about the evolution of IaC and how Terraform has influenced modern cloud infrastructure management. Mitchell provides a unique perspective on the past, present, and future of IaC.
- **Challenges and Solutions:** The conversation explores the common hurdles teams face when implementing IaC and how Terraform addresses these challenges. Mitchell’s practical advice is invaluable for both new and seasoned DevOps practitioners.
- **Future of Cloud Infrastructure:** Mitchell shares his vision for the future of cloud infrastructure and the role of emerging technologies. This forward-looking discussion is perfect for those who want to stay ahead of the curve in cloud engineering.


Tune in to gain a deeper understanding of the foundations of the cloud from one of the leading minds in the industry. Whether you're a DevOps engineer, a platform engineer, or simply passionate about cloud technology, this episode offers a wealth of knowledge and inspiration.
