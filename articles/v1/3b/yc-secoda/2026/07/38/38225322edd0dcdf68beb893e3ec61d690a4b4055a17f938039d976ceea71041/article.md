# Why AI drives better data experiences in Secoda

We recently refreshed the Secoda homepage to highlight[Secoda AI](https://www.secoda.co/secoda-ai) more prominently. This update reflects what we’ve consistently observed in product usage: organizations that enable Secoda AI see higher adoption and deeper engagement across teams. In fact, viewers who use Secoda AI return to Secoda **three times more often** than those who don’t.


Our goal is to help teams expand access to data, encourage[self-service](https://www.secoda.co/self-service-business-intelligence) , and speed up the time to value.


Secoda AI is more prominently accessible via the Homepage


## A closer look at viewer behavior


Many *viewers* in Secoda are business users including product managers, marketers, and customer success managers. These individuals are not always part of the technical team, but they’re becoming more self-sufficient with the help of Secoda AI.


We’re seeing clear usage patterns where business users are using AI to:


- Write or troubleshoot SQL queries
- Explore[lineage](https://www.secoda.co/blog/a-complete-guide-to-data-lineage) to understand how dashboards are built
- Navigate glossaries and metrics to clarify definitions
- Ask follow-up questions that build on prior context


These behaviors indicate that AI is empowering more users to find answers independently and interact with data more confidently.


## How to configure Secoda AI for success


Giving people the confidence to work with data starts with a thoughtful AI setup. Admins play a critical role in shaping how AI behaves within their workspace. We’ve made it easy to tailor Secoda AI so that it aligns with existing tools,[governance](https://www.secoda.co/blog/data-governance-the-ultimate-guide) requirements, and team-specific processes.


Here’s how admins can configure AI to support their organization:


- **Apply filters:** Restrict AI access to specific schemas, resources, or tags based on your security model.
- **Set custom instructions:** Define AI behavior for tasks like search, documentation, and persona-based answers. You can customize the tone, scope, or preferred data sources.
- **Enable memory:** Let AI remember commonly used dashboards, definitions, or tools for faster future responses.


These capabilities are available today and can make a significant impact on how easily teams access and use data.
