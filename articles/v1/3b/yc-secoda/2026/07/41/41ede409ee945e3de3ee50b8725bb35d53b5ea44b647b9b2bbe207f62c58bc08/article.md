# AI Chrome Extension | Secoda

**Better, faster, and more powerful data discovery - Introducing Secoda’s AI powered Chrome Extension**


Secoda’s new Chrome Extension is live! Get it set up[here](https://docs.secoda.co/features/chrome-extension) .


At[Secoda](https://www.secoda.co/old-home-6) , we want to give everyone the confidence to use data, and we understand the need to seamlessly integrate your tools into your daily workflow.


Today we’re launching our Chrome Extension to enhance your Secoda experience, and make finding and understanding your data quicker and easier. Say goodbye to unnecessary tabs, and hello to a world where Secoda seamlessly integrates into your Chrome browser.


With this powerful new extension, you’ll be able to:


- **Find and edit Secoda metadata** directly in the most popular BI and data warehouse tools such as Looker, Tableau, Snowflake, BigQuery, and more
- **Access and search resource lineage** from your BI and data warehouse tools
- **Start conversations** and ask questions to Secoda AI
- **Grab text from any website** , and search Secoda


… all right from your Chrome browser!


Even if you have dozens of tabs and windows open (we’re guilty of this as well…), Secoda is now instantly accessible, and the extension is packed with features that will help you and your team work even faster.


## View and Edit Resource Metadata


Access and make changes to resource metadata from the most popular BI and data warehouse tools, right from the tab you’re already in. If you’re working away on a table or view in Snowflake for example, the Secoda Chrome extension allows you to view and edit the metadata of the table and its columns directly in Snowflake.


Whether you’re looking at a dashboard and need to find a definition, make a quick change to the owner of a table, add a missing tag, or find a column description, you can rapidly find context and make changes that will be reflected in Secoda, right from the Chrome extension.


## Access and Search Resource Lineage


See the upstream and downstream lineage of the resource without having to leave the other tools you’re working in. The extension allows you to see lineage relationships at different depths, and access the lineage graph within the Secoda UI, so you can quickly understand what you’re working with.


## Chat with Secoda AI


Secoda’s AI Assistant helps your team make sense of knowledge that is spread across various databases, warehouses, transformation tools, BI dashboards, and more. With the new Chrome extension, you can ask your Secoda AI Assistant any question about any data resources.


Not sure what tables have been transformed in dbt? Or when the documentation on a table was last updated? Need to know what is the best dashboard to find updated revenue numbers? Ask your Secoda AI Assistant, right from your browser, and get answers immediately.


## Search any text from any website on Secoda


Search Secoda for context for any information from **any** website. Simply highlight the text you want to learn more about, right-click, and search in Secoda. This is especially useful when coming across information you may be unfamiliar with, as a quick check to see if there is any context in Secoda to get you up to speed, fast.


Supercharge your Secoda experience by installing the Secoda Chrome Extension now, and unlock a new level of efficiency!


Stay tuned for more exciting updates and enhancements - we’re continually improving features and functionality to this extension to make you and your team even more productive, and save time.
