# Introducing Autopilot Mode

# Run Tusk on Autopilot


Tusk is now a top contributor for some of our most active product squads, with **48.9% of all monthly merged PRs** contributed by our AI coding agent.


This number is rising now that we're allowing our agent to automatically triage and then trigger PR creation for Linear, Jira, Notion, or GitHub issues that it is confident in completing.


Autopilot mode in action on a real ticket


Autopilot is an **opt-in mode** for customers on our paid plans who want to clear their backlog of UI polish tickets faster. While Tusk will auto-generate PRs for issues, it will not merge them. Instead, it'll notify you via your project management app to review the PR and then merge it.


If you're a product engineer or technical PM who's tired of having to constantly push product quality tickets to the next sprint, message us atfounders@usetusk.ai and we'll sort you out. We'd rather you wake up in the morning with 3 PRs ready for review as opposed to 3 anxiety-inducing customer complaints.
