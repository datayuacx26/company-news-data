# Tusk Is Sunsetting

## A new chapter


To our customers and partners:


After 2.5 years of building Tusk, we are embarking on a new chapter of our journey. Tusk is winding down, with big news coming soon. Our team will continue the mission of helping engineering teams ship reliable code faster, now at a larger scale.


## What this means


As informed before, we are sunsetting the Tusk product on Jun 10, 2026. Customer support will continue for a month after for your inquiries. Please reach out tosupport@usetusk.ai if you have any questions or requests.


Our team will be removing the Tusk GitHub App, which automatically uninstalls it from your GitHub organization. Tusk's historical pull requests and pull request comments will remain in place. If you are using Tusk Drift, uninstall the Tusk Drift SDK from your services and delete the CI workflow running the Drift tests.


## Transition


We understand Tusk played a valuable part in your SDLC. In its place, we recommend using Cognition's[Devin](https://devin.ai/?utm_source=tusk) for test automation and code review, among other coding use cases. If you're interested in this transition, please refer to this guide on[migrating from Tusk to Devin](https://docs.usetusk.ai/devin-migration) .


Thank you for being a great partner to us. We appreciate your collaboration, and are excited to share what's next for the Tusk team.
‍


– Marcel, Sohil, and the Tusk team
