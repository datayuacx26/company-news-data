# Alarm.com and CHeKT Earn SSI Most Valuable Product (MVP) Award for Remote Video Monitoring Integration

*Award recognizes Alarm.com and CHeKT collaboration unifying video analytics and professional monitoring*


TYSONS, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Alarm.com (Nasdaq: ALRM), the leading platform for intelligently connected properties, today announced that its Remote Video Monitoring (RVM) Services integration with the CHeKT Monitoring Portal has been named a Security Sales & Integration (SSI) MVP Award winner. The award recognizes innovative products that advance the security industry and deliver meaningful value to integrators, monitoring centers, and end customers.


Built on CHeKT’s industry‑leading platform for proactive visual security, the award honors the Alarm.com and CHeKT integration which brings Alarm.com’s advanced Video Analytics and intrusion events directly into CHeKT’s monitoring workflow, creating a unified, operator‑friendly experience.


“We’re excited to receive this recognition for the Alarm.com and CHeKT integration,” said Dan Kerzner, President of Platforms Business at Alarm.com. “CHeKT is the leading remote video monitoring solution in the industry, and we’ve enabled our partners to deliver professional video monitoring across virtually any property type, using the cameras and systems customers already have.”


**New Standard for Remote Video Monitoring**


The upcoming integration extends Alarm.com’s RVM and Visual Verification capabilities into CHeKT’s best‑in‑class remote video monitoring platform. This joint approach enables monitoring centers to visually verify activity in real time and respond with greater confidence.


Within a single interface, operators can review live and recorded video, view event snapshots, access integrated site maps, and engage using two-way audio. Alarm.com’s Advanced Video Analytics and ClearSight™ verification work with CHeKT’s monitoring workflows to help operators focus on meaningful events, reduce false alarms, and enable faster, more accurate responses during potential incidents.


**Value Across the Security Chain**


Examples of RVM in action include:


- **For homeowners and business owners:** RVM enables faster, more informed responses to suspicious activity, supported by real-time visual verification from professional monitoring teams.
- **For integrators and dealers:** The upcoming integration unlocks new recurring revenue opportunities through premium video monitoring services without requiring additional on‑site hardware. The solution works with existing Alarm.com‑connected cameras, simplifying deployment and scaling.
- **For monitoring centers:** The unified Alarm.com and CHeKT workflow reduces operator workload, minimizes false alarms, and provides richer context during events, empowering teams to assist customers more effectively.


**Designed for Efficiency and Customer Control**


The Alarm.com and CHeKT RVM integration is delivered as a turnkey, cloud‑based solution. Arming and disarming status sync between Alarm.com and CHeKT is handled in the cloud, eliminating the need for hardwired connections or additional video control panels. As demand for proactive video monitoring continues to grow, the collaboration helps security providers deliver scalable, operator-efficient services without requiring customers to replace existing hardware.


Customer transparency and control are built into the experience. During events, customers receive notifications and can actively participate using Cancel/Verify options in the app. Operator actions, such as live video access or two‑way audio use, are logged in the customer’s activity feed, and live view access can be removed on a camera‑by‑camera basis.


The SSI MVP Award winners were announced at PSA TEC, recognizing products that demonstrate technical innovation, market impact, and real-world value for security professionals.


Alarm.com’s Remote Video Monitoring and Visual Verification integration with CHeKT is currently in Beta release and will be available in Early Access this summer through Alarm.com integrators and dealers. For more information, visit[www.alarm.com](https://alarm.com/) .
