# Alarm.com Expands Video Security Portfolio with New AI Capabilities and Flexible Camera Solutions

*New AI‑driven software and expanded camera options help customers gain faster insights and extend protection across more properties*


TYSONS, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Alarm.com (Nasdaq: ALRM), the leading platform for intelligently connected properties, today announced significant updates to its video security suite, introducing new AI‑driven software capabilities alongside expanded camera hardware options. Together, AI Video Event Search, the ADC‑V731B Battery Spotlight Camera, and the ADC‑VDB775 Video Doorbell improve how residential and commercial customers search video, deploy cameras in more locations, and respond to activity across their properties.


“Our latest video updates are shaped by how customers and partners use the platform every day,” said Dan Kerzner, President of Platforms Business at Alarm.com. “They’re designed to help users get answers faster, extend coverage, and stay in control of their video.”


**Find What Matters Faster with AI Video Event Search** AI Video Event Search allows customers to quickly locate important video activity using natural‑language queries. Rather than scrolling through hours or days of footage, customers can simply describe what they’re looking for, such as “red pickup truck last week” or “grocery bag at front door Friday,” and Alarm.com will surface the most relevant saved clip thumbnails.


Designed as an opt‑in feature for security‑related use cases, AI Video Event Search helps customers investigate incidents, confirm deliveries, and manage daily operations more efficiently. Searches run only across saved clip thumbnails from the customer’s own Alarm.com‑integrated cameras (including supported third‑party devices) and results can be refined by date range or camera. Mobile users can also submit searches using voice‑to‑text.


AI Video Event Search is available now to U.S. customers on Premium Video and Commercial Video Plus service packages*.


**Extend Video Coverage with the ADC‑V731B Battery Spotlight Camera** The ADC‑V731B Battery Spotlight Camera expands video coverage to locations where wired power isn’t available, with optional solar panel support for extended operation and reduced maintenance. Designed for indoor and outdoor use in sheds, garages, outbuildings, and other hard‑to‑reach areas, the NDAA‑compliant camera delivers professional‑grade video security without the need for complex electrical work.


The ADC‑V731B captures clear 4MP video with a 140° horizontal field of view and supports color night viewing through an integrated spotlight. Two‑way audio enables real‑time interaction, while solar compatibility helps keep the camera powered for longer deployments with minimal battery changes.


As part of Alarm.com’s unified platform, the ADC‑V731B works alongside other video devices and services, giving customers consistent control over how video is recorded, reviewed, and shared to support core security and monitoring needs across properties.


**Smarter Front‑Door Security with the ADC‑VDB775 Video Doorbell** The ADC‑VDB775 Video Doorbell delivers a more personalized and proactive front‑door security experience. Designed for customers who want greater awareness at the entryway, the video doorbell combines high‑resolution video with advanced detection features to help surface important activity at the door.


When enabled on the ADC-VDB775, Familiar Face recognition* allows customers to identify known visitors and distinguish them from unfamiliar arrivals. Customers control which faces are enrolled and how alerts are generated, helping reduce unnecessary notifications while staying informed about relevant activity.


In addition, the doorbell supports detection of people, packages, vehicles, and animals, along with onboard microSD recording for continuous video capture. Proactive deterrence features such as two‑way audio and Perimeter Guard® alerts allow customers to respond to activity in real time, with optional AI Deterrence services available for added protection.


**A Unified Platform Built for Security and Trust** Alarm.com’s unified video platform brings intelligent software, flexible hardware, and professional monitoring together in a single experience designed to scale across homes and businesses. These updates are built to support real‑world security needs, helping customers act on video more efficiently while maintaining clear control over how their systems operate.


AI‑driven capabilities are purpose‑built to support security and monitoring functions, with features enabled at the customer’s discretion and used in the context in which video is generated. As a result, customers gain faster insights, broader coverage, and greater confidence in their video systems, while service providers benefit from new ways to expand deployments and offer advanced services on a platform designed for responsible use.


*AI Video Event Search and Familiar Face recognition are not available to customers in Illinois and certain other jurisdictions.
