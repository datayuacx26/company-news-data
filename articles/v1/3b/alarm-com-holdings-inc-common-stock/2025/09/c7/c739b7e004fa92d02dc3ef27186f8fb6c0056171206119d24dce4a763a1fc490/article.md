# Alarm.com Brings Pro-Grade Video Security to More Homes and Businesses with the New ADC-V516 Indoor Wi-Fi Camera

*Compact, affordable, and loaded with AI-powered features, the ADC-V516 makes professional video protection simple and accessible*


TYSONS, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Alarm.com (Nasdaq: ALRM), the leading platform for intelligently connected properties, today announced the ADC-V516, a compact and budget-friendly indoor Wi-Fi camera. As part of Alarm.com’s professional security ecosystem, the ADC-V516 makes advanced performance and trusted reliability accessible for more households and small businesses.


Alarm.com launches the ADC-V516 1080p Indoor Wi-Fi Camera.


The ADC-V516 pairs 1080p HDR video with intelligent video analytics, two-way audio, and proactive deterrence tools—all fully integrated with the Alarm.com ecosystem. Designed for quick, flexible installation on walls, ceilings, or shelves, it empowers customers to monitor what matters most from anywhere, at any time.


“With the ADC-V516, we’ve taken the intelligent video capabilities trusted in professional systems and made them simple, affordable, and accessible to everyone—from first-time homeowners to growing businesses,” said Dan Kerzner, President of Platforms Business at Alarm.com.


**Professional Features at an Entry-Level Price**


The ADC-V516 offers:


- **Clear, Detailed Video** – 1080p HD resolution with HDR and night vision for detailed footage day or night.


- **Smarter Detection** – AI-powered analytics distinguish people, animals, and vehicles for accurate identification in any lighting.


- **Two-Way Audio** – Instantly speak with visitors, family members, or employees.


- **Proactive Threat Response** – A full suite of proactive deterrence features including the Remote Video Monitoring Console, AI Deterrence, Perimeter Guard®, and more deter intruders before incidents escalate.


- **Flexible Recording Options** – Choose cloud recording, onboard storage, or integration with an Alarm.com Stream Video Recorder (SVR).


- **Seamless Ecosystem Integration** – Works with other Alarm.com security, automation, and video devices, all on one platform.


**Real-World Security, Made Simple**


- **At Home:** Receive a video alert confirming the kids arrived safely from school, then greet them through the camera’s two-way audio—even if you’re still at the office.


- **For Small Businesses:** Keep eyes on your café 24/7, use deterrent sounds to scare off after-hours intruders, and review any incident remotely, all while staying within budget.


**Peace of Mind with Professional Installation and Monitoring**


Unlike many DIY cameras, the ADC-V516 is deployed as part of a professionally installed, professionally monitored system across North America and select international markets. This integration enables advanced services such as Visual Verification and Remote Video Monitoring, helping reduce false alarm fees and ensuring priority response when it matters most.


For more information about the ADC-V516 or Alarm.com’s complete range of connected security cameras and services, visit www.alarm.com or contact your local Alarm.com service provider.
