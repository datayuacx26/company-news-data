# Blue Bird Teams Up With Xos to Unveil Electric Stepvan at 2024 ACT Expo

We, at Xos, are pleased to share in the[announcement of Blue Bird's all-electric stepvan](https://www.blue-bird.com/blue-bird-debuts-electric-step-van-at-2024-advanced-clean-transportation-act-expo/) , unveiled at the 2024 Advanced Clean Transportation (ACT) Expo. This collaboration between Xos,[Blue Bird Corporation](https://www.blue-bird.com/) , and[Morgan Olson](https://morganolson.com/) marks a significant step forward for commercial EVs. The prototype vehicle, built on a Class 5-6 stripped chassis, showcases our shared dedication to zero tailpipe emission transportation solutions.


At Xos, our commitment to innovation in commercial electric vehicles (EVs) since 2016 has been unwavering. Our involvement in this project highlights our proven expertise and reinforces our position as a leader in the electric truck manufacturing industry. The stepvan showcases Xos'[sophisticated powertrain technology](https://0.0.7.229/06/10/introducing-powered-by-xos-our-new-powertrain-division) , which has undergone continuous enhancements and[real-world deployment since 2018](https://www.xostrucks.com/testimonials) .


Blue Bird's prototype electric stepvan will be on display at the ACT Expo in the Blue Bird booth, hosted from May 20-23, 2024, at the Las Vegas Convention Center. This collaboration showcases the harmonious integration of Xos' fleet electrification expertise,[Blue Bird's](https://www.blue-bird.com/) vast experience in vehicle manufacturing, and[Morgan Olson's](https://morganolson.com/) superior work truck body construction. It highlights our combined strengths and commitment to enhancing last-mile delivery with innovative solutions.


This strategic partnership underscores our ongoing efforts to decarbonize commercial transportation and make fleet electrification more accessible and achievable for businesses. As the demand for commercial EVs continues to grow, Xos remains at the forefront, offering cutting-edge solutions that meet the evolving needs of the industry.
