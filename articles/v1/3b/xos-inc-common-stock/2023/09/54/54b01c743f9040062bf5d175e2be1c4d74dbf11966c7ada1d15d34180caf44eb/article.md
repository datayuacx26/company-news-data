# Save Big on the Xos Hub™ with California's CORE Incentive Project

Off-highway fleet operators in California have a unique chance to save thousands of dollars on their charging infrastructure projects. Recently,[the Xos Hub™](https://www.xostrucks.com/xes/#mobilecharging) by[Xos Energy Solutions](https://www.xostrucks.com/xes) has become eligible for the California Clean Air Resources Board's (CARB)[Clean Off-Road Equipment Voucher Incentive Project](https://californiacore.org/) (CORE). This initiative is designed to encourage the purchase or lease of zero-emission off-road equipment. If you're planning to invest in clean energy solutions, the Xos Hub is an excellent option to consider, and the CORE project can help you save a significant amount of money.


The CORE project provides a point-of-sale discount to help offset the high cost of zero-emission technology. The Xos Hub, in combination with the CORE incentive, can help customers save up to $160,000 on their purchase. This is a massive financial advantage for off-highway fleet operators who are keen to adopt eco-friendly solutions and need charging infrastructure right away.


The Xos Hub™ charges three off-highway Wiggins E-Bulls at SSA Pacific, Inc.


Importantly, there is no scrappage requirement when availing the incentive, allowing customers to repurpose their existing equipment while moving towards a cleaner future. Furthermore, additional funding is available for charging and fueling infrastructure, equipment deployed in disadvantaged communities, and small businesses. This makes the CORE project an all-around assistance that is highly beneficial to off-highway fleet operators.


Danny Marquez, the Director at Xos Energy Solutions, expressed excitement about being approved for the CORE project and the ability to offer the incentive to Xos customers. "We are committed to providing our customers with the best possible solutions to charge their fleets, and this incentive is a great way to help them save money and get charging quickly," he said.


The Xos Hub, combined with the CORE project, is an ideal solution for those intending to expedite their charging infrastructure projects quickly and cost-effectively. California has already begun allocating funds for CORE, and the time to invest in this excellent opportunity is now. If you're an off-highway fleet operator looking to adopt zero-emission technology, then you shouldn't wait any longer before availing of this incentive!


The CORE project is one of the most significant incentives available in California, and it's a major benefit to off-highway fleet operators in terms of charging infrastructure. The Xos Hub, being eligible for CORE, is an excellent opportunity for those wishing to save money and invest in sustainable equipment.
