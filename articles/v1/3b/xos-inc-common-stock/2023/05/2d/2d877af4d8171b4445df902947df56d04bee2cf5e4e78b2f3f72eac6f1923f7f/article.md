# Efficiently Streamline Your Fleet Management with Xosphere's Vehicle Inspection Feature

Performing daily vehicle inspections is crucial to maintain the excellent condition of fleets, minimizing accidents, and achieving peak performance. To meet these needs and streamline daily fleet management, Xosphere®, our intelligent fleet management platform, now includes a Vehicle Inspection feature.


The Vehicle Inspection feature tool is included for all Xosphere users which allows fleet operators to maintain digital records of each vehicle's condition, damage, or critical issues. This proactive step enables the identification and reporting of issues before they escalate into major problems, saving money and time. More importantly, it ensures enhanced safety for everyone on the road. Improve the efficiency of fleet management by streamlining the process with the new tool from Xosphere, available right now.


Xos is committed to delivering durable and reliable electric trucks and fleet electrification solutions to help its customers transition from internal combustion engine vehicles to battery-electric. Xosphere and the Vehicle Inspection feature are prime examples of that commitment. Fleet operators seeking to experience the benefits of this feature can easily access it by logging in to their Xosphere account, downloading the user-friendly Xosphere Go mobile app, or contacting a knowledgeable Xos representative for more information on Xosphere. To learn more, visit[www.xostrucks.com/xosphere](http://www.xostrucks.com/xosphere) .
