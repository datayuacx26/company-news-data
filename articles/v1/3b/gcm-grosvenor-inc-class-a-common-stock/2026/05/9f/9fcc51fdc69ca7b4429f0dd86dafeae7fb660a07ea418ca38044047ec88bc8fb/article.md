# GCM Grosvenor to Present at the William Blair 46th Annual Growth Stock Conference on June 2, 2026

CHICAGO, May 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — GCM Grosvenor (Nasdaq: GCMG), a global alternative asset management solutions provider, announced today that Michael Sacks, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of GCM Grosvenor, will present at the William Blair 46th Annual Growth Stock Conference on Tuesday, June 2 at 1:20PM CDT.


A link to the live audio webcast of the presentation will be available on GCM Grosvenor’s[public shareholders website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=HIlnQTus26eLn6_8x0R493lVrSpcWRn-ECe51F7LYdY9aXKAX9jNYY_RQ84Pb_rxHbalPCKxvxapTyaX2YRcg-7avZEEGqKy5TfZV7-chNlXLXDSYz14aCFTpRC9Z_6bge6swvH7TYs-xB2EQ6siDw==) and[the event website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=T9xLFIbFckS8uItQbtJTI5n-GRK6z1LSXlh_xJ942N-iun5oAlOqEJ_-kKTdivGKvvBCHdP74NFVmKfNfYuq9nOmxh34LrI5ljG-qRMDjgqhgQq9fkmfFbYfjiMXTJnsnRhhblR4UBinFv4IUq7NhUclCRYnGR52e8iyZw_0C_E=) . For those unable to listen to the live audio webcast, a replay will be available for 90 days following the presentation.


**About GCM Grosvenor**


GCM Grosvenor (Nasdaq: GCMG) is a global alternative asset management solutions provider with approximately $91 billion in assets under management across private equity, infrastructure, real estate, credit, and absolute return investment strategies. The firm has specialized in alternatives for more than 50 years and is dedicated to delivering value for clients by leveraging its cross-asset class and flexible investment platform.


GCM Grosvenor’s experienced team of approximately 560 professionals serves a global client base of institutional and individual investors. The firm is headquartered in Chicago, with offices in New York, Toronto, London, Frankfurt, Tokyo, Hong Kong, Seoul and Sydney.
