# Join the tech revolution

The circular economy is a new approach to our current linear model of "take-make-waste". It aims to minimise waste and protect our environment throughout the entire product life cycle. In essence, it is a regenerative system where resources are kept in use for as long as possible, minimising waste and pollution in the process.


E-waste refers to electronic waste generated from our everyday gadgets such as smartphones, laptops, and tablets. According to the Ministry for Sustainability and the Environment, Singapore generates about[60,000 tonnes of e-waste every year](https://go.nowcircular.sg/blog/change-how-you-see-tech/clkn/https/www.towardszerowaste.gov.sg/ewaste/?ref=blog.nowcircular.sg) (or the equivalent of 70 mobile phones per person). Most e-waste ends up in landfills, polluting the soil, air, and water. This is where the circular economy comes in.


The idea behind the circular economy is to create a closed-loop system where products can be recycled, repaired, or refurbished, giving them a new life instead of ending up in landfills. Tech devices such as iPhones and laptops are perfect candidates for circularity as they are made out of valuable materials. If these materials were collected effectively, they could be used to make new devices with the same quality as a new one.
