# Top 5: Sustainable toys for kids

Let's dive into our list of the top 5 eco-friendly toys for kids, where sustainability and entertainment beautifully intertwine. Ok, there are more than 5 but we couldn't resist!


**Sustainable wooden toys:** Wooden toys have always been a beloved classic, but their eco-friendly nature makes them a modern-day must-have. These sustainable toys, made from renewable sources like bamboo and using non-toxic finishes, offer a safe and environmentally responsible option for your child's playtime. From educational wooden puzzles to creative balance boards, they're a fantastic way to promote eco-awareness from a young age.


**Recycled and recyclable toys:** Embrace the revolution of recycled toys that teach kids about sustainability. Toys made from recycled plastics and materials like cardboard are not just fun; they're a lesson in environmental stewardship. They also highlight the importance of the circular economy in everyday life, making them a smart choice.


**Green Toys - play with a purpose:** Discover the joy of upcycled play with Green Toys. These toys, crafted from recycled materials like milk jugs, are not only safe (being BPA and phthalate-free) but also a testament to sustainable living. Their diverse range, from eco-friendly toy trucks to charming tea sets, makes them a fantastic choice for environmentally aware parents.


Shop Green Toys on[Lazada](https://www.lazada.sg/green-toys/?ref=blog.nowcircular.sg)


**PlanToys - eco-innovative playtime solutions:** PlanToys takes sustainability to the next level with their eco-friendly toys made from rubberwood trees. Their use of non-toxic, water-based dyes and non-formaldehyde glue ensures these toys are safe for kids and kind to the planet. With a variety of options, including eco-friendly building blocks and dollhouses, PlanToys is leading the way in sustainable play.

It has the power to teach children about the beauty of flowers and vegetables, whilst also teaching them valuable life lessons like patience and self-confidence.
