# How to Use Our Scale: Innovative Ideas

Bottomless started because of a simple yet common problem: our founders love coffee but kept forgetting to buy more from their favorite roasters, forcing them to settle for generic supermarket coffee while waiting for their next shipment.


We’ve all been there - waddling groggily into the kitchen, already angered by the early hour, only to have your dreams of coffee crushed by an empty bag. 😣


#### Too Much of a Good Thing


Or on the flip side, they were getting too much of their favorite products. They loved Soylent and had a monthly subscription but couldn’t drink enough by the next shipment.


They kept receiving new packages in the mail and eventually had to throw out several expired bottles.


They knew there had to be a better way.


#### **The Solution**


This led to the design of our original scale, which was created to ensure they would never run out of their favorite products again.


Bottomless started as a reordering solution for coffee only. It was the perfect product to test the tech because of its popularity, varying usage, and packaging.


As we expanded beyond coffee to include pet food, supplements, sodas, and more, we developed a range of scales and innovative products like smart containers, trays, and wine racks to meet these diverse needs.


### **How to Use Our Devices**


Our devices are easy to use. We ship you a device with your first subscription order. Here’s what you’ll receive:


- Your new smart scale (or container), of course!


- an introduction


- a charger


- setup instructions with FAQs


- and a sticker… if you’re lucky 😉


Setting up the device takes just one minute. For detailed guidance and troubleshooting, visit[our support page](https://support.bottomless.com/en/articles/909568) .


#### **More Information About Our Device**


Our devices learn your habits! They are designed to learn from your usage patterns to predict when you’ll need a refill. I love how my scale feels like a personal shopper, always knowing what I need.


Its battery lasts about 1.5 years, so you might not need the charger often— be sure to store it somewhere safe, like a random junk drawer. 😏


One of my favorite features is that you can place any container on the scale. The setup guide includes instructions on this, allowing for endless creativity, like the stories I will share below!


#### **Our Muse**


Our customers inspire us to continue to create. They come up with great ideas that have us kicking ourselves for not thinking of them sooner.


One of the reasons that I love our devices is that they become longtime friends in your kitchens! This allows you to get to know the devices as intimately as we do, and because of that, you come up with some creative ideas.
