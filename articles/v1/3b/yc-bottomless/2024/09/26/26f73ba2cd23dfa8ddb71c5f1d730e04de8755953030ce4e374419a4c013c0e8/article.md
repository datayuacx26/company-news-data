# Rotation Engine: A New Era for Subscriptions

Subscription models are the cornerstone of modern brands, providing a consistent revenue stream while driving strong customer loyalty. Retention is the ultimate goal, after all.


If you’ve ever had customers asking for variety with every order, leaving you to frantically curate product lists and make selections each time, you're in good company! Many brands find themselves facing the same struggle.


We’ve been there ourselves—like the time we accidentally sent the same bag of coffee to a customer twice in a row—yikes!


That’s when we knew it was time to revolutionize the subscription game.


### **Introducing** **The** **Rotation Engine**


If you’re looking to transform a list of products into a seamless rotation with automated selections for every order, this is just the thing you need!


Whether you're managing rotations for wet cat food, loose tea varieties, or protein powder flavors, the Rotation Engine streamlines the fulfillment process and keeps customers happy with a consistent variety of products.


### **Features at a Glance**


**Creating the Rotation Product:** Once you’ve compiled a list of products to rotate through, you can create the rotation product in Shopify and add it to your shop.
