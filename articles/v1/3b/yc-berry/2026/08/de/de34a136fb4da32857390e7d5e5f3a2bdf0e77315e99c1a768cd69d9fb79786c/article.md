That gap matters more than it sounds. Speed of service has become one of the clearest differentiators in QSR, and the brands winning right now are the ones that stopped estimating and started measuring. A few extra cars served per hour, per store, sounds small in isolation. Multiplied across a year, across a portfolio, it isn't.

Those are real, measured outcomes. But every restaurant is different, and the honest answer to "what would this be worth for my stores?" depends on a handful of specifics: how many locations you run, how much faster your lanes could realistically move, and what your average ticket looks like.
