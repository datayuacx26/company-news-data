# What a 1,000-Location Vision AI Rollout Signals for QSR

**🚀 We’re thrilled to announce our brand-wide partnership with Culver’s — deploying our Vision AI platform across all 1,000+ locations nationwide.**


This is a milestone we’re incredibly proud of — and it reflects something bigger happening across the industry: Vision AI is no longer a pilot program. It’s becoming core infrastructure for QSR brands that want to compete at the highest level.


## What We’re Bringing to Culver’s


Across every Culver’s location, our platform delivers real-time visibility into what’s actually happening on the floor — so teams can stop reacting and start leading. That means live measurement of:


- Service execution quality
- Vehicle flow and throughput
- Performance metrics across drive-thru, dine-in, and pickup
- All within a privacy-first framework that protects guests and team members


Our technology plugs directly into existing workflows and POS ecosystems — so there’s no ripping and replacing. Operators at every level get the intelligence they need, right when they need it.


## In Culver’s Own Words


> *“Delivering real, genuine hospitality and a consistently excellent experience requires deep insight into every interaction. Berry AI’s vision technology will elevate our guests’ experiences by empowering our restaurant teams to make informed decisions that improve speed and ensure accuracy.”* **— Rich Modjeski, Chief Operating Officer, Culver’s**


## The Results We’ve Seen Across Deployments


Our partners across the QSR industry have seen real, measurable impact:


- **📈 Up to 70%** jumps in drive-thru comps
- **⏱️ 20–40%** reduction in drive-thru service times
- **🚗 Up to 20%** improvement in overall throughput


These aren’t theoretical projections. They’re the result of operators having the right data, at the right time, to make better calls on the floor.
