# ASTRA™ Arvis® Wins Auggie Award

We’re honored to share that ASTRA™ Arvis® has won the 2026 AWE Auggie Award for Best Healthcare & Wellness Solution.


AWE (Augmented World Expo) Auggie Awardsrecognize innovative solutions across the XR industry. Enovis' ASTRA™ Arvis® was nominated in the “Best Healthcare & Wellness Solution” category, and competed against mixed reality rehabilitation from ACS surgery, XR fitness and wellness apps, AR distraction for donating blood, and more.


ASTRA™ Arvis®, our pioneering augmented reality (AR) technology designed specifically for orthopedics, was recognized for the best use of spatial computing in healthcare—spanning hospitals and operating rooms, medical training, diagnosis and telemedicine, patient and home care, rehabilitation, and wellness.


Born from the concepts and principles of rocket science, the ASTRA™ Arvis® wearable tech simplifies OR setup, maximizes space, and is fully controlled by surgeons through subtle movement and voice command.
