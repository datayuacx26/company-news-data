# Enovis Healthcare Solutions recognized with industry award

We’re proud to share that Enovis™ Healthcare Solutions has been recognized as a winner in the[2026 TAG Awards for MedTech](https://www.tagawards.co/news/announcing-the-class-of-2026-tag-awards-for-medtech-life-scieences-and-biotech/) , an industry program produced by The Aebli Group. This year’s awards celebrate organizations that are pushing the boundaries of innovation across MedTech, Life Sciences, and BioTech - turning bold ideas into real-world impact.


Among a highly competitive field, Enovis™ Healthcare Solutions received the top honor in the Patient Transparency category for our +VeriPro® software, which provides real-time insurance verification for patient eligibility. With +VeriPro® patients are presented up-front with their durable medical equipment (DME) estimated patient responsibility so that they can make more informed decisions about the cost of their care. This recognition highlights our continued commitment to delivering solutions that not only meet the needs of today’s healthcare landscape, but actively improve outcomes for patients, providers and partners.


The TAG Awards were established to spotlight companies that are transforming innovation into action. Winners are selected by a panel of experienced industry judges and evaluated against rigorous criteria, including design excellence, real-world relevance, market value, usability, and measurable impact.


Earning this distinction reinforces the strength of our mission and the dedication of our teams. The +VeriPro® platform exemplifies how we are leveraging technology to enhance transparency, streamline processes, and build trust across the continuum of care.
