# NSN Cycling Team announces strategic partnership with Compex to elevate performance and recovery

NSN Cycling Team is proud to announce a new partnership with Compex, a world leader in portable EMS muscle stimulators and recovery technology. Compex, part of the global medical technology company Enovis, has been helping athletes optimize performance and recovery for more than 40 years.


As the Official Recovery Partner of NSN Cycling Team, Compex is equipping riders with its extensive range of recovery equipment and tools to support training, race preparation, and post-competition recovery, including the EMS Muscle Stimulator with TENS, Fixx™️ Massage Guns, and Ayre™️ Compression Boots. The partnership reflects a shared dedication to innovation, performance, and helping athletes perform at their highest level, backed by Compex’s unparalleled expertise with 40 years in the industry.


“We are thrilled to partner with Compex, a true industry leader in muscle stimulation and TENS technology,” explained NSN Cycling Team Medical Coordinator Andrew Gerrans. “Their devices will play an integral role in our rehabilitation protocols, helping our athletes bounce back quickly from injury. On top of that, their recovery boots and massage guns have already become essential fixtures in our riders' daily Tour de France routines.”


Designed for athletes on the go, Compex’s product line is particularly suited to the long travel days at the Tour de France, allowing riders to begin their recovery the moment they get on the team bus post-stage.


Arnaud Marie, Compex General Manager, said "At Compex, we are committed to helping athletes recover smarter, train harder, and perform at their highest level. NSN Cycling Team represents the same relentless pursuit of excellence that has driven our innovation for over 40 years. We are proud to support their riders with proven recovery and electrical muscle stimulation technology that can make a real difference throughout the demands of a long cycling season, from daily training sessions to the world's biggest races."


### About Compex


Compex is a global leader in electrical muscle stimulation (EMS) technology, pioneering science backed solutions that support performance, recovery and rehabilitation. Founded in Switzerland in 1986, Compex has become a trusted partner for professional athletes, healthcare professionals and active individuals worldwide. Its clinically proven EMS technology is designed to activate muscles more effectively, support training efficiency, aid recovery and help manage pain, making Compex an essential tool across sports performance, fitness and rehabilitation.


Today, Compex is part of Enovis, a global medical technology innovator dedicated to improving lives by developing clinically differentiated solutions that enhance patient outcomes and restore motion for life.

### About NSN Cycling Team


NSN Cycling Team is a Swiss-Spanish cycling team racing in the WorldTour, the highest level of professional cycling. Born from a joint collaboration between global sports and event company NSN (Never Say Never), co-founded by Spanish football legend Andrés Iniesta, and Swiss investment firm Stoneweg, the team races under a Swiss flag, and operates from Spain. As a WorldTour team, the team races around 250 days a year in over 20 countries, including the Giro d’Italia, Tour de France, and Vuelta a España. With two teams in the NSN family - NSN Cycling Team and NSN Development Team - the team is home to 48 cyclists from around the world, including history maker Biniam Girmay, and around 120 support staff, making it one of the most international cycling teams in the peloton.
