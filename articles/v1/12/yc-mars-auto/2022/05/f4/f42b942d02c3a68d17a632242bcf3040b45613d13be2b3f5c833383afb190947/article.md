Today we’re excited to announce our $12M Series A round, led by[GFT Ventures](https://www.gft.vc/) . Existing investors Mirae Asset Ventures and Zeno Ventures have also participated in the round, together with Ian Rust (CEO, SixWheel, previously Founding Engineer of Cruise), Jarrett Streebin (CEO, EasyPost), Charles Songhurst (former Head of Strategy at Microsoft), and many other great entrepreneurs. This was our first equity round after participating in Y Combinator, and a meaningful milestone for the company.

The autonomous vehicle landscape has seen its ups and downs since we started this company, and it seems each day there are more and more AV companies pivoting to trucks. While there are skeptics about autonomous technology in general, our team has been focused on making our truck drive better each day. There is still a lot of work to be done, but increasingly we’re becoming confident in our data driven approach to solve autonomous driving.


With the new capital in hand, we’re ready to double-down on our development efforts as well as expand our truck fleet operations. In many ways it feels like the beginning of building something new again. We’re aggressively hiring across many positions, and this is a great time to join our team and work on one of the toughest engineering challenges of this generation. Our team size is still very lean (and we intend to keep it that way), and we have an engineering-driven culture across the whole company. Check our[website](https://marsauto.com/careers) for current open positions, and our[hiring post (Korean)](https://www.notion.so/marsauto/Mars-Auto-is-hiring-de1d2a9da06f4845a6100b82d692a689) for more details.


We’re grateful to all the people who believed in us, and also to the team who made all this possible.
