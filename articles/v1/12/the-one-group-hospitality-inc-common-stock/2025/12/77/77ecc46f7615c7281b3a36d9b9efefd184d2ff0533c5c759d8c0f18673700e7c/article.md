# THE ONE GROUP PARTNERS WITH FLOCK FOODS TO LAUNCH BENIHANA-INSPIRED CHICKEN SNACK CHIPS

# THE ONE GROUP PARTNERS WITH FLOCK FOODS TO LAUNCH BENIHANA-INSPIRED CHICKEN SNACK CHIPS


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_e0b1ba3ff5a1dd02ef3dfd8dcfe9de73/togrp/news/2025-12-09_THE_ONE_GROUP_PARTNERS_WITH_FLOCK_FOODS_TO_LAUNCH__553.pdf) December 09, 2025


Benihana Teriyaki Flock Crisps — a high-protein, delicious, low-carb snack — now available nationwide


**DENVER (12/9/25)** —The ONE Group is proud to unveil a new collaboration between **[Benihana](http://www.benihana.com/)** , the nation’s leading operator of teppanyaki restaurants, and **[Flock Foods](https://flockfoods.com/)** to develop the brand’s first-ever direct-to-consumer chicken snack, thoughtfully inspired by the brand’s signature flavors. The partnership combines Flock Foods’ high-protein, better-for-you snacks with Benihana’s culinary heritage, creating a snack fans can enjoy anytime, anywhere.


“This collaboration marks an exciting milestone as we continue expanding the Benihana experience beyond our restaurant walls. By partnering with Flock Foods and their innovative approach to better-for-you snacking, we’re giving fans a convenient new way to enjoy the taste they love anytime, anywhere,” remarked Emanuel “Manny” Hilario, President and CEO of The ONE Group.


Available now, the new Benihana x Flock Foods chicken snack can be purchased online at[FlockFoods.com](https://flockfoods.com/products/teriyaki) , allowing for an easy and convenient way to bring the signature Benihana taste home. The snacks will be available at select retailers in early 2026. Made from 100% real chicken and crafted with a bold teriyaki flavor profile, the Flock Chicken Skin Crisps are crispy and crunch with the texture of a potato chip, without the carbs, each serving has 13 grams of protein, one carb and less than one gram of sugar, the ultimate guilt free, keto-friendly, low carb and high protein snack.


“Partnering with Benihana gave us the opportunity to integrate their iconic teppanyaki flavors into a high protein snack that aligns with our mission to deliver exceptionally flavorful chicken snacks to consumers everywhere,” said Jake Drwal, COO of Flock Foods. “We’re thrilled to introduce this bold new addition that captures the unmistakable Benihana experience in every bite.”


This latest launch builds on Benihana’s growing portfolio of direct-to-consumer offerings, reinforcing the brand’s commitment to giving fans more ways to enjoy Benihana beyond the restaurant.

**About Benihana**


Benihana, through its subsidiaries, is the nation’s leading operator of teppanyaki and sushi restaurants with more than 100 restaurants operating under the brands BENIHANA® and RA SUSHI®, including franchised Benihana restaurants in the United States, the Caribbean, Central America, and South America. Additional information about Benihana can be found at[www.benihana.com](http://www.benihana.com/) .


**About The ONE Group**


The ONE Group Hospitality, Inc. (Nasdaq: STKS) is an international restaurant company that develops and operates upscale and polished casual, high-energy restaurants and lounges and provides hospitality management services for hotels, casinos and other high-end venues both in the U.S. and internationally. The ONE Group is recognized as one of “America’s Greatest Companies” (NEWSWEEK, 2025) and Benihana honored as Forbes Best Brands for Value. The ONE Group’s focus is to be the global leader in Vibe Dining, and its primary restaurant brands and operations are:


- STK, a modern twist on the American steakhouse concept with restaurants in major metropolitan cities in the U.S., Europe and the Middle East, featuring premium steaks, seafood and specialty cocktails in an energetic upscale atmosphere.
- Benihana, an interactive dining destination with highly skilled chefs preparing food right in front of guests and served in an energetic atmosphere alongside fresh sushi and innovative cocktails. The Company franchises Benihanas in the U.S., Caribbean, Central America, and South America.
- Samurai, an interactive dining experience located in sunny Miami, FL, provides a distinctive dining experience where skilled personal chefs masterfully perform the ancient art of teppanyaki right before your eyes.
- Kona Grill, a polished casual, bar-centric grill concept with restaurants in the U.S., featuring American favorites, award-winning sushi, and specialty cocktails in an upscale casual atmosphere.

- Benihana Express, a small footprint casual concept showcasing the best of Benihana but without teppanyaki tables or bar.
- RA Sushi, a Japanese cuisine concept that offers a fun-filled, bar-forward, upbeat, and vibrant dining atmosphere with restaurants in the U.S. anchored by creative sushi, inventive drinks, and outstanding service.
- ONE Hospitality, The ONE Group’s food and beverage hospitality services business develops, manages and operates premier restaurants and turnkey food and beverage services within high-end hotels and casinos currently operating venues in the U.S. and Europe.
