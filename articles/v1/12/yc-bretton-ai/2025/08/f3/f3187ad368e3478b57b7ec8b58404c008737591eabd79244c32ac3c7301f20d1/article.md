# Bretton AI's Business Due Diligence product is now live in Alloy

We’re excited to announce that our **Business Due Diligence product is now live and available within**[Alloy](https://www.alloy.com/) , marking a major step forward in helping compliance and product teams automate business onboarding with speed and confidence.


This launch follows a close partnership with Alloy’s team and reflects our shared mission: helping financial institutions scale faster without compromising on risk.


### Why Business Due Diligence still feels broken


Onboarding a new business account often means navigating fragmented data, long review cycles, and mounting pressure on compliance teams to “just get it right.” Many financial institutions still rely on manual checks, static rules, or black-box scoring systems that make it hard to understand or explain decisions, let alone operationalize consistent policies.


That’s where Bretton AI comes in.


### A new approach to KYB: powered by intelligent agents


Bretton AI’s Business Due Diligence product leverages intelligent agents to automate KYB workflows by:


- Classifying and interpreting supporting documents
- Extracting key facts and context using LLMs
- Generating AI-driven summaries and risk indicators
- Explaining risk decisions in plain language


The result? Teams can focus on the edge cases, not the routine. Business applicants get faster decisions, and compliance teams maintain transparency and control.


### Now seamlessly integrated in alloy


Alloy customers can now access Bretton AI's Business Due Diligence product directly within their existing workflows.


Through the integration, teams can:


- Automate business verification and risk assessment
- Reduce manual review time by up to **70%** ‍
- Gain clearer visibility into why an application is high- or low-risk
- Maintain audit-ready transparency at every step


Alloy has long been the infrastructure powering identity decisions for top financial institutions and fintechs. We’re proud to enhance that experience with actionable AI KYB capabilities - no additional lift required.


### Looking ahead


This is just the beginning. We're continuing to evolve our platform with new AI agents, deeper integrations, and expanded coverage for global business onboarding.
