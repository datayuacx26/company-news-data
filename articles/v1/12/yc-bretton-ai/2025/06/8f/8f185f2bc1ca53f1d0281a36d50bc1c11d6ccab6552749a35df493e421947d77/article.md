# Streamlining KYB: Bretton AI partners with Middesk to accelerate due diligence

#### **Bretton AI is excited to announce our new partnership with Middesk**


As compliance demands increase and businesses seek faster, more reliable ways to verify their partners and customers, enhanced due diligence needs to be smarter and more automated. Today, Bretton AIand Middesk are proud to share a strategic partnership that helps compliance teams streamline KYB and business verification by bringing trusted licensing, registration, and ownership data directly into enhanced due diligence workflows.


We first connected with Middesk through shared customers and immediately saw how well our solutions complement each other. By integrating Middesk’s authoritative business data with Bretton AI's automation capabilities, compliance teams can now automatically pull verified information into their reviews accelerating decision making and reducing manual effort.


##### Bringing value to compliance teams


With this integration compliance teams can expect to:


- Accelerate enhanced due diligence workflows
- Reduce manual data collection and errors
- Keep risk profiles current with automatically updated business data

This partnership underscores Bretton AI and Middesk's shared commitment to making compliance more efficient and reliable.
