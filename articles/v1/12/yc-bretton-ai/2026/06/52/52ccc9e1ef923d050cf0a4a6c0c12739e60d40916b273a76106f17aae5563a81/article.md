# Bretton AI partners with Equifax to streamline compliance operations

*Compliance teams can now bring trusted Equifax data directly into Bretton AI's investigation workflows, reducing manual effort, improving consistency, and producing audit-ready outputs for their day-to-day operations.*


The pressure is real: volumes are growing, regulatory expectations are rising, and analysts spend too much time gathering information that should already be at their fingertips. This partnership is about fixing that; helping teams apply the data they already trust, more efficiently and at scale.


## Bringing Equifax data into compliance workflows


Most financial institutions already rely on Equifax for identity, risk, and verification. The challenge is rarely access to data. It is putting that data to work consistently and efficiently inside compliance workflows.


Through this partnership, Equifax customers can connect their existing data directly to Bretton AI's compliance automation platform. The result is a more connected investigation experience, without adding new systems or duplicating effort.


### **Capabilities**


**Seamless data integration** : Equifax data flows directly into investigations, eliminating manual lookups.


**Reduced analyst workload** : Less time spent gathering and reconciling information across sources.


**Consistent policy application** : Reviews follow the same logic every time, regardless of analyst or volume.


**Regulator-ready documentation** : Every decision is explainable, auditable, and traceable.


Bretton is built to work alongside existing compliance tools helping teams get more from the data they already have, without disrupting the stack they already run.


## Turning trusted data into decision-ready outcomes


Bretton AI automates compliance work that is time intensive by nature: screening reviews, transaction monitoring, and customer due diligence. The results are measurable. Customers have cut Enhanced Due Diligence case handle time by over 80%. Some customers have seen transaction monitoring triage drop from more than 30 minutes to under five.


By bringing Equifax data into these workflows, teams move through cases faster while keeping full control over policy, reasoning, and outcomes. Every decision is explainable and audit-ready built on Bretton's Trust Infrastructure, which provides model risk management, continuous AI evaluation, and rigorous QA testing.


The result is faster case resolution, a smaller backlog, and more consistent decisions without trading away explainability or regulatory alignment.


## Designed for real world compliance operations


This partnership is built with operational reality in mind. Bretton AI integrates into existing case management and review processes, supporting both automated decisions and analyst oversight. Equifax data remains the trusted input. Bretton helps teams apply it more efficiently, at scale, and with greater confidence.


For institutions operating under OCC, FDIC, or Federal Reserve oversight, that combination of speed, consistency, and auditability matters. Bretton agents are regulator-aligned from day one.


## A practical step toward modernized compliance


Compliance volumes will keep growing. Expectations will keep rising. Teams need solutions that help them do more with the tools and data they already have, not more systems to manage.


The Bretton AI and Equifax partnership is focused on exactly that: practical integration, operational efficiency, and decision ready outcomes. For Equifax customers looking to reduce investigation time and scale compliance operations without disrupting their existing environment, this integration offers a clear path forward.
