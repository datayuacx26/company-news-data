Compliance investigations often hinge on how well teams can interpret complex risk signals, connect disparate information, and document their reasoning in a way that stands up to regulatory scrutiny. LexisNexis® Risk Solutions plays a critical role in these moments, particularly in sanctions reviews, PEP assessments, adverse media analysis, and enhanced due diligence investigations.


Bretton AI has allied with LexisNexis® Risk Solutions to help compliance teams apply this investigative data more consistently and efficiently within their day-to-day workflows.


### **Supporting Investigation-Led Compliance Work**


LexisNexis® Risk Solutions is commonly used when a deeper context is needed to assess risk and support escalation or clearance decisions. Analysts rely on this data to understand relationships, evaluate adverse information, and form well-supported conclusions.


Through our combined efforts with LexisNexis® Risk Solutions, customers can integrate their existing data directly into Bretton AI’s compliance automation platform, allowing investigative findings to be incorporated into active cases rather than reviewed in isolation.


#### **This helps teams:**


- Apply LexisNexis® Risk Solutions data more consistently across investigations
- Reduce manual synthesis of complex research
- Improve documentation quality and decision traceability
- Produce regulator-ready narratives aligned to internal SOPs


Bretton does not replace investigative judgment. It helps structure, apply, and document judgment at scale.


### **Built Around Customer-Defined Policies**


Bretton operates as a processor on behalf of its customers and executes investigations according to each institution’s policies, risk thresholds, and procedures.


LexisNexis® Risk Solutions data is used only as directed by the customer. Bretton helps structure and document investigative work while helping ensuring decisions remain policy driven, explainable, and auditable.


### **Supporting Investigation Quality at Scale**


By incorporating LexisNexis® Risk Solutions data directly into investigation workflows, compliance teams can reduce variability across analysts while preserving the depth and context required for high-risk decisions.


The result is faster investigations, clearer rationale behind outcomes, and stronger audit trails that help meet regulatory expectations.


For LexisNexis® Risk Solutions customers looking to scale investigation capacity without compromising rigor, the alliance with Bretton AI provides a practical path forward.
