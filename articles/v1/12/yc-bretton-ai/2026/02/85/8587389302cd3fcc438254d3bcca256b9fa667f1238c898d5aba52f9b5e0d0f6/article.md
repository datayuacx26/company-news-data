# Maximizing ROI when evaluating agentic AI solutions for compliance

Agentic AI is changing how financial institutions handle compliance. These agents investigate alerts, compile evidence, and produce audit-ready outputs—work that has traditionally required large teams of analysts. Enterprise banks spend an estimated $500 million to $1 billion annually on these primarily manual efforts, much of which involves clearing alerts that never should have been flagged.


While agentic AI has entered the market quickly, the products that leverage this technology vary widely in architecture, capabilities, and regulatory readiness. Knowing how to find a solution that aligns with your operational needs is its own challenge, let alone finding one that will maximize your return on investment.


Our white paper,[The Compliance Leader's Guide to Agentic AI](https://www.bretton.com/compliance-leaders-guide-download) , helps clarify that decision. But a few key distinctions are important to understand before you start evaluating vendors.


## Regulatory rigor varies widely across solutions


Not every agentic AI solution is built to withstand regulatory scrutiny. The level of auditability, model validation, and ongoing monitoring differs significantly across products—despite vendors' use of similar language to describe their solution’s capabilities.


For institutions regulated by the OCC, FDIC, and Federal Reserve, these differences directly affect how much additional governance infrastructure you'll need beyond what the product provides. They also determine how prepared you'll be when regulatory examiners audit your AI agents' decisions. To ensure ROI, explainable compliance decisions are non-negotiable.


## Architecture shapes your operational flexibility


How an agentic AI solution connects to your existing systems affects time to value and adaptability as you scale. Some products make it easy to go to market right away and offer flexibility across capabilities like sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, and enhanced due diligence. Others take more time to integrate with existing systems and tie you to your provider’s roadmap rather than your own. The architecture you choose today will determine the speed and control you retain over your operations, as well as how easily you can expand automation as your needs grow.


## Scope determines your future capabilities


The depth and breadth of an agentic AI solution is an essential consideration for maximizing ROI because it will determine how much work you can actually automate. Some products handle a single workflow well. Others connect data and insights across your full operation. The depth of a solution matters immediately, but so does how far it can scale. Choosing a vendor that only delivers in one area becomes a constraint when you're ready to expand. Ongoing ROI depends on a solution that grows with your institution, not one you outgrow.


## The right solution turns compliance into a growth lever


As agentic AI matures, the compliance function is shifting from a scaling constraint to a strategic advantage. Financial institutions are already seeing ROI with faster investigations, lower operational costs, and the capacity to serve more customers without adding headcount. The evaluation you make now sets the foundation for how far your investment can ultimately go. With the right agentic AI provider, compliance enables long-term growth.
