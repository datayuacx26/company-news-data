# Tackling AML efficiency: Bretton AI partners with Sandbar for smarter compliance

#### ‍ **Bretton AIis thrilled to announce our new partnership with Sandbar!**


As financial crime grows more sophisticated, it's clear that banks and fintechs need more than just larger teams - they need to nurture a resilient, proactive culture of compliance. Today, Greenlite and Sandbar are proud to share a strategic partnership to help financial institutions flag suspicious activity; conduct thorough, auditable investigations; and strengthen their compliance programs across AML workflows.


We first connected with the Sandbar team at ACAMS this year and saw how naturally our solutions fit together. By combining Sandbar's real-time monitoring combined with Bretton AI's AI-driven investigative automation, we're enabling banks and fintechs to manage the entire investigation workflow with confidence.


As Brock Bontrager, CEO of Sandbar, put it:


> "This partnership lets us both demonstrate the power of smarter investigative tools in helping institutions stay a step ahead of financial crime."


##### Bringing Value to Compliance Teams


With this partnership, compliance teams can expect to:


- Reduce time spent on manual investigations
- Strengthen workflows aligned with regulatory policies
- Maintain a proactive, unified edge against financial crime


##### Powering the Future of AML Compliance


This partnership represents a key step in Bretto and Sandbar's shared commitment to helping financial institutions build efficient, resilient compliance programs. Together, we're paving the way for a future where compliance teams can tackle complex, evolving financial crime with ease.
