# Enterprise grade security: Bretton AI is now SOC 2 Type 2 certified

For banks and fintechs, security isn't just a feature; it's the foundation of trust and reliability. At Bretton AI, we're proud to announce a significant milestone in our commitment to unparalleled security - achieving SOC2 Type 2 certification. This achievement is more than just a badge; it's a testament to our unwavering dedication to safeguarding your data and ensuring the highest standards of privacy and security.


## Understanding SOC2 Type 2


SOC2 Type 2 is not just any certification; it's a comprehensive evaluation of a company's information security measures, focusing on the principles of security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. Achieving this certification means that Bretton AI has undergone an extensive audit, demonstrating our systems are designed to keep our clients' sensitive data secure and our operations reliable. Here's what the certification assesses:


- Security: Ensuring protection against unauthorized access and potential threats.
- Availability: Verifying our systems are accessible as committed.
- Processing Integrity: Confirming our processing is complete, valid, accurate, and timely.
- Confidentiality: Protecting information designated as confidential.
- Privacy: Safeguarding personal information in accordance with privacy principles.


Our SOC2 Type 2 certification is a clear signal to regulated financial institutions that we're not just meeting basic expectations; we're setting new benchmarks.


## Our commitment to security


Our journey to SOC2 Type 2 certification involved rigorous evaluations of our data handling practices, ensuring they meet the highest standards of security and compliance. This isn't a one-time achievement but a continuous commitment to excellence. We implement robust security measures, conduct regular audits, and foster a culture of transparency and accountability. This relentless pursuit of security excellence means that every interaction with our platform is built on a foundation of trust.


## Bringing AI to every financial institution


Our mission extends beyond securing data. We're on a path to transform compliance in finance by making AI accessible to every financial institution, regardless of its size. "We believe that AI has the potential to transform the financial sector, and we're here to bridge that gap. Our SOC2 Type 2 certification is a stepping stone towards building a future where every financial institution, empowered by AI, can thrive in a secure digital ecosystem," Alex Jin adds.
