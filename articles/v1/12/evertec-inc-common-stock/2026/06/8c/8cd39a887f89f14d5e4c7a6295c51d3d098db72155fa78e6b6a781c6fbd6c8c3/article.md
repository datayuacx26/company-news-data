# Acquiring: From Operational Function to Growth Engine in the Digital Economy

*Acquiring is becoming a strategic capability for driving growth, financial inclusion, and digital transformation in Colombia.*


In today’s digital economy, payments are no longer just the final step of a purchase. They have become a key factor influencing customer experience, conversion rates, and merchant performance.


A transaction approved at the right moment can mean a completed sale. An unnecessary decline can result in lost revenue and, in many cases, the loss of a customer altogether. For this reason, improvements in approval rates, platform stability, and security have a direct impact on business results, particularly in high-volume digital environments.


## **A New Approach to Payments**


For many years, acquiring was primarily associated with card acceptance. Today, that view is no longer sufficient. Modern acquiring encompasses authorization management, fraud prevention, payment orchestration, tokenization, authentication, and omnichannel payment experiences. Together, these capabilities influence both sales outcomes and the quality of the customer experience.


Digital consumers expect payments to work seamlessly: quickly, securely, and without disruption. For merchants, meeting those expectations requires more than infrastructure. It requires data, continuous optimization, and the ability to adapt operations to evolving consumer behavior.


## **Why Processing Matters** **?**


Processing remains one of the most critical layers of the acquiring value chain. It directly affects approval rates, risk management, service availability, and the ability to support different transaction types.


When processing works well, customers rarely notice it. When it doesn’t, the consequences are immediate: sales are lost, experiences are interrupted, and operational efficiency suffers.


This is why capabilities such as intelligent routing, real-time analytics, advanced authentication, and data-driven decision-making have become increasingly important. They help banks, fintechs, and merchants improve conversion rates without compromising security.


## **Trends Expanding the Role of Acquiring**


Embedded finance, open finance, and integrated payments are reshaping how payments fit into the customer journey.


Rather than being a separate step, payments are becoming part of the overall experience. The simpler, faster, and more contextual the payment experience is, the greater the value it can generate for both businesses and customers.


In this environment, card networks continue to play an essential role in establishing standards, enabling interoperability, and maintaining trust across the payments ecosystem.


## **Technology as Part of the Growth Strategy**


Banks, fintechs, cooperatives, and other financial organizations increasingly need solutions that combine security, scalability, and flexibility.


Technology partners play a critical role in supporting this evolution by connecting ecosystem participants, accelerating new business models, and helping organizations operate more efficiently in an increasingly digital market.


As a result, these relationships are becoming less transactional and more strategic. In many cases, technology is no longer just an operational enabler, it is part of the growth strategy itself.


## **New Channels, New Models**


The evolution of acquiring is also creating opportunities for models such as digital banking correspondents, where merchants, digital wallets, and platforms can serve as access points to financial services.


This expands the reach of the financial ecosystem while reducing dependence on traditional banking infrastructure. It also creates new opportunities for financial inclusion and for payment experiences that better reflect the realities of local markets.


## **The Future of the Payments Ecosystem**


Digitalization continues to reshape the relationship between businesses and consumers. As a result, acquiring is becoming increasingly important because of its direct influence on growth, conversion, and competitiveness.


As digital payments continue to expand across Latin America, opportunities for innovation and value creation will continue to grow. Competitive advantage will increasingly depend on the ability to turn every transaction into an opportunity to improve customer experience, strengthen revenue performance, and support the evolution of the digital economy.
