As organizations strive to modernize their IT environments, the need for practical, hands-on expertise has never been greater. Broadcom expects every Broadcom Knight to have an environment, a solution lab, where they can demonstrate the value of VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) and their role as a Knight. The Broadcom team built the Reference Architecture (RA) of the solution lab, providing a foundation that partners could use to customize and extend to meet the specific needs of their Knights and the customers that they support.


With Broadcom’s reference architecture, partners can build large, shared solution labs for all their Knights while others design smaller or even home labs. 27Virtual built a solution lab for our use that is accessible to all 27 Virtual Knights.

Broadcom is empowering its partners to help organizations meet these challenges. The VCF Knights Solution Lab is a direct response to this endeavor, providing a platform where partners can gain the hands-on experience required to deliver real valuable business outcomes.

## Inside the 27 Virtual VCF Knights Solution Lab


The VCF Knights Solution Lab kit documents the step-by-step process of building a 3-node VCF solution lab with a focus on hands-on deployment and configuration. It covers hardware selection (MINISFORUM MS-A2 nodes, MikroTik CRS309 switch), network setup with multiple VLANs, and detailed ESXi and VCF 9.0.2 installation procedures. The guide includes practical troubleshooting tips, lessons learned (such as hardware compatibility and network configuration), and emphasizes the value of the Broadcom Knights program for technical enablement and customer solutions. The result is a fully functional VCF lab environment suitable for demonstration, learning, and testing most of the advanced VMware VCF technologies.


The VCF Knights Solution Lab provides a practical, repeatable environment where Broadcom VCF Knights can build, test, validate, and showcase VMware Cloud Foundation solutions. This lab is not just about standing up infrastructure; it enables partners to:


- **Accelerate Learning:** Partners can deepen their technical skills by working directly with VCF in a controlled, hands-on setting.
- **Validate Solutions:** The lab allows for real-world testing of architectures and use cases, ensuring interoperability and performance before customer deployment.
- **Support Customer Engagements:** Partners can demonstrate end-to-end VCF capabilities live, moving beyond slideware to show actual outcomes.
- **Create Repeatable Frameworks:** The lab provides a blueprint for delivering consistent, proven solutions across multiple customer environments.


The VCF Knights Solution Lab also provides tangible value for customers including:


- **Reduced Risk** : Partners can test and validate designs in the lab, ensuring that proposed architectures work as intended before they are deployed in production.
- **Faster Problem Solving** : Issues encountered in the field can be replicated and resolved in a safe, controlled environment.
- **Stronger Demonstrations** : Customers gain a clearer understanding of how VCF capabilities support modernization, cyber resilience, and operational efficiency through live demonstrations.
