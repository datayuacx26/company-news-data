# Broadcom and Nationwide Deepen Strategic Partnership to Power Group-Wide Private Cloud Transformation

Expanded collaboration will establish a unified private cloud platform across the enlarged organisation


-
-
-


**LONDON — June 24, 2026** — Broadcom Inc., a global technology leader that designs, develops and supplies semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions, and Nationwide Building Society, the world’s largest building society, today announced an extension of their strategic partnership to support Nationwide’s continued technology transformation and the evolution of its hybrid cloud strategy.


At the heart of this next phase is the adoption of VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF), as the foundation for a fully integrated, enterprise-grade private cloud platform. The move strengthens Nationwide’s ability to deliver more secure, resilient and scalable digital services to its members, while supporting coordination and integration across the organisation following its acquisition of Virgin Money.


[VMware Cloud Foundation](https://www.vmware.com/products/cloud-infrastructure/vmware-cloud-foundation) is a unified, private cloud platform with integrated, compute, storage, networking, management, and security with automation and intelligent operations. For Nationwide, this provides a standardised foundation for running traditional, cloud native and eventually AI applications, while maintaining the performance, governance and compliance requirements of a large banking provider in the UK.


“Our extended partnership with Broadcom represents a significant step forward in our technology strategy,” said Paul Walsh, Director of Infrastructure and Service Delivery at Nationwide. “As we continue to evolve as a business, including integrating Virgin Money into the group, it is vital that we have a resilient, scalable and secure technology foundation. A private cloud built on VMware Cloud Foundation enables us to simplify operations, accelerate innovation and deliver seamless digital experiences for our members, while maintaining the trust and stability that define the Nationwide brand.”


The extension of the partnership reflects Nationwide’s commitment to building a modern private cloud that balances agility with operational control. It will enable the organisation to coordinate infrastructure across brands while preserving the trust and stability associated with the Nationwide name.


“Nationwide is taking a deliberate and strategic approach to private cloud that balances agility with control and innovation with resilience,” said Joe Baguley, EMEA Chief Technology Officer, Broadcom. “By extending our partnership and adopting VMware Cloud Foundation as a consistent platform across the group, Nationwide will be able to integrate operations more efficiently, accelerate service delivery and reduce operational complexity, while maintaining the security and governance expected of a leading UK financial services brand.”


As Nationwide continues to modernise its technology estate, VMware Cloud Foundation will serve as the core infrastructure layer underpinning service continuity, integration and long-term operational efficiency across the organisation.


## About Nationwide


Nationwide is the world's largest building society, with over 16 million members. Following its acquisition of Virgin Money UK PLC, Nationwide is connected with one in three people in the UK and is the second largest provider of mortgages and retail deposits. It is also a major provider of current accounts, credit cards, personal loans and business banking. Customers can choose to manage their finances in a branch, via mobile app, the internet, telephone, and post. Nationwide has around 25,000 employees and its head office is in Swindon, UK.


As a financial services provider owned by its members, not shareholders, Nationwide's purpose is: Banking - but fairer, more rewarding, and for the good of society. For more information see[About Us | Nationwide](https://www.nationwide.co.uk/about-us/) .
