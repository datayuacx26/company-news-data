# Mainframe Power, Modern Control: A New Era of Mainframe DBA Productivity

-
-
-


The mainframe is the bedrock of the global economy, the unparalleled engine driving transactions and securing the world's most critical data. Its power, reliability, and security are unmatched, and as the platform continues to evolve, so must the experience of the professionals who manage it.


For years, the power of Db2 for z/OS has been managed through robust, high-performance tools. Our focus is enabling the interface to this power to be as advanced as the platform itself. That’s why we’re elevating the administrative experience with **[CenterStage for Database Management](https://www.broadcom.com/products/mainframe/database-management/centerstage)** .


Our mission is simple: Provide tools that maximize the inherent value of the mainframe. CenterStage is the next-generation, web-based interface for Db2 tools, specifically engineered to enhance the efficiency of seasoned mainframe Database Administrators (DBAs) and accelerate the readiness of every new mainframer.


**CenterStage: Enhancing Core DBA Capabilities**


CenterStage is designed for maximum productivity right out of the box. Built on modern REST APIs and leveraging the open architecture of Zowe, it moves the DBA experience to a flexible web environment. This strategic evolution directly supports the continued growth and agility of mainframe operations by:


- **Accelerating onboarding:** The intuitive, web-native experience aims to provide immediate accessibility, reducing the time it takes for new DBAs to master the Db2 environment and contribute effectively.
- **Maximizing DBA productivity:** CenterStage consolidates essential tasks, allowing DBAs to efficiently navigate the Db2 catalog, investigate object relationships, and seamlessly execute commands all within a single, unified view. This focus on workflow optimization has the potential to drive significant productivity gains.
- **Providing instant insight:** Designed to give DBAs immediate, quick access to object detail;, can fluidly run Db2 commands; execute SQL statements; and generate crucial DDL and hierarchical DDL through a streamlined, powerful console.


**Integrating With Modern DevOps: Code4z for Developers**


Our investment in the modern mainframe experience extends beyond the administrator. We know that data management is inextricably linked to development velocity. To support the shift toward faster, integrated enterprise DevOps practices, we introduced the **Code4z DBM-Db2 VS Code Extension** .


This extension brings the core functionality of Db2 tooling right into the familiar environment of VS Code, empowering developers to:


- **Streamline development workflows:** Developers can perform essential tasks like schema provisioning directly from their preferred IDE.
- **Achieve seamless integration:** By embedding robust Db2 tooling into VS Code, we enable application development to naturally integrate with enterprise DevOps pipelines, maximizing agility and collaboration.


**The Future of Modern Mainframe Development**


The mainframe continues to thrive as the essential system that secures and processes the world’s most critical workloads. CenterStage and Code4z are crucial components of this future, delivering powerful, flexible, and integrated tools that match the performance and strategic importance of Db2 for z/OS.
