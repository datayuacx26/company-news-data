# Securing Your Network in Today’s Data-Driven World

-
-
-


In today's connected, data-driven world, business success relies on delivering dependable digital services. Data is crucial for services and decisions, and it all travels through the network.


As organizations expand, networks become larger and more complex making it increasingly difficult to find and fix issues. This complexity can cause outages, bad customer experiences, or compliance problems.


Additionally, digital transformation (moving apps to the web and mobile) has increased mainframe workloads and highlighted the need for strong network performance. And at the same time, the mainframe workforce is shrinking as experts retire—leaving IT generalists to manage mainframe networking alongside other duties (often without specialized expertise).


So, the question becomes: How do we address these challenges while bolstering enterprise network security?


**Addressing Network Security Challenges**


That’s where NetMaster® Network Intelligence comes in. NetMaster is a powerful tool with features specifically designed to help you manage and monitor the security of your enterprise network. It can also secure large-scale network infrastructures, providing deep visibility and leveraging machine learning and automation to help find and fix problems proactively.


NetMaster simplifies daily tasks like monitoring devices and connections, giving you a clear intelligent view of your network's overall health. The solution gathers historical data to help spot unusual activity early and includes SmartTrace for faster and easier troubleshooting. It also can automate file transfers for critical apps and tracks performance from a business perspective to enable you to meet your service goals.


Other key capabilities include:


- Instant notifications for network **security** events, allowing you to catch suspicious activity as it happens
- Use of machine learning and historical data to identify unusual patterns that may signal a breach or insider threat
- Monitoring the encryption status of mainframe connections in real time, helping you enable data to be protected and compliant with regulations
- Visualizing and managing mainframe connections, including volume and patterns of network traffic and the security protocols used to secure the connections.
- Providing rapid packet tracing, which is essential for diagnosing issues that could be related to security incidents
- Integration with and sharing of critical network data with your mainframe security tools assists to reduce blind spots in overall security operations


## **Navigating Digital Certificates**


Customers and organizations are also facing critical challenges surrounding the management of third-party digital certificates, largely driven by more frequent certificate renewal requirements and the increasing scale of digital infrastructure. Lifespans for TLS/SSL certificates are rapidly shortening, creating a "perfect storm" for operational teams who must now manage renewals nearly ten times more often than in previous years.


Unfortunately, many organizations lack a centralized certificate inventory, leading to "hidden" and non-compliant certificates. In fact, research shows that many organizations may not know exactly how many certificates they have or where they are located. Many organizations have experienced unplanned service disruptions due to expired certificates or configuration errors.


NetMaster’s security-centered features can address these challenges, too.
