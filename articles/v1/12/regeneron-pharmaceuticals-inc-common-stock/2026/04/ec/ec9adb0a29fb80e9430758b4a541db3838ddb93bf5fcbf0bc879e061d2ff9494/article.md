# Responsibility at Regeneron: A Look Back and a Look Ahead

### So, what’s next?


Completing our 2025 goals isn’t the finish line. It’s the moment to ask how we can raise the bar.


To find the answer, we brought together colleagues from across the business to examine how we affect the world and how the world affects us. The result?[New responsibility goals](https://www.regeneron.com/responsibility/our-approach) focused on **advancing our mission to improve lives** , with ethics and integrity woven throughout.


Naturally, our strategy leads with science. We will continue to apply innovation to deliver groundbreaking medicines. And, through the[Regeneron Genetics Center®](https://www.regeneron.com/science/genetics-center) , we’re committing to sequencing 10 million samples and integrating artificial intelligence (AI) and big data tools to advance drug discovery and digital health solutions.
