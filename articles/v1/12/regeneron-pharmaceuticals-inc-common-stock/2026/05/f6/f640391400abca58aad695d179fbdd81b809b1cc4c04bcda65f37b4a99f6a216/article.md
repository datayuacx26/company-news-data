# Rethinking Itch: A Window into Immunology & Inflammation

What if itch isn’t just a symptom, but a signal of something deeper? In this episode of The Nucleus, a Regeneron podcast, Dr. Seblewongel Asrat, PhD, Director, Immunology & Inflammation, Regeneron sits down with Dr. Brian Kim, MD, FAAD, professor of dermatology at Mount Sinai, to explore the biology of itch and immunology, and its profound impact on patients’ lives. From uncovering distinct itch pathways to understanding its link to type 2 inflammation and the atopic march, they discuss how rethinking itch is reshaping how we study, treat, and potentially prevent disease.
