# Sommer McKinley receives 'Most Influential Women in Business' honor

Thursday, May 15, 2025


Sommer McKinley, CFP®, AIF®, Director of Wealth Management for Mechanics Bank, has been named one of 2025's "Most Influential Women in Business" by the *San Francisco Business Times* , a leading business publication in Northern California.


Evaluating nearly 400 nominations, the publication chose 80 honorees from various industries and communities across the San Francisco Bay Area.


"Sommer's accomplishments remind us that if you follow your instincts and pursue a profession you love, great things will happen," and Tony Kallingal, Chief Banking Officer. "She is an inspiration to her team and our entire organization."


McKinley oversees the Bank's Wealth Management unit which provides customized offerings in trust and estate services, investment management and brokerage services, retirement plan services, employee benefit plans, specialty asset management and private banking.
