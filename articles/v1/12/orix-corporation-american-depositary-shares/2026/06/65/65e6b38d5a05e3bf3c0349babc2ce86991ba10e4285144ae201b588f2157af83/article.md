Bhavna Goyal, Senior Director on our Credit Risk Management team, was recently featured in NAIOP’s *Summer 2026 Magazine.* In the article, “When Occupancy Misleads: Uncovering the True Cost of Economic Vacancy,” Bhavna explores why economic vacancy can be a more meaningful indicator of asset performance than physical occupancy and what investors, lenders and asset managers should consider when evaluating risk across CRE portfolios.
