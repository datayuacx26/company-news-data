# Ted Denniston Quoted in Pitchbook: Private Credit Lenders Finding Opportunities in Lower Middle Market


[Ted Denniston](https://www.orix.com/people/ted-denniston/) , Co-Head of our direct lending subsidiary[NXT Capital](https://www.nxtcapital.com/) , recently spoke with *Pitchbook* on market and deal trends within the lower middle market.


Highlights from Ted:


- “In our view, the LMM has always been more stable than the core and upper middle markets from a deal activity perspective,” said Ted Denniston, co-head and senior managing director of NXT Capital, attributing the LMM’s appeal to conservative deal structures.
- Denniston added that the strategies around many borrowers in the lower middle market often involve add-on acquisitions. This activity can blunt the decline in new-issue M&A and buyouts.
