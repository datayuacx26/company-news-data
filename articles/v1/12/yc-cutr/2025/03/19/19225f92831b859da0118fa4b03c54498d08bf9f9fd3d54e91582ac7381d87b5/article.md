# Cabinet configurator: custom design and quotes in minutes

We are excited to introduce our latest product: the Cabinet Configurator. This tool empowers you to design custom cabinets effortlessly, tailored precisely to your preferences and space requirements.

### What is the Cabinet Configurator?


The Cabinet Configurator is an intuitive online tool that allows users to create bespoke cabinet designs and get quotes for them in minutes. Whether you're a builder, cabinet maker, or a professional designer working on a project, this tool simplifies the design and quoting process, making it accessible to everyone.

### Key features


- **User-friendly interface** : Navigate through the design process with ease, thanks to a clean and intuitive layout.
- **Customization options** : Adjust dimensions, select materials, and choose interiors to match your aesthetic and functional needs.
- **Real-time visualization** : View your design as you make changes, providing a clear understanding of the final product.
- **Almost instant pricing** : Receive quotes within minutes after request based on your design choices, aiding in budget planning.


‍


### How it works


1. **Start your design** : Begin by selecting the Cabinets and Shelves section on Cutr platform.
2. **Add dimensions** : Input the specific measurements to ensure a perfect fit for your space.
3. **Add modules and interiors:** Create modules, drawers, interios, doors as per your needs.
4. **Select materials and finishes** : Choose from a wide range of materials and finishes to match your style.
5. **Review and adjust** : Use the visualization to review your design and make any necessary adjustments.
6. **Get almost instant quote** : Once satisfied, request for quote and receive a price within minutes for your custom cabinet.
7. **Place order** : Happy with the price? Accept the quote to proceed with order, and our team will handle the rest, ensuring your cabinet is crafted to your specifications.
