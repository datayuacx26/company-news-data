# AVAX One Announces Financial Leadership Change

**WEST PALM BEACH, FL, Aug. 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) —** AVAX One Technology Ltd. (Nasdaq: AVX) (“AVAX One” or the “Company”), today announced the departure of Chief Financial Officer (“CFO”) Chris Polimeni, effective August 17, 2026. Stephanie Brady, the Company’s Controller, has been promoted to Chief Accounting Officer and will oversee the Company’s financial reporting function.
