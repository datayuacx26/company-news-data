### Stewart Information Services Corporation Announces Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Conference Call


Jul 13, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s27.q4cdn.com/679580685/files/doc_news/Stewart-Information-Services-Corporation-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Earnings-Conference-Call-2026.pdf)


HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Stewart Information Services Corporation (NYSE: STC) announced today it will hold a conference call to discuss second quarter 2026 earnings at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time on Thursday, July 23, 2026. The call will follow the company’s release of earnings after the close of trading on Wednesday, July 22. Individuals wishing to participate can dial (800) 420-1459 (USA) and (203) 518-9861 (International) – access code STCQ226. The conference call replay will be available from 11 a.m. Eastern Time on July 23, 2026, until midnight on July 30, 2026, by dialing (800) 839-9307 (USA) or (402) 220-6085 (International). Additionally, participants can listen to the conference call through STC’s Investor Relations website at[https://investors.stewart.com/news-and-events/events/default.aspx](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.stewart.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54568522&newsitemid=20260713854804&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.stewart.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents%2Fdefault.aspx&index=1&md5=7053c8ac531459f0991c1d5f8e76dcf9) .
