HOUSTON


, March 2, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Stewart Information Services Corporation ( *NYSE:STC* ) today announced that its Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.525 per share for the first quarter 2026, payable March 31, 2026, to common stockholders of record on March 16, 2026.


**About Stewart** Stewart Information Services Corporation ( *NYSE:STC* ) is a global real estate services company, offering products and services through our direct operations, network of Stewart Trusted Providers™ and family of companies. From residential and commercial title insurance and closing and settlement services to specialized offerings for the mortgage industry, we offer the comprehensive service, deep expertise and solutions our customers need for any real estate transaction.
