### CNA Financial to Report Second Quarter 2026 Results on August 3


07/14/2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/873173963/files/doc_news/CNA-Financial-to-Report-Second-Quarter-2026-Results-on-August-3-2026.pdf)


CHICAGO


, July 14, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- CNA Financial Corporation (NYSE: CNA) will report second quarter 2026 results before the market opens on Monday, August 3, 2026. The news release, earnings presentation and financial supplement will be available on CNA's website at


[www.cna.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4730423-1&h=1189621921&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.cna.com%2F&a=www.cna.com)


via the


[Investor Relations](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4730423-1&h=1505403427&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor-relations.cna.com%2Fhome%2Fdefault.aspx&a=Investor+Relations)


section. Along with these documents CNA will post a transcript of earnings remarks, which will include commentary from the Company's Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Douglas M. Worman, and Chief Financial Officer, Scott R. Lindquist.


CNA invites shareholders and analysts to submit questions for management in advance of the earnings release. Management may address some or all of these questions in the posted earnings remarks. Questions may be submitted to


Ralitza.Todorova@cna.com


.
