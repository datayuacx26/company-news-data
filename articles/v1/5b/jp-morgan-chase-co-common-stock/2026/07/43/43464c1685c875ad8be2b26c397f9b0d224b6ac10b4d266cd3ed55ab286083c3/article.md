# Registration opens for San Francisco’s 40th JPMorganChase Corporate Challenge


The 3.5-mile run/walk is scheduled for Thursday, September 10, at Chase Center


SAN FRANCISCO


---


July 09, 2026


Registration is now open for the 40th running of the JPMorganChase Corporate Challenge in San Francisco.


The 3.5-mile run/walk will bring together hundreds of Bay Area companies on September 10 at 6:45 p.m. at Chase Center.


Open exclusively to employees of local businesses and corporations, the event welcomes colleagues of all fitness levels to walk, jog, or run the course—building camaraderie and a tradition that extends beyond the workplace.


After the race, company teams will celebrate inside[Chase Center](https://www.chasecenter.com/) , home of the Golden State Warriors and the Golden State Valkyries, with an on-court post-race experience.


[SF New Deal](https://sfnewdeal.org/) will serve as the 2026 JPMorganChase Corporate Challenge San Francisco beneficiary. Since its founding in March 2020, SF New Deal has deployed $59 million to 2,200+ small businesses across the city through innovative programs like Vacant to Vibrant and Neighborhood Vibrancy Funds, which invest directly in local businesses to activate commercial corridors and strengthen the cultural and economic fabric of San Francisco’s neighborhoods.


“The JPMorganChase Corporate Challenge is about teamwork—on the course and in the community,” said Tim Moffet, Corporate Challenge Team Captain, JPMorganChase. “For more than 120 years, JPMorganChase has supported the Bay Area by investing in the communities where we live and work. As we celebrate the 40th running in San Francisco, we’re proud to support SF New Deal as the 2026 beneficiary—helping strengthen the small businesses that are essential to the city’s vibrancy and resilience.”


## Registration


Company teams can register on the[JPMorganChase Corporate Challenge](https://www.jpmorganchasecc.com/en/home/our-locations/san-francisco) website through Thursday, August 13, at 6 p.m. (or until capacity is reached, whichever comes first). The $85 entry fee per participant includes a drink ticket, meal ticket, and access to Chase Center post-race party spaces. Teams need at least four participants.
