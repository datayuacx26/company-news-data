## **Why Financial Spreading Remains the Bottleneck in Credit Underwriting**


Financial spreading—the process of extracting, standardizing, and analyzing financial data from borrower documents—has been the silent productivity killer in commercial lending for decades. Despite advances in digital banking and fintech innovation, most financial institutions still rely on manual processes that are:


**Time-Intensive:** Traditional financial spreading can take days or weeks per complex deal, creating delays that cost institutions both deals and operational efficiency.


**Error-Prone:** Manual data entry introduces calculation errors, misclassifications, and inconsistencies that compound throughout the underwriting process.


**Resource-Heavy:** Banks and fintechs are forced to hire specialized analysts just to handle basic data extraction and formatting tasks.


**Scalability-Limited:** Manual processes create bottlenecks that prevent financial institutions from growing their commercial lending portfolios efficiently.


These challenges explain why financial spreading has become the primary target for AI automation in credit underwriting. The question isn't whether AI will transform financial statement analysis—it's how quickly financial institutions can adopt solutions that deliver both speed and accuracy.
