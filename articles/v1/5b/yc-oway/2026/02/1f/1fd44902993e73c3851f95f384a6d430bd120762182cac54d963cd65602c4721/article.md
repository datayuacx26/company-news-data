With stagnant broker rates, underutilized capacity, and increased operational pressures, they sought a way to boost profitability without adding complexity to their workflow.**

###### **The Challenge**


RTD Delivery Systems had long operated with brokers like STG and T Force, whose rates hadn’t kept pace with market realities. Despite consistently running their most popular lanes at 75–80% capacity, RTD often faced inefficiencies — with some routes operating half-full. To make up for this underutilization, the team relied on time-consuming cold outreach and manual broker coordination to backfill empty space, pulling resources away from growth and creating operational drag.

No calls, no negotiations, and no surprises at pickup or delivery — just efficient, frictionless freight.**
