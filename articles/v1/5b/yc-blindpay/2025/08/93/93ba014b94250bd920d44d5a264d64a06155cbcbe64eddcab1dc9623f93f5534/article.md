# BlindPay Raises $3.3M Seed Round

BlindPay (YC W25) raised $3.3M in a seed round from[Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/blindpay) ,[468 Capital](https://468cap.com/) ,[Circle Ventures](https://www.circle.com/circle-ventures) ,[Bitso Business](https://bitso.com/business) ,[Transpose Platform](https://www.transposeplatform.vc/) ,[Acacia Venture Capital Partners](https://acaciainvest.com/) , and angel investors including Jawed Karim (YouTube cofounder), Raphael Dyxklay, and Caetano Lacerda (co-founder Barte).


Our mission is simple: build a better global payment infrastructure with stablecoins, giving businesses the tools to move money across borders, stay compliant, and open virtual US accounts in minutes, all through one API built for developers.


In 2025 alone we've already processed over $125 million in transfers, with $46 million in July alone, and this is just the beginning.


Thank you to everyone who has supported us along the way. We're incredibly excited to keep building the future of global payments!
