# What Hotplate does

## What is Hotplate?


We get this question a lot because Hotplate powers the sale of food in a way that is new and unfamiliar to most people.


The short version: we’re on a mission to make it possible for anyone to start and scale a profitable food business.


Businesses on Hotplate can start same-day, with almost no capital or money in the bank, and will make 50-80%+ profit margins, depending on the kind of food they make and sell.


The longer version goes a little something like this:


## What does Hotplate do?


Hotplate automates everything for your business — from creating your website to communicating with customers, managing inventory so you don’t oversell, processing payments, generating order tickets, calculating your prep list, and surfacing actionable insights.


Focus on the food, let Hotplate handle the rest.


Orders through Hotplate are *pre-* orders, which means customers purchase their food *before* coming to pick it up.


## What does Hotplate *not* do?


We are not a consumer marketplace. This means we don’t advertise you in a sea of other businesses for customers to browse through. Each chef or baker on Hotplate gets their own storefront that is a 1:1 connection between them and their customers.


Choosing not to be a marketplace is an intentional choice. And it’s one that aligns with our values:


-


**Our mission is to make food businesses more profitable, not less**


Marketplaces often kill small businesses, taking a 25%+ cut from every order.


-


**You should own your own customer data**


Marketplaces own the customer relationship, you do not. At Hotplate, we believe we should continuously work for the opportunity to support your business. At any point, if you want to download your list of customers and go somewhere else, that’s entirely your purview. Other marketplaces won’t let you do that.


-


**Your individual brand should shine**


Most marketplaces minimize your brand so that consumers are more connected with the marketplace than with your individual business. At Hotplate, we believe individuality is what helps businesses cultivate a loyal customer base and scale it.


This does mean that chefs on Hotplate hustle to build their own customer base. That said, we help accelerate your efforts. A number of our features are designed to help grow your subscriber list, reward your regulars, keep everyone in the loop, and save you time.


And because Hotplate drops are way more fun and socially engaging (we’ll talk about the Customer experience in a sec) than your typical methods of placing an order, they naturally lead people to spread the word. Friends don’t let friends miss out on the best food in town. 🤷‍♀️

You get a lot included with Hotplate:

Anytime you want to sell, you set your menu, inventory, schedule when orders open and close, and when and where customers will pickup.


-

We automatically notify your list whenever your orders open, and allow you to send additional messages for promotions or customer touch points.

We let you chat with customers 1:1 via the Hotplate Inbox in case you need to get in touch directly.

We send automated reminders so your customers don’t forget to pickup their order

**Last but not least…**

## How much does Hotplate cost?


It’s completely free to create and hold an account on Hotplate. We only make money when a customer places an order.


Hotplate chefs only pay payment processing: 2.9% of the transaction + 30 cents, which goes straight to our payment processor, Stripe.


To cover the features we provide, we charge a fee to your customer: 5% of their subtotal + 55¢. We never charge on tip or tax. This comes out to $1.80 on a $25 order.


If you’d like to take on more of the fees, or pass more onto your customer, you can actually customize the split. That said, 80% of Hotplate chefs keep the default as-is.


## Who is Hotplate for?


Hotplate is built first and foremost for pop-ups, cottage food operations (CFOs), microenterprise home kitchen operations (MEHKOs), home-based restaurants and cafes, mobile food trucks, and folks selling food from commercial kitchens.


We are primarily built for food-sellers who don’t have a restaurant or brick & mortar and who need more flexible systems than a standard point-of-sale.


We are a pre-order platform — not an in-person point-of-sale system.


We also help brick & mortars manage long lines and sell outs, allowing customers to order ahead, pickup and skip the walk-in line. Because Hotplate is purpose-built for pre-orders and designed to handle limited inventory and heavy demand, it’s especially helpful for holiday rushes. Think cookie boxes, Thanksgiving pies, or Mother’s and Father’s Day specials.


## How does Hotplate work?


An order through Hotplate is a pre-order. They are placed through *drops* .


A *drop* is a typical way of selling concert tickets, sneakers, and limited edition merch, but it’s not the traditional way of selling food.


Here’s how a drop works:


**Setting it up**


-


You create a drop with the menu, inventory, pickup locations and times.


-


You schedule when orders open and close.


**Customer experience**


-


Right before your pre-orders open, Hotplate sends a text to your subscribers with the link to your drop.


-


Customers click the link and see a short countdown before orders open. There’s a fun, anonymous chat you can enable to let customers get hyped up.


-


Once orders open, customers add items to cart as quickly as they can to beat out the competition.


-


Customers select their pick up day and time and checkout.


-


If a customer abandons their cart or takes too long, their cart timer expires and their items will be released so that someone else can buy it.


-


After placing their order, customers receive an order confirmation text and email.


-


Your customer receives pickup reminder(s) as their pickup approaches.


-


Customers leave a glowing review after their pick up, and post about their experience to drive some extra traffic to your storefront!


**Your experience**


-


As soon as your customer places their order, that revenue is available to you to payout. You can do so at anytime.


-


We create a prep list based on all your orders, and a series of customer tickets so you can see who ordered what, alongside all of their pickup details.


-


Most Hotplate chefs will space out their prep over a few days in preparation for a later pick-up date.


This cycle continues, your customer list grows, and your drops sell out faster and faster.
