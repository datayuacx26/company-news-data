# First Mid’s Workplace Culture Continues to Earn National Recognition

### First Mid’s Workplace Culture Continues to Earn National Recognition


August 3, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/386638223/files/doc_news/2026/Aug/03/Press-Release-First-Mid-s-Workplace-Culture-Continues-to-Earn-National-Recognition.pdf)


**Mattoon, IL (August 3, 2026)** At First Mid, creating a great workplace has always been about fostering an environment where employees can build rewarding careers, support one another, and make a meaningful impact. This year, that approach has once again earned national recognition.


First Mid has received its fourth consecutive Top Workplaces Financial Services Industry award, along with seven Top Workplaces Culture Excellence awards recognizing its strengths in Compensation & Benefits, Employee Appreciation, Employee Well-Being, Innovation, Professional Development, Purpose & Values, and Work-Life Flexibility.


Together, these awards reflect a workplace defined by trust, collaboration, and the everyday contributions of employees.


“One of First Mid’s greatest strengths has always been our people," said Matt Smith, Chief Executive Officer and President at First Mid. “As we continue to grow, our focus is on ensuring First Mid remains a place where employees can expand their skills, take on new challenges, and see a future for themselves. That foundation is what allows us to serve our customers and communities with excellence.”


Presented by Energage, the Top Workplaces Financial Services Industry award recognizes organizations that excel in workplace culture within the financial services sector. The Top Workplaces Culture Excellence awards recognize organizations that stand out across key aspects of the workplace. All Top Workplaces awards are determined entirely by confidential employee feedback.


Learn more about career opportunities at[www.firstmid.com/about/careers](https://www.firstmid.com/about/careers) .
