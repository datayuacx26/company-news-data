### First Mid Insurance Group Retains Best Practices Agency Recognition for 2026


August 17, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/386638223/files/doc_news/2026/Aug/17/Press-Release-First-Mid-Insurance-Group-Retains-Best-Practices-Agency-Recognition-for-2026.pdf)


**Mattoon, IL (August 17, 2026)** First Mid Insurance Group (FMIG), a leading provider of insurance solutions, is proud to announce it has once again been recognized as a Best Practices Agency for 2026, marking the seventh time it has achieved this honor. This recognition places FMIG among an exclusive group of independent insurance agencies nationwide.


The Best Practices Agency designation is awarded to participants in the Best Practices Study, a comprehensive analysis of the business operations and performance of the insurance industry's top-performing independent agencies. More than 1,190 independent insurance agencies across the United States were nominated during this study cycle. For the 2026 study year, only 300 agencies qualified for and maintained their Best Practices Agency designation.


“Being recognized as a Best Practices Agency for the seventh time reflects the consistency, expertise, and commitment our team brings to serving our clients,” said Clay Dean, Chief Executive Officer of First Mid Insurance Group. “We’re proud to be recognized among the nation’s top-performing independent insurance agencies, and we remain focused on delivering personalized service, creating value for our clients, and supporting the communities we serve.”


The Best Practices recognition follows another significant milestone for FMIG. In July, the company earned a place on Business Insurance’s prestigious Top 100 U.S. Brokers list for the first time, ranking No. 95 among insurance brokerage firms nationwide. Together, these recognitions reflect FMIG’s continued growth and commitment to strong performance while maintaining a high level of service for its clients.


Since 1993, the Independent Insurance Agents & Brokers of America (IIABA or the Big “I”) and Reagan Consulting, an Atlanta-based management consulting firm, have partnered to identify and benchmark the nation's leading independent insurance agencies across seven revenue categories. Agencies are selected as Best Practices Agencies every three years through a highly competitive nomination and qualification process. To earn the designation, each agency must rank among the top performers within its respective revenue category. To maintain their Best Practices status throughout the three-year study cycle, participating agencies submit detailed financial and operational information for annual review. The 2026 study year marks the second year of the current three-year cycle.
