# First Mid Earns Greater St. Louis Top Workplaces Award for Third Consecutive Year

### First Mid Earns Greater St. Louis Top Workplaces Award for Third Consecutive Year


June 22, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/386638223/files/doc_news/2026/Jun/22/Press-Release-First-Mid-Earns-Greater-St-Louis-Top-Workplaces-Award-for-Third-Consecutive-Year.pdf)


**St. Louis, MO (June 22, 2026)** First Mid Bancshares, Inc. (First Mid) has been named a Greater St. Louis Top Workplace for the third consecutive year. The award, presented by the St. Louis Post-Dispatch in partnership with Energage, recognizes organizations that prioritize a strong workplace culture and employee engagement.


The Greater St. Louis Top Workplaces award is based entirely on employee feedback gathered through a confidential survey conducted by Energage, an independent research company specializing in workplace culture and employee experience.


“This award is a reflection of the engagement, teamwork, and commitment our employees bring to First Mid each day,” said Joe Dively, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer at First Mid. “Their dedication to our shared values continues to create a workplace where employees feel supported, connected, and empowered to succeed.”


The annual survey measures several aspects of workplace culture, including alignment, connection, execution, and employee engagement. Organizations recognized as Top Workplaces are identified based on employee responses and workplace experience benchmarks.


“We’re proud to be part of the Greater St. Louis community and honored to receive this recognition again,” Dively said. “Our employees are the foundation of our success, and we remain committed to fostering an environment where people can grow personally and professionally while continuing to serve our customers and communities.”


In addition to the Greater St. Louis Top Workplaces recognition, First Mid recently earned a third consecutive Top Workplaces USA award. The organization continues to invest in professional development and employee well-being while supporting strong business performance and community impact. For more information about career opportunities, visit[www.firstmid.com/about/careers](https://www.firstmid.com/about/careers) .


**About First Mid Bancshares, Inc.:** First Mid Bancshares, Inc. is the parent company of First Mid Bank & Trust, N.A., First Mid Insurance Group, and First Mid Wealth Management Company. First Mid is a $9.3 billion community-focused organization that provides financial services including banking, insurance, wealth management, brokerage, and ag services through a network of locations in Illinois, Iowa, Missouri, Texas, and Wisconsin, and a loan production office in Indiana.
