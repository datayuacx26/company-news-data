### First Mid Wealth Management Welcomes Financial Advisor Kyle Wagenbach


May 11, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/386638223/files/doc_news/2026/May/11/Press-Release-First-Mid-Wealth-Management-Welcomes-Financial-Advisor-Kyle-Wagenbach.pdf)


**Burlington, IA (May 11, 2026)** First Mid Wealth Management, a division of First Mid Bancshares, Inc. and a branch office of Raymond James, announces that Financial Advisor Kyle Wagenbach has joined its team.


Wagenbach brings a unique and analytical perspective to financial planning, drawing on a diverse background in engineering, economics, entrepreneurship, and agriculture. He specializes in long-term investment strategies and risk management, developing customized solutions tailored to each client’s unique objectives.


“We are thrilled to welcome Kyle to our team,” said Eric Benne, SVP, Director of Trust at First Mid Wealth Management. “His commitment to client-first service, combined with his diverse background, strengthens our ability to deliver thoughtful, personalized financial strategies. As we continue to grow, adding professionals of Kyle’s caliber reinforces our dedication to providing our local communities with high-quality financial guidance and a personal touch.”


Wagenbach holds Series 7 and 66 licenses and earned a degree in Chemical Engineering from Iowa State University. His technical expertise and problem-solving mindset support his ability to help clients navigate complex financial decisions with confidence.


“I am excited to join the First Mid team and value the customer-centered focus of the organization. I look forward to providing quality service and building long-term relationships with clients in our local community,” said Wagenbach.


First Mid Wealth Management is committed to providing comprehensive financial solutions backed by the strength and stability of First Mid Bank & Trust and the resources of Raymond James.
