# Purse Enhancement Partnerships increase purses on Hall of Fame Weekend; Partnership with CertainTeed, SRS and JTR Roofing, Inc. adds $3,500 to each race July 25

Canterbury Park corporate partners CertainTeed, SRS and JTR Roofing, Inc. will add $3,500 to each purse on the eight-race program Saturday, July 25. A total of $31,000 will go directly to purses on the Hall of Fame Weekend. The Saturday card includes the Ralph Strangis, Minnesota Turf Distaff and Mr. Dale Filly & Mare Turf Sprint, all turf allowance races. Sunday features the Victor S. Myers Stakes and Frances Genter Stakes sponsored by[Kiger Equine Insurance](https://kigerinsurance.com/) , with $1,500 added bringing the value to $51,500 each.


“Working with partners to create added value exposure and increase purses for racehorse owners and trainers is beneficial to everyone,” Director of Corporate Partnerships Kip Rakos said. “We are pleased to make Hall of Fame Weekend a more lucrative event for the Minnesota racing industry while also hosting a partner event in our expo center.”


The JTR Roofing, Inc. Expo D ay is scheduled for Friday and Saturday, July 24 and 25 in Canterbury Park’s Event Center.[JTR Roofing](https://www.jtrroofinginc.com/) , Inc. is a locally-owned residential and commercial roofing and exterior building company serving the Twin Cities and western Wisconsin.[CertainTeed](https://www.certainteed.com/) is North America’s leading brand of exterior and interior products, including roofing, siding, trim, insulation, gypsum, and ceilings.[SRS](https://www.srsdistribution.com/en/) is a leading distributor of roofing and building materials with branches across the U.S.


Representatives from each company will be onsite during the free Expo. Interested home and business owners can view products and seek advice.


Racing begins at 5:10 p.m. on July 25 and at 1:10 p.m. on July 26.
