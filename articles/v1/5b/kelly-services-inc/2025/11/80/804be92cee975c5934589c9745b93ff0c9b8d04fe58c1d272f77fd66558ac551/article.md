# James Vallone Named President of Kelly Technology Solutions Division, Motion Recruitment

TROY, Mich. (Nov. 25, 2025) – Kelly® (Nasdaq: KELYA, KELYB), a leading specialty talent solutions provider, has named James Vallone president of its technology solutions division,[Motion Recruitment](https://motionrecruitment.com/) . In this role, Vallone now leads the strategic expansion of the company’s IT solutions and staffing services, following Kelly’s acquisition and integration of Motion Recruitment.


“Stepping into this role is a tremendous opportunity,” Vallone said. “Motion’s foundation is built on deep technical understanding and a commitment to solving real business problems. By integrating more closely with Kelly, we can broaden the solutions we deliver and support clients across strategy, talent, and execution in ways that create meaningful and lasting impact.”


In his 23 years at Motion Recruitment, Vallone has witnessed the company's evolution through several market shifts and has led several successful business transformations in a fast-paced industry. After beginning his career as an entry-level recruiter, Vallone quickly expanded his roles and responsibilities, transforming top teams in Chicago, New York City, Philadelphia, and Los Angeles. With each promotion, he helped grow Motion Recruitment’s footprint, establishing a company culture of success nationwide.


Vallone’s strategic perspective led him into corporate leadership, where he spearheaded the launch of the company’s enterprise IT contract business, a key evolution in Motion Recruitment’s market strategy. His leadership was the driving force in making the move an unquestioned success, growing that business exponentially from $5 million in revenue to over $100 million in under five years, followed by consistent 20% year-over-year growth, even in challenging economic environments.


Since Vallone joined the corporate team, Motion Recruitment has grown from a company that was described as “the best kept secret in recruiting” to one of the industry's most prominent players in IT staffing. Kelly’s acquisition of Motion Recruitment enabled the business to significantly grow its services and capabilities, leapfrogging competitors. The combined business now ranks as the 11th largest IT staffing firm in the U.S., according to Staffing Industry Analysts.[Most recently, it was named a Leader and Star Performer by Everest Group](https://www.kellyservices.com/press-releases/kelly-motion-recruitment-recognized-as-it-contingent-staffing-leader-star-performer-by-everest-group) , which highlighted the company’s impactful investments in IT managed services and AI-powered technical solutions.


“Motion’s strength lies in its ability to connect highly specialized technology talent with organizations that drive innovation,” Kelly CEO Chris Layden said. “With James at the helm, Motion will continue to advance Kelly’s position as a leader in technology staffing and consulting, combining deep technical expertise with the agility and scalability clients need in today’s evolving market.”


Vallone will lead Motion Recruitment into its next chapter by expanding its technology solutions and strengthening alignment with Kelly’s specialty portfolio of digital infrastructure, engineering, and science. He remains focused on the core principles that have defined Motion’s success for decades: listening closely to clients, building long-term partnerships, and delivering solutions tailored to each organization’s unique needs. By combining Motion’s deep expertise with Kelly’s scale and resources, the Kelly technology solutions division is poised to deliver the most impactful, future-ready experience for clients across North America.
