# Scaling workforce operations for a high-performance AI hardware manufacturer.

#### **Client overview.**


A high-performance AI computing manufacturer powering hyperscale environments and mission-critical workloads across industries.


#### The challenge.


The rapid adoption of AI and large-scale investments in high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructure are straining multiple segments of the supply chain. As the manufacturing client’s execution team grew quickly to meet demand, they needed strategic support to manage onboarding and the talent lifecycle across increasingly complex roles, from materials handling to assembly, installation, burn-in, troubleshooting, and debug.


#### The solution.


The Kelly Digital Infrastructure (KDI) team provided focused resource delivery support to the client’s account team. With 4-5 dedicated professionals in place, the engagement centers on administrative and operational assistance that supports talent development continuity and execution of broader programs.


KDI’s performance has helped open the door for ongoing conversations around long-term workforce development and potential upskilling initiatives, as the client explores new ways to grow and optimize internal talent pipelines.


#### **Strategic impact.**


Though not directly tied to data center builds, this program reflects the foundational role of talent in enabling global digital infrastructure. KDI’s ability to support critical business operations at scale demonstrates its readiness to expand into deeper workforce development partnerships in the hyperscale ecosystem.


#### **What Kelly delivered.**


###### Outcomes


- Enabled rapid ramp-up of a skilled workforce aligned to complex AI infrastructure buildouts


- Supported end-to-end role coverage including logistics, assembly, install, and QA functions


- Provided vetted professionals who understand the nuances of large-scale delivery organizations


- Created opportunities for future engagement in talent development and strategic workforce planning
