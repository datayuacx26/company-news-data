# Engineering talent fuels modular power systems for critical infrastructure.

#### **Client overview.**


A $1.8B+ engineering and technology firm with over 5,000 employees and a 75-year legacy partnered with Kelly Digital Infrastructure (KDI) to hire specialized engineering talent for its manufacturing and modular integration operations. Known for powering mission-critical environments, including data centers, hospitals, and airports, the client needed direct-hire support across design, manufacturing, and electrical construction teams to deliver their plug-and-play modular power systems.


#### The challenge.


To support rapid growth in mission-critical sectors, the client required highly skilled engineering professionals for modular manufacturing operations in Virginia. These roles supported the design and build of advanced power systems for environments where downtime is not an option. The talent challenge was intensified by a hyper-competitive labor market, strict technical criteria, and regional hiring constraints.


#### The solution.


The Kelly Digital Infrastructure (KDI) team partnered closely with the client’s Director of Talent Acquisition to align on both near-term hiring needs and longer-term workforce strategy. Despite internal turnover within the client’s recruiting team, KDI maintained consistency and delivered niche engineering and leadership talent to support Modular Mission-Critical (MMC) builds in Caroline County, VA. This talent enabled the client’s scalable, offsite construction model, reducing reliance on field labor and accelerating deployment timelines.


#### **Strategic impact.**


By securing critical engineering talent early in the design-build lifecycle, KDI enabled the client to scale modular system production and maintain leadership in infrastructure delivery. The partnership improved operational agility and positioned KDI as a trusted partner for future national expansion through programmatic staffing and field service solutions.
