# Scalable staffing solutions for data center construction in emerging markets.

#### **Client overview.**


A leading energy infrastructure and engineering firm supports the construction and development of critical systems across industries, including the rapidly growing data center sector. Their efforts contribute significantly to high-power infrastructure in emerging markets like Salt Lake City, where energy demands are accelerating.


#### The challenge.


The client needed to rapidly scale its project management and technical workforce to meet the explosive growth of data centers, particularly in markets with limited local talent pipelines. Their direct-hire-only model created challenges with retention and ramp-up speed, leading to project delays and costly turnover.


#### The solution.


The Kelly Digital Infrastructure (KDI) team guided the client through a strategic shift from a traditional direct-hire model to a more agile, retention-focused Statement of Work (SOW) structure. This enabled them to deploy talent quickly while maintaining operational stability across critical project phases in regions like Salt Lake City.


#### **Strategic impact.**


By enabling a scalable workforce model, KDI positioned itself as a strategic partner throughout the data center lifecycle. The partnership empowered the client to secure early-stage infrastructure contracts in emerging markets like Salt Lake City and prepare for future expansion in growth hubs such as Mesa, AZ. The success demonstrates how KDI supports clients from construction through commissioning, ensuring workforce readiness at every step.


#### **What Kelly delivered.**


###### Key services


- Transitioned from direct hire to SOW-based engagement


- Placed project schedulers, construction managers, mechanical designers, and engineers


- Delivered recruiting support across Wisconsin and Salt Lake City, UT


- Delivered field support talent for high-capacity data center construction efforts in key emerging markets


###### Outcomes


- Improved worker retention and assignment longevity


- Faster delivery of qualified personnel in a competitive labor market


- Maintained project timelines for high-growth data center clients
