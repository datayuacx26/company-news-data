# Check a Thousand Domains at Once with Bulk Search

We're excited to announce the launch of our new[Bulk Domain Search](https://instantdomainsearch.com/bulk-domain-search) tool, enabling domainers to search up to 1,000 domains at once.


## What's New


For nearly 20 years, our[Domain Search](https://instantdomainsearch.com/) tool has expanded a single query into hundreds of domain options. Now, our new Bulk Domain Search tool consolidates hundreds of domains into a simple list. Filter by availability, price, TLDs, and more.


Whether you're working with AI-generated domain lists, migrating from spreadsheets, or checking variations of your brand, you can now validate entire lists instantly.


No signup, no payment gates. Just paste your list or CSV and instantly check availability at scale. It's 100% free.


Instead, we make commissions when people use our affiliate links to purchase domains. This helps keep our tool free forever. Thank you!


## Key Features


**Search up to 1,000 domains simultaneously**


Import via CSV or paste directly. Our domain search tool delivers results instantly using the same[sub-10ms infrastructure](https://instantdomainsearch.com/learn/research/how-we-make-it-fast) that powers our main domain name search tool.


**Live availability preview**


Domains show availability status as you type them, even before you run the bulk search.


**Comprehensive filtering**


Filter results by availability (available/taken/premium), TLDs (domain extensions), or price range. You can also sort alphabetically.


**Export functionality**


Download your domain name search results as CSV with availability status and pricing data for offline analysis.


**Domain intelligence**


Click into any domain to view SEO metrics including Domain Authority, backlinks, and historical data.


## Use Cases


The tool addresses several specific workflows:


- Validating lists generated by LLMs or[domain generators](https://instantdomainsearch.com/domain-generator)
- Checking brand variations and typos for trademark protection across all[domain extensions](https://instantdomainsearch.com/domain-extensions)
- Evaluating[premium domain](https://instantdomainsearch.com/aftermarket-domains) portfolios before acquisition
- Migrating from spreadsheet-based domain tracking


## Privacy


The bulk search tool is powered by our own internal database of domain availability. This means no bulk queries leave our servers to retail registrars if you don't click to visit their referral links.


## Getting Started


Simply navigate to[instantdomainsearch.com/bulk-domain-search](https://instantdomainsearch.com/bulk-domain-search) and start typing or paste your domain list. You can also upload a CSV file if you prefer. Results appear instantly, showing availability status for each domain. From there, filter by what matters to you - available domains only, specific TLDs, or price ranges. Click any domain to see detailed information and pricing from our registrar partners, or export everything as a CSV for further analysis.
