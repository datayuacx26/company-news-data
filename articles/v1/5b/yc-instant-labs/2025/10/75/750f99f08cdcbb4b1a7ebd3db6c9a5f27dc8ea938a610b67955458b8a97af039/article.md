# Instant Domain Search Raycast Extension | Instant Domain Search

Our domain search tool is now available in Raycast. Check availability without leaving your command bar.


## What's New


We've built a **Raycast extension** that brings domain search directly into your workflow. Start typing and see results populate at the speed of keystroke: powered by the[same fast API](https://instantdomainsearch.com/learn/research/how-we-make-it-fast) that runs[instantdomainsearch.com](https://instantdomainsearch.com/) . Keep your domain searches private from retail registrars, 100% free.


## How it works


**Search Domains:** Start typing a name; 60 results stream in across our 3 search tools: Extensions (TLDs), Domain Name Generator, and Aftermarket Domains. Use **⌘ + P** to filter any of the results.


## Ask @instant-domain-search (MCP)


You can also chat directly with our MCP in Raycast using **@instant-domain-search** . Ask for domain ideas, check availability, or refine a shortlist. It's backed by our Domain Search MCP Server:[instantdomainsearch.com/mcp](https://instantdomainsearch.com/mcp) .


## Why we built the extension


- **Speed:** Quick checks while coding or naming a repo/product, without switching apps.
- **Focus:** Keyboard-first workflow with instant feedback.
- **Consistency:** The same index, pricing, and partner data as our website.
- **Privacy:** Searches run against our infrastructure. We don't send bulk queries to registrars unless you click through to a partner to buy.


## Getting started


Open the Raycast store and search **"Instant Domain Search"** to install the extension.


Run **Search Domains** , start typing, and use **⌘ P** to filter between *Extensions* , *Generator* , and *Premium* . Mention **@instant-domain-search** in the Raycast chat to brainstorm names with our MCP.
