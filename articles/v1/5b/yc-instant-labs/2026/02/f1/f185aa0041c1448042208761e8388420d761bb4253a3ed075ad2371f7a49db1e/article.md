# Smart Partner Routing: Domain Search Just Got Smarter

Instant Domain Search now intelligently matches you with the right registrar for any domain extension.


## How It Works


When you search for a domain, we check your TLD against every registrar in our network: **GoDaddy, Spaceship, Namecheap, Porkbun, Hostinger, Network Solutions, and Dynadot** . The result? You only see purchase options from partners that actually support your domain extension.


Search for` marksmarketing.page` ? We show you GoDaddy, Spaceship, Namecheap, and Porkbun. Partners that don't support` .page` are grayed out with a clear explanation, so you know exactly what's available before you click.


## Why This Matters


- **Every link works.** Every "Register" button takes you somewhere that can complete your purchase.
- **Niche TLDs, fully supported.** We've expanded to 800+ TLDs, and the routing keeps up.
- **Total transparency.** See exactly which registrars support your domain before you click.
- **Getting even smarter.** Soon, smart routing will also factor in pricing to help you find the best deal.
