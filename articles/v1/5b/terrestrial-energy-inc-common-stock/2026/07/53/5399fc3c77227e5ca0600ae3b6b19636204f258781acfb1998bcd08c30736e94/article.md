# Terrestrial Energy Appoints Senior Industry Executive Pamela Cowan as EVP of Engineering

## Terrestrial Energy Appoints Senior Industry Executive Pamela Cowan as EVP of Engineering


July 30, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.terrestrialenergy.com/node/7971/pdf)


CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 30, 2026--


Terrestrial Energy Inc. (Nasdaq: IMSR), a developer of proprietary Generation IV small modular nuclear power plants, today announced the addition of Pamela Cowan as EVP of Engineering, effective immediately. She will be based at the Charlotte, N.C. headquarters and report to Terrestrial Energy CEO Simon Irish.


Cowan brings more than thirty years of experience in the nuclear industry, where she has held executive roles at Westinghouse Electric Company, with responsibility for operating plant services in the Americas, global engineered products and services, and outage field services. She has served as a senior vice president at Holtec International and was chief operating officer of one of Holtec’s principal businesses, leading its operations and the integration of acquired nuclear plant assets. Earlier, she held leadership roles at nuclear generating companies, including Exelon Generation (Constellation), American Electric Power and PSEG, where she held a senior reactor operator (SRO) license.


“Pam adds exceptional industry leadership and technical experience at an ideal time as the company expands its engineering and regulatory programs,” said Simon Irish, CEO of Terrestrial Energy. “She brings decades of experience in nearly every relevant area of nuclear technology development and plant operation.”


“I am excited to be part of Terrestrial Energy’s plans to bring the IMSR plant to market at this exceptional moment for the nuclear industry,” said Cowan. “The company is positioned as a best-in-class SMR provider of nuclear energy to multiple industry sectors, with the goal to enable a future of clean, firm and abundant energy.”


Cowan joins Terrestrial Energy as the company advances its IMSR Plant design through NRC regulatory review and grows its pipeline of projects. She will be leading the company’s engineering program, serving as the company's designated technical authority for nuclear safety and licensing, and working with commercial leadership to strengthen customer and supplier relationships supporting IMSR Plant deployment.


**About Terrestrial Energy**


Terrestrial Energy is a developer of Generation IV nuclear plants that use its proprietary Integral Molten Salt Reactor (IMSR). The IMSR captures the transformative operating benefits of molten salt reactor technology in a plant design that represents true innovation in capital efficiency, cost reduction, versatility and functionality of nuclear energy supply. IMSR plants are designed to be small and modular for distributed supply of low-cost, reliable, dispatchable, clean, high-temperature industrial heat and electricity and to be customized for a dual-use energy role relevant to many industrial applications, such as petrochemical and chemical synthesis and data center operation. In so doing, IMSR plants extend the application of nuclear energy far beyond electric power markets. Their deployment will support the rapid growth of clean firm heat and power, delivering energy self-reliance, grid reliability and economic growth. Terrestrial Energy uses an innovative plant design together with proven and demonstrated molten salt reactor technology and readily available and inexpensive standard-assay low-enriched uranium in its fuel for a nuclear plant with a unique set of operating characteristics and compelling transformative commercial potential. Terrestrial Energy is engaged with regulators, suppliers, industrial partners and energy end users to build, license and commission the first IMSR plants in the early 2030s.
