# Tortoise Capital Announces Correction to Previously Reported Net Asset Values for Tortoise Global Water ETF (TBLU)

**OVERLAND PARK, Kan.** – August 5, 2026 – Tortoise Capital Advisors, L.L.C, investment adviser to the Tortoise Global Water ETF (NYSE: TBLU) (the “Fund”), today announced that it identified an error in the Fund’s previously reported net asset values (“NAVs”) for July 20, 2026, and July 21, 2026. The error caused the Fund’s NAVs to be overstated on the affected dates. The previously reported NAVs for the respective dates will be corrected and restated.


The Corrected NAVs are as follows:


**Valuation Date** **Previously Reported NAV/Share** **Corrected NAV/Share**


July 20, 2026 $ 52.31 $ 51.05


July 21, 2026 $ 51.81 $ 50.58
