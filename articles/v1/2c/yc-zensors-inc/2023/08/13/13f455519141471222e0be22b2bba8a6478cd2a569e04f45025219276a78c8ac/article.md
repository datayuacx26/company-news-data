# Zensors AI paints the future of aviation at ForsaTEK

‍


On March 9 the Emirates Group hosted the first annual innovation event called ForsaTEK. Held at Emirates Group Headquarters in Dubai, this program pulls together two startup programs -[Aviation X Lab](https://www.theaviationxlab.com/) and Intelak - that are aimed at driving the future of aviation and travel.


‍


As one of the finalists in Aviation X Lab, Zensors had the opportunity to pitch to an audience of high-profile tech and industry partners and then demonstrate its[Computer Vision and AI solutions](https://www.zensors.com/vision-ai-soltuions/zensors-vision-ai-airport-solutions) in one of several exhibition booths. In addition, Zensors will implement its Lounge Management solution with Emirates in the business class lounge at Dubai International Airport.


‍


For more information and video of the event see[Emirates post](https://twitter.com/emirates/status/1634177539642912770) and a summary on[Emirates site](https://www.theemiratesgroup.com/media-centre/the-emirates-group-builds-innovation-platform-to-spearhead-the-future-of-aviation-and-travel/) .
