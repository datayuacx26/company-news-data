# Cardiff Oncology to Participate in the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference

## Cardiff Oncology to Participate in the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference


June 1, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.cardiffoncology.com/node/12251/pdf)


SAN DIEGO, June 01, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Cardiff Oncology, Inc. (Nasdaq: CRDF), a clinical-stage biotechnology company leveraging PLK1 inhibition to develop novel therapies across a range of cancers, today announced that company management will present and participate in 1x1 investor meetings at the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference on June 2-4, 2026.


Details of the presentation can be found below.


**Format:** Fireside Chat
**Presenter:** Mani Mohindru, PhD (President and CEO)
**Date:** 06/04/2026
**Time:** 10:30 -11:00 AM ET


Interested parties can register for and access the live webcast for the conference by visiting the “[Events](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=wz3xeBu9Q8zG7q16FFxiE8QxMIzyL8XmsG1x96NQqDdy0C96fEhAVci3yYhVqcW1UerOcWwHteU8gUokT7TMm2zXJodmbpmFbztD8fNNbb_hZ7tpV1Ah2H1YdGZSoMls) ” section of the Cardiff Oncology website. The webcast replay will be available after the conclusion of the presentation.


**About Cardiff Oncology, Inc.**


Cardiff Oncology is a clinical-stage biotechnology company advancing innovative cancer treatments focused on PLK1 inhibition, a validated oncology target with practice-changing potential. Our lead asset, onvansertib, is a highly specific, oral PLK1 inhibitor currently being evaluated in a Phase 2 trial for first-line treatment of RAS-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC), addressing a large, underserved patient population with high unmet need. Onvansertib is also under investigation in other PLK1-driven cancers through ongoing investigator-initiated trials and has shown robust single agent clinical activity in hard-to-treat tumors. By targeting tumor vulnerabilities, we aim to overcome treatment resistance and deliver improved clinical outcomes for patients.
