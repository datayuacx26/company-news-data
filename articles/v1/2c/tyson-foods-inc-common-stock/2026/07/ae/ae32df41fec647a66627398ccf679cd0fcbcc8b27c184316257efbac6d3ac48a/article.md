# Turning Up the Heat: Consumers Can’t Get Enough of Bigger, Bolder Flavors

From hot honey and spicy beverages to jalapeño-topped burgers and chili crisp everything, it’s clear that bold flavors are having a moment.


Spicy foods have evolved from a niche preference into a mainstream culinary trend, showing up across restaurant menus, social media feeds and grocery store aisles. Today’s diners aren’t simply looking for more heat; they’re looking for exciting experiences that combine spice with sweetness, smokiness and savory depth.


Research across Tyson Foods categories continues to show growing interest in spices, particularly among younger shoppers eager to explore new tastes and combinations. As preferences evolve,[our family of brands](https://www.tysonfoods.com/our-brands) is helping satisfy that appetite through[innovation](https://www.tysonfoods.com/innovation) and new product lines.


##### **A Trend That’s Heating Up**


The appeal of this trend goes beyond heat alone.


Today, we are all increasingly interested in flavor discovery, and spicy ingredients often make it easy to elevate familiar foods. Jalapeño, chipotle, hot honey, chili crisp and sweet-heat combos are now staples in home kitchens and restaurants alike. And online chatter has only accelerated the trend, encouraging people to experiment with new ingredients.


At the same time, profiles that combine sweet, smoky and spicy elements continue to gain popularity because they offer both familiarity and excitement. It’s a trend influencing innovation across the food industry, and one that Tyson Foods is leaning into across brands and meal occasions.


##### **Bringing Bold to Breakfast**


For those looking to start their day right,[Jimmy Dean](https://www.jimmydean.com/) ® has introduced products that bring the spice to the breakfast table.[Jimmy Dean Premium Hot Bacon](https://www.jimmydean.com/products/premium-bacon/00077900003400) delivers a bolder take on a breakfast favorite, while the[Jimmy Dean Chipotle Protein Bowl](https://www.jimmydean.com/products/protein-options/00077900003509) combines chipotle-seasoned chicken thighs, fluffy scrambled eggs and shredded Monterey Jack cheese in a convenient, protein-packed option.


These offerings reflect a growing appetite for[breakfast options](https://www.jimmydean.com/products) that pair convenience with more adventurous tastes.


##### **Turning Up the Heat at the Grill**


Spice is also finding its way into one of America’s favorite summer traditions: the backyard cookout.


[Ball Park ® Bun Size Jalapeño Beef Franks](https://www.ballparkbrand.com/products/hot-dogs/00054500000480) bring a flavorful kick to the hot dog category, capitalizing on strong consumer familiarity and interest in jalapeño-inspired products. Category insights show jalapeño remains one of the most recognized and appealing flavors among shoppers, making it a natural addition to the grill lineup.


As we all search for new ways to elevate classic cookout foods, products that blend comfort and bold concepts are bringing a fresh energy to these favorites.


##### **Smoke, Sweet Heat and Sausage Innovation**


Perhaps nowhere is the spicy trend more evident than in the sausage category, where Tyson Foods brands are introducing a variety of new flavor-forward options.


[Wright](https://www.wrightbrand.com/) TM Brand recently expanded its lineup with[Sweet Heat Peach](https://www.wrightbrand.com/products/sausage-links/00079621005718) and[Red Pepper Pitmaster](https://www.wrightbrand.com/products/sausage-links/00079621005671) smoked sausage links, pairing real wood-smoked sausage with sweet-and-savory combos inspired by adventurous taste buds.


But for those who enjoy even a little more kick,[Wright TM Smoked Jalapeño & Cheddar Sausage Rope](https://www.wrightbrand.com/products/sausage-ropes/00079621005701) combines smokiness with jalapeño heat and cheddar cheese, while[Hillshire Farm](https://www.hillshirefarm.com/) ® introduced products such as[Jalapeño Cheddar Sausage Links](https://www.hillshirefarm.com/products/sausage-links/00044500004434) ,[Spicy Italian Sausage Rope](https://www.hillshirefarm.com/products/smoked-sausages/00044500003253) and[Hot Smoked Sausage](https://www.hillshirefarm.com/products/smoked-sausages/00044500341751) —bringing a boost to weeknight meals and backyard gatherings alike.


Together, these innovations show how demand for spice is influencing product development across categories.


##### **What’s Next for Spice?**


While food trends come and go, these flavors show few signs of slowing down.


Whether dining out or cooking at home, people continue to gravitate toward foods that offer something unexpected. Whether it’s a touch of pepper, a sweet-and-spicy combination or a smoke-forward profile, daring products are becoming an important part of everyday meals.


At Tyson Foods, we’re continuing to explore new ways to meet those evolving tastes across our portfolio. From breakfast to barbecue and everything in between, our brands are helping bring more excitement to the table, one (hot) bite at a time.
