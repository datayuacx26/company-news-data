As families look for ways to make the most of their grocery dollars without sacrificing flavor, pork stands out as a smart and versatile option. Lean, tender and quick to cook, it offers the quality and flexibility home cooks need for everything from weeknight meals to weekend gatherings. Whether paired with seasonal produce, grilled alongside fresh vegetables or dressed up with bold sauces or rubs, pork makes it easy to create memorable meals while keeping mealtimes simple.


##### **Tips for Cooking**


One reason pork tenderloin is a tried-and-true favorite among home chefs is its simplicity. But being in the know on a few basic techniques can help anyone achieve juicy, flavorful results every time:


- ***Don’t Overcook It*** ***:*** Most cuts of pork cook quickly, so using a meat thermometer can help ensure the perfect result. For the best eating experience, cook until the internal temperature reaches 145 °F, and allow cuts, like a pork tenderloin, to rest before slicing.
- ***Season with Confidence:*** Pork pairs beautifully with herbs, spices, fruit-forward ingredients and bold rubs. From garlic and rosemary to coriander and apple-based flavors, there are countless ways to customize the center of your meal.
- ***Let it Rest:*** Allowing pork to rest after cooking helps retain its juices, resulting in a more tender and flavorful bite.
- ***Embrace the Grill:*** Grilling brings out pork’s natural flavor while adding a delicious smoky char. It’s also a great way to prepare vegetables and side dishes to round out the meal.
