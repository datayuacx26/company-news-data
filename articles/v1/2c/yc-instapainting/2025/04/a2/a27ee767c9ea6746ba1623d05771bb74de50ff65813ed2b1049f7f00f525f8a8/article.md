# Instapainting Review: Perfect for Pets

Recently Instapainting got[reviewed by dogster](https://www.dogster.com/lifestyle/instapainting-custom-oil-painting-review) for an order of a custom pet dog portrait oil painting! Take a look at the review if you’re wondering what the process is like and what results you can expect as an actual customer. It goes into detail from the ordering process to the artist communication that happens afterwards, to the packaging and delivery of your custom made painting to your doorstep.
