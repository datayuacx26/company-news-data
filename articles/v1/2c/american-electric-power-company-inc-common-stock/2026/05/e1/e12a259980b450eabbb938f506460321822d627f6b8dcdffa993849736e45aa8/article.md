# Smart Energy Tips for Gas Stations & Food Marts During the Memorial Day Rush

[Back to Blog](https://www.aepenergy.com/blog)


*3 MIN READ*


Memorial Day weekend marks the unofficial start of summer — and one of the busiest travel periods of the year. With millions of drivers hitting the road, gas stations and food marts become essential pit stops. High traffic means high opportunity, but it also means higher energy consumption, longer operating hours, and more pressure on your equipment.


The good news: a few smart energy moves can keep your site efficient, comfortable, and profitable during the holiday surge.


1. **Optimize Your Refrigeration Before the Crowds Arrive**


Refrigeration is one of the biggest energy drains in a convenience store, especially when customers are constantly opening cooler doors.


- Check door seals — A loose or cracked gasket can waste hundreds of dollars in cold air loss.
- Pre-cool your units — Lower temperatures slightly the night before peak traffic so units don’t struggle to recover.
- Stock efficiently — Avoid overpacking shelves, which restricts airflow and forces compressors to work harder.


A well-tuned cooler keeps drinks cold and customers happy without spiking your energy bill.


1. **Use Smart Lighting to Handle Extended Hours**


Lighting can account for up to 40% of a gas station’s energy use during busy weekends.


- Switch to LEDs — They use up to 75% less energy and last longer.
- Install motion sensors in low-traffic areas like storage rooms and back offices.
- Adjust canopy lighting schedules — Bright enough for safety, but not wasteful.


Good lighting boosts visibility, safety, and curb appeal — all essential when travelers are choosing where to stop.


1. **Keep HVAC Running Efficiently in High-Traffic Conditions**


With doors opening constantly, your HVAC system works overtime.


- Replace or clean filters before the holiday weekend.
- Use smart thermostats to maintain consistent temperatures.
- Seal gaps around doors to reduce cool-air loss.


A comfortable store encourages customers to linger — and buy more.


1. **Manage Fuel Pump Energy Use**


Fuel pumps run continuously, but you can still reduce waste.


- Check pump canopy sensors to ensure lights aren’t running during daylight.
- Inspect pump air compressors for leaks that cause unnecessary cycling.
- Use energy-efficient pump lighting to cut overnight costs.


Small improvements add up fast when pumps run 24/7.


1. **Prepare Your Ice Machines and Freezers for Heavy Demand**


Ice is one of the top-selling items during Memorial Day weekend — and one of the biggest energy hogs.


- Clean condenser coils to improve efficiency.
- Empty and sanitize bins so machines don’t overwork.
- Pre-produce ice during off-peak hours when electricity rates may be lower.


A reliable ice supply keeps customers happy and boosts impulse sales.


1. **Train Staff on Quick Energy Wins**


Your team is your first line of defense against energy waste.


- Teach simple shutdown routines for equipment not needed during peak hours.
- Assign cooler-door checks to prevent propping or unnecessary openings.
- Encourage fast restocking to minimize open-door time.


A few minutes of training can save hours of wasted energy.


1. **Use Data to Predict and Prepare for Peak Times**


If your POS or fuel system tracks traffic patterns, use that data.


- Identify peak hours and pre-cool or pre-stock accordingly.
- Adjust staffing to reduce bottlenecks and keep doors closed more often.
- Monitor energy spikes to catch equipment issues early.


Data-driven prep keeps your site running smoothly even when the parking lot is packed.


1. **Promote Your Energy-Smart Upgrades to Travelers**


Customers love knowing they’re supporting a business that’s efficient and environmentally conscious.


- Highlight LED upgrades on signage.
- Advertise efficient refrigeration as part of your “coldest drinks in town” message.
- Share sustainability efforts on social media.


Energy efficiency isn’t just cost-saving — it’s a marketing advantage.


**Final Takeaway**


Memorial Day weekend is a massive opportunity for gas stations and food marts. With smart energy practices, you can handle the surge, reduce operational stress, and keep your energy costs under control — all while delivering a better experience for travelers.
