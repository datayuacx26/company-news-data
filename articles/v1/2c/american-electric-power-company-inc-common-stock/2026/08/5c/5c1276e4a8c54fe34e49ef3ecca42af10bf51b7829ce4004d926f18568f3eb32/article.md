# Get Your Home Ready for Back-to-School Season

[Back to Blog](https://www.aepenergy.com/blog)


As the back-to-school season arrives, many households are settling back into routines after a busy summer. Schedules fill up, mornings become a little more hectic, and laptops, tablets, and other devices often see more daily use.


While school supplies and packed lunches may be top of mind, the start of the school year is also a great opportunity to revisit some important safety habits around the home. Taking time to review safety essentials can create a safer, more organized environment and provide peace of mind throughout the months ahead.


**Create a Safe Space for Learning**


Whether assignments are completed at a kitchen table, a home office, or a dedicated study area, it’s important to set up the space with safety in mind.


Take a few minutes to:


- Check that desks, chairs, and shelving are sturdy and in good condition.
- Organize cords and chargers to reduce tripping hazards.
- Ensure electronics are plugged into appropriate outlets or power strips.
- Use adequate lighting to reduce eye strain.


An organized workspace not only makes it easier to stay focused, but it can also prevent common household accidents.


**Check Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarms**


Smoke alarms and carbon monoxide detectors work quietly in the background, so it’s easy to forget about them until they’re needed.


Before the school year begins, test alarms throughout your home and replace batteries if necessary. Also, make sure detectors are not blocked by furniture or storage items that could prevent them from functioning properly.


Regularly checking these devices can keep your home protected year-round.


**Review Your Emergency Plan**


Many families spend time reviewing schedules and routines before a new school year begins. Consider adding emergency preparedness to that list and taking time to review:


- Emergency contact information
- Fire escape routes
- Severe weather procedures
- Family meeting locations


Having a plan in place can ensure everyone feels prepared if an unexpected situation occurs.


**Organize for Safer Mornings**


As the school year picks up, so does the amount of everyday clutter that can accumulate around the home.


Backpacks, lunch boxes, sports gear, shoes, and other items often end up in hallways, entryways, and on staircases. Creating designated storage areas can help keep frequently used items organized and reduce the risk of trips and falls during busy mornings.


Taking time to organize before school starts can make mornings feel less rushed.


**Practice Safe Technology Habits**


For many households, back-to-school season also means increased use of electronics. Between laptops, tablets, phones, printers, and gaming systems, charging stations can quickly become overcrowded.


To keep devices operating safely:


- Use manufacturer-approved chargers when possible
- Avoid overloading outlets.
- Keep electronics away from water.
- Avoid charging devices under blankets, pillows, or other materials that can trap heat.


Practicing these habits can protect your devices while reducing potential electrical and fire hazards around the home.


**Stay Alert During Back-to-School**


The return to school often means busier roads, crowded parking lots, and more pedestrians and cyclists sharing the roadway.


Whether you’re commuting to work, running errands, or heading to after-school activities, remember to:


- Obey school zone speed limits.
- Watch for pedestrians and cyclists.
- Follow school bus safety laws.
- Minimize distractions while driving.
- Allow extra travel time to avoid rushing.


Back-to-school traffic can be unpredictable, making awareness and defensive driving more important than ever.


**Prepare for Seasonal Weather Changes**


Back-to-school season also signals the transition toward fall, bringing the potential for thunderstorms, high winds, and other changing weather conditions.


Make sure your household is prepared for unexpected events by checking flashlights, replacing old batteries, and confirming that emergency supplies are easy to locate. Portable phone chargers can also be helpful during power outages.


**Start the School Year with Safety in Mind**


The back-to-school season involves more than shopping for supplies and updating calendars. It’s also an opportunity to review household safety habits to ensure your home is ready for the busy months ahead.
