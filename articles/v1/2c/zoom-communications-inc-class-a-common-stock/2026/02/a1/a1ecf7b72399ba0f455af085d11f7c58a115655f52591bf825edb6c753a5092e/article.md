# Zoom recognized across multiple categories in G2’s 2026 Best Software Awards

Zoom Communications, Inc., today announced it has been named as an award recipient in multiple categories by


[G2’s 2026 Best Software Awards](https://www.g2.com/best-software-companies?utm_source=g2&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=best-software-2026) . As the world’s largest and most trusted software marketplace,


[G2](https://www.g2.com/?utm_source=g2&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=best-software-2026) reaches over 100 million buyers annually. Its annual Best Software Awards rank the world’s best software companies and products based on authentic, timely reviews from real users.


Zoom received the following recognition in the G2 Best Software Awards:


- Zoom – Top 100 Global Seller


- Zoom Workplace – Top 100 Best Software Products


- Zoom Workplace – Best Office Management Products


- Zoom Workplace – Best Education Products


- Zoom Workplace – Best Collaboration & Productivity Software


- Zoom Phone – Best Collaboration & Productivity Software


- Zoom Phone – Highest Satisfaction Products


- Zoom AI Companion – Best Agentic AI Software


The recognition validates Zoom’s mission to provide an AI-first work platform for human connection.


“Being recognized in G2’s Best Software Awards is a tremendous honor and a testament to Zoom’s commitment to delivering a system of action that provides meaningful connection and collaboration for organizations of all sizes, from solopreneurs to enterprises,” said Kim Storin, chief marketing officer at Zoom. “We are deeply grateful for the positive feedback from our users on G2, which drives us to continually innovate and enhance the Zoom experience.”


“As buyers increasingly shift to AI-driven research to discover software solutions, being recommended in the ‘answer moment’ must be earned with credible proof,” said Godard Abel, co-founder and CEO at G2. “Our Best Software Awards are grounded in trusted data from authentic customer reviews. They not only give buyers an objective, reliable guide to the products that help teams do their best work, but they’re also the proof AI search platforms rely on when sourcing answers. Congratulations to this year’s winners, including Zoom. Earning a spot on these lists signals real customer impact.”


**About G2’s Best Software Awards**


G2’s 2026 Best Software Awards feature dozens of award lists, ranking software vendors and products using G2’s proprietary algorithm. The results are based on G2’s verified user reviews and publicly available market presence data. To be eligible for the Best Software Awards, a software company or product must have received at least 10 approved reviews during the 2025 calendar year. Scores reflect only data from reviews submitted during this evaluation period.
