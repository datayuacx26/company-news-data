# Zoom Delivers Mission-Critical Phone Resiliency for Federal Agencies with Zoom Phone Local Survivability

Today, Zoom announced the general availability of Zoom Phone Local Survivability (ZPLS) for Zoom for Government (ZfG), bringing Zoom’s resilient cloud telephony continuity to U.S. federal agencies.


For agencies operating Cisco, Avaya, or Poly desk phones on legacy on-premises PBX systems, transitioning to cloud calling has often raised concerns about maintaining voice continuity during network or cloud disruptions. ZPLS addresses these challenges by extending core Zoom Phone services into agency-controlled environments while operating in conjunction with Zoom Phone in the cloud. This hybrid approach enables agencies to maintain local calling capabilities during service disruptions while supporting cloud modernization. ZPLS also integrates with existing telephony handsets and Microsoft Teams environments, helping agencies preserve existing infrastructure investments as they transition to a modern communications platform.


Key benefits include:


- **Reduced infrastructure complexity:** Eliminate the need for complex router-based survivability deployments, reducing hardware, maintenance, management, and testing.


- **Continuity when it matters most:** Essential voice services remain available during cloud or network outages — critical for agencies with continuity of operations (COOP) requirements baked into their RFPs and mission plans.


- **Hybrid cloud and local architecture:** ZPLS operates in conjunction with Zoom Phone in the cloud and integrates with Microsoft Teams, enabling agencies to support hybrid communications environments while maintaining local calling capabilities.


- **FedRAMP-certified and government-ready:** Built for federal security and data sovereignty requirements, with scalable architecture supporting up to 5,000 registered devices per module across agency sites.


“The launch of ZPLS reflects Zoom’s continued investment in the Zoom for Government platform,” said Chris Boyd, Head of US Federal Sales at Zoom. “In addition to Zoom Workplace and Zoom Contact Center, which have already been FedRAMP certified, Zoom has made large strides the past year in expanding its portfolio of FedRAMP-certified capabilities, including Zoom Node, Zoom AI features, Zoom Canvas, Zoom Chat enhancements, and more, further strengthening its secure communications platform for government customers.”


With ZPLS now generally available, federal agencies no longer have to choose between the resilience of on-premise calling and the efficiency of cloud modernization. Zoom delivers both — on a FedRAMP-certified phone system built for the way the government actually operates.
