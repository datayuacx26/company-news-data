# Zoom Named a Leader in the 2026 IDC MarketScape for Worldwide Virtual Events Applications

Zoom is proud to share that Zoom Events has been named a Leader in the


**IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Virtual Events Applications 2026 Vendor Assessment (doc #** **US54140026, July 2026)** .


In a rapidly changing landscape where virtual events are increasingly serving as a critical touchpoint for pipeline generation, customer retention, and brand engagement, Zoom believes this recognition reflects its ongoing commitment to delivering an intuitive, reliable, and multi-functional event platform for organizations of all sizes.


### **Redefining Virtual Events as Engagement Platforms**


The virtual event market has undergone a fundamental shift. Today’s audiences expect more than passive viewing; they want interactive, meaningful engagement.


As noted in the report:


*“Zoom views this as an engagement platform, not just a ‘broadcast’ channel.”*


According to the IDC MarketScape evaluation:


*“Much like its conferencing product, Zoom Events was designed not only ‘to just work’ but also to meet the fast-evolving requirements of event producers and their stakeholders.”*


Zoom believes our position in the Leaders Category stems from a deliberate focus on reducing technical friction for hosts and attendees while giving event organizers the deep engagement, branding, and content management capabilities they need.


### **Key Capabilities Driving Enterprise Value**


The report noted the following under Zoom’s strengths section:


“Familiarity translates to ease of use, especially for attendees. Zoom Events pricing is attractive and considered highly transparent, even with the optional Custom AI capabilities. Zoom has accelerated its feature rollouts, and the product will continue to be a strong pillar for the company.”


“Zoom Video Management and Account-level grouping address two needed features and will continue to be enhanced over time.”


“Zoom Events operates under Zoom’s comprehensive compliance framework, which includes SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001/27018, FedRAMP authorization, and TX-RAMP Level 2 certification, along with government-specific standards across the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Spain, and Japan, as well as support for HIPAA (non-native) and PCI compliance.”


### **What the IDC MarketScape Recommends for Technology Buyers**


When evaluating virtual event platforms, the IDC MarketScape advises organizations to consider how platforms fit into their broader engagement and technology ecosystem:


*“Consider Zoom Events when your organization is looking for an easy-to-use virtual events platform, or when it is already standardized on Zoom and wants a familiar, low-friction path to virtual events, with strong in-session engagement tools, professional production capabilities, and a continuous release cadence.”*

### **About IDC MarketScape**


IDC MarketScape vendor assessment model is designed to provide an overview of the competitive fitness of technology and service suppliers in a given market. The research utilizes a rigorous scoring methodology based on both qualitative and quantitative criteria that results in a single graphical illustration of each supplier’s position within a given market. IDC MarketScape provides a clear framework in which the product and service offerings, capabilities and strategies, and current and future market success factors of technology suppliers can be meaningfully compared. The framework also provides technology buyers with a 360-degree assessment of the strengths and weaknesses of current and prospective suppliers.
