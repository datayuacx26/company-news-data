# Zoom and Nayeema Raza Partner to Explore the Future of Work Through Original Conversations and Storytelling

Today, Zoom announced *Smart Girl Dumb Questions* x Zoom, a new collaboration with journalist and podcaster Nayeema Raza to explore the future of work through original video interviews, podcast storytelling, and live experiences. Together, Zoom and Nayeema will spotlight the entrepreneurs, creators, executives, and innovators shaping how people build businesses, lead teams, and collaborate in a rapidly changing world.


This partnership is part of Zoom’s strategy to earn a place inside conversations and platforms Zoom doesn’t own or control. Nayeema’s work will remain editorially independent as she explores the themes that shape the future or work and leadership culture with her signature curiosity, journalistic rigour, and cheeky wit. Alongside the Soloprenuer 50 program and Zoombassador program, it reflects a deliberate bet that the future-of-work story lands harder coming from Nayeema’s objective voice than from Zoom’s.


It also reflects Zoom’s belief that understanding the future of work starts by listening to the people shaping it. This is especially true now, as AI reshapes what a team, a business, and a workday even look like. Across social-first interviews, a special podcast series, and potential live experiences, Zoom and Nayeema will bring together diverse voices to explore how technology, leadership, entrepreneurship, and changing workplace expectations are transforming the way we work.


As work continues to evolve, Zoom is investing not only in technology, but also in the conversations that help people better understand what’s changing, what’s working, and what’s next.


## **Why Nayeema?**


Throughout her career, Nayeema has built a reputation for asking thoughtful questions that challenge assumptions and uncover new perspectives. Through


*Smart Girl Dumb Questions* , she’s created a trusted platform for conversations about business, technology, leadership, and culture.


As an entrepreneur herself, she understands firsthand what it means to build something independently, making her a natural partner as Zoom expands its investment in stories and conversations about the future of work.


“We could tell this story on our own channels and call it done. But the future-of-work story lands differently when it comes from someone people already trust as an expert, not just a brand,” said


**Kimberly Storin** ,


**Chief Marketing Officer** at Zoom. “That’s why we wanted to be part of Nayeema’s world.”


“I’m interested in the big cultural questions shaping our future,” said


**Nayeema Raza** . “A central question for workers, business leaders, creatives and our whole economy is what work looks like ahead: how do we do it, why do we do it, how much identity do we derive from it, and what should/will it look like in 5 or 15 years. I’m excited to partner with Zoom to explore these questions with unabashed curiosity, creativity, and independence”


## **What’s Coming**


The collaboration will come to life across three connected experiences that explore the future of work from different perspectives and across multiple formats.


### **Zoomterviews**


Beginning the week of July 20, Zoom and Nayeema will launch


**Zoomterviews** , a new social-first interview series featuring members of the Solopreneur 50.


Filmed entirely on Zoom, each conversation highlights an entrepreneur redefining what it means to build a successful business, from florists and coaches to designers, artists, and even cat trainers. Through thoughtful questions and candid conversations, the series explores the creativity, resilience, and individuality behind today’s growing solopreneur movement.


New interviews will premiere weekly across Zoom’s social channels, with extended conversations available on YouTube.


### **“What Even Is Work?”**


Later this summer, Zoom will present


**What Even Is Work?** , a special limited series part of


*Smart Girl Dumb Questions* .


Hosted by Nayeema, the series will feature conversations with business leaders, entrepreneurs, and cultural voices exploring how technology, leadership, flexibility, and changing workforce expectations are reshaping modern work. Each episode invites listeners to think differently about the ideas, trends, and people defining what’s next.


### **Zoomtopia**


The partnership will also come to life on stage at


**Zoomtopia** , where Nayeema will host a live


*Smart Girl Dumb Questions* conversation with customers, creators, and business leaders exploring the ideas shaping the future of work.


Together, these three experiences create an ongoing platform for conversations that extend beyond a single interview, podcast episode, or live event, bringing together diverse voices to explore how work is changing and where it’s headed next.
