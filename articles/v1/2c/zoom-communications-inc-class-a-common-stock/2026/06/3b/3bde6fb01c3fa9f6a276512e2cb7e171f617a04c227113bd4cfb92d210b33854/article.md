# Zoom CX at CCW 2026: New AI contact center tools for visibility, resolution, and ROI

At CCW Las Vegas 2026,[Zoom CX](https://www.zoom.com/en/audiences/cx/) delivered a clear message to contact center and customer experience leaders: deploying AI is only the beginning. The real challenge is visibility: knowing whether AI is working, where it's failing, and how to improve it.


In this blog, we'll explore the latest innovations from Zoom CX, including what launched this week at Customer Contact Week (CCW), and real customer outcomes proving the platform story.


## What is Zoom CX?


Zoom CX is Zoom's contact center and customer experience platform, bringing together[Zoom Virtual Agent](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/virtual-agent/) (AI-powered self-service),[Zoom Contact Center](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/contact-center/) (omnichannel routing and agent desktop),[AI Expert Assist](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/ai-expert-assist/) ,[CX Insights,](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/cx-insights/) Workforce Management and Quality Management into a single, unified system. Unlike point solutions that require separate vendors for automation, analytics, and compliance, Zoom CX brings each layer of the contact center onto one platform, with one customer data model, one agent desktop, and one administrative layer.


## The big idea: "Deployed isn't done" at CCW 2026


One of the most anticipated sessions at CCW 2026 was a fireside chat with[Kentis Gopalla](https://linkedin.com/in/kentisgopalla?skipRedirect=true&miniProfileUrn=urn%3Ali%3Afs_miniProfile%3AACoAAABZGU4BR6_q1DVPJl5l-QKB4e1ZbaQms7g) , Head of Product for WEM and the ZCX Ecosystem.


Gopalla tackled the question on everyone's mind: you bought the platform, you ran the pilot, you presented the business case. So why are customers still repeating themselves, agents still drowning in wrap-up work, and leadership still asking where the ROI is?


His message was clear: **deploying AI is not the finish line. It's the starting line.**


> "You don't have an AI problem. You have a visibility problem. The data to improve your customer outcomes already exists inside your conversations, your operations, your workforce metrics. The question is whether you're using it."


## Kentis Gopalla, Head of Product for WEM and the ZCX Ecosystem


Systems built for isolated interactions often lack shared memory, persistent context, and a clear path to resolution. Gopalla identified four stages where AI investment stalls:


- **No data to work with:** Point solutions give one slice of the picture. Without a unified data model, AI has nothing meaningful to act on.
- **Data locked in silos:** The data exists across systems, but fragmented stacks mean it can't be acted on in real time.
- **No insights tool:** Data alone isn't enough. You need something that surfaces patterns and signals, not just exports and reports.
- **No feedback loop:** If findings never route back into AI systems, coaching queues, or knowledge bases, the loop never closes.


Gopalla then laid out a practical framework for closing the gap: **drive self-service resolution, optimize in real time, and close the loop with insights.**


The goal isn't managing more conversations. It's completing them.[Zoom CX is built to deliver with persistent context across every channel](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/ai-connects-conversations/) and less behind-the-scenes friction burning out agents.

## Scale your human agents with AI Expert Assist 3.0


Zoom Contact Center's[AI Expert Assist 3.0](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/ai-expert-assist/) is a hybrid agentic and intent-based system: a meaningful step beyond classic intent-only tools that surface suggestions without acting on them. Four capabilities define this release:


**Smart orchestration.** When an agent handles a complex interaction with guidance coming from multiple sources, Smart Orchestration consolidates that guidance into a single, unified action path. Agents get one clear next step, not a list of competing suggestions.


**Workflow automation.** AI Expert Assist can now complete multi-step tasks in third-party systems — with human approval required before execution: CRM updates, order lookups, and case creation on the agent's behalf. Agents review and confirm before the system acts, keeping humans in the decision loop while eliminating repetitive task work.


**Redesigned Expert Assist Agent Builder.** The configuration interface has been rebuilt around guided, generative prompt-driven setup. Building a new Expert Assist workflow that previously took weeks now takes minutes.


**Agent tracing.** Every AI action within Expert Assist generates an auditable record: what the AI reasoned, which tools it used, and what decision it reached. Agent Tracing gives supervisors and compliance teams the visibility they need to trust that AI is working as intended, and to identify and correct it when it isn't.


**Live AI interpreter:** Zoom Contact Center now supports bi-directional simultaneous interpretation, natively built into the platform. It works across both voice and digital channels, eliminating language as a constraint on which agents can handle which customers.


## Chris Morrissey on Zoom's AI-native contact center vision


General Manager of Zoom CX,[Chris Morrissey,](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cjmorrissey/) sat down with CX Today for an on-site video interview at CCW. The conversation covered Zoom's AI-native approach to the contact center, and why a unified platform produces fundamentally different outcomes than a stack of point solutions.


The interview covered five themes:


**AI that is native, not bolted on.** It's not about the number of AI features on a product sheet. It's whether[AI is embedded natively across the full contact center stack](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/contact-center/resources/contact-center-ai/) , so automation, agent assist, analytics, and compliance all share the same data model and can act on context that siloed tools can't see.


**Outcomes over features.** The conversation was anchored in measurable results, not positioning. Resolution rates, containment, CSAT, and cost per interaction were the benchmarks, not feature counts.


**The unified agent experience.** Most agents are still tabbing between multiple windows in their contact center. When Zoom Virtual Agent escalates to a live agent, the full customer context (issue type, prior interactions, sentiment signals) travels with it, with no reintroduction and no data handoff gap.


**Human-first AI.** Human oversight is a design principle in every AI capability Zoom ships. AI is built to assist and elevate agents, not replace them: Workflow Automation requires human approval before executing tasks, and Agent Tracing gives supervisors a full audit trail of every AI decision.


**The tension CX leaders face.** Boards want AI ROI, customers want to feel heard, and agents are asked to deliver both at once. Morrissey shared his perspective on where the line sits and what "human-first, AI-powered" actually means when the pressure is on.

## Zoom CX FAQ


**What is Zoom CX and what does it include?**


Zoom CX is a contact center and customer experience platform that brings AI-powered self-service, omnichannel routing, agent assistance, operational analytics, and[workforce engagement management](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/workforce-engagement-management/) together under one platform. Rather than requiring separate vendors for each layer of the contact center stack, Zoom CX runs automation, agent desktop, quality management, compliance, and insights on a shared data model and administrative layer.


The platform includes Zoom Virtual Agent for AI-powered self-service; Zoom Contact Center for omnichannel routing and the agent desktop; AI Expert Assist for real-time agent guidance and workflow automation; CX Insights for operational intelligence; and[Zoom Compliance Manager](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/compliance-manager/) for regulated-industry archiving and governance. The unified architecture means every layer shares the same customer profile: context travels with the interaction from virtual agent to live agent without gaps.


**How does Zoom Virtual Agent's agent performance suite help contact centers improve over time?**


Agent performance suite gives contact center teams three feedback mechanisms that most AI deployments lack. Agent performance enables pre-deployment simulation and post-launch real-time dashboards tracking resolution rates, containment, CSAT, and cost per interaction. Quality management for Zoom Virtual Agent scores AI agent interactions continuously, surfacing where automation is falling short before failure compounds. Knowledge base (KB) suggestions extracts insights from escalated interactions that human agents resolved and recommends knowledge base updates, with human oversight applied before changes take effect.


Together, these three capabilities create the feedback loop that converts a deployed AI agent into an improving one. Organizations can identify and address Virtual Agent gaps in near real time rather than waiting for a quarterly review.


**What does "outcome-based pricing" mean for Zoom Virtual Agent?**


Outcome-based pricing ties Zoom Virtual Agent's cost to resolved interactions rather than seat counts, channel fees, or per-minute consumption. Organizations pay when the AI successfully resolves a customer request, not for the infrastructure it runs on.


For finance leaders evaluating AI ROI, outcome-based pricing converts the investment from a fixed platform cost to a variable cost that scales with successful resolution. It also changes the vendor relationship: Zoom's commercial interest is aligned to making the AI work better because better resolution rates directly affect per-interaction cost.


**How does Zoom CX handle AI quality and human oversight?**


Every AI capability in Zoom CX is designed with human oversight as an explicit architectural principle. Workflow automation in AI Expert Assist completes multi-step tasks in third-party systems but requires human approval before execution. Agent tracing generates auditable records of AI reasoning, tool usage, and decision flow, giving supervisors and compliance teams the ability to review and correct AI behavior. KB suggestions applies recommended knowledge base updates only after human review. Quality management for Zoom Virtual Agent scores AI interactions and surfaces issues to supervisors rather than applying fixes autonomously.


The principle is consistent across the platform: AI handles the work that benefits from automation; humans retain oversight over decisions that carry consequence.


**What is CX Insights and how is it different from standard contact center reporting?**


[CX Insights](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/running-your-business-with-cx-data/) is Zoom Contact Center's conversational intelligence and operational analytics layer, designed to replace manual reporting workflows: weekly spreadsheet exports, end-of-week coaching reviews, and static dashboards that show what happened but not why.


The practical difference is interaction speed and context. A supervisor who sees a CSAT dip can click on that signal and CX Insights automatically scopes the relevant transcripts and opens an investigation, with no export, no filter setup, and no waiting. Memory learns team structures, KPI definitions, and investigation patterns over time, **enabling the platform to provide increasingly relevant insights** as it accumulates organizational context.


**What is Agent Architect?**


Agent Architect is a prompt-driven creation tool in[Zoom Virtual Agent](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/virtual-agent/) that lets teams build production-ready AI agents from a single description. It automatically interprets intent, connects data sources, and generates deployable workflows in minutes. Unlike standard builders, Agent Architect uses reasoning and orchestration to create[agents that can collaborate and act across systems](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/ai-connects-conversations/) , not just follow scripts.


**What is the Agent Performance Suite?**


The Agent Performance Suite is a performance optimization layer for Zoom Virtual Agent that tracks[resolution rates, containment, and customer satisfaction](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/from-reactive-to-proactive-how-ai-reshaped-our-customer-experience-at-zoom/) across every interaction. It combines real-time dashboards, quality management, and[continuous learning](https://ev.zoom.us/webinars-events/next-gen-virtual-agents-unlocking-ai-powered-customer-value/) to help organizations measure and improve both virtual and human agent performance.


**What's new in AI Expert Assist 3.0?**


[AI Expert Assist 3.0](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/ai-assistant/resources/privacy-security/zoom-contact-center/) introduces agentic task execution, allowing AI to not only suggest actions but complete them with agent approval. It consolidates guidance from multiple sources into a single action path, generates automatic interaction summaries, and provides real-time coaching insights, helping live agents work faster and with more consistent, relevant guidance.
