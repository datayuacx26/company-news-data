## Are Zoom AI Companion features still available?


Meeting summaries, smart compose, transcription, call summaries, voicemail prioritization — all the capabilities that used to carry the AI Companion name are still built right into Zoom Workplace, still included with eligible paid plans. They're just called by what they do now. When Zoom AI summarizes your meeting, it's a meeting summary. When it helps you compose a chat reply, it's chat compose. Same features, same experience, just built into Zoom Workplace.


## ZoomMate is the next chapter of Zoom AI


We launched[ZoomMate](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/what-is-zoommate/) on top of everything Zoom AI already does, designed to move work from conversation to completion.


Where the built-in AI features handle specific tasks within specific products (summarizing a meeting, composing a message), ZoomMate operates across your entire workday. It captures context in real time, remembers what matters, and turns decisions into action.


What ZoomMate can do:


- **Agentic search** — finds answers across Zoom, the web, and your connected enterprise systems (Salesforce, Jira, Slack, ServiceNow, Workday, Google, and Microsoft apps), returning synthesized, cited responses rather than a list of links
- **Orchestration** — automates follow-up actions like scheduling meetings, updating records, creating tasks, and routing workflows, collapsing the time between agreement and execution
- **Content creation** — generates presentations, documents, spreadsheets, and reports directly from meeting context through the[Zoom AI Productivity Suite](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/ai-productivity-suite/) (Zoom Slides, Zoom Sheets, Zoom Paper, and Zoom Canvas)


The core idea: you lead with intent, ZoomMate handles the follow-through. Every conversation can become finished work. Here's what that looks like in practice: you finish a meeting where your team agrees on next steps. The built-in AI gives you the summary. ZoomMate takes it further — scheduling the follow-ups, drafting the deliverables, and updating your project tracker. One is the intelligence. The other is the action. And both live inside Zoom Workplace — no separate platform, no additional governance concerns, no tool sprawl.


## How has Zoom AI evolved over time?


The story of AI at Zoom began long before generative AI became mainstream. From the beginning, our focus was on solving practical problems that made communication more effective and collaboration more natural.


### It started with features nobody thought of as AI


The features people used every day were powered by machine learning all along. **Noise suppression** filtered out background distractions in real time. **Virtual backgrounds** used computer vision to replace your backdrop without a green screen. **Real-time transcription and closed captions** — powered by automatic speech recognition (ASR) — became available to all accounts. This was foundational: the same ASR technology would later power meeting summaries and everything that followed. Low-light enhancement, auto-framing, speaker focus, and gesture recognition rounded out the era.


As these technologies matured, Zoom expanded AI beyond the meeting experience. We launched Zoom Revenue Accelerator — to analyze customer conversations, surface deal insights, and provide coaching recommendations. We also introduced Zoom Virtual Agent, bringing AI-powered self-service experiences to customer support.


### Generative AI arrived and expanded across Zoom Workplace


We built our AI architecture around a federated approach — combining our proprietary models with leading third-party LLMs from providers like OpenAI. We expanded our AI features with **Chat Compose** for drafting messages, **Email Compose** for contextual replies, and **Meeting Summary** for automatic takeaways without requiring recordings.


As generative AI matured, we brought AI capabilities to[Zoom Phone](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/voip-phone/) (voicemail and call summaries),[Zoom Events](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/event-solutions/) (session descriptions and follow-up content), and[Zoom Canvas](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/collaborative-docs/) (smarter content workflows). Ask AI gave you a conversational interface to query meetings, chats, and documents. We published a deep dive on our federated AI approach — explaining how the combination of proprietary models, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta's open-source models could deliver better results than any single LLM alone.


### AI Companion learned to remember and act on context


AI Companion could summarize a meeting, but it couldn't remember what you talked about last week. Each interaction started from scratch. **AI Companion 2.0** changed that — transforming it into a persistent, context-aware experience in a sidebar across Zoom Workplace.


- **Context and memory** — it remembered previous conversation history and synthesized information from Zoom Meetings, Zoom Chat, Zoom Canvas, and Zoom Mail
- **Web-based answers with citations** — powered by real-time web search, pulling in external information with sources
- **Zoom Tasks** — designed to surface, track, and recommend action items


AI Companion had evolved beyond meeting summaries into persistent work memory.


### Enterprises got the controls to make it their own with Custom AI Companion


Large teams needed custom terminology and specific integrations. **Custom AI Companion** launched as an add-on:


- **Personalized templates and custom dictionaries** — tailored to company terminology and workflows
- **Third-party integrations** — connected tools like Jira, ServiceNow, and internal knowledge bases
- **Personal coaching and custom avatars** — AI-driven guidance based on communication patterns


### AI Companion 3.0 brought early agentic capabilities


Everything up to this point was about understanding your work. **AI Companion 3.0** took a step toward acting on that understanding with new capabilities that hinted at what agentic AI could do.


- **Agentic retrieval** — searched across meetings, chats, and Zoom Canvas to pull context-aware insights
- **Personal workflows** — automated recurring tasks like generating daily summaries or triggering actions based on natural language instructions
- **My Notes** — a cross-platform notetaker for Zoom Meetings, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and in-person conversations
- **Expanded federated AI** — now incorporating models from[OpenAI, Anthropic, and NVIDIA](https://news.zoom.com/zoom-launches-ai-companion-3-0/) alongside our proprietary LLMs


These capabilities represented the most advanced version of AI Companion — and they laid the groundwork for what would become a fundamentally different product: ZoomMate, which would take the concept of agentic action much further.


## AI that works without thinking about it


When we shipped our first noise suppression model, the goal was simple: make the technology work without asking anyone to think about it. No setup, no toggle, no "AI" badge. Just a better meeting.


That same instinct runs through everything that came after. Meeting summaries that are automatically drafted. Transcription that just works. And now, ZoomMate — which takes the context from your conversations and finishes the work.


The tools have gotten dramatically more capable. But the philosophy hasn't moved: make the technology practical and make it accessible.

## Frequently asked questions


### Are AI Companion features still available?


Yes. Features that were part of AI Companion like meeting summaries, chat compose, transcription, call summaries, voicemail prioritization are still built directly into Zoom Workplace. You'll find them now under their functional names rather than the AI Companion brand.


Custom AI Companion customers will continue to be supported.


### Are AI features included in my plan?


Eligible paid Zoom Workplace plans continue to include core AI features at no additional cost. This has been Zoom's approach since we launched AI Companion — making core AI capabilities available to paid customers.


### What's the difference between built-in AI features and ZoomMate?


The built-in AI features in Zoom Workplace handle specific tasks within specific products — summarizing a meeting, composing a message, transcribing a call. ZoomMate operates across your entire workday: it captures context in real time, remembers what matters, and takes action on it — scheduling follow-ups, drafting deliverables, updating records, and generating finished work from meeting context.


### Where can I learn more about ZoomMate?


We've published[a dedicated getting started guide](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/zoommate-getting-started/) that covers ZoomMate's capabilities, use cases, and how to get started.


### Do I need to do anything to keep using AI features?


No. You can continue using the included AI features in your Zoom Workplace plan. ZoomMate is a new add-on product — you can explore it when you're ready, but your existing AI capabilities remain fully functional.
