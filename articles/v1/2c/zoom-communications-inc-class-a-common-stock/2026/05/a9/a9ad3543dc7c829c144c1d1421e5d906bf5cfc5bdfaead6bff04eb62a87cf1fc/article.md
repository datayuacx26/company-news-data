# Zoom wins Newsweek AI Impact Award for the second year in a row

AI is reshaping how people work, but the real measure of progress isn't the technology itself; it's the outcomes it delivers. That's why we're proud to share that[Zoom has won the Newsweek AI Impact Award for "AI Workplace: Best Outcomes, Digital Workspaces"](https://www.newsweek.com/newsweeks-ai-impact-awards-2026-winners-11934612) for the second consecutive year. This recognition reflects what we hear from customers every day: AI features in Zoom Workplace help teams turn conversations into plans, insights, and action, keeping work moving forward without the busywork.

## Recognized for real-world AI impact — again


The[Newsweek AI Impact Awards](https://events.newsweek.com/aiimpact-us-2026) identify and recognize practitioners' unique use of AI to solve everyday business challenges. The program celebrates AI in action, not just big ideas, with winning entries selected by a panel of expert, cross-industry judges and written about by Newsweek editorial staff.


"Winning this recognition for the second consecutive year validates what we've built — a federated AI approach that orchestrates the best models for each task and meets people wherever they work. With breakthroughs in ASR and agentic search powering our Conversation to Completion capabilities, our AI features in Zoom Workplace are proof that practical AI — designed around real outcomes rather than raw scale — is what moves organizations forward."


## Xuedong Huang, chief technology officer, Zoom


## What the Newsweek AI Impact Awards recognize


Launched in 2025, the Newsweek AI Impact Awards honor organizations across more than a dozen categories for their outstanding achievements in applying AI to solve complex challenges, improve operations, and deliver meaningful outcomes. The awards focus on real-world results, evaluating how companies are using AI ethically, effectively, and with clear impact.


In 2025,[the inaugural awards recognized 38 organizations across 13 categories](https://www.newsweek.com/newsweeks-ai-impact-awards-2025) , with Zoom winning the AI Workplace category for Best Outcomes: Digital Workspaces. This year, the program has continued to grow, and we're honored to have been selected again by the expert judging panel.


## How Zoom is transforming the digital workspace


AI features in Zoom Workplace are designed to help people focus on meaningful work by turning conversations into outcomes. Whether it's a meeting, a chat thread, or an in-person working session, these AI capabilities help you capture what matters and turn it into something actionable. And they're included at no additional cost for users with paid Zoom Workplace plans.


AI features work across the Zoom platform, including Meetings, Chat, Mail, Phone, Whiteboard, Canvas, Events, and more, helping you stay productive wherever your day takes you:


- **Meeting summaries and smart recording:** Meetings end, but progress shouldn't stop. With meeting summaries and smart recording, you can capture what happened, surface action items, and answer questions about the discussion, so your team stays aligned without having to replay entire recordings.
- **Note-taking and idea capture:** Important thoughts pile up throughout your day.[My Notes](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/ai-assistant/features/ai-note-taking/) helps you capture and organize ideas with AI-powered assistance, whether it's in Zoom Meetings, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet — or during in-person meetings — turning notes into summaries, plans, or documents that keep every conversation connected to your next step.
- **Chat and thread summaries:** Catching up on conversations takes time you don't have. Message, doc, and thread summaries get you up to speed fast, draft replies, and surface relevant context so you can stay in the loop without scrolling through everything.
- **Email and content generation:** Repetitive writing tasks slow you down. Email compose and content generation help you draft emails, complete sentences, and create content across Zoom Canvas, Mail, and more, so you can focus on the message, not the mechanics.
- **Scheduling assistance and workflow automation:** Calendar coordination doesn't have to eat up your day. Quick scheduling helps you set up meetings, automate routine tasks, and keep workflows moving, giving you time back for work that matters.
- **AI web surface:** The conversational web interface pulls context from your Zoom data and from connected third-party apps like Google Drive and Meet, as well as Microsoft OneDrive and Teams, to answer questions, summarize content, and help with writing, all in one place.


These capabilities reflect our belief that AI should be practical, accessible, and focused on turning conversations into real outcomes, the same principles the Newsweek AI Impact Awards are designed to celebrate.
