## The Readiness Gap


While regulations like the REAL Health Providers Act may not be in effect just yet, compliance is not a switch that can be flipped overnight. Meeting new Federal, CMS and state requirements will call for health plans to build data verification, reporting, and governance capabilities that do not exist today.


## Closing the Gap


For most organizations, these requirements demand capabilities that take time to build. Waiting until compliance deadlines approach leaves little time to modernize and implement the infrastructure needed to support continuous verification and reporting.


The organizations that treat provider directory accuracy as a strategic initiative today will be best prepared when compliance requirements take effect and when performance becomes publicly visible.
