# NSTAR Electric Company Declares Preferred Dividends

## NSTAR Electric Company Declares Preferred Dividends


June 18, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.eversource.com/node/37921/pdf)


**BOSTON (June 18, 2026)** – The Board of Directors of NSTAR Electric Company, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Eversource Energy, announced the declaration of the following preferred stock dividends payable August 1, 2026 to holders of record as of July 10, 2026:


- A quarterly dividend of $1.0625 per share on NSTAR Electric Company’s cumulative preferred stock, 4.25% series; and
- A quarterly dividend of $1.195 per share on NSTAR Electric Company’s cumulative preferred stock, 4.78% series.


The Company’s preferred stock is quoted on the OTCID Market.
