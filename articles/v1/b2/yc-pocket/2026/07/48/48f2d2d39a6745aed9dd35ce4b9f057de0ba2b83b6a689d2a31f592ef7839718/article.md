# Ask Pocket Can Read Your Files Now

Maclean here from Pocket, Ask Pocket can now work with files. Drop in a PDF, image, CSV, text file, audio clip, or video, then ask Pocket to reason over it alongside your conversations. Try things like "what changed in this deck since the meeting?" or "turn this brief and the call into next steps."


We also added a model picker in Ask Pocket. Everyone now starts on Thinking, the new default, and Pro users can switch to a stronger model when the question is messy or the stakes are high.


Here's some other highlights from these past two weeks:


**✨ New**


- Ask Pocket file attachments
- Ask Pocket model picker
- Google Drive sync
- OneDrive sync


- Upcoming meetings in the Mac tray
- Transcript audio player
- Search in the labels sheet
- Liquid glass mobile tab bar
- New recording tile on mobile


- Organization admin controls, webhooks, user filters, and member tags

- Auto Record is faster and more reliable when a call starts

- Recording audio quality improved on desktop
- Mobile sync now shows byte-level progress, speed, and ETA
- Calendar sync and cancellation handling are more reliable
- Merged recordings now respect your language
- Daily Insights now respect your language
