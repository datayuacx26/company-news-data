# Your Summaries Just Learned to Draw

Maclean here from Pocket, your summaries can now draw things. When a meeting has numbers, a sequence, a branching decision, or a process, Pocket puts a visual in the summary instead of describing it in text. It also works in Ask Pocket. Ask "what did we decide about pricing?" and you might get a decision tree back instead of paragraphs.
