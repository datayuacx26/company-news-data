# 🚀 What we shipped — December 15

## Pocket App(v0.5.21)


Our latest update is packed with productivity features, deep organizational tools, and a completely reimagined summary experience that makes Pocket smarter, faster, and more powerful than ever.


### ✍️ Ask to Edit Summaries (Adaptive Editing)


Edit summaries by simply describing what you want changed—inline, fast, and contextual. Pocket updates the summary instantly, without breaking your flow.


**What makes this truly powerful is what happens next:**


Every time you customize an edit, Pocket learns.


It starts to understand your tone, your structure, and your preferences.


Future summaries come out closer and closer to how you naturally write—with fewer taps, fewer corrections, and eventually none at all.


### 🎊 Pocket Wrapped


- **Pocket Wrapped** : A recap experience highlighting your usage and insights over time.
- See highlights from your recordings, summaries, action items, and activity across days, weeks, or longer stretches.


###


### ✍️ Summary


- Manual summary editing with a smoother editor, better gestures, and a dedicated toolbar.
- Refined and polished UI


### 📋 Global Action Items Management


Never lose track of a task again. Access all your action items from every recording in one unified view.


- **Smart Filtering** : Filter by status, due date, recording, or tags to focus on what matters
- **Sorting** : Sort by priority, due date, creation date, or recording
- **Search** : Find any task instantly with a powerful search


- **Status** Tracking: See completed vs. pending items at a glance


## 🗣️ Global Speaker Management


Take full control of voice identification across all your recordings:


- **Centralized Dashboard** : Manage all speakers and voiceprints from one place
- **Smart Renaming** : Rename a speaker once, update everywhere instantly
- **Merge Duplicates** : Combine multiple voiceprints of the same person
- **Memory Count** : See how many recordings each speaker appears in


**In progress** : A deeper redesign of speaker recognition is underway, aimed at improving accuracy and handling complex speaker scenarios more reliably.


## 📝 Custom Summary Templates


Design summaries that match exactly how you work:


- **Template Builder** : Create custom formats for meetings, lectures, interviews, or any content type
- **Reusable Formats** : Design once, apply to any recording
- **Edit & Refine** : Modify templates anytime to improve your workflow


### 🏷️ Recording Tags


Organize your content library with powerful tagging:


- **Create & Assign** : Add custom tags to any recording


- **Tag Management** : Edit, rename, or delete tags globally
- Search Recording with a specific tag


## 📱 Widgets


- **Recording widget** for quick capture
- **Action Item widget** for staying on top of tasks


### 🔔 Controlled Notifications


Fine-tune your Pocket experience with granular notification controls:


- **Sync Nudges** : Get notified when recordings are ready to sync from your Pocket Device
- **Morning Welcome** : Start your day with a personalized summary of pending tasks


### 📦 Bulk Export


Export multiple recordings at once for archiving or sharing:


- **Multi-Select** : Choose as many recordings as you need
- **Format Options** : Export as audio files or PDF transcripts


### 📖 Word Dictionary


Teach Pocket your specialized vocabulary for perfect transcriptions:


- **Custom Vocabulary** : Add names, technical terms, industry jargon, or acronyms
- **Improved Accuracy** : Better transcription for words that Pocket hasn't learned yet


From medical terminology to unique client names—now Pocket speaks your language.


### 🔤 Text Scaling (Accessibility Enhancement)


- Unified accessibility improvements, including better text scaling, layout stability, and clearer interaction states across the app.


### 🎨 New Home Screen Header


- **Export Button** : Quick access to bulk export
- **Import Button** : Seamlessly import audio files from your device
- **Meetings Calendar** : Jump to your calendar integration
- **Sync Status** : See device sync progress at a glance
