# Improvements to Pocket

[All Announcements](https://feedback.heypocket.com/announcements) 29 Mar, 2026


Maclean here from Pocket, we just shipped a bunch of improvements to Pocket the last two weeks! You can also see these updates and chat with the team and other users in our[Slack community](https://join.slack.com/t/pocket-community/shared_invite/zt-3ou34fut1-efRn~G_jsV5AYQ1~O~n2jQ) ! Here's the highlights:


**✨ New**


- Say phrases like "Hey Pocket, remind me to..." or "Pocket, draft me an email for..." to generate actions.
- Import your memory from other apps like ChatGPT (Settings > Connections > Import)
- Draft messages and emails in Ask Pocket


**🚀 Improvements**


- Summaries and action items now finish at the same time, and 10-30 seconds faster than before
- Title generation and calendar event matching is now faster and more accurate
- Web search in Ask Pocket is better


**💁‍♂️ In case you missed it**


- Ask Pocket got rebuilt from the ground up in February, give it another chance if you haven't already!
- The actions tab also got re-vamped in February with message and email drafts
- You can try out our desktop app in beta
- View your recordings on the web here
- Give tools like Claude and Cursor access to your recordings with our MCP server
- Upload documents and write notes during meetings to improve your summary
