# Find Pocket Is Now Live!

Maclean here from Pocket. If you ever misplace your Pocket, Find Pocket can now help you get it back. It shows the last place your Pocket was on a map, gives you a live Bluetooth radar to walk you back to it when you're close, and can ping you the moment you leave it behind. Turn it on in Settings → Find Pocket.


We also gave the app a real dark theme across mobile, including the transcript editor, and added Japanese as a fully localized language.


Here's some other highlights from these past two weeks:


**✨ New**


- Find Pocket — map, live Bluetooth radar, and left-behind alerts
- Dark theme on mobile, including the editor
- Japanese localization
- "Still recording?" check-in when Pocket picks up a long stretch of silence (Pocket Pro)
- Native Mac meeting notifications that auto-stop the recording when your meeting ends
- Participant names pulled in automatically from Zoom, Meet, and Teams on Mac
- Faster, on-device live transcription for desktop recordings
- Personalized Getting Started guide tailored to your role
- Redesigned desktop search command menu, with an "Ask Pocket" tab that starts a chat from whatever you typed
- Obsidian sync
- Merge duplicate speakers, plus speaker names that stick across transcripts
- Folder colors and an "Uncategorized" filter in the folder picker


- Drag to reorder summary cards on desktop


**🚀 Improvements**


- Faster Zoom, Meet, and Teams in-call detection, so Auto Record kicks in sooner
- Smoother Ask Pocket scrolling that stays pinned to the latest message
- Ask Pocket history is now paginated and sorted by your most recent chat
- Better multi-photo selection when attaching images to a chat


- Copy Summary on desktop now keeps tables intact
- Chat titles wait for the full response before generating


- Lots of recording, sync, and performance fixes
