# 🚀 What we shipped - November 12

## Pocket App(v0.5.1)


Our latest update brings smarter, faster, and more connected experiences across Pocket:


- **🌍 Global Ask** : Search and access your content instantly—no matter where it lives.


- **📂 Pocket Import** : Seamlessly import audio files directly from your system.
- **📰 Daily Summaries** : Get concise, personalized overviews to keep you in the loop.


- **📅 iOS Calendar Integration** : Sync and manage events directly from your Pocket app.
- **📅 Delete Calendar Account** : Full control, remove linked calendars anytime.
- **📂 Device File System Access** : Browse, manage, and transfer files with ease.
- **🔄 Selective File Syncs** : Choose exactly what to sync, saving space and time.
- **🐞 UX Enhancement & Bug Fixes:** Squashed a handful of bugs to make your Pocket experience more stable, faster, and reliable than ever.


**P.S.** Make sure you log in with the same email you used to purchase your Pocket Device to receive your Founding Member benefits. If you signed in with Apple, note that Apple uses a private relay email, so your benefits may not be automatically linked. If that happens, just reach out to us at hey@heypocket.com and we’ll sort it out.


## Pocket Device(Firmware v1.0)


Performance just got a serious boost:


- **⚡ 50% Faster Bluetooth Transfers:** Move files in half the time.
- 🔌 **USB-C + Pocket Web Support** : Sync conversations faster and connect directly to Pocket Web. Try now here at[https://app.heypocket.com/](https://app.heypocket.com/) and make sure to update the firmware first.
