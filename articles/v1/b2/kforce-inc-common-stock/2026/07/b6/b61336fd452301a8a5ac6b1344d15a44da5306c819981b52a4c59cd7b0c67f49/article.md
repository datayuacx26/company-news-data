# Kforce Brand Refresh Established Firm as a Workplace of the Future

*The refreshed logo will be proudly displayed on Kforce's new headquarters in Midtown Tampa.*


TAMPA, FL (Dec. 14, 2021) –[Kforce](https://www.kforce.com/) unveiled its new logo and core values Monday as part of a brand refresh that reflects the staffing and solutions firm’s ongoing evolution.


The new brand elements, including a redesigned website, show a commitment to the factors distinguishing Kforce as a workplace of the future: technology, flexibility, adaptability and choice.


Each of these was created and shaped by input from teams throughout Kforce. The logo is inspired by tech design trends and aligns the brand as a solutions firm in the digital age. The[core values](https://www.kforce.com/core-values/) highlight the firm’s proud history while also providing a vision for how to continue to succeed in this new age of work. The values are combined with actions and stories that help people understand exactly how these concepts are lived out at Kforce.


“These changes represent our focus on solutions and our identity as a state-of-the-art technology organization that is all about people,” Kforce President Joe Liberatore said. “We achieve our greatest success as a firm when we empower our people to be the best versions of themselves.”


The company, one of the first in Tampa Bay to commit to a hybrid work style, is dedicated to providing the best possible environment for its people and identifying itself as a leader in the technology space.[Earlier this month](https://investor.kforce.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2021/Joseph-J.-Liberatore-to-Succeed-David-L.-Dunkel-as-CEO-of-Kforce-Effective-January-1-2022/default.aspx) , Kforce announced Liberatore will assume the role and responsibilities of CEO effective Jan. 1, 2022. Under his leadership, the firm is experiencing record-setting performance in 2021, with the stock price at an all-time high. Quarter 3 financial results showed nearly 30% organic growth in technology revenues on a year-over-year basis.


Kforce’s people are thriving, too.[In an ongoing video and multimedia series](https://www.kforce.com/this-is-my-kforce/) , employees share how they’ve taken the freedom Kforce provides to design their best lives and bring their careers to the next level. Employees hope that sharing their personal stories will inspire teammates at Kforce and peers throughout the industry to create a new life balance that works best for them – all while achieving professional success and making an impact with their careers.


**About Kforce**
