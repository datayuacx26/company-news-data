# **Kforce opens AI & Innovation Studio to help clients prove AI value before they scale**


*As part of the Kforce Innovation Experience, clients can validate AI use cases, prioritize investments and move forward with solutions shown to deliver real business value.*


**TAMPA, FL (April 23, 2026) —**


Kforce’s AI & Innovation Studio is now open, giving leaders an in-person environment to work through real business challenges and determine where innovation will deliver measurable results. Inside the studio, clients work alongside Kforce experts and domain practitioners to design, test and refine real-world AI solutions.


The Tampa studio is the cornerstone of the[Kforce Innovation Experience](https://www.kforce.com/the-kforce-innovation-experience/)


, a proven engagement model that helps organizations move beyond experimentation to credible, evidence-backed outcomes. Clients pressure-test ideas in real time, interact with working solutions and identify which AI investments deliver business value.


Clients can also engage with the Kforce Innovation Experience through Regional Innovation Hubs, Idea-to-Outcome Workshops and the global AI Center of Excellence. Together, these components support hands-on ideation, rapid prototyping and solution refinement. Clients collaborate with Kforce technologists, architects and industry practitioners to minimize decision risk and understand how solutions come together before broader adoption. Teams leave with tangible artifacts, including prioritized AI investment roadmaps, clickable prototypes, architecture direction and early validation of feasibility, value and risk.


“The heart of the AI & Innovation Studio is the ability to make AI real,” said Kforce Consulting Solutions Vice President Brad Boyd, who leads the firm’s data and AI practice. “We’re not talking about ideas in isolation. Here, clients can see, touch and manipulate solutions in a controlled environment. They leave with a clearer understanding of how these pieces come together to support their strategy.”


Sessions are engineered to help leaders evaluate which investments deliver real-world value in their industry and operating context.


Client teams are introduced to Kforce accelerators: prebuilt frameworks and solution components informed by prior client work and industry validation. These accelerators help organizations move faster by applying proven approaches tailored to their environments. Assets already created through this experience include the AI Value Realization Engine, which connects AI initiatives to business outcomes, prioritizes investments and aligns stakeholders.


For organizations looking to turn AI ideas into practical, scalable outcomes, the Kforce Innovation Experience provides a trusted environment to validate value, reduce risk and move forward with confidence.


**About the[Kforce Innovation Experience](https://www.kforce.com/the-kforce-innovation-experience/)**


This curated engagement model includes four components that guide clients in unlocking AI value.


- **Kforce AI & Innovation Studio** ****


Located at our headquarters in Tampa. Dedicated to deep dives, rapid prototyping and live solution development aligned to industry-specific challenges.


- **Regional Innovation Hubs** ****


30 nationwide hubs established within our field office network for hands-on collaboration and custom solutions creation, tailored to regional and industry realities.


- **Idea-to-Outcome Workshops**


Kforce experts visit client offices to determine where AI delivers practical business value within their industry.


- **Global AI Center of Excellence**


Where the work comes to life. Our experts in Pune expand prototypes, strengthen architecture and prepare solutions to scale responsibly across client enterprises.
