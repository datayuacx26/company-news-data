# Enterprise Scale, Tech-Powered Finance: The Future of FinOps

Click[here](https://youtu.be/PvEwD9uLD4c) to watch


‍


### **Episode Summary**


‍


In this episode, Ammar Alkhouli engages with Youssef Salem to explore the intersection of scale, automation, and financial operations in large enterprises. Youssef emphasizes the increasing need for finance leaders to complement technical expertise with strategic storytelling and stakeholder management. The discussion highlights how automation and technology deployment enable high-performance teams to do more with less, optimizing financial processes at scale. Youssef also sheds light on the growing importance of data science and computer science skills in finance, allowing professionals to build and implement tools that drive efficiency. As enterprises scale, leveraging automation becomes critical for maintaining agility, reducing costs, and enhancing decision-making.


‍


### **Key Takeaways**


‍


#### **Blending Finance Expertise with Strategy:**


- A strong financial skill set is amplified by the ability to craft compelling narratives and manage stakeholders effectively.
- Finance leaders must balance technical rigor with strategic communication to drive business impact.


#### **Scaling with Automation:**


- Enterprises must leverage automation to improve efficiency and reduce reliance on manual processes.
- Deploying technology effectively allows finance teams to optimize resources and focus on strategic initiatives.


#### **The Role of FinOps in High-Performance Teams:**


- Financial operations are evolving with automation, enabling teams to manage large-scale transactions with fewer resources.
- The right tech stack empowers teams to operate more efficiently and adapt to growth challenges.


#### **Data Science & Engineering in Finance:**


- Professionals with data science and computer science skills have a competitive advantage in financial roles.
- The ability to build and integrate financial tools enhances decision-making and process efficiency.


#### **Technology as a Competitive Edge:**


- Companies investing in finance automation gain a strategic edge in managing complexity at scale.
- Digital transformation in finance leads to faster, data-driven decision-making and better resource allocation.


#### **Future of Finance Roles:**


- As automation takes over repetitive tasks, finance professionals will shift toward strategic analysis and value creation.
- The finance function is increasingly becoming a technology-driven discipline, requiring adaptability and innovation.
