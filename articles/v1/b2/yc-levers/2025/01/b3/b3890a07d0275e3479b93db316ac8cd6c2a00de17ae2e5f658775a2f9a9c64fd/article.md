# Revolutionizing Financial Collections: Automation, AI, and Strategic Credit Policies

Click[here to watch](https://youtu.be/yooOimER9Ps)


‍


### **Episode summary**


‍


In this episode, Ammar Alkhouli engages with Hussein Shohdy and Badr Alyamani to discuss effective strategies for improving collection results within financial companies. The conversation emphasizes the critical role of well-defined credit policies aligned with an organization's risk appetite. The speakers highlight common challenges such as data quality issues and reliance on manual processes that hinder efficient collections. They advocate for the adoption of automation and advanced technologies like Robotic Process Automation (RPA) to streamline operations and enhance data accuracy. Looking ahead, they envision a future where artificial intelligence (AI) transforms the collections function, shifting human roles towards strategic and analytical tasks. Additionally, maintaining positive customer relationships through professional and empathetic collection practices is underscored as essential for successful outcomes.


‍


### **Key take aways**


‍


**Define and Align Credit Policies:**


- Organizations must identify their risk appetite and develop credit policies that align with their strategic objectives.


- Collaboration between business and financial teams is essential to create comprehensive and effective policy documents. **‍**


‍


**Enhance Data Quality:** ‍


- High-quality data is foundational for successful collections. Poor data input during the application process leads to difficulties in reaching delinquent clients.


- Integrating with government portals and locking critical data fields can minimize errors and ensure data accuracy from the outset.


‍


**Leverage Automation and Technology:**


- Automation tools like RPA and workflow management systems can significantly improve the efficiency of collection processes.


- Reducing manual interventions not only speeds up operations but also reduces the burden on collection teams.


‍


**Minimize Human Intervention:**


- Reducing the need for manual data entry and interventions helps in maintaining up-to-date and accurate client information.


- Automated systems can handle repetitive tasks, allowing human resources to focus on more strategic activities.


‍


**Future of Collections with AI:**


- AI advancements are expected to automate traditional collection functions, potentially eliminating the need for conventional collection roles.


- Human roles will evolve towards collection analytics and digital strategy, focusing on managing and optimizing automated processes.


‍


**Maintain Positive Customer Relationships:**


- Professional and empathetic collection practices can preserve and even enhance relationships with clients.


- Offering solutions such as payment rescheduling demonstrates a customer-centric approach, fostering goodwill and successful debt resolution.


‍


**Digital Transformation of Processes:**


- Transitioning from paper-based to electronic systems for promises and legal processes increases efficiency and transparency.


- Electronic signatures and automated notifications streamline the collection workflow and reduce delays.


‍


**Strategic Role of Collection Professionals:**


- As automation takes over routine tasks, collection professionals will shift towards strategic planning and process management.


- Emphasizing strategic roles ensures that human expertise is utilized in areas requiring critical thinking and decision-making.
