# Nest recognized on this year's Digital Health 100!

**Nest Genomics Named to the 2025 New York Digital Health 100 by Digital Health New York (DHNY)**


*DH100 recognizes the companies driving healthcare innovation in New York*


**New York, NY (February 6, 2025)** – **Nest Genomics** today announced that it has been named to the **2025 New York Digital Health 100 (DH100)** , a recognition that highlights the most exciting and innovative startups transforming healthcare in New York. **Digital Health New York (DHNY)** publishes the annual list in conjunction with the **2025 New York Healthcare Innovation Report** , which analyzes investment trends, challenges, and opportunities in the digital health sector.


*"We are honored to be recognized as one of New York's leading digital health startups. At Nest Genomics, we are committed to seamlessly integrating genomics into routine care—helping providers and patients make better use of genetic information in clinical decision-making. This recognition highlights the importance of our work in making genomic medicine more accessible, scalable, and actionable,"* said **Moran Snir, CEO & Co-Founder of Nest Genomics** .


*"Now in its sixth year, the New York DH100 has evolved and grown, and in that time, the digital health ecosystem in New York has also reached unprecedented levels of innovation and impact,"* said **Bunny Ellerin, co-founder and CEO of DHNY** . *"We are honored to celebrate these organizations, whose dedication to transformation and progress is driving the future of healthcare."*


## About Nest Genomics


Nest Genomics is a leading digital health company specializing in **scalable solutions for integrating genomics into routine patient care** . Nest's **HIPAA-compliant platform** supports **risk assessment, patient education, test ordering, and clinical decision support** , empowering providers and health systems to streamline **genetic services at scale** . By embedding **just-in-time provider education** and **clinical decision support** , Nest makes it easier for clinicians to identify at-risk patients, navigate genetic testing, and implement personalized care strategies. Nest is trusted by **health systems, population health programs, and research initiatives** nationwide.

## About Digital Health New York (DHNY)


[Digital Health New York](http://www.dhny.co/) (DHNY) is a connected community of digital health leaders who share ideas, spark new directions and create success across the entire ecosystem. As an organization, we seek to increase the visibility of New York City as a leader in healthcare innovation and showcase the companies and leaders creating the future of healthcare. Our flagship event, DHNY Summit, brings together an elite group of entrepreneurs, investors, payers, providers and executives to cultivate the New York digital health community and drive impactful conversations around the current and future state of digital health. DHNY was founded in 2022 in collaboration with AlleyCorp.
