# Marijuana Delivery Guide: Everything You Need to Know

## So, How Does it Work?


Once you have found a delivery service, you will need to set up an account and enter your personal information, including; your name, address, phone number, and email address. At FLOWER CO., we take great care to ensure all information is protected, so that your privacy is never a concern.


After you've set up your account, you can browse all products available for delivery. Then, place the items you want in your cart and check out. From there, a driver will be dispatched to the address provided. Once they arrive, they'll verify your identification and hand you your order. All you need to do next is sit back, relax, and enjoy your purchase.


Delivery times vary depending on the delivery service you are ordering from. Most will provide an estimated delivery time when you place your order. In general, most deliveries will arrive within a few hours to 1-2 days. We will note that hours of operation of each service may differ.
