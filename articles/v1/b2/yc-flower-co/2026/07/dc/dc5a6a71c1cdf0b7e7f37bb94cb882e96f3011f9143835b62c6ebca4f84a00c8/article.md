# What are the Top Ten Glass Bong Brands?

## Top Ten Glass Bong Brands:


1. Grav Labs A winner in the “best value” category, Grav Labs is among the top bong brands due to its excellent quality and accessible price point. This Texas-based company makes classic bongs, pipes, and water pipes but is best known for the Graviton, a glass gravity bong.


Aficionados can opt for the Stax collection, which includes mouthpieces, percolators, and bases.


You can even buy the components to build a custom smoking system.


Otherwise, try the 8” Flare Waterpipe with Fixed Downstream i.e. a "bubbler", a stand-out for style, function, and price.


2. Illadelph Glass Combining beauty and technology, Illadelph is consistently listed among the best glass bong brands. Headquartered in Philadelphia since 2002, this company creates glass artworks and fits them with state-of-the-art percolators for unbeatable aesthetics and functionality.


Choose between unique bowls, showerhead percolators, coil condensers, pyramid percolators, and other water pipes, or go straight to their bestselling Mini Inline Water Pipe. Illadelph bongs are expensive, but the perfect performance justifies the premium price point.


3. Roor One of the most famous bong brands globally, this German company has been producing hand-blown classic tubes from durable borosilicate glass since 1995. Their collection of large bongs with straight and beaker bases will appeal to those looking for impeccable functionality and heavy-duty glass density. To combat counterfeit wares, Roor attaches an authentication code to each original piece, including the popular beaker-style Little Sista Ice.


4. Zob Glass No bells and whistles, just unbeatable functionality with a pop of color: That's the hallmark of this California company. The classic beaker bong featuring a long bowl and downstream made with heavy-duty German borosilicate glass is ideal as a moderately-priced starter pipe—the upgraded model that includes their innovative Zobello Perc is more of a splurge. Otherwise, you can take their unique Wubbler for a spin, a hybrid water pipe, and a bubbler that offers the science-based performance that has placed Zob Glass among the best bong brands.


5. Empire Glassworks If you're looking for a whimsically creative bong, look no further. Known for its unique designs by glass artists, Empire Glassworks is among the most popular bong brands for its beautiful and playful bongs, mini rigs, pipes, and other one-of-a-kind smoking accessories.


Pieces are hand-blown, and many feature custom-mixed colors and glowing dichroic glass.


6. The China Glass The China Glass wears its cultural heritage on its sleeve with a line of elegant bongs inspired by Dynastic-era vases and porcelain. If you think “made in China” means sketchy quality, think again: Not all good bong brands are headquartered in the U.S. and Europe.


These exquisite pieces are hand-crafted by master artisans in China's northern Hebei province—known for its fine glass production for over a century—and make for a stunning addition to any collection. Head-turners include the delicate white Ming Dynasty Vase bong and the opulent gold-on-black Tang Dynasty Vase bong.


7. Snoop Dogg Pounds What is Snoop Dogg doing when he's not kicking back with Martha Stewart? He's designing a luxe spacecraft-theme line of bongs and bubblers, of course!


The California-made Pounds line features quality hand-blown borosilicate glass construction, spaceship-inspired names, and the famous fist bump logo.


Try the entry-level Rocketship tube water pipe or go all-in with the high-end Battleship or Muthaship models from this line that is quickly gaining traction as one of the best bong companies.


8. Mobius Glass This California glass-blowing studio headed by John M/Circle is known for its top-shelf bongs with sophisticated style and unique Matrix cylindrical percolator. Durable and functional—but also beautifully sleek in flawless clear glass—each piece is marked with a hologram serial code authenticity sticker, so you know you're getting the real deal from one of the most trusted bong brands out there.


9. UPC Glass With affordable prices and no-nonsense designs, UPC (Until Prohibition Ceases) bongs carry their unique barcode logo and make ideal entry-level pieces. Opt for the 18” straight tube for a reliable, high-quality bong that will set you back you less than $100.


10. Diamond Glass Considered one of the best bong brands for its high-quality, precision cut and blown glass, Diamond Glass is based in California, and its bongs and other products are all American-made. Their beaker and tube water pipes in clear or colored borosilicate glass are crowd-pleasers for their unfussy styles and ease of use.
