# Weed Delivery App: Cannabis and Medical Marijuana on Demand

## The Future of Weed Delivery Apps


If the current landscape of weed apps seems so dire, then what does the future have in store? Don't fret, we have good news. The innovative approach to dispensing cannabis on-demand via delivery apps may seem niche right now, but it's widely expected that states will loosen their laws on marijuana over time. While the weed delivery app world faces many challenges, the ease and popularity of these apps can't be denied. The big change in the future will inevitably be that cannabis delivery will become more common and popular than it already is. Word has it that both Amazon and Uber are considering cannabis delivery apps.


The bad news is that when this technology does take off nationwide, smaller dispensaries are going to have to fight to get new orders. As more dispensaries decide to list themselves online or develop their own weed delivery app, it could lead to pricing wars which leads to smaller fish struggling to secure new business. Unfortunately, the expansion of weed selling apps largely lies at the feet of lawmakers at both the Federal and state levels and only time will tell what the future of this innovative technology will look like.
