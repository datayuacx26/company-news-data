# Smoking Weed While Sick - What You Need To Know

## Tips For Using Weed When Sick


If you're thinking about smoking weed with a cold for the benefits, you may want to ingest cannabis in ways that do not involve smoke and heat as these could be more irritating. Some alternatives to smoking with a cold are:


**Eat It**
Edibles will give you all the advantages of CBD, THC, and other cannabinoids, without the negative side-effects of smoking.


**Drink It**
Combine the soothing aspect of tea with the symptom-relieving qualities of cannabis by making weed tea. Drinking weed tea is an even better alternative to smoking weed with a fever.


**Oils**
Cannabis tinctures are oils made with extracts of the herb. They usually come with a dropper and a recommendation for the number of drops that you should put under your tongue.


**Topical Application**
Great for those aching muscles and fatigue, applying cannabis topicals directly onto the skin is a good alternative to smoking weed while sick.


**Vaporizers**
A high-quality vaporizer will remove the carcinogens in the smoke, resulting in a better alternative to smoking cannabis.
