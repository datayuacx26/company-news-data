# The Best Way To Get Weed Out of Your System

## How Long Does Marijuana Stay in Your Body?


Consuming marijuana will make you feel its effects immediately but if you want to clean your system of weed, prepare for a longer process. Marijuana metabolites will remain in your system long after the effects are gone. Cannabis remnants are known as cannabinoids, and they are detectable in the hair, urine, fingernails, and saliva. This can make it quite a challenge to get THC out of your system.


The active ingredient in cannabis is a chemical substance known as THC that gets absorbed into your bloodstream, making it challenging to get weed out of your system fast. THC breaks down in the liver, leaving more than 80 metabolites behind. As a result, getting weed out of your system takes time.


Weed drug tests search for these metabolites since they stay in your body much longer than THC. It's important to note cleaning your system of weed can take months after you've stopped using. But eventually, THC and all its metabolites will get out through stool and urine flushing marijuana out of your system.


Determining how long THC remains detectable in your body depends on your eating habits and metabolism, body mass index (BMI), quantity and frequency of weed use, exercise routine, etc. All these factors make it hard to come up with a precise estimate of how long it takes to get marijuana out of your system. THC can stick around anywhere between several days up to several months.
