# Posh Featured in World Economic Forum: 5 Battery Storage Innovations Powering the Clean Energy Transition

The **World Economic Forum** highlighted Posh Energy as one of the battery storage innovators helping pioneer the next phase of the global clean energy transition.


The piece featured five battery storage breakthroughs being scaled to:


- Decarbonize sectors ranging from data centers to road transport
- Store surplus renewable power and stabilize the grid
- Reduce reliance on fossil-fueled peaker plants during high-demand hours


Posh’s commercial battery energy storage systems (BESS) were called out for their role in **commercial and industrial demand charge management** and resilient power for high-load operations.
