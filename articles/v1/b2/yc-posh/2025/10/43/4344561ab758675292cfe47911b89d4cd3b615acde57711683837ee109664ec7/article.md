# XCF Global and Posh Energy Sign Letter of Intent to Deploy Flex-Fuel Gensets

POSH Energy and XCF Global (NASDAQ: SAFX) signed a letter of intent to deploy up to **10MW** of flex-fuel gensets that convert renewable fuels into clean, dispatchable electricity. The deployment is targeted at meeting the energy needs of AI data centers facing multi-year delays on traditional grid interconnection.


### Why it matters


- Grid interconnection wait times for new data centers in the US have stretched to **5–7 years** in many regions.
- Renewable fuel-powered gensets are 80% more efficient per unit of fuel than conventional diesel, with **zero NOx/SOx** emissions.
- Modular, rapidly deployable on-site generation is becoming the bridge solution for compute-heavy buildouts that cannot wait for the grid.


The collaboration combines XCF Global’s renewable fuel production with Posh’s flex-fuel genset technology — a model for how the AI-era energy build-out can move at the speed it requires.
