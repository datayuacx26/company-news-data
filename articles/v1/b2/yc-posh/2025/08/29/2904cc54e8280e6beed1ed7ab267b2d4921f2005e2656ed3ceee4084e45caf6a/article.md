# Posh Electric Awarded Singapore EMA Grant for 1MWh Sodium-ion Battery Trial

Posh Energy’s wholly owned Singapore subsidiary, **Posh Electric** (“POSH”), has been awarded a grant from the **Energy Market Authority (EMA) of Singapore** to deploy a **1MWh sodium-ion battery energy storage system (BESS)** — part of a **S$7.8M** package to better harness energy storage in Singapore’s grid.


POSH will lead the development and deployment of the system to manage solar intermittency, in partnership with the **Institute of Materials Research and Engineering (IMRE)** of A*STAR and **Utraco Pte Ltd** , at Utraco’s factory. The BESS integrates with **600 kW of rooftop solar arrays** to store renewable energy, providing **load-shifting** capabilities — storing power during off-peak hours and releasing it at peak demand — while mitigating the intermittency of solar generation. The trial is equipped with liquid-cooled thermal management and certified to internationally recognized standards.


> “We have, over the years, developed unique battery control hardware and software solutions to manage a wide variety of battery chemistries, including sodium-ion batteries. By harnessing sodium’s natural abundance and integrating it with renewable energy solutions, we aim to provide a sustainable and scalable alternative to conventional battery technologies.”
>
>
> — **Dr. Wesley Zheng** , CEO, Posh Electric


### Why sodium-ion matters


- Sodium is **over 1,000 times more abundant than lithium** , with a simpler, lower-impact extraction process — sodium-ion chemistry could meaningfully lower long-run BESS cost.
- SIBs offer strong thermal stability and enhanced safety, operating effectively in higher-temperature environments.
- The deployment is a **real commercial trial** , not a lab demo — collecting fire-safety and performance data in Singapore’s demanding climate to accelerate broader adoption.
- It arrives as **global lithium demand is projected to rise twenty-fold by 2030** , putting pressure on supply chains and raising the value of diversified storage chemistries.


### What this means for Posh customers


The grant validates the part of Posh that travels to every site: our **battery control hardware and software** that manages multiple chemistries on one platform. That’s the same intelligence behind our commercial products.


- **Posh Station** — the smart dispatch, load-shifting, and liquid-cooled thermal architecture proven in this trial is what cuts demand charges and delivers reliable backup power for commercial and industrial sites.
- **Energy Management Cloud + Power Monitoring Node** — the chemistry-agnostic control layer EMA funded is what lets us optimize and monitor whatever battery best fits your economics.


A national energy regulator and a leading materials research institute chose Posh to prove out next-generation storage in one of the world’s most demanding grid environments. For US commercial and industrial operators, this complements Posh’s lithium iron phosphate (LFP) deployments and establishes sodium-ion as a credible second chemistry for cost- and safety-sensitive projects — backed by a control platform with government-validated results.
