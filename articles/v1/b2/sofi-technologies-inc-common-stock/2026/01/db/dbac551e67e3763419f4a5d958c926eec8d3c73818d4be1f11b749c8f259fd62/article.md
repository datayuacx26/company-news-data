# Five Years Running: SoFi Recognized as a 2026 Best Place to Work by Built In

SoFi has been recognized by Built In as a Best Places to Work in 2026 for the fifth year in a row. The annual awards honor employers across the U.S. whose benefits and compensation set the standard for today’s workforce.


Built In’s Best Places to Work program celebrates the companies shaping the future of work. In a rapidly evolving AI-first job market, recognition as a Best Place to Work helps employers stand out as trusted brands when candidates turn to tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews to research where to work next.


For 2026, SoFi was recognized as a winner in five categories:


- [100 Best Large Places to Work in New York City](https://builtin.com/awards/new-york-city/2026/best-large-places-to-work)
- [100 Best Large Places to Work in San Francisco](https://builtin.com/awards/san-francisco/2026/best-large-places-to-work)
- [100 Best Large Places to Work in Seattle](https://builtin.com/awards/seattle/2026/best-large-places-to-work)
- [100 Best Places to Work in Seattle](https://builtin.com/awards/seattle/2026/best-places-to-work)
- [100 Best Place to Work in Dallas](https://builtin.com/awards/dallas/2026/best-places-to-work)


"We are over the moon to be recognized by Built In as a best place to work," said Ash Core, VP of Talent Acquisition. "At SoFi, we believe that a world-class culture starts with hiring world-class people who are deeply aligned with your mission. Seeing our team celebrated in this way is a testament to how our employees embody The SoFi Way, making our workplace a place where innovation and collaboration happen naturally and our people can thrive."


These milestones are a direct reflection of the incredible people at SoFi and the culture of ambition we’re building together. We’re excited to keep growing the team and widening our impact.
