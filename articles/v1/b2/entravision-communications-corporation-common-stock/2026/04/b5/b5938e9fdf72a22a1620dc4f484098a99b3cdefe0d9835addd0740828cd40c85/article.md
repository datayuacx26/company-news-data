### Entravision to Announce First Quarter 2026 Financial Results


April 23, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s29.q4cdn.com/283296970/files/doc_news/Entravision-to-Announce-First-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-2026.pdf)


BURBANK, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Entravision (NYSE: EVC), a media and advertising technology company, announced today that it will release its first quarter 2026 financial results after market close on **Tuesday, May 5, 2026** . The company will host a webinar to discuss its results followed by a question-and-answer session at **1:30 p.m. PT/ 4:30 p.m. ET** the same day.


The webinar may be accessed on the company’s Investor Relations website at[investor.entravision.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.entravision.com%2Fevents-and-presentations&esheet=54521505&newsitemid=20260423652339&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.entravision.com&index=1&md5=a9c48114846a1f026ad63c68e61ed366) or via[webinar registration](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fentravision-sso.zoom.us%2Fwebinar%2Fregister%2FWN_kSfAKNhDQs6i-xfUFrSEqg&esheet=54521505&newsitemid=20260423652339&lan=en-US&anchor=webinar+registration&index=2&md5=11fc9549d0b3aa204800ac63312628d9) .


The webinar will also be archived on the company’s Investor Relations website under the[Events](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.entravision.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54521505&newsitemid=20260423652339&lan=en-US&anchor=Events&index=3&md5=b83fadff7c44ce9ef4c85f4b79978070) section.


**About Entravision**


Entravision (NYSE: EVC) is a media and advertising technology company. In the U.S., we maintain a diversified portfolio of television and radio stations and digital advertising services that target Latino audiences. Our advertising technology business consists of Smadex, our programmatic ad purchasing platform, and Adwake, our mobile growth solutions business. Entravision is the largest affiliate group of the Univision and UniMás television networks. Shares of Entravision Class A Common Stock trade on the NYSE under ticker: EVC. Learn more about our offerings at[entravision.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fentravision.com&esheet=54521505&newsitemid=20260423652339&lan=en-US&anchor=entravision.com&index=4&md5=5fcc6a534f6d61c66f4dbcfbd4666931) .
