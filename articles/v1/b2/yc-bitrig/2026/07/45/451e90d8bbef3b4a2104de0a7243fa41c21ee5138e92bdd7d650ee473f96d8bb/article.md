# Try Bitrig’s New, More Powerful Free Plan

Bitrig and the AI agents it uses have gotten much more powerful. They’ve also gotten more token-hungry. Frankly, our free plan hasn’t kept pace.


### Today, we’re fixing that:


-


If you have your own Claude or ChatGPT subscription, you can now send up to 10 messages per day on the Bitrig Free Plan.


-


If you use the credits included with the Bitrig Free Plan, we’ve removed the daily limit. You can now use your entire monthly allotment in a single extended session.


The goal of the free plan is to give you enough room to take Bitrig for a genuine test drive and experience our vision for the future of app development.


We release a major new update every week. In the last few weeks, we’ve added[diff review and manual code editing](https://bitrig.com/blog/review-diffs-and-edit-your-code) ,[installing apps directly to your Home Screen with Bitrig Remote](https://bitrig.com/blog/bitrig-remote-install-and-distribute-apps) , and[simulator use so Bitrig can interact with the apps it builds](https://bitrig.com/blog/bitrig-simulator-use) .


If it’s been a minute since you last opened Bitrig, give it another try. And if there’s a feature or two you still need,let us know .


We’re building fast.
