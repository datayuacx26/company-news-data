# Bitrig Remote Can Now Install and Distribute Your Apps

The iPhone is nearly 20 years old, but before Bitrig there was no real way to build a native app for your phone on your phone.


Bitrig Remote turns your iPhone into a professional app development environment, with on-the-go access to frontier AI agents, the full Xcode toolchain, the latest Apple SDKs, and streaming simulators.


But development doesn’t stop at the simulator.


Today, we’re excited to share:


## Bitrig Remote can now install apps directly on your Home Screen and publish builds to TestFlight.


Tap “Install on this iPhone,” and Bitrig Mac compiles your app, signs it, and uploads the build to Bitrig’s server for installation on your device.


In just a few moments, you have the latest version of your app. Gestures under your thumb, haptics in your hand, the camera, the keyboard, and all the other details you need to test on a real device to understand how your app really feels.


And when you’re ready to share, Bitrig Remote can now help with that too.


Tap “Publish to TestFlight,” and Bitrig Mac uploads an archive of your app to App Store Connect. From there, you can distribute the build with TestFlight and even submit it to the App Store.


You can now start with an idea, code it with AI, test it in the simulator, install it on your device, and publish it to TestFlight.


All from Bitrig Remote on the iPhone in your pocket.
