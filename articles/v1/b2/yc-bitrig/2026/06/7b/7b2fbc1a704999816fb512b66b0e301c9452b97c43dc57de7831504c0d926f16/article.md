# Join Us for Bitrig Hacks and Bitrig Bash at WWDC26

Next week is WWDC. It’s also Bitrig’s first birthday. Last year at WWDC, we demoed the very first version of Bitrig and handed out waitlist passes.


To celebrate, we’re hosting two community events: a hackathon and a party. The entire Bitrig team will be in town, and we’d love for you to join us.


## Bitrig Hacks


On Wednesday, June 10, we’re hosting[Bitrig Hacks](https://events.ycombinator.com/bitrighacks-june2026) at the YC office in Mountain View from 11 AM–5 PM.


It’s an afternoon of hacking with Apple’s newly released developer betas. We’re bringing together developers, designers, founders, and students to explore what clever things can be built while the APIs are still fresh.


The goal is not to build a complete app. The goal is to find something newly possible and make it real enough to demo.


We’ll have lunch, snacks, prizes, a great panel of judges, and a room full of people excited to build with Apple’s new SDKs.


For more information or to sign up, visit[here](https://events.ycombinator.com/bitrighacks-june2026) .


## Bitrig Bash


Then on Thursday night, we’re hosting[Bitrig Bash](https://luma.com/hbytg7n8) at El Jardín on Santana Row from 5 PM–10 PM.


It’ll be a night of great food and drinks, good vibes, and even better company. Meet longtime indie developers, Apple engineers, other Bitrig users, and people from across the Apple developer community.


RSVP[here](https://luma.com/hbytg7n8) .


## See You at WWDC


A big part of WWDC has always been meeting people in person.


Sometimes it’s someone who lives on the other side of the world, and sometimes it’s someone who lives down the road but you only met because you both showed up for the same week in June.


After a year of working on Bitrig, it would be really nice to meet more of you in person.


Come hack with us on Wednesday. Come celebrate with us on Thursday.


We hope to see you next week.
