## How Creative Collaboration Drives Better Results in Healthcare Teams


[Josh Linkner](https://joshlinkner.com/) will present


*Co-Flow: The Harmony of Creative Collaboration* . His work draws on his more than 30 years of experience as a business leader and musician.


He has founded five technology companies and supported over 100 startups; he has also performed in more than 1,000 concerts as a jazz guitarist.


This mix of experience shapes Linkner’s perspective on teamwork. In both business and music, success depends on how people create together in real time.


Many healthcare organizations face a gap. Teams have strong individuals, yet their results fall short of what that talent would suggest. Creativity exists. Collaboration exists. But they rarely work in sync.


Linkner’s concept of co-flow addresses that gap. It describes a state where teams align, engage deeply, and build ideas together.


This is not theory. It reflects how high-performing groups act under pressure. They listen, adapt, and contribute without waiting for permission.


## Why Healthcare Leaders Need Faster Innovation and Stronger Teamwork


[Patient experience](https://nrchealth.com/solutions/patient-experience/) now shapes how organizations are judged. Scores affect trust, growth, and long-term success. Leaders need teams that can solve problems quickly and think in new ways.


Many teams fall into familiar patterns: they rely on hierarchy, avoid risk, and protect the status quo. Over time, progress slows.


Linkner challenges the outdated expectation of many leaders that a single expert can drive change. His[HUB26 keynote](https://nrchealth.com/events/hub/speakers/) replaces that belief with the concept of “assembled genius,” which shifts focus to the group.


Under this concept, every team member holds value. Many ideas remain hidden in daily work, and strong leaders create space for those ideas to surface.


So what changes when teams adopt this mindset? They generate more ideas. They test them more quickly. And they move forward with shared ownership. The result is steady progress, not stalled plans.
