## Why Multigenerational Teams Matter in Healthcare


[Dan Collard](https://healthcareplussg.com/team/dan-collard/) will present


*Genfluence: Leading Your Team from Boomers to Zs* . His session centers on leading teams that span multiple generations.


Collard brings more than 30 years of healthcare experience, having worked in operations, consulting, and technology startups. He also served as executive vice president and chief growth officer at TeamHealth, one of the nation’s largest physician practice groups. His work focuses on building strong organizations across key measures such as patient experience, staff engagement, and financial performance.


Today, many healthcare teams include Baby Boomers, Generation X, Millennials, and Generation Z. Some teams include five generations at once.


Each of these groups holds a different view of work. Some value structure and clear hierarchy, while others expect flexibility and fast feedback. These differences can create tension—but when managed well, they can also drive better results.


## Turning Generational Differences into Strengths


Many leaders treat generational gaps as a problem. Collard takes a different view, showing how these differences can support stronger teamwork and better outcomes.


His keynote includes practical tools, data, and real examples. He explains why each generation behaves the way it does, and connects those behaviors to workplace performance. He also addresses some common myths. For example, some leaders believe younger staff lack commitment; others assume that older staff are likely to resist change. These ideas limit progress.


Collard replaces those assumptions with clear guidance, emphasizing learning how to value each group and use strengths appropriately. So what changes when leaders adopt this mindset? Teams communicate more clearly. Trust grows. And staff members feel respected. These changes lead to higher engagement and better retention.


## What You’ll Learn at This HUB26 Keynote Session


This session gives leaders a number of tools they can apply in daily work.


Key learning points include:


- Describing traits that define each generation in today’s workforce


- Applying cultural building blocks to support multigenerational teams

The session focuses on clear action, not abstract ideas. Attendees will leave with steps they can test in team meetings, hiring practices, and communication plans.
