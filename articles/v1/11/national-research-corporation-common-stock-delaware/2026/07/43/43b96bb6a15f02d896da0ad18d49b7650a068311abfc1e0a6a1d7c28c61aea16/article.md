# NRC Health to Report Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on July 28th

LINCOLN, Neb., July 7, 2026 – NRC Health (Nasdaq: NRC), a leader in healthcare experience improvement solutions, today announced it will release its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, on Tuesday, July 28, 2026, at approximately 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time. The second quarter 2026 results will be posted on the NRC Health Investor Relations website at


[https://nrchealth.com/investor-relations/](https://nrchealth.com/investor-relations/) .


NRC Health CEO Trent Green and CFO Shane Harrison will host a conference call to discuss the financial results on Tuesday, July 28, 2026, at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time. A live webcast link and call replay details will be available on the NRC Health Investor Relations website at[https://nrchealth.com/investor-relations/](https://nrchealth.com/investor-relations/) .
