## What Can Healthcare Leaders Learn from World-Class Consumer Experience?


Healthcare leaders can learn that the principles behind great experiences are remarkably consistent across industries.


At


[HUB26](https://nrchealth.com/events/hub/) ,


[Elizabeth Daugherty, Founder and Principal Consultant of SpurConnX](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eadaugherty) , and


[Douwe Bergsma, Chief Growth Officer at Piedmont Healthcare](https://www.linkedin.com/in/douwebergsma) , will share perspectives shaped by leadership roles with organizations including Delta Air Lines, American Express, Georgia-Pacific, Procter & Gamble, and The Home Depot. Their message is straightforward: organizations earn trust when they listen closely, understand people deeply, and act consistently.


The session examines how leading consumer organizations use experience design, feedback loops, and cross-functional collaboration to


[deliver on their brand promise](https://nrchealth.com/blog/aligning-brand-and-patient-experience-in-healthcare/) .


Healthcare leaders can apply those same disciplines while honoring the unique responsibilities of patient care.


## How Is Piedmont Healthcare Applying Consumer Experience Principles?


Piedmont Healthcare is applying proven consumer experience strategies to


[strengthen patient experience](https://nrchealth.com/solutions/patient-experience/patient-experience-guide/) while maintaining high-quality care with safety at its core.


During the session, attendees will learn how the health system listens to patients, caregivers, and frontline teams and translates those insights into meaningful action.


The result is an approach designed to build trust, strengthen loyalty, and create experiences that reflect what people value most.


The discussion will explore how organizations can:


- Use experience strategy to deliver on brand promises


- Build trust and affinity with patients and communities


- Harness patient listening and closed-loop feedback


- Break down silos that limit collaboration and improvement


For hospital and health system leaders, the value lies in practical application.


## Why Does Patient Listening Remain a Leadership Priority?


[Patient listening remains a leadership priority](https://nrchealth.com/blog/healthcare-leadership-patient-experience-best-practices/) because understanding people is the foundation of improving experiences.


One of the session’s learning objectives focuses on harnessing patient listening and closed-loop feedback to inform decisions.


Rather than collecting feedback and moving on, the discussion will explore how leading organizations transform insight into action. The strongest organizations don’t simply collect feedback—they create systems that help teams hear, understand, and respond.


### **How Can Healthcare Organizations Break Down Experience Silos?**


[Healthcare organizations can break down experience silos by connecting insights across departments and aligning teams around shared goals.](https://nrchealth.com/blog/metrics-that-matter-brand-experience-trust-loyalty/)


The session identifies collaboration as a key component of delivering better outcomes. When marketing, operations, clinical teams, digital experience leaders, and frontline staff work from a shared understanding of patient needs, organizations can move from isolated improvements to coordinated action.


[Patient experience](https://nrchealth.com/platform/listen/) ,


[employee experience](https://nrchealth.com/solutions/employee-experience/) , and


[community trust](https://nrchealth.com/solutions/community-insights/) are deeply connected. Improving one often strengthens the others.


That is one of the session’s most important observations: people experience an organization as one organization, not a collection of departments.
