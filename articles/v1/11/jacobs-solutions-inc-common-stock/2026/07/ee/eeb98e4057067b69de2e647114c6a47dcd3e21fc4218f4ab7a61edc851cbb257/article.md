# Jacobs declares quarterly dividend

DALLAS – The Board of Directors of


[Jacobs](http://www.jacobs.com/) (NYSE: J) has declared a quarterly cash dividend payable to shareholders in the amount of $0.36 per share of Jacobs common stock. This dividend will be paid on September 18, 2026 to shareholders of record as of the close of business on August 21, 2026.
