# Jacobs to drive transportation improvements in Georgia

Jacobs has been selected by the Georgia Department of Transportation (GDOT) for a five-year


General Engineering and Design Services


contract supporting transportation infrastructure across GDOT District 2 in East Central Georgia.


Jacobs will provide multidisciplinary engineering and design services for transportation projects across the district's 27 counties. The work will support roadway and infrastructure improvements that enhance connectivity and support economic growth across the region.


GDOT District 2 is one of the state's largest


districts and supports key transportation


routes critical


to regional mobility and economic activity. With key freight corridors and some of Georgia’s fastest growing communities, the improvements are an important driver of the state's economy. The contract will support GDOT's efforts to deliver critical infrastructure improvements safely and efficiently across District 2.


The selection builds on decades of collaboration with GDOT and a growing portfolio of transportation projects throughout Georgia. Recent work includes GDOT's use of


[real-time traffic analytics during major construction activities in metro Atlanta,](https://www.jacobs.com/projects/traffic-management-gdot) enabling data-driven decisions on lane closures and traffic management strategies that reduce disruption for motorists during construction.


### **Advancing mobility through innovation**


As transportation systems become increasingly connected, agencies are looking for smarter ways to improve network performance and support growing transportation demands. Jacobs provides planning,


engineering


and digital solutions that improve how transportation networks operate and perform.


We’re combining engineering with data-driven insights to improve safety while keeping people moving.


"


Communities across Georgia continue to grow, creating new demands on transportation networks," says Americas Executive Vice President Tom Meinhart. "Working alongside the Georgia Department of Transportation, we're delivering practical solutions that improve connectivity, support economic development and create long-term value for communities across the region."


### **Shaping the future of Atlanta**


Jacobs is also contributing to projects that are redefining how people experience Atlanta’s urban core.


One example is the


[15th Street Extension in Midtown Atlanta](https://www.jacobs.com/newsroom/news/street-purpose-jacobs-vision-more-connected-midtown-atlanta) , a collaborative effort involving GDOT and Midtown Alliance. The project provides a new east-west connection between the Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit Authority (MARTA) Arts Center Station and surrounding streets, improving connectivity for pedestrians, cyclists, transit users and drivers while supporting continued growth in one of Atlanta's most active districts.


The award-winning initiative reflects a broader shift toward safer multimodal transportation solutions that enhance access and create more connected communities.


Jacobs brings a


continued commitment to


transportation improvements across Georgia, from major highway and freight corridors in East Central Georgia to innovative mobility solutions in the heart of Atlanta.
