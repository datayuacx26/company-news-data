# Gibraltar to Announce Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on August 5

[PDF Version](https://ir.gibraltar1.com/node/25041/pdf)


BUFFALO, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 22, 2026--[Gibraltar Industries, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gibraltar1.com&esheet=54574391&newsitemid=20260722367128&lan=en-US&anchor=Gibraltar+Industries%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=7f84d1405287b9c51baf3136ac14e461) (Nasdaq: ROCK), a leading manufacturer and provider of products and services for the residential, agtech and infrastructure markets, announced today that it expects to release its second quarter 2026 financial results at approximately 7:30 a.m. ET on Wednesday, August 5, 2026. It also expects to discuss the results on a conference call that will be webcast live that same day starting at 9:00 a.m. ET. Hosting the call will be Chief Executive Officer Bill Bosway and Chief Financial Officer Joe Lovechio.


Those who wish to listen to the conference call should visit the Investors section of the Company’s website at[www.gibraltar1.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.gibraltar1.com&esheet=54574391&newsitemid=20260722367128&lan=en-US&anchor=www.gibraltar1.com&index=2&md5=0abb7b1f0ad646cebb5dc978d9685320) . The call also may be accessed by dialing (877) 407-3088 or (201) 389-0927. For interested individuals unable to join the live conference call, a webcast replay will be available on the Company’s website for one year.


**About Gibraltar** Gibraltar is a leading manufacturer and provider of products and services for the residential, agtech, and infrastructure markets. Gibraltar’s mission, to make life better for people and the planet, is fueled by advancing the disciplines of engineering, science, and technology. Gibraltar is innovating to reshape critical markets in comfortable living and productive growing throughout North America.
