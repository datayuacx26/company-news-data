# Rogers Expands 5G Service to Matane-sur-Mer, Quebec

**New tower brings wireless coverage to west end of Matane**


MONTREAL, August 5, 2026 – Rogers today announced it has expanded its 5G wireless network to Matane‑sur‑Mer in Matane, Quebec, improving safety and access to reliable communications for residents and businesses.


“As Canada’s most reliable wireless network, we’re investing in expanding 5G service to more places like Matane‑sur‑Mer,” said Edith Cloutier, Regional President, Quebec & Atlantic, Rogers. “This new 5G service will strengthen connectivity for residents and local businesses.”


Matane-sur-Mer is a key business hub in Quebec’s Bas-Saint-Laurent region and is home to the Port of Matane and the Matane Industrial Park – major economic contributors to the city, region and province.


Over the past decade, Rogers has invested nearly $40 billion in its networks. This expansion builds on these investments, delivering improved 5G wireless coverage to Matane-sur-Mer’s growing business community, residents, and visitors.
