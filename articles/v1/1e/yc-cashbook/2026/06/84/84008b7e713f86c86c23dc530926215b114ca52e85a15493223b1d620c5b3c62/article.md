# How to Download Wallet and Virtual Account Statements on CashBook

A complete guide to the five reports you can generate in CashBook UPI, from a single transaction list to a consolidated company account statement built for your CA and year-end audits.


## What you can do with reports in CashBook UPI


CashBook UPI gives your business a Virtual Account and a UPI wallet for each team member, so you can fund staff, approve their spends, and keep every rupee accounted for. The Reports section is where all of that activity turns into clean, shareable statements.


Instead of asking your accounts team to stitch together bank statements and screenshots, **you can download exactly what you need in a few clicks** : a member-wise spending summary, a UPI transaction report, a Virtual Account statement, or one consolidated company account statement that covers everything in a single file. This guide explains all five reports, when to use each, and how to download them.


## Who these reports are for, and when to use them


Most CashBook UPI customers run team and field-staff spending through member wallets. These are the moments the reports are built for:


-

-

-

1.


#### Transactions report


The Transactions report lists all UPI payments, refunds, and collections made by your team. It is the report to reach for when you need a straightforward record of what was paid, when, and by whom, for month-end reconciliation or sharing with your accounts team.
**Available in Excel or PDF.**


2.


#### Approvals report


The Approvals report gives you a per-member summary of spending against approvals, which is ideal for reviewing field-staff and team expenses. For each member, it shows:
- Total amount spent
- Approved amount
- Rejected amount
- Pending, flagged, and resubmitted amount
Use it when you want to see Spent, Approved, and Rejected amounts for all members in one view.
**Available in Excel or PDF.**


3.


#### Virtual Account report


The Virtual Account report shows every money movement in and out of your business Virtual Account: top-ups from your bank, money sent out to member wallets, and money pulled back from member wallets into the Virtual Account.
If you want a single report covering your Virtual Account and all member wallets together, use the Consolidated company account statement instead.
**Available in Excel or PDF.**


4.


#### Individual Wallet report


The Individual Wallet report gives you the full transaction history for any one team member's UPI wallet. When you generate it, you pick the member whose wallet you want.
If you want all members' wallets in one report, use the Consolidated company account statement instead.
**Available in Excel or PDF.**


5.


#### Consolidated company account statement


The Consolidated company account statement is a single report covering every money movement across your Virtual Account and all member wallets in one place. If you have ever wanted one clean file that tells the whole story of your company's money on CashBook UPI, this is it.

## How to download a report in CashBook UPI on Web.


All reports are generated from the same place: the All Reports page. Here is the standard flow.
1. Go to **All Reports** from the left navigation, under Cashbook UPI.


2. Click **Generate New Report** on the top right.


3. Pick a **Report Type** from the dropdown.


4. Choose a date range, and any other filters the report offers (for example, the member for an Individual Wallet report).


5. Click **Download** . Your report appears in the All Reports table once it is ready, and you can download it from there.


## Other places you can download reports from


The All Reports page is the main hub, but a few reports can also be downloaded right where you are already working:


1.


**All Transactions page** : apply your filters (date, member, status, and so on) and download a transaction report directly from that view.


2.


**Virtual Account page** : download a Virtual Account statement filtered by date. To open it, click the Virtual Account card on your Dashboard.


3.


**Member wallet detail page** : open any member's wallet and download that wallet's transaction report from there.


4.


**All Approvals section** : download the Approvals report from here too, under Approvals in the left navigation.


No matter where you start, every report you generate also appears on the All Reports page, so you can always find it and download it again later.


## How to get bills along with your UPI transaction report


When you generate a Transactions report or a Consolidated company account statement, you can choose to include your transaction bills. This is what your CA will usually ask for at audit time. You get two options on the file format step:


1.


**Excel with bill links** : the Excel report includes a link to each transaction's bill. These links expire in 7 days, so this option is best for quick access.


2.


**Excel and bill files as ZIP** : the Excel report plus all the bill files, bundled into a ZIP and saved straight to your device. These never expire, which makes them best for sharing with your CA or for year-end audits.


To choose your option:


1.


Click **Generate New Report** on the All Reports page.


2.


Select **Transactions or Consolidated company account statement.**


3.


Pick your date range.


4.


On the file format step, choose Excel with bill links or Excel and bill files as ZIP.


5.


Click **Download.**


You can also download a transaction report with bills directly from the All Transactions page after applying your filters.


## Where to find the reports you have already generated (Web)


Go to All Reports from the left navigation, under Cashbook UPI. The page shows every report generated by you and your team, with these details for each one:
- Report Type
- Report Name
- Generated By
- Duration (the date range it covers)
- Download Status: Ready, Generating, Failed, or Expired


You can filter the list by Generated At, Type, Generated By, or Status. Click any row marked Ready to download it again.


## Frequently asked questions


**Q1. What reports can I download in CashBook UPI?**
You can download five reports: the Consolidated company account statement, Transactions, Approvals, Virtual Account, and Individual Wallet. All five are generated from the All Reports page in Web.


Q2. **How do I get all Virtual Account and wallet transactions in one report?** Use the Consolidated company account statement. It is the only report that covers your Virtual Account and all member wallets in a single file.


Q3. **Which report should I share with my CA for an audit?** The Consolidated company account statement, generated with bills included as a ZIP. It gives your CA the complete money trail, plus every bill file in one download that does not expire.


Q4. **How do I see how much each team member spent?** Use the Approvals report. It shows each member's total spent, approved, rejected, and pending amounts in one view. You can download it from the All Reports page or the All Approvals section.


Q5. **Can I download CashBook UPI reports as PDF?** Yes. Transactions, Approvals, Virtual Account, and Individual Wallet reports are available in Excel or PDF. The Consolidated company account statement is available in Excel only.


Q6. **Where do my generated reports go?** Every report you generate appears on the All Reports page, no matter where you started it. You can filter, find, and re-download it there any time.
