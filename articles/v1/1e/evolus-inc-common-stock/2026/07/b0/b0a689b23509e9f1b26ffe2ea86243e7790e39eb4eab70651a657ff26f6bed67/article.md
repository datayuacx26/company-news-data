## Question #1: When should I start getting injections?


Like many things in life, this depends - on you, your aesthetic goals, your current appearance and the conversations you have with your injector.


**Despite what you might have read on the Internet, there’s no “set age” to start getting injections.** They’ve been approved for adults 18 and older.


**Generally, whenever you become concerned about your moderate to severe glabellar lines** (those pesky 11 lines between your brows) is a good time to start.


**YOU (and your injector) get to decide when (or if) to start neurotoxin treatments.** It’s all about what you’re comfortable with.


## Question #2: Are injections painful?


It depends. For the majority of people, pain is minimal.


You might feel a slight pinch or prick. Patients report that the pain is typically[mild and easily managed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30119866/#:~:text=Adverse%20events%20associated%20with%20injections,redness%2C%20swelling%2C%20and%20bruising.) .


Before ever getting a needle close to your face, your injector will chat with you about what to expect, your pain tolerance and how you’ll manage discomfort. It’s more than likely **your injection will be over quicker than you anticipated.**


You may receive a topical anesthetic and/or or ice packs to numb your face before and after the injection. This helps reduce potential pain and for some patients, eliminate it altogether.


**After you’ve received your injection, you may notice some tenderness and swelling, but this shouldn’t last long.**


## Question #3: Once I start getting treated with injectables, am I locked in forever?


Nope! 😅


**Your results will last for three to four months** (and some folks prefer to go up to 6 months between injections). However, retreatment is necessary to maintain your results.


Depending on how many “units” of treatment you receive and your desired outcome, you and your injector will create an injection schedule that works to keep you looking refreshed, relaxed and ready to take on the world.


If you don’t love your results for any reason, don’t panic. **The results of injectable neurotoxins are by nature temporary.**


Once the tox wears off, your face will gradually return to how it looked before you started injections.


Exactly how long it takes to get back to your pre-injection look varies from person to person.
