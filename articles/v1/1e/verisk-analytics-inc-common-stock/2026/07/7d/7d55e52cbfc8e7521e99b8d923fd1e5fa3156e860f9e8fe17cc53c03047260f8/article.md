# Restoration, Not Just Estimation: Why Our Name Is Changing

In an era of record-breaking weather, shifting policyholder expectations, and intense pressure on loss costs, “estimating” no longer tells the full story of the work we do together. The new name for Verisk Property Estimating Solutions, now Property and Restoration Solutions, reflects the full arc of property restoration, from first notice of loss (FNOL) to the moment a family or business is made whole again. The name is new; your tools, the data and our commitment to your results are not.


## What the new name really says about your work


For years, “estimating” captured one critical, technical step in the property claim process. But the work runs far wider, from FNOL and initial triage through mitigation, rebuild, and quality review. The result is a fully restored property and satisfied policyholder.


Our new name speaks to the end‑to‑end journey and the broad ecosystem of professionals who make it possible:


- **Carriers and adjusters** resolving claims with more clarity, speed, and confidence
- **Restoration contractors and mitigation pros** moving seamlessly from assessment to work authorization and completion
- **Mortgage servicers, real estate professionals, suppliers, and investors** managing property risk across portfolios
- **Policyholders** counting on a faster, fairer, and more transparent path back to normal


Property and Restoration Solutions emphasizes outcomes, not just tools. It signals a data-rich, AI‑enabled, connected ecosystem designed to make every step more accurate, faster, and more defensible.


## What stays the same


The way you work with us stays the same. You can continue to rely on the same people, solutions, and support you use today, with no changes to how your business runs or how we show up for you.


Your day-to-day logins, tools, and workflows work just as they did yesterday. Documentation will adopt the new branding over time.


## What this means for you


You may notice the updated name in communications, presentations, and on our website. Nothing about how you work with us changes.
