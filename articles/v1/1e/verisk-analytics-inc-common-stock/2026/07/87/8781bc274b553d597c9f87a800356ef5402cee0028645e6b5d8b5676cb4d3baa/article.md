# Modern Forms Management for a Faster Insurance Market

Forms are the foundation of every insurance product. So why does managing them still feel like the slowest part of your business?


In a recent live webinar, *Smarter Forms, Faster Decisions: A New Era for Mozart and GenAI* , the Verisk team walked through the state of forms management today, demoed the redesigned Mozart Composer® platform, and previewed what’s coming in 2026. Here’s a recap of what came up, and where you can go deeper.


## Forms management complexity is compounding


Regulatory change isn't slowing down. ISO® updates, multi-state filings, proprietary endorsements, and emerging exposures keep coming, and for many carriers, each one lands in a workflow still running on Word documents, SharePoint sites, shared drives, and email chains that can no longer keep up with the pace or complexity.


The friction shows up everywhere:


- **Coverage uncertainty.** It's hard to answer how your forms compare to ISO or to a competitor when the analysis lives in someone's head.
- **Update lag.** Regulatory and ISO changes can take days or weeks to propagate across affected forms.
- **Limited scale.** Manual review caps how much your team can analyze, no matter how skilled they are.
- **Audit risk.** Version control breaks down when edits live in inboxes and local drives.


A[Guidewire 2026 trends piece](https://www.guidewire.com/resources/blog/industry-trends/insurance-technology-trends) put it bluntly: in 2026, the industry is moving away from "inbox-driven" underwriting (such as fragmented PDFs and email threads) toward unified command centers. Forms management sits right in the middle of that shift.
