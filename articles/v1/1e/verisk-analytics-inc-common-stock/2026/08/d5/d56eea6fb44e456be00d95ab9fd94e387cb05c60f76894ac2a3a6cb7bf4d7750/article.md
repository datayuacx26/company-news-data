# AI in Property Estimating Workflows: Moving from Exploration to Adoption

In our first article in this series,[AI in Estimating and Claims Workflows: Where Efficiency Begins](https://www.verisk.com/blog/ai-in-estimating-and-claims-workflows-where-efficiency-begins/) , we explored how AI can reduce manual effort and help industry professionals move more efficiently through everyday claims and estimating workflows. Property claims, estimating, and restoration teams are being asked to manage more work, move faster, and maintain quality. That creates the opportunity to reduce routine work related to important decisions, so professionals can spend more time where their judgement creates the most value.


Identifying these use cases is only the first step—carriers, contractors, and other organizations across the property claims ecosystem need to implement AI tools in a practical way. Adopting AI does not have to begin as a major transformation initiative. It can start with practical, focused use cases that reduce friction in workflows teams already understand.


## The gap between experimenting with AI and creating value


Piloting an AI tool may be simple, but turning it into a useful, trustworthy part of everyday work is more challenging. Organizations may introduce general tools without clearly defining expected outcomes. Employees may be asked to change or duplicate established workflows to pressure test.


Adoption tends to stall for reasons that have little to do with technology itself:


- **No defined problem.** Organizations introduce general **** tools without a clear job to do or expected outcomes.
- **Workflow disruption.** Users are asked to leave familiar systems and re-enter information into new or multiple tools.
- **Lack of trust.** Validating outputs from AI tools requires additional time and work.


The fix isn’t a bigger initiative, but rather a narrower starting point: addressing a specific source of workflow friction.


## Start with work your teams already understand


To identify initial AI use cases, look for tasks that people perform frequently, take time to complete, and deliver outputs that an experienced professional can review quickly.


In claims and restoration workflows, such tasks include:


- Organizing and labeling photos
- Creating property sketches
- Surfacing relevant estimate information
- Summarizing assignment activity


None of these sound like transformation projects, and that’s the point. When AI reduces the time spent on a routine task someone already does today, the impact is immediate and tangible that drives further adoption and deeper engagement with AI solutions.


Recent feedback from XactAI™ users reinforces this pattern. Photo labeling and descriptions, along with Sketch Scan, stood out as areas of practical interest and use. Both replace clearly defined manual steps while keeping the professional in control of the result.


## AI should strengthen human claims expertise, not replace it


Effective adoption isn’t about removing people from the process. Instead, it’s about giving them a better starting point.


- Instead of labeling every photo individually, you review suggested labels.
- Instead of building a sketch from scratch, you validate and adjust a generated one.
- Instead of searching across notes and activity, you start from a summarized view.


In every case, the professional still applies judgment, checks the information, and makes the call. What changes is the amount of time and resources that facilitate that decision.


## Connected data makes each decision easier


The value compounds when AI works across the full picture rather than one isolated task. When capabilities span line items, sketches, rebuild requirements, pricing, and broader business trends, they stop being separate features and start functioning as a connected claims ecosystem, where each input makes the next decision more informed.


## Begin with one problem and build from there


You don’t need to solve every AI use case at once. A practical path:


1. **Identify** one recurring source of manual effort.
2. **Introduce** an AI capability within that existing workflow.
3. **Measure** whether it saves time or improves consistency.
4. **Expand** into adjacent work once users trust the output.


XactAI supports this approach by bringing AI-powered capabilities into the Xactware® suite of products your teams already use. From photo labeling and sketching to assignment summaries, line-item guidance, pricing, and business insights, you can start with the capability that addresses your most immediate need.


AI transformation may be the long-term destination. Meaningful adoption starts by making today’s work easier.
