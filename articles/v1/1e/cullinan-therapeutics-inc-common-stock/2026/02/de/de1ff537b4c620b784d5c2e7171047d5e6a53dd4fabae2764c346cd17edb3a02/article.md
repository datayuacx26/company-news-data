# Cullinan Therapeutics to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences

CAMBRIDGE, Mass., Feb. 24, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Cullinan Therapeutics, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=KSwwOnLajpyxuHNkziMVlqN-TQJO6d-Gw2y3mHwoFDjBBzIszDXmLF_4b7wPy7uDP9h-Yv4uPATftQB4a8q2-QFb2tAjOU8NWaSNC3kV2Q7vlRmWTPPn_mHNzRT8elCN) (Nasdaq: CGEM; “Cullinan”), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company accelerating potential first- or best-in-class, high-impact therapies in autoimmune diseases and cancer, today announced its participation in the following upcoming investor conferences:


- Nadim Ahmed, Chief Executive Officer, and Jeffrey Jones, M.D., M.B.A., Chief Medical Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at the TD Cowen 46th Annual Healthcare Conference on Tuesday, March 3, 2026, at 1:10 p.m. ET.
- Nadim Ahmed, Chief Executive Officer, and Jeffrey Jones, M.D., M.B.A., Chief Medical Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at the 2026 Leerink Global Healthcare Conference on Tuesday, March 10, 2026, at 4:20 p.m. ET.


Webcasts of the fireside chats will be available under the Events and Presentations section of the Company’s investor relations website at[https://investors.cullinantherapeutics.com/events](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=SM-nF3erIL6xVcFR3JMgOp2_voeH6zgX8IBz2qzJ2mwP88_YIJtT2tnM-iPy9WVy7h0bSKU0fhK8yhQYu4gmRwtFbKswPN_8arM6Tfz3zm9u4G_d9ib06JcfdA24EQyhuDy_1LDrgltXVuDK8sg0P5zlHw7AXPNtrIC20y70TJw=) .
