# Intellicheck To Present at the Planet Microcap Showcase in Las Vegas

MELVILLE, NEW YORK – May 27, 2026 –[Intellicheck](http://www.intellicheck.com/) , Inc. (Nasdaq: IDN), an industry-leading identity company delivering proprietary on-demand digital and physical identity validation solutions, today announced that CEO Bryan Lewis will present at the Planet Microcap Showcase 2026 taking place on Wednesday, June 17, 2026 at the Bellagio Hotel & Resort in Las Vegas, Nevada. Joining him at the conference will be CFO Adam Sragovicz.


The presentation will begin at 3:00 p.m. PT on Wednesday, June 17. A webcast of the presentation will be available at:
[https://event.summitcast.com/view/bpjo3VVjZ25pp6SXpUua92/CkqDC2MahLMw5ATycVoAhm](https://event.summitcast.com/view/bpjo3VVjZ25pp6SXpUua92/CkqDC2MahLMw5ATycVoAhm) ‍


If you would like to book one-on-one investor meetings with Intellicheck CEO Lewis and CFO Sragovicz and attend the Planet MicroCap Las Vegas 2026 conference please make sure you are registered here:[REGISTER](https://www.meetmax.com/sched/event_130817/conference_register.html) ‍


One-on-one meetings will be scheduled and conducted in person at the conference venue: Bellagio Resort & Hotel in Las Vegas, Nevada.

‍ **About Intellicheck**
Intellicheck (Nasdaq: IDN), the industry leader in identity verification management, prevents the use of unauthorized IDs to stop identity-based fraud. Intellicheck is the only SaaS-based validation and proofing service that uses a unique and proprietary analysis of DMV-issued IDs to create trusted, real-time customer identity verification experiences across a wide variety of sectors, both in-person and digitally. Intellicheck is processing identity transactions for almost half the adult population in the United States and Canada annually with state-of-the-art technology solutions that are providing a seamless, invisible ID verification experience while delivering 99.975% decisioning in under a second when a customer is using our tools to capture the document.
