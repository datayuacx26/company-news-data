# How to use demand generation marketing to your advantage

Demand generation marketing is an effective strategy for promotions, gaining more customers, customer retention and account-based marketing (ABM), and more. Here’s how to do it right.


For businesses to thrive in this cut-throat environment, they must get the word out about their offerings to anyone willing to hear.


Some brands start with lead generation marketing to gather the contact information of their prospects so they can be contacted later. In comparison, many other businesses take the demand generation approach to build brand awareness, create meaningful relationships with customers, and maintain an online presence.


These days, businesses need demand generation marketing, an amalgamation of techniques that create a bona fide desire for your products in your market. From a robust online presence to improved customer experience, you must put your intended audience at the core of your demand generation strategy.


## What is demand generation?


Demand generation marketing refers to the process that helps you create awareness and generate demand for your brand’s products and services. It expands your audience base and drives relevant traffic to transform your viewer’s interest into action through the sales funnel.


Furthermore, the demand generation approach is about creating an anticipated sales pipeline for your sales force. The ultimate goal is to[transfer the qualified leads through the sales funnel](https://www.podium.com/article/what-are-leads-in-marketing-and-how-to-generate-more/) until they convert into a new customer.


Eventually, it’s essential to let your target market recognize the value that you bring to the table. This gets them interested in your brand so that you can nurture leads and convert them[into long-term customers](https://www.cloudtalk.io/blog/5-ways-to-turn-customers-into-your-biggest-advocates) .


All in all, the prime goal of demand generation marketing is to create a need for your offerings in the target audience’s mind. It helps you boost brand awareness, build trust, and foster deeper connections with your prospective clients.


### Demand Generation vs. Lead Generation


Demand generation and lead generation are separate functions, and they look different in practice. Here’s a look at the distinctions:


**Focus**
Demand Generation focuses on awareness and piquing interest in an offering. Lead generation, however, is all about following up with potential buyers who’ve expressed interest.


**Objective**
Demand generation is rooted in brand awareness and bringing in leads. Lead Generation is concerned with moving prospects through the funnel into customers.


**Stage in the Buyer’s Journey**
Demand generation is better suited for reaching people in the awareness and consideration stages. Lead generation is appropriate for the consideration and decision stages.

## 4 Tips to Create a Demand Generation Strategy


Implementing successful and robust demand-generation marketing requires more than merely creating digital ads and developing creative content and analytics. You need a foolproof plan rooted in lead scoring to move prospects through the sales funnel.


A comprehensive approach can deliver remarkable results while accelerating customer relationship management, acquisition, and[customer retention](https://www.profitwell.com/customer-retention/guide) when done correctly.


If you aim to achieve long-term profitability and similar results, here are some of the steps for laying the groundwork for your demand generation plan.


### Step 1: Set Realistic Goals


Every demand generation marketing plan starts with setting quantitative, clear, and realistic goals and objectives. You need to know the purpose of your campaigns and activities. Also, set relevant key performance indicators (KPIs) to gauge the effectiveness of your strategy.


Your approaches and plan will constantly evolve with the changing needs of your customers, and you may also need to adjust and align your demand gen objectives accordingly.


### Step 2: Define Your Audience


Developing your[ideal customer profile (ICP)](https://marketingkeep.com/blog/ideal-customer-profile/) and defining your audience helps you get a clear understanding of your market, precisely your intended audience. This strategic document details all the essential information you can refer to when developing content messaging, campaigns, etc.


The more you understand your customer profile, the more effectively you can plan and execute. Buyer personas are comprehensive profiles based on factual details of your potential accounts within your current audience base.


When you know a person’s job title, education level, job position, company details, etc., you can better understand their pain points, goals, and purchase influences. Simultaneously validate your findings with the sales team to know that you are on the right path to optimize demand gen.


### Step 3: Map Your[Buyer’s Journey](https://www.podium.com/ebooks/10-ways-to-text/)


You need to consider the purchasing process of your customers. Mapping your buyer’s journey helps you outline the essentials that you need to incorporate in your demand generation marketing plan. It documents each customer persona’s typical actions and needs through research, consideration, awareness, decision stages, and overall sales funnel.


This helps you identify gaps in engagement or content where you might lose your potential buyer’s interest. A buyer journey helps you with targeted and innovative content to bridge these gaps and enhance your lead-generation efforts.


Also, it helps you ramp up your SEO, increase your PPC spend, and align your content with the demand generation plan to build brand awareness. You can create eBooks, infographics, and webinars appropriate according to the buyer’s stage in the sales funnel.


### Step 4: Generate High-Quality Content


An effective demand generation plan requires you to generate customized content for your targeted buyer personas that address their pain points. Build high-performing campaigns by incorporating buyer personas and their purchasing stages into your content plan.


When you have your ideal customer profile, you can[focus your marketing efforts](https://www.podium.com/article/brand-management/) on the clients who can provide the most value to your business.


The ideal customer profile or buyer personas help you create compelling and targeted campaigns that reach potential clients and[generate optimal business revenu](https://www.podium.com/article/scaling-your-business/) e. It also enables you to deploy ABM resources more efficiently.


## 3 KPIs to Measure Your Demand Generation Strategy


Sales teams can use the following KPIs to evaluate the success of a demand generation strategy:


**Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** : This one helps you determine how much you spent to gain a customer. Knowing this number can help you monitor your marketing activities and determine where you need to make adjustments. A lower CPA tells you that you’re on the right track.


**Demand Generation Cycle Length** : This number tells you how long it takes to turn a lead into a customer. The cycle length can indicate lags in your sales funnel. You can then use this information to refine marketing efforts to bring in more sales faster.


**Customer Lifetime Value (CLV** ): CLV captures the total revenue a customer brings in over their time with your company. By keeping the focus on bringing on and keeping high-value customers, you’ll enjoy longer-term success.


These KPIs can be useful in helping sales teams take stock of their demand-generation strategies and make more informed decisions that help them land more long-term customers at a lower cost.
