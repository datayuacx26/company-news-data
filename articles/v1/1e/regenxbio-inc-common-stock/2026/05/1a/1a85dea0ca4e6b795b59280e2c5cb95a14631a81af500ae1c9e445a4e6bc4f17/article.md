# REGENXBIO Announces Presentations at the American Society of Gene & Cell Therapy 2026 Annual Meeting

ROCKVILLE, Md.


, May 7, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- REGENXBIO Inc. (Nasdaq: RGNX) today announced presentations at the 2026 American Society of Gene & Cell Therapy Annual Meeting ("ASGCT 2026") taking place May 11-15, 2026, in Boston, Massachusetts.


**Oral Presentations:** The oral presentations include an overview of recent advancements in production titer and product quality achieved through


REGENXBIO's NAVXpress ® manufacturing platform and an encore presentation of


topline pivotal data from RGX-202, the company's next generation gene therapy program for


Duchenne muscular dystrophy.
