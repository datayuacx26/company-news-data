## Zero to Global: Scaling AI-Native Startups with Twilio


As a Developer Evangelist at Twilio, I’ve had a front-row seat to the AI revolution, spending countless hours in the trenches with brilliant founders and engineering teams.The energy in the Asia-Pacific region right now is electric 🌏✨. From meetups to startup events, hackathons, and more, everyone is keen to get their hands on, and build with AI.


Picture this: It’s 2026. A developer in a Bengaluru high-rise is pushing code to optimise a fleet in Jakarta, while an R&D lab in Shenzhen fine-tunes an AI agent for a factory in Hanoi. Meanwhile, we’re building huge data centres in Australia. This isn’t a "future vision", it’s the daily heartbeat of APAC.


And being an Aussie, I’m especially stoked to see how we’re punching above our weight. There is however, a recurring theme I see founders realise far too late: Building a model that can think is only half the battle. When you’re ready to take that model global, you hit the "Growth Tax."


### The "Last Mile" Nightmare 🛑


I’ve seen incredible teams hit a wall because the "plumbing" of their product couldn't keep up with the brilliance of their AI. Building great AI is only half the battle, the other half is infrastructure. It usually looks like this:


-


**The Trust Tax:** If a user in Manila waits 30 seconds for an OTP that never arrives, you haven't just lost a lead, you've lost your reputation.


-


**Regulatory Spaghetti:** Lean teams burn 40% of their roadmap trying to "stitch together" ten different local vendors and fragmented compliance laws. It’s a massive drain on your burn rate.


-


**The "AI Pause"** : In markets like Japan or Singapore, a 2-second lag ruins the magic. For an AI agent to feel human, the voice needs to be close to instant. 🎙️🤖


### Turning Infrastructure into Your "Cheat Code" ⚡


The most successful AI-native startups I work with stay lean by using programmable infrastructure to scale without 10x-ing their headcount. They use Twilio as the "Nervous System" for their AI. This gives their models the ability to interact with the physical world.


Using Twilio you can accomplish a lot:


-


**The Mouth (Low-Latency Voice):** Using ConversationRelay for lifelike, full-duplex interactions. No more awkward pauses.


-


**The Ears (Real-Time Intelligence):** Forking live audio to your models via Media Streams to detect sentiment or dialects mid-call.


-


**The Memory (Contextual RAG):** Using Segment as your AI's long-term memory, so it remembers every interaction across WhatsApp, SMS, and Email.
