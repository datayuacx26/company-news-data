# Standing Beside People When Healthcare Access Matters Most

#### Standing Beside People When Healthcare Access Matters Most


In Bangladesh, access to healthcare is nothing but a race against time and distance. Although public healthcare is promised to all, there are only 7 doctors available per 10,000 people1 compared to the World Health Organization’s recommended 22.8 doctors per 10,000 people. Such a shortage of healthcare providers results in long waiting times, overcrowded facilities, delayed treatments, and inconsistencies in the quality of the care provided. This situation puts a heavy burden, especially on rural people, individuals with disabilities, and families from lower-income groups. For them, barriers such as distance, cost, and lack of specialized services turn what might otherwise be manageable healthcare issues into serious threats to lives and well-being.


Recognizing these realities, Banglalink, as a people-first company deeply rooted in care, has shaped the MyBL Super App, the country’s most trusted telco app, accessible across operators, to stand beside people with timely access to medical support, anytime and from anywhere. This is not about adding more features or following trends, but about understanding what people genuinely need and responding with intent.


Through a single platform, users can access over 1,000 doctors and specialists from 41 medical departments. Whether you need a general physician at midnight, a pediatrician for your child’s sudden fever, or a consultation with a diabetes specialist, support is just a tap away – 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.


The platform offers a complete solution for all types of medical needs. Users have the option to consult with both general practitioners and medical specialists, including pediatricians, gynecologists, dermatologists, psychiatrists, nutritionists, diabetes specialists, and so on. Users can search for specialists based on their symptoms from 23 different categories and 41 departments, which cover more than 30 clinical areas. What sets MyBL Care apart is its empathetic, people-first approach, designed not just around technology, but around real human needs, comfort, and accessibility.


To make sure that the experience is as professional and reliable as it would be in-person, every step of the process, including consultations, prescriptions, and follow-ups are recorded within the app. This means users’ health history is accessible at all times, creating a detailed record of their health progress that grows with them over time. Users don’t need to worry about data protection, as strict access control and anti-tampering measures are in place, taking the users’ data security up a notch.


MyBL Care functions as a complete health management system at affordable cost. Users can also order essential medicines for home delivery and manage their health insurance, all within one secured platform. The system helps to reduce anxiety and stress, which people experience during medical emergencies, by providing them with constant access to assistance.


Mehrab Turhan, a young professional living in a busy part of Dhaka, starts feeling unwell late in the evening. With traffic and long wait times at clinics, visiting a doctor immediately feels difficult. Instead, he turns to the MyBL Super App. Within minutes, he navigates to the healthcare section and books an online consultation with a licensed doctor. He selects a convenient time slot and joins the session from home. The doctor listens to his symptoms, provides guidance, and shares a digital prescription through the app.


Following the consultation, Mehrab orders the prescribed medicines through an integrated partner service. He also schedules a home sample collection for basic tests, avoiding the need to visit a diagnostic centre. Throughout the process, the app keeps everything in one place, from consultation history to prescriptions and reports. What could have taken hours across multiple locations is completed seamlessly within a single platform.


This experience shows how the MyBL Super App is making healthcare more accessible, reducing friction, and supporting people in moments that matter. With a growing community already trusting MyBL Care for their healthcare needs, and strong daily and monthly engagement on the platform, Bangladeshis are discovering a new way to take control of their wellbeing. For families juggling work, childcare, and elderly parents, the MyBL Care offers the most invaluable thing: reassurance. People no longer need to feel anxious about finding a doctor at midnight. People no longer need to spend money on costly trips to urban centers for medical experts. They no longer need to stress about their ability to afford quality medical guidance during critical moments.


While digital platforms cannot take the place of hospitals entirely, they still play a supportive and complementary role, especially in a country like Bangladesh. Healthcare accessibility shouldn’t be a privilege; it should be a right. With MyBL Super App's integrated healthcare services, Banglalink is making that right a reality for millions across Bangladesh, ensuring that quality care is no longer defined by distance or circumstance, but by accessibility.
