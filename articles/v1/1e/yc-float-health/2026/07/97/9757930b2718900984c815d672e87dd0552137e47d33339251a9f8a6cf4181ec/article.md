# New From Float: AI-Driven Portal Updates, 45K+ Home Visits Completed, 100+ Partnerships (and More!)

We launched Float for one simple reason: to modernize the specialty pharmacy industry to the benefit of everyone involved, from patients to nurses to payers to prescribers to hospitals. Today, we’re both celebrating our rapid progress toward achieving this vision and doubling down on our recent momentum with a series of announcements:


- The release of AI-driven product updates for nurses and pharmacies
- Fresh data on our performance so far
- Our 100th pharmacy partnership
- Plans to expand into Florida in Q2


Together, these achievements strengthen our ability to move all the care that doesn’t need a hospital to the home. Here’s a closer look at what’s new for our network and why we’re more confident than ever in the future of on-demand specialty pharmacy.


---


## New For Nurses: Mobile App Updates


We’ve always described ourselves as a marketplace built by nurses, for nurses. At a time when healthcare workers[struggle to find](https://www.businessinsider.com/nurses-gig-work-apps-jobs-hospitals-pay-uber-2025-1) safe, reliable, and empowering gig work, we’ve doubled down on deploying a nurse-first approach that prioritizes flexible schedules, enhanced compliance, and patient-centric care (more on this approach in our latest blog). Significant enhancements to our mobile application strengthen this commitment. New features in our nurse mobile app allow nurses to:


- Receive and accept home infusion opportunities with greater ease
- Review comprehensive clinical details for patients
- Manage their schedules
- Track upcoming payments for completed visits
- Access our Learning Management Software (LMS) for upskilling opportunities


These updates weren’t created in a vacuum—they’re the direct result of ongoing feedback from our nurse community, which will continue to drive future updates.


## New For Specialty Pharmacies: a Refreshed Portal


Our tech-focused approach also streamlines how specialty pharmacies coordinate and deploy at-home care. These partners send home infusion patient referrals to Float, and we connect these patients with highly qualified nurses who deliver treatments in the comfort of patients’ homes. This approach makes routine medicine 12x cheaper than hospital-based alternatives while improving treatment adherence and reducing hospital admissions.


Today’s announcement includes an updated specialty pharmacy portal, which leverages AI to:


- Share performance metrics to optimize operations
- Automate document creation, including patient charts and prescriptions that require doctor sign-off
- Secure qualified nurses for patient home visits within minutes
- View comprehensive patient visit schedules
- Monitor treatment progression in real-time
- Supply medication based on automated insights


By centralizing all relevant information in one intelligent platform, we’ve eliminated the administrative nightmare of lengthy email chains, redundant communications, and chart bounceback—creating efficiencies that directly improve patient care and pharmacy operations.


## Rapid Momentum: More Home Visits, High Nurse Retention, 100+ Partnerships


Last year, we celebrated a[$10M Series A](https://floathealth.com/blog/series-a/) funding round and expanded operations in California, Arizona, and Texas.


Now, at the end of Q1 2025, our numbers tell a compelling story:


- **45,000+ home visits completed:** We now complete over 3,000 home visits per month! By Q1 2026, we aim to target 8,000 home visits per month.
- **A retention rate 4x higher than industry average:** Float’s 12-month nurse retention rate is now 68%, while the industry average for hospital RNs is[18](https://www.nsinursingsolutions.com/Documents/Library/NSI_National_Health_Care_Retention_Report.pdf) %. This is thanks to our competitive pay, customizable schedules, and supportive resources.
- **100+ partnerships with specialty pharmacies:** We just inked our 100th partnership! Our customers now include some of the biggest names in specialty medicine, such as Optum, CVS, Option Care, Alliance RX (Walgreens), Kroger, Kabafusion, Soleo Health, NuFactor, Care Fusion, CA Specialty Pharmacy, and most recently, Advanced Infusion Care.
