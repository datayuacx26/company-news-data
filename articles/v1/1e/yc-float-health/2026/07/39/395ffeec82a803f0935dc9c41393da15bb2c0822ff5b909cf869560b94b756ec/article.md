# Float Health Achieves SOC 2: The New Standard for Safe, Scalable Home Infusion Nursing

[Float Health](http://floathealth.com/) is excited to share a big milestone: **we are now SOC 2 certified.**
This achievement is emblematic of how Float is a partner that takes security, compliance, clinical safety, and patient trust as seriously as we take our nursing quality.


Specialty Pharma is constrained by nurse supply. Pharmacies want to fill every referral that comes in, and quickly. However, the market is composed of tiny agencies operating in the dark ages that lack the requisite controls and compliance necessary to properly handle PHI and prevent costly HIPAA breaches. These fractured and fossilized nursing sources confer outsized liability and auditing burdens on pharmacies.


As one of the only SOC 2-certified nurse supply partners, this isn’t just a badge for our website.
Float has redefined what ***safe to scale*** nursing partnership needs to be for Specialty Pharmacies, providers, and the patients who depend on them.


## Why SOC 2 matters for home infusion nursing


Home infusion is one of the most sensitive environments in healthcare. High-risk therapies, complex regimens, and real-world unpredictability all collide in the living room. Every visit touches PHI, clinical decision-making, and patient safety - and every visit is happening far from the controls of providers and pharmacies.


For Specialty Pharmacies, this means you’re not only coordinating logistics, you’re managing risk.


Historically, the home nursing landscape has made this an area ripe for HIPAA breaches.


Specialty Pharmacies rely on a patchwork of dozens, or even hundreds, of agencies. Some pharmacies might try buying expensive middleware vendors that digitize a rolodex but fail to consolidate supply or accountability. This forces pharmacies to carry all the exposure. They take on risk across:


- dozens of micro-vendors
- rogue documentation habits that result in “shadow PHI” in email, texts, and random printouts
- uncontrolled PHI pathways that produce printed papers and faxes
- uneven credentialing and training
- variable devices accessing sensitive patient info


It’s risky, expensive, and not built to support a pharmacy’s growth.


SOC 2 changes that calculus, it verifies the safest way to put nurses in the home, clinically and compliantly.

They aren’t.


They don’t aggregate, develop, or maintain clinical supply. They don’t absorb any operational or compliance risk. And they don’t take responsibility for the outcomes that happen in the home.


Float does.


**Float is a true supply aggregator.**
**Middleware systems are digitized rolodexes.**


**Float:**


- Builds, vets, and maintains a national network of infusion-capable nurses
- Handles onboarding, credentialing, quality checks, and visit notes
- Owns the nurse relationship, ongoing training, and clinical documentation expectations
- Standardized workflows for scheduling, PHI access, communication, and visit notes


**Middleware vendors:**


- Do not create or maintain supply
- Simply list agencies or independent nurses who pharmacies already contract with

Middleware adds yet another cost center. Float owns the end-to-end clinical workflow to improve your bottom-line and adds nurses that grow your top-line.
