# Float Health Launches in Arizona

Float Health, a venture-funded innovator of healthcare solutions, is thrilled to announce the expansion of its services to cover all of Arizona. This strategic move represents a significant milestone in Float Health’s mission to move all the visits that don’t need a hospital to the home.


As part of this expansion, Float Health is bolstering its existing coverage by partnering with large Specialty Pharmacy providers to better serve patients in Arizona. This initiative will provide comprehensive support to patients throughout their healthcare journey, ensuring they receive the specialized care they need, right in the comfort of their own homes.


Float Health’s decision to expand into Arizona is driven by the state’s diverse population demographics. While Float’s patients span ages from newborns through geriatrics, Arizona’s population makeup makes this move especially impactful as it expands access to care that promotes overall well-being and longevity.


Moreover, the unique geography of Arizona makes it an ideal fit for Float Health’s innovative model. With a single metropolitan hub in the state, many patients face challenges accessing hospital and infusion centers, causing them to miss routine medications that keep the symptoms of their diseases at bay. By deploying expert nurses directly to patients’ homes, Float Health not only ensures treatment adherence but also reduces the costs of care and alleviates strain on overburdened healthcare systems.


Float Health remains committed to driving innovation and improving healthcare access for patients across the nation. With this expansion, Float Health continues to lead the charge in revolutionizing healthcare delivery, one visit at a time.
