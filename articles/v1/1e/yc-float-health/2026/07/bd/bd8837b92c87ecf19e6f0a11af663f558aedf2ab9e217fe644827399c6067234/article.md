# Float Celebrates Pride Month: Standing with the LGBTQ+ Community

At Float, we believe in equality. Full stop.


We proudly stand with the LGBTQ+ community—this month and every month.


In a time when divisiveness is being stoked from the top, we want to be clear about where we stand:


🏳️‍🌈 We support love in all its forms
🏳️‍⚧️ We affirm everyone’s right to exist as their full selves
🤝 We believe dignity, safety, and opportunity aren’t up for debate


## Healthcare Built on Trust and Respect


We know that healthcare—especially in the home—is built on trust, respect, and deep human connection. That means showing up with our values, not hiding them.


When Float brings specialty care into patients’ homes, we’re not just providing medical treatment. We’re creating moments of human connection between skilled nurses and people who need care. The interactions require an environment built on trust, where everyone feels seen, respected, and valued for who they are.


Our network of thousands of nurses across California, Arizona, Texas, and Washington represents the diversity of the communities we serve. We’ve built Float to be a place where healthcare professionals can bring their authentic selves to work, because we know that authentic care providers deliver better outcomes for patients.


## Not Neutral. Not Quiet.


So we’re not neutral. We’re not quiet.


This is not virtue signaling, it’s **virtue stating** . We’re a diverse team building a company that reflects the world we want to live in—one where all people are seen, respected, and included.


As we continue expanding access to home-based specialty care, we’re doing so with the conviction that healthcare is a human right that extends to every person, regardless of who they love or how they identify.


## Empathy: The Foundation of Nursing


Nursing is a profession built on a foundation of empathy. The practice of nursing requires recognizing the needs of others, then addressing those needs with compassionate care. Today, our LGBTQ+ community needs allies more than ever. Float is proud to show up as one. We stand with you, we see you, and we’re committed to building a healthcare system—and a world—that does too.


## Building the Future We Want to See


Healthcare transformation requires more than just better technology and logistics—it requires a commitment to human dignity at every level. As we work to move all the care that doesn’t need a hospital to the home, we’re building systems and communities that reflect our values of inclusion, respect, and equality.


Happy Pride 🏳️‍🌈


—Team Float
