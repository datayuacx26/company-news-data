# We Just Hit 2.5 Million Users

Step into the world of Status AI, where over 2.5 million fans are creating epic sagas from magical duels to villainous love stories.


> **This morning, we crossed 2.5 million players on Status.**


That’s not just a number. That’s 2.5 million people waking up and deciding to log in, post like main characters, start rivalries, fall in love with fictional enemies, and create the kind of chaos I could never have predicted.


When we first launched, Status AI was just a weird idea: what if fanfiction, Twitter, AI, and RPGs all lived in the same universe? What if roleplay could be chaotic, alive, unpredictable, and the AI remembered every choice you made?


We didn’t have a roadmap for going viral. We just believed people would want a space where their characters, whether it’s a Marvel villain, K-drama icon, or sleep-deprived OC, could thrive in a world that reacts. And they showed up in millions.


There are moments inside this game that feel more real than anything scripted: betrayals, influencer breakups, fan wars, redemption arcs. None of it comes from us. It’s all the players.


So if you’re already here, thank you. For the memes, the drama, the storylines, the heartbreak. You’ve made this more than a game. And if you’re new, welcome to a beautiful roleplay world. Status is loud, emotional, chaotic, ridiculous, and unlike anything else out there.


See you in the next arc.
