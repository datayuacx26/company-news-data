# Sangoma vs Dialpad: Choosing the Right Business Communications Provider

Dialpad leads with AI-powered collaboration for cloud-native teams. Sangoma covers cloud, hybrid, and on-premises deployment with full ownership of the stack underneath it. The right pick depends on which of those two things your operations actually need more.


## **Why Compare Sangoma and Dialpad?**


Dialpad and Sangoma both show up on cloud phone shortlists, but they solve different problems. Dialpad is built for teams that want AI-first collaboration on a clean cloud-only setup. Sangoma delivers cloud, hybrid, and on-premises deployment from the same platform, with AI-powered analytics and transcription, full-stack hardware ownership, and one vendor accountable for the entire communications environment.


Three questions run through the rest of this comparison. Can your sites keep running voice during an internet outage, or is cloud-only sufficient? Does pricing stay predictable as you add contact center and sales dialing, or does the invoice fragment across separate products? And when something breaks, are you reaching a human or a chatbot?


This guide compares the two on pricing predictability, deployment options, hardware control, support model, and survivability.

## **Reliability and Local Survivability**


The deployment gap has direct consequences for reliability. Dialpad is cloud-only, so when the internet goes down, so do calls. It can reroute calls externally, but it has no mechanism for keeping local office communications running during a WAN outage: no local PBX continuity, no native POTS failover, no LTE/4G branch survivability.


Sangoma’s[StarBox appliance](https://sangoma.com/products/communications-platform/hybrid/) addresses this directly, as part of Sangoma’s hybrid UC. It keeps internal calls running, provides POTS and secondary internet failover, and supports optional 4G/5G failover. For a healthcare facility, a retail chain, or a distributed business with locations in areas with unreliable connectivity, the difference is not theoretical.


User reviews also report dropped calls, delayed ringing, Bluetooth instability on mobile, and inconsistent voice quality across Dialpad’s platform.

## **How Does the Pricing Compare?**


Dialpad’s entry pricing is low, but the total cost of ownership shifts considerably once contact center, analytics, or advanced support capabilities enter the picture. Sangoma’s model is per-seat and all-inclusive from the start.


### **How Dialpad Pricing Works**


Dialpad Connect starts at $15/user/month on the standard plan. The structure fragments from there. Contact center (Dialpad Support) runs $80–150/user/month. The sales dialer (Dialpad Sell) adds another $39–150/user/month. CRM integrations are locked behind the Pro tier. Outbound SMS has volume caps on lower plans. International calling is metered, and per-minute charges apply in the contact center. For a team that starts on Connect and grows into contact center and sales dialing, the invoice at renewal looks very different from the original quote.


### **How Sangoma Pricing Works**


Sangoma’s[Business Voice and Business Voice Plus](https://sangoma.com/business-phone-systems/hosted-pbx/pricing/) plans are priced per seat and include voice, video, messaging, collaboration, and 24/7 support without add-ons. Contact center capability is built into the platform with no separate product license. There are no per-ticket support fees, no hosting surcharges, and no surprise line items at renewal.

## **AI Features**


Both platforms invest in AI, but the focus differs. Dialpad built DialpadGPT, a proprietary LLM that transcribes calls in real time, scores sentiment, generates post-call summaries, and coaches agents mid-conversation with live prompts. Inside sales teams and coaching-driven contact centers get the most out of DialpadGPT because the real-time guidance directly improves rep performance on active calls.


Sangoma took a different route. Instead of coaching agents on live calls,[Sangoma’s AI](https://sangoma.com/products/sangoma-ai/) rworks two ways: cutting the number of calls agents handle, and speeding up the ones they do. CX can run AI virtual agents and chatbots through a Google Dialogflow integration, letting callers handle routine requests themselves rather than waiting for an agent. In hospitality, that means guests booking rooms or asking about amenities; in healthcare, patients getting answers without sitting on hold. Agents get transcription and sentiment scoring automatically and ready-to-edit draft replies across SMS, WhatsApp, and email. An internal knowledge bot (@ASKSangoma) pulls answers from Sangoma documentation, so support teams spend less time searching for product documentation.

## **Sangoma vs Dialpad FAQs**


**What happens to phone calls if a location loses its internet connection?**


With Dialpad, calls can be rerouted externally, but local office communications go down. With Sangoma, the StarBox appliance keeps internal calls running, supports POTS and secondary internet failover, and includes optional 4G/5G failover so that a WAN outage does not become a voice outage.


**Does Dialpad offer on-premises or hybrid deployment?**


No. Dialpad is cloud-only. Sangoma supports cloud, hybrid, on-premises, and virtual machine deployments from the same platform, with a straightforward migration path between them.


**How does Dialpad’s pricing compare to Sangoma at scale?**


Dialpad starts at $15/user/month, but contact center capability runs $80–150/user/month as a separate product. Sales dialing adds another $39–150/user/month. CRM integrations are gated to Pro tier. Sangoma’s per-seat model includes all of these capabilities with no product-line fragmentation.


**Which platform is better for healthcare or other regulated industries?**


Sangoma operates on a PCI-certified, HIPAA-compliant private network with a 99.999% uptime SLA. Dialpad documents HIPAA support but carries a 99.9% SLA and cloud-only infrastructure, which limits its fit for clinical environments where uptime and data residency are compliance requirements.


**Does Sangoma have AI features comparable to Dialpad?**


Both platforms offer transcription, sentiment analysis, and post-call summaries. Dialpad goes further on live call coaching with DialpadGPT, a proprietary LLM that prompts agents in real time during active calls. Sangoma goes further on operational automation with conversational IVR that handles routine inbound calls without human involvement, AI-drafted replies across SMS, WhatsApp, and email, and an internal knowledge bot for support teams. Dialpad’s AI requires cloud; Sangoma’s runs on cloud, hybrid, and on-prem.


**How difficult is it to switch from Dialpad to Sangoma?**


Sangoma’s white-glove onboarding and partner network handle the migration, including number porting, hardware transition, and deployment configuration. Every Switchvox cloud seat includes a free P325 desk phone. The process is guided rather than self-directed.


**Which platform handles multi-location businesses more effectively?**


Sangoma is built for multi-location environments. StarBox provides local survivability at each site, 4G/5G and POTS failover keep branches connected during outages, and Sangoma’s managed networking services give IT teams visibility across the full communications environment. Dialpad’s cloud-only model means each location’s voice reliability depends entirely on its internet connection.
