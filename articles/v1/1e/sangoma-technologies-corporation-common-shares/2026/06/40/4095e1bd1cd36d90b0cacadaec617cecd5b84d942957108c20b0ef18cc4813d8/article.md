# Sangoma vs RingCentral: Full Comparison for an Informed Decision

***Both Sangoma and RingCentral are established UCaaS providers, but they are built on different philosophies around deployment, pricing, and support.*** This comparison helps SMB buyers compare deployment options, core capabilities, pricing, and support. It does not cover custom enterprise quotes or plan-specific exceptions.


## **Why Are We Comparing Sangoma and RingCentral?**


RingCentral appears on almost every UCaaS shortlist by default. Sangoma earns its place differently: through deployment flexibility, transparent pricing, and owned infrastructure from phones to network management.


The comparison matters because these two platforms diverge sharply on questions that create friction later: What happens to calls when the internet goes down? What will this actually cost in month twelve? Who picks up when something breaks?


## **Sangoma vs RingCentral: Quick Take**


**Choose Sangoma if** you need hybrid or on-premises deployment, want a single vendor for UC and networking, need unlimited SMS without paying more, or are managing costs carefully.


**Choose RingCentral if** you are fully cloud-committed, have an IT team comfortable with a complex platform, and value a large integration marketplace over cost predictability.

RingCentral caps SMS at 25 messages per user per month on the Core plan, rising to 100 on Advanced and 200 on Ultra. International calling is always metered regardless of plan.

## **Security and Compliance**


RingCentral highlights an extensive portfolio of compliance certifications, which can be especially helpful for teams that rely on documented attestations and audit reports during vendor selection.


Where Sangoma has a genuine advantage is at the infrastructure layer. Because Sangoma owns UC, managed networking, SD-WAN, and firewall services under one vendor, security is not a separate conversation with a separate team. Network-level threat detection, access controls, and call data all sit within the same managed environment. When something needs to be investigated or audited, there is one vendor accountable for the full stack rather than a hand-off between a UCaaS provider and whoever manages your network separately.


RingCentral’s security scope covers the UCaaS layer only. Network security, firewalls, and infrastructure protection are the customer’s responsibility or a third party’s.

## **Sangoma vs RingCentral FAQs**


**What are the best alternatives to RingCentral?** Sangoma, Nextiva, Vonage, 8×8, and Zoom Phone are the most commonly evaluated alternatives for SMBs. Sangoma stands out for hybrid and on-premises deployment, US-based support, and end-to-end vendor ownership including networking and security.


**How difficult is it to transfer from RingCentral to Sangoma?** Sangoma handles porting with both phone and email support, and customers keep full control of their numbers without hidden fees or delays. RingCentral customers frequently report porting difficulties including long delays, contract-related refusals, and number loss risk if service is canceled before porting completes.


**Which is the better choice for security and compliance?** Both platforms support encryption, SSO, and HIPAA via BAA. Sangoma’s edge is at the infrastructure layer: managed security and network controls come from the same vendor, which simplifies audits and removes third-party dependencies. For regulated industries, ask both vendors specifically about data residency and audit log retention.


**Which platform suits different types of businesses?** RingCentral suits cloud-first businesses with stable infrastructure and an IT team that can manage a complex platform. Sangoma suits businesses that need deployment flexibility, have locations with unreliable internet, want one vendor for UC and networking, or are in industries like manufacturing, healthcare, or field services where calling reliability is non-negotiable.


**Does RingCentral offer deployment flexibility?** No. RingCentral is cloud-only with no hybrid or on-premises option. Businesses that need local survivability or on-premises data control cannot meet those requirements with RingCentral.


**How should I choose between Sangoma and RingCentral?** Start with deployment. If local survivability or on-premises control is a requirement, RingCentral is off the table. If cloud works for your environment, model the full cost of RingCentral’s Advanced plan (where most features actually unlock) including administrative fees and SMS exposure, and compare it to a Sangoma quote. Then check independent support ratings on G2 or GetApp for both vendors before signing anything.
