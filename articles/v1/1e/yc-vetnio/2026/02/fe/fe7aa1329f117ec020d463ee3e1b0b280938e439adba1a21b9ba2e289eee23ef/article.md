## Understanding Vetnio's Commitment to GDPR and Data Security


For any` GDPR compliant AI software for European veterinary clinics` , security isn't just a feature—it's the foundation[\[1\]](https://www.vetz.ai/blog/ai-gdpr-veterinar) . Vetnio was designed from the ground up with a security-first approach, ensuring that every function is underpinned by robust data protection measures. This deep-rooted commitment makes Vetnio a trustworthy partner for clinics across Europe.


### EU-Centric Data Storage and Processing


A cornerstone of Vetnio's compliance strategy is data residency. We primarily process and store all personal data within the European Union (EU) or European Economic Area (EEA)[\[2\]](https://vetnio.com/home/en/dpa) . This means your clinic's data is protected by the EU's world-class data protection standards[\[3\]](https://vetnio.com/datasecurity) . In the rare case that data must be transferred outside the EEA, Vetnio uses Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to maintain full GDPR compliance, ensuring your data is always protected[\[4\]](https://landing.vetnio.com/home/en/dpa) .


### Your Clinic is the Data Controller, Vetnio is the Processor


Under GDPR, it's crucial to know who is responsible for your data. When you use Vetnio, your clinic always remains the "Data Controller"—you own and control your data. Vetnio acts as the "Data Processor," operating strictly on your behalf and according to your instructions[\[21\]](https://vetnio.com/home/en/privacy-policy) .


This entire relationship is formalized in our clear and transparent[Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](https://vetnio.com/home/en/dpa) . By reviewing and accepting the DPA when you join, you get legal clarity and peace of mind about how your data is handled[\[6\]](https://landing.vetnio.com/home/en/privacy-policy) .


### A Multi-Layered Technical Security Framework


Vetnio protects your data with a robust security framework that meets the highest industry standards. Our technical measures include:


-


**Enterprise-Grade Encryption:** All clinical notes, calls, and recordings are protected with strong encryption, both while in transit over networks and at rest in our databases[\[7\]](https://vetnio.com/product) .


-


**Regular Penetration Testing:** Our systems are regularly tested by independent, external security experts who proactively find and fix potential vulnerabilities in a controlled environment[\[8\]](https://vetnio.com/home/en/data-and-safety) .


-


**Secure Backup Practices:** We use industry-standard data backup practices to prevent data loss and ensure your critical information is always accessible when you need it[\[9\]](https://landing.vetnio.com/home/en/data-and-safety) .


-


**Strict Access Controls:** Data is further secured through strict internal access restrictions, regular security audits, and binding confidentiality agreements for all employees[\[2\]](https://vetnio.com/home/en/dpa) .
