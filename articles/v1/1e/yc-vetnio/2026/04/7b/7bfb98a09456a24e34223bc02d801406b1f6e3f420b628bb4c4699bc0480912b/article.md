# Save Hours Daily with Cloud-Based Veterinary Practice Tools

The administrative burden on veterinarians is heavier than ever. Between consultations, procedures, and client communication, the hours spent on medical record documentation can feel overwhelming, often extending long after the last patient has gone home. While traditional practice management software (PIMS) helped digitize records, the real revolution in time-saving is happening in the cloud — and it goes far beyond your basic PIMS.


As of May 2026, the most efficient veterinary practices are not just using a single piece of software. They are building a modern "tech stack" that combines a foundational, cloud-based PIMS with specialized AI-powered tools. This approach automates the most time-consuming tasks, freeing you to focus on what truly matters: providing exceptional care to your patients. This guide will walk you through the essential components of this modern toolkit and how to implement them to reclaim hours in your day.


## The Foundation: Your Cloud-Based PIMS


The central hub of any modern practice is a cloud-based Practice Management System. Unlike server-based systems that tie you to a specific computer in the clinic, cloud software gives you the flexibility to access schedules, patient records, and billing from any internet-connected device, anytime.


Key benefits include:


- **Accessibility:** Review a patient's history from home or check your schedule on the go.
- **Automatic Updates:** The software provider handles all updates and security patches, so you're always using the latest, most secure version.
- **Scalability:** Cloud systems can easily scale with your practice, whether you're adding a new doctor or another location.


If your practice is still using an on-premise server, migrating to a cloud-based PIMS is the single most impactful first step. There are many excellent options available, each with unique strengths:


- [Provet Cloud](https://www.provet.cloud/veterinary-software-solution)
- [Digitail](https://digitail.io/)
- [Shepherd](https://www.shepherd.vet/features)
- [VETport](https://vetport.com/solutions)
- [Onward Vet](https://www.onwardvet.com/)
- [VetManaged](https://www.vetmanaged.com/)


## The Game-Changer: AI for Clinical Documentation


While a cloud PIMS provides the foundation, the most significant time savings come from tackling the biggest administrative bottleneck: clinical documentation. Manually typing or dictating SOAP notes is a tedious process that consumes a huge portion of a veterinarian's day. This is where AI copilots are making a revolutionary impact.


### How to Reduce Time Spent on Medical Record Documentation


The answer lies in tools that listen, understand, and write for you. The best AI tools for automating clinical notes in veterinary practice use ambient intelligence to capture the natural conversation between you, the pet parent, and the patient.


These systems are designed as **veterinary practice management tools that auto-generate SOAP notes** by:


1. Securely recording the consultation audio.
2. Transcribing the conversation with high accuracy for veterinary terminology.
3. Using AI to identify and structure the relevant information into a complete SOAP note.


This process transforms a 15-20 minute documentation task into a 2-minute review-and-approve workflow. For example, some clinics have found that an AI scribe[saves veterinarians 15 minutes per consultation](https://vetnio.com/home/en/blogs/veterinartidningen-sodra-djursjukhuset) .
