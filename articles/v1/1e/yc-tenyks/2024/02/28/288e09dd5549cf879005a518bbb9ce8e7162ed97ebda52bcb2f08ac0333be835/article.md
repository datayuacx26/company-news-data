# Top 10 MLOPs Conferences in 2024 for Computer Vision Engineers

In the rapidly evolving field of Computer Vision, staying current is key. In this post we short list, ordered by date, the go-to conferences for professionals looking to stay ahead, offering a platform to discuss cutting-edge research and real-world applications of AI.


‍


### 1.[World AI Cannes Festival](http://www.worldaicannes.com/)


**Location:** Cannes, France


**Dates:** February 8 — 10


Join the World AI Cannes Festival 2024 for a unique experience, bringing together[individuals](https://www.worldaicannes.com/en/the-speakers) and[companies](https://www.worldaicannes.com/en/startup-pitchs) at the forefront of crafting[impactful AI strategies](https://www.worldaicannes.com/en/demo-sessions?dates%5B%5D=2024-02-08&q=) . This third edition is not just about profits but aims to celebrate the diverse future of AI. Whether you’re looking for inspiration or to contribute to the next generation of leaders, this event offers a valuable opportunity. The festival stands out for its commitment to honoring and advancing the varied facets of AI, making it a essential for anyone interested in staying abreast of technological innovation.


[www.worldaicannes.com](http://www.worldaicannes.com/)


‍


### 2.[ReWORK AI Summit](http://ai-summit-west.re-work.co/)


**Location:** Santa Clara, California, USA


**Dates:** February 13 — 14


The ReWORK AI Summit is a vital nexus of technology and practicality, showcasing the latest AI advancements and their real-world applications. Attendees connect with[AI pioneers](https://ai-summit-west.re-work.co/speakers) driving innovative research while delving into tangible case studies that unravel AI’s business value. The event’s[distinctive fusion](https://ai-summit-west.re-work.co/why-attend) of academia and industry provides a unique platform to explore the theoretical and practical dimensions of AI. For those keen on understanding AI’s transformative potential in both research and business landscapes, the ReWORK AI Summit is an essential rendezvous, offering a rich blend of insights and networking opportunities.


[www.ai-summit-west.re-work.co](http://ai-summit-west.re-work.co/)

### 3.[Computer Vision Summit — AI Accelerator Institute](http://world.aiacceleratorinstitute.com/)


**Location:** San Jose, California, USA


**Dates:** April 16 — 17


Discover the forefront of technological innovation at the Computer Vision Summit by AI Accelerator Institute, where attendees delve into the extraordinary world of applied computer vision. Uncover the latest engineering marvels, from synthetic data to real-time latency challenges and the transformative impact of transformers in computer vision. This summit is a[must-attend](https://world.aiacceleratorinstitute.com/location/sanjose/speakers) for those seeking a comprehensive understanding of groundbreaking developments. Explore the practical implications across diverse industries through in-depth discussions and gain[valuable insights](https://world.aiacceleratorinstitute.com/location/sanjose/whyattend) from fellow end-users. Elevate your knowledge and stay at the forefront of the evolving landscape of computer vision at this exceptional event.


[www.world.aiacceleratorinstitute.com](http://world.aiacceleratorinstitute.com/)

### 4.[Embedded Vision Summit](http://embeddedvisionsummit.com/)


**Location:** San Jose, California, USA


**Dates:** May 21 — 23


Experience the pinnacle of innovation at the Embedded Vision Summit, the leading conference for visionaries integrating[computer vision and edge AI](https://embeddedvisionsummit.com/attend/) into products. Tailored for a global audience of technology professionals, the summit is essential for those developing computer vision and edge AI-enabled products, spanning embedded systems, cloud solutions, and mobile applications.[Over three days](https://embeddedvisionsummit.com/speakers/) , delve into a wealth of knowledge from tutorials to Deep-Dive Day, covering the latest technical insights, business trends, and vision technologies. What sets this event apart is its[practical focus](https://embeddedvisionsummit.com/deep-dive-days/) on deployable computer vision and visual/perceptual AI, seamlessly bridging academic theories from conferences like CVPR to the concrete needs of innovators shaping real-world products.


[www.embeddedvisionsummit.com](http://embeddedvisionsummit.com/)

### 5.[AI World Congress](http://aiconference.london/)


**Location:** London, United Kingdom


**Dates:** May 30 — 31


Discover the forefront of artificial intelligence at the AI World Congress 2024, featuring keynote[speakers](https://aiconference.london/speakers/) from industry giants like WPP, Telus, McKinsey, SingTel, and other notable entities. Attendees will gain valuable insights from influential leaders, making it a must-attend event for professionals eager to stay abreast of AI’s latest trends and innovations. The conference promises a[comprehensive lineup of experts](https://aiconference.london/agenda/) , ensuring a diverse range of perspectives on the transformative impact of AI across various sectors. Don’t miss the opportunity to be part of this dynamic gathering where knowledge meets experience, shaping the future landscape of artificial intelligence. Stay tuned for the full list of speakers, promising a stellar lineup for this premier AI conference.


[www.aiconference.london](http://aiconference.london/)


‍


### 6.[CVPR (Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Conference)](http://cvpr2023.thecvf.com/)


**Location:** Seattle, Washington, USA


**Dates:** June 17 — 21


The IEEE/CVF Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Conference (CVPR) stands as the foremost annual event in computer vision, encompassing the main conference along with co-located[workshops](https://cvpr2023.thecvf.com/Conferences/2024/workshop-list) and short courses. The significance lies in its ability to offer exceptional value to students, academics, and industry researchers. Attendees can expect an unparalleled opportunity to engage with cutting-edge research, fostering collaboration and knowledge exchange. CVPR’s commitment to affordability ensures that participants, regardless of their background, can access a platform that encapsulates the latest advancements in computer vision and pattern recognition.


[www.cvpr2023.thecvf.com](http://cvpr2023.thecvf.com/)


‍


### 7.[ICML (International Conference on Machine Learning)](https://icml.cc/)


**Location:** Vienna, Austria


**Dates:** July 21 — 27


Participate in the International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), the paramount gathering for professionals committed to advancing the field of machine learning within artificial intelligence. Attendees are drawn to ICML for its global reputation in showcasing cutting-edge research across various aspects of machine learning, spanning artificial intelligence, statistics, and data science. The event’s distinctiveness lies in its comprehensive coverage of application areas like machine vision, computational biology, speech recognition, and robotics. Notably, ICML is recognized as one of the fastest-growing AI conferences globally. The diverse participant base, ranging from academic and industrial researchers to entrepreneurs, engineers, graduate students, and postdocs, creates a dynamic environment for collaborative exploration and learning.


[www.icml.cc](https://icml.cc/)


‍


### 8.[ECCV ( European Conference on Computer Vision )](http://eccv2024.ecva.net/)


**Location:** Milano, Italy


**Dates:** Sep 29 — Oct 4


Attend the European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), a biennial pinnacle in Computer Vision and Machine Learning, orchestrated by the European Computer Vision Association (ECVA). The event, occurring in even years, serves as a convergence point for the scientific and industrial communities in these domains. Since its inaugural gathering in 1990 in Antibes, France, ECCV has showcased groundbreaking research, with subsequent editions hosted across Europe. The most distinctive feature lies in its ability to unite experts, fostering collaboration and knowledge exchange.


[www.eccv2024.ecva.net](http://eccv2024.ecva.net/)


‍


### 9.[MLOps World](http://mlopsworld.com/)


**Location:** Austin, Texas


**Dates:** Nov 7 — 8


Join MLOps World, an international[community](https://mlopsworld.com/speakers/) created by the Toronto Machine Learning Society (TMLS) to foster a deeper understanding of deploying ML models into live production environments. This initiative serves as a nexus for the wider AI ecosystem, connecting companies, practitioners, academics, and open-source contributors. With an inclusive approach, MLOps World addresses the unique needs of its 9,000+ members, comprising ML researchers, professionals, entrepreneurs, and engineers. The most special aspect lies in its commitment to re-imagining a connected community, providing support, fostering growth, and ensuring inclusion for all participants.


[www.mlopsworld.com](http://mlopsworld.com/)


‍


### 10.[NeurIPS (Neural Information Processing Systems)](http://nips.cc/)


**Location:** Vancouver, Canada


**Dates:** Dec 8 — 12


Make your mark in the field of machine learning at NeurIPS 2024 in Vancouver, an esteemed conference founded in 1987. This multi-track interdisciplinary event features invited talks, demonstrations, symposia, and presentations of refereed papers, offering a comprehensive exploration of the latest advancements. What sets NeurIPS apart is its professional exposition, emphasizing machine learning in practice, providing attendees with invaluable insights. The series of tutorials and topical workshops create an informal yet enriching environment for the exchange of ideas. Attendees, including researchers, practitioners, and enthusiasts, are guaranteed a unique opportunity to stay at the forefront of machine learning trends and applications.


[www.nips.cc](http://nips.cc/)
