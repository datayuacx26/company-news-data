# Summer Whites


Velontra is proud to have hosted two midshipmen from the United States Naval Academy, Sam Anglen and Ethan Schneider, for a summer internship and collaboration. During their time with us, they were exposed to real engineering projects, proposals for the USN, a congressional staff visit, a test fire, and retired flag officer mentoring sessions. This collaboration between Velontra and USNA supports and benefits both organizations as we endeavor to expand the understanding of midshipmen in research efforts and challenges.
