# TD SYNNEX Accelerates Investment in U.S. Public Sector Business at Annual Red, White & You Event

### TD SYNNEX Accelerates Investment in U.S. Public Sector Business at Annual Red, White & You Event


May 12, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/109490932/files/doc_news/TD-SYNNEX-Accelerates-Investment-in-U-S--Public-Sector-Business-at-Annual-Red-White--You-Event-2026.pdf)


*New leadership, certification and location enhancements designed to empower partner success in the U.S. public sector*


FREMONT, Calif. & CLEARWATER, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


TD SYNNEX (NYSE:SNX) today kicked off the 20th edition of its annual U.S. public sector customer event, Red, White & You (RWY). Throughout the 3-day event, leaders from TD SYNNEX and its subsidiary,[DLT Solutions](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dlt.com%2F&esheet=54534177&newsitemid=20260512914047&lan=en-US&anchor=DLT+Solutions&index=1&md5=b5f6e2def8d631084cedb954909607a0) , will explore the latest trends in technology across the U.S. public sector and share how TD SYNNEX helps equip partners to take advantage of the growing opportunities across the market.


As a leading distributor and solutions aggregator in both the commercial and public sector market, TD SYNNEX is well-positioned to help partners navigate the complexities within the federal, state and local government agencies, and has made a series of recent strategic investments to expand that support. One key investment is the recent addition of industry veteran Wendy Welch as Senior Vice President, Public Sector Sales. Welch has an extensive background across the channel, including at Lenovo, where she delivered back‑to‑back market share records in the U.S. Public Sector and shaped Lenovo’s North America channel strategy. In this new role, she will lead the Federal and SLED businesses, including DLT Solutions, and guide strategy, execution, and organizational development.


“The addition of Wendy reflects our continued investment in TD SYNNEX Public Sector as a strategic growth engine,” said Gary Palenbaum, EVP, Revenue & Customer Success, TD SYNNEX North America. “We are expanding our capabilities across high-growth areas like AI, security, and cloud, while strengthening the operational foundation that partners rely on, from logistics to secure supply chain execution. Wendy brings deep expertise and an ecosystem perspective that will help us further scale our impact, deepen partner engagement, and deliver consistent, accountable results across federal, state, local and education markets.”


Welch added, “Public Sector is at an inflection point, with agencies balancing rising service expectations, cybersecurity threats, and the need for responsible modernization. TD SYNNEX is uniquely positioned at the center of the ecosystem, and we are doubling down on specialization to help partners deliver durable, secure solutions at scale. I’m excited to join the team at this moment and help further strengthen how we support partners, simplify execution, and enable long-term outcomes for the communities they serve.”


This investment builds on the success of DLT achieving Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Level 2[earlier this year](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fnews.tdsynnex.com%2Fnews%2Fdlt-solutions-achieves-cmmc-level-2-certification-strengthening-secure-supply-chain-support-for-the-public-sector%2F&esheet=54534177&newsitemid=20260512914047&lan=en-US&anchor=earlier+this+year&index=2&md5=294046c190e75bb9f5dce9a0078e14f8) , demonstrating the organization’s ability to securely handle Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) through mature cybersecurity controls, formalized processes, and continuous monitoring aligned with U.S. federal government requirements.


TD SYNNEX also shared plans on expansion of their DC-area office space to better accommodate training and demo opportunities for customers in the public sector space through the DLT Innovation Center.


To learn more about the TD SYNNEX Public Sector or DLT Solutions, visit[https://www.tdsynnex.com/na/us/td-synnex-public-sector](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tdsynnex.com%2Fna%2Fus%2Ftd-synnex-public-sector&esheet=54534177&newsitemid=20260512914047&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tdsynnex.com%2Fna%2Fus%2Ftd-synnex-public-sector&index=3&md5=5f5c7f598ca8ff22753a02c148a4b46f) .
