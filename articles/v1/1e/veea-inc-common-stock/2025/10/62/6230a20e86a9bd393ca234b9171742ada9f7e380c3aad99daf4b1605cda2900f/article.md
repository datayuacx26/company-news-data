# MetaLynx: Unifying Building Connectivity and Control at the Edge

In my 20+ years of experience delivering in-building networks, the constant challenges of complexity and cost have frustrated building owners and managers. Managing all the interconnected systems within a building, from the convergence of Wi-Fi and cellular to the convergence of IT and OT, places a heavy burden on teams that were not built for it.


Too often, the response is to piece together solutions based on immediate needs or the lowest upfront cost. The result is a patchwork of disparate networks from multiple vendors, forcing staff to juggle dashboards and data sources just to understand what is happening inside their buildings.


That is why we built[MetaLynx](https://www.veea.com/solutions/metalynx) , a unified platform designed to eliminate that complexity once and for all.


MetaLynx is a hyper-converged connectivity and control solution that brings building automation, IoT networks, and state-of-the-art Wi-Fi into a single piece of hardware: the VeeaHub.


Powered by our VeeaONE platform, a single VeeaHub replaces an entire stack of traditional equipment. It acts as an IoT gateway, wireless router, and local compute node all at once. The system runs locally but can be monitored and updated from anywhere, making data control smarter, more resilient, and more cost-efficient while significantly reducing total cost of ownership.


# **Key Capabilities at the Edge**


- **Edge Intelligence**
Run building automation directly at the edge in real time to reduce latency, improve reliability, and keep operations running even without a cloud connection. *(Integration with Tridium’s Niagara Framework enables advanced building intelligence in a containerized, modern format.)*
- **AI and Machine Learning**
Deploy autonomous AI agents in secure containers and use federated learning to train models locally, keeping data private by default and enabling continuous optimization on site.
- **Universal Connectivity**
Support for 5G, Wi-Fi, LoRa, BLE, Zigbee, and Matter ensures seamless interoperability across devices and systems without vendor lock-in.
- **Built-In Security**
End-to-end Zero Trust architecture anchored in hardware-based trust domains safeguards every layer of operation, from the edge node to the cloud.

Whether you are deploying systems or managing them, MetaLynx simplifies operations across the entire building lifecycle.


For Installers and Partners


The VeeaONE platform consolidates a full stack of equipment into one device, making deployment faster, more reliable, and more cost-effective. Systems go live in a fraction of the time, reducing truck rolls and complexity while boosting profitability.


For Building Owners and Managers


Real-time monitoring optimizes energy use, enhances safety, and improves tenant comfort. The platform is future-proof by design, scaling with your needs and staying secure through remote software updates and centralized management.


With MetaLynx, the era of fragmented, patchwork systems is over. Deployments are faster, more reliable and more cost effective. Buildings become intelligent by design, from a single unified platform, saving time, money and headaches.
