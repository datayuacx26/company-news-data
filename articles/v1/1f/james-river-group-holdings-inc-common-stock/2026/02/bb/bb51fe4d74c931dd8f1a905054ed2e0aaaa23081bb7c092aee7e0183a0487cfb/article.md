# James River to Hold Its Fourth Quarter Earnings Conference Call on Tuesday, March 3, 2026

CHAPEL HILL, N.C., Feb. 04, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- James River Group Holdings, Inc. ("James River" or the "Company") (NASDAQ: JRVR) will release fourth quarter earnings after the market closes on Monday, March 2, 2026. It will also host an earnings conference call on Tuesday, March 3, 2026 beginning at 8:30 a.m. (Eastern Time).


The conference call may be accessed by dialing (800) 715-9871, conference ID 6910670, or via the investor website at[https://investors.jrvrgroup.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=HGGr0U60ucHckVv6UZE1EXzPWJja0Uvd-SvjaeF1gzcQ8q6q86Vf7fsXtj8NX0CO8Jkqw0btrndLgryUn-G2EMwGJWrxtvJtHy7Q3_ZuGFRPfE9NiIWWKcMkr8V1rcNm) . A replay will also be available in the same location.


**About James River Group Holdings, Inc.**


James River Group Holdings, Inc. is a holding company that owns and operates a group of specialty insurance companies. The Company operates in two specialty property-casualty insurance segments: Excess and Surplus Lines and Specialty Admitted Insurance. Each of the Company’s regulated insurance subsidiaries are rated “A-” (Excellent) by A.M. Best Company.
