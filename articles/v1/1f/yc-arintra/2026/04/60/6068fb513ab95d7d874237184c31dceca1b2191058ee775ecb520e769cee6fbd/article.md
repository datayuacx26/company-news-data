# Learn More arintra-receives-a-partnership-rating-in-klas-emerging-company-spotlight-report

**SAN FRANCISCO, April 23, 2026** – ****[Arintra](https://www.arintra.com/) , a GenAI-native autonomous medical coding platform for health systems and physician groups, today announced the publication of a KLAS Emerging Company Spotlight report based on customer experiences with the Arintra platform. The report validates that customers selected Arintra for its vision and transparency, saw real-world results, and committed for the long term.


The report,[Arintra Autonomous Medical Coding 2026](https://www.arintra.com/klas-emerging-spotlight-report) , is based on interviews with Arintra customers across large health systems, physician groups, and more.


Arintra received an overall performance score of 93* out of 100, with A* and A+* grades across the majority of key performance indicators, including an A+* on partnership and fair and transparent charging, and A* grades on solution capabilities and likelihood to recommend. Customers told KLAS they selected Arintra for its detailed product roadmap, vendor honesty, and the ability to grow with the solution, with customers noting that Arintra's functionality was ahead of other vendors they evaluated. 100%** of respondents reported that Arintra is part of their long-term plans and that they would purchase the solution again.


One director interviewed by KLAS spoke to the strength of the partnership: "Arintra's strength is their interactive support or their reaction to support. Their turnaround times are really good, and the quality of the outcomes is very strong. My account manager does an excellent job. They are great to work with, and they really partner with us well."


100%* of interviewed organizations have adopted direct-to-bill autonomous coding and EHR-embedded coding automation, across customers on Epic and Athena. Arintra received an A* grade on solution capabilities, with most organizations using chart-level explainability that ties coding decisions directly to clinical documentation. The majority of customers reported seeing outcomes immediately, with the remainder achieving them within six months, including revenue uplift, reduction in pre-A/R days, and identification of areas where providers were falling short on documentation.


"In healthcare, trust is non-negotiable," said Nitesh Shroff, CEO and Co-Founder of Arintra. "This emerging company spotlight validates that we’ve earned it. Customers told KLAS that they chose us for our honesty, partnership, and roadmap, and 100% say the solution is part of their long-term plans. They’re seeing real results and planning to grow with us. That’s what we’re building on as we move into the next phase.”


*“* It is always rewarding to publish insights on emerging and disruptive technologies. This report highlights how emerging AI-driven automation is beginning to meaningfully reshape medical coding. Early customer experiences with Arintra point to faster workflows, improved accuracy, and strong partnership. Arintra is demonstrating that autonomous coding isn’t just aspirational, it’s operational today. With high customer satisfaction and rapid time to value, the company is setting a new bar for what healthcare organizations should expect from AI in the revenue cycle space *.”* Everton Santos, Vice President of Client Engagement at KLAS Research


The KLAS Emerging Company Spotlight report independently validates Arintra’s growing position in the autonomous medical coding market.

## **About Arintra**


Arintra provides the industry's leading GenAI-native autonomous coding platform, enabling health systems and physician groups to get paid accurately and efficiently for the care they deliver, driving revenue assurance at scale. By combining cutting-edge AI with deep clinical expertise, Arintra autonomously codes charts with high accuracy, without human intervention. Unlike bolt-on tools, Arintra works directly within the EHR, ensuring seamless adoption with zero workflow changes or data integrity risk. Arintra holds the Epic Toolbox designation and is available on the Athena Marketplace. Health systems use Arintra to unlock missed revenue due to coding gaps, documentation issues, and unchallenged denials - achieving 5%+ uplift in revenue, 64%+ reduction in pre-A/R days, and 43%+ fewer denials.
