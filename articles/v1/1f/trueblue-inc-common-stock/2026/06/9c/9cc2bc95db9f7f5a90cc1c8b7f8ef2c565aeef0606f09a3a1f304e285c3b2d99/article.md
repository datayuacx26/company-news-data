# TrueBlue’s PeopleScout Ranks Among Top Global MSP Providers on HRO Today’s 2026 Baker’s Dozen

# TrueBlue’s PeopleScout Ranks Among Top Global MSP Providers on HRO Today’s 2026 Baker’s Dozen


June 22, 2026 8:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_5a1e30192d8c9846b564b6dbce9819e7/trueblue/news/2026-06-22_TrueBlue_s_PeopleScout_Ranks_Among_Top_Global_MSP__467.pdf)


*Client feedback drives No. 1 rankings in Breadth of Service and Size of Deal, underscoring differentiated capabilities*


TACOMA, Wash. — June 22, 2026 — TrueBlue (NYSE: TBI), a leading provider of specialized workforce solutions, today announced that its PeopleScout brand has been recognized for leadership in Managed Service Provider (MSP) solutions on HRO Today’s 2026 MSP Baker’s Dozen. PeopleScout ranked No. 3 overall and earned No. 1 rankings in both the Breadth of Service and Size of Deal categories.


The MSP Baker’s Dozen rankings are based solely on client feedback and evaluate providers across three key dimensions: breadth of service, size of deal, and quality of service.


“PeopleScout continues to distinguish itself as a leader in the MSP Baker’s Dozen rankings,” said Elliot Clark. “Their ability to deliver comprehensive workforce solutions, tailored to meet the unique needs of their clients, demonstrates a strong commitment to customer success and innovation in contingent workforce management.”


Backed by more than three decades of workforce expertise, PeopleScout’s MSP programs span the full contingent workforce lifecycle—from sourcing and engagement to supplier management, compliance and invoicing—helping organizations build agile workforce strategies that balance quality, cost, and speed.


“This recognition reflects the strength of TrueBlue’s enterprise strategy and the power of our integrated approach to workforce solutions,” said Taryn Owen, President and CEO of TrueBlue. “By combining specialized expertise, technology, and scale across our portfolio, we help organizations navigate increasing complexity and build more agile, resilient talent strategies that drive long-term performance.”


PeopleScout's MSP solutions leverage Affinix®, TrueBlue's proprietary total talent technology suite, to integrate with leading Vendor Management Systems (VMS) and give PeopleScout a unified view of program health, supplier performance, and help identify risks before they become problems. Affinix fuels dynamic workforce planning models that help organizations anticipate demand, optimize their contingent labor mix, and make faster, more confident talent decisions.


“As workforce needs continue to evolve, organizations are looking for partners that can provide both strategic guidance and operational agility,” said Rick Betori, President of PeopleScout. “We’re proud to be recognized by HRO Today and grateful to our clients for their partnership and confidence in our team.”


To learn more about PeopleScout’s MSP solutions, visit[peoplescout.com/solutions/msp/](https://peoplescout.com/solutions/msp/) .
