# Versant to Present at the 2026 Evercore Global TMT Conference

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 18, 2026-- Versant Media Group, Inc. (Nasdaq: VSNT) today announced that Anand Kini, Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer, is scheduled to present on June 2, 2026, at 9:10 a.m. PT at the 2026 Evercore Global TMT Conference in San Francisco, CA.


A live webcast of the presentation will be available on the Versant Media Investor Relations website at[investors.versantmedia.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.versantmedia.com&esheet=54537873&newsitemid=20260518735966&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.versantmedia.com&index=1&md5=0f3abab944157dc351c56b295e257f2c) . A replay of the webcast will be available on the website for a limited time following the conclusion of the presentation.
