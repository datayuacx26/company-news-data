**Social Security Documentation**


- Social Security card
- W-2 forms (serve dual purpose for income verification)
- Tax returns showing full SSN


**Proof of Legal Status** (if applicable)


- Green card
- Work visa
- Naturalization certificate

### Income Verification Documents


**For W-2 Employees:**


- Most recent 2 pay stubs (must show year-to-date earnings)
- W-2 forms from the past 2 years
- Tax returns (1040) from the past 2 years with all schedules
- Employment verification letter on company letterhead


**For Self-Employed Borrowers:**


- Tax returns (1040) from the past 2 years with all schedules
- Business tax returns (1120, 1120S, or 1065) from the past 2 years
- Profit and loss statements for the current year
- Business license and registration documents
- CPA-prepared financial statements (if available)


**For Commission or Bonus Income:**


- 2-year history of commission statements
- Employer letter confirming commission structure
- Tax returns showing consistent commission income


**Additional Income Sources:**


- Rental property income (Schedule E from tax returns)
- Social Security award letters
- Pension or retirement account statements
- Alimony or child support documentation
- Investment income statements

## Digital Upload Best Practices for 2026


### File Format and Quality Standards


**Optimal File Formats:**


- PDF (preferred for multi-page documents)
- JPEG or PNG for single-page items
- Avoid HEIC files from iPhones (convert to JPEG first)


**Quality Requirements:**


- Minimum 300 DPI resolution
- All text must be clearly readable
- No shadows or glare on documents
- Full document visible (no cut-off edges)
- Color documents in color, black and white acceptable for most items


Generative AI in mortgage processing can extract data more accurately from high-quality document scans, making proper formatting crucial for fast processing ([Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/how-generative-ai-is-changing-mortgage-process/) ).


### Security and Privacy Considerations


Chestnut’s platform uses 256-bit encryption for all document uploads, meeting or exceeding industry security standards. However, borrowers should also take precautions:


- Use secure Wi-Fi networks for uploads
- Avoid public computers or networks
- Clear browser cache after uploading sensitive documents
- Verify the website URL before entering personal information


### Mobile Upload Tips


**Smartphone Photography:**


- Use document scan apps for better quality
- Ensure adequate lighting
- Hold phone steady and parallel to document
- Take multiple shots if needed
- Review images before uploading


**Tablet Considerations:**


- Larger screen makes document review easier
- Better for multi-page PDF creation
- More stable for photographing documents

## Common Document Mistakes That Delay Approvals


### Incomplete Bank Statements


- Missing pages or partial statements
- Statements older than 60 days
- Online screenshots instead of official statements
- Redacted or blacked-out information


### Poor Image Quality


- Blurry or pixelated documents
- Shadows or glare making text unreadable
- Cropped edges cutting off important information
- Wrong orientation or upside-down images


### Missing Required Information


- Unsigned tax returns
- Pay stubs without year-to-date totals
- Insurance policies without declarations pages
- Bank statements without account numbers


### Inconsistent Information


- Name variations across documents
- Address discrepancies
- Employment information that doesn’t match
- Income figures that don’t align


AI systems excel at catching these inconsistencies quickly, but they can also flag applications for manual review when discrepancies appear ([Tidalwave](https://www.tidalwave.ai/blog/national-mortgage-news-agentic-ai-is-turning-into-the-next-big-mortgage-trend) ). Ensuring consistency across all documents helps maintain the speed advantage of digital processing.
