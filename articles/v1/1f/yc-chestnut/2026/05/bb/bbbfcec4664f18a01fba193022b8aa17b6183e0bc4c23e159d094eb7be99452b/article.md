## The Current State of Mortgage Closings: Why Speed Matters


**Mortgage Closing Cycle Time** serves as a key performance indicator (KPI) in the lending world, measuring the average number of days required to process and fund a loan ([OpsDog](https://opsdog.com/products/cycle-time-mortgage-loan-closing) ). Traditional mortgage workflows involve countless touchpoints, manual document reviews, and long approval chains that can drag out closing timelines. The closing process itself has two main phases: escrow and the final signing day. Escrow starts once the buyer and seller ink a purchase agreement ([Cross Country Mortgage](https://crosscountrymortgage.com/mortgage/resources/how-long-does-it-take-to-close-on-house/) ).


Chestnut tackles these pain points head-on by automating routine tasks, accelerating decisions, and offering real-time status updates. This tech backbone allows the company to deliver faster closings without sacrificing accuracy or compliance.
