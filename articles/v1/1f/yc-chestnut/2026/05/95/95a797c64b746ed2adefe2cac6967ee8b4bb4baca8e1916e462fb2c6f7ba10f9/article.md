### How to Use the 45-Day Rate-Shopping Window


FICO scoring models recognize legitimate rate shopping through special algorithms that bundle mortgage inquiries. With newer versions of the FICO Score, the rate-shopping window expands to 45 days, allowing borrowers to compare multiple lenders without cumulative score damage.


Chestnut’s soft-pull approach maximizes this protection by delaying hard inquiries until borrowers commit to specific loans. Traditional banks, conversely, often trigger hard inquiries immediately, starting the 45-day clock before borrowers have compared all options. This timing difference can mean the distinction between protected rate shopping and multiple score-damaging inquiries for borrowers who need extended search periods.
