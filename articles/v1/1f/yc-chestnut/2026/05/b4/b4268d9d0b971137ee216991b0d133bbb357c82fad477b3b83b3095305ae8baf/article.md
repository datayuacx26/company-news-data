## What Factors Determine Mortgage Rates for Frisco Borrowers?


Mortgage rates are shaped by forces both inside and outside your control. Understanding these factors helps you position yourself for the best possible offer.


### Macro-Level Influences


- **Treasury yields:** Mortgage rates move with[10-year Treasury bond yields](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/how-to-get-the-best-mortgage-rate/) , which reflect investor sentiment and economic trends.
- **Inflation and Fed policy:** Rates tend to rise when the outlook is for fast economic growth, higher inflation, and low unemployment.
- **Lender costs:** Each lender sets rates based on their overhead, risk appetite, and operational efficiency, which is why[rates vary from lender to lender](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/mortgages/how-are-mortgage-rates-determined) .

### 15-Year vs 30-Year: Payment vs Interest Trade-Offs


The choice between a 15-year and 30-year term involves a classic trade-off: higher monthly payments versus lower total interest.


“Over time, a 30-year mortgage is substantially more expensive than a 15-year loan.” —[Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/15-vs-30-year-mortgage/)


Term Current Avg Rate Monthly P&I on $500K Total Interest


30-year 6.21% ~$3,070 ~$605,000


15-year 5.47% ~$4,080 ~$235,000


A 15-year mortgage means larger monthly payments but a[lower interest rate](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/15-vs-30-year-mortgage/) . You build equity faster and pay your home off twice as fast. However, a 30-year mortgage offers a more affordable monthly payment and is[generally easier to qualify for](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/15-vs-30-year-mortgage/) . One hybrid strategy: take a 30-year loan and make[higher or more frequent payments](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/15-vs-30-year-mortgage/) to reduce total interest without locking yourself into the higher required payment.

## When Should You Lock—or Float—Your Rate Heading into 2026?


Timing your rate lock can feel like a high-stakes decision. Here is how to think about it.


### What Is a Rate Lock?


A mortgage rate lock keeps the interest rate on the loan from changing for a certain period of time, ensuring you[won’t pay more if rates rise](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/what-is-mortgage-rate-lock/) before you finalize the loan. Typical lock windows run[30 to 120 days](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/what-is-mortgage-rate-lock/) , with some lenders offering longer terms for new construction.


### Float-Down Options


A float-down option allows borrowers to get a lower interest rate if they have already locked their mortgage rate. “That means that if interest rates drop after you’ve locked your rate but before you’ve closed on your mortgage, you can adjust your rate to the lower one.” —[The Mortgage Reports](https://themortgagereports.com/96660/mortgage-float-down-explained)


Float-down fees typically range from[0.25% to 1%](https://themortgagereports.com/96660/mortgage-float-down-explained) of the total loan amount. Some lenders only allow you to exercise the option if rates fall by a specified margin.


### 2026 Outlook


Fannie Mae’s Economic and Strategic Research Group expects mortgage rates to end 2025 at approximately[6.3% and 2026 at 6.2%](https://www.fanniemae.com/research-and-insights/forecast/economic-developments-march-2025) . Mark Palim, Fannie Mae Senior Vice President and Chief Economist, noted, “We expect the recent pullback in mortgage rates will provide a small boost to home sales this year.” —[Fannie Mae Newsroom](https://www.fanniemae.com/newsroom/fannie-mae-news/mortgage-rates-expected-move-lower-2025-and-2026)

## Refinancing in 2025: Evaluating If a New Rate Makes Sense for Frisco Owners


With[30-year rates steady at 6.2-6.3%](https://mortgagetech.ice.com/publicdocs/mortgage/imt-december-2025-mortgage-monitor-report-ELe47663kky.pdf) in November, refinancing may make sense for homeowners who locked in at higher rates during 2023 or early 2024.


### When Refinancing Makes Sense


- **Rate-and-term refinance:** Ideal if you can drop your rate by at least 0.5-0.75 points and recoup closing costs within a few years.
- **Cash-out refinance:** Mortgage holders withdrew[$55.9 billion in equity in Q3 2025](https://mortgagetech.ice.com/publicdocs/mortgage/imt-december-2025-mortgage-monitor-report-ELe47663kky.pdf) , split between cash-out refinances and second liens—the highest quarterly total in three years.


Home affordability is at its[best levels in nearly three years](https://mortgagetech.ice.com/resources/data-reports/december-2025-mortgage-monitor) , but remains stretched. Refinance rates and mortgage purchase rates are[often the same](https://zillow.com/refinance/tx/frisco) , though refinance rates can occasionally be higher. Most lenders require a home appraisal during the refinancing process, so factor that cost into your break-even calculation.


**Key takeaway:** If you are sitting on a rate above 7% and plan to stay in your Frisco home for at least three to five years, run the numbers on a rate-and-term refinance.
