## What down-payment assistance programs can Brighton buyers use?


Colorado offers several programs that pair well with competitive mortgage rates.


The[Colorado Housing and Finance Authority (CHFA)](https://www.chfadownpaymentassistance.com/) provides down payment and closing cost assistance to qualifying residents. Eligible first-time buyers can access[up to $25,000](https://www.chfainfo.com/homeownership) in assistance through non-repayable grants or deferred second mortgage loans.


- **Grant option:** No repayment required
- **Second mortgage option:** Deferred until sale, refinance, or title transfer
- **FirstGeneration program:** Up to $25,000 for first-generation homebuyers


CHFA invested[$76.8 million in down payment assistance](https://www.chfainfo.com/homeownership) and $1.9 billion in first mortgage loans in 2024, serving over 5,291 homeowners.


### Key eligibility rules


To qualify for CHFA programs, borrowers must meet specific criteria:


- **Credit score:** Minimum[620 or higher](https://www.fha.com/first-time-home-buyers-program/colorado-housing-finance-authority-homeaccess-program) ; borrowers with no credit score may be permitted
- **Income limit:**[Statewide cap of $174,440](https://www.chfainfo.com/getattachment/4d5eaa5e-ba08-4bd2-8729-46d2d2cd1512/CHFA-HomeAccess-Program-Matrix.pdf) regardless of county or household size
- **DTI ratios:** Maximum 50% for scores 620-659; maximum 55% for scores 660 and above
- **Homebuyer education:** All borrowers must complete a CHFA-approved course before closing
- **Minimum contribution:** $500 for HomeAccess (can be a gift); $1,000 for SmartStep Plus


Eligible property types include single-family homes, PUDs, condominiums, and manufactured housing on a permanent foundation.
