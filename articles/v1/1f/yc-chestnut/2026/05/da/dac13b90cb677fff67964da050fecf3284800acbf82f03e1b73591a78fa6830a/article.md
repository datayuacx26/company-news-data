## Can a 640 credit score qualify for a conventional loan in 2026?


Yes, a 640 FICO score meets the[minimum 620 requirement](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/fha-vs-conventional-loans) for conventional conforming loans. However, qualifying and getting competitive rates are two different challenges.


### Conventional Loan Requirements at 640


Lenders evaluate multiple factors beyond credit score:


- **Minimum down payment:** Typically 5% at 640 (versus 3% for higher scores)
- **DTI limits:**[36% preferred](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/conforming-loan/) , up to 50% with compensating factors
- **Loan limits:** $832,750 in Austin for 2026
- **Reserves:** 2-6 months of payments often required


### Loan-Level Price Adjustments (LLPAs)


Your 640 score triggers significant pricing penalties through LLPAs. These risk-based fees translate directly into higher rates:

5% +1.25%


10% +1.00%


15% +0.75%

### PMI Costs at 640


Private mortgage insurance becomes expensive with lower credit scores. At 640, expect:


- **Monthly PMI:** 0.8-1.2% annually (versus 0.3-0.5% at 760+)
- **Cancellation:** Automatic at 78% LTV, or request at 80%
- **Payment protection:** None -[PMI only protects lender](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/conforming-loan/)


[Lenders prefer borrowers](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/conforming-loan/) with debt-to-income ratios of 36% or less, though they may allow up to 50% with specific compensating factors like substantial assets or stable employment history.


### The 640 Reality Check


While technically qualified, 640-score borrowers face:


- Interest rates 0.5-1.5% higher than prime borrowers
- Stricter underwriting scrutiny
- Limited lender options
- Higher total monthly payments despite lower base loan amounts

## How can you lower Austin mortgage costs before applying?


Strategic preparation can dramatically improve your mortgage terms, even with a 640 credit score. Small improvements yield outsized savings in today’s rate environment.


### Credit Score Optimization


[Paying off debts](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/what-credit-score-do-i-need-to-buy-a-house) can not only lower your DTI but also help improve your credit score, primarily by reducing your credit utilization rate. Focus on:


**Quick wins (30-60 days):**


- Pay credit cards below 30% utilization
- Dispute any errors on credit reports
- Become an authorized user on established accounts
- Avoid new credit applications


**Medium-term strategies (3-6 months):**


- Pay down installment loan balances
- Let recent inquiries age
- Establish payment history on thin files


### Debt-to-Income Reduction


[Lenders calculate DTI](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/what-credit-score-do-i-need-to-buy-a-house) by dividing monthly debt payments by gross monthly income. Target these areas:


1. **Pay off small balances:** Eliminate entire monthly payments
2. **Consolidate high-rate debt:** Reduce total monthly obligations
3. **Increase income documentation:** Include bonuses, overtime, side income
4. **Time large purchases:** Delay car loans until after closing


### Down Payment Strategies


[A higher down payment](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/what-credit-score-do-i-need-to-buy-a-house) can help make up for a lower credit score and can even qualify you for a lower interest rate:


- **5% vs 3.5%:** Reduces PMI/MIP costs
- **10% threshold:** Unlocks better conventional pricing
- **Gift funds:** Both FHA and conventional accept documented gifts
- **Down payment assistance:** Austin offers multiple programs for qualified buyers


### Austin-Specific Advantages


The local market correction creates unique opportunities:


- Negotiate seller concessions (up to 6% on FHA)
- Request rate buydowns from motivated sellers
- [Consider FHA loans](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/what-credit-score-do-i-need-to-buy-a-house) with 10% or more down for scores as low as 500
- Time purchases during slower winter months
