**My First Texas Home Program:**


This TDHCA program offers[assistance ranging from 2% to 5%](https://welcomehome.tdhca.texas.gov/uploads/My-First-Texas-Home-Program-Matrix-TMS.pdf?v=1738018341) of the total mortgage loan amount. Eligibility requirements include:


- Texas residency
- No primary residence ownership in the past three years (veterans exempt)
- Minimum 620 credit score
- Income and purchase price limits apply
- Completion of a homebuyer education course
