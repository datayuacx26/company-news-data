### Understanding Rate Components


Effective AI rate shopping requires understanding the components that influence mortgage pricing beyond the base interest rate. These include:

- **Property Type** : Single-family homes often receive better pricing than condos or investment properties


Chestnut’s AI system factors all these variables when generating quotes, but borrowers who understand these relationships can better evaluate their options and make informed decisions ([Chestnut Mortgage](https://chestnutmortgage.com/news/rates-work-get-best-one) ).
