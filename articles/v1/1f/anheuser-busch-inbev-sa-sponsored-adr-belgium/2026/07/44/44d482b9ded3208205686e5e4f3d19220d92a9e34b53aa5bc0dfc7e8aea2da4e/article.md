**About Cutwater**


Cutwater, the most awarded canned cocktail brand in the U.S., is a San Diego-based distillery known for its lineup of 20+ canned cocktail classics made from its portfolio of bottled spirits that span nearly every spirits category. **FAQs**


**1. What is Cutwater Long Island Iced Tea?**


Cutwater Long Island Iced Tea (13% ALC./VOL.) is a ready-to-drink cocktail featuring four award-winning spirits, citrus notes and a splash of cola. It delivers a perfectly mixed take on one of America's most recognizable cocktail classics.


**2. What makes Cutwater different from other canned cocktails?**


Cutwater is a distillery first. The brand uses its own award-winning spirits to create bar-quality, perfectly mixed cocktails in every can, giving consumers both convenience and quality.


**3. Why is Cutwater focusing on hosting and entertaining?**


Cutwater believes hosting should be effortless


and enjoyable. Its portfolio of perfectly mixed cocktails is designed to help consumers spend less time mixing drinks and more time enjoying the occasion with friends and family.


**4. Where is Cutwater made?**


Cutwater is a San Diego-based distillery that operates a 50,000-square-foot distillery and production facility, as well as the Cutwater Tasting Room & Kitchen.


**5. How large is the Cutwater portfolio?**


Cutwater offers more than 20 canned cocktail varieties spanning nearly every major spirits category, bringing classic cocktail experiences into a convenient ready-to-drink format.


**6. How widely available is Cutwater?**


Cutwater products are distributed nationwide and available in all 50 states
