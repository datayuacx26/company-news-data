**ABOUT NATURAL LIGHT**


Natural Light was introduced in 1977 as Anheuser-Busch’s first reduced-calorie light beer. It’s brewed with a blend of premium hops and a combination of malt and corn, producing a clean flavor, light body and satisfying refreshment. In 2023, Natural Light introduced a new, permanent can nationwide, a vintage, yet sleek look and feel that’s inspired by enjoying life’s simple joys.
