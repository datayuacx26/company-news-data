# Cutwater Strengthens Anheuser-Busch's Position in Spirits as Brand Continues Rapid Growth

***Key Facts***


- - Cutwater has more than doubled in size in 2026, growing 104% year-over-year *
- - Cutwater is the #1 fastest-growing brand across Total Alcohol (Spirits, Hard Beverage, Beer and Wine) *
- - Cutwater is the #1 spirits-based cocktail brand, with nine of the top 10 spirits-based cocktail SKUs and sales more than 4.5x larger than the next competitor *
- - Cutwater delivers approximately 75% of spirits-based cocktail category dollar growth *
- - Cutwater is leading Anheuser-Busch's emerging leadership in the spirits category *
- - Cutwater will air a national commercial during the big game in February 2027


**SAN DIEGO, CA – August 6, 2026** – Driven by strong consumer demand, consistent execution, and increased investment, Cutwater has emerged as the #1 fastest-growing brand in the total U.S. beverage alcohol industry (Spirits, Hard Beverage, Beer and Wine).* The brand has more than doubled in size in 2026, growing 104% year-over-year.* As consumers increasingly seek premium spirits-based options across a wider range of occasions, Cutwater is leading Anheuser-Busch's spirits portfolio.


Today, Cutwater is the largest spirits-based cocktail brand in the United States, more than 4.5 times larger than its nearest competitor, with nine of the top 10 spirits-based cocktail SKUs and approximately 75% of all dollar sales growth within the spirits-based cocktail category. * The brand continues to capture spirits occasions and fuel Anheuser-Busch's emerging leadership in the spirits category.


### **Investing in Long-Term Growth**


Cutwater continues to drive incremental growth across the Anheuser-Busch portfolio through spirits-based occasions and to continue fueling that momentum, A-B is expanding its investment across marketing, innovation, partnerships and operations including:


- - Expanded national media support with 40% more media investment during key summer months
- - Partnerships across major sports properties including MLB, PGA TOUR, NFL teams and NBA teams
- - A robust innovation pipeline featuring new cocktails, flavor extensions and variety packs
- - Expanded production capacity and investments across multiple brewing and production facilities to support accelerating consumer demand
- - Cutwater will be featured during the big game in February 2027 with a national commercial, bringing one of the fastest-growing brands in alcohol to one of the biggest stages in sports and entertainment.


**Jake Kirsch, President, Beyond Beer at Anheuser-Busch** said: "Cutwater's momentum is helping us reach consumers in more occasions and solidify A-B's leadership in the broader beverage alcohol landscape. As demand continues to grow, we're investing behind the brand in a major way, from innovation and expanded production capacity to national media, sports partnerships and cultural moments like the big game. Cutwater is a remarkable brand that consumers love: it's an important part of how we're building our spirits business, complementing our leadership in beer, and driving long-term growth."


### **Momentum Across the Portfolio**


Cutwater's growth is being driven by a portfolio of category-leading cocktails. Among the brand's top performers:


- - Lime Margarita 4-pack is the #1 largest and fastest-growing spirits-based cocktail SKU, +87% *
- - Mango Margarita 4-pack is the #2 spirits-based cocktail SKU, +135% *
- - The Long Island Iced Tea 4-pack ranks #4 among the category, +119% *
- - Lemon Drop Martini 4-pack has become the #2 fastest-growing spirits-based cocktail SKU *
- - Espresso Martini (+76%), White Russian (+87%) and Mai Tai (+57%) continue to post strong double-digit growth*
- - New in 2026, Banana Mudslide became the #4 new SKU in spirits-based cocktails *


The company's investment extends beyond the shelf. At Cutwater's San Diego tasting room and kitchen, guest counts are up +24%, foot traffic has increased +25%, and net revenue has grown +28% versus last year. **
