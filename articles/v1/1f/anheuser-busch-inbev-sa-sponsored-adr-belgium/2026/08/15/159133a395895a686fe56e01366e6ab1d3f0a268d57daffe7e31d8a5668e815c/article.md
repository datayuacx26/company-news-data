# Anheuser-Busch was the #1 Fastest Growing Alcohol Supplier Throughout FIFA World Cup 2026™

***Key Facts***


- · *Anheuser-Busch was the #1 fastest-growing alcohol supplier during the World Cup* *1*
- · *Michelob ULTRA grew 12.4% total volume across U.S. World Cup host cities* *2* **
- · *Stella Artois was a top 5 share gainer at bars and restaurants across the country during the tournament* 3
- · *NUTRL grew 21.5% in host cities during the tournament* 5


**ST. LOUIS, MO – August 12, 2026** – Over the six weeks of the FIFA World Cup 2026™ this summer, **Anheuser-Busch held the position of #1 fastest-growing alcohol supplier during the tournament.** 1 ****


America’s #1 top-selling and fastest-growing beer **Michelob ULTRA** outperformed the industry as the “[Superior Player of the Match](https://www.anheuser-busch.com/newsroom/MichelobULTRAChiefTrophyOfficer) ” throughout the tournament:


- · Michelob ULTRA grew **12.4% total volume across U.S. host cities** , including **+33% growth in Boston** and + **32% growth in Miami.** 2
- · Michelob ULTRA **gained 2.62 volume share points** **on draft,** and **1.61 share points in package** , further solidifying its position as the #1 beer in bars and restaurants across the country.3 ******
- · Leading non-alcohol beer Michelob ULTRA Zero was a breakout performer, ranking as **the fastest-growing brand (+49%)** among ALL top 50 beer families,2 and nearly tripling its volume share in bars and restaurants. 3
- ·[Michelob ULTRA was honored with its own “Golden Boot” for being the most-ordered beer on DoorDash across the full tournament](https://stacker.com/stories/food-drink/futbol-fever-beer-choices-team-merch-doordash-data-reveals-how-fans-enjoyed) , leading beer orders across 13 states, including Texas, Florida, Arizona, Louisiana and Tennessee.4


**Stella Artois** was another key contributor throughout the World Cup:


- · During the tournament, Stella Artois was a **top 5 share gainer** at bars and restaurants across the country, gaining +35% in draft volume in June alone.3
- · Stella Artois also saw growth in key host cities, with standouts like Atlanta (13%) and Miami (7%).2


**NUTRL** brought great vibes and kept it tasty at matchday moments, **growing 21.5% in host cities** and **9.7% overall** during the tournament.5


**Kyle Norrington, Chief Commercial Officer, Anheuser-Busch** said: *“Anheuser-Busch's momentum is driven by a consumer-first strategy rooted in focus, consistency and connecting fans with our brands during high-impact moments, such as the FIFA World Cup, which proved to be a growth driver for the beer industry. Our performance underscores a simple truth: when we connect with consumers authentically during the moments that matter most, we can drive meaningful momentum for our brands and for the industry.”*
