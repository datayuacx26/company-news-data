# Anheuser-Busch is the #1 Fastest-Growing Spirits Company with the #1 Fastest-Growing RTD Portfolio

Following its Q1 2026 earnings update, America’s top brewer Anheuser-Busch continued to grow market share across beer, wine and spirits outperforming the overall U.S. alcohol industry and strengthening its position as the #1 fastest-growing spirits company.


As the #3 Hard Beverage company in the U.S., Anheuser-Busch's success is driven by the #1 fastest-growing Wine & Spirits RTD portfolio, which includes three of the top 10 fastest-growing RTD brands by dollar sales: Cutwater (#1), BeatBox (#5) and NÜTRL (#8).


Some fast facts:


- - Cutwater holds its position as the #1 spirits-based cocktail, with sales up 120.9% so far this year, and is now more than five times the size of its nearest spirits-based cocktail competitor.
- - Cutwater is also the #1 dollar‑share gainer in Spirits and the #2 fastest‑growing brand in the total alcohol industry.
- - BeatBox is the #4 RTD brand and the #5 fastest-growing, having grown nearly 50% in 2025.
- - NÜTRL is now the #4 seltzer brand in the U.S. and the only top five brand growing double digits (+21.9% YTD).


**Jake Kirsch, President, Beyond Beer at Anheuser-Busch** said: “Anheuser-Busch’s momentum is driven by a consumer-first strategy rooted in focus, consistency, and connecting fans with our brands across a wide range of occasions. We’re proud to have two of the top five fastest growing RTD brands by dollar sales - Cutwater (#1) and BeatBox (#5), while NÜTRL continues to stand out as the only top five seltzer brand growing double digits. This year is off to an incredible start for our Beyond Beer portfolio, and we’re just getting started.”
