# Anheuser-Busch Delivered More Than 100,000 Cans of Emergency Drinking Water to U.S. Territories in the South and West Pacific Following Typhoon

**May 29, 2026 – St. Louis, MO –** At the request of the American Red Cross, Anheuser-Busch delivered more than 100,000 cans of emergency drinking water to the U.S. territories of Guam, American Samoa, and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands to support residents and first responders in recovery efforts following Typhoon Sinlaku, which heavily impacted the region.


The donation of emergency drinking water was shipped to Anheuser-Busch wholesaler partner Ambros Inc., serving U.S. territories in the region, which partnered with the American Red Cross to distribute the delivery to community members and organizations in need.


“The American Red Cross is deeply grateful to Anheuser-Busch and its wholesaler partners for their generous donation and delivery of safe drinking water to Guam, American Samoa, and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands in response to Typhoon Sinlaku,” **said Lauren Ulrich, Executive Director of Operations Management and Logistics for the American Red Cross** . “We appreciate their partnership and ongoing support of our mission.”


The 100,000+ cans of water were sourced from Anheuser-Busch's brewery in Cartersville, Georgia, which alongside the Fort Collins, Colorado brewery, periodically pauses beer production to can emergency drinking water and help communities in times of disaster.


Since the inception of the emergency drinking water program in 1988, Anheuser-Busch and its wholesaler partners have donated more than 100 million cans of clean, safe drinking water to U.S. communities and volunteer fire departments in support of ongoing disaster relief and preparedness efforts.
