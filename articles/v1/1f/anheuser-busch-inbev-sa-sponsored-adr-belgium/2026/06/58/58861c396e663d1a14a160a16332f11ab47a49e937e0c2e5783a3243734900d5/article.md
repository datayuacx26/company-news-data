# Budweiser is Covering the Tab for America's 250th Birthday

**Key Facts:**


•


Budweiser is raising a beer to Americans across the country by covering America’s tab for the Fourth of July, offering 21+ Americans a digital reward to enjoy a Budweiser in celebration of America’s 250th birthday, until the tab runs out.


•


21+ Americans can visit us.budweiser.com/americatab, enter their information, and if verified, they’ll receive a $6 digital gift card to purchase a draught beer on Budweiser to celebrate the Fourth of July, while supplies last.


•


Budweiser is also celebrating the Fourth of July through longstanding support of Folds of Honor and limited-edition patriotic summer packaging that honors the people and moments that bring Americans together.


**St. Louis, MO** **–** **June 29, 2026** **–** **** This summer, Budweiser is raising a beer to Americans across the country by covering America’s tab for the Fourth of July, in celebration of America’s 250th birthday. As part of the program, 21+ Americans can enjoy a Budweiser through a digital reward, until the tab runs out.*


From backyard barbecues and summer concerts to ballgames and holiday weekends, Budweiser has been part of the moments that bring Americans together for the last 150 years. Now, as the country celebrates its 250th birthday, Budweiser is saying thank you with a beer on them.


Beginning June 29, 21+ Americans can visit[us.budweiser.com/americatab](https://us.budweiser.com/americatab) , enter their information, and if verified, they’ll receive a $6 digital gift card to purchase a draught beer on Budweiser to celebrate the Fourth of July, while supplies last.


**Todd Allen, SVP of Marketing for Budweiser at Anheuser-Busch** said: “For years, Budweiser has shown up for the moments that bring Americans together. This summer, as America celebrates its 250th birthday, we wanted to do more than mark an anniversary; we wanted to honor the people who make those moments possible. Covering America’s tab is our way of turning gratitude into action: to thank the hardworking people who’ve made Budweiser part of everyday life, and to give them a chance to share a beer, reconnect and celebrate the resilience and spirit that have defined this country for generations.”


In addition, Budweiser is celebrating the Fourth of July through longstanding[support of Folds of Honor](https://www.anheuser-busch.com/newsroom/Budweiser-150th-Anniversary-American-Summer-Campaign) and limited-edition patriotic summer packaging that honors the people and moments that bring Americans together.


As part of Budweiser’s “American Beers for American Heroes” program, a portion of proceeds from all Budweiser sold in bars and restaurants, up to $1.5 million**, will support Folds of Honor’s mission of providing educational scholarships to the families of fallen or disabled military service members and first responders. Together, Anheuser-Busch, its industry leading brands and dedicated wholesaler partners, have proudly donated $37 million, helping fund 7,400 educational scholarships for Folds of Honor recipients over the last 16 years.


Budweiser will continue its nationwide summer celebration through major cultural moments and the Folds of Honor tour, with appearances planned throughout Fourth of July weekend, including:


• Chicago Cubs vs St. Louis Cardinals at Wrigley Field


• New York Times Square Ball Drop


• Los Angeles 250 Block Party


To learn more about Budweiser’s summer celebrations and America’s Tab, visit us.budweiser.com.
