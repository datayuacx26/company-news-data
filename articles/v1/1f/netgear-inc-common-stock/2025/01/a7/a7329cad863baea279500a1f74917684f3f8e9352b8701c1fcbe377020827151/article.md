# WiFi 7 Networking - Orbi 870 Mesh System Upgrade

**NETGEAR Expands Award-Winning WiFi 7 Home Networking Portfolio with Orbi 870 Series** **Mesh System**


*New Orbi provides uncompromising WiFi performance for large homes that require multi-gig speeds and advanced security features*


**San Jose, California – January 7, 2025** – NETGEAR®, Inc. (NASDAQ: NTGR), a leading provider of innovative, security-driven solutions for people to connect and manage their digital lives, today announced the launch of the Orbi 870 Series Mesh System – the latest addition to its growing portfolio of mesh networking systems delivering the enhanced power of WiFi 7 to homes of all sizes.


Building on nearly 30 years of WiFi innovation expertise, NETGEAR designed the Orbi 870 series to deliver high-performance mesh networking powered by WiFi 7. Created to meet the needs of today’s connected homes, the Orbi 870 ensures exceptional speeds, reduced latency and ample capacity for even the most demanding households. Whether streaming ultra-high-definition content, working remotely, or gaming online, families can rely on Orbi 870’s advanced WiFi technology to support a smooth experience across up to 150 connected devices. Meanwhile, Advanced Router Protection, only available from NETGEAR, helps ensure router security out of the box with advanced safety features and automatic security updates designed to help protect routers against cyberattacks and other security threats.

## **Orbi 870 Technical Specifications:** ****


- **Ultra-Fast WiFi 7 Tri-Band Speeds up to 21 Gbps\[1\]** – Experience combined Multi-Gigabit speeds up to 21 Gbps with 2.4GHz, 5GHz, and 6GHz bands.
- **Higher Capacity & Robust WiFi** – Supports WiFi 7 technology enhancements like 320 MHz, 4K QAM, Multi-Link Operation (MLO) and preamble puncturing.
- **Multi-link Operation** – Combines two WiFi bands at once, for both Enhanced Backhaul (shared 5GHz & 6GHz) and fronthaul (5GHz & 6 GHz) to improve network reliability, reduce latency, and ensure data is delivered with maximum speed.
- **Multi-Gig Speeds with 10 Gig Internet Port** – Use the 10 Gig internet port for multi-gig cable and fiber plans. Compatible with any internet service provider.
- **More Multi-gig Wired ports on Router and Satellites** – Plug in wired devices to improve performance or setup a multi-gig wired connection between the router and satellites via four 2.5Gbps Ethernet ports on the router, and four 2.5Gbps Ethernet ports on the satellites.
- **Do It All with the Orbi® App** –Easily manage your network from anywhere, including pausing the Internet, running Internet speed tests, tracking your Internet data usage, setting up separate guest and IoT networks, and more.


#### **Security Made Simple**


Powered by state-of-the-art encryption protocols and advanced threat detection mechanisms, NETGEAR routers stand as the first line of defense against evolving cyber threats and include key security features such as automatic firmware updates, VPN support, cutting-edge WPA3 security, access control, and guest and IoT WiFi network options.


The Orbi 870 series also includes NETGEAR’s exclusive Advanced Router Protection feature. This feature helps protect the router from threats using a combination of AI heuristics and advanced exploit prevention to help recognize and block exploitation attempts. Additionally, it enables the ability to quickly deploy live patches for threats, bypassing the need to wait for a new firmware release. This rapid response mechanism significantly reduces the time it takes to address security issues, reinforcing NETGEAR’s position as a leader in delivering cutting-edge security across its router and mesh product portfolio.


For added protection, the Orbi 870 series includes a 30-day trial of[NETGEAR Armor™ Powered by Bitdefender](https://www.netgear.com/home/services/armor/) ™, which provides an additional automatic shield of security for connected devices and added privacy with VPN. Unlike traditional endpoint antivirus products, Armor is available on the router as an even further enhanced security solution, helping to protect computers, security cameras, baby monitors and other IoT devices on the network.


The 870 series also comes with[NETGEAR Smart Parental Controls™](https://www.netgear.com/home/services/smart-parental-controls/) to easily manage kids’ time online across their connected devices and promote good online habits for the family. Basic features are available free of charge. A subscription fee applies for a Premium Plan.
