# NETGEAR Expands Pro Av Managed Switch Portfolio With Four New Models At InfoComm 2025 Booth 4401

## New M4350 switches simplify AV network deployments for Pro AV and broadcast applications


June 4, 2025 – SAN JOSE, Calif. – NETGEAR®, Inc. (NASDAQ: NTGR), a global leader in intelligent networking solutions designed to power extraordinary experiences partnering with approximately 450 manufacturers, is expanding its M4350 managed switch portfolio with four new models. These new products, designed specifically for Pro AV and broadcast applications, will be revealed at InfoComm 2025 booth 4401, taking place June 11-13 at the Orange County Convention Center in Orlando, FL.


### Expanded M4350 Portfolio for Pro AV and Broadcast


The four new M4350 switches will address specific needs within the Pro AV market, particularly for live event and broadcast applications. All four models feature advanced capabilities for any AV over IP installation:


· Multi-color LED Port Identification: A novel feature that displays user-selected colors physically on each port, matching colors assigned in the NETGEAR AV OS™. This innovation allows technicians to easily identify which cables should be connected to specific ports by finding the corresponding LED color. This significantly simplifies deployment, reducing setup time, and decreasing the possibility for error.


· Enhanced SMPTE ST 2110 Support: All new models include built-in grandmaster clock and boundary clock functionality, critical for SMPTE ST 2110 broadcast applications while maintaining transparent clock capabilities across the entire M4350 and M4250 switch lines. As a result, the new switches can serve as the primary timing source for a broadcast network.


· Secure Power Connection: A new mechanism to secure power cables to the chassis, preventing accidental disconnections during critical live events.


· TAA Compliancy: The new models all comply with the strict government policy regarding the Trade Agreement Act to ensure products originate from approved countries.


“These new additions to our M4350 line demonstrate NETGEAR AV’s ongoing commitment to addressing the specific needs of Pro AV and broadcast professionals,” says Laurent Masia, Senior Director of Product Line Management for Managed Switches at NETGEAR. “The enhanced SMPTE ST 2110 support and innovative features like multi-


color LED port identification make these switches ideal for demanding applications where reliability and ease of deployment are essential.”


### Product Availability


The new M4350 Phase 3 switches will be officially launched during InfoComm in June, with availability expected in July 2025. Models include:


· M4350-8M2V (MSM4310): 8x 2.5G ports with 4x 25G uplinks, half width


· M4350-24M4X4V (MSM4332): 24x 2.5G and 4x 10G/Multi-gig PoE++ ports with 4 25G SFP28 uplinks


· M4350-24F4X (MSM4328F): 24x 1G/2.5G SFP ports with 4x10G Combo Copper / SFP+ ports


· M4350-40F4C (XSM4344FC): 40x 10G SFP+ ports with 4x 100G QSFP28 uplinks


These new models complement NETGEAR’s existing M4350 portfolio while providing expanded options for different network requirements. For US government customers, there are separate SKUs available for each of these new switches that meet TAA requirements. NETGEAR is organizing an InfoComm Sneak Preview on June 4.
