No sponsored placements. No pay-to-play. Just direct feedback from implementation managers, customer success teams, and onboarding specialists who shared their honest experience with the platform.

#### **What Is G2, and Why Do These Badges Matter?**


G2 is the world's largest software review marketplace, used by millions of buyers, including procurement teams at Fortune 500 companies, to evaluate software based on peer reviews rather than vendor marketing.


A G2 badge isn't purchased or negotiated. It's earned through aggregated ratings from verified users, which is precisely what makes it a credible signal for software buyers. If you're currently evaluating customer onboarding software, these badges reflect the experiences of professionals solving the same challenges you face.


#### **What Each Badge Tells You About Onboard.io**


##### **High Performer**


This badge reflects consistent, measurable results. Customers reported reduced time-to-value, better project visibility, and fewer onboarding delays. It speaks to outcomes, not just features.


##### **Highest User Adoption**


A tool only delivers ROI if your team... and your customers actually use it. Onboard.io earned this badge by fitting naturally into existing workflows without a steep learning curve. Higher adoption means faster time-to-value and stronger returns on your onboarding investment.


##### **Easiest to Use**


Both internal teams and external clients need to navigate the platform with minimal friction. Our interface is designed to be clean and intuitive, tasks get completed, progress gets tracked, and onboarding doesn't stall because someone can't figure out where to go.


##### **Easiest to Do Business With**


This badge reflects the full buying and contracting experience — transparent pricing, flexible agreements, and a team that operates as a genuine partner throughout the relationship.


##### **Easiest Setup**


Onboard.io is designed to work out of the box. That means you can move from signed contract to actively onboarding customers faster than you would with general-purpose project management tools that require significant configuration before they're useful.


##### **Best Support**


Our support team is consistently recognized for being responsive, knowledgeable, and solutions-focused, going beyond documentation to ensure customers get real outcomes from the platform.


#### **What Customer Feedback Is Telling Us**


Each badge represents a pattern of consistent feedback from onboarding professionals at B2B SaaS companies. A few themes that surface repeatedly:


-


Teams have **one centralized place** to track onboarding across all customers, replacing disconnected spreadsheets and project tools


-


Customers value real-time visibility into their own onboarding progress, which reduces status requests and accelerates time to launch


-


Onboarding managers are able to scale operations without proportionally increasing headcount, because the platform handles the coordination work


#### **Reviews Inform Our Roadmap**


Every G2 review helps us understand where we're delivering value and where we can improve. That feedback directly shapes our product decisions and ensures we stay focused on what matters most to onboarding teams.


If you've used Onboard.io and haven't yet shared your experience on G2, we'd appreciate hearing from you. Your review helps other onboarding professionals make more informed software decisions.
