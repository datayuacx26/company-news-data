# Onboard.io Now Supports MCP: Connect Your Onboarding Data to AI Agents

Onboard.io now supports the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** , the open standard that lets AI agents like Claude and ChatGPT connect directly to your onboarding data, managing and updatingreading projects, drafting client emails, scheduling meetings, and flagging at-risk accounts, all inside the AI tools your team already uses.


### **The hardest part of onboarding isn't the work. It's the busywork around it.**


Customer Success teams don't lose time because onboarding is hard. They lose it to the overhead around it, the reporting, the cross-checking, and the constant reassembling of context that no single tool hands you ready-made. The answer to a simple question, like which clients need attention today, is rarely in one place. It's spread across your onboarding platform, your CRM, your inbox, and your calendar.


Multiply that by a portfolio of 30, 40 or more accounts being onboarded and a real share of the day goes to stitching systems together by hand instead of spending time with the clients themselves.


The costs are predictable:


-


**Manual reporting.** A weekly status rollup or executive scorecard means exporting from several systems and stitching them together by hand.


-


**Slow follow-through.** You know a client is slipping. Turning that into a sent email, a booked call, and a logged note still takes a dozen clicks across a dozen tabs.


-


**Scattered context.** The full picture of a client is spread across your CRM, inbox, calendar, and files. Assembling it for even one account takes minutes you repeat dozens of times a day.


The information needed to move faster already exists. It's just spread across tools that don't talk to each other. That's the gap MCP closes, across your platforms.


### **What is the Onboard.io MCP integration?**


The Onboard.io MCP integration lets any MCP-compatible AI tool securely connect to your Onboard.io account and work with your live onboarding data, managing projects, tasks, phases, and client status, and taking actions like posting updates or drafting communications on your behalf.


####
**What is MCP?**


The Model Context Protocol is an open standard[introduced by Anthropic in November 2024](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol) that lets AI tools securely connect to external software and data sources. Think of it as a universal plug that lets an AI tool like Claude communicate with your business applications, reading data, taking actions, and working across platforms the way a skilled human assistant would.


Because MCP is an open standard with a[publicly available specification](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/) , the integration isn't tied to a single AI vendor. It works with any MCP-compatible tool. Throughout this post we use Claude and ChatGPT as the examples, since they offer native connector support you can set up from their settings panel without developer configuration, but the same integration works with other MCP-enabled platforms, and more are adopting the standard over time.


### **What your team can do with it**


Once Onboard.io is connected alongside your other tools, the AI you already use, like Claude, can complete work in seconds that used to take 20 to 45 minutes of manual assembly:


-


**Help manage your onboarding projects** within[Onboard.io](https://onboard.io/) - the MCP is tooled to support frequent activities (e.g. updating tasks), which can be done with your approval.


-


**Generate a portfolio status report** across every active onboarding project, with completion percentage and current phase.


-


**Draft personalized client emails** using real contact names, tasks, and go-live dates, not generic templates.


-


**Surface at-risk projects** that are stalled or behind schedule, with the business impact and a recommended next step.


-


**Build a client health scorecard** that merges onboarding progress with deal values from your CRM into a single Green/Yellow/Red view.


-


**Schedule milestone meetings** based on each project's phase, with a video link and a tailored agenda.


### **Ask questions that span your systems**


Onboard.io already automates the repeatable parts of onboarding: creating projects, managing document signing and forms, sending welcome sequences, scheduling milestones, keeping your CRM in sync. Those run on their own. What no automation can anticipate is the judgment call that depends on data from several systems at once.


That's where the AI you already use fits. Because a tool like Claude can read your live Onboard.io data alongside your CRM and inbox, it answers questions that would otherwise mean opening three tools and piecing the answer together by hand. You type something like:


> *For each of my onboardings, pull the deal value from the CRM, the completion status from Onboard, and the sentiment of the last few client emails, then tell me which accounts are the biggest risk to revenue.*


The answer doesn't live in any one place, so the AI pulls it together across your connected tools in one pass:


-


**Reads completion status from Onboard.io,** where each active project stands and what's overdue or blocked.


-


**Pulls deal value from your CRM,** how much revenue each account represents.


-


**Reads the sentiment of recent client emails,** which relationships feel strained, hesitant, or quiet, regardless of what the project status says.


-


**Weighs the three together** and hands you a ranked list of the accounts where revenue is most at risk, with the reason for each.


That's the case a person would otherwise catch only by luck, or by spending an afternoon cross-checking systems account by account. The AI does it in seconds.


### **Bringing it together**


Onboard.io keeps running the onboarding work you rely on: creating projects, sending sequences, tracking milestones, and syncing your CRM. Our MCP adds a layer on top, so the AI tools your team already uses, like Claude and ChatGPT, can read your live onboarding data alongside your other systems and answer the cross-system questions that no dashboard or automation was built for.
