# Phillips 66 Aviation Fuel Brand and Branded FBOs Receive Recognition in 2026 Professional Pilot PRASE Survey

Phillips 66® Aviation proudly announces the Phillips 66 branded FBOs that were recognized in the 2026 Professional Pilot PRASE[Survey](https://propilotmag.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/PRASE_2026-NewsRelease.pdf) . Additionally, Phillips 66® Aviation was once again ranked as one of the top Best Fuel Brands. In this year’s survey, three Phillips 66 branded FBOs were named among the prestigious list of the best U.S. FBOs, with additional recognition across the best small FBO chain and best large FBO chain categories.


“We’re proud to see our branded FBO network recognized in this year’s PRASE Survey,” said Jennifer Claunch, Manager, Aviation Sales, Phillips 66. “This recognition speaks to the performance of our FBOs and the level of service they deliver every day across the aviation community.”


As the only refiner in the branded aviation space, Phillips 66® Aviation brings a fully integrated fuel supply system built for reliability and scale. This refinery-backed infrastructure provides greater visibility and control across the supply chain, supporting consistent fuel availability across its branded network. This approach helps enable the level of service and operational consistency reflected in this year’s survey results.


The 53rd annual survey ranked both national and international FBOs for overall excellence. *Professional Pilot* subscribers scored a total of 1,150 U.S. FBO selections from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent) in six categories: line team, customer service representatives (CSRs), facility, amenities, promptness and efficiency, and value for price. The annual *Professional Pilot* PRASE (Preferences Regarding Aviation Services and Equipment) Survey is an annual tabulation of customer opinions of aviation ground services. Executives in charge of flight departments, aviation managers, chief pilots, CEOs, and other qualified subscribers to *Professional Pilot* magazine are polled once a year in order to determine the PRASE Winners’ List. *Pro Pilot’* s PRASE is the gold standard of aviation ground service leaders.
