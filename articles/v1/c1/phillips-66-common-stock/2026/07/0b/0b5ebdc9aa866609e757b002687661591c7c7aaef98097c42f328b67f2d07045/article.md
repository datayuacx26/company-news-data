# Phillips 66 Aviation Fuels & Lubricants Set Course for EAA AirVenture Oshkosh 2026

*Show-only specials, expert sessions and AirVenture traditions await at the world’s largest fly-in convention*


**HOUSTON –** Phillips 66® Aviation and Phillips 66® Lubricants are headed to EAA AirVenture Oshkosh 2026 with show-only specials, technical expertise and fan-favorite traditions for one of aviation’s most anticipated weeks. From July 20–26 at Wittman Regional Airport (KOSH), AirVenture is expected to welcome 700,000 visitors from 94 countries, with more than 800 exhibitors on the grounds and nearly 10,000 aircraft taking to the skies.


“Every year, AirVenture brings together the pilots, owners, mechanics and enthusiasts who keep aviation moving,” said Jennifer Claunch, Manager, Aviation Sales, Phillips 66. “From reconnecting with longtime customers to meeting new faces and celebrating the Young Eagles Leadership Award recipient, it’s one of the most energizing weeks on the aviation calendar, and we’re proud to be part of it.”


Phillips 66® Aviation Fuels & Lubricants will be located at tents #430 and #431. Attendees can connect with the brand throughout the week through the following opportunities.


### **Celebrate This Year’s Phillips 66 EAA Young Eagles Leadership Award Recipient**


- Presented by Phillips 66® Aviation, this annual award honors a volunteer or organization that has made an extraordinary impact on the Young Eagles program. This year’s recipient of the Phillips 66 EAA Young Eagles Leadership Award will be announced Tuesday, July 21, at 5 p.m. CT during the Young Eagles Volunteer Banquet at EAA Founders’ Wing. *This event is invite-only, and pre-registration is required.


### **Join Lively and Engaging Educational Sessions Presented by Phillips 66 Representatives**


- **Topic: Bob Hoover: Perfecting Flight**


- Speaker: Keith Clark, Senior Specialist, QC and Technical Support, Phillips 66® Aviation
- Thursday, July 23 at 8:30 a.m. CT (Location: EAA Museum Skyscape Theater)


- **Topic: Aviation Oil: Dispelling the Myths**


- Speaker: Molly Merritt, Specialist, Technical Services, Phillips 66® Lubricants
- Friday, July 24 at 11:30 a.m. CT (Location: Workshop Classroom B)


### **Cool Off at the Phillips 66® Aviation Annual Popsicle Happy Hour, An AirVenture Favorite**


- Stop by tents #430 and #431 Monday, July 20, through Thursday, July 23, at 1:30 p.m. for a complimentary popsicle and a chance to connect with Phillips 66 representatives and fellow aviation enthusiasts. Popsicles available while supplies last.


### **Hear from Phillips 66® Aviation & Lubricants on EAA Radio**


- Phillips 66® Aviation Fuels & Lubricants guests will join EAA Radio throughout the week. Keith Clark will share perspectives on aviation safety and quality control, Molly Merritt will discuss lubricants, and the Young Eagles Leadership Award recipient will talk about their aviation journey and passion for the program. Tune in for the latest conversations on aviation fuels and lubricants.


- Keith Clark’s Interview: Monday, July 20 at 11:30 a.m.
- Young Eagles Leadership Award Winner Interview: Wednesday, July 22 at 11:45 a.m.
- Molly Merritt’s interview: Friday, July 24 at 9 a.m.
