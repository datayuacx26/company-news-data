# Western Gateway reaches final investment decision

Western Gateway has reached a final investment decision, advancing a project designed to create a new fuel supply path to western markets and strengthen long-term reliability, flexibility and resilience across the region.


**Summary**


- Phillips 66, Kinder Morgan and HF Sinclair are advancing the project with a focus on safe, disciplined execution.
- Western Gateway will create a new refined products supply path from St. Louis, Missouri, and expanded Gulf Coast origin points to markets, including Phoenix, Arizona and California.
- The project combines new and existing pipeline assets to improve supply flexibility, strengthen logistics and support long-term fuel reliability across the West.


---


The[Western Gateway Pipeline](https://investor.phillips66.com/financial-information/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Phillips-66-Kinder-Morgan-and-HF-Sinclair-Announce-Final-Investment-Decision-for-Western-Gateway-Pipeline/default.aspx) has reached final investment decision, moving the project from planning into execution and marking a major milestone for a system designed to strengthen fuel supply reliability across the western United States.


**Why it matters**


For Phillips 66, the milestone reflects more than progress on a pipeline project. It represents a long-term investment in the infrastructure needed to connect American-made fuel with growing demand, expand market flexibility and support the communities and customers that depend on reliable access to transportation fuels.

**A new path to the West**


Western Gateway is a proposed refined products pipeline system that will create a new supply path from St. Louis, Missouri, and expanded Gulf Coast origin points to western markets, including Phoenix, Arizona and California.


**The details**


The project combines an approximately 900-mile new build pipeline from Borger, Texas, to Phoenix with Kinder Morgan’s existing SFPP West Line from Phoenix to Colton, California, which will be reversed to move product west into California. Phillips 66’s Gold Pipeline will also be reversed to help move refined products toward Borger and supply the system.


The system is designed with an initial capacity of 230,000 barrels per day. It is also being developed to allow for future expansion as demand requires, with limited additional capital and without the need for a new pipeline.


Phillips 66 will construct and operate the new pipeline segment from Borger to Phoenix. Kinder Morgan will operate the pipeline from Phoenix to California. The project is targeting completion in 2029, subject to receipt of all permits and regulatory approvals.


**Focused on safe, disciplined execution**


The project also reflects Phillips 66’s focus on disciplined execution and safe, reliable operations as development advances.


“Reaching FID is an important milestone, but it is also the start of the work ahead,” said Todd Tanory, senior vice president, midstream operations, Phillips 66. “Our focus is on executing this project the right way, with safety, operational excellence and strong coordination across every stage of development.”


**Built for changing market needs**


The final investment decision follows a[successful second open season](https://investor.phillips66.com/financial-information/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Phillips-66-and-Kinder-Morgan-Advance-Western-Gateway-Pipeline-Project-Following-Successful-Open-Season/default.aspx) that secured long-term shipper commitments sufficient to move the project forward. That commercial support underscores the need for greater supply flexibility in western markets and reinforces the value of a system designed to connect refining centers with demand growth in Arizona and California.


**For customers, that matters.**


“Customers need confidence that supply can move where and when it is needed,” said Michael Andrew, vice president, midstream commercial and business development, Phillips 66. “Western Gateway is designed to improve optionality, strengthen logistics and create a more dependable path to serve western markets over the long term.”


Together, Phillips 66, Kinder Morgan and HF Sinclair are advancing a project intended to create a new supply path to the West at a time when reliability, resilience and flexibility matter more than ever.


With the final investment decision now in place, Baldridge says, “Western Gateway is entering its next chapter with a clear objective: to fuel the West safely and reliably.”
