# Phillips 66 contributes $200,000 to support active wildfire response in Washington

Support is helping address urgent needs across the Spokane community


As Spokane, Washington, continues to battle multiple wildfires that have burned thousands of acres, destroyed hundreds of structures and forced widespread evacuations, Phillips 66 is contributing $200,000 to support organizations leading active response efforts across the community.


****Why it matters****


As response efforts continue in Spokane, access to emergency shelter, food, supplies and recovery resources remains critical. By supporting organizations actively serving those affected, Phillips 66 is helping provide relief where it is needed most while strengthening community resilience during this time of need.


**Supporting organizations on the frontlines**


Spokane continues to battle three major wildfires that have burned more than 10,000 acres, destroyed more than 900 structures and forced the evacuation of roughly 65,000 people.


This contribution includes $150,000 to the[American Red Cross](https://www.redcross.org/about-us.html) and $50,000 to the[Spokane Fire Department Foundation](https://sfdfoundation.org/) , which together will support emergency shelters, meals, supplies and other critical services for displaced residents and help firefighters and emergency personnel meet urgent needs as wildfire response efforts continue.


“Our hearts are with everyone in Spokane who has been affected by these fires, especially the families who have been displaced and the first responders working tirelessly to protect this community,” said Clint Loobey, director, area operations, North Central midstream, Phillips 66. “We hope this support helps meet immediate needs and stands alongside the people and organizations responding every day.


****Investing in community resilience****


Wildfires can have an immediate and lasting impact on families, first responders and entire communities.


Supporting communities during times of crisis is a key part of Phillips 66’s commitment to building strong, resilient communities. Through relationships with trusted local and national organizations, the company helps communities respond to immediate challenges and recover after natural disasters.


By working with these organizations, Phillips 66 aims to help expand available resources for Spokane residents and first responders during this difficult time and throughout the recovery process.
