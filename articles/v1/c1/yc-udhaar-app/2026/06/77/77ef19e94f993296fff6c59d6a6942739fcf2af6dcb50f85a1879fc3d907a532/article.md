Before paying, it’s important to check your LESCO bill online to confirm your latest amount and due date. Follow these quick steps:


1. Visit the official website: www.lesco.gov.pk


2. Find the “Bill Inquiry” section


3. Enter your 14-digit reference number


4. Click on “View Bill”


Your latest LESCO electricity bill online will appear instantly. You can download or print a copy if needed.
