### 2. Pay via MEPCO Web Bill Portal


You can also pay your MEPCO bill directly through the official MEPCO web portal.

### 3. Pay Through Online Banking Apps


Many Pakistani banks, including HBL, UBL, Meezan Bank, and Bank Alfalah, allow online MEPCO bill payments.


Steps:


1. Log in to your bank’s mobile app.


2. Go to Bill Payment.


3. Select Electricity, then choose MEPCO.


4. Enter your consumer number.


5. Confirm and make the payment.


While banking apps are convenient, they usually don’t offer cashback rewards like Udhaar Book does.

## **What is MEPCO?**


The Multan Electric Power Company (MEPCO) is one of Pakistan’s major electricity distribution companies under WAPDA. It supplies electricity to South Punjab’s 13 districts, including Multan, Bahawalpur, Dera Ghazi Khan, and Muzaffargarh.
Through its digital services like MEPCO online bill check, MEPCO duplicate bill download, and MEPCO complaint online, customers can manage their electricity bills with ease.

### How to Check if Your MEPCO Bill is Paid or Not


To verify whether your MEPCO bill has been paid:


- Check the transaction history in your Udhaar Book or banking app.


- Visit the MEPCO MIS (Management Information System) portal to confirm your bill status.


- Contact the MEPCO complaint number for verification assistance.


### **MEPCO Bill Calculator**


The MEPCO bill calculator helps users estimate their monthly electricity cost before the bill arrives.
You can input your electricity units and view an approximate total for your MEPCO bill Multan or any region under MEPCO’s network.
This feature is helpful for managing electricity usage and predicting future MEPCO bills.


### **MEPCO Complaint and Helpline Information**


If you face any issues such as billing errors, delayed connections, or electricity faults, MEPCO provides several complaint channels.


- MEPCO Complaint Number: 0800-63726
- MEPCO Head Office Multan: Khanewal Road, Multan
- MEPCO Complaint Online: Available on the official MEPCO website


You can also submit a complaint through MEPCO’s online portal for quick resolution.


### **MEPCO New Connection and MIS Portal**


For a new electricity connection, MEPCO offers an online application form on its website.


Through the MEPCO MIS portal, consumers can track application status, review past bills, and monitor complaint progress — all without visiting the MEPCO head office Multan.
