## **Check LESCO Online Bill by Reference Number**


The

### **What Is the Reference Number?**


It is a


**14-digit number** printed on every LESCO electricity bill. This number uniquely identifies the consumer connection.


### **Information Available Through Reference Number**


- Current bill amount


- Units consumed


- Meter readings


- Taxes and surcharges


- Late payment charges (if any)


For accurate and up-to-date results, the reference number method is recommended.

**Peak and Off-Peak Hours**


Electricity charges vary depending on usage timing.


### **Peak Hours**


Peak hours are periods of high electricity demand. Units consumed during these hours are charged at higher rates.


### **Off-Peak Hours**


Electricity used outside peak times is billed at a lower rate.


LESCO revises peak and off-peak schedules periodically, so consumers should check official updates. Shifting heavy appliance usage to off-peak hours can significantly reduce monthly costs.


## **How to Reduce Your Electricity Bill?**


While tariffs are fixed, usage habits play a major role in the final amount.


### **Practical Ways to Control Costs**


- Use heavy appliances during off-peak hours


- Switch to energy-efficient devices


- Turn off unused lights and electronics


- Monitor monthly unit consumption


- Compare current and previous bills


Small adjustments over time can lead to noticeable savings.
