# Miracle featured by AWS: Accelerating clinical trials with real-time transparency

We’re honored to be featured by[Amazon Web Services (AWS)](https://aws.amazon.com/startups?lang=en-US) in a recent customer story spotlighting how Miracle is helping biotech teams run faster, more transparent clinical trials.


In the article titled "Clinical trials get the Miracle treatment," AWS highlights how we’re working with global biopharma companies to:


Eliminate time-consuming, error-prone spreadsheet trackers Improve cross-system visibility across clinical trial platforms (i.e. Electronic Data Capture, central labs, safety/PV, recruitment vendors) Accelerate clinical trial timelines Maintain security and compliance at scale, including SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR
