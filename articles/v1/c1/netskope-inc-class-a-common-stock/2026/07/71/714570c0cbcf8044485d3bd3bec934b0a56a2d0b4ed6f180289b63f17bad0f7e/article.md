# Security Consolidation, Improved Efficiency, and Network Performance Make Bechtel AI-Ready

Data quality is another top priority for Bechtel, particularly because effective AI depends on high-quality data. “We are continually refining our data strategy to improve quality and unlock the full value of AI,” Noon says. This effort brings together security and networking teams across the organization.


“I don’t operate with silos,” Noon explains. “That’s key to success. Whether it’s security, infrastructure, cloud, software delivery, or project delivery, we collaborate and make decisions together. Everyone understands what we’re working toward.” Deploying Netskope One has strengthened this collaboration by unifying network and security teams on a common platform.


“Bechtel’s relationship with Netskope is fantastic,” Noon concludes. “I appreciate that Netskope’s AI innovation is grounded in practical use cases. They focus on delivering a few capabilities well – ones we can rely on – before expanding further. The alignment between Netskope’s vision and Bechtel’s needs gives me confidence in implementing new technologies.”
