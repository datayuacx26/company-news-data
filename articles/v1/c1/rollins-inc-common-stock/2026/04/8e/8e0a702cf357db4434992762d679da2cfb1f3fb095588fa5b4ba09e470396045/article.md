# ROLLINS ACQUIRES ROMEX PEST CONTROL

ATLANTA


, April 2, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Rollins, Inc. today announced that it has acquired[Romex Pest Control](https://romexpest.com/) . Romex employs over 200 teammates and is headquartered in Pleasant Grove, UT. Romex is a "top 40" pest management company according to PCT 100 rankings.


Founded in 2016, Romex provides services for residential and commercial customers across four states. In addition to door-to-door marketing, Romex has grown through data-driven decisions and is known for eco-friendly operations.


"The acquisition of Romex Pest Control will contribute to our long-term growth strategy in new regions across the United States," said Jerry Gahlhoff, Rollins' President and CEO. "Romex provides Rollins entry points into new markets. While joining our portfolio of brands will enable Romex to further scale operations and expand service offerings to existing customers, they will maintain their brand identity given their strong reputation as a high-quality, customer-focused business in the areas in which they operate."


Rollins Executive Vice President and CFO, Kenneth Krause, added, "The acquisition of Romex is another example of our successful M&A playbook in action. We continue to add high-quality businesses to our premier portfolio of brands through a disciplined and strategic approach. The runway for continued growth from acquisitions remains solid, supported by a robust pipeline of opportunities that exist in a highly fragmented and growing market."


"We didn't choose Rollins because they were one of the biggest names in the industry," said Sam Crossley, Chief Operating Officer of Romex. "We chose them because they share the same commitment to our customers and communities with which we've built our business. Our Romex customers will see the same faces, the same service, and the same standards, with even more resources behind them."
