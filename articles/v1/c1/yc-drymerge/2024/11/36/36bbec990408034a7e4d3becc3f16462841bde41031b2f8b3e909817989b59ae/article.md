# Simplified Workflow Automations for Your Team

Taking a step back on workflow automation: how does DryMerge play a role in simplifying the way you automate your workflows?


From drag and drop to AI agents, at DryMerge we believe in the power of simplifying automations. DryMerge sets up AI agents by connecting your apps and turning chat prompts into powerful automations. The goal? For you to save time on operational tasks.


For example,[marketing](https://drymerge.com/blog/drymerge-for-marketers) and sales teams ask DryMerge things like “When a new demo gets added to Google Calendar, figure out who the attendees are, add them to our CRM, and ping the team on[Slack](https://drymerge.com/apps/slack/) “.


## Enabling your apps to talk to each other


DryMerge can automate a wide range of operational workflows where different systems talk to each other. Below, three use cases that help teams and individuals save time and resources.


## Automating CRM updates based on reliable customer information


This is one of the most time consuming tasks we see across[marketing](https://drymerge.com/blog/drymerge-for-marketers) and sales teams. Sales representatives can spend up to 20% of their time updating their CRM, spending on average 5.9 hours per week manually inputting the right information in allocated CRM fields. (Autopylot, 2023) Good thing DryMerge can take care of that for you.

## Enabling personalized outreach at scale


Why spend thousands of dollars for an AI outbound platform when you can save time and money (i.e. less than a tenth of the cost) doing it with DryMerge? Ask DryMerge to write hyper personalized emails simply based on a leads’ email. It takes less than 2 minutes.

## Reducing churn with customized email follow ups


Self-serve users will churn if they don’t get the support they need in time. DryMerge enables you to set up automated email flows based on various user journeys that you can set up in seconds. Ask DryMerge to warm up leads, welcome new sign ups, or reactivate churned users. All of this can be done with personalized communication to make the onboarding process smoother, and turn early adopters to evangelists.
