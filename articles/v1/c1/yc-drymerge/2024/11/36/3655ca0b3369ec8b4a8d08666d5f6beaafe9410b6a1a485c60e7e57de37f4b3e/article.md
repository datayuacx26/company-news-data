## The Evolution of CRM Systems


CRM systems began as simple databases for tracking customer interactions, primarily used by accounting teams. Over time, they evolved into robust platforms for sales and marketing teams, offering tools to manage growing customer demands.


The challenge? Data overload.


Modern CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive generate a vast amount of data. Without efficient tools, this data can overwhelm teams rather than empower them. AI has emerged as the solution, offering features that simplify workflows, analyze data, and enhance customer insights.


However, even these solutions come with their challenges:


• Cost Implications: Many advanced AI features are locked behind high-tier plans, making them inaccessible to small and mid-sized businesses. For instance, Salesforce’s AI-powered tools like Einstein GPT and Einstein Conversation Insights are available only in premium plans.


• Complexity: AI tools often require extensive training and setup, creating a steep learning curve for users.
