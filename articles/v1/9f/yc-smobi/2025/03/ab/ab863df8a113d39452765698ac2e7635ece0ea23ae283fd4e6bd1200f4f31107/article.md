# Smobi’s Top 5 Tips to Boost Cart Checkouts and Get More Sales with RCS Instant Messaging

Let's face it, a stressful and long-winded checkout process is a serious sales killer.


Every extra click, every unnecessary form, every unexpected cost is a potential roadblock between your product reaching the hands of a happy customer.


More than ever, online shoppers demand a simple, fast, and efficient checkout experience- anything less may steer them in the direction your competitors.


Enter the hero of this story- Rich Communication Services (RCS) instant messaging (the next-generation of SMS marketing). RCS offers a dynamic solution to address buyer hesitation during the final stages of a purchase.


Why is this so important? Well, having a direct line of communication to a hesitant buyer is a game-changer for closing the deal. It's the difference between a lost sale and a loyal customer.


Think about it- doubt creeps in, questions arise, and suddenly, that “add to cart" and "purchase" enthusiasm starts to wane.


With RCS, you can intercept that hesitation in real-time. Answer pressing questions, address concerns, and ultimately, guide buyers to completing their purchase.


Even with the power of RCS at your fingertips, a poorly designed cart checkout can still sabotage your sales.


It's like having a super-fast delivery service but a warehouse full of unmarked delivery packages. No matter how quickly you could offer sales support, the underlying problem prevents success.


So, let's talk about the common checkout challenges that lead to abandoned carts, and how to fix them, while leveraging the power of RCS.


### **Step 1: Offer Multiple Payment Options at Checkout**


Flexibility is paramount when it comes to payments. Customers have diverse preferences, and offering a variety of options caters to everyone.


Support a wide range of payment methods, including:


- Credit and debit cards
- Popular digital wallets like Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal
- Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) services
- And even, direct bank transfers


Providing this level of choice empowers customers and removes potential issues that prevent the completion of a purchase.


### **Step 2: Enable One-Click Checkout to Boost Your Sales**


One-click checkout is a game-changer for conversions.


By streamlining the buyer's journey and eliminating the tedious tasks of navigating multiple payment windows, you significantly reduce doubt and encourage boost cart checkouts.


Think about aspects like filling in forms, no one enjoys doing this again and again. If repeat customers only need to enter their information once, and your online store securely stores this information for future transactions, they’re more likely to develop loyalty.


RCS can further enhance this experience by offering one-tap checkout links with discount codes for returning customers. The less steps involved, the higher your conversion rate will be.


### **Step 3: Use Real-Time Customer Support**


Hesitations and uncertainties can easily derail a purchase. Addressing these concerns in real-time is crucial.


Common challenges that deter a purchase a include:


- Shipping costs
- Delivery times
- Insufficient product information
- Payment issues


RCS tackles this challenge by providing a form of instant communication, allowing customers to chat with your support team directly from the checkout page.


This is a speedy and effective way to eliminate doubts and keep buyers engaged in the purchasing process.


You can even take this a step further for larger scale order queries and enhance your RCS communication with RCS AI-agents that can handle more generalized queries and frequently asked questions (FAQs), while human agents can step in for more complex inquiries, providing a robust customer support experience.


### **Step 4: Send Personalized Reminders and Limited-Time Offers With RCS**


Creating a sense of urgency can be highly effective in helping a hesitant customer to make a purchase decision. .


If a potential buyer has abandoned their cart you can use RCS messaging to send personalized reminders about items left in their carts, along with exclusive offers, free shipping incentives, or time-sensitive discounts.


Remember, a well timed message like, "complete your purchase in the next 30 minutes for 10% off!" can be the nudge a customer needs to finalize their purchase.


### **Step 5: Provide Order Confirmation and Post-Purchase Engagement with RCS**


Building trust is a non-negotiable for all e-Commerce stores! Sending an RCS message with an instant purchase confirmation reinforces trust in your brand’s ability to deliver and RCS as the messaging medium.


Always include detailed order information, such as:


- Estimated delivery dates
- Tracking links
- Order number
- Returns policy link


But, don't stop there! Follow up with personalized product recommendations or loyalty rewards to encourage repeat business and build relationships with your customers.


RCS instant messaging offers a powerful edge for eCommerce stores who want to create experiences that target the direct needs of their customers, in real-time, for greater service satisfaction and purchase confidence.


Offering multiple payment options, enabling one-click checkout, providing speedy customer support, and sending personalized reminders and offers, is precisely what your online store needs to significantly bolster conversion rates and increase your sales.


On that final note, remember that RCS is more than a means to close a deal, it’s the new marketing standard for building long-term loyalty and trust, one smooth checkout at a time.
