# Hello World!

### Let's make data more accessible for everyone


High demand for data insight has become a bottleneck for many businesses **.** Ad hoc questions overwhelm analysts; emails, tasks and workchats generate noise and friction and self-serve dashboards quickly become obsolete with shifting business needs.


Analysts have very little time left for deep, impactful analysis. We believe Shape can remove the bottleneck by automating answers without sacrificing accuracy or governance.
