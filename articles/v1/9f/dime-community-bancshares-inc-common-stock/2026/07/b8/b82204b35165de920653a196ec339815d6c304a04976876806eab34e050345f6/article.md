# Dime Commercial Bancshares Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend for Series A Preferred Stock

HAUPPAUGE, N.Y., July 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Dime Commercial Bancshares, Inc. (NYSE: DCOM, DCOM PR and DCBG) (the “Company”) announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.34375 per share on the Company’s 5.50% Fixed-Rate Non-Cumulative Perpetual Preferred Stock, Series A, payable on August 14, 2026 to holders of record as of August 7, 2026.


**ABOUT DIME COMMERCIAL BANCSHARES, INC.**


Dime Commercial Bancshares, Inc. is the holding company for Dime Commercial Bank, a New York State-charted trust company with approximately $15 billion in assets and the number one deposit market share on Greater Long Island (1).
