# The Eras Collection: A Journey Through the Generations and Courtyard’s First-Ever Memorabilia Drop

**TL;DR:** The Eras Collection is a series of themed Pokémon pack drops that travel through every generation, from Kanto to Paldea. It also introduces[Courtyard's](https://courtyard.io/) first-ever memorabilia drop: signed, framed, authenticated soccer jerseys from some of the greatest players in history, hidden as bounties inside the packs.


As the World Cup brings nations from across the globe together, we're celebrating the icons and eras that shaped the world of Pokémon with **The Eras Collection** .


From Kanto to Paldea, it's a journey through the generations that built the franchise and it comes with a first for Courtyard: authenticated, physical memorabilia you can pull straight from a pack.


## **What Is the Eras Collection?**


The[Eras Collection](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine?category=limited_drop) is a series of themed Pokémon pack drops, each celebrating a different chapter in Pokémon history. Rip your way through the generations, from the cards that started it all to today's biggest chases.


Each drop is built around an era:


- **Horizons** — Alola, Galar & Paldea
- **Ascension** — Unova & Kalos
- **Evolution** — Hoenn & Sinnoh
- **Origins** — Kanto & Johto


## **A First for Courtyard: Memorabilia**


The World Cup felt like the perfect moment to introduce something completely new to Courtyard.


For the first time ever, we're dropping physical memorabilia – signed, professionally framed soccer jerseys from some of the greatest players to ever play the game. Every jersey is officially authenticated and hidden[inside The Eras Collection packs and all Limited Drop Packs](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine?category=limited_drop) on Courtyard.


## **The Lineup**


Just like the packs journey through Pokémon generations, the jerseys span the greatest eras of soccer:


- Lionel Messi — Argentina World Cup Jersey (Beckett Witnessed)
- Cristiano Ronaldo — Real Madrid (Beckett Witnessed)
- Kylian Mbappé — Real Madrid (Icons Authentic)
- Lamine Yamal — FC Barcelona (Beckett Authenticated)
- Pelé — Brazil (PSA/DNA)


From Pelé to Yamal, every era of soccer is represented.


Every jersey is hidden as a bounty across **The Eras Collection** , giving every Era Pack a chance to reveal one of these iconic pieces. Pull a jersey, and it's yours- signed, professionally framed, and authenticated.


Once you've pulled it, the choice is yours: keep it safely vaulted, list it on the Marketplace, accept a buyback offer, or have it shipped directly to your door.


Each jersey is a true one-of-one on the platform. Once it's been pulled, it's gone.
