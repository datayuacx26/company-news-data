# The Final Whistle: Argentina vs. Spain Packs and Signed Jersey Bounties

*TL;DR The World Cup Final is here, and we’re celebrating with*[two new $79 packs](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine?category=limited_drop) *built around Argentina and Spain. Both feature a largely shared lineup of graded soccer cards, with select Messi and Yamal chases are exclusive to their respective packs. Hidden throughout the packs are signed and authenticated jersey bounties featuring Messi, Yamal, and more.*


A champion is about to be crowned.


Throughout the World Cup, The World Stage has been our way of celebrating the tournament from every corner of the collecting world. We’ve dropped packs featuring soccer’s biggest stars, journeyed through all nine generations of Pokémon, introduced our first authenticated memorabilia, and even let the community build its own Pokémon soccer squad.


Now, with the tournament down to Argentina and Spain, we’re going all out for the finale.


The Final Whistle brings two new limited-edition packs to[Courtyard](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine?category=limited_drop) , each representing one side of Sunday’s World Cup Final. Pick the country you’re backing, chase the players who defined the tournament, and take your shot at signed jersey bounties worthy of the biggest match in the world.


## Pick Your Side


The Final Whistle is available in two flavors:


- [Argentina Finals Pack](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine/soccer-final-whistle)
- [Spain Finals Pack](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine/soccer-final-whistle-2-pack)


Both packs are priced at **$79** and feature a largely shared lineup, giving you a chance to pull graded cards from stars across the tournament, including Cristiano Ronaldo, Kylian Mbappé, Jude Bellingham, and more.


But your choice still matters. Some of the biggest Argentina and Spain cards are exclusive to their respective packs. Choose Argentina for additional chances at Lionel Messi. Back Spain for exclusive Lamine Yamal chases.


Same final. Same price. Different side of history.


## Signed Jersey Bounties


A normal chase card didn’t feel big enough for a World Cup Final, so we added something extra.


Hidden across the Final Whistle drop are signed, framed, and authenticated jersey bounties featuring some of the biggest names in the sport.


The Argentina pack includes a chance to uncover a signed Lionel Messi jersey, while the Spain pack features a signed Lamine Yamal jersey waiting to be found. Additional signed jerseys are also hidden throughout the packs.


Once you've pulled it, the choice is yours: keep it safely vaulted, list it on the Marketplace, accept a buyback offer, or have it shipped directly to your door.


## Messi’s Second or Yamal’s Arrival?


Forty-eight teams started the tournament. Now only two remain.


Argentina enters the final with a chance to defend its title and give Messi a second consecutive World Cup. Spain arrives behind one of soccer’s brightest young stars, with Yamal looking to announce a new era on the game’s biggest stage.


One legend chasing another chapter. One teenage superstar trying to write the first page of his own.


Pick your side, pull your pack, and see whether you walk away with one of the jerseys made for this moment.
