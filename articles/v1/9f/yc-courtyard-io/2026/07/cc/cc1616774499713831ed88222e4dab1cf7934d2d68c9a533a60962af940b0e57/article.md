# Bussin’ With The Boys x Courtyard: Taylor Lewan’s National Pack

*TL;DR: The $77 Bussin’ With The Boys National Pack drops July 29 with six bounty cards valued at up to nearly $8,000. Pull one and hold it through August 7 to also receive a football signed by Taylor Lewan.*


The biggest sports card show of the year deserves a big opening act.


On Wednesday, July 29, as collectors from across the hobby arrive in Chicago for The National, Courtyard is kicking things off with the **Bussin’ With The Boys National Pack** , a limited football drop curated by Taylor Lewan.


Taylor hand-picked six bounty cards for the pack. Pull one, hold it through August 7, and you’ll also receive a football signed by Taylor himself.


Six bounty cards. Six signed footballs. One pack built for The National.


## A Pack Curated by Taylor Lewan


Taylor already brings the energy to his weekly live streams, ripping Courtyard packs, chasing the elusive ’Chu, and bringing the Bussin’ With The Boys audience along for the ride.


This drop takes it to the next level, enabling Taylor to share that passion in a more personal way through curating a pack of his own.


Taylor went out and personally selected six football cards to serve as the heavy hitters. The lineup includes some of the game’s biggest stars, ultra-rare inserts, coveted Gold parallels, and cards valued at nearly $8,000.


And because Taylor wore No. 77 throughout his NFL career, the pack is priced at just $77.


## The Six Bounty Cards


Hidden throughout the drop are six bounty cards chosen by Taylor:


- 2025 Topps Chrome Kaiju Cam Ward PSA 10 (Est. $7,929)
- 2025 Panini Donruss Gold Downtown Lamar Jackson PSA 10 (Est. $6,942)
- 2022 Panini Donruss Optic Gold Downtown Rob Gronkowski PSA 10 (Est. $5,435)
- 2019 Panini Absolute Gold Kaboom Nick Bosa PSA 10 (Est. $4,355)
- 2024 Panini Donruss Horizontal Gold Downtown DJ Moore/Rome Odunze PSA 10 (Est. $3,575)
- 2021 Panini Clearly Donruss Downtown Lamar Jackson PSA 10 (Est. $1,330)


From Kaijus and Kabooms to Gold Downtowns, it’s a stacked lineup that makes one thing clear: Taylor knows his cards.


## Pull a Bounty. Get a Signed Football.


Pull any of the six bounty cards and you’ll also receive a football signed by Taylor Lewan.


To qualify, the bounty card must remain in your Courtyard collection through **August 7** . Signed footballs will be processed directly through Courtyard’s redemption system and shipped by **August 21** . No separate claim process. No manual shipping coordination. Just hold the bounty and we’ll take care of the rest.


## Kicking Off The National


The National is the biggest gathering in sports cards, bringing together collectors, dealers, creators, athletes, and some of the best cards in the hobby under one roof. We wanted to start the week with something that matched that energy.


The Bussin’ With The Boys National Pack goes live on opening day, giving collectors everywhere a chance to join the chase whether they’re walking the show floor in Chicago or ripping from home.


And this is only the beginning. With **Rippin’ With The Boys** bringing even more cards, collecting, and pack-opening chaos into Taylor’s world, we’re excited to keep building new ways for fans to get involved.


Attending The National? Come find Courtyard at **Booth #2316** from July 29 through August 2 for giveaways, activations, and a few surprises throughout the show.
