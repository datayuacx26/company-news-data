# The Amazing Chase Returns: Five Days of Pokémon Bounties

*TL;DR: A new Pokémon pack drops every day for five days. Each pack contains four bounty cards, and pulling one earns you the card plus 100,000*[Courtyard](https://courtyard.io/) *Points.*


The response to the first Amazing Chase was too good not to run it back. This time, we’re going bigger.


A new Pokémon pack will drop every day for five days, starting at $19 and building toward a $99 finale. Each pack contains four hidden bounty cards, bringing 20 total bounties to the chase.


Pull one of them and you’ll earn the card plus 100,000 Courtyard Points.[All five packs](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine/?category=limited_drop) will remain live through the end of the event, so the chase only gets bigger as the week goes on.


**Important:** For the 100,000 Courtyard Points bounty reward, winners must keep their bounty card in their collection until verification is complete. This will take place on Friday, August 14 at 12am ET. Selling or transferring the bounty card beforehand will result in disqualification.


[Day 1: The Classics](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine/pkmn-amazing-chase-day1)


We’re kicking things off with some familiar faces. Bounties include a Celebrations Charizard, Blastoise ex from Pokémon 151, the 2002 McDonald’s Promo Zapdos, and Mew ex.


**Day 2: Electric and Psychic**


Day 2 brings Jolteon VMAX, Raichu from Paldea Evolved, Team Rocket’s Mewtwo ex, and Mega Alakazam ex into the hunt.


**Day 3: Promo Paradise**


The third drop is loaded with fan-favorite promos and modern grails. Look out for Greninja Gold Star, Special Delivery Charizard, Pikachu & Zekrom GX, and Mewtwo GX from Shining Legends.


**Day 4: Going Mega**


The stakes climb on Day 4 with Mega Charizard X ex, Mega Mewtwo ex, Mega Gengar ex, and the Pikachu with Grey Felt Hat Van Gogh promo.


‍ **Day 5: The Final Stop**


We saved some of the biggest chases for last. The finale features Rayquaza VMAX from Evolving Skies, Mew ex, Pretend Grunt Pikachu, and another Pikachu with Grey Felt Hat, with the top bounty valued around $4,000.


‍ **The Chase Keeps Growing**


We love finding ways to bring something extra to our normal pack drops, and The Amazing Chase quickly became one of our favorites.


Now it’s back with[five new packs](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine/?category=limited_drop) , 20 hidden bounties, and even more reasons to follow along each day. Keep track of what's been pulled[here](https://help.courtyard.io/en/articles/16100308-the-amazing-chase) . We can’t wait to see what gets found in Round 2.
