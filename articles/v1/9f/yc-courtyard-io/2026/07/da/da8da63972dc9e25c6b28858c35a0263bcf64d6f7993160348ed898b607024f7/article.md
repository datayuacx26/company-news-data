# The Road to the National: A Week of PSA 10s

*TL;DR: Four PSA 10 sports packs drop this week as we count down to the National. Find Courtyard at Booth #2316 for exclusive activations, giveaways, and more. ‍* The[National Sports Collectors Convention](https://www.nsccshow.com/) brings the entire hobby together for five days of cards, trades, grails, giveaways, and more.


This year,[Courtyard](https://courtyard.io/) will be there at[Booth #2316](https://www.nsccshow.com/interactive-map) with some of our biggest activations yet. But before the show begins, we’re bringing the National experience to you.
‍
Starting today,[four PSA 10 sports packs](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine?category=limited_drop) are dropping across baseball, basketball, football, and multi-sports, featuring some of the biggest athletes and most sought-after cards in the hobby. Each pack features Spicy odds, giving you a stronger shot at landing premium hits from some of the biggest athletes and most sought-after cards in the hobby.


[‍ Wednesday: Baseball PSA 10](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine/baseball-psa10)


The Road to the National starts where the sports card hobby began.


The Baseball PSA 10 Pack features grails from some of the biggest stars, rookies, and prospects in the game:


- 2013 Calbee Star Card #S-61 Shohei Ohtani
- 2017 Topps Gypsy Queen Autographs #AJ Aaron Judge
- 2020 Bowman Chrome Prospect Autographs #CPABWJ Bobby Witt Jr. Refractor


**Thursday: Basketball PSA 10**


Day two belongs to the hardwood, with PSA 10 rookies, rare parallels, iconic inserts, and cards from basketball’s past, present, and future:


- 1996 Stadium Club Class Acts #CA1 Jerry Stackhouse/Michael Jordan Refractor
- 2025 Topps Chrome Autograph Issue Rookies #TAIR-CF Cooper Flagg Refractor
- 2023 Panini Prizm #136 Victor Wembanyama Pulsar


**Friday: Football PSA 10**


The road to the National hits the gridiron Friday with gem mint case hits, rare parallels, and rookie cards from some of football’s biggest names:


- 2017 Panini Donruss Optic #177 Patrick Mahomes II Holo
- 2024 Panini Donruss Optic Downtown! #21 Caleb Williams
- 2021 Donruss Optic Peyton Manning Black Pandora Downtown
- 2021 Panini Donruss Optic Downtown #DT18 Peyton Manning Black Pandora


**Saturday: Multi-Sport PSA 10**


The final stop brings every sport together.


The Multi-Sport PSA 10 Pack combines baseball, basketball, football, hockey, and more into one stacked lineup. Chases include:


- 2024 Topps Chrome Cosmic Planetary Pursuit #6 Paul Skenes Jupiter
- 2003 SP Authentic Sign of the Times #WG Wayne Gretzky
- 2017 Panini Donruss Optic #177 Patrick Mahomes II Red/Yellow


The first three packs will each be available for just 24 hours. The final multi-sport pack drops Saturday and will remain available until the National begins on Wednesday, July 29, or until supplies run out.
