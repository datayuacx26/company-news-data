# Grand Voyage: Five One Piece Drops. One Growing Bounty Hunt.

***TL;DR:*** *Grand Voyage is*[live on Courtyard](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine?category=limited_drop) *, with a series of limited-time One Piece packs that follow the Straw Hats from East Blue through the Grand Line and New World. Each stop adds new crew members, bigger chases, and hidden bounty cards worth* ***100,000 Courtyard Points*** *when pulled.*


Set a course for the Grand Line! Starting today[One Piece: Grand Voyage](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine?category=limited_drop) takes over Courtyard with a multi-day series of packs inspired by the Straw Hats’ journey from East Blue to the Grand Line and beyond.


Each stop brings a new pack, new crew members to chase, and a new set of hidden bounties worth **100,000 Courtyard Points** if you find one.


## **The Voyage, Mapped Out**


Grand Voyage begins in East Blue and moves forward from there:


🏝️ **East Blue Pack** Where the journey begins, with early Straw Hats like Luffy, Zoro, and Nami leading the way.


🌊 **Grand Line Pack** The crew grows and so do the chases, with Sanji, Chopper, Nico Robin, and more entering the mix.


🌌 **New World Pack** The voyage reaches its biggest stage yet, bringing more of the crew and some of the biggest cards of the event.


Each pack will be available for a limited time.


## **The Crew Gets Bigger as You Go**


Grand Voyage is built to follow the Straw Hats’ journey.


East Blue starts with the earliest members of the crew. As the voyage moves into the Grand Line and New World, more Straw Hats join the chase and the cards escalate with them.


A few of the cards waiting along the way include a **PSA 10 Championship Celebration Pack Nami** , a **PSA 10 1st Anniversary Tony Tony Chopper** , and a **PSA 10 Anime 25th Collection Monkey D. Luffy Alternate Art** .


By the time you reach the New World, the whole crew is on board.


## **Find a Bounty. Earn 100,000 Points.**


There’s another reason to keep an eye on each stop.


Every Grand Voyage pack has its own **bounty cards** hidden throughout the drop.


Pull one of that pack’s active bounty cards and **hold it in your Courtyard vault until August 23** to earn **100,000 Courtyard Points** on top of the card itself.


Each bounty can only be claimed once. Once someone pulls it, that bounty is off the board.


## **The Voyage Starts Here**


East Blue is live now in the **Courtyard app** , with the Grand Line, New World, and more unlocking throughout the week.


New packs. New crew members. New bounties.


[Open the app and set sail.](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine?category=limited_drop)
