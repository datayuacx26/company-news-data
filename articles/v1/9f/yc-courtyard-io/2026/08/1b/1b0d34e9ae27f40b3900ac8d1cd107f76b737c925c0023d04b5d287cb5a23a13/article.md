It’s that time again: A new NFL season is right around the corner. Let’s go!


Before we get into the latest training camp brawls, 2026 season predictions, and fantasy drafts, let’s talk about how we got here.


The Seattle Seahawks—and team legend[Marshawn Lynch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8rHK18Jdysc) —are still celebrating after putting the hurt on Drake Maye and the Patriots en route to the franchise’s second Super Bowl victory.
