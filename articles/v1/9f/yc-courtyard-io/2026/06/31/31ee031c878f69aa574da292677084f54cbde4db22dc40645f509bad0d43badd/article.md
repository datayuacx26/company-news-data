# The Amazing Chase: Daily Drops, Hidden Bounties, and a $4.5K Grand Prize

*TL;DR: A new*[Pokémon pack](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine) *drops daily, each with hidden bounty cards that unlock bonus rewards when pulled. Plus, every point earned during The Amazing Chase counts as an entry to win a $4,500 PSA 10 Moonbreon.*


This week, we’re adding a new twist to limited-time Pokémon packs on[Courtyard](https://courtyard.io/) .


In collecting, a bounty is a reward attached to a specific card before it gets pulled. Find the card, unlock the reward. That’s the idea behind **The Amazing Chase** .


A new Pokémon pack will drop every day this week. Each daily pack will include **bounty chase cards** . Pull one, keep the card, and instantly unlock bonus rewards.


And all week long, every Courtyard Point you earn becomes an entry into the Grand Prize Giveaway for a **PSA 10 Umbreon VMAX from Evolving Skies** , valued at around **$4,500** .


## **How Bounty Cards Work**


Each daily Pokémon pack will include **4 bounty cards** hidden inside.


You’ll be able to see which bounty cards are available on the pack page before ripping.


If you pull one of that pack’s bounty cards, you win:


**A sealed Shining Fates V Tin case** Estimated value: around **$500**


**200,000 Courtyard Points** A massive boost toward the Grand Prize Giveaway


## **Every Point Is a Grand Prize Entry**


The bounty cards are not the only way to join the chase.


Every Courtyard Point you earn during the event (Monday, June 1 9AM EST through Sunday, June 7 12AM EST) counts as **1 entry** into the Grand Prize Giveaway.


You can earn points by claiming Daily Points in the Rewards Hub, ripping packs, referring friends, and completing Quests.


That means everyone has a shot at the **$4,500 PSA 10 Moonbreon** , whether they pull a bounty card or not.


But Bounty Finders get a serious advantage: each bounty comes with **200,000 Courtyard Points** , which means **200,000 Grand Prize entries** .


The Grand Prize winner will be announced by Wednesday **June 10** during a Courtyard Livestream.


## **Packs Stay Live All Week**


Packs from the Amazing Chase can stay live throughout the event up til Sunday June 7 at 12am ET. That means multiple bounty hunts may be active at the same time.


Packs remain live until they sell out or every bounty card from that pack has been pulled.


Across the event, there will be **20 bounty cards** hidden in Courtyard packs.


## **Important Eligibility Note**


To stay eligible for bounty rewards, winners must keep their bounty card in their Courtyard collection until verification is complete. This will take place by Monday June 8 at 12am ET. Selling or transferring the bounty card before verification will result in disqualification.
