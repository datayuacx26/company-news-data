# Introducing Courtyard Rewards: The More You Rip, the More You Unlock

‍ *TL;DR: Courtyard Rewards launches June 29, giving members a new way to earn monthly perks like Free Packs, Guaranteed Value Pulls, exclusive drops, events, shipping credits, and VIP Support. The more you rip, the higher your tier, and the more you unlock.*


From raffles and giveaways to free packs and surprise drops, we’re always looking for new ways to give back to the Courtyard community. Courtyard Rewards is the next step.


Starting June 29, our new rewards program gives our most active members even more to unlock: monthly free packs, guaranteed value, exclusive drops, member-only events, shipping perks, and priority support.


The more you rip, the more your status grows. And the higher your status, the more you get back.


## **How Rewards Work**


Courtyard Rewards has four tiers: Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond.


Your tier is based on your qualifying pack spend during the season. You can track your tier, progress, and benefits anytime in your[Rewards Hub](http://courtyard.io/rewards) .


Benefits are added at the start of each month, so once you hit a new tier, your new rewards will be waiting in your Rewards Hub the following month.


## **What You Unlock**


Every tier comes with monthly rewards, and the perks stack as you climb.


*Platinum = 5 free shipments // Diamond = 10 free shipments


## **What’s a Guaranteed Value Pull?**


A Guaranteed Value Pack is exactly what it sounds like: a pack where you’re guaranteed to get at least the price of the pack, with all the upside of pulling a chase.


Each month, eligible members can claim a GVP from their Rewards Hub and choose from qualifying packs up to their tier’s value.


## **Exclusive Packs and Events**


Gold members and above get access to members-only packs and events that are not available to the general public.


These drops are reserved for Courtyard Rewards members, and your tier is what gets you in.


## **Find Everything in Your Rewards Hub**


Your[Rewards Hub](http://courtyard.io/rewards) is where you can:


- See your current tier
- Track progress toward your next tier
- Claim your monthly Free Pack
- Claim your monthly Guaranteed Value Pack
- View shipping credits


On mobile, your tier badge will also appear in the navigation bar, so your status is always visible.


## **A Few Things to Know**


Seasons run for three months.


Your tier is based on qualifying pack purchases only. Marketplace activity does not count toward tier progress.


Rewards are granted at the start of each month.


## **Ready to See Your Rewards?**


Courtyard Rewards goes live June 29. See[here](https://help.courtyard.io/en/articles/15698457-courtyard-rewards-faq) for full FAQ, and head to your[Rewards Hub](http://courtyard.io/rewards) to check your tier, track your progress, and see what’s waiting for you.
