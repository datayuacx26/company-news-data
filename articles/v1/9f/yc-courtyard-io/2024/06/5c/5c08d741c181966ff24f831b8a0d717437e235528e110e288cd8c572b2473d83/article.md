# Introducing the First-Ever CGC-Graded Comic Book Vending Machine

***TL;DR:*** *We just launched the first-ever*[comic book Vending Machine](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine/comic-pack) *with CGC — 1,200 packs at $200 each, each containing a vaulted, CGC-graded comic with full transparency and instant buyback offers.*


We’ve teamed up with[CGC](https://www.cgccomics.com/) , the leader in comic grading, to launch something the comic book world has never seen before: a[virtual vending machine](https://courtyard.io/vending-machine/comic-pack) **stocked entirely with real CGC-graded comic books** .


As of today, June 17, our new Comic Vending Machine is[officially live](https://www.cgccomics.com/) . It includes **1,200 packs** , each priced at **$200** , and each containing one CGC-graded comic. Just like our flagship experiences with graded Pokémon and sports cards, the price is set to reflect the **expected value** — some pulls will be worth more, some less, but the **average across all packs is $200** . It’s our way of keeping the experience fun *and* fair.


All comics are graded by CGC, vaulted and insured with Courtyard at no cost, and receive an instant 90% FMV buyback offer once revealed. As packs are purchased, odds and top hits update in real time, giving collectors full visibility into what they can receive.


> *“We’re thrilled to be partnering with Courtyard to make CGC-graded books more accessible, and I love the transparency of the pricing,” said* ***Matt Nelson*** *, CGC President. “When it comes to vending machines, trading cards have had all the fun for too long. I’m excited to see comics in this innovative collecting space.”*


This first drop features a curated lineup of **modern keys and vintage grails** , with highlights including Amazing Spider-Man 129, Incredible Hulk 181, and more.


*Head to Courtyard.io to see the full list of heavy hitters.*


This marks our **first major expansion beyond trading cards** , which now power over **$50 million in monthly GMV** on Courtyard. It’s part of our bigger mission to make **collectibles more accessible** — whether you’ve been collecting for decades or are just now getting into the hobby.
