# Itron Announces Participation at Upcoming Investor Conference

## Itron Announces Participation at Upcoming Investor Conference


July 23, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.itron.com/node/26906/pdf)


LIBERTY LAKE, Wash., July 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Itron, Inc. (NASDAQ: ITRI), which is innovating new ways for utilities and cities to manage energy and water, announced today that it will participate virtually in the **Oppenheimer 29th Annual Technology, Internet & Communications Conference** to be held Aug. 11, 2026.


Joan Hooper, senior vice president and chief financial officer at Itron, and Joel Vach, vice president of tax, treasury and merger, divestitures and acquisitions at Itron will participate in a fireside chat at 12:25 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time. Interested parties may listen to the chat live via webcast, which can be accessed through the company’s website at[Itron Investor Events & Presentations](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=IYuS1h-b1FdmkZLmmYo6IBaf6zd6_6La_Y2sz7ZYgkaUrNLtDueJlqX6T-HLF3HgVfGfm0t3dNbvAZRTftpAROGuwFU4m87pUkREn6j349diqqd1V0zDz0q9Qi12XT5x3drKGNQlzR4Ez1mTMRP0AQ==) .
