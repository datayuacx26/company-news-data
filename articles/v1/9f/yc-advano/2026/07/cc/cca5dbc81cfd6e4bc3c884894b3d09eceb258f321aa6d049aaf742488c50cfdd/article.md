# Advano appoints J. Christopher York as Chief Executive Officer to lead the transition to an application oriented enterprise advancing silicon-carbon composite battery materials

New Orleans, Jan. 11, 2024 —


Advano, a silicon solutions provider, is accelerating the commercialization of its proprietary silicon anode “drop-in” technology, REALSi™. The transition to an application-oriented enterprise has been made possible with the new leadership of J. Christopher York and further advancements in Advano’s silicon-carbon composite battery materials.
