# Claim Health announces $4.4m Seed Round led by Maverick Ventures

### **Claim Health raises $4.4M Seed to modernize home-based care operations**


Claim Health announced a $4.4 million Seed round led by Maverick Ventures, with participation from Peak XV, Y Combinator, and DHVP. The funding accelerates the company's mission to eliminate the administrative chaos that comes with running a home health, hospice, or home care agency.
