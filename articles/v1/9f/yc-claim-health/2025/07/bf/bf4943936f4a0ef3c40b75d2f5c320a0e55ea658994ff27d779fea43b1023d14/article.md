# Claim Health Partners with the Pennsylvania Homecare Association

Home-based care providers are navigating an increasingly complex operating environment, facing tighter reimbursement, growing administrative burden, and rising expectations for care quality. As the industry evolves, access to modern operational infrastructure has become essential for agencies of all sizes.


To support providers across the state, **Claim Health has partnered with the Pennsylvania Homecare Association (PHA)** to help home-based care organizations reduce administrative burden and get patients into care faster.


‍


### Supporting PHA Members Across Pennsylvania


The Pennsylvania Homecare Association represents hundreds of home care and home health organizations serving patients throughout the Commonwealth. Through this partnership, Claim Health will work alongside PHA to support member agencies with tools, insights, and education focused on simplifying intake workflows and improving operational visibility.


The collaboration is centered on helping providers better understand their admissions process, identify operational friction, and reduce the manual effort required to move a referral to start of care. This enables teams to focus more time on delivering high-quality care and supporting their staff.


‍


### Reducing Operational Burden in a Complex System


Despite the critical role home-based care plays in the healthcare system, many agencies continue to rely on fragmented systems and manual processes to manage referrals, documentation, and eligibility. These inefficiencies can lead to delayed admissions, incomplete patient records, and unnecessary strain on administrative teams.


Claim Health was built to help modernize these workflows through automation and real-time insights, giving providers greater clarity and control over every admission.


By partnering with PHA, Claim Health aims to make modern, automation-driven infrastructure more accessible to agencies navigating an increasingly complex operating environment.


‍


### Looking Ahead


As home-based care continues to expand, collaboration between technology providers and industry associations will be critical to ensuring agencies are equipped to operate sustainably as demand grows.


Claim Health looks forward to working with the Pennsylvania Homecare Association and its members to support more efficient, resilient, and patient-centered care delivery across Pennsylvania.
