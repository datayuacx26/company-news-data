# Claim Health Launches on Y Combinator

### Claim Health Launches on Y Combinator


Claim Health officially launched on Y Combinator, introducing its AI-powered platform designed to support at-home care providers navigating increasing operational complexity.


The launch highlights Claim Health's mission to help home health and hospice agencies modernize admissions by reducing manual work, improving operational visibility, and getting patients into care faster. As at-home care continues to grow in importance, providers are under mounting pressure from staffing shortages, fragmented referral sources, and outdated back-office systems.


Launching on Y Combinator marked an early milestone for Claim Health and underscored growing investor and industry interest in modern infrastructure for post-acute and home-based care.


### A Note from the Founders


“Home-based care is one of the most important and operationally complex parts of healthcare. We started Claim Health to give providers the modern tools they need to operate efficiently, operate sustainably, and continue delivering high-quality care.”
