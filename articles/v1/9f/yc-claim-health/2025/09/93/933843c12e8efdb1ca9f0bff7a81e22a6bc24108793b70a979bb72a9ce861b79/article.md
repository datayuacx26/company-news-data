# Claim Health CEO Kevin Calcado Named a 2025 Future Leader by Home Health Care News

### Recognizing the next generation of leaders shaping home-based care


Home Health Care News named Kevin Calcado, Co-Founder & CEO of Claim Health, as a 2025 Future Leader, recognizing emerging executives under 40 who are helping shape the future of home health and home care.


The annual Future Leaders program highlights individuals driving meaningful progress across the industry — from care delivery and operations to technology, reimbursement, and leadership. Honorees are selected based on their impact, vision, and commitment to advancing home-based care.


Kevin was recognized for his work building Claim Health, an automation platform designed to help home health and hospice agencies manage the growing administrative complexity of getting patients into care. Claim Health aims to reduce administrative burden while getting patients on service faster.


In the feature, Kevin shares perspectives on why home-based care remains underserved by modern technology, the importance of understanding frontline workflows, and how automation will be essential as agencies face tighter margins and increasing payer complexity.


This recognition reflects a broader shift across the industry: financial resilience and operational excellence are becoming critical to sustaining high-quality care.


‍


### A Note from the Founders


“It’s an honor to be recognized by Home Health Care News alongside so many leaders who care deeply about this industry,” said Kevin Calcado, Co-Founder & CEO of Claim Health. “Home-based care providers are under immense operational pressure, and we believe thoughtful, operator-first technology can help them focus more time and resources on patient care.”
