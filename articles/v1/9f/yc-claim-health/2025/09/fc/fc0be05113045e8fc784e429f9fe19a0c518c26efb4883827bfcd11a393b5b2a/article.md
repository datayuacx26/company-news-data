# Claim Health listed as one of Sapphire Ventures’ “80 Hottest AI Companies in Healthcare”

### The AI Healthcare Stack: Sapphire Ventures on a Generational Shift


In a major new market map of the AI healthcare landscape, Sapphire Ventures identifies 80 standout companies driving transformation across the sector. Claim Health is featured under the Denials & Underpayments category—recognised for tackling one of the most costly and error-prone stages of the revenue cycle.


The article outlines three major areas where AI is making a generational impact: activating siloed healthcare data, automating repetitive admin workflows, and augmenting clinicians through agentic AI. Sapphire also explores how startups like Claim Health are delivering measurable ROI through faster reimbursements, reduced denials, and less manual rework.


This is a strong signal that AI-first RCM is moving from edge-case to essential infrastructure.


‍


### A Note from the Founders


“We were delighted to see Claim featured in Sapphire’s market map alongside so many ambitious teams,” said Kevin Calcado and JJ Ram, co-founders of Claim Health. “It’s validating to see the problem of denials and underpayments get real attention—and even more so to be recognised for the impact we’re having in solving it with automation.”
