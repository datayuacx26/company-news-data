# Claim Health Featured by Every.io on AI Transforming Post-Acute Care Operations

### Transforming Post-Acute Care Operations with the Power of AI


Every.io featured an in-depth look at how artificial intelligence is reshaping operational workflows across post-acute care. The article explores how modern AI tools are being applied to some of the most complex and manual areas of healthcare operations, including billing, eligibility, authorizations, and claims management.


Many post-acute care providers — including home health, hospice, and skilled nursing organizations — continue to rely on fragmented systems and labor-intensive processes that slow reimbursement and create operational risk. As reimbursement pressure increases and administrative teams are stretched thin, AI-driven automation is emerging as a critical lever for efficiency and financial resilience.


The piece highlights Claim Health as an example of how AI can be used to automate core revenue cycle workflows, reduce manual rework, and surface real-time operational insights. By integrating with EMRs, clearinghouses, and payer systems, AI-first platforms are helping providers streamline back-office operations while improving speed to cash.


The article also emphasizes the importance of building technology grounded in real-world workflows — ensuring automation complements, rather than complicates, the day-to-day realities of post-acute care operations.


### A Note from the Founders


“Post-acute care providers are under immense operational pressure, and AI has the potential to meaningfully reduce the administrative burden they face every day. When built thoughtfully, automation can help teams operate more efficiently while keeping the focus where it belongs — on patient care.”
