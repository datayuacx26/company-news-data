# Claim Health Partners with the National Alliance for Care at Home

The home-based care industry is undergoing significant transformation. Agencies nationwide are contending with policy shifts, workforce constraints, and financial pressures that require new approaches to how care is delivered and sustained.


To help providers adapt to this changing landscape, Claim Health has partnered with the National Alliance for Care at Home (the Alliance) to support member organizations with resources and technology that address operational challenges.


‍


### Collaborating with the Industry's National Voice


The National Alliance for Care at Home represents more than 10,000 home health, hospice, palliative care, and home care organizations across the United States. As the unified voice for providers serving millions of Americans at all stages of life, the Alliance plays a central role in shaping policy, advancing standards, and connecting agencies with the resources they need to succeed.


Through this partnership, Claim Health will work with the Alliance to provide member agencies with practical tools, educational resources, and operational guidance focused on building stronger back-office capabilities and achieving greater operational predictability.


‍


### Strengthening Operational Foundations Across the Continuum


Agencies of every size and specialty face common challenges: administrative complexity, limited visibility into performance, and the need to do more with constrained resources. For many organizations, addressing these issues requires not just incremental improvements but fundamental shifts in how operations are managed.


This partnership is designed to give Alliance members greater access to technology and expertise that can help modernize legacy workflows, surface actionable insights, and create more sustainable operating models—without requiring wholesale organizational change or significant capital investment.


‍


### Moving Forward Together


The Alliance's commitment to advancing the home-based care industry aligns closely with Claim Health's mission to help agencies operate more effectively and confidently.


Claim Health looks forward to supporting the National Alliance for Care at Home and its member community as they navigate the opportunities and challenges ahead.
