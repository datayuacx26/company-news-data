# Claim Health Partners with The Home Care Association of Florida

Florida is home to one of the largest and most diverse home care ecosystems in the country. Providers across the state serve a wide range of patient populations while navigating unique regulatory, operational, and reimbursement dynamics that continue to evolve year over year.


To support providers operating in this environment, Claim Health has partnered with The Home Care Association of Florida (HCAF) to help member organizations strengthen their operational foundation and adopt more modern approaches to managing admissions.


‍


### Supporting a Growing and Diverse Provider Community


The Home Care Association of Florida represents a broad network of home care and home health organizations dedicated to advancing quality, compliance, and sustainability across the state. Through this partnership, Claim Health will work with HCAF to support members through education, shared insights, and practical resources focused on improving back-office operations.


Rather than prescribing one-size-fits-all solutions, the collaboration is designed to help providers better understand how operational workflows impact the pace and quality of care, especially as agencies scale across regions, payers, and service lines.


‍


### Enabling Smarter Operations Through Visibility and Automation


As agencies grow, manual processes around referrals, documentation, and eligibility become increasingly difficult to manage. Limited visibility into these workflows often leads to delayed admissions, increased administrative workload, and operational blind spots.


Claim Health works with providers to bring greater transparency and automation to these workflows, helping teams identify inefficiencies and make more informed operational decisions. Through the partnership with HCAF, these capabilities and insights will be more accessible to providers seeking to modernize their operations without adding complexity.


‍


### A Shared Focus on Education and Long-Term Sustainability


A core part of this partnership is a shared commitment to education and long-term sustainability for Florida's home care providers. By combining HCAF's deep industry expertise with Claim Health's operational perspective, the partnership aims to help agencies better prepare for ongoing change across reimbursement, regulation, and care delivery.


Claim Health looks forward to working with The Home Care Association of Florida and its members to support more informed, resilient, and scalable home care operations across the state.
