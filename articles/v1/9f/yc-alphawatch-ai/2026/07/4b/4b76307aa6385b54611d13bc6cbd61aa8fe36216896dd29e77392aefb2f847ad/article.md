# Kratos (KTOS) Announces $400M in New Government Funding

Another notable development in the defense robotics space this week:


Kratos Defense (NASDAQ: KTOS) announced it has received


**approximately $400 million in new U.S. government funding** for hypersonic systems and other national security programs.


According to management, the funding is expected to:


-


Accelerate program execution


-


Support organic growth


-


Improve cash flow by reducing working capital tied to inventory and receivables


This follows another announcement just one day earlier of an approximately


**$100 million production contract** for a ground-based space domain awareness system.


Taken together, it's another reminder that investment in autonomous systems, hypersonics, space, and next-generation defense technologies continues to accelerate.
