Q2 instruments and accessories revenue increased 18% to $1.73 billion, making it roughly 60% of total quarterly revenue.


15% da Vinci procedure growth and 36% Ion procedure growth.


Intuitive placed 468 da Vinci systems during Q2, including 246 da Vinci 5 systems. A year earlier, it placed 395 systems, including 180 da Vinci 5 systems. The worldwide da Vinci installed base has now reached approximately 11,710 systems.


Worldwide da Vinci procedure growth was approximately 15%, compared with roughly 17% in Q2 2025 and 16% in Q1 2026.


Management maintained its full-year 2026 da Vinci procedure-growth outlook at 13.5%–15.5% and expects results around the midpoint. Management did improve its 2026 non-GAAP gross-margin outlook to 68%–69% from its previous expectation of 67.5%–68.5%.
