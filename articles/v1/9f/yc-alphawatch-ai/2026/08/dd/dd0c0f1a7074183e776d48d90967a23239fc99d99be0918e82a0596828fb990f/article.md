Palladyne added approximately $13 million of new contract awards during Q2, allowing backlog to grow 43% even after recognizing a record quarter of revenue.


Management expects the majority of that backlog to convert to revenue over the next 12–18 months.


Palladyne reiterated full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $24M–$27M


That would represent approximately 357%–415% year-over-year growth from $5.2 million in 2025.


Management also expects revenue to increase sequentially again in Q3 as backlog converts and new programs ramp.


The company maintained expected 2026 operating cash burn of $32M–$36M, or an average of approximately $8M–$9M per quarter.


Management expects cash usage to decline through the remainder of the year as revenue and margins increase.

##


One Operator. Four Drone Manufacturers


The SwarmOS demonstration may be particularly important to the longer-term robotics story.


During the Army's Ivy Mass exercise, Palladyne integrated SwarmOS into the Army's next-generation command-and-control ecosystem.


The system enabled one operator to control drones from four different manufacturers, including Palladyne's Gremlin-X.


Palladyne remains an early-stage company, and losses and cash consumption remain significant.
