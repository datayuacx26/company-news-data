# Palladyne (PDYN) Makes a Major Move Through Strategic Partnership with Israel Aerospace Industries

On


**June 8** , Palladyne announced a strategic partnership with Israel Aerospace Industries (IAI) under which Palladyne will have exclusive U.S. rights to manufacture, integrate, and market IAI's:


-


HARPY


-


HAROP


-


Mini HARPY


loitering munition systems ("kamikaze drones") to the U.S. government. The agreement includes U.S.-based manufacturing and integration activities and positions Palladyne to pursue Pentagon programs requiring domestically produced systems.


###


he HARPY and HAROP are:


-


Combat-proven systems


-


Already fielded internationally


-


Designed for suppression/destruction of enemy air defenses (SEAD)


-


Relevant to several current U.S. military modernization priorities


The significance is that Palladyne is moving from being viewed primarily as an AI/autonomy software company into a company with potential exposure to:


-


Autonomous weapons systems


-


Loitering munitions


-


U.S. defense procurement programs


-


Domestic defense manufacturing


Breaking Defense reported that Palladyne intends to integrate its autonomy/swarming software into these platforms and pursue future U.S. programs.


What investors should remember:


-


No government contract was announced.


-


No revenue value was disclosed.


-


The partnership creates opportunity, but future economic value depends on actual procurement awards and program adoption.


-


The announcement does, however, significantly elevate Palladyne's strategic position within the rapidly growing defense-autonomy ecosystem.
