# Interface Journal Features a Presentation by Marco Santoro, Manager of Global FM Applications

On-board top-of-rail friction modifiers are transforming rail operations by reducing wear, noise, and fuel costs while simplifying maintenance compared to traditional wayside systems. Marco's presentation provided real-world results and explained why railroads and transit systems are increasingly adopting this technology.


L.B. Foster's[KELTRACK® On-Board (KOB)](https://lbfoster.com/rail/friction-management/friction-management-products/top-of-rail-and-wheel-tread-friction-management/keltrack-on-board) is a vehicle mounted Top-of-Rail (TOR) spray application system that dispenses an atomized stream of KELTRACK® Friction Modifier (KELTRACK® AP EX) to the railhead.
