# L.B. Foster’s Subsidiary, CXT ® Inc., Manufactured Precast Concrete Restrooms for the Cherokee Nation

The Cherokee Nation Reservation is a 7,000 square-mile reservation headquartered in Tahlequah, OK, that spans 14 counties in the northeastern corner of Oklahoma. The many parks, community centers, schools, ball fields, and other sites on the reservation needed safe and sanitary public restrooms for citizens to enjoy.[CXT’s precast concrete buildings](https://lbfoster.com/infrastructure/precast-concrete-buildings/about-cxt-buildings) were able to meet the needs of the reservation and enhance the locations in which they were installed. This year, CXT supplied 15 restroom buildings to the Reservation and installed eight in 2023. The buildings provided included both flush and vault restrooms, and two of the restrooms also included concession areas.


CXT manufactures precast concrete restroom, shower, concession, utility, and multipurpose[buildings](https://lbfoster.com/infrastructure/precast-concrete-buildings/about-cxt-buildings) that are simple to install, ready to use, and easy to maintain.
