# L.B. Foster Sponsored UPMC Children's Hospital Foundation's Walk for Children's 2026

Back row (L-R) Bill Thalman, Executive VP & COO, Mark, and Bella. Front row (L-R) Alex, Diane, Heather, and Jack the family dog.


The annual walk is[UPMC Children’s Hospital Foundation’s](https://secure.givetochildrens.org/page/72738/donate/1?ea.tracking.id=donate-redirect) largest fundraising event bringing together healthcare workers, families, friends, and community members to support children’s health through fundraising and awareness. L.B. Foster employees, and their families and friends, were proud to take part in a day focused on making a difference for children and families in our community.


Bryan McSorley, Senior Manager, Inside Sales - Rail, shared, “This was my family's first time participating in the walk and it was such a pleasant surprise. The event had such a friendly and positive atmosphere and there was so much for my young children to do and see.”


Bryan continued, “The event highlighted what the community is doing to support children in need. My nephew, Isaac, has been receiving treatment for Leukemia there for a few months now and it was comforting knowing that he and kids like him have so much support.”


We are proud to support organizations that make a difference in our community. For more information about Walk for Children's visit:[Walk for Children's 2026 - UPMC Children's Hospital Foundation](https://fundraise.givetochildrens.org/site/TR/WalkforChildrens/WalkforChildrens2026?pg=entry&fr_id=1270&gclsrc=aw.ds&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=1020274751&gclid=Cj0KCQjw0JnRBhDJARIsALobnXY2ad23hgARlr8YsrXqMDkyxFKEMj-6x3ZNqsYJeSA80s2Q0bPKtNoaAjt6EALw_wcB) .
