# Watch: Introducing the L.B. Foster 320' Rail Consist

Transported using a five-car consist, this innovative rail solution eliminates the need for field welding while offering greater installation flexibility and significant cost savings. Unlike traditional 80' rail or continuous welded rail, the 320' can move in manifest freight traffic - delivering efficiency without the need for dedicated trains.
