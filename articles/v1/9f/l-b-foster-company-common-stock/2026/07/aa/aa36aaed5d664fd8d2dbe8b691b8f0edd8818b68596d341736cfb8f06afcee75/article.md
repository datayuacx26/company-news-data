# CXT® Inc. Precast Concrete Buildings Powered by ANATOMY™

ANATOMY Asset Intelligence™ is a centralized, cloud-based technology platform that powers connected infrastructure solutions through real-time visibility, remote asset management, and remote-control capabilities. Serving as a shared intelligence layer across multiple products, ANATOMY connects devices and data in a single system, turning operational information into actionable insights that help improve asset performance, streamline maintenance, and support more efficient operations.


This offering combines ANATOMY’s secure, cloud‑based platform with CXT’s precast concrete structures and purpose‑built, factory‑integrated hardware engineered by CXT to deliver remote monitoring, access control, and operational visibility for distributed public facilities. As a factory‑integrated smart building solution, CXT Buildings Powered by ANATOMY enables true turnkey deployment, with monitoring and access control fully operational at the time of installation.


Public agencies responsible for remote restroom operations often face challenges related to limited visibility, staff constraints, and high travel costs. CXT buildings powered by ANATOMY addresses these challenges by enabling operators to securely monitor facility status, control access, including the ability to schedule lock and unlock times, and respond to operational and security events without requiring constant site visits.


The ANATOMY platform connects facility hardware that captures key operational signals such as lock state and occupancy. Secure cellular connectivity transmits signals to a centralized cloud application, where authorized users can view live facility status, receive notifications, and remotely lock or unlock facilities as needed.


Field personnel can access the ANATOMY portal through a mobile-friendly interface, allowing teams to acknowledge alerts, coordinate response efforts, and resolve incidents from the field or the office. This shared operational view supports faster response times, improved routing efficiency, and greater accountability across distributed sites.


CXT precast concrete buildings equipped with ANATOMY are designed for organizations managing one or more remote public facilities where travel time, operating costs, and security risks are significant. Target users include local, state, and federal parks departments, public works and utilities organizations, transit and aviation authorities, and tourism and recreation operators overseeing public-facing sites.


By integrating ANATOMY’s real-time intelligence into CXT buildings, public agencies can reduce unnecessary site visits, enhance safety and security through controlled access, improve facility uptime, and support compliance with time-stamped activity logs and consistent reporting.


This launch further extends L.B. Foster’s commitment to delivering durable, performance-driven infrastructure solutions. By combining CXT’s proven facility designs with Anatomy’s digital platform, CXT precast concrete buildings powered by ANATOMY transforms public restroom facilities into connected, intelligent assets designed to support efficient operations, lower operating costs through reduces site visits and improved resource utilization and deliver long-term performance.
