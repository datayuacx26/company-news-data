## Canadian Pacific Railway: Delivering Millions in Annual Savings


One of the most comprehensive studies of friction management was undertaken by **Canadian Pacific Railway (CPR)** .


By applying lubricant to the wheel-rail interface, particularly on the gauge face of the high rail in curves, CPR significantly reduced friction and curving resistance. The result was simple but powerful: locomotives required less energy to move trains.


### Additional Benefits Through Top-of-Rail Friction Management


The implementation of advanced[Top-of-Rail (TOR) friction modifiers](https://lbfoster.com/en-gb/rail/friction-management/friction-management-products/top-of-rail-and-wheel-tread-friction-management/trackside-top-of-rail-friction-management) delivered even greater gains, generating an additional **13%–28% reduction in fuel consumption** on test sections.

## British Columbia Railway: Greater Curvature, Greater Opportunity


A comprehensive study conducted by **British Columbia Railway Company** demonstrated a clear link between track curvature and the fuel-saving benefits of friction management.


The findings showed that as curve density increased, so did the opportunity to reduce fuel consumption.


### Fuel Savings Achieved


Up to 2.3% fuel savings on tangent track


Up to 10.5% fuel savings on routes with higher curve density


For railways operating through mountainous terrain, heavy-haul corridors or networks with frequent curves, friction management represents a significant opportunity to unlock further operating efficiencies.


## Shuohuang Railway: Rapid Return on Investment


Covering a **1,176 km round-trip corridor** between Shenchi South and Huanghua Port, Shuohuang Railway undertook a field trial to evaluate the impact of friction management technology on locomotive energy consumption.


The results were striking.


Data collected throughout the programme demonstrated an average reduction in locomotive energy consumption of **6.9%** .


Following the trial, L.B. Foster conducted a detailed cost-benefit analysis based on deploying friction management technology across **33 locomotives** .

### Project Payback in Less Than One Year
