# RevenueCat and AB180 partner to support South Korea’s app community

South Korean app users are among the highest-spending mobile users in the world, with the average person[spending $143 per year](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.businessofapps.com/data/south-korea-app-market/&sa=D&source=docs&ust=1786527396341915&usg=AOvVaw0F4t-_bmSKgMrKnYCU8nDj) on apps. Now, developers in South Korea have dedicated support to capture more of that — more revenue, more retained subscribers, faster growth.


[We've partnered with AB180](http://revenuecat.com/ab180) to give South Korean developers hands-on local support and help getting the most out of RevenueCat's tools, backed by a team that already works with some of South Korea's biggest apps and games like NAVER WEBTOON, MUSINSA, Amorepacific, and Nexon.


AB180 is a South Korea-based marketing technology company with over 10 years’ experience in MarTech and AdTech, and the team behind the Airbridge attribution platform. The partnership makes it easier for South Korean app developers to get local support, expert guidance, and access to world-class tools for managing and optimizing in-app subscriptions.


## Local expertise, backed by a global platform


AB180 will support all RevenueCat customers in South Korea, from first integration through to scaling monetization, at no extra cost. It's included for every RevenueCat customer in the region.


If you're building in South Korea, here's what AB180 can help with:


- Setting up and integrating RevenueCat across iOS, Android, and the Web
- Moving over from another provider or your own in-house system
- Digging into your performance data to understand what's working and where you stand against benchmarks
- Rolling out RevenueCat's growth tools: paywall builder, web-to-app funnels, and experiments
- Joining a growing network of local developers building and scaling subscription apps


With this step, RevenueCat is continuing to expand its[global partner network](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/company/revenuecat-turkiye-partnership/) and empowering developers everywhere to monetize smarter, scale faster, and focus on building apps users love.
