# X Best Airtable Alternatives: Competitors Worth Considering in 2026

Airtable stands out as a valuable relational database management tool. However, as projects expand, users may find it challenging to manage increasing data complexities. This article explores potential limitations of Airtable and provides a curated list of 14 alternatives and competitors that cater to various project management needs.


## Airtable's Limitations


Despite its effectiveness as best project management software and a digital organizer, Airtable may pose challenges for users dealing with extensive projects. Notable shortcomings include limited project management features, collaboration hurdles, the need for paid extensions, shortcomings in reviewing features, and cost concerns for larger teams.


### Limited Project Management Features


While Airtable excels at organizing data, it falls short in managing comprehensive projects. Key project management features, which prioritize tasks such as project planning, time tracking, and critical path analysis, are noticeably absent, requiring advanced database skills to implement.


### Collaboration Challenges


Collaborating on Airtable can be likened to solving a puzzle. Real-time collaboration is possible, but each database has its collaboration and comment limits. This can hinder seamless teamwork and communication within the platform.


### Paid Extensions and Budget Constraints


Airtable offers various work views, but some, like Gantt view and timeline view, are available only in higher-priced plans. The basic plan limits the number of extensions per base, which may restrict users, particularly those with a budget, from accessing essential tools for note-taking, content creation, and project reporting.


### Reviewing Features


Airtable may not be the optimal choice for tasks involving reviewing images, texts, or creative assets. Its limitations in handling visual and interactive content can pose challenges for design teams and other creatives-heavy users.


### Expense for Larger Teams


While Airtable is suitable for solo users with its free package, it becomes expensive for larger teams. Starting at $20 per user per month, the costs can accumulate rapidly for businesses planning to scale in the future.
