## How the Base44 credit system works (message vs integration credits)


Base44 does not price by seats. Instead, it uses a dual credit system that governs both building and running ai apps. Understanding how credits work is essential for accurate budgeting.


Message credits are consumed when prompting the AI to build or edit applications - scaffolding a new app from natural language prompts, modifying UI layouts, debugging logic, or iterating on features. Complex or longer prompts consume more credits. These message credits effectively cap how much AI-assisted development you can do per billing period.


Integration credits are spent when your deployed app triggers live actions: making LLM or API calls, sending emails, generating images, processing file uploads, or running backend workflows. Standard database reads and writes generally do not consume integration credits. Each integration action typically costs one integration credit. A complex app may use 50–100 integration credits per active user monthly, depending on how AI-heavy the features are.


Key behaviors to keep in mind:


- Credits reset each billing cycle. For the free plan, message credits reset daily (up to 5/day). For paid tiers, both message and integration credits reset monthly.
- Unused credits do not roll over to the next billing cycle. Over-provisioning wastes budget.
- When either credit type runs out mid-month, relevant functionality pauses until the next billing cycle or a plan upgrade.

Built for high-traffic production apps, ai platforms, and teams that need more credits without interruption. The elite plan may also offer sub-levels with adjustable credit caps.

Many evaluators wonder whether Base44's free plan is sufficient to validate the platform. The answer depends on your pace and ambitions.

The free plan's daily message credit cap (5 per day, up to 25/month) and tiny integration allotment (100/month) tend to stall progress once you start iterating seriously. Most active builders outgrow it within days rather than weeks.


The Starter plan at $16/month billed annually is better suited for continuous learning, small internal utilities, and personal tools where you need enough message credits to iterate and enough integration credits to demo to a handful of users. It also unlocks app code edits and unlimited apps.


Rules of thumb:

- If you are simply exploring whether Base44 fits your workflow, the free plan is the right plan for a weekend test - but do not expect to ship complete apps on it.

## Total cost of ownership: what actually drives your Base44 bill


The list price for a base44 plan is only part of the story. Your real-world spend depends on how heavily you build with the AI, how many live users you have, and whether you rely on external APIs.


Key cost drivers beyond the monthly price:


- **Message credit burn during development.** Frequent prompt-based changes, experimentation, and debugging consume message credits fast. If you are developing complex applications, you may exhaust Starter's 100 message credits before finishing a first version.
- **Integration credit consumption from user traffic.** Every time your live app makes an LLM call, sends an email, processes a file upload, or triggers an automation, it will consume integration credits. Integration credits can deplete quickly with active user interactions - especially if your app is AI-heavy.
- **External API costs.** If your app calls OpenAI, Anthropic, or transactional email providers, those vendors bill you separately. Base44's integration credits cover orchestration, not third-party usage fees.
- **Domain renewals.** Builder and above include a free custom domain for the first year on annual billing, but renewal costs after that year are your responsibility.


Base44 does not charge per seat, which can be attractive for teams with many non-technical users. But heavy credit usage can still push you into a higher plan. The fact that credits do not roll over means choosing the wrong plan - either too high or too low - wastes budget.


To estimate your annual budget, map expected credit usage to plan allowances. For example: a small internal tool with 10 staff using 100 integration actions each per month needs ~1,000 integration credits/month (Starter suffices). A customer-facing AI agent with 200 active users at 50 actions each needs ~10,000/month (Builder minimum). These are conservative planning assumptions, not guarantees, so always leave headroom for hidden costs you may not anticipate.

### How does Base44's annual billing discount actually work?


Base44's annual plans reduce the effective monthly price by roughly 20% compared to paying month-to-month. For example, the Starter plan costs $16/month billed annually versus $20 on a monthly plan. Builder runs $40 versus $50, Pro is $80 versus $100, and Elite is $160 versus $200. Buyers pay for the full year up front. If Base44 supports switching from monthly to annual mid-cycle, the system typically prorates remaining time, but confirm exact details in Base44's current[billing documentation](https://docs.base44.com/Account-and-billing/Billing-and-plans?utm_source=openai) .


### Can I downgrade or cancel my Base44 plan without losing my apps?


Generally, when you cancel or downgrade, your apps and data are preserved, but access to features like higher credit allowances, custom domains, and premium support may be lost. Annual plans are usually not refunded mid-term, while a monthly plan allows more flexible cancellation at the end of the billing period. Monthly billing is safer while you are still evaluating Base44.


### Does Base44 offer student, startup, or nonprofit discounts?


As of July 2026, Base44 does not prominently advertise student or nonprofit discounts, though some reports suggest academic email holders may receive ad-hoc discounts. Reach out directly to Base44 with details about your use case, as many SaaS vendors occasionally grant credits or reduced pricing even when not listed on their pricing page.


### What happens when I run out of Base44 message or integration credits?


When message credits are exhausted, you cannot send new prompts to the AI app builder until the next billing cycle or a plan upgrade. When integration credits run out, live app actions that depend on integrations may fail or be throttled. Monitor your credit dashboards closely, and stage rollouts to avoid being forced into unplanned upgrades mid-month.


### How do Base44 credits relate to external API costs like OpenAI or email services?


Base44's integration credits cover the platform's orchestration work, but they do not replace third-party vendors' own fees. Using OpenAI, transactional email services, or other external APIs will generate separate bills from those providers. Factor both Base44 subscription costs and expected external API spend into your total cost estimates, especially for AI-heavy apps making frequent LLM calls. Policies and pricing can change, so always verify current terms directly on Base44's website before making a long-term commitment.
