# Upgrading Product Onboarding

When it comes to crafting exceptional product onboarding experiences, there's no need to reinvent the wheel for the sake of reinventing the wheel. We can learn a ton just by studying other products and learning from their growth experiments and product decisions.


That's why we initially launched ProductOnboarding.com as a curated visual library showcasing the best onboarding examples from across the web. Today, we're thrilled to announce a major redesign of our website and new features that make it an even more invaluable resource for product managers, designers, marketers, and engineers.


## What's New?


### Fresh Design and Improved Navigation


We've given ProductOnboarding.com a complete makeover. The new design not only looks great but also enhances usability, making it easier than ever to find the inspiration and resources you need. With a more visual layout and improved navigation, you can quickly browse, filter, and sort through our library of onboarding examples.


### New Article Section


We're expanding our offerings with a brand-new article section. Here, you'll find insights, tips, and best practices from our team and industry experts on how to create effective onboarding experiences. Whether you're looking to improve user activation, drive feature adoption, or enhance overall user experience, our articles will provide you with the knowledge you need to succeed.


### Component Overviews


In addition to showcasing onboarding examples, we're now offering detailed overviews of key components used in these experiences. This new section will help you understand how different elements work together to create seamless onboarding flows. Each component overview includes practical advice and real-world examples to guide your implementation.


### Ongoing Updates and Resources


Our commitment to providing you with the best resources doesn't stop here. We're continually updating our library with new examples, making it easier for you to replicate successful patterns. Plus, with our new article and component sections, you'll have even more tools to support your product onboarding journey.


## Let us know what you think!


We hope you explore our redesigned site, dive into our new content, and share your feedback with us. Your insights are invaluable as we continue to improve it. Visit ProductOnboarding.com today to discover the latest patterns in product onboarding.
