# Anna McDonough joins Frigade

We are thrilled to welcome Anna McDonough, our first intern, to the Frigade team! She's with us for the summer before starting her MBA at the Stanford Graduate School of Business.


Anna has a background in management consulting for Deloitte, where she led change and project management for enterprise-wide technology implementations. She's enthusiastic about human-centered design and excited to help shape the Frigade go-to-market and sales strategies.


Outside of work, Anna loves to bake and is a volunteer counselor for the Crisis Text Line.


Welcome, Anna!
