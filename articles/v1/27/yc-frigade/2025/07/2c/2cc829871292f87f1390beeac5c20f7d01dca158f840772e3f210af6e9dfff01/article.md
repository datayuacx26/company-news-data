# Elton Rego joins Frigade

We are delighted to welcome Elton (Elty) Rego to the Frigade engineering team!


Elty is a seasoned full-stack engineer with experience in building user interfaces and service architecture for teams at LinkedIn, Gantri, and Veeva. He's got a passion for building products from the ground up and is excited to help scale Frigade.


Outside of work, he likes to restore old cars and is the current Young Member Chair on the Board of Directors for Porsche Club of America's Sacramento Valley Region.


Welcome to the team, Elty!
