# Sunsetting Dolphin

Dolphin is sunsetting. Our coding agent approach is obsolete with the rise of text-controlled remote computers (OpenClaw). The AI coding world moves quickly, and we need to evolve with it. New project creation has been disabled starting today.


## What This Means


- **New projects** have been disabled starting today.
- **Existing customers** will continue to be supported for as long as they need to successfully create and deploy their apps. We are committed to continuing to support our customers.


## Thank You


We launched Dolphin with the vision of putting a startup in a box. We wanted to make it possible for anyone with an idea to ship a real product to their first customers in an hour. We are incredibly grateful to everyone who believed in that vision and built with us.


Thank you to the 200 users and 360 projects that you all have created since we launched in November.
