# Mobile view - Relate Changelog

Today, we are launching our first mobile view through PWAs (Progressive Web App). With our mobile view, you can access customer data and history on the go. Most data and features will be accessible.


## How it works


With the new mobile view, you can access organization, contact, and deal data, read notes, check email, and update deals.


## How to install Relate mobile PWA app


Load[Relate](https://app.relate.so/) in your smartphone browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.) and click \`...\` or \`Share\` and \`Add to homescreen\`.


Once the site is installed on your home screen, you can use it as a mobile app. Sign in with your ID. It will keep your login details even after you exit.
