# Send emails later - Relate Changelog

You can now schedule emails. Click the dropdown icon next to` Send` and choose the date and time.


We've created popular choices like 'tomorrow morning,' 'tomorrow afternoon,' and next 'Monday morning.' You can also customize your schedule.


### Other improvements


-


Faster loading speed - experience much faster loading speed across the app.


-


In-app settings moved to the application. Workspace-level settings like Billing, Import, Members are still in your[Admin console](https://admin.relate.so/)


-


Various bug fixes
