# Slack integration - Relate Changelog

You can now get Relate notifications via Slack. Our Slack integration has two major parts: team and personal notifications.


**Team notification**


-


Receive notifications for activities in your workspace.


-


Customize and select Slack public channels for notifications.


**Personal notification**


-


Receive personal notifications in your Inbox.


-


Emails are excluded from the notification.


### How it works


Go to your Workspace Settings and find \`Team notifications\` under Workspace and \`Notifications\` under Account. Then, connect your Slack workspace and customize each notification.
