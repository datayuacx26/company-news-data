# File Management - Relate Changelog

### Overview


You can now manage files you share with your customers.


### How It Works


You can easily upload, download, and update every file you share with your customers at organization detail page.


Simply click on the 'Upload file' button to upload PDFs, Words, PPTs, and many more.


For each file, you can view the file type, uploaded time, and the uploader.
