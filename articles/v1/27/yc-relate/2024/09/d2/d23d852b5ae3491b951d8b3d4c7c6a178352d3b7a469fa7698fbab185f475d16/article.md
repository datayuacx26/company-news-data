# Integrate with Google contacts in Personal workspace - Relate Changelog

## Overview


You can now integrate Google Contacts in your personal workspace. This makes it easier to connect and manage your personal contacts in Relate.


## How it works


### How to get started


1.


Go to your personal workspace admin page. (in the app, tap` My workspace` and go to` Manage workspace` ) (or on the web, access[the admin page](https://admin.relate.so/) and select Personal workspace)


2.


Under the Workspace section, in Integrations,` select Google and click Connect` .


3.


Select the account you want to integrate with, sign in to Google, agree to provide permissions, and the integration is complete.


## 2. Quality upgrade


We've also been working on improving Relate's product quality.


1.


We've improved` the Sequences tab on the Organization detail page` to let you to see sequences centered on contacts.


2.


Sequences can be added from` individual contacts` in an Organization via` 'Add to sequence'` .


### Added Reporting weekly option


The` weekly option` has been added` to the column chart by x axis` on the Reporting page to allow you to view data on a weekly basis.
