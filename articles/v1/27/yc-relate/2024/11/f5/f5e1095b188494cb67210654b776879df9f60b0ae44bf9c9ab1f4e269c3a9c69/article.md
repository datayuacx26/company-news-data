# Campaigns - Relate Changelog

## Overview


Relate Campaigns is a simple email marketing tool for B2B marketing teams. You can use Campaigns to send marketing emails to prospects and customers, or share company news.

### Relate campaigns


Here are three ways we're different from traditional email marketing tools.


-


Email creation and sending is simple. Create beautiful emails like Notion and send them to your customers quickly and easily.


-


**Email Deliverability** is easy to take care of. Email Deliverability reduces your chances of being labeled as spam. (How to set it up:[Domain record setup guide](https://www.relate.kr/docs/marketing/dns-records) )


-


You can unify sales and marketing data. Since Relate CRM integrates both sales and marketing functions, it enables seamless customer management.
