# Upgrade your Relate experience - Relate Changelog

## Overview


This update includes UX changes and new features across the app. We've only included the major UX improvements and features in this changelog.


## UX Update


We've updated the overall UX of the app, including the sidebar, inbox, hyperthread notifications, and more. We've also added new features like notifications settings and mail merge.


### New & Improvements


-


New: Removed existing views section and added` Views menu to sidebar`


-


New: Added` Notifications settings page`


-


New:` Mail merge` is now available


-


Improved: Emails notifications in inbox are now contact-centered


-


Improved: Support for contacts without organization (when adding a contact, change organization to optional)


-


Improved: When hyperthread notifications in Inbox, participants can now be viewed in the right sidebar and added to contact


-


Improved: You can now see the number of contacts in the Organization column


-


Improved: In List/Process, custom fields in organization/contact can be viewed in customize columns and sort/filter
