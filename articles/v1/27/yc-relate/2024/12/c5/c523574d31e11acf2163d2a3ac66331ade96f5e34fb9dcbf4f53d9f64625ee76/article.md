# Smarter B2B Sales Data Management with AI

## **Managing Customer Data Shouldn't Be This Hard**


B2B sales teams know the struggle of managing customer data. The process is tedious, time-consuming, and easy to overlook. Because we rely too much on manual updates, important details often get missed, and your CRM ends up filled with outdated, incomplete, or inaccurate information.


That’s where Relate comes in.


Relate’s latest AI feature,[AI field suggestions](https://www.relate.so/docs/features/ai-suggestions?ref=relate.so) , simplifies data management for B2B sales teams.


Just write your sales meeting[notes](https://www.relate.so/docs/features/notes?ref=relate.so) , and Relate automatically extracts key details and recommends updates to your CRM fields. With a single click, you can keep your CRM accurate, complete, and ready to drive action.


In this post, we’ll show you how to:


- Structure your CRM fields effectively.
- Use Relate’s AI to automate[custom field](https://www.relate.so/docs/features/custom-fields?ref=relate.so) updates from your meeting notes during discovery calls.


---


## **How to Automate CRM Updates**


### **1. Focus on the CRM data that matters**


Your CRM can track endless amounts of data, but what really drives results?


The key is to focus on data that drives your sales process—details that help you qualify leads, understand their needs, and close deals.


Use proven frameworks like[BANT](https://www.relate.so/blog/what-is-bant/) (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeframe) or[MEDDIC](https://www.relate.so/blog/what-is-meddic/) (Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion) during[discovery calls](https://www.relate.so/blog/discovery-call/) to capture the right information.


Instead of letting this valuable information get buried in notes, turn it into structured fields you can track and analyze. Having the right fields in place makes it easier to find what matters, follow up effectively, and keep your sales process running smoothly.


### **2. Create custom CRM fields in Relate**


Relate provides you with various types of custom fields that can help you organize key information about your leads and sales opportunities in a way that works best for your team.


1. Go to **Workspace > Settings** .
2. For each category of data—Organizations, Contacts, Deals, Processes, and Lists—you’ll find options to add and manage **Custom fields** .
3. Add fields that align with your sales process.


💡


For details about configuring custom fields in your Relate workplace, see[here](https://www.relate.so/docs/features/custom-fields?ref=relate.so) .


Below are a few CRM custom fields you can start with. Feel free to add/remove/edit any of the fields below so that it's tailored to your product!


- **Team size** :` 1-5` ,` 6-10` ,` 11-50` ,` 51-100` ,` 100+`
- **Company stage** :` Startup` ,` SMB` ,` Mid-market` ,` Enterprise`
- **Pain points** (Multi-select):` Slow workflows` ,` High operational costs` ,` Outdated tools` ,` Poor data mgmt` ,` Low conversion rates`
- **ICP fit** :` Strong` ,` Moderate` ,` Weak`
- **Lead type** (Multi-select):` Outbound` ,` Inbound` ,[MQL](https://www.relate.so/docs/wiki/mql?ref=relate.so) ,[PQL](https://www.relate.so/docs/wiki/pql?ref=relate.so)


### **3. Write better meeting notes**


Sales meeting notes are the foundation for AI-driven updates. The better your notes, the smarter your AI suggestions will be.


#### **Tips for writing effective notes** :


- Use frameworks like **BANT** or **MEDDIC** to guide your discovery calls.
- Align your notes with your custom CRM fields for easy updates.


Use tools like[Gong](https://www.gong.io/?ref=relate.so) ,[Fellow.app](https://fellow.app/?ref=relate.so) , or[Chorus.ai](http://chorus.ai/?ref=relate.so) to record meetings and capture insights.


💡


At Relate, our team uses ****Gong**** to streamline note-taking and get the most out of every meeting.


### **4. Use AI field suggestions to update your CRM data**


After writing your notes, Relate’s AI steps in to analyze them and recommend **AI Suggestions** for your CRM.


**How it works**


- Review the AI-suggestions for your notes.
- Click` Apply` to update individual fields. If a suggestion isn’t relevant, simply click` Ignore` .
- For bulk actions, you can choose` Apply All` or` Ignore All` .


No more copying and pasting. No more manual errors and overlooked details. Just clean, accurate CRM data—updated in seconds to help you focus on building relationships and winning deals.
