# Reporting upgrade - Relate Changelog

## Overview


You can now track prospect status conversion rates (qualification rate), pipeline entries, and win rates.


Relate now displays pipeline funnel metrics to track deals through pipeline stages.


## How it works


Go to the navigation menu and click your workspace name. Click` Reporting` to access your reporting page.


The new charts will be at the top of the reporting page.
