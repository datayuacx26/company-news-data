# View Linked Organization Information in Contact Lists and Processes - Relate Changelog

Custom Field values of linked **Organizations** can now be viewed directly within **Lists** and **Processes** of type *Contact* .


Previously, important lead qualification data was stored only at the organization level. This made it difficult to identify high-potential leads and take timely action.


With this update, leads can now be **filtered and followed up on based on their organization's information** — such as data collected from **lead capture forms** . For example, sequence emails can be sent only to leads whose organizations meet specific criteria.


This allows for easier lead qualification with minimal form input, helping improve response rates **without requiring detailed company information upfront** .
