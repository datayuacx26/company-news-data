# Airbnb.org offers free emergency housing to first responders after the earthquake in Colombia

To support those impacted by the earthquake in Colombia,[Airbnb.org](http://airbnb.org/) —a nonprofit founded by Airbnb to provide emergency housing in times of crisis—is offering free emergency housing to first responders staging in the affected areas. Stays are free for these organizations, and are funded by Airbnb.org and the generosity of donors.


In this initial stage, Airbnb.org is working with Defensa Civil Colombiana, and nonprofits, including CENACED, Fundación Renacer, and Save the Children Colombia to support their work as they respond to the earthquake in Colombia.


## How it works


For first responders, Airbnb.org is working directly with relief organizations to provide stays close to the areas where they’re needed most, giving crews a place to rest and recharge between shifts.


> **A stable place to stay gives first responders a place close to the affected areas to rest and recover before heading back out to help. Alongside our partners, we’re already working to provide free stays for some organizations responding to the earthquake in Colombia as they help those affected begin the road to recovery** .
>
>
> Christoph Gorder, Executive Director of[Airbnb.org](http://airbnb.org/) .


> **This network turns the diversity of its members into a shared course of action that multiplies the impact of aid. Its strength lies in the fact that each member contributes its capabilities toward a common purpose: responding to emergencies and disasters together, and effectively. The support of airbnb.org is an essential boost in strengthening our brigades’ response capacity and ensuring that those on the front line of care have housing during critical moments** .
>
>
> Cynthia Espinoza Aranguré, Director General of CENACED


> **During the emergency, the risks of sexual exploitation and trafficking increase. That’s why we’ve started prevention efforts in the shelters. But our response also seeks to support affected families: we’re helping some who lost their homes with dignified temporary housing in Pereira and Quibdó, with support from Airbnb. Fundación Renacer is also leading a campaign with the Gestora Social de Quibdó to receive donations that support the rebuilding of some homes, because the emergency doesn’t end when the crisis passes—now comes recovery** .
>
>
> Luz Stella Cárdenas, Director, Fundación Renacer


> **Thousands of homes have been damaged or reduced to rubble, and schools, community centers, and hospitals—the places children and adolescents depend on to feel safe—have been damaged. For thousands of children and adolescents, their entire world and sense of safety have been torn away** .
>
>
> María Mercedes Liévano, National Director of Save the Children in Colombia


Airbnb.org uses the Airbnb platform to connect people who need a place to stay in times of crisis with Airbnb hosts. 100 percent of all donations go directly to providing emergency housing in times of crisis. Since its founding in 2020, Airbnb.org has provided 1.6 million nights of free, temporary housing, including during[wildfires in Los Angeles](https://airbnb.org/lawildfires) and[France](https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-org-provides-free-emergency-housing-to-people-displaced-by-wildfires-in-france/) ,[hurricanes in the Caribbean](https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-org-offers-free-emergency-housing-for-hurricane-melissa/) ,[floods in Brazil](https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-org-supports-rescue-efforts-in-rio-grande-do-sul-brazil/) , and[earthquakes in Japan](https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-org-support-for-evacuees-impacted-by-the-noto-peninsula-earthquake/) and[Venezuela](https://news.airbnb.com/ea/airbnb-org-ofrece-alojamiento-para-personal-de-emergencia-tras-los-terremotos-en-venezuela/) .


## How to help


Anyone interested in supporting those impacted by the earthquake in Colombia can visit[Airbnb.org](http://airbnb.org/) to either:


- If you’re an Airbnb host, you can sign up to provide discounted Airbnb.org stays.
- Donate to Airbnb.org—100% of donations go directly toward connecting people in crisis with emergency housing.
