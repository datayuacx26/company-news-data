# Discover celebrity local favorites in FIFA World Cup 2026™ host cities

Throughout the FIFA World Cup 2026™, Airbnb has been more than a place to stay, but a way for millions of guests to authentically connect with the people and neighborhoods they’re visiting. To celebrate the tournament, celebrities are highlighting their “Local Favorites”—a series of host city guides featuring their everyday haunts—from their favorite coffee shops and local parks, to secret neighborhood food spots. These curated guides are created to help fans discover the unique magic of host cities during the tournament and on future trips.


## [Seattle Local Favorites: Megan Rapinoe](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZqrCRaNm8V/)


Former U.S. Women’s National Team star and Seattle Reign FC legend Megan Rapinoe is opening up her personal playbook to her home city. Her curated guide takes you beyond the tourist spots to the exact local businesses, eateries, and scenic overlooks she loves:


- [Airbnb Seattle listing](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/1581495859019910074?source_impression_id=p3_1780606407_P3QQqvG5hx9KFDSG) with an unforgettable view – This 5-story luxury townhome in Lower Queen Anne has 220° views of Lake Union, downtown, and the Space Needle from a private rooftop deck, plus a full outdoor kitchen, and soaring 40-ft windows.
- [Caffe Vita](https://www.facebook.com/CaffeFioreSeattle) – This independent, hand-roasting coffee company is rooted in the city’s arts and music scene, and known for its farm-direct sourcing and vintage Probat roasters.
- [Betty Restaurant and Bar](https://bettyrestaurant.com/) – A beloved neighborhood spot on Queen Anne Ave serving elevated American comfort food with a daily happy hour. Warm, unpretentious, and a local staple.
- [Glasswing](https://glasswingshop.com/?srsltid=AfmBOooym4qzdzKVLHZ3QePHEApniJ5BG9cDLBczJ6OA4PoYRyBzDwHP) – A curated Melrose Ave boutique carrying women’s and men’s clothing, shoes, jewelry, home goods, and plants from independent designers worldwide.
- [Queen Anne Farmers Market](https://qafm.org/) – Seattle’s only independent farmers market, held Thursdays from 3:00–7:30 PM atop Queen Anne Hill with all-local vendors, live music and chef demos.
- [Walrus and the Carpenter](https://thewalrusbar.com/) – A walk-in only Ballard oyster bar known for its piles of Pacific Northwest oysters, tallboys, natural wine, and a lively, no-frills vibe.
- [Kerry Park](https://www.seattle.gov/parks/parks/kerry-park) – Seattle’s most iconic viewpoint, offers sweeping views of downtown, the Space Needle, Elliott Bay, and Mount Rainier on clear days.


## [New York City Local Favorites: Josh Hart](https://www.instagram.com/jhart/reel/DZ5RjKSAhGJ/?hl=en)


New York Knicks guard and Roommates Show co-host Josh Hart is sharing the exact NYC spots he has genuinely made part of his daily routine. As Josh puts it, New Yorkers judge you on three things: your coffee spot, your slice, and your bagel. This guide reveals exactly where he goes to secure his favorites:


- [L’industrie Pizza](https://www.lindustriebk.com/) – Josh calls it “fire”—thin, crisp slices with the perfect crunch. His order is classic New York: pepperoni.
- [Apollo Bagels](https://apollobagels.com/) , Kips Bay – His go-to for bagels: fluffy without being too fluffy, dense without being too filling.
- [Deux Luxe](https://www.deuxluxeny.com/) , West Village and[Café Deux](https://www.cafedeuxny.com/) , Harrison – Josh’s love for Deux Luxe started with Café Deux, then carried over once he tried the “amazing” 100% Wagyu smash burger.
- [Sisters Caribbean Cuisine](https://sisterscuisineharlem.com/) , Harlem – A regular stop once or twice a week; his order is curry goat, rice and peas, and mac and cheese.
- [Acker Wines](https://www.ackerwines.com/?srsltid=AfmBOorRAVl5mKCB1ff6Enf0924TYsP8eboDMiwkGM76I71M26AwbC95) , Lincoln Square – Josh’s pick for an “amazing” wine selection covering nearly anything a guest could want.
- [Kith](https://kith.com/) , Nolita – Josh’s nod to NYC’s fashion culture—style that’s personal and only works when worn comfortably.
- [Arcane Coffee](https://arcaneestatecoffee.com/) , West Village – Part of Josh’s coffee rotation, reflecting the city’s deep coffee scene.
- [Caffè Panna](https://www.caffepanna.com/) , Brooklyn – His order: Cookies ‘n Panna, also a favorite of his wife’s.


## [Mexico City Local Favorites: Diego Boneta](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZ7yZqwR94W/)


For actor Diego Boneta, discovering the true magic of Mexico City lies in the local spaces where design, culture, and community meet. From historic plazas to his go-to taco spots, these are the local gems he recommends for anyone looking to experience CDMX like a true local:


- [Plaza Río de Janeiro](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/plaza-rio-de-janeiro/?lang=en) – Diego’s mornings often start here in Roma Norte, amid Porfirian architecture, green space, and cafés steeped in history and calm.
- [Café Tormenta](https://www.instagram.com/tormenta.cafe/?hl=en) – A renowned specialty coffee spot where Diego chats with founder Lucho over a Latte Cold Brew or Cold Brew with tonic.
- [Ultramarinos Demar](https://www.instagram.com/ultramarinos.rest/?hl=en) – A seafood-focused gastronomic landmark with a relaxed vibe; Diego’s a fan of the fresh pasta dishes too.
- [El Remolkito](https://www.instagram.com/elremolkito/?hl=en) – A Roma Norte taco spot born near Estadio Ciudad de México, connecting football tradition with iconic tacos.
- [Maizajo](https://www.maizajo.com/?srsltid=AfmBOooNOaEQM3Fv7VnsNdIj481tF_sOh7OzCG602KKBjen_vAh_017n) – A Condesa restaurant honoring Mexican corn traditions, where diners can sit inside the kitchen and watch dishes made.

- [Chito’s Red Tacos](https://www.instagram.com/chitosredtacos/?hl=en) **** – A permanently parked Wynwood food truck serving authentic birria, al pastor, asada, lengua, and vampiro tacos for ~$4.45 each.
- [Moxie’s Brickell](https://moxies.com/news/restaurants-brickell-miami/?srsltid=AfmBOopGx5fS19HDn9-HAHxhnWShdabfYRofANqBohwhVy-k0EIsrvgu) – Serena’s go-to for sitting at the bar and snacking on nachos in Mary Brickell Village.
- [Myka](https://mykagreek.com/) **** – **** Miami Beach’s newest froyo obsession—this Madrid-born Greek yogurt brand blends premium Greek yogurt with kefir for a creamy, probiotic-rich, additive-free treat.


## [Los Angeles Local Favorites: Emma Chamberlain](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DajLfNNPuAP/)


As a true Californian, Emma Chamberlain is pulling back the curtain on Los Angeles’ best hidden gems. Her picks—from locals-only restaurants to under-the-radar vintage shops—offer fans a way to experience LA like a true local:


- [Chamberlain Coffee](https://www.instagram.com/chamberlaincoffee/?hl=en) **** – **** Emma’s own brand has a bright, laid-back Venice café home—great for specialty coffee, matcha, or seasonal drinks before Abbot Kinney or the beach.
- [Cookbook Market](https://www.cookbookla.com/) – A chef-favorite grocer and specialty shop stocked with California produce; Emma’s go-to is the Greek salad at the Larchmont café.
- [Kitchen Mouse](https://kitchenmousela.com/) – A Highland Park favorite for colorful, gluten-free, vegetable-forward meals. Emma’s order is the Breakfast Tacos with Tofu Scramble.
- [The Mart Collective](https://themartcollective.com/) – A Venice vintage “treasure hunting” spot near Abbot Kinney with mid-century furniture, vinyl, denim, and one-of-a-kind décor.
- [Cannonball & Tilly](https://www.instagram.com/cannonballandtilly/?hl=en) – A Fairfax District vintage shop Emma calls a “time capsule” of timeless denim and designer finds.
- [Marvin](https://www.chezmarvin.com/) – A “dark and moody” Beverly Boulevard spot for deep conversations, French-California cooking, and natural wine. Emma loves the Rigatoni Spicy Vodka.
- [Santa Monica Antique & Vintage Market](https://www.santamonicaairportantiquemarket.com/) – A twice-monthly open-air market for antique furniture, vintage fashion, and rare collectibles near the Pier.
- [Brentwood Farmers Market](https://www.facebook.com/BrentwoodFarmersMarket/) – A Sunday staple for Southern California produce and artisan goods. Emma’s picks: Brothers Products’ hummus and Delices du Chef’s carrot salad.
