# Coca-Cola EP PLC - Total Voting Rights

Total Voting Rights and Capital


This notification is made in conformity with DTR 5.6 of the FCA's Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules.


As at 31 July 2026, Coca-Cola Europacific Partners plc had


442,228,029


ordinary shares of €0.01 each in issue, each with one vote attached. No shares were held in treasury.


The total number of voting rights is


442,228,029


and this figure may be used by shareholders and others with notification obligations as the denominator for the calculations by which they will determine if they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, Coca-Cola Europacific Partners plc under the FCA's Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules.
