# The Time for Persistent Global Intelligence is Now: Why Sovereign Sensing is Critical

**The world of tomorrow is not going to look like the past.** EK: The biggest misconception in power right now is the belief that the future will look like the past. The pace of technological change (including what we saw with the Anthropic model withdrawal in mid-June) means business-as-usual procurement and strategic-investment cycles aren’t going to keep up. Governments are starting to understand that. They’re just not moving fast enough yet.
TW: I’d add, I rarely comment on what we’re getting wrong. But I will say what keeps me up. It isn’t the adversary I can see. It’s the behavior I’m missing. The world is too big, and we’ve barely cracked the top of the iceberg on sensing. The belief that we have ubiquity, that we have a complete view, is itself a vulnerability.
