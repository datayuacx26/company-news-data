# How to choose a POS system for your small business in the UAE

If you're a small business looking for a point-of-sale (POS) system, chances are you've reached the point where cash payments aren't enough anymore.


Customers expect to pay by card or mobile wallet. You want a faster checkout experience. And you need a reliable way to collect payments without making your business more complicated.


The problem is that "POS system" can mean two very different things:


- A card terminal to accept card payments in person


- A complete point-of-sale platform that manages payments alongside inventory, staff, reporting, and orders


Choosing the wrong option can leave you paying for hardware, software subscriptions, and operational features your business doesn't actually need.


For many UAE-based small businesses, the first question therefore isn't what the best POS system is. It’s whether you actually need a POS system in the first place – and what that might look like in practice.


This guide helps you decide. We’ll discuss:


- Does it actually make sense to get a POS system for your small business?

## Does it actually make sense to get a POS system for your small business?


Maybe. But first, let’s get clear on what you actually need.


If your main goal is simply to let customers pay by tapping their card or mobile wallet, a traditional POS machine can be a lot of hardware for one job.


Traditional POS terminals may involve hardware purchase or rental costs, setup arrangements and ongoing fees, depending on the provider and merchant agreement. Delivery and activation timelines can also vary. There could also be significant maintenance costs that merchants have to bear annually.


If you have a growing team, that’s more devices to pay for, wait on, and lug around.


In cases like these, it might actually be less costly and faster to use a feature called…


###

### Network International for accepting in-person payments with a dedicated terminal


If you want the classic card machine experience at your counter, Network International’s N-Genius terminals are a reliable choice.


These terminals go beyond basic cards. They support Buy Now Pay Later, instalments, and even let customers pay via QR code if they prefer.


1


If you already use software to run part of your business, you can often connect these terminals to your existing systems so your data stays connected.


This setup is great if you have a permanent checkout area and want a dedicated device that stays put. Pricing is tailored to your merchant agreement and might include terminal rental, statement fees, and processing fees based on your sales volume.


1a


### Foodics for connecting restaurant orders with kitchen and front-of-house operations


If you run a café, restaurant, bakery, or cloud kitchen, Foodics can help connect what happens at the counter with the rest of the service.


For example, a lunch order can move from the server to the kitchen, update its table or takeaway status, and appear in your restaurant reports.


2


That makes Foodics a stronger fit when you need more than payment acceptance alone.


Foodics builds its UAE quotes around the hardware and software your restaurant needs, so your total cost will depend on the setup you choose.


2a


### Sapaad for managing dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and online ordering


If your customers order through a mix of dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and online channels, Sapaad can help you manage those orders in one place.


For example, an online order and a table order can feed into the same restaurant system, while ingredient tracking and food-cost reporting help you keep an eye on what each sale means for the wider business.


3


Sapaad also offers an offline mode, so your team can keep taking orders during an internet interruption and sync the data once the connection returns.


Pricing depends on the software, devices, and restaurant modules you choose.


3a


### Mahaal for keeping sales, products, and customer records together


Mahaal can help boutiques, salons, repair shops, or other smaller storefronts manage transactions alongside products, orders, customers, and multiple stores.


4


When you make a sale, for instance, you can keep the payment connected to the product and customer records you use to run the business rather than updating separate systems afterward.


Mahaal offers a seven-day trial, so you can test the platform before committing. Your paid plan and any additional hardware costs will depend on the setup you choose.
