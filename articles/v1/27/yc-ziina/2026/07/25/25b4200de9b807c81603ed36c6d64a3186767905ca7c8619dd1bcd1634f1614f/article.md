## Stripe


Stripe


1 is a global payment platform designed to handle card payments in 139 different currencies.


1a Stripe allows you to unify your online and in-person sales to create a more consistent customer experience, offering access to over 100 payment methods.


To help local conversion, Stripe uses Adaptive Pricing to present prices to your customers in their own local currency based on the latest exchange rates.


When using Stripe for international processing, it's helpful to understand their fee structure:


- Transaction fees:


Successful domestic card transactions cost 2.9% + AED 1.00. You will pay an additional 1% for international cards, and another 1% if currency conversion is required.


1a
- Dispute management:


It costs AED 60 to receive, counter, or submit evidence for a payment dispute, though this fee is returned if you win.


1a
- Invoice generation: Y


ou can set the system to automatically generate invoices after a purchase for 0.4% of the transaction total, capped at a maximum of $2 USD per invoice.


1a
- Currency handling:


The system automatically converts incoming funds into your home country’s default currency unless you manually adjust the configuration.


You can also use your own domain while hosting your payments page or customer portal on Stripe for $10 USD per month.
