# Top 9 payment gateways in the UAE: A guide to choosing the right solution

You’ve set up your e-commerce store in the UAE and need to start accepting payments. The next step is to find a payment gateway that integrates seamlessly with your website builder, such as Shopify or WooCommerce.


A payment gateway is a tool that allows you to safely accept payments online. It acts as a transaction processor, enabling your customers to pay using cards, digital wallets, bank transfers, and other payment methods.


Chances are, you’ve already come across a few big-name options like Stripe during your research. The issue is that many payment gateways are built for established companies, not e-commerce sites that are just starting out.


In fact, you’ve probably experienced some of these challenges before:


- During signup, you’ve been asked for a lot of extensive documentation you don’t yet have


- If you’ve passed onboarding, the long payout timelines and high transaction fees make it difficult to manage your cash flow


- If you sell on other channels, you may still need separate tools for payment links, QR codes, and in-person sales


What you really want is an all-in-one payment solution that’s simple and fast to set up, with a clear fee structure and solutions that support your business growth in the UAE.


We’ve written this guide to help you find exactly that. We’ll cover the nine best payment gateways in the UAE, and how they compare in terms of payout speed, available features, and more.
