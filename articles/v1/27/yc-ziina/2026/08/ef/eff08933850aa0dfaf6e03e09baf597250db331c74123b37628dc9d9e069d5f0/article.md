## Amazon Payment Services


What is it:


Amazon Payment Services


2 is a digital payments platform. It’s designed for e-commerce businesses, SMEs, and enterprises across the world.


Key features:


- APS provides customizable tools, including single API integrations and mobile SDKs, to fit into your website or app


- The platform provides a centralized dashboard where you can manage transactions, track your sales performance, and create payment insights and reports


- Merchants can generate and send secure payment links to customers via email, SMS, or social media


- Supports payment methods such as Buy Now Pay Later, UAE bank cards, and digital wallets


- Supports automated billing for subscriptions or repeat-payments


Fees:


Payments typically cost 2.80% + 1 AED per transaction for domestic transfers. There are also add-on options: for example, you can choose to let your customers pay in installments for an extra 4 AED per transaction.


2a


Settlement speed:


APS automatically sends out settlements in the UAE on a weekly basis. For the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, it takes 1-2 business days.


2b

PayPal


3 is a payment gateway that helps businesses of all sizes process both domestic and international payments. It offers coverage for over 200 countries and supports 24 currencies.


You can choose between hosting checkout pages on your website or letting PayPal host the checkout on their secure servers.


3b

3c

## Telr


What is it:


Telr


4 is a payment gateway that supports startups and large enterprises for processing international payments.


Key features:


- Telr supports 130+ currencies, and their services are available in 30+ languages, including English and Arabic


- Telr lets you collect payments through payment links and QR codes


- Telr accepts major cards and digital wallets, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, PayPal, Samsung Pay, Mada, STC Pay, Tabby, and more


- Telr’s hosted payment forms are PCI-DSS v4.0 Level 1 certified, and they support all the main card scheme anti-fraud measures


4b


Fees:


Telr’s pricing depends on the plan you choose. For example, if you’re earning under 20,000 AED per month, there are no transaction fees, just a monthly fee of 349 AED. If you’re earning slightly more (between 20,001 and 50,000 AED per month), you’ll be charged 2.69% + 1 AED per transaction in addition to a 149 monthly fee.


4a


The exact onboarding times aren’t disclosed, though the process includes contacting Telr online, providing the necessary documents, and waiting until they finish the KYB process. You can speed things up by integrating Telr’s APIs while they’re reviewing your documents and website.


4c

## Mamo


What is it:


Mamo


6 is a UAE-based fintech platform designed primarily for freelancers and SMEs. Their mobile-first platform is tailored to the UAE market.


Key features:


- Add your own branding to your checkout and choose what data to collect from your customer. You can also control what the customer sees after payment


- Offer multiple payment options like credit and debit cards, digital wallets, and buy now, pay later (BNPL) automatically enabled on all payment links


- All sales made through Mamo’s payment gateway are automatically tracked and fed back to you as reports


Fees:


Mamo charges 2.9% + 1 AED per transaction for domestic transfers on their Growth plan, and if you process non-AED currencies, they charge an extra 2%. Their Premium plan, which costs 99 AED per month, comes with transaction fees of 2.7% + 0.8 AED and 1.5% extra for non-AED currencies.
