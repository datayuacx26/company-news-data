# Pidilite Industries and Kyndryl complete successful IT modernization with Google Cloud

**BENGALURU, July 28, 2026** —[Kyndryl](https://www.kyndryl.com/us/en) (NYSE: KD), a leading provider of mission-critical enterprise technology services, today announced the successful completion of[Pidilite Industries Limited's](https://www.pidilite.com/) IT modernization with[Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/) .


Kyndryl has migrated Pidilite’s non-ERP workloads from on-premises and existing cloud environments to Google Cloud, completing the company’s data center exit, and now provides ongoing cloud managed services. With the new environment fully live in production, Pidilite operates on a more agile and resilient IT foundation that supports its evolving business needs.


The project has delivered a 20% improvement in service response, a 30% reduction in infrastructure provisioning and turnaround times, and more consistent performance across Pidilite’s application landscape.


“As part of our digital transformation journey, we are building a more agile, resilient and scalable IT environment that not only supports our growth strategy, but also lays the core infrastructure foundation for our data- and AI-led transformation," said Vivek Sharma, CIO, Pidilite Industries Limited. "Kyndryl’s strong expertise in managing cloud environments and its knowledge of Pidilite’s IT infrastructure, built over a decade-long partnership, played a decisive role in enabling that strategy."


“By working with Kyndryl and leveraging Google Cloud, we simplified our operations, improved service responsiveness and enabled our teams to focus on driving innovation and growth,” said Ajay Kak, Chief — IT Infrastructure, Network and Technical Security, Pidilite Industries.


“Pidilite’s cloud modernization journey reflects the growing focus among Indian enterprises on using digital platforms to drive business agility and progress,” said Lingraju Sawkar, President, Kyndryl APxJ (Asia Pacific Excluding Japan). “By applying a structured migration methodology to modernize Pidilite’s hybrid cloud environment, Kyndryl has built a flexible, resilient technology foundation that supports long-term business growth.”


The collaboration drew on Kyndryl’s expertise in[hybrid and multi-cloud services](https://www.kyndryl.com/us/en/services/cloud) , including cloud migration, governance and ongoing managed services. Kyndryl applied proven migration methodologies and cloud-native management practices to improve Pidilite's operational visibility, automate routine tasks and enable faster responses to changing business requirements.
