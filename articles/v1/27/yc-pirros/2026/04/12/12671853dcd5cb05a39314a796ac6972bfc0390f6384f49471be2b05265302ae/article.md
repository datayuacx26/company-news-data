# How BIM Management Shapes the Construction Industry

In today's rapidly growing and ever-changing world, there is no room for errors. Today's buildings are equipped with endless services, automation, and time is the most important and expensive commodity. With all these factors in place, BIM and BIM Management play a pivotal role in shaping the construction industry.


BIM Management is a process of managing BIM (Building Information Modeling), which brings efficiency in the planning of buildings, their design, and construction. BIM assists in creating the model of a building before the physical structure is developed. BIM Management guarantees proper coordination and interaction of all the stakeholders, ranging from the design phase to the construction phase and the operational phase.


BIM management enables everyone involved in the project to work with the same data. This helps in achieving communication, leading to faster decision-making, minimizes conflicts, and assists in the completion of a project within the required time and cost. Furthermore, it aids in the confirmation of systems and workflow.

### Core Components of BIM Management


The core components of BIM management play a key role in ensuring the success of a project. They help in ensuring that all aspects of the project are well planned, coordinated, and executed. These components include:


**a. Project Coordination and Collaboration**


All project stakeholders need to have access to the latest project information and ensure that all the construction changes are properly tracked and managed. This is done through project coordination and collaboration. The key activities involved include having regular meetings to ensure all the project team members are on the same page and using tools like BIM 360 and Plannerly to manage tasks and track progress.


**b. Data Management and Integration**


Data management and integration entail the use of data from various sources to create a digital representation of the project. The key activities involved include data collection and storage, data integration, and data validation.


**c. Quality Control and Assurance**


Using quality control and assurance tools facilitates ascertaining that the BIM models in use are of the right quality, in addition to identifying any problem areas and correcting them before they become major issues. The main activities are model audit; clash; and compliance checks.


**d. Scheduling and Cost Estimation**


In BIM Management, making sure that the project is finished within the set time and cost estimates is important. This can be done by linking both time and cost data into the BIM models where the BIM managers can give more accurate and dependable project schedules and cost estimates. The key activities involved are 4D Scheduling, 5D Scheduling, and resource allocation.

### Benefits of Effective BIM Management


**a. Enhanced Project Efficiency and Productivity**


Through BIM management, stakeholders are able to easily coordinate, thus reducing the project timeline and lowering the total project cost.


**b. Improved Communication Among Stakeholders**


BIM management has tools that guarantee all the team members are in possession of the updated information. This assists in avoiding misinterpretation and ensures that everyone is in harmony with the target achievement.


**c. Reduced Project Risks and Errors**


Since BIM management is all about processing information that is accurate and up-to-date, it is easy to identify risk factors within the project and put preventive measures in place to avoid or minimize the risks, hence cutting down on the project cost.


**d. Better Resource Management and Cost Savings**


BIM management aids in optimizing resource allocation by providing detailed information on the project requirements.


**e. Increased Project Transparency and Accountability**


Through BIM management, every stakeholder is able to access project information. This fosters transparency and accountability, making it easy for every team member to be accountable for their actions.

### BIM Management Tools and Software


Several tools and software solutions are available to support BIM Management. These software and tools are integrated with BIM management to allow efficient exchange of data and ensure all the project team members can easily access the information they need. Some of the most widely used tools include:


**a. Pirros**


Helps in streamlining workflows and improving data consistency by integrating BIM data with other construction management systems.


**b. Plannerly**


A collaborative platform for managing BIM projects, Plannerly helps teams to plan, manage, and monitor BIM deliverables and tasks.


**c. Guardian**


Helps in managing BIM content and ensuring compliance with the set standards.


**d. Unifi (Content Catalog)**


A cloud-based platform for managing BIM content. It provides a centralized space for storing, sharing, and managing BIM objects and libraries.

### The BIM Management Process


**a. Developing a BIM Execution Plan (BEP)**


The BIM Execution Plan (BEP) is supposed to give goals, processes, and roles of all the interested parties in the project related to the usage of BIM. It also encompasses the detailed objectives of the project, identifies the use of BIM, and outlines procedures for sharing and interacting with information.


**b. Setting Up a Common Data Environment (CDE)**


This step is regarded as important as it helps in the control and dissemination of project information. CDE is a place where all the project information, documents, and models are consolidated and managed.


**c. Managing Project Information and Data Workflows**


This involves the coordination and direction of the movement of information across the phases of a project. It ranges from the initiation of data governance, policies on data, and requirements on the format of the data, to the processes on how the data can be validated and have its quality assured.


**d. Coordinating with Design, Construction, and Operations Teams**


In the implementation phase of the project, BIM plays a crucial role in the interrelation of various disciplines of design, where the structural design and mechanical design can be made to check for clashes and optimize the design. As for the benefits of BIM models during the construction phase, they include planning, scheduling, and organization for construction to decrease the need for construction failure and redoing work. In the case of the operation phase, BIM offers the ideal information regarding the management and maintenance of the facilities.


**e. Ensuring Compliance with Industry Standards and Protocols**


Adhering to industry standards such as ISO 19650 is important as it assists in minimizing risks and enhancing the performance of projects. Routine audits and performance reviews, which are done in this phase, also help in ensuring that the project’s data and processes are in compliance with the standards.

### Key Responsibilities of a BIM Manager


The key responsibilities of a BIM Manager include but are not limited to:


- Ensuring that BIM execution plans are produced and maintained for each project.
- Leading and supporting the use of digital technology to create BIMs in the Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) sector.
- Managing the BIM budgets.
- Maintaining the efficiency and effectiveness of the project by serving as a bridge between designers, clients, and architects.
- Ensuring that BIM execution plans have been produced and maintained for every project.

### Common Challenges Faced by BIM Managers


**I. Data Integration and Interoperability**


The use of different BIM tools and software by various stakeholders has resulted in difficulties in seamless data exchange and consistency.


**II. Resistance to Change**


Many times, stakeholders get used to the normal way of working, and implementing new BIM management strategies becomes challenging.


**III. Maintaining Data Integrity and Quality Control**


Lack of coherent and precise data eventually results in program delays and increased costs, posing another challenge for BIM managers.


**IV. Collaboration and Communication**


Working in large, diverse, and geographically dispersed teams can lead to misunderstandings.


**V. High Initial Costs**


Due to high implementation costs represented by the cost of implementing BIM management software, training, and efficient resource management, the implementation of BIM management is often a challenge for many organizations.


‍


### Strategies to Overcome These Challenges


**a. Standardizing BIM Protocols**


Adoption of similar industry practices will assist in providing a coherent structure for BIM management.


**b. Effective Change Management**


Periodic sensitizations on the use of BIM management tools and their advantages will enhance the task of easy acceptance of the tools by the stakeholders since they will have improved knowledge on the same.


**c. Robust Data Management Practices**


Including regular data audits, validation checks, and quality control measures, are vital for maintaining data integrity. Utilizing centralized data environments, such as a Common Data Environment (CDE), can help ensure consistency.


**d. Enhancing Collaboration and Communication**


Encouragement of collaboration and communication necessitates the adoption of interactive tools and regular meetings. Clear communication guidelines are essential.

### Skills and Training for BIM Managers


In order to fulfill their responsibilities, BIM managers need to possess a set of skills to excel in this role. These skills will aid them in the implementation and management of BIM processes in construction. Some of the technical expertise and soft skills they need to have include:


**a. Technical Proficiency**


- **BIM Software** : Fundamental knowledge of BIM tools such as Autodesk Revit, Navisworks, and other BIM-related tools and software is crucial. They should be fully aware of how to develop, administer, and navigate BIM models.
- **Data Management** : Data management is central to BIM management processes. It plays a role in enhancing the integrity of the data and enabling easy retrieval. BIM managers must know how to store, retrieve, and integrate this data.


**b. Project Management**


- **Planning and Scheduling** : BIM models are very detailed when it comes to time and cost. BIM managers should be able to easily manipulate project schedules using BIM models.
- **Resource Allocation** : Efficient resource management eliminates wastage and lowers costs. Skills in ensuring the right utilization of available materials, manpower, and equipment through the help of BIM models are ideal.


**c. Communication and Collaboration**


- **Team Coordination** : All parties involved in a project need to align with the project’s objectives. This is possible only if the BIM manager applies good communication skills necessary for the group.
- **Conflict Resolution** : It is essential for a BIM manager to have the competency to solve all the conflicts that the project members are likely to present.


**d. Problem-Solving and Analytical Thinking**


- **Issue Identification** : Through the use of the BIM model, the BIM manager must be able to assess and identify potential issues in the early stages of a project.
- **Solution Development** : A BIM manager must be able to develop effective solutions in order to prevent problems from escalating, thus ensuring project smoothness.


**e. Continuous Learning and Professional Development**


- **Staying Updated** : In the ever-changing technology world, a BIM manager must remain up to date with the latest BIM tools, trends, and best practices.
- **Training and Certification** : BIM managers must undergo continuous training and certification to enhance their skills.

### Future Trends in BIM Management


**a. Integration of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning**


AI and machine learning integration in BIM management can enhance the automation of various tasks such as data analysis and risk assessment.


**b. Use of Digital Twins**


Digital twins, which are digital replicas of physical entities, will increasingly be used in BIM management to improve project visualization and monitoring.


**c. Enhanced Collaboration with Cloud-Based Platforms**


Cloud-based platforms will foster better collaboration among project stakeholders by providing easy access to project data from any location.


**d. Implementation of Augmented and Virtual Reality**


AR and VR can be used to provide immersive project visualizations, enabling better decision-making and client presentations.
