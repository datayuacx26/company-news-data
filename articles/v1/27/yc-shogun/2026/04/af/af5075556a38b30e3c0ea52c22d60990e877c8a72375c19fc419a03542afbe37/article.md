# Complete Database of Shopify Price Testing Apps (2026)

[Setting the right price](https://getshogun.com/learn/everything-you-need-to-know-about-price-testing-on-shopify) is one of the highest-leverage decisions any ecommerce merchant can make-yet most Shopify store owners still rely on gut instinct,[competitor benchmarking](https://getshogun.com/learn/ecommerce-competitor-price-monitoring) , or rough margin math. Price testing changes that. It replaces assumptions with actual data about what your customers are willing to pay.


This database catalogs the tools currently available for Shopify merchants to run structured price tests. Each entry includes a a breakdown on the feature set, pricing, and some company details of the app developers.

## Shogun A/B Testing


**Overview:** Shogun is widely known for its page builder, but its[A/B Testing app is a standalone product](https://getshogun.com/solutions/shopify-price-testing-app) —no page builder subscription required. The price testing capability uses an AI-assisted onboarding process to integrate with your existing theme. Prices are delivered dynamically without product or variant duplication, keeping your catalog clean.


Merchants set up test variants using Shopify’s native theme editor, which reduces the learning curve. The platform also supports template testing, which runs experiments across all pages using a given template simultaneously, useful for stores spread across many SKUs with distributed traffic. Audience segmentation (location, device, referring URL) is available on paid plans.


**Key Features:**


- Product price testing without duplicate product creation
- AI-assisted theme integration (sandboxed setup)
- Native Shopify Theme Editor variant creation
- Template testing (one test across multiple pages)
- Audience segmentation by location, device, referring URL
- Page, section, and element A/B testing
- Flicker-free delivery
- Free plan (1 active test)


**Pricing*:** Shogun A/B Testing offers four plans: Starter (Free) with 1 active test, Pro ($39/month) with 3 active tests and template/theme testing, Advanced ($119/month) with 5 active tests and advanced analytics, and Unlimited ($499/month), which includes multi-variant testing, data export, and price testing features. To get access to the price testing feature, you’ll want to be on the Unlimited plan.


**Location:** California, United States


**Date Launched:** March 21, 2025


**Shopify App Store:**[apps.shopify.com/shogun-ab-testing](https://apps.shopify.com/shogun-ab-testing)


## Intelligems


**Overview** : Intelligems is built around the argument that most A/B testing focuses too narrowly on conversion rate when the more useful signal is profit. The platform lets merchants evaluate product prices, shipping rates, free shipping thresholds, discount structures, subscription prices, and promotional offers.


Results are tracked using COGS-adjusted metrics, so a “winning” variant is evaluated on whether it increased actual profitability-not just order count.


The analytics dashboard provides real-time data with custom success metric definitions. Merchants can segment tests by audience and roll winning variants out across channels. Intelligems also integrates with subscription platforms to keep pricing consistent across recurring billing cycles.


**Key Features:**


- Product price A/B testing across entire catalog
- Shipping rate and free shipping threshold testing
- Offer and discount testing without discount codes
- Combination testing (price + shipping simultaneously)
- Audience segmentation
- COGS tracking and profit-per-visitor analytics
- Klaviyo integration
- Headless / Shopify Hydrogen support (limited)


**Pricing*:** A free “Redirect Only” plan is available for stores under 3,000 orders per month (URL redirect testing only). Paid tiers are: Core at $79/month ($59 annually), Plus at $499/month ($374 annually), and Blue at $999/month ($749 annually). Important:[price testing](https://getshogun.com/solutions/shopify-price-testing-app) is only available on the Plus plan and above – not included in Core.*


**Location:** New York, United States


**Date Launched:** December 1, 2021


## Shoplift


**Overview:** Shoplift is a no-code testing platform that integrates at the Shopify theme level rather than through client-side DOM overlays. Tests live in your theme’s Liquid code, which makes them stable through theme updates, developer deployments, and app changes. It is the first Shopify Plus Certified CRO tool of its kind.


[Price testing](https://getshogun.com/learn/everything-you-need-to-know-about-price-testing-on-shopify) is available on the Advanced and Pro plans. Because tests run at the theme level, pricing stays consistent across the product detail page, cart, checkout, and BNPL integrations. Initial setup requires a one-time low-code integration (estimated 10–15 minutes), after which marketing teams can manage experiments independently.


The Lift Assist feature auto-generates test variants based on your brand and proven patterns from hundreds of top brands, which is useful for teams that want data-backed starting points rather than building experiments from scratch.


**Key Features:**


- Theme-level A/B testing (no DOM overlays)
- No-code variant creation in Shopify Theme Customizer
- Lift Assist: auto-generated test recommendations
- Price testing (Advanced and Pro plans)
- Revenue Per Visitor and AOV tracking
- Statistical significance handled automatically
- Anti-flicker resolution at 90–150ms
- Audience segmentation by device, location, UTM source


**Pricing*:** Starts at $99/month (or $74/month billed annually) for the Core plan. Advanced is $299/month (or $224 annually); Pro is $699/month (or $524 annually), annual only.[Price testing](https://getshogun.com/learn/beauty-brand-price-testing-strategy-find-your-perfect-price-point) is included in Advanced and above. Annual plans save 25%. 14-day free trial available. Pricing is visitor-based and scales with monthly store traffic.


**Location:** New York, United States


**Date Launched:** September 15, 2022

## Split A/B Testing


**Overview:** Split is a straightforward tool covering prices, discounts, shipping rates, and content. It does not duplicate products-a complaint with older-generation testing tools-which keeps the catalog clean. The app lives inside the Shopify admin and is designed to feel native to the platform.


For merchants running their first pricing experiment without a large budget commitment, Split is consistently cited as a practical entry point.


**Key Features:**


- Price, discount, and shipping rate testing
- Content and SEO A/B testing
- Inventory synchronization
- No product duplication
- Real-time analytics


**Pricing*:** Split A/B Testing offers a free plan with access to its core testing features, including unlimited A/B tests, real-time performance insights, multi-product and variant testing, support for up to five traffic audiences, and up to 250 product variants per test. The free plan is limited to 10 orders.


The Premium plan costs $10/month and includes the same core features plus unlimited orders and 24/7 customer support.


**Location:** London, Great Britain


**Date Launched:** December 3, 2024

Tests can be paused or reverted easily.


**Key Features:**


- A/B price testing
