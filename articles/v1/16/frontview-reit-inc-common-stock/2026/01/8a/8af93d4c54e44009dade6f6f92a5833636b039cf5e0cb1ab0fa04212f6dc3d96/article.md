**About FrontView REIT, Inc.**


FrontView is a net-lease REIT that acquires, owns and manages properties with frontage leased to a diversified group of institutional credit tenants. FrontView is differentiated by an investment approach that focuses on properties that are in prominent locations with frontage on high-traffic roads that are highly visible to consumers. As of September 30, 2025, FrontView owned a well-diversified portfolio of 307 properties with frontage across 37 U.S. states. FrontView's tenants typically include service-oriented businesses, such as medical and dental providers, quick service restaurants, casual dining, financial institutions, cellular stores, automotive stores, convenience stores and gas stations, discount retail, automotive dealers, fitness operators, car washes, pharmacies, home improvement stores, as well as professional services tenants.
