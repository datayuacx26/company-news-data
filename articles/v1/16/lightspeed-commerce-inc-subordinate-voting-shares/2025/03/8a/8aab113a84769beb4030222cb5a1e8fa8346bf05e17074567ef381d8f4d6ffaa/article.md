What started in 2005 as a bold idea to transform how businesses operate has become a global force in commerce technology. Today,


[Lightspeed Commerce Inc.](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/) , the unified point of sale and payments platform powering the world’s best businesses proudly celebrates its 20th anniversary.


“Lightspeed was built to empower businesses. Twenty years later, that mission remains, but our transformation has been extraordinary. As commerce evolves, we’ll continue to innovate, scale, and help business owners navigate what’s next.”


—Dax Dasilva, Founder & CEO of Lightspeed.


## **20 years of transformation**


From its early days to becoming a global leader, Lightspeed has continuously evolved to meet the needs of retailers, restaurants, and golf operators.


At a time when business technology was fragmented and inefficient, Lightspeed introduced a more intuitive, streamlined approach. As digital transformation accelerated, the company expanded beyond retail, launching industry-leading solutions that simplified operations across multiple industries.


**From Canadian startup to global commerce leader**


Initially designed for independent retailers, Lightspeed quickly set a new standard for POS software innovation, unifying sophisticated tools into a single, easy-to-use platform.


The launch of


[Lightspeed Retail](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/retail/) , the company’s first cloud-based POS solution, followed closely by


[Lightspeed Restaurant](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/) and


[Lightspeed Golf](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/golf/) , marked a major shift in how Lightspeed helped businesses to operate—eliminating traditional barriers to efficiency and scalability. Over the years, Lightspeed introduced a series of groundbreaking innovations, including


[Lightspeed Insights](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/retail/analytics/) ,


[Lightspeed Loyalty](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/retail/loyalty/) ,


[Lightspeed Payments](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/retail/payments/) , and


[Lightspeed Capital](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/retail/retail-capital/) , empowering businesses with greater control and flexibility.


**Accelerating growth, expanding innovation** ****


2019 and 2020 were pivotal years for Lightspeed, with its public listings on the Toronto Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange, reinforcing its position as a global industry leader. Strategic acquisitions—including Chronogolf, iKentoo, Kounta, Gastrofix, Upserve, ShopKeep, Ecwid and NuORDER—further strengthened its product ecosystem.


By 2024,


[Lightspeed surpassed $1 billion in revenue](https://investors.lightspeedhq.com/English/newsroom/news-details/2024/Lightspeed-Announces-Second-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results-and-Raises-Adjusted-EBITDA-Outlook-for-Fiscal-2025/default.aspx) (trailing twelve months ending December 31, 2024), an achievement that reaffirmed its sustained growth and commitment to helping businesses thrive in an evolving digital age.


Today, with over 3,000 employees supporting 160,000+ Customer Locations


1


in over 100 countries, Lightspeed continues to drive sophistication at scale worldwide.


## **Delivering a lasting industry impact**


Across retail, hospitality, and golf, Lightspeed has redefined how businesses operate—streamlining workflows, enabling data-driven decision-making, and helping enhance profitability. From advanced inventory management to fast, reliable payments, Lightspeed continues to push the boundaries of what a modern POS and payments provider can deliver.


**Maximizing business success**


Industry-leading analytics tools like


[Benchmarks & Trends](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/benchmarks-and-trends/) give merchants critical insights, empowering them to optimize operations, improve margins, and plan for future growth.


“Lightspeed’s performance reports have helped us to challenge our sales targets by product category,” explains Camille Grange, Operations Manager of


[Lightspeed Restaurant](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/benchmarks-and-trends/) customer


La Micheline. “As a result, we have increased our alcohol sales by over 5% since we started focusing on the analytics provided by Lightspeed.”


Edson Enriquez, Owner of


[Lightspeed Retail](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/retail/) customer Limbo Jewelry, echoes this sentiment:


“Every store we use Lightspeed in, we know we’re saving money, and making better informed decisions that are helping us grow faster.”


**Simplifying workflows**


Lightspeed has been at the forefront of automation, AI, and cloud-based solutions, empowering businesses to work smarter and faster.


By integrating essential tools—such as inventory management, embedded payments, and marketing automation—into a single platform, Lightspeed enables merchants to eliminate inefficiencies, automate routine tasks, and focus on growth.


“The efficiencies that Lightspeed has created for our staff have been tremendous. Plus,


[Lightspeed Payments](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/golf/payments/) really makes the payment processing process seamless and easy,” comments Chris Humeniuk, President of TPC Toronto at Osprey Valley, a


[Lightspeed Golf](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/golf/) customer.


### **A trusted global partner**


Lightspeed has cultivated an extensive network of industry-leading partnerships, offering seamless integrations with top business tools and service providers—strengthening its operational ecosystem.


“Lightspeed is like a brain. We integrate as many platforms as possible into it, and thanks to Lightspeed, they all operate seamlessly,” comments


Marta Tokarz, Head of Beverage and Bar Operations at Cubitt House


, a


[Lightspeed Restaurant](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/) customer.


**Future forward, leading with innovation**


As Lightspeed celebrates 20 years of growth, the company remains focused on the future—continuing to drive innovation, efficiency, and transformation in commerce.


With AI-driven automation, enhanced data intelligence, and an expanding suite of commerce solutions, Lightspeed is committed to equipping businesses with the tools they need to scale in an increasingly complex market.


Beyond technology, Lightspeed’s culture remains a defining force behind its success. With a deep commitment to fostering an inclusive and innovative workplace, the company champions an environment where employees can bring their authentic selves to work, collaborate, and push boundaries. This dedication to people and progress fuels Lightspeed’s ability to lead industries forward.


As the next chapter unfolds, one thing remains clear—Lightspeed’s commitment to innovation and its customers has never been stronger. With a relentless focus on supporting businesses, driving growth, and shaping the future of commerce, the next 20 years promise to be even more transformative.
