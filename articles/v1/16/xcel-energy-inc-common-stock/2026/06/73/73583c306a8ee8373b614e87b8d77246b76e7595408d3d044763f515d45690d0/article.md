# Xcel Energy submits quarterly cost adjustments for natural gas and electric customers

**DENVER** (June 15, 2026) – Xcel Energy—Colorado customer bills for the third quarter of 2026 are expected to be flat compared to the second quarter of the year, with customers seeing a slight drop in their electric bills, but a slight increase in natural gas bills. The company submitted its proposed quarterly fuel price adjustments to the Colorado Public Utilities Commission.


Weather, storage and regional prices have been drivers of natural gas prices in Colorado and nationally.


For electric customers under the proposed quarterly Electric Commodity Adjustment for the third quarter, residential customers are expected to see a slight decrease of $2.35 per month while small businesses would pay approximately $6.64 less on a typical monthly bill compared to the second quarter of 2026.


Under the quarterly Gas Cost Adjustment, residential natural gas customers will see a slight increase compared to the second quarter, paying an average of $1.28 more on their typical monthly bill. Small businesses will see an average monthly increase of $7.39, as compared to the second quarter of 2026.


With minor exceptions, costs associated with increases or decreases through these electric and natural gas adjustments are passed along to customers on a dollar-per-dollar basis. Xcel Energy does not profit from an increase in natural gas or electricity fuel prices. If approved by the CPUC, new rates would go into effect on July 1, 2025, and would remain in effect for three months.
