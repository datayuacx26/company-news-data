### *By combining Hackett AI XPLR™ with the ServiceNow AI Platform, enterprises can evaluate AI opportunities based on expected business impact, implementation complexity and existing technology investments*


**MIAMI – June 17, 2026 –**[The Hackett Group, Inc.](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/) (NASDAQ: HCKT), an ROI-led[AI transformation](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/glossary/ai-transformation/) firm, today announced it has joined the ServiceNow Partner Program as a Consulting and Implementation partner to help organizations identify high-value AI opportunities and accelerate workflow transformation with Hackett AI XPLR™ and the ServiceNow AI Platform. Together, the partnership enables enterprises to move beyond AI experimentation by pinpointing the highest impact AI initiatives and driving execution at scale.


As AI investment accelerates, many organizations face a fundamental challenge: determining which opportunities will deliver meaningful business value and warrant enterprise-scale investment. Hackett AI XPLR™ helps leaders assess and prioritize the initiatives with the greatest potential impact by evaluating them within the context of their existing processes, automation footprint and data readiness. Combined with the ServiceNow AI Platform, organizations can move more quickly from opportunity assessment to execution, accelerating workflow transformation and value realization across the enterprise.


Now, organizations can apply these insights across end-to-end enterprise processes – for example, improving employee experiences, optimizing customer service workflows, reducing[operational costs](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/glossary/operational-costs/) and increasing workforce productivity – before scaling execution through the ServiceNow AI Platform.


“Organizations are investing billions of dollars in AI, yet many still lack a clear understanding of where those investments will create the greatest business value,” said The Hackett Group® Chairman and CEO, Ted A. Fernandez. “AI transformation is client-specific and process-first, not technology-led. Organizations that understand which workflows drive strategic and economic value will be better positioned to scale AI successfully. Together with ServiceNow, we’re helping clients move from[AI opportunity](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/glossary/ai-opportunities/) identification to operational execution faster and with greater confidence.”
