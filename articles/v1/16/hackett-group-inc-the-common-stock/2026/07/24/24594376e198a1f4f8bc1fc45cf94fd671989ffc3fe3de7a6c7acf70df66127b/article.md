### *Advanced technologies automate over 70% of processes across the hiring lifecycle while improving time-to-fill outcomes by 32%*


**MIAMI – July 28, 2026 –**[The Hackett Group, Inc.](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/) (NASDAQ: HCKT), an ROI-led[AI transformation](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/glossary/ai-transformation/) firm, today released its 2026 Talent Acquisition Vendor Assessments, focusing on the[talent acquisition](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/glossary/talent-acquisition/) technology marketplace. The report examines how talent acquisition technology providers are significantly affecting their customers’ recruiting outcomes, including time-to-fill and cost per hire.


The report is released at a time when organizations are relying more heavily on technology to support increasing workloads in human resources (HR) operations. According to[The Hackett Group’s 2026 HR Key Issues Study,](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/insights/2026-hr-key-issues-2601/) organizations are expecting an average 9% increase in workload while facing reductions in full-time equivalent (FTE) and operating budgets. To address this challenge, organizations are increasing their technology spend by 9%. Talent acquisition technology providers are speeding innovation through artificial intelligence (AI) and advanced automation to meet the new workload demands.


The 2026 Talent Acquisition Vendor Assessments provide an in-depth guide to some of the market’s most impactful technology providers. The report examines how their solutions improve companies’ recruiting operations through advanced automation,[agentic AI](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/glossary/agentic-ai/) , and candidate relationship management capabilities designed to speed and enhance the recruiting process for both recruiters and candidates. The report evaluated 12 providers for their capabilities and the value they deliver to customers based on vendor briefings and extensive customer interviews across 28 criteria.


Capability ratings assess a vendor’s capacity to provide recruiting solutions across critical operational areas. These include candidate experience, recruiter experience, candidate relationship management, internal mobility, interview solutions, candidate assessment, onboarding and analytics, as well as core platform functionalities such as user interface quality, platform flexibility and data integrations.


Value realization ratings assess a vendor’s impact across key metrics for recruiting performance, including implementation experience, cost per hire, time-to-fill, time-to-hire, automation improvement, qualified applicant improvement, internal mobility adoption, quality of hire, candidate diversity, customer service, overall satisfaction, and business value and pace of innovation.


Recruiting operations that use advanced technologies are realizing significant value across the hiring lifecycle. In critical metrics such as time-to-fill and time-to-hire, end users report an average improvement of 32%. Automation of hiring activities improved by an average of 72%, and organizations report a 70% increase in recruiter efficiency in their daily tasks. The efficiency gains are notably helping organizations maintain a strong recruiting function amid increasing workloads and reduced FTE support.


[AI adoption](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/glossary/ai-adoption/) reticence is continuing to wane among organizations for their talent acquisition function, and the embrace of these technologies is starting to make a meaningful impact on critical recruiting performance metrics. AI solutions – such as job description generation and candidate matching – remain the most frequently cited use cases, but customers are starting to expand their use in areas such as advanced analytics reporting to enable strategic workforce planning.


Talent acquisition technology providers are investing heavily in[AI solutions](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/glossary/ai-solutions/) , particularly agentic, over the next 12-24 months. These solutions are designed to speed recruiters’ daily tasks while improving the candidate experience through conversational interfaces that provide a personalized, guided approach.


“AI adoption in talent acquisition is ramping up in organizations from small and medium-sized businesses to large enterprises,” said Matthew Merker, senior research director for HCM Solution Intelligence at The Hackett Group®. “As AI solutions become more readily accepted, talent acquisition technology providers are blazing forward with new capabilities to improve candidate matching and recruiting operations while protecting organizations from risks such as candidate fraud.”


By using this comprehensive analysis, talent acquisition professionals can make informed purchasing decisions aligned with the strategic priorities and critical metrics they seek to impact in recruiting outcomes.
