## Health benefit costs are rising, with medical trend forecast to exceed 11% globally in 2026, but cutting benefits is not the only answer.


This guidebook helps HR, benefits and total rewards leaders take a more strategic approach — one that controls cost, protects employee value and reflects local market realities.


### To address health benefit cost challenges, focus on three key steps:


- **Align benefits to strategy** : Define philosophy before redesigning. Measure trade-offs before cutting.
- **Optimize operations and financing** : Right-size financing (fully insured vs. self-insured), eliminate vendor duplication, simplify administration.
- **Improve outcomes** : Shift to prevention and early intervention. Connect employees to appropriate care.
