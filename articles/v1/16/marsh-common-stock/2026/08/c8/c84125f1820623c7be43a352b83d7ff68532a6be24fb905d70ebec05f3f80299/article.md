# Designing global health strategies that improve workforce resilience

In partnership with Marsh, Reward and Employee Benefits Association's (REBA) latest report, *Designing global health strategies that improve workforce resilience* , examines how employers can take a more strategic approach to health and benefits — using digital innovation to shift from reactive spending to preventative measures, improve employee access and navigation, and balance global consistency with local relevance.


## A more connected approach to workforce health


Reward and benefits leaders face mounting pressure to do more than manage renewals and costs; they must also influence workforce resilience, employee experience, and long-term business sustainability. This challenge is especially tough for multinationals, as health systems, digital maturity, regulations, norms, and expectations vary widely across countries. Strategies effective in one market may fail elsewhere.


[AI and digital tools are creating new possibilities](https://www.marsh.com/en/services/employee-health-benefits/insights/how-to-leverage-ai-in-employee-benefits.html) for efficiency, insight, and employee support. For reward and benefits leaders, the opportunity is not simply to digitize what already exists — it is to build a more connected, prevention-focused, and resilient global benefits strategy.
