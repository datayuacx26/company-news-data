# Meet the Team Behind Marriott’s 10,000th Property

When people hear that Marriott has opened its 10,000th property, it’s easy to focus on the number. Ten thousand hotels. Ten thousand milestones. Ten thousand places where associates welcome guests every day.


But every hotel opening starts the same way: with a group of people coming together to build something from the ground up.


At the newly opened[JW Marriott Ranthambore Resort & Spa](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/jairj-jw-marriott-ranthambore-resort-and-spa/overview/) in India, associates spent months preparing for the moment the first guests would arrive. For many, it was their first time being part of a hotel opening. For all of them, it was a chance to be part of Marriott history.

For Aishwarya K., Assistant Manager of Food & Beverage Service, one of the most memorable parts of the journey wasn’t a single moment. It was the people who made it possible.


“There is a unique sense of excitement and anticipation that comes from knowing you are building something from the ground up and preparing to welcome the very first guests,” she says.


Courtesy of the JW Ranthambore team


Working alongside associates from different states and backgrounds, Aishwarya says the pre-opening experience was both challenging and rewarding. Together, the team learned from one another, built relationships, and began creating the culture that would define the hotel.


For Arpit S., Front Office Executive, some of his favorite memories came during training.


“When the trainers arrived and gave us such a warm welcome, it was truly one of the most memorable moments for me,” he says. Through simulations and hands-on learning, associates prepared for opening day while building confidence in their new roles. “I learned a lot while having fun throughout the journey.”


Courtesy of the JW Ranthambore team


Proud? Nervous? Excited? If you’re wondering how it would feel to be part of a milestone like this, the JW Marriott Ranthambore team has the answers.


“I feel proud and grateful to be part of this historic moment,” says Melusulu T., Guest Service Associate. “It’s exciting to know that my work contributes to a milestone that will always be a part of Marriott International’s history.”


For Indranil, Housekeeping Executive this is not just a future sweet memory but a career-defining moment. “It gives me immense pride to be part of such a historic milestone,” he says. “Being part of the pre-opening team has been a huge milestone in my career as well.”


Courtesy of the JW Ranthambore team


The guests may see a beautiful new resort. What they won’t always see are the months of collaboration, training, preparation, and teamwork that made it possible. But for the associates who helped bring Marriott’s 10,000th property to life, that’s the part they’ll never forget.
