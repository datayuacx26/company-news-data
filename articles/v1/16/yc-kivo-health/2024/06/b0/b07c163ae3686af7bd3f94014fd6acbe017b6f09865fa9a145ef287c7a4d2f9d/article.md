# Kivo Health Participates in House Ways and Means Committee Discussions

Kivo Health had a productive visit to DC last week.


We participated in a hands-on discussion with Members and staff on the House Ways and Means Committee before the hearing "Enhancing Access to Care at Home in Rural and Underserved Communities."


We had a number of productive conversations with policymakers around the importance of telehealth for improving access, including for rural populations, and how to reduce overall costs of care with pulmonary rehab.


We commend the bipartisan support for continued coverage of Medicare telehealth services and look forward to continuing to work with policymakers to innovate around respiratory care.
