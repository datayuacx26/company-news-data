# Priority Health Partners with Kivo Health

We’re partnering with[Priority Health](https://www.priorityhealth.com/) , Michigan’s second-largest health plan - covering 1.3M lives - to bring our AI-powered virtual COPD care program to members across the state. Together, we’re delivering AI-powered, scalable, in-home care to empower people with COPD avoid preventable hospitalizations and manage their condition from home, resulting in reduced costs and healthier seniors. This partnership is a major step toward scaling value-based, outcomes-driven virtual care for seniors with high cost chronic diseases nationwide.
