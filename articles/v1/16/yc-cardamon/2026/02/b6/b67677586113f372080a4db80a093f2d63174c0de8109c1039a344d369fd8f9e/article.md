# Partnering with Microsoft

We’re pleased to announce that we’ve partnered with Microsoft as part of Microsoft’s AI Partnership Program.


We’re working alongside Microsoft to bring enterprise-grade, secure AI to organisations operating in highly regulated environments.


This partnership enables us to combine startup speed with Microsoft’s trusted infrastructure, security, and scalability - helping regulated businesses deploy AI safely and effectively at scale. If you’re a regulated organisation looking to automate up to 95% of regulatory compliance workflows, we’d be happy to explore how we can support your transformation.
