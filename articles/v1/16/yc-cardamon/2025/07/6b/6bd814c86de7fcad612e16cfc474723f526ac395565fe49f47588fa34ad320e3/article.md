# How is GenAI changing how firms manage regulatory change?

Managing regulatory change has always been one of the most painful parts of running a regulated financial firm.


New laws, consultations, and guidance are constantly being issued across multiple jurisdictions. Teams are forced to track regulator websites, newsletters, and even social media posts to stay up to date. Some adopt legacy RegTech solutions, but the feedback we hear is consistent: *the tools aren’t tailored* . They provide a feed of everything - but not the insight of what actually matters.


The result?


-


It’s reactive.


-


It’s manual.


-


And worst of all - it’s slow.


In a world where regulatory velocity is only accelerating, that’s no longer sustainable.


## Where GenAI Comes In


Generative AI is reshaping this picture. Its highest leverage lies in **customisation** . Instead of drowning compliance teams in undifferentiated updates, AI can filter, interpret, and contextualise changes so that firms only see what’s truly relevant to them.


Imagine moving from a generic news feed to a *personalised regulatory radar* - one tuned to your products, jurisdictions, and entity structure.


At Cardamon, we’ve built exactly that. Our platform doesn’t just track updates. It interprets them, explains the “why,” and maps them directly onto the firm’s obligations and controls. This unlocks a whole new level of automation.


## What’s Possible Today


The most forward-thinking firms are already moving beyond passive monitoring. With GenAI, they can:


-


**Interpret new regulations across jurisdictions in minutes** - not months.


-


**Extract obligations automatically** from laws, guidance, and consultations.


-


**Map changes to impacted controls, policies, and business units** with precision.


-


**Suggest remediation actions** tailored to the firm’s product suite and regulatory footprint.


This shifts compliance from a reactive, manual process into a proactive, automated capability.


## The Competitive Edge


The impact is tangible. At Cardamon, we’ve seen clients cut the time needed to assess a major regulatory change from **months to days** . That’s not just an efficiency win - it’s a competitive advantage.


-


**Faster launches.** Products can go to market sooner without waiting on lengthy compliance reviews.


-


**Smarter spend.** Legal and consulting costs drop dramatically.


-


**Reduced risk.** Firms gain confidence that nothing material slips through the cracks.


## The Bigger Picture


The future of regulatory change management isn’t about drowning teams in more data. It’s about empowering them with *better* data - structured, contextualised, and auditable.


GenAI won’t replace compliance officers. But it will upgrade them, giving them leverage to manage global regulatory velocity at scale. The firms that embrace this shift early won’t just keep up with regulators - they’ll move ahead of competitors.


Because in compliance, speed and confidence aren’t just nice to have. They’re survival.
