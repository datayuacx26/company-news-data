🚨 **Cardamon x OKX -** compliance at OKX speed - powered by Cardamon **** 🚨


We are excited to share that **Cardamon is partnering with OKX** , one of the world’s leading crypto exchanges with over 60 million customers worldwide, to help transform their regulatory compliance capabilities using AI.


As global regulation ramps up and clear regulatory framework is becoming a reality, compliance is no longer a back-office function, it’s a competitive advantage.


OKX is at the forefront of this shift, and we’re proud to support their efforts to move faster **without ever compromising on compliance** .

"We are excited to continue leveraging Cardamon’s leading-edge AI to drive even greater impact across our compliance program.”


OKX joins a growing list of forward-thinking firms using Cardamon to simplify compliance in a fast-moving, high-stakes industry.
