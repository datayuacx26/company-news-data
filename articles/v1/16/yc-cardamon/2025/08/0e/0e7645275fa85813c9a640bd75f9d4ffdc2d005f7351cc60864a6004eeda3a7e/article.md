# Cardamon receives ISO27001 certification

Cardamon is proud to announce that we have achieved **ISO 27001 certification** , the leading international standard for information security management.


This milestone reinforces our commitment to the highest standards of **data protection, risk management, and global compliance** . For our clients, it means added confidence that sensitive information is safeguarded with industry best practices as we continue to deliver AI-powered compliance solutions.


Achieving ISO 27001 is more than a certification - it’s a reflection of Cardamon’s dedication to building secure, resilient, and trustworthy infrastructure for regulated financial institutions worldwide.
