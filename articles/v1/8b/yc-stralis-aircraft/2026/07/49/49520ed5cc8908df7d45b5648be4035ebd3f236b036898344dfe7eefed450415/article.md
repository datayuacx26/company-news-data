# Stralis achieves the first spin of AULD MATE, powering ground test bed Clyde, with an updated system that's twice the power and half the size

This week we hit a major step forward at Stralis: the first spin of AULD MATE on Clyde, our ground test bed. The system we're taking to flight later this year is now twice the power and half the size, which is exactly what aircraft applications demand. Prove it on Clyde. Move it into Bonnie. Start taxi testing. Keep pushing. In parallel, we're scaling the platform for drones, where the market pull is real: operators want more range, more endurance, and fewer battery constraints.
