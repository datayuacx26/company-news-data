# The best alternative to Beautiful.ai: Try Slidely!

Beautiful.ai has been a popular choice for AI-powered presentations, especially for teams looking for design automation and smart templates. But it's not the only option, and depending on your workflow, it might not be the best fit.


Slidely offers a different approach to AI PowerPoint presentation creation, one that prioritizes speed, document conversion, and flexibility over rigid design templates. If Beautiful.ai feels limiting or doesn't match how you actually work, here's what makes Slidely worth considering.


## **What does Beautiful.ai do well?**


Before comparing alternatives, it's worth acknowledging what Beautiful.ai brings to the table. It's built around automated design and smart slide formatting.


- **Strong design automation** : Beautiful.ai automatically adjusts layouts as you add or remove content. Slides reflow, text resizes, and spacing adjusts without manual intervention.
- **Template-driven workflow** : The platform offers pre-designed templates for common presentation types, pitch decks, reports, and project updates. You pick a template and fill in content.
- **Team collaboration features** : Multiple users can work on the same presentation, with version control and commenting built in.
- **Brand consistency tools** : Teams can upload brand assets and ensure all presentations follow the same visual standards.


These features work well for teams that:


- Need heavily designed, visually polished decks.
- Follow consistent presentation formats regularly.
- Prioritize automated design adjustments over manual control.


But Beautiful.ai's template-first approach creates friction for certain workflows, which is where alternatives like Slidely fit better.


## **Where does Beautiful.ai fall short?**


No tool fits every use case. Beautiful.ai's strengths in design automation come with trade-offs that frustrate some users.


- **Limited flexibility** : The automated design system controls layout decisions. If you need specific spacing, custom arrangements, or unique visual structures, you're fighting the tool rather than using it.
- **Template dependency** : Beautiful.ai works best when your content fits its templates. If your presentation structure doesn't match pre-built formats, customization becomes tedious.
- **Document conversion gaps** : Turning existing documents, reports, or text files into slides isn't Beautiful.ai's primary focus. You'll spend time manually copying content rather than uploading and converting.
- **Pricing structure** : Beautiful.ai's pricing scales with team size and features, which can get expensive for smaller teams or individual users who need occasional presentation work.


According to[Gartner research, 60% of employees](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2021-11-02-gartner-survey-finds-60-percent-of-employees-experience-frustration-with-new-software) report frustration with new software, especially when tools prioritize automation over user control and default behaviors don’t match real workflows.


These limitations matter most when:


- You're converting documents into presentations regularly.
- You need quick iterations without template constraints.
- You want control over layout decisions.
- You're working solo or with a small team.
