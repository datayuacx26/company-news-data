## **How do you write prompts that generate better slides?**


Free[AI tool to make PPT](https://slidely.ai/) platforms work better with clear, structured prompts. Vague instructions produce vague slides. Specific prompts produce usable results.


**Start with structure** : Instead of "make a presentation about marketing," try "create a 10-slide presentation about digital marketing basics covering SEO, social media, email marketing, and analytics."


Structured prompts help the AI understand:


- How many slides do you need?
- What topics to cover?
- What order makes sense?
- What level of detail to include?


**Specify your audience** : Adding context about who will see the presentation improves tone and depth. "Explain cloud computing for beginners" produces different slides than "explain cloud computing for IT managers."


**Request specific formats** : If you need particular slide types, mention them. "Include one comparison slide, two data slides with charts, and three explanation slides" gives the AI clear direction.


**Keep prompts concise** : Detailed prompts work better than paragraph-long descriptions. Break complex presentations into sections and generate them separately if needed.


**Example prompt structure** :


"Create an 8-slide presentation about remote work productivity. Cover communication tools, time management, workspace setup, and team collaboration. The target audience is new remote workers. Include practical tips on each slide."


This prompt specifies slides, topics, structure, audience, and content type. The AI has enough information to create something useful without guessing.

## **How do you maintain brand consistency with free tools?**


Free tools rarely include advanced branding features, but you can still create brand-inclined presentations with manual adjustments.


- **Set design standards early** : Pick your fonts, colors, and logo placement before generating slides. Apply these consistently across all slides after AI generates the structure.
- **Use brand assets strategically** : Even if the tool doesn't store brand templates, you can manually add logos, use brand colors, and maintain consistent font choices across presentations.
- **Create a style reference** : Keep a simple document with your brand guidelines (hex codes, font names, logo files). Reference it each time you create presentations to maintain consistency.
- **Refine after generation** : Let the AI handle structure and content placement, then apply brand elements manually. This workflow separates content creation from brand application.


Free tools require more manual work for branding, but the time saved on structure and layout still makes them worthwhile for occasional use.

## **When should you upgrade from free to paid AI tools?**


Free AI PowerPoint presentation tools have clear use cases, but they reach limits quickly for regular users.


**Upgrade when you need** :


- **Regular presentation creation** : If you create slides weekly, paid tools save more time with features like document uploads, brand templates, and faster processing.
- **Team collaboration** : Free versions rarely include sharing, version control, or multi-user editing. Teams need paid plans for workflow efficiency.
- **Advanced customization** : Brand templates, custom layouts, and detailed design control require paid tiers.
- **Document conversion** : Turning reports and text files into slides works better with paid tools that handle uploads directly.


Paid AI tools specifically target these time-consuming activities, making the cost worthwhile for frequent users.


**Stay with free when** :


- You create presentations occasionally (monthly or less).
- Basic formatting meets your needs.
- You're comfortable with manual brand application.
- You're testing AI workflows before committing.


The transition point usually happens when the time saved justifies the subscription cost. For someone creating one presentation per month, free tools suffice. For someone creating three presentations per week, paid tools pay for themselves in hours saved.
