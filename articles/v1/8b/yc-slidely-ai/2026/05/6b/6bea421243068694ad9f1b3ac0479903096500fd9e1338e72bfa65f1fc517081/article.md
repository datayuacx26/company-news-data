# Tired of Fixing Manus Slides? Here Are 7 Better Tools for Business Presentations in 2026

Manus arrived with serious momentum. The promise of an autonomous AI agent that breaks your brief into hundreds of micro-tasks, sends parallel research agents across the web, synthesizes everything, and hands you a finished presentation sounded like exactly what business teams had been waiting for.


Then people used it for actual work.


The research output is genuinely impressive. Manus digs deeper than most dedicated presentation tools, pulling from multiple sources simultaneously with a level of coverage that general-purpose AI can't match. The problem is what comes after.[Users consistently report](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/manus-ai-reviews) buggy PowerPoint exports, design inconsistencies, random gaps in slide layouts, misaligned images, broken text formatting, and no way to enforce brand standards across the output. One reviewer described it bluntly: using Manus for presentations is like using a Swiss Army knife to carve a steak. It technically *works, but you'll have a better experience with the right tool for the job.*


For business teams in the US where presentations go to enterprise clients, investor committees, board members, and procurement teams with strict security requirements, "it technically works" is not a standard that closes deals or keeps contracts.


The deeper issue is structural.[Manus is a generalist agent](https://www.lindy.ai/blog/manus-ai-review) trying to do a little bit of everything. It supports limited integrations, offers minimal output customization, and frequently hits context length limits mid-task. For broad research coverage, it's a capable tool. For a focused, on-brand, PowerPoint-native business presentation that needs to survive a boardroom, it consistently falls short.


This guide compares 7 tools that go all the way to finishing not just to the first draft. Each is evaluated on the criteria that matter for serious business use: PowerPoint export quality, brand consistency, agentic workflow depth, security compliance, and US enterprise fit. Slidely AI is the lead recommendation for each of these, and this review explains exactly why.


## **Why Business Teams Are Moving Away from Manus**


Before comparing alternatives, it's worth being specific about what Manus gets wrong because the right replacement depends on which failure mode is costing you the most time.


**Export quality is the most common complaint.** Manus's PPTX files frequently require manual reconstruction. Shapes and charts need to be recreated. Text formatting breaks when opened on different machines. Slide designs include random gaps below content and misaligned image-paragraph layouts that would not survive a client review. The[Alai blog's March 2026 analysis](https://getalai.com/blog/best-manus-alternatives) found that even Manus's high-quality Nano Banana Pro mode introduces its own constraint: you cannot mix those premium-design slides with regular slides in the same deck, a significant limitation for complex business presentations.


**No brand automation.** Manus has no mechanism to apply or enforce brand standards. Every deck it generates is a new visual experiment. For organizations where presentation consistency is a compliance or brand governance issue, this is disqualifying.


**Not built for enterprise security.** US enterprise procurement teams ask for SOC 2 Type II certification as a baseline. Manus does not offer it. For teams in finance, healthcare, legal, or any regulated environment, this creates friction before a single slide is generated.


**Parallel research ≠ persuasive narrative.** Comprehensive content coverage and a coherent business argument are different outputs. Manus tends to produce the former and leave the latter to you. Distilling parallel agent research into a focused, executive-grade narrative is editorial work and it frequently costs more time than the generation saved.


**It gets stuck.**[Real-world testing](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/manus-ai-reviews) shows Manus frequently hitting walls: paywalled articles, CAPTCHA blocks, context length limits mid-task. For time-sensitive business workflows, unpredictable failure points are expensive.


The tools below each address one or more of these gaps. One addresses all of them.
