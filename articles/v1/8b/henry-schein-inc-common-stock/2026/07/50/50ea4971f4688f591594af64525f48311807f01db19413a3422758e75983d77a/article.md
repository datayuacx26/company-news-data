# The Connected Practice

Strong operational systems help patients get through the door, but long-term growth depends on trust, communication, and patient follow-through. This session explores how high-performing practices create consistent patient experiences through clinical clarity, financial transparency, and proactive reappointment workflows. You’ll learn strategies for improving case acceptance, strengthening retention, and building long-term patient relationships that support both patient outcomes and practice growth.
