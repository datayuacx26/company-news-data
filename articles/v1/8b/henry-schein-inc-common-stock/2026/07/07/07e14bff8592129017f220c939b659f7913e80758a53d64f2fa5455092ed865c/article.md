# 2026 Dental Trends Outlook

### A foreword by Brian Weatherly, CEO, Henry Schein One


Every thriving practice stands on three pillars—patients, people, and process. When these are in sync, profitability isn’t just a possibility; it’s the natural outcome. Today, technology is the thread weaving these pillars together, reshaping how we deliver care, empower our teams, and build lasting financial strength.


As you read through this report, you’ll see a common theme echoed by our expert panel: **the practices that lean into new technologies and treat today’s challenges as opportunities to build for tomorrow are the ones that will lead the way.**


Allow me to call out the top three emerging trends that will shape our path forward:


- AI and automation will be your growth engines.
- Cohesive technology will be your best defense against margin pressure.
- The current climate is your moment to stand apart.


This report reviews these trends through the lens of patients, processes, people, and profits — and explains why the time is now to invest in your team and tools. By doing so, you'll build a strong platform for growth and shape the industry's future. This report is your guide forward.


Best wishes for success in 2026.
