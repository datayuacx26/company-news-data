# What If You Could Build Without Limits? With 50 kb DNA, Now You Can

A little more than a year ago, we launched Ansa Biotechnologies’ very first commercial[DNA Synthesis products](https://ansabio.com/products/) : Ansa Clonal DNA and Ansa DNA Fragments up to 900 bp long. It was a critical milestone in the life of our company, but we knew that the real magic for customers would come when we could deliver significantly longer, high-quality synthetic DNA constructs..


Today, we are proud to announce the commercial availability of our high-quality clonal DNA products as long as 50 kb — almost 100-fold the size of where we began! This 50 kb synthetic DNA is manufactured using our proprietary enzymatic DNA synthesis platform, ensuring the same accuracy, reliability, and sequence fidelity that customers have come to expect from Ansa Biotechnologies.. And it’s available at market-beating prices, as little as 28 cents per base pair with turnaround times less than 25 business days.


If you’ve read the[Ansa blog](https://ansabio.com/resources/blog/) before, you probably know that our company was founded by scientists who got so frustrated by the inability to get the DNA required for their experiments that they set out to develop a novel[DNA synthesis technology](https://ansabio.com/technology/) — one that would expand access to far more sequence designs and perform more reliably than conventional approaches. The platform they created is the reason that Ansa can deliver DNA no one else can.


Today’s most widely used DNA synthesis services generally can’t produce constructs longer than 10 kb. That’s a huge limitation for scientists, who must either assemble shorter fragments themselves—an error-prone, time-consuming process—or abandon the longer sequences they need for critical synthetic biology and genetic engineering research.


Our enzymatic DNA synthesis technology overcomes these barriers, enabling us to produce ultra-long and complex synthetic DNA constructs that often trigger rejections from other vendors. It reflects our steadfast commitment to the scientific community. You shouldn’t have to compromise your research because of a vendor’s limitations. If you need long or complex DNA to move your work forward, you should be able to count on your synthetic DNA vendor to deliver. That’s why we introduced the Ansa On-Time Guarantee, ensuring that customers receive their orders in full and on time or the whole thing is free.
