# Bellabeat Launches Spring

## Bellabeat launches Spring – the first smart water bottle powered by AI


SAN FRANCISCO, CA (May 30, 2017) – On Tuesday, May 30, Bellabeat is launching Spring, the first smart water bottle powered by AI. Spring is the next product within Bellabeat’s ecosystem of health devices for women, designed to help them with meaningful data about their health and give guidance to form healthy habits that improve their well-being.


Bellabeat Spring is the first smart water bottle powered by AI that can tell you exactly how much water you should be drinking based on your needs. It takes into account how active you are, how much you sleep, your reproductive health and stress sensitivity. It syncs wirelessly with the app where you get a snapshot of your daily progress. By learning from your lifestyle habits, Spring tailors your goals and teaches you how to reach them with notifications and reminders that help you form the habit of effortless hydration.


Key features include:

- **Tailored intake goals** – Gives meaningful insights and goals based on the user’s lifestyle.
- **Hydration tracking** – Informs users about their hydration throughout the day. Spring logs daily water intake and makes sure users are properly hydrated at all times.
- **Drinking reminders** – Gives reminders to drink more frequently with helpful notifications leading you to create a[healthy hydration habit](https://bellabeat.com/lifestyle-beauty/healthy-family-habits/) .


“With Leaf Nature and Leaf Urban, our health trackers for women, we received valuable feedback from our users and together with a huge dataset of users’ drinking behaviours and motions, we were able to train a powerful artificial intelligence algorithm that will accurately predict your water intake through a set of smart sensors.” said Urska Srsen, co-founder and CPO at Bellabeat.


Spring is made out of light borosilicate glass which secures the purity of taste and makes the bottle lightweight and easy to carry. The carefully designed anti-slip grip surface helps prevent breakage. (16 fl.oz)


There’s no charging as Spring uses a 6-month replaceable battery and users will be able to customize Spring to make it their own by choosing different colors of interchangeable rubber grips and lids. A range of various Spring accessories are also going to be available soon.
