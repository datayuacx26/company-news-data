## Join Fortrea at European Respiratory Society (ERS) from 5th till 9th of September


Join us to learn who Fortrea are and how we are striving to be the best CRO partner to innovative Biotech and Pharma companies to drive forward clinical trials with ease. The **European Respiratory Society (ERS)** is the largest scientific and clinical organization in Europe focused on respiratory medicine and lung health. It brings together physicians, researchers, healthcare professionals, and other experts working in respiratory diseases such as asthma, COPD, lung cancer, sleep disorders, and interstitial lung diseases.
