# ChinaTrials 18 Conference

Fortrea is excited to participate in ChinaTrials 18, one of the leading forums focused on the future of clinical development in China and across global markets. As China continues to emerge as one of the fastest and most capable environments for clinical trials, this event brings together clinical development leaders, investors, and biopharmaceutical innovators to explore the strategies, partnerships, and development models shaping the next generation of global research.


Visit Fortrea at Booth # 8


to connect with our experts and learn how we help sponsors accelerate development programs through deep therapeutic expertise, operational excellence, and extensive experience navigating complex global and cross-border clinical trials. Join us in Shanghai as industry leaders discuss faster trial execution, stronger data quality, and the opportunities driving China's growing role in global drug development.
