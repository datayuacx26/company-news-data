Fortrea’s Clinical Research Unit (CRU) in Madison, Wisconsin, is a purpose-built facility designed to support the complexities of early-phase clinical trials. The site features an on-site GMP pharmacy, radiolabeled trial capabilities, laboratory services, consultation rooms, telemetry and monitoring stations, and a fully implemented electronic bedside data capture solution. With an 88-bed capacity, it is one of four dedicated Fortrea CRUs and plays an important role in supporting the company's early-stage clinical research capabilities.
