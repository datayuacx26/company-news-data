# Outsourcing in Clinical Trials Southern California 2026

## Outsourcing in Clinical Trials (OCT) Southern California 2026


Fortrea will attend the 14th Annual Outsourcing in Clinical Trials (OCT) Southern California, September 15–16, 2026, in San Diego, CA. This premier industry event brings together pharma, biotech, and CRO professionals to discuss outsourcing strategies, sponsor–CRO collaboration, and innovations driving clinical development.


Fortrea will be on-site to connect with industry peers and discuss how our flexible, patient-centric solutions help sponsors optimize clinical trial execution and accelerate development. With 450+ attendees and 30+ expert speakers, OCT SoCal provides valuable opportunities to share insights and explore the future of clinical research.
