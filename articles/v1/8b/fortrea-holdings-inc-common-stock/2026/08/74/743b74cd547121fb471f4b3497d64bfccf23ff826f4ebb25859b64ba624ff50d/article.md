# Fortrea Innovation & Technology Summit – EU

## Experience the future of clinical development.


As scientific innovation accelerates and clinical development grows increasingly complex, organisations must harness the power of technology, data, and artificial intelligence to bring therapies to patients faster and more efficiently.


Join industry peers and Fortrea leaders at the **Fortrea Innovation & Technology Summit – EU** where together, we will explore how emerging technologies are reshaping the future of clinical trials and discuss practical approaches to driving innovation across the development lifecycle.


Through expert presentations, executive discussions, and peer networking, attendees will gain insights into the latest trends influencing clinical research, including AI-enabled solutions, digital transformation, operational excellence, and patient-focused innovation.


**Where:** Basel Marriott Hotel | Basel, Switzerland
**When:** Monday, November 2, 2026 | 13:00 - 20:00


To learn more and register your interest, please visit the[Fortrea Innovation & Technology Summit – EU Event Page](https://hub.fortrea.com/fortrea-innovation-technology-summit-eu) .
