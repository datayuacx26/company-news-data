# SCRS Global Site Solutions Summit 2026

Fortrea is proud to participate in the **SCRS Global Site Solutions Summit 2026** , where research sites, sponsors, CROs, and industry representatives come together to shape the future of clinical research. The event offers a valuable opportunity to exchange ideas, strengthen partnerships, and collaborate on innovative approaches to clinical trial delivery.


As a leading global CRO, Fortrea works with sponsors to navigate complex development challenges through flexible, patient-focused solutions. Stop by **Booth #506** to meet our team and learn how we're helping advance clinical research around the world.


#### Presentation


**Date:** Saturday, October 17th, 2026


**Time:** 3:30pm-4:30pm


###### **Recruitment Revolution: Innovation & Recovery**


Traditional recruitment processes aren't keeping pace with enrollment demands. This session tackles two critical angles: deploying innovations that drive enrollment success and rapidly recovering when challenges emerge. Learn to recognize early warning signs within 30-60 days, master lead conversion, navigate budget advocacy, and implement collaborative problem-solving. Sites and recruitment specialists share stories with enrollment data, costs, and honest discussion about what's working, what's backfiring, and how to pivot quickly.
