## **Retail Media Networks Demand Data**


The rise of retail media networks dominated conversations,


[emphasizing the critical need for measurable, data-driven insights](https://nrf.com/blog/golden-age-retail-media-networks) . Standard AI equips retailers and brands with the tools to track media effectiveness and refine strategies in real time, ensuring that in-store media generates measurable ROI. Data isn’t just helpful, but it’s essential for success in this rapidly evolving space.


## **Smarter Stores Start with Actionable Insights**


The smartest stores don’t just sell – they learn. Retailers are embracing solutions that allow them to act quickly and strategically. From product placement to shopper behavior, actionable insights are enabling teams to make data-backed decisions that drive sales and build loyalty.


[Standard AI’s VISION platform](https://standard.ai/) ensures retailers have the tools they need to optimize every square foot of their stores.


## **Balancing Innovation with Human-Centric Solutions**


Technology and human expertise are not mutually exclusive—they enhance one another. Major retailers showcased how blending advanced technology with team expertise creates a competitive edge. At Standard AI, we’re focused on solutions that enhance both: giving teams actionable insights while ensuring shopper experiences remain personalized and meaningful.
