# Conduent Recognized on TIME's America's Best Companies 2026 List

*Award based on employee satisfaction, financial performance and sustainability transparency*


[Conduent Incorporated](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.conduent.com%2F&esheet=54569165&newsitemid=20260713766178&lan=en-US&anchor=Conduent+Incorporated&index=1&md5=34ade4b2171a10ab88bf57765e8fe2ab) (Nasdaq: CNDT), a global technology-driven business solutions and services company, today announced it has been recognized on TIME’s inaugural edition of American’s Best Companies list. The list can be viewed on time.com at[https://time.com/article/2026/07/09/americas-best-companies-2026/](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftime.com%2Farticle%2F2026%2F07%2F09%2Famericas-best-companies-2026%2F&esheet=54569165&newsitemid=20260713766178&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Ftime.com%2Farticle%2F2026%2F07%2F09%2Famericas-best-companies-2026%2F&index=2&md5=22d758a65e4c4312efdad18391271d10) .


For the first time, TIME and Statista are awarding the titles of “America’s Best Companies 2026” through an independent survey assessing the nation’s top-performing organizations across key dimensions.


The TIME America’s Best Companies 2026 ranking identifies top-performing U.S. companies using a data-driven evaluation across these areas:


- Employee Satisfaction: Based on ~217,000 employee surveys assessing workplace culture, pay, conditions, and employer reputation
- Financial Performance: Analysis of revenue growth, profitability, and asset performance using multi-year financial data
- Sustainability Evaluation of environmental impact, social responsibility, and governance practices using standardized ESG metrics


“Being named one of TIME’s America’s Best Companies reflects what matters most, our people, our performance, and our principles. As we transform into a faster, more accountable, more outcomes‑driven organization, our associates continue to bring the empathy, innovation, and ownership that define who we are,” said Harsha V. Agadi, President and Chief Executive Officer, Conduent. “This recognition reinforces that our commitment to our people and to responsible, high‑performance execution is both resonating internally and delivering measurable impact for our clients and the communities we serve.”

**About Conduent**
Conduent delivers digital business solutions and services spanning the commercial, government and transportation spectrum – creating valuable outcomes for its clients and the millions of people who count on them. The Company leverages cloud computing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation and advanced analytics to deliver mission-critical solutions. Through a dedicated global team of approximately 48,000 associates, process expertise and advanced technologies, Conduent’s solutions and services digitally transform its clients’ operations to enhance customer experiences, improve performance, increase efficiencies and reduce costs. Conduent adds momentum to its clients’ missions in many ways including disbursing approximately $80 billion in government payments annually, enabling approximately 2.0 billion customer service interactions annually, empowering millions of employees through HR services every year and processing over 14 million tolling transactions every day.
