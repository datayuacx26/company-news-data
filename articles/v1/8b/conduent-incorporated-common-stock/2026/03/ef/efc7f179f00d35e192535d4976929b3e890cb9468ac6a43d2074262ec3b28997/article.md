# Conduent Again Named to 'GovTech 100' List of Companies for Helping Governments Deliver Effective Solutions and Services

*Conduent recognized for the fifth consecutive year*


*Honored for improving how governments and businesses interact with the patients, customers, and employees they serve*


FLORHAM PARK, N.J. --[Conduent Incorporated](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.conduent.com%2F&esheet=54436405&newsitemid=20260303582801&lan=en-US&anchor=Conduent+Incorporated&index=1&md5=15d445b457591bc3f37fffb06175be41) (Nasdaq: CNDT), a global technology-driven business solutions and services company, has been named to the[2026 “GovTech 100” list](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.govtech.com%2F100&esheet=54436405&newsitemid=20260303582801&lan=en-US&anchor=2026+%26%238220%3BGovTech+100%26%238221%3B+list&index=2&md5=b30bc2ff3b323aad4706773de6a0b14b) compiled by *Government Technology* magazine and GovTech.com. This marks the fifth consecutive year Conduent has been included, recognizing the company’s role in helping governments and businesses improve interactions with patients, customers, and employees.


Launched in 2016 and updated annually, the GovTech 100 is developed through data analysis and editorial review by *Government Technology* , informed by market experts, government leaders, and investors. The[2026 list](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.erepublic.com%2Fpress%2Fgovernment-technologys-2026-govtech-100-highlights-a-market-ready-to-scale%2F&esheet=54436405&newsitemid=20260303582801&lan=en-US&anchor=2026+list&index=3&md5=807ba77d17d814098f0f34517ed1ee9d) “highlights companies helping agencies demonstrate value, improve service delivery, and support more informed decision-making across core functions.”


“Being recognized on the GovTech 100 list for the fifth consecutive year reflects our strong commitment to helping government agencies deliver effective, accessible solutions and services to the people they serve,” said Anna Sever, President, Government Solutions at Conduent. “This achievement illustrates our associates’ expertise and their passion for service innovation, which drives our everyday impact on people’s lives.”


Conduent’s[Government Solutions](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.conduent.com%2Fgovernment-solutions%2F&esheet=54436405&newsitemid=20260303582801&lan=en-US&anchor=Government+Solutions&index=4&md5=1fff34f9de41644228ab478186d3782e) business provides U.S. agencies with solutions and services for healthcare claims administration, government benefit payments, eligibility and enrollment, and child support. The company helps administer critical benefit programs such as Medicaid, the Children’s Health Insurance Program (CHIP), the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), and Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF).


Conduent’s[Transportation Solutions](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.conduent.com%2Ftransportation-solutions%2F&esheet=54436405&newsitemid=20260303582801&lan=en-US&anchor=Transportation+Solutions&index=5&md5=46a2ab22c8fb90a2e54404e368d8ea21) business provides solutions and services to state and local governments including road-usage charging and mass transit in urban areas. Government clients in the United States and around the world use Conduent Transportation, supporting a range of transportation agencies and operators.


**About Conduent**
Conduent delivers digital business solutions and services spanning the commercial, government and transportation spectrum – creating valuable outcomes for its clients and the millions of people who count on them. The Company leverages cloud computing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation and advanced analytics to deliver mission-critical solutions. Through a dedicated global team of approximately 51,000 associates, process expertise and advanced technologies, Conduent’s solutions and services digitally transform its clients’ operations to enhance customer experiences, improve performance, increase efficiencies and reduce costs. Conduent adds momentum to its clients’ missions in many ways including disbursing approximately $80 billion in government payments annually, enabling approximately 2.0 billion customer service interactions annually, empowering millions of employees through HR services every year and processing over 14 million tolling transactions every day.
