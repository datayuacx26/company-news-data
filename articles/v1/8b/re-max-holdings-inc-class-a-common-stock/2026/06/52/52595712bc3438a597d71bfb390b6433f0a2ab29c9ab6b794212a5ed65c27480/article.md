# REMAX Golf Lifestyles Helps Agents Connect with Golf-Focused Buyers and Sellers

For a growing number of buyers and sellers, real estate decisions start well before square footage or zip codes enter the picture. They start with lifestyle. For golf‑focused consumers, that often means choosing a club, community and experience first, then finding the right place to call home.


That’s the thinking behind REMAX Golf Lifestyles, a first-of-its-kind program designed to help agents connect with homebuyers and sellers whose moves are shaped by golf and country club living.


**Meeting consumers where lifestyle comes first**


REMAX Golf Lifestyles (RGL) is built on the foundation of Golf Life Navigators, an established platform that has already connected thousands of golf-focused buyers and sellers with communities that match their lifestyle preferences. Through RGL, REMAX agents can become certified real estate professionals specializing in golf community living to better serve buyers and sellers interested.


“What this platform does is organize something that’s before been very fragmented,” says Jason Becker, REMAX Vice President of Lifestyles, co-founder of Golf Life Navigators and PGA of America Golf Professional. “It helps consumers clearly define what they’re looking for in a golf lifestyle and then connects them with REMAX agents who actually understand those communities. That’s better for consumers, and it’s a meaningful differentiator for agents.”


Through a guided questionnaire on the[Golf Life Navigators website](https://golflifenavigators.com/) , consumers can weigh factors that matter most to them in finding a golf club or golf-course property, from the number of courses and practice facilities to dining options, social activities and opportunities for junior members. From there, they’re matched with golf communities that align with their priorities and with agents who understand their lifestyle and clubs.


“Golf isn’t a niche sport; it truly is a lifestyle,” says Chris Lim, REMAX President and Chief Growth Officer. “This program offers outstanding education for agents to learn the different clubs, how they operate and what buyers may be looking for.”

**Why it matters for agents**


For agents, REMAX Golf Lifestyles is about sharpening their expertise and insights on the golf operations to meet clients’ specific needs.


“For me, it’s an opportunity to help those who are already club members but looking to move or retire to a different state,” says Nathan Dart, Team Leader of Dart Homes and a certified REMAX Golf Lifestyles agent with REMAX Realty Group in North Potomac, Maryland. “They’re often looking for the same golf club experience they currently have. This program provides the club knowledge and expertise, so I can help get them to the right place.”


Dart, who has been part of the REMAX network for a total of more than 20 years, sees the program as a way to add value by helping consumers connect their next move with the lifestyle they already enjoy.


“People often know the club they want before they know the house or even the state,” he says. “This gives REMAX agents a way to lead that conversation and be the guide.”


By being recognized as preferred agents within club communities, REMAX agents can build additional credibility and long‑term relationships with buyers and sellers who value insider knowledge and trusted advice.


“That trusted advisor role is core to who REMAX is,” Dart says. “This program just puts a new structure and scale around it.”


**A different kind of consumer experience**


For consumers, REMAX Golf Lifestyles offers a more personal and intuitive way to approach a major life move, one that recognizes the emotional drivers behind relocation and retirement decisions.


“Everybody is looking for something different,” Dart says. “This helps narrow that down and connect people with agents who truly get it.”


REMAX Golf Lifestyles is available to REMAX affiliates in the United States and Canada (excluding Quebec). Annual membership includes education on golf course operations, direct club and community networking, and connections to buyers and sellers interested in golf course living. Learn more at[https://www.remax.com/usa/en/lifestyles/golf](https://www.remax.com/usa/en/lifestyles/golf) .
