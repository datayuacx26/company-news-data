# REMAX Agent Bridget Hensley Opens REMAX POINTE, Expanding Agent Support in Greater Houston Area

REMAX has welcomed a new office to its network with the opening of REMAX POINTE, led by Broker/Owner Bridget Hensley. The name REMAX POINTE was chosen intentionally to reflect guidance, direction and purpose – values Hensley believes are central to helping clients navigate some of life’s most important decisions and transitions.


Located at 118 S. 18th St. in West Columbia, Texas, the brokerage serves clients across Austin, Brazoria, Fort Bend, Harris, Matagorda, Montgomery, Waller, Wharton and surrounding counties. The office launches with nine agents and plans to grow, offering expertise across residential real estate, including first-time buyers, rural properties, investment opportunities, coastal homes and more.


Hensley says opening REMAX POINTE represents the next step in her professional growth and long-term vision for supporting agents and clients.


“Staying with REMAX was important to me,” Hensley said. “The brand, tools and network support independent agents in building productive, scalable businesses, and I want to bring that to REMAX POINTE.”


## Bridget Hensley Headshot


Bridget Hensley, Broker/Owner of REMAX POINTE


“REMAX POINTE is designed to give agents the support and structure needed to grow productive businesses while delivering a high level of service to clients,” Hensley said. “The name reflects our commitment to providing guidance, direction and purpose, whether we’re helping clients through a major life transition or supporting agents as they build successful careers.”


Hensley began her real estate career in 2017 with a small real estate company and joined REMAX in February of 2018. She has since consistently built her business within the REMAX network, earning recognition as a top-producing agent, earning the REMAX Platinum Club and 100% Club Awards.


In 2024, she launched The Premier Group, a collaborative faith-based team focused on agent development and client service. Reflecting a shared commitment to excellence, collaboration and client satisfaction, the team has been recognized as a REMAX Titan Club Team.


With an established leadership foundation and plans for continued recruiting and expanding its agent base, REMAX POINTE is positioned to grow its presence and support agents across the region.
