# REMAX Affiliates Recognized for Top Production on the 2026 AREAA A-List

The Asian Real Estate Association of America (AREAA) has released its 2026 A-List, recognizing the top-producing AREAA member agents and teams serving Asian American, Native Hawaiian and Pacific Islander (AANHPI) communities.


The 2026 ranking includes several REMAX affiliates, recognized for their strong sales performance in 2025 across the four categories: Individual Volume, Individual Sides, Team Volume and Team Sides.


Among the honorees, Cecily Cao with REMAX One Realty in Moorestown, New Jersey, ranked No. 1 in Individual Sides, and Tina Guerrieri with REMAX Central in Blue Bell, Pennsylvania, ranked No. 18 in Individual Volume. Lisa Nguyen, Team Leader of the International Group with REMAX Professionals in Lakewood, Colorado, landed in the top 30 for both Team Sides and Team Volume.


REMAX is proud to partner with AREAA in supporting homeownership opportunities and serving diverse communities across the United States. Through initiatives such as the


[State of Asia America Report](https://news.remax.com/new-areaa-report-finds-structural-shift-reshaping-u-s-housing-market-for-aanhpi-households) , agents have access to research and insights that can help them better understand housing trends and the evolving needs of AANHPI consumers.


As the 2026 A-List honorees celebrate their achievements, their success serves as an example of what is possible when production, professionalism and community leadership come together. Congratulations to all REMAX affiliates on the 2026 AREAA A-List!
