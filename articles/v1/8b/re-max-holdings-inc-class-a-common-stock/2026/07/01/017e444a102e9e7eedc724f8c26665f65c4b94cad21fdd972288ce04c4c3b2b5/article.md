# 2026 REMAX Broker Owner Conference Heading to Nashville

Registration is open for the 2026[REMAX Broker Owner Conference](https://www.remaxevents.com/event/2026REMAXBOC/home-2) (BOC), set for August 16–18 in Nashville, Tennessee. The annual event brings together Broker/Owners and managers from across the REMAX network to share strategies, explore industry trends, and connect with peers and company leadership.


The three-day conference will take place August 16–18, 2026 at the Gaylord Opryland Resort & Convention Center, with an agenda focused on technology, business performance, growth and network collaboration.


This year’s BOC will include a unique element: The leadership team from Real Brokerage will be on hand to connect with attendees and share additional context on its pending agreement to combine with REMAX.

This year’s conference will address timely topics, including:


- Navigating growth in a changing market environment


- Recruiting and retention strategies


- Incorporating technology, including AI, into daily operations


- Accelerating brokerage profitability and efficiency


Sessions will emphasize practical applications that Broker/Owners and Managers can adapt within their own businesses. Key events include:


- General sessions featuring company leaders and industry perspectives


- Breakout sessions with hands-on opportunities


- Peer-to-peer mastermind sessions for shared business enhancements


- A supplier marketplace highlighting business tools and services


- Networking opportunities focused on building relationships


Attendees will be able to engage with REMAX senior leaders and connect with peers in similar business ventures. These conversations provide space to exchange ideas, share best practices, and explore approaches that can be applied across markets.
