# Global REMAX Week Returns Oct. 4-10, Highlighting Annual Week of Service Across Network

Global REMAX Week will return October 4-10, 2026, inviting REMAX affiliates worldwide to give back to their local communities.


“One of the strengths of the REMAX network is the way affiliates in different countries can rally around a shared purpose while serving their communities in meaningful, local ways,” says Shawna Gilbert, Senior Vice President of REMAX Global & Commercial. “Global REMAX Week highlights that combination of international connection and local action, and it shows how service can bring our network together across markets.”


During the weeklong event, affiliates across the REMAX network are encouraged to volunteer, support local causes and share their efforts as part of the broader initiative.


[Past Global REMAX Weeks](https://news.remax.com/remax-affiliates-around-the-world-rally-to-support-local-communities-during-global-remax-week) have seen REMAX affiliates participating in local blood drives, donation collections for local animal shelters, bingo nights at senior living communities, and fundraising or volunteer work for established organizations like Habitat for Humanity and


[Children’s Miracle Network](https://news.remax.com/support-for-cmn-hospitals-flourishes-as-remax-donation-milestone-reaches-new-heights) , a REMAX philanthropic partner since 1992.


As this year’s event approaches, REMAX affiliates are encouraged to think big, share often and participate in collective storytelling by posting photos and videos of their volunteer efforts on social media using


**#GlobalREMAXWeek** .


With more than 145,000 agents in over 8,500 offices across more than 120 countries and territories, REMAX is a global network, yet real estate remains deeply local. Global REMAX Week offers a simple but meaningful idea: the strongest networks make the greatest impact when they show up in the communities they serve.


“Global REMAX Week reflects something important about our network: Our reach is global, but our impact is built market by market, community by community,” says Chris Lim, REMAX President and Chief Growth Officer. “When affiliates come together around service, it reinforces the value of connection, strengthens brand visibility and showcases the professionalism that helps agents build


lasting relationships.”


The fundamentals of real estate remain rooted in local knowledge, trusted relationships and visible community presence. Global REMAX Week brings those ideas into focus on an international scale, offering an opportunity to demonstrate how real estate professionals can make an impact far beyond the transaction.
