# Restaurant Brands International Inc. Releases Annual Restaurant Brands for Good Report

## Restaurant Brands International Inc. Releases Annual Restaurant Brands for Good Report


Company Release - 4/29/2026 12:00 AM ET


Download the PDF version PDF Format (opens in new window)


*Annual report highlights meaningful progress across Food, Planet, and People & Communities pillars across four iconic global brands*


**MIAMI, FLORIDA, April 30, 2026** — Restaurant Brands International Inc. ("RBI" or "Restaurant Brands International") (TSX: QSR) (NYSE: QSR) released its[Restaurant Brands for Good Report](https://www.rbi.com/English/sustainability/our-vision/default.aspx) , an annual sustainability report outlining progress and goals across its three pillars: Food, Planet, and People & Communities.


“We want to build a business with lasting impact, for the guests we serve and the communities we operate in, while taking care of the planet we all share. We believe those things build on each other,” said Josh Kobza, Chief Executive Officer at RBI. “With over 33,000 restaurants in more than 120 countries and territories, our four iconic brands — Tim Hortons®, Burger King®, Popeyes®, and Firehouse Subs® serve high-quality, convenient, and affordable food to millions of guests every day. Our Restaurant Brands for Good framework continues to guide how we use our scale to drive positive impact.”


**Highlights from the Restaurant Brands for Good Report:**


- All four RBI brands completed and published updated Green Building Standards, embedded in design guidelines for new restaurant builds and remodels globally.
- RBI procured 100% renewable electricity for all corporate-owned and directly controlled facilities globally in 2025.
- RBI empowered cattle ranchers in the U.S. to adopt more efficient practices that lower emissions through a collaborative supply chain pilot program and reached over 24,700 coffee-farming families through the Tims Coffee for Communities program across six countries.
- Tim Hortons raised C$50M+ through charitable campaigns in 2025, including Smile Cookie, Holiday Smile Cookie, Camp Day, Special Olympics Donut, and Orange Sprinkle Donut. The Tims Classrooms Program reached 10,750 students across seven Canadian provinces.
- Popeyes Foundation distributed $1.5M through its SERVING WITH LOVE® program to support food access initiatives and team members in need.
- The Burger King Foundation and the Firehouse Subs Public Safety Foundation each marked their 20th anniversary, two decades of consistent investment in students and first responders.


The full Restaurant Brands for Good Report is available at[www.rbi.com/sustainability](http://www.rbi.com/sustainability) .
