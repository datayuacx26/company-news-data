# CEO Jack Fusco joins CNBC at Gastech 2025

Cheniere President and CEO Jack Fusco sat down with CNBC on the sidelines of Gastech 2025 to discuss Cheniere’s leading role in supplying reliable U.S. LNG to Europe and how the company is expanding its production capacity to meet the world’s growing energy needs.


At the 53rd edition of the conference, which was held Sept. 9-12 in Milan, Fusco also participated in a panel discussion on energy security and the increasingly vital roles that natural gas and LNG play in the energy mix.


Gastech is considered one of the world’s largest exhibitions and conferences dedicated to natural gas, LNG, hydrogen, climate technologies and AI for energy.


It drew more than 50,000 attendees, including a delegation of about 40 Cheniere leaders and representatives from multiple teams.
