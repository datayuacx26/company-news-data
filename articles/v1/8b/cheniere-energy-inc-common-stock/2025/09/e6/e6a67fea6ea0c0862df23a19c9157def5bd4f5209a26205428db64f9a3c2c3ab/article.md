# Cheniere achieves 4000th cargo-milestone

In March, Cheniere produced and exported its 4,000th cargo of U.S. LNG, loading the Maran Gas Ithaca at the Sabine Pass Liquefaction facility in Louisiana, making Cheniere the fastest company to accomplish the feat.


“


Producing our 4,000th cargo in just over nine years of LNG operations is yet another achievement enabled by the commitment to reliability, safety and operational excellence shared by everyone at Cheniere,” said Cheniere’s President and Chief Executive Officer, Jack Fusco.


​ “


We are proud to reach this milestone faster than any LNG producer in history and look forward to safely and reliably producing the next 4,000 cargoes for our customers as we expand to meet the world’s growing energy needs.”


Cheniere exported its first LNG cargo in 2016. Today, Cheniere LNG has reached more than 40 markets on five continents and accounts for approximately 50% of U.S. LNG exports. Cheniere is committed to providing its customers with reliable and secure LNG.
