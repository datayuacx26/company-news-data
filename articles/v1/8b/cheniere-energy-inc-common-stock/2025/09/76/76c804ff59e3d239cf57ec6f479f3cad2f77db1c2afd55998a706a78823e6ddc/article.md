# Cheniere signs long-term supply deal with JERA

In August, Cheniere entered into a long-term sale and purchase (SPA) agreement with JERA Co., Inc. (JERA), Japan’s largest power-generation company and one of the world’s largest buyers of LNG.


Under the terms of the SPA, JERA agreed to purchase approximately 1 million tonnes per annum (mtpa) of LNG from 2029 through 2050.


“


We are pleased to enter into this multi-decade agreement with JERA, the largest power producer in Japan and one of the largest buyers of LNG in the world,” said Jack Fusco, Cheniere’s President and Chief Executive Officer.


​ “


This SPA fortifies our longstanding relationship with JERA, which is based upon years of cooperation and mutually beneficial LNG trade. We look forward to providing our flexible, reliable and cleaner-burning LNG to JERA through 2050 under this new long-term agreement.”


The agreement formalized the Heads of Agreement (HOA) the two companies announced in June 2025 at the U.S. Department of Energy.
