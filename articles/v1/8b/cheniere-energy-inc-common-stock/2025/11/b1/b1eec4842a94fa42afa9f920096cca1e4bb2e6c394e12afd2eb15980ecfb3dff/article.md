# Extel’s 2025 All-America Executive Team Rankings

Cheniere has topped Extel’s All-America Executive Team rankings for the Natural Gas & Master Limited Partnerships for a fourth consecutive year.


President and CEO Jack Fusco, CFO Zach Davis and VP of Investor Relations and Communications Randy Bhatia have been recognized again as best-in-class, with Cheniere’s board of directors, IR program, IR team and ESG program also garnering No. 1 rankings.


Cheniere also was named one of the survey’s Most Honored Companies.


The 2025 All-America Executive Team honors reflect the input from nearly 4,000 institutional investment professionals across the buy-side and sell-side.


The rankings highlight the ongoing trust and support of the global investment community in Cheniere’s vision, strategy and execution.
