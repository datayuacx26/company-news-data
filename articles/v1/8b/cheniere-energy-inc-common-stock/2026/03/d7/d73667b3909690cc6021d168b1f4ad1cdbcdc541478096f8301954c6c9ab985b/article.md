# CEO Jack Fusco joins CNBC’s ​‘Power Lunch’

Cheniere President and CEO Jack Fusco joined CNBC’s


​ “


Power Lunch” on Feb. 24 from Washington, D.C., to mark the 10th anniversary of Cheniere’s first LNG export cargo.


In a wide-ranging conversation with hosts Kelly Evans and Brian Sullivan, Fusco highlighted Cheniere’s pioneering role in leading the modern era of U.S. LNG and advancing energy security for Europe and allies worldwide.


​ “


What a glorious day it is to be a Cheniere employee,” Fusco said.


Fusco was in Washington to speak at the Transatlantic Energy Security Council meeting, where the 10th anniversary of the first cargo was commemorated. The event was attended by U.S. Interior Secretary Doug Burgum, U.S. Energy Secretary Chris Wright and leaders and ministers from more than 12 European countries.
